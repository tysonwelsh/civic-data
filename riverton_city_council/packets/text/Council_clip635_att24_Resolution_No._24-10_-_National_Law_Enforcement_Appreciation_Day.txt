                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-10

   A RESOLUTION COMMEMORATING THE NATIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT
                 APPRECIATION DAY, JANUARY 9, 2024

        WHEREAS, on January 9, 2016, Concerns of Police Survivors, Inc. (C.O.P.S.) united
several partnering organizations, including: Concerns of Police Survivors; FBI National
Academy Associates; Fraternal Order of Police; International Association of Chiefs of Police;
Officer Down Memorial Page; Law Enforcement United; National Law Enforcement Officers
Memorial Fund; International Conference of Police Chaplains; and the National Troopers
Coalition, in support of law enforcement officers nationwide to promote a National Law
Enforcement Appreciation Day; and

       WHEREAS, annually, across our nation on January 9th, we celebrate National Law
Enforcement Appreciation Day in honor of all the brave law enforcement professionals who
work diligently and tirelessly to protect the public and their property; and

        WHEREAS, our dedicated law enforcement officers undergo rigorous training in order
to best serve and protect the residents of our state and the nation; and

        WHERAS, responsibilities of law enforcement officers require an abundance of physical
strength, courage, and stamina, and an abiding and selfless concern for the safety of others; and

        WHEREAS, these dedicated public servants always answer the call of duty and come to
the aid of those in need of assistance, regardless of the dangers they themselves may face and all
too often pay the ultimate price in order to protect us; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council acknowledges the selflessness and courage of
this Commonwealth's and nation's law enforcement officers; and

        WHEREAS, C.O.P.S. and affiliated organizations seek the support of National Law
Enforcement Appreciation Day in myriad ways, from sending a card of support to their local law
enforcement agency, to wearing blue clothing in support of officers, to sharing a positive story
about law enforcement on social media; and

        WHEREAS, to follow the example of C.O.P.S., the Riverton City Council finds it
appropriate to commemorate National Law Enforcement Appreciation Day; send their profound
gratitude to the men and women in this Commonwealth who give of their own personal safety to
protect others; and grant best wishes to all those law enforcement officers who strive to make our
families, our communities, and our workplaces safe;
     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:


      1. The Riverton City Council hereby commemorates National Law Enforcement
         Appreciation Day (L.E.A.D.) on January 9, 2024, and dutifully honors the law
         enforcement officers who are so integral to our way of life in Riverton City.

      2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
January, 2024 by the following vote:
