MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Nick Whittaker, MCMP
Date: 2025-11-20
Re: Public Hearing: Sunshine Preschool Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
Amendment Request (Administrative Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of an Amended Home Occupation Conditional
Use Permit for approximately 1.61 acres located on the east side of 700 West at
approximately 11501 S. 700 W. The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential
Agricultural). The applicant is requesting that an amendment to their Home Occupation
Conditional Use Permit to operate a limited preschool be approved to increase the
number of students from ten (10) to sixteen (16) per session.




Findings for approval:

  1. The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC Section 9-5-
     080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the imposition of
     reasonable conditions.


Findings for denial:


  1. The proposal does not comply with all standards for approval found in DCMC
     Section 9-5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are not able to be sufficiently
     mitigated through the imposition of reasonable conditions.
