Council Business

Approval of Minutes

              Council Member Bailey, seconded by Council Member Pazell, moved to approve
the minutes of the Copperton Metro Township Council meetings held on January 16, 2019,
February 11, 2019, and February 20, 2019. The motion passed unanimously.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Fiscal Items – Approval of Expenditures

                Council Member Pazell, seconded by Council Member Clayton, moved to approve
legal fees of $3,397.00. The motion passed unanimously.

               Council Member Bailey asked if they are getting itemized expenses from the
County for the services it provides to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD).

                Mayor Clayton reported that they wrote a letter to Salt Lake County and Mayor
Jennifer Wilson has promised to address the issue. The Metro Townships have been subsidizing
services for Millcreek, Cottonwood Heights, and Taylorsville; the GSLMSD is working on
correcting the issue.

Olympia Hills

               Council Member Bailey read a letter that she sent to Salt Lake County regarding
the Olympia Hills development, expressing her concern that the development be studied before

residents.

Legislative Update

               Nathan Bracken reviewed the following bills from the 2019 legislative session:

      Senate Bill 34 – Affordable Housing Modifications
      Senate Bill 187 – County Planning and Services Amendments
      Senate Bill 124 – Local Government Administration Amendments
      Senate Bill 60 – Automatic Local District Withdrawal Amendments
      House Bill 93 – County Formation Amendments

             Council Member Pazell stated he was able to get a $20,000 appropriation for
Copperton Metro Township. He would like to see it used to create a “smart” park.

               Council Member Pazell, seconded by Mayor Clayton, moved to use the
appropriation to create a smart park and create a five-member committee of Council Members
and community members to look into this matter before the disbursement of funds in August. The
motion passed unanimously.

            Mayor Clayton, seconded by Council Member Severson, moved to appoint Council
Members Pazell and Bailey to the Smart Park Committee. The motion passed unanimously.

        Planning Commission

               Curtis Woodward stated he and the Copperton Metro Township Planning
Commission have been reviewing the Salt Lake County zoning ordinances, so they can be
converted to Copperton Metro Township ordinances. He asked the Council if it wanted an open
space ordinance as part of its zoning and adopt a planned community zone, particularly if there
are large areas of land that Rio Tinto may want to annex into Copperton Metro Township.

                 Council Member Pazell stated a planned community zone may be premature
considering the current annexation discussions with Rio Tinto. He would like to table this matter
until a later date.

               Mr. Woodward proposed having a work session to go over historic ordinances.

              Mr. Bracken stated his law firm, Smith Hartvigsen, is the land use counsel for the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) and will be providing training for the
planning commissions and recommended holding off on certain issues until after the training in
April. The Council will need to determine whether it or a hearing officer will serve as the appeal
authority.

        Annexation Committee

                 Council Member Pazell stated Rio Tinto has asked for a proposal on how they
could have vested rights in the commercial land. Mr. Bracken will put together a proposal, which
will likely provide some protections requiring property owner consent before commercial zoning
could change.

           Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

             Mayor Clayton stated they are in process of bringing planning department over to
the MSD. This should be completed by August 31, 2019. He will be focusing on timely billing by

           Southwest Mayors

                  Mayor Clayton stated meeting with southwest mayors during the Legislative
session were very good meetings and well attended by the area delegation. The mayors will
continue to meet monthly now that the Utah State legislative session is completed. The meetings
include Herriman, Bluffdale, Riverton, South Jordan, West Jordan, and Copperton. Kearns asked
to join but it is not in the Southwest, so its request was rejected.

           Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)
