                 WATER SHARES PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT

      THIS WATER SHARES PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”), is
made and entered into as of the ____ day of ____________, 2023 (the “Effective Date”) by and
between PROPERTY RESERVE, INC., a Utah corporation (“PRI”), and RIVERTON, UTAH, a
municipal corporation of the State of Utah (the “City”).

                                            RECITALS

       A.     PRI owns and operates a farm within the boundaries of the City, situated generally
north of 132000 South, between Bangerter Highway and Mountain View Corridor (the “PRI
Farm”). PRI owns a certain number of shares of stock in Welby Jacob Canal Company (the
“Shares”), which historically have been used by PRI to irrigate the PRI Farm.

        B.      PRI anticipates the winding down of its farming operations at the PRI Farm, and
anticipates that certain of the Shares will be available for sale to others.

        C.      The City desires to purchase from PRI any Shares that become available for sale,
up to 1,000 Shares in total, for use in the City’s secondary irrigation system, and the parties desire
by this Agreement to document the mutual understanding of the parties regarding the sale of a
portion of the Shares by PRI to the City.

                                          AGREEMENT

       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, and other good and valuable
consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, PRI and the City
agree as follows:

       1.      DEFINITIONS. All capitalized terms used herein and which are not herein
otherwise defined shall have the meanings set forth in Exhibit A attached hereto.

         2.      PURCHASE AND SALE TRANSACTIONS. PRI hereby agrees to sell, set over,
grant, transfer, and convey Shares to the City, and the City agrees to purchase Shares from PRI, in
tranches or phases (each a “Take Down”), in such numbers, at such times, and at such Purchase
Prices set forth herein, all subject to the further terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement.

                  2.1 First Take Down.        PRI agrees to sell to the City, and the City agrees
purchase from PRI, five hundred (500) Shares as part of the first Take Down, subject only to the
City’s right to terminate this Agreement as to the first Take Down pursuant to Section 5.4 below.
The Due Diligence Period for the first Take Down shall commence on the Effective Date of this
Agreement. On or before the conclusion of the Due Diligence Period for the first Take Down, the
City shall either (i) terminate this Agreement as to such first Take Down, pursuant to Section 5.4
below, or (ii) deliver to PRI a Notice of Purchase for such 500 Shares. Failure to timely deliver a
Notice of Purchase shall be deemed an election by the City to terminate this Agreement as to the


                                                  1
first Take Down. The Closing for such first Take Down shall be scheduled by mutual agreement
of the parties, but shall occur not later than December 1, 2023.

                  2.2 Second Take Down. On or before May 1, 2024, PRI shall determine the
number of Shares it has available to sell to the City during calendar year 2024, which number may
be up to, but shall not exceed, two hundred fifty (250) Shares, and shall notify the City of the
number of available Shares in writing not later than May 1, 2024. The Due Diligence Period for
such second Take Down shall commence on the date of such written notice. On or before the
conclusion of the Due Diligence Period for the second Take Down, the City shall either (i)
terminate this Agreement as to such second Take Down, pursuant to Section 5.4 below, or (ii)
deliver to PRI a Notice of Purchase specifying the number of Shares to be purchased by the City
in the second Take Down. Failure to timely deliver a Notice of Purchase shall be deemed an
election by the City to terminate this Agreement as to the second Take Down. The Closing for
such second Take Down shall be scheduled by mutual agreement of the parties, but shall occur not
later than July 15, 2024.

                 2.3 Third Take Down.          On or before May 1, 2025, PRI shall determine the
number of Shares it has available to sell to the City during calendar year 2025, which number may
be up to, but shall not exceed, two hundred fifty (250) Shares, and shall notify the City of the
number of available Shares in writing not later than May 1, 2025. The Due Diligence Period for
such third Take Down shall commence on the date of such written notice. On or before the
conclusion of the Due Diligence Period for the third Take Down, the City shall either (i) terminate
this Agreement as to such third Take Down, pursuant to Section 5.4 below, or (ii) deliver to PRI a
Notice of Purchase specifying the number of Shares to be purchased by the City in the third Take
Down. Failure to timely deliver a Notice of Purchase shall be deemed an election by the City to
terminate this Agreement as to the third Take Down. The Closing for such third Take Down shall
be scheduled by mutual agreement of the parties, but shall occur not later than July 15, 2025.

                 2.4 Limited PRI Commitment. The number of Shares to be sold to the City
during calendar years 2024 and 2025, if any, shall be determined by PRI in its sole and absolute
discretion, based on PRI’s unrelated needs and commitments, including without limitation the
number of Shares needed by PRI to meet the City’s water exaction requirements in connection
with PRI’s development of the land currently occupied by the PRI Farm. PRI’s obligation to sell
Shares to the City is limited to the initial 500 Shares to be sold in connection with the first Take
Down.

       3.      PURCHASE PRICE. The Purchase Price to be paid by the City to PRI for the
Shares shall be as follows:

              3.1 For the Shares sold and purchased during the first Take Down, Two
Thousand Four Hundred and no/100 Dollars ($2,400) per Share, for a total Purchase Price of
$1,200,000.

               3.2 For any Shares sold and purchased during the second Take Down, Two
Thousand Five Hundred Twenty and no/100 Dollars ($2,520) per Share.




                                                 2
              3.3 For any Shares sold and purchased during the third Take Down, Two
Thousand Six Hundred Forty-six and no/100 Dollars ($2,646) per Share.

               3.4 The Purchase Price for each Take Down shall be deposited by the City with
the Escrow Agent on or before each Closing Date and shall be paid to PRI at the Closing.

        4.      DEPOSIT.       The City shall, upon execution of this Agreement, and again
concurrent with the delivery to the City of a Notice of Purchase in connection with the second and
third Take Downs, deposit with the Escrow Agent a good faith deposit in an amount equal to ten
percent (10%) of the Purchase Price to be paid at the next succeeding Closing (each, a “Deposit”).
Each Deposit shall be invested as directed by the City. Each Deposit (including any interest
earned thereon) shall be (i) applied to the Purchase Price at the Closing, if consummated, (ii)
returned to the City in the event of a PRI Default, or (iii) forfeited to PRI as liquidated damages
in the event of a City Default.

        5.     DUE DILIGENCE. The City may, in connection with each Take Down, conduct
due diligence on the Shares to be purchased as part of such Take Down, as more fully set forth
below.

                 5.1 Due Diligence Period.            During each Due Diligence Period, the City
and its agents, engineers, consultants and representatives (collectively, “City's Agents”) shall
have the right to: (a) confirm that the Shares to be purchased are owned by PRI; and (b) make
investigations with regard to points of diversion and facilities affecting use of the Shares by
the City. The City shall rely solely upon its own due diligence examinations and inquiries
and acknowledges that PRI has made no warranties or representations with respect to the
Shares upon which the City has relied.

                   5.2 PRI Cooperation. PRI shall reasonably cooperate (at no cost,
expense, or liability to PRI) with the City in its performance of the City’s due diligence during
each Due Diligence Period, including without limitation, in any requests to obtain information
about the Shares then in the possession of PRI or its consultants, agents, employees, and contractors.
In particular, after commencement of each Due Diligence Period, PRI shall promptly deliver
to the City evidence that the Shares are owned by PRI, together with any other
documentation reasonably requested by the City regarding ownership, encumbrances and
the like.

                  5.3 Delivery of Notice to Purchase.           If, based on its due diligence review,
the City determines to purchase Shares, it shall, on or before the conclusion of each Due Diligence
Period, deliver a Notice of Purchase to PRI, and shall concurrently deliver the required Deposit with
the Escrow Agent.

               5.4 City Determination Not to Purchase.        The City reserves the right
to determine not to purchase the Shares in connection with the relevant Take Down if the
City raises articulable objections to PRI’s ownership of the Shares or related


                                                   3
encumbrances, and PRI is unable or unwilling to remedy such objections. In the event the
City determines not to purchase Shares in connection with a Take Down, it shall notify PRI in writing
of such decision and terminate this Agreement as to such Take Down. In all events, if the City has not
delivered to PRI a Notice of Purchase prior to the expiration of the Due Diligence Period, then this
Agreement shall be deemed terminated as to such Take Down.

                   5.5 Inspection of Facilities.       With respect to the City's right to inspect
the facilities related to use of the Shares, the City agrees that (i) PRI shall receive not less than
48-hours prior notice of each inspection, (ii) each inspection shall be performed during normal
business hours or at such other times as PRI and the City shall mutually agree, and (iii) the City
and the City's Agents shall use all reasonable efforts to minimize any disruption to the farm
operations or any other use found on the PRI Farm. All inspections, examinations and other
reviews conducted by the City or the City's Agents pursuant to this Agreement shall be performed
at the sole risk and expense of the City and the City shall be solely responsible for the acts or
omissions of the City and any of the City's Agents brought on, or to, the PRI Farm by the City.
In addition, the City shall defend, indemnify and hold PRI harmless from and against all loss,
liability, damage or expense (including reasonable attorneys' fees and court costs) resulting
from claims for bodily injury, wrongful death or property damage against PRI arising from any
act or omission by the City or any of the City's Agents in connection with any inspection of the
Shares, any facilities, or other records by the City or the City's Agents.

       6.      USE OF THE SHARES.

               6.1    Use Prior To Closing. Prior to each Closing, PRI shall use the Shares and
the water available under the Shares as provided in this Section 6. All consents required to be
given by City under this Section 6 may be withheld, conditioned, or delayed in City’s sole and
absolute discretion.

              6.2      Maintenance of Shares. Prior to each Closing, PRI shall continue to manage
and use the Shares in the manner in which they have historically been used and managed.

                6.3     Prohibited Actions. Prior to each Closing, PRI shall not, without the prior
written consent of the City: (i) enter into any contract, agreement, or obligation relating to the
Shares that will be binding on the City after such Closing, except in the ordinary course of business
and which is terminable on thirty (30) days’ notice without penalty; or (ii) enter into, permit, grant
or effect, as applicable, any transfer, sale, lien, encumbrance, assignment, conveyance, easement,
license or other agreement of any kind (whether written or oral) in connection with the Shares, or
any part thereof.

                11.1 City’s Remedies. In the event of a PRI Default, the City shall give PRI

and Sale Transaction; (ii) cancel this Agreement in accordance with Section 12 below, in which
event any Deposit will be returned to the City and PRI will reimburse the City the actual costs
incurred by City related to its Due Diligence Review; or (iii) in the event the PRI Default relates

action for damages against PRI in law or equity. The City’s rights and remedies set forth in this
Section 11 shall be cumulative and the pursuit or obtaining of any one remedy does not preclude

above, in the event the City terminates this Agreement at any time due to a PRI Default which has
not been cured during the cure period described above, PRI shall be liable to the City for all out-
of-pocket costs incurred by the City with respect to each Purchase and Sale Transaction, including
without limitation, (a) all costs related to the City’s Due Diligence Review, (b) any and all
attorneys’ fees, and (c) all costs of any third party consultants.

                 11.2 PRI’s Remedies. In the event of a City Default, PRI shall give the City
written notice of such default and thereafter the City shall have ten (10) Business Days to cure

to exceed thirty (30) days, and then only if the City commences such cure within the initial ten
(10) Business Day period, and thereafter diligently pursues the cure to completion). In the event
the City fails to cure a City Default within said period, PRI’s sole remedy, in PRI’s sole discretion,
is to terminate this Agreement and to retain any Deposit as liquidated damages, PRI and the City
hereby agreeing that it would be impracticable and extremely difficult to fix the amount of PRI’s
actual damages and further agreeing that the Deposit is a reasonable estimate of the amount PRI
might be damaged as a result of the City’s failure to perform under this Agreement.
