MEMO
To: City Council
From: Mike Barker, City Manager
Date: 2025-02-18
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1634


Comments:

Mayor Walker serves on multiple board and commissions and looks out for the best
interest of the City, its residents and businesses. Mayor Walker’s extensive service on
these boards and commissions not only exceeds his basic duties but has also been
instrumental in safeguarding Draper City’s interests. His leadership on the Point of the
Mountain State Land Authority (POMSLA) is particularly noteworthy, as it positions Draper
to effectively address the significant impacts of the state’s relocation of the prison and
the development of The Point project, thereby ensuring that the needs of our residents
and businesses are prioritized in these crucial decisions. His service often comes at the
expense of time spent with his family and at a personal financial sacrifice as a self-
employed attorney.
