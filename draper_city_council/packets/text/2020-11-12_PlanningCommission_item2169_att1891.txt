BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 19.969 acres located on
the west side of Lone Peak Parkway, at approximately 267 West 11915 South (Exhibit B). The property is
currently zoned R3. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow a
modification to the southern boundary line of the subdivision in order to comply with a court order that
set the location of the boundary line.

A subdivision plat was approved for the property in 2013 in order to subdivide the property into three
separate lots. Two of those lots were developed as the American Preparatory Academy (APA) charter
school, and the other is a vacant piece of property that is used to house animals. On June 13, 2019, the
applicant obtained a plat amendment to combine the two school lots within the subdivision. The current
subdivision contains two lots, one with the charter school campus and one vacant.

APA has had an ongoing property line dispute with the property owners to the south of their campus. As a
result of this dispute a court order has set the location of the shared property line between APA and the
property owner to the south. This has necessitated a plat amendment to modify the plat to reflect the court
ordered south boundary line location.

The property has been assigned the R3 zoning classification, supporting approximately three dwelling
unit per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the
purpose of the R3 zone is to “permit medium density residential development without special mitigation
requirements. This zone is intended to provide incentives to foster residential development with little
impact on its surroundings and on municipal services, and to generally preserve the semirural character
called for in the density element of the general plan.” The R3 zoning abuts the subject property on the
north. The CBP (Business/ Manufacturing Park) abuts to the east. The CSD-LP (Lone Peak Commercial
Special District) zone abuts on the south. To the west is the UTA Frontrunner line and the A5
(Agricultural, 5 acre lot minimum) zone.

Subdivision Layout. The plat amendment will relocate the southern boundary line north about 7-feet to
10-feet. This relocation will happen along all of Lot 3 and a portion of Lot 4. The area will equal 0.136
acres or 5,932 square feet. Lot 4 will be renamed Lot 5 and Lot 3 will be renamed Lot 6 with this plat.
Both lots will still comply with minimum lot sizes, frontage, and lot width standards within the R3 zone.
No other changes are being proposed to the property. (Exhibit E)

request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:
