PUBLIC HEARING/ISSUANCE OF INDUSTRUIAL DEVELOPMENT REVENUE
BONDS/RESOLUTION:
     PUBLIC HEARING AND CONSIDERATION OF A RESOLUTION APPROVING
     AND AUTHORIZING THE ISSUANCE OF INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT
     REVENUE BONDS, SERIES 2020, IN AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $7,500,000
     BY THE CITY OF ST. GEORGE, UTAH, AS ISSUER, FOR THE CONSTRUCTION
     AND RELATED IMPROVEMENTS OF A HEALTHCARE FACILITY FOR
     SOUTHWEST UTAH COMMUNITY HEALTH CENTER, INC. DBA FAMILY
     HEALTHCARE, A UTAH NON-PROFIT CORPORATION, IN ORDER TO PROMOTE
     THE GENERAL WELFARE OF THE RESIDENTS OF THE STATE OF UTAH.

       City Attorney Shawn Guzman explained this resolution would authorize the issuance
       in an amount not to exceed $7,500,000 bond under the Utah Industrial Facilities and
       Development Act with the City as the conduit issuer. He explained the proceeds will
       be used for the construction of a new facility in the City for the Southwest Utah
       Community Health Center, Inc. dba Family Healthcare. The City acts as a conduit
       under the Act and does not create an obligation to the City, but rather is payable
       solely from the bond payments made by the Southwest Utah Community Health
       Center, Inc.

       Lori Wright with Family Healthcare spoke about what services their facility provides
       for the members of the community. They have been in business since 2002 and
       have served over 14,000 individuals, with 50,000 visits in Washington and Iron
       counties. The proposed building is about 33,000 sq. ft. and will be located at 2300
       South Riverside Drive.

       Councilmember Randall commented she is excited for the new facility.

      Councilmember McArthur noted the public may not be aware of how much of an
      asset the Family Healthcare facility is.

      Aaron Wade with Gilmore Bell noted a public hearing is required. Because the bonds
      are conduit bonds for a 201C3, there are specific requirements for the hearing. He
      explained the resolution allows the City to authorize up to a $7,500,000 industrial
      revenue bond and establishes a path to finalize the terms. Part of the agenda packet
      is the draft agreement; it is a 3-party loan agreement with the City, Family
      Healthcare as the borrower, and Zions Bank as the lender. There is no recourse to
      the City, the responsibility is on the borrower to make the payments.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman commented, if the Council is inclined to approve the
      resolution, it should be subject to approval by the Legal Department as there is some
      language to worked out.

      Mayor Pike opened the public hearing.

      Mr. Wade noted as part of the requirements for this hearing a 1-800 phone number
      is required to be available. He used his firm’s phone number and there are no
      comments on that line.

      Mayor Pike closed the public hearing.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember McArthur to approve the
                    resolution authorizing the issuance of Industrial Revenue Bonds Series
                    2020 in an amount not to $7,5000,000 by the City of St. George, as
                    issuer, for the construction and related improvements of a healthcare
                    facility for southwest Utah Community Health Center, Inc dba Family
                    Healthcare for the general welfare of the residents of the State of Utah
                    subject to approval by the Legal Department.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.
      VOTE:

                    Councilmember Randall – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.

COMPREHENSIVE ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT:
    Presentation of the audited Comprehensive Annual Financial Report (CAFR)
    for Fiscal Year 2020.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard spoke about the tremendous job Finance Manager
      Tiffany LaJoice has done on this report.

      Finance Manager Tiffany LaJoice introduced Steve Palmer with Hinton Burdick CPA's
      & Advisors and provided a history for Mr. Palmer.

      Steve Palmer with Hinton Burdick CPA's & Advisors explained his firm issued an
      unmodified opinion on the financial statements. With regards to the report on
      Internal Control over Financial Reporting and on Compliance, there are no findings to
      be included. The last report on is legal compliance – there were no significant items,
      but there were two (2) minor items in relation to the Central Business District CDA
      reporting a deficit fund balance and the Ft Pierce EDA No. 1 over expended its
      budget by a bit. He pointed out these findings and management’s response can be
      found on page 174 of the CAFR. The federal single audit report has been delayed
      due to Covid-19. He explained the City received a significant amount of coronavirus
      relief funds and the federal compliance supplement has not yet been released giving
      them instruction on how to audit the funds. The State Auditor has allowed an
      exception for this report to be issued separately from the CAFR; it will be due in
      March. He mentioned that Trevor Coombs has been working with the grant
      administrator at the state level to make sure the funds have been properly spent.

      Mayor Pike noted he has heard that the City is retuning Cares Act funding.

      Mr. Palmer added all required reports will be submitted by the end of the year; the
      federal single audit report will be submitted by the end of March.
