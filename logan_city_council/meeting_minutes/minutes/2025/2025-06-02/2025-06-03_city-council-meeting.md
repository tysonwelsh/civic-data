Logan Municipal Council                 Logan, Utah                         June 3, 2025

Minutes of the meeting of the Logan Municipal Council convened in regular session on
Tuesday, June 3, 2025, in the Logan Municipal Council Chambers located at 290 North
100 West, Logan, Utah 84321 at 5:30 pm. Logan Municipal Council Meetings are
televised live as a public service on Channel 17 and the City of Logan YouTube channel
at: https://bit.ly/LoganCouncilMeetings

Vice Chair Mike Johnson, Councilmember Amy Z. Anderson, Councilmember Ernesto

Daines, City Attorney Craig Carlston, Finance Director Richard Anderson, and City

Chair Simmonds welcomed those present. There were approximately 18 in attendance at
the beginning of the meeting.
OPENING CEREMONY:
Cache Refugee and Immigrant Connection (CRIC) – Mary DaSilva, Member and Board
of Trustees for CRIC provided the opening ceremony and led the audience in the pledge
of allegiance.
Ms. DaSilva thanked the Council for the opportunity to highlight World Refugee Day on
June 20. CRIC has been supporting refugees and immigrants since its foundation in 2014.
They are an official resettlement organization, and despite recent setbacks, they will
continue to support the immigrant and refugee communities. There is a global refugee
crisis, and it is one of the most pressing humanitarian challenges of our time. CRIC will
celebrate World Refugee Day at Willow Park on June 14 and invite the community to
participate. They recognize the contributions of immigrants and refugees to our society,
and they reaffirm their commitment to protecting their rights and ensuring their well-
being. CRIC has welcomed 200 refugees from 12 different countries. They need the
community, businesses, educators, and civic leaders to teach the community, to foster
empathy and the continued support of CRIC.

QUESTIONS AND COMMENTS FOR MAYOR AND COUNCIL:

Chair Simmonds explained that any person wishing to comment on any item not
otherwise on the agenda may address the City Council at this point by stepping to the
microphone and giving his or her name and address for the record. Comments should
be limited to not more than three (3) minutes unless additional time is authorized by
the Council Chair. Citizen groups will be asked to appoint a spokesperson. This is the
time and place for any person who wishes to comment on non-agenda items and items
that are germane or relevant to the authority of the City Council. Items brought
forward to the attention of the City Council will be turned over to staff to respond to
outside of the City Council meeting.
Matthew Fatuesi, a resident of Logan implored that the Council be open to different
possibilities in regard to renewable resources.
Kristi Richards, a resident of Logan was not in favor of the decision made by the Council
in regard to the Light and Power resolution and expressed concerns about climate change.
Patrick Belmont, a resident of Logan reiterated his previous statements and again stated
that he is not in favor of the decision made by the Council in regard to the Light and Power
resolution. He also expressed concerns about climate change.
Gail Hanson, a resident of Logan reiterated she too was opposed to the decision made by

Mayor Daines announced that Sadie Boyer, Civil Engineer with the City of Logan was
awarded the Outstanding Young Professional of the Year Award from the Utah Chapter
of the Public Works Association. Nominations were gathered from around the entire
State of Utah and Sadie was selected. She was hired as an Engineering Intern while
working on her undergraduate degree at Utah State. U graduation she was hired as a
fulltime Civil Engineer as she continued to work on her Master’s Degree. Ms. Boyer has
managed some of the city’s most complex Capital projects including the 100 West Bridge
and Corridor, Main Street Pedestrian Underpass, 100 North Bridge, and many other
Road, Water and Sewer projects.

Update: Main Street Underpass Status – Paul Lindhardt, Public Works Director
(22:05)
Public Works Director Paul Lindhardt addressed the Council and gave an update on the
Main Street Underpass. In conjunction with UDOT, a pedestrian underpass will be
constructed at approximately 600 South. Construction is anticipated to begin this
summer. There is not a detailed construction plan available yet, but the contract has been
awarded to ACME Construction with Stanley Consultants acting as overseers of the
project.
The overpass is being constructed out of precast concrete box covert and confirming a
date for delivery is difficult. The anticipation is the boxes will begin to arrive the first
week of September with construction beginning at the end of July or August. Main
Street will be closed to one lane in each direction for up to 60 days, and up to 10 nights
for full closure. If on schedule, it is anticipated the tunnel will be completed by the end of
October or the beginning of November. There is a Public Relations Manager who has
started meeting with the adjacent businesses and homeowners in the project area and
prepare them for the impacts.
Chair Simmonds requested signage be posted further ahead of the project area such as the
Logan River Golf Course or at 100 West alerting drivers of the road closure.
Mr. Lindhardt confirmed there will be requirements for the contractor to have traffic
control in place and the closer to the project the more signage there will be.
