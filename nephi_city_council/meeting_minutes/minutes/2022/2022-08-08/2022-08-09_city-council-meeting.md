                         Public Hearing/Work Session Minutes
                                    August 9, 2022
                                       7:00 p.m.
                                      21 E. 100 N.
                                   Nephi, UT 84648

1.    Mayor Seely opened the public hearing to accept public comment on the amended
     budget for fiscal year ending June 30, 2022. Mr. Atkinson explained each line item on
     the amended budget. One of the main amendments included establishing budget
     amounts for a newly created Fleet Fund.
     No public comment was given.
     Mayor Seely closed the hearing at 7:05 p.m.

2.    Jim Ockey addressed the council about the history of the City and the Ockey family
     concerning water coming from Bradley Springs and the easements across the Ockey
     property. Mr. Ockey did not like the draft agreement prepared that would begin charging
     the Ockey’s for raw water they are using from Bradley Springs. Mr. Ockey said that
     historically the Ockey family had always received free water in exchange for Nephi City
     being able to have easements across his property to access Bradley Springs.

3.    The council discussed the 138kV electrical line project. The council directed staff to
     move forward with the negotiations with property owners for easements for the project.
     The negotiations include annexing several properties into the city. Councilor Memmott
     expressed his concern about the different costs of the annexations and thought we
     would be better to negotiate a price instead of annexing and providing services to the
     property owners. The other council members thought that the annexation route was the
     best option.

4.    The council discussed the concept zoning map. Mr. Atkinson showed where the high
     voltage power lines are located on the east side of I-15.

5.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed an IT contract with Rocky Mountain Technology Services. The
     council directed staff to go forward with the Mayor signing the contract. The contract
     would be brought back in a regular meeting for ratification.

6.   Mr. Atkinson reported that one of the big pine trees in Pioneer Park has beetles and
     needs to be removed.

7.   Mayor Seely asked the council if they wanted to be a sponsor at the football games.
     The council agreed to be a tailgate sponsor.

8.    Mayor Seely updated the council about the day of service on September 11, 2022. He
     had two projects that were being considered. One being cleaning the trail between Orgill
       ball field and Canyon View ball field and the other project would be cleaning up litter on
       the west bench.

   9. Councilor Worwood asked Mr. Atkinson about adding more “No Dogs Allowed” signs at
      Vine Bluff Cemetery.

   10. Councilor Parady reported that Representative Burgess Owens will be in town this next
      week.

   11. 9:00 Mayor Seely opened a regular meeting.

   12. Councilor Ostler moved to approve the amended budget for FY ending June 30, 2022.
      Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
       vote.

   13. Councilor Memmott moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the engagement letter with
      Larson and Company. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
      unanimous vote.

   14. Councilor Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character
      and competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Parady seconded the
      motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

   15. Meeting adjourned at 9:05 p.m.
