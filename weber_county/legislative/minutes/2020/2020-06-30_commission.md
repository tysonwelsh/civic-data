G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. REQUEST FOR RATIFICATION OF THE 2020 TAX SALE PROPERTIES
      This item was held until next week.
   2. CONTRACT WITH ROBERT FAVERO FOR FAVERO’S LEGACY CLUSTER SUBDIVISION IMPROVEMENTS
         Felix Lleverino, of the County Planning Division, stated that this item was reviewed to ensure that the Zoning,
         Subdivision, and the previous Cluster Subdivision ordinance regulations/standards were adhered to and that this
         includes the public improvements being dedicated to the county (e.g., roadway, public utility easements & a
         preservation easement at open space areas). The financial guarantee is in the amount of $56,323.56. On
         3/12/2019 the Planning Commission recommended approving this item and Planning staff also recommends
         approval with the conditions stated in the staff report.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the Subdivision Improvements Guarantee Agreement with Robert
         Favero for Favero’s Legacy Cluster Subdivision Phase 2; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
   3. FINAL APPROVAL OF SUMMERSET FARMS SUBDIVISION PHASE 1, CONSISTING OF 16 LOTS
         Tammy Aydelotte, of the County Planning Division, stated that this subdivision is located at 2267 S. 3500 W.
         in the A-1 Zone, that the Planning Commission recommended approval last summer, and that since then the
         developer has been installing improvements. This approval includes acceptance of road dedication, both
         continuation of existing county roads and newly created roads. The developer has received final approval for
         water from Taylor West Weber and Hooper Irrigation, and has annexed into Central Weber Sewer. In the staff
         report Planning staff recommended approval with the final condition that a fence must be installed along the
         Wilson Canal, and that if they do not install it, they need to escrow for it.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to grant final approval of Summerset Farms Subdivision Phase 1 (16 lots) with
         staff’s conditions for fencing; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
   4. RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF CROSS-APPEALS FOR 2020 CENTRALLY ASSESSED PROPERTIES
      – RESOLUTION 16-2020
         Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney, stated that each year the State Tax Commission assesses properties
         within each county that cross county lines. For these centrally assessed property appeals, the counties have the
         ability to request to join that appeal process. PacifiCorp has filed an appeal for property in Weber County.
         Taxpayers have about a 90 day window to appeal and the county has 60 days to request joining that appeal.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 16-2020 authorizing the filing of cross-appeals for 2020
         centrally assessed properties; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    5. CONTRACT WITH POST BROS. LLC, TO PROVIDE STORAGE/MAINTENANCE BUILDING AND OUTDOOR
       STORAGE FOR WEBER COUNTY PROPERTY MANAGEMENT
         Bryce Taylor, of County Property Management, stated that this is a 6-month extension agreement for $1,850.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Post Bros. LLC, to provide storage/maintenance
         building and outdoor storage for Weber County Property Management; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    6. CONTRACT WITH SKAGGS TO ESTABLISH SKAGGS AS THE PRIMARY VENDOR FOR SHERIFF’S UNIFORM SERVICES
         Kristen Durrant, of the County Sheriff’s Office, stated that this item was put out to bid and there were three
         responders. Skaggs had the best prices by a significant amount. Ms. Durrant addressed Commissioner Jenkins’
         question providing the terms and the opt-out option.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contract with Skaggs to establish Skaggs as the primary vendor
         for the Sheriff’s Office uniform services; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    7. CONTRACT WITH ZOOMGRANTS TO ESTABLISH TERMS RELATED TO THE USE OF THE ZOOMGRANTS
         ONLINE APPLICATION AND GRANT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
         Holin Wilbanks, of County Economic Development, stated that this is the application process program to
         administer the Weber Cares Act funding grant. It is a 1-year subscription for $5,000.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with ZoomGrants to establish terms related to the use of
         the ZoomGrants online application and grant management system; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    8. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO OGDEN VALLEY PLANNING COMMISSION – RESOLUTION 17-2020
         Rick Grover, County Planning Division Director, stated that there were eight applicants for the two positions.
         The commissioners interviewed the candidates yesterday for this and the next two items, and they stated that
         they were all highly qualified.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 17-2020 reappointing John L. Lewis and to appoint Ron J.
         Lackey to the Ogden Valley Planning Commission through 6/30/2024; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    9. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO WESTERN WEBER PLANNING COMMISSION – RESOLUTION 18-2020
         Rick Grover, County Planning Division Director, stated that there were four applicants for the three positions.
         Chair Froerer moved to adopt Resolution 18-2020 appointing Bruce L. Nilson, Jed J. McCormick, and Sarah R.
         Wichern to the Western Weber Planning Commission through 6/30/2024; Commissioner Harvey seconded stating
         that Jannette Borklund, an applicant, had served for 12 years and had great institutional knowledge and integrity.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
   10. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT – RESOLUTION 19-2020
         Rick Grover, County Planning Division Director, stated that there was one full time position and one alternate.
         Neal Barker was the only applicant and Mr. Grover recommended him for the full time position. He has reached
         out to Jannette Borklund, who has a wealth of knowledge, for the alternate position but had not heard from her
         yet.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 19-2020 appointing Neal Barker to the Board of Adjustment
         to the full time position through 6/30/2024, and that if Jannette Borklund agrees, that she be appointed to the
         alternate position (which she subsequently did); Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
