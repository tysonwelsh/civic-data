Explanation of Emigration Canyon’s Local Government, How it all Works Together and its Unique
Purposes
Mayor Smolka stated the purpose of this meeting is to discuss leadership in the canyon
and how each entity works together.
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)
Bart Barker stated the GSLMSD is the service provider for the Metro Townships and the
residents of unincorporated Salt Lake County. The GSLMSD receives its funding from the Metro
Townships and unincorporated County and contracts with Salt Lake County agencies to provide various
services. Funding come from sales tax, Class B and C highway funds, fees, grants and other revenues.
The GSLMSD Board allocates funding, establishes policies, and sets service levels. The GSLMSD is
made up of the Metro Township Mayor’s and a Salt Lake County Council Member. The GSLMSD role is
to evaluate the needs of all the Metro Townships and unincorporated County and to use the funding to
accomplish those needs. The GSLMSD is the service provider and they oversee all contracts with
agencies to provide the services. The Board oversees property tax authority over the District. Payroll and
benefits will be moved from Salt Lake County to the GSLMSD and by the end of August 2019, Salt Lake
County Planning and Development Services will be moved over to the GSLMSD.

Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission
Robert Pinon, Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission, stated the
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission is comprised of seven commissioners and are
appointed by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council. The Planning Commission reviews and
makes recommendations on the general plan, FCOZ, planning, building and permitting that requires
additional public hearings, and conditional uses. Salt Lake County helps with administrative support.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emigration Canyon Community Council (ECCC)
Steve Borst, Chair, Emigration Canyon Community Council, stated the ECCC is a
nonprofit organization providing grass roots representation and a voice for the residents of Emigration
Canyon. The ECCC produces a newsletter, announcements, social media, and reviews matters that
impact Emigration Canyon. It organizes the annual Firewise event that educates the public on fire safety.
Firewise will be held on May 4, 2019. The ECCC consists of 12 elected volunteer members that are
Emigration Canyon residents. Members are elected for four-year terms and six members are up for
election every even year. The ECCC has working groups that take on special issues like trails.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emigration Improvement District (EID)
Dave Bradford, Emigration Improvement District, stated the EID is a special service
district that levy’s taxes and are responsible for water and sewer services. The EID is directed by an
elected three-member board of trustees and a manager to over see day-to-day activities.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council
Mayor Smolka stated the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council is a five-member
board that oversees local ordinances and municipal services. It receives sales tax and class B road
money. The Council hired its own legal counsel, David Church. Council Members sit in a voting capacity
on the following boards:

years. He has been a commissioner for two months.
