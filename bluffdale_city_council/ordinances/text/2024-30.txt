                                CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH

                                 ORDINANCE NO.2024-30


 AN ORDINANCE OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL AMENDING 7.50.010 TO INDICATE
 THAT A DISPOSITION OF SIGNIFICANT REAL PROPERTY EXCLUDES THE CONVEYANCE
     OF NON-PERPETUAL EASEMENT, LEASE, OR LICENSE FOR A PUBLIC PURPOSE

       WHEREAS,Chapter 7.50 of the Biuffdale City Code governs the disposition of
significant parcels of real property;

       WHEREAS,the City Council desires to amend the definition of disposition in
7.50.010 to exclude conveyances of non-perpetual easements, leases, or licenses for a
public purpose;

NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:


       Section 1.     Adoption. Ordinance 2024-30 is hereby adopted, amending Chapter
7.50.010 of the Biuffdale City Code as shown in Exhibit A.

       Section 2.      Amendment of Conflicting Ordinances. If any ordinances,
resolutions, policies, orzoning maps of the City heretofore adopted are Inconsistent
herewith they are hereby amended to comply with the provisions hereof. If they cannot be
amended to comply with the provisions hereof,they are hereby repealed.

       Section 3.     Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable,such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and
independent provision, and such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance
shall be severable.


      Section 4.     Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED: December 11,2024

7.50.010 Definitions


DISPOSITION: Any sale, exchange,lease, encumbrance or other conveyance of property.
excluding the conveyance of a non-perpetual easement, lease, or license for a public
purpose.


PROPERTY: Real property, including land and improvements thereon.
