SCOPE OF SERVICES:

Services will include but are not limited to the following:

Firm is to annually review all the City’s products and services.

The Firm shall assign a personal account representative. The representative is to have a
minimum of five (5) years of experience and a minimum of three (3) years with the proposing
Firm.

Firm will be responsible for soliciting and coordinating proposals from insurance carriers,
coordinating and providing responses to potential insurance carriers, evaluating bids or
proposals from insurance carriers, and make recommendations to the City.

Firm is to ensure that all contracts, plan documents and summary plan descriptions
are produced and executed in a timely manner. Review and advise the City as to
whether the contract meets the terms negotiated.

Firm is to regularly review carrier(s) and/or TPA(s) options and recommend implementation
strategies to manage and/or reduce cost

Firm is to analyze and present plan design and plan utilization information on a
quarterly basis to the Benefits Insurance Committee.

Firm is to secure renewal estimates for the upcoming plan year by February of each
year the contract is in effect. Evaluate the renewal proposals and report findings to
the City in writing. The consultant should have the ability and skill to negotiate the
renewal proposals when appropriate.

Every year the firm is to create, print and present to City employees an “Employee Benefits
Guide.”
Firm is to be a resource to City administration regarding benefits and insurance.

Firm is to be a liaison between insurance providers and the City.

Firm is to work with insurance providers in the resolution of claims and billing reconciliation.

Firm will be involved in reviewing current City policies and procedures for compliance with the
Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”), recommending policies
and procedures for the City to comply with HIPAA.

Firm will be involved in reviewing current City policies and procedures for compliance with the
Health Insurance requirements of the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act of 2010
(PPACA), recommending policies and procedures for the City to comply with PPACA.

Firm is to provide complete COBRA compliance and client/carrier coordination that may include
but not limited to the following: setup; qualifying event notification; beneficiary billing, collection,
processing; takeover; initial COBRA notification; HIPPA certificates of credible coverage;
monthly administration & renewal.

Firm is to provide complete management of CAFT Section 125 plans which may include but are
not limited to Health Reimbursement Accounts and Flexible Spending Accounts.
Firm may be required to coordinate, conduct and participate in employee meetings, trainings,
benefit/health fairs and wellness programs.

Firm is to hold multiple training seminars throughout the year to help City Staff stay in
compliance in areas such as benefits, human resource, risk management, etc.

Firm is to provide a web-based software tool that is a complete employee communication
system used for open enrollment, benefits coordination, and submissions.

Firm is to provide human resource services to help eliminate possible wrongful employment
liabilities. Services may include, but not limited to the following: HR assessment, HR consulting,
assistance with new hire/termination and health wellness programs.
