     2. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT WITH THE UTAH
         REGIONAL BALLET FOR TOURISM PROMOTION
         (Track A, 18:48)
Jill Moulton, Operating Board President of Utah Regional Ballet, explained they have been a non-profit
organization in Utah County for 35 years. She said they changed their name to Utah Metropolitan Ballet
in the last 60 days. She described the volunteers that have helped the organization. She said they are
looking for funds to promote tourism that will encourage participation from out of state. Commissioner
Lee explained the process that occurred to come up with the amount for the promotion. Commissioner
Ivie commended the organization on the quality of their application. Commissioner Graves suggested
the amount authorized should be doubled due to the quality of their productions. Paul Jones explained
this agreement was drafted so that these funds are granted under a reimbursement basis. He
questioned whether this would be unsurmountable for the ballet. Commissioner Graves countered this
is not what has been always done in the past. He suggested it be modified this to where they prove the
use of the funds. Paul explained that is why he brought this up. Jill commented this would be more
conducive to their operating process rather than reimbursement. Commissioner Lee recommended
there be a report back after the production on the use of the funds. Paul confirmed what would need to
be amended on the agreement. Commissioner Graves amended his earlier statement that these funds
should not be a one-time allocation. He recommended the ballet come back again to apply for more
funds.
         Commissioner Graves made the motion to approve and authorize the commission to execute
         the agreement as stated with the following changes that we strike the paragraph beginning
         with payments made to the ballet and amend the following paragraph that says the ballet will
         submit to the county a final report and add the sentence that it has expended funds in
         compliance with the purposes and amounts by category established herein and
         accomplishments of ballet’s Nutcracker performance by March 1, 2018. The motion was
         seconded by Commissioner Ivie and carried with the following vote:
                                                         AYE: Bill Lee
                                                                 Greg Graves
                                                                 Nathan Ivie
                                                         Nay: None
