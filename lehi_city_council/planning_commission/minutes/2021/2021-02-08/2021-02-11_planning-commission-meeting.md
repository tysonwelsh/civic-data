2.5 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review and
recommendation of a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 26, Accessory Uses,
adding conditions for detached garage ADU’s (Accessory Dwelling Units) and increasing
the maximum height of accessory structures.
    Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Hemmert stated that he is not in favor of this amendment, especially with it
    abutting up against the lot line. Commissioner Everett stated that he does not like putting the
    burden of determining significant impact on the Commission. He agrees with Commissioner
    Hemmert and is not in favor of the amendment. Commissioner Ellis clarified that this would
    include new and existing builds. Jacob Curtis, City Planner, stated they would restrict the
    half acre lot designation. Commissioner Nielsen stated he could understand converting a
    habitable use.

    The applicant, Lehi City, was present.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

    The reason this amendment came about was because Myer's allowed an attached garage to
    have an ADU, but realized many with existing garages could not utilize the approved code.
    The Commission discussed that this could create issues with building an attached garage,
    waiting, and coming back at a later date to obtain a permit for an ADU that is right next to
    the lot line. Kim Struthers stated they could put language in the amendment that contains
    existing structures that also includes a variance setback away from the lot line, although there
    are standards that have to be met to do that. Ross Dinsdale stated that most builders won't go
    to the expense of building without following the conditional use if there was a setback.
    Commissioner Peterson stated it does open up a lot of subjectivity for the planning
    commission, and depending on who is on the planning commission at the time it could leave
    a negative impact on the neighbor.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked how many legal forming ADU's are in the City right now.
    Kim Struthers stated there were 120 licensed and 170 applications. Commissioner Hemmert
    expressed his concern for the amount of subjectivity and leeway this amendment could have.
    Commissioner Ellis wasn't as concerned with the amendment where it is a conditional use
    and it could be addressed as a case by case basis. Commissioner Nielsen asked for
    clarification that limits buildings to one story with the exception on lots with an approved
    detached garage accessory dwelling unit. Commissioner Bunker expressed his concern about
    the amendment and that variances would be the best course of action. Kim Struthers stated
    there are specific reason attached to the property that warrant an exception to grant the
    variance and the hearing officer would have to look at those reasons.

    Lehi City withdraws their application due to the Commissions discussion on the agenda item.
