7-11-010: DEFINITIONS:

SERVICE PROVIDER: The city, its employees, or a county-licensed waste disposal contractorperson
working for the city engaged in providing solid waste, refuse, or recycling collection service for any
location within Draper City.



7-11-030: CITY COLLECTION SERVICES:

A. Requirements:

1. Refuse Collection Services: Except as provided in 7-11-030(B), a service provider shall collect,
remove, and dispose of refuse by providing once aweekly collection at all residences within the city on a
schedule and route to be determined by the city.

2. Recycling Collection Services: Except as provided in 7-11-030(B), a service provider shall collect,
remove and dispose of recyclable items by providing bi-weekly collection every two weeks at all
residences within the city on a schedule and route to be determined by the city.

B. Subdivisions Accessible by Private Streets (Private Subdivisions): Effective November 1, 2025, Draper
City will not directly provide collection services to Private Subdivisions. The city may arrange collection
services for Private Subdivisions currently serviced directly by the city through a qualified third-party
provider. Collection charges for these subdivisions will be subject to the applicable service charge, as
outlined in the city’s consolidated fee schedule, which may differ from public street rates to account for
the additional costs of private street collection. Alternatively, Private Subdivisions or individual residents
may choose to independently contract with another service provider for refuse and recycling collection.
To ensure uninterrupted service, those opting for independent contracts must notify the city in writing.:
Subdivisions that are only accessible only by private streets may not qualify for city collection services
provided by the city. The city may determine whether these subdivisions are eligible for refuse and
recyclable item collection. In the event the city determines the subdivision is not eligible for collection
by the city:

1. The city shall provide collection services through a third-party service provider, subject to the
applicable service charge established in the city’s consolidated fee schedule, which may differ from rates
for public streets to reflect additional costs.

2. Alternatively, the subdivision or individual residents therein may elect to contract independently with
another service provider for collection of refuse and recyclable items. Subdivisions or residents electing
to contract independently must notify the city in writing to ensure continuity of service.
