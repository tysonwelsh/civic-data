RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 21-25 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Parcel Boundary Adjustments, Police Outside Employment Services and Parks
Pavilion Rental Cleaning Deposit Fees.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs. The proposed change for the Joining Lots is to replace “Lots” with “Parcels”. There is no
change for the fee.

The proposed fee change for the Police Department for the police office outside employment services is to
consolidate the two different fees into one rate of $58 per hour. The one fee will help with internal tracking
and billing processes and will also help with the requesting organization. The $58 per hour fee covers all
expenses related to providing these services.

The proposed fee change for the Pavilion rental is for the refundable cleaning deposit. The current deposit fee
of $25 does not cover the cost and time for staff to clean and prepare the pavilion for the next rental. The
proposed fee will cover these costs if cleaning is needed.

Only the items or sections that are proposed for changes are listed in Exhibit A. Items highlighted in blue are
the new proposed fees or description.
