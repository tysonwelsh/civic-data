B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-09-02 Springlake 2026 Budget 
Claudia made a motion to approve the Springlake 2026 Budget. April seconded the 
motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
Springlake Apartments is a 13-unit property that receives financial assistance through the 
USDA. Each year, an annual budget is required by the USDA and must be approved by the 
HAUC Board of Commissioners. The 2026 budget was reviewed by HAUC staff on a li ne-by-
line basis. The 2026 budget was determined by utilizing the actual expenses incurred year to 
date (January 2025 – August 2025), and the anticipated market conditions for 2026. 
 
C. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-09-03 4
th on 4th Demolition 
Steve made a motion to approve the 4th on 4th Demolition Contract. Claudia seconded 
the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC purchased a commercial property in Orem, Utah, containing 2 existing buildings. As  
part of the due diligence process to purchase the property, HAUC received demolition bids 
from local contractors to remove the building and site improvements. Following the purchase 
of the property, HAUC staff continue to work with the State and other stakeholders to prepare 
the buildings and property for demolition. HAUC determined that the best approach for the 
property is to demolish the buildings but leave the asphalt, concrete, and trees in place at this 
time. This will help improve the conditions for the contractor staging at the property when the 
property is ready for development. 
 
D. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-09-04 COLA 
April made a motion to approve the COLA. Claudia seconded the motion. The motion 
passed unanimously. 
 
 HAUC provides a Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA) to all staff when market conditions 
warrant and it is approved by the Board of Commissioners. A COLA is a percentage increase 
of one’s income designed to household income maintain purchasing power. Purchasi ng power 
erodes when market prices escalate on goods and services due to inflation.  Currently , the 
2026 COLA is anticipated to be 2.8%. Furthermore, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics just 
released its Consumer Price Index (CPI) report, which indicates the 12-month average 
percentage increase as of August 2026 for all items less food and energy of 3.1%.  
HAUC’s management recommends a COLA be approved for HAUC staff effective 9-08-2025.  
 
E. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-09-05 EHV Funds 
April made a motion to approve the EHV Funds. Claudia seconded the motion. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC administers an Emergency Housing Voucher Program. The program began in May 
2021 as part of the American Rescue Plan Act of 2021 (ARPA). PIH Notice 2025 -19 indicates 
the programs will end in 2026. Prior to this notice, HUD created a policy indicating PHAs 
administering EHV were not to issue any new vouchers in the EHV program. Due to the timing 
and change in policy, a voucher being ported was classified by HUD as a new issuance,  which 
was beyond the date established by HUD. HUD recently concluded, following its review of the 
HAUC-provided data, that HAUC is to return an amount of non-program funds to HUD to 
reimburse for this specific single voucher.
