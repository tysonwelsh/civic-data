City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1673

On September 2, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1673 approving
the Big Willow Creek 6th Development Agreement Amendment for Approximately 9.3
acres of property located at approximately 11670 S. Wildrye Field Way within Draper
City. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
