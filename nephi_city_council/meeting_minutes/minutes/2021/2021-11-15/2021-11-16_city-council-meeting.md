presented the mayor and council with a copy of the financial statements and audit reports.

MANGELSON PROPERTY ZONE CHANGE ORDINANCE 11-16-2021-A APPROVED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reported that property owned by the Mangelson’s at approximately
100 West and 750 North was zoned as R-3 and they had requested to add the zero lot-line overlay to the
current zone. He explained that there are similar zones in the area as well with the zero lot-line overlay.
The zero lot-line over lay would allow the property owner to construct twin homes on the property. He
stated that the planning commission had reviewed the request and a public hearing was held and the
commission is recommending the change.
   Councilor Worwood moved to grant the request for the addition of the zero-lot-line overlay to the
Mangelson property and adopt Ordinance 11-16-2021-A. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

POWELL YARDS ZONE CHANGE ORDINANCE 11-16-2021-B APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reported that The Powell Yards subdivision was currently working through the approval
process with the Planning Commission. He explained that this is a unique subdivision submittal that has
been received in between the Sun Ridge Ranches and the Phase A & B of the Red Cliffs subdivision. The
previous owner of this property, Dean Powell, was approached about developing while the two phases
of the Red Cliffs subdivision were proposed. Negotiations were ultimately not reached between the
developers of Red Cliffs and the Powell property. This left a narrow parcel of land that was difficult to
develop. Mr. Powell tried various ways to develop the property but could not come to a satisfactory
proposal before his passing.
   Mr. Jordan Jones has now purchased the property and is proposing a subdivision that will enable
adjacent landowners to purchase parts of the property and expand their own lots. The DRC has
recommended the following conditions that would need to be recorded in a development agreement:
     No primary dwellings can be constructed on the auxiliary lots.
     No auxiliary exterior dwellings can be constructed on the auxiliary lots.
     The new subdivision lots can only be merged with lots that are contiguous with lots of plat A, &
        plat B of Red Cliff Subdivision, or Sun Ridge Ranches Subdivision.
     Animal rights will only apply to lots that have merged and are 0.5 acre minimum in size.
     No utilities shall be installed in the Powell Park property division. Utilities may be brought from
        an existing primary dwelling to the contiguous property.
     The 15' access easement shall be covered with 6" of road base.

In this way, the property could be developed in a unique way and only Lot 1 would contain a buildable
lot for a primary dwelling. This lot would face 1000 South.
   This subdivision needed a zone change from the R1-H zone to the R-1 zone to comply with the lot
sizes proposed. Staff feels this is the most appropriate way that this parcel of land could develop.
Ordinance 11-16-2021-B would approve the zone change.
   Councilor Memmott moved to grant the zone change and to adopt Ordinance 11-16-2021-B.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 11-16-2021 TEMPORARY LAND USE PROHIBITING SUBDIVISON DEVELOPMENT
APPLICATIONS ADOPTED:

   The council at an earlier work session had discussed the uses in the Combined Use Zones of the city.
They were wanting to review the code and to make changes that better fit their vision of the city. City
Attorney Kasey Wright drafted an ordinance to prohibit development in the Combined Use Zone for six
months while changes can be made to the code. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 11-16-
2021. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 11-16-2021-C AUTHORIZING CITY ADMINISTRATION TO ASSIGN PHYSICAL ADDRESSES WITH
THE CITY ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the Juab County Recorder had requested that an ordinance be
adopted authorizing the city administrator to assign addresses within the city in order for an address
change that needed to be made on a commercial site on the south end of the city. Councilor Memmott
asked about the naming streets names instead of numbers and if we could codify the policy of only
numbers being used for street names. Mr. Atkinson said that the city could put that practice in the
general plan in the transportation section.
   Councilor Ostler moved to adopt Ordinance 11-16-2021-C. Councilor Memmott seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

GORDON MCPHERSON EASEMENT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

 Mr. Atkinson reported that an agreement had been reached with Gordon McPherson to obtain power
easements across his property north of the hospital. The following three easements were obtained:
       1. 99’ Road ROW/Utility easement for 1750 N
                a. This easement runs east and west and will be underground to allow ground to
                continue to be farmed.
                b. This will allow power from substation #3 to be delivered to development on the
                north side of Nebo Heights. This also helps have a looped connection for any homes and
                businesses on the north that are fed from Substation #1.
       2. 25’ utility easement along the fence by railroad tracks for power poles from substation to the
       Mona turnoff
                a. Power will be run aerially
                b. This easement will be used for a transmission line from Rocky Mountain Power’s
                substation to Substation #3.
       3. 25’ utility easement along the fence by railroad tracks for power poles along Old Highway 91
       from Hansen Fabrication to the Oberg property.
                a. Power will be run aerially
                b. This easement will be used for a transmission line from Rocky Mountain Power’s
                substation to Substation #3.

Councilor Worwood moved to authorize Mayor Pro Tem Justin Seely to sign the Gordon McPherson
Easement Agreement. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

GENERAL ELECTION CANVASS:

  City Recorder/Election Officer Lisa E. Brough presented to the council the official election results and
explained that Utah code states that the mayor and the municipal legislative body are the board of
municipal canvassers for the municipality. The board of canvassers shall review the summary reports
prepared by the election officer and certify the results. After reviewing the election results, Councilor
Memmott moved to certify the official election results as presented. Councilor Ostler seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote. (copy attached)
