                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 26-09

AN ORDINANCE REZONING APPROXIMATELY 2.82 ACRES LOCATED AT 12317,
12325 & 12341 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD FROM R-4 AND RM-6-SD TO RM-6, ERIC
                         SAXEY- APPLICANT.

  WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
        recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map to RM-6 for the approximately 2.82-acre
parcel(s) located at 12317, 12325, & 12341 Redwood Road .

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is,
                           amended to reflect the described changes as shown in
                           Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.             This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 19th day of
May 2026 by the following vote:
