BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a site plan application for approximately 0.36
acres located at approximately 690 E 12200 S (Exhibits B & C). The property is zoned O-R. The
applicant is requesting that a site plan be approved to allow for the development of the
property as an office and residential mixed-use building.

The property was historically residential, with a manufactured home previously located on
the site which has recently been removed. The proposed mixed-use building consists of an
office and garage on the main floor and two (2) residential units located above on the second
floor. The garage is intended as a flexible space for the office use and could be used as
additional office or meeting space or for the storage of business-related equipment.

According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-11-080, the purpose of the O-R
zone is to “provide administrative, clerical, and professional offices, of a residential scale and
character, to serve nearby residential and commercial areas, as well as the city as a whole.
These uses are characterized by low volume, direct daily customer contact. This district also
provides for accessory medium-high density residential (0 to 12 du/ac). This district is
designed to be a transitional zone, and should be used to buffer low density residential uses
from more intense land uses, districts, and heavily traveled transportation routes. The
property development standards, while strict in order to protect adjacent low density
residential uses, are designed to be flexible enough to allow experimentation in office and
housing design, and to allow housing constructed within this district to incorporate its own
protection from more intense adjacent uses.”

Site Plan Layout. The site plan (Exhibit F) shows the location of the two-story, 3,791 ft2 mixed-
use building on the 0.36 acre property. Parking is provided to the west and south of the
building. The placement of the mixed-use building complies with minimum setbacks of 10 ft
on the north, east and west sides, and 15 ft on the south side where the property is adjacent
to the RA1 zone, thus meeting the setback requirements for the O-R zone. Additionally, per
DCMC Section 9-11-080(C), the O-R zone requires that residential uses shall not occupy more
than forty-nine percent (49%) of the total building square footage. Per the submitted floor
plans (Exhibit G) the residential units occupy forty-nine percent (49%) of the total building
square footage and meet the requirement.

Landscaping and Lot Coverage. The O-R zone requires that developments with residential
uses provide a minimum of thirty percent (30%) of the net lot area in open space. The site
plan shows that 31.1% of the net lot area has been provided as open space. The landscape
plan (Exhibit H) shows where the proposed landscaping will be placed around the mixed-use
building. Landscape areas cover 22% of the gross site, which exceeds the minimum
requirement of 20% in ordinance. The O-R zone also requires that private outdoor living
space be provided for each of the proposed units. The size of the private outdoor living space
for second floor units must be a minimum of five percent (5%) of the gross size of the
dwelling unit. The proposed plans show the private outdoor living spaces for each unit will
be provided through patios and balconies, of a size equal to 10% of the gross size of each
dwelling unit, which is in excess of the minimum requirement. Six (6) existing trees will be
removed from the site. These trees are located in areas where proposed buildings or
vehicular and pedestrian access necessitates their removal per DCMC Section 9-23-070(F).
The proposed landscaping complies with the development standards of the zone in DCMC
Section 9-11-080(D) and other landscaping requirements of DCMC Chapter 9-23.

Criteria For Approval. The criteria for review and potential approval of a Site Plan request are
found in DCMC Section 9-5-090(E). This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

         E. Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a
             site plan:
            1. The entire site shall be developed at one time unless a phased development
               plan is approved.
            2. A site plan shall conform to applicable standards set forth in this title, including
               but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking,
               landscaping, and building materials.
            3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the
               general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
            4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the
               subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
            5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards

               drainage, and utilities concerns.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request based on the suggested
findings and the criteria for approval, or denial as listed within the staff report. The Planning
Commission should also, hear from the applicant, and receive public comment prior to
making a decision.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the Site Plan request, staff recommends they
include the following conditions of approval:

         1.        That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                   Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                   site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
         2.        That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
                   development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
         3.        That the required reciprocal cross access easement for access to and from
                   parcel No. 28-29-304-018 be recorded prior to issuance of a land disturbance
                   or building permit, or a Zoning Administrator decision.
