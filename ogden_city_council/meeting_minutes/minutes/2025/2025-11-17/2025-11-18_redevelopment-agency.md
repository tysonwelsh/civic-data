Proposed Resolution 2025-15 approving the project master plan for a
mixed-use development within the Trackline Economic Development
Project Area, located at 768 West 24th Street
          A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding the proposed MU Zone Master Plan
came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated the request before the City Council and RDA involves three
coordinated actions for the property at approximately 768 West 24 th Street: (1) a proposal to amend the zoning code to establish
site-specific standards for a new Mixed-Use (MU) zone; (2) a proposal to rezone four parcels from single-family (R-1-5) to MU;
and (3) a proposal to have the Ogden Redevelopment Agency (RDA) adopt a master plan for the property, which is located within
the Trackline Redevelopment Project Area. The petitioners, Tyler M. Gray and Teresa McCandless, have proposed developing 16
three-story live/work townhomes with ground-floor commercial uses and residential units above. On May 7, 2025, the Planning
Commission considered the proposal and recommended approval with conditions to ensure design quality, maintain multiple land
uses, and align with the West Ogden Community Plan’s vision for walkable mixed-use development along 24th Street.
          Planning Director Brierley summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the third
component of the proposal, the proposed MU Zone Master Plan. He noted the City Code requires RDA approval of a master plan
for a complete area within a redevelopment district that has MU zoning. He reviewed the same information provided during the
earlier City Council meeting regarding the proposed zoning and development plan and concluded by asking for Board support of
the proposed resolution approving the master plan. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the
meeting.
          Chair White introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2025-15, entitled:
                   “A resolution of the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency Board approving the project master plan for a mixed-
                   use development within the Trackline Economic Development Project Area, located at 768 West 24 th Street.”
          Chair White then called for public input regarding the proposal.
          No persons came forward to be heard
