ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
ZT-7-2021   Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zoning ordinance text change
            to define and establish parking standards for townhomes in Sections 7-1-103
            and 7-9-104. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
            ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal is from the City and the first text
                 change being proposed is to add a definition for townhomes. This definition
                 will help with regulating the parking concerns associated with townhome
                 developments. Steve said that the City Council is proposing the parking
                 standard for townhomes be comparable to those for single-family dwellings.
                 He explained that there is an existing requirement of two parking spaces per
                 unit. City Council is wanting to add a second requirement stating that
                 dwelling units not on or adjacent to a public street with no more than two
                 lanes shall also provide an additional 0.75 spaces per unit. He explained that
                 the reason the Council is proposing this standard is that the appreciation of the
                 housing market is causing families to purchase more affordable townhomes
                 instead of single-family homes.

                 Commissioner Fuller asked Steve how someone could park a car in 0.75 of a
                 space. Steve explained that the ratio is 0.75 spaces/unit so, you will multiply
                 the total number of units by 0.75 to get the number of extra spaces required
                 for that development. Commissioner Lovato added that there would be three
                 additional parking spaces for every four units.

                 Commissioner Winters said that he is supportive of this proposal. He stated
                 that the two parking spaces per unit are already required, right? Steve said that
                 is correct. Commissioner Winters asked if that included the garage parking.
                 Steve said that is correct; the requirement for a townhome is a two-car garage.
                 However, the Council’s concern is that the garage is not being used for
                 parking, causing owners to park elsewhere.

                 Steve explained that there was a negotiation over parking for most proposed
                 townhome proposals last year. The Council wanted to set more precise
                 standards to eliminate the parking negotiation for every proposed
                 development.

                 Commissioner Winters wonders if parameters for the two required parking
                 spaces should also be added to the parking standards. Steve said there
                 currently is not a minimum dimension for the garage. However, we can add a
                 provision that addresses the garage parking. Commissioner Lovato said he
                 would support that idea.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SA-8-2021    Petition by GARY CHRISTENSEN requesting preliminary approval for the
             Lake Park Place Subdivision Lot 1 Amended. The subdivision will amend lot
             1 of the original subdivision to create a new building lot. The subdivision
             consists of 2 lots on 32 acres, is zoned BRP (Business/Research Park) and M
             (Manufacturing) and is located at approximately 4099 West Lake Park
             Boulevard. (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-
             ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman presented the original plat to the Commission. He explained
                 that the applicant subdivided one lot into two lots. Steve said that Lot 2 was
                 developed, and Lot 1 remained vacant. He stated that the subject property,
                 Lot 1 of the original plat, applicant would like to subdivide this lot into two
                 parcels. The parcel to the north is labeled Lot 1B, and the southern lot is Lot
                 1A.

                 Steve said that the applicant is proposing a shared drive off of Lake Park
                 Blvd. He explained that they were looking to develop this property several
                 years ago and asked public works if they could open up the median, located
                adjacent to the access, allowing for complete turning movements. After
                evaluating the location, Steve stated that public works would permit the
                median to be opened up.

                Commissioner Winters asked Steve if there were any proposed tenants. Steve
                asked Jody Knapp to provide the answer. Jody said that Lot 1A would be a
                secured site rented by a medical manufacturing company, and Lot 1B would
                be developing a four-story office building. She stated that she isn’t sure who
                will be renting that office building.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-22-2021   Petition by VESTAR requesting a conditional use for Outdoor Events at the
            Valley Fair Mall. The property is located at 3601 S. Constitution Blvd. on
            43.83 acres and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at
            801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Jody Knapp stated that this proposal is for approval of outdoor event uses at
                the Valley Fair Mall. She explained that the applicant is proposing to hold
                these events in the JC Penney parking lot and the plaza area on the west side
                of the Mall. Jody presented the list of proposed events to the Commission.
                She explained that the summer concert series has caused noise complaints in
                the past, which prompted conversations about adding conditions to certain
                outdoor events.

                Jody presented the Salt Lake Valley Health Department Regulation #21
                regarding noise regulations. She stated that the proposed events could be to
                follow the same rules. Jody said another option would be for the City to
                develop their noise regulations naming these outdoor events “Municipal
                Approved Events.” However, the City would be responsible for all noise
                complaints.
              Jody said we also could limit the number of events held during the year or use
              the event list provided as an exhibit. She asked the Commission what their
              thoughts were.

              Chair Meaders asked if these events were new to the Valley Fair Mall. Jody
              said that the car show has been running for a while, but most Town Center
              events are new. She explained that the Parks Department is working with the
              applicant on the farmer’s market.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if the majority of the complaints regarded noise
              levels. Jody said that the example she was given was about the noise level.

              Commissioner Winters said that the noise level would be his biggest concern,
              and a decent noise ordinance should be in place. Jody stated that all outdoor
              events should be held during mall hours, so the latest they would run would be
              9:00 pm. She said a condition could be added stating that all outdoor events
              will run during mall hours.

              Steve Pastorik feels that a condition regarding event times should be added.
              He explained that if an event were to run outside mall hours, they would need
              to provide restrooms.

              Jody noted that she thinks the proposed events are a great idea with the proper
              conditions outlined in writing.

C-23-2021     Petition by ST. CLAIR SIGNS, requesting conditional use approval for two
              pole signs. The property is located at 4770 S 5600 W on 36.53 acres and is
              zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
              brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that this application is a request for two-pole signs.
              He pointed out that this proposal will remove the two existing monument-
              style signs and construct two identical multi-tenant pole signs. Brock stated
              that the proposed signs look similar to monument signs. However, they are
              too large to meet the City’s monument sign requirements. He said the
              applicant would not be able to add any additional pole signs on the subject
              property.

              Chair Meaders asked if the proposed signs would be illuminated. Brock said
              they might have lights directed towards the signs, but they will not be
              illuminated.

C-24-2021     Petition by ARCHITECTURAL COALITION, requesting a conditional use
              amendment for an office building and outside storage yard. The property is
              located at 7011/7041/7055 W Gates Ave on 1.31 acres and is zoned M
                 (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
                 brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Brock Anderson explained that the subject property is made up of three
                 parcels zoned M (Manufacturing), have the same property owner and are
                 being used for a stucco business.

                 Brock explained that this proposal will be constructed in three different
                 phases. He stated that the applicant will first remove the garage from the
                 existing office building to begin the proposed two-story office building
                 located more in the center of the three parcels. Once the new two-story
                 building is constructed, they will remove the building on the west side of the
                 subject property and add the proposed parking. Brock said the last phase
                 requires the residence on the eastern side of the property to be removed. He
                 explained that this residence is currently occupied and there is an arrangement
                 that allows the occupant to remain in the home until they move or pass away.
                 Brock stated that once the building to the east is removed, they will finish the
                 proposed parking on that side.

                 Brock noted that the property in question is surrounded by agricultural and
                 residential uses. Therefore, the applicant is required to have a 20 foot
                 landscape buffer and a 6 foot masonry wall on three sides.

                 Brock explained that he has not received much information about what the
                 applicant will be storing outside. He stated that the biggest concern would be
                 the surfacing and screening. Brock does not feel there will be much of an
                 issue with the screening given the addition of the 6 foot masonry wall. Brock
                 stated that if it's okay with the Commission, he will just include in his staff
                 report that any outside storage needs to meet the surfacing and screening
                 requirements outlined in the code.

Potential Zone Change

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik said that last year staff had brought to the Planning Commission
              properties that were zoned R-1-8 or some other zone that would allow future
              homes to be built. He reminded the Commission that the legislature removed
              several of the design standard requirements during the legislative session last year
              unless they are part of a development agreement.

              Steve gave an example of a property where the owner had agreed to enter into a
              development agreement containing the design standards. Therefore, this property
              would not need to be rezoned. Steve stated that he has communicated with the
              property owner several times and still has not received the development
              agreement back from him. As it sits now, Steve explained, the property owner
              could decide to subdivide the property and build homes on the lots that would
              only need to meet the current design standards. He asked the Commission if they
              feel this should be brought back for a rezone.

              Chairperson Meaders said it should be added to a future agenda to be rezoned.

              Commissioner Winters asked Steve if the issue was the property owner not
              responding. Steve said he has communicated with the property owner on several
              occasions; however, he has not sent the development agreement back yet.
              Commissioner Winters asked Steve how long he had been waiting to receive the
              document. Steve said about a year. Commissioner Winters feels after a year; it
              should be brought back to the Commission.
