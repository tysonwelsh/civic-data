WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 5, 2023

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 9:15 a.m. on October 5, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow,
County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Gil Almquist was excused.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: GMG Jerry Curtis, US Navy, Retired

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3180, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS BY NAMING

WASHINGTON COUNTY AS THE LEAD AGENCY FOR THE ADMINISTRATION OF
THE GRANT UNDER THE SAFE STREETS INITATIVE

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Table Resolution No. R-2023-3180, A
Resolution Approving the Recommendation of the Council of Governments by
Naming Washington County as the Lead Agency for the Administration of the
Grant Under the Safe Streets Initative. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3181, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENTS FOR  GRANT-FUNDED MENTAL

HEALTH PROVIDERS

Children’s Justice Center Director Kristy Pike explained that the Children’s Justice Center (CJC)
contracts with community providers for mental health providers. This Resolution would allow the
Commissioners to blanket approve these providers as recommended by the CJC.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3181, A
Resolution Approving Professional Services Agreements for Grant-Funded
Mental Health Providers. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION _ NO._R-2023-3182, A RESOLUTION OF
APPOINTMENT OF A WASHINGTON COUNTY REPRESENTATIVE AND _AN

ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIVE FOR THE UTAH COUNTIES INDEMNITY POOL
ANNUAL MEMBERSHIP MEETING

Commissioner Victor Iverson explained that the Utah Counties Indemnity Pool (UCIP) requires
that an official representative and an alternate be appoint to be Washington County’s official
representative at the UCIP Membership Meeting in November. It is recommended that
Commissioner Iverson be the official Washington County representative and Commissioner Snow
be the alternate representative.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3182, A
Resolution of Appointment of a Washington County Representative and an
Alternate Representative for the Utah Counties Indemnity Pool Annual
Membership Meeting. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3183, A RESOLUTION PROVIDING
FOR OVERFLOW _ PARKING DURING THE 2023 HUNTSMAN WORLD SENIOR

GAMES

Deputy Washington County Attorney Devin Snow stated that Washington County is a sponser of
2023 World Senior Games, and as such is willing to allow the parking garage to be opened for the
participants of the Senior Games.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3183, A
Resolution Providing for Overflow Parking During the 2023 Huntsman World
Senior Games. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3184, A RESOLUTION AWARDING
A GRANT OF FEDERAL FUNDING UNDER THE AMERICAN RESCUE PLAN ACT TO
TUACAHN CENTER FOR THE ARTS

Devin Snow explained that American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds may be used to respond to
negative economic impacts of COVID-19. Tuacahn Center for the Arts is a non-profit corporation
that has experience negative economic impacts, and the County desires to provide Tuacahn a grant
of ARPA funds to provide assistance.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3184, A
Resolution Awarding a Grant of Federal Funding Under the American Rescue
Plan Act to Tuacahn Center for the Arts. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and
Iverson voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:31 a.m.
