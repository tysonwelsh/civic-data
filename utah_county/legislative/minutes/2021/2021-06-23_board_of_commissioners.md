GORDON HAIGHT, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR/UTAH REDISTRICTING COMMISSION
(GAVE PRESENTATION) ~ 1N 2018 THE STATE PASSED A REFERENDUM TO CREATE
AN INDEPENDENT COMMISSION TO REDISTRICT AFTER THE 2020 CENSUS THAT
COMMISSION HAS BEEN FORMED. THEIR RESPONSIBILITY IS TO TAKE THE
CENSUS WHEN IT IS PROVIDED AND THAT LOOKED LIKE AUGUST 16™ IS WHEN
WE'RE TOLD THE CENSUS DATA WILL BE AVAILABLE. WE'VE BEEN WORKING
WITH ESRI (ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS RESEARCH INSTITUTE) AND IT APPEARS
IT’LL BE ANOTHER WEEK OR TWO BEFORE THE DATA IS REFINED ENOUGH TO BE
USED AND SO AUGUST THROUGH SEPTEMBER WE'RE GOING TO BE CREATING
MAP FOR THE CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT, THE STATE HOUSE, THE SENATE AND
THE STATE SCHOOL BOARDS. IT IS CRITICAL THAT WE HAVE PUBLIC INPUT
BECAUSE THIS IS GOING TO SET THE VOTING DISTRICTS FOR THE NEXT DECADE
IN HOW THE ELECTIONS ARE CARRIED OUT AND IT’S GOING TO BE A BIG IMPACT.

UTAH COUNTY FROM WHAT I UNDERSTAND IS GOING TO GROW BY ABOUT $1
MILLION PEOPLE OVER THE NEXT 10-20 YEARS AND THIS IS GOING TO BE
SIGNIFICANT FOR YOU AND HOW YOU'RE REPRESENTED ON THE FEDERAL AND
STATE LEVEL SO WE THINK IT’S IMPORTANT TO MAKE YOUR RESIDENTS AWARE
OF THE EFFORT TO REDISTRICT THE STATE AND TO GET THEIR INPUT.
