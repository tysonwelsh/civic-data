NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—September 11th @ 7pm.

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Granite Library manager, Kim Welch, shared information about upcoming classes
          and events at the Granite Library.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood gave some City related highlights.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Williams gave some informational comments about the Jordan
          River.

       6. INFORMATION.
             a. Civilian Review Board Quarterly Report
                Civilian Review Board Chair, Joy Glad, gave a review of the CRB’s 2nd quarter
                report that spanned from April-June of 2024. The CRB reviewed a total of 47
                cases and found 45 cases to be within Federal and State regulations, as well
                as City policy. One case was found to be inconclusive, and one case was
                found to not be within policy. The latter came with a formal letter of
                recommendation from the CRB to the police department.
