24t St Interchange, Master Plan discussion

Presenters John Jensen, McKayla Dunfey and Delaney Silman presented the Gateway to Ogden
plan, highlighting UDOT's redesign of the I-15 and 24th Street interchange to address traffic needs.
The plan includes community feedback from two workshops, emphasizing preserving Ogden's
unique character, revitalizing historic areas, and promoting walkable, connected neighborhoods.
Key themes were community identity, economic development, and transportation. The future land
use map includes categories like business flex, commercial, and mixed-use. The plan also features
a detailed action table and a plant list for the 24th Street Corridor. Public comments suggested
improvements in re-wording and adding revitalizing single-family housing.

Zone Ogden discussion on Special Use Standards

Presenters John Jensen and Delaney Silman gave an overview with slides of the Special Use
Chapters (60-91).

The next series will cover development standards in the zoning regulations, with an overview of
each chapter and specific questions to be addressed.
