Unified Police Department

              Chief Brian Lohrke updated the Council on the following:

    Youth academy was started last week with 27 students.

    Amendments to a gambling machine bill is currently being considered at the State
     Legislation. The bill is in the House of Representatives

    The State Legislature is also working on a bill relating to body cameras.

    Promotions resulted in a vacant Domestic Violence Detective position.

    Interviews are being held for Traffic Enforcement Officer.

    Arrests were made for a violent crime that was committed in December 2019. The arrests
     solved two shootings in Kearns, a carjacking in Magna, and a homicide in West Valley.

    Narcotics investigations are ongoing in Kearns.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Parks and Recreation

              Wayne Johnson updated the Council on the following:

    Parks and Recreation will look to the Council for direction relating to parks within its
     jurisdiction. He will provide input on what is happening in other areas, particularly the use
     of bathrooms. Part-time staff will be assigned to work at parks starting the first of April.

               Mayor Bush stated she would like to keep the bathrooms closed for the parks
within the Kearns Metro Township until we see what happens with the Coronavirus.

    Oquirrh Park should be finished on August 1 of this year. This is a regional park.

    David Gourley Park received funds to rebuild the restrooms and pavilion and fund new
     playground equipment. Bids should be submitted within the next 30 to 45 days.

    The widening of 4700 South will take 30 to 40 feet of the Welcker Memorial Park, so funds
     have been obtained to realign and construct that park.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                 Battalion Chief Russell updated the Council on the following:

    Representatives from the UFA have been working hard at the legislature to get H.B. 101
     relating to handheld devices while operating a vehicle and S.B. 56 relating to additional
     Tier 2 retirement benefits passed.

    Hiring process to replace the Assistant Chief for the Kearns area. The new person should
     be in place by the first of June.

    There are 32 candidates participating in the recruitment camp.

    Fire school will be held in May.

    The Emergency Operations Center was activated today due to the threat of the Coronus
     virus. Contingency plans are being made.

    Pancake breakfast will be held on May 9th from 8 to 11 a.m.

Council Business

Music Festival

               Council Member Schaeffer stated the music festival will be held on May 9th after
the pancake breakfast. He is still looking for a place to hold the festival. This noise level will be
minimal since the only thing on microphones will be the vocals.

               Council Member Snow asked what budget this would come out of.

               Council Member Schaeffer stated it would come out of the Council budget.

              Council Member Snow stated she would hate to spend this much money and only
have a handful of people attend. She asked how many bands were coming.

                Council Member Schaeffer stated currently there are six bands scheduled. He
is trying to get eight bands total. The stage is only 20 x 24 with a shade awning. This agenda
item could be tabled for one month in order to get more information from the community council.

                Council Member Peterson, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to table
this item for one month. The motion passed unanimously.
