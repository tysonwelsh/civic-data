                                  Council consideration of Ordinance 23-03, an ordinance revising Title 21 of the Sandy City 
                                  Municipal Code Chapter 26, Section 8, allowing temporary signs on fences on private 
                                  property in residential zones. James Sorenson, Community Development Director 
                                  presented the Ordinance and responded to Council questions.

                                  Public Comment opened:
                                  Ms. Pat Jones spoke in support of the proposed amendments to the temporary sign 
                                  ordinance. She spoke about first amendment and personal property rights which are 
                                  scared rights. She does not believe that beautification justifies limiting the rights of 
                                  residents
                                  Public Comment closed.

                                  A lengthy Council discussion followed. Council received 70-80 emails from residents who 
                                  were not supportive of the proposed amendments. The outreach from the public is clear 
                                  and the message is unmistakable. Residents wanted protection from undue signage. 
                                  Many residents expressed concerns that too many signs make our city look trashy. 
                                  Council and Legal discussed that our current ordinance is in compliance with the law and 
                                  does not violate any residents' first amendment rights. Council members expressed their 
                                  thoughts and concerns with the proposed amendments.

                                  A motion was made by Cyndi Sharkey, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa to not adopt 
                                  Ordinance 23--03. Council discussion on the motion followed.

                                  Discussion included tabling the item. There was a clarification and discussion on the 
                                  motion - if approved, the effect of the current motion, would keep the current city 
                                  ordinance in place with no amendments. Council wanted to give the residents and 
                                  Administration more clarity on the Council's position on the proposed amendments. The 
                                  City and Council Attorneys provided additional insight. Council spoke about the residents' 
                                  feedback on this issue, which was not in favor of the proposed changes. Council Member 
                                  Stroud explained why she was going to Abstain from voting.


                                  A motion was made by Cyndi Sharkey, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa to not adopt 
                                  Ordinance 23-03, an ordinance revising Title 21 of the Sandy City Municipal Code 
                                  Chapter 26, ""Signage and Outdoor Advertising", Section 8, "Temporary Signs" to 
                                  allow temporary signs on fences on private property in residential zones, also 
                                  providing a saving clause and effective date for the ordinance...The motion
