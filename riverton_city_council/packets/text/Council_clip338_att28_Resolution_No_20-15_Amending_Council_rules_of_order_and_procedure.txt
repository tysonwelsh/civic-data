                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-15


  A RESOLUTION AMENDING SECTIONS 2.4 AND 4.1 OF THE RIVERTON CITY
             COUNCIL RULES OF ORDER AND PROCEDURE


       WHEREAS, in the year 2011, the Utah Legislature adopted House Bill 267 which
requires political subdivisions to adopt rules of order and procedure, and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council had adopted the Riverton City Council Rules of
Order and Procedure (the “Rules”) prior to the passage of House Bill 267, but the Rules
nevertheless complied with the standards pronounced by House Bill 267; and

     WHEREAS, House Bill 267, codified under Utah Code § 10-3-606, permits adoption of
amendments to the Rules from time to time; and

       WHEREAS, Section 3.3, Paragraph 1 establishes who may direct that items be placed on
the agenda of a Riverton City Council meeting; and

      WHEREAS, presently an item may be placed on the agenda by the City Manager, the
Mayor, or one City Council Member; and

       WHEREAS, to provide for the more efficient enactment of items placed onto the
agenda, the City Council finds and determines that the number of Council Members who may
place an item onto the Agenda be amended to require that two Council Members must agree to
place an item onto the Council Agenda;

       WHEREAS, in July of 2019, the Riverton City created and activated the Riverton City
Police Department; and

       WHEREAS, Section 4.1 of the Rules sets parameters by which the Riverton City
Council interacts with executive and other City staff to obtain information about municipal
operations; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the sensitive nature of
information collected by the Riverton Police Department during the course of investigations
requires that the Council amend Section 4.1 the Riverton City Council Rules of Order to allow
the Police Department to preserve confidential information during the course investigating
matters brought to the attention to the Riverton Police Department; and



       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1. Amendment. Section 3.3, Paragraph 1. of the Riverton City Council Rules of Order and
   Procedure is hereby amended to read as follows:
    3.3        Council Meeting Agendas and Paperwork

       1. Regular Council Meeting Agendas. Items may be placed on Council agendas by the City
          Manager, the Mayor or two Council Members, who must individually communicate this
          request to the City Recorder as herein provided:

           a. Submittal to Recorder and to Mayor. Requests to be placed on an agenda and supporting
              documents should be delivered to the City Recorder for a regular Tuesday Council
              meeting, no later than 5:00 p.m. on the preceding Tuesday. A courtesy copy of
              information submitted to the Recorder for the agenda should be delivered to the Mayor
              at or before the time such information is submitted to the Recorder.
           b.


2. Amendment. Section 4.1, Paragraph 12. of the Riverton City Council Rules of Order and
   Procedure is hereby amended to read as follows:

   4.1 Interaction with City Staff/Officials:

       12.     Council Members may periodically choose to contact Department heads to discuss
       ideas, express concerns on behalf of constituents, request information, etc. It is acknowledged
       that the Department head is naturally in a somewhat difficult position in communicating with
       Council Members. Department heads formally report to the City Manager, yet are charged with
       managing their departments within resources provided by the Council. There may be
       circumstances where the Department head would like to say yes to the request made by a
       Council Member, but is unable to do so because of differing management directives from the
       City Manager; inadequate budget resources; applicable law; ordinance restrictions; concerns
       about setting a precedence (saying yes to this request would mean that the service must be
       provided Citywide and that is not possible within existing resources); or other reasons. In those
       instances, the Council agrees and understands that the Department head may request that the
       Council Member’s request be processed through the City Manager. The Council recognizes that
       when seeking information related to matters under investigation or management by law
       enforcement, protecting the integrity of case investigations and maintaining safety for all
       involved are of paramount concern. Accordingly, when requesting information related to an
       ongoing law enforcement investigation, emergency response, or other significant matters
       involving law enforcement, Council Members shall refrain from involving themselves in the
       matter by undertaking such actions as (without limitation) entering crime scenes, speaking with
       witnesses, speaking with law enforcement personnel, or seeking time and attention of the
       Riverton Police Chief. Rather, the inquiring Member(s) of Council shall await a response from
       the Mayor or designee of the Mayor to communicate with Council Member(s) at a time deemed
       appropriate by the Mayor about law enforcement-related matters.


3. Legislative Copy. A Legislative Copy which delineates the amendments made in Sections 1
   and 2 of this Resolution is attached as Exhibit A to this Resolution.

4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.
