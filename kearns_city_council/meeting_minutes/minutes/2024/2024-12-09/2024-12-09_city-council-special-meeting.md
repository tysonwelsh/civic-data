3. Citizen Public Input
There was no public comment.
4. Council Business
a. Consider approval of Ordinance 2024-12-01 Adopting an Airport Overlay
Zone — Curtis Woodward, Senior Planner
Curtis Woodward, Senior Planner, reported that collaboration with the Salt Lake City
Airport Planning Office has been ongoing to develop an airport overlay zone specifically
tailored to the needs of a smaller airport, such as the South Valley Airport. He explained
that, although the airport is not located within Kearns, federal and state regulations require
the establishment of an overlay zone due to its proximity.
Mr. Woodward provided an analysis of the Staff Report. Mayor Bush requested that
planning staff remain engaged in the airport's expansion efforts.
