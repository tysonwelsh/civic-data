 FRC Minutes 
W
 March 26, 2026 

 MINUTES 
 Wasatch Front Regional Council 
 March 26, 2026 
 WFRC Council meeting was held at WFRC Offices, 41 N Rio Grande Street, Salt Lake City and via Zoom. 
 1. W
 elcome & Consent Agenda (00:00:03)
 At 1:34pm, Commissioner Bob Stevenson, Vice Chair of WFRC Council, welcomed the group to the meeting.
 Introductions were made around the room and online. (00:05:30) 1a. ACTION: Commissioner Stevenson brought
 attention to the minutes of the Council meeting held on Jan 22, 2026 and the financial statements for December
 2025 and January 2026 and entertained a motion to approve these items. Commissioner Jim Harvey made the
 motion and Mayor Dustin Gettel seconded, and the affirmative vote was unanimous. Commissioner Stevenson
 excused WFRC Chair Dawn Ramsey from today’s meeting.
 2. C
 hair Report (00:06:30)
 Commissioner Stevenson turned the time to Miranda Jones Cox, WFRC Government Affairs Manager, to share
 some information regarding session outcomes. Senator Wayne Harper (00:20:33) , and Representatives Ariel
 Defay (00:24:12) and Cal Roberts (00:25:30) also provided their perspectives on key pieces of transportation,
 housing, and growth related legislation.
 3. P
 ublic Comment (00:38:41)
 Commissioner Stevenson opened the meeting to public comment. There were no comments made.
 4. R
 egional Growth Committee (RGC) (00:39:13)
 Commissioner Stevenson introduced Mayor Tami Tran, who provided an update from the March 19 RGC meeting.
 4a: ACTION: Station Area Plans (SAP) Certification (00:39:51)
 Mayor Tran introduced Byron Head, WFRC Community Planner, who reviewed the Midvale City portion of the SAP.
 (00:46:56) ACTION: Mayor Tran made a motion to certify the Midvale Fashion Place West Station Area Plan. The
 motion was seconded by Mayor Dustin Gettel and the motion passed unanimously.
 4b: Transportation and Land Use Connection Program 2026 Awarded Projects (00:47:22)
 Mayor Tran asked Meg Padjen, WFRC Community and Economic Development Director, to present this item.
 5. T
 ransportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) (00:56:17)
 Commissioner Stevenson then turned the time to Mayor Mark Shepherd for the Trans Com update, who introduced
 Ben Wuthrich, Transportation Improvement Program Coordinator at WFRC.
 5a . Board Modifications to the 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Mr. Wuthrich stated
 that since the last meeting of the Wasatch Front Regional Council (WFRC), requests were received to modify the
 current 2026-2031 TIP. The modification required action from Trans Com (since WFRC was not meeting) and the
 Utah Transportation Commission but did not require a new air quality conformity analysis or a 30-day public
 comment period. The modification is attached to the approved resolution.
 5b. ACTION: Board Modifications to the 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) [00:58:08]
 Mr. Wuthrich continued the report by briefly describing the modification requests to the current 2026-2031
 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). [01:04:40] ACTION: Mayor Shepherd made a motion to approve the
 modifications as discussed. Carlton Christensen, UTA Trustee, seconded the motion and the affirmative vote was
 unanimous.
 5c. Report on obligations of federal transportation funds during FY25 [01:05:55] Mr. Wuthrich then presented
 information on the annual obligations of federal transportation funds during FY25.
 6. W
 FRC Budget Committee (01:13:57)
 Commissioner Stevenson then brought the group’s attention to Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton, WFRC
 Budget Committee Chair, for the WFRC Budget Committee report.
 6a . ACTION: Release the Draft FY27 WFRC Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) for
 public comment
 Councilmember Winder Newton gave a brief introduction and then turned the time over to Andrew Gruber, WFRC
 Executive Director, who provided an overview on the process for the FY27 Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning

 ork Program (UPWP). Mr. Gruber provided a high-level summary of WFRC’s FY27 Goals and the UPWP, and 
W
 then passed the presentation to [01:16:09] Marian Florence, Chief Financial Officer at WFRC, who presented the 
 FY27 Draft WFRC Budget. [01:22:10] ACTION: Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton made a motion to release 
 the draft FY27 WFRC Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) for public comment. 
 Commissioner Jim Harvey seconded the motion. With no further discussion, releasing the three FY27 documents 
 for public comment was approved unanimously. 
 7. Reports [01:22:38] 
 7a. Wasatch Front Economic Development District (WFEDD) update 
 Commissioner Gage Froerer, Chair of WFEDD, provided an update on the meeting held March 12. There was a 
 presentation on addressing brownfield areas (land that was previously developed as industrial or commercial use, 
 that may require more rehabilitation before redevelopment) and potential funding sources for communities. 
 7b. Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) 2026 Awarded Projects [01:26:22] 
 Christy Dahlberg, WFRC Community Planner, shared the projects that will potentially be awarded for the 2026 
 CDBG funding cycle. 
 7c. Executive Director’s Report [01:28:00] 
 Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, recognized two employees for reaching five years of service: 
 Marcia White, Regional Economic Development Planner, and Miranda Jones Cox, Government Affairs Manager. 
 Mr. Gruber then introduced two new staff members: 
 Joel Temple, joining WFRC’s Long Range Planning group, and Paulo Aguilera, joining WFRC’s Community and 
 Economic Development group. 
 Mr. Gruber acknowledged two awards received by Suzie Swim and Bill Hereth, both with WFRC’s Analytics group, 
 and reminded the Council of the upcoming ninth annual Golden Spoke Bike, which will be held on May 16, 2026, 
 and invited them to attend. 
 8. Other Business and Adjournment [01:34:22] 
 Commissioner Stevenson reminded the group that the next Council meeting will be held on Thursday, May 28, 
 2026 and adjourned the meeting at 3:04pm. 

 A recording of this meeting, as well as meeting packet materials, may be found on the WFRC website at www.wfrc.org
