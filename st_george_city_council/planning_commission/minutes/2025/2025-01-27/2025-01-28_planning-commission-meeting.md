PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT/PUBLIC HEARING:
     Consider a request for a Planned Development (PD) amendment to the PD-R
     (Planned Development Residential) zone, specifically to remove the
     previously approved stacked units and convert all units to townhomes,
     resulting in some modifications to the layout. This will also reduce the
     number of units from 206 to 196. The property is generally located on the
     west side of the intersection of Canyon View Drive and Gap Canyon
     Parkway. The applicant is Unified Business Alliance, and the representative
     is Jared Huish. The project will be known as Rillisante. Case No. 2025-PDA-
     006 – PUBLIC HEARING. (Staff – Dan Boles).
