OAM2026-001631 – A proposal to amend the City of Kearns’s general plan to include an official map for
the City as an element of the general plan pursuant to §§ Utah Code 10-20-102(54) and 10-20-408. Planner:
Brian Tucker (Discussion/Recommendation)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Senior Planner Curtis Woodward provided an analysis of
the amendment to the Map for the City of Kearns.

Commissioners, staff, and Councilmember Colby had a brief discussion regarding the color coding,
topography layer, Industrial Park was uncolored because it is unnamed, elevation change, and ADA
Compliance and accessibility. Linear parkway lots.

Commissioner Thomas motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public is present to speak.

Commissioner Thomas motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Commissioners and counsel had a brief discussion regarding the disclaimer on the bottom of the map and
if it should be City of Kearns’ name and logo, and if MSD should be removed.

Motion: To recommend file #OAM2026-001631 A proposal to amend the City of Kearns’s general plan to
include an official map for the City as an element of the general plan pursuant to §§ Utah Code 10-20-
102(54) and 10-20-408 to the Kearns Council for approval with recommendations on topographic layer
removed, ADA compliance, remove MSD name and disclaimer and replace with city of Kearns and city of
Kearns disclaimer.
       Motion by: Commissioner Reynolds
       2nd by: Commissioner Nelson
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor


Commissioner Thomas adjourned.
