PUBLIC HEARINGS
A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2020-2021
     BUDGET
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled April 6, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding re-opening the FY 2020-2021 Budget.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

          State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
          allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The West
          Valley City holds public hearings on budget amendments on a quarterly basis
          each fiscal year.

          A Public Notice was posted March 18, 2021 in (the Salt Lake Tribune and the
          Deseret Morning News) general circulation to the general public. Notice was
          given that a public hearing is to be held April 6, 2021 at 6:30 p.m., West Valley
          City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah.

 Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing.

 Jim Vesock stated that approximately 6 million dollars have been added to the
 budget since the original budget was approved. He indicated that the City needs to
 plan and stick to the original budget.

 Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.

 ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 21-11, AMEND THE BUDGET OF WEST
 VALLEY CITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2020 AND
 ENDING JUNE 30, 2021 TO REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET
 FROM        INCREASED          REVENUES           AND       AUTHORIZE           THE
 DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
 The City Council previously held a public hearing regarding proposed Ordinance
 21-11 that would amend the budget of West Valley City for the Fiscal Year beginning
 July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021 to reflect changes in the budget from increased
 revenues and authorize the disbursement of funds.

 Councilmember Buhler stated that the original budget was approved during a pandemic
 when the City was unsure what money could be spent. Mayor Bigelow stated that the
 City’s process is a good one and indicated that there are rollovers, grants, etc. that
 cannot be anticipated. He indicated that the City changes and evolves throughout the
 year and it’s important to have a moving budget that can continually be evaluated and
 modified.

 Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of
 the City Council, and he called for a motion.

        Councilmember Buhler moved to approve Ordinance 21-11.

        Councilmember Christensen seconded the motion.

ORDINANCE 21-12: AMEND TITLE 22 OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY
MUNICIPAL CODE TO EXCLUDE THE PARKING OF VEHICLES WITHIN
FIVE FEET OF A DRIVEWAY
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Ordinance 21-12 that would amend Title 22 of the West
Valley City Municipal Code to exclude the parking of vehicles within five feet of a driveway.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       If approved, this ordinance will amend Subsection 22-2-117(2)(a) to include a
       prohibition on parking within five feet of a public or private driveway.

       (2) Standing or Parking Prohibited. No vehicle shall be standing or parked,
       whether occupied or not, except to temporarily pick up or discharge a passenger
       or passengers, provided the driver remains in the vehicle and the engine remains
       running:

       a. In front of or within five feet of a public or private driveway

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Lang moved to approve Ordinance 21-12.

       Councilmember Fitisemanu seconded the motion.

RESOLUTION 21-44: AWARD A CONTRACT TO DIG IT EXCAVATION LLC
FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF TECUMSEH STREET STORM DRAIN
PROJECT
     MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING APRIL 6, 2021

Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-44 that would award a Contract to Dig It
Excavation LLC for the construction of Tecumseh Street Storm Drain Project.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Bids for the project were opened on March 9, 2021, a total of six (6) bids were
       received. The lowest responsible bidder was Dig It Excavation LLC.

       This project will install drainage improvements to collect and covey storm water
       from the Westward Terrace subdivision into the existing storm drain system in
       4800 West. Tecumseh Street (4840 West) is located within the Westward Terrace
       subdivision near 3700 South. Several areas within the subdivision drain to
       Tecumseh Street which is currently a dead-end road with no drainage inlets.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Bigelow there were no further questions from members of the City
Council, and he called for a motion.

       Councilmember Christensen moved to approve Resolution 21-44.

       Councilmember Fitisemanu seconded the motion.
