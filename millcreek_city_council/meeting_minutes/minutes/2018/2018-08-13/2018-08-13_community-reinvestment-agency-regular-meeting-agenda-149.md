REGULAR MEETING — 5:00 p.m.

Chair Silvestrini called the meeting to order.

1. Welcome, Introduction and Preliminary Matters
1.1 Public Comment
Chair Silvestrini extended an invitation to the public to address the Board. There was no
public comment.

2. Business Matters
2.1 Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 18-08, An Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement Regarding the Transfer of the West Millcreek Urban Renewal Area
from the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County to the Millcreek Community
Reinvestment Agency
Mayor Silvestrini said the Resolution transfers the responsibility of the RDA from Salt
Lake County to Millcreek. He said the Agency made a recommendation to the County on
the Millcreek Commons project previously. This agreement will transfer the right to
$372,947.30 worth of tax increment, which is what is expected to be generated from this
project. Winder corrected the Chair by saying that $372 thousand is what has already
been generated. He said the money generated in that area must be spent in that area, but
the City can borrow from it for other areas. The County’s RDA will consider this transfer
on August 21.

Board Member Catten moved to approve Resolution 18-08, An Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement Regarding the Transfer of the West Millcreek Urban Renewal Area from the
Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County to the Millcreek Community Reinvestment
Agency. Board Member Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. The motion
passed unanimously.

2.2 Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 18-09, Engaging Lewis Young for
$15,000 for Preparing Plans and Budgets for Millcreek Center CRA, Canyon Rim
Commons CRA, and Olympus Hills CRA

Chair Silvestrini said this study is basically to meet the requirements of state law. The
City put out an RFP and there were two bidders with the other being Zions Public
Finance at $32,000. Winder said Lewis Young had the lower bid at $25,000, which
included $10,000 for the blight study on Millcreek Center. He said at the last meeting the
City contracted Lewis Young for the blight study at $10,000. He said normally it is about
$10,000 per plan and budget but they will do it for $15,000 for the three.

Board Member Jackson moved to adopt Resolution 18-09, Engaging Lewis Young for
$15,000 for Preparing Plans and Budgets for Millcreek Center CRA, Canyon Rim
Commons CRA, and Olympus Hills CRA. Board Member Uipi seconded.

Chair Catten clarified that the $15,000 was in addition to the already approved $10,000. Winder
said yes, so a total of $25,000.

Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. The motion passed unanimously.

2.3 Approval of July 26, 2018 Special Meeting Minutes
Board Member Catten moved to approve the meeting minutes for July 26, 2018 Special

Meeting. Board Member Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. The
motion passed unanimously.

ADJOURNED: Board Member Uipi moved to adjourn the meeting at 5:12 p.m. Board
Member Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. The motion passed

unanimously. .
