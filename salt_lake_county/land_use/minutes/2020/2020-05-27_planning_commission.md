PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Represents Hi-Country Estates Homeowner’s Association 
Name: Andrew Blonquist 
Address: 5292 South College Drive, Murray 
Comments: Mr. Blonquist said will hook up to Herriman City secondary water and has an agreement with 
Herriman City to provide utilities and not have to use culinary. No open space in the subdivision and need 
to use a portion of one of the lots. Next to the subdivision of Herriman City and the County, the location 
and facilities make sense and use water most efficiently. HOA and Herriman City are obligated to maintain 
this. No reason for entry or site falling into disrepair with the two entities. 
 
Commissioner Cohen  asked if the secondary water is in the subdivision now. Mr. Blonquist  said only 
singular culinary water source. Herriman City can provide further details. Commissioner Cohen asked if 
the pump house will be connected to Herriman City water and will it be connected to a secondary water 
source. Mr. Blonquist said not sure if they must install additional secondary water source. Hi-Country goes 
up hill and there is not enough pressure. Hi-Country already has their own wells and water source, and 
this is an add on to secondary water source.  
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Donald Calvert 
Address: 13431 South 7530 West 
Comments: Mr. Calvert asked if a new well would be drilled. 
 
Mr. Blonquist said no new well. Mr. Calvert asked just a transfer station. Mr. Blonquist said the pumps will 
be electric and will not be any noise or concern above a normal home or regular levels and will not be an 
issue.  
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Barbushev seconded that motion. 
 
Speaker # 3: Citizen 
Name: Sheldon Hamilton 
Address: 1536 West Green Apple Street  
Comments: Mr. Hamilton said he owns 10 acres and spent thousands of dollars for water rights and to drill. 
Are they going to be able to water all the five-acre pasture with this secondary water and will they be 
impacted. If it doesn’t impact him, he spent thousands of dollars and already had to drill down more. 
 
Speaker # 4: Citizen 
Name: Donald Calvert 
Address: 13431 South 7530 West 
Comments: Mr. Calvert asked if they drill another well will it suck the existing wells, but if just a transfer 
pump station, has no problem. 
 
Speaker # 5: Represents Hi-Country Estates Homeowner’s Association 
Name: Andrew Blonquist 
Address: 5292 South College Drive, Murray

Comments: Mr. Blonquist said this is for conditional use and not to decide the allocation of water rights or 
the water source. Does not know where Herriman City gets their source. Just allocation and water rights are 
beyond the scope of conditional use. Each landowner will have separate rights to use water and how much. 
  
Commissioner Cohen said the plans do not show any wells being drilled, just the connection. Mr. Blonquist 
confirmed no new wells. 
 
Commissioner Elieson motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Cohen seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion r egarding recommendation number two, review process 
and mitigation, objective conditions. County noise ordinance complied with and technical review and how 
the county noise ordinance is complied with, no more than 60 decibels and an objective standard.  Mr. 
Cook confirmed there haven’t been any comments from the neighboring property within 100 feet from the 
pump house.  
 
Motion: To approve application #31092 with staff recommendations and amend condition two, adding: In 
accordance with county noise ordinance. 
Motion by: Commissioner Cohen 
2nd by: Commissioner Elieson 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
