PRESENTER:             Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director


ITEM:


Ordinance No. 26-09 - “Saxey Rezone” An application to rezone approximately 2.82- acres located at
12317, 12325 and 12341 South Redwood Road, currently zoned R-4 and RM-6-SD to RM-14-D, and to
amend the General Plan from Medium-High Density Residential to High-Density Residential.


On April 23, 2026, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this item. Minutes from that
meeting are included below. The Planning Commission voted to recommend approval, but with the
recommended zone at R-6 rather than RM-14-D as requested. The Commission recommended the following
motion:
“I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance 26-09, rezoning 2.82-acres located at 12317, 12325 and 12341
South Redwood Road from R-4 and RM-6-SD to RM-6, as shown in Exhibit “A”.”


SUMMARY:


This item is a public hearing for a Rezone of three adjoining parcels located on the east side of Redwood
Road at 12317, 12325 and 12341 South. The parcels are narrow and deep with dimensions of about 80-ft
wide and 480-ft deep. Each parcel has a small house located near the front of the lot.
Current Zoning
The Current Zoning for the two (2) northern parcels is RM-6-SD, which was established by City Ordinance
22-04 in January 2022. The RM- 6 Zone only allows single family detached housing on lots of at least
5,000-sf and density not to exceed 6 units per acre. The “SD” added requirements:
1- No more than 4 lots adjacent to the north boundary, and lots must align as closely as possible to the
adjacent existing lots to the north. And
2- A masonry fence will be installed along the south property line prior to issuance of any building permits
on the property. (See attachments for Ordinance 22-04)
The third parcel in this rezone request is currently Zoned RR-22 which is a single-family residential zone
with ½-acre lots and animal rights. The other parcels similarly situated on the east side of redwood road
are also zoned RR-22.
Zoning in the area is a mix of residential zones with some Commercial Downtown zoning nearby. To the
east is R-3, to the north is R-4, across Redwood to the west is also R-4.
General Plan
The General Plan for properties on the east side of Redwood Road from about 12310 South to about 12530
South (north of the church) designates all these properties as Medium-High Density Residential (5-8
Dwelling units per acre) and includes a Historic Preservation designation. The Medium-High Density
designation suggests the properties could be zoned to RM-6 or RM-8 zones. Both zones allow single family
detached housing on small lots, the RM-8 Zone also allows attached housing such as townhomes or
condominiums. The General Plan designation for Historic Preservation was put in place in 2020 with an
idea to consider the existing small homes facing Redwood, but without specific requirements.
Requested General Plan and Zoning
The applicant is requesting the three subject parcels be rezoned to RM-14-D, which requires the General
Plan be amended to High Density Residential. The RM-14-D zone is a multifamily zone that allows a
variety of housing types such as small lot detached, townhomes, and condos with a maximum density of 14-
units per acre.
