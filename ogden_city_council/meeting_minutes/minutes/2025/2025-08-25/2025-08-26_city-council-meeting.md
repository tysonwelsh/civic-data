Proposed Ordinance 2025-27 revising provisions to the Ogden City Motor
Vehicle Statutes
          A memo from the Police Department regarding a proposed ordinance allowing for enforcement of motor vehicle statues
on Ogden School District (OSD) property came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated Section 53G-4-401 of the
Utah State Code identifies local school boards as “bodies corporate” and “legal subdivision[s] of the state, vested with the powers
and duties of a government entity.” Section 53G-4-407 specifies that school board property is “exempt from general and special
taxation and from local assessments.” School district property is under the jurisdiction of the school board and not subject to rules
promulgated by the local government entity (with the exception of certain land use provisions). Utah Code § 53G-8-604, which
relates to enforcement of city criminal and traffic ordinances on school property, authorizes municipalities to enact an ordinance
authorizing local law enforcement to enforce traffic laws on properties controlled by the state board of education, the local
education board, a vocational center, or the Utah Schools for the Deaf and the Blind. However, adoption of the ordinance must be
“at the request of the responsible board of education or institutional council.”
          Police Chief Sube summarized the memo and stated the proposed amendments to Ogden Municipal Code Title 10,
Section 1 makes the following changes:
               • Clarifies that any penalties imposed for violations of the Motor Vehicle Act (Title 41, Section 1), Uniform
                   Driver License Act (Title 52), the traffic code (Title 41, Chapter 6a), the Financial Responsibility of Motor
                   Vehicle Owners and Operators Act (Title 41, Section 12a), and the Off Highway Vehicles Act (Title 41,
                   Chapter 22) may not exceed those imposed under city ordinance. (State penalties may be higher than those set
                   by city code);
               • Authorizes enforcement of City vehicular traffic and the City’s other traffic-related ordinances on school
                   district property;
               • Removes language relating to motor vehicle insurance;
               • Indicates that school district property shall be treated as a public street, road, or highway for purposes of
                   enforcement of City traffic rules, regulations, and penalties; and
               • Makes technical changes to revise the index and align the ordinance language with the Legal Department’s
                   style guide.
          Police Chief Sube briefly touched on the violations for which Ogden City Police Department will not have enforcement
authority on OSD property.
         Chair White introduced in writing proposed Ordinance 2025-27, entitled:
                “An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah, amending the Ogden Municipal Code Chapter 1 of Title 10 to revise
                provisions to the Ogden City motor vehicle statutes; to make technical changes; to appropriately revise the
                index and by providing that this ordinance shall become effective immediately upon posting after final
                passage.”
         ON A MOTION BY COUNCIL MEMBER MYERS AND SECONDED BY VICE CHAIR HYER, ORDINANCE
2025-27 WAS ADOPTED UPON THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE – COUNCIL MEMBERS

Public Comments
          Laura Lewis referenced the discussion from tonight’s work session regarding a rezone at 853 7th Street; there are so many
problems associated with the project and by the time those problems are resolved, it could easily have morphed into something
else. However, once the property is rezoned, someone else can purchase it in the future and abide by the same zoning. She added
she is concerned about Fire Department access to the property due to the steepness of the hill; there will also be traffic problems in
the area due to the steep grade of the road. She urged the Council to consider something like a nature preserve in the area; this
would eliminate the safety concerns and provide something beautiful for the public to enjoy. She then reminded everyone that
they can return their election ballots on election day at a polling location rather than returning by mail.
          Heath Satow also addressed the rezone application for the property on 7 th Street; he generally supports zone changes and
subsequent development projects that provide for an increase in the affordable single family home inventory in the community. He
agreed that some of the issues raised by Ms. Lewis should be addressed before the project is allowed to proceed. He then
addressed the discussion during the work session regarding the 24 th Street interchange on Interstate-15 and noted that he hopes the
City will work with the Arts community to come up with an attractive ‘Welcome to Ogden’ sign.

Mayor Comments
         Mayor Nadolski thanked Ms. Lewis and Mr. Satow for their comments about the 7 th Street rezone, noting he is not very
familiar with the project at this stage, but will look into it further and become better informed.

Council member Comments
         Vice Chair Hyer stated he is fairly familiar with the property on 7 th Street, and he is aware of some of the hurdles that
need to be overcome in order to develop it. He is willing to give the developer time to address those hurdles and work to put the
property to good use. Council member Choberka agreed and added that the zone change, and possible conditional overlay zoning
would only apply to the specific application and not future land use applications.
         Council member Myers highlighted recent positive media attention on Mayor Nadolski and the Ogden Airport.


       There being no further business to come before the Council, VICE CHAIR HYER MOVED THE MEETING
