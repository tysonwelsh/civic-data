Alta Town Council

Staff Report:
Business License Study

To: Town Council
From: Jen Clancy, Deputy Town Clerk
Date Written: September 2, 2021 (updated September 21, 2021)



Zions Public Finance conducted a business license study for the Town of Alta. The draft
study was presented to the Town Council at the July 2021 meeting and discussed again
at the August and September meetings and work sessions. The draft report has been
circulated to the Council for review and an updated version is attached as an exhibit to
the draft ordinance. If the Town chooses to adopt the study and set a new fee schedule
where disproportionate fees are charged as part of the fee then an ordinance and a
resolution need to be adopted.

Here is some additional information to consider:
   •   The draft Business License Study was updated after the September 8, 2021 Town
       Council meeting to address comments made by Councilmember Curry regarding
       the omission of indirect fees calculated from the Administration department.
       The revised draft has a September 2021 date on it.
           o This update resulted in ~ $3 being added to the base administration fee
              that is carried through the report. Appendix C was also added to the
              report and documents the update.
           o The only other changes to the report were minor text edits to help
              explain Zion’s process in preparing the report.

•   Option A in Appendix A: Mayor and staff are proposing this option for the fee
    schedule. The option is being presented in an effort to raise the $18,000
    revenue in the approved FY22 budget.
        o In-kind services from businesses in Categories 1 and 8 that are provided
            to our community (such as snow removal and search and rescue
            operations) were considered. These two business categories also provide
            sales tax revenue to Town. We note that the sales tax revenue budgeted
            in the 2022 budget is $1.251 million. If you take the average revenue
            generated by the $16,000 difference between our proposed business
            license fees and the maximum fees we could charge, and divide by the
            sales tax rate of 8.75% you get $183,000. Any sales tax generated above
            that figure — expected to be about $1.06 million — represents that the
            Town really is not subsidizing the businesses (rather, the customers of
            the business are paying for the Town’s operations).
        o The temporary license fee recommendation is $50. We receive roughly
            8-15 temporary applications each year. These are mainly from vendors
            selling stuff at on-snow events or summer running races.
        o The non-profit fee exemption was carried forward in this proposal. There
            is a revenue estimate provided should the council decide not to extend
            this exemption.
•   Options B and C in Appendix A are being presented to offer additional
    alternatives that broaden the range of fee options for the council if needed for
    discussion and consideration.
•   The purpose of this study is to inform the Town about the costs to the Town for
    administering business licenses as well as other financial impacts to the Town’s
    budget such as enhanced and disproportionate fees. The study identifies the
    maximum fees that can be charged to each respective category. The Town may
    choose to extend a discount to a category of businesses for in-kind services.
•   This study did not analyze or consider liquor license applications and fees.
•   In-kind contributions are not included in business license study reports. The
    report’s purpose is to establish the actual cost to the Town so a benefit received
    from in-kind contributions would be outside of the scope of such a report.
•   Additional information on comparable fees that was requested by
    Councilmember Davis was added to the draft report.
•   Home occupation businesses: The State regulates these businesses and says we
    can’t charge a business license fee. If however, a home occupation business
    requests a license we are entitled to charge the base admin fee of $65.31.
