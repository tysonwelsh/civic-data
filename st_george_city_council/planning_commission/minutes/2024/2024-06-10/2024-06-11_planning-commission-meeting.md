REQUEST FOR ZONE CHANGE:
     Consider approval of a request to amend the City Zoning Map by amending
     the zone from RE-37.5 (Residential Estates 37,500 square feet minimum lot
     size) to PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) on approximately 1.2
     acres located on the northwest corner of Sugar Leo Road and Pioneer Road
     to establish a use list and approve a site plan for construction of a parking
     lot and placement of a future office building. The project will be known as
     Dixie Power Parcel SG-BR-1-58 – Applicant – Dixie Power, representative,
     Craig Coats – Case No. 2024-ZC-003 - PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: This is a request for a zone change in
      anticipation of a future office building. Dixie Power is requesting to change the zone
      from RE 37.5 (Residential Estates) to PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) to
      accommodate a new office building and parking lot. In this application they are only
      requesting the approval of the use list and the layout of the parking lot. The project is
      divided into two phases. Phase one will provide 18 overflow parking spaces for Dixie
      Power’s current office building. Phase two will include a new office building, additional
      parking, and a small garage area that will accommodate the expansion of their
      facilities. Staff recommends approval of the zone change from RE-37.5 (Residential
      Estates) to PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) with the condition that before a
      building is constructed, they come back with a Planned Development Amendment.
      Case No. 2024-ZC-003 (Staff – Brenda Hatch).

REQUEST FOR HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a request for a Hillside Development Permit to adjust
     the location of the ridgeline and ridgeline setback on lot 33 of the South Rim
     at Foremaster Ridge Phase 2 subdivision on an existing residential home.
     Applicant – Kevan Webb - Case No. 2024-HS-008.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: This is a request to obtain a hillside
      development permit for the property located at 1618 E. Howard Lane. There is a
      retaining wall that has been built in the ridgeline setback. The rear of the property
      abuts the ridgeline of Foremaster Ridge. The applicant is requesting to review the
      location of the wall to see what needs to be done to address the wall which sits in the
      ridgeline setback and was installed without approval by the city. The applicant is
      looking for a change to the ridgeline and/or possible solutions to address the wall’s
      current location. The Hillside Committee met on May 22nd to review this item. There
      was discussion on when the wall was built and why there was no permit obtained to
      build the retaining wall. The wall was built in the changing of hands of ownership, the
      current individual purchasing the home did not have anything to do with the wall. The
      Hillside Committee recommended approval of the permit with a vote of 3-0 and the
      following conditions: “we recommend acceptance of the non-conforming retaining wall,
      the face of the existing non-conforming retaining wall needs to be painted, and smooth
      out the transition in the southeast corner of the property where there is a near vertical
      transition between the non-conforming retaining wall and the adjacent property, and
      either do it with a slope or radius the retaining wall back about 10 feet from the
      southeast property corner (Staff – Mike Hadley).” Case No. 2024-HS-008.
