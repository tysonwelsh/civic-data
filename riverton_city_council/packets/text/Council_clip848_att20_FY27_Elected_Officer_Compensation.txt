                                                                Ordinance No. 26-14
                                                          Adopting Compensation for
                                                       Elected and Statutory Officers for
                                                                   FY 26-27


Under State Code 10-3-818, before a governing body may adopt a final budget or a final amended
budget that includes a compensation increase for elected and statutory officers and other municipal
officers the body must hold a public hearing. As a fulfillment of the requirements of this code,
Riverton City will be giving all statutory officers and other municipal officers the same percentage
increase as all other civilian employees, namely a 3% cost-of-living increase (COLA). The Mayor and
City Council have chosen not to receive any compensation increase for fiscal year 2026-2027.
