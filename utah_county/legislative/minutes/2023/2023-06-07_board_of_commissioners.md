COMMISSIONER GORDON– ANNOUNCED THE UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEE OF THE
MONTH FOR JUNE 2023, GREG TUTTLE (read nomination)
I would like to nominate Greg Tuttle for employee of the month. Greg is currently
assigned as a bailiff in a 4th district court. Greg consistently provides coverage for his
assigned commissioner and actively investigates potential threats within the courtroom.
Greg's current assignment has him dealing with citizens at the final stage of their
divorces/child custody cases and emotions often run high. Greg does a great job
managing these situations. When not in court Greg goes out of his way to assist fellow
Deputies with breaks both in the courtroom and at the front desk.

Recently Greg's commissioner lost her husband to health issues. Greg stayed in contact
with her throughout the process and assisted in any way possible to help her through
the process. Greg was able to set up extra patrols of her residence while she was out of
town to relieve some worry from her. In Speaking with the commissioner afterward she
stated her appreciation for Greg and all he had done to help her over the last few weeks.

Nomination by Lt Eldon Packer

1. DISCUSS AND REVIEW PROPOSED AMENDMENTS TO UTAH COUNTY CODE 7.03
LARGE PUBLIC ASSEMBLIES
       -Ben VanNoy, Sheriff

(Timestamp: 5:35)

BEN VANNOY, UTAH COUNTY ATTORNEY – UTAH COUNTY ATTORNEY INTRODUCED
THE PRESENTATION – STATING THAT THE CURRENT ORDINANCE REQUIRES A
PERMIT IF A GATHERING IS OVER 12 HOURS LONG, WHICH ACCORDING TO PUBLIC
WORKS, ONE HAS NOT BEEN ISSUED IN RECENT MEMORY. THESE LARGE
GATHERINGS CAUSE SIGNIFICANT HEALTH AND SAFETY CONCERNS.

MIKE SMITH - STATED THAT IT IS NOT THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE PURPOSE TO
DISSUADE PEOPLE FROM HOLDING THESE LARGE EVENTS, BUT TO ENCOURAGE
PEOPLE TO DO THESE EVENTS IN A SAFE AND PROPER WAY.

BEN VANNOY, UTAH COUNTY ATTORNEY – (Gave Presentation)

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER STATED HER CONCERN ABOUT THE
POSSIBILITY OF UNDULY PENALIZING AN EVENT COORDINATOR IF THE EVENT
GREW BIGGER THAN ANTICIPATED.
