Action / Discussion Items

2024 Magna Metro Township Budget

               David Brickey, Administrator, stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (MSD) put together a comparison sheet together, comparing the 2024 requested budget
and the 2023 actual budget. A couple of Trustees have differing opinions on Magna’s proposed
budget. Stewart Okobia, Finance Director, MSD, is requesting that the Council review the
proposed budget and verify the amounts. Mr. Brickey reviewed the following line items:

     Training and Seminars

        The Council could cut $10,000 and still have sufficient funding for training. Mr. Brickey will
report $15,000 as the proposed budgeted amount.

     Travel/Mileage

       Mr. Brickey will report $6,500 as the budgeted amount.

     Contributions/Special Events

                Council Member Hull stated the UPD Court is not a program; however,
Magna/Kearns Youth Court is, and it is funded through the Community That Cares Coalition. The
UPD Youth Academy is not funded through the coalition but by the city. The cost is $5,000 and is
split with Kearns. The Council was also talking about holding additional community events.

               The Council agreed upon $172,000 for the proposed budget amount.

     Professional and Technical

               Paul Ashton stated Yuzawa is part of the budget this year.

              Council Member Hull stated the Council was considering hiring a coordinator for
Main Street USA. She recommended $128,000.

               Mr. Brickey stated he will report $128,000 for the proposed budget amount.

7200 West Snow Removal & Repairs

                 Council Member Hull stated that she attended the Utah Transportation Authority
(UTA) long-range planning event. A constituent at the meeting addressed problems with snow
removal on 7200 West from 2100 South to 3500 South. She confirmed that snow removal has
not been done well. Magna and West Valley City own portions of the road, whose responsibility
is it to provide snow removal on that road.

               Council Member Pierce stated the issue may not be the quality of the road that
causes it to be plowed at different times, but road priority.

                 Ms. Hull proposed considering having the driver plow the entire road instead of
dividing it into sections.

Condition of the Magna Park

               Council Member Hull expressed concerns about the condition of Magna Copper
Park, specifically noting that half of the lawn is overgrown with weeds.

            Council Member Barney clarified that Magna Copper Park is the responsibility of
Magna Metro Township, with maintenance contracted to the Municipal Services District (MSD).
He recommended reevaluating the contract due to the park’s current condition and service level.

              Council Member Prokopis stated there are a lot of improvements that need to be
made at the park. The Council needs to make the park more of a priority.

                Mayor Peay suggested putting the maintenance contract out to bid to consider
alternative options.

              Council Member Barney stated Salt Lake County Park & Recreation should be
held accountable for the maintenance of Magna Copper Park.

               David Brickey, Administrator, stated that he will follow up with necessary parties
to discuss the condition of the park.

Utah Transit Authority (UTA) Work Session Request

              Council Member Hull stated she attended the Utah Transit Authority (UTA)
Academy and a meeting where it talked about its long-range plans. She would like UTA to come
to a Council Workshop Meeting to discuss Magna’s needs. Magna is far out from getting new bus
lanes and new bus routes. Currently, the Magna Metro Township has one bus route. With the
new high school and development, more bus routes are needed.

            Council Member Prokopis stated UTA wants to serve the most people possible
and may consider more routes in Magna Metro Township once it has grown to meet UTA’s
threshold.

              Council Member Hull stated she thought the Council could justify the need for
more bus routes.

Storage Unit for Mountain Biking Club

               Council Member Hull stated there is a group of approximately 15 kids at the high
school and elementary that would like to start a mountain biking club in Magna. It will be a long
process to get the club up and running. Mountain bike clubs and mountain bikes are expensive.
Meetings have been held with a national mountain biking organization. There are schools that will
donate their used bikes when they get new ones. Once the club starts acquiring equipment, it will
need a place to store it. The high school will not store the equipment on its property. She
suggested getting a storage container and storing it on the Webster Center’s property.

               Council Member Barney stated the Council does not have jurisdiction to approve
that because it simply leases space at the Webster Center. The club would need to have that
conversation with the Webster Foundation.

              Council Member Pierce asked how the equipment would be transported to and
from the necessary locations.

               Council Member Prokopis asked why the club was not making that pitch to the
school.

             Council Member Hull stated that it is not a Utah High School Activities Association
(UHSAA) sport.

               Council Member Prokopis asked how other schools had solved this problem.

                Council Member Hull stated kids in other communities are wealthy and own their
own bikes, so they do not have this need. The Magna Metro Township community could not
afford to buy their own mountain bikes, so the coalition is trying to help with that piece to ensure
all kids who wanted to participate could do so. The coalition sees this club as an activity for kids
to get involved with, keeping them off the streets and away from criminal behavior.

                Council Member Barney stated this was the responsibility of the mountain biking
club, and it should solve these problems.

Manager Updates

                  David Brickey stated Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling has put out its notice
of its landfill vouchers, which will be mailed this year. The voucher is for a 4-foot by 8-foot load.
If anyone does not get a voucher in the mail, they can call his cell phone and he will help them try
to get that voucher.

                Council Member Hull asked if Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling was going to
provide the libraries with vouchers.

               Council Member Barney stated Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling is no longer
going to do that. The paper voucher is going away and will be replaced by a digital voucher. The
reason for that change is to track which areas were using it. The Wasatch Front Waste and
Recycling Board is also looking into whether the Seasonal Container Reservation Program is
effective.

               Mr. Brickey also made the following additional announcements:

 Residents in the Magna Metro Township are getting really frustrated with the construction going
on. There are a lot of road closures, but not much notice was provided. Today, he reached out
to Scott Baird, Director, Salt Lake County Public Works about the complaints, and Mr. Baird was
willing to have two of his staff meet tomorrow at 10:00 AM, and inspect the roads and road
closures, and see what notices were provided.

 The Magna Metro Township will be changing to Zoom Webinar for its meetings to prevent
hackers, while still being convenient for people to use.

 He is in the process of getting bids back from CivicPlus for the plus option for the Magna Metro
Township Council’s software or presence on the web. CivicPlus provides an enhanced service,
which makes buttons and drop down menus larger and easier to see, and allows the ability to
