                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-10

         A RESOLUTION APPOINTING VOLUNTEER MEMBERS TO THE
         RIVERTON CITY EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS COMMITTEE

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance, the Mayor shall appoint with
advice and consent of the City Council, residents of Riverton City to serve on the Emergency
Preparedness Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nominations for the following volunteer
appointments; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the appointees are
residents of Riverton City and are willing and capable of serving on the Emergency Preparedness
Committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointments of the Mayor's
nominations to the Emergency Preparedness Committee.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointment of Dennis
          Walker and Cameron Hess to the Riverton City Emergency Preparedness
          Committee, which terms shall end December 31, 2021.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.


       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 21st day of
