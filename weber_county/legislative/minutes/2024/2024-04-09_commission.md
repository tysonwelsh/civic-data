G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVE CREATION OF A BANK ACCOUNT AT RAYMOND JAMES & ASSOCIATES, INC.
      PER ‘WEBER COUNTY POLICY 2-900 BANK ACCOUNTS’ FOR THE EXPRESS PURPOSE OF
      ACCEPTING DONATIONS OF STOCK FOR THE WEBER COMMUNITY FOUNDATION.
            John Bond (County Treasurer): About five years ago we had a large donation that came in to the Egyptian
            Theater for over a million dollars in stock. We opened up a brokerage account in Chicago through a firm
            from there and was able to facilitate selling that stock and getting cash then giving it to the Egyptian Theater
            their improvements. We've actually got another donation that's come in for $12,500 this year and $12,500
            next year; but the brokerage account has since gone dormant. I've gone through the preliminary work to get
            another brokerage account set up with Raymond James in Salt Lake. The bank account policy approved a
         couple years ago, 2- 900, requires any request for a bank account be approved by the Clerk/Auditor and the
         Treasurer, which approvals have been received. This broker's account is considered equivalent to a bank
         account because it will be a transaction from converting stock to cash; then that cash will be delivered to the
         County. Now we come to the County Commissioners to determine if the account should be opened. Today
         I'm asking for you to consider allowing me to move forward with opening that account. After your approval,
         the Commission would then ask the county attorney's office to create a resolution defining who signs for that
         account and that type of thing. You would then approve that resolution at a later date.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve creation of a bank account at Raymond James & Associates, Inc. and
         accompanying Resolution per ‘Weber County Policy 2-900 Bank Accounts’ for the express purpose of
         accepting donations of stock for the Weber Community Foundation; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye
    2. APPROVE RESOLUTION 24-2024 APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN VALLEY
       RECREATION/TRANSMISSION SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT.
         Stacy Skeen (Commission Office): One application was received for two vacancies. This District struggles
         to get board members to serve. This resolution also proposes a reduction from seven board members to four
         that includes three plus one Huntsville member.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 24-2024 appointing Brent G. Ahlstrom to the Ogden
         Valley Recreation/Transmission Special Service District and reducing Board membership from seven down to
         four members; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye

    3. APPROVE RESOLUTION 25-2024 FOR AN INTER-LOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
       WITH HUNTSVILLE TOWN RELATING TO THE DELIVERY OF MUNICIPAL SOLID WASTE
       TO THE WEBER COUNTY TRANSFER STATION.
         Sean Wilkinson (Director, Community Development): This is the last of the solid waste agreements with
         cities in the County. It will be approved by Resolution.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 25-2024 for an Inter-local Cooperation Agreement with
         Huntsville Town relating to the delivery of municipal solid waste to the Weber County Transfer Station;
         Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye
    4. APPROVE PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY
       AND INNOVATIVE EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT, INC. FOR THE WEBER COUNTY
       MITIGATION PLAN.
         Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): This is letter, which is more like a participation
         agreement, is for an economic impact study funded by a transportation grant through the WFRC (Wasatch
         Front Regional Council). Weber County has offered to match the funding.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a Letter of Concurrence and Match Agreement with Wasatch Front
