1.    Welcome to the Planning Commission Meeting
      a.   Prayer: offered by Alan Hancock

           of the Commission. She also welcomed John Ford as the new alternate member

2.    Taco Bell- Site Plan
      a.     Staff Presentation
      Shauna introduced Miguel Medina with Ensign Engineering via video chat. She
      explained that the site for Taco Bell is north of Maverick and is .7 acres. They have been
      working with our gas and electrical departments in order to follow code. They are
      required to have 24 parking stalls, but they actually have 28 stalls. They have the
      required dumpster, and standard plantings. Shauna asked if any of the Commissioners
      had any questions after reviewing the DRC notes. Skip asked about access through the
      parking lots between businesses. Shauna and Seth explained the access points and
      also mentioned the retaining wall. Karl asked about where the 4” drain pipe located
      behind the retaining wall exits, with a property next to them. Shauna directed the
      question to Miguel, to which he replied it is a percolated pipe and will dissipate into the
      ground below. Skip and Alan don't think that a pipe like that is up to code. Karl said it’s
      dependent upon the amount of soil behind the retaining wall. Shauna said that any
      concerns will be discussed and resolved with Kyle Marchant and Ensign Engineering.
      She told Miguel that she would have Kyle get a hold of him to discuss the matter. Karl
      mentioned to Miguel that the storm box system comes from Australia and wondered why
      they chose that particular system. Miguel replied that they are simply familiar with the
      company and one of the advantages of that particular system is its large capacity. He
      also mentioned that it’s a retention system, not a detention system.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Ann asked if the Commissioners had any questions. Shauna that Ensign has met all the
     qualifications for a site plan. Fran asked if they were hoping to get the site plan approved
     today, to which Shauna confirmed. Shauna said the next step is to apply for a building
     permit.

     c.      Action Item- Motion
     Karl made the motion to accept the site plan for Taco Bell along with the DRC notes.
     Fran asked if we needed a contingency for the drainage system, to which Skip
     confirmed. Ann asked for a motion to accept the site plan for Taco Bell in conjunction
     with the DRC notes, and the addition of the information tonight about the retention
     system and drainage that will be discussed with Kyle.

     Made by: Karl Brough Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Passed Unanimously

     Shauna thanked Miguel and excused him from the meeting.

4.   General Plan Update
     a.    Open the Public Hearing
     Ann opened the public hearing at 7:22pm. She stated that this is a continuance of the

public hearing for the general plan update from last month.

b.       Staff Presentation
Seth discussed the importance of the general plan. He mentioned that even though it’s a
lengthy document, he appreciated the Commission taking the time to review it. He also
stressed the importance of the executive summary at the beginning of the document. He
stated that it contains implementation strategies to accomplish their goals. Blaine
Malquist asked where he could find this document, if he wanted to read it. Seth told him
the link is contained within the Commission agenda, and it will ultimately be posted
online. Ann stated that it really bothers her that the most recent data reflected is from
2019. She is hoping that there will be an addendum or a checklist that includes more
recent data. Seth recognizes the challenge of keeping things up to date. Skip reminded
her that they are hoping to revisit and update the plan sooner than later. Seth is hoping
for a 5 year update. Ann did mention that she likes the format of the document. Shauna
pointed out that the history and staff needs to be updated. Ann has agreed to work on
updating the history. Cory pointed out that JD Parady is missing from the City Council.
He also mentioned that “transportation” should include identifying problem areas that we
have, as well as goals for safety reasons. Seth said that the City Council budget would
be good to include in the general plan. Cory had a question about the percentages
reflected in Table #5 on page 49. Ann also pointed out that the table title should be
above the table. Cory pointed out that the park in Nebo Heights was not included and
that the future park at Red Cliffs shows as existing, when it hasn’t even been built yet.
Discussion was had concerning formatting and accuracy issues with various tables and
figures throughout the document. Karl said he was expecting a little more accuracy, but
likes the overall format/presentation of the document. Blaine Malquist wondered, as a
resident, if 76 pages is too complex? Ann pointed out that everything included are
expectations of the state. Cory also reminded him of the executive summary at the
beginning, that helps navigate the document. Fran brought up the fact that these are
Seth’s marching orders, and that the fact that some numbers are not completely up to
date doesn’t change those marching orders. She feels that there is a hole in our zoning
that would really change those marching orders and could really affect the direction that
we go and our ability to take control of the direction that we go. She wonders if we need
a multi-density zone or a ½ acre lot zone. Do we need an agricultural zone? Seth
pointed out the housing implementation opportunities listed on page 10 and told her that
it’s on the list of things to work on. Shauna agreed with Fran’s comments and much
discussion was had on this topic. (Recording and transcript are available for full
conversation). Shauna pointed out that zoning is one of the tools that cities have to set
regulations. She also said that there has been a swing in the legislature. She said it’s
important to set aside pieces of property and types of living situations for all stages of
life. Fran thinks the Commission’s purpose is to be purposeful and thoughtful about
decisions, and that it’s not just about making money as a city. Seth agreed, and said that
we also need to be strategic with planning and zoning.

c.     Close (or continue) the Public Hearing

     Ann asked for a motion to close or continue the public hearing. After some discussion,

     Seth spoke to the fact that since a portion of the annexation for the 138 kV project is

     b.       Commission Discussion
     Discussion ensued and Alan asked if we can just leave it as R15, to which Skip
     confirmed. Seth said they can leave it alone, but he just wanted the Commission to be
     prepared for such requests. Fran stated that it is the gateway to our city, and it’s one of
     the prettiest spots in town. She also pointed out that we have opportunities for diversity
     across the street. Fran feels that the Commission should stick to their guns, and Skip

6.   Title 10 Updates
     a.      Open the Public Hearing

       Continuation of Public Hearing opened at 8:24pm.

b.       Staff Presentation
Seth said there has been much discussion about the RV parks in town. There are
varying types of RV parks and the discussions have revolved around the 30 day stay
limit. The struggle has been the different purposes of people wanting to stay longer than
30 days. Seth introduced the new code that includes a minimum of 5 acres, hard
surface access roads, common areas, can’t exceed density, spacing requirements,
registration, dump station, laundry facilities, and no permanent structures. He also stated
that there would be a fee for violations which would, in turn, be used to pay for regular
inspections.

c.      Receive Public Comment
Blaine Malquist stated that he noticed all of the handicapped people at the public hearing
regarding this topic. He said it was heartbreaking to note all the people who don’t have
the ability to constantly move every 30 days, and can’t afford anything else.

d.     Close (or continue) the Public Hearing
Ann closed this portion of the public hearing at 8:47pm. She also asked for a motion to
continue the Title 10 Public Hearing on the remaining issues that need to be discussed.

Motion: Fran Peterson Second: Karl Brough Outcome: Passed Unanimously

e.       Commission Discussion
Karl asked where the 30 day requirement came from. Shauna stated that it’s always
been the requirement and isn’t sure when it originated. Karl wondered why the tenant
can’t just sign a 90 day contract. Seth explained that a contract like that limits the
landlords’ rights. He also explained that a 30 day rolling contract protects the tenants
and the landlords. Cory mentioned that he knows of several people who prefer a 30 day
rolling contract for potential temporary work purposes. Seth mentioned that the other
benefit is that the RV tenants still have to pay transient taxes with a 30 day rolling
contract, which benefits the city. Fran asked if the existing RV parks will be
grandfathered into this ordinance. Seth stated that they’re all being prosecuted right
now. If we adopt this code, and they adhere to it then the charges will be dropped with
no criminal record. Skip said we need to adopt a code that benefits everyone. Alan and
Fran both like the 30 day rolling period. Fran also likes the part about not being mobile
and not having any “appendages.” Alan asked if the city has recourse for the problem
residents. Seth said their business license fee goes up, and Skip said that it falls on the
owner to take care of it. Karl asked if this would be used in combination with our
nuisance code. He stated that we have a problem with enforcement of the current
nuisance code and feels it needs to be updated. Karl asked how the RV parks that are
grandfathered in would interact with this code. Seth said it was an agreement with their
annexation. Karl pointed out that we require 2 inspections per year- what does this
inspection entail? Most of these structures are permanent. Seth explained that the

       inspectors will be making sure the rules are being followed, spacing, checking for safety
       hazards, registrations, no skirting, etc.

       f.    Action Item- Motion
       Ann asked for a motion to accept ordinance 07-17-23 as written in regards to RV parks.

       Motion: Alan Hancock Second: Fran Peterson Outcome: Passed Unanimously
