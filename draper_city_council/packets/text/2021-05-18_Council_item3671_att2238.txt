                                           RECITALS
      A.    Assignor and Assignee are parties to that certain Purchase Agreement dated
[________________], 2020 (as amended, “Purchase Agreement”).
        B.     Assignor is the owner of that certain real property located in the City of Draper,
State of Utah, described on Exhibit ”A” attached hereto (“Real Property”).
        C.       The Real Property is subject to each of the following documents: (i) that certain
Agreement for Development of Land, dated March 25, 2013, between the Agency and Draper
Holdings, as assigned pursuant to that certain Assignment and Assumption Agreement for
Development of Land dated January 30, 2015, between Draper Holdings, LLC (“Draper
Holdings”) BG Vista and the Agency, as supplemented by that certain Directive Letter
(Agreement for Development of Land) dated January 30, 2015, executed by Draper Holdings and
the Agency, as supplemented by that certain Letter Agreement dated January 30, 2015, executed
by BG Vista and agreed to by the Agency and the City (the “Letter Agreement”), and as

Entry Number 11985471 in Book 10292 at Page 8279 in the Recorder’s Office (collectively, the

the City, the Agency and Draper Holdings, as assigned by that certain Assignment and Assumption
of Master Reimbursement Agreement dated January 30, 2015, by and between Draper Holdings,
BG Vista, the Agency and Draper City, and as supplemented by the Letter Agreement
(collectively, the “Master Reimbursement Agreement”), (iii) that certain Master Area Plan dated
January 8, 2013, as required by Draper City Ordinance 9-14-069, as amended by that certain
Resolution of Approval of Amendment to Master Area Plan and Related Agreement for Vista
Station, Resolution 15-05 (collectively, the “MAP”; and together with the Development
Agreement, the ADL and the Master Reimbursement Agreement, the “Development
Agreements”)
       D.      Assignor is conveying to Assignee and Assignee is acquiring from Assignor all of
Assignor’s right, title, and interest in and to the Real Property and certain additional real and
personal property interests (collectively defined in the Purchase Agreement as the “Property”),
but excluding (i) right and obligations related to the real property having tax parcel ID number 27-
36-353-002 (the “Commercial Parcel”), including, without limitation, the Impact Fee Waiver
(defined below), and (ii) the Reimbursement Rights (defined below).
        E.    Assignor is not assigning to Assignee, and Assignor is retaining (collectively, the
“Retained Rights”): (i) the entire right, title and interest of Assignor to receive any tax increment,
including proceeds of any bonds which are secured by proceeds of tax increment, impact fees or
other amounts which are required to be paid to Assignor under the terms of the ADL or Master
Reimbursement Agreement, whether such payment obligations relate to the construction of any
existing improvements or any other improvements which may be constructed in the future (the
“Reimbursement Rights”), (ii) the waiver of the transportation impact fee pertaining to the
Commercial Parcel (the “Impact Fee Waiver”), and (iii) the rights and obligations relating solely
to the development of the Commercial Parcel under the Development Agreements.
        F.      In connection with the transfer of the Property, and as part thereof, Assignor desires
to delegate, transfer and assign to Assignee, and Assignee desires to accept the assignment of, all
of Assignor’s right, title and interest in and to the Development Agreement (but excluding the
Retained Rights) as of the date that the Property is transferred to Assignee (“Transfer Date”),
upon and subject to the provisions and conditions of this Assignment.
       G.      The Agency and City, as applicable, desires to consent to the assignment of all of
Assignor’s right, title and interest in the Development Agreements to Assignee as of the Transfer
Date, and to release Assignor from all obligations and liabilities under the Development
Agreements as of the Transfer Date (but excluding the Retained Rights), and (ii) Assignor’s
continued ownership of the Retained Rights.
                                          AGREEMENT
        1.     Upon and subject to the provisions and conditions of this Assignment, as of the
Transfer Date, Assignor delegates, assigns and transfers all of Assignor’s right, title and interest
in the Development Agreements (but excluding the Retained Rights) to Assignee. Notwithstanding
the foregoing to the contrary, this Assignment will not limit Assignee, or its successors in interest
from entering into any separate agreements with the Agency and City, if agreed by the Agency
and City, permitting Assignee to receive tax increment or other reimbursements from the Agency
and City, provided, any such agreements will not reduce or limit the amounts payable to Assignor
and will be subject and subordinate to the obligations of the Agency and City to pay the Retained
Rights to Assignor.
        2.     Upon and subject to the provisions and conditions of this Assignment, Assignee
hereby accepts the assignment and transfer of all of Assignor’s rights, title and interest in the
Development Agreements (but excluding the Retained Rights) as of the Transfer Date and assumes
and agrees to be bound by and perform all of the terms, conditions and requirements of Assignor
under the Development Agreements (but excluding the Retained Rights) arising with respect to
the period from and after the Transfer Date.
       3.      Agency and City, as applicable, hereby approves and consents to the assignment of
the Development Agreements (excluding the Retained Rights) from Assignor to Assignee and the
assumption by Assignee from Assignor of the Development Agreements (excluding the Retained
Rights), upon and subject to the provisions and conditions of this Assignment.
       4.      Upon and subject to the provisions and conditions hereof and the provisions and
conditions of the Directive Letter, from and after the Transfer Date, Agency and the City shall
continue to pay all amounts owing to Assignor under the ADL and Master Reimbursement
Agreement on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth therein.
        5.      Upon Assignor’s request, at all times during the term of the ADL and Master
Reimbursement Agreement, Assignor shall have the right to audit the books and records of Agency
with respect to the amounts payable under the ADL and Master Reimbursement Agreement.
Agency shall deliver to Assignor copies of all relevant backup materials required by Assignor with
respect to the amounts payable under the ADL and Master Reimbursement Agreement. Agency
shall cooperate with any such audit and shall maintain complete books and records with respect to
the amounts payable under the ADL and Master Reimbursement Agreement.
       6.     Effective as of the Transfer Date, Agency and the City hereby agrees that Assignor
shall be and is hereby released from all obligations and liabilities under the Development
Agreement, excluding those related solely to the Retained Rights.
        7.      Assignor agrees to indemnify and hold Assignee harmless from and against any
and all losses, costs, liabilities, damages and expenses, including, without limitation, attorneys’
fees, arising out of the Development Agreements and accruing prior to the Transfer Date.
        8.      Assignee agrees to indemnify and hold Assignor harmless from and against any
and all losses, costs, liabilities, damages and expenses, including, without limitation, attorneys’
fees, arising out of the Development Agreements and accruing on or after the Transfer Date, but
excluding obligations related to the Commercial Parcel.
        9.      From and after the Transfer Date, Assignee shall use best efforts to comply with all
of the provisions and conditions of the Development Agreements, including obligations under the
ADL and Master Agreement to the extent related to the Property, but excluding obligations related
to the Commercial Parcel. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing, Assignor will take all
actions required by the ADL and Master Development Agreement which are required to be
performed after the date hereof, and will not fail to take any action which suspends or eliminates
the rights of Assignor to continue to receive the Reimbursement Rights under the ADL and Master
Reimbursement Agreement.
         10.    Notwithstanding Assignor’s retention of an interest in the Retained Rights,
Assignee and Agency and/or the City shall have the right to modify or amend the Development
Agreements or resolve any disagreements or disputes regarding the Development Agreements,
excluding disputes regarding amounts payable under the Development Agreements, so long as
such modifications or amendments do not adversely affected Assignor’s Retained Rights. Without
limiting the generality of the foregoing, Assignee, the City and the Agency, will not directly modify
or amend the Master Reimbursement Agreement or ADL without the written consent of Assignor in a
manner that: (a) decreases the amount of reimbursement or tax increment payable to Assignor under the
Reimbursement Agreement or the ADL; (b) increases the period of time over which the amount of
reimbursement or tax increment are paid to Assignor under the Master Reimbursement Agreement or
the ADL; or (c) any release of City or Agency under the Master Reimbursement Agreement or ADL. In
addition, Assignee, the City and the Agency shall not amend the Master Reimbursement Agreement or
the ADL: (i) to grant to any third party the right to receive all or any portion of the amounts payable to
Assignor under the ADL or the Master Reimbursement Agreement in any manner which would decrease
the total amount of reimbursement or tax increment payable to Assignor; (ii) to decrease the amount of
reimbursement or tax increment payable to Assignor under the ADL or the Master Reimbursement
Agreement with respect to the buildings and improvements constructed prior to or after the date hereof
in any manner which would decrease the total amount of reimbursement or tax increment payable to
Assignor; (iii) to decrease the amount of reimbursement or tax increment payable to Assignor under the
ADL or the Master Reimbursement Agreement with respect to the buildings and improvements once
constructed in any manner which would decrease the total amount of reimbursement or tax increment
payable to Assignor, except the foregoing shall not require Assignee to obtain Assignor’s consent in
connection with any construction, demolition, reconstruction, addition, alteration or any other change to
such buildings or improvements or zoning or land use laws relating thereto with respect to the Property
(but excluding the Commercial Property); (iv) permit the City or the Agency to waive, forgive or refund
impact fees or tax increment on property subject to the Community Development Project Area Plan (as
defined in the ADL) including the Property, in any manner which would decrease the total amount of
reimbursement or tax increment payable to Assignor; (v) to remove or exclude any property which is
subject to the Community Development Project Area Plan as of the Transfer Date; or (vi) modify (A) the
interest rate payable to Assignor under the ADL or the Master Reimbursement Agreement, (B) the
percentage of reimbursement or tax increment payable by the City or Agency, or the priority of the
allocation of such payments, to Assignor under the ADL or the Master Reimbursement Agreement
(including, without limitation, a modification to the provisions of Section 4.3 of the ADL), or (C) the
percentage share of the reimbursement or tax increment payable (directly or indirectly) to Assignor, in
each case in any manner which would decrease the total amount of reimbursement or tax increment
payable to Assignor.
        11.      Agency and City hereby certifies as follows: (i) the Development Agreements
constitutes the entire agreement between Agency and/or the City, as applicable, and Assignor with
respect to the matters set forth therein; (ii) the Development Agreements are in full force and
effect; (iii) the Development Agreements have not been assigned, supplemented, modified or
otherwise amended; (iv) the Development Agreements are enforceable against Agency and the
City, as applicable, and Assignor in accordance with its terms; (v) all TSD System Improvements
and other Improvements required by the Development Agreements have been completed and
accepted by the City, and (vi) no defaults have occurred and no conditions exist, that, but for the
passage of time or the giving of notice or both, would result in a default by the Agency and/or the
City, as applicable, or Assignor under the terms of the Development Agreements, and there are no
unresolved or pending disputes between Agency and Assignor with respect to the Development
Agreements
        12.     Each party shall execute and deliver such further documents, and perform such
further acts, as may be reasonably necessary to achieve the parties’ intent in entering into this
Assignment.
        13.     If any party to this Assignment is required to employ counsel to enforce any of the
terms of this Assignment or for damages by reason of any alleged breach of this Assignment or
for a declaration of rights hereunder, the prevailing party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable
attorneys’ fees, expert fees and court costs incurred.
        14.    This Assignment (a) shall be binding on, and inure to the benefit of the parties
hereto, and their successors in interest and assigns; (b) shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of Utah; and (c) may be executed in several counterparts, all
of which taken together shall be deemed the original.
       15.     Assignor shall, at the written request of Assignee and at no cost, expense or liability
to Assignor, execute and deliver to Assignee all other and further instruments necessary to vest in
Assignee all of Assignor’s right, title and interest in or to the Development Agreements (but
excluding the Retained Rights). Assignee shall, at the written request of Assignor and at no cost,
expense or liability to Assignee, execute and deliver to Assignor all other and further instruments
necessary to vest in Assignee all Assignor’s right, title and interest in or to the Development
Agreements (but excluding the Retained Rights).
