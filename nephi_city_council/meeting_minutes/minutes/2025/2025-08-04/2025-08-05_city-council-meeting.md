ANNEXATION AREA MAP CHANGE
       For the last two months, the Council has changed several
       large parcels of land to the west of the current city limits
       from a Level 3 to a Level 2 in the annexation area map in
       preparation for a Level 2 analysis. This area contained land
       that is of interest to several businesses and public
       entities. One of the businesses is a developer who
       prepares sites for future data centers. This business is Set
       Square and the city entered a non-disclosure agreement
       with them several weeks ago. In addition to the change in Levels they requested at the
       last Council meeting, they have also requested that an area (highlighted in blue) be
       moved from a Level 3 to a Level 2 in the Annexation Area Map. A change in the levels
       will allow the area to be studied and an in-depth analysis completed. This will also
       provide an opportunity to show how utilities can be brought to the area along with the
       other areas that were recently changed to a Level 2 to the north of the highlighted
       area.
       Councilor Skip Worwood made a motion to move the highlighted area from a level 3 to a
       level 2 for further analysis. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The
       motion passed on unanimous vote.

Work Session
  1. City Pool Slide
          a. There was an incident at the pool involving the slide. A child fell off and the
             parent was concerned. Pool management closed the slide until they received
             some guidance from council.
          b. Craig Oswald (Recreation Director), Jeanna Camp (Pool Manager), Clark Wood
             (government buildings manager), and several pool employees attended to
             discuss what to do with the slide.
          c. Currently the slide has a pad installed at the bottom of the slide as padding for
             such incidents as well as a railing to hold when climbing up.
          d. The council and Swimming pool staff discussed options including: more signage,
             stencil on ground near slide specifying parental supervision, or tear slide down.
             Only one child is allowed on the slide at a time.
          e. The council also discussed that with any activity there are inherent risks that
             parents should be aware of and make decisions based on those risks.
          f. Lisa Brough mentioned the statistics--this was one incident out of the hundreds
             of children that go down the slide each day.
          g. The council decided to keep the slide open and to continue to mitigate
             foreseeable risks.
