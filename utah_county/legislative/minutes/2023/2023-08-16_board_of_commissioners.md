THIS PARCEL DID NOT MEET THE AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
FOR THE TWO YEARS PRIOR TO THE ROLLBACK IN DECEMBER OF 2022. ONCE A
LANDOWNER RECEIVES A ROLLBACK NOTICE, THIS NOTICE INFORMS THEM THAT
THE PROPERTY IS NO LONGER IN GREENBELT.

THERE HAS BEEN A WELL-ESTABLISHED PATTERN THAT THIS PARCEL HAS NOT
BEEN IN GREENBELT AS OF THE DATE OF THE ROLL BACK. THIS WAS FURTHER |

CONFIRMED THROUGH THE DENIAL OF THE APPEAL BY THE BOARD OF
EQUALIZATION. ONCE A PARCEL HAS BEEN DROPPED OUT OF GREEN BELT STATUS,
THE OWNER MUST ESTABLISH THE REQUIRED PRODUCTION, TWO YEARS PRIOR
TO THE ROLLBACK IN ORDER TO QUALIFY FOR GREENBELT STATUS.

THIS PARCEL IS NOT CURRENTLY IN GREEN BELT STATUS, AND IT IS
RECOMMENDED, BEFORE ANY DEFERRAL AGREEMENT OR ABATEMENT IS
ADDRESSED, THAT THE OWNERS APPLY ONCE AGAIN FOR GREEN BELT STATUS.

A ONE-MONTH EXTENSION IS RECOMMENDED TO ALLOW THE PROPERTY OWNER
A CHANCE TO APPLY FOR GREENBELT. THIS APPLICATION DOES NOT GUARANTEE
GREENBELT STATUS.

IF THE PARCEL IS IN GREENBELT STATUS, THEN A DEFERRAL AGREEMENT CAN BE
PUT TOGETHER, BUT IT MUST BE IN GREENBELT FIRST. ONCE THERE IS A
GREENBELT DETERMINATION, THEN THE EXISTING ABATEMENT APPLICATION
CAN BE REUSED.
