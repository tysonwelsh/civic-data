In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

F. ACTION ITEMS:

     1. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 13-2022 APPOINTING A MEMBER TO THE GRAMA APPEALS
        BOARD.
            Stacy Skeen (Commission Office): Submitting James Ebert to fill existing board vacancy.
            Chris Crockett (Civil Attorney): Explanation of GRAMA appeals process.
            Commissioner Froerer moved for approval Resolution 13-2022 appointing James Ebert to the GRAMA Appeals
            Board; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
     2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 14-2022 FOR AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT WITH WEBER
        STATE UNIVERSITY FOR A SUMMER 2022 LAW ENFORCEMENT ACADEMY.
            Chief Nealy Adams (Sheriff Department, Support Services): Corrections staffing short 33 people. Only 20
            training slots available locally. This agreement opens up 20-30 new training spots. Paying for 20 spots with
            10 additional spots at no extra cost. Money already set aside in training budget.

            Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of Resolution 14-2022 for an Inter-local Agreement with Weber
            State University for a Summer 2022 Law Enforcement Academy; Commissioner Freorer seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
     3. APPROVAL OF FINAL READING OF ORDINANCE 2022-08 TO CHANGE THE NAME OF
        STREETS WITHIN UNINCORPORATED AREAS OF WEBER COUNTY’S UPPER VALLEY.
            Brett Badley (Surveyor Office): No changes since First Reading (conducted 2/22/22).
            Chair Jenkins moved to approve the final reading of Ordinance 2022-08 to change the name of streets within
            unincorporated areas of Weber County’s Upper Valley; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye-
