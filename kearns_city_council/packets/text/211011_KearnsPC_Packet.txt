             Rules of Conduct for Planning Commission Meetings
PROCEDURE FOR PUBLIC COMMENT

1.   Any person or entity may appear in person or be represented by an authorized agent at any meeting of the
     Commission.

2.   Unless altered by the Chair, the order of the procedure on an application shall be:

     a. The supporting agency staff will introduce the application, including staff’s recommendations and a
        summary of pertinent written comments and reports concerning the application

     b. The applicant will be allowed up to 15 minutes to make their presentation.

     c. The Community Council representative can present their comments as applicable.

     d. Where applicable, persons in favor of, or not opposed to, the application will be invited to speak.

     e. Where applicable, persons opposing the application, in whole or in part will be invited to speak.

     f. Where applicable, the applicant will be allowed 5 minutes to provide concluding statements.

     g. Surrebutals may be allowed at the discretion of the Chair.

CONDUCT FOR APPLICANTS AND THE PUBLIC

1.   Speakers will be called to the podium by the Chair.

2.   Each speaker, before talking, shall give his or her name and address.

3.   All comments should be directed to the Commissioners, not to the staff or to members of the audience.

4.   For items where there are several people wishing to speak, the Chair may impose a time limit, usually 3
     minutes per person, or 5 minutes for a group spokesperson. If a time limit is imposed on any member or
     spokesperson of the public, then the same time limit is imposed on other members or spokespersons of the
     public, respectively.

5.   Unless otherwise allowed by the Chair, no questions shall be asked by the speaker or Commission Members.

6.   Only one speaker is permitted before the Commission at a time.

7.   The discussion must be confined to essential points stated in the application bearing on the desirability or
     undesirability of the application.

8.   The Chair may cease any presentation or information that has already been presented and acknowledge that it
     has been noted in the public record.

9.   No personal attacks shall be indulged in by either side, and such action shall be sufficient cause for stopping the
     speaker from proceeding.

10. No applause or public outbursts shall be permitted.

11. The Chair or supporting agency staff may request police support to remove offending individuals who refuse to
    abide by these rules.

12. After the public comment portion of a meeting or hearing has concluded, the discussion will be limited to the
    Planning Commission and Staff.

Request: Zone change from R-1-6 (Single-Family Residential) to C-2 (Commercial).

Planner: Molly Gaughran
Planning Staff Recommendation: Denial


                                         PROJECT DESCRIPTION
This is a proposal to rezone 0.19 acre of property from R-1-6 (Single-Family Residential) to C-2 (Commercial).
The property is currently used as a single-family residence with a currently active home business, where a
resident of the property provides service as a barber. The proposed rezoning would allow for the home business
use to be the primary use of the property, or for the owners to commercially lease the property.

                                         EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The R-1-6 zone allows for primarily single-family residential dwelling development, with few opportunities for
nonresidential uses, such as agriculture. The C-2 zone allows for a wide variety of community and neighborhood
commercial uses as permitted uses. Considerations for the impact of a rezone from R-1-6 (Single Family
Residential) to C-2 (Commercial) at this site include its location in a single-family residential neighborhood, its
frontage on 4015 West, and the proximity to the Kearns Town Center character area.

                            SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is a corner lot that fronts onto 4015 West and 5500 South. To the east and south are properties
zoned R-1-6 (Single Family Residential). To the east of the property is the municipal boundary of Taylorsville.
Across a private alleyway and to the north of the property are properties zoned C-2 (Commercial), which the
General Plan identifies as Kearns Town Center character area. Vehicle driveway access to the property is
available on both 5500 South and the private alleyway to the north.

                                 GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The Kearns General Plan was adopted in 2020. Character areas were identified in the General Plan to guide the
development of neighborhoods within Kearns.
        Request: Rezone from R-1-6 to C-2                                         File #: REZ2021-000430

The plan identifies the character area the property is located in as the Edgemont Park Residential character
area. The vision for this area is to continue to provide high-quality housing options for residents, as well as to
expand access to daily needs, such as grocery stores, parks, education facilities, and employment opportunities.

The area to the north of the property is identified in the General Plan to be the Kearns Town Center character
area. This area is zoned C-2 (Commercial) and provides strip commercial development. The vision of this area is
to “create a healthy gathering place that is representative of the diverse community, reflective of our history,
connects residents, business owners and visitors to each other, and provides opportunities for learning and
enrichment.” The General Plan (2020) and Kearns Town Center Master Plan (2019) envision this area to be the
primary shopping and dining center of Kearns.

Immense efforts were made by Kearns Metro Township and MSD Staff, among others, to create and adopt the
General Plan (2020), particularly in identifying areas for commercial development. Though the property is
proximate to the Kearns Town Center, the uses allowable by the C-2 zone would not meet the vision of the
current character area.
