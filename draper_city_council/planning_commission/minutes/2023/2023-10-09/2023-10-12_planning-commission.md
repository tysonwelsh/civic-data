       B.      Public Hearing: Draper Box N Lock Deviations Request (Administrative
               Action).
               On the request of Stephen Tripp Bill Gould, a Request for Approval of Deviations
               from Building Façade Standards for a New Self Storage Facility to be Built on
               Approximately 3.88 Acres. This property is located at approximately 15037 South
               Minuteman Drive. Known as Application 2023-4581-VAR. Staff Contact: Tevai
               Haunga, 801-576-6522, tevai.haunga@draperutah.gov.

Mr. Draper presented the Staff Report on behalf of Ms. Haunga and stated that the request is for
deviations for the Draper Box N Lock storage units, which was presented for site plan approval
about one month earlier. Aerial and Vicinity Maps were displayed to show the location of the
subject property. The land use is Community Commercial, and the zoning designation is M1
Industrial. The applicant was returning to the Planning Commission to request the following three
deviations:

       1.      Significant horizontal façade variation.
       2.      Minimum required number of primary materials; and
       3.      Height and roofline variations.

The variation requests are all for facades that are interior to the main facility and involve only the
single-story storage units. It was clarified that the deviations would not be for the main building.
The floor plans showing the significant horizontal variation occurring on the exterior were shown.
The request was reiterated that the horizontal variation not be required for the interior facades.
The applicant was also asking for a deviation to be allowed to have only a single primary material
for the facades. It was noted that three of the four facades for each building will be near one-
hundred percent CMU block. Although there will be two colors of CMU, it will be still be only
one single material. Representative elevations were presented and it was indicated that they are
similar for all of the single level storage buildings. On the interior facades, the applicant would
also like a deviation to do away with the required parapet variations. They would, however, still
be required to have these height and roofline variations on the exterior walls. It was clarified that
a reference on page eight of the Staff Report of “City Council” be changed to “Planning
Commission”.

An approval letter was included in the packet summarizing what was done on September 14 and
should have referenced 2023 rather than 2024. A question was raised regarding whether all of the
conditions of the approval have been completed. Mr. Draper stated that they are still being worked
through. The current request is separate. Vice-Chair Fowler recalled that the Planning

Commission in the past has approved buildings with docks that face one another. Deviations were
given in that case for the interior when they faced each other. This was a similar situation.

Reference was made to page six of the Staff Report where some of the applicable criteria are listed.
A question was raised as to whether they are the same for each deviation. Mr. Draper confirmed
that they are and stated that the Commission only needs to find that they meet three of the 10
criteria. He also confirmed that the building material calculation has been verified and stated that
the amount of CMU walls on three sides are 100%, which is greater than the 75% minimum
requirement. He explained that these criteria have been met. The exterior elevations were
displayed. Mr. Draper explained how the request complies with City Code.

The applicant, Stephen Tripp, handed out paper copies of building renderings and offered to email
the renderings to the Commission to be made part of the record after the meeting. At one point
they were trying to do a deviation on the easements between connecting the properties. He did not
understand at the last meeting what Planning Staff was asking for , which is why he is now needing
to come back for the deviation. Mr. Tripp lives in Draper and supports the new design criteria.
Because they are so close together where they do both sides of the building, it creates a design
problem because people have a difficult time moving trucks back and forth. They were excited
about the product and are willing to meet the requirements to fulfill the City’s requirements.

The Architect, Troy Anderson from Clarity Design Group was present. Reference was made to
page six of the staff report, which lists the three criteria for approval of a deviation. Commissioner
Squire asked a question with respect to criterion number nine. Mr. Anderson was asked to explain
how the building and architectural style meet the criteria. Mr. Anderson stated that it specifically
asks if they are a contemporary or modern style and if they utilize façade materials differently than
prescribed. Mr. Anderson began pontificating on the architectural context and construction of the
building facades and the intended visual effect of using differing textures and colors of CMU
Block. After several minutes, commissioner Squire stopped him and asked what specific
architectural style the building was constructed in. Mr. Anderson answered that the architectural
style is contemporary.

A question was raised by the Commissioners regarding the screening of rooftop units, Mr.
Anderson stated that none of the buildings have rooftop equipment as the units will not be heated
or cooled.

Vice-Chair Fowler opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public
hearing was closed.
