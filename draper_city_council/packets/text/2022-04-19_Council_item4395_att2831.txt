                                    ORDINANCE NO. 1536

       AN ORDINANCE VACATING THE SOUTH FIELD DITCH DRAINAGE
       CHANNEL EASEMENT

       WHEREAS, Draper City, hereinafter referred to as the "City," has heretofore been
granted and presently holds an easement for the maintenance, operation and repair of a ditch and
storm drainage facilities pursuant to an easement recorded November 25, 1996 as Entry No.
6513716 in Book 7542 at Page 1006 the official records of the Salt Lake County Recorder (the
“Easement”); and

       WHEREAS, the City no longer needs the Easement; and

       WHEREAS, The City has been requested to vacate the Easement and is willing to do so;

        WHEREAS, the City has recorded previous revocation and abandonment documents
related to other parcels within the City of the same Easement, such as recorded February 19, 2004
as Entry 8982396 in Book 8947 at Page 3460-3461 of the official records of the Salt Lake County
Recorder (the “REVOCATION AND ABANDONMENT OF EASTMENT”); and

        WHEREAS, the City completed the abandonment of ditch drainage facilities and is no
longer in use in the public storm drain system; and

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH:

        Section 1. Findings: The City Council finds that neither the public interest nor any person
will be materially injured by the vacation and there is good cause for the vacation.

       Section 2. Easement Vacation. In consideration of the mutual promises set forth herein,
and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby
acknowledged, the City hereby vacates and abandons the Easement to the owner or owners of the
dominant tenement, as their interests may appear.

        Section 3. Parcel Identification. The following described portion of the Parcel 28-33-
176-085 located in Section 33, Township 3 South, Range 1 East, Salt Lake Base and Meridian,
and as shown on the attached Exhibit A of the South Field Ditch Drainage Channel, no longer
serves a drainage facility purpose and can be vacated as a drainage easement and as shown on the
attached Exhibit B:
                                     See EXHIBITS A and B

       Section 4. Future Recording of Abandonment. The South Field Ditch Drainage Channel
Easement was recorded separately on several parcels and all instances of the recorded easement
have not been identified and vacated. The City Council finds that future vacation documents
meeting the recording requirements of the Salt Lake County Recorder may be prepared by city
staff and recorded to vacate the South Field Ditch Drainage Channel Easement as authorized by
this ordinance without additional City Council action.
      Section 5. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this
Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 6. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or the vacation is recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder, whichever
is closer to the date of final passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, ON THIS ____th DAY OF APRIL 2022.
