1. Zone Ogden and Plan Ogden Update

Planning Director Barton Brierley addressed updates to downtown mixed-use areas with a 40%
residential cap and encouraging commercial, retail, and institutional uses. Primary streets were
designated for non-residential development, while side streets could be 100% residential. The
commission also addressed House Bill 33, which prohibits banning political signs on park
strips. They considered reducing detached ADU sizes to 150 sq. ft. and discussed potential
park spaces in downtown. Upcoming events include a public showcase on June 24 and a public
hearing on the general plan on July 13.
