   1. ACTION ON A REQUEST (ZTA 2015-03) TO AMEND THE COUNTY LAND USE CODE SECTION 101-1-7
      (DEFINITIONS); TITLE 104, CHAPTER 29 (OGDEN VALLEY DESTINATION AND RECREATION RESORT
      ZONE); TITLE 108, CHAPTER 1 (DESIGN REVIEW); TITLE 108, CHAPTER 2 (OGDEN VALLEY
      ARCHITECTURAL, LANDSCAPE AND SCREENING DESIGN STANDARDS); TITLE 108, CHAPTER 8
      (PARKING AND LOADING SPACE, VEHICLE TRAFFIC AND ACCESS REGULATIONS); TITLE 108,
      CHAPTER 19 (ACCESSORY APARTMENTS); AND TITLE 110, CHAPTER 2 (OGDEN VALLEY SIGNS).
      (THESE PROPOSED AMENDMENTS WERE PREVIOUSLY DISCUSSED AT A PUBLIC HEARING HELD ON
            ND
      THE 22 DAY OF DECEMBER 2015) – ORDINANCE 2016-4

         Scott Mendoza, of the County Planning Division, referred to the full staff report before the commissioners
         on this item. He noted that to date this proposal has gone through two work sessions with the Ogden
         Valley Planning Commissions, two public hearings—one with the Ogden Valley Planning Commission
         and one with the Western Weber Planning Commission—and one with the County Commission. Since
         the County Commission’s public hearing the applicant has requested to modify slightly three of the many
         items that were presented:
         1) Adding language to the existing definition of lockout sleeping room. Currently, these are restricted to
             condominium units. The original proposal was to add lockout sleeping rooms to every type of
             accommodation that could house people. The modified ordinance will add attached lockout sleeping
             rooms to the uses in the Destination and Recreation Resort (DRR-1) Zone but will restrict them to
             condominium units and single family dwellings only, and limit them to two.
       2) Adding second kitchens to the list of uses in the DRR-1 Zone. The original proposal was to add
          second kitchens to the DRR-1 but it did not have a limit to the overall number of kitchens that one
          dwelling (of any kind) could have. The modified ordinance will still add second kitchens to the list of
          permitted uses in the DRR-1 but will clarify that there is a limit of two per dwelling.
       3) Adding accessory dwelling units (ADUs) to the list uses in the DRR-1. The original proposal was to
          add these to the list but did not clarify that an ADU represents one development unit when accounting
          for units that have been approved and available for development in this Zone.

       Tom Jolley, representing SMHG, LLC, stated that this amendment has been in the works for some time
       and that Mr. Mendoza explained it well. Greg Muaro, representing SMHG, LLC, stated that the genesis
       of this development is that they are embracing smaller structures overall. They feel that they are in a
       national park environment and want to develop in a way that is better for the environment.

       Commissioner Bell said that this would affect both of the ski resorts in the county. He had asked County
       Planning about a year ago to start working with Snowbasin Resort. He approves of these amendments.
       Commissioner Ebert appreciates that the petitioner has listened to the county and the community’s
       concerns. He believes the result is a good resolution.
       Commissioner Bell moved to adopt ordinance 2016-4 amending Section 101-1-7 (Definitions); Title 104,
       Chapter 29 (Ogden Valley Destination and Recreation Resort Zone); Title 108, Chapter 1 (Design
       Review); Title 108, Chapter 2 (Ogden Valley Architectural, Landscape and Screening Design Standards);
       Title 108, Chapter 8 (Parking and Loading Space, Vehicle Traffic and Access Regulations); Title 108,
       Chapter 19 (Accessory Apartments); and Title 110, Chapter 2 (Ogden Valley Signs) within the County
       Land Use Code; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
