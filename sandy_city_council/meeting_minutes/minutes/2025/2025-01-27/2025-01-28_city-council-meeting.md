Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions

                                  Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer welcomed those in attendance.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones offered the Prayer.
                                  Council Member Alison Stroud led the Pledge.

                                  Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.

General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                                  Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                                  Comment.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones expressed her support of starting council meetings with a prayer. Opening
                                  prayer reminds us that we share a common commitment to be united in good
                                  governance. Thank you for bringing back prayer. I hope it is a long tradition in our city
                                  council meetings.

                                  Ms. Marilyn Larson loves Sandy City. It was very upsetting to her that the prayer was
                                  omitted at a council meeting. Prayer is a huge part of our tradition. It is who we are. We
                                  need our prayers for guidance and a moment of silence doesn't do it. You represent the
                                  community and the community wants prayer.

                                  Mr. Craig Ulrich thanked the prior commenters for their comments. He gave a shout out
                                  to Council Members Houseman and Robinson for supporting the budget priorities
                                  workshop. It was a good decision and a very good exercise. Thank you for your
                                  leadership. He also mentioned that the recycling calendar on the website needed to be
                                  modified.

                                  Ms. Carola Michel expressed gratitude for keeping Sandy City a beautiful place. She
                                  expressed concerns that prayer was removed from the meeting last week and was
                                  grateful that it has been reinstalled.

                                  Council moved to the Consent Calendar Item on the Agenda.

Council Business

Informational Items


1.         25-025                 Public Utilities Advisory Board Bylaws Update

                   Attachments:   Memo - Public Utilities Advisory Board - updated by laws
                                  25-__C-Resolution 2025 PUAB

                                  Tom Ward, Public Utilities, provided a proposed update to the bylaws of the Public
                                  Utilities Advisory Board. The bylaws currently require monthly meetings. He proposed
                                  changing the bylaws to require a minimum of six meetings and adding additional
                                  meetings on an as needed basis which allows for greater flexibility. Council requested a
                                  redline version of the policy recommendations for their review.

2.         25-026                 Public Works Department providing the Council with an update on new
                                  departmental hires, fleet building construction progress, and Spring Bulk
                                  Waste Cleanup
                   Attachments:   PW Update January 2025

                                  Ryan Kump, Public Works provided a Winter 24/25 update on engineering staff new hires,
                                  construction Phase 2 progress, and the spring bulk waste program. He introduced Travis
                                  Ackermann, Assistant City Engineer and Ivan Hooper, City Transportation Engineer. He
                                  discussed the construction progress of the Phase 2 capital project. The new fleet shop
                                  will have 14,500 sq. feet of workspace, with increased bays to allow for concurrent
                                  repairs, specialized maintenance areas which will result in enhanced capacity for
                                  preventive maintenance. The expected projected completion for the Phase 2 project is
                                  July 2025. Mr. Kump thanked our Sandy partners for their work on the Phase 2
                                  construction project. He reviewed the Spring Bulk Waste program and schedule.
                                  Violations are trending downward as a result of educating residents. The new transfer
                                  station will create an opportunity to streamline the pickup process. The bulk waste
                                  pickup will begin in March and will go through May.Councl questions and comments
                                  followed. Council expressed their appreciation to the Public Department staff for their
                                  commitment and service to the city.

                                  Council Member Brooke D'Sousa was excused from the meeting at 5:50 pm.

                                  Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson mentioned that proposed amendments to the Short Term
                    Rental Ordinance will be presented to the Council at an upcoming Council meeting.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham expressed appreciation to all the Public Works
                    department staff who attended the meeting this evening and for showing their
                    commitment to their department. Thank you for all you do in service to the city.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer thanked the Police Chief for the tour of the department
                    crime center. He provided a brief update from the Legislative Policy Committee meeting.
                    He addressed the comments regarding prayers in the meetings and expressed a
                    commitment to respecting all voices.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud spoke about the Sandy Youth Council's participation in
                    the state-wide Youth Council event at the state capitol. The SYC was one of six youth
                    councils chosen to present at the Mock Committee. They were well prepared and we are
                    all very proud of them.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey invited the Council and the community to see the Arts
                    Guild production of Emma. She provided an update from the Legislative Policy
                    Committee.


Mayor's Report

                    The Mayor's report was given by the CAO.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO provided an update from the fire crew deployed to California. The Point
                    in Time Count event sponsored by the county is this weekend. Volunteers are welcome.
                    The Mayor's State of the City address will take place on Monday at the Waterford
                    School. The Economic Development Business Appreciation Open House is Thursday,
                    February 6th.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:29 pm.
