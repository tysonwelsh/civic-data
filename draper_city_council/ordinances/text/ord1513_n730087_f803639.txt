City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On January 18, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1513 amending the official
Land Use Map of Draper City for the Concord Lane Land Use Amendment. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 19th day of January 2022. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
