                                       PRESS RELEASE

  Cache County Council Unanimously Expresses Dismay with County Budget Process


       The Cache County Council announced Thursday that actions by the County Executive have
created tremendous dysfunction in County government and negatively impacted the citizens of the
County.

        During the Council meeting earlier this week, Cache County Executive David Zook presented a
dramatically different budget than the draft the Council had been working with since early September.
The tentative budget contains no line-item detail and appears to use aggressive sales tax revenue
projections and building fee increases to close the $2.4 million dollar deficit contained in previous
budget documents. In addition, it seems vital county services such as road projects and maintenance
have been significantly reduced. “Talk about confusion,” said Councilmember David Erickson.

        This new budget, which was emailed to the Council after the start of the meeting on Tuesday,
was unexpected. Although the Council has been actively engaged in the budget process throughout
the year, it has been challenging due to the lack of communication from the Executive. Councilmember
Karl Ward commented, “In my six years on the council, this is the worst budget year I’ve ever seen as
far as process.”

        After announcing the dramatically changed budget, Mr. Zook left the council meeting and did
not return to discuss the changes or engage in productive, public dialogue with the Council. This has
been a recurring theme throughout the year. He has canceled or refused to schedule budget
committee hearings since early this year and has been absent for a majority of Council meetings. This
hasn’t happened in the County’s history.

        Throughout the year, council members have expressed dismay that the historically collegial
relationship they’ve enjoyed with department heads has been severely curtailed. As a result, the
information needed to develop a sound and responsible budget has been lacking. Even today, the
Council doesn’t have the detail needed to perform their budget duties in an effective manner.
