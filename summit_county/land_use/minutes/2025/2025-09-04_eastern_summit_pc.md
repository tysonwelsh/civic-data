Commissioner Darcey sought clarification on several points regarding the findings of fact. First, what was the outcome with the contracting yard? Was it denied? Additionally, can a Conditional Use Permit be granted for the same parcel of land that contains a primary residence? Finally, are there any plans for the greenhouse? Commissioner Bowen inquired about the potential for this nursery to be open to the public. Applicant and Owner Jon Wiberg provided a response. (6:11 PM)

Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit to establish a Promontory soil repository site to be located off Painted Valley Pass, Promontory, Summit County, UT; Parcel SS-66 (782.61 acres); Applicant: Matthew Idema representing Promontory Development LLC (Francis Najafi); Administrative Process. Project #25-042.
Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.
(6:22 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Promontory - Soil Repository CUP - July 3, 2025 Eastern Summit County Planning Commission Meeting.pdf
Senior Planner Amir Caus a Conditional Use Permit to establish a Promontory soil repository site. (6:22 PM)
Matthew Idema, Director of Development at Promontory, and Shawn Potter, General Counsel at Promontory Development, introduced themselves and opened the floor for questions from the Commissioners. (6:25 PM)
Commissioner Bowen asked about noxious weeds and the hours of operation. Director Idema responded. (6:26 PM)
Commissioner Darcey asked, "Is this a big dirt pile?" Exhibit A3 contains the cut fill summary and the construction access design data. If I understand correctly, this is how big the dirt pile will be. What is the Concrete Wash area? Do you keep the topsoil on site? Director Idema responded. (6:28 PM)
Commissioner Sargent applauded the application. (6:33 PM)
Commission Chair Peterson inquired whether any remediation had been performed on the old site. Director Idema and Attorney Potter responded. (6:34 PM)
Commissioner Darcey had one more question regarding Exhibit A3, which details the volume of material being processed. If I understand it correctly, the exhibit specifies both a minimum and maximum height for the dirt pile. Additionally, condition of approval number 2 states that if offsite visibility from county and state roads is compromised, the applicant must cease operations until a suitable solution is implemented to address the issue. Where did that requirement originate? Director Idema responded. (6:38 PM)
Commission Chair Peterson opened the public comment at 6:42 PM.
No members of the public came forward to speak.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the public comment at 6:42 PM.
Bille Wilde made a motion to approve Promontory soil repository site pursuant to the findings of facts, conclusions of law and conditions of approval. (6:43 PM).The motion did not receive a second.
Debate ensued with Commissioners. (6:43 PM)
Bille Wilde made a motion to approve Promontory soil repository site striking item 2 in the conditions of approval pursuant to the findings of facts, conclusions of law and conditions of approval. (6:48 PM). David Darcey seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Marion Wheaton, Bridget Hayes.
