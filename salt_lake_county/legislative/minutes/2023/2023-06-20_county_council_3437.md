3.3. Other Elected County Officials
Ms. Bridget Romano , Deputy District Attorney, introduced Susanne Mitchell and 
Lorianne Szendre, District Attorney ’s Office, who were here to talk about a Victims 
of Crime Act (VOCA) grant.
Ms. Lorianne Szendre , Victim Support Services Division, District Attorney ’s 
Office, reported on the VOCA grant funding, the purpose of which is to serve 
victims of crime.  Last year, Victim Support Services was awarded $ 394,828, with 
which it funded six staff who provided direct services to victims of crime.  That 
helped serve an average of about 620 victims of crime every quarter.  Victim 
Support Services also met all of the target objectives that were outlined in the grant, 
both in terms of services provided and clients served.
Ms. Susanne Mitchell , Associate Division Director, Victim Support Services 
Division, District Attorney ’s Office, stated Victim Support Services received a 
second grant of $ 73,000, specific to the Children ’s Justice Center, which funded 
one-and-a-half staff - a child and family specialist, and a forensic interviewer 
working directly with the children.  The focus of their support is to advocate for the 
children, and make sure they are calm as they enter into the investigative and 
criminal justice process and become witnesses if they should have to go to court 
and testify.  With that funding, 1,300 child victims were served and over 4,000 
district services were provided to help them through the year.
