Citizen Comments


1.         22-216                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Houseman invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones believes the Council discussion on capital improvements and 
                                  infrastructure needs to be set aside. She expressed concerns with growing economic 
                                  concerns, inflation, rate hikes and possible recession. She spoke about the survey 
                                  results and expressed the need for additional feedback from residents and would like the 
                                  Council to consider having a third survey to gather accurate feedback from the whole city, 
                                  not just a small portion of the city.

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren agrees with Ms. Jones comments and thinks the survey should go 
                                  out to the whole city to provide feedback to the Council.

                                  Public Comment closed.

3.         22-219                 Building Projects: Priorities and Financing Discussion

                   Attachments:   Building Projects Discussion Memo for June 14.pdf
                                  How to pay for proposed capital projects through property tax 
                                  increases.pdf
                                  City Facilities Master Plan Draft Report - 2022 Edits.pdf
                                  Alta Canyon Sports Center (PDF).pdf
                                  Budgeting Priorities CIP and Financing Discussion.pdf

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director, provided an overview of the Sandy City Building 
                                  Replacement Projects proposed plan. He reviewed a 2018 Building Replacement Study 
                                  conducted by the City. Mr. Kelley reviewed the recommendations from the 2018 study 
                                  and the project impacts. He reviewed funding sources available for these capital projects 
                                  and the options and process for additional funding. He reviewed the Project 
                                  Recommendation list with the Council. Council questions followed the presentation. 
                                  Mayor Zoltanski provided additional comments regarding the need to address the 
                                  infrastructure priorities of the City.Council Member Mecham called for a Point of Order at 
                                  7:21 pm. Chair Houseman redirected the discussion back to the substance of the agenda 
                                  item. A lengthy Council discussion continued, and addressed the prioritization of the 
                                  infrastructure needs of the city facilities.

                                  A motion was made by Marci Houseman, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey to direct 
                                  staff to not bring back a resolution next week that would trigger a Truth in 
                                  Taxation Hearing...The motion failed by the following vote:

                                  Yes:   3    Marci Houseman
                                                  Cyndi Sharkey
                                                  Brooke D'Sousa

                                  No:    4     Alison Stroud
                                                  Zach Robinson
                                                  Ryan Mecham
                                                  Scott Earl

                                  Council continued the discussion following the vote on the motion.

                                  Council convened a recess at 8:25 pm.
                                  Council reconvened at 8:41 pm.

                                  Darien Alcorn, Senior Civil Attorney, in consultation with the City Attorney, responded to 
                                  questions regarding the truth in taxation hearing and provided legal guidance and direction 
                                  to the Council regarding the Truth in Taxation process. She also recommended that the 
                                  Council make an affirmative motion.



                                  A motion was made by Alison Stroud, seconded by Zach Robinson to direct staff 
                                  to bring back a resolution at the June 21, 2022 Council meeting, that would set a 
                                  Truth in Taxation Hearing on August 16, 2022 and would generate additional 
                                  revenue not to exceed $5 million for Council consideration...The motion carried 
                                  by the following vote:

                                  Yes:    4   Alison Stroud

Mayor's Report

                   Mayor Zoltanski spoke about her recent visit to Washington DC. She provided an uptdate 
                   on transportation infrastructure. Two Public Works projects obtained grant funding of 
                   approximately $219,000 and she acknowledged the Public Works staff for their efforts. 
                   Last Friday was the first Movie in the Park at Rio Tinto Stadium and had record 
                   attendance. This Friday at 1:00 pm she will be participating in a Press Conference with 
                   the Mayors and Police Chiefs around the County to kick off the  Slow Down in Your Town 
                   Initiative.

CAO Report

                   Cliff Strachan, CAO, provided the following updates: the City received a $46,000 grant, 
                   the Junior Fire Fighter Academy, the June 20th Entrepeneur Market at the Sandy 
                   Amphitheater, the iCanTri city race this Saturday and the July 4th preparations. 
                   Tomorrow is the Barbecue in Historic Sandy.

Adjournment
                   The Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 9:07 pm.
