                                  SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL
                                    MEETING MINUTES
                                       Thursday June 23, 2022
                                       Approved July 13, 2022

The following are the minutes of the special City Council Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The
meeting was held on Thursday, June 23, 2022, at 3:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers,
5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law,
was posted in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and
interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Lorin Palmer

Councilmembers Present: Teddy Hodges, Sherrie Ohrn, and Steven Shields

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Deputy City
Recorder Wendy Thorpe, Finance Director Alan Rae, and City Attorney Todd Sheeran.

Councilmembers Excused: Jared Henderson

3:00 PM (or as soon as possible thereafter):
   1. Call to Order
Mayor Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 3:03 p.m.

   2. Public Comment
There was no public comment offered.

   3. Discussion and Action Items
             3.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution amending an Interlocal Agreement
                  between Herriman City and the Community Development and Renewal Agency of
                  Herriman City relating to sales tax within the Auto Mall area – Nathan Cherpeski, City
                  Manager
City Manager Nathan Cherpeski presented an amendment to the Interlocal Agreement between the City
and the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City approved in 2019. This
amendment would remove the specified third party relating to the sharing of sales tax generated in the
participation area and replace the language with participants, as requested by the Council.

Councilmember Ohrn moved to approve Resolution No. R18-2022 approving an amendment to the
Interlocal Agreement between Herriman City and the Community Development and renewal Agency of
Herriman City relating to sales tax within the Auto Mall area. Councilmember Shields seconded the
motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Councilmember Jared Henderson Absent
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn        Aye
Councilmember Steven Shields      Aye
Councilmember Teddy Hodges        Aye
Mayor Lorin Palmer                Aye

The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Jared Henderson being absent.

             3.2 Discussion and consideration to establish the property tax rate for Herriman City for
                  the fiscal year 2022-2023 – Alan Rae, Finance Director
Financial Director Alan Rae explained during the previous meeting, the Council approved the tax rate
prior to the State Tax Commission setting the value. Since then, the State set a tax rate for Herriman City
and Director Rae looked for approval.

Councilmember Ohrn compared the home valuation increases. Finance Director Rae explained the
process of how the tax rate was set and noted it rate fluctuated to allow cities to receive approximately
the same amount year over year with a small increase allowance for growth.

Councilmember Hodges moved to approve Ordinance No 2022-29 revising the certified tax rate to
.000194 for the fiscal year 2022-2023. Councilmember Shields seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Councilmember Jared Henderson Absent
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn        Aye
Councilmember Steven Shields      Aye
Councilmember Teddy Hodges        Aye
Mayor Lorin Palmer                Aye

The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Jared Henderson being absent.

   4. Adjournment
Councilmember Ohrn moved to adjourn the Special City Council meeting at 3:10 p.m. Councilmember
Hodges seconded the motion, and all present voted aye.
