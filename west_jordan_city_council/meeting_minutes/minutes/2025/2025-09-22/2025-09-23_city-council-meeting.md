 4. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Whitelock opened the public comment period at 7:18 pm.
     Comments:
     Marilyn Borovatz, West Jordan resident, shared a problem she had held onto for the 47 years;
     speeding on 2700 West which had not improved in 47 years, but had gotten even faster. She
     referenced a letter of complaint sent to the City in June of 2020, and although her heart had
     been softened and she recognized the many responsibilities of the Police Department, she
     believed something needed to happen. Ms. Borovatz observed drivers, driving very fast,
     entering the turn lane on 2700 West earlier than necessary to turn left onto 7800 South, and
     suggested the Police Department place a portable speed limit sign at different places along 2700
     West, and occasionally position an officer to write a token ticket.
     Mitch Long, West Jordan resident, spent a lot of time on the Jordan River Trail (JRT) and
     observed a lot of behavior, some of which was not good. He suggested attention needed to be
     paid to what was happening or serious accidents would happen. Mr. Long believed the trail was
     not designed for high-speed use and many were using the trail inappropriately.
     Ann Marie Barrett, Draper City resident and Bingham Creek Library Manager, reported on
     participation in summer reading programs, and announced an upcoming winter reading
     program.
     Chair Whitelock closed public comment at 7:28 pm.


 5. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Ordinance No. 25-43 Amending the General Plan to Create a “Corridor” Land Use; and
          Ordinance No. 25-44 Amending the Future Land Use Map to Apply the “Corridor”
          Designation to Approximately 56 Acres of Land Along Redwood Road
          Senior Planner Tayler Jensen explained the proposals to create a “Corridor” land use in
          the General Plan, and to amend the Future Land Use Map (FLUM) to apply the Corridor
          designation to approximately 56 acres along Redwood Road. He emphasized the FLUM
          showed anticipated future land use of the City and was used to guide future land use
          decisions. The proposal did not include a zone change. Mr. Jensen showed a map of
          properties identified for potential application of the Corridor designation, and said any
          applications for zone change would first be considered by the Planning Commission, and
          then the City Council. He said the Planning Commission reviewed the two proposed
          ordinances and forwarded a positive recommendation.

           Chair Whitelock opened a public hearing at 7:32 pm.
           Comments:

       b. Ordinance No. 25-45 a Sub-Area Development Plan for Dry Creek Highlands Sub-Area 2
          Ross Dinsdale, applicant, showed the subject area on a map, and said the plan was to
          develop approximately 63 single-family homes, with improvements to Barneys Creek
          from 6400 West to the highway. Chair Whitelock asked if any of the homes would qualify
          as moderate income housing per the development agreement. Mr. Dinsdale said a handful
          of the proposed homes would be able to have basement apartments, which would go
          toward the moderate-income housing. Whether or not a basement apartment was
          included would depend on the purchaser. Mr. Dinsdale said the area directly west was
          planned to have a significant amount of moderate-income housing.
          Responding to a question from Council Member Jacob, Mr. Dinsdale confirmed the
          planned improvements included improvements to the trail along the creek. Associate

       c. Ordinance No. 25-46 Amending City Code Section 14-5-5 C (2) Regarding Traffic
          Calming Devices for New Residential Development
          Krista Reister with the Public Works Engineering Department explained that proposed
          amendments to City Code would help guide safer development. Current City Code was
          vague and open to interpretation for developers, City staff, and the public. Ms. Reister said
          a consultant had conducted a study of traffic calming measures, and recommended Code
          amendments.

           Council Member Jacob asked if “local streets” was defined in Code. Ms. Reister said the
           term was defined in the City’s Transportation Manual as residential streets with a 50-foot
           right-of-way and lower volume of traffic.

           Chair Whitelock opened a public hearing at 7:56 pm.
           Comments:
           None
           Chair Whitelock closed the public hearing at 7:56 pm.

           Council Member Green commented that the ordinance had not been updated since 2009,
           and expressed the opinion it should have been done sooner. He thanked staff for their
           work.

           Council Member Shelton reported on a visit to the West Jordan Citizen Academy and
           spoke of attending a West Jordan Youth Theatre production.

           Vice Chair Bedore spoke of participating in the recent Guns and Hoses event, shared his
           recent experience with the Passport Office, and said he had been invited to participate in

           mentioned that he wrote the Council Corner message for the upcoming City newsletter.
           He said he was rattled after the recent murder of Charlie Kirk, and even more rattled by
           public reactions to the tragedy. Council Member Jacob spoke of how deeply divided the
           nation was, and said he believed there was more to bring citizens together than to divide.

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson said each member of the Council would have an
          opportunity to write a message for the Council Corner in the newsletter. He reported
          updates to the Council webpage, and said staff were doing an audit of meetings on
          PrimeGov back to the change in government to ensure everything was documented
          correctly.

       c. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Burton spoke of attending a Naturalization Ceremony, reported a traffic signal was
          installed and operational at 7000 South and Airport Road, and a traffic signal was
          installed, but not yet operational at Gardner Lane and Redwood Road. Mayor Burton
          reported on recent events in the community, and encouraged participation at upcoming
          activities and events. He said a townhall meeting with Council Member Shelton was
          scheduled for October 2, 2025. Mayor Burton said a dedication ceremony was scheduled
          for the new Goldstar Memorial on October 18.

       d. City Administrator’s Report
          City Administrator Korban Lee reported on successful Days of Service, and gave kudos to
          Community Outreach Coordinator Glori Buchei for the 19 service projects in the City over
          three days. Mr. Lee reported Court operations would be unified in the same building
          beginning October 1, 2025.
