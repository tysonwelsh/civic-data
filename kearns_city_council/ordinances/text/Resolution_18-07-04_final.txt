KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH

RESOLUTION NO. 18-07-04 July 30, 2018

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE OFFERING OF A LETTER TO THE GREATER

SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT (MSD) SEEKING A MODIFICATION OF

THE MASTER INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BWEEN THE MSD, THE METRO

TOWNSHIPS, AND SALT LAKE COUNTY REGARDING FINANCIAL SERVICES

PROVIDED BY THE COUNTY

RECITALS

A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code Annotated
(“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District pursuant
to UCA §§ 17B-2a-1101 ef al.

D. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a county pursuant to Utah Ode Ann. Title 17 Counties,
and is the contracted service provider of certain municipal-type service via the MSD.

E. As amember entity of the MSD, who receives services from Salt Lake County via the Master
Interlocal Agreement between the MSD, the Metro Townships and Salt Lake County (the
“A greement’), periodically it is necessary to propose certain amendments to the MSD to the
Agreement to insure the services being provided are of a satisfactory quality and consistent
with the needs of the Council and the Community with the rules in the agreement for
handling such amendments being contained in Article II, section 2.4.

F. Kearns feels the offering of a letter proposing certain amendments for consideration by the
MSD greatly benefit Kearns and the MSD and improves effectiveness, efficiency, overall
quality, and better control costs of services.

RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Kearns Metro Township Council approves
the offering of a letter to the MSD seeking a modification of the Master Interlocal Agreement
between the MSD, the Metro Townships, and Salt Lake County regarding the provision of

financial services by the County to the Township allowing the MSD to provide those services
directly to the Township, and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the letter be composed by the Kearns Metro Township
Council Staff to the MSD at its Planning Meeting to be held on August 3, 2018 at 9:00 am asa
possible agenda item for their upcoming meeting on August 8, 2018.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Kearns Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah, this
30" day of July 2018.
