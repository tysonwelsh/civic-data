      13.2 ANNUAL REVIEW - Redevelopment Agency of the City of Orem (RDA) Bylaws
           The RDA Bylaws stipulate that the bylaws "shall be reviewed by the Board of
           Directors at each annual meeting" and may be amended "by majority vote of the
           Board of Directors of the Authority at a regular or special meeting." The Chief
           Executive Officer is not recommending any changes to the bylaws at this time,
           and recommends the Board of Directors adopt the bylaws as written.
