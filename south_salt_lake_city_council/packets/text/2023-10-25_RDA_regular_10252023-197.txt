                         RESOLUTION NO. RDA 2023-____________

       A RESOLUTION OF THE REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SOUTH
       SALT LAKE INITIATING THE PROCESS OF ADOPTING A
       COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT PROJECT AREA PLAN BY
       DESIGNATING A SURVEY AREA KNOWN AS THE “SOUTHWEST
       QUADRANT CRA SURVEY AREA”

        WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of South Salt Lake (the “Agency”) was created
by the South Salt Lake City Council (the “Council”) to transact the business and exercise all the
powers provided for by Title 17C of the Utah Code, “Limited Purpose Local Government
Entities—Community Reinvestment Agency Act” (the “RDA Act”); and
       WHEREAS, Utah Code 17C-1-102(58) defines “Survey area” as a “geographic area
designated for study by a survey area resolution to determine whether: (a) one or more project
areas within the survey area are feasible; or (b) a development exists within the survey area;” and
        WHEREAS, Utah Code 17C-2-101.5 authorizes the Agency to establish a survey area and
outlines the necessary requirements, including adopting a resolution that: 1) designates an area
located within the Agency’s boundaries as a survey area, 2) a statement that the survey area
requires study to determine whether: a) one or more urban renewal project areas within the survey
area are feasible, b) whether a development impediment exists within the survey area, and 3) the
resolution must contain a boundary description or map of the survey area; and
        WHEREAS, the Agency has identified an area located within its boundaries as a survey
area, a map of which is attached to this resolution as Exhibit A (the “Southwest Quadrant CRA
Survey Area” or the “Survey Area”); and
       WHEREAS, the Agency has determined that the Survey Area requires study to determine
whether one or more urban renewal project areas within the survey area are feasible and whether
a development impediment exists within the survey area; and
        WHEREAS, the Agency desires to initiate the process of adopting one or more community
reinvestment project area plans by adopting this resolution establishing the boundaries of a survey
area. The boundaries of the survey area are depicted on the map attached to this resolution as
Exhibit A (the “Southwest Quadrant CRA Survey Area”). The Southwest Quadrant CRA Survey
Area is located entirely within the Agency’s boundaries.
       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Redevelopment Agency of The City of
South Salt Lake as follows:

SECTION I: The Southwest Quadrant CRA Survey Area requires study to determine whether
project area development is feasible within one or more proposed community reinvestment project
areas and whether a development impediment exists within the Southwest Quadrant CRA Survey
Area. Agency staff, consultants and legal counsel are authorized and instructed to conduct such
study.

SECTION II: Agency staff, consultants and legal counsel are authorized to prepare a proposed
community reinvestment project area plan for each proposed community reinvestment project area
designated by them in the Southwest Quadrant CRA Survey Area, and to conduct any examination,
investigation, or negotiation regarding each proposed community reinvestment project area that
the they consider appropriate.

SECTION III: The above recitals, and all attached Exhibits, are by this reference made part of
this resolution. This resolution takes effect upon adoption.
