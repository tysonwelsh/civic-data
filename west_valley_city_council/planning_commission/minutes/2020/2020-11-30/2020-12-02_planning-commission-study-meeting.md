                 Commissioner Winters asked if this project is specific to the area’s east of Bangerter on 4100 South.
                 Steve said the project would go to Redwood Road, east of Redwood, and there are only back yards facing
                 4100 South.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if they have an existing fence that needs to be replaced in the front yard,
                 would they be required to use the rod iron fence. Steve said they would keep things uniform along that
                 street.
              Russ explained this project desires to improve the looks of the City. Financially we could not afford to
              put in new walls/fencing to cover the entire City at once, so we start with 2700 West and 4100 South.

ZT-9-2020     Petition by RANDY MOORE requesting a zone text change to define qualified 55 or older
              development and to allow properties with access and frontage on a street with a planned right-of-way
              width of at least 80 feet where a qualified 55 or older development is proposed to apply for the RM
              (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) zone. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)


Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained this application has to do with conditions to make a property eligible for RM
              zoning. Randy Moore is looking to loosen the requirements to include senior communities on an 80 ft.
              right-of-way with a density of 8 units/acre. Steve then pointed out the areas in the City that would
              become eligible to apply including along; Parkway Blvd, with the most vacant land to become eligible.
              He then pointed out the four current senior communities within the City. These developments are around
              seven units/acre. Staff recommended that the proposal be for seven units/acre instead of 8 units/acre.

              Commissioner McEwen asked what the qualifications are for a senior community. Steve gave the HUD
              definition provided by Randy Moore. A senior community must have one person aged 55 years or older
              in 80% of its units.

              Commissioner Winters asked if the proposed addition to the RM zone would be if the property needs to
              be a senior community on an 80-foot right-of-way. Steve said that was correct. They would still need to
              apply for a zone change even if the property is eligible for RM zoning.

              Commissioner Winters asked Steve if smaller communities fare well in the City. Steve explained that
              smaller developments of duplexes and fourplexes have not fared well over time. As a City, we have not
              had much luck with them as far as management and maintenance are concerned. Larger developments
              managed by one entity can spread the cost of maintenance over more units, so they tend to be more
              sustainable that way. Steve stated the smallest existing senior condo community in the City is four acres.

              Commissioner McEwen asked if there have been any complaints about younger families moving into the
              senior communities. Steve is not aware of any complaints. He feels this is because of the HUD
              designation. Steve stated there could be some federal oversight, and he can look into that.

              Commissioner Wood has a relative that lives in a senior community in West Valley City. That relative
              has neighbors that have their children and grandchildren living with them. Commissioner Wood has
              recently had a conversation with someone who wondered who controls the age requirement in a 55+
              community when the original owner sells or dies. So, she is curious if this can become a problem down
              the line.

              Brandon Hill explained the City usually doesn’t receive complaints like that where it is not governed by
              an ordinance or City regulation. He explained issues with younger people living in a senior community is
              privately enforced.

              Commissioner Fuller is concerned about a smaller community being able to enforce rules. He feels that
              the proposal should be for properties larger than two acres. Steve explained we could suggest the
              addition of properties being over 2 acres.
                 Steve reiterated this proposal is to allow them to apply for the zone change. The City would still have the
                 opportunity to approve or deny the request.



ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
Z-9-2020    Petition by ALLISON WITTE and BRETT GARRETT requesting a zone change from A
            (agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to M (manufacturing). The property is located at 2454 S 5600 W
            on 20.2 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik explained this proposal would rezone the subject property to the manufacturing zone. The
                 parcel to the east is also part of this development proposal, but it is already zoned manufacturing. The
                 request is to build a large distribution warehouse. Steve pointed out that the street to the south of the
                 subject property is; 2540 South. He explained that the City had secured funding to extend 2540 South to
                 the east and put in a new road extending north from 2540 South. So, the truck traffic will now have
                 access to 5600 West along 2540 South to go up to 2400 South, which then 2400 South connects to 5600
                 West. Steve said the staff recommends a development agreement where the developer would dedicate the
                 right-of-way to the City that will be used to develop. The developer would also reimburse the City for the
                 utilities that go in the street. This development agreement will make the road construction a smooth
                 transition.

                Commissioner Winters asked Steve to clarify the property location. Steve said until recently, this property
                was virtually landlocked in terms of a public street. Steve said that is why the County uses the address
                2454 South 5600 West. Once the new roads are put in and the buildings are under construction, the
                address will be off the new streets.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-14-2020   Petition by ROCKWORTH COMPANIES requesting conditional use approval for an apartment
            development. The property is located at 3036 and 3078 South Decker Lake Dr on 2.79 acres and is
            zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361)

Discussion:      Brock Anderson presented this application for an apartment development. He explained they have
                 exactly the number of parking stalls required by code, so they can't afford to lose any spots.
                 Commissioner McEwen recalled a discussion about the addition of parking spots on the land owned by
                 Rocky Mountain Power. Steve Pastorik stated the applicant could not agree with Rocky Mountain
                 Power, so they found a way to have all the parking stalls on their property. Brock explained there would
                 need to be an addition of a pedestrian walkway from the adjacent hotel property to the sidewalk on
                 Decker Lake Drive. Their current plan will replace the dirt with gravel, and the walkway will lead to a
                 parking stall on the subject property. Brock stated the applicant is revising the walkway plan.

                  Brock explained the development agreement has a list of materials that can be used; however, concrete
                 is not on that list. The plans show the parking garage with a concrete exterior. Brock told the
                 Commission he wanted to give them this information to see how they feel about using the concrete. He
                 stated color could be added to provide more variation. Brock pointed out one more issue that will need to
                 be addressed. The plans that he received this morning depicted exterior stairways that are not allowed by
                 code. The applicant will need to address this issue.

                 Commissioner Woodruff said he is okay with the concrete if treated somehow, such as a score pattern.
                 He would not want to see a rough exterior where the forms have been removed. Commissioner Winters
                 asked Brock if the parking garage can only be seen from the north and west-facing elevations. Brock
                 said that is correct and that it will be quite visible from the streets.

                 Commissioner Lovato said there is a way to plaster the concrete, so it looks better. He then asked if the
                 rest of the structure is a wood frame. Steve Pastorik said the parking garage is concrete, and the units on
                 the first two levels that wrap around the garage are wood-framed and everything above the parking
                 structure.

                 Commissioner Lovato stated seeing these wood-frame structures popping up all over Salt Lake City and
                 wondered if any had information on that frame's longevity. Steve Pastorik said the frame does meet
                 building code; however, he isn't sure about the longevity.

                 Commissioner Fuller stated when they enclose the exterior stairways, they could lose parking spots,
                 which could cause another issue. Brock agreed with Commissioner Fuller and said if that is the case, the
                 applicant will need to either lose units or find another way to add any lost parking stalls.

CA-4-2020        Petition by WASATCH FLEET SERVICE requesting conditional use amendment approval for an
                 expansion of an office/warehouse and van equipment installation facility. The property is located at
                 2290 S. 5600 W. on .37 acres and is zoned LI (Light Industrial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-
                 3497)

Discussion:      Jody Knapp explained, the applicant acquired property to the south, and the original plan was only to
                 have access to the subject property off 5650 West. There is a delay agreement in place for those street
                 improvements. With the expansion, the subject property can access 5600 South, which would solve fire
                 access issues. However, UDOT would most likely require a median and turn/decel lane. The applicant is
                 working with the fire department for what they need right now to open.

                 Commissioner Winters asked Jody if there is a street to the south that will be going in. Jody said the 5600
                 West access is to the east, and there will not be access to the south. Commissioner Winters asked why
                 the access off 5600 West needs to be a right in/out. Jody explained the access would be close to 201, and
                 this is a large traffic area. So, they don’t want people trying to make a left turn crossing many lanes of
                 traffic. Commissioner Winters pointed out the only alternative is making a U-turn, and he doesn’t feel
                 that is much safer. Jody stated the median length is still unknown, and they are still waiting for an
                 answer from UDOT.
