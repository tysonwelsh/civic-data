    1. FEE FOR FINGERPRINTING SERVICES IN THE POLICE STATION
           a. Seth Atkinson introduced the need for a finger printing service fee. The Police
              Department recently purchased a fingerprinting machine using capital savings.
           b. The Sheriff’s Department has a fingerprinting machine, but it is only available on certain
              days. Providing this service at the Police Department would allow the service to be
              available more frequently.
           c. The Police Department examined costs to administer the service and pay a portion of
              the equipment costs. The police department is recommending a flat fee of $25.00.
           d. The council felt the proposed fee was an adequate and reasonable amount to offer for
              the service.

BACK NINE GOLF COURSE EXPANSION CONTRACT DOCUMENTS
       City Administrator, Seth Atkinson, presented the contract documents for the back nine golf
       course expansion project. He explained the inclusion of a clause for reasonable extensions to the
       contract schedule and absence of liquidated damages. The council discussed using Sunrise
       Engineering for the project due to their familiarity with the water system. A discussion on the
       importance of proper stormwater management and the potential for a stormwater retention
       facility followed. Water usage was also brought up and a discussion ensued. Mike Booth
       explained that the new irrigation system’s design would minimize water usage during water
       restrictions. The possibility of using an irrigation-only well as an alternative water source for
       irrigation was mentioned. Also considered was finding a pipe for Monument Springs.
       Following the discussion, Councilor Tate Douglas made a motion to authorize the Mayor to sign
       the contract documents for the Back Nine Golf Course Expansion Project with Wagner Golf
       Works, LLC. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

REIMBURSENT AGREEMENT FOR THE 500 WEST SEWER IMPROVEMENTS
      Seth Atkinson detailed developments that have occurred along 500 West between 1500 North
      and 1250 North. These developments required a sewer line to be installed along 500 West. The
       developers requested a reimbursement agreement be set up to recoup a portion of those costs
       from other benefiting properties. Properties within the benefiting area are not required to pay
       anything if development does not occur in the 10 years associated with the agreement or
       afterward. New developments would be required to pay their share of the sewer improvements.
       Councilor JD Parady moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the reimbursement agreement with
       Patrick Painter, Russel Smith, and Korey Wright. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion.
       The motion passed on the following vote:
                Yes: Councilors Shari Cowan, Tate Douglas, Jeramie Callaway, JD Parady
                *Councilor Worwood recused himself from the vote due to a conflict of interest.

Work Session: 8:17 pm
   1. Annexations, land acquisition, and future planning
           a. Travis Worwood discussed recent conversations with Brent Boswell about companies
               interested in the area near Bar W. However, because that area is not annexed and shovel
               ready, they went elsewhere. The council discussed the zoning for the area if it annexed,
               should be zoned for clean food-grade operations to attract suitable businesses. Set
               Atkinson described an idea of Agi Tourism where farms have retail components for
               tourists to visit and purchase products directly.
           b. Mayor Seely inquired about the city’s current financial situation regarding land
               acquisition for industrial purposes. Mr. Atkinson explained that the city has cash
               available and is looking to invest in land for future development potential. Potential
               properties around the airport road and the 219 exit were mentioned. It was suggested
               to use the city’s cash to buy land at a low price and sell it later for a profit.
           c. Questions regarding the city’s ability to negotiate land purchases as part of annexation
               agreements with property owners was confirmed. It was suggested that the city’s cash
               be used for industrial purposes. The council wants to be proactive in land acquisition to
               support future development and economic growth.
