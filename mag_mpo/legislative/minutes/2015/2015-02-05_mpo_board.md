Draft TransPlan40 Financially Constrained Projects - Shawn Eliot, Chad Eccles, Jim Price The long regional transportation plan is updated every four years and is time we are calling it TransPlan40. The goal of the plan is to preserve future transportation corridors, understand traffic needs and emerging growth patterns, and develop funding strategies. All projects are fiscally constrained into need base phases, 2015-2024 Phase 1, 2025-2034 Phase 2, and 2035-2040 Phase 3. Utah County population is projected to have over million people by 2040. Staff has worked closely with city and county staffs to help develop the traffic and land use model that predicts where the future growth, employment and households will be. Phase 1, has 40 projects that are needed, 36 of the needed projects are funded, 2 needed projects are partially funded and 2 needed projects are unfunded. Funding costs may be adjusted and revised in the coming months. Highways: Project 9: Mountainview Freeway/SR-85: cost $295.9 m allotted $45 m for frontage roads Project 13: SR-73 Freeway/Eagle Mountain to Saratoga Springs: coat $351.7 m allotted $54.5 m for frontage roads Project 3: Lehi 2100 North Freeway/SR-85: cost is $105 m and is unfunded Project 16: I-15/800 South Interchange: cost $48.8 m and is unfunded Transit: Most of the transit projects that are in the current plan are now in the new plan, but many of those projects are now unfunded and are moved to vision or illustrative classification. This is because of the financial obligations of UTA’s state of good repair (SOGR) cost estimates. The draft 2015-2040 plan assumes approximately $150 million, unfunded transit balance total is $1.49 billion. The BRT is still funded in Phase 1 for $150 million. The travel model is used to determine ridership: Model warrants Light Rail Draper to Spanish Fork in Phase 2 Phase 2 Commuter Rail Provo to Payson BRT to Eagle Mountain has strong ridership Commuter Rail to Santaquin low ridership and it is now a vision project Staff is just finishing the North County Transit Study and will start and South County Transit Study later this year. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning February 5, 2015 Page 4 Bike/Ped: The bike/ped projects are in Phase 1 and are priority over the next 10 years. They consist of projects that will be built with Phase 1 road projects, stand-alone projects, and projects from cities core bike networks. The South County Bike/Ped Study will start this spring. Mayor Chris Pengra moved for the MPO Regional Planning Committee, based off the MPO Technical Advisory Committee recommendation to approve the draft listing of highways, transit, and bike/ped/trails. This approval allows the MPO staff to present the fiscally constrained based listing of the projects to the public knowing that this is a draft and further refinements may occur.
