ORDINANCE AMENDING THE PROHIBITED USE OF IGNITION
SOURCES/FIREWORKS MAP:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending the prohibited use of ignition
     sources/fireworks map.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted each year the City is required to identify areas of
      the City which are a fire hazard, identify those areas on a map and have the Council
      approve the area for restricting fireworks and other ignition sources from being used
      in those areas. Additionally, the map details parks approved for personal firework
      use. Staff recommends approval.

      Mayor Randall noted that the hard copy of the map looks as though most of the City
      is restricted but zooming in on the interactive map makes it much clear to see that
      there are places where fireworks are okay to be set off.

      After being asked by Councilmember McArthur, City Manager explained that David
      Cordero will do a social media campaign to outline where fireworks can be set off in
      City parks.

      Fire Chief Robert Stoker added staff is concerned with the clean up afterwards; he
      prefers for the fireworks to be lit off on paved services. The State and BLM have also
      issued restrictions as well. He asked that everyone be respectful of the map; if
      someone is in a restricted area, there is a reason why these areas are restricted.
      The Fire Department’s website provides safety tips for lighting off fireworks.

      Mayor Randall mentioned the City’s fireworks show will take place on July 3rd at Dixie
      State University.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Larkin to approve the
                    ordinance amending the prohibited use of ignition sources/fireworks
                    map.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Smethurst.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a roll call vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.

GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the general plan from FP (Flood
     Plain), MDR (Medium Density Residential) and COM (Commercial) to PD-C
     (Planned Development Commercial) on approximately 12.00 acres located
     at 2800 South River Road known as Horseman Center. Case No. 2021-GPA-
     007

      Planner Mike Hadley presented the request to the general plan from FP (Flood Plain),
      MDR (Medium Density Residential) and COM (Commercial) to PD-C (Planned
      Development Commercial) on approximately 12.00 acres located at 2800 South
      River Road known as Horseman Center. He provided a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Subject property; Existing general plan map; Zoning
      map; Floodway and 100-year floodplain. No vertical structures will be built in the
      floodway area. The applicant would be required to meet any FEMA guidelines and
      approvals. The Planning Commission recommended approval.

      Assistant Public Works Director Wes Jenkins explained the applicant has submitted a
      Conditional Letter of Map Revision (CLOMR). The applicant would have to have road
