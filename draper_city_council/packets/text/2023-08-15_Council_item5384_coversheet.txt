Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: August 15, 2023
Re: Resolution 23-45 Approving and Extending Proclamation of Local Emergency 23-02 Comments: On August 3, 2023 a localized storm cell dumped approximately 2" of rain on areas of Draper in under two hours.  The volume of rain in such a short period of time damaged city streets and infrastructure, including the storm water system.  Mayor Walker declared a state of emergency in order to marshal resources from the city, Salt Lake County, the state and federal governments.
Utah Code 52-2a-208(6) requires city council approval to extend the state of emergency beyond thirty days.  City departments are still evaluating the extent of damages and formulating a plan on how to repair damage, including assessing the financial, work force and materials required to complete repairs.
