3) West Bench Vision Survey Discussion 
 
Mr. Young provided an update.  Process to start with a vision , then public input, then create in a 
draft format.  Staff has  been working with the vision statements. Vision represents the property in 
what it will look like in the future. 
 
Mr. Young provided a document and input Commissioner Cohen’s comments, this will be in a survey 
format. Commissioner O’Meara said maybe the public would benefit from a map within their area. 
Mr. Young suggested showing the study area and its relationship between the valley and the cities. 
 
Commissioners and staff discussed an overall general statement for the west bench general plan 
draft vision, draft 2007 west bench plan, future generations, human/public resources, and districts. 
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Land Use Vision statement, mixed use, and co-location. 
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Housing Vision statement, affordable housing, higher end 
housing, partnerships, and air quality.  
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Transportation Vision statement , systems, mode equity and 
transportation nodes, forms of travel, collaboration with municipalities, new development and 
transportation system, and east/west connectivity. 
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Conservation Vision statement, water availability.  
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Recreation Vision statement , active and passive recreational 
opportunities, and trail planning and connectivity.  
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Economy Vision statement, jobs located in proximity to housing 
and transportation nodes.  
 
Commissioners and staff discussed Utilities and Public Safety  Vision statement , leap-frog 
development, minimizing stress on infrastructure systems. Changes agreed to, no new comments. 
 
Mr. Young advised that the County Council set the public hearing for August 4th to hear the proposal 
to amend County ordinance 19.05.010 to increase the planning commission from five to seven 
members.
