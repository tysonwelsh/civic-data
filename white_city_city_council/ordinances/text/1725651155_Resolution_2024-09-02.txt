                                      WHITE CITY, UTAH

                                  RESOLUTION NO. 2024-09-02

                 A RESOLUTION CONFIRMING THE APPOINTMENT OF
                THE WHITE CITY CIVIC CENTER COMMITTEE MEMBERS

WHEREAS, White City is designated as a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-201 et seq.;
and

WHEREAS, the White City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the municipality to
construct a Civic Center; and

WHEREAS, the White City Council intends to create a White City Civic Center Committee and appoint
the following individuals: Mayor Paulina Flint, Council Member Greg Shelton, Rori Andreason (City
Administrator), Chad Anderson (Engineer), Marla Howard (MSD General Manager), Daniel
Torres (MSD Economic Development Director), Morgan Julian (Long Range Planner), and Paul
Ashton (Legal Counsel) to serve until the committee is deemed no longer necessary;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Council as follows:

SECTION 1. The White City Council hereby establishes the White City Civic Center Committee and
appoints Mayor Paulina Flint, Council Member Greg Shelton, Rori Andreason (City Administrator),
Chad Anderson (Engineer), and Marla Howard (MSD General Manager) to serve until the committee
is dissolved.

SECTION 2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its passage.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 5th day of September, 2024 by the White City Council, White City,
