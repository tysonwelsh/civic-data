                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 20-14

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE RIVERTON CITY GENERAL PLAN LAND USE
          MAP, AMENDMENTS PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the amendments to the Riverton City General Plan Land Use
Map; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map as described herein.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map shall be, and hereby is,
                  amended to reflect the changes as shown in Exhibit “A”.

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day
of August 2020 by the following vote:
