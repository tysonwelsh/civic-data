County Planner
, Laura Kuhrmeyer, and
Community Development Director
, Pat Putt informed commissioners that they have selected two members to serve on the Eastern Summit Advisory Committee. They are also requesting that one commissioner be on the Snyderville Basin Advisory Committee. Mr. Putt stated that in addition to the Advisory Committee's there is a separate committee being formed for the Moderate-Income Housing group and one commissioner is needed to volunteer.  David Darcey volunteered to be on the Moderate-Income Housing group.
