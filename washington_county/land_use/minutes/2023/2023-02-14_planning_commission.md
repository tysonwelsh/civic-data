3. FINAL PLAT (PUBLIC MEETING-ADMINISTRATIVE). Review of final plat to subdivide
Parcel 3134-A-HV from one parcel to two. The parcel is approximately 27.9 acres, and located at
5028 N 100 E, Pintura, UT. It is zoned RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1-acre minimum lot size).
The applicant is Joel Gines/David Morgan, the subdivision is Snow Pine Subdivision.

Mr. Messel explained the Snow Pine subdivision is located near Pintura. The subdivision will
result in two lots being created. The lots have water and septic approval. Applicants are seeking
a waiver for Subdivision Road Improvements due to a waterline being installed by the
Washington County Water Conservancy District. The applicant will provide a letter from the
Washington County Water Conservancy District to verify what improvements will be completed
after the waterline is installed.

Staff has reviewed the Final Plat application and recommends the Planning Commission approve
the Final Plat for the Snow Pine Subdivision based on the following findings and conditions:
    1. The proposed Final Plat meets zoning requirements for RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1
         acre minimum lot size).
    2. Water is available to serve the new lot being created.
    3. Septic Feasibility has been completed on the new lot being created.
    4. The applicant will provide written documentation from the Washington County Water
         Conservancy District regarding planned road improvements of 100 East after the
         waterline is installed.
    5. The applicant will seek a waiver of road improvements from the Washington County
         Commission or will be required to complete road improvements along the subdivision
         frontage. Applicant will bond for Chip Seal with Public Works.
    6. The applicant will address nuisance complaints of junk, trailers, and equipment
         currently on the adjacent Stone Ridge parcels. Washington County Code Enforcement
         will verify the lot improvements are adequate to improve visual aesthetics. End of March

5. LOT LINE ADJUSTMENT (PUBLIC MEETING-ADMINISTRATIVE). The applicant is
proposing to move the lot line separating parcels 7130-B-3-NW and 7133-A-1-B-4-A-NW. Parcels
are zoned RE-40 (Residential Estate 40,000 sq ft minimum lot size). The adjusted lot line will
reduce the lot size of Parcel 7133-A-1-B-4-A-NW. Parcel 7130-B-3-NW will benefit by having
better road frontage and less area within the FEMA flood zone. The applicant has agreed to
creating a 26’ access and utility easement and hammerhead for emergency vehicle turn around.
The applicant is Rhonda Platten.

Mr. Messel explained the history in the Brookside area with home building. The roads are not
dedicated and many lots have floating access easements. The applicant owns 3 lots and would like
to move the lot line on the middle lot to create more buildable space. The number of lots will
remain as 3 lots. The applicant has agreed to create a 26’ access easement, which is an incremental
improvement.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the Lot Line Adjustment request for parcels 7130-B-3-NW and 7133-A-1-B-4-
A-NW and recommends the Planning Commission review, and if comfortable approve the Lot
Line Adjustment request based on the following findings and conditions:
    1. The applicant has submitted the Lot Line Adjustment Application and supporting
         documentation.
    2. The Lot Line Adjustment and new property descriptions have been documented by a
         Professional Land Surveyor.
    3. A Notice of Approval will be prepared by landowner, and signed by the Land Use
         Authority, for recording, with language acceptable to the County for compliance with
         State law.
    4. Conveyance documents shall be executed and recorded by the lot owner with the new

6. ZONE CHANGE AMENDMENT AND PROJECT PLAN (PUBLIC HEARING –
LEGISLATIVE). The applicant was granted a zone change on August 17th, 2021, by the County
Commission (Ordinance 2021-1186-O). The Ordinance placed 5.65 acres in PDR (Planned
Development Residential) and 15.73 acres in PDC (Planned Development Commercial) following
the recommendation of the Planning Commissioners and staff. The applicant appeared before the
Planning Commission again on October 11, 2022 and submitted a Concept Plan, which is part of
the procedural process for Planned Development applications. Feedback was provided to the
applicant regarding details missing from the Concept Plan and additional information required.
Because the planned development has changed over several revisions, amending the zone
designation is required to accommodate residential and commercial aspects. The newly submitted
Project Plan includes an updated site plan, phasing plan, utility plan, landscape plan, and
additional details regarding amenities, construction materials, and RV pad dimensions. Applicant
is Veyo, LLC.

Mr. Messel explained the applicant is asking to amend the Planned Development to grant 4.26
acres in PDR and 7.47 acres in PDC. The aesthetics of RV parks are a concern to the community
in Central. Several examples of RV parks were shared to show very high end parks and also the
very low end parks. Examples were shared showing outdoor living space, site amenities, and how
pads are positioned among trees. Examples were shown of different substrates varying from gravel
to pavement. Landscaping and lighting are important aspects needing to be considered for the
overall aesthetic of the development.

Mr. Messel further explained the position of the property describing how the PDR and PDC zones
only apply to specific parts of the parcel.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the project plan and zone amendment request. Staff recommends the Planning
Commission hold the required public hearing and make a recommendation of the Project Plan and zoning
amendment to the County Commission based on the following findings and conditions:

    1) The Project Plan and Zone Change were properly noticed.
    2) A Public Hearing was held regarding the zone change and Project Plan on February 14, 2023 in
        the regularly scheduled Planning Commission meeting.
    3) The Project Plan and zone change have been reviewed by Staff.
    4) The applicant has taken feedback from the Concept Plan review (October 2022) and incorporated
        those suggestions throughout the Project Plan.
    5) The project plan describes the acreage requested for PDR (Planned Development Residential) and
        PDC (Planned Development Commercial).
    6) The Project Plan includes a phasing plan with acreage for each phase of development.
    7) The applicant has received approval from the Northwestern Special Service District
    8) The applicant has received a Will Serve from Rocky Mountain Power.
    9) The applicant has completed a Traffic Impact Study.
    10) The applicant has provided water supply and septic plans.
