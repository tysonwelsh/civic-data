MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY

1. CALL TO ORDER
Vice Chair Kelvin Green called the meeting to order at 7:23 pm

2. PUBLIC HEARING
   a. Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West Jordan, Utah
      for Fiscal Year 2022
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck oriented the Council regarding the Municipal
Building Authority of the City of West Jordan noting the purpose of the authority was to own
properties that are then leased to the City. The Municipal Building Authority owns the Public
Works Building and represents the lease to the city and its enterprise funds.

Vice Chair Green opened a public hearing at 7:24 pm

Citizen Comments:
None

Vice Chair Green closed the public hearing at 7:24 pm

MOTION:       Board Member Christopher McConnehey moved to approving Resolution No.
              105 adopting the Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the
              City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2022 and set June 9, 2021 as a public hearing
              to receive public comment regarding the final budget.
              Board Member Kayleen White seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
