Resolution No. 2023-10-02 Date: October 12, 2023
A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
CORRECTING A SCRIVENER’S ERROR IN SECTION 19.28.020, SCHEDULE
OF PERMITTED USES MISTAKENLY INDICATING THAT SHORT TERM
RENTALS ARE ALLOWED AS PERMITTED USES IN THE R-1-7 AND R-1-8
ZONES.
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate
zoning and subdivisions in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103 (2);
and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has the authority to pass
all ordinances and rules, and make all regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for
carrying into effect or discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-
8-84; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township adopted an ordinance repealing and replacing
Titles 18 and 19 of the Kearns Metro Township Code of Ordinances, effective June 26,
2023; and
WHEREAS, early drafts of Title 19, specifically Chapter 19.28, Single Family Residential
Zones, had contemplated the possibility of Short Term Rentals in certain residential zones
and had consequently been included in the use table in subsection 19.28.030; and
WHEREAS, the record indicates that as part of repealing and replacing Titles 18 and 19,
the Kearns Metro Township Council discussed and deliberately chose to prohibit Short
Term Rentals in the Kearns Metro Township; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to that decision, subsection 19.42.310 of the was edited to prohibit
Short Term Rentals in all parts of the Kearns Metro Township; and
WHEREAS, the use table in subsection 19.28.030 was inadvertently omitted from the list
of requested edits and indicates, contrary to subsection 19.42.030 and the legislative intent,
that Short Term Rentals are permitted uses in the R-1-7 and R-1-8 zones.
NOW, THEREFORE LET BE IT RESOLVED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL, effective immediately, that the use table in subsection 19.28.030 be corrected
to reflect the legislative intent that Short Term Rental be prohibited throughout the Kearns

Metro Township. The use table will reflect this intent by marking each zone with an X for
prohibited for the Short Term Rental use.
PASSED AND ADOPTED this 9th day of October 2023.
