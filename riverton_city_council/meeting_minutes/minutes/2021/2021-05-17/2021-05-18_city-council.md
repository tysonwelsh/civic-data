likes the idea of the parade and is in favor of this parade.

Discussion Items

         Budget Discussion - Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director

Kevin Hicks said that the surge tank was budgeted in the current year budget under line item
53-71-715 for $165K. He said the bids for the surge tank had come in but it will not be
completed before the end of June because they are not able to get all of the supplies in time.
He said that he would like to have the surge tank on line item 53-71-710 in the new budget year
and asked the Councilmembers to discuss. All Councilmembers were in agreeance to move the
surge tank to the new budget.

Councilmember Wells asked why the community outreach budget was cut back. Mr. Hicks said
that line item was for Stormwater. He said they moved it to the Communication department
since it will be consolidated with the Communications. Councilmember Wells asked about
funding for educating the public on Ranked Choice Voting. Mr. Hicks said that this will be
included in the Elections line item in the Recorder’s budget. Councilmember Wells asked why
the mowing budget was cut. Mr. Hicks said that historically this line item was much too high and
so they cut it to be more realistic.

Councilmember Buroker asked for clarification on line item 10-60-312. Mr. Hicks said that line
item was for metering Christmas lights. Trace Robinson said that the Christmas lights will be
metered because Rocky Mountain Power is requiring metering. Councilmember Stewart asked
for Christmas lights to be put up on 13400 South.

Councilmember Buroker said she agreed with the Mayor’s budget for line item 10-65-271 in

Riverton Police Department.
