EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATION
PROHIBITING ANY NEW LAND USE DEVELOPMENT APPLICATIONS EXCEPT
APPLICATIONS FOR SINGLE FAMILY BUILDING PERMITS ON EXISTING BUILDING
LOTS.

WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council has reviewed its existing
general plan and zoning, subdivision, and land use ordinances and regulations and has realized that
they are either out of date and in need of amendment; and

WHEREAS, the Council has decided to adopt a new general plan and the maps, plans and
ordinances that regulate the development and subdividing of land in Emigration Canyon Metro
Township; and

WHEREAS, the Council does hereby determine that it would be against the public interest to
allow persons to apply for permits to develop or subdivide ground, with the exception of permits for
single family homes on existing lots, while the Council is considering new regulations, because to do
so would allow people to purposely avoid any new regulations by trying to apply before the
Township finishes updating its maps, plans and ordinances.

WHEREAS, the Town has authority under Utah Code 10-9a-504 to establish a temporary
land use regulation if the Town Council finds that an area of Town may be unregulated or unzoned;
and

WHEREAS, The Township Council finds that it is in the public’s interest to not accept any
applications or approve any new land use development until the Township’s general plan ordinances
and codes have been updated and appropriately passed but that it would be unfair to prohibit the
building of single family homes on existing lots during the time that the codes and regulations are
being reviewed and updated.

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Township Council of the Emigration Canyon
Metro Township, Utah, that:

1. Pursuant to Utah Code 10-9a-504 the erection, construction, reconstruction, or alteration of any
building or structure or any subdivision approval, except for single family homes to be built on
existing lots, is hereby prohibited within all areas of Emigration Canyon Metro Township. This
regulation shall take effect immediately upon the signing of this ordinance and shall expire of its own
accord six months from the date of signing (the day of 2019).

2. This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon signing by the mayor.

3. Immediately following this ordinance, the recorder shall make or cause to be made a
certificate stating the date of passage and of the date of publication or posting, as required.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Salt Lake
County, Utah, this 247 day of G obey, 2019.
