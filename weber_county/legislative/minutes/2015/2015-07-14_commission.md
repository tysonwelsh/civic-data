                                        Tuesday, July 14, 2015 - 2:00 p.m.

In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-7(1)(d), the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all citizens
who appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or
purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

F. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. CONTRACT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH, ATTORNEY GENERAL’S OFFICE, FOR SERVICES FOR THE
       CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER (CJC) PROGRAM – CONTRACT C2015-292

         Rod Layton, with CJC, presented this annual contract stating that the only change is that the State is
         moving to a 5-year contract. The amount for this year is $218,949, and it will change each year.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-292 with the State of Utah, Attorney General’s
         Office, for services for the Children’s Justice Center Program; Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. GRANT EASEMENTS TO THE FOLLOWING PROPERTY OWNERS WHOSE PRIVATE IRRIGATION PIPE IS
       NOW IN THE COUNTY RIGHT-OF-WAY DUE TO THE WIDENING OF THE CORRIDOR ON 3500 WEST:
        Roy Michael Atwater and Julie Atwater
        John Alton Turnbow and Linda M. Turnbow

         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that the existing private irrigation line will be within the
         county’s right-of-way. The easement spans both of these properties.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the grant easements to Roy Michael Atwater & Julie Atwater and
         to John Alton Turnbow & Linda M. Turnbow, whose private irrigation pipe is now in the Weber County
         right-of-way due to the widening of the 3500 W. corridor; Chair Gibson seconded
         Commissioner Ebert; Chair Gibson – aye
