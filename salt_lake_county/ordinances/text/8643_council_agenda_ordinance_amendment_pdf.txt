COUNTY COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING AMENDMENTS 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE LEGISLATIVE BODY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY, 
UTAH, AMENDING SECTION  2.04.080 OF THE S ALT LAKE COUNTY 
CODE OF ORDINANC ES, 2001, ENTITLED “ REGULAR MEETING S” TO 
ESTABLISH AND BETTER CLARIFY CERTAIN PRACTICES A ND 
CUSTOMS RELATED TO THE ORDER OF BUSINESS FOR THE COUNTY 
COUNCIL’S REGULAR MEETINGS. 
 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows: 
 SECTION I. The amendments made herein are designated by underlining the new 
substituted words. Words being deleted are designated by brackets and strike-through. 
SECTION II. Section 2.04.080, of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001 is 
hereby amended to read as follows: 
2.04.080 – Regular Meetings.  
A. Regular meetings shall be held at least weekly on Tuesdays in Salt Lake City at the 
C[c]ounty G[g]overnment C[c]enter[;], [provided, regular meetings may be] unless 
cancelled [as provided in ordinance or by the action] by majority vote of the council [at a 
previous meeting] as provided by the Plan.  
B. If approved by council vote, the council may hold occasional meetings outside the county 
seat as the public business requires.  
C. Public notice of each meeting shall be given in accordance with [ state law] the Utah Open 
and Public Meetings Act.  
D. Unless determined otherwise by the chair or  by a majority vote of council members present, 
the order of business for regular meetings shall be as follows:  
 2 
1. Call to order;  
2. Invocation, reading or thought, and pledge of allegiance;  
3. [Citizen public input ] Public comment;  
4. Report of [county mayor ] elected officials:  
  a. county council members; 
  b. county m ayor; 
  c. other elected county officials; 
5. [Council members’ reports] Proclamations , memorials, and other ceremonial or 
commemorative matters;  
6. [ Elected officials’ reports] Public hearings and issuance of public notices;  
7. Pending legislative  business, including [resolutions and ordinances, contracts, tax 
matters, public notices and hearings, and such other matters and business as may be 
necessary] consideration of ordinances, resolutions, policies, procedures, rules, and 
regulations, and all other proposals for adoption that are necessary and proper;  
8. [ Walk-on matters and other business] Ratification of legislative business and proposals 
for adoption previously approved during the council work session meeting;  
9. [Adjourn] Approval of tax letters[.];  
10.  Acceptance of ethics disclosures; 
11. Approval of council meeting minutes;  
12. Other business;  
 3 
13. Adjournment. 
SECTION III. This ordinance shall become effective fifteen (15) days after its passage and 
upon at least one publication in a newspaper published and having general circulation in Salt 
Lake County.
