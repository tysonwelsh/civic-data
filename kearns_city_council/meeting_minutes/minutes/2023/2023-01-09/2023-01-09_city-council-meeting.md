       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING
       THE KEARNS ACTIVE TRANSPORTATION PLAN

                                          RECITALS

        WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has the authority to pass all
ordinances and rules, and make all regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into
effect or discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-84; and

         WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council adopted the first General Plan since
Kearns’ incorporation in 2020 and that Plan set a goal to, “increase walkability, including
pedestrian and bicycle connectivity, accessibility, safety, and comfort for people of all ages and

       WHEREAS, well-planned active transportation facilities are found to improve safety for

potential budgeting prioritization, in order to facilitate a safe, efficient, and sustainable
transportation network.

        NOW, THERFORE, BE IT RESOVLED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL,
effective immediately, that the attached Kearns’ Active Transportation Plan adopted through
resolution on January 9th, 2022, is approved in its entirety.

       PASSED and ADOPTED this 9th day of January, 2023.

Moderate Income Housing

             The Council reviewed the following Resolution correcting a technical error in the
Kearns’ Moderate Income Housing Plan:

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated for the record
that he disagreed with the State Legislature’s legal interpretation of the statute and did not think
a resolution was necessary. Nevertheless, he felt the easiest thing was to move forward with the
technical adjustment.

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-01-02                                                  Date: January 09, 2023

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       CORRECTING A TECHNICAL ERROR IN KEARNS’ MODERATE INCOME
       HOUSING PLAN

                                            RECITALS

        WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has the authority to pass all
ordinance and rules, and make all regulations, no repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into
effect or discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-84; and

        WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council timely approved amendments to its
Moderate Income Housing Plan (“MIH Plan”) prior to October 1, 2022, in compliance with House
Bill 462 (2022); and

        WHEREAS, on November 22, 2022, the Utah Division of Housing and Community
Development (“Division”) notified Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) that its MIH Plan was
compliant with House Bill 462, but that the Municipality did not qualify for priority funding
consideration from the State for TIF, TIFF, and COVID Local Assistance funds, because the MIH
Plan cited and summarized rather than quoting one of the statutory strategy options; and




                                                 3
DATE           MONDAY                         JANUARY                                   09, 2023

        WHEREAS, Kearns desires to qualify for priority funding consideration from the State, and
the Division has indicated that Kearns can remedy the identified deficiency by the Kearns Council
adopting a Resolution that corrects the technical error in the MIH Plan by including the relevant
statutory provision in its entirety in the MIH Plan.

                                         RESOLUTION

         NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL,
effective immediately, that Kearns’ Moderate Income Housing Plan shall be corrected to include
the following statutory language in its entirety:

       “Apply for or partner with an entity that applies for state or federal funds or tax
       incentives to promote the construction of moderate income housing, an entity that
       applies for programs offered by the Utah Housing Corporation within that agency’s
       funding capacity, an entity that applies for affordable housing programs
       administered by the Department of Workforce Services, an entity that applies for
       affordable housing programs administered by an association of governments
       established by an interlocal agreement under Title 11, Chapter 13, Interlocal
       Cooperation Act, an entity that applies for services provided by a public housing
       authority to preserve and create moderate income housing, or any other entity that
       applies for programs or services that promote the construction or preservation of
       moderate income housing” (Utah Code 10-9a-403).

       PASSED and ADOPTED this 9th day of January, 2023.

Kearns Improvement District (KID)

                Greg Anderson, General Manager, KID, stated in 2022, KID experienced a
reduction in water use of 20.8 percent in 2020 and 8 percent from 2021. KID is the leading district
in Utah as far as conservation efforts. This is significant because Jordanelle and Deer Creek
Reservoirs are currently at 54 percent capacity. Last year they were at 63 percent. Jordan Valley
Water Conservancy District and the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District have been asked
by the State Legislature to release some of their reserved water. Although the current water year
has started off well, these conservation efforts will continue. The snowpack is currently at a 179
percent. Numerous bills pertaining to water conservation are in the works at the State Legislature.
KID does not support all of them but is working through Chamber West to provide its input.
Through the Federal Omnibus Bill that was passed in December of 2022, KID received a $1.76
million grant for its water replacement project on 5400 South.
