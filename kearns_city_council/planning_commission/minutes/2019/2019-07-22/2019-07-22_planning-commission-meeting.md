30983 - Rezone 8.19 acres of vacant land at 5622 South 5600 West from R-M to C-2. Property owner:
Wright Development Group. Planner: Curtis Woodward

Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Zoning Administrator Curtis Woodward provided
an analysis of the staff report and zone change.

Commissioner Robertson said it doesn’t force them to build commercial, but it opens the door for them.
Mr. Woodward said it allows conditional zoning. Commissioner Walton asked what the two spots in West
Valley are zoned. Mr. Woodward said the time it was annexed, it was C-2, but isn’t certain now.

Speaker # 1: Kearns Community Council
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
Comments: Mr. Snow said the Kearns Community Council gives favorable recommendation.

Speaker # 2: Citizen
Name: Nolan Stan
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Stan said he resides across from the north boundary. Busy street and anything put in there
to increase the traffic doesn’t think it’s a good thing. Small children living around there and people drive
like they don’t care. Anything that shuts off the traffic and not significantly increase traffic.

Speaker # 3: Citizen
Name: Evelyn Stan
Address: Not provided
Comments: Ms. Stan said there is crime in the area, if you put commercial stores and robberies could duck
there, crime rate would go up that they don’t need.

Speaker # 4: Citizen
Name: Holly Leany
Address: 5698 Suncrest View Place
Comments: Ms. Leany said mostly apartments and commercial, rather than single family dwellings.

Commissioner Robertson said if they did change, they could come and say we want to build all apartments
or all commercial, or both. Under R-M they could only build multi-family. Mr. Woodward said the R-M
allows apartments, office buildings, banks, and non-retail. Technically in R-M, subdivide and build single-
family, knowing what was paid you couldn’t convince them to do that. Access street of Chateau Way and
need discussion regarding road policies. Commissioner Robertson asked whether apartments and what’s
the impact to the township for retail component if they build or don’t for tax base. Mr. Woodward said the
townships have not enacted a property tax to pay for municipal services, now all services are all funded
from sales tax revenue. More sales tax, the better.

Speaker # 5: Citizen
Name: Cheryle Hatch
Address: 5445 South Rockford Street
Comments: Ms. Hatch said she lives in the community, thinks it would be a good idea for retail right there,
bringing planning to the planning commission and not concerned about traffic, traffic would get most of
the traffic and could handle that and have zoning conditions for retail and is for the application.

Commissioner Walton motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Hatch seconded that motion.
