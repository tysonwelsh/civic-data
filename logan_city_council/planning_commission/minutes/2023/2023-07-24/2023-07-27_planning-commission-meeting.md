PC 23-049 Finlinson STR [Conditional Use Permit] Kyle & Ariel Finlinson are requesting a
Conditional Use Permit to operate a 2-bedroom short-term rental in the basement of a home located
at 1685 East 1600 North in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 07-068-0028
(Hillcrest Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Senior Planner reviewed the request for a 2-bedroom short-term rental in the
basement of a home located at 1685 East 1600 North in the Hillcrest neighborhood. The
applicant/owners will continue to use the main floor as their primary residence. R. Holley reviewed
the proposed site plan and floor plan for the short-term rental and reviewed the two additional
conditions requiring a walkway from the proposed parking for the short-term rental to the entrance
on the west side of the home and a fence along the west property line.

PROPONENT: Kyle Finlinson said that there is an existing chain link fence along the west side of
the property and when he spoke to his neighbors to the west, their privacy concern was not due to
the lack of a fence, it is because the proponents home is higher than theirs and any short term rental
guests would be able to see into their backyard. Mr. Finlinson said that the guests will not be using
the backyard aside from parking. He also spoke about guests using the City sidewalk as a pathway
from the proposed parking area on the north side of the home to walk around to the front of the home
where there are existing stepping stones from the main front door of the home to the side basement
short-term rental entrance. Mr. Finlinson said they would also be willing to meet the guests at the
back door or garage door to let them into the short-term rental space.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.


COMMISSION: Commissioner Doutre said condition number six which requires a pathway needs to
stay because, without a clear path to the short-term rental entrance from the parking area, people
will park on the street. Commissioner Heare said that condition number seven needs to be amended
to require a fence along the west property line to the front plane of the house and asked the
proponent how he would like to address the issue. Mr. Finlinson said that his neighbors to the west
do not want the fence to extend to the south because that would be harder for them to remove the
snow. The neighbors were more concerned with tenants using their garbage cans that are right next
to the short-term rental entrance and putting garbage in the recycling can. Mr. Finlinson said that he
can include in the rental agreement what garbage cans are to be used.

The Commission discussed amending condition number seven to say that the western boundary
shall be clearly demarcated.

PC 23-050 Centrai Milling Office Building [Design Review Permit] Jason Ball/Central Milling LLC.,
authorized agent/owner are requesting a Design Review Permit to demolish the existing office and
build a new 2-story, 5,414 square foot office building at 122 East Center Street in the Town Center
Two (TC-2) zone; TIN 06-069-0009, -0010, -0024, - (Wilson Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Senior Planner reviewed the request for a new 2-story office building for
Central Milling located at 122 East Center established in 1867 and is the longest continuously
operated business in Utah. R. Holley reviewed the proposed site plan and elevations for the new
office building. The Commission will need to add a condition requiring a boundary line adjustment
to address the existing boundary line that runs through the proposed office building. The Land
Development Code requires 15 parking stalls for this site for office use, as proposed the project is at
9 parking stalls with the potential to add 3 more for a total of 12 parking stalls (a 25% reduction).
The existing adjacent streets lack sidewalk and pedestrian infrastructure, so the Commission will
need to determine what streetscape improvements would allow for better pedestrian access from
Pioneer Avenue.

Commissioner Guth asked what staff would propose for pedestrian connectivity. R. Holley said at
the least they should add new paint markings or a new section of sidewalk that pedestrians can
follow.

The commission discussed where curb could and should be required along Pioneer Avenue that
would still allow trucks to access the loading docks.

PROPONENT: Robert Hunsaker, Plant Manager at Central Milling said that the trucks use almost
all of the cement pad surface. They are fine with delineating a sidewalk with paint or cutting the


sidewalk, they would not like to put in a high back curb anywhere along Pioneer Avenue so the trucks
can maneuver in and out of the site. They can fit two to three angled parking stalls along the east
side of the office building bringing their parking stall count to 11 or 12.

Commissioner Guth asked if there would be more employees or additional visitors with the larger
office building. Mr. Hunsaker said no additional employees are being added; however, they are
adding a larger space for quality control, testing, etc., and employees that are currently working in
the existing Mill will move into that new space in the new office.

PUBLIC: Randy Penrod, who lives on Water Street spoke about his concern with some of the trucks
that block Pioneer Street. He would also like to see a sidewalk added along Water Street and asked
if the dumpsters could be enclosed to alleviate some of the noise from employees dumping in the
early morning hours.

COMMISSION: Russ Holley said that the proposed new office building is not affecting or changing
the use. Nothing is being changed along Water Street and there is not any room to add a sidewalk
along that side of the property.

The commission discussed removing condition number three regarding the fenestration on the
ground floor and replacing it with a condition requiring a boundary line adjustment, amending
condition number four to require 12 parking stalls, amending condition number 10 to require
streetscape improvements along Pioneer Street with a visually contrasting five-foot-wide pedestrian
walkway along the property frontage that also connects to the office building, striking engineering
condition number seventeen, and amending condition number eighteen to add “thick” to the wording.

MOTION: Commissioner Doutre made a motion to Conditionally Approve a Design Review Permit
