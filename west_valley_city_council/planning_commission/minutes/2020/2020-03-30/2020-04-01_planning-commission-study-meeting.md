ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
Z-2-2020 Petition by DANNY GUTIERREZ requesting a zone change from A (agriculture, minimum lot
         size ½ acre) to RE (Residential Estates). The property is located at 5670 West Cilma Dr. on
         7.98 acres. (General Commercial). (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

           Discussion:

           Steve Pastorik stated if the lot is rezoned RE, the code has the minimum lot size at 12,000 sqft
           as long as the average lot size is 15,000 sqft. Steve explained that with the proposed layout, the
           homes adjacent to Cilma Drive would be double frontage lots. He explained the road along
           Cilma Dr. looks to be a dead-end; however, this road will be finished and connect 5600 W to
           6000 W. Steve explained there is a slope down to the lot as you move north. He explained the
           home on the southeast corner of the lot will be demolished.
           Steve noted the application will need to come back to the Planning Commission for
           subdivision approval.
           Commissioner Fuller asked who would be responsible for the sliver of land on lot 11 extending
           to 5600 S. Steve explained the sliver of land is part of the lot in question and he will discuss
           the ownership with the applicant, to see if the land will belong to him or the adjoining
           landowner. Commissioner Fuller asked if there are three double fronting lots. Steve stated that
           all the homes on Clima Drive would be double fronting lots. Steve then explained the applicant
           is looking to put in vinyl fencing along the south side.

           Commissioner Woodruff asked what the triangle area on Lot 14 will be used for. Steve stated
           he will discuss the use of this land with the applicant.

           Commissioner Lovato asked what the code allows the applicant to do with the lot size being so
           large. Steve explained there would be room for a house and possibly an accessory building.

           Commissioner Lovato commented that it seems that the lot size is too big for just a home.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
C-5-2020 Petition by Diamond Plaza requesting a conditional use approval for a shopping center and
         buffet restaurant. The property is located at 2055 West 3500 South on 1.78 acres and is
         zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497)

              Discussion:

              Jody explained the applicant is looking to demolish the existing building and redevelop the
              whole site. Jody explained this lot is mostly surrounded by commercial buildings;
              however, they are residential homes adjacent to this lot. Jody explained the applicant will
              need to meet the code when they put in the fencing. Jody stated the applicant is looking to
              add a buffet to the new building and he is concerned about having enough parking for his
              customers. She explained the applicant asked for staff to look at the number of seats in the
              buffet compared to the parking spots. Jody stated she will be looking for more information
              about the building materials, she does know that they are looking to have stucco and some
              type of wood siding. Jody explained there will be a wall that goes up next to the residential
              area and the existing grass section will be part of the lot.

              Commissioner Lovato asked about the layout of the proposed building and if there will be
              some room around the building. Jody explained the proposal is for three retail buildings
              and a buffet.
              She stated the will need 116 parking spots for the buffet.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if the traffic coming and going from the parking lot would
              disturb the adjacent residential area. Jody stated that she spoke to one of the neighbors and
              they are happy for this build where currently it is just a vacant lot.
C-7-2020   Petition by Keith Klingenberg requesting a conditional use approval for outside storage in
           conjunction with auto service. The property is located at 2440 South 3400 West on 0.50
           acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497)

           Discussion:

           Jody explained this application started as an enforcement case where there were some
           violations, one being the outside storage. She explained the applicant is the property owner
           and has owned this land since 1995 and is now leasing the property. Jody explained the
           tenant and the owner have been very responsive. She explained the applicant is looking to
           install a chain-link fence to separate the outside storage. She explained the outside storage
           area will be used for vehicles that are waiting to be repaired/restored or are completed and
           are awaiting pick up and would remain on the site for more than 24 hours. Jody explained
           the landscaping has been kept up and looks good. She explained the dumpsters will need to
           be relocated and the carpet outside will be cleaned up as part of the enforcement case.

C-8-2020   Petition by MARTY JARAMILLO with Franklin Cover requesting a conditional use
           approval. The property is located at 2545 South Decker Lake Blvd on 1.04 acres and is
           zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361)

           Discussion:

           Brock explained the lot in question is zoned M along with the lots surrounding this
           property. Brock showed an aerial view that has many cars parked on the lot in question.
           Brock is not sure where the cars came from. Brock explained this lot became an
           enforcement case. Brock stated the applicant is looking to use the lot for outside storage.
           He stated there is landscaping that was added without approval from the City. Brock
           explained the applicant would like to store Commercial building equipment on the lot in
           shipping containers or cargo carriers. Brock explained the applicant might need to add a
           couple of trees to the landscaping and they will need to add slats to the chain-link fence
           that already exists. Brock stated some of the pavement is in rough shape and will need to
           be repaired and the areas where the shipping containers are kept will need to be paved.

           Commissioner Woodruff asked if there will be a limitation on the height of the materials
           being stored. Brock stated that most will be in containers, however, currently there is not a
           restriction on the height of the storage. Brock stated we could propose some additional
           landscaping if the Planning Commission feels that would help hide the storage area.
           Commissioner Fuller stated he has seen some racking storage up to 20 ft. high. Brock
           stated he will reach out to the applicant to see if he will be storing tall racking items.
           Commissioner Lovato also stated he has seen the rack storage at Boart Longyear.
           Commissioner Winters stated he was not as concerned with the height of the storage, where
           the surrounding area is zoned M.



SUBDIVISION APPLICATION
SA-1-2020     Petition by NORM DAHLE, representing Godfrey Trucking is requesting a plat
              amendment to the Godfrey Industrial Park Subdivision. The purpose for the amendment
              is to create an additional building lot. The property is located at 6219 West 2100 South
              and the property is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311)


              Discussion:

              Steve Lehman explained the property in question was approved for a subdivision in 2019
              and recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office in February 2020. Steve
              explained the applicant is now looking to amend the plat by adding 2 additional lots. One
              for a new building in the future and the other, to allow for a future expansion of the
              existing corporate offices. Steve explained the plat amendment would allow greater
              flexibility to secure financing if required.

              Commissioner Fuller asked if Lot 4 is where the original business was.
              Steve L stated that is correct and this expansion would make the existing parcel from 2.3
              acres to just under 3.5 acres.
