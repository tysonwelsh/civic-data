 1. CALL TO ORDER

       COUNCIL:    Chair Zach Jacob, Vice Chair Chad Lamb, Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom (remotely), Kelvin
                   Green, Kent Shelton, Kayleen Whitelock
       STAFF:      Council Office Director Alan Anderson, Community Engagement & Government Affairs
                   Director Tauni Barker, Mayor Dirk Burton, Public Works Director Brian Clegg, Public
                   Services Director Cory Fralick, Policy Analyst & Public Liaison Warren Hallmark,
                   Community Development Director Scott Langford, Fire Chief Derek Maxfield, Council

     join shortly.

 2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
     Kaliyah Krivanek led participants in the Pledge of Allegiance.

 3. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Jacob opened the public comment period at 6:02 pm.

     Council Member Bloom joined at 6:03 pm.

     Comments:
     Erin Barrow, West Jordan resident, said she was a mother of two, a former PTA President, a
     crossing guard, and a concerned citizen. It was her understanding that the renewable energy
     program was removed from the agenda that evening because of the Mayor’s intention to veto.
     She learned about the program from a third party and had never seen literature or articles from
     the City about the opportunity. She expressed concern that West Jordan residents were being
     left out. Ms. Barrow expressed the opinion that the City should not sit on the sidelines waiting
     for someone else to take the lead. She asked the Council to not let politics get in the way of doing
     something good, and encouraged them to educate themselves on the issue and listen to citizens.

     Kyle McKowski, resident of Taylorsville, expressed gratitude for the library and other services
     provided in the community.

     Kaliyah Krivanek, local high school student, was grateful for services provided, including
     running water.

     Alberto Jerom Miranda, West Jordan resident, generally supported initiatives to improve air
     quality, and supported goals that moved to increase availability of renewable power sources.
     However, he had concerns about the renewable energy initiative referred to earlier. Mr.
     Miranda said solar and wind had costly trade-offs, and should not be considered the only
     options. He believed the initiative did not consider all options, such as fissile or nuclear power.
     His main issue with the initiative was in the implementation.

     Council Member Jacob noted that comments could be submitted any time by phone or email.

     Chair Jacob closed public comments at 6:11 pm.


 4. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. City Council Reports
          Council Member Whitelock thanked the committee responsible for the food truck and
          market events.

           Council Member Green thanked the Mayor’s staff and everyone involved in the Western
           Stampede for a great event and really enjoyed the drone show.

           Council Member Bedore enjoyed the 4th of July and Western Stampede events. He felt the
           entertainment for the first night of the rodeo was inappropriate and not representative
           of West Jordan. Council Member Bedore was grateful staff made adjustments and noted
           the individual would not be back at the Western Stampede in the future.

           Council Member Shelton thanked staff and all involved in putting together the Western
           Stampede. He acknowledged that a lot of work, time, and love went into planning the
           event and he was grateful.

           Chair Jacob echoed other Council comments regarding the Western Stampede which was
           a great event.

           Council Member Bloom echoed the comments made about the Western Stampede and
           also appreciated the reaction and response from City staff regarding the inappropriate
           entertainment. She appreciated the text messages, phone calls, and emails that were
           evidence of how much the City was respected. Council Member Bloom thanked staff for
           an amazing event.

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson gave an update on Council Chambers, and thanked
          everyone involved in making Council Chambers functional. He said West Jordan was a
          leader in providing technology for the benefit of residents.

           Policy Analyst Warren Hallmark provided an update on the 2025 Youth Council.

       c. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Burton reported about business grand openings, a pedestrian bridge project, and
          a 1300 West street project.

       d. City Administrator’s Report
          Fire Chief Derek Maxfield reported on the recent 4th of July fireworks period, and
          answered questions from the Council. Council Member Lamb suggested the city enhance
          educational efforts regarding proper disposal of fireworks. Council Member Bloom was
          grateful for the firework restriction signage west of 5600 West. Mr. Anderson reported

          the Whole meeting in September.

 5. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Resolution No. 24-029 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule for FY 2025
          Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck explained that the proposed amendments
          to the Consolidated Fee Schedule for FY 2025 would include Development Review Fees
          for PIDs, Passport money orders, and Water Utility Rates. She answered questions from
          the Council.

           MOTION: Council Member Green moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 24-029
                   amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule for FY 2025.
                   Council Member Whitelock seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, Chad Lamb, Kent
                      Shelton, Kayleen Whitelock
           NO:
           ABSENT:
           The motion passed 7-0.
