Continued discussion and action regarding an appeal of the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission's revocation of a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) for a Recycling Facility, Class II, and Nursery/Greenhouse located at 7550 W. Lower Bowl Road, Parcel SS-70-11. Applicant/Business Owner: Kylee Mimbach. Property Owner: GT500KR LLC, Kristen Clayton as Registered Agent, represented by Kyle Reeder, Cannon Law Group; Amir Caus, Senior Planner; Helen Strachan, Deputy County Attorney (5:20 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Mimbach CUP Revocation Record Index.pdf
Attachment: Code Enforcement Master Inspection Reports-Mimbach.pdf
Attachment: Petitioners’ Handout
Attachment: Staff Handout
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, and Code Enforcement Officer Scott Buchanan continued discussing the appeal of the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission’s revocation of a Conditional Use Permit for Parcel SS-70-11. (5:21 PM)
Kyle Reeder, Cannon Law Group attorney, updated the Council on the compliance efforts. Attorney Reeder, along with owners Jared and Kristen Clayton, answered questions from the Council. (5:34 PM)
Council commented and asked questions. Planner Caus, Officer Buchanan, Engineer Brandon Brady, and the petitioners responded. (5:34 PM)
Christopher Robinson made a motion to suspend the CUP and that all work cease pursuant to the CUP and require that there be a six-month period ending on Wednesday, April 15, 2025, during which time the applicant must cure all Notices of Violation, except for #24, included in all of those Notices of Violation is the obtaining of all necessary Department of Environmental Quality permits and amendments to the CUP, including amendments to the site plan and amendments to the types of uses, if they desire, to go beyond the pure definition of green waste, including things like soil processing or taking the contents from vac trucks or anything else that's beyond the scope of the existing CUP, that there are proper amendments sought and obtained and a grading permit from Summit County that relates to the CUP and the amended site plan and that with respect to Notice of Violation #24, the failure to widen the Lower Bowl Road, that that be performed beginning May 1, 2026, and completed July 1, 2026, and that the road design be based upon a Notice of Violation #26, which is a failure to submit a traffic study that will determine how the road should be designed and that the road also be designed consistent with best practices and County engineering approved standards, and that in the event that during this suspension period, work continues to occur in violation of the stop work order that the County Code Enforcement promptly give notice of violation, copying Council, and the Council will take immediate action to alter the decisions made today, including possibly revoking ab initio the CUP. During the pendency of the stop work order, they will be allowed to perform such activities on the site as necessary to come into compliance with the CUP with reasonable consultation with the Summit County Planning Department just so they have some understanding if there are trucks moving around there, what those trucks are doing, what the purpose of that is, and that also, during the pendency of the suspension they will make the site available to County inspectors to ensure that there's compliance with this suspension, and no further violations. The work permitted during the suspension involves removing material from the site, not bringing in additional material. The site is closed to the importation of new material. Notices of Violation numbers 1 through 6 need to be cured within 60 days. (6:16 PM) Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
