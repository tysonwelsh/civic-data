HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO.2020-24

AN ORDINANCE ADDING TITLE 7 CHAPTER 8 PARK FACILITIES, TRAILS AND
OPEN SPACE SECTION 13 MIDAS CREEK PUMP TRACK RULES AND
REGULATIONS

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (“Council’’) met in regular meeting on July 8,
2020, to consider, among other things, adding Title 7 Chapter 8 Park Facilities, Trails and Open
Space Section 13 Midas Creek Pump Track Rules and Regulations; and

WHEREAS, the City has determined to add the provision of the Code dealing with the
Midas Creek Pump Track rules and regulations; and

WHEREAS, the City staff recommends the addition; and

WHEREAS, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the City to add
Title 7 Chapter 8 Park Facilities, Trails and Open Space Section 13 Midas Creek Pump Track
Rules and Regulations.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that Title 7 Chapter 8 Park
Facilities, Trails and Open Space Section 13 Midas Creek Pump Track Rules and Regulations be
added as follows:

HERRIMAN CITY CODE 7-8-13: Midas Creek Pump Track — Rules and Regulations

A. Definitions. As used in this section:
l. “Bicycle” means a wheeled vehicle:
i. propelled by human power by feet or hands acting upon pedals or cranks;
ii. with a seat or saddle designed for the use of the operator; and
iii. designed to be operated on the ground.

2. “Motorized Vehicles” means a self-propelled vehicle intended primarily for use and
operation on the highways, including a motor assisted scooter.

3. “Pump Track” means the Midas Creek Pump Track.
4, “Riders” means any user of the Pump Track.
5. “Skateboard” means any four-wheeled vehicle on which a person can be propelled by

human power.

6. “Skates” means boots or other fitted shoes that are attached to a rider and have one or
more fixed wheels,

ths “Scooter” means any two-wheeled vehicle on which the driver sits or stands and is
propelled through human power by pushing against the ground.

B. The Midas Creek Pump Track is a ride-at-your-own-risk, unsupervised, Herriman City park
facility designed for use with skateboards, skates, scooters and bicycles. Motorized vehicles,
motorized scooters, or other motorized wheeled devices are not permitted.

iC. The Midas Creek Pump Track may be closed at any time by Herriman City.

D. In addition to the General Park Rules and Regulations of this Chapter, as well as all other
applicable laws and ordinances, the following rules and regulations apply to the Pump Track:

I. All riders must wear a helmet. Other protective gear is strongly recommended.

2. Graffiti and vandalism of the pump track are prohibited and may lead to closure.

3. All trash is to be placed in the provided receptacles.

4. Animals are prohibited from the pump track facility at all times.

5. Persons are prohibited from riding on the pump track from dusk until nine o'clock (9:00)

A.M., Mountain Standard Time or Mountain Daylight Time as applicable.

6. Portable ramps, rails, platforms, or any other structures or modifications to the pump
track are not to be erected at any time.

7. The Midas Creek Pump Track ts closed for use when snow and ice are present.
E. These rules shall be posted in one or more conspicuous locations in and around the Pump Track.
F, The City’s law enforcement officers are hereby authorized and empowered to enforce the

provisions of this chapter and to issue criminal citations to any Riders found to be in violation of
this section.

G, A violation of this Section is punishable as an infraction and subject t to a $50.00 penalty for the
first offense, and a $100.00 penalty for each subsequent offense. Multiple offenses may result in the
offending individual being trespassed from the Pump Track

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Council of Herriman, Utah, this 8" day of July,
2020.

PUBLIC COMMENT POLICY AND PROCEDURE: The purpose of public comment is to allow citizens to address items on the agenda. Citizens requesting
to address the Council will be asked to complete a written comment form and present it to Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder. In general, the chair will allow
an individual two minutes to address the Council. A spokesperson, recognized as representing a group in attendance, may be allowed up to five minutes.
At the conclusion of the citizen comment time, the chair may direct staff to assist the citizen on the issue presented; direct the citizen to the proper
administrative department(s); or take no action. This policy also applies to all public hearings. Citizens may also submit written requests (outlining their
issue) for an item to be considered at a future council meeting. The chair may place the item on the agenda under citizen comments; direct staff to assist
the citizen; direct the citizen to the proper administrative departments; or take no action.
