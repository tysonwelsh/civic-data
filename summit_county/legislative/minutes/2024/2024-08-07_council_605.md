Discussion and possible action regarding a land use appeal-file #24-055, regarding 875 Westwood Road, Parcel SL-B-117; Gregory Dell, Appellant; Lynda Viti; Ray Milliner (5:13 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Dell Appeal.pdf
Canice Harte recused himself as required by statute. (5:13 PM)
Lynda Viti, Deputy Civil Attorney, presented the appeal of the Community Development Director’s denial of the low impact permit to Council. (5:14 PM)
Council Members asked questions. Deputy Viti responded. (5:24 PM)
Stephen Dell on behalf of Gregory and Kelley Dell appeared but didn’t offer an argument. Gregory Dell presented his appeal of the Community Development Director’s denial of the low impact permit. (5:30 PM)
Planner Ray Milliner responded to Council’s questions as well. (6:14 PM)
Christopher Robinson made a motion to deny land use appeal-file #24-055, regarding 875 Westwood Road, Parcel SL-B-117 and that we sustain the decision made by the Community Development Director and not find it in error that the Limited Impact Permit was denied and ask County Attorney's Office to draw findings of fact and conclusions of law consistent with the dialog for approval by the Chair and that some of those findings of fact will be the following: 1) the reason the Conditional Use Permit was approved in the first place was because of the assumption that there would be no commercial uses in any of the facilities; that was a bedrock requirement that has consistently been promulgated throughout any other activities or intersections between this property and Summit County's rules; 2) to allow nightly rentals to circumvent that overarching provision would undermine the initial provision of no commercial uses; 3) the findings listed by the Community Development Director in his order of 6/2024 are consistent with impacts that we would see from taking the facility from a private recreation facility to what is tantamount to a commercial facility and especially finding number 8 regarding noncompliance with the policies and goals of the general plan, and such other findings of fact and conclusions of law that are consistent with these arguments. (7:10 PM). Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Recuse: Canice Harte.
Attorney Margaret Olson commented on the proceedings and correspondence received leading up to the proceedings. (7:14 PM)
Appellant Dell objected to Attorney Olson’s statement. (7:16 PM)
Council Member Armstrong noted Deputy Viti’s professionalism and skill. (7: 20 PM)
