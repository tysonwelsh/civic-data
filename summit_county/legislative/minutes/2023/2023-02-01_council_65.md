Consideration of Approval (4:37 P.M.)
a.
Appoint member to serve on North Summit Recreation Special Service District Administrative Control Board (4:37 P.M.)
Malena Stevens made a motion to Appoint Wes Chappell to fill the unexpired term of Derrick Peterson on the North Summit Recreation Special Service District Administrative Control Board. Term of service to expire September 30, 2025 Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Appointment to NSRSSD
b.
Discussion and action regarding appeal of a Conditional Use Permit approved by the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission for a Distillery/Microbrewery, and a guest ranch or lodge intended to attract visitors/patrons on a daily basis or an extended stay at 2617 State Road 32; Brandon and Carly Coyle, Applicant; Lauren and Michael Halcik, Appellants; Ray Milliner, County Planner (4:38 P.M.)
Attachment: CUP Appeal
Justin Keys, Attorney, spoke on behalf of the applicants, Brandon and Carly Coyle. (4:59 P.M.)
Helen Strachan, Civil Deputy Attorney, provided background on the Conditional Use Permit application and timeline.
Council Chair Armstrong asked procedural questions. Dave Thomas, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney, responded.
Dallin Peterson, Attorney, spoke on behalf of the appellants, Lauren and Michael Halcik. (4:46 P.M.)
Helen Strachan, Civil Deputy Attorney, spoke on behalf of the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission. (5:06 P.M.)
Dallin Peterson, Attorney, spoke in rebuttal on behalf of the appellants, Lauren and Michael Halcik. (5:12 P.M.)
Ray Milliner, County Planner, commented. (5:18 P.M.)
Council Members asked questions and the parties responded.
Chris Robinson made a motion to deny the appeal of the Conditional Use Permit approved by the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission for a Distillery/Microbrewery, and a guest ranch or lodge intended to attract visitors/patrons on a daily basis or an extended stay at 2617 State Road 32 and directing County Counsel to draft findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions for the permit that specify that tasting room is an accessory use to the guesthouse and include further clarification of the difference between a tasting room and a restaurant, specifying that a tasting room is authorized, but not a restaurant to be approved by the Council Chair. Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Dendric Cidery CUP Findings of Fact & Conclusions of Law

Attachment: Instructions
Public Hearing (6:05 P.M.)
a.
Public hearing and action on a special exception from the Summit County Code, Sections 10.4.2: Environmental Criteria; 10-2-4.D.1/10-4-22: Building Height; 10-4-10: Transportation Infrastructure and Access; and 7-2: Excavation, Grading and Filling on Private Property to allow a site-specific over-lot grading plan for Lots 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 of the FJ Gillmor Subdivision and adjusted maximum building height elevations for Lots 1 and 6 of the FJ Gillmor Subdivision; Pat Putt
Caroline Rodriguez, Executive Director of High Valley Transit, John Angell, Public Works Director, and Patrick Putt, Community Development Director, presented to the Council.
Roger Armstrong Opened public hearing. (6:32 P.M.)
Taylor Leavitt commented. (6:32 P.M.)
Roger Armstrong closed the public hearing. (6:32 P.M.)
Council Members asked questions. Andy Garland, Mountain Regional Water Special Service District General Manager, Mr. Angell, and Ms. Rodriguez responded.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve a special exception from the Summit County Code, Sections 10.4.2: Environmental Criteria; 10-2-4.D.1/10-4-22: Building Height; 10-4-10: Transportation Infrastructure and Access; and 7-2: Excavation, Grading and Filling on Private Property to allow a site-specific over-lot grading plan for Lots 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 of the FJ Gillmor Subdivision and adjusted maximum building height elevations for Lots 1 and 6 of the FJ Gillmor Subdivision subject to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions stated. Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Staff Report - HVT Special Exception
