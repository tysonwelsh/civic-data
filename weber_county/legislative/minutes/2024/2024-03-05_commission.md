D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: There was a thing in college called a pop quiz. This will be a
   philosophy pop quiz from Charles Schultz, creator of the Peanuts comic strip. Name the five wealthiest people in
   the world. Name the last five Heisman Trophy winners. Name the last five winners of the Miss America
   Pageant. Name ten Nobel Peace Prize winners. Name a half dozen Academy Award winners for Best Actor or
   Actress. Name the last decade’s worth of World Series winners. Remember, none of us really remember the
   headlines of yesterday. Achievements and accolades are often forgotten. Here’s a second quiz. List the teachers
   that aided your journey through school. Name three friends that helped you through your difficult times. Name
   five people who have taught you something worthwhile. Think of a few people who have made you feel
   appreciated and special. Think of five people you enjoy spending time with. The lesson from Charles Schultz is,
   “the people who make a difference in your life are not the ones who have the most credentials...the most
   money…or the most trophies or awards. They are simply the ones who car the most.”

      schedule and under budget. The new tank increases capacity from 250k gallons to 750k gallons.

   1. APPROVE THE 2024 RAMP GRANT RECOMMENDATIONS RECREATION, ARTS, MUSEUMS
      AND PARKS BOARD.
            Lacy Richards (RAMP Tax Advisory Board, Chair): I am joined today by Kevin Burns who chairs the
            RAMP Recreation and Parks committee and Ernie Gonzalez who chairs the RAMP Arts and Museums
            Committee. The funding available in Major grant category was $2M which was enough to fund 31% of the
            nine requests received. These were all partial finding recommendations. Parks and Recreation had $1.8M in
            available funding for 76% of requested funds; 16 requests were recommended for funding. Arts and
            Museums also had $1.8M in available funding for 58% of requests received; 47 requests were recommended
            for funding. Applications for EZ grant funding are due by 5pm, March 22nd, 2024. $80k is available for
            funding for requests up to $2k. It is really a honor and a privilege to serve on this RAMP board. I would
            encourage everyone to look at the different ways that RAMP is enhancing our communities.
         Chair Harvey: Thank you Lacy Richards. We appreciate what you do as a leader and what your team does
         on behalf of everyone in Weber County as these funds are distributed countywide for a variety of wonderful
         projects.

         of volunteer time to make these recommendations. I learned about this long before I was a commissioner
         because I'm friends with Lacy and I knew that there were times in the year when she was spending hours and
         hours with the board. Thank you and please pass that on to the volunteer board.

         Commissioner Froerer: It looked like just about every city received some RAMP funding. A lot of those
         projects couldn’t be done and wouldn’t be done without RAMP. Thank you for taking the time spent and
