B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-11-02 2025 Proposed Budgets 
Sara made a motion to approve the 2025 Proposed Budgets. April seconded the motion. 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
The HAUC 2025 budgets were presented for approval. The approval will be the aggregate 
total of each category. Each line item within the category is listed and can be adjusted as 
necessary within the category. See the attached 2025 budgets. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-11-03 Spending Authority 
Steve moved to approve the spending limits as amended. Sara seconded the motion, 
which passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC staff is recommending an increase in the signing authority of the Executive Director 
from $40,000 to $50,000; the Operations Director from $5000 to $6000 and when signing with 
the accounting manager, together the amount increases from $8000 to $10,000. Project 
Manager signing authority up to $25,000. After discussion among the Board, the decision was 
made to allow the Project Manager signing authority up to $5,000 and the Executive Director 
must be a signer on all contracts. 
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-11-04 Subcontractor Agreement 
April made a motion to approve as amended the Subcontractor Agreement. Sara 
seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC engaged legal counsel to draw up a template contract to be used for subcontractor 
agreements for Housing Authority projects including rehabilitation, capital needs, and new 
construction undertaken by the in-house General Contractor. Following the discussion, the 
board approved an amendment to remove the personal guarantor language.  
 
E. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-11-05 2025 Employee Medical / Dental plans 
Steve motioned to approve the 2025 Employee Medical / Dental plans. April seconded 
the motion. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
HAUC provides medical and dental coverage for all full-time employees. The benefits are 
selected and contracted with providers on an annual basis. Bids were received from the 
medical and dental providers including providers of medical services for 2024. HAUC staff 
reviewed and analyzed the information and determined continuance with the current medical 
and dental providers for 2025.
