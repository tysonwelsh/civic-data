NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—August 27th @ 7pm.

              The filing period for the upcoming municipal election is currently open and will close
              on Tuesday, August 19th at 5 p.m.

              August 15th – Movies in the Park will take place at the Central Park Community
              Center with a screening of the movie, “IF’. Activities will start at 7 p.m.

              August 27th – A free ‘Harm Reduction Naloxone Training’ will be available at the
              Promise South Salt Lake Co-Op from 7-8 p.m. Registration is required.

              More information can be found on the City’s website: sslc.gov

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Granite Library branch manager, Kim Welch, gave some updates about upcoming
          events and classes at the library.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood spoke about the volunteer appreciation event held on August 7th.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Williams encouraged everyone to be aware of driving safely now
          that school’s are going back into session. She also spoke about August 14th as the
          National Navajo Code Talkers Day of recognition.

              Council Member Mitchell congratulated all the candidates who have filed for open
              seats in the municipal election.
