VARIANCE
B-2-2026       Russell Platt, representing property owner David Truong, is requesting a variance from
               the provisions in Section 7-6-302 of the West Valley City Code. This section requires
               that any structure have a minimum setback of 10 feet adjacent to a Residential Zone,
               Agricultural Zone or Residential Use. The applicant is asking for a setback variance of
               10 feet for a zero lot line. The property is located at 1277 West 3300 South, is zoned
               C2 General Commercial and is located within the Jordan River Overlay Zone. Staff-
               Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov.
