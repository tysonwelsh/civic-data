Council Member Bradshaw  read the following proclamation commending the service of 
Council Member Jim Bradley:
Whereas, Jim Bradley is a proud and life -long Utah Democrat who got his start in local 
politics during his time as a student at the University of Utah, working first on the Gunn 
McKay campaign for Congress and then by organizing the famed 1972 Wayne Owens for 
Congress walk across Utah; and  
Whereas, Jim spent the early years of his career working in Salt Lake County ’s office of 
drug and alcohol services, assisting residents facing addiction disorder and gaining a deep 
understanding of the need for criminal justice system reform.  This early experience as a 
County employee made Jim a never -wavering advocate for County employees and their 
working conditions; and  
Whereas, Jim later served Governor Scott M. Matheson as Director of the Utah Energy 
Office; and 
Whereas, in 1988 Jim ran to represent the residents of Salt Lake County on the County 
Commission.  The Salt Lake Tribune early print edition (they used to print newspapers !) 
called Jim the winner, but when the canvass was complete, he narrowly lost the election .  
Undeterred, Jim ran again in 1990, winning a County Commission seat by accusing the 
incumbent of building a Taj Mahal (aka the County Government Center - the same Center 
that Jim voted last Friday to help shutter!); and 
Whereas, Jim subsequently served as the County Commission chair from 1991-1994.  As 
a County Commissioner, Jim proudly:

A PROCLAMATION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL HONORING 
COUNCILWOMAN ANN GRANATO FOR HER YEARS OF SERVICE AND 
EXPERTISE TO THE COUNTY
WHEREAS, Councilwoman Ann Granato has represented Salt Lake County Council ’s 
District 4 since 2018.  She was elected to carry on her late husband ’s, Councilman Sam 
Granato, term and was re-elected in 2020.
AND WHEREAS, Councilwoman Ann Granato takes pride in her strong commitment to 
putting good policy ahead of politics, thereby continuing the legacy of her late husband, 
Councilman Sam Granato.
AND WHEREAS, Councilwoman Granato has been a resident of District 4 for over 60 
years. She is a mother of four, grandmother of nine, and the owner of Granato ’s Italian 
Deli, a local small business.
AND WHEREAS, Salt Lake County continues to see rapid growth and change, 
Councilwoman Granato dedicated her efforts to preserving a high quality of life for 
residents new and old.  She worked to increase access to vital community services 
including our libraries and recreation centers, support safety measures across the county, 
and ensure the county acts responsibly with taxpayer dollars.
AND WHEREAS, Councilwoman Granato collaborated with Councilman Richard 
Snelgrove to create the Salt Lake County Pre -Apprenticeship Program, which addresses 
construction demand as the county grows while creating good -paying jobs for trade 
workers.

to adjust the recreation area designation in the Wasatch Canyons, thereby consolidating

Council Member Winder Newton  read the following proclamation commending the 
service of Reid Demman, County Surveyor:
Whereas, Reid Demman is a lifetime SLCo resident, the youngest of two children raised 
by parents Fred and Donna Demman, and grew up in Salt Lake City and the Millcreek 
area.  He graduated from Granite High, attended the Utah Technical College, and the 
University of Utah. He graduated from the HET Engineering Certificate Program in 1984 
and passed his Professional Surveying License exams on his first attempt.   
 
Whereas, Reid started his career with Salt Lake County in 1976 working for public works, 
he was later hired in the County Surveyors Office as a rear chainmen. 
 
Whereas, Reid returned to Public Works for 22 years and managed the Engineering 
section in the Development Services department.   
 
Whereas, Reid was first elected to office in 2006, during his first term, he focused on 
collaboration, streamlining and improving services by restructuring the office. He created 
an interactive management team and improved on the use of modern technology and 
training to reduce overhead costs within his office.  
 
Whereas, Reid developed and implemented a process allowing private land surveyors to 
file their Records of Survey electronically. This process was the first nationally and 
facilitated more efficient use of County resources and allows the public more expedient

Whereas, under Reid ’s leadership the Salt Lake County Surveyors Office was the first 
public office to legally operate a drone in the State of Utah.  
 
Whereas, Reid has represented Salt Lake County on a number of boards and committees 
including, President of the National Association of County Surveyors 2020-2022, 
President of the Utah Association of County Surveyors, Chair of Salt Lake County GIS 
Steering Committee, Vice Chair of National GIS Committee for National Association of

Council Member Harrison  read the following proclamation commending the service of 
Wayne Cushing, County Treasurer:
Whereas, Wayne Cushing is a lifetime SLCo resident and grew up in the Sugarhouse area .  
He graduated from Highland High and the U of U and passed the CPA on his first attempt 
(better check with Wayne on this one).  He married a local girl and they have 4 successful 
children. Over the years he worked as an accountant sometimes auditing local 
municipalities for a couple of different CPA firms and later started his own business with 
some partners.  He was the Chief Financial Officer of their company.  His financial 
background in the private sector would end up serving Wayne well for his public 
responsibilities.
 
Whereas, 14 years ago Wayne, as an unknown in politics, was recruited by County 
personnel to run for public office as the Salt Lake County Treasurer.  Remarkably, in a 
group of three political candidates, Wayne won his primary at the convention with over 
60% of the vote on the first ballot.
 
During Wayne ’s tenure SLCo went over the $ 1 billion dollar tax collection amount and the 
Treasurer’s agency smoothly handled the increases each successive year.  This was all 
accomplished without additional personnel and a tightened budget.
 
Wayne saved SLCo taxpayer funds by automating the process of finding and locating 
taxpayers throughout the County to timely deliver their notices.  His agency worked 
seamlessly with the Salt Lake County Auditor ’s agency to make this happen.  Wayne also 
streamlined the taxpayer care process during the busy season of tax payments to better 
meet the needs of those walking up to pay taxes.  Wayne also initiated the new online tax 
payment program for taxpayer convenience.  Additionally, he spearheaded the early 
adoption of adding a QR code to the tax notice to streamline the online payment process 
even further.
