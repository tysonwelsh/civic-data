                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-07

      A RESOLUTION APPOINTING AN ADVISOR AND CO-ADVISOR TO THE
             RIVERTON CITY YOUTH COUNCIL ORGANIZATION

       WHEREAS, on November 12, 2024, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No.
24-26 which formally created the Riverton City Youth Council (“Youth Council”); and

        WHEREAS, the City Council created the Youth Council to provide a program to educate
the City’s youth about local government functions by attending conferences and undertaking
projects; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Code 2.130.020(2) gives the Mayor authority to appoint
individuals to serve as Youth Council Advisors on a voluntary basis; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the volunteer appointment of
Pam Henderson as Youth Council Advisor and Broc Stowe as Youth Council Co-Advisor; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Pam Henderson and
Broc Stowe are residents of Riverton City, and are willing and capable of serving as Advisors to
the Youth Council;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of Pam
          Henderson and Broc Stowe to serve as Advisor and Co-Advisor to the Youth Council.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 4th day of
February 2025 by the following vote:
