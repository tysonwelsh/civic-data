                                  TOWN OF ALTA

                           RESOLUTION NO. 2024-R-11

     A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE TOWN OF ALTA FISCAL YEAR
                     2024 PROJECTS PLAN

        WHEREAS, the Town developed a Fiscal Year 2024 Projects Plan, which
identifies projects to be implemented during current and future fiscal years;

        WHEREAS, this Projects Plan includes the Capital Projects Plan; and

        WHEREAS, recent events have indicated the need to update budgets for specific
projects in the FY 24 Projects Plan:

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE ALTA TOWN COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:




                                          T
       Section 1. The Amended Town of Alta Fiscal Year 2024 10-Year Projects Plan is
attached as Exhibit A.
