1.     Items for Commission Consideration.

       A.      Public Hearing: England Salon Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
               (Administrative Item)
               On the Request of Kayleen England, Property Owner, a Conditional Use Permit for a
               Home Occupation that Provides Personal Care Services on Approximately 1.34 Acres,
               Located at 13058 South 1300 East. Known as Application 2025-0418. Staff Contact:
               Maryann Pickering, (801) 576-6391, maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

Planning Manager, Todd Draper, presented the staff report and displayed the Vicinity, Aerial, Land
Use, and Zoning Maps, as well as site and floor plans. The subject property is designated Residential
Low/Medium Density and zoned RA2. The salon will be located in an accessory dwelling unit
(“ADU”) at the rear of the property.

The identified potential impacts include customer visits to the home, parking, and related traffic.
Proposed mitigations include allowing one customer at a time, by appointment only, between 9:00
a.m. and 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, and providing one designated off-street parking stall for
customer vehicles. Site photographs were displayed, indicating the designated parking stall and path
to the salon.

The applicant, Kayleen England, reported that the salon has two main rooms: the hair salon and an
aesthetics room. The parking stall is near the walkway and does not block the garage.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Fowler moved to APPROVE the Home Occupation Conditional Use
Permit, as requested by Kayleen England, property owner, Application 2025-0418-USE, based
on the following Finding for Approval and subject to the Conditions listed in the Staff Report
dated December 30, 2025.

       B.       Public Hearing: Western States Flooring Conditional Use Permit
                (Administrative Item)
                On Behalf of Marshall Peterson, representing Western States Flooring, a Conditional
                Use Permit Request to Operate a Limited Wholesale and Warehousing Business on
                approximately 6.80 acres located at 12453 South 265 West, Suite A. Known as
                Application 2025-0184-USE, Staff Contact: Nick Whittaker, (801) 576-6522,
                Nick.Whittaker@draperutah.gov

City Planner Nick Whittaker presented the staff report and displayed the Vicinity, Aerial, Land Use,
and Zoning Maps. The subject property is designated Industrial and Manufacturing and zoned CBP.
The applicant intends to store carpet at the location for pickup by installers. The floor plan shows the
main entrance and parking area at the north end of the building and the loading bay at the rear.

The proposed use of Limited Wholesale and Warehousing of carpet requires a Conditional Use Permit
(“CUP”) in the CBP Zone. Draper City Municipal Code (“DCMC”) 9-3-040 defines the use as
follows:

       An establishment that is engaged in the small-scale storage and sale of goods to other
       businesses for resale, excluding self-storage warehouses, major distribution centers, motor
       freight terminals, moving and storage firms and similar high volume, high turnover facilities.
       No outside storage is permitted.

Identified potential impacts include the following:

   •   Potential parking lot congestion due to large trucks delivering and collecting shipments.
   •   Potential for unauthorized storage outside of the building and suite.
   •   Potential for trucks or trailers to be parked on the property for lengthy periods of time.

Proposed mitigation:

   •   No manufacturing shall take place at the property.
   •   Shipping and receiving shall only be conducted at Unit A’s designated shipping bay.
   •   Shipping and receiving may not block or interfere with vehicular parking stalls, drive aisles,
       or the dock bays of other businesses on the property.
   •   All storage related to the wholesale and warehousing business operation must be located
       inside the subject suite.
   •   No trucks or trailers may be parked on the property for over 12 hours.

Commissioner Fowler inquired about other conditional uses on the property. Mr. Whittaker identified
two suites on the building floor plan that had been issued CUPs, one for Limited Wholesale and
Warehousing of cabinetry and one for the Nutrastrips company.

Chair Adams remarked that the proposed use would have a very low impact and asked why a CUP
was required. Mr. Whittaker clarified that it is a zone-specific requirement to mitigate any potential
negative impacts. However, this type of use is common in the vicinity.

The applicants, Marshall Peterson and Jamie Kempton, were in attendance. Mr. Peterson stated that
no sales would take place on the property. Ms. Kempton indicated that it will be used exclusively for
storage and is not open to the public. No adhesive or chemicals are stored onsite. Their installers
will pick up carpet and padding from the location to install in customers’ homes.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Shirey moved to APPROVE the Conditional Use Permit, as requested
by Marshall Peterson, representing Western States Flooring, Application 2025-0184-USE,
based on the following Finding for Approval and subject to the Conditions listed in the Staff
Report dated December 23, 2025.

Finding for Approval:

       1.      The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC Section
               9-5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the imposition
               of reasonable conditions.

Second: Commissioner Nixon seconded the motion.

Vote on Motion: 5-to-0 in favor.

       C.     Public Hearing: Bell, Peterson and Guston Land Use and Zoning Map
              Amendment Requests
              (Administrative Item)
              On the Request of Dave Johnson, Representing All Three Property Owners, To: 1)
              Amend the Land Use Designation for a Portion of 693 East 13800 South from
              Residential Low Medium Density to Residential Medium Density, 2) Amend the Land
              Use Designation for 13777 South Shadow Mountain Lane from Residential Medium
              Density to Residential Low Medium Density, 3) Amend the Zoning Designation from
              RA1 (Residential Agricultural) to RA2 (Residential Agricultural) and R3 (Single-
              Family Residential) for 693 East 13800 South, and 4) Amend the Zoning Designation
              from RA1 to RA2 (Residential Agricultural) for the Entire Property at 703 East 13800
              South.         Staff     Contact:   Maryann       Pickering,    (801)      576-6391,
              maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

Mr. Draper presented the Staff Report and displayed the Vicinity, Aerial, Land Use, and Zoning
Maps. He then reviewed the proposed changes.

693 East 13800 South

   •   Amend the Land Use designation on the front portion of the lot to Residential Medium Density
       and rezone this portion from RA1 to RA3.
   •   The rear portion of the property will retain the Residential Low/Medium Density designation
       but will be rezoned from RA1 to RA2.

703 East 13800 South

   •   Rezone the property from RA1 to RA2. No changes were proposed to the Residential
       Low/Medium Density designation.


13777 South Shadow Mountain Lane

   •   Amend the Land Use designation from Residential Medium Density to Residential
       Low/Medium Density. The property will retain the existing RA2 zoning.

The above changes were proposed to facilitate a boundary line agreement between the property
owners.

Site photographs were reviewed. Commissioner Fowler asked if the rezoned portion of 693 East
13800 South would be developable if the boundary line agreement was not finalized. Mr. Draper
stated that the parcel may be large enough to subdivide, but flag lots are not allowed on major arterial
streets, so that would prevent development. Community Development Director, Jennifer Jastremsky
clarified that the property would not be large enough to subdivide.

The applicant, Dave Johnson, stated that one property owner is older and does not want to take care
of so much land, and their neighbors have older children who need room to practice sports.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Nixon moved to forward a POSITIVE recommendation to the City
Council for the Land Use Map Amendment, as requested by Dave Johnson, representing the
property owners, application 2024-0408-MA, based on the following Findings for Approval and
subject to the Conditions listed in the Staff Report dated December 30, 2025.

Findings for Approval:

       1.      The proposed Land Use Map Amendment is consistent with the goals, objectives,
               and policies of the City's General Plan.

       2.      The proposed Land Use Map Amendment is harmonious with the overall
               character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.

       3.      The proposed Land Use Map Amendment will not adversely affect adjacent
               property.

       4.      There are adequate facilities and services intended to serve the subject property,
               including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and
               fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and
               wastewater and refuse collection.

       5.      The proposed Land Use Map Amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-2-
               020(F).

       6.      The legislative body may adopt any amendment to the General Plan Land Use
               Map that the legislative body considers appropriate.

Second: Commissioner Shirey seconded the motion.

Vote on Motion: 5-to-0 in favor.
