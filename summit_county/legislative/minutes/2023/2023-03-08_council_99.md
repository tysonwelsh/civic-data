The Council was called to order at the Richins Building for closed session. (2:19 P.M.)
Chris Robinson made a motion to enter closed session to discuss property acquisition. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Closed Session (2:19 P.M.)
Property Acquisition (2:19 P.M.)
The Summit County Council met in closed session from 2:19 P.M. to 3:03 P.M. to discuss property acquisition. The following people were present: Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Robinson, Hanson, Harte, Attorney Margaret Olson, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, Civil Deputy Attorney Lynda Viti, Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Public Lands Manager Jess Kirby, Executive Assistant Annette Singleton
Chris Robinson made a motion to leave closed session to discuss property acquisition and enter closed session to discuss litigation Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Litigation (3:03 P.M.)
The Summit County Council met in closed session fro m 3:03 P.M. to 4:04 P.M. to discuss litigation. The following people were present: Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Robinson, Hanson, Harte, Attorney Margaret Olson, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, Civil Deputy Attorney Lynda Viti, Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Executive Assistant Annette Singleton
Chris Robinson made a motion to leave closed session to discuss litigation and convene in open session Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Council traveled to Ecker Hill Middle School to continue the meeting.
