BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Magna Metro Township Council met in a
regular public meeting on January 3, 2017

                                            WITNESS

        WHEREAS, the incorporation of the Magna Metro Township requires the Council to
adopt a Land Use Plan and Code of Ordinance for Magna, per State Law; and

        WHEREAS, prior to the creation of the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County was
the governing jurisdiction responsible for writing these plans, rules and regulations for Magna;
and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County also adopted a Magna General Plan to guide land use
decisions in Magna; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County presented copies of the Magna General Plan, Land Use
Plan, and an amended copy of the Code of Ordinances used by Salt Lake County at a Metro
Township Council Training on December 3, 2016,

       WHEREAS, the Magna voters, in the November 3,2015 election, voted to incorporate as
a Metro Township and to join the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as
members of the District; and

       WHEREAS, accepting and adopting the Land Use Plan, General Plan, and Code of
Ordinance provides for a smoother transition and creates the basis moving forward for Magna to
modify and change these to better reflect the needs and best wishes of the people of Magna.
                                         RESOLUTION

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED,the Magna Metro Township Council does
hereby ratify and approve the Magna Land Use Plan, General Plan, and amended Code of
Ordinance as presented by Salt Lake County and the District Attorney.
