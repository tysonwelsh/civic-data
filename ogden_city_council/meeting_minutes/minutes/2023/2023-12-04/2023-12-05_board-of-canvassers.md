Consideration of certifying the November 21, 2023, Municipal General Co)
Election results
A memo from the City Recorder came before the Council to consider certifying the November 21, 2023, Municipal
General Election results. The memo stated Section 20A, Chapter 4 of the Utah State Code sets forth the election review process,
designates the legislative body — the City Council—and the Mayor as the Board of Canvassers and outlines the Board’s duties for
municipal elections. The City Recorder acts as the election official. The Board of Canvassers is responsible for certifying the
official election results of municipal elections. Canvassing ofreturns for a General Election must occur no sooner than seven (7)
days after the election and no later than fourteen (14) days after the election. After reviewing the returns, the Board must certify
the results of the election and file a report that includes the following information: ;
1. The total number of votes cast in the Board’s jurisdiction;
2. The name of each candidate whose name appeared on the ballot;
3. Each office that appears on the ballot;
4. The number of votes for each candidate;
5. The total number of votes given in the Board’s jurisdiction to each candidate;
6. The number of ballots that were rejected; and
7. A statement certifying that the information contained in the report is accurate.
City Recorder Hansen summarized the memo and reviewed the report of unofficial election results dated November 29,
2023. She then noted the total number of ballots cast was 11,904 and the City has 32,197 registered voters; this equates to a 36.98
percent voter turnout. The seats to be filled in the 2023 Municipal Election are Ogden City Mayor, At Large Seat C, and City
Council District 4. Candidates for the position of Mayor are Taylor Knuth and Ben Nadolski; Ben Nadolski received the highest
number of votes — 6,418 — and has been duly elected. Candidates for the position of At-Large Seat C were Shaun Myers and J.
Levi Andersen; Shaun Myers received 6,855 votes and has been duly elected. Candidates for City Council District 4 were Steven
Van Wagoner and Dave Graf; Dave Graf received the highest number of votes — 2,465 — and has been duly elected. Richard Hyer
ran unopposed for the position of City Council District 2 and has been duly elected. She briefly reviewed the report regarding the
number of ballots that were disqualified and cited the reasons for disqualification, noting that voters are notified of the potential
for their ballot to be disqualified and given time to rectify the issue from which the disqualification would result. She concluded
that it is her recommendation that the Board certify the results of the November 21, 2023 Municipal General Election. (
