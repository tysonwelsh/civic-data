      Ms. Abplanalp stated the top two (2) candidates - Michele Randall and Jimmie
      Hughes and the top four (4) candidates - Michelle Tanner, Greg Aldred, Vardell
      Curtis, and Natalie Larsen will move forward to the General Election. On election
      night, 13,680 ballots were counted; however, 2,365 eligible ballots were added for a
      total of 16,045 ballots which is 30% voter turnout. In the 2019 primary election,
      which was the first vote by mail for St. George, voter turnout was 28.46%; the 2017
      primary election which was in person voting, voter turnout was 11.35%. There were
      4,176 ballots that were not able to be counted: 510 were returned past the deadline
      of August 9th, 3,459 were returned as undeliverable, 207 were returned unsigned or
      had signatures that did not match voter records – voters were contacted but did not
      cure the situation. In person voting had 16 provisional all of which were counted and
      179 resident who showed up to vote in person; 142 were from St. George, the
      others were residents of neighboring cities. She explained what an overvote is and
      stated that these votes cannot be cured.

      Councilmember Smethurst commented there are more people that email him than
      vote.

      After being asked by Councilmember Hughes, Ms. Abplanalp stated there is not an
      automatic recount. The losing candidate must request a recount within three (3)
