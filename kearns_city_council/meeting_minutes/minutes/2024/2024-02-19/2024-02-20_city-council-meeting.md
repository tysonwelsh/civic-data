Council Business

Resolution Affirming Law Enforcement Agency Commitment

               Mayor Bush stated both agencies, the Salt Lake County Sheriff’s Office, and
Unified Police Department (UPD), had been given an opportunity to present before the Kearns
Metro Township Council the week before. The Council has decided to continue to utilize law
enforcement services through UPD. She asked each Council member to state, for the record,
their reason for deciding to stay with UPD.

              Council Member Butterfield stated she had been pleased with the services
provided by UPD so far and appreciates the hard work of everyone involved.

                 Council Member Snow stated Kearns is growing and needs to continue with the
staffing it currently has.

              Council Member Peterson stated he believes UPD works really well, and he sees
no reason to change things. There is no need to fix what is not broken.

               Council Member Schaeffer stated the Sheriff’s model did not impress him at all
compared to the UPD model. He was afraid that going with the Sheriff’s model would result in a
loss of resources.

                 Mayor Bush stated she has spent a lot of time working toward this goal. She had
concerns over officer safety and community safety. She was also concerned about returning to
the model Kearns Metro Township had six or seven years ago. The UPD model works. She
believes in it, and she believes in the officers. She is not willing to lose any officers.

             Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve Resolution 2024-02-02. The motion passed by a unanimous rollcall vote.

Resolution 2024-02-02                                                         February 20, 2024

A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP AFFIRMING KEARNS’ COMMITMENT
TO THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq; and

WHEREAS, Kearns is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code §10-3-205.5
(the “Council”); and

WHEREAS, on April 10, 2023, Kearns adopted Resolution 2023-04-02 Establishing Policy and
Providing Direction Regarding the Implementation of H.B. 374 (“County Sheriff Amendments”);
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to said Resolution 2023-04-02, the City Attorney, under the direction of the
Mayor, has been working with legal counsel for other metro townships and the Town of Brighton
to deal with the short and long term implications of H.B. 374, which requires a separation of the
Salt Lake County Sheriff from the Unified Police Department (“UPD”); and

WHEREAS, as a result of that separation it has become necessary for UPD to amend and restate
the interlocal agreement under which it was created, which restated and amended interlocal
agreement will govern the provision of law enforcement services to its members; and

WHEREAS, Kearns’s City Attorney has been asked to participate in the drafting of such restated
and amended interlocal agreement; and

WHEREAS, Kearns, as a result of its adoption of Resolution 2023-04-02, is on record as
supporting the UPD model, and now desires to affirm that position.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS METRO
TOWNSHIP:
