CONSIDERATION OF PARTICIPATION IN THE UTAH INTEGRATED
HEALTHCARE PILOT PROGRAM

Washington County Deputy Attorney Eric Clarke explained that the State of Utah is
looking at various options to expand Medicaid working towards the integration of mental
and physical healthcare. This pilot program will allow funds available from the State to be
used by Washington County to expand the network provider system as well as develop the
necessary resources to provide excellent healthcare.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve the Participation of
Washington County in the Utah Integrated Healthcare Pilot Program.
Motion seconded by Chairman Cox and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Cox and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2019-2496, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY AND THE USDA, FOREST SERVICE, DIXIE NATIONAL FOREST
FOR THE CONSTRUCTION AND MAINTENANCE OF CERTAIN FOREST

ROADS

Washington County Engineer Todd Edwards explained that certain roads within the
County are defined as forest roads, which are roads of primary importance for the
protection, administration and utilization of the National Forests. It is beneficial to the
County to work with the Forest Service to ensure that those roads are improved to adequate
standards to accommodate all traffic safely. This agreement identifies forest roads that are
to be maintained by the County.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-
2496, A Resolution Approving a Cooperative Agreement Between
Washington County and the USDA, Forest Service, Dixie National
Forest for the Construction and Maintenance of Certain Forest Roads.
Motion seconded by Chairman Cox and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Cox and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2019-2500, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING THE PURCHASE OF LOGIN EQUIPMENT FOR THE ST.
GEORGE SENIOR CITIZEN CENTER

Washington County COA Center Supervisor Jeanie Johnson stated that in order to qualify
for and obtain state and federal funds it is critical to track client attendance at the St. George
senior center. Council on Aging staff currently uses outdated methods of tracking the data
and recommends the purchase of a digital sign-in system to more accurately track client
usage.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-2500, A
Resolution Approving the Purchase of Login Equipment for the St.
George Senior Citizen Center. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox and
Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2019-2501, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF _ CORRECTIONS
INTERGOVERNMENTAL COUNTY JAIL AGREEMENT

Washington County Sheriff Cory Pulsipher explained that the County currently has an
agreement with the Utah Department of Corrections to house state inmates at the Purgatory
Correctional Facility. The state pays the County to house these inmates, and currently, there
are 130-140 state inmates being housed.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-
2501, A Resolution Approving the Utah Department of Corrections
Intergovernmental County Jail Agreement. Motion seconded by
Chairman Cox and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Cox and Iverson voting aye.
