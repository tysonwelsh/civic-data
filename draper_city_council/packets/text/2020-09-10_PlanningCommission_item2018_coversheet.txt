Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky
CC:  Date: September 10, 2020
Re: The Annex Plat Amendment Extension Comments: On September 12, 2019, the Planning Commission granted plat amendment approval for The Annex, located at 690 East 12200 South. The application number is SUBD-733-2019. Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 17-9-070 states:
If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.
To date, the applicant has not acquired a land disturbance permit or begun improvements to the property.  As such, he is seeking a six (6) month extension of his plat amendment approval.  Attached to this memo is the applicant’s written request for plat amendment extension, the original plat amendment approval letter, and the September 12, 2019 Planning Commission staff report.  Staff received the request for extension on September 1, 2020, prior to the expiration of the plat amendment.
The applicant has run into financing delays with the project and is currently working on securing additional financing. The extension to the request will allow the applicant to secure financing and begin construction on the project.
If approved, the plat amendment approval will be valid through March 12, 2021.    The findings are as follows: Sample Motion for Approval – I move that we approve the plat amendment extension, as requested by Clayton Morgan for The Annex Plat Amendment, application SUBD-733-2019, based on the findings listed in the memo dated September 10, 2020.
1.  The applicant has shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has occurred towards acquiring a land disturbance permit.
Sample Motion for Denial – I move that we deny plat amendment extension, as requested by Clayton Morgan for The Annex Plat Amendment, application SUBD-733-2019, based on the findings listed in the memo dated September 10, 2020.
1.  The applicant has not shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has not occurred towards acquiring a land disturbance permit.
