                                  Work Session Minutes
                                    October 8, 2024
                                        7:00 p.m.
                                       21 E 100 N
                                   Nephi, UT 84648


1.    Golf Director Mike Booth presented a model of a potential future golf course clubhouse.
     He asked the council if he could move forward with having a professional design a new
     clubhouse. Mr. Atkinson stated that there were funds available for the design in the
     capital projects fund.

2. The council discussed some Greater Nephi Chamber of Commerce stipulations for
   continuing contributions to the organization. Mr. Atkinson suggested the following
   stipulations:
       a. That the City receive the benefits of a Platinum membership without paying the
           annual fee for Council members and selected staff.
       b. The Chamber provide a detailed plan with demonstrable goals that would
           ultimately make the Greater Nephi Chamber of Commerce self-sufficient. These
           could include:
       c. Submit an annual financial report with an income statement
       d. A narrative describing exactly where city funds were used
       e. Actions the Chamber is taking in order to become self-sufficient, including
           membership number goals and goals for reducing the city subsidy
       f. Actions taken to seek additional funding from other entities benefitting from the
           Greater Nephi Chamber of Commerce
       g. Semi-annual reporting to the Council on progress toward goals by the Executive
           Director or Board leadership.
       h. Cooperation with all city events such as the Information Fair, Summer Reading,
           Halloweentown, Illuminate the Night Christmas tree lighting, recreation events,
           etc.
       i. Liaison to City meetings for items of interest to the business community.

     Councilor JD Parady suggested that we add a timeline to their membership goals and
     that the contribution be paired down over time. The council suggested that the
     contribution be paired down to eventually only contributing $2500.00 annually. They
     suggested having a goal of the chamber being self-sufficient within five years.

3. The council reviewed a draft of a short-term rental regulation ordinance. The Planning
   Commission would be reviewing the code change in their October meeting. Councilor
   Parady suggested that the parking requirements were too stringent and Councilor
   Cowan suggested not mentioning specific companies in the code.

4. The council discussed the newest draft of the Third-Party Billable Overtime Pay Policy.
   The purpose of the policy is to establish guidelines for compensating Nephi City
     employees who perform work involving billable third parties outside of regular work
        hours ensuring fair compensation for their time and efforts. The policy applies to all
        employees of the Nephi City who are required to work overtime due to billable
        situations involving third parties. Attorney Wright had changed some wording from
        the original draft and the council agreed with the policy.

   5. Neil Fraserr from JUB introduced himself to the council. He was passing through and
      thought he would take a minute to meet the council. He is working on the AWOS airport
      project.

   6. Seth Atkinson updated the council about the storm drain project along Main Street.

   7.   The council reviewed the recent business license applications.

   8.   The council agreed to put up the Halloween Town decorations on Thursday at 6:00 p.m.

   9.    Councilor Callaway updated the council on the interviews for the Gas Tech position. He
        also reported that he will be meeting with the county to discuss the Christmas lighting
        event.

   10. Shari Cowan moved to convene into a closed session to discuss the character and
      competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Parady seconded the motion.
      The motion passed unanimously. 7:45 p.m.

   11. The council reconvened in regular work session 8:03 p.m.

   12. The council discussed plans for the north end of the city that has an interested party in
      wanting to annex more property into the city.

   13. Councilor Callaway moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Travis Worwood
      seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

   14. The meeting adjourned at 8:14 p.m.
