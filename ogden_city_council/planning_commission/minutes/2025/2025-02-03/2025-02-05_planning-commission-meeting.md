6. Public Hearing Rezone, R-3EC to R-9 543-563 25" St

Petitioner Fillerup requested the rezone to enable them to make their project consistent with
the city. They have also been working with Planning as to be cohesive with the area and Plan Ogden.

Local resident Travis Pate voiced his concern that the townhomes would affect visibility and
block historic preservation.

Local resident Mike Vowles owns a nearby home and previously owned the subject lot and
supports this project.

Local resident Alan Toone expressed that with this property being right outside his back door
he feels it will affect fire access safety and fears the height will be constrictive.

Local resident Nick Milford said he thinks it is a good idea and location for the townhomes.

Local resident Deborah Darrington said this property is right out her back gate and is
concerned with fire access and safety issues as of late. Also, that the height would be distracting
from the historical nature of the area and her visibility, and that the value of her home would
depreciate if it were lined up with townhomes instead of cottages or something similar.

Assistant Planning Director Joe Simpson reassured that fire codes would have to be followed
to ensure safety. He further stated that the project was in accordance with the General Plan and staff
recommend the rezone to support current conditions.

Chair Safsten moved for a motion to close the public hearing.

COMMISSIONER SANDAU MADE A MOTION TO CLOSE THE PUBLIC HEARING.
COMMISSIONER ROSS SECONDED THE MOTION. ALL IN FAVOR — AYE
