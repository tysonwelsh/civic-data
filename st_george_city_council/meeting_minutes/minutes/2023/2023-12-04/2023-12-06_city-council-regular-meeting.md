CANVASS OF THE 2023 MUNICIPAL GENERAL ELECTION:
     BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Pursuant to §20a-4-301, Utah Code
     Annotated, the Mayor and City Council are the Board of Municipal Canvassers for the
     City of St. George. The Board of Municipal Canvassers shall meet to canvass the
     returns for the municipal general election, at the usual place of their meeting no
     sooner than 7 days after the election and no later than 14 days after the election.
