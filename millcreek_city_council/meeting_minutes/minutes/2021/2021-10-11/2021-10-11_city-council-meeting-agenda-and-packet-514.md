                                             The Millcreek      Arts Council voted unanimously               on July 1 3th, 202al to take

                                             Digital billboards     are not in alignment        with our current City plan nor are
they part of our City's motto 'Connected               by Nature'. As such, we do not believe that digital billboards                   wil!
follow the desired      culture and aesthetic        our community        agreed upon in 2019. In fact, we worry that digital
billboards    will be distracting,     intrusive,   and will take away from the culture and charm of our City.
In addition     to our concerns      with the digital component          of these signs- the size of these proposed

City Center.

Millcreek     City Center to be successful.         The Millcreek      Arts Council would like to work with Millcreek                City to

community's       events without      the blight of massive        digital billboards.

best interest to slow down this process             and find alternative       solutions      to decrease     the City's billboard

attempting      to change     ordinances    in order to make this deal with Reagan                OutdoorAdvertising         happen.
