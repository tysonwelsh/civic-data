                EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

        RESOLUTION NO. 2023-11-02                              DATE: November 28, 2023


 A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP’S
                     TENTATIVE 2024 BUDGET

      WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“Emigration Canyon”) is a
municipality and political subdivision of the state of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, as a metro township, Utah Code § 10-3c-202 requires Emigration Canyon to
comply with the Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act of Utah Cities as set forth in Title 10, Chapter 6
of the Utah Code.

        WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon is required to adopt a tentative budget for the upcoming
fiscal year pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-111; and

        WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon prepared a budget for the upcoming 2024 fiscal year and
desires to adopt it as its tentative 2024 budget and to schedule a public hearing to take public
comment on the same.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township
Council:

       1.      The attached budget is hereby adopted as Emigration Canyon’s tentative budget for
the 2024 fiscal year.

        2.     The Emigration Canyon Council will hold a public hearing on December 6, 2023,
at 7:00 p.m. at the Emigration Canyon Fire Station, 5025 Emigration Canyon Road, Emigration
Canyon, Utah 84108, to receive public comments on the tentative 2024 Emigration Canyon
budget.

       3.      Notice of the public hearing will be posted pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-113.

       4.        A copy of this tentative budget shall be made available for public inspection for a
period of at least ten days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-112.

       5.       This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
