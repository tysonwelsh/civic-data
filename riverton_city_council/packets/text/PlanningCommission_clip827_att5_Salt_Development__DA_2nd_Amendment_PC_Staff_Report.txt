This application is a decision item and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motion
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of the amended 2nd Amendment to the
Mountain View Place Development Agreement with the following conditions:

     1. The development agreement be amended to allow a total of 302 residential units.

Background

This item is a revision to a previously approved but unexecuted amendment to the Mountain
View Place Development Agreement. The property is zoned SLR as are all the surrounding
properties. It is about 5-acres and is located at the northeast corner of the Mountain View
Village Master Site Plan. It is bounded on the north by 13200 South (a public street), the Welby
Jacob Canal on the east and Mountain View Village to the west and south. A bridge across
the canal is planned south of this project and will be installed separately.

This project was approved as shown, with 7 stories and 282 residential units. As the
development agreement currently limits the total residential units to 282, the developer has
previously proposed and been approved for an amendment to that agreement to raise the
number to 288 to accommodate an additional six units. They are now requesting an additional
amendment increasing the allowed unit count to 302 units. The footprint of the building and the
exterior remains the same, and no changes are required to the civil design package for the
project. No additional parking is required beyond what is shown in the plan, as the original
design accounted for commercial space that is no longer included in the structure.

The Master Developer, CenterCal, has reviewed and consented to the proposed amendments.
Therefore staff is recommending approval with the condition outlined in the sample motion.
