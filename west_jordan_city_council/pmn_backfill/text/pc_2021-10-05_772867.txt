3.        Text Amendment-          Drinking Water Source Protection; Amend the 2009 City Code Title
          13 Chapter 6 Article F Drinking Water Source Protection Overlay Zones; City-wide
          applicability;      City of West Jordan ( applicant) [# 25426]

Larry Gardner said this overlay ordinance protects the drinking water recharge areas and areas around
wellheads from being contaminated by chemicals or other things that could render drinking water
useless. Some of the minor text changes include a review by the Utilities Manager for all conditional
use applications, maps will be amended through a public hearing process, and business licensing and
business activities are added to clarify that the ordinance does not only apply to new development. A
double walled alarm tank placed in an excavation site with an impermeable liner will replace the
requirement     for a concrete vault. The current and proposed maps were shown and explained.   It was

pointed out that this ordinance would only affect new business.

Based on the analysis and findings contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for this application.

Trish Hatch opened the public hearing.

Further public comment          was closed at this point for this item.


MOTION:             Corbin England moved, based on the information and findings set forth in this
                    staff report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to forward a
                    positive recommendation to the City Council to Amend the 2009 City Code Title
                    13 Chapter 6 Article F Drinking Water Source Protection Overlay Zones. The
                    motion was seconded by Matt Quinney and passed 6- 0 in favor. Jay Thomas was
                    absent.
