COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH, COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER, COMMISSIONER
GORDON AND RALPH BARNES, DIRECTOR OF HUMAN RESOURCES - DISCUSSED
ITEMS SUCH AS ELECTED OFFICIALS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES GETTING RAISES AT
THE SAME TIME. THE THOUGHTS THAT THEIR DEPARTMENTS WOULD BE
CONSIDERED FIRST FOR RAISES AND THEN WHOEVER WAS ELECTED WOULD THEN
ALSO SEE A PAY RAISE. QUESTIONS WERE ASKED IF THIS SHOULD WAIT UNTIL THE
MID-YEAR REVIEW TO BE SURE THAT THERE IS MONEY TO DO IT. IT WAS POINTED
OUT THAT THE ONLY REASON A PAY RAISE WAS BEING CONSIDERED FOR ELECTED
OFFICIALS IS BECAUSE EVERY EMPLOYEE’S PAY IN THE COUNTY IS BEING
REEVALUATED AT THIS TIME. THE RAISE IS COVERED IN THIS YEAR’S BUDGET. THE
OPTIONS ON HOW TO RAISE ELECTED OFFICIAL’S PAY ARE LIMITED. BY STATUTE,
ELECTED OFFICIALS MAY NOT EARN ANY MORE OR LESS THAN WHAT HAS BEEN
ALREADY APPROVED. OUR ELECTED OFFICIALS ARE CURRENTLY 20 PERCENT
BELOW MARKET IN THEIR PAY. THIS PROPOSAL, WHICH IS BASED ON THE COUNTY
ORDINANCE, WOULD BE RAISING THE ELECTED OFFICIALS PAY NINE PERCENT. THIS
IS STILL TEN PERCENT UNDER THE MARKET AND A CONSERVATIVE APPROACH.
DOING THIS IN CONJUNCTION WITH OUR EMPLOYEES WOULD BE GOOD, BUT IT
CANNOT BE DONE. EMPLOYEE PAY IS BEING BROUGHT UP TO THE MARKET LEVEL,
WHEREAS THE ELECTED OFFICIALS’ PAY RAISE WOULD BE SIGNIFICANTLY LESS,
ONLY COVERING HALF OF THE GAP BETWEEN WHERE PAY IS NOW AND THE
MARKET LEVEL. IT IS RECOGNIZED THAT THIS IS A DIFFICULT TOPIC. THE DESIRE
TO OPERATE UTAH COUNTY AS A PROFESSIONAL ORGANIZATION WAS EXPRESSED,
AND THE WISH TO NOT UNDERPAY OR OVERPAY OUR ELECTED OFFICIALS WAS
EXPRESSED. THE EFFORT PUT FORTH BY RALPH AND HIS TEAM IS ACKNOWLEDGED
AND APPRECIATED.

RABBI BENNY ZIPPEL - STATED THIS RESOLUTION IS A TRIBUTE TO A GREAT MAN,
RABBI MENACHEM MENDEL SCHNEERSON WHO BECAME THE LEADER OF CHABAD
LUBAVITCH MOVEMENT IN 1950. HE CONCENTRATED HIS EFFORTS ON REBUILDING
AND ENERGIZING THE GLOBAL JEWISH COMMUNITY AFTER THE DEVASTATION OF
THE HOLOCAUST. HE PAID GREAT ATTENTION TO THE NEEDS OF ALL HUMANITY.
THE REBBE TRAINED HIS STUDENTS TO MOVE AWAY FROM HIS COURT, TO GO OUT
TO EVERY CORNER OF THE GLOBE AND TO SET UP OUTPOSTS OF EDUCATION AND
TEACHING LIGHT AND LOVE. HE HAS REPRESENTATIVES ON EVERY CONTINENT OF
THE GLOBE IN OVER 100 COUNTRIES AND IN EVERY ONE OF THE 50 STATES. THE
FIRST CHABAD INSTITUTION WAS ESTABLISHED IN SALT LAKE CITY IN 1992 WHEN
RABBI SCHEERSON SENT MY PARENTS RABBI BENNY AND HIS WIFE SHARON ZIPPEL
AS HIS REPRESENTATIVES TO THE STATE TO OPEN CHABAD OF UTAH. THE CHABAD
OF UTAH HAS SINCE THEN BLOSSOMED TO FOUR DIFFERENT LOCATIONS AND FIVE
COUPLES SERVING AS EMISSARIES IN EVERY CORNER OF THIS GREAT STATE OF
UTAH. THE REBBE’S GLOBAL IMPACT WAS SO GREAT THAT HE IS THE ONLY RABBI
TO HAVE BEEN AWARDED THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL IN RECOGNITION OF
HIS OUTSTANDING AND ENDURING CONTRIBUTIONS TOWARD WORLD EDUCATION
MORALITY AND ACT OF CHARITY AND THE ONLY RABBI TO HAVE A NATIONAL DAY
OF REFLECTION PROCLAIMED ANNUALLY IN HIS HONOR. THE REBBE TAUGHT THAT
EVERY INDIVIDUAL AND IN FACT EVERY INDIVIDUAL ACTION HAS AN IMPACT ON
THE ENTIRE WORLD. THE RABBI EMPHASIZED THE IMPORTANCE OF EDUCATION
AND GOOD CHARACTER AND INSTILLED THE HOPE FOR A BRIGHTER FUTURE INTO
THE LIVES OF COUNTLESS PEOPLE IN AMERICA AND ACROSS THE GLOBE. THE RABBI
TAUGHT THAT EDUCATION IN GENERAL SHOULD NOT BE LIMITED TO THE
ACQUISITION OF KNOWLEDGE AND PREPARATION OF HER CAREER. INSTEAD, THE
EDUCATIONAL SYSTEM MUST PAY MORE ATTENTION, INDEED THE MAIN
ATTENTION TO THE BUILDING OF CHARACTER WITH EMPHASIS ON MORAL AND
ETHICAL VALUES. OVER THE LAST FOUR DECADES THE U.S. CONGRESS HAS SET
ASIDE EDUCATION AND SHARING DAY IN TRIBUTE TO HIS COMMITMENT TO
TEACHING THE NEXT GENERATION OF AMERICANS THE VALUES THAT MAKE OUR
COUNTRY STRONG. IT IS A BIPARTISAN DAY PROCLAIMED ANNUALLY BY PRESIDING
U.S. PRESIDENTS TO COINCIDE WITH THE REBBE’S BIRTHDAY WHICH IS FOUR DAYS
BEFORE THE JEWISH FESTIVAL OF PASSOVER. THIS YEAR IT IS OBSERVED ON
SUNDAY APRIL 2ND. EDUCATION AND SHARING DAY IS A TIME TO PAUSE AND
REFLECT ON OUR PERSONAL RESPONSIBILITY TO PROVIDE OUR YOUNG PEOPLE
WITH A MORAL EDUCATION, THEN TEACH THEM THE FOUNDATIONS THEY NEED TO
LIVE A LIFE OF SERVICE AND HIGHER PURPOSE. EDUCATION AND SHARING DAY IS
ABOUT CREATING CONVERSATIONS. IT’S ABOUT THE CONVERSATIONS WE HAVE
WITH OUR CHILDREN AROUND THE DINNER TABLE, THE CONVERSATIONS WE HAVE
WITH OUR STUDENTS IN THE CLASSROOMS AND THE CONVERSATIONS WE HAVE
DAY IN AND DAY OUT WITH OUR COLLEAGUES AND FRIENDS THAT HAVE AN IMPACT
ON PATHS AND MORALS OF LIFE. THE REBBE TAUGHT THAT IN TRUTH EVERY
PERSON IS AN EDUCATOR, AND WE MUST BE MINDFUL OF OUR ABILITY TO IMPACT
THE LIVES OF OTHERS. TODAY WE ARE PROCLAIMING THE RABBI’S BIRTHDAY
APRIL 2ND AS AN EDUCATION AND SHARING DAY IN UTAH COUNTY, CALLING UPON

23. APPROVE THE SIGNING OF A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING ADMINISTRATIVE FLOOD LEAVE FOR UTAH
COUNTY EMPLOYEES

       -Ralf Barnes, Human Resources

(Timestamp: 24:30)

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER – INTRODUCTION OF RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING ADMINISTRATIVE FLOOD LEAVE FOR UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEES. IT
WAS BROUGHT FORWARD AFTER THE GOVERNOR SIGNED AN EXECUTIVE ORDER
SAYING THAT STATE EMPLOYEES COULD USE UP TO EIGHT HOURS OF PAID LEAVE
TO HELP WITH FLOOD MITIGATION IN THEIR COMMUNITIES.

RALPH BARNES, DIRECTOR OF HUMAN RESOURCES – STATED WHAT IS BEING
AUTHORIZED IS THE ABILITY OF A DEPARTMENT HEAD TO APPROVE AN EMPLOYEE
TO TAKE UP TO EIGHT HOURS OF ADMINISTRATIVE FLOOD LEAVE. UTAH COUNTY
EMPLOYS MANY PEOPLE WHO ARE WILLING TO HELP THEIR COMMUNITY. THIS
RESOLUTION WILL ALLOW EMPLOYEES TO USE THIS LEAVE TO ADMINISTER FLOOD
RELIEF. HOPEFULLY THE FLOODING WILL STOP BY LATE SUMMER BECAUSE THE
RESOLUTION EXPIRES AT THE END OF AUGUST. THIS LEAVE IS AVAILABLE NOW.

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH STATED THAT UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYS AROUND 1,350
EMPLOYEES AND IF EACH ONE CHOSE TO HELP, THIS WOULD ADD UP TO AROUND
450 DAYS THAT COULD BE DEVOTED TO FLOOD MITIGATION.
