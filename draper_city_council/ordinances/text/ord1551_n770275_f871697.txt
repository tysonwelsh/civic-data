City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On July 19, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1551, amending the
official Zoning Map of Draper City and approving a Development Agreement,
otherwise known as the Fitzgerald Property Zoning Map Amendment and
Development Agreement. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 20th day of July
2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
