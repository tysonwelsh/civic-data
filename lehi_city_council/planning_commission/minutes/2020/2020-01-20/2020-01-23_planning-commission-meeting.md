3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Method Studio’s request for review of the
Dancing Moose Montessori School concept plan, a commercial development located at 2700
North Ashton Blvd.

Chairman Hemmert asked if there was enough information for them to move forward.
applicant- Mike Sibi (?)t. It's a small family business and this is there third location. He believes
it's a great location for them, and it will be a much needed service in the community.
Distributed a flyer with more information about what they offer.

Commissioner Peterson stated that the architecture needs to meet the Development Code
standards.

Sam Kerlerman- they want to make sure the architecture meets what's in the area. they are using
high quality materials; they want to blend in and enhance the area and want to ensure that
happens. They don't feel comfortable with the glaze windows in the front of the building, so they
are asking for an exception to this. They want to ensure safety.

Commissioner Everett suggested adding glass gaze higher.

Commissioner Peterson asked if it would be possible to add some different architectural features
instead of a flat wall.

The applicant said that it isn't completely flat and there is some landscape around there as well.

Commissioner Ellis asked about hours for drop-off

The applicant discussed that they've done traffic studies. They discussed that it is not as much a
centralized drop off. They anticipate that during the peak time there would be short stays with
about 80 cars. There other locations have about 300 students, but this location will be a little
larger.

Commissioner Nielsen asked about deliveries. The applicant stated they will take place on
Wildflower. Commissioner Nielsen also asked about outdoor play areas. The applicant pointed
out on the map the areas that will have the playgrounds.

The applicant added that there will be a fence and plenty of trees.

Commissioner Bunker asked if there was a tree protection zone. Applicant said that there would
be a tree construction zone to make sure that doesn't happen.

Mike Washburn went on record to state that he does support this project. He feels that it is a
great addition to the area and better for the community then just another office building.

Chairman Hemmert opened public hearing for public comment.

Rob Ludlow is a huge fan of Dancing Moose. He does feel that it is a world class school. Feels
like they are adding a complimentary resource to help families.

Chairman Hemmert closed public comment.

Commissioner Eyre's only concern is that they are taking about park space and that we need
more park space.

Commissioner Bunker is not sure he is sold on the exception for the glass, but he does think that
it's great for the area, but Commissioner Ellis is okay with the exception. He still feels like it
needs to fit in to the area.

Commissioner Hereth passes the other location and feels like that it looks nice and that he feels
this one would be just as nice.

Commission Nielsen noted that they needed to continue working with staff on design standards.

Commissioner Everett felt concerned about the exception and wanted a number of how much for
the exception.

Motion: Commissioner Nielsen recommended approval of Method Studio’s request for review
of the Dancing Moose Montessori School concept plan; a commercial development located at
2700 North Ashton Blvd. Commissioner Peterson seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Ellis, Yes; Commissioner Everett, Yes; Commissioner Peterson, Yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, Yes; Commissioner Nielsen, Yes; Commissioner Bunker, Yes; and
Commissioner Hereth, Yes. The Motion passed with all in favor.

3.4 Public hearing and recommendation of Dan Ford’s request for review of the Pine
Springs Concept, a 36-unit residential development located at 225 South 600 East.

The Commission asked some clarify questions to staff.

Dan Ford stated that his intention was wanting to know if they prefer just townhomes or some
different product type. There already are some changes in the concept to help mitigate traffic.
He was more looking for feedback on what is preferred. He is great with either one they want to
make it work.

Chairman Hemmert opened public hearing for public comment.

Brent Thompson asked one question if there is some kind of middle ground where maybe there
isn't so many townhomes going in or if there are other amenities that could go in.

Randy Staples was curious what the perimeters would look like for security purposes and how
the aesthetics would be for both sides. Asked about access points.

Judy Evans is more in favor of the concept with the solid townhomes and not the apartments. She
hopes they won’t saturate the area.

Chairman Hemmert closed public comment.

Mr. Ford said the plan is to have the open street scape.

Commissioner Eyre asked about sidewalk and fencing. He feels like a sidewalk would improve
that and he is more in favor of a mixed product and would like density more towards 200 South.

Commissioner Nielsen asked for staff to address the sidewalk question and who would take care
of that. Mr. Dinsdale stated that they wouldn't require the developer to do that, but they will
work with the property owner to get sidewalk in place.


Commissioner Bunker asked Mr. Dinsdale to address traffic concerns. Mr. Dinsdale stated that
when they get to actual site plan to make sure all the road requirements are current. He said they
would likely widen the radius at 600 East. There are certain triggers they must meet before they
can add a signal.

Commissioner Everett asked about parking to Mr. Dinsdale and about red curbing that area. He
also asked what the standards are to do that. Mr. Dinsdale stated that the best way to solve the
parking problem is to make sure internally there is enough parking on the inside of the property.

Commissioner Ellis wanted to find a way to have more open space.

Commissioner Peterson agreed with Mr. Struthers that this just be townhomes since it's only a
three-acre parcel. Chairman Hemmert agreed because lot size uniformity is the best option.

Commissioner Nielsen agreed with Commissioner Ellis and Commissioner Eyre even on the
micro a mixed use would be good. He doesn't feel like they have the space to do the amenities
and the mixed use helps give the ability to go vertical and put in amenities. He thought that you
doing that will still have uniformity. Commissioner Nielsen agreed sidewalks will help, but
parking is a traffic calming measure. He likes the homes facing to 6oo East.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to send a positive recommendation of Dan Ford’s
request for review of the Pine Springs Concept, a 36-unit residential development located at 225
South 600 East. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Ellis, Yes; Commissioner Everett, No; Commissioner Peterson, Yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, Yes; Commissioner Nielsen, Yes; Commissioner Bunker, Yes; and
Commissioner Hereth, Yes. The Motion passed.
