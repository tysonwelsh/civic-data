                      COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

      RESOLUTION NO. 2022-12-01                                      DATE: December 21, 2022

                          A RESOLUTION AMENDING
                THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S 2022 BUDGET

                                           RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, on December 15, 2021, Copperton adopted a budget for the 2022 fiscal year;
and

       WHEREAS, Copperton desires to amend the 2022 budget; and

        WHEREAS, Copperton provided notice pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-3c-202 and 10-
6-113 concerning proposed amendments to the 2022 budget and of the date, time, and place of a
public hearing on the same; and

        WHEREAS, Copperton made the proposed amendments to the 2022 budget available for
public inspection for a period of at least ten (10) days prior to the public hearing in accordance
with Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-3c-202 10-6-112, 10-6-127(2)(b), and 10-6-128; and

     WHEREAS, on December 21, 2022, Copperton held the public hearing on the proposed
amendments to the 2022 budget in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-127(2)(b) and 10-6-
128.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

       1.      That the amendments to the 2022 budget attached to this resolution are adopted.

       2.     The Copperton budget officer is directed and authorized to file the Amended 2022
Budget with the Utah State Auditor within thirty (30) days of the date of this resolution pursuant
to Utah Code § 10-6-118.

       3.      This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption
