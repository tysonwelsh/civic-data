Zone Ogden Memo, including the Zone Ogden-Overlays Chapters

Presenter John Jensen and Delaney Sillman went through a chapter review of the Overlay Zones and
Historic Resources chapters for code noting it is still in the drafting phases. It is a very long code,
and they want to make sure they are hitting every point. Slides were shown as they listed numerous
chapters such as: Limited Use, Flood Hazard Overlay, Sensitive Lands, Airport Overlay, East Central
Overlay, 12° St Corridor, Live/Work (Transit) Overlay, Historic Review and General Provisions,
Designation of Historic Appropriateness, and Historic Rehabilitation and New Construction
Standards. They are making minor changes and modifications that can encompass multiple zones
and tackle certain issues, with the criteria essentially remaining the same.

Formula Based Businesses Discussion

Planning Director Barton Brierley said City Council requested Formula Based Businesses be
discussed and how formula/chain stores can take away from local business support having an
impact on the small business community. Being aware of that and limiting how many formula-
based businesses are allowed, per block for example, can help.
