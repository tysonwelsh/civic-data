 3. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Lamb opened the public comment period at 7:01 pm

     Comments:
     Amy Motta, volunteer with Bailing Out Benji, spoke of a lack of veterinary care in puppy mills.
     She asked that the Council consider passing a humane pet store ordinance to prevent stores
     from selling puppies sourced from puppy mills.

     Cheneil Hill, Taylorsville resident and founder of Stand Against Fear and Exploitation (SAFE)
     explained that SAFE was founded to protect communities from abuse and exposure from
     organizations that used status or faith as a shield from accountability. She said she would go
     from city to city until real change was made. Cheneil stated offender laws were too lenient in
     Utah, and urged the City Council to stand with SAFE and support stronger local protections and
     advocate for statewide reform.

     Chair Lamb closed public comments at 7:07 pm

       a. Resolution No. 25-022 the 2025-2029 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)
          Consolidated Plan and Program Year 2025 Annual Action Plan, and Authorizing the
          Mayor to Sign Associated Agreements
          Assistant Planner Megan Jensen introduced a five-year CDBG Consolidated Plan and 2025
          Annual Action Plan explaining edits to the Citizen Participation Plan. She reported that
          the Housing and Urban Development Department would announce allocations on May 14,
          2025 and she would come back to the Council with an amended, more detailed budget at
          that time. The following priorities were identified in the Consolidated Plan: (1) decent,
          safe affordable housing; (2) neighborhood improvements; (3) public services; (4)
          economic development.

           Council Member Bloom commented that no applications for downpayment assistance
           were received in FY2023 and asked if there had been barriers. Ms. Jensen responded that
           it has been difficult in the current market for people to qualify under HUD requirements.
           Council Member Bloom asked if there were goals to improve community outreach. Ms.

       d. Ordinance No. 25-17 a petition from Ryan Simmons/Boyer Company requesting a
          rezone for 190 Commerce located at 7381 West New Bingham Highway (~192.32
          acres) to M-1 Zone
          Senior Planner Tayler Jensen presented the request to rezone approximately 193 acres
          from A-20 to M-1 Zone. He said the future land use designation for the property was light
          industrial, and the proposed zone would be in line with the General Plan. Mr. Jensen noted
          that staff supported the proposal.

           Responding to a question from Vice Chair Whitelock, Mr. Jensen said the applicants were
           not currently aware of entities wanting to move to the property, but a previous similar
           project had filled up quite quickly. He said Phase 1 would begin as soon as possible.
           Council Member Green suggested it would make more sense for the subject property to
           be zoned South West Quadrant (SWQ). Mr. Jensen noted that the SWQ allowed for
           residential, and the utility plan would be significantly different. Council Member Bloom
           asked if recorded easements would limit the site layout or access. Mr. Jensen said
           easements would be addressed with platting.

       e. Ordinance No. 25-19 Amending Section 13-7E-10 (Status of Conditional Use Permit)
          to add revocation language
          City Planner/Zoning Administrator Larry Gardner introduced proposed amendments to
          Section 13-7E-10 to add revocation language for conditional use permits (CUP). He
          explained that under State law, CUPs stayed with the land, not the user. Mr. Gardner
          believed Code Enforcement would be able to be more ambitious in tracking down when
          uses on properties approved for conditional use permit had changed and conditional use
          permits could be revoked.

           Mr. Gardner answered questions from the Council. Council Member Shelton expressed the
           opinion that the proposed process would still be too cumbersome, and he did not want to
           tie the hands of property owners with unnecessary notifications.

           Chair Lamb opened a public hearing at 7:44 pm
           Comments:
           None
           Chair Lamb closed the public hearing at 7:45 pm

           Council Member Green liked the direction of the proposed amendments, but he found the
           ordinance to be confusing. He pointed out and made suggestions to improve formatting
           inconsistencies. Council Member Green would be comfortable approving the language as
           proposed but would want to work on improvements right away. Council Member Bloom
           felt the language was efficient and understandable. Council Member Shelton clarified that
           he believed the noticing would be too cumbersome for staff but was comfortable with
           approval.

           MOTION:      Chair Lamb moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 25-19 amending Section
                        13-7E-10 (Status of Conditional Use Permit) to add revocation
                        language.
                        Council Member Bedore seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Chad Lamb, Kayleen Whitelock, Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Kelvin
                      Green, Zach Jacob, Kent Shelton
           NO:
           ABSENT:
           The motion passed 7-0
