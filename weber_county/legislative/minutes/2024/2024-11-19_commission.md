    4. APPROVAL OF ABATING TAXES FOR DIFFERENCE IN WHAT WAS PAID TO OWING, PUT
       BACK ON GREENBELT AND ABATE ROLLBACK TAXES, FOR YEARS 2020 – 2024, OWNER-
       DIXIE BUNOT.

         DeeDee Kimber (Assessor’s Office): No changes from last week’s presentation. $79,846 (?) abated
         recommended.
         Commissioner Bolos: I appreciate the work done by the committee. To the point of whether we tax someone
         incorrectly, part of the discussion needs to be... I would like to go with the committee recommendation.
         Commissioner Froerer: Was this land in production the entire time?
         Ms. Kimber: Yes, it was in production the entire time.
         Commissioenr Forerer: Was the $64k still owed all in penalties?
         Ms. Kimber: No, most of it was taxes at the market rate.
         Commissioner Froerer: This is a difficult position to be in. There is the letter of the law and the spirit of the
         law. ... Basically intent versus reality of the law. It appears the property owner abided by the intent with land
         being in production but did not follow statute... The owner is in default ...did not make sure the County knew
         the land was in production. But I feel $64k is a bit extreme in this case. I am in favor of lowering the money
         owed but not all of it.

         Chair Harvey: I feel torn in half on this one. Commissioners, I see both of your perspectives.
         Dixey Bunot: The only other thing I didn’t understand was the ability to put the parcels back into green belt
         before... I am grateful for the direction to speak with John Ulibarri who helped... Thank you for following the
         mottos on your wall...

         Commissioner Froerer moved to abate taxes for difference in what was paid to owing, put back on greenbelt
         and abate rollback taxes, for years 2020 – 2024 in the amount of $32,415.46 or 50% of recommended
         amountxxx.xx, Owner- Dixie Bunot; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – nay; Commissioner Froerer – aye
