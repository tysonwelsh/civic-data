PRESENTATIONS:

1) GPZ-1-2025: Petition by Sam Drowning requesting a General Plan change from General
   Commercial and Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) to Medium Density
   Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) and a zone change from C-2 (General Commercial) and
   R-1-12 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 12,000 square feet) to RM
   (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is located at 3201 S Redwood Road,
   on 3.18 acres. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov.

   Steve Pastorik presented second plan revisions since the February 26, 2025 Public Hearing.
   The main changes included adding a sidewalk on the north side of the road, moving the front
   door of the units facing Lester Street to the side, and removing the wall on Lester Street. The
   concern was raised about the potential for the area to become more residential, which could
   lead to conflicts with commercial uses in the future noting that economics often dictate which
   properties are redeveloped.

   The Commission discussed the possibility of having commercial frontage on Redwood and
   residential areas behind it. However, there were concerns about the minimum lot size for
   commercial sites and the awkward shape of the proposed site. The Commission also

2) C-3-2025 Petition by Swenson Development LLC requesting a conditional use for a self-
   storage facility. The property is located at 4053 South 7200 West on 2.96 acres and is
   zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-
   ut.gov.

   Jody Knapp presented a self-storage facility application for a property next to the West Valley
   City Animal Shelter, which was previously rezoned from agricultural to manufacturing and is
   subject to a Development Agreement. The site layout has been adjusted from the rezone review
   due to severe slope issues, with buildings staggered to accommodate the terrain. The design
   includes multiple buildings of varying heights, with Building A acting as a wall along the north
   property line. Building D, facing the road, is a three-story structure with 582 storage units that
   must meet the commercial design standards. The fire department has raised concerns about
   internal grades and turning radii, which may require further adjustments to the site configuration
   during the plan review process.

3) Review of Light Rail Station Area Plan Scenarios

  The City has retained a team of consultants comprised Ryan Wallace from MHTN Architects,
  Christian Snow and Matt Sibul from WSP with their specialties in transportation. The men
  presented six scenarios for three light rail transit stations in West Valley City, being Decker
  Lake, Redwood Junction and River Trail. State law requires that the City have a stationary
  plan within the half mile area around the light rail stations.

4) 2025 Mandatory Training from Dereck Jensen from Legal Department

   The topics presented were Commission members are West Valley City employees, sexual
   harassment, hostile work environment, discrimination, IT security training, safety & worker’s
   compensation, ethics, fraud, and storm water.
