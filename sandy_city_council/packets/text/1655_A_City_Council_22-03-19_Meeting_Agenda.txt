Saturday, March 19, 2022                                    8:00 AM                  Loveland Living Planet Aquarium


                                        Council and Administration Retreat



The Sandy City Council and Administration will be conducting a joint retreat on
Saturday March 19, 2022
Location: Loveland Living Planet Aquarium, 12033 Lone Peak Pkwy, Draper, UT 84020 in the Island Room

After arriving at the venue, members of the public should follow the signs for Sandy City or let aquarium personnel
know that they are there to attend the Sandy City Retreat.

There will be a quorum of Council Members present during the retreat, however no final decisions will be made on
any Sandy City business.
