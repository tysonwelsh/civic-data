                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 21-30

  AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING UPDATED INSURANCE STUDY FOR SALT LAKE
  COUNTY, AS APPLIED TO RIVERTON CITY, DATED NOVEMBER 19TH 2021,
AMENDING PRVISIONS FLOOD DAMAGE PREVENTION MEASURES, ADOPTING
SUNDRY AMENDMENTS TO THE FLOOD DAMAMGE PREVENTION ORDINANCE,
    TITLE 15, CHAPTER 45 OF THE RIVERTON CITY CODE AND RELATED
                              MATTERS


       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required, under federal law, to adopt federally-approved
Flood Insurance Rate Maps adopted by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA),
which identify high, moderate, and low flood risk areas within Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, adoption of said Flood Insurance Rate Maps is integral to setting
appropriate flood insurance policies required of homes and businesses with mortgages from
federally insured lenders;

        WHEREAS, in September of 2009, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No.
09-12, thereby participating in the National Flood Insurance Program, adopting Flood Insurance
Rate Maps, and implementing enforcement measures codified into Title 15, chapter 45, Riverton
City Code; and

        WHEREAS, since adoption of Ordinance No. 09-12, FEMA has updated and re-issued
its Flood Insurance Rate Maps, thereby requiring an update to Title 15, chapter 45, Riverton City
Code;

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that, inasmuch as the City
is responsible for protecting the health, safety and welfare of its citizenry, it is also responsible
for complying with federal law in updating the FEMA-issued Flood Insurance Rate Maps and
related ordinances; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Staff has recommended to the City Council that it update its
regulations pertaining to the FEMA Flood Insurance Rate Maps to comply with federal law;


NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as follows:

       Section 1.      Riverton City Municipal Code, Title 15, Chapter 45, is hereby amended as
                       described in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by
                       reference. The City Recorder is authorized to make amendments to this
                       Ordinance which do not alter any terms of this Ordinance but facilitate
                       cohesion during the codification of this Ordinance.

       Section 2.      This ordinance shall become effective upon posting.
