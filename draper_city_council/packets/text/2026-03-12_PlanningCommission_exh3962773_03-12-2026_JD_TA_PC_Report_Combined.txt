BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Text Amendment to Table 9-10-090
DCMC. The purpose of the request is to allow private schools as a conditional use in all
residential zones.

The application has been submitted by a representative of the Skaggs Catholic Center, but
is intended to apply to any private school in any residential zone.

Table 9-10-1: Permitted and Conditional Uses in Residential Zones. Table 9-10-1 outlines all
uses permitted, conditional or not permitted in residential zoning districts. This includes
the following districts: RA1, RA2, RH, R3, R4, R5, RM1and RM2. The table currently has a
subsection of pubic and civic uses which include charter schools, higher education facilities
and public schools. These three uses are all permitted uses in all residential zoning
districts.

The applicant is seeking to add the use of private school and designate it as a conditional
use in all zoning districts. By adding it as a conditional use, a public process will be
required as part of the public hearing. This would ensure that issues typically related to
school operations such as access, traffic, and parking can be mitigated in some way. The
Planning Commission has the authority to review and decide on all conditional uses.

Criteria For Approval.
A Zoning Text Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City
Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation
to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in
DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(2):

2.      Text Amendments:
        a.    Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and
              policies of the city's general plan;
        b.    Whether a proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of
              the zoning ordinance;
        c.    Whether the proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the
              request and there is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning
              ordinance;
        d.    The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or
              part of this title or the general plan;
        e.    Whether the potential effects of the proposed amendment have been
              evaluated and determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or
              welfare and represents an overall community benefit; and
        f.    The extent to which a proposed text amendment implements best current,
              professional practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices
