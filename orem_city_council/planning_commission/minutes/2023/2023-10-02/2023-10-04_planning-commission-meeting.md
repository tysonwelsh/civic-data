Chair Komen asked Ms. Wagstaff to introduce Agenda Item 3.1: (click here for recording)

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Cody Black for the Planning Commission to approve the final plat for Central Park West and the conversion to office condominiums plat for Central Park East Condominiums located generally at 1875 South State Street in the C2 zone.

 Staff Presentation:  The applicant is proposing to convert an existing office building at 1835 South State Street into five (5) individual office condominium suites. This is the east property of the commercial complex and is currently a lot of record. The plat will be called Central Park East Condominiums. There will be no change to the existing structure, it will just be a conversion to office condominiums. They received a letter from the Chief Building Official for Orem stating the building had been inspected and no current life-safety items were found.  (22-16)

The property on the west side of the commercial complex fronts on State Street and is also currently a lot of record. There will be no change to this property, but because there is no street access to the east property, this west property must also be platted. This will provide an access easement to the east property. This plat will be called Central Park West. 

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the final plat for Central Park West and the conversion to office condominiums plat for Central Park East Condominiums located generally at 1875 South State Street in the C2 zone.

Chair Komen asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Wagstaff. Mr. Low asked if Central Park West would also be converted to condominiums. Mr. Washburn answered no, it would not as it is under different ownership.

Chair Komen invited the applicant to come forward. Paul Washburn introduced himself.

Chair Komen called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Low moved to approve the final plat for Central Park West and the conversion to office condominiums plat for Central Park East Condominiums located generally at 1875 South State Street in the C2 zone. Ms. Kleinlein seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mike Carpenter, Gerald Crismon, James Hawkes, Helena Kleinlein, Madeline Komen and Murray Low. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Komen asked Ms. Wagstaff to introduce Agenda Item 3.2: (click here for recording)

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Kirk Williamson for the Planning Commission to vacate Florence Village Condominiums, Phases 1, 2, and 3; and approve the final plats for Milano 5 Apartments, Milano 6 Apartments, and Milano 7 Apartments located generally at 920 West 1055 North in the R8 zone.  

 Staff Presentation:  There are three separate buildings, all of which are currently divided into condominiums. The applicant is requesting that each building be platted by itself as one plat. This will remove the condominium designation from these buildings, turning them into apartments to be rented rather than condominiums units that can be sold separately, however, condominium units can also be rented. No physical changes are proposed for the site.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Florence Village Condominiums, Phases 1, 2, and 3; and approve the final plats for Milano 5 Apartments, Milano 6 Apartments, and Milano 7 Apartments located generally at 920 West 1055 North in the R8 zone.

Chair Komen asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Wagstaff.  There was a discussion regarding all the parking being with one building. Mr. Bench said the parking is reciprocal and all would have access to the parking. If not, staff will make sure it was reciprocal before the plat is recorded.

Chair Komen asked Ms. Wagstaff to introduce Agenda Item 3.4: (click here for recording)

Agenda Item 3.4 is a request by Rachel Wilkerson, Wilkerson Farms for the Planning Commission to amend Article 22-2-1 Definitions and Appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting Standard Land Use Code 7313 – Agritainment as a permitted use in the OS5/ROS zone.

Staff Presentation:  The applicant is proposing to amend the ordinance to allow agritainment in the OS5/ROS zone. This is due to seasonal festivals and carnivals not currently being permitted. This text amendment is intended to bring the current land use – fall festival – into compliance with the land uses allowed within the OS5 zone while allowing the business to continue to operate. Adding “agritainment” as a permitted use will serve this purpose. 
Agritainment is defined as farm-related tourism or farm-related entertainment activities that are permitted or authorized in return for a fee on agricultural land for recreational or educational purposes. Examples of agritainment enterprises include, but are not limited to, pick-your-own fruits and vegetables, farm markets, festivals/fairs, interactive animal displays, hay and amusement rides and corn-maze enterprises. 
The above definition is how agritainment will be defined under Article 22-2-1 Definitions in the Orem City Code. While this use is being applied for by Wilkerson Farms, it will apply to any area which is zoned OS5 as a permitted use.
?

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission amend Article 22-2-1 Definitions and Appendix A of the Orem City Code by enacting Standard Land Use Code 7313 – Agritainment as a permitted use in the OS5/ROS zone.

Chair Komen asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Wagstaff. Mr. Low asked if both agriculture and agritainment are allowed on this property and the OS5 zone. Ms. Wagstaff replied that yes, they would both be allowed. A discussion followed regarding farming and how the culture is changing with people eating out more and not cooking as much as they used to. Farmers are having to expand the business. Wilkerson Farm is open for forty days each fall for the corn maze, hay rides, carnival rides and pumpkin patch.
