                            MEMORANDUM
To:             City Recorder Office

From:           Planning Department

Date:           September 25, 2020

Subject:        June 9, 2020 Planning Commission Work Session


The Planning Commission held a work session on June 9, which was properly noticed and posted.
The work session was to discuss the proposed City-wide amendment to the General Plan Land
Use Map. At that meeting, the Commission reviewed and discussed the proposed amendments
to the Plan, and made final suggestions to staff in preparing the draft which would be presented
in the subsequent public hearing. The meeting was open, but no public comments were taken or
offered. All reviewed and discussed information was included in the public draft of the General
Plan Land Use Map and the presentation during subsequent public hearings.
