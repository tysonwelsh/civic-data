City Council Meeting Minutes April 19, 2022
Council Member Business
Council Member Zach Robinson requested an update on the public outreach for the Alta
Canyon Recreation Center proposals. Kim Bell provided some information.
Council Member Alison Stroud thanked Dan Medina and the Parks Department for their
maintenance and upkeep of our city parks and pickleball courts. She thanked Police for a
recent ride-a-long. She also acknowledged the Council staff and City staff for their help
with her school's recent field trip to City Hall.
Council Member Scott Earl spoke about the Dimple Dell Cleanup event this Saturday. All
are welcome to come out and help with this effort.
Council Member Brooke DeSousa provided an update from the Sandy Club board meeting
and spoke about the redistricting outreach pop-up event that she attended.
Council Member Cyndi Sharkey spoke about one of the redistricting pop-up events that
she attended and she thanked the Council Office staff for their efforts and expressed her
appreciation.
Council Member Marci Houseman also thanked Council staff for their efforts and attention
to detail. She also thanked Britney Ward and Amy Bryant for their help with a resident's
concern.
Mr. Tracy Cowdell, Council Attorney, expressed sadness and heartfelt condolences to
the family of the young man who lost his life while crossing the trax station. Mr. Cowdell
thanked the Canyons School District for their partnership and community outreach.
Mayor's Report
Ms. Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor, on behalf of the Mayor, expressed our thoughts and prayers
for the family of today's tragic accident.
She thanked Canyons School District for the recent Gala and thanked the Council for
hosting the custodial staff at dinner.
CAO Report
Cliff Strachan CAO, spoke about the UCMA meeting in St. George that he recently
attended. The Planning Commission will begin holding hybrid meetings and he
acknowledged the Public Utilities department staff for their efforts on the street light
program. He expressed Administration's commitment and assistance with outreach
efforts for the Alta Canyon Recreation Center.
Adjournment
Council unanimously agreed to adjorun the meeting at 6:28 pm.
