                                   RESOLUTION NO. 22-66

       A RESOLUTION APPOINTING EMILY DZIATLIK AS A REGULAR MEMBER
       OF THE DRAPER CITY SPECIAL EVENT ARENA COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies with the city as
deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are appointed
by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, Emily Dziatlik has expressed a willingness to serve as a member of the
Draper City Special Event Arena Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor recommended Emily Dziatlik to serve as a member of the Draper
City Special Event Arena Committee.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s appointment of
Emily Dziatlik to serve as a member of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

        Section 1. Appointment. Emily Dziatlik is hereby appointed to serve as a member of
the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee according to the laws, ordinances, and
regulations governing the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee and its members for the
term specified herein.

       Regular Member:
             Emily Dziatlik           December 13, 2022 through December 31, 2026

        Section 2. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 13TH DAY OF DECEMBER 2022.
