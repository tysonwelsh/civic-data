MAGNA CITY, UTAH
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-10-01
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL APPROVING AND
ADOPTING THE 2025 MAGNA CITY COUNCIL
REGULAR MEETING SCHEDULE
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Magna City (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-1-201.5 et
seq., and
WHEREAS, the Magna City Council (“Council”) is the municipal governing body for Magna
pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-3b-401 et seq; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act (the “Act”), the Council must
adopt and post an annual regular meeting schedule of the Council; and
WHEREAS, the 2025 Magna City Council Regular Meeting Schedule, hereinafter known as
Attachment “A” fulfills the requirements of the Act;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Magna City Council, that the Council approves and
adopts the attached 2025 Magna City Council Regular Meeting Schedule, hereinafter known as
Attachment “A” to this resolution, for the benefit of Magna City.
APPROVED and ADOPTED in Magna City, Salt Lake County, S$ f Utah on this 22" day of
October, 2024.
