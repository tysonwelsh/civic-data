CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3658, A RESOLUTION ADOPTING
WASHINGTON COUNTY’S CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE FOR ALL DISTRICTS
THAT LEVY A TAX

Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan explained that for new districts, including Infrastructure Financing
Districts, Public Infrastructure Districts, Tax Increment Financing Districts or Local Districts, it
creates work for multiple departments throughout the County, and it is recommended to update
the fee schedule to compensate the work done by each department. Matt Ence with Snow, Jensen,
and Reece asked the Commissioners to consider a one-time adjustment for the ZRMC PID to be
under the old fee schedule, as the PID is almost completed, and his clients did not anticipate the
new fee schedule.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3658, A
Resolution Adopting Washington County’s Consolidated Fee Schedule for all
Districts that Levy a Tax with the Exception of the ZRMC PID. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

12/26/2025 @ 1659, Injured Hiker, Red Mountain Trail, Red Cliffs Desert Reserve — Man
Hours — 36 (Non Certified) 2 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a female who had a sustained a reported
broken arm after falling on Red Mountain Trail. The female was located by SAR personnel and her
injury was stabilized. Due to the steep terrain, STAR was requested to assist with returning her to
Incident Command (IC). STAR9 utilized their hoist and returned the patient to IC without issue.
12/29/2025 @ 1744, Missing Person, St. George Area — Man Hours — 38 (Non Certified) 3
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to the south east section of St. George near the St. George
Air Port (SGU) to assist with locating a missing female that was suspected to be in the area. Multiple
groups had searched the area and were unable to locate her. SAR members were able to find a set of
tracks at the last known location of the female and tracked her for approximately 30 minutes before
they located her. She was then loaded in a vehicle and returned to the SGPD incident command where
she was evaluated in an ambulance and turned over to SGPD.

Additional Calls

11/26/2025 @1312, Injured Climber, Mo’s Valley — Man Hours — 19.5 (Non Certified) 3
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a male who had fallen while climbing in
Mo’s Valley and suffered a suspected leg injury. The male was located and evaluated. He was then
transported in litter basket to the trailhead where he was loaded in the ambulance and transported to the
hospital.

11/26/2025 @ 1540, Injured Hiker, Cottonwood Trail — Man Hours — 16.5 (Non Certified)
2 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Cottonwood Trail on a report of a female hiker who had
fallen and had a suspected ankle injury. STAR9 was requested due to the remote area she was located
in. SAR units responded to the location of the patient and her injury was evaluated and stabilized. She
was loaded in STAR9 and returned to Incident Command. She was then loaded in an ambulance and
transported to the hospital.

11/26/2025 @1713, Dirt Bike Accident, Warner Valley — Man Hours — 1 (Certified)

Deputies responded to Warner Valley on a report of a juvenile male who had sustained possible
ankle and rib injuries after crashing on his dirt bike. STAR9 was in the area and assisted with the call.
Deputies arrived and assisted with patient care until Hurricane Medical units arrived on scene. The
male was then loaded in an ambulance and transported to the hospital.

11/27/2025 @1657, Stranded Motorist, Kolob Reservoir— Man Hours — 2 (Certified)

Deputies responded to the Kolob Reservoir area on a report of a male who had been stranded
when his vehicle became stuck in a snow drift. Deputies assisted him with removing the vehicle from
the snow drift and followed him until he returned to the pavement.

12/08/2025 @1348, Stranded Hiker, Yant Flats — Man Hours — 12 (Non Certified) 2
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Yant Flats Trail on a report of an elderly female who
was stuck in a ravine and unable to get out. SAR members hiked to the patient location and utilized
ropes to remove her from the ravine. They then transported her back to her vehicle at the trailhead
where she declined medical evaluation.
12/16/2025 @1051, Stranded Hiker, Warner Valley —- Man Hours — 2.5 (Certified)

get off. STAR9 utilized their hoist to safely return the male to his vehicle. He declined medical

evaluation.
