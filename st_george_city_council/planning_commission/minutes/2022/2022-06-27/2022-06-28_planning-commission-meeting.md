         Consider a request for a Hillside Development Permit at the Commerce Point Development. The
         applicant is proposing to construct in the area shown on the slope map labeled 20-30%, 30-40% and
         40% and above. The property is located at Southeast quadrant of the intersection of Auto Mall Drive
         and Blackridge Drive. The property is currently zoned Highway Commercial (C-2). The applicant is
         Commerce Point, LC. Case No. 2022-HS-005. (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

         Wes Jenkins presented the following:

         Wes Jenkins – We met out there and they felt like the property that was in the red area was all man
         made and it could be disturbed. They had some concerns about the grading and made some
         recommendations and had them come back. One of the main concerns for the applicant was to get a
         mass grading to make the site look ready and nicer to get development there. The Hillside Review
         Board had concerns about them disturbing it and leaving it then it not being developed for 10, 15, 20
         years you’re just leaving it in a condition that is maybe not aesthetically pleasing and so there was a
         fair amount of discussion about that. In the end the developer indicated they were really after
         disturbing a portion of the site and the rest was to be in the future. A lot of the discussion was that
         portions of the property that are taller than 10 ft be mitigated with decorative landscaping, any slopes
         that are 2:1 or steeper than 3:1 being consistent with if they are not going to do walls to do some
         kind of rock to dress them up. There was some discussion that some of that may be behind buildings
         and may not be as visible, that was a recommendation. The applicant indicated that the area further
         south toward Black Ridge Drive would be left alone until they have further interest in the property.

         Commissioner Kemp – This project has been around for 20 years, there is an access at the top of
         Black Ridge Drive, will that be abandoned?
