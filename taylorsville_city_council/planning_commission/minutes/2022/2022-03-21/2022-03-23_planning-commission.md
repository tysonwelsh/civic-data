3.1         Ms. Karyn Kerdolff            presented this item.     The applicant, Meriam Grando, is requesting a non-
            administrative conditional use permit (CUP) to operate a Reception Center at 3193 West 4700 South and
            also an hours of operation extension to stay open until 1:00 a.m. The applicant would like to host non -
            alcohol receptions. The applicant’s CUP application includes the following, “A company that rents space
            for family events such as birthdays, baptisms, children’s parties, and expositions.”


            3.1.1      The property is in the professional office (PO) zone and per the Land Use Table ( 13.08.020) a
                       Reception Center requires a Non-Administrative Conditional Use permit granted by the Planning
                       Commission.


            3.1.2      The property is located within 150’ from a residential zone so in addition to the Reception Center
                       CUP, the applicant is requesting an hours of operation extension per Taylorsville Municipal code
                       13.23.200, which says “ Any commercial use located within two hundred fifty feet (250’) of a
                       residential district where such commercial use desires to operate after ten o’clock (10:00) P.M.
                       and/ or before six o’clock 96:00) A.M. shall require separate conditional use approval from the
                       Planning Commission”.


3.2         FINDINGS:
            3.2.1      This application was initiated by Miriam Grando.
            3.2.2      Per 13.08.020, a Reception Center in the PO zone requires a Non-Administrative Conditional Use
                       Permit granted by the Planning Commission.
            3.2.3      The applicant is requesting to stay open until 1:00 A.M. on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday nights.
            3.2.4      The property is located within 250’ of a residential zone which requires an hours of operation
                       extension granted by the Planning Commission.
            3.2.5      The applicant will not be serving alcohol and is not requesting an alcohol permit at this time.
            3.2.6      Conditional Use Permits are governed by Utah State Code and must meet the provisions outlined
                       in 10-9a-507.
            3.2.7      A similar request for a Reception Center was approved at this location by the Planning Commission
                       on November 13, 2018.


3.3         STAFF RECOMMENDATION:            Staff recommends approval of this application based on the information
            presented at the time of the report.


3.4         CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:
            3.4.1      The applicant complies with all requirements of all applicable reviewing agencies.
            3.4.2      The Conditional Use Permit is subject to review and revocation upon substantiated complaint or
                       citation.
            3.4.3      Any changes to the structure will require a building permit.
            3.4.4      The applicant shall remain complaint with all business licensing requirements.
            3.4.5      The business may only extend hours of operation to 1:00 A.M. on Thursday, Friday, and Saturday
                       nights. Otherwise, the business hours are limited to 10:00 P.M.
            3.4.6      Alcohol is not allowed on the premise without an alcohol license issued by the City of Taylorsville
                       and the DABC. If alcohol is found on the premise, the CUP will be revoked.
            3.4.7      All parking must be on site unless a shared parking agreement is documented with the City.
            3.4.8      The business must conform to Salt Lake County’s Noise Ordinance requirements.

  4.          7S21 – Consideration of a Plat Amendment to Lot 28 of the Heatherglen Subdivision Located at 4448-
              4450 South Edgeware Lane, Taylorsville, Utah. (Karyn Kerdolff/ GIS Planner)


4.1         Ms. Kerdolff presented this item.           Noradene Sorensen and Charles Dennis are requesting to amend
            the subdivision plat for their properties at 4448 (Lot 28 -A) and 4450 (Lot 28-B) South Edgeware Lane.
            These properties are located within the Heatherglen neighborhood and carry a unique set of conditions
            which the applicants wish to remove from their properties by recording a new plat.


            4.1.1      In 1978, a standard subdivision plat was recorded, however, in 1979 the plat was re -recorded as
                       a condominium plat. When the plat was re-recorded as a condominium plat, provisions were put
                       on the properties including complying with the Utah Condominium Ownership Act and
                       identifying the property as private, limited common and common areas. The original
                       condominium plat that was recorded identifies the area outside of the dwelling units as common
                       space, therefore, legally making the property accessible to both homeowners. Further
                       complicating the matter, is the fact that each lot is recorded as a phase of the condominium
                       project and each lot contains two units that carry their own homeowner’s association, a practice
                       that is uncommon. Many homeowners in the Heatherglen neighborhood are not aware of the
                       unique circumstances tied to their property until they try to sell or refinance the property.


            4.1.2      Due to these circumstances, Taylorsville City rezoned the Heatherglen neighborhood from A-1 to
                       R-2-10 (residential two units per 10,000 square feet) in an effort to restructure the land use so
                       that potential buyers or sellers could get financing. Additionally, the zone change allowed
                       homeowners to record a new subdivision plat that would supersede the original condominium
                       plat that was recorded in 1979 condominium plat. The General Plan designation is Medium
                       Density.

4.1                    Mr. McGrath presented this item. He gave a very informative Power Point presentation covering

            back to mid-summer sometime.          The State of Utah has required elements be included on land use,

            chapter and summarized them during his presentation.

4.2         Mr. McGrath answered Commissioner’s questions relating to changing out outdated street lighting,
            reviewed population and household size figures and the severe housing shortage in the valley. He advised
            it is time for all cities to get ahead of this problem or the State of Utah will take away control from the cities
            if it isn’t done. That may include high rise buildings in order to accommodate that, but Mr. McGrath said
            the City would be considerate of how high they are going to be built in order to maintain the integrity of
            the neighborhoods at the same time. He then said that it is important to upgrade all shopping centers.
            That small area master plans are needed for: 4800 S Redwood, 5400 S Redwood, 5400 S Bangerter, 4700
            South, I-215 as well as others. Commissioners wanted to make sure that natural areas are incorporated
            into future changes as well and efforts made to ensure water conversancy projects are included.
