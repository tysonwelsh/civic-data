                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-41

A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE REVISED 2025 WATER CONSERVATION PLAN

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 73-10-32, Riverton City is required to
establish a document known as a water conservation plan that contains existing and
proposed water conservation measures describing what will be done by a water provider,
and the end user of culinary water to help conserve water in the state; and.

      WHEREAS, on March 16, 1999, the Riverton City Council a water conservation
and management plan which featured a goal of reducing its per capita culinary water
consumption by half; and

       WHEREAS, since adoption of its original water conservation plan Riverton City has
exceeded its goal for reducing water consumption resulting in a decrease from 998 gallons per
day per connection in the year 2000 to 403 gallons per day per connection by the year 2013; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 73-10-32 further requires that the City update is water
conservation plan every five years; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the time is right for
renewing its water conservation plan; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a revised water
conservation plan presented by staff has been brought before the Council following a public
hearing, and pursuant to public notice as required by law; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the new goals set by
the water conservation plan will protect the health and safety of our community by continuing to
make water resources available for future use through more efficient means of delivery and
consumption of the same;

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, State
of Utah, as follows:

       1. That Riverton City approves the revised 2025 update to the Riverton City
          Water Conservation Pan, as attached hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
