A. CONSIDER AMENDING CHAPTER 17-7-9, CHAPTER 17-7-9.12.1,
CHAPTER 17-7-9.12.2, AND CHAPTER 17-7-9.12.3 TO ADDRESS
_ RIGHT-OF-WAY CONCERNS WITH THE LOADING AND UNLOADING
OF OVERSIZED AND COMMERCIAL VEHICLES
Hannah Tyler, Planning Consultant, said the proposed Zoning Ordinance Text
Amendment included herein intends to address concerns regarding the loading and
unloading of oversized and commercial vehicles within the Public Right-of-Way in the
Brigham Junction, Riverwalk, The Junction at Midvale, and Silver Refinery Overlay
Zones. The intent of the proposed changes is to create criteria and requirements for on
street and off-street loading areas in the subject Zones to ensure safe and orderly loading
and unloading of oversized and commercial vehicles.
The proposed text amendments will address the following:
¢ Establish requirements for off-street loading areas, including:
oO Location within the site (setbacks, etc.), and
oO Screening requirements.
¢ Establish requirements for on-street loading that will address the following:
Oo Location of on-street loading areas;
oO Impediment of vehicle travel lanes:
o Dimensions of on-street parking areas;
O Vehicle size restrictions:
o Driveway and intersection distance requirements; and,
o Establish review requirements by the City Engineer.

Adam Olsen said this request for reconsideration is made from The Gardner Company.
Representatives from The Gardner Company were unable to be at the December /th
meeting, where the Council approved an amendment to Chapter 17-7-10.12 of the
Midvale Municipal Code, allowing non-obscuring, open fencing around solar energy
systems; a prohibition added for chain-link material. The representatives would like to
address the Council on this added prohibition; clarifying or expanding on what exactly
— should be prohibited. Included in the report materials are exhibits from the representatives
for their desired fencing materials, as well as the draft ordinance approved at the
December 7th meeting. The body of the original report is contained below.
The City proposes an amendment to Chapter 17-7-10.12 (Jordan Bluffs Subareas 1-3) of
the Municipal Code. The requested amendment would allow non-obscuring, open fencing
around solar energy systems located within open space areas of Jordan Bluffs Subareas
1-3. Any fencing would be limited to 7’ in height.
Public notice has been sent to all affected entities as required in 17-3-9.B of the Municipal
Code. No objections have been received as of the writing of this report.
The Planning Commission reviewed the request at their November 10, 2021 meeting and
recommended approval of the amendment to the City Council.

COMPLETION AND RECEIPT OF INDEPENDENT AUDIT
Mariah Hill said an annual financial audit is required of all municipalities who have
revenues or expenditures of $1,000,000 or more. The audit must be submitted to the
State Auditor's Office by December 31 of the year. The independent auditor is responsible
for reporting whether or not the governing body’s financial statements are prepared in
conformity with generally accepted accounting principles. This report will be presented by
Jon Haderlie of Larson & Company, PC. The report has been submitted to the State
Auditor's Office in order to meet state mandated deadlines and will be available to the
public. An electronic version of the Annual Financial Report will be sent to the Council
prior to the meeting. A paper copy may be provided to the Mayor and City Council if
requested. An electronic version of the Annual Financial Report will be posted on the
City's website shortly after acceptance by the Council. As required in Utah Code section
— 10-6-152, notice will be given to the public that the audit has been completed and
available for inspection.
