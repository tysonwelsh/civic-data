                      Riverton High School Silver Rush Season 2025

 WHEREAS, Riverton High School has established the long-standing tradition of uniting
 and inspiring students and the surrounding community through, civic responsibility,
 social consciousness, and a vibrant spirit of service; and

 WHEREAS, The compassion, enthusiasm, dedication, and leadership of Riverton High
 School Students helps drive the success of Silver Rush, along with ingenious activities,
 community events, Silver Rush Boutique, "odd jobs", donations, and many more.

 WHEREAS, This sincere focus has elevated Silver Rush as one of the biggest fundraising
 programs of its kind; and

 WHEREAS, Silver Rush is recognized for its significant financial support to local charities
 that impact our community. However, it is appreciated even more for the personal
 change it makes in people's lives, including the lives of the students who serve by
 uniting as a force for good; and

 WHEREAS, Silver Rush is set to begin once again with Riverton City wholeheartedly
 supporting the efforts of Riverton High School students who endeavor to make an
 impact on the quality of life for others.

 NOW THEREFORE, I, Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton City, do hereby proclaim December
 1, 2025 through December 19, 2025, as "Riverton High School Silver Rush Season" and
 encourage residents within Riverton High Schools boundaries to support this
 commendable event.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 1st day of December 2025.
