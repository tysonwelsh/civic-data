3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Jared Osmond’s request for review of the Lehi
Pioneer Plaza Zone Change on approximately .09 acres of property located at
approximately 1000 South 1300 West from R-1-22 (residential/agriculture) to Commercial

Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Matt Brown with MW Brown Engineering, was present. They would like to move
forward with the office building. He does not have an answer for the little sliver piece of
property but will have a boundary survey done. The property that has been purchased includes
the zone change the applicant has requested. There will be a trail connection with the zone

had concerns with buffering.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommend approval of Jared Osmond’s
        request for review of the Lehi Pioneer Plaza Zone Change on approximately .09
        acres of property located at approximately 1000 South 1300 West from R-1-22
        (residential/agriculture) to Commercial zoning, including all findings stated in the
        report. Commissioner Bunker seconded.
        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to the General Plan Land Use Map and a Zone Change on approximately 1
acre of property located at approximately 975 South and 1350 West from a VLDRA (very
low density residential agricultural) and R-1-22 zoning to an Open Space land use
designation and Public Facility zoning.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Lehi City has done public re-zones in the past. When the concept plan was reviewed, there were
some buffering concerns, and it does make sense to clean up the area while it is being done.

Commission Chair Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval Lehi City’s request for
        review of an amendment to the General Plan Land Use Map and a Zone Change on
        approximately 1 acre of property located at approximately 975 South and 1350
        West from a VLDRA (very low density residential agricultural) and R-1-22 zoning
        to an Open Space land use designation and Public Facility zoning, including all DRC
        comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Mary Kelly’s request for review of
amendments to Table 05.030B, Chapter 21 & Chapter 39, allowing Dog Care Facilities as a
conditional use in the Neighborhood Commercial Zone and adding Mobile Personal
Services and some additional regulations to Temporary Uses and adding a definition for
Mobile Personal Services.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Mary, and Lee Kelly, were present. They would like all the dog care services
together. The mobile service would be on the property, not a public access, but a temporary use
is not the main use.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Scott Carlson wanted to clarify where the mobile service could park and what type of surface it
could park on.

Robert McFadden wanted to clarify that he is looking at purchasing the one acre property. His
intent is to build a boarding facility. The potential noise would be during the day, not at night. He
would like to partner with a vet, a grooming facility, and a boarding facility.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.
Commission Chair Newall asked if this addresses the concern of a commercial use and if the
noise ordinance still applies. Commissioner Everett is concerned with the operational use of a
mobile service and the hours permitted, and the intent. Craig Chambers stated the least restrictive
would take precedent.

The applicant stated they are happy to work with DRC on the wording of the mobile service.
They would like to work on site and would like the boarding.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to provide a positive recommendation of Mary
        Kelly’s request for review of amendments to Table 05.030B, Chapter 21 & Chapter
        39, allowing Dog Care Facilities as a conditional use in the Neighborhood
        Commercial Zone and adding Mobile Personal Services and some additional
        regulations to Temporary Uses and adding a definition for Mobile Personal
        Services, but in the interim recommend Staff to review mobile services and address hours
        of operations that add consistency with a separate request with written permission, not
        approving or denying, with this type of use in Neighborhood Commercial. including all
        DRC comments and findings stated in the report. consistent with what written
        permission. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3.7) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapter 37, Design Standards, changing the proximity
allowance for the Downtown Spacing Overlay Zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Chair Newall opened the public hearing. There was a comment online that was
opposed with concerns of traffic. Commissioner Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to table Lehi City’s request for review of a
        Development Code Amendment to Chapter 37, Design Standards, changing the
        proximity allowance for the Downtown Spacing Overlay Zone until staff has the
        information to research and seek data where other municipalities has added density to
        consider measurement by property line, by footprint of the building and distances, and
        the maximum number of multinumber units of the area. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
