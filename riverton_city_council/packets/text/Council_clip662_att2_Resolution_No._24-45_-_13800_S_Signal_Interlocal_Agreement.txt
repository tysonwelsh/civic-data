                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-45

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
   AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT LAKE COUNTY AND RIVERTON CITY FOR
  TRAFFIC SIGNAL INSTALLATION AT 13800 SOUTH SENTINEL RIDGE BLVD

       WHEREAS, Riverton City finds the need for a traffic signal to be located at 13800
South Sentinel Ridge Blvd (4500 West); and

       WHEREAS, the traffic signal is necessary for public safety for vehicular traffic and
pedestrians; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Interlocal
Agreement has been prepared for approval and execution by and between Salt Lake County and
Riverton City, which states the rights, duties, responsibilities, and obligations of the parties
described in this Resolution.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Salt Lake
          County, in substantially similar form to the agreement attached hereto, upon
          verification that the agreement has been approved as to legal form by the City
          Attorney.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 7th day of
May, 2024 by the following vote:
