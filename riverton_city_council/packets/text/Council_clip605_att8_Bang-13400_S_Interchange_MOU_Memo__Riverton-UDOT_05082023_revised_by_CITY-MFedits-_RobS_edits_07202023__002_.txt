                        MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Date:                 July 20, 2023

To:                   Trent Staggs, Riverton City – Mayor

From:                 Marwan Farah, UDOT - Bangerter Highway Project Director

Subject:              Project No.: S-0154(250)4 – Bangerter Highway South Interchanges
                      Location: 13400 South and Bangerter Highway

 The purpose of this Memorandum of Understanding is to document verbal agreements made between
 Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) and Riverton City (CITY). UDOT is preparing to design and
 construct a highway project identified as Project Number S-0154(250)4 (Project). This Project includes the
 construction of a grade separated interchange with Bangerter Highway going either over or under 13400
 South, in Riverton City (CITY), as shown in the State Environmental Study (SES). The CITY has
 requested that UDOT construct the grade separated interchange with Bangerter Highway going under
 13400 South. A grade separated interchange which runs under 13400 South results in a differential cost
 of about $20,000,000. UDOT and CITY mutually agreed for CITY to contribute $4,000,000.00 to help
 offset the cost to UDOT. This amount is consistent with the agreement with South Jordan that had a
 similar request for the 9800 South interchange. Further CITY agreed to look for opportunities in good
 faith to help offset the additional costs to UDOT. As UDOT continues to move forward with the design
 plans for this project, UDOT and CITY agree that:

        The $4,000,000 contribution has been agree do as follows:
             o CITY will pay $1,000,000.00 directly. CITY will pay $500,000 to UDOT by August 30,
                 2023. CITY will pay the remaining $500,000 to UDOT by December 29, 2023.
             o UDOT will transfer to the project funds $2,000,000.00 from the First Class Highway
                 Fund pursuant to Senate Bill 2, adopted by the 2023 session of the Utah Legislature.
             o CITY has negotiated with Suburban Land Reserve (SLR) to transfer to UDOT by
                 donation parcel 115 located on the south-west quadrant, which has been appraised by
                 UDOT to be approximately $1,000,000. If donation fails, UDOT will purchase said
                 parcel from SLR according to normal requirements; and CITY will negotiate with SLR
                 to transfer those funds to CITY (less and required taxes), which CITY will immediately
                 pay to UDOT. If both donation and purchase fail, UDOT and CITY will mutually agree
                 to another method to add $1,000,000 to Project.
 In addition to the above UDOT and CITY agree that:
      CITY allows UDOT to close the east/west traffic movements of 13400 South access, anticipated
         to last between 9 to 12 months (as indicated in the final SES), for the construction of the new
         interchange.
      CITY will pay 100% of design costs only for the CITY’s water lines impacted by this request.
         UDOT will coordinate and include all other utilities costs impacted by this request, in the Project.
      UDOT will deliver the main-span steel box structure from the existing 13400 S bridge (shown in
         EXHIBIT – B) located near Bangerter Highway to a location determined by the CITY when the
         structure is removed from its existing location. If the pedestrian structure at 2700 W or 9800 S
         is determined by the CITY to be more suitable to their needs, UDOT will deliver one of those
         instead. CITY will provide two parcels (shown in EXHIBIT – A) already identified as necessary
         for the project as consideration for the structure.
