Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion Items

    2.1 Discussion of updates to the Moderate Income Housing Element of the General Plan.

     Brittany Harris presented updates on demand and supply for affordable housing that pertained
    to the general plan.

    2.2 Discussion of possible regulations to allow accessory dwelling units in accessory
    structures.


    Sierra Pierson discussed the ADU regulations and if there were any changes the
    Commissioners felt would be necessary to mitigate parking issues and concerns. There was
    discussion on the limitations of unit rentals and if other cities have enforced that. Mike West
    stated it was more of a CCNR code to be enforced.

    2.3) Discuss Planning Commissioner presentations.

    The Commissioners liked the idea of providing presentation to get to know each other’s
    backgrounds better.

3. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Bunker moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
