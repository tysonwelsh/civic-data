           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Formal Bid 22-007 was posted from

           awarding the bid to Specialized Communication Services, LLC in the amount of
           $58,094.40.

      c. Consider approval to award bid to Bud Mahas Construction for the St.
         George Golf Clubhouse remodel.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Bids were opened for the St. George Golf
           Clubhouse on 3/8/22. The bids were as follows: Bud Mahas Construction
           $1,484,000; Watts Construction $1,485,256; and Grass Creek Construction
           $1,504,991. The tight bid grouping would indicate that the bids are in line with
           actual costs. The budget amount for this project is $1.3 million and was
           estimated in February, 2021. The construction amount in the bids does not
           include architect fees ($32,500), soils testing ($7,000) and FF&E (estimated
           $50,000). Since this project will span two budget years, the difference will need
           to be allocated in FY23 to cover the overage in construction costs. Staff
           recommends the council award the bid to Bud Mahas in the amount of
           $1,484,000 in the FY22 Budget.

      d. Consider approval to award bid to Aquify Systems Corp. for the Sand
         Hollow Aquatic Center leisure pool regenerative media water filter
         system.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This bid is for the replacement filtration
           system for the Sand Hollow Aquatic Center's leisure pool. The system uses a
           regenerative media filter. This was the lowest of three (3) public bids. The
           original bid for both the leisure pool and the lap pool came in at $437,178.00.
           The budgeted amount for the project was only $275,000. Staff recommends
           awarding the bid to Aquify Systems Corp. in the amount of $229,178 for the
           leisure pool only. The lap pool’s filtration system will be added to the FY23 draft
           budget.

      e. Consider approval to award bid to Western Rock for the Asphalt Patching
         Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Only one (1) bid was received this year.
           The bid amounts are similar to the previous bid in 2020 that was awarded to
           Western Rock which had a total of three (3) bids. Staff recommends awarding
           the bid to Western Rock in the amount of $349,100.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City has stockpiled approximately
           20,000 tons of asphalt and concrete chunks that will be crushed into road base

      g. Consider approval of FAA Grant AIP-42 Concessions Rent Relief Airport
         Rescue.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This grant was announced by the
           Secretary of Transportation on June 22, 2021. The St George Regional Airport is
           eligible for funds under the American Rescue Plan Act 2021 (Public Law 117-2)
           (ARPA). The purpose of these funds is to provide economic relief to airports in
           response to the COVID-19 pandemic. This money is for relief of concessions at
           airports. This grant will include three rental car companies: Hertz, Enterprise,
           and Budget/Avis. The FAA will distribute these grants under the new Airport
           Rescue Grant Program (ARGP). There is no financial obligation from the City.
           Staff recommends approval.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The ridgeline on the Foremaster Ridge

      no structure may be constructed within 30' of the abrupt edge, but the applicant's
      surveyor was unable to determine where the abrupt edge was located. The Hillside
      Review Board was then asked to determine the location of the ridge line. On

      surrounding area. On March 8, 2022, the Planning Commission met, had a public

AMEND REGISTRATION FEES FOR THE ST. GEORGE MARATHON/ORDINANCE:
    Consider approval of an ordinance amending the registration fees for the St.
    George Marathon.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The St. George Marathon is the City's
      signature event. The costs for bussing, awards, shirts, and other administrative
      activities associated with putting on the race have all increased. Marathon entrance
      fees were last amended in 2016. Staff has worked hard at keeping the quality high
      and the costs low for the St. George Marathon as it is the City's signature event.
      Marathon entrance fees were last amended in 2016. In comparison, marathons
      across the Country average around $115 per entry. As costs for bussing, awards,
      shirts, and other ancillary costs associated with putting on a race of this magnitude
      have increased, staff recommends increasing the registration fees for the Marathon
      from $15 - $35, depending on the entry type and registration date.
