Mr. Johnston stated the easement does not have a drainage channel in it. This is like having

South Point Development proposal since the last Work Session. Tom Ellison will share the 
changes to the plan and the review criteria, and following Mr. Ellison’s presentation, there 
will be a question and answer time for the Planning Commission. Staff recommends that

Tom Ellison, the representative for South Point Development stated Commissioner 
Sargent had requested more information about the decision criteria at the last Work 
Session. That will be addressed later in the meeting. 
On September 28, 2017, a revised site plan was finalized, a statement of the proposal was 
finalized, and a comparison to the Silver Creek project was also done. The project summary 
and the comparison to Silver Creek were given to the Planning Commission at the last

Work Session and are now included in the Staff Report. Also included in the Staff Report 
are a revised concept plan, a revised Open Space plan, a revised ten-lot reservoir plan, and 
a summary of the revised community benefits. All of these items were presented in 
September. 
The new information and changes include concrete housing data, through a Bonneville 
study and a County study, which is in draft form. New documents include: decision criteria, 
new South Point community benefits, and a supplemental traffic memorandum. 
The decision criteria analysis was discussed. There are six interrelated changed 
circumstances that justify having the South Point Development of the type being proposed 
in this location. Summit County has developed a housing shortage in the lower-cost range. 
Smaller units are not being constructed, creating a shortfall of housing just for employment 
being created in the county. This housing shortage forces workers to live elsewhere, 
creating traffic and air quality problems. This is being addressed by dedicating 250 units in 
the South Point Development to only people who are employed in Summit County. 
Commissioner Willoughby asked what kind of jobs are available in Summit County. Mr. 
Ellison answered the Bonneville study addresses forward looking targeted jobs by Summit 
County economic development, with five categories of high-income jobs that are targeted 
to be located in the county. Looking back, the bulk of jobs that have been created are low-
wage retail. Commissioner Willoughby stated there is still a need for those low-income 
jobs and a place for those workers to live. Mr. Ellison stated South Point would provide 
some of that employee housing.

Findings of Fact 
1. David and Lesley Thompson are the owners of Lot 45 of the Deer Crossing Subdivision 
located within the Promontory Specially Planned Area. 
2. The property is further identified as 8019 Sunrise Loop. 
3. The property is 1.56 acres in size. 
4. The topography of property varies.  The northeast area of the lot generally consists of 
slopes less than 30%.  The southwest area of the lot includes slopes in excess of 30%. 
5. The steeper (southwest) area of the lot is vegetated with stands of Oak trees. The 
northeast area of the lot has little or no tree cover and is primarily vegetated with 
sagebrush.   
6. The Promontory Architectural Review Committee documents establish a building pad 
disturbance area in less-steep, less-treed, northeast portion of the lot. 
7. The building pad disturbance area is bisected by a 10-foot-wide drainage easement. 
8. No existing drainage channel exists on Lot 45. 
9. The applicants request the drainage easement be shifted out of and adjacent to the 
building pad disturbance area to allow for a contiguous building pad area that is not 
divided by the existing drainage easement alignment. 
10.  Relocating the easement to the proposed location maintains a drainage easement for the 
benefit of the property owner, adjacent property owners, and master developer. 
11.  Relocating the easement to the proposed location will accommodate a future home site 
on the least steep portion of the lot. 
12.  Relocating the easement to the proposed location will preserve a greater amount of the 
existing oak stands on the lot. 
13. The Promontory Architectural Review Committee endorses the proposed plat 
amendment. 
14.  No public opposition to the proposed plat amendment was expressed at the December 7, 
2017 Eastern Summit County Planning Commission meeting. 
 
Conclusions of Law 
1. There is good cause for this amendment. 
2. The proposed plat amendment will not result in additional density. 
 
Conditions of Approval 
1. County Attorney review and approval of the plat for form and content is a condition 
precedent to recordation. 
2. County Engineer review and approval of a revised topographic map that provides for a 
flow of surface water equivalent to that provided by the original easement is a condition 
precedent to plat recordation and building permit issuance.
