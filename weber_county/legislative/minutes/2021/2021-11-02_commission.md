Public hearing to discuss amendments to the operating and capital budget of Weber County for the 2021
calendar year.

Scott Parke (Clerk/Auditor) explained occasional adjustments needed as the budget year progresses. Larger
of the proposed amendments to 2021 budget.

1. Finish second floor of the annex with contingency money included.

2. Winston Park sewer improvements; money to be recouped over time via impact fees.

3. Repairs to 9350 West; discovered opportunity do really make that road nice for just a little more

cost.

4. Employee compensation - $2/hr wage increase to make county more competitive

5. Grants received and money budgeted.

6. Golden Spike revenue increase leading to cost of sales increase that proposed budget covers.
Commissioner Jenkins: Health Department annex comes out of their budget. Sheriff warehouse built from
leftover funds. Property management pole barns offset by saving on multiple rentals.

Commissioner Froerer: These were all good financial business decisions. One question for the record, what
would you say the financial health of Weber County is today?
