Public Hearing

Resolution No. 20-12-02: 2021 White City Consolidated Fee Schedule

                 Paul Ashton reviewed the 2021 White City Consolidated Fee Schedule.

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                 Council Member Little stated he has concerns with the following fees:

   •   Business license fee being higher than neighboring communities
   •   Window and door replacement fee
   •   Civil penalty for weeds and tree removal is high
   •   Chicken fees should be changed and most of the other fees are not applicable to White

                Isaiah Johnson stated he is concerned about the window fee and the how
expensive it is.

               Amber Gustaveson stated fees that sports teams are charged at Big Bear Park

               Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

             Council Member Little motioned to eliminate the fee for window and door
replacement without any structural changes. The motion fails due to lack of a second.

              Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to approve the
following Resolution. The motion passed 3 to 1, showing Council Member Little voted “Nay”.

              Council Member Little stated he would like someone to attend the Council
meeting to explain the fees.

               Council Member Price asked Rori Andreason to put fee discussion on the next
agenda.

RESOLUTION NO. 20-12-02                                                Date: December 17, 2020

        A RESOLUTION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING
        THE 2021 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to
Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) Section 10-2a-401 et.seq.; and

         WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
legislative body for White City pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-501; and

        WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a local district
that provides certain municipal-type services to White City pursuant to UCA Section 17B-2a-1103
et al; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to fiscal policy for funding the 2021 White City Budget, the Greater
Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) adopted the 2021 Budget for the MSD on
October 22, 2020, releasing the funding for the 2021 White City Budget; and

       WHEREAS, the 2021 White City Budget, and recently adopted Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District Budget both used revenue projections based on the 2021
Consolidated Fee Schedule; and

        WHEREAS, to be in compliance with state law, White City must annually adopt a
