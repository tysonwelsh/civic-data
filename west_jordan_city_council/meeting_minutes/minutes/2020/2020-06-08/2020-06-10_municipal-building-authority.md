                            MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                           MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY MEETING

                               Wednesday, June 10, 2020 - 7:00 p.m.

         This meeting was held electronically via Zoom and streamed on West Jordan’s YouTube channel
                   base location at City Hall 8000 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088

               David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, and Melissa Worthen

CALL TO ORDER
Chair McConnehey called the Municipal Building Authority (MBA) board meeting to order at 9:01
p.m.

MINUTES REVIEW AND APPROVAL
The minutes of the May 13, 2020 MBA meeting were reviewed.

MOTION:        Board Member Whitelock moved to approve the minutes of the May 13, 2020
               MBA board meeting. Board Member Green seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Board Member Whitelock Yes
Chair McConnehey             Yes
Board Member Lamb            Yes
Board Member Worthen Yes
Board Member Jacob           Yes
Board Member Green           Yes
Board Member Pack            Yes

The motion passed 7-0.

    Danyce Steck presented the MBA FY 2021 Proposed Budget, and answered questions from the Board.

    Citizen Comments:
