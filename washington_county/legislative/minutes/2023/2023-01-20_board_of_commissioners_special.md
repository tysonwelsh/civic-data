WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JANUARY 20, 2023

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 9:30 a.m. on January 20, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah
Lloyd.

INVOCATION BY: Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Public Works Director Ron Whitehead

APPROVAL OF MINUTES FOR THE JANUARY 17, 2023 BOARD OF
COMMISSIONERS MEETING

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Minutes of January 17, 2023
Board of Commissioners Meeting. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

RATIFICATION OF ACTIONS TAKEN AT THE JANUARY 17, 2023 BOARD OF
COUNTY COMMISSIONERS MEETING

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Ratification of Actions Taken
at the January 17, 2023 Board of County Commissioners Meeting. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist
and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _FOR_ ADOPTION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2023-3067, A
RESOLUTION _OF THE COUNTY COMMISSION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY
UTAH, AUTHORIZING THE ISSUANCE AND SALE OF NOT MORE THAN $20,000,000
AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF SALES TAX REVENUE BONDS, SERIES

AND

2023A_AND_ TAXABLE SALES TAX REVENUE BONDS, SERIES 2023B;
RELATED MATTERS

Mark Anderson with Zion Public Finance explained to start the bonding process, a parameters
Resolution that signifies the Commissions intent to issue bonds needs to be passed. This Resolution
is proposing that the County may take on up to $20,000,000 with a term not to exceed twenty-five
(25) years. There is a public hearing scheduled on February 21, 2023.

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw stated that the Federal Courts are looking for office space
and expansion. The County has considered the Boulevard Building as new home for both the
Federal Courts and the Justice Court. If it determined that this is not in the best interest of the
County or the Federal Courts, it can be abandoned at any time.
