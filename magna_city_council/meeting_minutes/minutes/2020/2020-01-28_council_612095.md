Public Hearing

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following request:

Application #31096 – Dominion Energy is requesting approval of a zone change from A-20
(agricultural) to M-2 (manufacturing) on 120.0 acres of property located at 1731 South 8000 West.

              Diana Martinez, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD),
reviewed the application stating Dominion Energy is planning on building a liquid gas facility.

                Chris Balling, representative, Dominion Energy, stated the entire 160 acres has
been purchased from Kennecott; however, only a portion of the land will be used to build a liquid
gas facility. The facility will be built in the southwest corner.

                 Council Member Prokopis asked what the plans are for the 120 acres that will
not be used.


                                                 2
DATE            TUESDAY                                JANUARY                               14, 2020

                 Mr. Balling stated this type of facility requires a clearance area. There is no intent
to sell or build on the remaining acreage.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                Dan Cripps stated he supports the rezone and thinks it will be beneficial to have
in this area.

               Mark Elieson stated the rezone is ideal for this piece of land. Rezoning the land
to an M-2 zone makes a lot of sense. When the Planning Commission addresses the conditional
use portion, they will look into that deeper to make sure Magna is taken care of.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to approved
the following Ordinance:

ORDINANCE NO. 20-01-01                                                        Date: January 28, 2020

        AN ORDINANCE, AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED “ZONING” OF THE MAGNA
        METRO TOWNSHIP CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2017, BY RECLASSIFYING

Resolution – KMH Consulting LLC

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the contract of KMH
Consulting LLC to perform certain legislative lobbying work for the 2020 Utah State Legislature
General Session:

RESOLUTION NO. 20-01-03                                                   Date: January 28, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       THE CONTRACT OF KMH CONSULTING LLC TO PERFORM CERTAIN
       LEGISLATIVE LOBBYING WORK ON BEHALF OF MAGNA FOR THE 2020
       UTAH STATE LEGISLATURE GENERAL SESSION

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. With pending amendments upcoming during the 2020 Utah State Legislature General
      Session for Utah Inland Port, Metro Townships, the MSD and other concerns, Magna
      desires to retain the services of a Lobbying firm to work on their behalf on Capitol Hill to
      provide for the outcomes Magna needs on these key pieces of legislation.

   D. During the 2019 Legislative Session, Magna retained the services of KMH Consulting LLC
      to provide lobbying services for Magna.

   E. Based on the work performed, Magna desires to retain KMH Consulting LLC to represent
      them again for the 2020 Legislative Session.

   F. KMH Consulting LLC has responded with the attached contract for services (Attachment
      “A”).

   G. KMH Consulting LLC are highly qualified lobbyists who can professionally represent
      Magna’s interests on these important pieces of legislation.

                                         RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the contract between
