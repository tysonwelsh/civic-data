                                    KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
                                         RESOLUTION


    RESOLUTION NO. 2020-10-01                                               October 22, 2020

   A RESOLUTION ACKNOWLEDGING THE RE-APPOINTMENT OF TINA SNOW AS THE
   REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTEE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP TO THE BOARD
   OF THE MAGNA MOSQUITO ABATEMENT DISTRICT.

           WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township is a part of the Magna Mosquito Abatement

   District (the “District”); and

           WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) may appoint a

   member to the governing board of the District; and

           WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township appointed a member of the Council to the

    District board.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS
   METRO TOWNSHIP:

           1. That the Council does hereby re-appoint Tina Snow as its appointed representative on the
   board of the District, to serve until her term of office expires or until her successor is appointed or
   qualified, whichever is latest.

           2. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.


                                                    KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
