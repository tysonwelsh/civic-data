BACKGROUND

The State of Utah has unique tax
laws that were first passed in 1985
titled Truth-in-Taxation or TnT.

In short, these tax laws made it so in
Utah, agencies are not allowed to
collect more revenue than a
previous year on the existing
homes.

Taxing agencies only get additional
revenue by applying the existing tax
rate to new growth.

HOW UTAH PROPERTY
TAX WORKS

New growth drives all revenue for
taxing entities in Utah.

If there wasn't one new home built
or renovation done in Utah County
last year, County Government
would receive exactly the same
dollar amount in property taxes
REGARDLESS of how much your
home value changed.

However, inflation persists.

SO WHY DO MY
TAXES GO UP?

There's a couple reasons

1. One of the taxing entities you
live in (School District, City,
Water District, County) goes
through this Truth-in-Taxation
process and formalizes a rate
higher than what is calculated.

2. Your home value increased more
than the average of all other homes
in the taxing district.
