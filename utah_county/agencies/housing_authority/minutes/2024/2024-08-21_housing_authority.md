B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-08-02 Cedar View Loan Pay down 
Steve made a motion to approve the Cedar View Loan Pay Down. April seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
After a review of the properties financials, it has come to the accounting manager's attention 
that the property is holding an extra $64,000 in an operating account that can be used to pay 
down the loan to the Provo Home Consortium. This will help reduce the loan obligations of the 
property and will be used to pay down the principal. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-08-03 Legal Services Contract 
April made a motion to approve the Legal Service Contract. Suzanne seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
The Housing Authority needs to obtain legal services from time to time for contract review, 
evictions, etc. A request for proposal was created and a bids received.  Sumsion Business 
Law was selected. They will be able to complete the creation for the 501c3 and handle 
contracts and evictions. They require a 3-year contract with the ability to terminate with notice. 
A $5000 retainer is required upon contract signing.  
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-08-04 Contract for Bridgeway HVAC 
Sara made a motion to approve the Contract for Bridgeway HVAC. Suzanne seconded 
the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC staff has recently identified 20 units out of 36 1-bedroom apartments that have HVAC 
systems that are 29 years old and have not been replaced. Before these units fail and the 
pending market/manufacturing refrigerant changes in the HVAC equipment effective 
November 2024 it is proposed that HAUC replace these units. The RFP process was 
completed and a bid was received from a local contractor used by HAUC in the past.
