Subject:         PLZ-24-4006 “Pelaez Rezone,” an application to change the General Plan
                 designation and rezone approximately 0.96-acres located at 3516 West 12600
                 South from R-1 Residential Zone to C-G (Commercial Gateway) Zone.
                 Applicant- Daniel Pelaez


This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval:
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-24-4006, for
amending the General Plan and Rezoning a 0.96-acre parcel located at 3516 West 12600
South to change the General Plan designation from (Estate Density Residential) to
Community Commercial and changing the zoning from R-1 to C-G (Commercial Gateway).

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial:
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-24-4006, to amend
the General Plan and rezone 0.96- acres located at 3516 West 12600 South, thus keeping
the property designated as Estate Density Residential in the General Plan and keeping the
current zoning of R-1 (1-acre lots).


Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Mr. Pelaez, for about
0.96-acres located at 3516 West 12600 South. The applicant is requesting a change to the
General Plan and a rezone to accommodate an HVAC office, with a showroom and storage
warehouse. The requested General Plan designation is Community Commercial, and the
requested zone is C-G (Commercial Gateway Zone).

The subject property is a rectangle- shaped property located on the north side of 12600 South
with about 150 feet of frontage on 12600 South. There is currently a home and some sheds on
the property.

The subject property, and all the adjacent properties are part of the Riverton Meadows
Subdivision and are all zoned R-1. Properties to the south (across 12600 South) are zoned C-
G (commercial Gateway), and C-PO (Commercial Professional Office). Other properties
along 12600 South are zoned Light Manufacturing and Regional Commercial.
The General Plan for the Riverton Meadows Subdivision is Estate Density Residential. South of
12600 South Street the General Plan is Professional Office.



Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
webpage. Staff received comments from neighbors objecting to the rezone, expressing a desire
to keep the Riverton Meadows Subdivision zoned R-1.

When considering rezone applications, the Planning Commission makes a recommendation to
City Council. The City Council will make a final decision on this application in a future City Council
Meeting. The City will send additional notices to property owners within 1,000 feet to announce
the City Council meeting where a final decision will be made.
