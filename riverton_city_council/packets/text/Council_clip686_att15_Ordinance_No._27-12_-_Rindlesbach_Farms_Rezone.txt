                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 24-12

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN TO MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL
 AND REZONING ZONING TO R-4/SD THE 5.08-ACRE PARCEL LOCATED AT 2400 WEST
 11800 SOUTH, WITH THE SD DESIGNATION THAT THE NORTHERNMOST LOTS BE A
           MINIMUM OF 1/3 ACRE IN SIZE, MOORE HOMES, APPLICANT

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City General Plan to Medium Density Residential and the Zoning Map from R-3
R-46/SD for the approximately 5.08 acres located at 11800 South 2400 West.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended
                              to reflect the described changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

               Section 2.              The zoning includes the following Specific Development
                                       Designations:
                                       1.   The northernmost lot(s) shall be a minimum of 1/3 acre
                                            in size.

               Section 3.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of July
2024 by the following vote:
