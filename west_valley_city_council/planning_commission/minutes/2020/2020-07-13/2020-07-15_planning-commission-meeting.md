ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATION
ZT-4-2020 Petition by BRUCE SMITH AND FRED COX requesting an ordinance change to
          Sections 7-6-302 and 7-6-305 to allow the minimum zoning district size for the C-2
          (General Commercial) zone to be less than 5 acres if the district is adjacent to at
          least 5 acres of property zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
          963-3545)


                Discussion: Steve Pastorik explained that this application and the following
                application are contingent upon each other. If this application is denied, the next
                application will be denied by default. There needs to be at least 5 acres available
                to have a C-2 zone. This request would be an exception to the 5-acre
                requirement.

                The applicant's proposal was restrictive. Steve put together another rendition of

                  the proposal, and the applicant was amenable to the change. Steve's proposal read;
                  Property zoned M may be combined with property zoned C-2 to meet the
                  minimum district size of the C-2 zone. The main reason behind the 5-acre rule
                  was to prevent spot zoning. The City didn't want a ½ acre lot surrounded by
                  homes to be a C-2 zone. Commissioner Woodruff asked if this application would
                  affect other properties within the City. Steve P. said it would; however, the

                  are like zones, would there be any reason for concern with this proposal? Steve P

                  application is to change an M zone to a C-2 zone. The M zone does not allow for
                  medical office, whereas the C-2 zone does. Steve said there are two reasons why
                  medical office is not permitted in the M zone. One, The City is trying to keep
                  manufacturing in more of a commercial setting as opposed to and industrial
                  setting. Second, the City was trying to keep medical offices away from homeless
                  shelters.

ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
Z-5-2020  Petition by BRUCE SMITH AND FRED COX requesting a zone change from M
          (Manufacturing) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located at 5571
          W 2455 S on 1 acre. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

                 Discussion: Steve P. explained the proposed use for the subject property is a
                 multiunit building with retail, professional office, and medical office. The owner is a
                 dentist, so they are looking into that possibility also.
                 Commissioner Woodruff doesn’t feel this would be a good location for a medical
                 office. Trucking businesses surround the subject location. Steve P. explained that the
                 applicant is looking at the medical office for one potential use. This proposal is for a
                 commercial strip mall. Commissioner Lovato remembers an adjacent lot being
                 challenging to rent. Steve explained the lot he is speaking about did not have a left
                 turn in and out of the lot.
                 Steve explained the entrance to the subject property will have a left turn.
                 Commissioner Winters feels that the C-2 zone is reasonable in this location.
                 Commissioner Fuller asked if this proposal will open up the concern of being near a
                 homeless shelter. Steve explained that the interest at the time was when a homeless
                 shelter was being pushed upon the city. The location of this shelter would’ve been off
                 Redwood Road, near the river. That area is zoned to allow for medical offices, and
                 the city wanted to prevent medical offices built around the shelter.
