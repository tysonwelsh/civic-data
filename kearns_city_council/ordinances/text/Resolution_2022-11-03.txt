                         KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

        RESOLUTION NO. 2022-11-03                             DATE: November 28, 2022

                          A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE
                      KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP’S 2023 BUDGET

                                           RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, on November 14, 2022, Kearns adopted a tentative budget for the 2023 fiscal
year; and

        WHEREAS, Kearns provided notice pursuant to Utah Code Ann § 10-6-113 concerning
the tentative budget and of the date, time, and place of a public hearing on the same; and

       WHEREAS, on November 28, 2022, Kearns held the public hearing on the tentative budget
in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-114.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

       1.      That the tentative budget adopted on November 14, 2022, and attached hereto, be
and hereby is adopted as the Final Budget of Kearns for the 2023 fiscal year.

       2.      The Kearns budget officer is directed and authorized to file the final budget with
the Utah State Auditor within thirty (30) days of the date of this resolution pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-6-118(2).

       3.      This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
