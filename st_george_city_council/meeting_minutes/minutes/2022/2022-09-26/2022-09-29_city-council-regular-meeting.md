     a. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Automation
        N Controls for automation, instrumentation, and SCADA work at the
        Wastewater Treatment Plant.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Water Services Department is in the
          process of replacing and expanding the wastewater treatment plant automation
          and controls systems. We have been working with Automation N Controls for the
          past few years in replacing and expanding the system. This professional services
          agreement will cover the next 12 months of work on the project. Staff
          recommends approval of the agreement.

      b. Consider approval to purchase a 2022 Pierce Manufacturing 2,000 GPM
         107' aerial/quint Ladder truck through Siddons-Martin Emergency Group
         (Regional Pierce Vendor).

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This request is for an additional fire
          apparatus that is needed for the addition of new fire stations being constructed
          within the City. The apparatus will be purchased on the national Sourcewell
          purchasing contract. Staff from the Fire and Fleet Departments have been
          working on specifications over the past several months for two (2) requested fire
          apparatus. The requested 107' aerial/quint ladder truck will be similar to the
          apparatus purchased in 2016 from Pierce manufacturing and with the two (2)
          requested fire apparatus in the FY 2023 Budget would bring the total of Pierce
          apparatus to sixteen (16) in the Fire Department's fleet. The City has a long
          history with Pierce Manufacturing spanning over 30 years. Staff recommends the
          purchase of the 2022 Pierce Manufacturing 2,000 GPM 107' aerial/quint Ladder
          truck from Siddons-Martin Emergency Group in the amount of $1,491,097.28.

      c. Consider approval to purchase a 2022 Pierce Manufacturing 1,500 GPM
         Type 1 fire engine/pumper through Siddons-Martin Emergency Group
         (Regional Pierce Vendor).

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This request is for an additional fire
          apparatus that is needed for the addition of new fire stations being constructed
          within the city. The apparatus will be purchased on the national Sourcewell
          contract. Staff from the Fire and Fleet departments have been working on
          specifications over the past several months for two (2) requested fire apparatus,
          the requested fire engine/pumper will be similar to the previous six (6) pumpers
          purchased from Pierce manufacturing and with the two (2) requested fire
          apparatus in the FY 2023 Budget would bring the total of Pierce apparatus to
          sixteen (16) in the Fire Department's fleet. The City has a long history with Pierce
          Manufacturing spanning over 30 years. Staff recommends purchasing the 2022
          Pierce Manufacturing 1,500 GPM Type 1 fire engine/pumper from Siddons-Martin
          Emergency Group in the amount of $1,010,977.21.

PUBLIC HEARING/ROADWAY VACATION/DEDICATION PLAT:
     Public Hearing and consideration of a roadway vacation/dedication plat for
     Fort Pierce Area 5/7 Street Dedication and Vacation plat.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Fort Pierce Area 5 Street Dedication
      plat as shown on the official plat on file in the Office of the Washington County
      Recorder’s Office as Doc. No. 20130012409 was approved by City Council on January
      17, 2013. It included a roadway dedication consisting of approximately 800 feet long
      and 80 feet wide. Only a portion of the roadway improvements have been installed
      including about 28 feet wide paving down the center of the roadway. The Joint
      Utilities Commission recommended approval.
