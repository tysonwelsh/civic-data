    2. APPROVAL OF FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF
       WEBER COUNTY AMENDING SECTION 16-2-9 SOLID WASTE FEES OF THE WEBER COUNTY
       CODE.
         Sean Wilkinson (Community Development Director): Residential solid waste collection and disposal fees
         are being removed for the new Ogden Valley City. Fees in Uintah Highlands and western Weber County are
         being updated to reflect an agreement with Republic Services for yearly 3% increase. Residents with one
         container in western Weber County will see an increase of $1.02 per quarter and residents in Uintah
         Highlands will see an increase of $0.96 per quarter; the fee for more than one container will increase to $1.02
         in western Weber County and $0.99 in the Uintah Highlands.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve First Reading of an Ordinance amending Section 16-2-9 Solid Waste
         Fees of the Weber County Code; Chair Froerer seconded.
         Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye

         Charlie Ewert (Planning): It is proposed to move from the Board of Adjustment to an Appeal and Variance
         Hearing Officer. This is a single person and there can be more than one hearing officer. Each case will be
         heard by one hearing officer. Additional edits related to the change are included. Board of Adjustment is
         comprised of lay people, hearing officers come with experience in the field. Also, the Board of Adjustment
         does not meet very often and is sometimes difficult to constitute a quorum with a volunteer lay body that
         does not meet very often. A hearing officer on contract will be easier to schedule.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve First Reading of an Ordinance to repeal provisions related to the Board
         of Adjustments and to replace them with provisions for an Appeals and Variances Hearing Officer, and related
         administrative and clerical edits to align the code with state statutes; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye
