1.         22-353                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Chair Robinson invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comments.

                                  Ms. Rebecca Colley spoke about the impact of the increasing tax burden and inflation on 
                                  family budgets. She is not supportive of building a new recreation center and does not 
                                  think the city needs it.

                                  Ms. Marilyn Larson spoke about allocating funds to address the pertinent needs of the 
                                  city and and improvements that help our first responders.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones thanked the Mayor and City Council for their work on behalf of the City. 
                                  She reviewed the discussions at prior city council meetings addressing the capital 
                                  facilities needs of the city.

                                  Mr. Clint Juhl is supportive of allocating funds to remodel or rebuild Alta Canyon 
                                  Recreation Center. He is also supportive of the Public Works and Fire Station 31 
                                  projects. He was disappointed that the infrastructure needs of the city had been 
                                  politicized. He is willing to pay his fair share for a nice recreation facility.

                                  Mr. Wiley Sharp is opposed to the Woodhaven Estates projects. He expressed concerns 
                                  about traffic and access and thinks the development would negatively impact the 
                                  residents of neighboring Midvale City. 

                                  Mr. Lenny Sharp is opposed to the Woodhaven Estates development. He expressed 
                                  concerns about the increased traffic and the impact on school children walking to school. 
                                  He would like to see access to the development from the East, through Sandy City. He is 
                                  concerned that there is not enough parking stalls for the project residents.

                                  Ms. Kristine Takomon requested that the city consider changing its ordinances to allow 
                                  for residents to own mini pigs. She has a mini pig as a family pet. She provided 
                                  information to the Council.

                                  Mr. Matthew Powers is opposed to the Woodhaven rezone. He expressed concerns 
                                  related to the high density of the project and the impact on traffic and access to the 
                                  development. 

                                  Mr. Rhett Hamilton is opposed to the Woodhaven project. He expressed concerns related 
                                  to the impact on wildlife and green space in the area.

                                  Mr. Riley Hamilton is opposed to the Woodhaven project. He is concerned about the 
                                  impact on traffic and the lack of visitor parking in the proposed development.

                                  Ms. Farrah Edwards expressed concerns about the access to the Woodhaven 
                                  development. 

                                  Mr. Chris Gundersen agreed with the comments made earlier regarding the Woodhaven 
                                  development. He expressed concerns with the impact on traffic and the environment and 
                                  was concerned with the storm water drainage associated with the project.

                                  Public comment closed.

2.         22-349                 Introductions from Congressmen John Curtis and Burgess Owens

                                  Chair Robinson welcomed Congressman Curtis and Congressman Owens to our City 
                                  Council meeting this evening. Mayor Zoltanski welcomed and thanked the Congressmen 
                                  for their support and accessibility to our city. On behalf of the City, the Council and 
                                  Mayor the Congressmen were acknowledged for all they do on behalf of our city.

                                  Congressman Curtis expressed his gratitude. He said it was an honor to be at the Sandy 
                                  City Council meeting this evening. He has a great deal of respect for Sandy City 
                                  residents and leaders. Congressmen Curtis represents one of the largest districts, 
                                  area-wise, in the country. 

                                  Congressman Owens expressed the importance of representing Utah and spoke about 
                                  the dedication of our federal representatives and the importance of retaining the harmony 
                                  and unity in our state. He thanked the Council and Mayor for all they do on behalf of 
                                  Sandy City residents and he is happy to be of service to our city and his district.

4.         22-348                 Administration requesting that the Council discuss the operational funding 
                                  and governance of the Alta Canyon Sports Center and Special Service 
                                  District
                   Attachments:   Presentation

                                  Shane Pace, CAO, provided an overview of this evening's presentations. Brian Kelley, 
                                  Finance Director, presented on the Alta Canyon Sports Center Special Service District 
                                  funding and governance. He reviewed the basic assumptions regarding the rebuild costs 
                                  and debt service associated with Alta Canyon, He provided a comparison of other cities' 
                                  recreation center funding and provided options related to the Special Service Districts. Mr. 
                                  Kelley reviewed the next steps for Council consideration and future action. Council 
                                  questions and comments followed. 

                                  Shane Pace provided an overview of the Zap tax funding and projects allocation from 1997 
                                  through 2013. He presented a summary of projects funded by the Zap tax in 2016. 
                                  Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Ordinance #22-11, an ordinance amending and fixing the boundaries of a zone
