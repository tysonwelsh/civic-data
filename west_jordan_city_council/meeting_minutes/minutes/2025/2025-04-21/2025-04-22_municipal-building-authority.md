 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Resolution No. 115 adopting the Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building
          Authority of the City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2026 and setting May 6, 2025, as
          the date for a public hearing regarding the adoption of the final budget.
          Budget & Management Analyst Becky Condie introduced the item explaining that the
          Municipal Building Authority was formed to acquire projects on behalf of the City with
          the Council serving as its board. The proposed Tentative budget includes the debt service
          payments for the bond established to pay for the Public Works Building. There is a fixed
          repayment schedule to collect lease payments from the general fund and the utility fund.
          For 2025, some interest earnings were received due to favorable interest rates. The
          reserve balance is expected to remain at $1.9 million after the 2026 debt service payment
          of $1,852,900. The bond is scheduled to be fully repaid in 2039.

           Staff requested the board adopt the tentative budget and set May 6th for public hearing.

           MOTION: Board Member Green moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 115 adopting the
                   Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West
                   Jordan for Fiscal Year 2026 and setting May 6, 2025, as the date for a public
                   hearing regarding the adoption of the final budget.
                    Board Member Whitelock seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, Kayleen Whitelock, Bob Bedore, Chad Lamb, Kent
                      Shelton
