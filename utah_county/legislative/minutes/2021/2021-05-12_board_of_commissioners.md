KIRK BERTELSEN, UTAH COUNTY FIRE MARSHAL - WHAT THESE 2 CHANGES DO

MEET THE FIRE PROTECTION CODES IN THE WILDLAND URBAN INTERFACE. THE

OVERHANG TO SEAL IT SO THAT HOT EMBERS CAN'T GET INTO TO SO FIRE
CANNOT GET INTO THE ATTIC.

KIRK BERTELSEN - THE SECOND ONE IS JUST ADDING SO THAT THEY CAN PUT A
FIRE RATED VENT IN AN EAVE. RIGHT NOW, OUR CODE SAYS YOU CANNOT AND
WHAT I'M TRYING TO DO IS OPEN THAT UP BECAUSE THERE ARE COMPANIES
NOW THAT MAKE FIRE RATED VENTS THAT DO WHAT WE NEED IT TO DO AND
THEY CAN STILL VENT THEIR ATTIC WITHOUT HAVING TO PUT MORE VENTS ON
THE ROOF OF THE HOUSE.
