2.     Public Hearing: Section 10.15.15 Recreational
       Vehicles and Recreational Vehicle Parks -
       Proposed ordinance prohibiting the occupation and
       use of a recreational vehicle as a residence, unless
       it is within an area designated by the city as an RV
       park; licensed with the city as being a business for
       RVs; and at a location zoned for that purpose.
       a.       Open Public Hearing
       Ann opened the public hearing at 7:02pm.

       b.      Staff Presentation
       Shauna explained that there is a need to be more
       straightforward in this ordinance. She also mentioned
       that there needs to be a definition for “camping.” She
       presented staff recommendation as well as law
       enforcement input, based on their experience in the
       City. The discussion included the use of motorized
       vehicles, recreational vehicles, semi trucks, and tents.
       She is asking for Commission input at this time.

     c.       Commission Discussion
     Ann Peterson asked for clarification on the length of
     time that an RV can be parked on private property.
     Heather Robertson asked about the difference between
     29 and 30 days. John Ford stated that he has noticed
     several state-regulated rest areas along I-15. He
     wonders if we could have something like that as an
     option for truckers in our area. Skip and Shauna both
     stated that UDOT is in charge of those projects. Terry
     brought up the fact that truckers often stop due to
     logbook requirements, and he would like to give them
     consideration in order for them to be safe. Terry also
     expressed concern about having sufficient law
     enforcement to handle these code violations. Shauna
     explained that law enforcement won’t actively be out
     looking for violations, but they need to have some clear
     definitions for when someone calls in a complaint in
     order to enforce the code. Terry pointed out that
     sending truckers to stay outside of the community hurts
     our economy. Shauna mentioned that an idea that has
     been discussed quite a bit, is allowing people to live in
     an RV on temporary services for the duration of a home
     renovation or a new home build. Staff did not
     recommend allowing that right now, but Shauna asked
     the Commission to think about what they would like to
     see allowed. Ann opened the floor for public comment.
     Catherine Malquist presented public comment
     regarding property owners’ rights. Blaine Malquist
     presented public comment about land use,
     ordinances, animal rights, pesticide use, and secondary
     buildings on a landowner’s property. Donald Ball
     presented public comment about allowing persons in
     need to stay on his property. He feels that it should be
     allowed indefinitely, if the property owner allows it and
     it’s not bothering anyone else.

     d.     Action Item- Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to continue this hearing, at the
     request of staff.

     Motion: Heather Robertson Second: Fran Petersen
     Outcome: Motion Carries

4.   Introduction of upcoming discussion item to look at
     possibly updating the General Plan Concept Zoning
     map in the area located from approximately
     Meadow Lane to 700 N, and from Airport Road to
     500 W.
     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna explained that she and Seth had discussed this
     topic and the advantages of zoning for some type of
     commercial or industrial land around an airport. She
     showed the Commission the land in question, and
     explained that it’s currently zoned as mixed density
     residential. She said there is currently an application for
     commercial use, and Skip pointed out that the property
     in question has not yet been annexed into the City. He
     stated that the purpose of discussing this is for future
     planning.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Fran Petersen feels like this would be a huge
     contradiction to what the Commission has been trying
     to do. Shauna stated that the General Plan and Zoning
     Map should be updated simultaneously. She said that
     it’s something the Commission needs to look at and

     consider, because there have been several requests to
     use the land as industrial. Heather expressed concern
     regarding the current homeowners in this area. Fran
     expressed concern over having industrial parks at both
     entrances to the City. John expressed concern about
     zoning for residential so close to the airport. Terry
     spoke about FAA requirements that will need to be
     looked at, as well as potential future growth of the
     airport. Much discussion was had about coordinating
     with the County in order to accomplish zoning goals.

     c.     Action Item - Motion
     No action item needed.
