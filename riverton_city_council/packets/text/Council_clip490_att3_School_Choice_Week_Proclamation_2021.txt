                                     School Choice Week

WHEREAS, all children in Riverton should have access to the highest-quality education
possible; and

WHEREAS, as a community, we recognize the important role that an effective education plays
in preparing all students to be successful adults; and

WHEREAS, quality education is critically important to the economic vitality of our city; and

WHEREAS, Riverton is home to a multitude of excellent education options from which parents
can choose for their children; and

WHEREAS, educational variety not only helps to diversify our economy but also enhances the
vibrancy of our community; and

WHEREAS, our school district has many high-quality teaching professionals who are
committed to educating our children; and

WHEREAS, School Choice Week is celebrated across the country by millions of students,
parents, educators, schools, and organizations to raise awareness of the need for effective
educational options; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim January 23 –
January 29, 2022, as School Choice Week in Riverton.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 12th day of January 2022.
