PUBLIC HEARING - REZONE – Consideration of a proposed rezone of
approximately 31.39 acres located at 1000 West 200 South from Commercial (CS)
and Commercial (COM) to Mixed Use (MU) – Ordinance 22-11 – Russ Holley,
Planner

Planner Russ Holley reviewed the proposed rezone which was a workshop item at the
May 3, 2022 Council meeting.

RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission recommend approval to the Municipal
Council for a rezone of approximately 31.39 acres of property located at approximately
1000 West 200 South (TIN# 05-064-0013, 06-002-0003, 05-064-0028) from Commercial
Service (CS) and Commercial (COM) to Mixed Use (MU).

PROJECT
The proponent is requesting to rezone approximately 31-acres (three parcels) from COM
& CS to MU with the intent developing a future mixed-use project. All three properties
are currently vacant and have historically been used for agricultural purposes. The
relatively flat land may have critical land areas that could require further
mitigation/assessment. The surrounding area currently has a wide range of commercial
and industrial uses such as restaurant, office, light manufacturing, and storage. Located to
the southwest, single-family neighborhoods are found.

SUMMARY
Given the proximity to existing single family residential areas to south and the light
manufacturing areas to the northeast, a mixed-use project may act as transitional area or
buffer between incompatible land use areas. A large comprehensive project on this site
can be designed in a better manner than that of smaller projects, and would result in
                                                                                    3 | Page
               Logan Municipal Council Minutes ~ Logan, Utah ~ May 17, 2022
improved layout and traffic circulation, consolidated usable open space and strategic
commercial/residential interfacing. This location which is adjacent to Logan City’s
gridded core, offers better mobility and transportation choices for future residents and
puts less development pressure on the rural and agricultural lands farther out into the
valley. Being considered an infill project, infrastructure and utilities can be more
efficiently utilized as compared to outer fringe areas (sprawl) that excessively burden the
transportation and infrastructure networks with poor logistics, higher vehicle miles
driven, and lengthier pipes and utility lines.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
The Planning Commission based its decision on the following findings supported in the
administrative record for this project:
1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as MU.
2. This area is positioned along arterial streets with commercial uses and a mixed-use
project will not negatively impact that character.
3. Infrastructure and access are adequate to serve the future development of this
property.

On April 14, 2022, the Planning Commission recommended that the Municipal Council
approve the rezone. Planning Commissioners voted 5-1.

Chair Simmonds opened the meeting to a public hearing.

Michael Jewell with Triiio Group and proponent of the proposed rezone addressed the
Council. He referred to Envision Utah, which is dedicated to responsible growth in Utah,
and also published a guide on walkable centers. They refer to the benefit of Mixed-Use
with housing in the center and having a walkable area. He has heard discussions about
“worst case scenarios” and about 940 apartment units and 7,500 square feet
developments. Triiio engaged and spoke with Urban Design Associates who designed
Daybreak located in South Jordan. He said that Triiio is committed to creating an actual
Mixed-Use center with 60,000 to 80,000 square feet of commercial use, 300-350
apartment units and 100 townhomes. He brings up the housing issue because Mixed-Use
allows townhomes whereas Commercial and Commercial Services does not. They are
constrained by a few things such as they cannot sell the raw ground, but Triiio will need
to make some improvements to the current ground area. They have also figured out a way
to offer the townhomes for sale rather than for rent. The proposed density is 14 units per
acre. He said the Commercial Zone is just not flexible and Mixed-Use is the best zone
there is in Logan and requires the developer to be collaborative with the City. They
would also like to put a small grocery store in the development area as well.

Mr. Jewell said they have talked to the Planning Commission about voluntarily restricting
the property and Community Development Director Mike DeSimone stated that cannot
be done and is part of the rezone process. In his discussions with City staff this is the type
of project they had in mind when they developed the Future Land Use Plan as Mixed-
Use. He reminded the Council that the Planning Commission recommended approval for
a rezone to the City Council, and he now requests that the Council allow Triiio to bring
their vision to fruition. He knows there is talk to amend the Mixed-Use zone

get a new Mixed-Use ordinance in place, and then require that applicant to comply with
the new Mixed-Use ordinance.

PUBLIC HEARING – Consideration or an annexation filed by Reed Scow, on
behalf of Rivergate, LLC for the purpose of annexing 30 acres, more or less, into the
City of Logan. Property is located on the North side of 600 South at approximately
1900 West – Ordinance 22-12 – Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director

Community Development Director Mike DeSimone addressed the Council regarding the
proposed annexation. He explained that Reed Scow, on behalf of Rivergate, LLC, is
requesting the annexation of approximately 30 acres into the City of Logan. The property
is comprised of two separate tax parcels and is located on the North side of 600 South at
approximately 1600 West. The annexation plat will also include the adjoining right of
ways (600 South & 1900 West) to the centerline. The property is currently used for
farming/ranching activities.

The Future Land Use Plan (FLUP) Map identifies the site as Detached Residential (DR).
The proposed zoning is Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) which is consistent with the
Detached Residential FLUP designation and the adjacent development pattern directly
South.

City Recorder Tersea Harris certified the Annexation Petition on April 5, 2022. The
annexation was publicly noticed to the affected entities, including both school districts, as
required by State Law. The County Recorder also publicly noticed this annexation
request.

Chair Simmonds opened the meeting to a public hearing.

Logan resident Ben Jensen addressed the Councill and said he is the President of the
Logan River Trails HOA which is the area directly South and West of the proposed
annexation. He represents 53 homes in the area and while they don’t like to see the
farmland area go away, they appreciate they are proposing it as NR-6. The neighborhood
has concerns about infrastructure such as increased traffic, bike routes, the school
situation in which some are in Cache County Schools and others are in the Logan School
District. They would also like to have a park, trails, CVTD bus stops and fiber optic lines
closer to their neighborhood.

There were no further public comments and Chair Simmonds closed the public hearing
