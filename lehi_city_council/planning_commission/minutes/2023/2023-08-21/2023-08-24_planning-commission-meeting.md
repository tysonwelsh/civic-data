Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order

Chairperson Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Gardner-Plumb’s request for conditional use
approval of a double drive-thru for the View 21 East Retail Site Plan located at 3526 West
1940 North (Tabled from the July 13th meeting)

Applicant Howard from the Gardiner Company was available for questions. He addressed safety
concerns about glass, making suggestions while adhering to the code's emphasis on appearance.
He expressed satisfaction with the retail deal discussion. The double drive-thru involves one for
the multi-tenant building and another for a future soda kiosk, pending city approval.

Chairperson Newall opened the public hearing.
Chairperson Newall closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved approve Gardner-Plumb’s request for conditional use
approval of a double drive-thru for the View 21 East Retail Site Plan located at 3526 West 1940
North; including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report. The motion
was seconded by Commissioner Eyre.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Britton, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.2.) Consideration of ACG’s request for approval of the Mayflower Building 3 Site Plan on
0.6 acres located at 3387 West Mayflower Avenue.

The determination relied on neighboring building uses allowing parking bleed-over. The plat
permits cross-access, but imposing conditions on other buildings could be challenging. The
commission debated whether to require a traffic study for any change in use requiring more parking
for the proposed building, expressing concerns about potential penalties. Ultimately, they decided
to keep the condition for further review.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve recommendation for ACG’s request for approval
of the Mayflower Building 3 Site Plan on 0.6 acres located at 3387 West Mayflower Avenue;
including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff report. The motion was
seconded by Commissioner Bunker.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Britton, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3. City Business

Mr. Struthers reminded everyone of the EPA conference at the end of September on a Thursday
and Friday. The staff also discussed the Work Session items for September 7.

4. Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Brinton moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:20 p.m.
