26          1.1     (Project CUP-24-004) Request from Katie Orndorff for Conditional Use
27                  Approval to Operate a Home Business with Clients (Knockout Pilates) at 2363
28                  East 6710 South.
29
30   Administrative Hearing Officer, Michael Johnson, reported that the above request is a Conditional
31   Use to operate a home business with clients coming to the home.
32
33   City Planner, Maverick Yeh, presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is to operate a
34   Pilates studio from the home in a detached garage. An average of five customers per day were
35   expected to come to the home on Saturdays and Sundays. Each client will be served for 60 minutes.
36   The hours of operation are to be from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. No employees will come to the home.
37   Seven off-street parking spaces are available in the driveway and in front of the garage on the south
38   side of the property. No public comment was received by staff regarding the request. Staff found
39   that the business meets all requirements. No products will be sold from the home. The proposed
40   conditions and findings enumerated in the Staff Report were reviewed.
41
42   The applicant, Katie Orndorff clarified that the business will operate seven days per week and not
43   just on weekends. She did not expect to ever serve more than five clients per day and anticipated
44   closer to one or two. All classes will be private and semi-private.
45
46   Hearing Officer Johnson clarified that the recommended condition specifies that there shall be no
47   more than five clients per day. To go beyond that, the Conditional Use Permit (“CUP”) would need

12   Hearing Officer Johnson reported that home businesses are allowed everywhere in the City. Those
13   that have no clients coming to the home are approved automatically. The Code requires a hearing for
14   businesses that have clients coming to the home.
15
16   Hearing Officer Johnson moved to APPROVE Project CUP-24-004 subject to the following:
17
18   Conditions of Approval
19
20          1.      The applicant shall obtain and maintain an approved business license with
21                  Cottonwood Heights.
22
23          2.      Customers must use provided off-street parking, as the business may not rely upon
24                  any on-street parking. Customers must be made aware of these parking restrictions.
25
26          3.      The applicant shall adhere to the business hours of 7 am to 7 pm, Saturday and
27                  Sunday, with no more than five clients per day, as described in the written narrative
28                  in the official project file.
29
30          4.      Approval of this home occupation is subject to review upon complaint.
31
32          5.      No more than one non-resident employee is permitted at this business.
33
34          6.      All business activity shall be conducted in the loft above the garage. No business
35                  activity is permitted in the driveway area.

 6          1.2    Request by Church Stokes, Local Vacation Rentals, on Behalf of Michael
 7                 Williams, to Operate a Short-Term Rental at 3542 East Wasatch Grove Lane.
 8
 9   Hearing Officer Johnson reported that the above request is for a short-term rental to be located at
10   3542 East Wasatch Grove Lane. He noted that a number of licenses have been approved in the area.
11   One public comment was received on the request that was added to the record.
12
13   Hearing Officer Johnson opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public
14   hearing was closed.
15
16   Hearing Officer Johnson moved to APPROVE Project CUP-24-005 subject to the following:
17
18   Conditions:
19
20          1.     The applicant must complete the necessary steps to obtain a business license through
21                 the City of Cottonwood Heights. Obtaining a business license will indicate final
22                 approval of the short-term rental application. The short-term rental property will
23                 not be considered legal until a Business License is obtained.
24
25          2.     The short-term rental property is required to maintain compliance with all sections
26                 and subsections of Chapter 19.89 and Chapter 5.85 of the Cottonwood Heights
27                 Municipal Code, as hereafter amended from time to time, and all other legal
28                 requirements and applicable laws.
29
30          3.     The Operators shall provide a 24/7 hotline to resolve issues. Operators shall respond
31                 (in person, if appropriate) to telephonic complaints within one hour after such
32                 complaint is made. Inappropriate and/or non-response to such complaints shall
33                 constitute a violation.
34
35          4.     Occupancy in any short-term rental property shall not exceed the lesser of (a) up to
36                 two adults (persons aged 18 and above) and two related children (persons under age
37                 18) per bedroom, or (b) a total occupancy (adults and children) of no more than 12
38                 persons in the entire short-term rental property. It is prohibited to create artificial
39                 divisions or partitions for the purpose of increasing available occupancy of an
40                 otherwise standard dwelling unit.
41
42          5.     This short-term rental property will be approved for no more than four (4) bedrooms.
43                 The term “bedroom” means a room designated and used primarily for sleeping and
44                 resting on a bed.
45
46          6.     Upon issuance of a business license and conditional use permit, the applicant must
47                 display a copy of the Business License inside the main entry of the property.
