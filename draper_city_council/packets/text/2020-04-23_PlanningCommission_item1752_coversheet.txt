Re: The Avery Townhomes Final Subdivision Plat Comments: This application from Armando Alvarez is a request for approval of a final subdivision plat for approximately 2.48 acres located on the North side of 12225 South, at approximately 644 East.  The Planning Commission approved a site plan for 17 townhomes on the subject property on June 13, 2019, and approved the Preliminary Plat on February 27, 2020.  The entire project is encompassing two (2) undeveloped parcels that are accessed from an existing private lane off 12300 south. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan.
2.   The proposed development plans meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code.
3.   The proposed development conforms to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area.
4.   The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 4/17/2020 Cover Memo
