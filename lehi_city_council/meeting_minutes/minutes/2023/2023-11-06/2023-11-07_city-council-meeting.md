Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council and Planning Commission held Tuesday,
November 7, 2023, at Lehi City Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at
approximately 4:00 PM.

             Chris Condie, Council Member

Others Present: Beau Thomas, Deputy City Administrator; Attorney; Kim Struthers, Community
Development Director; Lorin Powell, City Engineer; Andy Gowans, Management Analyst; and
Teisha Wilson, City Recorder

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Koivisto joined virtually.

Mayor Johnson asked for a motion to adjourn into a closed session for the purpose of discussing
the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual.

Motion: Councilor Albrecht moved to adjourn into a closed session for the purpose of
discussing the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual.
Councilor Southwick seconded the motion.

Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

The meeting recessed at approximately 4:00 p.m.
The meeting resumed at approximately 4:30 p.m.

2. Fiscal Year 2023 PARC Tax Presentation and Reports
   The Council heard presentations from the Lehi Arts Council, Thanksgiving Point Institute, Lehi
   Historic Society and Archives, and Roots of Humanity Foundation on how they used the funds
