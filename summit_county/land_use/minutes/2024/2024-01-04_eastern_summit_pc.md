Public Hearing and possible action regarding a proposed Plat Amendment application for the Little Dipper Subdivision Lot 10, located at 7561 Escalante Loop, containing approximately 0.83 acres. Applicant: Jake Robert York. Administrative Review. Project #23-121.
Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planner. (6:01 P.M.)
Laura Kuhrmeyer,
County
Planner,
addressed Commission Members stating that new information had been received that morning preventing the planning department from providing a recommendation on the proposed plat amendment application. Planner Kuhrmeyer recommended the Commission open the public hearing and continue the item to a date certain of February 1st.
Commission members asked clarifying questions to which Planner Kuhrmeyer and Helen Strachan,
Attorney,
responded.
Commission Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:06 p.m.
No public comment was given.
David Darcey made a motion to move the item to a date certain of February 1st. Marion Wheaton seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Bill Wilde, Don Sargent, Alex Peterson.

Public hearing and action regarding a Final Subdivision Plat for the Promontory Hawk's Pass Subdivision (9 Lots), located off Double Deer Drive, Parcel SS-52 (396.98 acres - 5.7 acres used for the subdivision), Promontory, Summit County, UT; Applicant: Matthew Idema; Administrative Review. Project #23-178.
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, AICP.
Commission Chair Sonntag recused himself from discussion on agenda item number four (4).
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
presented the final subdivision plat for the Promontory Hawk's Pass Subdivision and recommended the Commission forward a positive recommendation to the County Manager for a final decision.
Commissioner Benson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:09 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commissioner Benson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:09 p.m.
Marion Wheaton made a motion to forward a positive recommendation for the Promontory Hawk's Pass Subdivision. David Darcey seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Bill Wilde, Don Sargent, Alex Peterson. Abstain: Rich Sonntag.
