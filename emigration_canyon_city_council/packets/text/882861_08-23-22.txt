                         The Meeting May Be Closed for Reasons Allowed by Statute

Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated 52-4-207(5) the undersigned, as Chair of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township
Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the electronic meetings of the Council will present a
substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:

The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under a “Medium Level of Transmission” alert stage for the
COVID-19 pandemic; and

The regular meeting place for the Council (the UFA Fire Station in the Canyon) does not have sufficient space in
the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing to keep people safe from infection.

This written determination shall expire 30 days from today. This meeting will not have a physical anchor location.
All attendees will connect remotely via Zoom.com (https://us02web.zoom.us/j/83328451408).
