Council Member Brems asked what Salt Lake County’s obligation is to the Metro

Mayor Smolka stated Lupita McClenning, Director, Salt Lake County Planning and
Development Division, will be assisting the Council until she can hire additional employees.
Council Member Paine stated hiring a part-time employee will not give the
Council what it needs like Ms. Weyher has.
Council Member Hawkes stated there has always been support staff to help.

support staff is already being paid for. Someone from Salt Lake County should be representing

Canyon and the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) should help pay those
wages. If someone is hired with no knowledge on how the Metro Townships work, it will take years
to train that person.
David Church stated the original concept for the Metro Townships was Salt Lake
County would be its staff and the Metro Townships would not need to hire employees. After the
Salt Lake County District Attorney’s Office was organized it was determined that they could only
provide certain services. The large Metro Townships felt that Salt Lake County was not working
in the best interest of the Metro Townships. The GSLMSD is becoming more independent from
Salt Lake County. Emigration Canyon Metro Township is not large enough to be able to hire its
own full-time staff. It will be a challenge to get the same type of service. Salt Lake County is doing
its best to protect its employees. The more independent the GSLMSD becomes the less Salt Lake
County employees are needed and the less Salt Lake County is willing to hire employees to help
with the GSLMSD.

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2 OF THE CURRENT APPLICABLE CODE
CONCERNING ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL IN THE TOWNSHIP
RECITALS
A. Utah State Code section 10-2a-414 provides that when a metro township is incorporated
that each county ordinance that is in effect on the day that metro township is incorporated
will remain in effect as a metro township ordinance until the metro township council
amends or repeals the ordinance.
B. The current code applicable in the Township establishes procedures and processes
regarding the administration and personnel management in the Township.
C. The Township Council desires to amend the provisions to fit the form of government in the
Township.
ORDINANCE
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:
I.

II.
III.

IV.
V.

The current code Title 2- Administration and Personnel is amended to read as shown in
Exhibit A hereto with the exception that Chapter 2.56 - COMMUNITY GREATER SALT
LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICTS AND COMMUNITY COUNCILS shall remain
in force and effect until modified.
This ordinance shall take effect upon publication and posting as required in Utah Code
10-3-711.
The Salt Lake County Clerk acting for the Township and municipal recorder/clerk pursuant
to Utah Code 10-3c-201(1)(a)(ii) is hereby authorized to provide appropriate title, chapter,
part, and section numbers for the provision of this ordinance and pursuant to Utah Code
10-3-713 shall record, in a book used exclusively for that purpose, this ordinance, along
with all ordinances passed by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township.
The recorder shall give this ordinance a number, if the Township Council has not already
so done.
Immediately following this ordinance, the recorder shall make or cause to be made a
certificate stating the date of passage and of the date of publication or posting, as required.
