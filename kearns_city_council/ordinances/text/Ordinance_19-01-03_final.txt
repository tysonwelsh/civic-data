TOWNSHIP FROM THE M-1 (INDUSTRIAL) ZONE TO THE R-M (MULTI-
FAMILY RESIDENTIAL) ZONE.
The Kearns Metro Township legislative body of Kearns Metro Township, State of Utah, ordains
as follows:
| Section 1: Section, 19.06.020, Zoning Maps of Kearns Metro Township Code of Ordinances
2018, is hereby amended, as follows:
The property described in Application #30737 filed by Greg Houge located at 4702 W. 6200
South within the Kearns Metro Township (the “Property”), is hereby reclassified from the M-1
(Industrial) zone to the R-M (Multi-Family Residential) zone, subject to the following zoning conditions
as set forth in section 19.90.060 of the zoning ordinance:
he The density shall not exceed 15 units per acre.
2. Residential uses are limited to single-family attached (townhome-style) and/or single family
detached structures.
