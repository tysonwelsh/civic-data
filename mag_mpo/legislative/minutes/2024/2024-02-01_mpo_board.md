The motion passed unanimously. Joint Policy Advisory Committee The Joint Policy Advisory Committee (JPAC) is the statewide forum for discussion of cross-agency transportation issues. JPAC also encourages seamless transportation systems by synchronizing long range plans and coordinating short range programs and considers financial strategies to fund projects. JPAC is comprised of both board members and executive staff from each of the four MPOs (MAG, WFRC, Cache, and Dixie), UDOT, and UTA. JPAC coordinates the development of the four MPO’s long-range transportation plans, as well as UDOT’s plans for the rural areas. Phase 4 was awarded $4,450,000 to widen from 300 East to 100 West in 2018. Phase 5 was awarded $5,270,000 in 2020 to extend from 100 West to 600 West and complete the project. In an effort to reduce costs and construction impacts, Santaquin has combined the two phases into one project. The project is fully designed and initially went out to bid with only one response totaling over $20m. Since that time, Santaquin went out to bid again with better results. Construction inflation raised the total cost to $17,726,127 and is requesting additional funds be added to the project. UDOT has already added $753,000 to aid with signal work and pavement preservation. Original Funds $9,720,000 UDOT Funds $1,753,000 New funding Needed* $5,195,160 New Project Total $16,668,160 Mayor Staheli moved to approve that $6,195,160 be added to the combined Santaquin Main Street Project. Mayor Johnson seconded the miotn. The motion passed unanimously. Airport Road, Eagle Mountain In 2022, Airport Road in Eagle Mountain was awarded county transportation tax funds to construct a new two-lane connection between SR73 and Eagle Mountain Town Center near Cedar Valley High School. This new project would add a new connection in a congested area. As the project was designed, it was determined that the intersection of SR73 needed to be modified to meet safety standards. In addition, cost estimates show additional funds are needed to cover construction inflation. Since the project was selected, a new school has been announced on the north side of the intersection. Eagle Mountain is requesting additional funds as well as a scope addition to accommodate the connection on the north side of SR73. TIP Selection Process, Project Ideas, and Study Approval (1:18:35) MAG is continuing the 2024 TIP selection process that began last year and will be completed in August. For the Project Idea Phase of the process, 50 projects were submitted with a total draft cost estimate of over $250M. On December 4th, TAC met to review and give feedback for each project. Additionally, MAG staff reviewed and rated each project based on the adopted selection criteria. The purpose of this phase is to help projects determine whether to continue with the process. No feedback or rating precludes a project from moving to the Concept Phase. Studies submitted through this process do not need to continue into the Concept Phase. The following studies are requesting to be funded.
