4.         22-279                 Administration recommending that the Council discuss staffing in the Fire 
                                  Department
                   Attachments:   Sandy Fire Staffing Memo.pdf

                                  Ms. Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor, introduced the speakers for tonight's presentation. Chief 
                                  Cline spoke about the staffing needs of his department and the impact staffing shortages 
                                  has on the mental and well being on the fire department personnel. He spoke about the 
                                  strain on his crews working with a two man crew. Mayor Zoltansk recently changed the 
                                  city policy, going to a 3-2 crew. Chief Cline spoke about the importance of maintaining 
                                  this staffing level and the impact on response time and the safety of our fire fighters. 
                                  Council questions and comments occurred throughout the presentation. Zach Whalen, 
                                  Finance, provided an overview of the funding sources that could be used to cover the 
                                  increase in personnel costs. Katrina Frederick, HR Director, provided additional 
                                  information and feedback from a recent survey taken by the Fire Department staff. 
                                  Council requested the survey information and  feedback - the Council has not seen the 
                                  survey results yet. Administration will provide additional information to the Council. The 
                                  Council expressed their support and commitment to addressing the needs of the Fire 
                                  Department. Mayor Zoltanski thanked the Council for their discussion this evening.  

                                  Council convened a recess at 7:36 pm.
                                  Council reconvened at 7:46 pm.

6.         22-275                 Community Development Department presenting progress regarding 
                                  moderate-income housing strategies and requesting further direction from 
                                  the Council to comply with the revised State requirements.
                   Attachments:   Recommended Strategies (7.14.22)
