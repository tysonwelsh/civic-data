Regular Session
(6:00 P.M)
1)
Public input for items not on the agenda or pending applications.
(6:01 P.M.)
Commission Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public input at 6:01 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Sonntag closed the meeting for public input at 6:01 p.m.
2)
Continued Discussion and Action for an amendment to a Conditional Use Permit to allow a Recycling Facility Class II at 7123 Lower Bowl Road Parcel # SS-72-2. Dan Meyer, Applicant. Project #23-173. Administrative Review.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner. (6:01 P.M.)
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner
, provided a brief overview of the proposed CUP and provided additional information from recent events. Planner Milliner reviewed items that had been raised during the prior meeting as well as amendments made to the conditional use permit.
Bill Wilde made a motion to approve the contractor yard and recycling facility on Lower Bowl Road per the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval as found in the staff report. Rich Sonntag seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Alex Peterson.
Attachment:
Meyer Staff Report Commission February 1, 2024.pdf
3)
Public Hearing and Action for a Conditional Use Permit for an Off-Site Parking Lot for an Event Center or Guest Ranch at 29499 Old Lincoln Highway Parcel #s NS-162-A, NS162-B and NS-162-C. Mike Phillips, Applicant. Project #23-171. Administrative Review.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner. (6:06 P.M.)
Planner Milliner presented a CUP for an off-site parking lot for an event center or guest ranch at 29499 Old Lincoln Highway.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions to which the applicant, Mike Phillips, and Planner Milliner responded.
Commissioner Sonntag opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:14 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commissioner Sonntag closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:14 p.m.
Commissioners asked follow-up questions to which the applicant, Mike Phillips responded.
Rich Sonntag made a motion to approve the matter with the amendment discussed. Marion Wheaton seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Alex Peterson.
Attachment:
Parking Blue Sky Staff Report Commission Feb 1.pdf
4)
Public Hearing and Action regarding the Crescent Lodge at Blue Sky Ranch Conditional Use Permit Amendment, located at 2071 South State Road 32, Wanship. Mike Phillips, Applicant. Project #22-167. Administrative Review.
Jennifer Strader, Senior County Planner. (6:20 P.M.)
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner
, addressed the commission and provided brief background information on the request for a CUP at the Crescent Lodge at Blue Sky Ranch and recommended the Commission approve the application.
Commission Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:23 p.m.
Val Bates was called to speak.
Mike Phillips,
Applicant
, responded to the comments made by Mr. Bates.
Commission Chair Sonntag closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:30 p.m.
Commissioner Darcy asked a clarifying question about possible traffic impacts with the addition of the project as well as maintenance of the road striping to which the applicant responded.
Rich Sonntag made a motion to approve the CUP. Marion Wheaton seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Alex Peterson.
Attachment:
Staff Report February 1, 2024.pdf
5)
**At the request of the Applicant this Public Hearing may be continued to a date uncertain.** Continued Public Hearing and Possible Action regarding a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) application for a Contractor's Yard located at 4782 W Browns Canyon Road, containing approximately 41.42 acres. Daniel Chavez, Applicant. Project #22-248. Administrative Review.
Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planner. (6:40 P.M.)
Rich Sonntag made a motion to postpone the item to a date uncertain. David Darcey seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Alex Peterson.
Commission Items
(6:49 P.M.)
Commissioner Darcy asked Director Putt if staff would be in attendance at the West Hills Incorporation meeting on February 12th.
Director Putt stated that staff would be attending the meeting.
Director Items
(6:42 P.M.)
Director Putt requested input from the Commission regarding the next Cedar Crest meeting at the Ledges and the large public hearing scheduled by the County Council for the Dakota Pacific project in Snyderville Basin. Both meetings were scheduled for February 15th. Director Putt stated he would work with staff to determine if the Cedar Crest meeting would move to February 22nd or February 29th.
Adjournment
(6:51 P.M.)
David Darcey made a motion to adjourn. Bill Wilde seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Alex Peterson.
