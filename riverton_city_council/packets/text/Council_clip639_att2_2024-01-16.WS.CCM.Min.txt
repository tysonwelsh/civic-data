 3            Resolution No. 24-11 – Approving an Amendment to the Riverton City Personnel
 4            Policies and Procedures Manual
 5
 6   Ryan Carter, City Attorney, discussed the City's default six-month probationary period for all
 7   newly hired employees. Recognizing that this timeframe may not be sufficient for certain roles,
 8   such as police officers who require more time to demonstrate their abilities adequately, the City
 9   would like to assess each job description individually. The goal is to determine whether a longer
10   probationary period, potentially up to a year, is necessary based on the specific job
11   requirements. This approach aims to be fair and tailored to the demands of each position,
12   ensuring that the probationary period aligns with what employees need to demonstrate before
13   transitioning to merit status. The decision on the probationary period length will be made at the
14   beginning of the employee's tenure, in consultation between the department head or city
15   manager.
16

20   discussion on the motion. Mayor Staggs called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows:

28            Building Use Fee Schedule
29
30   Sheril Garn, Events and Operations Director, discussed the need to revisit fees for the
31   community center. The existing fees date back to 2006, and she proposed updating them
32   before reopening the community center. She shared a spreadsheet comparing Riverton City's
33   fees with those of surrounding cities, highlighting that Riverton's fees were the most inclusive.
34
35   While not seeking a vote at the moment, Ms. Garn suggested taking a tour of the community
36   center during the next council meeting to provide a visual understanding of the available rooms.
37   Additionally, she sought input on rental fees for the pre-function area and whether it should be
38   considered a separate reservation space or included in the auditorium reservation.
39
40   Mayor Staggs asked if consideration for reserving the community center would be given to the
41   Arts Council or the Jazz Band. Ms. Garn explained that Riverton City's events take priority in
42   the schedule, followed by formally recognized organizations and then the general public. She
43   highlighted the current practice of not charging fees for certain community partners, such as the
44   South Valley Chamber, for using the council room. She also mentioned instances where state
45   legislators or groups conducting town hall meetings have not been charged a fee, considering it
46   a public service.
47
48   Councilmember Pierucci said he does not believe the City should aim to make a profit from
49   public facilities because these facilities are for public use. He voice support for maintaining a
50   low fee structure, even if it is dated. The majority of the Council agreed with Councilmember
51   Pierucci.

 4   Ms. Garn shared that despite initial considerations for changing the parade route to enhance the
