30   2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Rafael Pena’s request for conditional use approval
31   for JMR Trucks & Equipment with vehicle storage located at approximately 4250 North
32   Digital Drive.
33   Brittney Harris presented information on the item.
34
35   The applicant, Rafael Pena, was present. There was some confusion on the parcel record. Rafael
36   clarified he was asking for the permit for a food truck. The use will not be used during the
37   winter. The trucks are being parked next to Freedom Trucking School. The trucks parked to the
38   East and South of the area are not their trucks. The site is specifically for parking and not used
39   for mechanical purposes. The parcel is leased. There is a tent, and the Fire Marshall is coming
40   for the inspection.
41
42   Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.

69   2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Dave Scoville’s request for review of the
70   Newbold Estates zone change, 7.3-acres located at approximately 600 North 500 West,
71   changing the zoning from A-1 (agriculture) & R-2 (medium density residential) to R-1-Flex
72   (single family residential).
73   Brittney Harris presented information on the item.
74
75   The applicant, Dave Scoville, was present.
76
77   Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.
78
79   John Rockwell stated his power, gas, and cable lines are on the north side of his property and are
80   in the way of putting in the cemented ditch. The developer will be required to move his utilities
81   so there isn’t any interference. He is concerned that the development will encroach on his
82   property. He has spoken with Mr. Struthers. He is also concerned about the children’s safety
83   walking to and from school. He would strongly suggest a traffic study on 500 West. His property
84   is zoned agriculture, and he would like to keep the zoning. He respects property rights and would
85   just like the same in return.
86
87   Joseph West stated he is opposed to the development but understands it will change. He does not
88   want the property taxes to spike and would still like the view. He is also concerned about the
89   traffic and the size of the homes that are being built.
90

     Lehi City Planning Commission                   2                                  January 11, 2024
 91   Neil and Jana Newbolt are concerned about some property lines. The driveway will be affected
 92   by the development depending on the zoning. Mr. Struthers stated that zoning will need to be
 93   resolved before the county will record the property.
 94
 95   Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.
 96
 97   The applicant addressed the comments. He stated he has no control over the taxes that
 98   homeowners are responsible for paying. The ditch is being installed on the applicant's property.
 99   The applicant stated he has tried to communicate with the Newbolds with no success. Mr.
100   Scoville stated he bought the property from a trustee sale but is happy to work with the
101   Newbolds to work something out.
102
103           Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Dave Scoville’s
104           request for review of the Newbold Estates zone change, 7.3-acres located at
105           approximately 600 North 500 West, changing the zoning from A-1 (agriculture) &
106           R-2 (medium density residential) to R-1-Flex (single family residential); including all
107           DRC comments and findings stated in the report, with the recommendation to work with
108           neighboring property owners, specifically the Newbold’s to have a property line survey
109           done, and the Rockwell’s gas line and powerline to ensure suitable opportunity to work
110           through those issues. Commissioner Eyre seconded.
111
112           Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
113           Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
114           unanimously.
