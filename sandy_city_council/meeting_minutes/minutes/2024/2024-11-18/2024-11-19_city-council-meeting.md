General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                       Council Chair Robinson invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                       Public comment opened.

                       Mr. Josh Chandler expressed support for the Sister City item on the agenda. Today is the
                       1,000th day of the Russian invasion of Ukraine.The Sister City relationship gives us an
                       opportunity to provide some good for an area in Ukraine.

                       Ms. James Sweeten was involved with and an exchange with the Soviet Union while he
                       was in high school and in 1993 served an LDS mission in the area. He spoke about Deloy
                       Hansen's humanitarian efforts in Ukraine. Bucha, Ukraine is a suburb similar to Sandy
                       City. Sandy would be well served by having a Sister City relationship with Bucha.

                       Ms. Katherine Kair expressed concerns about Rocky Mountain Power proposed rate
                       hikes of about 30%.

                       Mr. David Edgel expressed support for establishing a Sister City relationship with Bucha,
                       Ukraine. They are proud people and we can learn so much from a partnership with Bucha,
                       Ukraine.

                       Mr. Bruce Roberts expressed support for a Sister City relationship with Bucha. He went
                       there to help displaced persons and after three years of visiting Ukraine, he has fallen in
                       love with the people of that country. Establishing a Sister City with Bucha would be very
                       meaningful for the people of Ukraine.

                       Public comment closed.

                       Council moved back to Item 1 on the Agenda and resumed the presentation.

1.         24-405                 Community Development Department presenting Appendix A (Station
                                  Area Planning) and Appendix B (Neighborhood Activity Centers) of the
                                  draft Pace of Progress: Sandy General Plan 2050, and addressing any
                                  follow up questions on previously reviewed sections of the General Plan.
                   Attachments:   Appendix A-Station Area Plans
                                  Appendix B-Neighborhood Activity Centers
                                  City Council Presentation-SAPs & NACs
                                  Memo-Land Use Table
                                  Memo-Union Park NAC

                                  Jake Warner, Community Development, introduced the team working on the General
                                  Master Plan. We are here to provide the information and listen to your feedback on the
                                  General Master Plan. We appreciate your time this evening. Last week, we spoke about
                                  the Moderate Growth Plan. This evening's evening's presentation will focus on Station
                                  Area Planning (SAP) and Neighborhood Activity Centers (NAC).

                                  Ms. Erika Chmielewski with GSBS Architects provided an overview of the Appendix A
                                  (Station Area Plans) and Appendix B (Neighborhood Activity Centers) of the draft Pace of
                                  Progress: Sandy General Plan 2050. She reviewed station area plan (SAP) requirements,
                                  objectives, methodology, and community engagement. Station area plan requirements
                                  establish a vision, and the actions needed to implement that vision, for the development
                                  of land within a station area. She reviewed HTRZ funding criteria and thresholds and
                                  reviewed the future land use map designations for SAPs. Ms. Chemielewski reviewed the
                                  following SAPs with the Council: Historic Sandy Station Area, Sandy Expo Center
                                  Station Area, Sandy Civic Center Station Area, South Jordan Frontrunner Station Area,
                                  and Crescent View Station Area.

                                  Council paused the presentation and moved to General Citizen Comment at 6:31 pm.
                                  Council resumed the presentation at 6:19 pm.

                                  Ms. Chmielewski provided an overview of Neighborhood Activity Centers (NAC). The life
                                  cycle of a NAC is as follows: emerges, growth, maturity, decline,
                                  revitalization/reinvention, and continued evolution. She reviewed the following NAC's:
                                  Union Park Center, Fiesta Village, Alta Canyon Center, Bell Canyon/Dimple Dell, Sandy
                                  Village Center. 79% of Sandy residents support the development of mixed-use
                                  community centers as described in NACS. NAC make up 304 acres of Sandy City.

                                  Council questions, discussion, and comments occurred throughout the presentation.

                                  Jake Warner thanked the Council for their time this evening.

                                  Council convened a short recess at 8:21 pm
                                  Council reconvened at 8:28 pm.
                                  Council Member Houseman was excused from the meeting at 8:30 pm.

2.         24-413                 First Reading: Council Member Mecham proposing the creation of a new
                                  Sister City relationship with the Ukrainian City of Bucha
                     Sponsors: Mecham

                   Attachments:   Bucha Memorandum
                                  Hansen Ukrainian Mission
                                  Draft Bucha Cooperation Agreement
                                  Hansen Ukrainian Mission - All Projects Overview 2024.11

                                  Council Member Mecham introduced the item and discussed the Sister City program with
                                  the Council. Currently, Sandy has two Sister City relationships with Piedras Negras,
                                  Mexico, and Riesa, Germany. The Piedras Negras program focuses mainly on
                                  humanitarian support. The Riesa, Germany program focuses mainly as a cultural
                                  exchange. Council Member Mecham discussed the reasons for establishing a Sister City
                                  relationship with Bucha, Ukraine. He spoke of the humanitarian efforts of Deloy Hansen,
                                  who is currently building housing developments for the Ukranians who have been
                                  displaced as a result of the ongoing war. A video presentation was shown detailing the
                                  housing projects in Ukraine. The city of Bucha is located within a hour of the these three
                                  housing projects showcased in the video. Council feedback and discussion followed.
                                  Council expressed overwhelming support for establishing a Sister City relationship with
                                  Bucha, Ukraine. Mayor Zoltanski expressed her full support for establishing a Sister City
                                  with Bucha, Ukraine. Council unanimously agreed to suspend the Council Rules of
                                  Procedure to allow them to take action and vote on directing the Council and Mayor to
                                  sign the Cooperation Agreement between Sandy City and Bucha, Ukraine, thereby
                                  formally establishing a Sister City relationship with Bucha, Ukraine.

                                  A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey to suspend
                                  the Rules of Procedure and the Council Legislative Policy...The motion carried
                                  by the following roll call vote:
