                               RESOLUTION NO. 21-33

      A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING ALAN KIRTON, KATIE OVARD-SMITH,
      AND NATE SHIPP TO THE DRAPER CITY SPECIAL EVENT ARENA
      COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has adopted Ordinances which provide for
the appointment of members to the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee;
and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, terms of office are set to be four-year terms; and

     WHEREAS, the Mayor proposes to reappoint Alan Kirton, Katie Ovard-Smith,
and Nate Shipp to serve as members of the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

     The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s
reappointment of Alan Kirton, Katie Ovard-Smith, and Nate Shipp to serve as
members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

        Section 1. Appointment. The following individuals are hereby reappointed to
fill the term listed below as a member of the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee according to the laws, ordinances and regulations governing the Draper
City Special Event Arena Committee and its members for the term specified herein.

             Four-year term:

             Alan Kirton                July 20, 2021, through July 31, 2025
             Katie Ovard-Smith          July 20, 2021, through July 31, 2025
             Nate Shipp                 July 20, 2021, through July 31, 2025

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 20TH DAY OF JULY, 2021.
