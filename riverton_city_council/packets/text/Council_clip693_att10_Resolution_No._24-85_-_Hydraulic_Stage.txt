                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-85

A RESOLUTION DECLARING THE CITY'S PORTABLE STAGE AS SURPLUS AND
TRADING THAT STAGE IN TOWARDS THE PURCHASE OF A USED HYDRAULIC
             STAGE FROM CITY CENTER SOUND & STAGE

        WHEREAS, the stage the City currently has is a liability because it is heavy and is
difficult to set up and tear down; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no
longer needed as surplus in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Recreation Director would like to purchase a hydraulic stage; and

        WHEREAS, City Center Sound and Stage would sell us a used hydraulic stage and buy
the City’s portable stage; and

     WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve this in a future budget
amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council declares the following equipment as surplus:

           SC90 4' x 8' stage platforms to make up a 40' x 60' stage and SC9600 bridging
           understructure, includes end frames, cross bracing, top plates, two (2) stairs with
           handrails, IBC guardrail and storage carts.

       2. The City Council also approves selling/trading in the surplus stage and purchasing a
          used hydraulic stage from City Center Sound & Stage.

       3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 20th day of
August, 2024 by the following vote:
