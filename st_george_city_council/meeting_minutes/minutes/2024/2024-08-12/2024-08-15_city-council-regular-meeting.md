CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Snow Canyon Construction for the St.
        George City Pool Outdoor Deck Replacement project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City pool is nearing its 50-year
          anniversary in 2025. Over the years it has provided a refuge from the heat,
          summer jobs, and a gathering place for reunions, parties, etc. The project was
          put out to bid on July 15, 2024; four (4) bids were received and opened on
          August 1, 2024. Snow Canyon Construction is the low responsible bidder in the
          amount of $269,312.25. This amount includes the Base Bid and Additive
          Alternate 1. Staff recommends approval.

INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT/RESOLUTION:
     Consider approval of Resolution No. 2024-026R approving an Interlocal
     Agreement between St. George, Hurricane, Washington, Santa Clara,
     Enterprise, and Washington County for building inspection services.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Utah legislature amended Utah Code
      Title 10, Chapter 6, Section 160 regarding fees for collection of construction approval
      and approval of plans. This amendment was effective May 1, 2024. The amendment
      requires that if a city cannot provide a building inspection within three business days
      after the on which the city receives the request for the inspection, the building
      permit applicant may engage a third-party inspection firm from the third-party
      inspection firm list (as described in chapter 6.) To meet this requirement, the City
      wishes to enter into an Interlocal agreement with various surrounding governmental
      entities. The agreement provides that the entities will be on each other's "third-party
      inspection firm list." In the event the City cannot perform the inspection within three
      days, the applicant can contact an entity on the list to do the inspection. The City will
      pay $87.00 per inspection to the entity performing the inspection, and vice versa.
      The interlocal agreement sets forth the participating entities and the procedures by
      which they will abide in order to fulfill the requirements of the amendment. The
      agreement terms are based on discussions among the participating entities' building
      officials and their legal counsels. Staff recommends approval.
