PUBLIC HEARINGS

2.     TXT-21-06; Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment; Citywide; Text Amendment
       Modifying Public Notice Requirements in Chapter 17-3 of the Midvale City Municipal
       Code; Midvale City (Applicant); Alex Murphy, Planning Director (Staff).

Mr. Murphy stated that earlier this year, the Utah State Legislature adopted changes to state code
that affect noticing requirements for land use regulations. As Staff reviewed the changes in state
code, it became apparent that the notice requirements in the City's zoning ordinance are out of
date and need updating. Furthermore, Mr. Murphy stated that in discussing legislative updates
with City Council, the council members wanted to add expanded requirements to noticing. Mr.
Murphy presented the proposed text amendment which modifies noticing requirements in
Chapter 17-3 to match or exceed current state code requirements including, removing outdated
language and references, adding definitions, and removing newspaper notice requirements.

                                               ***
PUBLIC HEARING:

Vice Chair Anderson opened the public hearing.

There were no comments.


MOTION:        Commissioner Snow MOVED to close the public hearing. The motion was
               SECONDED by Commissioner Benson with all voting in favor.

                                               ***
DISCUSSION:

Mr. Murphy stated that this item is a legislative action. The Planning Commission has some
flexibility when it comes to making a decision that they feel is in the best interest of the city.
Ultimately this will be a recommendation to City Council, who will then make the final
determination.

Commissioner Snow asked if the property owners of the subject property needed to sign some
form of documentation before a property is brought before the planning commission?

Mr. Murphy stated that it depends. He added that there is a process where staff can propose a
rezone without the owner's approval. Therefore, if the owner is not the party requesting the rezone,
then staff is required to provide them notice within a larger noticing process.

Mr. Murphy stated that staff took out the requirement of noticing through the newspaper as it does
not exhibit a substantial impact on notifying residents and impacted the scheduling process.

Vice Chair Anderson asked how the city can educate the residents on the various ways they can
find out what items are being brought before the Planning Commission and City Council.

Mr. Murphy stated that he can speak to the Communications Director on communicating to
residents the various ways to find out what items are being brought before the Planning
Commission and City Council. He also added that there will be more property postings with these
proposed changes, which will be visible to residents who may not have received the letter.

                                                ***
MOTION:        Commissioner Snow MOVED that we forward a recommendation to the City
               Council to adopt the proposed text amendment to modify public notice
               requirements in the City's zoning ordinance. The motion was SECONDED by
               Commissioner Benson. Vice Chair Anderson called for a roll call vote. The
               vote was as follows:
                      Vice Chair Anderson         Yes

3.     TXT-21-07; Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment; Jordan Bluffs Subareas 1-3
       Development Zone; Text Amendment Modifying Certain Development Review
       Procedures in the Jordan Bluffs Subareas 1-3 Development Zone; Midvale City
       (Applicant); Alex Murphy, Planning Director (Staff)


Mr. Murphy presented a text amendment for Jordan Bluffs Subarea 1-3. These amendments
would provide an alternate review path for small municipal facility projects and would remove a
requirement for a development agreement for all municipal facility projects. He added that at this
time this would only impact 2 future projects, the Center Street Boat Ramp and the Sewer
Vacuum Pump Station, but will allow for a more streamlined process if there are other projects
in the future.
