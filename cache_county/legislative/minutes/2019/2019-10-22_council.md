proposed Connector Route will be between the South and North WalMart’s with a loop to

« Discussion — Waste Collection Fees — Director Jensen reviewed the probable billing
costs and different billing methods and fees. He feels the automatic pay will be the least
costly to the county. Council member Borup suggested a no-fee option for those making
an annual payment instead of being billed monthly. The majority of Council members
noted their preference for a $3.00 admin fee for people receiving paper statements, a
$1.00 admin fee for people signed up for auto-pay, and no admin fee for people who pay
for the full year in advance. Council member White doesn’t like the idea of a larger fee
for people wanting to pay monthly in person by cash or check and said it seems like a
big jump from the $1.00 admin fee Logan was charging to a $3.00 admin fee for the
county. Council] members also expressed agreement with charging late payment fees in
the amount of 1.5% per month, or 18.0% per year.

e Discussion -- Request for additional personnel for Impound Facility — Sheriff

Jensen presented possible employee numbers and wages noting he doesn’t really know
how many employees will be needed until the facility has operated for a time. Nor does
he know the hire dates which depend on when the construction is completed. Jensen
has received the lease agreement and the architect has two sets of plans which will be
put out to bid. The bids may determine which of the plans will be selected.

Jensen also presented the number of proposed employees for a countywide probation
program — four fulltime employees (deputies) and one fulltime office specialist. He wants
to meet with other sheriff's about the countywide probation proposal. There may be
some state funding available. The Council is supportive of Jensen moving ahead.
