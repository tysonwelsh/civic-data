Robert McConnell, PEG’s legal counsel, focused on proposed Finding of Fact No. 134 and
addressed the issue of compatibility. He stated that compatibility is defined in the Code as the
“characteristics of different uses or designs that integrate with, or related to one another to maintain

compatibility include, but are not limited to, height, scale, mass, and bulk of a building; pedestrian
and vehicular circulation; parking; landscaping and architecture; topography, etc. He reported that
there is also guidance from the General Plan with respect to this project. The project is located within
the Resort Center planning area identified in the 2014 Park City General Plan, which expressly
contemplated the redevelopment of the property. The General Plan recognized that future
development within the Resort Center would create a denser village core.
