    RESOLUTION FINDING HAZARDOUS ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS AND
        RESTRICTION OF FIREWORKS WITHIN EMIGRATION CANYON

       WHEREAS, Utah Code $53-7-225, prohibits the discharge of Class C common state
approved explosives (fireworks) except around certain holidays including beginning on July 2
and ending on July 5, and beginning on July 22 and ending on July 25;

         WHEREAS, Utah Code $ 15A-5-202.5(lxb) allows municipalities to "close a defined
area to the discharge of fireworks due to a historical hazardous environmental condition" if the
"historical hazardous environmental condition has existed in the defined area before Julv I of at
least two of the preceding five years;"

       WHEREAS, the Emigration canyon council ("council") finds that the
City, throughout all areas, contains innumerable mountainous, brush-covered, forest covered,
and dry grass-covered areas which historically and, for at least two of the preceding five years
before July I st, are in an extremely flammable condition;

        WHEREAS, if existing or historical hazardous environmentalconditions exist within the
boundaries of the City, Utah Code $15A-5-202.5 allows the Council to prohibitthe ignition and
use of fireworks while these conditions exist in the following areas: (l) mountainous, brush-
covered, forest covered, or dry grass-covered areas; (2) within 200 feet of waterways, trails,
canyons, washes, ravines, or similar areas; (3) wildland urban interface area, which means the
line, area, or zone where structures or other human development meet or intermingle with
undeveloped wildland or land being used for an agricultural purpose; or (4) a limited area
outside the hazardous areas;

        WHEREAS, the Council finds that the entire Emigration Canyon consists of the above
listed hazardous areas and a map of the city is attached hereto as Exhibit A.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON
CITY COUNCIL OF EMIGRATION CANYON, UTAH, THAT:
        Section I: Pursuant to the provisions of Utah Code Ann. l5A-5 -202.5, and based on the
findings as noted above, the use of Class C fireworks are hereby restricted in all areas of
Emigration Canyon City as shown in the attached map in Exhibit A.

      Section II:        ve Date. This resolution shall be effective upon receipt of the attached
map (Exhibit B) by Salt Lake County and publication of the Resolution by the City Recorder.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the Emigration Canyon City Council, Utah, this 2l sr day
of April inthe year 2026.
