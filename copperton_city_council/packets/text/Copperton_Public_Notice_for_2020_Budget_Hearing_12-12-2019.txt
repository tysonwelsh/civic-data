       Copperton Metro Township
   NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARINGS TO
     CONSIDER THE 2020 BUDGET

The Copperton Metro Township Council will hold a public
hearing on December 20, 2019, to consider the adoption of
the proposed 2020 budget for the Copperton Metro Township.
The hearing will begin at 6:00 pm and will be held at the
Copperton Improvement District, located at 8565 W State
Hwy, Copperton, Utah, 84006.

All interested persons in attendance will have an opportunity
to provide comments on the proposed 2020 budget.

The proposed 2020 budget is available for public review on
the       Copperton       Metro      Township      website
(https://coppertonutah.org/). For copies of the budget and
additional information or questions, please contact Mayor
Sean Clayton at seanclayton@coppertonutah.org or (801)
615-3900.
