Citizen Public Comment
Kirtly Jones stated he is concerned about camps that are being erected with tents,
small structures and are littered with beer cans. There is a lot of deadwood that is off the ground
that looks like fire material. Rocky Mountain Power has not changed the mechanics or sturdiness
of the high-tension wire that was blown down onto a snowplow last winter. He is concerned that
the canyon needs more attention on forest fire risks and plans in the case of a fire.
Bill Tobey, Chair, Emigration Canyon Community Council, stated the community
will not have access to Unified Fire Authority’s chipping services anytime soon. A newsletter will
be mailed with information on resources to remove debris from resident’s property.

Emigration Canyon Community Council Report
Bill Tobey, Chair, stated Lisa Schneider has resigned from her seat on the
Community Council and Dan Anderson is co-chair. Firewise has indefinitely been postponed and
chipping services probably will not happen until fall. The CERT program and training is on hold
until access to the fire station is available. Those interested in the CERT training program can do
the training online. The newsletter went out on March 23 and another one will go out this weekend.
There is a planned roadside clean-up on May 16th, it will be a family or individual activity. A
preliminary list of potential restroom locations was completed and submitted to the Metro
Township Council.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes asked if Salt Lake County has the green waste cans that
can be put on the canyon road.
Mr. Tobey stated yes, one free trailer rental for residents is available in June. It is
first come first serve.

Annual Fee Schedule
The Council reviewed the following Resolution amending and adopting a fee
schedule for fees and other assessments for services provided by the Emigration Canyon Metro
Township:
RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-01

Date: April 23, 2020

A RESOLUTION AMENDING AND ADOPTING A FEE SCHEDLUE FOR FEES
AND OTHER ASSESSMENTS FOR SERVICES PROVIDED BY THE
EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
RECITALS
A. The Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“Emigration Canyon”) is a municipality pursuant
to Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq. and as such has to power to assess
and collect fees for services and to recover the reasonable cost of regulating certain
activity within the Township.
B. The Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body of Emigration Canyon pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.
C. The Township provides services and regulates activity within the Township and in this
regard has previously established or been the successor of Salt Lake County to previously
established fees and assessments for these services and activities.
D. The Council feels it is necessary to amend the previously adopted fee schedule to reflect
increase costs and other changes in the Township activities.
RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township
Council that the attached Exhibit A hereto is hereby adopted as the Fee Schedule for the
Emigration Canyon Metro Township to be in effect from the 23rd day of April 2020 until amended
or repealed pursuant to law.

Provisions to Support Businesses During COVID-19
Mayor Smolka stated this provision helps businesses that are experiencing
hardships due to COVID-19. It will allow extensions on various fees or bonds and possibly have
penalties removed. The Council has the discretion to allow the planners to decide what fees
should be postponed.
Mayor Smolka, seconded by Council Member Brems, moved to direct planning
staff to determine if fees from the fee schedule need to be reduced, deferred, postponed, or
penalties waived. The motion passed unanimously.

Hazard Mitigation Plan
The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting the 2019 Salt Lake County
Hazard Mitigation Plan:
RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-02

Date: April 23, 2020

A RESOLUTION OF EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
ADOPTING THE 2019 SALT LAKE COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATION
PLAN
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council recognizes the treat that
natural hazards pose to people and property within the Township; and
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township has participated in the creation of a multihazard mitigation plan, hereby known as the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan in
accordance with the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000; and
WHEREAS, the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan identifies mitigation goals
and actions to reduce or eliminate long-term risk to people and property in Emigration Canyon
Metro Township from the impacts of future hazards and disasters; and
