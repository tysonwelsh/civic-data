5. WORK SESSION
   5.1       The Forge
       Community Development Director Morgon Brim gave an overview of the
       Forge master plan.
       Jeff and Steve Borup with Dakota Pacific presented on a new master plan
       for The Forge.
            There was a discussion concerning why there is no viable interest in
       office growth at The Forge.
            There was a discussion concerning The Forge master plan fitting the
       intent of the general plan.
            There was a discussion concerning what investors have been interested
       in the property.
            There was a discussion concerning the proposed changes in office and
       residential ratio from the original development agreement.
             The presentation continued.
            There was a discussion concerning access from Vineyard Connector and
       Geneva Road.
            There was a discussion of whether the residential units will be rented or
       privately owned.
            The presentation continued.
            There was a discussion concerning affordable housing in The Forge.
            There was a discussion concerning the number of units in the Water’s
       Edge subdivision.
            The presentation continued.
            There was a discussion concerning the total amount of commercial
       proposed in The Forge.
            There was a discussion concerning parking requirements.
            There was a discussion concerning how to get development started.
            There was another discussion concerning the total amount of
       commercial area proposed in The Forge.
            The presentation continued.
       There was a discussion concerning how RDA money will be used.
             The presentation continued.
            There was a discussion concerning parks and green space.
            There was a discussion regarding the next steps for the project.

            There was a discussion regarding other developments requesting more
       residential units.
            There was another discussion regarding affordable housing.
           There was a discussion regarding the ratio of commercial to residential.
