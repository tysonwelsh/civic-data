      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: In 2018, when the Desert Color development

      Some of those commercial and residential areas were designated with an overlay such
St. George City Council Minutes

      as the resort area or the Horizontal Mixed-Use area. Approximately half of the subject
      property is designated straight Planned Development Commercial (PD-C) while the
      other half has the Horizontal Mixed-Use overlay. The property directly north is also
      designated with the Horizontal Mixed-Use overlay. The PD-C zone does not allow
      straight residential unless this overlay is applied. The developer is proposing to shift
      the Horizontal Mixed-Use overlay from the property to the north to the subject
      property so as not to lose any of the commercial zoning. This proposal is for an
      apartment development. The underlying general plan is Town Center (TC). The
      Planning Commission held a public hearing on the proposal on January 11, 2022 and
      recommended approval.

TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATION/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of a temporary land use regulation amending §10-8B-1,
     §10-8D-1, and §10-10-1 of the St. George City code removing amusement
     center, recreation, and entertainment facilities (indoor and outdoor) and car
     wash as uses in the zoning regulations and requiring all development
     applications to acknowledge water supply conditions.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This ordinance will temporarily prohibit
      certain land uses within the currently adopted zoning regulations of the code,
      specifically those uses with historically above average water usage to determine if
      other measures and standards can be or should be imposed to meet Goal VII of the
      General Plan. Staff recommends approval.
