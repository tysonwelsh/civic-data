**1.**

Questions to the Mayor from the City Council.

**Summary:**

Council Member Lopez Chavez asked Rachel Otto, on behalf of the Mayor, to address public concerns regarding federal collaboration, funding, data privacy, public safety, and a 287(g) Agreement between Salt Lake City (SLC), the Salt Lake City Police Department (SLCPD), and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), an agreement which gave local police the power to enforce federal immigration law.

Rachel Otto stated on behalf of the Mayor that Salt Lake City and the Salt Lake City Police Department collaborated with federal agencies on violent crime and public safety matters, but did not participate in civil immigration-related operations; the City did not provide public funding to ICE for civil immigration enforcement activities; the City had not entered into and would not enter into a 287(g) agreement with ICE; and both the City and SLCPD remained committed to community safety for all residents regardless of immigration status while supporting the right to peaceful protest.

Council Member Puy thanked the Mayor’s Office for their comments and noted the importance of supporting Salt Lake City residents.
