2. BUSINESS ITEMS
    a. Resolution No. 112 adopting the Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building
       Authority of the City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2025 and setting June 5, 2024 as
       the date for a public hearing regarding the adoption of the final budget.
       Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck explained that the Municipal Building
       Authority collects lease payments from the General Fund and Utility Funds for leasing
       the public works building. This lease revenue is used to pay the debt service on the
       building. The debt for the building's construction was issued on December 1, 2016, for
       $24,960,000, with a total repayment of $40,380,675 over a term of 23 years, requiring
       equal annual payments of $1,850,000. Revenues are generated from lease payments,
       and one year of debt service is held in a trust account, accruing interest, which is then
       used for debt payments. If the city chose not to lease the building to itself, it could lease
       it to another party, but the city would still be responsible for making the debt payments.

        MOTION:     Vice Chairperson Lamb moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 112

                    as the date for a public hearing regarding the adoption of the final
                    budget.
