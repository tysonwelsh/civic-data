#1 Public Hearing (5:45 p.m.): Birch Hollow Rezone 
 
Harrild reviewed the staff report. 
 
05:37:00 
 
Olsen arrived. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed notification of surrounding property owners and access. 
 
Duane Williams access has been approved through UDOT.  
 
Smith it was already subdivided when you bought it? 
 
Mr. Williams yes. It has to be rezoned to be useable and probably can only have 4 homes. 
 
05:46:00 
 
Smith motioned to open the public hearing; Parker seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
Alma Burgess made a comment about keeping the zoning agricultural, water, and notification of the 
Munk family. 
 
Lamont Poulsen made a comment about keeping the zoning agricultural, road concerns, and consistent 
application of the ordinance for rezoning for residential. 
 
Jeff West made a comment about water, utilities, access, and the land staying agricultural. 
 
Mr. Williams stated the top 5 acres would remain as an agricultural remainder, 4 homes would have a 
minimal impact to SR 218 for traffic.

Olsen motioned to close the public hearing; Smith seconded; Passed 6, 0.

#2 Public Hearing (6:00 p.m.): M & R Rezone 
 
Harrild reviewed the staff report. 
 
Staff and County discussed road maintenance. The Governor’s office of Economic Development is 
helping provide grant funds for the road. 
 
6:16 
 
Sands motioned to open the public hearing; Christensen seconded; Passed 5, 0. 
 
Brett Hawkins discussed the application for the rezone.  
 
Mel Torrie presented on ASI and agricultural robotics.  
 
Brett Hawkins mainly ASI is a software company. They make the software to partner with the robotics. 
 
Mel Torrie addressed the road concerns. There will be asphalt all along the road to the north. 
 
Kyle Yonk made comments supporting ASI and had concerns with the land being rezoned to commercial. 
 
06:25:00 
 
Sands motioned to close the public hearing; Smith seconded; Passed 5, 0. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the rezone and road. A Conditional Use Permit (CUP) will still be 
required. 
 
Sands motioned to recommend approval of the M & R Rezone to the County Council based on the noted 
findings of fact and conclusions; Parker seconded; Passed 5, 0. 
 
06:35:00 
 
#3 Nixon & Nixon, Inc. Conditional Use Permit 
 
Harrild reviewed the staff report.  
 
Staff and Commission discussed the irrigation line that goes through the property.

Jack Nixon suggested moving the irrigation line to the east end of the property but currently the gravel 
pit is staying 25 feet away from it.  
 
Christensen isn’t there a complication that doesn’t allow the irrigation to move it?
