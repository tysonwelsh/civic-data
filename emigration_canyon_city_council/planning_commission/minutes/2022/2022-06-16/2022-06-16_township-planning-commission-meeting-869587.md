2) Dark Skies Ordinance Working Update. Planner(s): Jim Nakamura/Matt Starley
Mr. Starley provided a PowerPoint presentation of the Dark Sky Ordinance Draft.
Commissioners and Staff had a brief discussion regarding non-conforming uses, use providing
information, rather than providing assistance. Indoor lighting regulations, use intrusion rather than
trespass as a violation of a vested property right. Legal should review the term “trespassing”.
Commercial properties should consider removing ”Unshielded”, as that would be used for large
parking lots.

PUBLIC HEARING(S)
OAM2022-000601 – An Ordinance amending the Emigration Canyon Land Use Code, Foothill Canyons
Overlay Zone (FCOZ), Section 19.72.130, “Stream Corridor and Wetlands Protection.” The proposed
amendment will amend the Perennial Stream Corridor Setback for Buildings, accessory structures, and
parking lots from 100’ to 50’ measured from the ordinary high-water mark. The proposed amendment will
eliminate setback requirements for on-site wastewater disposal systems, which are regulated by Salt Lake
County Health Department. Planner: Jim Nakamura (Motion/Voting)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jim Nakamura provided an analysis of the staff
report.
Commissioners and Staff had a brief discussion regarding Mayor Smolka request regarding citizens
restriction and the 100’ have caused problems, perennial stream definition, Emigration stream issues
different from other streams and studies done and to be done.
Commissioners are concerned with the lack of input and response from EID and the Health Department
and think should be continued until the July meeting. Consideration needs to be completed, nothing wrong
with more protection.
Commissioner Wallace motioned to open the public hearing.
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED
No one from the public present to speak.
