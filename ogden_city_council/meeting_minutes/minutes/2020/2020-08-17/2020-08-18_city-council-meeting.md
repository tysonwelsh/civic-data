Proposed Ordinance 2020-36 amending Municipal Code Subsection 15-34-
2. D to allow an accessory apartment in existing single-story buildings in the
CBD Zone
           A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding proposed text amendments for the
Central Business District (CBD) zone came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated Ogden City Planning is
proposing an amendment that would allow accessory apartments in the Central Business District zones, which include the CBD
and CED-Intensive zones. An accessory apartment is a dwelling unit that is contained in a structure that was originally designed
and is still used as a commercial building. The current CBD and CBD-I zoning standards allow for additional dwelling units to be
located above first floor commercial uses but not on the same floor in a single-story building. The proposed amendment is
intended to provide additional flexibility and reuse of existing single-story buildings in the Central Business District area.
          Planning Manager Montgomery summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on the
intent of the proposed text amendments. The item being presented for consideration is to allow an accessory apartment in a single-
story building in the Central Business District of the City; the dwelling unit would be allowed so long as it is located above first
