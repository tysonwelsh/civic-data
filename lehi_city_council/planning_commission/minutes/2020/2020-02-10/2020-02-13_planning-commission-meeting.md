3.1 Public hearing and recommendation of Innovation Pointe’s request for review of a
proposed zone change from Business Park to Commercial on 35-acres located at
approximately 1450 West Innovation Way.

        Chairman Hemmert asked Ross Dinsdale about the impact to traffic. Mr. Dinsdale stated
        that it shouldn't change much. Chairman Hemmert clarified about that it would add
        traffic during more hours instead of at certain times. Mr. Dinsdale agreed.

        Commissioner Nielsen pointed out that this had been denied because there wasn't enough
        landscape, but we are letting them come up with the lesser landscape option.

        Mr. Struthers discussed how the theme in landscaping has changed and their thought is
        that this is an area that we want to encourage more density and not as much landscaping.
        Commissioner Nielsen felt that if it fits with the existing then why change it.

        The applicant Kent England said that with the slope they can't hit the 30% so they were
        able to get around 20% so with talking with staff about other options this looked like the
        best way to go. Their plan is to put a third building and then potential retail on 1200
        West, but they aren't sure yet.

        Chairman Hemmert opened public hearing for public comment.

        Jonathan Madsen asked if more stuff goes in that area will there be more help to the
        intersection to make it easier to pull out.

        Chairman Hemmert closed public comment.

        Commissioner Peterson asked for Mr. Dinsdale to clarify Mr. Madsen's questions Mr.
        Dinsdale said that most likely that will become a right in/right out area so that people
        won't be able to cross there. Mr. Dinsdale noted that they have a traffic study for the area
        overall.

        Commissioner Nielsen doesn't feel that it is pedestrian friendly or walkable with a
        business park and that it is what we are encouraging and allowing them to do here.

        Commissioner Peterson discussed how the commission has been conflicted on this piece
        for a long time and the issue with topography.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to send this to the Council with a positive
        recommendation for Innovation Pointe’s request for review of a proposed zone change
        from Business Park to Commercial on 35-acres located at approximately 1450 West
        Innovation Way including the DRC comments, with the findings stated in the staff report.
        Commissioner Peterson seconded.


        Vote: Commissioner Eyre, Yes; Commissioner Everett, Nay; Commissioner Peterson,
        Nay; Commissioner Hemmert, Nay; Commissioner Nielsen, Nay; Commissioner Bunker,
        Nay; and Commissioner Hereth, Yes. The motion does not pass.

3.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Jessica Lloyd’s request for review of a proposed
General Plan amendment from Low Density Residential to Commercial on 1.4 acres located
at approximately 400 West Bull River Road.

        Applicant Jessica Lloyd stated her whole purpose is trying to meet the need for childcare
        for employees in businesses in the surrounding area.

        Chairman Hemmert opened the public hearing for public comment.

        Jonathan Madsen- Noted the traffic concern. He also discussed a concern with the
        topography, and he's hoping it won't go up to 40 feet. The other concern is the cul-de-sac
        easement in the left corner and parking.

        Melissa McAllister- felt that the retaining wall next to this business is the biggest eye
        sore and her concern is that she doesn't want people to be driving on her property. She
        doesn't feel Bull River Road is wide enough for people to be driving there and driving
        both directions. She is also concerned about the storage place next to a childcare facility.
        She felt this would make the property values go down.

        Allan Holladay- He was worried about similar concerns especially about the cul-de-sac
        where he lives to have parking in that area. He was extremely disappointed with the
        retaining wall. He doesn't want the same thing to happen with another retaining wall.

        Devin Noise- his concern is about the eyesore and the cul-de-sac and doesn't feel like
        Bull River could handle the traffic.

        Chairman Hemmert closed public comment.

        Chairman Hemmert stated that he had concerns with this area. He did not like the idea of
        continuing a retaining wall.

        Commissioner Peterson stated that there weren’t certain items that they could discuss
        because it isn't a site plan. His concern that they aren't looking at a site plan that if they
        change it to commercial, they are opening up to the entire table of the commercial.

        Mr. Dinsdale noted that they could require the access and not let it go through the
        neighborhood. Chairman Hemmert asked Mr. Dinsdale to discuss topography. Mr.
        Dinsdale said that they haven't seen any proposed grading, but Bull River is higher than
        the cul-de-sac and doesn't think that it would get bigger.

        Commissioner Nielsen asked about the plans to widen Bull River road. Mr. Dinsdale
        stated that there is no timeline on Bull River road, but it is a city project. Commissioner
        Nielsen asked about curb and gutter. Mr. Dinsdale said that he thought that they would
        have to complete the curb and gutter.
    Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved that this be sent to the Council with a negative
    recommendation of Jessica Lloyd’s request for review of a proposed General Plan
    amendment from Low Density Residential to Commercial on 1.4 acres located at
    approximately 400 West Bull River Road including the DRC comments, with the findings
    stated in the staff report. Also with the finding that the commercial is not compatible in this
    neighborhood and without a proposed site plan and elevations and knowing exactly what’s
    going in there they are opening up that area to more than just a building and adding that
    neighborhood commercial is a better fit for this property as mentioned by DRC.
    Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion.

    Vote: Commissioner Eyre, Yes; Commissioner Everett, Yes; Commissioner Peterson, Yes;
    Commissioner Hemmert, Yes; Commissioner Nielsen, Yes; Commissioner Bunker, Yes; and
    Commissioner Hereth, Yes. The motion passed with all in favor.

3.14 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of the following
Lehi City Development Code amendments:
    •   Chapter 17, Planned Unit Developments - clarifying open space, compatibility and garages
    •   Table 05-030-A, 05-030-B, and 05-030-C – adding Cannabis related businesses and Bakery,
        Retail as uses on the tables.
    •   Chapter 12, Development Standards – adding Cannabis related business regulations,
        requirements for Bed and Breakfast Inns, and Short-Term Rentals
    •   Chapter 37, Design Standards – updating signs and lighting regulations in the Historic
        District
    •   Chapter 39, Definitions – updating various definitions and adding definitions for Twin
        Home and Bakery, Retail

        Staff explained each of the development code amendments and the commission gave
        input and asked clarifying questions.

        The Commissioners brought up concerns for the sections on Bed and Breakfast about the
        wording for right next to schools when hotels have 500 ft. so there needs to be more for
        continuity.

        Commissioner Nielsen asked to clarify where it talks about responding to calls. If it needs
        to be physically responding and if we need to write that in the code.

        Chairman Hemmert opened public hearing for public comment.

        Chairman Hemmert closed public comment.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for review
of the following Lehi City Development Code amendments:
• Chapter 17, Planned Unit Developments - clarifying open space, compatibility and garages
• Table 05-030-A, 05-030-B, and 05-030-C – adding Cannabis related businesses and Bakery,
Retail as uses on the tables.
• Chapter 12, Development Standards – adding Cannabis related business regulations,
requirements for Bed and Breakfast Inns, and Short-Term Rentals
• Chapter 37, Design Standards – updating signs and lighting regulations in the Historic District
• Chapter 39, Definitions – updating various definitions and adding definitions for Twin Home
and Bakery, Retail. With adding the corrections, they discussed. Commissioner Ellis seconded.
