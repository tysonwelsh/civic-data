                                RESOLUTION 25-63



        A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ACCEPTING A QUIT
              CLAIM DEED FROM TRIPLE S INVESTMENT CO., LLC


      WHEREAS, Draper City owns real property throughout the city; and

       WHEREAS, Triple S Investment Co. LLC, as Grantor desires to convey real
property to the city for the purpose of cleaning up a small parcel remaining from an
earlier property conveyance; and

      WHEREAS, the city wishes to accept the conveyance of real property; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code §10-8-1 and Draper City Municipal Code 2-
1-030, the Draper City Council has the authority to hold title to property.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Approval. The City Council hereby approves Resolution 25-63,
accepting a quit claim deed from Triple S Investment Co., LLC identified in “EXHIBIT
A” as attached hereto.

     Section 2. Effective Date.        This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon passage.
