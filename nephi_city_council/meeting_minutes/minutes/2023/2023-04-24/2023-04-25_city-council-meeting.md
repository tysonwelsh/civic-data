                         NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                Tuesday, Apr 25, 2023
                                     21 E. 100 N
                                   Nephi, UT 84648


1.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed the preliminary tentative budget. He
     explained that in the general fund he has assumed a .25 cent increase in the dispatch
     fee. He reported that three big projects in the water fund are included in the budget.
     The projects include - Nebo Heights waterline project, refurbishment of the Worwood
     Well and the Firehouse Well, and a realignment of the waterline up the canyon due to a
     UDOT project The budget assumes a sewer base rate increase of $4.00. Electric
     department budget include the 138 Kv line. The gas department budget includes a new
     12” line through town, an upgrade of the Payson gate station and the construction of the
     power plant gate station. Mr. Atkinson reported that the solid waste fund will need to buy
     a new truck in 2025. He warned the council that someday we may need to add a
     second truck and second driver to meet demand. He suggested that the City implement
     small incremental raises in the solid waste fees. Atkinson reported that the PAR tax is
     doing well and that the interfund loans will be paid off in FY 2024. Councilor Memmott
     requested a summary sheet of the fee and rate increase budget assumptions.

2. The council discussed scheduling the budget hearing for June 6th at 7:00 p.m.

3. Mr. Atkinson reviewed police station options. Two options have presented themselves
   over the last couple of years. The first is on land that was formerly the Greenhalgh
   property (now called Crimson Flats) owned by the city. That property has since been
   sold to The Wright Direction LLC and the city has an agreement with that company to
   allow for land to be donated back to the city for the purposes of constructing a police
   facility. The second option is to partner with Juab County to construct a property as part
   of a larger prison/Sheriff’s station building.
       ○ Option with Juab County - Construction cost estimation is $7,000,000 based
            on sq. ft. costs from the architect working on the facility for Juab County. This
            does not include any lease costs for usage of land. If the City were to lease the
            facility from the County, the lease rate would be based on this construction cost
            as well as any costs for land. The facility will have shared spaces which may
            include a training room, a gym, and potentially a gun range. The facility will
            depend on a voter approved bond set for the November 2023 election. The total
            facility cost is estimated at somewhere between $50-$65 million. The County
            has a bonding capacity of approximately $36 million. The remaining $14 million
            to $29 million would need to be acquired from some other source.
                      Pros of this option include:
                ■ Working with other agencies to encourage intergovernmental
                      cooperation.
                ■ Shared facilities could save some costs.

   ○   Option with Crimson Flats property - Nephi City has options with this property
       and land for the Police Station will be donated. Options include leasing a
       building after construction by the Wright Direction, LLC at a cost of approximately
       $20,000/year depending on how much square footage that will be used by the
       NCPD. The City would need to construct tenant improvements at a cost of
       approximately $1.5 million with the lease option. The Wright Direction, LLC
       would allow the city to work toward a lease to buy option and purchase the facility
       in the future. The City could also construct the building on their own at a cost of
       approximately $5 million. The City could then lease parts of the building to other
       tenants that may include other governmental agencies or commercial
       businesses. The Wright Direction, LLC has offered to construct an indoor gun
       range as part of the facility.
            Pros of this option include:
            ■ Flexible options that could work well with the limited resources available
               to the city.
            ■ Costs that seem to indicate feasibility.
            ■ Nephi City would control their own timeline to some degree. The Wright
               Direction, LLC may decide to construct a facility with or without the city’s
               involvement.
               Cons of this option include:
            ■ May not include a direct partnership with the Sheriff’s department.
            ■ The facility may not have a Justice Court. T
            ■ There may exist duplicate shared facilities if the County facility is also
               constructed.

The City could potentially pay for either option with Public Safety impact fees, a property
tax increase, and/or potentially increased sales tax once Crimson Flats begins to be built
out.
Councilor Ostler said because of our track record with the county he did not want to go

Coucilor JD Parady said just on economics he would prefer not going with the county.

building but did not want to go with the county. Callaway said that he had researched

   4.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed the final draft of the 40-year water rights plan. Councilor Ostler
        said that he did not see the Worwood Well mentioned in the report. Mr. Atkinson said he
        would talk with Sunrise Engineering about that. Memmott asked after we adopt the plan
        what is our next step. Memmott said that the council had discussed requiring property
        owners to bring water rights with them at the time of annexation. Mr. Atkinson answered
        that those type of discussions can happen after the final adoption of the 40-year plan.

   5. City Administrative Updates: Regional Growth Summit will be held on May 25 sponsored
      by Six County Association of Governments. Seth Atkinson gave an update on the Reed
      Ridge Development.

   6.    City Council Updates: Councilor Callaway reported on a class he attended at ULCT
        water and cyber security. Councilor Memmott complimented the work that has been
        done on the hollow by the Streets Department. Mayor Seely complimented Ian Harward
        for the reverence he exhibited while preparing his mother’s grave. Mayor Seely also
        complimented Gerratt Bethers for his attention to detail with the flower beds. Councilor
        Worwood reported on a class he attended about communication in crisis management.
        Councilor Parady reported that the Chamber is having two ribbon cuttings on Friday, one
        for Holiday Inn Express and one for Lacey Bakes.

Mayor opened the regular meeting

      Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

      of real property and character and competence of an individual.
      Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 8:35 pm

   3. The council came out of executive session and convened into a regular meeting.
      Councilor JD Parady moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Ostler seconded the
      motion. The motion passed unanimously.
