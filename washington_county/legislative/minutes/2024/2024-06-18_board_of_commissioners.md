REPORT AND CONSIDERATION OF 2024 WASHINGTON COUNTY TAX SALE

Clerk-Auditor Ryan Sullivan reported on three properties that were sold during the 2024 Tax Sale.
Two properties, parcels 4191-A-2-L-HV and H-GANV-B-15-A were sold at auction. Any excess
funds collected over the amount owed are allocated to the recorded lien holders or property owner.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Ratify the Two Sold Properties During
the Washington County Tax Sale. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and
Snow voting aye.

Mr. Sullivan requested that property W-208-C be stricken to Washington County, as it is a portion
of a roadway in Washington City. Washington County will communicate with Washington City
to deed this roadway portion to the City.

CONSIDERATION OF THE 2024 CERTIFIED TAX RATES FOR WASHINGTON
COUNTY

Mr. Sullivan explained the certified tax rate for Washington County will be as follows: General
Operations rate is 0.000373; the Interest and Sinking Fund/Bond is 0.000010; the Library Rate is
0.000129 for a total budgeted revenue of $18,888,179. The County Assessing & Collecting Rate
is 0.000230 for a total budget of $8,589.926.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve 2024 Certified Tax Rates. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF PROCLAMATION NO. 2024-35, A PROCLAMATION
DECLARING JUNE 19, 2024 AS JUNETEENTH IN WASHINGTON COUNTY

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated that Washington County has recognized this holiday
since 2022, but this is the first time there has been a Proclamation regarding Juneteenth. He then

read the Proclamation aloud.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Proclamation No. 2024-35, A
Proclamation Declaring June 19, 2024 as Juneteenth in Washington County.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. _R-2024-3321, _A _ RESOLUTION
DEMONSTRATING WASHINGTON COUNTY’S SUPPORT AND APPROVAL OF TRT
FUNDING FOR MATCHING FUNDING FOR THE UTAH OFFICE OF TOURISM CO-
OP MARKETING PROGRAM

Greater Zion Marketing Manager Sara Otto explained there is an annual co-op funding process
from the Utah Office of Tourism, and this Resolution will affirm that Washington County will
match up to $250,000 in matching funds to attract out of state visitors to Washington County.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3321, A
Resolution Demonstrating Washington County’s Support and Approval of
TRT Funding for Matching Funding for the Utah Office of Tourism Co-Op
Marketing Program. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting

aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3322, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A MARKETING SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT WITH BLACK DESERT RESORT

Sports and Adventure Manager Chad McWilliams explained that Black Desert Resort is scheduled
to host annual PGA and LPGA tournaments beginning in 2024. Greater Zion has negotiated an
agreement for marketing sponsorship with Black Desert Resort, including placement at events and
over 50 commercials on the golf channel.

John Coolbaugh with Black Desert Resort relayed the excitement of the Black Desert team for the
upcoming events.

Koko Head, full time managing council for Black Desert, mentioned upcoming developments in
the Black Desert area, including homes and restaurants.

Dani Eilander discussed her excitement to live in Washington County full time and to be a part of
Black Desert.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3322, A
Resolution Approving a Marketing Sponsorship Agreement with Black Desert
Resort. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF _RESOLUTION NO. __R-2024-3325, A _RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING FUNDING FOR ENGINEERING SERVICES ON PHASE 1 OF THE
ZION CANYON SEWER EXTENSION PROJECT

Regional Tourism Regulatory Affairs Director Heath Hansen explained the Utah Legislature
previously appropriated $18 million dollars to the County to fund the Zion Canyon sewer extension
and trail expansion project. Ash Creek Special Service District and Sunrise Engineering have
negotiated to complete engineering design services for the first phase of a sewer line extension.
This resolution provides for the county’s reimbursement of costs for the design services.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3325, A
Resolution Appropriating Funding for Engineering Services on Phase 1 of the
Zion Canyon Sewer Extension Project with $202,100 going to Sunrise
Engineering and $5,000 going to Ash Creek Special Service District. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.
