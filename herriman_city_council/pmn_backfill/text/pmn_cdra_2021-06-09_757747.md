                                 COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND
                               RENEWAL AGENCY OF HERRIMAN CITY
                                          MINUTES
                                                 Wednesday, June 9, 2021
                                                  Approved July 6, 2021

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City. The
meeting was held on Wednesday, June 9, 2021 at 6:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair David Watts

Directors Present:         Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: Interim City Manager Wendy Thomas, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Community
Development Director Blake Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director
Alan Rae, Director of Operations Monte Johnson, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, City Attorney Chase
Andrizzi, Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, Deputy Director of Parks, Recreation and Events
Anthony Teuscher, City Planner Michael Maloy, Economic Development Manager Heather Upshaw, City
Engineer Jonathan Bowers, Building Official Cathryn Nelson, Unified Fire Authority Division Chief Anthony
Widdison, Deputy Chief of Police Cody Stromberg, and Sergeant Brent Adamson.

6:30 PM: (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER)
   1. Call to Order
Chair Watts called the meeting to order at 6:47 p.m.

                1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of
                      June 9, 2021
Director Smith moved to have the minutes of June 9, 2021 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Community Development
and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary, if after ten
days, there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes are made
and the board is in agreement. Director Shields seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    2. Discussion and Action Items
            2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution of the Community Development and
                 Renewal Agency of Herriman City to adopt the final budget for the fiscal year beginning
                 July 1, 2021 and ending June 30, 2022 – Alan Rae, Finance Director
Finance Director Alan Rae indicated the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City has
three differed Community Development Project Areas: Herriman Towne Centre, Herriman Business Park and
Anthem. He explained no expenditures had been incurred in the Herriman Towne Centre and noted all
reimbursements would be considered by the Community Development and Renewal Agency board prior to being
disbursed.

Director Ohrn moved to approve Resolution No. R2021-02 adopting the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman
City Final Budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2021 and ending June 30, 2022. Director Smith seconded the motion. (This
resolution was renumbered from R2021-01 to maintain sequential numbering)

The vote is recorded as follows:
Director Jared Henderson                           Aye
Director Steven Shields                            Aye
Director Sherrie Ohrn                              Aye
Director Clint Smith                               Aye
Chair David Watts                                  Aye
The motion passed unanimously.

    3. Adjournment
Director Smith moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 6:54 p.m. Director Shields seconded
the motion, and all voted aye.


I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on June 9, 2021. This document constitutes the official minutes for the
Community Development and Renewal Agency Meeting.
