Elizabeth Arnold said the property is in the MU zone with the 7200 S Overlay. Across Fort
Union to the north is a SF-1/DO with single family homes. To the south is a SF-1/DO zone
with several duplexes. There are MU zones to the east and west. All parcels along Fort
Union from State St to 700 East are in the 7200 S Overlay zone. Adjacent neighborhood

VIII. DISCUSSION ITEMS
       A,      DISCUSS A PROPOSED TEXT AMENDMENT TO THE MIDVALE
               MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING CHAPTER 13.16 STORM WATER
             ORDINANCE
Keith Ludwig said Clean Water Act. This required the City to get a discharge permit from
UDEQ with all its requirements, including creating a storm water management plan.

Each year the State (UDEO) conducts audits of various cities' and counties' storm water
programs to evaluate how well they are preforming their storm water duties.

Early last March staff had the opportunity to spend two days being interviewed by the
Division of Water Quality personnel. These meeting were held virtually. Prior to those
interviews, we sent them records, reports, and other paperwork regarding our storm water
program.

They also came out and did site inspections at our Public Works facility and the cemetery.
There were also two construction sites inspected: the Moda Union project on Cottonwood
and Millennium Drive Way, and the Holmes project at 700 West and Bingham Junction
Blvd.

On May 12th of this year, we received their report of our audit outlining the deficiencies
that needed to be corrected. We then had 30 days to respond with a corrective action
plan and a timeline in which to complete them. Most of the items had to do with creating
standard operating procedures (SOP) for our activities, documenting our activities, or
doing more inspections. There were several that dealt with our storm water ordinance
and changes or additions that needed to be made. Hence my visit with you this evening.
After reviewing their report, we worked up a corrective action plan with some items being
taken care of by August 30th and others by December 1st.

The items due by August 30th were completed and submitted well before the deadline
and have been acknowledged as being complete by the State. Eight of the remaining
twelve items deal with the storm water ordinance.

1      Update the ordinance to restrict non-stormwater discharges. Section 13.16.120
       Dumping and lllicit Discharge was changed to more explicitly define allowed and
       prohibited discharges into the storm drain system.
