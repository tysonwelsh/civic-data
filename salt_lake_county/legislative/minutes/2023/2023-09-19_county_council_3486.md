2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Mr. Tom Diegel , Chair, Central Wasatch Commission ’s Stakeholders Advisory Council 
on Millcreek Canyon, stated the County obtained a Federal Lands Access Program 
(FLAP) grant and identified different projects for the use of that grant, one of which was 
to improve the safety of the upper portion of Millcreek Canyon.  The elements identified 
for improving safety were widening the road with 10-foot lanes, striping the road, 
straightening the curves, and improving the sightlines.  He thought straightening and 
widening the road seemed misguided.  Research indicates narrower roads and less striping 
make drivers more attentive and reduces their speed.  There is also going to be a bike lane 
from the winter gate up to Elbow Fork, but no bike lane for the rest of the canyon.  He 
was concerned the road was not being made safe for all users.  Based on public input on 
records, neither the County, Federal Highway Administration, nor the Forest Service were 
listening to what people wanted.  This is a once in a generation opportunity to update the 
canyon appropriately.   
Council Member Bradley  stated Mr. Diegel raised some excellent points about the initial 
goals for the project.  He had also had concerns about widening and striping the road 
because that does increase speed.  He asked if the project could be adjusted.  
Ms. Catherine Kanter , Deputy Mayor of Regional Operations, stated the Forest Service 
owns the road and the County manages and maintains it.  The impetus for pursuing the 
FLAP grant was because a group wanted a shuttle in place in Millcreek Canyon, but the

portions of the road that are in serious need of repair.  She recommended putting this item

Discussion/Direction
Ms. Dina Blaes , Director, Regional Development Department, reviewed the 
following resolution authorizing execution of an interlocal cooperation 
agreement, whereby the Bluffdale Redevelopment Agency is contributing 
Housing Allocation Dollars to the County ’s Housing Trust Fund.  Week after 
week, the County gets reports about the shortage of housing units and the 
difficulty many families are having finding housing, so the County is trying to 
help find solutions while ensuring the health of the Housing Trust Fund.  It is 
trying to work with municipal partners to help solve this and help them meet 
the goals of their moderate income housing plans.  Two individuals from 
Bluffdale City will work with the County on the release of the city ’s funds .  
They will go to the Housing Trust Fund Advisory Board to see if it will issue 
a Request -for-Proposal (RFP) or Request -for-Application (RFA) for 
projects, depending on what Bluffdale City ’s needs are, and they will be 
integrally involved in helping the County choose the developers and the 
projects for the use of these funds.  
Mr. Grant Crowell , City Planner /Economic Development Director, 
Bluffdale City, stated Bluffdale City is looking to leverage its resources and 
get some success with moderate income housing, and maybe eventually other 
cities will join in.  The interlocal agreement is scheduled on Bluffdale City 
Council’s October 11, 2023, agenda for a vote.

RESOLUTION NO. 6145
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
AUTHORIZING EXECUTION OF A SUBDIVISION PLAT IN KEARNS 
METRO TOWNSHIP AND RELATED QUIT-CLAIM DEEDS
 
RECITALS
A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) has an ownership interest in real 
property located in Kearns Metro Township, in an area known as Camp 
Kearns (“Property”).
B. The County cooperated with several adjacent property owners to 
dedicate land for the development of public roads in Camp Kearns, resulting 
in the recording of the Camp Kearns Road Dedication Plat (the “Road Plat ”) 
on May 3, 2021.
C. After recording the Road Plat, the County still owned property in gaps 
between parcels owned by adjacent property owners and the dedicated 
public road (the “Gap Parcels”).
D. As part of the agreement with the adjacent property owners to create 
Page 12 of 15
County Council Meeting Minutes September 19, 2023
and construct the public roads, the County had agreed to convey its interest 
in these Gap Parcels to certain adjacent property owners.
E. After the construction of the public roads and recording the Road 
Plat, additional discrepancies were discovered concerning ownership 
interests to the public roads.
F. In order to address these ownership discrepancies and to complete the 
transfer of the County ’s interests in the Gap Parcels, several adjoining 
property owners and the County have prepared a subdivision plat (the 
“Plat”) establishing the final boundary lines between the properties located 
within the platted area and have agreed to quit -claim any interests outside of 
their individual lots to the owners of the individual lots in the new 
subdivision.
G. A copy of the Plat is attached hereto as Exhibit A, and copies of the 
quitclaim deeds to be signed by the County (the “Deeds”) are attached 
hereto as Exhibit B.
H. It has been determined that the best interests of the County and the 
general public will be served by executing the Plat and the Deeds attached to 
this Resolution.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the Plat, attached hereto as Exhibit A, is hereby 
approved, and the Mayor and County Clerk are hereby authorized to execute 
the Plat in substantially the form attached as Exhibit A for recording.
