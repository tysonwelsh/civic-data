NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—September 13th @ 7pm.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          South Salt Lake resident, Susan Bowlden, wanted to talk about the municipal
          election and how she is putting information about the candidates on a Facebook
          page, ‘South Salt Lake for Real Transparency’ that she is part of. There is also
          another Facebook page, ‘Stand Up for South Salt Lake’ that will have some
          information.

            Granite Library manager, Kim Bryant Welch, talked about some upcoming events
            and activities at the local library.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood talked about the Housing Plan that the City has been working on. As
          part of the process, there is a survey for residents to complete and take part of to
          give input. The goal is to reach 500 responses. The survey will be open until the end
          of September and can be found on the City’s website: sslc.gov

            September 18th - The ‘Get to the River Festival’ takes place from 6-8 p.m. along the
            Jordan Trail.

            September 23rd – Residents can join to paint the parkway along the Jordan River.

            September 29th – The City will host it’s 85th Anniversary Celebration from 5-8:30
            p.m. at the Central Park Community Center. There will be activities for all ages and a
            drone show at 8:30 p.m.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Huff talked about the ‘Grid City Festival’ that took place this
          weekend and shared how it was a fun event to attend.

            Council Chair Bynum talked about back-to-school season and reminded everyone to
            drive safely around school zones.

            Council Member Pinkney talked about the Black Physicians of Utah hosting an
            upcoming event the Co-Op center to talk to kids about mental health, bullying, etc.
