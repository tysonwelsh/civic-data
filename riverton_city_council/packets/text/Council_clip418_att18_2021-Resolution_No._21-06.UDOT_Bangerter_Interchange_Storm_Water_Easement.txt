                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-06

   A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE RELINQUISHMENT OF A STORM WATER
      EASEMENT INTEREST IN REAL PROPERTY TO THE STATE OF UTAH,
                   DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION
         (UDOT Project No. S-0154(84)14- Bangerter Highway Interchanges)

      WHEREAS, the State of Utah, Department of Transportation, (“UDOT”) is currently
engaged the process of designing and improving the interchange of Bangerter Highway and
12600 South Street, Riverton Utah (the “Project”); and

        WHEREAS, UDOT, has acquired right of way from property owners in the vicinity of
the project necessary to improve said interchange; and

       WHEREAS, in the month of February, 2006, Riverton approved and permitted to be
recorded the Riverton Crossing Commercial Subdivision Plat (the “Subdivision”), located
generally on the northwest corner of Bangerter Highway and 12600 South Street; and

       WHEREAS, the recording of the Subdivision in effect granted Riverton City a storm
water easement to allow for the above-ground flow of storm water between lots within said
subdivision; and

      WHEREAS, UDOT needs to acquire approximately 752 square feet of said storm water
easement to construct improvements required by the Project; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a storm water easement
interest in 752 square feet of real property is an insignificant property interest, its relinquishment
to UDOT will help facilitate construction of the project, and said relinquishment will not
adversely affect the flow of storm water within the storm water system originally designed for
the Subdivision.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves the Relinquishment of Easement
          Document, attached as “Exhibit A” which grants Riverton City’s storm water
          easement interest to UDOT. The Mayor is authorized to sign the Relinquishment of
          Easement Document upon verification that the same is approved as to legal form by
          the Riverton City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
