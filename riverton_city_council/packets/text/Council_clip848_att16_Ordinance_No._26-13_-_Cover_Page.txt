ITEM:
Ordinance No. 26-13 - Amending Sections 18.90, 18.05.030, and 18.195.060, adopting standards for
“Ambulance Service/Emergency Medical Services Facility” in the Commercial Gateway Zone, Amendments
proposed by Gold Cross Ambulance. - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director
On May 14, 2026, the Planning Commission held a public hearing on this proposed text amendment.
Minutes from that meeting are included below. The Planning Commission recommended approval, with the
following recommended motion

“I move the City Council adopt Ordinance 26-13, amending Sections 18.90, 18.05.030, and 18.195.060,
adopting standards for “Ambulance Service/Emergency Medical Services Facility” in the Commercial
Gateway Zone, as shown in Exhibit “A.”


SUMMARY:


Riverton City code includes a Table of Commercial Uses in Section 18.90.010. The Table of Uses lists
many Land Uses and lists whether each use is Permitted (P), Not Permitted (NP), or Conditional (C) in each
of the six commercial zones. From time to time the Table of Commercial Uses is reviewed and modified to
meet the current needs of the City.
This amendment, brought by the applicant seeks to amend the table of uses so that Ambulance Services
becomes a Conditional Use in the Gateway (C-G) zone and introduces language defining Ambulance
Services and includes conditions governing the Ambulance service use.
