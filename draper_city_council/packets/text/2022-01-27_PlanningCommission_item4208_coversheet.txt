Re: MD Aesthetics Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.04 acres located within the Cottages on Kimball’s Lane development located south of Kimball’s Lane, at approximately 11815 S Cottage View Ln. The property is currently zoned R3. The house was constructed in 1997 and is approximately 2,955 ft 2 in size, excluding the garage. The applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for a personal care services home occupation that provides personal cosmetic services to clients consisting of the administration of Botox and other dermal fillers by means of injection.   The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For Denial:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects, including traffic and parking.
