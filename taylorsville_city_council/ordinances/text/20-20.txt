                                       ORDINANCE NO. 20-20
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE AUTHORIZING THE CREATION OF
THE TAYLORSVILLE CITY POLICE DEPARTMENT TO PROVIDE LAW ENFORCEMENT
              SERVICES WITHIN THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE.
        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on December 2,
2020, to consider, among other things, authorizing the creation of the Taylorsville City Police Department
to provide law enforcement services within the City of Taylorsville; and
      WHEREAS, in June 2020, the City of Taylorsville notified the Unified Police Department
(“UPD”) of its intent to leave UPD and create its own police department; and
       WHEREAS, on July 1, 2020, the Council ratified the City’s intent to leave UPD via Resolution
No. 20-30; and
        WHEREAS, the City desires to now create its own police department in part to have more
influence and control over police services at a time when more direct involvement, more direct influence
over policy, and more direct responsibility and accountability are imperative to public safety; and
        WHEREAS, in addition to more local influence and control, the City desires to create its own
police department in an effort to more efficiently use taxpayer funds for law enforcement and public safety
services; and
        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest
of the health, safety and welfare of the City’s citizens to authorize the creation of the Taylorsville City
Police Department to provide law enforcement services within the City of Taylorsville.
         NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED that the Taylorsville City Council hereby authorizes
the creation of the Taylorsville City Police Department to provide law enforcement services within the City
of Taylorsville effective July 1, 2021. The Council further authorizes the Mayor and City Administration
to take the necessary steps to effectuate the creation of the Taylorsville City Police Department.
        This Ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 20-20, shall take effect immediately upon passage and
acceptance as provided herein.
