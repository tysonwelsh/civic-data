       1.1      Consideration             of ZM-24-010,              Request           to Remove            Zoning        Condition           at 877 E 4500                S,
       4430-4438            S 900 E, 851 E 4500                   S, 4402-4410            S 900 E, 814 E Williams                       Way,        and      739 E
       Williams          Way       #X      Planner:         Sean      Murray
       Sean Murray             said the removal              of a zoning         condition          goes through             the rezone          process.
       Millcreek         initiated       the rezone         application         for six separate             properties          to remove         the zone
       condition         and leave         the properties           zoned      as C-2.       The     zone      condition         for these properties
       limits      the uses to a finite            list of permitted,           conditional,             and prohibited             uses. The zone
       conditions           were     imposed        during        a rezone      process          in 1986.      The     conditions         limit     uses to
       smaller,       less intensive            cornrnercial        businesses          such      as newsstands,             wallpaper           stores,     five     and
       dime       stores,     to list    a few.     Many       cominercial         uses,         such as restaurants,               are absent       from      the list.
       Murray        showed          the commission               images      of the current          uses and highlighted                  the parcels             on a
       zoning       map.      The vacant          parcels      on Williams             Way       are owned           by Salt Lake          County.          Currently,
       the site is developed                 as a cominercial           shopping          center      with      many       restaurants           and other
       service       industries.         Many      of these businesses             were          approved        by Salt Lake           County        and
Millcreek       Planning           Commission            Meeting       Minutes                         18 September             2024         Page 2 of 6



    transferred          to Millcreek        during      incorporation.         If the use has not changed,                  business        licenses
    can be renewed                so long as they do not lapse.


    Murray       reported         that staff recomi'nended              removing         the zone condition             found     on the six
    properties.          The C-2 zone designation                  has better     standards          than the zone condition.                Removing
    the zone condition               would     make the corner            more uniforin             and fit with      the other zoning
    designations           surrounding         the properties.         A martial       arts studio         [Flash    Academy]          sought      a
    business       license        for space in the area, but the use is restricted,                        so that precipitated           the city
    seeking      properties          with    this condition         and removing           it.

    Jake Green described                 the proposed            changes        to retaining         walls.     The proposal             changed          the
    criteria      used to deten'nine           if a building           permit      is required        for a retaining            wall    from      measuring
    from       the bottom       of the foundation,               footing,       or wall     system      to measuring              the unbalanced                fill
    height.       This change updates                the code to be consistent                 with     recent        state code amendments                       to
    the International            Residential          Code (IRC).           The proposal            updated          submittal      and approval
    requirements             to better protect         the city from           retaining      wall     failures        due to poor design,
    changed        the maximum             height      of a single          retaining      wall     from      8 feet to 12 feet, allowed                    more
    than two walls            in a terraced          wall     system,       eliminated       a single         wall    height      requirement           of 6
    feet, and allowed            the terraced          wall     system        to be a max height               of 16 feet, and added new
    graphics        (needs further         updates)          to show the options              and restrictions.             He showed            images          and
    the new graphics             to illustrate        these changes.
