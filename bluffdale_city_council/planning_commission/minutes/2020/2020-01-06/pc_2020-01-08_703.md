6.     CONSIDERATION AND VOTE on a Site Plan Application (2019-49) for Bringhurst
       Station Townhomes Including 164 Townhome Units with Associated Streets and Open
       Space in the SD-X Bringhurst Station Proiect Plan, Located at Approximately 16201
       South Camp Williams Road. Edge Homes, Applicant. Jennifer Robison, Staff
       Presenter.

Mrs. Robison presented the staff report and reviewed the history of the SD-X Bringhurst Station
Zone and Project. A diagram of the project area was displayed. The main road into the project,
Bringhurst Boulevard, has been completed, along with trees. She also referred to the Transpo1iation
Plan that is included in the Project Plan. The internal streets will be 35-foot private streets. The
public streets will be 55 feet wide. Mrs. Robison noted that a Preliminary Plat was previously
approved for the entire project. The applicants have subsequently received Final Plat approval for
the Edge Homes development, which is being considered tonight. There are seven total plats that
will be built in phases. Some will consist of townhome units and others of singl e-family dwellings.
The applicant is seeking Planning Conu11ission approval of the site plan for the townhomes in
Phase 1. The total project will include 230 townhome units. The current application is for 164
townhome units in Plats 1, 2, 3, 5, and 6.

Mrs. Robison next reviewed the elevations of the different types of townhome units being proposed.
There will be some variation in the color palettes. The townhome units will consist of four-plex and
five-plex buildings. Mrs. Robi son explained that the townhomes will be front-loaded with two-car
garages and 20-foot driveways. The applicant's parking plan exceeds the City' s requirements. The
landscaping for the townhome units will encompass approximately 31 % of the property.

Mrs. Robison reviewed the fencing plan, which is compatible with the Project Plan. There will be
some black wrought iron fencing. The single-family homes against the canal will have a different
type of fencing to ensure the safety of the residents. Between the units, there will be a wing fence
to provide privacy in the back yards.

There will be a trail connection along the back of the townhomes to c01mect to the conunercial area.
The Bringhurst project also has the Jordan River Parkway Trail that runs tlu·ough it.

In response to Chair Cragun's question as to whether either of the entrances onto Redwood Road
will have traffic lights, Mrs. Robison said that a traffic light is plaimed at the north location where
there is currently no traffic light.

In response to Commissioner Brown's question regarding the tabulation for guest parking,
Mrs. Robison stated that the City standard is one space per four dwelling units. The 20-foot
driveways serve to alleviate the parking issues. The City standard calls for 41 parking stalls but the
applicant is proposing 74. Mrs. Robison clarified that the driveway does not count toward the guest
parking requirement. Therefore, the 74 stalls are in addition to any parking that might occur on the
driveways. The stalls have been spread throughout the project.

Jed Stewart gave his address as 13702 South 200 West in Draper and identified himself as a
representative of Edge Homes. Mr. Stewart stated that there are three different elevations to

       Associated Streets and Open Space Consistent with the Day Ranch Development
       Agreement, Located at Approximately 15200 South Harmon Day Drive. Woodside
       Homes, Applicant. Jennifer Robison, Staff Presenter.

Mrs. Robison presented the staff report and referenced the Project Plan that was included with the
Development Agreement. Over time there have been some changes. Notably, the developers
eliminated 82 lots and replaced them with a church meetinghouse. That left 488 units to be built.
Phase 2 will include the completion of the townhome units that are located in the northern area of
the project. No changes had been made to the Project Plan or the plat. Phase 2 includes two plats:
Plat A, which will consist of 125 units and Plat B, which includes 94 units. Mrs. Robison explained
that all of the plats have been reviewed and meet all City requirements and standards. Mrs. Robison
noted that Harmon Day Drive is a public street that accesses all of the prope1iy. All of the internal
streets are private and will be maintained by the HOA. There is a large open area that will function
as a detention pond, but it is also property the City owns for a potential park area. For now,
however, it will serve as a detention basin for the area.

The Development Agreement also stipulates that any of the rights-of-way along the road will also
be maintained by the HOA. There is a trail on the west side of the subject prope1ty, which will be
the responsibility of the City. The HOA will take care of the landscaping. Mrs. Robison noted that
the project is developing quickly. It includes an elementary school, which is completed, and a
middle school , which is under construction and wi ll be ready for fall 2020. There are also many
homes that have already been comp leted.
