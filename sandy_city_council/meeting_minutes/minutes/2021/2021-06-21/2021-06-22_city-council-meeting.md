3.         21-257                 Discussion and Informational Report:
                                  Effects of Eminent Domain Proceedings
                                  Amend Title 21, Chapter 2, General Provisions, of the Sandy Municipal
                                  Code
                   Attachments:   Staff report.pdf
                                  Exhibit A.pdf

                                  Ryan Kump, City Engineer, presented on this item. The Public Works Department is
                                  proposing to amend Title 21, Chapter 2, General Provisions of the Sandy Municipal Code.
                                  The purpose of the Code Amendment is to provide authority, guidelines, criteria, and
                                  procedures for the Planning Commission to review and grant special exceptions in
                                  connection with the city's eminent domain proceedings or negotiations. This would
                                  provide relief from hardships imposed upon the landowners as a result of an eminent
                                  domain process.

                                  Lynn Pace, City Attorney, provided a legal perspective regarding the creation and use of
                                  this tool. He explained why in certain circumstances a process granting special
                                  exception with a justification is warranted. The Planning Commission has the authority to
                                  approve the request. This amendment creates a mechanism to allow the city or resident
                                  to apply for a special exception and gives the city the flexibility and a tool to mitigate any
                                  potential impact resulting from eminent domain. James Sorenson, Community
                                  Development Director, provided additional information. Council questions and comments
                                  followed.

                                  The item presented tonight will be on next week's agenda for Council consideration and
                                  approval.

4.         21-258                 Discussion about an interlocal agreement with the Alta Canyon Special
                                  Service District regarding the operation of Alta Canyon Sports Center.
                                  Lynn Pace, City Attorney, presented on this agenda item. After a review of the agreement
                                  currently in place between Sandy City and the Alta Canyon Special Services District, the
                                  City Attorney is recommending a new contract between the two entities is necessary.
                                  The original agreement between the two entities was executed in 2012 as a one-year
                                  contract which expired in 2013. The ACSSD is a separate legal entity that owns the
                                  property the Alta Canyon Recreation Center is located on. The new contract will formalize
                                  the on-going agreement between ACSSD and Sandy City and will reflect the status quo
                                  regarding shared revenues and expenses, services, staffing, and maintenance and will
                                  detail the current relationship between the two entities. In the original agreement, ACSSD
                                  was responsible for expenses related to legal services, insurance and capital
                                  improvements.The Legal Department is currently drafting a contract which will
                                  memorialize the contractual arrangement between the two entities and will also clearly
                                  provide that Sandy City has no obligation to do major capital improvements at Alta
                                  Canyon Recreation Center, as reflected in the original agreement. Mr. Pace suggested
                                  that the Council revisit the current structure of the contractual relationship between the
                                  two entities prior to any decision to fund a major investment in the facility. Council
                                  expressed their support and thanked Mr. Pace for bringing this issue to their attention.



5.         21-253                 Discussion and Informational Report: Sandy City Fire Department will
                                  present the Amended and Restated Interlocal Agreement Regarding CAD
                                  and RMS Services.
                   Attachments:   Amended and Restated Interlocal Agreement - Sandy City 6-2-21
                                  Final - Clean Version.pdf
                                  21-30C Resolution Amending Interlocal Agr for CAD and RMS
                                  Services.docx
                                  Salt Lake City Dispatch Contract.pdf

                                  Chief Cline, Fire Chief, presented on this agenda item. He provided a brief history of the
                                  agreement between Sandy and Salt Lake City dispatch services. In 2012, the city
                                  decided to move to SLC dispatch services which began in 2013. He discussed the
                                  formula used to calculate the charges to Sandy by SLC for dispatch, CAD, RMS and
                                  mobile dispatch terminal services in the past. To help both cities estimate their revenues
                                  and expenses in each year's budget, the decision was made to revise the calculation
                                  used to charge cities for services rendered which will allow for more consistent annual
                                  revenue and expense stream for both cities and is based on a six year average of
                                  expenses paid to SLC. Based on the new calculation, Sandy will be charged
                                  approximately $600,000 per year by SLC. The new contract outlines a 7.12% annual
                                  increase going forward, only on the new charges. The revised calculation will allow for
                                  better budgeting for our fire and police departments in the future and both the fire and
                                  police chief support this new agreement. Council comments and questions followed. The
                                  resolution to adopt the new contract will be submitted to the Council next week for
                                  Council consideration and approval.

6.         21-255                 Sandy City Fire would like to add the two open areas east of 1300 E to our
                                  fireworks restricted area to designate east of 1300 E as our firework
                                  restriction area.
                                  (The Council Office notes that a final vote to adopt Ordinance 21-20
                                  requires a suspension of Rule of Procedure 1B pertaining to final voting in
                                  Work Session. With a noticed agenda item, a majority vote is required to
                                  suspend the Rules).
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Fireworks Memo Restricted Areas 6-17-21.pdf
                                  Prohibited adopted firework map 5_19_21
                                  FireHazardAreas_11x17_City_Final (002).pdf
                                  Proposed 1300 E Restricted Areas for
                                  Fireworks_1300E_FireHazardAreas.pdf
                                  Proposed Restricted Map with 1300 Border
                                  6-17-21_1300E_FireHazardAreas Final.pdf
                                  Ordinance 21-20 Discharge of Fireworks.pdf
                                  Signed_Ordinance_21-20.pdf

                                  Council Chair Sharkey asked the Council Members to hear the agenda item, and then
                                  consider making motions to allow for a suspension of the rules and a vote on this item.

                                  Chief Cline and Fire Marshall Robert DeKover presented on this item and requested that
                                  the Council consider designating two additional areas in Sandy City as fireworks
                                  restricted areas. He thanked the Council for their ongoing discussions over the past week
                                  regarding this issue. Chief Cline reviewed with the Council the process to restrict
                                  additional areas from fireworks based on "existing conditions" and he shared with the
                                  Council information regarding restricted areas designated by cities across Salt Lake
                                  County. Some cities have banned fireworks completely. Mr. DeKorver provided
                                  information about the process to restrict areas from fireworks and the criteria used by the
                                  fire department to designate these areas. He showed the Council the current fireworks
                                  restricted areas map approved by the Council in May and recommended adding two
                                  areas to the designation which would provide clarity to the residents and allow for a
                                  clearly delineated boundary along 1300 East which would also align with our neighboring
                                  cities to the north and south. The state statute allows for changing/adding restrictions by
                                  cities based on existing conditions. The Fire Marshall also reviewed the fines associated
                                  with shooting off fireworks in banned areas and importance of clearly defining a boundary.
                                  He reviewed which fireworks are legal and the process to cite residents.Council
                                  comments and questions followed.

                                  Chief Cline provided dispatch numbers for Police (801-799-3000) and Fire (801-799-4231)
                                  and encouraged the community to go to the city website for additional information.
                                  Residents should contact the fire department if they have any questions regarding
                                  fireworks restrictions. Fire could come back to Council prior to the July 24th holiday with
                                  additional recommendations and encouraged residents to observe fireworks safety
                                  guidelines and to attend a professional fireworks show this year. #Skipayear.
