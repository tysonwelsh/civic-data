            Ordinance No. 2025-14

            AN ORDINANCE ENACTING TITLE 2, CHAPTER 4, SECTION 13 OF THE
            MUNICIPAL CODE OF PARK CITY

            WHEREAS, The Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act for Utah Cities, Utah Code section 10-
            6-157, authorizes the Park City Council to create a director of finance position to
            perform certain duties established in the Act;

            WHEREAS, the Act requires a director of finance to be qualified and appointed and
            removed by the mayor, with the advice and consent of the City Council; and,

            WHEREAS, the City Council finds that creating a director of finance title will resolve
            internal equity discrepancies among similar internal services departments, and

            BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF PARK CITY, UTAH, THAT:

            Title 2, Chapter 4, Section 13 of the Municipal Code of Park City is adopted to read as
            shown in Exhibit A. This Ordinance shall become effective upon publication.

            PASSED AND ADOPTED this 12th day of June, 2025.

                 2-4-13 Director of Finance

                 There shall be a Director of Finance who shall be appointed and removed by the
                 Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council. The Director of Finance shall
                 report to and be supervised by the City Manager. The Director of Finance shall perform
                 the financial duties and responsibilities of the City Recorder as established by Utah
                 Code Section 10-6-157, as amended. The Director of Finance may not also serve as
                 the City Treasurer.
