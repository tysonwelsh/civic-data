                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 25-09

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 2, CHAPTER 105 TO
          ADJUST COMPENSATION OF ELECTED OFFICIALS


        WHEREAS, Title 2, Chapter 105 of the Riverton City Code governs the salaries of
elected officials; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Code §2.105.010 presently fixes the salaries of its elected
officials as based on a percentage of grade 20 employees; and

         WHEREAS, Utah Code §10-3-818 presently requires salaries for elected officials
undergo a separate public hearing process prior to approval, and which is contrary to the process
of setting salaries at a fixed percentage; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, finds and determines that Riverton City Code
§2.105.010 should be amended to more accurately follow the process required by Utah Code
§10-3-818, and eliminate the fixed percentages presently set in City Code;

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

      Section 1.      Title 2, Chapter 105, of the Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is,
                      amended to reflect the following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

      Section 2.      This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

2.105.010 Compensation and salaries.

The salary of the elected officials, officers and employees of this city shall be paid according to
salary grades and ranges in the amount as is below specified.

(1) Compensation Schedule. The compensation of elected officials, officers, justice court judges,
and all other employees of the city shall be according to the compensation schedule adopted by
the city council and in compliance with Utah Code §10-3-818 or successor statute. Amendments
or adjustments to the compensation schedule shall be conducted as part of the ordinary budgeting
process, or in the event an employment position is in need of a market adjustment as determined
by the Human Resources Department, and provided that budgeted funds are available to conduct
a market adjustment. The amounts adopted under the compensation schedule do not include
other compensation, such as retirement, medical benefits, or other compensatory items as
approved by the city. These other amounts shall be included in the calculation of total
compensation as part of the market study. Increases in compensation shall be given according to
the annual market study conducted pursuant to the Personnel Policy and Procedures Manual.
Also, annual incremental increases may be given based on merit, as provided in the Personnel
Policy and Procedures Manual.

(2) If the mayor or any council member misses more than four regularly set administrative
meetings or council meetings, per calendar year, a deduction of $100.00, per meeting missed,
shall be taken from the monthly salary of the mayor or any council member missing the meeting.

(3) For purposes of subsection (2) of this section, a “missed meeting” means nonattendance at
more than 20 percent of the total time in the administrative session, or nonattendance at more
than 20 percent of the total time spent in the council meeting.

(4) Notwithstanding the other provisions of this section, no council member’s or mayor’s salary
shall be reduced below $100.00 per month.

(5) In addition to the salary paid the officers and employees of this city, they shall receive the
following benefits:

       (a) The employee’s share of the Social Security tax.
       (b) Health and accident insurance for themselves and their families on such basis and cost
       to the employee or officer as the city council may from time to time establish by
       resolution.
       (c) Vacation and sick leave on such basis as the city council may from time to time
       establish by resolution.
       (d) Participation in the Utah State Retirement Program on such basis and cost as the city
       council may from time to time by resolution establish.

(6) Whenever any person serves in two or more positions either as officers or employees of this
city, unless otherwise specifically provided in the employment agreement, by ordinance or
resolution, the person shall receive the salary or compensation of the office or employment
paying the greater amount.
