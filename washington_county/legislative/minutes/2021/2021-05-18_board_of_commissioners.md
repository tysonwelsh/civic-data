CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2021-1177-0, AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING
AND IMPLEMENTING TITLE 5 CHAPTER 4 OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE
REGARDING KOLOB RESERVOIR FACILITY REGULATIONS

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that Washington County and the Water
Conservancy District are working together to pass an interlocal agreement and lease agreement
regarding Kolob Reservoir and management of Kolob Reservoir Facility. This Ordinance and
agreement will assist with the increased utilization and recreation at the Reservoir for this
upcoming season temporarily until a more permanent solution can be established.

Some of the new regulations include a reservation being required before camping can take place
at the Reservoir and two-stroke motors no longer being permitted for use on the water.
Commissioner Iverson clarified that the Sheriff's Office will be overseeing some of the
enforcement of the new regulations.

Commissioner Iverson made a motion to combine items 3 and 4.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Combine Items 3 and 4. Motion seconded
by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF _ RESOLUTION _NO.___R-2021-2772,_ A | RESOLUTION
ACKNOWLEDGING A SALES ORDER FOR THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE TO PURCHASE

TASERS AND EQUIPMENT FROM AXON ENTERPRISE, INC.

Undersheriff James Standley stated that Taser requirements have changed and more training is
now required for certified Sheriff's Office employees. The current Tasers in use will be phased
out within the following years and these older models will soon be unusable. It is recommended
to purchase a bundle packaging which included Tasers, cartridges, essential accessories and
various license plans to train deputies over the next five years. The total cost is $126,960 and will
be divided in payments over the next five (5) years.

THE EXECUTION OF INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENTS FOR

Election Supervisor Melanie Abplanalp explained that 2021 is a municipal election year and the
County will act as the election vendor for many of the municipalities in the County. The County
will program, print, mail, and count ballots as part of the interlocal agreements. The entities that
will be utilizing the County’s assistance are: Dixie Deer SSD, Gunlock Water Board SSD,
Northwestern SSD, Apple Valley, Hildale, Hurricane, Ivins, La Verkin, New Harmony, Rockville,

CONSIDERATION OF _ RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2021-2774, A | RESOLUTION

PROCLAIMING SEPTEMBER 13-20, 2021 AS IRONMAN VOLUNTEER WEEK IN
WASHINGTON COUNTY

Washington County Tourism Director Kevin Lewis provided a report of the IRONMAN 70.3
North American Championship that took place on May 1, 2021. Mr. Lewis thanked the volunteers,
medical teams, and community supporters who help to ensure the success of the event each year.

Washington County will host the upcoming IRONMAN 70.3 World Championship event taking
place in September. The Washington County School District has declared September 17" as a no
school day to help facilitate and support the World Championship Activities. Washington County
will proclaim September 13-19, 2021 as IRONMAN Volunteer Week, wherein, residents,
businesses and organizations are encouraged to enthusiastically support the event.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2774, A
Resolution Proclaiming September 13-20, 2021 as IRONMAN Volunteer
Week in Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting

aye.

CONSIDERATION OF PUBLIC WORKS REPORT

Washington County Engineer Todd Edwards provided a report of the second quarter road projects
for 2021. Upcoming projects include: chipping and sealing Highway 91 from the Gunlock Road
intersection West to the Utah/Arizona state line; chipping and sealing Sky Ranch subdivision, Cliff
Dwellers subdivision and portions of 1100 West in Washington County; beginning work on Kolob
Terrace Road safety enhancement for jersey barriers along the narrow shoulder portions of the
road through the area west of Sunset Canyon Ranch.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Accept the Public Works Report as

Presented. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
