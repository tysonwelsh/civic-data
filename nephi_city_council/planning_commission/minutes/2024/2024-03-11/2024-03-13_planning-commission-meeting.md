   1. Public Hearing: Public Hearing on an impact home occupation for a nail salon
      located at 562 E 1020 N for Brandee Wing DBA “Nail Saloon”.
         a. Open Public Hearing
                   TIME: 7:00 PM

          b. Staff Presentation
                    Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that Brandee Wing would like to open
                    a Nail Salon at her personal residence. Mrs. Wing will have an extra
                    parking spot at her home and has met the requirements for Nephi City
                    to move forward. Nephi City recommends that as the Planning
                    Commission move forward with approval.

          c. Take Public Comment/ Close Public Hearing

                      No public comment was given by any member or participant of the
                      Planning Commission. Move by Chairwoman Ann Petersen to close
                      the Public Comment and Hearing.

                      END TIME: 7:07 PM

          d. Commission Discussion

                      Mrs. Shauna Talbot: Asked the Commission to consider that the
                      Nephi City staff have the right to just approve these if they meet all

         the conditions of the Planning Commission. It will speed the process
         up for the community.

         Chairwoman Ann Peterson: Felt that it would be faster and quicker.

         Commissioner Cory Thomson: would like them to still come through
         the planning commission if they are in the home.

         Chairwoman Ann Peterson: Requested that the city please categorize
         the types of businesses so that one those that need to come to the
         planning commission are ones that need to be approved. She would
         like to see a better policy on the companies that are coming in, to limit
         the amount that come through on the Planning Commission.

         Mrs. Shauna Talbot: covered that some of the occupants coming
         through are wanting to do light industry, or next door in an accessory
         building. With parameters of what they are doing, bringing in, smells
         and so forth.

         Commissioner Heather Robertson: asked if there was one that has
         been contested. None that any member of the commission or the staff
         could remember in the past.

         Brandee Wing (Applicant): would like to eventually get a “she-shed”
         for her business, as a separate use to keep her children away from the
         business.

         Commissioner Cory Thomson: asked about the business license and
         that it was expired. He would like to see a current one be presented
         and given to Lisa Brough of the Nephi City.
