                                                    MINUTES
                           OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER COUNTY
                                       Tuesday, August 22, 2017 - 10:00 a.m.
                              Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons
who appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or
purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: James Ebert and Kerry W. Gibson. Jim Harvey was excused.
OTHER STAFF PRESENT: Ricky D. Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor, David C. Wilson, Deputy County Attorney;
and Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME – Chair Ebert
B. MOMENT OF SILENCE
C. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Joe Olsen
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY – Commissioner Gibson
E. PRESENTATIONS:
   1. PRESENTATION OF THE SEAL OF SERVICE AWARD TO ELEANOR DOUGHERTY (E.D.) GALLEGOS,
      VOLUNTEER AT THE SHERIFF'S OFFICE.

          Lt. Kevin Burns, of the County Sheriff’s Office, stated that Ms. Dougherty has been volunteering at the
          Sheriff’s Office for 20 years in one of the more difficult areas, and they wanted to recognize and thank
          her for her great service and dedication. Sandy Grogan, Ms. Dougherty’s supervisor, had submitted this
          recommendation and described Ms. Dougherty’s proficiencies with her difficult tasks. Ms. Dougherty
          expressed thanks. She loves to volunteer and enjoys the work and staff. The commissioners expressed
          their sincere thanks for her commitment and tremendous amount of work, which has greatly benefited the
          community. They presented her with a plaque.
     2. PRESENTATION ON COMMUNITY AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENTS.
          Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Department Director, outlined the many divisions
          within his department including, County Planning, Engineering, Building Inspection, Roads, Garage and
          Fleet, Transfer Station, Property Management, and Weber Economic Development. Mr. Wilkinson
          outlined the diverse services they provide, the significant work for which they have received numerous
          awards, the internal processes and improvements they continue to make and the resulting savings to the
          county. He emphasized that their ongoing goal is to provide the best services to the public. He expressed
          thanks to the employees who work so hard. The commissioners expressed their thanks to Mr. Wilkinson
          for his great leadership and asked him to pass along their appreciation to his staff. Kerry Serrano, from
          County Planning, was selected to use the Stall of Fame for one month for her good work.

    2. PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING PROPOSAL FOR CREATION OF AN AGRICULTURE PROTECTION AREA
       (APA) BY THE NORMA ESTHER BAILEY FAMILY TRUST, PARCEL 17-051-0046.
         Fátima Fernelius, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s Office, stated that the statutory requirements for
         noticing, posting, mailing and publishing had been fulfilled. APAs fall under State Code and
         allow/protect successful applicants to continue their agricultural activities, particularly when new
         subdivision developments rise up around these agricultural parcels. No objections or requests to modify
         the proposal were received. The Western Weber Planning Commission and the APA Advisory Board had
         no issues with the proposal and recommend approval. Last week the Commission heard from the
         applicant and other public comments in support of this proposal.

    3. Public comments: Chair Ebert invited public comments and none were received.
