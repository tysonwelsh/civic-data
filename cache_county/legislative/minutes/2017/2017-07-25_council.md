CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL MEETING
July 25, 2017

The Cache County Council convened in a regular session on July 25, 2017 at 5:00 p.m.
in the Cache County Council Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

ATTENDANCE:

Chairman: G. Gregory Merrill

Vice Chairman: David L. Erickson

Council Members: Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Jon White, Gina H. Worthen &
Gordon A. Zilles

County Executive: Craig “W” Buttars absent.

County Clerk: Jill N. Zollinger

Deputy County Attorney: Lee Edwards

The following individuals were also in attendance: Janeen Allen, Todd Ballard, Carson Falslev, Cindy
Falslev, Kevin Falslev, Spencer Gibbons, Alton Hebdon, Sharon L. Hoth, Clark Israelsen, Marsha
Israelsen, Andy Maughan, Kaylee Maughan, Director Bartt Nelson, Director Josh Runhaar, Sid Zollinger,
Tony Zollinger, Media: Jennie Christensen (KVNU).

OPENING REMARKS AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Council member Gina Worthen gave the opening remarks and led those present in the Pledge
of Allegiance.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF AGENDA

ACTION: Motion by Council member Ward to approve the amended agenda as written.
Erickson seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 7-0.

REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF MINUTES

ACTION: Motion by Council member Worthen to approve the minutes of the
July 11, 2017 Council Meeting as written. White seconded the motion. The vote was
unanimous, 7-0.

MINUTES FOLLOW-UP: Chairman Merrill asked if there has been any exploration of Chief
Hammer's Mobile Extractor Unit as a potential revenue source. Attorney Lee Edwards has met
with Chief Hammer and Hammer is checking with several vendors.

COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS

Greg Merrill indicated that the Public Relations Committee reported the county is taking on two
community service projects for employees to participate in: The 2017 Walk to End Alzheimer’s
on September 9 at 10:30 a.m. at Merlin Olsen Park and Meals on Wheels delivery for which
Council members will receive an agenda with dates to choose from. Merrill urged Council
members to participate in both service projects.

Cache County Council
07-25-2017

Jon White thanked the Council for help with the Employee Summer Party and remarked
agriculture is the only business that produces new money each year.

Dave Erickson recognized Jon White as the author of an article in the Cache Conservation
News.

Gina Worthen said an old picture of the Home Arts building has been found and the program
celebrating the buildings prior to their removal will be at noon on Saturday, August 12, 2017, on
the stage area of the Fair. The show will include the Benson Sisters, dancers, a barbershop
quartet, the Cache Community Band, Historians Ken Godfrey and Ross Peterson, tours of the
buildings and a display of photos.

Janeen Allen stated the microphones will be worked on next week and urged Council members
to remember to speak into the microphones when making comments.
