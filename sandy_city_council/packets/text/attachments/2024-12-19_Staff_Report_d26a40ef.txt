Request
 The applicant, Karla Mata representing the owner, X
 School Yard, LLC, is requesting preliminary subdivision
 approval for the School Yard Subdivision Amended plat.
 The proposal consists of subdividing an L-shaped property
 into two separate lots. See existing and proposed
 subdivision plats (Exhibit A).

Background
 This site was previously owned by the Canyons School
 District and then redeveloped into a commercial retail
 center. The property is located at 11020 S. State St. and is
 zoned Central Business District (CBD). This was the site of
 the abandoned Valley High School, which was the former
 Crescent Elementary School. In 2018, Wadsworth
 Development purchased the property and created the
 School Yard Subdivision, a three-lot commercial
 development.

 The property is surrounded by commercially zoned
 properties on all sides. State Street runs along the east while
 the Jordan and Salt Lake Canal runs along the west.

Public Notice and Outreach
  Notices were mailed to property owners within 500 feet of the subject
  property and affected entities. The notice was posted to the Utah Public
  Notice website and the city website. A physical copy of the notice was
  posted at City Hall, and a public notice sign was placed on the subject
  property.

Analysis
  The applicant is proposing the creation of an additional lot through
  subdividing the existing Lot 1 of the School Yard Subdivision plat. This
  will allow for separate ownership of the developed properties.

  The three lot School Yard Subdivision was recorded in 2019. Lots 1 and
  2 are fully developed while lot 3 has nearly completed construction.
  Subdividing the existing Lot 1 into two separate lots does not create any
  zoning impacts. Both lots have sufficient parking stalls for the existing
  uses.

  Staff Concerns
  Per 21-23-21(6)(c)(2), cross easements shall be required between commercial properties to permit perpetual use of common
  drives, parking areas, and service areas at no cost to the adjacent property owner. The applicant will need to provide evidence
  that such access easements have been recorded and/or amended.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine
  preliminary subdivision review is complete for the School Yard
  Subdivision Amended Plat located at 11020 S. State St. based on the
  following findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. That the proposed lot configuration is an efficient use of the
       land.

    2. That the proposed lot configurations conform to the
       requirements of the CBD Zone.

    3. That the various City Departments and Divisions have
       preliminarily approved the proposed subdivision plat.

Conditions:
   1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments
       and redlines throughout the final review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be recorded.

    2. That all City provisions, codes, and ordinances are adhered to during the review, construction, and operations process
