Council Work Session

Copperton Law Enforcement Services

              Mayor Clayton asked if anyone had anything to add to the letter.

              Nobody had anything to add.

               Mayor Clayton asked if the Council had any individuals it could recommend to sit
on the law enforcement steering committee.

              Council Member Severson suggested Randy Cox.

              Council Member Stitzer suggested Apollo Pazell and Amanda Doyle.

               Mayor Clayton suggested Joel Brienholt, Ben Villanueva, and Brian Vinton. He
plans on talking to the Lion’s Club on Thursday to see if anyone is interested. He asked the
Council members to send him the email addresses of the nominees.

              Mayor Clayton asked Council Member Olsen what he needs in preparation for the
next day’s meeting.

                Council Member Olsen stated the agenda includes a motion for “approval of
funding for new Chief of UPD reorganization.” He guessed it was probably to approve a salary
for Chief Jason Mazuran. Sheriff Rivera plans to take over his responsibilities and he will shift
exclusively to reorganization duties. This could be a good thing as Mazuran knows Unified Police
Department (UPD) well and can do a good job, but conversely, he will be more likely to create a
similar version of the UPD that already exists. He was not sure Chief Mazuran would keep
Copperton’s best interests at heart.

              Council Member Stitzer asked if the Board had considered any other options for
CEO.

              Council Member Olsen stated the only other candidates seriously discussed were
Lieutenant Justin Hoyal, whom he would prefer, and Chief Wayne Dial. He predicted most of the
Board would most likely go for Chief Mazuran, as he was Sheriff Rivera’s top choice.

              Mayor Clayton asked why Sheriff Rivera was still taking part in the discussion.

              Council Member Olsen stated the Board asked her to remain as an advisor
throughout the transition.

              Council Member Stitzer asked if there were any other items on the agenda.

              Council Member Olsen stated the only other items on the agenda were the
approvals of Chief Mazuran and Sheriff Rivera to their new positions, a discussion of an overfill
position, a UPD reorganization discussion, and an update regarding project tracking software.

               Later in the meeting, after reopening from closed session:

               Council Member Stitzer stated the Council had unanimously agreed to direct
Council Member Olsen to vote in support of the appointments of Chief Mazuran and Sheriff Rivera
to their new positions.
