1. Welcome, Roll Call, and Opening Comments
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, except for
Councilor Condie who is excused. Councilor Newall gave the opening comment.

2. Regular Agenda

     2.a Consideration of Resolution #2024-44 approving the request proposing the creation of
          a new school district and authorizing the City Recorder to notify the Utah County
          Commission and the Utah County Clerk.

The resolution would authorize putting the school district split on the November ballot for public
vote. Other nearby cities to the west have also formed an interlocal agreement to consider
splitting from the Alpine school district.

The Council expressed support for the resolution, noting the public input process and the
importance of letting the public decide.

    Motion: Councilor Newall moved to approve Resolution #2024-44 approving the request
    proposing the creation of a new school district and authorizing the City Recorder to notify
    the Utah County Commission and the Utah County Clerk. Councilor Hancock seconded the
    motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Absent; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Newall, Yes; and Councilor Stallings, Yes. The motion passed with 4 in favor and
    1 absent.
