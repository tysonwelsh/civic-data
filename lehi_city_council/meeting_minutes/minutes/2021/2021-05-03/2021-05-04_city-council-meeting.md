Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, May 4, 2021, at Lehi City
Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately 4:00 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present:     Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney; Kim Struthers, Community Development Director;
Lorin Powell, City Engineer; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Steve Marchbanks, Parks
Superintendent; Beau Thomas, Assistant to the City Administrator; Shaye Ruitenbeek,
Management Analyst; and Teisha Wilson, City Recorder;

1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Koivisto attended virtually. Paige Albrecht offered the opening comment.

2. Discussion Items
   2.a. Legislative Update- Chris Bleak
   Chris Bleak gave an update on the legislative session. He said that the State is really focused on
   providing affordable housing. He also noted that the State is willing to invest in infrastructure
   including transit.

   Motion: Councilor Hancock moved to adjourn into a closed Session to discuss the purchase,
   exchange, or lease of real property. Councilor Southwick seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
    Councilor Hancock, Yes; and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

The meeting adjourned for a closed session at approximately 4:20 p.m.
The meeting reconvened at approximately 5:00 p.m.

    2.b. Update and Discussion of Family Park, Mellor-Rhodes Park, and Willow Park.
    Steve Marchbanks, Parks Manager, presented the current plans for Mellor-Rhodes Park. He
    said that a lot of fill work needs to be done. At a minimum, it will cost 4.15 million. If they
    want to raise the fields to road level then it will cost 7.9 million. The Council agreed that they
    would prefer to have the fields lower to save money so long as there is not a drainage issue.
    Mr. Marchbanks stated that they could mitigate any drainage concerns.

    Mr. Marchbanks then showed an updated concept plan for Family Park. The park was very
    passive on the north and active on the south. A small amphitheater was also shown.

    Jason Bond with the Park Department, showed the Council potential concept plans for Willow
    Park. Mr. Bond stated that to have full cost recovery for trailer camp spaces they would need
    to have a large KOA type design. The Council decided that they prefer the more open concept
    with less spaces even though they may not get 100% cost recovery.

    2c. Discussion of the FY2022 Lehi City Budget - All City Departments
    Dean Lundell, Finance Director, reported on the budget process. The Council went through
    each department’s budget and asked questions.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
   at approximately 6:45 p.m.
