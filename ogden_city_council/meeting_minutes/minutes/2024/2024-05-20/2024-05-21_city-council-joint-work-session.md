         The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00
p.m.; discuss the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2024-2025 budgets for the Community and Economic Development Department, Fire
Department, Attorney’s Office, and Redevelopment Agency (RDA); discuss Council/Agency business; and hear Council/Agency
comments.

Agenda Review
        Acting Chair White and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council
meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Proposed Ogden City Fiscal (FY) Year 2024-2025 Budget
          Attorney’s Office: City Attorney Williams stated the City Attorney’s Department is funded primarily through the
General Fund. The department also has two positions that are partially funded by grants through the Victims of crime Act
(VOCA) and the Violence Against Women Act (VAWA). Significant budget line-item changes for the Attorney’s budget include
wage and benefit increases totaling $172,000 and contract maintenance charges totaling $15,275. For a copy of the presentation in
its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Discussion among the Council and Mr. Williams centered on the types of cases the City Attorney’s Office prosecutes;
events for which the City has hired outside legal counsel; case types that are handled by the Weber County Prosecutor’s Office;
and the area of the budget in which insurance and risk management costs are allocated.
          Fire Department: Fire Chief Mathieu used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the proposed FY2025
budget for his Department, which also includes the medical services enterprise fund. The Fire Department general budget will
increase by $807,600, or 7.5 percent when compared to FY2024 and the medical services enterprise budget will increase by
$303,750, or 2.4 percent, when compared to FY2024. Significant budget line-item changes for the Fire Department budget are as
follows:
                • General Fund
                         o Wage and benefit increase
                         o Uniform clothing increase
                         o Contract maintenance increase
                         o Small tools decrease
                         o Fleet replacement adjustment
             •     Medical Services Enterprise
                         o Wage and benefit increase
                                  ▪ Including the addition of four full-time positions
                         o Uniform clothing increase
                         o Fleet replacement adjustment
                         o Operating expense depreciation
                         o Fund balance/carryovers
          The FY2025 budget does not include new proposed positions. Position count remains at 82, matching the previous year.
In the medical services fund, the proposal is to add four AEMT/EMT Transport/Ambulance Technicians and to reclassify one
firefighter position to fire captain.
          For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          There was discussion among the Council and Chief Mathieu regarding general operations of his Department and reuse of
old Fire Department facilities that will be replaced once new projects are completed.
          Fire Department Capital Improvement Projects: The proposed FY2025 budget includes $2.5 million in funding for
Fire Department Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) projects. The funding source is the City’s Medical Fund and the funding
will be used to augment existing funds for the Fire/Public Safety Facility new training tower. Chief Mathieu briefly discussed
the scope of that project, which includes training equipment for the Police Department.
          Community and Economic Development Department: Community and Economic Development Director Johnson
used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the proposed FY2025 budget for his Department, which also
manages the following functions:
              • Administration
              • Economic Development
              • Union Station
              • Planning
              • Community Development
              • Building Services
              • Arts, Culture and Events
          Significant changes in the proposed FY25 CED expenditures related to:
              • No new full-time employee positions across the department are proposed.
              • Proposed funding for increased personal costs--COLA and merit increases and benefits. (The specific increases
                   are still being calculated based on an updated compensation study.)
              • One (1) reclassification for the Airport Maintenance Supervisor (G17) to Airport Deputy Manager position
                   (ADIVG24). This will provide additional coverage for after-hours or emergency call outs.
              • Funding for five airport CIP projects.
          Ogden City receives Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds from the U.S. Department of Housing and
Urban Development. Expenditures for CDBG Funds are itemized in the Annual Action Plan (AAP). Additional Grant funds have
also been received. The Department has requested use of BDO Lease Revenue funds and the portion of the proposed
appropriations of BDO Lease Revenue is equal to 41.3% of the total available funds.
          For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Discussion among the Council and Mr. Johnson centered on general operations of the Department; Airport operations,
including the recent launch of Breeze Airways at the Airport; and the City’s Code Services noticing process.

        Vice Chair White recessed the meeting at 5:50 p.m. to allow the Council to convene in their meeting. The work session
reconvened at 7:00 p.m.

         Discussion of the Community and Economic Development Department budget resumed with a focus on the Planning
division as Council members asked questions about the specifics of the General Plan update project.

       Community and Economic Development Department Capital Improvement Projects: Director Johnson explained the

            • CD007 Dumke Arts Plaza – Reserve Fund.
            • CD008 25th Street Streetscape Improvements.
            • CD086 Infill Development Infrastructure.
            • US034 Union Station Improvements.
            • RM024 Merci Box Car Restoration.

Proposed Redevelopment Agency Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Budget
        Community and Economic Development Director Johnson stated City Administration is requesting the Board adopt the
proposed FY2024-2025 Redevelopment Agency (RDA) budget totaling $20,280,075. The is an increase of 28.61 percent over the
FY2023-2024 budget. The RDA is funded primarily through tax increment received from the various redevelopment districts.
Contributions from BDO lease revenue are also included in the Agency’s revenue. A contribution from the Agency to the General
Fund covers services provided to the RDA by City administrative staff. Mr. Johnson used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to
provide detailed information about aging Redevelopment districts. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the
information packet for the meeting.
         There was discussion among the Council and Mr. Johnson regarding the process of creating a Redevelopment district and
the manner in which the base valuation of the property within a district is determined and also the timing of the redevelopment of
the Union Station campus.


         The meeting adjourned at 7:57 p.m.
