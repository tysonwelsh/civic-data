17. ADOPT AN ORDINANCE MODIFYING UTAH COUNTY CODE SECTION 26-3-9 REGARDING
      THE 20 YEAR REVIEW OF AGRICULTURAL PROTECTION AREAS AS A RESULT OF THE

and explained the language for a 120-day deadline is not clear in defining whether the timeline begins when the

4.     AUTHORIZE A CHANGE TO THE CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER STAFFING PLAN TO
       UPGRADE ONE (1) CAREER SERVICE HALF-TIME ASSISTANT CLINICAL COORDINATOR –
       CJC POSITION TO A THREE-QUARTER-TIME CAREER SERVICE POSITION [CHANGING
       THE FTE, POSITION CJC 017 FROM .50 TO .75]
(Track A, 21:50)
When Commissioner Graves asked whether the current half-time position to be moved to a three-quarter-time
position is already a benefitted position, both Merritt Fullmer of the Utah County Human Resources Management
Office and Rebecca Martell of the Utah County Children’s Justice Center confirmed from the audience. Ms. Martell
also talked about the need for this orientation person in the multi-disciplinary team, rather than leaving the duties to
individual police jurisdictions to educate new detectives. This increase in position will help to orient the detectives
to Children’s Justice Center statutes and Task Force policies, while also serving as support staff for the center.

8.      AUTHORIZE COMMISSION CHAIR TO EXECUTE LETTER TO UTAH STATE TREASURER
        AND TO THE UTAH STATE TAX COMMISSION CONCERNING NOTICE OF FUTURE
        INSTRUCTIONS REGARDING THE SCHEDULING OF THE TRANSFER OF UTAH COUNTY
        SALES TAX FUNDS DERIVED FROM UTAH CODE TITLE 59 PART 12 SECTION 2213 TO THE
        UTAH TRANSIT AUTHORITY
(Track A, 23:25)
Commissioner Ivie asked for an outline of the current agreements in relationship to the disbursement of Utah County
Sales Tax funds. Utah County Civil Division Chief Deputy Attorney David Shawcroft talked about an agreement
with the Utah Transit Authority (UTA) which allows for the direct transfer of funds from the state to UTA; this
letter does not change that agreement, but defines the scheduling of those transfers. Mr. Shawcroft also commented
on statutory provisions and payment dates with comments from Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson,
and Andrew Jackson of the Mountainland Association of Governments. Commissioner Graves noted UTA gets
their funds almost immediately but the state takes three months to distribute to the county. Mr. Thompson reviewed
the process for payments and Mr. Jackson commented on the UTA interlocal agreement.

           Commissioner Graves made the motion to authorize the letter to the Utah State
           Treasurer and the Utah State Tax Commission concerning the scheduling of the
           transfer of Utah County Sales Tax Funds as identified in Regular Agenda Item
           No. 8. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Ivie and carried with the
           following vote:
                                                                          AYE: William C. Lee

1.     PRESENTATION FROM UTAH COUNTY FIRE TEAM ON THE STATE COOPERATIVE FIRE
       AGREEMENT
(Track A, 30:35)
Jamie Nelson and Nathan Trujillo of the Utah County Sheriff’s Office – Fire Division presented plans for Wildland
Fire Prevention related to new legislation adopted last year. Ms. Nelson provided pamphlets to the commissioners
(copies of which can be found attached to these minutes) detailing Utah Division of Natural Resources policies and
Participation Commitment Actions. She and the commissioners discussed state participation fund requirements, and
Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson mentioned funds that are almost expired. Commissioner Graves
encouraged the department to use those and reviewed the possibilities with Utah County Sheriff James O. Tracy, and
Mike Forshee of the Utah County Sheriff’s Office. Merritt Fullmer of the Utah County Human Resources
Management Office also joined the conversation.
