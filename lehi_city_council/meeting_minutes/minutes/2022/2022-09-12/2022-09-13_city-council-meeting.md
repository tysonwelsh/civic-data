Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, except for
Councilor Albrecht who will join the Regular Meeting electronically at 7:00 p.m., and Councilor
Koivisto who will join at 4:30 p.m. Councilor Condie gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Bond Discussion
Nathan Roberts stated that they are beginning the process to issue bonds for the new bibrary and
city center building. Mr. Roberts stated that the bonds should be issued through a Local Building
Authority (LBA). The creation of the LBA is on the agenda tonight for consideration.

2.2 Station 84 Update
Chief Craft presented on the proposed renderings for the new Fire Station in west Lehi. He said
it’s a modern building that matches the architecture of the area.

2.3 Discussion of Water Efficient Landscape Standards
Kim Struthers stated that they began looking at standards or recommendations that would improve
water conservancy. He then reviewed the proposed changes with the Council.

2.4 Discussion of Stockpiling Materials in City Rights-of-Way
There was discussion regarding City Code that does not allow for landscape materials to be
dumped in City Streets or on the curb. Mayor Johnson stated that there should be some allotted
time to allow for residents to improve their yard, especially when the company making the
delivery of materials refuses to drive on the residential driveway.

Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome, Roll Call, Pledge of Allegiance
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Albrecht joined electronically. Councilor Southwick led the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. Presentations and Report
2.1) Constitution Week Proclamation
Katie Madsen presented on the upcoming Constitution Week. Mayor Johnson read and signed the
Proclamation.

3. 20 Minute Citizen Input
Dennis Schoonover stated that he has a business on State Street that is expanding, and he is being
required to place a pressurized irrigation meter on his building. He doesn’t believe this is fair

because he is not expanding his landscape and the city has already allocated money to retrofit
and install pressurized irrigation meters on homes and commercial buildings.

Anne Marie Mickelson expressed concerns with the street trees being removed in the Olympic
Park neighborhood. She was concerned that all trees were being replaced, even if they aren’t an
issue. She also felt that the $200 reimbursement was not enough. She would like to see more of a
phased approach to remove the trees.

Sterling Bronson informed the Council of the fundraiser event for the Boots and Badges
Foundation.

Ron Smith stated that he appreciated how the tax increase was handled. He also was pleased to
see the Mayor and Council at the Heritage Days event.

Austen Mecham was also concerned with the tree removal and believed it would decrease their
home values.

Sunshine Cardell disagreed with the tree removal. She would like to have more discussion with
the residents and explore other solutions.

Tori Cardell stated that she hopes to see a solution and does not want to see the trees removed.

Jordan Jensen stated that he would like to have more discussion on the issue with the residents.

Rob Craig suggested a neighborhood meeting for more discussion. He appreciates the efforts to
maintain the safety of residents. He’s more concerned with safety then removing the trees.

Shawn Dorsey was also concerned with removing the trees.
