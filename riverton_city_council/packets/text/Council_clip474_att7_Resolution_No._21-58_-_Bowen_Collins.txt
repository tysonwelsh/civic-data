                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 21-58

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
   WITH BOWEN COLLINS ASSOCIATES TO COMPLETE THE BUTTERFIELD
                       CREEK MASTER PLAN

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the City Engineer deems this project is necessary; and


       WHEREAS, by approving the Contract with Bowen Collins, Council will need to
approve $35,000.00 in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The Riverton City Council approves Resolution No. 21-58 authorizing the Mayor to
         execute an agreement with Bowen Collins Associates to complete the Butterfield
         Creek Master Plan.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 5th day of
October 2021 by the following vote:
