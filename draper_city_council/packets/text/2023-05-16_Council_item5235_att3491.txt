                               ORDINANCE NO. 1580

    AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL PROHIBITING THE
    DISCHARGE OF FIREWORKS IN SPECIFIED AREAS

WHEREAS, Utah state law and the International Fire Code allow the legislative body
of a municipality to prohibit the discharge of Class C common state approved
explosives (“fireworks”) and other ignition sources in specified areas if the City Fire
Marshal determines that existing hazardous environmental conditions necessitate
controlled use of any ignition source, including fireworks; and

WHEREAS, the City Fire Marshal has declared that existing hazardous
environmental conditions exist in certain areas of Draper City necessitate
controlled use of any ignition source, including fireworks; and

WHEREAS, the areas identified by the City Fire Marshall include mountainous,
brush-covered, forest-covered, or dry grass-covered areas; areas within 200 feet of
waterways, trails, canyons, washes, ravines, or similar areas; the wildland urban
interface area; and a limited area outside the hazardous areas described previously
in this paragraph to facilitate a readily identifiable closed area; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the minimal restrictions on public
convenience which would occur through a restriction on the use of personal
fireworks in designated areas is significantly outweighed by the need to protect the
general health, safety and welfare of the public;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION I. Restrictions on Personal Fireworks. Pursuant to the provisions of
Utah Code Ann. 15A-5-202.5 and based on the findings as noted above, the use of
fireworks is prohibited east of 1300 East Street, south of 13800 South Street, west
of Interstate 15, and in the area from 12300 South running north along 1000 East
Street to the north city boundary, then following the city boundary west to 700 East
Street, then north along 700 East Street to the north city boundary, then following
the city boundary west to Interstate 15, then south to 11800 South Street, then east
to the UTA TRAX line, then following the TRAX line to 12300 South Street, to be
known as the Mehraban Wetlands area, from the Wetlands Area following the TRAX
line east to 1300 East Street, also included as allowed in Utah Code Title 15A-5-
202.5 Section 1.1.2, is the East Jordan Canal waterway with a 200’ buffer on either
side through the remainder of the city, all as shown on the following map:

        [The map is attached as Exhibit A]

SECTION II. Duration and Scope. This Ordinance shall remain in effect until
October 15, 2023, unless terminated earlier by action of the City Council. This
Ordinance shall not apply to permitted public fireworks displays authorized by the
City and approved by the City Fire Marshal.

SECTION III. No Repealer. This Ordinance temporarily supersedes Draper City
Municipal Code 6-6-020(D) and does not repeal the same.

SECTION IV. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

SECTION V. Penalty. In addition to any other fines, penalties, or liability permitted
under Utah law, in accordance with Utah Code Ann. 53-7-225, a person is guilty of
an infraction, punishable by a fine of up to $1000, if a person discharges a Class C
common state approved explosive in violation of this Ordinance.

SECTION VI. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 16th DAY OF May, 2023.
