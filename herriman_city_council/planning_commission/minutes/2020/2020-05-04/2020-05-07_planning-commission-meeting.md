Item 3.2, Subdivision plat amendment was for a very small adjustment of less than three feet between houses.

Item 3.3, Review of a requirement for recessed windows at Parkside at Herriman Towne Center, which was a
request to not have to comply with the installation of recessed windows on all buildings. The recessed windows
were a condition of approval. Commissioners debated the requirement and possible design elements would add
dimension to windows. It was stated that recessed windows were better suited for some building materials, such as
brick and may be more difficult to install in others, but the City was looking for a higher quality of product. It was
offered that recessed windows add character and depth. Assistant City Planner Maloy interjected that conditions
were at times added to correct an exhibit. Recessed windows were in multiple developments throughout the City.
The request stated all windows facing the U road would be recessed. Commissioners offered the opinion that the
building lacked transition from the brick front, to the stucco on the side.

Community Development Director Blake Thomas discussed the requirement with a veteran City Planning Inspector
who offered that they require extra work and expertise, but they could be done. The concern he had was the Utah
climate could cause wood to move and joint movement may cause leaking. Commissioners discussed deep trim
benefits and appearance. Fake shutters were not preferred by the Commissioners, but they are a popular design
choice. The Commissioners agreed recessed windows should be in the brick fronts along the main road, and design
elements could be utilized to provide the appearance of depth in lesser seen areas. Commissioner Ferguson
mentioned that another way to get window depth was by having continuous exterior insulation on all the buildings.
Two inches of rigid insulation creates a deeper window improves energy efficiency. However, it does cost more.

Item 3.4 The applicant did not understand they were required to remove the existing vinyl fence. Thought they
would only need to install fencing were none was none. Neighborhood feedback was requested. The Ordinances
requiring six foot masonry walls, and prohibiting double fencing may not be waived by the Commission.
Construction could be deferred until lot two is developed, but the Commission was not fond of that possibility.

Item 3.5 Lot sizes were adjusted and the private street situation with a neighbor was resolved.

Assistant City Planner MacDonald reviewed the application and conditions. The Commission may choose to modify
the condition or not.

The applicant received approval for the Parkside project in October 2019. One of the conditions of approval required
the following:

1.      All windows shall comply with the Herriman Towne Center Design Guidelines for “Window Characteristics”
including a minimum window recess of three-inches to express building mass.

The applicant has some concerns with complying with this condition. They have submitted a letter which states that
“recessed windows on all buildings is problematic” (See Attachment B). They claim it will lead to leaking and
maintenance issues.
The applicant is proposing to build the elevations as they were approved by the Planning Commission, however they
are asking to not have to comply with the condition of recessed windows on all buildings. The recessed windows were
not shown on the approved elevations, it was a condition of approval that they change their elevations to add the
recessed windows. They have submitted an exhibit which shows where they can meet the recessed windows
requirement, and what all of the windows in the project will look like.

As stated previously by staff, the Design Guidelines do not specify the level of compliance that is necessary to warrant
Planning Commission approval.

Applicants Brandon Watson and Jaran Nicholls reviewed the request to remove some recessed windows was due to
May 7, 2020 – Planning Commission Minutes                                                               Page 7 of 21
leaking issues and was not to reduce cost. He stated some exteriors such as brick and masonry were better suited to
recessed windows. Others, such as stucco, were more likely to have leaking and warranty issues. Some elements were
proposed to create the look of recessed windows, such as shutters, roof over-hangs, and pop-outs to achieve depth.
Graphics displayed the proposed changes to townhomes and specific design elements were identified.

Commissioners talked through the proposed change and confirmed that flanged windows were to be used. Thicker,
extra deep trim would create more dimension and eliminate the need for all to be recessed. Specific street requirements
for Black Lotus and, Herriman Parkway were clarified. Commissioners expressed appreciation to the developer for
presenting a good product. It was requested for Commissioners to be very specific when they make a motion.
Commissioners stressed the importance for highly visible areas to be visually appealing.

Commissioner Jackson Ferguson motioned to approve item 3.3 file number C2008-014-26 Review of a Requirement for Recessed
Windows on Parkside at Herriman Towne Center and locations for recessed windows on the brick locations were acceptable for
Herriman and Black Lotus Drive. At the other Hardy Board locations double deep trim would be required at the windows, and at
stucco locations a two inch deep trim would be required at the windows, in lieu of extra depth at the windows, along all interior and
