Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then Councilor
Memmott offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by former Mayor Mark Jones and former council members Robert
Painter and Kent Park.

NEBO VIEW ELEMENTARY ROBOTICS TEAM PRESENTATION:

  The Nebobots robotics team from Nebo View Elementary presented to the council a skit about
renovating the Old Gym. The team consisted of 4th and 5th grade students who will compete at the end
of January with their presentation. Heather Westring and Tiffany Taylor are their advisors.

CENTRAL UTAH WATER CONSERVANCY DISTRICT (CUWCD) REPRESENTATIVE PRESENTATION:

   Councilor Memmott invited Rick Maloy, a Water Conservation Manager at CUWCD, to present water
conservation ideas that might work as an important tool in the future to help with water supply. He
explained the following ideas: water loss and control audits, Utah Water Savers Program, commercial
irrigation controller rebates, water conservation grants, irrigation design standards, new development
water conservation guides, restrictive covenants requiring water efficient landscapes, among other
ideas.

THREE NEW PLANNING COMMISSION MEMBERS APPOINTED:

   Mayor Glade R. Nielson introduced Ann Peterson and Kent Heap to the council and recommended
them along with Cory E. Thomson who was not present to be appointed as new members of the Nephi
City Planning Commission. Ms. Peterson and Mr. Heap stood and gave a brief history of themselves to
the council. Councilor Worwood moved to accept the appointment of Kent Heap, Cory E. Thomson, and
alternate Ann Peterson to the Planning Commission. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes of the regular council meeting held on 12-17-19, work session minutes 12-17-19, a list of
claims on the warrant register dated 1-7-20, and the sale of two burial rights to Roger and Patricia
Rasmussen and four burial rights Brad and Betty Ballow. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to approve the business license for Mt. Nebo Consulting, LLC. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

CR CIRCLE SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

   Councilor Jones moved to approve the final plat for CR Circle Subdivision. It was noted that the Clint
Robertson the developer had given the city a guaranteed improvement bond for the improvements that
still need to be completed. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

CURRENT FISCAL YEAR PRIORITIES/OUTCOMES REVIEWED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council a list of current fiscal year priorities and
the progress of each one. Many of the projects have been completed. A discussion ensued about
several of the projects.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reported that the JDRA had approved the adoption of a heavy industrial natural gas user
rate.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Seely reported that the JRDA is moving forward on the design of the transfer station at the
landfill.
  Councilor Ostler reported that the Miss Nephi Pageant will be held on April 17th. He also stated that
the committee would be holding a meeting on Monday, January 27th at 7:00 p.m. in the council
chambers.
  Councilor Worwood informed the council that the Ute Stampede will be held later than usual this
year. The dates of the Ute Stampede will be July 16th, 17th, and 18th.
  Councilor Jones said that he had visited the new eating establishment Ideal Dairy and had a positive
experience.
  Mayor Nielson reported on a meeting that he and staff had with the Juab County Commissioners and
some county employees about the easement issue with the county roads for the redundant natural gas
pipeline project. He felt like they had made forward progress and that there will be some give and take.
He noted that a 1990 agreement between the two parties about perpetual easements had never been
recorded.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Seely moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous
vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
