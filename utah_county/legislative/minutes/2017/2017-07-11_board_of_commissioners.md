28. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO SIGN AN AGREEMENT
       BETWEEN THE UTAH COUNTY DEPARTMENT OF DRUG AND ALCOHOL PREVENTION
       AND TREATMENT, AND PETER DEBENEDITTIS, TO PROVIDE TRAINING ON ALCOHOL
       EXPECTANCY AND MEDIA LITERACY
(Track A, 06:10)
Pat Bird of the Utah County Department of Drug and Alcohol Prevention and Treatment came forward to answer
any questions. Commissioner Lee expressed concern that the trainer is campaigning to be the next governor of New
Mexico, and has very progressive views. When Commissioner Lee noted Peter DeBenedittis promotes higher taxes,
Mr. Bird responded this lecture is about media literacy – not government taxes. Mr. Bird went on to talk about the
importance of identifying advertisement in the community, especially how it impacts youth views of alcohol.
Commissioner Ivie mentioned “hard” lemonades and solutions to prevent overconsumption. Mr. Bird explained this
training will address proposed solutions. Commissioner Lee asked how the presenters are chosen, and Mr. Bird
explained Mr. DeBenedittis was highly recommended at a national level for Public Health and Safety; Mr.
DeBenedittis has an evidence-based curriculum and the Utah Prevention Network is very interested in his research.
Commissioner Lee suggested Mr. DeBenedittis may not reflect the local county values and culture.

            Commissioner Ivie made the motion to continue Consent Agenda Item No. 28,
            regarding an agreement with Peter DeBenedittis to provide training on alcohol
            expectancy and media literacy, for one week to the July 18, 2017 meeting of the
            Utah County Board of Commissioners. The motion was seconded by
            Commissioner Graves and carried with the following vote:
