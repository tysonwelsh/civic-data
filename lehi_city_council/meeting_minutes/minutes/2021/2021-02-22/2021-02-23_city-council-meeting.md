Pre-Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Albrecht gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1) Department Goals and Reports Presentation
Cameron Boyle gave a presentation on department goals and reports. He asked the
Councilmembers to consider that goals and how they plan to support that goal. Cameron
introduced the platform, Bang the Table, the City will be using for public engagement
Community Development. Mr. Boyle reviewed each department’s goals and how to implement
those goals.

3. Agenda Questions
Councilmember Condie asked about item number seven and if it was possible to start with
questions and dialogue to Staff before opening the item for public comment. Mayor Johnson
agreed.

Ryan Wood clarified that the Councilmembers have a few different roles, the legislative role being
the most important. He explained the difference between legislative, administrative, and quasi-
judicial. Mr. Wood also discussed how the concerns of health, safety and welfare can be used in
the Councilmembers decision making.

Councilmember Condie asked if the language on resolution item thirteen can be changed before it
is printed from “her” to “his”.

7. Consideration of Ordinance #10-2021, approval of the Yeomans & Morgan General
   Plan Amendment, changing the land use designation from LDR & MDR to IHDR
   on approximately 5 acres of property located at 143 South 1100 West.
Adam Albrecht was the applicant.

Councilmember Condie expressed his thanks to the applicant for taking the feedback. He stated
he felt the joining of the neighborhoods was too dramatic. He stated the request states it is
consistent with general plan and land uses, sustainability and uses and existing uses identified by
the general plan. Councilor Condie does not feel this density is justified in this particular area.
Councilmember Hancock expressed that not only does the existing plan say that, but the
direction that they have given planning does not express that intent. Councilor Condie stated
twice now, the applicant has taken the feedback they have been given and taken the suggestions
and put them towards their development. Kim Struthers stated he doesn't feel like they have
promoted the future of the development, but rather there are some written best practices that they
may try to apply for. He clarified that Planning Staff makes recommendations for the applicant
and Planning Commission, and the Planning Commission is a governing body that recommended
denial. Kim stated the applicant is asking for a substantial more amount than that. Mayor
Johnson wanted to clarify the map was not just the focus of the general plan, that the written
documents also support the plan and seem to be in conflict at times. Councilmember Southwick
agreed with much of what Councilor Condie was saying. He appreciates what Roger and Adam
have done but doesn't feel this development fits.

Mayor Johnson opened the public hearing for comment.
Lehi City Council Meeting                        4                                 February 23, 2021
Drew Wilson stated he and his wife have owned Woody Berry's property since Sept of 2020.
Drew explained the development would affect their property dramatically. Drew and his wife
were concerned about the development and would like to preserve their animal rights. There are
many challenges that would come with the development such as no sidewalk, traffic, etc. Drew
said this development was not zoned for this type of TOD, and he understands this is Roger's
property and right, but this is a drastic change, especially for the reasons he listed earlier.

Chris Brown asked if there was a new general plan that was going to come out. Kim Struthers
stated the current draft hasn't been adopted yet, but not to the level that Mr. Albrecht would like.

Mayor Johnson closed the public hearing.

Councilmember Koivisto stated higher density doesn't always equate affordable housing. She
emphasized this does not fit the general plan. Councilmember Albrecht sated she does not feel
like the utilities can support the infrastructure.

Motion: Councilor Condie moved to deny Yeomans & Morgan General
        Plan Amendment, changing the land use designation from LDR & MDR to IHDR
        on approximately 5 acres of property located at 143 South 1100 West; with the
        findings and comments that the existing utility, and general plan is not in
        conformance and could have adverse effects with the surrounding properties.
        Councilor Southwick seconded the motion.

Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
Councilor Hancock, Yes; and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
