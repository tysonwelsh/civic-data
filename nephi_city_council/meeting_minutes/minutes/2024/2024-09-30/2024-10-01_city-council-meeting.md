ORIDNANCE 09-17-2024 LOVELESS APARTMENTS ZONE CHANGE ADOPTED:

    City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council a zone change request for a project property located on the northeast corner of the intersection of 200 W and 700 N.  The property is currently owned by Nikki Hansen, who has signed authorization to allow Priority Homes to represent the interests of her property.  Priority Homes is looking to build two more four-plexes along 200 W like the two they have already, south of Loveless Estates Plat A subdivision.  To have the opportunity to do so they are proposing the following:
	•	A rezone of parcel XA00-0775- (.53 acres) from R1-8 to R3-8 to allow for multi-family housing.
	•	A partial street vacation on 200 W from approximately 700 to 760 N, abandoning the right-of-way to 60 feet. The excess property would be placed in surplus by the city and could potentially be obtained by Priority Homes.
 
To achieve the density necessary to get to 8 units, a portion of the Loveless Estates, land would need to be deeded to the Loveless Apartments development.  This would require an amendment to the Loveless Estates Subdivision Plat.  Mr. Atkinson stated that there are other ways to achieve the density such as employing the city’s blight ordinance or through development agreement, but the cleanest way would be through a plat amendment.   Mr. Atkinson recommended to the council that they adopt the zone change ordinance and then at a later date they can decide about the street vacation.  Travis Ludlow, the developer of the property, stated that these future apartments units would be more handicap accessible. 

    Councilor Travis Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 09-17-2024 with the plat amendment option to gain the density needed.  Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion.  
The motion passed on the following roll-call vote:

RED CLIFFS STORAGE ANNEXATION PETITION ACCEPTED AND ALLOWED TO MOVE FORWARD:
   
    Mr. Atkinson stated that he had emailed out the analysis for the Red Cliffs Storage annexation area. 
In the Council meeting on September 3rd, the City Council elected to change the annexation level on some properties near the southern boundary of the city from a level 3 to a level 2 after adding the south triangle piece to the annexation area map as a Level 3.
     The annexation request was for two properties.  The northeast narrow triangle shaped property had already been developed to be an RV and boat storage facility.   This property will be accessed off Sheep Lane and the city has already purchased the electrical assets from Rocky Mountain Power.  The second parcel, to the southwest, is a triangle property just west of Owens Corning and is accessed from Elevator Road.  It is surrounded by the city limits on the south and east and by the freeway on the north.  Both parcels would most likely be zoned as Highway Commercial for the storage facility and industrial for the southern triangle.  Mr. Atkinson said that because the city does not know how the southern triangle piece will be developed, there might be some type of businesses that are allowed in the industrial zone that the council would not be in favor of along the highly visible corridor of I-15.  Mr. Atkinson suggested that the council might want to enter into a development or annexation agreement to assure the type of business that the council would be in favor of.  
    Councilor JD Parady made a motion to move both properties to a Level 1 on the annexation area map. Councilor Cowan seconded the motion.  The motion passed on a unanimous vote. 
    Councilor Skip Worwood moved to accept the Red Cliffs Storage Annexation Petition to allow it to move forward in the annexation process.  Councilor Callaway seconded the motion.  The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

WEST CENTRAL STRIKE FORCE (STRIKE FORCE) AGREEMENT APPROVED:

    Nephi City Police Department along with Payson City PD, and Santaquin City PD have agreed to form a drug task force named The West Central Strike Force. CRIMSON FLATS/RED CLIFFS PARK DESIGN CONTRACT WITH CIVIL SCIENCE APPROVED:

    Mr. Atkinson reminded the council that in the last council meeting they had approved awarding the bid to Civil Science as the vendor to design the Crimson Flats/Red Cliffs Park.  He then presented a professional service contract for their consideration.  The scope of work will include the design for both parks as well as public input sessions and presentation of the design to the City Council.  The contract total will be $70,600 and will be paid for from the planning projects line in the Capital Projects Fund.  Carryover funds from the prior fiscal year were enough to cover this cost. 
     Councilor Skip Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the professional service agreement with Civil Science.   Councilor Callaway seconded the motion.  The motion passed unanimously.
