17-79-406. Public uses to conform to general plan.

[History: Renumbered and Amended by Chapter 14, 2025 Special Session 1]

After the legislative body has adopted a general plan, the following public properties may not be constructed or authorized unless the public property conforms to the current county general plan:
(1) a street, park, or other public way, ground, place, or space;
(2) a publicly owned building or structure; and
(3) a public utility, whether publicly or privately owned.
