ACTION ITEM

Applicant:     South Salt Lake Neighborhoods Department, represented by Anthony Baimont

Address:       2508 South 500 East
South Salt Lake City Planning Commission Regular Meeting                                           3
July 11, 2024


Ms. Ungricht presented the Staff Report and explained that a Subdivision Plat application is before
the Planning Commission for the Bickley Park Plat. It is located at 2508 South 500 East. The
request is to consolidate two parcels into one lot. After consolidation, the property will be 0.87
acres. She explained that this is a City park and it is zoned Open Space. It is directly north of the
South Salt Lake Community Center. The reason for this application is to put in a new playground.
Some future improvements are desired in addition to the playground. For example, an additional
pickleball court. Images of the existing conditions were shared for reference as well as the
Preliminary Plat. The Open Space Zone does not require any parking. However, there is adequate
parking at the South Salt Lake Community Center, which is where most visitors park.

The Site Plan was shared. Ms. Ungricht explained that it illustrates what is currently on the site.
She reported that the application meets several General Plan goals, which include the following:

   •   Community Value Goal #1:
          o Ensure that existing and new development has equitable access to all services and
              amenities in South Salt Lake.
   •   Parks, Recreation, and Open Space Strategy #1:
          o Improve access and quality of parks, trails, and open space for all areas of South
              Salt Lake.
   •   Community Facilities Goal #2:
          o Provide community centers that promote health, education, and equity.

Staff recommends the Commission approve the Preliminary Subdivision Plat to consolidate two
parcels into one lot, located at 2508 South 500 East, based upon the information submitted by the
applicant, General Plan considerations, and Ordinance analysis. Staff proposes Findings of Fact,
Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval as stated in the Staff Report and on the record.

Commissioner Bellina asked if there are renderings of the playground to show what will be added
to the park. Ms. Ungricht clarified that the plans were not ready at the time that the Staff Report
was published, because the permit was submitted a few days ago, but she offered to send them to
Commissioners. Discussions were had about the slide that was previously installed at Lions Park.

Mr. Baimont reported that many of the parks in the City are chaotic in terms of the parcels. For
instance, Fitts Park has 40 parcels in addition to alleys, right-of-ways, and bodies of water. He
shared additional information about the current application and the playground that is proposed.
The building is scheduled to take place between July 30, 2024, and August 1, 2024. In addition to
the playground, there is the potential to add another pickleball court in the future opposite to the
existing court. Ms. Ungricht informed the Commission that she found some renderings of the
playground. She offered to share those but noted that what she has are construction drawings.

Chair Carter opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

 STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
 Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the preliminary subdivision plat to consolidate two
 parcels into one-lot, located at 2508 South 500 East, based upon the information submitted by the applicant,
 the general plan considerations and ordinance analysis and proposes the following Findings of Fact,
 Conclusions of Law and Conditions of Approval.

Findings of Fact:

 1. On May 1, 2024, the South Salt Lake Neighborhoods Department submitted a plat application to
     consolidate two parcels into one lot.

 2. The subject parcels are located at 2508 South 500 East and 2520 South 500 East.

 3. The two parcels are located in the Open Space land use district.

 4. The proposed consolidation plat will consolidate two parcels into a 0.87-acre lot.

 5. The plat will allow the installation of more park equipment like a playground.

Conclusions of Law:

 1. The use, as specifically conditioned below, is consistent with the South Salt Lake City General Plan.

 2. The use, as specifically conditioned below, is compatible in use, scale, and design with allowed uses in
    the Open Space land use district.

 3. The subdivision as specifically conditioned below, does not compromise the health, safety, or welfare of:
    Persons employed within or using the proposed development; Those residing or working in the vicinity of
    the proposed use or development; or Property or improvements in the vicinity of the proposed use or
    development.

 4. The subdivision, as specifically conditioned below does not impose disproportionate burdens on the
    citizens of the city.

 5. Staff has reviewed this subdivision for compliance with SSL Municipal Code Section “17.10.140
    Subdivision Development Lot Standards” and found that this subdivision is compliant with this section, as
    outlined above.

 6. Staff has reviewed this subdivision for compliance with SSL Municipal Code Section “17.10.150
    Subdivision and Condominium Plat Layout Requirements” and found that this subdivision is compliant
    with this section, as outlined above.

 7. Staff has reviewed this subdivision for compliance with SSL Municipal Code Sections 17.10.160, 17.10.170,
    17.10.180, 17.10.190, 17.10.200, and 17.10.210, and found that this subdivision is compliant with those
    sections, as outlined above.

Conditions of Approval:

 1. The applicant will continue to work with City staff to make all technical corrections necessary prior to
    recording;
