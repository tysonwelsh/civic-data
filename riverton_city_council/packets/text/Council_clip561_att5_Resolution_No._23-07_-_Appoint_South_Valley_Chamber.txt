                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-07

        A RESOLUTION APPOINTING A CITY REPRESENTATIVE FOR THE
                       SOUTH VALLEY CHAMBER

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has formally supported the South Valley Chamber; and

     WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint a representative for the South Valley
Chamber; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember Andy
Pierucci is willing to serve as the City’s representative for the South Valley Chamber.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of
          Councilmember Andy Pierucci to serve as the City’s representative for the
          South Valley Chamber until a successor is appointed.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
February 2023 by the following vote:
