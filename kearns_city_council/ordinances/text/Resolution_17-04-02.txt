KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A RULE THAT A METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
MEMBER MAY NOT ACT AS A “CONTROL PERSON,” AS DEFINED HEREIN, IN A
NONPROFIT ORGANIZATION, SUCH AS THE KEARNS COMMUNITY COUNCIL, IF
SUCH ORGANIZATION IS EITHER SEEKING A CONTRIBUTION OR WAIVER OF FEES
FROM THE METRO TOWNSHIP UNDER §10-8-2, OR PROVIDES RECOMMENDATIONS
ON LAND USE MATTERS.
RECITALS AND DEFINITIONS
A, The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (““UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq. and acts as the elected municipal legislative body for
Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.
C, A “Nonprofit Organization” is a type of business entity, such as the Kearns Community
Council, created for benevolent, artistic, educational, philanthropic, humane, patriotic, social
welfare, public health, environmental conservation, civic, or other similar organization which is
entitled to tax exempt status under the laws of the United States and the State of Utah.
D. A “Control Person” is a member of a Nonprofit Organization who has significant sway or
direct control over the decision-making processes of the organization including, but not limited
to, decisions on budgets, expenditures, use of organizational assets, or other control actions of
the organization.
E, “Contributions and Waivers” are monetary and non-monetary considerations the Council
may choose to grant to certain Nonprofit Organizations pursuant to UCA §§ 10-8-2 et seq.
RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP AS
FOLLOWS:

1 To avoid the appearance of impropriety and avoid potential for conflicts of interest,
from the date of this Resolution forward a member of the Kearns Metro Township may not serve
as a Control Person, as defined herein in a Nonprofit Organization, such as the Kearns
Community Council, if such organization shall either seek Contributions and Waivers from the
Metro Township Council or otherwise desires to make official land use recommendations to the
Metro Township Council.

2. Nothing contained herein waives any other legal requirement to avoid conflicts of
interests under Utah Code § 67-16-9 et seq. (“No public officer or public employee shall have
personal investments in any business entity which will create a substantial conflict between his

private interests and his public duties”) or other ethical standards adopted by the Kearns Metro
Township.
