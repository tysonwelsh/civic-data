                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 5:35 p.m.

Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission will hear a request to consider adopting proposed
Amendment plans for the areas within Kearns. The Amendment includes a supplementary Element on
Resilience + Infrastructure. This Element was included in the original General Plan scope of work but was
postponed so that the General Plan could be adopted by October 2020. Please note that this Amendment
does not delete, replace, or otherwise change any part of the adopted General Plan; the Amendment instead
proposes an additional Element focused on Kearns’ infrastructure and community resiliency. Planner:
Kayla Mauldin (Motion/Voting)

The public is invited to review and inspect the proposed Amendment at https://bit.ly/lrp-kearns or at the
Kearns website (https://www.kmtutah.org/) under “public hearings.”

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed Amendment plan after taking comments from the public
during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s rules
of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three minutes
per person.

Commissioner Nelson motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Hatch seconded that motion.
