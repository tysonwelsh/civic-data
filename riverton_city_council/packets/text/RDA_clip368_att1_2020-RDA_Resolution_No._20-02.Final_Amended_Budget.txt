                        RIVERTON REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                            RDA RESOLUTION NO. 20-02

      A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
   ADOPTING A FINAL AMENDED BUDGET FOR THE 2019-2020 FISCAL YEAR

       WHEREAS, State law requires the adoption of final amendments to the Fiscal Year 2019-
2020 budgets for each entity located within the State of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the amended budgets comply with the requirements set out in U.C.A. § 10-6-
128; and

       WHEREAS, the budgets have been reviewed, considered, and adopted by the governing
body and have now been hereby amended.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton Redevelopment Agency, State
of Utah, as follows:

       1. That Riverton Redevelopment Agency adopts the Final Amended Budgets for the
          Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2019 and ending June 30, 2020, as attached hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
