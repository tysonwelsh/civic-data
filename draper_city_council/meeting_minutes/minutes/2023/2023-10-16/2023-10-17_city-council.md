5.   Public Comments
     Gary McDougal, a Draper resident, said he and his partner applied in 1998 to
     develop Southgate Office Condominiums. He said they were told Draper did not
     have stormwater facilities on the property, so he and his partner engineered and
     built a pond to contain stormwater. A portion of the property, including the pond
     area, was subsequently purchased by UDOT, and UDOT said they would take care
     of the water that went to that pond. Mr. McDougal said the Owners Association
     President contacted and asked Draper City to remove storm drain fees from the
     utility bill for the property, and was told if the fees were not paid, the City would
     shut water off to the buildings. Mr. McDougal said he believed the City owed them
     an explanation, and a refund of the storm drain fees if the City was not, in fact,
     providing and maintaining storm drain facilities for the property. Mr. McDougal said
     if the City was maintaining storm drain facilities for the property, he believed Draper
     owed them for the cost of flooding that occurred when storm drain facilities were
     overrun.

     Stacey Williams, a Draper resident, requested the City either remove signs
     prohibiting parking in front of her home or provide a permit so her family could
     park in front of her home without being penalized.

     Jerry Wilson, a Draper resident, said he lived on the same street as Ms. Williams and
     echoed her parking concerns. He said it was nice residents could now park on the
     opposite side of the street, but he would like to be able to park in front of his home.
     He commented that fencing was not completed around the neighboring
     apartments, and suggested completion of the fence would help alleviate some of
     the parking issues.

     Brent Horrocks referred to the storm drain issue mentioned by Mr. McDougal and
     asked who in the City they could follow up with. Mayor Walker suggested Mr.
     Horrocks follow up with City Manager David Dobbins.

7.    Items for Council Consideration
7.a   Public Hearing: Ordinances #1590 and 1591, amending the Official Land Use
      Map of Draper City for approximately 3.89 acres of property from
      Neighborhood Commercial to Community Commercial and an Ordinance
      amending the Official Zoning Map of Draper City from CC (Community
      Commercial) to CG (General Commercial) located at approximately 687 West
      12300 South within Draper City, otherwise known as the Specialized
      Manufacturing Land Use Map and Zoning Map Amendment.
      Todd Draper, a Planner, explained the requested amendments to the Official Land
      Use Map and Official Zoning Map for the property located at approximately 687
      West 12300 South. He reported the Planning Commission reviewed the request and
      forwarded a positive recommendation. Mr. Draper answered questions from the
      Council.
