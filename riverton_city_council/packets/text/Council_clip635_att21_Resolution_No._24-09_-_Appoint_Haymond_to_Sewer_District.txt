                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      RESOLUTION NO. 24-09

 A RESOLUTION APPOINTING COUNCILMEMBER HAYMOND TO SERVE AS A BOARD
            TRUSTEE FOR THE SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT.

        WHEREAS, the residents of Riverton City receive sewer service from a local district known as
the South Valley Sewer District (hereinafter referred to as the “District”); and

      WHEREAS, the District is governed by a Board of Trustees, which generally consists of officials
who were elected to serve as officials for the municipalities that receive service from the District; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17b-1-304 vests the Riverton City Council, as an appointing
authority, with the power to appoint a member of the City’s governing body to serve on the District board
of Trustees; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-b-301, Riverton City is governed by a Six
Council Member form of Government; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember Haymond
service on the District’s Board of Trustees will benefit the residents who receive sewer service, and
therefore Councilmember Haymond should be appointed to the Board of Trustees.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

        Section 1.      The Riverton City Council hereby appoints Councilmember Haymond to serve
                        on the Board of Trustees for the South Valley Sewer until January 1, 2028.

        Section 2.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 2nd day of January,
