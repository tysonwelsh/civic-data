2. PUBLIC HEARING
        a. Resolution No. 232 amendments to the budget for Fiscal Year 2022
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck explained that at the beginning of the year Board
Members approved a budget from four of the RDA areas to contribute towards building a water tank
increasing storage capacity in the area at a cost of $4,990,000. The proposed budget amendment
would transfer funds from the RDA budget to the Water fund in order to manage the construction
of the tank. Once the tank is built the Water Fund would own that asset. The proposed transfer has
been reviewed by the City Attorney’s and RDA Attorney Adam Long who have confirmed it is legally
allowed. The transfer would remove the expenditure requirement to build the fund in the RDA
providing a net zero effect. The water tank was being designed by CRS Engineering.

City Attorney Rob Wall, responding to a question from Vice Chair Green asking for further
clarification on how the transfer is legal, described the transfer like a loan. The idea of loaning from
