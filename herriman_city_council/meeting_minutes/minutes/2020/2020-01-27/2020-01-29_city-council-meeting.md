The following are the minutes of the Joint City Council and Planning Commission Meeting held on
Wednesday, January 29, 2020 at 6:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Community Room, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the
City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council and Commission, media, and
interested citizens.


Presiding: Chair Andy Powell

Council Members Present: Steven Shields, Jared Henderson, Clint Smith, Mayor David Watts

Planning Commission Members Present: Andrea Bradford, Adam Jacobson, Jackson Ferguson, Lorin
Palmer, Heather Garcia

Excused Councilmembers and Commissioners: Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn, Commissioner Brody
Rypien, Commissioner Chris Berbert, and Alternate Commissioner Joy Kaseke

Staff Present: City Manager Brett Wood, Assistant City Manager Gordon Haight, Director of Administration
and Communications Tami Moody, City Attorney John Brems, Deputy City Recorder Wendy Thorpe, City
Planner Michael Maloy, Assistant City Planner Bryn MacDonald, Communications Specialist Jonathan
Lafollette, Planning Coordinator Nick Whittaker, Parks and Recreation Director Wendy Thomas. HPD
Lieutenant Brian Weidmer, HPD Officer Jose Lopez & HPD Marcus Beckstead




Chair Andy Powell called the meeting to order at 6:04 pm.
