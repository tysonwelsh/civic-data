3.1. Council Members
Page 1 of 20
County Council Meeting Minutes April 13, 2021
Council Member Alvord  expressed his sympathy to the deputies shot and injured 
at the Sheriff ’s Office.  Law enforcement officers put their lives on the line without 
working for a lot of pay.  He pledged support to make sure their salaries were 
competitive or above.  
He also asked the Mayor to look at reopening recreation facilities so people can 
exercise.  People face many health risks and Covid is just one of them.  He has also 
received complaints that the County facilities are not open for high school swim 
teams or hockey players.  The risk to youth is pretty low for Covid.  
Council Member Snelgrove  stated the County might be doing more harm than 
good by making its recreation centers and facilities less accessible rather than 
opening them up for greater usage.  One of the side effects of Covid has been an 
average weight gain of 18 pounds, and before Covid, obesity was the number one 
health issue in America.  
Council Member Theodore  stated she has received complaints about the 
recreation centers not being open or having reduced hours.  People are eager to 
start using them.  Physical fitness is good for health.
Council Member Bradley  stated he wanted to ensure people would not be at 
greater risk by reopening the facilities.   
Council Member Granato  stated she has been utilizing the Millcreek Recreation 
Center and commended the staff there.
3.2. Other Elected Officials
Mr. Sim Gill , District Attorney, announced that the District Attorney ’s Office has 
hired Jared Smith as the new Risk Manager to replace Jeff Rowley who left the 
County.  He has a wealth of experience as a Risk Manager.  Also, Jim Winder, 
Chief of Investigations, is leaving the County.  The District Attorney ’s Office will 
be appointing Joe Tarver, Assistant Chief, Murray City Police Department, to fill 
that position.  He will start the beginning of May.

the Salt Lake County Mayor Proclaiming April 18-25, 2021 
“National Infertility Awareness Week” in Salt Lake County
21-0455
Attachments: Staff Report
National Infertility Awarness Week
Council Member Newton read the following proclamation declaring April 
18-25, 2021, as National Infertility Awareness Week in Salt Lake County: 
PROCLAMATION
WHEREAS, infertility is a medical condition recognized by the World Health 
Organization, the Center for Disease Control and Prevention and the 
American Medical Association; and 
WHEREAS, infertility affects one in eight couples and does not discriminate 
based on race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic level; and
WHEREAS, the Utah Infertility Resource Center and RESOLVE ‒ The 
National Infertility Association work to bring awareness to those suffering 
with infertility; and
WHEREAS, families wanting to build their families must use resources such 
Page 3 of 20
County Council Meeting Minutes April 13, 2021
as adoption or medical intervention including invitro fertilization; and 
WHEREAS, cost and lack of insurance coverage make it difficult for many 
families within Salt Lake County and Utah to grow their families; and
WHEREAS, it is important to support these families and raise awareness 
about this devastating medical condition and work to remove barriers for 
families and help them access the resource needed to grow their families.
NOW, THEREFORE, the Salt Lake County Council, does hereby join with 
Mayor Jenny Wilson and our entire community in proclaiming April 18-25, 
2021, as
National Infertility Awareness Week
and urge community and faith leaders, public officials, healthcare providers 
and all people of Salt Lake County to recognize and remember those families 
struggling to build their families and support and help them during this 
difficult journey.

W I T N E S S E T H

cooperation agreement to act jointly and cooperatively on the basis of mutual 
advantage;
WHEREAS, during the 2015 General Session, the State Legislature amended 
Section 72-2-117.5 of the Utah Transportation Code (Utah Code Ann. §§ 
72-1-101 et seq .) to provide corridor preservation funds to local counties for 
disbursement to various cities and governmental entities, as recommended 
and endorsed by a council of governments (hereinafter “Corridor 
Preservation Funds”);
WHEREAS, by letter dated December 20, 2020 and attached hereto as 
ATTACHMENT A (the “Recommendation Letter ”), the Salt Lake County 
Council of Governments (COG), an association of local governments in Salt 
Lake County, requested that the County Council approve its recommended 
distribution to the City from the Salt Lake County Corridor Preservation 
Fund to enable the City to acquire property needed for a transportation 
project considered and approved by COG at approximately 6774 West 
Herriman Hwy ., Herriman, Utah consistent with the purpose and 
requirements of Section 72-2-117.5; and
WHEREAS, the County and the City now desire to enter into the interlocal 
cooperation agreement attached hereto as ATTACHMENT B (the “Interlocal 
Agreement”) providing for the transfer of Fifty-Four Thousand 
Five-Hundred and Seventy-Two Dollars ($54,572.00) of Corridor 
Preservation Funds to the City to be used by the City as described in the 
Interlocal Agreement and in accordance with Section 72-2-117.5 of the Utah 
Transportation Code.
