                                        Alta Canyon Sports Center Town Hall


This is a Town Hall presented by Council Members Zach Robinson and Scott Earl to discuss the Alta Canyon
Sports Center. This notice, with attached agenda, is intended to notify the public that the Town Hall may be
attended by a quorum of City Council Members. The Council will not convene a meeting nor take action on any
item.

(9565 Highland Drive, Sandy, UT 84092), and via Zoom Webinar. Interested parties may
