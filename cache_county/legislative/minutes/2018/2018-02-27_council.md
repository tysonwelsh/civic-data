OTHER ITEMS:

0) Legislative Update — Executive Buttars reported that SB136, first substitution, is a
concern. He received a notice from UAC seeking input. The bill impacts Cache County
because it sets percentages for the local option sales tax for counties with a transit
district. If other options are not implemented by 2020, then we lose the opportunity to do
so. Buttars has contacted Lincoln Shurtz at UAC.

Attorney Edwards suggested Council members go to the UAC website for an outline on
the bill for more information.

© Highway 30 Update - Executive Buttars and Council members Worthen, Ward and
Merrill met with Mike Romero, Carlos Braceras and Shane Marshall from UDOT to
discuss Highway 30 and were encouraged that there might be some things that can be
worked out. Buttars will contact UDOT Region Director Kris Peterson as well. Vice
Chairman Ward remarked UDOT gave the County some assignments relating to
justification and he spoke with L W Miller who said he can give the county a lot of
information on that subject. Buttars interposed that Jeff Gilbert could be a good source
as well.

Council member Merrill voiced appreciation for Box Elder County's support at the
meeting.

O Cache Events Center Update — Executive Buttars reported that the Event Coordinator
and his assistant have secured several different events for the building already. Buttars
is still meeting with the contactor and architect twice a month.

PUBLIC HEARING: FEBRUARY 27, 2018-5:30 P.M.-ORDINANCE NO. 2018-02 — CODE
AMENDMENTS-Amendments to Titles 17.01 and 17.02 regarding authority and plain language
Chris Harrild gave an overview noting that the changes were required to be compliant with state
code and best practice guidelines

There was much discussion by the Council on the removal of an extension option. Harrild and
Director Runhaar indicated that extensions are time and money consuming and unnecessary
because the applicant has a full year to accomplish what is needed. Most asking for an
extension have simply waited too long to begin the required process.

Other concerns related to the home-based business amendment were mitigated with a better
understanding of the basis for the amendment.

Chairman Erickson opened the Public Hearing and invited public comment. There was
none.

ACTION: Motion by Council member Zilles to close the Public Hearing-February 27,
2018-5:30 p.m.-Code Amendments-Amendments to Titles 17.01 and 17.02 regarding
authority and plain language. Worthen seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous,
7-0.

getting enough work done to allow him to do his work. Harrild indicated the applicant

sufficient justification.

COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS

Gina Worthen attended the UDOT STIP meeting and said projects coming up are: a snow
fence at the summit of Logan Canyon in 2019; Logan Main Street deep reconstruction in 2023
with a temporary fix in 2019; realignment of the intersection by Ted's; stoplights at 1000 West
and US 89/91, 1600 North and Main; 3100 North and Main.

The Herald Journal is doing a story on Councils that have public comment at the beginning of
each meeting and asked for the Council's views on whether they want to do that. Council
member Merrill spoke on behalf of the Council stating that the County has an open door policy
and it is more beneficial to the public to personally contact a Council member or the County
Executive about a concern so there is time to gather pertinent facts and give an informed
answer. Other Council members concurred.

Gordon Zilles received an email about the Cowboy Rendezvous in Hyrum this weekend and
urged Council members to attend if they can.

Dave Erickson asked if the budget process can somehow be pushed up and asked for input on
how that might be achieved.

Erickson met with the County Attorney's office and appreciates all they do.

Greg Merrill said the Sheriff still has a problem securing enough deputies. There are twelve
positions open, but the Academy will have only seven graduates.

There are still concerns that law enforcement officers who retire have to wait a year before
being employed in law enforcement again. Many find other jobs during that time and do not
return to law enforcement.

Barbara Tidwell reminded the Council of the Republican Lincoln Dinner this Friday at the
Riverwoods Conference Center and the Republican Women’s luncheon with the legislators on
March 27".

EMORANDUM

Date: 27 February 2018
From: Cache County Development Services

To:

Sui

County Council
bject: Extension of effective period of approval for the View of the Valley Subdivision

A request has been made to extend the effective period of approval of the View of the Valley
Subdivision for two (2) years (Attachment 1).

Current expiration: March 14, 2018
Requested extension expiration: March 14, 2020

The Cache County Land Use Ordinance §17.02.070 Effective Period for Land Use Authority
Approval, section B specifies that,

“At the discretion of the Land Use Authority, the effective period of approval may be extended
for up to two (2) years beyond the one year period of the original approval. Within that
extension no operation is allowed until the permit has been recorded and all conditions of the
permit have been met. To request an extension, an applicant must submit nonfinancial
justification to the Land Use Authority prior to the expiration of the original one year period of
approval.”

Mr. Doug Bassett’s justification for a request for extension (Attachment 1) identifies that the delay
in recording the approved subdivision plat within a year of approval was due to:
1. The delayed development and associated improvements of the Nautica Subdivision by
Lifestyle Homes.

The Development Services staff has completed a review of the justifications for the extension of the
effective period of approval and has identified the following:

1. The required improvements attached as conditions to the County Council’s approval include
the design, review, and construction of improvements to the county road 1600 West as
required by the County Road Manual.

2. The road improvements required for the View of the Valley Subdivision are also a part of the
road improvement requirements as conditions of the Nautica Subdivision approval.

3. The Nautica Subdivision approval was initially set to expire on June 14, 2017 but was
extended to October 18, 2017. Prior to that date, the proponent for Nautica Subdivision
entered into an improvement agreement with the County for the conditioned improvements
and was then able to record that subdivision plat.

4. Mr. Bassett has not entered into an improvement agreement and has not submitted plan sets
for the required improvement of the access road to the View of the Valley Subdivision.

5. A letter detailing the improvements required prior to recording the plat and the option to
enter into an improvement agreement were sent to Mr. Basset on March 16, 2017 and again
referenced in the expiration notice sent February 6, 2018.

Based on Mr. Bassett’s request and the details above, staff has been unable to find that Mr. Bassett
has provided justification sufficient to warrant an extension of the effective period of approval. Staff
therefore recommends the County Council deny the request to extend the effective period of
approval.

Failure to record the plat by March 14, 2018 will void the Land Use Authority approval of the View

of the Valley Subdivision. If the plat is not recorded and the approval is void, a new land use
application is required for the proposed subdivision.
