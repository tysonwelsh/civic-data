PUBLIC HEARING: TO ADOPT THE FINAL 2022 WASHINGTON COUNTY BUDGET

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Open the Public Hearing to Adopt the
Final 2022 Washington County Budget. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson
and Snow voting aye.

Ms. Susan Lewis explained several changes that took place to the 2022 budget including in
response to the challenges in recruitment of new employees and the retention of existing
employees, a market adjustment were made to the employees’ wages. Additionally, search and
rescue calls have increased in Washington County and as a result, it was determined that SAR
volunteers would be paid $50.00 to $100 for call-outs. A one time money appropriation for the
Emergency Medical Transport was also made in order to assist with transition services along SR-

18.
The full budget is available from the Clerk/Auditor’s Office upon request.
Seeing no one else come forward, Commissioner Iverson closed the public hearing.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2021-1198-O, AN ORDINANCE REPEALING

THE PURCHASE OF THE ZION NATIONAL PARK LLC

Washington County Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that Zion National Park, LLC has
owned and operated the URL ZionNationalPark.com since its inception. The LLC approached the
County about purchasing the URL as it is one of the top five (5) most relevant internet search
engine results related to Zion National Park and the domain drives large amounts of organic visitor
traffic. The TAB Board recommended purchase of the URL, digital assets, content, backend data,
social media channels and design work for $200,000.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2880, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT TO PARTICIPATE IN THE STATE WIDE OPIOID SETTLEMENT

Mr. Snow explained that there are two (2) nationwide opioid settlement agreements wherein, if it
participates in the settlements, Washington County could receive up to 7.5 million dollars over
time. Prominent opioid distributors presented a settlement agreement to the State of Utah which
requires a 95% participation rate of the population of all litigating governmental entities to
maximize the settlement amount received. The County will participate only if the 95% threshold
is met.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2880, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement to Participate in the State Wide Opioid
Settlement. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Snow voting aye.
