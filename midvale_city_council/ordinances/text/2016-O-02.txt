                                   MID VALE CITY, UTAH

                                 ORDINANCE NO. 2016-0-02

              AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2, ADMINISTRATIVE CODE,
                        OF THE MID VALE MUNICIPAL CODE

       WHEREAS, the City Council desires to amend Title 2 of the Midvale Municipal Code in
regards to Tier 2 appointed employee and committee member designation under the Utah
Retirement System; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council desires to amend sections of Title 2 of the Midvale
Municipal Code to update changes previously made to the Board of Adjustments and Employee
Appeals Board; and

     WHEREAS, the City Council finds it in the City's best interest to amend Title 2,
Administrative Code, to comply with Utah State Code and provide a cohesive municipal code;
and

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah, finds it appropriate to adopt such
an ordinance in an effort to provide comprehensive information to the citizens of Midvale.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Midvale City, Utah
as follows:

      Section 1.    The City Council desires to adopt Title 2, Administrative Code of the
Midvale City Municipal Code as indicated in Exhibit 'A'.

       Section 2.      This ordinance shall be effective upon date of first publication.

       PASSED AND APPROVED this 1 St day of March, 2016.

2.16.335 Utah Retirement Systems (URS) Classification


For purposes of Utah Retirement Systems (URS) coverage, the city classifies all Tier 2 appointed employees

as full-time. Eligibility for retirement coverage under Utah Retirement Systems shall be administered in

accordance with the statutory rules governing Utah Retirement Systems.




2.24.200 Formation of Appeal Board Hearing Officer Membership Meetings.


A.    Employee Membership. Appeal Board. The appointed employee appeal board shall consist of a hearing

officer appointed by the mayor with the advice and consent of the city council.


B.    Compensation. The hearing officer may receive compensation for services.


C.    URS Coverage. For purposes of Utah Retirement Systems (URS) coverage, the city classifies a Tier 2

appointed hearing officer as part-time. Eligibility for retirement coverage under Utah Retirement Systems shall

be administered in accordance with the statutory rules governing Utah Retirement Systems.


D. Q., Terms. The term of the hearing officer shall be for a period of three calendar years.


E. D. Eligibility for Reappointment. The hearing officer shall be eligible for reappointment or reelection.


F. E. Jurisdiction. The hearing officer shall have the right and obligation to hear appeals from discharges or

disciplinary transfers of all officers and employees who are not at-will or covered under the provisions of

Section 10-3-1106 of the Utah Code Annotated, or its successor provision. (Ord. 2013-03 § 1 (part): Ord.
