1. Zone Change, 2850 Washington Blvd, Rear from CP-3 to R-3/CO (public hearing closed,
continued for discussion of conditional overlay
Planning Director Barton Brierley recommended approving the zone change with the conditional
overlay and the development agreement for the units to be divided for individual sale, which the
applicant agreed to.

COMMISSIONER SOUTHWICK MOVED TO APPROVE THE ZONE CHANGE WITH THE
CONDITIONAL OVERLAY AND DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY
COMMISSIONER SANDAU AND PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTTING

NO — COMMISSIONERS AABERG AND SHALE STATING THEY DID NOT THINK THE LOCATION WAS
