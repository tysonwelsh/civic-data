 RESOLUTION NO. R2025-10                                      Date: September 29, 2025

              A RESOLUTION CANCELING THE MAYORAL RACE
                IN THE 2025 MUNICIPAL GENERAL ELECTION

       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 20A-1-206(3) permits a municipal legislative body to
cancel a race in a local election if, for each municipal office appearing on the ballot, the
number of candidates does not exceed the number of open positions and no valid write-in
candidates have filed; and

       WHEREAS, only one candidate has declared candidacy for the office of Mayor,
and no valid write-in candidates have filed for the race; and

      WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Council finds that the mayoral race in the
2025 Municipal Election is uncontested;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Emigration Canyon Council of Utah,
in conformity with Utah Code § 20A-1-206 and no later than 20 calendar days before the
scheduled election, that the 2025 mayoral race is hereby canceled; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED and certified that the ballot for the mayoral race would
not include any contested races or ballot propositions and the candidate who qualified for
the ballot is considered elected as follows:
   •   Mayor: David Brems
