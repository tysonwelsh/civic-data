RECOMMENDATION:

Recommend approval of the Assessment-In-Lieu Agreement for the Larkin Residence


BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) 10-3-020(C) requires that any residential parcel not within a
subdivision shall construct frontage improvements and allows the owner to comply with DCMC 9-27-110(C)
where an owner may pay an assessment in lieu of public infrastructure construction.

Kenneth D. and Rebecca J. Montague have a building permit to construct a single-family residence at 12626
South 1700 East. Per the city code, the Montagues have requested to execute an assessment in lieu agreement.
The parcel is in an area where adjacent curb, gutter, and asphalt is not continuous and would create issues if
it were installed. The city staff determined it would best serve the owner and the city to proceed with an
assessment-in-lieu of construction of public infrastructure rather than having the owner construct the
improvements.

Staff recommends approval of the assessment in lieu of improvements for the parcel. Having the city
construct the frontage improvements will ensure that the seamless design and construction of the
improvements in the area and potentially save the city costs associated with modifying the city’s design by
incorporating infrastructure not presently constructed.
