Mayor and Council Informal Meeting

COUNCIL MEETING – 5:45 PM

Call to Order

Mayor Staggs called the Riverton City Council Meeting to order at 5:48 PM and conducted a roll
call. Councilmembers Buroker, McCay, McDougal, and Wells were present.

Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance

Casey Saxton offered the invocation and Chief Watkins led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Citizen Comment

Mayor Staggs called for public comments;

Nicholas Hoffman commented on the new parking ordinance in section 10.10.50. He said he
lives on Kimber Lane and cannot comply with this code because there is no other place to park
his cars during winter storms. He believes South Jordan’s winter parking code is more
reasonable and asked staff and councilmembers to look at their ordinance.

Mayor Staggs called for additional public comments; there being none, he closed the Public
Comment period.

Councilmember Wells thanked Public Works for their hard work in plowing roads and shoveling
sidewalks after the large winter storm.

Councilmember McDougal also thanked Public Works for their efforts in dealing with snow

ANDY PIERUCCI

Councilmember Wells asked Mr. Pierucci if he was a lobbyist for his company. Mr. Pierucci said
he worked in state and local government relations and public relations for an aerospace
company. He said he has interacted with elected officials in Utah and around the country and
has been grateful to learn from experienced people.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his
time as a councilmember. Mr. Pierucci said he would like to ensure that people feel like their
voice are heard.

Mayor Staggs asked what distinguished him from the other applicants. Mr. Pierucci said he has
been privileged to work with many different elected officials. He graduated in public
administration and is able to maintain relationships. He is not a rookie to the process of people
disagreeing, however he is not afraid to make conversation and listen to both sides.

Councilmember McCay asked what his opinion was on property rights and land use. Mr.
Pierucci said there needs to be consistency in this process. He would like the City to have a
long-term plan and stick to that plan. He believes when the process changes then this can
create problems. He errors on the side of personal property rights.

Councilmember Buroker asked what he would spend $100,000 of discretionary funds on. Mr.
Pierucci said he would use the money on ideas that would conserve water such as flip the strip.
He is concerned about water and appreciates the City’s work to build redundancy when it
comes to water.

JESSICA DAVIE

Councilmember Wells asked why she was interested in serving in city government. Ms. Davie
said she wanted to make an impact for her children.

Councilmember McDougal asked what she believes is the City’s greatest strength and greatest
need. Ms. Davie said Riverton City was very diversified and has strong businesses and
residential areas. She believes the greatest need is infrastructure to travel to the City’s west
side.

Mayor Staggs asked what her philosophy was on city government. Ms. Davie said she likes the
City Council’s role along with the City Manager.

Councilmember McCay asked what her opinion was on property rights and land use and what
she would do if residents were opposed to a certain application. Ms. Davie said that she would
consider each situation and decide once she knew all of the facts. She expressed concern
about the City taking away an access road and putting in a dog park.

Councilmember Buroker asked what she would spend $100,000 of discretionary funds on. Ms.
Davie responded that it would depend on where the greatest need is from year to year.

SPENCER KELLIS

Councilmember Wells asked how long Mr. Kellis had lived in District 1. Mr. Kellis said he
moved to Riverton in 2007. He believes identity as Riverton residents is an issue in District one

Councilmember Mcdougal asked what he would like to accomplish while serving. Mr. Kellis said

JEFF WALSTON

Councilmember Wells asked Mr. Walston to explain what he meant by him choosing “people
first, rather than things.” Mr. Walston explained that he considers people the top priority. He
would be more willing to use budget for another individual than for a building.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there were one thing he would like to accomplish during his
time as a councilmember. Mr. Walston said he would like to improve the quality of life for the
residents.

Mayor Staggs asked what makes him stand out. Mr. Walston said he has been in the hi-tech
industry and understands needs, training, and technology. His concern for people also makes
him stand out.

Councilmember McCay asked his opinion on property rights, land use, and affordable housing.
Mr. Walston said that the City needs to incentivize land use opportunities so that people will
come to Riverton. He does not believe the City should take property or things away from
residents.

Councilmember Buroker asked what he would spend $100,000 of discretionary funds on. Mr.
Walston said he would spend the money on something that would have a longer impact than
just one year. He would lean toward taking out the park strips and replacing them. He would
also lean toward hiring a business development person which would grow business and
increase taxes.

DANIEL BARON

Councilmember Wells asked Mr. Baron to explain what he meant with “share what he has
learned with the council.” Mr. Baron said he would share his background and history in
collaboration and personal experience. If he is given the opportunity, he would also share what
he knows about budgeting.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his

Mayor Staggs asked what makes him different from other applicants. Mr. Baron said the

JAMES ANDERSON

Councilmember Wells asked how his background suits him to serve on the City Council. Mr.
Anderson said he is an attorney. He doesn’t know how everything in the City works but he
could problem solve and resolve concerns of the residents.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his
time as a councilmember. Mr. Anderson said he would like to gain a full understanding of the
City and try to help out any way he can.

Mayor Staggs asked if he was aware of the City vision, mission statement and plan. Mr.
Anderson said he was aware that the City had a new Costco and was planning to improve the
Community Center. He believes that expansion and solving traffic issues is important.

Councilmember McCay asked what his opinion was on property rights and land use. Mr.
Anderson said it is important to balance the interest of the private owners and public use. He
believes the City needs to build more condos and apartments to bring down the price for
housing.

Councilmember Buroker asked what he would spend $100,000 of discretionary funds on. Mr.
Anderson said he would spend the money on advocating for affordable housing.

RUSSELL TERRY

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his

Mayor Staggs asked what he could contribute to the Council. Mr. Terry said he talks to the
public every day in his profession as a chiropractor. He believes he is good at empathizing,
listening, and quickly figuring out what is important to an individual.

JONATHAN GATLIN

Councilmember Wells asked if he could name one of the strategic priorities for the City. Mr.
Gatlin said he could not list any of the strategic priorities.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his
time as a councilmember. Mr. Gatlin said he would guarantee that Riverton is a safe and happy
place for citizens to live and a prosperous place for businesses to grow.

Mayor Staggs asked about his background as a statistician and how that could help the Council.
Mr. Gatlin said his ability to analyze data and digest facts could come in handy. He could
provide analysis and critical thinking for many issues.

Councilmember McCay asked what his opinion was on property rights and land use. Mr. Gatlin
said he understands both sides of the fence. He knows that each situation would be different
and he would try to understand the facts from both sides. He said he would not be afraid to
make hard decisions.

Councilmember Buroker asked what he would spend $100,000 of discretionary funds on. Mr.
Gatlin said he would most likely put funding into parks and finding ways to conserve water.


ELLIOT PERSON

Councilmember Wells asked about his philosophy on city government and specifically, his
statement that the city should “ensure quality and uninterrupted utilities.” Mr. Person explained
that his wife is from South Africa and from hearing about her experiences, he believes that the
City must do their part with what they can control to provide clean water and utilities.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his
time as a councilmember. Mr. Person said he would like to get his feet wet and get assimilated
and try to work well with the other Councilmembers. He said there is so much growth in the
area and trying to get to the west side is cumbersome. He wants to help with future road plans
and work with other cities to ease the traffic flow.

Mayor Staggs asked what was key in differentiating him from the other applicants. Mr. Person
said he grew up in the Maryland/DC area and has been to many different places. He has a
diverse background and can draw on a lot of different experiences and communities that he has
lived in and loved. He has been in Riverton for just over 4 years. He would like to be in
Riverton a long time and believes he will bring a lot of passion to this community.

Councilmember McCay asked what his opinion was on property rights and land use. Mr.
Person said the Council must look at the overall good of the City and make some sacrifices for

RICH WILLS

Councilmember Wells asked what he would bring to the Council, given his background. Mr.
Wills said he is a public education teacher and understands the public workforce. He also
worked in the parks department for Riverton City during the summer. He wants to be an
empowering voice for residents in his district.

Councilmember McDougal asked if there was one thing he would like to accomplish during his
time as a councilmember. Mr. Wills said he wants to help with growth and traffic because all of
the growth has already impacted residents in his district.

Mayor Staggs asked if he was familiar with any of the strategic priorities and goals. Mr. Wills
said he was not aware of the strategic priorities. He wants to focus on the concept of
community outreach. He would like to build more opportunities for the community to come
together.

Councilmember McCay asked what his opinion was on property rights and land use. Mr. Wills
said he would lean more toward personal property rights but would base his decisions on case
by case situations. He recognizes that what works for one person may take away from others
and he would weigh the negative and positive.

Councilmember Buroker asked what he would spend $100,000 of discretionary funds on. Mr.
Wills said he would lean toward funding for the parks and art council. He teaches fine arts in
junior high and believes the arts are a way to express ourselves. Because he worked for the
parks department, he knows how much work goes into making these areas places where
people want to visit.

Discussion/Action Items

         Final Selection of District 1 Councilmember

Councilmembers returned from Closed Session and were given ballots for a secret ballot
election.

Jamie Larsen, City Recorder, collected the first round of votes and votes were as follows:
Councilmember Buroker-Elliott Person, Councilmember McCay-Andy Pierucci, Councilmember
McDougal-Elliott Person, and Councilmember Well-Andy Pierucci. Mayor Staggs cast a vote
but his vote was not counted in the first round.

Votes were verified by Ms. Larsen and David Brickey, City Manager, both agreed there was a
tie vote, two votes for Elliott Person and two votes for Andy Pierucci, and both agreed that a
second round of voting would be required.

Mayor Staggs asked the Council if they would like to ask the two applicants additional
questions. Councilmembers Buroker and McCay asked a follow up question and both
applicants were able to speak to the Council before the second round of voting.

Councilmembers were asked to vote again in a second round. Ms. Larsen collected the second
round of votes and the votes were as follows: Councilmember Buroker-Elliott Person,
Councilmember McCay-Andy Pierucci, Councilmember McDougal-Elliott Person, and
Councilmember Well-Andy Pierucci.

Votes were verified by Ms. Larsen, Mr. Brickey, and Ryan Carter, City Attorney, all agreed there
was a tie vote again, two votes for Elliott Person and two votes for Andy Pierucci.

Pursuant to Utah Code, Mayor Staggs was asked to break the tie vote. Mayor Staggs voted for
Andy Pierucci.

Andy Pierucci was appointed to serve as the District 1 Councilmember through the end of 2023.
