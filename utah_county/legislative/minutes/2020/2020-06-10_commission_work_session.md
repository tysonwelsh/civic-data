            REQUIRES THAT A REPORT BE MADE TO THE COUNTY COMMISSION TWICE A YEAR ON THE
            CRIME CATEGORIES, TYPES OF SERVICES PROVIDED AND PROGRAM ACCOMPLISHMENTS.

            2.WORK SESSION TO DISCUSS APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO
            SIGN AN APPLICATION FOR LAND FOR RECREATION AND PUBLIC PURPOSES WITH THE
            UNITED STATES BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT FOR THE WEST MOUNTAIN ATV TRACK
            AND SHOOTING RANGE.
            -Richard N

            RICHARD NIELSON, DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC WORKS – THIS PROJECT STARTED OVER 12 YEARS
            AGO. HAS GONE THROUGH VARIOUS STAGES OF THE PROCESS. ABOUT 2 YEARS AGO THERE
            WAS A CHANGE IN THE LOCAL LEADERSHIP OF BLM AND WE BEGAN TO RECEIVE MORE
            FAVORABLE RESPONSE FROM THE BLM. THEY REACHED OUT AND SAID THEY WOULD LIKE
            TO WORK ON THIS AGAIN.

            THIS AREA IS LOCATED ON THE SOUTH END OF WEST MOUNTAIN JUST ABOVE THE PAYSON
            LANDFILL. IT’S AN AREA THAT IS FREQUENTED BY SHOOTERS AND ATV AND MOTORCYCLE
            RIDERS, OFTEN AT THE SAME TIME AND IN THE SAME LOCATION WHICH CAN CAUSE
            SIGNIFICANT PROBLEMS. IN WORKING WITH THE BLM, THERE ARE A COUPLE OF ITEMS
            THAT SHOULD BE NOTED:
               • ABOUT 2/3 OF THE PROPERTY WHICH IS APPROXIMATELY 620 ACRES WOULD BE
                  LEASED THROUGH THE RP&P PROCESS BECAUSE OF UNDERLYING MINING CLAIMS.
               • ABOUT 400 ACRES WOULD HAVE A PATENT ISSUED FROM THE BLM TO THE COUNTY.
                  THIS IS THE NORTHERN SECTION OF THE PROJECT. THIS IS WHERE THE SHOOTING
                  LOCATION WOULD BE.

            WHAT THIS PROJECT WOULD PROVIDE:
              • BUILD A SHOOTING RANGE SIMILAR TO WHAT WE CURRENTLY HAVE ON LAKE
                 MOUNTAIN THAT WOULD HAVE ORGANIZED SHOOTING LANES, BENCHES, TARGETS
                 WITH BERMS AROUND IT TO BE A PROTECTED AREA.
              • ATV/MOTOCROSS TRACK AND TRAIL AREA THAT COULD BE USED FOR
                 COMPETITIONS FOR MOTOCROSS EVENTS AND ALSO A TRAINING AREA, OPEN RIDE
                 AREA ON DESIGNATED TRAILS THAT WOULD BE SEPARATE FROM THE SHOOTING
                 RANGE.

            MAY NEED TO ‘TWEEK’ A BIT BECAUSE THERE IS A WATER TANK AND WELL AREA THAT
            WAS DESIGNATED IN THIS APPLICATION AND IT MAY NEED TO BE LISTED IN A SEPARATE
            APPLICATION SPECIFICALLY FROM PAYSON CITY BECAUSE IT WILL BE A PAYSON CITY
            FACILITY WHEN IT IS COMPLETED.

            SARA BENNETT, EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF TRAILS UTAH – GAVE A LITTLE BACKGROUND OF
            THEIR ORGANIZATION.

            THEY ARE WORKING A LOT WITH PUBLIC LAND MANAGERS, COUNTY GOVERNMENT AND
            MUNICIPALITIES, TO CREATE PARTNERSHIPS AND LEVERAGE MONIES. WORK WITH TRACK
            FUNDS, STATE PARKS AND RTP FUNDS TO RAISE MONEY TO BUILD TRAILS THAT ARE
            ENVIRONMENTALLY SUSTAINABLE AND ARE ENJOYABLE TO USE.

            HAVE BEEN INVOLVED IN A LOT OF PLANNING PROCESSES OVER THE YEARS INCLUDING
            BACK IN 2016, THE MOUNTAIN ACCORD PROCESS IN SALT LAKE COUNTY – THAT WAS
            SPEARHEADED BY THE SALT LAKE CITY MAYOR AT THE TIME. IT ENDED UP GETTING
            FEDERAL FUNDING OF APPROXIMATELY $500,000. THAT WAS A VERY THOROUGH PROCESS –
            3 YEARS OF MEETINGS WITH 4 DIFFERENT ADVISORY GROUPS (RECREATION, ECONOMY,
            ENVIRONMENT AND TRANSPORTATION). THE THING THAT REALLY PUSHED THAT EFFORT
            FORWARD WAS CONFLICT ABOUT TRANSPORTATION IN CENTRAL WASATCH COUNTY IN
            THE HEAVILY VISITED MILLCREEK CANYONS. THAT GOT THE PROJECT ROLLING AND
            RECREATION WAS A BIG PART OF IT. FROM THE VERY ONSET THERE WAS A CONCERN FOR
            THE BONNEVILLE SHORELINE TRAIL WHICH IS ARGUABLY THE MOST IMPORTANT
            REGIONAL TRAIL IN NORTHERN UTAH. THERE’S ABOUT 75 MILES OF TRAIL THAT’S BEEN
            IDENTIFIED WITH FUTURE ALIGNMENTS AND EXISTING ALIGNMENTS HERE IN UTAH
            COUNTY. THIS TRAIL WILL SOMEDAY APPROACH SEVERAL HUNDRED MILES ALL THE WAY
            FROM THE BOARDER WITH IDAHO DOWN PAST NEBO. THERE’S MUNICIPALITIES AND
            COUNTIES ALL ALONG THE WASATCH FRONT THAT ARE USING THE BONNEVILLE
            SHORELINE TRAIL CONCEPT AS THE CENTRAL ORGANIZING FEATURE OF THEIR OPEN
            SPACE AND TRAILS PROGRAMS. VERY IMPORTANT OUTDOOR RECREATION CONCEPT.
