CC:  Date: March 10, 2022
Re: Boondocks Addition Site Plan Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan Amendment for approximately 8.5 acres located east of Minuteman Drive and North of Southfork Drive, at approximately 75 E. Southfork Drive. The property is currently zoned CR. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan Amendment, together with deviation requests from building design standards, be approved allowing for the expansion of the existing building in order to add a bowling alley, outdoor climbing structure, and to make related adjustments to the parking layout and landscaping on the site. The findings are as follows: Deviation Request from DCMC Subsections 9-22-040(C) –Façade Articulation
For approval:
1. The variations are consistent with the purpose of DCMC Chapter 9-22 and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
2. The project is required to create a consistent and compatible design involving an addition to an existing building.
3. The building design is a specific architectural style which utilizes facade materials differently than prescribed in DCMC subsection 9-22-040(F) and is designed by a licensed architect.
For denial:
1. The variations are not consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
2. The project is not required to create a consistent and compatible design involving the addition to the existing building.
3. The building design is not a specific architectural style which utilizes facade materials differently than prescribed in DCMC subsection 9-22-040(F).

Site Plan
For approval:
1. The entire existing site is developed and the amendment represents redevelopment of a portion of the site.
2. The site plan amendment will conform to applicable standards, including but not limited to building height, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3. The proposed site plan meets requirements and provisions of Draper City Municipal Code, including Section 9-5-090(E).
4. The site can accommodate any change in the number of people on the site and any change in impact on surrounding infrastructure.
For denial:
1. The proposed site plan does not meet the requirements and provisions of Draper City Municipal Code, including Section 9-5-090(E).
