MEMO
To: City Council
From: Todd Taylor
Date: 2025-03-04
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1635


Comments:

This application is a request for adoption of the Draper Town Center Station Area Plan as
an additional component of the Draper City General Plan.

Findings for approval:
  1. A Public Hearing before the Planning Commission was held in accordance with Utah
     State Code Section 10-9a-404.
  2. The General Plan Amendment is consistent with the requirements of Utah State
     Code and is appropriate.

Finding for denial:
  1. The General Plan Amendment is not consistent with the requirements of Utah State
     Code and is not appropriate.

The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their February 27, 2025 meeting. They
forwarded a positive recommendation on a vote of 3-0 and noted their concern about the
density and the mix of densities between the City site and the UTA site.
