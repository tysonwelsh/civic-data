MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper, AICP
Date: 2026-05-28
Re: Public Hearing: City Initiated Draper-Highland Boundary Adjustment #2 Land Use Map
and Zoning Map Amendment Requests (Legislative Items).


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of Land Use Map Amendment and Zoning Map
Amendments for approximately 0.31 acres located on the southeastern boundary of the
City at approximately 16377 S. Suncrest Drive. The property was recently incorporated
within the Draper City boundaries and the intent of the application is to officially assign
Land Use and Zoning Designations for the property.

Findings for Approval:
  1. The proposed amendments are consistent with goals, objectives and policies of
the city's general plan.
  2. The proposed amendments are harmonious with the overall character of existing
development in the vicinity of the subject property.
  3. The proposed amendments will not affect the standards of any applicable overlay
zone.
  4. The proposed amendments will not adversely affect adjacent property.
  5. There will be adequate facilities and services to serve the subject property within
Highland City.
  6. The proposed Land Use Map amendments comply with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
