SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
PUD-2-2022  Petition by CAMERON SCOTT, representing David Weekly Homes, is
            requesting preliminary approval for the Westview Townhomes. The proposed
            project consists of 64 units on 5.5 acres. The subdivision is located at 2876
            South 5655 West and is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff
            – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Steve Lehman explained that this proposal is for a new townhome development
                directly adjacent to the 5600 Hotel. He stated that Glen Eagle Drive, to the
                south of the subject property, will continue to the north connecting to the street
                just north of the subject property. Steve said that the Stub road on Balmoral
                will connect to the subdivision. He noted that the site plan is showing a gate at
                the access to the project. Steve explained that there has been talk of gating the
                project, however, they have yet to decide. Steve explained that the development
                agreement was approved in 2020. As part of that agreement the applicant was
              given a 5% reduction in landscaping and a 4.2 stall/unit parking ratio. Steve
              stated that the current parking ratio is 4.7 units/acre, however, when the
              development agreement was approved the average town home parking ratio was
              between 3.5 – 3.8 units per acer. He mentioned that he discussed the parking
              with the Legal Department and Steve Pastorik, CD Director, and the applicant
              will need to meet the parking ratio outlined in the development agreement.
              Steve presented the building elevations and said that a few enhancements need
              to be made, but overall staff approves of what is being proposed. The applicant
              is required to provide four amenities and are proposing five. Steve pointed that
              the applicant is wanting to acquire some property just south of the subject
              location that is owned by UDOT. He stated that nothing has been decided at
              this point.

              Commissioner Wood asked if these units would include basements. Steve said
              that the water table is too high and would not permit basements.


PUD-3-2022    Petition by ROBBIE THOMPSON, representing the Sonrise Baptist Church, is
              requesting preliminary approval for the Sonrise Townhomes. The proposed
              project consists of 33 units and 2 parcels on 5 acres. The proposed
              development is located at 3550 South 4400 West and is zoned RM (Multiple
              Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311or
              steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:   Steve Lehman explained that there is an existing church on the subject property.
              He stated that the applicant proposes three, four, and six-unit buildings with
              basements. Steve noted that the applicant would build to meet the multi-family
              design standards per the development agreement. The existing parking for the
              church will be moved to the west, and they will also have parking to the south.

              Steve said that the applicant is willing to add a pavilion with outside seating. He
              suggested they add a waking path and would like feedback from the
              Commission regarding the pathway. He explained that the elevations had not
              been reviewed per city ordinance yet; however, it appears more stone will need
              to be added. The color of the building is still being discussed. The site plan is
              showing white without any additional colors.

              Commissioner Fuller asked if the open space was sufficient for the proposed
              project. Steve pointed out that they are offering a patio space fronting each unit.
              However, the open space is relatively low. Commissioner Fuller asked if the
              parking lot between the church and the development would be considered open
              space. Steve said it would not be factored into the percentage.

              Commissioner Winters said that the shared green space between the subject
              property and the church makes it feel like they are not lacking any open area.
              Steve noted that the Commission could view that as an offset for the open space.

              Commissioner Wood asked if they would be adding a sidewalk. Steve stated
              that the site plan does not show a sidewalk along the south side, so they will
              need to adjust the plan to add that sidewalk. He noted that the parking ratio
              would be 4.75 stalls/unit.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-8-2022  Petition by SANSONE GROUP requesting conditional use approval for a
          warehouse building. The property is located at 6048 W Beagley Rd on 16.54
          acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-
          3361or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson stated that this request for a warehouse building. He explained
              that 5900 West and Beagley Road are in the process of construction, and the
              subject property would have access to both. The west side of the building will
              have bay doors and truck parking. Brock said that the access to the north is still
              being discussed. It is possible that the property owner to the north will be
              putting in a road that they would use as an access, or they may need to change
              its location. Brock noted that the applicant is requesting a landscaping exception
              due to the canal running through the property. He said If the exception is
              granted, the staff suggests a 10’ wide formal landscaped area next to the Lee
              Creek Channel that includes shrubs 3’ in height, spaced closely enough to grow
              to create a hedge row for screening. Also, one street tree shall be provided every
              30’ along the frontage. Brock stated that the applicant proposes one large
              structure; however, they could divide the building into four different units. The
              proposed building for phase II should be similar to that of phase I.


C-9-2022       Petition by BRAD JONES requesting conditional use approval for outside
               storage. The property is located at 2181 S 7200 W on 2.0 acres and is zoned M
               (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
               brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson stated that this request is for outside storage. He explained that
              the applicant sells and recycles landscape products.

              Brock said that the applicant is proposing a garage and an office building. He
              would need to work with the Building department to ensure all requirements are
              met.

              Brock noted that the applicant had mentioned opening a plant shop. If that is
              proposed, more parking stalls would be required. The Public Works Department
              is reviewing the plans now. Brock feels adding slats to the existing chain link
              fence along the front of the building would be the easiest way to attain proper
                screening.
