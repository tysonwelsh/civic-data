BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.43 acres located on
the northeast corner of 800 East and Pioneer Road, at approximately 12391 South 800 East (Exhibit B).
The property is currently zoned TC (Town Center). The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be
approved to allow for two parcels to be combined into one.

The site includes two properties. These properties are part of the Draperville Plat and more specifically
Lot 4 within Block 12. The subject property encompasses a portion of Lot 4, which was divided at some
point prior to City Incorporation in 1978. This plat dates back to 1878. Until recently, the properties
contained residential uses. The property owner demolished the homes earlier this year in anticipation of
commercial development.

      1. 28-29-310-006- 0.2 acres, contained one house built in 1931
      2. 28-29-310-005- 0.234 acres, contained a duplex built in 1928

The applicant is proposing to develop the property as the Draper Cove Phase 2. This commercial and
office development received Site Plan approval from the Planning Commission on July 9, 2020 (Exhibit
G). The Planning Commission included a condition of approval that the properties be consolidated into
one since the building will cross over the property line.

ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Town Center land
use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the TC zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to Draper City
Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the TC zone is to “provide a method for
implementing special provisions found within the general plan and the Draper City conservation area
master plan regarding the establishment and promotion of a historic core area of the community
characterized by stringent design standards for buildings, public spaces, site design and landscaping with
a harmonious mix of commercial uses, limited high density residential uses and civic area.” The TC
zoning abuts the subject property on the north, south, east, and west. The RM2 (Multi-family
Residential, up to 12 dwelling units per acre) zone also abuts the property on the west.

Plat Amendment. The Plat Amendment will eliminate the shared property line between the two subject
properties and combine them into one 0.43 acre lot. No changes to the exterior boundary lines of the lots
are proposed. Exhibit E of this report shows the proposed plat. The resulting lot will comply with DCMC
and will not result in the creation of any non-conformities.
