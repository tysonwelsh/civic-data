A slide showed the evaluation of the economic and fiscal impacts.  Graphics were then 
shown that demonstrated what is meant by economic impacts.  There are three impact 
types.  They are direct, indirect, and induced impacts.     
 
Mr. Prosser began with the economic analysis.  It was explained that direct impacts are 
the estimates of the value of the activity that would occur on a specific project site.  The 
rental of hotel rooms is an example.  Indirect impacts are the value of economic 
activities that occur between businesses.  An example would be an office within the 
project buying paper and ink from an office supply store.  Induced impacts are the 
impacts generated by the site’s workers.  This is when they buy gas or go to lunch.  He 
has looked at what the impacts will be for Summit County in all three categories.  A 
fiscal impact analysis was also done.  This analysis includes the taxes generated from 
the sales of property.   
 
Mr. Prosser said they have summarized the demographics of the area.  The number of 
residents and the probable income was calculated.  These figures help them with 
determining the fiscal analysis.  He said the project has 1,100 housing units.  This will 
generate approximately 2,000 to 2,700 new residents.  The average income will be 
approximately $80,000.  To reach this determination, they looked at the housing units 
and the number of square feet.  The salaries have been based on the affordable housing.   
They also figured what an average household would have to earn to afford an average 
market-rate house.  The non-residential uses will generate about 1,032 jobs with an 
annual average wage of ~$56,000.  These figures are based on the local economy.   
 
Mr. Prosser said they have estimated the proposed project will generate over $5 
million.  It is estimated the Tech Center would have generated over $2 million.  The 
property tax for both developments would be approximately the same.  The sales tax 
would be the biggest difference.  Households will spend more money at the surrounding 
retailers than someone who commutes.  Other ways that additional revenue is created 
was listed, such as the hotel.

Mr. Prosser said they use a model called implant to calculate the total economic activity 
and benefits.  There were two things measured.  There will be a one-time economic

the number of jobs over the time the project is being built.  There won't be 1,990 jobs at

It is estimated this project will bring $358 million economic benefits to the area, with an
