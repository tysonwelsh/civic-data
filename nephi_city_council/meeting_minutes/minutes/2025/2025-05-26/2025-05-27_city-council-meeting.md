                        NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                   May 27, 2025
                                     7:00 P.M
                                    21 E 100 N
                                  Nephi, UT 84648




1. Derrik Jacobson purchased a home on 590 S 400 W. That home has city utilities
   although it is in the county. He was planning to build a new home on his property and
   wondered if he could still connect to city utilities. The council told him the only way
   would be to annex the property into the city. He had spoken with surrounding residents
   to see if they would be interested in annexing. He is concerned about growth in the
   area if he annexes. He was told they could zone it R1-1/2 for larger lot sizes.


2. Brent Bluth introduced a new housing product aimed at addressing the housing crisis
   without government subsidies. He explained the flex home concept, designed to be
   affordable and expandable starting with a base unit of 1200 ft2 with options to expand
   to 1800 ft2. He outlined the innovative design and engineering approaches used to
   reduce costs and make homes affordable.

3. Terry Messersmith was requesting a zone change for his Northridge Subdivision from R1-
   1/4 to R1-8. When the subdivision was proposed, it was assumed by the City Council
   that as the subdivision progressed eastward, the lots would get larger. Terry was now
   wanting them the same as the previous plat. Seth Atkinson proposed a possible trade of
   the 15 foot easement to be used for utility access for pathways within the subdivision to
   the sewer manholes. Mayor Seely and the council express their previous plans of using
   the original 15 ft easement for a trail system. They wanted to think about it and table
   the decision for now.

4. Mr. Atkinson described one of the outcome priorities from the City Council in the
   current biennium is to institute a storm water utility. By doing so, the city can create an
   equitable method for distributing storm water throughout the community, to maintain
   current storm water infrastructure, and construct new facilities to convey storm water.
   The Staff recommended a phased approach to the utility. The initial fee would be
   enough to raise funds for maintenance of the current storm. Then, over the next several
   budget cycles, institute increases to the storm water fee to begin saving for larger
       capital needs. An initial fee of approximately $3.00 to $6.00 per month would begin to
       cover most or all of the maintenance needs of the storm water system.


   5. Seth Atkinson presented fee updates for the cemetery and water service irrigation
      callouts. Fees for cemetery burial rights would increase slightly for residents, but
      substantially for non-residents. The fees consider non-residents who are utility
      customers to not pay the full non-resident fee.

   6. Administrative and Council Updates
         a. Mr. Atkinson mentioned it is a state requirement to provide coverage for public
            safety and firefighters’ surviving spouses and suggested approving a policy for
            annual participation and if wanted the mayor can sign the participation policy
            next council meeting. The council agreed.
         b. Mr. Atkinson said the golf course only has one more closing date.
         c. Councilor Shari Cowan announced Miss Nephi would be this Saturday, May 31 at
            7:00.
         d. Mayor Seely stated that each year Andrea Holm tries to get a sweeper for the
            Stampede, but it is always a struggle. Last year they resorted to having
            recreation employees go down main street and do the cleanup. He mentioned if
            the sweeper falls through again having the employees do the cleanup again.
         e. The Mayor and Councilor Parady worried about the road construction on Main
            Street interfering with the Stampede.
         f. Convened to Closed Meeting for character and competence of an individual and
            real property. 8:30 pm.
         g. Meeting adjourned 9:56pm
