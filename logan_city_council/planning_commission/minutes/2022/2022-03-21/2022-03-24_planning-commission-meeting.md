                                   PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
                                              Meeting of March 24, 2022
                             Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session
Thursday, March 24, 2022, Commissioner Lucero, called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Jessica Lucero, Roylan Croshaw, Jordy Guth, Ken Heare, David Lewis,
Eldon Peterson, Sara Doutre

Commissioners Excused:

Staff Present: Russ Holley, Mike DeSimone, Mohammed Abdullahi, Tom Dickinson, Jeannie
Simmonds (City Council)

Commissioner Peterson moved to approve the minutes from the March 10, 2022 meeting as
submitted. Motion seconded by Commissioner Guth. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING

 PC 22-016 BSR Roofing Warehouse [Design Review Permit] Jace Skabelund/BSR Property
 Holding LLC, authorized agent/owner, request a design review permit to construct a new 6,000
 SF warehouse on a vacant .5-acre lot, located at 1097 West 200 South in the Commercial
 Services (CS) zone; TIN 05-107-0012. (Ellis Neighborhood)


STAFF: R. Holley review the request for a Design Review Permit to construct a new 6000 SF steel
building located at 1097 West 200 South on a vacant half-acre lot. The building will be used for a
roofing warehouse and office space. The proposed building mirrors the existing building to the south
that the applicant also owns. There is new access proposed onto 1080 West and a cross-easement
between the two properties is required in condition number three. The east façade does not meet
the 30% transparency required in this zone. As proposed, it is at 5%, the commission can use its
discretion to allow an adjustment.


PROPONENT: Courtney Wallace, architect on this project said that the owner would like the new
building to mirror the existing building. He mentioned an alternative of adding an 8-foot tall trellis to
the east side to help break up that façade. He spoke about the landscaping on the north and east
sides being open space. Commissioner Peterson asked about the black chain link fence on the
west side of the existing building and if they would be mirroring that on the east side of the new
building. Mr. Wallace said they do not intend to put up a fence on the east side of the new
building.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Guth mentioned setting a precedence by allowing an adjustment with
transparency. Commissioner Doutre spoke about the transparency of the surrounding buildings.
Commissioner Heare said that he is not inclined to approve the adjustment to mirror the existing


building, there is latitude, but 25% is a lot. He would rather leave the condition to meet the 30% or
have the applicant return with an alternative. Commissioner Lewis and Commissioner Guth agreed.

MOTION: Commissioner Heare moved to conditionally approve a Design Review Permit for PC
22-016 with the conditions of approval and findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner
Lewis seconded the motion.

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Design Review and are available
    in the Community Development Department.
2. The east façade shall have 30% transparency.
3. A cross easement shall be recorded on this property and the property to the west that
    accommodates this design layout and cross-vehicular circulation.
4. A minimum of 9 parking stalls and a bike rack shall be installed on-site.
5. A performance landscaping plan, prepared in accordance with §17.39 of the LDC, shall be
    submitted for approval to the Community Development Department prior to the issuance of
    the building permit. The plan shall include the following:
          a. Street trees required along adjacent streets every 30’.
          b. Open Space and Useable Outdoor areas shall total a min. of 20% (4,181 SF). Storm
               Water ponds without any landscaping do not count as open space.
          c. A total number 9 trees and 24 shrubs, perennials and grasses shall be provided.
          d. The landscaping areas between the parking lot and 200 South shall comply with
               type “B” standards (LDC 17.39.070).
6. Any dumpsters near the street shall be visually screened or buffered with landscaping and/or
    fencing.
7. Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast and reviewed and approved prior to
    the issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.
8. No signs are approved with this Design Review Permit. All signage shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.
9. No fences are approved with this Design Review Permit. All fences shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.
10. Prior to issuance of a Building Permit, the Director of Community Development shall receive a
    written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that their
    requirements have been satisfied:
      a. Water - contact 716-9622
            • The buildings water main needs to have a RP (ASSE1013) installed and tested on the
                 water main after the water meter, as it enters the building before any branch offs or
                 possible connections inside or outside of building. Properly sized drain required to serve
                 the backflow assemblies dump port.

            •   All landscape irrigation system’s fed from Logan City water must have a high hazard
                rated backflow assembly installed and tested. All backflow assemblies must be tested
                within 10 days of turning in water to them and annually thereafter.
            •   Fire suppression systems that are connected to Logan City water (with no added
                chemicals) must have a minimum DC (ASSE1015) installed and tested. Fire risers and
                B/F assemblies must be installed as per Logan City standards.
            •   All points of use of water must comply with the 2018 IPC and State of Utah Amendments
                and the Utah Admin Code 309-305 during and after construction. (Cooling, heating,
                processing, treating, chemicals and irrigating etc.) Project shall comply with all current
                plumbing codes, Utah State Amendments, Utah Division of Drinking Water rules and


              regulations including, but not limited to, those pertaining to backflow protection and cross
              connection prevention
     b. Engineering - contact 716-9153
          • Provide water shares or in-lieu fee for all increased demand to City system in
              accordance with City Code and Utah Administrative Rule R309-510-7.
          • Westfield Subdivision was designed as a commercial site with a private Lift Station that
              originally included Amber Fields Subdivision, since then the owners of the lift station
              have allowed the Mohr Commercial Service Park Subdivision to connect to this system.
              This lift station is at capacity and is undersized for this rezone and development density.
              Provide City with sewer flows and lift station upgrades to meet all existing and planned
              flows as part of permit design review. No occupancies will be granted until these
              upgrades have been constructed.
          • The original subdivision was design for storm water detention as required when
              approved. Since then the City storm water standards have changed, development shall
              provide storm water detention/retention for any addition requirements for storm water
              storage to meet new standards. This shall also include the onsite retention of the 90%
              storm through the use of Low Impact Design methods.
          • The stormwater pond is proposed as being shared with the property to the West.
              Provide a written agreement that will be recorded on both properties. This shall be
              signed by both parties and describe the maintenance responsibility of each property
              owner.
     c. Fire - contact 716-9515
          • If the Warehouse is not provided with fire sprinklers; storage is not allow higher than 12
              feet for common commodities and 6 feet for high hazards such as plastics, tires.
     d. Environmental - contact 716-9761
          • Garbage and recycling containers must meet size and placement requirements.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. The conditioned project is compatible with surrounding land uses and will not interfere with the
   use and enjoyment of adjacent properties because of the building design, materials,
   landscaping, and setbacks.
2. The Design Review Permit conforms to the requirements of the CS zone and Title 17 of the
   Logan Municipal Code.
3. The conditioned project provides adequate open space and useable outdoor space in
   conformance with Title 17.
4. The conditioned project provides adequate off-street parking.
5. The project met the minimum public noticing requirements of the Land Development Code and
   the Municipal Code.
6. 200 South street provides access and are adequate in size and design to sufficiently handle
   automobile traffic related to the land use.

Moved: D. Lewis                  Seconded: E. Peterson                        Approved: 7-0

Yea: Lucero, Croshaw, Guth, Lewis, Heare, Doutre, Peterson                   Nay:             Abstain:


 PC 22-017 Reeder Warehouse [Design Review Permit] Courtney Wallace/Mark Reeder Holding
 LLC, authorized agent/owner, request a design review permit to build a new 12,000 SF
 warehouse on a vacant .74-acre lot, located at 815 West 1455 North in the Industrial Park (IP)
 zone; TIN 04-193-0001. (Bridger Neighborhood)


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Design Review Permit to construct a new 12,000 SF
warehouse that will be divided into 2 spaces to lease. This project is on a .75-acre lot located at 815
West 1455 North. The fenestration on the west facade is at 29%, which is 1% less than the required
30%. The commission will need to allow that adjustment. Commissioner Lewis asked if this project
required bike parking. R. Holley said that bike stalls are not required for industrial or manufacturing
uses per the Land Development Code.

PROPONENT: Courtney Wallace, the architect for this project said that he could add a small
window to the emergency exit door on the east side but asked the commission to allow the
adjustment in fenestration. Mr. Wallace said that they did not include a 3-foot planting strip along
the south façade because of the 4 entry doors and 2 garage doors on that side.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: There was discussion about requiring a planting strip between the doors on the south
side and how the sidewalk would then have to jog out into the parking lot around those planning
areas. This jog would then cause the parking lot to shift to the south and take away from the
landscaping along 1455 North. The condition requiring landscaping along the south façade of the
building was removed. The Commissioners discussed allowing the adjustment in the fenestration.
Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director said that the commission needs to look at the
nature and function of the building and allow adjustments based on their determination of the design
and if it is meeting the intent and spirit of the standard.

MOTION: Commissioner Croshaw moved to conditionally approve a Design Review Permit for PC
22-017 with the amended conditions of approval and findings for approval as listed below.
Commissioner Doutre seconded the motion.

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Design Review and are available
   in the Community Development Department.
2. Pedestrian walkways shall be constructed to connect the main pedestrian entrances on the
   south facade and the 800 West sidewalk.
3. Planning commission permits 29% fenestration on the east façade of the building.
4. A performance landscaping plan, prepared in accordance with §17.32 of the LDC, shall be
   submitted for approval to the Community Development Department prior to the issuance of

          a. Minimum of 6,446 SF of both open space and usable outdoor space shall be

          b. A total number 15 trees and 37 shrubs, perennials and grasses shall be provided.

       a. Water - contact 716-9627

        g. Light and Power - contact 716-9722
             • Requires 1-Line Diagram, A Logan City Load Data Sheet, A digital site plan in Auto

             • Contact Matt for power location and a meeting on site.

 PC 22-018 WM Fuel Logan, UT #4272 [Design Review Permit] Kimley Horn Associates/Wal-
 Mart Stores Inc., authorized agent/owner, request a design review permit to build a new fuel
 station that includes 6 fuel pumps, canopy, and kiosk. There will also be a new entrance along
 Highway 89. This project is located at 1150 South 100 West in the Southwest corner of the
 Walmart parking lot in the Commercial (COM) zone; TIN 02-086-0027. (Woodruff
 Neighborhood)


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Design Review Permit for a new fuel station that
includes a 440 SF kiosk, and 6 fuel pumps with an eighteen-foot tall canopy overhead. The project
site is located on the Southwest corner of the existing Walmart parking lot. The proposed new
entrance from HWY 89/91 at the Northwest corner of the property will have to be approved by UDOT.
The proposed project does not alter the existing pedestrian connection and is not visible from the
street, so fenestration does not apply. Commissioner Doutre said that there does not seem to be
enough room for car stacking to the north.

PROPONENT: Jacob Glaze with Kimley Horn Associates said that they chose to go with 6
pumps instead of 8 based on a market study that was conducted for this location. They will be
working with the Logan City Engineering department on the turning radius. Commissioner
Peterson asked if the station would be open 24 hours. Mr. Glaze said that generally, their fuel
stations operate the same hours as Walmart.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare asked if the project would come back to the commission if
UDOT approved the entrance on HWY 89. R. Holley said that Walmart has met with UDOT and the
answer they received on the entrance request was no. There is always a chance the decision could
be appealed. Mr. Glaze said they proposed the entrance for convenience purposes. He said that
it is a safe bet that Walmart will not be pursuing the entrance after receiving the denial letter from
UDOT. Commissioner Doutre asked if they considered orienting the fuel station to have the pump
lanes run east and west. Mr. Glaze said they did look at turning the pumps, but the fuel truck would
not have access. They would have had to move the sidewalk and it would have caused issues with
the existing parking lot. He also said that if there are any issues with car stacking in the future they
will be addressed by Walmart. Commissioner Lucero asked if there are any stacking requirements
for gas stations. R. Holley said there is no stacking standard for gas stations in the Land
Development Code.

22-018 with the amended conditions of approval and findings for approval as listed below.
Commissioner Guth seconded the motion.

    for either the overall site or just the 0.65-acre gas station location.
4. The sidewalk connecting the building to 100 West shall not be blocked or altered for this
    project.
5. The building and canopy design are approved as submitted.
6. A minimum of 352 parking stalls shall be provided on-site
7. No new open storm-water detention basins shall be placed in the yard between the building
    and the adjoining streets.
8. All dumpsters shall be visually screened or buffered from public streets by using fencing,
    walls, and landscaping.
9. Rooftop mechanical and/or building wall mechanical equipment shall be placed out of view
    from the street or screen from view from the street.
10. Exterior lighting, including gas canopy lighting, shall be concealed source, down-cast and
    shall not illuminate or cast light onto adjacent properties.
11. Standard streetscape improvements will be required along Main Street with driveway access

12. No signs are approved with this Design Review Permit. All signage shall be approved and

13. No fences are approved with this Design Review Permit. All fences shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

      a. Environmental - contact 716-9760
