                         MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                        MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY MEETING

                             Wednesday, June 24, 2020 - 5:30 pm
                                 Approved May 12, 2021

      This meeting was held electronically via Zoom and streamed on West Jordan’s YouTube channel
                Base location at City Hall 8000 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088


BOARD:         Chris McConnehey, Chair; Board Members Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, Chad
               Lamb, David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, and Melissa Worthen

STAFF:         Interim Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Community Engagement
               Director Tauni Barker, Mayor Dirk Burton, Senior Engineer Todd Johnson,
               Development Services Director Scott Langford, Chief Administrative Officer
               Korban Lee, Fire Chief Derek Maxfield, IT Administrative Assistant Rachel
               MacKay, Assistant City Attorney Duncan Murray, Capital Projects Manager
               David Murphy, Parks Division Manager Dave Naylor, IT Manager Michael
               Oliver, City Recorder Cindy Quick, Finance Director Danyce Steck, City
               Attorney Robert Wall, Police Chief Ken Wallentine


1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair McConnehey called the Municipal Building Authority (MBA) meeting to order at 5:39
p.m.

2. BUSINESS ITEMS
   a. Consider approving the minutes of May 27, 2020
The minutes of the May 27, 2020 MBA meeting were reviewed.

MOTION:        Board Member Whitelock moved to approve the minutes of the May 27, 2020
               MBA meeting. Chair McConnehey seconded the motion, which passed by
               unanimous vote (7-0).

   b. Consider approving Resolution No. 104 adopting the Final Budget for the Municipal
      Building Authority of the City of West Jordan, Utah for Fiscal Year 2020-2021
Danyce Steck presented the Final Budget for the MBA for Fiscal Year 2020-2021.

MOTION:        Board Member Green moved to approve Resolution No. 104 adopting the
               Final Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West
               Jordan, Utah for Fiscal Year 2020-2021. Board Member Whitelock seconded
               the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Board Member Green           Yes
Board Member Jacob           Yes
Board Member Lamb            Yes
Board Member Pack            Yes
Board Member Whitelock Yes
