                                      ORDINANCE NO. 1470

       AN ORDINANCE VACATING THE RIGHT-OF-WAY OF WHEATFIELD
       WAY WHEATFIELD CIRCLE IN THEIR ENTIRETY LOCATED WITHIN
       THE GREENFIELD FARMS SUBDIVISION

       WHEREAS, the City has received a request by Exeter Draper Land LLC (“Developer”), who
owns all of the adjacent lots, requesting the rights-of-way of real properties known as Wheatfield
Way (40 West) and Wheatfield Circle (13015 South) be vacated as public streets; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the request for the street vacations and has found
there is not a current or future demand for the right-of-way where the entire area of the subdivision
will be fully incorporated into a future development; and

        WHEREAS, the proposed street vacations set forth herein have been reviewed by the City
Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been held in accordance with Utah law to obtain
public input regarding the proposed public interest in the street.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH:

        Section 1. Findings: The City Council finds that neither the public interest nor any person
will be materially injured by the vacations and there is good cause for the vacations.

       Section 2. Street Vacation. The following described right-of-way of Wheatfield Way (40
West) and Wheatfield Circle (13015 South) in their entirety located within the Greenfield Farms
Subdivision, and as shown on the attached map, no longer serves a public purpose and can be
vacated as public rights-of-way:
                                      See EXHIBIT A

       Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Ordinance
and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final passage
and the quitclaim deed has been recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder.

    PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 15th DAY OF DECEMBER 2020.
