3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Jose Arias’s request for review of the Arias
General Plan Amendment on 1.97-acres of property located at 7944 N 9550 W
(approximately 2300 West Pioneer Crossing), changing the land use designation from
Neighborhood Commercial (NC) to Heavy Commercial (C-H).
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item. DRC had seven redline comments. This
is a legislative item.

The applicant, Jose Arias, was present. He would like to change the zone so he could run his
landscape business.

Commissioner Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Katie read the online comment from Debra Bigler regarding vehicles on the property and traffic
and the condition of the site. She is opposed to the general plan amendment.

Commissioner Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Bunker asked if there was a size limit to the heavy equipment. Ross said the State
had a meeting for the widening process for Pioneer Crossing and this section will be a five to six
lane highway and this will be at the corner of a very busy highway. Residential is right next to
the proposed amendment. The land designation to the West is Saratoga Springs property. The
Commission was concerned with the change and not knowing what could be built if the
designation was changed to heavy commercial. Landscaping would fall under Construction
Services for the table of uses in the code. The staff and commission discussed the benefit of a
development agreement.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommend denial of Jose Arias’s request for
        review of the Arias General Plan Amendment on 1.97-acres of property located at
        7944 N 9550 W (approximately 2300 West Pioneer Crossing), changing the land use
        designation from Neighborhood Commercial (NC) to Heavy Commercial (C-H);
        including all DRC comments and the findings stated in the report. The Commission
        recommended the City Council explore methods to achieve a desired result for the use of
        the property with the possibility of a development agreement. Commissioner Everett
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3.2) Consideration of DR Horton’s request for review of the Salix Park Concept Plan on 11.2
acres located at approximately 670 North 3080 West.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.


The applicant, Jeff Stephenson with DR Horton, was present. Jeff indicated this will be a City
park. The park will be built by DR Horton and will be reimbursed by impact fees from the City.
Commissioner Everett had questions about on street parking.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of DR Horton’s request
        for review of the Salix Park Concept Plan on 11.2 acres located at approximately 670
        North 3080 West; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Also
        including recommendations to move the kayak slip closer to the parking and reevaluate the
        parking stalls for a better amenity. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code amendment to Tables 05.030-A and 05.040-B, updating allowed residential uses and
the bulk and intensity standards.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Hannah Sheek asked if the item was approved when it would go into effect.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Everett asked if there was merit to make it a conditional use.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of a Development Code amendment to Tables 05.030-A and 05.040-B,
        updating allowed residential uses and the bulk and intensity standards; including all
        DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Noting the change of language to read
        “conditional” instead of “permitted” to allow for flexibility for certain uses and setbacks
        to be measured from top back curb and adjust the distances accordingly. Commissioner
        Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code amendment to Chapter 12, updating standards for Bed and Breakfasts to correspond
with the Table of Uses.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

Commission chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Diedre Clark does not feel like this is necessary and will not fit into the designated zone.

Commissioner chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to send a positive recommendation of Lehi City’s
        request for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 12, updating
        standards for Bed and Breakfasts to correspond with the Table of Uses; including all
        DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed 4 – 1.
