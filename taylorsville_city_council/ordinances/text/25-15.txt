                                      TAYLORSVILLE,          UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE         NO. 25- 15


 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 15, CHAPTER 15. 08 OF THE TAYLORSVILLE
 CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO ADOPT THE UTAH WILDLAND URBAN INTERFACE
     CODE AND RELATED PROVISIONS FOR WILDFIRE RISK MITIGATION

           WHEREAS, in the 2025 Utah Legislature General Session the Legislature amended
Utah Code section § 65A- 8- 203 and related sections to require municipalities to adopt and
enforce wildland urban interface building standards for wildfire prevention; and

           WHEREAS,     City of Taylorsville (" City") desires to maintain eligibility for state
cooperative fire protection agreements and funding from the Division of Forestry, Fire, and State
Lands; and


           WHEREAS, adoption of these standards promotes public safety in high-risk wildland-
urban interface areas; and


           WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the City Council has determined that amending
adopting these standards is in the best interest of residents of the City of Taylorsville' s health,
safety, and welfare.

           NOW, THEREFORE, the City Council of Taylorsville, Utah, ordains as follows:

Section 1. Amendment       to Title 15, Chapter 15. 08— Adoption of Utah Wildland Urban
Interface Code.



Title 15, Chapter 15. 08 of the Taylorsville City Municipal Code is amended by adding a new
Section 15. 08. 060, to read as follows:


15. 08. 060 Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code Adopted.


The Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code, issued by the International Code Council, with the
alternatives or amendments approved by the Utah Division of Forestry, Fire, and State Lands, is
hereby adopted as a construction code.

Title 15, Chapter 15. 08 of the Taylorsville City Municipal Code is amended by adding a new
Section 15. 08. 070, to read as follows:


15. 08. 070 Adoption of Map

The attached map ( Exhibit A) showing the boundaries of high- risk wildland- urban interface
property and what constitutes wildland- urban interface property that is not high- risk is hereby
adopted.


Section 2. Effective Date.
