   5. ZONING REGULATION AMENDMENT (ZRA) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      Consider a request to amend portions of the city zoning ordinance, Title 10, to add hobby garage units as
      a permitted with standards (PS) use in the Planned Development Commercial (PD-C) zone and to add
      the specific standards for this use. The applicant is Ryan Thomas, DSG Engineering Inc. Case No. 2022-
      ZRA-003 (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Carol Davidson presented the following:

      Carol Davidson – The first part is basically adding the use. Right now, we don’t have hobby garages
      allowed at all. The plan is to put it in the PD-C zone only and as a permitted with standards. A lot of the
      standards are similar to what we require for regular storage units. Carol read the standards that are
      included in the packet. Staff recommends approval as it is written.

      Chair Draper – Is there a reason why they can’t have a shower there?

      Commissioner Kemp – They don’t want you living there.

      Chair Draper – Do we have any like this now?

      Carol Davidson – Yes, we have 2 currently, there were no standards for those they came through a long
      time ago.

      Commissioner Kemp – I think one of the benefits of these is that people can own them, so I don’t think
      the one with only one owner makes sense, you’re basically turning it into self-storage. If we are going
      to have hobby garages then people should be able to own them.

      Commissioner Anderson – I agree.
