                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 22-08

  AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF RIGHT-OF-WAY LOCATED AT
 APPROXIMATELY 3600 WEST 11820 SOUTH CONSISTING OF 0.02 ACRES (1,032
 SF) TO THE ADJACENT PROPERTY OWNER, PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY
       WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton, Utah, has the authority, pursuant to Utah Code
10-9a-609-3, to vacate a subdivision or portion of a subdivision within Riverton City; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has complied with the public notice requirements of Utah
Code sections 10-9a-208; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider the proposed vacation
of an existing undeveloped right-of-way on 3600 West, as shown and described in Exhibit “A”,
and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that there is good cause to vacate
the portion of the subdivision dedicated to Riverton City, as described herein, and that said
vacation will not be detrimental to the public interest;

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Governing Body of Riverton City that:

       1. The undeveloped right-of-way portion of 3600 West as shown and described in
          Exhibit “A” is hereby vacated and the City does hereby revoke any acceptance of the
          dedication of any easements as shown on the recorded plat. The private rights-of-way
          and easements therein, if any, of any lot owner and the franchise rights of any public
          utility shall not be impaired by this action.

       2. The said property is hereby vacated to the adjacent property owner to the west.

       3. This ordinance shall take effect upon execution.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 15th day of
February 2022 by the following vote:
