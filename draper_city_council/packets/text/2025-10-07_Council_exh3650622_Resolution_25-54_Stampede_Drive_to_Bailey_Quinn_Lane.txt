                                RESOLUTION 25-54

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL RENAMING STAMPEDE
                           DRIVE, A CITY STREET

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County Ordinance 2.49.090 establishes a process for cities
using the county addressing system to rename streets; and

       WHEREAS, the aforementioned ordinance requires a municipality to make a
finding that renaming a street is in the public interest; and

     WHEREAS, the aforementioned ordinance requires 75% of the property
owners with structures that front a street to approve a street name change; and

      WHEREAS, there are no structures that front Stampede Drive and therefore
the requirement of the ordinance is deemed satisfied; and

        WHEREAS, many of the citizens of Draper in the neighborhood where
Stampede Drive is located have petitioned the city to rename the street in honor of
Bailey Quinn Mallard, a Draper citizen and child in the neighborhood who perished
in a tragic accident; and

        WHEREAS, the city finds that renaming Stampede Drive to Bailey Quinn Lane
is in the public interest.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Approval. The City Council hereby approves Resolution 25-54,
requesting that Salt Lake County rename Stampede Drive to Bailey Quinn Lane.

     Section 2. Effective Date.        This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon passage.
