                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-49

A RESOLUTION APPROVING A COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE
            OF UTAH, DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

       WHEREAS, the State of Utah, Department of Transportation, (“UDOT”) has obtained
and operates a stormwater maintenance pond in the vicinity of 11851 South Redwood Road,
which collects and detains stormwater originating from Redwood Road (the “UDOT Pond”); and

        WHEREAS, in the year 2022, Riverton City obtained approximately 2.7 acres of
property in the vicinity of 1536 W Laurel Chase Drive (the “City Parcel”) for the purpose of
developing said property into a regional stormwater detention pond, which collects stormwater
from neighborhoods to the west of Redwood Road, and in the vicinity of approximately 11800
South. The City parcel presently receives accretion flows of ground water year-round in addition
to the stormwater collected from surrounding neighborhoods; and

        WHEREAS, stormwater which arrives at the UDOT Pond, once detained, eventually
flows into the native drainage system which runs through the City Parcel; and

       WHEREAS, the City staff has evaluated the salient features of the City Parcel and has
completed several concept designs for the development of the City parcel into a regional
stormwater detention pond, and has determined that with proper improvements, the City Parcel
may receive the full, un-detained capacity of the UDOT pond in addition to the neighborhood
stormwater and existing accretion flows; and

        WHEREAS, the western border of the City Parcel is located less than 300 feet to the east
of the eastern border of the UDOT Pond; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a UDOT Pond located
less than 300 feet from the location of a City-owned detention pond -which contains the capacity
to manage waters of both ponds- serves as a shining example of inefficiency in governmental
operations; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council further finds and determines that consolidating
the management of stormwater into a single pond will facilitate a more efficient use of land in
the vicinity of the City-Parcel, which is presently under development; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds and determines that it should execute an agreement
with UDOT to accept the stormwater contained within the UDOT Pond, upon acceptance of
adequate consideration from developers in the region surrounding the UDOT Pond and the City
Parcel to help with the cost to improve the City Parcel into a regional detention pond;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves the Cooperative Agreement with the
          State of Utah, Department of Transportation, attached to this Resolution as “Exhibit
          A”, authorizing Riverton City to collect and detain stormwater presently directed to
          the UDOT Pond. The Mayor is authorized to sign the Cooperative Agreement once
          the following conditions have been met: a) Riverton City receives Forty Thousand
          Dollars, payable to Riverton City, from developers in the vicinity of the City Parcel to
          assist with the cost of developing the City Parcel into a detention pond; and b) upon
          verification that the Cooperative Agreement is approved as to legal form by the
          Riverton City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 21st day of May,
