                             EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL
                               NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING


 Pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3-818, notice is hereby given that a Public Hearing will be
 held in the Emigration Canyon Fire Station located at 5025 E. Emigration Canyon Road, Salt
 Lake City, UT 84108 during the City Council Meeting on Tuesday, May 27, 2025, at 7:00 pm
                                            MST.
   The purpose of the hearing is to discuss the City of Emigration Canyon’s Fiscal Year 2026
                                             Budget.
 At the date and time fixed for the public hearing all who appear will be given an opportunity to
 express their views on the estimates of revenues and expenditures or any item in the proposed
City’s FY 2026 Budget. Those who wish to attend and speak at the hearing may do so. A copy of
 the budget will be available for inspection at least 7 days before the public hearing at the front
counter of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District office located at 860 W. Levoy Dr.,
Suite 300, Taylorsville, UT 84123. A copy will also be posted with this notice on the Utah Public
                         Notice Website listed at the bottom of this notice.
  In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the City of Emigration Canyon will
 make reasonable accommodations to participate in the hearing. Requests for assistance can be
           made by calling 385-377-9466 within three working days of the meeting.
                               Dated this 15th day of May, 2025
