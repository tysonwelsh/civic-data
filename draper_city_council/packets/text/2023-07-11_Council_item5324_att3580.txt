          REPORT TO DRAPER CITY COUNCIL REGARDING A
         PROPOSED PROPERTY TAX RATE INCREASE FOR 2023
                                     July 11, 2023
Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District is proposing a property tax increase for its fiscal
year 2023/2024 budget.
A public hearing is scheduled for August 9, 2023, at 6:00 p.m., where citizens will be able to
attend and ask questions or make comments.
A growing service area and increasing water demands require additional water supplies and
infrastructure, costing millions of dollars.
Summary information regarding Jordan Valley’s proposed property tax increase:
