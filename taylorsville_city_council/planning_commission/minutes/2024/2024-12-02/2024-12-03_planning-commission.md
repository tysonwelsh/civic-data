Chair Wilkey called the special work session to order at 5: 40 p. m. in Room # 202.            She

then turned the time over to Long -Term Planner Mark McGrath.

   1.   Discussion    Regarding the Taylorsville General Plan Update Project -
        Chapter 5: Economic          Development;   Chapter 6: Parks and Recreation;           and
        Chapter 7: Neighborhoods. (       Mark McGrath,      AICP /     Long -Range Planner)

Chapter 5: Economic Development ( renamed to Economic Prosperity & Resilience
Mr. McGrath first reviewed the five guiding principles of the Plan:
   1) Balance, Diversity, and Social Equity
   2) Stewardship
   3)   Resilience
   4)   Civic Beauty and Character
   5)   Health and Mental Well- being

He described several areas which had been identified as priorities for economic
development:


        4700 South from 1- 215 to 2700 West ( currently under- utilized)
           o   Westwood    Village
           o   Taylor's Landing
           o   LDS Temple
           o   State Office Complex ( major employment base)
           o   Beltway West
           o   Commercial/ Residential area east of temple (existing neighborhood)

Mr. McGrath indicated that the Taylorsville Expressway Station Area Plan was expected

A brief break was taken from 7: 15 p. m. to 7: 26 p. m.

Chapter 6: Parks,    Recreation

An Existing Conditions Report had been created for each park in Taylorsville and Mr.
McGrath highlighted both Azure Meadows and Cabana Parks during his discussion.

Chapter 7: Neighborhoods

He then pointed out that maintaining neighborhoods in the city was perhaps the most
important responsibility that city leaders held, including increasing park spaces within
them. This chapter of the General Plan was focused on that premise.

The meeting adjourned at 7: 54 p. m.
