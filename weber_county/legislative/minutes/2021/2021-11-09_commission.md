APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 48-2021 OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER
COUNTY REQUESTING DESIGNATION OF THE LEGACY HIGHWAY ROUTE AS A HIGH
PRIORITY TRANSPORTATION CORRIDOR.

Courtlan Erickson (Attorney) spoke with Lisa Wilson from UDoT (Utah Department of Transportation) and
she explained designation:
e Facilitates closer cooperation between the county and UDoT in preserving the transportation corridor.
e Emphasizes importance of that corridor to Corridor Preservation Authority Committee.
Approval of this Resolution moves the cooperation forward one step.
Commissioner Jenkins: Why are we approving this one month before the Western Weber County Master Plan
meeting?
Commissioner Froerer: All this does is say this is a high priority for Weber County. This likely will not be
on UDoT’s next session but we need to get on that list sooner than later.
Commissioner Jenkins moved to postpone for two months. No second.


Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of Resolution 48-2021 of the County Commissioners of Weber
County requesting designation of the Legacy Highway Route as a High Priority Transportation Corridor;
Chairman Harvey seconded.

Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — no; Chair Harvey — aye

5. AMENDMENTS TO AN ORDINANCE OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER
COUNTY AMENDING VARIOUS SECTIONS OF THE COUNTY’S SUBDIVISION ORDINANCE
TO ENCOURAGE PUBLIC STREET CONNECTIVITY, TO PROVIDE RELATED AMENDMENTS
REGARDING THE PROVISION OF STREET AND LOT DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS, TO
STREAMLINE THE APPROVAL PROCESS, AND TO INCLUDE GENERAL ADMINISTRATIVE
AND CLERICAL AMENDMENTS TO CREATE A MORE EFFICIENT ORDINANCE.

Charlie Ewert (Planning) clerical matter — roll call vote did not occur during July 6, 2021 Commission Meeting.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve of amendments to Ordinance 2021-23 of the County Commissioners
of Weber County amending various sections of the county’s subdivision ordinance to encourage public street
connectivity, to provide related amendments regarding the provision of street and lot development standards,
to streamline the approval process, and to include general administrative and clerical amendments to create a
more efficient ordinance; Commissioner Froerer seconded.

Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

6. MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY PROSPERITY
CENTER OF EXCELLENCE AND MIDTOWN COMMUNITY HEALTH CENTER TO
IMPLEMENT THE ICAN MODEL OF RESOURCE INTEGRATION FOR MIDTOWN
IDENTIFIED ELIGIBLE FAMILIES EXPERIENCING INTERGENERATIONAL POVERTY.

Melissa Freigang (Center of Excellence) took a moment to share appreciation for the attention paid to our
veterans. Scaling existing program with ICAN model, Aly Martinez from Midtown will share a few words.
Aly Martinez (Midtown) shared that it is a rare occasion when Midtown gets to give some money. Midtown
aims to connect individuals with services. Very unique opportunity provided through ARP dollars.
Commissioner Jenkins: Grateful to Midtown for faith in the Center of Excellence. We pledge to you to earn
our money and do a good job for you.

Commissioner Jenkins moved for approval of Memorandum of Understanding with Midtown Community
Health Center to implement the ICAN model of Resource Integration for Midtown identified eligible families
experiencing intergenerational poverty; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
