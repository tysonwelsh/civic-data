MEMO
To: City Council
From: John Vuyk, Finance Director
Date: 2025-08-13
Re: Public Hearing: Proposed Property Tax Increase - Salt Lake County


Comments:


Draper City has proposed to set the certified tax rate for FY2026 (Tax Year 2025) equal to
the combined tax rate for FY2025 (Tax Year 2024). This will set the equalized tax rate at
0.001022 generating an additional $935,965 of tax revenue citywide, with total anticipated
property tax receipts of $11,122,761.

For Salt Lake County, Draper City would receive an additional $891,036 in property tax
revenue per year as a result of the change from the County certified tax rate. This change
would represent an increase of 8.97% of budgeted property tax revenue over last year’s
property tax budgeted revenue excluding eligible new growth.

This hearing is an opportunity for residents living in Salt Lake County to provide public
comment on the proposed tax rate change.

No action will be taken on this item.
