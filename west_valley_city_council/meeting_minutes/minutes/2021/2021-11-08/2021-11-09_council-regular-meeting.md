CONSIDER APPLICATION SMI-5-2021, FILED BY MIKE GARZA,
REQUESTING FINAL PLAT APPROVAL FOR THE CREWE 2 LOT
SUBDIVISION LOCATED AT 3588 SOUTH 5450 WEST
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed application SMI-5-2021, Filed by Mike Garza,
Requesting Final Plat Approval for the Crewe 2 Lot Subdivision Located at 3588 South 5450
West.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

        Mr. Mark Garza, is requesting final plat approval for the Crewe 2 Lot Subdivision.
        The subject property currently has an existing dwelling that will be removed to
        accommodate two new single-family dwellings. The property is bordered on the
        north, east and south by existing residential uses along 5450 West and the Erin Hills
        PUD to the west.

        The applicant is the developer of the Erin Hills PUD and the owner of the lots
        proposed in this application. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on .42 acres.
        Although zoned R-1-8, both lots are approximately 9,250 square feet in size.
        During the rezoning process for the Erin Hills project, the developer requested an
        exception to the frontage for these two lots. An exception was granted by the City
        Council that each lot would be approved with 66 feet of frontage.

        Access to the subdivision will be gained from 5450 West. At the present time, this
        street is dedicated and improved with asphalt only. Although there are a few
        sections with curb, gutter and sidewalk, the majority of this street is void of those
        improvements. Staff has discussed the long-term vision of this right-of-way with
