       A. Welcome, Roll Call, and Introduction: By Mayor Dawn Ramsey

       B. Invocation: By Council Member Tamara Zander

Council Member Zander offered the invocation.

       C. Mayor and Council Coordination

The mayor and council discussed the conference beginning tomorrow.

Council Member Brad Marlor discussed the roads and how the winter has left them in bad shape.
After discussion with staff regarding availability of hot mix materials to properly address
potholes, he asked if that information could be shared with the residents so they know city staff
is aware of the pothole issues, and that they will be doing those more permanent hot mix repairs
as soon as those materials are available.

Council Member Patrick Harris asked about flooding in the city, and if there were any current
concerns.

City Manager Gary Whatcott responded that the majority of the city is not in danger. The few
areas that were identified, mostly along Midas Creek, have already had doors tagged with
information on how and where to get sandbags. He also noted that with the recent development
in Herriman along Midas Creek, most of the flooding potential has been eliminated. In addition,

         E. Discussion Item

             E.1. Art’s Council member appointment. (By Director of Recreation Janell Payne).

Director of Recreation Janell Payne introduced Rachel Nay to the council as the newest
appointee for the Art’s Council.

Rachel Nay – has been interested in art her whole life, her brother is an artist as well. There was
nothing as amazing as the South Jordan Arts when she was growing up, so she was never able to
showcase her art in the past. She has been showcasing her art for about three years and she loves
doing it, as well as seeing everyone else’s art here in the city. She does mostly photography now,
and loves taking pictures of wildlife, sunsets, anything that gives her a reason to get out. She
agreed that the times and dates for the Art’s Council Meetings would work for her and she would
be available to help with events when needed. She lives here in the city as well.

The council thanked her for her willingness to serve.
