                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-55

 APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATIVE
  AGREEMENT WITH THE JORDAN SCHOOL DISTRICT REGARDING SCHOOL
                       RESOURCE OFFICERS

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council ("Council") met in regular session on August 16,
2022, to consider, among other things, a resolution approving an Interlocal cooperative
agreement with the Jordan School District regarding school resource officers; and

       WHEREAS, the Utah Interlocal Cooperative Act (Utah Code Ann.§ 11-13-101, et seq.)
(the "Act") provides that any two or more government entities are authorized to enter into
agreements with each other to do what each agency is authorized by law to perform; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City ("Riverton") and the Jordan School District ("District") are
government entities as contemplated by the Act; and
        5WHEREAS, Riverton and District are authorized to enter into agreements with each
other for cooperative action; and

       WHEREAS, an Interlocal cooperative agreement has been presented to the Council for
review and approval, a copy of which is attached hereto ("Agreement"); and

        WHEREAS, the Agreement sets forth the purpose thereof, the extent of participation of
the parties, and the rights, duties, and responsibilities of the parties; and

        WHEREAS, the Council finds that it is in the best interests of the inhabitants of Riverton
to enter into the Agreement.


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

       1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign an Interlocal Agreement with the Jordan School
          District, in substantially similar form to the copy attached to this Resolution, upon
          verification that said agreement is approved by the Riverton City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage and acceptance as
          provided herein.
