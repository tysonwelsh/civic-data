FY2020 Library Annual Update Staff Report:
Adriane Juarez, Library Director, presented this item. Juarez indicated the library was
operating in unusual times during COVID-19. Staff increased the number of available
electronic materials and online programs, and offered curbside pickup for physical
materials. Staff also uploaded YouTube videos for patrons to view. They had received
positive feedback from the public. Juarez reviewed library statistics from FY 2020. The
library used CARES Act funds to expand the WI-FI signal onto the patio outside the
coffee shop, into the reading garden and throughout the interior of the building. The
library also offered Local Authors Nights, a self-guided play area for children and
several other programs.

Juarez stated library staff had received extensive training during the year. She also
noted the meeting rooms and Santy Auditorium had received electronic enhancements.
She thanked the Friends of the Library and the Library Board members for all their work
and support.

Council Member Worel stated she had been helping seniors register to receive the
COVID vaccine, and indicated many seniors used the library for email and other
services. She felt the library was so important for the many patrons who utilized it.

Council Member Gerber stated she missed the library and was grateful for all that the
library staff offered to the community. Juarez stated staff missed seeing the patrons
and had heard about the different demographics that used the library computers. She
noted there were laptops that could be checked out for those who needed them.

Council Member Henney stated the library was a community asset and asked how the
library staff dealt with concerns during this pandemic. Juarez stated staff was putting out
their services on social media to get the word out to residents. She felt benchmarks

Rentals as a Conditional Use in the Lower Rossi Hill Sub-Neighborhood of the

would correct a previous oversight in nightly rentals. She reviewed these rentals were
prohibited in this area in 2015. In 2017, the Lower Rossie Hill area was rezoned and by
default nightly rentals were prohibited. She stated the surrounding areas allowed nightly
rentals. This amendment would make nightly rentals a conditional use.
