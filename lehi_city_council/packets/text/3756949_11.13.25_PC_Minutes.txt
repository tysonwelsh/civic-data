52   3.1) Public Hearing and Recommendation of the Downtown Revitalization Plan and State
53   Street Station Area Plan including parts of Main Street and State Street (tabled at the
54   October 23, 2025 meeting).
55
56   Kim Struthers introduced Caden Petersen from the planning department. Petersen presented the
57   item. He explained that this has been a joint project with input from many parties, including the
58   city, the public, and independent contractors.
59
60   Commissioner Kunze asked if the plan has been updated since the last meeting. It has been
61   adjusted based on DRC comments. Struthers explained that many attempts have been made to
62   solicit public feedback, but that there weren’t as many responses as the city would have liked.
63
64   Sam Taylor began his presentation of the plan with the changes that have been made since the
65   last meeting. Based on feedback from DRC, unit counts have been changed from exact numbers
66   to “up to” counts. The Station Area land use map better matches the concept. The green loop
67   bike and trail concept has been better integrated into the plan. Minor grammatical revisions have
68   also been made.
69
70   A variety of community input has been gathered so far, including an initial survey (statistically
71   valid with 542 responses), a technical committee, an advisory committee, focus interviews with
72   business owners in the area, public open house meetings, a project website with a space to leave
73   comments, and a draft plan review and questionnaire (with 54 responses). Based on this, Taylor
74   explained that residents are eager for more restaurants, parks, and entertainment options, and
75   want to make the area into a family-friendly community hub. People are overall in support of
76   mixed-use development in the area. There were split opinions on transportation and parking
77   options.
78
79   Taylor explained that the plan has been adjusted and fine-tuned based on these responses. The
80   community desire for mixed-use dining and retail in the downtown area will need increased foot
81   traffic to be viable, which is why housing is suggested to be developed in the area as well. While
82   there is currently ample parking in the area, it isn’t as convenient as residents would like. Main
83   Street walkability was favored by the public, but engineering design and analysis will need to be
84   utilized to balance traffic flow. Optimally, regional traffic will be redirected to other routes as
85   development occurs. Taylor urged the Commission to remember that this is a long-term gradual
86   plan that will allow for ongoing evaluation and adjustments as it is implemented.
87
88   Commissioner Eyre asked if there was a generally accepted definition of statistically relevant.
89   There are generally accepted definitions and methods to determine if a survey or study is
90   relevant. Commissioner Eyre shared his concerns over the fact that only 0.5% of Lehi was
91   represented in the survey. Kim Struthers explained that Y2, the analytics company hired to do
92   the study, is a reputable company that specializes in this kind of statistical analysis.
93
94   Commissioner Jones asked staff if they had enough time to look over the plan. They felt that they
95   had.
