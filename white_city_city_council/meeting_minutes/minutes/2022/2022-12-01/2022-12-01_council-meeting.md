Unified Fire Authority

                 Captain Ken Aldridge updated the Council on various construction projects. Chief
Higgs is retiring after 45 years, and Chief Durn will be promoted to Assistant Chief on January 1,
2023. The holiday safety message is a reminder to cut two inches off the bottom of Christmas
trees, water it, and keep it away from fire places and space heaters.

Unified Police Department

                 Detective Josh Smith reviewed the November calls for service. The high calls
for service for November were welfare checks and suspicious activity. Shop with a Shield will be
held on December 10, 2022, at the Midvale Walmart.

               Chief Randy Thomas stated there was a challenge between UPD, UFA, and
Midvale to see who could get the largest donations for the food bank. The Greek Orthodox Church
donated money to Hillcrest High Schools food pantry.

RESOLUTION NO. 22-12-02                                              Date: December 1, 2022

       A RESOLUTION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING
       AMENDMENTS TO THE 2022 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP BUDGET

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to
Utah Code Section 10-2a-401 et.seq.; and

         WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
legislative body for White City pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-501; and

       WHEREAS, Utah State Code, Sections 10-6-109, 10-6-127, and 10-6-128 of the Uniform
Fiscal Procedures Act for Utah Cities, requires that increases in appropriations for operating
budgets of the General Fund and other funds be made by resolution of the governing body; and

      WHEREAS, the required public notice was properly notices on the State Public Notice
Website and City Website; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to notice, the public hearing was held on the 1st day of December,
2022; and

     WHEREAS, in compliance with statutory requirements, the White City Metro Township
amends The 2022 White City Metro Township Budget as detailed in Attachment “A”.

        THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
as follows:

      SECTION 1. The White City Metro Township Council hereby amends the 2022 White City
Budget, Attachment “A”.

      SECTION 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage thereof.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 1st DAY OF DECEMBER, 2022 by the White City Metro
Township Council, White City, Utah.

White City Metro Township Council Vacancy

               Paul Ashton stated it is necessary to take steps to fill the remainder of former
Council Member Little’s term. Prior to the meeting where the Council will decide on the vacancy,
a 14 day notice is required. Applicants need to file a letter of interest and send it to Rori Andreason.
The Council will need to conduct interviews at its next regular business meeting and then make
the appointment.

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Cardenaz, moved to direct
Rori Andreason to notice the vacancy and have applications close on December 22, 2022 at 5:00
pm. Interviews will be conducted by the Council on January 5, 2022. Following interviews, the
Council will appoint one of the applicants. The motion passed unanimously.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)/Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

               Council Member Perry stated the Salt Lake County Mayors office has made a
budget recommendation to stop funding UFA services in Brighton. Salt Lake County believes that
since Brighton and Alta have incorporated, they should pay for their own services. If it is approved,
UFA will have to either decrease service levels, Brighton funds themselves, or UFA members
provide a subsidy.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                  Council Member Price stated the final meeting was on December 12, 2022. Every
three years an employment survey is completed. The district falls below the wage range enough
where it felt it needed to provide raises of 2 percent merit and 4.6 percent cost of living.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)/Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Council Member Cardenaz stated discussions continue regarding the removal of
Sheriff Rosie Rivera as the Chief Executive Officer of SLVLESA. Midvale City wants better budget
transparency. The CFO has stepped down and a new person has been hired.
