DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Jake Buxton, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to allow for a locksmith shop
on the property located at 8169 S. 700 E. (See Exhibit #1: Application Material). The applicant
has been operating a mobile locksmith shop for 2 years and needs more space.

The subject property is located on 0.2 acres (8,712 square feet) in the Boulevard Commercial
Zone (BC). The building is approximately 1,841 square feet and contains two levels. The
applicant is proposing to utilize the space as a workspace to duplicate keys, work on lock repairs,
provide a showroom for specialty order keys and safes, and provide a place for people to
schedule mobile service appointments. The proposed operating hours of the business are from
8:30 AM – 5:30 PM Monday through Friday. The applicant currently has no employees and is
not currently proposing to have any.

The property is located on the east side of 700 E between 8125 S and 8270 S. The property is
located in the Boulevard Commercial (BC) zone with properties to the north, east, and south
being single family homes zoned BC and R-1-8. To the west is 700 E and then an existing office
building also zoned (BC). The property is accessed via a driveway on the south side of the
existing building.

NOTICE
A neighborhood meeting was held on December 7, 2021. Notices were mailed to property
owners within 500 feet of the subject property. Two neighbors attended the meeting and were
supportive of the project. In addition, staff received no emails or phone calls regarding the
project.

ANALYSIS
Under Section 21-08-02(B) of the Sandy City Land Development Code the proposed Locksmith
use is classified as a “Commercial Retail Sales and Services” and requires a conditional use in
the BC zone.

Section 21-24-8 (b) – Parking Space Requirements
This property was originally developed in 1999 as a real estate office with an approved
parking ratio of 3.5 stall per 1000 square feet. The site currently has six standard parking
stalls as well as one ADA stall. The locksmith shop is considered a commercial retail
sales and services use which requires a parking ratio of 5 per 1000 square feet.

The applicant is not currently proposing to have any employees and he is called out to
sites for the majority of jobs. Due to the low projected traffic impact of this property staff
is comfortable with this specific business and its operations with the existing parking
stalls.

described in the staff report based upon the following findings and subject to the
