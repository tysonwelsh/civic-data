4.1 Update on the Transfer of Agency Funds to the Utah Heritage 
Foundation (dba Preservation Utah) in, or for the benefit of 
the, the Magna Main Street Project Area
20-0766
Mr. Ruedigar Matthes , Regional Economic Development Research 
Page 1 of 3
Redevelopment Agency Meeting Minutes August 11, 2020
Coordinator, Economic Development Division, reviewed the transfer of the 
Magna West Main Street Project Area fund balance to Preservation Utah for 
an earthquake loan fund, which is now up and running.  The Economic 
Development Division is doing outreach to inform the public about the use of 
those funds.  Preservation Utah has started accepting applications, but he did 
not know how many it had received.  He would update the Council on that at 
a later date.
Director Bradley  asked if the Ethnic & Mining Museum of Magna had been 
damaged in the earthquake.  The County provided funding for disability 
upgrades, and he wanted to make sure that was intact.
Director Jensen stated that building was not damaged.
Ms. Dina Blaes , Director, Office of Regional Transportation, Housing, and 
Economic Development, stated the County received a list from the State 
Historic Preservation Office of all the buildings that were damaged, which 
she could share with the Council.  Magna Main Street was nominated for the 
National Register of Historic Places, which the Utah Division of State History 
has hired a consultant to finalize.  That would help business owners with 
Federal Rehabilitation Tax Credits.  Additionally, the Utah Governor ’s Office 
of Economic Development became a member of the National Main Street 
Program, which will help with earthquake damage as well as provide 
economic development opportunities.
4.2 Discussion and Possible Approval of Use of Agency 
Administrative Funds to Increase Part -Time Position to 
Full-Time
20-0767
Attachments: MSD .75-1 FTE RDA
Mr. Ruedigar Matthes , Regional Economic Development Research 
Coordinator, Economic Development Division, reviewed the request to 
increase a three -quarter position hired to work for the Greater Salt Lake 
Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) to a full -time position.  This would 
allow the individual in that position to help the Redevelopment Agency with 
administrative functions, outreach to businesses, and other RDA work.  One 
quarter of the FTE position will be paid for from the RDA Administrative 
Budget, which is estimated to have an impact of a little over $20,000.

Director Jensen  asked if the employee was employed by the GSLMSD, 
and if that person would continue to do GSLMSD functions.  
Mr. Matthes stated the person in that position is a County employee, and 
the GSLMSD contracts with the County for his services.  It is a time -limited 
position, reviewed every year by the GSLMSD.  Three-quarters of his time 
will be spent doing GSLMSD work, and that will be paid for by the 
GSLMSD.
