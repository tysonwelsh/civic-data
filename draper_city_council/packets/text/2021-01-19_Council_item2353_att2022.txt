                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-08

                A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL STATING
                    ITS INTENT TO PARTICIPATE IN THE MUNICIPAL
                 ALTERNATE VOTING METHODS PILOT PROJECT (a.k.a.
                 RANKED CHOICE VOTING) FOR 2021 AND REQUESTING
                  ELECTION SERVICES FROM SALT LAKE COUNTY TO
                 PARTICIPATE IN THE MUNICIPAL ALTERNATE VOTING
                             METHODS PILOT PROGRAM


        WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature has made available a pilot project for participation
in the alternate voting methods for municipal elections beginning January 1, 2019 and ending
on January 1, 2026, known as Municipal Alternate Voting Methods Pilot Project (the “Pilot
Project”), or Ranked Choice Voting; and

        WHEREAS, House Bill 35, as passed during the 2018 General Session of the Utah
Legislature, allows municipalities to conduct nonpartisan races using Ranked Choice Voting in
accordance House Bill 35, codified in Utah Code Section 20A-4-601 et seq of the Utah Code and
all other applicable provisions of law, during any odd-numbered year that the Pilot Project is in
effect; and

        WHEREAS, House Bill 277, as passed during the 2019 General Session of the Utah
Legislature, amended Utah Code Section 20A-4-601 thereby enabling municipalities to provide
a notice of intent to participate in the Pilot Project to the office of the lieutenant governor by
April 15 of the odd numbered year in which a municipal election occurs; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to said House Bill 277, a notice, if submitted, may also be
withdrawn by April 15 of the odd numbered year in which a municipal election occurs;
and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 20A-4-601 also requires that the notice of intent to
participate in the Pilot Project include a document signed by the municipal election officer
stating that the municipality has the resources and capability necessary to participate in the
pilot project; and

       WHEREAS, House Bill 277 amended Utah Code Section 20A-5-400.1 to permit
municipalities to enter into a contract with any other local political subdivision of the state,
regardless of the other political subdivision’s location, to conduct an election during which the
municipality is participating in the Pilot Project; and

        WHEREAS, the Draper City Council finds and determines that the feasibility of
participating in the Pilot Project hinges, in part, upon the ability of the participating
municipality to obtain cost effective election services to conduct such an election; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City ordinarily contracts with Salt Lake County to receive
 municipal election services from the County; and

         WHEREAS, Draper City desires to explore the possibility of obtaining municipal
 election services from Salt Lake County to participate in the Pilot Project, but may have to seek
 election services form another political subdivision if Salt Lake County cannot provide a
 ranked choice voting system;

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. The Draper City Council hereby intends to participate in the Municipal
Alternative Voting Methods Pilot Project for the 2021 municipal election.

        Section 2. The Draper City Council hereby expresses its desire to continue to maintain
a contract with Salt Lake County to receive election services from the County, and directs the
Draper City Manager to initiate discussions with the Salt Lake County Clerk’s office to obtain
pricing for elections services that facilitate the Pilot Project.

        Section 3. The Draper City Council hereby requests that the terms of pricing and a
contract to perform election services for a ranked choice voting system in the 2021 municipal
election be brought to the Draper City Council for consideration with sufficient time for the
Council to fulfill its noticing requirements with the lieutenant governor’s office.

         Section 4. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated §20A-4-602 the City Recorder, as the City’s
election officer, is directed to provide the lieutenant governor with a copy of this Resolution and
a letter signed by the City Recorder stating Draper City has the resources and capability necessary
to participate in the pilot project.

       Section 5. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid,
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this
Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable..

       Section 6. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, THIS THE 19TH DAY OF JANUARY, 2021.
