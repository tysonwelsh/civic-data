REPORT AND CONSIDERATION OF 2021 WASHINGTON COUNTY TAX SALE

Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen reported on the outcome of the 2021 County Tax Sale mentioning that
there were five (5) properties included in the sale. Three (3) parcels were sold and two (2) received
no bids and will be stricken to the County, it is recommended to approve the sale as presented.
Any excess funds collected over the amount owed are allocated the recorded lien holders or

property owner.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Accept the Recommended Results of the
2021 Tax Sale Report. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF THE 2021 CERTIFIED TAX RATES FOR WASHINGTON
COUNTY

Mr. Hafen recommended that the tax rates for the General Operations to be 0.000493; Interest and
Sinking Fund/Bond at 0.000058; and the library at 0.00170 for a total budgeted revenue of
$16,810,313.00.

Mr. Hafen explained that it is being recommended for the tax rate to decrease for the County
Assessing and Collecting levy from 0.000313 to 0.000302 for a total budgeted revenue of
$7,174,089. This is due to the County collecting sufficient funds to operate the assessing and
collecting departments.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Approve the 2021 Certified Tax Rates for
Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox and Iverson voting

aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2786, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_AGREEMENT_BETWEEN_ WASHINGTON COUNTY AND SOUTHWEST
BEHAVIORAL HEALTH CENTER REGARDING THE CONSTRUCTION AND
OPERATION OF A SUBSTANCE ABUSE AND MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS CENTER

County Attorney Eric Clarke explained that Utah State Legislature has been providing funding for
mental health and substance abuse construction projects. Hospitals and jails are not best equipped
to handle individuals in crisis. It is proposed to for Washington County and the Southwest Health
Center to construct and operate a substance abuse and mental health crisis center.

Sheriff Cory Pulsipher stated that the support and equipment needed to help individuals change
are not readily available at the jail and a crisis center would be a great asset for the community.

Mr. Clarke stated that it is important for individuals with mental health or substance abuse
problems to have a safe place off of the streets and out of the house to go and be monitored by
mental health professionals.

The new facility will be located in close proximity to the Purgatory Correctional Facility in
Hurricane.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2786, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington County and
Southwest Behavioral Health Center Regarding the Construction and
Operation of a Substance Abuse and Mental Health Crisis Center. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Cox and Iverson voting aye.

AN_AGREEMENT_ WITH MRW_DESIGN_ ASSOCIATES, INC. TO COMPLETE
ENGINEERING AND ARCHITECTURAL DESIGNS SERVICES FOR WASHINGTON
COUNTY EMERGENCY SERVICES AND LEGACY PARK BUILDING

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that the County utilized CARES money to

storage and training building located at the Legacy Park.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2788, A RESOLUTION SELECTING
ARCHITECTURE AND CONTRACTING FIRMS FOR A NEW SUBSTANCE ABUSE
AND MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS CENTER

Ms. Felshaw explained that along with the partnership between the Southwest Behavioral Health
Center a facility will be constructed. It has been determined that the design-build model of
contracting will allow for the best result and a cost savings for the center. A Request for
Qualifications (RFQ) was advertised and four (4) bids from qualified design-build teams were
received. After an in depth review process by a panel of multidisciplinary project stakeholders, it
is recommended for Watts Construction and MRW Design, Inc. to be the awarded design-build
team.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2788, A
Resolution Selecting Architecture and Contracting Firms for a New Substance
Abuse And Mental Health Crisis Center. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Cox and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox and
Iverson voting aye.
