Community Development Director, Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner

Matt Wagner said that even before COVID, there were a lot of concerns about trailhead 
congestion.  A broad group of people were put together to help in the trail access 
planning process.  This group included Summit County animal control, public works, the 
Sheriff's office, and other experts.  The committee is tasked to develop strategies for 
managing trail head congestion.   
 
Mr. Wagner said the committee wanted to talk about three different categories.  The 
first category was transportation.  This included where overflow parking would be 
directed.  Enhancing existing transit was discussed along with potential recreational 
shuttles.   
 
Mr. Wagner said that dispersing users was the second category.  Mapping would allow 
citizens to make decisions about where they want to recreate.  This may help to avoid 
overcrowding.   
 
Energy and enforcement was the third category.  The County Council requested to focus 
on having an enforcement presence at trailheads.  This may include hiring an 
enforcement officer or having trailhead ambassadors.  Permitting may be an option.   
 
Mr. Wagner said that an enforcement officer was hired to manage some of the 
problematic areas in Sun Peak and Summit Park.  This has been very successful; they 
have seen reduced congestion at the trailheads.   
 
Mr. Wagner said they set a timeline on a document that will guide their work for the 
upcoming year.  A strategic action plan was created.  This includes building new 
facilities and maintaining a level of service.  There is a trails master plan and guidelines 
for open space.   
 
Mr. Wagner said that in the long term, they are looking for guidelines that will consider 
the social, economic, and environmental elements.  He said with the social aspect, they
