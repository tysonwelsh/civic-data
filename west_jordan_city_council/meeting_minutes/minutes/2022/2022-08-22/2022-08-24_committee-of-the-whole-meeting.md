 1. CALL TO ORDER

 2. DISCUSSION TOPICS
         a. 2023 Election Discussion: Ranked Choice Voting, Annual financial disclosure for
            elected officials and candidates, Council district and precinct boundary adjustment
            update
            Ranked Choice Voting – City Recorder Tangee Sloan explained that each year through
            2026, the Elections Officer must inform the County whether the City wanted to opt into
            Ranked Choice Voting in any election. She asked if the Council wished to discuss the
            Ranked Choice Voting option for the November 2023 election. A majority of the Council
            indicated a desire to learn more about the option.

            Council Member Pack arrived at 5:33 pm

            Ms. Sloan proposed voter education efforts during Winter 2022, with the City notifying
            the County of the decision to opt in or opt out in April 2023. West Jordan City opted out
            of the Ranked Choice Voting Pilot Program in 2019 and 2021. Twenty-three cities
            participated in the Pilot Program in 2021 (nine in Salt Lake County). Ms. Sloan said costs
            for 2021 had been under-estimated and were expected to increase for the next election.

            Ms. Sloan shared feedback from Sandy City regarding ranked choice voting for the 2021
            election.

               •   Pros: shorter campaign time; less expensive
               •   Cons: voter confusion; citizens overwhelmed by number of candidates; over 4,000
                   exhausted ballots out of 21,000 (winner did not necessarily receive the majority
                   of all votes); too long to declare a winner in a close race

          Ms. Sloan defined an “exhausted ballot” as a ballot that was not filled out correctly. She
          shared the following feedback from Draper City.

              •   Pros: shorter campaign time; less expensive; no primary; some residents loved it
              •   Cons: no way for a hand recount; voter education; ballots not counted if filled out
                  incorrectly; residents were not part of the decision; City Recorder is supposed to
                  confirm each round was done correctly

          Ms. Sloan wanted sufficient time for voter education. Staff answered questions from the
          Council. Vice Chair Green stated a resolution would be required for the City to formally
          opt-in, and City ordinance requiring a primary election would need to be amended.
          Council Member Pack asked if the option could be placed on the next ballot. Chair
          Whitelock said she would not be in favor of placing the question on the next ballot because
          West Jordan had no other issues requiring an election.

          Chair Whitelock and Council Members Worthen, Pack, and McConnehey said they were
          not in favor of participating in ranked choice voting at that time. Council Member Jacob
          said he was in favor of continuing down the road. Vice Chair Green stated he was 100%
          opposed. Council Member Bloom said she was 100% in favor.

          Chair Whitelock said she believed some level of voter education could occur even if the
          Council did not choose to participate that year. City Administrator Korban Lee said staff
          were preparing a citizen survey that would go out in 2022 and asked if the Council would
          want a question about ranked choice voting included in the survey. A majority of the
          Council indicated support for including a question on the citizen survey.

          Annual Financial Disclosures – Current elected officials were required to submit an
          updated campaign finance report no later than June 1st of each year they held office to
          disclose campaign contributions and expenditures made or received since the immediate
          prior reporting period. Ms. Sloan explained the request to change the submission deadline
          from June 1st to May 15th to provide the Recorder’s Office time for processing and
          reviewing disclosures. Chair Whitelock expressed support. Vice Chair Green commented
          that the ordinance would need to be amended.

          Council District and Precinct Boundaries Update – In April of 2022, the Council passed
          Resolution No. 22-011, equalizing districts based on population. The Resolution was sent
          to the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office to update precincts within the districts. Ms. Sloan
          reported that precincts would be updated after the County election in November 2022.

       b. Chamber of Commerce Service Agreement Discussion
          Economic Development Director Chris Pengra commented that there seemed to be
          general consensus to continue the current relationship between West Jordan and
          ChamberWest. He explained proposed amendments to the Chamber of Commerce Service
          Agreement, including a provision previously recommended by the Council to allow the
          Chamber to use municipal facilities at no charge without the Council needing to approve
          a fee waiver.
