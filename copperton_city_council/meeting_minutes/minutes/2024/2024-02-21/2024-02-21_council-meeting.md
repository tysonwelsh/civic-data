SLVLESA and UPD Interlocal Discussion

              Council Member Stitzer stated the County Sheriff and Unified Police Department
were both given an opportunity to present during the work session earlier in the month. Council
Member Olsen had stated UPD might draft an interlocal agreement, but this did not happen. The
Council has been working with legal counsel on drafting a resolution of intent going forward.

             Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated the interlocal
agreement from UPD was still in process, but the Council was ready to decide.

               Council Member Stitzer read the resolution:

                        COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

RESOLUTION NO. 2024-02-01                                             Date: February 21, 2024

          A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP AFFIRMING
       COPPERTON’S COMMITMENT TO THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT MODEL




                                              2
DATE           WEDNESDAY                           FEBRUARY                           21, 2024

WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

WHEREAS, Copperton is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code § 10-3-
205.5 (the “Council”); and

WHEREAS, in 2023, the Utah Legislature passed H.B. 374 (“County Sheriff Amendments”), which
requires the Salt Lake County Sheriff to leave the Unified Police Department (“UPD”); and

WHEREAS, Copperton’s City Attorney, under the direction of the Mayor and the Council, has
been working with legal counsel for other metro townships and the Town of Brighton to deal with
the short- and long-term implications of H.B. 374; and

WHEREAS, as a result of the Sheriff’s pending departure from UPD, it has become necessary for
UPD to amend and restate the interlocal agreement under which it was created, which restated
and amended interlocal agreement will govern the provision of law enforcement services to its
members; and

WHEREAS, Copperton’s City Attorney has been asked to participate in the drafting of such
restated and amended interlocal agreement; and

WHEREAS, the Copperton Council supports the UPD law enforcement model, and now desires
to affirm that position.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE COPPERTON METRO
TOWNSHIP:

1. The Council affirms its desire to remain a member of UPD.

2. The Council authorizes the City Attorney, under the direction of the Mayor, to assist in the
   drafting of a restated and amended interlocal agreement for the Unified Police Department;
   and

3. Upon completion of the drafting of such agreement, to present the same to the Council for
   review and adoption.

ADOPTED AND APPROVED at a duly called meeting of the Copperton Metro Township Council
on this 21st day of February, 2024.

Update on Metro Township Bill

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated the State
Senate passed HB 35 unanimously earlier that day. There is an optional amendment in the bill,
which will go back to the house, and he does not anticipate it will affect Copperton. He noted
there will be multiple land use bills in the coming week that will most likely limit local control.
