BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of an Amended Development Agreement for
approximately 26.94 acres located at the southwest corner of the intersection of Pony
Express Rd. and Golden Harvest Rd., at approximately 13004 S. Pony Express Rd. (Exhibits
C and D). The property is currently zoned M1. The applicant is requesting that an Amended
Development Agreement be approved to allow for a Fast Food Restaurant as a Permitted
Use under the agreement.

The original development agreement including the zone change to M1 was executed in
April 2020 and the overall site was subsequently cleared of the previously existing
residential homes and consolidated into a single lot (Exhibit B). Currently one industrial and
one commercial building are under construction on the site.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Business and

The property has been assigned the M1 zoning classification (Exhibit F). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the M1 zone is to
“provide areas for uses involving processing and assembly of manufactured goods, warehousing,
and material storage. Uses which generate excessive noise, vibration, odor, dust, and fumes are
excluded from this zone” The RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 ft2 min. lot size) zoning
abuts the subject property on the west, the CBP (Business/Manufacturing Park) zoning is
found to the north across Golden Harvest Rd., Pony Express Rd. and the I-15 freeway are to
the east, and RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 ft2 min. lot size) and DC (Destination
Commercial) zoning is located to the south of the subject property.

Previous Conditions of Approval. The City Council placed the following comments/conditions
of approval on the Development Agreement approved on April 7, 2020:

    1. Planning Review
       a. The Development Agreement alters the permitted and conditional uses typically
          granted to the M1 zoning district. Those changes add a higher level of
          restriction/allowance typically afforded the M1 zone aside from “Retail, general”
          which it sets as a permitted use rather than the M1 standard of conditional use.
          The changes are consistent with the general area and add a greater level of
          predictability regarding how it will be used and redeveloped. All other zoning
          regulations and standards remain consistent with DCMC and the M1 zone.

    2. Engineering/Public Works Review
       a. Traffic Impact Study (TIS) is required for the site.
       b. In the development agreement, section 8, Upsizing/Reimbursements to Master
          Developer; the city will not pay “in advance” for infrastructure upsizing. The city
          requires and will execute a mutually acceptable reimbursement agreement prior
          to the installation of any upsizing, but will not compensate Master Developer

       c. Proposed site layout / master plan shows several access points onto public right-

          Engineering has provided some redline comments on a proposed site layout for
          your consideration.
       d. Existing drinking water infrastructure surrounding the proposed site may need
          to be upsized depending on an analysis of the proposed development demands.
          For example, the drinking waterline diameter in portions of Golden Harvest Rd is
          six inches and would not provide adequate fire flow for some M1 uses.

    3. Fire Review
       a. The Draper City Fire Department has reviewed the proposed Master
           Development Agreement and at this time has no concerns. A more in depth
           review will be done during the site plan and building permit phases.

    4. Legal Review
       a. The request complies with federal and state laws and Draper City ordinances.

Criteria For Approval.
DCMC does not contain criteria for review and potential approval of a Development
Agreement. It does, however; contain a list of items that are required as part of an
Agreement and limitations to a Development Agreement, as found in DCMC Section 9-5-
200(C) and (D). This section depicts the required materials for such requests as:

        C. Procedure: All development agreements, upon proper execution, shall be recorded
           with the respective County Recorder's Office, shall run with the land, and shall be
           binding on all successors in the ownership of the affected property. A development
           agreement shall contain, at a minimum, the following:
               1. A legal description of the land subject to the development agreement.
               2. The restrictions or conditions to be attached to the property including
                  development standards and the provision of public facilities.
               3. The configuration of the project as shown on a project master plan.
               4. A statement of the benefits and value the development agreement will have
                  for the City as a whole, including, but not limited to, assurances of design
                  standards, dedication and improvement of open space, parks, trails,
                  amenities, or infrastructure such as public rights-of-way or utilities.

APPLICANT STATEMENTS
                           Statement of Revisions Requested

       The sole purpose of this Development Agreement Amendment Application is to
amend the Exhibit D (PROPOSED PERMITTED AND CONDITIONAL USES FOR
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT) in the existing development agreement titled
“MASTER DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR EPG PONY EXPRESS,” approved by
the City Council of Draper City, State of Utah, on April 7, 2020, and recorded as entry
13359812, book – 10998, page 918-954 of the Official Records of Salt Lake County,
Utah.

       Specifically, the requested amendment aims to make the single revision to
change the “Restaurant, fast food” use from “Not Permitted” to “Permitted.” This change
will add a permitted use to the property already allowed under a typical M-1 zoning.

    Importantly, no other revisions to the existing development agreement are being
requested by this proposed amendment, and as such, many of the checklist items
typically submitted with a development agreement application have been intentionally
omitted. Among these items include:
    - The restrictions or conditions to be attached to the property including
        development standards and the provision of public facilities.
    - The configuration of the project as shown on a project master plan.
    - Assurances of design standards, dedication and improvement of open space,
        parks, trails, amenities, or infrastructure such as public rights-of-way or utilities.
    - The time frames for performance by the parties.
    - Provisions for enforcement of the terms and conditions of the development
        agreement.
    - Provisions for making amendments to the development agreement.
    - The time limitation of the agreement.
    - Such other terms as may be proposed and agreed to between the city and
        developer or subdivider.

   These items are already addressed within the existing development agreement, and
are not intended to be altered or revised with this proposed amendment. As such,
language has been included in the proposed amendment which states, “All provisions of
the MDA [Master Development Agreement], except as specifically amended by this First
Amendment shall remain in full force and effect.”

    - Aiding the family-friendly environment within this retail space.
