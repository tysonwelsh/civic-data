                             RESOLUTION NO. 22-06

     A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING A NON-
EXCLUSIVE LICENSE AGREEMENT BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND GOOGLE FIBER
UTAH, LLC FOR THE INSTALLATION OF NETWORK FACILITIES IN THE PUBLIC RIGHT-
OF-WAY

      WHEREAS, Google Fiber Utah, LLC has proposed to provide network facilities
and services in Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, in order to provide these services, it is necessary for Google Fiber
Utah, LLC to obtain a non-exclusive license agreement (“License Agreement”) with
the City to install its facilities in the public rights-of-way belonging to the City; and

       WHEREAS, the City has jurisdiction over the use of the public rights-of-way in
the City in which it now or hereafter holds any property interest; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed and considered the attached
License Agreement between Draper City and Google Fiber Utah, LLC; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has determined the License Agreement to be in
the best interest of the City and will be beneficial to the health, safety and welfare
of citizens of the City; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor is authorized to execute agreements.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Draper City Council as follows:

      Section 1. Resolution. The License Agreement between Draper City and
Google Fiber Utah, LLC, attached hereto as Exhibit A and by this reference made a
part hereof, is hereby approved, and the Mayor is authorized to sign the License
Agreement on behalf of Draper City.

     Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, THIS 1ST DAY OF FEBRUARY 2022.
