2. TXT-21-05; Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment; Citywide; Text Amendment
Modifying Development Standards in the RC and CI Zones; David Burton / Grip6
LLC (Applicant); Alex Murphy, Planning Director (Staff).
Mr. Murphy presented a request for a text amendment to update the Regional Commercial and
Clean Industrial zones proposed by applicant David Burton with Grip6. This request is a follow
up to the rezone request heard at the last planning commission meeting. The requested
amendment has three parts that affect both zones: allow manufacturing use as a permitted use;
remove the assembly use; and update parking standards.

Mr. Wilcox stated that the remaining items are all administrative items. If the applicant meets all

the requirements of the statute, then the Planning Commission is required to approve this

application. If there are issues, then the Planning Commission is required to seek mitigation of
those issues. Only if issues presented can’t be mitigated can the Planning Commission deny the
application.
