CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products for the 3000
        East and Crimson Ridge Drive Traffic Signal.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This traffic signal project was a formal
           bid and four bids were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to
           Interstate Rock Products in the amount of $241,897.85.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products for the River
         Road and Commerce Drive Traffic Signal.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This traffic signal project was a formal
           bid and five bids were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Interstate
           Rock Products in the amount of $238,426.50.

      c. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products for the
         Pioneer Road at Man O War Road Traffic Signal Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This traffic signal project was a formal
           bid and five bids were received. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Interstate
           Rock Products in the amount of $290,211.

      d. Consider approval to award bid to Caliber Contractor for the Reuse
         Center Crushing Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City has stockpiled 14,400 tons of
           asphalt and concrete chunks that will be crushed into rock aggregate material
           that can be used on city projects. There were three bidders on this project. Staff
           recommends awarding the bid to Caliber Contractor in the amount of $112,300.

      e. Consider approval to award bid to Feller Enterprises for the Man O War
         Bridge Foundation Repair Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Five bids were received for this project,
           with Feller Enterprises being the lowest bidder. The environmental permit for this
           project will not allow work to commence until after September 1. At that time,
           river flows are typically at a minimum, which will also facilitate the ease of
           construction. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Feller Enterprises in the
           amount of $235,488.

      f.   Consider approval of purchase for IT Network Equipment Package for the
           new City Hall.

FY 2025-26 CITY MANAGER RECOMMENDED BUDGET:
      Submission and filing of the FY 2025-26 City Manager Recommended Budget
      with the City Council (Discussion only).

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: State Law requires the recommended
      budget to be presented to the Mayor & City Council on or before the first regular
      meeting in May of each year and to adopt the final budget on or before June 30th of
      each year. Tonight's item will be for the submission and filing of the FY 2025-26 City
      Manager Recommended Budget with the City Council. This year's proposed key dates
      includes: May 14th for the FY 2026 Budget Work Meeting, May 22nd for tentative
      adoption of the FY 2026 Budget and setting of the public hearing dates, May 30th for
      the FY 2026 Budget Open House, June 5th for the first public hearing on the FY 2026
      Budget, June 19th for the second public hearing on the FY 2026 Budget, and June
      19th for final adoption of the FY 2026 Budget. A copy of the budget will be available
      for public review following tonight's City Council meeting on the City's website at
      www.sgcityutah.gov and at City Hall in the City Recorder's Office. Tonight's item
      requires no action of the City Council.
