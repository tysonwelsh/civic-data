MAY 2020 EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH — ANDREA BEFUS

NOMINATIO
TT IS OUT OF GREAT RESPECT THAT WE NOMINATE ANDREA BEFUS (GIS SYSTEMS ANALYST) FOR THE

UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH AWARD. ANDREA HAS BEEN EMPLOYED AT UTAH COUNTY
JUST OVER A YEAR, IN THAT TIME SHE HAS BECOME A FRIEND TO ALL, AND ONE OF THE MOST WELL-
RESPECTED EMPLOYEES THROUGHOUT THE COUNTY.
SOON AFTER ANDREA BEGAN WORKING AT THE COUNTY A NEED SURFACED FOR A BETTER WAY TO
SHOW THE ELECTION RESULTS PUBLIC FACING ONLINE. SHE SPENT COUNTLESS HOURS WORKING
WITH THE ELECTIONS DEPARTMENT TO CREATE AN INTERACTIVE ONLINE DASHBOARD THAT
ALLOWED FOR VOTING RESULTS IN REAL TIME. A SCRIPT WAS WRITTEN TO LOAD THE DATA INTO
THE ONLINE DASHBOARD, AS SOON AS ELECTIONS DELIVERED THE DATA TO HER IT WAS POSTED IN
LESS THAN 2 MINUTES FOR THE PUBLIC TO VIEW AND INTERACT WITH. ELECTIONS DIRECTOR ROZAN
MITCHELL SAID, “THE TOOL WAS SO SUCCESSFUL AND WELL DONE THAT SEVERAL CITIES
ABANDONED THEIR RESULTS TOOLS AND DIRECTED VOTERS TO OUR WEBSITE.” ANDREA SOON
REALIZED THAT THIS DASHBOARD WAS NOT MOBILE FRIENDLY AND TOOK IT UPON HERSELF TO
CREATE AN ADDITIONAL MOBILE FRIENDLY APP TO DISPLAY THE ELECTION RESULTS ON TABLETS
OR SMART PHONES. THROUGH HARD WORK AND DILIGENCE, THE PUBLIC COULD NOW VIEW
ELECTIONS RESULTS WITH EASE, BUT IT DIDN’T END THERE.
DROP BOXES WERE PLACED THROUGHOUT THE COUNTY TO COLLECT VOTING BALLOTS, ONCE AGAIN
THERE WAS A NEED AND IT WAS MET. AN APPLICATION FOR THE ROVERS WAS CREATED WHERE THEY
WOULD CAPTURE THE DATA AT PICKUP, AND THEN IT WOULD BE CAPTURED AGAIN WHEN THEY
WERE DROPPED-OFF TO BE COUNTED. THIS APPLICATION HAS GREATLY INCREASED THE ACCURACY
OF BALLOT TRACKING REASSURING THE SAFETY OF THEIR BALLOTS, THIS BRIEFLY TOUCHES THE
WORK THAT WENT INTO THIS PROJECT, WHICH IS STILL IMPROVING. ANDREA AND THE ELECTIONS
TEAM WERE ASKED TO PRESENT THIS PROJECT AT THE 2020 ESRI USER CONFERENCE IN SAN DIEGO,
CALIFORNIA.
IN RESPONSE TO THE CURRENT COVID-19 PANDEMIC, ANDREA ONCE AGAIN HAS EXCELLED AT
CREATING SEVERAL MAPPING SOLUTIONS INCLUDING CALL CENTER TRACKING, PPE AVAILABILITY,
AND HOTEL ROOM AVAILABILITY FOR QUARANTINE DASHBOARDS, AS WELL AS SEVERAL SURVEYS
THAT DRIVE DATA IN THESE APPLICATIONS, ALL OF THIS INFORMATION IS HOUSED IN TWO WELL
DONE ARCGIS PORTAL SITES CREATED BY HER.

PETER QUITTNER COMMENTED:
“I HAVE BEEN WORKING WITH ANDREA BEFUS ON MULTIPLE EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT PROJECTS-

FOR THE LAST YEAR. BECAUSE OF OUR GREAT WORKING RELATIONSHIP, WE REQUESTED THE
SUPPORT FROM HER AS WE STOOD UP OUR EMERGENCY OPERATIONS CENTER TO MANAGE THE
COVID-19 CRISIS IN UTAH COUNTY. WE IDENTIFIED AN EXTREME NEED TO DEVELOP SOME CUSTOM
SOLUTIONS TO HELP TRACK, AND STREAMLINE OUR EFFORTS. THIS WAS A CHALLENGING TASK FOR
MANY REASONS. ANDREA WAS WILLING, EAGER, AND MORE THAN ABLE TO COME IN TO DEVELOP
THOSE SOLUTIONS AS A WORKING MEMBER OF OUR EMERGENCY OPERATIONS CENTER. ANDREA HAS
A GREAT ABILITY TO THINK OUTSIDE THE BOX, TO TRANSLATE HER ABILITY AND KNOWLEDGE, WITH
GIS, INTO QUICKLY DEPLOYABLE SOLUTIONS FOR A REAL-WORLD CRISIS. HER SOLUTIONS MADE A
DRAMATIC IMPACT, NOT ONLY IN UTAH COUNTY, BUT THE REGION AND STATE AS WELL. SEVERAL
LOCAL AND STATE AGENCIES WERE INTERESTED IN HER PRODUCTS AND IDEAS. HER CONTRIBUTIONS,
RELATING TO THE COVID-19 RESPONSE, IN UTAH COUNTY, WILL NEVER BE FULLY UNDERSTOOD TO
THOSE OUTSIDE OF THE EOC. SHE WAS GIVEN THE OPPORTUNITY TO PROVIDE VALUABLE SOLUTIONS,
AND SHE RESPONDED AS A LEADER IN THE GIS COMMUNITY. THIS CRISIS MAY HAVE TURNED ONE OF
OUR UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEES INTO A PRICELESS DIAMOND.“
