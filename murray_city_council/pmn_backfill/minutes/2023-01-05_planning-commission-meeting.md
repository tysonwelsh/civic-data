BMW of MURRAY/Young Electric Sign Company – 4615 South State Street - Project #22-165

Ms. Nixon presented the application for Conditional Use Permit approval to allow the
construction of an Electronic Message Center (EMC) sign on the subject property. The applicant
would like to refurbish an existing pole sign into an electronic message center. Section
17.48.200.7. states: "Pole signs are only allowed in the Commercial and Manufacturing Zones for
parcels or developments that are directly adjacent to or abutting either Interstate 15 or Interstate
215 and must be freeway oriented." The existing pole sign is "legal nonconforming" to the
current sign regulations. Section 17.48.090: Nonconforming Signs, states the following: "B.
Maintenance: Excluding normal maintenance, repair, or removal, a nonconforming sign shall not
be moved, altered (including face and structural changes), or enlarged unless it is brought into
complete compliance with the current Sign Code. The following alterations are exempt from this
provision: 1. Content changes to a previously approved sign." This provision allows the sign to
be modified to an EMC sign provided a Conditional Use Permit approval is granted by the

an Electronic Message Center Sign and a survey of the surrounding area, staff concludes the

STUDIO 56 - 170 West Winchester Street - Project #23-004

Ms. Nixon reviewed the request for an amendment to the Conditional Use Permit for Studio 56 to
allow a dumpster enclosure within the front setback of the property. Land Use Ordinance Section
17.76.170.F. states "that no refuse collection areas shall be permitted between the street and the
front building line except as allowed by the Planning Commission." On April 7, 2022, the
Planning Commission granted a Conditional Use Permit for an expansion of the existing dance
studio. The applicant is now proposing to amend the CUP to allow a dumpster enclosure to be
located in front of the dance studio. The applicant proposes to construct a 9' wide X 12' length
dumpster enclosure (72 ft2 ) in the front of the dance studio parking lot area. The proposed
location will eliminate two (2) parking stalls. With the proposed dumpster location reducing 2
stalls, the net total will be 21 parking stalls. Staff has no concerns with this parking. Based on
the information presented in this report, application materials submitted and a site review, staff
recommends that the Planning Commission APPROVE an Amendment to Conditional Use
Permit #22-046 to allow a dumpster enclosure between the street and the front building line for
Studio 56 addressed 170 West Winchester Street.
The applicant, Amy Moore, stated she has read the conditions of approval and will comply with
the conditions.

Public comment was opened. No comment was made and public comment period was closed.

Mr. Hacker made the motion to grant Studio 56 a conditional use permit at the property address
170 West Winchester Street, subject to the following conditions:

     1. The project shall comply with all applicable building and fire code standards, including
        meet the IFC 2018 Fire and Building Codes.
     2. The applicant shall obtain a building permit.
     3. The applicant must meet all Murray City Power Department requirements and provide
        required easements for equipment and Power lines.
     4. Meet the original conditions of approval for the Conditional Use Permit (#22-046) granted
        on April 7, 2022.

DOGS DELIGHT MOBILE GROOMING - 844 West Cherry Oak Circle- Project #23-003

Mr. Smallwood presented the application requesting approval to have more than one on-site
employee for their Major Home Occupation as a mobile dog grooming business. The applicants
would like to obtain approval to have more than the one (1) allowed on-site employee. During the
Business License Application review it was determined that they anticipate three (3) additional
employees that come to the home for a total of four (4). The applicant states that these
employees come to the home to obtain supplies, submit timesheets, and pick up paychecks. No
dog grooming is being conducted at the home.

The applicant had a Murray City Home Occupation business license until May of 2020, when city
code changed to not license home occupations unless the business is a major home occupation
or the applicant requests to "opt in" to have a business license.

The applicants state that they would like to have up to a total of four (4) on-site employees. The
on-site employees will only come to the home for supply pickup, timesheet turn in, and to receive
their paycheck. Any vehicles related to the business will be stored off-site. The applicants have
applied for a business license to store their vehicles in a commercial location. Planning Division
Staff does not anticipate any significant impact caused by the additional employees as they are
only at the dwelling for a short time. The applicant has obtained the required signatures from
adjacent neighbors for the Major Home Occupation license, but because they are requesting to
have 3 additional employees, it requires Planning Commission approval. Based on the analysis
of the application for a Major Home Occupation to allow the addition of three (3), for a total of
four (4), on-site employees on the subject property and a survey of the surrounding area, staff
concludes the following:
    1. The proposed use for a mobile dog grooming business is allowed in as a Home
        Occupation business in the R-1-8 Zone.
    2. With conditions as outlined in the staff report and the applicant's narrative, the proposed
        additional on-site employees will not significantly impact the surrounding neighbors.
    3. With conditions, the proposed use as a Major Home Occupation will not be contrary to
        the goals and objectives of the Murray City General Plan in this area.

Based on the information presented in this report, application materials submitted and a site
review, staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the request for a Major Home
Occupation to allow three (3) additional on-site employees for business from the residential
property addressed 844 West Cherry Oak Circle.

Kevin O'Brien, applicant, approached the podium and indicated they could comply with the
conditions.

Public comment was open. An email was read from Daniel Schoell and Jill Johnson. They
confirmed that they have not been impacted at all by Dog’s Delight Grooming and their
employees..

Mr. Richards made the motion to grant Dog’s Delight Mobile Grooming at conditional use permit
at the property address 844 West Cherry Oak Circle, subject to the following conditions:

   1. Customers shall not be allowed to come to the property.
   2. The four (4) total on-site employees shall only be allowed at the dwelling to pick up
      supplies, turn in timesheets or receive their paychecks. No grooming, or other business
      activity shall be allowed.
   3. The applicant shall park any business-related vehicle to the rear of the dwelling on the
      concrete driveway. Parking any business-related vehicles or trailers on the public street
      is not allowed. No large trucks or equipment allowed on the site other than one work
      truck, weighing less than 12,000 pounds, and one trailer.
   4. All materials shall be located within the dwelling, no accessory structures may be used.
   5. The applicant shall comply with all requirements of Fire and Building Codes, and all State
      and County Health Department requirements.

THE POINTE @ 53rd OFFICE CONDOMINIUMS - 5217 South State Street - Project #23-005

Mr. Smallwood presented the application requesting preliminary subdivision approval to
subdivide Lot 1B of the Pointe @ 53rd Subdivision and create 16 office condos. The proposal is
part of an on-going effort to modernize the commercial complex. The intent is to create sixteen
(16) new condo units. Twelve (12) of which will be office units that could be sold, four (4) are the
ground floor retail units, these could in theory be sold as well, but the applicant has not stated
their intention to do so. The CMU Zone does not have a minimum square footage for a
commercial use. Each unit ranges between 1700 and 2089 square feet. Six (6) notices were
mailed to property owners within a 300’ radius. As of the writing of this staff report, there has
been no comments. Public comment was opened and closed, with no comment.
Based on the information presented in this report, application materials submitted and a site
review, staff recommends that the Planning Commission grant preliminary condominium
subdivision approval for the Pointe at 53rd Office Building Condo Conversion on the property
located at 5217 South State Street.

The applicant approached the podium and indicated he has read and can comply with the
conditions.

Mr. Nay acknowledged there are probably a lot of questions that will need to be answered during
the preliminary phase that will need to be answered by building officials. Mr. Pehrson indicated
to those present that the commission did have a previous meeting to discuss property division.
