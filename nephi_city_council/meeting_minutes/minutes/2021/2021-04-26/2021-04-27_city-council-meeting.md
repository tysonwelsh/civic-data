                                       Nephi City
                                 Work Session Minutes
                                     April 27, 2021
                                        8:30 p.m.
                              Nephi City Council Chambers

1.    Resident Kyle Marchant addressed the council about his concerns of RV park violations
     next door to his home. He stated that occupants of the park are staying longer than the
     30-day limit stay put on RV parks by the Nephi City Code. City Administrator Seth
     Atkinson said that it has become necessary to be more aggressive in enforcing the code
     on local RV parks.

2.    Mr. Atkinson gave an in-depth analysis for a piece of property near the mouth of the
     canyon. The analysis addressed the annexation policy plan criteria for annexation. After
     reviewing the analysis with the council, Mr. Atkinson recommended moving forward with
     a change from Level 2 to Level 1 in preparation for a potential annexation request. The
     council directed staff to add this item to next week’s city council meeting agenda.

3. The council discussed the need to update the city’s building permit fees due to having to
   use a new temporary building inspector who is charging a plan check fee. The council
   directed staff to add a new master fee resolution to next week’s city council meeting
   agenda.

4.   Mr. Atkinson informed the council about a request from the Parks & Cemetery
     Superintendent and the Power Superintendent for permission to purchase new light pole
     Christmas decorations.

5. Mr. Atkinson reported that an employee had broken his leg while on-duty and will be out
   of work for several weeks.

6. Councilor Ostler said that he has received complaints about the condition of the road on
   400 East due to the water project construction.

7. Councilor Memmott reported on a meeting he attended with the Central Utah Water
   Conservancy District.

8. Councilor Seely reported on the next JRDA meeting agenda and the classes that he
   attended at the Utah League of Cities and Towns conference.

9. Mayor Nielson reported on a class he attended at the ULCT conference about rapid
   growth in cities and the importance of open space within developments for the well-being
   of the residents.

10. The council reviewed the financial reports.
