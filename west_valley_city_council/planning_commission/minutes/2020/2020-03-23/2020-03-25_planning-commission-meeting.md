C-3-2020 – continued from the March 11, 2020 Public Hearing
Albarran Storage in Accessory Building for Home Occupation
3790 S 3600 W
R-1-8 Zone (0.42 acres)


The applicant, Pedro Albarran, is requesting conditional use approval for storage within an
accessory building associated with a home occupation at 3790 S 3600 W. The zoning for the
property is R-1-8, Single Unit Dwelling Residential. The zoning for adjacent parcels is R-1-8 to the
east and west, A to the north, and C-2 to the south. The West Valley City General Plan designation
is low density residential for the area.
The applicant operates a landscaping business and will need to obtain a business license for a home
occupation on the property. The code specifies that there can be one vehicle associated with the
home occupation, up to the equivalent of a one-ton pickup truck, and one trailer. Heavy trucks and
similar equipment are not allowed in a residential area. Typically, garages cannot be used for a
home-based business, except for parking of the one business vehicle and trailer. The code allows
for other business-related storage to occur in a garage only with conditional use approval, so long as
the home retains two parking spaces for residents, and the garage is still usable for parking.
The only equipment that can be stored on the property within a garage is something that can be
carried or moved by the pickup truck or loaded onto the permitted trailer. A new detached garage
will be built on the property to accommodate additional storage. The proposed structure shall meet
all provisions in the code for accessory buildings, and a building permit shall be obtained. The rest
of the site needs to be maintained, and not be used for storage.
The Planning Commission continued this application during the March 11th Public Hearing. They
had some concerns about the design of the garage and the visibility of the structure from both 3600
West and 3800 South. The Commission also had questions on the size of the proposed driveway
and landscaping on the property. The applicant has since submitted revised garage elevations, as
well as an updated site plan. The east, west and south building elevations will now have two (2) 20
square foot windows per side. The applicant also indicated that the roof will overhang 24 inches
from the building. The existing chain link fence with slats will remain to the east of the new garage.
The revised site plan shows the width of the drive approach at 16’ and then the concrete will flare
out to the corners of the garage. Lawn and shrubs will be added in the areas to the sides of the
driveway.
Staff Alternatives:
Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well
as the following conditions:

 1. A business license shall be obtained.
 2. The garage shall meet all applicable requirements for accessory buildings in
    residential zones.
 3. Stucco shall be used on the garage exterior to match the house, windows, two (2) 20 square
    foot windows per side, shall be installed on the east, west and south building elevations, and
    there shall be a 24” roof overhang.
 4. The garage shall not be used for living space.
 5. A building permit shall be obtained for the garage.
 6. No outside storage of equipment or supplies shall be allowed.
 7. The vehicle associated with the business on the property shall be no more than the
    equivalent of a one-ton pickup truck.
 8. Only one trailer as defined in 7-7-115(2)(e) shall be allowed for the business on the
    property.
 9. No heavy trucks shall be stored inside or outside of the garage.
 10. The driveway and landscaping shall be installed per the approved site plan.
 11. Driveway surfacing shall be in accordance with 7-9-114 of the City Code.
 12. The property shall be maintained, and the site and business shall meet all applicable
     requirements of Title 7 of the West Valley City Municipal Code.
 13. A permit shall be obtained from the Public Works Department for a new driveway
     approach from 3800 South.
 14. All requirements of affected departments and agencies shall be met.
 15. This approval is subject to review upon a valid complaint.

C-6-2020
2100 South Speculative Warehouse Buildings
6941 W 2100 S
M Zone (24.8 acres)

The applicant, ARCO/Murray, is requesting conditional use approval for two speculative
warehouse buildings at 6941 West 2100 South. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and
the West Valley City General Plan designates the area as Light Manufacturing. The zoning for
all adjacent parcels is also M.

The proposed development will consist of two (2) warehouse buildings, totaling 404,800 sq. ft.
Each building will accommodate one to four tenants. The buildings will be constructed primarily
of tilt up concrete. This facility is not required to meet the Commercial Design Standards due to
the use being limited to a manufacturing zone, however, there are some design requirements
outlined in the development agreement for the property (see attached). The proposed building
elevations meet these requirements.

The landscaping requirement in the M zone is 5%. About 8% of the site will be formally
landscaped. In the north-west corner of the parcel are existing wetlands that will remain, and in
the north-east corner will be a retention pond. Any issues associated with the wetlands on the
property will need to be handled by the applicant with the Army Corps of Engineers. The south
portion of the property shall meet the requirements of 7-10 of the City Code for landscaping along
high-image arterial streets. The parking areas along the frontage will also need to be screened with
a berm as outlined in section 7-9-112 of the code.

The applicant is proposing three entrances to the property from the frontage road. Two will be
primarily for customer and employee parking and the other for truck access. They are proposing to
put a monument sign by each entrance, which falls in line with the code requirement of allowing
one monument sign per 200 feet of frontage. A building permit will need to be obtained for these
signs, as well as any future wall signs on the buildings.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and the following
conditions.

   1. The building and site shall be constructed per the approved plans.
   2. There shall be adequate parking on site for the proposed tenant mix.
   3. The landscaping shall be completed per the approved plan and in accordance with
      applicable standards in the West Valley City Municipal Code including but not limited to
      the Standards for Landscaping Along High Image Arterials.
   4. Signage shall comply with the West Valley City Code.
   5. All requirements of the Development Agreement on the property shall be met.
   6. All requirements of effected departments and agencies shall be met.
   7. Subject to review upon valid complaint

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

Discussion: The applicant, Michelle Krogius stated that she did not have anything else to add. She
then confirmed the landscaping on the site plan will be added.


Motion: Commissioner Lovato motioned for approval of C-6-2020 subject to the 7 staff
alternatives. Commissioner Winters seconded the motion.
