   1. HILLSIDE PERMIT (HS) Administrative

       Consider a request for a hillside development permit for the extension of Flowers Way. The property is
       currently zoned ASBP (Airport Supporting Business Park) & Open Space. The site is located at the end
       of the existing Flowers Way. The applicant is Desert Canyons LLC/Curt Gordon. Case No. 2023-HS-
       004. (Staff – Mike Hadley)

       Mike Hadley presented the following:

       Mike Hadley – Mike showed the location of the road with a vicinity map. Mike presented the slope map.
       The bulk of the road 97 to 98 percent is in that area. There are just a couple of small areas outside of that
       slope range. The Hillside Committee met and recommended approval to Planning Commission with no
       recommendations.

       Chair Kemp – So this would be the frontage road.

       Commissioner Chapman – It appears that there is a small portion of slopes near the roads, this is not for
       approval where the dotted red line is the more significant amount of slope?

       Mike Hadley – That is correct it is well out of that area.

   2. PRELIMINARY PLATS (PP) Administrative

      Consider a request for a four (4) lot residential subdivision known as Old Farm located the north-west
      corner of 2450 South and 3000 East. The property is 76.04 acres and is zoned A-20 and pending R-1-20.
      The applicant is Bush and Gudgell, and the representative is Bob Hermandson. Case No. 2023-PP-005
      (Staff – Dan Boles)

      Dan Boles presented the following:

      Dan Boles – This will look familiar because we looked at a portion of this property with a zone change
      last meeting. Dan described the area where this is located. The zone change that you recommended a
      positive recommendation on will go to City Council this Thursday. If for some reason the City Council
      says no then this preliminary plat will stop here. Lot 1 is proposed to be 14.5 acres, lot 2 is proposed to
      be 5.99 acres. Lots 3 and 4 will meet the A-20 requirements. Lot 2 is proposed to be a church. The next
      phase of this development will be lot 1. We talked to them about the cross section on the 25 ft. Wes
      talked to the applicant about narrowing that down to try and control the speed on the street to address
      some concerns the neighbors had at the zone change meeting. Staff is recommending approval.

      Commissioner Chapman – This is an approval of a 4-lot subdivision. This is not the detail of the one that
      will be going into planning. If they are going to do a trail it will be included in that one?

      Dan Boles – Yes.

      MOTION: Commissioner Rogers made a motion to recommend approval of the preliminary plat Item 2.
      SECOND: Commissioner Chapman
      ROLL CALL VOTE:
      AYES (5)
      Chair Kemp
      Commissioner Andrus
      Commissioner Anderson
      Commissioner Chapman
      Commissioner Rogers
      NAYS (0)
