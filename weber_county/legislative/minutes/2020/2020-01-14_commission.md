 G. ACTION ITEMS:
       1. FINAL READING OF AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING WEBER COUNTY RESIDENTS TO UTILIZE THE URBAN
          FARMING PROVISIONS OF UTAH CODE ANNOTATED PART 59-2-17 – ORDINANCE 2020-1.
            John Ulibarri, County Assessor, noted that in 2019 the Legislature expanded the Urban Farming statute to
            include all counties whereas previously it only included Salt Lake County. Urban farming functions exactly
            like greenbelt with the following exceptions: acreage requirements; production requirement; and application.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Ordinance 2020-1 authorizing Weber County residents to utilize the
            urban farming provisions of Utah Code Annotated Part 59-2-17; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer– aye
       2. RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING A TRUSTEE TO THE BOARD OF THE WEST WEBER-TAYLOR CEMETERY
          DISTRICT – RESOLUTION 2-2020.
            Stacy Skeen, of the Commission Office, stated that she posted the vacancy according to statute and received
            two applications. Roger Heslop has been fulfilling the term of a board member who recently resigned.
            Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 2-2020 reappointing Roger Heslop to the West Weber-Taylor
            Cemetery District through 12/31/2023; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
       3. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN MUSICAL THEATRE ADVISORY BOARD –
          RESOLUTION 3-2020.
            Kassi Bybee, Ogden Eccles Conference Center General Manager, presented this item.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 3-2020 reappointing Taylor Knuth and Mickie Larsen and
            appointing Janette Bischoff, Jackie Hoff, Rhett Long, Pam Parkinson, and Scott Parkinson to the Ogden Musical
            Theatre Advisory Board all through 12/31/2023; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
       4. CONTRACTS FOR SERVICES RELATED TO THE PRODUCTION OF RAGTIME: CALEB SAUNDERS; TERESA
          SANDERSON; OGDEN CHAMBER ORCHESTRA.
            Kassi Bybee, Ogden Eccles Conference Center General Manager, presented this item.
            Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contracts for services related to the production of Ragtime with
            Caleb Saunders, Teresa Sanderson, and the Ogden Chamber Orchestra; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    5. CONTRACT WITH US FOODS, INC. FOR PURCHASING, WAREHOUSING AND DISTRIBUTION FUNCTIONS AND
       OTHER RELATED SERVICES FOR FOOD AND RELATED NON-FOOD PRODUCTS FOR WEBER COUNTY VENUES.
         Jennifer Graham, of County Culture, Parks & Recreation, stated that the county is tag-teaming with the State
         contract through USU for the county to use US Foods. County legal counsel worked on this for close to a year.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with US Foods, Inc., for purchasing, warehousing and
         distribution functions and other related services for food and related non-food products for Weber County
         venues; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    6. AMENDED CONTRACT WITH GRUBER FAMILY BAND FOR ENTERTAINMENT AT 2020 HOF GERMANFEST.
         Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, noted that the band’s flights were more expensive than in the contract.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the amended contract with the Gruber Family Band for entertainment
         at the 2020 Hof Germanfest; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    7. CONTRACT WITH WEBER STATE RODEO FOR HOURLY PRACTICES IN 2020 IN EXCHANGE FOR COMMUNITY SERVICE.
         Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, presented this contract.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Weber State Rodeo for hourly practices in 2020 in
         exchange for community service; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    8. CONTRACT WITH INTERSTATE DETECTIVE AGENCY FOR AN INDIGENT DEFENSE ACT.
         Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, stated that this contract is for investigation services.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Interstate Detective Agency for an Indigent Defense
         Act; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    9. CONTRACT WITH GOLDENWEST CREDIT UNION FOR DETENTION POND MAINTENANCE.
         Tucker Weight, of County Engineering, stated that through this contract the Ogden Valley credit union is to
         maintain its stormwater detention facilities, and it allows the county to go on the property and inspect the
         facilities.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Goldenwest Credit Union for detention pond
         maintenance; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    10. CONTRACT WITH GOLDENWEST CREDIT UNION FOR MAINTENANCE OF THE PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY.
         Tucker Weight, of County Engineering, stated that this agreement is for the credit union to maintain the future
         right-of-way, and once the road goes through the development, the county will take over the maintenance.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contract with Goldenwest Credit Union for the public right-of-
         way; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    11. RESOLUTION APPOINTING A MEMBER TO UTAH ASSOCIATION OF COUNTIES (UAC) BOARD – RESOLUTION 4-2020.
         Chair Froerer noted that currently there were two county commissioners serving on the board, but due to a recent
         by-law change, membership has been reduced to one. Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor, concurred noting
         that for about the past seven years the county has been more represented in proportion to its population.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 4-2020 appointing Commissioner Scott K. Jenkins to the
         Utah Association of Counties Board; Chair Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
