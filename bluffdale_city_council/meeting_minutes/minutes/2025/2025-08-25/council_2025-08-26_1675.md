BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL BOARD OF CANVASSERS MEETING .

1. Roll Call,

In the absence of Mayor Hall, Acting Mayor Pro Tempore Aston called the meeting to order at

4:30 PM.

All members of the City Council were present with the exception of Mayor Hall, Council Member

Lord, who were excused.

2. Consideration and Vote on Resolution 2025-75 certifying the Canvass of the Official
Election Results for the 2025 City of Bluffdale Municipal Primary Election,
Conducted by Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office, Elections Division.

Council Member Austin moved to APPROVE Resolution 2025-75, certifying the Canvass of

the Official Election Results for the 2025 City of Bluffdale Municipal Primary Election.

Council Member Wilding seconded the motion. Vote on motion: Mayor Pro Tempore Aston-

Aye, Council Member Austin- Aye, Council Member Crockett- Aye, Council Member

Wilding- Aye. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

3. Adjournment.

Council Member Aston moved to ADJOURN. Council Member Wilding seconded the

motion. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

The Board of Canvassers Meeting adjourned at 4:32 PM.
