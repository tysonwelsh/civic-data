4:38 P.M.
STUDY MEETING

       A. Welcome, Roll Call, and Introduction: By Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey

Mayor Ramsey welcomed everyone present and introduced the meeting.

       B. Invocation: By Council Member Patrick Harris

Council Member Harris offered the invocation.

       C. Mayor and Council Coordination

Mayor Ramsey brought up Local Officials Day for The League, at The Salt Palace on January
17.

       D. Discussion/Review of Regular Council Meeting

Mayor Ramsey noted that she will be leaving a little early from the council meeting and asked to
amend the agenda for the council meeting to nominate a Mayor Pro Tempore, along with
switching Items H.1. and H.2.

Assistant City Manager Jason Rasmussen reviewed the reasons for wanting to switch testing labs
for drinking water samples, and the reasons for wanting to avoid the bidding process.

Mayor Ramsey continued reviewing the council meeting agenda.

Council Member McGuire asked if there was any additional feedback from the open houses held.

Council Member Shelton added that there have been some challenges with the bike courses at
Bingham Creek, and the board has been consulting with some mountain bike clubs to make some
modifications to the course to allow for better use. He suggested talking to some of those same
groups for input on this proposed course.

City Manager Dustin Lewis responded that three of our city engineers are mountain bike club
coaches, with access to that type of input, and they are the ones who have been helping design
this project.

Council Member Shelton motioned to recess the Combined City Council & Redevelopment
Agency Study Meeting and move to Combined City Council & Redevelopment Agency
Executive Closed Session. Council Member Harris seconded the motion; vote was 5-0,
unanimous in favor.
