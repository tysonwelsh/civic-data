2)
Project #23-125: Address: 3419 Olympic Parkway, Summit County, UT; Parcels PP-63-A (254.40 acres) and PP-65-A (124.94 acres) Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit to improve portions of the Utah Olympic Park hillside into two new ski runs; Applicant: Jamie Kimball representing Utah Athletic Foundation; Senior Planner: Amir Caus, AICP
(6:07 P.M.)
Planner Caus presented project #23-125 to the Commissioners recommended approval as outlined in the staff report.
Applicant, Jamie Kimball, representing the Utah Athletic Foundation addressed the Commission and provided additional information regarding the project.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:17 P.M.
Jorge Velarde was called to speak.
Adam Loomis was called to speak.
James Duckworth was called to speak.
Stacy Mackintosh was called to speak.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:26 P.M.
Jamie Kimball, Applicant, Amir Caus, Planner, and Collin Hilton with the Utah Olympic Legacy Foundation responded to the questions raised by members of the public.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions and provided additional comments.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the UOP CUP for phase three ski runs based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval as found in the staff report. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Makena Hawley. Abstain: Christopher Conabee.
