                                           MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                                            REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY MEETING
                                             Wednesday, April 28, 2021 - 5:30 pm
                                                  Approved June 9, 2021
                            West Jordan City Council Chambers • 8000 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088


   BOARD:          Chair Zach Jacob, Kelvin Green, Chad Lamb, Christopher McConnehey, David Pack, Kayleen
                   Whitelock, and Melissa Worthen
   STAFF:          Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Mayor Dirk Burton, City Administrator Korban Lee,
                   Community Development Director Scott Langford, Economic Development Director Chris
                   Pengra, and City Attorney Robert Wall

   CLOSED SESSION
    1. The West Jordan City Council may temporarily recess the Redevelopment Agency Board
       meeting to convene in a closed session to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real
       property; or the sale of real property as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205
   At 8:45 p.m., Board Member Green moved that the Redevelopment Agency go into a closed session to
   discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property; or the sale of real property as provided by Utah
   Code Annotated §52-4-205 and adjourn from there.
   Board Member Lamb seconded the motion.

   The vote was recorded as follows:
   Council Chair Zach Jacob                       Yes
   Council Vice Chair Kelvin Green                Yes
   Council Member Chad Lamb                       Yes
   Council Member Chris McConnehey                Yes
   Council Member David Pack                      Yes
   Council Member Kayleen Whitelock               Yes
   Council Member Melissa Worthen                 Yes
   The motion passed 7-0

   The closed meeting began at 8:55 pm.

   Board Members discussed the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property as follows:
   RDA Property #1: 8:56 to 9:29 pm
   RDA Property #2: 9:29 to 9:33 pm

    2. ADJOURN
   Board Member Green moved to adjourn the closed session. Board Member McConnehey seconded the motion.
   All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously.
   The closed meeting adjourned at 9:33 pm

I, Cindy Quick, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent an accurate summary of what occurred at the
meeting held on April 28, 2021. This document constitutes the official minutes for the Redevelopment Agency of West
Jordan City.

Cindy M. Quick, MMC, Council Office Clerk

Approved this 9th day of June 2021
