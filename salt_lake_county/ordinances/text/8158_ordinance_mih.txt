ORDINANCE NO.                                                            , 2022 
 
AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING UNINCORPORATED SALT LAKE COUNTY 
MODERATE INCOME HOUSING PLAN UPDATE 
 
 The County legislative body  of Salt Lake County, State of Utah, ordains as follows: 
 
 
 Section 1:  The 2019  Unincorporated Salt Lake County Moderate Income Housing Plan is hereby 
replaced by the 2022  Moderate Income Housing Plan for Unincorporated Salt Lake County , which is 
attached hereto as Exhibit 1  and incorporated herein as if fully set forth.   
 Section 2:  This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at least 
one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Salt L ake County, and if 
not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately upon its first publication.  
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Salt Lake County Council has approved, passed and adopted this 
ordinance this                 day of                                         , 2022.
