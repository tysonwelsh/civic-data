 2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
     Chief Derek Maxfield led participants in the Pledge of Allegiance.


 3. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Jacob opened the public comment period at 6:01 pm.
     Comments:
     Larean Borrowman, West Jordan resident, said she had watched West Jordan grow from a
     farming community to one of the largest cities in Utah. She said caring for her property had
     become more difficult with age. Ms. Borrowman and her husband desired to subdivide their
     property and construct a twin home in which two of their children would live. Her resident
     children would then be available to help care for the property. Ms. Borrowman felt a twin
     home would be the best option for the shape of her property and would be a great benefit and
     personal help.
     Chair Jacob closed public comments at 6:06 pm.


 4. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. City Council Reports
          Council Member Shelton spoke of a recent ride along with Code Enforcement and
          encouraged fellow Council Members to do the same. Council Member Shelton reported
          on a recent ribbon-cutting, and reported on a meeting with two residents regarding

           help the speeding situation.

           Council Member Green believed the meeting with residents regarding traffic speeds was
           productive and he would continue to work with the Mayor to improve the situation.
           Council Member Green reported attending a grand opening. He thanked staff for an
           amazing job during the days leading up to and including election day. Council Member
           Green expressed gratitude to Geographic Information Systems Administrator Clint
           Hutchings and amazing staff whose work helped the Council make data-driven
           decisions.

           Council Member Whitelock thanked the Economic Development team it was exciting to
           see so many businesses coming into West Jordan. She thanked interns in the GIS
           Department for their work.

           Council Member Bloom spoke of time spent with Utility staff in the last week and was
           impressed with all they do to manage the city utilities and was grateful for the
           experience. She encouraged fellow Council Members to do the same.

           Council Member Bedore thanked City staff for their amazing efforts. He thanked Larean
           Borrowman for attending and sharing comments with the Council.

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson announced the upcoming launch of the Adopt-a-
          Storm-Drain Program, with a full-scale rollout planned for the spring.

       c. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Dirk Burton reported on recent and upcoming events and programs in the
          Community. He recognized services provided by West Jordan’s award-winning GIS team,
          and turned time to Geographic Information Systems Administrator Clint Hutchings. Mr.
          Hutchings expressed enthusiasm about his team’s love for their role to provide valuable
          data to enhance various city operations. He described GIS as the combining of data and
          city maps to tell valuable stories.

           Mayor Burton reported that EDC Utah had merged with the Salt Lake Chamber, noted
           the recent renewal of the ZAP Tax, and provided updates on streetlights along Redwood
           Road. Additionally, he informed the Council that the Utah Transit Authority (UTA) had
           accelerated plans for service on 5600 West by one year.

       d. City Administrator’s Report
          None
