 VARIANCES
 B-5-2021                     MR. BOB DANCE, representing the property owner, is requesting a variance from Section
                              7-9-112(1) which states that the required landscaping for parking lots with 5 or more vehicles
                              be 20 feet in depth from property line. Mr. Dance is requesting a reduction from 20 feet to 10
                              feet, thus a variance request to reduce the required landscaping from 20 feet to 10 feet. The
                              property is located at 2601 South Constitution Boulevard and is zoned M (manufacturing).
