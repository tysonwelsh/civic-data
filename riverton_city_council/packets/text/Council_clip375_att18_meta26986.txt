FROM:          Ryan Carter, City Attorney

ITEM:          Public hearing regarding the issuance and sale of not more than
               $18,700,000 aggregate principal amount of water revenue bonds

SUMMARY:

In June of this year, the State of Utah, Department of Natural Resources awarded a loan to
Riverton City in the amount of $11,985,000 to install secondary water meters on all customer
connections (the “Project”). The interest payable is set at the annual rate of 1%. As a condition
to receiving this loan, Riverton City must secure the loan by issuing bonds, secured by revenues
collected as water rate fees. The Department of Natural Resources also expects Riverton City to
provide $2,115,000.00 to the Project. Additional conditions of receiving this loan are enclosed
with a notification letter which Riverton City received from the Department of Natural Resources.

On October 15 of this year, the City Council adopted a resolution authorizing the City to proceed
with the process to issue water revenue bonds which will in turn guarantee the repayment of the
loan. Bond issuance itself is governed by the Local Government Bonding Act, Title 11, Chapter
14, Utah Code (the “Act”). Before closing on a transaction to issue revenue bonds the Act
requires: 1) Riverton City give notice of its intent to issue such bonds; 2) hold a public hearing to
receive input from the public. The public hearing must receive information, if any, regarding the
issuance of the bonds and “what the potential economic impact that the improvement, facility or
property for which the bonds pay all or part of the cost will have on the private sector.” Utah Code
Ann. § 11-14-318 (1) (b) (ii).

City staff has provided notice of its intent to issue the water revenue bonds, in compliance with
the act. The purpose for this item to appear before the Council on the December 3, 2019 meeting
is to hold the public hearing described above. Once the hearing is conducted, no further action
is required at the meeting of December 3.
