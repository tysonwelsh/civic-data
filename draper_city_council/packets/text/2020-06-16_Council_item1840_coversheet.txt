Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: June 16, 2020
Re: Resolution 20-29 Comments: A municipality has authority to close roads falling within its jurisdiction as defined by and pursuant to the Utah Highway Jurisdiction and Classification Act as codified by Utah Code §72-3-104. The city may close any class C road to ensure the health, safety, and welfare of its citizens. Closing this portion of Upper Corner Canyon Road to vehicular traffic enhances the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper because the road is located in the wildland urban interface zone where potential for a wildfire is high, and because vehicular use of roads is a significant contributing cause of human-caused wildfires. This resolution closes a portion of Upper Corner Canyon Road to vehicular traffic pursuant to the authority of the city to do so.
