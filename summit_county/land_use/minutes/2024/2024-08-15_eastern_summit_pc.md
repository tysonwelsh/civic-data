Continued public hearing from August 1, 2024, and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for the property located at 7550 Lower Bowl Road, Browns Canyon, Summit County, Utah. Parcel: SS-70-11 (44.59 acres) Applicant: Kylee Mimbach; Administrative Review. Project #23-194. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.
