                              RESOLUTION NO. 22-24

            A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING
             THE DRAPER PLAZA FIRST AMENDMENT TO AGREEMENT

WHEREAS, the City entered into an Agreement with Draper Plaza, L.L.C., dated April
4, 1999, for the development of property located at approximately 11400 South
State Street (the “Property”); and

WHEREAS, the Agreement addresses the development of the property as a planned
commercial center known as Draper Plaza (the “Project”); and

WHEREAS, the Agreement adopted development requirements for the Project; and

WHEREAS, since entering the Agreement the City Council has amended the Draper
City Municipal Code such that the uses associated with the Project are permitted
uses and no longer require a conditional use permit; and

WHEREAS, the City and the successor-in-interest to Draper Plaza, L.L.C., desire to
amend the Agreement in accordance with the terms and conditions of the First
Amendment to Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, approving the First Amendment to the Agreement promotes the health,
safety, and welfare of the residents of Draper City

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

Section 1. Amended Agreement. The Draper City Council approves the First
Amendment to Agreement attached as Exhibit A and authorizes the Mayor to
execute the same on behalf of Draper City.

Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage by the City Council.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 17TH DAY OF MAY 2022.
