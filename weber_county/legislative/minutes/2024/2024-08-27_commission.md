D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: I have a follow up story about my brother who ran a 100 mile
   race where his pacer ran 43 miles with him. That same brother had a massive stroke after a 50k. He walked out
   of rehab yesterday to go home and finish his recovery. His recovery was going so well, his therapist offered to
   let him walk and ride bikes on some of his favorite trails. One Sunday they went to the river parkway after a rain
   and the trail was slick. His friends lined the trail on the cliff side to catch him if he fell. I asked him who those
   people were and he said his running friends. It just goes to show how important it is to have good people around
   you. We always have good times with good friends but sometimes we have bad times and we need good friends.
