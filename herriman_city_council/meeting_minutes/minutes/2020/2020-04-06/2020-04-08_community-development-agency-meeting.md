                            *This Meeting will be conducted Electronically*

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City. The meeting
was held on Wednesday, April 8, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman
Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s
website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: David Watts

Directors Present:         Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: City Manager Brett Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder
               Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, Director of Operations Monte Johnson, Public Works
               Director Justun Edwards, City Attorney John Brems, Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette,
               City Planner Michael Maloy, Assistant City Planner Bryn MacDonald, Police Lieutenant Cody
               Stromberg, and Unified Fire Authority Precinct Chief Dom Burchett.


 7:00 PM: (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER)
    1. Call to Order
    Chair David Watts called the meeting to order at 7:46 p.m.

                 1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of April
                       8, 2020
Director Smith moved to have the minutes of April 8, 2020 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Community Development
and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary, if after ten days,
there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes are made and the
board is in agreement. Director Shields seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

     2. Discussion and Action Items
             2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution approving an Interlocal Cooperative Agreement
                  between Herriman City and the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman
                  City regarding the Herriman Innovation District property tax – Heather Upshaw, Economic
                  Development Manager
Note: Item 2.1 and 2.2 were discussed simultaneously.

Economic Development Manager Heather Upshaw offered a brief overview of the Herriman Innovation District
Community Reinvestment Project Area. She noted staff had changed the language in the Participation Agreement to
reflect the reimbursement of infrastructure costs rather than having it notated as an incentive. Manager Upshaw added
Salt Lake County and the Unified Fire Service Area have not approved Interlocal Cooperative Agreements for this
project; however, there would be enough funds to be able to reimburse the following infrastructure costs: Midas Creek
Storm Drain Improvements, Rocky Mountain Power main line extension, Dominion Energy main line extension,
11800 South improvements and 7080 West improvements.

Director Ohrn moved to approve Resolution No. R2020-02 approving an Interlocal Cooperative Agreement between Herriman City and
the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City regarding the Herriman Innovation District property tax. Director
Smith seconded the motion.

The vote is recorded as follows:
Director Jared Henderson                          Aye
Director Steven Shields                           Aye
Director Sherrie Ohrn                             Aye
Director Clint Smith                              Aye
Chair David Watts                                 Aye
The motion passed unanimously.
