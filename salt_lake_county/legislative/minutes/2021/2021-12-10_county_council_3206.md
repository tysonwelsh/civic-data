1. CALL TO ORDER
Order of Salt Lake County Council Chair Steve DeBry Calling a Special Meeting of the 
Salt Lake County Council on December 10, 2021 at 09:30 AM.
Dear Colleagues:
Consistent with Utah Code Ann. §17-53-205, Section 2.09 of the Optional Plan for Salt 
Lake County Government, and Section 2.04.090 of Salt Lake County Ordinance, I hereby 
exercise my prerogative as the Chair of the Salt Lake County Council to order a special 
meeting of the Salt Lake County Council to be held on December 10, 2021, at 09:30 AM 
in room N1-100 of the Salt Lake County Government Center.
The purpose of the proposed meeting will be for the Salt Lake County Council to 
formally initiate proceedings to amend ordinance, pursuant to Utah Code Section 
17-27a-508(1)(a)(ii)(B), by submitting proposed amendment to planning commission for 
recommendation pursuant to Salt Lake County Code Section 19.90.01. The proposed 
ordinance will amend section 19.12.030 of the Salt Lake County Code to eliminate mineral 
extraction and processing as a conditional use and explicitly prohibit the same and similar 
use in the Forestry and Recreation zones; amend section 19.72-190 to eliminate mineral 
extraction and processing as a use for which waivers can be granted; amend section 
19.76.030 regarding classification of permitted and conditional uses not listed in title 19; 
and enact related regulations. A copy of the proposed agenda for this special meeting is 
attached to this order.
Council staff shall provide notice of this special meeting to the Salt Lake County Council 
and Salt Lake County Mayor, and shall also provide notice to the public and media as 
required by the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-101 et seq ., 
and Salt Lake County Ordinance. A copy of this order shall be included in the minutes of 
the special meeting.

Council Member Stringham  asked legal counsel if this would affect 
anybody who is currently operating under this code. 
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated he believes the 
existing uses in the zone would likely be grandfathered in under the 
nonconforming-use doctrine. There is a statute in Utah Code that allows 
existing uses prior to changes to carry on if certain criteria are established. 
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated this is an important special meeting and if 
the County did not act boldly and swiftly, it would end up with outcomes 
that threaten our quality of life.  This amendment is necessary to ensure 
preservation, safe recreation, and the mitigation of health risks to Salt Lake 
County residents. 
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council
