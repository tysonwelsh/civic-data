2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Ms. Susan Sandack  thanked the Council for the financial support it has offered to 
the Sundance Institute over the years.  These contributions have made the festival 
affordable for people in the community.  She suspected the Sundance Film Festival 
left Utah due to the State Legislature ’s unwelcoming positions on diversity, equity, 
and inclusion issues.  However, she still felt the Council should be gracious and 
keep its funding promise of $150,000 for 2025.

Investigations Unit, on March 18th, 2025, for its role in solving a homicide that

Presenters: SLCo Sheriff Rosie Rivera. SLCo Chief Deputy Matt Dumont.

Discussion/Direction
Chief Matthew Dumont  delivered a PowerPoint presentation entitled Salt 
Lake County Sheriff ’s Office Funding Request to Open Oxbow Jail ’s Third 
Pod April 1, 2025.  He reviewed the reason why the third pod was being 
opened; what it will take to open the pod; a cost breakdown; the opening 
timeline; and an update on the state hospital contract.  Chief Dumont noted 
the County population has increased by 300,000 residents since new jail beds 
were added.  
Council Member Winder Newton  stated implementing the “fifth fifth” to 
create more jail beds was only a short -term solution.  It is inefficient to 
transport laundry and food back and forth between the two jails.  The Metro 
Jail must be expanded.  She did not want to go against the will of the voters, 
but she was willing to put this issue back on the ballot in another two years.
Sheriff Rosie Rivera  stated population growth, along with crime 
enhancements that were implemented during the last legislative session, will 
result in a greater need for more jail beds.  This one pod will not be enough.  
Council Member Stewart  stated he believed the jail bond ballot initiative 
failed because too much was put into it.
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated the County has been working on 
investments that go beyond merely creating more jail beds.  These 
investments include affordable housing and mental health services.  She 
emphasized the need for systemic reform.

8.8 Budget Adjustment: Flood Control Requests to Transfer 
$125,000 from the Surplus Canal Rehabilitation Project to 
Flood Control Engineering's Operating Budget for the 
Purchase of Two Remote-Operated Robotic Mowers
25-2752
Attachments: Staff Report
34507 - Surplus Canal Mowers
Presenter: Hoa Nguyen, Council Budget and Policy Analyst.
(Approx. 2:15PM, Less than 5 Min.)
Discussion/Direction
Ms. Hoa Nguyen , Budget and Policy Analyst, Council Office, reviewed the 
budget adjustment.
Council Member Bradshaw  asked if under -expend from the completed 
canal rehabilitation project was being used for this purchase.
Mr. Kade Moncur , Director, Engineering and Flood Control Division, 
stated the canal rehabilitation project was ongoing.  It requires the division to 
remove and maintain vegetation on 18 miles of levee.  These remote control 
operated robotic mowers would be very helpful in removing weeds that can 
grow to be eight feet tall.  The work to keep the weeds under control is 
intensive and constant, and purchasing the mowers would free up heavy 
equipment operators for tasks that require a higher level of skill.  While the 
mowers would be used primarily for the canal rehabilitation project, the 
division will be able to use them for other facilities as well.  The funds need 
to be moved from the project budget to the equipment budget.  
Council Member Winder Newton asked how much the mowers cost.  
Mr. Moncur stated they cost around $60,000 each.
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council 
Member Stewart, that this agenda item be approved. The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.

8.12 Consideration of Rescission of the 2025 Sundance 
Contribution of $150,000
25-2764

Harrison. 
(Approx. 2:40PM, 5 Min.)

Council Member Theodore stated during the budget discussion that took 
place last fall, it was decided that if the Sundance Film Festival left Utah, the 
funds allocated by the Council would be rescinded.  This decision was not 
meant to be punitive in any way; it had already been previously determined.
Council Member Harrison  stated the Council just went through a very tight 
budget year, and it is important to her to invest in organizations that have a 
long-term commitment to the County.  This was about fiscal responsibility .  
She noted this contribution was separate from the $ 130,000 of ZAP funding 
that the Sundance Film Festival received.
Council Member Pinkney  stated she was not present during the budget 
discussion, so she would likely vote “nay” to rescinding the contribution.
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated that from a personal perspective, and as a 
former Sundance employee, this was a sad moment for her.  A lot of Utah ’s 
recognition as a state has come through the Sundance brand.  As a mayor, 
she assured everyone that the County will backfill the economic impact of 
this, just as it did when Outdoor Retailer left Utah.  The nature of 
independent film has changed greatly and there is simply less locally created 
talent to be acquired.  Still, she believed in the art of story -telling, and there 
are still many opportunities to be had.

1.06.020 - Holidays.
A. The following days are legal holidays during which County offices are 
closed:
The First Day of January New Year's Day
The Third Monday of January Martin Luther King Day
The Third Monday of February President’s Day
The Last Monday in May Memorial Day
The Nineteenth Day of June Juneteenth Independence Day
The Fourth Day of July Independence Day
The Twenty-fourth Day of July Pioneer Day
The First Monday of September Labor Day
The Eleventh Day of November Veteran’s Day
The Fourth Thursday of November Thanksgiving
The Fourth Friday of November Day After Thanksgiving
The Twenty-fifth Day of December Christmas Day

preceding Friday. In the event that a holiday falls on a Sunday, it shall be 
observed on the following Monday. [ If June 19 is on a Tuesday,

19 is on a Saturday or Sunday, the Juneteenth Independence Day holiday

C. Where public business requires, some County offices may be required to 
remain open for business on legal holidays.
