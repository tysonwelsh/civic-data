2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Hutchings Museum Annual Report
Daniela Larsen, Hutchings Museum Director, presented on the museum’s programs what they’ve
accomplished in the last year. She also reported on the upcoming events for the year.

Power Purchase Plans update was heard next.

2.3 Power Purchase Plans update
Dean Lundell, Finance Director, stated that there are issues with acquiring resources for power
and natural gas increases that have increased the cost of power. They are working on a contract
to lock in the cost of natural gas for the next 5 years, and they are requiring a letter of credit for
about $2 million. This will allow for stability in pricing. It’s not something that the Council
needs to approve, but it’s a big enough issue that he wants to make the Council aware of it.
Crystal Robinson reported that they are working to hedge into more security and limit market
risk. She also presented on future power market options.

2.2 Fiber Network
Shaye Ruitenbeek gave an update on the fiber network project.

2.4 Water Management Plan Presentation
