Near 14600 South and Noell Nelson Drive is King's Cross Brixton. The owners of the property

Industrial zone. The RDA successfully partnered with Covent Garden to make the old gravel road

Phase 2 to extend the street to Noell Nelson Drive. Funding is available with five property owners,
