                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-53

    A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE RIVERTON CITY
            PERSONNEL POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the
“Manual”) is used to set acceptable parameters for employment operations, and define employee
benefits; and

       WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or
in employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined some miscellaneous
terms need to be updated to remain current with existing personnel operations on matters related
to procedures for invoking voluntary retirement; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would set clearer standards by which an employee voluntarily
retires;

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual which describe the terms and conditions by which
the City’s employees may receive benefits described in this Resolution.


        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual, Section 2.13,
          Termination Procedures for Specific Types of Retirement attached to this Resolution
          is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution. The Mayor is hereby
          directed to ensure that the Manual is accordingly revised and made available to all City
          employees within a reasonable time from adoption of this Resolution.


       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

2.13 TERMINATION PROCEDURES FOR SPECIFIC TYPES OF TERMINATION

VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT
Employees who desire retirement shall notify Riverton City three (3) months in advance. The City
Manager may authorize an employee to provide a shorter notification of an employee’s retirement, but
solely upon the City Manager’s determination that the employee’s need to retire at a sooner date is
medically necessary. The City Manager shall find that an employee’s retirement prior to tendering a
three-month notice if the employee can demonstrate s/he would qualify for leave under the Family
Medical Leave Act at any time during the hypothetical 3 month notification period.

Riverton City should communicate the status of each employee's retirement benefits upon request.
Riverton City should carefully explain to the employee what options are available to the employee upon
retirement (such as Cobra and Retirement Plan Options). Riverton City should give the employee ample
time to review the retirement plan. Riverton City should have the employee sign a release, or at least a
declaration statement, to the effect that they are electing retirement of their own free will.

After a five (5) year vesting period with the City, and if the employee complies with the 3 month
notification or is granted an exception by the City Manager as described in this section, an employee will
receive a one-time payment equal to five percent (5%) of their current annual wage in their final check
when the employee is retiring from Riverton City and also retires under the Utah Retirement System
(URS). Once an employee is vested, this retirement incentive will increase one-percent (1%) for each
year following vesting, up to a max of ten percent (10%) after ten (10) years of continued employment.
The incentive is not prorated but will only be awarded after each completed year.
