                            National Vietnam Veterans Day 2023

WHEREAS, the United States Congress in 2017 set aside March 29th as "National Vietnam War
Veterans Day" to honor Vietnam Veterans and their families for their service and sacrifices
during the Vietnam War from November 1, 1955 to May 15, 1975; and

WHEREAS, Commemoration of the 50th Anniversary of the Vietnam War Presidential
proclamations and National Vietnam Veterans Day State Proclamations have been made; and

WHEREAS, millions of Americans served their country in various capacities during this period
of war, demonstrating courage, patriotism, and altruism; and

WHEREAS, we pay our respects to those who went missing, those who have not returned, and
those who became casualties of war to be remembered always; and

WHEREAS, the heroism of Vietnam veterans should never be forgotten; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim March 29th,
2023, as National Vietnam War Veterans Day in Riverton and urge our community to
remember and recognize the sacrifices of all Vietnam veterans, including those residing in
Riverton.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 21st day of March 2023.
