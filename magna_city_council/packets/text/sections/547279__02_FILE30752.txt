                                            PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is requesting approval to build a secondary water storage reservoir next to the existing water
towers.
****Review of the landscaping and fencing requirements for the Conditional Use Requested -Secondary Water Storage
Reservior.

                             SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
This property is located in the southwest corner of Magna. The surrounding land to the north and west is part of the newly
approved Little Valley Gateway project.
The property to the direct south is Unincorporated County and is currently vacant.
The new High School will be built just to the northwest of this site.



                                PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
The Planning Staff is recommending approval of the proposed fencing (see sample picture below) to be placed
around the perimeter of the project. Staff also believes the applicant should not be required to landscape the
project area, but should reclaim the property to its original vegetation state, since it will be behind an opaque
fence.
