                                                               FY26 Budget Amendment




Honorable Mayor and Council,


Background
The City utilizes specialized camera equipment mounted in a truck to inspect storm water systems. This
equipment allows staff to identify deficiencies and needed repairs proactively, helping to address issues
before storm events occur. This process significantly reduces the City’s exposure to claims related to
storm-related damages.
The current camera system was purchased in 2017 and has an estimated useful life of approximately
10–15 years. However, the equipment has experienced ongoing operational failures and is no longer
functioning properly. As a result, inspections have been delayed, limiting the City’s ability to proactively
assess storm water infrastructure.
Over the past two years, the City has spent approximately $9,400 on repairs to the existing camera
system, and it is currently inoperable. A recent estimate to repair the system is approximately $15,000.
Due to the age of the equipment, replacement parts would need to be custom manufactured,
contributing to the high repair cost with no guarantee of long-term reliability.
Recommendation
Staff recommends approving an amendment to the FY 2025–2026 budget within the Storm Water CIP
Fund (460) to increase budgeted expenditures by $155,000 for the purchase of a new camera inspection
system. This purchase will be funded using available fund balance.
The existing truck will be repurposed to accommodate the new camera system, avoiding the additional
expense of purchasing a new vehicle and resulting in cost savings to the City.
