G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. APPROVAL OF AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY AND
        THE MARRIOTT-SLATERVILLE COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AGENCY TO REBATE 60%
        OF NEW TAXES COLLECTED, UP TO $1.5 MILLION OVER THE NEXT 15 YEARS.

            Roger Brunker shared that everything is in order for the interlocal agreement.
            Commissioner Froerer has concerns about affordable housing plan and how to encourage cities to implement
            such housing when economic development pursued. Will support approval when affordable housing plan
            provided.

            Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Inter-local Cooperation Agreement with Marriott-Slaterville
            Community Reinvestment Agency to rebate 60% of new taxes collected, up to $1.5 million over the next 15
            years with the added requirement that an Affordable Housing Plan be provided; Commissioner Froerer
            seconded with comment that the affordable housing plan needs details related to zoning, multi-family
            housing/entry-level housing, and details going beyond 10% requirement; await signing for plan.
            Resolution will be brought back for vote later and interlocal voted on today.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
