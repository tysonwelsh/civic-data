                                   EMIGRATION CANYON
                                   RESOLUTION 2024-07-01

   A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS OF THE EMIGRATION PLANNING
       COMMISSION TO ENSURE STAGGERED EXPIRATION OF TERMS

WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon code 19.05.010 requires the Mayor, with the advice and
consent of the City Council, to appoint five members to the Emigration Canyon Planning
Commission (“Commission”); and
WHEREAS, the terms of the terms for Planning Commission members is from March 1st of
each year for a three year term, which term shall expire on March 1st of the last year of the term;
and
WHEREAS, the terms of at least one member, and not more than two, shall expire each year.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF EMIGRATION
CANYON, UTAH that the following individuals are hereby appointed as members of the
Emigration Planning Commission with terms as follows:
   •   Andrew Wallace for a ____ year term. (expiring March 1, 20__)
   •   Jim Karkut for a ____ year term. (expiring March 1, 20__)
   •   Dale Berreth for a ____ year term. (expiring March 1, 20__)
   •   Jodi Geroux for a ____ year term. (expiring March 1, 20__)
   •   Tim Harpts for a ____ year term. (expiring March 1, 20__)

This Resolution shall take effect immediately

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Emigration Canyon, Utah, City Council this _____ day of
________________ in the year 2024.
