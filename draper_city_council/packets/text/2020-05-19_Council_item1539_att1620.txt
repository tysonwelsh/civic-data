                                    ORDINANCE NO. 1421


AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 14 OF THE
DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING STOPPING, STANDING, AND
PARKING.


       WHEREAS, Title 14 regulates vehicle traffic; and

        WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to revise certain terms of the Draper City
Municipal Code to address provisions to provide for the health, safety, and welfare of the
citizens of Draper; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text amendment and finds good
cause to revise the terms and provisions of Title 14.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following findings
to amend Title 14 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) The proposed amendment is in
compliance with the Utah Code Annotated. 2) The proposed amendment is in the best interest of
the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.

        Section 2. Revision. Title 14 of the Draper City Municipal Code is hereby amended to
read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

        Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to correct any
punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to submitting the Ordinance
to Sterling Codifiers.

        Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final
passage.

14-5-010: RESTRICTIONS AS TO SPECIFIED AREAS:

  A. For purposes of this section, an intersection is defined as the area embraced within the
     prolongation or connection of the lateral curb lines, or, if none, then the lateral boundary
     lines of the roadways of two or more roads, alleys, or highways that join one another.
     Intersections begin at a stop bar, or in the absence of a stop bar, at the terminus of the
     points of curvature for the adjacent sidewalk or landscaping. See the diagram labeled “Fig. 1”
     below.

  B. This section covers restrictions and exceptions in addition to, or more restrictive than, those
     covered in the Utah State Traffic Code. Parking or stopping a vehicle is prohibited:

         1. In front of or within five feet of a public or private driveway;

         2. Within an intersection or within thirty feet of any intersection;

         3. Upon a highway in such a manner that parking or stopping leaves less than twelve
            feet of unobstructed street width for free movement of vehicular traffic;

         4. Within a marked traffic travel lane;

         5. On any highway or alley where the width of the highway or alley is less than twenty
            feet; or

         6. In a marked shoulder or bike lane or designated parking area if any portion of the
            vehicle protrudes over or upon a solid white edge line of a traffic travel lane.

  C. The restrictions of subsection (B) above shall not apply:

         1. When necessary to avoid conflict with other traffic; or

         2. When parking or stopping in compliance with the directions of a police officer or
            traffic control device.

14-5-020: WINTER PARKING:

No person shall park or leave a vehicle or trailer on any highway while snow is on the street from
November 1 through April 30. Vehicles or trailers that are parked on a highway in violation of the
provisions of this section may be removed at the owner’s expense.


14-5-030: PARKING FOR CERTAIN PURPOSES:

No person shall park a vehicle upon any highway within the jurisdiction of Draper City for the
principal purpose of greasing or repairing such vehicle, other than repairs necessitated by an
emergency.

14-5-040: PROHIBITED PARKING OF VEHICLES LEAKING FLUIDS:

No person shall park on any highway any vehicle which is leaking any measurable quantity of oil,
grease, transmission fluid, radiator coolant, battery acid or any other fluid, other than water, onto the
street. Owners of vehicles parked in violation of this section are guilty of an infraction.

14-5-050: STORING VEHICLE ON STREET:

Except as otherwise provided herein, no person shall park a vehicle or other conveyance upon any
highway within the jurisdiction of Draper City for more than a continuous twenty four (24) hour
period.

14-5-060: HAZARDOUS PARKING:

No persons shall stop, stand or park a vehicle at any hazardous or congested place within the
jurisdiction of Draper City as properly designated by the city with official signs or markings. In no
event shall any person stop or park a vehicle in such a manner as to interfere with the free flow of
traffic along the highway upon which it is parked.

14-5-070: TRUCK PARKING:

No person shall park any vehicle having a total gross weight, loaded or unloaded, in excess of ten
thousand (10,000) pounds, or having a total length in excess of twenty four feet (24'), within any
residential zone or along any collector or arterial street within the jurisdiction of the city, except while
actually loading or unloading cargo on or in said vehicle.

14-5-080: OWNER'S RESPONSIBILITY FOR PARKING VIOLATION:

The person in whose name a vehicle is registered shall be prima facie responsible for violations of
this chapter and subject to the penalties of any ordinance prohibiting or restricting parking.

14-5-090: PENALTY:

Except as otherwise provided, any violation of this chapter shall be an infraction. This section shall
not limit the authority of any other relief as may be appropriate and lawful under the laws of the state
of Utah. Any person receiving multiple notices for violating the parking provisions of this chapter, or
those set forth in Utah Code Annotated Title 41, within the jurisdiction of Draper City, within a one
hundred eighty (180) day period, which have not been dismissed, may be subject to additional fines.
