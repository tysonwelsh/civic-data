                                  history of her family's property which was purchased in 1993. The parcel at the time was

                                  unaware the zoning designation change of the property. She also spoke about finding
                                  solutions and working with the city to ensure compliance of their property and the
                                  structures located on the property.

                                  Public comment opened.
                                  Public comment closed.

                                  designation, setbacks, the impact to the neighborhood of rezoning the property. The
                                  applicant further shared with the Council, that this property was designed for his family to

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the Historic Committee. The
                      Sandy Youth Council met with the Public Works Department at their last meeting and
                      took a tour of the facility. She attended the coat drive kickoff event at River Oaks and the
                      Coffee with Cops gathering. Both events went very well.

                      Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer requested an item to be added to a future agenda for
                      the discussion of the city's financial reporting requirements for political PACs.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. The Guild
                      is planning four productions for 2026. She attended the recent UDOT press conference.
                      UDOT issued 22,000 stickers to drivers that had their cars and tires inspected and met
                      the requirements to drive the canyon roads during the winter. She provided an update
                      from the Legislative Policy Committee (LPC) and briefed the Council on some of the
                      upcoming legislative issues expected to be discussed in the 2026 session: property tax
                      restrictions, housing, homelessness, and cost of living.She also provided an update from
                      the Salt Lake Convention and Visitors Bureau and discussed the possible expansion of
                      the Mountain America Expo Center to accommodate larger convention.

                      Council Member Brooke D'Sousa expressed her gratitude to her fellow Council Members,
                      Administration, staff and residents. She was looking forward to serving on the Council for
                      the next four years and was excited about what the future holds.

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Monica Zoltanski announced the retirements of Police Chief Greg Severson, Parks
                      and Recreation Department Head, Dan Medina, and Deputy Mayor Kim Bell. She wished
                      them well in their retirement. The kickoff event for the coat drive and the coffee with cops
                      events both went very well. She expressed gratitude to the residents on her re-election
                      and is honored to serve a second term. She called for the need for better accountability
                      and financial reporting requirements of political PACs.
