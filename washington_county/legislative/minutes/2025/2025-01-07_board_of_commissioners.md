WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JANUARY 7, 2025

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on January 7, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow,
County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Gil Almquist was excused.
INVOCATION BY: Greg Wright, West Side Baptist Church
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Cadet Conner Ricks, Junior ROTC

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3421, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_ VENUE OPERATOR AGREEMENT FOR THE SOUTHERN UTAH SHOOTING
SPORTS PARK

Legacy Park Director Susi Lafaele explained that Washington County owns the Southern Utah
Shooting Sports Park with independent operators running the various shooting disciplines offered
at Shooting Sports Park. This agreement would be for a one-year term with Red Rock Bowman.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3421, A
Resolution Approving a Venue Operator Agreement for the Southern Utah
Shooting Sports Park. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3422, A RESOLUTION APPROVIN G
WASHINGTON COUNTY TO ASSUME ST. GEORGE CITY’S REVENUE PLEDGE
FOR TUACAHN CENTER FOR THE ARTS

Mr. Snow stated that in 2015, St. George City pledged $25,000 per year to provide Tuacahn with
financial support. The city pledged $25,000 per year for twenty years, and Washington County
will now assume the remaining ten years of the city’s pledge.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3421, A
Resolution Approving Washington County to Assume St. George City’s
Revenue Pledge for Tuacahn Center for the Arts. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3423, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_LEASE FOR A LOCAL CONGRESSIONAL OFFICE IN THE COUNTY
ADMINISTRATION BUILDING

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw stated that Senator John Curtis office would like to lease
office space in the Washington County Administration Building, occupying the space that was
recently vacated by Senator Mitt Romney. The lease would be for a term of 72 months, subject to
attorney review.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3423, A
Resolution Approving a Lease for a Local Congressional Office in the County
Administration Building. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. __R-2025-3425, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING A BOARD MEMBER TO THE HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL
SERVICE DISTRICT ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD

Mr. Snow explained that Councilmember Clark Fawcett from Hurricane City recently stepped
down from the Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District (HVFSSD) Administrative Control
Board to serve in other capacities. Hurricane City recently nominated Drew Ellerman to serve on
the board to fill the vacancy left by Councilman Fawcett’s resignation, with a term ending on
December 31, 2027.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3425, A
Resolution Appointing a Board Member to the Hurricane Valley Fire Special
Service District Administrative Control Board. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2025-3426, A RESOLUTION OF
OPPOSITION TO THE TRANSFER OF ADDITIONAL PRIVATE LAND TO THE USS.
BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT

Mr. Snow stated in early 2024, a private landowner sold approximately 715 acres in Northwestern
Washington County located near the Cougar Canyon and Slaughter Creek areas to The Wilderness
Land Trust. The Trust has applied to donate the private parcels to the U.S. Bureau of Land
Management (BLM). Under the Washington County Resource Management Plan, the county
opposes the creation of new wilderness areas in the county. This resolution formally opposes the
donation.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3426, A
Resolution of Opposition to the Transfer of Additional Private Land to the
U.S. Bureau of Land Management. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and
Snow voting aye.
