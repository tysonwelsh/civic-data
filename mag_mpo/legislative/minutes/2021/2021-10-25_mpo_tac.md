TIP Amendment: Saratoga Springs- Foothill Blvd. - Bob Allen, MAG The scope of the Foothill Blvd. project is to build a new 3 lane road from Pony Express Pkwy to Lariat Blvd. The project creates an additional north south connection through the area and will alleviate significant congestion on Redwood Rd. The completed project will eventually become half of a collector system for a future major arterial. The project is currently 95% designed and is underfunded. Saratoga Springs is asking that an additional $2.9m of contingency funds be added to the project. Original MAG Funds $8.5m Contingency Funds $2.9m SS Local Funds $4.9m Project Total $16.3m Project Selected: 2020 Total Awarded: $8,500,000 County Funds MPO Technical Advisory Committee October 25, 2021 Page 3 MAG Staff recommend the requested funds be added to the project. Aaron Wilson moved that TAC recommend $2.9m be added to the Foothill Blvd Project. Richard Nielson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Utah Valley Express (UVX) Before and After Experience - Eric Rasband, UDOT Just before UVX was built, the Regional Transportation agencies UDOT, UTA, MAG and WFRC realized we had a unique opportunity to measure the impact that building and operating a bus rapid transit (BRT) line would have on a corridor and surrounding areas. A study was commissioned to provide us with a data driven approach to evaluate its effectiveness and to guide us in the potential assessment and implementation of future additional BRT lines. UDOT has been leading this study effort which is still ongoing and provided a preliminary report on some of the findings. Utah Road and Transit Cost per Trip Study - Shawn Seager, MAG An overview of the joint study between the MAG, UDOT, UTA and WFRC was provided. The purpose of the Road and Transit Cost Study is to provide agencies, policy makers, and the public with clear, neutral information on relative transportation costs as they discuss transportation options and trade-offs. MAG Staff ask TAC to consider cost per trip by mode, space available for new capacity and community vision when selecting transportation projects.
