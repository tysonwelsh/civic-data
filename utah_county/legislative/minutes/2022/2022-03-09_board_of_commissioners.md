18. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF AGREEMENT WITH FAIRFIELD CITY

FOR TRCC/TRT EXPENDITURE OF FUNDS FOR CAMP FLOYD MUSEUM.
-Adam Beck, Attorney

(Timestamp: 9:21)

COMMISSIONER LEE - THIS HAS BEEN A LONG TIME COMING. I’M GLAD THAT
WE'RE FINALLY AT THIS SPOT.

MAYOR BRAD GURNEY, FAIRFIELD TOWN - I THINK THIS PROCESS HAS TAKEN
ABOUT 7 YEARS. WHEN WE ASKED FOR THIS ORIGINALLY IT WAS JUST BEFORE
COVID AND WE THOUGHT WE WOULD HAVE ENOUGH MONEY TO BUILD IT, BUT
AS COVID CAME UP IT BLEW OUR BUDGET SO WE HAD TO GO BACK. ONE THING
THAT SAVED US IS THAT I WENT TO THE STATE LEGISLATURE AND ASKED FOR
MONEY. THEY GRANTED US $350,000. WE WERE SUPPOSED TO RECEIVE THAT IN
JULY AND THEY GAVE IT TO SOMEONE ELSE THAT THEY WEREN'T SUPPOSED TO
GIVE IT TO. FINALLY AFTER SOME WORK AND APPLYING FOR 2 MORE GRANTS,
WE FINALLY GOT THE $350,000 FROM THE STATE.

WE WOULD LIKE TO ASK FOR $400,000 WITH $325,000 GOING TOWARDS THE
BUILDING AND $75,000 GOING TOWARDS EXHIBITS. THAT WILL PUT US WHERE
WE NEED TO BE TO GET THE MUSEUM OPEN. THERE’S STILL MORE MONEY THAT
WE'LL NEED FOR MORE EXHIBITS IN THE FUTURE. I THINK STATE PARKS WILL
HELP WITH THAT IN THE FUTURE.

DR. ROGER BLOOMQUEST, FRIENDS GROUP BOARD - (EXPLAINED THE
HISTORICAL IMPORTANCE OF CAMP FLOYD) UTAH PLAYED AN ABSOLUTE
CRITICAL POINT IN AMERICAN HISTORY BEFORE THE CIVIL WAR AND WHAT
WAS HAPPENING HERE HELPED SET UP HOW THE CIVIL WAR WAS FOUGHT. WE
HAD THE LARGEST MILITARY FORT IN THE COUNTRY. CAMP FLOYD WAS THE
LARGEST MILITARY ESTABLISHMENT IN THE COUNTRY BEFORE THE CIVIL WAR.

COMMISSIONERS, MAYOR GURNEY AND DR. BLOOMQUEST DISCUSSED THE
HISTORY SUCH AS PONY EXPRESS, BASEBALL, MASONS, SOLDIER SUMMIT, ETC.

MICHELLE TUCKER, FRIENDS GROUP VICE-PRESIDENT - I LOVE TO LOOK AT THE
WOMEN’S HISTORY. THE STAGE COACH IS ONE OF THE MOST WELL-PRESERVED.
I'VE STUDIED INN’S AND PONY EXPRESS STATIONS ALL ACROSS THE OVERLAND
TRAIL. MOST ARE GONE, FORGOTTEN OR PRIVATELY OWNED AND THIS IS A REAL
TREASURE.

COMMISSIONER LEE: MOTION TO APPROVE THE AMOUNT OF $400,000
COMMISSIONER GARDNER: SECOND
AYE: ALL IN FAVOR

PASSED: 3/0
Agreement 2022 - 198
