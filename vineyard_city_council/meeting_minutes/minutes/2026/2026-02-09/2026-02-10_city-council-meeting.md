3.   PRESENTATIONS/RECOGNITIONS/AWARDS/PROCLAMATIONS

4.   WORK SESSION
     4.1. HOA / Neighborhood Committee
          Councilmember Wood will lead a discussion on establishing an HOA / Neighborhood
          Committee.

     Councilmember Wood presented the proposed committee.

     Council discussed the proposed committee and asked clarifying questions.

    Chief Deputy Holden Rockwell, added that the city had success with their neighborhood
watch program, and that it could be beneficial to use that as a tool when putting together the
Page 2 of 5; February 10, 2026, City Council Minutes
proposed committee. A discussion ensued.

      The council discussed the formation of the committee and explored with residents how best
all the neighborhoods could be represented.

   Ms. Blake expressed concerns about going through the HOA’s to find representatives for the
committee.

    Ms. Reid expressed concerns with over occupancy and the impact it would have on any
neighborhood vote as a result of a higher number of renters.

    Ms. Mix echoed concerns of other residents regarding over occupancy and agreed that the city
needed to find new ways of investigating and enforcing existing statutes.

    The council discussed integrating or using best practices from the existing neighborhood
watch program. They agreed to continue the discussion.

     4.2. Predatory Towing Practices & 24-Hour Notice Discussion
          Councilmember Wood will lead a discussion regarding changes to towing
          enforcement.
    Councilmember Wood led a brief discussion regarding proposed changes to
towing enforcement. There was discussion amongst the council and residents
present how to best implement changes as well as how to work with HOAs. The
council decided to continue the item and hold a longer discussion at a later date.


     4.3. Budget Process Discussion
          Councilmember McCumber will lead a discussion on the upcoming fiscal year's
          budget process.

     Councilmember McCumber’s presented on the Fiscal Year Budget process for 2026-
2027 and proposed a timeline for that process.

    The council discussed the proposed timeline and agreed to hold a budget retreat meeting
on February 26th, 2026 to begin the process.

     4.4. RDA Reimbursement Expenses and Audit Discussion
          Councilmember Lauret will lead a discussion regarding RDA Reimbursement
          Expenses and Authorization of an Audit.


     Councilmember Lauret presented a proposed resolution authorizing the RDA board to
entering into an agreement with Chris Harding to conduct an additional audit of specifically
RDA payments made to developers.

     The council discussed the proposed resolution and discussed changes in the language. The
changes included wording changes regarding the finding of fraud, Mr. Harding’s credentials and
the pausing of payments to developers. There was also a short discussion regarding the benefits

done by the RDA.


     Nate Hutchinson with Flagship invited the council to meet with the developer regarding
audit and that it was his hope that his company and the city could work collaboratively. There
