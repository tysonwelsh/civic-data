Proposed Resolution 2024-3 amending Fiscal Year 2023-2024 in the
amount of $1,052,200 to appropriate additional funds to the CBD Mall,
South CBD Mall, 12th Street, Ogden River, South Wall, and Trackline
project areas
          A memo from the City Comptroller regarding proposed year-end adjustments to the Fiscal Year (FY) 2023-2024 budget
came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated Administration is proposing to transfer funds from several expired
Redevelopment Agency (RDA) districts to the Central Business District (CBD) Mall district to pay down debt, to recognize
proceeds from the sale of the Pioneer Heritage building, and to fund additional costs related to the Exchange Building in the
Trackline RDA Area.
          Comptroller Sorensen summarized the memo and stated three RDA areas have expired and no longer receive tax
increment. It is proposed in conjunction with closing the areas to move any remaining fund balance in the South CBD Mall and
12th Street areas. The South CBD Mall and 12th Street areas were haircut districts, and it is proposed to transfer the remaining fund
balance in these two funds to the CBD Mall area to be used for the CBD Mall debt. The remaining balance in the South Wall area
is proposed to be put into a contingent account in the South Wall area until a use is determined at which point a budget opening to
move the funds will be brought to the Board but will take place in FY2025. The Ogden River Area is recognizing a transfer in
from the General Fund for a property sale where the Piccadilly building property is located. The property was sold at appraised
value. The funds from the sale will be used in the Ogden River area to purchase additional land, a portion of which will later be
transferred to the Parkway Group which the RDA entered into an land transfer development agreement with in November 2023
and included this proposal. Additionally, the historic Exchange Building is in need of funds to stabilize the building. There are
currently funds available in the Trackline area, but the bids came back much higher. The proposal is to transfer excess Business
Depot Ogden (BDO) Lease Revenue Fund balance into the Trackline area to help fund the repairs to the building.
          Board member Graf inquired as to the accounting for the money being allocated to the Exchange Building. Mr. Sorensen
stated that the quoted amount for the stabilization of the building is approximately $1.5 million; there is $300,000 available to be
allocated to that project.
          Chair Richey introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2024-3, entitled:

                   detailed in the body of this resolution; and increasing the appropriations for a gross increase of $1,052,200 as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution.”
