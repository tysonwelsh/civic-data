Attachments: 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan; Town of Alta Annex to
SLCO Plan (via in-text links below); Resolution 2020-R-19 Adopting the 2019 Salt Lake
County Hazard Mitigation Plan




Background

The Town of Alta participated in a project led by Salt Lake County Emergency
Management updating its Hazard Mitigation Plan in 2019. All Salt Lake County
municipalities created their own annex to the county plan, including Alta. Staff
presented the Alta Town Council with the key component of the Town of Alta Annex, a
table of hazard mitigation actions, at the December, 2019 meeting and requested input
on the table. Salt Lake County submitted its complete plan including the Alta annex to
the U.S. Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) in late 2019, and FEMA
approved the county plan earlier in 2020.

Action Required

Salt Lake County is asking all municipalities in the county to adopt the full 2019 Salt Lake
County Hazard Mitigation Plan. Municipalities become eligible for certain types of
mitigation project and disaster relief funding by adopting the plan. To view the entire
plan, click here; and to view a complication of all the municipal annexes, including Alta’s,
click here. A resolution by which the Alta Town Council can adopt the plan is attached
to this staff report and included in the meeting materials for the August 12th council
meeting.

Both the county plan and the municipal annexes are very large documents; we do not
recommend you read them in their entirety. Please contact Chris Cawley if you have
questions about the county plan or the Alta annex.
