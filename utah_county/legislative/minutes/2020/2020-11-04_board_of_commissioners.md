COMMISSIONER LEE – THE COMMISSION HAS ESTABLISHED THE HEART OF
UTAH AWARD. THIS RECOGNITION IS GIVEN TO A RESIDENT WHO TRULY
PERSONIFIES THE MEANING OF UTAH’S HEART AND WE HOPE TO RECOGNIZE

FUTURE OF THOSE ON A FREQUENT BASIS TO REMIND PEOPLE THAT THERE IS
GOOD NEWS IN THE WORLD EVEN THOUGH WE SEE A LOT OF BAD NEWS.
THERE’S A LOT OF GOOD PEOPLE WHO ARE DOING GOOD THINGS AND ALONG
WITH THIS THERE’S BEEN SOME DONATED LITTLE CERTIFICATES FOR EATING
ESTABLISHMENTS - $50 WORTH TO GO TOWARDS KIND OF THAT RECOGNITION
THAT’S BEEN GIVEN FROM SOME OF THE GREAT RESTAURANTS HERE IN OUR
AREA. SO WITH THAT, OUR FIRST UTAH COUNTY RECIPIENT IS LOUANN
LOUGHTON. SHE’S ON THE PHONE. I WISH SHE COULD BE HERE. SHE’S QUITE A
BUSY PERSON. THERE’S A LOT GOING ON IN HER LIFE AND SO I’M GOING TO
JUST READ A LITTLE BIT ABOUT HER.

LuAnn Loughton is a resident of Pleasant Grove and has been playing the piano since she was a
young child. She has used her skill and passion to pass on this gift to others for 45 years as a
beloved piano teacher. Loughton began playing piano at 5 years old and continued to refine her
skills until she eventually majored in music education with an emphasis on piano at Brigham
Young University.
  For the past several years, she has had about 40 students each year, serving as a teacher,
mentor and friend. She teaches students as young as first graders and all ages up through
adulthood, including many older adults who have never taken lessons before but want to learn.

  Since the COVID-19 pandemic, Loughton has become creative in her teaching strategies by
utilizing FaceTime and Facebook Messenger to teach her students through technology.

made the world a more beautiful, colorful, and harmonious place.

UNDER THIS PRESSURE - THAT THEY CAN JUST GO AHEAD AND SHARE THEIR
GIFTS WITH OTHERS. I THINK MUSIC AND LEARNING TO PERFORM, EVEN
THOUGH THAT SOMETIMES ISN’T THE FAVORITE THING OF STUDENTS, IT HELPS
THEM TO GET PAST THEIR FEAR OF MAKING A MISTAKE IN FRONT OF OTHERS
AND INSTEAD, GIVING THEM ENJOYMENT, MUSIC IS SUCH A WONDERFUL THING
AND I’VE BEEN GRATEFUL TO BE INVOLVED IN THAT AND I REALLY DO
APPRECIATE YOU’RE USING THAT AS AN EXAMPLE TO RECOGNIZE PEOPLE
TRYING TO DO SMALL THINGS THAT THEY ADD UP AND HELP OUR SOCIETY AND
I THANK YOU SO VERY MUCH.

To the Employee of the Month Committee:
   We enthusiastically nominate Alice Black for November 2020 Employee of the Month.
Alice Black is the embodiment of public service and has been an invaluable team member
at Utah County for the past decade. In her extensive time here, she has worked with the
records management office and recently with department heads to serve as the
Commission Clerk. She has been the president of the UCEA, where she hosted myriad
events to connect people across the County and improve team morale.
   Alice transitioned from the Records team to her current role as Commission Clerk in April

She quickly became familiar with all relevant statutory obligations and is not afraid to ask
