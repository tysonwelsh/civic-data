Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                           Dustin Fratto, Council Director, updated the council on upcoming items.


Council Member Business

                           Council Member Zach Robinson invited the public to attend the current Sandy Arts Guild 
                           production of the Descendants at Mt. Jordan Middle School. He also mentioned that the 
                           River Oaks Golf Course was open and the course was in great shape.

                           Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the General Master Plan 
                           Housing Subcommittee and spoke about the great feedback received from the resident 
                           survey.

                           Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the CDBG Committee.


Mayor's Report

                           Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the Arts and how the City is alive with artistic expression. 
                           The Sandy Arts Guild production of the Descendants is sold out. The Canyons School 
                           District student art competition is coming to Sandy City Hall. She thanked the students 
                           at Sandy Elementary School for inviting her to come and read to their first grade 
                           students. And she reminded residents to start preparations ahead of any spring runoff 
                           and to check the city social media channels for up to date information. 

CAO Report

                           Shane Pace, CAO, had no report.

                           Council moved to Item 15 on the Agenda - Closed Session.
