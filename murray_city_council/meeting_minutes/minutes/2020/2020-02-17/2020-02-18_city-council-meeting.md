Business Items
   1. Consider an ordinance amending Section 3.10.370 of the Murray City Municipal Code
       relating to apprenticeship preference in the procurement of City building improvements
       and public works projects.

          Staff Presentation: Councilmember Dale Cox
          Councilmember Cox said this amendment lowers a contractors bid by 2 ½%, not to exceed
          $75,000, if they have a federally approved apprenticeship program on projects over
          $3,000,000. If a contractor gets a bid using this preference and does not fulfill their
          obligation, there is a 1% penalty to them.

          Councilmembers Turner and Martinez expressed their support for this ordinance and
          apprenticeship programs.

          MOTION: Councilmember Dominguez moved to adopt the ordinance. The motion was
          SECONDED by Councilmember Turner.

               Council roll call vote:
               Ayes: Councilmember Cox, Councilmember Dominguez, Councilmember Turner,

Mayor’s Report and Questions
Mayor Camp reported on the following items:
