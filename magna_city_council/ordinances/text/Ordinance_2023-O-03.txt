                           MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

     Ordinance No.2023-O-03                                  Date: January 24, 2023

              AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE MAGNA ACTIVE
                      TRANSPORTATION PLAN 2022

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Ann. § 10-2a-401, et seq; and

WHEREAS, Magna, the Kearns Metro Township, the Wasatch Front Regional Council
Utah Transit Authority, and others have jointly worked to develop a plan for active
transportation in the region including and around Magna and the Kearns Metro
Township, known as the Magna Active Transportation Plan 2022 (the “Active
Transportation Plan”); and

WHEREAS, the currently adopted 2021 Magna General Plan anticipates and is compatible
with the proposals and information in the Active Transportation Plan; and

WHEREAS, on April 13, 2022, and October 5, 2022, open houses on the Active
Transportation Plan were held to provide opportunity for the public input; and

WHEREAS, on January 24, 2023, the Magna Metro Township Council (“Council”) held
a public meeting to discuss and consider the Active Transportation Plan; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council has reviewed the Active Transportation
Plan as recommended by Greater Municipal Services District staff and determined that it
is necessary to adopt the Active Transportation Plan in the best interest of Magna and its
residents.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL:

1.    The attached Magna Active Transportation Plan 2022 and all other related
documents and appendices are approved in substantially the form attached hereto as
Exhibit A.

2.    A copy of the Active Transportation Plan will be filed and retained by the Magna
Metro Township Clerk, Recorder, and Auditor and posted to the Magna Metro Township
website.

3.     This Ordinance is effective upon posting as required by law.
