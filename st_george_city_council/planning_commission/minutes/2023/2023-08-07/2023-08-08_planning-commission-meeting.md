   1. Sage Haven Emerald Pool Park at Desert Color Planned Development Amendment – PUBLIC
      HEARING: Bob Hermandson, representing Desert Color St. George LLC. is requesting approval for an
      amendment to the Desert Color Planned Development (PD). The application is a request to develop a park
      with/swimming pool and other amenities. The site is approximately 1.91 acres and is generally located
      between Emerald Point Dr, Green Mountain Dr, Kelly Ln, and Pebble Glen Ln. Case No. 2023-PDA-013
      (Staff – Dan Boles)

       Dan Boles presented the following:

       Dan Boles – The zoning map shows this is zoned Planned Development Residential; Desert Color is
       required to have 5% of civic space in the TNZ zone. We looked at that and they have 7%, the other
       requirement is that if the civic space is over an acre it must come through Planning Commission and City
       Council, less than an acre is staff approval only. On the north end of the civic space is a pool. There are
       places to sit and lounge. There is also a building that will house pool equipment, restrooms, and storage.
       The other side has a splash pad and a play area for kids. There will also be bocci ball courts on the south
       end. They are proposing some parking for golf carts. The building is single story, it ties back to the main
       clubhouse, same type of materials and colors. They haven’t given specific information on the playground
       area. Staff does recommend approval. We did receive one letter that is in favor of the space, but they were
       hoping the applicant would move the pool to the south side.

       Commissioner Chapman – Water for the pool, is that coming from them or is that coming from City water?

       Dan Boles – I believe it is coming from their own.

   2. Fire Station #10 Zone Change Map Amendment – PUBLIC HEARING: Carlos Robles, representing
      The City of St. George is requesting approval to change the zone from C-2 (Commercial) to PD-C
      (Planned Development Commercial) on approximately 2.83 acres located at 3270 E Flowers Way in order
      to construct a fire station and a firefighter training station site. This is a City facilities project. Case No.
      2023-ZC-010 (Staff – Mike Hadley)

      Mike Hadley presented the following:

      Mike Hadley – They are building a training tower for the firefighters to train with their ISO training, the
      tower will be 60 ft. The main building will be single story.

      Commissioner Chapman – The station itself is one story?

      Mike Hadley – That is correct.

      Commissioner Andrus – Is there fire involved in the training on the tower? I think it’s a great location.

      Brad Esplin – If you look around at Joule Plaza and City View, they are very tall. We want to put guys on
      a tower where they can practice their skills to rescue people in those instances. There is a small section
