                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-33

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING UPDATES TO THE RIVERTON CITY PERSONNEL
                POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the
“Manual”) is used to set acceptable parameters for employment operations, and define employee
benefits; and

       WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or
in employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined that the personal
appearance section needed to be updated to remain current with existing personnel operations;
and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would correct the same to reflect current personnel operations;

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual attached to this
          Resolution is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution. The Mayor
          is hereby directed to ensure that the Manual is accordingly revised and made available
          to all City employees within a reasonable time from adoption of this Resolution.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 3rd day of
May, 2022 by the following vote:

6.6 PERSONAL APPEARANCE

Riverton City expects its employees to dress appropriately and present a professional demeanor in
accordance with their duties for the day. Professional dress and appearance is expected during any
contact with both the public and fellow employees. Standards of dress and appearance shall be
appropriate to the job and tasks to be accomplished as determined by their department head.

The City Manager may designate days of the week where all employees may dress casually. However,
neat, clean socially acceptable, professional, and modest attire is always expected, even with casual
wear.

In order to maintain a professional atmosphere and appearance, all employees, including those who
wear uniforms, shall maintain the following minimum standards:

    1. Employees must maintain a high standard of personal hygiene. Employees must appear neat
       and clean and have no offensive odors. An employee’s hair must be clean and groomed.
    2. Employee’s dress and appearance must be appropriate to their employment. Appropriateness
       may vary, depending upon the nature of work performed, safety concerns, and the degree of
       public contact.
    3. Employees must wear clothing that is clean and neat. Employees must avoid clothing that is
       unduly revealing, immodest, or otherwise inappropriate for a professional office setting or other
       work environment.
    4. Tattoos/Branding
            Employees are not permitted to have tattoos or branding on their face or head, except
                for permanent makeup, such as eyeliner, eyebrows, or lipstick.
            Tattoos or branding that violate the City’s harassment policy or include content found to
                be offensive, obscene, vulgar, depict graphic nudity, or are prejudicial to any segment of
                the population are strictly prohibited.
    5. Body Piercing or Alteration – Body piercing (other than ears or a nose stud) or alteration to any
       area of the body visible in any authorized uniform or attire that is a deviation from normal
       anatomical features and which is not medically required is prohibited. Such body alteration
       includes, but is not limited to:
            Tongue splitting or piercing
            Ear gauging
            The complete or transdermal implantation of any material other than hair replacement
            Abnormal shaping of the ears, eyes, nose or teeth
            Branding or scarification
    6. Employees are directed to consult their supervisor with any questions related to appropriate
       dress under this personal appearance policy before coming to work.

Any claimed inconsistencies between employees and other departments will be reviewed by the
department heads, Human Resources, an ultimately the City Manager, as needed. Any irreconcilable
differences related to this personal appearance policy shall be decided by the City Manager. The City
Attorney will advise as needed.

5.5 HOLIDAY LEAVE

Public Safety Service Employees will receive 52 hours banked each January and July for holiday leave
(104 hours total for the calendar year). Unused holiday leave bank for each employee will be reduced to
zero on December 31, before adding new holiday leave for the following year. Upon termination, a
Public Safety Service Employee’s holiday leave balance will be paid to the employee.

For all other employees, the following holidays will be observed:

    1.   New Years Day         January 1st
    2.   Human Rights Day      3rd Monday in January
    3.   Presidents Day        3rd Monday in February
    4.   Memorial Day          Last Monday in May
    5.   Juneteenth            Monday, June 19th, or Monday prior to June 19 (if June 19 falls on a
                               Tuesday-Friday), or the Monday after June 19th (if June 19 falls on a
                               Saturday or Sunday
    6. Independence Day        July 4th
    7. Pioneer Day             July 24th
    8. Labor Day               1st Monday in September
    9. Veteran’s Day           November 11th
    10. Thanksgiving Day       4thThursday in November
    11. Day after Thanksgiving Day after 4thThursday in November
    12. Christmas Eve          December 24th
    13. Christmas Day          December 25th

If any of the above holidays fall on Saturday, then the preceding Friday shall be the holiday. If any of the
above holidays fall on Sunday, then the following Monday is the holiday.

Holidays that fall on Monday-Thursday will be paid at 9 hours. Friday Holidays will be paid at 8 hours.
