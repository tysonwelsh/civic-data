                            RESOLUTION NO. 21-31

A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF DRAPER DECLARING SUPPORT FOR THE LOCALLY
PREFERRED ALTERNATIVE FOR THE POINT OF THE MOUNTAIN TRANSIT PROJECT.



     WHEREAS, the Utah Transit Authority, the Cities of Bluffdale, Draper, Lehi,
     Sandy, & South Jordan, Mountainland Association of Governments, Point
     of the Mountain State Land Authority, Salt Lake County, Utah Department
     of Transportation, and Wasatch Front Regional Council have jointly
     prepared a Transit Study which identifies a future Bus Rapid Transit
     alignment connecting the FrontRunner Draper Station to The Point and
     the Highline City Center Station in Draper and then south to the South
     Triumph Station and FrontRunner Lehi Station in Lehi.

     WHEREAS, the demand for transit service will increase as population
     continues to grow within Draper and Lehi and the existing transit network
     needs additional high quality, timely transit service with a direct
     connection to regional destinations in Draper and Lehi.

     WHEREAS, the City has considered various alignment alternatives as part
     of the Transit Study and hereby recommends the Locally Preferred
     Alternative for the Point of the Mountain Transit Study to be implemented
     as described below:

           The Locally Preferred Alternative would begin at the FrontRunner
           Draper Station and travel in mixed flow traffic to The Point via
           Frontrunner Boulevard, Vista Station Boulevard, and 13490 South.
           From north of Bangerter Highway, the route would travel across The
           Point to the Highline City Center via new exclusive lanes, and south
           to Triumph Boulevard in Lehi via the UTA owned right of way in
           exclusive transit lanes. From Triumph Boulevard, the route would
           travel to the FrontRunner Lehi Station via Ashton Boulevard in
           mixed flow traffic.

           Stations to be implemented as part of the Locally Preferred
           Alternative include:
               •   FrontRunner Draper
               •   The Point Station One
               •   The Point Station Two
               •   Highline City Center
               •   Gravel Pit Redevelopment Site
               •   North Lehi
               •   Lehi/Traverse Mountain
               •   South Triumph
               •   Ashton Boulevard
               •   FrontRunner Lehi

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Draper City Council that the City
      supports the Locally Preferred Alternative for the Point of the Mountain
      Transit project connecting the FrontRunner Draper Station to The Point and
      the Highline City Center Station in Draper and then south to the South
      Triumph Station and FrontRunner Lehi Station in Lehi, as described and
      shown in the figure attached hereto as Exhibit A.

      This resolution shall take effect upon passage and approval.
