SURPLUS POLICE EQUIPMENT
      The city’s surplus policy requires City Council approval for items valued over $1,000. The police
      department has been looking for ways to improve their efficiency. They have identified that the
      current rifles they use, MP-7s, while very helpful in accomplishing their design (close quarter
      combat), are very costly to maintain. The ammunition can be difficult to obtain and is very
      expensive. The rifles can be sold on the market for a decent price and the Police department
      would like to sell them a few at a time and use the proceeds to purchase a rifle that uses the
      more common, and widely available, 9 mm round.
      There are 12 rifles total and the staff is requesting that all 12 be declared surplus. The sale of
      the rifles may take place over a longer period of time. By switching out the rifles for something
      less expensive, they will be able to save the funds for other needs and lower their operating
      costs from year to year.
      Councilor Skip Worwood moved to approve the surplus of 12 MP-7 rifles in the Police
      Department. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on
      unanimous vote.
