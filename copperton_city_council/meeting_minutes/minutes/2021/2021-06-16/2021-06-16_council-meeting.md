Fiscal Items – Approval of Expenditures

Wasatch Regional Update - West Bench General Plan

                 Council Member Stitzer stated County Regional will put out an RFP for a
consultant to find a location for the Bonneville Shoreline Trail on the west side. Currently, they
have it going through Copperton. Using old railway lines may be possible. She suggested re-
routing the trail to the west of Copperton.

Storm Drain

               Paul Roundhouse stated his concern about a storm drain that is potentially
running under his garage. The drain runs through the highway, through his property and is causing
a sink hole that damaged the garage. Salt Lake County referred the matter to the Copperton
Council.

               Mayor Clayton stated he will reach out to MSD engineering to get a map of their
storm drain system. There are some questions as to who built the drain, whether it’s the County
or a previous owner.

Copperton Improvement District Water Conservation (CID)

               Mayor Clayton stated he received information on water usage from the CID and
would like to see the information transposed with weather.

               Nathan Bracken reviewed the fee penalties.

               The Council also discussed watering the park if CID’s policies hit an emergency
level when restrictions will apply to everyone.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Arborist Plan Presentation

               Daniel Allen reviewed his plan for Copperton Park. If given $50,000 he would first
remove and mitigate hazards. He would then work on diversifying age and species structures.
That would involve removing some trees species that represent more than their share of species
in the park (sycamores, maples, etc.). He would remove the worst of those. His proposal is to
address 20 percent (50 trees) of the trees and then begin managing the trees in a cyclical fashion.

               The Council also discussed hiring Mr. Allen as a consulting urban forester.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−

the fireworks ban. The motion passed unanimously.

        Council Member Clayton

           Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

                Mayor Clayton stated Olympia Hills is trying to annex into Herriman. He
anticipates that they will likely go there. This will put Olympia Hills out of the MSD’s hands, making
it Herriman’s headache.

He will request the same MSD budget for Copperton as in the past. He is also working to get
flashing lights for the crosswalks. The crosswalks are poorly placed for elementary kids. The fact
that it is in the highway, which is owned by UDOT, creates complications.
