                                       TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                       ORDINANCE NO. 21- 16


AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE AMENDING TAYLORSVILLE
    MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER SECTION 9. 61. 080: HOURS OF USE OF PARKS

        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville               City Council ( the " Council") met during its regularly
scheduled
              meeting   on   October      20,    2021,   to   consider,   among   other   things,   amending
TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 9. 61. 080: Hours of Use of Parks; and


       WHEREAS, the City desires to amend the hours of use of City parks to be consistent with
the hours of use for Salt Lake County parks located within the City; and

       WHEREAS, the purpose of this amendment is to minimize confusion as to when City
parks are open for use and when City parks are closed to the public; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it would be in
the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the City' s citizens to amend TAYLORSVILLE
MUNICIPAL CODE Section 9. 61. 080: Hours of Use of Parks as proposed.


        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 9. 61. 080: Hours of Use of
Parks   are   herebyapproved

9. 61. 080: Hours of Use of Parks:


City parks shall be open to the public from six o' clockk6: 00 a.m.) until ten o' clock ( 10: 00) p.m.
and shall be closed to the public from ten o' clock ( 10: 00) p.m. to six o' clock( 6: 00 a.m.).

The hours and days that a park is open and closed are as follows:

  A.    Parks shall be closed beginning November 1 through March 31 from eight o'clock ( 8: 00)
                                       f the next day and shall be open from seven o'clock ( 7: 00)
A.M. until eight o'clock ( 8: 00) F. M.

  B.    Parks shall be closed beginning April 1 through October 31 from ten o'clock( 10: 00) P. M.

until ten o' clock( 10: 00) P. M.


It is unlawful for any unauthorized person to be in a City park, either on foot or in any type of
motor vehicle or other conveyance, during the hours that a City park is closed.
