AWARD

Full‐time employees will receive sixty‐four (64) hours of personal leave at the beginning of each year.
Regular part‐time employees will receive a pro‐rated amount according to their average weekly hours.
New employees will receive a pro‐rated amount based on the remaining time left in the year. All
personal leave must be used during the calendar year or forfeited on December 31st, however, any
personal leave balances at the end of the calendar year will be automatically transferred into the
employee’s sick leave bank..

5.5 HOLIDAY LEAVE

Public Safety Service Employees will receive 48 hours banked each January and July for holiday leave (96
hours total for the calendar year). Unused holiday leave bank for each employee will be reduced to zero
on December 31, before adding new holiday leave for the following year. Upon termination, a Public
Safety Service Employee’s holiday leave balance will be paid to the employee.

5.8 CAREGIVER LEAVE

Full‐time and regular part‐time employees may be granted up to eighty‐hours (80) of paid Caregiver
Leave. For part‐time employees the hours will be prorated. This leave is only available once over a
rolling 12‐month period, regardless of the number of eligible events that arise. Leave can be taken
consecutively or intermittent as approved by the City Manager. In order to be eligible for Caregiver
Leave, employee must have successfully completed the probationary period.

Leave is available for the employee or to care for the employee’s spouse, domestic partner, child step‐
child, parent, step‐parent, mother or father‐in‐law, with the approval of the department head. Care
for other relations may be approved at the discretion of the City Manager. The existence of Caregiver
Leave as a benefit for employees does not affect an employee’s use of other leave for the same
purpose.
