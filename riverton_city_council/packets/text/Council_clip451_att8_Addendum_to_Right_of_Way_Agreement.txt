                                ADDENDUM TO AGREEMENT

       THIS ADDENDUM TO AGREEMENT (herein “Addendum”) is dated as of May
_____, 2021, between the Utah Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) and Riverton City
(“Riverton”), collectively referred to herein as the “Parties.”

                                           RECITALS

      a. In May 2017, Riverton and UDOT entered that certain Right of Way Contract (the
      “Original Contract”) in which Riverton agreed to convey Parcels 214, 214:2E, 213,
      213:E, 215:E, 215:2E, 215, 216, 216:E, 216:2E, 219:E, 224:ST, and 224 on UDOT
      Project S-0068(95)41, also known as SR-68, Bangerter Highway to 12600 South, to
      UDOT in exchange for improvements in the way of aligning city streets identified as
      Christian Way and Riverton Ranch Road.

      b. Riverton has conveyed title to UDOT for Parcels 214, 214:2E, 213, 213:E, 215:E,
      215:2E, 215, 216, 216:E, 216:2E, and 219:E.

      c. UDOT and Riverton City recently ascertained that Parcels 224:ST and 224 were not
      owned by Riverton City at the time the Original Contract was signed. Those parcels
      were owned by the Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City Utah (“RDA”).

      d. As a result of the mutual mistake of UDOT and Riverton City referenced in the
      preceding Recital, Riverton has been unable to convey Parcels 224:ST and 224 in
      completion of its obligations under the Original Contract.

      e. By this Addendum the Parties desire to correct the mutual mistake and arrange for
      conveyance of Parcel 224 and a perpetual easement, Parcel 224:E, to UDOT and
      payment of substitute compensation for Parcel 224:ST.

                                ADDENDUM TO AGREEMENT

     NOW THEREFORE, for valid consideration and in order to correct Riverton’s and
UDOT’s mutual mistake, the Parties agree as follows:

      1. Recitals. The above Recitals are incorporated herein and made part of this
      Addendum.

      2. Parcel 224. In order to avoid future litigation and other valuable consideration,
      Riverton agrees to acquire Parcel 224 and a perpetual easement, Parcel 224:E, from the
      RDA and convey said parcels to UDOT free of all liens and encumbrances except
      recorded easements.

      3. Parcel 224:ST. Riverton agrees to pay UDOT the sum of $25,000.00 (twenty-five
      thousand dollars) in lieu of conveying Parcel 224:ST to UDOT.
4. Additional Acts. Riverton agrees to promptly execute and deliver the attached Quit
Claim Deed and Easement. The Parties agree to promptly execute and deliver such
other documents and perform such other acts as may be reasonably necessary to carry
out the purposes and intent of this Addendum.

5. Counterparts. This Addendum may be executed in multiple counterparts, each of
which shall be deemed an original, and all of which taken together shall constitute one
and the same document and agreement. Counterpart signatures may be exchanged by e-
mail (pdf format) with the same force and effect as originals.

6. Authority. Each person who signs this Addendum represents and warrants that
he/she is duly authorized to sign this Addendum on behalf of the entity indicated, and to
bind said entity to the terms of this Addendum.

7. Entire Agreement. This Addendum sets forth the entire understanding of the Parties
with respect to the matters set forth herein.

8. To the extent the terms of this Addendum modify or conflict with any provisions of
the Original Contract, the terms of this Addendum shall control. All other terms of the
Original Contract not modified by this Addendum shall remain the same.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Addendum as of the date first
set forth above.
