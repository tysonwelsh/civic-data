PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on July 12, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2023-14 amending Title 10 of the Herriman City Code to clarity existing zoning definitions an standards for administrative interpretations, weeds, lot widths, walls, fencing, off-street parking, commercial signage, accessory dwelling unit license requirements, and minimum landscape with in the auto mall special district. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
