Transportation Director Miller addressed the Council and presented an Interlocal Agreement for funding for State Route 32.
Council Members asked brief clarifying questions to which Director Miller and Chief Deputy Civil Attorney, Dave Thomas responded.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve the interlocal agreement between Summit County, City of Kamas, City of Oakley and City of Francis for funding State Route 32 Corridor and City Centers Plan. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Christopher Robinson.

Discussion and approval of Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee's 2024 grant recommendations; Eric Bradshaw (5:00 P.M.)
Attachment: Cover Page

Eric Bradshaw, Secretary , presented to the Council and provided the Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee's 2024 Restaurant Grant Committee Recommendations.
Council members asked questions and thanked Mr. Bradshaw for his service on the board.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve the Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee's 2024 Grant Recommendations as contained in the packet. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Christopher Robinson.
