                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 21-23

A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN UPDATE TO THE RIVERTON CITY PERSONNEL
                POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL
                   (Amendment to Retirement Benefit)


       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the
“Manual”) is used to set acceptable parameters for employment operations, such as the incidental
personal use of City-owned property; and

       WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or
in employment practices arise; and

        WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the terms by which employees may retire from
Riverton City, and determined an amendment should be made to the retirement benefit so that it
will be incrementally increased over time; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would encourage employees to maintain a longer career prior
to retirement; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual which describe in greater detail the terms and
conditions by which the City’s employees may receive benefits upon retirement from Riverton
City.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. Part 2 (2.13) of the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual attached
          to this Resolution is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution. The
          Mayor is hereby directed to ensure that the Manual is accordingly revised and made
          available to all City employees within a reasonable time from adoption of this
          Resolution.

       2. Any provisions to Part 2 of the Manual which are inconsistent with the revisions
          approved by this Resolution are hereby repealed. The City Recorder is authorized and
          directed to reformat the Manual as appropriate, to incorporate the revisions to the
          Manual so approved by this Resolution, and to eliminate said provisions found to be
          inconsistent with the provisions of this Resolution.

       3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

2.13 TERMINATION PROCEDURES FOR SPECIFIC TYPES OF TERMINATION
VOLUNTARY RETIREMENT
Employees who desire retirement should notify Riverton City three (3) months in advance.

Riverton City should communicate the status of each employee's retirement benefits-upon request.
Riverton City should carefully explain to the employee what options are available to the employee upon
retirement (such as Cobra and Retirement Plan Options). Riverton City should give the employee ample
time to review the retirement plan. Riverton City should have the employee sign a release, or at least a
declaration statement, to the effect that they are electing retirement of their own free will.

After a five (5) year vesting period with the City, an employee will receive a one-time payment equal to
five percent (5%) of their current annual wage in their final check when the employee is retiring from
Riverton City and also retires under the Utah Retirement System (URS). Once an employee is vested, this
retirement incentive will increase one-percent (1%) for each year following vesting, up to a max of ten
percent (10%) after ten (10) years of continued employment. The incentive is not prorated but will only
be awarded after each completed year.
