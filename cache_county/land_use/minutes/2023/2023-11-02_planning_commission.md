#5 Geneva Rock – Newton Pit Conditional Use Permit 
Zetterquist reviewed the updated staff report for the Geneva Rock – Newton Pit Conditional Use Permit. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed access, future road improvements, reclamation of slopes,  
Bill Gammell commented on the line of sight for oncoming traffic and the addition of deceleration lanes 
required by UDOT. 
Christensen asked about possible reduced speed limits and extra signage regarding trucks entering 
roadway. 
Mr. Gammell responded he doubted reduced speed limits would happen but believes extra signage will 
be required. 
Bill King commented on slopes and reclamation. 
 
 
        2 November 23               Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 5 
Mr. Gammell commented on the 2:1 reclamation and asked about the County holding the bond until 
vegetation is at an acceptable rate. 
Sands commented on a 3:1 reclamation vs. a 2:1. 
Mr. King commented on reclamation practices and best management practices. 
Mr. Gammell commented on slopes being reseeded before the pit is fully closed during phased mining. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed holding the bond until the reclamation is satisfactory. 
Mr. King commented on the reclamation bond and releasing the bond due to reclamation. 
Commissioners discussed reclamation. 
Christensen asked about the noise study. 
Mr. King asked why a noise study is being required. 
Christensen asked what other counties require. 
Mr. King reviewed the process they have met in the past for noise ordinances. 
Sands explained that the noise study is a baseline for if there is a compliant. 
Commissioners discussed the noise study requirement. 
Mr. King explained that his company feels the noise study requirement has a lot of ambiguity around it. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the noise study requirement. 
Christensen asked about dust control being regulated by the State. 
Mr. Gammell responded that dust control is monitored by the State. 
Rigby asked about the storm water plan if there is rain and snow. 
Mr. King responded best management practices will be used to keep water on the site and they will meet 
the requirements of the Storm Water Permit issued by the state.  
Rigby commented on water leaving the property. 
Mr. Gammell commented on discharging water and the mine using a pit for water retainment. 
Rigby asked about building berms on the top portion of the property to help with water runoff. 
Mr. Gammell explained how they plan to use berms, comprehensive site reviews, and monthly 
inspections. 
 
 
        2 November 23               Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 4 of 5 
Phillips commented on the types of storm water and discharge from the site and explained what is 
regulated by the State and what is regulated by the County. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the noise study requirement and conditions to mitigate issues being 
reasonable. 
Parker motioned to approve the Geneva Rock Newton Pit Conditional Use Permit with the removal of 
condition #13, and include 2:1 Max Slope ratio for reclamation to the condition #6; Rigby seconded; 
Passed 4, 1 (Sands voted nay).

#7 Utah Flour Milling Conditional Use Permit 
Zetterquist reviewed the Utah Flour Milling Conditional Use Permit (CUP) staff report. 
Ed Kavorik commented as a representative of the owner with no concerns on the conditions. 
Wendy Christensen commented with concerns regarding water, light, and commotion that comes with 
the project. 
Christensen asked about noise. 
Mr. Kavorik commented that the project is enclosed and the truck noise should be equivalent to what is 
there now. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed noise. 
Sands motioned to approve the Utah Flour Milling Conditional Use Permit based on the 19 conditions 
and 2 conclusions; Parker seconded; Passed 5, 0.
