Hadfield American Fork Mayor Howard Anderson Cedar Fort Mayor Lonny Ward Genola Mayor Mark Thompson Highland Mayor Jeff Acerson Lindon Mayor Richard Brunst Orem Mayor Kirk Hunsaker Santaquin Mayor Steve Leifson Spanish Fork Mayor Wilford Clyde Springville Mayor Randy Farnworth Vineyard Council Member Bill Colbert Draper Council Member Shellie Baertsch Saratoga Springs Mr. Chris Bleak UTA Board Hugh Johnson UTA Matt Sibul UTA Council Member Kendall Crittenden Wasatch County Mayor Trever Johnson Coalville Mayor Lewis Marchant Kamas Mayor Bob Kowallis Midway Mr. & Mrs. Anderson Excused: Mayor John Curtis Commissioner Kent Millington Mayor Rick Moore Mayor Bert Wilson Commissioner Gary Anderson Mayor Brian Wall Mayor Mike Daniels Staff Shawn Seager Andrew Jackson Steve Johnson Nan Kuhn Mayor Gygi called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning May 22, 2014 Page 3 Adopt the Unified Planning Work Program FY-2015 - Shawn Seager The Work Program is an annual budget. It illustrates to our members, Federal Highway Administration, Federal Transit Administration, and the general public where our planning resources are allocated. It describes the planned work, budget, and funding sources. The FY-15 budget is $3,223,000, an increase of $557,100 from FY-14 budget. This is primarily due to an increase in pass-through contracts and special studies requested by members. The budget proposes to spend approximately $1,134,000 on new special studies:  American Fork Meadows Connect Road Study  Orem State Street Corridor Study  Payson US-198 Corridor Study  Provo I-15/800 North Interchange Study  South County Trail Plan  I-15/Springville 1600 South Interchange Study. The Saratoga Springs Bike/Ped Plan, Eagle Mountain Bike/Ped Plan, North County Transit Study and a Lindon Bike/Ped Plan are studies from previous years for $565,000. It also proposes to spend the remaining $1,524,000 of the budget on twelve full time equivalent MAG staff. Staff will continue to work on Air Quality Conformity, Financial Planning, Travel Demand Modeling, updating the Regional Transportation Plan, Census projections and manage the studies mentioned above. Revenue for this $3.2 million budget is approximately 94% federal funds from the United States Department of Transportation while 6% is local funds form UTA, UDOT and our local government member. The MPO Technical Advisory Committee recommends Regional Planning adopt the Unified Planning Work Program FY-2015. Commissioner Larry Ellertson moved to adopt the Unified Planning Work Program FY-2015. Mayor Wilford Clyde seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Other Business a. The next meeting is scheduled for June 5, 2014. Commissioner Witney moved to adjourn the meeting at 7:10 p.m., Mayor Farnworth seconded. *Meeting minutes are recorded with a digital recorder. A hard copy of the attendees, a brief summary, and all motions made during the meeting will be approved at the next meeting. A CD of the entire meeting is available upon request.*
