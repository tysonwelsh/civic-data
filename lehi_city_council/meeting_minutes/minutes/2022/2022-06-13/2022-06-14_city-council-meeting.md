Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.

Councilor Albrecht. Councilor Condie gave the opening comment.

Dean Lundell presented on the questionnaire related to internal controls. The City does a self-

the City low-risk.

and the second project is the cycle track on 700 South.

15. Consideration of Preliminary and Final subdivision approval of Family Park Estates, a
    9-lot development located at approximately 600 East and Airport Drive in an R-1-15
    (residential) zone. (Tabled at the May 24th meeting)
    Kim Struthers and Lorin Powell discussed that it was tabled about the geotech report and
    traffic study. All 9 lots are buildable with some mitigation.

    Josh Gibbons with Hale Engineering did a traffic study for the whole park and included the
    nine lots. He felt that they were very conservatively high on traffic estimates. They found
    that the nearby intersections would act well, but recommended some turning lanes and a few
    other recommendations for worst case scenario.

    Council asked staff and Mr. Gibbons clarifying questions.

    Tony Trane discussed the Geotech study and answered questions. He felt that the biggest
    thing was keeping it consistent on each lot.

    Mayor Johnson asked for public comments.

    Gina Christofferson stated that she had to set a reserve for her budget and so does the city.
    She felt that the city could take a period of time and replace it back. Her second
    recommendation was to look at other areas of the city that could be developable.

    Dave Christofferson inquired about the traffic and geotech study. He suggested that the
    houses go on the bottom not on the top of the slope.

    Nathan Buck expressed concerns with the traffic.

    Todd Spencer stated that he would like to hear about the ideas that were presented and to
    reiterate what was said.

    Michelle Stallings stated that she doesn't understand why they don't have to replenish other
    reserves.

    There was discussion regarding the items that were brought up by residents.

    Mr. Trane gave an explanation on the pit and the expansion questions. Mr. Powell stated that
    staff would look at the no parking zones and people parking there.

    Councilor Koivisto expressed gratitude for those who brought ideas, but she felt it would be
    irresponsible not to sell those lots. That despite the bad situation that was the best option.

    Councilor Hancock talked through dropping the lots to the bottom and what would that take.
    He stated that to shift midflight isn't fair to the homeowners on the south side and this has

    part of cities to bare the cost. He felt that the easiest way to get the money back was to sell
    the acres back to the Pecks, but that isn't what he wants to do.

    Councilor Southwick would rather not sell the property back. He is unsure about the 9 lots
    and suggested expanding the overlook.

    Councilor Condie is not in agreeance with 9 whole lots. When the PARC tax happened he
    felt they need to fund Family Park and Mellor-Rhodes. I felt that one thing that hasn't been
    talked about is replacing it from the rainy day fund, but feels like there are several options to
    be reimbursed. He felt like there are so many projects that need to be funded that the PARC
    tax shouldn't be used as the only funding mechanism for Family Park. His preference is 6
    lots.

    Mr. Trane and staff clarified the road, turning areas, and spacing.

    Councilor Koivisto expressed concerns with going from 9 lots to 6 lots. She feels that if they
    are going to do this they need to go all in. She feels it's irresponsible to go to that amount and
    it's a disadvantage.

    Councilor Hancock felt that doing less lots helps still reimburse the money, but also gives
    more views.
