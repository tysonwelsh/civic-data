                                    CEMETERY INFORMATION

   The software program that Nephi City uses is called Spatial Generations. The file path on Lisa’s
    computer is Cemetery(\\Server1)(Z:)>cemeter>NEPHIprojuploadNIS
   If the funeral home calls about a burial:
         o Open Spatial Generation
         o In the Edit Record tab
                  Search By—DECEASED
                  Search Value- type RES (last name of deceased)
         o If the deceased has a burial right reserved in their name, then look in the notes in the
             RESERVED box to see what fees have been paid. If it says ALL FEES PAID, then you tell
             the funeral home that. If it says nothing, then it usually is an older family owned right
             and the family owes Opening and Closing Fee and Perpetual Care fee. Ask the funeral
             home if the deceased person is a resident or non-resident and then refer to the fees
             schedule for the proper fees to charge. Ask the funeral home if they want the city to bill
             them for the charges or to bill the family. If says Perpetual Care Paid O/C not paid, then
             only charge for opening and closing.
         o Get the date and time of the burial.
         o Print out a map of the burial space and give to the Cemetery Superintendent.
         o Complete a white “Information for Burial Permit” card found in the right-hand top
             drawer of Lisa’s desk.
         o If we need to bill the funeral home then make an invoice in Caselle in the A/R module
             and mail to the funeral home.
         o When payment come in, receipt it into Caselle and record payment on the “Information
             for Burial Permit” card.
