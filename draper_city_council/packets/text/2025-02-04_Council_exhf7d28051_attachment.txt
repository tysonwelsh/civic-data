                                         RESOLUTION NO. 25-05

        A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE ADOPTED BUDGET OF DRAPER CITY
        FOR FISCAL YEAR 2024-2025.


        WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City has adopted Resolution 24-25 which adopted the
final budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025, in accordance with the
requirements of the Utah Code Annotated; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City wishes to amend the fiscal year 2024-2025 budget;
and

         WHEREAS, a public hearing to consider the appropriations has been noticed and held and all
interested persons were heard, for or against the appropriations; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City hereby finds this action in the best interest of the
public’s health, safety and general welfare.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.       Purpose. The purpose of this Resolution is to amend the budget of Draper City, as
approved and finalized by Draper City Resolution 24-25.

        Section 2.   Authorization. The Mayor is authorized to sign any agreements to implement the
budget amendments adopted by this Resolution.

         Section 3.       Adoption of Amendments. The budget amendments attached hereto and made a
part of this Resolution shall be, and the same hereby are adopted and incorporated into the budget of Draper
City, Utah for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025, in accordance with the
requirements of the Utah Code Annotated.

        Section 4.       Filing of copies of the Budget Amendments. The Budget Officer is authorized and
directed to certify and file a copy of said budget amendments in the office of the City Recorder which
amendments shall be available for public inspection.

         Section 5.        Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid, or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and
all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 6.        Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.

                                • FY25-29           The Police Department is proposing to install dash cameras in each police vehicle. Video evidence in vehicles is
                                      Police Dash   becoming increasingly important as violence on the roads increases. Police dash cams generate admissible
                                      Cameras       evidence, provide additional visibility for officers and supervisors, and allow for greater community transparency.
                                                    The benefits of police dash cams include:
                                                    • Safety: Police dash cameras help keep officers safe.
                                                    • Training: Another key benefit of police dash cams is their role in officer training. The footage they provide is an
                  DRAPER CITY


                                                       invaluable resource for officers and supervisors, aiding in their ongoing professional development.
FI NA NC E DEPA R T MENT




                                                    • Transparency: Over the years, dash camera videos have been made available to the public, and the footage
                                                       provides a valuable look into how police approach their duties. This helps with community transparency and
                                                       trust.
                                                    • Liability protection: Community complaints are a reality of policing. The International Association of Chiefs of
                                                       Police (IACP) reports that officers are exonerated 93% of the time when video evidence is available, making the
                                                       presence of police dash cameras an active form of protection for law enforcement officers against false or
                                                       misleading complaints.
                                                    • Conviction: Police dash cameras have evidentiary value. Video footage of incidents is admissible evidence and
                                                       helps prosecutors tell the story and gain convictions. I have seen this first hand in court as police shootings were
                                                       presented to juries.
                                                    The Department receives funding from the state for alcohol enforcement and is proposing to direct this grant
                                                    funding for the purchase and ongoing maintenance of the cameras. The revenue was already recognized in the
                                                    budget in prior years and is in restricted fund balance.
