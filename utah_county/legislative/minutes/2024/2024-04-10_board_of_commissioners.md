Resolution No. 2024 - 305

UTAH COUNTY COMMISSION RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF THE UTAH
COUNTY CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER AND DESIGNATING THE MONTH OF
APRIL AS CHILD ABUSE PREVENTION MONTH

WHEREAS. in 2023, 1.861 primary victims of child abuse were served at the Utah County
Children's Justice Center; and

WHEREAS, child abuse and neglect is a serious problem affecting every segment of our
community, and finding solutions requires input and action from everyone; and

WHEREAS, child abuse can have long-term psychological. emotional. and physical
effects that have lasting consequences for victims. of abuse: and

WHEREAS, we acknowledge that we must work together as a community to increase
awareness about child abuse and contribute to promoting the social and emotional well-being of
children and families in a safe, stable, and nurturing environment; and

WHEREAS, the Utah County Children's Justice Center leads in collaborative efforts to
protect each child, advance justice, promote healing. and educate our community; and

WHEREAS, in 2023 in the Utah County Children's Justice Center:

¢ Served 3.993 victims, both primary and secondary

e Facilitated 829 forensic interviews

¢ Performed 226 medical exams

e Conducted 2,046 follow-ups with caretakers, and

e Screened 426 children aged 10-17 for their trauma level and suicidality

NOW, THEREFORE. we, the Utah County Commission. recommit to ensuring that every
child grows up in a safe, nurturing environment that is free from abuse and neglect. We. the
County Commissioners of Utah County, Utah. do hereby proclaim the month of April 2024 as
CHILD ABUSE PREVENTION MONTH in Utah County and urge our citizens to recognize this
month by dedicating themselves to the task of improving the quality of life for all children and

RESOLVED AND ORDERED this 10th day of April, 2024.

UTAH COUNTY. UTAH
