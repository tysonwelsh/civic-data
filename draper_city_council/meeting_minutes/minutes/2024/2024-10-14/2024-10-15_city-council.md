Study Session

       Presentation: Salt Lake County - Public Safety Bond
       Katherine Fife, Associate Deputy Mayor of Salt Lake County, shared
       information regarding a Public Safety Bond intended to fund consolidation
       and improvement of the County Jail, including mental health and rehabilitation
       services. She said the $507 million bond would create taxpayer savings, said
       the bond was part of a larger improvement effort, and answered questions
       from the City Council.

       Discussion: 700 West options - Scott Cooley
       City Engineer and Public Works Director Scott Cooley said the Council
       approved a significant budget some time ago to widen and improve 700 West
       between 11400 South and 12300 South. He said there had been many resident
       comments in opposition to the project, and the project was temporarily put
       on hold. Mr. Cooley said the road would essentially function the same whether
       it was widened or not, but the project as it was proposed would add curb,
       gutter, and sidewalk. He said traffic stacking had not seemed to be an issue
       along that portion of 700 West in the last year or two.

7.    Items for Council Consideration
7.a   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1625, an Ordinance vacating City right-of-
      way located at approximately 984 E. Rosefield Lane.
      Councilmember F. Lowry asked to be recused from this item due to a conflict
      of interest, he was excused from the discussion and left the meeting.



                                                                        3|Page
City Manager Mike Barker said the applicant, who resided at 987 Old English
Road, was requesting the City Council vacate the right-of-way (ROW) adjacent
to his property, dedicated to the City for use as a future road upon approval
of the Wild Road Subdivision – Phase 2 plat in 1995. In 1995, the City entered
into a development agreement with the developer that permitted owners of
the two properties adjacent to the ROW to make use of the ROW as part of
their property until the City utilized the ROW as a road to connect to Country
Lane, a private lane abutting the ROW to the east.

Mayor Walker opened a public hearing.

William Mark, Draper resident, said he lived just above Country Lane and
frequently used the pathway on the ROW. Mr. Mark said he was comfortable
with what the applicant was trying to do, but expressed concern with the
possibility of future property owners restricting public use of the pathway.
Mr. Mark suggested the current applicant’s home owner insurance may
require access to be fenced off. He suggested tabling a decision to learn
more about applicable public access rights. Mr. Mark asked if there would be
a way for the City to maintain limited public access rights.

Scott Hinkley, Draper resident, said he frequently used the ROW access and
spoke in favor of preserving public access.

Janet Astle, Draper resident, said she shared the same concerns already
expressed, and spoke in favor of protecting public access.

David Nixon, applicant, said he did not intend to remove public access to the
pathway, but intended to improve the access at some point in the future. He
explained a desire to extend his existing detached garage to provide indoor
playing space for his family.

Councilmember T. Lowery expressed concern for connectivity, and asked if
Mr. Nixon would be willing to work with the City to protect public access. Mr.
Nixon said he would be willing to work with the City, and said his only
concern would be for future building setbacks.

Mayor Walker closed the public hearing.

     Councilmember Green said he was interested in tabling the issue to allow
     time to more fully understand the easement issue. Councilmember Roberts
