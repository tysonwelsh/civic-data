          and necessary renovations to preserve its historical character and development
          as a historical site. Staff recommends approval.

      b. Consider approval of lease agreement for the property at 356 North Main
         Street.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Purchase of this property is pending
          Council approval. The lease agreement is for the sellers to occupy the property
          on an occasional basis. The property will principally be used to store seller's
          personal items which may have historical value to the City, and which seller may
          elect to donate to the City for historical purposes.

      c. Consider approval of a Participation Commitment with the Utah Division
         of Forestry, Fire and State Lands for the Cooperative Wildfire System for
         2025.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City has previously entered into an
          agreement with the Utah Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands. In this
          agreement the State will complete an annual Wildfire Risk assessment based on
          wildfire hazard areas in our jurisdiction, and the Fire Department's average
          historical fire cost provided by the state, based on the past 10 years eliminating
          the low and high years. This risk assessment will generate a monetary value that
          the city would be responsible for. Through approved projects such as mitigation,
          prevention, and preparedness efforts, the city will receive credits to reach the
          required value. There will be no additional cost to the city. In return, the state
          will provide assistance, at no cost, in the event of a major incident in the City
          when City resources have been depleted. The Participation Commitment
          Statement for 2025 is $61,600. Staff recommends approval.

      d. Consider approval of the minutes from the meetings held on January 16,
         2025 and January 23, 2025.

PUBLIC HEARING/SUBSTANTIAL AMENDMENT TO THE 2024 CDBG ANNUAL
ACTION PLAN:
     Public hearing and consider approval of a substantial amendment to the
     2024 CDBG (Community Development Block Grant) AAP (Annual Action
     Plan) the first year of the of the City's five-year Consolidated Plan.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The AAP (Annual Action Plan) outlines
      proposed projects by the City’s Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)
      program to address identified needs during PY2024, the first year of the City’s 2024-
      2028 five-year Consolidated Plan. The City has received $623,056 in CDBG funding
      from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) for PY2024,
      which began July 1, 2024. The City will also plan for the use of prior year resources
      totaling $266,247.56. The following depicts projects to be undertaken with said
      funding. The purpose of the amendment is to increase the funded amount allocated
      to activity 2 CDBG: Public Facilities Infrastructure (2024) from $320,000 to
      $435,511.82, as well as increasing the funded amount allocated to activity 4 CDBG
      Housing from $106,972 to $128,909.

      generally located on Pioneer Road, south of Nighthawk Drive. (Case No.
      2024-PDA-009 - Mojave Crossing)

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This vacant lot was previously approved for
      the SunRiver Patio Furniture project in December of 2021. That project failed to
      progress, and now the Mojave Crossing project is being proposed. This item was
      presented to the planning commission on September 10, 2024, at a public hearing.
      During the public hearing there were 318 written public comments presented and an
      additional 20 public comments were made at the meeting. The comments raised
      concerns about the impacts of this development, mainly privacy, traffic, noise and
      safety issues for the current residents in the area. The public hearing was closed and
      the Planning Commissioners voted to continue this item to a date uncertain with a
      request that the applicant reconfigure the site plan and provide graphics showing the
      views from the third floor of the proposed hotel building. The applicant returned at
      the December 10, 2024 Planning Commission meeting, where the Planning
      Commission recommended approval with a 6-0 vote, with a condition.

     Atkinville Interchange Planned Development Commercial (PD-C) zone on
     approximately 17.85 acres generally located north of Southern Parkway on
     Desert Color Parkway at approximately 4700 South. (Case No. 2025-PDA-
     004 - Costco)

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This amendment proposes two things. First,
      the amendment would add the use “Retail, wholesale, including membership stores
      and manufacturer’s outlets (>40,000 ft²)” to the use table in the VC1 and VC2 land
      use districts of the Atkinville PD-C zone. The second aspect of the proposed PD
      amendment is to allow the review and potential approval of site elements such as
      site layout (setbacks, parking, traffic flow, etc.), building elevations and renderings,
      and landscaping. The Planning Commission reviewed these two requests and held a
      public hearing on January 14, 2025 and recommend approval with conditions.
