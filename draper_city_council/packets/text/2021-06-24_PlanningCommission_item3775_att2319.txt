BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of an Amended Subdivision Plat for
approximately 0.5 acres located on west end of Twin Peaks Lane, at approximately 276 E.
Twin Peaks Lane (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned R3. The applicant is requesting
that an Amended Subdivision Plat be approved to allow for the consolidation of a parcel
into the existing lot.

This application represents the second amendment to the Twin Peaks at Draper No. 3
Subdivision (Exhibit G). The first amendment, also known as the Amended Plat of Lot 307
Twin Peaks at Draper No. 3 Subdivision (Exhibit F) divided Lot 307 of that plat into 4 lots
and a remainder parcel. The single-family home on the subject property was built on lot 4
of the current subdivision in 2011. This amendment seeks to incorporate the remainder
parcel from the first amended subdivision into Lot 4 from the same amendment. To reduce
confusion regarding the naming of the amended plats this plat amendment will be known

ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Medium
Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the R3 zoning classification, supporting approximately
three (3) dwelling units per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code
(DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the R3 zone is to “permit medium density residential
development without special mitigation requirements. This zone is intended to provide incentives
to foster residential development with little impact on its surroundings and on municipal
services, and to generally preserve the semirural character called for in the density element of
the general plan.” The R3 zoning abuts the subject property on all sides of the property.

Subdivision Layout. The layout of the proposed amended subdivision plat is shown in
Exhibit E. The lot is approximately 0.44 acres or 19,166 ft2 in size and the parcel proposed



«Project_Name»                                                   App. # SUBD-0056-2021
Amended Subdivision Plat Request               2
for incorporation into the lot is 0.06 acres or 2,614 ft2 in size. The total lot size after the
amendment would be approximately 0.5 acres or 21, 780 ft2 in size. No changes to the
location of the existing private lane or utility easements are being proposed. A small error
in the platted location of a previously deeded sewer easement as shown on the previous
plat is being corrected on this plat and is noted in the plat notes. This adjustment in the
drawing causes no impacts to the current functionality or potential future development on
the lot or to the existing infrastructure improvements within that easement.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of an amended
subdivision plat request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
