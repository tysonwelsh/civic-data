APPROVED                                            CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL
                                                               SPECIAL MEETING
                September 30, 2022 at 12:00 p.m. - Cache County Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.
 In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinions or purported facts.
             The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


MEMBERS PRESENT: Vice Chair Paul Borup, Councilmember David Erickson, Councilmember Gordon Zilles, Councilmember Gina
Worthen, Councilmember Karl Ward
MEMEBERS EXCUSED: Chair Barbara Tidwell, Councilmember Nolan Gunnell
STAFF PRESENT: Shawn Milne
OTHER ATTENDENCE:

Special Meeting

1. Call to Order – Vice Chair Paul Borup

2. Items of Special Interest ATTACHMENT 1
     a. Request for Approval of County Economic Development Advisory Board’s Recommendations – Shawn Milne
     Discussion: Shawn Milne addressed the council 0:38. Councilmember discussion ensued 7:25.
     Action: Motion made by Councilmember Karl Ward to approve the request for the Economic Advisory Board’s
     Recommendations; seconded by Councilmember Gina Worthen 25:46
     Motion passed.
     Aye: 5 Paul Borup, David Erickson, Gordon Zilles, Gina Worthen, Karl Ward
     Nay: 0
     Absent: 2 Barbara Tidwell, Nolan Gunnell

3. Adjourn – approximately 12:30 p.m.
