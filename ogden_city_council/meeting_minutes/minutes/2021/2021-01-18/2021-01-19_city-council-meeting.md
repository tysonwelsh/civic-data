               The purpose of the work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00p.m.;
      receive annual required training regarding sexual harassment, distracted driving, cyber security, stormwater pollution prevention,
      Open and Public Meetings, Ethics and Conflicts of Interest, and the Government Records Access Management Act (GRAMA);
      discuss Council business; and hear Council comments.

      Agenda Review
              Chair Blair and members of Council staff briefly reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
      scheduled to begin at 6:00p.m.

      Annual Trainings
            Open and Public Meetings, Ethics and Conflicts of Interest, and Government Records Access Management Act
      (GRAMA): City Attorney Williams provided the City Council with annually required training regarding the Open and Public
      Meetings Act (OPMA) for the State of Utah as well as the Municipal Officers' and Employees' Ethics Act. He discussed the types
      of actions the Council could take that could be construed as a violation of the OPMA as well as any activity in which an individual
      Council member could be involved that would be a conflict of interest and a violation of the Ethics Act. He provided the Council
      with guidance regarding the types of activities or private business relationships that Council members should disclose to avoid a
      claim of a conflict of interest. Finally, he provided an overview of the intent ofGRAMA, the types of records the City maintains,
      the retention schedule that provides guidance relative to records classification and the length of time the City must retain a record.
                Sexual Harassment: Human Resources Manager Judkins used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide training
      regarding harassment; the presentation addressed topics such as the type ofbehavior that can be defmed as harassment, protected
      classes, the steps a victim of harassment should take to address the situation they are in, and how to combat harassment and
      discrimination in the workplace.
                Distracted Driving: Ms. Judkins then addressed distracted driving; this is any activity that could divert a person's
      attention away from the primary task of driving. She presented statistics regarding increases in crashes resulting from distracted
      driving and discussed steps City employees can take to prevent distracted driving.
                Cyber Security: Information Technologies Manager Lefgren used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to discuss cyber
      security; all individuals are responsible for cyber security, should know where their data is being stored, and who has access to
')    their data. He offered suggestions each Council member can implement to protect their data and information as well as to protect
      their devices from viruses and other malicious software. He encouraged each Council member to reach out to him if they have
      questions about technology or equipment that they are issued by the City.
                Stormwater Pollution Prevention: Public Services Deputy Director Anderson used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation
      to provide training regarding stonnwater pollution prevention, which is mandated by Utah Law. Utah Pollutant Discharge

Elimination System (UPDES) is a program created by the United States Environmental Protection Agency (USEPA) to address
water pollution by regulating sources that discharge pollutants to waters of the United States. The City is required to comply with
UPDES rules and regulations through six minimum control measures:
              • Public Education/Outreach
              •   Public Involvement and Participation
              •   Illicit Discharge Detection and Elimination
              •   Construction Site Stormwater Runoff Control
              •   Post Construction Stormwater Management
              •   Pollution Prevention and Good Housekeeping
         The City's Stormwater Management Plan is available at www.ogdencity.com/419/Storm-Water.

Council Business
          Committee Assignments: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported that she has provided the Council with a list
of committee assignments for their review; any assignment adjustments should be fmalized by this Thursday and she will provide

          Graffiti: Council member Stephens stated he has noticed an increase in graffiti in his neighborhood and other areas of
the City. He asked who residents can contact to report this problem. Public Services Deputy Director Anderson indicated Council
member Stephens can email him directly; there is also a graffiti hotline for City residents to contact and he indicated he will
provide Council member Stephens with that information.
