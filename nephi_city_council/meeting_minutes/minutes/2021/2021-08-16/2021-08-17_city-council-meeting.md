Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then
Councilor Memmott offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

 The Pledge of Allegiance was led by the new Juab High School girls wrestling coach, Billy Cox. Mr. Cox
gave a brief introduction of himself and expressed his goals as the new coach.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting held on 8-3-21, work session held on 8-3-21, work session held on 8-10-
21. a list of claims on the warrant register dated 8-17-21. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

   City Recorder Lisa E. Brough reviewed two business license applications for Carson’s Aeration and The
Goal Done Girl LLC. Councilor Memmott moved to approve the licenses. Councilor Worwood seconded
the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Brad Hancock from Fountain Green addressed the council about the mismanagement of the gun
range. He said that it was a slap in the face for Nephi City to be charging non-residents $500 a year to
use the range.
  Rod Hansen said that he was a city councilman for Fountain Green and he said when he saw the signs
at the gun range indicating the new fees. He also thought it was a slap in the face. He said he spends
most of his money in Nephi at the grocery store and gas stations. He asked if the council would
reconsider the fee structure.
   Kyle Garrett asked the council for approval of selling beer at special events. He said there are many
checks and balances in place to obtain a liquor license. He stated that the State of Utah helps enforce
the strict laws governing the sale of alcohol. He said that the people drinking whiskey in the parking lot
are the ones that cause problems. He said that the Chief of Police and the County Sheriff both said that
last year’s Xtreme Bull Riding event where beer was sold was one of the best events they have ever
been involved in for controlling illegal activities related to alcohol. Mr. Garrett stated that he is in favor
of a special event’s liquor license. He said he knows each of the councilors and thought it was time for
them to broaden their horizons.

FISCAL YEAR 2021 FRAUD RISK ASSESSMENT APPROVED:

  City Recorder Lisa Brough reviewed the Fraud Risk Assessment for fiscal year 2021. The Utah State
Auditor’s Office (USAO) has developed a five-level assessment score that is intended to communicate
the entity’s risk of undetected fraud, abuse, or noncompliance. Ms. Brough stated that Nephi City
scored much higher this year because elected officials committed in writing to abide by a statement of
ethical behavior and that all governing body members completed online training for municipal officials.
Nephi City has a moderate risk level based on this assessment. Councilor Worwood moved to authorize
the Mayor to sign the Fraud Risk Assessment. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion
passed on unanimous vote.

BYRCE’S LANDING SUBDIVISION PLAT A APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson presented the final plat for Bryce’s Landing Subdivision Plat A to the council for approval.
He stated that the road widths along with other issues had been worked out with the developer. The
subdivision includes six commercial lots and will be zoned Combined Use. Councilor Memmott moved
to approve the final plat for Bryce’s Landing Subdivision. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NORTH RIDGE PHASE B ZONE CHANGE -ORDINANCE 08-17-2021 APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the developers of North Ridge Phase B had requested a zone
change from R1-H zone to R1-8 zone. The proposed zone change would be consistent with the zone
change that occurred to the west of this development for North Ridge Phase A with the exception of the
zero-lot line overlay. It could also be consistent with the city’s current General Plan. This area was
designated as higher density residential within the General Plan’s conceptual zoning. Councilor
Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 08-17-2021, an ordinance amending the zone map. Councilor
Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 08-17-2021 DECLARING A BLIGHT AREA ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a request from Alex Johnson to designate property at 720
South Main as a blight area. The council agreed that the property did meet the criteria of a blight area
outlined in blight ordinance recently adopted. Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Resolution 08-17-
2021 and declare the property a blight area with the recommendation that no landfill fees be waived
and the bonus density incentive be the only incentive given. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 08-17-2021A ADOPTED-AMENDMENT TO THE BEER LICENSE CODE:

   A discussion ensued about prohibiting beer licenses at special events. The City Council had recently
examined two ordinances regarding beer licenses for special or one-time events. One ordinance would
have allowed beer licenses for these events under certain conditions, and the second ordinance would
have prohibited a beer license for these events.
   The Council introduced both ordinances and sought public input. The public input was mainly in favor
of an ordinance prohibiting beer sales for special or one-time events. Councilor Seely questioned if the
issue could be added to the ballot at the general election in November through a referendum. City
Attorney Kasey Wright thought that it was too late to put on the ballot.
   Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Ordinance 08-17-2021A- Amendments to Beer License Code.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following roll-call vote:
                                 Ostler-Yes
                                 Worwood- Yes
                                 Memmott- Yes
                                 Seely- Nay

STREET VACATION WAS REMOVED FROM THE AGENDA

letter stated that the city understands that UDOT will require that 1450 North at the intersection of

UDOT CORRIDOR AGREEMENT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson presented two agreements associated with the state highways that run through the city.
UDOT would like to enter into a corridor preservation agreement for these two highways. The
agreements mainly identified future potential traffic lights along these state roads. UDOT would retain
the right to designate an intersection to be signalized but would be done in consultation with the city. It
does not mean that another intersection couldn’t be signalized or that each of the identified
intersections would be signalized. In addition, there may be conditions where certain intersections
being signalized would be dependent on whether nearby intersections would be signalized. Councilor
Memmott moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the two UDOT Corridor Agreements. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Seely moved to adjourn into executive session to discuss the character and competence of
an individual. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:25 p.m.
