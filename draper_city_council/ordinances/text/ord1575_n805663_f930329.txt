City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On January 10, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1575, correcting
duplicate ordinance numbers for Ordinance #1020 and Ordinance #1043. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 11th day of January 2023. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
