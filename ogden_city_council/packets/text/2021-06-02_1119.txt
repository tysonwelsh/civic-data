                                            NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
 Notice is hereby given that the Ogden City Planning Commission will meet Wednesday, June 2, 2021 at
 5pm with an anchor location in the City Council Chambers, 3rd Floor, Ogden Municipal Building, 2549
 Washington Boulevard, in Ogden City, Weber County, Utah, and during that meeting will then and there
 will conduct a public hearing to consider the following:
      1. Petition to rezone property at 978 Washington Boulevard from C-2 to C-3/CO to allow impound
           lot for credit union
      2. Petition to rezone property at 768 W 24th from R-1-5 to MU to allow a townhome development.
      3. Proposed Ordinance Amendment to 15-7-9 to allow minor amendments for conditional uses to be
           approved by Planning Staff.
      4. Proposed Ordinance Amendment to 15-7-4 to revise notification requirements to conform with
           State law.
 At the above time and place any person or persons interested in the final passage of said action will be
 given full time to be heard.

 This meeting will also be held electronically. Any person or persons interested in these items may
 participate electronically by following the instructions below or by attending at the anchor location.
