PRESENTER:                Craig Calvert

                          Resolution No. 25-54 - Authorizing a purchase order contract be issued to
ITEM:                     Core Industrial Group for the completion of the 4800 Pump House Manifold
                          Replacement Project - Craig Calvert, Purchasing Manager

SUMMARY:

Towards the end of this year's watering season a pipe on the pressure side of the 4800-pump station
manifold ruptured. The Water Department was able to put a temporary patch on the pipe to get through
the watering season. The manifold is constructed out of epoxy coated and lined steel pipe. The pipe that
ruptured was rusted through. The Water Department assumes that all of the existing pipes are in the
same condition and wants to replace all of them instead of just one pipe.

In the FY26 budget, they have been approved to replace a canal crossing pipe, as well as do some
operational maintenance on some pumps. They would like to use those funds to replace the 4800-pump
station manifold instead and do the canal crossing pipe and pump maintenance in next FY budget.

The project was bid out, and the tabulation is attached.
