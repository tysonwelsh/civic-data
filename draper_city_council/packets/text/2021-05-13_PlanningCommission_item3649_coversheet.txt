Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: May 13, 2021
Re: Willow Creek Industrial Properties Lot 2 Amended Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an Amended Site Plan including deviations from site and architectural design standards for approximately 2.06 acres located on the north side of 12300 South, at approximately 432 W. 12300 S. The property is currently zoned CBP. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Site Plan be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site with an industrial building. The findings are as follows: Deviation Request from DCMC Section 9-22-050(A)(1) – Building Placement
For Approval:
1. The overall percentage of dedicated public or private landscape and open space area exceeds the required amount by more than ten percent (10%).
2. The variations are consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
3. The proposed project creates a consistent and compatible design in this case which involves a new building within a previously approved phased project.
4. The amount of primary materials used for a facade is at least ten percent (10%) greater than the minimum required amount.
For Denial:
1. The variations are not consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
2. The proposed project does not create a consistent and compatible design within the previously approved phased project.
Deviation Request from DCMC Section 9-22-040(C)(2) – Facade articulation
For Approval:
1. The overall percentage of dedicated public or private landscape and open space area exceeds the required amount by more than ten percent (10%).
2. The variations are consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
3. The proposed project creates a consistent and compatible design in this case which involves a new building within a previously approved phased project.
4. The amount of primary materials used for a facade is at least ten percent (10%) greater than the minimum required amount.
For Denial:
1. The variations are not consistent with the purpose of this chapter and any applicable master plan or ordinance.
2. The proposed project does not create a consistent and compatible design within the previously approved phased project.
Amended Site Plan
For Approval:
1. The site plan amendment represents completion of the phased development plan for the overall site.
2. The proposed site plan conforms to all applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the CBP zone.
4. Public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of the DCMC, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns.
For Denial:
1. The proposed site plan does not conform to all applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
