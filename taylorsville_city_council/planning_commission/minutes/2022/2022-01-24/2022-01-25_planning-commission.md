             Mr. Spung reviewed with the Commissioners what the Utah State Code § 10- 9a- 103( 8) states regarding
            conditional    uses as: "   Conditional use means a land use that, because of the unique characteristics or
             potential impact of the land use on the municipality, surrounding neighbors, or adjacent land uses, may not
             be compatible in some areas or may be compatible only if certain conditions are required that mitigate or
            eliminate the detrimental impacts."       Utah State Code § 10- 9a- 507 says: "   A land use authority ( Planning
            Commission in this City), shall approve a conditional use if reasonable conditions are proposed or can be
            imposed, to mitigate the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use in accordance with
            applicable standards."  He also reviewed in detail the code regarding location restrictions for uses within
            two hundred fifty feet of a residential district, (Land Development Code § 13. 08. 020) and Land Development
            Code § 13. 33. 030( D) regarding non -administrative conditional use preliminary review by the Planning
            Commission.

3. 1        Mr. Spung presented this item.       Q
            3. 1. 1     It is a Non -administrative Conditional Use Permit because it is commercial and located right next
                        to a residential area. Therefore, the Planning Commission must review and approve the project.
                        Mr. Elliott Smith is requesting on behalf of Mister Carwash to have Planning Commission review
                        and approval of a preliminary non -administrative conditional use permit and site plan to allow the
                        construction and use of a car wash on this site. The applicant' s proposal includes the removal of
                        existing structures (the existing Village Inn Restaurant) to make way for the construction of a new
                        tunnel car wash facility to be owned and operated by Mister Carwash. The property is in the
                        Community Commercial (CC) zoning district. According to City Code, a " car wash" land use requires
                        conditional use review in that zoning district. The subject property is also located within 250 feet
                        of a residential zoning district requiring that the conditional use permit request be reviewed and
                        decided by the Planning Commission.

          3. 1. 2     Mr. Spung showed a list of review criteria for all conditional use permit requests, with the main
