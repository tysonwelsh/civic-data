                                 PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
                                              Meeting of June 10, 2021
                           Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session on
Thursday, June 10, 2021. Chair Dickinson called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Roylan Croshaw, Regina Dickinson, Sandy Goodlander, Jordy Guth, Ken
Heare, David Lewis

Commissioners Excused: Jessica Lucero

Staff Present: Aaron Smith, Tanya Rice, Russ Holley, Ben Anderson, Issa Hamud, Tyler Richards,
Kymber Housley, Bill Young, Jeannie Simmonds (City Council Liaison)
Commissioner Guth moved to approve the minutes from the May 27, 2021 meeting as submitted.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Goodlander. The motion was approved unanimously.

Commissioner Goodlander moved to continue PC 21-028, Shepard Development to the June 24th
meeting. Commissioner Heare seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING

 PC 21-030 Jenny Sanchez Daycare [Conditional Use Permit] Jenny Sanchez/Jose Hernan
 Marroquin, authorized agent/owner, request a conditional use permit to allow up to 16
 children in a home daycare located at 684 East Center Street in the Traditional
 Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 06-088-0026. (Wilson Neighborhood)


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a conditional use permit to allow up to 16 children in a
home daycare located at 684 East Center Street in the NR-6 zone. The fire marshal has inspected
the home and verified that the 1000 sq ft being used for the daycare meets the requirement of 36
sq ft per child. Two parking stalls are required for the home and two additional stalls are required
for the daycare, one for the employee that will be there during the day, and one designated for
drop-off and pick-up. The parents will be required to stagger times because of the parking stall
availability. there is also an option to expand the driveway for additional parking. Commissioner
Guth asked if there are any requirements for fencing. R. Holley said that the health department will
regulate the fencing for the outdoor play area.

PROPONENT: No comments were made.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Croshaw asked if there was a fence around the property.
Commissioner Dickinson said that the state has regulations for daycares that are stringently
monitored. K. Housley stated that the outdoor play area, wherever they decide to put it, will have
to be fenced. Commissioner Guth asked how a drop-off would work if there was already someone
parked in the drop-off spot. R. Holley said a condition could be added regarding the parking, and
how it would work if the one spot was already in use. Commissioner Goodlander made a motion to


add to condition number 3 that an additional parking stall will be added on the west side of the
driveway. Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

MOTION: Commissioner Croshaw moved to conditionally approve a conditional use permit as
outlined in PC 21-030 with the amended conditions and findings listed below. Commissioner Lewis
seconded the motion.

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
   available in the Community Development Department.
2. No outside activities permitted earlier than 7:00 am.
3. Drop-off and pick-up times shall be located in the driveway and scheduled and staggered so that
   no more than one (1) client vehicle is at the home at any one time. An additional parking stall
   shall be established in a side-by-side location west of the existing driveway and outside of
   setbacks.
4. Not including the garage, four (4) off-street parking stalls shall be provided on-site in conforming
   locations (two for the residence and two for the daycare use)
5. The proponent shall comply with all State and local regulations and licenses regarding in-home
   daycare facilities for up to 16 clients.
6. Prior to issuance of Business License, the Director of Community Development shall receive a
   written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that their
   requirements have been satisfied:
     a. Fire —contact 716-9515
           • Inspection required prior to opening for 16 children.
     b. Engineering —contact 716-9153
           • When Center Street and Mountain Road were reconstructed by the City around 2000, all
               on street parking was removed on Center Street. A quick check on the City aerials shows
               the existing width from the yellow center turn lane to the toe of the curb is approximately
               13’. Travel lanes on this type of collector road are between 11-12 feet in width. Based on
               the 13’ measurement there is not enough room to allow on street parking of any type or
               time along Center Street.
     c. Water/Cross Connection—contact 716-9627
           • A hazard assessment should be done at this property to look for the possibility of a
               cross connection to ensure the safety of the water for the home owner, the children,
               and the City’s water supply.
     d. Environmental —contact 716-9761
           • Applicant will want to work with neighbors/customers on garbage day to coordinate the
               garbage cans are placed where they can be emptied without being blocked.
FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. This daycare is compatible with surrounding land uses and zoning designations and, as
   conditioned, will not interfere with the use and enjoyment of adjoining properties.
2. As proposed, the streets providing access to the subject property has adequate capacity for the
   proposed use and parking/drop-off/pick-up management will be provided and regulated.
3. Other infrastructure to the subject property has adequate capacity, or suitable levels of service,
   for the proposed use.
4. The Conditional Use Permit conforms to the requirements of Title 17 of the Logan Municipal
   Code as an identified conditional use.
5. The project was noticed as required by State and City regulations and proper procedure was
   followed in processing this request.


Moved: R. Croshaw             Seconded: D. Lewis             Approved: 6-0

Yea: Croshaw, Dickinson, Goodlander, Guth, Heare, Lewis             Nay:            Abstain:


 PC 21-032 Mountainside Estates [Subdivision Permit] Brent J Lawyer/Sugar Plum Logan
 LLC, authorized agent/owner, request to subdivide 26.14 acres into 64 single-family
 residential lots, located at approximately 1350 North 1200 East in the Suburban
 Neighborhood Residential (NR-4) zone; TIN 05-011-0006. (Hillcrest Neighborhood)

STAFF: T. Rice reviewed the request for a subdivision permit to subdivide 26.14 acres into 64
single-family residential building lots. The project meets the density and lot size requirements.
The south access to 1200 East is too close to 1300 North and will need to be moved. A
landscaped plan will need to be provided for the open space and detention pond. Commissioner
Goodlander asked about moving the entrance from 1200 East to the north. T. Rice said the
entrance needs to move north 300 feet from 1300 North. Commissioner Guth asked about the
sloped lots on the south and east sides of the parcel and the possibility of sluffing. T. Rice said
that there are very specific criteria that must be met to build on a slope. B. Young, City Engineer
talked about the critical land section in the land development code. Lots with a steep slope
(greater than 30%) need to be marked on the plat map with a note referencing the land
development code that lists the requirements to develop. An evaluation by a geotechnical
engineer is required to be submitted for approval along with a report showing what is proposed
before a building permit can be obtained.

PROPONENT: Matt Nielson asked if there were any questions from the committee.
Commissioner Croshaw asked if there was enough room to shave off some slope. Matt said they
have plenty of space to work with to take the slope down.

PUBLIC: Ray Elliott, a homeowner that lives next to the project, talked about the slope,
composition of the soil, and how he has watched the slope move 10 inches over the past 10 years.
He spoke about having a 15-foot setback to the toe of slope and it being listed on the final plat on
every affected lot, as well as recorded on the deed of every affected lot. He asked if there would
be a community mailbox and if so, could it be located inside the development to avoid traffic
congestion. He asked if there could be a condition set for the fence setbacks so there is not a zig-
zag line of fencing along 1400 North and 1300 East and also so the views of the homeowners to
the east do not get blocked. He asked who will maintain the park strip along the back of the homes
and If there will need to be a traffic light at 1400 North 1200 East. T Rice said that is Conditioned
with a traffic study. He talked about concerns with obtaining a bond or irrevocable letter of credit.

Tim Wright asked about the entrance on 1500 North and if it was far enough away from 1400 East.
Commissioner Dickinson said that was looked at by the Engineering department. He talked about
a fence being placed on the lot at the top of 1400 North potentially blocking the view of traffic, and
the possibility of people not purchasing the steep-sloped lots on the south end of the subdivision
and them becoming an eyesore.

Morris Poole talked about the detention pond and the street water that he has seen flood out 1400
North several times over the past years. He said the city needs to address the street water, so
these new homes do not get flooded. He also asked that it be included in the CC&R’s that the
homeowners maintain the abutting parks strips along the back of the homes.


Jeannie Simmonds mentioned that the commission can request to review any changes made to
the project before approving.

Erick Lamalfa talked about the amount of open space and if it is enough. He said all the
engineering on these sloped lots needs to be done before this gets approved, so the homeowner
doesn’t have to deal with the issues after they have purchased it. He would also like to see more
connectivity from the subdivision to nearby parks.

Rick Humphreys talked about his concerns with potential sliding issues and agrees that the
engineering needs to be done before approval of the project.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Dickinson wanted to address the questions brought up during
public comments. She stated that that the fencing is addressed in condition number 15, the
landscaping maintenance of the rear setback is addressed in condition number 12, and the
Geotechnical report is included in condition 17.b, Engineering, bullet 11. B. Young said that there
are two reports required, one is a general site report for the entire development, and one is lot-
specific once it is known what will be built, this report cannot be done upfront. K. Housley
referenced Land Development Code 17.24.070 B that lists 9 requirements to be met on lots that
have a slope of 30% or greater.

Commissioner Guth asked about the proponent moving dirt at the site to try and even out the
slopes. B. Young said that the Geotechnical report would have to be submitted before any type of
disturbance can be done on lots with a slope of 30% or greater. A. Smith asked if there was a
specific permit that needs to be pulled. B. Young said that a land disturbance permit would have to
be obtained and that is when the Geotechnical report and plan would be checked.

Commissioner Heare asked where the stormwater is diverted to at the bottom of 1500 North. B.
Young said that there is a storm drainpipe in the road that connects to the canal. There will be
piping installed with the curb and gutter under the entrances to the subdivision so the stormwater
can be fed into that system.

Commissioner Dickinson asked if “the property owner” could be removed from condition number 12
so that it states the maintenance of the rear setbacks is the responsibility of the HOA only.

Commissioner Guth said she liked the comment regarding connectivity and asked if there were any
easements to connect the subdivision to the neighborhood to the east. K. Housley said there are
no city easements, the property owners could get together and create a path.

Commissioner Goodlander asked if fencing can be conditioned. K. Housley said that the only
requirement is compliance of the fence code.

Commissioner Croshaw asked about the plat map and it being clear where the slopes are. A.
Smith said that it is listed in the conditions under engineering bullet 3 that these areas must be
marked on the plat. Commissioner Lewis asked if this notation included the 15’ setback from the
toe of slope. A. Smith said it does not include that, it is only for the heavily sloped areas, 30% or
greater, and a note that references the code for the development requirements in these areas.

Commissioner Dickinson asked about a landscaping bond. K. Housley said that the Engineering
Department will request a bond if needed. Commissioner Lewis said that he is still concerned
about the fencing along the main roads, and the buildability on the sloped lots. He would like to
see the project come back with the issues addressed. Commissioner Heare said he would also
like to see the revised project come back to the committee.


MOTION: Commissioner Goodlander moved to continue PC 21-032 to the June 24th meeting for a
decision on the revised plans for the entrance and the landscaping around the detention pond.
The public hearing is closed. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

(K. Housley clarified with the Committee that the continuance is for a decision and not public
hearing).
Moved: S. Goodlander                  Seconded: R. Croshaw                   Approved: 5 -1

Yea: Croshaw, Goodlander, Guth, Heare, Lewis          Nay: Dickinson         Abstain:


 PC 21-031 Logan City Compost Facility [Conditional Use Permit] Tyler Richards/Logan
 City, authorized agent/owner, request a conditional use permit to allow a new Logan City
 compost facility located at approximately 2100 West 200 North in the Public (PUB) zone; TIN
 05-057-0001; -0004; -0005; -0006. (Ellis Neighborhood)

STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a conditional use permit to allow a new compost facility
at 2100 West 200 North that includes a green waste drop-off. The green waste will be airyated in
static piles and rotated until it becomes compost. Material will be extracted from the new
wastewater treatment center to the west and amended into the compost material to create a better
blend of compost. This site is further away from residential homes than the current site, next to the
landfill, that will be phased out. The biggest concern would be with the smell as the compost piles
are turned. R. Holley also talked about the future phases to include a garden area, parking lot,
barn, and landscaping berm along the highway. Commissioner Guth asked about the entrance
being so far east of the project. R. Holley said that UDOT determines where the entrance will be.
The entrance to the facility will be a two-lane driveway, there will not be a curb and gutter installed.

PROPONENT: Madeline Tennant presented a slide show of the new facility, how it will work,
potential issues that may come up and how they will be handled. Paul Wilardson talked about the
phasing of the project and the time frame.

PUBLIC: Brent Bryner, Owner of the closest property to the southeast of the facility. He is in favor
of the project, only concern is with the entrance being lined up with the access to his property.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Guth asked if the entrance needed to be added to the conditions.
R. Holley said that is listed in condition number 3, access specifications will be determined by
UDOT.

MOTION: Commissioner Heare moved to conditionally approve a Conditional Use Permit as
outlined in PC 21-031 with the conditions and findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner
Guth seconded the motion.

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Design Review and are available
   in the Community Development Department.
2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes the Logan City Compost Facility on the indicated
   properties and in accordance with the proposed plans.
3. Vehicular access specifications shall be determined by UDOT.
4. The Planning Commission reviewed and approved the parking plan showing 36 parking stalls
   as proposed for the barn and community garden.


5. Employee parking for the compost facility, which is currently two employees, can be
    accommodated at the wastewater treatment operation building or on the perimeter of the
    compost facility.
6. The final barn design will be reviewed and approved by the Planning Commission when that
    phase is proposed for construction at a later date.
7. The landscaping berm and screen shall be utilized for the wastewater treatment facility and
    the compost facility to visually screen uses/structures. The barn and community gardens
    should not be visually screened and designed to be a welcoming landmark to the valley.
8. Lot coverage shall not exceed 50%.
9. Prior to a building permit being approved and issued, a stamped licensed property survey
    shall be submitted verifying 25-foot river setbacks (top of bank), other required setbacks and
    overall property dimensions.
10. Prior to issuance of a Building Permit, the Director of Community Development shall receive
    a written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that
    their requirements have been satisfied:
      a. Engineering —contact 716-9153
            • The Environmental Department will need to schedule a meeting with UDOT CAMP for
               access requirements to SR-30.
            • Will coordinate any water/sewer connections during design review for building permits.
               This will include water shares or in-lieu fees for water.
            • Must comply with current storm water design standards and construction permits with
               State
      b. Water/Cross Connection—contact 716-9627
            • If Logan City water is to be used for this project, the line serving this project must be after
                a RP (ASSE1013) backflow assembly for containment protection. (assembly in heated
                building for year-round use). Then points of use protection will be needed per code.
            • All points of use of water must comply with the 2018 IPC and State of Utah Amendments
                and the Utah Admin Code 309-305 during and after construction.
            • Project shall comply with all current plumbing codes, Utah State Amendments, Utah
                Division of Drinking Water rules and regulations including, but not limited to, those
                pertaining to backflow protection and cross connection prevention.
FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. The proposed project as conditioned is compatible with surrounding land uses and will not
    interfere with the use and enjoyment of adjacent properties because of the site layout, lot
    coverage, landscaping and setbacks.
2. The Conditional Use Permit conforms to the requirements of Title 17 of the Logan Municipal
    Code.
3. The proposed project provides off-street parking in compliance with the LDC.
4. The project met the minimum public noticing requirements of the Land Development Code and
    the Municipal Code.
5. The surrounding streets provide access, utilities and are adequate in size and design to
     sufficiently handle all traffic modes and infrastructure related to the land use.
Moved: K. Heare                 Seconded: J. Guth                Approved: 6-0

Yea: Dickinson, Croshaw, Goodlander, Guth, Heare, Lewis                  Nay:             Abstain:
