4.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Ivory Development’s request for review of the
Holbrook Farms Area Plan Amendment changing the land use map and changing the
location of residential units located at approximately Hudson Way and Redwood Road.

Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Kim Struthers clarified if there was a way on the corner to ensure it wasn't going to be large light
industrial buildings, they are willing to commit on the corner to limitations of the scale of the
building size.

The applicant, Spencer Moffit with Boyer Company, was present on behalf of Brad Mackay. They
agreed to scale down their buildings and are hopeful to enhance the community.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall summarized the online comments that mostly consisted of parking.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommend approval of Ivory Development’s
        request for review of the Holbrook Farms Area Plan Amendment changing the land
        use map and changing the location of residential units located at approximately
        Hudson Way and Redwood Road; including all DRC comments and findings stated in
        the report. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Mammoth Holding’s request for approval of the
zone change, 1.95-acres located at approximately at 26 South 2300 West changing the zoning
from TH-5 (transitional holding) to Commercial.

4.5) Public hearing and consideration of Mammoth Holding’s request for approval of the
Wiggy Wash car wash concept plan located at 26 South 2300 West.

Brittney Harris presented 4.4 and 4.5 simultaneously.

The applicant, Brent Wignall, was present. Commissioner Everett asked why they wanted this site
for this use. Mr. Wignall stated he has seen this property for a long time and had thought it would
be a great asset to Lehi and a car wash would be a great asset.

Commission Chair Newall opened public hearing to 4.4

Commission Chair Newall closed public hearing to 4.4

Commission Chair Newall opened public hearing to 4.5

Commission Chair Newall acknowledged the online comment.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing to 4.5

Commissioner Carlson asked about road access. The applicant stated there are triggers that have
to be met before the purchase of the property is solidified. The applicant answered clarifying
questions regarding noise and light pollution, stacking, and water usage. The home was built in
the early 1900's and Commissioner Jackson would like to know if the applicant had a chance to
talk to the historical society with the home being so old.

Commissioner Everett asked with the proposed ingress/egress if it lined up with the 7-11 across
the drive and if it was allowed. Ross Dinsdale stated it would be allowed.

        Motion for 4.4: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Mammoth
        Holding’s request for approval of the zone change, 1.95-acres located at
        approximately at 26 South 2300 West changing the zoning from TH-5 (transitional
        holding) to Commercial; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

        Motion for 4.5: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Mammoth Holding’s request
        for approval of the Wiggy Wash car wash concept plan located at 26 South 2300 West;
        including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Also recommending the
        applicant meet with the Local Historical Society for preservation of the building.
        Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4.6) Consideration of EA Architects request for approval of the Holbrook Farms LDS
Meetinghouse located at approximately 710 North Pearl Drive.

Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Chad Spencer with EA Architecture, was present. Commissioner Carlson asked

about vegetation and planter strips.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve EA Architects request for approval of
        the Holbrook Farms LDS Meetinghouse located at approximately 710 North Pearl

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4.7) Public hearing and consideration of Howland Partner’s request for approval of the
Ridge Shopping Center Future Phase Concept Plan including 19.55-acres of apartments,
retail, and restaurants at approximately 2200 West Ridge Drive in a Commercial zone.

Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Dana Howland and Tina Francom with Howland Partners, were present. They are
planning on doing cross access parking so there will be plenty of parking. There will be access to
the trailhead from the patio. They have been working with UTA and feel fairly confident they will
be granted access.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair acknowledged the online comments.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale talked about walkability and how it would help.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to approve Howland Partner’s request for
        approval of the Ridge Shopping Center Future Phase Concept Plan including 19.55-
        acres of apartments, retail, and restaurants at approximately 2200 West Ridge Drive
        in a Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. The
        development improve trail and bike are connections and crossings, parking access involved
        with each use is improved, when the project comes back for site plan the applicant give a
        presentation on why the units require an exception. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.


4.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code Amendment to Chapter 35, adding standards for water efficient landscape
requirements.

Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Bunker is opposed to the code change and listed many reasons why it is detrimental
and expressed his opposition and frustration with the new landscape requirement from the state.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall closed public hearing.

Kim stated the adoption date was March 1, 2023.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to table the item to the January 26, 2023 meeting
        directing the city arborist, planning, and other staff to consult with Commissioner Bunker
        for information about the code amendment. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
