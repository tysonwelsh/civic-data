Shawn Seager Provo City Corridor Preservation Application for Funds – Andrew Jackson Andrew Jackson reviewed the questions identified at the March 1, 2018 MPO Regional Planning Committee meeting regarding the Provo City Corridor Preservation Application for Funds, and the clarifications to those questions made at the March 7, 2018 MPO Financial Committee meeting. Additional comments and clarifications were made by Committee Members and attendees. 1. Fund Balance: • Estimated revenues available are about $9,513,000. • Solid numbers should be available by end of March. • Taxes paid in 2015 are credited in 2016 and made available by the county for use in 2018. • The account has more than enough funds in it to make this purchase. Local Corridor Preservation Funds is a more restrictive funding source to purchase the entire Right of Way (ROW) that requires the developer to build the roadway, saving tax payer dollars by not using 3rd quarter cent funds. Provo City is requesting $1,809,257 of Local Corridor Preservation Funds for purchase of the ROW along the Lakeview Parkway Corridor. There is approximately $9 Million in reserves and approximately $2 Million in usable funds. Estimated available revenues are $9,513, 000. 2. Dynamics of the Project: • An alternative to Geneva Road, since historic properties on Geneva make it difficult to widen. • Create a belt loop corridor on the west side of Provo to take the pressure off Geneva Road. • Get traffic out of the urban corridor and move it to the outside edge. • Reserve ROW for 5 lanes and build 3 at this time. • Provides an alternate route for south county if there is a shut down on Geneva Road or I-15 in this area • Purchase the full right-of-way and developer will construct the road. The Lakeview Parkway Corridor will provide an alternative route for north and south corridor. The remaining section of road is curved because it is going around a wetland. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee March 14, 2018 Page 4 3. Timing and Width of the Project: • Purchase now as raw ground. • 2021 for construction to start. • 3 lanes now will go to 5 lanes. 60-70% of the 6-mile Lakeview Parkway Corridor is complete. The project is a continuation of the road that is already completed and will close the gap connecting both sides. Provo City has paid $8-10 Million into the project. 4. Options Available if Delayed 2 months: • Might not have a willing seller. • Delay could cause a change in direction and process. • Increase the cost. The neighborhood is no longer in favor with the project, therefore not funding the project could delay it indefinitely. 5. Density Bonus/Other Options: • Density bonuses have been applied. Density bonused were used for road acquisition. The tradeoff is Provo City purchases the land and Ivory Homes would build the road. Renegotiating the density bonds at this point would adversely affect the timing of the project. 6. Potential Consequence if not Funded: • Demands on the potential road way. • Minimize the impact on the 3rd Quarter tax by using more restrictive Corridor funds. • Density bonus has been considered and worked into the current development agreement and entitlement, changing the credits now would be difficult at this point in the process, since the neighborhood make up has changed. • Increase cost. Terri Newell stated at the March 7, 2018 MPO Finance Committee meeting that each corridor preservation opportunity is different and the cities don’t have the same tools or developers which creates a challenge. It is hopeful that the cities will use every tool possible to better utilize tax payer’s dollars. Mayor Brunst moved that the Mountainland Association of Governments authorize and approve Provo City’s request for $1,809,257 of local corridor preservation funds for purchase of the described Rights of Way along the Lakeview Parkway Corridor. Mayor Kaufusi seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Mayor Mann moved to adjourn the meeting at 11:25 am. Mayor Child seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. *Meeting minutes are recorded with a digital recorder.
