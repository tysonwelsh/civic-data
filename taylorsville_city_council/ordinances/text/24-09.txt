                    SUMMARY:     The property located at 4768 South Redwood Road is a deep commercial
                                 lot with an approximately 1, 800 square foot building with a rear garage, a
                                 paved and striped rear parking lot, and approximately 10, 000 square feet
                                 of undeveloped land on the rear, most western portion. The applicant, Elias
                                 Canas contacted the city to inquire about opening a used car dealership at
                                 this location. The property is zoned Community Commercial which
                                 prohibits the use. The applicant( potential tenant) has applied for a zoning
                                 map amendment with the permission of the property owner to request the
                                 property zoning designation be changed from Community Commercial to
                                 Boulevard Commercial, which would allow the operation of a used car
                                 dealership with the approval of a nonadministrative conditional use permit
                                 and business license.


                                 New zoning districts and zoning map were adopted as part of the Land
                                 Development Code update in 2012. Properties fronting on Redwood Road
                                 from 4700 South to 5000 South formerly zoned C- 2 were rezoned to
                                 Community Commercial, Boulevard Commercial and Mixed Use. The Land
                                 Development        Code defines        the districts   relevant to this application      as:


                                 Current   zone:    Community Commercial                 District(   CC):   This district is

                                 established to allow for retail businesses and related uses to be grouped
                                 together into well planned and designed planned commercial centers.

                                 Requested      zone:    Boulevard        Commercial      District( BC): This district is

                                 established to provide guidelines for the development ofproperties fronting




                                                           1
                                      on Redwood Road between 4100 South and 5000 South. Regulations                 are

                                      intended to allow a selective variety of uses within planned commercial
                                      centers in a manner that will contribute to efficient traffic flow and
                                      architectural elements compatible with adjoining residential neighborhoods.

                                      The subject property is adjacent to property zoned BC to the south and CC
                                      to the north. The property meets the location criteria in the BC definition.
                                      The BC district allows more intense retail/         service uses than in the

                                      Community Commercial ( CC) zone, some of which are elevated to require
                                      nonadministrative         conditional use review.


                                      The Planning Commission met on September 10, 2024, and voted 5- 2 to
                                      forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for the zoning map
                                      amendment request.

       WHEREAS,       the   Taylorsville   City   Council ( the " Council")   met in regular session on
October 2, 2024, to consider, among other things, approving a zoning map amendment to the
property located at 4768 S. Redwood Rd. in Taylorsville, Utah from Community Commercial to
Boulevard Commercial; and


       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 040, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was posted on Taylorsville City' s website
on August 29, 2024; and


         WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on September 10, 2024, during which the Planning Commission voted
6- 1 to recommend that the Council approve the proposed zoning map amendment; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on October
2, 2024; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE,              BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed zoning map amendment is hereby approved for the property described below:
