                                RESOLUTION #21-47

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING A TEMPORARY POLICY FOR THE
      PROCEDURAL IMPLEMENTATION OF ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT
      (ADU) PERMITS.

      WHEREAS, Utah State law grants to Draper City the authority to regulate uses
of property by enacting land use regulations, among other methods; and

     WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to amend certain terms of the
Draper City Municipal Code to comply with changes in state law; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City has found good cause to amend the
terms and provisions of the Land Use and Development Code and to enact new
provisions in response to changes in Utah State Code; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City did adopt Ordinance #1499
regulating the use of Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) within Draper City, and

      WHEREAS, Ordinance #1499 requires all property owners of single-family
dwellings with an ADU to obtain an ADU permit, and

       WHEREAS, Draper City desires to allow for an orderly transition to the new
regulations, including but not limited to the requirements that property owners of
single-family dwellings with an existing ADU apply for and obtain an ADU permit, or
for the abatement and removal of illegally created accessory dwelling units.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH:

     Section 1. Enactment.        The temporary policy for the procedural
implementation of ADUs as set forth in Exhibit A is hereby enacted.

      Section 2.    Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution and all provisions, clauses and words of this
Resolution shall be severable.
      Section 3. Effective Date.         This Resolution shall become effective
simultaneously with the effective date of Ordinance #1499.

     Section 3. Expiration Date. This Resolution shall expire March 31, 2023, or
18 months from the effective date of Ordinance #1499, whichever comes later.

TEMPORARY POLICY FOR THE PROCEDURAL IMPLEMENTATION OF ACCESSORY
                   DWELLING UNIT (ADU) PERMITS

ADU Permit Procedural Implementation Policy

  1. Application Window: All property owners, including new property owners,
     with property that contains an existing ADU (legal or not) will be given 6
     months from the date of the enactment of Draper City Ordinance #1499 to
     file a complete application for an ADU permit during which time there will be
     no enforcement actions taken against any owner or property for not having
     the required permit.

  2. Effects of not applying: Property owners with existing ADU’s that do not
     apply within the applicable application window, will not be given the
     extended grace periods detailed in this policy. If a property owner did not
     apply during the applicable application window and an existing ADU is found
     to have not been legally established, not be in compliance with ordinance, or
     not be permitted at the property, the property owner shall be notified of
     such and shall be given no more than 90 days to bring the property into
     compliance with the ordinance requirements and/or to remove a prohibited
     ADU from the property before further enforcement actions are taken.

  3. Extended grace period: For property owners that make a complete
     application during the applicable 6 month application window, an extended
     grace period of 12 months against enforcement actions for non-compliance
     with required standards shall be given:
        A. Applicable only to those who apply within the applicable 6 month
            application window.
        B. Grace period begins from the date the complete application for the
            ADU permit is submitted.
        C. Each ADU application received during the applicable 6 month
            application window will be reviewed for and extended the following:

              (1) Legal Status –
      (a) Owners of properties with ADUs that were not legally
          established or are not legal non-conforming will be
          notified that either:
             I. The ADU was not legally established or is not legal
                 non-conforming, and will be required to conform
                 to current ordinance or must be removed; or
            II. That the ADU was not legally established or is not
                 legal non-conforming, is prohibited under the
                 current ordinance, and must be removed.
      (b) Property owners that are notified that their ADU was not
          legally established or not legal non-conforming can be
          given up to 12 months from the date of such notification
          to correct any issues related to the legal establishment of
          an ADU under the new ordinance provisions, or to
          remove the ADU from the property if necessary.
      (c) ADUs that are legal non-conforming will be notified of
          their non-conforming status and the specific
          requirements applicable to their ADU. Permit will be
          processed and issued with the annual renewal date
          corresponding to the date the permit was issued and not
          the date the complete application was submitted.
      (d) Property owners may appeal a decision or provide
          additional information that might amend or alter a
          decision regarding the legal status of their ADU.

(2) Owner/Resident Occupancy –
      (a) If an ADU complies in all other aspects, but is not
          currently owner occupied as required by ADU standards,
          or for some legal non-conforming ADUs not compliant
          with applicable occupancy standards, the owner can be
          given up to 12 months from the date of notification by
          the City that they are not in compliance to bring the
          property into compliance. This can be accomplished
          under the new ordinance by occupying the primary
          dwelling as their primary residence, by
          transferring/selling ownership of the property to
                      someone who will occupy the primary dwelling as their
                      primary residence, or by ceasing use of the ADU. In some
                      cases where an ADU is internal to the primary dwelling,
                      removal of the ADU and restoration of physical
                      passageway connections to the remainder of the single-
                      family dwelling may be required.

            (3) Compliance with all other requirements –
                  (a) Building permits and/or demolition permits will be
                      required for any construction, or demolition work done to
                      bring an existing ADU into compliance, or for removal an
                      ADU and restoration of the property to that of a single-
                      family dwelling. Property owners in these circumstances
                      can be given up to 12 months from the date of
                      notification that the ADU is not legal or not in compliance
                      to obtain the applicable permit(s) and complete the
                      construction or demolition work.
                  (b) As needed, additional information may be requested
                      from the applicant to demonstrate compliance with
                      ordinance requirements.
                  (c) As needed, building, life-safety, and/or zoning inspections
                      may be required, especially in instances where remodel
                      work has already taken place to create the ADU for which
                      there was no building permit issued.
                  (d) Building, life-safety, and/or zoning inspections may be
                      required for properties where work done to create the
                      ADU was completed without the requisite permits.

4. Applications to create an ADU on a property without an existing ADU:
   The procedures listed in ordinance and typical processing methods and
   timeframes will be applied to ADU applications seeking to establish a new
   ADU that is not currently in existence on the property.
