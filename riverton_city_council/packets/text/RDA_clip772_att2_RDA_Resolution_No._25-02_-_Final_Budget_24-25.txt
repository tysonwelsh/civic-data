                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                              RDA RESOLUTION NO. 25-02

     A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
             ADOPTING FINAL AMENDED BUDGETS FOR THE
                        2024-2025 FISCAL YEAR

      WHEREAS, State law requires the adoption of final amendments to the Fiscal Year 2024-
2025 budget for each municipality located within the State of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the amended budgets comply with the requirements set out in U.C.A. § 10-6-
128; and

       WHEREAS, the budget have been reviewed, considered, and adopted by the governing body
and have now been hereby amended.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton Redevelopment Agency, State
of Utah, as follows:

       1. That Riverton City Redevelopment Agency adopts the Final Amended
          Budgets for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025,
          as attached hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
Agency this 17th day of June, 2025 by the following vote:
