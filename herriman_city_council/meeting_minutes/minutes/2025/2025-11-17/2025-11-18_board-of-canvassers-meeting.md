                              SPECIAL BOARD OF CANVASSERS
                                        MINUTES
                                      Tuesday, November 18, 2025
                                      Approved December 10, 2025
   The following are the minutes of the City Council Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The meeting
   was held on Tuesday, November 18, 2025, at 5:00 p.m. In the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers,
   5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by
   law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media,
   and interested citizens.

   Presiding: Mayor Lorin Palmer

   Councilmembers Present: Terrah Anderson, Jared Henderson, Teddy Hodges, and Sherrie Ohrn

   Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, City Recorder
   Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran, Communications
   Manager Jonathan LaFollette.

5:00 PM - SPECIAL MEETING:
   1. Call to order
Mayor Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.

   2. Results of General Election
           2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution certifying and declaring the results of the 2025
                municipal general election – Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
   City Recorder Jackie Nostrom offered a brief overview of the General Election held November 4, 2025. She
   reported that Lorin Palmer had received 6,884 votes, totalling 75.23 percent, for Herriman City Mayor, while
   Ty Brady had received 2,267 votes, or 24.77 percent. For Council District 2, Teddy received 1,959 votes as the
   sole candidate. In City Council District 3, Matt Basham received 1,583 votes (56.94 percent), and Heather Garcia
   received 1,197 votes (43.06 percent). For Council District 4, Terrah Anderson received 1,431 votes, running
   unopposed.

   City Recorder Nostrom provided detailed statistics regarding the canvas, noting a voter turnout of 31.4 percent,
   with 29,200 active registered voters and 9,174 total ballots counted. She explained that 8,895 ballots were
   received by mail, one person voted early in person, 257 voted on Election Day, and five ballots were received
   from military and overseas voters. City Recorder Nostrom reported that 16 provisional ballots were counted
   out of 17 received, with one rejected due to residency issues. She also mentioned an unusual circumstance
   involving a “fleeing voter” who began the voting process but did not finalize their ballot. Recorder Nostrom
   further detailed that 159 vote-by-mail ballots were challenged, with 114 ultimately rejected. Of those, 102 were
   rejected for signature mismatches, 11 for unsigned envelopes, and one for other reasons.

   City Recorder Nostrom declared that the elected candidates for the 2025 Herriman City Council are as follows:
   Lorin Palmer, Mayor; Teddy Hodges, Council District 2; Matt Basham, Council District 3; and Terrah Anderson,
   Council District 4.

   Councilmember Hodges moved to approve Resolution No. R59-2025 certifying the canvass of the 2025 Herriman City Municipal
   General Election. Councilmember Anderson seconded the motion.

   The vote was recorded as follows:
   Councilmember Terrah Anderson        Aye
   Councilmember Jared Henderson        Aye
   Councilmember Teddy Hodges           Aye
   Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn           Aye
   Mayor Lorin Palmer                   Aye

   The motion passed unanimously.

   3. Adjournment
   Councilmember Ohrn moved to adjourn the Herriman City Board of Canvasser’s meeting at 5:05 p.m.
   Councilmember Hodges seconded the motion, and all voted aye.


   I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a
   true, accurate and complete record of the meeting held on November 18, 2025. This document constitutes
   the official minutes for the Herriman City Board of Canvasser’s Meeting.
