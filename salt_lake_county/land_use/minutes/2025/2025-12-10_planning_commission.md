PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
 
Consideration of Proposed Amendment to Salt Lake County’s West General Plan and Sandy Hills General 
Plan to add a Water Use and Preservation Element to Each Plan. Presenter: Garrett Stone with GSBS 
Consulting (Motion/Voting) 
 
GSBS Consulting, Planning Consultant Garrett Stone provided an analysis of the proposed water use and 
preservation element plan. 
 
Commissioners raised several questions about the role of the water element in the general plan and how 
it fits into the general plan overall. They were concerned that including it might lead to additional 
regulations at a time when project approvals—especially in the southwest portion of the Valley—are 
already experiencing delays.  Staff clarified that the document is intended as a guiding document and that 
policymakers were still empowered to balance the interests of all stakeholders when considering 
proposed ordinances and policies. 
 
Commissioners also asked about policy 1155, which outlines considerations for projects requesting tax-
increment financing. One recommendation within the water element is for the County Council to consider 
water conservation as a factor when applying policy 1155. Commissioners again expressed concern that 
additional regulations could make the approval process less efficient and increase delays.

Staff emphasized that state statute requires the County to include a water element into its General Plan, 
and this effort is meant to help meet that statutory requirement.  They also noted that any future decisions 
involving policies or ordinances would be reviewed by the Planning Commission and County Council, 
ensuring that all relevant issues are taken into account. 
 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Collard seconded that motion. 
Commissioners voted unanimously.
