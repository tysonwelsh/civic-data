Re: Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Special Use Permit for W8 Distributing

Comments:

W8 Distributing Inc dba Jade Bloom located at 357 W Life Science Way Suite 600 is seeking
Local Consent for a Special Use Permit to store and use alcohol in the manufacturing of
products.

The findings for Local Consent approval are as follows:

-Per DCMC 6-4-040 (B) (2), a special use permittee operating on a for-profit basis must not
be located within three hundred feet (300') of a community location (schools, churches,
public libraries, public playgrounds and parks) as measured from the nearest patron
entrance of the outlet by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the
property boundary of the community location.

The applicant has met this requirement.

-DCMC 6-4-070 (E) places no restrictions on the total number of special use permits that
can be granted by the City.
