PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on July 10, 2019 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2019-22 authorizing a text change to the Land Development Code to allow cannabis production as a conditional use in the A-1 and M-1 Zones. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
