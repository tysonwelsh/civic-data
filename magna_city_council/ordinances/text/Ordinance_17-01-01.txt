AN ORDINANCE RATIFYING WORK PERFORMED, PRIOR TO
INCORPORATION, FOR THE FORMAL CREATION OF THE MAGNA METRO
TOWNSHIP.
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Magna Metro Township Council met in a
regular public meeting on January 3, 2017
WITNESS
WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature, during the 2015 General Session, passed Senate
Bill 199 (SB199) creating the Metro Township form of municipal government and setting an
election for the voters of Magna on November 3, 2015 to choose between incorporating as a
Metro Township or Incorporating as a City; and
WHEREAS, SB199 also asked the voters to choose whether Magna would join the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as members of the District; and
WHEREAS, the Magna voters, in the November 3, 2015 election, voted to incorporate as
a Metro Township and to join the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as
members of the District; and
WHEREAS, the Magna Voters elected the five residents to serve as their representatives
on the Magna Metro Township Council on November 8, 2016; and
WHEREAS, to complete the incorporation process, per State Law, the Magna Metro
Township Council was required to file a notice of incorporation letter, a notice of boundary
action, and a Final Local Entity Plat with the Lieutenant Governor’s Office to achieve the final
certification of the Magna Metro Township as a municipal entity; and

WHEREAS, this work had to be performed prior to the Council’s official terms of office
commencing; and

WHEREAS, the elected members of the Magna Metro Township Council approved the
creation and filing of this work and authorized their chair to sign the required documents; and

WHEREAS, the required documents were filed with the Lieutenant Governor’s Office on
December 16, 2016; and

WHEREAS, the Lieutenant Governor certified the creation of the Magna Metro
Township on December 27, 2016; and

WHEREAS, the Certificate and Final Local Entity Plat were recorded on December 30,
2016 with the Salt Lake County Recorder, completing the incorporation process and officially
creating the Magna Metro Township.

RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council does
hereby ratify and approve the finished work attached to this ordinance, creating the Magna Metro
Township and accepting the date of January 2, 2017 as the official creation date of the Magna
Metro Township.
