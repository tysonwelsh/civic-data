                                                        MINUTES
                                            WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                            Tuesday, March 13, 2018 - 10:00 a.m.
                                  Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported
facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

E. RECOGNITION OF WEBER COUNTY & OGDEN CITY MOTHER OF THE YEAR RECIPIENTS:
     MARILYN FROERER, WEBER COUNTY; MARILOU WOLTHUIS, OGDEN CITY
    Chair Harvey stated that these mothers were honored recently by the American Mother Association and their
    contributions were now being recognized in the community. Ms. Wolthius, who has five sons, said that it is a
    blessing to be a mother and it has been her life. She referred to a quote from a church leader that motherhood is
    not a hobby but a calling from God. Her four words about being a mother are to teach, hug, love, and repeat 24/7.
    Ms. Froerer is honored for this recognition and is grateful for her family and her many blessings. The
    commissioners presented them with the book, The Art of Motherhood. They expressed their respect and
    appreciation for these mothers for their selflessness, great examples, leadership, and sacrifices.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
    1. 2018 GRANT FUNDING RECOMMENDATIONS FROM COUNTY RECREATION, ARTS, MUSEUMS & PARKS BOARD.
         Shelly Halacy, of the County Commission Office, gave an overview of RAMP. For 2018 there were
         $3,639,529 available and she outlined the distribution. The RAMP Board spends countless hours to make this
         a great process. Chair Harvey recognized the members. The commissioners noted the tremendous community
         involvement and benefit and recognized Ms. Halacy’s countless hours on this process as well.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the 2018 grant funding recommendations from the Weber County
         Recreation, Arts, Museums and Parks (RAMP) Board; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    2. FIRST READING OF FEE ORDINANCE AMENDMENT RELATING TO THE WEBER COUNTY COMMUNITY &
       ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT – SOLID WASTE DIVISION (SEC. 16-2-9 SOLID WASTE FEES).
         Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Director, presented the need to amend the Transfer Station
         fees (to increase $1.24/ton from March to June and an additional $1.24 beginning in July) due to increased
         transportation costs. The fee changes were presented to WACOG. He addressed Commissioner Ebert’s
         question stating that this item had gone before the subcommittee, which recommended approval.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the first reading of a fee ordinance amendment relating to the County
         Community and Economic Development Department, Solid Waste Division; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    3. REQUEST FOR CONTRACT WITH ES&S FOR PURCHASE OF VOTING EQUIPMENT FOR COUNTY ELECTIONS.
         This item was held.
    4. FINAL APPROVAL OF RACKLIFF SUBDIVISION, CONSISTING OF 1 LOT, WITH REQUEST TO DEDICATE TO THE
       COUNTY 1,190 SQ. FT. IN AREA TO 1800 SOUTH STREET & TO DEFER ASPHALT, CURB, GUTTER & SIDEWALK.

         Felix Lleverino, of County Planning, stated that area will be dedicated along the public right-of-way for future
         expansion of 1800 S. The Western Weber Planning Commission recommended final approval with the request
         to dedicate area to the county and deferral of curb, gutter and sidewalk. A letter from the Taylor West Weber
         Water is required indicating that ½ a share of secondary water has been held by the District for providing
         culinary water.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to grant final approval of Rackliff Subdivision, one lot, with the request to
         dedicate to the county 1,190 square feet in area to 1800 S. Street and to defer asphalt, curb, gutter and sidewalk;
         Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    5. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH PLAIN CITY FOR THE 3600 WEST PROJECT.
         Brooke Stewart, of weber e.d.p., outlined this project. Plain City is contributing $210,642 and has received
         $2.9+ million from WFRC and STP funds. The City is requesting $124,108 from corridor preservation funds
         and $420,360 from sales tax. This request was approved in 2017 by WACOG and the County Commission.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Local Transportation Funding Agreement with Plain City for the
         3600 West project; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

       This item was held.

    11. CONTRACT WITH PAPER LANTERN INVESTMENTS, LLC, DBA BEAN-A-COLADA FOR LEASE OF COMMERCIAL
        SPACE IN THE WEBER COUNTY MAIN LIBRARY FOR THE PURPOSE OF OPERATING A CAFÉ/COFFEE SHOP.

         Lynnda Wangsgard, County Library Director, stated that this company was selected through a bid process and
         has been the successful bidder on three library projects. The contract was approved by the Library Board. Ms.
         Wangsgard responded to Commissioner Ebert’s question stating that the county does not subsidize the café.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the contract with Paper Lantern Investments, LLC, dba Bean-a-Colada
         for lease of commercial space in the Weber County Main Library (expiring 3/31/2021) for the purpose of
         operating a café/coffee shop; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
