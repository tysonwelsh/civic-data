                   SUMMARY: The property owner is requesting to amend the zoning map to change
                            the zone of the property located at 4645 S. 1175 W. from R-1-10 to R-
                            1-5. The property owner has expressed interest in pursuing a two-lot
                            subdivision on the property if the amendment is approved.
               BACKGROUND: Svetlana Bryner, property owner, is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to
                                rezone the property at 4645 S. 1175 W. from R-1-10 to R-1-5. The R-1-10
                                zoning district is characterized as a residential district allowing one dwelling
                                unit on a minimum 10,000 square foot lot. The R-1-5 zoning district allows
                                one dwelling unit on a minimum 5,000 square foot lot. The applicant has
                                expressed interest in pursuing a two-lot subdivision for this property if the
                                map amendment is approved. Review and consideration of a potential
                                subdivision is not being considered at this time.
     STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends approval of Ordinance No. 20-14 based on the
                           findings stated in the staff report.

                                ORDINANCE NO. 20-14
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING MAP
AMENDMENT FROM RESIDENTIAL SINGLE-FAMILY MINIMUM 10,000 SQUARE
FEET (R-1-10) TO RESIDENTIAL SINGLE-FAMILY MINIMUM 5,000 SQUARE FEET
              (R-1-5) FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT 4645 S. 1175 W.

       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on June
17, 2020, to consider, among other things, approving a zoning map amendment from Residential
Single-Family Minimum 10,000 Square Feet (R-1-10) to Residential Single-Family Minimum
5,000 Square Feet (R-1-5) for property located at 4645 S. 1175 W; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13.05.030(A)(2),
notice of a public hearing before the City’s Planning Commission was published in the Salt Lake
Tribune on May 29, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City’s Planning Commission was
also published on the State Public Notice Website on May 28, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, the City’s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendment on June 9, 2020 and voted 7-0 to recommend that the City Council deny
the proposed amendment; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendment during its regularly scheduled meeting on June 9,
2020; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendment.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposal to amend the City’s zoning map from residential single-family minimum 10,000 square
feet (R-1-10) to residential single-family minimum 5,000 square feet (R-1-5) for certain real
property located at 4645 S. 1175 W., a legal description of which is attached as Exhibit “A’ and
incorporated by this reference, is hereby approved.

       This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 20-14, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the city recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
