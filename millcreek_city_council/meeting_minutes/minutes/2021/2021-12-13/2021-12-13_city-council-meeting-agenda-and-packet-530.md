            2.1 First      Reading:        ZT-21-008,              Zoning        Code    Text       Amendment              Allowing         a Caretaker
            Dwelling        as a Permitted             Use within           Commercial              Zones;        Robert      May,     Long         Range
            Planner
            Mayor       Silvestrini       said the Council's            practice        s to have a first read of planning                    matters
            then consider           them at the next Council                 meeting.        Robert        May     said an applicant              initiated          a
            text amendment             request       allowing        for a caretaker           dwelling       as a permitted          use within         the
            commercial          zones. He highlighted                 a map of the commercial                     zones in the city. He
            proposed       a single-family            dwelling        within       the commercial            building,       not detached            as an
            accessory       unit.     A caretaker's          residence         is already      recognized          in the Light       Manufacturing
            Zone,      but this application            would        change that definition                 to match      with    the commercial
            zones. The applicant's                proposed         definition       was, "caretaker            dwelling         means a single
            Dwelling       Unit     that is under          common        ownership           with     and     integrated        into a cornrnercial
            building      and is used by an employee                     of the commercial                 building      for security        or
            maintenance           purposes."        Staffs        proposed        definition        was, "caretaker           dwelling       means no
            more than one dwelling                  unit that is under             common        ownership            and is attached         or internal
            to the main building              and its commercial                and is used for the accommodation                           of an
            employee,       business       owner        and person          under     contract        of the commercial              use and main
            building      for security        or maintenance            purposes."           The caretaker            dwelling       would        be subject
            to these specific          use standards:             1) property       owner       shall record          an affidavit       stating      the
            caretaker      dwelling       will     not be leased, rented,               or sold separately;             2) caretaker         dwelling
            shall consist       of a minimum             habitable       floor      area of 800 square feet and not to exceed                               a
            maximum         habitable       floor      area of 1,500            square feet; 3) shall be subject                   to the off-street
            parking     requirements             chapter     19.80     of the Millcreek              Code;       4) parking        spaces for
            caretaker     dwelling        cannot       be included          in any parking            reduction        request      including         shared
        parking;         and 5) home businesses                     shall be prohibited.


        May         shared that he created               a matrix      of comparables               with     other cities that he included                      in
        the staff report.             Mayor       Silvestrini        asked if other cities             allowed        for detached        caretaker
        dwellings.          May       said some codes did not specify                        attached       or detached.         He noted that
            attached     was in the intent            of the application.             Mayor         Silvestrini       noted that detached
        dwellings          would       not always          be appropriate           in coinmercial            zones. He suggested                  it may be
        worthwhile             to allow     a construction            trailer      on site. May         said a temporary             use could be
        considered.


        May         said the community               councils        provided        input     on the concept.             Each council           wanted         to
        allow         home businesses             in a caretaker         dwelling        that did not a have a customer                      base, which
        would          have the least amount               of impact         to surrounding            uses, and was limited                to office         uses
        that only required              internet       and phone.           They     also felt a minimum                and maximum                square
        footage         range was appropriate                 for caretaker          dwellings         and they should             be subject         to the
        off-street        parking       requirements.             The Planning          Commission               discussed       parking,      home
        businesses,            size ranges,        and attached         versus       detached         dwellings.        They recommended
        approval          of the ordinance            changes        with      the following          standards:        home businesses               are
        allowed         with    no on-site         customer         base, dwellings            range from          600-1,500         square feet, and
        caretaker         dwellings        be internal/attached.                 Staff recommended                 approval        of the ordinance
        change based on the staff findings                           in the staff report,            and the input          from     the community
        councils         and Plaru'iing          Coini'nission.

            acquisition             on Millcreek              Corninon.            The process           is that a developer                gives       staff     a recent

            square         feet short         of plaza         space.        He highlighted              the site plan        with       the proposed              plaza
            spaces.         The conducted appraisal said the property    was worth about $100 a square foot.

            was eligible             for the cash in lieu                  option      because          of the proximity               to Millcreek             Cornrnon.
