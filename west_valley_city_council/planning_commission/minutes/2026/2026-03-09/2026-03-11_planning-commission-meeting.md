Property Size: 51.8 Acres

VanTrust Real Estate has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for six parcels
totaling 51.8 acres located at 6290 W Parkway Blvd. The two parcels to the north are currently
zoned A (Agricultural, minimum lot size ½ acre) and the remaining south parcels are zoned A-
1 (Agricultural, minimum lot size 1 acre). The current General Plan designation is Large Lot
Residential (2 to 3 units/acre). The proposed zone is M (Manufacturing) and the proposed
General Plan designation is Light Manufacturing.

Surrounding zones include M to the north and a portion of the east and A-1 to the south, west,
and remaining east portion. Surrounding land uses include an industrial development to the
north, an industrial development under construction and Magna Water well property to the
east, a church and single unit dwellings to the south, and a single unit dwelling and a City
wetland park to the west. The subject property is currently vacant.

Development Proposal

The intended development for this property is an industrial project with a total of 721,161
square feet of buildings as reflected in the concept plan submitted by the applicant. In the M
zone, all uses on properties greater than 10 acres are reviewed as a conditional use. Hence, if
this application is approved, the applicant would need to apply for a conditional use permit to
pursue the proposed industrial development.

Current Conditions for Consideration

Since the adoption of the City’s first General Plan in the 80’s, the Riter Canal has been the
dividing line between industrial uses to the north and residential uses to the south in the
northwest part of the City. However, much has changed in the northwest part of the City since
the 80’s. The following list of current conditions may warrant consideration of M zoning on the
subject property:

    •   The Northwest Economic Development Area (EDA), which was created in 2012,
        includes the subject property. The Northwest EDA covers approximately 1,000 acres in
        the northwest part of the City and makes available incentives for commercial
        development.
    •   As indicated on the City’s Major Street Plan, the City plans to extend 5900 West
        between Beagley Road and Parkway Blvd. on the Rocky Mountain Power property
        directly west of Mountain View Corridor. This road extension will enable industrial traffic
        from the subject property and in the area north of Beagley Road to access Mountain
        View Corridor without using Parkway Blvd. Design work for the road is underway.
        Public Works anticipates construction starting this year with the project completed in
        the spring/early summer of 2027 assuming an easement with Rocky Mountain Power
        can be secured.
    •   The City’s wetland park to the west and the Magna Water property to the east could be
        seen as a buffer between the potential industrial development on the subject property
        and nearby residential uses.
    •   The City recently widened Parkway Blvd. to 5 lanes between Mountain View Corridor
        and 6400 West.
    •   Some of the property to the east has been approved for industrial development.
    •   At some point, a transition between residential and industrial is unavoidable on this
        property. If the subject property were to develop as residential, the transition will
        happen along the east edge adjacent to VanTrust’s property to the east. If the subject
        property develops as industrial, the transition will happen either between the southern
        and northern portions of the property, or it will happen along Parkway Blvd.

Partial Approval Possible

As mentioned above, the applicant has included smaller flex industrial buildings on the south
parcel as a transition between the larger industrial buildings to the north and the church and
homes to the south. While these buildings are smaller, the Commission may be concerned
about industrial on the south parcel for the following reasons:

   •   There are homes and a church on the south side of Parkway Blvd.
   •   There is a home on the northwest corner of Parkway Blvd and 6400 West.
   •   The City constructed Beagley Road to the north with the intent of keeping commercial
       traffic off Parkway Blvd. The flex buildings would have direct access to Parkway Blvd.
   •   There are no commercial uses along Parkway Blvd between 7200 West (the City’s
       western border) and the Mountain View Corridor.

As pointed out earlier, a transition between industrial and residential is unavoidable on this
property. The question is where the transition would be most effective. If the Commission
approved the M zoning on only the north 5 parcels, the south parcel could potentially be
rezoned for and developed as residential which would result in no commercial access on
Parkway Blvd. A transition between the south parcel and the north 5 parcels could be made
through the use of a mix of things like a solid fence, large setback, landscaping, and a change
in elevation where the residential grade is higher than the industrial grade.

Access

As mentioned above, Beagley Road was constructed with the intend to direct truck traffic away
from Parkway Blvd. and toward 7200 West, 5600 West, and the SR-201 south frontage road. If
this property is to develop as industrial, staff believes that access to Beagley Road is essential
and should be required by development agreement. Securing access to Beagley Road will
require the acquisition of or at least an easement on the two northernmost parcels, one of
which is owned by the City and the other by Kennecott.

Staff received the following preliminary comments from Public Works regarding access and
improvements for this project:
   • The developer needs to line up access points on Beagley Road.
   • The same park strip and 10-foot wide trail being done to the east on the south side of
        Beagley will also be required on this project. Sidewalk will likely be required on 6400
        West. Streetlights will also be required along roadways where they are not already
        installed.
   • Access locations on Parkway Blvd. will need to be reviewed. They will need to line up or
        meet spacing standards.
   • The proposed access on 6400 West would need to line up with the wetland park access
        road.
