      A. DISCUSSION OF 2023 MIDVALE MUNICIPAL ELECTION PROCESS
Rori Andreason stated Midvale City along with all other municipalities within Salt Lake
County have contracted with the Salt Lake County Election Division to provide election
services for municipal elections for several years. The 2023 Midvale municipal election
will include the seats of Council Districts 1,2, and 3. Salt Lake County's estimated cost
structure for the types of election process has changed to include an additional cost if a
municipality chose to pursue Ranked Choice Voting (RCV) as their election process.

The additional costs will be based on how many entities are participating in RCV. lf
Midvale City pursues a traditional voting process, ,VOf RCV, and has a primary election
and general election, the estimated cost would be $22,865. lf a primary election is not
needed, the estimated cost for the General Election ONLY would be $14,830. P/ease

       B.       DISCUSSION OF A PERPETUAL ACCESS EASEMENT AGREEMENT
                BETWEEN MIDVALE CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE
                CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE, AND OPERATION OF TWO
                CONCRETE BOAT RAMPS ADJACENT TO THE JORDAN RIVER
Glen Kennedy said Salt Lake County is requesting a perpetual easement allowing them
to access prope(y at approximately 1041 West Howe Cove, which is adjacent to the
Jordan River and owned by Midvale City. The purpose of the easement is to allow the
County access to said property for the installation, construction, maintenance, and
operation of two concrete boat ramps in the Jordan River. ln addition, the easement would
allow public use of the boat ramps to portage across the property after project completion.
Staff will discuss the details of the proposed agreement during City Council meeting.

Council Member Dustin Gettel asked who owns the parcel. Glen Kennedy said it is owned
by Midvale.

Council Member Quinn Sperry said he would like signs put up to remind the public to still
wear life jackets.

The Council agreed to put on the consent agenda for approval at the next city council
meeting.

       C. DISCUSS EMPLOYEE BONUS PROGRAM
Matt Dahl said Staff is seeking feedback from the City Council regarding a proposal for
an employee bonus program. The program is designed to reward employees that go
above and beyond their normal job responsibilities to develop, implement, improve, or
perform projects, policies, or procedures that reduce Midvale City expenses or increase
revenue.

Supervisors seeking to recommend an employee to receive the bonus through the
proposed program would submit a written request to the City Manager for consideration.
The written request would detail the relevant work of the employee and the financial
benefit received by the City. The awarding of the bonus would be at the discretion of the
