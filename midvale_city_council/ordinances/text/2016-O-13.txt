ORDINANCE NO. 2016-O-13
AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATIONS
PROHIBITING AMUSEMENT HOUSES IN THE SF-1 AND SF-2 SINGLE
FAMILY ZONES AND THE RM-12 AND RM-25 MULTI-FAMILY ZONES.

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the existing land use regulations do not
adequately address Amusement Houses uses within single family neighborhoods; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds it appropriate to explore this issue further in
determining whether such uses are appropriate in single family neighborhoods; and

WHEREAS, the City Council hereby directs Staff to research whether or not
such uses can be permitted in single family neighborhoods in such a way as to mitigate
any negative impacts related to traffic, noise and light; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the public interest to review the
potential land use regulations related to this type of development in single family
neighborhoods to determine an appropriate form of action to address this issue; and

WHEREAS, with these findings, Section 10-9a-504 U.C.A. grants the City
Council the authority to establish, by ordinance, a temporary land use regulation that may
prohibit or regulate the erection, construction, reconstruction, or alteration of any
building or structure located in any part of the City to provide the City time to address
this land use issue.

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the City Council of Midvale City, Utah
the following:

(1) This temporary ordinance shall apply to all applications for Amusement
Houses within theSF-1 and SF-2 Single Family Residential, RM-12 and RM-25 Mutli-
Family Zones, as illustrated on the Midvale City Zoning Map.

(2) During the effective period of this temporary ordinance, no new
development applications or building permits for Amusement Houses in the zone districts
stated above shall be accepted by the Midvale City Department of Community
Development for review and consideration.

(3) This temporary ordinance shall become effective upon adoption and
signature and shall be in effect until six (6) months following said adoption date.

PASSED AND APPROVED this day of Avot Ber 2016.

Ure
Ann B. Seghini, Mayor
