WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 7, 2025

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Mayor Nanette Billings at 3:30 p.m. on August 7, 2025, in Hurricane City Council
Chambers, 147 North 870 West, Hurricane City, Utah. Those in attendance were Hurricane
City Mayor Nanette Billings, Hurricane City Councilmember Kevin Thomas, Hurricane
City Councilmember Clark Fawcett, Hurricane City Councilmember Joseph Prette,
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow,
Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and
several members of the Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District Board.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION ABOUT SERVICES WITH HURRICANE CITY.

Commissioner Iverson and Mayor Billings spoke about the USU Desert Research Center
concept. Commissioner Iverson asked for a letter of support from the Mayor/Hurricane
City Council.

The history of why and how the Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District Board was
formed was discussed, and the recent tax increase was addressed.

Hurricane City had concerns with adequate representation on the Hurricane Valley Fire
SSD board. The Washington County Commission will share some options for the
Hurricane Valley Fire SSD to review going forward.

Minutes Prepared by Jayanne Lewis,
Deputy Clerk, Washington County
