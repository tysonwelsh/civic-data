WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JULY 1, 2025

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on July 1, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, County
Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Chad McWilliams

CONSENT AGENDA:

commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2025-1298-O, AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING
AMENDMENTS _TO_ TITLE 5, CHAPTER 1 OF WASHINGTON COUNTY
ORDINANCES RELATING TO PARKING ON COUNTY PROPERTY, SILVER REEF
MUSEUM

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales relayed that Silver Reef Museum has been recently used
as a public parking lot impacting the County museum and the city of Leeds. This Ordinance would
put regulations on the County owned parking lot.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2025-1298-O, An
Ordinance Adopting Amendments to Title 5, Chapter 1 of Washington County
Ordinances Relating to Parking on County Property, Silver Reef Museum.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3547, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE RENEWAL OF THE CARNIVAL AGREEMENT FOR THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY FAIR

County Fair Director Susi Lafaele stated for the past five years, the Fair has contracted with
Carnival Midway and it is requested that the contract be renewed for another three years.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3547, A
Resolution Approving the Renewal of the Carnival Agreement for the
Washington County Fair Subject to Attorney Review. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3551, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_CONTRACT FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ZION NATIONAL PARK SEWER
IMPROVEMENTS PHASE I

Regional Tourism Regulatory Affairs Director Heath Hansen reported construction is ready for
Phase I of the Zion Sewer Project. Five bids were received, and the lowest responsible bidder was
Trico Construction out of Draper Utah. This contract is to construct sewer improvements from
Zion Lodge to the Grotto in Zion National Park.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3551, A
Resolution Approving a Contract for the Construction of Zion National Park
Sewer Improvements Phase I. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and
Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3552, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A CONTRACT WITH MEDICI LAND GOVERNANCE FOR PARTICIPATION IN ITS
RECORD MANAGEMENT PILOT PROGRAM

Recorder Gary Christensen stated this is an opportunity for a pilot program that is AI driven to
effectively manage land records. This program would allow the Recorder’s Office to automatically
index documents as they come into the Recorder’s Office to become searchable. This software
would also index historical documents currently housed in the office. The initial contract is for six
months with an option to renew.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3552, A
Resolution Approving a Contract with Medici Land Governance for
Participation in its Record Management Pilot Program. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3553, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY, UTAH, AND
THE CITY OF ENTERPRISE, UTAH FOR WASHINGTON COUNTY SHERIFF’S
OFFICE TO PROVIDE PATROL SERVICES

Mr. Clarke explained the Washington County Sheriffs Office has patrolled Enterprise City for
years and this contract would renew and update the pricing that Enterprise City would pay
Washington County for services.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3553, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement Between Washington County,
Utah, and the City of Enterprise, Utah for Washington County Sheriff's Office
to Provide Patrol Services. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3555, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
TRT FUNDING FOR A BASKETBALL TOURNAMENT IN WASHINGTON COUNTY

Sports and Adventure Marketing Manager Chad McWilliams explained the Tourism Tax Advisory
Board (TAB) recently voted to sponsor a basketball tournament that will be held in Washington
County from July 24-26. The TAB Board recommended a sponsorship of $15,000 - $20,000.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3555, A
Resolution Approving TRT Funding for a Basketball Tournament in
Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting

aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3556, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AMENDMENT TO THE AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
BOWEN, COLLINS AND ASSOCIATES, INC. FOR ENGINEERING SERVICES FOR
REHABILITATION OF THE FROG HOLLOW DEBRIS BASIN

Public Works Director Todd Edwards stated this project has been in the works for several years,
which is reconstruction of a failing dam. The National Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)
have reviewed the initial area, and suggested an additional study to identify more areas for
construction services with estimated additional costs of $359,629. NRCS will likely reimburse the
County for all costs associated with this amendment.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3556, A
Resolution Approving an Amendment to the Agreement Between Washington
County and Bowen, Collins and Associates, Inc. for Engineering Services for
Rehabilitation of the Frog Hollow Debris Basin. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:55 p.m.
