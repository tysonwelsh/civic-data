                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-55

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO RATIFY AN AMENDMENT TO
      THE FIREWORKS CONTRACT WITH FIRESTORM PYROTECHNICS,
                     CONTRACT NO. 20-1065-08

       WHEREAS, because of COVID-19 the 2020 re-scheduled Town Days event was
canceled; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council would still like to have a firework show; and

      WHEREAS, two additional locations were added, and the type of fireworks selected
changed; and
       WHEREAS, in order to get the fireworks ordered and the event scheduled, staff had to
authorize Firestorm to proceed.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council approves Resolution No. 20-55 - Authorizing the Mayor to ratify an
          amendment to the fireworks contract with Firestorm Pyrotechnics, Contract No. 20-
          1065-08.
       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 18th day of
August 2020 by the following vote:

                                             YES    NO
