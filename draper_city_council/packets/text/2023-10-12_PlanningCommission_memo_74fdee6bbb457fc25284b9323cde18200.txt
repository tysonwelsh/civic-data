MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Tevai Haunga
Date: 2023-10-12
Re: Public Hearing: Draper Box N Lock Deviations Request (Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of Deviations from building façade standards for
a new self-storage facility to be built on approximately 3.88 acres. This property is located
at approximately 15037 S. Minuteman Drive. The property is currently zoned M1. The site
plan was conditionally approved by the Planning Commission on September 14, 2023.
The applicant is requesting approval of Deviations for significant horizontal articulation,
number of primary materials, and height and roofline variations for buildings B, C, D, E, F,
G, H, I, J, and K.


The findings are as follows:

Significant Horizontal Façade Articulation
For approval:

1. The development application meets three (3) or more of the criteria of subsection 9-22-
030(B) and that the deviation is a benefit to the city.

For Denial:
1. The development application does not meet three (3) or more of the criteria of
subsection 9-22-030(B) and that the deviation is a benefit to the city.

Number of Primary Materials
For approval:

1. The development application meets three (3) or more of the criteria of subsection 9-22-
030(B) and that the deviation is a benefit to the city.

For Denial:
1. The development application does not meet three (3) or more of the criteria of
subsection 9-22-030(B) and that the deviation is a benefit to the city.
Height and Roofline Variations
