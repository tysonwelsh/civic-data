                                       RESOLUTION NO. 21-09

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE CITY OF DRAPER TO
             APPOINT BRET MILLBURN AS ASSISTANT CITY MANAGER
                          FOR THE CITY OF DRAPER.

        WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint BRET MILLBURN as Assistant City Manager of
the City of Draper in accordance with the appointment procedures provided by law and City
Ordinance; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 3-1-045 requires that the Assistant City Manager be
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and



        WHEREAS, BRET MILLBURN has the credentials, experience and professionalism necessary
to be the Assistant City Manager.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent to the
appointment of BRET MILLBURN as Assistant City Manager and the Mayor is hereby authorized to
appoint BRET MILLBURN as Assistant City Manager, in accordance with appointment procedures
provided by law and City Code.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution,
and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect on February 16, 2021.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS 16th DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2021.
