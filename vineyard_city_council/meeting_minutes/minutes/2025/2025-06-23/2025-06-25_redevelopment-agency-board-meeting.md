5. CLOSED SESSION
No closed session was held.

P Mr. Daniels stated that he wanted to rescind the memo sent out before the last RDA meeting.
He said that he research state code and found that in 2009 state law required that Taxing Entity
Committee (TEC) should meet annually. State law was changed in 2011 to create an option for
RDA’s to issue annual informational reports and that was why they discontinued the annual
meetings. Those reports are available on the Utah County Auditor’s website. In 2020 the law
changed requiring RDAs to issue annul informational data reports to the Governor’s Office of
Economic Opportunity, which are available online through the GOEO website. He noted that he
was working on having those reports available on the city website.
