                                        RESOLUTION NO. 21-13

        A RESOLUTION APPROVING A NINTH AMENDMENT TO THE MASTER
        DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR HIDDEN CANYON ESTATES, MERCER
        MOUNTAIN ESTATES, AND LAKEVIEW HEIGHTS PROPERTIES FOR
        APPROXIMATELY 35.06 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT
        APPROXIMATELY 2563 EAST CANYON EDGE DRIVE WITHIN DRAPER
        CITY.

       WHEREAS, a Development Agreement was approved on April 13, 2015 for the properties Hidden
Canyon Estates, Mercer Mountain Estates, and Lakeview Heights; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the City to
modify some of the standards of the Development Agreement for the Lone Peak Canyon Subdivision of
Hidden Canyon Estates; and

         WHEREAS, the existing Development Agreement requires permanent wrought iron fencing be
installed along the City Owned Property prior to building permit issuance; and

        WHEREAS, the Ninth Amendment to the Development Agreement will allow for temporary chain
link fencing to be installed along the City Owned Property prior to building permits issuance; and

       WHEREAS, the Ninth Amendment to the Development Agreement will require permanent
wrought iron fencing be installed along the City Owned Property prior to issuance of a certificate of
occupancy; and

       WHEREAS, the Ninth Amendment to the Development Agreement will protect from
encroachment upon or entering into the City Owned Property; and

        WHEREAS, the proposed Ninth Amendment to the Development Agreement set forth herein has
been reviewed by the City Council, and all appropriate public meetings have been held in accordance with
Utah law regarding the proposed modifications to the Development Agreement; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council has found the proposed Ninth Amendment to the Development
Agreement to be consistent with the City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

       Section 1.   Development Agreement Amendment. The City of Draper approves the
Development Agreement Amendment provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as the Lone Peak Canyon
Ninth Amendment.

        Section 2.        Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution and
all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.       Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final passage.
