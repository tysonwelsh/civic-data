                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 21-17

    AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE MASTER DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
     APPROVED BY ORDINANCE NO. 16-01, BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND
                   SUBURBAN LAND RESERVE, INC.

        WHEREAS, in January of 2016, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 16-
01, thereby approving that certain Master Development Agreement and related exhibits and
design guidelines by and between Riverton City and Suburban Land Reserve, Inc.; and

       WHEREAS, said Master Development Agreement governs the development of
approximate 550 acres of land generally at the intersection of Mountain View Corridor and
13400 South; and

        WHEREAS, as Suburban Land Reserve has advanced its planning designs for the
project area, the parties to the Master Development Agreement have identified the need to make
certain modifications to the rate of impact fees and adjust the locations in which park space
should be located within the broader project area; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it is in the best interest
of the public to adopt the Amendment to Master Development Agreement as described herein;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1.     The Amendment to the Master Development Agreement, and all
                      associated documents shall be, and hereby is, approved as described in the
                      attached Exhibit “A”. The Mayor is authorized to sign the Amendment to
                      the Master Development Agreement, upon verification that the same has
                      been approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       Section 2.     This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 15th day of
June, 2021 by the following vote:
