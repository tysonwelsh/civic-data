Request for Deliberation-Zoning Ordinance amendment, Limiting multiple family dwellings in C-2/CP-
2 and C-3/CP-3 zones.

Chair Sandau-The petitioner on this is Ogden City Administration. Last time this came before
the commission there was public comment, we took it and discussed it further, we made certain
changes and at this point, we are going to take public comment again because it is going to the City
Council.

Barton Brierley from the Planning Division gave a staff report regarding a request for
deliberation-zoning ordinance amendment, limiting multiple family dwellings in C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3
zones.

The petitioner/developer’s request is to amend the Zoning Ordinance for C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-
3 zones to do the following:

1. Prohibit new multiple-family dwellings, single-family rowhouses, and duplexes in these zones
except (1) senior multiple-family dwellings, (2) multiple-family dwellings as allowed in adopted OGX
station area plans along Harrison Boulevard, and (3) mixed-use projects on lots at least 10 acres with
at least 75% of ground floor area in an allowable nonresidential use.

2. Update standards related to senior and mixed-use multiple-family dwellings.

Chair Sandau-We're sending this to City Council because we've done all we can do. If something
doesn't make sense and it should be housing, the appropriate path is to ask for a rezoning. That's better
path than zoning that allows housing broadly.

Commissioner Safsten-!’ll say amen that and with exclamation marks as we pass this on to the
City Council.

Chair Sandau-There are rezoning options. This is not a permanent change; we can reconsider
when we do the code and general plan. This is a chance to back up and think a little bit.

Commissioner Southwick-The solution is for people to come back in and request rezones.
