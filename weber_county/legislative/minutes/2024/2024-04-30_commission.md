D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: When my youngest daughter was a senior in high school, she
   ran track. We had four hurdlers in the family. The 300 meter hurdles is especially hard because you have to
   sprint for 300 meters while jumping over hurdles. Our youngest was in a regional meet trying to qualify for state
   when she hit a hurdle and fell for the first time ever; her delay in getting up led her to not qualify for the state
   meet. Our oldest hurdler felt bad and said, “I never taught her how to fall.” I recently read a book written by
   Seth Godin called Linchpin. One of the quotes from the book was, “You become a winner because you’re good
   at losing.” That reminded me of our youngest; she didn’t have many opportunities through high school to lose,
   to fall, to mess up, or to get a bad start. She always did her best each race. So when she fell, she did not know
   what to do; she did not know how to get up and move on quickly. In life, we become winners because we are
   good at losing.

    2. APPROVE A CHARITABLE DONATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY AND THE
       FRIENDS OF THE WEBER-MORGAN CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER FOR THE FUNDING OF
       A NEW CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER.
         Chris Crockett (Lead Civil Attorney): This is one of final pieces in funding for the new Children’s Justice
         Center. We have worked with our friends at the Children’s Justice Center to put the current CJC building up
         for sale. I believe that has been listed now. The proceeds of that sale will be placed in a trust and used for
         the new CJC, likely in the form of reimbursing the County for its costs. We have received all of the ARPA
         funds from all of the participating cities. Part of this agreement will be to negotiate a long term lease for
         space within this new building for the Friends of the CJC to utilize for their monthly meetings and to
         continue fund raising efforts in support of the CJC.
