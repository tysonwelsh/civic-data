                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 25-57

  A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE THE IDENTIFIED EQUIPMENT AS SURPLUS,
 AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF THE ITEM AS HE DEEMS
                          APPROPRIATE


       WHEREAS, the identified equipment has already been replaced and/or is no longer
needed; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets as surplus in
a public meeting before discarding.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares the following equipment as surplus, authorizing
          the City Manager to dispose of as he deems appropriate

          EO6625 – 2016 Ford Explorer, Vin #1FM5K8D80GGB36625

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 2nd day of
December, 2025 by the following vote:
