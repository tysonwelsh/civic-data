City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 3, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1588, adopting the
2023 Draper City Parks, Trails, and Recreation Master Plan. The complete ordinance
is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 23rd day of October 2023. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
