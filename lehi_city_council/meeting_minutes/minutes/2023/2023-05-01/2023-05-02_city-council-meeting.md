Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, May 2, 2023, at Lehi City
Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately 4:00 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present:     Jason Walker, City Administrator; Cameron Boyle, Assistant City
Administrator; Craig Chambers, Assistant City Attorney; Kim Struthers, Community
Development Director; Lorin Powell, City Engineer; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Steve
Marchbanks, Parks Superintendent; Beau Thomas, Assistant to the City Administrator; Andy
Gowans, Management Analyst; and Teisha Wilson, City Recorder

1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
   Hancock offered the opening comment.


2. Review and Discussion of Budget Request Items
   Every City Department was available to present their budget requests to the Council. The
   Council asked clarifying questions and made suggestions to be included in the final budget.

3. Emergency Management Response Discussion
   Scott Daybell, Lehi City Emergency Management Coordinator, answered questions and
   concerns about what is being done for emergency management. Here was also discussion about
   pre-mitigation planning and training for city staff and elected officials.

4. Consideration of Ordinance #27-2023 approving a Temporary Land Use Regulation
   prohibiting the construction of ADUs in a Planned Community Zone.
   Mr. Chambers stated that the city is doing this due to recent State Legislation.

   Lorin Powell, City Engineer, expressed safety concerns with additional traffic in the Traverse
   Mountain canyons with the limited ingress and egress. He stated that the potential for ADUs in
   the Planned Community Zones would create an over capacity in the sewer system in some areas.

   Mayor Johnson stated that there is property damage with sewer failure that’s concerning. He
   stated that he hopes the Legislature will amend their legislation that caused this concern.




Lehi City Council Work Session                  1                                     May 2, 2023
    Motion: Councilor Southwick moved to approve Ordinance #27-2023 a Temporary Land
    Use Regulation prohibiting the construction of ADUs in a Planned Community Zone.
    Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
   at approximately 7:07 p.m.
