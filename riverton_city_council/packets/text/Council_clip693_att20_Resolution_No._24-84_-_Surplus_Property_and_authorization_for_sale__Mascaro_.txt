                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 24-84

  A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE CITY OWNED PROPERTY, KNOWN AS THE
 MASCARO PROPERTY, AS SURPLUS, AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO
        DISPOSE OF THE PROPERTY AS HE DEEMS APPROPRIATE

        WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare city owned property that
is no longer needed as surplus in a public meeting.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares the City owned property, located at the address of
          4123 West 12600 South (Parcel No. 27312270070000), known as the Mascaro
          Property, as surplus, authorizing the City Manager to sell as he deems appropriate;
          provided; a) the sale of the property meets appraised value; b) sale of the property
          otherwise complies with procedures undertaken by the City Contracts Administrator
          to obtain best terms available to the City; and 3) all contract and closing documents
          created for sale of the property are approved as to form by the City Attorney.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
August, 2024, by the following vote:
