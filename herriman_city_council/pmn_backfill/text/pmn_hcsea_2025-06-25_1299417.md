3. Public Hearing
    3.1. Public hearing and consideration of a Resolution amending the Herriman City
         Safety Enforcement Area budget for fiscal year 2026
Finance Director Kyle Maurer explained that this amendment was necessary to account for
two additional police officer positions that had been authorized by the Herman City Council
previously. Since the City operates on a two-year budget cycle, the fiscal year 2025 budget
had already been amended to include these new positions. However, the fiscal year 2026
budget, which had been adopted the previous year, did not reflect the hiring of these officers
because the decision to add the positions had not yet been made at that time. To correct this,
the proposed amendment transferred funds from the anticipated fund balance to cover the
salaries for the two officers in FY 2026.

Trustee Hodges raised a question regarding the source of the funds. He asked whether the
money was coming from restricted funds tied to a tax increase approved a few years ago.
Director Maurer confirmed that this was correct, explaining that the funds were from a
restricted allocation specifically designated by that tax measure, and that the allocation
would support the full-time employee positions.

Chair Palmer opened the public hearing.

No public comments were offered.

Trustee Ohrn moved to close the public hearing. Trustee Anderson seconded the motion and
all voted aye.
