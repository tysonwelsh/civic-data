                    Nephi City Council Work Session Minutes
                                  February 28, 2023
                                       7:00 p.m.
                                      21 E 100 N
                                   Nephi, UT 84648


1. Mike Sperry came forward and presented a letter from his lawyer addressing his
   properties located at approximately 515 N. 200 E. Mr. Sperry had his lawyer review the
   city code that dealt with internal accessory dwelling units (IADU).
    Mr. Sperry’s properties consist of two twin homes located in the North Ridge
   subdivision that is zoned R1-8 zone with a zero-lot line overlay. The lawyer’s letter
   pointed out that IADUs are a permitted use in the zone, that the city has not adopted
   IADU provisions of the state code, and that IADUs will benefit the City.

    Mr. Atkinson reviewed the state code governing IADU and the background information
   of this annexed area and the intent of the council at time of annexation and as well as
   the international residential code.
   Mr. Atkinson stated that twin homes are separate from duplexes in that each side can
   be individually owned and has a lot line dividing the units but the homes are physically
   attached or share a common wall. Each side has its own frontage and lot with open
   space and could potentially be considered single family residential. A section of the
   Nephi City Code governing Planned Unit Developments states:

  “......for the purposes of this chapter, single-family, attached dwellings such as
  townhouses or zero lot line lots shall be considered single-family dwellings and
  may be permitted in a planned unit development.” (emphasis added )

  The twin homes in the North Ridge subdivision are not part of a Planned Unit
  Development but if the code above was interpreted to include the zero-lot line lots in
  other zones, then they could be considered single family residential and contain IADUs.

  The state law, Title 10-9a-530, governing IADUs defined primary dwellings to be owner-
  occupied and to be detached. The law stating that they needed to be detached would
  seem to preclude the twin homes from having IADUs. The Utah State Adopted
  International Residential Code 20.42.040 Definition of “Detached” means, “any
  residential structures on the same lot that are not connected and have a fire separation
  of no less than six (6) feet as defined in Section R302 of the IRC…”. The twin homes
  are technically on separate lots. There is a legal lot line between the units, but it does
  not meet the physical definition of “detached” and does not seem to be the original intent
  of the IADU law for where IADUs would be allowed.

  The owner of the twin homes, the Sperry Family Trust, has requested that the City
  Council interpret the code to allow for IADUs in twin homes by considering them
  “detached”.

  Mayor Pro Tempore Nathan Memmott stated based on the research of state, local, and
  building code that the homes do not meet the criteria of detached and possibly not
  owner occupied so therefore IADU’s are not allowed.
       Mr. Sperry stated that he has a lot of money invested in the properties and hoped that he
       could have IADU’s in each of the two twin home structures.
       Each councilor expressed that they thought the structures were very nice but that the
       intent of the council was not to have that dense of housing located in that area and
       according to state law IADUs were not allowed because the homes were not detached
       and wanted to remain consistent with the original intent of the property.

    2. Meeting adjourned at 9:00 p.m.
