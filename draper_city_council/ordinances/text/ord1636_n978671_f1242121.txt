City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1636

On March 4, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1636 amending the
official zoning map of Draper City for approximately 0.15 acres of property from A2
(Agricultural) to RM2 (Multiple Family Residential) zone and approving a development
agreement for the property located at approximately 285 W. River Chapel Road within
Draper City, otherwise known as the Urbana Zoning Map Amendment and
Development Agreement Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 6th day of March 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
