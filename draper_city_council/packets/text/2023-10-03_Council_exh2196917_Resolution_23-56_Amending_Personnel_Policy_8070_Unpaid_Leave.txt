to Policy 8070 of the Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual as attached hereto as Exhibit
“A.”

this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, THIS THE 3RD DAY OF OCTOBER, 2023.

                                  Section 8070 – Unpaid Leave
                                     (Amended 10/03/2023)

   Unpaid personal leave may be granted to full-time employees who have completed their
new hire probationary period for special circumstances outlined in this policy. Unless otherwise
required by law, unpaid personal leave is a privilege and not a right.

Policy:

   1. Unpaid Personal Leave Time Off. An employee may be granted unpaid personal leave
      for a specified period of time, not to exceed 90 days. At the expiration of unpaid
      personal leave, the employee shall return to the same position, where feasible, or to a
      similar position. If city service is negatively impacted by the absent employee, the
      Department Director may request the employee return to work prior to the expiration of
      the approved unpaid personal leave. If the employee cannot return to work, the
      Department Director may request separation of employment. Separation of employment
      requires City Manager approval. The city may separate, provided no FMLA, Workers’
      Compensation or other applicable laws are being violated.

   2. Special Circumstances for Unpaid Personal Leave.

          2.1   For previously scheduled commitments of new employees that are agreed to in
                writing at time of offer.
          2.2   For education purposes, so as long as their absence will not cause a hardship for
                their department or the city, and the employee agrees to return to work at the end
                of the unpaid personal leave.
          2.3   For bereavement leave not covered by the bereavement leave policy Section
                8030.
          2.4   To attend to a qualifying dependent as stated in Section 8010 or 8060 when such
                absence qualifies for FMLA. All family and medical leave must be exhausted.

   3. Request for Unpaid Personal Leave. Employees must apply for unpaid personal leave in
      advance using the Request for Unpaid Personal Leave form, giving as much detail about
      the absence as possible and providing supporting documents where applicable. The
      Department Director and Human Resource Director will review the request in
      consideration of: (i) purpose for which the leave is requested; (ii) length of time the
      employee will be away; (iii) effect the leave will have on the ability of the department to
      carry out its responsibilities; (iv) the employee’s performance prior to the submission of
      the request; and (vi) if the plan provides for coverage of job duties and has no impact to
      the department budget. The Department Director and Human Resource Director will
      forward their recommendation to the City Manager for final approval.

   4. Employees shall not be entitled to the accrual of vacation or sick leave, or holiday pay
      during the period of unpaid personal leave, unless otherwise required by law. Employees
   on unpaid personal leave are required to pay the employee premium for continuation of
   their insurance benefits during the unpaid personal leave. Unpaid personal leave may
   impact URS service credit.

5. Failure of the employee to report on the date agreed upon on the Request for Unpaid
   Personal Leave form shall be considered a voluntary resignation.
