Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: April 7, 2020
Re: Lone Peak Business Park CSD TA #2 – Text Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request to amend various sections of Chapter 9-18G of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) as it pertains to the boundaries of the Lone Peak Commercial Special District (CSD-LP).
The Planning Commission heard this request on February 27, 2020 and forwarded a positive recommendations to the City Council with one recommended change.  The Planning Commission recommended that Section 9-18G-050(A)(1)(g) is modified to specify decorative masonry or concrete for the trash enclosures.  That change is reflected in the ordinance being considered tonight.  The motion to forward a positive recommendation was unanimous.
The findings are as follows: For approval:
That Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the text of the DCMC.
That this text amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
That the proposed text change is consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
For denial:
That this text amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
That the proposed text change is not consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
