2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Update on UTA's Station Area Plan study for the Thanksgiving Point Frontrunner Station
property.
Kim Struthers gave an update on the UTA Station Area Plan. He reminded the Council that when
they approved the concept plan for the TOD, the Council wanted to make sure that UTA did a
study on their property. He also stated that UTA is working closely with the developer as they try
to determine the best land use for UTA's 11-acre parcel. He reported that they are starting with the
community engagement phase and met with business owners in the area. They will also have an
open house with stakeholder groups and are hoping to wrap up by mid-September.

Mike West explained the requirements for the Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zone. He said
that there is a great demand for apartments, with some retail. Mr. West introduced some possible
design and land use options for the site. He said that as they look at the design it's important to
create a sense of place. They really want to make this a good self-sustaining development.

2.2 Housing Affordability Discussion- Planning Department
Brittany Harris stated that for priority funding they need 6 moderate income housing goals. The
State really wants the cities to be proactive on their goals and noted that the reporting requirements
are more in depth.


Lehi City Council Meeting                         1                                       July 12, 2022
Ms. Harris stated that they are required to have a Station Area Plan Goal at Thanksgiving Point,
which is already underway. Other goals may include creating a Housing and Transit Reinvestment
Zone (HTRZ); amending land use regulations to eliminate or reduce parking requirements for
residential development; or amending land use regulations to allow for higher density or new
moderate income residential development in commercial or mixed-use zones near major transit
investment corridors. Other goals could also include amending development code to create a
moderate-income housing overlay zone, allowing twin homes in all residential zones, remove or
reduce the downtown overlay which would allow more residential units on Main Street, and allow
detached ADUs on 1/3 acre lots. There was discussion about eliminating the garage requirements
on detached ADUs which could make it easier to construct. There was discussion about changing
the definition of family to occupants which would allow for single room occupancy. Ms. Harris
stated that she will bring back the final goals for the Council’s approval.

8. Consideration of Ordinance #35-2022, a General Plan amendment on 1.63-acres located
   at 870 North 2300 West, changing the land use designation from VLDRA to
   Neighborhood Commercial.
   Mr. and Mrs. Gordan were the applicants. Mrs. Gordan stated that there is a great need for
   childcare in Lehi. They already have two centers, and this would be the third. She said that this
   property is larger than the others so there would be a good buffer between their center and the
   neighboring residents. Mrs. Gordan stated that the traffic is minimal because they have children
   being dropped off throughout the day and not at the same time. Mrs. Gordan requested a
   development agreement to ensure that no other use would be allowed.

    Mayor Johnson asked for public comments.

    Krista Hansen stated that some of her concerns were resolved. She stated that she would prefer
    that the zone remain but noted that a development agreement would be reassuring.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to approve Ordinance #35-2022, a General Plan
                amendment on 1.63-acres located at 870 North 2300 West, changing the land use
                designation from VLDRA to Neighborhood Commercial, with the direction that
                staff and the applicant work together on a development agreement which states that
                the childcare center is the only allowed use, and note that the General Plan
                amendment becomes affective only when the development agreement is approved
                and executed. Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Absent; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed with 4 in favor
    and 1 absent.
