CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following:
    • Jeff Young of YESCO signs shared his opinions on the proposals, stating he does not agree with the longer hold
        times proposed.
    • Paul Evans stated his concerns regarding signs on North University Avenue and referenced correspondence he
        had sent to the Planning Commission prior to the hearing.
    • James Carpentier shared his support for most of the proposed changes but does not agree with the longer hold
        times or the reasoning of safety for the changes.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Laurie Urquiaga asked about the map associated with the proposal, showing changes in permitted areas and multi-
       tenant areas. She thought the difference between long hold and short hold may be too much, but that fifteen second
       holds were appropriate.
   • The Commission asked about billboards as compared with on-premise signs and how the regulations differ.
   • Lisa Jensen asked about nonconforming rights with the proposal and if height limits were affected. She has
       concerns over proposed hold times and current brightness standards, she suggested a nighttime off standard for
       future discussions with the Council Sign Committee.
   • Brian Henrie stated hold times are not a concern for him, but that he would be interested on future changes to
       brightness standards.
   • Deborah Jensen stated her support for the changes and referenced the emails received from the public regarding
       concerns with bright signs. She also supports having “off hours” regulations come in the future. She related to the
       Commission that sign regulations are an important part of the City and attention to the visual impact needs to be
       a focus on future discussions and proposals.
   • The Planning Commission directed staff to look at brightness standards and other regulations that could address
       concerns of neighbors in future discussions with the Sign Committee.
   • The Planning Commission stated their support for Daniel Gonzales to serve on the Sign Committee to represent
       the Planning Commission in future discussions.

 ITEM 2       Alan Poulson requests Project Plan approval for renovation of the commercial building located at 655 E
              300 S in the General Commercial (CG) zone. Maeser Neighborhood. Dustin Wright (801) 852-6414
              dwright@provo.org PLPPA20200423



The following action was taken by the Planning Commission on the above described item at its regular meeting of March
10, 2021:


                                                  CONTINUED
 On a vote of 8:0, the Planning Commission continued the above noted application to March 24, 2021.

Motion By: Laurie Urquiaga
Second By: Dave Anderson
Votes in Favor of Motion: Laurie Urquiaga, Dave Anderson, Lisa Jensen, Daniel Gonzales, Brian Henrie, Robert Knudsen,
Deborah Jensen, Russ Philips.
Dave Anderson was present as Chair.


STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations.

 CITY DEPARTMENTAL ISSUES
 • The Coordinator Review Committee (CRC) has reviewed the application.

 NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING
 • A neighborhood meeting was held.

 NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
 • The Neighborhood Chair was not present or did not address the Planning Commission during the hearing.
 • The meeting was opened for public comment and there were no comments made so the public comment was closed.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
 Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:




                                                        Page 1 of 2
•   The business that would operate from the site is an architectural woodworking business that has a sales floor, office
    space, and assembly of their product.
•   A neighborhood meeting was held, and they hand delivered notice to neighbors within 1,000 feet.
•   They do cabinet work and architectural woodwork.
•   There is a similar dust collection system that SLCC has adjacent to residential zoning.
•   The hours of operation would be 7a.m.-5p.m. Monday-Friday.
•   There was a neighborhood meeting and there was a positive response for their business to go to this location. The dust
    collection system was discussed with the neighbors.
•   The system can’t be located inside the building because the intake would reduce the fresh air in the building.
•   There is typically less than one delivery a week which is much less that the grocery store.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
• The noise levels of the dust collector were discussed, and staff was asked to work with the applicant to find the decibel
   levels from the manufacturer and see if they would comply with the City noise ordinance.
• The use of the proposed business was discussed to see if it fit in the zone. The SLU code was 5700 and 5711 and staff
   was asked to examine this to see if this proposed business would be permissible.
• The Planning Commission wanted to better understand what the applicant would do to reduce the noise levels and
   how effective those measures would be in reducing the sound.
• The Planning Commission explored the idea of locating the dust collection unit to the southeast of the existing
   building so that it would not be located near the residential homes on 200 South.
