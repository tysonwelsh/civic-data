MEMO
To: City Council
From: Travis DeJong
Date: 2024-04-09
Re: Action Item: Providing Local Consent for an Off-Premises Beer Alcohol License for
Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban Smokes.



Comments:

Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban Smokes located at 437 E 12300 S Suite B is seeking
Local Consent for a Off-Premises Beer Alcohol License.

They have provided the following:
- Floor map showing where alcohol is to be served and stored.
- Sales tax license.
- Background check conducted by the Bureau of Criminal Identification.

Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban Smokes has also applied for and is in the process of
obtaining a Draper City commercial business license.

The findings for Local Consent approval are as follows:

DCMC 6-4-040 requires that an off-premises beer outlet be at least three hundred feet
(300') away from any community location (schools, churches, public libraries, public
playgrounds, and public parks) measured by the shortest pedestrian route taken from
the nearest patron entrance of the outlet to the property boundary of the community
location.

Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban Smokes has met this requirement.

DCMC 6-4-050 limits the number of off-premises alcohol licenses that can be granted by
the City to no more than one license per one thousand (1,000) Draper City residents
based on the latest population estimate provided by the Utah Department of Workforce
Services. The Utah Department of Workforce Services currently estimates Draper City's
population to be 51,017, giving Draper City the ability to grant up to 51 off-premises
alcohol licenses.

Draper City has currently granted a total of twenty-two (22) off-premises alcohol licenses,
meaning twenty-nine (29) additional off-premises licenses can be granted.
