                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-09

          A RESOLUTION GRANTING A PERPETUAL EASEMENT TO DRAPER
          IRRRIGATION COMPANY FOR THE INSTALLATION OF A WATERLINE
          ON DRAPER CITY PROPERTY LOCATED AT 12460 S 1130 E TO SERVICE
          THE BAINBRIDGE SUBDIVISION.

      WHEREAS, Ivory Development, LLC (“Ivory”) intends to develop Bainbridge
Subdivision (“Bainbridge”); and

          WHEREAS, Ivory must provide culinary water to Bainbridge; and

      WHEREAS, Draper Irrigation Company (‘WaterPro”) is the culinary water
provider for Bainbridge; and

      WHEREAS, to provide the required water pressure for Bainbridge, Ivory must
access the WaterPro mainline off of 12460 S; and

      WHEREAS, the only way for Ivory to access 12460 S is through Draper City
property; specifically, parcel number 28-29-452-029-0000; and

     WHEREAS, Ivory is requesting the Draper City Council grant a waterline
easement (“Easement”) to WaterPro as depicted in Exhibit A attached hereto.

      WHEREAS, Draper City will receive consideration in the amount $43,291.27 for
the Easement.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1. Perpetual Easement. The City of Draper grants a Perpetual
Easement to WaterPro as provided in Exhibit A. Final survey quality control may make
required technical adjustments to the exhibits prior to construction of the waterline.

     Section 2. Agreement. The Mayor is authorized to sign any agreement or
document necessary to effectuate the grant of and payment for the Easement.

       Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any


Resolution No. 24-09                       1       Perpetual Easement to WaterPro for Bainbridge Subdivision
other portion of this Resolution and all provisions, clauses and words of this
Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 4. Effective   Date.   This   Resolution        shall       become           effective
immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THE 6th DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2024.

           BAINBRIDGE WATERLINE AGREEMENT AND EASEMENT

       THIS BAINBRIDGE WATERLINE AGREEMENT AND EASEMENT
("Agreement") is made this _____ day of _________, 2024, by and between Draper City, a
Utah municipal corporation located at 1020 East Pioneer Road, Draper, Utah (“Grantor”) and
Draper Irrigation Company, a Utah corporation located at 12421 South 800 East, Draper,
Utah 84020 ("Grantee").

       A.     Ivory Development, LLC is developing the Bainbridge subdivision located at
approximately 1085 East 12650 South, Draper, Utah. The Bainbridge subdivision is located
within Grantee’s service area.

       B.      In order to provide culinary water to the Bainbridge subdivision, Grantee
needs to run a waterline south from Pioneer Road to 1030 East.

        C.     Grantor owns property through which the waterline must be run and is willing
to grant an easement across its property to Grantee for the construction of the waterline
pursuant to the terms stated in the easement attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

       NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy
of which is hereby acknowledged, Grantor and Grantee agree as follows:

       1.     Grant of Easement. Grantor hereby grants, transfers, and conveys to Grantee
an easement located on its property as stated in the Easement attached as Exhibit 1.

        2.     Payment. Grantee agrees to pay Grantors upon execution hereof the sum of
forty-three thousand two hundred ninety-one dollars and 27 cents ($43,291.27) for the
approximately 7,703.30 square feet of Grantor’s property required for the easement, which
was calculated at $11.24 per square foot representing 50% of the appraised land value per
Salt Lake County Assessor records at $489,600.00 per acre. The 50% figure is the same as
has been paid and received by Draper City in other easement agreements.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the
date first appearing above.
