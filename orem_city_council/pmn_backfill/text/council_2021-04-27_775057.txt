 1             DISCUSSION - Defederalization of the Economic Development Administration
 2             Revolving Loan Fund
 3
 4   Mrs. Lewis provided background of Orem’s RLF Program which included the following:
 5       The City of Orem Revolving Loan Fund Program was established in 1986
 6       The fund was made up in cash contributions of $400,000 (60%) from the EDA and
 7          $265,000 (40%) from the City of Orem, totaling $665,000.
 8       The purpose of the RLF is to provide low cost, short term gap financing to small Orem
 9          businesses and to create permanent long-term jobs within the city.
10       August of 2020, a new Orem RLF was created. The City of Orem CARES Act RLF,
11          funded through the Cares, Aid, Relief, and Economic Security (CARES) Act in the
12          amount of $500K, provides low-cost, short-term gap financing to small Orem businesses
13          that have suffered economic injury caused by the coronavirus (COVID-19) pandemic.
14       January 2020, the Federal Government enacted the Reinvigorating Lending for the Future
15          Act.
16       This Act gives more control back to the fund operators and allows the funds to be used
17          for broader economic development purposes to support business development and job
18          growth.
19       The Orem EDA RLF qualifies for the release of the federal interest in the loan program.

20   Mrs. Lewis concluded that the defederalization of these funds will allow the economic
21   development division to use the current EDA RLF funds to cover the cost of a comprehensive
22   update to the 2014 EDSP and potentially hire a consultant for business recruitment. This
23   beneficial change will give Orem broader economic development opportunities now and into the
24   future.
