        1. RATIFY CONTRACT WITH THERMAL WEST INDUSTRIAL, INC., FOR ASBESTOS REMOVAL & REMEDIATION
             Lynda Wangsgard, County Library Director, presented a contract for the removal or abatement of all
             asbestos and asbestos-containing materials at the main library, noting that the work includes general
             demolition, encapsulation, and some other forms of work. The contractor was prequalified and
             thoroughly vetted and will be required to comply with all laws and regulations of federal, state, regional
             and local authorities. The Library Board is taking a “belt and suspenders” approach to the project,
             ensuring a third party also monitors the air and surrounding spaces to verify compliance with OSHA,
             AHERA, and that the work area is safe for public occupancy.
             The contract term continues throughout the course of the 2½ years renovation project, although the bulk
             of the abatement will take place this spring and into the summer. The total contract amount is $686,694.
             Commissioner Ebert moved to ratify the contract with Thermal West Industrial, Inc., for asbestos removal
             and remediation; Chair Gibson seconded.
             Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    3. RESOLUTION AMENDING THE RESOLUTION THAT ESTABLISHED THE ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL
       BOARD & DELEGATED CERTAIN POWERS TO THE BOARD TO GOVERN THE OPERATIONS OF THE
       WEBER AREA DISPATCH 911 AND EMERGENCY SERVICES DISTRICT – RESOLUTION 4-2016
         David Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, stated that this board was established in 2006. Prior to that, Roy
         City, Ogden City and Weber County all operated their own separate dispatch services. A special service
         district was eventually created and the County Commission is the ultimate authority over the board. The
         Commission is given statutory authority to appoint an administrative control board, which governs the
         day-to-day affairs. The statutory regulations regarding appointments of this board’s representatives have
         changed and these amendments are intended to formalize the current statute.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 4-2016 amending the resolution that established the
         Administrative Control Board and delegated certain powers to the board to govern the operations of the
         Weber Area Dispatch 911 and Emergency Services District; Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
