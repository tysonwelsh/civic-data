Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
    Commissioner Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2) Training
Mike West gave a presentation on systematic safety, bike safety, and usage of bike lanes. He stated
new standards of design would be looked at with future design plans when MAG was involved.

3) Discussion of portable storage containers
Ross Dinsdale presented information on portable containers and storage. The commissioners
asked clarifying questions.

4) Discussion of gas station regulations

5) Adjournment
