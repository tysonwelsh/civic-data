            5. RATIFY THE SIGNATURE OF THE COMMISSION CHAIR ON A PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH
            INTERWEST SAFETY SUPPLY, LLC. FOR THE PURCHASE OF 3 VARIABLE MESSAGE SIGNS
            -Josh Daniels

            COMMISSIONER LEE – WILL BE VOTING ‘NO’. STATED IN OUR GUIDANCE FOR COVID TO BE
            NEEDED OR NECESSARY AND THINKS ALTHOUGH YOU COULD JUSTIFY A NEED, FEELS IT
            GOES TOO FAR ON THE NECESSARY GUIDELINE

            JOSH DANIELS, CLERK/AUDITOR’S OFFICE – UNDER RECENT CHANGE IN STATE LAW THAT IS
            ESSENTIALLY A TEMPORARY SUSPENSION OF TRADITIONAL ELECTION DAY POLLING
            LOCATIONS, EACH COUNTY IS IN A POSITION OF DECIDING WHETHER TO HOLD AN
            ALTERNATIVE METHOD OF ELECTION DAY VOTING WHICH IS A DRIVE-THRU PROCESS. FOR
            OUR COUNTY, WE HAVE 4 DRIVE-THRU LOCATIONS. AS WE HAVE PLANNED EACH OF THESE
            DRIVE-THRU LOCATIONS IN CONSULTATION WITH PUBLIC WORKS AND WITH PUBLIC
            SAFETY OFFICIALS, BOTH LOCALLY WITH CITIES AND WITH THE COUNTY SHERIFF’S
            OFFICE, IT WAS DETERMINED THAT CERTAIN LOCATIONS NEEDED THESE TRAFFIC SIGNS
            TO MANAGE THE TRAFFIC AND TO NOTIFY THOSE ON THE ROAD THAT WERE COMING INTO
            THESE LOCATIONS. EXPECTING SOME CROWDS. THAT’S WHERE THE DETERMINATION
            CAME TO OBTAIN THESE SIGNS FOR THIS PURPOSE. THOSE SIGNS WILL BE VERY USEFUL
            FOR THIS ELECTION AS WELL FOR OTHER EMERGENCY RESPONSE-RELATED TRAFFIC SUCH
            AS MEMBERS OF THE COMMUNITY COMING TO THE COUNTY TO RECEIVE PPE AND OTHER
            SITUATIONS WHERE YOU HAVE TO MANAGE TRAFFIC FLOW. THERE IS ALSO ANTICIPATION
            OF POTENTIAL PUBLIC VACCINATION EVENTS THAT WOULD POTENTIALLY BE A DRIVE-
            THRU VENUE AND THEN AGAIN PLANNING POTENTIALLY PLANNING FOR THE NOVEMBER
            GENERAL ELECTION WHERE THESE MAY BE NEEDED. THEY WILL CONTINUE TO BE USEFUL
            AS THE COUNTY RESPONDS TO THE NEEDS DUE TO COVID-19

            COMMISSIONER IVIE: MOTION TO RATIFY
            COMMISSIONER LEE: SECOND FOR THE VOTE
            AYE: ALL IN FAVOR – COMMISSIONER IVIE AND COMMISSIONER AINGE

                                                          REGULAR AGENDA


            1. APPROVE AND ADOPT A RESOLUTION AMENDING AND INCREASING THE 2020 BUDGETS IN
            UTAH COUNTY’S GENERAL FUND AND VARIOUS OTHER BUDGETARY FUNDS FOR THE
            CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDING (CARES ACT) RECEIVED FROM THE UNITED STATES
            DEPARTMENT OF TREASURY
            -Commissioner Ainge

            DANENE JACKSON, UTAH COUNTY FINANCIAL OFFICER - THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT, IN
            RESPONSE TO THE IMPACT OF COVID-19 ON LOCAL GOVERNMENTS, PASSED THE CARES
            ACT. THE CARES ACT HAS SEVERAL DIFFERENT FEDERAL FUNDING OPPORTUNITIES
            AVAILABLE TO THE COUNTY. THE LARGEST ONE THAT THE COUNTY RECEIVED IS FROM
            THE CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUND THAT WAS DISTRIBUTED FROM US DEPARTMENT OF THE
            TREASURY. THIS WHOLE BUDGET AMENDMENT IS JUST TO APPROPRIATE THOSE FUNDS
            RELATED TO THE CARES ACT. THE COUNTY ALREADY HAS THE $111,630,342 AND THIS IS JUST
            GOING THROUGH THE PUBLIC PROCESS TO SPEND THOSE FUNDS.

            THERE ARE OTHER GRANT OPPORTUNITIES THAT MAY BE AVAILABLE SUCH AS AN
            ELECTION GRANTS THROUGH THE CARES ACT. CONGRESS HAS STILL BEEN TALKING ABOUT
            FINDING ADDITIONAL FUNDING FOR LOCAL GOVERNMENT.

            COMMISSIONER LEE – THERE WAS SOME PRE-LOADING OF MONIES. THESE ARE JUST PRE-
            LOADED MONEYS AMOUNTS TO START THE PROCESS?

            DANENE – YES. THE BUILDING MAINTENANCE PROJECTS OR IT HARDWARE THAT WOULD
            BE NEEDED AS PART OF THE CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUND STILL NEED TO BE APPROVED
            THROUGH THE INTERNAL SERVICE FUNDS.

            1. WORK SESSION DISCUSSION REGARDING THE APPOINTMENT OF A DIRECTOR OF
            ADMINISTRATION AND PUBLIC AFFAIRS
            -Ezra, Commission Office -

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – WHEN IT COMES TO EXTERNAL ORGANIZATION OR JUST THE
            PUBLIC AT LARGE, IT CAN BE DIFFICULT TO COMMUNICATE WITH A 3-MEMBER
            COMMISSION. THIS HAS BEEN A CHALLENGE. THERE ARE SOME LOGISTICAL CHALLENGES
            OF US COORDINATING. ESPECIALLY DURING COVID-19, IT’S BEEN A CHALLENGE TO UNITE,
            GET TOGETHER GIVEN SOME OF THE PUBIC MEETINGS AND QUORUM REQUIREMENTS, TO
            RESPOND IN A QUICK AND EFFICIENT MANNER TO SOME OF THE NEEDS THAT WE NEED TO
            GET OUT TO THE COMMUNITY.

            WE’VE ALSO GONE THROUGH SOME SIGNIFICANT CHANGES OVER THE PAST YEAR IN HOW
            WE INTERACT WITH SOME OF THESE EXTERNAL FUNCTIONS.

            AT THE END OF LAST YEAR’S BUDGET PROCESS, WE TERMINATED OUR AGREEMENT WITH
            AN ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT CONSULTANT THAT WAS OUT ENGAGING WITH THE
            COMMUNITY.
