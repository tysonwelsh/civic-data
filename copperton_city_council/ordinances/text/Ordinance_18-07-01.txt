COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ORDINANCE # 2018-01-94 |
July 18, 2018
AN ORDINANCE GOVERNING THE ISSUANCE, RENEWAL, AND FEE SCHEDULE
FOR BUSINESSES
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council (the
“Council’”) met in a regular public meeting on July 18, 2018.
WITNESS

WHEREAS, the Council, in the interest in economic development and a commitment to
the economic freedom of its residents, desires to amend its procedures for the regulation of
businesses within the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”); and

WHEREAS, the cost burden in issuance and renewal of licenses does not result in revenue;
and

WHEREAS, the current business license fee charged does not significantly offset the
expenditure made in processing license applications, and the fee amounts present undue burdens
on small businesses;

WHEREAS, the Council desires to amend Copperton’s business license fee schedule to
zero dollars; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to convert Copperton’s business license requirements into
a business listing.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL OF THE

COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP AS FOLLOWS:
1. The Copperton Metro Township’s (“Copperton”) business license requirements are
hereby converted to a business listing with the following requirements:

a. In lieu of obtaining a business license, business owners that are in compliance with
all other applicable laws and regulations may provide Copperton or its agents with
written notice that they are conducting business in Copperton, provided that such
notice must include the name of the business, the nature of the business, and all
relevant contact information for the business:

b. Businesses that require inspections will pay for all inspection costs;

c. Copperton will continue to issue business licenses to those business that require
such licenses for other purposes, provided that such businesses must request a
license in writing; and

d. Copperton will not charge any fees for business listings or licenses.
2.  Copperton staff are hereby directed to make all changes that may be needed to the
Copperton Code to effectuate these changes.
3. Amendments to the Copperton Code required by this resolution will take effect January
1, 2019.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
18" day of July, 2018.
