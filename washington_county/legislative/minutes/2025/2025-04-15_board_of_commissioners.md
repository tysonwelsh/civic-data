MEETING MINUTES
APRIL 15, 2025

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on April 15, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3499, A RESOLUTION APPROVING

VIRGINIA CLASS SUBMARINES

* USS UTAH will be 28" of 40 Virginia class fast attack submarines to be built.

* SSN Primary Missions
* to seek and destroy enemy submarines and surface ships
* project power ashore with Tomahawk cruise missiles and Special Operations forces
* carry out Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance missions;
* support carrier battle group operations; and engage in naval mine warfare.

* Virginia class submarines are named after states.
Formerly, battleships of the US Navy were named after states (USS UTAH BB-31 ).
