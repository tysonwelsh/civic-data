MEMO
To: City Council
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
Date: 2023-10-17
Re: Approval of Resolution #23-63


Comments:

On August 3, 2023, Mayor Troy K. Walker, in his capacity as Draper City’s chief executive
officer, issued a Proclamation of Local Emergency No. 2 of 2023 (“Proclamation”) declaring
a state of local emergency in the City concerning damage to roads and city infrastructure
caused by flooding due to torrential rains (the “Rain Storm”), as authorized by the
Disaster Response and Recovery Act, UTAH CODE ANN. 53-2a-201 et seq. (the “Act”).

Utah Code Ann. 53-2a-208(6), provides that a local emergency shall not be continued or
renewed beyond the initial 30 days without consent of the governing body of the
jurisdiction subject to the declaration. The Draper City Council adopted Resolution 23-45
approving the Proclamation and extending it to November 10, 2023.

Because (a) the Proclamation will expire on November 10, 2023, absent action of the
City’s governing body (the “Council”) to extend it; (b) city roads and infrastructure remain
unrepaired; (c) the danger of additional damage to city roads, infrastructure, and private
property occurring should additional rain fall; and (d) plans are still being developed to
complete the necessary repairs to city roads and infrastructure, an extension of the
Proclamation's end date is needed.
