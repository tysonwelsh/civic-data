V. Financial Statement Update 
 Accounting reports this month was very smooth and nothing heavy to report. Bridgeway had the 
final draw request submitted for the roof replacements. All of the approved write offs have been 
taken care of and will be listed on the bottom of the respective statements.  B.A had a good year 
and is using direct labor costs. All properties are performing well and have reserves built up. HUD 
funded programs are coming in slightly negative. HCV has spent all the money that was received 
as part of the agency annual contributions contract. CoC program no longer has the master lease 
program so no revenue is generated but still has costs in order to administer the program.  
VI. DISCUSSION ONLY ITEMS 
A. 2024 – HAUC Board Meeting Schedule 
The board discussed continuing on with the schedule as is which will be the 3 rd Wednesday of 
each month at 12:00(noon) for the monthly board meetings. Everyone agreed this scheduled 
worked with their other obligations. A yearly calendar invite will be sent. 
B. HAUC Fuel Cards 
HAUC received a letter from the County indicating that they will no longer be allowing our 
agency to use the fuel pumps at the Utah County Fuel Station. Research is underway to find a 
solution to this at a lower per gallon rate than HAUC was paying to the County. Updates will 
be forthcoming.

B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-01-02 HAUC Board Elections  
April made a motion to approve the HAUC Board Elections. Suzanne seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
Board elections for the upcoming 2024 year. After discussion it was recommended to elect 
Steve White, Board Chair and Sara Hubbs, Vice-Chair. Both agreed.  
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-01-03 Grant Submission 
Sara made a motion to approve the MAG Grant submission. Suzanne seconded the 
motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC staff is asking the board to approve the submission of a grant through Mountainland 
Association of Governments (MAG). The preliminary grant application was discussed in the 
December 2023 meeting. HAUC will submit the full application on February 12, 2024. HAUC 
will be asking MAG for $450,000 for a property acquisition and rehab of 2 HAUC owned units. 
Once the rehab and acquisition is complete these 4 units will be set aside for survivors of 
domestic violence. 
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-01-04 Authorize Real Estate Purchase 
April made a motion to approve the authorization of a real estate purchase. Suzanne 
seconded the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
It is proposed to allow the Executive Director to sign a real estate purchase agreement. The 
purchase agreement will not exceed a given purchase price and the given property will be 
located within Spanish Fork or Springville.  Additional conditions include completing an 
inspection and legal review of the purchase agreement.
