PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
Thursday, September 15, 2022
Approved December 1, 2022
The following are the minutes of the Herriman Planning Commission meeting held on Thursday,
September 15, 2022 at 6:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.
Presiding: Chair Chris Berbert
Commissioners Present: Andrea Bradford, Heather Garcia, Brody Rypien, Alternate Forest Sickles,
and Adam Jacobson
Commissioners Excused: Jackson Ferguson, Andy Powell, and Alternate Darryl Fenn
Staff Present: Communications Specialist Mitch Davis, City Planner Michael Maloy, Planning Manager
Clint Spencer, Assistant City Attorney Matt Brooks, Deputy Recorder Wendy Thorpe, Planner | Talia
Wright, Planner ll Sheldon Howa, Engineer Josh Petersen, Planning Intern Windya Welideniya, Trails
and Open Space Coordinator Joe Zimmerman, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, and HPD
Operations Commander Brent Adamson.
1. 6:00 PM - Work Meeting (Council Chambers)
Chair Berbert called the meeting to order at 6:04 p.m.
Commissioners Bradford and Sickles arrived at 6:05 p.m.
1.1. Review of City Council Decisions

City Planner Maloy reported the Council discussed the Auto Mall MDAs in the work meeting portion of
last night’s Council meeting. Council adopted the AMSD Garden plots rezone from A-1 to C-2 without
zoning conditions.

1.2. Review of Agenda Items — Michael Maloy, City Planner

No questions or comments were offered.

Trails and Open Space Coordinator Joe Zimmerman reviewed the plan to align and cennect Eric’s Trail
to the Bonneville Shoreline Trail (BST). He identified one parcel which was within 300 feet of the trail.
About a dozen residents were at the neighborhood meeting, most were against the trail due to loss of
privacy and trespassing. Commissioners asked the distance between other trails and homes. Trails and
Open Space Coordinator Zimmerman responded some were around one hundred feet from property
lines.

1.4. Land Use Training on Group Homes — Todd Sheeran, City Attorney
City Planner Maloy informed the Commission that City Attorney Sheeran was scheduled to present but
was unable to attend tonight. The Land Use Training will be rescheduled to a later meeting.
The Commissioners agreed to watch a training video.
The Commission adjourned the work meeting by consensus at 6:57 p.m.
2. 7:00 PM - Regular Planning Commission Meeting (Council Chamber)
Chair Berbert called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m.

2.1. Invocation, Thought, Reading and/or Pledge of Allegiance
Deputy Recorder Thorpe led the audience in the Pledge of Allegiance

2.2. Roll Call
Full Quorum Present.

2.4. Conflicts of Interest
No conflicts of interest were reported

2.4. Approval of Minutes for the July 21, 2022 Planning Commission Meeting
Commissioner Garcia moved to approve the July 21, 2022 Planning Commission minutes as written,
Commissioner Jacobson seconded, and all voted aye.
