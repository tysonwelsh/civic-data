I.      Welcome – Mayor Dahle.
Mayor Dahle called the meeting to order at approximately 6:20 p.m. Mayor Dahle read into the
minutes the Determination of the Mayor concerning Public Meetings of the City Council of the
City of Holladay regarding holding them electronically due to the current COVID-19 pandemic
with a virtual anchor location.

II.   Public Comments.
No public comment was submitted.

III.    Public Hearing on Proposed Text Amendment to Title 13 Regarding Grading and
        Retaining Wall Standards.
Paul Allred, Community Development Dir., introduced the proposed text amendment. Council
Member Quinn asked for clarification about the applicability of the text amendment. Mr. Allred
stated that the amendment was for retaining walls in setback areas, specifically areas near fence
lines. The intent was to amend the process for the applicant to go to the hearing officer to seek a
variance.

Mayor Dahle opened the public hearing at 6:09 pm and there were no comments. Mayor Dahle
closed the public hearing at 6:09:30 pm.

IV.   Consent Agenda.
      a.    Approval of Minutes – May 14, 2020.
Council Member Fotheringham moved to approve the minutes of May 14, 2020. Council
Member Durham seconded the motion. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the
Council.

V.     City Manager Report – Manager Chamness.
There was no City Manager Report.

VI.     Council Reports and District Issues.
Council Member Durham commented on the sidewalk work done on 2000 East and Gunderson
Lane. He had received comments about the traffic control devices from his constituents and
asked for feedback. He also asked if the Council would consider inviting the Tree Committee to
an upcoming work meeting to discuss their thoughts and suggestions. Specifically, he mentioned
trees near City Hall that need maintenance. Mayor Dahle suggested that the Tree Committee
attend a Work meeting in August.

Council Member Quinn reported that she has received emails regarding speeding and golf carts
on the south end of Wallace Lane and other locations. She had received help on these issues
from staff. She also mentioned neighborhood concerns with an AirBnB in her area that she
would like to discuss with staff and the City Attorney.

Council Member Gibbons reported that he recently attended a Cottonwood Heights Planning
Commission Meeting regarding the AJ Rock development that abuts Canyon Cove. The meeting
was heavily attended by Holladay residents. They propose adding a new traffic intersection that
will impact Holladay residents. He was working to resolve the issues.

Council Member Fotheringham was also contacted by a constituent regarding a boarding house
in the area they are concerned about. He asked if staff could reach out to them and address their
concerns. He also mentioned a maintenance concern regarding a speed flashing light. Finally,
he asked for advice on an Accessory Dwelling Unit (“ADU”) request. Ms. Chamness agreed to
discuss the matter with him further at a later time.

Mayor Dahle asked if there were any concerns about fireworks this year. Council Member
Gibbons mentioned that after an initial burst, the fireworks seemed subdued. Council Member
Quinn received comments both in support of and against fireworks.

Mayor Dahle reported that he met with the Interfaith Council and this year they intend to
schedule the Annual Interfaith Service on the Sunday before Thanksgiving but it will be
primarily live streamed. He commented on the Governor’s presentation on COVID-19 and
mentioned that no real changes were made regarding face masks and school will be in session
with mask requirements. Ms. Chamness mentioned the Governor’s recognition of increasing
COVID-19 numbers statewide. The Governor challenged residents to reduce the spread to
around 500 cases per day on average before August 1 by continuing to wear masks and
implementing other basic precautions.

Council Member Fotheringham moved to recess the City Council Meeting and reconvene in a
Work Meeting. Council Member Gibbons seconded the motion. The motion passed with the
unanimous consent of the Council.

VII.   Recess City Council to a Work Meeting.
       a.     Presentation by Property Watch – Rashelle Hobbs, Salt Lake County Recorder.
Salt Lake County Recorder, Rashelle Hobbs, provided a background on the County Recorder’s
Office. She then presented the purpose and process for Property Watch, which is a program that

was developed internally. The purpose of the program is to enable residents to keep track of any
recorded changes to their property. This will help owners protect their property and makes it
easier to track recordings on their property. Ms. Hobbs asked for help in getting the information
out to the citizens by putting a link on their website or include information in the newsletters..

       b.      Presentation on I-80/I-215 Project – Rebecka Stromness, UDOT Project
               Manager, and Katie Kourianos, Public Involvement.
UDOT Public Involvement Specialist, Katie Kourianos, introduced the upcoming I-80/I-215
Project. UDOT Project Manager, Rebecka Stromness, provided an overview of the project
elements. She explained that the main purpose is to replace the aging pavement that is planned
on portions of both I-80 and I-215. New bridges will also be reconstructed under new standards
at 1300, 1700 & 2000 E. They will also be adding two eastbound lanes to 1-80 and two lanes to
I-215 at the split. The environmental study is wrapping up and should be available for public
viewing in the coming months. The project was expected to extend out for a couple of years.
Upcoming meeting dates would be provided.

Ms. Chamness asked about driving conditions during construction and how the project will
interface with other projects. Ms. Stromness stated that the contractors will strive to coordinate
with each other on various projects. She added that during construction, driving conditions will
likely fluctuate during different aspects of the project, however, contractors are incentivized to
keep traffic moving.

Council Member Quinn commented on prior experiences with a concrete batch plant.

       c.       UDOT Little Cottonwood Canyon EIS Draft Alternatives Presentation – Josh
                VanJura, Project Manager.
UDOT Project Manager, Josh VanJura, introduced the SR-210 project and described the
projected mobility situation. He briefly discussed the proposed alternative components to
address the concerns. He explained that the enhanced bus service option with a widened bus
lane in Little Cottonwood Canyon would provide the most time-efficient process but also has the
highest capital cost. The project is wrapping up the public comment period and the draft EIS
process will continue through the spring of 2021.

Council Member Gibbons commented on the people transit ratio and proposed parking stalls.
Mr. VanJura explained how those numbers were determined. There was also discussion about
the acquisition of the property east of the mobility hub.

Mayor Dahle asked about the funding source for the project. Mr. VanJura stated that the funding
will be processed through the Legislature but the funding has yet to be requested because the
selected component has not been chosen. There was discussion about the commitment to the
project.

Council Member Fotheringham commented on the differences between Options 1 and 2 and
incentives for riders to take the bus.

VIII.  Closed Meeting Pursuant to Utah Code Section 52-4-204 and 205 to Discuss
      Personnel Issues, Potential Litigation and Property Acquisition and Disposition.
Council Member Quinn moved to go into Closed Session pursuant to Utah Code Section 52-4-
204 and 205 to discuss potential litigation and property acquisition and disposition. Council
Member Fotheringham seconded the motion. The Council roll call vote was as follows: Council
Members Durham, Fotheringham, Quinn, Gibbons and Mayor Dahle voted in favor. The Council
convened in a closed session at 8:21 p.m.

Those in attendance in the Closed Session included Council Members Durham, Fotheringham,
Quinn, Gibbons and Mayor Dahle. Others present included Gina Chamness, Todd Godfrey,
Stephanie Carlson.

The minutes of the Closed Session were taken and are on file as a Protected Record.

Council Member Fotheringham moved to adjourn the Closed Session. Council Member Quinn
seconded the motion. The Council roll call vote was as follows: Council Members Durham,
Fotheringham, Quinn, Gibbons and Mayor Dahle voted in favor. The motion to go out of closed
session at 8:30 p.m. passed with a unanimous vote.

IX.    Adjourn.
 Council Member Fotheringham moved to adjourn the City Council Meeting. Council Member
 Durham seconded the motion. The Council voted unanimously in the affirmative.

The City Council Meeting adjourned at approximately 8:32 p.m.
