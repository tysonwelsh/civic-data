 3. SPECIAL RECOGNITION
        a. Recognition of West Jordan High School Girls Softball as State Champions
           Council Office Director Alan Anderson introduced West Jordan High School Athletic
           Director Lei Lolohea, who shared that the West Jordan High School Girls Softball Team
           won both Region and State Titles. She announces that one of the team’s coaches was
           named Deseret News Coach of the Year, and one of the pitchers was part of the elite nine
           in the State and named Gatorade Player of the Year. Ms. Lolohea said all seniors on the
           team had offers to play at the next level, and she was proud to work with the team. The
           Council took the opportunity for photographs.

       d. Ordinance No. 25-24 General Plan Text Amendment – Moderate Income Housing
          (Chapter 8 Update)
          City Planner Larry Gardner explained the proposed ordinance would amend the strategy
          portion of the Moderate Income Housing Plan. Strategies A, G, J, and V would be modified,
          Strategies F, M, O, and T would be removed, and Strategy R would be added. Mr. Gardner
          introduced Strategy E regarding reduced regulations related to internal or detached
          accessory dwelling units in residential zones, which was not included in the proposed
          ordinance, but could be included before August 1, 2025 if the Council desired.

           Chair Lamb opened a public hearing at 7:33 pm.
           Comments:
           None
           Chair Lamb closed the public hearing at 7:33 pm.

           Council Member Green wanted the Legislature to note that West Jordan had tried to make
           positive changes in moderate income housing, and one of the hurdles had been that the
           City could not find partners. He believed cities should not continue to be blamed for
           creating the housing crisis as long as finding partners was required. Council Member
           Green noted that West Jordan zoned for a project that would allow for almost 600 units,
           and the developer said they could not build it. He stated density and the cities were not
           the issue. Council Member Green thanked Mr. Gardner for his work.

           MOTION:      Vice Chair Whitelock moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 25-24 General
                        Plan Text Amendment – Moderate Income Housing (Chapter 8 Update),
                        including Strategy E as outlined.
                        Council Member Green seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Chad Lamb, Kayleen Whitelock, Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Kelvin
                      Green, Zach Jacob, Kent Shelton
           NO:
           ABSENT:
           The motion passed 7-0.

 7. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Resolution No. 25-032 Court Services Interlocal Agreement with Taylorsville City
          City Administrator Korban Lee spoke of the process in the last year of finding a way to
          continue to provide the community with court services and require less subsidy from the
          General Fund. Mr. Lee said Taylorsville City had also struggled with some of the same
          questions, and it was determined that proceeding together would benefit both
          communities, sharing overall operating and overhead costs. He introduced Taylorsville
          Mayor Kristie Overson and City Administrator John Taylor, both in attendance
          representing Taylorsville. Mr. Lee asked that the City Council approve Resolution No. 25-
          032 and support the Interlocal Agreement.

           better services at a lower cost to residents. He said West Jordan staff had been most

 8. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. City Council Reports
          Council Member Green mentioned litigation over PFAS chemicals used for firefighting in
          airport properties across the country, and spoke of an opportunity to serve on a
          remediation advisory board. He said he would get some flyers to distribute if anyone was
          interested.

           Council Member Bedore read aloud a text sent to him by a Taylorsville resident
           expressing appreciation for helpful interaction with West Jordan Officer Morgan.

           Vice Chair Whitelock reported on a recent ChamberWest meeting, noting they offer many
           resources for small businesses. A guest speaker from the World Trade Group provided
           helpful information on tariffs and international business. She invited anyone interested
           in overseas business support to reach out to her.

           Council Member Shelton said he attended a neighborhood meeting regarding the Sunrise
           Trails neighborhood (formerly Welby West), where residents complained that homes had
           been built on higher ground or foundations, allowing newer residents to look into
           neighboring backyards. He said he felt frustrated for the residents, being there was
           nothing the City could do to help the situation. Council Member Shelton thanked Mayor
           Burton for making sure the residents felt they had been heard.

       b. Council Office Report
