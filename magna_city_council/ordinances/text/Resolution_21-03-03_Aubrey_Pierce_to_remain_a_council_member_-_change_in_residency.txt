MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
Resolution No. 21-03-03 Date: MARCH 23, 2021
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL,
PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE §10-3-301(6), CONSENTING FOR AUDREY
PIERCE TO REMAIN AS COUNCIL MEMBER FROM DISTRICT #5
PENDING TEMPORARY ABSENCE FROM DISTRICT DUE TO
CHANGE IN RESIDENCY
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Utah municipality subject to
Title 10 of the Utah Code, including the requirements of Section 10-3-301of said Code
requiring Council members to live within the boundaries of their respective districts, with
minor exceptions, and
WHEREAS, Audrey Pierce is the duly elected Council Member representing
District #5 on the Magna Metro Township Council, and
WHEREAS, due to no fault of her own Audrey Pierce lost her home within the
boundaries of District #5 and has, thus far, not been able to move back into a home with
the boundaries of District #5, and
WHEREAS, Audrey Pierce continues to live within the boundaries of Magna, to
faithfully represent District #5 interests in the Council, and is in the process of finding a
home within the boundaries of District #5 to move into, and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code §10-3-301(c), Audrey Pierce as requested
additional time to reestablish her residence within District #5, and
WHEREAS, the Magna Council can grant additional time for an elected officer of
the municipality to reestablish residency within a district by following the requirements
of Utah Code §10-3-301(6)(b), and

WHEREAS, after a public meeting on March 23, 2021, duly noticed and the
receiving public comment, if any, at such meeting, the Council has determined it is in the
best interests of both Magna and District #5 that Audrey Pierce be given additional time
to reestablish her residency within District #5. NOW THEREFORE BE IT

RESOLUTION

RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby consents and agrees
that Audrey Pierce, the duly elected Council Member for District #5, may continue to
serve on the Council while she seeks to reestablish residency within District #5, which
consent shall continue through November 16, 2021.
