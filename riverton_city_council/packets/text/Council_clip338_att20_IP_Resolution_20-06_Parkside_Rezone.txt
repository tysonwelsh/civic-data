ITEM:                    Approximately 4245 W. 12600 S., From Rm-14-Sd With Elderly Housing

                         Riverton Ranch Llc, Applicant – Jason Lethbridge, City Planning Managerr

SUMMARY:

This is a public hearing for a Rezone of an existing 6.12 acre parcel located south of 12600 South
near 4245 West. The subject property is a triangle shaped parcel south of the Rocky Mountain Power
Corridor, just east of the Welby-Jacob Canal. The Property was rezoned to RM-14 (see Ordinance
No. 17-04) with an elderly housing overly as part of the application that rezoned the property to the
north as RM-14 SD. The north properties that were zoned RM-14-SD became Parkside Phase 1 and
Phase 2. This development is currently under construction with single family detached and multi-
family units. Residential.



The applicant is requesting the Elderly Housing Overlay be removed from the subject parcel, but is
not requesting the same SD designation that exists on the other Parkside Properties.
