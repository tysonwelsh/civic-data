History of SuperTrees – SuperTrees Incorporated has been serving communities and helping grow urban forests
    throughout the Western United States since 2005. Our company provides trees and arborist services for several
    cities in the northwest including Salt Lake City, Seattle, Boise, and Denver.
    We help cities with tree selection and developing their master plans so that they can diversify their Urban Forest.
    Once a plan has been developed, our nursery puts the trees into production so that the tree varieties and quantities
    are available to successfully execute the master plan. Our two farms, located South of the Portland metro, produce
    over 100,000 trees in 150+ varieties.
    For the past 10 years SuperTrees has been the exclusive supplier of trees for Salt Lake City. When we started
    working with Salt Lake City 10 years ago, we supplied roughly 300 trees per year. This year we will provide and plant
    over 2000 trees for Salt Lake City.
History of Daniel Allen; SuperTrees Utah Division Manager – My current ISA credentials include Board Certified Master
