23
24   Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by the Department of Development Services for the City to amend portions of Section
25   22-24-2 The Hub District (HD) zones of the Orem City Code pertaining to enacting the Edge zone adjacent to
26   University Parkway and modifications to the overall height requirement and permitted uses in the Edge zone and other
27   amendments including enacting Sections 22-24-2(J) Sidewalks and Landscaping and 22-24-2(K) Alternate
28   Development Standards for the HD-E Zone. In addition, amending Section 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City
29   of Orem by rezoning portions of the properties located generally at 314 East University Parkway, 212 East University
30   Parkway and 1315 South State Street from the HD zone to the HD-Edge zone.
31
32   Staff Presentation: The Development Services Department requests the city amend portions of Section 22-24-2
33   pertaining to the HUB District ordinance. One of the main purposes of the amendments is to create a new Edge zone
34   within the HUB District. The proposed Edge zone would be in three general locations adjacent to University Parkway
35   with a depth of 200 feet from the back of the sidewalk creating an area for retail uses with no minimum height
36   requirement.
37
38   Currently, the Core zone within the HUB District requires a minimum height of three stories for new structures. The
39   Edge zone would allow one story structures which are limited to the retail uses outlined in the proposed ordinance.
40   The purpose for the change is to allow property owners an opportunity to develop pad sites adjacent to University
41   Parking while maintaining leases for tenants in the larger retail spaces that usually have restrictions in their leases that
42   limit the height of structures located in the front of the development. The Edge zone would allow a property owner to
43   maintain these leases while at the same time encourage additional retail development along University Parkway
44   frontage.
45
46   Additional changes include updating setback requirements for parking lots adjacent to the street. A minimum six foot
47   (6’) landscaped setback would be required from the back of the sidewalk when adjacent to parking stalls, drive isles
48   or drive thru stacking lanes.
49
50   Furthermore, enacting Sections 22-24-2(J) and 22-24-2(K) will clarify sidewalk and landscaping requirements as well
51   as alternatives in developing in the Edge zone.
52
53   In addition to the ordinance amendments the Development Services Department requests the city rezone the properties
54   located generally at 314 East University Parkway, 212 East University Parkway and 1315 South State Street from the
55   HD-C zone to the HD-Edge zone as outlined on the proposed map.

 1   The Development Services Department held a neighborhood meeting through Zoom on October 22, 2020 at 5:50 p.m.
 2   with two individuals plus city staff in attendance. The two attendees made positive comments regarding the proposed
 3   changes. They represented property within the proposed Edge zone.
 4
 5   Recommendation: Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City
 6   Council to amend portions of Section 22-24-2 The Hub District (HD) zones of the Orem City Code pertaining to
 7   enacting the Edge zone adjacent to University Parkway and modifications to the overall height requirement and
 8   permitted uses in the Edge zone and other amendments including enacting Sections 22-24-2(J) Sidewalks and
 9   Landscaping and 22-24-2(K) Alternate Development Standards for the HD-E Zone. In addition, amending Section
10   22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by rezoning portions of the properties located generally at 314 East
11   University Parkway, 212 East University Parkway and 1315 South State Street from the HD zone to the HD-Edge
12   zone.
13
14   There was discussion on the item. Some of the key issues that were discussed were:
15       ● In regards to the portion of this rezone that is east of State Street, parking was discussed. It seemed that the
16           existing parking lot there was a little cramped. It was asked that if the property owner wanted to develop that
17           parking lot, would they still need to meet the current parking requirements. The answer was yes.
18       ● It was asked if this was also true of the portion west of State Street. Yes it was.
19       ● It was asked if this rezone allows for new buildings to be built right up to University Parkway. It would be
20           required.
21       ● It was asked why the corners at the intersection of University Parkway and State Street were not included in
22           this rezone. The City still wants larger buildings brought to the front of this intersection.
23       ● The parking concern was brought up again. The existing parking is now terrible at some of these locations in
24           the rezone. It was asked what the required parking rate is and if it was currently being met. Mr Bench
25           explained that before this area was rezoned into the Hub District, the parking rate was 4 per 1000ft2 of the
26           building. The Hub District requires a rate of 3 per 1,000ft2, a reduction. A parking study was conducted two
27           years ago prior to that change to see if it made sense to reduce the parking rate. That study revealed that at
28           the time, the commercial centers in this area were parked at 80%. That information is what guided the decision
29           to change the parking rate.
30       ● Ms. Jensen asked what the overall goal for the Hub District was. Mr. Bench answered that it is to be a
31           walkable area. There is currently a good transit line in the area that is well used by many demographics. A
32           similar bus line will likely go down State Street in the future. However, the City also wants this area to have
33           convenient parking. The City believes that many of the existing traffic and parking issues in this area could
34           be solved if people felt comfortable parking in one location and walking around the area instead of continually
35           getting back in the car to drive to a store a block away.
36
