    4) Update on general planning progress and timeline

        Ms. Davies and Ms. Jordan provided an update to the general plan progress, timeline, and provided
        a PowerPoint presentation. Commissioner Seiger-Webster asked if a report will be put out or will
        this just be in the general plan. Ms. Davies said just providing background data, and well focus on
        writing a chapter and open how you want to proceed, by chapter or outline draft. Commissioner
        Seiger-Webster said she would like to see a draft document before the steering committee moved on
        to other items. Commissioner Spagnuolo said could get that information and if it needs to be
        presented in a pc meeting, as anyone from the public can attend the steering committee and does
        not just want the document presented at a meeting. Commissioner Millen said chapters would be a
        good idea, time to handle each component instead of a whole document to digest for
        recommendations. Ms. Davies said the next meeting is June 23rd and digging into the land use
        element and happy enough to go through the transportation element and provide some feedback.
        Ms. Jordan said could move the transportation and take it back to the steering committee at a later
        date. Commissioner Seiger-Webster would like to provide feedback through a planning commission
        meeting. Commissioner Millen said it should be noted in a meeting. Commissioner Seiger-Webster
        said can put on an agenda business item once they have had time to review and provide feedback.
        Ms. Davies said schedule a meeting after they have had a few weeks to review the document. Aiming
        for two weeks prior to the meeting and have a meeting in June.

        Ms. Davies said the general plan timeline components and the elements required are housing,
        transportation, and land use. Resilience and infrastructure could be included, and an economic
        development component would be a smart idea. Commissioner Millen asked about a deadline with
        the state. Ms. Davies said the end of the year is reasonable to have written up and adopted. Ms.
        Davies said covered a lot of ground, hoping to have land use element done in 3-4 months.
        Commissioner Seiger-Webster asked if the presentation could be emailed out.
