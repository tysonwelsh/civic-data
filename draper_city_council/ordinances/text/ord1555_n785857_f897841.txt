City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 4, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1555, amending the
Official Land Use Map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Avery Townhomes Land
Use Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s
Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 5th day of October 2022.
Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
