                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE        NO. 24- 08


AN ORDINANCE OF RIVERTON CITY ADOPTING A COMPENSATION SCHEDULE
               FOR ELECTIVE AND STATUTORY OFFICERS AND ALL OTHER
                                       MUNICIPAL OFFICERS


            WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10- 3- 818, a Public Hearing was held on June
4, 2024 for the purpose of receiving public comment to consider the compensation schedule for
statutory officers and other municipal officers for Fiscal Year 2024- 2025; and,

            WHEREAS, a notice of the Public Hearing regarding the compensation schedule for
elected and statutory officers and other municipal officers was published and posted as required
by law; and,

            WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to adopt the compensation schedule for elected and statutory officers and other municipal
officers.




        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
that the compensation schedule for statutory officers and other municipal officers for Fiscal Year
2024- 2025, as attached hereto, is hereby adopted.

       This ordinance, assigned No. 24- 08 shall take effect immediately upon passage and
acceptance as provided herein.

            PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
June, 2024 by the following vote:
