NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of
          upcoming events, meetings, activities, etc.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS. None

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS. Mayor Wood thanked the Council for listening to
          the Community Value Survey results. These results are important in knowing
          which areas Mayor Wood and staff need to work on this year. The reports will
          be uploaded to the Council’s Dropbox and the information will be added to
          the City’s website.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS. None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS. Council Member deWolfe said with the
          storming of the Capital a lot of people can become numb to these tragic events
          and if these things were happening in another country it would be shocking.
          He suggested that we all pause and reflect on these attacks and understand the
          weight of it. Democracy is something that we all need to work at and fight for.
          We can’t control a lot of the things that go on around us but we live in a great
          City that values diversity and practices inclusion.

            Council Member Pinkney said a police officer was beaten to the point of death
            while domestic terrorists stormed the Nation’s capital and she wanted to take a
            moment to acknowledge these events for what they were. Community
            members were disgusted by what happened and she hopes that all the elected
            officials in the Nation and in this City are held accountable for their actions.

            Council Member Huff thanked the Council for their comments. She agrees
            that the storming of the Capital last week should not be something that is
            normalized.

       6.    INFORMATION – COVID-19 UPDATE. Fire Chief, Terry Addison, gave
            the Council a current update on the COVID-19 pandemic. He also gave the
            Council an update on COVID-19 vaccine.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS

   1. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Repealing Chapter 4 of
      the Downtown South Salt Lake City zoning Ordinance and Design
      Standards; Repealing Chapter 4 of the East Streetcar Neighborhood form
      based code; amending Chapter 17.03 of the South Salt Lake City Code to
      Integrate the Land Use Matrices from Chapter 4 of both the Downtown
      South Salt Lake City Zoning Ordinance and the east Streetcar Neighborhood
      form based code; Amending Chapter 17.04 permitted use review and 17.05
      Conditional use review of the South Salt Lake City Land Use and
      Development Regulation ; all as part of a Comprehensive effort to update
      and consolidate South Salt Lake City Land Use regulations. Community
      Development Director, Alex White, gave the Council a brief overview of this
      Ordinance. A copy is attached and incorporated by this reference. This Ordinance
      is part of the code cleanup project the City staff is working on. The Land Use
      Matrix is currently located in three different locations of City code. There are no
      new Land Use Matrix processes added to this update nor were there any definition
      changes.

       Council Member deWolfe moved to approve this Ordinance.
