Z-5-2025: Petition by VALOR PROPERTIES LLC requesting a zone change from LI (Light
Industrial) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 2342 South 5600 West and is 0.6
acres in size. Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented request for the zone change. The property currently features an
existing building and pavement, with planned improvements including streetscape enhancements,
painting, roofing, fascia work, and potentially window replacements.

The primary reason for this request is the desire to establish an auto service business and outdoor vehicle
storage facility, which are currently prohibited in the LI (Light Industrial) zone.

Anderson explained that the area was rezoned from Manufacturing to Light Industrial approximately 8
to 10 years ago as part of a beautification and redevelopment initiative. He noted that the area had
historically been used for salvage yards. He also pointed out that two nearby properties had successfully
been rezoned back to Manufacturing, with extensive development agreements requiring significant
improvements.
If the Planning Commission approves the rezone, the applicant would then need to apply for a
conditional use permit for outside storage.

Nancy raised concerns about the increasing number of mechanic shops and storage units in the area. In
response, Anderson clarified the historical prevalence of auto service and storage uses in that particular
area.


ZT-3-2025: Petition by EMANUEL SHAOULIAN requesting an amendment to Section 7-6-301 of
the zoning ordinance and the addition of Section 7-7-117.5 to the zoning ordinance to allow
medical and dental office in the M (Manufacturing) zone on properties that are partially or
entirely within 500 feet of a residential use or residential zone. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-
3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve presented a proposal to amend West Valley City zoning code restrictions to allow
medical and dental offices in manufacturing zones within 500 feet of residential zones. The department
reviewed historical rationale behind the 2017 ban on medical offices in manufacturing zones. Key
reasons for the ban included their distance from residential areas and concerns regarding homeless
shelters being located near medical services. Steve noted that the new proposal would only permit these
offices in areas where residential and manufacturing zones are within 500 feet of each other. The
Planning Commission discussed the implications of this Code change. Martell asked if there are
potential drawbacks. Steve addressed concerns related to homeless shelters and their associated services.

Brandon expressed his view that the state would override local control regarding shelter placement,
regardless of the proposed ordinance. Martell and Nancy then discussed the 500-foot radius requirement.
Brandon added that the current radius was intentionally narrow to avoid overreach.


ZT-4-2025: Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to amend the zoning ordinance to comply with
changes made to State Code during the 2025 legislative session. Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-
3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov.


Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented code changes in response to legislative updates from the 2025 state
session. He explained that some of these changes were minor and largely aligned with current practices,
such as allowing persons desiring unlisted uses to amend the code. Other changes involved referencing
state code directly, a strategy designed to reduce the need for frequent updates to the City's own code.

Assistant City Attorney Brandon Hill emphasized that these adjustments would not significantly alter
daily operations, noting that even relevant interest groups had expressed agreement with the proposed
changes. Steve Pastorik committed to including the specific state code references in the staff report.

C-10-2025 Petition by SUPER AUTOSS requesting conditional use approval for auto service. The
property is located at 5418 West 3500 South on 0.70 acres and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial).
Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented Super Autoss, conditional use application for an existing auto sales
business sought to add auto service to its operations. The need for a conditional use permit stemmed
from the property being adjacent to residential zones

The proposed auto services include tires and wheels, shocks and struts, batteries, brakes, auto glass,
window tinting, and oil changes. The service operation hours would be Monday through Saturday, from
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The applicant provided a written commitment that overhead doors would remain
closed during auto service hours, and there would be no outside storage. The site underwent a permitted
use review in 2023 for a parking lot expansion. At that time, significant improvements were made,
including new parking, landscaping, and the installation of curb, gutter, and sidewalk along the frontage,
all coordinated with UDOT. This current application focuses solely on the use of the existing building
for auto service.

The property's existing parking lot could accommodate the two proposed service bays, which are located
behind the main Super Autos sales area. The landscaping and sidewalk are already complete. The auto
service operations would take place in the tan section of the building, which is situated behind the blue
section and remains out of sight from the street. Both the entire site and the building are well-
maintained. No architectural requirements were suggested for the building, as the applicant has
proactively addressed all operational concerns in their written submission.

C-11-2025      Petition by NV PLUMBING requesting conditional use approval for outside storage.
The property is located on three parcels at 7055 West, 7041 West, and 7011 W. Gates Ave on 1.31
acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented a request for a conditional use permit to allow outside storage on
three parcels located near Gates Avenue. These parcels are situated amidst a mix of existing uses,
including single-unit dwellings and manufacturing facilities.

The current owner, who operates a plumbing business, intends to convert an existing home on the
property into an office and utilize the remaining area as a storage yard. Anderson presented the site plan,
emphasizing required setbacks, landscaping, and right-of-way improvements. He also outlined proposed
modifications for ADA compliance and adjustments to the parking area.

The commission agreed to add a condition requiring the removal of the garage door, as it would no
longer be functional. The recent cleanup of the site was acknowledged as a positive development toward
meeting city requirements.

adjourned at 4:25 PM.
