AGREEMENT NO. 2018-241

4.     ADOPT (OR DENY) AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE
       ORDINANCE, SECTION 6-3, RELATED TO THE WATER SUPPLY REQUIREMENTS FOR
       PLANNED SUBDIVISIONS
(Track A, 06:55)
Brandon Larsen of the Utah County Community Development Office explained the applicant behind this proposed
ordinance received approval for reduced irrigation water requirements several months ago and is now requesting an
additional reduction of 0.85 from the current requirement of 1.0 acres per square foot. When Commissioner Graves
expressed concerns regarding fire suppression, Mr. Larsen noted this exception would only apply to properties that do
not require fire hydrants.

Provo resident Robert Stevens came forward to state he is opposed to this ordinance. He read a report from the United
Nations detailing the destruction of farmland through irrigation, and spoke of a book by a famed biologist and apostle
of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints commending ‘dry’ farming. Commissioner Graves asked why Mr.
Stevens would object to an ordinance which reduces water requirements and Mr. Stevens clarified the water
requirements are still too high. Mr. Stevens and Commissioner Ivie discussed irrigation and aquifers.

           Commissioner Lee made the motion to adopt the ordinance amending Utah
           County Land Use Ordinance Section 6-3, related to the water supply
           requirements for planned subdivisions, as identified in Regular Agenda Item No.
           4. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Graves and carried with the
           following vote:
                                                                            AYE: Nathan Ivie
                                                                                     William C. Lee
                                                                                     Greg Graves
                                                                            NAY: None

(Track A, 17:35)
Orem resident Phil Snelgrove came forward to ask the commissioners for assistance with a transaction regarding
Lincoln Point on the Utah Lake shorelines: a missing letter from the Utah County Health Department Division of
