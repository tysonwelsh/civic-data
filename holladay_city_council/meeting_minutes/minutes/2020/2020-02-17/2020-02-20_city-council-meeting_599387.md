XI.   Recess City Council to a Work Meeting.
Mayor Pro Tem Petersen called the meeting to order.

       a.      Policy Discussion on Accessory Dwelling Units (“ADUs”).
Mayor Dahle introduced a discussion on Accessory Dwelling Units (“ADU”). Mr. Allred
introduced the staff report and stated that staff is looking for general direction from the Council.
The Council is required to identify three ways to address affordable housing to come into
compliance by December 1, 2020. The Mayor asked if the Council could change the Code and still
meet the State compliance requirements. Mr. Allred stated that the State requirements for
compliance are not clear but the City may be able to meet the compliance requirements by adjusting
some definitions in the Code. There was discussion regarding specific Code definitions.

Mr. Allred informed the Council that any changes to the ordinances will have to go to the Planning
Commission. The Planning Commission will have public hearings and ultimately come to the City
Council. Staff needs the Council to provide a scope to begin the process. A comment was made
about what the Council needs to do to meet compliance. It was mentioned that transportation is
very connected to affordable housing. Holladay does not have large open spaces to develop, which
is a unique feature.

Staff provided the Council with proposed questions to be answered and discussed potential answers
to each. It was mentioned that the City should look at what other municipalities are doing to come
into compliance. It was suggested that the Council may want to consider allowing ADUs and
adopting standards. Mr. Allred reported that the Council has the ability to suggest a policy be
presented to the Planning Commission.

Mayor Dahle asked staff to provide a list of what specifically is needed from the Council to move
forward to meet compliance with HB 34 and what other cities are doing Council Member Petersen
commented on the Council’s ability to enforce any proposed standards on ADUs. The Council
would like to get this to the Planning Commission by April.

        b.     Discussion on Proposed Changes to the Noise Ordinance.
Staff discovered inconsistencies in the Noise Ordinance between Millcreek and the City of Holladay
regarding the time construction is allowed to commence. Council Member Petersen stated that the
Council’s intention was for construction to begin at 8:00 a.m. Staff suggested that large
construction projects on the border of the City commence no earlier than 7:00 a.m. It was
mentioned that temporary permits allowing construction at 7:00 a.m. are available. The Council did
not agree that construction should be able to start at 7:00 a.m. at 3900 South in general. The
Council wished to explore the possibility of allowing start times prior to 8:00 a.m. with narrow
exceptions.

        c.     Discussion on Skateboarding in the Plaza.
The City has received numerous complaints from local businesses about skateboarding and safety
issues. Mayor Dahle discussed reaching out to Rim Rock about their private property concerns. It
was mentioned that more signage prohibiting skateboarding might help. Mayor Dahle will also
discuss the concerns with the Olympus Jr. High Principal.
