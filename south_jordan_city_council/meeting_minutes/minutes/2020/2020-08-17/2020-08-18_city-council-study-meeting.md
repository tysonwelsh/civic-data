STUDY MEETING

       A. Welcome, Roll Call and Introduction: By Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey

Mayor Ramsey called the meeting to order. She excused Council Member Zander, who will be
arriving late. She introduced the electronic meeting.

       B. Invocation: By Council Member Don Shelton

Council Member Shelton offered the invocation.

       C. Mayor and Council Coordination

Mayor Ramsey said they are not doing a Utah League of Cities and Towns in person conference.
There will be some chances for tours and some online options. It will be done similarly to how the
Salt Lake Chamber of Commerce held their conference. It will be broadcast.

Council Member Zander joined the meeting at this time.

       D. Discussion/Review of Regular Council Meeting

The City Council reviewed tonight’s agenda.

Council Member Marlor asked about the project being done at 9800 South 2200 West. Was there a
line there? Engineering Director Klavano said no, it was deterioration of the sidewalk. He said the
goal was to get the project done before school started, which it will be. He also noted the T
intersection installed at 3200 W. He said people are confused over turning left out, but overall it
seems to be working well in that area.

Council Member Zander said a resident called her about the project on Vermillion. Mr. Klavano said
that is the 2nd part of the project for the storm water out of the ponds on Millerton.

Mayor Ramsey said proponents of the ranked choice voting want to meet with the Mayor and City
Council again and make their proposal. She said Vineyard and Payson have tried it. Mayor Ramsey
said she has not received an answer on how they would have a separate ballot from the rest of Salt
Lake County.

Council Member Harris said South Jordan is such a large city; they need to see results and data from
more cities before they should do it. Mayor Ramsey concurred. Council Member Marlor said he is
opposed to the concept. He said he would be willing to meet with them for 10-15 minutes. Council
Member McGuire concurred and said he does not want to meet with them. Council Member Zander
concurred. She said the two cities that have tried it are so small and would not be relevant for their
city. Mayor Ramsey said there are challenges if they are the only city in Salt Lake County doing it.
Council Member McGuire said this is not being pushed by residents of the city, it is an outside third
party organization, and that makes him hesitant. Council Member Shelton said he is open to listening
to them, but he does not see the benefits of it and is not sure why they would consider it.

         E. Discussion Item: Police update (By Police Chief, Jeff Carr)

Police Chief Carr reviewed a prepared presentation (Attachment A)

Council Member Harris asked if officers have a use of force incident and do a report, do they do the
report independently of each other? Police Chief Carr said they are filled out independently. It was
noted that if there are discrepancies in the report, it is addressed.

Council Member McGuire asked how soon after an incident do they fill out a report? Chief Carr said
typically before the end of their shift.

Council Member Zander asked how many people sit on the community advisory board and what is
the length of their term? Chief Carr said there are 7 members and they rotate every couple of years.
They can stay a second term if needed, but it is unlikely the board members would remain after 2
years.

Council Member McGuire asked how do they find residents to participate on the board? Chief Carr
said last time, they advertised on social media. He said they review applications and try to find as
much balance as they can on the board. He said he would get the names of the community advisory
board to the City Council.

Police Chief Carr said ideally, they like an officer to stay on a beat so that they get to know an area.
That does not always happen. He said he can give the City Council a map of which officers are in
which beat.

Police Chief Carr noted that the police department is accredited. That is not a state requirement, but
is important for South Jordan.

Council Member Shelton asked if implicit bias training is required? Chief Carr said yes, it is part of
the mandatory 10 hours.

Katie Olson reviewed some data and statistics relative to 2019 arrests. She said they took the race
code, and took a random sample to represent the population. She said they pulled variables that they
thought were significant. She said they looked to see how much variation in the data was explained
by those variables. They found nothing. She said race was not a significant predictor; someone being
armed or use of a controlled substance was a significant predictor.

Council Member Shelton said in the policy manual, does it include a policy on the use of the armored
vehicle? Chief Carr said they are almost done with that policy. They have been using the federal
agreement.

Council Member Harris said regarding the recommended future hires for staff, is that recommended
over additional officers? Police Chief Carr said some analysis would need to be done to determine
that. For example, the use of force with a mental situation, having a social worker does not change
the fact that we still send multiple officers to that call. A position might prevent them from hiring an
officer, but ideally, it would be in addition to officers.

Mayor Ramsey thanked the police department for their outstanding service.

Chief Carr said crime is occurring in every city in the county. He is grateful they still have people
willing to do this work.

Council Member Zander thanked the police officers. She said it is a valuable report for the City
Council. She is grateful to have this data to share with the public. She said she is an advocate for the
police. The police need their support.

Council Member Harris said he appreciates that they are not getting complaints from the residents to
defund the police. He said the people he has talked to have respect for the police officers.

ADJOURNMENT

Council Member McGuire made a motion to adjourn. Council Member Zander seconded the
motion. The vote was unanimous in favor.

The August 18, 2020 City Council meeting adjourned at 6:14 p.m.
