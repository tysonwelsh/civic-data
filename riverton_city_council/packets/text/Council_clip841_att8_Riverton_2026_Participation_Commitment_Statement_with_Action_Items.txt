                        Why are these actions the most impactful way to reduce wildfire risk for this PE?
             Consider the highest wildfire risk areas within the boundary and the values to protect from the threat of wildfire.

The selected actions focus on maintaining low wildfire risk within Riverton while ensuring the community remains prepared
for potential wildfire incidents. While Riverton has limited Wildland Urban Interface exposure compared to other
jurisdictions, areas along river corridors, open space, and undeveloped parcels still present potential wildfire risk to nearby
homes and infrastructure. The identified mitigation, preparedness, and prevention actions prioritize community awareness,
operational readiness, and targeted fuels reduction where appropriate. Continuing these efforts allows Riverton to
proactively manage wildfire risk while maintaining a low overall exposure and PC requirement. UFA will remain engaged
with the city to identify emerging risks and support future wildfire mitigation, preparedness, and prevention needs.
