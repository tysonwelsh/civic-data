MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
Resolution No.: 21-04-04 DATE: April 27, 2021
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING THE 2021 FIREWORKS RESTRICTION MAP FOR MAGNA
RECITALS
WHEREAS, pursuant to State Law, the Magna Metro Township Council (the
“Council”) is required annually to adopt a fireworks restriction map (the “Map”) to be
used by the fire department to reduce the risk of fires by limiting the locations in Magna
where private fireworks can be set off during legal times for fireworks; and
WHEREAS, the UFA Fire Marshall’s Office has presented Magna with the
proposed fireworks restriction map for 2021, hereinafter known as Exhibit A; and
WHEREAS, the Council, accepts the expertise of the Fire Marshall’s Office in
recommending the map for adoption; and
WHEREAS, the map improves the protection of the health and safety of Magna’s
residents; now therefore be it
RESOLUTION
RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council approves the attached
fireworks restriction map (Exhibit A), and this resolution shall serve as notice to the
Unified Fire Authority and Unified Police Department of the Council’s approval to use
and enforce the restrictions in the areas identified by the map.
PASSED AND APPROVED by the Magna Metro Township Council in Magna,
Salt Lake County, Utah this 27 day of April, 2021.
