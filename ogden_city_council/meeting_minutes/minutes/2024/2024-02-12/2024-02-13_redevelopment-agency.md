         A moment of silence was observed.

          Acknowledgements: Black History Month – Pastor Charles Petty. To commemorate Black History Month, the Ogden
City Diversity Commission recognized Pastor Charles Petty for nearly 40 years of service to the Ogden community. Pastor Petty
stated that he has been successful due to the amazing group of people that have surrounded him for the past 40 years. Many of the
accomplishments he has achieved were due to volunteer efforts and he is grateful to the Executive Director and Board for taking
time to acknowledge the work that has been done and to commemorate Black History Month.
          Board members thanked Pastor Petty for his efforts to effect change around him and for being a man of wisdom, insight,
and influence in the Ogden community.

Public Comments
          Adrienne Zubiller expressed her pleasure with the City’s acknowledgement and recognition of Pastor Petty.
          Teresa Bramwell addressed Vice Chair White, noting that she voted for her in her last campaign for office in Ogden City;
she voted for her because she was not originally from Ogden, she is a woman, and she seems very educated and informed on the
issues facing Ogden City. She felt Vice Chair White could make changes that would benefit women in Ogden City. She feels as
though Ogden City has been in a ‘strangle hold’ for decades and some people are able to make money while others are not.
Women are in the ‘not category’. She asked Vice Chair White to think about those things when she is voting on issues that come
before her. This morning there was a story on Good Morning America regarding home ownership; in 2023, 19 percent of all
homes were purchased by single women and only 10 percent were purchased by single men. Prior to 1974 a woman could not
obtain a mortgage, but the current generation of women can own homes. Women are buying homes because they want stability in
their lives, and they are willing to make financial sacrifices that allow them to afford a home. In East Central Ogden, the
homeownership rate is just 40 percent, while the rate in Utah and Weber County is 70 percent.
          Heath Satow thanked Board member Blair for raising the question regarding the Brown Ice Cream Building in tonight’s
work session, but it appears that there will not be an answer provided tonight. He also referenced a question he asked a few weeks
ago regarding the projects underway in the City being managed by Q Factor and he asked Administration to provide an answer to
that question as well.
          Angel Castillo thanked Pastor Petty for the work he has done in the Ogden community, and she thanked the Executive
Director and Board for honoring him. She then noted that during the last City Council meeting she had a question about minimum
unit sizes in the group dwelling ordinance. She asked that the City carefully consider the minimum square footage for a dwelling
space in a group dwelling development. She then noted that she was thrilled to hear Board member Hyer ask about deed
restrictions in tonight’s work session because she has been suggesting the City use that mechanism for many years to impose
restrictions on certain types of developments where an applicant is asking for some sort of concession from the City.

Executive Director Comments
          Executive Director Nadolski also thanked Pastor Petty for his contributions to the community; he also thanked the Board
for participating in recognizing him. He then stated that he is working to get answers regarding the Brown Ice Cream building;
Administration has reached out to the current owner to try to learn of his plans for the building and to determine if the obligations
agreed to by the previous owner will be passed on to the new owner. He added that he is also trying to get updated information
regarding the Northen Utah Wildlife Rehabilitation Center and will be providing an update to the Board soon regarding the timing
of the Center’s relocation as well as the Dinosaur Park’s expansion plans. He then asked Chief Administrative Officer Brown to
address Mr. Satow’s question about the Q Factor projects in the community. Ms. Brown stated the building on 25th Street has been
secured to ensure the safety of the surrounding area. Relative to the Ogden Business Exchange project, they are likely in default
and Administration is working to evaluate the City’s next steps.

Board member Comments
          Board member Blair thanked Pastor Petty as well as the Diversity Commission for the valuable work they are doing in
Ogden City. One of his favorite parts of being an elected official is being able to associate with so many great people that are
working to impact the community they live in. He then noted he attended the groundbreaking ceremony for The Wonder Block
project last Friday and it was very exciting, and he looks forward to the future of that project.
          Vice Chair White thanked Ms. Bramwell for elevating the matter of homeownership by women in the Ogden community.
This is something that is very important to her, along with elevating women to leadership positions, and she is happy to be part of
a government that values those issues. She noted that the City completed a study three years ago to evaluate whether women were
being paid equally to their male counterparts and the results of that study were positive; however, it may be time to conduct a
similar study again in the near future.


     There being no further business to come before the Agency, BOARD MEMBER MYERS MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 6:28 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.
