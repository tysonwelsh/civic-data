Minutes of the Work Session of the Lehi City Council held Tuesday, August 3, 2021, at Lehi

1. Tour of Lehi High School
   The Mayor and Council met at Lehi High School to tour the recently remodeled school.

2. Regular Agenda
   2.a. Consideration of Resolution #2021-47, approving an interim licensing and operating
   agreement between Bird Rides and Lehi City.

   Motion: Councilor Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2021-47, approving an interim
   licensing and operating agreement between Bird Rides and Lehi City. Councilor Koivisto
   seconded the motion.

   Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
   Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

    2.b. Tax Increment Finance 101- Economic Development Director
    Marlin Eldred, Economic Development Director, reviewed the requirements and options for
    Tax Increment Financing. He also updated the Council on some changes that the Legislature
    made for Redevelopment Agency incentives which includes incentives for affordable housing
    near a transit station.

    2c. Consideration of Adjourning into a Closed Session to discuss the purchase, exchange, or
    lease of real property.

    Motion: Councilor Condie moved to Adjourn into a Closed Session to discuss the purchase,
    exchange, or lease of real property. Councilor Koivisto seconded the motion.

3. Joint Work Session with the Planning Commission
   The Council and Planning Commission sat in tabletop groups while the Planning staff
   presented the proposed timeline for the General Plan Adoption that included an Open House
   and Public Hearing. Staff also presented a map that showed the density differences between
   the old plan and what is being proposed. They tried to focus the densities near TOD areas and
   preserve and limit density in environmentally sensitive areas and open space. The Engineering
   Department explained the studies they did to determine the max capacities for utilities.

4. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
