                            MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                           MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY MEETING
                               Wednesday, April 12, 2023 - 7:46 PM
                                    Approved April 26, 2023
                                      Thomas M. Rees Justice Center
                                         8040 S Redwood Road
                                         West Jordan, UT 84088

MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY
 1. CALL TO ORDER
       BOARD: Chairperson Chris McConnehey, Vice Chairperson Pamela Bloom (absent), Kelvin
              Green, Zach Jacob, David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, Melissa Worthen
       STAFF: Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Mayor Dirk Burton, City Attorney Josh
              Chandler, Budget & Management Analyst Rebecca Condie, Assistant City Manager
              Jamie Davidson, Policy Analyst & Public Liaison Cassidy Hansen, City Administrator
                Korban Lee, Council Office Clerk Cindy Quick,
Chair McConnehey opened the meeting at 7:46 pm

 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
      a. Acceptance of the proposed Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of
         the City of West Jordan for the Fiscal Year 2024
         Becky Condie oriented the Board with the Fiscal Year 2024 tentative budget. The budget
         pays debt service on a 2016 revenue bond for the Public Works building. She reminded
         them April 26, 2023 was scheduled for adopting the tentative budget and a public
         hearing was scheduled for May 10, 2023. The budget will be posted on the City website
         for public access.

           MOTION: Board Member Green moved to accept the Tentative Budget for the Municipal
           Building Authority of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2024. Board Member Worthen seconded
           the motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously (6-0).

 3. CONSENT ITEMS
      a. Approve Minutes
         • June 22, 2022 – Municipal Building Authority Meeting
           MOTION: Board Member Green moved to approve the minutes. Board Member
           Whitelock seconded the motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed
           unanimously (6-0).

 4. ADJOURN
MOTION: Board Member Green moved to adjourn the meeting. Board Member Whitelock
seconded the motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously (6-0).

The meeting adjourned at 7:49 pm
