                                       PROJECT DESCRIPTION
        On June 11, 2015, the applicant’s partner, Scott Gailey, acted as the applicant for a Planned Unit
Development (PUD) subdivision (Magna Hamptons) for 80 two-family dwelling units. The project was approved
with the assumption that the applicant would be acquiring the two additional parcels to the south which
connected to Parkway Boulevard (2820 South).
        The applicant, Dee Bates, has not been able to acquire those two parcels to the south (2710 South 7200
West and 2802 South 7200 West). As a result, the applicant has decided to come back to the Planning
Commission to ask for approval to amend the PUD. This includes reconfiguring the remaining available land to
include a second access point for ingress/egress to the subdivision and adding ten additional lots.
        Increasing the entire PUD density by ten units would allow for a density of 8.8 units per acre on the
7.51-acre site. The original conditional use approval limited the maximum units per acre to eight (8).
        The proposed configuration of the property includes the roadway going straight out to 7200 West,
rather than going south and connecting to Parkway Blvd (2820 South) as previously designed. Therefore, the
PUD’s roadway would be a horseshoe shaped drive with both entrances from 7200 West. Please see attached
PUD plat.

19.84.050 Approval/Denial Authority
The planning commission has the authority to approve, deny, or approve with conditions conditional use
applications.

    1. Planning Commission Approval.
           1. The planning commission shall review and approve or deny each application during a public
              meeting.
           2. The planning commission's decision shall be based on information presented through the public
              meeting process, including: the materials submitted by the applicant, the recommendation of the
              director or director's designee, and input from interested parties and affected entities.
           3. If conditions are specified, the director or director's designee shall issue a final approval letter upon
              satisfaction of the planning commission's conditions of approval.
           4. If the applicant fails to meet all conditions of approval within twelve months of the planning
              commission's decision, the application is deemed denied. A twelve-month extension may be
              granted upon the payment of an additional filing fee equal to the original filing fee.
           5. A planning commission decision shall be made on a complete conditional use application within a
              reasonable time frame, not to exceed ninety days. The planning commission is authorized to

19.78.110 Amendments To The Development Plan
The division director or designee may authorize minor changes in the location, siting, or character of buildings and
structures if required to resolve an engineering or other technical issue, or other circumstances not identified at
the time the final development plan was approved. No change authorized under this section may cause any of the
following ("major changes"):

    1.    A change in the use and/or character of the development.
    2.    An increase in the overall density and/or intensity of use.
    3.    An increase of more than five percent in overall coverage of structures.
    4.    A reduction or change in character of approved open space.
    5.    A reduction of required off-street parking by more than five percent.
    6.    A detrimental alteration to the pedestrian, vehicular, bicycle, circulation, or utility networks.
    7.    A reduction in required street pavement widths.
    8.    An increase in building height.
    9.    A decrease in building setback.

Any major changes must be proposed to the planning commission after receipt of a recommendation by planning
staff. Proposals under numbers 1. through 9. above require the filing of a new application. Generally speaking, any
major changes must be recorded as amendments in accordance with the procedure established for adopting the
final development plan.

                              PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
The MSD Planning Staff recommends that the Magna Planning Commission approve the request to amend the
Conditional Use and PUD Subdivision (Magna Hamptons) allowing for a density of up to 9 units per acre by
allowing for Phase #3 to have ten additional lots, subject to the following conditions:
        1. That the applicant be required to and comply with the recommendations from all reviewing
            agencies.
        2. That the applicant be required to install a 6’ high solid visual barrier wood, masonry or other
            material designated by the Planning Commission, along the property line around the residential
            dwelling units.
        3. All individual private yards be fenced entirely for privacy.
        4. That the applicant complies with the Development Standards for Recreation Facilities and Open
            Space for the PUD project.
        5. That the applicant be required to submit a Final Landscape Plan for the proposed new lots to staff
            for approval.
        6. That the applicant be required to comply with the parking requirements for guest parking.
        7. That the applicant be required to meet all County Engineering Standards for the private roadway
            within the project.
        8. That the HOA Covenants, Conditions and Restrictions (CCR’s), note that there is to be no on-street
            parking for residents or guests, and that signage to reflect “No Parking” be put along the roadways.
            Applicant should submit a copy of the CCR’s to Planning Staff.
