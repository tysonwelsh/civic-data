RESOLUTION 20-01: AUTHORIZE THE ISSUANCE AND SALE OF NOT MORE
THAT $5,500,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT OF TAX INCREMENT
AND FRANCHISE TAX REVENUE REFUNDING BONDS; AND RELATED
MATTERS
Chairman Christensen discussed proposed Resolution 20-01 that would authorize the
issuance and sale of not more than $5,500,000 Aggregate Principal Amount of Tax
Increment and Franchise Tax Revenue Refunding Bonds; and Related Matters

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Analysis of existing debt and economic conditions has identified significant savings
       for the city by refunding the outstanding Tax Increment and Franchise Tax Revenue
       Bond 2010B Series.

       For the purpose of refunding the Refunded Bonds, funding a deposit to a debt
       service reserve fund, if necessary, and paying costs of issuance of the Bonds, the
       Agency hereby authorizes the issuance of the Bonds which shall be designated the
       “Redevelopment Agency of West Valley City, Utah Tax Increment and Franchise
       Tax Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series 2020, in the initial aggregate principal
       amount of not to exceed $5,500,000. The Bonds shall mature in not more than
       seven (7) years from their date, shall bear interest rate not to exceed five percent
       (5.00%).

Upon inquiry by Chairman Christensen there were no further questions from members of
the Agency, and he called for a motion.

       Mr. Buhler moved to approve Resolution 20-01.

       Mr. Huynh seconded the motion.

       A roll call vote was taken:

       Mr. Fitisemanu                Yes
       Ms. Lang                      Yes
       Mr. Buhler                    Yes
       Mr. Huynh                     Yes
       Mr. Nordfelt                  Yes
       Chairman Christensen          Yes

       Unanimous.
