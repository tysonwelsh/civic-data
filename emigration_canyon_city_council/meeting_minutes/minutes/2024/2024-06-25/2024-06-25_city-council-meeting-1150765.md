RESOLUTION NO. 2024-06-02

Date: June 25, 2024

A RESOLUTION OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL ADOPTING A FEE SCHEDULE
FOR FEES AND OTHER ASSESSMENTS FOR SERVICES PROVIDED BY THE
EMIGRATION CANYON CITY FOR 2024-2025 AND REPEALING AND REPLACING
RESOLUTION 2023-11-01
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon City (“Emigration”) contracts with the Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Services District ("MSD") to provide planning and development services; and
WHEREAS, in addition to Emigration Canyon, the MSD provides planning and
development services to the cities of, Kearns, Magna, White City, the Town of Copperton and
the Town of Brighton as well as unincorporated Salt Lake County; and
WHEREAS, Section 3.42.040(A)(l) of the Emigration Canyon Code states that all "fees
to be charged by [an Emigration Canyon] office or agency shall be proposed by the agency
each year as part of the annual budget processes;" and
WHEREAS, Section 3.42.040(A)(3) states that "[u]pon review and approval of fees by
the Emigration Canyon Council, the fee schedule shall be placed on file with the Emigration
Canyon Clerk;" and
WHEREAS, the MSD board approved the attached 2024-2025 fee schedule for the
planning and development services the MSD provides; and
WHEREAS, The Council feels it is necessary to repeal and replace the previously
adopted fee schedule to reflect increased costs and other changes in the city activities with the
attached 2024 - 2025 fee schedule.
THAT:

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL

1. The 2024 Fee Schedule which was adopted by resolution 2023-11-01 is repealed.

Code, including but not limited to the civil penalties set forth in Section 19.94.070.

6. Council Business

6.1 Presentation of the Audited 2023 Financial Statements
Stewart Okobia, Director of Finance for the Municipal Services District, reviewed
Emigration Canyon's audited 2023 Financial Statement. Mayor Smolka confirmed that the
canyon is in good financial health. Mr. Okobia explained that due to transitioning from a
Metro Township to a City status, the budget is now revised from a calendar year of January
2024 to December 2024 to a fiscal year, commencing July 1, 2024, and ending June 30,
2025.
6.2 Emigration Canyon Planning Commission Membership and/or Terms
The Council discussed the current planning commission appointments and term limits.
The discussion focused on amending the existing ordinance to permit longer terms and
implementing staggered appointments.
6.3 Emigration Canyon Community Council Committee
Cameron Platt, Legal Counsel, noted that the Council's most recent action was the
creation of the committee, which is currently unchaired and unstaffed. He emphasized the
need for a minimum of three members on the committee to establish a quorum for voting,
recording minutes, and taking action. The committee's authority is to function as the
executive branch of the government, implementing programs and actions approved and
budgeted by the Council. Additionally, the committee may make recommendations on
beneficial programs.
Mr. Platt outlined two options for committee appointments:
1. An executive branch committee appointed by the mayor provides flexibility as it does
not require a Council meeting.
2. A Council committee, which requires a Council vote to add or remove members.
In either case, the committee can include council members, the mayor, city staff, and
volunteers.
Council Member Harris reported on the duties previously handled by the now-dissolved
Emigration Canyon Community Council. A discussion followed regarding stipends for
committee members. Mayor Smolka suggested appointing staff from the Municipal

6.4 Discussion with Emigration Canyon Improvement District Regarding Recent Mill
and Overlay in the Canyon and the Chain of Communication
Eric Hawkes, Emigration Improvement District, reported that risers were not installed
prior to the recent mill and overlay on Skycrest Road, causing approximately 13 valves and
one manhole to be covered under asphalt. He requests the Council's support in sending a
bill to the County to uncover the valves. He also inquired about the communication process
regarding road improvements in Emigration Canyon.
Mayor Smolka responded by noting that there is a springtime meeting scheduled
with the Salt Lake County Public Works Department and ensured Mr. Hawkes would be
included in the next meeting.
The council expressed support for Mr. Hawkes to seek indemnification from Salt
Lake County for repairs needed due to the covering of the valves and manhole.
