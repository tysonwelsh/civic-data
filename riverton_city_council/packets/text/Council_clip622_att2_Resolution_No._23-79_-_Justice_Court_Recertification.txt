                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-79

          A RESOLUTION REQUESTING THE RECERTIFICATION OF THE
                   RIVERTON MUNICIPAL JUSTICE COURT

        WHEREAS, Utah Code annotated § 78-7-103 requires that Justice Courts be recertified
at the end of each four-year term; and

       WHEREAS, the term of the present Court shall expire on the 1st of February 2024;
and

       WHEREAS, the members of the Riverton City Council have received an opinion letter
from the Riverton City Attorney, which sets forth the requirements for the operation of a Justice
Court and feasibility of continuing to maintain the same; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
City to continue to provide for a Justice Court; and

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RFSOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1.     Riverton City hereby requests recertification of the Riverton Municipal
                      Justice Court by the Justice Courts Standards Committee and the Utah
                      Judicial Council.

       Section 2.     The City Council of Riverton City hereby affirms their desire and
                      willingness to continue to meet all requirements set forth by the Judicial
                      Council for continued operation of the Riverton Municipal Justice Court
                      for the next four- year term of court, except as to any requirements waived
                      by the Utah Judicial Council.

       Section 3.     The Riverton City Council hereby directs an acceptable local security plan
                      with the Court Security Director, of the Administrative Office of the
                      Courts for the state of Utah.
       .
       Section 4.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
