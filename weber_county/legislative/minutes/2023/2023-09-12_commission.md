B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Duncan Olsen
C. INVOCATION: Commissioner Bolos
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: We all remember where we were on September 11th, 2001. To
   me, this was the event of my generation. I believe this as significant an event as Pearl Harbor. I think everyone
   would agree the world changed on that day and not necessarily for the better. I think it’s important to recognize
   the people that died. I give credit to the Brent Taylor Foundation for the displays that gave recognition to the
   people that died that day. 2996 people died that day with citizens from 78 different countries, 343 fire fighters, 23
   police officers, and 190 died at the Pentagon. I think it’s important that we remember and remind the younger
   people the importance of this day and what we have in terms of protected freedoms we continue to enjoy and teat
   we will continue to enjoy in the future if we remember the past.
