WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 239, 2024

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Almquist at 9:02 p.m. on October 29, 2024, in the Overflow Conference
room 421, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Chairman Gil
Almquist, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County
Attorney Devin Snow, Chief Ryan Anderson, Chief John Hennessy, Chief Eric Phillips,
Chief Brandon Moody, Chief Charles Guthrie, Chief Robert Hardy, EOC Director Jason
Whipple, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Jayanne Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:
DISCUSSION WITH FIRE CHIEFS ALONG SR-18 CORRIDOR

Commissioner Almquist welcomed everyone in attendance and thanked the Fire Chiefs for
attending. Commissioner Almquist proposed creating a memorandum of understanding
between Washington County and the fire districts along the SR-18 corridor. This would
allow for a more uniform operating procedure when a multiple-agency response is needed.
Each district would also benefit from additional training opportunities and allow training
uniformity between the districts. Blue Card training was recommended for all the districts
to attend.

Several Chiefs highlighted achievements and noteworthy items from the previous months
in their own districts and expressed interest in creating a memorandum of understanding.
Several Chiefs also shared important feedback about working with fellow SR-18 districts.
The Commissioners encouraged there be several meetings between the districts to create
more uniformity among the districts. It was also recommended each district fire chief
attend a future combined meeting to further discuss SR-18 fire operations.

Commissioner Almquist thanked everyone for attending and adjourned the meeting at
10:06 am.

Y ANNE LEWIS GIL ALMQUIST
PUTY CLERK CHAIRMAN
