3.9) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request to amend the Lehi City
Master Transportation Plan, adding a new master planned roadway alternative connecting
Clubhouse Drive to Redwood Road.

Chairperson Newall opened the public hearing.

Mark Balliger- He respectfully disagrees that this will help the residents in the area. He believes
it will be a much higher traffic area. He feels that Silicon Slopes is becoming more like Silicon
Valley. Lehi must preserve large plots of green areas.


William Hereth- He would like to voice his support for this crossing. He knows that it is faster
for him to ride his bike than sit in traffic. He agrees that they do need green space but feels that
this is needed.

Michelle Stallings- She wants to make sure they look at all the alternatives before settling on this
one.

Philip Wheatley- Why in the world would the city give up a resource like the Thanksgiving Point
golf course? Any extension is not financially sound and will not help traffic.

General Joey Green- wanted to get a point of general clarification on whether it was on 2700
North. House Bill 265 signed by March 14- Land Use request needs to be done to be compatible
with the military base. Wants to make sure we preserve his training site.

Amanda Hardman Wilson with Hardman Property submitted yesterday a plan to be developed in
the 70 acres next to Camp Williams. If this plan is allowed their property would not be able to
develop the way they are hoping to and that would have a significant impact. We need to
understand the problems this could create.

Lisa Hardman- 2100 North was designed to expand the commuter road. She feels that 2100 north
should be finished as it was designed before adding a new road.

Laura Hardman- feels the Planning Department is ignoring the fact the Holbrook Farms goes to
Redwood Road. Thinks you should consider the roads that are already there.

Kylene Payes- She understands the need to help alleviate the traffic. There is no reason to take
taxpayer property to add a new road when there is existing that is already there.

Chairperson Newall closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to approve Lehi City’s request to amend the Lehi City
Master Transportation Plan, adding a new master planned roadway alternative connecting
Clubhouse Drive to Redwood Road; including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in
the staff report and noting the abysmal traffic needs with stating that this is not an endorsement
of the precise road, but the idea of as a whole and strongly urges the council to consider all the
dozen options as the environmental study moves forwards The motion was seconded by
Commissioner Carlson.

Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
Commissioner Carlson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
