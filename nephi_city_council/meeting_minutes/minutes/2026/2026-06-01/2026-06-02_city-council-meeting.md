      first baseball game would be on June 20, 10:00 am, at the high school baseball field. This is in

PUBLIC HEARING – FY 2027 ENTERPRISE FUND TRANSFERS
       Seth Atkinson explained the enterprise funds transfers. The transfers include dividend payments
       from the utility funds to the general fund. The dividend transfers are the result of the
       community’s investment in these utilities, and the transfers help cover the costs of important
       city services, including police, streets, library, recreation services, parks, cemeteries, and other
       city functions. The transfers are based on the percentage of expenditures for each fund.

PUBLIC HEARING – COMMUNITY IMPACT BOARD PROJECT HEARING
       City Administrator, Seth Atkinson, explained that the city has submitted an application to the
       Community Impact Board (CIB) to request funding for the Police Station Project. The new Public
       Safety building would provide specialized security for evidence storage, have proper holding
       areas and private rooms for interviews. It would be built to allow for Nephi’s future growth.
       Following is a summarization of the request:
           • $7,500,000 requested as CIB Funds; $7,500,000 as City Contribution from private
               financing.
           • Request for a shared facility between Nephi City Police Department, Utah Highway
               Patrol, Utah State Driver’s License Division, and Adult Probation and Parole.
           • Schedule is:
                    o Schematic Design: April 2026 – July 2026
                    o Design Development: July 2026 – November 2026
                    o Construction Documents: November 2026 – January 2027
                    o Bidding / Permitting: January 2027 – April 2027
                    o Construction: April 2027 – October 2028
                    o Occupancy: October 2028
       Funding, if granted, would be awarded in October. Otherwise, the project would be funded
       through private financing. A CIB loan would be issued at a significantly lower rate than market
       which would aid in the bottom line of the project. Rent revenues from other agencies leasing
       space in the building will help cover debt service costs. The city is also proposing a property tax
       increase to supply funding for the project.

Work Session
   1. DENSITY DISCUSSION
           a. Developers have been requesting higher densities in various zones. This is mainly due to
              the housing pressures that exist in Utah and the difficulty in finding a supply of housing
              for younger generations. Past Planning Commissions and City Councils have been clear
              about keeping density limits and even reducing density in the city’s residential zones.
              The council discussed where and if density should be increased.
           b. Seth Atkinson introduced the idea of an R4 zone, which would allow higher density
              around transportation corridors and freeway exits.
           c. Concerns were raised about the need for affordable housing and challenges with having
              owner-occupancy requirements to keep affordable housing from being bought and then
              rented out by investors.
           d. Discussion ensued about avoiding high density developments on Main Street but
              possibly allow it in the CU2 zone. The vertical discount and the potential for commercial
              and residential mixed-use developments in the CU1 and CU2 zones.
           e. Mayor Seely invited input from realtors and developers who were in attendance on
              affordable housing solutions. Smaller lot sizes, reducing building permit costs, and
              allowing twin homes and duplexes in multiple zones.
           f. The council questioned affordability of smaller homes and explored other housing
              solutions: allowing external ADUs, reducing setback requirements, zero-scape, and low
              maintenance options.
   2. FRANCHISE FEES
           a. A new fiber optic provider is interested in coming to Nephi. They want to come under
              the cable franchise agreement. The federal cable act only requires them to pay if
              someone signs up for cable television.
           b. The mayor and council discuss the need for the provider to pay franchise fees for all
              services provided. Current providers are paying the franchise fee for all services.
           c. The company will be presenting to the council at the work session on June 9.
   3. BUILDING LIGHTING OPTIONS
           a. The city is planning to put lighting around city hall and the bowery at the park. The lights
              can be used for year round holiday lighting or as lighting for safety and park
              illumination.
           b. Four bids were received from different companies. All were similar in price. Versa Lights
              was recommended to be awarded the bid for their lifetime warranty and cost-
              effectiveness.
