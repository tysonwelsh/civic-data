                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                    Tuesday, April 11th, 2023 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Gage Froerer, James “Jim” H. Harvey, and Sharon Bolos
STAFF PRESENT: Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and Craig
D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.

A. WELCOME: Chair Froerer
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Shelly Halacy
C. INVOCATION: Commissioner Harvey
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: My thought this week goes back to my graduate school days.
One of the classes I remember well was a statistics class talking about trend lines. Back in ‘82/’83, we saw significant
snow with subsequent flooding problems. A professor at that time said the trend line was for continued flooding in the
future and money was spent on two pumps out at the Great Salt Lake. The pumps were used one year. I think we get
caught up in saying what we see today is going to continue in the future. Mother Nature is in charge and no one is in
charge of Mother Nature.
E. PUBLIC COMMENT:
   1. Jennifer Pennington, lives near Weber State Campus: Two things I want you to be aware of. I noticed the
      work sessions have been pulled from the YouTube channel. Could you repost all of your prior work sessions?
      The second thing is my husband is an engineer and has reviewed the artificial glacier evaporation system and
      based on his knowledge and experience it does not look like a viable situation for our county. I look forward
      to meeting with you in the future. And, oh, glorious day of spring!
   2. Doug Hansen, I farm in West Weber and Warren: The water issue is a concern for us. The Weber River runs
      into the bird refuge and I just learned the gates are open. We need to make sure the canals and dams gates on
      the other side of the bird refuge are open as well.
      Chair Froerer: We met last week with several agencies and the gates are open and debris has been removed.

G. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:

      they are!
   2. Chair Froerer: We actively involved with all emergency agencies in preparation for potential mitigation this
      county will take for flooding. Our roads department has spent countless hours in last seven days opening the
      Ogden Divide. There is an 18 foot snow wall there they have bene cutting through. I hope the members of
      the public are patient with us with the flooding and know we are very concerned and taking the appropriate

      will be patient as this is the highest the water has been in years potentially picking up new debris beyond what
