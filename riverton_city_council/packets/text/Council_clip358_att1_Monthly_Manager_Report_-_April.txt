                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                             April 2020
April was defined as the month which felt the full impact of policing during the COVID-19
pandemic. As expected, our calls for service were at a lower level than previous months.
Domestic violence and fraud calls remained consistent with our previous months, which would
be expected with more folks staying home from work and the added stress of the outbreak.
Reports of traditional property crimes such as vehicle burglaries, shoplifting, and residential
burglaries have reduced to the lowest levels since our inception.

Surprisingly, even our traffic accidents and traffic offenses have dramatically reduced even
though there are theoretically more cars in our community. I believe this is due to less through
traffic as a result of fewer people commuting from our surrounding communities.

We were able to make 7 arrests for DUI during the month even though we are making far fewer
pro-active traffic stops to keep our officers safe. This is a testament to the ability of our officers
to focus on the drivers who are most likely impaired and keeping our streets safe as a result.
