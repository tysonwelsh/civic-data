Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the
electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those
who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under a “Medium Level of
Transmission” alert stage for the COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does
not have sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing
to keep people safe from infection.

WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“Township”) recognizes it is at risk to a
wide range of natural, technological, and man-made hazards and such events require ongoing
emergency operations planning by all jurisdictions of government within the Township, county
and state;
WHEREAS, the Township Council (“Council”) finds that it is in the public interest to have
an emergency operations plan;
WHEREAS, Township officers, employees, or designees will execute the emergency
operations plan;
WHEREAS, Utah Code § 53-2a-807 requires the Township to adopt an emergency
operations plan, designate an emergency manager and three emergency interim successors and
the specific order of succession for each officer, and designate an emergency alert plan and
system;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP COUNCIL, UTAH, THAT:
SECTION I: The emergency operations plan attached as Exhibit 1 is hereby adopted as
the emergency operations plan for Emigration Canyon Metro Township, including its designation
of an alert plan, and alert system.
SECTION II: The Emergency Operations Manager and order of succession for Emigration
Metro Township is as follows:
1. Emergency Operations Manager – Mayor Joe Smolka;
2. Mayor Pro Tempore – Jennifer Hawkes;
3. Chief Law Enforcement Officer – Sheriff Rosie Rivera
SECTION III: Effective Date. This resolution shall take effect upon publication.
PASSED AND ADOPTED by Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council, Utah, this 27th
day of September in the year 2022.

ARPA Funds
Mayor Smolka stated he asked Public Works Engineering to gather information
on updating the FEMA maps. Three bids were received with the final cost being $84,800.

Franchise Tax
Council Member Brems stated the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service
Area (SLVLESA) Board has been discussing the possibility of the Metro Townships collecting a
franchise tax and allocating some of those funds to SLVLESA. If the Council adopts a franchise
tax, it is not obligated to allocate any of those funds to SLVLESA, the tax can be used for other
things. The amount that could be collected would be approximately $85,000.
Mayor Smolka stated the Council can consider using part of the funds for
SLVLESA and part of it for other projects in the canyon.
Council Member Harris asked who collects the money and where is it held.
Mayor Smolka stated the tax is collected by energy providers and kept by the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD).
Council Member Brems stated there is no timeline to approve the implementation
of the tax. He suggested drafting the ordinance, having a discussion, and setting the public
hearing for November.
