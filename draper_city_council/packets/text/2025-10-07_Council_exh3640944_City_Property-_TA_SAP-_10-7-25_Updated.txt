                                  Riverton City Sandra N. Lloyd Community Center
                    DRAPER CITY


                                  12830 S. Redwood Rd
CO MMUNI T Y DEVELO PMENT




                                    • Size of facility and Parking
                                           •   The facility is 15,809 sq ft.
                                           •   1930’s gym, auditorium, cafeteria for the old Riverton school.
                                           •   Parking lot: shares parking lot with the adjacent City Hall, park, and senior center.
                                           •   Property size is 5.75 acres, including the park and City Hall.
                                    • Uses:
                                           •   Indoor event space in auditorium, and three meeting rooms
                                           •   City Hall Plaza is a 1,479 sq ft plaza.
                                           •   Adjacent to City park with playground.
                                           •   Auditorium has capacity to seat 327 or banquet dinning for 120, Meeting room capacity ranges
                                               from 40 people to 70 people.
                                    • Rental Costs:
                                           •   Auditorium is $600-$1,200 per day or $80-$160 hourly, Meeting rooms are $45-$90 hourly, plaza
                                               is $600-$1,200 per day or $160-$320 hourly (resident -non-resident)
                                    • Cost and payment for facility?
                                           •   The total cost was $2.6 million, with $1.7 million going towards the plaza, and just under
                                               $900,000 to the community center renovation. This included a TRCC (Tourism, Recreation,
                                               Cultural, and Convention Facilities) grant from Salt Lake County of $1.2 million, plus City public
                                               works doing some of the work.
