                        RIVERTON REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                             RDA RESOLUTION NO. 25-03

     A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
          ADOPTING A FINAL BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2025-2026

      WHEREAS, State law requires the filing of a final budget for each Redevelopment
Agency located within the State of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the tentative budget complies with the requirements set out in U.C.A. § 10-
6-118; and

       WHEREAS, the budget has been reviewed, considered, and tentatively adopted by the
governing body.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
OF RIVERTON CITY, STATE OF UTAH, as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Redevelopment Agency adopts the Final Budget for the
          Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2025 and ending June 30, 2026, as attached
          hereto.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
Agency this 17th day of June 2025 by the following vote:
