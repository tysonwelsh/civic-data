BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 1.89
acres located on the west side of Alan Point Dr., at approximately 849 W Alan Point Dr.
(Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Plat
Amendment be approved to vacate a wetlands no build area in the rear yard.

The house on the property was built in the year 2000 and it part of the Alan Point
Subdivision. The Alan Point Subdivision is a 22 lot subdivision recorded in 1998 with lot
sizes around one half acre to over an acre in size. The recorded plat includes on lots 11-19
a large wetland area crossing all of the rear yards and is recorded as a “wetlands and no
building zone”.

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately
two dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little
impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the
City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these
zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by
commercial and industrial uses.” The RA2 zoning designation surrounds the subject
property.

        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no Public Street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.”

               The review area is comprised entirely of dry land (i.e., there are no waters or water features,
               including wetlands, of any kind in the entire review area). Rationale: The property encompasses
               approximately 1.89 acres with 0.71 acre of vacant land. The U.S. Geological Service (USGS)
               topographic map shows a blue line on the west side of the property. This blue line is the
               Jordan River which is located approximately 400-feet to the west of the west boundary line. A
               second blue line can be observed to the east of the property. This 2nd blue line is a stream that
               was piped between 1997 and 2002, possibly as a result of the construction of the housing
               development on the east side of Alan Point Drive, across the street from the subject property.
               No records for piping of the stream were found from an ORM search. The U.S. Fish and Wildlife
               Service (USFWS) National Wetland Inventory (NWI) mapping shows no indications of wetlands
               within the property. The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Natural Resource Conservation
               Service Soil Survey shows hydric soils (Chipman Series) within most of the property with no
               hydrologic soils (Magna Series and Hillfield-Taylorsville Series) on the northeast and southwest
               ends of the property. A review of Digital Globe aerials and Google Earth aerials focused on the
               winter through early summer months with no hydrology signatures identified with the review
               area. This office verified No Permit Required (NPR) projects SPK-2020-00169 on
               February 26, 2020, and SPK-2021-00069 on March 12, 2021, two and three houses north of
               the subject site. A site visit for SPK-2020-00169 and a review of the site conducted virtually
               using remote tools for SPK-2021-00069 for the NPR reviews resulted in the identification of no
               aquatic resources in the area. LIDAR data retrieved on March 9, 2021, revealed that the
               elevations along the subject site and the neighboring properties are consistent.

               The property owner provided a report dated February 2021 with photos of the site. Based on
               the information from the photos, active construction is taking place around the property.
               Although the subject area is at a lower elevation than the adjacent properties due to
               construction, the photos did not reveal the presence of potential aquatic features and clearly
               illustrate the lack of wetland indicators. Based on a review of the information provided by the
               property owner, the USGS topographic maps, USFWS NWI wetland mapper, USDA soils maps,
