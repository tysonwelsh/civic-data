                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RDA RESOLUTION NO. 21-01

      A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                ADOPTING TENTATIVE BUDGETS FOR THE
                         2021-2022 FISCAL YEAR

        WHEREAS, State law requires the filing of a tentative budget for each municipality located
within the State of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the tentative budget complies with the requirements set out in U.C.A. § 10-6-
111; and

       WHEREAS, the budget shall be reviewed, considered, and tentatively adopted by the
governing body and may be amended or revised in such manner as is considered advisable prior to
public hearings.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton Redevelopment Agency, State
of Utah, as follows:

       1. That Riverton City Redevelopment Agency tentatively adopts their Tentative
          Budgets for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2021 and ending June 30, 2022.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
Agency this 4th day of May, 2021 by the following vote:
