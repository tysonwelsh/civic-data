                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       ORDINANCE NO. 24-16

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING AND CLARIFYING RIVERTON CITY ORDINANCE NO. 21-
22, VACATING AN EXISTING UNDEVELOPED PORTION OF A PUBLIC RIGHT OF WAY,
                  BRINLEY PEAK LANE, IN RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton, Utah, has the authority, pursuant to Utah Code 10-
9a-609-3, to vacate a subdivision or portion of a subdivision within Riverton City; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has complied with the public notice requirements of Utah Code
sections 10-9a-208; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council on August 17, 2021 held a public hearing to consider the
proposed vacation of an existing undeveloped portion of right-of-way in the Heritage Farms Phase 5
Subdivision at approximately 3351 West Brinley Peak Lane, as shown on Exhibit “A”, and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council determined that there was good cause to vacate the
portion of the subdivision dedicated to Riverton City, as described herein, and that said vacation would
not be detrimental to the public interest, and therefore adopted Ordinance 21-22, as shown on Exhibit
“B”, vacating the roadway as described.

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Governing Body of Riverton City that:

               1.     In clarification of the previously adopted Ordinance 21-22, it was and remains the
                      intent of Riverton City that the portion of Brinley Peak Lane vacated by that
                      action be deeded in its entirety to the owner of Lot 522 of the Heritage Farms Ph
                      5 Subdivision, immediately adjacent to the south.

               2.     This ordinance shall take effect upon execution.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 3rd day of
September, 2024 by the following vote:
