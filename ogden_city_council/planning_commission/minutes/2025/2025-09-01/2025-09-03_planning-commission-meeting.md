3. Nonconforming Use Expansion, Auto recycling, 770 W 17" St
Planner Alyssa Girardo stated the petitioner was requesting to tear down an existing building
and rearrange the site, adding storage and more parking to the side. She said the changes will
fit in with the area and actually make it more conforming. Staff recommended approval with
some conditions including meeting ADA regulations and providing a solid fence.
Petitioner Richard Geertsen agreed the changes would be an improvement as they plan to clean
up the auto dismantling and recycling salvage yard site.

COMISSIONER SANDAU MOVED TO APPROVE THE NONCONFORMING USE
EXPANSION SUBJECT TO STAFFS RECOMMENDED CONDITIONS BASED ON FINDING THE
APPLICABLE CRITERIA ARE MET. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER SHALE AND
PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE — COMMISSIONERS
AABERG, ROSS, SANDAU, SHALE, AKHMEDOV, AND CHAIR SHINODA. VOTING NO — NONE.

4. Preliminary Subdivision, Porter Seven Subdivision, 566 24" St
Petitioner Chaz Schlange addressed the plan to build small, individual cottage homes and add a
sidewalk.
Planner Jennifer Patrick stated that staff recommended approval of the cottage overlay with
conditions such as dedicating 8.88 feet of property for a sidewalk and meeting zoning
requirements.
Local residents Jeremy Peterson and Kevin Schneider were concerned about on-street parking
and adding a sidewalk.
Planning Director Barton Brierley and Principal Engineer Dalton Richins addressed the fact that
the city prefers not to install dead end sidewalks but where the property abuts that of the
Lutheran Church, and the church would have to dedicate the right of way to extend the sidewalk
on to their property, they would rather install what sidewalk they can as to enhance pedestrian
safety.
