MEMO
To: City Council
From: Mike Barker, City Manager
Date: 2025-12-16
Re:

Approve Resolution #25-75



Comments:

This proposed boundary adjustment with Highland City affects two slivers of property --
Utah County parcels 11:017:0157 and 35:544:0050. Highland City owns parcel
11:017:0157, which is located in Draper City. Alpine Joint Venture LTD owns parcel
35:544:0050, which is located in Highland City. Alpine Joint Ventures, LTD also own
property adjacent to parcel 35:544:0050. The proposed boundary adjustment would
place the Highland-owned parcel in Highland City and the Alpine Joint Ventures, LTD
parcel in Draper City boundaries adjacent to other property Alpine Joint Ventures, LTD
owns in Draper City.
