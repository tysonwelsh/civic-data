Approval of Minutes
         Board member Blair stated she had reviewed the minutes of the regular meeting of February 13, 2024, and found them to
be accurate to the best of her recollection.
         BOARD MEMBER BLAIR THEN MOVED TO APPROVE THE MINUTES AS PRESENTED. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER HYER, ALL VOTING AYE.

Presentation of the Redevelopment Agency Annual Report for Fiscal Year
2022-2023
         The Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Fiscal Year (FY) 2022-2023 Annual Report came before the Council for
consideration.
         Community and Economic Development Interim Director Johnson use the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to discuss
rules and policies governing the RDA; development issues addressed by RDAs; tax increment generation and use; the number of
current and proposed project areas in the Ogden RDA; major Agency activities; and project area profiles for various RDA project
areas. For a copy of the PowerPoint presentation in its entirety see the information packet.
          BOARD MEMBER HYER MOVED TO ACCEPT THE REPORT FOR REVIEW. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.

Executive Director Comments
        Executive Director Nadolski thanked Mr. Johnson for providing an overview of the Redevelopment Agency Annual
Report.

Board member Comments
        Vice Chair White also thanked Mr. Johnson for his report and for taking the recommendations of the Board under
advisement relative to activities and projects being pursued by Agency staff.


     There being no further business to come before the Agency, BOARD MEMBER HYER MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 7:35 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER MYERS, ALL VOTING AYE.
