                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                          September 2020
September was highlighted by more widespread virus outbreak in our community as our schools
were impacted and some of our city employees tested positive for the virus. This has prompted a
renewed focus on our safety protocols and procedures to mitigate the potential spread of the virus
throughout our ranks. At the time of this writing, we have not experienced a positive test amongst
our members and we are keeping our mitigation strategies in place for the foreseeable future.

We are still experiencing an above average number of vehicle burglaries in the city and we are
focusing our patrol efforts toward those areas where vulnerable vehicles are located. We are also
continuing our outreach efforts to educate our citizens to lock their vehicles and not keep valuable
items in their vehicles.

Additionally, we conducted more speed enforcement operations during the month in response to
several complaints from citizens about speeding in their neighborhoods. This resulted in a
significantly higher number of issued citations as our officers spent time monitoring the speed of
vehicles on our roadways and found many vehicles traveling in excess of the posted speed limit.

We also conducted our annual firearms training and our members spent an entire day on the range
qualifying and familiarizing themselves with all of our weapons systems.
