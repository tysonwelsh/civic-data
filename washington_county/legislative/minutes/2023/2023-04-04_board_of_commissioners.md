CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3105, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
TTD OT A FENN APTRUVING
THE COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS RECOMMENDATIONS

County Engineer Todd Edwards the Council of Governments (COG) serves as a recommending
body to Washington County and oversees the expenditures of local transportation corridor
preservation funds. There are three (3) projects being recommended for funding which are: The
approval of a Hurricane City funding request of $850,000 from the Preservation Fund for roadway
right-of-way acquisition for the 1100 West corridor. The second request is for the approval of a
Washington City funding request of $675,000 from the Construction Grant Fund to acquire SITLA
property for the Washington Parkway (Northern Corridor) alignment located in the Red Cliffs
Desert Reserve. The third request is the approval of a Dixie Metropolitan Planning Organization
(Dixie MPO) of $200,000 from the Construction Grant Fund to pay the 20% match required to
obtain a $1,000,000 grant.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3105, A
Resolution Approving the Council of Governments Recommendations.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2023-3109, A
RESOLUTION APPROVING RAVEN MANAGEMENT AS RECOMMENDED BY THE

HCAC

Mr. Rognan explained Washington County has many ravens in the County that eat desert tortoises,
specifically juvenile tortoises. Fish and Wildlife Services will prepare a study to find the best
course of action on how to implement the raven depredation control permit.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3109, A
Resolution Approving Raven Management as Recommended by the HCAC,
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

RESOLUTION TO APPROVE A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING THAT

CONNECTION WITH THE BLM’S DEVELOPMENT OF THE 368 ENERGY
CORRIDOR ___ RESOURCE _§ MANAGEMENT PLAN AMENDMENT _ AND
ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STATEMENT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained the Section 368 Energy Corridor is a federal
management plan that provides for various energy corridors along the western United States. This
is amemorandum of understanding to establish a cooperating agency status for the county as part
of the environmental reviews associated with proposed amendments to the Section 368 Energy
Corridors.
