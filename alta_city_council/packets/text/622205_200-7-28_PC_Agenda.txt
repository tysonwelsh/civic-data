  DETERMINATION REGARDING CONDUCTING TOWN OF ALTA
   PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING WITHOUT AN ANCHOR
                     LOCATION

The Planning Commission Chair hereby determines that conducting a meeting with an
anchor location presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be
present at the anchor location pursuant to Utah Code section 52-4-207(4) and Resolution
2020-R-12. The Chair relies upon the facts put forth by the Mayor in his July 7, 2020
Determination.

Attendees can watch the meeting on YouTube via alink that will be posted on the Town’s
                         website at the start of the meeting here:
