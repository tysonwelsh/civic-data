PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
No one from the public present to speak. 
 
Commissioner Alder motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Winkler seconded that motion. 
  
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To recommend the Copperton Metro Township General Plan to the Copperton Metro 
Township Council for approval with one amendment to remove the incorrect R-1-6 label from the 
Zoning Map. 
Motion by: Commissioner Alder 
2nd by: Commissioner Taylor 
Vote: Commissioner Breinholt abstained, all other commissioners voted in favor. Motion 
passed. 
 
Ms. Jordan said she has been working on getting the Copperton website page with information and surveys, 
planning events and the general plan and have it linked to the Metro Township.
