Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner
Jackson.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Richard Thurman’s request for review of the
Thurman Zone Change on 1.54-acres located at 805 and 913 East Cedar Hollow Road,
changing the zoning from RA-1 residential/agriculture) to R-1-Flex (residential).
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant was not present but gave prior notice to the Commissioners for his absence.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Richard Thurman’s
        request for review of the Thurman Zone Change on 1.54-acres located at 805 and
        913 East Cedar Hollow Road, changing the zoning from RA-1
        residential/agriculture) to R-1-Flex (residential); including all DRC comments and
        findings stated in the report. Commissioner Newall seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Britton, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of John Johnston’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of the Johnston Subdivision, a 3-lot residential subdivision located at 2301
North 600 West.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, John Johnston, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of John
        Johnston’s request for preliminary subdivision review of the Johnston Subdivision,
        a 3-lot residential subdivision located at 2301 North 600 West; including all DRC
        comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Britton, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code Amendment to Chapter 23 Signs, changing the approval process for civic institutional
signs.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Nielsen opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of Lehi City’s
        request for review of a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 23 Signs,
        changing the approval process for civic institutional signs; including findings stated in
        the report. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Britton, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
