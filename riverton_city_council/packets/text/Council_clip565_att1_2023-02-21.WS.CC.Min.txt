 6            Ordinance No. 23-05 – Amending Section 18.235, Water Efficiency Standards,
 7            Updating and Amending Existing Language, Amendments Proposed by Riverton
 8            City
 9
10   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained the Riverton City Council has
11   previously adopted water efficient landscaping standards for development within the City. The
12   standards are based on similar standards put forward by the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy
13   District and others. The standards as currently adopted recommend methods of reducing water
14   use within landscaped areas, both residential and commercial. This can be accomplished both
15   by reducing or eliminating more water intensive landscape materials, and also by reducing the
16   overall landscaped area.
17
18   Mr. Lethbridge said this proposed amendment updates the standards for commercial and multi-
19   family development, and for front and side yard landscaping in new construction for single-
20   family residential development, from “recommended and encouraged” to required as part of the
21   site review process for such projects. This is consistent with development practices on recent
22   projects. The standards are based on both requirements of the Utah State Code and
23   recommendations from the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District. The requirements do
24   include a limitation on the total percentage of landscaping that be lawn (sod) in new residential
25   construction front and side yards, and a prohibition on sod/turf in park strips in new construction.
26
27   Mayor Staggs called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public Hearing.
28
