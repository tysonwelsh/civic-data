A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF SALT LAKE 
COUNTY APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING EXECUTION OF AN

COUNTY AND UTAH STATE UNIVERSITY FOR A CONTRIBUTION 
OF TRCC FUNDS TO HELP FUND USU BASTIAN AGRICULTURAL 
CENTER BASTIAN NATURAL RESOURCES ADVENTURE TRAIL
RECITALS
A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) and Utah State University (the 
“USU”) are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation 
Act, Utah Code Ann. §§ 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Act”), and, as such, are 
authorized by the Act to enter into this Agreement to act jointly and 
cooperatively on the basis of mutual advantage in order to provide facilities 
in a manner that will accord best with geographic, economic, population and 
other factors influencing the needs and development of local communities. 
B. The County receives funds (“TRCC Funds ”) pursuant to the Tourism, 
Recreation, Cultural, Convention, and Airport Facilities Tax Act, Utah Code 
Ann. §§ 59-12-601 et seq. (the “TRCC Act ”).  The TRCC Act provides that 
TRCC Funds may be used, among other things, for the development, 
operation, and maintenance of publicly owned or operated recreation,

cultural, or convention facilities.
C. USU requested TRCC Funds from the County to help it fund the 
project described in its TRCC Application.  More specifically, the USU 
requested TRCC Funds to help fund construction of its USU Bastian 
Agricultural Center Bastian Natural Resources Adventure Trail (the 
“Project”).  The County Council appropriated TRCC Funds for this 
purpose.  
D. USU and County now desire to enter into the Interlocal Cooperation 
Agreement attached hereto as ATTACHMENT A (the “Interlocal 
Agreement”) wherein the County agrees to grant TRCC Funds to the USU to

23-0819

Presenter: Councilmembers Dave Alvord, Aimee Winder Newton, Arlyn 
Bradshaw, Sheldon Stewart, Dea Theodore, Laurie Stringham, Suzanne

Major Brent R. Taylor Battalion - United States Naval Sea Cadets 
Corp opened the celebration of American and Founders and Constitution 
Month presenting the colors and leading the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag 
of the United States of America.
Ms. Ella Dickson sang the National Anthem.
Mr. Carlos Moreno gave an inspirational thought and invocation.
Council Member Alvord  read the following proclamation declaring the 
month of September as American Founders and Constitution Month. 
Proclamation by The Salt Lake County Council to recognize September as 
American Founders and Constitution Month
 
Whereas, the Utah State Legislature passed HB 179, which declares that the 
month of September shall be commemorated annually as American Founders 
and Constitution Month; and 
Whereas, HB 179 encourages “all civic, fraternal, and religious organizations, 
and public and private institutions, to recognize and observe this occasion 
through appropriate programs, teaching, meetings, services, or celebrations 
in which state, county, and local governmental officials are invited to 
participate;” and
Page 12 of 15
County Council Meeting Minutes August 29, 2023
Whereas, by joint resolution approved December 8, 2004 (36 U.S.C. 106), 
the Congress of the United States has designated the 17th day of September 
of each year as Constitution Day and Citizenship Day in commemoration of 
the signing on September 17, 1787, of the Constitution of the United States 
and in recognition of all who, by coming of age or by naturalization, enjoy 
the privileges and assume the responsibilities of citizenship; and
Whereas, America is founded on moral principles, including the sovereignty 
of the people and the belief that all people are created equal and have 
unalienable rights bestowed upon them by their Creator; and
Whereas, the American Constitution, which guarantees priceless freedoms to 
us as citizens of the United States and to our posterity, and sets forth a 
concept of liberty that has contributed to the advancement of human rights 
globally; and
Whereas, it is essential that the rising generation, citizens of all ages, and 
candidates for citizenship, understand our history and form of government 
so it can be honored and perpetuated; and
Whereas, we laud the desire of anyone to fulfill the dream of becoming a 
citizen of the United States of America and welcome all who naturalize into 
our community to join us in writing the next great chapter of the American 
story; and
Whereas, we acknowledge and express gratitude to God and the Founding 
Fathers of this nation for establishing the Constitution and all liberties and 
freedoms derived therefrom 
Now, therefore, we, the Salt Lake County Council and Mayor Jenny Wilson, 
do hereby recognize and celebrate the month of September, 2023, as 
American Founders and Constitution Month.
A motion was made by Council Member Alvord, seconded by Council Member 
Harrison, to adopt the proclamation.  The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  announced that Welcoming Week, a nationwide celebration of 
new immigrants and refugees to the country, will be held September 8-17, 2023, and it will 
begin with the swearing in of 75-100 new citizens on September 8th.  That celebration 
culminates with Constitution Day and Citizenship Day on September 17, 2023.  This is

appropriate as it prompts people to reflect on the promise of America to all who seek it .  
She also recognized Joseph Genda, New American Refugee Liaison, and relayed his 
journey to come to America to find liberty.
Congressman Burgess Owens  read about the Preamble of the Constitution of the 
United States, and reflected on this Country ’s great foundation; the founders’ wisdom 
and foresight encapsulated in the Constitution; the governance of the Constitution, which 
remains a guiding light for generations to come; the founders’ vision of America as a land 
of freedom and liberty; and the need to strive to be a more perfect union, and honor the 
founders by accomplishing the goals and values they set out and preserving those liberties 
they fought so hard to secure.  
Congressman Chris Stewart  delivered an inspirational speech acknowledging Mr . 
Moreno and other immigrants’ sacrifices to come to America, and the profound 
opportunity he had to witness the swearing in of 30-40 new Americans and see from their 
faces what it meant to them.  He shared the results of a poll demonstrating 40 percent of 
young Americans ages 14-24 were not proud of anything in this nation ’s history, stating 
the reasons for that sentiment need to be corrected and celebrations like this were a 
reminder of that.  He also relayed Nathan Hale ’s experience as an American Patriot and 
spy for the United States at the age of 21, and his demise when caught, explaining that the 
familiar phrase Nathan Hale said when marching to the gallows “I regret that I have but 
one life to give to my country,” was not all of the quote.  According to British soldiers, he 
also said, “If I had 10,000 lives to give, I would give them all to my wounded and 
bleeding nation .”  The purpose of the story is to remind everyone that the strife happening 
in America today, is not the first time this nation has been a wounded and bleeding nation .  
This nation will get through the challenges because the Constitution was inspired by God, 
and it will protect the Country.
Council Member Winder Newton  led a round of applause for Congressman Stewart 
and his service to this country, stating he only has about two weeks left in office.
