                       NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                January 28, 2025
                                    7:00 P.M
                                   21 E 100 N
                                 Nephi, UT 84648


1. Budget Requests Presentations:

        a. Nephi City Police Department -Chief Michael H. Morgan
            Chief Morgan reviewed the mission statement of the police department and
            outlined the following budget requests:
                i.  One new patrol officer $130,000 annually
               ii.  Mental Health Counseling Services $5600 annually
              iii.  K-9 Officer Compensation $3600-$6,000 annually
             iv.    Master Officer Compensation Plan $36,763 annually
               v.   7.5% wage increase for all Police Department employees $160,379
                    annually
             vi.    Compensatory Time Payout $50,000 annually
             vii.   Body/Vehicle Cameras $30,000 annually
            viii.   New Server $2460
             ix.    Technology upgrades $20,000
               x.   Fleet Modernization an additional $40,713 annually
   Councilor Parady asked Chief Morgan if he were to prioritize his requests what would his
   priorities be. Chief Morgan answered he had prioritized them in the order they were
   listed.

      b. Water/Sewer Department – Superintendent Justin Blackett
         Water/Sewer Department Superintendent Justin Blackett reported to the council
         that he has a very good crew that is working hard and helping other departments
         as needed. He stated that he has plans to send out a request for qualifications
         for needed sewer upgrades. The council had a short discussion on possible
         sewer upgrade solutions to accommodate the growth the city is experiencing.

      c.    Public Works – Director Kyle Marchant
           Director Kyle Marchant outlined only one funded budget request:
              i.   Wide Format Touchscreen Workstation $14,000
           The workstation would be used by the Development Review Committee to be
           able to see large electronic plans and to make noted revisions to the plans. The
           budget request would be funded out of the Capital Projects Fund.

      d.    Electric Department – Superintendent Rust Finlinson
           Superintendent Finlinson reported to the council that the latest hire to the
           department, Jordan Johnson, was working out very well. Mr. Finlinson presented
     a slide show of pictures of projects that his department is working on and
     reported on some hot spots they had found using the infrared camera the
     department had purchased a few years ago. He had the following budget
     request:
        i.    One additional full-time employee at the apprentice level

e.   Streets Department – Superintendent Ed Park
     Superintendent Ed Park outlined the following budget requests:
       i.   Backhoe $150,000
      ii.   Continue saving for a new water truck and garbage truck

f.   Treasurer/Finance/HR/Justice Court Department- Lisa Brough and Gale
     Bryan
     Finance Director Lisa Brough, Treasurer Gale Bryan, and Office Specialist Nicole
     Douglas presented a slide show outlining their budget requests and information
     related to their departments.
         i.  Copier- $5,000 funded through the Capital Projects Fund line-item City
             Hall Projects
        ii.  2025 Municipal Elections $25,792 if contracted with Juab County to
             administer the election.
       iii.  Finance Director Transition Costs $32,000 funded through the Capital
             Projects Fund line-item Personnel Contingency
       iv.   Docuware software upgrade $6,670 funded through the Capital Projects
             Fund line-item City Hall Projects
     Mr. Brough reviewed the Justice Court revenues and expenses from the past six
     years and indicated that the revenue dropped off significantly since after the
     COVID year of 2020. The new contract with the county seemed to be working
     better for the city than the previous contract but the Justice Court is still losing
     money. She also showed a slide showing how many citations had gone through
     the Justice Court in the past seven months. Treasurer Gale Bryan presented two
     slides one showing the amount the city has invested in Moreton Investments and
     in the PTIF and the interest rate the investments are earning. She also showed a
     slide with that showed only $5,836 had been written-off in bad debts for FY24
     and FY25.

g. City Administration – City Administrator Seth Atkinson
   City Administrator Seth Atkinson presented the following two budget requests:
      i.   Airport Manager $65,000 annually
     ii.   ICMA Conference FY 2026 $2500
   Mr. Atkinson said that the city could possibly join with Ephraim/Manti airport to
   share in the services and costs of an airport manager. Mr. Atkinson also stated
   that the ICMA Conference could be funded from carryover amounts from his
   operating budget. He said that he feels the need to attend the conference
   because he is currently in the Utah City Management Association leadership.
2. Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the Children’s Justice Center (CJC) had recently
   received sufficient funds, through generous donations and grants, to construct a new
   facility in Nephi City. They have secured land and would like to build the facility (which
   will look like a typical single family residential home) on the south end of the city. The
   CJC board is asking the city to assist in their endeavor by waiving impact and utility
   connection fees. The council discussed the request and agreed they were in favor of
   helping in some way. Mr. Atkinson said that he would work out an agreement to make
   sure that if CJC ever sold the home that the new owner would pay the city for the waived
   fees at time of purchase.

3.    Mr. Atkinson updated the council about a large development project that would impact
     the city’s gas system. The developers would need to do an audit on the portion of the
     gas system that would be affected by the project. The estimated cost of the audit is
     $87,000 that the developer would be required to pay. The developer would also pay for
     any system upgrades needed and the city would charge the company a tariff for the gas
     to transmit through the city’s gas system.

     Councilor Shari Cowan entered the meeting at 8:50 p.m.

4. Administrative and City Council Updates:
     a. Mr. Atkinson encouraged the council to review the financial statements.
     b. Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about completing the conflict-of-interest forms.
     c. Mayor Seely said he had received another complaint about the air traffic noise.
     d. Mayor Seely informed the council that the state will be doing a homeless count
         on January 30, 2025. He said that it is suggested that the city provide a Code
         Blue shelter for any homeless individuals. Mr. Atkinson said that in his previous
         job they had opened City Hall foyer area for a Code Blue shelter.
     e. Councilor Shari Cowan informed the council that the Miss Nephi Pageant hasd
         been scheduled for March 15, 2025, and that the parent meeting was scheduled
         for February 3, 2025 at 6:30 p.m.

5.   Mayor Seely opened a regular meeting.

6.    Councilor Skip Worwood moved to convene into closed session to discuss the character
     and competence of an individual and real property Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the
     motion. The motion passed unanimously.

7. Meeting adjourned at 9:02 p.m.
