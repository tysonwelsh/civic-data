2. WORK SESSION
2.1 Mayor's Budget Presentation 23-1004
Attachments: Staff Report
Presenter: Jennifer Wilson, Mayor of Salt Lake County. Darrin Casper, Deputy 
Mayor of Finance & Administration. (Approx 90 Min)
Informational
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  delivered the 2024 budget presentation.  The 2024 
budget is balanced, and despite it being a challenging budget this year, she 
was committed to continuing to provide outstanding services to residents 
and supporting the staff who provide those services.   With a slowdown in 
tax revenues this year due to declining revenue growth of less than 2 percent, 
and the downward bias of property tax from inflation and the high cost of 
goods, in addition to a 10 percent increase in the cost of health care, she was 
recommending the following strategies to avoid layoffs or program cuts:  1) 
implement a hiring freeze and eliminate vacant full -time positions that have 
remained unfilled for six months or more, excluding positions currently in the 
hiring process; 2) restrict personnel expenditures by 3 percent; 3) place a 2 
percent restriction on spending in program budgets; 4) cut countywide travel 
expenses in half, resulting in savings of approximately $ 1 million; and 5) deny 
90 percent of general fund requests.  Her recommended budget included 
funding for the 2024 Presidential election, as well as new proposals made 
possible through dedicated funding streams, i .e., opening recreation centers 
to all kids in Salt Lake County free of charge, using Tourism, Recreation, 
Cultural and Convention (TRCC) Fund revenues, and eliminating library late 
fees for kids.  Her recommendation also included a 2.75 percent salary 
increase for general employees, additional pay to sworn officers, one -time
