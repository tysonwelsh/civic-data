G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVAL OF THE WEBER COUNTY POLICY GOVERNING CAPITAL BUDGETS - POLICY 6.2.
         Scott Parke, County Comptroller, stated that the Clerk/Auditor’s Office has brought several policies before the
         Commission to help improve them and follow best practices. This policy will help the Clerk/Auditor establish
         a more formal process for providing recommendations to the Commission during the budget cycle. Any large
         project that the county is interested in will be vetted. The policy creates a committee comprised of various
         county offices and defines the scope. Mr. Parke addressed Commissioner Ebert’s questions noting that the
         county is always looking to improve its processes. Commissioner Ebert said that projects now have to go
         through a very vetted process before going on the prioritization list and the county has worked on this for 18
         months. John Bond, Treasurer, said that the committee has a great group of individuals with expertise.
         Commissioner Gibson expressed gratitude to Mr. Parke for the new accounting software, it has been a very
         positive step, and he appreciates bringing this group of financial experts together and the long term perspective.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve Weber County Policy 6.2; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
