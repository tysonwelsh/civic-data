                      Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                       February, 2022




I wanted to lead my report with the above photograph of a ceremony which was recently held at
the department. South Jordan Police Chief Jeff Carr presented six members of our SWAT Team
with one of South Jordan’s highest awards, in recognition of their professionalism and dedication
in the face of imminent threat of deadly force during a standoff with a violent and unstable
suspect who was in possession of explosives and numerous firearms. Although this incident
occurred in 2020 and this recognition was delayed due to COVID, it gives me the opportunity to
recognize our SWAT Team and spotlight our South Valley SWAT Team. The South Valley

      SWAT Team is comprised of members from the Riverton Police Department, Herriman Police
      Department, and South Jordan Police Department. The team was created when we became a
      police department in 2019 and has quickly become one of the elite SWAT Teams in the State of
      Utah. In a competition held last summer which tests skills and capabilities of SWAT Teams
      from all around the Western United States, the South Valley SWAT Team placed in the top 3
      and has quickly developed a reputation as one of the most capable and reliable teams in the state.
      Surrounding jurisdictions routinely call upon our team to assist when situations become too large
      or complex, as recently occurred with a tragic situation in the city of Millcreek, when our team
      spent hundreds of man hours helping to resolve that incident.

      The citizens of Riverton can be confident that the SWAT Team which serves them will be able
      to handle any situation which may arise with true professionalism and a commitment to saving
      lives.

      The statistics for the month of January did not reveal any unexpected numbers. I was pleased to
      see the number of vehicle burglaries reduced significantly from the previous month. We will
      continue to focus attention on educating our citizenry and patrolling our neighborhoods to keep
      these types of crimes at a minimum. Additionally, we renewed our speed suppression efforts in
      our community to address complaints from citizens and these efforts resulted in a significant
      increase in citations and warnings issued, including an increase in DUI arrests.
