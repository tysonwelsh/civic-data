Mayor Smolka read the following statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-202:
Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration Canyon Metro
Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the electronic meetings of the
Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the anchor
location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under an “Yellow” alert stage for the
COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does not have
sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing to keep people
safe from infection.
