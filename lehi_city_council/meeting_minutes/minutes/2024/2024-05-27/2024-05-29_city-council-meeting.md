6:00 PM Welcome
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
    Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone to the Joint Work Session and acknowledged the
    presence of all the cities represented.

2. North Utah County Transportation Presentation and Discussion
   Mayor Mark Johnson noted the planned transportation routes and various options that Lehi
   City had for future transportation. He discussed population and traffic numbers that would
   substantially impact the surrounding area.

   Council members of the cities expressed their willingness to collaborate to understand the
   facts and work toward good solutions. Council members discussed the concerns and benefits
   of each city and how they would vary for each one.

   Saratoga City Manager Mark Christensen presented data gathered on different bus routes and
   transit corridors for the area.

   UTA Commissioner Jeff Acerson shared his perspective on public transportation and
   emphasized the need for it.

3. Discussion
   Council members discussed dates for future joint meetings.
