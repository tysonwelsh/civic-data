o       DISCUSS MAIN STREET ART STUDY
Nate Rockwood said Main Street neighborhood is an area to be redeveloped and
revitalized. ln conjunction with the CDA and to further support the goal of redeveloping
Main Street, the City adopted the Main Street Small Area Plan and the Form Based Code
on December4th,2018, and March 3rd,2020, respectively.

Through analyzing case studies and other nearby areas with similar development
impediments, Staff believes adding more arts and culture-based developments and
programs will aide in the redevelopment through placemaking. This will not just make the
area more economically successful but will restore Main Street to its former status as a
center of activity for residents and for individuals throughout the county.

As part of the preparation work for future arts and culture-based developments and
programing for the Main Street CRA, the Midvale Redevelopment Agency
commissioned a demographic study evaluating the potential of Main Street as an Arts,
Food and Cultural District.

       Drafting - Station Area Plan Requirements
           -  Representative Waldrip is preparing a bill thatwould require the adoption of
              station area plans for all fixed guideway transit stations (e.9., light rail, front
              runner, bus rapid transit, etc.). Rep. Waldrip's goal is to encourage housing
              development. While still in its formative stage, the bill would require cities,
              UTA, and MPOs to work together in developing the plan. lt will also be tied
              into the current housing plan and reporting framework developed in SB34
              (2019). Additionally, Rep. Waldrip wants the bill to include principals that
              would need to be included in each station area plan. These may include
              affordable housing, density, sustainability, transit support, etc. At this stage
              it is unclear what the impact of the bill will be, but it could be significant.
