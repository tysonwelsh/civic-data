NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—March 12th @ 7pm.

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          None

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood spoke about the City’s partnership with the local non-profit
          organization, Circles Salt Lake, and the work that they do.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          City Attorney, Josh Collins, informed the Council that he and the City’s top staff have
          been keeping track of any legislative updates that could potentially affect the City
          and would share an update once the legislative session is over.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Williams shared some comments about the importance of
          investing time with the senior community in the City.

              Council Chair Bynum shared some highlights about attending the Lionheart Gift
              Foundation’s Winter Gala.

       6. INFORMATION.
             a. Communication & Flash Vote Survey Results
                Neighborhoods Deputy Director, Julie Taylor, and Communications
                Manager, Joseph Dane, presented an overview of the City’s communications
                team and their responsibilities that include city communications, resident
                engagement, and department support.

                     Ms. Taylor also shared an overview of the City’s demographics, the primary
                     channels of communications, and other channels that are used on a less
                     frequent basis.

                     Mr. Dane then went over the results of the most recent FlashVote survey
                     that was conducted throughout December 2024. They received a total of
                     215 respondents who answered the following questions:
                         i. Rate how we’re doing on communicating news and decisions

                        j. What channels do you use to get City news?
                        k. How would you prefer to receive City news?
                        l. What topics are you most interested in learning more about?

                    Mr. Dane reviewed the key findings, takeaways, and how they will work to
                    better reach the City’s unique communities.

                    There is a new ‘Community Values’ survey and they encouraged everyone to
                    participate and share the link on their social media accounts.

                    They briefly went over the FY26 plan and the needs of their division that will
                    allow them to employ additional communication tools to reach a wider
                    audience, get graphic design support to help all City departments with
                    communication outputs, and invest in targeting and boosting messages.

Unfinished Business
    1. A Resolution of the South Salt Lake City Council to Participate in the Municipal
        Alternate Voting Methods Pilot Project (Ranked Choice Voting).
        City Recorder, Ariel Andrus, went over the information that was discussed in the
        preceding Work Meeting.

        The City has successfully used Ranked Choice Voting for the last two municipal elections
        and would like to continue for this year’s upcoming municipal election.

        The Council Members had no further comments or questions on the matter.
