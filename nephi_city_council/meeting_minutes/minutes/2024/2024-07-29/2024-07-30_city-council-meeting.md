Mayor Justin D. Seely welcomed all present and opened the special city council meeting.

NEW ANIMAL CONTROL OFFICER THOMAS TALBERT INTRODUCED TO THE COUNCIL:

   Thomas Talbert introduced himself to the City Council. Mr. Talbert is the newly hired Animal Control
Officer. He announced that he had recently recertified with POST and will also be able to work patrol as
well as acting as the Animal Control Officer. The council welcomed him to Nephi City and expressed
their appreciation to him for accepting the job.


PROSECUTOR AGREEMENT WITH TYSON SKEEN APPROVED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council that in a previous meeting, discussions
were held about having a prosecutor specific for the Nephi City Justice Court. These duties have been
handled by City Attorney Kasey Wright. Mr. Atkinson stated that by having a specific prosecutor
dedicated for the Justice Court, it will free the City Attorney to focus on other municipal legal matters.
   The city sent out a request for proposals a couple of months ago. After that process, Tyson Skeen
was selected and recommended to the City Council to be the Nephi City Justice Court prosecuting
attorney. Tyson has an extensive background in prosecution, is familiar and has a relationship with the
defense attorneys being used for the Nephi City Justice Court and has been used by the Juab County
Justice Court for prosecution services as well.

  City Attorney Wright drafted a contract for Mr. Skeen that he has agreed with. Mr. Skeen is set to
begin his services on August 1, 2024.
  Councilor Skip Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the prosecutor agreement with Tyson
Skeen. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

HIVE TOURNAMENT COORDINATOR AGREEMENT WITH SHANE BLACKETT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated that council that for the last year, the city had contracted with an individual to
bring various tournaments to the Hive as a way to help pay for the operational and capital cost of the
facility. This individual didn’t have much success in organizing those tournaments for various
reasons. The city mutually parted ways and would like to try the same approach with a local resident.
  Shane Blackett owns a local business in Nephi and is involved in youth athletics. He has expressed an
interest in contracting with the city to assist in running tournaments at the Hive. He would also
maintain the social media accounts associated with the Hive that were created under the previous
contract. A contract was presented to the council that would set up similarly to the previous contract
with Mr. Travis Story.

 CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES:

        Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the now parking signs were up on Main Street near
         Maverik. Councilor Skip Worwood said there also needs to be signs along Main Street near Mt.
         Nebo Market. Councilor Travis Worwood asked if we could request from UDOT some additional
         signal lights on Main Street.
        The council discussed the safety issue of fast-moving E-bikes. Officer Nisha King stated that E-
         bikes must abide by the laws of the road and should not be riding on sidewalks.
        Councilor Parady said that he had sat down with the Chamber board and Economic Director
         Brent Boswell to review the suggestions from the Well Being Survey. There was a suggestion
         that if we are going to try to attract higher paying jobs, we need more amenities, such as a trail
         system, in our city.
        Officer Nisha King presented to the council a sheet of statistics on the ATV/OHV, distracted
         driving patrol shifts from April to June.

ADJOURNED INTO CLOSED MEETING:

  Councilor Travis Worwood moved to convene in a closed session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual, acquisition deposition of real property and collective bargaining.
Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote. 7:40 pm

Work Session 8:35 p.m.

    1.   The council discussed contributing additional funding to The Greater Nephi Chamber of
         Commerce.

    2. The council discussed the following three business grand openings that were to be held within
       the week: Jenson’s Cold Storage; North Pointe Dance Academy; and State Farm Insurance.

    3. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that there would be a very short council meeting on August
       6th beginning at 6:00 p.m. so that the council could attend the Juab County Fair BBQ.

    4. Councilor Skip Worwood said he would be out of town during most of August and would be
       missing the August meetings.

    5. The council discussed the pros and cons of allowing electronic meetings.

    6.   Councilor Skip Worwood moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Parady seconded the
         motion. The motion passed unanimously.

    7. The meeting adjourned at 9:54 pm.
