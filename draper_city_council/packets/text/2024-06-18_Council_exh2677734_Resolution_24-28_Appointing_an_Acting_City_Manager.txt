                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-28

               A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL
                  APPOINTING AN ACTING CITY MANAGER

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has created the position of City Manager to
administer the day-to-day operations of the City and its services according to the policies
and programs established by the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the current City Manager, David Dobbins, has submitted his
resignation effective June 28, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, the City Manager position has powers, duties, responsibilities, and
authority as delegated by the City Council; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Draper City Municipal Code 2-3-080, the City Council
desires to appoint Mike Barker as the acting city manager effective on June 28, 2024, to
exercise the powers, duties, responsibilities, and authority assigned to the City Manager
under statute and ordinance; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has determined it is in the best interest of the
health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Draper to appoint Mike Barker as acting city
manager based on his abilities, tenure with the City, prior experience relating to the duties
of the office and managerial capabilities.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. Mike Barker is appointed Acting Draper City Manager
with all the power, duties, responsibilities, and authority specified by statute and
ordinance. The appointment is effective June 28, 2024, and shall continue until the City
Council appoints a permanent City Manager.

       Section 2. Concurrent Duties. Mike Barker shall continue to exercise the
power, duties, responsibilities, and authority of his position as Draper City Attorney.

        Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution
shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.
     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 18th DAY OF JUNE, 2024.
