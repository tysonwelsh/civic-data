                                    PUBLIC NOTICE

Please be advised that on February 6, 2025, the City Council of the City of St
George approved the following ordinance(s):


Ordinance No. 2025-007 requesting the vacation of a portion of an existing municipal utility
easement located between Lots 16 and 25, Woodhaven Estates Subdivision.

Ordinance No. 2025-008 amending the existing Atkinville Interchange Planned Development
Commercial (PD-C) zone on approximately 17.85 acres generally located north of Southern
Parkway on Desert Color Parkway at approximately 4700 South.

Ordinance No. 2025-009 amending the city General Plan future land use map from MDR
(Medium Density Residential) and LDR (Low Density Residential) to (COM) Commercial on
approximately 7.92 acres generally located on the northwest corner of 2450 South and
3000 East.

Ordinance No. 2025-010 amending the city General Plan future land-use map from FP
(Flood Plain) and LDR (Low Density Residential) to COM (Commercial) on approximately
5.43 acres generally located 500 feet west of River Road on the south side of George
Washington Boulevard.

Ordinance No. 2025-011 amending the city zoning map by amending the zone from A-1
(Agricultural) to RE-12.5 (Residential Estates 12,500 ft² minimum lot size) on approximately
6.2 acres generally located on the east side of 3000 East at approximately 1800 South.
