Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: September 7, 2021
Re: Request for enactment of a temporary policy for the procedural implementation of ADU permits Comments: The attached resolution sets forth a temporary policy for the procedural implementation of ADU permits within Draper City, allowing for an orderly transition to the new regulations created through the passage of Draper City Ordinance #1499. The intent of the resolution is to provide for a grace period or periods during which property owners of properties with existing ADUs may obtain temporary relief from enforcement and abatement actions as they work towards compliance with the regulations.
Staff recommends that the Draper City Council consider the attached resolution at their September 7, 2021 meeting together with the public hearing for Ordinance #1499 and after the public hearing has been held, continue both items to the September 21, 2021 meeting for a final decision by the Council.  The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. The temporary policy provides adequate relief to current property owners of properties that contain existing ADUs; and
2. The temporary policy is in the best interest of the citizens of Draper City.
For Denial:
1.  The temporary policy is not in the best interest of the citizens of Draper City.
