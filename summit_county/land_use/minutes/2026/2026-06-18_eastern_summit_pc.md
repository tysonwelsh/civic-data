Principal Planner Ray Milliner updated the Commissioners on the proposed amendment to create the Lost Creek Community Zone. (6:11 PM)
President of Ivory Development Chris Gamvroulas; attorney Wade R. Budge of Snell & Wilmer; and architect and Ivory Community Planner Eric Langvardt of Langvardt Design Group LLC presented the proposed amendment to create the Lost Creek Community Zone. (6:14 PM)
Commissioners questioned the need for a new zone, possible unintended consequences, legal implications, process stages, acreage, and open space. They also suggested a flow chart for public clarity. Planner Milliner, Attorney Strachan, President Gamvroulas, and Attorney Budge responded. (6:13 PM)
