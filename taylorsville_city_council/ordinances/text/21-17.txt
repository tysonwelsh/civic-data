                                     TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 21- 17


      AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE AUTHORIZING THE
  CREATION OF A COMMUNITY ADVISORY BOARD FOR THE TAYLORSVILLE
                                    POLICE DEPARTMENT.


        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville   City Council ( the " Council") met during its regularly
scheduled meeting on November 17, 2021, to consider, among other things, authorizing the
creation of a Community Advisory Board for the Taylorsville Police Department; and

       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville Police Department desires to create a Community Advisory
Board for the purpose of conducting independent reviews of the Taylorsville Police Department
and to offer insights about suggested actions related to improved training/education, technology,
and wellness/ safety; establishing open culture between the TVPD and the community; providing
independent citizen oversight as to activities and response to high-acuity incidents; and ensuring
accountability and public transparency as to any findings and/ or best practices; and

       WHEREAS, the Community Advisory Board shall consist of five ( 5) members appointed
by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the Taylorsville City Council and who are considered
as credible in their employ or service and respected by peers; and

        WHEREAS, Community Advisory Board members shall serve a term of two( 2) years
and may be re- appointed to the Board at the end of each term; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it would be in
the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the City' s citizens to authorize the creation of
a Community Advisory Board for the Taylorsville Police Department.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
Taylorsville Police Department is hereby authorized to create a Community Advisory Board
whose roles and responsibilities shall be governed by an administration policy that may be
amended from time to time.


        This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 21- 17, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
