ACTION: RESOLUTION NO. 22-72, AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER
INTO A DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT WITH MOORE HOMES, LC
FOR APPROXIMATELY 10.97 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT
APPROXIMATELY 4590 SOUTH 5600 WEST
Steve Pastorik, CED Director, discussed proposed Resolution 22-72 that would
authorize the City to Enter into a Development Agreement with Moore Homes, LC for
Approximately 10.97 Acres of Property Located at Approximately 4590 South 5600
West

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
information as follows:
MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – MAY 3, 2022

       Randy Moore has submitted a zone change application (Z-2-2022) on 10.97
       acres to change the zoning from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre)
       to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and R-1-10 (Single Unit
       Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 10,000 square feet). The Planning
       Commission recommended approval of the zone change subject to a
       development agreement.

       The proposed development agreement addresses the following topics:

                  Maximum number of townhome units
                  Minimum townhome unit sizes
                  Architecture
                  Interior finishes
                  Amenities
                  One-acre of property to be dedicated as a City park
                  Standards for the two new single unit dwellings
                  Multi-unit residential design standards from City Code

Councilmember Christensen stated that there was concern several years ago on the
previous proposal from residents about a multi-family development at this location.
Steve replied that some of the issues brought up by residents were addressed which
include units being further from the fence, addition of a park, increased size of units
and basements, etc. Councilmember Nordfelt asked what type of fences would be
installed. Steve replied the applicant is proposing a pre-cast wall along 5600 West
which also includes small strip of landscaping. He noted that the fencing next to
residential is proposed as vinyl. Councilmember Harmon asked what the small
private street in the southeast corner of the proposal is. Steve replied that this is a
private drive along an easement for Dominion Energy to access their property.
Councilmember Nordfelt asked how often the train goes through this area. Steve
replied the answers have varied but residents living nearest this proposal have stated
it’s daily. Mayor Lang asked if there is enough open space proposed in the project
without the park option. Steve replied yes. Councilmember Whetstone asked what
the density per acre for this project is. Steve replied concept 1 (with the park) is 8.3
units/acre and concept 2 (without the park) would be 9.1 units/acre. Councilmember
Harmon stated that the City doesn’t have many large parcels left and he’d like to
see those go toward RE or RS zones. Steve replied that this property would not be
eligible for RS due to its size. Councilmember Nordfelt asked when the Council
would vote on this item. Steve replied that this was noticed prior to the Council’s
request for a three-week process so it would be considered by the Council next
week on May 10, 2022. Councilmember Harmon asked if a traffic study has been

       conducted. Steve replied no. Councilmember Nordfelt stated that he doesn’t see
       traffic being a concern at this location. Councilmember Whetstone agreed and
       noted that Mountain View Corridor has helped with traffic on 5600 West.

       The City Council will consider Ordinance 22-17 and Resolution 22-72 at the
       Regular Council Meeting scheduled May 10, 2022 at 6:30 P.M.

ORDINANCE 22-20: AMEND SECTIONS 1-2-102 AND 1-2-107 OF THE WEST
VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO UPDATE CERTAIN PARKS AND
RECREATION FEES
Nancy Day, Parks and Recreation Director, presented proposed Ordinance 22-20 that
would amend Sections 1-2-102 and 1-2-107 of the West Valley City Municipal Code to Update
Certain Parks and Recreation Fees.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The Parks and Recreation Department has a variety of fees for services that may
       require small changes at times based upon costs associated with providing these
       services and programs. Department staff work to efficiently monitor these fees and
       determine if changes need to be made. Due to the quantity of these type of fees, a
       change was approved which authorized the City Manager to review and approve
       certain fees for community and recreational services based on several conditions.
       This is found in the Consolidated Fee Schedule under section 1-2-107 (7) entitled
       Community and Recreational Services Fees.

       The Parks and Recreation Department would like to include several additional fees
       within this section. These include:
           Family Fitness Center:
                   o Childcare Fees
                   o Kidz Kamp Program Fees
                   o Kindergarten Kamp Fees
                   o Afterschool Kamp Fees
           Golf Courses:
                   o Green Fees
                   o Cart Fees
                   o Club Rental Fees
                   o Driving Range Ball Bucket Fees
                   o Tournament Fees
                   o Player Pass Fees
           UCCC:
        MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – MAY 3, 2022
                             -6-


                    o Rental Fees

Councilmember Christensen asked why park user fees were left in the proposal. Nancy
replied that staff left some of the fees that they felt Council would like to review. Mayor
Lang asked if there is a fee to utilize the football fields. Nancy replied that these are usually
reserved by contract with third party groups and are not part of the fee schedule. Mayor
Lang asked if the users of the fields are West Valley City residents. Nancy replied that
there may be some crossover but the youth sports are tied to school district boundaries.
Councilmember Whetstone asked if this proposal to allow the City Manager the option of
reviewing and raising these fees is because they are common and less unique. Nancy
replied yes.

The City Council will consider Ordinance 22-20 at the Regular Council Meeting scheduled
May 10, 2022 at 6:30 P.M.

COMMUNICATIONS
A.  BUDGET/PROGRAM MOD UPDATE
    Wayne Pyle, City Manager, stated that there have been no changes. He indicated
    that there are different variables that could change in the coming weeks that will
    not impact the bottom line. Mr. Pyle noted that program mod proposals can be
    adjusted between now and the adoption of the final budget as well. Mayor Lang
    asked how much the proposed tax increase will be. Wayne replied around 9% which
    equates to $5 a month or around $60 a year. Councilmember Whetstone asked if
    there could be a small discretionary fund balance for each councilmember to utilize
    in their district. Wayne replied that there are funds available but these can be
    divided or modified as well if the Council wishes. The Mayor and Council
    discussed and agreed that any Councilmember could bring a proposal to the Council
    as a whole for consideration.
