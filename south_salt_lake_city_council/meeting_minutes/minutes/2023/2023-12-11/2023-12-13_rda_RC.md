New Business/Unfinished Business

    1. 2024 RDA Meeting Schedule

        City Recorder, Ariel Andrus, went over the proposed meeting schedule for 2024. Considerations
        were made for uneven months, as well as conferences, that the Board Members attend
        throughout the year. It was noted that RDA meetings are officially added on the City’s website
        calendar on an ‘as needed’ basis and the dates are placeholders that parallel with the City
