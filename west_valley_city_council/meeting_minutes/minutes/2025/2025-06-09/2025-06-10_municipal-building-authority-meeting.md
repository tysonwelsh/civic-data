    PUBLIC HEARINGS
    A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2024-2025
         BUDGET
         Chairperson Huynh informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
         Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 10, 2025, in order for to
         hear and consider public comments regarding Re-Opening the FY 2024-2025
         Budget.

           Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
           information as follows:

                  State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
                  allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
                  West Valley City Municipal Building Authority holds, as needed on a
                  quarterly basis, public hearings to amend the annual budget.

           Chairperson Huynh opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in
           favor or opposition, Chairperson Huynh closed the Public Hearing.

           ACTION: RESOLUTION NO. 25-04, AMEND THE BUDGET OF THE WEST
           VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL
           YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2024 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2025 TO REFLECT
           CHANGES IN THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED REVENUES AND
           AUTHORIZE THE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
           The Board previously held a public hearing regarding proposed Resolution 25-04
           that would amend the Budget of the West Valley City Municipal Building Authority
           for the Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2024 and Ending June 30, 2025 to Reflect
           Changes in the Budget from Increased Revenues and Authorize the Disbursement of
           Funds.

           Upon inquiry by Chairperson Huynh there were no further questions from members
           of the City Council, and he called for a motion.

                  Mr. Harmon moved to approve Resolution 25-04.

                  Ms. Wood seconded the motion.

                  A roll call vote was taken:

    B.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2025-2026 BUDGET
         Chairman Huynh informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
         Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled June 10, 2025, in order for the
         Board to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2025-2026 Budget.

         Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
         information as follows:

                This resolution adopts the final budget for the Municipal Building Authority
                of West Valley City for the 2025-2026 fiscal year after receiving public
                comment at a hearing on June 10, 2025.

                The Municipal Building Authority adopted a tentative budget that was made
                available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
                Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
                before adopting this final budget for FY 2025-2026.

         Chairman Huynh opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
         or opposition, Chairman Hounh closed the Public Hearing.

         ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 25-05, ADOPT THE ANNUAL
         BUDGET OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING
         AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1, 2025
         AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2026
         The Municipal Building Authority previously held a public hearing regarding
         proposed Resolution 25-05 that would adopt the annual budget of the West Valley
         City Municipal Building Authority for the fiscal year commencing July 1, 2025 and
         ending June 30, 2026.

         Upon inquiry by Chairperson Huynh there were no further questions from members
         of the City Council, and he called for a motion.
