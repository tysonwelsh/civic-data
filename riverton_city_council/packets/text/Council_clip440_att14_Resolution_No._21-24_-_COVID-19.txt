                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-24

A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR TERMINATING STATE AND LOCAL
          ORDERS THAT REQUIRE A MASK BE WORN IN PUBLIC


      WHEREAS, during the 2021 session of the Utah Legislature, HB 294 passed both the
House of Representatives and the Senate by overwhelming majorities; and

       WHEREAS, HB 294 is entitled Pandemic Emergency Powers Amendments and
provides for the termination of emergency powers and public health orders related to protecting
the public against the spread of COVID-19; and

        WHEREAS, under the terms of HB 294, COVID-19 positivity rates, case rates, intensive
care facility capacity rates, and vaccine dosages must reach certain thresholds before public
health orders relating to COVID-19 are eliminated; and

        WHEREAS, HB 294 also requires that state or county health department regulations
requiring the use of a mask in response to the COVID-19 emergency are “terminated on April
10, 2021;” and

       WHEREAS, the elimination of mask mandates found in public health orders is subject to
an important exception, which is “a local health department, with approval from the relevant
county legislative body, may issue a public health order requiring the wearing of a mask”; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a public health order
issued by Salt Lake County relating to a mask mandate for the entire county would amount to a
disparate form of treatment to citizens where there is a low rate of infection; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that individual citizens
should be empowered to decide for themselves as to whether they personally need to use masks;
and

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

           1. The Riverton City Council hereby supports the wisdom of HB 294 inasmuch as it
              eliminates mask mandates issued by public health orders, as of April 10, 2021.




                                               1
          2. The Riverton City Council hereby requests that Salt Lake County allow local
             municipalities to make determinations that are in the resident’s best interests
             following the standards set forth by HB 294.
