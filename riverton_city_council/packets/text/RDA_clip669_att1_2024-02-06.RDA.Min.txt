24   Call to Order
25
26   Chair Staggs called the Riverton Redevelopment Agency Board Meeting to order at 7:47 PM
27   and conducted a roll call. Board members Buroker, Haymond, McCay, McDougal, and Pierucci
28   were present.
29
30   Discussion/Action
31
32          Minute Approval: January 16, 2024
33
34   Board Member McDougal MOVED that the City Council approve the minutes from January
35   16, 2024. Board Member Haymond SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion
36   on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows: Board
37   Members Buroker-Yes, Haymond-yes, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, and Pierucci-Yes. The
38   motion passed unanimously.
39
40   Closed Session
41
42      Discussion of the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real property, including any
43      form of a water rights or water share.
44
45   At 7:48 PM, Board Member McDougal MOVED to suspend the Redevelopment Agency Meeting
46   and convene in a Closed Session to discuss the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real
47   property, including any form of a water rights or water share. Board Member McDougal also
48   MOVED to adjourn the Redevelopment Agency meeting at the conclusion of the Closed Session.
49   Board Member Pierucci SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion on the
50   motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes,
51   Haymond-yes, McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, and Pierucci-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
