FROM:          Trace Robinson, Public Works Director

ITEM:          Resolution Approving Reimbursement Agreement with Luke Peterson to pay a
               cost to cure for improvements to the frontage of Peterson Farms Property.


SUMMARY:

Included with the 2020-4000 W Overly Project, the PW Department determined that widening the
road and the placement of curb and gutter along the frontage of Peterson Farms was essential to
the Project. This will allow the City to restripe the road through this section to better match the
alignment with South Jordan lane configuration to the north. In order to construct these
improvements, the City worked with Luke Peterson to regrade the frontage of his property and
relocate his fence. It was felt by both the City and Mr. Peterson that it would be in the best interest
of both parties to pay Peterson Farms a cost to cure for the work and have Mr. Peterson do the
work. This would enable them to complete the work in a manner they felt would better serve their
needs. Luke Peterson provided a cost to do said work which was compared to City competitive
bids of similar items. We felt his proposed cost was in line with costs we would have receive from
the bidding process.


FISCAL IMPACT: $ 20,000

FUNDING SOURCE: 21-60-665

   3. Resolution
