PUBLIC HEARING(S)
Meeting began at – 8:30 a.m.
Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding accelerating the adoption of the
ordinances. The planning commission (the “PC”) would like to consider big ticket items recommended to
the council and continue looking at the documents for clarification. They need to understand existing
ordinances versus the proposed, implement a process focusing on differences and eliminations and
additions. The PC wants to review the final draft of the ordinance prior to staff taking the recommendation
to the council.
Spend time on policy at meetings, staff and counsel implement the ordinance and send it to the PC to review
for consideration and follow a timeline for the PC to review. Asking for smaller sections to review at any
one time and would like the ordinances cleaned up. Will provide a date for cleanup and receive feedback
from the pc with a reasonable time to review. Provide a clear distinction between original and amended.
Creating policy, cleanup, and review to accomplish the policy direction is implemented and have an honest
report for the council.
