                                            PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 52:35)
Brad Freeman for the Lone Peak Fire District came forward to speak on Regular Agenda Item No. 14, regarding an
agreement with Lone Peak Public Safety District to provide fire protection services within the unincorporated area of
Utah County. Mr. Freeman explained how Lone Peak assesses their fees by household rather than square footage,
which is how cities determine rates. The contract approved by the Board today is for $15,000 less than prior years,
though Lone Peak’s costs average $250 per house to provide full services. Commissioner Graves asked why the
district does not assess based on property value, since that is how fire damage is calculated, and Mr. Freeman explained
how all nine firefighters – the district’s full resources – are utilized on every call, whether the property is $150,000 or
$1,000,000. They discussed the fairness of charging on value versus household, with Mr. Freeman reiterating Lone
Peak is only seeking to recover actual costs.

(Track A, 1:00:50)
Commissioner Lee asked about Brad Freeman’s comment that the previous Board of Commissioners had agreed to an
additional $15,000 payment for fire protection services. Commissioner Lee asked for a copy of the contract wherein
that amount was negotiated, and Mr. Freeman noted there was a verbal agreement. Commissioner Graves declared
he would never agree to payment on a verbal agreement. Mr. Freeman explained his understanding that the county
was to write and present a contract for signatures following the verbal agreement, but he was not included in that full
process. Mr. Freeman mentioned a proposed amendment regarding the funds which was intended to be included as
part of today’s contract, but it does not seem to have been attached to the agenda request. When Mr. Freeman
mentioned the additional payment was made for the last two years in a row, Utah County Fire Marshal Jack Snow
confirmed. Commissioner Lee expressed concern payments were made without a written contract. Mr. Snow also
pointed out the written references to the additional funds in the current agreement, mentioning former Commissioner
Larry A. Ellertson had requested the payments. Commissioner Graves stated no one is questioning the great service
provided by the district, but he is offended and finds it inappropriate that a politician would make a promise and not
put it in writing. The commissioners, Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson, and Fire Marshal Snow
discussed the quarterly payment process.
