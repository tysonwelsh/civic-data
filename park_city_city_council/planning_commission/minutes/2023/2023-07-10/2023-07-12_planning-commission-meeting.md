The approval document for 35-13167 (a49017) contained the following language:

"The applicant is strongly cautioned that other permits may be required before any development of this
application can begin and it is the responsibility of the applicant to determine the applicability of and
acquisition of such permits."

For water right issues, the letter of the law indicates the State Engineer must approve a location for a
person to drill. The reason for this, though, is for the proper administration of all water rights with a
primary concern over water availability.

The State Engineer has jurisdiction over the proper construction of the well. The State Engineer's
authority in the well drilling rules (R655-4) seems to be focused on the construction method and
materials, not the location.

If a well is drilled in a wetland area, I believe concerns regarding wetlands will fall under jurisdiction of
the Army Corps of Engineers.

If a well is drilled in an area that is subject to local ordinances, I think it is up to the entity where those
ordinances apply to enforce those ordinances.
