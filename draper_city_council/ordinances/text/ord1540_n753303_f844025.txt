City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On May 3, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1540, amending the Land
Use Map. Otherwise known as the Norman Family Flag Lot Land Use Amendment.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 4th day of May 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper
City Recorder.
