A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP AFFIRMING KEARNS’
COMMITMENT TO THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
§§ 10-2a-401 et seg; and

WHEREAS, Kearns is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code §
10-3-205.5 (the “Council’”); and

2580

WHEREAS, on April 10, 2023, Kearns adopted Resolution 2023-04-02 Establishing
Policy and Providing Direction Regarding the Implementation of H.B. 374 (“County Sheriff
Amendments”); and

WHEREAS, pursuant to said Resolution 2023-04-02, the City Attorney, under the
direction of the Mayor, has been working with legal counsel for other metro townships and the
Town of Brighton to deal with the short and long term implications of H.B. 374, which requires
a separation of the Salt Lake County Sheriff from the Unified Police Department (“UPD”); and

WHEREAS, as a result of that separation it has become necessary for UPD to amend
and restate the interlocal agreement under which it was created, which restated and amended
interlocal agreement will govern the provision of law enforcement services to its members; and

WHEREAS, Kearns’s City Attorney has been asked to participate in the drafting of such
restated and amended interlocal agreement; and

WHEREAS, Kearns, as a result of its adoption of Resolution 2023-04-02, is on record
as supporting the UPD model, and now desires to affirm that position.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS
METRO TOWNSHIP:

1. The Council hereby authorizes the City Attorney, under the direction of the Mayor, to
assist in the drafting of a restated and amended interlocal agreement for the Unified Police
Department; and

2. Upon completion of the drafting of such agreement, to present the same to the Council for
review and adoption.
