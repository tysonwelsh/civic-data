   B. Consider a request to change the Zoning from A-1 (Agricultural) to PD-R (Planned Development Residential) to
      add acreage to the existing Park at Temple View PD-R (Planned Development Residential), to allow for a new
      residential development. The total property is approximately 63.10 acres. This property is generally located east
      of 3000 East Street and north of 1140 South Street. The applicant is Suburban Land Reserve, and the
      representative is Rollin Johnson. The project will be known as The Fields at Mall Drive Residential PD. Case No.
      2021-ZC-084. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

       Carol Davidson presented the following:

       Carol Davidson – We already approved the Fat Cats near here. We changed the general plan here previously.
       The general plan is MDR. At this time, they want to change it to a PD-R but there are no elevations, they will
       come back with another zone change when they have that information.

       Pro tempore Chair Larsen – Will that road be put in?

       Carol Davidson – I think part of it will go in, but there are some heavy utilities in it.

       Pro tempore Chair Larsen – So they won’t be conditioned on that road going in to get this PD?

       Carol Davidson – No

       Commissioner Kemp – My question is why are we doing a PD-R if we have nothing to approve? A PD is
       supposed to have all the plans to approve.

       Dan McKay -The thought was that we would bring it in so that we didn’t bring it in by itself. We want to put a
       pin in it, this is the only way the City has to do this. Normally we would do a master plan. I have a power point,
       it isn’t very long, that can show what the densities will turn out to be. We want to make sure that all of the
       particulars are shown at another time.

       John Willis – If you look at some of the other ones we’ve done they knew a lot of what they were doing, then
       some they didn’t so we gave certain pods a unit count and they came back with the details.

       Commissioner Anderson – Do we know what the unit count is?

       Commissioner Fisher – They aren’t asking for that tonight.

       Dan McKay – Described the density on each area of the project.

       Commissioner Fisher – But to be clear we are not approving a unit count tonight?

       Dan McKay – No, just the PD-R designation.

       Pro tempore Chair Larsen – I am concerned about the density right there.

       Commissioner Fisher – Again we are not approving a unit count tonight, we are only approving the PD
       designation.

       Dan McKay – Your point is made though, and we will take all of the comments into consideration.

       Discussion on densities continued.

   2. ZONING CHANGE AMENDMENT (ZCA) (Public Hearing) Legislative

   A. Consider a to amend the Southern Hills West PD-C (Planned Development Commercial). The applicant is seeking
      approval to expand the commercial property and reduce the residential property by 0.6 acres. The applicant is also
      seeking approval of the design and site plan for a new 30,000 square foot commercial development. The total
      property area is approximately 5.1 acres. The project is located on the northeast corner of River Road and White
      Dome Drive. The applicant is Prime Directive Development Inc., and the representative is Ryan Thomas. The
      project will be known as White Dome Commercial. Case No. 2021-ZCA-082. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

       Carol Davidson presented the following:

       Carol Davidson – The general plan is commercial. They want to get the site plan and the buildings approved
       tonight. They will have a C-store with gas pumps and a carwash. They have 178 stalls in this parking lot they
       have determined it as 60% restaurant and 40% retail, they exceed the parking requirements. They are adjacent to
       residential on the east side so they will need the privacy wall and the landscape buffer. Carwash is allowed in this
       PD. Staff’s main concern is that the noise level with carwash is usually pretty loud. The colors they are using are
       similar to the apartments that you will see in the next item. As far as staff recommendations we want to make
       sure all the zoning regulations are met.

   B. Consider a request to amend the Southern Hills West PD-R (Planned Development Residential). The applicant is
      seeking approval to expand the commercial property and reduce the residential property by 0.6 acres. The
      applicant is also seeking approval of the design and site plan for a new apartment development. The total
      property area is approximately 14.96 acres. The project is located on the northeast corner of River Road and
      White Dome Drive (Please see map on back). The applicant is Prime Directive Development Inc., and the
      representative is Ryan Thomas. The project will be known as White Dome Apartments. Case No. 2021-ZCA-
      083. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Carol Davidson presented the following:

      Carol Davidson – The general plan is HDR. For this development, they will be 3 and 4 stories. They are required
      to have 2 amenities each, they both will have the two amenities with one having an additional amenity of a
      clubhouse. They are required to put in 654 spaces, they are only providing 638 stalls. They are asking for a
      reduction of 1.96 per unit instead of 2.0 per unit. The buildings will be the same architectural design, but they
      will use different colors to make them distinct. They are using the same colors as the commercial development.

      They are asking to go up to 49 ft in height. There are 3 conditions, they meet the zoning code, the parking is
      reduced to 1.96 per unit and they will max at 49 ft in height.

      Commissioner Fisher – How close are the colors on the screen to the true colors?

      Ryan Thomas – They are actually pretty close. We went out there and looked at the colors banded on the hills,
      then matched them to Sherwin Williams paint colors. When I had originally met with Carol and staff to talk
      about this, I had enough parking, but it just looked like a sea of asphalt. I wanted to get some more trees in there.
      I still comply with the guest parking.

      Commissioner Larsen – How many bedrooms are in those units?

      Ryan Thomas – There are 8 apartments per floor they will each have 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms. The total unit
      count is 280. There will be a two-story townhome project near these also.

      Commissioner Larsen – I am concerned about parking. When I go by the apartments by Smiths there is a lot of
      parking on the street, and I don’t see much street parking for this.

      times.
