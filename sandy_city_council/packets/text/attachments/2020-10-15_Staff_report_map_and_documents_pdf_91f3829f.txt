DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Synergy Development, is requesting preliminary subdivision review for plat
amendment to subdivide two commercial lots into six commercial lots. The applicants are not
requesting any overlays or special exceptions in association with this application. All provisions
of the base zone will be adhered to through the review and approval process.

BACKGROUND & SITE CONDITIONS
The subject property is approximately 23.55 acres and is located in the ID zone (Industrial
Development District) and fronts onto Sandy Parkway on the east. The bordering
properties to the north, east, and south are all zoned ID. The west side is bordered by a
canal with residential properties, Jefferson Place Condominiums, in the PUD (12) zone
across the canal.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel as per
Sandy City Land Development Code requirements, to notify them of the Planning Commission
Synergy Canyons Subdivision 1st Amendment, Amending Lots 1 & 2 - SUB-07-20-5884 Page 2


meeting. A neighborhood meeting was not deemed necessary as the use of the properties is not
changing, it is only changing the boundary lines around the existing and proposed buildings.

SUBDIVISION ANALYSIS
The ID zone is an industrial zone. There is no change to the proposed use of the property. All
standards of the zone (setbacks, building height, uses, etc.) will conform to the requirements of
the ID zone. The buildings are being used as “industrial flex-space”, which allows office, retail,
warehousing, and manufacturing uses.

The subject property is comprised of lot #1 and lot #2 of the original Synergy Canyons plat,
which was originally envisioned to be a six-building complex of industrial flex-space. Now that
some buildings have been built, and some are under current construction, the plat amendment is
being done to allow the applicants financial flexibility by having each commercial building
contained on its own parcel of property.

STAFF CONCERNS
The original plat contains a cross-access easement, which they have maintained on this amended
version of the plat. However, because there will be six unique parcels, there will need to be a cross-
access easement granted to allow for traveling between all six parcels.

Also, the original plat calls out the protected 30% slope areas. This amended plat does not. For the
sake of consistency and thoroughness it should show the protected slope areas on this plat as well.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine Preliminary Subdivision review is
complete for the Synergy Canyons Subdivision 1st Amendment, Amending Lots 1 & 2 plat,
located at 9150 S. and 9270 S. Sandy Pkwy, based upon the following findings and subject to the
following conditions:

Findings:

   1. The lots meet all underlying requirements of the ID zone, and the buildings all meet the
      setback requirements of the zone.

   2. That the various City Departments and Divisions have preliminarily approved the
      proposed subdivision plat.

Conditions:

   1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout
      the final review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be
      recorded.

   2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to during the review,
      construction and operations process of this project.

   3. That additional cross-access easements be granted and shown on the plat to allow for
      vehicular and pedestrian access between all lots.
