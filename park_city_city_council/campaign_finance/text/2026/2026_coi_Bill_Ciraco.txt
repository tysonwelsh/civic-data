Money deposited in the program banks is insured by the FDIC. FDIC deposit insurance and SIPC coverage are very different. FDIC insures depositors against loss of principal value of a deposit in the event of the insolvency of the bank that issued the deposit. SIPC coverage
protects against a disappearance of securities that results from the insolvency of a broker-dealer and a loss of customer cash held as a general obligation of the broker-dealer up to the above stated limits. SIPC coverage does not protect against a decline in value of securities, and
provides no coverage for balances held on deposit at a bank, even if held through a broker-dealer. Further information about SIPC may be found on their website at www.sipc.org or by calling (202) 371-8300. Further information about the FDIC may be found on their website at
