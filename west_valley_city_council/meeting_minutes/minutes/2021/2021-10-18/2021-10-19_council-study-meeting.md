      RESOLUTION 21-183: APPROVE A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
      BETWEEN WEST VALLEY CITY AND THE FEDERAL BUREAU OF
      ALCOHOL, TOBACCO, FIREARMS, AND EXPLOSIVES (ATF) TO JOIN THE
      JOINT ATF TASK FORCE
      Scott Buchanan, Deputy Police Chief, presented proposed Resolution 21-183 that would
      approve a Memorandum of Understanding Between West Valley City and the Federal Bureau
      of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives (ATF) to Join the Joint ATF Task Force.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
      follows:

             In an effort to coordinate local law enforcement resources in the investigation of
             firearms and explosives trafficking and firearm and explosives related violent crime
    MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – OCTOBER 19, 2021
                           -5-


       and their associated organizations and networks, law enforcement agencies of the
       greater Salt Lake Valley, State and Federal agencies have united efforts and
       resources to form the ATF Task Force. The Police Department seeks to assign one
       (1) investigator to this position. This agreement clarifies the responsibilities of
       participating agencies to establish the groundwork for the administration and
       operation of the taskforce.

       The mission of this task force is to focus on the reduction of violent crimes in the
       West Valley City Metropolitan area. This mission will be generally accomplished
       through the maximum effort of investigation, arrest, detention, enhanced
       prosecution, and forfeiture of assets of criminally active violent offenders with
       emphasis on criminal activity supporting violent gangs. Specifically, efforts will
       focus on violent street gangs, felons in possession of firearms, armed career
       criminals, armed robberies (Hobbs Act Violations) major theft and armed
       carjacking rings, murder-for-hire, Racketeering Influenced and Corrupt
       Organizations (RICO), and related violations under Utah Criminal Statutes. This
       endeavor will be enhanced by targeting violent crime problems through the teaming
       of federal, state, and local law enforcement officers and prosecutors to conduct
       short and mid-term, proactive and intelligence driven investigations.

Councilmember Huynh asked if the City has a close connection with the Agency. Deputy
Chief Buchanan stated that the task force works very closely together and has a very strong
partnership. Councilmember Huynh asked what the benefit is in joining the task force.
Chief Buchanan stated that it allows the City to prioritize certain cases from inception to
prosecution. He noted that Federal funds are scarce so entering into this agreement is very
important. Councilmember Huynh asked what the City could do to support this effort.
Deputy Chief Buchanan stated that approval of this agreement would be the best thing the
City could do to support the cause. Mayor Bigelow commended the department on keeping
agreements like this in place so that the City can benefit from these task forces.

The City Council will consider Resolution 21-183 at the Regular Council Meeting
scheduled October 26, 2021 at 6:30 P.M.

RELATED EQUIPMENT FROM FREEDOM LED
Wayne Pyle, City Manager, presented proposed Resolution 21-184 that would authorize
the purchase of a Marquee and related equipment from Freedom LED.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The Marquee is located on the eastside of I-215 in the Maverik Center parking lot
       located at 3200 S Decker Lake Drive.

       Bids for construction were solicited and five bidders responded:

               Freedom LED                             $389,952.24
               YESCO                                   $455,197.00
               Daktronics                              $398,864.00
               Creative Signs & Graphics               $476,964.36
               Allied Electric Sign                    $482,931.61
               Impact Signs                            $537,830.00
               Graphix Display                         $759,711.00

       Freedom LED was selected as the lowest responsible bidder. Construction will
       begin 7 to 8 weeks.

Mayor Bigelow clarified that this proposal is to replace the marquee and it will be the same
size and located in the same place. Wayne replied yes. Mayor Bigelow asked if Freedom
LED has been used in the past. Jim Fuoco, Maverik Center, stated that they have a good
reputation and did the signage at the new Hale Theatre and many Mountain America
locations. Councilmember Fitisemanu asked what average lifespan of the signs are. Jim
replied that this is the third sign. He noted that the first sign was not a good quality and did
not last as long as the other two. Jim stated that the two signs have lasted over 27 years and
the lifespan is on average 10 years so they have managed to keep them operational for a
good amount of time. He added that extended warranties are requested and are included in
the bids.

The City Council will consider Resolution 21-184 at the Regular Council Meeting
scheduled October 26, 2021 at 6:30 P.M.
