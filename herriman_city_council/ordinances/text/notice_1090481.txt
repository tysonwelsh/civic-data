PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on June 18, 2026, at 4:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2026-14 approving and adopting a final budget; making appropriations for the support of the City of Herriman for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2026, and ending June 30, 2028; adopting a job classification plan and salary plan; and determining the rate of tax and levying taxes upon all real and personal property within Herriman City, Utah. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
