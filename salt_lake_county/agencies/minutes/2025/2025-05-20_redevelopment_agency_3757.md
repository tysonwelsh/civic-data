RESOLUTION NO. 152
RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE 
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY APPROVING 
AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH THE 
COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AGENCY OF MAGNA FOR THE 
TRANSFER OF TWO REDEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREAS AND ITS 
RELATED REAL PROPERTY, RIGHTS, INDEBTEDNESS, 
OBLIGATIONS, TAX INCREMENT, AND OTHER ASSETS AND 
LIABILITIES
RECITALS
WHEREAS, on December 10, 2024, the Board of Directors of the 
Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “County RDA ”) passed 
Resolution No. 149, which directed its staff to begin negotiating an interlocal 
cooperation agreement with the Community Reinvestment Agency of Magna 
(the “Magna CRA ”) to transfer its active project areas located within the 
boundaries of Magna City to the Magna CRA;
WHEREAS, since that time, the County RDA has: a) dissolved its inactive 
project area; b) created a plan to wind up its management of the two active 
project areas located in Magna City; and c) identified its real property, rights, 
indebtedness, obligations, tax increment, or other assets and liabilities that 
would be involved in, or otherwise affected by, such a transfer;
WHEREAS, the County RDA now desires to transfer the remaining active 
project areas to the Magna CRA, namely:
 
· The Magna/Arbor Park Urban Renewal Area; and 
· The Magna Main Street Community Development Area;
WHEREAS, the County RDA and Magna CRA are “public agencies” as 
defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Ann. §§

11-13-101, et seq . (the “Interlocal Act ”), and, as such, are authorized to enter 
into agreements to act jointly and cooperatively in a manner that will enable 
them to make the most efficient use of their resources and powers;
WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17C-1-205 authorizes the County RDA and 
Magna CRA to enter into an interlocal agreement, for County RDA to 
transfer or assign to the Magna CRA all the County RDA ’s real property, 
rights, indebtedness, obligations, tax increment, or other assets and liabilities 
related to the project areas;
AND WHEREAS, the County RDA ’s real property, rights, indebtedness, 
obligations, tax increment, and other assets and liabilities to be transferred to 
the Magna CRA are more particularly described in the interlocal cooperation 
agreement attached hereto as Attachment “A.”
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Board of 
Directors of the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County that:
A. The transfer of the Magna /Arbor Park Urban Renewal Area and the 
Magna Main Street Community Development Area to the Community 
Reinvestment Agency of Magna, along with its related real property, rights, 
indebtedness, obligations, tax increment, and other assets and liabilities as set 
forth in Attachment “A,” is hereby approved; 
B. The Interlocal Agreement between the Redevelopment Agency of Salt 
Lake County and the Community Reinvestment Agency of Magna is hereby 
approved, in substantially the form attached hereto as Attachment “A”; 
C. The Chairperson is authorized to execute the Interlocal Agreement, as well 
as the ten quitclaim deeds attached thereto as Exhibits “A” to “J,” and the 
eight assignments attached thereto as Exhibits “K” to “R,” all of which are 
incorporated herein by this reference. 
D. That the Interlocal Agreement will become effective as stated therein. 
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 20th day of May, 2025.
