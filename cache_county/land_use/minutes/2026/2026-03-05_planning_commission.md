#5. Public Hearing (6:05PM or soon thereafter) – Water and Sewage Requirements Ordinance 
Amendment 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the proposed amendment for the Water and Sewage Requirements Ordinance 
Amendment. 
 
Commissioners and Staff discussed the public outreach to help with concerns that had been raised 
previously. 
 
Sands motioned to open the public hearing for the Water and Sewage Requirements Ordinance 
Amendments; Daugs seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Val Jay Rigby, Chris Sands, Brady Christensen, Kurt Bankhead, Morris Poole, Nate Daugs 
Nays: 0 
 
Jordan Mathis director of the Bear River Health Department (BRHD) commented as the applicant and 
explained their amendments from the previous draft. 
 
Gunnell asked about systems over 5,000 gallons and 100-year storm concerns. 
 
Mr. Mathis responded anything over 5,000 gallons is regulated by the state and this does not add any 
new systems. If a new system or expanded system is put in, a geologist would have to sign off on it. 
 
Poole asked about the geologist report. 
 
Mr. Mathis responded the geologist report should address those concerns and that this is only addressing 
current systems that are already there. 
 
Bankhead asked about discharge from property in zone 2. 
 
Mr. Mathis responded it would have to be piped outside of the zone. 
 
Christensen asked about the protections for property owners who have source protection areas suddenly 
happened. 
 
Mr. Mathis responded there is a process for public input regarding those situations before source 
protection areas are finalized. 
 
Brady Bodily mayor of Lewiston commented in support of this and the protection it provides.
