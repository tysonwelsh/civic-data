This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-24-4009,
“Riverton Under Car Properties Rezone,” by rezoning a 0.88-acre parcel located at 2284
West 12600 to C-G (Gateway Commercial).

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-24-4009, for
rezoning 0.88- acres located at 2284 West 12600, thus keeping the current zoning of RR-22
(1/2 -acre minimum lots).


Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Thom Jakab, for two
parcels totaling about 0.88-acres located near 2284 West 12600 South. These properties are
on the north side of 12600 South Street just west of the existing Master Autotech building.
The property is currently zoned RR-22 which is a single-family residential zone with minimum
lot size of 1/2-acre, the requested zone is C-G which is Gateway Commercial. The General
Plan designation for this property is Business Park.

The subject property is made of two (2) rectangular parcels. The east parcel has a small
redbrick home, the western parcel has no structures. Both parcels have been used as a
vehicle storage and RV parking (as can be seen from the aerial images). The city has received
complaints about the parking and RV storage on a residential property. This rezone would only
resolve the zoning, the applicant would also need to get approval for a commercial site plan for
the property.

The subject property is zoned RR-22, a hold-over from long ago. The properties to the east
and west are zoned commercial and have commercial uses. Property to the east is zoned C-G.
Property to the west is zoned C-PO (Commercial Professional Office). The General Plan for the
north side of 12600 South, from the canal to 2700 West is Business Park which corresponds
with both C-G, and C-PO zoning.

To the north of the subject property is the Country View PUD, which is a mix of single family
and townhome units.



Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
webpage. No public comments have been received.

When considering rezone applications, the Planning Commission makes a recommendation to
City Council. The City Council will make a final decision on this application in a future City Council
Meeting. The City will send additional notices to property owners within 1,000 feet to announce
the City Council meeting date.
