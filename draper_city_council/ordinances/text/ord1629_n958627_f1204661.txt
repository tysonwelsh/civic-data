City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1629

On December 3, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1629 amending
the compensation for statutory officers of Draper City. The complete ordinance is on
file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published
this 5th day of December 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
