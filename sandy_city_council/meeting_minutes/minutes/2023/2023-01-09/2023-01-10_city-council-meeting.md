Citizen Comments


1.         23-019                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item.

                                  Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comments.

                                  Mr. Bruce Blanchard agreed with Council Member Sharkey's proposal regarding the 
                                  installation of campaign signs following a candidate's election filing. He spoke about the 
                                  state's consideration of the elimination of property taxes which he feels will 
                                  disproportionately impacts lower income individuals. He expressed support of Jim 
                                  Edwards to serve as an alternate to the Planning Commission. He spoke of his friend, Mr. 
                                  Paul Pratt, who recently moved out of Sandy City. Mr. Blanchard thanked the elected 
                                  officials for the many hours of service they provide to this great community of Sandy City.

                                  Ms. Dawn Sidwell expressed concerns regarding the proposed amendments to the 
                                  Pledge of Fair Campaign Practices considered in Item 2 on the Agenda. She feels it is 
                                  her first amendment right to have the ability to put up signs on her private property.

                                  Mr. Aaron De Keyser believes in private property rights and is not supportive of the 
                                  amendments to the pledge which will be introduced in Item 2 on the Agenda. He 
                                  expressed support for Mr. Jim Edwards appointment to the Planning Commission.

                                  Ms. Anita Bennet has lived in Sandy for 40 years and appreciates all the hard work of the 
                                  Council Members and City staff. She expressed support for Jim Edwards appointment to 
                                  the Planning Commission. Mr. Edwards is a hard working, fair, and honest person. 

                                  Ms. Susan Richardson is supportive of Jim Edwards appointment to the Planning 
                                  Commission. She trusts him and thinks he is of good character.  

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren is supportive of Jim Edwards appointment to the Planning 
                                  Commission. 

                                  At the request of Council Member Sharkey, the Council Director read an eComment into 
                                  the record sent in by Ms. Katie Johnson. Ms. Johnson is not supportive of any changes 
                                  to the Pledge of Fair Campaign Practices and does not think any changes should be 
                                  made during an election year. She has run for City Council in a prior year and spoke 
                                  about attack adds. She thinks the City should be careful before making any changes 
                                  during an election year and she spoke about her First Amendment rights.

                                  Public Comment Closed.

Informational Items


2.         23-018                 First Reading: Council Member Sharkey requesting that the Council 
                                  consider making additions to the Pledge of Fair Campaign Practices
                     Sponsors: Sharkey

                   Attachments:   Memorandum_Sharkey.docx
                                  Redline
                                  Resolution_Final.pdf

                                  First Reading: Council Member Sharkey introduced the item and requested the Council 
                                  consider amendments to the Pledge of Fair Campaign Practices. She reviewed the 
                                  current Pledge and the proposed amendments. The pledge is voluntary and optional for 
                                  candidates to sign. A lengthy Council discussion followed.  The City Attorney and 
                                  Council Attorney provided some guidance and insight into municipal sign ordinances and 
                                  suggested that Council may want to review the city's current sign ordinance. Council 
                                  provided feedback to Council Member Sharkey.

3.         23-016                 Administration presenting the Council with a preview of the upcoming 2023 
                                  Legislative Session
                   Attachments:   Legislative Report.pptx

                                  Evelyn Everton, Consultant with Sandy City, reviewed the legislative priorities for Sandy 
                                  City. Ms. Everton provided a handout that detailed the lobbyists currently contracted with 
                                  Sandy City and the issues they focus on. She reviewed upcoming legislative issues and 
                                  spoke about the impact to Sandy City. Some of the issues that will be addressed during 
                                  this legislative session include the following: affordable housing, short-term rentals, the 
                                  Great Salt Lake, public safety, homelessness, and public notice requirements. Council 
                                  thanked Ms. Everton for her presentation and work on behalf of Sandy City.

                                  Council convened a recess at 7:14 pm.
                                  Council meeting reconvened at 7:20 pm.

4.         23-015                 City Council interviewing Dave Hart to serve as an Alternate Member of the 
                                  Sandy City Planning Commission
                   Attachments:   Council Powers.docx
                                  Planning Commission Role.docx
                                  PC RESOLUTION 23-02M Hart.doc

                                  Mayor Zoltanski introduced Mr. Dave Hart and Mr. Jim Edwards for Council consideration 
                                  and appointment to the position of Alternates on the Planning Commission. Mr. Hart is an 
                                  architect and has served as a planning commissioner for Salt Lake City. Mr. Edwards is 
                                  very active in the Sandy community and has a good overall pulse of the community. She 
                                  thanked the Council for their consideration of Mr. Hart and Mr. Edwards for the Planning 
                                  Commission appointments.

                                  Mr. David Hart provided information on his background and experience. He spoke about 
                                  buildings he has designed in the Salt Lake area. Council questions and comments 
                                  followed. 

                                  Mr. Jim Edwards spoke about his experience and knowledge of the city which he feels 
                                  would lend a good perspective on the planning commission. He has substantial 
                                  community involvement with the city over many years. Council questions and comments 
                                  followed. 

                                  This is an informational item. Council will consider these appointments for Advice and 
                                  Consent at the January 17, 2023 City Council meeting.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report


                              Dustin Fratto, Council Director, reviewed upcoming agenda items with the Council.

Council Member Business

                              Council Member Zach Robinson requested some information from Administration 
                              regarding some inquires he received from residents. The Arts Guild's upcoming 
                              production is Steele Magnolias and he invited the public to see the show. He also 
                              mentioned that Canyons School District has many community resources on their website 
                              that are available to the public. He also gave a shout-out to the Canyons Schools Drill 
                              Teams for their exceptional performances in a recent competition. 

                              Council Member Cyndi Sharkey attended the ribbon cutting and opening of Dollies Soup 
                              And Salad. She provided an update from the Legislative preview luncheon sponsored by 
                              the South Valley Chamber. She also spoke about a Metro Water project in the Willow 
                              Creek area which will help with the recharge of the natural aquifer and allow for more 
                              water storage.

Mayor's Report

                              Mayor Zoltanski expressed her gratitude for all the residents who attended this past 
                              Saturday's Legislative breakfast. It was a great exchange and represents the best of 
                              Sandy. She spoke about the public-private partnership to fund the ski buses up the 
                              canyons and provide this service to our community and visitors. Skiers can reserve a spot 
                              on the bus and the bus is the first one up after an avalanche is cleared. She encouraged 
                              the public to utilize this service as they head to our ski resorts.

CAO Report


                              Shane Pace, CAO, spoke about the employee engagement survey and provided an 
                              overview of the results to the Council. He is very pleased with the results and will send 
                              the summary to the Council for review.

                              Council moved to Item 6 on the agenda.

Adjournment
                              The Council meeting adjourned immediately following the closed session.
