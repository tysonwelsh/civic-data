Consideration to Remove Fencing Around Property Located at 5600 West 6015 South
Mayor Kelly Bush stated there had been multiple complaints on the K-Town
(Kearns Metro Township) Facebook page regarding the fencing located at 5600 West and 6015
South. This property belonged to the Utan Department of Transportation (UDOT), but Kearns
was renting it. Residents often complain that it is an eyesore, and it collects garbage that blows
up against it.
Council Member Snow stated she was inclined not to remove the fence because
doing so would result in people dumping trash, which was happening before.
Council Member Butterfield stated she felt the fence was not doing any good
anyway, and wondered if there might be another way to prevent dumping.
Mayor Bush stated a local landscaping company offered to monitor the area and
clean up the garbage.
The Council elected to bring this issue back to the agenda next month so it could
receive more suggestions from the community.

COOPERATION BETWEEN PUBLIC ENTITIES GOVERNING THE UNIFIED POLICE
DEPARTMENT OF GREATER SALT LAKE.

WHEREAS, Kearns is a member of the Unified Police Department (“UPD”), along with
the Town of Brighton, Copperton Metro Township, Emigration Canyon Metro Township,
Holladay City, Magna Metro Township, Midvale City Corp., City of Millcreek, and White
City Metro Township (collectively, the “Members”) and
WHEREAS, several of the Members as well as other entities no longer affiliated with the
UPD, established the UPD via interlocal agreement effective January 1, 2010, which was
also subsequently amended (“the 2010 Interlocal’); and
WHEREAS, the original membership of the UPD has changed over the course of its
history, and the cities of Bluffdale, Herriman, Riverton, and Taylorsville have exited UPD,
while Midvale, Millcreek, Brighton, Copperton, Emigration Canyon, Kearns, Magna, and
White City have joined UPD; and
WHEREAS, throughout these changes and since its creation in 2010, the UPD has been
a provider of law enforcement and related services to its member municipalities and
unincorporated Salt Lake County; and
WHEREAS, H.B. 35 Metro Township Modifications passed during the 2024 Utah
Legislative Session and is awaiting an anticipated signature by the Governor, which, if
signed, will convert Copperton, Emigration Canyon, Kearns, Magna, and White City from
metro townships into cities and towns on May 1, 2024; and
WHEREAS, in July of 2024, Salt Lake County will exit membership of the UPD and UPD
will no longer be rendering service to Salt Lake County to comply with the requirements
of H.B. 374, which the Utah Legislature passed during the 2023 Utah Legislative Session;
and
WHEREAS, the remaining UPD Members want to continue receiving law enforcement
and related services from UPD; and
WHEREAS, because of the significant changes in UPD’s membership, the upcoming
departure of Salt Lake County, the potential conversation of metro townships to cities and
towns, numerous amendments to the 2010 Interlocal, as well as lessons learned since its
creation in 2010, the Members agree that a revised and restated interlocal agreement is
the best means of setting forth the terms and conditions of the continued existence and
governance of the UPD; and

WHEREAS, the 2024 attached Revised and Restated Interlocal Agreement Between

Public Entities Governing the Unified Police Department of Greater Salt Lake (the “2024

Lease Agreement with Granite School District
Mayor Kelly Bush stated Kearns has moved forward with the lease for Oquirrh
Hills Elementary School. Five inspectors will examine the school from top to bottom tomorrow at
3:00. A list of required repairs will then be taken to the Granite School District. She has selected
two companies that will submit bids for the necessary repairs.
The proposed lease is based on $10 per square foot at 45,000 square feet, with a 95 percent
reduction, totaling $4,500 a month for 20 years with additional 10 year increments. Kearns would
be in full control of the building. There would be a clause that Kearns can pull out of the lease at
any time if there are any large maintenance issues. She noted there are other entities interested
in subletting the space from Kearns.
She received a request from Ben Horsley, Communications and Community Outreach Director,
Granite School District, that anyone who might know where the time capsule is located reach out
to him.
Mayor Bush asked the Council if she had authorization to proceed with what was discussed. The
Council stated she did.
