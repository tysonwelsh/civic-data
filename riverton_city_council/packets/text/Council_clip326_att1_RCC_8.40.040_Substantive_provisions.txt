RCC 8. 40. 040 Substantive provisions.

2) Specific Noise Restrictions ( h) Motor Vehicles


ix) Standing Motor Vehicles. No person shall operate nor shall any person cause,
allow, permit, or fail to control the operation of any motor vehicle to remain in an idling
mode or condition for:


     A) A consecutive period of 15 minutes or longer;


      B) Any period of time while the vehicle is left unattended; or

      C) So as to cause   a noise   disturbance.
