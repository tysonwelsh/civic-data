Public Works Director, Shane Paddock, reported that there is a specific dollar amount available
for Day Ranch Park and arena improvements. He presented the concept that was presented last
year with improvements proposed by staff. There was a desire to add the bleachers as part of the
initial phase. Bathroom locations within the arena were described. Mr. Paddock had spoken with
the City ' s bleacher representative who indicated that now is a bad time to purchase bleachers
because of steel prices. Currently, there is also a minimum seven-month build.

Mr. Paddock presented preliminary numbers and reported that the current seating capacity is 2,400,
which was exceeded this year at the Old West Days Rodeo. The estimated cost of 3,200 24-inch
tread bleachers was $910,000. Adding backs to the bleachers would add eight inches and cost
approximately $36,000. The cost includes the concrete under the bleachers. The angle frame
design is less costly, but the I-beam provides more space. According to the bleacher representative,
they can use a combination of the two, which would increase the functionality of the bleachers.
The approximate cost would be $80,000 with that number already having been included in the
aforementioned cost. Discussion ensued regarding the possibility of expanding the bleachers. This
type of bleacher allows for expansion upward for greater capacity. Mr. Reid commented on the
ability to add more sections as needed going forward in the future. They will add as much width
as possible to staii with and then move upward as needed. Mr. Paddock explained that the premium
seating will be on the lower portion at an additional cost of $90,000. They would, however,
provide more legroom and be very comfortable. This year with the Old West Days Rodeo, they
made ADA improvements, including ramps, paving, and seating.

The current proposal with the additional bathrooms, added bleachers to allow for a capacity of
3,500 seats and concessions at a cost of approximately $3 .79 million. Mr. Paddock stated that it
is possible to complete the project in phases. It was recommended that construction begin in
January for the bleachers to be ready for the rodeo in August. Timing issues were discussed. Dave
Kallas asked about the possibility of replacing the rotting wood benches with new wood, making
it last a little longer, and put more money toward the park to get that project completed since it is
used more frequently.

Council Member Aston agreed and considered Day Ranch Park to be the priority. With steel prices
being high she felt that delaying the project would be beneficial. Council Member Crockett asked
to see phasing options. Mr. Paddock agreed to prepare a Phasing Plan for the Council to review.
He agreed that waiting until next year would be beneficial. Because timing is an issue, a decision
would need to be made soon for a January start. Mr. Paddock reported that there are products that
can be used on the current wood bleachers to prevent splinters that would be much less costly than
replacing them.

Consent agenda item 3. 7 was next addressed. Dave Kallas asked for background information as
he was unable to access it in the packet. His understanding was that the desire was to change a
policy and suggested that there be more di scussion. Mr. Reid stated that the purpose is to allow a
former employee to train a new employee. Without this change, Mayor-Elect Natalie Hall is not
allowed to work until January 4, 2022. Mayor Timothy stated that the other issue is that the City
Council has not reviewed the resolution. As a result, it needs to be tabled.

Mark Hales commented that the issue of what is to happen after an election was not addressed.
Potential changes were discussed. Mr. Sheeran explained that Utah Code is not clear regarding
the timing between Election Day and when someone is sworn into office. Staff felt it would be
appropriate to clari fy that in the City policy. The proposed language specifies that an employee
may resume working the day after an election. If elected, an employee must resign their staff
position by Inauguration Day. The new language clarifies what an employee can do during that
time.

Administrative Services Director, Bruce Kaiichner explained that because Bluffdale is a small
city, one of the challenges is that when someone takes a leave of absence that is forced by Code,
they are without someone in that position for a period ohime. Not knowing what the outcome of
the election will be, they hold it open and do not fi ll it, which presents challenges. It would be
beneficial to allow Mayor-Elect Hall to work to fill the position while they search for a
replacement. He advocated for the change to allow work to be perfonned while simultaneously
looking for a replacement until Inauguration Day.

Jeff Gaston expressed concern with the Council voting on something that was not reviewed or
commented on by the public. He supported tabling the matter. Dave Kallas wanted to avoid the
appearance of non-transparency.
