Matters for Discussion

       To receive public input regarding the City of South Salt Lake proposed increase to
       property tax revenue.

       The South Salt Lake City tax on a $427,000 residence would increase from $521.37 to
       $626.34, which is $104.97 per year

       The South Salt Lake City tax on a $427,000 business would increase from $947.94 to
       $1,138.81, which is $190.87 per year.

       If the proposed budget is approved, South Salt Lake City would increase its property
       tax budgeted revenue by 20.21% above last year’s property tax budgeted revenue
       excluding eligible new growth.
