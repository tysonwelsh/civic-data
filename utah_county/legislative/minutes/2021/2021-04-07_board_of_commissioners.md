1. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER A PETITION TO VACATE A PORTION OF THE PLAT OF ELBERTA

TOWNSITE, SPECIFICALLY VACATING THE PORTION OF THE RIGHTS-OF-WAY IDENTIFIED AS

"WHITNEY AVENUE", "WILSON AVENUE", AND ALLEY RUNNING THROUGH BLOCK 43, PLAT OF

ELBERTA TOWNSITE, WHICH PORTION IS IMMEDIATELY ADJACENT TO LOTS 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 AND 9,

BLOCK 43, PLAT OF ELBERTA TOWNSITE, AS A DEDICATED PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY, PURSUANT TO

UTAH CODE SECTIONS 17-27A-208 AND 72-3-108. (PUBLIC HEARING SET FEBRUARY 24, 2021.)
-ROBERT J. MOORE, Attorney

BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATE - THIS IT OUT IN THE ELBERTA
AREA.

(Spoke about Public Hearing items #1 and #2 together)

IN THE ELBERTA AREA WE HAVE AN OLD TOWNSITE PLAT WHICH WAS RECORDED 100 YEARS
AGO IN THE EARLY 1900’S AND AS PART OF THIS TOWNSITE PLAT, IT WAS LAID OUT IN A
NUMBER OF BLOCKS, STREETS AND ALLEYS, MOST OF WHICH HAVE NEVER BEEN BUILT AND
WHAT WE FOUND IS THE PEOPLE THAT OWN THOSE LOTS HAVE CONSOLIDATED THEM INTO
OWNERSHIP THROUGH DEED AND PART OF THOSE DEEDS ALSO COVER THESE PUBLIC RIGHT-
OF-WAYS AS PER THE PLAT AND SO WE HAVE FOUND THAT MANY OF THE PEOPLE OUT THERE
ARE USING THEIR PROPERTY IN SUCH A WAY THAT THEY'RE NOT FOLLOWING THE ORIGINAL
OWNERSHIP LINES OF THE PLAT AND SO THE CORRECT WAY TO DO THAT IS TO DO A PLAT
AMENDMENT TO COMBINE THEIR LOTS. NOW IF THIS PLAT AMENDMENT INVOLVES
INCORPORATING SOME OF THESE RIGHT-OF-WAYS THAT HAVE NEVER BEEN BUILT THEN WE
HAVE TO GO THROUGH THIS VACATION PROCESS WHICH NECESSITATES THE PUBLIC HEARING
WHICH WE’RE HERE FOR TODAY.
