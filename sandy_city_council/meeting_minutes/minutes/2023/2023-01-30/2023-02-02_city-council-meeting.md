                                  The below minutes serve as a summary of the discussion, questions, and feedback that 
                                  occurred at the FY 24 Budget Retreat. Please reference the audio recording for a full 
                                  review of the day's presentations and discussions.

                                  9:00 am
                                  Council Chair Alison Stroud welcomed all those in attendance for the Sandy City FY 24 
                                  Budget Retreat.

                                  Eric Richards, Communications Director
                                  Mr. Richards introduced the Idea Incubator and ground rules which is a program 
                                  developed by Marc Fox, that encourages and promotes a collaborative approach for 
                                  today's budget retreat.

                                  Shane Pace, Chief Administrative Officer
                                  Mr. Pace welcomed everyone to the retreat. He reviewed the Vision statement for Sandy 
                                  City - "Connecting an active, vibrant, and prosperous community, where people flourish, 
                                  and you belong" and the Mission statement - "Sandy City strives to be responsible 
                                  stewards dedicated to providing high-value quality services, with an emphasis on safe 
                                  neighborhoods, smart planning, preservation, and economic diversity."

                   Dustin Fratto, Council Director, led the next workshop: Budget Prioritization Exercise.

                   3:17 pm
                   Closing Remarks
                   Mayor Monica Zoltanski thanked everyone for coming - we have a lot of shared priorities. 
                   She invited the Council to spend time with the employees out in the fields and to keep 
                   the budget conversation going. Administration will base the FY 24 tentative budget on the 
                   feedback and priorities gathered from today's FY 24 Budget Retreat.
