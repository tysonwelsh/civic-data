PRESENTATION:

SMI-4-2025
Spencer Hymas representing the property owner is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Quik Trip
7497 Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 5 acres. The proposed subdivision is located at
2155 South 3270 West and is zoned Manufacturing. Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented a proposal to subdivide a 5-acre property at 2155 South and 3270 West,
currently owned by Zion's Bank, into two lots. The larger lot of about 3.5 acres is intended for a Quick Trip
convenience store and gas station (which excites Brandon Hill). The plan includes shared access between the
two lots and potential modifications to landscaping along the rights-of-way. Steve noted that all
improvements are in place, with possible bonding for new drive approaches and removal of an old one. The
subdivision is described as straightforward, and the site plan will be processed as a permitted use.

There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting was adjourned
at 3:40 PM.
