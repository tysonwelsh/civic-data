Background


Riverton City code includes a Table of Commercial Uses in Section 18.90.010. The Table of Uses
lists many Land Uses and lists whether each use is Permitted (P), Not Permitted (NP), or Conditional
(C) in each of the six commercial zones. From time to time the Table of Commercial Uses is
reviewed and modified to meet the current needs of the City.

This amendment affects several areas in the Land Uses in the Table of Commercial Uses.

     1. Adding a Land Use Category for “Churches, religious and cultural buildings”. This same
        language appears in several of our Residential Zones as a “Conditional Use”, but it does not
        appear in our Commercial Table of Uses. The existing churches in Commercial zones were
        approved as conditional uses in other Land Use Categories which are similar to a church.
        The inconsistent categories creates confusion. Adding this Land Use Category unifies the
        city code across multiple zones, and eliminates confusion, but will not change how or where
        churches, religious and public assembly buildings are located. This use will be “Conditional”
        in all commercial zones.

     2. Change “Bed and Breakfast” Land Use Category to “Not Permitted” in all Commercial Zones.
        With the emergence of Short-Term Rentals as Conditional Use Home Occupations, the use
        of Bed and Breakfast in commercial zones can be eliminated. This creates a clear distinction
        between residential Short-Term Rentals, and Commercial Hotels.

     3. Eliminate four (4) categories that reference specific outdated activities that can reasonably
        be included in other existing categories.
            a. Apparel alteration and Shoe Repair
            b. Apparel and accessories retail and rental. (apparel, accessories, tailoring, fur,
                shoes).
            c. Artist and related services.
            d. Books, stationery, art/hobby supplies retail.

     4. Change permissions for “General retail and wholesale” to Conditional in the C-N and C-PO
        zones to add a layer of scrutiny to protect the character and placement of these zones.

     5. Change permissions for “Manufacturing, fabrication, and product assembly, internal use
        only” to Conditional in the C-R zone to expand available uses in the C-R Zone.

     6. Add text to clarify process for evaluating uses not listed in the Table of Commercial Uses
        including a process for interpreting and amending the table as required by Utah Code 10-20-
        507

18.90.005 Interpretation and Amendment

(1) Interpretation Authority
    The Zoning Administrator shall have the authority to interpret the Table of Commercial Uses as
    adopted in this Title. Such interpretations shall be made in a manner consistent with the intent of
    the zoning regulations, the Comprehensive Plan, and the purpose statements of the applicable
    zoning districts.
    (a) The Zoning Administrator may determine the appropriate classification of a use that is not
        explicitly listed in the Table of Commercial Uses by identifying a similar listed use based on
        characteristics including, but not limited to, scale, intensity, traffic generation, and operational
        impacts.
    (b) All interpretations shall be documented in writing and maintained as part of the official records of
        the municipality.
    (c) A use which is not listed in the Table of Commercial Uses, and which is determined by the
        Zoning Administrator to be unique in nature and not substantially similar to any listed use, shall
        be deemed Not Permitted (NP) within the applicable zoning district.
    (d) Interpretations made by the Zoning Administrator may be appealed in accordance with the
        procedures established in Section 2.80.50

(2) Petition for Amendment
        Any person, property owner, business entity, or authorized agent may petition to amend the
        Table of Commercial Uses.
    (a) Petitions shall be submitted in writing to the Planning Department on forms provided by the
        municipality and shall include:
            i. The proposed amendment or change;
           ii. A description of the use(s) affected;
          iii. Justification for the amendment, including consistency with the Comprehensive Plan; and
          iv. Any supporting documentation deemed necessary by the Planning Department.
    (b) Upon receipt of a complete application, the proposed amendment shall be reviewed in
        accordance with the procedures for zoning text amendments as set forth in this Title.
    (c) The Planning Commission shall review the petition and make a recommendation to the City
        Council.
    (d) The City Council shall have final authority to approve, approve with modifications, or deny the
        proposed amendment following a public hearing.

Utah Code


    Effective 11/6/2025
    10-20-507 Classification of new and unlisted business uses.
    (1) As used in this section:
      (a) "Classification request" means a request to determine whether a proposed business use
           aligns with an existing land use specified in a municipality's land use ordinances.
      (b) "New or unlisted business use" means a business activity that does not align with an existing
           land use specified in a municipality's land use ordinances.
    (2)
      (a) Each municipality shall incorporate into the municipality's land use ordinances a process for
           reviewing and approving a new or unlisted business use and designating an appropriate zone
           or zones for an approved use.
      (b) The process described in Subsection (2)(a) shall:
        (i) detail how an applicant may submit a classification request;
        (ii) establish a procedure for the municipality to review a classification request, including:
           (A) providing a land use authority with criteria to determine whether a proposed use aligns
                with an existing use; and
           (B) allowing an applicant to proceed under the regulations of an existing use if a land use
                authority determines a proposed use aligns with that existing use;
        (iii) provide that if a use is determined to be a new or unlisted business use:
           (A) the applicant shall submit an application for approval of the new or unlisted business use
                to the legislative body for review;
           (B) the legislative body shall consider and determine whether to approve or deny the new or
                unlisted business use; and
           (C) the legislative body shall approve or deny the new or unlisted business use, within a time
                frame the legislative body establishes by ordinance, if the applicant responds to requests
                for additional information within a time frame established by the municipality and appears
                at required hearings;
        (iv) provide that if the legislative body approves a proposed new or unlisted business use, the
              legislative body shall designate an appropriate zone or zones for the approved use; and
        (v) provide that if the legislative body denies a proposed new or unlisted business use, or if
              an applicant disagrees with the land use authority's classification of the proposed use, the
              legislative body shall:
           (A) notify the applicant in writing of each reason for the classification or denial; and
           (B) offer the applicant an opportunity to challenge the classification or denial through an
                administrative appeal process established by the municipality.
    (3) Each municipality shall amend each land use ordinance that contains a list of approved or
        prohibited business uses to include a reference to the process for petitioning to approve a new
        or unlisted business use, as described in Subsection (2).
