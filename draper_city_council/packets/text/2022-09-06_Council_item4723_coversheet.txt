Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: September 6, 2022
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1560 Comments: DR Horton has recently purchased land adjacent to the southern border of Draper City. They also own land in Lehi City directly across the municipal border. The City has been approached by DR Horton about adjusting the municipal boundary with Lehi City. The request would remove approximately 1,035.49 acres of land from Draper City and relocate the land into Lehi City.  DR Horton is looking to develop the 1,035.49 acres as part of a larger development taking place within Lehi City.
Per Utah State Code 10-2-419, Draper City approved Resolution 22-33 outlining its intent to adjust the common boundary between Draper City and Lehi City on June 21, 2022.
The approximately 1,035.49 acres of land does not have direct access to any existing road system or utility system within Draper City. Providing municipal services to the 1,035.49 acres may be more efficiently and economically provided by Lehi City.
In addition, DR Horton has proposed a 100.98 acre conservation easement along the north portion of the 1,035.49 acres in order to protect open space. This easement is on the agenda as Resolution 22-50.  The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. It is more efficient and economical for Lehi City to provide municipal services to the 1,035.49 Acres once they are developed.
2. The property owner has indicated a willingness to protect 100.98 Acres of open space via a conservation easement.
2. The City Council adopted a Resolution on June 21, 2022 outlining its interest in reviewing a municipal boundary adjustment with Lehi City.
3. This Ordinance complies with Utah State Code 10-2-419 requirements.
4. T he City has not received a written protest to the boundary adjustment by any title holder of any state-owned land or the Utah State Developmental Center Board as listed in State Code 10-2-419(5).
