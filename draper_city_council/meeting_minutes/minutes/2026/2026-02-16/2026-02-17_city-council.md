      Report: Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District
      John Richardson, Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District (JVWCD)
      Boardmember, introduced JVWCD Chief Operating Officer Shazelle Terry. Ms.
      Terry provided an update on current JVWCD projects and policies and
      answered questions from the Council. She reported that the levels of most
      reservoirs supplying Salt Lake County were approximately 10% lower than the
      same time the previous year. Ms. Terry also reported that a Drought
      Monitoring Committee, consisting of representatives from member agencies,
      would convene in the coming weeks. She stated that current conditions
      reflected extreme drought and reported that several years of normal
      conditions would be required for water levels to return to a comfortable
      position. Mayor Walker thanked Ms. Terry for the presentation.

      Discussion: Short Term Rentals
      Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky reported that a short-
      term rental was defined as a rental with occupancy of 30 days or less. She
      stated that she recommended the City allow apartment complexes to conduct

short-term rentals but not allow subleasing of apartments. The Council
discussed the possibility of imposing a three-night minimum stay
requirement.

Ms. Jastremsky stated that the short-term rental license application process
would collect owner information, urgent response contact information, a floor
plan, and parking arrangements. She reported that license restrictions would
include maximum occupancy based on square footage, noise standards, and
a minimum urgent response time.

The Council discussed adding a provision allowing a license or permit to be
revoked through Code Enforcement if a rental property was out of compliance.
Ms. Jastremsky reported that enforcement in Draper was complaint-based.
Chief Ferguson stated that the Police Department received complaint calls
when parties were held in short-term rentals. Councilmember F. Lowry stated
that he believed short-term rental restrictions were necessary and thanked
Ms. Jastremsky for helping move the item forward.

Discussion: Legislative Update
City Manager Mike Barker reported that, as of that morning, more than 900
bills were under consideration during the Legislative Session. Mayor Walker
stated that he met with Senator McKay to discuss property tax bills and
reported there was pressure to abolish property taxes. The Mayor also stated
that municipal fund balances might be restricted to 25%, compared to the
current 35%. He provided an update on several bills that could impact Draper
City, including proposed restructuring of UTA.

Discussion: Phebe Brown Trail
The Council did not discuss this item.

Discussion: City Commissions and Committees
The Council discussed the role and scope of the Tree Committee.
Councilmember T. Lowery stated that the Tree Committee was capable of
addressing more than trees and suggested that the term “landscape” would
be more appropriate. She reported that public education should be the
committee’s top priority. The name Trees, Landscape, and Beautification
Committee was suggested as a better description of the committee’s role.
