CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2578, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
CKC ASPHALT FOR RANGE GRADING AT THE SHOOTING SPORTS PARK

Public Works Director Ron Whitehead explained that CKC Asphalt has been selected as
the lowest responsive bidder to provide range grading services at the Shooting Sports Park.
The County Road department was able to move a large amount of dirt near the Purgatory
Correctional Facility to the Shooting Sports Park in order to help save costs for the project.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2578, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington County and
CKC Asphalt for Range Grading at the Shooting Sports Park. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2579, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
BRADCO BUILDERS FOR CONSTRUCTING A MAKERSPACE ADDITION TO
THE WASHINGTON LIBRARY BUILDING

Washington County Library Director Joel Tucker explained that the Washington County
Library systems desires to construct and operate a Makerspace at the Washington City
branch. A Makerspace allows members of the public to create things ranging from wood
working, 3D printing, sewing, and videos. In order to create a functioning space, an
addition needs to be added onto the existing building. It is recommended for Bradco
Buildings to complete the project.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-
2579, A Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington
County and Bradco Builders for Constructing a Makerspace Addition
to the Washington Library Building. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2580, A RESOLUTION
MODIFYING THE MANNER _ OF SELECTING MEMBERS TO THE

ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD

Chairman Iverson explained that there has been an ongoing conversation between the
Special Service Districts along the SR-18 corridor regarding emergency services and how
that can be improved in the area moving forward.

The Dammeron Valley Special Service District has recently experienced a significant
turnover in their board members. Previously, Board members have been established via the
election process. The Commission has determined that it will be more beneficial for the
district to become an appointed board rather than an elected Board and will oversee the
Board until new members are appointed.

Commissioner Cox expressed gratitude for those that have served on the board and stated
that it is his hope that this will be a beneficial option for the SSD moving forward.

Gabriel Bilek, Clerk of the SSD Board explained that Amanda Baliff and Denise Stewart
have resigned from the Board. The remaining Board members advertised for the vacancies
and received several qualified applications for the vacant positions. Mr. Bilek questioned
the best way for the Board to move forward.

Chairman Iverson explained that if the resolution passes, the Board would dissolve entirely
and be reappointed via the County Commission.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2580, A
Resolution Modifying the Manner of Selecting Members to Dammeron
Valley Fire Special Service District’s Administrative Control Board.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox

voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Almquist reported that he and Commissioner Iverson attended the National
Association of Counties (NACo) Conference in Washington DC during the first week of
March.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 11:33 a.m.
