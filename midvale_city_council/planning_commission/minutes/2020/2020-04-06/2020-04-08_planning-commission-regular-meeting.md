PUBLIC HEARINGS

   1. CUP/SUB-22-30-403-049; LENNON LANE SUBDIVISION; 455 E 7500 S;
      CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT AND PRELIMINARY SUBDIVISION PLAT FOR
      FLAG LOT SUBDIVISION WITH TWIN HOMES ON 6 RESIDENTIAL LOTS;
      SF-1 ZONE WITH DUPLEX OVERLAY (SF-1/DO); GARY CANNON
      (APPLICANT).

Ms. Jana Ward presented that the applicant, Gary Cannon, is requesting a conditional use permit
and preliminary subdivision plat approval to reconfigure an existing parcel located at 455 East
7500 South into a 6-lot subdivision with three new twin homes: one occupying the rear lots and
two on the front lots. This proposal is a revised proposal of the previous Lund Lane subdivision
and conditional use permit application that requested 3 lots in 2019.

The parcel is approximately 0.64 acres with 138 feet of frontage along 7500 South. The property
is zoned Single Family Residential with Duplex Overlay (SF-1/DO). There is an existing single-
family home and detached garage on the existing lot. This proposal will require the demolition
and removal of the existing home and detached garage. All new development will be required to
comply with the standards of the SF-1/DO zone.

The subject properties are surrounded by other property in the SF-1/DO zone, developed with
single family residential dwellings. The properties directly south are in Sandy City and are also
zoned residential.

ORDINANCE CONSIDERATIONS:

Conditional Use Permit for Twin Homes and Flag Lot

One of the proposed twin homes are located on flag lots, therefore the following requirements
must also be satisfied. These requirements should be stated on the plat and will be verified
through Building Permit reviews in the future.

   1. Setbacks: Setbacks for new development on a flag lot are fifteen feet (15’) for each of the
      front, side and rear yards.
   2. Height: The maximum proposed structure is twenty-five feet (25’).
   3. Stories: A structure shall not exceed one (1) story.
   4. Parking/driveway access: Each flag lot shall provide two parking spaces on the side or
      rear yard of the property. The driveway shall have a paved width of twenty feet (20’).
   5. Fire department access: Each dwelling must adhere to the Fire Code.

No direct conflicts with the applicable provisions of this title, State, or Federal Law have been
identified with this proposal, although some minor changes will need to be made to fully satisfy
all development requirements. The proposed structures comply with the applicable Development
Standards, including setbacks, build-to line, height, parking requirements, and utilities for both
standard lots and flag lots. The following items will need to be addressed as part of the
development process to guarantee compliance with the applicable standards of the SF-1/DO
zone:

The Planning Commission typically requires a sight-obscuring fence be constructed around flag
lots to minimize the potential for conflict with existing neighbors, unless the property owners
come to a written agreement otherwise. Staff recommends including this condition, with the
caveat that any existing fences complying with the condition are allowed to remain and adding
this as a note on the plat for fencing to be installed when a house is constructed.

This project is in an area developed on a scale similar to the proposal, so no adverse impacts are
expected to arise due to compatibility issues. The flag lot requirements limit the new structure to
1 story with 15’ setbacks, minimizing the potential for conflict.

Sufficient utility capacity will need to be verified and guaranteed through provision of the water
and sewer duty to serve letters required by the subdivision process. Water service for the project
is provided by Midvale City Public Utilities and sewer service is provided by Midvalley

impacts to traffic or off-site lighting impacts are expected to result from this project.

Subdivision:

The proposed subdivision includes six lots: two base lots and one flag lot, each divided into two
parcels to accommodate a twin home.

With the exceptions listed below, the proposed subdivision complies with the design standard
requirements of Title 16 Subdivisions. The lots have been verified to conform with the SF-1/DO
zone requirements; no new streets are proposed; and curb, gutter, and sidewalk are existing along
7500 South.

      final subdivision plat.

   2. CUP-21-36-477-006; 2 MECHANICS AUTO REPAIRS; 8452 S STATE ST;
      CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT FOR VEHICLE REPAIR; STATE STREET
      ZONE (SSC); MIGUEL DIAZ/2 MECHANICS AUTO REPAIR (APPLICANT).

Ms. Jana Ward presented that Mr. Miguel Diaz is requesting approval of a conditional use permit
to establish and operate a vehicle repair business in an existing commercial building located at
8452 S State Street. The parcel is 1.56 acres and contains three tenant spaces. The property
location is in the State Street (SSC) zone.

The applicant proposes to occupy a 3,200 ft² tenant space with three bays for auto repair. There
will be two employees and hours of operation are proposed to be 8:45 AM to 5:45 PM, Monday
through Friday. The back portion of the property has a conditional use permit to operate a
vehicle repair shop. The front tenant is currently a flooring wholesaler.

The subject property is adjacent to other State Street (SCC) zoned properties to the north and
south, Sandy City commercial zone to the east and Copperview Elementary to the west.

ORDINANCE CONSIDERATIONS:

The State Street (SCC) zone allows vehicle repair uses under a conditional use permit, provided
the Planning Commission concludes that the application fully mitigates all identified adverse
impacts and complies with the following general standards:

   1. The application complies with all applicable provisions of this title, State and Federal
      Law;

PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION APRIL 8TH, 2020
   2. The structures associated with the use are compatible with surrounding structures in
       terms of use, scale, mass and circulation;
   3. The use is not detrimental to the public health, safety and welfare;
   4. The use is consistent with the Midvale City General Plan, as amended;
   5. Traffic conditions are not adversely affected by the proposed use including the existence
       of or need for dedicated turn lanes, pedestrian access, and capacity of the existing streets;
   6. Sufficient utility capacity;
   7. Sufficient emergency vehicle access;
   8. Location and design of off-street parking as well as compliance with off-street parking
       standards provided for in §17-7-13.8;
   9. Fencing, screening, and landscaping to separate the use from adjoining uses and mitigate
       the potential for conflict in uses;
   10. Compatibility of the proposed mass, bulk, design, orientation, and location of the
       structures on the site; including compatibility with buildings on adjoining lots and to the
       street;
   11. Exterior lighting that complies with the lighting standards of the zone and is designed to
       minimize conflict and light trespass with surrounding uses; and
   12. Within and adjoining the site, impacts on the aquifer, slope retention, and flood potential
       have been fully mitigated and the proposed structure is appropriate to the topography of
       the site.

Staff has not identified any apparent conflicts with provisions of the zoning ordinance or state
and federal law. The business will be conducted within the existing development and tenant
space. No modifications to the building or site are proposed to be made. If any future changes to
the site or building are proposed, another review will be required. To comply with other
requirements of the Midvale City Municipal Code, a building permit for tenant improvements
may be required and a business license will need to be obtained. Staff recommends including
these two items as conditions of approval.

No apparent concerns regarding public health, safety, or welfare have been identified.

For vehicle repair uses, the minimum parking requirement is 1 space per employee and 4 spaces
per service bay. The applicant intends to have 2 employees and operate with the existing 3
service bays, resulting in a minimum of 14 parking stalls required. A prior conditional use permit
for a vehicle repair use on the property requires an additional 15 stalls, in addition to stalls
required for current and future permitted uses. The submitted site plan shows 49 parking stalls,
which would leave 20 stalls to be distributed between uses in the remaining buildings on the
property. However, the stalls are not drawn to scale and will not fit on the property once the
proper dimensions are applied. Given the size of the property, Staff believes the required stall
counts can be provided and recommends requiring the applicant to prepare a revised parking
plan, drawn to scale, showing an accurate count of the available parking area.

The proposed use is adjacent to other commercially zoned properties, with Copperview
Elementary directly west across the canal. No concerns regarding compatibility with surrounding
structures; public health, safety, and welfare; traffic; utility capacity; emergency vehicle access;
conflicts with adjacent uses; or impacts to the aquifer, slopes, flood potential, or topography have
been identified.
