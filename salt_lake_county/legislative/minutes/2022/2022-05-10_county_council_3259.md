A PROCLAMATION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
RECOGNIZING PEACE OFFICERS MEMORIAL DAY AND 
NATIONAL POLICE WEEK 2022
WHEREAS, May 15, 2022, has been proclaimed throughout the country as 
Peace Officers Memorial Day and May 11 through May 17, 2022, as National 
Police Week to honor police officers and to remember those killed in the line 
of duty; and
WHEREAS, one of the most important roles of government is to provide 
public safety, and here in Salt Lake County we rely on our dedicated law 
enforcement officers to keep our neighborhoods, schools, families, and 
public places safe, to uphold the rule of law and to respond in times of crisis; 
and
WHEREAS, unfortunately, since the first recorded death in 1786, more than

23,000 law enforcement officers in the United States have been killed in the 
line of duty, including 147 officers here in Utah.  Through these sacrifices, 
we are especially mindful of all those who have been killed or disabled in the 
line of duty.  These heroes and their families will forever be in our thoughts 
and prayers; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County will continue to promote and protect our law 
enforcement officers and we shall ensure they always have the necessary 
resources to protect our families and communities.  These local guardians of 
peace never falter in the face of danger, so we must never waver in our 
support for them; and 
WHEREAS, in honor of Peace Officers Memorial Day and Police Week, 
we express our deepest gratitude to all law enforcement officers in Salt Lake 
County, Utah, and throughout the country; 
Now, Be It Resolved:
The Salt Lake County Council does hereby call upon all citizens of Salt 
Lake County to honor our peace officers that fearlessly uphold our laws 
and place themselves in the line of fire to safeguard our communities 
and families.  We owe them our endless support for the service they have 
given to Salt Lake County.

22-0443
Attachments: Staff Report
Juneteenth Holiday Amended Ordinance
ORDINANCE NO. 1896 
ESTABLISHING THE DATE ON WHICH THE JUNETEENTH 
INDEPENDENCE DAY HOLIDAY IS OBSERVED
AN ORDINANCE OF THE LEGISLATIVE BODY OF SALT LAKE 
COUNTY, UTAH, AMENDING SECTION 1.06.020 OF THE SALT 
LAKE COUNTY CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2001, ENTITLED 
“HOLIDAYS” ESTABLISHING THE DATE ON WHICH THE 
JUNETEENTH INDEPENDENCE DAY HOLIDAY IS OBSERVED. 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows: 
SECTION I.  The amendments made herein are designated by underlining the 
new substituted words.  Words being deleted are designated by brackets and 
strike-through.
SECTION II.  Section 1.06.020, of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances, 2001 is hereby amended to read as follows: 
1.06.020 - Holidays.
A.  The following days are legal holidays during which County offices are 
closed:
The First Day of January - New Years Day
The Third Monday of January - Martin Luther King Day
The Third Monday of February - President’s Day
The Last Monday in May - Memorial Day
Page 5 of 11
County Council Meeting Minutes May 10, 2022
The Nineteenth Day of June - Juneteenth Independence Day
The Fourth Day of July - Independence Day
The Twenty-fourth Day of July - Pioneer Day
The First Monday of September - Labor Day
The Eleventh Day of November - Veteran’s Day
The Fourth Day of November - Thanksgiving
The Fourth Friday of November - Day After Thanksgiving
The Twenty-fifth day of December - Christmas Day
B.  In the event that a holiday falls on a Saturday, it shall be observed on the 
preceding Friday.  In the event that a holiday falls on a Sunday, it shall be 
observed on the following Monday.  If June 19 is on a Tuesday, Wednesday, 
Thursday, or Friday, the Juneteenth Independence Day holiday shall be 
observed on the immediately preceding Monday.  If June 19 is on a Saturday 
or Sunday, the Juneteenth Independence Day holiday shall be observed on 
the immediately following Monday.
C.  Where public business requires, some County offices may be required to 
remain open for business on legal holidays.
SECTION III.  This ordinance shall become effective fifteen (15) days after 
its passage and upon at least one publication in a newspaper published and 
having general circulation in Salt Lake County.
