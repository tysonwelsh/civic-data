1. Discussion of proposed Code amendments for setbacks from streams and wetlands – 
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Planner Milliner presented the staff report and recalled that the issue of setbacks from streams, 
lakes, and ponds came up at a public hearing with the County Council, where people requested 
that the Council look at possible amendments to the Code regarding setbacks.  Council directed 
Staff to look at amending the Code to allow people to do things on their property that may not be 
allowed currently in the Development Code.  The current setbacks are 40 feet from a wetland, 
100 feet from a stream, 150 feet from East Canyon Creek, and 100 feet from lakes and ponds.  
Staff has created definitions of ponds, lakes, irrigation canals, streams, ditches, etc., and defined 
what the requirements would apply to.  The new setbacks would apply to rivers, perennial 
streams, intermittent streams, lakes, natural ponds, and wetlands, and they would not apply to 
canals, ditches, irrigation ditches, streams in a culvert, reservoirs, artificial ponds, isolated 
wetlands, and ephemeral streams.  He discussed riparian areas and explained that they have been 
grouped into three categories.  Area A would be 50 feet back from the high water line, and very 
limited activities, such as maintenance and removal and planting of trees, activities allowed by 
the Army Corps of Engineers, etc., would be allowed in that area.  Area B would be between 50
