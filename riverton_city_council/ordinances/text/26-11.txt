                                           RIVERTON    CITY, UTAH
                                           ORDINANCE NO. 26- 11


   AN ORDINANCE REZONING . 49 ACRES LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
 NORTHWEST CORNER OF TRESTLE LANE AND 13400 SOUTH FROM RR-22 R-4
  AND AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN DESIGNATION TO MEDIUM DENSITY
                            RESIDEDNTIAL, DAVID KING - APPLICANT.


  WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
   recommendation regarding the above listed zoning and general plan amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map R-4 and the General Plan Designation to Medium
Density Residential for the 0. 49- acre parcel( s) located at approximately the northwest corner of
Trestle Lane and 13400 South.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
                  Section 1.                 The Riverton City Zoning Map and General Plan shall be,
                                             and hereby is, amended to reflect the described changes as
                                             shown in Exhibit " A"


                  Section   2.               This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
