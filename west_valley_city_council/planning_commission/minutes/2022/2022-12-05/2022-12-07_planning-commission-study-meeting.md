ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-5-2022  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to amend Sections 7-6-301 and 7-6-308 of the
           zoning ordinance by adding use restrictions and design standards to the BRP
           (Business/Research Park) zone. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve Pastorik stated that this proposal is a request from the City to amend a couple of
                  sections in the code regarding the BRP (Business/Research Park) zone. Steve reviewed
                  the proposed additions to the standards.

                 Overhead doors shall not be oriented toward or facing High-Image Arterial Streets as
                  listed in Section 7-10-102 including all of Lake Park Blvd.
                 No more than 60 percent of the square footage of Buildings on a BRP Lot shall be used
                  as a Warehouse.
                 No more than 60 percent of the square footage of Buildings on a BRP Lot shall be used
                  as Light Industrial.
                 All Warehouses in the BRP zone shall meet the Commercial Design Standards in Part 3
              of Chapter 7-11.

              Commissioner McEwen pointed out that it would be challenging to meet the commercial
              design standards on the side of the building that has overhead doors. He asked if there
              would be any leeway on that side of the building. Steve said that only the sides visible
              from the street need to meet the commercial design standards, so there would be some
              leeway.


ZT-6-2022     Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to add restrictions on the use of lawn in landscaping
              to Chapter 7-16 of the zoning ordinance. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
              steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that the state legislature passed House Bill 121, a lawn
              replacement program, earlier this year. He stated that the legislature appropriated five
              million dollars for this program and those monies should be available in the spring of
              2023.

              Steve noted that two standards need to be met to be eligible to apply for the lawn
              replacement program. First, lawn would be prohibited in park strips and areas less than
              ten feet. Second, no more than thirty-five percent of the front and side yard landscaped
              area shall be lawn.

              Steve stated that the City Council wants West Valley City to be eligible to apply for the
              lawn replacement program and has asked us to move forward with this proposal.

              Commissioner McEwen noted that the standards looked to apply only to residential
              developments. He asked Steve if businesses would also be eligible to apply for this
              program. Steve said he also had that question and was informed that even though the
              standards apply only to residential, there is a connection permitting anyone to use. He
              noted that he isn’t sure how that connection is made.

              Commissioner McEwen asked if a contractor is building a new subdivision, would they
              be eligible to apply for the program? Steve explained that where this is a lawn
              replacement program, it would not apply to new construction. He noted that typically the
              homeowner is responsible for the landscaping with a new subdivision.


ZT-7-2022     Petition by CABCO 5600 LLC an amendment to Section 7-14-404 of the zoning
              ordinance to change the location of the restrictions on automotive sales, automobile
              service, and automotive parts sales from between 2455 South and 3100 South to
              between the Riter Canal and 3100 South. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
              steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik said the area in question is within the 5600 West Overlay Zone, which
              prohibits auto-related uses. He stated that the applicant proposes to leave the vocabulary
              for the restrictions of the Overlay Zone as is but move the restricted area from 2455
              South to 3100 South to 3100 South to the Ritter Canal. Steve noted that the applicant
              feels this would be a positive change because, generally speaking, the Ritter Canal is the
              dividing line between retail and industrial uses.
               Commissioner Lovato asked if this proposal would be more challenging where the
               subject location has manufacturing and commercial zones. Steve explained that the
               property owner requested to change the zone to C-2 (General Commercial) on the front
               portion of the property to allow medical office uses. He pointed out that most of the
               property behind the subject location has storage units used for storage, so there should
               not be any issues.


ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-11-2022 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone change from M (Manufacturing)
          to BRP (Business/Research Park). The property is located within the Lake Park
          Corporate Centre on 384.8 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
          steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal is a request from the City. He stated that Lake
               Park Corporate Center, the subject property, contains both the M (Manufacturing) and the
               BRP (Business/Research Park) zones. Steve stated that this proposal would rezone all M
               zones within Lake Park to BRP, and the uses allowed in the BRP zone are more
               restrictive than those of the M zone. Steve pointed out that rezoning this property would
               keep it more aligned with the General Plan. He said that most of the subject property is
               already developed for office uses, and this proposal would protect that property.

               Steve explained that when the park was established, use restrictions were applied.
               Suburban Land Reserve developed Lake Park and held a controlling interest in deciding
               what uses would be permitted. Steve said they have recently sold the property, and a
               Board now makes land use decisions, so there is more of a possibility that the land uses
               will become less restrictive.

               Steve noted that he had received emails from property owners within Lake Park, and the
               property owner of the Home 2 Suites Hotel and the two adjacent parcels had expressed
               some concerns. The property owner is considering building a restaurant with a drive-
               through; drive-throughs are not permitted within the BRP zone. Steve informed the
               Commission that he would not suggest they look to make any changes to satisfy this one
               business owner. However, if the Commission is concerned about drive-throughs not
               being permitted to be used within the BRP zone, there are ways to address this issue. One
               way would be to exclude specific properties from the rezoning. Steve said that the staff is
               not in favor of this option. He stated another option would be to add this land use into
               ZT-5-2022, which is on this agenda.

               Commissioner McEwen said that the pandemic had increased the demand for drive-
               through options, and he feels it warrants a discussion.

            Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 7 lots on 1.9 acres. The subdivision is
            located at approximately 3789 South 5200 West and is zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit
            Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff – Steve Lehman at
            801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

               with three of the lots being flag lots. He pointed out that there is an existing home on the

               Council has condemned the use of private streets, it was challenging to develop a design

               Steve noted that the curb cut still needs to be added; however, all other improvements are
               in place along 5200 West. He mentioned that the applicant had already been granted an

               Steve stated that the fence would need to be moved and relocated. He said he had not had
               the opportunity to discuss this issue with Mr. Milne. However, he will follow up with him
               before the public hearing. He believes that the applicant will put up fencing around the
               proposed project.
