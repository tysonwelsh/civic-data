       a. Under current conditions, conducting an Orem City Planning Commission meeting with an anchor
           location presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the anchor
           location.
       b. This determination is based on the facts that 1) COVID-19 is a worldwide pandemic threatening the
           health and safety of the residents of Orem; 2) the State of Utah has determined that people should
           maintain social distancing of at least six feet to help mitigate the spread of COVID-19; and 3) the City of

           convene at the usual anchor location – the Orem City council Chambers.

Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to amend the existing final plat. The biggest change proposed is to
create limited common areas that would function as a private backyard for each unit. Previously, each backyard was
held in common with the entire subdivision. This change would allow each property owner to be able to construct a
fence in their backyard if so desired.

A few other minor changes are proposed as well. Two lots will be removed in order to increase the size of a few
building pads and backyards in the subdivision. Some minor changes to the utilities are proposed as well.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City Code.
Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Abbey Road Phase 1 Subdivision, approve the final plat of Abbey
Road Phase 1, Amended and approve the final plat of Abbey Road Phase 2 Subdivision located at 759 East
Timpanogos Parkway in the PRD zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding, and there were none.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to introduce himself. Roger Dudley, Dudley & Associates, Inc., introduced
himself. He said in the sales process, they have found that people like their own back yards with fences instead of it
being an all-common area and they also like three car garages.

Chair Cochran then opened the public hearing and invited those who wanted to make a comment on this item to send
in their comments via chat.

When there were no comments/chats, Chair Cochran closed the public hearing and asked if the Planning Commission
had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Ms. Bentley moved to vacate Abbey Road Phase 1 Subdivision, approve the final
plat of Abbey Road Phase 1, Amended and approve the final plat of Abbey Road Phase 2 Subdivision located at 759
East Timpanogos Parkway in the PRD zone. Ms. Jensen seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley,
Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts and Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.3:

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by Skyline Development for the Planning Commission to vacate Lot 1 of Northgate
Village Subdivision, Plat D and approve Villa D’Este II Condominiums, Plat A located generally at 1140 West 950
North in the PD22 zone.

                                       Staff Presentation: The site plan to construct one hundred condominium
                                       units in five new buildings for this location was approved by the Planning
                                       Commission on February 3, 2021. A zoning text amendment to allow this site
                                       to be a residential only land use instead of mixed use was approved by the
                                       Planning Commission and then the City Council on September 29, 2020.
                                       This plat amendment will vacate the plat that allowed a church to be built on
                                       this property and create a plat for the condominiums to be built on this site.

This plat will be for Building A which will be the first of the five buildings. This plat will be the only one to come
before the Planning Commission as it is vacating the original plat. Subsequent plats will be supplemental plats to
this one and will not need Planning Commission approval.

Building A will be four floors high with four units per floor. Total square footage for the building is 15,000 square
feet.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Lot 1 of Northgate Village Subdivision, Plat D and
approve Villa D’Este II Condominiums, Plat A located generally at 1140 West 950 North in the PD22 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding, and there were none.

Chair Cochran then opened the public hearing and invited those who wanted to make a comment on this item to send

had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Ms. Jensen moved to vacate Lot 1 of Northgate Village Subdivision, Plat D and

Cochran seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Marisa Bentley, Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen,
Barry Roberts and Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Harding to introduce Agenda Item 3.5:

Agenda Item 3.5 is a request by Development Services for the Planning Commission to amend Article 22-5-3(A)
and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of approximately 4.00 acres from the NVD-Res-B
zone to the R8 zone located generally at 1637 North to 1753 North along 400 West.

                                             Staff Presentation: The residents of the homes along the west side of
                                             400 West from 1657 North to 1753 North have requested that the City
                                             rezone their properties from the NVD-Res-B zone to the R8 zone. This
                                             would be a reversal of the zoning that took place approximately three
                                             years ago.

                                               Each property owner except one has provided a signed letter requesting
                                               the rezone. The one who did not provide a letter is the southernmost
property included in this rezone request. It is owned by the same owner of the commercial properties to the south and
west.

Staff held a zoom neighborhood meeting on February 9, 2021. There were about 30 residents in attendance. The
neighborhood was in favor of the proposed rezone.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of
Orem by changing the zone of approximately 4.00 acres from the NVD-Res-B zone to the R8 zone located generally
at 1637 North to 1753 North along 400 West.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Harding. Ms. Bentley said the Abrahams
house is not one of these applicants, can his neighbors have his house rezoned. Mr. Harding said the City has the right
to rezone any property as long as they go through the right procedure. Mr. Cochran asked if changing this area back
to the R8 zone creates additional hardship for adjacent properties. Mr. Bench said there are now two standards for
setbacks that developers will have to meet. It will either be seventy-five feet back with the thirty-five height cap or
the height of the building, not both added together.

Chair Cochran then opened the public hearing and invited those who wanted to make a comment on this item to send
in their comments via chat. Six comments were received and read aloud (see attached).
