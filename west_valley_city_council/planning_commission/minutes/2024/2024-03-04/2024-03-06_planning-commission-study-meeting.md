CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-3-2024  Petition by MOCA, LLC requesting conditional use approval for an Industrial
          Office/Warehouse Business Park. The property is located at 6507 West 5400 South on
          27.18 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or
          jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp stated that this request includes two parcels totaling 27.18 acres. She
              pointed out that further down on the agenda, Steve Lehman will be presenting the
              subdivision for this property. She pointed out that the property is in a gravel pit
              area, so there are significant grade changes; however, they do not affect this
              application.
              Jody explained that the applicant proposes adding two warehouse buildings, with
              thirteen to fourteen percent office space. She stated that the office parking area
              would be on the east side with bay doors and truck parking on the west. Jody
              noted that they are proposing the access as a private drive.
              Jody stated that the proposed buildings are typical office/warehouse buildings
              with tilt-up concrete. She reviewed the proposed materials and noted that both
               buildings are designed similarly. Jody pointed out that they do have access on a
               high-image arterial street, so they would need to add the required landscaping for
               a high-image arterial on both 5400 South and the Mountain View Corridor. Jody
               said they would need fifty percent live plant material in all landscaped areas. She
               pointed out where the applicant needs to add landscaping to meet the 7-10
               requirements. Jody said that the applicant is not proposing any signage and would
               need to return to the Commission if they decide to add a pole sign. She noted that
               she suggested they put in an entrance feature. Jody presented images and pointed
               out where to add more plant material.
               Commissioner Winters asked if the Commission was reviewing this property
               because the City had changed the zoning. Jody said the Commission is reviewing
               this application because the subject property is over 10 acres and in the
               manufacturing zone.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
PUD-1-2024 Petition by DANIEL BALSAM with Alpine Homes is requesting preliminary
           subdivision approval for the Kiowa Court PUD. The subdivision will consist of 21 units
           on 4.3 acres. The subdivision is located at 5143 West 3500 South and is zoned RM
           (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
           steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman noted that this property should look familiar to the Commission.
               Chris Clifford came before the Commission last year proposing to rezone this
               property to RM for a twin-home development. Steve stated that it was approved
               with a development agreement. He explained that UDOT owns 3500 South, so the
               applicant will work with them on the proposed access off 3500 South that would
               align with the one across the street. Steve presented the site plan and noted that
               the applicant is in the process of changing from a twin home to a single-family
               detached development. Steve mentioned that the staff are in favor of that change.
               Steve noted that the street running through the development would end in a cul-
               de-sac. He pointed out that the setback of the proposed home adjacent to 3500
               South would need to be increased. Steve mentioned that the applicant is proposing
               a sidewalk around the perimeter of the development as an amenity.
               Chair Fuller said it is a single-family development in the RM zone, allowing
               smaller setbacks between homes. Steve said that was correct. The applicant is
               proposing ten feet between each house. Chair Fuller asked if the orange boxes on
               the site plan indicate a playground area. Steve said yes, they are required to have
               some amenities. He explained that they are proposing a walking path around the
               perimeter of the development, a playground area, and a pavilion area. He said the
               amenities still need to be reviewed before the final plat to ensure they meet the
               requirements outlined in the development agreement.
               Commissioner Winters feels this would be a great addition to that area.
S-1-2024       Petition by BRYAN AND CHANTEL BRADFORD requesting minor subdivision
               approval for the Bradford Estates Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 3 lots on
               .70 acres. The subdivision is located at 3816 South 3200 West and is zoned R-1-8 (Single
               Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff – Steve Lehman at
               801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman said the subject property had been rezoned to R-1-8 with a
               development agreement. He noted that an existing home on the property will
               remain; however, the detached garage will be removed. Steve stated that the
               development agreement states what needs to be done to clean up and maintain the
               property as well as the existing home: the existing detached garage needs to be
               removed, a new one built, a second single-family home constructed, and a set-
               back exception.
               Steve stated that this proposal is to subdivide the property into three lots for two
               single-family homes.


SMI-1-2024     Petition by REID WINTERSTEEN, representing MOCA LLC, is requesting minor
               subdivision approval for the MOCA Mountain View Business Park. The subdivision will
               consist of 2 lots on 27 acres. The subdivision is located at 6507 West 5400 South and is
               zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
               steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman noted that this item correlates with the conditional use application
               presented by Jody Knapp at the start of the meeting. He explained that this
               proposal is to subdivide the property into two lots. Steve stated that minor
               improvements were installed along 5400 South. Steve explained that provisions
               would be made to the subdivision plat accounting for the access to Rocky
               Mountain Power’s property. He stated that we are proposing a temporary turn-
               around be placed on the adjacent Geneva property due to the length of the subject
               property. Steve noted that there has been mention of connecting the two
               properties in the future.
               Commissioner Layton asked if the Mountain View Corridor could access the
               property in question. Steve said that there isn’t a connection between the two.
