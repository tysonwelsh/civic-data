Repeal of Storm Water Fee   

The Council reviewed the following Resolution repealing Title 17, Article VII of the Copperton Metro Township Code Regarding stormwater facilities impact fees:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-06-02   Date: June 19, 2019

A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL REPEALING TITLE 17, ARTICLE VII OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP CODE REGARDING STORMWATER FACILITIES IMPACT FEES

WHEREAS, Article VII of Title 17 of the Copperton Metro Township Code imposes a stormwater facilities and impact fee to fund stormwater facilities; and

WHEREAS, Salt Lake County originally adopted Article VII prior to Copperton’s incorporation and the provision became part of the municipal codes of Copperton and the other four metro townships pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-2a-414(3), which specifies that each County ordinance in effect on the day a metro township incorporates remains in effect as a metro township ordinance until the metro township amends or repeals the ordinance; and

WHEREAS, the stormwater fees are subject to a number of requirements and limitations, including: (1) annual impact fee reports to the State Auditor; (2) provisions that require that stormwater impact fees may only be used to reimburse a storm drain system improvement identified in an impact fee facilities plan in the same drainage basin where the fee was collected; and (3) regulations that require the reimbursement of a stormwater impact fee to a developer if the fee is not used within six years of the date it was obtained from the developer; and

WHEREAS, complying with these requirements entails significant administrative cost, which are estimated to be 10% of the total fee collected, on average; and

WHEREAS, on July 20, 2018, the Board of Trustees for the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (“MSD”) determined that the administrative costs associated with stormwater impact fees were excessive when compared to value of the fees collected, and requested that the individual metro townships repeal Article VII of Title 17 of their respective municipal codes; and

WHEREAS, to conform to the MSD Board’s determination, the Council desires to repeal Article VII of Title 17 of the Copperton Metro Township Code.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

1.Article VII of Title 17 of the Copperton Metro Township Code, consisting of Sections 17.20.710 through 17.20.760, is repealed.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this 19 day of June 19, 2019.

By: /s/ SEAN CLAYTON
      Mayor

Storm Water Fee Analysis Report  

Mayor Clayton stated this report, which he will put on the metro township website, was done by an engineering firm with the idea of considering a monthly storm water fee for Copperton residents.  This would cost approximately $120 per year per residence and it is unclear what Copperton would get for it.  The metro township could raise money other ways to pay for a storm water facility.

Council Member Bailey stated this seems like a way to get more money. 

Council Member Pazell stated he was opposed to the fee.  The Council can find another way to raise the funds.  He also did not like a utility monopoly benefiting from a public subsidy. 

Council Member Patrick stated the point of this was to free up some money. 

Mayor Clayton stated he has already told the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) that Copperton will likely be a “no” on this issue.  Other metro townships may also oppose it as well.  He has sent this report to Rio Tinto.

Matt Howes, Rio Tinto, thanked the Mayor for the report.  This is not a significant amount in Copperton, but it is very significant in unincorporated areas.  

Mayor Clayton stated a resolution will be drawn up stating Copperton’s opposition to the fee.

Nathan Bracken stated a resolution was not necessary unless the Council wanted to express a policy statement to the GSLMSD.

South Valley Sewer District Resolution    

The Council reviewed the following Resolution rescinding Resolution No. 19-05-01 regarding the annexation of certain property within the Township to the South Valley Sewer District:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-06-01  Date: June 19, 2019

A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL RESCINDING RESOLUTION #2019-05-01 REGARDING THE ANNEXATION OF CERTAIN PROPERTY WITHIN THE TOWNSHIP TO THE SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT

WHEREAS, on May 25, 2019, the Copperton Metro Township Council (“Council”) approved Resolution #2019-05-01 (the “Petition”), which petitioned the South Valley Sewer District to annex certain parcels of land within the Township boundaries; and

WHEREAS, in approving the Petition, the Council promised to rescind the Petition if any property owners of the land subject to the proposed annexation protested or raised concerns regarding the annexation;

WHEREAS, after adopting the Petition, the Council learned that the primary owner of the land in question has concerns about the annexation; and

WHEREAS, a consistent with its commitment to honor the desires of the property owners who are subject to the Petition, the Council desires to rescind the Petition. 

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

Resolution #2019-05-01 is rescinded; and

Copperton Metro Township staff will provide a copy of this Resolution to the property owner in question and to the South Valley Sewer District.
