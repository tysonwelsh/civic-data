                          Benefitting Draper
                          • Draper will be able to put its reuse water to beneficial use, whereas it is
                            currently being wasted in the Jordan River.

                          • Approximately one-third of Draper’s reuse water could be used to irrigate
                            Galena Park, saving the city approximately $70,000 per year on irrigation
                            costs for the park alone.

                          • City staff can work with WaterPro to draft an agreement that will outline the
                            terms in which WaterPro could use the city’s reuse water.

                          • Such an agreement would likely include using the annual savings of irrigating
                            Galena Park with reuse water to pay for the infrastructure needed to bring
                            the reuse water to Galena Park (estimated to cost $500,000).
