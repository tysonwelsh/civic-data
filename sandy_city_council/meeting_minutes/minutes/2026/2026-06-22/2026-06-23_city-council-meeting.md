Council Member Business

                    Council Member Kris Nicholl invited the Council to attend the Sandy Club Carnival on
                    Thursday evening at 5:30 PM.

                    Council Member Brooke Christensen commented on the increase of squirrels in her
                    neighborhood and spoke about the issue.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud mentioned the Sandy Youth Council will be volunteering at
                    the Sandy Club Carnival and the Emergency Management Exercise this week. She
                    attended a Be Ready Emergency Prep class with the City. It was an excellent class and
                    she encouraged the community to attend these monthly classes which occur on the third
                    Wednesday of every month. The next class is in August. She also attended the Plein Air
                    art competition, the Farmer's Market and the Police Swearing In Ceremony.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa presented Chris Edwards with Council Cash on behalf
                    of the entire Council and thanked her for her work with the Council Outreach Program.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman thanked the Parks Department for the upgrades at a
                    city park. She provided an update from the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District.
                    The organization received a clean audit report. Herriman is formally withdrawing from the
                    District and they are exploring the option to provide curbside pickup service for bulk
                    waste. She expressed support for getting community input and feedback regarding the
                    Crescent View Middle School proposed purchase and would support placing this issue on
                    the ballot so the people have a voice in what happens next.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer attended the Mayor's Intersection Safety Committee.
                    He received great information. He also wanted the community to know that he
                    empathized with the residents' concerns regarding fireworks and the fire danger. State
                    law limits the city's authority and preempts city code. We cannot ban fireworks
                    throughout the City. Please encourage people to exercise extreme caution and be safe.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Convention Facilities
                    Advisory Board meeting. Occupancy at both the Salt Palace and the Mountain America
                    Expo Center are well above the national and international averages with 1 million visitors
                    at the Salt Palace and 800,000 at the Expo Center. The Salt Palace will fully shut down
                    in September 2027 for renovations and was planned to reopen in 2030, offering new and
                    different kinds of space for conventions. She attended the 104th birthday party for Phyllis
                    Okerman, a long time Sandy resident. She attended the last hike of the Sandy Hiking
                    Club and she encouraged the community to register for the August hike club.

                    Council Chair Sharkey invited Lynn Pace, City Attorney, to provide information on the
                    city's limitation in banning fireworks in the city.

                    Lynn Pace provided details on the limitations placed on cities by the State of Utah
                    regarding banning of fireworks within city limits. In order for the city to ban fireworks
                    across the entire city, the Governor would need to change the law. Council Member
                    Sharkey reviewed the areas in the City where there was a fireworks ban in place - East of
                    1300 E and some additional pocket areas West of 1300 E. The Fire Marshall, Gerald
                    Strebel, also provided additional information regarding designated areas where fireworks
                    are banned and the process to ban fireworks. He also spoke about the fines imposed for
                    fireworks violations.

                    Council Member Kris Nicholl was excused from the meeting at approximately 6:30 PM.
