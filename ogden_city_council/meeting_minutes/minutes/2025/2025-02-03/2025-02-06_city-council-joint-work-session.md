         The purpose of the Joint Work Session was to receive presentations from the Ogden School District Board of Education
regarding partner work in athletics and the state of the District, as well as District Teen Centers; receive presentations from Ogden
City regarding School Resource Officers; and to discuss items of joint interest.
         Prior to moving to agenda items, Board member Jamie Nunley was administered the Oath of Office.

Partner Work in Athletics
         Assistant Superintendent Carpenter used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to report on Ogden City and District staff’s
attendance at a recent sports summit event; the workshop brought together City officials, School District leaders, and community
stakeholders to develop a strategic framework aimed at increasing athletic participation among students and parents in the
community. The workshop addressed barriers, highlighted the academic and behavioral benefits of sports participation, and
engaged participants in creating guiding principles, common objectives, and a long-term action plan. The goals of the workshop
were as follows:
              1. Develop guiding principles that reflect the shared values of the City and School District.
              2. Identify common objectives for increasing athletic participation over the next one to five years.
              3. Draft a strategic framework addressing key barriers and leveraging community resources.
         The presentation expounded on the benefits of athletic participation and barriers to participation in athletics. The group
also heard input from various City and District staff members that participated in the summit regarding the next steps in working
to increase sports participation in the community and the resources that can be offered by both entities.
State of the District
         Superintendent Rasmussen used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to report on the State of the District. The
presentation focused on demographics of the District; the District’s ‘Portrait of a Graduate’ program; graduation rates for High
Schools in the District; biliteracy rates for students and graduates in the District; college and career readiness; early literacy and
numeracy efforts, starting in first grade; international baccalaureate programs in District secondary schools; positive behavior
support; talent development; and employee culture in the District.

Update on School Resource Officers
          Ogden Police Chief Sube provided the group with an update regarding School Resource Officers (SRO); his vision for
the SRO program is to increase engagement between the Officers and youth in the schools and throughout the community during
the school year as well as during the summer months. He looks forward to working with the District on these efforts, which are
aimed at reducing high risk behavior of young people in the City. He is building programs and policies that can be used to guide
the SROs, and he thanked the District for their partnership.
          Members of the group engaged in discussion of different youth programs they have had personal experiences with and
discussed the status of these programs. Several members also commented on improvement of the SRO program and thanked the
City for their efforts to facilitate meaningful relationships between students and law enforcement. Mayor Nadolski commented
that it has been Chief’s Sube’s goal to have the best trained, most qualified law enforcement officers in the State of Utah and the
City’s leadership is very supportive of that goal.

Discussion of District Teen Centers
          Superintendent Rasmussen presented a copy of the December 2024 newsletter for Ogden School District Family & Teen
Centers; the newsletter also provided statistics regarding use of the four centers: Ben Lomond Teen Center, Odyssey Family
Center, Ogden High Teen Center, and OTECH High Teen Center. The Centers have been received very well by the community
and many community partners are providing support at the Centers. The goal of the Centers is to help students overcome various
barriers to success.
