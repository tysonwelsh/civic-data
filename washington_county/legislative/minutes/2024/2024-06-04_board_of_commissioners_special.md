WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
JUNE 4, 2024

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Commissioner Victor Iverson at 1:02 p.m. on June 4, 2024, in Conference room
421, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Chairman Gil
Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Treasurer David
Whitehead, Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales, Public Works Director Todd
Edwards, Assistant Public Works Director Dean Wulffenstein, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Jayanne Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION AND CONSIDERATION OF CENTRAL CEMETERY UPDATES.

Commissioner Iverson welcomed Clint Hardman, Tom Esplin, and Mike Bonner from
Central, Utah. Commissioner Iverson also shared a brief history of how Washington
County acquired possession of the current Central Cemetery.

Mr. Hardman explained that two of the existing road ways in the cemetery have been
encroached by occupied graves. To establish the width needed to define the previous
roadways, headstones and graves will need to be moved. There is a large portion of land
that has cleared and prepped for new plots and roadways. Mr. Hardman asked if the county
could help with a new survey and plotting of roadways in this newly prepped portion. This
would allow the existing roadways to remain untouched and become walking paths.

Mr. Bonner shared the current cemetery financials showing an available balance for survey
costs but noted the small reserve that would be left for upkeep. (See attached) It was
discussed by Ms. Hales that the cemetery should explore options to become a 501(c) 13.
Ms. Hales will continue to keep in contact with Mr. Hardman and Mr. Bonner for support

during the process.

There was an agreement made with Public Works Director Todd Edwards he would work
with Mr. Hardman and Mr. Bonner to acquire a survey company to perform the survey
work. There will be further meetings to discuss the survey results and options moving
forward.

With nothing further the meeting was adjourned at 1:57 pm.

Current Status:

1. There are 17 remaining plots for sale in the original Cemetery. The cost of a plot is $250 for residents
and $350 for non-residents. The sale of plots is the sole source of income for the Cemetery. The open
and close fees are $450 for caskets and $100 for urns, which represents the actual cost paid by the
Cemetery for these services. These costs are reimbursed by the mortuaries.

2. The operating cash balance is $12,705.47 as of 5/20/24 and has not changed since then. Per David
Whitehead, there is an additional $19,459.10, which represents three CD’s that matured in January of
2021. Per his email of 6/3/24: “The funds have been set aside and flagged as Central Cemetery funds.
We will have to manually calculate the interest that has accrued on those funds since the CD’s were
closed” Total cash is $32,164.57 plus the accrued interest mentioned above.

3. See the attached Income and Expense Detail which covers the period of January 2021 through May
2024.

4. The Cemetery has not established a 501(c)13 for various reasons.
5. Counsel has reviewed the draft Maintenance and Landscape Agreement and has strongly advised

against signing such an agreement. It’s unreasonable to expect a group of unpaid volunteers to expose
themselves to the responsibilities and liability contemplated in the agreement.
