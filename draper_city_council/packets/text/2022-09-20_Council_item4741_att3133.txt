                               RESOLUTION NO. 22-48

     A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING ROBERT
     MARKLE TO SERVE AS THE PRIMARY DELEGATE, AND HAZEL DUNSMORE
     TO SERVE AS ALTERNATE DELEGATE, TO THE TRANS JORDAN LANDFILL
     BOARD OF DIRECTORS.

      WHEREAS, Draper City is a member community of the Trans Jordan Landfill,
an entity created pursuant to an Interlocal agreement; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City is required to designate a principal and alternate
delegates to the Board of Directors of the Trans Jordan Landfill; and

      WHEREAS, Robert Markle is to be appointed as the designated principal
delegate replacing Steve Siddoway who is retiring; and

      WHEREAS, Hazel Dunsmore is to be appointed as the alternate delegate.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Appointment. Robert Markle is hereby appointed as the primary
delegate to represent Draper City at any and all meetings of the Trans Jordan
Landfill Board. Hazel Dunsmore is hereby appointed as the alternate delegate.

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of the Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.



     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 20TH DAY OF SEPTEMBER, 2022.
