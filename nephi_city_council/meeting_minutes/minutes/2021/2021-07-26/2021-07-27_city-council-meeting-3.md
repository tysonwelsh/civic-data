Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order.

ZONE CHANGE ORDINANCE 07-27-2021 ADOPTED:

   The Planning Commission had held a public hearing and recommended the zone change from a R1-H
to R2-Z zone on two parcels of land that was approximately 10 acres and known as the Bowles property.
Representatives from Priority Homes, Garrison and Brett Ludlow, informed the council that they are
looking to possibly purchase and develop the property. The Ludlows answered questions from the
council about their future development plans.
  Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 07-27-2021: AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE ZONE
MAP OF NEPHI CITY, STATE OF UTAH. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

OUT OF STATE TRAVEL APPROVED:

  Recreation Director Craig Oswald requested permission to travel to Colorado to take a test for
certification as a Professional Recreation Director. Councilor Ostler moved to approve the out-of-state
travel. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion.

  Chief of Police Michael Morgan requested permission for some officers to travel to Mesquite for
training in drug enforcement. Councilor Ostler moved to approve the out-of-state travel. Councilor
Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Regular Council Meeting adjourned at 8:50 p.m. and reconvened into work session to further discuss the
RV park code issues.
