ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-1-2021 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone text change to amend
          residential landscaping and recreation space standards in Titles 7 and 9. (Staff –
          Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

              Discussion: Steve Pastorik explained that the City has standards for open space
              that are not consistent for every zone and development. Steve then discussed the
              differences between open space definitions for multi-unit residential, planned unit
              developments, and the city center zone. He then stated the purpose of this
              application is to make these standards more consistent. Steve said they would
              change the definition of open space to recreation space. When looking at the
              taller building developments, there is open space inside the building that is not
              currently being factored into the definition. He explained recreation space would
              include outdoor space, indoor space, private outdoor space. Steve then presented

               the difference between recreation space and landscaping. He noted there is a
               possibility of overlap between the two. Steve explained the standards for
               recreation space and landscaping for developments up to 20 units/acre, 20-30
               units/acre, and over 30 units/acre.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if these developments will look similar to those at
               Fair Point. Steve said the City is not set on a particular development style at this
               time. Commissioner Winters asked if there are any current development plans
               that would not meet the new criteria. Steve explained that the percentages are not
               always provided; however, they will be required to submit those numbers if these
               criteria passed. Commissioner Winters feels having more amenities gives more of
               a neighborhood feel. Steve clarified that currently, the ordinance states a certain
               number of amenities are required depending upon the development's size, and that
               will not change.

               Commissioner Fuller said most amenities are not tailored to children, and he
               would like to see more of those amenities in the City. Steve stated the
               developments add amenities that will entice the demographic they want living
               there. Commissioner McEwen said it should be up to the Commission and the
               City to balance amenities for adults and children. Steve stated the possibility of
               having one required amenity for children if that is something the Commission
               would like to discuss.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if the criteria for recreational space and landscaping
               are similar to surrounding cities. Steve explained the different cities define and
               record these spaces differently, so it is hard to make an exact comparison. He
               stated with the current research; West Valley City meets and, in some cases,
               exceeds their requirements.

               Chair Meaders asked if a daycare could be an amenity added to a multi-family
               residence. Steve said that is a possibility, and the daycare would need to be only
               for the residents of the multi-family development.

               Steve asked the Commission if they would like to continue this discussion in a
               future meeting. Chair Meaders feels that would be a good idea.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-3-2021 Petition by STRUCTURAL PRESERVATION SYSTEMS,
         LLC requesting conditional use approval for outside storage. The property is
         located at 3270 W 2200 S on 0.25 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff –
          Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Brock Anderson explained this application is for outside storage.
               The applicant plans to store construction equipment such as; trailers, concrete
               mixers, scaffolding, shipping containers, structural steel, and reinforcing steel.
               Brock stated the applicant is proposing crushed limestone for this site. Brock said
               asphalt is typically required for vehicles with wheels, and he would make that
               recommendation to the applicant.

               Brock stated that the applicant is proposing a six-foot-tall chain-link fence. The
               code states outside storage needs to be blocked from public view. Brock will
               recommend slats be added to the fence.


C-4-2021       Petition by SANSONE GROUP requesting conditional use approval for a
               warehouse building. The property is located at 2454 S 5600 W on 20.22 acres and
               is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
               brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Brock Anderson explained this application goes along with SA-2-
               2021 that Steve Lehman will present later in this meeting. He stated that Beagley
               Road would extend to the subject location. There will be two lots; however, they
               are only looking to develop Lot 1 for a warehouse building at this time.
               Brock noted that this building does not need to meet commercial design
               standards. He will recommend the addition of trees along Beagley Road.

               Commissioner Winters asked what the applicant is required to do with the
               wetlands. Brock said that the Army Corps of Engineers and West Valley City’s
               public works department would would need to review any issues related to the
               wetlands.

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SA-2-2021 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY ENGINEERING DIVISION requesting
          preliminary approval for the Cabco 5600 West Subdivision 3rd Amended. This
          application will amend a portion of lot 1A, lot 2 and a portion of Anna Caroline
          Drive of Cabco 5600 West Subdivision Lot 1 Amended. The purpose of the
          amended subdivision is to correct the alignment of Anna Caroline Drive. The
          subdivision is located at approximately 2530 South Anna Caroline Drive and is
          zone M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
          steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Steve Lehman explained this application is to adjust the alignment of
               Anna Caroline Drive. Steve stated the portion of Anna Caroline Drive installed as
               part of UDOT’s Subdivision was not built according to plans. It was three feet to
               the east of where it should have been placed to connect to the bridge over the
               Ritter Canal. Steve said a small portion of Anna Caroline Drive would need to be
               vacated on the west. The right-of-way would shift easterly with a public sidewalk
               easement on the east side of Anna Caroline Drive. A portion of the right-of-way
               will be dedicated to attain the correct alignment from the Riter Canal to 2400
               South.
