Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on March 5, 2024, in the Washington County Commission

Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.

Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2024-3273, _A RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING EXPENDITURES OF FUNDS COLLECTED AS WASHINGTON
COUNTY’S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that as part of the RAP Tax collection
established via ballot in 2014, Washington County receives a portion of funding to be used in the
unincorporated areas for recreation, arts and parks. Applications are collected at the end of each
year, wherein, identified projects are prioritized by the applicant. A total of $97,699 is
recommended for projects submitted by qualified applicants from Silver Reef Foundation,
Diamond Valley and Gunlock Rodeo.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3273, A
Resolution Appropriating Expenditures of Funds Collected as Washington
County’s Share of the Recreation, Arts and Parks Sales Tax. Motion seconded
by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

Robin Smith to be their representative, and the county is making the appointment with a term to
expire on December 31, 2026.
