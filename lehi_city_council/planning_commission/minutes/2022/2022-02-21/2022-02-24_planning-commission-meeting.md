3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and consideration of Heart of Country Swing’s request for approval of a
conditional use for a Public Dance Hall to be located in an existing building at 3940 North
Traverse Mountain Boulevard.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jessica and Addison Hildebrandt, were present. The hours would be used as an
office space during the day and receptions as an open venue during the evening. The term of the
lease is one year, with the opportunity to renew. As a conditional use the applicant would not
need to come back each year for the application process. The applicants will be anticipating 100-
150 people at a time. The use will be indoor and outdoor use. DRC did not have any issues with
parking or building capacity. Commissioner Newall suggested a sign letting patrons know they
were in a residential area.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Everett moved to approve proposed conditional use, with the condition that the
applicant come back for renewal at a year of operation. Including all DRC comments and
findings stated in the report. Newall seconded.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to approve Heart of Country Swing’s request
        for approval of a conditional use for a Public Dance Hall to be located in an existing
        building at 3940 North Traverse Mountain Boulevard; with the findings the proposed
        public dance hall use will not be detrimental or injurious to property or improvements in
        the vicinity, can have reasonable conditions included in the approval to mitigate against
        potential harm to surrounding properties, will be located and conducted in a manner in
        compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the purposes of
        this Code; including all DRC comments. Commissioner Newall seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Mark Garza’s for preliminary subdivision
review of Somerset Hills, a 5-lot residential subdivision located at 795 East 760 North.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

Ross Dinsdale addressed the drainage concern. There would be three different sumps, and it is a
small area that contains drainage and should have minimal impact. Ross stated they couldn't
require an easement where it isn’t directly related to their property.
The applicant, Mark Garza, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Quincy Bar was concerned about drainage. There have previously been two storm events on the
property's that are proposed for development have been flooded. He stated two of the adjacent
properties have an offset and he would like to make sure existing property is not undercut. He
also stated there is a phone line and wants to make sure they are marked and identified before
development.

Victor Anderson stated there is a strip of land adjacent to lot 5 and inquired whose property that
was and asked who would be taking care of it.

Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged the online comments and closed the public hearing.

The applicant stated the wall that is on the property will be left as a rock wall. The overhead
powerline will be grounded and buried under the ground. The strip of land would be a low
maintenance type of feature that would be maintained to an adjacent property or have an HOA
take care of it. The applicant stated he is happy to talk to the neighbors to see if they would be
interested in buying the strip of property. He knows there is water drainage from existing
properties and the development will be graded to drain with the flow route and into the street.

Ross Dinsdale stated the Public Work Department has worked with the property owners of the
Church. There is an easement secured with the church so Public Works can maintain and clean
the drainage. Ross stated those improvements will help immensely, as well as the development.
Ross said there is an existing lot that is wider than the road needs to be and part of the proposal
will be included in lot 5, but that is at the discretion of the property owner.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval of Mark Garza’s for
        preliminary subdivision review of Somerset Hills, a 5-lot residential subdivision
        located at 795 East 760 North; including all DRC comments, with the findings that the
        proposed subdivision meets the lot requirements of the R-1-8 zone, and meets the
        requirements of the Lehi City Development Code in regards to subdivision approvals.
        Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of SDM Development’s request for review of The
Falls at the Point General Plan Amendment, changing approximately 11.1-acres located at
approximately 4200 North Digital Drive from Light Industrial and Commercial to Mixed-
Use.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The Commissioners and staff discussed the zone change and uses. The DRC comments
suggested priority areas for uses.

Ross Dinsdale addressed questions on traffic and infrastructure on the mixed use. Ross stated
there are currently no utilities to this property. Any density that is put on this property would
have to come from allocated units.

The applicant, Curtis Miner, was present and provided a presentation on the proposed request.
He recognized the zone does not allow the use. He stated the General Plan Amendment will open
the door for rezone and help determine uses for the site plan concept. Steven Mecham with SDM
Development stated they had contacted the City Council when the General Plan Amendment was
being amended. He stated the concept is appropriate for the area. Some of the issues are
connection to utilities, pedestrian crossings, residential density configuration and commercial
configuration. Trail connectivity has not been worked through in detail, but they know if they
elevate the property can be connected. Approximately 6 acres would be residential, and 5 acres
commercial. (Inaudible) stated engineering with other developments did not pencil out. There are
three property owners with this concept. Mr. Mecham stated 3.5 and 3.4 were related items.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Kim Struthers stated there is a fixed finite number that would be hard to add to due to utilities
and water caps. In the mixed use zone, a public notice process will be provided if the General
Plan Amendment is changed. TOD and connectivity were discussed at length with the proposed
development.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of SDM Development’s
    request for review of The Falls at the Point General Plan Amendment, changing
    approximately 11.1-acres located at approximately 4200 North Digital Drive from Light
    Industrial and Commercial to Mixed-Use; including all DRC comments, excluding
    number 5, with the findings that the proposed General Plan amendment is in conformance
    with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the General Plan and its various elements, is
    consistent with the land uses and zoning of nearby and adjoining properties, will not have a
    substantial adverse effect on surrounding property, the affected site is physically suitable, and
    the provision of public services to accommodate any potential future uses, including all
    landscaping, loading areas, open spaces, parking areas, setbacks, buffering/screening, walls,
    fences, yards, and other required features, and the proposed amendment to the Lehi City
    General Plan is not detrimental to the public health, safety, and welfare of Lehi City. Also
    noting that the zone request does not allow residential. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, no;
    Commissioner Newall, no. The motion passed 4 – 3.

3.6) Public hearing and recommendation Fort Street Partners’ request for review of the
Fort Street Zone Change on 4.79-acres of property located at approximately 3300 North
1200 West from TH-5 (transitional holding) to Commercial and R-1-10 (residential).
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jim Balderson, was present. The commercial use would be for a medical facility.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Steve Millard is concerned about the development and the prior zone change. He is concerned
with property value and the size of the development. He would like the property to be residential
and feels there is a great need for housing right now.

Kate Montigue also stated the property was designated as LDR and the zone had recently
changed. She stated she purchased her property with the prior zone being LDR and since it has
now changed to Commercial. She would like to see the property be residential. She is concerned
about traffic, noise, pollution, etc. There are children that cut through her property to get to Fox
Hollow and she does not believe the kids will use a trail. She stated there are also considerations
of zoning changes. The majority of the surrounding properties are residential. She is also
concerned about privacy and safety.

Darren Wright stated he would prefer residential. He stated developments that are built are
already impeding on other neighbors.

Tanner Jardine stated the property has been graded and terraced. Tanner's house sits below the
property. He is concerned with privacy and his property. The previous property owner has not
maintained the land.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale stated the request does meet the General Plan. There are buffering and landscape
standards that would need to be met. The concept has a trail on 1200 West for pedestrian
connectivity. There will not be a road for connectivity. Noticing is not required for General Plan
Amendment but noticing did take place and the City did due diligence. Craig Chambers there are
seven "items" that need to be met to determine if zoning can be changed.

Commissioner Nielsen re-opened public hearing.

Kate Montague stated she is happy the General Plan is considered for the City to be safe legally.

Commissioner closed the public hearing.

The applicant, Jim, stated he didn't get into the specific site plan because it was not on the
agenda. A General Plan Amendment was implemented and allowed the applicant to make a
site/concept plan. They have 7 lots to develop as well as a trail. There will be a medical building
and are using the slope to construct the building, as it will not be built on a pedestal and will
comply with standards. The applicant stated the demand for medical centers is high and there is
limited land left for commercial with a commercial corridor.

    Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to send a positive recommendation of Fort Street
    Partners’ request for review of the Fort Street Zone Change on 4.79-acres of property
    located at approximately 3300 North 1200 West from TH-5 (transitional holding) to
    Commercial and R-1-10 (residential); including all DRC comments, with the findings that
    the proposed zone change will not have a negative impact on the character of the surrounding
    area, is consistent with the General Plan Land Use Map designations, is consistent with the
    goals and policies of the General Plan Land Use Element, will not unduly affect the uses or
    proposed uses for nearby and adjoining properties. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.7) Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for an update to the Lehi City Standard
Details.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Scott Dunn, was present. The geotechnical engineer is aware of the DRC
comment and is working to resolve the issue. There is a pole in one of the yards and what is done
with it will be left up to the owner as far as requirements.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Jessica Knowlton stated her house will face the road that will be connected. She is concerned
that once the road is connected the traffic will increase. There is a park near her home with fields
for soccer and softball. There is a blind corner and there has been speeding issues with traffic.
There are also some dirt hills that the neighborhood loves, and she feels that connecting the road
would be destroying a unique place.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale stated he is aware of the area and the dirt hills. The road has always been intended
to connect, and it will increase traffic. Ross expressed that parking near parks and open spaces is
an issue across the city where there is open space. Ross stated there is a trail crossing that should
help discourage some of the road crossing.
