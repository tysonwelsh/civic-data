Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                                  Mike Applegarth reviewed the upcoming agenda calendar.

Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Zoltanski asked about getting an update on City related COVID-19
                                  expenses and economic projections. Council Member Christensen agreed with getting
                                  economic trend data. She also recommended that the Council resume in-person
                                  meetings.

Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn requested time for Deputy Mayor Evelyn Everton to report on the
                                  County's disbursement of federal CARES act funding.

CAO Report

                                  CAO Matt Huish reported on the launch of the new website, and the transition from
                                  "orange" to "yellow." Scott Earl then provided further information on the opening of City
                                  parks and recreational programs.

Information Items


1.         20-157                 Council discussion of the FY 2020-21 Tentative Budget compensation
                                  plan.
                   Attachments:   FY 2020-21 Tentative Budget

                                  The Council opted to take items 2 and 3 first. This item (1) was heard following the 6:00
                                  PM Citizen Comments portion of the meeting.

                                  Administrative Services Director reviewed the compensation plan for the FY 2020-21
                                  Tentative Budget. Human Resources Director also spoke about the Administration's
                                  approach to the compensation plan before and after pandemic restrictions. Council
                                  questions and comments followed.

2.         20-156                 Public Works Department recommending adoption of Resolution 20-13C
                                  authorizing the execution of an agreement between Salt Lake County and
                                  Sandy City to lease and demise a portion of City property to the County.
                   Attachments:   20-13c Ground Lease Agreement resolution
                                  Salt Lake County Ground Lease - HHW (final 4-23-20)

                                  Public Works Director Mike Gladbach introduced the agenda item and referenced the
                                  agreement and resolution attached.

                                  Dustin Fratto gave instruction about how to participate in public comment for this item.
                                  Steve Van Maren mentioned the $1 cost for the lease agreement.

                                  Council Member Zoltanski asked about insurance required on the facility and the dollar
                                  amount on the lease agreement. Council Member Sharkey asked about the development
                                  process. Mike Gladbach noted that the County facility will go through Sandy's planning
                                  process.
                                  A motion was made by Kristin Coleman-Nicholl, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey, to

3.         20-146                 Recess of the City Council meeting and convene a meeting of the
                                  Redevelopment Agency of Sandy City.
                   Attachments:   Agenda Packet 5-19-20.pdf

                                  A motion was made by Kris Nicholl and seconded by Marci Houseman to recess the City
                                  Council meeting and convene a meeting of the Redevelopment Agency of Sandy City.
