D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: You will see up here on the dais all these little ducks. They
   mean several different things. They mean that, yes, we here at Weber County have our ducks in a row. I was
   thinking the other day people come here and think we have some big issues. I have learned in life that I wouldn’t
   change my problems for someone else’s. My sweet Jane showed me this video the other day and I thought others
   need to see this video (click or copy hyperlink; video starts at 7:50 mark). Sometimes being taught by youth is a
   great thing. A six year old orphan and his Aunt reported when he went out and started giving, it’s done wonders
   for him. I would ask all of you remember the example Jaden has given us. And when you give, even when you
   are going through a hard time, it will do wonders for you as well.

    1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 05-2024 APPOINTING TRUSTEES TO THE TAYLOR WEST WEBER
       PARK DISTRICT.
         Stacy Skeen (Commission Office): Received notice from the Taylor West Weber Park District that two
         positions expired December 31st, 2023. Two applications have been received; one from Haley Toon seeking
         reappointment and one from Braden Felix would be a new appointment.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 05-2024 reappointing Katie Toon and appointing Braden
         Felix as trustees to the Taylor West Weber Park District; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – excused; Commissioner Froerer - aye
    2. APPROVE RESOLUTION 06-2024 APPOINTING A TRUSTEE TO THE WEST WEBER-TAYLOR
       CEMETERY DISTRICT.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 06-2024 appointing Roger Heslop as trustee to the West
         Weber-Taylor Cemetery District; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – excused; Commissioner Froerer - aye
    3. APPROVE RESOLUTION 07-2024 APPOINTING TWO MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN VALLEY
       PLANNING COMMISSION.

       PROVIDE PARAMEDIC SERVICES TO WEBER COUNTY:
                                 WEBER FIRE DISTRICT
                                 ROY CITY FIRE DEPARTMENT
                                 OGDEN CITY FIRE DEPARTMENT
         Eli Johnson (Sheriff’s Office): Two years ago we commissioned a study by an outside organization that
         assessed the performance of the paramedic services. Those services are provided County wide through this
         contract with the County subsidizing the costs. The study offered recommendations to potential changes that

         basis.
