I.     Call to Order – Chairman Gibbons.
Chair Gibbons called the RDA Meeting to order at approximately 7:07 p.m.

II.    Public Comments.
There were no public comments.

III.   Presentation of 2022-23 Budget
The recommended tentative budget for the RDA was included in the budget presented to the
Council earlier.

IV.    Consideration of Resolution RDA 2022-04 Acknowledging Receipt of and Adopting
the Tentative 2022-23 Fiscal Year Budget & Setting a Public Hearing for June 2, 2022
Board Member Fotheringham moved to approve RDA Resolution 2022-04. Board Member
Brewer seconded the motion. The roll call vote was as follows: Board Members Brewer, Quinn,
Durham, Fotheringham, Dahle and Chair Gibbons in favor. Resolution RDA 2022-04 was adopted
by unanimous vote

V.     Other Business.
There was no additional business.

VI.   Adjourn RDA Meeting.
Board Member Brewer moved to adjourn the RDA Board Meeting and reconvene in a City
Council Meeting. Board Member Quinn seconded the motion. The motion passed with the
unanimous consent of the Board

The RDA meeting adjourned at 7:10 pm.
