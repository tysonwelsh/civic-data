Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Co-Chairman Everett welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Parking Presentation
Jakob Suafilo gave a presentation on parking. The Commissioners discussed the pros and cons of
having parking minimums, and the advantages and disadvantages of having form based codes. The
definition of the parking paradigm was also discussed.

3. Legislative update from State Legislative Session
Kim Struthers gave an update on HB82 and Local Government Regulations. ADU’s in the City
of Lehi were also discussed and what the State has decided to regulate.

4. Adjournment
Update on Engage Lehi and May 2021 is Bike Month.

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Hemmert moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Everett seconded the motion. The
motion passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
