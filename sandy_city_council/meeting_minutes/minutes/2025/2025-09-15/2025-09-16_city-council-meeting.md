4.         25-293                 Public Works Department presenting the Council with an update on the
                                  Wasatch Blvd HAWK crossing
                   Attachments:   Wasatch Blvd HAWK Recommendations Memo
                                  Wasatch Blvd HAWK Crossing Presentation

                                  Britney Ward, Assistant Director for Public Works, presented the traffic study conducted
                                  by the Public Works Department. The staff studied three potential pedestrian hybrid
                                  beacon (commonly known as HAWKS) locations on Wasatch Boulevard. The locations
                                  were as follows: 1950 East, 2220 East, and 2250 East. Staff recommended the 2220
                                  East location for the implementation of the HAWK because it has the highest utilization
                                  potential and met the requirements set forth in the American Association of State
                                  Highway and Transportation Officials (AASHTO) and the Manual on Uniform Traffic
                                  Control Devices (MUTCD). The 2220 East location is located near Lone Peak Elementary
                                  School and is designated as part of the school's safe walking route. Staff presented on
                                  the proposed project purpose and needs. Council questions occurred throughout the
                                  presentation. Council expressed support for the staff's recommendations.

                                  Following the presentation, Council moved to General Citizen Comments.

5.         CA08262025 Amendments to Title 21 of the Land Development Code related to
           -0007027   Brewpubs
           (CC WS)
                   Attachments:   Staff Report on Brewpubs with Exhibits A and B
                                  Exhibit C - Map of State-Required Buffers
                                  Exhibit D - Map of Eligible Zones for Brewpubs
                                  Planning Commission Minutes 09.04.2025 (DRAFT)
                                  Brewpub Presentation 9-16-2025

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, presented on code amendments listed in
                                  Agenda Items 5 and 6. On April 29th, the City Council adopted Resolution 25-16C,
                                  directing staff to consider streamlining the permitting process lowering the regulatory and
                                  financial barriers to entry and to evaluate areas that brewpubs could be allowed to operate
                                  that are outside the downtown Cairns District. In follow up, Community Development
                                  department staff presented recommendations to the Council at the July 15, 2025
                                  meeting. Council directed staff to prepare the code amendments and proceed with the
                                  standard process for adoption. Ms. Anderson and Ms. Casaril reviewed the proposed
                                  amendments, zoning, and licensing process and changes. They reviewed the permitted
                                  vs. the conditional use for brewpubs. Some of the proposed amendments ensure the
                                  alignment of the city code with state regulations. Council questions occurred throughout
                                  the presentation. A public hearing was held on this item. The Planning Commission
                                  forwarded a positive recommendation. This is an information item this evening. The item
                                  will come back to the Council for consideration and action at a future Council meeting.

                                  Following this presentation, Council moved to the Consent Calendar.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Council Director: Next week's Council meeting is cancelled. This Friday is
                      the ribbon cutting for the Utah Mammoth Training Facility.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. Bright
                      Star is their next production with shows in October. America West Symphony now
                      collects donations at their concert instead of selling tickets. She attended the Sandy
                      Senior Center barbecue and car show. It was a great event. Council hosted state
                      legislators in the Sandy Suite for the RSL game. She provided an update from Legislative
                      Policy Committee (LPC) and spoke about upcoming issues: possible changes to the
                      homeless mitigation fund, laws impacting property tax increases, combining school board
                      elections with municipal elections, and detached ADU's. LPC will be sending out a survey
                      to members to gather feedback.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the Historic Committee. The
                      Heritage Festival and Horse Parade is on September 27th. She had questions for the
                      Administration regarding possible cuts to the mitigation fund and requested information.

                      Council Member Marci Houseman spoke with police officers this week and felt they were
                      very calming to the community. Thank you for the sacrifices our law enforcement make
                      every day. She thanked city staff and volunteers for coordinating the Healing Fields event.
                      She assisted in taking down the flags yesterday and reflected on the lives and service of
                      those honored. Healing is needed and her thoughts and prayers were with the students
                      and faculty at Utah Valley University. She praised Charlie Kirk for his leadership, faith,
                      and civility. She is grateful to those who serve our country. God Bless America.

                      Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged the Planning staff for all their work on the
                      brew pub amendments. He thanked the Public Works staff for the great job they did with
                      the bulk waste clean up. He wanted to acknowledge Mike Gladbach, our former Director
                      of Public Works. He offered his condolences to his family and shared thoughts on Mike's
                      service to the City. He was a fantastic man and he always appreciated the time he spent
                      with Mike and was greatly saddened by his passing.

                      Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked Council staff for all they do in service to the
                      Council.

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski: The community is invited to the Heritage Festival and Horse Parade on
                      Saturday, September 27th. Please join us on October 11th for the opening of Fire Station
                      31 and a pancake breakfast. The Utah Mammoth Training Facility will have it's grand
                      opening tomorrow. She offered her condolences to Mike Gladbach's wife and family. He
                      served Sandy City for 20 years and prior to that he served in the military for 20 years. He
                      was always willing to take on any challenge and our thoughts and prayers are with his
                      family.
