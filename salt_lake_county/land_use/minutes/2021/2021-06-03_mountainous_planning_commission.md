Commissioner Cohen motioned to continue to July 1st agenda regarding the FR/FA zone changes regarding

Salt Lake County Counsel Zach Shaw provided an analysis of the Ordinance Amending sections 19.12.020

uses in the FA zone). 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Citizen 
Name: Ed Marshall 
Address: 6451 East Millcreek Canyon Road 
Comments: Mr. Marshall said at the last meeting he pointed out designated watershed vs protected 
watersheds used for culinary purposes  and that designated may be protected someday but is not currently 
protected. Millcreek is unprotected and 100’s of dogs are brought into Millcreek and hike to the top to the 
lake and no one cleans up and  they go everywhere and can’t be kept on private property. Wrong result to 
bring a dog or horse in Millcreek but owners cannot keep on their own property. If council adopts ordinance, 
request that “protected” be inserted in section F1 and G. 
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Ronna Hoffman 
Address: 7626 Mountain Top Road 
Comments: Ms. Hoffman thanked everyone for taking this seriously and allowing time. 
 
Speaker # 3: Citizen 
Name: Sheila Adler 
Address: 14777 South Shaggy Mountain Road 
Comments: Ms. Adler said she is the president of the board for HOA. Appreciates taking ordinance back 
for review and obtain answers to key questions.  Questions application of ordinance to protected watershed 
versus irrigation and federal regulation being referred to.  
 
Commissioner Cohen asked Ms. Adler about federal regulations in the ordinance. Ms. Adler said ordinance 
does not refer to federal regulations , but was told by  Zach Shaw that part of t he reason the update was 
being done is to comply with federal regulations. Mr. Shaw said staff will address in the staff report. 
 
Commissioner Jones motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Reid seconded that motion.  
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To continue the  ordinance amending the following sections of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances, 2001: 19.12.020 (permitted uses in the FR zone), 19.12.030(g) (conditional uses in the FR 
zone), and 19.54.020 (permitted uses in the FA zone) to the July 1st meeting to create a staff report. 
Motion by: Commissioner Cohen 
2nd by: Commissioner Palmer 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor

An ordinance of the Salt Lake County Council amending the boundaries of the Mountainous Planning 
District (to exclude the Town of Brighton) and amending the membership requirements of the Mountainous 
Planning District Planning Commission; and making other related changes. Presenter: Zach Shaw 
(Motion/Voting) 
 
Salt Lake County Counsel Zach Shaw provided an analysis of the Ordinance Amending  the boundaries of 
the Mountainous Planning District. 
 
Commissioner Jones asked about an unincorporated seat. Mr. Shaw said prior statute did indicate there 
would be unincorporated members of the planning commission. Commissioner Derr asked if there will still 
be a MPDPC and if not, when will it no longer be functioning. Mr. Shaw said MPD is what the MPDPC 
has jurisdiction over. No sunset on MPD or MPDPC. Commissioner Reid asked about the population of 
MPD. Mr. Young said is in the hundreds not thousands of permanent residents.  
 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding surrounding city participation. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing. Commissioner Reid seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Citizen 
Name: Ed Marshall 
Address: 6451 East Millcreek Canyon Road 
Comments: Mr. Marshall said it appears ambiguity of sections D. D5 remaining four members incorporated 
or unincorporated. Section D2, say specifically two members shall reside within canyon area. Indefinite 
number allows more than two. Members from the mountains should not be limited to two. If approved as 
drafted would have to have two commissioners step down.  
 
Commissioner Omer motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Reid seconded that motion.  
 
Commissioner Reid excused herself at 4:58pm. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Commissioners had a brief discussion regarding current vacancies, confirmed vacancies are SLC and 
Sandy, designations in cities, and recruiting.  
 
Motion: To recommend approval to the Salt Lake County Council amending the boundaries of the 
Mountainous Planning District (to exclude the Town of Brighton) and amending the membership 
requirements of the Mountainous Planning District Planning Commission , with Hol laday City being 
notified of at-large vacancies in the Commission; and making other related changes. 
Motion by: Commissioner Palmer 
2nd by: Commissioner Hutchings 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor
