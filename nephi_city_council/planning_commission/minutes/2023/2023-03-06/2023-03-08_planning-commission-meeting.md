2.    Mountain Country Equipment (a.k.a. Raccoon Eye) Site Plan - Joe Humphrey
      a.     Staff Presentation
      Shauna stated that this is our second look at this Agribusiness site plan. Mountain
      Country Equipment is currently located on Main Street, and will be relocating to Highway
      132. Shauna stated that the DRC notes have been updated, and Ann remarked that
      those notes are very thorough.

      b.      Commission Discussion
      Karl asked for clarification for what would be done in the manufacturing plant building;
      Joe Humphrey replied they would be welding. He claimed that there will not be any
      powder coating done at this time. Karl also asked what would be stored in the “storage
      building.” Joe H. replied that it was just labeled as such at this time- there is no current
      usage plan for the building.

      c.      Action Item - Motion
      Ann asked for a motion to accept the Mountain Country Equipment (a.k.a. Raccoon Eye)
      Site Plan along with the DRC notes.

      Motion: Cory Thomson           Second: Alan Hancock            Outcome: Unanimous

3.    Costa Vida Site Plan - Legend Engineering
      a.    Staff Presentation
      Shauna introduced the representatives in attendance for Costa Vida: Bryan Russell

     (Architect), Gary Moore (Owner), Kipp Johnson (General Contractor), and Lonny Reed
     (Civil Engineer). Mr. Reed was present via Zoom. Shauna stated that this site plan is for
     Lot 3 in the Reed Ridge Subdivision.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Shauna turned the time over to Gary Moore. He expressed his excitement about
     bringing a Costa Vida to Nephi. He is aware of the soil issues, and said they are in the
     process of taking care of that. Karl Brough had questions about the electrical and the
     storm drainage systems. Lonny Reed stated that it’s a corrugated HDPE underground
     retention system that will allow storm drainage to percolate into the ground. Kipp
     Johnson said that he’s installed a lot of these Storm Tech systems. He said that they
     have been used for about the last 15 years, are under the parking lot, and work very
     well. Karl asked if they typically get a percolation rate test to determine how well the
     design will work, to which Kipp confirmed.

     c.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept the Costa Vida Site Plan, along with any outstanding
     DRC notes.

     Motion: Karl Brough Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Passed unanimously

4.   Cornerstone 1-lot Subdivision - 4SI Group
     a.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna stated that the Commission reviewed the Vicinity Plan for Cornerstone in
     November. The location is just south of Maverick. She said that since the last meeting,
     there was a land exchange between Korey Wright, 4SI Group and Nephi City. She said
     the City is currently in the process of negotiating some entrance options with UDOT.

     b.      Commission Discussion
     Skip asked Joe Nielson what the site plan is for, to which Joe replied it will be a car
     wash. Joe showed the Commission a photo of the proposed building that he had on his
     phone. He said there will be 3 automatic bays- two touchless and one with brushes. He
     is planning on processing 45-50 cars per hour, and would like to offer a membership that
     would include any of those bays. As Joe already owns a local car wash, Karl asked him
     where the dirt goes once it’s washed down the drain. Joe said that there are grease
     traps that are pumped out on a regular basis. The chemicals that are used are water
     soluble, making them environmentally friendly. Joe said that his current car wash does
     not recycle water, but they are looking at potentially doing that at his new car wash. Joe
     claimed that most cities prefer their residents to use a car wash rather than washing their
     vehicles at home. He said the high pressure of a car wash actually reduces the amount
     of water used, versus using a garden hose at home. Joe expressed his desire to provide
     a system that will cater to the typical muddy vehicles of a rural area, without
     compromising the equipment. John Ford asked what happens to the salt that comes off
     of the cars in the winter. Joe replied that it accumulates with the water in the grease

     traps. Cory asked if the utility easement that runs through the middle of the property will
     be a problem. Joe replied that it will actually be beneficial to them and there will be
     blacktop over it. Fran asked Shauna if the City staff had any concerns about this project,
     and Shauna denied any concerns.

     c.     Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept the Cornerstone 1-lot Subdivision, along with the DRC
     notes.

     Motion: Alan Hancock       Second: Fran Petersen       Outcome: Passed Unanimously
