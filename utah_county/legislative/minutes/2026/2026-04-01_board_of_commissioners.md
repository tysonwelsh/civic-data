5. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF A RESOLUTION APPROVI N.G THE
UTAH COUNTY STRATEGIC PLAN.
         -Ezra Nair, Commission

(Ti mestamp: 4:20)

Commissioner Powers Gardner explained that the strategic plan is something we
have needed for years. At the rate of growth in our county, not having a strategic
plan on what our priorities are made us incredibly reactive instead of proactive.
This plan aligns the vision of our resources and forces us to think in a long-term
and multi-year strategy. She thanked everyone that had something to do with this.
She said the 4 pillars of the plan are transparency and organizational efficiency,
public safety and security, quality of life and place, and economic
competitiveness.
