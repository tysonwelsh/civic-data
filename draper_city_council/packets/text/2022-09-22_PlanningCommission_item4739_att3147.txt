BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement (see Exhibit
G for full text) for approximately 2.44 acres located between 12300 S. and 12220
S. on the north and south, and Placer St. and 670 E. on the east and west, at
approximately 638 E. 12225 S. (Exhibits C & D). The property is currently zoned
OR.

The property is subdivided into lots on the plat named The Avery Townhomes.
Previously it was part of the Victoria Woods subdivision. The Avery Townhomes plat
was approved by the Planning Commission on December 10, 2020. This year the
applicant submitted applications requesting changes to the City’s Land Use Map and
Zoning Map that would allow the applicant to build 21 units instead of the
previously approved 17. Those applications were given a positive recommendation

Development Agreement process. The applicant is requesting in this Development
Agreement that the city agree to rezone the property to RM2 in exchange for the
applicant agreeing to limit the residential units constructed to 21.

Agreement. It does, however; contain a list of items that are required as part of an

as:

       C.     Procedure: All development agreements, upon proper execution, shall
              be recorded with the respective County Recorder's Office, shall run with
