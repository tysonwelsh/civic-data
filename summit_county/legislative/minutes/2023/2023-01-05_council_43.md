a.
Discussion and possible approval of the County Manager Employment Contract; Dave Thomas and David Warnock
Council Chair Armstrong provided background information regarding the search process for a new County Manager.
Dave Thomas, Civil Chief Deputy Attorney, provided an overview of the Employment Contract for the new County Manager, Shayne C. Scott.
Incoming County Manager, Shayne Scott, addressed Council Members.
Chris Robinson made a motion to appoint Shayne Clay Scott as the new manager of Summit County, to approve the Employment Contract presented and to authorize the Chair to execute such Employment Contract Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
