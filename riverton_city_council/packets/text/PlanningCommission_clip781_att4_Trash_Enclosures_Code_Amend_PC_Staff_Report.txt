Background

In April of 2025 Riverton City made substantial changes to the City’s Code for Commercial
Architectural Standards. These changes in title 18 impacted eight (8) chapters and multiple
sections of City code. With all the deletions and additions to code, Dumpster Closures were
overlooked and mistakenly removed from the code, without being replaced. This proposed code
amendment seeks to restore standards for commercial trash encloures.

Text removed by Ordinance 25-12
(d) Trash Enclosures, Storage Areas, and External Structures. Trash enclosures, storage areas,
and other external structures shall be screened by landscaping, fencing, berms or other devices
integral to overall site and building design. Trash and storage areas shall be comparable to the
proposed or existing building and with surrounding structures. These areas shall be well
maintained and oriented away from public view. The consolidation of trash areas between
businesses and the use of modern disposal and recycling techniques are encouraged. Chain link
fences and fencing with vinyl slats are prohibited.




Proposed Text
(5) Dumpster Enclosures and outdoor storage areas. Any garbage dumpster, garbage can, or
recycling bin shall be enclosed by a solid wall or fence constructed with durable materials that are
designed consistent with the building as approved by the land use authority.         The enclosure
must surround all four sides and be accessed by a gate. The enclosure wall and gate shall be a
minimum of twelve inches (12”) higher than the trash receptacle bin. These areas shall be
oriented away from public view and shall be well maintained. Where a garbage dumpster is
used, the enclosure shall be a solid masonry wall and the gate shall be painted or powder coated
metal. The land use authority may approve an enclosure with a solid wood or solid vinyl fence
and gate for an encloser that is exclusively for residential style garbage cans or recycle bins that
will be rolled to the street for pick up.
