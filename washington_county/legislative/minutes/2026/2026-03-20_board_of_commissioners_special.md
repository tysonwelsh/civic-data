WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
MARCH 20, 2026

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 10:05 a.m. on March 20, 2026, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.
INVOCATION BY: Jamie Allen

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3724, A RESOLUTION APPROVING

A_PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR MARKET VALUE APPRAISAL
SERVICE FOR A PRACEL IN THE RED CLIFFS NCA

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained this is the last private inholding in Zone 3 of the
Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP). As outlined within the HCP, the County is obligated to facilitate
the purchase of lands by the federal government.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3724,
A Resolution Approving a Professional Services Agreement for Market Value
Appraisal Service for a Parcel in the Red Cliffs NCA. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3725, A RESOLUTION APPROVING

A HOSTING/SPONSOR AGREEMENT WITH HOLBROOK, LLC

Greater Zion Sports Marketing Manager Chad McWilliams stated this Resolution is for a
hosting/sponsorship agreement with Holbrook for use of the pickleball and wellness deck of the
County Parking Garage.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3725,
A Resolution Approving a Hosting/Sponsor Agreement with Holbrook, LLC.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3728, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_MEMORANDUM_OF UNDERSTANDING WITH THE BLM _ DESIGNATING
WASHINGTON COUNTY AS A COOPERATING AGENCY FOR THE DEVELOPMENT
OF THE HIGH DESERT TRAIL ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT

Destination Development Manager Leslie Fonger explained the Bureau of Land Management
(BLM) are conducting an environmental assessment for the High Desert Trail, and Washington
County would like to be established as a cooperating agency.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3728, A
Resolution Approving a Memorandum of Understanding with the BLM
Designating Washington County as a Cooperating Agency for the
Development of the High Desert Trail Environmental Assessment. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
