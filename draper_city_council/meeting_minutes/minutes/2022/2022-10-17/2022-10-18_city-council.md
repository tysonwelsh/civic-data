3.0   Public Comments

3.1   Han McMullan, Draper resident, said he was currently a 10-year-old member
      of the Wetland Rangers, a group of kids who lived in the neighborhoods
      around Mehraban Wetland Park in Draper. He spoke of an algae bloom that
      was in the pond for the third year in a row and said the algae looked like
      sewage and it was bad for the fish. Mr. McMullan said the Wetland Rangers
      and members of the Sunset Pond HOA were asking for the City’s help in solving
      the problem. He presented the Council with a letter explaining concerns and
      suggestions.

3.2   Mayor Pro Tem Lowry commented that the City was aware of the algae
      problem at the pond and was working on a solution.

6.0   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1566, an Ordinance of Draper City amending the
      text of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code
      relating to standards for the location, design, and construction of temporary
      parking, and parking for recreation vehicles and trailers.

6.1   City Planner Todd Draper explained the proposed ordinance would amend
      surfacing requirements and would clarify that temporary parking would be
      allowed only while a temporary use permit was in effect. The Planning
      Commission forwarded a positive recommendation.

6.2   Mayor Pro Tem Lowry opened a public hearing.

6.3   Steve Crook, Draper resident, asked if the proposed language would apply to
      his personal RV, and whether or not he could park his RV on grass. Mr. Draper
      responded that permanent parking of a personal RV on grass would be a
      violation.

6.4   Mr. Crook asked for a definition of temporary RV parking. Staff responded the
      City did not have a definition for temporary RV parking in City Code, except as
      it related to temporary use or special event permits. Code enforcement in
      Draper City was complaint driven.

6.5   Mayor Pro Tem Lowry closed the public hearing.

6.6   Councilmember Green moved to approve Ordinance #1566. Councilmember
      T. Lowery seconded the motion.

7.0   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1567, an Ordinance amending the Official Zoning
      Map of Draper City for approximately 3.3 acres of property from RA1
      (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot minimum lot size) to RA2
      (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot minimum lot size), located at
      approximately 13101 S. Boulter Street within Draper City, otherwise known as
      the Patterson Property Zoning Map Amendment.

7.1   Planning Manager Jennifer Jastremsky showed an aerial view of the property
      and surrounding neighborhood and explained the request to rezone the
      property to RA2 Zone (two units per acre).

7.2   Mayor Pro Tem Lowry opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing
      seeing that no one came forward to speak.

7.3   Councilmember T. Lowery moved to approve                  Ordinance    #1567.
      Councilmember Vawdrey seconded the motion.

7.4   A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
