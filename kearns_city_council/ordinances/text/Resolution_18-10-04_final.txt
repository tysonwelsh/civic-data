A RESOLUTION OF THE METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AUTHORIZING A DONATION
OF A TRAILER FOR TO THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT
RECITALS
A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) As a Utah municipality, may pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-8-2, appropriate money of the municipality for any purpose that in
the judgment of the Metro Township Council provides for the safety, health, prosperity,
moral well-being, peace, order, comfort or convenience of the inhabitants of the Township.
B. The Kearns Metro Township receives its police services from the Unified Police Department
C. The Unified Police Department currently provides some traffic enforcement with the
Township by way of a motorcycle patrolman.
D. The Council feels it is in the benefits the Township to encourage the Unified Police
Department to continue its motorcycle patrol in the Township.
E. The Unified Police Department is more likely to continue the motorcycle patrol in the
Township if it has a trailer to use in conjunction with the motorcycle.
F. The Township has funds available within its current budget to pay for such a trailer.
G. The Township is willing to gift the trailer to the Unified Police Department if the Unified
Police Department is willing to accept such gift.
RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council that it
does hereby finds:
That the purchase of a trailer and its gift of the trailer to the Unified Police Department
will enhance the health safety and welfare of the residents of the Kearns Metro Township; and
That the Mayor of the Township is hereby authorized to effectuate the gift a trailer to the
Unified Police Department so long as the total value of the trailer does not exceed
es

This resolution shall take effect upon passage.
