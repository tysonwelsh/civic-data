D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: You have heard the phrase “people are a product of their
   circumstances”. I have a different thought that says, “You are not a product of your circumstances; you are a
   product of your decisions.” I think it’s easy to fall back on our circumstances when things go wrong or get
   difficult or when we make poor decisions. I believe we can all overcome bad circumstances and make ourselves
   a product of our decisions regardless of our circumstances.

    5. ADOPT THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL BUDGET FOR WEBER COUNTY FOR
       THE 2025 CALENDAR YEAR.

         Scott Parke (County Comptroller): This process started in August to begin gathering information for the
         2025 budget. A copy of the proposed budget will be available on the Weber County website until the public
         hearing on November 26th where we will receive input from the public. A final budget will be adopted on
         December 17th. Some highlights from the proposed budget:
              Employees would receive 1.5% COLA and 2% pay-for-performance increases,
              Health insurance costs increased by 9.5%,
              Couple of new positions in the:
                      o Attorney’s office,
                      o Two resource officers for the new high school and junior high school,
                      o Two full time workers in the Animal Shelter
                      o Various grant-funded positions
              Also included is $1.25M set aside for capital improvements under Culture, Parks, And Recreation
                 that comes from tourism funds.
              There is $10.5M set aside for other infrastructure and facility improvement projects paid from
                 various funds, some of which are restricted for those specific improvements.
              There is about $16M in ARPA funds left to be spent this year, some of which will spill over in to
                 2025.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to adopt the tentative operating and capital budget for Weber County for the 2025
         calendar year; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye

                  Scott Parke (County Comptroller): The 2024 Budget has adjustments being proposed. Anytime we
                  have an increase in revenue or expenses, by statute we have to have a public hearing, even in cases
                  such as this one where we are increasing revenues and expenses by the same amount.
                  We have received additional grant revenue of $2.4M that we need to spend. We received $100k
                  donation to the Animal Shelter that we would like to use on capital improvements. And we have
                  additional revenue to the Ice Sheet, Shooting Complex, and Building Inspection that we are asking
                  permission to spend to help cover the cost of providing those services. There are other capital
                  projects and repairs such as infrastructure at Eden Acres that will come from impact fees, repairs to
                  paramedic vehicles coming from fund balance earmarked for that purpose, and some money that
                  comes in for CRAs that we are required to pass through to the responsible agency. And one of the
                  final items is a $6M request that is not actually a budget increase for the Ogden Eccles Conference
                  Center renovation but simply moving money from 2025’s budget to 2024 since that project is a little
                  ahead of schedule.
