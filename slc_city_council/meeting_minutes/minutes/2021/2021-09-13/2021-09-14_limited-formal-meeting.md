**Resolution: Local Emergency Declaration Extension (COVID)**
The Council will consider adopting a resolution that would extend the Mayor’s August 20 proclamation that declared a local emergency related to COVID-19, rising cases, and resurgence of the Delta variant. If extended, the related emergency order for masks in K-12 schools, would also be extended.

**FYI – Project Timeline:** (subject to change per Chair direction or Council discussion)
Briefing - Tuesday, September 14, 2021
Set Public Hearing Date - n/a
Hold hearing to accept public comment - n/a
TENTATIVE Council Action - Tuesday, September 14, 2021

  Staff Recommendation - Suspend the rules and consider motions.

**Motion:
Moved by Councilmember Wharton, seconded by Councilmember Dugan to **adopt Resolution 32 of 2021, Local Emergency Declaration (COVID) Extension for 30 days and review again at the October 12, 2021 meeting.****

**AYE:** Dennis Faris, Daniel Dugan, Darin Mano, Amy Fowler, Ana Valdemoros, Chris Wharton

**NAY:** James Rogers

**Final Result:**6 – 1
