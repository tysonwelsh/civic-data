                                    PROCLAMATION

                                May 2023 Bike Month

Whereas, throughout the month of May, Draper City will celebrate biking and the
freedom, the joy, and the well-being it imbues within us, as well as the power that
more people riding bikes has in making life better for everyone; and

Whereas, May 15-21, 2023 is “Bike to Work Week” and May 19, 2023 is “Bike to
Work Day”; and

Whereas, throughout the month of May, the residents of Draper City and its
visitors will experience the joys of bicycling through group rides, races, commuting
events, educational programs, charity events, and/or by simply getting out and
going somewhere by bike; and

Whereas, bicycling has been shown to improve citizens’ health, well-being, and
quality of life, growing the economy of Draper City attracting tourism dollars and
local business spending and reducing pollution, congestion and parking costs on
our streets and roads; and

Whereas, Draper City will be joining the League of American Bicyclists in promoting
bicycling during the month of May 2023; and

Whereas, we are also promoting the use of the bicycle as both a means of
transportation and recreation year-round to attract more visitors to enjoy our local
parks and trail systems, as well as restaurants, hotels, retail establishments, and
cultural and scenic attractions; and

Whereas, we are also promoting greater public awareness of bicycle operation and
safety education during Bike Month and year-round in an effort to reduce collisions,
injuries and fatalities and improve health and safety for everyone on the road; and

Now therefore, I, Mayor Walker do hereby proclaim May 2023 as Bike Month
in Draper City and I urge all residents to join me in this special observance.

Attest:                                             Draper
