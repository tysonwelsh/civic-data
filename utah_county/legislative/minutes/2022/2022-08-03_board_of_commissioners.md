DISCUSSION BETWEEN BURT HARVEY - TAX ADMINISTRATION SUPERVISOR,
MARLON L. BATES, ATTORNEY ON BEHALF OF SECURITY SREVICE FEDERAL
CREDIT UNION, PAUL JONES - DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY, COMMISSIONER
SAKIEVICH, COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER AND HALEY ADAMS -
REPRESENTING HER MOTHER, MRS. COLLINS.

PAUL JONES CONFIRMED THAT WE DID NOT NOTIFY THE TRUSTEE PRIOR TO THE
TAX SALE SO THE PROTEST IS VALID. HOWEVER, THERE ARE EXCESS PROCEEDS
FROM THE SALE OF THE PROPERTY THAT COULD SATISFY THE TAX, MAKE THE
CREDIT UNION WHOLE AND HAVE SOME FUNDS AVAILABLE FOR MRS. COLLINS.

PAUL JONES EXPLAINS THE NEW MUNICIPLE CODE REGARDING EXCESS
PROCEEDS - (MUNI CODE 3.04.150 - EXCESS PROCEEDS). THAT IN ORDER TO
RECEIVE THE PROCEEDS, HE WOULD NEED TO PETITION THE FOURTH DISTRICT
COURT AND GET A COURT ORDER RELEASING THE FUNDS TO HIS CLIENT.

MR. BATES CLARIFIES HOW TO MAKE A CLAIM ON THE EXCESS FUNDS BASED ON
THE NEW ORDINANCE. AS THE REPRESENTATIVE OF THE CREDIT UNION, HE
WOULD FILE A PETITION WITH THE FOURTH DISTRICT COURT TO GET A COURT
ORDER RELEASING THE FUNDS TO HIS CLIENTS.

PAUL JONES CONFIRMS.

MR. BATES STATES THEY ARE FINE PETITIONING THE COURT SO IT IS OKAY FOR
THE COUNTY NOT TO SET ASIDE THE TAX SALE.

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH CONFIRMS THAT MR. BATES IS OK THAT THE COUNTY
DENY THE TAX SALE PROTEST.

MR. BATES CONFIRMS THAT IF THERE ARE PROCEEDS SUFFICIENT TO PAY HIS
CLIENT THE APPROXIMATELY $22,500.00 THEY ARE OWED, HE WOULD BE
WILLING TO WITHDRAW THE PROTEST OF THE TAX SALE ITSELF.

PAUL JONES ENCOURAGED A CLEAR STATEMENT FROM MR. BATES THAT HE IS
WITHDRAWING HIS APPEAL IF THAT IS HIS REQUEST.

MR. BATES AGAIN RECOUNTS THAT HE IS WILLING TO GO THROUGH THE COURT
TO RECOVER THE EXCESS FUNDS AVAILABLE TO SATISFY THE CREDIT UNION AND
WITHDRAW THE PROTEST OF THE TAX SALE.

HALEY ADAMS, SPEAKING FOR HER MOTHER MRS. COLLINS, STATES THEY WANT
THE TAX SALE TO GO THROUGH, SATISFY THE TAXES, SATISFY THE BANK AND
THEN HELP HER MOTHER WITH HER LIVING SITUATION.

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH CONFIRMS THAT THEY ARE OK WITH THE COUNTY
DENYING THE PROTEST.

HALEY ADAMS REPONDS IN THE AFFIRMATIVE.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER CONFIRMS THAT THEY REALIZE THAT THE
COUNTY WILL HOLD THE FUNDS UNTIL THE BANK GETS THEIR EXCESS FUNDS
