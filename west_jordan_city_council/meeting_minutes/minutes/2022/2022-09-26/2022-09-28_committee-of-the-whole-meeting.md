 1. CALL TO ORDER
Vice Chair Green called the Committee of the Whole to order at 5:33 pm. He noted that Council
Member Bloom was present remotely via Zoom, and Council Members Whitelock and Worthen were
excused. He explained that there must be four votes in favor to pass any resolution, ordinance, or
formal action. Procedural votes were approved by simple majority.


 2. DISCUSSION TOPICS
         a. Discussion Regarding At-Large Council Seat Amendments
            Council Office Director Alan Anderson said the Council requested that staff research at-
            large council seat elections. Currently, for at-large council seats in West Jordan, the top
            three candidates were selected. He explained that in Salt Lake County, the at-large seats
            were designated as A, B, or C, and candidates ran for a specific at-large seat. Mr. Anderson
            advised the Council to keep in mind the financial impacts a change would have. City
            Recorder/Elections Administrator Tangee Sloan showed costs to the City of the last at-
            large election, and estimated that a change to designated at-large seats would increase
            the cost for each seat by 1%-5%. She commented that there would be a new County Clerk,
            and possibly a new interlocal agreement and contract. Ms. Sloan said the Council would
            be presented with the ranked choice voting question every year through 2025, and said
            ranked choice voting would also involve a cost increase.

          Responding to a question from Vice Chair Green, Ms. Sloan noted anticipation of voter
          confusion with the designated plurality method. Administrative Services Director Danyce
          Steck shared election costs of previous years.

          Council Member Jacob commented that Salt Lake County staggered the at-large seat
          elections so they were not all in the same year, with six-year terms. He said he believed
          city councils were required by the State to have four-year terms. He said he would prefer
          at-large elections to be staggered, and suggested two at-large seats and five district seats.
          Council Member Jacob expressed concern that with three designated at-large seats open
          in the same election, potential candidates would file to run for the designated seat with
          the “weakest” candidates. Ms. Sloan said she had the same concern.

          Council Member Pack said there were pros and cons for both methods and said he did not
          see an inherent clear advantage to changing. Council Member Bloom expressed
          agreement, and said she was not in favor of changing to designated at-large seats.

          Council Member McConnehey said he felt the current method treated the at-large seats

          problem or a detriment. Council Member McConnehey expressed the opinion that the
          current method may discourage some individuals from running out of a desire to not take
          votes away from specific incumbents. He said he would like to see all seats treated equally,

          Vice Chair Green said he agreed with Council Member McConnehey. He commented that
          the current Council had rejected ranked choice voting multiple times, but the current at-
          large election method was as close as you could get to ranked choice voting. Vice Chair
          Green expressed the opinion that with the current method of at-large voting, candidates
          were not able to focus on the issues. He spoke of possible collusion with the current
          method. Council Member Jacob said he believed the same thing could happen with
          designated at-large elections. Council Member Bloom said she agreed with Council
          Member Jacob and said she did not see a change to designated at-large seats drastically
          changing how individuals campaigned.

          Responding to a question from Council Member Pack, Council Member Bloom stated she
          would not have campaigned differently if the previous election had been for designated
          at-large Council seats. Council Member McConnehey said he would have approached a
          designated at-large election differently as a voter and said he did not believe the current
          method was as reflective of the will of the people. Vice Chair Green said he agreed, and
          compared the current method to a popularity contest. Vice Chair Green said he would
          have campaigned differently with designated at-large seats.

          Council Member McConnehey left the dais at 6:07 pm, and returned at 6:09 pm

          Ms. Sloan repeated that she believed the proposed change would be confusing to the
          public. Council Member Jacob said he did not like the idea of ranked choice voting for
          multiple-winner seats.

          Council Member McConnehey said the biggest reason he would like to see the seats
          broken out individually was to give the voters recourse to hold their elected officials
          responsible and accountable.

          Council Member Jacob suggested the Council needed to research the legality of
          designating current at-large Council seats as A, B, and C, and the legality of dictating an
          incumbent could not run for a different at-large seat. Council Member McConnehey asked
          why the Council would want to limit which specific at-large seat an incumbent could
          campaign for.

          Council Member McConnehey said if changes were made, he would want to change the
          one-year district residency requirement for district seats to one-year residency in the
          City. Council Member Jacob said he believed the district residency requirement was State
          law.

       b. General Plan Process for Council
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson said the draft General Plan had been introduced to
          the Planning Commission. He said he anticipated bringing the draft General Plan to the
          Council in December 2022, and asked how the Council wanted to plan their review of the
          Planning Commission’s recommendation.

          Council Member Pack said he appreciated the open houses that had been held, and
          certainly wanted additional public hearings. He expressed appreciation for the committee
          that had worked hard on the General Plan, and said he wanted to ensure the tenor of the
          meeting was not to micromanage, but to gain more information and get everyone on the
          same page of understanding. Vice Chair Green commented that even vetting and verifying
          would take considerable time.

          Council Member McConnehey suggested the Council Members read through the
          document in advance of the meetings and bring their lists of points for discussion, rather
          than dedicating a meeting to each chapter. He expressed the opinion that town hall
          meetings and continued online engagement would be better for soliciting public feedback
          than traditional public hearings. Council Member Jacob said he was willing to leave the
          decision to Council Leadership. Council Member Bloom said she agreed with Council
          Member McConnehey’s suggestion, and said she would be willing to read through the
          document at home and bring a list of desired topics to the discussion.

          Vice Chair Green proposed scheduling Committee of the Whole meetings on regular
          Council meeting evenings, with Council Members directed to read the document and
          submit comments/concerns to the Council Office for consolidation prior to the meeting.
          Council Member McConnehey said he agreed, but would want each Council Member to
          take a few minutes at the meeting to explain their comments and concerns. Council
          Member Pack expressed support for providing as much information as possible to the
          public and Council prior to Council meetings.

          Council Member McConnehey suggested inviting members of the General Plan Committee
          to be in attendance to share reasons and perspectives. Mr. Anderson demonstrated note-
          taking and marking tools available with PrimeGov. Mr. Wall described a comment-sharing
          method he had seen in another city.

          Vice Chair Green commented that a celebration dinner needed to be planned for the
          General Plan Committee. City Administrator Korban Lee said he agreed appreciation
          needed to be shown.
