RESOLUTION 22-126: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A
MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH OTHER AGENCIES TO
CREATE THE SALT LAKE VALLEY REGIONAL PUBLIC ORDER UNIT
Chief Colleen Jacobs presented proposed Resolution 22-126 that would authorize the City
to Enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with Other Agencies to Create the Salt Lake
Valley Regional Public Order Unit.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       All Parties to this MOU have a Public Protection Unit or Public Order Unit (POU)
       concerned with preserving the public peace, preventing crime, arresting offenders,
       and protecting the rights of persons and property. The Parties desire that the POUs
       work as a team pursuant to this MOU under appropriate circumstances for the
       purpose of obtaining maximum efficiency in cooperative law enforcement
       operations.
       All parties are authorized to enter into this MOU pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-
       202, Agreements for Joint or Cooperative Undertaking.


       Local law enforcement agencies encountering recent civil unrest determined that
       resources were a primary concern. Established protocol for collective efforts was
       not in place and requests for assistance came in emergency situations and lacked
       proper planning and coordination. Establishment of the SLVR POU addresses these
       concerns and provides the ability for participating agencies to identify protocols for
       training, personnel, equipment, transportation, communication, and other
       resources.

Councilmember Harmon asked who the two other team leads are. Chief Jacobs replied
Utah Department of Public Safety and Salt Lake City. Councilmember Whetstone asked
what this unit might be used for. Chief Jacobs replied that a good example would be when
the City assisted Salt Lake City with the riots in downtown Salt Lake City in 2020. She
indicated that if the City were to experience a concert at the mall that got out of hand, for
example, we could also ask for assistance from this unit. Mayor Lang stated that West
Valley City received a lot of praise for their actions and professionalism in the 2020 Salt
Lake City riots.

The City Council will consider Resolution 22-126 at the Regular Council Meeting
scheduled August 16, 2022 at 6:30 P.M.

                     dedicated is part of the H2O Lofts development and is located at
                     approximately 2860 South 2050 West

                     Planning Commission in March 2022. During the City’s review of

                     sides of the street and sidewalk on one side.

                     However, with new residential development going in east of the
                     existing dedicated street, additional right-of-way is needed for
                     parkstrip and sidewalk improvements. The existing curb and gutter
                     will remain and will be modified only where new access points are
                     proposed.
