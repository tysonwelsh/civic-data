                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-28

A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING UP TO 1.00 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT
   APPROXIMATELY 12727 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD TO BE DEEDED TO
 RIVERTON CITY AS OPEN SPACE, BEING A PART OF THE RESIDENCES AT
                     PARK AVENUE PHASE 2

        WHEREAS, Riverton City had previously deeded approximately 1 acre of land located
at approximately 12727 South Redwood Road as to the Residences at Park Avenue to be
maintained as open space; and

      WHEREAS, that property was subsequently impacted by widening of the Redwood
Road corridor and other factors and has become under-maintained and unsightly; and

       WHEREAS, the City desires to make improvements to the property to maintain the
Riverton City Downtown area in a beautified state; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that acceptance by deed of
up to 1.00 acres of land, as shown on the attached Exhibit “A”, is in the general public interest;

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City
as follows:
        1. The Riverton City Council hereby accepts by deed up to 1.00 acres of separated
           from the Residences at Park Avenue Phase 2 as described herein.
        2. Riverton City accepts maintenance of and responsibility for the existing storm
           water ponds on that property.
        3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
