Planner Tiffanie Northrup-Robinson said this was discussed at a previous Public 
Hearing held in March.  The proposal impacts certain lots of the Aspen Creek Crossing 
Subdivision, which was approved in the year 2000.  It was designated to be a 
hotel/lodging site.  It was to have cabins with a central check-in area; however, this 
plan never came to fruition.  This was later modified to a single-family home 
subdivision.    
 
A map was displayed of the Aspen Creek Subdivision.  The lots with current residences 
were highlighted.  Some are over the allowed square footage.  In 2005, a planner at that 
time, Nora Shepard, issued a memo allowing exclusions to what would be included in 
the calculations of the maximum square footage.  The memo allowed for exclusions that 
the land use and zoning (LUZ) chart did not allow for.     
 
Planner Northrup-Robinson said when they received applications to build single-family 
dwellings in the last couple of years the gross square footage was included.  This was 
according to the LUZ.  There was a difference between the square footage the LUZ 
included and the Nora Shepard memo.  Any changes to the LUZ had to go through the 
proper process.  This did not take place; therefore, the Planning Department has to 
apply the regulations of the LUZ.  This means that everything has to be applied to the 
gross square footage.   
 
Bruce Cummings submitted an application for an amendment to the Canyons LUZ 
chart on behalf of multiple property owners in March 2017.  The amendment would 
state:  
“The following areas shall not be included in the calculation of Maximum 
Gross Building Area: (a) up to 650 square feet of garage area, and (b) 
basement area as defined by applicable codes.”
