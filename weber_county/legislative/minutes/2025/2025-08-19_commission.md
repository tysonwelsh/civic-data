                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                   Tuesday, August 19th, 2025 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Sharon Bolos James “Jim” H. Harvey, and Gage Froerer (via Zoom)
STAFF PRESENT: Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Chris Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and Craig D.
Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME: Chair Bolos
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: All in attendance were invited to recite the Pledge.
C. INVOCATION/ MOMENT OF SILENCE: Shelly Halacy
D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Employee Recognition – Alicia Gamon from the Weber-Morgan Health Department
      Brian Cowan (Weber-Morgan Health Department Director): We are excited to recognize Alicia for the
      efforts she makes for the Health Department. She was hired a few years ago is a community health worker
      position as part of our Covid response. Through her efforts and others working in that community health
      worker position we learned of gaps we had in connecting our resources to our community members. Alicia
      thrives at helping our department to reach out to our community and work with some of the population we do
      not typically engage on a regular basis. She has helped build those connections and get those resources to
      community members that need them most. We really appreciate the work she does.

E. PUBLIC COMMENT: NONE.

   2. Warrants #105719-105770 and #492471-492567 in the amount of $1,192,721.86.
   3. Summary of Warrants and Purchase Orders.
   4. New business licenses.
   5. Refund $231.98 on Parcel #40-305-0520. This is a state assessed property. Under State Code 59-2-1321 and

   7. Sponsorship agreement with MTN OPS, LLC. MTN Ops, LLC will place branded non-permanent signs in the
      Archery classrooms and the sponsors employees will be permitted to use the range during business hours at no

        RENOVATION FEASIBILITY STUDY SERVICES.
