MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Tevai Haunga
Date: 2024-04-11
Re: Public Hearing: Bench Internal Accessory Dwelling Unit (I-ADU) Deviation Request
(Administrative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Deviation for approximately 0.54 acre lot
located on the south side of 13200 S., at approximately 968 E. 13200 S. The property is
currently zoned RA2 and is located within a mapped area where I-ADU's are prohibited
without approval of a deviation. The applicant is requesting that a Deviation be approved
to allow for the creation of an I-ADU within a proposed primary dwelling on the subject
property.




The findings for approval as are follows:



  1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-
     thousand square feet (20,000 ft²);
  2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
  3. The proposed I-ADU meets all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-
     045, including other applicable requirements of the chapter; and
  4. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are
     provided for use by occupants of the I-ADU.
