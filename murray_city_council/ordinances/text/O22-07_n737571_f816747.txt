ORDINANCE NO. 22-07

i AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 17.112.020 OF THE MURRAY
CITY MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO INCLUDING TWIN-HOMES AS
A PERMITTED USE IN THE R-2-10 ZONE

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Murray City Municipal Council as
follows:

Section 1. Purpose. The purpose of this Ordinance is to amend chapter
17.112.020 of the Murray City Municipal Code relating to including twin-homes as a
permitted use in the R-2-10 zone.

Section 2. Amendment. Chapter 17.112.020 of the Murray City Municipal Code
relating to including twin-homes as a permitted use in the R-2-10 zone is amended to
read as follows:

17.112.020: PERMITTED USES:

A. All uses and structures contained herein are listed by number as designated in the
Standard Land Use Code published and maintained by the Planning Department.

i B. The following uses are permitted in the R-2-10 Zone:
