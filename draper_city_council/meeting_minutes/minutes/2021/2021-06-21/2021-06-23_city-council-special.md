Business Session

1.0   Call to Order: Mayor Troy K. Walker



2.0   Action Item: Ordinance #1495, an Ordinance of Draper City Council Prohibiting
      the discharge of Fireworks in specified areas.
2.1   Mayor Walker introduced the item, stating that fireworks have been the “hot
      topic” in the state. He reminded the Council that based on the state code,
      Draper cannot ban fireworks in the city. The Council can restrict fireworks and
      has for several years. Using a map displayed on the screen, he pointed out the
      red area where the ignition of fireworks is prohibited. It is a crime and a $1000
      penalty and Draper City will enforce the law. The State law allows cities to
      protect themselves as best they can.
2.2   Fire Chief Clint Smith will present the recommendations for the restrictions.
      The Chief used the map to show where fireworks were restricted in past years.
      The areas in red are a high-risk area since much of it is in the Wildland Urban
      Interface Area.
      Chief Smith showed on the map, the references to the state code that allows
      cities to change the restriction map after the May 1st deadline for the map to
      be published.
      Chief Smith pointed out the areas to be included in the new restrictions map.
      The areas to be added are overgrown and have accessibility issues. Chief
      Smith reviewed the fuels in Utah and Energy Release Component which
      measures the fuels. Utah has the highest rate in the country.

2.3    Mike Barker, City Attorney, asked that if the council passes this ordinance, that
       the code box on the map be updated to include 1.1.3
       Mayor Walker also mentioned the code reference 1.1.1 should also be added

2.4    Lieutenant Harris stated the police have four days covered with four extra
       people for those shifts with an understanding of zero tolerance.

2.5    Fire Chief Clint Smith stated the fire department will have about 10 additional
       staff scheduled for the weekend
2.6    Councilmember T. Lowery thanked Chief Smith and the police department for
       their preparedness.
2.7    Mayor Walker reminded everyone that Draper City is offering 3 free fireworks
       shows during Draper Days and asked if people could take this year off from
       personal fireworks.
2.8    Councilmember F. Lowry wanted to encourage residents to be vigilant and be
       careful and to educate each other.
       Mayor Walker stated there had already been a fire, started by fireworks set off
       in a banned zone, in Utah this year.

2.9    Councilmember T. Lowery moved to approve Ordinance #1495.
       Councilmember Vawdrey seconded the motion.
2.10   Councilmember Vawdrey asked if the addition of 1.1.1 and 1.1.3 should be
       added to the motion.

       Councilmember T. Lowery stated that she would amend her motion to include
       the addition of 1.1.1 and 1.1.3 in the Utah Code box of the map.
       Councilmember Vawdrey stated that her second still stood.

2.11   A roll call vote was taken with Councilmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
       and Vawdrey, voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

2.13   Don Buckley, Fire Marshal, stated that a map had been created with a QR Code
       that once scanned, takes the user to an app, he or she can put in their address
       and it will tell them if their address is in a restricted zone. These maps will be
       everywhere fireworks are sold in Draper.
