MEMO
To: City Council
From: Mike Barker, City Manager
Date: 2024-10-15
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1625


Comments:

David Nixon, who resides at 987 Old English Rd, is requesting the City Council vacate the
right-of way adjacent to his property. The ROW was dedicated to the City for use as a
future road upon approval of the Wild Rose Subdivision - Phase 2 plat in 1995. At the
time the City also entered into a development agreement with the developer that
permitted the two properties owners adjacent to the ROW to make use of the ROW as
part of their property until such time as the City utilized the ROW as a road to connect to
Country Lane.

Country Lane is a private lane that abuts the ROW to the east. Owners of property
adjacent to Country Lane own the property upon which Country Lane lies.

Pursuant to Utah Code 72-5-105 title to the vacated ROW "shall vest to the adjoining record
owners, with one-half of the width of the highway, street, or road assessed to each of the adjoining
owners." If the City Council vacates the ROW, title to half of the ROW will vest, by operation
of law, with Councilmember Lowry and his wife as his property is adjoining the south
boundary of the ROW.
