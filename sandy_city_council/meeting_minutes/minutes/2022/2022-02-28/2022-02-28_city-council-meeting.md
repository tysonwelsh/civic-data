# City Council Meeting — 2022-02-28
> Source: https://sandyutah.legistar.com/Calendar.aspx
> Meeting date: 2022-02-28
> Format: text

---


Minutes:



Meeting began at 6:00 PM and was held in the city council chambers at Sandy City Hall

Council Attendance: Brooke D’Sousa, Alison Stroud, Monica Zoltanski, Ryan Mecham, Cyndi Sharkey,
Scott Earl

Other Attendance: Richard Benham, Tom Ward, Justin Dietrich, Abi Holt, Mayor Zoltanski, Scott Ellis

Agenda:

    1. Welcome & Introduction – Mayor Zoltanski

Mayor Zoltanski introduced the townhall and welcomed the public.

    2. Town Hall Program & Water Overview – Tom Ward, Public Utilities Director

Tom Ward gave directions on how to participate in the meeting and introduced the topic of the town
hall.

    3. Conservation Programs, Rebates & Resources – Abi Holt, Water Business and Conservation
       Manager

Abi Holt updated the public on what was accomplished related to water conservation over the past year,
including conservation programs.

The meeting was opened for Q&A from the town hall attendees.

    4. Rate Structure Review – Justin Dietrich, Bowen Collins & Associates

Justin Dietrich presented information regarding the water conservation rate structure.

Tom Ward covered the next steps in the city conversation surrounding water and water rates.

Richard Benham led the attendees through a poll aimed at gaining an understanding of how the
attendees view the future of water rates in sandy city in the future.

    5. Open Water Q&A

General Q&A was opened for attendees.

7:45 Council Attendance: Brooke D’Sousa, Alison Stroud, Monica Zoltanski, Cyndi Sharkey, Scott Earl

Mayor Zoltanski left closing remarks regarding water in Sandy City

The meeting adjourned at 7:
