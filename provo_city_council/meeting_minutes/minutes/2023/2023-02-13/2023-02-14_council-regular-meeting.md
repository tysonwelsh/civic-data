2.         A public hearing regarding HOME ARP and accompanying funds. (23-027) (18:07)

Melissa McNalley, Community Grant Administrator, provided brief history of the funding and discussed
what it is intended for. She reminded council that the funding could be used throughout Utah County
and is not just for Provo. She also shared what the next steps are in developing the plan on how to use
the funds.

Councilor Fillmore asked if there were numbers for the concentration of homeless persons in the
county.

Ms. McNalley said she could provide those numbers but to her knowledge that the higher concentration
would be here in Provo due to most of the services being offered in Provo.

Councilor Fillmore added that having those numbers would be helpful for considering where to allocate
the majority of the funds.

Chair MacKay said that council is anxious to see the numbers from this year’s count from the Continue
of Care.

Ms. McNalley explained that when funds are used, there must be justification so the numbers will play a
big role in how they plan to use the funds.

Councilor Handley asked if she agreed with what Ms. Wilkey said in her public comment about there
being a gap in mid-range housing type. He also asked if the County is involved in the decision-making
process to force other cities to participate or if it would end up being a city issue.

Ms. McNalley responded and said the County has not been involved but she would like them to be.

Councilor Handley suggested they coordinate and plea with the County for involvement.

Councilor Whipple asked how much longer the ARP funds would be available.

Ms. McNalley said there is a deadline for the plan on how the funds would be used. She added that after
the plan is submitted and accepted, the funds could be used through 2030.

Chair MacKay thanked Ms. McNalley for her presentation and work.
