PRESENTATION: PARAMEDIC OF THE YEAR: COLLEEN HOMER

Dammeron Valley Special Service District Chair Mack Sorenson explained that Colleen Homer
was honored as the paramedic of the year for the rural areas off Utah. She has trained over twenty
(20) paramedics during her time and contributes a great deal to the community.

The Commissioners expressed their gratitude for her service to the Dammeron Valley community.

PRESENTATION: TWCA ROBOTICS TEAM

The Team Without a Cool Acronym (TWCA) is a nine (9) member team from ages 13-18 that
builds robots for various competitions. The team presented to the Commission about their recent
robotics competition, the 2022 For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology
(FIRST) Championship in Houston Texas, and were ranked in the top two percent (2%). The team
presented the Commissioners with plaques as a thank you for their sponsorship and support of the
team.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1208-O, AN ORDINANCE REZONING A
CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY ON HIDDEN VALLEY ROAD
NEAR LEEDS, FROM THE A-20 (AGRICULTURAL 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE)
ZONE, TO THE A-5 (AGRICULTURAL 5 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE,
HEREINAFTER, FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS ORDINANCE (ZION VINEYARDS)

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the applicants are proposing to
change the zoning designation of a parcel near the Town of Leeds in order to utilize the property
for a future winery vineyard that would also include agritourist uses. The Planning Commission
reviewed the zone change and unanimously recommended to approve the zone change request
from the A-20 zone to the A-5 zone.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2022-1208-O, An
Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County on Hidden
Valley Road Near Leeds, from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot
Size) Zone, to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 Acre Minimum Lot Size) zone,
Hereinafter, Fully Described in this Ordinance (Zion Vineyards). Motion
seconded by Chairman Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson an Almquist voting aye.

Mr. Messel explained that the applicant is proposing to change the zoning designation of a parcel
located near Enterprise, in order to build a single family residence. The Planning Commission
recommended approval of the zone change based on the following conditions: The zone change
and public hearing were properly noticed; The public hearing was held on April 12, 2022 in the
regularly scheduled Planning Commission Meeting; The proposed zone change to the A-5
(Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone would allow the property owners to build a home and
utilize their property in a beneficial way; Bench Road and 3200 East shall be dedicated as county
roads by the applicant; The adjacent Parcel 7004-A-1-A-1-NC shall also record the dedication of
Holt Canyon Road as a county road in conjunction; The northern 2.55 acre portion of the parcel
shall not be permitted for a dwelling; Septic density has been approved by the Washington County
Water Conservancy District; Utilities are available to the property; and the proposal is consistent
with the Washington County General Plan.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2022-1209-O, An
Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County at
Approximately Bench Road and 3200 East near Enterprise, from the A-20
(Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone, to the A-5 (Agricultural 5
Acre Minimum Lot Size Zone), Hereinafter Fully Described in this Ordinance
(Applicant Kyle Preston). Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson an Almquist voting

aye.
