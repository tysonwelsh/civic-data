COMMISSIONER AINGE - WE NOW HAVE A PUBLIC HEARING TO DISCUSS THE
ADOPTION OF A RESOLUTION FIXING THE SALARIES OF UTAH COUNTY
ELECTED OFFICIALS. I'M HAPPY TO GIVE A LITTLE BIT OF AN INTRODUCTION
TO THIS ONE BEFORE WE GO INTO THE PUBLIC HEARING. THERE'S TWO
COMPONENTS. ONE IS AS IT RELATES TO ALL OF THE ELECTED OFFICIALS, THE
OTHER IS AS IT RELATES TO THE COUNTY COMMISSION. WE HAVE NOT HAD A
CHANCE TO DISCUSS THIS, REALLY AT ALL, AS MEMBERS OF THE COUNTY
COMMISSION. SO, I'LL SHARE A FEW THOUGHTS.

THE FIRST, AS IT RELATES TO THE OTHER POSITIONS - I KNOW OUR HUMAN
RESOURCES DIRECTOR AS WE GEARED UP FOR THIS BUDGET PROCESS, AND
WE'VE BEEN TALKING ABOUT MARKET STUDIES AND COST OF LIVING, HE
REALLY ENCOURAGED US TO LOOK AT THE SALARIES OF ELECTED OFFICIALS
