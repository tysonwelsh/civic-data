E. PUBLIC COMMENTS:
   --Brent Hipwell, of West Weber, said that West Weber residents have been having discussions and they urge
   the Commission to amend the West Central Weber County General Plan but for now to follow the 2003 Plan as
   it was written, that many people took a lot of time and effort to provide it, that residents believed that the
   Commission would follow it, and where amendments are being made, it is like a slap in the face to the people
   of unincorporated areas who felt it was a document to protect and help them steer their area’s growth. He
   recommended a moratorium on large development until the General Plan is amended and residents have
   opportunity for input. He would like a policy for area signs noticing meetings/issues as many residents do not
   have opportunity to attend/watch online meetings. Rick Grover, County Planning Division Director, stated
   that County Planning sends out public notices, most are courtesy notices, and is working to get A-frame signs
   for subdivision and conditional uses and this should be in place soon. Commissioner Ebert recommended
   using the signs for General Plan amendments and rezones and Mr. Grover concurred.
   --Lori Brinkerhoff, Weber County Republican Women president, extended an invitation to the November 5th
   meeting to hear an engaging speaker, Brian Besser, on opioids in Utah.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
      1. CONTRACT WITH FFKR ARCHITECTS TO PREPARE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION DOCUMENTS FOR THE
         DEMOLITION OF THE WEBER COUNTY PARKING STRUCTURE, ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN OF THE WEBER
         CENTER’S EAST BUILDING ELEVATION, AND DESIGN OF A NEW SURFACE PARKING LOT.

            Scott Mendoza, with County Community Development, presented the contract for $121,125 and outlined
            the work to be performed. The county had structural evaluations in 2013 and 2015 of the Weber Center
            parking structure, which was built in 1964. Both studies found damage and deterioration including
            delaminated traffic coatings, cracking concrete, corroded reinforcement, and deterioration of structure
            members. The reports also found that currently the structure is still safe for general load carrying capacity
            and stability. Woodbury Corporation, the owner of 25% of the space in the Weber Center will be
            participating in the contract by paying about $30,000. He noted that Attachment E, cost proposal, that was
            accidentally inserted by the architect and needed to be removed from the contract.
            Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve contract with FFKR Architects to prepare design and
            construction documents for the demolition of the Weber County parking structure, architectural design of
            the Weber Center’s east building elevation, and design of a new surface parking lot, delaying the design
            of the surface project 30 days, and removing Attachment E; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
            Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

    3. AMEND THE ORDINANCE GOVERNING LIBRARY POLICIES AND PROCEDURES – ORDINANCE 2018-18.

         Bryan Baron, Deputy County Attorney, noted that this ordinance was originally adopted in 2003
         establishing the Library Board and giving them authority to pass policies for the Library. Since that time,
         the Board has passed a policy regulating free speech/expressive activity by placing time, place and manner
         restrictions on free speech. This Spring the State legislature passed a new law, H.B. 36, which states that
         if a political subdivision has a policy regulating free speech it needs to have an ordinance that guides the
         adoption of a policy regulating it on public grounds. This amendment is for compliance with the new law.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Ordinance 2018-18 amending the ordinance governing library
         policies and procedures; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
