1)
Discussion and direction from Council regarding the Richins Annexation Petition (Parcel NS-831-B) into the Town of Henefer, Utah; County Planner: Laura Kuhrmeyer (4:10 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: NS-831-B Annexation into Henefer Staff Report
Laura Kuhrmeyer, Planner, presented the proposed annexation. Council Members commented and did not oppose the annexation.
2)
Discussion and direction from Council regarding the Burton Ranch Annexation Petition (Parcels CD-590, CD-599, and CD-600) into Francis City, Utah; County Planner: Laura Kuhrmeyer (4:13 PM)
Attachment: Burton Ranch Annexation into Francis Staff Report 6.5.24
Attachment: Cover Page
Laura Kuhrmeyer, Planner, presented the proposed annexation. The Council Members asked questions and commented. John Angell, Public Works Director, provided clarification. Council stated that they had no objections assuming the road issues are resolved.
3)
Discussion and Council direction, regarding Utah Retirement Systems Tier 2 pick up; Matt Leavitt and David Warnock (4:29 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report - URS Tier 2 increases - 2024.docx
David Warnock, Personnel Director, and Matt Leavitt, Financial Officer explained the current situation with Utah Retirement Systems and the Tier 2 system. Council members commented and asked questions. Director Warnock and Officer Leavitt responded. Council Members agreed with the proposal to make the employees whole with respect to the financial impact on the employees.
4)
Discussion and approval of Proclamation 2024-05, a Proclamation Declaring the month of June 2024, as Building Safety Month in Summit County, Utah; Richard Butz (4:41 PM)
Attachment: 2024 Building Safety Proclamation.pdf
Attachment: Cover Page
Richard Butz, Chief Building Official, presented the proposed Proclamation.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to approve Proclamation 2024-05 declaring the month of June 2024 Building Safety Month in Summit County, Utah. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0).
Shellie Barrus, Park City Area Home Builder’s Association, read Amy Herrold’s letter supporting the Building Safety Month
Attachment: Proclamation 2024-05 executed

Discussion and adoption of Resolution 2024-07, a Resolution Authorizing the Issuance and Sale of Approximately $30,000,000 of Sales Tax Revenue Bonds for County Capital Projects and Related Matters; Ryan Stack and Matt Leavitt (5:15 PM )
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report-2024 June 5 Sales Tax Bond Parameters Resolution.pdf

Ryan Stack, Deputy Civil Attorney, Matt Leavitt, Financial Officer, Brian Baker, Zion’s Bank Vice President, and Shenelle Salcido, Gilmore Bell Associate, presented a Resolution for issuance of bonds to fund the purchase and renovation of the Skull Candy building. The Public Hearing was set for June 26, 2024. Council Members asked questions, and the presenters responded.
Canice Harte made a motion to adopt Resolution 2024-07 authorizing the issuance and sale of approximately $30,000,000 of Sales Tax Revenue Bonds for County capital projects and related matters. (5:32 PM). Christopher Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0).
