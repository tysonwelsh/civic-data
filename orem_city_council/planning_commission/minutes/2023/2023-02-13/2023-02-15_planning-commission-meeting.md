 52   Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to
 53   demolish the old Chuck-A-Rama building at 1408 South
 54   State Street and build a new Mister Carwash branded
 55   tunnel carwash. Access to this project will be from 1400
 56   South and there will be no access, in or out, from State
 57   Street.
 58
 59   Architecture: The exterior of the building will be stone
 60   veneer, concrete block (CMU) and metal panels. The
 61   roof will be finished standing seam metal. This meets the
 62   building materials requirements outlined in Section 22-8-
 63   8(B)(5)(d)(i) for the State Street Corridor.
 64
 65   Entrance/Exit: Vehicles will enter and exit the property
 66   from 1400 South on the north side of the property. There
 67   will be no access to or from State Street on the east side
 68   of the property. The exit on the tunnel will be facing
 69   State Street, so the noise from the blowers will be
 70   minimal for surrounding businesses and the residential
 71   complex south of the project.
 72
 73   Parking: There is a specific parking requirement for car washes. It is one (1) parking stall per 600 square feet of
 74   gross floor area (excluding bay areas) is required, plus one stall per employee. However, it also says that “in no
 75   circumstance shall the number of parking stalls required by this Section be less than that required by the zone in
 76   which the use is located” which in this case is one (1) per 250 square feet. This project is meeting both requirements
 77   (22-15-4(G)(5)(a):
 78            • Office-Equipment area: 2,122 square feet / 250 = 8.5, so 9 required parking spaces
 79            • Parking provided: 12 parking spaces
 80
 81   Traffic: A Traffic Impact Study (TIS) was completed. After review by the City Engineer, the TIS was accepted and
 82   the proposed site plan reflects any related items of the Traffic Impact Study.
 83
 84   Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
 85   Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the site plan and final plat of Orem Mister Carwash
 86   located at 1408 South State Street in the C2 zone.

167   Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by J5 Infrastructure Partners for AT&T for the Planning Commission to recommend
168   approval of the conditional use permit of a telecommunications monopole facility to be located on a field light on
169   the East side of the football field at Timpanogos High School located generally at 150 East 1430 North in the R8
170   zone.
171
172   Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to replace an existing field light pole at Timpanogos High School
173   with a 100’ tall monopole with a lightning rod. The field light bank would be re-installed at its current height (71
174   feet) on the new pole. AT&T antennas and equipment would be installed at the top. The installation would also
175   include an equipment shelter to be built near the base of the monopole.
176
177    Monopoles are allowed to be placed in a residential
178   zone given that it is place on a lot which is not used for
179   a residential use, such as a school. Monopoles are
180   required to be at least 115% of the poles’ height from
181   any residential lot line. The proposed location of this
182   monopole at Timpanogos High School is at least 225’
183   away from surrounding residential lots. The location of
184   the proposed monopole at Timpanogos High School
185   was selected by the applicant based on the need for
186   service for this area. Since there is a light pole already
187   at this location, the proposed monopole would be
188   considered to be utilizing an existing structure per
189   Orem City Code (22-13-9), which outlines the priority
190   given to locations for wireless telecommunication
191   facilities.
192
193   The proposed placement of this monopole requires a
194   Conditional Use Permit for two reasons: height and the
195   distance to an existing monopole. According to Orem
196   City Code monopoles are allowed to be 75’ high, but in
197   the circumstance of need for greater coverage or to allow for collocation the height may be increase up to 110’. The
198   applicant is requesting a pole of 100’. Per Orem City Code (22-13-11(D)), monopoles are to be placed 1000’ feet
199   apart from each other. There currently is another monopole located on the East side of the football field at
200   Timpanogos High School by T-Mobile, this pole is already at maximum capacity for telecommunications
201   equipment. The proposed monopole is approximately 175’ from the existing pole, and will provide
202   future opportunities for collocation.
203
204   In considering the approval of a conditional use permit, the factors to be considered
205   are outlined in Orem City Code (22-4-4).
206
207   Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
208   Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for the
209   conditional use permit of an AT&T telecommunications monopole facility located on a field light at Timpanogos
210   High School located generally at 150 East 1430 North in the R8 zone.

229   Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by the Neighborhood Improvement Team for the City to amend portions of Section
230   22-6-10 Miscellaneous Regulations for Residential Zones pertaining to parking and storage of trucks, trailers and
231   RVs and Section 22-14-7(B)(2) Supplementary Regulations, Conservation of Values in residential zones.
232
233   Staff Presentation: The Neighborhood Improvement Team requests the Planning Commission review and make a
234   recommendation to the City Council to amend portions of Section 22-6-10 Miscellaneous Regulations for
235   Residential Zones and Section 22-14-7(B)(2) Supplementary Regulations, Conservation of Values in residential
236   zones to allow for parking on gravel surfaces/parking strips on residential property. 22-6-10 & 22-14-7
237
238   The change is requested due to the high cost of concrete and the challenging supply chain issues affecting the
239   availability of concrete. The change is also being requested due to the number of residents that are already parking
240   on gravel surfaces/parking strips on their property. Due to the sheer number, it is almost impossible to enforce the
241   current codes restricting parking on gravel. Several City Council members have also requested this Code change.
242
243   The proposed ordinance amendment would limit the location of the gravel parking area and the depth of the gravel.
244   The gravel parking strip must be maintained and free of weeds of any kind and no gravel shall cross onto
245   neighboring properties. A concrete or other fixed barrier at least four inches tall shall be placed between a
246   neighboring property and the gravel parking strip if it is within three feet of the property line. A vehicle parked in
247   the gravel parking strip may not block the clear view of the neighboring driveway.
