                              RESOLUTION NO. 24-41

A RESOLUTION DECLARING AS SURPLUS A PARCEL OF CITY PROPERTY LOCATED
AT 1300 EAST 13595 SOUTH

     WHEREAS, Draper City owns Parcel No. No. 34-04-101-045-0000 located at
approximately 1300 East 13595 South (the “Property”); and

      WHEREAS, Draper City no longer needs the Property outside of the right-of-
way; and

      WHEREAS, to best serve the interests of Draper residents the City Council
desires to surplus the Property and make it available for purchase at a future date.

      NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Council of Draper City:

      Section 1. Surplus Property. The Property described in Exhibit A is no
longer needed by the City for a public purpose and is declared surplus and its
disposal is authorized at a future date.

       Section 2. Severability Clause. If any section, part, or provision of this
Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall
affect any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of
this Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 20th DAY OF AUGUST, 2024.
