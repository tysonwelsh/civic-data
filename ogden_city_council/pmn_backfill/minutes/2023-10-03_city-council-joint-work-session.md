        The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agendas for the Special Redevelopment Agency (RDA) and City
Council meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.; discuss Fiscal Year (FY) 2024 Annual Action Plan Amendment #1 and FY
2023 Consolidated Annual Performance Evaluation Report (CAPER); discuss Council business; and hear Council comments.

Agenda Review
         Chair Choberka and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m. There was a brief focus on the agenda item relating to the proposed zone text amendments relating
to use and development standards in the M-1 and M-2 zones; the Council indicated a plan to table the agenda item for one
additional week to give all Council members the opportunity to attend and vote on the matter.

Fiscal Year 2024 Annual Action Plan Amendment #1
          Project Coordinator Fuentes explained as a requirement to receive federal funding from the U.S. Department of Housing
and Urban Development (HUD), the City’s Community and Economic Development Department prepares and submits to the
Council what is known as a five-year Consolidated Plan (ConPlan) and an Annual Action Plan. The Annual Action Plan (AAP) is
used to outline how federal Community Development Block Grant funds, HOME funds and other federal and non-federal program
funds will be used by the City in the upcoming fiscal year. When the Annual Action Plan is submitted to HUD each year, specific
dollar amounts for program income funds and carry-over funds are estimated for the remainder of the fiscal year. Part way through
the fiscal year, after the specific dollar amounts are known or when program income from loans and other programs are received,
an amendment must be made to the plan to update the Annual Action Plan budget. Often when the mid-year amendment is
proposed, changes in funding levels within certain programs and line items are proposed as well. The amendments proposed with
this mid-year update address the following:
               • Adopt the 2023 HUD HOME Homeownership Value Limits - $380,000 to $428,000;
               • Increase the Own in Ogden HOME budget by $250,000 with an additional $100,000 to the HOME match funds
                     with an increase to the goal for down payment assistance to 50 per year;
               • Adopt a one-year CDBG overall Low- and Moderate-Income Benefit Certification Period; and
               • Adopt an overall decrease of $34,385 to the budget.
          Ms. Fuentes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on the specific factors that haver contributed to the
proposed Plan amendments and she engaged in discussions with the Council throughout regarding program guidelines for the
HOME, Own in Ogden, and HOME match housing programs.

Fiscal Year 2023 Consolidated Annual Performance Evaluation Report
(CAPER)
        Project Coordinator Fuentes explained when the City receives Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds and
HOME Investment Partnership grant funds, a Five-year Consolidated Plan (ConPlan) and an Annual Action Plan that outline the
work that is to be done with the funds are required. As part of the review process required by U.S. Department of Housing and
Urban Development (HUD), a Consolidated Annual Performance Evaluation Report (CAPER) is required to evaluate the progress
that the City has made in using the funds and implementing the goals set forth in the ConPlan and the Annual Action Plan. As part
of the process, the City’s Community Development Division provides a review of the CAPER that is submitted to HUD.
          Ms. Fuentes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide a summary of the CDBG and HOME Investment
Partnership and HOME American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) grant funds.
