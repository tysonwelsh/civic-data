APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
• This request would be consistent with the Franklin Neighborhood Plan.
• This will bring new residential to the area.
• It will be a positive contribution.
• There will not be a duplex or townhome.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
• A member of the Commission wanted to see what the home that would go here would look like.
• The applicant was asked what the existing home would do for garage parking once the lot was split and the existing
   garage was no longer on the same lot. The applicant explained they would bring it into compliance by providing a
   carport on the lot.
• The infill project will be a nice incremental change that fits into the existing neighborhood especially with soaring
   home prices.
