Motion: To approve application #31056 with the following amended staff recommendations: 
1. The Conditional Use Permit for the Concrete Mixing Plant will expire one year from this approval 
of February 24, 2020.  Business must cease and clean up on the property should return to the original 
state.  An extension on the business time may be requested in writing and can only be approved by 
the Planning Director. 
2. The applicant shall maintain a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) and notice of 
intent (NOI) as approved by the MSD and Salt Lake County per County Standards, to keep dust 
suppressed during any period of construction, reconstruction or operation. 
 
Salt Lake County Planning Commission – January 15, 2020 – Meeting Summary Page 5 of 6 
3. The applicant shall maintain and operate as necessary all emission units and air pollution systems 
required by the Department of Environment Quality (DEQ).  In addition, applicant must obta in a 
permit from the DEQ. 
4. Hours of operation shall be from seven a.m. to 6:00 p.m., with limited operation between ten 
p.m. and seven a.m. per Health Department.  No operation shall take place on Sundays or legal 
holidays. 
5. Health Department approval for Operation during the period of 2/4/2020 and 2/4/2021 is for the 
additional time range between ten p.m. and seven a.m. and has a restriction of NO rock crushing 
between this time frame.  BMP’s for noise must be followed per Health Department requirements. 
6. A debris control plan to be maintained, showing how debris will stay on-site and how clean up of 
“track-out” onto 700 West will happen at end of each work shift. 
7. Development must meet all local building and fire code requirements. 
8. Review upon complaint by Planning Staff for non-compliance of conditions. 
9. Agency reviewers’ requirements and recommendations are to be complied with prior to final 
approval. 
10. The Landscaping requirement has been waived by the Planning Director, since thi s use a 
temporary use with a time limit of one year from the date of final approval.  Condition of property 
must be returned to the original condition. 
11. No concrete foundations will be used according to plant operator.  Concrete foundations will 
require additional review and approvals. 
12. At the time of permits: 
• Applicant needs to submit a SWPPP and an NOI (addressing stockpile management 
in SWPPP). 
• Applicant needs to submit Sediment and Erosion Control Plans 
• Applicant needs to submit the corrected Civil Drawings. 
• Follow final drainage and grading plan during building review process. 
13. All bonds must be posted prior to final approval. 
14. Building permits are required for the installation of the temporary equipment, the scale house, 
any site electrical work, etc.  At the time of building permit application, provide complete building 
plans showing compliance with current building code. 
15. Any modifications to these approved plans must be approved by the Planner in writing prior to 
the changes being implemented in the field. 
16. No signs are approved with this Land Use approval.  Signs require a separate application and 
approval.
