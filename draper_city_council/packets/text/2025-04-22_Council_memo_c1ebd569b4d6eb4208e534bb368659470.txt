MEMO
To: City Council
From: Nicole Smedley, City Recorder
Date: 2025-04-22
Re: Approval of Resolution #25-17


Comments:

The City Council is being asked to consider approval of Resolution #25-17, appointing
three members to the Administrative Control Board for the Traverse Ridge Special Service
District.

The terms of J. Simon Cantarero and Doug Pilkington have expired, each previously
serving a four-year term ending April 30, 2025.

Additionally, Benjamin Frederick, whose term was set to expire February 28, 2027, has
resigned, creating a mid-term vacancy.

The proposed appointments include two new members to serve four-year terms ending
April 30, 2029, and one member to complete the remainder of the unexpired term ending
February 28, 2027.
