Comments:

Next year America will be celebrating 250 years as a nation. As part of this celebration
Utah State has launched Utah250 (https://america250.utah.gov/), a program to engage
people and hold events over the next year. One of the programs is Utah250
Communities, wherein local communities join in the celebrations and hold events in
conjunction with the statewide events. The Draper Historic Preservation Commission
discussed during their last meeting the potential for events that the Commission may be
able to hold or partake in. The broader discussion is if this is something that the City
Council is interested in, either officially becoming a Utah250 Community, or allowing
individual Commissions to hold or join events. Attached is a document that lists some
possible events and provides information on Uta250 Communities.
