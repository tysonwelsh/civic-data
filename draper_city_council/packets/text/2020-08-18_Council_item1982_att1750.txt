                  REQUEST FOR COUNCIL ACTION

To:                         Mayor & City Council
From:                       David Dobbins
Date:                       August 14, 2020
Subject:                    Deer Ridge Drive Update and Improvement Plan
Applicant Presentation:     N/A

Staff Presentation:         Scott Cooley

RECOMMENDATION:

That the City Council discuss options for improving Deer Ridge Drive.


BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:

Deer Ridge Drive needs to be repaired and the process, timing and cost of the improvements should be
discussed by the City Council. In 2018 the City Council designated property in the Deer Ridge area as
surplus with the intent to sell the property and use the proceeds to pay for the Deer Ridge Drive
improvements.

The 10-acre parcel designated for sale is now on the market for purchase but has not yet been sold. In
anticipation of the sale, and funds being made available, it is time to start preparing for the road
improvements which necessitates discussion and direction on the next steps. If improvements are going
to be made in the 2021 construction cycle, the design and bidding process needs to start now. The City
Council will need to appropriate funds in September for this project, which could be taken from other
projects until the Deer Ridge property is sold.




PREVIOUS LEGISLATIVE ACTION:

   •    The City Council designated property as surplus in 2018 which would be sold to help pay for the
        cost of improving Deer Ridge Drive.
