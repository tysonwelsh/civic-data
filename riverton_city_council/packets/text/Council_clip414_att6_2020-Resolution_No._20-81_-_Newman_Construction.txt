                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 20-81

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
 WITH NEWMAN CONSTRUCTION FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE 1300 WEST
  "S" CURVE IMPROVEMENTS (1300 W AND LOVER'S LANE INTERSECTION)
                            PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the City Engineer would like to have this project completed; and

      WHEREAS, Purchasing had issued and Invitation for Bid for this project; and

      WHEREAS, Newman Construction was the lowest responsive, responsible bidder.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Newman
         Construction for the completion of the 1300 West "S" Curve Improvements (1300 W
         and Lover's Lane Intersection) Project.
      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
December 2020 by the following vote:
