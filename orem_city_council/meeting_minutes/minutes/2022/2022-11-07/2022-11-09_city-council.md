started by giving the study overview and explained that UDOT is evaluating roadway

PUBLIC HEARING / ORDINANCE — Enacting Section 22-11-64 PD51 (River District — 720
North 1550 East) zone, and Appendix “YY” of the Orem City Code and amending Article
22-5-(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by changing the zone of 3.15 acres from
the Business Park (BP) zone to the PD51 zone at approximately 720 North 1550 East.

Mr. Bench stated that the Planning Commission held a Public Hearing for the enactment of
Section 22-11-64 PD51 (River District - 720 North 1550 East) and Appendix “YY” of the
Orem City Code and amending the zoning map of the City of Orem was held September 7,
2022. It was determined that more time was needed for the Planning Commission to
discuss this item and to see whether Provo had reviewed and approved their part of the
project since a large portion of the overall project is located in Provo. The item was
continued to September 21, 2022, when the Planning Commission held a meeting and
voted on the project.

The applicant is proposing this rezone and map amendment from the Business Park
zone to the PD51 zone in order to build a planned development of attached residential
dwelling units, consistent with the property to the south which is located in the Provo City
boundaries. If the rezone is approved, they will come back to the Planning Commission at
a future date for approval of the site plan and improvements.

The proposed zone is designed to be applied only to Lot 2 as designated in Appendix YY
of the Orem City Code.

There will be twenty-five (25) units in the residential portion of the new development. This
will be the area that is being rezoned to the PD51 zone. The townhouse style units will be
two or three stories high. The area to the north will remain the Business Park (BP) zone.
There will be a private street that each of the units will access.

The new PD51 zone will be more than five hundred (500) feet from 800 North as is
required for a new PD zone containing a residential use (22-11-1(3)). It is close to one
thousand (1,000) feet from 800 North. Since this is currently zoned BP, a PD zone is an
option for this development containing residential uses (22-11-1(3)). The exterior finishing
materials for the project include wood siding, stone and standing seam metal roofs (see
proposed ordinance text). The overall height for structures in the PD51 zone can be up to
three stories and no higher than forty feet (40’) above grade. The applicant proposes a
height of thirty-six feet nine inches (36’9”) as noted on the elevations (see proposed
ordinance text).

Parking for the development is required to provide 2.5 spaces per unit per 22-7-12(J)
Planned Residential Developments (PRD) of the Orem City Code. For the twenty-five units
