Request for zoning ordinance amendment, Limiting multiple-family dwellings in C-2/CP-2 and C-3
zones.

The applicant is Ogden City Administration.

Barton Brierley from the Planning Division gave a staff report regarding a request for zoning
ordinance amendment, limiting multiple family dwellings in C-2/CP-2 and C-3.

Amend the Zoning Ordinance for C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3 zones to do the following:
1. Prohibit new multiple-family dwellings, single-family rowhouses, and duplexes in these zones
except (1) senior multiple-family dwellings, (2) multiple-family dwellings as allowed in adopted OGX
station area plans along Harrison Boulevard, and (3) mixed-use projects on lots at least 10 acres with
at least 75% of ground floor area in an allowable nonresidential use.
2. Update standards related to senior and mixed-use multiple-family dwellings.

The Planning Commission reviews Zoning Ordinance amendments for:
1. Consistency with General Plan policies.
2. Consistency with the purposes of the Zoning Ordinance.
3. Consistency with state and federal laws.
