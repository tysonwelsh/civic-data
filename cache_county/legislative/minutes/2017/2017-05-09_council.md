OTHER ITEMS:

© North Logan 6f Conversion by the new high school is still in the rezone process with
North Logan. Once that is done, the county will declare the property as surplus and
dispose of it according to proper protocol. The funds received will be used to pay off the
note at the American West Heritage Center.

© Events Attendance by County Executive — Executive Buttars indicated he and his
spouse are frequently invited and expected to attend and represent the county at many
functions and asked if the Council approves using county funds to pay for his dinner(s).
Council member White expressed support for the request and the Council concurred.

© Events Center Update — The county is in discussion with the engineers concerning
increased costs for steel in the building — possibly 40%-50% over the initial projection.
The county is trying to negotiate a better price and Executive Buttars will keep the
Council informed.

© Economic Development — EDCUtah has a new computer program utilizing analytical
data such as, spending patterns and demographics, and would like to work with Cache
County; however, the county is not currently a member. The funds can be found in the
budget and Executive Buttars will come back to the Council with a formal request for
membership dues.

© Building Maintenance — Executive Buttars informed the Council that there will be
ongoing maintenance on the Historic Courthouse cupola for a few months

1 Cache County Library Update — Executive Buttars has had discussions with the State
Librarian who has agreed to work with the county providing information from their
computer programs to help set up the Apollo program to manage the county's collection.

The Bookmobile is funded until June. The Council voted to discontinue funding for the
Bookmobile, but the county is still looking for ways to find funding. Chairman Merrill
suggested a workshop on how the new Apollo program will help meet the needs of
county citizens.

0 Flooding response - Executive Buttars noted that Sunday's response to a flooding
issue in Richmond was excellent, both from the county and from the local citizenry. The
cause was a faulty culvert and Buttars believes the repair can be added to the current
FEMA application. A representative from FEMA will be here Thursday, May 11, 2017 at
2:00 p.m. at the Emergency Operations Center.

ITEMS OF SPECIAL INTEREST

>» Report on Potential Spring Valley Flooding — Brian Mcinerny, Hydrologist, NOAA’s
National Weather Service reviewed past high water vs average water years and

concluded that flooding problems will depend on how warm it gets for how long. If there
are extended higher than average temperatures, there will be flooding — mostly the

Logan River. However, if temperatures fluctuate between warm/cool/warm, the flooding
potential will be minimal. Mclnerny forecasts probable extended high temperatures and

urged the county to warn residents that spring runoff is a very dangerous time near any
waterways in Cache County.

Section VI -- Employee Classifications/Status and Volunteers

A, Exempt and Classified Positions:

All positions in the county government shall be either exempt or classified. Exempt positions
shall be those positions which are to be filled by election or appointment as provided herein,
and such other positions which are responsible for the formulation or execution of policy
where the nature of the policy formulated or the latitude for execution of policy clearly
required unity of purpose and philosophy with the county executive and the heads of offices,
departments, agencies, boards and commissions. All other positions shall be classified.

1. Exempt or classified (non-exempt) employees are identified as one of the following:

B. Personnel Rules and Regulations:

The office of county executive shall prepare for adoption by the county council rules and
regulations to effectively administer personnel. The rules shall classify all positions for pay
setting purposes. The rules shall set forth policies regarding qualifications, selection,
disciplinary action, removal, grievance procedures, vacation, sick leave, personnel records,
etc.

or her employment.

Elected Official

An Elected Official is defined as one who is selected by popular vote at officially
designated elections held within Cache County. Any person appointed to fill an elected
position which has been vacated is also deemed to be an Elected Official.

a. All elected officials who work 40 hours or more in a week (Full-time Status) will be
eligible for benefits.

Merit Employee

An employee who is hired in accordance with the provisions of the recruitment and
hiring procedures (see Section V) for Cache County and has a regular work schedule of
thirty (30) hours or more per week is considered a Merit Employee. A Merit Employee
must satisfactorily complete the Probation Period.

Merit Employee on Probation

An employee who meets the criteria of a merit employee and is still within the first six
(6) months of their hire date or twelve (12) months of their hire date if in a Public Safety
position, are considered working within their Probationary Period as explained in
Section V. A Probationary Period is required of all employees hired as merit employees.
No employee may be hired as a merit employee and classified as a merit employee
without successfully completing probation as a merit employee.

a. Service as a Part-time Non-merit or Seasonal Employee will not be given credit
towards the Probationary Period of a merit employee. If a Part-time/Non-merit
employee is hired as a merit employee, the Probationary Period for that employee
begins when the employee is re-hired as a merit employee.

i. Public Safety Employees (sworn Law Enforcement Officers (LEO) and Basic
Correction Officers (BCO)) are required to serve a twelve (12) month Probation
Period and they must successfully achieve the required LEO or BCO
certification(s).

i. Public Employees (all other merit employees) are required to serve a six (6)
month Probation Period and they must successfully achieve any licenses or
certification(s) required to function within their job classification.
