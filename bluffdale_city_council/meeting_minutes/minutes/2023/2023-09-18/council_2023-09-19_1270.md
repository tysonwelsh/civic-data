NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Bluffdale Board of Canvassers will hold a meeting Tuesday,
September 19, 2023 at the Bluffdale City Hall, 2222 West 14400 South, Bluffdale, Utah, scheduled to
begin promptly at 7:15 p.m. or as soon thereafter as possible. This meeting will also be broadcast live
to the public at: www.bluffdale.com. Notice is further given that access to this meeting by the Mayor
and/or City Council may be by electronic means via telephonic conference call.

Member Dave Kallas, who were excused.


2.       Consideration and Vote on a Resolution Certifying the Canvass of the Official
         Election Results for the 2023 Citv of Bluffdale Municipal Primary Election.
         Conducted by Salt Lake County Clerk's Office. Elections Division.

Council Member Aston moved to APPROVE Resolution 2023-47, certifying the Canvass of
the Official Election Results for the 2023 City of Bluffdale Municipal Primary Election.
Council Member Crockett seconded the motion. Vote on motion: CouncU Member Aston-

Aye, CouncU Member Crockett-Aye, Mayor Pro Tempore Hales-Aye, CouncU Member
