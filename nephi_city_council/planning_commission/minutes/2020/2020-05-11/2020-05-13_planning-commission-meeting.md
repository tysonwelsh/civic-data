Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
                            May 13, 2020
Chairman Greenhalgh opened the meeting at 7:00 p.m.

Opening prayer offered by Karl Brough.

Commission reviewed minutes from regular meeting held April 8, 2020. Minutes were accepted on
a motion by Kent Heap and a second by Ann Peterson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Revised Cedar Ridge PUD application was reviewed by Commission. Construction of one 5-plex will
be allowed as subdivision is developed. Kent Heap expressed concern of having correct elevation
before any structure is built. Approval of revised Cedar Ridge PUD application was motioned by
Brent Arns which Karl Brough seconded. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Commission discussed zero lot line modifications, specifically the removal of term ‘percent’ to an
actual amount.

Modifications involving political signs were discussed by Commission. They would like to see the
removal of ‘no signs posted longer that six weeks prior to election’ with the addition of requirement
they be taken down within five days after election. Ann Peterson motioned for a public hearing
involving modifications to both zero lot line and political signs be held in June. Motion was
seconded by Kent Heap. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Commission reviewed proposed site plan requirement revisions. They would like more time to
study this and will begin compiling comments on a Google Doc to offer to Kyle Marchant.

Chairman Greenhalgh asked Commission for thoughts on a ½ acre lot with 122ft frontage, in R-2-8
zone, wanting an additional dwelling. Commission would like homeowner to find a way to make it
reasonably connected.

Chairman Greenhalgh informed Commission the City Council is seeking to potentially amend
Annexation Policy and Annexation Map. The Commission will be asked to study this in the future.

Noting no additional items of business, Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 7:50pm.
