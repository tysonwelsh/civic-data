30          1.2     (Project CUP-21-004) – Request from Jere and Krista El-Bakri for
31                  Conditional Use Approval of a Wall Height Extension at 7731 South Forest
32                  Creek Lane.
33
34   Associate City Planner, Gabrielle Regenhardt, presented the staff report and stated this the request
35   is from Jere and Krista El-Bakri for conditional use approval of a wall height extension on property
36   within the RR-143 zone, which is a single-family residential zone with a maximum wall height
37   allowance of eight feet or up to 12 feet as a Conditional Use with neighbor consent. The applicant
38   has requested a proposed height of eight feet. The neighbor to the south has expressed concern
39   about erosion and privacy once the applicant has installed a pool. Staff conducted a privacy
40   analysis and determined that an eight-foot fence will not create any new nuisances and supported
41   the concerns of the neighbor. A property rendering was displayed. No comments from residents
42   were received by staff. Staff recommended approval with the conditions set forth in the staff
43   report.
44
45   The applicant, Krista El-Bakri, stated she has discussed the proposal with the adjacent neighbors
46   and have taken their suggestions. She asked about the procedure in the event the fence exceeds
47   the eight-foot requirement by a few inches. Mr. Johnson explained that generally if the fencing

 9   Findings:
10
11          1.     There is clear and convincing evidence shown by the property owner relating to
12                 privacy and safety that will be substantially minimized or eliminated by the
13                 implementation of the requested wall and fence combination; (19.76.050.E.1.a)
14
15          2.     That construction of such wall and fence is the most reasonable solution under
16                 the circumstances. The additional fence height is necessary to ensure mitigation
17                 of aforementioned issues.
18
19          3.     The conditional use permit is granted by the director or his designee following
20                 an administrative hearing preceded by all required notifications.
21                 (19.76.050.E.1.b)
22
23          4.     The evidence presented with the proposed conditional use has been found to be
24                 compliant with the requirements of section 19.84.080 (Conditional Uses –
25                 Determination) and section 19.76.050.E (Miscellaneous – Fences).
26
27          5.     There is sufficient evidence that the standards for the issuance of a conditional
28                 use permit as outlined in Section 19.84.080 CH Code have been satisfied.
29
30   Conditions:
31
32          1.     A building permit and all necessary inspections shall be obtained and completed.
33
34          2.     The building permit will clearly state materials, design, height of the fence in
35                 elevation grade format (i.e. 4500’), and shall not exceed a combined and total
36                 height of eight feet. If any portion of the combined height fence and wall after
37                 design extends above eight feet in height as measured by City Code, the applicant
38                 obtain a notarized consent letter from the adjacent neighbor.
