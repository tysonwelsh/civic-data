Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome, Roll Call, Pledge of Allegiance
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Albrecht joined virtually. Mayor Johnson led the Pledge of Allegiance.


2. Action Item
2.a. Consideration of Resolution #2022-58, proposing a Tax Rate for the City of Lehi for Fiscal
Year 2023.
Dean Lundell presented on the property tax increase. This is just one step in the process. The public
hearing will be held in August. It would be a difference of about $1 month for the average home
in Lehi. Mr. Lundell also explained that if property values increase then the tax rate actual goes
down.

Motion: Council Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2022-58, proposing a Tax Rate for the
City of Lehi for Fiscal Year 2023. The motion was seconded by Councilor Koivisto.

Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time, Councilor Koivisto
   moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Condie seconded the motion. The motion passed
   unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:15 p.m.
