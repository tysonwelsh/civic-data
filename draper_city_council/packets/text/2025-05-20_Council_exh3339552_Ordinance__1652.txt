                                  ORDINANCE NO. 1652

         AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER CITY
         FOR APPROXIMATELY 0.85 ACRES OF PROPERTY FROM CBP (BUSINESS/
         MANUFACTURING PARK) TO M1 (LIGHT MANUFACTURING), LOCATED AT
         APPROXIMATELY 13488 SOUTH 200 WEST WITHIN DRAPER CITY,
         OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE GRANITE CONSTRUCTION ZONING MAP
         AMENDMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and
Zoning Map to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

      WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Zoning Map to
keep pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of
economic uses; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change set forth herein has been reviewed by the
Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been
held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to
the Zoning Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper
City, and the City Council has found the proposed zone change to be consistent with the
City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

       Section 1.      Zoning Map Amendment. The following described real property
located at approximately 13488 South 200 West within Draper City, Salt Lake County, State
of Utah, previously zoned CBP as shown on the Draper City Zoning Map, as depicted in
Exhibit “A” hereto, is hereby changed and rezoned to M1:

Beginning at a point South 89°37'14" West 936.25 feet and South 00°32'04" West 1795.12
feet and South 00°32'04" West 69.55 feet from the North quarter corner of Section 1,
Township 4 South, Range 1 West, Salt Lake Base and Meridian; and running thence South
00°32'04" West 720.18 feet more or less to the North line of 13800 South Street; thence
North 89°59'33" West 50 feet more or less; thence North 00°32'04" East 679.92 feet more
or Less; thence Northeasterly along a 2166.59 foot radius curve to the Right 64.48 feet
(Chord Bears North 51°22'26" East 64.48 feet) to the point of beginning.

        Section 2.   Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
