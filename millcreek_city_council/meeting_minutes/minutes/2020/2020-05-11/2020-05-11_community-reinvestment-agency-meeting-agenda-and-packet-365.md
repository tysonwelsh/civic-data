Minutes of the
Millcreek Community Reinvestment Agency
May 11, 2020
7:00 p.m.
Regular Meeting

The Community Reinvestment Agency of Millcreek, Utah, met in a regular public meeting on
May 11, 2020 at City Hall, located at 3330 S. 1300 E., Millcreek, UT 84106. The meeting was
conducted electronically per Millcreek State of Local Emergency Directive No. 2 of 2020 and
live streamed via the City’s website with an option for online public comment.

REGULAR MEETING — 7:00 p.m.
TIME COMMENCED: 8:04 p.m.

Chair Silvestrini called the meeting to order.

1. Public Hearing on Fiscal Year 2020-21 Community Reinvestment Agency Fund
Tentative Budget
Mike Winder said there had not been any changes since the budget was last presented to
the Board.

Board Member Catten moved to open the public hearing. Board Member Jackson
seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The motion
passed unanimously.

While waiting for online public comment, Chair Silvestrini said he had been working with city
staff on an administrative parking enforcement process. It would allow a hearing officer to do a
ticket appeal process and code enforcement officers would be able to issue tickets. He said the

2019 H.B. 411 Community Renewable Energy Act process meetings had slowed down because

2. Approval of April 27, 2020 Meeting Minutes

Board Member Jackson moved to approve the minutes of April 27, 2020. Board Member
Marchant seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes.
The motion passed unanimously.

ADJOURNED: Board Member Jackson moved to adjourn the meeting at 8:11 p.m. Board
