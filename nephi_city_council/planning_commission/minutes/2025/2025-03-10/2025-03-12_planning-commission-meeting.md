   3. Public Hearing for an impact home occupation permit for Erica Greenhalgh, DBA
       SueZQ’s Dog Salon to operate a business for dog grooming services at her home
       located at 346 S 200 E.
              a. Open Public Hearing
                  TIME: 7:03 PM

              b. Staff Presentation
                 Mr. Seth Atkinson presented that similarly the notices have gone out and
                 nothing has come forward for concern from the neighbors. It is the staff
                 recommendation that the home occupation permit be granted.

              c. Public Comment
                 Chairman Cory Thomson asked if the applicant, Erica Greenhalgh would like
                 to present anything about the business to the commission.

                  Mrs. Erica Greenhalgh presented that she is excited and there is a great need
                  as she has seen it in the local social media pages. She is also an ex-vet
                  technician, and she has worked in the South County Shelter. She is aware of
                  the need of care for those that are needing re-homing and adoption.

                  Commissioner Heather Robertson inquired from Mrs. Greenhalgh how many
                  dogs she will be able to facilitate at one time. Mrs. Greenhalgh responded that
                  she is only planning one-on-one grooming, and will not be housing dogs at her
                  home.

                  Commissioner John Ford inquired on the services that Mrs. Greenhalgh is
                  going to provide. Mrs. Greenhalgh responded that she will be doing grooming
                  and all that entails such as ear cleaning, bathing, nail trims, sanitary clips,
                  blow outs, de-shedding.

                  Commissioner John Ford requested additional information on the size of the
                  dogs that Mrs. Greenhalgh will offer services for. Mrs. Greenhalgh responded
                  that is something that she is willing to explore. Size is not really the concern;
                  the temperament of the dog is.

                  Commissioner Terry Cook inquired where the services is only one-on-one if
                  there was adequate parking for the clients that will be at the business? Mrs.
                  Greenhalgh responded that there is enough parking for 5 cars.
