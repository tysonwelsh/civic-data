       b. Resolution No. 243 authorizing the execution of an Agreement for Sale and
          Purchase of Real Property for the Redevelopment Agency’s purchase of real
          property located at 8071 S Redwood Road.

          Board Member Green questioned who would be liable for eviction costs if the tenants
          decide not to vacate.

          RDA Attorney Adam Long noted that they would not close on the property if a tenant was
          still in the property. He felt it would be appropriate to include language requiring the
          property be vacant.

            Chairperson Jacob pointed out language in the contract stating that the property “shall be
            vacant as of date of closing”. Board Member Green appreciated the clarity.

            Board Member Shelton believed it made sense to rent the property while they were
            waiting to develop it. Real Property Administrator David Clemence noted that they had
            conversations about doing that and noted it could be a possibility. He also noted
            challenges with doing so in the past. However, he believed it would be helpful to look at
            the viability of holding on to the property. He reported it would be a de facto shelter.

            Board Member Green expressed concerns regarding the appraisal price and indicated he
            would be voting against the proposal.

            MOTION: Board Member Jacob moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 243 authorizing the
                       execution of an Agreement for Sale and Purchase of Real Property for the
                       Redevelopment Agency’s purchase of real property located at 8071 S Redwood
                       Road
                       Board Member Lamb seconded the motion.
            The vote was recorded as follows:
            YES:       Zach Jacob, Chad Lamb, Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Kent Shelton,
                       Kayleen Whitelock
            NO:        Kelvin Green
            ABSENT:
            The motion passed 6-1.
