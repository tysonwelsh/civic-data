MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper, AICP
Date: 2024-06-13
Re: Public Hearing: Hold Tyte Studios, LLC Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
Request (Administrative Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
for approximately 0.66 acres located on the east side of 1300 East, at approximately
13575 S. 1300 E. The property is currently zoned RA1. The applicant is requesting that a
Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for a combination cottage business and
personal instruction service home occupation that produces artwork for sale and teaches
art classes.


Findings for Approval:
  1. The reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed conditional use are
     substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions.

Findings for Denial:
  1. The reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed conditional use are
     not able to be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions.
