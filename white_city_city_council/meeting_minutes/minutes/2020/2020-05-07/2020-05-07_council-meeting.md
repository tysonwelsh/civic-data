Resolution

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution identifying the intent to amend and
establish special land use rules, regulations, and management for FEMA-identified flood zones
located in White City to apply for inclusion in the National Flood Insurance Program;

RESOLUTION NO. 20-05-01                                                       Date: May 7, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       IDENTIFYING THE INTENT TO AMEND AND ESTABLISH SPECIAL LAND USE
       RULES, REGULATIONS, AND MANAGEMENT FOR FEMA-IDENTIFIED FLOOD
       ZONES LOCATED IN WHITE CITY TO APPLY FOR INCLUSION IN THE
       NATIONAL FLOOD INSURANCE PROGRAM

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council’) is the municipal governing body
      for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Federal Emergency Management Agency (“FEMA”) is an agency of the United States
      Government tasked with assisting and supporting State, County, and Local Governments
      with disaster preparation, training, incident management support, and possible emergency
      recovery efforts.

   D. Pursuant to the protection of people and property, and as part of a statewide flood zone
      identification study FEMA performed, there may be locations in White City that are
      susceptible to a threat of flooding (“Flood Zones”).

                                               3
DATE          THURSDAY                             MAY                                    7, 2020


 E. Identified flood zones include certain key facilities the public needs for the general health
    and safety of White City.

 F. Prior to the incorporation of White City on January 1, 2017, all land use decisions in flood
    zones were made and administered by Salt Lake County as the “municipal government”
    of White City.

 G. The land use ordinances and regulations regarding flood zones became White City’s
    ordinances and regulation and may need to be amended due to White City’s incorporation.

 H. Identifying, evaluating and pursuing mitigation efforts within possible flood zones, and
    recognizing flood zones in official actions relating to land use as we move forward, and
    addressing issues in the FEMA-identified flood zones in the municipality, it is the intent of
    the White City Council to ensure flood zone maps land use ordinances, regulations and
    hazard management are in compliance with FEMA regulations such that its residents can
    benefit from the National Flood Insurance Program.

 I.   Pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-3c-103, 68-3-12.5, and 10-9a-501 et al, the Council has the
      statutory authority to adopt land use and control measures to reduce future flood losses.

                                        RESOLUTION

      THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the White City Metro Township Council,

      1. The Council assures the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA)
         that it will enact as necessary, and maintain in force, in those areas having
         flood hazards, adequate land use and control measures with effective
         enforcement provisions consistent with the criteria set forth in Section 60 of the
         National Flood Insurance Program Regulations; and

      2. Vest the Flood Control Engineer with the responsibility, authority and means
         to:

         (a) Assist the Director of Planning and Development Services (the “Director’ or
         “Administrator”), at his/her request, in the delineation of the limits of the area
         having special flood hazards.

         (b) Provide such information concerning present uses and occupancy of the
         flood plan, mudslide (i.e., mudflow) or flood-related erosion areas as the
         Director may request.

         (c) Maintain for public inspection and furnish upon request, for the
         determination of applicable flood insurance risk premium rates within all areas
         having special flood hazards identified on a Flood Hazard Boundary Map
         (FHBM) or Flood Insurance Rate Map (FIRM), any certificates of floodproofing,
         and information on the elevation (in relation to mean sea level) of the level of

           the lowest flood (including basement) of all new construction or substantially
           improve structure has been floodproofed, the elevation (in relation to mean sea
           level) to which the structure was floodproofed.

           (d) Cooperate with Federal, State and local agencies and private firms which
           undertake to study, survey, map and identify floodplain, mudslide (i.e.,
           mudflow) or flood-related erosion areas, and cooperate with neighboring
           communicates with respect to management of adjoining floodplain, mudslide,
           (i.e., mudflow) and/or flood-related erosion areas in order t prevent aggravation
           of existing hazards.

           (e) Upon occurrence, notify the Administrator in writing whenever the
           boundaries of the community have been modified by annexation or the
           community has otherwise assumed or no longer has authority to adopt and
           enforce floodplain management regulations for a particular area. In order that
           all FHBM’s and FIRM’s accurately represent the community’s boundaries,
           include within such notification a copy of the map of the community suitable for
           reproduction, clearly delineating the new corporate limits or new area for which
           the community has assumed or relinquished floodplain management
           regulatory authority.

           (f) Submit an annual report to the Administrator concerning the community’s
           participation in the program, including, but not limited to the development and
           implementation of floodplain management measures.

       3. Appoints the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District with the
          responsibility, authority, and means to implement the commitments as outlined
          in this Resolution.

       4. Agrees to take such other official action as may be reasonably necessary to
          carry out the objectives of the adopted floodplain management measures.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County,
Utah this 7th day of May, 2020.

Salt Lake County Animal Services Advisory Board

                Council Member Little stated Salt Lake County Animal Services has been closed
to the public because of COVID-19. Starting May 11, it will start to make appointments for people
to come in. There are over 90 animals that have been fostered out and 27 animals at the shelter.
The call volume has been steady and nuisance calls have been down. Complaints are coming in
for people not cleaning up after their pets at the trail heads. There have been a lot of dead animal
calls, if someone sees a dead animal, it would like a call so the animal can be scanned for a chip.
Animal Services received a grant to help pet owners who have tested positive for COVID-19.
There is a free drive-through pantry for animals at the shelter. There is training available on
Facebook for the public. For the 12th time, Salt Lake County Animal Services has received the
Best of State award.
