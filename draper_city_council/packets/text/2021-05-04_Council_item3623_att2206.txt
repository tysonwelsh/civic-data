Applicant Presentation:   none
Staff Presentation: Laura Oscarson, City Recorder
RECOMMENDATION:

Approve Ordinance #1489 amending Title 2 of the DCMC, regarding publication of approved
ordinances and election notices to address recent changes to Utah State Code.




BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
Senate Bill 201 made changes to how approved ordinances and elections are to be noticed by removing
the requirement of publishing the notices in newspapers of general circulation. Ordinance #1489
removes the newspaper publications from Draper City Municipal Code.
