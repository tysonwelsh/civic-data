Business Meeting

1.0    Call to Order: Chairman Summerhays

2.0    Action Item: Resolution #MBA 19-03, adopting the Annual Budget for Fiscal Year
       Ending June 30, 2020.

2.1    Bob Wylie, Finance Director, explained that there were no changes in the Annual Budget
       from what was presented in the Tentative Budget.

2.2    Boardmember Walker moved to approve Resolution #MBA 19-03, the Annual Budget
       for Fiscal Year ending June 30, 2020. Boardmember Vawdrey seconded the motion.

2.2    A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Summerhays,
       Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

       seconded the motion.

14.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Summerhays,
       Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

14.3   The meeting adjourned at 8:00 pm.
