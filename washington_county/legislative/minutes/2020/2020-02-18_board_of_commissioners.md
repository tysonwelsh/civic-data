MEETING MINUTES
FEBRUARY 18, 2020

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on February 18, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Commissioner Dean Cox, County Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle
Felshaw, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

RECOGNITION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY ATTORNEY BROCK BELNAP

Chairman Iverson explained that Washington County Attorney Brock Belnap has resigned
his position and expressed gratitude for his time serving with Mr. Belnap. The
Commissioners presented Mr. Belnap with a plaque and wished him well in his new
endeavors.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2571, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING _AN__AMENDMENT_TO AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN
WASHINGTON COUNTY COUNCIL ON AGING AND VALLEY SERVICES
INC. REGARDING FOOD SERVICES PROVIDED TO THE COUNCIL ON

AGING

Washington County Council on Aging Director Jacob Browning explained that in 2016, a
Request for Proposal (RFP) was issued for a food service, and a two-year contract was
signed with Valley Food Services, Inc. with the option to extend one (1) year up to three
(3) times. This is the third year, with a two point five percent (2.5%) increase which is in
line with the consumer price index.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2020-
2571, A Resolution Approving an Amendment to an Agreement
Between Washington County Council on Aging and Valley Services,
Inc. Regarding Food Services Provided to the Council on Aging.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2572, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING GLOBAL TEL*LINK CORPORATION AGREEMENT

Undersheriff James Standley explained that Global Tel*Link provides electronic
communications within the Purgatory Correctional Facility in order for inmates to
communicate with friends and family. This also simultaneously helps the Sheriff's Office
preserve order and security within the facility. This contract will provide a person onsite to
assist with operations of the program.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Approve Resolution No. R-2020-2572,
A Resolution Approving Global Tel*Link Corporation Agreement.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2573, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A COMPLETE REDUCTION OF PAST PROPERTY TAXES FOR
A PARCEL LOCATED IN THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

County Attorney Eric Clarke explained the James Doyle acquired property currently
located in the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve several years ago and has allowed the property to
remain in the Reserve while he waits for the federal government to acquire his land in order
to protect the Mohave Desert Tortoise. As such, he has accrued several years of property
taxes on his property and he has requested that those be reduced to zero as he has been
unable to utilize the land.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Dean Cox reported that he will be attending the UCIP Board and the Utah
Department of Transportation (UDOT) meeting this week in Salt Lake.

Chairman Iverson reported that the Legislative sessions are ongoing.

Purgatory Correctional Facility that was transported by the Road Department for use at the

Shooting Sports Park, which saved the County a large amount of money.
