3)
Public Hearing and Action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for a Rocky Mountain Power transmission line upgrade North of the S.R. 248 corridor area going eastbound from Quinn's Junction to Kamas (~2,000 feet), Snyderville, Summit County, UT; Parcels S-1067 (15.45 acres), and SS-64-1000-UP-X (area of work ~0.68 acres); Applicant: Rocky Mountain Power (Andrew Badger); Administrative Review. Project #24-053. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.

Attachment:

Attachment:

Amir Caus,
AICP Senior Planner,
introduced the conditional use permit for a Rocky Mountain Power transmission line upgrade.
Andy Badger with Rocky Mountain Power briefly introduced himself to the Commission.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for Public Hearing at 6:07 p.m.
No members of the public came forward to speak.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for Public Hearing at 6:07 p.m.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the application pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Matthew Nagie seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Makena Hawley.
4)
Public Hearing and Action regarding a Plat Amendment for Lot 2, The Preserve, Phase 2 Subdivision, located at 312 West Deer Hill, Summit County, UT; Parcel PRESRV-2-21 (12.30 acres). Applicant: Ted Mason. Owner: Paul Kirincich & Kelly Kruse. Administrative Review. Project #24-118. Jennifer Strader, Principal Planner.
(6:08 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
The Preserve Lot 21 Plat Amendment Staff Report 9.10.24.pdf
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner
, introduced a plat amendment for Lot 2, The Preserve, Phase 2 Subdivision for public hearing and possible action.
Commission Chair Conabee and Vice-Chair Mooney asked clarifying questions of Planner Strader to which she responded.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:14 p.m.
No members of the public came forward to speak.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:14 p.m.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the application with the change as mentioned previously where the building pad is 30,000 feet and the limit is 30,000 feet pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report and the 2024 vs. 2004 date change. Tyann Mooney seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Makena Hawley.
