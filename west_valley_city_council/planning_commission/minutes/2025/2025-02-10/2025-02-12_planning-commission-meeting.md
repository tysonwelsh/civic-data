The subdivision is located at 2574 South Redwood Road and is zoned RM.

ISSUES:

Ability Inclusion Services is proposing to construct a 12-unit apartment building on 3 existing
lots in Lake Park No. 1 Subdivision. The proposed subdivision will consolidate said 3 lots into
one lot totaling .62 acres. A conditional use application was reviewed by the Planning
Commission in November 2024. As a reminder, AIS is a nonprofit organization that provides
services for adults with disabilities.

Lake Park Subdivision No. 1 was recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office in 1971.
A number of public utility easements and a right-of-way easement were recorded as part of the
original subdivision lots. Due to the future AIS building, the public utility easement located
between lots 26 and 27 will be vacated. The right-of-way easement providing access to the west
property will remain.

Access will be gained from Lake Park Drive. Street improvements along Redwood Road and
Lake Park Drive are already existing. However, improvements along these rights-of-way are in
poor condition and will be replaced with new improvements per the conditional use application.
Improvements along Redwood Road will be modified to accommodate the Redwood Road
Streetscape. The City will also require the dedication of a 20-foot radius at Redwood Road and
Lake Park Drive.

There are currently two drive approaches on Lake Park Drive. The west approach will remain
and will become the primary access for the new subdivision. It will also continue to serve the
property to the west. A circular drive will be constructed and will utilize the second approach as
an exit point which is located on the east side of the subdivision.

2)   SMI-1-2025: Adis Alagic is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Alagic-Mahir
     Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on .46 acres. The proposed subdivision
     is located at 3753 South 4400 West and is zoned R-1-8. Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-

Adis Alagic, is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Alagic-Mahir Subdivision. The
subdivision will create one new additional building, a lot with frontage on Brookside Drive. An
existing dwelling occupies the front portion of the property along 4400 West and will remain as
part of the subdivision. The subdivision is bordered on all sides by existing single-family housing.

       along the frontage of Brookside Drive.

      Revisions to final plat are required.

ISSUES:

      The applicant is proposing to subdivide an existing .46-acre property to create an additional
       building lot. The property is not in a recorded subdivision but is located in an area that
       was platted as part of the Sunnyvale Park Subdivision. Said subdivision was recorded with
       the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office in 1960. It appears this property and the property
       to the north were not part of Sunnyvale Park Subdivision. However, the property to the
       north has subsequently been divided similar to what is being proposed here.

      Although zoned R-1-8, both lots will be over 10,000 square feet in size. The existing
       dwelling will continue to be accessed from 4400 West with the new lot gaining access from
       Brookside Drive. The applicant will be required to improve 4400 West with curb, gutter
       and sidewalk. Rolled gutter currently exists on Brookside Drive but sidewalk does not. As
       part of the subdivision, the sidewalk will need to be installed in front of the new lot.

      An irrigation structure is located in the southwest corner of the property along 4400 West.
       From the civil drawings, it appears to empty into the storm drain in 4400 West. It does
       encroach the sidewalk a bit, so that will need to be coordinated with the City Engineering
       Division. If the structure is level with the sidewalk, the city has allowed these to remain in
       place. From the photos, the structure looks to be a bit higher than the sidewalk, in which
       case, it will need to be relocated.

      As with all new developments, there is a concern for groundwater. A soils report was not
       submitted with the application but in cases where only one new lot is being proposed, the
       City has allowed the Building Official to do a site visit to determine what the basement
       floor elevation should be. That will be a recommendation of the Building Division as noted
       above.
