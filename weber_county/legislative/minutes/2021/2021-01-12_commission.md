    6. RATIFICATION FORM OF AGREEMENT BY/BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY/LIBRARY AND JARDINE MALASKA
       CONSTRUCTION SERVICES FOR EARTHQUAKE DAMAGE REMEDIATION
       Lynnda Wangsgard, County Library Director, stated that this agreement would provide general contractor
       services to remediate damages to all five library buildings that resulted from the March 18, 2020, earthquake.
       A team of architects and engineers had evaluated each facility and prepared detailed drawings and specifications
       for repairs that were then advertised to the public in a competitive request for proposals (RFPs). Jardine
       Malaska Construction Services was the low bidder and met all of the RFP requirements. The plans and process
       for selecting the general contractor were reviewed by the Library Board’s legal counsel as assigned by the
       County Attorney, who also spent considerable time and effort onsite with the architect and their team of
       engineers. UCIP approved the entire base bid contract amount of $538,536.20 with provisions for evaluating
       the cost of any additional, unforeseen damages that may be discovered during the remediation process. After
       process review and recommendation by legal counsel, the Library Board approved the agreement and
       recommended ratification.
       Commissioner Jenkins moved to ratify the form of Agreement by/between Weber County/Library and Jardine
       Malaska Construction Services for earthquake damage remediation; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
       Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    7. CONTRACT WITH J BRAD BARTO CONSTRUCTION INC. FOR WILDWOOD BRIDGE RECONSTRUCTION
        Ashley Thoman, of County Engineering, stated that this contract was for some work on the Wildwood bridge—
        the wood deck was failing. J Brad Barto put forth two proposals (for concrete and wood) and the county decided
        on concrete deck replacement. Commissioner Jenkins stated that it had been difficult to get a contractor for
        this item; the only bids received were for total bridge replacement. A County Commission many decades ago
        signed on a plan that the county would take some responsibility for bridge maintenance, not replacement.
        Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contract with J Brad Barto Construction Inc. for the Wildwood
        bridge deck reconstruction; Commissioner Froerer seconded, making clear that this was an issue resulting from
        a past Commission decision which he normally would be opposed to—the bridge is for a private development
        off of a public road but an agreement that was signed many decades ago obligated the county. He further
        wanted it clarified that once this work is completed, that the county has no further obligation for any related
        improvements. Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, stated that the intent was to confirm that this
        item was to be completely in private hands and his office is working to ensure that there are no further legal
        obligations. Commissioners Jenkins and Froerer stated that this absolutely needed to be the case, that the county
        had fulfilled its responsibility, that this needed to be confirmed prior to authorizing construction, and if needed,
        that the residents of that development should sign a document that the county had no further obligation. The
        commissioners reiterated that if it wasn’t for what was written on the plans, the county would not be involved.
        Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
