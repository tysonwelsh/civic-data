ORDINANCE NO. 2021-0-14
AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF NORTH UNION AVENUE,
MIDVALE, UTAH.

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated Sections 10-8-8 and 10-9a-609.5
and Chapter 12.16 of the Midvale City Municipal Code, the City has authority to vacate
and dispose of city-owned rights-of-way; and

WHEREAS, a request has been made to vacate a portion of North Union Avenue
right-of-way described in Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, each City Department has reviewed the request to determine impact
to the City and made a recommendation to approve the request to vacate; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing
on January 3, 2021, which meeting was preceded by notice of publication in the Salt Lake
Tribune on January 2, 2021, to review the request to vacate, and, after considering all the
information received, made a recommendation to approve the vacation request to the City
Council; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing on June
1, 2021, which meeting was preceded by notice of publication in the Salt Lake Tribune on
May 16, 2021; and

WHEREAS, after taking into consideration citizen testimony and _ the
recommendations from City Staff and the Planning Commission, the City Council finds
that the vacation of a portion of North Union Avenue will allow for better use of the
adjacent properties and no person nor the public interest will be materially injured by the
proposed vacation.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
MIDVALE CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. The foregoing recitals are true and correct;

Section 2. The portion of North Union Avenue described in the attached
Exhibit A are hereby vacated;

Section 3. Ownership of the rights-of-way being vacated shall revert to the
adjacent property owner on the north side of the North Union Avenue right-of-way;

Section 4. The Midvale City Council does hereby authorize the Mayor to sign
any documents needed to complete this vacation; and
