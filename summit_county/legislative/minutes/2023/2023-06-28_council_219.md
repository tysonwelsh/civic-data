Work Session (1:41 P.M.)
1)
Pledge of Allegiance (1:41 P.M.)
2)
Discussion regarding Transient Room Tax; Chris Robinson and Jennifer Wesselhoff (1:42 P.M.)
Attachment: Transient Room Tax Presentation.pdf
Jennifer Wesselhoff, President of the Park City Chamber of Commerce, and Rhonda Sideris, incoming Chair of the Park City Chamber of Commerce, led a discussion on the Transient Room Tax, expectations for the coming year, and current efforts. Council Members commented and asked questions. Ms. Wesselhoff and Ms. Sideris responded.
3)
Discussion regarding the Pinebrook Townhomes Rezone and Conditional Use Permit for a 22-unit townhome project on Parcel PP-43-A-7 (1.11 acres), located at 3085 Pinebrook Road, Pinebrook, Summit County, UT; Sean Steinman, Applicant; Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner (2:46 P.M.)
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, provided background on the Pinebrook Townhomes Rezone.
Sean Steinman, Applicant, updated Council on the changes to the project. Council Members asked questions to which Mr. Steinman and Mr. Caus responded. Council requested a future site visit.
Attachment: Staff Report-Pinebrook Townhomes Rezone and CUP.pdf
Break
4)
Discussion regarding childcare needs assessment in Summit County; Jeff Jones, Kristen Schulz, and Joel Zarrow (3:45 P.M.)
Attachment: Staff Report-Childcare Needs Assessment.pdf
Kristen Schulz, Early Childhood Alliance Coordinator, provided information on the local childcare needs. Jeff Jones, Housing and Economic Development Director, presented the childcare assessment he prepared. Joel Zarrow, Park City Community Foundation President, supported the presentation. Council Members asked questions and commented. Ms. Schulz, Mr. Zarrow, Mr. Jones, and Dave Thomas, Civil Chief Deputy Attorney, responded.
Attachment: Childcare Demand Presentation-Jeff Jones.pdf
Attachment: Early Childhood Alliance and PCCF Childcare Update Presentation.pdf
5)
Community benefit update from Intermountain Park City Hospital; Amy Tuddenham and Lori Weston (5:04 P.M.)
Lori Weston, Park City Hospital Administrator, and Amy Tuddenham, Intermountain Marketing and Communications Manager, provided an update to the Council regarding the 2022 Charitable Activities and the 2023 Charity Plan and Report. Council Members asked questions and commented. Ms. Weston, Ms. Tuddenham, and Dave Thomas, Civil Chief Deputy Attorney, responded.
Attachment: Park City Hospital Presentation.pdf
Attachment: Park City Hospital Fact Sheet.pdf
Break
6)
Presentation of second quarter financial update; Matt Leavitt (5:28 P.M.)
Matt Leavitt, Financial Officer, updated the Council on the County’s current financial position. Council Members asked clarifying questions. Mr. Leavitt responded.
Attachment: Staff Report-2023 2nd Quarter Report.pdf
Attachment: 2023 2nd Quarter Presentation.pdf

Public hearing regarding an amendment, to the Snyderville Basin Development Code, to allow multifamily residential as part of a commercial development; Ray Milliner (6:08 P.M.)
Council Chair Armstrong reviewed the background on the proposed amendment that had previously been presented to Council.
