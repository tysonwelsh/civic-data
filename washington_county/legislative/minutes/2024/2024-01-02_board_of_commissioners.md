WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JANUARY 2, 2024

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on January 2, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Jayanne
Lewis.

INVOCATION BY: Pastor Greg Wright of Westside Baptist Church

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: David Whitehead, Washington County Treasurer

YEAR

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Open the Public Hearing for
Community Development Block Grant 2024 Fiscal Year. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

Commissioner Almquist opened the public hearing for the Community Development Block Grant
2024 fiscal year. Dan Cooper shared comments to the effect that help should fall back on
community members. Having no other public comments Commissioner Almquist asked for a
motion to close the public hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Close the Public Hearing for Community
Development Block Grant 2024 Fiscal Year. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3232, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT TO FUND THE DESIGN OF A PORTION OF THE
ZION CORRIDOR TRAIL IN LA VERKIN CITY

Jamie Allen Tourism Operations Manager explained that this Resolution would approve the design
phase of the Zion Corridor Trail in La Verkin City. Funding for the engineering and design will
be provided by Washington County using TRT funds.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. 2024-3232, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement to Fund the Design of a
Portion of the Zion Corridor Trail in La Verkin City. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3233, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
MULTIPLE OPIOID SETTLEMENT AGREEMENTS

Devin Snow stated that Washington County and several counties in Utah are participating in a
lawsuit related to the opioid crisis, and these settlement agreements would resolve claims against
some defendants but not all of them. Outside counsel and the county attorney’s office recommend

approval.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. 2024-3233, A
Resolution Approving Multiple Opioid Settlement Agreements. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

Commissioner Almquist stated the Commissioners would like to start holding quarterly Town Hall
meetings in various locations throughout Washington County. The first Town Hall Meeting will be
held on March 12, 2024 at 06:00 pm with the location to be shared at a later Commission Meeting.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 04:36 p.m.
