2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of X Development’s request for review of the 2100
North Subdivision Concept, a 65-lot residential development located at approximately 2100
North 2300 West.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Eyre asked what the density would be without the bonus. Staff stated it would be
63 and the applicant is asking for 66.

Commissioner Ellis asked about driveways. Mr. Struthers discussed that these would be more
shared driveways. Chairman Nielsen asked if they require sidewalks. Mr. Struthers discussed
that they don't need sidewalks. Chairman asked about the trail and stated he feels like it isn’t a
short trail segment that could essentially be a sidewalk.

Applicant Eric Towner discussed that they do like PUD because it grants some open space with
the trail, but they generally think they can landscape it to look nice.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Angie Loveridge is concerned about the access. She gave an example to Ivory Ridge and the
right turn situation. It does not solve the people wanting to come west on 2300 west. There will
be students coming all at one for Northpointe.

Russ Franklin stated he would like to keep it LDR and asked why add more to it where it is
already a busy intersection. He isn’t against development but keep it as it is zoned.

Bryan McQuiston stated the uses already allowed are meant for this neighborhood or small
commercial use for the neighborhood. It would be nice to see the city property master plan on
how this would connect.

Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing.

Applicant stated that the only comment he had was that they are trying to conform with the
general plan. The Commissioners asked a few clarifying questions to the applicant and discussed
the pros and cons of the request. Staff made some clarifications.

    Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to send a negative recommendation X
    Development’s request for review of the 2100 North Subdivision Concept, a 65-lot
    residential development located at approximately 2100 North 2300 West; based on the
    information that it is not in conformance with the existing general plan and the PUD concept
    that is being proposed exceeds the required density and the concerns relative to access and
    size of a private roads that are fronting multiple property’s; including all DRC comments
    and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Jackson, no; Commissioner Bunker, no; Commissioner Hereth, no;
    Commissioner Eyre, no. The motion failed.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to forward a positive recommendation of X
    Development’s request for review of the 2100 North Subdivision Concept, a 65-lot
    residential development located at approximately 2100 North 2300 West; contingent
    upon the 2100 North access and whether or not it is approved, contingent upon the houses
    that are setback with adequate parking and larger garages, requiring a traffic study for 2300
    West and 2100 North, recommending that the developer talk with the school district
    regarding a crosswalk; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
    Commissioner Eyre seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Ellis, no;
    Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed 5 – 2.

2.11) Public hearing and consideration of Dennis Schoonover’s request for approval of the
Lehi Drive Thru concept plan, a commercial development located at 600 West State Street.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

                Motion: Commissioner Everett motioned to extend the meeting until the agenda
                was completed. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

                Vote: All in favor.

The applicant, Adam Naylor, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Cory Gurney stated he owns property to the north. He has large trees and doesn't want to have any
destruction. He would like to see a sidewalk put in and wished it could be a community garden.

Melissa Nokes and Darlene Logan spoke together. They don’t feel like a light would justify the
traffic. They are against this happening. They are concerned about traffic and the lights shining
into their homes.

Brad Gurney is concerned about the size and asked why not put one building instead of two
buildings. He wants a walkable community and drive-thru would make that nonexistent. He felt
there would be a detrimental impact, especially if you don't know what will be there in 5 years.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Brad Kenison clarified some of the concerns for 600 West and discussed UDOT not installing a
signal.

The applicant decided to table the item.

    Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to table Dennis Schoonover’s request for approval
    of the Lehi Drive Thru concept plan, a commercial development located at 600 West State
    Street; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Everett
    seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Eyre, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
