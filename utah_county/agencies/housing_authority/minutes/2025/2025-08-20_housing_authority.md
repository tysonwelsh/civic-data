B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-0-02 HCV Administrative Plan Update 
Steve made a motion to approve the HCV Administrative Plan Updates. Sara seconded 
the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HUD notified Housing Authority of Utah County (HAUC) in June 2025 the Emergency Housing 
Vouchers (EHV) program is ending. The notice indicates Housing Authorities should try to 
convert any EHV families to the Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) program, provided the 
Housing Authority HCV program is not in shortfall. According to PIH notice 2025-19 to transfer 
families from EHV to HCV, Housing Authorities are required to amend their Administrative 
Plan. Prior to amending the Administrative Plan, the PHA must hold a 30-day public comment 
period on the proposed changes. During this public comment period, HAUC also reviewed and 
restructured all preferences given to applications on the waitlist. The proposed changes for 
preferences would be living or working in Utah County, living in an agency-owned housing 
unit, current EHV participant. No public comments were received during the 30-day public 
comment period. 
 
C. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-08-03 Carillon Court Roofing Contract 
April made a motion to approve the Carillon Court Roofing Contract. Suzanne seconded 
the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
The Housing Authority of Utah County operates a 16-unit senior property located in Orem, 
damaged by hail in August 2024. HAUC received three bids to replace all the shingle roofing 
on all structures. The three bids obtained are to remove the existing 2 layers of roofing 
material, install new asphalt shingles, and install new rain gutters and downspouts. The 
majority of the cost will be covered from the insurance claim proceeds, except for the rain 
gutters that are not part of the claim, since they were not present prior, this cost will be 
covered by HAUC.

F. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-08-06 Merit Policy PM Position. 
Sara made a motion to approve the Merit Policy PM Position. April seconded the 
motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC added a new project management position in 2024. In an effort to hire and retain well -
qualified personnel, HAUC desires to add an additional element to the Project Manager 
position within HAUC. In conjunction with the annual employee merit review for this position, it 
will now include an evaluation of the projects completed during the given year. A maximum 
threshold was set and may be awarded annually to the Project Manager based on the 
Executive Director's analysis of the project(s) completed on time, within budget. If the project 
is in progress, the status of the project relative to budget and delivery will be assessed by the 
Executive Director.
