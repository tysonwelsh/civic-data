Public Hearings and Possible Council Action

               Max Johnson, Planner, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services
Division, reviewed the following rezone application:

              Application #30887 – Bryon Price of Ivory Development requesting approval of a
rezone of approximately 9.10 acres from A-1/zc to R-1-4, located at 2851 S 8000 W, 7774 and
7834 W 2820 S, and 7701 W U-201.

The Magna Metro Township Planning Commission has recommended approval based on the
Planning Commissions findings that include; the rezoning development pattern will be consistent
and compatible with the adjacent property to the south. He has not received any feedback from
the neighborhood.

             Bryon Price, Ivory Development, stated he has worked with Salt Lake County staff
and the Magna Metro Township staff to see how to incorporate the 9.1 acres into the Gablers
Grove development agreement without changing it.

                Council Member Prokopis, seconded by Council Member Peel, moved to open
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Jeff Stevenson, Kennecott, stated Kennecott is in support of the rezone.

                Rick Nuesmeyer stated he is concerned about the 9 acres by 9000 west and
whether it will be left as industrial or will it be added to the agreement.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Paul Ashton stated Ivory Homes was looking to redo the master development

Allocation of Funds to Magna Town Council, Arts Council of Magna, Magna 4th of July, and Magna
Chamber of Commerce

            The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the release of funds to
the Magna Town Council, Arts Council of Magna, Magna 4th of July, and the Magna Chamber of
Commerce:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-03-01                                                    Date: March 26, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       THE RELEASE OF FUNDS PURSUANT TO THE APPROPRIATIONS OF 2019
       CONTRIBUTION FUNDING TO THE MAGNA TOWN COUNCIL, ARTS COUNCIL
       OF MAGNA, MAGNA 4TH OF JULY, AND MAGNA CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-
      2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. On December 18, 2018 as a part of the 2019 budget approval process, the Council
      approved and adopted the 2019 Magna Budget which included appropriations via
      contributions to the Magna Town Council, Arts Council of Magna, Magna 4th of July, and
      Magna Chamber of Commerce, as requested by the organizations and set forth in
      Attachment “A.”

   D. All four organizations have submitted their 2018 financials for consideration of the
      Council as a part of the approval and release of funds process.

   E. It has been determined from the review of financial data and other information provided
      by the organizations, they have met the Magna Contribution Policy requirements, and
      are hereby authorized to receive their budget 2019 contributions.

                                        RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby accepts the reports of the Magna
Town Council, Arts Council of Magna, Magna 4th of July and Magna Chamber of Commerce and
authorizes the release of the funds to these organization to continue their community work for
2019.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this 26th day of
FEBRUARY, 2019.
