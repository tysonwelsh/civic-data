AN ORDINANCE                    AMENDING          SECTIONS      18. 50. 050 AND 18. 55. 050 OF THE RIVERTON
           CITY CODE RELATING                     MINIMUM         PARCEL SIZE FOR MULTI -FAMILY
             DEVELOPMENTS.                  AMENDMENTS            PROPOSED          BY RIVERTON   CITY..


           WHEREAS, the Planning Commission and City Council have held a public hearing to
consider said text amendments;              and


           WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council on recommendation from the Commission has
determined that it is in the best interest of the public to amend Riverton City Code Sections
18. 50. 050 and 18. 55. 050 as described in Exhibit " A".


           NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:


           Section    1.       Riverton City Code Sections 18. 50. 050 and 18. 55. 050 shall be, and hereby are,
                               amended as shown in Exhibit " A.".


           Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
