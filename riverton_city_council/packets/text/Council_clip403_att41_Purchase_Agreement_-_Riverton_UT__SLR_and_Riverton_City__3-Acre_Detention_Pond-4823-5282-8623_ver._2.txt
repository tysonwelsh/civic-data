                                      PURCHASE AGREEMENT

        This PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is made and entered into this ____ day of
____________, 2020, by and between THE CHURCH OF JESUS CHRIST OF LATTER-DAY SAINTS,
a Utah corporation sole, f/k/a CORPORATION OF THE PRESIDING BISHOP OF THE CHURCH OF
JESUS CHRIST OF LATTER-DAY SAINTS (“Church Company”), and RIVERTON CITY, a Utah
municipal corporation (the “City”).

                                            RECITALS

     A.     Church Company owns certain real property located in Riverton City, Utah (the “Church
Company Property”).

        B.      The City desires to acquire a portion of the Church Company Property, which portion is
more particularly described on Exhibit A (collectively, the “Subject Property”), attached hereto and
incorporated herein by this reference.

        C.       Under the threat of condemnation, Church Company is willing to convey the Subject
Property to the City subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

                               TERMS AND CONDITIONS

        NOW THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration the receipt and sufficiency of which
are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

        1.      Purchase and Sale. Church Company agrees to sell and convey to the City, and the City
agrees to purchase from Church Company, the Subject Property.

        2.       Purchase Price. The purchase price for the Subject Property shall be $950,000.00 (the
“Purchase Price”). The City shall pay the Purchase Price to Church Company within seven (7) business
days after the Closing Date (as defined below) by wiring the Purchase Price to Church Company.

         3.      Opening of Escrow. The purchase and sale of the Subject Property shall be completed
through an escrow (the “Escrow”). The Escrow shall be opened at Old Republic National Title Insurance
Company, Attn: Hillary Morgan, 898 North 1200 West, Suite 101, Orem, Utah 84057, Phone: 801-735-
7711, email: hmorgan@oldrepublictitle.com (“Escrow Agent”). This Agreement shall constitute the
escrow instructions for the Escrow, provided that upon the request of the Escrow Agent, the parties shall
execute additional instructions as the Escrow Agent may reasonably require in order to carry out the
provisions of this Agreement. In the event of any conflict between the provisions of this Agreement and
such general escrow instructions, the provisions of this Agreement shall control. The parties reserve the
right to provide Escrow Agent with supplemental closing and title instruction

        4.      Closing. The closing and consummation of the purchase and sale transaction
contemplated herein (the “Closing”) shall occur on or before November 30, 2020 (the “Closing Date”).
Church Company and/or the City, as appropriate, will execute, notarize, and deliver to the other party, the
following on or before Closing:

        (a)     That certain quitclaim deed, in the form of Exhibit 4(a), attached hereto and
                incorporated herein by this reference (“Quitclaim Deed”), conveying the Subject
                Property to the City; and

         The City, at its sole option and cost, may elect to obtain an owner’s policy of title insurance for

        5.     Recording the Quitclaim Deed. In no event will the City be permitted to record the

Upon satisfaction of the foregoing conditions, the City shall be permitted to record the Quitclaim
Deed. If the Quitclaim Deed is recorded without the City receiving written authorization from
Church Company, then Church Company, in its sole and absolute discretion, may unilaterally
terminate the Quitclaim Deed without notifying the City by executing and recording a termination
of quitclaim deed in a form determined by Church Company.

         6.       Condition to Closing.

               6.1    Conditions to the City’s Obligations. The City’s obligation to purchase the
Subject Property, and proceed to Closing, shall be subject Church Company having deposited with
Escrow Agent all documents required of Church Company by this Agreement.

               6.2     Conditions to Church Company’s Obligations. Church Company’s obligations to
convey the Subject Property and proceed to Closing, shall be subject to the City having already deposited
with Escrow Agent all sums and documents and funds required of the City by this Agreement.

         7.       “AS IS” PURCHASE.

                  7.1     Disclaimer. Except as may be otherwise set forth in this Agreement, Church
Company has not made, and the City acknowledges that Church Company has not made, any warranty or
representation, express or implied, written or oral, statutory or otherwise, concerning the Subject
Property, including, but not limited to, the following: (i) the condition of title to the Church Company
Property; (ii) the nature, physical condition or any other aspect of the Church Company Property; (iii) the
existence of hazardous materials in, on, about, under or affecting the Church Company Property; or (iv)
the compliance of the Church Company Property with any environmental laws or any other federal, state
or local laws, ordinances, statutes, rules or regulations.

            7.2  Acceptance. SUBJECT TO THE EXPRESS TERMS OF THIS AGREEMENT,
THE CITY ACKNOWLEDGES FOR THE CITY AND THE CITY’S SUCCESSORS AND ASSIGNS,
THAT THE CITY WILL BE ACQUIRING THE SUBJECT PROPERTY BASED UPON THE CITY’S
OWN INVESTIGATION AND INSPECTION THEREOF. CHURCH COMPANY AND THE CITY
AGREE THAT, EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, THE SUBJECT
PROPERTY SHALL BE SOLD AND THE CITY SHALL ACCEPT TITLE AND POSSESSION OF
THE SUBJECT PROPERTY ON THE CLOSING DATE “AS IS, WHERE IS, WITH ALL FAULTS”
WITH NO RIGHT OF SET OFF OR REDUCTION IN THE PURCHASE PRICE, AND THAT
EXCEPT AS OTHERWISE SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, SUCH SALE SHALL BE
