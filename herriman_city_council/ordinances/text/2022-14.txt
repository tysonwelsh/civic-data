HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-14

Z2022-025 - ORDINANCE TO AMEND CITY CODE TITLE 10 TO ALLOW
INTERNAL ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS IN R-2 ZONES

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to Utah State Code, may adopt an ordinance to
establish land development regulations, which includes the adoption or amendment of zoning
regulations or map; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning regulation amendment to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior to
the said public hearing to recommend any zoning regulation amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, a notice of a Planning Commission public hearing on a zoning regulation
amendment was posted in three public places on March 7, 2022, for a meeting to be held on March
17, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission made a recommendation for the zoning regulation
amendment in a public meeting held on March 17, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chamber;
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council to
consider the zoning regulation amendment and Planning Commission recommendation in a public
meeting; and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on April 13, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. to
discuss the zoning regulation amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of Herriman
City to adopt the zoning regulation amendment, which was recommended by the Planning
Commission and documented in City File number Z2022-025;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:

Title 10 of Herriman City Code shall be amended as set forth in the attached Exhibit A, and
all other sections and provisions of Title 10 shall remain unaffected by this amendment.

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 2022-14 shall take immediate effect as soon as it shall be published
or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s recorder.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 23" day of April 2022.
