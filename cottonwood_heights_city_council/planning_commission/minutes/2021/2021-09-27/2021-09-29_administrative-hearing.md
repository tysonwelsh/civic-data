                                    COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY
                                    DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

                                    ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING MINUTES
                                    September 29, 2021

The Cottonwood Heights Community and Economic Development Director held an Administrative
Hearing Meeting on Wednesday, September 29, 2021, at 12:00 p.m. in the Cottonwood Heights City
Council Chambers located at 2277 E. Bengal Blvd., Cottonwood Heights, Utah 84121.

Present: Community & Economic Michael Johnson, Deputy City Recorder Heather Sundquist, Associate
Planner Samantha DeSeelhorst

Others Present: Applicant Jeff Bettinson

Community and Economic Development Director/Hearing Officer, Michael Johnson, welcomed those in
attendance and explained the Administrative Hearing process.
No Public Comments was received for this project and no one from the public attended the meeting.

12:00 p.m. ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING MEETING
           1.0 Business Items
                    1.1. (Project CUP-21-013)
                          Request from Jeff Bettinson for a conditional use permit to operate a short-term
                          rental at 3548 E. Wasatch Hills Ln.
Community & Economic Michael Johnson approved the conditional use permit and instructed the
applicant to work with staff to finish up the licensing and inspection processes.
           2.0 Consent Agenda
                    2.1. Approval of Minutes for September 29th, 2021
                (The Director will move to approve the above-mentioned minutes after the following process is met. The recorder will
                prepare the minutes and email them to the Director and present staff members. The Director and members will have
                five days to review the minutes and provide any changes to the recorder. If, after five days there are no changes, the
                minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until the changes are made and the
                Director andmembers agree, at which time the minutes shall be deemed approved.)

Meeting was adjourned at 12:02 pm
