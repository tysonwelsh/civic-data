## 1. Statistical corpus screen (both bodies, per-year)

  **0** across both corpora. Draper is born-digital text PDF throughout — confirmed.

## 7. Election sanity — PASS

- **Single-seat margins = winner − runner_up, exact:** 2021 council 3,105−2,335=770; 2025 council
  4,518−3,606=912; 2025 mayor 5,910−2,259=3,651; 2013 mayor 2,127−1,970=157. ✓
- **Multi-seat handled correctly:** `runner_up` = highest loser (first non-winner), `margin` =
  last-winning-seat − runner_up (the seat-deciding margin). 2023 council (3 seats) verified against
  by-candidate: winners Lowry 4,443 / Roberts 4,377 / Johnson 3,429; first loser Davis 1,949;
  margin 3,429−1,949 = **1,480 ✓** (matches races.csv). Same pattern checks out for 2007/2019.
- **Primaries flag advancers, not seats:** by-candidate `is_winner` count = 2×n_seats for every
  primary (advancers to general), = n_seats for **every** general. Correct semantics, not a bug.
- **2019 & 2021 raw-SOVC recoveries and the 2025 re-parse reconcile:** 2025 by-precinct sums
  Dahlin 4,518 + Byington 3,606 = 8,124 = races.csv totals, and the Utah-vintage `25DR0N`-style
  precinct labels (e.g. `25DR02`) that the canonical long file dropped **are present** here.
  Draper's own election layer is the corrected authority; the SLCo-normalizer undercount is a
  documented **upstream** cities.db caveat, logged in root `TODO.md` line 561 — **not a Draper defect**.

## Ranked fix list

1. **[WARN — minor data loss] Dropped Board-of-Canvassers roll call.**
   `meeting_minutes/minutes/2025/2025-08-25/2025-08-26_city-council-canvassers.md` contains a
   **standard named grid** — Resolution #25-42 (certifying the 2025 primary canvass): Green/Johnson/
   T.Lowery/Vawdrey `Yes`, **F. Lowry `Absent` → 4-0** — using the ordinary "Councilmember X" Yes/No/
   Absent layout, yet produces **zero** rows in `all_votes.csv`/db. Cause: the "City Council – Board of
   Canvassers" title/body is skipped by the extractor. Scan confirmed this is the **only** such case
   (the other 3 vote-less meetings are genuinely roll-call-free retreats/special). Fix: ingest the
   canvassers meeting (optionally as a distinct body, cf. RDA/MBA elsewhere) **or** record an explicit
   documented exclusion. Low volume, but a real named roll call is silently lost.

2. **[WARN — cosmetic/quality] `motion` text over-capture + 600-char cap.** Some narrative-format
   motions (2020–2021) run on into the next agenda item's preamble (~96 MM / 21 PC motions carry 2+
   "moved to" or embedded "roll call vote was taken"), and `motion` is hard-capped at 600 chars (243 PC
   motions truncated at the cap). **Vote rolls, movers/seconders, and results are unaffected** (all
   verified exact); the impact is readability of the motion description and weaker referral
   subject-matching (see the misleading `related_title` blobs in `db/referrals_audit.csv`). Improve
   motion-boundary segmentation / raise the cap.

3. **[Doc nit] No per-city top-level `CLAUDE.md`/`README.md`** at `draper_city_council/`. Repo-root
   CLAUDE.md calls each city's own CLAUDE.md "authoritative for that city," but Draper has only
   `SOURCES.md` + subfolder CLAUDE.md (`geo/`, `election_results/`). Add a city-root CLAUDE.md/README
   capturing the quirks (5 at-large + non-voting mayor, Thursday PC, Board-of-Canvassers, recap/minutes
   selector, county-straddle precincts, folder-eve-date convention).

## Audit blind spots (completeness critic)

- Ground-truth sampling was rich for 2020/2021/2024/2025; **2022 and 2023 had no motion-level
  ground-truth** (corpus stats for those years are clean, and 2023 supplied the vote-less-meeting
  checks). One split-word/hyphen outlier per corpus was reasoned-about, not source-diffed.
- No live re-fetch of `source_url` was performed (all 292 born-digital files present on disk with 100%
  recorded URLs; not re-GET-verified against Granicus this pass).
- Election by-precinct was reconciled for 2025 only (the re-parse year); earlier years reconciled at
  the race/by-candidate level, not precinct-summed.
