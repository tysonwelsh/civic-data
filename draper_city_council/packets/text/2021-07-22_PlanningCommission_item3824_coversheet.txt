Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: July 22, 2021
Re: Scalar Office Expansion Site Plan Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan Amendment for approximately 1.34 acres located on the south side of Pioneer Rd., at approximately 874 E. Pioneer Rd. The property is currently zoned TC. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan Amendment be approved to allow for the expansion of the existing office building on site. The findings are as follows: Site Design Standards Modification – Front Setback
For approval:
1. The constraints of the site do not lend themselves to achieving full compliance with the terms of the TC zone and have complied to the greatest extent possible;
2. The development meets the intent of the general plan and the terms of the TC zone;
3. The development is harmonious with surrounding development; and
4. Adequate provisions have been made to improve the site beyond the minimum requirements of the TC zoning classification.
For denial:
1. The constraints of the site do not preclude achieving full compliance with the terms of the TC zone;
2. The proposed development does not meet the intent of the general plan or the terms of the TC zone;
3. The proposed development is not harmonious with surrounding development; and
4. Adequate provisions have not been made to improve the site beyond the minimum requirements of the TC zoning classification.

Deviation from Parking Requirements
For approval:
1. Adequate parking will be provided;
2. The total number of spaces that would otherwise be required in the development is overly burdensome for the site specific factors of the application;
3. There is an adequate availability of approved on street or shared parking for all associated uses;
4. Site or use specific conditions or factors do not provide for compliance with the parking calculation used or parking requirements outlined in section 9-25-100, table 9-25-1 of the DCMC;
5. Transportation alternatives, including proximity to transit stations, provide adequate transportation to offset any reduction in parking;
6. Any potential for future expansion or addition to the development will have or will provide adequate parking for that expansion or addition; and
7. ADA compliant parking requirements are not proposed for adjustment.
For denial:
1. Adequate parking will not be provided;
2. The total number of spaces that would otherwise be required in the development is not overly burdensome for the site specific factors of the application;
3. There is not an adequate availability of approved on street or shared parking for all associated uses;
4. Site or use specific conditions or factors are able to provide for compliance with the parking calculation used or parking requirements outlined in section 9-25-100, table 9-25-1 of this chapter;
5. Transportation alternatives, including proximity to transit stations, do not provide adequate transportation to offset any reduction in parking;
6. Any potential for future expansion or addition to the development will not have or will not provide adequate parking for that expansion or addition; and
Site Plan Amendment
For approval:
1. The proposal represents development of the entire site at one time.
2. The proposed plans conform to applicable standards set forth in DCMC Title 9, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
