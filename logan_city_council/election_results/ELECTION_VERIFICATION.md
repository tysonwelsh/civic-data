## Notes

### 2023 election-integrity investigation + recount (special attention)
- Cache County administered Logan's 2023 election for the first time; the canvass (held ~Dec. 5, 2023) named **Anderson, Johnson, and Simmonds** as winners of the three at-large seats.
- The two trailing finishers — **Joe Needham** and **Katie Lee-Koven** — finished within the 0.25% statutory recount margin. **Katie Lee-Koven** (along with a Hyrum City candidate) requested/petitioned for a recount.
- A formal recount was conducted **Dec. 13–14, 2023** under joint supervision of an ad hoc Cache County election team and representatives from the Lt. Governor's office (Deidre Henderson).
- **The recount did NOT change the winners.** Simmonds retained the third seat. Per Cache Valley Daily, Simmonds finished **~16 votes ahead of Needham** (the first-loser), and Needham finished ~8 votes ahead of Lee-Koven. The repo records Simmonds 2,419 vs Needham 2,400 = **19-vote margin**; the small discrepancy (16 vs 19) reflects pre- vs post-recount/canvass tallies. Either way, the outcome (Anderson/Johnson/Simmonds win; Needham is first-loser by a razor-thin margin) is **confirmed**. (See also SLTrib: https://www.sltrib.com/news/politics/2023/12/20/these-two-cache-county-cities-had/ and UPR: https://www.upr.org/utah-news/2023-12-11/logan-city-to-recount-ballots-wednesday-in-city-council-race)

### Vote-total discrepancies (winners unaffected)
- 2019 council vote totals differ slightly between the repo (official canvass: Anderson 3,837 / Simmonds 3,221 / Jensen 2,546) and election-night news reports (Anderson 3,796 / Simmonds 3,173 / Jensen 2,522). These are unofficial-vs-official rounding differences; **the set of winners and their rank order are identical.**
- All primaries in the CSV are advancement contests (top 2N advance), not final winners, and are consistent with the general-election fields above.
