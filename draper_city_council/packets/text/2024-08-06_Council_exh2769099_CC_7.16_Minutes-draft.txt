Study Session

       Update: Major Road Capital Improvement Projects
       City Engineer Scott Cooley provided an update on current and planned road
       capital improvement projects and answered questions from the Council.

       Presentation: 2024 Moderate Income Housing Report
       The Moderate Income Housing Plan was adopted into the General Plan in
       2022, with an annual report and update as required by the State. Todd Taylor,
       Planner III, presented a 2024 Moderate Income Housing Report.

       Responding to a question from Councilmember Green, staff said the State had
       not been specific about the number of residential units expected to be
       included in Station Area Plans (SAPs). Mr. Taylor said the current draft of the
       Town Center SAP included approximately 560 residential units.
       Councilmember Green said he wanted to include as few residential units as
       possible. The Council discussed that the City’s annual Moderate Income
       Housing Update must be approved by a third party (Wasatch Front Regional
       Council) with somewhat ambiguous approval standards. Councilmember T.
       Lowery asked to see the most recent draft of the Town Center SAP. The Council
