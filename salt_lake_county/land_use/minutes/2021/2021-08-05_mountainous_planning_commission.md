amending Section 19.90.020 of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001, Entitled “Hearing - Notice” 
to require the posting of physical signs informing members of the public about certain land use hearings,

The Planning Commissions will act on the proposed ordinance amendments after taking comments from 
the public during their respective public hearings. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the 
planning commissions’ rules of conduct. Public comments will be limited to three minutes per person. The 
public is also invited to review and inspect the proposed ordinances at 
https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html under the respective Planning Commissions’ agendas. 
 
Salt Lake County Counsel Zach Shaw provided an analysis of the Ordinance of the Legislative Body of Salt 
Lake County, Utah, amending Section 19.90.020 of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001, 
Entitled “Hearing - Notice” to require the posting of physical signs informing members of the public about 
certain land use hearings, and related matters.  Mr. Shaw said he looked to FCOZ for conflict ; no FCOZ 
provision that this ordinance would run afoul of. Also reviewed the sign ordinance for conflicts and learned 
that two types of signs could fit the proposed notice , and both types are allowed in the MPD, namely 
property signs allowed in all zones, and temporary signs allowed in the FR, FM, and CV zones. Didn’t see 
conflicts with FCOZ design standards or sign ordinance applicable to the zones. Ordinance is j ust for 
rezone applications and doesn’t apply to administrative applications. Rezone applications are legislative, 
which involve broad public policy interest and would therefore be appropriate to give notice to the broad 
public interest. Recommend the PC recommend approval of the or dinance for the reasons discussed and 
not carve out the MPD from the proposed ordinance. 
 
Commissioner Reuling asked if ordinance only applied to rezoning applications. Mr. Shaw said applies 
strictly to rezone applications. Mr. Shaw read the applicable language of the ordinance. 
 
Commissioners and counsel had a brief discussion regarding rezones and jurisdictions, stand ard policy, 
and single lot rezones.  
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner  Omer seconded that motion.  
Commissioners voted unanimous (of commissioners present)
