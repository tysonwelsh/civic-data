                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-13

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE RE-APPOINTMENT OF COMMISSION
          MEMBERS TO THE RIVERTON CITY PLANNING COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance 2-95-020, the Mayor shall
appoint with advice and consent of the City Council residents of Riverton City to serve on the
Planning Commission; and

       WHEREAS, due to the expiration of the four-year term for Commission Members that
has occurred on the Planning Commission, it has become necessary to re-appoint Commission
Members; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms her nomination for re-appointment of Darren
Park, District 4, Gary Cannon, District 5 and Crystal Keele, At-Large; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the re-appointments of the Mayor's
nominations to the Planning Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Mayor's re-appointment of Darren Park, District 4, Gary Cannon, District
          5 and Crystal Keele, At-Large, be made with the advice and consent of the
          City Council to serve as Planning Commissioners, which shall expire January
          31, 2030.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
April 2026 by the following vote:
