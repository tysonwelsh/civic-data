 2. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. MBA Resolution No. 116 adopting the Annual Budget for the Municipal Building
          Authority of the City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2026.
          Budget and Management Analyst Becky Condie explained that the Municipal Building
          Authority (MBA) was established to fund the Public Works Building. The MBA leases the
          building to the city, and the city makes the debt service payments through lease
          payments.

           Chair Lamb opened the Public Hearing at 6:24 pm
           Comments:
           None
           Chair Lamb closed the public hearing at 6:24 pm

           MOTION: Board Member Green moved to APPROVE MBA Resolution No. 116 adopting

                   Jordan for Fiscal Year 2026.
                    Board Member Whitelock seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, Kayleen Whitelock, Bob Bedore,
