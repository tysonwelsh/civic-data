MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
Date: 2023-10-26
Re: Public Hearing: Draper Medical Condominiums Amended Subdivision Plat
(Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of an Amended Subdivision Plat for approximately 0.81
acres located on the south side of 12450 S., at approximately 114 E 12450 S. The property is
currently zoned CC (Community Commercial). The applicant is requesting that an Amended
Subdivision plat be approved to allow for the existing office building to be subdivided into
commercial condominiums through approval and recordation of the proposed Draper Medical
Condominiums Plat.




The findings for approval as are follows:



  1. Good cause exists for the subdivision plat amendment, and no Public Street, right-of-way, or
      easement has been vacated or amended.



The findings for denial as are follows:



  1. Good cause does not exist for the subdivision plat amendment.
