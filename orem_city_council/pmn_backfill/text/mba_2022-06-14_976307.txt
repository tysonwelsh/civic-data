APPROVAL OF MINUTES AND ANNUAL REVIEW
Minutes of the Annual Review of the MBA Bylaws

Tom Macdonald moved to approve the consent items. Jeff Lambson seconded the motion. Those
voting aye: David Spencer, LaNae Millett, Terry Peterson, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald and
Debby Lauret. The vote was unanimous, motion passed. :

ADJOURN TO A CLOSED MEETING

Mr Macdonald moved to adjourn to a closed door meeting to be held in Conference Room #107,
Orem City Hall, 56 N State Street, Orem, Utah, for a a strategic session to discuss pending or
reasonable imminent litigation pursuant to Utah Code Section 52-4-205(1)(a) and to discuss the
character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual pursuant to Utah
Code Section 52-4-205(1)(a). Ms Millett seconded the motion. Those voting aye: David Spencer,
LaNae Millett, Terry Peterson, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald and Debby Lauret. The vote was
unanimous, motion passed.

The meeting adjourned at 8:10 p.m.
