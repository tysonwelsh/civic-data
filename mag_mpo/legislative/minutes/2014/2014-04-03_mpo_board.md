Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Project Selection Process Initial Call for Project Ideas and TAC Voting Results - Bob Allen The TAC committee met on March 24th to review the 105 projects and 8 study ideas submitted by cities, UDOT, and UTA for a total of $152.3 million. The MPO and County funds available is $50 million. The UDOT projects generally do not receive MPO and County funds, but their ideas were scored like with the rest of the other ideas. Each jurisdiction was given one vote each. Ideas that received 50 or votes can move on the Concept Report phase. Project ideas that receiver below 50 can still submit a Concept Report if the sponsor wants too. Six of the eight studies receive over 50 votes, Concept Reports are not required for studies. 43 project ideas received 50 or more votes. Concept Reports are due April 24, 2014. A tour to all the projects with their sponsor is scheduled May 19th. TIP Project Selection will be May 27th. FY-15 TAC Requested/Recommended Special Studies and Draft Jurisdictional Cash Assessment Shawn Seager TAC Recommended studies for the 2014-2015 Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) for your approval: AMERICAN FORK - MEADOWS CONNECTION ROAD STUDY - $250,000 Federal $233,075 | Local Match $16,925 Studies a new connection road over the I-15 freeway in the Meadows shopping area connecting Pacific Drive west of State Street over the freeway to tie into Pioneer Crossing and possibly into the future Vineyard Connector. OREM - STATE STREET CORRIDOR STUDY - $100,000 Federal $93,230 | $6,770 Local match Orem. Studies land use, redevelopment potential, highway, and transit options (including Light Rail and Bus Rapid Transit) along State Street. Orem will management the Study. Partners (other than MAG) funding this study include; Orem $100,000, UDOT $50,000 and UTA $50,000. Total cost $300,000 PAYSON - HWY 198 CORRIDOR STUDY - $150,000 Federal $139,984 | Local Match $10,155 Projections show the need to widen this corridor through Payson. With the many challenges associated Mountainland MPO Regional Planning April 3, 2014 Page 3 with widening a road through a narrow, developed area, this study would look at alternatives, impacts, and potential intersection realignments. I-15/PROVO 800 NORTH INTERCHANGE STUDY - $300,000 Federal $279,690 | Local $20,310 University Parkway will soon fail and Provo Center Street is congested. This study would look at a new interchange at Provo 800 North as well as other locations. This is the highest densely population area in Utah County with the current interchanges over 4 miles apart. BYU and Utah Valley Intermountain Hospital are directly served by this proposed interchange. Preliminary modeling shows that a new interchange would significantly lower traffic on the adjoining interchanges. SOUTH UTAH COUNTY TRAIL PLAN - $160,000 Federal $149,168 | Local match $11,170 The south county cities and the county have come together to study major trail corridors in the south county. The goal is to study connections between the south county and Provo, create connections to the canyons, and create an implementation plan. I-15/SPRINGVILLE 1600 SOUTH INTERCHANGE STUDY - $50,000 Federal $50,000 | $3,385 Local Match UDOT. UDOT will provide an additional $50,000. Continue work at this proposed interchange to further design the interchange and ramps with the constraints of the new bridge and the short distance between the US-6 and Springville 400 South interchanges. Also will further develop the corridor design into Mapleton, Springville, and Spanish Fork. Commissioner Doug Witney moved to have the transportation studies recommended by the MPO TAC Committee be approved for MAG funds totaling $1.01 million and be placed in the draft Unified Planning Work Program. Mayor Richard Brunst seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously. Update Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) Scheduled for Approval June 2015 - Bob Allen The Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) is updated every four years which show roads, transit, and bike/ped improvements. There are 3 phases, Phase 1-2015-2025, Phase 2-2026-2035, and Phase 3- 2026-2040. After meeting with all the cities' staff, we have learned: Lehi, Saratoga Springs, and Eagle Mountain will have a population of 300,000 by 2040; Mountain View Freeway in Saratoga Springs needed by 2025; SR-73 Freeway to Eagle Mountain needed by 2015; Light Rail around the Point Phase 2; Commuter Rail South Phase 2, and BRT or Bus Plus on State Street in American Fork, Orem, Provo to Spanish Fork.
