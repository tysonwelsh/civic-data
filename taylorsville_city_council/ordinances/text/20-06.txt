AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING AMENDMENTS
  TO TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE CHAPTER 5. 24: ALCOHOL AND BEER


       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on July

1, 2020, to consider, among other things, approving amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL
CODE Chapter 5. 24: Alcohol and Beer; and


        WHEREAS, recent changes to state statutes regulating alcohol and beer sales in the state
have created conflicting provisions between state statutes and City ordinances regarding business
licensing and regulation of the sale of alcohol and beer; and

       WHEREAS, the City desires to amend its ordinances regulating alcohol and beer to
become consistent with state regulations in order to provide consistency to applicants regarding
the requirements to obtain an alcohol or beer license and the regulations associated with each; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 5. 24 are hereby approved and
are designated by interlineating the words to be deleted and underlining the words to be added, as
follows:

       5. 24.45140: CIVIL FINE:


       In addition to any civil or criminal remedy available to the city to enforce the provisions of this
       chapter, a person who violates any provision of this chapter is subject to a civil fine of up to one
       thousand dollars ($ 1, 000. 00) for each violation. A civil fine is due and payable within twenty

        20) days of the date of citation from the city giving notice of the civil fine. Notice of a civil fine
       shall be given by personal service or by posting in the United States mail, postage paid, certified
       mail, return receipt requested. Unpaid civil fines are a debt owed to the city and may be the
       subject of a civil action filed in any court having legal jurisdiction.
