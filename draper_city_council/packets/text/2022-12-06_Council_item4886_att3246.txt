Study Session

The Council took Study Session Item 2 before Item 1 for scheduling reasons.

1.0   Presentation: The Point – Alan Matheson

1.1   Alan Matheson, Executive Director of the Point of the Mountain State Land
      Authority, updated the Council on development plans for The Point.

2.0   Council/Manager Reports

2.1   Councilmember T. Lowery asked about the potential for a performing arts
      facility in Draper. A possible location was discussed. Staff commented that if
      the City were to pursue a performing arts facility, it should be included in the
      City’s Station Area Plan. Staff recommended that if the Council wanted to put
      a general obligation bond on the ballot in 2023 for a performing arts center,
      planning for the project should begin as soon as possible. Mayor Walker
      suggested including a “civic center” or “cultural center” on the Station Area Plan
      for now and recommended staff hire a consultant to help put together ideas
      without the intention to put a bond on the 2023 ballot.

2.2   Councilmember F. Lowry suggested holding a joint meeting with the City
      Council and Planning Commission.

5.0   Public Comments

5.1   Chad England, Draper resident, said he lived on a private lane just north of
      Summit Middle School. He said he built his driveway to specifications provided
      by the City to enable garbage collection to continue to occur on the private
      lane, but upon moving in he received a notice that garbage pickup on the
      private lane would be discontinued. Mr. England asked that garbage collection
      service resumes for the private lane, and said he would be willing to sign
      liability waivers if needed.
