4.    Public Hearing: Public Hearing Mother and Mine Massage Home Occupation
      Conditional Use Permit (Administrative Action)
      On the request of Sariah Boyer for a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for
      a personal care service business on 0.34 acres of property located at 447 E Kimball
      Ln. Application USE-193-2022. Staff contact is Jennifer Jastremsky, 801-576-
      6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

      Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, presented the project. The applicant Ms.
      Sariah Boyer has requested a conditional use permit to have a massage business in
      her home. The massage procedure would be in the home in a spare bedroom of
      the house. There is a separate driveway in front of the home where clients will park
      when they come for a visit. The application stated that she would have seven clients
      per week.

      Applicant: Ms. Sariah Boyer, requested to add the possibility of more clients. She
      currently is working Tuesday through Thursday from 9am.to 12pm. She may want
      to add more hours allowing more clients at some point. The amount would not be
      more than fifteen clients per week.

      Chairman Adams opened the public hearing. No comments were made. Chairman
      Adams closed the public hearing.

      The Commissioners discussed what the next step would be if they approved the
      applicant for seven clients now and she wanted to expand in the future. Ms.
      Jastremsky stated that the applicant would have to come back with a new
      conditional use permit application and ask for more clients. The Commission
      discussed modifying the request to approve the additional clients at this time to up
      to fifteen.

      Motion: Commissioner Hawker motioned to approve the Mother and Mine
      Massage Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit as requested by Sariah
      Boyer representing Mother and Mine Massage. Application USE-193-2022.
      Based on the finding and subject with the change in the total amount of fifteen
      visitors per week.

      Second: Commissioner Fowler

      Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 5-0 in favor of the
      motion. Commissioners Fowler, Ogden, Squire, Hawker, and Bingham voted,
      “Aye”.

5.    Public Hearing: Living Planet Aquarium Science Learning Center Amended
      Site Plan (Administrative Action)
      On the request of Brent Anderson, representing Loveland Living Planet Aquarium,

      for an Amended Site Plan for 2.05 acres of property located at 12047 South Lone
      Peak Parkway. Application SPR-113-2022. Staff contact is Jennifer Jastremsky,
      80-576-6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

      Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, presented the project. The proposed
      building addition will be built to the east of the claw. This addition will include
      space for class rooms, office space, and event space. Parking spaces will be added
      as well as a fire lane around the entire building. The parking will include 751
      spaces and is in compliance. A parking study was done in 2019 when the
      Aquarium built the claw, which included this addition. For events with 3,000 or
      more people the Aquarium would have to obtain a special event permit and address
      parking needs. The addition will have minimal landscaping, and will mostly match
      the exterior of the existing building. The front of the building will have a unique
      design. For any signage the Aquarium would have to apply for a signage permits
      separately.

      Applicant Brent Anderson stated the amount of 3,000 people for events is only
      during the operating hours. The largest event they have had was about 1,200
      people. Even when they have had events during the operating hours, they do not
      exceed 3,000 people.

      Chairman Adams opened the public hearing. No comments were made. Chairman
      Adams closed the public hearing.

      The Commission discussed the need to clarify the 3,000 people is as any one time.
      Mike Barker, City Attorney stated since the conditions of approval are showing
      existing conditions on a previous approval they can’t amend them. The
      Commission decided to provide clarifying language in the motion so there is
      something on the record.

      Motion: Commissioner Squire moved to approve the amended site plan as
      requested by Brent Anderson representing the Living Planet Aquarium for
      the Living Planet Aquarium Science Learning Center. Application SPR-113-
      2022. Based on the findings and subject to the conditions listed in the staff
      report dated November 1, 2022. Additionally, the applicant and city both
      acknowledge that the intent is that no more than 3,000 people would be in
      attendance without obtaining a special event permit from the city.

      Second: Commissioner Ogden

      Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 5-0 in favor of the
      motion. Commissioners Fowler, Ogden, Squire, Hawker, and Bingham voted,
      “Aye”.

6.    Public Hearing: City Initiated Lone Peak Marketplace Freeway Sign Text
      Amendment (Legislative Action)
      On the request of Draper City for a Text Amendment to DCMC Section 9-26-140,
      the Sign Code’s Freeway Frontage Designated Development Area exhibits, for
      7.71 acres of property located at 11479 South State St. Application TEXTMAP-
      197-2022.      Staff   contact    is   Jennifer   Jastremsky,     801-576-6328,
      jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

      Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, presented the project. The text
      amendment requested was to edit exhibits within the sign code. Any business
      located adjacent to the freeway is allowed additional signage than would otherwise
      be allowed. The Freeway Frontage Signage code has different subzones, A-D, with
      each zone setting height and size standards. Zone A signage rules are 40 ft. in
      height, and 200 sq. ft. in area. Zone B signage rules are 30 ft. in height and 100 sq.
      ft. in area. The requested amendment will modify the property along 11400 South
      and State Street from the Zone B area standards to the Zone A area standards.
      The applicant is Draper City, the land/business owner will file a signage permit
      application at a later time.

      Chairman Adams opened the public hearing. No comments were made. Chairman
      Adams closed the public hearing.

      The Commission asked staff to clarify the potential impacts of such a change. Ms.
      Jastremsky stated the impacts would be to the one property and would allow a sign
      that is taller and larger then currently required. She showed potential signs that
      could be built if the amendment was approved.

      Motion: Commissioner Hawker motion to forward a positive
      recommendation to the city council as requested by Draper City for the City
      initiated Lone Peak Marketplace Freeway Sign Text Amendment.
      Application TEXTMAP-197-2022. Based on the conditions and subject to the
      changes listed in the staff report dated October 21, 2022.

      Second: Commissioner Bingham

      Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 5-0 in favor of the
      motion. Commissioners Fowler, Ogden, Squire, Hawker, and Bingham voted,
      “Aye”.

      Jennifer Jastremsky reminded the Commissioners to complete their training, and
      reminded the Commissioners about the meeting on November 17, 2022.
