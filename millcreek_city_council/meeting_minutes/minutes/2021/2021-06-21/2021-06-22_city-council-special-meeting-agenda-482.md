Mayor       Silvestrini        called         the special     work    meeting          to order.         He said the purpose              of the meeting
was to get a report             on the Promise              Program      from      the new Promise                  Program        Manager,           Kayla
Mayers.


1. Discussion            on Standard                Procedures        of Operation               for the Millcreek                Promise       Program
Kayla       Mayers       said after meeting               with     the Mayor,      volunteers,             and council         members,             volunteer
management,             structure,        and action        felt like the most important                     things      for Millcreek          Promise         to
focus on. She would                 like to restructure            the committees               for quicker         action.       She would          like to
combine          the Leadership            Committee             and the Promise              Council      into only the Promise                Council,           and
they will        hold quarterly           meetings        to update      stakeholders             on the work            of the comi'nittees,             which
are Safety,          Economic        Wellbeing,           Health,     and Education.               Each of the committees                    will     work      on
projects,        such as safe routes                to schools,     a job fair, digital            inclusion,        and mental          health.       Mayers
said she reworked              the Promise             Program       mission,      vision,        and promises            to be shorter.            The
proposed         mission      is, "We          aim to mobilize         and support              Millcreek         residents       to build     a thriving
community."             The proposed             vision     is,"   We do this througli                  mobilizing        our collaborative               groups
towards      Collective         Impact           to improve        community            conditions          in the areas of safety,                 health,
education         and economic             wellbeing.        This program              will    work       to create equitable            community
programs         with    the goal of bringing                the community              together          to help one another.              Millcreek
Promise       will      accomplish            this work     by and supporting                  current     programs,          creating      programs          and
