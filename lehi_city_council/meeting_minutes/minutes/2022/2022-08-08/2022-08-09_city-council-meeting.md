2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Truth in Taxation Update
Dean Lundell, Finance Director, explained how the property tax rate works in the State of Utah.
He noted that Lehi’s allotment is only a small portion of what people pay in total for their
property taxes. He stated that anytime a city proposes a higher rate then what the certified rate is,
then we must go through a Truth in Taxation process. He said that he plans to present at the
public hearing next week. He also explained that if that tax passes, then it will go to fund two
new Police Officer positions.

2.2 Discussion of green waste collection termination
Dean Lundell explained that there is no green waste collection right now because it is no longer
being accepted. Residents still have a green waste can and may use it for green waste and any
other garbage. The issue now is that the city is still charging a lesser green waste fee which
doesn’t cover the actual cost to pick up the can. Options were discussed such as charging the
regular garbage rate for the can, or offering to pick up the cans. Blake Leonelli, with Waste
Management, stated that picking up the cans could be a logistical challenge. He said that they are
exploring some other options with includes a possible site that may be willing to accept the green
waste. He will provide updates to the Council after they’ve explored other options.
