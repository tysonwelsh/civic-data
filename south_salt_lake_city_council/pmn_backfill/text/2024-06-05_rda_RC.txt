Public Hearing – 6:05 p.m. (Or As Soon Thereafter As Possible)
To consider adopting the annual budget of the Redevelopment Agency of the City of South Salt Lake for
the period commencing July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025.

information to add other than what has already been discussed at the last couple of Redevelopment
Agency meetings. Additionally, there have been no new questions submitted by the Directors.

There were no comments.
