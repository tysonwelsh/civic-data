Economic Vitality
St. George is the region’s economic center for commerce and industry and will provide a solid foundation for the growth to come.
St. George will implement the following to maintain and enhance the economic success of the community:
3.1 SUPPORT A THRIVING AND RESILIENT ECONOMY
a. Support local businesses and entrepreneurs.
b. Support initiatives in collaboration with our public and private sector partners to strengthen our economy, build local assets, and enhance our quality of life.
c. Cultivate the retention and expansion of existing industry and small business.
d. Actively recruit businesses in targeted industry clusters.
e. Serve as the primary prospect handler for economic development projects.
f. Institute land use policy and zoning that enables businesses to thrive throughout the city. Align with goals identified in Responsible Growth.
i. ii. Implement recommendations from the Downtown Area Plan. Support a mix of uses, recognizing that commercial and residential developments are symbiotic, and a mix of uses can solidify economic vitality.
g. Take advantage of legislative tools, and funding as available to support redevelopment and moderate income housing expansion within St. George.
h. Maintain and enhance opportunities for a diverse mix of businesses by creating an environment where businesses can thrive.
i. ii. iii. iv. v. vi. Support infrastructure improvements that facilitate development in strategic areas of the City. Include opportunities for clean industry and manufacturing. Consider opportunities to integrate light or boutique manufacturing and a mix of uses in commercial zone districts, as appropriate. Target high-tech industries and partner with other local entities to support tech innovation. Foster and support businesses that have a reduced environmental impact. Prioritize businesses that discourage high water use and provide high paying jobs.
3.2 PROVIDE SERVICES TO SUPPORT THE SKILLED WORKFORCE AND CREATE A RESILIENT FOUNDATION OF WELL-PAID EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITIES
a. Coordinate with the Governor’s Office of Economic Opportunity, the St. George Area Chamber of Commerce, and other community partners to amplify efforts to grow the workforce and increase access to quality job opportunities, job training, job placement, and attainable housing.
b. Work closely with the Washington County School District to ensure access to education throughout the city.
c. Work closely with Utah Tech University, Dixie Technical College, and other local institutions to support career and technical training in alignment with the current and future needs of St. George.
3.3 3.4 EXPAND TOURISM AND VISITOR SERVICES TO IMPROVE BOTH THE VISITOR AND RESIDENT EXPERIENCE WITHIN THE CITY
a. Enhance wayfinding, and access to information to guide visitors to target destinations and ensure they can move effectively through the city.
i. ii. iii. iv. Implement the recommendations within the Downtown Area Plan. Support branding and wayfinding across all Neighborhood Areas. Encourage the development of a diverse range of hospitality options to encourage a variety of preferences and budgets. Continue investment in the development and maintenance of parks, trails and recreational facilities.
b. Enhance visitor services
i. ii. Collaborate with neighboring cities, Greater Zion and Utah Department of tourism to promote the City of St. George. Connect with community organizations supporting activities and events across the city.
c. Support a comprehensive web and social media presence with events, activities, destinations, and information about the City of St. George. d. Implement the recommendations to expand access to art and culturally significant elements across the city. Align with goals identified in Arts and Culture Integration.
PROVIDE INFRASTRUCTURE TO SUPPORT THE ECONOMY
a. Provide adequate infrastructure to support new and future development. Align with goals identified in Responsible Growth. i. Augment the transportation infrastructure to improve the link between the local workforce and the local jobs with a focus on improved transit and active transportation access. Align with goals identified in Accessible City.
b. Ensure access to information technology that supports the remote work revolution.
c. Protect the airport from development that would hinder future growth and economic opportunity in this district.
i. Limit residential development within flight paths to minimize conflicts between airport operations and residents.
d. Work with developers on infrastructure financing programs that will support city policies.
