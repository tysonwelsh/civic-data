INITIAL PROPOSAL FOR CONSIDERATION OF ACTION
e Resolution No, 2020-20 — Amending the Cache County Sheriff's Office Fee

Schedule - Sheriff Chad Jensen thanked the Council for the Fair and noted there were
only a few minor reports involving his office.

Sheriff Jensen explained the fees must be adopted by resolution. Some are set by
statute and others are set by his department. He has tried to be consistent with what

other entities are charging.

Council member White asked who pays for inmates medical expenses above the co-
pay. Jensen stated medical costs for inmates housed for other entities are paid by the
entity and county inmates pay for theirs. A bill was passed last year that lets inmates
apply for Medicaid thirty days before their release date which may help with the costs.

Council member Zilles referred to a fee for an alcohol monitor and asked what that is.
Sheriff Jensen said they can be handheld or a wrist monitor. According to any court
order dates that the monitoring must be accomplished are programmed in to the device;
the person blows through a tube attached to the device and the results are recorded and
transmitted to the proper authority.

COUNCIL MEMBER REPORTS

Dave Erickson and all Council members praised the Fair and Rodeo and the citizens who
attended.

Barbara Tidwell has sent pictures to the Council of the banners for the Cache Celebration of
Women. It will be August 26, 2020 at 6:30 p.m. on the steps of the courthouse. It will last one
hour and there will be speakers and demonstration kiosks. The Celebration is free as is the film
on women’s suffrage at the Utah Theater that evening.

Gina Worthen observed people in lines at the Fair kept physical distancing, were careful and
seemed to have a good community spirit. Events in other areas have not shown spikes in
COVID-19 cases afterward. The determination to have the Fair was not made lightly; the
county looked at related matters in-depth and was careful about the decision.

Gordon Zilles mentioned LaMont Poulsen, Rodeo Chair, wants the Council to know he never
doubted that the Council would support him no matter what.

Paul Borup said his report last time on disease mitigation measures authored by D. A.
Henderson, who stopped the smallpox virus around the world, ends with this statement:
“Experience has shown that communities faced with epidemics and other adverse events
respond best and with the least anxiety when the normal functioning of the community is least
disrupted.” Borup thinks the Fair showed that and he complimented Sheriff Jensen and his
deputies and the citizenry of Cache County.

Discussion on other matters:

o CARES Applications — Council member White wanted to know the status of the
CARES applications by local entities. Executive Buttars said UFO has resubmitted their
application and the county reviewed it and sent a letter approving it. CAPSA’s
application was also approved as were several other entities. A report specifying those
entities will be presented at a future Council meeting.

In response to Vice Chair Worthen’s question, Buttars outlined the process as follows:
The county distributed the application form to interested parties and made it available on
the county website; as those forms come in, a committee (Craig Buttars, Bryce Mumford,
Cameron Jensen, Philip Noble) reviews them to see if they qualify under the guidelines;
the Executive makes the decision whether they qualify, if they will be funded and at what
