CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2023-1237-O, ANORDINANCE REZONING A
CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
1800 WEST 3600 NORTH ON KOLOB FROM THE OSC-20 ZONE (OPEN SPACE
CONSERVATION 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE), TO THE OST-20 ZONE (QPEN
SPACE TRANSITION 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE), HEREINAFTER FULLY
DESCRIBED IN THIS ORDINANCE (APPLICANT KEN GRAFF)

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the applicant owns approximately
one hundred and sixty (160) acres near the Washington County / Iron County line. The OSC-20
does not allow for a dwelling on the property, however, the OST-20 does. The Planning
Commission unanimously recommended approval.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2023-1238-O, ANORDINANCE APPROVING
VACATION OF A PORTION OF THE ORIGINAL REX LAYNE ROAD, A PLATTED
RIGHT OF WAY IN WASHINGTON COUNTY, UTAH

Mr. Messel addressed the next two items. He stated in the Dixie Deer Estates Subdivision in
Central there is a road called Rex Layne Road. The Road was never completed, and did not tie into
SR-18. The property has been an enforcement problem, with the house being built over the
property line and into various other property lines. The Planning Commission recommends
vacation of the original right of way. The next item would approve an updated plat map once this
vacation has been finished.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2023-1238-O, An
Ordinance Approving Vacation of a Portion of the Original Rex Layne Road,
a Platted Right of Way in Washington County, Utah. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow and Iverson voting aye.

ROAD IN THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

2020, it was done at the same time the Northern Corridor was passed. Part of the HCP included
locations for tortoise crossings. This contract would be to study the best location for these

crossings.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3165, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE EXECUTION OF INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH
HURRICANE CITY REGARDING AN OFFICIAL BALLOT DROP BOX TO ENHANCE
THE SAFETY AND SECURITY OF BALLOTS AND ELECTIONS

Washington County Clerk-Auditor Ryan Sullivan explained this Resolution would approve an
Interlocal Agreement with Hurricane City to become compliant with state law regarding official
ballot drop boxes within each precinct. This allows two drop box locations in Hurricane City, one
at the Hurricane library and one in Hurricane City Hall.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3165, A
Resolution Approving the Execution of Interlocal Cooperation Agreement
with Hurricane City Regarding an Official Ballot Drop Box to Enhance the
Safety and Security of Ballots and Elections. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow and Iverson voting aye.
