Work Session
(4:30 P.M.)
1)
Work Session regarding a Master Planned Development for Outlets Park City, a 19-building mixed use redevelopment project located at 6699 N Landmark Dr (Parcel FSE-1, containing 37.32 acres); Kimball Junction, Summit County, UT. The applicant is proposing to Rezone Parcel FSE-1 from the Town Center Zone to the Neighborhood Mixed-Use 1 (NMU-1) Zone. Applicant: Elliott Workgroup, representing SRE Ontario LLC; Administrative and Legislative Review. Project #24-087. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.
(4:32 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Outlets Park City MPD-NMU-CUP-LIP Staff Memo - March 25, 2025.pdf
Attachment:
Junction Commons Presentation
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
provided an overview and a brief history of the project to update the new commission members.
Planner Caus stated that the primary focus of the work session would be on traffic, parking, and multimodal transportation. (4:32 P.M.)
Craig Elliott with the Elliott Workgroup presented the Junction Commons plan for work session. (4:36 P.M.)
Adam Greenebaum with SRE Ontario LLC briefly addressed the Commission and outlined current happenings, new and potential tenants for the property. (4:37 P.M.)
Mr. Elliott continued the presentation. He reviewed the project design concept and vision for housing, parking, multimodal transportation, and connectivity. (4:40 P.M.)
Peter Tomai, SRE Ontario LLC, provided additional information about bike lanes and trails, and connecting points that have been identified. (4:54 P.M.)
Mr. Elliott provided detailed information about parking updates, phases, and calculations. (4:58 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy asked a clarifying question about initial data gathered during the pandemic and whether or not updated data was available to which Mr. Tomai and Mr. Greenebaum responded. (5:07 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley asked for clarification regarding the proposed build out of stalls. Additionally, she asked about multimodal data in the traffic report. (5:09 P.M.)
Commissioner Young asked questions about long-term projections regarding parking and peak seasons. Mr. Greenebaum, Mr. Elliott, and Mr. Tomai responded to Commissioner Young's concerns.
Commissioner Young asked for additional information about goals for creating a sustainable community to which Mr. Elliot replied. Mr. Tomai provided further information about environmental standards. (5:10 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy asked additional questions about the 10% reduction in trip generation and affordable housing. Mr. Elliot and Peter Barnes, Community Development Director, responded to Commissioner Peteroy’s questions. (5:16 P.M.)
Justin Keys with Hoggan Lee Hutchinson provided additional information surrounding the discussion of housing and parking in a commercial center. (5:22 P.M.)
Commissioner Jeffrey asked a question regarding the number of units to which Planner Caus and Mr. Elliott responded. (5:24 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney thanked Commissioners for their comments and concerns and thanked the applicants for their time and the information provided during the work session. (5:30 P.M.)
