WORK SESSION

               Daniel Allen, SuperTrees Inc., delivered a PowerPoint presentation regarding his
ideas for the Copperton Park. He visited the park recently and feels it is a special resource. The
park has a few dead trees and lacks diversity of species. He recommended going with a slower
growing tree and more of a quality tree.

                Eric Behunin, HortusGroup LLC, delivered a PowerPoint presentation regarding
his ideas for the Copperton Park. He noted his company strives to be long term partners with
clients.

              Josh Shroyer, FLAMINGTREE Solutions, LLC, presented his ideas regarding the
Copperton Park. His company will work with the objectives of the Council. They work with all
communities to diversify the species of trees to no more than 10 percent for each species.

                 Steven Geist, SavATree Consulting Group, presented his ideas regarding the
Copperton Park. He has 35 years of experience. Although his company is large, they create
small working groups for easy communication with clients. In addition, surveys are custom made
for each client.

SLVLESA Working with Utah Legislature to Modify Property Tax Cap

               Mr. Frank Nakamura, District Administrator, SLVLESA, stated he forwarded an
information paper to the Council for discussion. He is not asking for any action today. He asked
for the Council’s support and input for three reasons:

   1. This matter does not involve asking for property tax increases.
   2. Copperton is a strong supporter of law enforcement.
   3. This issue emphasizes local control.

Existing law puts a cap on tax rates, which was set 14 years ago. SLVLESA is up against the
cap, partly due to tax increases, but also because of devaluations in centrally assessed
properties. The only revenue for townships is property tax and SLVLESA needs to have the
option to raise taxes in the future if necessary.

                Council Member Pazell stated he has seen the budgets and SLVLESA saying
that there is no other option is not true. It is because they ran out of tax increases. There is a cap
on it so SLVLESA will live within their means. He will tell the legislature he does not agree with
this proposal and he encouraged other Council Members to do the same.

Franchise Tax Update

               Nathan Bracken stated the metro townships were created as quasi-municipalities,
which expressly excluded the ability to get franchise taxes. This is an issue because Copperton
needs a broad base of revenue to be successful. The League of Cities and Towns asked him to
put together a presentation, but they were not able to get to it during their last meeting. This has
not been a big issue for Copperton, but it has been for other metro townships.
