Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                                 July 14, 2020

The regular meeting of the Washington County Planning Commission was conducted on Tuesday,
July 14, 2020 from the Commission Chambers located in the Washington County Administration
Building, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Also, via electronic means through Zoom,
Chairman Kevin Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of Allegiance and
explained how the meeting would be conducted.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate), Shayne Durrant, Reed Scow
(Alternate acting as a voting member), Doug Solstad, and Jon Walter. Slade Hughes, and Mark
Owen attended the meeting via Zoom.

Excused: Shayne Durrant.

County Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Natalie Nelson, Deputy
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Cheyenne Bentley, Deputy Clerk at
host location. Todd Edwards, Engineer; Kurt Gardner, Building Official.


1.   PUBLIC HEARING/ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS. Review, discuss, and hold a hold
a public hearing for amendments to the Washington County Ordinances Title 10, Chapter 4:
Definitions; and Title 10, Chapter 8B: Seasonal Forest Residential; and Title 10, Chapter 13:
Supplementary and Qualifying Regulations for a Building Permit. County initiated.

Mr. Messel explained that the code allows for an accessory dwelling on any property as a
conditional use. However, we did not put a cap on that number. On parcels larger than 5-acres,
multiple accessory dwellings were allowed. What we are proposing is to amend 10-13-18-4:
Accessory Dwelling. The proposed changes are to allow an additional accessory dwelling on larger
parcels at one per every five acres with a maximum of four. More than four accessory dwellings
may be approved as part of Planned Development (PD) Project He cited the proposed change to
the definitions.

The next item we addressed was shipping containers. There is a lot of ambiguity on how the county
should handle shipping containers. Staff worked to address that issue. We created a definition and
outlined criteria to address the shipping container use requirements. He cited the proposed
ordinance.

A discussion, clarification, and suggestions regarding the ordinance changes occurred.

Chairman Jones opened the public hearing.

her business, as they are stacked onsite now. Otherwise, she would need a large amount of acreage

2.     DISCUSSION ITEM. Discussion of the Zion Under Canvas Facility.

Mr. Messel provided the existing Zion Under Canvas site plan. He explained that the applicants
are requesting to add restrooms to the existing cabins. There will not be any additional structures
constructed. The applicants will need to meet all of the state and county water and wastewater
requirements. In addition, they will need to work with the building department to add those
improvements.

The reason behind bringing this to the commission is for their input on how the planning
commission would view the change and if it were significant enough that the conditional use
permit should be amended.

Mr. Hughes asked if there were a need for a conditional use, under our current code, would that in
turn require them to go to the PD Rural Recreation zone. Mr. Messel answered that his first
response would be no, it would just be an amendment to the existing conditional use permit.

A short discussion regarding the legalities and process to amend the conditional use and the
proposed amendment occurred.

The commission agreed to allow the project to move forward without any additional requirements
to change the conditional use permit.


3.     DISCUSSION ITEM. Discussion of the following items:

            •      County Code Conditional Use Permit Section on Gravel Pits.

Mr. Messel explained the existing code for gravel pits and quarries. There have been several
concerns brought up regarding the size of the pits and limitations as to how they could expand
without coming back to the county for approval and reclamation. Staff has reviewed how the state
and other agencies address the issue. He asked that the planning commission review the draft
ordinance proposal to determine moving forward with the changes and a possible public hearing.

Mr. Owens provided some feedback regarding the parcel minimum property size and placing a
berm around the property.

            •      Planning Commission procedures.

Ms. Nelson explained the changes to the planning commission procedures. Currently, the code
does not address tie votes nor, does the planning commission have any by-laws. The code allows

Commission on planning items.

Mr. Messel reported that following the last planning commission meeting, Mr. Jerry Eves
withdrew his application for the RV Park. The zone change request for the Cemetery Hill RV

The commission reviewed the vote for the RV Park and the results.
