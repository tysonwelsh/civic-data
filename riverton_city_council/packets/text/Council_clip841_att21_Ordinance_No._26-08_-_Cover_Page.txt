                                       City Council Meeting Agenda
                                                May 5, 2026



PRESENTER:             Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director


ITEM:


Ordinance No. 26-08 - Adoption of Water Conservation Element of General Plan, Correcting the previously
adopted Water Conservation and Efficiency Standard to be included, as an element of the General Plan,
proposed by Riverton City
On April 23, 2026, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to consider this item. The Planning
Commission at that hearing voted to recommend approval of this item, with the following recommended
motion:

“I move the City adopt Ordinance 26-08, adding the Water Conservation and Efficiency Standards as an
element of the Riverton City General Plan.”


SUMMARY:
Riverton City has previously adopted an update to the City’s Water Conservation and Efficiency
Standard. The purpose of this standard is to both coordinate Riverton City’s water system to projected
growth within the City, and to determine methods by which conservation goals and needs can be
achieved. The standard was adopted in August of last year as an update to the previous version adopted in
1999. However, State law requires cities to have this information and standards as an element to their
General Plans. The adoption of the plan in August did not specifically reference the General Plan, and this
is corrected by this action.
 The Plan itself was created with the assistance of a consultant team, and no corrections are proposed or
required at this time. The motion simply corrects the adoption to include this as an element of the Riverton
City General Plan.
