                                MEMORANDUM
To:              Honorable Mayor and City Council
From:            Planning Department
Date:            November 16, 2021
Subject:         Ordinance 21-28, Amending Section 18.45, RM-6, Amending The RM-6 Zone To
                 Individual Lot And Detached Units Only, Amendments Proposed By Riverton City


On September 23, 2021, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this proposed
text amendment. Minutes from that meeting will be available once transcribed. The Planning
Commission voted to recommend approval, with the following suggested motion:

I move the City Council adopt Ordinance 21-28, amending Section 18.45, limiting development in the RM-
6 Zone to individual lots and detached units only, with associated standards as shown in Exhibit “A”.

Background

Riverton City currently has in its code the RM-6 Zone, which allows for multifamily development at a
maximum density of six units per acre. That code allows for both attached units within commonly held open
space, as is more typical with multi-family, or subdivision of property into individual lots. In its application
of this Zone, Riverton City has repeatedly only approved it with a Specific Development designation limiting
development to individual lots. As such, staff is proposing the zone itself be amended to only allow
development utilizing individual lots. The Council clarified the previous proposed draft to also limit the zone
to detached units. This is reflected in the proposed amendment.

The standards for individual dwelling lots are already contained in this section, and so the amendment is
primarily deletion of the sections for attached units and common open space developments.

The Planning Commission recommended approval of this amendment, and staff concurs with that
recommendation.
