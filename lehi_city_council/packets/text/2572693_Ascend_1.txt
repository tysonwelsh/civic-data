  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests approval of the Ascend at Skye Concept Plan located in the Skye Planned Community in the
Mixed Use 2 area.

The concept plan includes 302 apartment units with several amenities. The concept plan meets most of the site
requirements and the applicant will work with staff to ensure they meet all requirements with their future site plan.
The Planning Commission’s review of the concept plan is to approve substitutes for the required amenities.

The Code says that amenities can be combined or upgraded at the discretion of the Planning Commission. DHI
Communities proposes providing the amenities listed below.
    • Clubhouse- totaling 7,500 square feet with a fitness room, yoga and spin room, restrooms and showers,

    •    Pool- 1,800 square feet in size
    •    2 Picnic Areas- at the pool deck, with built-in barbeque area with prep-counter
    •    2 Sports Courts
    •    2 playgrounds and a large lawn for games

The Planning Commission should consider if the updated clubhouse size and amenities provide an adequate substitute
for the picnic areas and playgrounds/ dog parks. And if the larger pool is a substitute for two pools.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

This item is not a public hearing item. It is administrative, meaning if they meet the Code it must be approved. The
Planning Commission is considering whether the proposed amenities are equivalent or an upgrade from those
required by Code.


  POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may approve or deny the proposed amenities and concept plan. Any motion should include
findings. Possible findings include:
     1. The proposed amenities are/ are not of equivalent or greater value than those required by the Code.
     2. The proposed concept is/is not consistent with the Lehi City Development Code.
     3. The proposed concept does/ does not conform to the goals and policies of the General Plan.
     4. Other findings based upon information presented at the public hearing.
