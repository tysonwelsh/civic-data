Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                                 April 13, 2021

The Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting was held on Tuesday, April 13, 2021 in the
Council Chambers of the Washington County Administration Building located at 197 E.
Tabernacle, St. George, UT. Also via electronic means through Zoom. Chairman Brandon
Anderson opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of Allegiance and then explained
meeting protocol.

Commissioners present: Kevin Jones, Todd Jones, Slade Hughes, Frank Leuck, Reed Scow, and
Mark Owens.

County Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Todd Edwards, Engineer; Kurt
Gardner, Building Official; Kim Hafen, County Clerk.

Chairman Anderson recused himself for Items 1 and 2 as he had a potential conflict. Commissioner
Hughes recused himself for Item 1 as he had a potential conflict. Vice Chair Reed Scow took over
the meeting.


1. CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT FOR A NATURAL GAS LINE PROJECT. Review a
conditional use permit request to install a 20” steel natural gas distribution pipeline along SR-18
from the Central Compressor Station to the south end of Veyo. The applicant is Dominion Energy.

Mr. Messel reported the applicant, Dominion Energy, has applied for a conditional use permit for
the purpose of installing 8 miles of a new 20-inch steel natural gas distribution pipeline from the
compressor station in Central, UT to the southern end of Veyo. He explained that public utilities
are a conditional use in all zones within Washington County. He provided a power point
presentation that included the route map of the project point out that the pipeline will be installed
within Washington County Roads along Highway 18. The project will be done in phases beginning
this summer with completion planned for next year.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the requested conditional use permit for the proposed 8 mile 20” steel natural
gas distribution pipeline from the Central Compressor station to the south end of Veyo and would
recommend the planning commission approve the request, based on the following findings and
conditions:

 1. Public utilities are a conditional use in all zones within the county unincorporated area.
 2. The distribution line will provide natural gas services to residents within Washington
    County.
 3. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan.
 4. The proposal meets the applicable Washington County Codes.

There were some technical difficulties with the Zoom program that caused a delay in the meeting.

2. CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT AMENDMENT. Review a request to amend an existing
conditional use permit of an existing gravel extraction and crushing operation on Parcel 7161-NW
and 7161-D-NW in the M-1 (Manufacturing) zone. The property is located at 686 E. Chad Ranch
Rd. in Veyo. The applicant is Feller Enterprises/Allen Feller.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation that included the site overlay and photos. He
explained that in 2018, the applicant, Feller Enterprises/Allen Feller, received approval for a gravel
crushing and rock extraction operation on Parcels 7161-NW and 7161-D-NW, which are located
at approximately 686 West Chad Ranch Road in the M-1 (Manufacture) zone. Recently, some
concerns were brought to staff’s attention regarding the operation. Staff then contacted the
applicant regarding those concerns. The applicant was responsive and indicated he wanted to
comply with the county’s requirements to extend the site outside of the approved 3.79 acre area.
The applicant is now requesting an amendment to his conditional use permit to expand the
operation’s area to the south and east of the property. The crusher will remain where it has been
located in the past. The additional space would be used to stock pile product at this time and
crushed in the future as needed. Mining operations are a conditional use in the M-1
(Manufacturing) zone. Water trucks will be used to control dust along the access road and
sprinklers will be used within the excavation site. The operating hours will remain as before,
Monday through Friday and some Saturday’s if needed, from dawn to dusk. Kim Coburn of the
State Gas, Mining and Mineral office has been working with the applicant and is attending by
Zoom if anyone has questions regarding State compliance. He noted that staff received pictures
and letters of concerns for the operation which he provided to the commission.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the proposed conditional use permit amendment for the work expansion area
and would recommend approval based on the following:

 1.    The existing mining operation was approved in 2018 and has been operating continuously.
 2.    The site is expanding to provide additional area to stock pile product and future extraction.
 3.    The use is consistent with the M-1 (Manufacture) zone.
 4.    Water trucks shall be on site for dust control.

Commissioner Scow asked if there have been many concerns or complaints since the operation
started in 2018. Mr. Messel answered there was one complaint filed in 2019 regarding blasting that
was being done. Since that time, the applicant has used a different blasting company. Until recently,
there has not been any issues.

Allen Feller stated this has been an interesting process and they need to resolve the issue and reclaim
the 50’ buffer area along the fence line. They have been in contact with the Ms. Coburn from the
State Gas, Mining and Mineral office will be meeting with her to determine what needs to be
completed for some reclamation work. He announced that he would like to withdraw the request at
this time in order to complete the necessary requirements in order to move forward and be a good
neighbor. They will come back for approval of the amendment in the future.

A brief question and answer discussion occurred. A motion was not needed.
Chairman Anderson returned to the meeting and Vice Chair Scow yielded the floor to him.

to the RA-5 (Residential Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone. The parcel is generally located
at 500 West 200 North in Veyo. The applicant is Lemuel Leavitt.

Mr. Messel stated this zone change request and the following subdivision request are for the same
property and project. He noted they would discuss the items together and hold the public hearing
together but separate motions for each item will need to be made. He provided a power point
presentation of the project that included a map of the location and the preliminary subdivision plat.
He reported the applicants would like to rezone and subdivide a 53.5 acres portion of the 100 acres
of Parcel 8183-B-NW. The proposed rezone request is from the A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre
minimum lot size) zone to the RA-5 (Residential Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone. The
proposed subdivision will consist of 6 lots approximately 8 acres in size. The proposed roads will
be public right-of-ways that will be built to county standards and then dedicated to the county.
Water to each lot will be provided by Veyo Culinary Water. A will serve letter has been provided
by the applicant. The proposed lots will be on a septic system.

Zone Change Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the zone change request from the A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre minimum lot size)
zone to the RA-5 (Residential Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone and recommends that the
Planning Commission hold the public hearing. Then, if they are comfortable with the rezone
moving forward, may recommend to the County Commission to approve the zone change request,
based on the following findings:

    1. The public hearing and zone change request was properly noticed.
    2. The public hearing was held on April 13, 2021 in the regularly scheduled Planning
       Commission Meeting.
    3. The zone change would allow the applicants to proceed with their proposed low density
       residential subdivision.
    4. The proposal is consistent with the predominate agricultural and lower density residential
       uses in Veyo.

Preliminary Plat Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the preliminary plat application for the proposed 6-lot subdivision and the
Planning Commission should hold the public hearing and may approve the preliminary plat
application may approved the 6-lot subdivision based on the following findings and conditions:

    1. That the zone change is approved to allow for the proposed lots.
    2. The preliminary plat application meets the requirements of Title 11 Subdivision Regulations
       of the Washington County Code.

Mr. Messel asked if there were any questions. There were none.

Todd Edwards County Engineer pointed out that the proposed preliminary plat before the
commission is not the most current. The most updated one was provided.

The commission had questions on the length of the cul-de-sac and if the project would require curb
and gutter. Mr. Edwards stated curb and gutter would not be required but the road will need to be
asphalt.

The septic system was brought into question on what type of system.

John Leavitt, representing the applicant made some clarifications to the proposal. The roads will be
private gated roads but will meet the county requirements and the HOA will maintain the road. He
pointed out and explained the portion of land within the hundred acres and where the subdivision
would lie within that 100 acres.

Mr. Leavitt explained that each lot would have its own individual treatment septic system.

Mr. Messel stated the county has no preference as to the road being private or public but they
strongly discourage gated communities. Given that the road only accesses the properties that are in
subdivision, there are properties surrounding the proposed lots that in the future may develop that
would make it challenging to tie into the road. Mr. Leavitt indicated they would like to keep them
private. A discussion and clarification regarding the roads occurred.

Additional discussion regarding minor technicalities of the plat took place. Mr. Edwards reported
he had no issues with the preliminary plat.

Chairman Anderson declared the public hearing open for both the zone change and
subdivision request.

There was no one who wished to comment on either item.

Chairman Anderson declared the public hearing closed for both the zone change and
subdivision request.
