2.1 Presentation of Mayor Jenny Wilson’s Proposed 2025 Budget 24-2244
Attachments: Staff Report
Presenters: SLCo Mayor Jenny Wilson. Darrin Casper, SLCo Deputy Mayor of 
Finance & Administration CFO.
(Approx. 10:00AM, 90 Min.)
Informational
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  presented the 2025 budget, addressing rising costs 
and limited tax revenue growth. Despite these challenges, the budget 
maintains a balanced approach, ensuring the County ’s AAA bond rating . 
Key ongoing initiatives include support for the Human Services, 
Homelessness, and Criminal Justice Action Plan, funding for drug 
enforcement administration officers, the My County Rec Pass for children, 
and the Jail Resource and Reentry Program. The budget also addresses 
substance use disorders amid the fentanyl crisis, with funding for the Metro 
Narcotics Task Force. 
Important initiatives include the removal of late fees for children and teens at 
the County libraries, which improves access to resources, and the Jail 
Resource and Re -entry Program, which has assisted nearly 17,000 
individuals transitioning back to the community. To enhance jail staff safety, 
the budget recommends $ 225,000 for bulletproof and stab vests. Affordable 
housing remains a priority, with a goal of adding over 1,000 units in five 
years and a $711,000 investment in the Housing Trust Fund.
The Larry H. & Gail Miller Family Foundation donated $ 25 million for the 
new performing arts center in South Jordan. Additionally, the budget 
allocates $ 100 million for deferred maintenance at the Salt Lake County jail . 
The proposed Public Safety Bond seeks $ 507 million to finance an expanded 
County jail and a new Justice and Accountability Center, with an estimated 
monthly property tax impact of $ 4.91 per resident. Employee compensation 
is also addressed, with recommendations for a 3.5 percent salary increase for 
all employees and 4.75 percent for sworn officers, while managing healthcare 
costs.
