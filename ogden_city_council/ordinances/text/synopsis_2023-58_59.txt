                                              AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                        SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE



         I, Jason Gould, the duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber County, State
of Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its November 7, 2023, meeting adopted the following:

ORDINANCE NO. 2023-58: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY AMENDING THE BUDGET FOR THE FISCAL
YEAR JULY 1, 2023 TO JUNE 30, 2024 BY DECREASING THE ANTICIPATED REVENUES FOR A GROSS
DECREASE OF $34,450 FROM SOURCES AS DETAILED IN THE BODY OF THIS ORDINANCE; AND
DECREASING THE APPROPRIATIONS FOR A GROSS DECREASE OF $34,450 AS DETAILED IN THE BODY
OF THIS ORDINANCE; AND PROVIDING THAT THIS ORDINANCE SHALL BECOME EFFECTIVE
IMMEDIATELY UPON POSTING AFTER FINAL PASSAGE.

ORDINANCE NO. 2023-59: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE OGDEN MUNICIPAL
CODE BY AMENDING SUBSECTION 15-38-2.B; BY AMENDING SECTION 15-38-3, BY AMENDING
SUBSECTION 15-38-4.B; BY AMENDING SECTION 15-38-5 TO REVISE C-2 AND CP-2 ZONING PROVISIONS
FOR HIGH IMPACT USES ADJACENT TO RESIDENTIAL USES; AND BY PROVIDING THAT THIS ORDINANCE
SHALL BECOME EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY UPON POSTING AFTER FINAL PASSAGE.

         Copies of all ordinances in their entirety are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office located
on the second floor of the Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard #210 during regular business hours.

The undersigned, duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, does hereby certify that the above notice was posted in
accordance with Utah State law on this 17th day of November 2023.


                                                                         JASON GOULD
