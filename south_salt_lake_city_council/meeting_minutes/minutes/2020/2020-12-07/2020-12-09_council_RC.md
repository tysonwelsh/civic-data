NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of
          upcoming events, meetings, activities, etc.

       2. CITIZEN’S COMMENTS. None.

       3. MAYOR’S COMMENTS. Mayor Wood thanked the Council and City
          employees for the services they provided amidst a pandemic this year. There
          was no decrease in services this year all while running a semi-virtual City. She
          highlighted some of the things employees have done during the year and
          wished everyone a happy and safe holiday season.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS. City Attorney, Hannah Vickery, wished
          everyone Happy Holidays.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS. Council Member Siwik wished everyone
          Happy Holidays and hopes 2021 will be better than 2020. She thanked
          Council Chair Bynum for all of her efforts and pointed out how fair she is and
          appreciates all she does.

          City Council deWolfe thanked Council Chair Bynum and City Council Mila
          for being the chair of the Council and RDA. They both have worked hard and
          helped everything run smoothly. He thanked Mayor Wood for her comments
          and all City staff for everything they have done. He is a part of the committee
          for the General Plan and he is looking forward to that.

          Council Member Mila thanked everyone for their hard work this year and
          wished everyone Happy Holidays.

          Council Member Pinkney said this was her first year on the Council and she
          has learned a lot and continues to learn. She thanked everyone for their
          patience and for their help. She is excited for 2021 and Happy Holidays.

          Council Member Thomas echoed all the thoughts and feelings expressed by
          the Council. She thanked Public Safety for all their hard work. She thanked
          City staff and Council Chair Bynum as well.

          Council Chair Bynum said she feels everyone has worked well together this
          year.

       6. INFORMATION – COVID-19 Update. Fire Chief, Terry Addison, gave the
          Council a brief update on the COVID-19 pandemic.

NEW BUSINESS

   1. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Repealing Chapter 4 of
      the Downtown South Salt Lake City Zoning Ordinance and Design
      Standards; Repealing Chapter 4 of the East Streetcar Neighborhood form
      based code; Amending Chapter 17.03 of the South Salt Lake City Code to
      Integrate the Land Use matrices from Chapter 4 of both the Downtown
      South Salt Lake City Zoning Ordinance and the East Streetcar
      Neighborhood form based code; Amending Chapters 17.04 permitted use
      review and 17.05 Conditional Use Review of the South Salt Lake City Land
      Use Development Regulation; all as a part of a Comprehensive effort to
      update and consolidate South Salt Lake City Land Use Regulations.
      Community Development Director, Alex White, gave a brief overview of this
      Ordinance. A copy is attached and incorporated by this reference. The City has
      three land use matrices in code and this Ordinance will simplify those by
      combining them into one section of the code, Chapter 17.

       Council Member Siwik made a motion to move this item to Unfinished Business
       on the January 13, 2021 Regular meeting
