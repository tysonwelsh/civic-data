                           EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP ORDINANCE


         Ordinance No. 20-11-01                                                             Date: November 17, 2020


                 AN ORDINANCE REPEALING CHAPTER 3.52, ENTITLED "SUBDIVISION AND
                 ZONING FEES," AND REPLACING IT WITH A NEW CHAPTER 3.52 THAT
                 REFERENCES THE PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT SERVICES FEE
                 SCHEDULE APPROVED BY THE TOWNSHIP COUNCIL PURSUANT TO
                 TOWNSHIP ORDINANCE 3.42.040. THE ORDINANCE ALSO AMENDS
                 SECTIONS 3.48.020, 3.48.025, AND 3.48.030 OF THE ENGINEERING FEES
                 CHAPTER TO LIKEWISE INCORPORATE PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
                 SERVICES' FEE SCHEDULE.

                 The Metro Township Council of Emigration Canyon Metro Township, State of Utah, ordains as
                 follows:

                 SECTION I: The amendments made herein are designated by underlining the new substituted

         language. The language being deleted is designated by brackets and interlineations.

                 SECTION II: Chapter 3.52 of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Metro Township Code

         of Ordinances entitled "Subdivision and Zoning Fees," is hereby repealed and replaced, as follows:

         Chapter 3.52 - Subdivision and Zoning Fees

         3.52.010 - Fees as set forth in fee schedule.

                 Subdivision and zoning fees shall be established in accordance with Section 3.42.040 of these

         ordinances. These fees shall be set out in the Planning and Development Services Fee Schedule, which

         shall be approved by the Metro Township Council and shall be on file with the Greater Salt Lake

         Municipal Service District.

                 SECTION III: Section 3.48.020 is hereby amended as follows:

         3.48.020-Plat filing and engineering checking fees.

       Any and all persons filing plats with the County Recorder’s Office shall first have paid all fees required

       in Chapter 3.52. In addition, persons filing plats shall pay to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services

       District the Plat Filing and Engineering C hecking Fee set out in the Planning and Development Services Fee

       Schedule, which shall be on file with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District.

                 SECTION IV: Section 3.48.025 is hereby amended as follows:

        3.48.025-Engineering checking fee for subdivisions where no plat must be recorded.

                 Any person proposing to subdivide a parcel of land where no plat must be recorded (a nonregular

        subdivision) shall pay to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District the Engineering

        Checking Fee for Subdivision with no plat set out in the Planning and Development Services Fee,

         Schedule. which shall be approved by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council and shall be on

         file with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District.

         SECTION V: Section 3.48.030 is hereby amended as follows:

          3.48.030-Engineering checking fees for non-subdivision development.

                 Any person proposing to develop a parcel of land, either as a permitted use or conditional use,

         but not a Planned Unit Development or a subdivision, shall pay to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services

         District the Engineering Checking Fee for non-subdivision development set out in the Planning and

         Development Services Fee Schedule, which shall be on file with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
         District

                 SECTION VI: This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at

         least one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Salt Lake County,

         and if not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately upon its first

         publication.

         IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council has approved, passed and

         adopted this ordinance this 17th day of November 2020.

                                                         SUMMARY OF

          EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP ORDINANCENO.20-11-01

          On the 17TH day of November, 2020, the Township Council of Emigration Canyon Metro Township
          adopted Ordinance No. 20-11-01 repealing Chapter 3.52, entitled "Subdivision and Zoning Fees," and
          replacing it with a new Chapter 3.52 that incorporates the Planning and Development Services Fee
          Schedule approved by the County Council pursuant to County Ordinance 3.42.040. The ordinance
          also amends Sections 3.48.020, 3.48.025, and 3.48.030 of the Engineering Fees Chapter to likewise
          incorporate Planning and Development Services' Fee Schedule.
