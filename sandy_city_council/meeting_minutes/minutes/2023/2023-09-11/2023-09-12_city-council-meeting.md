1.         23-363                 General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:30 PM)

                                  Council Chair D'Sousa invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment

                                  Mr. Joel Frost provided estimates on homelessness. He is supportive of the proposed 
                                  transitional shelter and proud of the city for considering the shelter. He thanked the 
                                  Council for their thoughtful leadership on this issue. Compassion and service is a great 
                                  direction to move the city in and the shelter serves a positive and unifying opportunity for 
                                  the city.

                                  Father Steven Tilley, Pastor of St John the Baptist Church, spoke in support of the 
                                  transitional shelter. His parish started a hygiene collection program to provide these 
                                  items to the homeless. He is proud of Sandy City for considering the proposal and feels 
                                  this is a wonderful program and an awesome opportunity to show mercy and love to the 
                                  least and most vulnerable among us.

                                  State Representative Steve Eliason, District 43, has worked on homelessness issues for 
                                  many years. He has volunteered with the Road Home Shelter. One city cannot do it all. 
                                  This is an opportunity for Sandy City to play an important role in taking care of our fellow 
                                  citizens. Approval of the shelter would also eliminate the current mitigation funds the city 
                                  pays in annually.

                                  Ms. Terry Carroll agrees with earlier comments and is supportive of the shelter. She 
                                  shared a story about her daughter who was homeless for a time. We need to do this. 

                                  Ms. Wendy Garvin is a resident of Riverton and with Unsheltered Utah. She is supportive

3.         CA07282023 Amendment to Title 21, of the Land Development Code to the definition of 
           -0006578   a "Transitional Housing Facility"
           (CC-WS)
                   Attachments:   Staff Report and Exhibits
                                  Presentation Slides
                                  SLC Winter Overflow Review 2021-2022 (1)
                                  Calls for Service 1659 West NorthTemple
                                  Fire Department MVP Memo

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, reviewed the proposed amendments to Title 
                                  21, Land Development Code on behalf of the applicant, Laurie Hopkins of Shelter the 
                                  Homeless. The purpose of the code amendment is to clarify that a "Transitional Housing 
                                  Facility" provides non-congregate interim housing for no more than 24 months on a 
                                  referral (non-walkup) basis only, with on-site supportive services to persons experiencing 
                                  homelessness. Ms. Anderson reviewed the details of the proposed amendments. The 
                                  Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation to the City Council.

                                  Council Member Marci Houseman arrived to the Council Meeting at 8:38 pm.

                                  Following Ms. Anderson's presentation, Council engaged in a lengthy discussion. Council 
                                  questions to staff and the applicant followed.

                                  Mr. Wayne Niederhauser, the Homeless Coordinator for the State of Utah responded to 
                                  questions. He spoke about the need for this type of shelter and the shelter mitigation fund 
                                  to help offset any costs of the city. He spoke about the formula used to calculate the 
                                  funds received from the mitigation fund.

                                  Fire Chief Bassett provided information on the impact this shelter may have on the city's 
                                  fire department resources. At this point we dont know what the exact impact would be 
                                  but he did anticipate there would be a need for emergent care since all in the facility 
                                  would be considered medically vulnerable.

                                  Shane Pace, CAO, spoke about the timeline and important dates associated with getting 
                                  the facility up and running by this winter. 

                                  Community Development and Legal staff responded to Council questions.

                                  Public comment period opened:
                                  Ms. Melinda Cutler expressed support of the shelter. Sandy is a community that takes 
                                  care of its own. It is a privilege to take care of those in the community that need help. 

                                  Ms. Sydney Porter Williams echo'd the earlier comments. She has worked in shelters 
                                  and is supportive of the proposed MVP shelter. All deserve dignity. 

                                  Mr. Jeff Porter stated how wonderful it was to watch the process and discussion this 
                                  evening. His family volunteers to help the homeless in shelters around the valley. It is not 
                                  an easy fix but he was proud of the city for considering this proposal. 

                                  Several Council Members felt more time was needed to gather information and educate 
                                  the community. Many expressed a need for more time.

5.         23-359                 First Reading: Council Member Zach Robinson presenting a proposal 
                                  regarding short term rentals
                   Attachments:   Short Term Rental Memo

                                  Council Member Zach Robinson asked for a Council discussion and feedback regarding 
                                  the challenges and issues related to the short term rentals in Sandy City. He spoke 
                                  about providing the code enforcers with the tools they need to enforce the laws related to 
                                  short term rentals. He is not trying to eliminate short term rentals in the city, but he 
                                  would like for the city to be more proactive in the enforcement of the city ordinances. He 
                                  would like to continue to work with staff on developing better tools for code enforcement 
                                  and he asked the Council for feedback and direction. Council expressed support for 
                                  Council Member Robinson to work with staff to revise and strengthen the code to allow for 
                                  better enforcement for short term rentals.
