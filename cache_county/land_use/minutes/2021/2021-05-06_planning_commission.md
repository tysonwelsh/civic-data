#7 Discussion: Amendments to Title 17 regarding a new use type for Alcohol Production (i.e, 
winery) 
Harrild informed the Commission that the County Council recently amended the county code to allow 
alcohol production in the unincorporated zone and the timeline for draft ordinance language. 
Commissioners and Staff discussed ideas for language for the ordinance regarding limiting the size of 
the operation and possible importing of product. Staff will research the questions that have been raised 
and bring the answers back to the Commission at June’s meeting. 
Harrild reviewed some draft language that has been discussed with the County Council.  
 
6 May 2021                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 4 of 4 
 
Keith Meikle commented about wine being a blended product, federal and state regulations, and 
restrictions based on the number of cases per year. 
Spackman asked about a small scale limitations. 
Mr. Meikle commented on limiting by cases produced. 
Lee asked about using percentages. 
Mr. Meikle commented that last year due to a hail storm the quality of the grapes was incredibly poor.  
Christensen asked about state regulations regarding local grown products. 
Mr. Meikle commented that in order to meet the regulations he would have to change his label and that 
the federal government regulates that, not the state. State code also requires local restaurants to buy the 
product from the winery’s building and pick it up; it cannot be shipped. 
Commissioners and Staff discussed the next steps for a draft ordinance.
