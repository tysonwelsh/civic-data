                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO 21-04

                 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A
            PIONEERING/REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENT WITH IHC HEALTH
                 SERVICES, INC AND RIVERTON BUSINESS PARK, LLC

       WHEREAS, HIC Health Services, Inc and Riverton Business Park, LLC are partners in
the construction of the Riverton Business Park located at approximately 12650 S 4150 W; and

        WHEREAS, said project will provide public infrastructure including roads, storm drains,
water, and pressureized irrigation as part of the development; and

       WHEREAS, it is plausible that the public infrastructure provided by the developers
could benfit adjacent property owners as they develop their land; and

       WHEREAS, the developers of Riverton Business Park desire to be reimburst in part by
adjacent property owners that may wish to take advantage of the mentioned infrastructure; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City oversees and regulates development of property; and

       WHEREAS, the developers of Riverton Business Park has provided the City a
reimbursement agreement and requested the City collect a proportonate share of costs from
developers that take advantage of the constructed infrastructure to reimburse the developers of
Riverton Business Park; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City is willing to require payment from future developers that take
advantage of said infrastructure and reimburse the developers of Riverton Business Park the
collected funds; and

       WHEREAS, this reimbursement has no psyical or binding impact on the City; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that execution of the
enclosed Reimbursement Agreement will enable the City to aid the developers of Riverton
Business Park in obtaining partial reimbursement for public infrastructure they constructed as
part of the development;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1.     The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to to execute the enclosed
       Reimbursemnet Agreement with IHC Health Services, Inc and Riverton Business Park,
       LLC upon verification that the agreement has been approved as to legal form by the City
       Attorney.
2.    This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 19th day of
January 2021 by the following vote:
