                          Nephi City Council Work Session Minutes
                                     November 8, 2022
                                         7:00 p.m.
                                        21 E. 100 N.
                                      Nephi, UT 84648


1. City Administrator Seth Atkinsonreviewed an agreement with the developer on a piece of
   property located at 400 East 300 North. The council directed staff to bring the agreement
   back to the next regular council meeting for approval.

2. The council discussed The Hive fee schedule. Recreation Director Craig Oswal
   presented a draft of a fee schedule that he and City Attorney Kasey Wright had drafted
   The council wanted to change the family pass amount from $80/month to $40/month and
   eliminate the annual family pass.

3. Mayor Pro Tempore Memmott advised the council to look at the financial reports.

4. Mr. Atkinson asked the council how they felt about only scheduling the fourth Tuesday
   work session as needed. The council agreed with the idea.

5. Mr. Atkinson said that he was very pleased with the Hive grand opening.

6. Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the natural gas prices and reported that he
   locked in a three year natural gas rate of $5.40 which is up from where we were
   historically at $2.63. He had estimated that during the winter months the average
   natural gas bill would increase between $40 to $50 dollars per month.

7.    Mr. Atkinson discussed with the council the personnel policy that addressed outside
     employment. The policy states that outside employment must be approved by the city
     council. He stated that common practice had been that the city administrator had been
     approving outside employment. He stated that he personally has been offered to be a
     consultant for the Six County Association of Governments for smaller cities that do not
     have a city administrator. Councilor Memmott expressed his concern about Mr. Atkinson
     being overloaded currently and taking on more responsibilities that might affect his job
     with Nephi City. Councilor Worwood stated that he would want more information about
     how much time Mr. Atkinson would be spending consulting. Councilor Ostler said that if
     Mr. Atkinson could handle it, he should go for it.

8. Mr. Atkinson discussed with the council about offering cash in lieu as an option to opt out
   of health insurance coverage. The council directed staff to draft a policy and bring it
   back to the council for approval.

9. City Recorder Lisa Brough informed Councilor Parady and Councilor Callaway about the
   required online training for municipal officials on the State Auditor website. They said
   they would complete the training and email Ms. Brough their certificates.
   10. Councilor Callaway reported on the November JRDA meeting and on the Illuminate the
       Night event progress.

   11. Councilor Parady reported on theRural Opportunities Community Grant committee
       meeting. .He stated that they didn’t really receive any viable applications. The
       committee decided to advertise more about the grant opportunity and to hopefully get
       more applications.

   12. Councilor Parady moved adjourn the meeting. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
       The meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m
