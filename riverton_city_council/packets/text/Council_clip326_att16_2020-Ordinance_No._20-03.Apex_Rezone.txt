 AN ORDINANCE REZONING PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2435 WEST 12600 SOUTH
    FROM RR-22 (RESIDENTIAL HALF ACRE LOTS) TO C-PO (COMMERCIAL
   PROFESSIONAL OFFICE). ALICIA DAVIS, BRANDON JOHNSON AND RYAN
                BRINTON REPRESENTING APEX INSURANCE

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and

     WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said zoning
amendment; and

Professional Office) approximately .5 acres located 2435 West 12600 South as described herein.

as follows:

                  following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”
