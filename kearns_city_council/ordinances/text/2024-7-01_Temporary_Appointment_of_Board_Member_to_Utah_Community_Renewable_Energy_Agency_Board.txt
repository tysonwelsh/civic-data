KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-7-01 DATE: July 8, 2024
A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL APPROVING A TEMPORARY
APPOINTMENT OF BOARD MEMBER TO UTAH COMMUNITY RENEWABLE
ENERGY AGENCY BOARD

WHEREAS, the City of Kearns (“Kearns”) is a municipality and member of the Utah
Community Renewable Energy Agency (“UCREA”); and

WHEREAS, Kearns has one elected official from the Kearns City Council appointed to
represent Kearns on the UCREA Board; and

WHEREAS, the current Kearns UCREA Board member is Patrick Schaeffer; and

WHEREAS, Patrick Schaeffer is taking an extended leave of absence effective
immediately; and

WHEREAS, Kearns City Council seeks to appoint Chrystal Butterfield as a temporary
UCREA Board Member in Patrick Schaeffer’s place until such time as he returns from his leave
of absence or his term ends, whichever is sooner.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that by the Kearns City Council that effective
immediately:

1. Chrystal Butterfield will act as a temporary replacement for Patrick Schaeffer
on the UCREA Board.

2. The replacement will take effect immediately after the approval and adoption
of this Resolution.

3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately after adoption.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 8th day of July, 2024.

KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
Kelly . Mayor
