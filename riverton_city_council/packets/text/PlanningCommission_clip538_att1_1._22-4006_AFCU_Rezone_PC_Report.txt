This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on
reasonably debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City
Council which has final decision authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motions
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-22-4007 Rezone of
7.93-acres of land located at 12653 South 3600 West, thus changing the Zoning to C-G
(Gateway Commercial).

I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-22-4007 Rezone of
7.93-acres of land located at 12653 South 3600 West, thus keeping the Zoning as C-PO
(Professional Office Commercial).


Background

This is a public hearing for an application to Rezone 7.93-acres of land located at 12653 South
3600 West. The property is the southeast corner lot and has frontage on 12600 South and
3600 West. The rezone includes all, or portions of, parcels 27-32-200-072, as shown on the
attached exhibit.

The property is rectangular and roughly 466-ft wide and 750-ft deep. The applicant proposes to
put an America First Credit Union branch on the northwest corner, and the rest of the property
will be left undeveloped to be subdivided and sold at a future date.

The property is currently zoned C-PO, which is Professional Office Commercial. Several
surrounding properties are zoned RR-22. There is also a hospital, church, and school in the
near vicinity.

The current General Plan Designation for the property and area is “Professional Office
Commercial” and Business Park.
