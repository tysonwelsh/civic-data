                                BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF UTAH COUNTY, UTAH

                                                   100 East Center Street, Provo, Utah
                                                     August 05, 2020 - 03:00 P.M.

                      *Board members may participate electronically at will, with the anchor location as stated above.*

                  To minimize the potential spread of COVID-19 and consistent with provisions of the Utah Public and Open
             Meetings Act, Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207, the Board of County Commissioners of Utah County has determined by
                resolution that County Commission meetings will be temporarily conducted electronically; that members of the
                 public are required to participate in Commission meetings electronically and may offer citizen public input at
             appropriate times through approved electronic means as explained below; and that the Board may temporarily waive
              the OPMA requirement that physical space and facilities be provided for members of the public to attend the open
                                                  portions of an electronic meeting of the Board.

              Members of the public are invited to view and participate in the electronic Commission meetings by viewing live
                               streams of the meeting on the Utah County Government YouTube Channel.
                                 https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCkRYDnXVDLGv9792bhWn9Wg

               If a member of the public would like to provide comment electronically during a public comment portion of the
                    meeting or as otherwise allowed by the Board, then the public may participate electronically by calling
               (385) 323-8760. Comments will be limited to three (3) minutes per individual unless otherwise approved by the
                     Board. Please state your name at the beginning of the comment. Please no foul or abusive language.

              If a member of the public is unable to provide comment electronically then they may contact the commission clerk
                        to submit written comments for the record by contacting commissionclerk@utahcounty.gov
                                                          or by calling 801-851-8111.

            2. DISCUSS LIVE MARINA CAMERA FEEDS AT THE MARINAS AROUND UTAH LAKE PRESENTED
            BY ERIC ELLIS, UTAH LAKE COMMISSION
            - Richard Nielson, Public Works Director -

            RICHARD NIELSON, PUBLIC WORKS – UTAH LAKE COMMISSION LOOKING AT PARTNERING
            UP WITH MARINAS TO HAVE CAMERAS AROUND THE LAKE TO SHOW WEATHER
            CONDITIONS, BUSINESS OF THE RAMPS, ETC.

            ERIC ELLIS, UTAH LAKE COMMISSION – LOOKING AT HAVING LIVE CAMERA FEEDS FOR THE
            PUBLIC’S USE, MARINA USE AS WELL AS THE UTAH LAKE COMMISSION. CAN TRACK
            WEATHER AND RAMPS AS WELL AS FOR SECURITY. CAMERA’S CAN BE SET UP IN A WAY
            THAT ONLY ONE LOCATION NEEDS INTERNET AND THEN THE REST FEED ALL INTO THE ONE
            COLLECTION UNIT LOCATED LIKELY AT THE AMERICAN FORK BOAT HARBOR WHERE
            THERE IS ALREADY INTERNET AVAILABLE.

            WOULD BE SEEKING FUNDS THROUGH THE MOTORBOAT ACCESS GRANT. HAVE SPOKEN
            WITH THEM AND THEY ARE IN SUPPORT. DEADLINE IS IN OCTOBER 2020.

            ADDITIONAL BENEFIT IS TO HAVE TRAFFIC CAMERAS AT EACH OF THE LOCATIONS TO GET
            A FEEL OF VISITATION AT LAKES THROUGHOUT PUBLIC MARINAS.

            SMALL POWER BILL WOULD BE INVOLVED (APPROX. $100/YEAR) AND AN OCCASIONAL
            CLEANING OF THE CAMERA LENSES.

            LINCOLN BEACH IS THE ONLY LOCATION THAT IS A COUNTY OWNED FACILITY.

            4. DISCUSS THE UTAH COUNTY CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM
            - Danene Jackson, Clerk/Auditor - Finance -

            PATRICK WAWRO, DIRECTOR OF INFORMATION SYSTEMS/MEMBER OF THE
            CAPITAL PROJECTS PLANNING COMMITTEE - COMMITTEE BEGAN IN
            DECEMBER WITH MEMBERS: PATRICK WAWRO, RUDY LIVINGSTON (BUDGET
            MANAGER), DANENE JACKSON (FINANCE MANAGER) AND RICHARD NIELSON
            (PUBLIC WORKS DIRECTOR). DANENE HAS SINCE STEPPED DOWN FROM THE
            COMMITTEE.

            PURPOSE OF THE COMMITTEE IS TO GATHER INFORMATION ON ALL THE
            POSSIBLE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROJECTS TO BE ADDRESSED OVER THE
            NEXT DECADE.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – NOTED THAT THIS, ALONG WITH A FEW OTHER
            POLICIES, CAME FROM THE COUNTY’S OUTSIDE FINANCIAL ADVISOR. CAPITAL
            IMPROVEMENT PROJECTS, IF NEGLECTED, CAN BECOME A LIABILITY AS REAL
            AS DEBT BECAUSE THEY HAVE TO BE FUNDED APPROPRIATELY.

            PATRICK WAWRO – THE COMMITTEE BEGAN MEETING ACTIVELY LAST
            DECEMBER.
              • INVITED EVERY DEPARTMENT TO MEET WITH THE COMMITTEE AND
                 DISCUSSED WHAT PROJECTS THEY SAW AS BEING NEGLECTED.
              • DEVELOPED A FORMAL APPLICATION PROCESS/PDF FORM WHERE THESE
                 PROJECTS COULD BE FORMALLY DESCRIBED, JUSTIFIED, AND IDENTIFY
                 OTHER FUNDING SOURCES, TIMELINES. ETC.
              • COMPILED THE INFORMATION INTO A CENTRAL DATABASE. THESE
                 PROJECTS HAVE A $100,000 MINIMUM TO QUALIFY AS A CAPITAL
                 PROJECT.

            RUDY LIVINGSTON, BUDGET MANAGER – THIS PLAN ALLOWS US TO PLAN FOR
            THE FUTURE. CURRENTLY WORKING ON GETTING A 10-YEAR SPENDING PLAN.

            THE SCHEDULE THAT HAS BEEN PUT TOGETHER DOES NOT INCLUDE ROADS
            AND STREETS. THESE PROJECTS HAVE A SEPARATE FUNDING SOURCE THAT IS
            SPECIFIC TO ROADS AND STREETS. PLUS, WITH ALL THE OTHER PROJECTS
            THAT HAVE COME IN, HAVE NOT HAD TIME TO COMPILE ALL THE
            INFORMATION. THIS INFORMATION WILL BE AVAILABLE WITHIN THE NEXT
            TWO WEEKS AND WILL BE INCLUDED IN THE FINAL CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT
            PLAN.

            VEHICLES/MOTOR POOL – PUTTING TOGETHER DIFFERENT OPTIONS AND
            POTENTIAL REVISIONS WHICH WILL ALSO BE INCLUDED IN THE FINAL PLAN.

            DISCUSSED THE DIFFERENT FUNDS AND IDENTIFICATION.
