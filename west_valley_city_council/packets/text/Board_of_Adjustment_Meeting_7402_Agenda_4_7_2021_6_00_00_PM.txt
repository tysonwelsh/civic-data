 VARIANCES
                              RICHARD HUNT, representing Hunt Electric is requesting two variances from the West
                              Valley City Code. The first variance is regarding property at 1935 West Alexander Street. For
                              this variance, the applicant is seeking relief from Section 7-9-112(1) which states that the
                              required landscaping for parking lots with 5 or more vehicles be 20 feet in depth from property
                              line. Mr. Hunt is requesting a reduction from 20 feet to 10 feet. The second variance is
                              regarding property at 1940 West Alexander Street. For this variance, the applicant is seeking
                              relief from Section 7-7-119(3) which requires that all outside storage areas be screened from
                              public view. (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311)
