MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper, AICP
Date: 2026-02-26
Re: Public Hearing: City Initiated Highland Boundary Adjustment Land Use Map and
Zoning Map Amendment Requests (Legislative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Land Use Map Amendment and Zoning Map
Amendments for approximately 0.25 acres located on the southeastern boundary of the
City at approximately 16199 S. 3300 E. The property is currently zoned A5 (Agricultural)
and is located in the Hillside Sensitive Overlay Zone.

Draper City and Highland City are mutually adjusting their municipal boundary lines in
the subject area to incorporate a small parcel of land that was purchased by Highland
City for the creation of a trail into Highland City, and to include a nearby sliver of land
located in Highland City into Draper City. It is anticipated that the area being annexed
into Draper City will be designated as the Residential Hillside Low Density Land Use, the
A5 zone and the Hillside Sensitive Overlay zone consistent with the adjacent property in
the city. Draper City is seeking to amend the Draper City land use and zoning maps to
remove the zoning and land use designations from the property that will become part of
Highland City.

Findings for Approval:
  1. The proposed amendments are consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the
     city's general plan.
  2. The proposed amendments are harmonious with the overall character of existing
     development in the vicinity of the subject property.
  3. The proposed amendments will not affect the standards of any applicable overlay
     zone.
  4. The proposed amendments will not adversely affect adjacent property.
  5. There will be adequate facilities and services to serve the subject property within
     Highland City.
  6. The proposed Land Use Map amendments comply with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
