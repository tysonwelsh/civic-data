Request
 The applicant, Jorge Saavedra, with permission from
 the property owner, Kenneth Beel, is requesting
 approval of a conditional use permit for non-
 mechanical automotive service use for a property
 located at 720 E. 10600 S. The applicant is proposing
 to have a business doing window tinting for
 automotive, residential, and commercial. (Exhibit A)
 and application materials (Exhibit B)

Background
 The subject property is approximately 15,000 square
 feet and is located in the Neighborhood Commerical
 (CN) zone. The building was built in 1979 and is
 approximately 7,000 square feet with six suites. There
 are five businesses operating in the building that
 include a range of commercial sales and services
 businesses. Properties to the north, south, east, and
 west are also zoned CN. There is a gas station to the
 west, a restaurant to the southwest, and vacant
 properties to the south and east.

Public Notice and Outreach
 This item has been noticed to property owners within 500 feet of the subject area by
 mail. A neighborhood meeting was not held as this is a similar use to an existing
 business. Staff have not received any emails or phone calls regarding the project.

Analysis
 The applicant is proposing a window tinting business in the CN zone. This use is
 classified as non-mechanical automotive service and repair in Sec. 21-8-2(b) of the
 Sandy City Land Development Code and requires a conditional use permit in the CN
 zone. The applicant is proposing to do window tinting for automotive, residential,
 and commerical windows. The business has an attached garage next to the customer
 and office area and will be used to do window tinting for vehicles. The proposed
 business hours are 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday through Saturday. The window
 tinting services will typically be done through scheduled appointments. Appointments
 are completed in around an hour. The garage can hold approximately three
 cars. The proposed business does not have any business vehicles parked
 onsite.

 Parking
 There are currently four other businesses within the building. This site was
 developed in 1979, however staff do not have record of the site plan
 approval. There are 21 parking spaces around the building with 13 along the
 front of the building and eight on the south side of the building. The parking
 along the south side of the property encroaches into the adjacent property
 but the parcels are under the same ownership. The owner of the property
 does not have a parking limitation agreement for any of the tenants, and the
 parking spaces are shared between the businesses.

 Parking space requirements are in Sec 21-24-8(b) of the Sandy City Land
 Development Code. The building is approximately 7,000 square feet with a
 variety of commercial and retail businesses. Additionally, there is a garage
 included as part of the applicants’ proposed business, though the garage does not contribute to the parking requirement.
 As a result, the parking calculation is based on approximately 6,000 square feet. The requirement for both retail sales and
 services and automotive service and repair is five parking spaces per 1,000 square feet. This would require 30 parking
 spaces for the building. The adjacent parcel to the south could be striped with additional parking to meet this requirement.
 The owner would need to record an off-site parking agreement, found in Sec. 21-24-3(a)(1), since the parking spaces are
 on another parcel, or seek a property line adjustment to consolidate the parcels. .

  Conditional Use Standards
  The City may impose conditions on a conditional use based on certain
  standards of review found in section 21-33-04 of Sandy City Land
  Development Code. The following items from that list are applicable to this
  particular request and merit discussion or additional consideration by the
  Planning Commission (staff analysis is found below in italics):

Sec. 21-33-4. Conditions.
     In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City
may impose conditions that address:
     (1)    Size, configuration and location of the site and the proposed site plan
            layout.
     No site improvements are being proposed. There is a garage attached to the business.
     (5)    Site circulation patterns for vehicular, pedestrian and other traffic.
     There are 21 parking spaces on site and 30 are required.
     (12) The regulation of operating hours for activities affecting normal schedules
          and functions.
     The applicant is proposing to operate Monday through Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
     (14) Measures to assure compliance with all conditions and requirements of approval, including, but not limited to, bonds,
          letters of credit, improvement agreements, agreements to conditions, road maintenance funds, and restrictive
          covenants.
      To be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.
     (15) Such other conditions determined reasonable and necessary by the City to allow the operation of the proposed
          conditional use, at the proposed location in compliance with the requirements of this title.
  That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.
  Staff Concerns
  Staff is concerned that the building does not meet parking requirements. There are 21 parking spaces and 30 is what
  would be required for the uses in this building.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for non-mechanical automotive
  service use and as described in the staff report for the property located at 720 E. 10600 S. based on the following findings
  and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. The proposed use meets the intent of the CN zone.

    2. The current 21 parking spaces available onsite do not meet the parking requirements.

Conditions:
   1. On-site parking be used only by employees and customers with any overnight storage of a vehicle will take place
       inside the proposed building

    2. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.
