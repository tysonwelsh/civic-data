3) MSD Planning staff will continue the Title 19 Code Update Project, including a review of the
Foothill and Canyons Overlay Zone as well as Site Development Standards. Planner(s): Matt
Starley and Brian Tucker (Discussion)
1. Distinguishing between “Private Access Roads” and “Driveways”
Mr. Hessell reviewed an email regarding developers calling private access roads, driveways
and making sure emergency accessible. Emergency vehicles are okay with pulling in and
backing out up to 150 feet. The driveway serves only one home and private access roads
servicing more than one home, or two single family detached dwelling units and longer than
150 feet would be a shared driveway. Confirm expected minimum width per fire department
requirements. Clarification on which of the WUI Codes is followed. Mr. Starley will reach
out to the UFA for clarification. Fire department to weigh in on path forward for future
developments. Revisit Title 14.
2. The bonding process.
Mr. Rudowski advised that there was a proposal for bonding associated with grading
permits. Issues in the canyon and not making good faith efforts as agreed. Change made
when grading permit is only issued when a retaining wall permit is issued. Grading permits
are dependent on the retaining wall for a stabilized site. The recommendation by MSD Staff
is not to proceed with bonding and the council would wait to see if internal changes made
correctly solve the problem. If bonding is incorporated the process would hold up given the
time allowed to build in the canyon. Could request a condition for bonding on a case-bycase basis. Add language in the ordinance for bonding to ensure that if things don’t work
out, developers are still held accountable. Bring this back to the council for feedback and
how to proceed. Commissioner Berreth will bring this to David. Consider looking at similar
communities for bonding requirements.
Commissioners and staff discussed extended foundation walls that serve as a retainer for soil for a
walk out basement. Distance added to the ordinance. Limits to tall retaining walls, aesthetics, and
limit to one story foundation.
The council reached out to Long Range Planning following up with ideas for the Sun and Moon.
Capital improvement list engineering report for Emigration Canyon Road and may wrap into
transportation plan. Commissioner Berreth suggested the city should acquire the Crompton
property above the Sun and Moon.
4) Other Business Items. (As Needed)
