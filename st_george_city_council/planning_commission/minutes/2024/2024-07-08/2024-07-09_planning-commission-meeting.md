REQUEST FOR A PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of a request for a three (3) lot preliminary plat known as
     Jamestown, located approximately at 960 W Manzanita Rd. The property is
     1.15 acres and is zoned R-1-10 (Single Family Residential minimum 10,000
     sq. ft. lots. Applicant – Jack Morby, Civil Science, representative, Brandee
     Walker. Case No. 2024-PP-015.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: This preliminary plat was originally reviewed
      and approved by the City Council on December 15, 2022. When the preliminary plat
      was originally approved it was approved with two conditions: 1. A drainage study is
      done on each lot before building. 2. Money is put into an account with the city for curb
      and gutter. This application is for an amended preliminary plat and a request from the
      applicant that the condition for the delay agreement and the money for the curb and
      gutter removed as a requirement for development of the preliminary plat. This is a
      preliminary plat, typically the Planning Commission is the approval authority for
      preliminary plats. Since the preliminary plat was approved with conditions by the City
      Council, the planning commission will need to send a recommendation to the City
      Council. Case No. 2024-PP-015. (Staff – Mike Hadley).

      Link to presentation from Planner Mike Hadley, including discussion between the
      Planning Commission Members, and Mr. Hadley: 00:36:26

REQUEST FOR A HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a request for Hillside Development Permit to allow
     disturbance of areas in the 20-30%, 30-40%, and 40% and above slope areas
     and review possible construction in a rockfall hazard overlay area. Applicant
     – DSG Engineering, representative, Mike Terry. Case No. 2024-HS-003.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: In October 2020, Phase one of Juniper Cove
      was recorded creating the adjacent lot to the west of the subject property. There is a
      small outcropping of rock that is in the 20-30% slope category requiring a review by
      the Hillside Review Board. Additionally, the property falls within the rockfall study zone
      requiring a study to be conducted on the property. Case No. 2024-HS-003. (Staff –

REQUEST FOR A PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of a request for a preliminary plat to allow for a single lot
     in the R-1-8 zone. Applicant – DSG Engineering, representative, Mike Terry.
     Case No. 2024-PP-009.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: The property at hand, if approved, would
      create a single lot in the Juniper Cove development. This parcel would need to be
      platted to make it a buildable lot. The zoning on the property is R-1-8 which requires
      each lot to be a minimum of 8,000 ft2. The proposed lot would contain 11,366 ft2. The
      Hillside Review Board met on site in February to discuss the proposal and recommend
      approval of the request on the condition that a letter regarding rockfall potential is
      written specifically for this property. Case No. 2024-PP-009. (Staff – Wes Jenkins).
