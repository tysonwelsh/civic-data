City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On April 19, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1538, amending the
Draper City Municipal Code relating to excavation permits. The complete ordinance is
on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 20th day of April 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
