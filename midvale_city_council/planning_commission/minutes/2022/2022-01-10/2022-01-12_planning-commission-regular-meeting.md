GENERAL SESSION

Chair Tippetts called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. She
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS


1.     Aaron Trejo/Pure Auto Sales requests Conditional Use Permit approval for vehicle
       sales located at 7554 South State Street in the State Street Commercial (SSC) zone.
       (21-014; Adam Olsen).

Mr. Olsen stated that the applicant requests conditional use permit approval for a vehicle sales
business located at 7554 S State St in the State Street Commercial (SSC) zone. Per Table 17-7-
7.2, “vehicle sales and service” is an allowed use in the SSC zone, through a conditional use
process. Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. No
objections have been received as of the writing of this report.

APPLICANT:

The applicant was not present.

PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Tippetts opened the public hearing.

There were no comments.


MOTION:         Commissioner Anderson MOVED to close the public hearing. The motion was
                SECONDED by Commissioner Snow with all voting in favor.

                                                  ***
DISCUSSION:

Commissioner Costello asked if the site would provide vehicle services?

Mr. Olsen stated that the site as it is proposed is strictly for vehicle sales and finance.

2.      Ms. Arnold stated that M. Adam Lankford, representing Wasatch Residential
        Group, requests Large Scale Master Plan approval for a property located at 8090 S
        Seghini Dr in the Jordan Bluffs Zone Subarea 4 (JB-4) zone. (21-011; Elizabeth
        Arnold)

Ms. Arnold stated that the applicant requests large scale master plan approval for Phase III of
Jordan Bluffs located at 8090 S Seghini Dr. in the Jordan Bluffs Subarea 4 (JB-4) zone. A large
scale master plan is required for Jordan Bluffs Subarea 4 as a whole prior to any specific
development applications. The original Development Agreement for Jordan Bluffs included
Phase III, and no changes are being requests as a part of this application.

APPLICANT:

Mr. Lankford with Wasatch Residential Group stated that this is the last phase of the project and
they are really excited for the project to move forward.

                                                ***

PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Tippetts opened the public hearing.

There were no comments.


MOTION:        Commissioner Smallwood MOVED to close the public hearing. The motion
               was SECONDED by Commissioner Snow with all voting in favor.

                                                ***
DISCUSSION:

Mr. Wilcox, Deputy City Attorney stated that this is an administrative decision. If the request
meets the conditions of approval, the Commission will need to approve the application.

3.     Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment to amend text in Chapters 17-2, 17-7-1, 17-7-2,
       17-7-3, 17-7-4, 17-7-5, 17-7-7, 17-7-8, and 17-7-15 of the Midvale City Municipal
       Code.

Ms. Tyler stated that the proposed Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment included herein intends
to address required updates to Title 17 Zoning based on amendments to the Land Use,
Development, and Management Act (LUDMA) established by HB 1003 during the 2021 Utah
State Legislative Session. HB1003 addresses municipalities’ ability to implement design review
regulations for single-family dwellings and duplex dwellings (with some exceptions).

                                             ***

The applicant did not provide any additional comments.
