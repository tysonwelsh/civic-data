        The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agendas for the special City Council, Redevelopment Agency
(RDA), and special Municipal Building Authority (MBA) meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.; discuss proposed Fiscal Year
(FY) 2023-2024 Budget Amendments; discuss proposed Ogden City FY 2024-2025 Budget; discuss the Proposed FY2023-2024
RDA Budget Amendments; discuss Council/RDA/MBA business; and hear Council/RDA/MBA comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair Richey and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Proposed City Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Budget Amendment – Year End
Adjustments
          Comptroller Sorensen explained the proposed amendment recognizes revenues from various sources in the General Fund,
Tourism, Capital Improvement Plan, Airport, Golf, BDO, Fleet and Facilities, and Miscellaneous Grant Funds. Sources of revenue
vary from use of fund balance, sale of assets, taxes, transfers—both within the Fund and to other Funds--grants, and internal
billing. Expenditures in the General Fund are for compensation adjustments, professional and technical expenses, contract
services, and building and other facility improvements. Mr. Sorensen used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to detail the
revenue sources involved in the budget amendment, as well as the proposed use of funds. He concluded the total fiscal impact to
the budget is an increase of $5.8 million. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          During the presentation, there was a focus on the budget amendments related to a transfer from the General Fund to cover
wage overages in the City Attorney’s Office and Public Services Department. There are also transfers to projects from the Police
Department, Fire Department, and Community and Economic Development Department. Mr. Sorensen explained the reasons for
the wage overages vary from Department to Department but are typically related to temporary assignments of projects that were
not foreseen when the fiscal year budget was initially developed. There was also discussion about status of the Exchange Building
in the Trackline Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Area; Community and Economic Development staff reported on ongoing
negotiations and plans to publish a notice inviting proposals from developers interested in partnering with the RDA to redevelop
the property and/or preserve the historical building.

Proposed RDA Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Budget Amendment – Year End
Adjustments
          Comptroller Sorensen explained here are three districts that expired at the end of the 2022 calendar year. These include
the South-Central Business District (CBD) Mall Area, the 12th Street RDA Area, and the South Wall RDA Area. With increment
no longer coming and no additional RDA activity happening in the areas, Administration is proposing to move the remaining
funds from those three areas. He used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on the details of the transfers, concluding
that the total fiscal impact is an increase to the FY24 RDA budget of $1,052,000. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see
the information packet for the meeting.

Proposed Ogden City Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Budget
         General Budget Discussion: Council Deputy Director Symes explained the purpose of this agenda item is for the
Council to have an opportunity to discuss the proposed Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Tentative Budget and the proposed Schedule A
changes. Council members discussed the topics that were reviewed during their small group meetings aimed at general budget
review, which led Council staff to provide an overview of the remaining steps in the budget adoption process. Council member
Choberka stated that it is conceivable that the Council could adopt the tentative budget as an operating budget prior to the June 30
deadline, but additional changes can be made to the budget prior to the August truth in taxation hearing when the final budget
must be adopted. The Council discussed the inclusion of water utility rate increases in the budget, with Council Executive Director
Eller-Smith noting that formal action on the rates is required before Administration can proceed with bond issuance for the Ogden
Canyon waterline project.
         There was also discussion about Administration’s proposal to amend the ordinance that governs the amount of Business
Depot Ogden (BDO) lease revenue that can be utilized each year to fund the City’s budget; Ms. Eller-Smith noted that the
Mayor’s proposed budget includes a $1.8 million transfer of BDO lease revenue to the General Fund and is proposed for
employee compensation increases. The Council can choose to allow an increase in lease revenue this year, but not amend the
ordinance to allow for the same among of lease revenue funding in future years. The Council concluded they do not want to
change the ordinance at this time and communicated to Council staff their desire to continue to discuss the issue over the next
couple of months; however, they did indicate they want to fund the employee compensation increases as proposed in the budget
document. Ms. Eller-Smith noted that the June 18 work session meeting will be dedicated solely to continued budget discussion
and these issues can be explored in greater detail that night.


         The meeting adjourned at 5:59 p.m.
