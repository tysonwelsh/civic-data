WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
JUNE 1, 2022

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 1:00 p.m. on June 1, 2022, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow via Zoom, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

INVOCATION BY: Tim Martin, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Public Works Director, Ron Whitehead
REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:
REQUEST A CLOSED SESSION TO DISCUSS THE LEASE OF REAL PROPERTY
MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Move into a Closed Session. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.
MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Leave Closed Session and Resume the

Special Commission Meeting. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and

Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF WHETHER TO SUBMIT AN OFFER TO LEASE PART OF THE
BOULEVARD BUILDING TO THE UNITED STATES

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Issue a Lease Offer Based on the Terms
Discussed in the Closed Session, Authorize the Chair to Sign Related
Documents, and Allow Legal Counsel to Make Modifications and Adjustments
to the Documents as Necessary in Order to Submit. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 2:10 p.m.

CHEYENNE BENTLE OVICPOR IVERSON
DEPUTY CLERK CHAIRMAN
