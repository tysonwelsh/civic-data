Proposed Rezone – 335 7th Street
          Deputy Planning Manager Simpson explained PS Real Estate Investments, LLC has submitted a petition to rezone six
parcels, totaling 2.10 acres located at 335 7th Street, from R-3 (multiple family) and C-2 (community commercial) to R-2S (two-
family and small lot residential). The developer’s intent is to establish zoning on the property that allows for owner occupied
townhomes. The Planning Commission has made a recommendation to adopt the proposed rezoning ordinance based on the
finding that the proposal is consistent with the General Plan and the Lynn Community Plan.
          Mr. Simpson used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on the applicant’s development plans for the subject
property. For a copy of the PowerPoint presentation in its entirety, see the information packet. Discussion among the Board and
Mr. Simpson centered on the topic of using deed restrictions or a development agreement to ensure a developer satisfies the intent
of the project as it was initially presented to the Council, though no direction was given to draft a development agreement for this
proposal.
          Council Policy Analyst Burton indicated that the application could move forward to the March 12 City Council meeting
for action.

Proposed Street Name Change – Renaming 1500 West to 3030 South
          Planning Manager Brierley explained Ogden City Engineering requests a street name change for 1500 West, near
Midland Drive, to be designated as “3030 South”. The purpose of the request is to make the street numbering more consistent with
the established grid along Midland Drive. In January of 2024, the Planning Commission recommended that the Council approve
the petition, finding that the proposal eliminates duplication and avoids confusion which would have a negative impact upon
providing of emergency services. He used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to identify the area subject to the street name
change and discussed the implications of the petition. For a copy of the PowerPoint presentation in its entirety, see the information
packet.
          Council Policy Analyst Burton stated the next step is for the City Council to hold a public hearing regarding the petition,
after which an action can be taken to either approve, reject, or table the petition. The public hearing can be held March 12, 2024.
