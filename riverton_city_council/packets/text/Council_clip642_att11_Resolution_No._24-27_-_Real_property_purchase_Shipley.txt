                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 24-27

         A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO EXECUTE A
            RIGHT OF WAY AGREEMENT WITH WAYNE AND PAMELA
           SHIPLEY FAMILY LIVING TRUST TO PURCHASE FRONTAGE
        LOCATED BY PARCEL NUMBER 27-33-476-042, AT THE ADDRESS OF
                13328 SOUTH TRESTLE LANE, RIVERTON, UTAH

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has a need to improve Trestle Lane immediately to the north
of 13400 South Street, to provide the same with curbing gutter, sidewalk and related
improvements; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that acquisition of real
property which fronts Trestle Lane from Wayne and Pamela Shipley Family Living Trust, owner,
will be necessary for the purpose of making needed improvements to Trestle Lane; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an agent for Riverton City
has negotiated an agreement to purchase frontage from Wayne and Pamela Shipley Family Living
Trust, located in the vicinity of 13328 South Trestle Lane (the “Subject Property”) and said purchase
will enable improvements to Trestle Lane; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the purchase price for the
Subject Property is reasonable and of fair market value, based upon comparable prices of similar
properties recently purchased and sold within the general vicinity of the Subject Property;

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Right of Way Agreement with Wayne and
          Pamela Shipley Family Living Trust, to acquire frontage property identified by Parcel
          Number 27-33-476-042, at the address of 13328 South Trestle Lane, Riverton, Utah,
          once it is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney. In addition, the Mayor is
          authorized to execute any additional documents at closing if the same are necessary to
          facilitate closing, do not culminate in a substantial alteration to the material terms of the
          Right of Way Agreement, and are approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
