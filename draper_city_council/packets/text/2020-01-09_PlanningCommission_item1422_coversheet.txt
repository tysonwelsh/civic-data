Re: Lofts at Creekside Final Plat Request Comments: On September 12, 2019, the Planning Commission approved the preliminary plat for this project.  This request is for the final plat approval.  The Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) allows final plats to be approved by the planning commission as an administrative action item, meaning that no public hearing is required. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  That the proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan such as:
a.  Support a balance between jobs and housing by integrating housing, employment, and supporting infrastructure in mixed-use centers located at appropriate locations.
b.  Maintain a citywide balance of land uses that support changes in community vision/dynamics over time.
2.  That the proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City Municipal Code.
3.  No public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
4.  That the proposed development conforms to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area.
