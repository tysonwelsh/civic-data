                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                    Tuesday, June 10th, 2025 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

C. INVOCATION: Stephanie Russell
D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
      1. Commissioner Froerer: We attended the Meadowbrook open house last night for the 46 housing units
         made available through ARPA funds. This is a major improvement for Ogden City and those citizens
         looking for housing. I was taken back by the quality of that building and really what that building will
         mean to people to have a facility they can call home. I wanted to give a shout out to Andy Beadles of
         Weber Housing Authority, Scott Parke, Chris Crockett, and our Weber County staff that had a hand in
         making this happen. I am proud we had a part in providing critical housing for citizens in this county. And
         it’s a major improvement over what was on that property before.
      2. Commissioner Harvey: I would only hope those who say “government doesn’t do enough” would see the
         fit and finish, the layout, and how it was done. They have a great, safe place to better their lives. It made
         me proud to be a part of this County.
E. PUBLIC COMMENT: NONE.
F. PRESENTATIONS:
   1. YCC Update on Domestic Violence Services. [See attached presentation]
      Denette Stanger (Executive Director): YCC is entering its 80th year of being in Weber County and we see
      the County as one of our strongest partners. We just opened our brand new transitional housing facility last
      fall where people can stay for up to two years. Five families are in housing of their own already. Child care
      center is on site available to anyone in the community. Today we are providing and update on growth and
      changes in the County. In 2023, Utah State legislature passed HB117 that requires use of LAP by law
      enforcement when responding to domestic abuse cases. I have seen firsthand how that helps with interaction
      in domestic abuse cases. Last year, all domestic violence service providers worked together to lobby the
      legislature for additional funding. Despite this support, the funding was not appropriated by the State. I am
      here today asking for whatever level of support Weber County can offer.
            Commissioner Froerer: Have you visited individual cities? What level of support are they offering?
            Ms. Stanger: Last year Ogden donated $35k, North Ogden $14k, Marriot-Slaterville sent I believe $2k, and
            Morgan County donated $10k. We have data for unincorporated Weber County. Our plan is to visit every
            city in Weber County.
            Commissioner Froerer: It might be good to attend our budget sessions with a breakdown of unincorporated
            Weber County.
            Commissioner Harvey: You made a statement that rang home when you said you help bring law
            enforcement costs down. Before you make that budget presentation, maybe spend some time with the Weber
            County Sheriff and their budget team to put together how those savings are seen.

H. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVAL OF FIRST READING OF FEE ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS RELATING TO THE
      WEBER COUNTY TRANSFER STATION.
         Sean Wilkinson (Community Development Director): The tipping fee for demolition and construction waste
         increases from $50 to $52.24 per ton. There was no increase last year. The increase covers costs of
         transportation and disposal costs from Republic Services and increased Weber County personnel and
         operations costs. Mattresses are free for the first two and $15 for each additional mattress. Delivery fees for
         the compost and wood products will increase for western Weber County with higher increases for Ogden
         Valley and areas outside of Weber County.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve First Reading of Fee Ordinance amendments relating to the Weber
         County Transfer Station; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    2. APPROVAL OF ORDINANCE 2025-11 AMENDING THE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR
