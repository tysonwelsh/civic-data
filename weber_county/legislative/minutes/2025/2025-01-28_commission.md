   1. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 07-2025 APPOINTING AN INTERIM REPLACEMENT TO THE
      POSITION OF COUNTY ASSESSOR.
            Weber County Commissioners: The prior Weber County Assessor resigned to take another job opportunity
            this month. The Republican Central Committee chose Jared Preisler as an interim replacement.
            Chris Crockett (Lead Civil Attorney): This interim appointment will last until the next general election even
            though Mr. Ulibarri’s term was for four years.
            Jared Preisler: I am excited to get started and to fill some big shoes. I am excited to work with the office staff
            and continue the progress John (Ulibarri) has been working on. I have known John for many years. I have
            been a certified residential appraiser for 28 years. I had a private practice. I worked as a Chief Appraiser for
            a technology company. I teach 18 different courses for the Appraisal Institute as well as the standards course
            for USPAP (Uniform Standards of Professional Appraisal Practice) that appraisals, including ad valorem
            valuations, are performed. I am excited to have this opportunity in my career to contribute and serve the great
            citizens of Weber County.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 07-2025 appointing an interim replacement to the position
            of County Assessor; Commissioner Froerer seconded.

    6. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH NORTH VIEW FIRE DISTRICT FOR PARAMEDIC
       SERVICES COUNTYWIDE.
         Eli Johnson (Sheriff’s Department, Homeland Security): This is part of our phased expansion of our county
         wide paramedic system. Based on our independent audit done two years ago, there was some holes in response
         times. One of those areas identified was in the northwestern part of the County. We agreed to add a paramedic
         rescue unit to that area. This is a contract to establish an interlocal agreement to provide paramedic services
         in that part of the County. This contract takes effect July 1st. We are required to have the interlocal in place
         before getting licensing from the state Bureau of EMS.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with North View Fire District for paramedic services
         countywide; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – excused; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    8. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 09-2025 APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO AN INTERLOCAL
       AGREEMENT FOR THE AIRPORT COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT PROJECT AREA.
         Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): This is a Resolution to amend the Ogden Airport CRA
         interlocal agreement with Ogden City. This interlocal is in compliance with current CRA policies. This change
         drops us down to 75% from 95% and a $17M cap over 18 years. One additional change to the interlocal
         requested by the Commission reads as follows:

                  The agency agrees that tax increment generated from the county shall be used for infrastructure and
                  related improvements including for the repayment of bonds which financial infrastructure and related
                  improvements. No later than December 31st of each year, the agency's manager shall provide the
                  county an itemized list detailing the infrastructure that was financed by the tax increment during the
                  year. Failure of the agency to timely provide the itemized list to the county shall be considered a
                  material breach of this agreement.

         There is really good economic opportunity at the airport for both the County and Ogden City. The airport has
         a new manager and they are making significant changes to their overall master plan including an expansion
         that create really good job opportunities for citizens here.
         Commissioner Froerer: It appears this money will be well spent. Just to reiterate, this money is only for
         infrastructure.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 09-2025 approving an amendment to an Interlocal
         Agreement for the Airport Community Reinvestment Project Area; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – excused; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
