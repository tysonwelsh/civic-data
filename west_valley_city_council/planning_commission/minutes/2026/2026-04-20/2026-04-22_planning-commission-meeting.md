BACKGROUND:

      Robbie Thomson with Utah Land Company has submitted a General Plan/zone change
application on 1.9 acres located at 5548 West 4100 South. The property is currently zoned RB
(Residential Business) with a General Plan designation of Non-Retail Commercial. The
proposed zone is RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and the proposed General Plan
designation is Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre). For reference, the RB zone
allows several non-retail commercial uses such as banks/credit unions, commercial day cares,
and office buildings.


Surrounding zones include R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000
square feet) to the north and east; C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial) and RB to the south; and
C-2 (General Commercial), C-1, and RB to the west. Neighboring uses include single unit
dwellings to the north, east, and portion of the south; a CVS pharmacy for the remaining south
portion; and American First Credit Union, Starbucks, AutoZone, and a church to the west. The
property is currently vacant.


The applicant is proposing to construct a 22-unit, three-story, for-sale townhome development.
The proposed density of the project including the R-1-8 zoned north area of the property
needed for the road and the two narrow strips of property extending to 4100 South is 9.6
units/acre (22 units on 2.3 acres). All units would be front-loaded with 2-car garages and
include 3 bedrooms. All garages have driveways that are deep enough to park in. The attached
concept plan and application materials from the applicant provide details on and support for
the proposed project.


At times developers will try to make an existing product fit a difficult site, yielding what is, in
staff’s view, an awkward layout. Most townhomes are deeper than they are wide; however, in
this situation, the applicant has developed a townhome layout that is wider than it is deep to
better fit a site that is relatively narrow east to west.


In reviewing this application, staff suggests that there are two primary questions to answe
   1. Is this the right location for a townhome development?

   2. Assuming the location is appropriate, does the quality of the project further City goals?


These two questions are explored below.
Is this the right location for a townhome development?

Does the quality of the project further City goals?


City Code requires requests for the RM zone to be accompanied by a development agreement
proposal that addresses dwelling unit sizes, exterior materials, architecture, landscaping, and
project amenities. These items, along with other considerations, are addressed below.


Dwelling Unit Sizes
In the zoning ordinance, the minimum unit size for 3-bedroom townhomes is 1,400 square feet.
The proposed townhomes, which are 3-bedroom units, are approximately 1,720 square feet
based on the floor plans.


Exterior Materials
For materials, the City’s multi-unit residential design standards state: “All multi-unit residential
Building exteriors shall be brick, stucco, stone, tile, metal panels or fiber cement siding. With
the exception of brick or stone which may be used on the entire Building exterior, no single
material shall comprise more than 60 percent of a Building’s exterior. Where stucco, tile, metal
panels or fiber cement siding are used, at least 20 percent of the Building’s exterior shall be
brick or stone. Wood and vinyl may be used as trim, soffits and/or accent materials only.”


The applicant is proposing a combination of stone, stucco, and fiber cement siding. According
to the applicant, the stone percentage is 27% and both the stucco and fiber cement siding are
each less than 60% of the exterior.


Architecture

Based on a preliminary review of the proposed building architecture by staff, the proposed
units meet or exceed the architectural requirements in the City’s Multi-Unit Residential Design
Standards except for the roof pitch. The code requires a minimum roof pitch of 6/12 and the
proposed roof pitch is 3/12. The proposed buildings are somewhat unique in that they have at

Landscaping
The ordinance requires a total of 50% landscaping and recreation space. According to the
applicant’s concept, the proposal would exceed this percentage.
Project Amenities
For a project of this size, the ordinance requires one amenity. The proposed project includes
one amenity from the ordinance – an outdoor covered pavilion with picnic table.
Garages/Parking
The ordinance requires all townhome units to include a 2-car garage. The overall parking
requirement for townhomes is 4.75 with 2 in a garage, 2 in a driveway, and an additional 0.75
within the project. All of the units have a two-car garage and a driveway deep enough to park
in. For the additional 0.75 stalls, 16 are required and 14 are provided. However, unlike most
townhome developments where driveway placements make parking on the street impossible,
there are areas along the proposed road, like the south end of the cul-de-sac and portion north
of unit 1, where on-street parking would be possible. Assuming parking occurred along one
side of these portions of the road, an additional 11 cars could be parked.


Other Considerations
Building Height and Setbacks
The RM zone includes the following standard: “For properties adjacent to residential Uses with
Buildings with two stories or less, the maximum Building Height at the minimum Side and Rear
Yard setback shall be 25 feet. For each foot of height over 25 feet, Buildings shall be set back
an additional foot from the minimum setback, with a maximum total Building Height of 75 feet.”
The rear yard setback in the RM zone is 20 feet.
The proposed townhomes are 31.5 feet in height and are adjacent to one story homes to the
east. The proposed rear yard setback varies between 25.5 to 25.9 feet, and the requirement
would be 26.5 feet. On the west side, a 10-foot setback is proposed; however, these units are
adjacent to commercial uses. For comparison, the current RB zone would allow a building
height of 35 feet and setback of 10 feet for a side yard and 20 feet for a rear yard.

BACKGROUND:

West Valley Land Holdings, LLC is requesting approval for the Wasatch West Valley
Subdivision. The subdivision will also amend lots 2 and 3 of the WVLH Subdivision.
The subdivision is located at 3548 South Constitution Boulevard and is zoned CC (City
Center).

ISSUES:

The WVLH Subdivision was recorded in October 2023. The subdivision was created to
consolidate old property lines, establish new lots for the police headquarters building,
future commercial uses and a parking lot. The property was previously in the ownership
of the Redevelopment Agency of West Valley (RDA). Once the subdivision was
approved and recorded, the land, other than the police headquarters lot, was transferred
to West Valley Land Holdings, LLC.

Wasatch Commercial Management submitted a subdivision application, the Wasatch West
Valley Retail Subdivision, in August of 2025 that the Planning Commission, and later, the
City Council, approved. That plat was never recorded due to some changes that the
owners wanted to make to the subdivision. They recently acquired three parcels previously
owned by the West Valley City RDA, north of the office tower. They now want to include
those parcels in this subdivision, as well as the office tower parcel itself, so a new
subdivision application was submitted.

The purpose of the current application is to amend two parcels from the 2023 WVLH
Subdivision, as well as take the remaining parcels owned by West Valley Land Holdings,
LLC, and create a new subdivision consisting of 5 lots. The subdivision will modify the
parcel lines where the current office tower sits and create 4 new lots for future commercial
land uses.

Shared access to the subdivision will be gained from Lehman Avenue, which will be
handled through an existing easement from the WVLH Subdivision, and from Constitution
Blvd through a proposed access easement that will be maintained in common by the
