                                 LBA RESOLUTION NO. 2025-04

                   A RESOLUTION ADOPTING A BUDGET AND MAKING
                   APPROPRIATIONS FOR THE SUPPORT OF THE LOCAL
                   BUILDING AUTHORITY OF THE CITY OF HOLLADAY FOR THE
                   FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING ON THE AUTHORITY’S CREATION
                   DATE AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2026.

       WHEREAS, the Chair of the Local Building Authority of the City of Holladay, as required
by law, previously submitted to the Board of Trustees of the Local Building Authority of the
City of Holladay a budget including all supporting schedules and data for the Fiscal Year
beginning the Authority’s creation date and ending June 30, 2026; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to law, a copy of the Budget was placed on record in the City
Recorder’s Office for inspection by the general public during normal office hours, at least ten
days prior to its final adoption; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to law, a public hearing to receive public comment and consider
adoption of the Budget was held on October 2, 2025 at 6:30 p.m. in a Local Building Authority
meeting; and

        WHEREAS, all interested persons in participating in the public hearing were given an
opportunity to be heard, for or against, the estimate of revenues and expenditures or any item
thereof in the Budget; and

       WHEREAS, it is the intent and desire of the Local Building Authority of the City of
Holladay to comply with all applicable State and local laws regarding the adoption of the
Budget; and

        WHEREAS, the Local Building Authority of Holladay City finds that it is in the best
interests of the citizens of the City of Holladay to adopt a Budget for the Authority.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of Trustees of the Local Building
Authority of the City of Holladay, Utah as follows:

                                          SECTION I
                                       BUDGET ADOPTION

       A. The budgeted amounts shown in Exhibit A, the Local Building Authority of the City
of Holladay 2025-2026 Budget, attached hereto and by this reference incorporated herein, are
hereby appropriated for the corporate purposes and objects of the Authority for the fiscal year
commencing on the Authority’s creation date, and ending June 30, 2026, and are hereby adopted
as the Budget of the Local Building Authority of the City of Holladay, Utah for the Fiscal Year
2025-26.
        B. Pursuant to law, a copy of the Budget for each fund within the Budget shall be
certified by the Chair as the “Budget Officer” and shall be filed with the State Auditor within 30
days after adoption of the Budget.

      C. Pursuant to law, a certified copy of the Budget shall be filed in the office of the City
Recorder and shall be available for public inspection during regular business hours.

                                         SECTION II
                                       FURTHER ACTION

       In addition to the foregoing, the Chair is hereby directed to implement any other
necessary actions pertinent to the adoption of the Budget. Such actions may include, but are not
necessarily limited to, notification, reporting, and publishing as required by and consistent with
applicable law.

                                          SECTION III

                                           SEVERABILITY
        If any provision of this Resolution is declared invalid by a court of competent
jurisdiction, the remainder shall not be affected thereby.

                                         SECTION IV
                                       EFFECTIVE DATE

       This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon posting, as required by law, deposited
and recorded in the office of the City Recorder, and accepted as required herein.

       PASSED AND APPROVED this              day of October, 2025.

Fund Description
In December 2025, the Local Building Authority (LBA) is scheduled to issue bonds generating $10,260,000 in construction proceeds. Together with a $5,800,000 contribution from the City of
Holladay, total available funding for capital projects will equal $16,457,927. Debt service on the LBA bonds will be funded from lease revenues received by the LBA from other City funds.

These resources will be applied to two major initiatives:
  • City Hall Renovation and Seismic Retrofit
  • Spring Lane Elementary Adaptive Reuse

In addition, during 2025 the City entered into a 30-year lease agreement with Granite School District for use of the former Spring Lane Elementary site. This agreement provides the City with the right
to develop a park at the location in conjunction with the adaptive reuse project.
