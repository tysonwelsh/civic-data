                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 22-46

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A GROUND
 LEASE AGREEMENT WITH GOOGLE FIBER UTAH, LLC, WHICH ALLOWS FOR
     THE INSTALLATON OF A UTILITY HUT ON CITY-OWNED PROPERTY.

       WHEREAS, Google Fiber, Utah LLC, (the “Provider”) desires to provide
communications services, including Broadband Internet Services and other data transmission
services (“Services”) within the City; and

        WHEREAS, on December 14, 2021 the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No.
21-65, authorizing the City to execute a Franchise Agreement with Provider, which authorized
Provider to install a voice and data transmission system within the rights of way under City
jurisdiction; and

        WHEREAS, Provider has a need to install nodes known as “utility huts” which are
interspersed throughout Provider’s network to fully integrate the network of voice and data
transmission lines installed by Provider under authorization of said Franchise Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the City is the fee title owner of property located at 4008 West Innovation
Drive, Riverton, Utah ( the “Subject Property”) which is suitable for the installation of a utility
hut for Google’s network; and

        WHEREAS, City staff has negotiated the terms of a Lease Agreement with Provider to
locate a utility hut on the Subject Property, as specified in Exhibit A to this Resolution; and

        WHEREAS, Provider has obtained approval by the Riverton City Planning Commission
to install a utility hut as contemplated in the attached Lease Agreement, subject to approval of
the Agreement; and

       WHERAS, the City Council finds and determines that entry into the attached Lease
Agreement with Provider is for fair market value, enables the City to improve the Subject
Property where the utility hut will be located, and further serves the public with enhanced
telecommunications services within the City.


     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Ground Lease Agreement with Google
          Fiber Utah, LLC in substantially similar form as provided in Exhibit A attached to this
          Resolution, after the same is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney. Exhibits
          D-2 and D-2 shall be completed in a manner consistent with terms and conditions of
          approval granted by the Riverton City Planning Commission in its issuance of site plan
          approval for the Subject Property on the date of May 26, 2022.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
