ITEM 1
PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT:
      Consider a request for a Planned Development (PD) amendment to the Sun
      River Commons Master Sign Plan. Proposed changes include adding two 30

      representative is Ryan Cain. The project will be known as Sun River
      Commons Signs. Case No. 2025-PDA-005 - (Staff – Brett Hamilton).
