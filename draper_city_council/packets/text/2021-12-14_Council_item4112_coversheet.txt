Re: Providing Local Consent for a Special Use Permit for Summits Sips Wine School LLC Comments: Summit Sips Wine School LLC located at 1964 E Fielding Hill Way is a home-based business seeking Local Consent for a Special Use Permit for educational purposes.
The business specializes in teaching about wine at client locations and will not operate out of the home (besides completing office work).  The findings are as follows: Draper City Code 6-4-040 states the following:  c.   No off-premises beer retail license, manufacture license,  or special use permit for a special use permittee operating on a for-profit basis shall be issued wherein the premises would be located within three hundred feet (300') of a community location as measured from the nearest patron entrance of the outlet by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the property boundary of a community location.
However, due to the businesses mobile status, these proximity requirements do not apply. According to the DABC, the following items do not apply to Summit Sips Wine School LLC and are not included in the application for a Special Use Permit:
1. Proximity maps
2. A floor plan showing where alcohol is consumed, stored, and sold.
3. Insurance coverage.
4. A bond payment.
