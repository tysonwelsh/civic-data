1. LocalLink           Project         Final          Report;          Lynn      Jacobs,      SLC        Project         Manager         and Company
Lynn      Jacobs said two years ago Salt Lake City,                                   South     Salt Lake City,               Millcreek,       and Holladay
partnered       on a grant to study how to improve                                active     transit      through           the communities.        The study
focused      on looking           at the facilities              for those who walk,                bike,     and take transit           to connect
Sugarhouse,          Millcreek,            and Holladay.               They      came up with            goals and priorities              for the study by
looking      at the planning               documents             each entity        had for the area. From                    those, they analyzed            four
transit    modes:      streetcar,          light      rail, bus rapid           transit,    and enhanced                bus. He explained          the
differences      between           the modes                ranging      from     dedicated         street space to shared street space. The
study went through                a detailed           technical         analysis     that reviewed              costs, ridership,          and impacts        on
rights-of-way         with     each mode                   of transit.    The consultants             then surveyed              the public      and had over
900 respondents.             The streetcar                 option      was the favorite         but there was opposition                      to the idea in
some neighborhoods.                  The consultant                 recomtnended             starting        with     an enhanced          bus on Highland
Drive     that could       convert             to something            more      in the future.          The enhanced            bus service       would      run
more frequent          and be nicer than the existing                            service.     In the analysis,               the enhanced        bus and

2. First       Step House           Update         Presentation;            Shawn      McMillen,           Executive         Director
Shawn         McMillen        introduced          Dan Mulcahy,            REACH          Program          Manager,        to the Council.          He said
the First       Step House         was founded             in 1958.    It provides        treatment,         housing,       case management,
health       care, employment              services,       and long-term           recovery       management.             First     Step House
purchased         a REACH           facility      at 950 E. 3300 S. in 2020 through                        a low interest           loan fund to
address health            disparities      through         Intermountain        Health         Care. He thanked             Millcreek          staff and
Council        for helping         make the facility            possible.     In the winter            of 2020, the building,               through       a
lease arrangement,              was used to help homeless                    individuals.         In May       2021 the REACH                  facility
became         operational.        They        currently     served    39 individuals             for substance          abuse treatment.             He said
the population            they serve often           faces difficulty         with     accessing          adequate       treatment         and care to
effectively        meet their needs and reduce                    recidivism.         Salt Lake County                identified      a social problem
with       high risk/high       need individuals              and associated          incarceration          rates. The county               went through
a request       for information            and a request          for proposals          process       for a program          that would         help this
population         and that is where             the REACH            (Recovery,         Engagement,           Assessment,            Career
Development,              Housing)        program          came in. The program                serves adult males             diagnosed         with
substance        use disorders            who are exiting          the criminal        justice      system        and are at high risk of re-
 Millcreek            City    Council         Meeting          Minutes                                28 February           2022       Page 3 of 8



 offending.           The treatment            is two-fold;         to target those risk factors              that contribute         to recidivism             and
 target those need factors                    that address substance              use disorder         and cooccurring              mental      health
issues. The services                are highly           assessment        driven.    The two-year            program       was part of a 4-year
 study with           the county.       One hundred              and eighteen         individuals       were served in the 2021 fiscal                     year.
