                                        Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 8-11-20
                                            7:00 p.m.
                                   Nephi City Council Chambers


        1. The council discussed an OHV and Golf Cart ordinances drafts. Police Chief Michael Morgan
           said he would like to add additional penalties to the code. Councilor Memmott expressed
           that he didn’t not want government overreach on this issue. Other Councilors expressed
           their concern for the safety of underage youth and others not complying with wearing
           helmets, speeding, and having too many people on the vehicles.

        2. Several members of the Ute Stampede Committee discussed with the council an Extreme
           Bull Riding Event that will be held on September 19, 2020. The committee asked the council
           how they felt about beer being sold at the event. A discussion ensued about who has
           jurisdiction over the sale of beer, the county or the city. Kyle Garrett said that the State of
           Utah requires a letter of consent from the governing body to obtain a liquor license. The
           council instructed staff to work with the Ute Stampede Committee on complying with city
           code to be able to sell beer at the event.

        3. Shane and Kendra Jackson addressed the council about the building of Substation III near
           their home. They expressed concern about the safety of their family and animals living that
           close to a substation. They said the location was not ideal because of the train crossing and
           the sharp bend in the road that each winter drivers slide into the fence near where the
           substation would be located. Mayor Nielson said he is working on obtaining a letter from
           Utah Municipal Power Agency addressing the Jackson’s concerns.

        4. Mr. Atkinson discussed with the council about the need for another full-time gas
           department employee. The state standards and requirements to paint every gas meter in
           the city has forced the issue of needing another employee. He indicated that the gas
           department has the funds to pay for another employee. The council instructed staff to
           advertise internally and externally for the position of a Gas Department Utility Tech II.

        5. Mr. Atkinson reported on the Fall Classic baseball tournament held last weekend. He said
           that it was very successful and plans are being made to have this as an annual event.

        6. Mayor Nielson informed the council that he had been approached by Quinton Kay from
           Mona about going in together with Mona City and purchasing law enforcement support
           signs. A discussion ensued about whether to purchase some and have residents be able to
           purchase their own yard sign. The council instructed staff to purchase two banners that
           read “We Support Our Law Enforcement.”

        7. Mr. Atkinson gave several updates and presented the latest financial statements to the
           council. He said that the city is eligible for $550,000 from the CARES Act. The council
           instructed staff to apply for the funding and then if the city does not have a need for all of
           the funding then the city can turn the money back.

        8. Adjournment 9:40 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Kasey
