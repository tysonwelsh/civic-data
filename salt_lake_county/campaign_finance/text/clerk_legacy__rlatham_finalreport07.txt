                   To Whom It May Concern:

                   Please find enclosed the final financial disclosure report for the Latham for
                   District Attorney campaign committee.

                   In preparing the final report, I identified a duplicate entry for a $100 expenditure
                   for voter calls. The $100 expenditure indicated in the September 15th,2006
                   report was already included in the $300 expenditure for voter calls indicated in
                   the June 20th report.

                   Therefore, the total expenditures made for the September 15th,2006 report
                   should have been $1,674.00, for a sum total to date of $4,040.33, and a balance
                   at close of reporting period of $346.94. Concomitantly, the sum total to date for
                   the October 31st,2006 report should have been $5,184.19, a balance at beginning
                   of reporting period of $346.94, a subtotal of $1,565.80, and a balance at close of
                   reporting period of $421.94.

                   The balance in the campaign committee's separate bank account, which has been
                   closed, is zero. I have sent to your office by email an Excelspreadsheet checked
                   against the reports and itemizing contributions and expenditures, which both add
                   up to $6,134.07.

                   Please consider this letter to be an amendment to the aforementioned reports. I
                   invite you to contact me if I may be of further assistance.
