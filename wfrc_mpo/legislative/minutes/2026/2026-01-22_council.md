 FRC Minutes 
W
 January 22, 2026 

 MINUTES 
 Wasatch Front Regional Council 
 January 22, 2026 
 WFRC Council meeting was held at WFRC Offices, 41 N Rio Grande Street, Salt Lake City and via Zoom. 
 1. I ntroductions & Consent Agenda [00:00:05]
 a. ACTION: Minutes of the WFRC meeting held October 23, 2025, current financial report and financial
 statements for Sept, Oct, and Nov 2025 (at end of packet)
 At 1:32pm, WFRC Council Chair, Mayor Dawn Ramsey, welcomed the Wasatch Front Regional Council members
 and guests to the first Council meeting in 2026. Introductions were made around the table, and online. New
 Council members attending were asked to briefly introduce themselves.
 [00:07:20] ACTION : Mayor Ramsey brought attention to the first item on the agenda - the minutes of the October
 23, 2025 meeting and current financial report and financial statements for September, October, and November
 2025. Commissioner Stevenson made a motion to approve all the documents as presented and Mayor Mark
 Shepherd seconded the motion. There was no discussion and the minutes and financial statements were approved
 unanimously.
 2. C
 hair Report [00:08:30]
 a. ACTION: Appointments to WFRC Council and Committees
 Mayor Ramsey, as the WFRC Chair, presented the appointments of WFRC Members to the Regional Growth
 Committee (RGC), Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com), WFRC Budget Committee, Active
 Transportation Committee (ATC), Wasatch Front Economic Development District (WFEDD) and Joint Policy
 Advisory Committee (JPAC). Appointments to WFRC and its Committees are also made by the County Councils of
 Governments (COGs) and other appointing authorities that partner with WFRC . [00:15:08] ACTION: Mayor Joy
 Petro made a motion that the members of the Council endorse the appointments to WFRC Council and other
 Committees as listed. UTA Trustee Carlton Christensen seconded the motion and the vote was unanimous in the
 affirmative.
 b. ACTION: 2026 State Legislative Session and Congressional preview [00:16:28]
 Mayor Ramsey introduced Miranda Jones Cox, WFRC Government Affairs Manager, who explained the legislation
 that is pertinent to the region. Ms. Jones Cox stated that the group would first get to hear briefly from the legislative
 members of WFRC - [00:17:25] Senator Wayne Harper and [00:19:30] Repr esentative Ariel Defay, on some key
 themes and priorities for the session. WFRC members can keep themselves apprised of notable legislation by
 subscribing to WFRC’s Government Affairs emails which are frequently distributed during the session. Members
 may also reference WFRC’s bill tracker, found on WFRC’s website, and/or provide input on these pieces of
 legislation by attending our weekly policy meetings on Thursday mornings at 8 AM at the Utah State Capitol
 building. [00:33:39] ACTION: Mayor Ramsey asked for a motion to endorse the recommended positions as
 presented. Mayor Monica Zoltanski made a motion and Mayor Tami Tran seconded the motion. The vote was
 unanimous in the affirmative.
 3. P
 ublic Comment [00:34:12] Mayor Ramsey opened the meeting for public comment. There were no public
 comments. 
 4. R
 egional Growth Committee (RGC) [00:34:40]
 Mayor Ramsey called on RGC Chair, Mayor Tami Tran, to provide a report on that group’s meeting on Jan 15,
 2026. Mayor Tran turned the time to Ted Knowlton, WFRC Deputy Director & Chief Planner, who discussed the
 criteria that WFRC uses to phase the projects in the Regional Transportation Plan.
 a. ACTION: Station Area Plan Certification [00:39:10]
 Mayor Tran continued the RGC report, stating that tremendous progress has been made by communities in our
 region over the past year to develop Station Area Plans. Last week the Regional Growth Committee reviewed the
 final Plans from 2025 and is making a recommendation to the Council to certify 18 plans in six cities. First, Meg
 Padjen, WFRC Community and Economic Development Director, will highlight the overall accomplishments in
 station area planning over the past three years. [00:46:15] Then, Byron Head, WFRC Community Planner,
 presented information for the consideration of the SAPs for certification. [00:58:06] ACTION: Mayor Tran made a
 motion to certify the Station Area Plans, Resolutions of Impracticability, and Resolutions of Prior Action for the

 cities of Ogden, South Salt Lake, Millcreek, Murray, Bountiful, and Salt Lake City. Commissioner Bob Stevenson 
 econded the motion. The vote was unanimous in the affirmative.

 5. Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) [00:58:57] 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Mayor Mark Shepherd, Trans Com Chair. Mayor Shepherd called on Ben 
 Wuthrich, WFRC Transportation Improvement Program Coordinator, to provide the information to the group. 
 a. Report: Board modifications to 2026-2031 TIP 
 Mr. Wuthrich noted that since the last meeting of the Council, WFRC staff received requests to modify the current 
 2026-2031 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). The modification required action from Trans Com (since 
 WFRC was not meeting) and the Utah Transportation Commission, but did not require a new air quality conformity 
 analysis or a 30-day public comment period. The modifications are included in the meeting materials, and Mr. 
 Wuthrich stated that if there were any questions, he would be available to answer them. 
 b. ACTION: Board modifications to 2026-2031 TIP [00:59:23] 
 Mr. Wuthrich continued, reporting that WFRC staff has again received several requests to modify the current 
 2026-2031 TIP and he briefly discussed each project on the list, also included in the meeting materials. [01:08:05] 
 ACTION: Mayor Mark Shepherd made a motion to approve the resolution to modify the 2026-2031 TIP as 
 requested. Mayor Joy Petro seconded the motion and the voting was unanimous in the affirmative. 
 6. Reports [01:08:57] 
 a. Wasatch Front Economic Development District (WFEDD) update 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Marcia White, WFRC Regional Economic Development Planner, to provide the 
 report in Chair Gage Froerer’s stead. Ms. White stated that the WFEDD year concluded with a discussion about 
 the Regional Transportation Plan and how the RTP supports economic growth. Trustee Carlton Christensen from 
 the Utah Transit Authority shared the value transit brings to Utah, not just in terms of ridership, but also the 
 economic impact and development around station areas, like on the S Line in Salt Lake County, the OGX in Weber 
 County, and others. As was noted at the beginning of the meeting, several committee changes are being made, 
 including the WFEDD committee. This year the WFEDD will have both a public-sector and private-sector co-chairs. 
 b. Executive Director’s report [01:11:15] 
 Mayor Ramsey introduced Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, for his report. Mr. Gruber spoke on the 
 following items: looking ahead to the working groups that will be created in preparation for the 2034 Olympic and 
 Paralympic Games, [01:14:55] the positive outcome of WFRC’s Quadrennial Review performed recently by the 
 Federal Highways Administration (FHWA), [01:16:55] the decision of WFRC to extend our building space lease 
 another 10 years (current lease expires Sept 2028) at a reduced annual interest rate, [01:20:29] recognized Suzie 
 Swim for 20 years of service at WFRC, [01:21:45] and, along with Johnnae Nardone, WFRC Communications 
 Manager, apprised the group of WFRC’s website refresh. 
 7. Other Business and Adjournment [01:24:35] 
 Mayor Ramsey asked the group if there was interest in having a field trip and if so, where would they like to go. 
 There was some discussion and WFRC staff will pursue suggested options. 
 Mayor Ramsey then continued, asking the group if there were other business items to bring up. Mayor Erin 
 Mendenhall invited the group to a celebration watch party between the City and County Building and Salt Lake 
 Main Library on the evening of February 6, 2026. [01:35:23] Mayor Jenny Wilson mentioned some potential 
 federal land acquisition issues. 
 [01:39:41] Mayor Ramsey then drew attention to the final item on the agenda, announcing that the video link, 
 Overview of the Utah Open and Public Meeting Act, is part of WFRC's compliance with annual training 
 requirements. The link is included on the agenda, in the initial Council meeting email and will also be added to the 
 follow up email that will be sent next week. Please take the time to watch it in order to fulfill the WFRC Training 
 requirements. 
 The next Council meeting will be held on Thursday, March 26, 2026. Upcoming events, listed at bottom of agenda, 
 include notice of the WFRC 101 webinar- a virtual orientation that will be held on February 26, 2026, at 1:30pm. 
 Mayor Ramsey entertained a motion to adjourn, which was made by Mayor Mark Shepherd and seconded by 
 Mayor Sheri Bingham. The meeting adjourned at 3:22pm. 

 A recording of this meeting, as well as meeting packet materials, may be found on the WFRC website at www.wfrc.utah.gov .
