Amendment 3b – Low Hills Tank Vault

       During excavation for the new booster pump station needed for Inverness and Texas Instruments
        developments, significant damage was discovered in the Low Hills culinary water tank. These repairs
        were able to be completed by the booster pump station vendor. The installation of new water tank
        vaults will allow better operational maintenance of this tank in the future.

Amendment 3c – General Fund Reserves Transfer

       Utah Law caps reserves in the general fund to be 35% of one year’s revenues. This amendment
