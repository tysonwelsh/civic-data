                                           LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
                                           Hearings began at – 5:39 p.m.

RWD2021-000289- Salt Lake County is requesting approval of a Roadway Dedication Plat for the Camp
Kearns Area Roads. Location: 4700 South 5200 West (Approximate). Zones: M-1 & M-2 (Manufacturing)
and C-3 (Commercial). Planner: Diana Martinez (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Diana Martinez
provided an analysis of the staff report.

Mr. Preece said Diana covered and be aware part of delays threatened litigation by individuals, bulk of
them signed off on the plat, the owners saw reason and signed off.

Commissioner Nelson asked if there were any concerns on widening 4700. Ms. Martinez said nothing
reflecting on this plat and county engineering said no issues with this plat being involved in that.

Motion: To recommend file #RWD2021-000289 Roadway Dedication Plat to the Kearns Council for
approval as presented.
       Motion by: Commissioner Hatch
       2nd by: Commissioner Nelson
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor
