        Motion by: Commissioner Richards
        2nd by: Commissioner Weight
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)



REZ2021-000256 - Ivory Homes is requesting a Rezone from the A-20 (Agricultural Zone) to the P-C

on plan and development agreement.
       Motion by: Commissioner Richards
       2nd by: Commissioner Weight
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)


31239- (PUD2020-000063)- Holmes Homes (Trish Smith) is requesting Final Approval of a Conditional
Use/PUD (Planned Unit Development) to allow for 74 townhomes on the subject property, and to amend
the plat for Arbor Park Commercial Subdivision, Lot 1. Acreage: 6.61 acres. Location: 8285 West 3500
South. Zone: C-2 (Commercial) & R-M (High Density Residential). Planner: Diana Martinez.
(Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding warranty bonds and improvement and inspection,
landscaping improvement bonds, plant life and recreation areas, fencing materials.

CUP2020-000093- Gerald Burt is requesting preliminary approval of a Conditional Use allowing for a 16-
unit dwelling group on the subject property. Acreage: 1.12 acres. Location: 7237 West 3500 South. Zone:
C-2 (Commercial). Planner: Diana Martinez. (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding block wall, and vinyl along north and west sides,
barrier rather than chain link, keep under one ownership and rentals,

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Gerald Burt
Address: 3045 East Louise Avenue, 84109
Comments: Mr. Burt said will have assistant manager there, and management group will handle. Rental
around $1500 per month, all three bedroom.

Commissioner Richards asked on fencing thoughts. Mr. Burt said will be using vinyl on the two sides. 15-
foot length on east, south corner gap with chain link and will replaced with vinyl, solid barrier all the way
around. There is an existing easement with Reams, finishing up now. Commissioner VanRoosendaal
confirmed intent to have fencing with anti-graffiti. Mr. Burt said playground on the west may move to the
north a bit. Commissioner Richards said no on street parking and must park in driveway.
