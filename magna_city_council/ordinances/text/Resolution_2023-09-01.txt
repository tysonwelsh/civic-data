                               MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 23-09-01                                     DATE: September 12, 2023


            A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
            AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN
            INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN MAGNA METRO
            TOWNSHIP AND SALT LAKE COUNTY RELATING TO THE
            CONDUCT OF THE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK
            GRANT PROGRAM, EMERGENCY SOLUTIONS GRANT
            PROGRAM AND THE HOME INVESTMENT PARTNERSHP
            PROGRAM

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code Ann.§
10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and

       WHEREAS, on June 13, 2023 the Magna Council made a motion to approve the Interlocal
Cooperation Agreement between Magna Metro Township and Salt Lake County relating to the
Community Development Block Grant Program, Emergency Solutions Grant Program, and the Home
Investment Partnership Program; and

        WHEREAS, the Department of Housing and Urban Development (“HUD”) has informed the
Magna Metro Township that the City of Herriman withdrew from this Interlocal Agreement so a new
resolution authorizing the Mayor to sign the agreement with that change is required; and

        WHEREAS, the Magna Council finds it is in the best interest of the citizens of Magna Metro
township to enter into an Interlocal Agreement between Magna Metro Township and Salt Lake County
relating to the conduct of the Community Development Block Grant Program, Emergency Solutions
Grant Program, and the Home Investment Partnership Program,


      NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council,
Magna, Utah:
        Section 1.     The Magna Metro Township Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to enter
into an Interlocal Agreement between Magna Metro Township and Salt Lake County relating to
the conduct of the Community Development Block Grant Program, Emergency Solutions
Grant Program and the Home Investment Partnership Program as set forth in Attachment A.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Magna Metro Township Council, in Magna, Salt
Lake County, Utah this 12th day of September 2023.
