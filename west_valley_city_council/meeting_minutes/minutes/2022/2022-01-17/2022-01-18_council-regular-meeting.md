COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Jim Vesock stated that he would like to recognize West Valley City Arts. He
    indicated that they do a lot of great events and promote a lot of diversity. He stated
    that he like the presentation by Chief Jacobs in the Strategic Plan and appreciated

    do the same. Mr. Vesock stated that he would like to see overpasses on Bangerter
    Highway so the City could use the money that would need to be raised for an
    underpass to be used elsewhere.

       Randy expressed concern about loud vehicles and suggested that the City put

A.  PUBLIC INTERVIEW OF QUALIFIED CANDIDATES WHO HAVE
    APPLIED FOR THE OPEN COUNCILMEMBER DISTRICT 3 SEAT ON
    THE WEST VALLEY CITY COUNCIL
    Wayne Pyle informed a midterm vacancy has occurred in District 3 with the
    election of Karen Lang as Mayor. He indicated that public notice was given through
    the month of December 2021 and qualified candidates submitted their applications
    January 3, 2022- January 7, 2022.

       Mr. Pyle noted that the Mayor and Council have received a binder that includes a
       declaration of candidacy, a cover letter/letter of intent, a resume, and answers to the
       following questions:

              1. What do you think about taxes and special fees?
              2. Suppose the City received a $1 million grant. What would you do with
                 the money and why?

         3. Please tell us about any activities, specialized skills, knowledge, or
            professional experience that would contribute to your effectiveness in
            serving on the City Council.
         4. What goal would you like to accomplish in your 2-year term and why
            do you think it’s important to accomplish this goal? Will you use your
            own decision-making process or will you seek other opinions?
         5. What are the three most important issues facing the City and why are
            they important? What are your recommended solutions?
         6. West Valley City has grown exponentially in terms of population and

  Mr. Pyle stated that a single question was provided for this questionnaire by each
  member of the Council.

  Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
  information as follows:

         The election of Karen Lang as Mayor of West Valley City has left the office
         of Councilmember District 3 unfilled with a remaining term of two years.
         Section 20A-1-510 of Utah State Code sets forth the procedure for replacing
         midterm vacancies in municipal offices. The City has complied with all
         requirements in the Utah Code including providing proper notice for at least
         two weeks before the meeting to fill the vacancy. The Council has collected
         and reviewed each application for the position and interviewed each
         candidate regarding their qualifications and desire to serve as
         Councilmember District 3.

  The candidates were each given a two-minute open introduction and were then
  asked the following two questions:

         1. Why do you want to be a member of the West Valley City Council?
         2. Describe your vision for West Valley City 50 years from now.

  The candidates were interviewed by the West Valley City Council as follows:

         William Whetstone

         Mr. Whetstone introduced himself to the Mayor and City Council. He
         provided his career background, volunteer background, and explained how
         he believes his experience will help him as a member of the West Valley
         City Council. Mr. Whetstone stated that he is passionate about affordable

       housing and described his goals to maintain a healthy balance of that in
       West Valley City.

       Mr. Whetstone stated he wants to be a member of the West Valley City
       Council because he has always had an inclination to participate in civic
       duty. He stated that he wants to be involved in the City, has grown up with
       the City, and wants to continue the great progress that has already been
       made.

       Mr. Whetstone stated that West Valley City has one of the most diverse
       Cities in Utah. He indicated he would like to see more developments that
       bring different cultures, thoughts, and diversity in the next 50 years. He
       stated he would like to encourage the residents to take pride in the City and
       be involved in creating a strong future.

       Alan Naumann

       Mr. Naumann introduced himself to the Mayor and City Council. He
       provided his career background and volunteer background. Mr. Naumann
       stated that his primary objective as a Councilmember would be to create a
       sustainability department and to help West Valley City become a leader in
       reducing emissions and energy conservation.

       Mr. Naumann stated that he wants to be a member of the West Valley City
       Council because it is the decision-making body and would provide him an
       opportunity to lead. He stated that the Council provides an organizational
       vision to the residents and he feels West Valley City needs to be stronger
       leaders as the second largest City in the State.

       Mr. Naumann stated that he would like to see more community gardens
       since the City will always need that. He indicated that the City will need
       good engineers to develop and design West Valley City for the future. He
       noted that his shorter term 10-20 year goals would be to reduce emissions.
       Mr. Naumann noted that he believes West Valley City will be a strong
       community.

       Rick Anderson

       Mr. Anderson stated that he is a 4th generation resident of West Valley City
       and his father served as Councilmember and Mayor. He indicated that he
       lives and works in District 3. Mr. Anderson discussed the mentors he has in

       involved in the process of repairing the Meadowlands Park.

       Mr. Anderson stated that he has a lot to bring to the West Valley City
       Council. He indicated that he is involved in almost every business or
       industrial business in the City. He stated that the City has a great Police
       Department but crime needs to be addressed. Mr. Anderson stated that most
       large businesses exist in District 3 and he feels it’s important to keep big
       companies coming into the City as well as revenue.

       Mr. Anderson stated that this vision for the future is on the youth of today.
       He indicated that there are some amazing kids in the community and he
       would like to see them continue the legacy that has been built here. Mr.
       Anderson stated that he would like to provide more programs or spaces (like
       the skate park) for the youth of West Valley City.

       Michael Finch

       Mr. Finch stated that he believes elected and appointed officials should be
       visible to the community. He indicated that he will attend every event he is
       able within the City, the County, and the State to represent the residents. He
       noted that he would work to improve the infrastructure of the City and
       would work with the other members of Council as well as staff to make
       good decisions.

       Mr. Finch stated that he wants to be a member of the West Valley City
       Council because he wants to give back to the community. He stated that if
       he is not selected as Councilmember, he still wants to find ways to
       participate and be involved. Mr. Finch stated that he wants to help those in
       need.

       Mr. Finch stated that he likes the track the City is on and would like to see
       that path continue into the future. He stated that infrastructure is important
       and he would want to ensure there is enough water, power, and internet (at
       reasonable costs) for future generations. Mr. Finch noted that he would like
       to continue to see diversity expand and participate in the community.

       Matt MacPherson

       Mr. MacPherson stated that he has lived in West Valley City for 15 years.
       He provided his career background, volunteer background, and explained

       how he believes his experience will help him as a member of the West
       Valley City Council.

       Mr. MacPherson stated that he has the time, devotion, and care to be a
       member of the West Valley City Council. He indicated that he takes pride
       in the City and wants to be more involved. He indicated that the City has
       the best employees and he would like to contribute to bringing in more
       businesses.

       Mr. MacPherson stated that the future of the City starts smaller. He stated
       that when neighborhoods take pride in their community, engage with
       government, and participate in conversations with one another, much can
       be accomplished. He stated that the City does a good job at what they do…
       infrastructure, roads, taxes, communication with the public, zoning, etc. He
       indicated that the best the City and the residents do at meeting those basic
       needs, the stronger, more prosperous, and more beautiful the City will be.

       Jack Matheson

       Mr. Matheson stated that he is proud to live in West Valley City. He
       indicated that he has own 3 homes here, voted for the name of the City when
       it incorporated, and raised his 4 children here. Mr. Matheson stated that he
       served West Valley City on the Planning Commission for 23 years and
       would be honored to serve the residents in this capacity.

       Mr. Matheson stated that he wants to be a member of the Council because
       he knows how the system works. He stated that as a member of the Planning
       Commission, he would make land use recommendations to the Council. He
       noted that he is familiar with the ordinances, City staff, and the
       infrastructure of the City. Mr. Matheson stated that he designed some of the
       roads in West Valley City as an engineer. He noted that he wants to support
       the City and its residents as well as the Council and their efforts over the
       years.

       Mr. Matheson stated that he believes West Valley City will still be the
       second largest City 50 years from now. He indicated that there are still many
       lots that can be infilled, but he would want them to be with nicer homes.
       Mr. Matheson stated that it is also important to bring businesses so the City
       can grow and utilize corporate taxes. He indicated that redevelopment of
       older areas will need to occur. Mr. Matheson stated West Valley City is the
       best City to live in today and will be in 50 years as well.

         John Harvey

         Mr. Harvey stated that he was born and raised in West Valley City. He
         indicated that he has had many opportunities at leadership roles in school,
         church, boy scouts, and professionally. Mr. Harvey indicated that he has
         been selected in leadership roles or been granted awards at various stages
         in his life which shows that his peers believe in him and know that he can
         be an asset.

         Mr. Harvey stated that he has thought about serving on the City Council for
         many years. He indicated that he has helped previous councilmembers
         successfully campaign and felt this was his opportunity to participate. He
         discussed a recent shooting at Hunter High school and stated that there is a
         lot of intolerance in the world. Mr. Harvey stated that he would like to
         bridge the difference, create a different view of government, provide mental
         health support to those that need it, and be involved in the community.

         Mr. Harvey stated that he believes West Valley City will still be the second
         largest City in the State. He indicated that there will be more diversity and
         that will be an important aspect then as it is now. Mr. Harvey stated that
         there is only so much land left to develop in West Valley City so it will be
         important to continue to beautify what we do have. Mr. Harvey stated it’s
         important the City be safe and accessible and West Valley City will still be
         a great place to be.
