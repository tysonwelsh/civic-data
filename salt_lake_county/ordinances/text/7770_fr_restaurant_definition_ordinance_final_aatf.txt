ORDINANCE NO. __________________    ________________________, 202 2 
 
AN ORDINANCE OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL AMENDING  
SECTION 19.04.462 OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY CODE TO INCLUDE 
WEDDING AND RECEPTION USES IN THE DEFINITION OF 
RESTAURANT, LIQUOR LICENSE; AND TO ENACT RELATED 
REGULATIONS  
 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows:  
SECTION I. The amendments made here are designated by underlining the new 
substituted words.  Words being deleted are designated by brackets and interlineations.  
SECTION II.  Section 19.04.462  of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances is amended 
as follows in order to include wedding and reception uses in th e definition of Restaurant, Liquor 
License; and to enact related regulations . 
19.04.462 Restaurant liquor license.  
"Restaurant liquor license" means a public restaurant authorized by the state alcoholic beverage 
control commission as a liquor outlet allowing the storage, sale and consumption of liquor and 
alcohol on the premises. Weddings, receptions, and similar events are included in such use. 
SECTION III. This ordinance shall become effective fifteen days after its passage and 
upon publication of the ordinance, or a summary thereof on the Utah state noticing website.
