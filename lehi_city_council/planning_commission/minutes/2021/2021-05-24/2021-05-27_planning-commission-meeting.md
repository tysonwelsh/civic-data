3. Regular Agenda

3.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Jacob Hansen’s request for review of a General
Plan amendment on 3-acres located at 2029 West 900 North, changing the designation from
VLDRA (very low density residential agriculture) to Commercial.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Hemmert verified that it was all VRLDA. Sierra clarified that there was
commercial overlay, but it does not reach the property.

The applicant, Jacob Hansen, was present. A presentation was prepared and given on Lehi indoor
tennis and pickleball courts. He stated they would like to rezone from VLDRA to Commercial.
They are close to the existing commercial property and feel they can maintain the integrity of the
neighborhood.

Commissioner Hemmert wanted to make sure the applicant knew the difference between the
zones. He also clarified that it was for profit purposes. Commissioner Nielsen asked Kim
Struthers about a DRC comment regarding Neighborhood Commercial and Commercial and if it
would fit the zone. Kim stated it would be well more than the 5,00 foot commercial use. The
applicant stated the buildings have a larger footprint. Kim stated the actual use may not be
incompatible, but the only other option may be a development code amendment with a
conditional use.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

James Anderson stated they have no intention of selling and asked if it was possible to put in
some sort of clause showing they have no intention of selling and are showing they are hoping of
breaking even and are hoping it never goes away. They have no intention of flipping it and going
away. They want to build it and make it pretty for the City and build property values for the City.
They are open for ideas. He asked if it was a reasonable approach. Commissioner Hemmert
stated right now was not the appropriate time for that, and that it may be an amenity. James
stated they are open to ideas to benefit to the community and are hoping that it breaks even and it
a long term commitment and are hoping to find a creative way to keep it open for the
community.

Matt McCallister asked Mr. Anderson about Farmington and Lagoon and having free access and
asked it was legal to be able to make an agreement like that and if so is it something that can be
considered. Commissioner Nielsen stated that it was legal, but the decision was not affected by
that. Matt also asked about Ivory Ridge being similar. Kim stated a planned community zone is
private and must agree to provide some incentives as part of the development. Matt asked about
selling amenities for profit. Matt clarified if there were any agreements made to a club or school.

Alan Wofford stated he has been the tennis coach in Lehi High School since 1991. He stated his
interest in this is purely for the kids. Kids must have something to do, and they have to have a
place to do it. The tennis courts at Willow Creek are full and people want to practice on them.
Tennis is one of the best sports to take care of themselves.

Chase Jones is on the Lehi High School tennis team. He feels like it could bring a lot of
opportunity to kids to succeed.

Caroline stated she is also on the tennis team and would like to see indoor courts. She stated the
wait for indoor courts are hard to find and would like to see courts approved so they can play all
year long.

Megan Warren stated her mom ran a club growing up and she has lifetime friendships and is a
member at Ivory Ridge, but the youth can't play unless they are members at the club. They can't
play unless they have memberships and are members and it would be a great opportunity for the
kids.

Jeremy Albrecht is a coach at Lehi, and he stated it is a big jump from VLDRA to commercial.
He stated he has tried to get into Ivory Ridge many times and feels that it is a profitable concept
for the developer as a commercial use in a residential area. The crowd that tennis attracts is
different than other sports. It won't attract a late night or rowdy crowd, so if an exception is
going to be made, this would be a good exception. This is a different facility where everything
will be contained. This is not a typical commercial business that an exception would be made. It
would be a good use that attracts good people. He would propose that Lehi residents and Lehi
High School students get priority. he would hope that the zoning change would be considered
and thinks that this fits the neighborhood and the access roads.

Lori Le stated she feels there is a large need for pickleball. A girl named Callie Smith won in the
US Open and it was a huge success for the pickleball sport. Lori stated the sport is big in Utah
and it is getting more popular.

Rochelle Moon stated she has five kids, and she loves tennis, and she grew up in Provo where
there was an indoor tennis facility. Her husband and her became a member of Ivory Ridge a few
years ago and they can't get into the courts because it is too full. She stated it is a huge need and
would love it to be part of more people’s lives. She loves the idea of having less fees.

Quinn Jones has lived in Lehi for 15 years and love tennis. There is def a shortage of courts in
Lehi. They have taken lessons and have seen the level of tennis improve when the courts are
available. He would suggest to def consider the development and feels that it would be a benefit
to the community. He pushes his support for it and hopes to get a vote for it.

Dan Reeve stated the site selection has helped Jacob find a site for months and feels that this is
the suitable site for the price. He feels this will be a neighborhood amenity and there will be a for
profit component and that there is a strong need for it. He thinks there are legal ways to make
this happen with a development agreement.

Rudabaugh stated he is in favor of the development and feels that it would be a good asset to the
area if it is done correctly.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation to approve Jacob
    Hansen’s request for review of a General Plan amendment on 3-acres located at 2029
    West 900 North, changing the designation from VLDRA (very low density residential
    agriculture) to Commercial, contingent on the Council on finding a development
    agreement to restrict the usage that the amendment is not detrimental to the public health,
    safety, and welfare of Lehi City, is in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions
    of the General Plan and its various elements, is consistent with the land uses and zoning of
    nearby and adjoining properties, will not have a substantial adverse effect on surrounding
    property; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
    Hemmert added that the nature of the use is unique and no one in the public that was in
    attendance is in opposition and a deficit for recreational opportunity related to this particular
    use. Commissioner Hemmert seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Hemmert,
    yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed 6 – 1.

3.3) Public hearing and consideration of Thomas Pannell’s request for conditional use
approval of the Caliber Collision site plan, a commercial development located at 1614 North
Boston Street.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

Everett asked about the road exception, which was already granted.

The applicant, Thomas Panell, was present. Hemmert asked about off hour deliveries. Tim
Craftson stated 90% of the deliveries would not have afterhours drop. He would like to address
the request for removal for the adjoining lot. There is an electric power fence where the parking to
the south is for service.

Commissioner Nielsen opened and closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Hereth asked about the road connection and the trail connection. Is the trail going
through the property? Kim stated it would be at the south and at a min would still need to provide
a ped connection. Commissioner Hemmert stated it is a permitted use and would need to establish
operating hours and light pollution. The applicant stated it would be code minimum.

Ross asked about the connection to the south. Kim stated the street connection that was granted to
the access aisle would grant cross connectivity. Ross stated the drive aisle can be extended to the
property line and if it isn't installed now, we are saying it is okay to not have it in the future.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker recommended approval of Thomas Pannell’s request for
    conditional use approval of the Caliber Collision site plan, a commercial development
    located at 1614 North Boston Street; noting the cross access connection to the south;
    including conditional use items and the directional lighting so as to not have any excess light
    pollution into residential area, and to limit deliveries from deliveries 6am - 8pm.; including all
    DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
    yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
