Example Motion

I move the Planning Commission approve application #PLZ 26-8010 “Extra Space Storage
Redwood” Amended Commercial Site Plan with the following conditions:

    1. Site Plan may be amended by adding solar panels as shown, with no roof-top screening
       required.

Background

This application is an amendment to an approved site plan for Extra Space Storage (formerly
Towne Storage) located at 13658 South Redwood Road, in the southeast section of the city.
The site is zoned C-G, Gateway Commercial. The applicant proposes to amend the site plan by
adding solar panels to the southeast side of the large central building. All other elements of the
previously approved site plan would remain in place.

In reviewing the proposed site plan amendment, staff identified an area in need of clarification.
The City’s commercial development code requires screening roof-top mechanical, but solar
panels are not specifically mentioned. As solar panels will continue to be added to more
commercial buildings, planning commissioners should evaluate and decide what this
requirement means for solar panels. Residential solar is not required to be screened.

The applicant proposes to install solar panels on the largest building in the center of the site.
This building is separated from residences to the north by approximately 80-feet and from
residences to the south by over 100-feet. There are buildings to the north and south of the
center building that will not have solar panels and will help screen the center building. The
building is approximately 8-feet tall, and the solar panels will be flat on the top with a 4-foot
clearance around each edge of the building.
