#2 Upper High Creek Conditional Use Permit (Alex Vaz)  24 
 25 
Nelson reviewed Mr. Alex Vaz’s request for approval of a conditional use permit (CUP) to allow the 26 
piping of Upper High Creek Canal and the addition of two structures, located along Upper High Creek, 27 
north of Richmond (Agricultural (A10) Zone).   This use falls under “6220 Utility Facility, Distribution” 28 
and is a permitted use in the A10 zone.  Part of this project does fall within Richmond City’s jurisdiction.  29 
The intent is to pipe the existing canal to conserve and better manage the water supply.  The proposed 30 
pipeline will generally follow the existing canal alignment and will range in size from 36” to 15” in 31 
diameter.  At two locations, the new pipeline deviates from the existing canal and follows the County 32 
rights-of-way on High Creek Road and Upper Richmond Road.  There are two buildings proposed to 33 
house irrigation valves and turbines.  This facility will typically operate on a daily basis and include 34 
inspection and maintenance as necessary.  Access to the site is via High Creek Road and Upper Richmond 35 
Road and is adequate.  Any road cuts and work done in the county right-of-way must comply with county 36 
standards.  This has been reviewed by the storm water department of the county and the review indicates 37 
that said realignment may impact adjacent properties and/or structures dependent on the actions of the 38 
property owners in filling in, or leaving and maintaining the abandoned portions of the canal as is.  39 
Notices were sent out and no comment has been received.   40 
 41 
It is common for a conditional use permit (CUP) to be sought when a canal company is going to pipe 42 
water.  This can be a contentious and concerning project and standard permitting process might miss 43 
things.  A CUP requires that the company sit down with everyone and go through all the issues.  The 44 
pipeline is about 3 miles long and some of that pipeline is in Richmond City’s jurisdiction.  Staff noted 45 
that condition number two states that where the existing canal alignment is to be abandoned the Canal 46 
Company must notify those property owners that the canal will no longer be there.  Any problem that may 47 
happen after the canal is moved it is the private property owner’s responsibility and is no longer the 48 
problem of the Canal Company.  Condition number two was edited to read that the Canal Company will 49 
provide evidence of notice to the property owners.   50

#4 Discussion: Title 17.07 - 5100 Recreational Facility 31 
 32 
Runhaar reviewed the current definition of Recreational Facility.  Currently the definition states that 33 
recreational facilities must charge a fee and the county has no way to determine if places are charging fees 34 
so staff is recommending that the fee be taken out and a or be added into the definition. 35 
 36 
Larson motioned to recommend approval to the County Council for the changes to the Recreational 37 
Facility definition as noted; Smith seconded; Passed 5, 1 (Watterson opposed). 38 
 39 
Staff and commission discussed what constitutes a recreational facility.  If you have a family that builds 40 
motocross track and only they use it that is not a recreational facility.  However, if the family builds a 41 
track and opens it and several people every weekend and start to charge to use it; that turns into a 42 
recreational facility.  43
