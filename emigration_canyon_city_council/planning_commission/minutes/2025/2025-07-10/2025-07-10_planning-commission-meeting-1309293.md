OAM2025-001434 – Consideration of an ordinance repealing and replacing Chapter 19.02, General
Provisions and Administration, of the Emigration Canyon Code. Planner: Brian Tucker
(Discussion/Action)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker an analysis of the code
amendment.
Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding renumbering, 19.02.060 and 19.02.080
are two of importance and cleanup of metro and town.
19.02.040.B.2 and E. dwelling groups definition and replace “controls” with “supersedes”, capitalize
building official and planning commission and director, replace “will not be” with “shall not”, and periods
of time calendar days not working days.
19.02.120, rename to Permit and Licensing Requirements, capitalize Zoning Ordinance throughout.
19.02.130 will be removed when 19.16 chapter is amended. 19.02.130.C.3. will keep as is and confirm with
state code with an amendment of 19.16.
19.02.140 replace sidewalk language with walkways, bonds should be determined by project not director
or designee, engineering services should be a defined term, and municipal should be changed to city.
19.02.150 identify scale, revision change direction of north point to cardinal point and add hydrants to
location of existing structures.
