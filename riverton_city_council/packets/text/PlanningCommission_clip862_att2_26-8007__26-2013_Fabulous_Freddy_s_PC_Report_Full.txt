Background

This application is for property located at 4623 West 12600 South in the Commercial Gateway
Zone. The property is 3.79 acres. The applicant is proposing construction of a gas
station/convenience store, with an associated car wash. The property to the south is owned by
the Jordan School District. The property to the west is zoned commercial and is occupied by a
tire store. The property to the east across 4570 is zoned Commercial Neighborhood and is
currently undeveloped. Access to the site is through both 12600 South and 4570 West.

The proposed project includes two building pads. One is the combined c-store and car wash,
and the second is an office/storage building. There is consistent architecture and design
between the two buildings. The convenience store includes a drive-through, and there is outside
patio area. The drive-through lane is separate from the car wash lanes. The site also includes
vacuum stations on the north and south sides of the property.

The architecture is consistent with Riverton City’s commercial architectural standards, and with
the adjacent commercial building. The applicant included in their submittals design drawings for
a pole sign, which requires a site of at least 5 acres. This project area combined with the
adjacent tire store site would meet that threshold, and the two projects share parking and
access. However, the applicant does not have any agreements in place with the owner of the
adjacent property, which are required for a shared pole sign to be approved. Therefore, this
project does not qualify for a pole sign and no approvals granted for one as part of this site plan.

Ingress and egress from the site has been reviewed by the City and complies with traffic
management standards. Internal circulation, including drive through and car wash lanes, have
been reviewed by staff, including the Unified Fire Authority, and are consistent with City
standards and the International Fire Code.

Staff is recommending approval of this application with the conditions outlined above, and will
present additional information and detail at the public hearing.

Recommendations

Staff representing Planning, Fire, Engineering, Water and Storm Water have reviewed these
plans and are recommending approval with the conditions listed above.
