                            MEMORANDUM
To:            Honorable Mayor and City Council
From:          Planning Department
Date:          November 15, 2022
Subject:       Ordinance 22-24 “Funaro Rezone and General Plan Amendment”
               Proposed amendment of the General Plan to Medium Density Residential and
               the zoning from RR-22 to R-4 for 0.5 acres at 1794 West 11800 South.
               Applicant- Peter Funaro.


On October 13, 2022, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this rezone
application. Minutes from that meeting are included in this packet. The Planning
Commission voted to recommend approval, with the following recommended motion:

I move the City Council adopt Ordinance #22-24 amending the General Plan to Medium
Density Residential and the zoning from RR-22 to R-4 for 0.5 acres at 1794 West 11800
South.

Background
This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant for .5 acres at 1794
West 11800 South. The subject property is a residential ½ acre lot with about 125 ft of frontage
on 11800 South at 1794 West. Neighboring properties to the east and west are all zoned RR-
22 but the property sizes range from .3 to .4 acre lots, less than the 0.5-acres required in the
RR-22 Zone. The property to the north is a 1.4-acre lot zoned RR-22. Properties to the
south across 11800 South are zoned in a mix of zones including one lot of RR-22, and several
lots of R-4 and RM-8.

The property is currently zoned “RR-22” (Residential ½ acre). The applicant is requesting this
rezone to R-4 (Residential ¼ acre). There is a small home on the west front of the lot, and the
lot has two accesses onto 11800 South. The lot size is approximately 21,780 sf and would be
appropriate for a private lane and a subdivision. The final result would be two lots of 11,000 sf
and a private lane.

The General Plan designation for the area is Residential with Estate Density (21,780 sf), which
is compatible with the current zoning of RR-22. The applicant is requesting the General Plan
Designation for the property be changed to “Medium Density Residential” which is compatible
with the requested R-4 zoning.

The Planning Commission held a public hearing on this item and voted to recommend approval,
and staff concurs with that recommendation. At the time of this report no public comments have
been received.
 Relevant Ordinances


 18.135.020 Zoning Amendments.
(1) The zoning map and use restrictions may be amended by the city council from time to time, but any
amendment shall be first submitted to the planning commission for its review and recommendation. No
proposed amendment affecting the number, shape, boundary or zoning classification of any zone shall be
adopted unless the proposed zoning ordinance amendment complies with the following criteria:

     (a) The proposed amendment will place all property similarly situated into the same zoning
     classification or in complementary classification.

     (b) That all uses permitted under the proposed zoning amendment are in the general public interest
     and not merely in the interest of an individual or small group.

     (c) All uses permitted under the proposed zoning classifications amendment will be appropriate in the
     area to be included in the proposed zoning amendment.

     (d) The character of the neighborhood will not be adversely affected by any use permitted in the
     proposed zoning classifications.

     (e) The proposed zoning amendment is consistent with the city’s master plan.
