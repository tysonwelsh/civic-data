B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-07-02 Partial 2024 Budget Amendment 
April made a motion to approve a 2024 Budget Amendment. Sara seconded the motion. 
Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUc Board of Commissioners approved the 2024 budget in December 2023. The budget 
included a line item of approximately $87,300 to be used in the creation of a new website for 
HAUC. During 2024 it has become increasingly apparent that our current software is lacking 
and will not support future growth of the agency. HAUC staff is proposing a budget 
amendment in the amount of $87,300 to be redirected toward new software for the agency. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-07-03 Software Contract 
April made a motion to approve the new Software Contract. Sara seconded the motion. 
Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC has conducted substantial research into new software for the agency. This new 
software will manage all HCV and voucher related special programs, Owned Housing property 
management, maintenance work orders, and accounting software in one location. HAUC staff 
has reached out to other agencies to determine the capabilities of the new software and 
HAUC is recommending moving to Yardi Software. 
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-07-04 Springlake Contract 
Sara made a motion to approve the Springlake Contract. April seconded the motion. 
Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC owns and operates a 13-unit complex in Springlake. The property currently has a septic 
system in place that is failing. HAUC employed an engineer to assist in identifying the reasons 
and one of them being an undersized unit. The same engineer assisted in having a new septic 
system installation drawn and submitted to the County Health Department for approval. HAUC 
has researched directly connecting into the sewer system of Payson City for a long-term 
solution but the distance involved is making is prohibitively more expensive. HAUC is 
proposing moving forward with the septic system contract to replace the current failing system.  
 
E. Discuss/Approve Resolution 2024-07-05 Policy: Sale of Used Equipment 
Sara made a motion to approve the Policy: Sale of Used Equipment. Amelia seconded 
the motion. Motion passed unanimously. 
HAUC has used equipment from time to time, needing a policy to dispose consistently, timely 
and eliminate wasteful storage of equipment. Policy outlines valuation, offering to staff and 
public, and the limits of the policy.  Discussion ensued and all questions were answered.
