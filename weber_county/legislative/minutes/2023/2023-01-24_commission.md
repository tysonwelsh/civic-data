                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                   Tuesday, January 24th, 2023 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

       the difficulty in every opportunity. An optimist sees the opportunity in every difficulty.”

G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 10-2023 APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN VALLEY PARK
        SERVICE AREA.
            Stacy Skeen (Commission Office): Five positions are expiring. One position is filled by Huntsville and they
            have appointed William Artie Powell. Three additional applications received. Reposted for the one remaining
            position with a February 19th deadline.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 10-2023 appointing William Artie Powell, Gregory
            Graves, Tim Brown, and Charles Graff to the Ogden Valley Park Service Area; Commissioner Froerer
            seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye

     2. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH RURAL COMMUNITY CONSULTANTS FOR THE WEBER
        COUNTY PRE-DISASTER MITIGATION PLAN UPDATE.
            Lisa Schwartz (Sheriff’s Office): Five year mitigation plan that we are working on for which $125k grant was
            awarded. Through committee review of RFPs, this contract came in at $40k leaving money for contingencies
            should they arise. This has been in work for quite some time.

            Commissioner Bolos moved for approval of a contract with Rural Community Consultants for the Weber
            County Pre-Disaster Mitigation Plan Update; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     3. APPROVAL OF AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENTS WITH THE FOLLOWING FOR
        PARAMEDIC SERVICES:

                                                                    OGDEN FIRE DEPARTMENT
                                                                    WEBER FIRE DISTRICT
                                                                    ROY CITY FIRE DEPARTMENT
         Eli Johnson (Sheriff’s Office): All three contracts are result of budget negotiations that took place this past
         fall. With changes in the county allotment for funding this program as well as modifications to services
         provided by Ogden Fire Department, it was thought to best to reissue the contracts. All three fire departments
         are on board and in agreement.
         Commissioner Bolos moved for approval of an Inter-local Agreements with the above for Paramedic Services;
         Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Freorer – aye
