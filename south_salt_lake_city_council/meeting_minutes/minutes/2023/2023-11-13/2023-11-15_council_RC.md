NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—December 13th @ 7pm. The City Recorder also
          informed the Council and public that Election Day is on Tuesday, November 21st.
          Residents can vote or drop their ballots off at the Salt Lake County Building and the
          Granite Library. City Hall will also be closed on November 23rd and 24th for the
          Thanksgiving holiday.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Visual Art Institute Director, Hana Janatova, talked about the 45-year-old nonprofit
          to the Council. It moved to its newest location in the City in April of this year. It was
          one of the grant recipients for the Art Councils mini grant program. She said that the
          funding was used towards their grand opening on August 19th and was helpful in
          providing free art activities for children and families at the event. She thanked Arts
          Council Director, Lesly Allen and the City for its support as they continue with their
          mission to provide after school art education to children and youth.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood noted that there was currently an opening for a Civilian Review Board
          alternate and that the City is accepting applications on sslc.gov at this time.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          City Attorney, Josh Collins, informed the Council that Brie Brass would be coming
          back as the Deputy City Attorney.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.

              Council Member Huff also wished a happy holiday to everyone.

              welcome to the Council’s newest member representing District 5, Paul Sanchez.

              Council Member Pinkney also wished a happy holiday to everyone and extended her
              welcome to the Council’s newest member, Paul Sanchez.

      6. INFORMATION.

             a. Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District Presentation and Discussion on
                the Regional Water Treatment Plant Drought, Wildfire, and Earthquake
                Resiliency Project
                Deputy General Manager for the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District,
                Jacob Young, went over the project with the Council and asked for their
                feedback. JVWCD supplies 60% of the City’s water supply and serves
                800,000 people throughout Salt Lake County. He highlighted some of the
                Resiliency Project’s focuses and said that they are looking for feedback as
                they continue working on it. A copy of the presentation is attached and
                incorporated by this reference.

              b. Lung Cancer Awareness Month Proclamation
                 Mayor Wood presented the proclamation in recognition of November as
                 Lung Cancer Awareness Month and the importance of awareness, early
                 detection through screenings, and research in lung cancer affecting women
                 and lung cancer health disparities.

              c. Finance Certificate of Achievement for ACFR for FY 2022 Audit
                 Mayor Wood informed the Council that the City has received the certificate
                 of achievement for excellence in financial reporting that’s given by the
                 Government Finance Officers Association for the 2021-2022 fiscal year.

              d. Annual Fraud Analysis
                 Finance Director, Crystal Makin, talked about the annual analysis that is
                 done under the direction of the Utah Office of the State Auditor to assess
                 the fraud risk for the City. The focus of the assessment is to determine if
                 adequate separation of duties exist within each function of cash handling,
                 purchasing, and payroll. The City’s risk level was marked as ‘Very Low’,
                 which is the lowest level that exists in the assessment.

             e. Parks Master Plan Presentation
                Parks Project Manager, Anthony Biamont, showed the Council a PowerPoint
                presentation on the City’s Master Plan for Parks and Recreation. A copy of
                the presentation is attached and incorporated by this reference.

             f.   Neighborhoods/ Arts Council Presentation
                  Neighborhoods Director, Sharen Hauri, spoke about some highlights from
                  this year and gave the floor to Arts Council Director, Lesly Allen, to present
                  the PowerPoint to the Council. A copy of the presentation is attached and
                  incorporated by this reference.

ACTION ITEMS

    Unfinished Business

    1. A Resolution of the South Salt Lake City Council Requesting the Recertification of the
       South Salt Lake Justice Court.
       Justice Court Administrator, Lisa Forrester, gave a summary on this item. The City’s
       Justice Court is due for a recertification with the AOC (Administrative Office of the
       Courts) to show the State that the City will continue to support and fund the court for
       the next four years. It was discussed in the preceding Work Meeting and is ready for the
       Council to take action on.

        Council Member Mila made a motion to approve the Resolution.
