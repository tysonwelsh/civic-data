6.     APPROVE THE FOLLOWING CHANGE TO THE ATTORNEY-CIVIL DIVISION STAFFING
       PLAN: ADD ONE (1) FULL-TIME CAREER SERVICE PARALEGAL I POSITION [ADDING JOB
       CODE 5701 TO ATTY-CIVIL] (Continued from the January 3, 2017 Commission meeting)
(Track A, 08:09)
Utah County Attorney Jeffrey Buhman mentioned if the Board approves this staffing plan change today, it will be a
couple of months before the new person is hired; he will work out the metrics with Utah County Human Resources
Director Cammie Taylor before the position is filled. Commissioners Lee and Graves questioned whether a time-
limited position could fulfill the Attorney’s Office needs, and Mr. Buhman responded there is a lot of training and
associated equipment with the new hire. The commissioners and Mr. Buhman discussed GRAMA requests. Mr.
Buhman noted he does not have overtime funds for his staff; he could offer compensatory time, but the additional
workload is being placed on the attorneys moreso than the support staff.
UTAH COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS
MINUTES – January 10, 2017
Page 5

Commissioner Lee noted it was a surprise to see this request on the agenda; the Commission Office had been
discussing options for the restricted funds. Commissioner Lee pointed out the request should have been made
during the 2017 Budget process to fully fund the position, rather than place it in restricted accounts, if the Attorney’s
Office anticipated this need. Jeffrey Buhman stated he had not realized the Board would want to wait on filling the
position. The commissioners and Mr. Buhman continued discussing metrics, with Mr. Buhman noting the metrics
would be more easily measured if the work is being handled by one person rather than splitting it up among the
office as they are currently doing. Commissioner Ivie asked why Mr. Buhman considers this staffing plan change
the best long-term solution for his office, and Mr. Buhman addressed the increase in GRAMA requests over the last
decade. Mr. Buhman also mentioned that while another attorney will be needed to keep up with the growth of the
county, this addition to the office could delay that need for five or ten more years.

            Commissioner Graves made the motion to continue Regular Agenda Item No. 6
            regarding the Attorney-Civil Division Staffing Plan for two weeks to the
            January 24, 2017 meeting of the Utah County Board of Commissioners. The
            motion was seconded by Commissioner Ivie and carried with the following vote:
                                                                          AYE: William C. Lee
                                                                                   Greg Graves
                                                                                   Nathan Ivie
                                                                          NAY: None

8.      APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO SIGN A MEMORANDUM OF
        UNDERSTANDING WITH THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF WORKFORCE SERVICES FOR
        ESHARE SYSTEM USE (Continued from the December 6, 13, and 20, 2016 Commission meetings)
(Track A, 17:50)
Utah County Information Systems Director Neil Peterson came forward with Utah County Health Department
Director Ralph Clegg and Utah County Deputy Attorney Carl Hollan to address the EShare System contract details.
Mr. Peterson and Commissioner Lee discussed security and online access, with Mr. Peterson explaining there will
be no wireless accessibility whatsoever and the machine will belong strictly to Utah County. Mr. Clegg pointed out
only two individuals will have access on desktop computers: the secretary and the supervisor in the Nurse Family
Partnership program. Commissioner Lee and Mr. Peterson reviewed the changes to the agreement since the first
draft, and Commissioner Ivie asked about the benefit to the county. Mr. Clegg explained access to the records will
help identify who is eligible for financial assistance, and be more efficient in helping needy families.
