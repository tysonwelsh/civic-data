                                     PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant, Merrick Wright, is requesting to add an in-line second story addition to a noncomplying
structure on Parcel #16-01-377-017 which is known as 3876 Emigration Canyon Road. This parcel is
approximately .86 acres in size. The noncomplying structure is an existing single-family dwelling that
does not comply with current setbacks from a stream corridor. Section 19.72.030(J) of the Salt Lake
County Ordinance requires a minimum of 50 feet from a stream corridor. Salt Lake County records
indicate that this house was originally built in 1949 which pre-dates the Forest & Recreation, FR-0.5
Zone along with the Foothills and Canyons Overlay Zone, FCOZ.


                                     EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The application appears to meet the provisions stated in section 19.88.070 of County Ordinance
              - Additions, enlargements, moving and reconstruction of a structure.

A. A noncomplying structure or building occupied by a nonconforming use shall not be added to or
enlarged in any manner or moved to another location on the lot or reconstructed at another location
on the lot except as provided by subsection B of this section unless such additions and enlargements
comply with the regulations and intent of this title.

                                       PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
19.88.150 – Application to have a structure declared a noncomplying structure.

Whenever a structure is in violation of the height or setback provisions of this title, the owner may file
an application with the director or director’s designee to have the structure declared noncomplying.
The director or director’s designee shall approve the application when the evidence clearly establishes
the following:

    a. The structure has existed at its currently location, with the same size, height and setbacks for at
       least ten years:
    b. The structure is found by the county building official or designee to pose no threat to the health
       or safety of persons in or around the structure, and:
    c. Salt Lake County has not taken enforcement action for the violation for a period exceeding five
       consecutive years during which the violation existed.

The MSD Planning Staff has assessed the age of the building which dates back to 1949. This satisfies
item 19.88.150(a) and predates the ordinance that it is noncomplying to.

The proposed (Second story) addition will not encroach any closer to Emigration Canyon Creek than
the existing structure. The addition to the existing structure will not pose any new threat to the
health/safety of those occupying and neighbors located within the vicinity of the new structure. This
satisfies item 19.88.150(b)

Our records indicate that no enforcement action or violation has ever taken place on this property. This
satisfies item 19.88.150(c).

Scope of Work - Remodel expansion of existing home

We hope to obtain approval to complete an in-line vertical addition to the home,
which was built in 1946, and then expanded in 1986. The property is essentially flat
upon nearly one acre with Emigration stream running through the property. The
rear of the property parallels Sunnydale Lane, and the Frontage is along Emigration
Canyon road. The land to building ratio gives the site a large land area compared to
the improvements, on of the largest in the lower canyon.

The home is located within 50 feet of the creekbed, which is “officially designated as
an “intermittent stream” (according to SL tribune Sept 08, 2018 quoting Emigration
Township mayor Joe Smolka). We respectfully ask for approval to make vertical
addition to the home. This will not increase the floor area of the ground level of the
home except beyond 50 feet from the stream. Our plan will not impinge upon the
stream beyond what has been historically present at the site, and will not increase
environmental impact .

The existing home is a 3 bedroom 3 bath home served by a 2,000 gallon septic
system which has been inspected and deemed to be in good condition.

There is a fire hydrant located less than 500 feet from the property. Access to
emergency response is easy, as there are two entrance gates on emigration canyon
road, and the site is fairly flat with a shoulder of 10 feet along frontage.

Premlinary discussion with County health dept. has indicated that the size of the
existing system is sufficient to serve the addition as anticipated, as there will not be
an increase in the number of bedrooms.

The proposed addition will be built above existing living area along the stream side,
extending forward 10 feet (5 feet of which is covered walkway now) towards
Emigration canyon road frontage to the North side of the home.

The net result of the addition/remodel will be that the home will continue to be a 3
bedroom home, with one additional ½ bath.

There will be no addition above about ½ of the main level living area, and garage.

Spaces on the main level would be enlarged and opened up allowing for a larger
family/gathering, entertainment area around the expanded kitchen/dining area, as
is desired in modern open flow living environments.

The addition would have a metal roof, and cement board exterior finish, affording
enhanced fire protection, as well as new windows and insulation that will mitigate
the effects of sound from the Roadway, which has increased geometrically since the
home was built.
Most of the homes below Ruth’s restaurant, are located along the stream, and a
significant majority are more than single level structures. Most stream side homes
have been excavated to provide two levels of home facing the creek, and some have
3 levels. I have taken pictures of the homes up canyon and down canyon and
illustrated a plat map showing in orange, the homes that have more than 1 story in
the attached package.

The size of the subject property, and its flat geography provide a site that can
accommodate vertical expansion of the improvement, without imposing any burden
on the surrounding environment.

It is reasonable to approve this request as there will be no increase in
encroachment upon the stream-way, and the improvements will be made with
modern materials designed to enhance safety, and enjoyment for the property
owner.
