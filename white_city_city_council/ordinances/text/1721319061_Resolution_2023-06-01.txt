White City Metro Township
RESOLUTION NO.: 2 3-06-01 DATE: June 1, 2023
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO COUNCIL
APPROVING THE APPOINTMENT OF ROBERT FRAILEY AND
NICK WEST AS FULL-TIME PLANNING COMMISSION
MEMBERS OF THE PLANNING COMMISSION FOR THREE-
YEAR TERMS ENDING FEBRUARY 28, 2028
Whereas, the White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.
Whereas, pursuant to Ordinance 17-06-02, Planning Commission Members are appointed by the
Metro Township Council to serve three-year terms;
Whereas, on this date, the White City Metro Council desires to appoint Robert Frailey and Nick
West as Full-Time Planning Commission Members with terms ending February 28, 2028.
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
approves the appointment of Robert Frailey and Nick West as Full-Time Planning Commission
Members with terms ending February 28, 2028.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
1* day of June 2023.
