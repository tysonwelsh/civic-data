REQUEST
The Sandy City Parks and Recreation Department has submitted an application for Master Plan
approval and a preliminary site plan review for Main Street Park. The park consists of .92 acres.
The applicant is requesting that the Planning Commission evaluate the phasing/master plan for
future modifications to the park. The applicant is also requesting that Phase A be approved with
an approval by the Commission and then move forward to final review with staff immediately
after the Commission’s approval.

BACKGROUND
The proposed project is located at 70 E. 8760 South. The park is located in the R-1-7.5(HS) zone.
The park was established prior to the current Sandy City ordinance that an official site plan review
be conducted by the Planning Commission. Now they are proposing to update portions of the park
as some conditions have either changed or deteriorated. Proposed changes include refurbishing the
pavilion, relocating a power panel, installing a new irrigation system, replacing the curb and gutter,
new monument signs, and also replacing the four-foot sidewalk with a new five-foot sidewalk.
This proposal is accompanied by a request for a conditional use permit which is also on this
meeting’s agenda (CUP12032021-006219).

ANALYSIS

Overview:
The master plan is proposed with five stages, labeled A through E. They do not have to be done in
a particular order. A breakdown of the proposed improvements is below:

       Phase A: Includes refurbishing the existing pavilion as well as relocating the existing power
       panel away from being adjacent to the sidewalk and relocating it to the south side of the
       pavilion. The modifications to the pavilion also call for removing the grills currently
       located inside, as there is not sufficient venting for them. Instead there will be grilling areas
       located at the southwest and southeast corners of the pavilion, but not within it. A new
       monument sign in the northwest planter is also proposed in this phase.

       Phase B: Includes installing sidewalk along the playground area to make the playground
       more walkable. A water meter will also be relocated. A new monument sign in the northeast
       planter is also proposed in this phase.

       Phace C: Replacing the irrigation system for the lawn. Also installing a concrete slab that
       will ultimately support the new stage.

       Phase D: Replacing curb and gutter, as well as removing the existing four-foot sidewalk
       and replacing it with a new five-foot sidewalk. City standard bulb-outs will be constructed
       on the corners.

       Phase E: Installation of the stage, which will remain there permanently.

What is being proposed is a master plan for future improvements. That means that when each
phase is to be initiated the applicant will work with City staff for final approval for each particular
phase of development.

Access/Parking:
The park can be accessed from all sides of the parcel. There is parallel parking available on each
side of the park.

Open Space:
All of the current open space will remain except for the small portion that will comprise the park’s
permanent stage. The new irrigation system will aid in adequately supplying water to the existing
open space/live plant material.

CONCERNS
Staff has no concerns with the proposed phased master plan and site plan.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the Master Plan and determine that the
preliminary site plan review is complete for the proposed Main Street Park, located 70 E. 8760
South, based upon the following conditions:

Conditions:

    1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout the
       preliminary and final review process and that all issues be resolved before final site plan
       approval for each phase.

    2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to during the review,
       construction and operations process of this building.

    3. That each phase of development go through the final review process with staff before the
       improvements of each phase are initiated.
