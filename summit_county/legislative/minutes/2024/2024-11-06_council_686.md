2)
Discussion and action regarding an appeal of an Amendment to a Conditional Use Permit for Major Accessory Building located at 7914 N. Greenfield Drive, in Silver Creek Estates; Appellants Cyrus and Julie DeVere; Lynda Viti, Deputy County Attorney (4:29 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Lynda Viti, Civil Deputy Attorney, presented history and background information regarding an appeal of an Amendment to a Conditional Use Permit for a Major Accessory Building for discussion and action.
Robert A. Saunders, representing Cyrus and Julie DeVere, Appellants, addressed the Council and provided additional information for discussion.
Cyrus DeVere, Appellant, addressed the Council providing information about the setback of the building location.
Council Chair Stevens asked questions about the standards for approval for the amendment to a CUP. Planner, Jennifer Leslie responded to Chair Stevens question.
Dave Thomas, Civil Chief Deputy Attorney, provided additional information to the Council for discussion.
Julie DeVere, Appellant, addressed the Council.
Peter Barnes, Community Development Director addressed the Council. Discussion continued.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to grant the appeal and find that the planning commission erred in denying the amendment for the conditional use permit and grant the amendment for the conditional use permit with the added conditions being that to mitigate the impacts of buffering on other land uses by placing the barn of the accessory building with a setback of 90 feet from the rear yard setback, the east line, and 20 feet from the side yard north setback line, rotate the barn and make this conditioned upon a new drawing showing the exact location consistent with these findings and conclusions to be prepared by the appellant, reviewed by the planning staff, with the ultimate decision that it complies in the Chair; in that the County Attorney's Office has requested to make findings and conclusions consistent with the Council's discussion and decision Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Abstain: Roger Armstrong.
Discussion was held on the motion regarding the rotation of the barn with the gabled ends facing east and west.
