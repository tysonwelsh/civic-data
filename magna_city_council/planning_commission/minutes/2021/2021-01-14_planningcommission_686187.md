CUP2020-000104 - Robert Boldt is requesting Conditional Use approval for an Oversized Accessory
Building (2,000 square foot garage), for a parcel greater than ½ acre. Acreage: 0.98 acres. Location: 9047
West 3200 South. Zone: R-1-6 (Residential). Planner: Diana Martinez (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Richards asked if the accessory buildings are under 25%. Ms. Martinez confirmed the only
thing on the property is a home. Commissioner Richards asked about the garage, Commissioner Lockwood
confirmed it is a new drive and new garage. Commissioner Cripps confirmed he is building outside of the
easement. Commissioner Richards asked if there is a requirement for fencing. Ms. Martinez said there is
not a requirement and have never put a requirement on accessory buildings.

to abide. For storage, trailer, or boat. Improve the property, western skill, quality, everything will look good
and upkeep the property.

Commissioner Richards asked about the side awning and open. Mr. Boldt said covered on west and south
side and just the front will be open and enclosed. Looking at a carport, 1200 square foot building with

setbacks and property line. Commissioner Lockwood confirmed purchasing from western steel, metal
siding. Mr. Boldt confirmed prefab, low maintenance. Commissioner Richards asked if there is electricity
to the building. Mr. Boldt confirmed just electricity and water, possibly a propane tank.

EXP2020-000118 - Tamara Gunderson is requesting approval of a Special Exception to have a Non-
Conforming Use of a two-family dwelling declared legal. Acreage: 0.20 acres. Location: 3348 South 7780
West. Zone: R-1-6 (Residential). Planner: Diana Martinez (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Lockwood asked if this makes this legal, only for this property. Ms. Martinez confirmed that
is correct. Commissioner Lockwood said typical for ADU is owner occupied and do not understand their
property. Commissioner Cripps said according to requirements, they must have a building permit and
review and require upgrades and meeting code requirements. Commissioner Lockwood said number one is
difficult and discouraged. Mr. McNulty said six months out with ADU ordinance, being proactive, but will
not happen for six months for Magna, the planning commission can look at conditions they feel are needed
to mitigate the impacts and can stand on their own. Commissioner Weight asked if the basement parking
comes in from the neighbor’s driveway. Ms. Martinez confirmed it does not and is their property.
Commissioner Richards asked if there has been issues with code enforcement. Ms. Martinez confirmed went
back 10 years and there has never been an issue.
