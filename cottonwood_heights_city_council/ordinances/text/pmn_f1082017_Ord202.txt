COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-09
A RESOLUTION APPROVING A BANK ACCOUNT

WHEREAS, the city council (the “Council”) of the city of Cottonwood Heights (the
“City”) met in regular session on 6 February 2024 to consider, among other things, approving a
new account (the “Account’) for the City with Zions First National Bank (“Zions”) and those
authorized to sign on and make changes to the Account; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interests of the City and its constituents to so act;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the city council of the city of Cottonwood
Heights that the City is authorized to open the Account with Zions as described in the proposed
letter to Zions from the City’s manager (the “Manager’) attached as an exhibit to this resolution
(the “Lefter”); and be it

FURTHER RESOLVED by the city council of the city of Cottonwood Heights that the
Manager is authorized and directed to execute and deliver the Letter to Zions, and that the City’s
officers and employees are authorized and directed to take such further actions as Zions may
require to open the Account as described in the Letter.

This Resolution, assigned no. 2024-09, shall take effect immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND APPROVED effective 6 February 2024.
