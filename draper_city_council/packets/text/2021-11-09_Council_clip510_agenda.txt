Public Hearing: Ordinance #1505 and #1506 A request by Ralph Johnson of Utah Capital LLC to create a new commercial special district and rezone the subject property as the Crosspoint Commercial Special District.  The proposed district standards will be located in Chapter 9-18 of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) and the new zoning designation would be Crosspoint Commercial Special District (CSD-CP).  The property is located at approximately 166 E. Highland Drive in the Regional Commercial (CR) zoning district. This application is known as Crosspoint CSD Zoning Map and Text Amendments (Legislative Action).  Staff report by Maryann Pickering. a 6. Public Hearing: Pye Plat Amendment Public Access Easement Modification (Legislative Action) On the request of Jason Pye for a modification to the public access easement located at approximately 1842 East 12200 South. Public Hearing: Ordinance #1507 A request by Nathan Abbott, representing Galloway & Co., Inc. for approval of a text amendment in the CSD-136C zone regarding text changes to Title 9-18L of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) development standards, parking design, lighting design, and sign sections. This application is known as the Kum & Go Store #2500 Text Amendment Request (Legislative Action).  Staff report by Maryann Pickering.  a 8. Action Item: Resolution #21-60 A resolution stating the intent of Draper City to hold a public hearing on the request to adjust the common boundary line with Lehi City. This municipal boundary adjustment would relocate approximately 163 acres of land from Draper City into Lehi City. The properties are parcel numbers 110-150-007 and 110-150-008 (Legislative Action).
