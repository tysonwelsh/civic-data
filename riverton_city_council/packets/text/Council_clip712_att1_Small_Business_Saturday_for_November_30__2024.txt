                                 Small Business Saturday 2024

WHEREAS, The importance of small businesses should not be taken for granted as they make
essential contributions to our local economy and community: and

WHEREAS, According to the United States Small Business Administration, there are 32.5
million small businesses in the United States, they represent 99.7% of all firms with paid
employees and they are responsible for 62% of net new jobs created since 1995; and

WHEREAS, it is estimated that at least $0.55 of every dollar spent in the local economy stays in
the local community; and

WHEREAS, Riverton’s economy is supported by a vast number of small businesses that are key
to supporting the economic vitality of our great city and prosperity of our citizens; and

WHEREAS, Riverton assists small businesses by eliminating fees for regular businesses and
actively participating in the South Valley Chamber; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim November 30,
2024, as Small Business Saturday in Riverton and ask the residents of our community do their
part to support small businesses and merchants located in Riverton on Small Business
Saturday and throughout the year.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 12th day of November 2024.
