         The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agenda for the special City Council meeting scheduled to begin
at 6:00 p.m.; receive an update regarding the Zone Ogden project; discuss proposed Lester Park improvements; discuss the
dissolution of expired redevelopment project areas; discuss Council/Board business – specifically proposed Council rules and
norms amendments; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair White and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m. There was a focus on item 4.a, proposed amendments to the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026
budget pertaining to Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) funding.

Update – Zone Ogden
          Senior Planner Rypien explained the purpose of the Zone Ogden project is to update the City’s development codes; the
project began in summer of 2023 and is currently in the drafting stage and Planning staff is aided by consultants from the firm
Logan Simpson. In the fall of 2023, the consultant performed an assessment of the City’s existing development codes to identify
areas of the code that should be amended to comply with Utah Code and to find areas that could be simplified and updated based
on best practices. Since Fall of 2023 and throughout 2024, the Planning Division and consultant have held several public open
house meetings to inform attendees and to receive public input. Planning and the consultant have also had focus group discussions
with residents, landowners, developers, architects, engineers, contractors, and realtors to understand current trends of the
development industry and to seek input on the code update. The Planning Commission has reviewed the draft chapters of the
Unified Development Code during their monthly work session meetings. The last date the Planning Commission reviewed the
draft chapters was June 18, 2025. Once the code is completely drafted there will be a final public open house, and the Planning
Commission will hold a public hearing to consider the changes to existing development codes. Mr. Rypien then used the aid of a
PowerPoint presentation to provide an overview of the major changes and reorganization of the code.
          For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Throughout his presentation, Mr. Rypien engaged in discussion of the proposed updates with the Council regarding
public involvement opportunities in the code update process and proposed development standards in different zones of the City.
Chair White informed the Council there will be future meetings scheduled with Planning staff and there will be multiple
opportunities for the Council to ask more questions of Planning staff regarding the proposed code updates. She thanked Mr.
Rypien for the information he provided tonight.

Proposed Lester Park Improvements
          Public Services Executive Director Anderson explained the redesign of Lester Park has been underway for several years
and the Administration has worked with IO Landarch Landscape and Urban Design Studio to create concept design and vision
documents to show proposed improvements to Lester Park. The Administration has participated in community events and has
conducted outreach surveys focused on gaining public input about the park’s redesign and is seeking council input before moving
forward with the design. Mr. Anderson introduced Shalae Larsen of Landarch, who used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to
review the history of the project, present updated conceptual designs, and solicit input and feedback from the Council regarding
the design and vision for the project. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Discussion among the Council, Mr. Anderson, and Ms. Larsen centered on shifts in the design since the Council’s last
review of the proposal; new amenities added to the project; and the costs/staff time associated with maintenance of the new
facility.

Dissolution of Expired Redevelopment Project Areas
         Economic Development Director Meess explained City Administration is proposing the Board approve a resolution
dissolving and terminating five expired redevelopment and economic development areas. Formal termination of expired areas is
required by state law. The collection period for these project areas has expired and no additional tax increment is being collected
by the Agency. The areas proposed for termination are:
               • 12th Street Redevelopment Project Area – 1987-2022;
               • South CBD Redevelopment Project Area – 1987-2022;
               • South Wall Economic Development Area – 2013-2022;
               • Lincoln Redevelopment Project Area – 1987-2024;
               • Fairmount Redevelopment Project Area – 1992-2024.
         Ms. Meess used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide information on the value increase in each of the areas
over the course of the project area’s life. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Discussion among the Board and Ms. Meess centered on some of the improvements projects completed in a few of the
project areas.

Council/Board Business
          Proposed Council Rules and Norms Amendments: Council Senior Policy Analyst Burton explained that during a
Council strategy session in April 2025, the Council expressed interest in updating its Rules and Norms to give the Chair discretion
to adjust time limits for public input, in order to help manage meetings more efficiently. In addition to this proposed change,
Council staff have proposed further updates to the Rules and Norms, including language addressing the use of employee images
on social media and clarifying that the use of media recordings is not permitted during the public comment portion of meetings..
          Mr. Burton used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to display specific language amendments in the Rules and Norms
document; for a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting. The Council engaged in brief
discussion of the merits of the proposed amendments and concluded to proceed with consideration of the proposal at a future
business meeting.
          Hof, Germany Trip: Council staff provided the Council with information about the upcoming Hof, Germany trip
itinerary and the best way to communicate with one another during the trip.
          Council Calendar: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reviewed the Council calendar for the month of July, with a
focus on the week of the Pioneer Days events.
          Roy City Annexation Policy Plan Amendment: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported Roy City has been
considering amendments to their annexation policy plan, which could have some impact on the City due to Roy City’s proximity
to the Ogden Airport. Council leadership has asked Council staff to draft a letter to the Roy City Council expressing Council
concerns regarding the matter and Council staff will give the entire Council the opportunity to review the letter before it is sent.


         The meeting adjourned at 6:00 p.m.
