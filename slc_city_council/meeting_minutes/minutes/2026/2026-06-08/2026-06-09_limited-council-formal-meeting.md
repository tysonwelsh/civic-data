**Resolution: Appointing a Member of the Salt Lake City Council, District Four Vacancy**
The Council will consider adopting a resolution appointing a new member of the Salt Lake City Council to fill the term until January 4, 2028 of the vacated office representing District Four and administration of the Oath of Office.

**FYI – Project Timeline:** (subject to change per Chair direction or Council discussion)
Briefing - Tuesday, June 9, 2026
Set Public Hearing Date - n/a
Hold hearing to accept public comment - n/a
TENTATIVE Council Action - Tuesday, June 9, 2026

  Staff Recommendation - Suspend the rules and consider motions.

**Summary:**

Council Member Puy noted a Scrivener’s error in the agenda language and clarified that the term end date was January 3, 2028.

Keith Reynolds, City Recorder, executed the Oath of Office for Jennifer Napier-Pearce to the Salt Lake City Council, District Four, with a term ending January 3, 2028.

 **Motion:
Moved by Council Member Wharton, seconded by Council Member Dugan to **adopt Resolution 11 of 2026, appointing Jennifer Napier-Pierce as a Member of the Salt Lake City Council, to fill the remaining term ending January 3, 2028 of the vacated District Four seat.****

**AYE:** Victoria Petro, Alejandro Puy, Chris Wharton, Erika Carlsen, Daniel Dugan, Sarah Young

**Final Result:**6 – 0 Pass
