NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—July 31st @ 7 p.m.

           July 15th – Energy Savings Workshop will be held at the South Salt Lake Community
           Center from 6-7 p.m.

           July 29th – Monday with the Mayor will take place at Fitts Park from 6:30-7:30 p.m.

           August 6th – National Night Out will take place at the Central Park Community
           Center from 5-7:30 p.m.

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          None

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood spoke about the cooling down zones that the City has prepared for
          residents and the importance of staying cool and hydrated this summer.
          Information about the locations can be found on the City’s website and social
          media.

           She talked about how a group of city employees and their Mayor from Evansville,
           Indiana came to tour and take notes from the Promise South Salt Lake’s facilities as
           the recipients of the Federal Promise grant.

           She also recognized South Salt Lake employee, Richard Bullock, for his role in calling
           in a suspicious situation and contacted the South Salt Lake dispatch. Thanks to his
           actions, an arsonist that had an APB (All Points Bulletin) out for them was identified
           and arrested.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Williams thanked all the staff and volunteers who worked to put
          the July 4th celebrations together.

           Council Member Bynum also thanked the staff and volunteers who helped with the
           July 4th celebrations. She also reminded the Council Members about honoring the
           Code of Conduct, Section 2 where it talks about limiting communication with city
           staff to business hours and scheduling meetings in advance in order to respect the
           work-life balance and prevent any unhealthy power dynamics.

           Council Member Huff also thanked the staff and volunteers who helped with the
           July 4th celebrations.
