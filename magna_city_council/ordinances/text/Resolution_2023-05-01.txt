MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 2023-05-01 DATE: May 9, 2023
A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
GRANTING AN EXCEPTION TO THE REQUIRMENT TO
INSTALL PUBLIC IMPROVEMENTS ON 2820 SOUTH STREET
FOR DAKOTA LANE ONE LOT SUBDIVISION
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and
WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah
Code Ann.§ 10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and
WHEREAS, the Dakota Lane One Lot Subdivision Amended plat was approved by the
Magna Metro Township Planning Commission on October 14", 2021; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to section 14.12.020 of the Magna Code, installation of street
improvements, consisting of curb, gutter, sidewalk, and asphalt tie-in for the property frontage
along 2820 South Street was required as a condition of subdivision approval; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Public Works Engineering Division has reported to
the Magna Metro Township Council that a federally funded street improvement project has
been approved and is under way for the improvements, consisting of curb, gutter, sidewalk,
and asphalt tie-in, on the north side of said 2820 South Street; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Public Works Engineering Division has further
reported that the installation of improvements on 2820 South Street along the frontage of the
Dakota Lane One Lot Subdivision would at this point impede rather than aid the
aforementioned street improvement project; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 14.12.150 of the Magna Code, the Magna Metro
Township Mayor (acting as chair of the Magna Metro Township Council) may grant
exceptions to the requirements of Chapter 14.12 after receiving recommendations from the
planning commission and the public works engineer; and
WHEREAS, the planning commission and public works engineer have both forwarded
positive recommendations regarding the request for exception submitted by the subdivider; and
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council believes that installation of the
improvements along 2820 South Street in one, contiguous funded project would result in the
installation of better quality improvements than if done through multiple projects.

NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council,
Magna, Utah as follows:
An exception to the requirement to install public street improvements along the frontage of 2820
South Street for the Dakota Lane One Lot Subdivision is hereby granted, provided that the
property owner sign an authorization form granting access to the subject property for the
installation of the improvements associated with the street improvement project.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Magna Metro Township Council, in Magna, Salt
Lake County, Utah this 9" day of May 2023.
