   4. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE POWDER MOUNTAIN WATER & SEWER DISTRICT –
      RESOLUTION 4-2016 7
       Stacy Skeen, of the Commission Office, stated that she had posted notices for the three vacancies.
       Commissioner Gibson noted that at the onset of this board there were no full-time residents. Chris Crockett,
       Deputy County Attorney, had not seen the applications but stated that the requirements would be that the
       applicants either live in the district or be an owner/agent of land receiving services from the district. If 50%
       or more of the homes there are considered seasonable then the requirement is that half of the board members
       be permanent residents and receive services. This changes if the County Commission wants to sit on that
       board—they can then appoint between 1-3 individuals to the board and the remaining have to be land
       owners/agents. One current member on this board is a permanent resident and the other is an owner/agent
       of land. Chair Ebert invited interested parties to speak and no one spoke.
       Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 4-20167 reappointing Commissioner Kerry Gibson, and
       appointing Jeff Werbelow and Michael Geary to the Powder Mountain Water and Sewer District;
       Commissioner Gibson seconded.
       Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
