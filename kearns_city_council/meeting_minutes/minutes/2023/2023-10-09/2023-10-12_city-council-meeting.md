Presentation – SLVLESA Tax Increase

                Rachel Anderson, Legal Counsel, Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service
Area (SLVLESA), reviewed the Resolution. She explained there is a provision in Utah Code that
requires SLVLESA to report to municipalities and seek their approval prior to implementing a tax
increase. If any municipalities do not approve, SLVLESA may still proceed provided it receives a
majority vote and a two-thirds vote from the Salt Lake County Council. She has already reported
to Magna Metro Township, and the municipalities will vote in a couple weeks.

Ms. Anderson explained the tax increase is necessary due to the implementation of HB374 and
inflation. Equipment, vehicles, and personnel are all more expensive.

              Richard Moon, Treasurer, SLVLESA, distributed a handout entitled SLVLESA
Tax Increase 2024. He reviewed: Information for the Tax Increase; Purpose of the Tax Increase;
SLVLESA History 2021-2024; Law Enforcement History; Fiscal Year 2022 UPD Budget for
SLVLESA; Fiscal Year 2023 UPD Budget for SLVLESA; Fiscal Year 2024 Budget for SLVLESA;
Fiscal Year 2024 UPD Budget for Kearns.

                                  KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO. 2023-10-01                                                 DATE: October 12, 2023

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       A 2024 TAX RATE IN EXCESS OF THE CERTIFIED TAX RATE BY THE SALT
       LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA

WHEREAS, the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (“SLVLESA”) was created by
Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council dated August 18, 2009; and

WHEREAS, the area of the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is included in the boundaries of
SLVLESA for the funding and provision of law enforcement services to the areas within the
boundaries of SLVLESA; and

WHEREAS, SLVLESA has proposed to budget a 7% increase in the tax rate in excess of the
certified rate as defined in Section 59-2-024 of the Utah Code in 2024 to fund the increased
costs of providing local law enforcement services and has proposed to increase property taxes
solely for the purposes of maintaining its fund balance, paying increases in the costs assessed
to SLVLESA by the Unified Police Department and, as needed, to other providers such as a
newly reinstituted Salt Lake County Sheriff’s Bureau, and allowing new growth in its tax base to
be used for the provision of required new services; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 17B-I-1003 of the Utah Code, SLVLESA submitted a report on
the proposed tax increase to the Kearns Council (“Council”) at a duly noticed meeting on October
9, 2023; and

WHEREAS, the Council allowed time during the duly noticed meeting on October 9, 2023, for
comment on the proposed tax increase from members of the Council and the public; and

WHEREAS, Section 17B-2a-903 of the Utah Code requires the prior approval of an increase in
the certified rate by SLVLESA from the legislative bodies of any municipality and county whose
territory is located within SLVLESA, or approval of a majority of the municipalities and two-thirds
(2/3) of the County legislative body; and

WHEREAS, before the SLVLESA Board of Trustees considers to levy a tax rate that exceeds
the certified tax rate, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees shall hold a public hearing to provide to
all interested parties the opportunity to be heard regarding the proposed tax rate increase; and

WHEREAS, SLVLESA shall provide notices of the public hearing as required by Section 59-2-
919 of the Utah Code; and

DATE           THURSDAY                             OCTOBER                               12, 2023

WHEREAS, after considering all public input from the public hearing and all other information
available to them, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees will vote on whether to levy a tax rate that
exceeds the certified tax rate.

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL that effective immediately:

   1. The Kearns Metro Township Council hereby approves for 2024 the budgeting of a 7%
      increase in property tax revenues in excess of revenues budgeted in the prior year and
      the imposition of property taxes in excess of the certified tax rate to generate the increase
      in budgeted property tax revenues as defined in Section 59-2-924 of the Utah Code.

Planning/Engineering

File Number REZ2023-000970 Rezone of property located at 5622 South 5600 West

               Brian Tucker, Planning Manager, Municipal Services District, delivered a
PowerPoint presentation on a rezone of approximately eight acres, located at 5622 South 5600
West. The presentation was entitled Kearns Council – October 12, 2023. He reviewed REZ2023-
000970; background; the general plan; existing conditions; the proposed solution; an analysis of
the general plan; analysis of the status quo; analysis of the Neighborhood Mixed Use (NMU);
analysis of the commercial shopping center (C-2); analysis of the corridor mixed use (CMU);
review procedure and criteria; staff recommendation; OAM2023-000974; a summary; MSD
recommendation; resolution; summary; and recommendation.

            Mayor Kelly Bush stated her main concern was density, as Kearns is already the
densest community in Salt Lake County.

              Council Member Peterson stated he preferred to table this and refer it back to
the Planning Commission.

                Council Member Schaeffer stated he, like Council Member Butterfield, would
prefer not to have apartments. He would prefer to rezone to a CMU because there is no tax base
in that area.

              Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to
approve ordinance no. 2023-10-01. The motion carried by the following rollcall vote:

Aye:   Council Member Butterfield, Council Member Schaeffer, Mayor Bush

Nay:   Council Member Peterson

                                          RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the five Metro
Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of Brighton; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate zoning
and subdivisions in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103(2); and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township has authority to adopt zoning regulations, including a
zoning map pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-501 in accordance with the Municipal Land
Use, Development, and Management Act, (“MLUDMA), Title 10, Section 9a, Utah Code, to
protect public health, safety, and welfare, and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission held a public hearing on
September 18, 2023, took public comment, and made a recommendation as required by Utah
Code Ann. § 10-9a-503(2); and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission recommended that the property
at 5622 South 5600 West be rezoned to the Shopping Center Commercial (C-2) zone based on
a desire to increase the commercial tax base and eliminate the potential for exclusively
residential development of the property; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council may adopt or reject a land use regulation
recommended by the Planning Commission or make revisions that the legislative body considers
appropriate pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-502(2); and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council has received additional information that
indicates that residential uses can be built in the Shopping Center Commercial (C-2) zone when
part of a Mixed Use Planned Unit Development at that, unlike the Corridor Mixed Use (CMU)
zone, there are no density maximums in the Shopping Center Commercial (C-2) zone; and

WHEREAS, the Council deems it necessary to amend its zoning map to allow and encourage
commercial development in order to increase the commercial tax base, and for the protection
and preservation of the public health, safety and general welfare.

1.       Section 19.14.020, The Zoning Map of the Kearns metro Township is hereby amended
as follows:

Council and located within the Kearns Metro Township, is hereby reclassified from the
Residential Multi-Family (R-M) zone to the Corridor Mixed Use (CMU) zone, said property being
described as follows:

                                 KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO. 2023-10-02                                               DATE: October 12, 2023

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       CORRECTING A SCRIVENER’S ERROR IN SECTION 19.28.020, SCHEDULE
       OF PERMITTED USES MISTAKENLY INDICATING THAT SHORT TERM
       RENTALS ARE ALLOWED AS PERMITTED USES IN THE R-1-7 AND R-1-8
       ZONES

                                          RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate zoning
and subdivisions in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103(2); and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has the authority to pass all
ordinances and rules, and make all regulations, not repugnant to law, necessary for carrying into
effect or discharging all powers and duties pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §10-8-84; and

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township adopted an ordinance repealing and replacing Titles
18 and 19 of the Kearns Metro Township Code of Ordinances, effective June 26, 2023; and

WHEREAS, early drafts of Title 19, specifically Chapter 19.28, Single Family Residential Zones,
had contemplated the possibility of Short Term Rentals in certain residential zones and had
consequently been included in the use table in subsection 19.28.030; and



                                               8
DATE           THURSDAY                             OCTOBER                              12, 2023

WHEREAS, the record indicates that as part of repealing and replacing Titles 18 and 19, the
Kearns Metro Township Council discussed and deliberately chose to prohibit Short Term Rentals
in the Kearns Metro Township; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to that decision, subsection 19.42.310 of the was edited to prohibit Short
Term Rentals in all parts of the Kearns Metro Township; and

WHEREAS, the use table in subsection 19.28.030 was inadvertently omitted from the list of
requested edits and indicates, contrary to subsection 19.42.030 and the legislative intent, that
Short Term Rentals are permitted uses in the R-1-7 and R-1-8 zones.

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL, effective immediately, that the use table in subsection 19.28.030 be corrected to
reflect the legislative intent that Short Term Rental be prohibited throughout the Kearns Metro
Township. The use table will reflect this intent by marking each zone with an X for prohibited for
the Short Term Rental use.

PASSED AND ADOPTED this 12th day of October 2023.
