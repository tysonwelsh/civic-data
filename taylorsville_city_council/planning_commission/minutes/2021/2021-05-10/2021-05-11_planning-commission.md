                                            SUBDIVISION AMENDMENT


       3.    File #2S21 – Consideration of a Request to Amend the Taylorsville Professional Plaza Condominium
             Plat at 1951 West 4700 South. (Presented by Jim Spung/AICP/Senior Planner)

            3.1    Mr. Spung presented this item. Terry Martin (property owner), is requesting consideration to
                   amend the Taylorsville Professional Plaza Condominium Plat at 1951 West 4700 South. The

                   tenant professional office building. The proposed subdivision amendment would subdivide the
                   existing building into four separately owned suites. Development improvements to the site or
                   building are not being proposed at this time.

         3.2       FINDINGS:
                   3.2.1  This application was initiated by Terry Martin.
                   3.2.2  The property is zoned Professional Office (PO).
                   3.2.3  The applicant is requesting to amend the Taylorsville Professional Plaza Condominium
                          Plat.
                   3.2.4  The proposed plat amendment meets all zoning standards in the City Code.
                   3.2.5  The Taylorsville Building Department requires that fire-rated wall(s) be constructed as
                          per IBC 706 along the new property line.
                   3.2.6  A building permit must be obtained for the wall construction/modifications.
                   3.2.7  The Taylorsville Planning Commission is the decision-making body related to
                          subdivision amendment requests.

         3.3       CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:
                   3.3.1  The Taylorsville Building Department requires that fire-rated wall(s) be constructed as
                          per IBC 706 along the new property line.
                   3.3.2  A building permit must be obtained for the wall construction/modification prior to
                          recording the plat amendment with Salt Lake County.

         3.4       Staff recommends approval based on the Findings and Conditions of Approval.

         3.5       APPLICANT ADDRESS:               Laurie Gale represented the applicant. She said the separation
                   was done 27 years ago based on a previous falling out between partners and based on a court
                   order they actually already have a separation. The present action is just to make it official in
                   order to get separate tax identification numbers, the reason for which is the two owners are
                   wanting to sell their units and need separate tax identification numbers to do so. Commissioner
                   Burggraaf asked about the requirement for the fire rated wall and a time-line when it would be
                   constructed. Ms. Gale said she did some research about that and based on that it looked like
                   there was an exception where it could even be waived because this just involves a change in
                   ownership, which occurred in 1995 by court order.          To answer the question, she said she
                   did not know how long that would take but felt it would be an issue of how soon they could find
                   a general contractor available to do that job. She had talked with the Building Official who said
                   the fire wall should not be that complicated and he recommended having a drawing done by an
                   architect first. She recognized the problem would be getting someone not too busy to do that
                   job. Commissioner Burggraaf asked if her client was aware of that issue though and she said he
                   was. Commissioner Burggraaf asked her if her client was opposed to doing that wall and she
                   said partly but he was willing to do whatever is necessary. She noted there was an 18” gap in
                   between the two units already in a certain portion of it and on that there is a question as to
                   whether it would even be necessary in that particular part of it. From what she saw on the
                   drawings it looked like there was an 8” gap in the other parts as well. She was unsure how that
                   would work out in the City Code.        Her client too was still wondering if that work was really
                   going to be necessary.


         3.6       SPEAKING: Kristopher Foltz spoke saying he owns the property right across the street and had
                   no issue with this proposal.

         3.7       Commissioner Burggraaf closed the public hearing and opened it up for Planning Commission
                   discussion.

         3.8       DISCUSSION:

                   3.8.1     Commissioner Wendel said her question was for Staff. Because this is going to be an
                             official plat, there is the International Building Code requirement and not necessarily an
                             issue for the Planning Commission tonight. To help the applicant understand this, she
                             stated that means bringing the property up to Code. Mr. Spung said essentially it
                             could be looked at as having one house and dividing it into separate houses which
                             would require some separation there. It includes much more than just the fire wall,
                             i.e., the utilities must be separate, etc. The Building Code provides a menu of ways to
                             achieve compliance and it is just a matter of selecting the best way to do that and as
                             Ms. Gale mentioned, hiring an architect to go in and look at it to determine how to
                             retrofit it if necessary is the best solution. He said that timing would be as soon as
                             there is a building permit submittal, within about two weeks review comments are
                             available and/or an approval. Then each subsequent submittal has a two week turn-
                             around time. He guessed that contractors are probably four to six weeks out at the
                             present time.

                   3.8.2          Commissioner Burggraaf asked if the Commission has to consider the condition. If
                             there is an exception, he did not want to impose a condition if they are able to find a
                             suitable exception or some deviation from that condition. Mr. Spung said if there is an
                             exception, it would be articulated in the Building Code. He felt what the City is saying is
                             that the applicant must comply with the International Building Code and with whatever
                             mechanism they chose to do that with, whether it is constructing those improvements
                             or having an exception. In answer to the larger question, Staff would recommend that
                             the Commission does have the condition, just to have it on the written record because
                             the Building Department does not have to sign off on the plat and they have indicated
                             that Planning Staff cannot approve the plat unless it complies with the Building Code.
                             The property line cannot be established if it doesn’t satisfy the Building Code
                             requirements.          Commissioner Burggraaf advised that the City of Taylorsville has
                             essentially adopted the International Building Code. He asked then if the Commission
                             can be more generic and require the condition be that the applicant complies with the
                             International Building Code, that way in looking at the issue further, they find some
                             other way to comply that satisfies the City, they would be in compliance with the
                             condition. He just didn’t want to say they have to build the fire rated wall and then an
                             exception is found later and the imposed condition by the Planning Commission still
                             exists.

                   3.8.3     Commissioner Quigley added that they are applying for a building permit so that would
                             have the requirement to build the fire rated wall. He felt that no matter what the
                             Commission decides this should be worded to indicate that at the end of the day the
                             applicant must get a building permit and that will require them to update the building
                             to meet the current code to meet the condominium code requirements, i.e., fire wall,
                             utilities, etc. Therefore, he did not see this to be an issue of whether or not that is
                             included tonight by the Planning Commission because the applicants will be required to
                             obtain building permits anyway. This was split back in 1995 but now they want to
                             make it official as an amendment to the plat and in order to do that they must comply
                             with the current Building Code.

                   3.8.4.    Commissioner Burggraaf wondered what would happen then if they do find an
                             exception that doesn’t require a building permit. Commissioner Quigley asked Mr.
                             Spung to answer that. If they find an exception that Staff agrees to, would they
                             withhold the building permit or make a requirement thereto because in obtaining the
                             building permit they must meet all Code requirements. Mr. Spung said what Staff is
                             trying to say is that before the City can record the plat amendment it must verify that
                             the owners are in compliance with the Building Code, whether that is through an
                             exception or building permit or whatever mechanism that allows. Staff would prefer to
                             have that condition written down as part of the approval so if a year or two later
                             whoever is looking at this will know that is an issue that must be resolved before it can
                             be recorded with the County. He was okay with the more general language inclusion.
                             Commissioner Burggraaf suggested saying, “that they are compliant with the
                             applicable Building Code”. Mr. Spung said that was correct, but it would be in there so
                             that current and future Staff will know that is a requirement before it can be finalized.

                   3.8.5     Commissioner Burggraaf said he noticed that Staff’s conditions don’t actually mention
                             anything about a building permit and was informed by Staff that actually was part of
                             Condition #2. Commissioner Burggraaf wanted to know if that would be necessary if
                             the Commission just says that it must be compliant with the Building Code. Mr. Spung
                             said that was a condition that the Building Department requested to be added because
                             generally that is the way that the Building Department would verify that the wall is
                             constructed properly, with review and inspection connected to the building permit.
                             That if there were a way to achieve compliance without a permit that would be
                             acceptable but based on Mr. Spung’s conversation with the Building Official, it would
                             be considered a requirement. Mr. Spung said they could say that the Taylorsville
                             Building Department requires that the wall separating the two units be compliant with
                             the International Building Code and that, if necessary, any permits or approvals as
                             required by the City be obtained. Commissioner Burggraaf advised he was okay with
                             that language. If a permit is required, they would comply. For example, the Building
                             Official hasn’t been in that building, so if they find something different he did not want
                             to lock them in with a condition. They may need a fire wall and may well need a
                             building permit but based on a situation where they don’t, he felt it was worth the
                             extra re-wording and the five minutes taken on this issue to resolve it to clarify those
                             conditions of approval.

                   3.8.6     Commissioner McElreath felt there was nothing wrong with the conditions for
                             approval. If they can find a different way to solve the problem and it meets the
                             Building Code, then they don’t have to get a building permit.                Commissioner
                             Burggraaf said it is just that the Commission would have conditioned approval of the
                             subdivision so essentially they would need to come back. Commissioner Quigley
                             advised the Commission was not really conditioning it to that but to getting a building
                             permit and that issue would be resolved in the process of getting that permit. There is
                             nothing that is not going to let them be flexible with Staff. It just says they have to get
                             a building permit and in order to do that Staff is going to require them to meet
                             whatever requirements they are obligated to but if there is an exception then Staff can
                             require them to meet that obligation. Commissioner Burggraaf offered with his “lay”
                             opinion was with a legal background and advised that is not how this is looked at. That
                             is completely false. The subdivision approval does not go through if the conditions
                             have not been met. That is why he wondered if the Commission is even permitted to
                             do so in this case. In the ordinance it deals with subdivisions where it says the
                             Commission can impose conditions but if the Commission is going to impose conditions

                             come back for further approval or to amend the approval to allow for the subdivision,
                             which would delay them even more because the Commission has been way too specific
                             when there may be some question. The Commission is not the Building Official and if
                             that person were here tonight it would have been helpful but that is not the case. So,
                             the Commission does not know some aspects of how the Building Code may apply. His
                             opinion was that if they get an architect as has been suggested, they may say what has
                             to be done but the applicants would still need to get a building permit.        Mr. Foltz
                             (audience) commented that Commissioner Burggraaf was exactly right that a building
                             permit is required for any work done on structures.            Commissioner Burggraaf
                             suggested that the language be reworded for clarity. It still accomplishes what is
                             intended to be done, which is to satisfy what is required by the Building Code without
                             the applicants having to come back for approval if it is something different than what is
                             worded in the document tonight.

                   3.8.7     Commissioner Wright said he agreed on the topic but felt the question was how to
                             word that properly to include in this process. Commissioner Burggraaf advised that
                             the wording would be up to whomever makes the motion. That he would be
                             comfortable doing it and liked the language Mr. Spung previously suggested.

                   3.8.8     Commissioner McElreath asked Mr. Spung if the City Legal Department approved the
                             language being proposed tonight under the Conditions of Approval.              Mr. Spung
                             said that he had sent them the staff report but received no comments back. Mr. Spung
                             that he felt Commissioner Burggraaf’s interpretation is correct that the letter of the law
                             must be applied with the Conditions of Approval. Therefore, if it says a permit must be
                             obtained, then the applicant must do so, whether that means they have to do work or
                             that permit just verifies some other mechanism is to be seen. He felt going with the
                             more general language is fine and Staff supported that.

                   3.8.9     Commissioner Quigley asked Commissioner Burggraaf who would be doing the
                             wordsmithing on that and suggested that in light of Commissioner Burggraaf’s legal
                             background he would be logical to do so.      Commissioner Burggraaf said he would
                             be willing to propose the motion and have someone else adopt or change it.

                   3.8.10    Commissioner Wendel          wondered if something as simple as, “Unless exempted in
                             the International Building Code, Taylorsville Building Department requires that fire
                             rated walls be constructed as per and keep the building permit there.” Commissioner
                             Quigley that meant to just add the word “unless exempted”. Commissioner Wendel
                             added, ”Unless qualified for exemption unless proven exemption”. She was just
                             looking for three or four words. Commissioner Quigley suggested, “Staff determines
                             an exemption”. Commissioner Burggraaf gave his suggestion as being following the
                             proposed motion but amend the conditions of approval to state that the applicant will
                             comply with Building Code requirements, including, if necessary, obtaining a building
                             permit.” Commissioner Quigley asked if “Obtaining all necessary permits” would
                             suffice. Commissioner Burggraaf agreed that would simplify what the condition
                             actually is and say, “Comply with any applicable building codes and obtain any
                             necessary permits before recording.”             He asked Mr. Spung if that worked for
                             him, to which he replied that it did.
