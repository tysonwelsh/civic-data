Public hearing and action regarding the Grant Richins Preliminary Subdivision application to create four lots from Parcel NS-795 (30.0 acres), located north of the town of Henefer off West Henefer Road, Summit County, UT; Applicant: Victoria and Colby Richins. Jennifer Leslie,
Planner
, began the discussion by reviewing the new Ordinance (Ord. 983) governing Preliminary Subdivision Application Submittals. Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions regarding the new process.
Ray Milliner,
Senior Planner,
addressed the questions raised by Commissioners regarding the new process.
Planner Leslie proceeded to present the Grant Richins Preliminary Subdivision application for public hearing and possible action.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions. Planner Leslie and the applicants, Colby Richins and Grant Richins, responded to their questions.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:20 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:20 p.m.
Commissioners asked follow-up questions.
Rich Sonntag made a motion to approve the Grant Richins subdivision subject to the findings of fact, conclusions of law in the staff report as conditioned with the change to North Summit Fire Department. David Darcey seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Bill Wilde, Clinton Benson.
