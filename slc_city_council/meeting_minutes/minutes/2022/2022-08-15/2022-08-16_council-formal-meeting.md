**Ordinance: R-2 Text Amendment: Building Coverage**
The Council will consider adopting an ordinance that would amend the zoning text of Subsection 21A.24.110.F of the *Salt Lake City Code* pertaining to building coverage in the R-2 Single- and Two-Family Residential District.
