City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1649

On May 6, 2025, the Draper City Council Approved Ordinance #1649 amending the
official land use map of Draper City for approximately 0.046 acres of property from
residential low-medium density to residential medium-high density, located at
approximately 13040 South 1300 East within Draper City otherwise known as the
Lingo Land Use Amendment. The complete Ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/.

Published this 5th day of May 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
