                                           WITNESS

       WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature, during the 2015 General Session, passed Senate
Bill 199 (SB 199) creating the Metro Township form of municipal government and setting an
election for the voters of Kearns on November 3, 2015 to choose between incorporating as a
Metro Township or lncorporating as a City; and

       WHEREAS, SD 199 also asked the voters to choose whether Kearns would join the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as members of the District; and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns voters, in the November 3, 2015 election, voted to incorporate as
a Metro Township and to join the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as
members of the District; and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Voters elected the five residents to serve as their representatives
on the Magna Metro Township Council on November 8, 20 I6; and

       WHEREAS, to complete the incorporation prnccss, per State Law, the Kearns Metro
Township Council was required to file a notice of incorporation letter, a notice of boundary
action, and a Final Local Entity Plat with the Lieutenant Governor's Office lo achieve the final
certification of the Kearns Metro Township as a municipal entity; and
       WIIEREAS, this work had to be performed prior to the Council's official terms ofoffkc
commencing; and


       WHEREAS, the elected members of the Kearns Metro Township Council approved the
creation and filing of this work and authorized their chair to sign the required documents; and

       WHEREAS, ihe required documents were filed with the Lieutenant Governor's Oflice on
December 16, 2016; and

       Wl!EREAS, the Lieutenant Governor certified the creation of the Kearns Metro
Township on December 27, 2016; and

       WI ICREAS, the Certificate and Final Local Entity Plat were recorded on December 30,
2016 with the Salt Lake County Recorder, completing the incorporation process and officially
creating the Kearns Metro Township.

                                         RESOLUTION

       NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Kearns Metro Township Council does
hereby ratify and approve the finished work attached to this ordinance, creating the Kearns
Metro Township and accepting the date of January 2, 2017 as the official creation date of the
Kearns Metro Township.
