CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-23-2022 Petition by FORZA DEVELOPMENT requesting a conditional use for the Carpe
          Diem Shopping Center. The property is located at 5530 South 5600 West on 1.3
          acres and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-
          3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that this is a conditional use proposal for a shopping center
              located on the Carpe Diem Auctum II Subdivision Lot 1. She stated they are
              proposing a four-tenant building. Jody said the far west unit would be a Café Rio
              Restaurant. She noted that the tenants for the remaining three units would be retail
              or office uses. Jody stated that if they were to add another restaurant, the parking
              would be reassessed. Otherwise, they would have sufficient parking.

              Jody noted that the subject location’s address had been updated to 5518 South
              5600 West which was determined during the subdivision process. She explained
              that UDOT would not permit four access points off 5600 West. Therefore, UTA
              would provide one shared access off 5600 West, branching to the two lots to the
              north and south.

              Jody said some elements would need to be added to meet the commercial design
              standards. She noted that adding some color changes to the structure would help
              to meet the design treatment standards. Jody stated that the exterior mechanical
              equipment would need to be screened. She pointed out that burming or screening
              needs to be added next to the proposed drive-thru. She pointed out that 5600 West
              is a high-image arterial, and the applicant must meet those landscaping
              requirements.

C-24-2022     Petition by READY MADE CONCRETE requesting a conditional use for parking
              and outside storage. The property is located at 2806 S. Constitution Boulevard on
              .95 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497
              or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that this is a conditional use request for an overflow
              storage area. She said the applicant would be parking trucks and trailers on the
              subject property. Jody pointed out that the 305-foot lot is located in the Rocky
              Mountain Power Corridor, and the applicant is proposing to use 210 feet of that
              property.

              Jody stated that the property would need to be paved with either concrete or
              asphalt. She noted that they would need to meet the high-image arterial
              landscaping requirements where this lot is on Parkway Boulevard. She said the
              park strip would need to be improved, and they would need to add the required 20
              feet of landscaping. Jody explained that the existing fence would need to move
              back 20 feet to meet the setback requirement.

              Commissioner McEwen pointed out that there is a large amount of dust and dirt
              tracking onto Constitution Boulevard from the applicant's existing site. He asked
              Jody if the business would be responsible for cleaning the road. Jody said that
              Public Works does not want debris on the road. She noted that a company with
              gravel pits and larger concrete areas would have some type of environmental
              permit. Jody stated that this issue would be addressed by Public Works
              enforcement.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:42 p.m.
