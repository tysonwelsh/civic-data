PUBLIC HEARING—AMENDED 2025 BUDGET 7:00
      Mayor Seely opened the public meeting and turned the time to City Administrator Seth
      Atkinson. Mr. Atkinson explained the need for amendments due to large changes,
      primarily adding revenues and expenses to the local building authority. He explained
      that is the fund we make the debt service payment for the Public Works building. They
      were making the payments out of the direct departments, but made the technical
      changes to ensure the debt service payment is made correctly.
      Mayor Seely opened the floor for public comment. No comments were made. The
      public hearing was closed. 7:02 pm

DRUG TASK FORCE PRESENTATION
   o Nephi Police Department Drug Task Force Commander Mike Nielson and Taylor Hurst,
      Nephi PD Drug Task Force presented the yearly West Central Task Force Statistics. The
      task force is comprised of Nephi, Payson, and Santaquin departments.
   o The task force seized a significant number of drugs, including 143 pounds of
      methamphetamine, 22 pounds of marijuana, and various other substances.
   o The total street value of seized drugs estimated street value of $1,642,664.89.
   o Officer Nielson discussed the trends of drug use in Nephi, noting that
      methamphetamine is still the most prevalent drug. The task force has seen a decrease in
      heroin cases and an increase in fentanyl cases.
   o Officer Nielson addressed the challenges in maintaining consistent efforts due to limited
      resources and burnout among officers. The need for additional help and resources is
      emphasized to maintain effective drug enforcement.

RESOLUTION 07-15-2025—MASTER FEE RESOLUTION
      A. In the Work Session on 7-8-25 the council discussed instituting the Shared Solar
         Program from the Utah Municipal Power Agency (UMPA). This program enables
         customers to participate in a solar energy program without having to install the solar
         panels on their homes. It locks in a solar rate for a portion of the customer’s usage.
         Currently the rate is less than the amount charged for typical residential power
         through the city. The proposed rate is $21.24 per 200 kWh or $0.1062/kWh.
      B. There are a number of homes and businesses that are currently receiving city
         utilities who are outside the city limits. Due to the additional distance required to
         maintain the utility service lines or solid waste pickup, additional fees are
          appropriate. It was recommended to increase out-of-city utility service rates that
          would be effective on the October billing of this year.
       C. The City Council has a policy to not allow new water connections outside of the city
          limits. These out-of-city fees would not change that policy.
       D. Councilor JD Parady made a motion to approve Resolution 07-15-2025—Master Fee
          Resolution including the Shared Solar Fee and the increase to out-of-city utility
          service rates. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on
          unanimous vote.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP CHANGE
     Seth Atkinson discussed the request to change the level of annexation for the Set Square
     data center from a level three to a level two. This will allow for an area analysis which
     includes a detailed review of the utility needs and potential impacts. The council
     discussed the potential use of natural gas and other fuel sources for the data center.
     Councilor Skip Worwood made a motion to approve the annexation level change to level
     two. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

AMENDED 2025 BUDGET
     Based on the size of some of the line items in the 2025 budget, the amended budget
     was brought back for approval. Mr. Atkinson described a few of the changes. For the
     general fund there was some developer bond interest. Also, the recreation department
     had some bigger expenses than anticipated. The council discussed the possibility of an
     increased fee for those out-of-town participants in the programs. The county paid a fee
     in the past to offset those costs, but they are no longer providing that. Mr. Atkinson also
     mentioned the Building Authority Fund described in the Public Hearing.
     Councilor JD Parady made a motion to approve the Amended 2025 Budget. Councilor
     Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

Work Session 7:56 pm
      1. Mr. Atkinson reported that several code changes are taking place and will be brought
          to the council for review and then at a later council meeting they will be brought for
          approval. City Attorney Brittany Tellez discussed two of those changes:
          a. Nuisance Code Update—The changes include adjusting the fees for the standard
              nuisances and allowing for emergency abatement.
          b. ATV Code Change—The ATV code changes specify that helmets are not required
              for type two vehicles, such as side-by-sides.
      2. The mayor discussed the request of a citizen to replace the flagpole at the city park.

          the need for stricter rules. The council discusses the potential need for background
