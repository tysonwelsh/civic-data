2. CITIZEN PUBLIC INPUT
Mr. Amid Ahmadzadeh  stated he was assaulted last week at the Courthouse because he 
was filming in the lobby, which is not prohibited, and because he was visibly from the 
middle east.  A sergeant from the Sheriff ’s Office pushed him down and the assault was 
taped.   He asked for medical help and was refused, so he called the Salt Lake City Police 
Department himself.  They sent two officers and an ambulance.  The police called him an 
expletive and would not help him.  He has tried to call Sheriff Rosie Rivera, but she has 
been unresponsive.
Council Member Stringham  asked Mr. Ahmadzadeh to fill out a complaint form 
documenting what happened so there can be an investigation.  A member of the Sheriff ’s 
Office provided the form. 
Mr. Mark Lewon  stated he was still unhappy with the staffing of lifeguards at his local 
recreation center.  He had tried to communicate with the Parks and Recreation Department 
directly, but they have not responded.  He had also spoken with Martin Jensen.  He felt 
the County requires more on -duty lifeguards than are necessary for early morning hours, 
and he did not want to have to go to another pool to swim.  
Mr. Jeff Barron stated Utah Code §72-7-503 specifically outlaws the placement of
