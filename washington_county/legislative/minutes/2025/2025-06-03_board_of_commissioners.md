The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on June 3, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Victoria
Hales, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Adam Snow was excused.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2025-1294-O0, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
WASHINGTON COUNTY ORDINANCE, TITLE 8 CHAPTER 5 REGARDING
TRANSIENT ROOM TAX

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales stated a state law allows for an increase in transient room
tax rates from 4.25% to 4.5% as early as October 1, 2025. This Ordinance would implement the
allowed increase but not go into effect until July 1, 2026.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2025-1294-O, An
Ordinance Amending Washington County Ordinance, Title 8 Chapter 5
Regarding Transient Room Tax. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3538, A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING
AND_ SELECTING THE RFP PROPOSAL PREPARED BY NORRIS DESIGN AND
PRIEFERT COMPLEX DESIGNS FOR THE LEGACY PARK MASTER PLAN

Ms. Felshaw explained that Legacy Park is a 14,000-acre multi-use event space that hosts a number
of activities. Legacy Park has aged and has significant facility needs to meet both the growing
demand and improve current infrastructure. Norris Design and Priefert Complex Designs have the
expertise and experience to conduct the feasibility study and provide a master plan for the future

of the Legacy Park Facility.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3538, A

Design and Priefert Complex Designs for the Legacy Park Master Plan.
