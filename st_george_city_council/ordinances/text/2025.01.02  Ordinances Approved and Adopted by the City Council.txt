                                 PUBLIC NOTICE

Please be advised that January 2, 2025, the City Council of the City of St George
approved the following ordinance(s):

Ordinance No. 2025-002 amending an approved PD-C (Planned Development
Commercial) for two new commercial buildings on 2.15 acres in the Atkinville
Interchange Area Master Plan generally located on the east side of Pioneer Road
north of Sun River Parkway and north of Chevron and Terribles.

Ordinance No. 2025-003 amending the city zoning map by changing the zone from
A-1 (Agricultural, 40,000 sq ft minimum lot size) to OS (Open Space) on
approximately 0.56 acres generally located near the St. George/Washington border
near the Paseo Development.

Ordinance No. 2025-004 amending a Development agreement affecting
approximately 5.03 acres located south of the existing southern terminus of Gap
Canyon Parkway at the southern boundary of PA-2 in the Divario development.
