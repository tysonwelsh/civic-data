PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on December 13, 2017 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2017-54 repealing Title 10 Land Use Regulations and Title 11 Subdivision Regulations of the Herriman City Code of Ordinance and Enacting a new Title 10, and related matters. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
