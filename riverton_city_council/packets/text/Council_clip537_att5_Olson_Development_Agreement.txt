                              DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

         Preserve at Lovers Lane - Development/Preservation of Natural Features

       THIS DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR THE PRESERVE at LOVERS
LANE (“Agreement”) is made and entered into on the ______ day of March, 2022, between
Riverton City, a municipal corporation and political subdivision of the State of Utah (the
“City”); and KSO Trust and Tyson Olson, the owners of the subject real property (collectively
the “Owners” or “Developer”). The City and the Owners are sometimes collectively referred to
herein as the “Parties.”

                                              RECITALS:

       WHEREAS, the real property covered by this Agreement is located in adjoining
parcels encompassing approximately 6 acres (collectively referred to as the “Property”). The
legal descriptions that describe the Property are set forth in Exhibit “A” attached hereto.

       WHEREAS, the real property is generally located as shown on an aerial map of the
general area attached as Exhibit “B”.

       WHEREAS, the current property is approved for two homes where owner can have up
to 3 homes on the private Lane Exhibit “C”

       WHEREAS, the owner desires to develop the Property to include 8 single family
homes. The conceptual layout for the proposed development of the Property is attached hereto
as Exhibit “D.”

                                            AGREEMENT:
       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Recitals above and the terms and
conditions set forth below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties covenant and agree as follows:



                                                    1
1.   Effective Date. This Agreement shall become effective on the date it is recorded in the
     Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office after being executed by the Owners, and the City
     (the “Effective Date”).

2.   Affected Property. The Property that is the subject of this Agreement is described in
     the legal descriptions attached hereto as Exhibit “A.” This Agreement shall be recorded
     against the Property.

3.   Settlement Of Claims. This development agreement is made in connection with claims
     by Owners against Riverton City and Symphony Homes, which are pending in the
     Third District Court, in and for Salt Lake County, State of Utah, Case No. , 210900684,
     and is intended to resolve all issues relating to the subject Property in the referenced
     case.

4.   City agrees that Owners’ Landscaping and Grading Plans will meet City Code
     standards for preservation of natural features, as described in City Code Section,
     17.05.040:

             a. Conservation of Natural Features. Trees, native land cover, wetlands,
                natural watercourses, and topography shall be preserved where possible.
                Subdivisions shall be so designed as to prevent excessive grading and
                scarring of the landscape in conformance with RCC Title 18. The design of
                new subdivisions shall consider, and relate to, existing street widths,
                alignments and names.16.
5.   Up to three homes are allowed on the Property with a private lane in accordance with
     the following:

             a. The private lane will be modified to 20 total feet in width (easement/cross
                section) per attached Exhibit (C);
             b. The private lane for phase one, described in subsection 5. c., below, will
                meet the standard for a 24-ton vehicle being constructed of crushed asphalt
                and or limestone crusher fines for the surface;
             c. Owner shall subdivide the property into three lots by subdivision plat
                attached as Exhibit D.);
             d. The private lane will seek to preserve the natural features as per City Code;
             e. Stormwater discharge, if any, within the project shall be managed within the
                project to equal predevelopment hydrology, unless the Developer can
                demonstrate with a hydrology study that additional capacity is available in
                Butterfield Creek, in which case the developer may discharge additional
                stormwater into Butterfield Creek in a manner which complies with the
                Jordan Valley Municipality Permit No. UTS000001, issued by the State of


                                                  2
         Utah, division of water quality (this is intended as part of the settlement of
         Owners’ stormwater runoff claims against Riverton City;
      f. Direct stormwater runoff into Butterfield does not include runoff from hard
         surfaces which thereafter flows through landscaped or natural ground or
         vegetation, whether or not such runoff eventually flows into Butterfield
         Creek.
      g. Provided Phase 1 does not cause direct stormwater runoff into Butterfield
         Creek, no retention or detention ponds will be required for the three homes
         and private lane approved in Phase 1 due to the anticipated low impact
         where stormwater will disperse into landscape areas to prevent water from
         running directly into Butterfield Creek;
      h. The private lane will be posted for no parking at the entry from Lovers
         Lane, within 20 feet of the bridge and at the fire turn around;
      i. While there are no more than three homes constructed, or under
         construction, Owners shall provide for the upkeep and maintenance of the
         private lane; and
      j. At the time there are more than three homes constructed, or under
         construction, Owners shall create a home owners association to provide the
         upkeep and maintenance of the private lane.
      k. The development shall prevent the off-site discharge of the precipitation
         from all rainfall events that are less than or equal to 0.50 inches. For
         example, the grading of the private, or installation of swales, where
         appropriate, lane may be sufficient to meet this requirement. It is expected
         that the improvements required for phase II of the subdivision will require
         installation of improvements which may not be required for phase I.
6.   Development of property not developed in phase 1 shall be developed in phase 2
     and shall be subject to the following conditions:
      a. The Property is approved for up to 8 homes total including existing homes
         in phase 1, with a private lane from Lovers Lane (Concept Exhibit D);
      b. At the time more than three (3) homes are being built, the private lane cross
         section will be increased to 25 feet and the entire private lane shall be paved
         with asphalt (25 feet total cross section including easement);
      c. The private lane is approved at up to 1400 ft. long with one access from
         Lovers Lane to the fire turn around;
      d. The fire turn around shall be located within 150 feet of the last home on the
         lane, as per exhibit D;
      e. All homes will have adequate parking per City Code;
      f. The entire length of the private lane will be posted for no parking;
      g. The private lane will be approved at the staff level;
      h. If the property east of the Property develops with a public road prior to
         actual physical development of the Property, the City will require as part of
         the new development a stub road servicing the Property as part of the
         adjacent development, which will be located at the proximity of the center
         of the Owners’ east property line;
      i. When, and if, such a stub road is constructed, a 25 foot private lane from
         the Owner’s east property line is approved from the adjacent property to the
         east and the new private lane shall not be required to tie to Lovers Lane or

                                          3
          to the existing private lane from Lovers Lane to the Olson home currently
          under construction;
      j. In the event a new stub road to the Owners’ east property line is
          constructed, the maximum of eight homes on the private lane shall be
          waived as long as all other zoning requirements for lot size for the
          underlying zone are met;
      k. The new private lane is approved to be within lot boundaries and lot square
          footage;
      l. Prior to constructing any home accessing the new private lane, Owners will
          amend an existing home owners association, or create a new home owners
          association, to provide the upkeep and maintenance of the new private lane
      m. The development of the overall Property is low impact development where
          no publicly owned retention or detention (ponds or swells) shall be required
          as all storm water will disperse to landscape areas off the side of the new
          private lane or from homes and driveways.
7.   Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this development agreement,
     nothing shall prohibit Owners from developing a standard subdivision on the
     east portions of the Property once a stub road is provided from the adjacent
     property to the east of the Property.
8.   If Owners are able to acquire property or access from the east that connects to
     the proposed stub road located at the River Ridge Lane East Storm Drain Pond
     location (retention pond), Riverton City will pay the cost of the improvements
     for the stub road at that location along with any condemnation of property
     required.
