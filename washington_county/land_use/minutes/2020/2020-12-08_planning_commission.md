Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                              December 08, 2020
The Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting was held on Tuesday, December 08, 2020
in the Council Chambers of the Washington County Administration Building at 197 E. Tabernacle,
St. George, UT. Chairman Kevin Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of
Allegiance and then explained meeting protocol.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Mark Owens, Reed Scow (Alternate), and Doug
Solstad.

Excused: Shayne Durrant, Dan Briggs (Alternate), Slade Hughes, and Jon Walters.

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy County
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Todd
Edwards, County Engineer; Kim Hafen, County Clerk.


 1. 2021 PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING SCHEDULE. Review and approve the
 2021 Planning Commission Meeting Schedule.

 Mr. Messel presented the 2021 Planning Meeting Planning Commission Schedule. He noted there
 might be changes on the dates if necessary. He asked if there were any questions or comments.
 There was none.

 MOTION:       Commissioner Solstad MOVED to accept the 2021 Planning Commission
               Meeting Schedule as provided. Commissioner Anderson SECONDED.
               Chairman Jones asked if there were any further comments. There being none,
               he called for a vote. The motion carried unanimously.


 2. CONCEPT PLAN. Discussion of a conceptual planned development plan for a rural
 recreation grounds and facility on Parcels 1143-A-NP-HV & 1148-A-NP-HV located on Dalton
 Wash Road. The applicant is Kylee Chenn. (No action or approval will be taken on this item).

 Mr. Messel explained the concept plan review process. This information is being presented for
 the planning commissioner’s information and feedback.

 He provided maps and submitted documents. The applicants are proposing a PDRRG&F
 (Planned Development Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities) development on approximately
 50 acres of property located in the Dalton Wash area. The property is adjacent to BLM land on
 the west and north. Zion History Association owns the property to the east. The property to the
 south is privately owned. The concept plan is attached to this report. The first step in the PD
 development process is the concept plan review. Staff meet with the applicants in the December
 1, 2020 staff meeting to discuss the project. The Planning Commission should review and provide

 The applicants, Kylee and Andrew Chenn, attended via Zoom.

 There was discussion of the concerns and challenges related to the project that included the

 3. JOINT DEVELOPMENT STAFF MEETING REVIEW. Report of the Land Use
 Authority Joint Utility Development Staff Meeting held on December 01, 2020.

Mr. Messel reported on the Joint Utility Development Staff Meeting held December 01, 2020.
Staff reviewed the item above. They also reviewed and approved two conditional use permit for
accessory dwellings in New Harmony. One on Parcel NVRS-3-6-A-NS and the other on Parcel
BRR-174-NS. There were two zone change/subdivision requests reviewed. One located in
Dammeron Valley at 180 N. Pinion Hills Dr. The other for the Thiriot project in Pine Valley at
1200 E. Main Street. They will not be pursuing the rural recreations ground and facility project
they originally requested. Instead, they will be moving forward with a subdivision of the property.
They also reviewed updated information for the Above Zion project.

Some clarification questions regarding the projects in Dammeron Valley and Pine Valley were
asked and answered.


 4. MINUTES. Consider approval of the minutes of the regular planning commission meeting
 held October 29, 2020

 Chairman Jones asked if there were any comments or changes to the minutes. If not, he would
 entertain a motion.

 MOTION: Commissioner Owens MOVED to approve the minutes of the October 29, 2020
         planning commission meeting as written. Commissioner Solstad SECONDED.
         The motion carried with Commissioner Scow abstaining as he was not in
         attendance of the meeting.

Mr. Messel stated there was nothing to report.

Mr. Messel stated that with COVID there have been no events coming through the county so there

initiative forward.
