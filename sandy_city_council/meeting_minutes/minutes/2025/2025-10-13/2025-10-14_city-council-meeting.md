1.         25-314                 Building our Future: A Community Housing Workshop Series, Workshop
                                  Four - Prioritizing tools and tactics
                   Attachments:   October 14, 2025 Memo_Housing Workshop Series
                                  Workshop 4 presentation
                                  UMH_MMH-Toolkit_BuildingTypes
                                  UMH_MMH-Toolkit_SiteDesign_Guidelines

                                  Council Chair Brooke D'Sousa welcomed those in attendance. The Sandy City Council
                                  held the fourth Housing Workshop during a regular Council Meeting. The workshop was
                                  moderated by Sandy City Attorney Lynn Pace.

                                  Lynn Pace opened the meeting by reviewing the draft vision statement created at the
                                  previous housing workshop:

                                  To ensure continued sustainability and to maintain the character of our city: Our goal is
                                  to encourage housing and housing affordability for residents across all stages of life. We
                                  want to encourage, allow and incentivize a larger variety of housing (including housing for
                                  seniors, first time home buyers, middle housing and workforce housing, more housing (in
                                  the right locations) and we want the housing to be owner occupied.

                                  The draft vision statement was compared to elements of the Sandy City General Master
                                  Plan.

                                  Jake Warner, Community Development, provided the background and purpose of
                                  Workshop #4. Workshops 1 and 2 focused on the"why" behind the city's housing needs
                                  and were educational. Workshop 3 focused on goal prioritization and the development of
                                  a vision. Workshop 4 shifts the focus to the "what" (types of housing) and "where"
                                  (locations for housing"). A fifth workshop has now been added to focus on the "who" and
                                  "how" implementation will occur.

                                  A framework was introduced to guide the discussion this evening on the types of housing
                                  (the "what"). Sandy lacks "missing middle housing" when compared to Salt Lake County
                                  and the broader tri-county area. Six broad categories of housing were presented ("what"):
                                  detached accessory structures, small-lot single-family, twin homes (duplex), mansion
                                  style home, townhome, small mid-rise buildings. Mr. Warner then reviewed details
                                  associated with each type of housing.

                                  Mr. Warner then reviewed the "Where" and explained the four categories on the Future
                                  Land Use Map: neighborhoods, corridors, centers, and Cairns. Neighborhoods consist of
                                  low and medium density homes. Corridors are seen as opportunity areas for housing
                                  growth and higher density housing. Centers include Neighborhood Activity Centers (NAC)
                                  and Station Area Plans (SAP). The Quarry Bend development was used as an example.
                                  The Cairns is an area of higher density, urban core with mixed use development designed
                                  to support vibrant, walkable communities.

                                  Council Members participated in a prioritization exercise facilitated by the Council
                                  Director, Dustin Fratto. After a brief recess convened by the Council at 6:29 pm, the
                                  Council reconvened at 6:36 pm. The results of the prioritization exercise were shared with
                                  the Council. A lengthy Council discussion followed. Questions from the Council occurred
                                  throughout the evening's presentation. Discussion included the impact of transitioning to
