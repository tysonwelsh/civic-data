                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-13

  A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL PROVIDING ITS ADVICE
     AND CONSENT TO THE MAYOR’S APPOINTMENT OF THE RIVERTON
                  MUNICIPAL JUSTICE COURT JUDGE

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council (“Council”) met in regular meeting on February
7, 2023, to consider, among other things, approving a resolution advising and consenting to the
Mayor’s appointment of a justice court judge for Riverton City Municipal Justice Court
(“Court”); and

         WHEREAS, the Mayor has determined to appoint Christopher Bown as the judge of the
Court;

       WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds and determines that Christopher Bown has
extensive legal training and courtroom experience in criminal, civil, and appellate matters;

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds that Christopher Bown has a positive reputation in
the legal community as well as a strong record of community service, reliability, and respect for
diverse groups of people;

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds that Christopher Bown is qualified to be the
justice court judge of the Court;

         WHEREAS, the Council has given advice for such appointment; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the citizens of Riverton City to consent to and ratify such appointment.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Council hereby consents to the
appointment of Christopher Bown as judge of the Riverton Municipal Justice Court.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
February 2023 by the following vote:
