 4.     BUSINESS ITEMS
        4.1. Discussion and Action - Proposed Tentative Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Budget
             RDA Director Josh Daniels will present the Tentative Fiscal Year 2024-2025 RDA
             Budget. The RDA Board will act to adopt (or deny) the proposed tentative budget and
             set a public hearing for May 22, 2024.

      RDA Director Josh Daniels presented a general overview of the tentative budget.

     Boardmember Holdaway asked about items listed in the budget and wanted to know if there
was a way to get a more detailed breakdown. Mr. Daniels explained this was just a presentation of
the tentative budget and that more specific line items would be included in any final budget
documents.

    Chair Fullmer outlined the timeline for finalizing the budget and the steps that were taken
during the process of adopting a final budget, including a public hearing.

   Motion: BOARDMEMBER SIFUENTES MOVED TO ADOPT THE PROPOSED
TENTATIVE FISCAL YEAR 2024-2025 RDA BUDGET AS PRESENTED. WITH A PUBLIC
HEARING SCHEDULED FOR MAY 22ND 2024. BOARDMEMBER CAMERON SECONDED
THE MOTION. CHAIR FULLMER, BOARDMEMBERS CAMERON AND SIFUENTES
VOTED YES, AND BOARDMEMBER HOLDAWAY VOTED NO. BOARDMEMBER
RASMUSSEN WAS EXCUSED. THE MOTION CARRIED WITH ONE ABSENT.

    Boardmember Cameron wanted further discussion on the process. Chair Fullmer answered
questions about the process and there was a discussion about the process before the vote for the
motion was finished.
