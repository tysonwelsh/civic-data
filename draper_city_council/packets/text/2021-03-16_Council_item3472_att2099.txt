                                       RESOLUTION NO. 21-22

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE CITY OF DRAPER TO
            APPOINT LINDA PETERSON AS COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTOR
                           FOR THE CITY OF DRAPER.

        WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint LINDA PETERSON as Communications Director
for the City of Draper in accordance with the appointment procedures provided by law and City
Ordinance; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 3-1-045 requires that the Communications Director
be appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, LINDA PETERSON has the credentials, experience and professionalism
necessary to be the Communications Director.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent to the
appointment of LINDA PETERSON as Communications Director and the Mayor is hereby authorized
to appoint LINDA PETERSON as Communications Director, in accordance with appointment
procedures provided by law and City Code.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution,
and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect on March 16, 2021.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS 16th DAY OF MARCH, 2021.
