11/10/2025 @ 1405, Side by Side Rollover, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 10.5 (Non
Certified) 1.5 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a side by side rollover on Sand Mountain. The
individual who crashed had rolled down into a ravine and his group was unable to get to him to
properly assess his condition. Due to this, Life Flight was requested to assist. SAR units, in conjunction
with Hurricane Valley Fire medical units and recreation rangers responded to the location of the patient.
He was removed from the side by side and stabilized. Life Flight landed nearby. He was then carried on
a backboard to the location of the helicopter where he was loaded and transported to the hospital via
Life Flight.
11/02/2025 @ 2009, Missing Person, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 45 (Non Certified) 3
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) members responded to a Female Missing Person on Sand Mountain.
It was reported that a female had become upset, and was possibly intoxicated, and started to walk out in
to the desert and was not able to be located. UHP helicopter STAR9 also arrived and helped look for
her. The female was located by SAR members and returned to her family members.

10/26/2025 @ 1140, Injured Mountain Biker, Bear Claw Poppy Trail — Man Hours — 24
(Non Certified) 1.5 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a female who had been involved in a
mountain bike accident on Bear Claw Poppy Trail. The patient was located and stabilized by SAR
members. She was then transported back to Incident Command at the trailhead. She was loaded in an
ambulance and transported to the hospital.

Certified) 3 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Smithsonian Butte on a report of two hikers who were
stuck on the mountain and unable to get down. Due to the steep terrain, UHP Helicopter STAR9 was
requested to assist. SAR members made contact with the males and they were evaluated. They were
then hoisted back to Incident Command (IC) by STAR9 where they declined medical transport.

10/29/2025 @ 1218, Injured Mountain Biker, Guacamole Trail — Man Hours — 1
(Certified)

crashed his mountain bike and suffered an apparent leg injury. Life Flight was requested do to the
remote location. The Life Flight crew were able to locate the patient and he was loaded in the
helicopter. He was then transported to the incident command and transferred to a waiting ambulance.
He was then transported to the hospital.

11/06/2025 @1359, Lost Child, St George - Man Hours — 48 (Non Certified) 4 (Certified)

searched the surrounding area for the missing child. The child was located by law enforcement officers
shortly after SAR units cleared the call.

11/07/2025 @1826, Lost Hiker, Water Canyon — Man Hours — 36 (Non Certified) 4
(Certified)

length of time that it would take to get to the lost male, UHP Helicopter STAR9 was requested to assist.
STAR9 located the male and were able to land nearby. He was then loaded in the helicopter and
returned to his vehicle. He declined medical evaluation.
