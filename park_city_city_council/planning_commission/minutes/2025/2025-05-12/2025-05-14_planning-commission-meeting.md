                                                           20
 considered the Housing Element of the General Plan. For that reason, it is important that
 the Commission review and approve it and provide feedback. The State dictates certain
 implementation strategies that are key to the components of the Moderate-Income




                                                    8.

                                   05

                      ed

            v

         ro

 numeric goal to reconfirm that they want 15% of the workforce to be able to live in the
 City. Currently, it is only 12.1%.
