RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE ACQUISITION OF REAL PROPERTY BY
EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP THROUGH THE USE OF THE
POWER OF EMINENT DOMAIN (CONDEMNATION)
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township hereinafter referred to as “the Township,” is a
Utah Municipal Corporation.
WHEREAS, the governing body of the Township has determined that certain real property
interests needs to be acquired in order to reconstruct, widen, and repair a public right of way
along Killyons Lane in the Township, hereinafter referred to as the “public purpose;” and
WHEREAS, it is desirable and in the public interest to undertake and carry out the acquisition
of certain real property for the public purpose described herein; and
WHEREAS, the Township has, through its contracted agents from Salt Lake County, negotiated
with the property owners in an effort to acquire the property interest; and
WHEREAS, the Township has authority under Utah Code 78B-6-504 to acquire property for
public purposes through the use of eminent domain; and
WHEREAS, the Township has invited the property owners to attend a Council Meeting to
discuss the possibility of acquiring the property through the use of eminent domain and has given
the property owners 14 days written notice of the date time and place of such meeting and
WHEREAS, the real property, which is sought by the Township, is described in the attached
Exhibit A:
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of the Emigration Canyon
Metro Township that the Township is hereby authorized to acquire the property interests and,
complying with the statues relating to eminent domain, if necessary to use the power of
imminent domain to do so, and, if necessary, to make application in the Court having jurisdiction
for an Order of Immediate Occupancy permitting the Township to take immediate possession of
the identified parcel of real property or interest therein for public purposes described above.
