Contract 17‐1049‐04 was established to provide financial advisory services for RLESA.

The Riverton Fire Service Area, known as RFSA, was established with an effective date of October 13,
2020, to provide fire protection, emergency medical and related functions and services to the
incorporated areas of Riverton City.

Financial advisory services are needed for RFSA. The Scope of Services required are the same as RLESA.
For that reason, the City would like to amend the contract adding Financial Advisory Services for RFSA.
All parts of the contract, except for the payment section, will apply to RFSA.

Payment section of the contract will be changed to the following:
      Payment:
               ‐ RLESA – Not to Exceed Amount of $6,000.00 per year.
               ‐ RFSA – Not to Exceed Amount of $6,000.00 per year.
