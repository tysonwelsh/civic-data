Project Description
This project would involve a competitive biddinB process to select a consulting firm to perform a
Recreation Center feasibility study. Some of the items to be looked at include, but are not limited to:
1. Market analysis and survey of public needs related to Recreation
2. Develop several programming and build scenarios for a Recreation center.
3. Schematic designs/conceptual plans based on the market analysis noted above.
4. Project revenue generation, operating expenses, subsidy rewquirements, and construction cost
estimates for each scenario.
5. Determine and propose funding models and bonding scenarios (if needed).
Study could also be expanded to include general recreational opportunities (outside of fitness center)




Project Justification and Need

County the need for the Center, as the City works to fund the project through the Zoo, Arts, and Parks (ZAP)
grant. Salt Lake County has already told the City that a number of other municipalities will be seeking
Recreation Centers during the next round of zAP funding. ln addition, the City Council has identified
general recreation programs as a top priority. Thls study would help identify programs that would benefit
the residents, constraints the City faces, and also potential subsidies needed to run a recreation program.
