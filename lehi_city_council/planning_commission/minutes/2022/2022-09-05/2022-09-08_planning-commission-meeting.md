3.3) Public hearing and consideration of Jeff Southard’s request for approval of the
Bellaview Place Concept Plan, a commercial development located at the corner of 1200
West and 3200 North in a Commercial zone.

Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jeff Southard, was present. He talked about some of the staff recommendations,
specifically making more walkability. The concept plan would be phased into three phases. He
felt it was appropriate to bring all of it in for concept review. The drive-throughs are important
for the entire project and will be part of the traffic project. There will be significant slope and
will be brought down to the elevation of the surrounding area. There have not been any
landscape designs done yet.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing and acknowledged the online comments that
were both in favor and opposed of the development.

Jeremy Scow stated he understands there are three phases. There is a sixteen year lease on the
Fabian House that he wanted the Commission to be aware of.

Derek Owens, a Lehi resident, left a comment online. Belleview Assisted Living has more of a
Neighborhood Commercial vibe. He stated the Lehi Clinic Instacare did not justify zone
changes, but Bellview Place is a far cry from Commercial, especially with the park like campus.
Kids that are traveling to Fox Hollow Elementary should be thought of, especially taking 1200
West and 3200 West into consideration. His opinion on low density residential and the neighbors
in the community are that if the Commissioners fail to enforce the General Plan and change the
zoning, he will be very upset. He does not want commercial in the neighborhood.

Mike Shriner lives in Fox Run and would like to make a point that a drive through is the exact
opposite of walkability. There are hundreds of kids walking to school every day. He doesn't
believe there should even be an argument to change Neighborhood Commercial to Commercial.
IF the development is going to happen, please don't make exceptions to code, above and beyond
(precast walls, etc.) make it fit with the surrounding buildings.

Tyler Anderson would like to see the buildings kept the same height, as much as possible, as the
rest of the surrounding buildings. He would like to see a plan that fits within the current zoning
to keep the impact as minimal as possible. He understands it is zoned Commercial but would
prefer it to be Neighborhood Commercial. He would prefer a compromise with the front half
being Neighborhood Commercial. If the zone is considered to be changed, he would like to see it
contingent on a specific plan. One of the most important items is the buffering within the zoning.
He would like to see an 8 foot stone wall fence to reduce noise. The garbage can is against the
back fence that borders his house, and he would like to see that moved. He would like to know
what the timing requirement is with the fencing if they are running a business. He expressed the
detention pond is poorly maintained and would be in favor of a park.

Shawn Goodwin stated the thought and idea of having a family friendly area or park would be
nice. He welcomes the idea of dining and retail.

Landon Laycock wanted to reiterate that drive though are not allowed in Neighborhood
Commercial and was meant to transition residential to Neighborhood Commercial. A three story
building would be a big issue, especially where the building would be adjacent to residential
buildings and is elevated already. If drive-throughs are level with the street, there would be a
good chance that someone could get hit by a vehicle.
Troy Sheffield lives south of the retention pond. He stated if the way the pond has been
maintained is any indication of how the development will be taken care of, then it will be tall
weeds. He stated 1200 West has gradually transitioned from residential to Neighborhood
Commercial to Commercial and it will end up in an accident, and it won't be at the expense of
the builder.

(inaudible) stated he is surprised there has not been a bad accident on 1200 West and 3200
North. There have been near close accidents with school kids. He stated there have also been
safety issues at night involving the nursing home. Adding more businesses will just increase the
chances of accidents and speeding and the risk of things getting worse.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

The applicant addressed the concerns from the public. He is more than willing to look at the
sidewalk along 3200 North where the kids walk to school. He will talk to staff to address the
issues. He is fine to meet the requirements of the Neighborhood Commercial and Commercial.
The applicant does not own the detention pond and is not responsible for maintaining it. Staff
stated they would re-evaluate and let the Commissioners know. The Commissioners and the
applicant discussed ways to improve the concept and design options. Staff talked about ramp
up/ramp down continuous sidewalk for the pedestrian safety as the best option to implement.
Staff stated a key switch has been installed where pedestrian crossing time has been increased
and the yellow arrow has been disabled.

Motion to reopen by Commissioner Eyre. Commissioner Jackson seconded. All in favor.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Zayn Carter brought up the concern about the sidewalks being too small and narrow, along with
any knowledge with the companies coming in such as a vaping or CDC company.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Kim Struthers stated there is a limit on tobacco shops per population. Commissioner Britton said
the concept plan feels a lot like the development near the park and ride north of I-15 and she
feels the developer can do better. Commissioner Newall can see where exceptions can be made
but does not feel like this is the right area for this where there is no residential near them. She
would rather see a walk up restaurant. She doesn't like the threat of storage being put right next
to residential and a huge source of traffic can be prevented and can be walkable. Commissioner
Ellis is not comfortable with the drive throughs with this concept. Commissioner Bunker feels
things could be done better to have things done all at once, with things that we are approving and
not approving. Commissioner Nielsen said the drainage area could be improved, along with the
parking. Commissioner Ellis said there are conditions and exceptions that need to be
included/not included in the motion moving forward, specifically with the drive throughs.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to approve Jeff Southard’s request for
        approval of the Bellaview Place Concept Plan, a commercial development located at
        the corner of 1200 West and 3200 North in a Commercial zone; including all DRC
        comments and findings stated in the report. Noting the public comments, specifically
        pointing to no commitment to the drive throughs, placing value on walkability and
        connectivity to the entirety of the project (through the neighborhood, to the retention
        pond, and to the Assisted Living area), moving the garbage area, cutting back on the
        parking since it is over the minimum requirement, a strong division between the drive
        through and walkability should be considered, considerations of the sidewalk on 3200
        West with specific thoughts for the kids walking to school, the retention pond to use for
        an attractive draw for the community, recommend developer to work with Staff for the
        buffering and variance, conform Neighborhood Commercial to adjust with the
        surrounding area, building needs to fit the current zoning. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; no; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed 5 – 2.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of the
Downtown Civic Campus zone change on various City owned properties changing the
zoning from R-3 (high density residential) and Mixed Use to Public Facility.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing.

Jeff Sermon represented Lois Littleford. Jeff asked if this would be affecting Lois's property. Jeff
asked if the City owned any of the surrounding properties. The City stated they owned one of the
properties.

Marlin Peterson stated he owns the property south of the parking lot of the Legacy Center. He is
concerned what will happen with his property where his fence has been moved two feet and if
the property will be affected. The Fire Department asked him to move his fence two feet and he
is concerned if the property will still be his. He also asked about garbage bins in the parking lot.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to send a pos rec with the findings stated in the report. City
held to standards and compliance by own code. Encourage to work with City staff to rectify lot
lines. seconded by Commissioner Jackson. All in favor.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to send a positive recommendation of Downtown
        Civic Campus zone change on various City owned properties changing the zoning
        from R-3 (high density residential) and Mixed Use to Public Facility; including all
        DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Noting the City be held to standards and
        compliance of the City code and encourage City Staff to rectify lot lines. Commissioner
        Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
