           1. 2         Mayor' s Report



Mayor Kristie Overson reported that various elected officials had attended the ChamberWest Gala
where Sen. Wayne Harper was named Legislator of the Year. Taylorsville' s Nothing Bundt Cakes
was named Small Business of the Year.

The Mayor had attended the monthly hearing of the Administrative Law Judge. She was reminded
how hard the Code Enforcement division worked and what remarkable communication they
fostered with residents. The goal was not to collect fines but instead to gain compliance and to
improve the city.

Det. Christensen, Chief Cottam and the Mayor watched students at Bennion Elementary get sworn
in as safety patrol officers for the new school year. This was always a fun event, and she enjoyed
being a part of it.

What was once known            as BRT      would   now   be known as the MVX, which stood for "
                                                                                  Mid-Valley
Express."Mayor Overson had attended a kick- off event for this project which would eventually
connect Murray, West Valley and Taylorsville via public transportation.

The      sculptures      displayed   for   the   Plaza +    Art   program   would   soon   be   rotated   out.   She

recommended that anyone interested in seeing the current pieces make a point to visit them before
the end of the month.


An annual Day of Service would be celebrated soon. She encouraged everyone to check the city' s
website and social media for volunteer opportunities. She pointed out that this would be an
excellent opportunity to find a new way to serve the community.

The WFWRD dumpster program had begun the day before and would continue through the end of
the month.


The week before, Mayor Overson had represented Taylorsville at the Big Bend Habitat Restoration
project. She was pleased to join Congressman Burgess Owens, West Jordan' s Mayor Burton, and
the Jordan River Commission in celebrating this beautiful recreation area.

The mayor wished to thank UFA firefighters for their hard work on a Taylorsville house fire. She
appreciated the care and compassion they exhibited while doing their jobs.

Mayor Overson asked Ms: Roberts about possibly moving the glass recycling container that was

WFWRD was considering the possibility of leasing trucks in the future as a cost- saving measure.
They were also testing the emissions of new diesel trucks and comparing them with the output of
CNG trucks. If it was now comparable as some were suggesting, it might make sense to use more
diesel trucks in the future since they were less expensive. There was also a higher likelihood that
the diesel trucks could be re- sold when WFWRD was through with them.
