Public Hearing (6:01 PM)
1)
Public hearing regarding a Special Exception to Section 11-6-8 (G) of the Code as well as County Ordinance 181-D, Appendix B, Section 3. The Applicant is requesting the use of an existing driveway that has slopes over 12%, driveway width of 10 feet, average grade of the driveway over 11%. This driveway is 889 feet in length which would also require turnouts every 200 feet because it is less than 20 feet in width. The property is located at 1207 East Chalk Creek Road. Jarime Nance
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report and Submitted Doccuments.pdf
Attachment: Louder Special Exception Request Hearing Documents 9-18-2024
Jarime Nance, Public Works Inspector, presented the request for a Special Exception. (6:02 PM)
Applicant’s representative Dirk Rockhill presented the request for the exception. (6:03 PM)
Applicant Braeden Louder elaborated on the request. (6:08 PM)
Council Members commented and asked questions. The Applicant and his representative along with Inspector Nance responded. (6:13 PM)
Chair Stevens opened the public hearing. (6:36 PM)
David Wright provided input on the proposed exception. (6:37 PM)
Chair Stevens closed the public hearing. (6:45 PM)
The applicant will consider further modifications to the driveway, and the Council took no action. (6:49 PM)
