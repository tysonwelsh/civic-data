                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-14


   A RESOLUTION OF RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL FORMALLY TERMINATING
       RIVERTON CITY’S EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT WITH KONRAD
               HILDEBRANDT, RIVERTON CITY MANAGER


WHEREAS, On March 27, 2018, the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No 18-27;
approving an Employment Agreement with Konrad Hildebrandt authorizing Mr. Hildebrandt to
serve as the Riverton City Manager; and
WHEREAS, the Employment Agreement established an at-will employment relationship
between Mr. Hildebrandt and Riverton City; and
WHEREAS, the Employment Agreement establishes that it may be terminated with or without
cause; and
WHEREAS, under the terms of the Employment Agreement, Mr. Hildebrandt’s employment
may be terminated without cause when “a majority of the governing body votes to terminate the
Employee at a duly authorized public meeting… .”
WHEREAS, upon deliberation, the Riverton City Council has elected to shift the direction of
the City in fulfillment of its purpose to provide municipal services


       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:


Section 1. Riverton City Council hereby “votes to terminate” the Riverton City’s employer
           relationship with its City Manager, Konrad Hildebrandt, in fulfillment of Section 9.
           A. of the Employment Agreement, first approved by adoption of Resolution No. 18-
           27.
Section 2. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
