       Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
       Meeting scheduled January 24, 2023, in order for the City Council to hear and
       consider public comments regarding application Z-11-2022, filed by West Valley
       City, Requesting a Zone Change for Property Within the Lake Park Corporate Centre
       from M (Manufacturing) to BRP (Business/Research Park) on 384.8 Acres.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

          Staff is proposing this zone change for the following reasons:

                 The General Plan designation within the Lake Park Corporate Centre
                  for the areas outside of the golf course is Business Park.
                 A portion of the Lake Park Corporate Centre is already zoned BRP.
                 The Lake Park Corporate Centre is largely developed as an office park.
                 Suburban Land Reserve (SLR) is no longer involved in the governing
                  board of the Lake Park Corporate Centre.
                 The Council recently rezoned a parcel within Lake Park that was
                  partially zoned M to BRP.
                 There is significantly more land within the City zoned M than BRP.
                 Only one of the 12 landowners that would be affected by this
                  application expressed concerns about the change. Those concerns are
                  addressed in the proposed revisions to the BRP zone standards
                  (application ZT-5-2022).

       Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing.

       Fred Cox stated that he is speaking in favor of this rezone with the proposed
       amendments recommended by the Planning Commission. He indicated that he

C.     ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION ZT-5-2022,
       FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING TO AMEND SECTIONS
       7-6-301 AND 7-6-308 OF THE ZONING ORDINANCE BY REVISING THE
       USE RESTRICTIONS AND DESIGN STANDARDS FOR THE BRP
       (BUSINESS/RESEARCH PARK) ZONE
       Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
       Meeting scheduled January 24, 2023, in order for the City Council to hear and
       consider public comments regarding application ZT-5-2022, Filed by West Valley
       City, Requesting to Amend Sections 7-6-301 and 7-6-308 of the Zoning Ordinance by
       Revising the Use Restrictions and Design Standards for the BRP (Business/Research
       Park) Zone

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:
MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING JANUARY 24, 2023
                       -5-

     Staff’s original proposal added the following standards to the BRP zone:
          limit the amount of warehousing to 60% of all buildings on a lot,
          limit the amount of light industrial to 60% of all buildings on a lot,
          prohibit overhead doors from facing high-image arterial streets, and
          require warehouses to meet the commercial design standards.

     After receiving input from Lake Park Project, LLC, a property owner within
     Lake Park Corporate Centre who expressed concerns about the proposed zone
     change from M (Manufacturing) to BRP, the Planning Commission
     recommended that the following land uses be allowed in the BRP zone:
          brew restaurant, brew restaurant and liquor retailer, and small brewer;
          fast food establishment with drive-up window; and
          indoor event center.

     Staff is forwarding the proposed amendments for two reasons. First, the two
     main areas of the City with BRP zoning – Lake Park Corporate Centre and
     Metro Business Park – are predominantly office uses. The proposed use
     restrictions will help prevent these areas from becoming predominantly
     industrial. Second, given the large amount of property zoned to allow
     industrial uses, there are plenty of opportunities for exclusively industrial use.
     Adding the proposed standards to the BRP zone will further differentiate it
     from other zones.

  Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing.

  Fred Cox stated that he is speaking in favor of this application. He noted that the
  property owner he represents would be in line with the proposal as outlined.

  Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

RESOLUTION 23-06: APPROVE A SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH UTOPIA TO
CONNECT THE RIDGE GOLF COURSE
Wayne Pyle, City Manager, discussed proposed Resolution 23-06 that would approve a
Service Agreement with UTOPIA to Connect the Ridge Golf Course.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Installing fiber to The Ridge will give the golf course the reliability needed to
       provide services to the public. UTOPIA will provide 1G internet services and also
       a 1G link to City Hall for access to servers and services.

       The Ridge’s existing internet services are through a wireless radio that has proven
       unreliable sometimes. All other city-owned facilities have direct UTOPIA services,
       which have proven highly reliable and effective for connecting our resources and
       providing excellent internet services.

Councilmember Harmon stated that he will be voting no on this item because he has a hard
time with budget openings. Councilmember Huynh asked if Starlink is still an option.
Wayne replied that it is but staff wouldn’t recommend it because the existing wireless
internet would be a close comparison at lesser cost. Councilmember Harmon stated that

Starlink does have a 30-day money back guarantee. Councilmember Nordfelt stated that
Starlink will cost the City more money in the long run. He indicated that it will be $500
month while UTOPIA will now be $50 a month with the system running through City Hall.
He noted that the initial cost is more with UTOPIA. Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if
