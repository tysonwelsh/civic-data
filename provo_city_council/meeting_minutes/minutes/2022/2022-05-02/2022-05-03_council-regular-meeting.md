An introduction video of Mayor Kaufusi was shared.

John Borget, Director of Administrative Services, presented the tentative budget.

Councilor Handley asked if the graph reflects potential increases of other cities.

Mr. Borget responded that the graph shows Provo’s increase but does not reflect other cities’ possible
increases.

Councilor MacKay asked about the current city match to Downtown Provo, Inc.

Councilor Whipple asked what Granicus is.

Councilor Ellsworth asked about the total of the part-time Reclamation Positions.

Councilor Fillmore asked where the significant increase is coming from. He also asked where the
revenue would be coming from to cover the increase.

Mr. Borget provided summary.

Councilor Whipple thanked Councilor Fillmore for asking those tough questions and shared her concern
for overspending the ARPA funds.

the details of the ARPA funds. He echoed Mr. Borget’s statements of being able to provide more
information to council after that meeting.

Councilor MacKay asked if the study that was being done at the airport had been completed.

Dave Decker, Director of Public Works, responded and said the study had been completed.

Melanie McCoard, of Provo, recommended more positions for the Police Department. She also
recommended a separate department for the Airport. She shared her concern for the salaries of some of
the staff members in the mayor’s office. She added that the funding around the roundabouts on Slate
Canyon Drive should be used instead for zoning enforcement staff.

8.         A resolution authorizing Provo City to enter into an interlocal agreement with the
           Redevelopment Agency of Provo City authorizing the use of tax increment in the Freedom
           Plaza community development project area. (22-045) (1:39:49)

      Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-22, as currently constituted, has been
                   made by council rule.

David Walter, Redevelopment Director, provided background information.

Councilor MacKay asked where the tax increment would be going to and how long the agreement would
be for.

Mr. Walter responded that it would help offset the cost of providing parking. He stated the increase
agreement would last for 15 years.

Councilor Ellsworth asked about the agreement that was approved at the beginning of the development.

Mr. Walter gave history of the development.

Councilor Fillmore clarified that this agreement has not changed from the beginning of the
development.

Chair Hoban opened item for public comment. With none, he closed public comment.

Councilor Shipley asked about the parking arrangement and if it allowed for public parking.

Councilor Fillmore asked if it would be paid parking.

Mr. Walter said he did not believe that had been worked out yet. He added that the city has some
influence and is hoping they will allow for free public parking when not in use for conventions.

Councilor MacKay asked how many parking spots would be available.

Mr. Walter said he believed it to be just over four hundred.

Chair Hoban reopened public comment as there was someone online that did not get the opportunity to
speak.

Melanie McCoard, of Provo, shared her concern that the council has not received sufficient briefing
prior to tonight’s presentation.

Chair Hoban brought discussion back to council.

Mr. Jones shared some information about a lawsuit that resulted in this resolution being drafted as part
of litigation.

Councilor Shipley said he would appreciate a discussion regarding the parking history in the city. He also
asked if the city is receiving value from these types of agreements.

Councilor Fillmore asked Mr. Parker what the administration’s position is to address the downtown
need for parking.

Mr. Parker said that these agreements are a step forward to address the solution, but each agreement
can not solve all parking issues.

Mr. Jones suggested the council get their questions answered prior to voting.

Mr. Parker clarified that the decision was made in 2018 but the agenda item is to solidify the document.
He also said he would be happy to share any documents with council to help them gain more
understanding.

With no other discussion, Chair Hoban called for a vote.

      Vote:        The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                   Mackay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.
