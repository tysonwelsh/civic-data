1.     PUBLIC HEARING – An Application to Approve the Extension of a Final
       Subdivision Plat Approval to Amend a Three-Lot Plat, Located at 3798 South 700
       East, 3802 South 700 East, and 3838 South 700 East.

Action Item

Applicant:     C.W. Urban, represented by Chase Freebairn
Address:       3798, 3802, 3838 South 700 East

City Planner, Tereza Bagdasarova, presented the Staff Report and explained that the application is
for the Center Square Subdivision Plat Amendment extension for 3798-3838 South 700 East. On
February 6, 2023, Ian Cahoon, representing C.W. Urban, submitted a Plat Amendment application
to amend a three-lot subdivision located at 3798 South, 3802 South, and 3838 South 700 East. On
June 1, 2023, the Planning Commission unanimously approved the Preliminary Subdivision Plat
Amendment. On June 15, 2023, the Planning Commission approved the Final Subdivision Plat
Amendment. That came with a condition to amend the Center Square Subdivision Plat, which is
owned by Medical Leasing LTD as part of the C.W. Urban redevelopment to the west.

On January 19, 2024, C.W. Urban submitted a plat application to petition the Planning
Commission for a 365-day extension of the Final Subdivision Plat for the Center Square
Subdivision Plat. There were some missing documents that were required for the Center Square
Subdivision Plat extension, which is why the item is now before the Planning Commission. C.W.
Urban has been actively working to fulfill the Conditions of Approval. The condition to amend the
Center Subdivision Plat, which is owned by Medical Leasing LTD, took longer than anticipated
to sign because the lot is not owned by C.W. Urban. The C.W. Urban Plat Extension application,
along with all supporting documents and analysis of the code and General Plan goals, does not
include any changes from the original application. Ms. Bagdasarova reported that C.W. Urban has
submitted the Amended Plat application for the Center Square Plat application. The existing
conditions, Site Plan, Final Plat, and the recently submitted Amended Plat were all reviewed.

C.W. Urban's Plat Extension application, along with supporting documentation and analysis of the
pertinent code sections and General Plan goals, does not include any changes from the original
application and Staff Report for the Final Subdivision Plat Amendment. The General Plan
considerations remain unchanged and are enumerated in the Staff Report. Staff recommends the
Planning Commission approve the petition for an extension lasting one year from the date of this
request, focusing on the Final Subdivision Plat Amendment to amend an existing three-lot
subdivision, located at 3798 South, 3802 South, and 3838 South 700 East, based upon the

2.      PUBLIC MEETING - An Application for Final Plat Approval to Consolidate Two
        Existing Parcels into One Lot, for a Parking Lot Expansion, Located at 245 West
        Plymouth Avenue.

Action Item

Applicant:     Brett Bryson
Address:       245 West Plymouth Avenue and 3791 South 300 West, Sevillo Fine Foods

City Planner, Jed Shum, presented the Staff Report and explained that the application is for the
Sevillo Spring Brands Subdivision Plat. This is for the Final Plat as the Preliminary Plat was
approved in December 2023. The site is located at 3791 South 300 West and 245 West Plymouth
Avenue. Mr. Shum shared an image of the two lots. The white parcel is the current food processing
use structure on the south side. The yellow parcel will be combined and will become the parking
lot. He reported that the current zone is TOD. The current site does not provide sufficient parking,
but with the new parking lot, it will become more conforming with the code.

Mr. Shum shared the Vicinity Map and images of the existing conditions. The parking lot is
currently unpaved because a Building Permit cannot be issued for the pavement until the lots are
platted. Currently, there are no vehicles parked on site. The Proposed Final Plat was shared. It
includes a right-of-way dedication on the north touching Plymouth Avenue. On the north edge,
there is only 5 feet of easement instead of 10 feet, because the existing structure is only 5 feet away
from the property line. The Proposed Site Plan for the parking lot was reviewed. During the
South Salt Lake City Planning Commission Regular Meeting                                           5
February 15, 2024

Planning Commission Meeting for the Preliminary Plat approval process, the property owner to
the west asked questions about access to the Sevillo site. City Staff double-checked and there is an
existing easement for the Sevillo access. The applicant has been speaking to the property owner.

The General Plan considerations were shared, which included the following items:

     •   Community Values Goal 1: Ensure that existing and new developments have equitable
         access to all services and amenities in South Salt Lake.
            o Analysis: The proposed new plat will provide direct road access to Plymouth
                 Avenue for the business located at 3791 South 300 West, which currently does not
                 have public street access and must access the site through another property.
     •   Transportation Goal 1: Improve the active transportation system to enhance South Salt
         Lake as a safe and enjoyable place to walk and bicycle.
            o Analysis: The workers at Sevillo currently tend to park on the roadside or at
                 Meadow Brook Station and then walk over to the site. The approval of this plan
                 will indirectly improve the active transportation system within the City by reducing
                 traffic flow and parking cars in the active transportation system.

Based upon the information submitted in the application, supporting documentation, and analysis
of the relevant code sections and General Plan goals, Staff recommends the Planning Commission
move to approve the Final Subdivision Plat Amendment to consolidate the two unsubdivided
parcels, located at 3791 South 300 West and 245 West Plymouth Avenue, based on the Findings
of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval for the subdivision in the Staff Report.

Motion to APPROVE the application by Brett Bryson, on behalf of Sevillo Foods, for a Final
Subdivision Plat Amendment to consolidate an unsubdivided parcel, located at 3791 South

Report and with the conditions enumerated in the Staff Report and on the record:

3.       PUBLIC HEARING - An Application for Preliminary Plat Approval for a Two-Lot
         Plat, to Construct and Operate Flex Buildings, Located at 800 West 3300 South.

Action Item
South Salt Lake City Planning Commission Regular Meeting                                            6

Applicant:     Dakota Pacific, Represented by Will Clark
Address:       800 West 3300 South

Deputy Community Development Director, Eliza Ungricht, presented the Staff Report and
explained that the application is for the Central 15 Preliminary Plat. Last February, the City
Council approved a zone change on this property from Commercial Corridor to Flex. The applicant
is present to consolidate the properties via a plat application. The existing conditions were shared.
The existing parcels cover part of the golf course and part of the sewer treatment plant. The
property to the south of Central Valley Road is the area that Dakota Pacific has leased and will
develop. Due to the complexity of the easement, there will be parcels on the plat, which is not
normally seen. The Preliminary Plat was shared. Ms. Ungricht reported that the code differentiates
a lot from a parcel, so in order to receive a Building Permit, there needs to be a legal lot. For the
parcels, it is not possible to obtain a Building Permit unless those become lots.

Currently, two lots are being reviewed. There is access on 3300 South and 900 West. Ms. Ungricht
shared the ALTA Survey and explained that the yellow line shown through the center is a 30-foot
sewer easement. It is one of the only easements that will remain in place. The proposed
development will not build over that easement. She next shared the Site Plan. The red box
highlights the first phase of the project. For the two other buildings to the east, the applicant will
need to come back and plat that property before that phase can be built. Proposed renderings were
shared. Ms. Ungricht reported that the buildings proposed meet all of the design standards.

The project meets multiple General Plan considerations, including the following:

   •   Land Use and Neighborhoods Strategy 2:
          o Identify areas of the City for higher-density employment-based uses to take
              advantage of existing and planned infrastructure.
   •   Land Use and Neighborhoods Strategy 4:
          o Require a high level of design within the public realm of commercial and mixed-
              use areas.
   •   Land Use and Neighborhoods Strategy 6:
          o Implement best practices in the regulation of development and land uses.
   •   Community Values Goal 3:
          o Take advantage of opportunities relating to the City’s location at the center of the
              regional transportation, transit, trails, open space, library, recreation, and school
              systems to serve residents, businesses, and visitors.
   •   Economic Development Goal 1:
          o Maximize South Salt Lake’s social and economic equity through value-based
              decision-making.
   •   Economic Development Strategy 8:
          o Target industries for South Salt Lake's growing business hubs including Downtown
              and the 3300 South corridor.
   •   Parks, Recreation, & Open Space Goal 2:
          o Identify opportunities to add parks, open space, and recreation amenities in
              currently underserved areas of the City.
   •   Environmental, Social, & Economic Sustainability Strategy 4:

             sustainable landscaping.
