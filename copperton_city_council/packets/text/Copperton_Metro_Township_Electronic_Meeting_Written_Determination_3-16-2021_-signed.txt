                         Electronic Meeting Written Determination
                                           of the
                                Copperton Metro Township
                                            for
                          Regular Council Meeting to be Held on
                                      March 17, 2021


Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(4), I, Mayor Sean Clayton, have determined that the
Copperton Metro Township Council will hold its regular Council meeting on March 17, 2021, at
6:30, without an anchor location due to the COVID19 pandemic and the risks it poses to the
health and safety of those persons who would be present at an anchor location. This written
determination shall expire 30 days from today.
Dated this 16th day of March, 2021.


                                          _________________________________
                                          Sean Clayton, Mayor
