GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-1-2023 Petition by RUSS NELSON requesting a General Plan change from Non-Retail
           Commercial to General Commercial and a zone change from RB (Residential
           Business) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located at 4543 and
           4547 S 4000 W on 0.73 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik stated the subject property consists of two lots, which might look
                 familiar to the Commission because there was a request to rezone the same
                 parcels a year ago. Steve said at the time, and the applicant proposed adding an
                 access off 40th West. The Public Works Department said the proposed access
                 was too close to the existing access, and they would need to work with the
                 neighboring owner to share the current access. Unfortunately, they could not
                 agree, and the applicant requested that the proposal be denied to get them out of
                 their contract.
               Steve stated that this request is for a quick Quack Carwash. He noted that there
               is an existing daycare on the subject property. Steve said the applicant is
               proposing an entrance-only access on 40th West, hoping they would be granted
               an exception. Steve explained that Public Works could, once again, require
               shared access. He noted that the application could be tabled or pulled depending
               upon the decision of the Public Works Department.

               Commissioner McEwen expressed concern about the water that runs into the
               street from the carwash causing slippery and icy roads. Steve asked if he was
               referencing the water dripping off the cars or coming from the carwash.
               Commissioner McEwen said he was talking about the water dripping off the
               vehicles.

               Commissioner Winters pointed out that many newer carwashes are adjacent to
               residential areas. He asked Steve if the City had received any complaints
               regarding the noise level of the vacuums. Steve said he was unaware of any
               complaints but will look into it. Jody Knapp said she had been involved with
               developing a previous Quick Quack Carwash business in West Valley City. She
               pointed out a central location inside the building that houses the vacuum
               equipment, and the hoses are run from there to be used outside. Jody said the
               carwash is quiet relative to road noise. She noted that the business would have
               specs if the Commission were interested in viewing those.

               Commissioner Lovato asked how they would cue drive-up traffic using a shared
               access. Steve used the concept plan to show how cueing could be possible.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SA-2-2023   Petition by ROMAN FRAZIER is requesting preliminary approval for the
            Mountain View Business Plaza Subdivision Amended. This application will
            amend and extend lot 3 of said subdivision and will consist of 1 lot on 1.8
            acres. The proposed amended subdivision is located at 3320 South 5600 West
            and zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311
            or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman explained that the applicant, Roman Frazier, is the gentleman
               who converted the abandoned Kmart on 5600 West to inside storage. As part of
               that, he purchased several other empty lots for the Mountain View Business
               Plaza Subdivision. Steve stated that Mr. Frazier had recently acquired an
               adjacent lot with an existing carwash. Steve said he intends to combine that lot
               with lot 3 of the Subdivision creating lot 3A. He noted that Mr. Frazier wants to
               keep the carwash in operation until he finds a better use for the property.

               Steve Pastorik pointed out that the existing sign is missing panels. He said the
               applicant would be making the repairs.
There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:46 p.m.
