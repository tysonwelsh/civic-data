ACTION
 ITEMS 
• Chair Bernal asked if everyone had a chance to review the Agenda and the Action Items.  
 
• Resolution #2025-1138: Approving Disposition of Interfund Balances (DPCU):  
o Chief Financial Officer Aniban presented the Resolution.  The overall discussion will provide context for 
the two resolutions, the Interfund Balances for Component Units and the Interfund balances on Various 
Grants. If you recall (the September presentations with the auditors), one of the findings we had was the 
Interfund balances and the finance team was tasked to resolve this by the end of the year (2025). It is 
trying to get those entities or programs that owe Housing Connect, be it an entity or a program within 
Housing Connect, to pay back money to our main disbursement account. We normally set up Operatin g 
Reserves and Replacement Reserves at the properties. We can use the Operating Reserves to pay for the 
deficits. If it’s used for Capital Improvements, we can  use Replacement Reserves. We will set up a notes 
receivable to document this liability.  
o We operate on a reimbursement basis, meaning we advance the money first, and then we seek 

2 | Page  
 
reimbursement from the entities or programs . Ideally, these funds, both operationally and financially, 
should zero out. But that is not always the case, due to nuances or timing issues. There were some items 
that were not fully matching up, meaning we advanced the money more than we collected, and this 
resulted in the accumulation of the Interfund Balances. We are changing this,  and we have opened and 
will continue to open bank accounts for each program, whenever practical. This will serve as a trigger for 
us to ask if the program has the money to pay us back.  Otherwise, we can put a hold or a flag there and 
then we can go to the investor and let the investor know, we’re overspending here – we need to be paid 
back. We have identified two properties under the Discretely Presented Component Units we are 
proposing for write-off. It is Bud Bailey Apartments and Bodhi, which as of September 2025 owe Housing 
Connect $752,000 (Bud Bailey Apartments I, LLC) and $509,875 (Bodhi Salt Lake City, L.P.) 
o Commissioner Ramos made the motion to Approve the Disposition of Interfund Balances (DPCU); Vice -
Chair Nguyen seconded the motion. All Board members present (Chair Bernal, Vice -Chair Nguyen, 
Commissioners Johnson, Leonelli, and Ramos) voted In Favor of Approving Disposition of Interfund 
Balances (DPCU) with none opposed. The motion has passed, and the Disposition of Interfund Balances 
(DPCU) is approved. 
 
• Resolution #2025-1139: Approving Disposition of Interfund Balances on Various Grants:  
o Chief Financial Officer Aniban presented the Resolution. These are Interfund Balances related to the local 
and State grants, specifically relating to Salt Lake City HOME (Home Investment Partnerships Program), 
Salt Lake City HOPWA (Housing Opportunities for Persons with AIDS), and the RESH (Refugee Employment 
Subsidized Housing) grant. This has accumulated over the years, since 2011. We are proposing to write 
off these balances as the funds have been spent and it’s a matter of cleaning up the books. This doesn’t 
impact us financially if we do this now. CEO Kimball added, we knew the HOPWA funding was ending as 
that was a part of a change in state funding. We knew we were going short there as we were able to move 
those households around and continue their housing assistance with other funding. Chair Bernal added 
that one way to look at it, it is our (Housing Connect’s) contribution. 
o Vice-Chair Nguyen made the motion to Approve the Disposition of Interfund Balances on Various Grants; 
Commissioner Ramos seconded the motion. All Board members present (Chair Bernal, Vice-Chair Nguyen, 
Commissioners Johnson, Leonelli, and Ramos) voted In Favor of Approving Disposition of Interfund 
Balances on Various Grants with none opposed. The motion has passed, and the Disposition of Interfund 
Balances on Various Grants is approved. CFO Aniban thanked everyone, and made note there are other 
funds being analyzed. Once the analysis is completed, we might come back to the Board with a resolution 
or direction in disposition of the other funds.
