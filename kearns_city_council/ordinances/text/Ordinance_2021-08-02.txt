                               KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP

ORDINANCE NO.: 2021-08-02                                          DATE: August 9, 2021


    AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADDING
    USED CAR DEALERSHIP AS A PERMITTED USE TO THE C-2 ZONE IN THE
               KEARNS TOWNSHIP CODE SECTION 19.62.020

                                          RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Ann. § 10-2a-401, et seq; and

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (“Council”) is required to approve
changes to the Kearns Municipal Code of Ordinances; and

        WHEREAS, a request has been made to add “Used Car Dealership” to the Permitted Use
table in the C-2 zone; and

        WHEREAS, staff and legal counsel believe this use is appropriate in the zone and
consistent with the other uses allowed within the C-2 zone; and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Planning Commission has recommended that the Council amend
the C-2 Zone to include used car dealerships after holding a duly noticed public hearing on August
9, 2021, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-502, during which time it took public comment on
the proposed change.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED: by the Kearns Metro Township Council the
following ordinance amendments, repeals, and additions to the Kearns Municipal Code of
Ordinances:

   A. “Used Car Dealership” is added to section 19.62.020.

   B. This Ordinance will become effective immediately upon its passage and its publication on
      the Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1)
