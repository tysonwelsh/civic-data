ISSUES:

      The applicant is requesting preliminary subdivision approval for the Han Subdivision. This
       is the first residential sustainability subdivision staff has processed. This zone was
       established to allow lots of 8,000 square feet but would require home builders to build
       homes with high energy efficiency and low water use.

      The subdivision will consist of 15 lots on approximately 4.4 acres. This equates to an
       overall density of 3.4 units per acre. Lot sizes range from 8,003 to 12,680 square feet. The
       average in the subdivision is approximately 8,700 square feet. Some of the unique
       requirements of the RS zone are as follows:

            o    A solar system capable of generating power for the entire home.
            o    Increase the R value of all insulation for the home.
            o    Tankless water heater.
            o    Electric car charger to be located inside the garage.
            o    WaterSense fixtures to be used for all water sources.
            o    Exterior of the home will have 80% brick
            o    Roof of each home will have an 8:12 roof pitch
            o    Special consideration for yard landscaping to reduce water consumption.

       There are also interior requirements that builders will incorporate into the dwelling that
       will upgrade and improve the overall quality of the dwelling.

      Access to the subdivision will be gained from 3200 West. The subdivision will be served
       by one road which terminates at the south end with a cul-de-sac. The street serving this
       development will be dedicated and improved to a 54-foot half width including curb, gutter,
       sidewalk and parkstrip.

       The preliminary plat shows a gap along the south side of approximately 8 feet between the
       road and adjacent property to the south. By default, this creates a protection strip which is
       not allowed by City ordinance. Staff is recommending that the right-of-way shift to the
       south to abut the adjacent property. The intersection at 3200 West would be redesigned to
       accommodate the new alignment. There would be a small section at the southeast corner
       of the intersection that would be stamped concrete to accommodate this change. There is
       an additional benefit of shifting the road to the south which is that the lots along the north
       side of the main road would increase in size by approximately 650 square feet.

      The subject property has been vacant for years. Staff does not believe it has actively been
     irrigated for quite some time. The developer will coordinate any irrigation issues that may
     be part of this property with the Engineering Division.

    The applicant has submitted a soils report which indicated that groundwater was
     encountered at depths ranging from 6.5 to 9.5 feet. City ordinance requires that the lowest
     floor slab is to be 3 feet about the high water table. Lots in the cul-de-sac will have a more
     shallow basement, but as you move northward, lots should be able to have a more typical
     basement depth.

    The subdivision contains a small parcel to manage storm water runoff. Based on new
     federal regulations for Low Impact Development (LID), storm water is to be treated
     differently than what we’ve done previously. The goal of LID is to mimic natural processes
     such as infiltration, filtration, storage by focusing on conservation and use of on site
     features and stormwater management practices. The site is to contain the 80th percentile
     storm event with the remaining 20 percent allowed to leave the site.

    The City Engineering Division has suggested eliminating the pond with the LID volume
     being stored under the sidewalk or in a smaller parcel behind the sidewalk using an
     underground storage system like stormtech or similar. This would eliminate the problems
     associated with standing water and could allow what is being shown as Parcel A to be
     absorbed into the adjacent lot. The City would require an easement to manage the storm
     water from the street.

    The storm water retention issue is pretty fluid at this point, and a final determination has
     yet to be made. Staff and the City Engineering Division will continue to work with the
     developer and their engineer to come up with a solution that works for the conditions of
     the subdivision and to meet LID requirements.

    The subdivision borders certain properties to the east that are still zoned A. City ordinance
     will require that a 6-foot chain link fence be installed along those boundaries.

C-20-2021
Sansone Group - 2215 South Industrial
2215 South 7200 West
M Zone (18.09 Acres)

The applicant, Sansone Group, is requesting a conditional use for a warehouse facility at 2215
South 7200 West. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General
Plan designates this area as Heavy Manufacturing. The surrounding zones are (M),
Manufacturing including the property to the west, which is located within Magna. The
surrounding uses are Mountain West Trucking to the south, Pull-N-Save to the south and east,
excavating and sand/gravel uses to the north (including Kilgore).

The following request is for approval of a warehouse building. The development will consist of
one 328,000 sqft building that could potentially be divided into four tenants. The building will be
constructed of tilt up concrete panels that will be painted with a variety of gray tones. The office
portions of the buildings are enhanced with large windows and relief in the façade and roof line.
These features will be repeated along all sides of the building for added detail. This facility is not
required to meet the Commercial Design Standards due to the use being limited to a
manufacturing zone.

These facilities typically also include some office areas so the uses/tenant mix will be monitored
as building permits and business licenses are submitted and the parking will be evaluated
periodically to ensure that the site is in compliance.

The required landscaping for this site is a minimum of 5%. The plan currently indicates there is
approximately 20% however, that calculation also includes a large retention pond and drainage
area on the west side of the overall site. The 5% area must be improved and irrigated landscaping
so that will be updated as part of the plan review to ensure that requirement is being met.

The current design of the retention and open drainage channel were originally developed with the
previous use that was approved in 2012 for Teancum under C-45-12. The topography of this area
is very flat and Public Works has indicated that the open channel design is preferred at this time
due to this topography as well as maintenance issues with this sort of system. The right-of-way
width for 7200 West is currently 80’ however this area is part of the 2050 Regional
Transportation Plan and does have the potential to be further improved in the future (86’ ROW
with bike lanes, etc.). Therefore, there is a delay agreement for formal improvements that is
being coordinated through Public Works and the formal landscaping is requested to be installed
behind the retention pond adjacent to the parking areas. The area is approximately 146’ with
landscaping along a 10’ strip. That landscaping needs to include 1 tree every 30’ and 50% live
plant material not including tree canopies. The landscaped areas along the north and east sides of
the site must be a minimum of 5’ in width with 1 tree every 60’ and 50% live plant material not
including tree canopies. The southern boundary includes the truck parking areas and the drainage
ditch/easement. Therefore, the applicant has requested to not formally landscape this area and
will just maintain the natural vegetation in the drainage area. Additional landscaping will also
be added throughout the parking areas and along the building to satisfy the minimum
landscaping requirements outlined in the City Code.
Details of signage have not been discussed at this stage in the development. All signage shall
comply with the requirements set forth in the Sign Ordinance and require a building permit prior
to installation. Outside storage has not been requested at this time and all dumpster areas must be
screened or designed in accordance with the standards in 7-2-118.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and the following
conditions.

   1) The building must be constructed per the approved plans. This shall include relief in the
      façade and roof lines as indicated on the submitted renderings and elevations.

   2) There must be adequate parking on site for the proposed tenant mix.

   3) There shall be a minimum of 5% landscaping (improved and irrigated) throughout the
      site including the following:

           a. Formal landscaping is not required directly along the 7200 West
              retention/drainage area but there shall be a minimum of 10’ of improved
              landscaping along the improved parking area with 1 tree every 30’ and 50% live
              plant material not including tree canopies.
           b. A minimum of 5’ of landscaping with 1 tree every 60’ and 50% live plant
              material not including tree canopies is required along the north and east side of
              the property.
           c. The south side setback contains the drainage ditch/easement. Therefore, no formal
              landscaping is required in this area and it shall be maintained as natural
              vegetation.

   4) All drainage areas shall be maintained free of garbage and weeds.

   5) All signage must comply with the requirements set forth in the West Valley City Sign
      Ordinance and require a building permit prior to installation.

   6) All dumpster locations and mechanical equipment on site must be properly screened.

   7) Must meet requirements of all effected departments and agencies.

CA-9-2021
Intermountain Bobcat Expansion (Outside Storage)
2129 S. Technology Park Way
M Zone (1.08 acres)

The applicant, Tom Stuart Construction, representing Intermountain Bobcat, is requesting a
conditional use amendment for outside storage at 2129 S. Technology Park Way. This property
is zoned M, manufacturing and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light
manufacturing. The surrounding zone is M, manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses
include light industrial/outside storage to the east (Rasmussen Equipment), storage units to the
south and office/medical technology uses to the west.

This site was originally developed for Intermountain Bobcat in 2011 (C-6-11). The approval was
for the northern part of the site and included an office, display areas and service with outside
storage on the eastern portion of the site. The property was to be improved per the attached plans
including the landscaping for High Image Arterials along 2100 South. At this time they would
like to expand the outside storage portion to the south. The improvements would include a
crushed limestone/gravel surface similar to the existing outside storage area. The storage will be
screened from public view with a six-foot chainlink fence with slats. The landscaping along the
frontage will be improved and the sidewalk connection will be installed.

There is currently a loading ramp/dock located within this area and several other structures. The
Building Department has indicated permits are required for these structures and will work with
the applicant to get the appropriate approvals. The applicant noted that the area between the
existing chainlink fence and the area to be landscaped will remain unused/undeveloped.
However, Staff recommends that since the loading ramp and maneuvering area is located within
this section it is being used so it should be improved and re-designed so it is also screened or
relocated within the storage yard. That would allow for flexibility of uses like the remaining site
and then the area would be screened and kept maintained free of weeds, etc.

The existing site and landscaping will remain as originally approved and the landscaping will be
updated and maintained to comply with the original approval.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

           1. The approved use for this application is expansion of outside storage.

           2. The outside storage area must be surfaced with gravel/limestone and shall be
              maintained free of weeds throughout the site.
           3. Outside storage is only permitted in areas behind the fencing and must be
              screened from public view.

           4. The (un)loading ramp and equipment maneuvering area shall be relocated and
              incorporated into the storage area or redesigned at its current location so it is
              improved and screened from public view.

           5. The existing landscaping on site must be maintained and improved per the
              original plans.

           6. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

           7. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

north and south and office/medical technology uses to the west.

The storage area will be located on the east side of the site, behind the building, and is not visible
from public view. No storage will be allowed in the parking areas in front of the building. The
other portions of the site and landscaping will be improved per the original plans.

              from public view.

           4. The site and landscaping must be improved per the original plans.
