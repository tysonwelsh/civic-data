                                     ORDINANCE NO. 1453


AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ENACTING SECTION 3-1-320
OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE CREATING THE DRAPER CITY
DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION ADVISORY BOARD.


       WHEREAS, Title 3 of the Draper City Municipal Code (“Title 3”) regulates general
provisions regarding boards, commissions, and committees; and

        WHEREAS, the City is seeking input regarding diversity, equality and inclusivity within
the City, therefore, it is necessary for the city to create the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion
Advisory Board; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text and finds good cause to
amend Title 3 to create the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Board.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.Findings.The City Council of Draper City has made the following findings to
amend Title 3 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) the City is seeking input for initiatives that
cultivate an inclusive culture and stimulate socio-economic equality for the greater community.
2) The City is seeking input regarding diversity, equality and inclusivity within the City. 3) The
proposed amendments are in compliance with the Utah Code Annotated. 4) The proposed
amendments are in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.

       Section 2. Enactment. Section 3-1-320 of the Draper City Municipal Code is hereby
enacted to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

        Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to make non-
substantive corrections to the attached Exhibit A, which may include corrections of punctuation,
spelling, formatting, or similar errors prior to submitting the Ordinance to American Legal
Publishing.

        Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final
passage.

3-1-320 DRAPER CITY DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION ADVISORY BOARD:

A. Creation: There is hereby created the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Board
to act as an advisory board on identifying issues faced by diverse groups and creating
opportunities for an inclusive community within the City.

B. Members:
    1. Number: The Advisory Board shall be comprised of up to nine (9) but not less than five
(5) members who shall be appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City
Council.

    2. Term: Members shall be appointed to terms of two (2) years. In order to provide for
proper staggering of terms so that all appointments do not expire at the same time and to provide
appropriate opportunities for involvement by a variety of interested persons, two (2) initial
membership positions shall be for a term of one (1) year, three initial membership position shall
be for a term of two (2) years.

C. Organization and Procedure:
   1. Chair: The Mayor shall appoint one of the members to serve as Chair of the Advisory
Board for a term of two (2) years. The Chair shall oversee the proceedings and activities of the
board.

   2. Rules: The Advisory Board may adopt reasonable rules in accordance with this chapter to
govern the conduct of its business.

   3. Meetings: The Advisory Board shall hold meetings quarterly or as the Chair deems
necessary as properly called with notice given to each board member. Meetings shall comply
with the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act.

D. Duties:
It shall be the duty of the Advisory Board to act in a voluntary and advisory capacity to the City
regarding diversity, equality and inclusivity activities within the City, including, but not limited
to:
    1. Act as a liaison to inform the City of issues faced by diverse communities and groups.

   2. Advocate and voice support for initiatives that cultivate an inclusive culture and stimulate
      socio-economic equality for the greater community.

   3. Create educational opportunities through guest lectures at local schools and community
      workshops to raise awareness of diversity, equality and inclusivity issues.

   4. Host events for the greater community to celebrate and educate the public about various
      cultures and diverse groups; and

   5. Assist with developing resource materials related to topics of diversity, equality, and
      inclusivity for the City.
