35            Approximately 3.25-Acres Located at 11944 South 3600 West from the General
36            Plan Designation to Low Density Residential to Medium-High Density Residential,

38
39   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director explained this was a public hearing for a
40   General Plan Amendment and Rezone application brought by the applicant for three adjoining
41   parcels totaling 3.25-acres located near 11944 South 3600 West. The subject property sits on
42   the west side of 3600 West on the north bank of Midas Creek. To the north of this property is
43   the recently approved Ivory development “Harvest Gardens”. Ivory Homes rezoned the property
44   that makes up Harvest Gardens to RM-6 SD. During the rezone hearing it was mentioned that
45   the Kunz property may also want to develop in the near future. Part of Ivory’s approval was to
46   include a stub road south to Kunz property, but the Kunz property was not a part of the Ivory
47   rezone, or the Harvest Gardens development. The subject property is currently zoned R-3,
48   which is a single-family residential zone, with 14,000-sf lots. The same R-3 zoning applies to the
49   subdivision to the west, and the next few parcels to the south. To the north is the RM-6 SD
50   mentioned above. To the east across 3600 West Street the Zoning is RR-22 and R-2 both
51   single-family residential zones with large lots over 20,000-sf.
