WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 19-12-01 DATE: December 5, 2019
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ADOPTING THE 2020 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP TENTATIVE
BUDGET
RECITALS

A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council” is the municipal legislative body
for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

C. The Council approved the 2020 White City Metro Township Tentative Budget on November
17, 2019 and set the public hearing for the 2020 Tentative Budget for December 5, 2020
7:00PM at the White City Water Improvement District Boardroom located at 999 East
Galena Drive White City, UT 84094.

D. Pursuant to fiscal policy for funding the 2020 White City Budget, the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) adopted the 2020 Budget for the MSD on October
23, 2019, releasing the funding for the 2020 White City Budget.

E. The noticing for the public hearing to consider and copies of the 2020 White City Tentative
Budget, (Attachment “A” to this resolution) were made available for public consideration at
least ten (10) days prior to the date of the public hearing for the Budget.

F. Adoption of the 2020 Budget will allow White City to comply with State Law and the
Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act for Cities.

RESOLUTION

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby adopts the 2020 White City Tentative
Budget, Attachment “A.”

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this - day of December, 2019.

Paulina F. Flint, Mayor
