Minutes of the Regular Session of the Lehi Local Building Authority held Tuesday, October
11, 2022, at 9:00 p.m. at the Lehi City Administration Building, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah.

Members Present:        Mark Johnson, Chairman
                        Paige Albrecht, Member
                        Mike Southwick, Member
                        Chris Condie, Member
                        Paul Hancock, Member
                        Katie Koivisto, Member


Others Present: Jason Walker, City Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney, Marlin Eldred,
Economic Development Director; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Teisha Wilson, City Recorder.

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Chairman Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all members were present. Councilor
   Condie joined virtually.

2. Regular Agenda
   2.a) Consideration of Resolution #L2022-01, adopting Bylaws for the Local Building
   Authority of Lehi City; confirming the election of Officers for such Authority; and authorizing
   other action on behalf of the Authority.
   Dean Lundell stated that the bonds will be issued in the name of the Local Building Authority
   and it will lease the new city center/library building to the city.

   Motion:     Mr. Hancock moved to approve Resolution #L2022-01, adopting Bylaws for the
               Local Building Authority of Lehi City; confirming the election of Officers for such
               Authority; and authorizing other action on behalf of the Authority. Mr. Condie
               seconded the motion.

   Roll Call Vote: Mr. Hancock, Yes; Mr. Southwick, Yes; Mr. Condie, Yes; Ms. Koivisto, Yes;
   Ms. Albrecht, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

   2.b) Consideration of Resolution #L2022-02, authorizing the issuance and sale of not more
   than $29,000,000 aggregate principal amount of lease revenue bonds, series 2022; and related
   matters.

   Motion:     Mr. Southwick moved to approve to approve #L2022-02, authorizing the issuance
               and sale of not more than $29,000,000 aggregate principal amount of lease revenue
               bonds, series 2022; and related matters.. Mr. Condie seconded the motion.
