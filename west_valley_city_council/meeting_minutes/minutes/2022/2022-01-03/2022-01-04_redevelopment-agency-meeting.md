     APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING HELD DECEMBER 14, 2021
     The Board considered Minutes of the Special Meeting held December 14, 2021. There
     were no changes, corrections or deletions.

     Mr. Christensen moved to approve the Minutes of the Special Meeting held December 14,
     2021. Mr. Fitisemanu seconded the motion.

     A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

     ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2022
     MINUTES OF RDA ANNUAL MEETING – JANUARY 4, 2022
                           -2-

Temporary Chairman Lars Nordfelt stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson
for 2022 had been scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation
schedule had been used in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an
option for the Board and not mandatory.

Temporary Chairman Nordfelt called for a motion.

Ms. Lang moved to elect Scott Harmon as Chair of the Redevelopment Agency for 2022
and Tom Huynh as Vice Chair of the Redevelopment Agency for 2022. Mr. Christensen
seconded the motion.

A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.


MOTION TO ADJOURN
Upon motion by Ms. Lang all voted in favor to adjourn.


THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE WEST VALLEY
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY THE ANNUAL MEETING OF TUESDAY, JANUARY
4, 2022, WAS ADJOURNED AT 7:00 P.M. BY TEMPORARY CHAIRMAN
NORDFELT.
