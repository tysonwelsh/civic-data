City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 3, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1589, amending Title
3 of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding a liaison to the Draper Historic
Preservation Commission. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 6th day of
October, 2023. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
