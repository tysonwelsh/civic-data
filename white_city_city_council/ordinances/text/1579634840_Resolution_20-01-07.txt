A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ACCEPTING AND APPROVING THE BID FOR INSURANCE FROM THE UTAH
LOCAL GOVERNMENTS TRUST AND AUTHORIZING PAYMENT OF THE
PREMIUM UPON RECEIPT OF THE BILLING INVOICE
RECITALS
A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.
B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council’) is the municipal legislative
body for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.
C. The Utah Local Governments Trust (“ULGT”) is a Nonprofit Interlocal Corporation
that provides insurance coverage to Local Governments pursuant to UCA 11
Chapter 13a — Governmental Nonprofit Corporations Act.
D. ULGT provides General Liability and Workers Compensation Insurance to
White City.
E. ULGT provided White City with the 2019 Bid for Insurance in December 2019 for
the year 2020.

F. White City is required to accept the bid prior to payment of the insurance premium.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves this resolution
accepts the Bid for Insurance from the Utah Local Governments Trust, hereinafter known
as Attachment “A,” and approves the payment of the insurance premium for White City
upon receipt of the invoice.
