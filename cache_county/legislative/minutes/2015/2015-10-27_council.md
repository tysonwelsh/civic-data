* Clerk/Auditor’s Office - 2015 County Fair Report —‘Cache County Fair and Rodeo-

The Greatest Show Around” — Clerk/Auditor Zollinger reported there was great

balloon that landed north of Evanston, Wyoming.

horse show at the Fair and presented her with a plaque. Zollinger thanked Page for her
many years of service.

Individuals in other states have contacted him wondering how Cache County can show
such good attendance and raise the prize money amounts. The rodeo came in just
under budget. Poulsen announced the committee has decided to go with all reserved

Janeen Allen - Logan City RAPZ 2014 Project - Bridger Park Phase III

From: Russ Akina <russ.akina@loganutah.org>

To: Cameron Jensen <Cameron.Jensen@cachecounty.org>
Date: 10/21/2015 4:21 PM

Subject: Logan City RAPZ 2014 Project - Bridger Park Phase III

Cameron,

Logan City would like to re-direct a portion of its $160,000 of the 2014 RAPZ project awarded by the
County to construct pickle ball courts instead of a basketball court, tennis court, and splash pad. The
remaining amount will still be used for landscaping and walkways at Bridger Park.

Our justification is that we have experienced a dramatic increase in requests for pickle ball courts. And,
while we have 2015 RAPZ funding for two pickle ball courts planned as part of the re-construction of
the tennis court/basketball court area at Merlin Olsen Park as a multi-purpose court/winter ice sheet,
pickle ball enthusiasts tell us that more courts are desired. I also believe that we could accommodate
more people with pickle ball than we would for tennis. Pickle ball dimensions are smaller than tennis
allowing for two pickle ball courts (8 players) for every tennis court (four players). As for basketball,
while it would be good to have a court, there are nearby courts on the Bridger Elementary School
playground accessible to the public.

So, I wanted to check with you to know if we can move in that direction. Mayor Petersen has also been
approached by pickle ball enthusiasts eager to raise money for the courts and possibly lighting, if we can
re-direct the 2015 funds to help with that effort, Based on cost estimates provided to us, the City could
build up to 8 courts with the remaining balance. 8 courts cost approximately $150,000 to build. One
tennis court is $40,000 to build with in-house labor, $75,000 contracted out.

Currently, there is $145,000 remaining. We plan to use $20,000 for landscaping and walkways, leaving
$125,000 to match private donations for possibly eight courts at Bridger Park. Please let me know if
there are any questions regarding this request.

Russ Akina
Director of Parks and Recreation
City of Logan

MEMORANDUM

Date: 26 October 2015
From: Cache County Development Services
Subject: Providence City Request for Access to County Road 800 South

Providence City has submitted a request (Exhibit A), dated October 26, 2015, to allow municipal
development access to County Road 800 South. The Development Services Department has reviewed
this request and recommends that the County Council approve said request contingent upon the intent to
annex as outlined by Providence City.

Furthermore, prior to annexation, the Development Services Department will handle. encroachment
permit requests for any development within the existing county right-of-way, or at their request,
Providence City may do the same in place of the county for the portions of the roadway that are
proposed for annexation.

Cache County Council,

Providence City requests that the Cache County Council consider the approval of the use of this
right-of-way while Providence City proceeds to annex a portion of the 500 North (800 South
County). In order to provide city street access to the municipal subdivision (Providence Hollow).
Providence City will begin the annexation process, incorporating the northern portion of 500
North to the existing property lines on the north.

Item 5.6 [B] of the Cache County Manual of Roadway Design and Construction Standards
specifies that the Cache County Council must give written permission for a municipal
development to access a county roadway. Providence City thus petitions the Cache County
Council for this approval.

If approved, the developer would be permitted to place infrastructure prior to the winter months,
thus not delaying the construction process and allowing time for the annexation to be completed.
Please consider this request.
