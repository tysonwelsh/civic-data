2.4 Council Discussion on American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) 
Priorities
21-1477
Attachments: Staff Report
Sponsors: Council Member Aimee Winder Newton and Council Member Steve 
DeBry (Approx. 30 mins).
Informational
Council Member Newton  stated she sent each of the Council Members a 
list of suggested priorities for using American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) 
funds.  She wanted the Council to have a process in place as it makes 
decisions regarding these one -time funds.  She asked that Council Members 
notify her of any verbiage it might want to add.    
Council Member DeBry  reviewed the list of criteria that is in no particular 
order: quantifiable returns on investment (ROI) that would derive from 
ARPA expenditures; improvement of quality of life outcomes; joint initiatives 
that result in additional funding and buy -in from state and local governments; 
investments in Salt Lake County’s future; and public health.

6. CITIZEN PUBLIC INPUT
The following citizens spoke in opposition to the mask mandate order, which was issued 
by Angela Dunn, Director, Salt Lake County Health Department, on Friday, January 7, 
2022.  The reasons given were because the mental health of children is being affected; 
masks cause anxiety and depression; children are afraid to go to school; children are 
being isolated, bullied, and humiliated by teachers and other children for not wearing

masks, even those with mask exemptions; relationships between children and teachers are 
strained; some medical associations oppose the KN 95 masks, while others oppose 
masking children entirely; masks are contaminated with pathogens and dangerous levels of 
carbon dioxide, which can cause headaches, sleepiness, poor concentration, loss of 
attention, increased heart rate, and nausea; masks prevent communication and social 
interaction, and impair learning and language skills; wearing masks while exercising 
vigorously has caused deaths or loss of consciousness; mandates are unconstitutional and 
government overreach; and masks have not been shown to reduce transmission of Covid .

including lies about the numbers of people hospitalized and actual reasons they were

the flu; vaccine mandates are affecting the economy because people are not willing to
