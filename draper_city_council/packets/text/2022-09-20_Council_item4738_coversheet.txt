Re: Providing Local Consent for an Off-Premise Beer alcohol license for Kum & Go LC Comments: Kum & Go LC dba Kum & Go 2500 located at 13639 S 200 W is a commercial business seeking Local Consent for an Off-Premise Beer License.
They have provided the following:
1. Background check
2. Floor plan showing where alcohol is stored and sold.
The applicant is currently in the process of obtaining a Draper City commercial business license for their 13639 S 200 W location.
The findings are as follows: Draper City Code 6-4-060 states the following:
c.  No  off-premises beer retail license , manufacturer license, or special use permit for a special use permittee operating a for-profit basis shall be issued wherein the premises would be located within three hundred feet (300') of a community location as measured from the nearest patron entrance of the outlet by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the property boundary of the community location.
There are currently no viable pedestrian routes to the nearest community locations. However, measuring in a straight line to the boundary of each nearby community location, none are closer than 3,190 ft away from the approximate front door of Kum & Go LC.
C. Restriction On Licenses: The total number of off-premises licenses in the City shall not exceed one per each one thousand (1,000) population of the City based on the latest population estimate provided by the State of Utah Department of Workforce Services.
As of 2020, the Department of Workforce services puts Draper City's population at 51,017, giving the city the ability to grant a maximum 51 off-premise licenses. There are currently twenty (20) active off-premise licenses within the city.
