                  February 08, 2022 at 5:00 p.m. - Cache County Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.
 In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinions or purported facts.
             The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

 b.       Discussion– Cache Resource Center 31:46
          Councilmembers Borup and Worthen expressed their concerns regarding the state appropriations process regarding a
          potential Cache Resource Center and a gap in communication between the executive and legislative branches. Specifically,
          at issue was the amount of the required grant match and lack of council approval. Councilmember Worthen explained her
          side of the situation while Executive Zook with Economic Development Director Shawn Milne explained theirs.

     c.   Discussion – Designation of what constitutes a “significant parcel of real property” and “reasonable notice”
          for purposes of the surplus and disposition of real property 1:03:12
          County Attorney John Luthy discussed with Council on how they would like him to move forward with what is
          a “significant parcel of real property” and what measures should be taken to give “reasonable notice” to the public. Matt
          Phillips public services director also spoke to the issue.

d-j.      Ordinance(s) 2022-05 through 2022-11 1:24:10
          Discussion: County Attorney John Luthy presented on all seven ordinances up for approval to council. All ordnances are to
          update county code to match with the state code.
          Action: Council, with recommendation of Luthy did not perform action on any ordinances and will be moved to the next
          meeting on Feb 22.
