                               RESOLUTION NO. 2025-47



  A RESOLUTION OF THE LEHI CITY COUNCIL APPROVING THE CERTIFIED TAX
 RATE FOR FISCAL YEAR 2025-2026, AS REQUIRED BY UTAH STATE CODE §59-2-
                                   924

        WHEREAS, the Lehi City Council is authorized and required under the
provisions of Utah Code Annotated §59-2-924 and related sections to establish a
certified property tax rate annually; and

        WHEREAS, the Utah State Tax Commission has provided Lehi City with its
certified tax rate calculation for the 2025-2026 fiscal year; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed certified tax rate and
finds it to be in compliance with applicable statutory requirements and aligned with the
financial needs and obligations of the City for the upcoming fiscal year; and

       WHEREAS, approval of the certified tax rate is necessary to fund municipal
operations, services, and capital needs in accordance with the adopted budget;

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the city council of Lehi city, utah, as
follows:

       1.      The certified tax rate for Lehi City for fiscal year 2025-2026 (FY2026), as
calculated and submitted by the Utah State Tax Commission, is hereby approved and
adopted as follows:

Certified Tax Rate: 0.001163

          2.   This resolution shall take effect immediately.

Approved and Adopted by the City Council of Lehi City, Utah this 10th day of June,
2025.
