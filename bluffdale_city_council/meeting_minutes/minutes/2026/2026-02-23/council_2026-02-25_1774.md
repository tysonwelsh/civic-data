8.1 Ordinance 2026-01 - An Ordinance Amending Chapter 9.10.020 Related to the

Adoption of the Wildland Urban Interface Building Standards. (Presenter,
Fred Donaldson).
Mr. Donaldson reported that as part of the Utah Cooperative Wildfire System, the City is required
to adopt building standards “consistent with the 2006 edition of the Wildland Urban Interface
(“WUI”) Code issued by the International Code Council and with the alternatives or amendments
approved by the Utah Division of Forestry, Fire, and State Lands and subsequent amendments
thereof.” This code will be administered by the Building Official and apply to construction in high
hazard WUI areas.
In response to a question raised by Mayor Hall, Mr. Donaldson reported that a public hearing is
required because the item is a land use ordinance change.
Mayor Hall opened the public hearing period. There were no comments. The public hearing was
closed.
In response to a question raised by Council Member Austin, Mr. Donaldson stated that the only
WUl area in the City is a small area near Bringhurst Station, and it is not a high hazard zone. There
are currently no areas in Bluffdale that will fall under the ordinance, but it must be adopted in order
to participate in the program. The State is considering adopting an updated version of WUI Code,
which will be automatically updated in City Code and not require an amendment.
Council Member Wilding moved to APPROVE Ordinance 2026-01 - An Ordinance
Amending Chapter 9.10.020 Related to the Adoption of the Wildland Urban Interface
Building Standards. Council Member Austin seconded the motion. Vote on Motion:
Council Member Wilding-Yes, Council Member Smith-Yes, Council Member Lord-Yes,
