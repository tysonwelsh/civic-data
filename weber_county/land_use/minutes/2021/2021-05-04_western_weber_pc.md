2. ZMA 2021-02 A public hearing for consideration of a requested rezone located at approximately
  3718 N Wolf Creek Resort in Eden from the O-1, FR-3, and RE-15 zone to the FR-3,
  O-1, and CVR-1 zone
Staff presenter: Steve Burton

Motion: Commissioner Howell made a motion to recommend approval to the County Commission for ZMA 2021-02 A public hearing for consideration of a requested rezone located at approximately 3718 N Wolf Creek Resort in Eden from the O-1, FR-3, and RE-15 zone to the FR-3,O-1, and CVR-1 zone subject to staff recommendations and findings in the staff report.
