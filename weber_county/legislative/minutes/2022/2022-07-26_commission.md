D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Jenkins: We just finished a celebration on the 24th (Pioneer Day) and
what a celebration it was! The top of the celebration for me was riding the scooters Commissioner Harvey got for us
to ride on the parade route. A lot of pain associated with the trek west; they lost a lot of people along the way. We got
a little pain associated with our Commission this morning, one of our associates lost her husband over the weekend.
Anytime anything like that happens, it’s hard. We want her to know our compassion goes out to her this morning.
Things like this are tough pills to swallow in your life. We wish her the best.

G. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 36-2022 APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE OGDEN VALLEY
       PLANNING COMMISSION.
         Rick Grover (Director, Planning): Three vacancies on the Ogden Valley Planning Commission, including one
         for Jack Howell who passed away, and Jeff Burton and Shanna Francis whose terms expired. Fourteen
         individuals applied. A month was taken to vet all applicants fairly.

         Commissioner Froerer: The process was long and intensive. Fourteen applied, one was ineligible due to
         location. I asked Planning to place current and prospective members’ locations on a map to ensure fair
         representation across the valley. Applicants were asked to respond to a series of questions. Some were asked
         to participate in a phone interview. All were very qualified people and this was a tough decision.
         Commissioner Harvey: I really appreciate Jack’s service to the very end. The map showing where
         current and potential members reside was very helpful to me.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 36-2022 appointing Don Stefanik, Janet Wampler, and
         Jeff Burton as members to the Ogden Valley Planning Commission; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
