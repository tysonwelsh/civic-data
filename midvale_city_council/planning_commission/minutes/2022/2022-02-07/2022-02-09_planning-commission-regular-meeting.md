GENERAL SESSION

Chair Snow called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS


1.      Boyd Brown requests Minor Subdivision/Conditional Use approval to divide
        property into two lots located at 6871 S 185 E in the Single Family
        Residential/Duplex Overlay (SF- 1/DO) zone. (21-010; Wendelin Knobloch)

Mr. Knobloch stated that the applicant requests approval of the Carlsen Subdivision with the purpose
of establishing a plat consisting of Lot 1, which contains the existing single-family home and fronts onto
the publicly owned 190 East Street, and Lot 2, which is a Flag Lot situated behind Lot 1 and served by a
twenty-foot-wide lot elongation (the “flag”) that connects the main portion of Lot 2 (the “banner”) to 190
East Street.

Chair Snow asked if the existing road is expected to continue east?

Mr. Knobloch stated that he has not heard anything regarding the street as it does not pertain to
this request.

Commissioner Liedtke asked if the existing home would remain?

Mr. Knobloch stated that he is unsure and added that, that level of detail is not involved in this
type of request.

APPLICANT:

Cameron Burnside, representing the applicant, stated that he is happy to answer any questions
the Commissioners may have regarding this project.

2.     Jake Tate, representing Anderson Wahlen & Associates, requests Rezone,
       Preliminary Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit approval for a project located at
       1122 E Fort Union Blvd to allow for a 5-story apartment building with structured
       parking and street level retail. (22-003; Elizabeth Arnold)

Ms. Arnold stated that this staff report will consider three applications: a rezone, preliminary site
plan, and conditional use permit. The preliminary site plan and conditional use portions below
will review the project as if the rezone request was granted. The concept plan for the project
includes a 5-story apartment building with 5,000 square feet of retail space to be built where the
former Babies R’ Us used to exist.

APPLICANT:

Mr. Stephen Usdan stated that his company acquired the Fort Union center in 2018. Since then,
he has recapitalized a portion of the area for redevelopment, this includes the vacant Babies R Us
location. He added that none of the existing businesses are planning on relocating with this
proposed project except for Desert Book. The applicant then provided the Commission with the
history of his investment into the area and his desire to redevelop it. He added that to address the
missing parking required for this project, he plans on utilizing the space between Bed Bath and
Beyond and the Images Studio building to meet the set requirements.

Commissioner Anderson stated that the big concern she has is the flow of traffic in and out of the
center which as it stands is an issue.

Mr. Usdan stated that his team is working on adding a connection in and out of the center. This
concern is something he is aware of, and he is trying to address it in the ways he can.

Mr. Ryan Simmons, representing the Boyer Company stated that their interest in is infill
locations that are owned and managed for the next 50 years. He added that his team has
identified areas within the center that would provide ample parking options for the tenants and
guests of the proposed project. He stated that in their final proposal they will show how they are
acquiring the needed 57 stalls along with any necessary agreements.

Commissioner Liedtke asked if there would be designated parking spots?

Mr. Simmons stated that it will be signed but it will allow for cross parking needs.

Commissioner Anderson asked how much retail space would be available?

Mr. Simmons stated that it would be about 5000 sq ft. He added that this would be a live, eat,
work and play location.
