WORKSHOP at 6:00 PM

UPD LABOR COMPENSATION DISCUSSION
Chief Randy Thomas introduced Chief Jason Mazuran, Chief Jason Ackerman, and
Richard Moon to discuss the dilemma regarding the law enforcement labor market. Chief
Ackerman said the UPD's recruitment pool is very small. UPD has a great reputation;
however, the new recruits do not know this. He said their competitors are recruiting
individual officers, which has never happened before. He said UPD is flush right now but
they need to stay competitive with wages so that doesn't change. He said UPD has a 12
year pay scale until the officers are topped out. Their competitors have an 8 year pay
scale which is enticing to officers who are looking to retire soon so they can get their pay
increased for retirement.

Council Member Paul Glover said he would like to see UPD move up to the fourth spot to
stay competitive. He said the pay needs to be raised somewhat so the officers know the
city cares about them.
