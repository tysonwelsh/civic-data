     SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL MEETING
     STAFF APPOINTMENT – Fire Department Director – Chief Marc Sanderson

Mayor Brunst welcomed all that were in attendance both at the City Center and those participating
via Zoom meeting.

Mr. Davidson stated it was a pleasure to be in attendance in the City Council Chambers as they
recognize and extend support to the new Fire Chief as he takes the Oath of Office. He indicated
that behind the camera where the public and Council could not see, were fire personnel having a

representative from each fire station of Orem along with representation from the Police
Department. It is his pleasure to show his support for Fire Chief Mark Sanderson and recommend
his appointment as Orems next Fire Chief. He turned the time over to the Mayor and Council for
their consent and recommendation.

Mayor Brunst stated they went through a lengthy process in interviewing several candidates and
feel they have made an excellent choice in Mr. Sanderson. He has a great background in fire,
police, nursing and flight services in training at Utah Valley University. He had the opportunity
in talking with Mr. Sanderson and learned about his vision for the City of Orem in making the
department one of the best in the state by increasing the retention and recruitment process while
increasing moral.

All City Council Members echoed the Mayors comments and welcomed Mr. Sanderson to the City
of Orem team. They voiced their excitement in getting to know him and look forward to see what
he will bring to the City of Orem Fire Department.

Mayor Brunst moved to approve the consent and recommendation of Marc Sanderson as the
Director of Orem City’s Fire Department. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting aye:
Richard F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Debby Lauret, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson, David Spencer
and Brent Sumner. The motion passed.

Mayor Brunst turned the time over to the City Recorder to administer the Oath of Office.

Newly appointed Director of the City of Orem Fire Department was called forward and Mrs. Bates
completed the Oath of Office. Mr. Sanderson was then invited to make a few comments.

Mr. Sanderson stated he appreciated the opportunity he has to return to the City of Orem as the
Fire Chief. He looks forward to meeting and getting to know everyone and taking the fire
department in a new direction. He thanked the former Fire Chief Scott Gurney for the years of
experience he gave to the program and hopes that he can bring his own direction to the department
and keep them moving forward.


ADJOURNMENT

Mayor Brunst moved to adjourn the meeting. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting
aye: Richard F. Brunst, Jeff Lambson, Debby Lauret, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson, David
Spencer and Brent Sumner. The motion passed.


The meeting adjourned at 7:20 p.m.
