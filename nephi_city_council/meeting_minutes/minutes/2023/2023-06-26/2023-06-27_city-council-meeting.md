                         NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                Tuesday, June 27, 2023
                                      7:00 p.m.
                                     21 E. 100 N.
                                   Nephi, UT 84648

1. The council reviewed proposed changes to Title 10 Land Use Code.

2. The council reviewed proposed changes in uses in the HC-1 zone. The Planning
   Commission is recommending some additional uses in the HC-1 zone. The uses include
   banking, financial institutions, higher learning institutions and facilities and light industry.

3. The council directed staff to schedule a regular council meeting on July 11, 2023, and a
   work session for July 18, 2023. There will be no meeting held on July 4, 2023.

4.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson and City Attorney Kasey Wright reported on a meeting
     they had with an individual that has experience running tournaments and who gave
     some ideas about bringing events to the Hive. Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they
     would like the City to send out a request for proposals so that the Hive could be better
     utilized during the summer months. The council directed Mr. Atkinson to move forward
     with the idea.

5. Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that Northridge Plat D is coming upon its one-
   year mark to receive final approval and the developers are asking to extend the final
   approval date of Plat D.

6. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the final sales tax and PAR tax numbers. He said that the
   numbers exceeded the budget predictions.

7. City Council Updates:

           Mayor Seely reported on the APPA conference he had attended in Seattle.
           Councilor Memmott reported that there are several roads and curb and gutters
            that are sinking in the Chapel View Subdivision. Mr. Atkinson said they are
            aware of the problem and the street crew is planning to fix the problem areas.
           Councilor Ostler reported on a property on 200 West and 200 South that is a
            nuisance.
           Mayor Seely reported on plans for the 911 Day of Giving.

8.   Mayor Seely opened the regular council meeting at 8:00 p.m.

9.   Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Resolution 06-2-2023- Fireworks Restrictions.
       Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.


   10. Councilor Parady moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
       competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
       The motion passed unanimously. 8:02 p.m.

   11. The Council convened in regular meeting and Councilor Ostler moved to adjourn the
      meeting. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

Meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
