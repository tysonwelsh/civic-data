4.1 Proclamation in Support of a Future Olympic Games 22-0741
Page 1 of 8
County Council Meeting Minutes August 9, 2022
Attachments: Staff Report
Olympic Resolution
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  introduced the proclamation, as Utah hopes to 
host the winter Olympic games in 2030 or 2034. The International Olympic 
Committee has asked for a proclamation specific to ideals, such as 
intentional inclusivity and wastelessness.  The County wants to put its best 
foot forward.  She read the following proclamation: 
P R O C L A M A T I O N
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County successfully hosted the 2002 Olympic 
Winter Games and Paralympic Winter Games and the communities of Salt 
Lake City, Kearns Metro Township and West Valley City were critical to this 
success; and 
WHEREAS, facilities put in service for the Games continue to be utilized to 
this day, including the Salt Lake Sports Complex in Salt Lake City, Utah 
Olympic Oval in Kearns, Maverik Center in West Valley City, and many 
more; and 
WHEREAS, we are working in partnership with the Salt Lake City -Utah 
Committee for the Games “SLC-UTCG” in developing beneficial operating 
principles for Games in Utah’s communities; and 
WHEREAS, we desire to ensure that the next Games hosted in our 
communities prioritizes diversity, equity, inclusion, and access, is 
climate-positive, and net -zero waste, and acts as a catalyst for achieving 
major community initiatives; and 
WHEREAS, we believe such a vision will be accomplished in a multitude of 
ways including ensuring that:
• everyone employed to help prepare for the Games or employed during the 
Games will be paid a living wage;
• the Games are welcoming, inclusive, and accessible for all, with universally 
accessible venues and events, and disability awareness training for 
public-facing staff and volunteers, and that opportunities should be created 
for residents of all income levels to participate in the Games and 
Page 2 of 8
County Council Meeting Minutes August 9, 2022
accompanying events;
• prioritizing use of existing infrastructure and venues, developing a 
carbonmanagement plan for Games -related activities, striving for a Games 
that is net -zero waste, and forming a sustainability committee to ensure that 
sustainability goals are achieved for the Games;
• maximizing use of public transportation, including clean and active 
transportation modes, for all Games stakeholders;
• the SLC -UTCG will be a model of transparency, robustly engaging the 
public in the planning process, making regular meetings open to the public 
and sharing information.
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED,  that officials in Salt Lake 
County, look forward with great anticipation to being selected as the host 
city for a future Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that officials in Salt Lake County, are 
committed to working with our community partners and the SLC -UTCG to 
create a Games that strives for diversity, equity and inclusion in all Games 
activities, delivers a net -zero waste and climate -positive Games, and acts as a 
catalyst for achieving major community initiatives.
Ms. Catherine Raney -Norman, Chair, Salt Lake City Olympic Bid Efforts, 
stated Utah is very well positioned to host the winter Olympic games a 
second time.  Over the last few months, the committee has been working 
tirelessly with our communities, venues, the US Olympic and Paralympic 
committees, and the International Olympic Committee (IOC).  The IOC is 
very impressed with the bipartisan support Utah has and the enthusiasm of 
the community.  The County needs to be forward thinking and inclusive in its 
planning and bid.    
Mr. Brennan Smith , Utah Olympic Legacy Foundation, stated there are 
many opportunities at the Olympic Oval for kids to learn to skate and 
potentially become athletes.  The Healthy Kearns initiative is helping kids, 
especially on the West side of the valley, to be healthy and active.  The IOC 
recently performed a study and every Olympic venue that was built for the 
2002 games is still in use.

6.6 A Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council Supporting 
Efforts to Preserve the Great Salt Lake

Great Salt Lake Resolution -- Updated (RAFL)
RESOLUTION NO. 5996 
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
SUPPORTING EFFORTS TO PRESERVE THE GREAT SALT LAKE
WHEREAS, the Great Salt Lake is a treasured natural resource; and
WHEREAS, the Great Salt Lake is a world class refuge for shore and 
migratory birds; and
Page 5 of 8
County Council Meeting Minutes August 9, 2022
WHEREAS, the Great Salt Lake is an important source of minerals and 
fertilizer; and 
WHEREAS, the Great Salt Lake is one of the few places on earth that is 
available for the harvesting of brine shrimp; and
WHEREAS, in recent years the Great Salt Lake has lost significant volume 
and is currently at a historically low level; and
WHEREAS, the Great Salt Lake is noted for its impact on “lake effect ” 
snows which are a vital part of area weather patterns that include increased 
snow yields and helps to produce “The Greatest Snow on Earth” for Utah ’s 
ski industry, and which also contribute to the county’s watershed; and
WHEREAS, the receding waters of the Great Salt Lake endanger migratory 
bird habitat, mineral extraction and brine shrimp industries, and the quality of 
life of County residents; and 
WHEREAS, the lakebed exposed by the receding waters creates the 
potential for dust storms to carry mercury, cadmium, and other toxic heavy 
metals into the airshed of the Wasatch Front, further degrading the 
environment and posing a public health hazard; and
WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature and Governor Spencer Cox have 
recognized these concerns by passing and signing House Bill 410, which law 
creates a water trust for the Great Salt Lake and appropriates $ 40 million for 
its preservation; and
WHEREAS, Utah ’s congressional delegation has sponsored legislation and 
appropriated $ 10 million to study the problems associated with the Great Salt 
Lake and propose solutions; and
WHEREAS, state leaders have held and will continue to hold summits on the 
Great Salt Lake in order to find solutions, and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council finds that there are serious 
concerns with the depletion of the Great Salt Lake, and
WHEREAS, the County Council believes that Salt Lake County should act in 
Page 6 of 8
County Council Meeting Minutes August 9, 2022
concert with the state and federal governments in this matter;
NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved that the Salt Lake County Council 
pledges to work with State of Utah and the United States federal government 
to find policy solutions that will preserve the Great Salt Lake as the precious 
natural resource that it is; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Salt Lake County Council, acting as 
legislative body for Salt Lake County, intends to use its budgetary and 
policymaking authority in full support of this effort.
