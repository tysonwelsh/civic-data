MINUTES OF THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL WORK SESSION
HELD TUESDAY, JANUARY 26, 2023, AT 9:02 A.M. IN THE COTTONWOOD
HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL WORK ROOM LOCATED AT 2277 EAST BENGAL
BOULEVARD, COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS, UTAH

Members Present:     Board Chair Mike Weichers, Board Member Scott Bracken, Board Member
                     Douglas Petersen, Board Member Shawn E. Newell (via zoom), Board
                     Member Ellen Birrell

Staff Present:       CDRA CEO Tim Tingey, City Attorney Shane Topham, Secretary Paula
                     Melgar, Community and Economic Development Director Michael
                     Johnson, Administrative and Financial Services Director Scott Jurges,
                     Systems Administrator Alex Earl.


1.0    WELCOME – Board Chair Mike Weichers

Board Chair Mike Weichers welcomed those present and called the meeting to order.

2.0  CLOSED MEETING TO DISCUSS LITIGATION, PROPERTY ACQUISITION
AND/OR THE CHARACTER AND PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCE OR PHYSICAL
OR MENTAL HEALTH OF AN INDIVIDUAL

Motion to discuss Property Acquisition by Council Member Birrell. Second by Council Member
Petersen. All “Yes”. Motion passed unanimously. CDRA Meeting Closed at 9:03 a.m.

Motion to close the close meeting open the CDRA Meetings and adjourn by Board Member
Bracken. Second by Board Member Birrell. All “Yes”. Motion passed unanimously.

3.0    ADJOURN

Motion to close the close meeting open the CDRA Meetings and adjourn by Board Member
Bracken. Second by Board Member Birrell. All “Yes”. Motion passed unanimously.
The CDRA Meeting adjourned at 10:16 a.m.
