Order of Business

Ordinance No. 2021-08-03 – Adjusting Compensation for the Mayor, Vice-Chair, & Council
Members

             THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following Ordinance adjusting the compensation of the Mayor, Vice-Chair,
and Council Members:

               Council Member Snow stated Kearns Metro Township Council does not have
administrator to handle the day-to-day operations. Mayor Bush handles all those duties as well
as her own. This ordinance is to increase the stipend for the Mayor, Vice-Chair, and Council
Members.

              Council Member Schaeffer agreed that the stipend should be increased.

              Council Member Butterfield agreed that the stipend should be increased.

               Council Member Schaeffer, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
open the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                 Council Member Schaeffer suggested waiting five minutes to provide enough
time for citizens to join the meeting and make comments.

ORDINANCE NO. 2021-08-03                                                     Date: August 16, 2021

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADJUSTING
       THE COMPENSATION OF THE MAYOR, VICE-CHAIR, AND COUNCIL
       MEMBERS

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-818(1), the elective and statutory officers
of Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) receive such compensation for their services as the Kearns
Council (“Council”) may fix by ordinance adopting compensation or compensation schedules
enacted after public hearing; and

         WHEREAS, the Council desires to adjust its compensation levels to account for inflation
and recent increases in workload and responsibilities, particularly for the Mayor, who is performing
all of the administrative duties for Kearns in the absence of a city manager or administrative staff;
and

        WHEREAS, the Council provided notice of its intent to adjust its compensation and held
a public hearing on August 16, 2021, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-818.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL:

        1.     Mayor Compensation. The Mayor’s compensation will be $3,000.00 per month.
The Mayor’s compensation will be reduced to $1,700 per month effective on the first of the month
following the date on which: (1) the Council hires a city manager after creating such a position via
ordinance; or (2) the Council budgets money for an administrative assistant to the Mayor and the
Mayor appoints the administrative assistant after consulting with the Council.

       2.     Deputy Mayor Compensation. The compensation for the Deputy Mayor will be
$1,500.00 per month.

        3.     Council Member Compensation. The compensation for the other Council Members
will be $1,200.00 per month.

        4.     The foregoing compensation of the Mayor, Deputy Mayor, and Council Members
of the Kearns Metro Township Council (“Council”) will remain in effect until otherwise amended
by the Council or by statute, provided that the Council will review its compensation each year.

       5.      This Ordinance will become effect immediately upon its passage and its publication
on the Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §10-3-711 (1), and the Mayor,
Vice Mayor, and Council members will receive the full amount of their compensation for the month
in which this Ordinance goes into effect.
