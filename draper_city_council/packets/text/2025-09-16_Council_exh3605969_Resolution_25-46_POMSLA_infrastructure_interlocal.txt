                               RESOLUTION NO. 25-46

    A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING AN
  INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH POINT OF THE MOUNTAIN STATE
  LAND USE AUTHORITY REGARDING PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE AND
                          SERVICES

        WHEREAS, consistent with Utah Code Title 11, Chapter 13, Interlocal
Cooperation Act, Draper City is authorized to enter into a enter into one or more
interlocal agreements with one or more local government entities for the delivery of
services to the point of the mountain state land (“The Point”); and

        WHEREAS, Draper City has negotiated an interlocal agreement (“the
Interlocal”) with Point of the Mountain State Land Use Authority (“POMSLA”) that
addresses the design, construction, ownership and maintenance of certain public
infrastructure, as well as the delivery of certain public services to The Point; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council finds entering into the Interlocal with POMSLA
promotes the public health, welfare and safety of Draper residents and businesses.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Approval of Interlocal Cooperation Agreement. The Interlocal
Cooperation Agreement between POMSLA and Draper City attached hereto as Exhibit A
is hereby approved by the Council and the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute the
same on behalf of Draper City.

        Section 2. De Minimus Changes. The City Manager is authorized to attach the
final exhibits to the Agreement and correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical,
or de minimis errors or omissions consistent with the City Council’s approval of this
Resolution prior to submitting the Interlocal for the Mayor’s signature

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 16th DAY OF SEPTEMBER, 2025.
