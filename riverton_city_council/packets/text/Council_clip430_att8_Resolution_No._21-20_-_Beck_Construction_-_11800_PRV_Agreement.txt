                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 21-20

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
    WITH BECK CONSTRUCTION TO COMPLETE THE 11800 SOUTH WATER
                    METER/PRV VAULT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the City Water Director has determined that this project is needed.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Beck
         Construction to complete the 11800 South Water Meter / PRV Vault Project.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.


      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
March 2021 by the following vote:
