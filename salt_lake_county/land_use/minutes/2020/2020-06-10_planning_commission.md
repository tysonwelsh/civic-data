                              PUBLIC HEARINGS 
Hearings began at – 8:36 a.m. 
 
The Salt Lake County Planning Commission will hold a public hearing to consider recommending a 
proposal to amend County Ordinance 19.05.010 which affects the Unincorporated Salt Lake County 
Planning Commission. The proposed change increases the number of Planning Commission Members from 
five to seven. The provision of two alternates remains the same. However, the required number of 
Unincorporated Members increases from three to four members. Planner: Jake Young 
 
Salt Lake County District Attorney’s Office, Counsel Zach Shaw introduced the change. Regional 
Development Planner Jake Young provided an analysis of the proposed ordinance amendment  and 
additional training. County Council has given the approval to start this process. Mr. Shaw said reason for 
changes since the ordinance was modified is the Olympia Hills project and the West Bench general plan. 
 
Commissioner Cohen asked who from unincorporated county is currently on the commission; three people 
must be from the unincorporate d area. Mr. Shaw said Commissioner Cohen, Commissioner Vance, and 
Commissioner O’Meara. Commissioner Cohen asked if Commissioner O’Meara is in the Town of Brighton. 
Mr. Shaw  confirmed he is , and County attempting to replace Commissioner Cohen  and Commissioner 
Vance. Commissioner Cohen said always had a problem filling positions. Mr. Young said he has received 
some referrals, but no applicants, but has heard from city planners in the valley that people are interested. 
Mr. Shaw  said staff recognized that the  Olympia Hills project  and West Bench general plan  will spur 
interest to participate. Mr. Young  said he has been speaking with cities with representation from the 
southwest. Commissioner Vance said they will not be members of unincorporated. Mr. Shaw said there can 
be three members from incorporated and two alternates from incorporated. Commissioner Barbushev  
asked about unincorporated areas bordering Herriman. Mr. Shaw  said parts of Hi-Country are 
unincorporated. Mr. Young said additional unincorporated areas are in between West Jordan and west 
valley Oquirrh highlands, but some of that area could potentially be annexed. Commissioner Barbushev 
asked if either have a community council. Mr. Young said he is not sure but Hi-County HOA is highly 
involved. Mr. Hartsell named the unincorporated community councils. Commissioner Cohen said this is the 
planning commission for unincorporated, it’s always been difficult to find people to serve especially for 
unincorporated and as it stands not in compliance with the current ordinance and would still have the same 
challenges in filling. Granite wanted everyone to be from unincorporated. Commissioner Barbushev asked 
what the quorum will change to. Mr. Shaw said from three to four and anticipates will not be difficult with 
big projects coming. Need four unincorporated, the two we have are on expired terms. Commissioner Cohen 
said if the proposed ordinance is enacted, when will it be effective. Mr. Shaw said 15 days after adoption, 
thereafter Commissioner Cohen  and Commissioner Vance  could continue to serve until replaced.  
Commissioner Collard asked any requirements for quorum, regardless of unincorporated or incorporated. 
Mr. Shaw said just total number of people, no requirements  for unincorporated quorum . Commissioner 
Barbushev said technically out of compliance, how does that affect business conducted. Mr. Shaw said with 
the ordinance allowing Commissioner Vance  and Commissioner Cohen  to serve until replaced and 
allowance of incorporated members, fine to continue to do business and make decisions and will not impact 
decisions. Mr. Young will get Commissioner Barbushev an application since she has someone interested. 
Mr. Young said after today will get the word out and advertise. Commissioner Elieson mentioned he is an 
alternate. Mr. Young said that happened when Commissioner Collard became chair, county council made 
that effective just before that planning commission meeting. Commissioner Elieson said he will participate 
in every meeting regardless if he is an alternate or not. Commissioner Collard asked with the requirement 
of 4 unincorporated, would open seats be filled before Commissioner Cohen and Vance’s replacements.
