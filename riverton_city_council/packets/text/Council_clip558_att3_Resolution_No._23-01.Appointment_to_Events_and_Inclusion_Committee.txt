                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-01

          A RESOLUTION APPOINTING VOLUNTEER MEMBERS TO THE
             RIVERTON CITY EVENTS AND INCLUSION COMMITTEE

        WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance, the Mayor shall appoint with
advice and consent of the City Council, residents of Riverton City to serve on the Events and
Inclusion Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nominations for the following volunteer
appointments; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the appointees are
residents of Riverton City and are willing and capable of serving on the Events & Inclusion
Committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointments of the Mayor's
nominations to the Events and Inclusion Committee.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointment of Broc Stowe
          to the Riverton City Events and Inclusion Committee, which terms shall end
          December 31, 2025.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 17th day of
