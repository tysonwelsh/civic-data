PUBLIC HEARINGS
A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC COMMENT REGARDING APPLICATION GPZ-1-2021,
     FILED BY ALEX WINDER, REQUESTING A GENERAL PLAN CHANGE
     FROM COMMERCIAL/OFFICE AND OFFICE/EDUCATION TO LIGHT
     MANUFACTURING AND A ZONE CHANGE FROM C-2 (GENERAL
     COMMERCIAL) TO LI (LIGHT INDUSTRIAL) FOR PROPERTY
     LOCATED AT 5718 WEST 3500 SOUTH
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled March 9, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear
     and consider public comments regarding application GPZ-1-2021, filed by Alex
     Winder, requesting a General Plan Change from Commercial/Office and
     Office/Education to Light Manufacturing and a Zone Change from C-2 (General
     Commercial) to LI (Light Industrial) for property located at 5718 West 3500 South.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              The applicant is proposing to develop the subject property as flex space with
              80 to 90% warehouse space and 10 to 20% office. The current C-2 zone
              allows a building with up to 50% warehouse with the balance being office.
              The proposed LI zone allows for a building to be 100% warehouse. Access
              to the property is gained through two easements – one from the east going
              through the Alpine Storage property to 5600 West and the other to the south
MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING MARCH 9, 2021

        connecting to 3500 South.

        One of the design standards in the LI zone is that “Overhead doors shall not
        be oriented toward High-Image Arterial Streets...” The Mountain View
        Corridor is one of the High-Image Arterial Streets and the proposed building
        does have overhead doors that face the Mountain View Corridor. The
        proposed development agreement accompanying this General Plan/zone
        change allows for a limited amount of overhead doors along the west side
        of the building in exchange for additional design features.

 Mayor Bigelow opened the Public Hearing.

 Alex Winder stated that he is available for questions. He indicated that it was
 difficult to find a good use for this particular property and he feels this will address
 a needed market.

 Jim Vesock stated that he feels this will be a good use for the property and agreed
 that it is needed in the City.

 Mayor Bigelow closed the Public Hearing.
