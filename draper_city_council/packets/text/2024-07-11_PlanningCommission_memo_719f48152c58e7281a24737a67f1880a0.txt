MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2024-07-11
Re: Public Hearing: Carol Lee Subdivision Plat Amendment and Deviation Request
(Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment and Deviation for
approximately 1.07 acres located on the north side of 12600 South, at approximately 995 East
12600 South. The property is currently zoned TC. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat
Amendment be approved to modify the common property line between two lots and a Deviation to
keep the street improvements as they currently are.



The Carol Lee Subdivision was recorded in 1998. The properties were zoned RA2 (Residential
Agricultural, 20,000 sq ft lot minimums) at that time. They were rezoned to the TC zone when that
zone was created. The original plat included three lots. This application is looking only at Lots 2 and
3. Lot 3 was illegally subdivided in 2003 into a 0.13 acre piece and a 0.34 acre piece by the owner of
Lot 3 at that time. The current property owner of Lot 3 now wishes to legally amend Lot 3 and sell a
portion of the lot to the owners of Lot 2.



The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their June 13, 2024 meeting and continued the
request. The request at that time sought to create three lots out of the Lots 2 and 3. The Planning
Commissioners determined that creating a third lot that could be developed under the TC zone was
inappropriate given the residential nature of the street and asked the applicant to bring back an
amended plat showing two lots. The plat has been amended as requested.
