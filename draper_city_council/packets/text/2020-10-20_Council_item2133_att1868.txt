                                     ORDINANCE NO. 1461


AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING SECTION 3-1-320
OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO PROVIDE ALTERNATE MEMBERS
ON THE DRAPER CITY DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION ADVISORY BOARD.


       WHEREAS, Title 3 of the Draper City Municipal Code (“Title 3”) regulates general
provisions regarding boards, commissions, and committees; and

       WHEREAS, the City previously adopted an ordinance creating the Draper City Diversity
and Inclusion Advisory Board; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to have alternate board members on the Draper City
Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Board.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following findings
to amend Title 3 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) the City is seeking input for initiatives
that cultivate an inclusive culture and stimulate socio-economic equality for the greater
community. 2) The City is seeking input regarding diversity, equality and inclusivity within the
City. 3) The proposed amendments comply with the Utah Code Annotated. 4) The proposed
amendments are in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper.

       Section 2. Amendment. Section 3-1-320 (B) of the Draper City Municipal Code is
hereby amended to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by
reference.

        Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to make non-
substantive corrections to the attached Exhibit A, which may include corrections of punctuation,
spelling, formatting, or similar errors prior to submitting the Ordinance to American Legal
Publishing.

        Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final
passage.

3-1-320 DRAPER CITY DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION ADVISORY BOARD:


B. Members:

     2. Term: Members shall be appointed to terms of two (2) years. In order to provide for
proper staggering of terms so that all appointments do not expire at the same time and to provide
appropriate opportunities for involvement by a variety of interested persons, four (4) two (2)
initial membership positions shall be for a term of one (1) year, five (5) three initial membership
positions shall be for a term of two (2) years.

    3. Alternates: In addition to the regular members of the Advisory Board, the Mayor may
appoint up to three (3) alternate members to the Advisory Board with the advice and consent of
the City Council. Alternate members shall serve one (1) year terms or until appointed to fill a
vacancy on the board. Alternate members may be reappointed for successive terms. In the event
a regular member is absent from a meeting or event, an alternate member may be called upon by
the Chair to appear at the meeting or event in the place of the absent regular member. Alternate
members shall have no right to vote unless they have been duly called by the Chair to participate
in the meeting in the place of an absent regular member.
