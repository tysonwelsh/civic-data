    1. CONTRACT WITH OGDEN CITY TO FUND SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS FOR 17TH STREET IN OGDEN; WALL
       AVENUE TO OGDEN CITY’S WEST BOUNDARY – CONTRACT C2015-63
         Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, noted that this item and the
         next were approved by the County Commission as part of the 2014 annual local option transportation
         funds application process. This subject area has limited lengths of curb and gutter and much of the
         infrastructure is in very poor condition. He said that $387,500 will come from the quarter preservation
         fund and $4,083,330 from the third quarter cent sales tax for transportation fund.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-63 with Ogden City to fund safety improvements
         for 17th Street in Ogden; Wall Avenue to Ogden City’s west boundary; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. CONTRACT WITH OGDEN CITY TO FUND CAPACITY AND SAFETY IMPROVEMENTS FOR SKYLINE
       DRIVE IN OGDEN; QUAIL RUN DRIVE TO 4600 SOUTH – CONTRACT C2015-64
       Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, noted that there were a
       couple of segments of Ogden City’s portion of Skyline Drive in this area. The City recently completed a
       project that acquired much of the right-of-way needed for the roadway. The completed project will offer
       a secondary north-south route to Weber State University and 36th Street, allow for bicycling, realignment
       of the intersection of Skyline Drive and Country Hills and connect Skyline Drive with Harrison Blvd.
       The project is being funded with $50,000 from the quarter preservation fund and $2,950,000 from the
       third quart cent sales tax for transportation fund.
       Commissioner Bell moved to approve Contract C2015-64 with Ogden City to fund safety improvements
       for Skyline Drive in Ogden; Quail Run Drive to 4600 South; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    4. CONTRACT WITH LAWANA W. MALAN TRUSTEE OF THE LAWANA W. MALAN TRUST FOR THE
       RELOCATION OF THE COMMON BOUNDARY LINES BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY AND THE LAWANA W.
       MALAN TRUST – CONTRACT C2015-66

       Kevin McLeod, of County Operations, stated that the county purchased property off of 12th Street about
       one year ago. This is to relocate the property lines between the county and the Malan’s property on the
       north side of the County Sheriff’s Complex. There had been some question as to where the property lines
       were legally; there were two monuments that showed a significant difference. He said that county staff
       met with Claude Malan, who was in agreement with the final legal description for the boundary lines.
       Commissioner Bell moved to approve Contract C2015-66 with Lawana W. Malan Trustee of the Lawana
       W. Malan Trust for the relocation of the common boundary lines between Weber County and the Lawana
       W. Malan Trust; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
    5. QUIT CLAIM DEED FROM WEBER COUNTY TO SOUTH OGDEN CITY
       Nate Pierce, County Operations Department Director, noted that the county owns about 200 small pieces
       of properties that are of no use to the county, and appropriate action is taken when possible.
       This is a follow up to the consent item approved today. This property was deeded to the county in 1896
       by the Ogden Land Company for future roadway development, which never occurred and is not likely to
       come to fruition. It is surrounded by property owned by South Ogden City, which has expressed a desire
       to acquire it to facilitate development of the area. Mr. Pierce said that it is in the interest of both parties to
       deed the property to the City. This parcel has no parcel number; it has a legal description.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the Quit Claim Deed from Weber County to South Ogden City;
       Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
    6. QUIT CLAIM DEED FROM THE COUNTY TO SOUTH OGDEN CITY FOR PARCELS #06-019-0002 & #06-019-0010
       Nate Pierce, County Operations Department Director, stated that these two parcels on 4400 South
       Washington Blvd. were deeded to the county in 1958-1959 for a roadway to connect Washington Blvd.
       and Adams Avenue. These two parcels lie entirely within Sough Ogden City’s boundary and the City
       maintains that roadway.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve a Quit Claim Deed from Weber County to South Ogden City for
       Parcels #06-019-0002 and #06-019-0010; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    8. REVISED AUDIT COMMITTEE POLICY 8.1 & INTERNAL AUDIT POLICIES AND PROCEDURES 8.2

       Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor, presented these revised policies. The Audit Committee Policy seeks
       to have varied representation, including a citizen member, and for the Committee to be as independent as
       possible. The Internal Audit Policy addresses who the internal auditor reports to, how internal jobs are
       decided and conducted and the reporting process.
       Commissioner Bell moved to approve the revised Audit Committee Policy 8.1 and the Internal Audit
       Policies and Procedures 8.2; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
