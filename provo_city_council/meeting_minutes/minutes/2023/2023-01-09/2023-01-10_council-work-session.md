(3:07:30) After a break, the Council moved onto discussing legislative priorities. Mr. Handley
introduced this portion of the discussion. He highlighted the change the Council will have this
year by eliminating Council committees; this will shift some of the burden of responsibility to
the Council to make policy changes happen. Under Council policy, any two Councilors can
request an item to be scheduled for the Work Meeting. The goal is for the entire Council to be
more efficient, so that items aren’t being researched in a committee and then experiencing a
redundant process once they come to the full group. The new process would require more work
from each Councilor to build consensus in order to move items forward that are important to
each of them. Staff are prepared to assist the Council, but it may require some flexibility initially
as everyone gets used to the new process.

Michael Sanders, Policy Analyst, explained the process the Council would use to prioritize
several of the discussed items using a dot sticker exercise. Staff responded to clarifying questions
from Councilors about how the exercise would work.

Following the exercise, Melia Dayley, Policy Analyst, reviewed how the process would work for
