ORDINANCE NO. 2025-O-13 DATE: September @@, 2025
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 9, CHAPTER 90, SECTION 025 TO CORRECT
A SCRIVENERS ERROR IN THE CODE LANGUAGE AND TO DESIGNATE THE
ALL OF EMIGRATION CANYON IN THE WILDLAND URBAN INTERFACE AREA

WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon previously adopted the 2006 Wildland Urban
Interface Code in Ordinance No. 2022-03-02 on April 5, 2022; and

WHEREAS, the code language contains the incorrect municipal name for Emigration
Canyon; and

WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon desires to correct the error, AND

WHEREAS, Utah law requires municipalities to designate a map of the Wildland Urban
interface area and to record the map.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON CITY COUNCIL
AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1: Title 9, Chapter 90, Section 025 is hereby amended to read as follows:
9.90.025 Wildland-Urban Interface Code
The Utah Wildland-Urban Interface Code, 2006 Edition, published by the International Code
Council, is hereby adopted as the Wildland-Urban Interface Code of the—Fown—of
BrightenEmigration Canyon for the regulation and governance of the mitigation of hazard to life
and property from the intrusion of wildland exposure, fire from adjacent structures, and prevention
of structure fires from spreading to wildland fuels in town.
Utah Wildland-Urban Interface Code, 2006 Edition, published by the International Code Council,
together with any future amendments thereto shall, be maintained as public records with the Towa
ClerkCity Recorder are incorporated as a part of this Section.
Section 2: All property shown on the attached map of Emigration Canyon is hereby designated as
a Wildland Urban Interface area.
