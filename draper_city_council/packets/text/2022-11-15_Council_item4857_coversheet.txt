Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From:
CC:  Date: November 15, 2022
Re:  Comments: Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) 10-3-020(C) requires that any residential parcel not within a subdivision shall construct frontage improvements and allows the owner to comply with DCMC 9-27-110(C) where an owner may pay an assessment in lieu of public infrastructure construction.
In order to comply with the code, the owners requested they pay the assessment in lieu of construction.  The city staff determined it would best serve the owner and the city to proceed with an assessment-in-lieu of construction of public infrastructure rather than having the owner construct the improvements.
Staff recommends approval of the assessment in lieu for the following locations.  In the future, having the city construct the frontage improvements will ensure that the seamless design and construction of the improvements in the area and potentially save the city costs associated with modifying the city’s design by incorporating infrastructure not presently constructed.
Chris Noorda property located at 13770 South Fort Street
Chris Noorda property located at 13712 South Fort Street
William B. Warner property located at 12539 South Relation Street
Wilson Simons property located at 189 East 13560 South
Scott Godfrey property located at 12435 South 1700 East
Exeter of Draper Land LLC property located at 13004 South Pony Express Road
