**Resolution: Budget for the Local Building Authority for Fiscal Year 2024-25**
The Board will accept public comment and consider approving a resolution that would adopt the final budget for the Capital Projects Fund of the Local Building Authority of Salt Lake City, Utah for Fiscal Year 2024-25.

The LBA is a financing tool for cities and government entities, like libraries, to bond for capital projects at better interest rates.
