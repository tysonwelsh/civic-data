Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order

Commission Chair Eyre welcomed everyone to the meeting, and excused Commissioner Jones.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Terry Bishop’s request for review of the
Bishop Development Agreement, allowing to increase the number of lots in a subdivision to
13, for a 7-acre parcel located at 2424 West 900 North.

Jacob Curtis presented the item. Previously, the Commission approved a zone change. The City
Council denied it and sent it back with instructions to enter into a development agreement
instead.

Terry Bishop, the applicant, was present. He thanked staff for their work in creating the
agreement.

Commissioner Eyre opened and closed public comment.

The Commission discussed the reasoning behind the number of lots and the explanation for the
original zone change.

                Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to give a positive recommendation to the
                Lehi City Council with the findings that the Development Code amendment will

                protect migratory birds in Lehi, that the Development Code amendment is in
                conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the General Plan and its
                various elements.

                Vote: Commissioner Gehman, yes. Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner
                Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes. Commissioner Roberts, yes.

                Motion passed unanimously.

3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request review of amendments to
the Lehi City Development Code Chapter 35, Community Forestry, adding requirements
for trees affected by migratory birds and clarifying the City’s recommended public tree
planting list.

Jacob Curtis presented the item. Minor changes to wording have been made, and a paragraph
referencing federal and state laws protecting birds was added.

Jessica Smith, the applicant and an urban forester for Lehi City, was present.

Commissioner Eyre opened and closed public comment.

The Commission discussed the particulars of how this would affect development if it were
invoked.

                Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to recommend approval with the findings
                that it is in accordance with the General Plan and the goals of the city. He
                included all DRC comments. Commissioner Jones seconded.

                Vote: Commissioner Gehman, yes. Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner
                Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes. Commissioner Roberts, yes.

                Motion passed unanimously.

3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to Chapter 26 of the Development Code, Accessory Uses updating the
requirements for Detached Accessory Dwelling Units (DADUs) to meet 2026 State
Legislation.

Jacob Curtis presented the item. This update brings City Code into compliance with State Code.

Commissioner Eyre opened public comment.

Polo Su’a-Filo spoke. He is against the small lot size but understands that the purpose is to
comply with State Code.

Marshall Gary spoke. He appreciates that this makes it easier to create DADUs, even though he
is not in favor of the State forcing cities to change code. He asked the Commission if the city was
in favor of DADUs or against them. He shared some recommendations from his reading list that
inform his opinions on this. He discussed incremental development, and provided a link to the
Strong Towns website that offers resources to homeowners hoping to do incremental
development on their properties (Exhibit A). He noted his confusion over the requirements for
on-street vs. off-street parking.

Commissioner Eyre closed public comment.

City staff discussed the changes made, and sizing and setback issues that can arise from creating
DADUs in small lots.

The Commission discussed the need to represent all citizens, who have varying opinions of
DADUs. They then discussed the requirements for ADUs, including contents and height
setbacks.

                Motion: Commissioner Roberts moved to give a positive recommendation to the
                Lehi City Council with the findings that it will bring the city into compliance with
                State Code, that it will add clarity to the requirements for ADUs, and that it
                conforms to the general intent and purposes of the General Plan. He included all
                DRC comments. Commissioner Kunze seconded.

Commissioner Eyre asked for the formatting to be revisited.

                Amended Motion: Commissioner Roberts moved to give a positive
                recommendation to the Lehi City Council with the findings that it will bring the
                city into compliance with State Code, that it will add clarity to the requirements
                for ADUs, and that it conforms to the general intent and purposes of the General
                Plan. He included all DRC comments. He requested that staff make formatting
                readjustments to help clarify the text. Commissioner Kunze seconded.

                Vote: Commissioner Gehman, yes. Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner
                Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes. Commissioner Roberts, yes.

                Motion passed unanimously.
