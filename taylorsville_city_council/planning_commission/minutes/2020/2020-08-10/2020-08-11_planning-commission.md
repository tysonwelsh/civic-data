                                          BRIEFING SESSION – 6:30 P.M.

1.   The briefing session to review the Agenda was conducted by Mr. Jim Spung. The Agenda consists of a Zoning Map
     Amendment to consider a Conditional Use Permit for the operation of an automotive service and repair, minor
     business within 250 feet of a residential district. Ms. Kerdolff explained her item, adding that the applicant will
     do no repair work on site and recommended approval. Commissioner Wright advised that he was concerned
     about putting this type of use on this highly visible corner. He realized this is a very difficult property to develop
     but felt this use would not say, “You have arrived in Taylorsville” in a positive way. Commissioner Wendel
     commented that this is an important intersection especially considering what improvements they are making
     across the street on the City office site property. She did feel with the right design standards and landscaping it
     could be made to look good though. Commissioner Willardson agreed with that comment. Commissioner
     Barbieri advised this is difficult for her because she could envision cars being left on the site in disrepair, which
     would reflect negatively on the City site across the street, especially with the Arts Center being almost built.
     Commissioner Russell        was concerned that the gas tanks from the previous use were still underground and
     was informed they had been removed. Commissioner Quigley commented that the canal there will always
     present a problem with any development. General questions were made by the Commissioners such as
     Commissioner Willardson wanted to know if he could go into the neighborhoods and receive input and was
     informed of the proper procedures necessary to comply with State statutes. He also asked Staff to get him the
     agenda and packet sooner so he can be better prepared. Commissioner Burggraaf advised him that it is posted on
     the City web site and the public notice of the hearing is posted on the State of Utah site 10 days before the
     meeting.       Commissioners asked that Staff keep them up-to-date on the projects more frequently, which Mr.
     Spung said would be done.

                                                  ZONING MAP AMENDMENT

  3.            35C20 – Erik Hoffman (Quick Emissions) - Consideration of a Conditional Use Permit for the Operation
                of an Automotive Service and Repair, Minor Business within 250 feet of a Residential District at 2724 W
                5400 S. (Karyn Kerdolff/GIS Planner)


       3.1             Ms. Kerdolff presented this item. She said the applicant, Erik Hoffman, is proposing a new multi-
                 tenant development for the subject property. The lot is currently vacant but was previously a gas station.
                 The applicant is proposing to develop a Quick Emissions at the location. Mr. Hoffman owns and operates
                 a Quick Emissions in Taylorsville located at 2570 West 4700 South and is looking to relocate that business
                 to 2724 West 5400 South. Under Taylorsville City Code, Quick Emissions would be considered an
                 automotive service and repair, minor. However, the applicant asserts there would be no repairs or oil
                 changes done on site. A public notice was sent to all affected entities and residents within 300 feet of the
                 property and as of this date, no public comments were received. Staff recommends approval based on
                 the Findings.

       3.2 FINDINGS:
           3.2.1  This application was initiated by Erik Hoffman on behalf of Quick Emissions.
           3.2.2  A conditional use permit for the operation of a business that would be classified as an automotive
                  service and repair, minor with 250 feet of a residential district.
           3.2.3  The proposed automotive services will cause little to no disturbances (odors, light, noise, etc.) for
                  surrounding residential districts.
           3.2.4  The property is directly adjacent to a Mixed-Use district on all sides, providing a buffer between the
                  subject area and residential districts.
           3.2.5  The site was previously an automotive self-service station.
           3.2.6  The applicant’s request improves a currently underutilized property along 5400 South.

       3.3 APPLICANT ADDRESS: Erik Hoffman                 reaffirmed that there will be no repair work or oil changes done on
           site. He added that they have three locations, the most recent being in Layton, Utah, which looks like what
           this one will. The elevation of that building was shown. This use will only take up a portion of the building
           and he will lease out the rest, after very careful screening of the tenants as to appropriateness for their
           business on this site. The lot is difficult to develop due to the easements in place for the canal, gas and water.
           Also, UDOT requires right-in, right-out access.

             3.3.1   Commissioner McElreath asked him if they would be doing vehicle inspections to which he replied it
                     would only be administering the emission tests, with absolutely no repairs being done.

             3.3.2   Commissioner Quigley said that he is familiar with this operation. There is a waiting room and
                     counter. The technician takes the cars around the back to conduct the test. He felt this was an
                     upgrade. He asked Mr. Hoffman if he had any letters of intent from potential tenants and he replied
                     that he did not but was not actively pursuing any at this juncture. Mr. Hoffman advised that he
                     would utilize 2,000 square feet and the rest of the 5,500 square feet would be finished in phases.

             3.3.3   Commissioner Wendel said she originally thought that 46 stalls was a lot of parking and she wanted
                     to make sure the landscaping requirements were sustained with that amount of parking. She
                     understood that the first building would be 5,500 square feet, of which he would utilize 2,000, with
                     the rest being finished in phases. She asked if then another building was anticipated to be built to
                     the west in the future, which Mr. Hoffman said was correct. Commissioner Wendel wanted to know

    CITY COUNCIL MEETING DISCUSSION: Commissioner McElreath gave his report for his attendance at the August
    5, 2020 City Council meeting.      Commissioner Barbieri also made some comments, including they will
    announce the name of the person who will fill the vacancy existing on the City Council on September 30, 2020.
          She also reviewed the status of projects in Taylorsville, i.e. Target’s soft opening will be in October, the
    Performing Arts Center is a little bit behind because the seats were being made outside of the Country and
    shipment has been held up. The Temple site has been leveled and the Church is hoping to have a ground-breaking
    date soon. Swig and Nothing Bundt Cakes are coming into Taylorsville and Macey’s will open here soon. Casa
    Linde Apartments has been razed so the Bus Rapid Transit system can go through there to Salt Lake Community
    College. Redwood Road beautification will happen in Spring 2021.

    DISCUSSION:          Commissioner Wendel talked about the conditional use permit process saying that she
    recalled from Planning Commission training that the Commission is obligated to approve them unless there is
    something that the applicant can’t mitigate. She wanted to revisit that in the future, so Commissioners are
    conscientious of what the application is actually for.         Commissioner Burggraaf           said it looks like the
    City is no longer using Sterling Codifiers and asked what the reason was for that shift. He asked because he liked
    the functionality of it being an embedded version of City Code into the web site. Ms. Kerdolff said the new
    organization that does it for the City, American Legal, apparently bought out Sterling Codifiers or enacted a merge
    with them. She also noticed that some of the functionality has declined and she has been working with them to
    upgrade it to work better. Mr. Spung said it was not a City-initiated action but between the two companies.
