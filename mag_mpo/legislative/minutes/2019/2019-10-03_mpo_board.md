Public Comment None Utah Transit Authority Board Appoints New Executive Director -Kent Millington, UTA After a six-month national search, the UTA Board of Trustees announced that they have chosen Carolyn Gonot as the agency’s new Executive Director. Gonot is a 25-year transit professional with a background in Civil Engineering. She will join UTA after 22 years with Santa Clara Valley Transit Authority (VTA) where she led the funding and planning for the 16-mile, six-station extension of the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) system into San Jose. Corridor Preservation Request: Payson Main St. I-15 Interchange Modification -Jim Price, MAG On September 5, 2019, the Regional Planning Committee approved the Payson City/UDOT request for $453,000 from the Corridor Preservation Fund. UDOT has recently received an infusion of $7 million into its Corridor Preservation Fund and no longer requires funds from Utah County for this important purchase. MAG Staff recommends that UDOT purchase this property with its funds, but that the original approval stand for the time being. No action is needed by the committee at this time. Lehi TIP Modification 700S/Rail Trail Phase 3 -Robert Allen, MAG Lehi put its TIP funded Rail Trail Phase 3 project out to bid recently, but the lowest bid received was 15% over budget. The city desires to re-bid the project this fall/winter when lower prices may be forthcoming, but desires to have additional funds in place to cover anticipated higher costs. The city requests approval to transfer up to $300,000 from the existing Lehi 700 South Cycle- Track project to the Rail Trail project. Due to recent roadway improvements from residential development along 700 S, the city believes that the Cycle-Track project can remain whole or easily be made so even after this transfer. MAG Staff recommends that approval for the transfer of TIP funds be approved. Mayor Pray moved that the TIP be modified to reflect the transfer request as proposed. Mayor Wright seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Regional Planning Committee October 3, 2019 Page 4 Spanish Fork TIP Modification: Provo Sub Railroad-Scope Modification-Robert Allen, MAG Spanish Fork City was awarded $669,780 of exchange funds in 2018 for design work on the Provo Sub railroad consolidation project. The funds are available in 2021. The city would like to modify the scope of the project to include ROW acquisition for the following reasons: ● Union Pacific has slowed significantly and full design and environmental work at this time is not recommended. ● There is a previously platted development where ROW for the project must be acquired. ● Railroad projects are not eligible to use existing corridor preservation funds. Spanish Fork would like to use $300,000 of the approved funds to purchase the needed ROW and use the rest to forward the permitting process prior to design and environmental work. MAG Staff recommends the scope be modified to include ROW acquisition. Commissioner Ainge moved that RPC approve scope of the Provo Sub Railroad project be modified to include ROW acquisition. Mayor Westmoreland seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. TIP Project Selection Process Modifications - Robert Allen, MAG The Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) lists all funded regional transportation projects (MPO, UDOT, UTA, locals). The MPO selects projects for State Exchange Funds, MPO Federal Funds, County Transportation Sales Tax Funds every two years. The next selection cycle will begin this fall and select projects to be funded in 2023 and 2024. At the beginning of each selection cycle, the process is reviewed, and adjustments are considered. On August 26 the following topics were discussed: Adjustments and recommendations made by TAC on September 23, 2019 are as followed: 1. TAC Approved: Projects must be included in a local plan to be eligible. 2. TAC Approved: Airport projects that increase capacity are eligible. 3. TAC Denied: County transportation tax funds to be distributed by population or other method. 4. TAC Approved: Costs and scope may be reviewed by a 3rd party. MAG may fund a third party to review the scope and cost estimates of projects. 5. TAC Approved: Project selection voting will be made public after the voting is completed. MPO Regional Planning Committee October 3, 2019 Page 5 6. TAC Approved: MAG Staff scoring will be added to the TAC voting results to rank each project and create the initial ranked project list (25% Technical/75% TAC) Mayor Mann moved that the TIP project selection process be amended as presented. Mayor Christensen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously Updates Federal Highway Administration Steve Call, FHA, reported that the Statewide Transportation Improvement Program (STIP) was approved October 1, 2019.
