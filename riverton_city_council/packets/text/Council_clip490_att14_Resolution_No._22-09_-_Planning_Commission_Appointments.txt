                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-09

A RESOLUTION APPOINTING AND REAPPOINTING COMMISSION MEMBERS TO
             THE RIVERTON CITY PLANNING COMMISSION

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance 2-95-020, the Mayor shall
appoint with advice and consent of the City Council residents of Riverton City to serve on the
Planning Commission; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for appointment of Evan
Matheson-At-Large, Darren Park-District 4, and Jon Gilchrist-At-Large; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for re-appointment of Gary
Cannon-District 5; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointments of the Mayor's
nominations to the Planning Commission.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointment of Evan
          Matheson as Commissioner, which term shall end January 31, 2024 and the
          appointment of Jon Gilchrist and Darren Park as Commissioners, which term
          shall end January 21, 2026.
       2. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the re-appointment of Gary
          Cannon as Commissioner, which term shall end January 31, 2026.
       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of
January, 2022 by the following vote:
