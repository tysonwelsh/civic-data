5. CBD Site Plan, for existing fourplex at 2112 Reeves Avenue. (Attachment 5)

  Mr. Scott Broussard stated he has recently purchased this property with the intent to renovate the
  existing multi-family structure. He indicated he has reviewed the Staff recommendations and finds
  them acceptable. He noted he has previously received a variance to allow a 12’ right-of-way in order to
  allow vehicle access to the parking lot. He stated the property consists of a single-family home to the
  north and an existing multi-family structure, as well as a neighboring shed which extends over the
  property line.

  Mr. Simpson stated there had previously been a nonconforming use permit for the fourplex, but due to
  vacancy, the nonconforming right has been lost and the project must comply with the development
  standards of the CBD zone. He stated records indicate the two homes had been on separate lots but
  had recently been combined without the City’s approval. He stated the fourplex has been abandoned
  and has been before the Mayor for demolition in the past. The use now subject to standards of the CBD
  zone in terms of parking and access. The reduced access was approved by the Board of Zoning
  Adjustment as a one-way access upon the condition the building is brought up to current building code
  regulations. The applicant has submitted a proposed site plan which shows the 12’ access as well as


                                                                                           Page 5 of 15
on-site parking, but does not show a trash receptacle or lighting. The CBD zone requires all trash
canisters to be screened from the public view and either a dumpster or individual cans must be
screened with materials compatible with the CBD zone, which would eliminate one parking space. As
it is typical for lights to provide security for parking, a lighting plan is required and fixtures must be
directed downward so light is contained on the property. Landscaping is shown which is a combination

recessed and space provided for front stairs. The existing building material of brick, cement and stucco

to be replaced with brick, an removal of the plywood to be replaced with cement fiberboard. While the

alternate transportation is available. As the main Frontrunner station is within a block of this property,

8. Public Hearing, Petition to Rezone from R-1-6 to R-1-5 property at 480 E 7th Street. (Attachment 8)

  Mr. Mike Bastian stated this is an abandoned church property which is for sale and is currently zoned R-
  1-6. In designing residential lots to meet this code, remnant parcels would be created. The request is to
  rezone the property to R-1-5 with the intent to develop single-family lots. Because of the reduced lot
  width being 55’ rather than 60’, there is a better fit and no remnant parcels are created. He stated many
  of the existing lots in this neighborhood have only a 55’ lot width and the proposed lot area would fit
