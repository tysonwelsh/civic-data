                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-25

          A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE RIVERTON CITY EMERGENCY
                           OPERATIONS PLAN

       WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to adopt an Emergency Operations Plan (“EOP”) which
describes how governmental entities will respond to an emergency; and

        WHEREAS, the EOP will be activated and implemented when the Riverton City Mayor
or designees declares an emergency that requires the same to activate, or when an emergency is
considered imminent or probable, and the implementation of the EOP and the activation of the
Riverton City Emergency Operations Center is considered a prudent, proactive response; and

      WHEREAS, an EOP has been prepared and will be used to train staff in the context of
emergencies; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best interests
of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to adopt said EOP.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council that the EOP is
hereby adopted and will take effect immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 18th day of April
2023 by the following vote:
