                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-32

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN INTERLOCAL
  AGREEMENT WITH SOUTH JORDAN CITY TO SUPPLY SECONDARY WATER

        WHEREAS, Riverton is the owner and operator of waterworks providing intake and
distribution of canal grade water for irrigation purposes to Riverton residents; and

        WHEREAS, South Jordan has identified a limited regional area within its municipal
territory which currently does not have access to canal grade water for irrigation purposes; and

         WHEREAS, South Jordan has determined that as it works to improve irrigation water
facilities servicing the Area, South Jordan may temporarily provide water service for irrigation to
its residents by connecting to the existing waterworks owned by Riverton; and

       WHEREAS, South Jordan and Riverton are each authorized to enter into this Interlocal
Agreement to provide water service, pursuant to the Interlocal Cooperation Act of Utah, Utah Code
Ann. §§ 11-13-101 et seq.; and

        WHEREAS, South Jordan desires to obtain a supply of irrigation water from Riverton in
order to provide adequate supplies of irrigation water to South Jordan residents located within the
Area described in Exhibit “A.”

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1. Interlocal Agreement. The Riverton City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to execute with
   South Jordan City an Interlocal Water Service Agreement in substantially similar form to the
   Agreement attached as Exhibit A, after the same is approved as to form by the Riverton City
   Attorney.

2. Effective Date, Termination. This Resolution shall become effective immediately.
