Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session
Thursday, June 9, 2022, Commissioner Lucero, called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

 PC 22-028 Suite Maple-Short Term Rental [Conditional Use Permit] John & Tanya Rice
 request a Conditional Use Permit to rent out a portion of their home as a short-term rental
 located at 1414 Maple Drive in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 07-
 002-0011 (Hillcrest Neighborhood).


STAFF R. Holley reviewed the request for a Conditional Use Permit for a new short-term rental (STR)
in a portion of the basement within this single-family home. The proposed STR will have a separate
entrance on the east side of the home and will include a kitchenette, living area, bathroom, and one
bedroom. The maximum occupancy for a one-bedroom short-term rental is four people. Parking
spaces shall be provided at one space per bedroom with a minimum of two parking stalls for the
STR, in addition to the two parking spots for the homeowner. The application includes a total of four
parking stalls located on the property. Two within the garage and two within the driveway. All parking
stalls being proposed are outside of the setback areas and sized at 9’x18’. As proposed, the parking
stalls comply with the standards in the LDC. A new portion of sidewalk will be added on the east
side of the home to connect the parking area to the short-term rental entrance on the east side of


 the home. This application complies with the minimum proximity regulations as the proposed STR
 is not located within 500 feet of another permitted STR.
 PROPONENT: John Rice, the homeowner, stated that they are creating this living space to mainly
 be used by his father-in-law. When the space is not occupied by family, they plan to rent it out to
 professors visiting the university as Mr. Rice is affiliated with the University and the Water Lab.

 PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

 COMMISSION: R. Holley clarified the requirements to extend a driveway to get to additional parking
 stalls and the setbacks where parking is not allowed.

 PC 22-029 The Rock House-Short Term Rental [Conditional Use Permit] John & Janene
 Petersen request a Conditional Use Permit to rent out a portion of their home as a short-term
 rental located at 317 East 300 North #A in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential (NR-6)
 zone; TIN 06-046-0008 (Adams Neighborhood).


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Conditional Use Permit for a new short-term rental in a
single-family home at 317 East 300 North #A. The house is two stories and has four bedrooms, two
bathrooms, and two family rooms. The proposal is to rent out three of the four bedrooms for the
short-term rental. The proposal shows three off-street parking stalls accessed by a shared driveway.
The commissioners will need to determine how the applicant will prohibit the rental of the fourth
bedroom.

Commissioner Lewis asked if it could be required that the fourth bedroom be locked. And if there
are annual inspections on the short-term rentals. Conditions can be placed that mitigate any
potential negative impacts directly associated with the request. There is one inspection done at the
time of business licensing. Commissioner Croshaw asked if the additional bedroom can be used by
the owner for storage. R. Holley said that is possible, or the room could be remodeled into another
type of space other than a bedroom to be part of the rental.

PROPONENT: Janene Petersen, owner of the property said that they are wanting to remodel the
fourth bedroom into a bathroom, or possibly turn bedroom one into a family room. She also said that
the windows in the basement bedrooms are not up to the building code so they cannot rent them out
until they are updated. She thought that they would rent out the 2 bedrooms upstairs until they get
the basement remodeled then convert the upstairs bedroom into a living space and ultimately have
the entire home as the three-bedroom short-term rental they applied for.


PUBLIC: Larry Hips, the neighbor across the street gave some history about this home and the
home directly to the north. He spoke about the large London Plane trees in the front yard being cut
down and how it would take 50 years to replace them.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare asked if a short-term rental portion of a home has to be
separated from the rest of the home. R. Holley said that is not required in the Land Development
Code, someone could rent out one bedroom of their home as a short-term rental if they wanted to.

Commissioner Lucero asked why they are looking at a three-bedroom rental when the basement
cannot be rented as-is. R. Holley stated that the windows would have to be upgraded at the
building/business licensing permit stage.

M. DeSimone said that part of the business license plan is to label what bedrooms are being used
as a rental, the committee can make a condition that the 4th bedroom cannot be included in the short-
term rental. They can only get a business license for the number of bedrooms they will be using.

There was a discussion amongst the commissioners if a continuance is needed for the applicant to
come back and show the exact floorplan for the short-term rental or amend this conditional use permit
to a two-bedroom short-term rental. The applicant can reapply in the future to add additional
bedrooms if they choose. The applicant amended their request to a two-bedroom short-term rental
and will re-apply when they are ready to update the basement bedrooms.

Based on the discussion, the Planning Commission amended Condition no. 2 to state that the short-
term rental will only occupy two bedrooms of the home, Condition no. 3 will be amended to authorize
a total of six occupants, and Condition no. 4 will be amended to require a minimum of two parking
stalls.

 PC 22-030 Mower-Short Term Rental [Conditional Use Permit] Chris Andrus/Daniel & Cheristy
 Mower, authorized agent/owner request a Conditional Use Permit to rent out a portion of their
 home as a short-term rental located at 730 North 200 East in the Traditional Neighborhood
 Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 05-023-0021 ( Adams Neighborhood).


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Conditional Use Permit for a new short-term rental in
the basement portion of a single-family home at 730 North 200 East. The proposed short-term rental
has a separate entrance, bathroom, kitchen, living area, and two bedrooms. The parking
requirements for this proposal include two parking stalls for the main home and two stalls for the
short-term rental, totaling a minimum of four on-site parking stalls. The applicant provided a parking


 plan showing two stalls in the garage, one in the rear of the home, and one parallel parking space
 along the driveway. The stall in the rear is too small, it is required to be at a minimum of 9’x18’. The
 parallel stall along the driveway reduces the drive aisle width to below the minimum of 12 feet. As
 conditioned, the proponent will need to create a minimum of two additional parking stalls that comply
 with the Land Development Code requirements.

 PROPONENT: There were no comments made by the proponent.

 PUBLIC: Marilyn Griffin quoted the staff report, “the aspects of approving a Conditional Use Permit
 include substantiating the “preservation and enhancement of neighborhood character” and
 “compatibility and interference with use and enjoyment of neighboring properties”. This short-term
 rental will interfere with the use and enjoyment of the property to the south. It does not preserve and
 enhance the character of the neighborhood, and it does not preserve the neighborhood

 Keith Walker asked if he would be able to apply for a summer-only short-term rental in the apartment
 complexes he owns. R. Holley said that you can have one short-term rental unit within an apartment
 complex. You would have to have a short-term rental license that would be in effect during the off
 months of the regular landlord license for the long-term lease.

 COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare asked if the permit is affected when this home is sold. R.
 Holley said that the Conditional Use Permit runs with the property, if the new owner does not want
 to have a short-term rental, they can let it expire.

 Mohamud Abdullahi stated that a Conditional Use Permit has to be approved if the required
 mitigation is reasonable and can be done.

 Commissioner Croshaw asked about compact parking stalls. R. Holley said there is a compact stall
 dimension but to qualify for compact stalls, a parking lot has to have at least 50 regular size stalls.
