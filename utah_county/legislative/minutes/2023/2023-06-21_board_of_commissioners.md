COUNTY, UTAH
-Burt Harvey, Auditor

(timestamp: 3:37)

BURT GARFIELD, UTAH COUNTY ASSESSOR - STATED THAT 68% OF PROPERTIES
ARE SINGLE FAMILY HOMES SO THE VALUE DIDN’T REALLY INCREASE FROM LAST
YEAR. THERE WAS A LITTLE BIT OF INCREASE IN HIGH DENSITY VALUE SUCH AS
TWIN HOMES/TOWNHOMES/CONDOS. COMMERCIAL PROPERTIES WHICH ARE
12% OF PARCELS BUT 20% OF TAXABLE VALUE WERE RAISED QUITE A BIT
(APPROXIMATELY 12%).

BURT HARVEY, PUBLIC SERVICES & TAX ADMIN DIVISION MANAGER - STATED
THAT THE HOMEOWNERS HAVE BEEN BASICALLY BEARING THE BRUNT OF THE
PROPERTY TAX INCREASE FOR THE LAST FEW YEARS BECAUSE THAT’S WHERE
MOST OF THE MARKET VALUE OF SURGING - SINGLE FAMILY HOMES. THOSE
VALUES HAVE NOW STABILIZED. HIGH-DENSITY HOSING AND COMMERCIAL IS
NOW INCREASING TO MATCH WHERE THEIR MARKET VALUE SHOULD BE.

PAUL JONES, UTAH COUNTY DEPUTY ATTORNEY - WANTED TO NOTE THAT THE
TITLE OF THE RESOLUTION REFERENCES 2022 BUT SHOULD BE UPDATED WITH
2023 AND THE ATTEST LINE SHOULD BE LISTED AS THE CLERK INSTEAD OF
AUDITOR.
