A. CONSIDER 18-LOT PRELIMINARY SUBDIVISION PLAT FOR THE
JORDAN HEIGHTS AT VIEW 78 (FORMERLY JORDAN BLUFFS
CONDOS) PROJECT LOCATED AT 752 W BINGHAM JUNCITON BLVD
Alex Murphy said Mark Garza, representing Holmes Jordan Bluffs LLC, is requesting
approval to subdivide property located at 752 W. Bingham Junction Blvd into 18 lots as
part of the Jordan Heights at View 78 (formerly Jordan Bluffs Condos) project. The lots
created by the subdivision will simplify platting the project into condominium units as
buildings are completed. The subject property is 7.18 acres in size and is bounded by
Bingham Junction Boulevard on the south and west, the Jordan Bluffs cap slope on the
north, and Main Street/700 West on the east.
The proposed subdivision is subject to Midvale City Municipal Code Title 16
(Subdivisions), and Chapter 17-7-10.12 (Jordan Bluffs Subareas 1-3 Development Zone).
This subdivision is considered a large subdivision and subject to the review process in
§16.04.040 of the Midvale City Municipal Code. Staff has found the proposed subdivision
generally complies with the design standard requirements of Title 16 Subdivisions. No lot
standards apply for the zone. A Large Scale Master Plan (“LSMP”) and Site Plan for the
project has been approved pursuant to the development process outlined in the JB
Subareas 1-3 zone. This approval contains street tree and infrastructure requirements to
be addressed during construction. The approved layout considered the access driveways
through the project under the parking lot requirements of the zone. These driveways are
not considered private streets under the zoning ordinance but have been identified as
such on the plat for emergency response and addressing purposes. No new streets are
proposed. Staff recommends including notes on the plat addressing street trees,
infrastructure requirements, and clarification about the private roads be included on the
plat.
The subdivision ordinance requires that duty to serve letters from the water and sewer
provider (Midvale City) be provided and courtesy notices be sent regarding utility
easements to Dominion Energy, Rocky Mountain Power, Xfinity, Utopia, and CenturyLink
prior to final plat approval. Staff recommends including this requirement as a condition of
approval.
The applicant’s proposal was sent to the City Engineer and Unified Fire Authority for
review and comment. The Area Fire Inspector approved the preliminary plat. The City
Engineer approved the preliminary plat with technical corrections to be made as part of
the final plat submittal. The Planning Commission considered the preliminary plat in a
public hearing on October 28, 2020 and recommended the City Council approve the plat
with the following conditions:
