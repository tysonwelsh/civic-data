PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
                                                  Hearing began at – 3:12 p.m. 
 
OAM2024-001114 Accessory Dwelling Unit Ordinance Amendments: Salt Lake County is considering 
amendments to Chapter 19.15 of County Code, Accessory Dwelling Units. The proposed amendments make 
changes to required lot size, setbacks, height, and parking for accessory dwelling units (ADUs). The intent 
of the proposed ordinance amendments is to further the implementation of Salt Lake County’s Moderate 
Income Housing Plan and reduce barriers to ADU construction in Unincorp orated Areas. Planning Staff 
will give a presentation on the proposed amendments. Following the presentation, the Commission will 
hold a public hearing to gather public input on the proposed amendments. After the public hearing, the 
Commission may recommend that the Salt Lake County Council adopt, adopt with revisions, or not adopt 
the amendments to Chapter 19.15. Planner: Kayla Mauldin (Hearing / Motion) 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Kayla Mauldin provided an analysis of 
the amendment and proposed changes. 
 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding new structure and setbacks, on-site parking, 
percentage increase of additional properties, water contracts and other barriers, separate metering, and 
water availability by water provider requirement.   
 
Commissioner Sorenson motioned to open the public Hearing, Commissioner Omer seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING OPENED 
No one from the public was present to speak. 
 
Commissioner Sorenson motioned to close the public Hearing, Commissioner Omer seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding reducing the lot size, creation of ADU’s, lots and 
percentages between lot sizes.  
 
Motion: To recommend approval of file # OAM2024-001114 Accessory Dwelling Unit Ordinance 
Amendments: with inclusion of the Salt Lake County planning commission recommendation and adding 
a provision that ADU approval is subject to water  and other utility  availability from the water /utility 
providers. 
Motion by: Commissioner Omer 
2nd by: Commissioner Bullock 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present) 
 
Commissioner Burke adjourned.
