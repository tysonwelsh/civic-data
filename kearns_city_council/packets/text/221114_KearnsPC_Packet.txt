             Rules of Conduct for Planning Commission Meetings
PROCEDURE FOR PUBLIC COMMENT

1.   Any person or entity may appear in person or be represented by an authorized agent at any meeting of the
     Commission.

2.   Unless altered by the Chair, the order of the procedure on an application shall be:

     a. The supporting agency staff will introduce the application, including staff’s recommendations and a
        summary of pertinent written comments and reports concerning the application

     b. The applicant will be allowed up to 15 minutes to make their presentation.

     c. The Community Council representative can present their comments as applicable.

     d. Where applicable, persons in favor of, or not opposed to, the application will be invited to speak.

     e. Where applicable, persons opposing the application, in whole or in part will be invited to speak.

     f. Where applicable, the applicant will be allowed 5 minutes to provide concluding statements.

     g. Surrebuttals may be allowed at the discretion of the Chair.

CONDUCT FOR APPLICANTS AND THE PUBLIC

1.   Speakers will be called to the podium by the Chair.

2.   Each speaker, before talking, shall give his or her name and address.

3.   All comments should be directed to the Commissioners, not to the staff or to members of the audience.

4.   For items where there are several people wishing to speak, the Chair may impose a time limit, usually 3
     minutes per person, or 5 minutes for a group spokesperson. If a time limit is imposed on any member or
     spokesperson of the public, then the same time limit is imposed on other members or spokespersons of the
     public, respectively.

5.   Unless otherwise allowed by the Chair, no questions shall be asked by the speaker or Commission Members.

6.   Only one speaker is permitted before the Commission at a time.

7.   The discussion must be confined to essential points stated in the application bearing on the desirability or
     undesirability of the application.

8.   The Chair may cease any presentation or information that has already been presented and acknowledge that it
     has been noted in the public record.

9.   No personal attacks shall be indulged in by either side, and such action shall be sufficient cause for stopping the
     speaker from proceeding.

10. No applause or public outbursts shall be permitted.

11. The Chair or supporting agency staff may request police support to remove offending individuals who refuse to
    abide by these rules.

12. After the public comment portion of a meeting or hearing has concluded, the discussion will be limited to the
    Planning Commission and Staff.

                                    DESCRIPTION OF THE PROBLEM

The Municipal Services District (MSD) current planning staff is proposing to revise setback standards
for detached accessory dwelling units (ADU). The purpose of these revisions is to make detached
accessory dwelling unit construction more feasible for Kearns' residents. The proposed revisions will
address new build ADUs as well as ADUs being constructed in existing detached structures. This report
identifies the issues regarding the current setback standards for ADUs, and it outlines the proposed
changes to these standards as well as how these changes will comply with building regulations.

After receiving several applications and inquiries, the MSD current planning staff has recognized that
the existing setback standards for detached ADUs have created barriers for Kearns' residents when
constructing ADUs in existing structure on their property: particularly in existing detached garages. The
current setback standards for detached garages do not align with the current setback standards of
detached ADUs (see attachment 1). To remedy this issue without making the resident move their
existing structure to meet setback standards of an ADU, the MSD current planning staff have
considered allowing residents to build within their existing structure, by locating the nearest interior
wall of the ADU at the minimum required distance from the property line (see Attachment 2). This
approach creates wasted space and is an inefficient solution for the homeowner.

                            DESCRIPTION OF THE PROPOSED ORDINANCE
       Request: Ordinance Amendment                               File #: OAM2022-000542

The MSD current planning staff is proposing that the setbacks for ADUs in existing accessory structures
comply with the accessory building setback standards at the time the structure was erected (see
attachment 3) so long as the structure can meet building code requirements (see attachment 4). The
additional building code requirements are primarily focused on fire separation (1-hour fire-resistance
rated wall) for buildings within 5 feet of a property line. The MSD current planning staff is purposing
that any new build ADU be located a minimum of 5 feet from the rear and side property line, primarily
in an effort to allow more properties (particularly those with limited space in the rear yard) options to
accommodate an ADU. The 6-foot separation between the ADU and the primary dwelling will remain
in place for both existing structures and new build ADUs. The parking requirements for ADUs will also
remain.

Residential properties in Kearns are uniquely platted with many parcels having small rear lots. The
proposed setback requirements for both existing accessory structures as well as new build ADUs will
give Kearns' residents more options in utilizing their property. With the current shortage of housing
options, creating more feasible housing opportunities for Kearns' residents by relaxing setback
standards for ADUs and allowing residents to utilize existing resource (i.e. existing detached structures)
for ADUs, will benefit the Kearns Metro Township.

The Kearns Planning Commission must hold a public hearing and review and recommend an action to
the Kearns Metro Township Council. The Council must then hold a public hearing, after which they may
adopt, adopt with revisions, or reject the ordinance amendment recommended by the Planning
Commission.

(Current) 19.15.050 Setbacks

Side yard setbacks on all ADUs and all other setbacks on internal ADUs shall be consistent with setbacks
for a single-family dwelling in the zone. Rear yard setbacks on detached ADUs are a minimum of ten (10)
feet. Detached ADUs must also be a minimum of six (6) feet from the main dwelling. For detached
ADUs, if existing public utility easements are greater than the required setback, the minimum setback
shall be the public utility easement boundary.

(Proposed) 19.15.050 Setbacks

Side yard setbacks on internal ADUs shall be consistent with setbacks for a single-family dwelling in the
zone. Side yard setbacks on detached ADUs shall be a minimum of five (5) feet. Side yard setbacks on
existing detached structures to be converted into an ADU shall comply with the accessory building
setback standards at the time the structure was erected. Rear yard setbacks on detached ADUs shall be
a minimum of five (5) feet. Detached ADUs shall also be a minimum of six (6) feet from the main
dwelling. Rear yard setbacks on existing detached structures to be converted into an ADU shall comply
with the accessory building setback standards at the time the structure was erected. For detached
ADUs, if existing public utility easements are greater than the required setback, the minimum setback
shall be the public utility easement boundary.
