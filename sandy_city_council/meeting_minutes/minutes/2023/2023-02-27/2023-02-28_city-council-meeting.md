1.         23-082                 General Citizen Comment Period

                                  lanes across Sandy City, especially along Highland Drive. 

                                  Ms. Wendy Davis is a member of the CDBG Committee and spoke in favor of the 
                                  proposed amendments to the sign ordinance and in support of private property rights.

                                  Ms. Shirley Hutson appreciates all the Council Members do. She expressed concerns 
                                  with the ILA and does not understand why Salt Lake County would run our elections. 

                                  Ms. Pat Jones thanked the Council for all their efforts on behalf of Sandy City. She is 
                                  happy the General Citizen comment time at 6:00 pm. She expressed support of the

5.         23-081                 City Recorders Office presenting on an Interlocal Agreement (ILA) with Salt 
                                  Lake County for 2023 election services.
                   Attachments:   Sandy 2023 Elections ILA

                                  Wendy Downs, City Recorder, presented the Interlocal Agreement (ILA) with Salt Lake 
                                  County for the 2023 election services. Salt Lake County has been administering our city's 
                                  elections since 1995. She reviewed the ILA and attached exhibits with the Council, and 
                                  provided a good faith estimate of the cost for the election services. She spoke about the 
                                  costs associated with any potential recounts and of the election. Council questions 
                                  followed.

6.         CA01172023 Amendments related to Temporary Signs:
           -0006470   Amend Title 21, Chapter 26, Signage and Outdoor Advertising, Section 8, 
           (CC-WS)    Temporary Signs, of the Sandy Municipal Code
                   Attachments:   Staff Report on Temporary Signs
                                  Exhibit A - Temporary Signs - Red-lined Version
                                  Exhibit B - Temporary Signs - Clean Version
                                  Exhibit C - Request to amend temporary sign code
                                  Exhibit D - Comment from Peggy Brown
                                  02.16.2023 PC Minutes (DRAFT)

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, reviewed proposed amendments to 
                                  Temporary Signs: Title 21, Chapter 26, Signage and Outdoor Advertising, Section 8, 
                                  Temporary Signs, of the Sandy Municipal Code. The proposed amendments removes the 
                                  restrictions for placing signs on fences on private property in residential zones. Ms. 
                                  Anderson reviewed the types of signs allowed under the proposed amendments and the 
                                  length of time the signs can be posted. Lynn Pace, City Attorney, provided additional 
                                  legal insight. A lengthy Council discussion followed. Mayor Zoltanski provided comments 
                                  and expressed her support for the proposed amendments.

                                  Following the presentation and discussion, Council moved to Standing Reports.

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report


                           Dustin Fratto, Council Director, will send out an updated agenda planning calendar. 
                           tomorrow. The Employee Appreciation luncheon will take place tomorrow from 10 to 2 
                           pm.

Council Member Business

                           Council Member Marci Houseman expressed appreciation for those who showed up for 
                           the Run for the Love of Education 5k. She thanked the code enforcement team who 
                           recently helped a resident with an issue. 

                           Council Member D'Sousa provided an update from the Sandy Ambassador Committee. 
                           The committee members are very engaged and informed residents. She also let the 
                           Council know about the planning process for an upcoming Autism Awareness event with 
                           first responders and the community that she and Council Member Earl are working on

                           Council Member Ryan Mecham invited all to a District 1 Town Hall Meeting on March 6th. 

                           Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Legislative Policy 
                           Committee. She spoke about issues discussed. She also provided an update from the 
                           Wasatch Waste and Recycling District. The WFWRD will be testing electric dump trucks 
                           in March. 

                           Council Member Alison Stroud thanked the Public Works department for all their service 
                           to the city and our residents. She spoke about her ride-a-long with department staff.


Mayor's Report

                           Mayor Zoltanski expressed her appreciation to the city's snow plow drivers and the 
                           Communications team for all they have done for the city to keep our roads safe and 
                           communicate important information out to our residents during these recent storms. She 
                           acknowledged and thanked the Council for recognizing the drivers at the dinner earlier in 
                           the evening. Mayor Z congratulated Police Chief Severson who was awarded the Chief of 
                           the Year Award by the Fraternal Order of Police. Your leadership is very much valued by 
                           everyone at Sandy City. She spoke of launching the new Sandy Service Ambassador 
                           program and thanked all the residents serving on the committee. She also thanked 
                           Willow Creek Country Club for hosting the Sandy employee luncheon tomorrow.

                           Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor, announced that Sandy City is the first city to participate in the 
                           Period Project. Beginning tomorrow, all city facility restrooms will have period products 
                           readily available.

CAO Report

                           Shane Pace, Chief Administrative Officer, provided an update on the implementation of 
                           the new traffic management process at Little Cottonwood Canyon Road. The City directed 
                           cars to line up along Wasatch Blvd. in a single file line while they wait for the Canyon to 
                           reopen. There have been anywhere from 180 vehicles to 616 vehicles waiting in this line. 
                           The new process has been a huge improvement to the traffic situation at LCC and he 
                           spoke about the positive feedback the city has received.
