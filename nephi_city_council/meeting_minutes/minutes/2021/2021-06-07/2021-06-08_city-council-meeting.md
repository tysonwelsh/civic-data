Nephi City
 Work Session Minutes
June 8, 2021
7:00 p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers

	•	Travis and Garrison Ludlow addressed the council about changing the zone on some property they have under contract.  They asked the council what type of development they would be interested in on the property.  The council said they had originally allowed annexation of the property under the pretense that it would be developed commercially.  The council indicated to the Ludlows that they were not interested in changing the zone for residential development at this time.  

	•	 Juab County Commissioner Richard Hansen and ex-commissioner Byron Woodland both addressed the council about their feelings on the county’s continued participation in the Central Utah Water Conservancy District.  

	•	Several residents of the Clothes Spin RV Park addressed the council about their desire to be able to continue residing at the park.  The RVs are in violation of the Nephi City Code that states they cannot stay longer than 30 days at a RV park. 

	•	Golf Director Michael Booth reported to the council that the 20+ year old golf course tractor needs major repairs that would cost approximately $11,000.  Mr. Booth asked for permission to purchase a new tractor costing approximately $35,000 instead of fixing the old one.  The council agreed to the tractor purchase out of the Capital Projects Fund. 

	•	The council discussed the water rights process and possibly contracting with Sunrise Engineering to help secure existing water rights at the state level and to ask for a change application on the seasonal nature of the city’s water rights with the Division of Water Rights.   

	•	Adjournment at 9:25 p.m.
