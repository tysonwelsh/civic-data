Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session
Thursday, April 27, 2023, Commissioner Heare called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Ken Heare, Jordy Guth, Roylan Croshaw, David Lewis, Eldon Peterson,
Sara Doutre

Commissioners Excused: Jessica Lucero

Staff Present: Russ Holley, Tanya Rice, Mike DeSimone, Darren Farar, Mohammed Abdullahi,
Amanda Pearce, Alex Oliphant, Paul Lindhardt, Holly Daines, Jeannie Simonds (City Council)

Agenda Approval/Consent Agenda: The Commission removed the Lion’s Den Daycare project
(PC 23-025) from the consent agenda to discuss some of the concerns that were brought up in the
public comments received on the project.

Commissioner Croshaw made a motion to approve the minutes from the April 13, 2023 meeting as
submitted. Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 23-024 Ebenezer Church -Requesting continuance to the May 11, 2023 meeting. [Design
Review & Conditional Use Permit] Deluxe Homes Corp/Mary Perez, authorized agent/owner is
requesting a design review and conditional use permit to construct a new 7,930 square-foot church
building that will connect to an existing home that is currently being used as a church, located on a
.51-acre lot at 1231 West 2200 South in the Commercial (COM) zone; TIN 03-005-0018 (Woodruff
Neighborhood).

MOTION: Commissioner Doutre motioned to continue PC 23-024 to the May 11, 2023 meeting.
Commissioner Guth seconded the motion.

Moved: S. Doutre Seconded: J. Guth Approved: 6-0
Yea: Heare, Guth, Croshaw, Lewis, Peterson, Doutre Nay: Abstain:

PC 23-023 City of Logan-Transportation Master Plan [Plan Review] Darren Farar/Logan City is
requesting the review and adoption of Logan City’s 2023 Transportation Master Plan.

Jeff Gilbert with the Cache Metropolitan Planning Organization (CMPO) reviewed the Cache County
Western Corridor Study that was completed in April 2023. The study can be viewed at the following
website: https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/de3c98cObfa54c808127769f82b828a7.

STAFF/PROPONENT: Darren Farar, Logan City Engineer reviewed the proposed Logan City
Transportation Master Plan and pointed out a few updates made to the plan since it was presented
at the last meeting.


PUBLIC: There were no public comments made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Peterson said that the Logan City Transportation Plan should match
the Cache County plan. Commissioner Doutre talked about knowing where the future U-Dot roads
are being planned for when the commission is approving future subdivisions where those roads are.
D. Farar said that the Logan Transportation Plan has to be a living document that can be updated
as needed based on the information received in the future. Commissioner Doutre asked if the 2023
West Corridor Pan could be added to Logan’s Transportation Plan. Paul Lindhardt, Public Works
Director said that language could be added recognizing the County corridor plans that are in
progress, and when those plans are completed, Logan’s Transportation will be updated as needed.

Commissioner Guth asked how this plan is integrated with the Logan City Bike and Pedestrian Plan.
D. Farar said that trails, pedestrian facilities, and bus stops have been identified and will be integrated
into this Transportation Plan.

MOTION: Commissioner Doutre moved to forward a recommendation of approval on the Logan
City Transportation Master Plan (PC 23-023) to the Municipal Council with the added language
discussed above. Commissioner Peterson seconded the motion.

Moved: S. Doutre Seconded: E. Peterson Approved: 6-0
Yea: Heare, Guth, Croshaw, Lewis, Peterson, Doutre Nay: Abstain:

PC 23-025 Lions Den Daycare [Conditional Use Permit] Siuana Pauni/Lopiseni Pauni, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a conditional use permit to operate an in-home daycare for up to 16
children at 1624 East 1140 North in the Suburban Neighborhood Residential (NR-4) zone; TIN 07-
026-0002 (Hillcrest Neighborhood).

STAFF: Tanya Rice, City Planner reviewed the request for a conditional use permit to operate an in-
home daycare for up to 16 children in the Hillcrest Neighborhood at 1624 East 1140 North. T. Rice
reviewed the site plan, floor plan, and hours of operation. There have been several comments
received from the neighbors stating they are not in favor of this daycare due to the previous daycare
that operated at this location and the problems it created with trash, poor upkeep of the home, parking
issues, and over occupancy. T. Rice said that there were code enforcement cases with the previous
tenant that have all been addressed and resolved with the homeowner.

Commissioner Peterson asked if condition number five should be updated to state that the two
parking stalls in the garage shall be maintained.

PROPONENT: Siuana Pauni said she has a set schedule that the children will follow, the pick-up
and drop-off of the children is staggered so there shouldn't be an issue with traffic. Roxanne Housley,
the homeowner said that her previous tenant that was operating the daycare was renting out rooms
that she was not aware of. The house had to be renovated since the previous tenant moved out
because it was not taken care of. Now that the owners are living in the home it will be better
maintained.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.


COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare said that the past business cannot affect their decision today,
and if the-same issues continue the neighbors can report them to the City so that the issues can be
addressed.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson moved to conditionally approve a conditional use permit for PC
23-025. Commissioner Lewis seconded the motion.

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
available in the Community Development Department.

2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a daycare for up to 16 children.

3. No outside activities permitted earlier than 7:00 am.

4. Drop-off and pick-up times shall be located on 1140 North or in the driveway and staggered so
that no more than three (3) client vehicles are at the home at any one time.

5. Two parking stalls shall be established and maintained in the garage of the home.

6. The proponent shall comply with all State and local regulations and licenses regarding in-home
daycare facilities for up to 16 clients.

7. Prior to issuance of the Business License, the Director of Community Development shall receive
a written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that their
requirements have been satisfied:

a. Fire —contact 716-9515
1. A fire inspection needs to be conducted for a daycare of up to 16 children.
b. Water/Cross Connection—contact 716-9627
1. Allpoints of use of water downstream of this property's water meter should meet Utah’s current
IPC plumbing code and amendments. This would help minimize the risk of contamination of
the water for home occupants and Logan City’s water system. This also would help minimize
liabilities to the homeowner.
c. Environmental —contact 716-9761
1. Additional residential cans can be provided upon request.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. This daycare is compatible with surrounding land uses and zoning designations and, as
conditioned, will not interfere with the use and enjoyment of adjoining properties.

2. As proposed, the streets providing access to the subject property has adequate capacity for the
proposed use and parking/drop-off/pick-up management will be provided and regulated.

3. Other infrastructure to the subject property has adequate capacity, or suitable levels of service,
for the proposed use.

4. The Conditional Use Permit conforms to the requirements of Title 17 of the Logan Municipal
Code as an identified conditional use.

5. The project was noticed as required by State and City regulations and proper procedure was
followed in processing this request.

Moved: E. Peterson Seconded: D. Lewis Approved: 6-0

Yea: Heare, Guth, Croshaw, Lewis, Peterson, Doutre Nay: Abstain:
