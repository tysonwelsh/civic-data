   4.1 Site Plan Amendment – Lot 5, Buildings C at Eastlake at Geneva
       Rachel Stevens presented a site plan amendment for Lot 5, Building C at Eastlake
       Geneva. Vineyard Properties of Utah requested approval of a site plan amendment for
       Lot 05 of Eastlake at Geneva. This amendment proposes removing architectural
       elements such as roof trims, awnings, endcaps, and concrete parapets and changing the
       color scheme. There were no further questions.
             MOTION: Craig Bown made a motion to approve the site plan amendment with the
