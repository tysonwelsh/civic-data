   3. ZONING REGULATION AMENDMENT (ZRA) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      Consider a request to amend portions of the city zoning ordinance, Title 10, and the Planned
      Development – Traditional Neighborhood Zone Design Manual and form-based code, to amend various
      setback and parking standards, and to make other clarifications to various chapters of Title 10 of the St.
      George City Code. The applicant is the City of St. George. Case No. 2022-ZRA-006 (Staff – Dan-Boles)

      Dan Boles presented the following:

      Dan Boles – This came as a result of the discussion we had with City Council in a work meeting that we
      had with them. There was some concern from this body that we have heard regarding parking. We took
      what we learned in that meeting with City Council, and this is the resulting ordinance. Really what you
      will see is that the City Council asked us to change the setbacks on alleys from 5 ft to 25 ft. to allow for
      parking of any size vehicle.

      Commissioner Anderson – Now the code says you can have the 5ft setback so now we are going to 25
      ft, so essentially we can’t have alleyways.

      Dan Boles – Dan went through the code changes listed in the packet per title and chapter of the City
      Code.

      Commissioner Kemp – So within each project there should be guest parking within 400 ft of every
      single door?

      Dan Boles – Yes.

      Commissioner Fisher – And for the 2 parking stalls per unit only 1 has to be within 200 ft.

      Dan Boles – Right.

      Commissioner Fisher – And the other stall has to be within 400 ft? Or is that just the guest parking?

      Dan Boles – That’s just the guest parking.
