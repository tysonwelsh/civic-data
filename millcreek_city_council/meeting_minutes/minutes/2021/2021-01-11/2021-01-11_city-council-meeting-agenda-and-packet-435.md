b. ZM-20-009,                Rezone      Request            from       R-1-10     to R-1-5        at 4396 S. Garden                 Drive;      Samah
    Safiullah,          Planner
Samah       Safiullah         said the applicant              was proposing            to rezone property              located       4396 South Garden
which      was currently            zoned      R-I-10.         The parcel         to the south of the property                  was zoned         as R-1-5.
The surrounding               properties        make-up            a mixture      of R-1-10,        R-2-10,        R-M,      and R-4-8.         The parcel
has a vacant           and boarded         up single-family                dwelling.        The applicant          has the intent            of subdividing
the parcel       into two narrow               lots and building             two small         side-by-side         single-family            homes,      subject
to a development                agreement        with       the City.      The development               agreement          would      place regulations
on the future           development            of the lot including               setbacks,      height,        slope of roof,       building         envelope,
lot coverage,           etc. She showed             the Council           a site plan which             incorporated         feedback          from     the
cornrnunity           council     with     setbacks          and lot coverage.             She said the rezone              proposal         does not
contradict       the Future         Land Use Map as described                          in the General           Plan. The property              is designated
as Neighborhood                 1, primarily        single-family           detached          homes,     1-2 stories.        She went          over the
development             agreement         considerations.              There was a neighborhood                     meeting         held on November
24, 2020 and a lot of residents                      were in support              of the rezone.         Safiullah         said one anonymous
public      comment           against     the proposal             was received.          Council       Member         Catten       asked about the
anonymous             comment.          Safiullah       said the cornrnent             did not state why they were opposed                            to the
rezone.      Lilly      said he received            comment            that expressed          concerns         about height         and density.


Safiullah       said the Millcreek               Community              Council      recommended                approval     of the rezone            with       a
zoning      condition          of a 30-foot         front     yard setback.          The Planning           Commission              recommended
approval       of the rezone            with    the recommended                   zoning      conditions.         Mayor      Silvestrini        asked if
adoption       of the rezone            should      be with         a zone condition             or the development                agreement.         Safiullah
said the development                 agreement.          Her analysis             was that a development                   agreement         needed      to be
developed        based on feedback                  from      the City,      the public,         and the Planning            Commission;              specific
design      standards         to prevent        a monotonously               designed         home will         be necessary,          especially        as the
homes       face the public            right-of-way            along Garden            Avenue.      If there was fiirther              concern        that
something            would      be done to negatively                  impact     the neighborhood,               staff would         recommend
imposing        a zone condition.               Staff did not believe               that this development                  would     bring     detrimental
harm or effects              to the surrounding              neighborhood            and properties             and the enforcement              of a
development             agreement         and the standards               of the Residential             Compatibility           Overlay        Zone would
help prevent           a detrimental           effect    to neighbors.            Safiullah      recornrnended             approval        of the rezone.
