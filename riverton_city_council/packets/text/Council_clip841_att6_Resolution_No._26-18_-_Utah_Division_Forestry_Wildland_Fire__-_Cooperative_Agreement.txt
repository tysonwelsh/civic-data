                                     RIVERTON, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-18

A RESOLUTION APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO A COOPERATIVE
 AGREEMENT WITH UTAH DIVISION OF FORESTRY, FIRE AND STATE LANDS

        WHEREAS, during a meeting of the Riverton City Council held on July 19, 2022, the
Riverton City Council heard a report from Unified Fire Authority Battalion Chief Watkins
detailing, among other things, the benefits of entering into a cooperative agreement with the Utah
Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has considered the terms of a Cooperative
Agreement with the Utah Division of Forestry, Fire, and State Lands, executed and renewed the
same upon expiration of prior agreements; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that mutual aid among
firefighting agencies is a crucial component for each participating agency to provide a sufficient
emergency response to a wildland fire event; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the agreement, if
executed, would require Riverton City to continue to maintain, revise when necessary, and
implement established wildland fire prevention, preparedness, and mitigation plans, which would
serve to protect the health, safety and welfare of the community; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that by entering into the
Cooperative Agreement, Riverton City can avail itself of aviation assets under the ownership of
the Utah Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands, which are highly effective in fighting
wildland fire; and

        WHEREAS, the Utah Interlocal Cooperative Act, Utah Code. § 11-13-101, et seq.
provides that two local governmental entities are authorized to enter into agreements with each
other, upon a resolution to do so by their respective governing bodies, to do what each agency is
authorized by law to perform; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code. § 65A-8-203, “The governing body of any
[municipality] may enter into a cooperative agreement with the division to receive financial and
wildfire management cooperation and assistance from the division;” and

        WHEREAS, all the Parties, as defined in the Cooperative Agreement attached to this
resolution are governmental entities with fire safety resources which would be of value for use in
response to a wildland fire; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton has determined that it is best interests of the inhabitants of
Riverton to enter into the Cooperative Agreement to provide for mutual aid- noting that the latest
version of the Cooperative Agreement contains a contract term of five years- a copy of which is
attached hereto.
   NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign a cooperative agreement to provide for mutual aid in
      substantially similar form to the copy attached to this Resolution, upon verification that
      said agreement is approved as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage and acceptance as
      provided herein.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 5th day of
