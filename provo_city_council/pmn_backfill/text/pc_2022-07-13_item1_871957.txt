Memo
Date: 7/14/2022
Re:   July 13th Planning Commission Hearing, Item #1


The following action was taken on July 13th for Item #1, A clarification of decision
for Item #1 of the June 22nd Planning Commission meeting (Planning Commission
review and classification of a sign per Section 14.38.110, Provo City Code, for an
apartment building in the CMU (Campus Mixed Use) zone, located at 1902 N Canyon
Road. Pleasant View Neighborhood. Aaron Ardmore (801) 852-6404
aardmore@provo.org)

Motion by Robert Knudsen:

1.    That Section 14.38.085, North University Avenue Riverbottoms Design
Corridor and Specialty Support Commercial (SSC) Sign Standards, be applied to the
sign permit.

2.    That the size of the sign be no larger than eighty-seven (87) square feet.

3.    That the brightness of the sign does not exceed the Nits of a street light.

Seconded by Melissa Kendall

Votes in Favor of the Motion: Robert Knudsen, Melissa Kendall, Lisa Jensen, Daniel
Gonzales



Approved 4:0
