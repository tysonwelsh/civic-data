     DISCUSSION – Utility Master Plan

Mr. Tschirki stated the Water, Sewer, and Storm Water Master Plans were approved by the Orem
City Council in 2016 after an 18-month development process that included engineering analyses
by Bowen, Collins & Associates, professional staff engagement, public involvement, and formal
presentations to the Public Works Advisory Commission and Orem City Council. These capital
facility plans were used by Orem's Financial Advisor, Lewis, Young, Robertson, and Burningham
to develop financial models and multiple supporting utility rate solutions. These updated master
plans will include additional analyses pertaining to State Street nodes, southwest density increases,
irrigation canal abandonment, and Vineyard City water disconnection. An overview and discussion
about guiding principles will take place.

SCHEDULES ITEMS

       PUBLIC HEARING
       RESOLUTION –Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Projected Use of Funds for
       CARES ACT CDBG-CV funding and approval of a substantial amendment to the 2019-2020
       Annual Action Plan

Mr. Kenneth Ransom, CDBG Advisory Commission Chairperson stated in response to the
Coronavirus Pandemic (COVID-19), the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development
Community Development Block Grant provided the City of Orem with a CDBG allocation of
$411,983 to be used to prevent, prepare for, and respond to COVID-19. Based on the
recommendations from the CDBG Advisory Commission, the City of Orem would like to allocate
$132,000 for COVID-related Senior Friendship Center improvements and approximately $5,000
to Community Action Services and Food Bank for COVID-related technology needs. The balance
of the funding of approximately $202,032.66 will be noticed and allocated at a future date.

Mayor Brunst opened the public hearing at 6:11 p.m.

Karen McCandless:
I want to express my appreciation to the CDBG Commission for its recommendation of
allocating $5,000 to Community Action Services and Food Bank to help with COVID-related
technologies. I also appreciate your consideration of its recommendation.
We at Community Action are seeing a significant increase in the number of community members
who need help with basic needs. Fortunately, Community Action has been able to remain open
to serve our community. We do not know what the future holds and there may be a time when
we would have to temporarily close.
We will be using the money to purchase hardware and software so our employees can continue
to serve our community remotely. While we cannot provide food pantry services remotely, we
can provide rent, utility, mortgage, and emergency shelter assistance remotely, provided we have
the necessary technology. The technology will not only be able to be used if we are closed but
can be used while we are open to minimize contact with the public.
Again, thank you for your consideration.
Hearing no further comments, Mayor Brunst closed the public hearing at 6:12 p.m. and brought
the discussion back to the City Council.

Mayor Brunst moved to approve by resolution Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)
Projected Use of Funds for CARES ACT CDBG-CV funding and substantial amendments to the
2019-2020 Annual Action Plan. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Richard
