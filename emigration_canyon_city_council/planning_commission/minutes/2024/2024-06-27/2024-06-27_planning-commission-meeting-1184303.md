LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
Meeting began at – 8:33 a.m.
WVR2024-001086 – Chaunceton Bird is requesting a slope waiver to build on a slope ~37%. Acres: 0.79.
Location: 4365 East Laneview Court. Zone: FR-1. Planner: Justin Smith (Motion/Voting)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the Staff Report.
Commissioners, staff, counsel and Mr. Bird had a brief discussion regarding septic drain field, functioning
slope, section cut and drawing, typo is revised to not detrimental on page three, second paragraph,
undeveloped lots in Emigration Place constraint whether slope or access. Adjacent lot off Laneview is
steeper. Health department approval doesn’t show dimensions. Site plan approval to occur as a condition
to approving the slope waiver.
