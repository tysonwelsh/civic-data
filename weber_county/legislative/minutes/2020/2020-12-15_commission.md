    11. RESOLUTION ADOPTING OPERATING & CAPITAL BUDGETS OF WEBER COUNTY FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2021 –
        RESOLUTION – 45-2020
         Scott Parke, County Comptroller, presented items G.10 and G.11 together. This budget’s public hearing was held
         on 11/24/2020 where the need to increase taxes for those who live on top of Powder Mountain to pay for snow
         removal were presented. He subsequently received a fair amount of telephone calls on the tax increase but there
         was no negative input. Snow removal for the lower valley is about $1,000/mile, $7,000/mile for Ogden Valley, and
         $50,000+/mile for Summit. Massive equipment is needed to handle the snow on Summit. This increase allows
         those residents to pay for their service and not be subsidized by the other unincorporated areas.
         The regular operating budget had also been presented at that time and a couple tweaks were made since then, which
         included adding one employee to the Center of Excellence for $85,000, adding $100,000 to Economic Development
         for land-related transactions, adding one employee to Culture Parks & Recreation Department for marketing
         demands, adding $140,000 for Wildwood bridge maintenance, $85,000 for an Ogden Valley sewer study. Mr.
         Parke stated that debt service was decreased by $1M in cash because the county refinanced so many bonds and thus
         saved the $1M. There was no Municipal Services Fund then.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 44-2020 adopting the Summit Road Overlay District property
         tax increase for calendar year 2021; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 45-2020 adopting the operating and capital budgets of Weber
         County for calendar year 2021 under protest because the county is paying for the Wildwood bridge maintenance
         out of the Municipal Services Fund due to a past commission’s decision at which time they committed the entire
         county and now it is being paid only by about 25,000 unincorporated taxpayers; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Froerer – aye

         $6.4M is being added to the budget related to paying off the 2012 and 2014 bonds, and various other adjustments,

    3. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER AND TAKE ACTION ON ZTA 2020-07, A REQUEST TO AMEND THE WEBER
       COUNTY LAND USE CODE TO ADD A HEIGHT LIMIT FOR WEEDS AND TURF GRASSES
         Charles Ewert, of the County Planning Division, noted that there was no current standard for these heights. Ogden
         Valley Planning Commission had expressed concern originally on how tightly this was worded regarding
         consideration of native vegetation. The language was amended slightly. Both Planning Commissions approved.
    4. A PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER & TAKE ACTION ON ZTA 2019-06, A REQUEST TO AMEND COUNTY LAND USE
       CODE TO CREATE STANDARDS FOR STORAGE UNITS IN COMMERCIAL ZONES & OTHER CLERICAL EDITS
       Charles Ewert, of the County Planning Division, stated that as staff reviewed the commercial chapters relating to
       storage units, they noticed that there are so many similarities between the chapters for commercial zones out west and
       the chapter for Ogden Valley, and the differences could become a point of interpretation conflict in the future. As
       commercial amendments were made to the Valley zones, the same amendments were not proportionately made to
       those out west. Both zoning types are basically put into one chapter but each maintains its appropriate zones. He
       outlined a couple of language adjustments made with the Attorney’s Office but had not made it to the Commission
       packet: definition of outdoor storage, not allowed in commercial zones and only in M-1 and M-3 Zones, and adding
       recreation or boat storage in the uses table. The commissioners thanked Mr. Ewert for his good work.
