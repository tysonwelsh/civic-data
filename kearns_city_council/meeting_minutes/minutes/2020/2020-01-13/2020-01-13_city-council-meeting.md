     railroad tracks – completed

Unified Police Department

                                             3
DATE          MONDAY                         JANUARY                                  13, 2020


              Chief Brian Lohrke updated the Council on the following:

    Thanked the council and community council for meals that were brought in for Christmas

    Offered condolences to Sandy City who recently lost its police chief

    Fourth quarter statistics have been sent out. The report is broken down so the specific
     services in Kearns are readily available.

    Reviewed the promotions within the Kearns precinct

    The precinct is fully staffed as of January 1, 2020

    Working on budget for the 2021 fiscal year. An additional traffic officer FTE is requested

    The mid-year budget was sent to the Mayor. UPD is currently 5 percent under its budget.

    At the Driving Under Influence (DUI) check point, which was done in December there were
     410 vehicles stopped, which resulted in four DUI arrests, one opened container case,
     three marijuana possession cases, two juvenile cases, and one documented gang
     member. It was very successful and productive.

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

              Battalion Chief Russell updated the Council on the following:

    Stats were submitted showing the number of dispatches from October through December.
     The stats showed that 86 percent of the time the Kearns station was able to handle the
     calls and that 20 percent of the calls were calls requesting help from the Kearns station.

    There have been no major fire incidents during the past year in Kearns. There have been
     small fires.

Unified Police Department (UPD) Weighted Voting

               Council Member Peterson updated the Council on the following:

UPD Board meeting

    Three new officers have been recruited

    Shop with a Shield - $49,000 was raised and was spent on 108 children and 38 families

    There will be eight hours of training in March

    Audit of the UPD came back clean

    Larry H. Miller donated $5,000 for the Victim Advocacy Program

    Discussion on budget clarity and weighed voting

    Herriman settlement and release agreement from the UPD was approved

             Mayor Bush stated she wanted it on record that all Council Members agreed that
one person one vote should be the voting mechanism for the UPD board.

SLVSA meeting

    Tax increase was approved

    2020 budget was approved

    The new Kearns precinct building will be discussed at the next meeting. He would like the
     metro township council and the community council to decide on the preferred location for
     this building.

Emergency Operations Plan

             Mayor Bush stated Mark Woodward has been hired to prepare the Kearns
Emergency Plan. As part of the plan he will identify any hazards or concerns within the Kearns
community and see if there are any grants the Council could apply for. Kearns will run mock
emergencies throughout 2020.
