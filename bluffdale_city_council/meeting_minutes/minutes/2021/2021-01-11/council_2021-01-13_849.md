9.     Training on Open and Public Meetings Laws. Staff Presenter, Todd Sheeran. (LBA
       and RDA Open and Public Meeting Laws Training Held in Conjunction).

Mayor Timothy reported that the required annual training will also apply to the LBA and RDA
portions of the meeting. Mr. Sheeran conducted the training that lakes place during the first City
Council Meeting of each year. He reported that the Open and Public Meeting Act specifies that
State agencies and political subdivisions exist to aid in the conduct of the people's business. The
intent is to keep discussions deliberately open and to ensure that deliberations occur in the open.
All open meetings are to open to the publ ic unless a meeting is specifically closed.

A meeting is defined as when a quorum of the public body is present. A meeting is not a chance
or social gathering and a quorum represents a maj ority of the body. Mr. Sheeran stressed the
importance of following each aspect of the rule. Any time there is a quorum and City policy is
discussed, it must have been noticed or the group should separate. Mr. Sheeran commented on
discussions that take place after a meeting has adjo urned.

With regard to group emails and onl ine co llaboration, Mr. Sheeran consulted with other attorneys.
One opinion was if it pe1tains to City policy, it needs to be in the public. If it pertains to more
administrative issues, it does not pertain to the legislative aspect. Issues regarding the use of
personal emails were discussed. Any email having to do with City business will be subj ect to the
Govermnent Records Access and Management Act ("GRAMA"). He recommended that any City
business take place through Ci ty-issued email addresses.

Closed Meetings were next discussed. M r. Sheeran identified specific reasons fo r going into a
Closed Meeting and activities that are not appropri ate in a Closed Meeting. A violation is a Class B
misdemeanor. To go into a Closed Meeting, a motion must be made and seconded with a roll call
vote. The reason fo r going into a Closed Meeting must also be given. The Chair must state who
is participating and ce1tain record s must be kept of Closed Meeti ng that can only be requested by
court order. A vote must be taken to reopen the meeting. No decisions can be made in a Closed
Meeting. News reports of specific incidents were discussed. Procedural issues were discussed.

Mr. Sheeran reported that a conflict of interest is defined in Utah Code as when compensation is
received. Jt is a vio lation if there is no w ri tten or oral disclosure. Specific examples were given.
Mr. Sheeran 's preference was for decis ion-makers to be neutral and have rigorous ethical
standards. lt was noted tbat the Council follows Robe1ts Ru les of Order.
