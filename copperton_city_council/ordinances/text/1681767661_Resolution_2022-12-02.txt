                      COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

     RESOLUTION NO. 2022-12-02                                       DATE: December 21, 2022

                       A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE
               COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S FINAL 2023 BUDGET

                                           RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, on November 16, 2022, Copperton adopted a tentative budget for the 2023
fiscal year; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton provided notice pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-3c-202 and 10-
6-113 concerning the tentative budget and of the date, time, and place of a public hearing on the
same; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton made the tentative budget available for public inspection for a
period of at least ten (10) days prior to the public hearing in accordance with Utah Code Ann. §§
10-3c-202 and 10-6-112; and

       WHEREAS, on December 21, 2022, Copperton held the public hearing on the tentative
budget in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-114.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

        1.      That the tentative budget adopted on November 16, 2022, and attached to this
resolution, is adopted as the final budget of Copperton for the 2023 fiscal year.

       2.      The Copperton budget officer is directed and authorized to file the final budget
with the Utah State Auditor within thirty (30) days of the date of this resolution pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-6-118.

       3.      This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption
