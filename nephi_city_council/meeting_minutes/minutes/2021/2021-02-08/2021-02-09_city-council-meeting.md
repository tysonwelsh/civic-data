                                         Meeting Minutes
                                       Work Session 2-9-21
                                              6:30 p.m.
                                 Canyon Hills Golf Course Clubhouse


1. The council reviewed a list of possible projects next year that could be prioritized for eligible funding
   though the Community Impact Board. City Administrator Seth Atkinson suggested five possible
   projects. The council agreed to direct staff, after some discussion, to list storm water system
   improvements as a project for possible funding through the CIB.

2.    Mr. Atkinson provided a Budget Options for Prioritization document for fiscal years 22-23. The
     council discussed several of the budget request by the different department heads and then each
     councilor prioritized the requests on their own document. Mr. Atkinson tabulated the council’s
     priorities and displayed the results. The council asked Mr. Atkinson to email them the results so that
     they could study further what projects and priorities they would like to include in the budget.

3. The council and city administrator gave various updates.

4. Councilor Ostler moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character and
   competence of an individual and real property. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The
   motion passed on a unanimous vote.

5. Council reconvened into work session and discussed some requests from individuals to build a
   slaughterhouse. The council discussed location, zoning, impact on utility services, and other issues
   associated with a slaughterhouse. The council directed staff to move forward with a in depth study
   of these issues.

6. Adjournment at 10:50 p.m.
