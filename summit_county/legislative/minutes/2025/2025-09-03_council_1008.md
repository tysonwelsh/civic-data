WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 3, 2025
Meeting also conducted via Zoom.
1.
Closed Session (3:02 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to enter closed session to discuss property acquisition. (3:02 PM). Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
1) Property acquisition (3:02 PM )
Council members Hanson, Harte, Armstrong, and McKenna, along with Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, Housing and Economic Development Director Jeff Jones, Community Development Director Peter Barnes and Executive Secretary Annette Singleton met in closed session to discuss property acquisition. (3:02 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to leave closed session to discuss property acquisition and enter closed session to discuss personnel. (3:33 PM). Megan McKenna seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
2) Personnel (3:33 PM)
Council members Hanson, Harte, Armstrong, and McKenna, along with Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas and Executive Secretary Annette Singleton met in closed session to discuss personnel. (3:33 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to leave closed session to discuss personnel and enter closed session to discuss litigation. (3:42 PM). Megan McKenna seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.

Megan McKenna made a motion to leave closed session to discuss litigation and enter open session. (4:12 PM). Roger Armstrong seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.

3.
Convene as the Board of Equalization (4:27 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to convene as the Board of Equalization. (4:27 PM). Megan McKenna seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
1)
Discussion and approval of Board of Equalization recommendations; Chase Black (4:27 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: BOE Adjustments Staff Report 2025.09.03.pdf
Auditor Cindy Keyes and Auditor Technician Jessika O’Brien presented recommendations by the Board of Equalization to Council. (4:27 PM)
Council members commented and asked questions. Auditor Keyes and Technician O’Brien responded. (4:29 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to approve the Board of Equalization's recommendations as contained in the packet. (4:30 PM). Megan McKenna seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
2)
Discussion and approval of Bridge21 Park City Application for Exemption of 501(c)3 organization; Chase Black (4:30 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: 501c3 Exemption Staff Report 2025.09.03.pdf
Auditor Technician, Jessika O’Brien and Auditor Cindy Keyes presented an application for Bridge21s property tax exemption to Council. (4:30 PM)
Council members commented and asked questions. Technician O’Brien, Auditor Keyes and Assessor Stephanie Poll responded. (4:32 PM)
Megan McKenna made a motion to approve the new application for a property tax exemption submitted by Bridge21 Park City. (4:34 PM). Roger Armstrong seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
Roger Armstrong made a motion to dismiss as the Board of Equalization and reconvene as the County Council. (4:34 PM). Megan McKenna seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.

Council Chair Hanson presented possible Trustee nominations to the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District Board. (5:05 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to approve the letter to Governor Spencer Cox contained in the packet concerning the nominations to serve as one of the trustees on the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District Board with Chris Robinson first, Andy Garland second, and Lisa Hoffman third. (5:06 PM). Megan McKenna seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.

Continued discussion and adoption of Ordinance No. 987-A, repealing Ordinance No. 987; David Thomas, Chief Deputy County Attorney (5:07 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page

Chief Deputy County Attorney David Thomas updated Council on the Court’s decision, finding the matter moot. Council members commented. (5:07 PM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to adopt Ordinance No. 987-A, repealing Ordinance No. 987 in the form provided in the packet. (5:14 PM). Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
