MSD Planning Staff Recommendation: Approve request for conditional use with conditions




                                      PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is requesting a Conditional Use for a Trucking Yard. The subject property is Lot 4 of Jack
Ass Flats and approximately 2.16 acres. The property is currently filled with discarded vehicles of all
sizes, including a school bus, cars, trucks, trailers, and construction equipment.


                          SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located off 2100 South in the Jack Ass Flats subdivision in Magna. The property is the
interior southern lot of the subdivision.


                                   LAND USE CONSIDERATIONS
The conditional use is compatible with other uses, as most properties are zoned or used for
manufacturing/industrial.
        Request: Conditional Use                                     File #: 31131


                                GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The current General Plan for Magna Metro Township was adopted September 2012. The property is
located on a mapped General Plan corridor which is planned for an area of “moderate change” and
encourages compatible development.


                          ISSUES OF CONCERN/PROPOSED MITIGATION
There are no issues in the conceptual review. As part of the development process, a technical review
will be performed providing the request is granted preliminary approval by the Planning Commission.


                                    NEIGHBORHOOD RESPONSE
Notice has been sent to property owners within 300’ of property. No neighborhood response received
as of the writing of this report.


                                REVIEWING AGENCIES RESPONSE
Agency reviewers approved the preliminary plan, with standard revisions and conditions to be met.



                                    PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS

The conditional use is compatible with the General Plan and goals of the Magna Community.



                             PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Planning Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the conditional use for a Construction Storage
Yard, with the following conditions:

   1. Approval is for contractor vehicles and equipment only. No stockpile of materials is allowed with this
      land use application.
   2. The proposed use and development shall not present a traffic hazard due to poor site design nor
      anticipated traffic increases on the adjacent road network, nor exceed the amounts prescribed in the
      County Transportation Master Plan.
   3. The proposed use and site development shall not pose a threat to the safety of persons who work or
      visit the property nor pose a threat to the safety of residents or properties in the vicinity by failing to
      adequately address: fire safety, geologic hazards, soil or slope conditions, liquefaction potential, site
      grading/topography, storm drainage/flood control, high ground water, environmental health
      hazards, or wetlands.
