                                     RIVERTON, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-28

      A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL ESTABLISHING
   A FORMAL PERIMETER BOUNDARY FOR A PUBLIC PARK KNOWN AS THE
                        RIVERTON MAIN PARK

        WHEREAS, Riverton is the owner and operator of the Riverton Main Park which includes
an array of features, including pavilions, playgrounds, a meeting hall, and sports courts; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton Main Park is used for recreation and for ingress and egress and
by stakeholders surrounding the Riverton Main Park; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council accordingly desires to define roadways
surrounding the Main Park for the purpose of establishing their use by the public for general ingress
and egress; and

       WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City has included the frontage of the
Riverton Main Park along 12600 South street, to lie within the 12600 South Community
Redevelopment Area (the “CRA”); and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council accordingly finds and determines the need to
declare the space located within the overlapping boundaries of the CRA and the Riverton Main
Park as open space for the purpose of making future plans for land use; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has reviewed the attached perimeter boundary map
and finds and determines that it correctly distinguishes property used for park purposes and
property used for open space, and for public ingress and egress; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council now desires to approve the attached perimeter
map;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1. Adoption. The Riverton City Council hereby approves and adopts the map for the Riverton
   Main Park, as more fully shown on the map attached hereto as exhibit A and incorporated
   herein by reference.

2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately.
