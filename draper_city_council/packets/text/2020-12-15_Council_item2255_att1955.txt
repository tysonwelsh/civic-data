                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-57

               A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE DRAPER CITY ACTIVE
                          TRANSPORTATION PLAN


        WHEREAS, an active transportation plan assesses the current conditions, performance,
needs, and public priorities to establish better options for residents to get around the community
safely via walking, biking, scooters, equestrian trails, and public transportation; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City has not adopted an active transportation plan; and

       WHEREAS, the Wasatch Front Regional Council (“WFRC”), in partnership with Sandy
City and Draper City, has provided the majority of funding to create the Draper City Active
Transportation Plan (“ATP”); and

       WHEREAS, the WFRC contracted with Parametrix to create the ATP; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City created a steering committee to guide the direction of the ATP;
and

       WHEREAS, Parametrix conducted interviews with stakeholders and user groups for input
on the ATP; and

       WHEREAS, the City had an open house at the Tree Lighting ceremony in 2019, and
Parametrix also provided a web-based survey and information portal that was available for a month
for public input and was linked through the City website and social media pages; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City desires to adopt the ATP which will provide the City with
additional outside funding sources for the projects specified in the ATP; and

       WHERAS, adopting the ATP is in the best interest of public health, safety, and welfare.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1.      Approval. The City Council hereby adopts the Draper City Active
Transportation Plan as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

        Section 2.     Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.
PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 15th DAY OF DECEMBER, 2020.
