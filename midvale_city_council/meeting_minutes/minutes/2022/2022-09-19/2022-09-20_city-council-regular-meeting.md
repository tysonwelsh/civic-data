Meggie Troili said the city proposes an amendment to the Residential Development &
Housing section of the General Plan 2016. The amendment includes 3 phases for a
moderate-income housing element, six moderate income housing strategies, and a five-
year implementation plan. This moderate-income housing element is designed to meet
current and future housing needs for those households whose incomes fall on or below
80% of the area median income (AMl). Midvale City requests this amendment to be in
compliance with Utah State Code 10-9a-403 and 17 -27 a-403. Public notice was sent to
affected entities as required in '17-3-9 of the Municipal Code. No comments were received
as of the writing of this report.
