                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-28

  A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE THE IDENTIFIED EQUIPMENT AS SURPLUS,
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF THIS ITEMS AS HE DEEMS
                         APPROPRIATE

       WHEREAS, the identified equipment has already been replaced and/or is no longer
needed; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no
longer needed as surplus in a public meeting.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares the following equipment as surplus, authorizing
          the City Manager to dispose of this item as he deems appropriate

          RS-7 - 2012 Ford F-550 Truck, Vin#1FDUF5HT6CEC12481

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
February, 2024 by the following vote:
