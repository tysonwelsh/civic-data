            Ordinance No. 2020-30

                      AN ORDINANCE AMENDING LAND MANAGEMENT CODE § 15-10-5
                      TO ALLOW THE ALTERNATE BOARD OF ADJUSTMENT MEMBER
                                   TO COUNT TOWARD A QUORUM

                  WHEREAS, the Land Management Code was adopted by the City Council of
            Park City, Utah, to promote the health, safety, and welfare of the residents, visitors, and
            property owners of Park City;

                   WHEREAS, the Land Management Code implements the goals, objectives, and
            policies of the Park City General Plan to maintain the quality of life and experiences for
            City residents and visitors;

                  WHEREAS, Land Management Code Chapter 15-10 establishes the Board of
            Adjustment as the City’s land use appeal and variance authority;

                  WHEREAS, the Mayor appoints five members and one non-voting alternate to
            the Board of Adjustment with the advice and consent of the City Council;

                     WHEREAS, at least three Board of Adjustment members form a quorum;

                   WHEREAS, the alternate may vote when a regular member is absent, but the
            alternate cannot count toward a quorum;

                  WHEREAS, the Board of Adjustment does not have regularly scheduled
            meetings and it can be difficult to establish a quorum to hold meetings for land use
            appeal and variance applicants;

                  WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of Park City, Utah, to amend Land
            Management Code § 15-10-5 to allow the alternate to count toward a quorum in order to
            ensure that meetings for appeals and variances may be timely scheduled;

                  WHEREAS, the Planning Commission duly noticed and conducted a public
            hearing on May 13, 2020, and forwarded a positive recommendation to City Council;

                  WHEREAS, the City Council duly noticed and conducted a public hearing on
            June 25, 2020.

                    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Park City, Utah,
            as follows:

                    SECTION 1. LAND MANAGEMENT CODE § 15-10-5, ORGANIZATION. The
            recitals above are incorporated herein as findings of fact. Section 15-10-5 of the Land
            Management Code of Park City is hereby amended as outlined in Exhibit A.
