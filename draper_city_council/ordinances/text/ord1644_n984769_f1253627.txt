City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1644

On April 1, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1644 amending the
official zoning map of draper city for approximately 0.86 acres of property from a5
(agricultural) to ra2 (single family residential) zone for the property located at
approximately 2020 E. Pioneer road within Draper City, otherwise known as the Ty
Wadswoth zoning map amendment The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper
City Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/.

Published this 2nd day of April 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
