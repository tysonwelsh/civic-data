#3 Public Hearing (5:40 p.m.): Mountain Heights Rezone 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the restriction of the current lots.  
 
05:44:00 
 
Sands motioned to open the public hearing; Olsen seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
Micah Copener represented the applicant and asked that if the rezone requirement and road concerns 
were addressed would the application meet the requirements for the A10 zone. 
 
Harrild responded that if the developer met the road requirements, the council will not extend 
maintenance for that road and sees that as the only challenge that the applicant would have. The council 
does have the authority to make an exception but there are qualifiers for that exception to be granted.  
 
7 September 2017                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 5 
Mr. Copener commented that there is road maintenance to the corner of the property and a private road 
would be extended beyond that. 
 
Sands responded that because the road is access to the forest service area that the road would not be able 
to be privatized. 
 
Mr. Copener responded that a new road would be created and they would not use the forest service road. 
 
Staff and Commissioners discussed what would be needed for services for the road and the subdivision. 
 
Mr. Copener responded that if the proposal could be approved subject to the requirement being met 
would be helpful. 
 
Harrild responded that rezones cannot have conditional requirements attached to them. 
 
Randy Stacy commented that there have never been bus pickup or garbage services for that road. 
Garbage is picked up at the Mendon City building. Winter Maintenance ends at our driveway. 
 
05:56:00 
 
Christensen motioned to close the public hearing; Sands seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
Sands motioned to recommend denial to the County Council for the Mountain Heights Rezone with the 
stated findings of facts and conclusions; Olsen seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
Mr. Copener asked what services would be extended. 
 
Harrild responded that the services that would be extended would be for maintenance and the county 
cannot currently meet the maintenance needs. 
 
Mr. Copener asked if 9 lots were added how that would affect maintenance. 
 
Harrild responded that maintenance changes regarding the type of road, and the type of road is 
determined by the number of trips on the road. 
 
06:00:00 
 
#4 Public Hearing (5:55 p.m.): Pate Rezone 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report.  
 
06:04:00 
 
Sands motioned to open the public hearing; Christensen seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
Robert Pate asked about under soil drains and if there will be issues with those from the County. The 
land has been farmed for years and the family would now like to have a couple more building lots. 
 
Kim Johnson commented regarding drainage, irrigation ditches and the ditches being used wrong. 
Density, septic and the drainage for the homes are all a concern.

Inez Carricle commented on water and drainage, stressed infrastructure, irrigation ditches, and roads. 
 
Brian Johnson commented on adding more wells to the area, and traffic. 
 
Wendy Coombs commented on changing the base zone of the area, future home values, and other 
neighbors being concerned with additional houses being built. Most of the families on the road are not in 
support of increasing the density and the traffic. 
 
Wendy Coombs also left a letter from a neighbor in opposition of the project. 
 
Ron Moss commented on not wanting a subdivision across the road, drainage and flooding and asked 
about the remaining acreage that would be left over from the 1 acre lots. 
 
Mr. Pate commented regarding surface water, and drainage. 
 
Mr. Johnson commented regarding surface water and flooding. 
 
06:21:00 
 
Olsen motioned to close the public hearing; Christensen seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the RU2 zoning & location requirements for the zone.  
 
Sands motioned to recommend approval of the Pate Rezone to the County Council based on the 
conclusions recommended by staff; Olsen seconded; Passed 3, 1 (Watterson voted nay)
