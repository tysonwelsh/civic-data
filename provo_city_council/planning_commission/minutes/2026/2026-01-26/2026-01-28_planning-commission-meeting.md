OVERVIEW
The Provo Missionary Training Center (MTC) has a long history with the city. The first structures
were built in 1976. The MTC is in the Training Facilities Zone (TF), which was established in 2013.

FINDINGS OF FACT
   •    The proposed project is in the Training Facilities (TF) Zone
   •    The gross square feet of the buildings being demolished is 208,735.
   •    The new buildings will have a total of 180,000 gross square feet.
   •    An additional 19,000 square feet of landscaping will be added.
   •    There are currently 555 parking spaces. That number will be reduced to 535.

ANALYSIS
The Provo Missionary Training Center (MTC) has been a longstanding institutional presence in the
City of Provo since the original buildings were constructed in 1976. The property is located within
the Training Facilities (TF) Zone, which was established in 2013, and the proposed redevelopment
projects consistent with this zoning designation.

The project proposes the demolition of two buildings totaling 208,735 gross square feet. These
structures will be replaced with new facilities totaling approximately 180,000 gross square feet,
resulting in an overall reduction in building area. The redevelopment also includes the addition of
approximately 19,000 square feet of new landscaping to enhance site aesthetics and functionality.

Parking on site will be slightly reduced from 555 existing spaces to 535 spaces. While we currently
don’t know the number of employees at the facility, Provo City Code 14.17C.130 (5) states “The
parking ratio may take into account parking available on land outside of the zone if utilized by uses
within the zone.” There is a meeting house for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints just
to the north and has a connection to the MTC parking lot.

The proposed building heights comply with Provo City Code requirements in the Training Facilities
Zone which limit building height to one-half (½) of the horizontal distance from the building to the
nearest property boundary. The new cafeteria will be approximately 53 feet in height, and the new
administration building will be approximately 87 feet in height.

CONCLUSIONS
Staff have reviewed the Project Plan Proposal, and would recommend approval subject to the
following conditions, that all CRC comments are corrected and satisfactory to the departments.
