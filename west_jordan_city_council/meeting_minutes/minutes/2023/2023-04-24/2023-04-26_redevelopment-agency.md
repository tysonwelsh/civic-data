              David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, Member Melissa Worthen

Worthen was participating via Zoom.

 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Resolution No. 239 adopting the Tentative Budget for the Redevelopment Agency of
          West Jordan City for Fiscal Year 2024 and setting May 10, 2023 as the date for a
          public hearing regarding the adoption of the final budget.
          Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck proposed the adoption of the
          Redevelopment Agency (RDA) budget. She explained that the plan was to hold a work
          session on May 9th and hold a public hearing on May 10th to consider the adoption of the
          final RDA budget and finally adopting the RDA budget on May 24th. The RDA activity is tax
          increment received for each of the areas and the sale of property that had not been
          completed. She noted a couple of studies and fees for paying administrative staff to
          manage the agency, participation and incentive agreements, and area improvements for
          a water tank and improvements on Redwood Road. It will receive $300,000 in transfer
          from general fund in the form of Municipal Energy Tax to repay an incentive for Aligned
          Energy/Paypal and $50,000 that comes out of the sales tax for an incentive for
          Sportsman’s Warehouse.

          MOTION:     Board Member Green moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 239 adopting the
                      Tentative Budget for the Redevelopment Agency of West Jordan City for Fiscal
                      Year 2024 and setting May 10, 2023 as the date for a public hearing regarding
                      the adoption of the final budget.
                      Vice Chairperson Bloom seconded the motion.

          The vote was recorded as follows:
