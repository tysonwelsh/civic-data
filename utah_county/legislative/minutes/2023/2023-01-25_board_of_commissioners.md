RICHARD NIELSON – STATED THAT ALSO IN ADDITION TO WHAT THE

REED DIXON, VIVIAN PARK HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION – STATED HE
REPRESENTS OVER 300 PEOPLE WHO LIVE OR USE HOMES IN VIVIAN PARK AND
IS VERY APPRECIATIVE OF THE COUNTY LISTENING TO THE HOA’S CONCERNS
AND SUGGESTIONS. COMMENTED THAT THERE ARE SOME CONCERNS WITH
ASPHALT CAUSING LESS PARKING, SECTION OF RAILROAD TRACK BY RESTROOMS
THAT IS GOING TO BE REMOVED, PRESERVING OF AS MANY BIRCH TREES AS
POSSIBLE, SMALL PARK ON OTHER SIDE OF BRIDGE STAYING IN TACT, AND FEELS
THIS PLAN IS TOO MUCH – IT IS BEAUTIFUL AS IS SO DOESN’T NEED THIS MUCH
ENHANCEMENT.
