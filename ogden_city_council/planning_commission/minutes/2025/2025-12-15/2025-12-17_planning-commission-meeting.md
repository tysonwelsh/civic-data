1. Plan Ogden Discussion
Senior Planner Brandon Rypien presented the Sign Chapter found in the Development
Standards. Discussion was had as they touched on the Sign Chapter Organization topics:
-Purpose
-Applicability
~Procedures
-General Provisions
-Minor Signs
-Nonpermanent Signs
-Portable Signs
-Temporary Event Signs
-Signs allowed in Residential, Open space, and Community Facility Zones
~Signs allowed in Downtown Residential Community Campus and Mixed-Use Zones
-Billboards
-Signs for ‘The Junction” Development
-Spotlights and Searchlights
