04/24/2026 @1030, Side by Side Accident, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 32 (Non
Certified) 2 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Sand Mountain on a report of a female patient who had
reportedly accidentally driven off a cliff. She suffered a suspected spinal injury. Life Flight was
requested due to the nature of injury but they were unavailable. SAR members responded to the
location of the patient and she was stabilized on a backboard. She was then loaded in a stokes basket
and transported via side by side back to Incident Command. The patient was then loaded in the
ambulance and transported to the hospital.
04/26/2026 @1651, Injured Hiker, Welcome Springs Canyon — Man Hours — 67.5 (Non
Certified) 11 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Welcome Springs Canyon area near Lytle Ranch Rd on
a report of a female who had fallen into a hole approximately 10-15 feet deep. The patients injuries
were reported as a punctured lung and possible broken ribs. Due to the possible severe injuries Mercy
Air was requested to respond. SAR members responded to the location of the patient and she was
stabilized. Mercy Air was unable to land near the patient due to the terrain so they returned and landed
at Incident Command (IC). Due to Mercy Air being unable to land anywhere near the patient, STAR9
was requested to assist with hoisting the patient. SAR team members utilized a ropes system to get the
patient down the mountain to a better location for STAR9 to hoist from. STAR9 hoisted the patient
back to IC. She was then loaded in Mercy Air and transported to the hospital.

05/10/2026 @ 1525, Lost Hikers, Red Mountain, Red Cliffs Desert Reserve — Man Hours —
40 (Non Certified) 4 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Red Mountain on a report of a 53 year old male and a
44 year old female who were lost on top of Red Mountain. The male was experiencing what were
likely severe heat related symptoms. Due to the males condition and the remote area, deputies
requested that Life Flight respond to assist with quickly retrieving the male. Incident Command was
established at the Toe Trailhead in Ivins and SAR members began to respond to the patient location.
Life Flight located the hikers and were able to land nearby. The male was loaded in the helicopter and
returned to Incident Command and a waiting ambulance. SAR members located the female lost
individual and escorted her back down the mountain to Incident Command. Both individuals declined
medical transport.
