WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
JUNE 4, 2020

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Acting Chairman Gil Almquist at 9:35 a.m. on June 4, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Dean Cox, Deputy County
Attorney Brian Graf, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen,
and Deputy Clerk Rikki Almaraz.

Commissioner Iverson was excused from the meeting.
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Ed Bowler

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION _NO._R-2020-2614, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING TOURISM ADVISORY BOARD RECOMMENDATIONS

Washington County Tourism Director Kevin Lewis presented the recommendation brought
forth from the Tourism Tax Advisory Board (TAB), explaining that the Utah State Rodeo
Association is seeking funding to be used to supplement scholarships given out at the Utah
State High School Rodeo Finals. This event will be held at the Washington County Legacy
Park for the first time in forty-six (46) years.

Jade McAllister expressed appreciation for all the efforts made to be able to hold the event
in Washington County and stated that the projected economic impact of the event is
approximately $2 million dollars.

Mayor Brandon Humphries expressed appreciation for the support that they have received
in order to hold the state finals locally.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2614, A
Resolution Approving Tourism Advisory Board Recommendations.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at

Orv LMeiae—

CHEYRNNE BENTLEY GIL ALMQUIST

DEPUTY CLERK ACTING CHAIRMAN
