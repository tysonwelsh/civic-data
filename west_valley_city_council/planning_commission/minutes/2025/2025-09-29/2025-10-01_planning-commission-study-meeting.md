     MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING
                             OCTOBER 1, 2025

THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON
WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 1, 2025 AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600
CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED
TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY CHAIR MATHEW LOVATO.
THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:
     Mathew Lovato, Chair
     Martell Winters, Vice Chair
     Darrick Porter, Commissioner
     Nancy Drozdek, Commissioner
     Rob Durfee, Commissioner

PLANNING DIVISION STAFF PRESENT:
     Steve Pastorik, Community Development Director

PRESENTATIONS:

GP-6-2025 Petition by West Valley City requesting an amendment to the General Plan to add a Water
Element. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. The City is proposing a new water element in its General Plan,
required by state legislation to address water use and preservation. The plan focuses on coordinating
between multiple water providers within West Valley City, reducing water demand through efficient
landscaping and development practices, and improving municipal operations to eliminate water waste.
While the Plan addresses some new practices like reduced live plant material requirements, most
changes will build on existing practices, with the potential for more significant adjustments over time as
water conservation efforts become more aggressive.

GPZ-4-2025 Petition by Eloy Santana Perez requesting a General Plan change from Rural Residential
to Light Manufacturing and a zone change from A-1 (Agriculture, minimum lot size 1 acre) to M
(Manufacturing). The property is located at 2720 S 6750 W on 1.09 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented an application requesting a rezone from Agricultural to
Manufacturing use to accommodate an existing trucking business on the property. The property also
includes the applicant’s house and a detached structure. . Steve presented concerns for the rezone includ
the property's proximity to residential and recreational areas, existing zoning restrictions, and multiple
code enforcement violations. If the rezoning is approved, there would need to be improvements such as
installing 20’ of landscaping and a masonry wall along the south, east, and west sides and paving truck
parking areas. A zone change to Manufacturing zoning would make the home on the property a
nonconforming use. If the residential use of the home were to cease for a year or more, the home could
no longer be used for residential purposes. Steve proposed a development agreement that would require
the property owner to apply for a conditional use permit within a specified timeframe, with the option
that the site be cleared if the owner doesn’t apply by the deadline.

The Commission expressed concern about setting a rezone precedent for other non-compliant properties
in the area. They discussed the possibility of declining the zoning request which would force the
business to find another location. The conversation highlighted the challenges of balancing individual
property rights with City regulations, particularly in areas with a community use.

C-17-2025 Petition by Alec Shook requesting conditional use approval for a public utility installation
for Enbridge Gas Utah. The property is located at 3515 S 5700 W on 0.74 acres and is zoned R-1-8
(Single Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-
ut.gov)

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented a conditional use application by Enbridge Gas for a public utility
installation at 3515 South 5700 West. Brock presented the site plan which includes an inline inspection
station with above-ground piping and a 20-foot landscape buffer. The site will be gravel with some
above-ground piping, and an 8-foot precast concrete wall with gates for access. The landscape plan
includes 50% lawn coverage along 3500 South, which is a high-image arterial street, and mulch with
plants in other areas.

Commissioner Durfee asked about the status of an existing cinder block wall on the property line
between this lot and the new Take 5 property, since Enbridge plans to install a concrete wall right next
to it. He also asked who would maintain the site's lawn. Brock agreed to follow up on these issues and
provide clarification at the Public Hearing.

There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting was
adjourned at 4:40 PM.
