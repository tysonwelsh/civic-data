22. ADOPT AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND CHAPTER 12 OF THE UTAH COUNTY LAND
USE ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE EXCEPTIONS TO HEIGHT LIMITATIONS FOR CERTAIN
USES THAT ARE PRESENTLY ALLOWED IN SPECIFIED ZONES.

-Bryce Armstrong, Community Development

(Timestamp: 1:07:48)

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER - STATED THAT SHE HAD A COUPLE OF
CONSTITUENTS FROM THE COUNTY EXPRESS THEIR CONCERN THAT THIS
PARTICULAR ORDINANCE CHANGE WOULD BE TAKEN ADVANTAGE OF BY SOME
OF THE MINING OPERATIONS ON COUNTY LAND - THAT THEY WOULD BUILD
SILOS OVER 40’ TALL THAT WOULD CREATE AN EYESORE.

BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATE DIRECTOR -
STATED THAT THIS IS RELATED TO SPECIFIC HEIGHTS OF USES THAT ARE
ALREADY PERMITTED SO THIS IS NOT CREATING NEW USES THAT DIDN’T
ALREADY EXIST. RIGHT NOW IN THE MINING AND GRAZING IN THE INDUSTRIAL
ZONE, WE ALLOW BATCH PLANTS THAT ARE USUALLY ASSOCIATED WITH
MINING OPERATIONS, BY PERMITTED USE (MEANING THEY DON’T HAVE TO DO
TO A CONDITIONAL USE PUBLIC MEETING PROCESS), THEY CAN HAVE SILOS UP
TO 40’ WITH OUT A CONDITIONAL USE. THEY CAN GO ABOVE THAT WITH PUBLIC
MEETINGS. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT TRIES TO IDENTIFY THOSE HEIGHTS
THAT SEEM TO BE CONSISTENT WITH EITHER INDUSTRY STANDARDS OR WHAT
WE'RE REGULARLY SEEING AS REQUESTED CONDITIONAL USE.

COMMISSIONERS POWERS GARDNER: MOTION TO APPROVE
COMMISSIONER LEE: SECOND
AYE: ALL IN FAVOR
