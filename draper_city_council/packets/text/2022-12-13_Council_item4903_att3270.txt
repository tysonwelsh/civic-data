BACKGROUND:

       DE needs to provide additional natural gas capacity in the Suncrest area.
       DE will be tapping into their existing line near the Peak View trail head to install a medium
        capacity post-style station within the existing pipeline ROW.
       From this station an 8” plastic IHP line will be buried and run adjacent to the length of the
        roadway into the Peak View trail head, which falls within the Corner Canyon Easement.
       DE seeks a ROW and Easement from their existing supply pipeline ROW adjacent to the
        Peak View trail head access road.
