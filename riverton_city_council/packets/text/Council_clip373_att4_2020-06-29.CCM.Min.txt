29      3.a     Resolution No. 20-46 – A Resolution Approving and Adopting a Litigation
30              Settlement Agreement between Riverton City and the Defendant in a Civil
31              Action Filed by Riverton City and Herriman City Against the Unified Police
32              Department in the Third Judicial District Court, State of Utah, Case No.
33              190904012
34
35   Ryan Carter, City Attorney, explained the terms of the settlement agreement with UPD,
36   including a $150,000 cash settlement, a Faro Laser, and one Harley Davidson motorcycle. He
37   said that Riverton City maintained that there was no modified debt owed as a result of the years
38   UPD Officers served in Riverton. He also explained that the main ongoing discussion with UPD
39   had been regarding URS and future retirement funds.
40
41   Council Member Stewart said that UFA went through a similar exercise as they were preparing
42   a new interlocal agreement.
43
44   Mr. Carter stated that Riverton City had thoroughly considered the settlement agreement in light
45   of the continued expense of ongoing litigation against an entity with a questionable future.
46
47   Following brief discussion, Council Member Stewart MOVED the Riverton City Council
48   approve Resolution No. 20-46 approving and adopting a litigation settlement agreement
49   between Riverton City and the defendant in a civil action filed by Riverton City and
50   Herriman City against the Unified Police Department in the Third Judicial District Court,
51   State of Utah, Case No. 190904012. Council Member McDougal SECONDED the motion.
