REQUEST
Brennan Alldredge, Architect, of BA Architects, and owner Jorge Aleman, are requesting preliminary
commercial Site Plan Review for the proposed conversion of and addition to the former Scone Cutter
Restaurant Building and site, located in the Regional Commercial (RC) zoning district.  The 0.51-acre site
is located at 225 W. 9000 South St. Building architectural design review approval is also required.
The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for these requested actions.


BACKGROUND
The subject property has been used as a restaurant since 1977.  The initial restaurant did not include a
drive-up window.  City permitting records are somewhat incomplete during the interim timeframes.
Sometime in the past, a drive-up window was added on the east side of the building, and the restaurant
was licensed under the Scone Cutter brand since at least January 2012.  There is no residential zoning in
the area of this business.  The (RC) zoning exists on both sides of 9000 South St. east of the freeway up to
State St.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify them
of the Planning Commission meeting.  A neighborhood meeting was not held for this project, due to
the lack of residential zoning in the area.

Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation.  All street improvements and utilities are
existing from the prior site development.  There is an existing public sidewalk along the frontage of this
property.  The driveway access from 9000 South St. is existing and shared with the other developments of
the shopping center and driveway cross access agreements exist.  Vehicle access is available from all sides
of this property and will not change with this development.  The new owner is replacing the existing drive-
up window queuing lane with concrete to help define that area on the site plan and to improve the surface
storm water drainage.  New accessible vehicle parking and an accessible route for pedestrians is being
added to the northwest side of the building.  On-site vehicle and pedestrian access, and on-site circulation
is being improved and will be adequate for this development

Zoning Review.  The project will meet all of the requirements of the(RC) zoning district and the Sandy
City Development code.  Parking for this project will meet the City requirements on site and also the
site has access to shared parking agreements with the abutting shopping center development.  The
north end of the existing building is currently finished with an angled "Mansard" roof, which is backlit.
The owner desires to keep this feature and to place business identification signage on this surface as
shown on the building elevation drawing.  Staff has discussed this proposal with the owner's architect
to explain that roof signage is not allowed anywhere in Sandy City.  And since a mansard roof is a roof,
it therefore will not be possible to be a background for a sign here.

All required City Departments and Divisions have reviewed and recommend approval, subject to their
usual requirements and conditions.

Architectural Design Review.  The building's design, materials and colors were reviewed by the
Sandy Planning Staff as to compliance with the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards.  This project
includes a storage addition to the southwest side of the building and the raising of an existing screen
wall on the southeast side of the buidling to address screening the existing ground level mechanical
unit.   The existing brick walls and the new addition walls will be painted to match the the new color
scheme for the business.  A metal roof screen wall will be added to enclose the existing and proposed
roof top units and vents.

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the building architectural design, materials
and colors as now proposed by the applicant.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is complete
for the proposed Betos Restaurant modified site plan and building additions project, and approve the
building's architectural design, materials and colors as proposed, based upon the following two
findings and the following six conditions:

FINDINGS.
      A. That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements of the Sandy
         City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as to building
         architectural design, materials and colors.
      B. That the renovation, beautification, and reuse of this existing commercial site will improve
         the City's gateway entry along 9000 South St.

CONDITIONS.
   1. That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior to the
      start of any construction.  The final site plan shall comply with all Development Code
      requirements and any modifications required or allowed by the Planning Commission.
   2. That the development will comply with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes
      applicable to this type of use.
