            15.      CONSIDER VIVIAN PARK HOMEOWNERS ASSOCIATION’S PETITION TO VACATE THE
                     PUBLIC RIGHTS-OF-WAY IN THE VIVIAN PARK SUBDIVISION IDENTIFIED AS “FAIRFAX
                     DRIVE” AND “VIVIAN WAY”; TO CONSIDER VACATING ALL OTHER PUBLIC RIGHTS-OF-
                     WAY IN THE VIVIAN PARK SUBDIVISION; TO CONSIDER SETTING A PUBLIC HEARING TO
                     CONSIDER THE PETITION TO VACATE THE PUBLIC RIGHTS-OF-WAY IN THE VIVIAN PARK
                     SUBDIVISION, PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE § 17-27A-609.5; AND TO CONSIDER ADOPTING AN
                     ORDINANCE VACATING THE PUBLIC RIGHTS-OF-WAY IN THE VIVIAN PARK SUBDIVISION.
                    - Bill Lee, Utah County Commissioner -

            REED DIXON, VIVIAN PARK HOMEOWNER’S ASSOCIATION – (had slide presentation) THERE ARE
            APPROXIMATELY 75 HOMEOWNERS IN VIVIAN PARK. PART OF VIVIAN PARK WAS PLATTED
            IN 1911 AND PART IN 1917. FAIRFAX IS THE MAIN ROAD. VIVIAN WAY IS ALSO A RIGHT-OF-
            WAY. THE HOA WOULD LIKE IT VACATED BY THE COUNTY AND MADE PRIVATE. THE HOA
            WOULD THEN TAKE OVER CONTROL OF THE TWO ROADS AND WOULD DO SNOW REMOVAL,
            ASPHALT MAINTENANCE, ETC. THE ROADS PRIMARILY FUNCTION AS PRIVATE.

            (showed pictures of road). ROAD WAS LAST OVERLAID IN 1999. REQUESTING TO HAVE A
            HEARING REQUESTING THE COUNTY TO REPAIR ROADS TO GOOD CONDITION WITH
            OVERLAY AND/OR FULL-DEPT RECONSTRUCTION AND OTHER REPAIRS, THEN THE HOA
            WOULD TAKE OVER AND ASSUME CONTROL OF THE ROAD.

            RICHARD NIELSON, UTAH COUNTY PUBLIC WORKS – LOOKED AT THE REQUEST FROM A
            COST STANDPOINT OF WHAT IT WOULD TAKE TO DO AN OVERLAY. DID NOT LOOK AT FULL-
            DEPTH AREAS. FROM MAINTENANCE STANDPOINT, THE ROAD IS DIFFICULT BECAUSE OF
            THE SIZE OF THE COUNTY EQUIPMENT TO GO UP THE ROAD. WOULD BE IN FAVOR OF
            LOOKING AT THIS PROPOSAL. THERE IS ONE MORE ROAD ON THE 1917 PLAT, BEVERLY
            DRIVE, THAT HE SUGGESTED ALSO BE CONSIDERED. HAS NEVER BEEN BUILT, BUT IS ON THE
            PLAT.

            ROBERT MOORE, UTAH COUNTY ATTORNEY’S OFFICE – THIS ROAD HAS IS A POTENTIAL
            ACCESS POINT BETWEEN OUR TRAIL SYSTEM UP TO SOUTH FORK TRAIL, WHERE
            PROPERTIES HAVE STARTED TO BE ACQUIRED.
