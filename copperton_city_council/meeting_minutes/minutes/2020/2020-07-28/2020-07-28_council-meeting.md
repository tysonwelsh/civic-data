Council Working Session

Covid-19 Action Plan

                 Mayor Clayton stated the Council adopted a Covid-19 Action Plan on March 13,
2020. That plan is quite harsh. Copperton now has cases of Covid-19 in the community and that
will likely continue. The Council has been sent links to action plans of other communities. He
asked if Council Members wanted to adopt a new plan more in line with what other communities
are doing. Covid cases should not be treated differently regardless of their number.

              Council Member Severson stated he agreed with that. Guidelines should be
provided and then let people use their own judgement.

              Council Member Bailey stated the Council should educate instead of regulating.

              Mayor Clayton stated he has put some Covid information on the Copperton
website. He suggested the website include links to the school district, fire department, County
website, and health and mental health resources.

             Council Member Bailey stated if Mayor Clayton put some information together,
she would send it out.

              Mayor Clayton asked if there was something the Council needs to get approved
at the next meeting.

             Nathan Bracken stated the Council should think about how it will coordinate its
response and share information. They should be tracking the County dashboard about the
confirmed cases in Copperton.    The Council should consider who would coordinate,
communicate, and make assignments.

                Mayor Clayton stated those assignments can be made at the next meeting. The
health department tracks positive cases and does contact tracing. He did not feel it was the
responsibility of Council Members to contact people.

              Mr. Bracken stated the Council does not need to track the people and do the
health department’s job. It should have a chain of command, so the health department knows
who to contact and who is the backup.

                Mayor Clayton stated he did not want the Council to become the Covid police.
During the next meeting they will discuss points of contact, assignments, and who will take what
task. That will become Copperton’s Action Plan.

                Council Member Pazell stated that is appropriate. He is dealing with an outbreak
among his staff and their families. He felt there is nothing they can do as a government or Council
that can stop it.

              Mayor Clayton stated the Council can educate people so they can do what is best
for them. He asked Mr. Bracken what measures he has seen taken in other communities.

                Mr. Bracken stated the Council should coordinate with other entities and have a
clear chain of command in terms of response and who does what.

               Mayor Clayton stated for next month’s meeting, Council Members should think
about what they are willing and able to do. Copperton’s Facebook page should contain the same
information as the township website. There should be links or information for mental health
issues, alcohol problems, child abuse, social isolation, children staying home, etc. He asked that
ideas for good resources be shared with him or Council Member Bailey.
