                          RESOLUTION NO. 24-01

A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING AN
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY
AND LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES IN SALT LAKE COUNTY
REGARDING THE UTAH LAW ENFORCEMENT INFORMATION SHARING
ANALYTIC PROGRAM

       WHEREAS, the purpose of this Agreement (“Agreement”) is to define
the roles and responsibilities governing access to and disclosure of data
shared with, stored, or accessed through the Utah Law Enforcement
Information Sharing Analytic Program (“ULEISA”) and the program’s
designated software applications; and

       WHEREAS, this Agreement governs how law enforcement agencies in
Salt Lake County, including the Draper City Police Department, (“Agencies”)
share the Agency’s Data; the types of data that may be shared; how DPS may
use the Agencies’ Data, and the action that must be taken if the Agency
makes changes to the RMS or choose to limit the data they share; and

      WHEREAS, this Agreement also defines the roles and responsibilities
governing access to and disclosure of ULEISA Data; and

       WHEREAS, the Utah Department of Public Safety (“DPS”) is responsible
for the operation and maintenance of the ULEISA software and database;
and

      WHEREAS, this Agreement will be beneficial to the health, safety and
welfare of Draper City’s businesses and residents; and

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Approval of Agreement. The Date Sharing Agreement for
the Utah Law Enforcement Information Sharing Analytic Program, attached
hereto as Exhibit 1, is hereby approved and the Mayor is directed to sign the
same.
      Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this
Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or
unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and all
sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 9th DAY OF J, 2024.
