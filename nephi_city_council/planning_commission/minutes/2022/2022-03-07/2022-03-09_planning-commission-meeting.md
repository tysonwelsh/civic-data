NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                                     MEETING MINUTES
                                       March 9, 2022
                                         21 E. 100 N.
                                      Nephi, UT 84648
                                          7:00 p.m.
Planning Commission Chairman Ann Peterson called the meeting to order and welcomed all in
attendance.

PRAYER: Commissioner Karl Brough

PUBLIC HEARING JOSIE BELL HAIR SALON:
    Chairman Peterson opened the public hearing to receive comment on a home occupation business
license for Josie Bell Hair Salon located at 312 East 1100 South. There was no public comment given.

PUBLIC HEARING-REZONE PRIORITY HOMES, LLC & DOUBLE COUSINS INVESTMENTS PROPERTY
APPROVED:

    Chairman Peterson opened the public hearing to receive comment on a zone change request for the
Loveless Estates Subdivision. City Planner Shauna Talbot explained the zone request is a change from a
R2-Z to R2-PUd overlay. The PUD overlay zone would allow for the number of units and type of units
being proposed by the developers, Priority Homes, LLC, and Double Cousins Investments. The property
is located approximately 800 N to 900 N and 300 W. There was no public comment given.

LOVELESS ESTATES SUBDIVISION TRAFFIC STUDY NOT REQUIRED, ZONE CHANGE AND PRELIMINARY
PLAT APPROVED:

   Ms. Talbot reviewed the preliminary plat, and the Development Review Committee notes for the
Loveless Estates Subdivision with the commission. The commission discussed concerns about the
current placement of some power poles and parking requirements. The commission discussed whether
to require the developers to provide a traffic study. Brett Ludlow representing Priority Homes said that
a traffic study would take much more time and money. The development shows both an ingress and
egress.
   Commissioner Heap moved to not require a traffic study for the Loveless Estates Subdivision.
Commissioner Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
   Commissioner Fran Petersen moved to approve the zone change recommendation for Loveless
Estates Subdivision from R2-Z to a R2-PUD overlay. Commissioner Thomson seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.
   Commissioner Thomson moved to approve the preliminary plat for Loveless Estates Subdivision with
the suggestions from the DRC notes. Commissioner Hancock seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC HEARING FOR ZONE CHANGE REQUEST FOR WRIGHT DIRECTION LLC PROPERTY:

   Chairman Peterson opened the public hearing to receive comment on a zone change request for the
Bar W Subdivision property located at approximately 1111W Hwy 132 from HC to ID-2 and a portion of
the parcel to the ID zone.
   Dale Jackson stated that he is in favor of a slaughterhouse being built on the property because it is
very difficult and takes several months to get your cattle into a packing house, and there is a big
shortage of packing houses, and they overcharge for their services.
   Jeremy Lynn who is one of the developers of the property stated that the property was zoned as
Highway Commercial when the property was annexed and not ID-2, which zone allows for
slaughterhouses, because there needed to be a study done to analyze if the city’s sewer system could
handle the wastewater from a slaughterhouse. Now that the study has been completed and the study
indicated that the sewer system could handle the slaughterhouse, a zone change is needed for the
development.
  Travis Worwood another representative of the slaughterhouse developments stated that Sunrise
Engineering had given target BOD levels that needed to be met and their development plan hit the
targets and many components of the plant will clean the wastewater before it hits the sewer lagoons.
Worwood stated that there will also be a shut-off valve installed that would make it possible to shut off
the wastewater going out of the plant if the levels were not within guidelines. Mr. Worwood stated that
these provisions will be in a development agreement between Wright Direction LLC and Nephi City.
   Mr. Lynn showed the commission a set of plans of the facility and indicated that no more than 40
head a day will be killed. He explained the kill process and how the waste will be eliminated. A
discussion ensued about the process and designs for the facility. Mr. Worwood stated that they
wouldn’t be operational for another 18 months.
   Vicky Jackson stated that it is very important with the current economic conditions of the world that
residents become self-sufficient.

BAR W SUBDIVISION LOT 1 ZONE CHANGE APPROVED:

   Commissioner Brough moved to change the zone on the Bar W Subdivision Lot 1 from HC to ID-2.
Commissioner Thomson seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BAR W SUBDIVISION LOT 2 ZONE CHANGE TABLED:

   The developers or the Bar W Subdivision had requested a zone change from HC to ID for lot 2. After
some discussion, the commission concluded that because there were not clear development plans, they
would not approve the zone change. Jeremy Lynn and Travis Worwood were concerned about having to
put sidewalks in if the zone remained HC. The commission decided to table the issue for consideration
at a later date.

BAR W SUBDIVISION VICINITY CONCEPT PLAT APPROVED:

   Ms. Talbot reviewed with the commissioners that the Bar W Subdivision would need to complete the
state’s agricultural division process prior to recording the subdivision. She stated that the parcels along
the east side will qualify for this division. Commissioner Thomson moved to approve the vicinity
concept plan with the associated DRC notes. Chairman Peterson seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

JONES JUNCTON SUBDIVISION VICINITY CONCEPT PLAT APPROVED:

   Jake Jones and Mike Jones addressed the commission to review a vicinity plat for property they own
west of the southbound off-ramp of the middle Nephi 1-15 exit. The two-parcel Jones Junction
Subdivision would have a McDonalds in the front portion and possibly some other commercial lots in the
back. Mr. Jake Jones said that UDOT had pre-approved access across from Wendy’s. Commissioner
Brough moved to approve the vicinity concept plat for Jones Junction Subdivision. Commissioner Heap
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BARBAROSA SUBDIVISION PRELIMINARY PLAT APPROVED:

  Ms. Talbot explained to the commission that another two-lot subdivision preliminary plat had been
received for across the street from the Jones Junction Subdivision proposal. The Barbarosa Subdivision
would include a stand-alone Wendy’s Restaurant and utilities would need to be extended to the site.
The city has asked both the Jones Junction developers and the Barbarosa Subdivision developers as they
are extending utilities to their properties, to coordinate with UDOT that are planning a mill and fill road
project in the area Commissioner Fran Petersen moved to approve the preliminary plat for Barbarosa
Subdivision. Commissioner Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC HEARING – CHANGES TO TITLE 10 LAND USE REGULATIONS OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE:

   The commission had a lengthy discussion about the revisions to the Combined Use Zone, the new
recommended Industrial Zone 3 for light industry uses, and the definition for “open space”.
  There was no public comment.

CHANGES TO CU ZONE, ESTABLISHING AN ID-3 ZONE, AND OPEN SPACE DEFINITION APPROVED:

   Chairman Ann Peterson moved to approve the revisions to the CU zone as presented. Commissioner
Alan Hancock seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
   Commissioner Fran Petersen moved to accept the ID-3 Zone as presented. Chairman Peterson
seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.
   Chairman Ann Peterson moved to approve the definition for “open space”. Commissioner Kent Heap
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PROPERTY BOUNDARY DISPUTE RESOLUTION POLICY APPROVED:

  Ms. Talbot presented the following Property Boundary Dispute Resolution Policy:
        “ Before the Planning Commission or City Council will consider a zone change request or before an
annexation petition will be certified, all boundary line disputes, including but not limited to underlaps
and overlaps, shall legally be resolved by all affected property owners. Proof of resolution of boundary
line disputes must be provided by the zone change requester or annexation petitioner(s) to the Planning
Commission and City Council.”
   Chairman Ann Peterson moved to accept the Property Boundary Dispute Resolution Policy.
Commissioner Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MINUTES APPROVED:

   Commissioner Cory Thomson moved to approve the minutes from the Planning Commission meetings
held on 2-9-22 and 2-23-22 with the change of Councilor Skip Worwood being marked as present
instead of absent at the 2-23-22 work session meeting. Commissioner Fran Petersen seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
  Chairman Peterson announced if she was ever absent at a meeting, that the most experienced
commissioners Brough, Heap, and Thomson could act as chairman tempore. She would arrange with
one of them before a meeting that she would be unable to attend. They agreed to help.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

Commissioner Cory Thomson moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Fran Petersen seconded
the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 9:15 p.m.
