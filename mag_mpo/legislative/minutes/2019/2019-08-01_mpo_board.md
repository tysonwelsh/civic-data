Public Comment None Lehi's Experience with Development Acquired Public Facilities i.e. Right of Way for Roads - Mayor Mark Johnson and Lehi Planning and Zoning Staff Density bonuses for Right of Way (ROW) is used for subdivisions located along a master planned roadway or master planned trail. It needs to be recommended by the Planning Commission, approved by the City Council and submitted with a concept report. Flexibility is allowed for higher density, reduced lot size, reduced frontage, or other design considerations. Examples where shown of how the process is implemented. The density bonus process has been in place for 15-20 years with guidelines established to ensure fairness and consistency. Cities currently implementing density bonuses include Lehi, American Fork, Saratoga Springs and Eagle Mountain. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee August 1, 2019 Page 3 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Final Approval – Bob Allen, MAG MAG, as the Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) for urban Utah County, has the responsibility of preparing and approving the Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). The TIP lists all the regional transportation projects funded over the next 4 years and is prepared and approved annually. MAG selects and funds projects every two years with funding under its jurisdiction. The next MAG selection cycle will begin in the fall of 2019. This year only has changes and new projects proposed by UDOT and UTA. The TIP lists MPO transportation projects and programs for fiscal years 2020–2023 and conceptual projects in 2024. All regional projects, regardless of the selection agency or funding source, must be approved by the MPO committees and be listed in the TIP before adoption into the State Transportation Improvement Program (STIP). MAG held a 30-day comment period July 1, 2019 through July 31, 2019. A public open house was held on July 17, 2019 with no significant comments that require amending the Regional Transportation Plan. Mayor Brunst moved that MPO Regional Planning Committee, with the recommendation of MPO Technical Advisory Committee, approve the 2020 TIP as presented. Mayor Westmoreland seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. TIP Project Selection Process Discussion – Bob Allen, MAG Every two years the TIP adopts the project selection process which includes the project idea phase, concepts reports, funding and final TIP approval. MAG works with the cities elected officials and staff to get feedback on how to improve the process. Regional Transportation Plan and Air Quality Conformity Update –Shawn Eliot, MAG MAG recently conducted a 30-day comment period for TransPlan50, the Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) for urban Utah County, and for the Air Quality Conformity documentation of said plan. The draft plan was presented to the MPO Technical Advisory Committee in May and MPO RPC in June. RPC authorized MAG staff to begin a 30-day comment period. They approved the draft plan as final barring no comments that triggered a new Air Quality Conformity determination. MAG received 105 comments over the comment period with comments predominately in regards two projects in Provo; RTP 37 widening of Provo 2230 N and RTP 38 widening of Provo 820 N. Both projects come from Provo’s adopted transportation master plan, approved by the city council through the planning commission and their public process. Responses were made to each of the comments for these projects referring them to the city of Provo for future planning work in revising any future plan. Provo staff requested that the projects remain in the RTP. After reviewing the comments received, it was determined by MAG staff that none of the comments received required a new conformity determination, the plan and conformity determination were submitted to FHWA. FHWA issued a finding of conformity for the plan. The plan is final.
