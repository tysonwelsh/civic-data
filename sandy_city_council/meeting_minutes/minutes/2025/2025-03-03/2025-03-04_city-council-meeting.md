1.         25-059                 Sandy City Fire Department Providing a Wildland Fire Update

                   Attachments:   Wildland presentation 2025

                                  Sandy Fire Chief McConaghie provided a Wildland Fire update to the Council. He
                                  reviewed the fire department and city's objectives: Community Wildfire Protection Plan
                                  (CWPP), the Wildland-Urban Interface Building Codes (WUI), and the Emergency
                                  Management and Deployment Overview. The Fire Department will hold their annual
                                  Wildland Community Outreach event on May 17th at the Bell Canyon Preservation
                                  Trailhead. Chief Stuebner spoke about the CWPP which provides direction in identifying
                                  the best management practices for reducing wildfire risk through preparedness,
                                  prevention, and mitigation. Prevention focuses on public education and community
                                  outreach. Mitigation efforts are accomplished through clean up efforts and maintenance,
                                  like the Dimple Dell clean up effort. Fire Marshall Gerald Strebel spoke about the WUI
                                  and the mitigation efforts that include the reduction of wildland fuel, the need for proper
                                  water supply, a second means of egress from WUI aeas, and cleared roads. On the Fire
                                  Department's website there is a new webpage that features a Wildland-Urban Interface
                                  map. He reviewed the map with the Council. David Larsen, Sandy Emergency Manager,
                                  spoke on the management of an emergency and the utilization of the Emergency
                                  Operations Center. He spoke about the process for an emergency evacuation and the
                                  importance for residents to be prepared and stay informed. He encouraged all residents
                                  to sign up for emergency alerts and for families to be prepared. Families should also have
                                  a designated meeting location in the event of any emergency. He encouraged the
                                  community to attend the Cert Community Emergency meetings and Be Ready Sandy
                                  meetings to help them learn how to prepare for an emergency. Council questions
                                  followed. Firefighter Corey Carter spoke about his experience in his role helping with the
                                  fire fighting efforts with the Silver King Fire, Park Fire, Red Rock Fire, Logan Fire and the
                                  Palisades Fire. He gained very valuable training experience. In conclusion, the Fire
                                  Department staff stressed the importance of being prepared in the event of any
                                  emergency and to utilize the services offered by the city which can be found on the city
                                  website. The Council thanked them for their presentation and for their service to the city.

                                  Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

2.         25-058                 Fire Department presenting amendments to the Sandy City and Metro Fire
                                  Trustees Interlocal Agreement
                   Attachments:   Metro Trustees ILA
                                  Metro Fire Agency Interlocal Agreement 2025 redline (002)
                                  Metro Fire Agency Interlocal Agreement 2025

                                  Chief McConaghie presented amendments to the Sandy City and Metro Fire Trustees
                                  Interlocal Agreement (ILA) Bylaws. He reviewed the recommended changes with the ILA.
                                  The Metro Chiefs' operations meetings have been determined to be advisory in nature and
                                  therefore should not be required to follow the Open and Public Meeting Laws. The
                                  amendments to the ILA remove sections 8-f and 11-a. This is an information item and will
                                  come back to the Council for consideration and action at a future Council meeting.

Mayor's Report

                   Mayor Zoltanski thanked Kim Bell, Lynn Pace, and Council Members for their support
                   and engagement during this legislative session. She thanked all who attended the Health
                   and Safety Fair. The postcards alerting our residents of the spring bulk waste clean up
                   schedule were sent out. This year the program will begin on March 17th. The March
                   Newsletter was sent out. The Sister City event commemorating the relationship with our
                   city's newest Sister City, Bucha, Ukraine will be held on March 17th. The community is
                   welcome to attend. The contractor for the rebuild of the Alta Canyon Sports Center has
                   been selected. Layton Construction was chosen. She thanked the staff at our animal
                   shelter. Our animal services staff is dedicated to serving and helping animals. Just even
                   losing one animal is difficult for our staff.

CAO Report

                   Shane Pace, CAO, mentioned that the parks staff will be testing and recharging area
                   sprinklers in the next couple of week. The city conducted a review of the animal shelter
                   incident and concluded the staff handled the animal appropriately. Please reach out with
                   any questions.
