                               This amendment streamlines and clarifies the standards for operating a reception
                               center in Taylorsville. Exhibit A includes a tracked- changes version of the affected

                               sections of Title 13, including footnotes identifying changes from current code.
                               Exhibit B includes supplemental documents required for permitting. Below are key
                               changes:



                                    Updates use permissions to only allow reception centers in the Boulevard
                                    Commercial( BC); Community Commercial( CC); and Professional Office
                                    PO) zoning districts.
                                    Limits the number of reception centers to 1 per 10,000 population( a
                                    maximum of six reception center business licenses at any given time).
                                    Reception         center must be at least 1, 000 feet from another reception center.
                  SUMMARY:
                                    Maximum occupancy determined by Building Official and Fire Marshal.
                                    Hours of operation, based on proximity to residential zoning districts.
                                    Standards for when employees and/ or a licensed and insured security guard
                                    must be present on- site.
                                    Noise mitigation report required to identify potential noise impacts.
                                    Property owner must verify application information at submittal( Exhibit B).
                                    On- site posting requirements and mailed notice requirements( Exhibit B).
                                    New standards regulating on- site alcohol consumption.
                                    New parking standard requiring 10 vehicle parking spaces per 1, 000 square
                                    feet of assembly area.
                                    Updated definition for" reception center."

         WHEREAS,         the   TaylorsvilleCity Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on
December 4, 2024, to consider, among other things, approving a text amendment to Sections
13. 08. 020; 13. 11. 250; and 13. 36. 190, updating standards related to reception centers; and

         WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 050, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the city' s official website
on November 1, 2024; and


         WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on November 1, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on November 12, 2024, during which the Planning Commission voted
7- 0 to forward a positive recommendation to the Council regarding the proposed text amendment;
and


        WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on December
4, 2024; and


         WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




         NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed text amendment to Sections 13. 08. 020; 13. 11. 250; and 13. 36. 190, updating standards
related to reception centers, are hereby approved, as articulated in Exhibit" A."

         This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 24- 12, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited, and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.

 3            Reception centers with a gross floor area of 2, 500 square feet or more shall be subject to

 5       B.   Number and Proximity
 6            1.   No more than one reception center per 10, 000 population                       in the city shall be permitted.
 7                 Population      numbers      are based on the U. S. Census     Bureau' s annual         estimates.

10                 entry door of each business. This standard             does not apply if the properties          are bisected

16            2.   Consecutive or repeated similar events shall not be held by the same customer.'
17            3.   Events shall comply with the Salt Lake County Health Department Noise Regulations, as
18                 amended.'

19            4.   Events shall terminate,        meaning music is off and guests are vacating the premises,
20                 according to the following:
21                 a.   Events   held within      250 feet of a residential   district,    measured      from the nearest

22                      property line, shall terminate at 10: 00 p. m., unless otherwise approved by the
23                      Planning    Commission.        In no case shall an event continue past 12: 00 a. m. ( midnight).

24                 b.   Events   held more than 250 feet from a residential               district   shall terminate    no later

25                      than 12: 00 a. m. ( midnight).

26                 c.   In all cases, the premises shall be completely vacated by all persons no later than
27                      one hour after the event' s designated           termination      time.

28            5.   For events without alcohol, a minimum of one reception center employee shall be on-

29                 site for the duration of the event if it continues         past 10: 00 p. m. and 75 or more people

82   13. 36. 190: " R"   DEFINITIONS


83   RECEPTION CENTER: A building, together with its accessory buildings and premises, which is a place
84   of assembly with the predominant purpose of hosting single contracted private events ( not open to
85   the public) either occurring alone or as part of another activity or business. The noise ordinance
86   shall be enforced as provided in section 26A 1 114 of the Utah Code Annotated. Typical uses include
87   wedding receptions, birthday parties, bar mitzvahs, quinceaneras, conventions, and other similar
88   private events, etc. Consecutive or repeated similar events shall not be held by the same
89   applicant. This definition does not include religious assembly.
