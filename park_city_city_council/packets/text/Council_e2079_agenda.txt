                                                       AGENDA
                       Joint Meeting between Summit County Council and Park City Council
                                              Tuesday, January 24, 2023
             NOTICE is hereby given that the Park City Council and the Summit County Council will meet
                           in special session on Tuesday, January 24, 2023, at 8:30 AM, at
                             the Richins Building, 1885 W. Ute Blvd., Park City, UT 84098

                To view Council meeting, live, visit the “Summit County, Utah” Facebook page at 9:00 a.m.
                                                             OR
                   To participate in Council meeting: Join Zoom webinar: https://zoom.us/j/772302472
                                                             OR
                          To listen by phone only: Dial 1-301-715-8592, Webinar ID: 772 302 472

8:30 a.m.         Light breakfast and social time (30 min)

9:00 a.m.         Summit County discussion of Summit County’s County Visioning Process and the Summit County
                  Community Planning Lab, Derek Siddoway and Maddy McDonough (10 min)

9:10 a.m.         Presentation and discussion of Mountain Mediation Community Listening Session Data re: Olympic bid
                  concerns and opportunities; Gretchen Lee, Executive Director, Mountain Mediation Center (50 min)

10:00 a.m.        Olympic bid status update; Colin Hilton and Tom Kelly (10 min)

10:10 a.m.        Affordable and Workforce Housing updates - New approvals, new openings, current Park City and
                  Summit County affordable housing counts; Jason Glidden, Park City Affordable Housing Manager and
                  Jeff Jones, Summit County Economic Development and Housing Director (15 min)

10:25 a.m.        Status update on Park City/Summit County Housing Workgroup process; Jason Glidden and Jeff Jones
                  (10 mins)

10:35 a.m.        Park City Discussion re Launch of Bonanza Park Neighborhood Area Plan and the Community
                  Engagement Process Concerning Arts and Culture; Deputy City Manager Jen McGrath (20 min)

10:55 a.m.        Wrap up comments and schedule the next meeting
