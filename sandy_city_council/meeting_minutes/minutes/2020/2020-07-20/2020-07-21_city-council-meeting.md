2.         20-244                 Council Member Zoltanski proposing Site Sign Posting Requirements for
                                  Public Meetings on rezoning and other land-use decisions.
                   Attachments:   Memo
                                  Exhibit A: Sign Examples
                                  Public Comment_Davies

                                  Presentation:
                                  Council Member Monica Zoltanski presented a proposal regarding physical posting
                                  requirements for some land use hearings. The presentation was followed by Council
                                  comments and questions.

                                  Brooke Christensen suggested not including annexations, as there's already a process
                                  for that. She also recommended adjusting the fee schedule to adjust for the added costs
                                  of the proposal.

                                  James Sorensen spoke to including/excluding annexations as well as the general idea of
                                  increasing fees to cover the costs of the signs and posting them.

                                  Kris Nicholl agreed with the concerns expressed about including annexations in this
                                  proposal. She also expresses concern that Sandy won't be able to require those
                                  annexing from other jurisdictions to follow the rules in this proposal.

                                  Bob Thompson and James Sorensen agreed to research the question of jurisdictional
                                  authority related to this proposal.

                                  Marci Houseman agrees with what's been said about annexations. She wonders what
                                  the unintended consequences might be. She discussed the manageability of revisiting
                                  posted signs to ensure they haven't disappeared or been destroyed.

                                  James Sorensen and Bob Thompson responded with staff's impression of what the
                                  unintended consequences to this proposal may be.

                                  Alison Stroud expressed her love of this proposal.

                                  Zach Robinson expressed his agreement with the concerns that have already been
                                  raised. He's wondering how the 10 day posting requirement was come to. He suggested
                                  shortening that period. Overall he likes the proposal.

                                  Monica Zoltanski responded to Zach Robinson's questions. She also recommended
                                  removing annexations from the proposal. She also shared photos of signs from other
                                  jurisdictions.




                                  Public Comment:

                                  Jodi Monaco spoke in favor of this proposal.

                                  Dustin Fratto read into the record a letter from Jessica Davies, which spoke in favor of
                                  this proposal.
                                  A motion was made by Monica Zoltanski, seconded by Kristin Coleman-Nicholl to
