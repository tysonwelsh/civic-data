1   2. 95. 060 Organizations, actions and recommendations.

    The members of the planning commission shall adopt rules and regulations for their organization and for the
    transaction of business and the conduct of their proceedings.


    Reports of official actions and recommendations of the planning commission shall be public and made by the
    chair in writing to the governing body and shall indicate how each member of the commission voted with
    respect to such action or recommendation. Any member of the commission may also make a concurring or
    dissenting report or-recommendation to the governing body.

    City provided proper notice is given if required.
