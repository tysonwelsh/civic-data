ITEM 1
      ZONE CHANGE- Downtown Curio – PUBLIC HEARING
      Consider a request to change the zoning from Central Business District (C-4)
      to Planned Development Mixed Use (PD-MU) on approximately 3.24 acres to
      allow for a new mixed-use project. The applicant is Civil Science and the
      representative is Brandee Walker. The project will be known as Downtown
      Curio. Case No. 2025-ZC-013 (Staff – Brenda Hatch)
