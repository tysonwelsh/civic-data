    1. DISCUSSION ON COUNTY WIDE MITIGATION PLAN
         Lance Peterson, of County Emergency Management, stated that the county-wide mitigation plan has been
         revised. This item is on the agenda to open the 30-day public comment period. Plan hard copies are
         available at the Commission and Emergency Management offices, and an electronic copy is available at
         http://www3.co.weber.ut.us/MitigationPlan2015.php where the public can also submit comments. Chair
         Gibson invited public comments and none were offered.
    2. MODIFICATION TO THE CURRENT FY2015 COOPERATIVE LAW ENFORCEMENT ANNUAL OPERATING
       PLAN AND FINANCIAL PLAN – CONTRACT C2015-304
         Steffani Ebert, of the County Sheriff’s Office, stated that the Sheriff’s Office received a contract
         modification from the U.S. Forest Service on the law enforcement services the county provides to them.
         The original contract amount ($60,000+) was reduced (to $54,000) and this modification further reduces
         that amount to $48,000. This reduction was done mid-year, while the county has been filling the shifts at
         the rate they thought they had the money for. She believes the State was asked to cut their budgets 10%
         last year but apparently they reduced even further. The commissioners expressed frustration because the
         county has to provide the service, yet the amount never covers the county’s costs. They do not want to
         send the message that because they signed this contract that they are in agreement. Commissioner Bell
         believes there will be more discussion with the Forest Service later this month. He said that the county
         also provides services through the winter and receives no funding. This contract is effective through
         9/30/2015. Commissioner Ebert asked Ms. Ebert to convey to the Sheriff that this will be a topic of
         serious discussion about how to resolve the issue.
         Commissioner Bell moved to approve Contract C2015-304, modification to the current FY2015
         Cooperative Law Enforcement Annual Operating Plan and Financial Plan; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
