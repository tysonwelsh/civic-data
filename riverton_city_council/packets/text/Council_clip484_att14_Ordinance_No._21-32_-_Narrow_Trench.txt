                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 21-32

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY’S STANDARD SPECIFICTIONS
AND PLANS TO INCLUDE THE ‘NARROW TRENCH STANDARD’, AMENDMENTS
                   PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed amendment to the Riverton City Standard
Specifications and Plans; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public amend the Riverton City Standard Specifications and Plans to include the “Narrow Trench
Standard”, as described in Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Standard Specifications and Plans shall be, and hereby is,
       amended as shown in Exhibit “A.”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 14th day of
