    3. APPROVAL OF AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT FOR ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES WITH
       WEBER HOUSING AUTHORITY.
         Chris Crockett (Deputy County Attorney): A long time ago Weber Housing Authority was created and
         needed help to become the great organization it is today. For a number of years, Weber County has helped
         the Weber Housing Authority grow. Through amazing management and a very active Board, the Housing
         Authority has become self-sustaining. So the time has come to break up or let it go. The only two services
         Weber County provides that will continue after December 31st, 2024 are legal services and some audit
         assistance.

         Andi Beadles (Weber Housing Authority Director): We have an amazing Board that helped get us here but
         we would not be here without the support of Weber County. That support has been instrumental in getting us
         to a point where we are self-sufficient and can separate from the County to become our own entity. It is an
         exciting day. Thank you to everyone in the County for all of the administrative support provided.

         Commissioner Froerer: It’s a tough day when you see your kids leave the house; it’s bittersweet. My wife
         has been there since day one in 2008. It’s time to let them go out and fly on their own. With Andi’s
         direction, I have no doubt it will be successful. She brings a large amount of good projects to Weber County
         and Ogden City for people who need a hand up not hand out. I’ve got full faith and confidence for this will
         be successful for the future.

         Commissioner Bolos: Congratulations, it’s been a long time coming. We so appreciate your hard work.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve an Interlocal Agreement for Administrative Services with Weber
         Housing Authority; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   Stall of Fame Recipient: Angela Hill (Assessor’s Office)
    John Ulibarri (County Assessor): When Shelly asked who we wanted for our Stall of Fame recipient, choosing
    Angela Hill was easy. I asked Shelly how much time we had because listing the good things about Ang would
    take about an hour. She said we only had three minutes, so here it goes. Professionally, Angela shows a high
    degree of expertise and competency. Most importantly, she shows good judgment. Angela understands the purpose
    of the Greenbelt laws and does her best to assist those who need help complying before imposing a rollback tax.
    She walks those who ask through the requirements and what they need to do to stay in the program. If she is out
    of the office and someone needs help, you can call Angela at home. She will help you with that customer while
    also providing the customer’s details and what needs to be done from memory. Personally, Angela radiates
    kindness everywhere she goes. She treats everyone with respect and is just plain nice. People gravitate to Angela
    because of how pleasant she is. Here are a couple of comments from her teammates:

         “Her extensive knowledge spans all areas of the Assessor’s Office and the various activities within the
         county. Angela’s positive attitude, caring nature, and unwavering loyalty make her a standout team member.
         She excels in customer service, particularly as our Greenbelt specialist, and is always ready to assist in any
         capacity.”
         Another says: “Angela is always happy to help with any questions one might have. She also shares her
         personal life, which lets us see her personality and get to know her better. She always has a smile on her face
         and is fun to be around.”

There are so many good things to say about Angela, but I think this best sums it up: A day without Angela is like a
day without sunshine. You can survive but are a little poorer for it. In closing, Angela Hill is a fantastic
representative of the Assessor’s Office and Weber County. Receiving the Stall of Fame award is long overdue.
