                                 KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
         ORDINANCE NO. 2024-06-01                           DATE: June 10, 2024
 AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE “CITY OF KEARNS” AS THE OFFICIAL NAME
     FOR KEARNS AND ADOPTING AN ACCOMPANYING SEAL AND LOGO
                                          RECITALS

       WHEREAS, on January 1 2017, the community of Kearns (“Kearns”) incorporated as a
metro township, a form of municipal government similar to cities and towns; and
       WHEREAS, during the 2024 legislative session, the Utah Legislature passed H.B. 35,
which converted the Kearns Metro Township and the metro townships of Copperton, Emigration
Canyon, Magna, and White City into cities and towns based on their populations effective May 1,
2024; and
        WHEREAS, now that Kearns is a city pursuant to H.B. 35, the Kearns Council (“Council”)
desires to adopt the “City of Kearns” as the official municipal name for Kearns and to adopt an
accompanying seal and logo pursuant to its authority under Utah Code § 10-1-102.
     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

      1.     Enactment of Chapter 1.02. The following is adopted as Chapter 1.02 of the Kearns
Municipal Code:

           Chapter 1.02 OFFICIAL NAME

           1.02.010 Purpose and Scope

           The official name of the City shall be the “City of Kearns.” All official documents,
           agreements, letterheads, deeds, publications, and other City property shall bear the
           official name.

           1.02.020 Inconsistent Provisions

           The enactment of this chapter shall not be construed to repeal or invalidate prior acts
           the City made previously as the Kearns Metro Township or under any other corporate
           name. To the extent public documents or provisions of this code reference other public
           names, such documents and provisions shall be construed to incorporate the official
           name by this reference.

                                  ORDINANCE NO. 2024-06-01

      On June 10, 2024, the Kearns City Council enacted Ordinance No. 2024-06-01 to: (1) enact

municipal name for Kearns; and (2) adopt an accompanying seal and logo that uses the new official
municipal name.
