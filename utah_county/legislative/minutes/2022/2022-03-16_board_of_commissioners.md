-Tom Sakievich, Commission
(Timestamp: 7:09)

COMMISSIONER GARDNER - I NOTICED THAT THE CONTRACT TALKS ABOUT
THEM PROCURING ON BEHALF OF THE COUNTY. I’M WONDERING WHAT THE
PROCESS THEY’RE GOING TO USE TO PROCURE. I NOTICED THAT THE AMOUNT IS
$25,000. DID WE GET ANY BIDS ON THAT AND HOW DID WE COME UP WITH
THAT AMOUNT?

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH - THERE WAS A SOLICITATION THAT WENT OUT TO 4
GROUPS. TWO HAD RESPONDED, BUT THEY WERE NOT ABLE TO GET ANYWHERE
NEAR THIS AND SO WE WENT WITH THIS GROUP WHO ORGANIZED IT FOR US
LAST YEAR. IN PAST YEARS, WE HAD HIRED SOMEONE FOR $68,000 PLUS 10%
FOR ALL DONATIONS SO THIS IS SUBSTANTIALLY LESS. AFTER WE GOT THEIR
INFORMATION, WE WENT TO THE CIVIL DIVISION AND CONFERRED WITH THEM.

COMMISSIONERS AND ROBERT BAXTER (PURCHASING MANAGER), LISA
SHEPHERD (SENIOR POLICY ADVISOR), BRIAN VOEKS (SENIOR POLICY ADVISOR)
AND BEN VAN NOY (DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY) DISCUSSED PREVIOUS GROUPS
HIRED, OTHER BIDS, PROCUREMENT PROCESS, BIDDING PROCESS, PROCEDURE
LAST YEAR AND PREVIOUS YEARS, ETC.
