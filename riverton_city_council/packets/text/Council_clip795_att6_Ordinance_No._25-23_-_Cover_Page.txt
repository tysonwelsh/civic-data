PRESENTER:                Jason Lethbridge

                          Ordinance No. 25-23 - amending Sections 18.90.010 “Table of Commercial
                          Uses” & 18.70.030 “Conditional Uses” to allow “Auto Dealership (New or
ITEM:                     Used) as a Conditional Use in the Commercial Gateway Zone. Amendments
                          Proposed by Riverton City. - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services
                          Director

SUMMARY:

The proposed change would list Auto dealerships as Conditional in the C-G zone and add conditions in
the C-G Chapter. The proposed conditions would limit Auto Dealerships to properties of at least one (1)
acre with frontage along 12600 South or the Bangerter Highway.

FISCAL IMPACT:            N/A

Ordinance No. 25-23 - C-G Auto Dealership Code Amend Ordinance.pdf

RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

On September 25, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this text amendment.
Minutes from that meeting are included below. The Planning Commission voted to recommend denial of
the text amendment.

I move the City Council DENY Ordinance 25-23, proposed amendment to the Riverton City Table of
Commercial Uses and Section 18.70.030 allowing “Auto Dealerships (new or used)” as a Conditional
Use in the C-G Zone.
