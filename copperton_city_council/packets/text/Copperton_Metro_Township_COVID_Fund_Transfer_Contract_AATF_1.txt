        THIS INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) is between SALT
LAKE COUNTY (“County”) and the _____________________________.
                                   Copperton Metro Township               (“Metro Township”). The
County and the Metro Township may each be referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively as the
“Parties.”



   A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) received federal funds under section 601(a) of the Social
      Security Act as added by section 5001 of the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security
      Act (Public Law 116-136) (the “CARES Act”) to combat and address the effects of the novel
      Coronavirus Disease 2019 (“COVID-19”) within Salt Lake County.
   B. The County distributed CARES funds to municipalities within the County in two tranches to aid
      the municipalities in their response to the COVID-19 pandemic.
   C. The Metro Township received CARES funding from the County.
   D. Due to the restriction of the CARES Act and corresponding guidance, the Metro Township
      experienced difficulties in spending its full CARES Act allotments prior to the then existing
      spending deadline of December 30, 2020 and returned unused portions of allotments or declined
      additional allotments, or both.
   E. The County was able to properly expend the CARES Act funding the Metro Township returned
      to the County or declined and has a corresponding surplus in the County’s general fund budget.
   F. The Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Section 11-13-215 allows counties to share revenues with
      other cities, towns, or local political subdivisions.
   G. The County now wishes to transfer general fund dollars to the Metro Township in an amount
      equal to the Metro Township’s unused allocation of CARES Act funds to provide economic
      assistance to the Metro Township where it was unable to benefit from the CARES Act and to
      assist in efforts to combat and mitigate the health and economic impacts of the COVID-19
      pandemic.
   H. The Parties are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code
      Ann. §§ 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Cooperation Act”), and, as such, are authorized by the
      Cooperation Act to enter into this Agreement to act jointly and cooperatively in a manner that
      will enable them to make the most efficient use of their resources and powers.
