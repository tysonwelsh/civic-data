                               CITY OF BLUFFDALE,UTAH

                                ORDINANCE NO.2025-11


AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 3.10.040 AND 3.80.050 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY
CODE TO PROVIDE A PROCESS FOR NEW OR UNLISTED BUSINESSES TO BE CLASSIFIED
AS REQUIRED BY STATE LAW;TO REQUIRE ALL BUSINESSES TO COMPLY WITH
ADOPTED ZONING REQUIREMENTS,BUILDING CODES AND FIRE CODES;AND TO AMEND
THE DEFINITION OF HOME-BASED MICROSCHOOLS AND MICRO EDUCATION ENTITIES
FOLLOWING CHANGES TO STATE LAW DURING THE 2025 LEGISLATIVE SESSION.


WHEREAS,the 2025 Utah State Legislature passed Senate Bills 179 and 272,requiring
municipalities to provide a classification process for new or unlisted businesses and
amending the adopted definition(s) of microeducation entities and home-based
microschools;

WHEREAS,Title 3 of the Bluffdale City Code governs business licensing regulations;

WHEREAS,the City proposes amendments to Sections 3.10.040 and 3.80.050 of the
Bluffdale City Code to comply with requirements set forth by Senate Bills 179 and 272;

WHEREAS,a notice of public hearing for the proposed amendments was provided
pursuant to the Bluffdale City Code and Utah State Code;

WHEREAS,on May 7,2025,the Planning Commission conducted a public hearing and
unanimously forwarded a positive recommendation of the proposed Text Amendment to
the City Council.

NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:


       Section 1. Adoption. Ordinance 2025-11 is hereby adopted, amending Sections
3.10.040 and 3.80.050 of the Bluffdale City Code as shown in Exhibit A.

       Section 2. Amendment of Conflicting Ordinances. If any ordinances,
resolutions, policies, or zoning maps ofthe City heretofore adopted are inconsistent
herewith they are hereby amended to comply with the provisions hereof. If they cannot be
amended to comply with the provisions hereof,they are hereby repealed.

       Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable,such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and
independent provision,and such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance
shall be severabie.




      Section 4.     Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED: May 14,2025

     3.10.040 Application, An4 Fees, and Compliance Required
A. Application.

      1. An application will be processed when the application form is complete
         and all applicable fees are paid.

      2. Within 14 days of payment of fees, staff will notify the applicant if an
         application is not complete or other information is needed. The
         applicant will have 30 days to resubmit the application for processing.

B. Fees.

      1. If the applicant incurs any additional fees during the application
         process, those fees must be paid before the City will issue a business
         license. The City shall notify the applicant of its outstanding balance
           each month. A business license shall be denied if all fees are not paid
           within 90 days.

      2. All renewal license fees shall be due and payable each year after the
         approval of the business license. The City shall notify the applicant of
         its outstanding balance each month. A business license renewal shall
         be denied if all fees are not paid within 90 days.

      3. A business that is denied a license or denied a renewal due to its fees
           not being paid within 90 days will be required to apply for a new license
           and pay the additional application fees, applicable. The additional fees
           would include those associated with any special exception requests

      4. Fees shall be considered delinquent if not paid within 30 days.

C. Compliance with Buildino and Zoning Regulations.
      1. No license shall be issued for the conduct of any business, and no
           permit shall be issued if the premises and building use do not comply
           with the adopted Building Code. Fire Code, and Zoning Regulations.

           City Code) may apply for an Administrative Interpretation as described
           in Chapter 11.360 of the Bluffdale City Code to classify the proposed
           business and designate an appropriate zone or zones for an approved
