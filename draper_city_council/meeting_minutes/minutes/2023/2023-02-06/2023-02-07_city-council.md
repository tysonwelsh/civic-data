Study Session

1.0     Update: Capital Improvement Projects

1.1     Recreation Director Rhett Ogden reported on completed and ongoing capital
        improvement projects and answered questions from the Council.

1.2     The Council and staff discussed City trails and connectivity. Four Councilmembers
        indicated support for developing a new soft trail.

1.3     Staff mentioned concerns expressed by citizens related to trails and station area planning.

1.4     Staff reported that Lehi City desired to construct a trail that would go across Draper City
        property. Lehi City would be responsible for all cost and construction of the trail.

1.5     Staff spoke of a grant application for a disc golf course.

2.0     Council/Manager Reports

2.1     none

3.0     Closed Session

3.1     Councilmember F. Lowry moved to recess to a Closed Session to discuss pending or
        reasonably imminent litigation as allowed by State law. Councilmember Green
        seconded the motion.

3.2     A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.

5.0   Resolution #23-10, a Resolution of the City Council of the City of Draper, Utah,
      authorizing the issuance and sale of not more than $9,000,000 aggregate principal
      amount of Sales Tax Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series 2023; and related matters

5.1   Jason Burningham with Lewis, Young, Robertson, & Burningham, financial advisor to
      Draper City, gave a presentation regarding the recommendation to refund the City’s
      Aquarium bond issue. The debt would retain the same term, ending May 1, 2032.

5.2   David Robertson with Lewis, Young, Robertson, & Burningham explained proposed
      parameters to the Council.

7.0   Action Item: Resolution #23-08, a Resolution of the Draper City Council appointing three
      members to the Administrative Control Board for the Traverse Ridge Special Service
      District

7.1   Mayor Walker thanked all applicants for their willingness to serve.

7.2   Councilmember Green said he believed the relationship between the City and the
      Traverse Ridge Special Service District had come a long way. He said he did not want to
      disrupt that relationship with a change in personnel.

      Ben Frederick, and Andrew (Drew) Joosten to the Administrative Control Board for the
      Traverse Ridge Special Service District. Councilmember F. Lowry seconded the motion.

8.0   Public Hearing: Resolution #23-09, a Resolution amending the adopted budget of Draper
      City for the fiscal year 2022-2023
8.1   Jared Zacharias, Acting Finance Director, presented proposed amendments to the FY2023
      Budget. He explained that item FY23-44 included in the proposed Resolution should be
      removed.

8.2   The Council and staff discussed adding $20,000 for Spring flood mitigation if needed.

8.3   Mayor Walker opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing seeing that no
      one came forward to comment.

8.4   Councilmember F. Lowry moved to approve Resolution #23-09 with removal of item
      FY23-44, and with addition of $20,000 for possible flood mitigation if needed.
       Councilmember T. Lowery seconded the motion.

8.5   A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
