                                           MIDVALE CITY, UTAH

                                        ORDINANCE NO. 2021-O-21

         AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING A ROAD CUT MORATORIUM AND RESTORATION
             STANDARDS FOR QUALIFYING STREETS WITHIN MIDVALE CITY.

             WHEREAS, Midvale City spends considerable staff time and several hundred thousand dollars
    a year on street reconstruction and maintenance; and

             WHEREAS, Midvale City desires to keep their city streets in the best condition possible for as
    long as possible; and

           WHEREAS, road cuts, coring, and other activities that cause damage to the road surface and
    underlying materials have a negative effect on the road structure and rideability, causing premature
    aging ofthe road and an overall increase in maintenance costs; and

           WHEREAS, in an effort to keep City streets in a well-maintained condition, the City wishes to
    adopt a plan to eliminate, except for in certain circumstances, any work that would have a negative
    impact to the City's newly constructed or overlaid road network and apply reconstruction standards.

             NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Midvale City Council as follows:

                    The Midvale City Roadcut Moratorium be formally adopted, and the standards,
    limitations, and procedures contained therein be enforced.


             APPROVED AND ADOPTED thiS 9th dAV of November 2021
