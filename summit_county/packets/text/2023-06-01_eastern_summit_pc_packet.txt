STAFF REPORT 
 
To:   Eastern Summit County Planning Commission  
From:   Amir Caus, Senior Planner 
Date of Meeting: June 1, 2023 
Type of Item:  Conditional Use Permit Amendment - Public Hearing, Possible Action 
Process:  Administrative  
 
RECOMMENDATION:  Staff has reviewed the application for compliance with all standards in 
the Eastern Summit County Development Code and has found that it meets the minimum 
required for approval. Staff recommends that the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
review the proposed Conditional Use Permit Amendment, conduct a public hearing and vote to 
approve the Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension - Conditional Use Permit Amendment 
pursuant to the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions of Approval found in this 
Staff Report.   
 
Project Description 
 
Project Name:   Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension - Conditional Use Permit  
    Amendment 
Applicant(s):   Robert Wadman representing Rees’s Gravel Pit 
Property Owner(s):  Gary Pace Co-Trustee (Parcels S-415 and NS-124) and Gary Pace  
    (Parcel NS-125-A)  
Location:   ~1662 S West Hoytsville Rd, Wanship, Summit County, UT 
Zone District:   AG-10 
Parcel Number and Size: S-415 (65 acres), NS-124 (135 acres), and NS-125-A (103 acres) 
Type of Process:  Administrative 
Final Land Use Authority: Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
  
Proposal 
 
The Summit County Community Development Department has received an application for a 
Conditional Use Permit Amendment to the 1985/1990 Geary Construction/Wilkinson 
Construction Co. Inc. Gravel Pit Conditional Use Permit now known as Rees’s Gravel Pit. The 
Conditional Use Permit Amendment would allow for a ~13-pole (~1,300 feet in length and up to 
~50 feet in height) power line extension from the main Rocky Mountain Power distribution line 
(Parcel NS-125-A) to the Rees’s Pit, located at 1662 S West Hoytsville Rd (Parcels S-415 and NS-
124), Wanship, UT. The proposed line extension would eliminate the use of generators as the 
primary electricity source for the gravel pit. The permit is a retroactive permit requested by the

applicant. Rocky Mountain Power performed the work without a Conditional Use Permit which 
is required for utility towers and associated transmission and distribution lines. All 
improvements through Parcel NS-125-A would be located within the utility easement (Entry No. 
1180550). The remaining improvements would be located within the utility easement (Entry 
No. 00717806). No other changes to the Conditional Use Permit are being requested. 
 
Vicinity Map 
 
 
 
Background 
 
The Geary Construction/Wilkinson Construction Co. Inc. Gravel Pit Conditional Use Permit was 
originally approved in 1985 and amended in 1990. The gravel pit has been operational ever 
since.  
 
Rocky Mountain Power performed the line extension install without acquiring the requisite 
permits. After a neighbor made a complaint Staff reached out to Rocky Mountain Power and 
Rees’s Gravel Pit to apply for a Conditional Use Permit Amendment.  
 
Analysis and Findings 
 
Section 11-4-7(G) of the Eastern Summit County Development Code states; Amendments to 
Conditional Use Permits:

1.   Minor Amendment: A minor amendment is defined as an amendment that does not increase 
the square footage, density, or intensity of a previously approved conditional use permit. A 
minor amendment may be approved by the Community Development Director. No public 
hearing is required. 
 
2.   Major Amendment: A major amendment is defined as an amendment that increases square

Analysis: Per Section 11-3-16 Chart of Allowed Uses “Utility towers and associated 
transmission and distribution lines greater than 45 feet in height” are processed through 
a Conditional Use Permit.  
Section 11-4-7(B) of the Eastern Summit County Development Code states; Before an 
application for a conditional use permit is approved, the Planning Commission must conclude 
that factual evidence exists to verify the following findings: 
 
1. The proposed use, as conditioned, shall be appropriate in the particular location, taking into 
account the nature of the use, its relationship to surrounding uses and its impact on the 
natural environment. 
 
Analysis: Chapter 5 - Natural Resources / Environmental Quality 5.1 GOAL states: 
Develop codes and policies that promote proper stewardship of natural resources and 
address environmental issues of Eastern Summit County. 
 
a. Ensure that development occurs in a manner and location that protects natural 
resources, including but not limited to pollution prevention, erosion prevention, 
national forests, crucial wildlife habitat and corridors, agricultural lands, fisheries, 
water quality, wetlands, scenic view sheds, riparian areas, wildlife and clean air. 
 
By nature, powerlines are visible, however the use of dark-colored materials blends the 
poles into the background.  
 
Electric infrastructure is essential to accommodate the needs of both the current and 
potential future population of Summit County. This application seeks to provide 
infrastructure to meet safety standards and eliminate the use of diesel-powered 
generators for this site which also reduces pollution. COMPLIES

Recommendation 
 
It is Staff’s finding that the Conditional Use request meets the applicable standards in the 
Eastern Summit County Development Code. Staff recommends that the Eastern Summit County

Permit Amendment pursuant to the following Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and 
Conditions of Approval:  
 
Findings of Fact: 
 
1. The Summit County Community Development Department has received an application 
for a Conditional Use Permit Amendment to the 1985/1990 Geary 
Construction/Wilkinson Construction Co. Inc. Gravel Pit Conditional Use Permit now 
known as Rees’s Gravel Pit.  
2. The Conditional Use Permit Amendment would allow for a ~13-pole (~1,300 feet in 
length and up to ~50 feet in height) power line extension from the main Rocky 
Mountain Power distribution line (Parcel NS-125-A) to the Rees’s Pit, located at 1662 S 
West Hoytsville Rd (Parcels S-415 and NS-124), Wanship, UT.  
3. The proposed line extension would eliminate the use of generators as the primary 
electricity source for the gravel pit.  
4. The permit is a retroactive permit requested by the applicant.  
5. Rocky Mountain Power performed the work without a Conditional Use Permit which is 
required for utility towers and associated transmission and distribution lines.  
6. All improvements through Parcel NS-125-A would be located within the utility easement 
(Entry No. 1180550).  
7. The remaining improvements would be located within the utility easement (Entry No. 
00717806).  
8. No other changes to the Conditional Use Permit are being requested. 
9. Electric infrastructure is essential to accommodate the needs of both the current and 
potential future population of Summit County.  
10. This application seeks to provide infrastructure to meet safety standards and eliminate 
the use of diesel-powered generators for this site which also reduces pollution.  
11. The Eastern Summit County Development Code definition of “Structure” specifically 
excludes “poles, lines, cables, fences, on grade decks, driveways, and other similar 
features” and setback and height requirements do not apply.  
12. Section 11-4-7(G) of the Eastern Summit County Development Code states;

Project Description 
 
Project Name: MountainWest Pipeline Gas Line Upgrade Conditional Use Permit 
Applicant(s):   Yuka Jenkins, MountainWest Pipeline LLC 
Property Owner(s):  MountainWest Pipeline LLC via Easements shown in Exhibit B 
Location:               South Hoytsville Rd. corridor area (~2.4 miles) – see Vicinity Map 
Type of Process:  Administrative 
Final Land Use Authority: Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
 
 
Proposal 
 
The Summit County Community Development Department has received an application for a 
Conditional Use Permit for the MountainWest Pipeline Gas Line Upgrade. The Conditional Use 
Permit would allow for a ~2.4-mile gas transmission line upgrade of the existing 1949 twenty-
inch (20”) diameter transmission line, located in Hoytsville, UT (see Vicinity Map below). The 
proposed transmission line upgrade would bring the gas line into compliance with United States 
Department of Transportation. All improvements would take place within the existing 
transmission line alignment.

Background 
 
According to the applicant, the existing transmission line alignment, located South Hoytsville 
Road corridor area (~2.4 miles) has been in existence for at least 74 years.  
 
Per the Eastern Summit County Development Code, “Underground transmission lines exceeding 
12 inches diameter (including but not limited to gas, oil and water)” are reviewed under a 
Conditional Use Permit.

taking into account the nature of the use, its relationship to surrounding uses and its impact on 
the natural environment.

Recommendation 
 
It is Staff’s finding that the project meets the applicable standards in the Eastern Summit 
County Development Code. Staff recommends that the Eastern Summit County Planning 
Commission review and approve the MountainWest Pipeline Gas Line Upgrade Conditional Use 
Permit, conduct a public hearing and approve the application according to the following 
Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions of Approval: 
 
Findings of Fact: 
 
1. The Summit County Community Development Department has received an application 
for a Conditional Use Permit for the MountainWest Pipeline Gas Line Upgrade.  
2. The Conditional Use Permit would allow for a ~2.4-mile gas transmission line upgrade of 
the existing 1949 twenty-inch (20”) diameter transmission line, located in Hoytsville, UT 
(see Vicinity Map below).  
3. The proposed transmission line upgrade would bring the gas line into compliance with 
United States Department of Transportation.  
4. All improvements would take place within the existing transmission line alignment.  
5. Natural gas infrastructure is essential to accommodate the needs of both the current 
and potential future population of Summit County.  
6. This application seeks to provide infrastructure to meet safety standards.  
7. Per the Eastern Summit County Development Code, “Underground transmission lines 
exceeding 12 inches diameter (including but not limited to gas, oil and water)” are 
reviewed under a Conditional Use Permit in all zones. 
 
Conclusions of Law: 
 
1. The proposed use, as conditioned, is appropriate in the particular location, taking into 
account the nature of the use, its relationship to surrounding uses and its impact on the 
natural environment. 
2. The proposed use, as conditioned, is in compliance with the development evaluations 
standards in Chapter 2 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code. 
3. The applicant has presented evidence to show approval of the landowner for the 
particular use. 
4. There are reasonable conditions that can be imposed which mitigate the reasonably 
anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. 
 
Conditions of Approval: 
 
1. The proposed MountainWest Pipeline Gas Line Upgrade Conditional Use Permit shall be 
as per Exhibit A of this Staff Report.  
2. No outdoor storage is permitted. 
3. No modification to the approved Conditional Use Permit is permitted without the prior 
approval of the Summit County Community Development Department.

any construction activity, including but not limited any State of Utah and Federal 
requirements, Summit County Engineering Department, and the Summit County

7. All service provider requirements shall be met.
