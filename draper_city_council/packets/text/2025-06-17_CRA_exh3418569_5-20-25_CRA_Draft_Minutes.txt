      2.b   Public     Hearing: Fiscal     Year    2025-2026 Community
            Reinvestment Agency Tentative Budget
            Finance Director John Vuyk presented the proposed FY2026 Community
            Reinvestment Agency Tentative Budget, with a CDRA Fund budget of
            $12,297,900. He explained that the Sand Hill CDRA, which had expired,
            carried a fund balance of approximately $3.4 million. While the Sand Hill
            CDRA would no longer collect taxes, the City had the ability to spend the
            fund balance within the scope of the Sand Hill CDRA.

            Chair Pro Tem Green opened a public hearing, and closed the public
            hearing seeing no one come forward.

3.0   Adjourn to City Council Meeting
      Board Member Johnson moved to adjourn the CDRA meeting and return
      to regular Council meeting. Board Member Vawdrey seconded the
      motion, which passed by unanimous voice vote (4-0).

      The meeting adjourned at 8:00 p.m.
