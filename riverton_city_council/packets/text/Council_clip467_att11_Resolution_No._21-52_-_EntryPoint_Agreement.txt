                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 21-52

        A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN
AGREEMENT WITH ENTRYPOINT NETWORKS FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE
        PHASE 1 DESIGN OF A CITY OWNED FIBER NETWORK

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council required that the project must come back for final
approval before proceeding; and

       WHEREAS, Purchasing has issued an RFP requesting proposals from perspective firms;
and

       WHEREAS, the RFP scoring committee selected EntryPoint Networks.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with EntryPoint
          Networks for the completion of the Phase 1 Design of a City Owned Fiber Network.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of
September, 2021 by the following vote:
