                                            RECITALS

       A.      Riverton owns and maintains a parcel of real property located in Riverton City, at

the address of 12275 South Lampton View Drive Riverton, Utah (the “Riverton Park Property”)

which is used for general public park purposes, including games and tournaments organized by

amateur sports leagues. A more particular description of the Riverton Property is found in

Exhibit A, incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

       B.      The Riverton Park Property lies adjacent to a parcel of real property which is

owned by Owner (the “Owner Property”). The Owner Property is located at the address of

___________________ Lampton View Drive, Riverton, Utah. The Owner Property is used as a
single-family residence, with a home located upon the same. A more particular description of

the Owner Property is found in Exhibit B, incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

       C.      For a number of years, Owner has experienced difficulties in managing ground

water which percolates from subsurface springs which are endemic to the vicinity of the Jordan

River. Owner has deployed, at Owner’s sole expense, a number of methods to collect and

discharge subsurface waters from Owner’s property, to prevent the same from percolating into

the basement of Owner’s residence, but has been unsuccessful in keeping water from collecting

within the basement of Owner’s residence. Once water is collected in the basement, the same is

is pumped into a drain line which discharges directly into the gutter system in front of the Owner

Property. This system was created by permission granted from Riverton to Owner. The effect if

this continually discharged water into the public gutter system has caused nuisance conditions in

the gutter until such water empties into a Riverton-owned culvert which manages storm water.

       D.      The parties have disputed the root cause to the groundwater found on Mr. May’s

property. Owner has alleged ground water which percolates into Owner’s Property originates

from the Riverton Park Property, or is affected by Riverton’s use of the Riverton Park Property.

Riverton has denied this allegation. From this dispute, a settlement agreement was reached with

Mr. May in the year 2014, wherein Riverton agreed to install a subsurface drainage system

within the Riverton Park Property, which lies adjacent to the Owner Property. Its intended

purpose was to collect ground water before it migrated to the basement area of the Owner

Property.

       E.      Riverton completed its work under the terms of the Settlement Agreement.

Unfortunately, the subsurface drainage system was unsuccessful in collecting groundwater from




                                                2
the Owner Property, meaning said groundwater continues to percolate into the basement of the

Owner’s residence.

       F.      Owner and Riverton have held further discussions to address the problem of

managing ground water arising from Owner’s property. From these discussions, the Parties have

determined Owner and Riverton have a shared interest in correcting the drainage system. The

Parties believe the best method to manage the ground water percolating on Mr. May’s property is

to collect it as closely as possible from its current collection point in Mr. May’s basement, by

installing a drain system underneath Mr. May’s home, and routing the water collected directly

into Riverton stormwater system located below the surface of Lampton View Drive.

       G.      Owner and Riverton anticipate that the ground water drain pipeline installed

within the Easement Property shall become the property of Riverton, once it enters the public’s

right of way established for Lampton View Drive.

       E.      Riverton has obtained a bid from ___________ Construction Inc. to ascertain the

cost to construct an underdrain system on the Owner’s Property, and connect the same to existing

subsurface stormwater pipe located in Lampton View Drive. The bid delineates the work to be

done on Owner’s Property and work to be done in Riverton right of way.

       F.      The Parties desire, under the terms of this Agreement, to establish how a project

to install a subsurface drain system as described in this Agreement shall be managed; and to

determine how fees to conduct this work by contractor shall be paid.

       NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties, in consideration of the promises and covenants

contained in this Agreement, the receipt of which is acknowledged, covenant and agree as

follows:

       1.      Incorporation of Recitals. The Recitals hereto are incorporated herein by

reference to the same extent and with the same force and effect as if fully set forth herein.

       2.      Construction Management. Riverton and Owner Agree to act diligently and in

good faith, and shall cooperate in the construction and completion of an underdrain system as

described in Contractor’s Bid, attached hereto as Exhibit C, and incorporated herein by

reference. Owner shall be responsible for oversight of day-to-day construction activities

affecting owner’s property. Riverton shall be responsible for oversight of day-to-day

construction activities affecting Lampton View Drive, and adjacent park strip and sidewalk.

Owner and Riverton each agree to convey temporary construction easements on property

controlled by each property, to the extent necessary to enable contractor to commence and

complete the work. Once said construction of a groundwater drain line is complete, owner shall

own the drain line as it is found Owner’s Property and shall diligently maintain the same.

Riverton shall own the drain line once the same enters Lampton View Drive, as established by

back of curb, and shall diligently maintain the same.

       3.      Consideration. Owner agrees to pay Contractor the amount of

_____________________ as consideration to complete the work required on Owner’s Property.

Riverton agrees to pay Contractor the amount of ________________ as consideration to

complete the work required within Riverton right of way for value of the easements described

herein and the value of trees and improvements located within the Easement Property. Amounts

paid under this Paragraph shall be due and payable no later than the date of 30 days from receipt

of invoice from Contractor. Invoices submitted by Contractor to the Parties shall delineate the

work performed on Owner’s Property, and work performed within Riverton Right of way.

       4.      Schedule of performance. The underwater drain line shall be completed by

Contractor in accordance with the time schedule set forth in Exhibit D incorporated herein by

reference (the “Schedule of Performance”), subject to force majeure. Owner and Riverton agree

set forth therefor is an essential part of this Agreement, time being of the essence.

       5.      Restrictions; Governmental Permits. No construction shall commence or be

maintained upon the land unless the same conforms to and are consistent with applicable zoning

ordinances and statutory laws applicable to the work being conducted (including without

limitation any construction or land disturbance permit or other license, permit, or certificate

required to be issued by any governmental authorities in connection with the work). Before

commencement of construction or development of the improvements, Contractor shall, secure all

regulatory approvals and all other applicable permits, licenses and other approvals which may be

required by Riverton, or any other governmental authority having jurisdiction over such

construction, development or work. Riverton shall have no implied obligation to cause such

permits to be issued other than in the ordinary course of governmental business.

       6.      Surety. Prior to the commencement of construction of any portion of the

improvements within the Easement Property, the parties shall cause Owner’s contractor to

purchase (a) a performance bond in an amount of not less than 100% of the cost of construction

of such portion of the improvements, as approved by the Parties, naming the Parties as additional

insureds and executed by a corporate surety authorized to conduct business as a surety in the

conduct business as a surety in the State of Utah and approved by Riverton.
