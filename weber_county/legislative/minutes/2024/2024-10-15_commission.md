                                                     MINUTES - SPECIAL MEETING
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                  Tuesday, October 15th, 2024 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: James “Jim” H. Harvey, Sharon Bolos, Gage Froerer
STAFF PRESENT: Jason Horne, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office; Courtlan Erickson, of the County Attorney’s
office; and Craig D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME: Chair Harvey
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Kristy Bingham
C. INVOCATION: Gary Myers
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: All of us have to make hard decisions in life. If we make
   decisions logically and for the right reasons, we can make decisions we can stand by with no regrets. Decisions
   based on criteria of popularity or personal gain can be harder to stand by and, quite frankly, we may not
   remember why we are making them in the first place. But if we make decisions logically and for what we believe
   to be are the right reasons, we can keep our integrity and stay whole to who we are.
