2.1 Proclamation Honoring the Herriman Boys Cross Country 
Team on their National Win

Herriman 2023 Proclamation (002)
Sponsors: Councilmember Dave Alvord. Second by Councilmember Sheldon 
Stewart, Councilmember Ann Granato, and Councilmember Laurie Stringham . 
(Approx. 4:00PM, 15 Min.)
Informational
Council Member Alvord stated he was thrilled to recognize the Herriman 
Boys Cross Country team on its national win.  He invited Coach Doug Soles 
to the podium to speak about the team ’s journey and what the event meant 
for the team.
Mr. Doug Soles , Head Coach, Herriman High School, stated he had a good 
group of kids.  The team members knew they would have to use different 
strategies and get out of their comfort zones, due to the extensive rain and 
mud in Portland, Oregon, where the contest took place.  Seeing the team pull 
off the victory was impressive.  He has seen a lot of growth and 
advancement among the team members across the board.  The members of 
the team put in between 15 and 20 hours a week in training.

CROSS COUNTRY TEAM AS NATIONAL CHAMPIONS
WHEREAS, on Saturday, December 2nd, the Herriman High School Varsity 
Boys Cross Country Team achieved an incredible 2023 Nike Cross National 
championship win; and
WHEREAS, the team of seven runners made history as the first in the state 
and southwest region to win the competition known as the “ultimate cross 
country experience”; and
WHEREAS, the team beat twenty two clubs to claim the “Winged Goddess 
of Victory” championship statue; and
WHEREAS, the boys completed the 5K race at just over a five -minute pace 
per mile; and
WHEREAS, the team was not ranked going into the 2022 season and had to 
overcome many obstacles to become the 2023 champions; and
WHEREAS, Herriman High School Cross Country Team Coach, Doug 
Soles, was “the right coach” for the team and spurred them on to victory; 
and
WHEREAS, the Herriman High School Varsity Boys Cross Country is 
officially a team of Champions.
NOW, THEREFORE, be it proclaimed that:
The Salt Lake County Council commends, congratulates, and gives special 
recognition to the Herriman High School Varsity Boys Cross Country Team 
on their extraordinary achievement as 2023 Nike Cross National Champions 
and wishes them success in their future endeavors.
