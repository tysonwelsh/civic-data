GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-7-2023 Petition by CHRISTOPHER CLIFFORD requesting a General Plan change from
           Mixed Use and Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Small Lot Residential
           (4 to 7 units/acre) and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½
           acre) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is located at
           5143 West 3500 South on 4.34 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545
           or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov) (Continued from the August 9th public hearing)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik stated that this item was continued from last weeks meeting, and he
              has yet to receive any new information. However, he will review the proposal.

              Commissioner Winters asked Commissioner McEwen to explain his concerns
              with this proposal. Commissioner McEwen explained that the twin homes would
              look similar, and different styles of single-family homes would surround this
              development. Therefore, it would not be a fit in this location. He stated that he
              also does not like the adjoining wall. Commissioner McEwen noted that the City
              has many developments, such as the one proposed here, and he would prefer to
              see more single-family homes being constructed.

              Commissioner Winters asked Steve how the proposed twin homes compare in
              size to a single-family home. Steve explained that our previous housing standards
              were 2,000 square feet for a rambler and 3,000 square feet for a multilevel
              structure. So, the proposed buildings would be smaller than those standards.

              Commissioner Winters asked what the current housing standards are. Steve stated
              that the ordinance states 1,000 square feet for a single-family or twin-home
              development. He pointed out that if the Commission wanted higher standards than
              those proposed, those could be added to a development agreement.

              Commissioner Winters said that we know the RM and RS zones are permitted on
              the subject property, and the only other zone would be the RE zone, correct?
              Steve said yes, and pointed out that single-family detached homes are permitted
              within the RM zone, which is being proposed. He noted that the applicant could
              have requested a density as high as 20 units/acre.


 CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
 C-16-2023 Petition by TGI CORP. requesting conditional use approval for the shopping
           center project Shoppes at Lake Park Lot 4. The property is located at 2893 S.
           5600 W. on 3.01 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial). (Staff –Jody
           Knapp at 801-963-3311 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that this proposal would be located on the two remaining
              pads of the Lake Park Shopping Center. She noted that residential and multi-
              family uses are to the north and east of the subject property. Jody reviewed the
              elevation concept for the existing shopping center and stated that the proposed
               elevations would blend in nicely. She mentioned that her recommendation would
               be to add a change in elevation to the north side. Jody pointed out the areas where
               landscaping would need some maintenance or replacement. She noted that trees
               would be required along the north side of the property. Jody also stated that the
               applicant would need to clean up portions of the site.


 C-17-2023     Petition by DAVIS WARNOCK requesting conditional use approval for
               office/warehouse flex buildings. The property is located at 5653 S 6055 W on
               2.64 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-
               963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion:    Brock Anderson explained that this proposal is for office/warehouse flex
               buildings with outside storage. He stated that there is an issue with the concept
               plan regarding building D. Brock explained that a 20-foot setback is required
               where they are showing the outside storage area, and they would need more room
               to accommodate both. He reviewed some options to resolve that issue. Brock
               noted that the applicant had initially wanted two access points; however, the
               Public Works Department commented that only one would be permitted. He
               pointed out that a second access is shown in the concept plan, but that would only
               be used by emergency vehicles and is required. Brock presented renderings of the
               proposed structures, mostly made of metal materials. He explained that if the
               applicant only proposed warehouse buildings, they would not need to meet the
               Commercial Design Standards. However, there would be office space, so that we
               would use a hybrid approach. Brock suggested some additions to the building
               design for the office space and street side elevations that the Commission may
               want to consider adding. He pointed out that a lot of fill has been brought onto the
               site, and the applicant would need to address that for grading and drainage. He
               mentioned they want to replace the berm with an 8-foot masonry wall.

               Brock asked the Commissioners if they had thoughts on the hybrid design
               standards. Commissioner Winters said he liked what Brock had suggested earlier
               in this meeting.

               Steve said if the Commission is comfortable holding the study meetings in the
               CED Conference room, we can continue to have the study meetings here. The
               Commission is in favor of that idea.




There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:06 p.m.
