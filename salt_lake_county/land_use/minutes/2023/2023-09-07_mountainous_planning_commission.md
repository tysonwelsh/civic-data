ADMINISTRATIVE APPLICATION(S) 
                                                  Hearing began at – 3:02 p.m. 
 
CUP2023-000897 – Nima Mahak , representing Snowbird Ski Resort, has requested a conditional use 
permit for a minor ski resort improvement for Snowbird Ski Resort. The proposal is for four avalanche 
mitigation towers (Wyssen Towers) that are to be located south of the resort base and just north of the peak 
of Mt. Baldy. Acreage: ~96 acres. Location: 9410 East Little Cottonwood Canyon Road.  Zone: FR-20. 
Planner: Morgan Julian (Motion/Voting) 
 
Commissioners confirmed they received the letter from Save Our Canyons and no further discussion 
needed. 
 
Motion: To approve , with staff recommendations, application #CUP2023-000897 Nima Mahak , 
representing Snowbird Ski Resort, has requested a conditional use permit for a minor ski resort 
improvement for Snowbird Ski Resort. The proposal is for four avalanche mitigation towers (Wyssen 
Towers) that are to be located south of the resort base and just north of the peak of Mt. Baldy. 
Motion by: Commissioner Knoblock 
2nd by: Commissioner Staker 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
