    1. INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH MARRIOTT-SLATERVILLE CITY TO DEFINE TERMS RELATED TO
       PARTICIPATION IN A COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREA PLAN AND COMMITMENT OF TAX
       INCREMENT FINANCING – CONTRACT C2015-156

         Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, cited critical components
         from the Marriott-Slaterville Community Development Project Area Plan: Development of an
         approximately 43-acre parcel at the northwest corner of 12th Street and I-15 into a commercial center
         intended to provide retail products and services to the public, bringing with it roughly 35-50 jobs.
         Marriott-Slaterville is asking the county’s participation to facilitate investment primarily for
         infrastructure. Marriott-Slaterville is requesting that the county provide a portion of the new property tax
         revenue that will be generated by this development for a 15-year period at a rate of 65%. Currently, the
         county receives $105/year from this 43-acre parcel. Mr. Larsen noted that at $1 million of increased
         value, it would generate $3,300/year and at $10 million in increased taxable value, it would be $33,000-
         $34,000/year. Based on current rates, and the proposed participation rate, the county would receive 35%
         of the new generated tax, and at $10 million (in increased taxable value) that would be roughly
         $11,000/year. Additionally, as a taxing entity the county is not receiving sales tax revenue from this
         property, and at the advertised sales volume of $50 million/year it would be nearly $150,000 annually just
         for the county’s general sales tax portion. If there is no increase in value, or the project does not come to
         fruition, the county does not lose anything. He said that Marriott-Slaterville anticipates initial investment
         of about $15 million and estimates about $50 million in annual retail sales. He addressed Commissioner
         Bell’s question stating that this request is for property tax only. Marriott-Slaterville is participating with
         65% of its new sales tax revenue.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-156, Interlocal Agreement with Marriott-
         Slaterville City to define terms related to participation in a Community Development Project Area Plan
         and commitment of tax increment financing; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Bell – aye; Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
