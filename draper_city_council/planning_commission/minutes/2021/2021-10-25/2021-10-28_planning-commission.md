2.       Public Hearing: NCS Residential Development Plat Amendment Request
         (Administrative Item)

A request by Tyler Bodrero of Pace CM, representing Draper School Development for Approval
of a plat amendment to allow for a 54-lot single-family residential development on 9.8 acres. The
property is located at approximately 12197 South 300 East in the RM2 (Multiple Family
Residential) zone. Application SUBD-094-2021. Staff contact: Maryann Pickering at 801-576-
6391 or maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

Maryann Pickering, Planner III, presented an aerial map of the property. Currently the property is
known as the Rasmussen farm property and is located behind McDonalds and Chick-Fil-A. Last
December they saw a site plan for a charter school and a rezone request to RM2. It had been
rezoned to RM2. This is a plat amendment and details were shown on a slide including details
pertaining to the proposed plat.

     •   54 single family homes on 9.8 acres
     •   One public road connecting 12200 South on the East to the 300 East roundabout
     •   Lots range in size from 4,063 to 9,844 square feet
     •   Proposal conforms to the development agreement approved by City Council.

She showed the layout of the roads and where it connected at 12200 S and that it looped around to
the roundabout. She noted that the other roads within the development would be private streets

3.     Public Hearing: RL Smith Zoning Map Amendment (Legislative Action)

On the request of Leone Smith for a Zoning Map Amendment from the RA2 (Residential
Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) zone to the R3 (Single-family Residential, 13,000
square foot lot minimum) zone on 0.92 acres of property located at 13530 South 300 East.

Application. TEXTMAP-0142-2021. Staff contact Jennifer Jastremsky: 801-576-6328 or
jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, presented the zoning map amendment for this property.
She noted it is located on the corner of 300 East and 13500 South. It is 0.92 acres and is in the
residential medium density land use designation. The applicant is requesting to amend the map
from RA2 to R3. She noted that there are other neighborhoods in the vicinity with that type of
density. She offered to answer questions.

Vice Chairman Hawker asked if it was feasible to get 3 lots out of this property.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, indicated that it was possible.

Commissioner Van Hoff asked for more clarification on lot size. He noted they cannot split it into
thirds.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, clarified that the property could be split into three lots if
all the structures where torn down, including the house. If the existing house stays, only two lots
would be possible on the property.

Commissioner Fowler asked about the lot size on Ireland Lane.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, replied that most lots were 6,000 sq. ft. and compared that
with the applicant’s proposal to build lots on 13,000 sq ft.

Leone Smith, applicant, spoke about her proposal to sell this property.

Vice Chairman Hawker asked about road access.

Leone Smith, applicant, stated that access will be off of 13560 South.

Vice Chairman Hawker opened and closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Nixon motioned to approve the Zoning Map Amendment request
from RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot minimum) to the R3 (Single-family
Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum) zone on 0.92 acres, located at 13530 South 300
East known as application TEXTMAP-0142-2021.

Second: Commissioner Squire.

Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 4-0 in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Fowler, Squire, Van Hoff, and Nixon voted, “Aye”.

On request of Ben Seastrand, representing Gardner Company, for a Text Amendment to the
Highline Commercial Special District in order to update the Exhibits to allow office use on a
portion of the property located at 14761 South Future Way. Application TEXTMAP-0149-2021,
Staff contact: Jennifer Jastremsky, 801-576-6328 or jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, presented the 70-acre map. The Text Amendment will
affect about 6.5 acres of the CSD. This is an amendment to the overall CSD. It is within the
Commercial Special District Land use. It is zoned Highline CSD. They are amending the exhibits
and not effecting the design standards. She showed a map, there was a designation for the office
and retail uses and they are not proposing to change that. The C area designation was for
residential, and retail uses, and they are amending this designation to residential, retail, office, and
hotel, and they will build an office on that area. She showed the original site plan that showed a
hotel and retail building. Then she showed an update to the Pluralsight building. The applicant was
proposing C and D areas to be a single office building. The existing walkability map was shown.
They had amended that to show cross access between the two properties. The amenities plan was
updated from the Pluralsight area and shows the office and the existing trailways. The ground
elevations were provided by the applicant. She showed a cross point to the property on the map.
She showed a video rendering of the property and what they are proposing.

Vice Chairman Hawker asked about the height restrictions in zoning of the proposed site.

Mark Murdock with the Gardner Company stated that the height of the building was within zoning
requirements, and that the zone allows for eight stories and they were building five.

Commissioner Squire asked if the initial designations were the city’s preference or the applicant’s
preference.

Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager, stated they were provided by the applicant.

Vice Chairman Hawker opened the public hearing and then closed it because there were no
comments.

Commissioner Fowler asked if there were any red flags about the height of the proposed buildings.

Vice Chairman Hawker replied that he didn’t have any issues with the height.

Motion: Commissioner Fowler motioned to send a positive recommendation to City Council
for the Zoning Map Amendment request from RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 ft²
minimum lot size) zone to the CN (Neighborhood Commercial) zone. Known as TEXTMAP-
0141-2021 based on the findings and subject to the conditions listed in the staff report dated
October 19, 2021.

Second: Commissioner Van Hoff

Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 4-0 in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Fowler, Squire, Nixon and Van Hoff voted, “Aye”.
