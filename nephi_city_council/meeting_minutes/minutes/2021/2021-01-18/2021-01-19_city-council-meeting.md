Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then
Councilor Justin Seely offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
   Five -time PRCA World Champion Bareback Rider Kaycee Feild, Genola, lead the Pledge of Allegiance.
Following the pledge, Mayor Nielson gave about 15 minutes for visiting and autographs. Mr. Feild then
took a few minutes to tell about himself and his career and his service to the troops. He was then
presented with several gifts. Jeff Hansen who owns J Bar Custom Flags and Woodworking and is a
former Nephi City police officer presented him a wooden flag from Nephi City that could hold Mr. Feild’s
belt buckles. Ute Stampede Committee Member Chris Memmott presented him with a jacket and hat,
and Councilor Jones gave him gift certificates from some local businesses and a stack of 2 dollar bills.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting held on 1-5-21, work session held on 1-5-21, work session held on 1-12-
21, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 1-19-21. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.


RATIFICATION OF HIRING TALON ORTON AS A PATROL OFFICER:

  Councilor Jones moved to ratify the hiring of Talon Orton as a Nephi City patrol officer. Councilor
Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote. Mr. Orton introduced himself to
the council and said that he graduated from the police academy in September 2020 and that he has
been attending Utah Valley University studying criminal justice. He has a wife and a small child and is
looking forward to serving Nephi City.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

ANNEXATION AREA MAP REQUEST DISCUSSION:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council three different options on how the council
could proceed with the annexation area map. Councilor Memmott spoke up and said that he thought
the council needed to get a framework of how the city wants to move forward to help guide them in an
orderly and methodical manner. He stated he was in favor of option C from the staff report which
stated the following:

                                                                                               Page 1 of 3
“ Wait to make a final decision on allowing additional properties into the city’s annexation area map
until additional information is received from some or all the following items:
                  a) The City’s General Plan Update - This would include conceptual zoning for the area
                  surrounding the city limits.
                  b) Transportation Master Plan - The plan could be expanded to include areas outside the
                  city’s current growth zone
                  c) Study to determine the benefits vs. costs of how far to extend the city’s utilities
                  before those costs outweigh the benefits to the city
                  d) Discussions with the County Commission and County Planning Commission and an
                  agreement achieved governing growth around Nephi City
                  e) Water rights from developers or fees to developers in lieu of water rights are
                  calculated and adopted by the City Council
                  f) Impact Fee studies are complete for impact fees not yet adopted (e.g. Parks &
                  Recreation, Police, Sewer, Natural Gas, Transportation)

  Waiting until these items are completed will give the Council many tools that can be helpful in making
decisions on whether to include more properties in the city’s annexation area map. They can also be
helpful for properties already included in the annexation area map.”

   Councilor Worwood, Ostler, and Seely all expressed their support for option C also. Land developer
Jim McWilliams asked if his property could be moved to a level 3 on the annexation area map during the
information gathering process. Councilor Jones answered that he didn’t understand why they were in
such a hurry. He stated that the council needed to look at everything and listen to everyone. The
council needs to slow down, not stop, but we need to rein ourselves in. He went on to say we have a
great community and we need to protect it for the future.

    Public Works Director Kyle Marchant added that the city is all about organized planning and not
pushing our utility departments to the point where they can’t provide the services needed. Councilor
Ostler said that there was a subdivision in the county next door to Mona that now puts Mona in a
difficult position for future growth. Councilor Memmott explained that the County code has been
revised to make it more difficult to develop subdivisions in the county. Memmott also stated that the
city needs to zone for larger lot sizes to help keep the rural feel of the community.
   The council agreed to direct staff to carry out option C from the staff report. Mr. Atkinson said that
the County officials are willing to meet about this issue on Monday or Wednesday of next week.

T SHEPHERD ANNEXATION PETITION APPROVED TO MOVE FORWARD:

   City Recorder Lisa Brough presented the T Shepherd annexation petition to the council. The petition
includes 28.97 acres of land located ½ mile south of the Four Mile/State Route 28 intersection, west of
the new water tank. Councilor Memmott moved to allow the T Shepherd Annexation petition to move
forward in the annexation process. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

NEPHI SOUTH COMMERCIAL PARK ANNEXATION ORDINANCE 01-19-2021 ADOPTED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 01-19-2021: AN ORDINANCE ANNEXING CERTAIN
PROPERTIES TO NEPHI CITY AND ESTABLISHING ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR SAID PROPERTY.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote. The Nephi South
Commercial Park Annexation consisted of 72.88 acres with 95% of the property owned by Nephi City.
The approximate location is 3000 South 400 West. The property was annexed into the ID-Industrial
Zone.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the saddle was successfully welded onto the main gas line.
   Councilor Seely reported that the Information Fair organized by the library staff will be held Saturday,
January 20, 2021 from 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon. He also reported that Library Board Member Bonnie
Ingram had resigned from the board due to her relocation to Idaho.
   Councilor Ostler stated that the firework display for the State’s birthday earlier in January had been lit
by a technician and not the fire department. The Fire Chief would like to hire a technician to do the
fireworks at the Fourth of July celebration this summer.
   Councilor Jones said that he had been approached by two businesses asking about the CARES grants.
