Magna in Motion

                Keri Duckworth stated Magna in Motion received feedback about the October
challenge and learned that a lot of people liked the 30-day challenge. So, the next three
challenges are for the entire month. In April, the focus will be on nutrition and maybe activity.
Then, the May challenge will focus on mental and social, and the June challenge will emphasize
some aspect of health and include a weight loss challenge. The cost for each is $10, but if a
participant brings in a new referral, they will get $5 off for each one, up to two referrals. New
participants can get the same incentive for May and June. Then, a participant who does all three
challenges, will get a $5 discount. Magna in Motion is also responsible for the 5k this year and
will be doing early registration soon.

              Council Member Hull asked if people could register online.

               Ms. Duckworth stated people can pay online at magnainmotion@gmail.com using
PayPal, but will get a registration packet in the mail.

Fee Waiver Request

                 Greg Schulz reviewed the request of the Utah AFL-CIO for a fee waiver of $1,150
for park rental fees assessed by Salt Lake County for the use of Magna Copper Park for the 2018
Annual Labor Day Picnic and Car Show. Last year, the fee was increased to $1,500, but the Utah
AFL-CIO had not been informed of a fee increase and had only budgeted $350.

              Council Member Peel asked why the fee went up.

              Mr. Schulz stated the fees increase every year, but Salt Lake County Parks and
Recreation Division also charged the Utah AFL-CIO an additional amount for the use of the
pavilions.

              Council Member Peel asked how much the fee would have been based on the
schedule the Council just adopted.

              Mr. Schulz stated it probably would have been $500-$600.

                Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to waive the fee
of $1,150, but inform the Utah AFL-CIO of the new fee schedule for this year. The motion passed
unanimously.
