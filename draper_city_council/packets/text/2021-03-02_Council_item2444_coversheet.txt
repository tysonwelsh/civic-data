Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Scott Cooley, Pubic Works Director
CC:  Date: March 2, 2021
Re: Ordinance 1480 Comments: Draper City is currently collecting a storm water impact fee.  Since the fee was authorized, there have been changes to storm water management regulations which have impacted the way developments manage storm water.  These changes have significantly reduced the need for system improvements and future capital facilities to be funded by storm water impact fees.  Staff is working on a final analysis of future needs but feels collection of the storm water impact fee is not needed at this time.
