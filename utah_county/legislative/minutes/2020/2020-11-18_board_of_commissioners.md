THINGS THAT WE ARE SEEING THIS YEAR. WITH THE ASSISTANCE OF OUR VOCA
GRANT, WE ARE IN THE PROCESS OF CREATING A SOFT INTERVIEW ROOM AND

PROSECUTORS IN OUR OFFICE.

CASES SO FAR THIS YEAR. SINCE MARCH OF THIS YEAR WITH THE PANDEMIC
WE HAD TO PREPARE OVER 250 PROTECTIVE ORDERS OR NO CONTACT ORDERS
FOR DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND SEXUAL ASSAULT VICTIMS. WE HAVE ALSO
SEEN AT 15% INCREASE IN CALLS THROUGH SEPTEMBER OF THIS YEAR AND WE
STILL HAVE MORE TIME TO GO.

I KNOW THE COURTS AS YOU’RE AWARE, HAVE SHUT DOWN BASICALLY SINCE
MARCH, BUT THIS HAS NOT STOPPED OUR WORKLOAD FROM INCREASING. WE
ARE SEVERELY UNDERSTAFFED. WE CURRENTLY HAVE TWO FULL-TIME
EMPLOYEES THAT ARE PAID FOR BY THE COUNTY AND ONE VOCA GRANT
EMPLOYEE AND WE’RE CURRENTLY SEARCHING FOR SOMEONE TO FILL THAT
POSITION. WITH THE ADDITION OF ALL THE NEW PROSECUTORS IN OUR OFFICE
THAT HAS SIGNIFICANTLY INCREASED OUR WORKLOAD AS THEY ARE MORE
CAPABLE OF HANDLING THEIR CASES, THEY NEED OUR ASSISTANCE MORE SO
WE ARE TRYING TO IMPLEMENT A VOLUNTEER PROGRAM AND WE’RE
WORKING HARD TO FIND VOLUNTEERS THAT CAN ASSIST US IN OUR ROLES, BUT
THEY CANNOT MAKE UP OR REPLACE A TRAINED VICTIM COORDINATOR TO
ASSIST THEM THROUGH THIS PROCESS.
