Training Presentation .
1. Best Practices for Conducting Municipal Planning Commission Meetings —
Wilf Sommerkorn (Utah Land Institute)
Chair Don Russell welcomed participants attending both in person and online. Attendees
hailed from various planning commissions including Emigration Canyon, Salt Lake
County, Cottonwood Heights, Brighton, White City, Kearns City, and West Valley City.
Dina Blaes, Taylorsville’s Chief of Strategic Engagement introduced Mr. Sommerkorn,
describing his extensive background in planning, including roles as Planning Director for
Davis County, Salt Lake City, and Director of Regional Planning and Transportation at
Salt Lake County. He had been involved with the Utah Chapter of the American Planning
Association since 1990 and currently served on the Kaysville Planning Commission.
The training covered several key topics:
e The importance of bylaws in guiding commission operations
e Explanation of administrative vs. legislative actions and their different requirements
e Effective meeting management
e Preparing for meetings with staff review of agenda items
