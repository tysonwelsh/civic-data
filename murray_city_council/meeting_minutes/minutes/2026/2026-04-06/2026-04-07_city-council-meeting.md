Citizen Comments:
  Kathy Houston – Murray Resident.
  Ms. Houston thanked the Council for supporting the Murray Senior Center, praised its clean and healthy
  environment, and the variety of classes offered. She said senior artwork would soon be displayed at City Hall
  and suggested expanding the center to accommodate individuals on waiting lists for art classes.

 Sue Williams – Murray Resident
 Ms. Williams expressed concern about a neighbor who became a safety risk due to drug addiction. She stated
 the home had been condemned by the Board of Health and had become a drug house with high traffic. She
 requested the house be boarded up.

 James Lamb – Murray Resident
 Mr. Lamb was frustrated despite a meth house being foreclosed and condemned by the Health Department. He
 stated that he and his neighbors still feel unsafe after contacting Murray Police three times about ongoing
 issues. Neighbors are repeatedly harassed by individuals coming and going from the house, break-ins are
 continual and drug activity is still happening. He questioned why Code Enforcement was unaware of the
 foreclosure or why the City has not helped to board up the house or demolish it.

 Cassie Lamb – Murray Resident
 Ms. Lamb raised concerns about a meth house, describing it as unsafe, atrocious, poorly maintained, with debris

 Health and City agencies and no solutions to longstanding drug activity. She urged the City to board up the

 illegal backyard sheds were addressed while the condition of the house was neglected. He also raised concern

 Christy Arnold – Riverton Resident
 Ms. Arnold asked the City to close City Corner Store at 4300 South and Main, saying the owner had a criminal
 history and was selling alcohol, vapes, and THC to her underage son. She reported that police told her they could

 Mr. Arnold urged the City to revoke the business license of the City Corner Store, citing concerns that his great-

2. Consider an ordinance vacating two municipal utility easements located at approximately 4816 South
   State Street and 4837 South Poplar Street, Murray City, Salt Lake County, State of Utah. Community and
   Economic Development Director Chad Wilkinson presented a plat map showing two Murray Power utility
   easements in the center of Block One that must be vacated. Mr. Wilkinson stated that all other easements
   held by private parties must also be vacated as part of ongoing efforts to clean up the site and prepare for
   construction. He reviewed the construction timeline in the Rockworth development agreement to confirm
   that the project was on schedule, building permit applications had been submitted, plan reviews were
   completed, and next steps are to issue building permits and begin construction within 60 days. He confirmed

   that the Council approved the initial plan exactly one year ago to highlight significant progress.

Business Item:
 1. Consider a resolution authorizing and approving the amended and restated Power Pooling Agreement
     with the Utah Associated Municipal Power Systems for the pool project; authorizing execution and
     delivery thereof; and related matters. Mr. Bellon gave a review about the UAMPS (Utah Associated
     Municipal Power Systems) organization and the Pool Project, noting that UAMPS members were not
     required to purchase all power from UAMPS.

     He explained why the resolution was necessary reminding Council Members that UAMPS was part of the
     PacifiCorp East Balancing Authority Area and because PacifiCorp joined EDAM (Extended Day-Ahead
     Market) effective May 1, 2026, UAMPS was mandated to participate in EDAM also. Because UAMPS must
     participate in EDAM, the Pooling Agreement must be amended and restated to modernize a 40 year old
     process and make required operational changes.

     Mr. Bellon outlined the major differences between the 1980 agreement and the new amended and restated
     2026 agreement, pointed out other agreement provisions and emphasized that UAMPS participants can
     elect to withdraw their authorization for UAMPS to make purchases on their behalf in the Annual Purchase
     Plan.
