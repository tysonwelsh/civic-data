OGDEN VALLEY PLANNING COMMISSION                                                                            July 7, 2020

                 Minutes of the Ogden Valley Planning Commission for July 7, 2020. To join the meeting please navigate to the
                 following weblink at https://us02web.zoom.us/j/87315485682 the time of the meeting; commencing at 4:30
                 p.m.

       Present: John Lewis, Chair; John Howell, Chris Hogge, Shanna Francis, Jeffry R. Burton, Ron Lackey, and Steve Waldrip

       Staff Present: Rick Grover, Planning Director; Charlie Ewart, Principal Planner; Steve Burton, Planner, Scott Perkes, Planner;
       Courtlan Erickson, Legal Counsel; Angela Martin, Lead Office Specialist

                             Call
                             Pledge of Allegiance


                        Regular Items:

                        1. Election of Vice Chair for the Ogden Valley Planning Commission.

                            Commissioner Howell made a motion to Elect Commissioner Francis as Vice Chair. Commissioner Hogge
                            seconded the motion. Chair Lewis, Commissioners Burton, Hogge, Howell, Waldrip and Lackey voted aye.
                            (Motion carried)



                        Legislative Items:

                              1.    ZMA 2020-01: Consideration and action on a proposal to rezone approximately 15 acres of
                                   land located at 4706 E. 2650 N. from AV-3 to MV-1. Presenter: Charlie Ewert

                                   Director Grover would like to open this item to public comment.
                                   Mr. Ewert presented the proposed rezone to the Planning Commission. The details for this
                                   rezone is in the Staff Report.
                                   Director Grover discussed that they set down with the developer and they submitted the
                                   petition. If the applicant does not rezone this property, it will go back to the current zoning.
                                   There was a discussion on being too close to the river and Eden Water Works plant. They also
                                   discussed what if the boats do not drain before they store them, also how are they going to
                                   prevent quagga mussel concerns.
                                   Commissioner Lackey asked if there would be a dump station on site. The applicant said that
                                   was not proposed in this rezone.

                                   Closed public comment.

                                   Commissioner Burton went through the conditions in the staff report. He does not think that
                                   the applicant should be putting all his money into some of these conditions in the staff report.
                                   He thinks this is a good thing for the Ogden Valley.

                                   Chair Lewis commented that overall that over 120 units for storage units. He thinks that this
                                   would keep boats out of driveways. He also would like to change the two homes in that CV-2
                                   zone to be the same. Asphalt work better than pavers. The asphalt will be easier not to drain
                                   into the ground.

                                   Commissioner Waldrip made a motion to deny ZMA 2020-01. There was a discussion on why
                                   Commissioner Waldrip moved to deny this rezone. He said part of the issue is that we are
                                   approaching this at the wrong standpoint. He does not like where this site is. This is not the long
                                   term best use for this property. This does not feel like this goes with the General Plan. He feels like

                       2.   ZTA 2018-05: Public hearing to discuss and take comment on a proposal to amend the following
                            sections of Weber County Code: §102-1-5 and §102-5, regarding rezoning procedures and
                            legislative amendments. Presenter: Steve Burton

                                Mr. Burton presented to the Planning Commission the changes to amend the Weber County
                                Code. The Planning Commission had some changes and requested to see those changes at the
                                next meeting.

                                Commissioner Burton made a motion to table ZTA 2018-05: Public hearing to discuss and take
                                comment on a proposal to amend the following sections of Weber County Code: §102-1-5 and
                                §102-5, regarding rezoning procedures and legislative amendments. Commissioner Waldrip
                                seconded the motion. Commissioner Howell, Francis, Hogge, Chair Lewis, and Lackey voted
                                aye. (Motion carried)



                Adjourn to Work Session

                     WS1: Discussion about short-term rentals in Weber County and regulatory options.
                     Presenter: Scott Perkes

                     Mr. Perkes provided a presentation and lead a discussion regarding short-term rentals and how they are
                     currently impacting the Ogden Valley and how potential regulation may be needed in the near future.
                     Commissioners provided comments on the topic based on their collective experience with short-term
                     rentals they know are currently in operation and their impacts. Mr. Perkes indicated that a draft short-
                     term rental ordinance has been drafted by staff and will be presented to the commission for their review
                     in the next couple of weeks.


          Commissioner Hogge made a motion to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Francis seconded the motion.
          Chair Lewis, Commissioners Burton, Hogge, Howell, Francis, and Waldrip all in favor voted aye. (Motion carried)


      The meeting adjourned at 8:30 pm
