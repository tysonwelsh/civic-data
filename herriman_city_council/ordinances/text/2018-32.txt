HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2018-32

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2 OF THE HERRIMAN CODE OF
ORDINANCES ENTITLED BOARDS AND COMMISSIONS

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (“Council”) met in regular session on October 4,
2018, to consider, among other things, amending Title 2of the Herriman Code of
Ordinances entitled Boards and Commissions; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council finds that it is in the best interest of
the health, safety, and welfare of the inhabitants of Herriman to amend Title 2 of the Herriman
Code of Ordinances entitled Boards and Commissions.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that Title 2; is amended as
follows (designated by interlineating the words to be deleted and underlining the words to be
added).

Chapter 8
Veterans and Military Advisory Committee

2-8-1: CREATION:

2-8-2: DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

2-8-3: TERMS, COMPENSATION AND VACANCY:
2-8-4: ORGANIZATION, QUORUM AND COMMITTEES:
2-8-5: CITY STAFF SUPPORT:

2-8-6: MEETINGS:

2-8-7: LIMITATIONS:

2-6-1: CREATION:
There is hereby created a committee known as the Herriman Veterans and Military
Advisory Committee.

2-6-2: DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

The Herriman Veterans and Military Advisory Committee shall:

A. Make recommendations to the City Council with respect to veterans and military
service member related issues, ceremonies and other activities that occur within Herriman

City;

B. Provides a forum for discussion of issues relating to veterans and military families:

C. Acts as a conduit of communication and coordination between the local veteran and
military community:

|

D. Shall aid the City in the planning, development and implementation of veteran and
military community related special activities and programs in the City.

2-8-3: TERMS, COMPENSATION AND VACANCY:

The veterans and military advisory committee shall be comprised of at least three (3)
members, appointed by the City Council. Such members shall be appointed in a manner
providing balance in geographic, professional, neighborhood and community interests’
representation. Members of the veterans and military advisory committee may serve a
maximum of three (3) consecutive terms of three (3) years each. Notwithstanding, the
terms of the first members shall be staggered so that all member's terms do not expire in
the same year. Vacancies may be filled by the City Council, to any unexpired term or for
the full term as the case may be. Members of the veterans and military advisory
committee shall serve without compensation.

2-8-4: ORGANIZATION, QUORUM AND COMMITTEES:

A. Quorum: More than fifty percent (50%) of the veterans and military advisory
committee present shall constitute a quorum for conducting business of the veterans and
military advisory committee and the action can be had upon the vote of the majority

present.

B. Organization: The veterans and military advisory committee may elect a Chair,
organize committees, and adopt administrative rules, bylaws and procedures necessary to
accomplish its purpose. The veterans and military advisory committee shall submit such
rules, bylaws and procedures to the City Council for approval.

2-8-5: CIFY STAFF SUPPORT:

The City shall provide the committee with such administrative support staff and materials
as it deems necessary to assist in carrying out the duties and responsibilities of the
committee.

2-8-6: MEETINGS:

The meetings of the veterans and military advisory committee shall be open to the public.
2-8-7: LIMITATIONS:

The veterans and military advisory committee shall not have authority to bind the City or
impose any condition on the City or its citizens and is not vested with authority to make
decisions regarding the public's business.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 10" day of October, 2018.

HERRIMAN CITY

David Watts, Mayor
