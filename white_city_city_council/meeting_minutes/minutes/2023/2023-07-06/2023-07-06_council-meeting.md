                                            Workshop

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)
Update

                Paul Ashton stated UPD is moving forward with putting together different models
of policing. Chief Jason Mazuran is responsible for reorganizing UPD. Chief Mazuran is looking
at a three-city model or three-city and SLVLESA model. The law enforcement bureau is being set
up, and contracts are being reviewed. Salt Lake County’s human resources are going to get
estimates on how much it will cost to hire officers. UPD and Salt Lake County are understanding
the role of SLVLESA as an independent district.

                                   −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Walk White City Final Plan

               Erin O’Kelley, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD),
presented the final draft of the Walk White City Plan. She reviewed the summary of the pedestrian
network plan, existing conditions and community input, community feedback and alternate
scenarios, pedestrian network concept, Larkspur Drive example concept, examples of new
community places, implementation plan, and project prioritization.

In addition, the State approved the landscaping ordinance, and it meets the requirements. She
met with Parks and Recreation to discuss the Larkspur trailhead project. There is a lot of positive
feedback for this project. The Dimple Dell Master Plan is being drafted and she can work in
coordination with the plan to create the trailhead project.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Chief Ken Aldridge stated the UFA working group has begun working on the
strategic plan. UFA will be looking for participation from the White City Metro Township Council
or its designees. Wildland fire season has had a slow start, so crews have been working in various
communities doing fuel mitigation. An education trailer that will provide hands-on experience was
purchased. He hopes to have it available for Southeast Township Days. Clint Mecham,
Emergency Management, has retired and moved to Florida.

               Chief Jeff Bassett reviewed the calls for service in White City. The reports that
are sent out will continue to be upgraded with improved graphics. Seven new firefighters
graduated from camp and another four will be added. Station 31 is going through its design phase.
Sandy City is looking to sell the property at 9010 South 150 East.

               Council Member Perry asked if the goal of three-handed crews is still moving
forward.

               Chief Bassett stated all crews are three-handed and he will now be pushing to
increase them to four-handed crews. He is looking at staffing an additional ambulance and next
budget year fire stations, fleet, and employees will be reviewed.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                  Detective Josh Smith reviewed calls for service for the month of June. The
majority of calls coming in are public order calls, things like ambulance and citizen assistance,
civil situations, and welfare checks. He worked with the graffiti team, they finished cleaning up the
bridge. The speed trailer is out, and he is accepting requests to move it to different locations. The
motors will be out doing speed and traffic enforcement.

              Chief Randy Thomas stated a three-year-old drowned in a swimming pool at a
Midvale apartment complex. People need to practice water safety to avoid such tragedies. He
has been working on getting a directed enforcement unit sergeant for the last few years. A
sergeant was funded and has begun working on special projects.

Public Comments

                Howard Van Horn introduced himself stating he filed to run for the White City
Metro Township Council. He has lived in White City for 54 years. He has worked in government
