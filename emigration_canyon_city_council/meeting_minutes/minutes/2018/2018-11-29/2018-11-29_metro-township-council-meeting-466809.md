Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Captain Michael Conn provided a handout regarding holiday season safety.
Conversations are ongoing about UFA using existing cisterns when responding to situations. The
cisterns are hard to locate, and the access point has a different thread on the connections and
the hoses cannot be connected. The fire marshal has a code on how the cisterns should be
designed and maintained. The existing cisterns have been there since the 1980’s and have not
been maintained. UFA would not want to use them during a house fire, its own water would be
brought in and hydrants will be used.

Emigration Canyon Metro Township General Plan
Mayor Smolka stated the Council needs an update on Emigration Canyon Metro
Township General Plan so grant applications can be processed.
Council Member Brems stated it has been six years since the General Plan has
been updated. A general plan committee should be established with members of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission, Community Council, and a Council Member, with
the process starting next year.
David Church stated the general plan needs to include land use, transportation,
moderate income housing, and other various elements like recreation. The Planning Commission
will hold public hearings and then make a recommendation to the Council, at that time the Council
can approve, deny, or amend the general plan. Many entities hire an outside consultant to help
on the general plan.
Council Member Brems stated the current general plan is highly personalized to
fit Emigration Canyon. If funds are available to hire a consultant that would be beneficial and help
with a better public process.
Mayor Smolka stated the Council has $12,000 for a consultant that can be carried
over into 2019.
Council Member Paine, seconded by Council Member Brems, moved to direct the
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission to start updating and amending the
general plan.
Council Member Bowen, seconded by Council Member Brems, amended the motion to
direct the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission to start updating and
amending the general plan and to instruct the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning
Commission to be apprised of Utah State Code as an outline of what the Council expects of them.
The motion passed unanimously.
