Title 2, Chapter 2.56 – Community Districts & Community Councils Ordinance
The Council reviewed the following Ordinance amending chapter 2.56 of the
current code concerning community councils in the township:
ORDINANCE NO. 19-05-01

DATE: May 23, 2019

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 2.56 OF THE CURRENT APPLICABLE
CODE CONCERNING COMMUNITY COUNCILS IN THE TOWNSHIP
RECITALS
A. Utah State Code section 10-2a-414 provides that when a metro township is incorporated
that each county ordinance that is in effect on the day that metro township is incorporated
will remain in effect as a metro township ordinance until the metro township council
amends or repeals the ordinance.
B. The current code applicable in the Township establishes procedures and processes
regarding the establishment of a community council in the Township.
C. The Township Council desires to amend the provisions to fit the form of government in the
Township and preserve the existing community council for the Township.
ORDINANCE
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:
I.

The current code Title 2 Chapter 2.56 – COMMUNITY GREATER SALT LAKE
MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT AND COMMUNITY COUNCIL is hereby amended to
read as found in the attached Exhibit A. All provisions of the current Chapter 2.56 that are
not shown in the attached exhibit are repealed.

II.

This ordinance shall take effect upon publication and posting as required in Utah Code
10-3-711.

III.

The Salt Lake County Clerk acting for the Township and municipal recorder/clerk pursuant
to Utah Code 10-3c-201(1)(a)(ii) is hereby authorized to provide appropriate title, chapter,
part, and section numbers for the provision of this ordinance and pursuant to Utah Code
10-3-713 shall record, in a book used exclusively for that purpose, this ordinance, along
with all ordinances passed by the Emigration Canyon Metro Township.

IV.

The recorder shall give this ordinance a number, if the Township Council has not already
so done.

V.

Immediately following this ordinance, the recorder shall make or cause to be made a
certificate stating the date of passage and of the date of publication or posting, as required.

Legal Issues – Franchise Agreements
David Church presented the following two franchise agreements one from
Dominion Energy and the other from Rocky Mountain Power. These agreements have been
operating prior to the metro townships existence, with Salt Lake County. The agreements to be
updated to reflect the metro townships name.
Mayor Smolka stated some language in the agreement says town not metro
township. It also indicates $50 will be paid to the metro township upon acceptance of the
agreement. Should the amount be amended to $500 to cover legal costs?
Mr. Church stated the Council can approve the agreements with an amendment
to the amount being paid to the metro township.
 Dominion Energy
Council Member Paine, seconded by Council Member Brems, moved to approve
the franchise agreement with Dominion Energy with amendments that include changing “town” to
“Metro Township” and changing the amount paid from $50 to $500. The motion passed
unanimously.
