                   UPDATES TO CODE FOR NUISANCE LEGAL ACTION

                                CITY OF BLUFFDALE,UTAH

                                   ORDINANCE NO.2023-15




AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 11.300.060(B) OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY
CODE RELATED TO LEGAL ACTION FOR NUISANCES




WHEREAS the City of Bluffdale maintains criminal ordinances not covered by state code;
WHEREAS the City desires to protect the health and welfare ofthe community;
WHEREAS the City desires to amend its ordinance to better notify residents of nuisances
requiring remediation;

WHEREAS the City Council finds that the proposed amendment will further the public health,
safety, and general welfare;



NOW,THEREFORE,THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL HEREBY ORDAINS AS
FOLLOWS:


1L300.060(B^


        Criminal Action: It shall be unlawful for any person to maintain or assist in maintaining a
nuisance after receiving notice to abate the nuisance. Notice to abate a nuisance shall be given as
a prerequisite to prosecution by delivering a copy ofthe notice to abate to the offender by personal
service in the manner described in the Utah rules of civil procedure, or by mailing a copy of the
notice to abate the offending partv bv certified mail, return receipt requested mailing the notice of
violation to the person*s last known address, posting the notice conspicuouslv on or in front ofthe
propertv occupied bv the person, or delivering a copv of the notice of violation to the individual
personallv. The notice shall be considered received on the third dav after the date of mailing or
posting. The notice to abate shall reasonably describe the nuisance and the steps necessary to abate
the nuisance.
