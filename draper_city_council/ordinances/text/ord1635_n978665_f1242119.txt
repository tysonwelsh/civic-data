City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1635

On March 4, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1635 amending the
text of the Draper City General Plan to add the Draper Town Center Station Area Plan.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 6th day of March 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
