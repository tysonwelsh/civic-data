                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 24-55

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT BE ISSUED TO
    BLUE RIVER CONSTRUCTION TO REBUILD THE TWO JORDAN RIVER
                WALKING PATHS, DECKS AND RAILINGS

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

         WHEREAS, the two walking paths and decks that are located on the Jordan River Trail
are failing and need repair; and

        WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to start the project now using the
money left in the Fiscal Year 2024 Jordan River Trail Improvement budget and finish the project
using the Fiscal Year 2025 budget; and

     WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve this in a future budget
amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council approves a Purchase Order Contract be issued to Blue River
          Construction to rebuild the two Jordan River walking paths, decks and railings.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 4th day of
June, 2024 by the following vote:
