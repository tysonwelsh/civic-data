FINDINGS OF FACT:  
1. The LV1A parcel is within the Lower Village development area of the Canyons 
SPA.  
2. According to the current assessor’s parcels PP-102-C-2 and PP-102-B-3-A 
information, TCFC Propco, LLC is the owner of record for parcels, also known as 
LV1-A.  
3. An application for the Lower Village Development Area Lot LV1A Amended 
Subdivision Plat and the Canyons Village Team Building Final Site Plan and 
Condominium plat was submitted on April 5, 2017.  
4. TCFC Propco, LLC has authorized Spencer White, Replay Resorts to process the 
applications. 6 Lower Village Master Development Lot LV1A Plat & Canyons 
Village Team Building FSP– June 13, 2017  
5. The LV1-A parcel is zoned Specially Planned Area (SPA) and is within the 
Canyons SPA boundaries.  
6. The parcel is allowed to be a maximum of 2-3 stories in height, as described in 
the Canyons Development Agreement.  
7. The applicant has proposed to subdivide lot LV1-A into two lots.  
8. The applicant is proposing to construct a 12,932 square foot employee team 
building as part of the Canyons Resort operations and support.  
9. The Canyons DRC made a formal recommendation for the Canyons Village Team 
Building Final Site Plan and Condominium on April 4, 2017.  
 
CONCLUSIONS OF LAW:  
1. The proposed Lower Village Development Master Plat Lot LV1-A and the 
Canyons Village Team Building Final Site Plan complies with all requirements of 
the Snyderville Basin Development Code and the Canyons DA.  
2. The proposed resort operations and support structure, as conditioned, is 
consistent with the Snyderville Basin General Plan, as amended.  
3. The use is not detrimental to public health, safety and welfare, as the roads and 
public services in the area are sufficient to accommodate the increase in 
intensity of the use.  
4. The use is compatible with the existing neighborhood character and will not 
adversely affect surrounding land uses.

DRC UPDATES 
 
Commissioner Stevens said the Kimball Junction DRC focused at the last meeting on 
the east side of Highway 224.  The discussion centered on open space to mitigate the 
vast area of parking lots.  The group seemed to come together.   
 
Director Putt said they want to avoid bifurcating the community on the two sides of 
Highway 224.  It was noted that connectivity between the two sides does not exist.     
 
 
DIRECTOR ITEMS 
 
 
Director Putt reminded the Commission of the upcoming joint meeting with the County 
Council.  Director Putt said the County Manager approved the amendments to the (F6) 
Frostwood Final Site Plan and Condominium Plat.  He found they met the necessary 
standards.  In regard to them moving forward with construction before getting the 
plans approved, he applied a double fine on each of the units.     
 
Director Putt acknowledged that Commissioner DeFord had to resign due to conflicts 
with his work schedule.  Advertisements to fill his seat are out.  Chair Peck said he will 
be missed.       
 
ADJOURN
