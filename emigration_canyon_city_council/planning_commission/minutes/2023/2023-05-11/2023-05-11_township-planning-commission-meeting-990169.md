PUBLIC HEARING(S)
Hearing began at – 8:35 a.m.
OAM2022-000715 – Emigration Canyon Planning Commission will be holding a public hearing to hear
public comments on recommended amendments to the Emigration Canyon Municipal Code: 19.73.110
Night Lighting. The proposed ordinance language updates will update the existing language within the
Municipal Code as a subsection of the current Chapter 19.73 Foothills and Canyons Site Development and
Design Standards. Addressing Night Lighting Standards was identified as an action item from the 2021
Emigration Canyon General Plan. They are intended to help maintain the rural atmosphere and community
character of the Metro Township, protect ecological conditions and nocturnal wildlife within the Metro
Township, maintain nighttime safety, utility, and security, and encourage lighting practices that promote
energy conservation. Planner: Matt Starley (Motion/Voting)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Erin O’Kelley and Matt Starley provided an analysis
of the Ordinance requested changes.
Basement – Change to “An area of a building” to “The area of a building.” Change throughout the
ordinance.
BUG Rating – Greater than 60-degrees from Nadir. Add lines to graphic figure.
Candela – Intensity instead of strength.
Cut-off – spelling different throughout the document, should be cutoff throughout.
Full-cutoff fixture– add dash between fully and shielded in Fully shielded fixture. Eliminate the last sentence
after Nadir.
Glare – remove visual performance or.
Lumen – accept Commissioner Karkut’s change.
Religious Institutions Property – add S to structure, so structures.
Security Lights- accept Commissioner Karkut’s change.
19.73.112 C. – Amortization with Commissioner Karkut’s change using the word compliant spell out five
(5).
D.1.b – accept Commissioner Karkut’s change
D.2 – accept Commissioner Karkut’s change
E.1 – accept Commissioner Karkut’s change 2, last requirement singular
