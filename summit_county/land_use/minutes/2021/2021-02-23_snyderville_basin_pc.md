2. Discussion regarding possible amendments to Chapter 4, Application and 
Processes in the Snyderville Basin Development Code Amendments-  Jennifer 
Strader, Senior Planner   
 
Planner Strader said that changes were made to this Chapter by Staff to ensure 
consistency.  This is the chapter someone would go to if that person wanted to figure 
out how to apply for a Conditional Use Permit.  Staff tried to organize it in a way that 
would be user-friendly.  Anything dealing with processes was moved to this chapter. 
Planner Strader said most of the changes are not significant; however, one change that 
is significant is to eliminate the Board of Adjustment.  They are proposing to replace 
Snyderville Basin Planning Commission 
February 23, 2021 
Page 4 of 34 
 
this Board with a land-use hearing officer.  Planner Strader said there are a few reasons 
for this change.  One is that they have a difficult time trying to fill spots for the BOA.  
Another reason is there is a trend across the Country to have land-use hearing officer 
act as an appellant authority, relieving some pressure on the County Council.  This will 
also provide a more streamlined process in the case of variances.  This would be an 
appointed position.   
 
Planner Strader said that for this meeting, Staff would like to know if there are any 
areas the Commission would like to change.  The goal is to provide a streamlined user-
friendly Code.  Most of the changes are comprised of a lot of re-organization.   
 
Chair Dickey asked Planner Strader to describe what kind of appeals would the land-
use officer handle.  Is there any concern that one person would have too much power in 
this process.  That person could override decisions made by the County Manager.  
Planner Strader said that is one of the things that need to be talked through.  She said 
this could be an appeal of the Community Development Director decision, or a Planning 
Commission's decision.  Anything that would go to the County Council would now go to 
the land-use authority.   
 
Attorney Brackin added that this person would be an attorney trained in land-use law.  
They would hear appeals of Conditional Use Permits, plat amendments, or anything the 
Board of Adjustments would normally hear.  An appeal to the County Council generally 
takes more than one meeting and several hours.     
 
Commission Questions and Comments   
Commissioner Conabee said he thinks it is great to streamline the process, but he 
thinks if the Code is refined, with the Planning Commission being given more teeth, this 
may not be needed.  He is not in favor of a Staff Member being given this responsibility.  
To have a single person make this decision seems to be a dangerous position.

Commissioner Cooke said he agrees with Commissioner Conabee.  The idea that one 
person could decide on something that is controversial with the public, doesn't seem

Commissioner Kucera asked Attorney Brackin where an appeal of this person's

have the Planning Commission's input as well.   
 
Chair Dickey said he can see an argument for both sides.  He leans toward having the 
County Council as the appeal body, the way it is set up now.  If the County Council 
decides to make this change, he wouldn't object.  He likes the accountability of people 
being elected making the decision.   
 
Commissioner Cooke gave the scenario of a person who is granted a variance on a

Planner Caus said the applicants are requesting a General Plan Amendment to the 
Future Land Use Map and a rezone.  These properties are located in the Rural 
Residential (RR).  They wish to rezone the property to Community Commercial (CC) for 
a 410 multi-family unit and a single-family attached dwelling unit.  The property is 
located at the southwest corner of US 40 and I-80.  There are currently two lots of 
record.  An aerial slide was shared.   
 
Staff and the service providers have said there is not enough technical information for a 
Chapter 4 design review to occur.  The applicants would like to have a discussion with 
the Planning Commission to explain some of the revisions that have occurred since the 
October 13, 2020 work session.  Fundamental to this discussion will be the compliance 
with Policy 2.3 of the General Plan.  If the Planning Commission decides that the 
proposal complies with the approval criteria of Section 10-7-2 (of the General Plan),  
and 10-7-4 (rezone criteria) Staff will return at a future meeting with a set of Findings 
of Facts and Conclusions of Law to support a motion to recommend approval to the 
County Council.  The Planning Commission can also direct the applicants to return with 
additional information.
