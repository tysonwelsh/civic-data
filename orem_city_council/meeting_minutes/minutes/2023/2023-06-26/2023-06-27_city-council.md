Hillcrest Park Tour:

Those who attended the tour are Jason Bench, Debby Lauret, Mayor Dave Young, Brenn
Bybee, Lane Gray, David Spencer, Terry Peterson, Bryce Merrill, Tom Macdonald, Jeff
Lambson, Ryan Clark, and LaNae Millett.

Mr. Brent Potter and Mr. Cory Shupe with Blu Line started the exterior tour. They
explained the plans for Hillcrest Park including a splash pad, pickleball courts, walking path, and
open grass area. Mr. Bruce Fallon with WPA led the tour through the building. This building
will have a basketball court, snack bar and lounge area. Also involved with the tour is Gary
Williams with Bud Mahas Construction. The tentative completion date is October 31, 2023.

Fraud Risk Questionnaire: Presenter: Brandon C. Nelson, Finance Director

Mr. Nelson presented the Fraud Risk Assessment that is required by the State Auditor's
office. The City of Orem scored 335/395. This puts them in the low risk level. Mr. Nelson feels
comfortable with our risk level. The Risk Assessment also goes over the Basic Separation of
Duties.

Resolution to Place Renewal of Care Tax on Ballot: Presenter: Steve Earl, City Attorney

Mr. Earl explained that the current Care Tax will expire this year. The plan is to present
this in the August 1st General Session of City Code. Mr. Earl explained that the Care Tax is
allocated 50% to recreation and 50% to cultural arts. The funds are used for City Owned
facilities and operations but only operations cost for privately owned businesses.

There were discussions about protecting the allocated 50%. Mr. Earl explained that
changes could be made to the resolution before it goes to vote, but once it has been voted on the
City will be bound by the resolution. The resolution is for 10 years.

Utah Valley Earth Forum Award: Presenter: Jim Westwater, Chairman of Utah Valley Earth
Forum and Matt Taylor, Senior Planner

Mr. Taylor introduced Dr. Westwater with the Utah Valley Earth Forum. Dr Westward
explained that his group encourages cities and towns to get involved in sustainability work.
They send a survey to cities and towns to see which cities and towns are being conservative with
their natural resources. The Earth Forum group gave awards to cities and towns for their

conservative work. The award reads “In recognition of exemplary efforts to help the Earth’s
environment, which we all share, achieving important advances in Leadership & Community
Involvement, Energy Conservation, EV Charging, Efficient Construction, Recycling, Water
Conservation & Xeriscaping.”

PERSONAL APPEARANCES

Mr. Andrew Jackson is a citizen of Orem. Mr. Jackson gave the city council a copy of
the Utah Code 54-3-27 Public utility easement. He discovered that in 2009 the code was
changed for public easements. He says the code allows for structures to be built in the utility
easement area.

Ms. Cathy Young wanted to express her gratitude for the new benches in the community
park along the walking path and she reported she has seen Orem seniors using them.

Mr. Bennett Cookson had two concerns. First, he would like to see green waste cans
back in use and second, he is concerned about the parking tickets that were issued in his
cul-de-sac. The residents in that cul-de-sac are parking perpendicular to the curb and the code
only allows for parallel parking.

SCHEDULED ITEMS
Public Hearing: Amending City of Orem FY 2022-2023 Adopted Budget Presenter:

Brandon C. Nelson, Finance Director

Mr. Nelson presented that there are several items on the current budget that need to be
amended. The water fund was more expensive than previously planned and more funds were
needed. Orem’s general fund had exceeded the ceiling amount allowed and needed to be
reallotted. The funds are now in the capital project fund. The presentation noted other budget
amendments needed.

Public Hearing opened at 6:35 pm
No comment

Ms. Millett moved to amend the City of Orem FY 2022-2023 Adopted Budget . Ms.
Lauret seconded the motion. Those voting yes: David Young, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson,
David Spencer, Debby Lauret, LaNae Millett, and Jeff Lambson. The motion passed.

Public Hearing: Amending Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the City of Orem by
rezoning portions of the property located generally at 883 N State Street to either the C2
zone or C3 zone and amending Appendix A of the Orem City Code to make Standard Land
Use Code 1283 Transitional Treatment Homes a permitted use in the C3 zone. Presenter:

Jason Bench, Assistant Development Services Director

Mr. Bench is talking about the property at 883 N State. The property has C2 zones and
C3 zones and it is proposed that the front will be C3 zone and the back of the lot would be C2
zone. This will make the lots only one zone instead of 2 different zones in one lot. The council
asked clarifying questions about what this change will mean for the area. The SLU code for the
C3 zone will allow Transitional Treatment Homes to be a permitted use. The council had
concerns about what other Transitional Treatment Homes could be permitted.

Mr. Paul Washburn and Ms. Ashley Turner were present at the meeting and they
represent the Refuge Center For Women and Children in Crisis. They are wanting to expand their
shelter for Orem and Provo women and children. This expanded facility will help them meet the
need. This location is located close to many services the residents will need. Matt Nielson is
here with Evergreen Development. Evergreen Development will be purchasing the property and
