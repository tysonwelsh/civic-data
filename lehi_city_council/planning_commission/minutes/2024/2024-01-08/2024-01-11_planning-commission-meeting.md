1. Call to Order
Commission Chair Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Rafael Pena’s request for conditional use approval
for JMR Trucks & Equipment with vehicle storage located at approximately 4250 North
Digital Drive.
Brittney Harris presented information on the item.

The applicant, Rafael Pena, was present. There was some confusion on the parcel record. Rafael
clarified he was asking for the permit for a food truck. The use will not be used during the
winter. The trucks are being parked next to Freedom Trucking School. The trucks parked to the
East and South of the area are not their trucks. The site is specifically for parking and not used
for mechanical purposes. The parcel is leased. There is a tent, and the Fire Marshall is coming
for the inspection.

Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.


James Whitmore stated he applied for an application for the parcel that was shown but it was
under James Whitmore. He stated he is the East parcel but is a separate application. Parcel
number 5800202320 is the parcel that Mr. Whitmore owns and is adjacent to Mr. Pena’s
application.

Brittney clarified that there are three parcel owners. Freedom Trucking, James Whitmore, and
another owner. The parcels are all adjacent to one another but are separated by a fence.

Commission Chair Jackson invited James Whitmore and Rafael Pena to the podium to help
answer clarifying questions about the parcels. The applications are separate, and Mr. Whitmore’s
will be seen later.

Commissioner Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Rafael Pena’s request for conditional
        use approval for JMR Trucks & Equipment with vehicle storage located at
        approximately 4250 North Digital Drive; including all findings stated in the report and
        all DRC comments, noting the applicant needs to come into compliance with the fire
        marshal’s recommendation for the food truck operation. Commissioner Lockhart
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Dave Scoville’s request for review of the
Newbold Estates zone change, 7.3-acres located at approximately 600 North 500 West,
changing the zoning from A-1 (agriculture) & R-2 (medium density residential) to R-1-Flex
(single family residential).
Brittney Harris presented information on the item.

The applicant, Dave Scoville, was present.

Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.

John Rockwell stated his power, gas, and cable lines are on the north side of his property and are
in the way of putting in the cemented ditch. The developer will be required to move his utilities
so there isn’t any interference. He is concerned that the development will encroach on his
property. He has spoken with Mr. Struthers. He is also concerned about the children’s safety
walking to and from school. He would strongly suggest a traffic study on 500 West. His property
is zoned agriculture, and he would like to keep the zoning. He respects property rights and would
just like the same in return.

Joseph West stated he is opposed to the development but understands it will change. He does not
want the property taxes to spike and would still like the view. He is also concerned about the
traffic and the size of the homes that are being built.


Neil and Jana Newbolt are concerned about some property lines. The driveway will be affected
by the development depending on the zoning. Mr. Struthers stated that zoning will need to be
resolved before the county will record the property.

Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.

The applicant addressed the comments. He stated he has no control over the taxes that
homeowners are responsible for paying. The ditch is being installed on the applicant's property.
The applicant stated he has tried to communicate with the Newbolds with no success. Mr.
Scoville stated he bought the property from a trustee sale but is happy to work with the
Newbolds to work something out.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Dave Scoville’s
        request for review of the Newbold Estates zone change, 7.3-acres located at
        approximately 600 North 500 West, changing the zoning from A-1 (agriculture) &
        R-2 (medium density residential) to R-1-Flex (single family residential); including all
        DRC comments and findings stated in the report, with the recommendation to work with
        neighboring property owners, specifically the Newbold’s to have a property line survey
        done, and the Rockwell’s gas line and powerline to ensure suitable opportunity to work
        through those issues. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.4) Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code
Amendment to Table 37.080, Off-Street Parking requirements, removing a proximity
requirement for townhomes adjacent to on-street parking. (Tabled from the December
14th meeting)
Brittney Harris presented information on the item. The language is the same as proposed at the
last meeting.


The commissioners asked who would enforce the on-street parking ordinances. Guest street
parking would have to be provided. Additional on-street parking was added to the code.
Commissioner Eyre feels that the developer should be providing additional parking and that it
should not be the City’s responsibility. Commissioner Jackson inquired how this differs from a
private and public road.

Not a public hearing item.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for
        review of a Development Code Amendment to Table 37.080, Off-Street Parking
        requirements, removing a proximity requirement for townhomes adjacent to on-
        street parking; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, no; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
