On behalf of Midvale City, we extend our deepest appreciation for your dedicated service and
commitment to our state. We respectfully voice our concern regarding legislation that diminishes
municipal authority or imposes unfunded mandates, as such measures can hinder our city's ability
to effectively govern and provide for our community. We firmly believe that the foundation of great
communities lies in the collaborative partnership betvveen our residents, local government, and the
state. With your continued support and understanding of our local needs and challenges, we are
confident that together, we can build a brighter, more prosperous future for Midvale City. Your role
in this partnership is invaluable, and we look forward to working hand in hand to achieve greatness
for our community.
