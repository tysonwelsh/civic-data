Adams Community Reinvestment Area
          Proposed Amendment to Area Budget: The proposal is for the Board to approve a budget amendment to the Adams
Community Reinvestment Area (CRA) budget. In addition, the proposal is for each of the taxing entities to continue participating
at 100 percent until 2028 when participation rates would be reduced from 100 percent to 75 percent for the County and School
District and 90 percent for Ogden City. Also, the proposal includes a request that the School District extend its participation
through the end of the term to accommodate the reduction in participation. The School District’s participation currently ends in
2038. Approval of updated interlocal agreements would be done in separate but related actions. RDA staff are currently in the
process of negotiating updated interlocal agreements with the other taxing entities.
          Proposed Participation and Incentive Agreement for Adams Avenue Apartments: On January 9, 2018, the RDA Board
approved the final plan and budget for the Adams Community Reinvestment Area. The area’s boundaries are roughly between
23rd Street and 28th Street and between Washington Boulevard and Jefferson Avenue. Also on January 9, 2018, interlocal
agreements between the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency and Ogden City and between the Ogden City RDA and Ogden
School District were approved. The interlocal agreement between the Ogden City RDA and Weber County was approved on June
5, 2018. The proposal is to enter into a participation and incentive agreement with Alpha Adams, LLC to build a 101-unit market-
rate multi-family housing project at 2637 Adams Avenue. The project area consists of three parcels which include a single-family
residential building and a large surface parking area. The proposed incentive for the project is $4.7 million to be paid annually
post-performance through 2044. With the TIF incentive, the project is proposed to include structured parking, plazas and
walkways, and enhanced exterior finishes. The proposed project fits within the goals and objectives of the Adams CRA as well as
the Nine Rails Creative District.
          Economic Development Director Meess used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on both proposals
impacting the Adams CRA; she provided historical information regarding the creation of the Adams CRA plan and budget in
2018. She also summarized the vision and objectives of the Adams CRA and reported on projects that have been completed within
the CRA to date. The original budget for the CRA was fairly conservative and was capped at $10.8 million, with $8 million
available for development activities. The proposed budget amendment includes the following:
              • Projected investment increases from $95.6 million to $133.4 million.
              • Maintain project area term (through 2045).
              • Increase total budget from $10.8 million to $30.5 million.
              • Maintain current participation of 100% through Year 10, then decrease to 75% for County and Ogden School
                   District, 90% for City.
          Discussion among the Board and Ms. Meess centered on funding intended to be allocated to historic renovation of
buildings within the Adams CRA; how the City will work with Weber County and the Ogden School District to extend the
participation term and rates; and opportunities for pursuing a shared parking project with Weber County.
          Relative to proposed Participation and Incentive Agreement for Adams Avenue Apartments; the project is being referred
to as the Alpha Malakite project, a market rate multifamily housing at Adams Avenue and 26 th Street. It will be a mixed-use
setting with adjacent commercial renovation, containing 101 units that will be highly amenitized, 65 parking stalls, outdoor plazas
and walkways to enhance the Nine Rails Creative District, and high-quality exterior finishes to complement existing buildings.
She presented a concept plan for the project and compared that plan with photos to display the existing conditions of the project
site. She summarized the terms of the participation and incentive agreement as follows:
              • Estimated private investment of $35 million in Malakite apartments
              • Estimated to generate $4.7 million in Tax Increment Financing (TIF) by 2045
                        o Includes TIF from adjacent commercial renovation
                        o TIF required to make project financially feasible
                        o TIF will contribute to the cost of:
                                 ▪ $3.25 million: Structured parking
                                 ▪ $1 million: Plazas, walkways, and landscaping to enhance Nine Rails Creative District
                                 ▪ $1.5 million: High-quality exterior finishes
          Discussion among the Board and Ms. Meess centered on the proposed use of the adjacent commercial space and the
impact the proposed apartment project could have on the tax base of the CRA area.
          For a copy of Ms. Meess’ presentations in their entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.

Airport Community Reinvestment Area
         Proposed Amendment to Interlocal Agreement with Weber County: The proposal is for the Board to adopt an updated
interlocal agreement between the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency and Weber County. The updated agreement lowers the total
participation amount for the county to 75 percent and requires that the county’s portion be used only for infrastructure. The total
project budget remains the same and the maximum contribution of county tax increment remains at $17,000,000.
         Economic Development Director Meess used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on the proposed
amendment; the original agreement was adopted in June of 2023, but in the fall of that same year, Weber County shared their
concerns about some terms of the interlocal. Throughout 2024, the City and County worked to negotiate proposed amendments to
the agreement and the County voted to approve the agreement on their end in January of 2025. The amended interlocal agreement
does the following:
               • Restricts use of County tax increment to infrastructure (electrical, water, sewer, taxiways, apron, etc.).
               • Reduces County participation from 90 percent to 75 percent.
               • Maintains County maximum contribution at $17 million.
               • Maintains base year as 2020 and expiration in 2047.
               • Revises tax increment commencement date to the earlier of January 1 in the year that RDA triggers or January
                    1, 2026.
          For a copy of Ms. Meess’ presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Proposed Participation and Incentive Agreements for Williams International: On June 6, 2023, the RDA Board
approved the plan and budget for the Airport Community Reinvestment Area (CRA). The area consists of all airport property
within Ogden City as well as properties north of Hinckley Drive and east of Airport Road. The project area collection period is 25
years commencing on January 1, 2023. The total budget for the project area is $119,316,079 with Ogden City, Ogden School
District, Central Weber Sewer District, and Weber County participating. Within the Airport CRA, Williams International currently
houses its Ogden operation on the northern portion of the airport property with approximately 400,000 square feet of space and
900 employees. Williams International is based in Pontiac, Michigan and designs and develops small gas turbine engines used in
aircraft and cruise missiles and serves both the commercial and defense sectors. Williams International is looking to expand its
operations and is considering the Ogden location for the expansion. The proposal would add approximately 100,000 square feet of
building space and 300 new jobs. The draft participation and incentive agreement would incentivize the company’s expansion
with the use of up to $22 million in tax increment financing support. The incentive would include $17 million to support new
electrical infrastructure with an additional $5 million to support the new jobs. An additional $5.5 million of RDA funds would be
committed by the Agency to construct a new 24-inch waterline at the airport. The incentive would be paid annually post-
performance until the Airport CRA expires in 2048 or until the maximum incentive is reached, whichever is sooner.
          Economic Development Director Meess used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to discuss the terms of the proposed
Participation and Incentive Agreements in greater detail. She first reported that Williams International is considering expanding
their operations; the company produces gas turbine engines for small jets and cruise missiles, is a key supplier for national
defense, and has been operating in Ogden since 1978. Their proposed expansion is 100,000 square feet, will bring more than 300
high wage jobs to the area, and generate increased tax increment financing for the next several years. Ms. Meess discussed some
of the challenges Williams International has faced as part of their expansion, including labor and other operating costs are higher
than at other sites and electrical infrastructure upgrades are required for Ogden site. They have received an approved maximum of
EDTIF incentive from the State of 30 percent of new state tax revenues for 20 years. Their expansion is estimated to generate
$31.25 million in local TIF by 2047. The proposed incentive to be offered to Williams International is $27.5 million structured as
follows:
               • Direct post-performance incentive
                         o $17 million to support electrical infrastructure improvements
                         o $5 million for job creation and job training activities
               • Public infrastructure investment
                         o $5.5 million for new 24” waterline loop from airport well
          For a copy of Ms. Meess’ presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Discussion among the Board and Ms. Meess centered on the amount of water Williams International will use at the
Airport to justify the construction of the new 24-inch water line; the termination date of the agreement, which matches the
expiration of the Airport CRA or until Williams International reaches the maximum incentive amount; the amount of time the City
and State will wait to receive a return on the investment; opportunities for using the incentive agreement to encourage new
employees of Williams International to buy a home in Ogden; the potential need for additional space for future expansions at the
Airport or other sites in the community; and a general discussion of housing units available to different income levels in the
Ogden community.
