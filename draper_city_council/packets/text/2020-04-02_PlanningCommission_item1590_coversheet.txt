Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Pete Kane
CC:  Date: April 2, 2020
Re: Public Hearing - Burt Brothers Tire Remodel Plat Amendment & Site Plan Review Comments: The applicant is requesting approval of a Site Plan and Plat Amendment for the conversion of the former Firestone Service Center into a Burt Brother Tire with a remodel of the existing building, small addition/replacement of building's front area, and a new parking lot coupled with a merging of two lots on an approximately 1.2-acre site in the CR (Regional Commercial) Zone. The findings are as follows: For approval:
The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan.
The proposed development plans meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code.
The proposed development conforms to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area.
The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development.
The entire site shall be developed at one time unless a phased development plan is approved.
The site plan conforms to applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of the Draper City Municipal Code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns.
There is good cause for vacation or amendment to the plat; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
