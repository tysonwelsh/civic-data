RECITALS
A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
40] et seq.
B. The Magna Metro Township Council (“Council”) is a Metro Township Council pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-3b-501 ef ai.
C. Pursuant to the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act (the “Act”), the Council must adopt and
post an annual regular meeting schedule of the Council.
D. The 2020 Magna Metro Township Council Regular Meeting Schedule fulfills the
requirement of the Act.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township Council, the Council
approves and adopts the attached 2020 Magna Metro Township Regular Meeting Schedule
hereinafter to be known as Attachment “A” to this resolution, for the benefit of Magna.
