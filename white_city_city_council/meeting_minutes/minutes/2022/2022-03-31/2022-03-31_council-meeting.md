                                        Public Hearing

White City Draft General Plan

              Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(MSD), introduced herself and briefly reviewed the White City Metro Township General Plan.

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              Michelle Luckau stated she has concerns about the snow from the school being
pushed out onto Sego Lily creating an ice rink. There were piles of snow and the parking lot was
empty.

              Harold Vincent stated he is against mixed use housing.

              Rosella West asked what the Council is going to do about the dirt on the sidewalk,
there was supposed to be grass. People are not picking up their dog poop.

             Council Member Perry stated the burs and stickers along the canal trail are a
concern. The goal for landscaping is to have natural vegetation, there was not a plan to have
grass.

               A Citizen (name inaudible) made comments regarding fiber optics, sidewalks on
Sego Lily, and the new school being built.

                 Kent McClure stated on page 16 of the General Plan, it talks about community
input, I have addressed several of the issues during Council meetings. Under the paragraph titled
“Threats”, it talks about on street parking and lack of code enforcement. At the last council meeting
he spoke about the lack of code enforcement. The codes are not hard to understand and enforce.
There are a lot of things in the code that could mitigate some of these problems. There is a big
push for walkability; rather than putting in new sidewalks, the dilapidated sidewalks should be
fixed first. There are people with walkers that are struggling to navigate some of these sidewalks.
There are cars parked on the sidewalks near the school and it is forcing kids to walk in the street.
When will the community see action on the code enforcement issues?

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Cardenaz, moved to close
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                Mayor Flint stated the Council will take the comments into consideration and
discuss this at its next Council meeting.

               Ms. O’Kelley reviewed the White City General Plan by chapter and with more
detail.

               Council Member Little, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to reopen the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              Doug Denning stated there are homes that have had code enforcement tell them
that they cannot have something in their carports but then there are homes with a yard full of
weeds that are not being addressed. He is interested in where the funding is coming from. He has
brought up the speeding on Amber Lane at a previous Council meeting. There are other streets
with speed bumps that have helped slow drivers down.

              Charlene Berry stated she has concerns about the potholes in the road. Drivers
are coming down the hill at 45 MPH. There are a lot of safety issues that need to be addressed.
What options are being discussed for Sego Lily? When will the residents be able to connect to
Google Fiber?

               Tara Jensen stated homes are being bought to be used for rentals or Airbnb’s.
Those that are using our community for Airbnb’s should be held to a higher maintenance standard.

               A Citizen (did not provide his name) stated water comes down Sego Lily, heading
to the drainage ditch; however, there is not a new sidewalk with gutters and creates a big pond of
water. When the temperatures are low it creates an ice rink.

            Lynette Herb stated a home behind me was sold recently. She was told that a
homeowner had to live in part of the house to rent the bottom. How is the Airbnb and renting
working?

               Council Member Perry stated Airbnb’s are not legal. Stays 30 days or less is not
legal. They can rent the house as a whole or they can rent part of the house if it is owner occupied.

               Mayor Flint stated by adopting the General Plan, it will give the Council the ability
to create ordinances to address rental properties.

                Rosella West stated a house across the street is a split level and it has 15-16
people living there.

                Stephen Gee stated as the weather warms up, there are a lot of homeless
individuals that camp in the gulley and wander through the neighborhoods. I always contact the
police to make sure they have patrols going through at night. Watch your neighbors’ houses and
if the motion lights come on or dogs are barking, contact the police. He would like the police to
patrol our end of the gulley between 700 east and 1300 east to make sure there are not people
camping there. If there was fire someone may get hurt.

             Council Member Price stated the gully belongs to Sandy City; however, UPD is
very good about forwarding information to Sandy City Police and will occasionally patrol it.

                Judy Christensen stated when she asked why Sego Lily is a 25 MPH road, she
was told because there are not sidewalks. Now that there will be sidewalks will the speed limit be
raised and will property be taken to reconstruct Sego Lily.

               Council Member Cardenaz, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to close
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              Mayor Flint stated the Council will take all the comments under advisement and
continue the General Plan discussions at the April 7, Council meeting.
