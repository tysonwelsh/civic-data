Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Houseman read a statement from Mr. Blake Perez with the Central 
                                  Wasatch Commission, thanking Chief Severson and the Sandy Police Department for 
                                  their efforts and responsiveness. She invited the community to attend the upcoming 
                                  Community Connectedness workshop on October 14th at the Sandy Club, A Safe Place 
                                  for Boys and Girls. Council Members Christensen and Sharkey will join the workshop that 
                                  evening.

                                  Council Member Zoltanski mentioned that election ballots will be arriving next week. She 
                                  invited the public to attend the forum at the Gail Miller Center at Salt Lake Community 
                                  College tomorrow evening. 

Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn invited the public to attend the Sandy Arts Guild Visual Arts show taking 
                                  place at the Sandy Senior Center from October 12th through the 22nd.

                                  Sandy City.

6.         SPR0628202 The Orchard at Farnsworth Farms
           1-006089   11237 S. 700 East
           (CC2)      [Community #11 - Crescent]
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Ordinance #21-30 Farnsworth (9.30.21) (final).pdf
                                  CC Staff Report - Site Plan - Farnsworth Farms
                                  Exhibit B Site Plan Changes Provided by Applicant.pdf

                                  PC Maps and Materials.pdf
                                  PC Staff Report - Site Plan - Farnsworth Farms.pdf
                                  PC Staff Report - Sub - Farnsworth Farms.pdf

                                  This is a Public Hearing Item. Mr. Craig Evans presented Ordinance 21-30 for Council 
                                  consideration and action. 

                                  Public Comment opened:
                                  Mr. Mark Ciullo spoke bout his experience living in the Sandy City. He reviewed the 
                                  application process for the developer of the Orchard of Farnsworth Farms and provided an 
                                  overview of comments made at prior meetings regarding this project development. 
                                  Public Comment closed.

                                  Council discussion and questions followed. Council members acknowledged Mr. Ciullo's 
                                  efforts to inform the Council on this project and thanked him for providing the Council with 
                                  detailed information. 

                                  Council discussion followed.
                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey, to adopt 
                                  ordinance 21-30, option 1, an ordinance amending and fixing the boundaries of a 
                                  zone district of the Sandy City zoning ordinance; to wit:  rezoning approximately 
                                  10.07 acres from R1-40A "Single Family Residential District" to PUD(10) "Planned 
                                  Unit Development (10 units per acre)", located at approximately 11228 South and 
                                  700 East; also providing a saving clause and an effective date for the 
                                  ordinance...The motion carried by the following vote:

                          Yes:    5 -  Alison Stroud
                                       Kris Nicholl
                                       Marci Houseman
                                       Cyndi Sharkey
                                       Brooke Christensen

                           No:    2 -  Zach Robinson
                                       Monica Zoltanski

Adjournment
                                  Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:28 pm.
