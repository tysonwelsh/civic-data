3   BUSINESS ITEMS
    3.1 DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Tentative Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget
        Assistant Finance Director/Treasurer Mariah Hill will present the proposed Tentative
        Fiscal Year 2021-2022 RDA Budget. The Board will review and possibly act to approve
        the proposed Tentative Fiscal Year 2021-2022 RDA Budget and set a public hearing for
        May 26, 2021.

      Assistant Finance Director/Treasurer Mariah Hill reviewed the proposed budget. There was
a discussion about the Capital Projects. Mr. McHargue mentioned that they had triggered Phase 4
of the RDA tax increment.

    3.2 DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Underwriter for 2021 Bond
        City Manager Jacob McHargue will present a recommendation for a bond underwriter for
        the 2021 Bond. The board will take appropriate action.

     City Manager Jacob McHargue explained the underwriter selection process and what their
responsibilities would be. Staff recommendations were to appoint Stifel as the Senior
Bookrunning Manager with RBC Capital Markets as the Co-Manager.

There was a discussion about how the underwriters and bonds would work.

   Motion: BOARDMEMBER WELSH MOVED THAT WE TAKE THE
RECOMMENDATION FROM THE PRICING COMMITTEE AND APPOINT STIFEL AS
BOOKRUNNING MANAGER WITH RBC CAPITAL MARKETS AS CO-MANAGER FOR
UNDERWRITING THE 2021 RDA BONDS. BOARDMEMBER JUDD SECONDED THE
MOTION. CHAIR FULLMER, BOARDMEMBERS EARNEST, FLAKE, JUDD, AND WELSH
VOTED AYE. THE MOTION CARRIED UNANIMOUSLY.
