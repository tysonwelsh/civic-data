Proposed Resolution 2025-11 adopting the budget for the Redevelopment
Agency for Fiscal Year 2025-2026
          A memo from the Management Services Department regarding the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026
Redevelopment Agency (RDA) budget came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the proposed budget includes
costs for administration ($331,500), project districts ($15,220,300), and housing ($1,298,275) for a total budget of $16,850,075.
This represents a 16.91 percent decrease over the FY25 budget.
          Board Administrator Eller-Smith summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to offer Board
staff’s analysis of the proposed RDA budget for FY 2025-26; she identified the number of active RDA districts, as well as those
that have expired since the adoption of the last FY budget, which is the reason for the budget 16.91 percent decrease. She
indicated sources of project funding include property tax increment, Business Depot Ogden (BDO) lease revenue, interfund
transfers, interagency transfers, and interest income. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for
the meeting.
           Chair White introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2025-11, entitled:
                    “A resolution of the Board of Directors of the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency adopting the annual budget
                    for Fiscal Year 2025-2026 as required by Section 17C-1-601.5, Utah Code Annotated, 1953.”
          Chair White then opened the public hearing.

        There being no persons appearing to be heard, BOARD MEMBER RICHEY MOVED THE PUBLIC HEARING BE
CLOSED. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER GRAF, ALL VOTING AYE.
        ON A MOTION BY BOARD MEMBER RICHEY AND SECONDED BY VICE CHAIR HYER, RESOLUTION
2025-11 WAS ADOPTED UPON THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE – BOARD MEMBERS
BLAIR, CHOBERKA, GRAF, MYERS, RICHEY, VICE CHAIR HYER, AND CHAIR WHITE. VOTING NO – NONE.
