                                 PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
                                        Meeting of September 24, 2020
                            Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

 Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session on
   Thursday, September 24, 2020. Chairman Nielson called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Roylan Croshaw, Regina Dickinson, Sandi Goodlander, Jessica Lucero,
Dave Newman, Tony Nielson, Eduardo Ortiz
Staff Present: Mike DeSimone, Russ Holley, Tanya Rice, Debbie Zilles
Commissioner Croshaw moved to approve the minutes from the September 10, 2020 meeting as
submitted. Motion seconded by Commissioner Ortiz. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING

 PC 20-047 Hansen Childcare [Conditional Use Permit] Lisa K. Hansen, authorized
 agent/owner, is requesting a permit to increase the capacity of an in-home daycare from 8
 children to up to 16 children. The .63-acre property is located at 1306 West 350 South in the
 Neighborhood Residential (NR-4) zone; TIN 02-133-0307.

STAFF: Ms. Rice reviewed the request for a for a daycare with a maximum of 16 children. The
Land Development Code (LDC) allows daycare with a maximum of 16 clients in the Neighborhood
Residential (NR-4) zoning district as a Conditional Use. Lisa Hansen currently operates a daycare
with up to 8 children at the location, a permitted use in the zone. The daycare operates from 6:30
am to 4:15 pm. As a daycare with 1-8 children is a permitted use, the Planning Commission is
specifically reviewing the impact of a daycare use in a residence with up to 8 additional children.
The concerns with daycare use in a residence are typically parking, access, noise, and safety.

PROPONENT: Ms. Hansen advised that she only babysits for four families, with multiple siblings.
She would like an increase to watch older siblings if/when necessary and her grandchildren
occasionally. There are three adults in the home.

PUBLIC: Emails were received and distributed to the Commission prior to the meeting and are
included in the project file.

COMMISSION: None

MOTION: Commissioner Dickinson moved to conditionally approve a Conditional Use Permit as
outlined in PC 20-047 with the conditions for approval as listed below. Commissioner Goodlander
seconded the motion.
CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval are recorded and available in the Community Development
   Department.
2. No outside activities permitted earlier than 7:00 am.
3. Dedicated off-street parking within the driveway shall be provided during business hours for
   child drop off and pick up.


4. Drop-off and pick-up times shall be scheduled and staggered to ensure that no more than three
   (3) client vehicles are at the home at any one time and sidewalks and driveways are not
   blocked.
5. The proponent shall comply with all State and local regulations and licenses regarding in-home
   daycare facilities for up to 16 clients.
6. Prior to issuance of Business License, the Director of Community Development shall receive a
   written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that their
   requirements have been satisfied:
     a. Water/Cross Connection
        i. A hazard survey of the points of use of water should be done at this address to help
           ensure the safety of the water for the occupants and the City of Logan water supply.
FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. This daycare is compatible with surrounding land uses and zoning designations and, as
   conditioned, will not interfere with the use and enjoyment of adjoining properties.
2. As proposed, the streets providing access to the subject property has adequate capacity for the
   use and parking/drop-off/pick-up management will be provided and regulated.
3. Other infrastructure to the subject property has adequate capacity, or suitable levels of service,
   for the proposed use.
4. The project conforms to the requirements of Title 17 of the Logan Municipal Code as an
   identified conditional use.
5. The project was noticed as required by State and City regulations and proper procedure was
   followed in processing this request.
Moved: R. Dickinson Seconded: S. Goodlander       Approved: 7-0
