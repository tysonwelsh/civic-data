                                        PUBLIC APPLICATION(S)
                                        Hearing began at – 5:42 p.m.
SUB2024-001146 – Kent Withers is requesting an exception to roadway improvements for the Camp
Kearns Subdivision (Plat 2). Location: 5162 West Lightning Lane. Zone: M-1 (Manufacturing) Zone.
Planner: Jeff Miller (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jeff Miller provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding exterior lighting and existing streetlights.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Kent Withers
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Withers said he isn’t the owner, but the understanding is there is one on the south corner
of the lot where there is a power pole that doubles as a streetlight. The reason asking for the exception is
the main purpose of the plat is to clean up the title and various mis dedications of street right-of-way’s.
Purpose is to dedicate right-of-way on the corner and incorporate, small chunk owned by Salt Lake County
to dedicate back to the landowner. No plans to develop any other improvements.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Motion: To approve application #SUB2024-001146 Kent Withers is requesting an exception to roadway
improvements for the Camp Kearns Subdivision (Plat 2) according to 18.14.080 as presented.
      Motion by: Commissioner Nelson
      2nd by: Commissioner Koester
      Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor
