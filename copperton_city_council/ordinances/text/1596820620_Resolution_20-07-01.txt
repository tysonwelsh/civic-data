RESOLUTION NO. 20-07-01                                                            July 15, 2020

    A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
 CLARIFYING ACTIONS AND PROCEDURES TO ADDRESS CONFIRMED CASES
                  OF COVID19 WITHIN COPPERTON

       WHEREAS, on March 13, 2020, the Copperton Metro Township Council (the “Council”)
adopted an action plan and declared a public health emergency for the coronavirus disease of 2019
(“COVID-19”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code; and

       WHEREAS, the Council worked with the Salt Lake County Department of Health to
develop the action plan, which was based upon the best available information at the time;

        WHEREAS, following the Council’s adoption of the action plan, additional information
and resources have become available regarding COVID19 and the circumstances involving the
virus have also changed; and

       WHEREAS, the Council desires to rescind the action plan and develop a new plan to better
account for the current circumstances involving COVID19 and the most recent information.

       NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, effective immediately by the
Copperton Metro Township Council that the action plan adopted on March 13, 2020 is rescinded
and the Council and all applicable Copperton personnel are authorized and instructed to develop a
new action plan for the Council to consider.

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 15th day of July, 2020.

                                                        Sean Clayton, Mayor
