Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: July 14, 2022
Re: Summit Commerce Center Amended Subdivision Plat Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for approximately 26.94 acres located on the west side of Pony Express Road and south of Golden Harvest Drive, at approximately 13004 S. Pony Express Road. The property is currently zoned M1. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the existing single lot subdivision to be divided into 2 Lots. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. That there is good cause for the Subdivision Plat Amendment.
2. No public street, right-of-way, or easement is vacated or amended by the plat.
3. The uses for which cross access would be required are incompatible and the site cannot be otherwise designed to accommodate the cross access and avoid the conditions of such incompatible use.
For Denial:
1. Good cause does not exist for the Subdivision Plat Amendment.
2. The plat does not provide for required cross access, the uses for which cross access is required are compatible. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type P.C. Staff Report 7/8/2022 Cover Memo
