MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 20-06-02 DATE: June 9, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
TENTATIVELY APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE 2020
CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE TO INCLUDE CEMETERY SERVICES
AND SETTING THE PUBLIC HEARING FOR PUBLIC COMMENT ON
JUNE 23, 2020 AT 7:00PM DURING THE PUBLIC HEARINGS AND
POSSIBLE COUNCIL ACTION SECTION OF THE MEETING

RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna’’) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body
for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. On November 26, 2019, pursuant to state law, the Council approved and adopted the
2020 Consolidated Fee Schedule for Magna.

D. On May 13, 2020 ownership and operational control of the Pleasant Green Cemetery
was transferred to the municipality.

E. Pursuant to State Law, the municipality is required to amend its Consolidated Fee
Schedule to include the Cemetery Fee Schedule to fund the work performed in the
Pleasant Green Cemetery.

1

F. The fee amendments shall be made available for public inspection no later than ten
(10) days prior to the public hearing for the budget pursuant to State Law.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and
Council hereby tentatively approves the amendment to the 2020 Magna Tentative
Budget, attached to this resolution as listed as “Attachment-Cemetery Fee Schedule” and
sets the date, time and place for the Public Hearing to be as follows:
PLACE: Electronic Meeting pursuant to Governor’s Executive Order Dated March
18, 2020 to be heard during the PUBLIC HEARING AND POSSIBLE COUNCIL
ACTION Section of the Council Meeting.
DATE: June 23, 2020
TIME: 7:00PM: and be it further
RESOLVED, that a notice of the Public Hearing for the 2020 Magna Consolidated
Fee Schedule Amendment be printed a newspaper of general circulation and posted to the
Utah Public Meeting Notice Website no later than June 12, 2020; and be it further
RESOLVED, copies of the 2020 Magna Consolidated Fee Schedule Amendment be
available for public inspection no later than June 12, 2020 at the Salt Lake County
Clerk’s Office, located at 2001 South State St Suite N2-700 Salt Lake City, Utah 84114,
attached to the Notice of Public Hearing on the Utah Public Meeting Notice Website
https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html, and posted to the Magna Metro Township
Website www.magnametrotownship.org for public inspection..
