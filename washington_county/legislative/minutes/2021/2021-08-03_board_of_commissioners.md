REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2809, A RESOLUTION PLACING
TRUST IN INDIVIDUAL HEALTH DECISIONS AND ESTABLISHING A POLICY
AGAINST LOCKDOWNS AND MASK MANDATES

Chairman Almquist read aloud the aforementioned Resolution.

Washington County Health Director David Blodgett provided statistics from data collected by the
Southwest Health Department regarding vaccinations and mask wearing in regards to COVID-19.
The goal of the health department has been to allow health decisions to reside within individuals
to make the proper health related choices for themselves.

Undersheriff James Standley stated that the Washington County Sheriff's Office will not support
an enforcement of a mask mandate within Washington County.

County Attorney Eric Clarke stated that it is important for the community to feel empowered to
make their own personal health decisions and take steps to protect themselves.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2021-2809, A
Resolution Placing Trust in Individual Health Decisions and Establishing a
Policy Against Lockdowns and Mask Mandates. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

PRESENTATION: VICTIMS OF CRIME ACT GRANT: CHILDREN’S JUSTICE
CENTER

Children’s Justice Center Director Kristy Pike explained that the Children’s Justice Center (CJC)
collaborates with multidisciplinary partners to protect each child, advance justice, promote
healing, and educate the community. She presented on the various services provided by the
Children’s Justice Center in 2021. The Victims of Crime Act (VOCA) is a large source of funding
for the CJC, accounting for 55% of the overall budget; however, the VOCA funding is depleting
quickly and has decreased by 36%.

Victim Coordinator Solinda Larson explained that medical services are referred to the appropriate
places to ensure that adequate medical attention is given when needed.

Megan Bigwood explained the services provided by the Multidisciplinary Teams (MDT).

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2810, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT _BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND SOUTHWEST
BEHAVIORAL HEALTH CENTER REGARDING OFFICE SPACE SHARING FOR
RECOVERY COURT STAFF

County Attorney Eric Clarke explained that recovery court was formally known as drug court
which is jointly operated by the Southwest Behavioral Health Center, the Fifth District Court, the
Sheriff's Office, and the Attorney’s Office. The Southwest Behavioral Health Center has an
employee assigned to work full time in an administration role for the recovery court program which
is operated out of the Washington County Court Support Services Building. The interlocal
agreement is still under attorney review and will come before the Commissioners for signatures

when available.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution R-2021-2810, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington County and
Southwest Behavioral Health Center Regarding Office Space Sharing For
Recovery Court Staff Pending Final Attorney Review. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2811, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_ PROFESSIONAL SERVICE AGREEMENT FOR ENGINEERING AND TRAFFIC
STUDY SERVICES RE: KOLOB MOUNTAIN AND THE ABOVE ZION CUP PROJECT

Mr. Clarke explained that the Kolob mountain area has received an increase in visitation
necessitating further studies to provide increased recreation opportunities as well as safe access.
Several proposed projects in the area will also increase tourism and recreation rates further
impacting roadways inside and outside the project. An engineering firm has been selected to
analyze the traffic and roadways in the area to determine if they comply with the applicable
standards.

Chairman Almquist clarified that this will cover the entire Kolob region and not just the areas
identified by the proposed projects.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution R-2021-2811, A
Resolution Approving a Professional Service Agreement for Engineering and
Traffic Study Services re: Kolob Mountain and the Above Zion CUP Project.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting aye.
