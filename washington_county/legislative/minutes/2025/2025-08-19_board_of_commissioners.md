06/19/2025 @ 2156, Fire Evacuation, Pine Valley — Man Hours — 126.5 (Non Certified) 6
(Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to the town of Pine Valley on a request for assistance
evacuating the town of Pine Valley. SAR members responded to Pine Valley and were given
assignments to assist with advising all residents of the evacuation order. SAR members assisted the
citizens of Pine Valley with evacuating the are a for several hours. Once all citizens had been advised of
the evacuation and those that could were evacuated, all SAR members were accounted for and cleared

the scene.
07/12/2025 @ 2130, Lost Girls Camp Group, Near Kolob Terrace Rd — Man Hours — 63
(Non Certified) 5 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to the area of Kolob Terrace Rd near Zion Tiny Oasis on a
report of a girls camp group consisting of 24 individuals that was lost. GPS Coordinates were obtained
from the reporting persons cell phone. Prior to our arrival, the group split into two groups that were
approximately a quarter mile apart. Updated GPS coordinates were obtained via a drone. SAR
members responded to the location of the first group. There were two individuals in the group that were
in need of medical attention. One juvenile had a small laceration on her knee and an apparent sprained
wrist. The other was an adult female what was experiencing a diabetic emergency. Both individuals
were treated on scene and several individuals in the group were provided with fluids to stay hydrated.
Part of the rescue group then broke off and began to make their way to the location of the second
group, which was further up the ravine. SAR members located the second group and determined that
nobody in that group was in need of medical attention. They then began hiking the second group back
to combine it with the first. Once both groups were combined, SAR members guided them back to
Incident Command (IC). Several handlines were utilized due to the treacherous terrain. All parties
declined medical transport when they arrived at IC.

07/19/2025 @ 2120, Lost Hikers, West Rim Trail, Zion National Park — Man Hours — 85.5
(Non Certified) 12 (Certified)

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to West Rim Trail area off of Kolob Terrace Rd on a report
of four hikers that had gotten lost and were overdue. The group of hikers were supposed to be done
with their hike and check in several hours prior. Shortly after we received this report, we received a
satellite text message from the lost group. The group was located about two miles east of West Rim
Trailhead in a slot canyon. They explained that they were lost and may have gone down the wrong
canyon. They had supplies but were concerned about staying overnight in the wet conditions. UHP
Helicopter STAR8 was requested to assist but was unable to do night operations at the time. SAR
members responded to the location of the group and made it to a ledge above them. Two rescuers were
sent down an 800 foot cliffin an attempt to get to the location of the lost party. They made to another
ledge above the group but were unable to continue any further due to the terrain. When it became light
out, STAR8 was able to respond to the location of the lost group. They were able to hoist the group
back to Incident Command two at a time. All four members declined medical transport. SAR members
then utilized a pulley system to bring the two rescuers back up the 500 foot cliff. All SAR members
then returned to Incident Command by approximately 0930 and the call was complete shortly after.
