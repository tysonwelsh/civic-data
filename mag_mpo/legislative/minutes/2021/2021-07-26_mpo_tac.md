North Lakeshore Area Study Conclusion – Shawn Eliot, MAG The North Lakeshore Area Study reviewed traffic and commute patterns within the south Lehi and east lakeshore of Utah Lake. Freeway volumes are projected in the area and current infrastructure including Pioneer Crossing and I-15 cannot handle future demand. The study used a new UDOT process called Development Solutions looking at a holistic picture of the needs and wants of the region rather than just transportation need. Public and stakeholder input was used to gauge the MPO Technical Advisory Committee July 26, 2021 Page 3 importance of land use, transportation, the natural environment, safety, and community. This and further input were used to formulate different scenarios to review. The final story map report was reviewed at the meeting and can be found at https://mountainland.org/northlakeshore/ Further work will determine which corridors and policies to implement. The study recommended that Pioneer Crossing and Vineyard Connector evolve into a grade separated freeway system, Pony Express PKWY be completed as a minor arterial, and transit projects already identified in the regional transportation plan be implemented. Concerns were raised that the study did not look at fiscal impact. Pre-Disaster Mitigation Plan Update – Shauna Mecham, MAG MAG has been asked to update the Pre-Disaster Hazard Mitigation Plan for most jurisdictions in the MAG area. Participation makes communities eligible for FEMA grants such as the BRIC grant. MAG created a story map of various hazards and an analysis for each jurisdiction. MAG shared that information with contacts from each jurisdiction and will be meeting with each in June/July to review risk and brainstorm mitigation strategies to be included in the plan. Submitted strategies have a better chance of receiving Federal Grants, though there is no legal obligation to complete strategies. TIP Modification: Spanish Fork Center St. Funding Transfer- Bob Allen, MAG The Center Street Widening Project was selected in 2016 and was awarded $5,171,000 in County Funds. The project widened Center St to 5 lanes from 900 E to US6 and modified the intersection at 1150 E. Due to scope adjustments approved by TAC and RPC and unforeseen ROW requirements, the project cost $2,082,274 more than the original estimate. The US6 Pedestrian Crossing Project was selected in 2018 and was awarded $1,790,435. The project intended to build a pedestrian undercrossing at US6 to allow students at the nearby school and other pedestrians to cross US6. UDOT requested jurisdiction of the project and cost estimates have now risen to $6.5m. Since the project was selected, Nebo School District has modified school boundaries, no longer requiring students to cross the busy intersection. Due to the increased costs and reduced demand for the undercrossing, Spanish Fork is requesting that the US6 Undercrossing Project be eliminated and the existing $1,790,435 be transferred to the Center Street Widening Project to cover some of the cost overruns. Modifications will be made at 1430E to improve pedestrian safety and connectivity. Not part of this project. MAG Staff recommend US6 Undercrossing be eliminated from the TIP and $1,790,435 be transferred to the Center Street Widening Project. Jason Bond moved that TAC recommend the US6 Undercrossing project be eliminated from the TIP and the $1,790,435 be transferred to the Center Street Widening Project. Jeremy Lapin seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Technical Advisory Committee July 26, 2021 Page 5 2022 TIP Final Approval - Bob Allen, MAG The Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) of MAG has the responsibility of preparing and approving a Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) for the area. The TIP is prepared and approved every year. The TIP lists transportation projects and programs within the MPO for the fiscal years 2022–2026. Conceptual projects are listed through 2027. Municipalities, Utah County, UDOT, UTA, and others are eligible for MPO funds. All regional projects, regardless of the selection agency, must be approved by the MPO committees and be listed in the TIP.
