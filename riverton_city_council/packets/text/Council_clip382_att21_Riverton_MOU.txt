                         AMEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

       THIS MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING (the “MOU”), is entered into on
__________________, 2020 by and among Riverton City, a Utah Municipal Corporation, and
Riverton Arts Council, a Utah Nonprofit Corporation.

                                               RECITALS

       WHEREAS, the Riverton Arts Council has for many years served the residents of Riverton
City by providing periodic arts events;

       WHEREAS, the Riverton Arts Council has identified opportunities and a strategy for
growth and expansion;

        WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to support the Riverton Arts Council and its growth
plans, all in the best interests of the City and its citizens;

        WHEREAS, concurrent to the execution of this MOU, Riverton Arts Council has entered
into an agreement to lease (“Lease”) certain property and improvements from Riverton City, in
furtherance of the purposes of this MOU; and

        WHEREAS, the parties now deem it advisable and in their best interests to enter into this
MOU in order to memorialize their collective understanding, intent and objectives with respect
to the growth and expansion of the Riverton Arts Council.

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth below and for other
good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby acknowledged,
the parties hereto agree as follows.

                                             AGREEMENT

    1. Upgrade and Expansion of the Theater. The Riverton Arts Council intends to upgrade
       the existing Sandra Lloyd Community Center, as well as expand the theater to include
       separate new facilities, all in accordance with the concept drawings attached hereto as
       Exhibit A. This upgrade and expansion project will be phased as follows:

                a. Phase 1 shall include an interior rebuild and upgrade to the Sandra Lloyd
                   Community Center, including the installation of fixed seating and other theater
                   amenities;

                b. Phase 1a shall include the the construction of an Arts Lobby that shall connect the
                   Sandra Lloyd Community Center with Riverton City Hall, as located and shown
                   on the concept drawings attached as Exhibit A;

                c. Phase 2 of the expansion shall include construction of a new 28,000 square-foot
                   community theater, located directly adjacent to and north of the current Sandra

              Community Center and Riverton City Hall via the Arts Lobby, as shown on the
              concept drawings attached as Exhibit A.

           d. Timing of all phasing will vary with regard to Riverton Arts Council’s needs and
              availability of funding to pay for the project.

   2. Plan Amendments. The parties acknowledge that Exhibit A represents concept drawings,
      and plan changes to accommodate the needs of the parties are likely. Such changes, when
      agreed upon by the parties, shall be considered amendments to this MOU.

   3. Permitting. The parties acknowledge that further drawings and elevations shall be needed
      in order to obtain any permits required by Riverton City. This MOU does not represent
      any building or development permits. The parties shall cooperate in good faith to obtain
      land use permits.

   4. Financing the Project. The expansion and upgrade project will be financed as follows:

           a. Riverton City shall contribute a total of $500,000 toward the costs of the project,
              paid in annual installments of $100,000 per year for the first five years of the term
              of this MOU.

           b. Riverton Arts Council shall be responsible for the balance of funds, which shall
              be obtained through fundraising, contributions, arts programs, and other sources
              of financing.

   5. Naming Rights. Riverton Arts Council reserves the right to name the theater as part of its
      fundraising efforts or for any other reason.

   6. No Joint Venture or Agency. The parties renounce the existence of any form of agency
      relationship, joint venture or partnership express or implied between the Riverton City
      and Riverton Arts Council.

   7. Further Assurances. The parties shall provide such further assurances and take such
      further action, including the execution and delivery of any and all documents, certificates,
      filings and instruments, as may be reasonable, necessary or appropriate to effectuate the
      purposes and objectives of this MOU.

   8. Effective Date and Term. This MOU shall be effective as of the effective date of the
      Lease and shall expire upon the expiration or termination of the Lease unless mutually
      agreed and memorialized by the parties in writing.

      9.      General Provisions.

              (a)     Governing Law. This MOU shall be governed by and construed in
accordance with the laws of the State of Utah, excluding its choice of law rules.
