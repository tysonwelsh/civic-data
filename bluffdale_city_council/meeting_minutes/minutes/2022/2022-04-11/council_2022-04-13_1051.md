               Substations, City Initiated, Application 2022-06. (Staff Presenter, Ellen
               Oakman).

Associate City Planner, Ellen Oak.man reported that the above item pertains to the Public Utility
Substation Ordinance. The Planning Commission recommended that the amendments be approved
but wanted to leave out any mention of cell towers. Ms. Oak.man shared background information.
The GC-1 (General Commercial) Zone is not ideal for Pub) ic Utility Substations due to the limited
commercial frontage and the desire for more viable commercial uses and aesthetically pleasing
streetscapes. As a result, Public Utility Substations were removed from the GC-1 (General
Commercial) Zone. Ms. Oakman shared a zonjng map with the Council.

The proposed amendments would add Public Utility Substations as a definition in 1 l.20.020 .
There would also be updates to 11.160.160 - Public Utility Substations related to design and
landscape standards. That section previously stated that it was a conditional use. It was proposed
to be permitted with the approval of the Zoning Administrator. Additionally, the Land Use Tables
in 11 .350.020 and 11 .250.030 were updated. For landscaping, there was a proposed setback of a
minimum of 10 feet with medium-sized trees . Water efficiency standards must be met. There was
also information about a perimeter wall, which had an eight-foot minimum and a 12-foot
maximum.
