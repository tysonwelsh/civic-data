Re: Jewelry Everyday Home Occupation Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a home occupation conditional use permit application for approximately 3.82 acres located on the east side of Browns Pond Cove.  The property is currently zoned RA2.  The applicant is requesting approval to operate a jewelry making business in the home.  The applicant is requesting deviations to the standards outlined in Section 9-34-040 of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC).  Section 9-34-060 allows the Planning Commission to consider the requested use and make deviations to the standards.
The home was originally built in 1974 and is Lot 11 of the Brown Pond Phase II of the residential subdivision. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For denial:
