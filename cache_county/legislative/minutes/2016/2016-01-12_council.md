The Cache County Council convened in a regular session on January 12, 2016 at 5:00 p.m.
in the Cache County Council Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.

> Presentation of PILT Payment — Justin Dolling, Division of Wildlife Resources
(DWR) Northern Region Supervisor — Dolling presented Cache County a check for

$9,053.23 as payment in lieu of taxes for the approximately 21,000 acres it owns and
manages in Cache County. Revenue generated from hunting and fishing licenses funds
the payment. The DWR has four chapters for county resource plans — wildlife, aquatics,
current endangered species and predators.

Vice Chair Erickson and Council member White asked how the turkey problem is being
addressed in Cache County. Dolling replied baiting and trapping is going on, but there
needs to be more hunting pressure applied to turkeys.

Council member Zilles asked if the $50.00 bounty on coyotes is still in effect. Dolling
said it is and DWR is working with Wildlife Services for helicopter removal of coyotes as
well.

Vice Chair Erickson noted there is a large herd of over 140 elk at the base of Crow
Mountain that may move closer to the highway. Dolling said landowners in the area
have permits to take cow elk and DWR is monitoring the herd.

Council member White said the Conservation Districts are doing resource assessments
also and asked who he should contact. Dolling responded it will probably be the Natural
Resources Conservation Service and he will email White the contact information.

v Review of Roberts Rules of Order — Vice Chair David Erickson briefly reviewed
Roberts Rules and commended the Council on their usually proper decorum and
distributed written copies of Roberts Rules to the Council. Erickson reminded the
Council that any suspension of the rules requires a two thirds approval and any time a
Council member leaves the meeting, permission should be asked of the Chair before
leaving.

speaker, they should ask the Chair's permission to address the individual.

they concurred that Merrill's listed goals are theirs. Zilles asked for clarification on

summarize what the Council decided on new building requests/roads and present it at
the January 26, 2016 Council meeting.

¥ 2016 Council Member Boards and Committees Assignments — Chairman Merrill
emailed Council members their assignments earlier and asked for feedback. Council

member Robison has asked to be on the Public Relations Committee. Merrill asked if
there were any other requests.

Merrill suggested that the internal auditor serve on the Policy and Procedures
Committee. Clerk/Auditor Zollinger will speak with the internal auditor about that
possibility.

Zollinger noted that the new Cache County UAC Board members should be added to the
assignments list.

Council member Zilles asked about the Road Committee and Attorney Swink discussed
the Ordinance Review Committee.

Chairman Merrill recommended a new shuffling of assignments next year.
