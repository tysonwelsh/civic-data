Itis with great pleasure that we recognize Kaylie Lake as Utah County Health
Department's Employee of the Quarter. Since joining the department, Kaylie has
consistently demonstrated excellence, innovation, and dedication in her role as a Health
Educator. Her work has had a measurable impact on food access, public health education,
and community engagement across the region. Kaylie’s leadership in grant writing and
coalition building has been instrumental in advancing health equity. Notable
accomplishments include:

¢ Securing a $25,000 grant to develop a Healthy Food Access Action Plan.

¢ Founding and leading the Mountainlands Regional Food Access Steering
Committee.

¢ Serving as Treasurer for the Utah Chapter of the Society of Public Health
Education.

« Establishing partnerships that support food security, nutrition education, and
breastfeeding accommodations.

Her work spans a wide range of initiatives—from promoting farmers markets and SNAP
benefits to support community gardens and workplace nutrition policies. Kaylie’s ability
to connect with stakeholders, develop sustainable programs, and advocate for
underserved populations reflects her deep commitment to public health.

In addition to her professional achievements, Kaylie is admired for her character and
work ethic. She balances her responsibilities with grace, all while being a devoted wife
and mother to her young daughter. Her colleagues describe her as:

e Driven and creative, always bringing fresh ideas and solutions.

¢ Positive and approachable, fostering a welcoming and collaborative environment.

¢ Empathetic and thoughtful, making others feel seen, heard, and supported.

¢ Astatewide expert, frequently sought after for her knowledge in food security and
breastfeeding support.

Wendy Osbourne, Founder and Director of Tabitha’s Way Food Pantry, shared: “It has
been an honor working with Kaylie Lake. She has been a tremendous ally in helping our
organization fight food insecurity and is instrumental in addressing determinants of health
within our communities. Kaylie is passionate about helping organizations improve their
services and works tirelessly to accomplish this work.”

Additional comments from colleagues highlight her professionalism, warmth, and
impact:

¢  “Kaylie is a fantastic coworker—driven, cheerful, and full of great ideas.”

e “She has a wonderful can-do attitude and is always smiling.”

¢  “Kaylie is a ray of sunshine in the office and a genuine friend.”

* “She is professional, organized, and empathetic—making everyone feel cared for.”
*  “Kaylie is a strong asset to our division and a statewide leader in her field.”

Kaylie exemplifies the values of public service, leadership, and compassion. Her
contributions continue to elevate the work of the Health Department and improve the
lives of those in our community. We are proud to honor her as Employee of the Quarter
and look forward to her continued success.
