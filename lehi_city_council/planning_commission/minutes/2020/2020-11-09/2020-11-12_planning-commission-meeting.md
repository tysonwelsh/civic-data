2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Robert and Diane Myers’ request for review of
a Development Code Amendment to Section 26.020.E., allowing Accessory Dwelling Units
(ADU) units above garages on parcels greater than half an acre.
    Brittney Harris, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item. She clarified they
    would like it above and next to the garage with language to allow to that. ADU's allowed if
    garage is attached to the main structure. DRC concerns with safety such as units being set
    back from house with access for emergency vehicles and stairway access, specifically the
    Fire Marshalls comments.


    Commissioner Bunker asked about Power's concern being not in favor of the request.
    Brittney clarified it was the same as the Fire Marshals concerns. Commissioner Ellis asked
    about the concerns with the fire. Brittney clarified the concerns were with the lot size and
    compared it to a flag lot, mainly allowing general access to emergency vehicles.
    Commissioner Peterson asked for clarification on whether it was only being allowed above a
    garage. Brittney stated it was worded to allow only above a structure. Ross stated a key point
    was to limit parcels half acres or greater and the reasoning was to allow for additional
    parking. Commissioner Nielsen clarified this was for attached structures only. Kim stated the
    Fire Department did not make any recommendations for verbiage.

    The applicants were present. They addressed the concerns that DRC suggestions.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to forward a positive recommendation of Robert and
Diane Myers’ request for review of a Development Code Amendment to Section 26.020.E.,
allowing Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) units above garages on parcels greater than half
an acre with the request that Staff work with the Fire Department to add language deemed
necessary to mitigate the concerns with the DRC comments about access to those units and also
any other DRC comments that are noted. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes. The motion
passed unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Danny Gutierrez’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of Pioneer Heights, a 45-lot residential subdivision to be located at
approximately 1452 West 900 North.
    Brittney presented information on the agenda item. General plan amendment and zone
    change. DRC had a comment on 109 meeting road frontage and the applicant updated and
    meets the requirements. Subdivision drainage must be contained within the subdivision.

    Commissioner Nielsen asked about the half road with two way traffic. Ross clarified that half
    road is more like 2/3 road and the curb and gutter is essentially not maintained on one side.

    The applicant, Danny Gutierrez, was present. He discussed the half road and straightening it
    out to a full road.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

    Bart Peacock stated he has some concerns. One being a water easement that has been
    recorded illegally. He feels strongly that it needs to be addressed before moving forward.
    His other concern is the road that is stubbing to the west. He has a hard time comprehending
    why the road can't benefit all of the landowners down that way. He has not had contact
    with the developer about that, but he wants it on record that he doesn't agree with leaving
    that as a little road. He hopes that the comments are read and given proper consideration.
    The applicant addressed Mr. Peacocks comment and wants him to know that he has given the
    road consideration. He has worked with the planning department and hoped to make a
    recommendation to planning by making a variance with lot number 109 for leeway but it
    would be up to the Planning Commission and City Council. Ross stated 1220 are improving
    the frontage and planning on widening. The road that is stubbed to the west has
    connectivity standards but only requires block length. Ross addressed the water line
    easement that was recorded improperly and informed Mike West. Ross is not sure how
    this will affect the properties where it is clerical and suggested the City Attorney's look
    into it. Craig Chambers stated it is a separate issue and can be addressed separately.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to forward a positive recommendation of Danny
Gutierrez’s request for preliminary subdivision review of Pioneer Heights, a 45-lot
residential subdivision to be located at approximately 1452 West 900 North; including all
DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Peterson seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, Yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes. The motion
passed unanimously.
