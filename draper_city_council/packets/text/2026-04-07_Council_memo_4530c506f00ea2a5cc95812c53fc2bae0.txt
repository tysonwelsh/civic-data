MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2026-04-07
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1715


Comments:


On the request of Joe Colosimo, representing Skaggs Catholic Center LLC, for a Text
Amendment to Draper City Municipal Code Sections 9-3-040 and 9-10-090 Table 9-10-1
regarding Private Schools in residential zoning districts.


The application is known as the Juan Diego Text Amendment Request. This application is
a request for approval of a Zoning Text Amendment to Draper City Municipal Code
Sections 9-3-040 and 9-10-090 Table 9-10-10. The purpose of the request is to allow
private schools as a conditional use in residential zones.



The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their March 12, 2026 meeting and
forwarded a positive recommendation on a 5-0 vote with alternative language presented
by Staff and the recommendation that the text include a definition for the word Ancillary.
The alternative language presented by Staff included limiting private schools to the R3
zone rather than all residential zones, and adding a footnote with additional limitations,
as follows: "Limited to sites and properties 1 acre in size or less that are ancillary to an
existing private school campus within the city. Site coverage with buildings and structures
limited to no more than two-thousand square feet (2,000 ft²). Maximum height of buildings
and structures limited to twenty-five feet (25’)."

The applicant has suggested an possible change that would include a proximity or
distance standard for the new property to the existing private school. If the City Council is
concerned about there being no proximity standard, they can consider amending the
presented language with one. The applicant's concern is if there should be a different
standard if the property is abutting the existing private school versus property that does
not directly abut an existing private school.
