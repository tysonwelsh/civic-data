3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Kraig Johnson’s request for review of the
Johnson General Plan Amendment, amending 4-acres of property from LDR (low density
residential) with a NCN (neighborhood commercial node) overlay to HDR (high density
residential) with a NCN (neighborhood commercial node) overlay at 3333 West 1500 North.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Kraig Johnson, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross stated the difference is not a big property designation, but the area has a lot of density
already. He discussed the bonus density with the Commissioners. Commissioner Everett said that
a MDR might be a good compromise between the two, but that the applicant is asking for HDR.
Commissioner Nielsen would like some assurance that the system isn't being overburdened.
Commissioner Newall agreed with Commissioner Nielsen. Commissioner Jackson wanted to
hear the City side of the negotiation with the property. Kim Struthers stated he can speak to the
negotiation to the best of his ability. He was under the impression the piece was appraised and
once the piece was bought there may have been some type of verbal agreement on the property.
Commissioner Hemmert stated if this is approved, there needs to be a strong finding of facts.
Ross Dinsdale clarified the difference from MDR and HDR for this property. Commissioner
Nielsen stated a utility study could determine whether this property would overburden the
system. Ross stated this property being re-designated wouldn't have a huge impact, but it would
limit other areas, and that property is still needed for road widening, etc. Sierra clarified that
regardless, the commercial will require and approval. Ross stated the total difference is
approximately ten units if it goes to HDR.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to send a positive recommendation of Kraig
        Johnson’s request for review of the Johnson General Plan Amendment, amending 4-
        acres of property from LDR (low density residential) with a NCN (neighborhood
        commercial node) overlay to HDR (high density residential) with a NCN
        (neighborhood commercial node) overlay at 3333 West 1500 North, that as findings
        resulting from a public meeting, the Commissioners are taking consideration the
        applicants history of property and ownership in relation to the City of Lehi to date;
        including the findings that the infrastructure can handle the development, and also the
        opportunities the applicant may have had if there had not been encouraged sale of
        property to the City. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
        Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed 6 – 1.

3.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Patterson Homes’ request for review of the
Sunset Meadows Plat B Concept, a 3-lot residential subdivision located at 1980 West Snow
Spring Drive.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Ross Welch, was present. He stated there is not a need for another access because
there are already connectivity roads. He expressed frustration over some of the DRC comments.
He stated the problem can be solved by not allowing connectivity on that specific road. He stated
he hasn't had a chance to talk to Kim Struthers about it. Kim stated there is a provision where
they can make an exception. Craig Chambers stated the item would be best suited to be tabled.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing

Sarah Scorsby asked why the city is asking the developer to put a road in. Kim Struthers
addressed the comment by explaining connectivity standards. She stated a neighborhood near her
is a half cul-de-sac and she is frustrated that some developers have different standards. She
doesn't like the idea of a private street.

    Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to send a positive recommendation of Patterson
    Homes’ request for review of the Sunset Meadows Plat B Concept, a 3-lot residential

    consistent with the goals and policies of the general plan and including the DRC comments,

    seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
    Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;

3.11) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City's request for review of the 600 East

The Commissioners discussed the zone change. Commissioner Hemmert stated he was opposed
to the change since the change is not in conformance to the general plan, is not consistent with
land use and zoning, will have substantial adverse effect on surrounding properties, and is not
physically suitable.


Kim Struthers stated that there were recommendations for the general plan to preserve and area.
He stated it was a trade off from property and most of the property is slope.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing

    Motion: Commissioner Hemmert moved to send a negative recommendation for the request
    for review of the 600 East Family Park zone change from TH-5 (transitional holding) to
    R-1-12 (residential) on 4.29-acres located at approximately 600 East and Airport Drive;
    based on the recommendation that it provides minimal housing opportunities, is not in
    conformance of the general plan, is not consistent of the land use and zoning of adjoining
    properties, will have a substantial and adverse effect of surrounding properties, and is not an
    amenity to the immediate area. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert, yes;
    Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, no; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
    Commissioner Eyre. The motion passed 4 – 2.
