             Amanda Makakona stated she would like traffic calming measures to be taken

Resolution 19-06-02

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution transferring $1.5 million to Magna
from Salt Lake County to fund construction:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-06-02                                                   Date: June 25, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN MAGNA AND SALT LAKE COUNTY
       TO TRANSFER $1.5 MILLION TO MAGNA TO FUND THE CONSTRUCTION OF
       2550 SOUTH BETWEEN 7200 WEST AND 8000 WEST PURSUANT TO SB234
       (2018)

       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
§§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501; and



                                              3
DATE           TUESDAY                                 JUNE                              25, 2019

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “Counties;”
and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to SB234 “Inland Port Authority” (2018), the construction of 2550
South between 7200 West and 8000 West was identified by the legislature as a needed roadway
future development; and

        WHEREAS, the attached Interlocal Agreement (“Agreement”), hereinafter known as
Exhibit A, provides Magna $1.5 Million Dollars from the Local Option Transportation Sales Tax
the County has collected to fund projects identified by the Utah State legislature during the 2018
and 2019 General Sessions; and

       WHEREAS, to promote the future development of the property between U-201, 2550
South, 7200 West and 8000 West, the construction of 2550 South is necessary to Magna;

      NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves and authorizes the
Mayor to sign, subject to approval as to form by the Magna Attorney, the attached Interlocal
Agreement and accepts the $1.5 Million from Salt Lake County to construct the 2550 South
roadway between 7200 West and 8000 West.

Fee Waiver

              Greg Schulz stated the Cyprus-Ute Football Program was not included in the 2018
Magna Consolidated Fee schedule; therefore, they are requesting a fee waiver for $2,125 for use
of the Magna-Copper Park.

               Council Member Peel asked if the fee has ever been assessed before 2018.

               Mr. Schulz stated he did not know.

              Paul Ashton stated he did not believe the fee could have been assessed in 2018
because it was not on the Magna Fee schedule.

               Council Member Prokopis, seconded by Council Member Peel, moved to approve
the fee waiver for $2,150 for the 2018 fees imposed on the Cyprus-Ute Football Program for the
