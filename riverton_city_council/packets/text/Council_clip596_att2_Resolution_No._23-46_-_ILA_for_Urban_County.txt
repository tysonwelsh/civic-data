                                      RIVERTON, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 23-46

APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION
   AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY TO CARRY OUT COMMUNITY
DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT (CDBG), EMERGENCY SERVICES GRANT (ESG),
  AND HOME INVESTMENT PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM (HOME) PROGRAMS,
                   ACTIVITIES AND PROJECTS

        WHEREAS, the Utah Local Cooperative Act (UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-101, et seq.)
provides that two local governmental entities, or public agencies, are authorized to enter into
interlocal cooperation agreements with each other, upon adoption of a resolution to do so by their
respective governing bodies, to do what each agency is authorized by law to perform; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City (“City”) and Salt Lake County (“County”) are public
agencies, as defined by the Utah Local Cooperation Act; and

       WHEREAS, City and County therefore desire to enter into an Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit A, to grant approval for the CDBG, ESG, and HOME
program activities and projects; and

        WHEREAS, The City Council finds and determines that approval of the attached
interlocal cooperation agreement will be in the best interests of the City in facilitating accesses to
the associated grant and program funding.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. That the Mayor is authorized to sign an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with Salt Lake
      County to give authority to carry out CDBG, ESG, and HOME program activities and
      projects, in substantially similar form to the copy attached to this Resolution, upon
      verification that said agreement is approved as to legal form by the Riverton City
      Attorney.

   2. This Resolution, assigned No. 23-46, shall take effect immediately upon passage
      and acceptance as provided herein.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
