#3 Aspen Springs Storage Conditional Use Permit 
Zetterquist introduced Connor Smith. 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the Aspen Springs Storage Conditional Use Permit (CUP).

#4 Discussion: 2024 Code Amendment Priorities  
Nelson reviewed the list of suggested 2024 code amendment priorities.

Staff and Commissioners discussed private roadway standards, flag lots, grandfathering old lots, resort 
development standards, short term rentals, detached accessory dwelling units (ADUs), living on property 
while home is under construction, Campgrounds/RVs on property, identifying potential gravel pit areas, 
and noise ordinances. 
Phillips informed the Commissioners that there is policy regarding private roads not becoming county 
roads. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the road manual and bringing that before the Commissioners, zoning 
around Clarkston, how to regulate reservoirs and hydroelectric projects, civil code updates, and 
protecting animal habitat.
