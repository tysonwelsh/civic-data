3.   Text Amendment— Building      Height in Residential Zones; Amend the 2009
     West Jordan City Code to increase the maximum building height to 35 feet in
     Residential Zones; city- wide applicability; City of West Jordan ( applicant)
     Larry Gardner# 27899]

     Larry Gardner explained that recently the definition for building height was amended
     with the Wood Ranch approvals. There are not many issues when the home is on flat
     ground, but on sloped property it can be a problem. The former way to measure was to
     the mid- point of the slope, which is the practice of many cities. The new definition for
     height is the vertical distance under the roof or any roof element to the nearest
     measure of existing grade. If this amendment is not approved then some of the
     buildings may need to be lowered in height. However, 35 feet is standard in most of
     the adjacent jurisdictions. He showed an example of how height would be measured
     on a sloping property. Wood Ranch has an additional element that requires the
     building to be stepped.
