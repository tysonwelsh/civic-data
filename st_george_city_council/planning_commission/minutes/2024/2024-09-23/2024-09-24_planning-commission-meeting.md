REQUEST FOR A ZONING REGULATION AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request to amend a portion of the City of St. George
     Code, Title 10-8C-1, regarding the allowed Uses in the Manufacturing Zones.
     The applicant would like to use a small portion of their warehousing space
     for their esthetics, permanent makeup and tattooing. The applicant is
     Bowler Esthetics, INC. Case No. 2024 – ZRA - 016 - PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: Traditionally, commercial service
      businesses have not been allowed in the manufacturing zones. The City wants to
      preserve the manufacturing zones primarily for manufacturing uses. The applicant
      approached staff with the request for this type of business and upon review of the
      request staff felt the best path forward would be a code amendment. Commercial
      retail has been allowed in 20% of the building and staff felt this would be appropriate
      for commercial service businesses as well as an ancillary use to the primary
      business. Case No. 2024-ZRA-016 (Staff – Brenda Hatch).
