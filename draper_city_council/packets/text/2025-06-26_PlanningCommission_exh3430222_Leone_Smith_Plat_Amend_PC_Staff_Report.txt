SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 0.92 acres located on the northwest corner of 300 East and 13560 South, at
approximately 13530 S. 300 East (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned R3.
The applicant is requesting to move the property line between the two properties ten feet
to the east. The purpose for the change is to create a slightly larger property to the west
for a potential buyer.

The two-lot minor subdivision was approved administratively in July 2023. One lot is
currently vacant and one is developed with a single-family residence. The single-family
home was built around 1970 is located on the eastern lot and faces towards 300 East. This
application is the first amendment to the recorded plat.
