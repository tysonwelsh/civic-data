Work Session (3:51 PM)
1)
Pledge of Allegiance (3:51 PM)
2)
Update regarding the Climate Change and Public Health Speaker Series-- Physical and Mental Health; Dr. Phil Bondurant and Emily Quinton (3:53 PM)
Attachment: 9.6.23 Slides Climate Change Public Health Speaker Series.pdf
Dr. Phil Bondurant, Health Department Director, introduced the Public Health Department’s actions around Climate Change. Emily Quinton, Sustainability Program Manager, provided details about the upcoming speaker on the topic of the physical and mental impacts of climate change. Council Members commented and asked questions.
3.
Convene as the Governing Board of the North Summit Fire Service District (4:07 PM)
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to convene as the Governing Board of the North Summit Fire Service District. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
1)
Discussion and approval of changes to Sections 5, 6, 8 and 11 of the District's Personnel Policies; Ben Nielson and Tyler Rowser (4:07 PM)
Attachment: Staff Report-North Summit Fire Personnel Policies.pdf
Ben Nielson, North Summit Fire Chief, introduced the proposed policy changes, and Tyler Rowser, Administrative Captain, provided detail on the proposed changes. Council Members asked questions. Captain Rowser responded.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve changes to Sections 5, 6, 8 and 11 of the District's Personnel Policies with an amendment to the change in section 11.A.3 changing what is currently listed as part-time employee shifts beginning at 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM to 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
Canice Harte made a motion to dismiss as the Governing Board of the North Summit Fire Service District and convene as the Board of Trustees of Summit County Service Area #6. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
4.
Convene as the Board of Trustees of Summit County Service Area #6 (4:18 PM)
1)
Consideration and adoption of Resolution 2023-07A, a Resolution Annexing Certain Real Property into Summit County Service Area #6, Summit County, Utah; Mike Kendell, County Engineer (4:18 PM)
Attachment: Resolution 2023-07A-Service Area 6 Annexation (Snyderville Basin Subdivisions).pdf
Mike Kendell, Engineer, presented Resolution 2023-07A for Summit County Service Area #6.
Canice Harte made a motion to adopt Resolution 2023-07A annexing certain real property into Summit County Service Area #6. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
Attachment: Resolution 2023-07A executed
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to dismiss as the Board of Trustees of Summit County Service Area #6 and convene as the Board of Equalization. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
5.
Convene as the Board of Equalization (4:20 PM)
1)
Discussion and approval of 2023 stipulations; Cindy Keyes, Chase Black and Stephanie Poll (4:21 PM)
Attachment: Staff Report-BOE Stipulations 090623.pdf
Auditor Cindy Keyes presented the suggested stipulations with respect to assessed values.
Council members asked clarifying questions, to which Stephanie Poll, Assessor, responded.
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to approve the 2023 stipulations as presented. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
Canice Harte made a motion to dismiss as the Board of Equalization. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
6.
Consideration of Approval (4:41 PM)
1)
Discussion and action regarding a Discretionary Tax Abatement for Dirk and Trudy Gardner, Parcel CD-638-B; Stephanie Poll (4:41 PM)
Attachment: Assessor Statement, Gardner, Parcel CD-638-B.pdf
Attachment: Discretionary Tax Abatement Application, Gardner, Parcel CD-638-B.pdf
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to deny Discretionary Tax Abatement for Dirk and Trudy Gardner, Parcel CD-638-B. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
Stephanie Poll, Assessor, presented the matter to Council. Dirk and Trudy Gardner, the property owners, provided the history of their request for abatement. Council Members asked questions. Assessor Poll responded.
2)
Discussion and action regarding a Discretionary Tax Abatement for Lynn Postnieks, Parcel #KRD-16; Stephanie Poll (4:58 PM)
Attachment: Discretionary Tax Abatement Application, Postnieks, Parcel KRD-16.pdf
Attachment: Assessor Statement, Postnieks, Parcel KRD-16.pdf
Stephanie Poll, Assessor, introduced the Tax Abatement for Parcel #KRD-16.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve a Discretionary Tax Abatement for Lynn Postnieks, Parcel #KRD-16. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
3)
Discussion and approval of a Franchise Agreement between Summit County and Qwest Corporation dba CenturyLink QC; Michael Kendell and David Thomas (5:09 PM)
Attachment: Staff Report-Franchise Agreement Qwest Corporation dba CenturyLink QC.pdf
Canice Harte made a motion to approve a Franchise Agreement between Summit County and Qwest Corporation dba CenturyLink QC. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
David Thomas, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney, introduced the Agreement pending for Council’s approval. Council Members commented and asked questions. Mr. Thomas responded.
Attachment: Franchise Agreement w/ Qwest Corp. dba Centurylink QC executed
4)
Discussion and approval of Ordinance No. 964, an Ordinance of the County Council of Summit County, Utah, Enacting Title 5, Chapter 2, Section 8 to the Summit County Code, Trespass on Conserved Lands; Dave Thomas (5:21 PM)
Attachment: Ordinance No. 964, Trespass on Conserved Lands-corrected.pdf
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to adopt Ordinance No. 964, an Ordinance of the County Council of Summit County, Utah, Enacting Title 5, Chapter 2, Section 8 to the Summit County Code, Trespass on Conserved Lands including the changes discussed this evening. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Malena Stevens, Christopher Robinson.
David Thomas, Chief Civil Deputy, explained the origin of the proposed Ordinance and how it will assist in enforcing the restrictive covenant on the 910 Cattle Ranch property. Council members asked questions. Mr. Thomas responded.
Attachment: Ordinance 964 executed
