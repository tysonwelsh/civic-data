        Jeffrey G. Holland Day. Chair Harvey read the Proclamation as follows:
                            A Proclamation by the Davis County and Weber County Commissioners declaring July 8th, 2024 as
                            “Colonel Jeffrey G. Holland Day” in Davis and Weber Counties for his induction into the Order of
                                                                 the Western Big Hat.

                          Whereas, this is declaring July 8th 2024 as Colonel Jeffrey G Holland Day. Jeffrey G Holland
                          entered the Air Force and was commissioned from the Air Force Academy in the year 2000. He
                          served in every facet of the Air Force's field level aircraft maintenance enterprise in both garrison
                          and expeditionary environments and was promoted to the rank of Colonel on January 1st, 2020; and

                          Whereas, Colonel Holland is a 2008 graduated the Air Force Institute of Technology with a Master
                          of Science degree in Logistics Management, a 2014 graduate of the John F Kennedy School of
                          Government, Harvard University with a Master in Public Administration, and a 2017 graduate of the
                          Eisenhower School, National Defense University with a Master in National Security; and

                          Whereas, during his career he has served at Headquarters United States Air Force in Europe,
                          Headquarters of United States Transportation Command, and Headquarters United States Air Force,
                          as well as commanding at the Squadron, Group and Wing levels; and

                          Whereas, Colonel Holland is the 75th Airbase Wing commander and Installation Commander at Hill
                          Air Force Base where his Wing provides installation support for the Ogden Air Logistics complex,
                  two fighter wings, and 50 other associate units totaling 27,000 active duty civilian and contractor
                  personnel; and

                  Whereas, during his time at Hill Air Force Base he has commanded with grace, dignity, and a firm
                  commitment to his people and the vital national security missions accomplished at his installation.
                  He worked personally with the base and community leaders to establish the innovative and far-
                  reaching Project One program to increase mission readiness by the strengthening his airman's sense
                  of belonging and community; and

                  Whereas, during his distinguished and illustrious career he has been honored with numerous awards
                  and decorations, including the Air Force Achievement Medal, the Air Force Commendation Medal,
                  the Meritorious Service Medal with four Oak Leaf clusters, the Defense Meritorious Service Medal,
                  and the Legion of Merit; and

                  Whereas, Colonel Holland has been an exemplary leader, a dedicated family man, and a valued
                  friend to our community where he's dramatically improved conditions for the citizens, military
                  members, and military missions residing in Davis and Weber Counties. Now, therefore, the Davis
                  and Weber County Commissioners do hereby proclaim July 8th 2024 as Colonel Jeffrey G Holland
                  Day.

         Lauren Shafer (Elections Director): For the 2024 Republican primary election we had 63,380 registered
         Republican voters with a turnout of 46.2%. We counted 29,286 votes and we rejected 392 ballots. We had
         56 provisional ballots but only 38 of those counted. We rejected one because they had already voted, three
         were incomplete registrations, and fourteen were not affiliated with the Republican Party. For our By Mail
         Ballot stats, 64,162 ballots were mailed out, 28286 were cast, leaving 34840 ballots, or 54%, not returned by
         voters and 1,036 ballots, or 2%, that were returned undeliverable. We did not count 392 ballots; there is an
         error at the bottom which I will fix and get out to you. 131 of those were returned past the deadline; 195 had
         signatures that did not match the voter record; 24 of those were returned unsigned; there were 2 empty
         envelopes; we had 18 signed by someone other than the voter; and 4 ballots were returned with no envelope.
         All of the ballots that were not counted besides the ballots returned with no envelope were given a letter, a
         text, and an email to have time to cure their ballot. We cured as many as we could but not everyone got back
         to us in time.

         Chair Harvey: So it wasn't on your end, it was on the voter's end?

         Ms. Shafer: Yeah, it was on their end. I will now read the results of the Primary results for Weber County:

                  63380 registered voters and 29,285 ballots cast
                  US Senate John Curtis W with 12,986 votes
                  US House 1: Blake Moore with 19835 votes
                  Utah Governor and Lieutenant Governor: Spencer J Cox and Deedra M Henderson with 15592 votes
                  Attorney General: Derek Brown with 11,920 votes
                  State auditor: Ricky Hatch with 16873 votes
                  State School Board 3: Brent Strate with 2,432 votes

         Commissioner Bolos: Of the 195 signatures that did not match the voter record, does that take into account
         the ones that were cured?

         Ms. Shafer: No, those are the ones that were not cured. We had around 574 that we ultimately sent out but
         only around 374 were ones that did not get cured.

         Commissioner Bolos: One more question. ‘Ballot Mailed to Voters’ shows 64,162 with active registered
         voters of 63380. I'm wondering why there were more ballots mailed than registered voters?

         Ms. Shafer: We generate more ballots than we have active registered voters because we send replacement
         ballots. Some people can move in and out of the county before the registration period; we spoil ballots
         because they got mailed to the wrong address or their dog ate it or they lost it, and we send them a new
         ballot.

         Commissioner Froerer: I appreciate the detail that you have before us. I think it just makes us realize how
         secure we are in Weber County with our election process and procedures. Just another thing we do in Weber
         County that we are the best at and that is a result of our great people we have in the election office. I appreciate
         the work.
         Chair Harvey: I appreciate well in advance that there are opportunities for the public to witness the process.
         That process from the beginning to the end including the audit performed gives me confidence in what goes
         on in our elections in this County. I firmly defend our process. Thank you so much for all that you do.
         Commissioner Bolos moved to certify the Results of the 2024 Republican Primary Election; Commissioner
         Freorer seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye
