                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-15

     A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE RIVERTON CITY WATER SHORTAGE
                        MANAGEMENT PLAN

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-7-14 and applicable provisions of the Riverton
City Code, the Riverton City Council is authorized to adopt policies and plans necessary for the
administration and protection of municipal water resources; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City provides culinary water to its residents and is responsible for
ensuring a reliable and sustainable water supply during periods of drought, temporary shortage,
and potential supply interruption; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has developed a Water Shortage Management Plan to
preserve and protect the public health, safety, and welfare by establishing procedures,
conservation measures, and response actions during periods of water shortage; and

        WHEREAS, the Water Shortage Management Plan is intended to support and
complement existing water efficiency ordinances and the Riverton City Water Conservation
Plan; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that adoption of the Water
Shortage Management Plan will promote responsible water use, provide clear direction to water
users, and ensure effective management of limited water resources; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, determines that it is in the best
interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to adopt the Water
Shortage Management Plan as recommended by City staff.


        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Water Shortage Management Plan, as provided in Exhibit A of this
          Resolution.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
