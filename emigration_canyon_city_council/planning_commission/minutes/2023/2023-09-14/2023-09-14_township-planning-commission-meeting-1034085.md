Mr. Starley introduced updates to Title 18 and Title 19. Commissioners and staff had a brief
discussion regarding smaller sized lots, customization in commercial, supporting FCOZ, focus on
the policy questions and preciseness.
Hope the commission and council can work together within the timeframe. Timeline to have a
workshop in October, November, and December. Preparation for the workshops, highlighting
policy questions needing answers, and coming up with ideas to discuss.
Discussion on process of reviewing original document, changed document, and comments.
Differences between current code, modeled code, and tailored code to each community. First
modified draft, removed unused codes, tried to fit Emigration Canyon, and needs.
Opportunities to add more language, open space discussions and control where activities take place.
Zoning pattern and intended uses.
Commissioners feel a working meeting together would be beneficial before a workshop.
Determining to review the document in three phases or the full document.
