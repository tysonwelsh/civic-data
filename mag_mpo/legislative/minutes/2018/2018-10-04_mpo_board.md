Regional Transportation Plan Goals and Performance Measures Discussion - Bob Allen, MAG Bob Allen presented the goals and performance measures for the future Regional Transportation Plan, TransPlan50, that will be adopted June 2019. The current federal transportation bill, MAP-21, requires performance measures to link planning and programming. MAG recently met with each of the cities to get their input on current and future needs which were incorporated into the goals and performance measures. Local Plan Integration was added to ensure Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee October 4, 2018 Page 3 that after the Regional Planning Committee approves the plan, the i nformation is used and integrated in the transit goal to encourage communities to adopt a local plan. Suggestions were made in regards to the vision statement that include replacing fuel our economy with maintain quality of life or enhance quality of life. MAG encourages comments and suggestions as the goals and performance measures are still in draft form and are being prepared for adoption in the summer of 2019. Volkswagen Mitigation Plan & Government Entities Eligible for Project Funds - Lisa Burr, Utah DEQ Utah received a settlement as a result of 7,ooo registered Audi and VW with illegal software emitting 40 times more emissions. Through the process of public input and an advisory board, it was determined to spend 85% of the settlement money on the following: 1. Replace or repowering Class 4-8 local freight trucks. 2. Replacing or repowering Class 4-8 school buses, shuttle buses, or transit buses. 3. Purchasing, installing and maintaining light-duty electric-vehicle supply equipment. Applications to receive funding from the VW Settlement will be accepted October 1, 2018 through November 30, 2018. For applications, additional information and eligibility criteria go to https://deq.utah.gov/air-quality/volkswagen-settlement. MAG will assist with the application process upon request. For additional information contact: Lisa Burr: 801-536-4019, lburr@utah.gov Courtney Ehrlich: 801-536-4430, cehrlich@utah.gov Regional Transportation Plan - Draft Needs Based Project Lists - Shawn Eliot, MAG Shawn Eliot presented on the Regional Transportation Plan, TransPlan50. The plan is updated every 4 years and lists regional highway, transit, and active transportation projects, and is scheduled for completion June 2019. Information was collected from the meetings and transportation summits conducted this year with mayors, commissioners, city council members and planning commission members and staff. The information and proposed modeling from these meetings were presented in detail via www.mountainland.org/plandevelopment. Mayor Fugal moved that the MPO Regional Planning Committee, based on the MPO staff and TAC recommendation, approve the draft listing of projects. This approval allows MPO staff to present the needs-based listing of projects to the public knowing that it is a draft and further refinement could occur. Mayor Mann seconded the motion. Provo A Corridor Preservation Fund application was presented on a location within 820 North Provo Corridor, #19 on the Eligible Corridors Map listed on Phase 1 of the Regional Transportation Plan. Mountainland MPO Regional Planning Committee October 4, 2018 Page 4 The Corridor Study was completed November 2016 with recommended realignment and widening to 5 lanes for the preferred alignment of the future project. Owner is certified as a willing seller. The property is appraised at $256,000 with anticipated acquisition costs up to $7,500 making the total request $263,500. The project is a proper use of Corridor Preservation funds and has been recommended for approval from TAC. Mayor Brunst moved that this Provo City Corridor Preservation Program application be approved for funding as submitted, with said approval to expire one year from today. Mayor Child seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. b. Springvile A Corridor Preservation Fund application was presented on a location within 1200 West Springville Corridor, #34 on the Eligible Corridors Map listed on Phase 1 of the Regional Transportation Plan. The location was originally planned as a 4 -lane highway and later designed wit h split alignments. The northbound leg is funded in the TIP and the southbound leg is not. Owner is a certified willing seller. The property for both lots is appraised for $189,600 with anticipated acquisition costs of $3,900, making the total request $193,500. Springville City will be required to pay costs above the amount listed on the application request because the lots have utilities that need to be paid for. The project is a proper use of Corridor Preservation funds and has been recommended for approval from TAC. Mayor Frost moved that this Springville City Corridor Preservation Program application be approved for funding as submitted, with said approval to expire one year from today . Commissioner Lee seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
