       2.1     Discussion Related to The Point Project. (Presenter, Alan Matheson).

Alan Matheson and Lance Bullen were present to share information related to The Point project.
Mr. Matheson reported that Mr. Bullen is part of the Innovation Point Partners, which is the group
selected as the initial developers of the site. He reported that earlier in the year, there was a seven-
month process to choose the appropriate team. Ultimately, Innovation Point Partners was selected.
Innovation Point Partners was composed of Lincoln Property Company, Colmena Group,
Wadsworth Development Group, and CRTKL. That team had an impressive combination of
national and local expertise. There was a desire to make sure The Point project is done right.

A few weeks earlier, demolition of the site was initiated. Mr. Matheson reported that this included
the removal of a guard tower. He noted that the demolition process was underway and would
continue for several months. The previous week, the Board for The Point project was presented
with a Development Plan for Phase One. The intention was to review that Development Plan
during the Work Session. Mr. Bullen introduced himself and stated that he works for Colmena
Group. He reiterated that Colmena Group is one of the partners in Innovation Point Partners.
Many people were working on the project and he was excited to be involved in something that had
the potential to benefit others. Mr. Bullen shared a slideshow presentation with those present.

Mr. Bullen shared a map of the site and pointed out Phase One, which comprises 90 acres of the
600-acre site. The intention of Phase One was to connect River to Range, a trail connection system,
and Central Park. An urban, transit-ready area would be created. Along with the Framework Plan,
there was a desire to create a walkable shopping experience that passed parallel to River to Range.
It will have a good sense of character and would draw visitors to the area. Mr. Bullen shared a
Site Plan with the Council. He identified the River to Range Trail, Central Park, and the retail
experience that was discussed. It would be called the Promenade. For the development, the
intention was to start at the center and work outward. The Site Plan showed a transit-ready system.
Although transit is very important, there were still some transit-related details to finalize. He felt
it was important to allow for transit in the development.

the proposal, so a portion of the units would be offered at affordable rates. The idea was to match
the job and income profiles so that it would be possible for people to both live and work in the
area. Phase One would have more apartments but there could be for-sale opportunities in the future
as other parts of the development are explored.
