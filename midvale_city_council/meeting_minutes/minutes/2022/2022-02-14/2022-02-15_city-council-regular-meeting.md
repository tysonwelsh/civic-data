Adam Olsen said the proposed Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment included herein
intends to address required updates to Title 17 Zoning based on amendments to the Land
Use, Development, and Management Act (LUDMA) established by HB 1003 during the
2021 Ulah State Legislative Session. H81003 addresses municipalities' ability to
implement design review regulations for single-family dwellings and duplex dwellings
(with some exceptions). Design review is generally defined as the regulation of the
following types of architectural and site details:
        . exterior color;
        . type or style of exterior cladding material;
        . style, dimensions, or materials of a roof structure, roof pitch, or porch;
        . exterior nonstructural architectural ornamentation;
        . location, design, placement, or architectural styling of a window or door;
        . location, design, placement, or architectural styling of a garage door, not including
       a rearloading garage door;
        . number or type of rooms,
       . interior layout of a room;

       o    Design Review Requirements
