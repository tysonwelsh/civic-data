7. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE 2023
UTAH COUNTY BUDGET.
       -ADAM BECK, Attorney

(Timestamp: 2:20)
RUDY LIVINGSTON, BUDGET MANAGER – STATED THEY WILL BE MOVING ARPA
FUNDS INTO TWO DIFFERENT CIP FUNDS. THERE IS A NEED TO MAKE AN
ADJUSTMENT IN THE GENERAL FUND FOR SALARY AND BENEFIT EXPENDITURES
BECAUSE THE MARKET STUDY RESULTS CAME IN HIGHER THAN EXPECTED. HE
SUGGESTS THAT AN ADJUSTMENT IS MADE TO REDUCE THE BUDGETED SALES
TAX REVENUES BY THREE MILLION DOLLARS. THEY WILL BE MAKING AN
INCREASE IN THE BUDGET FOR THE RECORDER’S OFFICE RENOVATION. THE
PUBLIC DEFENDER’S OFFICE REQUESTS TO FUND ADDITIONAL PUBLIC DEFENDER
CONTRACTS.
COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH, COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER, COMMISSIONER
GORDON AND RUDY LIVINGSTON – DISCUSS THE BUDGET AND THE NEED FOR
ADDITIONAL FUNDS IN THE FUTURE. TRUTH IN TAXATION IS MENTIONED AND A
MODERATE INCREASE IN PROPERTY TAXES IS SUGGESTED.
COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER – EXPRESSES HER CONCERN WITH UTAH
COUNTY RUNNING A STRUCTURALLY IMBALANCE BUDGET. THE REASON THERE IS

MONEY, IS BECAUSE THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT PRINTED IT AND GAVE IT TO
UTAH COUNTY THROUGH THE CARES ACT AND ARPA. TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY
MILLION DOLLARS IS THE AMOUNT UTC WAS GIVEN AND THERE IS ONLY FIFTY
MILLION DOLLARS LEFT. UTAH COUNTY DOES NOT HAVE A STRUCTURALLY
BALANCED BUDGET AND IT IS NOT FISCALLY RESPONSIBLE TO CONTINUE TO
VOTE FOR MORE SPENDING.
COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH – STATED THAT HE FEELS IT IS NECESSARY TO USE
EITHER ARPA FUNDS OR MONEY FROM THE GENERAL FUND TO GET THROUGH
THIS DIFFICULT SITUATION. HE AGREES THIS IS FISCALLY IRRESPONSIBLE AND
ALSO AGREES THAT SOMETHING NEEDS TO BE DONE TO CORRECT THE
OVERSPENDING.
COMMISSIONER GORDON – STATED HE WAS A PART OF TRUTH AND TAXATION AS
A CITY COUNCILMAN. HE UNDERSTANDS THAT BALANCING THE BUDGET
SOMETIMES MEANS NOT LETTING THE PROPERTY TAX RATE FALL AND
SOMETIMES IT MEANS RAISING TAXES.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER – STATED SHE CAN SUPPORT BEING FISCALLY
RESPONSIBLE AND BALANCING THE BUDGET. SHE BELIEVES CONTINUING TO
HAVE A STRUCTURALLY IMBALANCED BUDGET IS IRRESPONSIBLE.

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH – STATED HE DOES NOT BELIEVE SUPPORTING THIS
IS IRRESPONSIBLE.

GREG ROBINSON, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH, DALE
EYRE, COUNTY ATTORNEY, COMMISSIONER GORDON, COMMISSIONER POWERS
GARDNER AND DUSTIN THOMPSON, CLENERA EMPLOYEE - DISCUSS THE

DECOMMISSIONING PLAN FOR THE FARADAY SOLAR ENERGY PROJECT. THE NEED
TO ENSURE THAT THERE WILL BE FINANCIAL RESOURCES AVAILABLE WHEN THE
TIME COMES FOR DECOMMISSIONING IS TALKED ABOUT. THE COST IS BASED ON
2022-2023 LABOR RATES WITH NO ALLOWANCE FOR INFLATION. IT IS POINTED
OUT THAT THERE ARE TWO THINGS THAT MAY HELP MITIGATE INFLATION. THE
FIRST ONE, THERE ARE LEASE AGREEMENTS WITH THE PROPERTY OWNERS THAT
REQUIRE FARADAY TO REMOVE ALL STRUCTURES AND SECOND, THE
CONDITIONAL USE REQUIREMENT SET BY THE COUNTY REQUIRES THEM TO
REMOVE ALL STRUCTURES. IT IS IMPORTANT TO HAVE THE AREA RESTORED TO
ITS PRE-SOLAR CONDITION. A PARENT GUARANTEE IS IN PLACE. THE SPECIFIC
REQUIREMENT OF A BOND OR AN ESCROW WAS NOT GIVEN AND THERE IS NO
WAY TO PREDICT THE FUTURE DECOMMISSIONING COST OF SALVAGE OR
PROFITABILITY OF A SOLAR PROJECT. THE COMMISSION NEEDS TO BE AWARE
THAT THE COMPANY DOES HAVE A DEVELOPMENT SCHEDULE THAT NEEDS TO BE
FOLLOWED. ZONING AND DEVELOPMENT RULES ARE DESIGNED TO PROTECT THE
CURRENT OWNERS, FUTURE OWNERS AND ALL SURROUNDING OWNERS, AND ALL
CITIZENS THAT MIGHT USE OR BE CONNECTED TO THE PROPERTY, THIS IS WHY
UTAH COUNTY IS INVOLVED IN THIS SITUATION. MOST OF THE PROJECT’S
MATERIAL IS STEEL AND THERE ARE NO HAZARDOUS MATERIALS. THE
LANDOWNERS DO NOT HAVE A DECOMMISSIONING GUARANTEE WITH THE
PARENT COMPANY. THE REVENUE OF THIS PROJECT, FROM A PRICING
STANDPOINT, IS KNOWN AND THE DECOMMISSIONING COSTS HAVE BEEN
CONSIDERED AND PLANNED FOR. IF THE COMMISSIONERS WISH, CLENERA IS
HAPPY TO SHOW EXAMPLES OF THEIR DEALINGS WITH OTHER GOVERNMENT
ENTITIES IN SIMILAR SITUATIONS.
