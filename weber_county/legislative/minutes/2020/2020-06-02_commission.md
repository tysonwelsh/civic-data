G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. DESIGNATION OF A REPRESENTATIVE TO REPRESENT WEBER COUNTY AT MEETINGS OF THE WEBER
      CENTER OWNERS ASSOCIATION
      Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, presented this housekeeping item stating that the Weber
      Center is part of the Weber Center Owners Association, of which there are three primary owners—Weber
      County, the Municipal Building Authority (MBA), and the Land of Og. A requirement of the Association
      calls for a representative from each of the owners to be designated to attend the condo association meetings.
      At the condo association meeting a management committee will be set up, which will include one
      representative from the County/MBA and one from Woodbury. Chair Froerer noted that the Woodbury
      Corporation is the other partner in the HOA and has a 25% ownership in the building. Commissioner
      Jenkins said that he and Commissioner Harvey had spoken and agreed that Chair Froerer should be the
      representative for various reasons, including his real estate background.
      Commissioner Jenkins moved to designate Commissioner Gage Froerer to represent Weber County at
      meetings of the Weber Center Owners Association; Chair Froerer seconded.
      Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     2. CONTRACT WITH FREEDOM RIDERS FOR THERAPEUTIC RIDING IN THE GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER
        Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, stated that this contract is back before the Commission because
        the original contract dates could not be honored due to COVID-19.
        Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contract with Freedom Riders for therapeutic riding in the
        Golden Spike Event Center; Chair Froerer seconded.
        Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS: Commissioner Jenkins said that there had been difficult issues in the past week
in the county and that things finally had started to slow down and the county was moving forward. A contract is
in the works to demolish the Weber Center parking structure. Chair Froerer said that that 99% of communications
that he receives from the public are positive about staff, and that he is energized about how well staff takes a
personal interest in serving the public.
