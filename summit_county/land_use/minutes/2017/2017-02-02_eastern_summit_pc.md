Findings of Fact:  
1.       The East Wanship Irrigation Company submitted a Conditional Use Permit application 
to construct an underground water transmission line exceeding 12 inches in diameter in 
and along portions of the East Wanship Ditch. 
2.       The proposed underground water transmission line is approximately 3 miles in 
length. 
3.       The applicant recorded easements along the existing ditch alignment in which the 
pipeline is to be constructed. 
4.       The proposed underground water transmission line deviates from the prescriptive 
use ditch alignment in 1 location.  The application has obtained and recorded easements 
from the property owners where said alignment deviation occurs. Community 
Development Staff will verify precise easement locations. 
5.       A public hearing on this matter was held on February 2, 2017. 
6.       A water Transmission line exceeding 12 inches in diameter is a Conditional Use 
Permit in all zones per the Eastern Summit County Development Code.

Conclusions of Law:  
1.       The proposed underground water transmission line use is appropriate in the 
proposed location, taking into account the nature of the use, its relationship to surrounding 
land uses and its impacts on the natural environment. 
2.       The proposed underground water transmission line use is in general compliance with 
the Development Evaluation Standards in Chapter 2 of the Eastern Summit County Code. 
3.       The proposed underground water transmission line use will not be in violation of any 
county, state, or federal laws. 
4.       The applicant has shown evidence of landowner approval for the proposed 
underground water transmission line use. 
5.       The applicant has demonstrated the requisite skills and experience to ensure that the 
proposed underground water transmission line use will be conducted in a safe and orderly 
manner. 
6.       The proposed underground water transmission line use will not adversely affect, in a 
significant manner, the health, safety, or welfare of the general public. 
 
Conditions of Approval:  
1.       All required engineering permits shall be obtained and revegetation bonding 
provided prior to commencement of construction activity. 
2.       A copy of the plans shall be provided to the North Summit Pressurized Irrigation 
Company to identify areas where the 2 pipelines intersect.
