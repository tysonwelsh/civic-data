     Motion:      An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-18, as currently constituted, has been

Mr. Borget provided summary of what the initiative entails. He explained that the funding would be
used for banners/signage, nametags for volunteers, safety vests, meals, and days of service.

Councilor Shipley asked how large geographically the initiative will be during this first year.

Tara Riddle, Ombudsman, responded that the funds would mostly be used to set up the Community
Resource Centers. She also discussed the two neighborhoods that are the targeted areas during the first
year.

Chair Hoban asked what the value the church is providing in comparison to the city’s contribution.

Ms. Riddle responded that the church is providing the facilities rent free and all the volunteers. She
stated that her belief is the city is receiving the better side of the deal.

more Police Officers.

Becky Bogden, of Provo, shared her support for this initiative.

roots down in Provo. He shared his belief that using funds to better the community is a good idea.

Paul Oscarson, of Provo, said he has heard nothing but positive things from the initiative in West Valley.

shared in a presentation. She shared her excitement for the initiative.

10.         An ordinance amending design standards for the West Gateway Zone. Citywide
            application. (PLOTA20220010) (1:50:38)

       Motion:      An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-15, as currently constituted, has been
                    made by council rule.

Dustin Wright, City Planner, shared the amendments being proposed.

Councilor MacKay shared her concerns with how expensive construction costs are and said she does not
want the cost to prevent the area from being developed.

Mr. Wright responded.

Councilor Fillmore said we need to make the area more welcoming and inviting. He said the only way to
accomplish that is to elevate the design standards.

Vice-Chair Handley opened public comment.

Quinn Peterson, chair of the design review committee, said this gateway is the first impression of our
city. He shared a recent experience with a development that changed their design in the area without
any design standards. He mentioned his desire is to make sure that does not happen again. He also said
he does not believe these design standards will deter developers from wanting to start projects in the
area.

            property, generally located at 1029 West 1020 North, from One-Family Residential (R1.8)
            to Low-Density Residential (LDR). Rivergrove Neighborhood. (PLRZ20220075) (2:04:42)

       Motion:      An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-16, as currently constituted, has been
                    made by council rule.

Dustin Wright, City Planner, presented.

Chair Hoban shared his distaste for one-off applications like this. He asked if this would provide any legal
precedents.

Brian Jones responded that this does open the door for others to apply. He also added that this would
allow anything that falls under that zone to be established in that area.

Bill Peperone, Director of Development Services, agreed that it does allow others to apply for zone
changes. He also mentioned the property is surrounded by duplexes and said it is fitting to entertain the
request.

Councilor Whipple added that the size of the lot prohibits many other options.
