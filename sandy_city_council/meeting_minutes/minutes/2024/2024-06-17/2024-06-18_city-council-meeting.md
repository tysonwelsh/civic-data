Public Hearing Continued.
Council considered proposed amendments to the FY2025 Budget and budget resolutions.
Council Member Stroud reviewed the Stroud budget amendments regarding funding the
Administrative Law Judge and funding a municipal buildings management study on office
space needs of department staff. These amendments have been discussed with the
Council at prior Council meetings. Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director,
recommended the utilization of a different funding source of one-time funds to allocate for
the buildings management study rather than utilizing funds from the Municipal Building
Fund. Council discussion and questions followed. Council expressed support for voting on
the ALJ amendment this evening.
Council Member Sharkey reviewed the Sharkey budget amendments related to funding
the city-wide Bulk Waste program. The Sharkey amendment listed as Item E was
reviewed with the Council at last week's meeting. Council Member Sharkey thanked the
Council for an informative discussion on the bulk waste funding at last week's meeting.
She is open for a vote by the Council on the proposed amendment, listed as Item E on
the agenda. She also presented an additional proposal related to the funding of the bulk
waste program, listed as Item C on the agenda. This amendment proposed a
restructuring of the fees accessed to the residents, and splits the fee into two parts: one
fee accessed on the customers of Waste Management for weekly waste pick up and a
separate fee accessed on all residents related to the costs associated with the bulk
waste program. Administration expressed support for the elimination of the transfer to the
bulk waste program and supported the tentative budget as proposed, with respect to the
funding of the bulk waste program. Brian Kelly, Administrative Services Director, provided
an additional explanation regarding the funding and fees associated with the increased
costs of the weekly waste pick up and the bulk waste program. Council questions,
comments, and discussion followed.
Council Member Mecham reviewed the Mecham budget amendment with the Council. He

Council Member Marci Houseman read an email from Kate Johnson, the Director of the
Mountain West Ballet, thanking the Sandy Youth Council for their help with the ballet
performances and acknowledging their service. They are great group of students and
helped make the ballet recital a huge success. Ms. Houseman acknowledged Council
Member D'Sousa for bringing back the Sandy Youth Council and thanked Chris Edwards
and Liz Theriault for coordinating the program for the Council Office.
Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked the Administration and all staff involved with the
Historic Sandy Community Barbecue. It was a great event. The Sandy Farmer's Market
was a wonderful and well attended event. Thank you to all who helped coordinate these
events.
Council Member Stroud mentioned the barbecue was a great event, as was the Farmer's
Market. The Barbie movie at America First Field was very well attended. She
congratulated those fire personnel who were recently sworn in and thanked the Fire
Department for coordinating the swearing in ceremony.
Council Member Sharkey was sorry to have missed the Historic Sandy Barbecue due to
not feeling well. She spoke about the kick off event for the La Caille Estates. It was a
great event. The Swearing In Ceremony for the Fire Department was a wonderful event
and she acknowledged those sworn in.
Council Member Marci Houseman also thanked the staff at Neuroworx for holding their
recent open house and tours. She enjoyed attending the event and learning more about
the amazing work done at Neuroworx and the impact they have on our community.
