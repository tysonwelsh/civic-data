                           NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                  November 12, 2024
                                      7:00 p.m.
                                     21 E 100 N
                                   Nephi, UT 84648

1. The council reviewed a draft ordinance amending the code that governs beer sales on
   Sunday.

2. The council discussed acquiring a piece of land that is available for sale that could
   possibly be used for a future fire station. Mr. Atkinson discussed entering into an
   agreement with the Juab County Fire District that they would purchase the land from the
   city when a new fire station is built. The council agreed with the bullet points for what to
   include in the agreement and directed staff to move forward drafting an agreement with
   the fire district and to present it to the Fire District Board. Councilor Parady suggested
   shortening the terms of the agreement. Councilor Travis Worwood asked why the Fire
   District meetings were not posted on the Utah Public Notice website.

3. Public Comment: Diane Jones presented to the council a map of property that she owns
   on the north edge of the city that she would like annexed into the city. She stated that
   they wanted to be treated as fair as the property owners who have annexations on the
   west side. She realized that the city needed the power line easements on the west
   side, therefore that due to easement agreements the city will annex the west side
   property. Ms. Jones stated that they have a similar situation because the city needs
   the easement across their property to access Nebo Heights on the north side . She has
   several buyers that are interested in her property but they all want the property annexed
   before they would consider purchasing the property. She showed the council a map of
   the Northridge Subdivision road approach that connects to their property. Because the
   road was not finished they would need to put in 1770 North because the Northridge
   Subdivision road wasn’t finished up to the property line. She stated that this has created
   a hardship for them. She is asking to annex the entire piece. She said that it could be
   zoned commercial from Main Street to 200 East and then zoned RH-1l east of 200 East.

     Mayor Seely asked Ms. Jones to submit a concept plan to the city administration staff to
     examine so they can make recommendations to the council.

4.   City Administrator Updates
        a. Seth reported on a conference that he attended where he learned the different
            uses of AI.
        b. Jeramie reported that he went on the tour of police stations with the police
            department and the architects. He said the Lehi police station was very
            impressive. He liked the separate entrance for evidence. Lehi separated the
            police operations and the public side of buisness. Pleasant Grove had a good
            use of shared spaces and joint uses with other entities such as the UHP.
           c. Councilor Callaway reported on the Christmas in County event. He said that the
              city needs to get a tree decorated for the tree fest. The tree needs to be ready by
              November 30th. The county would like some manpower to help decorate the
              tree out in front of the county building on Tuesday and Wednesday November
              26th and 27th. Jeramie suggested that Shirlene Buckley be the VIP. Each town
              will choose a VIP with a write up about each VIP and honor them and the activity.

   5.    Councilor Travis Worwoodmoved to convene into a closed session to discuss the
        character and competence of an individual . Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the
        motion. The motion passed unanimously.

   6. The work session meeting adjourned at 7:55 p.m.




The closed session adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
