                                MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 22-12-05                                        DATE: December 13, 2022


            A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
            DETERMINING THAT A REFERENCED WALKWAY LOCATED
            AT 3232 JEAN STREET DOES NOT EXIST AND RECOGNIZING
            A PAST VACATION OF SAID WALKWAY BY SALT LAKE
            COUNTY

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council under Utah Code Ann.§
10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a Mayor, and

        WHEREAS, in approximately 1968, before the municipal incorporation of Magna, a subdivision
plat was recorded with Salt Lake County ("County") that shows a parcel labeled “walkway,” whose legal
description is attached hereto, marked Attachment "A," which was common practice at the time with
regard to private lanes and private access rights of way; and

        WHEREAS, subsequent County documents were created that did not show the alleged
"walkway" and showed instead that two contiguous parcels to the "walkway" were consolidated into one
parcel upon which a house was constructed with the address of 3232 Jean Street, Magna, Utah ("Subject
Property"); and

      WHEREAS, sometime between 1968 and 2009 someone at the County once again inserted the
"walkway" onto the plat but noted thereon that it had been vacated; and

       WHEREAS, the documents of the alleged "vacating" of the "walkway" have been misplaced by
Salt Lake County and not recorded, nor indexed; and

       WHEREAS, Magna was duly incorporated after the occurrence of the forgoing events and is
now the jurisdiction over the Subject Property; and

       WHEREAS, the owners of the Subject Property have asked Magna to assist in clarifying the
confusion arising from documents relating to the "walkway" and desire to sell the Subject Property; and

       WHEREAS, after consulting with legal counsel, Magna believes there are legitimate questions
about whether or not the "walkway" ever came into existence or was legally and properly dedicated to
Salt Lake County; and

       WHEREAS, Magna further believes that even if the "walkway" had been legally and properly
dedicated to Salt Lake County, then at some point in time Salt Lake County vacated the "walkway" and
the mere fact that documents regarding the same cannot now be found should not be used to cast a cloud
upon the title of the Subject Property.

        NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council, Magna,
Utah as follows:

        (1) Any walkway that was contemplated in the initial plat for the subdivision whose legal
description is as set forth in Attachment "A" was never constructed or legally dedicated to Salt Lake
County.

      (2) Even if the alleged walkway was properly dedicated, it was vacated by action of the Salt
Lake County Council.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Magna Metro Township Council, in Magna, Salt
Lake County, Utah this 13th day of December 2022.
