Closed Session

              Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Barney, moved to go into a
closed meeting to discuss pending and reasonably imminent litigation. Council Members Prokopis
and Pierce were absent for the vote.

              The Council discussed the following agenda items:

    4th of July funding and composition
    Magna Metro Township redistricting map options

            Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to close the
workshop meeting and go into the business meeting. The motion passed unanimously.

Unified Police Department Report

                Chief Del Craig stated an Amber Alert was issued for a female individual that was
later found in Texas. UPD is in the middle of hiring new recruits and hiring lateral from other police
departments. The youth academy is coming up and it is trying to recruit kids from Cyprus and
Kearns High School.
