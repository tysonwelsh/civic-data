Proposed Resolution 2023-19 adopting Amendment #1 to the Annual Action
Plan for the period July 1, 2023, through June 30, 2024 pj

A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department came before the Council to consider a resolution -
adopting Amendment #1 to the Annual Action Plan (AAP) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2024. The memo stated as a requirement to
receive federal funding from the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), the City’s Community and
Economic Development Department prepares and submits to the Council what is known as a five-year Consolidated Plan
(ConPlan) and an Annual Action Plan. The Annual Action Plan (AAP) is used to outline how federal Community Development
Block Grant funds, HOME funds and other federal and non-federal program funds will be used by the City in the upcoming fiscal
year. When the Annual Action Plan is submitted to HUD each year, specific dollar amounts for program income funds and carry-
over funds are estimated for the remainder of the fiscal year. Part way through the fiscal year, after the specific dollar amounts are
known or when program income from loans and other programs are received, an amendment must be made to the plan to update
the Annual Action Plan budget. Often when the mid-year amendment is proposed, changes in funding levels within certain
programs and line items are proposed as well.

Community Development Manager Smith summarized the memo and stated the amendments proposed with this mid-
year update address the following:

e Adopt the 2023 HUD HOME Homeownership Value Limits - $380,000 to $428,000;

e Increase the Own in Ogden HOME budget by $250,000 with an additional $100,000 to the HOME Match funds
with an increase to the goal for down payment assistance to 50 per year;

e Adopt a one-year CDBG overall Low- and Moderate-Income Benefit Certification Period; and

e Adopt an overall decrease of $34,385 to the budget.

Chair Choberka called for a public hearing on proposed Resolution 2023-19, entitled:

“A resolution of the Ogden City Council adopting Amendment #1 to the Annual Action Plan for the period July
1, 2023 through June 30, 2024, and directing that this document be submitted to the U.S. Department of
Housing and Urban Development.”

Consideration of the appointment of Lisa Stout as Management Services
Director for Ogden City

A memo from Council staff explained Mayor Caldwell is requesting advice and consent approval of the appointment of
Lisa Stout to the position of Management Services Director for Ogden City. Lisa is currently serving as the City’s Comptroller.

Mayor Caldwell spoke to Ms. Stout’s knowledge, experience, and service to the City. The City conducted a thorough :
process to recruit applicants for the position and he feels strongly that Ms. Stout is the most qualified to fill the position vacated by
now Chief Administrative Officer Mara Brown.

Council member Nadolski commented on his experience with Ms. Stout since being elected to the position of City
Council; she has always been professional and honest in her dealings with the Council. She is connected to nearly every area of
the City due to her management of the City budget and he has never been concerned about her capabilities in that area of her job.

Chair Choberka stated that the Council has heard from residents questioning why this change is being recommended now
given the imminent changes in City Administration in the next several months; she asked Mayor Caldwell to address that matter. :
Mayor Caldwell stated that the Management Services roles is critical and should not be left open for a lengthy period of time. He
is simply presenting the best person for the position for Council consideration.

Council member White stated that there are not many people that can understand all of the areas of the City that Ms.

Stout will oversee; she feels Ms. Stout will be able to do that and she looks forward to working with her more. Mayor Caldwell
agreed; having people with that type of background is truly a gift to whoever is elected to the position of Mayor.

Council member Hyer stated that he appreciates Mayor Caldwell’s motives behind the appointments he has
recommended recently; he has always had great interactions with Ms. Stout, and he agreed she is the right person for this position.

Chair Choberka then called for public input regarding the proposed appointment.

Angel Castillo thanked the Council for allowing electronic participation in their meetings. She stated she has watched

; Ms. Stout since 2018 and found her to be a lovely, gentle, kind person who has answers or is able to find answers. There is no
_/ question that Ms. Stout is a good person and does a good job. However, in sports and business, when someone is putting together
aroster for their new team, they have a tendency to wait for the draft to ‘pick their quarterback’; she is not saying that Ms. Stout is
