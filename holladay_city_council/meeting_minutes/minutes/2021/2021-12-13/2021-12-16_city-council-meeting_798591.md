I.    Welcome – Mayor Pro-tem Durham.
Mayor Pro-tem Durham called the meeting to order at approximately 5:30 p.m.

II.     Consideration of Resolution 2021-39 Authorizing Registration as a Participant in the
        National Opioid Settlement
Manager Chamness explained that settlements have been reached in the opioids litigation brought by
many states, including Utah and several counties in the state. The settlements are structured so that
the more of each state’s political subdivisions participate in the settlements, the more of the settlement
funds will flow to that state. She noted that Salt Lake County is still negotiating with the state on
allocations. There really is no disadvantage to participating.

Council Member Petersen moved to adopt Resolutin 2021-39. Council Member Fotheringham
seconded the motion. Vote on motion: Council Member Fotheringham-Aye; Council Member
Durham-Aye; Council Member Quinn-Aye; Council Member Petersen-Aye. Resolution 2021-39
was adopted unanimously.


III.  Adjourn.
Council Member Petersen moved to adjourn. Council Member Fotheringham seconded the motion.
The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

The City Council Meeting adjourned at approximately 5:41 p.m.
