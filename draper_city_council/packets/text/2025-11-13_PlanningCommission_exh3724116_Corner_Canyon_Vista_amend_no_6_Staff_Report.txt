SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 0.34 acres located on the south side of Gray Fox Dr. at approximately 1952 E
Gray Fox Dr (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RSD-2-Corner Canyon Vista.
The applicant is requesting to remove the area of non-disturbance located on Lot 33 of the
Corner Canyon Vista Subdivision Plat.

The subject property is currently undeveloped. The Corner Canyon Vista Subdivision Plat
was recorded in 2003 and contains 62 residential lots which are approximately one third of
an acre to over one half of an acre in size. The original subdivision plat included an area of
non-disturbance on 12 of the lots.

According to DCMC Section 9-17B-010 the purpose of the RSD-2-Corner Canyon Vista zone
is to “provide a residential environment within Draper City that is characterized by a low
overall residential density with single-family detached dwellings providing a compatible
transition between neighboring residential, open space, and natural areas. Significant
features of the development include open space preservation, trailhead accommodation,
and a tie in to the local street network. (Ord. 623, 2-8-2005)”

Subdivision Plat. The applicant is requesting to remove the non-disturbance area located
on Lot 33 as recorded on the original Corner Canyon Vista Subdivision Plat (Exhibit F). The
property is 0.34 acres in size and the non-disturbance area of the subject property is
approximately 0.10 acres and is located at the south end of the property. Geotechnical
work was conducted and a report was provided to Draper City which indicates that the
subject lot will have adequate stability and suitable buildable area with regards to
geological hazards for the proposed development. A copy of the proposed Subdivision Plat
Amendment can be found in Exhibit G.

Staff notes that the applicants request to remove the area of non-disturbance from their
lot is not the first request of this kind in the history of the Corner Canyon Vista Subdivision
Plat. The two most recent plat amendments - Amendment No. 4 and Amendment No. 5 -
removed the area of non-disturbance from Lots 27 and 36 of the original subdivision
respectively.

The applicant has also included a street tree plan which can be found in Exhibit H of this
report.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request are found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat if the
        Planning Commission finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment,
        and no public street or municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request based on the findings
and the criteria for approval, or denial as listed within the staff report. The Planning
Commission should also review the request and hear from the applicant prior to making a
decision.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff notes the applicant is
required to comply with the following requirements pertaining to the plat and
development of the subdivision:

1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
         Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
         and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.       That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
         development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
3.       If the subdivision plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date
         of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not
         commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be
         extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period
         for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension
         prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year. See DCMC Section 17-9-070.
4.       The proposed plat shall be updated to reflect the current month and year and
         reflect the lot address prior to being printed to Mylar and obtaining the necessary
         signatures prior to recordation of the Amended Subdivision Plat.
6.       That the proposed lot shall reflect the approved street tree plan.
