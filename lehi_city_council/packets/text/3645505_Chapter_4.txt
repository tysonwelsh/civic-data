  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 04.060. Criteria for Approval.
No amendment to this Code, or the Zoning District Map(s), may be recommended for approval by the Commission
nor approved by the City Council unless such amendment or conditions thereto are consistent with the General Plan.
In considering a Code amendment, or Zoning District Map(s) amendment, the applicant shall identify, and the City
Staff, DRC, Commission, and the City Council may consider the following factors, among others:

    1.   The effect of the proposed amendment on the character of the surrounding area;
    2.   Consistency with the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    3.   Consistency and Compatibility of the proposed zone with the General Plan land uses of nearby and adjoining
         properties.
    4.   The suitability of the properties for the uses requested and their suitability for the existing uses identified by
         the General Plan;
    5.   Whether a change in the uses allowed for the affected properties will unduly affect the uses, or proposed uses
         for nearby and adjoining properties;
    6.   The gain to the public health, safety and welfare from the existing classification to the proposed amendment;
         and
    7.   The overall community benefit of the proposed amendment.


  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation of a proposed amendment to Chapter Four of the Development
Code. The amendment will allow applicants to submit exhibits with General Plan Amendments or Zone Changes.
Exhibits can illustrate their intended use for the property but will not receive review on the layout like a concept plan
does.

Planning Staff proposed this change because of the challenges with a concept plan. In the past applicants who wanted
to illustrate how they wanted to develop the property with a General Plan Amendment or Zone Change, would have
to submit a concept plan. Concept plans are approved by the approval body for the site plan or subdivision which in
most cases occurs at the Planning Commission level. This meant that the concept plan did not go to City Council,
even though the General Plan Amendment or Zone Change did. Allowing exhibits will allow an illustrative idea to
go to City Council with the legislative decision in a more straightforward way. The applicants will still be able to
choose to submit a concept plan if they want more feedback related to the layout.

The Reviewing Departments had no comments on the proposed Development Code Amendment.
  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Code Amendments are legislative items with discretion by the Planning Commission. They can propose
a change to the proposed language. They also have full discretion to recommend approval or denial to the City Council.

The public can also speak to this item with support or any concerns.


  POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may move to recommend approval or denial of the proposed amendments to the Lehi
City Development Code. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

    1.   The proposed amendment will add clarity for applicants wanting to show their intended use of the property
         with a General Plan Amendment or Zone Change Application.
    2.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provi-
         sions of the General Plan and its various elements.
    3.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.
