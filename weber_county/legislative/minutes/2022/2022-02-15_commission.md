In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

F. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. CONTRACT WITH STIRRIN’ DIRT RACING TO HOLD THE MAY MANIA DEMOLITION
        DERBY AT THE GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER.
            Marty Smith (GSEC): One of our largest and best customers. Packaged costs in lieu of separate line item
            billing.

            Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of contract with Stirrin’ Dirt Racing to hold the May Mania
            Demolition Derby at the Golden Spike Event Center; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
     2. CONTRACT WITH ROY CITY TO ESTABLISH THE TERMS RELATED TO THE ROY CITY 6000
        SOUTH PROJECT.
            Brooke Stewart (Community Development): Roy City intends to purchase right of way in advance of
            construction of a roundabout at the intersection of 6000 South and 3100 West. Funding was recommended by
            WACOG and approved by the Commission as part of 2021 Transportation Priority List. Roy City is providing
            $75K in matching funds and they received a CMAQ grant to construct the roundabout. This agreement
            establishes the terms for the Corridor Preservation Funds.
            Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of contract with Roy City to establish the terms related to the Roy
            City 6000 South project; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
     3. CONTRACT WITH WCG FOR OUR RIGHT OF WAY POOL.
            Gary Myers (Engineering, Manager): Contract to employ WCG to buy Right of Ways as needed. WCG is
            one of five successful applicants selected to be part of the Right of Way pool.
            Commissioner Harvey moved for approval of contract with WCG for our Right of Way pool; Commissioner
            Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    4. CONTRACT WITH GRANITE CONSTRUCTION TO OVERLAY THE EXISTING ASPHALT AND
       IMPROVE GUARDRAILS AS NEEDED OVER THE NORTH OGDEN DIVIDE ROAD.
         Ashley Thoman (Engineering): Two inch asphalt overlay and guardrail improvements along 4.5 miles stretch
         from North Ogden to 2900 East. $1.4M contract with money set aside from Prop 1 funds last year; held off
         beginning until UDOT completed work. Performance grade asphalt covering temperature range of our area.

         Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of contract with Granite Construction to overlay the existing asphalt
         and improve guardrails as needed over the North Ogden Divide Road pending required reviews form internal
         Weber County departments; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
