                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)

                                         Hearing began at – 5:45 p.m.
OAM2024-001300 – Consideration of adoption of Chapter 19.60 Airport Overlay Zone, with
accompanying map of the Airport Overlay Zone’s influence areas for the South Valley Regional Airport as
set forth in Utah Code 72-10-403. The proposed code includes 5 influence areas: Runway Protection Sub
Zone (RPSZ), Approach Sub Zone (AASZ), Noise Sub Zone (ANSZ), Horizontal Sub Zone (AHSZ), and
Conical Sub Zone (ACZ). Planners: Bianca Paulino & Curtis Woodward (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Senior Planner Curtis Woodward provided an analysis of
Chapter 19.60. Mr. Bracken touched on yearend and FAA requirements.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Senior Planner, Salt Lake City Department of Airports
Name: Paige Christensen
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Christensen said Curtis did a great job explaining the changes.

Commissioner Thomas motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

Speaker # 2: Citizen
Name: Judith Kvenvold
Address: 5498 S Morning Breeze Drive
Comments: Ms. Kvenvold said she wants the noise zone requirement. The weekends can be heard because
single engine planes fly so low.

Mr. Christensen said the noise zones are based on a day/night level “DNL” average, nighttime noise
exposure is increased but daytime is decreased. Aircraft is self-regulated and supposed to abide by the
standards, the pattern is 500 feet above ground level. Airport has applied for an air traffic control tower
and looks good it will be granted but it is a five-year process. Mr. Bracken said we don’t own or regulate
