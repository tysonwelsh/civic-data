                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-59

          A RESOLUTION GRANTING QUESTAR GAS COMPANY dba DOMINION
                ENERGY UTAH A RIGHT-OF-WAY AND EASEMENT ON
                          PARCEL I.D. # 34-10-100-006


       WHEREAS, Dominion Energy Utah is a public utility supplying natural gas to the
residents of Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, Dominion Energy Utah in order to provide natural gas to their customers,
which include the residents of Draper City, requires the need to develop infrastructure; and

       WHEREAS, The Council recognizes the importance of the service Dominion Energy Utah
provides to the residents of Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has found the terms and conditions thereof of the
Lease Agreement (Exhibit “A”), acceptable to Draper City.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       1. It does hereby approve the attached Right-of-Way and Easement Agreement between
          Draper City and Questar Gas dba Dominion Energy Utah.
       2. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable,
          such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution,
          and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.
       3. The Honorable Troy K. Walker, Mayor of Draper City, is hereby authorized to execute
          the agreement on behalf of Draper City and to act in accordance with its terms.
       4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 13th DAY OF DECEMBER, 2022.
