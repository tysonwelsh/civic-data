Request
 The property owner and applicant, Jalin Anderson, is
 requesting review of an accessory structure for a property
 located at 11 Northridge Way. The applicant is requesting
 a Conditional Use Permit for an increase in height to 25
 feet of an accessory structure and a setback waiver of 10 feet
 on both the side and rear property lines. The applicant feels
 the proposed request will not have a detrimental impact on
 the neighborhood as there are structures with similar
 proximity to the property line and there are many mature
 trees that mitigate the visual height impacts (see applicant
 letter, Exhibit “A”). The proposed structure is to be used
 as storage for vehicles, personal storage, and pool house (see
 building plans, Exhibit “B”).

Background
 This property is located in the Pepperwood 4 subdivision
 on a 0.59 acre (25,700 square foot) lot. The home is zoned
 PUD(1.62) with properties on all sides being single family
 homes zoned PUD(1.62). This area was platted in 1976
 and most homes in this area were built by the mid 1980’s.
 This portion of the Pepperwood development is not within
 the Sensitive Area Overlay Zone, but is within the
 Wildland Urban Interface area.

Public Notice and Outreach
 Notices were mailed to all property owners within 500 feet of the subject property. In addition, a neighborhood meeting
 was held on September 8, 2022. One neighbor attended was interested to see what the project was but raised no concerns.
 At the time of this writing staff has not received any emails or phone calls regarding the project.

Analysis
 The applicant’s plans indicate that they intend to build a detached garage and pool house in the southeast corner of their
 lot. The proposed structure would be approximately 1,437 square feet in size and approximately 25’ in height. They area
 proposing to site the building as close to the property lines as possible. This is part of several projects the applicants are
 pursuing. They are also proposing a deck and covered patio expansion off the south side of the home, and a future carriage
 house on the north side of the property. Those are not part of this conditional use permit request.

 Additional Setback
 The applicant is requesting a setback waiver of additional setback due to the increase of building height for both the side
 and rear property lines. Detached structures exceeding 15 feet in height are required to increase the minimum setback one
 foot for each foot of additional height up to the minimum setback for the primary dwelling (Section 21-11-2(a)(3)(b)). A
 setback of twelve (12) feet from both the side and rear property lines would be required for the requested height. However,
 the Planning Commission may waive this requirement through this conditional use review.

 Per the submitted plot plan (see image below), the applicant is proposing to place the structure as close as 12 inches to the
 property line. This setback is apparently allowed by the Pepperwood CCR’s. Staff has discussed with the applicant that this
 request is not possible per the Land Development Code which requires at least two feet (2’) from the side and rear property
 lines if the area between the structure and the property line is paved in a hard surface. The applicant is requesting a setback
 waiver of 10 feet for both the side and rear property lines. This will require that the building be shifted to the west and north
 and that pavers or other hard surfaces be placed between the exterior wall and property line.

  dwellings nearby and has several large mature trees nearby.

Sec. 21-33-4. Conditions.
     In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions that address:
     (1)   Size, configuration and location of the site and the proposed site plan layout.
  The applicant is proposing to locate the 1,437 square foot accessory structure that is 25’ tall in the southeast corner of the rear
  yard. The minimum setback must be at least two feet (2’) from the property line.
     (14) Measures to assure compliance with all conditions and requirements of approval, including, but not limited to, bonds,

          covenants.
  To be reviewed on legitimate complaint.
     (15) Such other conditions determined reasonable and necessary by the City to allow the operation of the proposed
          conditional use, at the proposed location in compliance with the requirements of this title.
  That the applicant complies with all Building and Safety, and Fire and Life codes
Staff Concerns
Staff feels the proposed application meets the intent of the Development Code and has no concerns.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for additional height of a detached
  structure as well as a setback waiver as described in the staff report for the property located at 11 Northridge Way based on
  the following finding and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. The proposed structure meets the intent of section 21-11-2.

    2. There are existing mature trees in the area that would mitigate the impacts of the additional building height.

    3. There is at least one nearby lot that has a similar minimal setback of a detached structure to the property line.

Conditions:
   1. That the proposed structure complies with all applicable Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes

    2. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City Development Code, including adjusting
       the setback of the detached structure to a minimum of two feet (2’) and place a hard surface between the exterior wall
       and the property line.
