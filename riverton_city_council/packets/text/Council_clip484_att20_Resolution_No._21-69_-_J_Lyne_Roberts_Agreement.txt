                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 21-69

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
  WITH J. LYNE ROBERTS & SONS INC. TO COMPLETE THE RIVERTON CITY
                PARK COURT IMPROVEMENTS PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the project exceeds the budgeted amount; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to award the base bid and alternates
1, 2 & 3; and

       WHEREAS, by awarding the base bid and alternates 1, 2 & 3, Council will need to
approve $354,500.00 in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council approves Resolution No. 21-69 authorizing the Mayor to execute
          an agreement with J. Lyne Roberts & Sons Inc. to complete the Riverton City Park
          Court Improvements, which includes the base bid and alternates 1, 2 & 3.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 14th day of
December 2021 by the following vote:
