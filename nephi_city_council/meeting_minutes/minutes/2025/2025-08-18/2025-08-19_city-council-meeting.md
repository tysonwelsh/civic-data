TRANSPORTATION PLANNING ASSISTANCE AGREEMENT—UDOT
      Shauna Talbot, Nephi City Planner, was successful in obtaining a grant from UDOT to update the
      city’s Transportation Plan. This update will occur mainly along the west area of the Nephi City
      limits out to airport road. The update will identify how local streets could be laid out in this area
      as growth and development occur. This will help with street placement as the city continues to
      see annexation requests and growth pressures in areas around the city limits.
        The city will be partnering with Jones & DeMille Engineering who helped to apply for the grant
        with UDOT. According to the agreement, the city will be responsible for expending $4,800 and
        the grant will provide $65,200 in planning assistance.
        Councilor Skip Worwood authorized the mayor to sign the Transportation Planning Assistance
        Agreement with UDOT. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed on
        unanimous vote.

SEWER PROJECTS
      1. NEBO HEIGHTS SEWER LINING PROJECT
             a. On May 13, 2025 during the regular session of Nephi City Council meeting the
                council approved the recommendation of employing Bowen Collins & Associates to
                design and provide support implementation of the sewer lining project for Nebo
                Heights.
             b. The project consists of the following: Sewer Rehabilitation Project to address critical
                deterioration in its existing 8-inch diameter reinforced concrete sewer system,
                originally installed in 1978. The affected sewer segment spans approximately 7,200
                linear feet, contained within the Nebo Heights subdivision. The aged pipe is
                deteriorating due to hydrogen sulfide attack, and rehabilitation is necessary to
                preserve system integrity.
             c. Bowen Collins & Associates delivered a contract for Nephi city to sign for the design
                and construction engineering with them. The cost estimate is $275,000.
             d. Councilor Shari Cowan made the motion to authorize the mayor to sign the contract
                so design can begin on the sewer lining project. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded
                the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.
      2. SEWER LAGOON PROJECT
             a. On May 13, 2025 during the regular session of Nephi City Council meeting the
                council approved the recommendation of employing Sunrise Engineering to design
                and provide support for implementation of the Nephi City Sewer Lagoon Expansion
                Project.
             b. The project consists of finding the proper location to expand the sewer lagoons to
                accommodate for continuing City growth. Sunrise Engineering was selected to
                design the new lagoons and modify existing lagoons. At this point in the process
                Sunrise Engineering has presented the City with a contract to perform feasibility and
                funding assistance phases of the lagoon project. This process will help the engineer
                   identify the best location for additional ponds and potentially help the city obtain
                   funding for modification and construction of new sewer lagoon ponds. This contract
                   does not include design or construction engineering cost. It is only to gain
                   information on the best location for the proposed project. A separate contract will
                   be presented by Sunrise Engineering once a proper location has been identified.
                c. Councilor Travis Worwood made a motion to enter into an agreement with Sunrise
                   engineering for the preliminary feasibility and funding to identify a proper location
                   and potential funding for new sewer lagoons. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded
                   the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

Work Session
    1. The city received a request by James Sinkovic to split a parcel that contains a non-conforming
        structure on the property. The current older home that exists on the parcel does not meet
        setbacks along the north and east side. There was originally another parcel to the west of the
        parcel also owned by Mr. Sinkovic. Both parcels are highlighted in the picture and have now
        been combined into one parcel. Mr. Sinkovic is now wishing to re-split the parcels.
       The home on the property to the east is an older home that
       was constructed prior to the current setbacks in the
       residential zone. The property is .82 acres. Mr. Sinkovic’s
       request would re-establish the parcel lines for the original parcel highlighted in yellow to the
       shape found in the PDF. This would allow it to become a buildable lot. Because the structure in
       the blue highlighted parcel is non-conforming, the request must be presented to the City Council
       for consideration according to the Nephi City Code 10.21.4.B.4.

       The parcel split still allows the blue highlighted area to conform to the residential zone for
       minimum acreage and doesn’t exacerbate the non-conforming front and east side setbacks that
       cause the historic home to be a nonconforming structure.

       The council decided to take a week to consider the request and possibly take action at a future
       meeting.
