REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PRESENTATION: FAIR LOGO CONTEST WINNER

Washington County Fair Director Susi LaFaele stated that a competition was held to design
a new logo for the County Fair. Many entries were received and of those, eleven (11)
finalists were selected to be voted upon. Over 27,000 votes were received within five (5)
days and Adriana Morgan was selected as the winner.

Ms. Morgan explained her vision behind the logo design and was awarded a $500 check
as a prize.

(See www.washcofair.net for new logo design)

PRESENTATION: CONFLUENCE PARK GRANT

Kathleen Nielsen explained that funding is needed for conservation and restoration work
in at least two (2) different locations of Confluence Park. Some of the work includes the
removal of invasive weeds as well as planting native vegetation and removing snags and
dead trees from public passage ways.

The Commissioners will consider the funding request in a future Commission Meeting.

PUBLIC HEARING: TO OPEN THE 2019 WASHINGTON COUNTY BUDGET
TO UPDATE FUND 27, ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT: KIM HAFEN

Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen explained that the St George Area Economic Development
budget is no longer under the County’s supervision as they are now a 501c6 organization.
However, $265,608 dollars were received by Washington County for the Economic
Development in 2018 that was designated for 2019. Funds will be transferred from Fund
10 and paid to the Economic Development from Fund 27 in order to ensure the budget is

balanced.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2464, A RESOLUTION
ESTABLISHING COST RECOVERY PROCEDURE RELATED TO THE OPIOID
EPIDEMIC AND DECLARING THE OPIOID EPIDEMIC AND ITS EFFECTS ON
THE COUNTY A PUBLIC NUISANCE

Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke explained that there have been additional costs to the
tax payers due to the over-prescription of opioids. The purpose of this resolution is to allow
the County to recover these costs and declare the opioid epidemic and its effects on the
County as a public nuisance.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _ R-2019-2466, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING _A_SUBLEASE _AGREEMENT__BETWEEN__WASHINGTON
COUNTY _AND_ THE HURRICANE VALLEY _ FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE
DISTRICT REGARDING A NEW FIRE ENGINE

Chairman Cox explained that the Washington County recently issued bonds in order to
purchase a new fire engine to be used and paid for by the Hurricane Valley Fire Special
Service District (HVFSSD). The Municipal Building Authority (MBA) issued the bonds
in order to lease the fire engine to Washington County. The County desires to sublease the
fire engine to the HVFSSD. The HVFSSD will assume all financial responsibility for
repayment of the bonds in order to pay for the fire engine.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-2466, A
Resolution Approving a Sublease Agreement Between Washington
County and the Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District
Regarding a New Fire Engine. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox,
Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2467, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
WATTS CONSTRUCTION FOR REMODELING THE EXTERIOR OF THE
COUNTY'S BOULEVARD OFFICE BUILDING

Commission Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained the Boulevard Building is in need
of a new roof. It was determined that the exterior of the building would be remodeled in
conjunction with the roofing project in order to save on costs. Bids for the project were
sought and obtained through a Request for Qualifications (RFQ) and bid process.

It is being recommended for Watts Construction to complete the roofing project and
remodel as they were the lowest bidder. The project is anticipated to take approximately

six (6) months to complete.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-
2467, A Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington
County and Watts Construction for Remodeling the Exterior of the
County’s Boulevard Building. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox,
Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Chairman Cox explained that the County is currently in the process of expediting the
resolution of RS 2477 roads within Washington County.

Mr. Clarke further explained that when federal lands have interests claimed on them, the
proper way to disclaim interests is to file an application for recordable disclaimer of
interests. The County and State will be filing an application for the right-of-way for the
Manganese Road in Washington County, which is an RS 2477 road.

Commissioner Almquist mentioned the successful IRONMAN held on Saturday, May 4,
2019.
