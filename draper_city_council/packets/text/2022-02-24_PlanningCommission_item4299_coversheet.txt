From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP

Re: 136 Center Additional Parking – Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 3.37 acres located at approximately 13697 South 200 West. The property is currently zoned CSD-136C . The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site as a parking lot to serve adjacent office buildings. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   The lot in the subdivision has been developed individually and is not part of a phased development.
2.   The site plan conforms to applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3.   The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
4.   The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5.   The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 2/17/2022 Cover Memo
