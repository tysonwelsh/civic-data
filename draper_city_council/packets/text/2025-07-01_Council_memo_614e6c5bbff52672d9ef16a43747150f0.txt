Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1664


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a 2 nd Development Agreement Amendment for
approximately 3.9 acres located on the west side of Lone Peak Parkway, at approximately 285 W.

This item is currently scheduled for the June 26, 2025 Planning Commission meeting.



For Approval:



  1. The proposed development agreement complies DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D).



For Denial:



  1. The proposed development agreement does not comply with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and
      (D).
