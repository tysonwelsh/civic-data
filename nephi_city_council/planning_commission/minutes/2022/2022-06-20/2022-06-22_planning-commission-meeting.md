NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                      WORK SESSION MEETING MINUTES
                           June 22, 2022 @ 6:30 p.m.
                         21 E. 100 N. Nephi, UT 84648

Attendance:
Staff: Shauna Talbot
City Council Member: Skip Worwood
Commissioners: Cory Thomson, Ann Peterson, Fran Petersen, Heather Robertson, Kent Heap
Excused: Alan Hancock, Karl Brough, Seth Atkinson
Scribe: Shari Cowan
Public: Donald Ball

Ann Peterson, Planning Commission Chair, opened the meeting and offered
the prayer.


   1. Title 10.16 Off Street Parking and Loading Discussion

      Shauna Talbot presented the Parking Generation Manual, 5th edition. She stated that this is the
      most recent publication. She demonstrated how to use this manual by searching by topic or type
      of establishment.

      Heather Robertson asked if the study took into consideration whether the property was rural or
      urban. Shauna confirmed that it is considered, and is listed on each data sheet.

      Shauna stated that if an establishment has too much parking, that’s not a problem. Our goal is to
      hit the minimum parking requirement.

      Shauna asked the commissioners to review the proposed parking requirements, and provide
      feedback for things they don’t see and might want. Heather asked for more clarification on
      residential additional guest parking. She would like the requirements to be more specific. Cory
      Thomson would like guest parking defined.

      Cory would like the distinction made between retail and professional (or non-retail). Kent Heap
      would like to first define the type of business, then determine the parking requirements. This will
      help match parking to the specific type of business.

      Shauna stated that she would like to define the gross floor area. She also wants to make sure we
      have enough parking for the Roderick Annexation.

      Fran observed that the office and medical parking requirements are the same.

      Donald Ball asked what type of office is least likely to have customers. Heather asked what
      happens if the office moves. Cory stated that if the use of the establishment changes, the
      parking requirements would also change.

  Fran feels that the hospital parking seems low. Shauna asked if the clinic requirement should be
  separate from the hospital. She also asked if there should be more parking requirements when
  additional services are added. She feels that as long as there is 1 parking space per employee, then
  there should be enough parking regardless of expansion of services within a building. Is there
  enough parking at the hospital since its expansion? Donald Ball stated that when a building
  increases in height, it doesn’t change the footprint. Heather asked how you divide the hospital and
  the clinic. Shauna stated that the square footage used by each service is self-reported. She said
  there could even be 3 uses if you include hospital and clinic office spaces. It’s a guessing game
  by both parties at this point.

  Fran wonders if 5 lanes of traffic should be required for all future commercial development. Skip
  Worwood stated that it is the UDOT’s responsibility.

  Cory Thomson circled back to retail vs non-retail office space and wonders if it can be
  determined by retail “floor area.”

  Shauna brought up in-home businesses and the high impact that might bring. She feels there
  needs to be a basic, or “catch-all” requirement for home occupation. Skip stated that if an in-
  home business expands to the point that there is not enough parking, it is time to move to a larger
  space. How much of an impact will the parking for an in-home business have? If it is deemed
  too much, the business may be denied. This is the reason we have public hearings for this type of
  business.

  Fran suggested that there should be a clause stating parking requirements can be determined by
  the planning commission in the case of unforeseen circumstances.

  Ann Peterson mentioned that the parking situation at The Hive is inadequate.

  Storage unit parking was brought up by Cory. Kent wonders if the requirements could be made
  according to rows vs parking spaces. Heather asked if the requirements would be separate and fall
  under “warehouse” for the storage units and “office” for the office space. Skip stated that very
  rarely will everyone be at their storage unit at the same time.

  Shauna stated that we need more solid standards that determine if a traffic study is mandatory.
  She knows that the proposed traffic requirements are not perfect, but would like to at least get it
  submitted. She advised the commissioners to review the requirements before the next meeting,
  and email her with any suggestions or concerns.


2. Misc. Topics

  Kent asked about the status of the chicken ordinance. Skip Worwood states that the chicken
  ordinance is a dead topic and was not even voted upon by the city council. Shauna felt the
  ordinance was reasonable, but can see the point of view of citizens who buy or build on property
  based on animal rights. Kent feels that a lot of domesticated animals are more of a nuisance than
  farm animals such as chickens. On that subject, Ann pointed out the fact that citizens who own
  more than 3 dogs need to obtain a kennel license.

  Shauna stated that she and Seth Atkinson have been “concept zoning” outside boundaries and
  annexation areas. She said it is important to plan ahead for several reasons. The “concept

  zoning” is actually what is holding up our general plan right now. There is a possibility of a
  future corridor for transportation. They would eventually like to add a transportation element to
  the general plan. Shauna proposed the possibility of combined use along corridors, similar to
  main street.

  Donald said he’s hopeful there won’t be residential zones too close to the airport. Ann said she
  had heard the church farm property on the east side of the freeway could be a possible future LDS
  temple site.

  Shauna would like to propose a new zone with ½ acre lots that could be subdivided into smaller
  lots in the future. Fran doesn’t love the potential future subdividing for the same reason as other
  commissioners don’t love the chicken ordinance. Ann agrees and states if it’s zoned to be a ½
  acre, it should stay ½ acre. Kent asked if anyone was familiar with a grid system? He explained
  a little about grid systems and the importance of the continuation of roadways. Skip said the city
  prefers grid systems and they are very important for addressing purposes.

  Shuana explained that the takeaway is that they want to expand industrial zoning. Kent wonders
  if we should expand industrial zoning around the airport so residential doesn’t take over the
  airport. Shauna said that once we get zoning done, we can discuss transportation. Kent would like
  to see the airport moved to dog valley and more residential in the current airport area. Fran is
  worried this new zoning would override anyone’s acreage in the county. Cory reminded her that
  this is “conceptual” zoning. Concerns about sewer were discussed.

  A meeting with UDOT would be helpful to find out what we would need to do for another exit?
  What justifies a new exit?

  Heather wonders how often this plan would be reviewed. Shauna said the city should revisit the
  plan every 3-5 years. New suggestions and revamps can be made at that time. Heather said she
  felt more comfortable submitting a plan with that knowledge. How can we zone so we expressly
  prohibit solar panels? This needs to be discussed with city attorney Kasey Wright. Fran feels we
  still need to create a zone with multiple lot sizes. Heather asked if we can put an expiration or
  mandatory review on the master plan? Shauna said the problem with an expiration is that, if you
  don’t follow through or don’t have the funds, you’re violating your code.

  Shauna asked the commissioners to review the master plan with the conceptual future zoning, and
  to email her with any thoughts or suggestions prior to the next meeting.

3. Adjourn
  Motion to adjourn the meeting at 8:34pm:
  Made by: Kent Heap      Second: Heather Robertson         Outcome: Unanimous vote
