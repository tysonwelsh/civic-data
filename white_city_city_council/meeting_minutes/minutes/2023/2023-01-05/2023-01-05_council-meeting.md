                                            Workshop

White City Metro Township Council Interviews

                Tyler Huish, Applicant, introduced himself stating he has lived in the community
for six years. He was chair of the Steering Committee and has worked with people with different
backgrounds and opinions. He was a member of the Walk White City Committee, has participated
in various open houses and surveys. He has a long term desire to live here and wants to help
shape the community. In his professional life he manages multi-million dollar budgets, numerous
properties, and has technology background. His experience would apply to duties on the Council.

                Greg Shelton, Applicant, introduced himself stating he has been involved in the
community in various ways. He actively participates in the White City Planning Commission and
Council meetings. He has continued to attend meetings and explore different opportunities to be
participate in. He has been involved with the White City Community Council, Community and
Economic Development Advisory Council (CEDAC), and Association of Community Councils
Together (ACCT). He has received training through ACCT and the Salt Lake Valley Law
Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA). He has a technology background.

               Mayor Flint asked both applicants what their philosophy is on volunteerism.

                Mr. Huish stated volunteering should not be forced but encouraged. Finds ways
to get the community involved. He makes sure that he is volunteering regularly and often and is
not looking to gain anything from it.

               Mr. Shelton stated volunteering should be selfless, the gain one gets out of
volunteering should be the fact that you helped make something better. More people should want
to volunteer.

                Council Member Price stated the council member position requires you serve on
certain boards. Some of those board meetings take place during the day. She asked both
applicants if they will be able to participate in those meetings.

              Mr. Shelton stated attending various meetings will not be a problem for him. He
works four days a week from home, and he has already made arrangements with his employer to
attend meetings when needed.

               Mr. Huish stated he has a flexible schedule and is looking forward to the
opportunity to serve in those various areas.

              Council Member Perry asked both applicants what they see as White City’s
strengths and weaknesses as a community.

               Mr. Shelton stated White City’s strength is how close the community is. There is
a lot of community involvement, people in White City care and want to be involved.
Communication is a weakness of White City. There needs to be an easier way to communicate
with citizens so there is not any misinformation out there. Better strides need to be made to
communication.

               Mr. Huish stated he is impressed by the passion in the community and how it
cares for each other. There is a peace and tranquility that exists when one drives into White City.
The community is quieter and close knit. Challenges come from the limited funding White City
gets. Communication and awareness are another of weakness where technology should be used
to better reach the community.

               Paul Ashton asked both applicants who their employer is and if there is a conflict
of interest.

               Mr. Shelton stated he is employed by CR England and does not have a conflict.

               Mr. Huish stated he is employed by Extra Space Storage and does not have a
conflict.

White City Facebook Page

               Paul Ashton stated he wanted to get the Councils thoughts on creating a White
City Metro Township Facebook page. The Council also has the option of using its webpage and
newsletters to distribute information.

              Rori Andreason stated Magna Metro Township pays $500 a month for someone
to manage its Facebook page. If managed correctly, Facebook is a great resource.



                                                2
DATE           THURSDAY                               JANUARY                          05, 2023

               Mr. Ashton stated Facebook that is used for government is treated differently than
individual Facebook pages.

              Mayor Flint stated if the Council wants to do a Facebook page, it needs to be done
right and administered by someone else besides the Council.

            Council Member Cardenaz asked if comments can be turned off and if the
Facebook page can be used for informational purposes only.

               Mr. Ashton stated no, comments cannot be turned off.

               Council Member Price stated the White City webpage is not easy to find.

               Council Member Perry stated the Council needs to do everything possible to get
information out to the community. The webpage is not efficient enough to get information out in a
timely manner.

             Mayor Flint stated a survey went out on how residents want to receive
communications and the number one response was through a newsletter and social media was
last.

             Mr. Ashton stated the Council will need to determine how much it is willing to pay
someone to manage a Facebook page then staff can see if there is someone willing to manage
a Facebook page for that amount.

               Council Member Perry stated he does not think a Facebook page should replace
newsletters or mailers. He wants to use all the tools available to reach the community.

            Mayor Flint stated she is willing to look into it but hiring someone to manage a
Facebook page will depend on how much it will cost.

               Council Member Perry stated being able to push information out within minutes,
is valuable.

                Ms. Andreason stated there is $3,200 in the communications budget and $2,500
in publications.

             Mayor Flint stated Maridene Alexander, Communications Manager, Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Services District (MSD), has offered to run a Facebook page for White City.

          A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP RECOGNIZING

Ordinance Requiring Per-Seat, Single Member At-Large Elections

                Paul Ashton reviewed an ordinance requiring per-seat, single member at-large
elections for the White City Metro Township Council. Rather than having candidates listed on the
ballot and citizens voting for the top two or three candidates, they will run against a specific
person. If the Council would like to consider this type of election, it will need to hold a public
hearing to get citizen input.

                                                 6
DATE           THURSDAY                               JANUARY                         05, 2023


              Council Member Cardenaz stated it may make it easier for people to see who is
running for Council.

               Council Member Perry stated currently there is a level of confusion on the ballot
on how many individuals to vote for; however, changing how candidates are elected may create
further confusion. He is not convinced one way or another and would like some more time to think
about it.

               Council Member Price stated having a candidate run against a specific person,
could create a negative campaign. She is open to having a public hearing and further discussion.

              Mayor Flint stated she is open to additional discussion and a public hearing.

             Council Member Shelton stated he would like additional information before
moving forward with a change.

                Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Cardenaz, moved to defer
the item to the February Council meeting. The motion passed unanimously.
