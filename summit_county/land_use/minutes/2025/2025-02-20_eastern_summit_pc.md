Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner
, introduced a Conditional Use Permit/Master Planned Development for a Guest Lodge near Echo for work session discussion.
Applicant, Michael Dee, addressed the Commission and provided a history of the property and presented his vision for the Guest Lodge as a residential treatment facility for people who are suffering from substance abuse.
Commissioner Sonntag asked a clarifying question about the flexibility of the CUP for other uses. Planner Kuhrmeyer and Mr. Dee responded to the concern raised about the potential for other uses of the property.
Commissioner Wilde raised a question about water on the property and inquired if it would be a family resort or just for patients. Mr. Dee responded stating the cabins could potentially be for employee housing and family members of those in treatment.
Kelvin Judd, A
pplicant
, answered the question about water rights, quantity, and quality.
Peter Barnes,
Community Development Director
, clarified information about the potential uses of the property.
Commissioner Darcey asked a question about the use of the area and access to the property. Commissioner Benson responded to a concern about the condition of the road accessing the property. Mr. Dee added additional comments about the road.
Commissioner Wheaton asked a clarifying question regarding the use of a guest lodge or guest ranch to which Planner Kuhrmeyer responded.
Commissioner Benson inquired about other locations for the project. He also asked about limiting the square footage. Planner Kuhrmeyer responded to the questions of square footage. Commissioner Benson asked a follow up question about the success rate of these types of programs. Mr. Dee responded. Julie Dee,
Applicant
, provided additional information about relapse rates.
Commissioner Chair Peterson asked a question about lighting and dark sky compliance and provided comments for future presentations regarding the proposed uses of the project. Director Barnes commented and suggested Planner Kuhrmeyer bring information about special events to the next discussion.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the work session and reopened the regular session at 7:12 p.m.
