38   Mayor Staggs introduced Carlton Christensen who is the UTA Board Chair and turned the time

41   Carlton Christensen showed the presentation entitled “Utah Transit Authority Update” which
42   provided an update on both the microtransit project and the five year service plan.
43
44   Jaron Robertson, UTA Innovative Mobility Solutions Director, talked about the UTA on Demand
45   by Via program. He explained that COVID-19 has caused a decline in ridership and said they
46   would extend the pilot through August 2021.

49   would replace microtransit?

 1   Mr. Robertson said that the two programs have operated side by side and now, due to demand,
 2   they will suspend the UTA flex routes.
 3
 4   Councilmember Stewart asked if suspension of the flex routes would be permanent.
 5
 6   Mr. Christensen said that they would give the public the chance to comment if they ever decide
 7   to suspend the service. He explained that they will suspend them right now but if it needs to be
 8   permanent then they will have public hearing.
 9
10   Laura Hanson, UTA Planning Director, showed the flow chart for the five year service plan. She
11   explained that when they asked members of the county what they would like, they asked UTA to
12   improve service on the west side, namely 3600 West, 5600 West and 3100 South by increasing
13   frequency and hours of service. She said they were considering microtransit in the south end of
14   the valley, which would include Riverton. She said the vision for their five year plan included
15   expanding rail service, expanding bus service, and providing a cleaner fleet.
16
17   Councilmember Stewart asked if there was decision on where they would expand the light rail
18   since the plan currently shows it running through Draper and he asked why everything was
19   being pushed to the East side? Also, what future projects did they have for the Riverton
20   community?
21
22   Mr. Christensen explained that they were working on the future plan with the County.
23
24   Councilmember McCay asked if there were any services near Mountain View Village.
25
26   Mr. Christensen said that the VA app was the current service and that through the app someone
27   can request pick up from their home and the driver will connect them to the rail. He also
28   explained that there are plans that show a connection at Daybreak but he is not sure if there are
29   more cost effective solutions than light rail.
30
31   Mayor Staggs said that the most telling slide in the presentation is the slide that showed a big
32   hole in the southwest valley for mass transit. He said that more service should be added to the
33   southwest side of the valley because this is where half of the undeveloped property is in Salt
34   Lake County.
35
36   Councilmember McDougal said that she echoed what the Mayor said about the hole in the
37   southwest part of the valley and explained that we need those services desperately. He asked
38   if funds were set aside for the project?
39
40   Mr. Christensen said that the recent transit tax funds are still there and available for some
41   construction to take place but the projects are a year behind.
42
43   Councilmember Stewart expressed concern over “ridership” saying that it is hard to increase
44   ridership without the infrastructure to do it. He said that UTA needs to come up with a different
45   factor than ridership.

 1            Latest Designs of the Potential Tracy Aviary in Riverton City - Mayor Staggs &
 2            Councilmember Buroker
 3
 4   Councilmember Buroker introduced the potential for having a satellite of the Tracy Aviary in
 5   Riverton City and explained that a significant amount of work had already been done and that
 6   there was still a lot to do but it has been a slow process due to COVID-19. She said she is an
 7   advocate of the project because Tracy Aviary is willing to take and maintain this space for
 8   Riverton City.
 9
10   Tim Brown, representative for Tracy Aviary, explained that 5-6 years ago the Tracy Aviary
11   Board looked at building another location close to the Jordan River. He said that
12   Councilmember Buroker convinced them that Riverton would be a great site and they were
13   excited about it. He said the potential Tracy Aviary near the Jordan River would be a nature
14   based site where during the day they would do fields trips and educate families and at night
15   there could be celebrations such as weddings.
16
17   Mr. Brown explained that the lease agreement has a lease boundary of about 4.83 acres. In
18   planning for this project, he said there are two budgets to consider, capital budget and operation
19   budget. He said he projects the capital to build the site would be about $4 million dollars and
20   would cost about $250,000 a year to operate.
21
22   Mayor Staggs said that the lease that was approved by the council in April 2020 and that the
23   lease boundary was slightly different than the drawings. He said that the Parks and Trails
24   Committee had provided feedback to adjust this boundary so that there was an easement for
25   the parking lot and that it was not too close to the clubhouse. He thought that initially the project
26   was about $2 million upfront but the presentation showed $4 million and asked if he could
27   explain the difference.
28
29   Mr. Brown explained that the initial numbers were incorrectly quoted. He said that the $4 million
30   estimate included the increase in construction costs and the anticipated fence that would be
31   built around the entire boundary of the project. He said that they would appreciate a contribution
32   from Riverton.
33
34   Mayor Staggs asked for any additional comments.
35
36   Councilmember Wells asked if the fencing would be chain link.
37
38   Mr. Brown said it would be a good quality fence and would not be chain link.
39
40   Mayor Staggs said that Riverton City has contributed to the design that was approved by the
41   Council and those funds have been applied to the design and research of this facility. He said
42   that the council would have further discussion about the design and costs.

17          Applicant

18   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that on Jan 19, 2021, the City
19   Council voted to reconsider a proposed rezone application, which had been denied by the City
20   Council on January 5, 2021. He said this is a public hearing for a Rezone of approximately two-
21   acres, located at 12814 Sunday Drive at the southeast corner of Sunday Drive and Dutchman
22   Lane (approximately 12800 South and 4400 West). Mr. Lethbridge explained that the property is
23   approximately 180-ft by 484-ft, with the long side facing Dutchman Lane. Mr. Lethbridge said

27   Dutchman Lane. He said that the widening of Dutchman Lane is not tied to the proposed

 1            Resolution No. 21-08 - Authorizing Purchasing to order the FY 21/22 Police
 2            Vehicles - Craig Calvert, Operational Director
 3
 4   Chief Hutson explained that in order to get the police vehicles for 2022, they need to order them
 5   now. He said the payment for the vehicles will be in July and will come out of the fiscal year
 6   2021-2022. He said that the lease contract with Zions Bank will go to City Council in July 2021
 7   and the first year's payment will not exceed $99,000.00 and will be made after the contract is
 8   executed.
 9
10   Councilmember Wells said he was surprised that the yearly mileage in the lease contract was
11   so low, to which Chief Hutson explained that they pay for a 50,000 mile lease and they feel
12   comfortable that they can stay within that range. He also explained that he monitors the mileage
13   and rotates the cars to make sure that they stay under the 50,000 mileage.
14
15   Councilmember McCay asked him to clarify whether they were buying seven cars total or 14,
16   and Chief Hutson explained that Riverton Police Department inherited 26 vehicles from Unified
17   Police and they ranged from being a few years old to very new. He said that they hired 35
18   officers when they began the police department and so they leased 11 vehicles initially,
19   because it is important to have a couple of vehicles in reserve in case you have incidents where
20   you need an extra marked car. He said that he believes leasing is the best option because it
21   keeps the cars in good shape and they don’t have to pay mechanical fees. The second year
22   they leased 12 vehicles and then they will lease 12 more for a total of 35 vehicles.
23
24   Councilmember McCay MOVED that the Riverton City Council approve Resolution No. 21-
25   08 Authorizing Purchasing to order the FY 21/22 Police Vehicles now. Councilmember
26   Wells SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion; there being
27   none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows: Councilmembers Buroker-Yes,
28   McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
