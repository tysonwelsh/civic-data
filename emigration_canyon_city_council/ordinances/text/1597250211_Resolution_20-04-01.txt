                  EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH

RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-01,                                                           April 23, 2020

A RESOLUTION AMENDING AND ADOPTING A FEE SCHEDULE FOR FEES AND
OTHER ASSESSMENTS FOR SERVICES PROVIDED BY THE EMIGRATION
CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP

                                           RECITALS

A. The Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“Emigration Canyon”) is a municipality pursuant
   to Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq. and as such has to power to assess
   and collect fees for services and to recover the reasonable cost of regulating certain activity
   within the Township.

B. The Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
   body of Emigration Canyon pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. The Township provides services and regulates activity within the Township and in this
   regard has previously established or been the successor of Salt Lake County to previously
   established fees and assessments for these services and activities.

D. The Council feels it is necessary to amend the previously adopted fee schedule to reflect
   increase costs and other changes in the Township activities.
