CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3401, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY, UTAH, AND THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES FOR
THE RED CLIFFS RESERVE — STATE LAND REJUVRA AND HERBICIDE
TREATMENT TO MITIGATE WILDFIRE FUELS IN THE RESERVE

Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan stated that the Utah Department of
Natural Resources (UDNR) along with the Bureau of Land Management (BLM) are responsible
for acquisition, management, and monitoring actions within the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. The
County and UDNR desire to enter a cooperation agreement for aerial herbicide treatments on state
lands in and around the reserve.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3401, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement Between
Washington County, Utah, and the Utah Department of Natural Resources for
the Red Cliffs Reserve — State Land Rejuvra and Herbicide Treatment to
Mitigate Wildfire Fuels in the Reserve. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist,
Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3403, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A CONTRACT FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE EMERGENCY OPERATIONS
AND LEGACY PARK STORAGE FACILITY

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw stated the County issued a Request for Qualifications and
selected three qualified bidders that were invited to bid for the construction of the project. Watts
Construction Inc. was the lowest responsive and responsible bidder on the project. It was
recommended that the performance and payment bond be waived for the project. It is
recommended that Washington County enter into a contract for the construction of the Washington
County Emergency Operations and Legacy Park Storage Facility.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3403, A
Resolution Approving a Contract for the Construction of the Emergency
Operations and Legacy Park Storage Facility, and Moving to Waive the
Performance and Payment Bond. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and
Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3404, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A CONTRACT WITH NFP RX SOLUTIONS FOR PHARMACY BENEFIT
MANAGEMENT COALITION SERVICES

Human Resources Director Joel Tucker explained the County went to bid for prescription drug
services. CVS was the low bid with a savings of over $40,000 to the County. NFP will be
administering benefit management between Washington County and CVS.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3404, A
Resolution Approving a Contract with NFP RX Solutions for Pharmacy
Benefit Management Coalition Services. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson,
and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3405, A RESOLUTION OF SUPPORT
FOR THE SR-18 FIRE CHIEFS ASSOCIATION AND JOINT TRAINING

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated there was a work meeting in October where the
County encouraged the Chiefs along SR-18 to resume regular meetings amongst themselves. Chief
Charles Guthrie of the Northwestern Special Service District has recommended the Blue Card
Incident Command training program as a key resource to help achieve a standardized and efficient
response strategy along the SR-18 corridor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3405, A
Resolution of Support for the SR-18 Fire Chiefs Association and Joint
Training. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

10/17/2024 @ 1057, Side By Side Rollover, Sand Mountain - Man Hours - 30
Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to an ATV rollover near top of the world. A female suffered
injuries as a result of the rollover. Search and rescue members assisted with loading her onto the Life
Flight Helicopter and she was transported to the hospital.

10/17/2024 @ 2246, Stranded Hikers, The Narrows, Zion National Park— Man

Hours - 70

Search and Rescue responded to a report of four adults and three minors stuck in the narrows
during a severe rain storm. Utah DPS helicopter Star 9 was requested to assist. All seven
individuals were hoisted by Star 9 and returned to Incident Command without incident.
10/19/2024 @ 1044, Injured Mountain Biker, Red Bull Rampage Site - Man Hours -

16.5

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to the Red Bull Rampage site on a report of a male who had
crashed on his mountain bike. The male had a suspected head injury. Life Flight was requested and
Incident Command was established at the staging area below the males location. Life F light also landed
at the staging area. SAR personnel, in conjunction with Hurricane Medical units, and Life F light
medics, responded to the location of the patient. He was stabilized and placed in a rescue litter. He was
transported via a rescue litter approximately 150 yards to a waiting Jeep. He was loaded in the Jeep and
transported to the location of the Life Flight Helicopter where he was transferred to the helicopter. The
patient was then transported to the hospital via Life Flight.

10/19/2024 @ 1819, Side By Side Rollover, Sand Mountain - Man Hours - 14

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of side by side rollover on Sand Mountain. SAR safely
transported all four patients back to Incident Command. Two patients were transported to St George
Regional for injuries. The other two declined medical transport.

10/22/2024 @ 2020, Boating Accident, Sand Hollow State Park — Man Hours - 16.5
Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a boating accident in Sand Hollow Reservoir. A
wake boat ran into the rocky shore and sunk as a result. All individuals in the boat were out of the water
by the time SAR arrived. The SAR Dive Team utilized lift bags to recover the boat and towed it to
shore without incident.

10/26/2024 @ 1903, Stranded Motorist, Beaver Dam Wash - Man Hours - 20

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a male stuck near Beaver Dam Wash

area. The male had no water and was diabetic. SAR team members located the male and were
able to give him water. They then escorted him back to the pavement in his vehicle out without
incident.

10/26/2024 @ 2208, Overdue Hikers, The Subway, Zion National Park - Man Hours

- 56

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of seven overdue hikers in Zion National Park. Utah
DPS Helicopter STAR 9 was called in to help locate the hikers. STAR 9 located the group and SAR
personnel hiked to their location. The lost hikers were then escorted out on foot without incident.

10/28/2024 @ 1611, Stranded Boater, Upper Sand Cove Reservoir - Man Hours - 5
Deputies responded to a report of a male who was stuck in his boat on Upper Sand Cove Reservoir due
to high winds. The wind had blown him to the side of the reservoir. They assisted him with getting back

to shore.
11/10/2024 @ 1331, Injured Mountain Biker, Red Bull Rampage Site - Man Hours -

36

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to the Red Bull Rampage site on a report of a mountain bike
accident involving a male. The patient had suffered significant injuries. Several medical helicopters, in
addition to STAR, were requested to respond. SAR personnel, in conjunction with Hurricane medical
units, responded to the location of the injured male and began evaluating and stabilizing him. Due to
the unavailability of a medical helicopter, the patient was hoisted from the location by STAR 9 and
loaded in an ambulance. He was then transported to the hospital via ambulance.

11/13/2024 @ 1649, Stranded Hiker, Padre Canyon, Snow Canyon - Man Hours - 18
Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a stranded female hiker near Padre Canyon in Snow
Canyon State Park. SAR personnel located the hiker and utilized ropes to lower her to a safe spot. She
was then escorted back to Incident Command without incident.

11/14/2024 @ 1742, Lost Hikers, Butterfly Trail, Snow Canyon - Man Hours - 2
Deputies responded to a report of two lost female hikers in Snow Canyon State Park. They located the
lost hikers on a gravel road and provided them with a ride back to their vehicle.

11/16/2024 @ 1514, Injured Hiker, Petrified Dunes Trail, Snow Canyon - Man

Hours - 34

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a female who had an ankle injury on Petrified Dunes
trail in Snow Canyon. SAR units in conjunction with Santa Clara Ivins Medical units responded to the
location of the female. She was evaluated and carried back to the road with a litter basket. She was then
loaded in an ambulance and transported to the hospital. All SAR units were accounted for and cleared

the scene.

11/17/2024 @ 1410, Injured Mountain Biker, Gooseberry Mesa - Man Hours - 10
Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Gooseberry Mesa on a report of a female who had crashed on
her mountain bike. The female had a suspected head injury. Life Flight was requested due to her
injuries. SAR arrived at the location of the female and landed Life Flight near the patient. The patient
was evaluated and loaded in the Life Flight helicopter. She was then transported to the hospital via Life
Flight.

11/22/2024 @ 1813, Lost Hikers, Red Reef Trail, Red Cliffs Desert Reserve - Man

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to Red Reef Trail on a report of two lost hikers. SAR members
hiked in and located the males. They then guided them back to the trailhead without incident.

Search and Rescue (SAR) responded to a report of a side by side rollover on Sand Mountain involving
a female patient with a suspected arm injury. Incident Command (IC) was established at the Water
Tanks. SAR, in conjunction with Hurricane Medical units located the patient. She was evaluated and
her injury was stabilized. She was then transported to IC where she was loaded in an ambulance and
transported to the hospital.
