Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

 The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Don Yates from Mona. Mr. Yates told the story of his brother
Dean Joseph Yates who was killed as a tail gunner of a ten-man crew of a B-17 in World War II. His
brother’s body was never returned home but was buried in France. The council expressed their
appreciation to Mr. Yates for sharing his story and leading the pledge.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting held on 4-6-21, work session held on 4-6-21, work session held on
 4-13-21, sale of burial rights Kathleen and Keven Broadhead, and a list of claims on the warrant register
dated 4-20-21. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS OR PUBLIC COMMENT

LIBRARY DIRECTOR RUTH BONZO GRANT REPORT:

   Library Director Ruth Bonzo reported to the council that she had received a $5,000 Technology
Enhancement Grant. She plans on using the grant funds to replace four patron computers and the
computer that is used for testing. Ms. Bonzo also reported on the spending of the other grants she has
received this year and on programs and events planned for the summer months.

ORDINANCE 4-20-2021-RED CLIFFS COMMERCIAL ANNEXATION ADOPTED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 4-20-2021: AN ORDINANCE ANNEXING CERTAIN
PROPERTIES TO NEPHI CITY AND ESTABLISHING ZONING CLASSIFICATION FOR SAID PROPERTY. The
property being annexed was approximately 57 acres located at 1300 S Main . The property is owned by
Nephi City. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 04-20-2021-A -AMENDING TITLE 10 CHAPTER 14 AND TITLE 11 CHAPTER 4 ADOPTED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Ordinance 04-20-2021: AN ORDINANCE OF NEPHI CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, AMENDING TITLE 10 CHAPTER 14 AND TITLE 11 CHAPTER 4 OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE. The
portion of the code being amended included revisions to the Commercial and Industrial Performance

GENERAL PLAN UPDATE CONTRACTS APPROVED:

   Rural Community Consultants (RCC) were chosen by the subcommittee to compile and oversee the
drafting of an updated general plan. A smaller portion of the update will be handled by Planning and
Development Services LLC (PDS). PDS has ties to the University of Utah and the Kem C. Gardner Institute
of Public Policy. PDS would prepare the demographic data, the housing element including the
Moderate- Income Housing component required by the state. PDS contract cost would be $4600 and
RCC cost would be $29,671. Nephi City has received a $15,000 grant from the Community Impact Board
for the general plan update. The remainder of the project will be funded from the Planning Projects
account in the Capital Projects Fund. Councilor Seely moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the general
plan update contracts with RCC and PDS. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Mayor Nielson reported on a meeting he attended with the County Commissioners, the Division of
Natural Resources, BLM, representatives of our congressmen in Washington, and other state officials.
He stated that much of the meeting was spent on water resource topics.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

  Councilor Seely moved to adjourn the meeting to convene into executive session to discuss the
character and competence of an individual and the acquisition and disposition of real property.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

The meeting adjourned at 7:55 p.m.
