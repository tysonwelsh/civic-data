DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, JP Marriott is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to allow for a 22-foot-
tall accessory structure for the property located at 9688 S. Colemere St. In addition, the
applicant is asking for setback waiver of one foot on the side property line. (See Exhibit
#1 -Application Materials).

The property is 0.32 acres, (13,861 square feet) in an R-1-8 zone. The proposed structure
will be 25 feet wide and 25 feet long. The proposed use for the structure is for additional
garage space and storage. The proposed materials include LP Smart panel siding or LP
Board and Batten siding, with either a galvanized base or concrete pad as the base. The
roof pitch, design and exterior styling will be congruent with the main dwelling, so it
blends in well with the neighborhood and existing structures. Access to the proposed
garage will be via the existing driveway and RV pad on the north side of the property.
(See Exhibit #2– Site Plan and Construction Drawings)

The property is Lot 6 of The Oakridge Heights 1 Subdivision. The subject property is
adjacent to single family homes zoned R-1-8 on the north, east sides. To the south and
west side is zoned R-1-10.

ANALYSIS

Section 21-11-2-(a)(3)(c) of the Sandy City Development Code states that an accessory
structure may be built taller, up to the maximum building height for a permitted dwelling
within the zone in which it is located, upon receipt of a Conditional Use Permit from the
Sandy City Planning Commission. The Planning Commission shall consider the scale of
the building in relation to the immediate surroundings, the nature of the zone and land
uses in the immediate vicinity, architectural design, landscaping access, proposed use,
impact upon adjacent properties, in addition to other criteria normally considered during
the Conditional Use Permit process. The Planning Commission may require additional
setback from side and rear property lines as a condition of approval. The proposed
structure is in an R-1-8 zone allowing the structure to be up to ten (10) feet high in the side
yard (Section 21-11-2-(a)(3)(a)). Because the structure is in the side yard and following
setbacks of primary dwelling, it is permitted to be up 15 feet in height. The applicant is
requesting that the Planning Commission approve the accessory structure to be 22 feet in
height with no additional setback from the typical setback of a primary structure (eight (8)
feet is this case).

Additional Setback Requirement. Detached structures exceeding 15 feet in height shall
increase the minimum setback one foot for each foot of additional height up to the
minimum setback for the primary dwelling (Section 21-11-2-(a)(3)(b). A setback of nine
(9) feet from both the side and rear yard property lines would be required for the requested
height. The applicant is proposing to place the structure eight (8) feet from the north side
property line. The applicant is requesting a setback waiver of one (1) foot on the side yard.

CONCERNS
Staff is concerned that the new garage will not match the current materials and
colors of the existing home.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for JP
Marriott for the property located at 9688 S. Colemere St. to allow for an accessory
structure with a maximum height of 22 feet as described in the application materials with
a setback waiver of one-foot (minimum setback of eight (8) feet from the property line)
based on the one finding and subject to the following conditions.

Finding

1. Staff finds that the proposed structure meets the intent of section 21-11-2 provided
   the applicant complies with the following conditions.


   Conditions

1. That the applicant repaint the house with the same color scheme of the proposed
   structure prior to issuance of a certificate of occupancy.

2. That the proposed structure complies with all applicable Building & Safety, and Fire
   & Life Codes.

3. That the structure shall not be used as an accessory dwelling unit.

4. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City
   Development Code, and all conditions of approval imposed by the Planning
   Commission.

5. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.
