3.2      FINDINGS:
         3.2.1 The subject property is made up of one parcel (parcel ID 21022260070000) with a
                cumulative area of approximately 23.95 acres.
         3.2.2 The subject property is in the Industrial (ID) zoning district.
         3.2.3 The subject property is in the “Business Park” designation in the City’s General Plan.
         3.2.4 The subject property is located within 250 feet of a residential district and is subject to non-
                administrative conditional use permit review, in accordance with Section 13.23.210 of the
                LDC.
         3.2.5 John Buckel, Badiee Development, submitted an application for non-administrative
                conditional use permit and site plan review on February 3, 2022.
         3.2.6 The City notified the applicant on February 8, 2022 that the submitted application was not
                complete and required additional information.
         3.2.7 A revised application was submitted to the City on February 25, 2022.
         3.2.8 This application was first introduced to the Planning Commission on May 10, 2022, as a
                discussion item. A public hearing was held and no formal action was taken.
         3.2.9 The applicant is requesting to construct five office/warehouse buildings that would create
                approximately 37,800 square feet of office space and 267,026 square feet of warehouse
                space on the currently undeveloped 23.95 acre site.
         3.2.10 The proposal requires right-of-way dedication through a subdivision plat and construction
                of a cul-de-sac to terminate South Riverboat Road.
         3.2.11 The applicant has submitted a subdivision plat application to the City to dedicate the
                required right-of-way to terminate South Riverboat Road.
         3.2.12 The subject property has a FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA) of Zone AE with a
                floodway and is located in the Jordan River meander corridor.
         3.2.13 The subject property is within the “High Liquefaction Area.”
         3.2.14 The subject property is within the “Flood plain Overlay Zone” and is subject to the
                standards in Chapter 13.16: Floodplain Overlay Zone, of the LDC.
         3.2.15 The subject property is within the “Sensitive Area Overlay Zone,” and is subject to the
                standards in Chapter 13.15: Sensitive Area Overlay Zone, of the LDC.
         3.2.16 The Jordan River Parkway Trail runs parallel to the Jordan River and traverses the site
                north/south along the eastern portion of the subject property.
         3.2.17 Section 13.33.040 of the LDC establishes the general review criteria for all conditional use
                permit requests in Taylorsville.
         3.2.18 The Jordan River and Jordan River Parkway are recognized assets and amenities in
                Taylorsville, as summarized in this report and the Taylorsville General Plan.
         3.2.19 Section 13.33.050 of the LDC summarizes the City’s authority to impose conditions of
                approval on a conditional use permit application.
