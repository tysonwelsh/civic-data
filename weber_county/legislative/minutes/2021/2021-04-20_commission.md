E. PUBLIC COMMENTS:

   hypothesis regarding the COVID-19 pandemic and that some doctors were being silenced. She handed the
   commissioners information which she said was from the National Center for Biotechnology Information that

   Carolyn Lietuvininkas, of North Ogden, said that silencing doctors not speaking the authorized narrative was

F. PRESENTATION: WEBER COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE
   County Sheriff Ryan Arbon thanked the commissioners for supporting our rights and freedoms. Matthew
   Harris, presidentially appointed US Marshal for the Utah District, presented the 2020 Jail Stakeholder Award
   plaque to outstanding staff at the Weber County Jail stating that the county houses nearly 40% of the entire
   State federal prisoner population, that the county makes every effort to assist them, that the pandemic created
   huge obstacles that inmate housing had to overcome and Weber County does an excellent job balancing all of
   the aspects and continues to facilitate US District and local video court hearings, without which the judicial
   process would be at a standstill, that Weber medical, a central part of inmate housing, has frequently assisted
   with difficult and detailed medical conditions, and that Weber’s expertise is a major contributor to the Jail
   winning this award. The public does not get to see what it takes to run a facility 365 days/year, that no one
   does it better than Weber County whose operation runs well and the federal judiciary needs are met. Sheriff
   Arbon said that most, if not all credit, went to Chief Perry and his staff. Chief Perry expressed gratitude to the
   Jail employees and their great dedication, which contribute to a successful operation. The commissioners
   expressed faith and gratitude in what the Sheriff’s Office does, their responsiveness, and their overall strides.

    5. FIRST READING OF ORDINANCE AMENDING FEES FOR COUNTY TRANSFER STATION (SEC 16-2-9 SOLID WASTE FEES)
       Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Director, requested reducing the construction and
       demolition waste fee to $40.49/ton which matches the general waste fee, and creating a household
       hazardous waste $5/load fee to help cover disposal costs. The C&D fee was raised to $46.10/ton a few
       years ago to encourage use of the county’s C&D landfill. The Transfer Station now has the
       equipment/transportation to handle more material and this should alleviate rate disputes for mixed loads.
       Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the first reading of an ordinance amending fees for the Weber
       County Transfer Station (Sec 16-2-9 Solid Waste Fees); Commissioner Froerer seconded.
       Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    6. CONTRACT WITH RANDY MARRIOTT CONSTRUCTION FOR CONSTRUCTION OF A PUBLIC DRAIN
       Joe Hadley, County Roads Director, stated that this is for a new drain ditch in western Weber County that
       will flow west between 7500 W. & 8400 W. then south into an existing drain for stormwater and irrigation
       water to relieve flooding issues. Commissioner Jenkins said that this is good for the area and that Mr.
       Marriott owns a fair amount of ground there and is digging it at no cost to the county.
       Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with Randy Marriott Construction for construction
       of a public drain); Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
       Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
