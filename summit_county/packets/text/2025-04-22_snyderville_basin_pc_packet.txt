Proposal 
The applicant, on behalf of the owner, is requesting conditional use approval for an Accessory 
Dwelling Unit (ADU) in Unit 2 of the Altitude Lofts Condominiums. The applicant states that the 
three-level unit will include a ground floor product warehouse, second-floor office and 
showroom, and third floor ADU.

Background 
Eric Fleming is the owner of Parcel ALTLC-2. The property is located at 4123 N Forestdale Dr #2. 
Unit #2 is located within a shell building (Building B).  Both buildings were approved as an 
amendment to a Low Impact Permit on May 26, 2022. In September of 2022, Buildings A and B 
were condominiumized and recorded as the Altitude Loft Condominiums plat. Altitude Loft 
Condominiums was previously known as Park City Business Center Lot 2 and is still subject to 
the Park City Business Center Development Agreement.   
 
The proposal for Unit 2 includes a warehouse, office, and showroom. The Unit will include 
space for warehousing supplies on the ground floor and office space and a showroom on the 
second floor. Additionally, the applicant proposes a 999.9 square foot Accessory Dwelling Unit 
on the third floor, accessed via a staircase from the second floor. Exhibit A includes the floor 
plan for this proposal.  
 
Section 10-2-10 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code states that Accessory Dwelling 
Units within the Service Commercial Zone are reviewed through Conditional Use Permits. Only 
one ADU may be permitted per commercial unit within the zone. Conditions of Approval have 
been included to mitigate the potential for two ADUs to be built within this unit. Staff and

Recommendation 
Staff recommends that the Snyderville Basin Planning Commission (SBPC) review the proposed 
Conditional Use Permit, conduct a public hearing, and approve the application, pursuant to the 
following Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval. 
 
Findings of Fact: 
1. On January 16, 2025, the applicant submitted a Conditional Use Permit application to 
construct an Accessory Dwelling Unit within the top floor of Unit 2 of the Altitude Loft 
Condominiums, located at 4123 Forestdale Dr Unit #2 (Parcel ALTLC-2).  
2. Parcel ALTLC-2 is owned by Eric Fleming. 
3. Parcel ALTLC-2 is described as 0.00 acres.  
4. The property is within the Service Commercial Zone.  
5. Unit 2 is located within a shell building (Building B) that was permitted via a Low Impact 
Permit on May 26, 2022.  
6. In September of 2022, Buildings A and B were condominiumized and recorded as the 
Altitude Loft Condominiums plat. Unit 2 is one unit within Building B of the Altitude Loft 
Condominiums.  
7. The applicant proposes tenant improvements for Unit 2. The proposal includes using the 
ground floor for warehousing, the second floor for offices and a showroom, and the 
third floor for an ADU. 
8. Section 10-8-5 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code states that ADUs are 
conditional uses within the Service Commercial Zone.  
9. Section 10-8-5 clarifies that ADUs shall be measured from the inside face of the 
perimeter wall to the inside face of the perimeter wall, including all wall partitions, 
mechanical rooms, hallways, and stairwells. The proposed ADU is 999.9 square feet, 
including the stairs that provide access to the unit from the second floor and mechanical 
equipment.  
10. Parking for Altitude Loft Condominiums was approved in accordance with a Parking 
Management Agreement, which states that the Condominium Owner’s Association must 
14
11 
 
review and attest to adequate parking for the uses proposed in each Unit prior to tenant 
improvements.  
11. A minimum of one off-street parking space is required for an Accessory Dwelling Unit.   
12. The Condominium Owner’s Association attested that there is sufficient parking for the 
use and the Accessory Dwelling Unit.   
13. Staff reviewed the proposal and found that it complied with all requirements in the 
Code.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. The use is in accordance with the general plan. 
2. The use conforms to all applicable provisions of the Code, including, but not limited to, 
any applicable provisions of Section 10-3-5, Chapter 4, and state and federal regulations. 
3. The use is not detrimental to public health, safety, and welfare. 
4. The use is appropriately located with respect to public facilities. 
5. The use is compatible with the existing neighborhood character and with the purpose 
provisions of the applicable zone district, and will not affect surrounding land uses. 
 
Conditions of Approval: 
1. The construction of the Accessory Dwelling Unit must be done in accordance with the 
plans included in Exhibit A. No modification to the Accessory Dwelling Unit is permitted 
without written approval from the Community Development Department and any 
relevant service providers.  
2. The third floor has been approved for residential use as an accessory dwelling unit.  No 
additional portion of the unit may be used for residential purposes.   
3. At least one of the reserved parking spaces for Unit 2 must be reserved for the tenant of 
the Accessory Dwelling Unit.  
4. Verification of business operation and Business License must be submitted prior to the 
Certificate of Occupancy for the Accessory Dwelling Unit. 
5. A certificate of occupancy for the Accessory Dwelling Unit shall only be granted 
concurrent with or after the issuance of a certificate of occupancy for the associated 
commercial use. The Accessory Dwelling Unit may not be occupied prior to the 
associated commercial use receiving all necessary permits and approvals from service 
providers.  
6. The Accessory Dwelling Unit may not be sold separately from the associated commercial 
use.  
7. The Accessory Dwelling Unit may not be rented by those without affiliation to the 
associated commercial use. At least one (1) resident of the Accessory Dwelling Unit 
must be either the owner or employee of the proposed business.  
8. Nightly or short-term rental of the Accessory Dwelling Unit is prohibited. The rental 
term of the Accessory Dwelling Unit shall be no less than thirty (30) days.  
9. Loading and unloading of materials related to the commercial use may not interfere 
with traffic circulation.  
10. Prior to building permit issuance for this tenant improvement project, all service 
provider requirements shall be satisfied.

Proposal 
The applicant is requesting conditional use approval for a Major Accessory Building to be 
located on lot 156 of the Silver Creek Estates Unit C subdivision, which is 8795 Redden Road.  
The applicant proposes to use this Accessory Building as storage for privately owned 
equipment.  Additionally, the Accessory Building will include a 999.99 square foot Accessory 
Dwelling Unit.   
 
According to 10-9-17 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code, this 4,780 square foot 
building is classified as a Major Accessory Building and thus requires a Conditional Use Permit.

Background 
8795 Redden Road has an existing single-family dwelling that is 2,704 square feet.  Access to 
the lot is from a private driveway from North Redden Road.  This property is zoned Rural 
Residential.   
 
Section 10-2-10 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code contains the Chart of Allowed and 
Permitted uses.  In the Rural Residential zone, a Major Accessory Building requires a Conditional 
Use Permit subject to review and approval by the Snyderville Basin Planning Commission. The 
applicant applied for a Conditional Use Permit on June 28, 2023.  The code defines a Major 
Accessory Building as: 
  
ACCESSORY BUILDING, MAJOR: A building or structure greater than two thousand 
(2,000) square feet but not exceeding twelve thousand five hundred (12,500) square

Analysis and Findings 
 
Section 10-3-18 (I) of the Snyderville Basin Development Code sets the following grounds for 
vacating or changing a plat: 
 
1. If the county manager or land use authority is satisfied that neither the public nor any 
person will be materially injured by the proposed vacation, alteration or amendment , 
and that there is good cause for the vacation, alteration, or amendment, the county 
manager or land use authority may vacate, alter or amend the plat, any  portion of the 
plat, or any road or lot. 
  
Analysis:  There is good cause for the amendment as the proposed subdivision 
boundary adjustment accurately reflects  the current property description of the 
Common Area Parcel H . Staff finds no evidence that the amendment would materially 
injure the public nor any person. COMPLIES

Proposal 
 
The Summit County Community Development Department has received a Conditional Use 
Permit application for a Rocky Mountain Power substation and transmission line upgrade at the 
Rocky Mountain Power Summit Park Substation, located 4969 W Kilby Rd. (Parcel S-59), Summit 
Park, Summit County, UT 84098. The scope of work would fall under the “Utility Facilities, 
Major” and “Utility Facilities, Aboveground” uses in the Snyderville Basin Development Code.  
 
The Snyderville Basin Development Code defines Major Utility Facilities as; “The provision of 
generating plants, electrical switching facilities and primary substations, refuse collection or 
disposal facilities, water and wastewater treatment plants, and similar facilities.” 
 
The Snyderville Basin Development Code defines Aboveground Utility Facilities as; “Utility 
towers and transmission and distribution towers greater than forty five feet (45') in height.” 
 
Summit County is a designated Fire High Consequence Area (FHCA), and the purpose of the 
project is to upgrade and expand the existing Summit Park Substation as part of an ongoing 
effort for wildfire mitigation. According to the applicant, the substation and associated 
equipment will be rebuilt to the up-to-date national electric safety codes and avian safe 
standards. The upgrade will also accommodate existing and future electric power needs for 
Summit County.  
 
The upgrade from 46 kilowatts (kV) to 138kV on the transmission line and the substation 
doubles the capacity of the Summit Park Substation. The substation would increase from a 5 
megavolt-amperes (MVA) transformer to a 10 MVA transformer. According to the applicant, 
the project’s primary purpose is to mitigate wildfire risks, however the increase in capacity is an 
unintended advantage. According to the applicant, another benefit of the project is the 
increased reliability and lifespan of the equipment.

meet the additional demand. Electrical facilities provide services that support health and safety 
throughout the Snyderville Basin. 
 
In order to meet the minimum clearance requirements for the increased operating voltage and 
larger transformer, the substation requires additional footprint at the Summit Park Substation.

been serving the Summit Park neighborhood area for ~50 years. As part of a larger county and 
statewide wildfire mitigation effort, Rocky Mountain Power is upgrading some of its older 
substations.  
 
As part of the widening of the development area, the process includes a retaining wall in order 
to construct a flat area required for the equipment. The retaining wall height ranges from ~2 
feet in height to ~26 feet in height.  
 
The substation is adjacent to Summit County’s Summit Park maintenance shed located on 
Parcel SU-K-18A-AM-X. Due to the larger amount of equipment required for the upgrade, more

Findings of Fact: 
 
1. The Summit Park Rocky Mountain Power Substation is located at 4969 W Kilby Rd. 
(Parcel S-59), Summit Park, Summit County, UT 84098.  
 
80
10 
 
 
2. The Summit Park Rocky Mountain Power Substation and previous equipment on the site 
have been serving the Summit Park neighborhood area for ~50 years.  
3. Rocky Mountain Power is the owner of Parcel S-59.  
4. Summit County is a designated Fire High Consequence Area (FHCA). 
5. As part of a larger county and statewide wildfire mitigation effort, Rocky Mountain 
Power is upgrading some of its older substations, including the Summit Park substation.  
6. The applicant is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to upgrade and expand the existing 
Rocky Mountain Summit Park Substation and associated transmission line as part of an 
ongoing effort for wildfire mitigation.  
7. According to the applicant, the substation and associated equipment will be rebuilt to 
the up-to-date national electric safety codes and avian safe standards.  
8. The upgrade will also accommodate existing and future electric power needs for Summit 
County.  
9. In order to meet the minimum clearance requirements for the increased operating 
voltage and larger transformer, the substation requires additional footprint at the 
Summit Park Substation.  
10. The upgrade from 46 kilowatts (kV) to 138kV on the transmission line and the substation 
doubles the capacity of the Summit Park Substation.  
11. The substation would increase from a 5 megavolt-amperes (MVA) transformer to a 10 
MVA transformer.  
12. According to the applicant, the project’s primary purpose is to mitigate wildfire risks, 
however the increase in capacity is an unintended advantage.  
13. According to the applicant, another benefit of the project is the increased reliability and 
lifespan of the equipment.  
14. The additional growth and demand for electrical services requires upgrades to 
equipment to meet the additional demand.  
15. Electrical facilities provide services that support health and safety throughout the 
Snyderville Basin. 
16. As part of the widening of the development area, the process includes a retaining wall in 
order to construct a flat area required for the equipment.  
17. The retaining wall height ranges from ~2 feet in height to ~26 feet in height.  
18. In order to meet the minimum clearance requirements for the increased operating 
voltage and larger transformer, the substation requires additional footprint at the 
Summit Park Substation.  
19. On March 25, 2025, the Snyderville Basin Planning Commission approved a Plat 
Amendment to incorporate ~3,300 sq. ft. from Parcel SU-K-18A-AM-X into parcel S-59 to 
accommodate for additional equipment.  
20. Summit County is the owner of Parcel SU-K-18A-AM-X.  
21. On March 27, 2025, the Summit County Board of Adjustment granted setback variances 
to accommodate for the additional equipment and security fencing required for the 
upgrade.  
22. The scope of work involves a combination of major utility facilities and tower upgrades.  
23. The scope of work would fall under the “Utility Facilities, Major” and “Utility Facilities, 
Aboveground” uses in the Snyderville Basin Development Code.

“Utility towers and transmission and distribution towers greater than forty five feet (45')

The accompanying conditional use  permit application is seeking approval to expand and rebuild 
the existing  Summit Park Substation located at the coordinates of  40.74916, - 111.607778 in 
Summit County. 
 
As part of Rocky Mountain Power’s ongoing wildfire mitigation work to harden the electrical grid 
which allows us to reduce wildfire risk making our system more resilient through various types of 
weather conditions. The Summit Park Substation has been identified to be located in side a Fire 
High Consequence Area (FHCA) and currently operates at 46,000-kilowatt transmission and 12.47 
distribution voltages.  
 
The existing substation yard will be expanded by 0.07 acre on the east and south sides. The purpose 
for the expansion is  to accommodate the new 138-kilowatt transmission and 12.47 distribution 
equipment. A new, engineered retaining wall will be constructed on the east and south sides of the 
yard. All new equipment will comply with the National Electric Safety Codes and avian safe 
standards. Neighboring landowners will be notified about the work that will take place. 
 
Protecting the communities  we serve while providing safe, reliable power is  one of  Rocky 
Mountain Power’s top priorities. This project will improve Rocky Mountain Power’s  electrical 
system, safety, and reduce wildfire risk in the area. This is one of several projects Rocky Mountain 
Power is working on and will be implementing over the next several years within FHCA areas. 
Along with these efforts, we have increased inspections and vegetation management near our 
facilities to mitigate wildfire risk. We look forward to working with the county on this and future 
projects to better serve the communities in Summit County.

STAFF MEMO 
 
To:   Snyderville Basin Planning Commission   
From:   Jennifer Strader, Principal Planner 
Date of Meeting: April 22, 2025 
Regarding: Silver Creek Village Design Review Committee 
 
On February 19, 2025, Staff asked the Planning Commission to appoint a member to serve on 
the Silver Creek Village Center Design Review Committee (DRC). Before Commissioner’s 
committed to the position, there were questions regarding the time commitment.  
 
Staff reached out to a representative from Silver Creek Village and were told that the time 
commitment varies based on the number of submissions that are received. They recommended 
budgeting 1 hour per submission (30 minutes for a pre-meeting review and 30 minutes for the 
meeting), but that is a conservative estimate.  
 
The role of the DRC is: 
 
•  To approve or disapprove proposed designs and plans for site planning, building 
construction, landscape, exterior lighting, signage, colors and materials, additions and 
other improvements; prior to submittal to Summit County and commencement of 
construction. 
•  To ensure construction or installation conformance to the plans as submitted and 
approved. 
•  To report inconsistencies with approved plans and recommend appropriate actions for 
Enforcement. 
•  To develop, review, publish and maintain minimum standards and guidelines for the 
community. 
 
Section 6.12 of the Silver Creek Village Center Development Agreement states:  
 
“Design Review Committee (DRC). Prior to the submission to the County of any Development 
Permit for a proposed development Parcel, the Developer shall submit its plans to the DRC to 
obtain the DRC's written recommendation in accordance with this Agreement and the Code. The 
Developer is required to obtain this recommendation prior to submitting any applications to the 
County. This recommendation shall be submitted to the Community Development Department 
as part of a development permit application package.”
