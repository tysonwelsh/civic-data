Pre Council, 6:00 p.m.
1. Welcome
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present except for
Council Condie who is excused.

2. Regular Agenda
2.a Consideration of Resolution #2024-31 proposing a Tax Rate for the City of Lehi for Fiscal
Year 2025 (July 1, 2024- June 30, 2025)
Dean Lundell, Finance Director, presented this item and explained how the property tax rates in
Utah are determined. He also answered questions. There was discussion on rate comparisons
with other cities. The Council directed staff to provide more research on rate comparisons and
employee salary comparisons.

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to approve Resolution #2024-31 proposing a Tax Rate
                for the City of Lehi for Fiscal Year 2025 (July 1, 2024- June 30, 2025). Councilor
                Newall seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Absent; Councilor Hancock,
    Yes; Councilor Newall, Yes; and Councilor Stallings, Yes. The motion passed with 4 in favor
    and 1 absent.


2.b Consideration of Resolution #2024-37 taking formal action to pick up required employee
contributions in the Public Safety and Firefighter Tier I and Public Safety and Firefighter Tier II
Contributory Retirement System.
Dean Lundell, Finance Director, presented this item.

                pick up required employee contributions in the Public Safety and Firefighter Tier I

3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time, Councilor Newall
   moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Hancock seconded the motion. The motion passed
   unanimously. The meeting was adjourned at approximately 6:20 p.m.
