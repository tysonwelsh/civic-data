BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.47
acres located on the south side of Walden Lane, at approximately 1128 E. Walden Lane
(Exhibits B & C). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a
Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow a six foot (6’) fence along a portion of the front
property line.

The home is part of the Sommerville in Draper No. 2 subdivision and was built in 1992.

The Residential Low-Medium Density land use designation is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, which allows both residential and
agricultural uses (see Exhibit D). According to DCMC Section 9-8-020 “The purpose of the RA1
and RA2 zones is to foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to
promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of
limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be
detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial
uses”.

Conditional Use.
The applicant intends to put a six foot (6’) vinyl privacy fence along a portion of the front yard
as well as a six foot (6’) iron gate that extends from the home to the fence. A layout of the
proposed fence location can be found in Exhibit F of this report. The 6’ portion of fencing would
extend beyond the designated front yard plane, which would require a Conditional Use Permit
per DCMC 9-27-080(A). The applicant is asking the proposed fence run along the north portion
of the front yard, adjacent to the sidewalk. The 6’ vinyl privacy fence would run from the rear
property line (at the NE corner) to the west toward the front of the home. The existing 3’ white
rail fence would be removed to accommodate the new 6’ fence.

Additional perimeter fencing is anticipated to stretch from the NE property corner to the SE
corner and from the same SE corner toward the west, along the southern property line. Again 6’
iron fencing with iron gates is planned for the north-south segments that stretch from the
house to the privacy fencing.


Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a conditional use permit request is found in
Section 9-5-080(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such requests
as:

         E. Approval Standards: The standards of this subsection shall apply to the issuance of a
             conditional use permit:
          1. A conditional use permit may be issued for a use to be located within a zone where
             the particular conditional use is allowed by the use regulations of the zone.
          2. Reasonable conditions may be imposed as necessary to substantially mitigate
             reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. These conditions
             may include conditions concerning use, construction, character, location,
             landscaping, screening, parking, hours of operation, and other matters relating to
             the purposes and objectives of this section. Such conditions shall be expressly set
             forth in the motion authorizing the conditional use permit.
          3. If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use
             cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to
             achieve compliance with applicable standards, the conditional use may be denied.
          4. The following factors shall be reviewed and considered in determining whether a
             conditional use permit application should be approved, approved with conditions, or
             denied because reasonable conditions cannot be imposed to achieve compliance
             with applicable standards:
               a. The harmony and compliance of the proposed use with the objectives and
                  requirements of the city's general plan and this title;
               b. The suitability of the specific property for the proposed use;
               c. Whether the proposed use or facility may be injurious to potential or existing
                  development in the vicinity;
               d. The economic impact of the proposed facility or use on the surrounding area;
               e. The aesthetic impact of the proposed facility or use on the surrounding area;

                                  APPLICANT STATEMENT

We petition approval for the placement of a 6’ white vinyl fence beyond the designated front
plan of the home, along Walden Lane in order to better secure the privacy of our family which
includes two adults, and two young children under 8 years of age. The proposed fence is to be
set along the sidewalk, running from the rear property line (at NE corner) toward the west
toward the front of the home. There is an existing 3’ white rail fence, and a section of this will
be removed to accommodate its replacement with the privacy fence.

We intend to maintain extensive front yard landscaping on the north plane of our lot toward the
west, as well as the full viewable are adjacent to Cindy Lane on the west. The fence post
stretching perpendicular from the NE corner of the home is more than 120’ from the street
intersection corner curbing. The length and setback of the fencing on the north ensure there
will be no limitation to the current street traffic sight lines.

Additional perimeter fencing is anticipated to stretch from NE property corner to SE corner and
from the same SE corner toward the west along the southern property line. 6’ iron fencing with
iron gates is planned for the north-south segments that stretch from the house to the privacy
fencing. We understand this section to be outside the scope of the conditional use permitting
process.
