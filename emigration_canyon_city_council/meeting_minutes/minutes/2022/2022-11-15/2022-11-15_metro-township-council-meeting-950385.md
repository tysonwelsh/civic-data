Municipal Energy Sales and Use Tax & Municipal Telecommunications License Tax
THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider the

License Tax.

Council Member Pinon, seconded by Council Member Brems, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.
Mayor Smolka stated the legislature passed a bill allowing metro townships to
collect franchise taxes. Metro townships are allowed to collect sales tax and Class B and C road
funds. The Council is considering the implementation of this tax to allow for better revenue
streams and to fund the metro township itself.
Polly McLean stated there are two taxes that are being considered tonight. Both
taxes have been permitted for municipalities to use and would be a first for Emigration Canyon.
The Municipal Telecommunications License Tax has a 3 ½% maximum taxable amount and the
Municipal Energy Sales and Use Tax has a 6% maximum taxable amount. Because this is a new
tax for Emigration Canyon, an estimated tax is not available. If passed, residents will begin being
taxed on April 1, 2023. Collected taxes would go into the general fund to be used in Emigration
Canyon.
Greg Peterson asked why the maximum taxable percentage was chosen instead
of going with a lower amount and what will the funds be used for.
Mayor Smolka stated Emigration Canyon is being subsidized and the collection
of these taxes will bring it closer to being able to fund itself.
Mr. Peterson asked if the subsidy will be going away or does the Council just want
to be able to fund itself.
Mayor Smolka stated it will help make Emigration Canyon self-sufficient. Funds
that help support Emigration Canyon are coming from Magna, Kearns, Copperton, and White City
Metro Townships and unincorporated Salt Lake County. Those jurisdictions will want to spend
those funds on their own areas.
Council Member Brems stated Emigration Canyon Metro Township is part of Salt
Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA), which collects property taxes and uses
those taxes to fund Unified Police Department (UPD) services in Emigration Canyon. As
SLVLESA projects into the future, it anticipates a shortfall in the next couple of years. The
legislature is telling SLVELSA that it will not change the tax structure until it has exhausted all its
taxing authority. SLVLESA’s member entities agreed to go back to its communities and discuss
the possibility of implementing these taxes. Some of the money that will be collected through the
Municipal Energy Sales and Use Tax & Municipal Telecommunications License Tax can be used
to fund police services.
Gray Rogers asked what the priority are.
Council Member Brems stated funds are short on some road projects that need
to be completed and there are numerous areas along the road where the hillside is sloping down.
Mayor Smolka stated some of the projects in the canyon are, drainage along the
highway to fix water that is running across the road, slope stabilization, and removing jersey
barricades. There is a need for more restrooms in the canyon to accommodate visitors and to
improve water quality. There is the possibility of widening the road to accommodate bike lanes.

Mayor Smolka stated this tax was not available to the metro townships when they

Ordinance Restricting Camping
The Council reviewed the following Ordinance restricting camping:
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-11-03

Date: November 15, 2022

ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 13 CHAPTER 04 SECTION 200 “CAMPING
RESTRICTED TO DESIGNATED AREAS”
WHEREAS, Utah Code 10-3b-501 vests the powers of municipal government with the
Metro Township Council (“Council”) and Mayor; and
WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“ECMT”) Council has enacted
ordinances to protect the public’s health, safety, welfare, and quality of life within the Town, and
WHEREAS ECMT has received reports and complaints about individuals camping in
vehicles and otherwise along public streets which create health and safety concerns.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP COUNCIL OF, UTAH, THAT:
Section I: Title 13 Chapter 04 Section 200 “Camping Restricted to Designated Areas” is
amended as follows:
13.04.200 Camping Restricted to Designated Areas
A. No person shall camp or lodge in any public street or public right of way, except in places
specifically designated and set apart for that purpose.
B. It is unlawful to park any motor vehicle, commercial vehicle, or recreational vehicle on a
public road, street, or private property, for purposes of human habitation or for overnight
camping from 1:00 AM to 6:00 AM. Any vehicle parked or left standing in violation of this
subsection for a period exceeding six consecutive hours may be impounded or removed
by a peace officer or other designated official, and the owner may be fined as provided in
municipal code.
