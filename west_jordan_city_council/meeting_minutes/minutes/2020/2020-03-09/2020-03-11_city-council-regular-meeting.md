                  DISCUSSION REGARDING WASTEWATER

    Korban Lee showed the trunk lines that service wastewater within West Jordan on a map,
    particularly the two main trunk lines( 9000 South and New Bingham Highway) that service
    the southwest portion of the City. He explained that wastewater, like stormwater, was
    sometimes     measured with        a   metric   called      an   Equivalent Residential Unit ( ERU), which
    represented    the   amount     of wastewater          an   average         home would produce ( One ERU =

    approximately 341 gallons of water and sewage discharged per day).

    The Future Land Use Map assumed certain densities of residential units and certain
    acreages of commercial and industrial land uses. The Wastewater Master Plan used the
    Future Land Use Map assumptions about land use and assumed certain Wastewater ERU
    needs. The Wastewater Master Plan then guided development of wastewater projects to
    build out a system to service the assumed ERUs.


    Mr. Lee explained that many potential land users were proposing development in the
    southwest part of the City that would require wastewater ERUs per acre far in excess of
    what   was   assumed in the      masterplans        for that     acreage.    He said there may have been some

extra capacity in the 9000 South and New Bingham Highway trunk lines, but not much,
and it was not spread equally.

Mr. Lee presented the following:

               CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS AND APPORTIONMENTS
               How should the remaining capacity in the 9000 South and New Bingham Highway
               wastewater transmission lines be apportioned?
               Should the City allow the apportionment of ERUs be transferred from one property
               owner      to   another   in the southwest    part of the City? If so,   how? Through   what
               mechanism?

               How should capacity be apportioned between existing land owners and vacant
               landowners?

               What about undeveloped land outside of the City' s current municipal boundary?

               OPTIONS AND OPPORTUNITIES
               Get aggressive on ERU caps and encourage wastewater capacity conservation
               Create a transferable ERU entitlement
               Expand the capacity of the 9000 South and New Bingham Highway transmission
               lines
               Look for ways to transfer the wastewater transmission to another line
               Develop a wastewater scalping plant and water re- use plant
               Look for ways to create downstream capacity
               Other possibilities

Tim Heyrend commented that a lot of the options were cost- driven. Mr. Lee answered
questions from the Council, and explained that Staff would come back to the Council at a
future time with policy decisions.
