CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3742, A RESOLUTION ADOPTING
THE AMENDED WASHINGTON COUNTY PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE DISTRICT
POLICY AND ADOPTING THE AMENDED CONSOLIDATED TAX DISTRICT AND
FEE POLICY FOR ALL DISTRICTS THAT LEVY A TAX

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that there was recently a meeting to approve
the Solara PID, and the applicant has requested one further change relating to the length of the PID
bonds. Washington County’s PID policy had the PID bonds not to exceed thirty years; however,
Solara has requested the PID bonds not to exceed forty years. This Resolution would update to
accommodate the requested change. Commissioner Almquist requested to repeal the PID policy
in it’s entirety. Ms. Hales stated that change is possible, but it would need to exclude the Solara
PID and any PIDs that happen within municipalities.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3742,
A Resolution Amending the County’s PID Policy and the Tax District and Fee
Policy as Attached and Additionally Move to Repeal the PID Policy Solely for
Future PID Applications Within the Unincorporated County, but Not
Affecting the Solara PID and Not Affecting the PID Policies that Apply to PID
Approvals in Municipalities. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3744, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A COOPERATIVE SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH THE USDA ANIMAL AND PLANT
HEALTH INSPECTION SERVICE, WILDLIFE SERVICES FOR WILDLIFE DAMAGE
MANAGEMENT, SUBJECT TO FINAL LEGAL REVIEW

Habitat Conservation Plan Director (HCP) Cameron Rognan explained the HCP does a number of
projects to help recover the Mohave Desert Tortoise, including protection from a common
predator, the raven. Oiling raven eggs is the primary form of remediation; however, in some
circumstances, finding nests is not possible. This interlocal cooperation agreement would be for
one year with wildlife service to help with higher conflict areas, not to exceed over $6,000.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3744,
A Resolution Approving a Cooperative Service Agreement with the USDA
Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service, Wildlife Services for Wildlife
Damage Management, Subject to Final Legal Review. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
