AMENDMENT TO NEPHI CITY COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AREA AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson informed the council that in December of 2017, Nephi City entered into an agreement
with the Nephi City Community Reinvestment Agency to provide tax incentives for a hotel project on the
south end of the city. Through many challenges, that project has now reached completion and the
Community Reinvestment Area (CRA) is set to begin.
    In the original agreement, a four-year time frame was included for the CRA to start. The final year
was 2021. The main reason for this provision was to encourage the developer to complete the
project. The developer was on track to meet that time frame when the COVID year came around. The
hospitality sector was hit especially hard. Despite this, the developer was able to secure financing from
a local bank in Utah County and was able to move forward with the project. The project was delayed
approximately one year, and the hotel was able to open in the latter part of 2022. Now that the project
is complete and the hotel developer has met their obligation, the CRA needs to begin but the time frame
provision needs to be amended. If the project had not yet been completed, a recommendation would
be made to extend the time frame. Since the project is completed, the recommended amendment to
the original CRA agreement with the city removes the time frame altogether.
     Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the amendment to the Nephi City
Community Reinvestment Area agreement. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed
unanimously.

PATRIOT FLEET LEASING AGREEMENT APPROVED:

    Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that approximately a year ago, the city went out to bid for
services on purchasing and selling vehicles for the city’s administrative fleet. The city and the selected
vendor, Patriot Fleet Leasing owner Ryan Wright, have been working under the terms of the submitted
bid. With each purchase and sale having different terms, staff felt it may be better to create some
parameters around these transactions using a formal agreement. This will make things easier for Patriot
when negotiating these transactions and help to maximize the city’s return on investment.

The attached agreement sets forth these parameters which include but are not limited to:
     Vehicles will be sold in the time frame of 10 to 14 months after original purchase.
     Minimum dollar amounts the city seeks to obtain when selling a vehicle and provisions for when
        this minimum cannot be met.
     How vehicles being sold will be marketed.
     A bonus for Patriot when a vehicle is sold over a certain amount from the purchase price.

Councilor Parady moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the service agreement with Patriot Fleet
Leasing. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
