# PMN backfill coverage — Orem City

Per-year meeting-DATE comparison: repo minutes layer vs Utah Public Notice (PMN) minutes, and the PMN-only dates recovered into this dataset. Window 2020–2026. Comparison is by meeting date with a ±4-day tolerance (the repo is a near-superset for Council & PC; PMN is the sole source for RDA/MBA/BoA/SSLD, which have no repo layer).

**39 unique meeting dates recovered** (44 raw files retained incl. draft/duplicate alternates), ~38 MB. All recovered documents yielded machine-readable text (born-digital or OCR).
