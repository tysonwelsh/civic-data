                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-19

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO SIGN AND EXECUTE A REAL
         ESTATE PURCHASE CONTRACT WITH THOM BELCHAK

  (Affecting Parcel No. 27291020120000 - 3807 West 11800 South Riverton, Utah 84065)


       WHEREAS, Riverton City has identified a number of safety concerns related to
pedestrian travel along and in the area of 3807 West 11800 South Riverton, Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has determined that in the interest of public safety it is both
reasonable and necessary to design, construction, operation, maintenance, use, repair,
replacement and improvement a sidewalk system which terminates on either end of property
located at the above-described address; and

        WHEREAS, on the date of November 16, 2021, the Riverton City Council adopted
Resolution No. 21-61 authorizing legal proceedings to condemn property necessary for
construction of the aforesaid sidewalk system, should negotiations fail to reach an amicable
solution with the property’s owner; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City entered into extensive negotiations with the property’s
owner, Thom Belchak to establish the fair market value of the Property and acquire, purchase or
otherwise obtain fee simple ownership of the Property; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Mr. Belchak has
agreed terms of a right of way purchase agreement in lieu of proceeding to defend an action in
eminent domain approved by the Riverton City Council, and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the terms of the
agreement are supported by the property’s fair market value and will advance the City’s
objective to enhance transportation safety in the community with an improved sidewalk system;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Real Estate Purchase Contract with
          Thom Belchak, to acquire right of way for a sidewalk system at the address of 3807
          West 11800 South Riverton, at a purchase price of $45,000, plus associated closing
          costs. In addition, the Mayor is authorized to execute any additional documents at
          closing if the same are necessary to facilitate closing, provided said documents do not
          culminate in a substantial alteration to the material terms of the Real Estate Purchase
          Agreement, and said documents are approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
