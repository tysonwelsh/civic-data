SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 1.16 acres located at
approximately 865 West Alan Point Drive (See Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2
(Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) Zone. The applicant is requesting that a Plat
Amendment be approved to vacate a wetlands no build area in his rear yard for future construction of a
barn on his property.


BACKGROUND
The Alan Point Subdivision is a 22 lot subdivision recorded in 1998 with lot sizes around one half acre to
over an acre in size. The recorded plat states in lots 11-19 that there is a large wetland crossing all of the
mentioned properties rear yard and is recorded as a “no build zone” without approval to vacate and build
from the Army Corp of Engineers.

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately two dwelling
units per acre (See Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the
purpose of the RA2 Zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and
preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals
and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings,
protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.”

The RA2 zoning designation is identified by the General Plan as a preferred zoning classification for the
Residential Low/Medium land use designation. RA2 zoning abuts the subject property on all sides.

Subdivision Layout. The lot is over an acre in size and has a single family home on the east side and a
large rear yard to the west. The non-buildable area of the subject property is close to half of the property
and almost all of the rear yard
