                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 22-61

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT BE ISSUED TO
 BOWEN, COLLINS & ASSOCIATES FOR THE ENGINEERING DESIGN AND THE
   CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT OF THE 11800 SOUTH WEST WATERLINE
            PROJECT, BETWEEN 2700 WEST AND 3600 WEST

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, South Valley Sewer District is doing a West Sewer Line Project on 11800 South
between 2700 West and 6000 West; and

        WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to replace an older waterline located
within that same corridor.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order Contract be issued to Bowen, Collins
            & Associates for the Engineering Design and the Construction Management of the
            11800 South West Waterline Project, between 2700 West and 3600 West.

        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 4th day of
October 2022 by the following vote:
