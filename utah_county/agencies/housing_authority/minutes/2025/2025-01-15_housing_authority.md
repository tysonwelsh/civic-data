C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-01-03 Unrestricted HCV Funds for HAP 
Steve made a motion to approve using unrestricted HCV funds for HAP. Sara seconded 
the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
During 2024 the HCV department went into a shortfall. HUD funds were not sufficient to 
support the HUD contracted number of vouchers issued to HAUC.  HAUC worked with the 
HUD shortfall team for 6 months to reduce the program as much as possible and to apply for 
additional funds from HUD. The additional funds were approved but delayed in being received, 
therefore HAUC is asking for the board to approve the use of unrestricted funds to be used to 
make Housing Assistance Payments (HAP) for January 2025.

E. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-01-05 HAUC 5-Year Pipeline 
Sara made a motion to approve the HAUC 5-Year Pipeline. Suzanne seconded the 
motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC Board of Commissioners requested the Executive Director to create a 5-year pipeline 
for development activities to increase housing units. The plan provides a visual format by year, 
project, and other details by which HAUC will focus its time, effort, and other resources to 
achieve. The plan will be updated annually or as frequently as deemed necessary. The Board 
of Commissioners supports the Executive Director to focus time and other resources to 
accomplish the plan.
