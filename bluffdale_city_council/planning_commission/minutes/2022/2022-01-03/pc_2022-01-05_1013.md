3.     CONSIDERATION AND VOTE on the Quail Ridge at Bluffdale Phase 1 2nd
       Amended Plat for 1.98 Acres located at approximately 14600 South 1695 West to
       Amend Three (3) Residential Lots and Create Six (6) Residential Lots in the R-1-10
       Infill Overlay (10) Zone, Applicant - Joe Wilkins (Application 2021-09) - Staff
       Presenter, Ellen Oakman.

Associate City Planner, Ellen Oakman, presented the staff report and identified the location of
the subj ect property, which is in the R- 1-10 Infill Overlay ("IO") Zone. The plat originally had
contained three lots. The applicant was proposing an increase to six lots. In August of 2020,
Ms. Oakman made a rezone modification to the IO Zone for the subj ect property because of the
topography of the land. The modifications consisted of adjustments to the setback requirements.

Ms. Oakman next reviewed the original pl at for Quail Ridge Phase 1. The three lots identified
on the plat would become six if the new configuration is approved by the Planning Commission.
She clarified that the property to the north of 14600 South is the only land being considered
tonight. Ms. Oakman then reviewed the Concept Plan that was originally approved to provide
context to the proposed changes. The setbacks were 20 feet in the front, 20 feet in the rear, and
10 feet on each side.

In response to Chair Cragun ' s question regarding what would be different from before,
Ms. Oakman stated that it was just an amendment of the plat from three lots to six lots in the
original Phase 1 property. Commissioner Luker noted that the southern half of the Quail Ridge
plat had already undergone some changes and he sought clarification on the proposed
reconfiguration of the northern half. Ms. Oakman explained that the draw ing she was using was
old and provided context for the proposed changes to the north.

In response to Commissioner Flynn's question regarding whether street parking was available for
the three original lots, City Planner/Economic Development Director, Grant Crowell, stated that
if they park on the street, they would be parking on a collector road that connects to Redwood
Road. It would likely have a "no parking" sign posted. There would still be no parking on
14600 South since it connects to Redwood Road. The proposed six lots would be on a cul-de-sac
that would provide more opportunity for on-street parking.

The applicant, Joe Wilkins, gave his address as 470 South Wood land Hill s Drive, in Woodland
Hill s. He commented that the project has been ongoing since about 20 18.

ln response to Commissioner Loumis' question regarding plans for secondary water and the
adequacy of water shares, Mr. Wilkins stated that the original property was previously set up
with secondary water and adequate water shares.

Ulisses Flynn moved to approve the Quail Ridge Phase 1, 2nd Amended Plat Application
2021-09 subject to the following:

Arnolds, along with the property owners to the west, have expressed interest in participating in
the road right-of-way if their zoning request is approved.

Originally, Mr. Crowell thought the road would start in the east and progress toward the west. It,
however, appeared more likely that the road will begin to the west and progress to the east
incrementally because there are still rights-of-way to be acquired to the east. Mr. Crowell said
he was hoping the road could be started next year. Whether it would be in phases or all of it, or
by whom, he could not say at this time.
