                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 6:07 p.m.
REZ2022-000621 - Jody Jones is requesting an amendment to the General Plan Future Land Uses and
Character Areas from the “Existing Commercial Character Area” to the “Medium to High Density
Character Area”. This reclassification would match the existing R-M zoning and allow for a density of 20
units per acre. Acres: 3.13. Location: 5000 West 5035 South. Zone: R-M. Planner: Justin Smith
(Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Justin Smith provided
an analysis of the staff report.

Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding codes and density, residential
development in a commercial zone, commercial needs to be on the first floor, recommendation to policy
change, traffic, and vacancies.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Kearns Community Council
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
Comments: Mr. Snow said Kearns Community Council gives an unfavorable recommendation to
amending the general plan. More in line when the codification of the code is complete. Bring back when
discussion of actual use for that area.

Speaker # 2: Applicant
Name: Jody Jones
Address: Not provided
Comments: Ms. Jones said city growing and experiencing multi-family and growth and affordable housing.
All struggling with traffic issues. Daycare is closing, commercial area is there and doesn’t make sense.
Trying to create more affordable housing in Kearns.

Commissioner Taylor motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Koester seconded that motion.

PER2022-000625 - Robert Vaccaro is requesting an exception be made for sidewalk improvements. Zone:
M-1 (Manufacturing). Location: 4748 South Cable Ridge Road. Planner: Morgan Julian (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Morgan Julian provided
an analysis of the staff report.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Kearns Community Council
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
Comments: Mr. Snow said there was not a recommendation for or against. Was not taken through the
community council.

Speaker # 2: Applicant
Name: Robert Vaccaro
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Vaccaro said proposing new development in the corner and leasing 37X55 foot area. New
development requires a sidewalk placed and requesting an exception to the code due to the area and only
taking up a small part of the front of the area. No public in the industrialized area. Unmanned shelter,
infrequently visited by technicians for google fiber equipment.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding Camp Kearns Plat dedication, more appropriate
time to add curb, gutter, and sidewalk when a bigger development comes in, discrepancy between build and
fix, ADA requirements and public access.
