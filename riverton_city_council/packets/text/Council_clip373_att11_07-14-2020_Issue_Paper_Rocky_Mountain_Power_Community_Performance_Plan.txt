            RIVERTON CITY WORK SESSION / COUNCIL MEETING



FROM:          Ryan Carter

SUBJECT:       Resolution Approving Community Performance Plant for the year 2020
               with Rocky Mountain Power.



SUMMARY:

Periodically, Rocky Mountain Power (“Rocky Mountain”) desires to execute a “Community
Performance Plan” document with the municipalities whose residents are served with electricity
produced by Rocky Mountain. The purpose of this document is to maintain relations between
Riverton and Rocky Mountain. The document presents somewhat of an opportunity for the City to
insert items of interest to it. To this end, staff has inserted language to “Provide for the installation of
a vehicle charge station at City Hall.”

This document comes to the City Council at a time when Rocky Mountain is in the planning stages to
construct a new power substation in Riverton. The draft presented to the Council omits any reference
to a substation, under recommendation by the City Attorney’s office. The reason for this
recommendation is the City needs to maintain its objectivity as it proceeds to fulfill its role as a land
use authority in any forthcoming applications for land use by Rocky Mountain.

While the City Attorney’s Office has prepared this issue paper and a Resolution to approve the
Community Performance Plan, additional questions related to this item would best be directed to Trace
Robinson, Public Works Director.
