3.     Presentation Related to the General Plan Update - Presenters, VODA Planning and
       Design - Staff Presenter, Grant Crowell.

City Planner/Economic Development Director, Grant Crowell reported that the process has been
ongoing for about one year. He introduced Mark Morris and Annaliese Eichelberger from YODA
Planning and Design.

Mr. Morris gave a PowerPoint presentation and stated that a public survey was administered in the
spring of 2021 with approximately 600 responses. An additional public event took place over the
summer with an Open House at City Hall a few weeks ago. Chair Cragun asked Mr. Morris if he
planned to cover things that have changed as a result of feedback received. Mr. Morris responded
that the main decisions were to NOT make the changes contemplated. He provided an overview of
the document structure, which is broken into the following six chapters:

       l.     Introduction and Plan Vision;
       2.     Land Use;
       3.     Housing;
       4.     Economic Development;
       5.     Open Space and Trails; and
       6.     Sustainability.

Each chapter is subdivided into the following components:

       •      Summary Snapshot: A single-paged concise summary.
       •      Existing Conditions: As of 2021 .
       •      Framework: Maps and over-simplified plmming approach to each of the topics. The
              maps are not intended to be binding; instead, they provide a general overview to
              assist with decision-making.
       •      Future Considerations: A look at what is coming down the road toward Bluffdale and
              how to address those things.
       •      Policies and Implementation: The meat of each chapter specifies the steps to follow
              for achieving each goal set for each chapter.
       •      Trends and Best Practices: Samples pulled from what other cities are doing well to
              inform decision making.

The intent was for the document to be useful and concise to inform the Planning Commission as
they consider proposals that come before them.

Mr. Morris next reviewed the Land Use Framework Map and reiterated that it was NOT the City's
codified Land Use but is intended to provide information to be considered, such as opportunities
and land uses that need to remain unchanged. He stressed that it is just a tool. The sample page
was broken into goals, with accompanying policies and implementation strategies for each.
