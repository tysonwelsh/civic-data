NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                                    MEETING MINUTES
                                     February 9, 2022

                                     Nephi, UT 84648
                                         7:00 p.m.
Planning Commission Chairman Ann Peterson called the meeting to order and welcomed all in
attendance.

PRAYER: Commissioner Heather Robertson

LOVELESS ESTATES VICINITY PLAN APPROVED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the Commission that the City Council had been shown the
Loveless Estates vicinity plan the previous night at council meeting because he didn’t want the
developers to go through the entire planning commission process if the council was not onboard for a
necessary zone change for this development. Mr. Atkinson said that the property is currently zoned as
R2-Z and based on the vicinity plan before the commission the property would need a R2-PUD zone. He
stated that there were also concerns over parking, both visitor and RV/OHV/ATV/trailer parking. Mr.
Atkinson said that the city has been interested in having a regional park in this quadrant of the city
based on the city’s Parks & Recreation Master Plan. Mr. Atkinson showed two different vicinity plans
one with a regional park and one without.
   Commissioner Fran Petersen expressed her concern about visitor parking. Representatives from
Priority Homes, who are the developers of Loveless Estates, added clarification on the parking needs and
expressed their desire to add whatever parking the commission thought was needed. A discussion
ensued about various aspects of the development.
    Commissioner Kent Heap moved to approve the Loveless Estates Vicinity Plan #2 (without the park)
with a contingency of changing the zone to a R2-PUD which hearing will be noticed next meeting.
Commissioner Karl Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BARBAROSA DEVELOPMENT VICINITY PLAN APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the Commission a vicinity plan for a subdivision near the city’s middle
freeway exit. The subdivision is dividing one lot into two in preparation to potentially construct a
Wendy’s restaurant. The parcel is in the HC-2 zone and no zone change request is anticipated. He
stated that the vicinity plan does not contain any concerns from the development review committee
and if the subdivision is approved any submitted site plan will undergo additional review. The developer
will work with UDOT for an access on the state highway.
   Commissioner Cory Thomson moved to accept the vicinity plan for the Barbarosa Development.
Commissioner Karl Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC HEARING-STORMY’S CREATIVITEES HOME OCCUPATION LICENSE.:

    Chairman Peterson opened the hearing and asked Stormy Jermain, the applicant for the license, to
briefly describe her business. She said that she would be printing on shirts, tumblers, magnets etc.

Chairman Peterson asked if there would be much traffic at her home due to the business. Ms. Jermain
said that she anticipated very little traffic to her home because of her business. There was no public
comment. Commissioner Heap moved to approve the home occupation business license for Stormy’s
Creativitees. Coummisioner Alan Hancock seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

COMBINED USE ZONE DISCUSSION:

  City Planner Shauna Talbot lead a discussion about possible revisions to the combined use zones. The
Commission discussed how many units per acre were desired and maybe defining high density, medium
density, lower density based on how many units per acre. They discussed solidifying allowed uses in the
CU zone and CU2 zone. They discussed having the General Plan coincide with the zones.

MINUTES APPROVED:

   Commissioner Ann Peterson moved to approve the minutes from the Planning Commission meeting
held on January 12, 2022. Commissioner Karl Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.
   Commission Kent Heap moved to approve the minutes from the Planning Commission meeting work
session held on January 19, 2022. Commission Kent Heap moved to approve the minutes.
Commissioner Alan Hancock seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

   The Commission decided to meet on February 16, 2022, at 6:00 p.m. for a work session.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

Commissioner Cory Thomson moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Ann Peterson seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 8:16 p.m.
