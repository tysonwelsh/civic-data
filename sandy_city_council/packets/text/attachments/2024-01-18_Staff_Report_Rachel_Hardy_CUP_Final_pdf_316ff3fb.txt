Request
 The applicant, Rachel Hardy, with permission from the property
 owner, Jeff Glauser, is requesting approval of a conditional use
 permit for commercial retail sales and services for a property
 located at 7821 S. 700 E. Suite #150. The request is for a massage
 therapy business (commercial retail sales and services) in a portion
 of an existing office building within the Boulevard Commercial
 (BC) Zone. See application materials (Exhibit A).

Background
 The subject property is located on the east side of 700 East, a
 major arterial state highway. The building was built in 1968. The
 property was rezoned from a residential property in the R-1-8
 zone to a commercial property in the current BC zone in 1989.
 The home was remodeled in 1990 for office use and has 1,664
 square feet.

 The property is located in the Boulevard Commercial (BC) zone.
 Properties to the north and south are in the BC zone with a gas
 station to the north and a vacant parcel to the south. The property
 across 700 E. to the west are commercial businesses in the CN
 (Planned Center – Neighborhood District) zone. Properties
 located to the east are residential homes in the R-1-8 zone.

Public Notice and Outreach
This item has been noticed to property owners within 500 feet of the subject area by mail.
A neighborhood meeting was held on January 2, 2024, and no neighbors attended the
meeting. The owners of the building attended and had the following comments:
    1. Owners take care of landscaping and snow removal.
    2. They think this will be a good complement to their business as it won’t create
        excessive traffic, noise or interrupt their business.
    3. Rachel has been reliable, and they are excited to work with her and feel they can
        work well together.
    4. Owners only use three of the eleven parking spaces for employees and do not have
        any customers come to their office.

In addition, no emails or phone calls were received.

Analysis
  The applicant is proposing to have a massage therapy business at 7821 S. 700 E located in the BC zone. Massage therapy
  fits into the commercial retail sales and services category and is a conditional use in the BC zone. The applicant will
  occupy one of the two suites in the building. The space has its own reception area and two rooms to be used for massage
  therapy services. The applicant also has two rooms in the basement that will be used for storage.

  Parking
  The Sandy City Landy Development Code requires that commercial retail sales and services have five (5) parking stalls
  per 1,000 square feet and a business office use requires four (4) spaces per 1,000 square feet. The proposed location has
  11 parking stalls, and the building has 1,664 square feet. If the entire building were to be used for commercial retail
  sales and services, nine (9) parking spaces would be required. The basement floor would only be able to have space for
  storage and no active commercial retail use. The other business in the building conducts business online and only uses
  three of the parking stalls for employee use. They do not have clients come to their place of business. The applicant will
  be the only employee and plans to have only one customer at a time.

  Conditional Use Standards
  The City may impose conditions on a conditional use based on certain standards of review found in section 21-33-04 of
  Sandy City Land Development Code. The following items from that list are applicable to this particular request and merit
  discussion or additional consideration by the Planning Commission (staff analysis is found below in italics):

Sec. 21-33-4. Conditions.
     In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions that address:
     (4)   Design, location and amount of off-street parking, loading areas and solid waste disposal and collection areas.
     Parking for the combined office and retail use or 100% retail use is sufficient at 11 parking stalls . See parking analysis above.

 Staff Concerns
  Staff has no concerns with the proposed commercial retail sales and services use in the BC zone.

Recommendation
 Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for commercial retail sales and
 services in the BC zone and as described in the staff report for the property located at 7821 S. 700 E. based on the
 following findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
    1. The proposed use meets the intent of the BC zone.

   2. The proposed use should have minimal to no impact on the surrounding uses.

   3. The number of available stalls meets parking requirements.

Conditions:
   1. Only the top floor may be used as active retail space. The basement may only be used for inventory or storage
       purposes in order to meet parking requirements.

   2. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fide & Life Codes.

   3. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City Development Code.

   4. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.
