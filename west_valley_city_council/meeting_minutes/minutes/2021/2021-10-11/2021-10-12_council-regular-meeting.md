COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Jim Vesock stated that he would like to see the City participate in HB411. He
    indicated he attended the final My Operation Hometown project of the year. Mr.
    Vesock expressed concern about temperature, trash build-up, locker room
    cleanliness, and user activity in the Family Fitness Center.

        Darrell Curtis stated that there was a 2021 report on the Moderate Income Housing
        Plan. He indicated that he is not aware of any home in the City that is available for
        $250,000 and stated that housing is just not affordable. Mr. Curtis stated that he
        would like more information on how to interpret the report.

        Mike Markham stated that he has picked up the tab of Police Officers in the past.

B.      CITY MANAGER COMMENTS
        Wayne Pyle, City Manager, stated the Moderate Income Housing report reflects
        market prices from 2018-2019. He noted that the City is aware that the market has
        changed.

B.      CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
        Upon inquiry, members of the City Council had no comments.

RESOLUTION 21-176: APPROVE A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR
THE MIDVALLEY BUS RAPID TRANSIT CONNECTOR PROJECT
Mayor Pro-Tem Buhler presented proposed Resolution 21-176 that would approve a
Memorandum of Agreement for the Midvalley Bus Rapid Transit Connector Project.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

        This resolution authorizes a MOA between the City, Taylorsville, Murray, Wasatch
        Front Regional Council, UDOT, Salt Lake Community College and UTA to
        establish roles and responsibilities for the Midvalley Connector bus rapid transit
        (BRT) project.

        UTA has been working with the aforementioned entities on the Midvalley
        Connector BRT project which will begin in Murray, travel through Taylorsville,

        and end in West Valley at the Fairbourne Station bus hub. This MOA establishes
        roles and responsibilities for this BRT project. The City’s involvement includes
        property for the expanded bus hub and improvements to Lancer Way.

Upon inquiry by Mayor Pro-Tem Buhler there were no further questions from members of
the City Council, and he called for a motion.

        Councilmember Christensen moved to approve Resolution 21-176.
