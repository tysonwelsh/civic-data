         The purpose of the Joint Work Session was to review the agendas for the City Council and Special Redevelopment
Agency meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.; receive a presentation of the Fiscal Year 2023–2024 Financial Audit Report and
Fiscal Year 2023–2024 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report; discuss the 2019–2024 Strategic Plan Report – City Image and
Fiscal Sustainability and Transparency, Public Services Department Accomplishments, and Management Services Department
Accomplishments; receive the Visit Ogden Annual Report; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair White and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agendas for the City Council and Special
Redevelopment Agency meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.




Fiscal Year 2023–2024 Financial Audit Report
        Jeff Miles, representing the City’s Audit Partner, HBME, used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide a
summary of the audit report, including the following:
             1. Report on Internal Control Over Financial Reporting:
                      a. No identified significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in internal control.
             2. Report on Compliance and Other Matters:
                      a. No identified instances of noncompliance with matters that are required to be reported under
                           Government Auditing Standards.
             3. Single Audit – Compliance for Each Major Federal Program:
                      a. Ogden complied in all material respects with requirements for the major federal award programs.
                      b. No identified significant deficient or material weaknesses in internal control.
                      c. The schedule of expenditures and federal awards is fairly stated in all material respects.
             4. The city was determined to be a low-risk auditee.
             5. Compliance and Report on Internal Controls Over Compliance as Required by the State Compliance Audit
                 Guide:
                      a. No identified significant deficiencies or material weaknesses in internal control.
                      b. There were two instances of noncompliance. These included:
                                i. City’s aggregate unassigned, assigned, and committed General Fund balance totaled
                                    39.42% of the total revenue of the city general fund for the current fiscal period. The
                                    maximum allowed by state law is 35%.
                               ii. For the year ended June 30, 2024, the auditors noted in the Weber Morgan Strike Force
                                    Fund that actual expenditures exceeded final appropriated expenditures by $53,698.
         For a copy of Mr. Miles’ presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Discussion between the Council and Mr. Miles centered on mechanisms and controls that can be implemented to
prevent similar noncompliance issues in future audit cycles. The Council expressed gratitude to Mr. Miles, City staff, and the
Audit Committee for their efforts throughout the audit process.

Fiscal Year 2023–2024 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report
         Assistant Finance Director Jensen used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide an overview of the Fiscal
Year (FY) 2022-2023 Annual Comprehensive Financial Report; this included:
             • Review of the status of interfund loans in the City.
             • Historical fund balances in the General Fund and the Redevelopment Agency Fund.
             • Highlights and challenges:
                        o Capital Asset Investment-$61,441,918.
                        o New Bond Debt-$127,210,000 for Wonderblock Development.
                        o Early implementation of GASB pronouncements.
             • Long term debt – government funds.
             • Long term debt – enterprise funds.
             • Receipt of the GFOA Certificate of Achievement for Excellence in Financial Reporting.
             • Ongoing challenges:
                        o New Governmental Accounting Standards.
                        o Federal Grant Standards and Reporting.
                        o Golf and Airport Cash Balance.
         For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Discussion among the Council and staff centered on the purpose of past interfund loans and whether to write the
debt off or maintain it in the City’s financial reporting.

Visit Ogden Annual Report
         The City Council has requested to receive information from local foundations and nonprofit organizations that have
a contractual agreement with Ogden City. These presentations help the Council to become more familiar with the objectives
and efforts of the organizations.
         Visit Ogden President/CEO Toliver used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to describe the organizational mission
and goals, successes and challenges of Visit Ogden over the past year and future plans for the organization. For a copy of the
presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.

session on February 4, 2025. A review of Economic Development and Recreation Strategic Directives occurred in the Council
work session on February 11, 2025.
