Continued discussion and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for a Utility structure and Underground transmission line for a secondary irrigation system for Henefer Town, located southwest of the Henefer Cemetery, Summit County, UT; Parcel NS-836-X (38.5 acres). Applicant: Chris Thomsen, JUB Engineers. Owner: Town of Henefer. Administrative Review. Project #25-047.
Jennifer Leslie, County Planner.
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Henefer Secondary Irrigation CUP Staff Report
Planner Ray Milliner stated that the ESCPC and Staff have had continued discussions over the past few meetings. He said that he had spoken with Engineer Brandon Brady, who had reviewed the information submitted to him and recommended the project’s approval with the findings, conclusions, and conditions of approval in the staff report. (6:02 PM)
Mayor Kay H. Richins of Henefer thanked the Commission and all the departments for their support in completing this project, which will benefit the citizens of Henefer. (6:03 PM)
Commissioner Seth Bowen asked if the modifications and concerns from the last meeting had been resolved. Planner Milliner and Civil Attorney Helen Strachan responded. (6:04 PM)
Commissioner David Darcy asked if the engineering calculations had been reviewed and validated and if so, can Condition One be struck? Engineer Brady responded. (6:05 PM)
Commissioner Bridget Hayes expressed her satisfaction and thanked everyone for their hard work. (6:08 PM)
Commissioner Don Sargent expressed his satisfaction and thanked the Mayor, all staff in Henefer, and the County staff for successfully closing this project., all staff in Henefer, and the County staff for closing this project. (6:08 PM)
Commissioner Vice Chair Wheaton expressed her gratitude to everyone who has worked on this project. (6:09 PM)
David Darcey made a motion to approve the conditional use permit for the Henefer secondary irrigation system and associated infrastructure located on Parcel NS-836-X and any other property associated with the secondary irrigation system as depicted in the exhibits, with striking condition number one of the conditions but approving the conditional use permit with the remaining finding of facts, conclusions of law and remaining conditions of approval in the staff report. (6:09 PM). Don Sargent seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Bill Wilde, Alex Peterson.
