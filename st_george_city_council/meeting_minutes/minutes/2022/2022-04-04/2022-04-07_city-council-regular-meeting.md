CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Gardner Brothers Drilling for the
        drilling of a new culinary water well located north of the City at the
        Homestead Well Site.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project will allow for the drilling of
          a new groundwater well at the Homestead site. The City's existing Mountain
St. George City Council Minutes

          Spring waterline runs through the site. We have worked with the State Engineer
          to identify this new well as an additional point of diversion for various existing
          water rights that are in the area. The well will not provide additional water but
          will provide redundancy and additional peaking source during the summer
          months. Staff recommends awarding the bid to Gardner Brothers Drilling in the
          amount of $896,840.

      b. Consider approval to award bid to Doug Hunt Construction, Inc. for the
         Airport Hangar 56J Construction/Reconstruction project.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Hangar 56J was surrendered to the City
          by Gustavson Enterprises. The Utah Highway Patrol will lease the hangar upon
          completion of construction. An invitation to bid (22-014) was posted on 3/15/22.
          Two (2) bids were received on 3/24/22. After reviewing the bids, staff
          recommends awarding the bid to Doug Hunt Construction in the amount of
          $321,574.

      c. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Jones and
         DeMille Engineering, Inc dba Campbell Architecture for the design of the
         Water & Energy Services Administration Building HVAC and roof
         replacement.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The HVAC system at the Water &
          Energy Services Administration Building is in need of repair. This Professional
          Services Agreement with Campbell Architecture is for the design of a new and
          upgraded HVAC system, as well as the replacement of a leaky roof system. Staff
          recommends approval of the agreement in the amount of $72,700. This project
          will also allow for new solar panels to be installed on the roof.

      d. Consider approval of a Cost Reimbursement Agreement with Quality
         Excavation for the upsizing cost of a waterline through the White Trails
         Subdivision.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Quality Excavation is currently
          constructing a phase of the White Trails Subdivision. As part of the subdivision,
          they are installing an 8-inch diameter waterline. Our Water Capital Facilities Plan
          identifies the need for a 16-inch diameter waterline in that area. The cost
          reimbursement will allow the City to pay for upsizing the 8-inch waterline to a
          16-inch waterline to meet the needs of future growth in the South Block area.
          Staff recommends approval in the amount of $48,040.

      e. Consider approval of a SASO Agreement with Executive Flight Academy
         to provide flight training at the St. George Regional Airport.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Due to increasing demands for flight
          instruction, Executive Flight Academy will provide another option for flight
          instruction to customers at the St. George Regional Airport. They will be
          instructing in Cirrus type aircraft, giving residents and future customers of

      and Sky Rocket Road, west of Dixie Drive, for a project to be known as The
      Townhomes at Grand View.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: In 2018, the applicant applied for a General
      Plan amendment on the subject property along with the rest of the Grand View
      property. In February of 2020, The City Council heard a request to change the
      general plan again on the property to medium density and commercial and remove
      the low density residential. In February of 2021, the property was granted PD zoning
      allowing a total of 431 units. Since that time, two of the areas defined in the PD

      This proposal is for a multi-family project consisting of 112 townhome units and 23
      additional single family and duplex units at another location in the PD. The
      underlying general plan is Medium Density Residential (MDR). At their January 11,
      2022 meeting, the Planning Commission held a public hearing and recommended
      approval.
