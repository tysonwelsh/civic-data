BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use Permit for approximately
4.19 acres located on the east side of 700 West, at approximately 12153 South 700 West
(Exhibit C & D). The property is currently zoned CC. The applicant is requesting that a
Conditional Use Permit be approved to designate specific areas on the property where
storage may occur for the pumpkin patch and thriller park structures and equipment
during the off-season.

The property has historically been used as agricultural and is considered a legal
nonconforming agricultural use. There are five permanent structures on the property, all
green houses. The applicant utilizes the property for a pumpkin patch, corn maze, and
haunted house during the fall months. The applicant obtains a Special Event Permit every
year for these fall activities.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a conditional use permit request is found
in Section 9-5-080(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

         E. Approval Standards: The standards of this subsection shall apply to the issuance of a
             conditional use permit:
          1. A conditional use permit may be issued for a use to be located within a zone where
             the particular conditional use is allowed by the use regulations of the zone.
          2. Reasonable conditions may be imposed as necessary to substantially mitigate
             reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. These conditions may
             include conditions concerning use, construction, character, location, landscaping,
             screening, parking, hours of operation, and other matters relating to the purposes
             and objectives of this section. Such conditions shall be expressly set forth in the
             motion authorizing the conditional use permit.
          3. If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use
             cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to
             achieve compliance with applicable standards, the conditional use may be denied.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission approve the request based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, as listed within the staff report. The Planning Commission should also review the
request, hear from the applicant, and receive public comment prior to making a decision.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

         1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                  Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                  site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
         2.       That all access to the site for events, storage, and agricultural uses shall be
                  from 700 West. Any access from Galena Park Blvd that utilizes adjacent
                  property will require written permission from the adjacent property owner
                  for use. Such written permission shall be provided to the City prior to usage.
                  If the site is reconfigured to provide access to Galena Park Blvd on the
                  Kuwahara’s property, such access shall be reviewed and approved by the
                  Draper City Engineering Division prior to usage.
         3.       Kuwahara’s Pumpkin Patch and Thriller Park and The Scared Haunted
                  attraction shall be required to store their structures, sets and props in such a
                  manner within the designated areas that they can be identified as temporary
                  and not permanent. If these requirements are not met for all areas listed, the
                  fire code official shall consider the property to have permanent structures
                  and no longer temporary. If this happens the fire code official shall enforce
                  all of the requirements of the fire and building codes to ensure fire and life
                  safety compliance.

The findings for approval as are follows:

         1.       Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to
                  alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.

The findings for denial as are follows:

         1.       Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to
                  alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.

       1.      The present and future requirements for transportation, traffic, water, sewer, and
               other utilities for the subject site do not appear to be detrimentally impacted as
               described. Given the location and proposed use, the use is not anticipated to
               generate a significant impact to water, sewer, or other potential utilities.
       2.      The number of trips generated by the site may have a detrimental impact to
               traffic in the area due the ingress and egress of vehicles from the site without
               an established drive approach on 700 West and no drive approach on Galena
               Park Blvd. In order to access Galena Park Blvd, the site is required to provide
               written permission from the adjacent business (Velocity Auto) to have
               employees and clients use their public street access, i.e. drive approach.
               Otherwise, no access is permitted to Galena Park Blvd. Access may continue
               from 700 West due to the parking area being delineated and asphalt millings
               being placed and maintained onsite. The applicant is required to keep all
               sediment onsite by controlling storm runoff and erosion and controlling dust
               during usage.
               a. Any future special use permits / event permits will have the same access
                   requirements and restrictions.
       3.      Parking is delineated in the materials provided with the CUP application. The
               parking will be used for employees and customers. The applicant indicated all
               clients will park in the delineated area when patronizing the business, including
               drop off and pick up. Parking may not impact traffic on public streets, 700 West
               and Galena Park Blvd.
       4.      The proposed use does not appear detrimental to the health, safety, or general
               welfare of the persons residing or working in the vicinity, or injurious to the
               property or improvements in the vicinity.
