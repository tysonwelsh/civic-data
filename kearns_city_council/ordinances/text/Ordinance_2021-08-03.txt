                              KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP

              ORDINANCE #2021-08-03                       DATE: August 16, 2021

         AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
        ADJUSTING THE COMPENSATION OF THE MAYOR, VICE-CHAIR,
                        AND COUNCIL MEMBERS

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-818(1), the elective and statutory officers
of Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) receive such compensation for their services as the Kearns
Council (“Council”) may fix by ordinance adopting compensation or compensation schedules
enacted after public hearing; and
       WHEREAS, the Council desires to adjust its compensation levels to account for inflation
and recent increases in workload and responsibilities, particularly for the Mayor, who is
performing all of the administrative duties for Kearns in the absence of a city manager or
administrative staff; and
       WHEREAS, the Council provided notice of its intent to adjust its compensation and held a
public hearing on August 16, 2021, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-818.
    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL:
   1. Mayor Compensation. The Mayor’s compensation will be $3,000.00 per month. The
      Mayor’s compensation will be reduced to $1,700 per month effective on the first of the
      month following the date on which: (1) the Council hires a city manager after creating
      such a position via ordinance; or (2) the Council budgets money for an administrative
      assistant to the Mayor and the Mayor appoints the administrative assistant after consulting
      with the Council.

   2. Deputy Mayor Compensation. The compensation for the Deputy Mayor will be $1,500.00
      per month.

   3. Council Member Compensation. The compensation for the other Council Members will
      be $1,200.00 per month.

   4. The foregoing compensation of the Mayor, Deputy Mayor, and Council Members of the
      Kearns Metro Township Council (“Council”) will remain in effect until otherwise
      amended by the Council or by statute, provided that the Council will review its
      compensation each year.

   5. This Ordinance will become effective immediately upon its passage and its publication on
      the Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1), and the
      Mayor, Vice Mayor, and Council members will receive the full amount of their
      compensation for the month in which this Ordinance goes into effect.
