OCTOBER 19, 2021

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on October 19, 2021, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commission Chair Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow,
Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk
Sarah Lloyd.

PRESENTATION: IRONMAN

Washington County Tourism Director Kevin Lewis presented an update on the 2021 IRONMAN
that was held in Washington County on September 18", 2021. There were 3,522 total athletes with
ninety seven-percent (97%) being from outside of Washington County and seventy-five percent
(75%) being first time visitors to Washington County. The total economic impact was eighteen
(18) million dollars.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2841, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT FOR ZION REGIONAL
COLLABORATIVE SERVICES

Destination Development Manager Leslie Fonger explained that the Zion Regional Collaborative
(ZRC) consists of the communities in the Zion Corridor. The ZRC desires to continue to work with
an independent contractor as the coordinator to take on responsibility for project management,
workshop coordination, facilitation of regional communication and other associated tasks.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2021-2841, A
Resolution Approving an Independent Contractor Agreement for Zion
Regional Collaborative Services. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and
Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ _RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2021-2842, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING A _ BOARD MEMBER _ TO THE HABITAT CONSERVATION

TECHNICAL COMMITTEE

Washington County Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Administrator Cameron Rognan explained
that the HCP calls for the establishment of a Technical Committee (TC) comprised of members
from the Division of Utah State Parks and Recreation, Utah Division of Wildlife Resources
(UDWR), the Fish & Wildlife Service, Bureau of Land Management (BLM), HCP, a biologist at
large, and a local biologist position. The HCP TC has recommended the appointment of Ron
Torgerson to fill the vacant position as a Local Biologist.

CONSIDERATION OF PUBLIC WORKS REPORT

Washington County Public Works Director Ron Whitehead stated that most flood repairs have
been completed at this time. Safety enhancements on Kolob Road have been finished. Flood
repairs to Pinto have been completed, Hebron Wash has been completed. Dalton Wash work has
been postponed due to flood repair, but now that all work has been completed, Dalton Wash is
underway again. Winter grading is beginning.

Commissioner Iverson reported on the plumbing issues happening today in the Administration

to the south side of the building.
