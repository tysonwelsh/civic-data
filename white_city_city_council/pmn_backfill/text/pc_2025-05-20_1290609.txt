                                              Hearing began at – 6:05 p.m.

OAM2025-001340 – An application to amend White City Code to create the Landscape Contractor Yard
use. This citizen-proposed ordinance change would allow the Landscape Contractor Yard use in the A-2
zone as permitted use. The proposal includes specific use standards and conditions associated with the use.
Applicant: Alex Smith. Planner: Shad Cook (Discussion/Recommendation)


White City Planning Commission – May 20, 2025 – Meeting Summary                                                         Page 1 of 4
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Shad Cook provided an analysis of the zone
amendment.

Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding the general plans perceived use for A-
2, 10600 south corridor for potentially mixed use, residential and small commercial, southern spur zoned
A-2 qualification, citizen initiation, permitted and conditional use difference, necessity use, and previous
rezone application on corridor mixed-use zone.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Alex Smith
Address: 1226 East 10600 South
Comments: Mr. Smith said he currently has a property business license for a nursery and is active, selling
public and plant materials. Main aim is to run landscape/contractor yard. Bring plants for their own purpose
to sell to clients. Skid steers come back and go to the job location. Trying to make this lesser impact than
the nursery operation with retail sales in and out and take down a notch and will not be a storage yard.
Secure fencing all around for materials in landscape projects. Property on 10600 meets the CMU
characteristics in the master plan, unsure of the denial. Understands the horse theme in the area with dimple
dell keeping it wild. Been in business 25 years and do good for the community and provide for our families.
Trying to get a home set up and neighbor has an A-2 and do something similar, and MJ is doing something
similar with an exception. Trying to stay within noise ordinance only properties on 10600, not private lane
down 1225.

Commissioner Seiger-webster asked if fencing meets the setback rules as far as visibility for turning out
onto roadways. Had applicable sections of ordinances, fencing is exempt from setback, 4–6-foot opaque
fence could meet the visibility. Smith said secure gates out of sight. Jay said text amendment. If it becomes
permitted use, it will come back to staff for approval. Hunsaker asked if the other two properties with
exceptions were located on 1225. Shad confirmed that they are on 10600.
