                                        EMIGRATION CANYON CITY COUNCIL

                       RESOLUTION NO. R2026-04                              DATE: February 17, 2026

                   A RESOLUTION APPOINTING A MEMBER OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON
                    CITY COUNCIL AS THE ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIVE APPOINTEE TO
                   THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL
                                         SERVICES DISTRICT

                       WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon is a member entity of the Greater Salt Lake
                 Municipal Services District (“MSD”); and

                         WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon City Council (the “Council”) may appoint
                 a Council Member to the Board of Directors of the MSD and an alternate representative
                 if primary representative is unavailable; and

                       WHEREAS, on January 20, 2026, the Council appointed Mayor Brems to the
                  MSD Board; and

                          WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint Council Member Jennifer Hawkes
                  as the alternate representative to the MSD.

                       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON
                 CITY COUNCIL:

                          1. The Council appoints Jennifer Hawkes as the Emigration Canyon alternate
                 representative on the MSD Board.

                         2. Emigration Canyon staff are authorized to provide the MSD with a copy of this
                 resolution announcing Robert Pinon’s appointment.

                          3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective upon passage and
                 publication.

            ADOPTED AND APPROVED at a duly called meeting of the Emigration Canyon, Utah City Council
            on this 17th day of February 2026.
