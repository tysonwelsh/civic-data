4.2. County Mayor
Mayor Wilson delivered her monthly report.
· The Salt Lake County Animal Services Division had a very successful Giving 
Tuesday, where it raised over $17,000 for its Injured Animal Fund.
· The Salt Lake County Parks and Recreation Division hosted more than 250 
people for a community Thanksgiving dinner at the Central City Recreation 
Center.  The meal was prepared by staff from Utah Valley University ’s Culinary 
Arts Institute, and served by the recreation center staff.  
· The Magna Recreation Center will be hosting “Santa Stacks” on December 23, 
2024, from 9:00 AM through 11:00 AM. Members of the community can enjoy 
free hotcakes, drinks, and games.
· The Office of Regional Development ’s Ebus map is up and running, which will 
allow viewers to see real -time data of non -polluting buses moving throughout the 
County.  The County is partnering on the air quality monitoring project to help 
policy makers better understand if there are sections of the valley more 
susceptible to bad air than others.
· The Mayor commended the Office of Regional Development team for its 
ongoing work on the Millcreek Canyon Federal Lands Access Program (FLAP) 
grant project.
· The Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) is conducting a study on 
traffic congestion in Big Cottonwood Canyon.  It is in the public comment 
stage.  The Mayor stated she would continue to promote common -sense 
solutions.

5.5 Public Works Operations Retention and Sign-on 
Compensation
24-2373
Attachments: Staff Report
PWOps Retention.Sign on compensation.12.10.2024
Presenters: Steven Kuhlmeier, Public Works Operations Director. Stephanie 
Ross, HR Business Partner Manager.
(Approx. 3:30PM, 10 Min.)
Discussion/Direction
Mr. Steven Kuhlmeier , Public Works Engineering Project Manager, 
Engineering and Flood Control Division, stated the Public Works Operation 
Division was having difficulty retaining employees, which made meeting 
contract obligations difficult.  The division utilized retention and sign -on 
compensation bonuses in 2023, and was looking to do the same now.  He 
reviewed the division ’s request for Council authorization of a $ 2,500 
retention and sign -on compensation bonus for merit and new employees, 
which would come with an obligation of a minimum of 15 months of tenure .  
Any employee who stayed on less than 15 months would have to repay the 
bonus.  This would help the division get through its snow removal 
obligations in the winter months, and could be paid for with under expend 
from the division’s current budget.  
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council 
Member Winder Newton, that this agenda item be approved. The motion 
carried by a unanimous vote.
