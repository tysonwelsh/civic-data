                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-05

         A RESOLUTION APPOINTING A CITY REPRESENTATIVE TO THE
                    UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY BOARD

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is a part of the Riverton Fire Service Area which utilizes the
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) for fire services; and

       WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint a representative to serve on the governing
board of the Unified Fire Authority; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember Tish
Buroker is willing to serve as the City’s representative on the Unified Fire Authority Board.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of
          Councilmember Tish Buroker to serve as the City’s representative on the
          governing board of the Unified Fire Authority Board until a successor is
          appointed.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
February 2023 by the following vote:
