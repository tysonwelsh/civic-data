WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
MAY 3, 2019

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Dean Cox at 3:00 p.m. on May 3, 2019, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf,
Commission Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.
Commissioner Almquist was excused as he was away on County business.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Commission Administrator Nicholle Felshaw

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2458, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING TWO HOST VENUE _AGREEMENTS__WITH__WORLD
TRIATHLON CORPORATION FOR IRONMAN RACES

Washington County Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that the current contract with
IRONMAN ends after the scheduled race on May 4, 2019. The contract has been negotiated
for an additional five (5) year term, which also includes two full IRONMAN races.

In addition to the five (5) year contract, Washington County has been selected to host the
World Championship in 2021. In order to qualify for that race, an athlete must win their
division in a previous IRONMAN race. It is anticipated that athletes from over 100
countries will compete in the IRONMAN World Championship.

Chairman Cox recognized Mr. Lewis and the Tourism team for all their efforts in working
with IRONMAN to ensure the ongoing commitment as well as the IRONMAN World
event.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution R-2019-2458, A
Resolution Approving Two Host Venue Agreements with World
Triathlon Corporation for Ironman Races. Motion Seconded by
Chairman Cox and Carried by a Unanimous Vote with Commissioners
Cox and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF UPDATING THE COUNTY BUILDING USE POLICY

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that meeting rooms can be scheduled for
conducting county business or by registered political parties. The Chambers may only be
used when the meeting cannot be accommodated in another space.

Emergency Services Operations Director Jason Whipple explained that the fire marshal
examined the Commission Chambers and determined that the maximum occupancy load
for the room is forty-nine (49).
