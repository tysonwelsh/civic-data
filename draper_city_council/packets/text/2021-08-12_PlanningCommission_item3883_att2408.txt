SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a plat amendment application for
approximately 0.32 acres located at the corner of Medina Drive and Sunset Stream Way,
located at approximately 797 E. Medina Drive (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned
RSD-5. The applicant is requesting a plat amendment to adjust the property line between
two lots, specifically lots 745 and 746.

The properties are part of the Bellevue Phase 7B which was recorded on March 20, 2013
(see Exhibit E). Both lots 745 and 746 were previously owned by a single owner. Because
he owned both lots, they had built improvements on both lots. The owner then decided to


Lot 745 Bellevue Mellor Residence                                    App. # SUBD-088-2021

sell the Lot 745 but wanted the improvements to remain with Lot 746. He applied for and
was approved by the Zoning Administrator for a lot line adjustment in September 2019 (see
Exhibit F). After that adjustment was recorded, it was difficult to sell the lot due to the
configuration of the lot line between lots 745 and 746. The applicant for this application,
Jim Mellor, purchased the lot and requested that the lot line be returned to its original
configuration. A deed was recorded reversing the lot line adjustment, but it was done
without city approvals. This request is to amend the plat and move the lot line back to its
original location so the new owner can build a home on the lot.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RSD-5 zoning classification (Exhibit D). Draper City
Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-17E-010 states: “the Bellevue project is a master planned
architecturally controlled residential development by the "developer". The residential

special district (RSD) zoning requirements allow for and permit flexibility in terms of
planning, site design, lot size and development through the ability to customize the zoning
and development standards to achieve unique development opportunity. The Bellevue
project seeks to utilize this RSD zone, which provides the flexibility required to make
possible significant project, community and system improvements and amenities. The
Bellevue RSD is located between 13200 South and Corner Canyon Creek, 300 East and the
East Jordan Canal.” Although the RSD zoning is not identified as a preferred zoning
classification for the Residential Medium Density land use designation, the land use
designation identifies master plan communities as a preferred zoning classification and the
RSD’s are simlar in nature. The subject property is surrounding by RSD-5 zoning with a
small area of RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot minimum) to the east.

Subdivision Layout. The proposed amendment will be adjusting approximately 1,054
square feet between Lot 745 from Lot 746 (see Exhibit G). Lot 745 will be slightly larger and
Lot 746 will be slightly smaller. The access for Lot 746 will continue to be from Medina
Drive and no new lot will be created with the adjustment of the square footage between
the lots. The proposed Lot 745 will be approximately 15,061 square feet and the proposed
Lot 746 will be appoximately 25,769 square feet. Both of the lots will comply with the
minimum standards of the RSD-5 zoning district and will not result in the creation of any
non-conformities.

Plat Amendment.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for
such requests as:

         The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by
         signing an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use
         Authority finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no
         public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
         Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
         and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.       Once final plat has been approved, a Land Disturbance Permit is required prior to
         construction activities onsite. Permit is obtained through the Engineering Division.
3.       Any work in a public right-of-way will require an Encroachment Permit obtained
         through the Engineering Division.
4.       If the plat amendment is not recorded within one year from the date of approval or
         construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced,
         such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be extended by the
         Planning Commission for up to one additional six month period for good cause
         shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the
         expiration of the original one year.

The findings for approval as are follows:

1.       There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
         way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
