WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 21-12-03 DATE: December 9, 2021
A RESOLUTION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ADOPTING THE 2022 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP FINAL
BUDGET
WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Section 10-2a-401 et.seq.; and
WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council’”) is the municipal legislative
body for White City pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-501; and
WHEREAS, the Council has complied in all respects with State Law, including holding public
hearings, in establishing budgets for the Calendar Year beginning January 1, 2022 and ending December
31, 2022; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to fiscal policy for funding the 2022 White City Budget, the Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) adopted the 2022 Budget for the MSD on October 20,
2021, releasing the funding for the 2022 White City Budget; and
WHEREAS, the noticing for the public hearing to consider and copies of the 2022 White
City Tentative Budget, (Attachment “A”) were made available for public consideration at least
ten (10) days prior to the date of the public hearing for the Budget; and
WHEREAS, the adoption of the 2022 Budget will allow White City to comply with State
Law and the Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act for Cities;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL as follows:
SECTION 1. The White City Metro Township Council hereby adopts the 2022 White City
Final Budget, Attachment “A”.

SECTION 2. That upon the final adopton, the budget shall be in effect for the budget year |
and subject to later amendment as provided by law. |
|
SECTION 3. A copy of the final budget shall be posted and made available to the public |
on the City’s website: www.whitecity-ut.org |
SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage thereof.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 9° DAY OF DECEMBER, 2021 by the White City Metro
Township Council, White City, Utah. |
|
