                                RESOLUTION NO. R2025-05

A RESOLUTION DESIGNATING A SURVEY AREA, AUTHORIZING THE
PREPARATION OF A DRAFT COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT PROJECT AREA
PLAN AND BUDGET, AND AUTHORIZING AND DIRECTING ALL NECESSARY
ACTION BY THE AGENCY, STAFF, AND COUNSEL.

WHEREAS Magna City, Utah (the “City”), created the Community Reinvestment Agency of
   Magna (the “Agency”) pursuant to the provisions of, and the Agency continues to operate
   under, Title 17C of the Utah Code, the Limited Purpose Local Government Entities-
   Community Reinvestment Agency Act (the “Act”) for the purposes of conducting project
   area development and economic development activities within the City, as contemplated by
   the Act; and

WHEREAS the Agency, having made a preliminary investigation and conducted initial studies
   and inquiries, desires now to conduct project area development activities in all or a portion
   of the area depicted on the map attached hereto as Exhibit A (the “Survey Area”), a portion
   of which may become part of a future community reinvestment project area (the “Proposed
   Project Area”), pursuant to UCA § 17C-5-103; and

WHEREAS the Agency desires to begin the process of adopting a project area plan for the
   Proposed Project Area by adopting this Resolution authorizing the preparation of a draft
   Project Area Plan, pursuant to UCA § 17C-5(103).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE
COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AGENCY OF MAGNA AS FOLLOWS:

1.    That the Agency hereby designates the Proposed Project Area as the Broadway Community
Reinvestment Project Area, the proposed boundaries of which will be within the Survey Area;

2.      That the Agency outside legal counsel and Agency staff, be and are hereby authorized and
directed:

       a.     to prepare a draft Community Reinvestment Project Area Plan;

       b.     to prepare a draft Community Reinvestment Project Area Budget;

       c.     to undertake all such actions as may be required by the Act, or which may otherwise
              be necessary or desirable to the successful establishment of the proposed
              community reinvestment project area, including, without limitation, the negotiation
              of agreements with taxing entities and participants, the preparation for all necessary
              hearings and the preparation, publication, and/or mailing of statutorily required
              notices; and

Basic CRA Creation Process:
   1. Identify area with potential for “project area development” (as deﬁned in Title 17C)
   2. Agency Board adopts “survey resolution” identifying location for potential project area and
       authorizing work by Agency staff, etc.
   3. Finalize boundaries of proposed CRA
   4. Prepare draft CRA plan and CRA budget
   5. Set date for public hearings on plan and budget
   6. Mail and post notices of hearing (30 days and 14 days in advance)
   7. Hold public hearings on plan and budget
   8. Agency Board adopts CRA plan by resolution
   9. City Council approves CRA plan by ordinance
   10. Agency Board adopts CRA budget by resolution
   11. Mail, post, and record required notices of plan and budget

Interlocal Agreement Process (if applicable)
    12. Negotiate interlocal agreements with participating taxing entities
    13. Agency and taxing entity approve each interlocal agreement by resolution
    14. Mail and post notices of interlocal agreements
    15. Obtain attorney certiﬁcation that interlocal agreements and adoption process were done
        correctly

Participation Agreement Process (if applicable)
   16. Negotiate participation agreement(s) with developers/property owners
   17. Agency board approves participation agreement by resolution
