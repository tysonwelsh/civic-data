CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following:
• The Rivergrove neighborhood chair informed the Commission that there was a neighborhood meeting held for this
    item and there was support for the rezone. There was a neighbor that was concerned about parking with an accessory
    apartment, and he felt like this concern had been addressed with off-street parking being provided.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
• The applicant answered a question from the Commission about the amount of parking being provided. The idea of
   there being more parking than required was shown to help address the concern about parking being on the street.
• The applicant is representing his mother as the property owner that would live in the home.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
• There was a concern about there being too much parking shown on the conceptual site plan.
• The ADU would be located within the new home and not detached as allowed in the LDR zone.
• There was a concern from a commissioner that this type of amendment could set a precedent and wanted to be
   reassured that there was a mechanism that would be able to handle this in the future if an issue arose. The fact that
   these amendments must go through this same process would be a sufficient tool to address issues in the future.
• Short-term rentals would not be allowed in the LDR zones.
• This proposal fits within the existing character of the neighborhood as there are duplexes and single-family homes on
   the same block.
