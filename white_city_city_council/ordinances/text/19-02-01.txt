| RECITALS
| A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
| 10-2a-401 et seq.
| B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.
| C. White City has a contract with the Salt Lake County District Attorney’s Office to perform
certain legal work on behalf of White City.
D. Pursuant to the contract, White City is required to send notice to the DA’s Office annually to
indicate White City’s desire to continue the contract and use of services.
E. White City desires to renew the use of services and continue the contract.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the reauthorization of the
contract by and between White City and the DA’s Office and authorizes the Mayor to sign the
attached letter, hereinafter referred to Exhibit A, to affect the same.
