WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
MARCH 18, 2026

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 6:00 p.m. on March 18, 2026, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy
County Attorney Victoria Hales, and County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw.

PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER THE CREATION OF SOLARA PUBLIC
INFRASTRUCTURE __ DISTRICTS NOS. 1-4, _ON PROPERTY IN THE
UNINCORPORATED COUNTY IDENTIFIED AS PARCEL NO. 3151-A-1-NW, ON THE
WEST SIDE OF J-15 BETWEEN EXITS NOS. 23 AND 27, AUTHORIZING THE BOARD
OF TRUSTEES, THE GOVERNING DOCUMENT, AND OTHER RELATED
DOCUMENTS AND MATTERS

Deputy County Attorney explained this meeting is for the creation of the Solara Public
Infrastructure District (PID). Zoning approvals have already happened for the first development,
Solara 75. Each subsequent zone change would need approval.

Aaron Tilton, legal counsel for Solara, spoke regarding the creation of the public infrastructure
district and taxing and assessment standards for PID’s.

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales cited state code 17D-4-201-6 that any financial burden of
the PID is solely the PID’s responsibility, so no debt could be accrued by the county for this PID.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Open the Public Hearing. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

Seeing no one come forward, the following motion was made:

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Close the Public Hearing. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3733, A RESOLUTION FOR THE
CREATION OF THE SOLARA PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE DISTRICTS NOS. 1-4,
AUTHORIZING THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES, THE GOVERNING DOCUMENT, AND
OTHER RELATED DOCUMENTS AND MATTERS

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3733, A
Resolution for the Creation of the Solara Public Infrastructure Districts Nos.
1-4, Authorizing the Board of Trustees, the Governing Document, and other

DISCUSSION _AND CONSIDERATION OF THE COUNTY’S PID POLICY AS IT

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 6:56 p.m.
