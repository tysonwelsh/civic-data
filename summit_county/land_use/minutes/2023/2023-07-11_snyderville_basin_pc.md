2.
Public hearing and action regarding a Plat Amendment to vacate a portion of property from Lot FJGILL-1-AM-X and add it to Lot SCO-C-10-2AM. The applicant also proposes to vacate a portion of property from Lot SCO-C-10-2AM and add it to Lot FJGILL-1-AM-X. Applicants: Marketplace at Silver Creek Center LLC and Summit County. Administrative process. Project #22-256. Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner. (6:04 P.M.)
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner
, presented a Plat Amendment to the Commission and recommended the Commissioners forward a positive recommendation to the County Council.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:09 P.M.
Henry Sigg,
Marketplace at Silver Creek property owner
, addressed the Commission.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:10 P.M.
Christopher Conabee made a motion to approve the proposed Plat Amendment for Lot 1 FJ Gillmor subdivision amended listed as FJGILL-1-AM-X and Lot 10 Silver Creek Commerce Center second amended subdivision listed as SCO-C-10-2AM according to the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval as found in the staff report. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Thomas Cooke.
Attachment:
7.11.23 SBPC Staff Report Plat Amendment.pdf
3.
Public hearing and recommendation regarding amendments to the Moderate-Income Housing Element of the Snyderville Basin General Plan. Jennifer Strader, Senior County Planner. (6:12 P.M.)
Planner Strader, provided background information regarding amendments to the Moderate-Income Housing Element of the Snyderville Basin General Plan. Commissioners asked clarifying questions, to which, Planner Strader and Jeff Jones,
Economic Development and Housing Director
, responded.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:30 P.M.
Bonnie Park was called to speak.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:35 P.M.
A lengthy discussion followed the public hearing.
Christopher Conabee made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to Summit County Council regarding the amendments to the Moderate-Income Housing Element of the General Plan. In doing so he wanted the Council to know the Planning Commission had some concerns regarding the restrictive nature of the words zone and rezone as it relates in multiple places in the document. Further, Council to review the new language carefully and work with staff to make sure the strategies included in the updated recommendation are achievable and realistic. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Thomas Cooke.
