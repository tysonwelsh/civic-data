**Resolution: Budget for the Capital Projects Fund of the Local Building Authority for Fiscal Year 2025-26**
The Board will accept public comment and consider approving a resolution that would adopt the final budget for the Capital Projects Fund of the Local Building Authority of Salt Lake City, Utah for Fiscal Year 2025-26.

The LBA’s Capital Projects Fund for Fiscal Year 2025-26 only includes the bond debt services for the Glendale and Marmalade Libraries. (Other Capital projects throughout the City are included in the Mayor’s Recommended Budget.) The LBA is a financing tool for cities and government entities, like libraries, to bond for capital projects at better interest rates. Capital projects are big projects like parks, public buildings, and street projects.
