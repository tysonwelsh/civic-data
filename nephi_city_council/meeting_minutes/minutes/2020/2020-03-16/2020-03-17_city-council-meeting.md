PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Nephi City Chamber of Commerce President Abby Ivory. Ms. Ivory
reported that the Chamber of Commerce had lost membership recently. Councilor Jones recommended
that new businesses be given information about the Chamber of Commerce when they are issued a
business license.

CITY RECORDER LISA BROUGH RECOGNIZED FOR EARNING HER CERTIFIED MUNICIPAL CLERK
DESIGNATION:

   Mayor Glade R. Nielson presented City Recorder Lisa E. Brough with a plaque from the International
Institute of Municipal Clerks (IIMC) that conferred her the title of Certified Municipal Clerk. Mayor
Nielson read a letter from IIMC that indicated that Ms. Brough had completed the necessary education
and experience qualifications to receive the title.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 3-3-20, work session minutes 3-3-20, a list of claims on the warrant
register dated 3-17-20, the sale of four burial rights to Oliver Kester. Councilor Worwood seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

   Councilor Jones reviewed a business license application for Beauty in Her Hands. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

ORDINANCE 03-17-2020-A AMENDING TITLE 10 CHAPTER 15 OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE ADOPTED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson explained to the council that the Planning Commission had met and
discussed changes to the RV Park section of the Nephi City Code after some feedback from the City
Council at their March 3rd meeting. The Planning Commission now recommended that the term ”hard
surfaced” be removed from Title 10-15-5 (B) (7) where it sets standards for roadways. They
recommended that it be replaced with “road base hardened with lithification, packed gravel, asphalt,
concrete, road base with magnesium chloride, roto mill, or double chip seal.” The Planning Commission
also recommended that roads be built to recommendations from a geotechnical report. Councilor
Worwood noted that the proposed ordinance did not include the recommendation about a geotechnical
report. Councilor Seely voiced opposition to accepting graveled roads within a RV Park and read a letter
from a Heber City high-end RV park. Seely said that a year ago when the council was researching high-
end RV parks that graveled roads was not an acceptable option. Councilor Memmott said that the list of
acceptable surfaces was good except he wanted magnesium chloride removed from the list.
   Councilor Worwood moved to approve Ordinance 03-17-2020-A with the amendment in Section 1
“road base with magnesium chloride” removed and the sentence added to the end of Section 1 to read
“The roads shall be built consistent with the recommendations of a certified geotechnical report, which
shall be obtained and paid for by the owner of the RV park.” Councilor Jones seconded the motion.
The motion passed on the following vote:

                                Yes: Jones, Ostler, Worwood, and Memmott
                                Nay: Seely

RESOLUTION 03-17-2020-WASTEWATER PLANNING PROGRAM RESOLUTION ADOPTED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the annual wastewater planning elf-
assessment report. The report helps the sewer department identify deficiencies and problems areas
within the city. The city is required to submit the annual report the Water Quality Board each year for
their review. Councilor Worwood questioned if we had completed a rate study in the past five years.
Councilor Ostler questioned why Brandon Aagard was not listed as a wastewater operator. Councilor
Jones moved to adopt Resolution 03-17-2020. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

MAYOR NIELSON PRESENTS EMERGENCY DECLARATION:

   Mayor Nielson presented to the council an Emergency Declaration/Coronavirus (COVID-19).
The declaration stated that as the Mayor is the chief executive officer of Nephi City and because the
nation and the State of Utah had declared states of Emergency and because of the wide-spread of the
infectious disease the Coronavirus (COVID-19), he felt is was necessary to declare a state of emergency
concerning COVID-19.

   Mr. Atkinson presented an ordinance for the council to consider adopting, declaring a state of
emergency. After some discussion, the council agreed to not adopt the ordinance at this time.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reported on a meeting that he attended with Six County and the Juab County
Commissioners about the CIB project prioritization. He said that Nephi’s project of infrastructure for the
industrial park had ended up third on the priority list after Levan’s dump truck and Eureka’s salt shed.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:
   Councilor Seely reported that the library will remain open for right now and that the patron traffic
had increased since the schools had closed.
   Councilor Jones said that the library is sanitizing the books as they come in and that the library staff is
offering curb-side service if requested.
   Councilor Memmott reported on a meeting he had with the governor where he learned that small
business association loans are available and that the governor had issued an executive order to close all
dine-in restaurants and bars.
   Mayor Nielson reported that the UMPA and ULCT conferences had been cancelled. He also reported
that the dig permit had been issued by Mona City for the completion of the redundant natural gas line.
Mayor Nielson stated that Travis Worwood, City Treasurer, had assembled a list of talking points about
the history and facts of the Nephi City natural gas system for the council to have for information sake.
   Councilor Worwood said that he was pleased with the budget prioritization session that the council
had held last week. He requested that the communication coordinator increased costs be moved up on
the prioritization list.
   Councilor Ostler stated that the Miss Nephi training clinics had been put on hold because of the
COVID-19 pandemic.

  COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competence of an individual. Councilor Seely seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous
vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:40 p.m.
