WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
ELECTRONIC WORK MEETING MINUTES
APRIL 28, 2020

The Electronic Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was
called to order by Acting Chairman Cox at 2:00 p.m. on April 28, 2020, in the Washington
County Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George,
Utah. Those in attendance were: Chairman Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf,
and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance included: Utah Division of Oil Gas and Mining Representative Kim
Coburn, Pine Valley Ranger Nick Glidden, Forest Representative Adam Solt, and Zion
National Park Superintendent Jeff Bradybaugh

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION __AND_ PERIODIC REVIEW __OF COUNTY RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included: Forest Management and Mining and Minerals.
The Wilderness section will be discussed at a future meeting.

Commissioner Cox questioned whether roadless areas differ from wilderness areas.
Nick Glidden explained that the areas are managed differently.

Each section was reviewed line by line and small revisions regarding wording and
grammatical corrections were suggested.

Updates to the RMP will be brought before the Planning Commission and then to the
County Commission for final approval.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
2:05 p.m.
