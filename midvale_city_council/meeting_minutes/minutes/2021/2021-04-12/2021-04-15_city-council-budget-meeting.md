I. DISCUSSION ITEM
A. FISCAL YEAR 2022 BUDGET
Staff reviewed the budget process, key issues for the FY 2022 budget. The Council and staff
discussed the budget at great length. Each department reviewed specifics regarding budget needs
for the upcoming budget. An economic development report was presented as well as a
Redevelopment Agency report. Capital projects and operating budgets were also discussed.
