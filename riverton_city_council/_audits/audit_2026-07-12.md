# Riverton City — independent data-quality audit

**Date:** 2026-07-12  ·  **Scope:** newly built `riverton_city_council/` (all datasets).
**Method:** audit-city-data skill (statistical corpus screen, larger ground-truth samples
for a NEW city, full redundant-representation reconciliation, derived-layer + election
sanity). Read-only; no data modified.

## Verdict: **SHIP** (one bounded, low-severity fix queued)

Riverton is a clean, faithfully-extracted repo. Corpus is born-digital text with **zero**
garble/OCR/encoding pathology in either body. Every doubly-stored fact reconciles exactly
(CSV↔db↔weeks↔motions_std↔elections). The Park-City-model mayor tie-break is captured
verbatim and the mayor is never fabricated into an ordinary roll. The **only** genuine
defect is **2 dropped winning-Aye votes** in Planning-Commission roll calls that were split
across an injected page-header line — outcomes unchanged, corpus-detectable, 2 files.

## 1. Corpus screen — CLEAN (both bodies, per-year)

`screen_corpus.py` on `meeting_minutes/minutes` (128) and `planning_commission/minutes`
(119): **0** cid_artifacts, replacement_chars, PUA_garbled, mojibake, long_tokens, stubs,
short files, duplicate_bodies, read_errors — in every year. Per-year dict/split/weird
medians are flat and neighborly (no Ogden-style single-bad-year). Confirms the recon's
"born-digital PMN, no OCR" claim.

Advisory flags investigated and dismissed:
- `ends_mid 128/128` (council): every file ends on an approval stamp (e.g. `Approved: CCM
  4.16.24`), not a truncated sentence. Complete minutes.
- `hyphen_breaks` council 2022-03-01, `split_word` PC 2021-02-25: line-break hyphenation
  (`six-foot pre-\ncase`) and prose dashes (`ordinances- especially`). Cosmetic.
- **PC 2020-06-09 short doc CONFIRMED legitimate**: a properly-noticed voteless General-Plan
  work-session **memorandum** (Jason Lethbridge / JL) — no votes to extract. Not a stub.
- **0 duplicate body hashes** across all 247 files → no mis-uploaded/duplicate docs.

**Folder-date convention (cosmetic, not a defect):** the containing folder is consistently
the day *before* the meeting (posting/notice date) — e.g. `2020-02-17/2020-02-18_city-council.md`.
The authoritative date agrees everywhere it matters: **filename == index date == header
`Meeting date` for all 247 files** (0 exceptions); all `minutes_index.csv` paths resolve.

## 3. Mayor non-voting — CONFIRMED

   their vote, dropping a **winning Aye**:
   - `planning_commission/.../2020-05-14_planning-commission.md` m1 — **Kent Hartley-Aye**

     lost (source 4 Aye/3 Nay = "passed 4-to-3"; extracted 3 Aye/3 Nay).
   Outcomes unchanged (both still passed). Fix: teach the PC extractor to stitch roll-call
   text across the injected page-header before name-vote parsing. A page-header-split
   detector already isolates exactly these 2 files corpus-wide.
2. **[cosmetic]** Motion-text bleed: some `motion` strings absorb the trailing
   *"Councilmember X SECONDED…"* sentence or full amendment prose (e.g. 2025-12-16 m3,
   2020-05-19 m7). Trim capture at the seconder boundary. Votes/result unaffected.
3. **[cosmetic]** Result-label loss: 2023-02-23 m2 `result` stored `Passed 4-to-1` while
   source read *"passed 4-to-1 with two abstentions"* — the abstain **votes are captured**;
   only the qualifier text was dropped.
