FY 2025-26 Budget Preview


                            Shane Pace welcomed all in attendance and thanked the Council for the opportunity to
                            hold a budget preview and workshop. The city is in good financial shape. Mr. Pace
                            provided an overview of this evening's budget presentations. He turned the time over to
                            Mayor Zoltanski.

                            Mayor Zoltanski welcomed the public and department staff to this evening's workshop.
                            Tonight's budget preview will provide you with an overview of the FY 2026 budget. She
                            spoke about shared goals and the importance of providing excellent core services. The
                            city staff is constantly evaluating the services. She spoke about the impact of rising
                            costs on the FY2026 budget. Our city's economic position is strong and Sandy will
                            continue to be a top tier city. She provided a brief overview of the capital projects currently
                            underway: Fire Station 31, the Monroe Street project, the Alta Canyon rebuild, and the
                            Pace of Progress General Plan. The City has maintained its AAA bond rating. She spoke
                            about fiscal responsibility and maintaining our core services without raising property
                            taxes.

                                  Year 2025-2026 budget

                                  The Administrative Services Director provided an overview of this evening's presentations
                                  to the Council.

                                  Dustin Fratto, Council Director, reviewed the City Council's Top Budget Priorities for FY
                                  2026. Earlier this year, the Council held two budget priorities workshop and developed
                                  their top budget priorities for FY 2026. The budget priorities were adopted by the Council
                                  by resolution at the February 25, 2025 council meeting. Mr. Fratto provided an overview of
                                  the Council's budget priorities: Economic Development, Employee Satisfaction,
                                  Community Safety, Quality of Life, and Fiscal Health.

                                  Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director reviewed the Guiding Financial principles of
                                  Sandy City. Service, efficiency and economy, generational fairness, taxes vs. fees, and
                                  maintaining reasonable fund balances. He reviewed the city's revenue policies, financial
                                  structure and the budget history. He provided a budget outlook and economic trends,
                                  challenges, and strategies for Fiscal Year 2026. Council questions occurred throughout
                                  the presentation.

                                  James Sorensen, Community Development, provided an update of projects occurring in
                                  the city, outside of the Cairns area. Currently there are approximately 60 projects
                                  underway in the city. He reviewed the details of some of the projects: Little Cottonwood
                                  East subdivision, senior housing subdivision, the Ridge of Dimple Dell, Alta Parks
                                  subdivision, Orchards at Dimple Dell, Hale Centre Theatre additional complex, Fire
                                  Station 31, Trans Jordan waste facility, Cypress Credit Union, and the Waterford School
                                  expansion.

                                  Kasey Dunlavy, Economic Development, said our business economy in Sandy is healthy.
                                  Next month, I will be presenting a year in review to the Council. Over 2,000 building
                                  permits were issues last year in Sandy City. He reviewed Cairns area projects currently
                                  underway: the Gardner project, Centennial Village, Red Sky, Summit, and the Wasatch
                                  Shadows project.

                                  Brian Kelley continued with his presentation. We have many city projects going on that
                                  are very exciting for the city. He then reviewed budget pressures, the impact of inflation,
                                  market volatility, and budget strategies.

                                  Katrina Frederick, Human Resource Director, presented on the City's employee
                                  compensation study and FY 2026 compensation plan next steps. The evaluation process
                                  considers many sources of data including turnover, economic, nation-wide public and
                                  private sector surveys and ULCT data. Ms. Frederick provided a detailed analysis and
                                  history on each data source. She reviewed the salary survey and takeaways which helps
                                  the city identify and ensure that our city's pay ranges are competitive with other cities.
                                  She also reviewed the Compensation Plan proposal and next steps.

                                  Council convened a recess at 6:57 pm
                                  Council reconvened at 7:09 pm.

                   upgrades, and adjustments for inflation. One time requests include fleet replacement and
                   safety equipment upgrades.

                   the following: overtime/gap pay, uniform and PPE, training, software upgrades, inflationary

                   members, equipment replacement, and fleet replacement.

                   the following: surveyor position, funding for road striping, budget reallocation, and a
                   position reclassification. One time requests include funding for capital projects, fleet and
                   equipment.

                   Dan Medina, Parks & Recreation, presented his department's budget requests which
                   include the following: field supervisor, staff salaries, increased expenses related to events
                   and recreation leagues. He also requested funding for capital projects including costs
                   associated with parks, irrigation, and facility rebuild

                   James Sorensen, Community Development, presented his department's budget requests
                   which include the following: position reclassification, technology upgrades, and
                   adjustments for increased costs due to inflation.

                   Tom Ward, Public Utilities, presented his department's budget requests which include the
                   following: a 5.5% rate increase and funding costs associated with water purchases, well
                   power and lights, line maintenance and repairs, irrigation assessments, SCADA system
                   maintenance, meter maintenance, and adjustments for inflationary costs. One time and
                   capital requests include well maintenance, fleet replacements, and mainline and
                   equipment replacements.

                   which include the following: future funding of Economic Development, budget reallocation,
                   and the development of an economic strategic plan.
