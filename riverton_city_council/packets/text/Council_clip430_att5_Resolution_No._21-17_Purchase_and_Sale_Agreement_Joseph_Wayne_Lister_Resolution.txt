                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-17


         A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A RIGHT OF
        WAY PURCHASE AGREEMENT WITH WAYNE JOSEPH LESTER FOR
             REAL PROPERTY LOCATED AT 13792 SOUTH 1300 WEST.

       WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to construct the “S” Curve Improvement Project
located at approximately 13750 S 1300 W; and

       WHEREAS, in order to construct the “S” Curve Improvement Project, Riverton City will
be required to purchase certain real property located at 13792 South 1300 West in Riverton,
Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Joseph Wayne Lester is the owner of said property and desires to sell it to
Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, a Purchase and Sale Agreement (“Agreement”) has been prepared for
execution by and between Riverton City and Joseph Wayne Lester. This Agreement, which is
attached hereto sets forth the rights, duties, and obligations of each of the parties thereto; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council does hereby determine that it is in the best interests
of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to execute said Agreement.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Agreement is approved in substantially the form attached, and the Mayor is hereby
          authorized to execute said Agreement for and in behalf of Riverton City, subject to
          approval of the final form of the Agreement by the City Manager and the City Attorney’s
          Office.

       2. The Mayor and the City Manager are hereby authorized to execute any additional
          documents that may be necessary to complete the transaction approved by this Resolution.

       3. The Mayor is hereby authorized to accept the deed to the above described property on behalf
          of the City and the City Recorder is authorized to record said deed and any appropriate
          related documents in the Office of the Salt Lake County Recorder.

       4. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of March
2021 by the following vote:
