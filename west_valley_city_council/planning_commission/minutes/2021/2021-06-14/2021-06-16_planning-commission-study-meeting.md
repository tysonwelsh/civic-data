ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-4-2021   Petition by GREG WILSON requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
           minimum lot size ½ acre) to BRP (Business/Research Park). The property is located
           at 1078 W Parkway Avenue on 0.1 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545
           or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:       Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property had come before the Commission
                  about a year ago requesting a rezone from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre)
                  to BRP (Business/Research Park). Steve said the rezone was granted. Steve said the
                  owners have started to design this property and realized they would need more room
                  to fit their plan, so they are requesting an additional 15 feet be added to the
                  business/research park BRP zone. Steve explained that the subject property and the
                  property to the east have the same owner and there will still be a 25-foot setback
                  between the subject property, and the property to the east.
GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-12-2021 Petition by Petition by MARCO DIAZ requesting a General Plan change from
            General Commercial to Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) and a zone
            change from C-2 (General Commercial) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential).
            The property is located at 4612 West 3500 South on 1 acre. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at
            801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:     Steve explained this proposal is for a townhome development. He stated the property
                boundary goes out to the center of the 35th South. Steve said where this is already
                improved a right-of-way dedication would be required. Steve stated that a large
                amount of fill has been added to the property. This fill has raised the grade of the land
                significantly. Steve presented a photo that showed the property next to a surrounding
                fence, and they were almost the same height. Steve explained the applicant is
                proposing to remove the fill to bring the property back to the original grade.
                Steve stated the proposal is for three stories where the upper levels would project
                over the ground level. Steve pointed out some concerns. The west side of the
                property would be considered the rear setback and according to the ordinance the
                setback should be twenty-four feet and the concept plan is showing fifteen feet and
                this is a large reduction. Steve presented the submitted floor plans, one showing the
                proposed materials and the other the proposed design. He said that he would prefer
                the applicant provide one elevation or rendering showing both materials and design.
                So, if the Commission feels this is a good use for the site Steve will request a new
                rendering be submitted. Steve presented a slide depicting another option from staff.
                Instead of having front-loaded townhomes, having them be alley-loaded and facing
                the park. Steve said having the townhomes on one side will increase the setback.
                Commissioner McEwen asked what the proposed setback is on the west side of the
                subject property. Steve presented the concept plan once more and it shows the
                setback being 10 feet.
                Chair Meaders asked Steve if the alley-loaded townhomes that staff presented as an
                option would have the same number of units as the front-loaded townhomes
                proposed. Steve said that they would, he just wanted the Commission to see there are
                other options.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-3-2021  Petition by GL HOME INVESTMENTS LLC is requesting preliminary approval for
            the Cottages on Merry Subdivision. The subdivision consists of 5 lots on 1.23
            acres. The proposed subdivision is located at approximately 3420 South Merry Lane
            and is zoned R-1-8(Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square
            feet). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion:     Steve Lehman said the LDS Church owned the subject property prior to a subdivision
                by meets and bounds, which severed the subject property off from the Church. Steve
                explained that the subject property was used as a community garden and eventually
                sold to the applicant.
                Steve stated that there had been trees recently added along Merry Lane, the east side
                of the property and staff would like them to remain if possible. He explained that
                Marry Lane, the street on the east side of the property, is improved with a curb,
                gutter, and sidewalk. However, Sunshine Drive, the road to the south of the property,
                has a gutter system but no sidewalk. Steve presented the proposed plan for the
                subdivision containing five lots. He said the applicant is requesting that lot 5 be a
                flag lot. He stated this request is not a problem where that lot is located on the back
                corner of the subject property. Steve explained the applicant would be required to
                install improvements on Sunshine Drive and any improvements on Marry Lane that
                are deficient, and lastly, some drainage issues.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
CA-4-2021  Petition by ALIGNED DATA CENTERS requesting a conditional use amendment
           for a data center and outside storage. The property is located at 6368 W. Beagley
           Road on 5.65 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-
           963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:     Jody Knapp explained the applicant purchased the subject property and they will be
                using the building that currently exists there. Jody stated that the applicant intends to
                put a data center at the subject location. She said the building is 98,000 square feet,
                and the applicant will be occupying about two-thirds of the building at this time
                because there is another company leasing the remainder of the building. Jody
                pointed out that the applicant has an option to buy the lease out if they can relocate
                them.
                Jody explained that this application is coming before the Commission because of
                their equipment yard located outside. She said this is a large building, and they will
                be using lots of power that will produce heat. Inside, they will house hundreds of
                server racks, and the equipment outside will cool down the equipment. Jody said
                there would be a wrought iron fence along the employee area to the south. She stated
                that a screening structure would be surrounding the outdoor equipment. The structure
                base will be four feet of concrete with a 19-foot metal screen structure on top of that,
                so more of a permanent fence.
                Jody explained that the data center would be using a significant amount of power;
                they will have their own power line connecting to the Rocky Mountain Power
                substation. She said this would require easements, and the applicant has been
                working closely with public works to accomplish that.
C-14-2021        Petition by PETERSEN STAGGS ARCHITECTS requesting a conditional use for a
                 multi-tenant retail center with drive-thru. The property is located at 2572 S. 5600 W.
                 (Winco Pad E) on 1.45 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody
                 Knapp at 801-963-3497, jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:      Jody Knapp explained the subject property is within the 5600 West overlay zone. She
                 said this proposal does include a drive-thru, and the overlay zone is subject to drive-
                 thru and fast food standard restrictions. She explained there would be three tenants
                 occupying the building. Jody stated the applicant would be restricting a bit of
                 landscaping for the pad and adding in a different location, but the site's perimeter is
                 fully landscaped. Jody explained the plans are showing three fast-food tenants with a
                 drive-thru and having the drive-thru does not allow for more fast food tenants than
                 other tenants. Jody has been working with the applicant extensively over the last
                 three months. She has let the applicant know and is waiting to hear back from them
                 about how they will address this issue. She said they need to comply with the code,
                 and she will add this information to the staff report.
                 Jody stated the building does meet the building relief standards; however, not the
                 design standards. Jody believes they will add some color change to meet the design
                 standard and follow the existing development agreement for the property. Jody
                 pointed out that usually, they stagger the rooftops to comply with the design
                 standards, and the applicant's plans are not showing that. She said if the Commission
                 would like the roof changed, she will let the applicant know. Commissioner
                 Woodruff said that he would have them adjust the roof to meet the ordinance.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:15 p.m.
