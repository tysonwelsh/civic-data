2)
Work Session regarding compensation for elected officials; David Warnock and Matt Leavitt (9:03 AM)
Manager Shayne Scott introduced the topic of elected official compensation. Council Members asked questions. Manager Scott, David Warnock, Personnel Manager, and Matt Leavitt, Financial Officer, responded. Clerk Eve Furse and Attorney Margaret Olson provided input.
3)
Work Session: Discussions regarding possible department budget appeals (9:26 AM)
1. Finance Department; Matt Leavitt (9:20 AM)
Matt Leavitt, Financial Officer, presented an appeal regarding the universal cuts to travel and training.
2. Treasurer; Corrie Forsling (9:34 AM)
Treasurer Corrie Forsling presented an appeal regarding funding for a property value website.
3. Public Lands; Jess Kirby (9:44 AM)
Jess Kirby, County Lands & Natural Resources, presented her appeal of the start date of the additional employee authorized.
4. Facilities; Mike Crystal (10:04 AM)
Mike Crystal, Facilities Director, explained the physical needs of the Coalville Courthouse and the cuts to overtime. Council Members asked questions to which Director Crystal responded. Attorney Margaret Olson provided input.
5. Fire Warden; Bryce Boyer and Kathryn McMullin (10:17 AM)
Kathryn McMullin, Emergency Manager, Bryce Boyer, Fire Warden, and Mike Erickson, Area Manager for Forestry, Fire, and State Lands, appealed the cut of the salary for the Junior Fire Warden. Council members asked questions. The presenters and Manager Scott responded.
6. Behavioral Health; Aaron Newman and Phil Bondurant (10:31 AM)
Director of Behavioral Health Aaron Newman presented the appeal of the cut to behavioral health funding. Health Director, Dr. Phil Bondurant, provided supporting details. Council Members asked questions to which Directors Newman and Bondurant responded.
7. Clerk; Eve Furse (10:40 AM)
Clerk Eve Furse presented an appeal of the delayed start date for the added position. Council Members asked questions. Clerk Furse responded.
5)
Senior and Childcare Funding. (11:01 AM)
Council Members discussed the requests for Senior and Childcare funding and set forth their preferences. Financial Officer Leavitt responded to questions.
6)
Closed Session (11:12 AM)
Roger Armstrong made a motion to enter closed session to discuss litigation. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Hanson, Harte, and Robinson, along with Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Attorney Margaret Olson, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, and Executive Assistant Annette Singleton met in closed session to discuss litigation.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to leave closed session (12:01 PM). Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
7)
Adjournment (12:01 PM)
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to adjourn. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
