Public Comment Presentation
Commissioner Carmichael recused himself from participating in the discussion of agenda item number 2.
Commissioner Carmichael made a motion to nominate Commissioner Hawley as acting chair to preside over the meeting for agenda item number 2. All voted in favor (5-0).
Madlyn McDonough,
Planner
, provided information on the proposed CUP for a Major Accessory Building on Old Ranch Road.
Commissioner Nagie asked a clarifying question about the 100-year event floodplain trigger and the floodplain permit process and requirements.
Engineer Brandon Brady responded to his concerns.
Commissioner Hawley asked a follow-up question regarding the pond on the property and its usefulness in flooding events to which Mr. Brady responded.
Commissioner Jeffrey asked procedural questions.
Commissioner Hawley opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:21 p.m.
Doug Ogilvy was called to speak.
Commissioner Hawley closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:27 p.m.
Lauren Bolger, attorney for the applicant, addressed the Commission and the issue of flooding. Commissioners Hawley and Jeffrey asked Attorney Bolger additional questions.
Lynda Viti,
Deputy Civil Attorney
, provided code to help clarify the concerns about conditioning the proposed conditional use.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to continue the item to a date uncertain.The motion did not receive a second.
Discussion was held on the motion.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to continue the item to a date certain of March 11, 2025. Tim Jeffrey seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Tyann Mooney. Recuse: Bruce Carmichael.
