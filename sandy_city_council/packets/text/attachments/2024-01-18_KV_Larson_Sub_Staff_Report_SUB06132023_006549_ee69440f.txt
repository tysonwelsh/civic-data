Request
 The applicant, Ron Larson, representing the property owner
 Verna V Larson Trust, is requesting preliminary subdivision
 review for the property at 963 E. 8600 S. The proposal
 consists of subdividing the property into eight (8) residential
 lots and dedicating a portion of land to create a public street.
 (see Exhibit “A”, application materials). The applicant is also
 requesting a special exception for the creation of a public
 street that is the standard width requirements and
 improvements on a portion of the proposed road.

Background
 The subject property consists of approximately 2.5 acres and
 is in the SD(R-1-7) Zone. The property contains an existing
 single family home and a few accessory buildings. The project
 is not within any overlay zones. The properties to the north
 and west are also zoned SD(R-1-7). Properties to the south are
 zoned R-1-8. Properties on the east that face onto 8600 South
 are zoned R-2-10. The remaining properties on the northeast
 are not within Sandy Cities jurisdiction. All adjoining uses are
 single family homes except for the northeast property which
 is a pump house owned by Salt Lake County Water
 Conservancy District.

Public Notice and Outreach
  Utah Code (Part 10-9a-2) requires that a notice of a public hearing be mailed and posted. Notices were mailed to property
  owners within 500 feet of the subject property and affected entities. The notice was posted to the Utah Public Notice website
  and the city website. A physical copy of the notice was posted at City Hall and a public notice sign was placed on the subject
  property (Exhibit “C”).

Neighborhood Meeting
  A neighborhood meeting was held on November 9, 2023, at 6:00 PM by the Planning Division. 4 people attended the meeting.
  Additionally, a neighborhood meeting concerning the rezone was held on January 30, 2023, at 7:00 PM. 11 people attended
  that meeting. Summaries of both meetings are attached (Exhibit “B”). In general, residents were in support of the project and
  the only wanted information on how negative impacts would be managed during construction.

Analysis
  The applicant is proposing the creation of an 8-lot single-family
  residential subdivision on approximately 2.5 acres. In 2023, the
  property was rezoned from R-1-8 to SD(R-1-7). This was done
  to allow for the development of additional lots on the north
  portion since the existing zoning would not provide the
  necessary widths to create these lots while also providing the
  necessary street improvements. The SD(R-1-7) Zone generally
  follows the regulations of the R-1-8 zone but allows for lots as
  small as 7,000 sq. ft. with an overall average of 7,500 sq. ft.

  In the mid 90’s, the Merewood Subdivisions 1 and 2 were
  recorded to the West and North of the subject property. These
  developments created stub street on either side for future
  connectivity. This project will connect 8560 South and 960 East
  and complete the road connectivity initially envisioned. As there
  is insufficient width on the north side of the property to provide
  the standard street improvements, the applicant is requesting a
  special exception to narrow the dedication from the standard 52-
  foot width to 42 feet. The standard five feet of sidewalk and five
  feet of parkstrip will be omitted on the east side of 960 East.
  Future development of the properties to the east would complete
  those omitted improvements and complete the sidewalk
  network.

  Salt Lake County Water Conservancy District holds an easement from 960 East to their pump house on the northeast corner
  of the development. The proposed street improvements will retain this access to their property and curb cuts will be
  provided to retain compliance with this easement.

  There is an existing home fronting onto 8600 South. The home will be retained, and staff has determined that the proposed
  property line changes will allow for compliance with the setback requirements of the SD(R-1-7) zoning district.

Staff Concerns
  The applicant has spoken with Salt Lake County Water Conservancy District about the requirements of maintaining their
  access easement to the north. The applicant must ensure that all requirements of the “Well-Site Agreement” are complied
  with during the construction of the project.

Staff Recommendation
Motion #1 Special Exception
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that a special exception for a less than full width public street
  is approved for the property located at 963 E. 8600 S. based on the following findings:

Findings:
    1. The precedence of adjoining improvements on 960 East envisioned a half-width street and splitting the requirements
        for road improvements between the properties. The proposal contains a full width pavement width.

    2. That the proposed configuration is an efficient use of the land with standard lot shapes.

    3. That the City Engineer and Director has recommended approval as it will be practicable to require the dedication and
       improvements to the other half when the adjoining property is developed upon reviewing a neighborhood master
       plan.

    4.     That the proposed street conforms with Fire Access Road requirements.

Motion #2, Subdivision Review
 Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that preliminary subdivision review for the KV Larson
 Subdivision located at 963 E. 8600 S. is substantially complete based on the following findings and subject to the following
 conditions:

               Department. That a majority of this roadway be built and dedicated to a 42-foot cross section with a waiver
               of parkstrip and sidewalk on the east side as shown in the proposed plans.

               proposed improvements that conforms to City requirements.

               and blowing soil/sand during construction.

       Agreement recorded against the property.
