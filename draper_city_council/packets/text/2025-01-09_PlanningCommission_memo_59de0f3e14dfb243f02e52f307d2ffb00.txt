Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 1.89 acres located along Draper Peak Cove, along the north side of 13400
South, at approximately 13367 S. Draper Peak Cove. The property is currently zoned RA2
(Residential Agricultural). The applicant is requesting to modify access easements for all
three lots within an approved subdivision. The purpose of the amendment is to
accommodate a proposed single family residence on the property at 13367 S. Draper
Peak Cove.



Findings for approval:

  1. There is good cause for the amendment, and no public street or municipal utility
     easement will be vacated or amended.
