Repeal of Storm Water Fee

             The Council reviewed the following Resolution repealing Title 17, Article VII of the
Copperton Metro Township Code Regarding stormwater facilities impact fees:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-06-02                                                       Date: June 19, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       REPEALING TITLE 17, ARTICLE VII OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP
       CODE REGARDING STORMWATER FACILITIES IMPACT FEES

      WHEREAS, Article VII of Title 17 of the Copperton Metro Township Code imposes a
stormwater facilities and impact fee to fund stormwater facilities; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County originally adopted Article VII prior to Copperton’s
incorporation and the provision became part of the municipal codes of Copperton and the other
four metro townships pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-2a-414(3), which specifies that each
County ordinance in effect on the day a metro township incorporates remains in effect as a metro
township ordinance until the metro township amends or repeals the ordinance; and

         WHEREAS, the stormwater fees are subject to a number of requirements and limitations,
including: (1) annual impact fee reports to the State Auditor; (2) provisions that require that
stormwater impact fees may only be used to reimburse a storm drain system improvement
identified in an impact fee facilities plan in the same drainage basin where the fee was collected;
and (3) regulations that require the reimbursement of a stormwater impact fee to a developer if
the fee is not used within six years of the date it was obtained from the developer; and

       WHEREAS, complying with these requirements entails significant administrative cost,
which are estimated to be 10% of the total fee collected, on average; and

        WHEREAS, on July 20, 2018, the Board of Trustees for the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (“MSD”) determined that the administrative costs associated with stormwater
impact fees were excessive when compared to value of the fees collected, and requested that
the individual metro townships repeal Article VII of Title 17 of their respective municipal codes;
and

        WHEREAS, to conform to the MSD Board’s determination, the Council desires to repeal
Article VII of Title 17 of the Copperton Metro Township Code.

    NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS, EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

       1.     Article VII of Title 17 of the Copperton Metro Township Code, consisting of
Sections 17.20.710 through 17.20.760, is repealed.

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 19 day of June 19, 2019.
