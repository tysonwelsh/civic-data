PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on July 13, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2022-31 An Ordinance amending Title 10 by adding a major corridor sign overlay zone to allow additional sign provisions for large commercial, manufacturing, and mixed-use sites and allowing planned center signs in the M-1 Manufacturing zone. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
