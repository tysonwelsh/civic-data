            1. DISCUSSION ON BRIDAL VEIL FALLS.
            - Bill Lee, Utah County Commissioner -

            MIKE PUGLIA AND JOSH SHIPLEY, RIVERS WEST – GAVE PRESENTATION.
            CONSULTED WITH SOME IMAGINERS INCLUDING SOME FROM DISNEY TO
            IMPROVE THE BRIDAL VEIL FALLS ATTRACTION AS WELL AS PROTECTING THE
            ENVIRONMENT AND NATURAL HABITAT AND ADDING IMPROVEMENTS TO THE
            TRAIL FOR SAFETY PRECAUTIONS. PRESENTED BUILDING HERITAGE AND
            HISTORICAL CENTERS SO THAT SCHOOL GROUPS CAN COME INTO THE AREA
            AND RECEIVE PRESENTATIONS ON THE HISTORY OF UTAH VALLEY. DID A
            SURVEY WITH THE PUBLIC.

            CURTIS MINER REPRESENTING RICHARD LOSEE – GAVE A HISTORY OF THE
            FALLS. WOULD LIKE TO RE-ESTABLISH THE TRAM. CONSULTED WITH FOREST
            SERVICE, HEALTH DEPT, BUILDING AND ZONING DEPARTMENT AS WELL AS
            UDOT, THERE WOULD BE A NUMBER OF TECHNICAL ISSUES TO BE WORKED
            OUT. PROPOSE TO PUTTING THE UPPER BUILDING WHERE IT WAS INITIALLY.
            THE LOWER BUILDING WOULD BE PUT NEAR THE UPPER PARKING LOT INSTEAD
            OF AT THE END OF THE RIVER. THE BOTTOM/BASE BUILDING WOULD
            PRIMARILY BE USED FOR TRAM OPERATIONS BUT COULD ALSO INCLUDE
            CONCESSIONS. THE TOP BUILDING WOULD BE FOR THE TRAM AS WELL AS A
            VIEWING AREA. ALSO WOULD HAVE A SEPARATE PART OF THE BUILDING FOR
            MR. LOSEE’S BUSINESS OF A DRUG & ALCOHOL REHABILITATION
            CENTER/SANCTUARY.
