5. Public hearing and possible action regarding a proposed Final Site Plan to expand the 
Basin Recreation Fieldhouse; 1388 Center Drive; Parcel NPRK-S-X; Rena Jordan on 
behalf of Snyderville Basin Recreation District, applicant – Amir Caus, County Planner  
 
Planner Amir Caus said the Newpark development was approved in 2001.  As part of that 
approval, 112,000 square feet was allocated for a recreational complex.  The existing field 
house was approved in two phases.  The first phase was 57,000 square feet.  The proposed 
expansion will be approximately 30,000 square feet.  The expansion will include:  
 A gymnasium with supporting facilities and office space  
 A pool with a warming tub 
 A stair enclosure on the north end of the field house.  One purpose is to enclose the 
stairway on the back portion of the building, which is used frequently by Staff.  This 
will help avoid a dangerous situation with the ice buildup.  This will also provide 
some additional storage.   
All development requirements, such as height and setbacks, are being met.  No additional 
parking spaces are being proposed.  Some slides were shown of the architectural renderings.  
A picture of the ice buildup was shown.  Staff recommended the Commission forward a 
positive recommendation to the Summit County Manager.

It is desired to combine these lots for the development of a transit hub.  This project was

is an existing area.  It will be non-conforming unless approval is granted.  Staff finds that the 
plat amendment will allow both parking areas to meet the minimum requirements for the 
setbacks.  If approved, the County will be able to move forward in building the transit hub.
