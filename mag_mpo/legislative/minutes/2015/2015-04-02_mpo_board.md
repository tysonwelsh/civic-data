Approval for the Draft TransPlan40 to Start Public Comment - Shawn Seager The draft TransPlan40 is the regional transportation plan for the next 25 years for Utah County. The regional transportation plan is updated every four year and staff collaborates with UDOT and UTA to develop a land use scenario, project lists, and funding estimates. The plan meets all federal requirements to be fiscally constrained and meets air quality conformity (depending on EPA decision). There are three phases: Phase 1 2015-2024, Phase 2 2025-2034, and Phase 3 2035-2040. There are 80 highway projects totaling $3.5b. The plan has $1.1b in 13 new transit capital projects. All highway and transit projects have been modeled in the phase they are needed. Trail/Bike/Ped project are not phased. The plan goals: enhance safety and health; preserve infrastructure, provide mobility and accessibility, achieve cost efficiency, promote economic vitality, and preserve our quality of life. Public Comment period will start on April 5th and run to May 4th, there will be a meeting for the public on April 29th, 4:00-6:00 pm, in the MAG Conference Room. Commissioner Larry Ellertson moved to approve the release of the Draft TransPlan40 for 30-day Public Comment Period starting April 5, 2014 to May 4, 2015, based on the MPO staff and MPO Technical Advisory Committee recommendations. Commissioner Kent Millington seconded the motion. The carried unanimously. Draft Conformity Determination Document for Public Comment - Susan Hardy All projects included in the 2040 regional transportation plan were modeled to determine their impacts on air quality. The plan is fiscally constrained – containing only projects that are with an identified funding source. In 2005, the State introduced a Trading Rule (R370 – 110) that allows the WFRC MPO to apply potential surplus in its budget for Primary PM10, to a potential short fall in its budget for NOX at a ratio of one-to-one. MAG has requested the State to expand this existing rule to Utah County as well. The new Rule addressing specifically Utah County - R370 – 111 was incorporated into the state code and became effective March 5, 2015. The State submitted this new rule with the appropriate technical support document to EPA’s Region 8 for their concurrence and publication in the Federal Register.
