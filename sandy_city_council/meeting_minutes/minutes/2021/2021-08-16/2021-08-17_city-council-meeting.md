1.         21-311                 Public Utilities presenting a 2021 Drought and Water Conservation 
                                  Update.
                   Attachments:   Memo - City Council RE Water Conservation
                                  2021 Drought and Water Conservation Plan update - Sandy Public 
                                  Utilities 08062021 FINAL
                                  Fact Sheet - Water Conservation 2021

                                  Tom Ward, Public Utilities Director, presented an update on the 2021 drought conditions 
                                  and provided data on water usage and conservation efforts. He thanked the Council for 
                                  their interest and support of water conservation efforts and he introduced public utilities 
                                  staff members. To date, Sandy City has saved over a half billion gallons of water over the 
                                  past year. This is the driest year on record. Mr. Ward provided an overview and history of 
                                  water use in Sandy and provided information related to water usage by connection type: 
                                  commercial, residential, parks, institutional. He introduced the Water Conservation Plan 
                                  with the Council and reviewed the submittal process. The plan is required to be updated 
                                  and submitted to the State every five years. Keith Larson with Bowen, Collins, reviewed 
                                  the Water Conservation Plan with the Council and provided information on water use 
                                  trends and historic and future conservation goals. Mr. Larson reviewed the proposed 
                                  conservation practices and timeline for implementation. Abi Holt provided information on 
                                  the local scapes rebate program. Council questions and comments followed.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Christensen spoke about the recent shootings in Sandy City. She 
                    thanked the police department staff for their efforts. She spoke about the neighborhood 
                    meeting held in June with Chief Severson. There is a follow-up meeting with the police 
                    chief on August 23rd. The police will present their plan and approach to handling crime in 
                    the area. She mentioned that two neighborhood watch groups have been formed and she 
                    encouraged the community to get involved in their neighborhoods. Please call Amy 
                    Bryant in the Police Department if you are interested in starting a neighborhood watch 
                    group. Alert neighbors help to protect our neighborhoods and she encouraged residents 
                    to report any suspicious activity to the police.

                    Council Member Zoltanski echoed those comments from constituents. When one 
                    neighborhood is hurting, all neighborhoods are hurting. The community is alarmed and 
                    upset at events. She asked Chief to provide information to the community and let them 
                    know what they should watch for or be alert to.

                    Chief Severson spoke about the recent shootings in Sandy - which are active, ongoing 
                    investigations. He urged residents to call the police and report any unusual activities. You 
                    will always receive a professional and prompt response from the Sandy Police 
                    Department. He encouraged residents to come to the meeting on August 23rd at 7:00 pm 
                    in Historic Sandy.

                    Council Member Houseman shared a post by Judy Allred who posted on Next Door 
                    asking people to list all the positive things in our community. There were many responses 
                    to her post which included: Hale Centre Theatre, happy smiles, the Healing Fields. Ms. 
                    Houseman invited the public to attend the Community Connectedness Workshop on 
                    August 25th at the Senior Center from 6:30 pm to 8:00 pm. Alison Stroud and Zach 
                    Robinson will also be in attendance to facilitate the workshop. She also thanked the 
                    Parks and Rec department for staging the Balloon Festival and Glow event.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud also invited the community to attend the upcoming 
                    workshop and help us define what connects a community. She also spoke about the 
                    Community Arts production of Freaky Friday - a wonderful performance. 

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Bradburn gave an update on Covid. There has been an uptick both locally and on a 
                    national level. He let the Council know that the city will be issuing new recommendations 
                    for employees and visitors to city facilities.

CAO Report

                    Matt Huish, CAO, thanked the Parks staff for holding the Balloon event. He spoke about 
                    the candidate filing deadline. Currently, we have thirteen candidates running for Mayor.

                    Chief Cline spoke to the Council about the fires in California. His department has sent 
                    three firefighters and our wildland truck to California to assist the fire departments out 
                    there. The City is reimbursed for all costs associated with this. He also spoke about the 
                    recent fire in Park City. Sandy City fire fighters also helped to fight that fire.

10.        21-303                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment:

                                  Ms. Dawn Sidwell commented on the recent shootings that occurred in Historic Sandy. 
                                  She spoke about a recent neighborhood meeting with the police chief but wanted 
                                  additional information on what the city was doing to address these issues. The Council 
                                  made her aware of an upcoming neighborhood meeting on August 23rd. Ms. Sidwell also 
                                  spoke about a code enforcement issue and asked for additional information about the 
                                  ordinances related to the number of cars allowed to be parked at a home and suggested 
                                  that changes in the ordinance may be needed. She wants Sandy City codes enforced 
                                  which will help make for a better community.

                                  Ms. Cathy Spuck expressed concerns with crime in Historic Sandy. She appreciated the 
                                  recent neighborhood meeting organized by Brooke Christensen and the police 
                                  department. She thinks it would be helpful to get more information about the police 
                                  response so that she can share that information with her neighbors. She is concerned 
                                  that people will disconnect with neighbors and move in response to all the crime in the 
                                  area. There are a lot of scared people in the community. Ms. Spuck thanked the 
                                  Council/City for all they do and appreciates their efforts. 

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren commented on the Consent Agenda Item #2. He believes that Item 
                                  2 is out of order due to the lack of noticing requirements as required by state law. He 
                                  checked the City Recorder's site and could not find the item posted there. 

                                  Ms. Jennifer Baker submitted her comment online. She expressed concerns with the 
                                  recent shootings in Historic Sandy. She asked why the Sandy City Police Department 
                                  was not involved with the Salt Lake City metro gang unit. 

                                  Public Comment closed.
