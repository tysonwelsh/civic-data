    2. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Amending Sections 17.03 And 17.07
       of the South Salt Lake City Municipal Code to Correct Technical Errors, Amend Certain
       Townhome Development and Design Standards.
       Deputy Community Development Director, Eliza Ungricht, provided some background
       information on this item to provide some clarity in response to some of the resident’s
       comments earlier in the meeting during the ‘Citizen’s Comments/Questions’ portion.

        She said that the changes would affect Section 17.03 which is the Land Use Districts and
        Matrix, and Section 17.07 which is the Design Standards for the Townhome Overlay.
        This was all approved a couple of years ago when the Townhome Overlay was created
                                               3
South Salt Lake- Regular Council Meeting                                        July 26, 2023


        so that townhome development projects would require City Council approval for
        building permits. This Ordinance isn’t to approve any new townhome project but is
        geared towards making some changes in the standards that the City has in its Code.

        Some of the changes include allowing the frontage road access on one street to be 128
        feet and another amount that would be determined with every project on the
        secondary road, allowing two buildings per development to have a lower number of
        units required, lowering the percentage of windows required, and lowering the
        percentage of dwelling units having vehicle access to the secondary roads from 90% to
        85%.

        The Planning Commission has also reviewed these proposed changes and has made a
        positive recommendation to the Council.

        Council Member Pinkney made a motion to approve the Ordinance.
