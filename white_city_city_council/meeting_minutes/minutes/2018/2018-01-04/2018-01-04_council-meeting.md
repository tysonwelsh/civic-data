               Council Member Dickerson, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to adopt
the following ordinance Creating Chapter 3.15 – Purchasing, Contracting, and Procurement and
White City Policy #2 – Purchasing, Contracting, and Procurement:

ORDINANCE NO. 18-01-01                                                 Date: January 4, 2018

       AN   ORDINANCE    CREATING CHAPTER  3.15   –  PURCHASING,
       CONTRACTING AND PROCUREMENT OF THE WHITE CITY CODE OF
       ORDINANCES, AND WHITE CITY POLICY #2 – PURCHASING,
       CONTRACTING, AND PROCUREMENT TO ESTABLISH THE PURCHASING,
       CONTRACTING AND PROCUREMENT ORDINANCE AND POLICY FOR THE
       WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP

                                         RECITALS

       A.      The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Metro Township form of
municipality pursuant to Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

      B.      The White City Metro Township Council (“Council”) is the Council for White City
pursuant to UCA § 10-2a-501 et al.

       C.     The County Auditor (“Auditor”), County Clerk (“Clerk”), and County Treasurer
(“Treasurer”) are the Auditor, Clerk/Recorder, and Treasurer respectively for White City
pursuant to UCA § 10-3c-203 et al.

       D.      Pursuant to UCA Title 63G Chapter 6a – Utah Procurement Code, White City is
required to adopt a policy to guide the purchasing of goods and services and the processes for
the contracting and procurement of other professional services.

       E.      UCA § 63G-6a-108, et al, allows a municipality to adopt any of all sections of
UCA Title 63G, Chapter 6a to direct the purchasing, contracting and procurement of goods and
services via resolution and/or ordinance.

       F.     In adopting a purchasing, contracting and procurement ordinance and policy, the
Council desires to promote an efficient, effective, transparent and equitable process for the
purchasing goods and services and contracting for other professional services.

                                       RESOLUTION

              BE IT HEREBY ORDAINED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby
approves and adopts the White City Metro Township Code Annotated Chapter 3.15 –
PURCHASING, CONTRACTING AND PROCUREMENT, and White City Metro Township
POLICY #2 – PURCHASING, CONTRACTING, AND PROCUREMENT, known hereafter as
Attachment “A” and Attachment “B,” respectively.

Master Interlocal Agreement

             The Council reviewed the following resolution by and between Salt Lake County,
the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District, The White City Metro Township and other
Metro Townships for the payment, operation and provision of certain municipal-type services.

RESOLUTION NO. 18-01-02                                               Date: January 4, 2018

       A   RESOLUTION   APPROVING   AN    INTERLOCAL   COOPERATION
       AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN SALT LAKE COUNTY, THE GREATER
       SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT, THE WHITE CITY METRO
       TOWNSHIP AND OTHER METRO TOWNSHIPS FOR THE PAYMENT,
       OPERATION AND PROVISION OF CERTAIN MUNICIPAL-TYPE SERVICES

                                        RECITALS

      A.    The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a Metro Township pursuant to
Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

         B.    The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal
legislative body for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

                                             6
DATE            THURSDAY                             JANUARY                               4, 2018

       C.      Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant UCA Title 17- COUNTIES.

       D.     The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “District”) is a local district
that provides for the delivery of certain municipal-type services to the White City Metro
Township pursuant to UCA 17B-2a-1101 et seq.

         E.    The County, District, White City and other Metro Townships worked together to
create a Master Interlocal Agreement (“Master Agreement”) by and between the County, the
District, White City and other Metro Townships for the operation and delivery of certain
municipal-type services, hereinafter known as Attachment “A” to this resolution.

        F.     The District approved and adopted the Master Agreement on December 13, 2017
during their regular meeting of the Board of Trustees, and the County and Metro Townships,
including White City, are reviewing and will adopt the Master Agreement in 2018.

      G.      With final adoption by all parties, the Master Agreement provides a good
framework for the operation and delivery of municipal-type services for the residents of White
City.

                                          RESOLUTION

               THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council
hereby approves this resolution and authorizes the Chair to sign on behalf of White City, the
Master Interlocal Agreement – Attachment “A” for the benefit of the residents of White City,
which Master Agreement will take effect upon the agreement to the same by all parties thereto.
