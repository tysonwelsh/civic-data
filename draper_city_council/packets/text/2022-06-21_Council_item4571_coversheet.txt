Re:  Comments: Vacating a right-of-way easement, located south of 12300 South on a parcel known as the Casdorph parcel.  A right-of-way was recorded on a warranty deed on June 28, 1989.  The owner is now requesting this easement, known as Easement #1, be vacated.  The city has no planned public right-of-way for this location and it is not included on the Draper City Master Transportation Plan.   The findings are as follows: That the public interest nor any person will be materially injured by the vacation and there is good cause for the vacation.
