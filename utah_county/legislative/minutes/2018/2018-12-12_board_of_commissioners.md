COMMISSIONER IVIE WELCOMED ALL PARTICIPANTS AND THANKED THE COMMISSIONERS
LEE AND GRAVES FOR THE OPPORTUNITY TO CONTINUE THIS. HE HOPES TO HAVE A
GREATER UNDERSTANDING OF THE PROPERTY TAX ON THE CITIZENS OF THIS COUNTY AND
HAVE THE CITIZENS UNDERSTAND WHY THE COMMISSION VOTED TO GO THROUGH THIS
PROCESS OF TRUTH AND TAXATION. EXPLAINED HOW TRUTH AND TAXATION WORKS. IN
THE 80’S A WISE BILL WAS PASSED TO FORCE PEOPLE TO LIVE ON A FIXED INCOME. THE
STATE WOULD HAVE A CERTIFIED TAX RATE THAT WOULD ADJUST TO THE PROPERTY
VALUES SO THAT GOVERNMENT ESENTIALLY LIVED ON A FIXED INCOME. ANY TIME
GOVERNMENT FELT THE NEED TO GARNER MORE TAX THEY WOULD HAVE TO GO THROUGH
A PROCESS CALLED TRUTH AND TAXATION. THIS YEAR WE ARE ASKING TO INCREASE THAT
RATE SO THAT WE WILL HAVE MORE FUNDS. EXPLAINED THE BUDGETING PROCESS. THE
CLERK AUDITOR PRODUCES A TENTATIVE BALANCED BUDGET. THEN THE OTHER
DEPARTMENT HEADS AND OTHER ELECTED OFFICIALS HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY TO COME
TO THE COMMISSION AND PRESENT THE NEEDS THEY HAVE THAT BALANCED BUDGET. THE
COMMISSION THEN DETERMINES IF THEY ARE TRULY NECESSARY. THE COMMISSION, IN
PUBLIC MEETINGS, ASKS THE DEPARTMENT HEADS TO RANK THE NEED OF REQUESTED
ITEMS. THIS YEAR THERE WAS ABOUT 12 TO 13 MILLION IN ASKS AND WE WERE ABLE TO CUT
THAT DOWN TO 7 TO 8 MILLION IN NEEDS. IT 1S IMPORTANT TO KNOW THAT UTAH COUNTY
AS A TAXING ENTITY SPENDS LESS PER CAPITA THAN ANY OTHER URBAN COUNTY IN THE
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA. ALL ELECTED OFFICIALS ARE WORKING VERY HARD TO KEEP
UTAH COUNTY GOVERNMENT SMALL, EFFICIENT AND OPERATING APPROPRIATELY. BUT WE
ARE FACING INCREASED DEMANDS BECAUSE OF RAPID GROWTH IN THE COUNTY. WE
CURRENTLY HAVE ABOUT 16 MILLION DOLLARS IN UNINCUMBERED RESERVES, THAT STATE
RECOMENDS THAT A GOOD HEALTHY GOVERNMENT KEEPS FROM 16 TO 25% IN THAT
INUNCUMBERED ACCOUNT. THOSE CURRENT FUNDS WE HAVE ARE REALOCATED TO
MAINTAING THE COUNTY NEEDS SO THAT WE DO NOT HAVE TO KEEP INCREASING TAXES.
WE HAVE BEEN ABLE REPLENISH THAT FUND AND KEEP IN BALANCE. IF WE ARE TO KEEP TO
OUR BUDGET WITH ALL THE NECESSARY ASKS, THAT 16 MILLION WILL DIP BELOW THE
REQUIRED PERCENTAGE. THAT IS WHY WE ARE GOING THROUGH THE TRUTH IN TAXATION
PROCESS. LARGE GROWTH IN THE COUNTY REQUIRES THE COUNTY TO HAVE INCREASED
EXPENSES THAT NEED TO BE COVERED TO MAINTAIN THE COUNTY ESENTIAL SERVICES. THE
COUNTY IS VERY WISE ON HOW WE DEVELOP. WE KEEP DEVELOPMENT IN THE CITIES SO
THEY PROVIDE SERVICES LIKE SEWER AND WATER. AS A COUNTY WE MAKE SURE THAT
CRIMINALS ARE IN A JAIL AND THAT WE HAVE A HEALTHY SHERIFF DEPARTMENT THAT
PATROLS. WE HAVE A HEALTHY ATTORNEYS DIVISION WHICH IS CONSTITUTIONALY
MANDATED TO PROVIDE A GOOD DEFENSE. WE HAVE MADE SURE THAT RECORDERS AND
ASSESSORS OFFICE ARE PROPERLY STAFFED SO THAT WE CAN KEEP ACCURATE RECORDS
PROPERLY ASSESS YOUR PROPERTIES WITH SKILLED PEOPLE. OUR CLERK AND ELECTIONS
DIVISIONS CAN RUN APPROPRIATLY. ELECTIONS IS A GREAT EXAMPLE OF AN AREA THAT
