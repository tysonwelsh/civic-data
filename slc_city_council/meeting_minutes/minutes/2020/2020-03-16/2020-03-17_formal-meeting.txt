MINUTES OF THE SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL
 TUESDAY, MARCH 17, 2020
 The City Council of Salt Lake City, Utah, met in Special Limited Formal
 Session on Tuesday, March 17, 2020 in Room 326, Committee Room, City
 County Building, 451 South State.
 The following Council Members were present (electronically) :
 Daniel Dugan Analia Valdemoros Chris Wharton
 Andrew Johnston Amy Fowler Darin Mano
 James Rogers
 Cindy Gust-Jenson, Council Executive Director (electronically) ;
 Jennifer Bruno, Council Executive Deputy Director (electronically) ;
 Rachel Otto, Mayor' s Chief of Staff; Cindy Lou Trishman, Council
 Administrative Assistant; Katherine Lewis, City Attorney; Lehua Weaver,
 Council Associate Deputy Director (electronically) ; and Kory Solorio,
 Assistant City Recorder; were present.
 The meeting was called to order at 7 : 03 p.m.
 OPENING CEREMONY 7:03:36 PM
 Councilmember Wharton presided at and conducted the meeting.
 CONSENT 7:04:48 PM
 Councilmember Rogers moved and Councilmember Fowler seconded to
 adopt the Consent Agenda excluding Item No. 4 . (Fern Sub-division Alley
 Vacation) , which motion carried, all members voted aye (roll call vote) .
 #1. Setting the date of Tuesday, April 7, 2020 at 7 : 00 p.m. to
 accept public comment on Budget Amendment No. 4 for Fiscal Year 2019-
 2020. Budget amendments happen several times each year to reflect
 adjustments to the City's budgets, including proposed project additions
 and modifications. The proposed amendment includes funding to mitigate
 impacts related to the ongoing COVID-19 outbreak, such as loans to small
 businesses and paid sick leave, among many other items. (Budget Amendment
 No. 4) View Attachments
 (B 20-5)
 #2 . Setting the date of Tuesday, March 24, 2020 at 7 : 00 p.m. to
 accept public comment and consider adopting an ordinance amending the
 final budget of Salt Lake City, including the employment staffing
 document, for Fiscal Year 2019-2020 . Budget amendments happen several
 times each year to reflect adjustments to the City's budgets, including
 proposed project additions and modifications. The proposed amendment
 includes funding for the purchase of real property. (Budget Amendment
 No. 5) View Attachments
 (B 20-4)

 #3 . Setting the date of Tuesday, April 7, 2020 at 7 : 00 p.m. to
 accept public comment and consider adopting an ordinance that would amend
 the Central Community Master Plan Future Land Use Map and the zoning map
 pertaining to a property at 1430 South 400 East. The rezone and
 amendments would allow the applicant to build a seven-unit development.
 The proposal would change the Central Community Master Plan Future Land
 Use Map from Low Density Residential to Medium Density Residential and
 rezone the property from RMF-35 (Moderate Density Multi-Family
 Residential District) to FB-UN1 (Form Based Urban Neighborhood
 District) . Although the applicant has requested that the property be
 rezoned to the FB-UN1 district, consideration may be given to rezoning
 the property to another zoning district with similar characteristics.
 Petition Nos. PLNPCM2019-00189 & PLNPCM2019-00190. View Attachments
 (P 20-10)
 #4 . EXCLUDED Setting the date of Tuesday, April 21, 2020 at 7 : 00
 p.m. to accept public comment and consider adopting an ordinance to
 vacate a City-owned alley known as the Fern Subdivision Alley located
 between 1000 East and 1100 East and between Wood Avenue and Logan Avenue.
 The east-west portion of the alley runs behind eight homes between 1019
 East and 1053 East Logan Avenue. The applicant originally wanted to
 vacate just the alley segment adjacent to her property, but during
 departmental review vacation of the entire alley was recommended due to
 lack of use and multiple encroachments into the alley. Petition No.
 PLNPCM2018-0046. View Attachments
 (P 20-9)
