3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code Amendment to Table 37.080 and Chapter 16 PRD updating the parking requirements
for multi-family projects.
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Everett was concerned about making policy on limited data. Commissioner
Jackson asked Sierra to clarify the sampling pool and how the data was collected. Sierra stated
the data was collected through Engage Lehi, social media, Lehi City Police Department and Lehi
City Streets Department. Kim Struthers stated this was meant for a general basis of policy.

Commissioner Jackson opened the public hearing.

Jeff Stephenson asked if any of the units are vacant because tandem parking spots are meant to
help fit a market segment and eliminating the least expensive unit that the parking allows. He
stated it could be borderline discriminatory to implement the new code. He was opposed to the
amendment.

Commissioner Jackson closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Bunker stated he agrees we need a variance of parking, but he knows every
community is dealing with the same issue of not having enough parking. He trusts in Kim
Struthers and the City departments comments and knowledge. He said this is a constant
complaint that there isn't enough parking, and we need to find a good balance. Commissioner
Newall is concerned if we are doing enough and projecting for the future to live in Lehi. She
stated a lot of the data included single family homes/residents. Commissioner Britton wanted to
echo what Commissioner Newall's comments. She stated that no matter what was built as
affordable, you should be able to park near your residence. Commissioner Everett stated there
should be stalls for single parent families, but it the rooms that are being rented out that are not

being accounted for parking. He had a comment on language for guest stalls and would like that
to be consistent in paragraph M.

Commissioner Jackson would like to re-open the public hearing.

Jeff Stephenson with DR Horton would like to clarify his comment on public parking and
tandem parking and affordable housing. Disallowing tandem parking behind the garage is a
concern for product type and it should be counted as a parking spot. There is also a bedroom
count toward parking.

Commissioner Jackson closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Jackson wanted to suggest Trevor conduct more research toward the parking
requirement code before any decision is made. Commissioner Bunker, Everett, and Newall
would like to move forward with a motion.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of a Development Code Amendment to Table 37.080 and Chapter 16
        PRD updating the parking requirements for multi-family projects; remove guest
        parking in paragraph “M” to remain consistent with the code, specifically changing 1 to
        every 6 parking stalls to 1 to every 3 parking stalls; including all DRC comments and
        findings stated in the report that these changes will and should improve parking in the
        City, and is in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the General Plan
        and its various elements. Noting the recommendation that Staff research distances for
        parking and best practices for amendments in the future. Commissioner Newall seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Jackson; yes; Commissioner Britton, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3.3) Discussion of the Skye Area Plan.
Sierra Pierson turned the presentation over to DR Horton.

Jeff Stephenson with DR Horton had a few notes he wanted to review with the Commissioners.
The report had a large traffic study and Geotech study included in the report. The area plan
amendment will be coming to the Commissioners on August 4, 2022, with the second area plan
amendment coming back within 4 - 6 months. DR Horton is working with UDOT and Lehi City
to make sure traffic and trails are being taken care of. They currently have several applications
submitted to the City. Howard Cook with Gardner Company was also present to represent the
commercial aspect of the project. Jeff presented on a timeline buildout for Viewpoint because of
the number kids that cross the street, mixed use, parking lots, commercial use, trails through
Texas Instruments and connectivity, City owned trails and HOA trails, open space and
landscaping, and the technical manufacturing buffer between Texas Instruments. DR Horton has
met with TI and they would like to do solar panels, and will allow some ancillary uses. There is
an 80/20 variance of storage per previous development agreement.
