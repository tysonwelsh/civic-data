Unified Fire Authority (UFA)/Introduction of Sandy City Fire Chief Cline

              Captain Ken Aldridge stated several promotions have been made throughout
UFA. He would like to work out a pancake breakfast during the Southeast Township Days. He
introduced Chief Bruce Cline of Sandy City Fire.

                Chief Cline, Sandy City Fire, stated he plans to come quarterly to update the
Council on Sandy City Fire issues and will provide detailed statistics to UFA to report on monthly.
Sandy City Fire provides fire prevention and safety training to the elementary schools around the
White City area. They have smoke detectors that the public can get, or the resident can call the
fire station and they will come out and install the smoke detector for free. They do free home
inspections and help individuals get tools to help avoid falls and other accidents. Next month a
packet that contain a substance to destroy medications will be available to the public. He would

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Chief Randy Thomas stated there has been a significant increase in transients.
He will provide the Council with quarterly reports on the precinct’s expenses. The church shooting
in Texas was a good example of the community being vigilant. He has spoken to a couple of
churches on how to handle a potential shooting.

              Officer Paula Stinson reviewed the statistics for December. There were only two
package thefts. There were several BB gun related vandalisms. UPD has two take back events
where the public can drop off their prescriptions.

White City Metro Township Council Member Reports

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Chief Randy Thomas updated the Council on the UPD and SLVLESA Board
meetings. The CFO presented all the budget adjustments and revenues that came back in. VECC
is having a hard time keeping all the channels open and working because of the low employment.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Perry stated the interlocal agreement was approved last month.
The Council submitted a letter of support to help UFA and UFSA apply for seismic grants. The
municipal emergency managers were introduced. Chief Peterson is addressing Tier II retirement,
distracted driving, and funding for fire academies at the legislature this year. UFSA entered into
a temporary agreement to allow Brighton Town to use its fire station to hold its meetings.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Price stated last month the 2020 budget was adopted and the
capital funds balance was decreased. It reviewed the policy on tuition reimbursement and decided
to provide $7,000 to an employee; however, the employee must stay for two years or pay the
tuition money back. Last year was a great year for safety.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

              Mayor Flint stated the GSLMSD almost has a complete compliment of employees.
Three additional employees will start in February. All assets have been transferred to the district.
The GSLMSD will be asking to transfer all of the Salt Lake County Engineers.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

               Council Member Dickerson stated the budget was reviewed and there will not be
a fee increase for 2020. WFWRD is encouraging residents to remove the garbage and recycling
cans from the curb on the day of service.

Staff or Committee Reports

Legislative Update

               Paul Ashton stated a meeting is being set up with the League of City and Towns.
A fact sheet has been created showing the evolution of the metro townships.

                Mayor Flint asked what the impact of the tax bill will be.

               Mr. Ashton stated the bill would allow a sales tax increase on food. The legislature
decided not to impose taxes on certain services. The public may pass a referendum to put the tax
increase on the ballot. He will also be working on moving the election of the mayor to be at-large.
Residents would be voting for the mayor position.
