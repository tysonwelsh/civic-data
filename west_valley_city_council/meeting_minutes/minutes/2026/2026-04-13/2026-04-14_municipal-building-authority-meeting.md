  APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF THE ANNUAL MEETING HELD JANUARY 13, 2026
  The Agency considered Minutes of the Annual Meeting held January 13, 2026. There were no
  changes, corrections or deletions.

  Mr. Huynh moved to approve the Minutes of the Annual Meeting held January 13, 2026. Ms. Lang
  seconded the motion.

  A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

  PUBLIC HEARINGS
  A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2025-2026
       BUDGET
       Chairperson Nordfelt informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
       Municipal Building Authority Meeting scheduled April 14, 2026, in order for to hear and
       consider public comments regarding Re-Opening the FY 2025-2026 Budget.

         Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
         follows:

                State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended, allows the
                City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The West Valley City
                Municipal Building Authority holds, as needed on a quarterly basis, public hearings
                to amend the annual budget.

         Chairperson Nordfelt opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor or
         opposition, Chairperson Nordfelt closed the Public Hearing.

         ACTION: RESOLUTION NO. 26-01, AMEND THE BUDGET OF THE WEST
         VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR
         BEGINNING JULY 1, 2025 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2026 TO REFLECT CHANGES IN
         THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED REVENUES AND AUTHORIZE THE
         DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
         The Board previously held a public hearing regarding proposed Resolution 26-01 that would
         amend the Budget of the West Valley City Municipal Building Authority for the Fiscal Year
         Beginning July 1, 2025 and Ending June 30, 2026 to Reflect Changes in the Budget from
         Increased Revenues and Authorize the Disbursement of Funds.

         Upon inquiry by Chairman Nordfelt there were no further questions from members of the
         Agency, and he called for a motion.

                Ms. Lang moved to approve Resolution 26-01.
