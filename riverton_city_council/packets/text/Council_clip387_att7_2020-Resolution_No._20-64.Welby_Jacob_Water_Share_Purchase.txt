                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 20-64

       A RESOLUTION RATIFYING THE PURCHASE OF 114 WELBY JACOB
                           WATER SHARES

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Water Director would like to purchase these Welby Jacob Water
Shares; and

       WHEREAS, a decision to purchase the shares had to be made prior to the next council
meeting or the offer would be made to a neighboring city; and
       WHEREAS, it was decided to purchase the 114 Welby Jacob Water Shares; and

       WHEREAS, the purchase has been made.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council ratifies the purchase of 114 Welby Jacob Water Shares from
          Gunner Garside.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 16th day of
October 2020 by the following vote:
