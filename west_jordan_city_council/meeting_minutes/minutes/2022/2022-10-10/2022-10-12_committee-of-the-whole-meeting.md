       b. Discussion pertaining to the contract between the City of West Jordan and Salt Lake
          City for the West Jordan Soccer Complex

          Work Session Recessed at 6:48pm

          11:15 pm Council reconvened the COTW

          Vice Chair Kelvin Green moved to suspend Council Rules to take agenda items out
          of order and begin with the Soccer Complex discussion prior to going into Closed
          Session. Council Member Chris McConnehey seconded the motion. All voted in favor
          and the motion passed unanimously.

          Council Member McConnehey oriented the Council regarding the contract for use of the
          West Jordan Soccer Complex. He displayed a map of the area pointing out the runway. He
          provided a brief history of the soccer park. The city pays $1/year for its use. In 2012, the
          Federal Aviation Agency performed an audit, and it was determined that fair market rates
          should be charged moving forward. A new lease for the complex will be amenable but at
          fair market rate. Options for how to proceed were outlined, including giving up the
          property lease, potentially expanding the runway zone, and considering other areas for
          use. He noted that there would be an open house for further discussion on Tuesday,
          October 18, from 6-8 pm and encouraged Council Members to participate.
