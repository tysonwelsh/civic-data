 2. Business Items
        a. Accept the Proposed Budget for the Municipal Building Authority for Fiscal Year
           Ending June 30, 2027
           Budget Analyst Becky Condie presented the proposed Fiscal Year 2027 budget for the
           Municipal Building Authority (MBA), explaining that the MBA exists solely to manage debt
           service for the Public Works building. Lease payments from the General Fund and utility
           funds are used to pay the annual bond debt service associated with the approximately $25
           million bond issued in 2016 for the facility.

           Ms. Condie reviewed historical bond information, noting the 23-year bond term, 5% interest
           rate, and annual debt payments of approximately $1.85 million. She explained that reserve
           funds would remain largely unchanged and would be held to make the final bond payment
           when the debt matures in 2039.

            MOTION: Board Member Jacob moved to Accept the Budget for MBA FY2027.
                    Board Member Lamb seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
            YES:        Bob Bedore, Annette Harris, Zach Jacob, Chad Lamb, Kent Shelton, Jessica
                        Wignall
            NO:
            ABSENT: Kayleen Whitelock
           The motion Passed 6-0.
