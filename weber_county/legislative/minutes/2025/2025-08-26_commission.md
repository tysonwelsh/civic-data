                   B. BOARD OF CANVASSERS- CERTIFY THE RESULTS OF THE 2025 PRIMARY
                      ELECTION.

                          Lauren Shafer (Elections Director): Only Ogden Valley City will be canvassed today as the other
                          municipalities canvassed their elections. There were 4,895 registered voters with 2,983 ballots counted
                          and only 31 rejected; this resulted in a 60.9% turnout which is high for a municipal election. 16
                          provisional ballots were cast with two rejections due to the form not being completed. 5,197 ballots
                  were mailed to voters and 3,014 were cast, which accounts for the 31 rejected. There were 2,183
                  ballots not returned and 166 were undeliverable ballots. Of the rejected ballots, 25 were returned past
                  the deadline, two had signatures not matching what was on records, one that was unsigned, and one
                  that was signed by someone other than the voter.
