DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicants, Bill and Carol Ann Whitworth, are requesting preliminary subdivision review for
a three-lot single-family subdivision. The 1.5 acre property is proposed to be split so that each of
the three lots will be approximately ½ acre in size and front onto Alla Panna Way. The applicants
are not requesting any overlays in association with this application, but they are requesting one
special exception to the City’s standard requirement that a parkstrip and sidewalk be installed on
their side of the street. All provisions of the base zone will be adhered to through the review and
approval process.

BACKGROUND & SITE CONDITIONS
The subject property is located in the R-1-20A zone and fronts onto Alla Panna Way to the
South. All of the properties surrounding the subject property are in the same R-1-20A zone,
including across the street on Alla Panna Way.
Montana Ranchos No. 6 Sub – SUB-09-20-5905 & SPEX-09-20-5918                              Page - 2 -


NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel as per
Sandy City Land Development Code requirements, to notify them of the Planning Commission
meeting. Additionally, a neighborhood meeting was held for this development on Monday,
September 21. 15 residents attended the meeting. The primary concerns were whether these
would be single family homes; the height of the homes; and whether these home were being built
to be rented out, or if the homeowners themselves would occupy the homes.

SUBDIVISION ANALYSIS
The R-1-20A zone is a traditional single-family zone. All standard requirements (setbacks,
building height, lot size, etc.) of the zone are typical when compared to other similar zones. The
applicants will be required to dedicate one foot of property along Alla Panna Way to the City so
that we have a half-width of 26 feet. This will allow the City, should it choose to at some point in
the future, to install its typical right-of-way cross section which would include a five-foot
parkstrip and five-foot sidewalk on either side of the road.

The home and detached garage that were on the site have already been demolished, so all three
newly-created lots will be buildable. All City departments have given preliminary approval of
the project.

SPECIAL EXCEPTION

Exception Request: Waiver to the requirement for parkstrip and sidewalk along their
frontage of the public road.
(n) Curbs, gutters, parkstrips, and sidewalks shall be required on all existing and proposed
public and private street frontage of any lot within a subdivision or legal buildable parcel in
conformance with the Standard Specifications. The Planning Commission may grant a special
exception to waive any of these improvements, after considering a recommendation from the
Director and City Engineer. They shall consider and evaluate the following criteria: (1) The
number of homes within the subdivision;

(2) The length of a cul-de-sac;
(3) The precedence of adjoining improvements;
(4) The configuration of lots;
(5) Where the only other alternative is a private road design;
(6) Flood control and storm drainage;
(7) Pedestrian safety and walkable element demands;
(8) The proposal equitably balances the needs of the public and presents the most efficient use of
land;
(9) The potential negative impacts created by the waiver(s); and
(10) The cumulative effect of all the waivers and any other exceptions requested for the
development.

Staff Analysis and Recommendation
The special exception requested by the applicants asking the City to waive the requirement for a
five-foot parkstrip and five-foot sidewalk is appropriate, in staff’s opinion. There is no sidewalk
anywhere in the rest of the surrounding neighborhood, so this will maintain continuity and will
   Montana Ranchos No. 6 Sub – SUB-09-20-5905 & SPEX-09-20-5918                             Page - 3 -


   not create a small island of sidewalk that connects to nothing. The City Engineer has provided a
   letter recommending approval of the special exception, which is attached to the staff report.

   STAFF CONCERNS
   Staff has no concerns with this proposed subdivision.

   STAFF RECOMMENDATION
   Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine Preliminary Subdivision review is
   complete for the Montana Ranchos No. 6 Subdivision located at 1933 E. Alla Panna Way, and
   also grant the special exception to waive the requirement for a five-foot parkstrip and five-foot
   sidewalk on their property, based upon the following findings and subject to the following
   conditions:

   Findings:

      1. That the City Engineer has forwarded a positive recommendation for the special
         exception request to the Planning Commission, based upon the precedence of adjoining
         improvements.

      2. The lots meet the requirements for size and frontage in the R-1-20A zone.

      3. The City Engineer, as well as other reviewing departments, have recommended approval
         of the particular layout.

   Conditions:

      1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout the
         final review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be recorded.

      2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to during the review,
         construction and operations process of this project.

      3. That the applicants dedicate the appropriate amount of property to ensure the City obtains
         a 26-foot half width of right-of-way.

                                  RECOMMENDATION FOR
                                    SIDEWALK WAIVER
      DATE:            September 24, 2020
      TO:              Craig Evans, Planner
      FROM:            Ryan C. Kump, P.E., City Engineer
      SUBJECT:         Project Name:        Whitworth Subdivision
                       Plan Case Number: SPEX-09-20-5918
                       Project Address:     1933 E. Alla Panna Way



      The Whitworth’s, developer of the Whitworth Subdivision, requests a waiver for typical sidewalk
      and parkstrip requirements.

      Recommendation for approval of this request is based on the existing conditions of the
      surrounding neighborhood. This is an established, built out, residential zone. The entire
      neighborhood was previously constructed without parkstrips and sidewalks. Requiring a new
      parkstrip and sidewalk on this midblock development would be inconsistent with rest of the
      community and provide little to no benefit, as there would be no connectivity now nor planned
      for the future.

      The city has no plans to install parkstrips and sidewalks in this neighborhood, and thus, do not
      feel it is necessary to require it at this location. The existing improvements and characteristics of
      this community would be best served to remain as is.

      Waiver of the parkstrip and sidewalk is thus recommended.
