a PowerPoint presentation on the COVID -19 Weekly Update, which 
included an overview, COVID -19 cases are 55 percent lower than in 2020, 
7-day case rate by age, 7,906 school-associated cases since school started,

district, hospitalizations are plateauing, Salt Lake County intensive care units

younger age groups lag behind in vaccinations, vaccinations for 5-11 
years-old, the County ’s southwest corner continues to drive cases, 71 
percent of Salt Lake County residents 12+ years-old are fully vaccinated, 
booster doses in Salt Lake County, and vaccination remains the strongest 
protection against severe illness.  
Mayor Jennifer Wilson stated the County needed to continue to look at 
ways to promote opportunities relating to paid time off when employees get 
the booster shots and become sick.
