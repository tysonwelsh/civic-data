GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-2-2023 Petition by NICK MINGO requesting a General Plan change from Large Lot Residential
           (2 to 3 units/acre) and Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) to Light
           Manufacturing and a zone change from A-1 (Agriculture, minimum lot size 1 acre) and A
           (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at
           5998 W Parkway Blvd. on 37.53 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-345 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman presented this application for Steve Pastorik. He explained that the
               applicant is requesting a zone change from A to M and would like to use the property for
               office/flex space. Steve stated that historically the Ritter Canal had been used to separate
               residential, to the south, from manufacturing to the north. He noted that before this
               application, there was a proposal for a residential development on the subject property,
               which was denied. Steve mentioned that a good question for the Commission is whether
               the RE zone is still a good fit in this area.
              Steve presented the proposed concept plan. He pointed out the three access points
              connecting to 5900 West and explained that they would be more challenging than
              depicted. Steve said 5900 West would run in a southeast direction, and Rocky Mountain
              Power owns the land between the subject property and 5900 West. Therefore, the
              applicant would need permission from Rocky Mountain Power for the proposed access
              points. Steve pointed out the right-of-way that ends in what is shown as a cul-de-sac just
              south of the subject property called Stegner Way. He noted that the General Plan has the
              property in question zoned for residential development, and if developed as such, the
              extension of that area where the cul-de-sac is, would be used as an access for the
              property. However, if this proposal is approved, we would not want to grant access and
              have industrial traffic mixed in with traditional and school traffic. Steve said that the
              applicant is still determining how to terminate the cul-de-sac. He said something for the
              Commission to consider is where the subject area sees more industrial-type uses. Does
              residential still fit here?

              Commissioner Winters noted that there isn’t any other land of this quantity within the
              City for residential development. He pointed out the property just north of 2700 South
              and asked if there would be any opportunities there. Steve explained that the majority of
              that property is either wetlands or owned by Magna Water. He stated that there is
              property to the west that could be for a residential development.

              Commissioner Lovato pointed out some homes on the other side of Beagley Road
              sandwiched between industrial uses and stated that he would not want to live there.
              Commissioner Lovato mentioned the issue of cars racing up and down Beagley Road,
              and he was hoping adding single-family homes would decrease that activity. Steve noted
              that Beagley Road is long and straight, making it challenging to add speed deterrents
              there.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if the wetland park is still being considered for
              development. Brock stated that it is still on their long-term plan.


ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-1-2023  Petition by SAMUEL WRIGHT requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
          minimum lot size ½ acre) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 7091 and
          7103 W Gates Avenue on 1.19 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
          steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman presented this application for Steve Pastorik. He explained that Mr. Wright
              had previously rezoned the two parcels to the left of the subject property from agriculture
              to manufacturing. Steve noted that Mr. Wright had his timber business on lot 7909, with
              the hours of operation from 8 am-6 pm. The applicant found that those hours did not
              work for his company, and he sold that lot. Steve pointed out that Mr. Wright is now
              proposing to change the zoning on the subject property from agriculture to manufacturing
              for his business, and the proposed business hours would be 7 am – 10 pm.

              Steve presented the site plan and noted that the Public Works Department found two of
              the proposed access points did not meet the City code, and they recommended that one of
              the three proposed accesses be removed.

              Steve noted that the City plans to add improvements along the south side of Gates Ave.,
               including Mr. Wright's property. He stated that the City would reimburse the applicant
               for the cost of the improvements.

               Chair Fuller asked how lots 7141 and 7129 connect to the South Frontage Road. Steve
               said it appears that the driveway for those lots extend out to the road. Chair Fuller asked
               who owned the property between those lots and the South Frontage Road. Steve said he
               would look into who owns that land, but he will follow up and have Steve Pastorik add
               the findings to his staff report.


ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-2-2023  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting an amendment to Section 7-7-119 of the
           zoning ordinance to add screening requirements for Outside Storage that occurs on
           property adjacent to a High-Image Arterial Street as listed in Section 7-10-102. (Staff –
           Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman presented for Steve Pastorik. He explained that this proposal would use a
               consistent screening from outside storage along High-Image Arterial Streets. Steve said
               that different screening types are currently being used along major streets. He then
               presented an image of the wall proposed by the City and explained that using the same
               color, style, and pattern would improve the aesthetics along the major streets.

               Steve noted that the Planning Commission would be permitted to waive the wall
               requirement when it would not be effective. He also mentioned that the back of a building
               could be sufficient screening.

               Commissioner Winters asked if this proposal is only addressing outside storage. Steve
               said that was correct.


ZT-3-2023      Petition by HH-SALT LAKE, LLC requesting amendments to Sections 7-1-103 and 7-6-
               301 of the zoning ordinance to remove the definition for Lingerie Store and to remove
               Lingerie Store from the Commercial and Manufacturing Use Table. (Staff – Steve
               Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Brandon Hill presented the application for Steve Pastorik. Brandon explained that the
               applicant had applied for and is operating a business within the Decker Lake Overlay
               Zone, which does not meet the definition of a lingerie store. He noted that the role of the
               Planning Commission regarding this application is to provide a recommendation from a
               land use perspective to the City Council. The Council will make the final decision.
               Brandon pointed out that any zoning decision in Utah has to have a rational basis. He
               explained that constitutionally, there's quite a bit of nuance to how uses and issues tend to
               be litigated at the Federal level. However, the basic rule is the harsher the restrictions, the
               more scrutiny the regulation gets.

               Commissioner Woodruff asked Brandon what the reason was for the City to remove this
               use from the overlay zones. Brandon said that Steve Pastorik could speak better about
               this in his staff report or next week's meeting. However, the purpose of the overlay zones
               is to keep complementary uses within those zones to prevent potential issues. He noted
               that an appropriate recommendation for the Commission would be to remove a specific
               restriction from the overlay zone.
              Commissioner Winters asked the difference between a lingerie store and a sexually
              oriented business. Brandon stated that the explanation would be noted in the City Code
              under definitions, but it relates to the amount of store floor space and the type of product
              available.

              Commissioner Winters asked Brandon if he agreed with the applicant's statement
              regarding the constitution and amendment violation. Brandon said he believes the City
              ordinance is constitutional and noted that this use is permittable within a large part of the
              City. He expressed that issues arise when certain uses are eliminated or heavily restricted
              within a city.

              Steve Lehman said that Steve Pastorik would include information regarding the overlay
              zones and why specific uses are permitted within those zones in his staff report. He noted
              that there are 9,000 acres of property within the city where this use is allowed.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-6-2023  Petition by MERRITT RINARD requesting conditional use approval for Pet Aquamation
          (Cremation). The property is located at 2052 West 2200 South on 1.47 acres and is zoned
          M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson presented this application for Jody Knapp. He explained that this
              application is coming before the Commission because cremation is considered a heavy
              industrial use. Brock stated that this application is for pet aquamation, which differs from
              traditional cremation. He said this process is a water-based process and is done by a
              machine approximately the size of a washing machine. Brock noted that environmentally
              safe products are used to deuterate the body.

              Brock presented the site plan. He explained that all appointments are scheduled, and the
              parking is sufficient for the business. Brock said the subject property was recently
              reviewed as part of a different business license process and met the code.


C-7-2023      Petition by SILVER FOX CONSTRUCTION, LLC requesting conditional use approval
              for a new commercial development with outside storage. The property is located at 5662
              W 4700 S on 3.75 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at
              801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that this application is coming before the Commission as a
              conditional use for outside storage. He noted that the other uses on the property are
              permitted. Brock said the applicant would share access with the adjacent 7-11 store. He
              said the applicant is working on sharing a second access with the owner to the west.
              Brock mentioned that if they cannot attain the second shared access, the fire department
              said it would be okay to have one shared access.

              Brock explained that they are proposing the storage area to be surfaced with either
              asphalt or concrete surfacing. He stated that he must look into the required screening for
              the property, as it is on a high-image arterial street and could be affected by the proposed
              text change.
                Commissioner Wood pointed out that they are proposing a large number of parking
                spaces. She asked if that is what is required. Brock said they are meeting the requirement
                but may have more than needed, but he will double check the numbers and put the
                information in his staff report. He noted that all cars stored longer than 24 hours would
                need to be kept in the fenced yard area.

                Commissioner Winters asked what the two properties located north of the subject
                property were zoned. Brock said the large triangle-shaped piece is zoned A, and the other
                portion is zoned M.


CA-1-2023       Petition by WEIR MINERALS requesting a conditional use amendment for outside

                (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)
