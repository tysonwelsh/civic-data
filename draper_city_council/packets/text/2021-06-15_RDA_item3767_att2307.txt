Business Meeting

1.0   Call to Order

2.0   Motion: Approval of May 4, 2021, and May 18, 2021, Redevelopment Agency
      Minutes

2.1   Boardmember Vawdrey moved to accept May 4, 2021, and May 18, 2020,
      Redevelopment Agency Minutes. Boardmember Roberts seconded the
      motion.

2.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0   Public Hearing: Fiscal Year 2021 Tentative Budget

3.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, presented the Fiscal Year 2021 Tentative Budget
      for the Draper City Redevelopment Agency. He explained that this budget was
      approved on May 4, 2021. It has been available for the public to review both
      online at the City’s website, as well as at the City’s offices. The overall budget
      for the RDA next year is $10.97 million for the project areas that have been
      described in the budget.
