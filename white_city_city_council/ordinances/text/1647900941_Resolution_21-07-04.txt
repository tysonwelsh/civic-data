THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 2 1-07-04 DATE: July 8, 2021
A RESOLUTION REQUESTING AMENDMENTS TO THE
MASTER INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
WITH THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES
DISTRICT AND SALT LAKE COUNTY AND OTHER
AGREEMENTS AS NEEDED
RECITALS

A. The White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah Code
§§ 10-2a-401 et seg., and

B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council’’) is the municipal legislative body
of White City pursuant to § 10-3b-501, and

C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District pursuant
to UCA §§ 17B-2a-1101 ef al., and

D. Salt Lake County (the “County”’) is a county pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Title 17 Counties
and is the contracted service provider of certain municipal-type service via the MSD; and

E. White City receives services from the MSD and from Salt Lake County (through the MSD),
pursuant to a Master Interlocal Agreement (the “Master Agreement”), by and between the
MSD, County and Metro Township members of the MSD (the "Parties"), which Master
Agreement is subject to amendments to ensure the services being provided are of satisfactory
quality and consistent with the needs of the Council and White City. See, eg., Article II,
section 2.4; and

F. White City desires certain amendments to the Master Agreement, together with other
interlocal agreements by and between the Parties to improve effectiveness, efficiency,
overall quality, and better control costs of services received by White City.

RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council,
that the Parties to the Master Agreement be notified of White City's desire for amendments to
the Master Agreement and other Interlocal Agreements, as needed. BE IT FURTHER
| RESOLVED the Council does hereby authorize the Mayor and City Attorney to give notice of
| the foregoing and to cooperativelywork with the Parties for amendments as provided for in the
Master Agreement and other Interlocal Agreements.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 8° day of July 2021.
