         The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00
p.m.; receive the Sustainability Committee annual report; discuss the proposed Sewer Master Plan; receive an Administrative
update; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair Richey and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Sustainability Committee Annual Report
         In November of 2018, the City Council adopted an ordinance that created the Natural Resources and Sustainability
Stewardship Committee. In 2024 the name of the committee was changed to the Sustainability Committee. Ogden Municipal Code
(3-31-1) states that the purpose of the committee is to “advise the City Council and the Mayor in matters pertaining to citizen
education programs relating to sustainable environmental practices and responsible stewardship of the City’s natural resources.”
         Sustainability Committee Chair Daniel Bedford used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the Committee’s
Annual Report, including a list of meeting dates, Committee accomplishments, key events that the Committee participated in, and
the Committee’s goals for the upcoming year. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the
meeting.
         The Council engaged in discussion with Mr. Bedford and other members of the Sustainability Committee regarding
sustainability efforts in the community and opportunities for additional sustainability programs in the future.

Proposed Sewer Master Plan
         The Engineering Division has submitted a transmittal for the Council to consider approving the 2024 Sewer Master Plan.
The plan was prepared by Sunrise Engineering, who began drafting the Plan in 2022. The Planning Commission held a public
hearing on June 5, 2024, and recommended that the Council adopt the plan. The current Sewer Master Plan was approved in 2013
and the Ogden City General Plan (2002) recommends that master plans for parks, trails, and infrastructure be reviewed and
adopted as part of the General Plan process. A resolution for approval of the Sewer Master Plan has been prepared for the Council
to consider.
         Public Services Assistant Executive Director/City Engineer Nelson and Sunrise Engineering Representative Cliff Linford
used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide a review of the proposed Sewer Master Plan; the purpose of the Plan is to
provide an understanding of key elements of the sewer system infrastructure; identify required improvements based on capacity or
maintenance needs; analyze the system’s capacity and flows from daily users, inflow, and infiltration; and to define dares of the
system that are out of compliance with current State requirements and identify a prioritized list of projects. For a copy of the
presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Throughout the presentation, there was discussion the Council regarding the laws and regulations that require the creation
of the Master Plan.

Administrative Update
         Public Services Executive Director Anderson provided the Council with an administrative update regarding the recent
feedback in the community regarding the conflicts between trail users and golf course patrons at Mt. Ogden Golf Course; the
Council acknowledged the safety concerns related to trail crossings on the golf course and the potential for the situation to impact
the business of the golf course. Administration is working to erect informational signage that will make trail users aware they are
entering the golf course and the danger inherent in that situation. They are also evaluating other options for moving trail users
through the area outside of the golf course boundaries.
