VI.       Discussion of Allocation of Housing set aside funds from Agency Project Areas and
Agency/City Affordable Housing Obligations
Todd Godfrey, Legal Counsel, explained that there is an obligation in the agreement for 100
units of affordable housing with a 20-year guarantee. There are very few locations in Holladay
for that to occur. The developer is proposing affordable units on property owned by Woodbury
Corp that is at the Millrock site. It would be co-located with market-rate units. Housing funds
would be used from Millrock and Holladay Hills projects to fulfill the obligation.

Board Member Fotheringham commented that this is a win-win situation for the city and
developer. He would like to study and take public comment on the proposal.

Board Member Brewer stated that he is a manager of a partnership and would have to recuse
himself from this issue and any subsequent votes.

Chair Gibbons noted that the ORD zone does not permit housing and would require a zone
change.

Board Member Dahle moved to direct City and Agency staff to draft a letter to be sent to the
Proposed Developer/Property Owner, stating the Agency/City position on the proposal as
follows:
    1. The City does not currently have any strong objection to the location of housing in the
       Millrock area as proposed. This position is qualified, as we have not heard any public
       comment on the proposal;

      2. The Agency/City believes the Proposed project could potentially meet Agency/City needs
         by providing highly desired, more affordable housing at a good location;

      3. The City currently does not have any objection to amending the ORD zone regulations to
         allow housing uses.
Board Member Fotheringham seconded the motion. The roll call vote was as follows: Board
Members Quinn, Durham, Fotheringham, Dahle and Chair Gibbons in favor. Board Member
Brewer recused himself from the vote.
