                                RESOLUTION NO. 2025-08



A RESOLUTION OF THE LEHI CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING A BOARD MEMBER
           TO THE TIMPANOGOS SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

      WHEREAS, Council Member Chris Condie has been the Lehi representative to the
Timpanogos Special Service District; and

        WHEREAS, at the February 4, 2025 Lehi City Retreat the Mayor and City Council chose
different Council assignments and it was recommended that Dave Norman, Lehi City Public
Works Director, serve on the TSSD Board; and

        WHEREAS, the Mayor of Lehi City wishes to appoint, along with the advice and consent
of the City Council, the following individual to the TSSD Board.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF LEHI, UTAH,
as follows:

       1. Appoint Dave Norman to the Timpanogos Special Service District, whose term will
          begin immediately, and expire December 31, 2028.

       2. The provisions of this resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.


PASSED and APPROVED this 11th day of February, 2025

Lehi City Corporation
