2.    North Ridge Plat D PUD Final Plat
      a.      Staff Presentation
      Shauna reminded the Commission that this is a condominium project that includes 40 units. She
      explained that the developer has addressed most of the DRC notes, and that any outstanding
      issues should not hold up the project.

      b.       Commission Discussion
      Ann asked how snow removal will be accomplished if the city isn’t responsible for the thru street.
      Terry Messersmith responded that it will be included in the HOA fees, along with garbage
      collection. Heather asked why the road is 1620 North instead of 1600 North. Shauna stated that
      even though the City tries to adhere to the grid system, there is now more expectation for the City
      to be flexible with developers. Skip Worwood said it also has to do with distance, since this will
      be a longer street. Terry said this question actually came up 4 or 5 years ago when he initially met
      with the Commission, and it was decided to go with 1620 North because of how the development
      is laid out. Shauna stated that the City can’t force a development to match the grid system. John
      Ford asked if there will only be one dumpster for the 40 families. Shauna stated that the City will
      not be involved with garbage pickup. She further stated that the HOA will be responsible for
      making sure there are enough dumpsters and will contract with someone to pick it up.

      c.     Action Item- Motion
      Ann asked for a motion to accept the Final Plat for North Ridge PUD Plat D with any outstanding
      DRC notes.

       Motion: Cory Thomson         Second: Fran Petersen       Outcome: Motion Carries

2-1.   City Council Request for Consideration
       a.       Collapsible Soil
       Skip presented the Commission with an item that was brought before the City Council.
       He said it has to do with being as transparent as possible about collapsible soil challenges, and
       that a GeoTech report should be completed before pulling a building permit. He said the Council
       isn’t leaning towards mandating this report, but they are wanting builders to know that if the
       GeoTech guidelines aren’t followed, the liability will fall solely on them. Ann asked if that could
       be part of the building permit. Shauna said that Staff is recommending more of a transparency
       approach rather than a firm requirement. Cory recommended that the Commission
       pull up the Staff Report from last night’s City Council meeting to get a better idea of what was
       discussed. Heather asked how this applies to a second homeowner. Skip stated that it’s ultimately
       up to the homeowner to do their due diligence, and that this information will be available at the
       City Offices. John stated that this should also be part of the due diligence of the realtor. Fran
       asked if developers will be held to the same standard. Cory reminded the Commission that this is
       just an item for them to start thinking about in order to start discussing it next month. Shauna
       stated that completing a GeoTech report is currently required for commercial and industrial
       developments, but not residential.

       Ann requested an item be added to next month’s agenda to honor and present Karl Brough with a
       plaque for his service on the Commission.
