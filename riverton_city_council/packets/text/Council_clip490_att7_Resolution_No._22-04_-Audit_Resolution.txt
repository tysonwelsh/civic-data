                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 22-04

 A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF RIVERTON CITY ACCEPTING
  THE FISCAL YEAR ANNUAL COMPREHENSIVE FINANCIAL REPORT (ACFR)
                    FOR YEAR ENDED JUNE 30, 2021

      WHEREAS, Riverton City hired HBME, LLC to perform an annual Audit Report for the
Year Ended June 30, 2021; and

       WHEREAS, HBME, LLC has performed the audit and presented Riverton City with their
findings; and

     WHEREAS, the Finance Department Staff has compiled statistical information required in a
ACFR presentation.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. Riverton City does hereby accept and approve the ACFR presented by
          HBME, LLC for the Year Ended June 30, 2021.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective upon passage.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of January,
2022 by the following vote:
