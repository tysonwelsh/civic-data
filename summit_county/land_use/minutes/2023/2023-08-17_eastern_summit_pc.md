4.
Public Hearing and Recommendation to the County Council for an amendment to chapter 11-4-5: Subdivisions, Condominiums, Subdivision Plat Amendments, Parcel Boundary Adjustments, and Divisions of Land for Non-Development Purposes of the Eastern Summit County Development Code.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner.
(6:46 P.M.)
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner
, presented a language amendment to Section 11-4-5 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code to provide an alternate method for a property owner to obtain lien holder consent when amending a subdivision plat, which is currently required via a signature on a mylar or certificate of consent before recordation.
Commissioners asked questions. Mr. Milliner and Helen Strachan,
Deputy Attorney
, responded.
Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public hearing.
(6:56 P.M.)
No public input was given.
Chair Sonntag closed the meeting for public hearing.
(6:57 P.M.)
Clinton Benson made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council, according to the findings of fact and conclusions of law as stated in the staff report. Alex Peterson seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Don Sargent.
