Just a clarification - the term variance has very specific standards associated with it. Variances
would not go to the Planning Commission, but have a formal process established through ordinance.
What we can set up in this Chapter is a special exception process that goes through the Planning
Commission. It would accomplish your needs without having to adhere to the strict state-mandated
criteria for variance approvals.
