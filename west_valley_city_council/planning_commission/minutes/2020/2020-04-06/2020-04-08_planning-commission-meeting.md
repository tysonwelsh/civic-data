Application #:         Z-2-2020
Applicant:             Danny Gutierrez
Address:               3846 South 5600 West
Request:               Zone change from A to RE
Property Size:         7.98 acres

Danny Gutierrez with MDT Inc. has requested a zone change on a 7.98-acre parcel at 3846 South
5600 West from A (agriculture, minimum lot size of ½ acre) to RE (residential estate).
Surrounding zones include A to the north, west and south and R-1-8 (single family residential,
minimum lot size 8,000 sq. ft.) to the east. The property is surrounded by homes to the east, a
new charter school to the south, vacant property to the west and a few homes and vacant property
to the north. The subject property is designated as large lot residential, which anticipates 2 to 3
units/acre, in the West Valley City General Plan.

Development Proposal

The applicant has submitted a concept plan, which is attached, that shows the subject property
being subdivided into 18 lots yielding a density of 2.3 units/acre. The existing home in the
southeast corner of the property would be demolished along with the associated outbuildings. A
masonry wall will be required along 5600 West and a chain link fence will be required along the
north given the A zoning to the north. Since the south lots will have double frontage, staff asked
the developer what type of fence will be installed along the south side. He indicated that he plans
to use vinyl fencing. Staff suggests including this in the development agreement. Included with
the concept plan is a letter from the applicant supporting this application.

During the study session the Commission asked a couple of questions regarding the concept
plan. The first question asked was about the stem on Lot 11 extending to 5600 West. The
applicant indicated that he can explore options with the adjoining landowner. The second
question was concerning a triangle area shown on Lot 14. The applicant indicated that this area is
in case they need a retention/detention basin.

Development Agreement

A development agreement is required for this project. Section 7-14-105 (3)(q) of the West Valley
City Zoning Ordinance states: “All new subdivisions involving a rezone of property, or a PUD,
shall participate in a development agreement that addresses housing size, quality, exterior finish
materials, streetscapes, landscaping, etc. The standards outlined in Section 7-14-105 (3)(l)(iii)
shall be used as a minimum in all development agreements to address housing quality and
exterior finish materials. These standards may be increased for a PUD.” For the development
agreement, the applicant is proposing to simply follow the City’s housing standards.

Staff Alternatives:

      Approval. The application should be approved as it complies with the General Plan. The
       approval is made subject to a development agreement including the standards proposed
       by the applicant and a requirement that vinyl fencing be installed along the south side of
       the property.

      Continuance.

C-5-2020
Diamond Plaza
2055 West 3500 South
C-2 Zone (1.78 Acres)

The applicant, H4 Design, is requesting conditional use approval for a shopping center at 2055
West 3500 South. The zoning for this area is C-2, General Commercial. The West Valley City
General Plan designates this area as General Commercial. The surrounding zone is C-2, General
Commercial on all sides with a portion to the west zoned A, agricultural and R-1-8, Single unit
8,000 sqft lot min. The surrounding uses include the Pep Boys to the north, Audio One to the
east, auto services to the south and single unit residential to the west.

This site was originally Kowloon Buffet. The current owners are planning to demolish the
existing building and redevelop the entire site. They would like to construct a new shopping
center that would include 3 retail tenants, a restaurant and a buffet restaurant use.

The new building design is required to meet the Commercial Design Standards. The façade will
include stucco in a variety of colors as well as Ipe wood siding, brick veneer, metal panel accents
and awnings.

The property has frontage along 3500 South so the streetscape requirements in 7-10 will be
installed as part of this application. Additional landscaping is provided throughout the site and in
the setbacks adjacent to residential to meet the Code. A 6’ masonry wall will also be installed
adjacent to the residential zone/uses on the west side of the property.

The applicant has indicated that there will be wall signage identifying the different businesses.
There was a pole sign on site that has been removed and no additional ground signage has been
requested at this time. All signage shall meet the standards set forth in Title 11 of the WVC
Municipal Code.

The proposed mix of uses requires 138 parking stalls. The applicant is requesting that the
required number of parking stalls for the buffet use be calculated based off seats and not floor
area. Buffet uses typically have a large percentage of the use for food prep and serving areas
compared to seating. This is consistent with how other buffet uses have been reviewed
previously and staff is supportive of this request.

The dumpster proposed for the site must comply with 7-2-118 and be located within a 6’ tall
masonry enclosure that is at least 20’ from adjacent residential uses. A lighting plan will be
evaluated to ensure the adjacent neighbors are not negatively impacted by any of the site lighting
and the light levels meet the standards in the City Code.


Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following conditions:

       1. The building must be completed per the approved plans and meet the requirements set
          forth in the Commercial Design Standards.

       2. The site shall be improved per the approved plans and must include the 3500
          Streetscape Standards per 7-10 of the WVC Municipal Code.

       3. There must be adequate parking within the commercial complex for the proposed
          tenant mix and the buffet use parking can be calculated based off the number of seats.

       4. Dumpsters must be located within a 6’ masonry enclosure and a minimum of 20’
          from adjacent residential uses/zones per 7-2-118 of the WVC Municipal Code.

       5. All signage shall comply with the West Valley City Sign Ordinance.

       6. All requirements of affected departments and agencies must be met.

       7. This use is subject to review upon a valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

C-7-2020
Utah State Collision (Outside Storage)
2440 South 3400 West
M Zone (.50 acres)

The applicant, Keith Klingenberg, the property owner at 2440 South 3400 West, is requesting
conditional use approval for outside storage. This property is zoned M, manufacturing and the
West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light manufacturing. The surrounding zone
is M, manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses include FedEx to the west, a
construction business to the north, Intermountain Traffic Safety to the east and Premier Plastics
to the south.

As part of the City’s cleanup efforts a thorough site inspection was conducted of this property
during a new business license review. A courtesy notice was issued for violations on this
property, including outside storage. The business on site, Utah Auto Collision, operates an auto
repair and vehicle restoration business. They would like to utilize the back western portion of the
property for outside storage, which is a conditional use in the M zone. Therefore, they have
submitted a conditional use application to get that violation resolved.

The business occupies the entire building on site. There are three overhead doors and service
bays used for auto repair which requires 9 parking stalls for this use. The outside storage area
would be to the west of the parking stalls behind a 6’ chain link fence. Slats will be installed in
this fence to screen the storage from public view. A gate will be added to the fence to allow for
access. The storage area will be used for vehicles that are waiting to be repaired/restored or are
completed and are awaiting pickup and would remain on the site for more than 24 hours.

The entire storage area must be surfaced with asphalt or concrete and the appropriate access shall
be maintained throughout the site. All work including repair/restoration of the vehicles will be
conducted within the existing building on site. All required parking and storage shall be located
on the site and shall not be out within the street or public right-of-way. The site shall not be used
for junk, salvage or as a vehicle recycling facility.

The dumpster will be relocated within this storage area, so it is also screened. All signage for the
business must comply with the West Valley City Municipal Code and the remaining site
maintenance issues are being resolved as part of the enforcement process.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

           1. The approved use for this application is for outside storage of vehicles for an auto
              repair/restoration business.

           2. The site shall not be used as a junk yard, auto salvage or a vehicle recycling
              facility.

           3. The outside storage area must be paved with asphalt or concrete and the
              appropriate access shall be maintained throughout the site.

           4. Outside storage is only permitted on the west side of the site, as outlined in the
              approved site plan. Storage shall not be located in any of the required parking
              stalls for the business and must be screened from public view.

           5. All dumpsters on site shall meet the standards set forth in section 7-2 of the West
              Valley City Municipal Code.

           6. All signage shall comply with the standards set forth in Title 11 of the West
              Valley City Municipal Code.

           7. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.
           8. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

C-8-2020
Franklin Covey Outside Storage
2545 S Decker Lake Blvd
M Zone (1.04 acres)

The applicant, Marty Jaramillo with Franklin Covey, is requesting conditional use approval for
outside storage. The property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General Plan
designates this area as light manufacturing. The surrounding zoning is also M. Adjacent land
uses include the Franklin Covey Campus to the south, a UPS facility to the east, and various light
industrial businesses to the north and west.
The subject property is located just north of the Franklin Covey campus. There are no previous
land use approvals on this property, but at some point, prior to 1997, landscaping was installed
along the street frontage. The existing landscaping and trees will remain. The M Zone requires
5% landscaping and the site has approximately 32% landscaping. The applicant would like to use
the site for outside storage. The property would most likely be used for storing things like
commercial building supplies and equipment, electrical and data spools, and pipes and fittings.
Most of these items would be kept inside shipping containers and semi-trailers.
There is an existing chain link fence at the edge of the landscaped area. The applicant is
proposing to put slats in the fence in order to screen the storage. Landscaping and weeds that
have grown through the fence shall be removed. The property was paved with asphalt at some
point. All parking and maneuvering areas, as well as areas used for storage containers and semi-
trailers, need to be paved with asphalt or concrete. Any existing asphalt that is in disrepair shall
be fixed or replaced. Other storage areas on the property may be surfaced with gravel, which is at
least four inches deep, contained with durable border, and kept weed free. There is an existing
area along the east property line that is not paved or maintained. Landscaping is not required
here, so the area shall either be replaced with appropriate surfacing or landscaped with 50% live
plant material.
Staff Alternatives:
1. The existing landscaping shall remain and shall include at least 1 tree for every 30’ of
frontage. The landscaped areas shall be maintained, and no outside storage shall be permitted
there.
2. Slats shall be added to the existing chain link fence for screening.
3. Areas near fencing and along the perimeter of the property shall be kept free of weeds and
litter.
4. Non-landscaped areas shall be maintained and appropriately surfaced.
5. Outside storage is permitted within the fenced area and shall be placed on appropriate
surfacing. All vehicular maneuvering and parking areas, as well as cargo container and semi-
trailer storage areas, shall be surfaced with asphalt or concrete. All other storage areas may be
surfaced with gravel which is at least 4” deep, contained by durable borders and kept weed free.
6. Asphalt areas shall be maintained in good repair.
7. An appropriate surfacing shall be installed along the eastern edge of the property or be
landscaped with 50% live plant material.
8. Requirements of all affected departments and agencies shall be met.
9. Subject to review upon valid complaint.
Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

SA-1-2020
Godfrey Industrial Park Amended
6219 West 2100 South
M Zone
4 Lots
37 Acres


BACKGROUND

Norm Dahle, representing Godfrey Trucking, is requesting a plat amendment to the Godfrey
Industrial Park Subdivision. The subject property is located at approximately 6219 West 2100
South and is bordered by a variety of manufacturing uses on the east, south and west.
ISSUES:

The Godfrey Industrial Park Subdivision was approved by the Planning Commission in 2019 and
recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office in February 2020. The purpose for the
amended plat is to create one additional building lot and to provide the applicant an opportunity to
expand the footprint of their corporate offices located at the northeast corner of the subdivision.

The original subdivision consisted of 5 parcels that were consolidated into 2 lots. The applicant
expressed to staff that on occasion and when the need for financial funding becomes critical to the
operation of the business, creating additional lots allows greater flexibility to secure financing if
required. In the case of this application, an additional building lot is being proposed and will be
known as lot 3 within the subdivision.

The applicant also expressed the possibility that the existing corporate offices could expand in the
future, so they’ve added this parcel into the subdivision as well. The existing size of that parcel is
approximately 2.3 acres and according to the plat, that parcel becomes lot 4 within the subdivision
and will be approximately 3.4 acres in size.

Access to the subdivision will be gained from 2100 South. Presently, there are two access points
that will serve this subdivision. It is anticipated that these access points will not change as they
provide access on both the east and west portions of the subdivision. Lot 2 does not have direct
access to 2100 South. Therefore, internal access and parking easements will need to be recorded
if not already in place.

Existing easements and areas denoted as Fema Flood Zone A will be shown on the subdivision
plat. Future development on these newly created lots will require additional staff reviews.
Depending upon the land use, these reviews could be conditional and/or permitted. In summary,
the original subdivision will be amended to create 2 additional lots and will provide information
on easements and Fema Flood Zone A areas.


STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

1.     Approve the amended plat for the Godfrey Industrial Park Subdivision subject to a
       resolution of staff and agency comments.

2.     Continue the application for reasons determined in the Planning Commission meeting.
