6.      WORK SESSION

       6.4. The Bluffs at Vineyard

            Planning Technician Madison Reed provided a presentation and overview of the
       project. She highlighted a few of the amenities.

               Commissioner Ostler asked questions about maintenance responsibilities.

       Planning Technician Reed responded that the city has taken over maintenance. There was
       a discussion about liabilities and maintenance.

             Director Ghandour responded that the permits that are filed with the state will
       handle any legal issues. He mentioned that trash cans and dog waste stations are placed
       on a frequent schedule.

        Commissioner Pearce asked questions about the volleyball court and wanted to know if
       there are other locations for volleyball courts.

             Chair Steele provided feedback on amenities and financing. Discussion about the
       volleyball court location ensued.

       Public access and reservations were discussed.

            Chair Steele requested for a group to help fundraise and establish a better
       experience. He opened up to the public.

             Resident David Lauret asked for clarification on land ownership and public
       meetings. He asked if it could be turn into parking.

             Ms. Evans asked about landscaping near Sunset Beach Park due to how steep the
       bluff is.

        Director Ghandour explained that the slope will be considered during the design.

             Resident Josh Hunt stated that he was thrilled that the city is working on this and
       loved a volleyball court. He mentioned that the southern access point to the lake is very
       popular and could benefit from amenities. He felt that the parking on 300 west was
       available enough to provide access for the site.

            Chair Steele requested some photos of the existing conditions.

       6.5.     Update to City Ordinances and Zoning Maps: Massage Establishments
       and Sexually-Oriented Businesses

            Business Licensing Specialist Kelly Kloepfer presented an update to explain that the
       city is in process of updating city ordinances and zoning maps regarding massage
       establishments and sexually-oriented businesses.
