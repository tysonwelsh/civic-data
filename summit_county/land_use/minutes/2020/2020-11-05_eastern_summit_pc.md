Findings of Fact:  
1. The applicant is requesting an amendment to Lot 5 of the Pace Acres Subdivision. Lot 5 is 
approximately 5.6 acres in size.  
2. The applicant would like to sell 2.94 acres to the adjacent property owner leaving 2.7 acres.  
3. Currently there is a single-family home and 3 or four agricultural buildings on the lot. It is 
bisected by a drainage area with the home on the north side and the agricultural buildings on the 
south.  
4. The property is in the Agriculture-5 (Ag-5) zone.  
5. The Minimum lot size in the AG-5 zone is 1 acre.  
6. The vacated area would be joined with parcel #NS-131-A directly to the south through a 
boundary line adjustment.  
7. This parcel is 1.13 acres in size, has a single-family home on it and is a legal grandfathered 
parcel.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. There is good cause for the proposed Plat Amendment.  
2. No one will be materially harmed by this Plat Amendment.  
3. The Plat Amendment is consistent with the Eastern Summit County General Plan, as amended.  
4. The effects of any differences in use or scale have been mitigated through careful planning.  
 
Conditions of Approval:  
1. This approval will expire one (1) year from the date of approval if it has not been recorded 
with the Summit County Recorder office.
