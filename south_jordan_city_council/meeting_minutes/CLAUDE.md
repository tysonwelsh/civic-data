# meeting_minutes/ — South Jordan City Council vote pipeline

## What's here
- `minutes/<year>/<week-monday>/<date>_<slug>.md` — **243** City Council minutes,
  2020-08 → 2026. Council meets Tuesdays; the folder is keyed on that week's Monday.
  Same-day Study + Regular + Budget + Combined-RDA meetings are **separate files** (so a
  date can legitimately carry >1 doc, and same-date meetings can reuse motion numbers —
  disjoint once keyed on `source`). Indexed in `minutes_index.csv` (13-col standard;
  `format=pdf-text` for every file — all born-digital, no OCR). `source` = `civicplus`
  (227, CivicPlus ArchiveCenter `Archive.aspx?ADID=<id>`) or `pmn` (16, Utah PMN 2020
  backfill).
- `raw/<date>_<slug>.pdf` — the retained source PDFs (never modified). **244 raw vs 243
  indexed** — see the ADID-232 mis-upload below.
- `minutes_unrecovered.csv` — meetings that exist but whose minutes were never posted
  (the honest 2020 Jan–Jul gap + the 2020-08-04 agenda-only meeting).
- `extract_votes.py` — the PURE deterministic parser (no LLM, no network; resumable).
  Run it to regenerate everything below.
- `votes/<year>/<week>/<date>_*.json` — one structured JSON per meeting (the resumable
  intermediate; `all_votes.csv` is rebuilt from these).
- `votes/_validation_report.txt` — integrity checks, per-year roster, mayor-vote flags,
  tally mismatches, contested-vote list (regenerated by `validate_votes.py`).
- `all_votes.csv` — long format, **one row per member-vote** (or one placeholder row per
  tally-only motion), schema:
  `date,year,title,body,motion_no,motion,motion_type,result,mover,seconder,member,vote,source`.

## Run
```
python3 extract_votes.py     # writes votes/*.json then rebuilds all_votes.csv
python3 validate_votes.py    # writes votes/_validation_report.txt
```
`all_votes.csv` is valid RFC-4180 (motion text contains commas/quotes — read it with a real
CSV parser, NOT `awk -F,`).

## Validation summary (`votes/_validation_report.txt`)

· **14 contested** · off-roster names **0** · outcome-vs-count inconsistencies **0**. Body

## Narrative-tally vote grammar — majorities honestly UNNAMED
South Jordan's clerk records votes as a **narrative tally**, never a standing per-name
roll-call block for the assenting side. So — exactly like Sandy's council inline-tally form
— the winning **majority is never named**; only **dissenters and absentees** are. The
parser therefore leaves the ayes unnamed (`names_recorded:false`) rather than guessing who
voted how. **There are ~0 named-Aye rows by design** on unanimous motions; the `f.tally`
validator's 43.2% full-roster match rate is this documented source style (2024-12-03 onward
the clerk began printing tabular full roll calls, which do sum exactly), not a parse loss.

Forms the parser handles:
- **Unanimous (tally only, no names → `names_recorded:false`, one placeholder row):**
  `"...seconded the motion. The motion passed with a vote of 5-0."` /
  `"...vote was 5-0 unanimous in favor."` / `"...vote was unanimous in favor."` (number-less).
- **Named dissent — two forms, both handled:**
  1. *Narrative:* `"...vote of 4-1; Council Member Harris voted 'No'."` /
     `"3-2. Council Member Johnson and Council Member McGuire voted No."` /
     `"Roll Call vote was 2-3, motion failed; Council Members Zander, McGuire and Harris
      with the no votes."` — the named dissenter(s) → `Nay`/`Abstain` rows; the majority
      stays unnamed.
  2. *Tabular one-member-per-line:* `"Council Member <Name> - Yes|No"` under a "Roll Call
     Vote" header, then `"The motion passed with a vote of X-Y."` — a full named roll call.
- **Named absentees** → explicit `Absent` rows (`"Council Member Zander was absent from the
  vote."`). **Died** → recorded with no members (`"...failed due to lack of a second."`).
- Page-break footers (`South Jordan City / <meeting> / <date> / <pagenum>`) that interrupt
  a roll-call block are flattened out before parsing.

`motion_type` uses the fixed 12-category taxonomy; `result` and the numeric tally are
**verbatim as printed**. Normalized outcome/tallies/type live **alongside** in
`motions_std.csv` (per the cardinal rules — verbatim is never overwritten).

## Council composition / the Mayor does NOT vote (except a real tie-break)
Six-member council form: **5 district members** (D1 Harris, D2 **Marlor 2020→2023 then
Johnson 2023+**, D3 Shelton, D4 Zander, D5 McGuire) + **Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey**, who
**presides but is NOT a routine voter** (max ordinary tally = 5). She is excluded from the
voting roster; only the five district surnames map to a vote (county/other-city officials
named in the narrative never do). "Mayor Pro Tem[pore] <Surname>" is a **councilmember**
acting as chair → mapped to that councilmember, never to the Mayor.

**The single mayor-vote in the corpus is a genuine statutory tie-break: 2025-06-17,
Ordinance 2025-09.** The council split 2-2 (Shelton/Johnson Yes, Harris/McGuire No, Zander
absent) and **Mayor Ramsey cast the deciding Yes → 3-2 pass**. When the source itself
records the Mayor casting a vote, it is captured **faithfully** (`Dawn R. Ramsey | Aye`)
and the motion is flagged `mayor_voted:true`; `validate_votes.py` lists it as the sole
6th-voter / mayor-vote event (Ramsey is otherwise absent from every vote list). This is the
single most important structural fact for the extractor — the Mayor is non-voting on
ordinary motions but may break a tie as a 6th vote.

## `body` column (RDA / MBA — in-meeting bodies)
Default `Council`. South Jordan's council sits **in-session** as the **Redevelopment Agency
(RDA)** and the **Municipal Building Authority (MBA)**: it recesses the Council, convenes
the RDA/MBA inside the same meeting (often a "Combined City Council & Redevelopment Agency"
minutes doc), acts, then reconvenes. The extractor detects those brackets and tags each
motion `body ∈ {Council, RDA, MBA}` — **1,007 Council · 21 RDA · 1 MBA**. There are **no
separate RDA/MBA minutes files** to acquire (unlike Ogden); the in-meeting captures are the
complete published record — not an acquisition gap.

## Faithful-capture anomalies (retained verbatim, NOT fixed in place)
- **Clerk error — 2025-08-19 motion 7.** `result` = `"4-1 Pass"` (city-verbatim) but only
  3 Aye + 1 Nay are named with 4 seated. Kept verbatim; surfaced in the validation report's
  tally-mismatch + roster-size sections. Any correction belongs in a documented override
  file, never an in-place edit.
- **Minutes mis-upload — ADID-232 (2021-10-19).** CivicPlus served the *Study* minutes in
  the *Regular* slot for 2021-10-19. The true regular minutes were recovered via Utah PMN
  (`utah.gov/pmn/files/784439.pdf`) and the index title states this verbatim; the wrong
  original (`raw/civicplus_ADID-232.pdf`) is **retained in `raw/` but not indexed** — hence
  244 raw PDFs vs 243 indexed rows.

## Coverage floor (2020) — honestly logged, not silently missing
Coverage begins **2020-08-18**. No portal retains pre-Aug-2020 SJ council minutes (CivicPlus
archive starts 2021; Municode 2022; Utah PMN 2020-08-04). The **2020 Jan–Jul span** and the
**2020-08-04 agenda-only meeting** are recorded in `minutes_unrecovered.csv` — never as
stub/wrong-doc rows.
