6.b   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1602, an Ordinance amending the Official
      Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 1.68 acres of property
      from CO2 (Professional Office) and CR (Regional Commercial) to CR,
      located at approximately 13673 South 100 East within Draper City,
      otherwise known as the Chick-fil-A Zoning Map Amendment.
      City Planner Todd Taylor showed a map of the subject area and explained
      the proposed rezone to Regional Commercial. He said the Planning
      Commission reviewed the request and forwarded a positive
      recommendation.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing.

      Craig Knight, resident of Sandy City, said he represented LD Bowerman
      Investments, which owns the Bangerter Crossing area. He said he was
      excited about the proposed plan and felt it would be great for the
      community.

      Mayor Walker closed the public hearing.

      Kevin Watson, representing the applicant, said the applicant was in the
      process of completing a traffic impact study and would work to address
      traffic concerns. He answered questions regarding planned access to the
      site.

6.c   Public Hearing: Ordinances #1603 and #1604, an Ordinance amending
      the Official Zoning Map and Land Use Map of Draper City for
      approximately 1,035.49 acres to remove the properties from both the
      Zoning Map and the Land Use Map. The properties are located at
      approximately 1950 E 16365 South and were part of a municipal
      boundary line adjustment with Lehi City in 2022 and are no longer
      located within Draper City jurisdiction, otherwise known as the City
      Initiated Draper-Lehi Zoning and Land Use Map Amendments.
      Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky explained the
      proposed ordinances would make the Zoning Map and Land Use Map
      consistent with city boundary changes made in 2022. She said the Planning
      Commission reviewed the proposed amendments and forwarded a positive
      recommendation.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing
      seeing no one come forward.

      Councilmember F. Lowry moved to approve Ordinance #1603 and
      Ordinance #1604. Councilmember T. Lowery seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
