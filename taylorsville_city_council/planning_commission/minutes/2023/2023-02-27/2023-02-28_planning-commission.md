  5.       1Z23/DCA-000310-2023 - Public Hearing and Recommendation to the City Council for a Zoning Text
  Amendment to Taylorsville Municipal Code Section 13.20.030, Related to Minimum Lot width and Lot Area
  Affecting the Two-Family Residential R-2-8 and R-2-10 Zoning Districts. (Jim Spung/AICP/Senior Planner)

5.1      This item was presented by Jim Spung.
         5.1.1     This is a request to amend the laws that govern the R-2-8 and R-2-10 Zoning Districts.          The
                   purpose is to amend the minimum lot area and lot width requirements in the R-2-8 and R-2-10
                   zoning districts to remove barriers that prohibit the conversion of some existing duplex structures
                   to twin homes. Definition of a Dwelling, Duplex is a building designed or arranged to be occupied
                   by two (2) families living independently, the structure having only two (2) dwelling units in one
                   ownership. This definition does not include a detached single-family dwelling with an approved
                   accessory dwelling unit (LDC Section 13.36.050). The definition of Twin Home is a residential
                   structure composed of two (2) dwellings set side by side and sharing a common wall and separation
                   wall. Each dwelling is constructed on its own building lot and is sold separately from the adjoining
                   attached dwellings. (LDC Section 13.36.210).

         5.1.2     Mr. Spung added that 16 of the original 30 lots in the Heatherglen Subdivision have already been
                   converted. This change, if implemented, will apply City-wide and not just this subdivision.
