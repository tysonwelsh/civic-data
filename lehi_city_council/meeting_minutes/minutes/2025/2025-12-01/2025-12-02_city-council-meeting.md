5) Discussion Items

5.1) Noise Control Ordinance Discussion

Kim Struthers led the discussion with the council on updating the noise control ordinance,
focusing on making it enforceable, fair, and aligned with modern technology. They agreed to
downgrade violations from a Class B to Class C misdemeanor. They clarified who can be cited
(businesses, property owners, and individuals causing or aiding the noise). They discussed who
can take the official measurements and the type of equipment used. There was discussion of how
long noise must last to count as a violation (e.g., a proposed 3-minute measurement period), how
to handle different zoning impacts (using the most restrictive/receiving property’s standard), and
whether nighttime hours should be 10 p.m.–6 a.m. or extended to 7 a.m., possibly seasonally.
They also reviewed exemptions for athletic/special events with permits and for unavoidable

5.2) Forebay Conservation Easement Discussion

The council reviewed the Conservation Easement with Payson City. Kim Struthers stated that
Lehi will act as the easement holder (grantee) while Payson remains the owner and “grantor” of
the recreation area. He explained that this is similar to how Draper holds Lehi’s easement and
highlighted that the council’s earlier conservation action helped inspire Payson to pursue similar
protections for high-value open space and watershed lands. This will be on the December 16th
agenda for approval.
