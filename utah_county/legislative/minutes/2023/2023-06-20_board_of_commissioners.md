JOINT COMMISSION MEETING MINUTES

JUNE 20, 2023

The Joint Special meeting of the Juab County Commission with the Utah County Commission
was held June 20, 2023, 9:00 a.m. in the Juab County Commission Chambers, 160 North Main,

Nephi, Utah.

Clinton Painter Commissioner

Marvin Kenison ------------------------ Commissioner
Marty Palmer --------------------------- Commissioner

Tanielle Callaway Clerk/Auditor

Others present: Norm Beagley (Santaquin City), Drake Underwood, Travis Kenison, Debbie
Zirbes, Melanie Cowan, Tom Sakievich (Utah County Commissioner), Brandon Gordon (Utah
County Commissioner), Anthony Canto (Surveyor), Dale Eyre (Utah County Attorney's Office),
Zack Buck, Paul Jones (Utah County Deputy Attorney), Lisa Shepherd, Myrna Trauntvein

Juab County Commissioners and Utah County Commissioners met to consider possible
proposals for a County Line Boundary Adjustment in Santaquin. Juab County and Utah County
met last year and discussed possible proposals and adjourned with plans of meeting in the
future.

There was some discussion of some property up near Eureka in the last meeting. That proposal
has since fallen through.

There are currently some homes in a Santaquin subdivision that divides over both county lines.
The subdivision contains 25 homes; 8 to 10 of the homes are in Juab County.

Chairman Painter indicated they need to find something amenable, value for value. The
estimated value on the taxes for those homes is $75,000 that Juab County would lose if moved
to Utah County.

Chairman Painter inquired if water rights could be an option. Utah County indicated it was a
good suggestion, and they would need to check further into that and see if they did.

Dale Eyre inquired further into the previously discussed Eureka parcels. Commissioner Kenison
stated he felt there were some issues with that. Chairman Painter stated they have not
responded further with the Commissioners, and the feeling was it was a no go. He indicated
they are looking for direction on what they are looking for so they can make a plan.


Juab County Assessor Zack Buck inquired if the maps had changed. He stated the last time he
reviewed the map, 12 homes were in Juab County, and 8-10 homes were split by the county
line with Juab and Utah.

Commissioner Palmer inquired how this came about. Dale Eyre indicated that cities can annex
into adjacent counties. Anthony Canto stated that Santaquin would have petitioned Juab
County for the annexation, and that Juab County accepted that. Debbie Zirbes, Zack Buck and
Commissioner Kenison and Chairman Painter all indicated that Juab County was not notified of
the annexation beforehand. Mr. Eyre stated that the county should have been notified of the
annexation. Anthony Canto indicated it would have been the Santaquin City Manager that
would have notified the county.

Commissioner Sakievich inquired if the water was an issue, what other options would Juab
County be interested in. Mr. Eyre asked if there were any other areas along the county line that
would be of interest for future development in Juab County. Commissioner Sakievich indicated
the growth is coming south, and Santaquin is expanding quickly. Mr. Eyre inquired if the city
planner and the county recorder could meet and discuss some options. Mr. Canto stated he
would like to be involved in any meetings to see if they can adjust boundary lines, so they
follow section lines and other definable items.

Debbie Zirbes and Zack Buck stated there may be some areas where the county line cuts and
dips that would make sense to straighten the line out, between Eureka and Goshen. Mr. Buck
also stated there are parcels near Rocky Ridge that are don’t make sense, where Utah County
assesses and Juab County assesses one and they’re in the wrong county. Commissioner
Sakievich indicated that just reaffirms the suggestion of a joint meeting with both surveyors,
recorders and assessors.

Ms. Zirbes indicated she would have some concerns taking property from Utah County up near

Eureka, due to the mines in the area. She stated she has been tracking Juab County's mines for
60-70 years, and Utah County has not been tracking their mining claims. Commissioner Gordon
asked if that was a liability for counties if that’s not being done. Ms. Zirbes stated there is.

Mr. Eyre indicated it would be a good idea to dial in the actual amount for the exact number of
houses and the taxable value and determine a tangible number. Mr. Canto advised he would
be willing to come down and meet with the assessor and recorder and look at maps to
determine that. Chairman Painter indicated they would like Tracy Painter involved in that
meeting, as well.

Chairman Painter indicated he believes the first step is to clear up the county lines on both
sides and look at potential areas for consideration. They will have a meeting and pinpoint a
value and come up with some options. Both counties will schedule a meeting and plan on
reconvening back to report their findings to the Commissioners.
