White City Policy #6: Asset Capitalization Policy

                The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving and adopting an asset
capitalization policy for White City:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-05-01                                                       Date: May 2, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING AND ADOPTING AN ASSET CAPITALIZATION POLICY FOR
       WHITE CITY

                                                3
DATE            THURSDAY                              MAY                                   2, 2019


                                            RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Pursuant to Federal financial regulations for local governments as contained in GASB 53,
      White City is required to adopt an Asset Capitalization Policy (the “Policy) as a part of its
      accounting for certain capital assets.

   D. The Government Finance Officers’ Association (“GFOA”) provides templates for local
      governments to consider when establishing their Asset Capitalization Policy.

   E. The attached policy, hereinafter identified as Exhibit “A,” is such a template and was
      recommended by the MSD Financial Officer for consideration and possible approval.

   F. When establishing as Asset Capitalization Policy, White City must set a financial asset
      value from which point certain assets may be considered for capitalization under the
      policy.

   G. Pursuant to the recommendations of the MSD Financial Officer, it is recommended White
      City set that value in their Policy as FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS ($5,000.00).

                                          RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby accepts and approves the attached
Asset Capitalization Policy for White City, Exhibit “A,” and establishes the base capital asset value
at FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS ($5,000.00).

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 2nd day of May,
2019.

OnSolve Corporation for CodeRED Municipal Telephonic and Electronic Communications
Platform

            The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving OnSolve Corporation for
the CodeRED municipal telephonic and electronic communications platform:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-05-02                                                       Date: May 2, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWDNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING A PROPOSED AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN WHITE CITY
       AND ONSOLVE CORPORATION FOR THE CODERED MUNICIPAL
       TELEPHONIC AND ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS PLATFORM

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Onsolve Corporation is a company that operates a multi-faceted communications platform
      municipalities can use for emergency and non-emergency communications with residents
      called “CodeRED.”

   D. The CodeRED product is the widest-used of its kind in the US and is currently coming
      online for use by the Salt Lake County Health Department.

   E. After previewing the product with representatives from Onsolve, the Administrator
      recommends the CodeRed product to the Council for White City’s use.

   F. The attached bid, hereinafter identified as Exhibit “A,” reflects Onsolve’s bid to provide
      their communications platform service to White City.

   G. Onsolve’s bid included an option to contract for several years at a lower cost.

                                        RESOLUTION




                                               5
DATE            THURSDAY                             MAY                                 2, 2019

        THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED the Council hereby accepts and approves the attached
bid for service from Onsolve, Exhibit “A,” sets the contracts term from the bid at five (5) years,
and approves the Mayor to sign the final contract subject to approval as to form by the White City
Attorney.

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 2nd day of May,
2019.
