ORDINANCE NO. 22-28

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE CITY'S FISCAL YEAR 2022-2023
BUDGET

On August 9, 2022, the Murray City Municipal Council adopted the City’s budget
for Fiscal Year 2022-2023. It has been proposed that the Fiscal Year 2022-2023 budget
be amended as follows:

1. Appropriate the following items from General Fund reserves:

a. $1,265,946 for prior year Class C Road maintenance and infrastructure
projects in process;

programs contributed and unspent in the previous year;

contributed and unspent in the previous year, and;

e. $5,849 part-time wages from Utah Humanities grant unspent in fiscal
2022, and;

Advisor.

and;

departments as necessary due to open enrollment changes.

Cultural and Community Engagement ARPA physical collection support grant

Section 10-6-128 of the Utah Code states that the budget for the City may be
amended by the Murray City Municipal Council following a duly noticed public hearing.
Pursuant to proper notice, the Murray City Municipal Council held a public hearing on
September 20, 2022, to consider proposed amendments to the Fiscal Year 2022-2023
budget. After considering public comment, the Murray City Municipal Council wants to
amend the Fiscal Year 2022-2023 budget.
