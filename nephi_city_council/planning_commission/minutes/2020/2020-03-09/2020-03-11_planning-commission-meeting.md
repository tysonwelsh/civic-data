Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
                           March 11, 2020

Chairman Greenhalgh opened the meeting at 7:10 p.m.

Opening prayer offered by Skip Worwood.

Commission reviewed minutes of both the public hearing and regular meeting held February 12,
2020. Minutes of each meeting were accepted on a motion by Cory Thomson and a second by Ann
Peterson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Discussion opened regarding public hearing held earlier in the evening involving a zone change
request for North Ridge Estates. Ann Peterson motioned to recommend the approval of zone
change for North Ridge Estates to City Council. Brent Arns seconded. Motion passed with a
unanimous vote.

North Ridge Estates Final Plat was reviewed by Commission, along with notes from DRC. Items such
as fire hydrant locations, utility lines, and a temporary turnaround were discussed. Karl Brough
motioned the approval of North Ridge Estates Final Plat. Ann Peterson seconded. Motion passed
with a unanimous vote.

Chairman Greenhalgh informed Commission members the City Council rejected their
recommendation of ‘acceptable road types’ for RV parks. City Council would like the Commission to
define acceptable road types and remove ‘hard surfacing’ from the ordinance. Ann Peterson
motioned the addition of allowable road types based upon recommendation from a required
geotechnical report. Motion was seconded by Karl Brough. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Commission reviewed Townhome Ordinance. Stewart Lamb would like to build townhomes on two
acres where Nephi Lumber batch plant was once located. He is requesting slightly under 12
units/acre. The ordinance currently allows no more that 8 units/acre. Items such as HOA, set-
backs, density, and driveway length were discussed. No actions were taken.

Planned unit development project on 400 East was discussed.

Chairman Greenhalgh informed Commission a zone change involving property next to Economy
Floors has occurred but no plans have been submitted.

Cory Thomson expressed his disapproval of Planning Commission being wrongly quoted as to
actions taken and/or approvals offered to City Council. Glenn said he would talk to City Council.

Commission discussed industrial park proposal for 100 South 200 West. It is not consistent with the
General Plan therefore no public hearing will be held.

Noting no additional items of business, Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 8:20p.m.
