RESOLUTION NO. 19-05-01                                                    DATE: May 15, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       REQUESTING THE ANNEXATION OF CERTAIN PROPERTY WITHIN THE
       TOWNSHIP TO THE SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT FOR THE PURPOSES
       OF PROVIDING SANITARY SEWER SERVICES TO THE AREA

BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council met in a
public meeting on May 15, 2019.

                                          WITNESS

        WHEREAS, the majority of the land area within the Copperton Metro Township receives
sanitary sewer services from South Valley Sewer District; and

        WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council desires that the all of the land within
 the Township receive sanitary sewer services from the South Valley Sewer District; and

          WHEREAS, state law authorizes the Copperton Metro Township Council to request
 annexation of the property not currently served by the South Valley Sewer District to the Sewer
 District by resolution; and

        WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council desires to respect the wishes of the
 property owners of the noted land proposed for annexation; and

                                               3
DATE          WEDNESDAY                                MAY                              15, 2019


         WHEREAS, barring any protests that may be filed by property owners of the noted land
subject to the proposed annexation, the Copperton Metro Township Council hereby finds that
the public interest will be served by annexation of the noted area to the South Valley Sewer
District.

    NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.      Request for Annexation. The Copperton Metro Township Council hereby
requests the annexation of that certain area more particularly described on Exhibit A, attached
hereto and incorporated herein by reference, to the South Valley Sewer District, for the specific
purpose of the District providing sanitary sewer services to the described area.

       Section 2.      Declaration. The Copperton Metro Township Council hereby finds and
declares that should the District annex the area requested within Exhibit A, the Copperton Metro
Township will be wholly located within the boundaries of the South Valley Sewer District.

        Section 3.    Severability. If any clause, provision, sentence, paragraph or term of this
Resolution or the application thereof, is deemed to be invalid as to any person, entity,
establishment or circumstance, such invalidity shall not affect the other provisions of the
Resolution which shall remain in effect.

       Section 4.     Protests. The Metro Township Council will rescind this petition if any
property owners of the noted land subject to the proposed annexation file a protest.

       Section 5.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective on the 1st day of
June, 2019.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah

Planning Commission Legal Representation


                                               4
DATE           WEDNESDAY                                 MAY                              15, 2019

               Mayor Clayton stated Nathan Brackens law firm was picked through an RFP
process to represent the planning commissions as its land use attorney.

                 Nathan Bracken stated a training was held in April for the metro township planning
commissions. His law firm has five individuals who handle land uses, each of those individuals
will be assigned to a metro township planning commission with the addition of a backup attorney.
The planning commissions will assign a commissioner as a point person to communicate with its
attorney before and after the meetings rather than having the attorney attend every meeting. With
planning and zoning it is important to have a good line of open communication. Each planning
commission will have an attorney who litigates and one who drafts ordinances. The attorneys will
attend their first meetings to introduce themselves.

             Mayor Clayton stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(GSLMSD) will be funding the land use attorneys.

Code Enforcement Process and Ordinance

                Nathan Bracken stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(GSLMSD) hired his law firm as the land use attorneys to help with planning and zoning.
Attorney’s will help the planning commission as it develops ordinances and disputes. There are
currently 37 code enforcement cases in Kearns and Magna Metro Townships that have been
pending for quite some time. The state statute does not allow counties to have the same powers
as municipalities when handling code enforcement cases. If a resident does not come in
compliance, Salt Lake County’s only recourse is a lawsuit. The cases can become drawn out and
costly, his firm is working on a more efficient policy and include a section for more serious code
violations. A draft ordinance has been sent to the GSLMSD Board for review. He would like the
ordinance to be reviewed by all the metro township planning commissions and then to the metro
township councils for approval.

                Council Member Pazell stated code enforcement cases can become a poverty
trap and fines are not the greatest solution. The ordinance should have language allowing citizens
that are on a fixed income while recognizing that there are some bad apples to deal with.
