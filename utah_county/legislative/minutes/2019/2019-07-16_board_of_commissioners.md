            1. PRESENTATION BY JOANNA LANDAU, DIRECTOR OF THE UTAH INDIGENT DEFENSE
            COMMISSION, ON IMPROVEMENTS FOR INDIGENT DEFENSE APPEAL SYSTEMS.

            JOANNA LANDAU/DIRECTOR OF THE UTAH INDIGENT DEFENSE COMMISSION – GAVE A
            PRESENTATION IN IMPROVING THE IMAGE AND DEFENSE APPEAL SYSTEM AND ADDRESSED
            THE CONSEQUENCES OF THE UTAH SUPREME COURT’S DECISION TO REQUIRE THAT
            ANYONE APPOINTED TO REPRESENT AN INDIGENT INDIVIDUAL ON APPEAL BEYOND A
            COURT-APPROVED ROSTER. UTAH COUNTY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY ARE EXEMPT DUE TO
            APPELLATE DIVISIONS. HOPE TO WORK WITH THE UTAH COUNTY APPELLATE DEFENDERS
            ABOUT PARTICIPATING IN CENTRALIZING.
