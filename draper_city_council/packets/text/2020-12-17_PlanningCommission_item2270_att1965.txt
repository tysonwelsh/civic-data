BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.92 acres located at
approximately 1079 E 13015 S (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is
requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the consolidation of Lots 210 and 211 into
one residential building lot.

The Rivermark Phase 2 subdivision plat obtained preliminary approval from the City Council on
November 13, 2018 and final plat approval from the Planning Commission on March 14, 2019. The plat
approved eleven single-family residential lots, each equaling at least 20,000 square feet in size. The
amended plat request would reduce the number of lots from eleven single-family residential lots to ten
single-family residential lots.


ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential Low-Medium
Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is characterized as
follows:

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately two dwelling
unit per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the
purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to promote and
preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals
and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings,
protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses”. The RA2 zoning abuts the subject
property on all four sides.

Subdivision Layout. The applicant is proposing to consolidate Lots 210 and 211 into one lot. The new lot
will be 40,328 square feet in size and comply with the standards of the RA2 zone, including minimum lot
size of 20,000 square feet. With this consolidation, the public utility easements that run along the
common side property line will be removed. This will allow the newly created lot to be fully utilized as a
building lot, wherein a house location is not encumbered by the utility easement. Easements along the
exterior property lines will not be altered. This includes a 20-foot wide storm drainage easement along
the north property line and a 20-foot wide irrigation easement along the east property line. The new lot

          The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing an

          good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has
          been vacated or amended.
