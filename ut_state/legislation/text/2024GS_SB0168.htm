7     LONG TITLE
8     General Description:
9          This bill modifies provisions facilitating affordable housing.
10     Highlighted Provisions:
11          This bill:
12          ▸     defines terms and modifies definitions;
13          ▸     adopts a statewide building code for modular building units;
14          ▸     modifies provisions related to reinvestment fee covenants or transfer fee covenants;
15          ▸     modifies provisions of the First-Time Homebuyer Assistance Program; and
16          ▸     makes technical and conforming changes.

39          Section 1. Section 10-9a-540 is enacted to read:
40          10-9a-540. Modular building.
41          (1) Title 15A, State Construction and Fire Codes Act, governs regulations related to the
42     construction, transportation, installation, inspection, fees, and enforcement related to modular
