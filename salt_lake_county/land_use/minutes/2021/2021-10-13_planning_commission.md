The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Travis Hair  provided an analysis of the 
ordinance amendment. 
 
Mr. Shaw emphasized that the ordinance carries over the work the Salt Lake County Planning Commission 
already did on the detached regulations, brings ordinance into compliance with bill. Commissioner Elieson 
asked if anything in the ordinance going after existing IADUs scattered all over the valley, who have never 
received business license or permits. Mr. Hair said if we become aware, code enforcement will talk to them 
and will work with them to come into compliance and go through the process. Mr. Shaw said that ADU 
required to have a business license renewed every year for tracking. Mr. Hair said ADU owner will record 
documents, and that state law allows County to  put liens on properties until they are compliant. 
Commissioner Cohen said on the Mountainous Planning District Planning Commission  they brought up 
language to correct in  section 19.15.110, subsection B,  ADU D esign Standards, changed to “building 
permit may not be issued”.  
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.
