DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
Mr. Jake Tate, P.E., representing Anderson, Wahlen and Associates Engineering, is requesting
that the Planning Commission review and approve a commercial site plan project to
develop a new commercial building on the south approximate one-half of Lot 1 in the School
Yard Commercial subdivision.  The property is located at approximately 11090 South State
Street, and the site is the currently vacant. This project is proposed as the third phase of the
School Yard Commercial development.  Phases 1 and 2 have been constructed previously. As
part of this review, the CBD zone requires architectural design review by the Planning
Commission, after recommendation by the Sandy City Architectural Design Review
Committee.  An 8,500 square foot commercial shell building is proposed for future occupancy
by commercial and restaurant uses, with the far west end of the proposed building being a drive-
up window restaurant.  A Conditional Use Permit is required by the CBD zone for a drive-

BACKGROUND
The northern approximately one-half of Lot 1 was previously approved by the Planning
Commission in 2018 as Phase 1 of this development.  The Phase 1 project involved the
remodeling of the old school building into a restaurant (Shake Shack in the southern one half of
the school building) and a future retail or restaurant space in the northern half of the school
building.  This site is the southern approximately one-half of Lot 1 and is located at
approximately 11090 South State Street, and is zoned Central Business District, (CBD). From
11000 S. Street, south to 11400 South Street is also zoned CBD.  North of 11000 S. Street is
Auto Mall (Commercial) zoning.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting.  A neighborhood meeting was not held for this project
due to the lack of residential zoning in the area. Due to the projects locaction within the CBD
zoning district, this project was reviewed by the Sandy City Architectural Design Committee in a
meeting held on March 24, 2021. (Please see the attached minutes of that meeting.)

ANALYSIS
Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation.  All street improvements, sidewalks,
landscaped parking strips and internal driveway connections are in place from prior development
of the Subdivision area in conjunction with Phase 1 and Phase 2 projects.  This commercial project
area has frontage along State Street and 11000 South Street.  The project also has a commercal
driveway connection to Auto Mall Drive on the southwest side of the project area.  This driveway
connection will be located along the rear (south side) of the proposed commercial building.  The
front of the commercial building will face north, adjacent to the new parking lot improvements
with this project. This is a shell building for future leasing to probable restaurant and retail users.
The west end of the building is being designed for a future drive-up window restaurant user.  As
such, the required queuing space in the drive-up window driveway is designed to meet City
requirements. Pedestrian access is provided to connect within the larger project area and also a
new sidewalk connection is being extended between this phase and Auto Mall Drive, under a
special lease agreement with Salt Lake City Public Utilities Department, crossing over the Jordan
and Salt Lake Canal property.  Access and on-site circulation will be adequate for the proposed
development.

Zoning Review.  The project will meet all of the requirements of the CBD zoning district and the
Sandy City Development code.  Parking for this project will meet the City requirements on site,
but also have shared parking rights to all other parking stalls in the commercial center
development.  All required City Departments and Divisions have reviewed and recommend
approval, subject to their usual requirements and conditions.  A drive-up window restaurant is a
Conditional Use in the CBD zoning district.

Architectural Design Review.  The building's architectural design, materials and colors were
reviewed by the Sandy Planning Staff and then presented to the Sandy City Architectural Design
Review Committee in a meeting held on Marh 24, 2021.  The review considered compliance with
the Sandy City Architectural Design Requirements.  The building architectural design, materails
and colors will be true to those used for the Phase 2 building, located east of this site and south of
the old school building fronting State Street.  The front of the building and the entrances and the
primary windows are oriented toward the north and the new parking lot.  Doing this helps orient
the new building to the center of the development. This is acceptable with the project being located
outside of the Cairn's Design District.

The proposed building meets the Architectural Design standards, except for the percentage of
stucco/EIFS material exceeding the 20 percent limit on the south (back) side of the building along
the connection driveway.   The south building elevation is proposed at 33 percent being
stucco/EIFS.  After explanation by the project architect, the Sandy City Architectural Design
Review Committee recommended that the Planning Commission appprove an exception to the 20
percent limit for the south building elevation due to the building orientation and location along the
driveway access, low off-site visibility and an overall building exterior percentage of stucco/EIFS
being less that 20 percent.  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the building
architectural design, materials and colors as now proposed by the applicant, including the
exception for the percentage of stucco/EIFS on the south side elevation.


STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that  preliminary site plan
review is complete for the School Yard, Phase 3, project, and approve the building
architectural design, material and colors as proposed by the applicant, based on this staff
report, and the three findings listed below and subject to the following three
conditions:

FINDINGS:
    A. That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer and the
       Transportation Engineer, have preliminarily approved the proposed site plan, subject to the
       City Department's requirements and conditions.

    B. That the proposed Phase 3 building will match the design of the adjacent Phase 2 building.

    C. That allowing one of the four building elevations to exceed the 20 percent limit on
       stucco/EIFS will not be detrimental to the project or the CBD zoning district.

4:00 p.m.    The School Yard Phase 3 Building (11020 South State Street) Jake
             Tate, Parker Robertson, and Colton Burton

Doug introduced everyone and gave a brief history of The School Yard project.
He explained that we are currently working on Phase 3 of the project, which is
located closer to Auto Mall Drive than State Street. It is about 8,000 sq. ft. and
includes a future restaurant/drive up window on the site plan.

Jake Tate explained they will start working on Building C, which is a multi-tenant,
retail/restaurant building. They are trying to work with the Canal Company to
get permission to put in a pedestrian sidewalk on the north side of the canal
bridge structure. Currently, they cannot guarantee they will get access. To get
signage for the back building, they put Building C on the same lot as the Shake
Shack. One of the issues they have and are aware of, is the constraints they
have with the parking. Trash has been an issue and they have received
approval from Planning Commission to put more trash enclosures in. Also, Phase
3 will be connecting into the existing storm water from Phase 1.

Lyle asked what will be put on the lot between Building C and State Street.

Parker Robertson stated he cannot disclose who those tenants are, but they are
two tenants with small building footprints and low parking needs.

Jake explained that they are going to continue to use the landscaping design
from the past phases for the Phase 3 project. Trees and shrubs will be put in the
landscaping islands. The only place they will have turf and sod is up against the
canal.

Colton Burton explained they took a lot of the elements from building B to tie
into Building C. He noted that the front view has storefront entrances for four
tenants with a drive-thru on the west side.


--- Page 6 ---
Doug said that the percentage of the stucco is showing 33% on the plans and
20% is the maximum. He continued to say that it might not make a huge
difference because it is seldom seen.

Parker said from the developer side of things, the costs keep going up and
wants this project to tie in with everything else. He wants to ask this committee
for a positive recommendation because he feels they are meeting all the
requirements of the design standards.

Lyle feels that the developer meets the requirements of the renderings.

Colton said that another thing they thought about for the rear side of the
building, was to use different elements like windows. They ended up not doing
this due to cost and said that tenants usually end up blocking those windows
with equipment anyways.

Doug asked if the windows on the south side would be transparent or spandrel?

Colton said they would be transparent just like Building B.

James spoke on behalf of Kris Nicholl and said she thought the building looks
good. She was not concerned with the extra stucco, where it is not as visible,
and recommends it going to Planning Commission.

Daniel said from a Planning Commission perspective, those changes would be
fine.

Motion to Vote:

Cheryl Bottorff made a motion to recommend approval to the Planning
Commission with this amount of stucco given that the whole building is less than
20%, and to approve the rest of the project. Lyle Beecher seconded. All in favor
of the motion.
