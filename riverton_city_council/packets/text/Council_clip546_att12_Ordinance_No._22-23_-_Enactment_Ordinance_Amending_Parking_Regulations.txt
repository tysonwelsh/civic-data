                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 22-23

               AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE SECTIONS
                            10.10.010 THROUGH 10.10.030
                             [PARKING REGULATIONS]

         WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-8-84, Riverton City provides for the safety of the
public and preserves and promotes the health and safety of the public by passage of ordinances to provide
for the prosperity, morals, peace, good order, comfort and convenience of Riverton City and its inhabitants;
and

         WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-60, Riverton City may “declare what shall be a
nuisance, and abate the same, and impose fines upon persons who may create, continue, or suffer nuisances
to exist;” and

        WHEREAS, under general statutory authority of Utah Code, Title 10, Chapter 3, Riverton City
has adopted a comprehensive set of ordinances for the purpose of regulating the operation of motor vehicles
within the territorial jurisdiction of Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the current Riverton Municipal
Code needs to be updated to: 1) prohibit parking of unregistered or inoperable vehicles on public streets;
and 2) provide additional enforcement remedies within its parking ordinances; and

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.      The following Code Sections are hereby amended and adopted as found in Exhibit
                A, attached hereto:

                Title 10, Chapter 10, Sections 010, 020, and 030

Section 2.      This ordinance shall take effect immediately.


       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of October,
