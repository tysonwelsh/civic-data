                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 24-27

AN ORDINANCE REZONING APPROXIMATELY 0.91-ACRES LOCATED AT 4191
WEST 13400 SOUTH FROM R-1 (RESIDENTIAL 1-ACRE MIN) TO C-R (REGIONAL
         COMMERCIAL). APPLICANT- JAY AND SHELLY ROBERTS

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map from R-1 to Commercial Regional (C-R) for the
approximately 0.91 acre parcel located at 4191 West 13400 South.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is,
                           amended to reflect the described changes as shown in
                           Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.             This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 3rd day of
December 2024 by the following vote:
