City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On September 6, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1559, amending the
Chapter 3 of the Draper City Municipal Code. The complete ordinance is on file at
the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this
7th day of September 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
