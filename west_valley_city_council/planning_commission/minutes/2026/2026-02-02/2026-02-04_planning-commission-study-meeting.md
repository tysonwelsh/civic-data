PRESENTATIONS:

S-1-2026      Chris Toronto is requesting preliminary approval for the Daybury North
Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 43 lots on 12.85 acres. The property is located at 5332
W Highbury Pkwy and is zoned R-1-8. Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Brock presented the application. The proposal includes 43 lots on 12.85 acres zoned R-1-8,
with lot sizes ranging from 8,100 to 19,000 square feet. Brock recommended staff propose a wrought
iron-style fence along the waterway and consistent Trex fencing for the lots, aligning with the
surrounding development. The development agreement from 2012 requires single-family homes only
and 8,000 square-foot lots, with current standards applying requiring only 1,000 square foot homes with
3-car garages. Commissioner Durfee asked if all of the lots are large enough to accommodate 3-car
garages and meet minimum lot frontage requirements. Brock confirmed that they are. Commissioner
Drozdek inquired if the plans will for high-end or luxury homes like another development nearby and
Brock clarified that while current standards are minimal, the development agreement doesn't specify
home size or architecture. Steve Pastorik added on a separate property near Monticello Academy, which
was previously considered for larger lots and homes but was never finalized. The applicant is now
looking to resurrect this project and have it finalized alongside the recently reviewed RE zone
application, aiming to keep both properties zoned RE.
SA-1-2026     Meridian Engineering, representing the Utah Department of Transportation, is
requesting vacating Lots 1-9, a portion of 5750 West Street & a portion of 3575 South Street of
Jonesdale Subdivision, vacating Lot 210 & a portion of 5750 West Street of Jonesdale Subdivision
No. 2, vacating Lots 301-303, a portion of 5750 West Street & a portion of Darle Avenue (3610
South) of Jonesdale Subdivision No. 3, vacating Lots 10-14, a portion OF 5750 West Street & a
portion of Darle Avenue (3610 South) of Jonesdale Subdivision No. 4, Vacating Lots 601-610, a
portion of 5750 West Street & a portion of Masters Street (5770 West) of Jonesdale Subdivision
No. 6, vacating Lots 811, 818-828 & a portion of Masters Street (5770 West) of Jonesdale
Subdivision No. 8. The property is located at 3565 S 5750 W and is zoned R-1-8. Staff Brock
Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.

Discussion: Brock presented the request from UDOT to clean up the Jonesdale Subdivision project
along the Mountain View Corridor, where several platted lots that were previously developed will be
consolidated into one large parcel. Brock explained that three roads and existing rights-of-way would be
vacated, requiring council approval, and noted that the area is already mostly developed as freeway. He
also noted that Lot 9A will stay reserved for a trail parking area. Brock did receive a few calls from
residents who were mailed Notice and their concerns were resolved when he explained to them that no
changes will be made to the existing development. Commissioner Durfee asked if UDOT plans to
maintain the properties. Brock confirmed they are and will continue to, noting that most of the right-of-
way areas are already part of existing developments.



There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting was
adjourned at 3:46 PM.
