                 NOTICE OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING OF THE
                     CITY OF HOLLADAY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                                THURSDAY, MAY 7, 2026

                                 Holladay Council Chambers - 3330 S 1300 E


PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Holladay Redevelopment Agency will hold a
meeting on Thursday, May 7, 2026 as close to 6:00 pm as possible. A member of the Board may
participate by electronic means. The Council Chambers shall serve as the anchor location. NOTE: State
Law requires that the City Council members constitute the Board of the Redevelopment Agency.

All documents that are available to the RDA Board are also available on the City’s website or are linked in this
agenda. Interested parties are encouraged to watch the Live video stream of the meeting –
http://cityofholladay.com/government/elected-officials/meetings-and-agendas/

Persons desiring to make public comments or to make comments during any public hearing may provide such
comments as follows:
1.      In-person attendance: at Holladay City Hall
2.      Email your comments by 5:00 pm on the date of the meeting to scarlson@holladayut.gov



                                                          AGENDA

1.        Call to Order –Chairman Gray

5.        Adjourn RDA Meeting

                                  RESOLUTION RDA NO. 2026-03

                A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE
                REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF THE CITY OF HOLLADAY
                ACCEPTING AND TENTATIVELY ADOPTING THE 2026-2027
                AGENCY TENTATIVE BUDGET
      WHEREAS, State law requires that the Agency adopt a budget for the receipt and use of
Agency funds; and
       WHEREAS, the Agency Board desires to accept a tentative budget to provide a period of
study and comment on the eventual Final Budget for the Agency for the operating year 2026-2027;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF THE
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF THE CITY OF HOLLADAY as follows:
         (1) That the FY 2026-2027 Tentative Budget submitted by the Agency Director to the
             Board is hereby accepted for further consideration by the Board.
         (2) That a public hearing to consider the proposed Final Budget be set for June 4, 2026, at
             6:30 p.m., or as soon thereafter as possible, at the Temporary City of Holladay Offices,
             3330 S 1300 E., Millcreek, Utah 84106.

       ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of the Redevelopment Agency of the City of Holladay,
Utah this __ day of May, 2026.
