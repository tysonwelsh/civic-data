                                                    Riverton City
                                               Notice of Public Hearing

Riverton City Council has determined it is necessary to transfer $2,503,275 from the Culinary Water fund to the
Secondary Water fund in order to write-off a debt between them. This debt was created by transfers from fiscal year
2006 through fiscal year 2018, used to pay debt service for the water revenue bonds. Utah Code 10-6-135 requires that
the City notify all utility customers of this transfer. A public hearing will be held on June 16, 2020, at 7:00 pm for the
2021 fiscal year. Due to restrictions in place related to COVID-19, this meeting will not be open for public attendance.
Audio and video of the meeting will be broadcast live and will be available following the meeting. Public comment for
these items may be submitted via email to recorder@rivertonutah.gov. All comments submitted by Noon on the date of
the hearing will be relayed to the City Council.
