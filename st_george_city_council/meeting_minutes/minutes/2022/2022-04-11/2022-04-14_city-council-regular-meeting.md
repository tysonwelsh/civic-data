LETTER OF INTENT:
     Consider approval of the corporate Letter of Intent (property purchase)
     between Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. and the City of St. George.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. has delivered to the
      City, a corporate Letter of Intent setting forth the intent of the Bank to sell certain
      real property to the City for a price currently being negotiated. The Agreement sets
      forth the general terms of the transaction (subject to a final purchase agreement).
      If the general terms are acceptable to the City, the City Council can approve and
      authorize the Mayor to countersign the Letter of Intent as a pre-cursor to the final
      purchase agreement. Staff recommends that the Council discuss the Letter of Intent

PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of the preliminary plat for Goodboro Townhomes, a 73-lot
     residential subdivision located within the Desert Color development just
     north of the roundabout at the intersection of Desert Color Parkway and
     Lagoon Parkway.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project is located within the Desert
      Color development. The general plan designation on this project is TC (Town Center)
      and the current zoning is PD-R - TNZ (Planned Development Residential - Traditional
      Neighborhood Zone - Resort). A zone change amendment for this project was
      approved on April 7, 2022. At its meeting held on March 8, 2022, the Planning
      Commission recommended approval.
