BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Final Subdivision Plat for approximately 11.91
acres located, at approximately 425 West 11400 South (Exhibit B). The property is currently
zoned R4. The applicant is requesting that a Final Subdivision Plat be approved to allow for
the development of 37 new single-family residential lots.

This property is part of the overall Big Willow Creek development. This development
originally obtained approval in January 2017 for 115 dwelling units on 49.75 acres. The
approval included a Development Agreement limiting the density to a maximum of 2.3
du/ac (dwelling units per acre), and requires a variety of lot sizes from 20,000 square feet to
10,000 square feet in size. Preliminary plat approval for all 115 lots was approved by the
Planning Commission on July 18, 2017. Phases 1A and 1B obtained final plat approval on
September 18, 2018, with an amended plat approved for these phases on November 21,

2019.

In April 2019 the applicant amended the development agreement (1st Amendment
Development Agreement) in order to add 18.1 acres of property into the Big Willow Creek
Development. The 1st Amendment of the Development Agreement rezoned some of the
additional 18.1 acres of property to the RM2 zone, but limited any development to a
maximum of 9 du/ac. The remainder of the property was rezoned to R4.

On December 10, 2019 the applicant obtained a rezone and land use amendment for an
additional 4.12 acres of land located adjacent to the development. This area was not
included in the development agreements at the time of rezone. On May 5, 2020, the
applicant obtained approval to amend the Development Agreement a second time (2nd
Amendment Development Agreement). The agreement added the 4.12 acres of property
that was rezoned on December 10, 2019 into the boundary of the agreement, set density
standards for development on the additional properties added in the 1st and 2nd
agreements, and set standards for the approval and development of Phase 2.

The applicant obtained approval for final subdivision plat for Phase 2 on August 27, 2020.
They also obtained approval for a minor subdivision in the Phase 6 area on April 7, 2021 to
reconfigure the access road from 11400 South. The road currently runs through Phase 1A
and 1B, but will be redirected through Phase 6. Phase 1b obtained approval to reconfigure
the existing roadway into a cul-de-sac on April 22, 2021. Phase 1A was vacated by the City
Council on April 6, 2020.

The development agreement (3rd Amendment) was amended on April 20, 2021 which sets
the standards for approval of the Phase 3. A copy of this agreement can be found as
Exhibit F of this report.



ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:

The property has been assigned the R4 zoning classification, supporting approximately four
dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section
9-8-020 the purpose of the R4 zone is to “permit medium to medium-high density residential
developments with single-family homes. These zones are intended to foster thriving and well
maintained neighborhoods that allow upscale single-family homes on smaller lots that require
less maintenance and provide a balanced lifestyle.” The R4 zone abuts the subject property on
the north, south, and west sides. To the east is the A5 (Agricultural, 5 acre lot minimum)
zone and the Frontrunner rail line.

Subdivision Layout. The proposed plat will add 37 new lots into the Big Willow Creek
development (Exhibit E). Per the original Development Agreement, lot will have a minimum
of 10,000 square feet. The proposed plat complies with this standard. The development will
connect to three existing stub streets within Phases 1B and 2, and create one new stub
road to the development to the south, Jensen Farms. All of the roadways are proposed to
be public and comply with the 60-foot width cross section standard for local roads.

3rd Amendment Development Agreement. The Big Willow Creek development has one access
point from 11400 South and a second access point to the south through the Jenson Farms
development. The Jenson Farms connection has been delayed pending the construction of
a bridge over Willow Creek. This has restricted Big Willow Creek to the 30 lots that were



Big Willow Creek Phase 3                                           App. # SUBD-068-2021
Final Subdivision Plat Request               3
approved with the Phase 1B plat as it only has one access until the bridge is built. In
anticipation of the bridge crossing obtaining approval later this year, and to help facilitate
construction of Phase 3, the applicant obtained a 3rd amendment to the Development
Agreement (Exhibit F). This amendment allows the applicant to obtain approval for Phase 3
and begin construction of the public infrastructure before the bridge in Jenson Farms is
completed. It does set specific standards as to when the plat can be recorded and does not
allow the Phase 3 plat to be recorded, or any building permits to be applied for, until the
bridge construction drawings are approved and a bond posted for the work, or the bridge
has been constructed and accepted by the City. This will allow the applicant to work on the
installation of utilities and the roads while the bridge is constructed. The intent is that once
the bridge is constructed, the applicant will be ready to record the plat and pull permits.

Criteria For Approval. The criteria for review and potential approval of a final subdivision
plat request is found in Section 17-4-060 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of
review for such requests as:

         “Within a reasonable time following the recommended approval of the final plat by the
         Zoning Administrator, the final plat shall be submitted to the Planning Commission for its
         review and consideration. A courtesy notice may be provided for the meeting in
         accordance with section 17-1-085 of this title, but is not required. The Planning
         Commission shall not be bound by the recommendations of the Zoning Administrator
         and may set its own conditions and requirements consistent with this title. If the Planning
         Commission determines that the final plat is in conformity with the requirements of this
         title, other applicable ordinances, and any reasonable conditions as recommended by
         the City's staff and Zoning Administrator or on the Planning Commission's own initiative,
         and that the Planning Commission is satisfied with the final plat, it may approve the final
         plat. If the Planning Commission determines that the final plat is not in conformity with
         this title or other applicable ordinances or any reasonable conditions imposed, it may
         disapprove the final plat specifying the reasons for such disapproval. No final plat shall
         have any force or effect unless the same has been approved by the Planning Commission
         and signed by the Mayor and City Recorder.”

              Division. Informational comment – no additional action is required prior to final
              subdivision approval.
       2.     Any work in a public right-of-way will require an Encroachment Permit
              obtained through the Engineering Division. Informational comment – no
              additional action is required prior to final subdivision approval.
       3.     Extend existing 15-foot SD easement on lot 322 to public ROW. This is an
              existing city SD pipeline that is required to be protected in place.
       4.     All utilities require a minimum of three-foot bury depth, specifically SD and PI.
              Culinary waterlines require a minimum depth of four feet. Verify SD and PI
              meet the minimum bury depth requirement. PI at station 13+77.56 9.50’ R

              sections of the PI may be required to be looped under SD pipeline.

              placement of ADA ramp. As noted, this will impact the house and driveway layout.
              This note is informational –no additional action is required prior to final
              subdivision approval.
