4.   Public hearing and possible action on a proposed two (2) lot minor subdivision; 
70 East 2100 South; NS-170 and NS-212; Sheldon Smith, applicant. – Jennifer Strader, 
Senior Planner 
 
Planner Strader shared the proposal to create two new lots on an existing lot of record. 
There are two parcels located in Wanship. The zoning is Agricultural Protection with a 
small portion being Highway Corridor. The parcels are NS-170 and NS-212. NS-212 has 
been bisected by I-80 with an eleven acre piece on the west side of I-80 and a six acre piece 
on the east side. Because of this, the parcel on the east side is a lot of record, the parcel on 
the west side is a lot of record and NS-170 is a lot of record. There are two existing houses, 
one on NS-170 and one on NS-212. The proposal is to split NS-212 into one additional lot. 
There will be a total of four lots within the subdivision. The applicant has placed all of the 
lots on the west side of I-80. The lot on the east side would not have any density associated 
with it right now. There are access easements to be able to access all four lots. This 
proposal has been reviewed by all of the service providers. There are no outstanding 
Conditions of Approval they have requested. Staff has found the proposal complies with all 
of the applicable standards in the development code. Water will be provided by wells. 
Septic systems will be utilized and have been reviewed and approved by the Eastern 
Summit County Water Conservancy District. Staff recommends ESCPC conduct a public 
hearing and forward a positive recommendation to the Community Development Director. 
Commissioner Willoughby asked for a clarification on the issue of no density on the east 
parcel (Parcel A). Planner Strader answered right now it is a lot of record so it has one unit 
of density associated with it. Once the subdivision plat is created the lots will all be created
