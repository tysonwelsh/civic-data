Herriman, Utah
Ordinance No. 2022-19

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2 OF THE HERRIMAN CODE OF
ORDINANCES BY ADDING CHAPTER 11, ENTITLED
DIVERSITY INCLUSION ADVISORY BOARD

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (“Council”) met in regular session on April 27,
2022, to consider, among other things, amending Title 2 of the Herriman Code of Ordinances by
adding Chapter 11 entitled, Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council finds that it is in the best interest of
the health, safety, and welfare of the inhabitants of Herriman to amend Title 2 of the Herriman
Code of Ordinances by adding Chapter 11 entitled, Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council that Title 2 of the Herriman
Code of Ordinances be amended by adding Chapter 11 as follows:

Chapter 11
Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board

2-11-1: Creation

There is hereby created a Herriman Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board.

2-11-2: Duties and Responsibilities

The Herriman Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board shall act in an advisory and voluntary
capacity to suggest to the City Council ideas for additional opportunities for engagement
and inclusion among the diverse residents and communities within the municipal
boundaries of Herriman City. The specific duties and responsibilities of the Diversity
Inclusion Advisory Board will be as set forth in the Committee bylaws, which shall be
adopted by the City Council, and which may be amended by the Council from time to
time.

2-11-3: Terms, Compensation, Removal, and Vacancy

The Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board shall be comprised of at least three (3) and no
more than nine (9) members, appointed by the City Council. Members may serve a
maximum of three (3) consecutive terms of three (3) years each. A mid-term vacancy
may be filled by the council for the unexpired term or for a full term (three years) at the
discretion of the City Council. Members may be removed at any time and without cause
upon a majority vote of the City Council. Members shall also serve without
compensation.

2-10-4: Organization and Quorum

A. Organization. The Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board may elect a chair,
organize sub-committees, and adopt administrative rules or procedures
to better accomplish its purpose. Any such rules or procedures,
including bylaws, shall be submitted to the City Council for review and
approval.

B. Quorum. More than fifty percent of the Diversity Inclusion Advisory

Board present shall constitute a quorum for purposes of discussing
items related to the Committee.

2-10-5: Limitations

The Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board shall not have authority to bind the City or
impose any conditions on the City or its citizens.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 27" day of April, 2022.

HERRIMAN CITY COUNCIL
