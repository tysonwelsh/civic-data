3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of CORE Architecture’s request for review of an
amendment to the Skye Area Plan adding design requirements for Civic Land Uses.

Applicant Curtis Miner did a presentation to show why they are requesting to amend some of the
aspects of the Skye area plans and requirements for Civic Land uses.

Commissioners asked the applicant clarifying questions.

Chairman Jackson opened public hearing.
Chairmen Jackson closed public hearing.

Commissioners asked several clarifying questions including questions about property ownership
and distinctions between a temple and a potential chapel. Mr. Miner clarified that the church
owns both properties. The commissioners expressed concerns about the lack of differentiation in
the document between the temple and chapel standards, especially regarding lighting and site
plans suggesting adding language to distinguish the two. Kim Struthers proposed defining
standards by building size. Mr. Miner agreed, stating that they could separate elements to ensure
clarity.

Further discussions involved practical considerations for site planning, including tree planting
requirements, bike stalls, and lighting standards. Some commissioners expressed concerns about
customizing standards too much, which could exclude other civic uses. They also discussed bike
parking, noting the importance of encouraging biking despite current habits.

In summary, the commissioners are working to ensure clear, distinct standards for the temple and
chapel, while considering practical site planning needs and maintaining flexibility for other civic
uses.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to table the of CORE Architecture’s request for
        review of an amendment to the Skye Area Plan adding design requirements for Civic
        Land Uses; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report with the
        following suggestions including that they retool some of the items discussed- changes
        being made of two classifications of the two buildings and what applies to each building
        i.e. square footage. They also suggest looking at lighting, bike parking, tree
        requirement/water-wise landscaping, parking, and to formally add any request and any
        other clarification that could be useful. Commissioner Lockhart seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
