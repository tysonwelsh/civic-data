Salt Lake County Urban County Community Development Block Grant Program

                 Paul Ashton stated White City Metro Township has previously opted into the Salt
Lake County Urban County Community Development Block Grant Program (CDBG). Every three
years the Metro Township has to authorize its participation. If the Council wants to continue to
participate in the program, it will need to authorize the Mayor to sign the interlocal agreement.

             Council Member Shelton, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to authorize
the Mayor to sign the interlocal agreement to participate in the CDBG Program. The motion
passed unanimously.

Form of Government Legislation

                Paul Ashton stated attorneys for the metro townships have been meeting on a
weekly basis to discuss various issues. The attorneys are recommending that the metro
townships approach the legislature, specifically the Political Subdivision Interim Committee, to
pass a statute that would automatically reclassify the metro townships as a city or town, based on
population. A letter is being drafted that each mayor will sign. There have been discussions with
Representative Jordan Teuscher, and he has agreed to sponsor a bill. If White City Metro
Township becomes a formal city, it will increase its ability to obtain funding. A provision will be
written in the bill to allow metro townships to remain members of the districts it currently belongs
to.

Title 18 (Subdivision) & 19 (Zoning) Ordinance Amendment

               Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(MSD), stated draft codes were presented to the planning commission, and a public hearing was
held at the end of April. She reviewed comments from the public hearing as well as emails she
received. She reviewed the following items with the Council:

   -   Allow pavers to be an exception from the impervious surface calculation for parking
       standards for residential lots in the off-street parking.
   -   Individuals using a private road and blocking access. There is nothing in the code that can
       regulate access to the road.
   -   How standards are set for the intensity of use for agricultural uses.
   -   Increase the maximum allowed lot coverage to 40% for all buildings on a residential lot.
   -   Allow Reiki as a permitted use in zones where commercial uses are allowed.
   -   Remove duplex as a permitted use in the A-2 zone.
   -   Allow IADU in the A-2 zone.
   -   Miniature horses.
   -   Guest or caretaker house in the A-2 zone.
   -   Add horse stables within 20 ft of a dwelling.
   -   Landscaping business and related traffic.
   -   Add standards for the parking of commercial vehicles in the A-2 zone.
   -   Add language in the landscaping, screening, and water efficiency chapter to be fully
       compliant with the State of Utah requirements.

                    SCOPE OF WORK - MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATOR


                                WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP

     GENERAL OVERVIEW
Under the direction of the Mayor, shall provide Municipal Administrator support to promote efficient and
effective work for the Council and Metro Township. Shall interface on behalf of the Mayor and Council
with the providers of municipal-type services to White City and offer guidance and communicate
administrative standards as it applies to the delivery of those services. Under the direction of the Mayor,
may work collaboratively with federal, state, county, other agencies, and community organizations for
the mutual benefit of the Metro Township.

EXAMPLES OF DUTIES TO BE PERFORMED UNDER SCOPE OF WORK
•   Advises the Council on a variety of Metro Township issues and assists in developing policies to
    address identified issues as directed by the Mayor and Council.
•   Coordinates with legal staff to perform paralegal work and provides administrative
    recommendations to ordinances and resolutions deemed necessary and appropriate for the best
    interest of the Metro Township.
•   Develop, plan, and communicate Council-approved Metro Township goals and objectives to the
    municipal service providers and the public.
•   Under the direction of the Mayor, represents the interest of the Metro Township before federal,
    state, county, other governmental agencies, and community organizations.
•   Provides administrative guidance and communicates expected community standards of service to
    the municipal service providers performing work for the benefit of the Metro Township.
•   Coordinates with Council fiscal staff, MSD fiscal staff, and Mayor to develop the Metro Township’s
    Annual Budget and provides guidance to the Council as to the administration of the Metro Township
    Budget.
•   Performs special projects for the Council on an “as-requested” basis.
•   Make recommendations to the Mayor and Council on hiring contract personnel to complete
    administrative tasks (i.e., cemetery administration, information technology, etc.)
•   Prepare agendas and meeting packets including creating ordinances, resolutions, and other items
    for consideration by the Council.
•   If appointed by the Council, may function as a Hearing Officer for certain matters.
•   Assists the Chair during public meetings as the parliamentarian to the Council.
•   Manages municipal election and coordinates with Salt Lake County, publishes required notices and
    reports.
•   Maintains City website and social media.
•   Attends all regular and special meetings of the Council as required.

   WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.; and
   WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for White City pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-501; and
   WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council has determined it is within the best
interest of the residents of the White City Metro Township to request the Greater Salt Lake

    Salt Lake Engineering Staff, presently providing services to the Metro Townships, to the MSD,
    and

    amend pertinent interlocal agreements needed to accomplish this transition.
