B. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-11-02 2026 Budgets: All Programs and Component Units 
Sara made a motion to approve the 2026 Budgets: All Programs and Component Units 
from the regular board meeting. Suzanne seconded the request. The motion passed 
unanimously. 
 
HAUC oversees the operations of three programs: Business Activities, Section 8, and 
Continuum of Care. HAUC also operates three component units and two sub-properties: 
Cedar View, Pacific Drive, Rosewood, Spring Lake, and Bridgeway.  The three programs and 
four components are each accounted for in their own defined set of books/financials.  
    
For purposes of approval, the 2026 budget presentation will consist of three programs, three 
component units, and two sub-properties presented individually while approval is under one 
resolution (2025-11-02).  Furthermore, the budget presentation will provide the line-item detail, 
however, the board will approve the aggregate section subtotal amounts.  The need for 
revised budget(s) in the future will be determined when the subtotal section amount is 
exceeded or will exceed the approved amount.  Note, the format of the budgets in 2026 
contains additional line items from the 2025 budgets due to HAUC switching accounting 
software in 2025.    
 
HAUC staff consisting of the Executive Director, Accounting Manager and Operations Director 
presented the proposed 2026 budgets to the Board.  Discussion ensued regarding the 
budgets.  An additional budget line was added to Business Activities for capital updates for 
portfolio units’ at the Executive Director’s discretion and pursuant to existing HAUC policy. All
