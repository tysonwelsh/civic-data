Re: Harmony Hills Assisted Living – Amended Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an Amended Site Plan for approximately 3.07 acres located on the north side of Pioneer Rd, at approximately 1393 East Pioneer Rd. An aerial of the property can be found as Exhibit B of this report. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Site Plan  be approved to allow for the modification of the sidewalk and parkstrip configuration. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. The entire site is being developed at one time.
2. The site plan conforms to applicable standards set forth in DCMC Title 9, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3. The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
4. The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5. The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 4/16/2020 Cover Memo
