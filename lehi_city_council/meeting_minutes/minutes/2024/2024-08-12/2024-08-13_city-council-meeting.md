Pre Council, 6:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1) Lehi Fiber Network Update
Shaye Ruitenbeek, Lehi Fiber Network Manager, provided an update. She reported that pre-
registrations for the fiber service had almost reached 9,500, which was close to 45% of the
projected take rate. She noted that customer subscriptions had reached a 23% overall take rate for
the areas that were open for sign-ups, hitting almost 2,000 subscriptions.
Mrs. Ruitenbeek explained that 19 areas, about 33% of the city, had been opened for sign-ups,
47% were under construction, and 20% of the city was yet to be opened, mostly on the west side.
When asked, Shay said she would look into providing more specific projections on when
different areas would be opened for sign-ups, as this was a common question from residents. She
acknowledged this would require coordinating with the construction team to determine how
certain they were about timelines for each area.

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
Dean Lundell, Finance Director, stated that the public hearing for the property tax increase will
be on August 20th. He explained that Utah property tax is governed by state code, which has
changed over time. He noted that a key term is the "certified tax rate", which is calculated

annually by the state and county assessors. The intent is for the certified rate to provide the city
with the same revenue as the previous year, not counting new growth.

Mr. Lundell reviewed Lehi's actual tax rate over the past several years. He explained that the
proposed rate for 2024 is higher than the certified rate, which would be considered a tax increase
under state law. He outlined Lehi's policy objectives when setting the tax rate, such as ensuring it
is reasonable compared to other cities and providing high quality services. He noted the city
typically aims to recapture inflation, rather than exceed it, when proposing a tax increase.

The primary reasons cited for the proposed tax increase were to address public safety salary
competitiveness and parks staff for park and facilities maintenance.

5. Consideration of Ordinance #49-2024 adopting Lehi City Municipal Code Title 5
   Chapter 6 (Hyperbaric Facilities) establishing requirements for the operation of a
   hyperbaric facility.
   Garion Rowett, Fire Marshal, presented this item to the Council. He stated that this Ordinance

   address safety concerns.
