                              NOTICE OF PUBLIC MEETING

                    COPPERTON METRO PLANNING COMMISSION

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code Title 10, Chapter 2, Section 401.5, that the
Copperton Metro Township Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”) will hold a
public meeting on February 15, 2022, beginning at 6:00 p.m. for the purpose of allowing affected
entities, as defined in Utah Code Title 10, Chapter 2, Section 401(1), to examine a proposed
annexation policy plan for Copperton (“Annexation Policy Plan”). Affected entities are invited
to attend the public meeting to provide input on the Annexation Policy Plan. If an affected entity
or member of the public is unable to attend the public meeting, they may submit written comments
on or before Friday, February 18, 2022.

The Planning Commission will later schedule a public hearing on the Annexation Policy Plan to
be held after February 18, 2022.

Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(5), Ranuta Alder has determined in her capacity as Chair
of the Copperton Planning Commission that this meeting and/or public hearing will be held
electronically without an anchor location given the ongoing Coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-
19) pandemic, which the Chair has determined to present a substantial risk to the health and safety
of those who may be present at an anchor location. To participate in the meeting and to make
public comments where indicated in the agenda, please use the below webinar instructions.

You may also obtain copies of the Annexation Policy Plan and submit written comments by
contacting Wendy Gurr at wgurr@msd.utah.gov or (385) 468-6707. Copies of the Annexation
Policy Plan are also available on the Utah Public Notice Website. Individuals needing special
accommodations (including ancillary communicative aids and services) during this public meeting
should contact Ms. Gurr three days before the public meeting.
