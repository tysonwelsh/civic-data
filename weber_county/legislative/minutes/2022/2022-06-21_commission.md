In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

F. PRESENTATION
      Recognition of the Ogden Musical Theater Advisory Board for their outstanding service.
      Kassi Bybee (Director, OECC): Ogden Musical Theater started in 2016 when Commissioner Ebert asked us
      to bring live musical theater back to Peery’s musical stage. Some said it would take 18 months to 2 years
      before you can actually start your first musical. But we started that August. That has progressed to us
      having a full season with spring, summer, and fall shows. The Board would like to see that continue to grow.

            Commissioner Harvey: One thing that sets us apart from most counties is having a great theater with so
            many qualified, talented people that care about perpetuating theater that care about their community. You
            have made an impact in getting sponsorships to help with lighting and seating and getting help from this
            Commission. Your legacy will matter to the grandchildren of our grandchildren. You are a spark plug for
            this effort.

            Kassi Bybee: You surround yourself with good people and they just buoy you up. It’s all possible because
            of the people that are here making it happen; I’m only a conduit to help move things forward. They sacrifice
            to help as they all have lives outside of the theater. Let’s introduce the people who make it happen:

            Jolene Zitto, former OMT Vice Chair
            Pam Higginson, OMT Chair
            Karen Bristol, current Vice Chair
            Rhett Long, Chair
            Pam Parkinson, serves on Board for Hospitality
            Lori West, Kids Act Up program and other children-focused efforts
            Annette DeFries, Hospitality
            Ernest Gonzalez, has served from the very beginning of OMT due to the positive influence theater had on his
            children.
            Sean Myers, newest member of the Board.
