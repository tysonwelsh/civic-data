                                                4:00 p.m. Work Session
                                                  September 15, 2020

The purpose of the joint work session includes presentations and discussions regarding:
    • Agenda review of the City Council meeting;
    • Nine Rails Art Plaza Update;
    • CARES Act Grant Funding:
        • Fiscal Year 2021 Budget Amendment
        • Interlocal Agreement with Weber County;
    • Apartment Design Standards in Commercial Zones;
    • Council business; and
    • Council comments.

Information Item:
     • Ogden City Water Production and Distribution


                            Any items not fully addressed prior to the City Council meeting,
                    which begins at 6:00 p.m., may be addressed immediately following that meeting.

                                             6:00 p.m. City Council Meeting
                                                  September 15, 2020

1. Roll Call.

2. Opening Ceremony:
   a. Pledge of Allegiance.
   b. Moment of Silence.

3. Recognition:
   a. Hispanic Heritage Month. Joint proclamation declaring September 15 through October 15 as “Hispanic Heritage
      Month” in Ogden City. (Adopt joint proclamation – voice vote)
   b. Domestic Violence Awareness Month. Joint proclamation declaring October as “Domestic Violence Awareness
      Month” in Ogden City. (Adopt joint proclamation – voice vote)

4. Common Consent: (Roll call vote)
   a. Continuing State of Emergency. Proposed Resolution 2020-24 authorizing the continuation of the State of
      Emergency as proclaimed by Mayor Michael P. Caldwell on March 16, 2020, which state shall continue until the
September 15, 2020

         later of January 8, 2021 or such time as Governor Gary R. Herbert lifts the State of Emergency for the State of
         Utah. (Adopt resolution)

5. Public Hearings:
   a. Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Budget Amendment – Homeless Service Advocate. Proposed Ordinance 2020-38
       amending the budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2021 by $114,250 for a new grant-funded Homeless
       Service Advocate position. (Adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)
   b. CARES Act Grant Funding Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Budget Amendment and Interlocal Agreement:
         i. Presentation
        ii. Public Hearing
       iii. Action Items:
            1. Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Budget Amendment. Proposed Ordinance 2020-40 amending the budget for
                Fiscal Year July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2021 by $5,598,675 for Coronavirus Aid Relief and Economic
                Security (CARES) Act purposes. (Adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)
            2. Interlocal Agreement with Weber County. Proposed Resolution 2020-25 authorizing and approving an
                interlocal agreement with Weber County to provide CARES funds for the City to grant to nonprofits and
                small businesses. (Adopt/not adopt resolution – roll call vote)

6. Reports from Planning Commission:
   a. Proposed Zone Text Amendments – Accessory Apartments in the Central Business District (CBD) Zone.
      (Continued from August 18, 2020) Proposed Ordinance 2020-36 amending Municipal Code Subsection 15-34-2.D
      to allow an accessory apartment in existing single-story buildings in the CBD Zone. (Receive input; adopt/not
      adopt ordinance – roll call vote)
   b. Proposed Subdivision Ordinance Amendments – Roadway Classifications and Streetlights. Proposed Ordinance
      2020-41 amending Municipal Code Sections 14-1-4, 14-3-1, 14-3-2, and 14-3-6 to amend public improvement
      requirements for subdivisions. (Receive input; adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)
   c. Proposed Subdivision Ordinance Amendments – Public Meetings and Small Subdivisions. Proposed Ordinance
      2020-42 amending Municipal Code Subsection 14-2-5 and Chapter 14-4 to amend the public hearing and approval
      process for small subdivisions. (Receive input; adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)
   d. Proposed Zone Text Amendment – Allow Online Auto Sales in C-3 Zone. Proposed Ordinance 2020-43 amending
      Municipal Code Subsection 15-38-5.A to allow online automobile sales as a permitted use in the Regional
      Commercial Zone (C-3). (Receive input; adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)

7. Reports from Administration:
   a. Engineering Standards:
        i. Public Right-of-Way, As-Built Projects, and Street Lighting. Proposed Ordinance 2020-46 amending the
           Municipal Code Section 7-6-1 and adopting a new Section 7-6-2 and 7-6-3 to revise engineering regulations,
           design standards, construction specifications and traffic control regulations. (Adopt/not adopt ordinance –
           roll call vote)
       ii. Traffic Flow and Stormwater Regulations:
           1. Site Triangle (Clear View Triangle) Distances. Proposed Ordinance 2020-44 amending the Municipal Code
                 Section 7-3-2 to revise the definition of site triangle area. (Adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)
           2. Stormwater Regulations. Proposed Ordinance 2020-45 amending the Municipal Code Section 9-7D-2 to
                 revise provisions regarding illegal discharges into storm drains. (Adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call
                 vote)

8. Public Comments. This is an opportunity to address the Council regarding concerns or ideas on any topic. To be
   considerate of everyone at this meeting, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person. Participants are
   to state their name for the record. Comments which cannot be made within these limits should be submitted in
   writing to the City Council Office (citycouncil@ogdencity.com).

    The Council encourages civil discourse for everyone who participates in our meetings. Comments pertaining to an
    agenda item that includes a public hearing or public input should be given during the meeting as that item is
    discussed.

9. Comments:
      a. Mayor.
      b. Council members.

10. Adjournment.
