ORDINANCE NO. 2020-10-01                                                  Date: October 22, 2020

       AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING A NEW GENERAL PLAN FOR THE KEARNS
       METRO TOWNSHIP

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) incorporated as a municipality in 2017;
and

       WHEREAS, the Municipal Land Use, Development, and Management Act (the “Act”)
requires each municipality to prepare and adopt a comprehensive, long-range general plan; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-403 within the Act requires each general plan to
include a land use element and a transportation and traffic circulation element, at a minimum; and

      WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature recently modified Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-403 to require
a moderate housing income element through the passage of S.B. 34 in 2019; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-403 also authorizes municipalities to include certain
optional elements within their general plans, including but not limited to an economic development
element; and

      WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council adopted the current general plan for Kearns on
September 11, 2012, when Kearns was an unincorporated community; and

        WHEREAS, on November 19, 2019, the Kearns Council amended the current general
plan to include a moderate housing element to comply with S.B. 34; and

      WHEREAS, on December 2, 2019, the Kearns Planning Commission (the “Planning
Commission”) oversaw the creation of a general plan steering group to develop a new and
updated general plan that would incorporate the moderate housing element and account for
Kearns’ status as an incorporated municipality (collectively, the “General Plan”); and

       WHEREAS, the General Plan that resulted from this process includes new land use,
transportation and traffic circulation, and economic development elements; and

       WHEREAS, on October 12, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to
receive public comment on the General Plan after complying with the procedures in the Act,

including but not limited to the public hearing and notice requirements of Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-

       WHEREAS, after taking public comment on the General Plan, the Planning Commission

 COUNCIL:

   1. The attached General Plan consisting of a land use element, transportation and traffic
      circulation element, economic development element, the moderate housing element
      adopted on November 19, 2019, and all other related documents and appendices is
      approved.

   2. The prior general plan the Salt Lake County Council adopted on September 11, 2012, is
      repealed in its entirely.

   3. A copy of the General Plan will be filed and retained by the Kearns Metro Township Clerk,
      Recorder, and Auditor and posted to the Kearns Metro Township website.

   4. If a court of competent determination determines that any of the sections, sentences,
      clauses or provisions of the General Plan are invalid, such a ruling will not invalidate the
      remaining portion(s) of the General Plan.

   5. Staff is directed to identify those portions of the Kearns Metro Township Code that need
      to be updated to conform to the General Plan and to recommend proposed changes to
      the Council.

   6. Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-3-711 and –712, this ordinance will take effect
      twenty (20) days after its publication in a newspaper published in and having general
      circulation in Salt Lake County or 30 days from the effective date of this ordinance,
      whichever is closer to the effective date.

Continuation of Council Business from October 12, 2020

CARES Act Amendment between Salt Lake County and Kearns Metro Township – Section
Distribution of Funds

                 Mayor Kelly Bush stated Salt Lake County would like to distribute another $1.1
million (2nd Tranche) of COVID-19 funding to the Kearns Metro Township and there is talk of an
additional 3rd tranche of $1.1 million. Kearns Metro Township is at a distinct disadvantage when
it comes to this funding because it does not have its own police department or fire department.
The Council has only spent $164,000 of the 1st tranche of $1.1 million. She has been invited to
meet with Mayor Jennifer Wilson, Salt Lake County, regarding this funding and is hoping that the
County can provide creative ideas for getting this money distribute to people who need it in
Kearns. This is on the agenda tonight for her to get permission from the Council to accept this
additional funding.

             Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to give
Mayor Bush permission to accept this money. The motion passed unanimously.

               Mr. Bracken stated he is concerned about accepting these funds and then not
being able to use them. He asked if Kearns Metro Township would be better off by not accepting
the funds but requesting the County to earmark them to be used for the township.

              Mayor Bush stated she felt better about the situation if Kearns accepted the
money and then returned it requesting that it be earmarked for Kearns. This way the County
would not be able to say that Kearns did not want the funds.

              Mr. Bracken stated the only problem with that idea is time is running out to use
these funds. He would recommend that Mayor Bush just ask the County to earmark the funds for
Kearns.

              Mayor Bush stated she would ask. Also, she wanted the Council to be aware,
from what she has heard, the 2nd tranche of funding will be mostly be earmarked for fire and police
departments.

Allocation of Compliance Funds to Kearns UPD Precinct for COVID 19 Spread Prevention

              Mayor Kelly Bush asked if all Council Members had an opportunity to review the
information she provided relating to this distribution and if there were any questions.

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township Council, stated he was
good with the information provided, there was a lot of detail. However, the last four expenses did
not indicate what they were used for and why they were needed. This information needed to be
provided. The Council needed documentation indicating the expenses would not have occur if
not for the pandemic.

It would be a good idea if the Council entered into an agreement with the UPD regarding the use
of these funds. The agreement should indicate that UPD is responsible for any cost that might
occur if the federal government deemed the use of the funds unacceptable. This would protect
both entities.

              Chief Levi Hughes stated the last four items on the list were hand sanitizer,
dispenser, paper towels, and paper towel holders. These are additional cost above and beyond
what the UPD budgeted for and would not have incur these costs if it were not for the pandemic.
Also, he would agree to enter into the agreement relating to the use of these funds.

             Mr. Bracken stated the Council could approve this tonight contingent upon an
agreement being entered into.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
approve the compliance funds for the Kearns UPD precinct in the amount $228,822.17 contingent
upon the agreement from UPD before the funds are released. The motion passed unanimously.
