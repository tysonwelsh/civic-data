City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1684

On November 18, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1684 amending
the official zoning map of draper city for approximately 0.89 acres of property from
ra2 (residential agricultural) to r3 (single family residential), located at approximately
1394 east 12900 south within draper city, otherwise known as the Fetzer zoning map
amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office
and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
