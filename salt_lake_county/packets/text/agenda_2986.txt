Members of the Municipal Building Authority may participate electronically. The public may 
attend. Meetings may be closed For reasons allowed by statute. Motions relating to any of the 
items listed below, including final action, may be taken.
Due to the ongoing public health emergency related to the COVID -19 pandemic and response, 
public gatherings have been limited to less than 20 persons and public access to the Salt Lake 
County Government Center has been limited on the orders of the Salt Lake County Mayor. 
Consistent with Salt Lake County Council Resolution 5714, approved March 24, 2020, the Salt 
Lake County Municipal Building Authority has made preparations to conduct its meetings 
electronically. In light of current public health orders and social distancing requirements, the 
Municipal Building Authority cannot provide physical space or facilities for members of the 
public to attend its meetings for the duration of the public health emergency.
Members of the public are invited to participate in the meetings electronically. Agendas, audio 
live-streams and recordings, and meeting minutes can be accessed at slco .legistar.com. The 
Council will continue to broadcast a live -stream of its meetings on Facebook Live, which can be 
accessed at http://www.facebook.com/slcocouncil/
