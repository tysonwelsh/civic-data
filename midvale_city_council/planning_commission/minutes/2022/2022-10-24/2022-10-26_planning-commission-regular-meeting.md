PUBLIC HEARINGS

     1.    Amendments to Title 17 of the Midvale City Municipal Code to address setbacks
          for garages, carports and accessory structures within the SF-1 and SF-2 zones.

          The City proposes amendments to the above listed chapters of the Municipal Code. The
          amendments will allow flexibility in setbacks for expansion of existing attached garages
          and/or carports that could not otherwise occur. Current Code allows a side setback of
          four feet (4’) for expansion of an attached garage or carport. Multiple areas within the
          SF-1 and SF-2 zones have existing structures within the 4’ setback, leaving many of
          these structures unable to expand as they are classified as “non-conforming”. The
          amendment, as proposed, will allow a side expansion to the property line as well as the
          ability to expand deeper into the lot at the current non-conforming setback line. For an
          expansion to a property line, proof of a licensed survey will be required, identifying the
          location of said property line. In addition, no openings on the side of the structure
          contiguous to the property line will be allowed, nor will drainage from a roof be allowed
          to discharge on an adjacent property.

          Setbacks for unoccupied accessory structures and External Accessory Dwelling Units
          (EADU’s) will remain at 2’ from side and rear property lines. However, the proposed
          amendment will allow the ability for no minimum setback; provided certain criteria are
          met (identified in draft ordinance).

        structures and placement of new unoccupied accessory structures and EADU’s in

APPLICANT:

Mr. Olsen explains that if the amendments are made to the Municipal Code, the expansions will
still need to be permitted. The surveyor would make the official property line part of the record
and assure the boundaries for future matters. The SF-1 and SF-2 Zones are largely developed and
hold many of the city’s aging structures. The Council members have asked for the Planning &
Zoning Department to look into this matter. There would be specific requirements to be met, in
order to expand the existing garage or carport. The 0’ setback would benefit the residents to
improve their homes or add additional storage who lack the space to currently do so.

DISCUSSION:

     Chair Snow and Vice Chair Anderson are concerned with the 0’ setback in fear of water
     leakage onto neighboring properties. They raise concern about the comfort of the neighbors.

     Mr. Olsen confirms that the ordinance would require that any rain gutters and or roofing
     run-off system would have to be directed away from the property line. He also reassured
     that any building permits issues regarding this matter would be surveyed to verify property
     lines. Any permits will only be issued to those that fit the requirements.

     Commissioner Erickson and Commissioner Tippetts voice that this amendment will directly
     affect them and those around them. The area is heavily populated and it makes it harder to
     keep growing families in Midvale or keeps bigger families from moving in. This will create
     more flexibility and hopefully draw in new homeowners for older homes and allow existing
     residents to make updates to their homes.
