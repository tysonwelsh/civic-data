                           RESOLUTION NO. 21-62

      A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL DECLARING THE
      RESULTS OF THE NOVEMBER 2, 2021 GENERAL ELECTION
      CANVASS

       WHEREAS, Draper City held a General Election on November 2, 2021
for the Mayor and two City Council positions; and

      WHEREAS, Utah Code Title 20A-4-304 requires that the board of
canvassers shall declare as “elected” those persons who had the highest
number of votes and shall certify the results of the election.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. The election results as shown in Exhibit A are accepted
and declared and certified to be the official canvass results for the November
16, 2021 general election for Draper City.

       Section 2. Troy K. Walker is hereby declared as “elected” as the
Mayor of Draper City. Tasha Lowery and Mike Green are hereby declared as
“elected” as Councilmembers for Draper City.

     Section 3. Effective Date.      This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, THIS 16TH DAY OF NOVEMBER, 2021.
