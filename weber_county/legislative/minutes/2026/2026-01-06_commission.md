D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. January’s Stall of Fame Recipient is Planning’s Tammy Aydelotte.
      Rick Grover (Planning Director): She went above and beyond the call of duty this past year working on the
      incorporation in Ogden Valley. She was able to help save the new city money by completing items under the
      County’s realm.

            Tammy Aydelotte: We have a stellar staff. It was definitely a team effort to take on most of the projects and
            complete them before handing them over to the new city. I am grateful for everyone in our office for all of
            their help and support.

            Commissioner Froerer: Weber County has the best employees in the state. Tammy, I know for a fact you
            put up with more than any staff or employee of any county. I'm referring to the conversations you had more
            than weekly with certain people from Ogden Valley that felt they were not being treated fairly by Weber
            County when in fact we save them a substantial amount of money and time. I would hope at one point in
            time they'll realize the work that you and your whole department put in making sure this worked to their
            benefit. Speaking not only as a Commissioner but as a citizen in Ogden Valley I apologize for the way some
            people treated you. But believe me, there are people in the valley that appreciate you.

            Commissioner Harvey: You didn’t settle. You have brought honor to your team. You have shown integrity.
            This is just a small way of saying thank you for what you have done.

            Chair Bolos: I hope you feel valued by your team. We appreciate you and hope you know that.

    3. APPROVAL TO ADD A POSSIBLE COMMUNITY BLOCK GRANT PROJECT TO WEBER
       COUNTY’S CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS FOR 2026-2030.
         Chad Meyerhoffer (Engineering): As part of the Community Development Block Grant process, the Weber
         Housing Authority needs a sponsorship from the County to receive Block Grant funding. Weber Housing
         asks Weber County be their sponsor for a grant for emergency rental and deposit assistance for low income
         households. The program will be used as prevention dollars to assist households remain stably housed rather
         than entering the homeless system. The funding would allow a one-time rental payment after the household
         has exhausted all of the resources and the household would be expected to participate in a budgeting class
         and must have the ability to pay the entire rent amount moving forward.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a possible Community Block Grant project to Weber County’s capital
         improvements for 2026-2030; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye
