                                   RESOLUTION NO. 24-02

         A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO SIGN A REAL ESTATE
        PURCHASE AGREEMENT FOR THE PURCHASE OF PARCEL # 28-33-203-007
               AND PARCEL #28-33-203-010 LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
         12695 SOUTH 1700 EAST CONSISTING OF APPROXIMATELY 10.87 ACRES


        WHEREAS, the Canyons School District owns two parcels of property: Parcel #28-33-
203-007 and Parcel #28-33-203-010 located at approximately 12695 South 1700 East in Draper
(the “Property”); and

       WHEREAS, the Property consists of approximately 10.87 acres; and

       WHEREAS, the Canyons School District has offered to sell the Property to Draper City
based on the terms of the Real Estate Purchase Contract (“REPC”) attached as Exhibit 1; and

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has determined the terms of the REPC are fair and
the purchase of the Property benefits the health, safety and welfare of Draper City’s residents.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Real Estate Purchase Agreement. The Mayor is authorized to execute the
Real Estate Purchase Contract attached hereto as Exhibit 1.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be
severable.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 20TH DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2024.
