            Brent Helestein spoke under “Citizen Public Input” stating as a point of information
he has had a few people in Council Member Schaeffer’s district tell him the Kearns Metro
Township website is very uninformative.

                Mayor Bush stated the Kearns Metro Township does not have a website anymore.
The person that was doing the website has quit, will not answer her phone, or take the website
down. The Council has now contracted with a professional agency whose expertise is working
on government agency websites. The new agency has tried to get a hold of previous individual
to get the license, but she will not give it to them. The Kearns Metro Township owns the license,
once the new website is up and running, David Church, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township
Council, will deal with her legally.

              Council Member Peterson stated the individual was notified that her services
were no longer needed.

               Mayor Bush stated actually the individual quit and then decided to bill the Council
four months later. She indicated that she had started working again on the website, without the
Council’s knowledge. The website has been up and down. The new website will have all the
metro township ordinances. It will be better, more professional,

             Mr. Helestein asked if the citizens in Kearns have been made aware of the
proposed code changes.

               Mayor Bush stated all the proposed code changes related to code enforcement
issues. The Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission will hold a public hearing on November
19, 2018, to receive public comments regarding the changes. Some of the changes relate to
weed issues and yard clean up. She has heard from several people that this is what is wanted.

One change relates to allowing gravel to put in the front yard. It no longer will need to be a hard
surface. This should help get cars off the road. The current ordinance only allows hard surfaces
in the front to park a car. People in Kearns cannot pay for that. It allows for three to four inches
of hard pack gravel. The changes address issues relating to Kearns, which will help the citizens.

The ordinance will go through the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission before they
come to the Council to be heard. Another change allows for a metal cover carport as long as it
looks professional. This change would allow people to park cars off the road. Parking in Kearns
has been a big issue. It is probably safe to say that at 37,000 residents would like to see that
changed.

Another possible change, that she is looking into, is increasing the penalty for code violations
from $50 day to $200. It is too easy for landlords to ignore the fines because about 80 percent of
the noncompliance homes are rentals.

              Mr. Helestein stated he had an issue with Mayor Bush and her attitude towards a
couple of people at a recent meeting.

               Mayor Bush stated that is between her and the people; however, she appreciated
his input.

Council Business

Tentative Approval of the 2019 Kearns Metro Township budget

                Mayor Bush stated the total budget for the Kearns Metro Township for 2019 is
$7,133,069, of which $6,823,369 will be pass through from the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Service District to cover the cost of contracted County services. The remaining $309,700 will be
used for administrative services.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
approve the following resolution adopting the tentative budget for 2019 fiscal year and setting a
public hearing for December 17, 2018, at 7 p.m. The motion passed unanimously.

RESOLUTION NO. 18-11-01                                                   Date: November 13, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING THE
       TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR THE 2019 FISCAL YEAR AND SETTING A PUBLIC
       HEARING THEREON

       A.      WHEREAS, a tentative budget has been prepared budget officer for the Township
               for the ensuing fiscal 2019 period and filed with the Township Council; and

       B.      WHEREAS, the Township Council has reviewed the tentative budget as required
               by law and now wishes to set a public hearing on the tentative budget prior to
               considering it for adoption as a final budget.

                                           RESOLUTION

      NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council that it
does hereby:

        1.     Adopt the attached Exhibit A as the tentative budget for the 2019 fiscal year
together with all supporting schedules to be made available as a public record in the office of the
Township Clerk, available for public inspection for a period of at least 10 days prior to the adoption
of a final budget, as hereinafter provided.

         2.     That the Township does hereby established that on the 17th day of December
2018, at the hour of 7:00 o’clock, a public hearing shall be held to consider its adoption and does
hereby order that notice of the public hearing be published at least seven days prior to the hearing
in at least one issue of a newspaper of general circulation published in the Salt Lake County; on
