 2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Henry McConnehey led participants in the Pledge of Allegiance.

 3. FILLING MID-TERM VACANCY FOR DISTRICT TWO
       a. Legal Review
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson provided an overview of Utah Code and the procedure
          for filling a mid-term City Council vacancy.

       b. Mid-Term Vacancy Process and Procedures
          Chair McConnehey outlined the process for the remainder of the meeting. City Recorder Tangee
          Sloan determined by coin toss that Robert Bennett would be interviewed first, followed by Bob
          Bedore. It was noted that Gary Leany, one of the three candidates, was unable to attend and
          withdrew his application.

       c. Candidate Interviews
          The Council interviewed Robert Bennett. Responding to questions from the Council, Mr.
          Bennett said he was interested in focusing more on what the Council could accomplish rather
          than what the Council had not accomplished. He believed every citizen who took the time to
          attend a Council meeting deserved to be heard. Mr. Bennett would personally feel honored to
          be able to sit on the Board of Canvass and accept the votes of the electorate. He spoke of issues
          he had participated in when previously on the City Council and mentioned a willingness to do
          what it took to give a voice to residents in District 2.

           Mr. Bennett had experience administering a multi-million-dollar budget from his previous time
           on the Council. He would be available to attend both scheduled Council meetings in December.
           Mr. Bennett referred to the many active construction projects in West Jordan and expressed
           concern for the potential of construction fatigue on the part of residents. He suggested
           increasing the ability of the City to involve the citizens in understanding the need of and how
           the city would benefit from the completion of the projects. Mr. Bennett appreciated the
           opportunity, respected the rule of law, was proud to be a citizen of West Jordan City, and would
           respect the decision of the City Council.

           The Council interviewed Bob Bedore. Responding to questions from the Council, Mr. Bedore
           referred to residents who had attended Council meetings regarding plans for 9000 South. He
           suggested Council Members meet with residents on the property and discuss the situation with
           them. He also suggested residents could possibly provide a solution. Mr. Bedore expressed a
           desire to get to work on the Council. He spoke of his work with the Midvale Arts Council, and
           his experience working with the theatre. He expressed the opinion that working with a multi-
           million-dollar budget was not very different from working with a family or business budget. He
           believed in asking whether a budget item was needed, and whether there was money for it. He
           expressed the opinion that if the answer to either question was no, a different solution was
           needed. He always wanted to do his best to avoid debt. Mr. Bedore believed there was a housing
           divide within the community and felt the Council could work to bring the community together.
           He noted that he would be out of town for the first Council meeting in December but would be
           able to join electronically. Mr. Bedore looked forward to working with the Council.

       d. Council Discussion
          The Council submitted votes electronically, with 50% cast for Bob Bedore and 50% for Robert
          Bennett. The Council voted electronically a second time, with the same 50/50 result. Following
          procedure, Ms. Sloan designated Mr. Bennett “heads” and Mr. Bedore “tails” and tossed a coin
          to break the tie vote. The coin landed “heads” up, and Mr. Bennett was selected to fill the mid-
          term vacancy in District 2.

       e. Resolution 23-070 appointing Robert Bennett to fill the vacancy on the West Jordan City
          Council District 2.

 4. PUBLIC HEARINGS
        a. City Budget Amendment
           Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck presented proposed amendments to the FY2024
           Budget. Council Member Whitelock expressed concern that postponing construction of a
           planned water tank would cost the City more in the long run, in terms of construction costs. The
           Council and staff discussed the future of the Water Fund. Ms. Steck answered questions from the
           Council. Council Member Bennett encouraged sharing news of grant awards and confirmation of
           technology/security with the public.

          Chair McConnehey opened a public hearing at 7:27 pm

          Comments:

          Wheels Park.
