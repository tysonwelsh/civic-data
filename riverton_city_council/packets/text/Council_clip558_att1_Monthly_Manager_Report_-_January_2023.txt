                       Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                         January, 2023
A summary of the year-end statistics for 2022 did not reveal any significant anomalies compared
to 2021. In fact, the crimes investigated and the total number of case reports generated were
remarkably similar. For instance, in 2021 we investigated 125 shoplifting cases and in 2022
there were 124. Vehicle burglaries showed a very slight increase, from 210 investigated in 2021
to 226 in 2022. Driving Under the Influence cases rose from 82 to 91 from 2021 to 2022. We
observed an actual decrease in stolen vehicles from 76 in 2021 to 64 in 2022. We saw a very
slight increase in our number of booking arrests, from 101 in 2021 to 112 in 2022. I was
encouraged to see a relatively dramatic decrease in burglaries, with 56 reported in 2021 and only
32 reported in 2022. This would indicate our public service campaign encouraging our citizens
to be more aware of securing their property and increasing their focus on home security may be
having a positive impact.

We did see a more significant increase in the number of citations issued as we increased our
speed enforcement efforts, from 1793 in 2021 to 2360 in 2022. This was largely due to a
renewed focus during the year on traffic enforcement and the fact we received grant money from
the State of Utah to fund speed enforcement shifts. This grant funding allowed us to compensate
our officers to work overtime shifts and focus on speed enforcement.

Our total cases investigated during the last two years was remarkably consistent. We
investigated 4824 cases in 2021 and 4856 cases in 2022, a difference of only 32 cases. I am
happy to report we did not investigate any homicides in our jurisdiction during the year.
Unfortunately, we did have one fatal traffic accident on our roadways during the year when a
Riverton High School student accidentally fell out of a moving vehicle and passed away from his
injuries.

We also had one of our officers involved in an Officer Involved Critical Incident as part of the
South Valley SWAT Team response to a barricaded gunman in Herriman. One of our officers
justifiably utilized deadly force to end that incident, arguably saving many lives as the suspect
exited his home, armed with an assault rifle and hundreds of rounds of ammunition, and
attempted to rush officers.

In addition to the incidents documented above, members of the department participated in many
multi-jurisdictional operations outside of our jurisdiction through our participation on several
task forces including; U.S. Marshal’s Violent Fugitive Apprehension Strike Team, Internet
Crimes Against Children Task Force, South Valley SWAT, and South Valley CRASH Team.
