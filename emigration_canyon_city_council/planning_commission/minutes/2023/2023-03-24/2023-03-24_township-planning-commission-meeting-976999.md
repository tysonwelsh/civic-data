Meeting began at – 8:50 a.m.
1) Updates to Municipal Code: 19.73.110 Night Lighting proposed draft language in anticipation of
the development of a complete draft preparation for public hearing. Planner: Matt Starley.
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding definitions.
Remove - “or Designee” from Director.
BUG Rating – recommend adding “behind the light fixture”.
Remove - “The appropriate glare rating is determined by the community” from the BUG Ratings
graphic figure 01.
Add “Nadir” definition – The direction pointing directly below a particular location.
Change Fixture Lumens – replace “processing of emitted light by” to “passing through the”.
