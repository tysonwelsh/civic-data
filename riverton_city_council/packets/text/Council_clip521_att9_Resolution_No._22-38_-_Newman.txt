                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 22-38

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
WITH NEWMAN CONSTRUCTION FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE ROSE CREEK
                 TRAIL IMPROVEMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council required that the project must come back for final
approval before proceeding; and

      WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to proceed with Rose Creek Trail
Improvement Project.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an Agreement with Newman
          Construction for the completion of the Rose Creek Trail Improvement Project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
May 2022 by the following vote:
