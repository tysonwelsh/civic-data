the General Plan. He stated that at the October meeting, after receiving feedback from the Council,

and utilities based on the General Plan.

established where density was added and where it decreased from the 2015 infrastructure study.

Stan Lockhart attended the meeting and was available to answer questions. Drew Penrose
attended virtually and was available to answer technical questions. Mr. Lockhart stated that they
have made it an option for cities to still have a Primary election if they choose. He stated that the
RCV system also allows for a friendly campaign.

5. Consideration of Ordinance #02-2021, approval of the Traverse Mountain Area Plan
   Amendment, changing the land use designation from Open Space to Public Facility on
   approximately 5.2-acres located at approximately 5700 North Flight Park Road (tabled
   on January 12, 2021)
   Brad Kenison, Assistant City Engineer, stated that they estimated that 50% of the usage from
   the culinary tank will be used for the Traverse Mountain Area Plan. Lorin Powell, City
   Engineer, also explained that the line item in the Traverse Mountain Area Plan financial
   documents was to be used only if they needed to purchase land from the State, but because of
   an amendment to the Area Plan, this proposed location is now open space and it’s a much
   better location for the public facilities.

    Mayor Johnson asked for public comments.

    Rob Ludlow stated that the proposed infrastructure will be to support the TOD and not Traverse
    Mountain. The original plan was to place these utilities off site, and now taking away this open
    space adversely affects the residents of Traverse Mountain. He agrees that this is the best
    location for the facilities, but the residents in Traverse Mountain need to be made whole for
    taking the open space. He said that the City should purchase the open space at it’s best and
    highest use and then that money should be reinvested into Traverse Mountain. He also noted
    that the appraisal for the Power Department’s portion was low and they should require another
    appraisal.

    Jared Morgan was representing the property owner. He stated that he understands this situation
    is unique, but he believes this is the right location and they want to move forward with a design.

    Curt Carmack wanted to reemphasize what Mr. Ludlow said. He agrees that this should be a
    public facility zone, but because the open space is being taken away, the city now needs to do
    something for the community in return. He also expressed concern with the appraisal value.

    Joel Eves, Power Director, clarified that the Power Department is already paying for their
    portion of the property and the developer has agreed to pass that through to the Traverse
    Mountain Trails Association. He noted that power pays for the property, but the water facilities
    were specifically addressed in the Area Plan which states that the developer would pay for that
    property. He also noted that he was not able to get a second appraisal in time for this meeting.

    The Council agreed that this is the right location for the public facilities zone. They discussed
    if the city should purchase the property for the water facilities and if so, how much should they
    purchase, because 50% of the water will be used by Traverse Mountain. They also discussed
    if a second appraisal was needed.

    Councilor Koivisto expressed concern for purchasing the property for water facilities because
    it’s not required by the Area Plan.

    Council Southwick stated that the City doesn’t need to pay for the water facilities, but to make
    it right for the open space being lost, the city could include additional funds in the City budget
    to dedicate to parks or trails in Traverse Mountain.

                With the findings that even though this is a low profile public facility, the residents
                there are still impacted because they were expecting open space; and

                They need another appraisal based on the highest and best use of the property, so
                they should do the appraisal based on it being a pubic facility;

                Also noting that 50% of the water facilities will benefit Traverse Mountain, so he
                is proposing that the remaining 50% be funded by the city to be paid to the Clyde
                Company by the Water Department;

                Due to this being an unplanned expense, the Power Department can pay for their
                portion this year and Water can pay for their portion in the next fiscal budget;

                Councilor Condie seconded the motion.

    Councilor Koivisto expressed concern for the wording of the appraisal. Councilor Hancock
    stated that his intention was for the appraiser to determine the best and highest use, which will
    either be open space or public facilities and the city will use the higher appraisal.

    Councilor Albrecht suggested that they take an average of the two appraisals in case one is an
    outlier. Councilor Condie stated that it’s possible that the second appraisal is actually lower
    than the first. Councilor Albrecht was concerned if the second appraisal is substantially lower
    or higher, then they are bound to a number they weren’t expecting. Councilor Koivisto was
    comfortable using an average of the two appraisals because she believes there won’t be a large
    difference between the two. Councilor Hancock agreed.

    There was discussion about the number of acreage that the city will pay for. It was determined
    that the water facilities will be half of the property, so water would be located on 2.6 acres, so
    the city would then pay for 1.3 acres for water’s portion. He was comfortable with water and
    power working out their portions of the property.

    Mayor Johnson was concerned with having loose ends to the motion.

    Councilor Condie preferred to pass it as is and instead use the budget to fund for more trails in
    Traverse Mountain. Councilor Hancock was concerned that it may not get added to the budget.
    He said the funding needs to be tied to this motion.
