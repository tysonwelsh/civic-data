 1   Community and Development Director, Michael Johnson reviewed the request from Ivory Homes
 2   for Preliminary Subdivision Plat and Conditional Use approval of a Planned Unit Development
 3   (“PUD”) on Property located at 3425 East Doverhill Drive in the Residential Single-Family (R-1-
 4   8) Zone. The 6.17-acre property is currently owned by the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day
 5   Saints with the existing building having experienced arson in 2018. A preliminary plat rendering
 6   was displayed. Mr. Johnson reported that Ivory Homes is proposing a 17-lot, single-family
 7   subdivision on 4.17 acres of the parcel with two acres being owned and maintained by the City as
 8   a public park. The City felt that retaining open space is critical for this neighborhood. At the time
 9   the property was listed, there was no agreement in place with regard to what would be offered to
10   the City or what would be retained as open space. The City was invited to submit an offer. After
11   numerous meetings, the owner made the final decision to offer the eastern two acres of land for
12   sale to the City and four acres to Ivory Development. There were concerns with the two-acre
13   layout, which does not provide walkable access. The desire was to ensure access from Doverhill
14   Drive to maximize the property frontage.
15
16   Mr. Johnson reported that numerous meetings have been held with Ivory Homes in an effort to
17   cater a design around the City’s requests to optimize the City park portion of the property. An
18   agreement was drafted to ensure that items are addressed that are not part of the Code subdivision
19   process. The request included maximizing park frontage, preserving the pavilion, and providing
20   for pedestrian access to be contemplated in the agreement. Mr. Johnson stated that the
21   Development Agreement will go to the City Council for discussion and potential consideration on
22   December 14, 2021. Staff felt that the terms of a preliminary submittal on the application
23   addresses many of the concerns raised by the City.
24
25   Mr. Johnson commented that the park configuration has been proposed differently than what was
26   offered to both parties for sale. A map depicting the two parcels was displayed. With over six
27   acres, the density is 2.75 units per acre with 17 lots. Two acres of open space with 12 lots accessed
28   from Summerhill Drive and five lots accessed from Doverhill Drive. A PUD must demonstrate
29   creative use of land that is distinct and different from what can be provided with a standard
30   subdivision. Use of the land must be efficient and utilize the smaller network of utilities and streets
31   and allow a greater amount of open lands. Staff found that the primary purpose of the PUD request
32   was to enhance the City park portion of the property beyond what was offered to the City. It also
33   helps preserve and make accessible fully public open space. Ivory requested a modification of lot
34   width and side setbacks to provide the proposed 17 housing units with no proposed variation to
35   reduce lot sizes below 8,000 square feet. The applicant requested a total of 10 feet in total or five-
36   yard side setbacks where 20 feet between both side yards is normally required
37
38   Commissioner Mills shared concern with the proposed layout and side setbacks. Mr. Johnson
39   stated that the project is required to go through the Architectural Review Commission (“ARC”)
40   for design compliance and receive a Certificate of Design Compliance prior to final approval. He
41   confirmed that ARC’s job is to ensure that the design goals of the PUD are met.
42
43   Mr. Johnson reported that the application has no proposed zoning change or lots smaller than 8,000
44   square feet in size and it is consistent with neighboring developments. A recommendation was
45   made to survey all existing trees and identify those that are healthy and not impacted by the actual
46   private development to be preserved. Because the property is located within the Sensitive Lands
