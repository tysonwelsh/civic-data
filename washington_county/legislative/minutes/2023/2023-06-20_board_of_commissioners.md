05/19/2023 @ 1240, Injured Hiker, Snow Canyon, Is. in the Sky — Man Hours - 6

SAR units responded to a 60 year old female that broke her ankle at the base of Island in
the Sky. Ivins EMS hiked in and stabilized the injury. SAR arrived as they were getting
back to the ambulance. She was taken to SGRH for treatment.

05/20/2023 @ 1330, Ill Hiker, Snow Canyon, Butterfly Trail — Man Hours — 14

SAR responded to Snow Canyon State Park near Butterfly Trail on a report of a 24 year
old female who's legs were seizing and needed help getting back to her vehicle. The female
and her party were located and an access road was utilized to remove the individuals
from the area and return them to their vehicle. The female declined medical evaluation.
05/21/2023 @ 1015, Stranded Swimmers, Bloomington, Virgin River — Man Hours — 12

SAR responded to St. George near Bloomington Walmart on a report of a 46 year old and
10 year old males stranded on a sand bar in the middle of the Virgin River. Two team
members swam to the sand bar. Throw bags and life jackets were utilized to safely get the
two subjects back across the river. Neither reported any medical problems. They were
given a ride back to their house by a SAR member.

05/21/2023 @ 1330, Drowning — Gunlock Falls— Man Hours — 8

Deputies and SAR responded to a drowning at Gunlock Falls. A 12 year old female was
unconscious and in the falls. EMS and deputies carried the subject to Life Flight. She
was taken to SGRH. She did survive and was released later that day.

05/24/2023 @ 1010, Injured Hiker, Aspiration Trail - Man Hours — 24

SAR responded to a 64 year old female on Aspiration Trail. The hiker had an injured
ankle. The female was carried out on a stokes by SAR personnel. She was taken by
ambulance to SGRH.

05/24/2023 @ 1056, Injured Hiker, Johnson Canyon Tr., Snow Canyon — Man Hours — 12

SAR responded to Johnson Canyon Trail on a report of a 76 year old female, The subject
tripped and fell causing her to hit her face. IC was established at the Johnson Canyon
Trail head. Medical units from SAR and Santa Clara Ivins Fire made contact with the
female. The subject elected to hike out with the assistance of SAR personnel. The patient
declined further medical evaluation.

05/24/2023 @ 1525, Injured Kayaker, Babylon Area — Man Hours — 38

SAR responded to a 37 year old female and 19 year old male on the river near Babylon
Arch Trail. The female got stuck in a current and fell. The male was reported as missing
on the river. SAR arrived and made contact with female. STAR 9 was utilized to locate
the male and transport both patients to the IC. One female was transported to St George
Regional Hospital for her injuries.
05/25/2023 @ 1719, Vehicle Rollover, Little Creek Mesa — Man Hours - 32

SAR responded to a report of a rollover accident on Little Creek Mesa Rd. The 33 year
old male was attempting to help a family member who was stuck on the road up the north
side of the Mesa. The driver appeared to have rolled off the edge of the cliff going
approximately 200 feet down a steep incline before coming to a stop. The driver was
evaluated by medical and transported to the hospital. Search and Rescue also assisted
with getting the other family members unstuck. All SAR units were accounted for and
cleared the scene.

05/26/2023 @ Stranded in River, Virgin River, Bloomington — Man Hours — 16

SAR responded to an area of the Virgin River in St. George for a 33 year old male laying
on a sand bar in the middle of the River. The male was non-compliant and potentially a
risk to SAR members. SAR remained on scene on standby. The male jumped into the
river and swam to shore. He was arrested by St. George Police.

05/26/2023 @ 1500, Injured Hiker — Water Canyon — Man Hours — 24

SAR responded to a 38 year old female that was injured while hiking in Water Canyon.
IC was set up in coordination with Hildale Fire. Due to the subjects location, DPS Star 9
cal called to assist. Star 9 located the subject and lifted her out. She was brought to EMS
and taken to SGRH for treatment.

05/27/2023 @ 1540, Ledged Hiker, Rockville Area — Man Hours - 42

SAR responded to the Rockville area on a report of a 76 year old male hiker that was
stuck on the edge of a cliff. The Search and Rescue high angle team was dispatched to the
call. SAR high angle was able to get to the male and safely get him down the cliff. He was
released to his family.

05/27/2023 @ 1643, UTV Accident, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 20

SAR responded to the Hurricane Sand Dunes on a UTV accident. SAR and Hurricane
Fire located the 24 year old and 19 year old males. They were evaluated by EMS and
transported in a side by side. SAR members assisted with the transport from the site of
the accident to the ambulance. Both patients were transported by medical.
05/28/2023 @ 1610, UTV Accident, Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 18

SAR responded to a UTV Accident on the Hurricane Sand Dunes. SAR responded in
with EMS and Deputies. They arrived, evaluated the 25 year old male, and call for Life
Flight to respond. SAR assisted with securing the patient to a back board and
transporting to the waiting helicopter. The male was flown to SGRH for treatment.

05/28/2023 @ 1642, Aircraft Crash, South of Enterprise — Man Hours — 18

Deputies and SAR responded to a report of a small aircraft crash in the mountains south
of Enterprise. Enterprise Fire Dept. was able to locate the 49 year old pilot and make sure
he was okay. The pilot refused medical attention and left in a private vehicle. The FAA
will contact deputies when they are ready to get information they need. The pilot was
informed he needed to arrange for the aircraft to be picked up.

05/28/2023 @ 2015, Ill Horse Rider, Toquerville Falls — Man Hours — 12

SAR and Deputies responded to a 16 year old male that was dehydrated at the Toquerville

location for transport to a waiting ambulance located at the Toquerville church. The
