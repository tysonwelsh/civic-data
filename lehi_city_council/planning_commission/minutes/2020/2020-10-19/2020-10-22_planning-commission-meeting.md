Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1. Public hearing and recommendation of Arive Homes’ request for review of the Taylor
Meadows General Plan amendment, changing approximately 18 acres of property from
VLDRA (very low density residential agriculture) to VLDR (very low density residential)
located at approximately 1500 South 500 West.
    Sierra presented information on the agenda item. City Council approved the general plan
    amendment. Would increase density without an incentive in return.

    The applicant, Dan Herzog, was present and addressed the Commission and Staff. The
    applicant distributed the concept plan. The applicant expressed the community’s interest in

    the concept plan. Commissioner Hemmert stated they were only voting on the general plan
    amendment, not the concept plan tonight.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    One comment was not in favor.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert stated he didn't see elements that override that seven elements of
    criteria that would illicit change for the general plan. Commissioner Everett appreciated the
    applicant sharing the concept plan so the Commissioners could see the context as to what
    would be proposed if the general plan amendment was approved. Commissioner Peterson
    stated VLDRA did not get changed and over the last few years economics has been a factor
    and he likes to see what the applicant is proposing and is more opposed to changing that
    because it limits lot size to the community. He likes the diversity that it brings to the
    community and is having a hard time being in favor of this. Commissioner Everett is
    concerned with being in favor of a PUD where it would be an island around this.
    Commissioner Nielsen said there was a lot of LDR surrounding the property on all four sides,
    but the concept plan is helpful and feels it fits in with the neighborhood that is already
    existing. Commissioner Bunker expressed he was conflicted because of past discussions and
    many of the lots are flag lots now.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to forward a positive recommendation of Arive Homes’
request for review of the Taylor Meadows General Plan amendment, changing
approximately 18 acres of property from VLDRA (very low density residential agriculture)
to VLDR (very low density residential) located at approximately 1500 South 500 West,
including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.
Amended to add a finding of fact with the additions and recommendations. Second still stands.

Vote: Commissioner Peterson, no; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, no; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner
Bunker, yes. The motion passed 4-3.

2.2. Public hearing and recommendation of Billy Meadows’ request for review of a
Development Code Amendment, to Chapter 39 and the Table of Uses 05.030.B, amending
the definition of Dog Care Facility.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Peterson asked about why small breed dogs were only allowed. Sierra
    clarified the applicant was specifically only looking to have a dog facility that took care of
    small dogs.

    The applicant was not present.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.
Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to forward a positive recommendation of Billy Meadows’
request for review of a Development Code Amendment, to Chapter 39 and the Table of Uses
05.030.B, amending the definition of Dog Care Facility; not including Kim’s Neighborhood
Commercial Zone but all other DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.4. Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 21, Temporary Uses, clarifying and updating
the duration allowance for food trucks.
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item. City clarified use of
    temporary us and standard duration of use and location. Allow up to one year on the
    same property with renewal of up to one additional year with written consent of
    property owner. The intent of the City is to allow the food services flexibility and
    affordability.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to table Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Chapter 21, Temporary Uses, clarifying and updating
the duration allowance for food trucks. Commissioner Peterson seconded.


Vote: Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Bunker, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
