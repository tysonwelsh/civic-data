BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 4 acres located on the
south side of 13800 S at approximately 540 E (See Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2. The
applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the addition of 5 lots, for a total of
7 lots, to the Jarvie Estates Subdivision.

The existing 2 lot Jarvie Estates Subdivision was recorded in 2001. Lot 1 is 3.47 acres and Lot 2 is 0.53
acres. There is a single family home on each lot and the majority of the larger lot is heavily wooded with
large trees. Lot 2 has a 30-foot utility easement where the proposed 30-foot private lane will be installed.

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately two dwelling
units per acre (See Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the
purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to promote and
preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals
and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings,
protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.” The RA1 zoning abuts the subject
property on the west, and RA2 abuts on the south, east and north.

Plat Amendment. The proposed subdivision plat will contain a total of 7 lots. In the RA2 zone lots must
be at least 20,000 ft² in size and have proper access from a public or private road. (See Exhibit E). Parcel
A will be the private road (Jarvie Lane) and is 0.808 acres. This private road will exceed DCMC
requirements by 2-feet at 30-feet wide and will have a Fire Turnaround at the end of the road. Lot 101
will not have access to 13800 S but will be accessed off the private road. Additionally Lot 107 will not be
able to have access off Beachwood Drive but will also have access off the private road. Both of these lots
contain existing homes. The private road will be owned and maintained by the Home Owners Association
and will serve as a private road that will maintain as a public utility and drainage easement. All 7 lots will
access Jarvie Lane.
