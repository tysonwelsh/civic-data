CALL TO ORDER
Chair Jacob called the work session to order at 5:31 pm

A.       City Hall Remodel Update
Public Service Director Isaac Astill updated the Council on the remodel of City Hall. He explained
that interior finishes and colors would be the same as the new Public Works Building to help with
maintenance efficiencies. The Council reviewed floor plans and proposed furniture, and provided
feedback. Council Member Whitelock expressed the opinion that not all of the existing furniture
needed to be replaced. City Administrator Korban Lee estimated that 90% of the existing furniture
in the building would remain. A representative from JRCA Architects explained planned updates to
Council Chambers. Council Member Whitelock said she did not believe new chairs behind the dais
were necessary. The Council and staff discussed the proposed concept for the lobby, and discussed
possibly exploring additional options with regard to the arches. Mr. Astill explained plans to move
City employee work spaces during the remodel.

B.       2022 Annual Meeting Schedule & Alternate Meeting Location Discussion
Vice Chair Green moved to suspend Council Rules to allow Council Member-Elect Pamela
Bloom to join the conversation. Chair Jacob seconded the motion, which passed by majority
vote (6-1), with Council Member Lamb dissenting.

Council Office Director Alan Anderson asked for Council input regarding the 2022 Council Member
meeting schedule. Council Member McConnehey said he was comfortable with the 2022 meeting
schedule shown by staff and said he would prefer to include only regular Council meetings on the
schedule adopted by resolution. Vice Chair Green suggested moving Council meetings to Tuesday
evenings, and Council Members Worthen and Whitelock responded that several other groups
already held meetings on Tuesdays. Council Members Worthen and Whitelock spoke in favor of
keeping Council meetings on Wednesdays.

Vice Chair Green said he would prefer to put all anticipated meetings in which the Council would be
involved on the schedule to keep citizens informed and aware of when the Council planned to meet
(e.g., extra meetings to discuss the budget). Council Member McConnehey said he would be fine with
   City Council Work Session Minutes – November 16, 2021                                                  Page 2

   adding the extra meetings to the calendar but would not want to include them as “regular” Council
   meetings.

   The Council discussed the meeting schedule during the November/December holiday season. Chair
   Jacob suggested scheduling regular meetings on November 2, 16, and 30, with one meeting in
   December on December 14. Council Member Whitelock said she would be comfortable adopting the
   meeting schedule as presented by staff. Council Member McConnehey questioned how productive
   a meeting on November 30th could be.

   Chair Jacob expressed support for only including the 24 regular Council meetings on the schedule
   approved by resolution. Vice Chair Green and Council Member Worthen expressed support for
   including extra meetings on the schedule adopted by resolution. Council Member Whitelock pointed
   out that extra meetings could be calendared without including them on the schedule adopted by
   resolution. Mr. Lee suggested the Council give staff an idea of when they wanted to meet for budget
   discussions.

   Vice Chair Green moved to give procedural direction to staff approving the 2022 tentative
   schedule as presented by staff, and calendaring May 18, June 1, and June 15, 2022 as
   additional budget meetings as necessary. Council Member Worthen seconded the motion,
   which passed by majority vote (6-1), with Council Member McConnehey dissenting. Council
   Member McConnehey said he did not agree with calendaring a budget meeting on June 1,
   2022.

   A majority of the Council expressed support for meeting as a Council in the Public Works Building
   during City Hall renovation. Council Member Lamb expressed a desire for further discussion and
   asked why staff recommended the Police Department Community Room. Council Member Pack said
   it had been suggested that it may be intimidating to the public to attend meetings at the Police
   Station. Mr. Anderson said he believed the Police Department Community Room was better suited
   with tech capability, an outside entrance, and location close to the City Center. He said the Public
   Works Training Room was not as accessible, and the tech was not set up as well for Council
   meetings. He emphasized that the Council would meet at the alternate site for at least eight months.

   Council Member McConnehey said the more he thought about the question, the more he believed
   the Police Department Community Room was the better option because of proximity to City Hall
   and available parking. Council Member Whitelock responded the Public Works Building had plenty
   of parking with neighboring soccer complex parking.

   Council Members Jacob, Worthen, and Whitelock expressed preference for the Public Works
   Building. Mr. Anderson said staff would do whatever the Council wanted, but repeated his
   recommendation to use the Police Department Community Room. Council Member Pack asked if
   the Fire Station would be a viable option. Mr. Anderson said he had not considered the Fire Station.
   A majority of the Council said they wanted to keep the Zoom option for Council meetings.

   Council Chair Zach Jacob adjourned the work session at 6:54 p.m.

I, Cindy Quick, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent an accurate summary of what occurred at the
meeting held on November 16, 2021. This document constitutes the official minutes for the West Jordan City Council
Meeting.
