 3            Land Use Ordinance Text Amendment to Section 11.120.060 to Allow Gravel Parking

6
 7   Planning Manager, Caitljoi Tubbs, presented the Staff Report and stated that the applicant proposed
8    establishing a seasonal haunted circus at 734 West 14600 South(Bluffdale Boulevard). The property
9    is located in the Heavy Commercial ("HC") Zone, and the proposed use falls under the Land Use
10   designation "Recreation and Entertainment, Outdoor," which is a Conditional Use in the zone. In
11   addition to applying for the Text Amendment for a gravel parking lot, the applicant will need to go
12   through the Conditional Use Permit("CUP") and Site Plan Review processes. They indicated that
13   the ability to utilize a gravel parking lot is pivotal to the feasibility of the proposal, so they were
14   requesting the Text Amendment prior to submitting the other applications.
15
16   Bluffdale City Code § 11.120.060(A) specifies that all parking lots and parking structures shall be
17   hard-surfaced with asphalt or concrete. The City Engineers have specific standards for materials and
18   construction methods in order to pass inspection. The applicant proposed the following Text
19   Amendment to allow for gravel parking lots to be utilized instead of the required paved parking
20   surface.

21
22   1.       EUgibiUty
23        • Gravel parking lots may be permitted in lieu of paved parking surfaces for projects requiring
24            a CUP,subject to the following conditions:
25        • The project must demonstrate that a gravel surface is more appropriate for the intended use
26            considering factors such as cost, environmental impact, or stormwater management.
27        • The proposed gravel parking lot must meet all City safety and drainage standards.
28
29   2.       Design and Construction Standards
30   Gravel parking lots approved imder this subsection must comply with the following standards:
31        • Base Preparation: A compacted subgrade and suitable base layer to ensure durability and
32            prevent erosion.
33        • Gravel Type: Crushed gravel with a size and composition approved by the City Engineer.

 1        • Drainage: A drainage plan, approved by the City Engineer, to manage runoff.
2
3    3.       Maintenance Requirements
4         • The property owner must maintain the gravel parking lot in good condition, free from
5             potholes, weeds, and debris.
6         • Regular grading may be required to ensure a level surface.
7
8    4.       Review and Approval
9         • Applications for gravel parking lots must be submitted as part ofthe CUP application.
10        • Approval is subject to review by the Planning Commission and compliance with all applicable
11            City standards.
12
13   Ms.Tubbs noted that, per State Code,Conditional Uses must be approved unless there are anticipated
14   detrimental effects that cannot be offset by reasonable conditions. The City of Bluffdale previously
15   moved common uses that do not have a significant negative impact on the Permitted Uses list. Items
16   left on the Non-Residential Conditional Use list in the Land Use Table are higher intensity uses such
17   as general manufacturing, hospitals, recreation and entertainment, etc. Residential Conditional Uses
18   include canine hobby licenses, equestrian facilities, golfcourses,and the sale ofagricultural products.
19   Ifthe City were to allow gravel parking lots, they would be allowed for all Conditional Uses.
20
21   The Engineering Department expressed concerns about trackout and stormwater impacts as gravel
22   migrates. They would want to ensure that measures are in place to keep the gravel on the site and out
23   of the storm drainage facilities. In addition, allowing gravel parking lots could result in additional
24   street sweeping and cleaning needs. They also expressed concem about long-term upkeep and the
25   ability ofthe surface to hold vehicle weight, particularly emergency vehicles.
26
27   Ms. Tubbs reviewed changes to relevant portions of Bluffdale City Code since the City was
28   incorporated in 1978 and noted that the requirement for paved surfaces has been consistently upheld
29   throughout all changes to the Code. Staff found that the proposed language may require additional
30   detail and formatting to detail things like who is responsible for overall upkeep and reinspection
31   requirements.

2    Commissioner Flynn asked if gravel is allowed in any zones. Ms. Tubbs reported that the Sand and
 3   Gravel Zone allows for the storage ofthose types of materials and there is some open storage that is
4    grandfathered in in Industrial areas. However, it is very uncommon in Bluffdale. In response to a
5    follow-up question, Ms. Tubbs clarified that if the City were to amend the City Code to allow for
6    gravel parking lots, conditions included in the CUP could designate a particular staff member or
 7   department to facilitate enforcement or reinspection. Ifit was not addressed in the CUP,either Code
8    Enforcement, the Building Official, or the Fire Marshall would inspect the property for compliance.
9
10   Chair Cragun eisked ifthe consistent requirement for asphalt or concrete has been a conscious decision
11   to deter gravel or just because no one had asked for approval to use it. Ms. Tubbs stated that the
12   minutes from meetings in which the specific Code sections were adopted did not specifically mention
13   someone requesting gravel.       However, when they recently updated the City Standards and
14   Specifications, the engineering manuals explicitly stated that a hard-paved surface is required. Extra
15   costs associated with street sweeping, etc., would be discussed during the review of the CUP
16   application, and conditions can be imposed based on the Planning Commission's observations and
17   Staffs findings.
18
19   In response to a question raised by Commissioner Swanson, Ms. Tubbs clarified that if approved, a
20   gravel parking lot could potentially be available to all Conditional Uses. In response to a follow-up
21   question, Ms.Tubbs stated that they would request more information about the specific type ofgravel
22   and road base to be used,the method ofinstallation and compaction,the plan for ongoing maintenance
23   and inspection ofthe surface,and a work plan to address any deficiencies found upon inspection. The
24   City's main obligation is to the health, safety, and welfare of the public, and any problems with a
25   gravel parking lot that would impede an emergency vehicle from responding would be a concern.
26   They would also need to do additional formatting and add specificity regarding installation,
27   warranties, etc. She clarified that the State requires the City to adopt all requirements in a standard
28   or Ordinance, so they cannot include a requirement for future approval by the Building Official or
29   City Engineer.

1    Chair Cragun asked if it would be possible to write the Ordinance to only apply to seasonal uses so
2    all Conditional Uses would not be allowed to have gravel parking lots. Ms. Tubbs stated that the
3    language could be made more specific, but she reminded the Commission that often ^temporary" is
4    not really temporary. When the Applicant first approached the City, they looked at the existing
5    Temporary Use Code,which provides for a maximum timeffame of30 days. The Applicant indicated

8    Commissioner Flynn asked if Staff felt that more time was needed to add more detail to the Text
9    Amendment. Ms.Tubbs stated that some issues can be corrected by modifying the language,but they
10   would need direction from the Planning Commission in order to proceed with other changes.
11
12   The applicant, Robert Tillotson, provided background on the company. He has been doing haunted
13   houses for 30 years and has had them in Provo, Ogden, Draper, American Fork, and three cities in
14   Texas. They understand the need to keep the parking lot clean, and they have had no complaints from
15   the cities they've worked with. His experience is that the gravel parking lot works well and is a good
16   compromise. The type of gravel that is used in the parking area is different from the type used near
17   the exit, so trackout is kept to a minimum. He believes the parcel will eventually have businesses on
18   it, so a paved parking lot would have to be demolished in the future.
19
20   In response to a question raised by Commissioner Swanson, Mr. Tillotson stated that sometimes they
21   have to bring in different soil to use as a foundation for the gravel. They would conduct a compaction
22   test to determine if that is necessary, but he believes the base layer at the location will not require
23   modification. It is their responsibility to ensure that the parking lot can support emergency vehicles
24   and semi-trucks. Ms. Tubbs clarified that those requirements would also be addressed in the
25   standards.

26
27   In response to a question from Commissioner Flynn, Mr. Tillotson stated that his Draper property
28   was on the frontage road between Harmon's and the interstate. The American Fork location is at 632
29   East 1500 South.
