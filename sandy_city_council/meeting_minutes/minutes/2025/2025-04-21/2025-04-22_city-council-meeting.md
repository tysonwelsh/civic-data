3.         25-110                 First Reading: Council Member Robinson proposing that the City Council
                                  direct city staff to draft amendments to the municipal code to both
                                  streamline the process, lower the financial barrier to entry, and broaden the
                                  locations where brew pubs are permitted within the City
                     Sponsors: Robinson

                   Attachments:   Brew Pubs Memo_Robinson

                                  Council Member Zach Robinson presented his proposal to expand permitted locations for
                                  Brew Pubs within Sandy City to extend beyond the Cairns District, which is primarily
                                  where these establishments are currently permitted. These businesses are already
                                  currently regulated by the state. He would like to direct staff to review our current code,
                                  compare it to current state code, and come up with recommendations and draft
                                  amendments to the city's current municipal code to both streamline the process, lower
                                  the financial barrier to entry, and broaden the locations where brew pubs are permitted
                                  within Sandy. He believes this change would result in enhancing economic development
                                  and offer additional amenities to our residents. It is a good time for this review in light of
                                  our newly passed general plan. Council provided feedback and was supportive of directing
                                  staff to review the city's municipal code and prepare recommendations to the Council.

4.         25-106                 Community Development Department presenting the 2025-2029
                                  Consolidated Plan and First Year Annual Action Plan (PY2025), including
                                  the proposed PY2025 project funding allocation, for the use of Community
                                  Development Block Grant funds as recommended by the Citizen Advisory
                                  Committee.
                   Attachments:   Goal and Activity Summary (proposed)
                                  Presentation (City Council 4/22/2025)
                                  2025-2029 Consolidated Plan and 2025 Annual Action Plan (draft)

                                  Public Hearing: Jake Warner, Community Development, presented the 2025-2029
                                  Consolidated Plan and 2025 Annual Action Plan. Included in his presentation was the
                                  Proposed Project Year 2025 funding allocation recommended by the Community
                                  Development Block Grant (CDBG) Program Committee. The purpose of the CDBG
                                  program is to develop viable communities by providing decent housing and a suitable
                                  living environment, and by expanding economic opportunities. The purpose of the HOME
                                  program is to fund a wide range of activities including building, buying, and/or
                                  rehabilitating affordable housing for rent or homeownership or providing direct rent
                                  assistance. Mr. Warner reviewed the CDBG National Objectives, the current areas in
                                  Sandy that qualify for funding, the eligible and ineligible activities for funding, and other
                                  federal regulations. The Annual Action Plan program year runs from July 1 through June
                                  20, 2026. He reviewed the process and timeline for approval with the Council, with the
                                  final submission date to HUD due in May of 2025 for the next program year.The available
                                  funding for the 2025-26 program year is approximately $352,069. Mr. Warner reviewed the
                                  recommended funding allocation by the CDBG Committee. Council questions and
                                  comments occurred throughout the presentation.

                                  Public Hearing comment period opened:

                                  Ms. Baleigh Dellos with the Medically Vulnerable People facility thanked the Council and
                                  CDBG Committee for the recommended funding allocation. The MVP facility provides for
                                  medically vulnerable people experiencing homelessness. She spoke about the program's
                                  success and outreach assistance.

                                  Mr. Jason Wheeler thanked the Council and expressed gratitude for the grant funding
                                  recommendation. He spoke about the services his organization provides, which includes
                                  emergency home repairs for residents who cannot afford the repairs.

                                  Ms. Kate Nielsen, COO for Habitat for Humanity, thanked the Council and the CDBG
                                  Committee for the recommended funding allocation. She shared details about the
                                  important work and services provided by her organization and thanked the City for their
                                  support.

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren thought the CDBG Committee did a good job with the
                                  recommended funding allocation.

                                  Public Hearing comment period closed.

                                  The Council thanked Jake Warner and the CDBG Committee for the thoughtful work and
                                  dedication to our community.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Marci Houseman mentioned that she was selected to be part of the
                    Hunt Institute Early Learning Policy Fellow inaugural cohort that brings together policy
                    makers specifically from municipal governments. In this role, she has also been able to
                    join the Utah's Early Care and Education Subcommittee with the State Board of
                    Education. She provided an update from the meeting she attended. She spoke about the
                    importance of access to high quality childcare and the impact this has on businesses in
                    our city and thought this was an issue that should be included and addressed in a city's
                    economic development master plan.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud spoke about the process used for the concrete pour for
                    the structural improvements currently underway at Bicentennial Park. She watched the
                    concrete pour and spoke with public works employees about the tension application and
                    process used. She said it was a very impressive process that helps with the longevity of
                    the surface. She spoke about the improvements at the park and the improved technology
                    available used to make playgrounds safer for kids.

                    Council Member Sharkey provided an update from the Utah League of Cities and Towns
                    Mid-year conference that she and Council Member D'Sousa attended. It was a record
                    turnout with over 500 participants. It was a productive, informative, and well-run
                    conference. She attended the recent RSL event in Sandy. It was an exciting event with
                    over 300 people in attendance. At the press conference, the Miller Group announced the
                    purchase of the Real Salt Lake and Royals teams.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski spoke of the passing of Pope Francis, a champion of justice for the poor
                    who was a leader on the world stage and stands out for his kindness and compassion.
                    He was the first Pope of the Jesuit Order and of Latin America. People around the world
                    are mourning his loss. This Saturday is Earth Day and Sandy Beautification Day.
                    Community volunteers are welcome. She spoke about the Elementary Art Show, National
                    Volunteer Week and thanked all of our city and community volunteers. She spoke about
                    upcoming events in Sandy and is excited for the many things and events coming to
                    Sandy City.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO had no report.
