3. Open Public Hearing
A motion was made by Council Member Winder Newton, seconded by Council Member 
Stringham, to open the public hearing. The motion carried by a unanimous vote.  Council 
Member Bradley was absent for the vote.
Mr. Steve Van Maren stated he had no argument with the pay increase for Salt Lake 
County employees.  He was also in favor of contributing $ 13,500 for the purchase of 
livestock from the 4H program.  It is a small amount.
4. Council Comments
Council Member Alvord stated he felt there was a major thing missing from the budget 
process and that was public input.  He was interested in ideas to get the public involved 
and asked Council Members to help him brainstorm for ways to make that happen. 
.
5. Close Public Hearing
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council Member Winder 
Newton, to close the public hearing.  The motion carried by a unanimous vote. Council 
Member Bradley was absent for the vote.

WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Mayor, acting as budget officers for Salt 
Lake County, has proposed amendments to the Salt Lake County ’s final 
budget for fiscal year 2021, including the partial restoration of certain 
year-over-year budgetary reductions that were initially imposed in response 
to the COVID-19 pandemic and the 2021 United States recession; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council has conducted public budget 
workshops on the proposed amendments to the Salt Lake County ’s final 
budget for fiscal year 2021, and has provided affected Salt Lake County 
offices, departments, and agencies more than five days’ notice and an 
opportunity to be heard concerning proposed adjustments to previously 
approved fiscal year 2021 budget appropriations, as required by Utah Code 
Ann. § 17-36-24; and
WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17-36-23 and 17-36-26, require that any 
increases or transfers in any of the several budgetary funds of the County be 
approved and adopted by resolution of the Salt Lake County Council; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council now finds it expedient to make 
certain amendments to the previously adopted final fiscal year 2021 budgets 
for Salt Lake County, to make certain fund transfers that reflect the County ’s
