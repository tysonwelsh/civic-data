#2 Buena Vista Ranch Phase 1 Subdivision 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the Buena Vista Ranch Phase 1 Subdivision. 
  
Staff and Commission discussed the daily trips report, undevelopable areas and how it affects 
development of the land.  
 
Lou Swain commented regarding access. 
 
Jon White asked if they planned to use Ant Flat Road. 
 
Mr. Swain answered yes. 
 
Mr. Swain commented on water, and approval for the septic. 
 
Gunnell asked about access. 
 
Harrild responded that a typical road for a cabin is required to be 12 feet.

Mr. Swain commented that the current roads are two lanes and have been maintained by the 
Swan and Petersen ranches. The road improvements needed, will be completed. 
 
Edwards asked if they were using the Weber County access. 
 
Mr. Swain responded yes and that it is the primary access for the Petersen Ranch. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed fire coverage and what the fire district requires. The 
subdivision plat will need to be updated to show the correct access point. 
 
Gunnell motioned to recommend approval for the Buena Vista Ranch Phase 1 Subdivision to the 
County Council with the stated findings of facts, conclusions, and the conditions with the 
changes to condition #4; Sands seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
05:58:00 
 
#3 Code Amendments 
 
Harrild reviewed the amendments to Titles 17.01 and 17.02 regarding authority and plain 
language. 
 
Edwards commented that contact for administrative projects should be handled by staff or at 
planning commission meetings. Contact for legislative projects should be handled by County 
Council. 
 
Olsen motioned to recommend approval to the County Council for the ordinance amendments 
for Titles 17.01 and 17.02; Gunnell seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
06:18:00 
 
Adjourned
