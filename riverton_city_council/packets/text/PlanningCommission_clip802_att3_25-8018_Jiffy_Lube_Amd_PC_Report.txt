This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application, the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motions
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-25-8018 “Jiffy Lube Retail Façade Upgrade”
amending the site plan of the existing commercial buildings located at 1625 West 12600 South
with the following condition:

    1. Building materials and color will match the submitted information as shown and
       approved by the Planning Commission.

Background

This application is for a minor façade upgrade to the existing Jiffy Lube retail business located at
1625 West 12600 South. That building was approved with trim and corner treatments that were
tan in color. Staff was made aware that the applicant was repainting the trim and corner
treatment from tan to black, and subsequently stopped work on that pending review and action
by the Planning Commission. The submitted photos so that partially completed work, so the
original tan color is shown with some of the elements already painted black.

The color itself can be found compliant with Riverton City’s architectural standards, and staff
does not have concerns or objections with the modification, but approval is required as an
amended Commercial Site Plan. The applicant will be present at the hearing to respond to
questions or concerns.

Recommendations

Planning staff have reviewed these development plans and are recommending approval.
