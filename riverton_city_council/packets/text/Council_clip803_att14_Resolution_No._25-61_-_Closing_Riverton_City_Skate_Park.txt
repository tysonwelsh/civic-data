                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-61

  A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE RIVERTON CITY SKATE PARK BE CLOSED
                      FOR USE BY THE PUBLIC

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council is empowered to manage the real property of
Riverton City, including “improve, protect, and do any other thing in relation to this property that
and individual could do;” pursuant to Utah Code § 10-8-2; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City owns and operates the Riverton Skate Park, located in the
Riverton City Main Park; and

        WHEREAS, the Skate Park has provided continual service to Riverton City residents
since its construction in the year 2003; and

      WHEREAS, given the number of years the Skate Park has served Riverton City, it is
showing signs of wear and has reached the end of its useful life; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City staff has explored options to repair areas of the Riverton
Skate Park which have experienced age related wear; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City staff has solicited bids and received reports from contractors
with expertise in the construction and repair of skate parks, and the recommendation received is
to remove and replace all Skate Park facilities because satisfactory repair of the same is not
plausible; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has received a report from the Utah Risk Management
Agency which verifies that the Skate Park is in an unsafe condition, and further recommends that
the park be closed for use by the public,

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby declares the Riverton Skate Park closed for use by
          the public until further notice. The Riverton City Manager is authorized and directed
          to take whatever steps are necessary to secure the Riverton City Skate Park in a
          manner which protects the public from gaining access to the same.

       2. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.
