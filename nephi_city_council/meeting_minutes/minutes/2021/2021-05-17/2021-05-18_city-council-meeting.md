ZONE CHANGE ORDINANCE TABELED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented Ordinance 05-18-2021 for adoption. The ordinance was written to change
the zone from R1-H to CU. The property was owned by Mr. Paul McPherson who plans on building some
higher density housing. The location of the property is approximately 845 South Main. Councilor Seely
mentioned he would like to see a development plan that included green space. The council agreed to
table the zone change until they receive more information about the planned development.

SUNRISE ENGINEERING AWARDED WASTEWATER MASTER PLAN UPDATE BID:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that the city’s current growth has made it necessary to
update the city’s Wastewater Master Plan. A master plan identifies projects that need to be completed
to keep the system efficiently operating and to handle the increase in usage due to growth. These
projects then inform the Council in their policy making when setting rates and instituting impact fees.
   Recently the city made a request for qualifications from interested engineers to assist the city with
the wastewater master plan update. The city received statements of qualifications from three very
qualified engineers, Jones & DeMille, Ensign Engineering, and Sunrise Engineering. All three submissions
were reviewed by a staff committee. After review, Sunrise Engineering was recommended to assist the
city with this master plan update. Nephi City has had successful projects completed with Sunrise,
including the current water project and the redundant natural gas pipeline project.
   Councilor Memmott asked about the scope of the Master Plan and if it would include the cost of
putting in a treatment plant. Councilor Worwood asked about the possibility of putting a fourth sewer
lagoon more on the south end of the city. Mayor Nielson asked if Sunrise Engineering had any
experience of the lining of the sewer lines.
     Councilor Jones moved to award the Wastewater Master Plan project to Sunrise Engineering.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

AIRPORT AGREEMENT ADDENDUM #1 – KAHUBE AERO, LLC:

   Mr. Atkinson informed that council that recently the city had tried to work with a potential hangar
owner to locate the construction of a new hangar on the airport property. After several discussions with
the hangar owner, it was decided that the best location may be on the south end of the current row of
hangars. This area is under agreement with the Fixed Base Operator (FBO), Kahube Aero, LLC to develop
in conjunction with the city. Kahube Aero has graciously agreed to move the area under agreement
further south to accommodate this hangar construction. An addendum to the original agreement was
necessary to make these changes. Councilor Ostler moved to authorize the mayor to sign the
addendum to the original agreement with Kahube Aero, LLC. Councilor Jones seconded the motion,
which passed on a unanimous vote.
