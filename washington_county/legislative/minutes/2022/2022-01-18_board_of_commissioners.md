CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1202-O AN ORDINANCE REZONING A
CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
1700 EAST ON THE NORTH SIDE OF SR 144 NEAR NEW HARMONY FROM THE A-
20_ (AGRICULTURAL 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE, TO THE RA-5
(RESIDENTIAL _AGRICULTURAL _5 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE
HEREINAFTER FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS ORDINANCE

Mr. Messel explained that the applicants are proposing a 30 lot subdivision near New Harmony.
Initially, the applicants requested an RE-40 (Residential Estates 40,000 square foot minimum lot
size) zone. The lots range in size from approximately 2.5 acres to 5.5 acres in size. After taking
public comment and deliberation the Planning Commission instead recommended the R-5
(Residential Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zone.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2022-1202-O, An
Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County Located at
Approximately 1700 East on the North Side of SR 144 Near New Harmony
from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone to the RA-5
(Residential Agricultural 5 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone, adopting all zoning
recommendations from the staff report and the planning commission. Motion
seconded by Chairman Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2894, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_COMPLETE REDUCTION OF PAST PROPERTY TAXES FOR A PARCEL
DONATED TO WASHINGTON COUNTY

Deputy Attorney Snow explained that Robert Brennan donated property located in the Red Cliffs
Desert Reserve to Washington County, and as a result of an error in the legal description in the
original deed, ownership under county records was not changed from private ownership to
Washington County. Since the date of the original deed, property taxes have accrued on the
donation parcel. The property owner is requesting that the property taxes along with any penalties
and interest to be reduced to zero.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2894, A
Resolution Approving a Complete Reduction of Past Property Taxes for a
Parcel Donated to Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and
Almquist voting aye.
