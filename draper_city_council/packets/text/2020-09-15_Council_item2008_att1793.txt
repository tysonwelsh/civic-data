An Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Draper City and Salt Lake County funding
construction by Salt Lake County of an automatic gate at the park entrance.

         THIS INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) is between SALT
LAKE COUNTY (“County”) and DRAPER CITY. (“City”). The County and the City may each be
referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

                                               RECITALS
   The County owns certain real property located approximately at 15300 S. Steep Mountain Drive in
   Draper City, more specifically identified as Parcel Nos 33-13-200-003 (the “Property”).
   The County has constructed a parking lot on the Property for the County owned Flight Park (the
   “Parking Lot”).
   The County and City have determined it to be in their best mutual interests for the Parking Lot to
   have an automatic gate that opens and closes at set times (the “Gate”).
   The County will plan, design construct, and install the Gate.
   The City will pay all costs associated with the construction of the Gate, up to a not to exceed
   amount.
   The Parties now desire to enter into this Agreement to transfer the City’s funds to the County to
   facilitate the construction of the Project.
   The Parties are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code
   Ann.§ 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Cooperation Act”), and, as such, are authorized by the Cooperation
   Act to enter into this Agreement to act jointly and cooperatively in a manner that will enable them to
   make the most efficient use of their resources and powers.

   The Parties agree as follows:
       The defined terms identified in the Recitals are hereby incorporated by reference into this
       Agreement.
       This Agreement shall be effective as of the date last signed by the either Party, as indicated by the
       date noted for that Party’s signature (the “Effective Date”). This Agreement will terminate upon
       full performance by both parties.
       The total not to exceed amount the City will pay under this Agreement is $60,000.00.
       County’s Obligations
               The County will design, construct, and own the Gate
               Opening and closing times will be set to comply with Draper City ordinance.
               The County will submit an invoice to the City after the County receives and pays the
               contractor the final payment for satisfactory construction of the Gate.
       City’s Obligations
               Within 30 days of receiving an invoice as described in section 4. above, the City will
               pay the County the invoice amount.
