Applicant Presentation: None

Staff Presentation:         John Vuyk, Finance Director

RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Resolution 25-29 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule of Draper City as proposed.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule lists fees charged by Draper City, the fees offset operational and
administrative costs based on current city operation and are being updated to reflect current costs.

The proposed changes will update the fees schedule to better align with costs associated with rental of
the Day Barn and the fees charged for water rates based on Ordinance #1657.
