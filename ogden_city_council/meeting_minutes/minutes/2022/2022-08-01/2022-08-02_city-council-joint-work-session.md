The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00
p.m.; discuss City Council and Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Board actions relating to the Land Use Transfer and Development
Agreement for property at 2524 Wall Avenue; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review

Vice Chair Lopez and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m. Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported that Council staff discovered a provision in the
State Law governing the Truth in Taxation process, which prohibits the Council from including any other items of business on
meeting agenda during which the Truth in Taxation hearing is scheduled. For that reason, staff scheduled a separate special
meeting at 5:55 p.m. to allow the Council to take action on consent items 3a and 3b be tabled until August 9, 2022. She also
summarized optional actions available to the Council at the conclusion of the Truth in Taxation hearing; this includes adoption of
the budget and associated tax increase as well as action on the salary scheduled for employees and elected officials.

Land Use Transfer and Development Agreement — 2524 Wall Avenue, City
Council and RDA action

Community and Economic Development Director Cooper presented the proposed Land Transfer and Development
Agreement and the associated Participation and Reimbursement Agreement for a townhome and multi-family loft development at
2524 Wall Avenue. The project would add five new townhomes to the area originally intended to be Phase II of the 25" Street
Union Square Condominiums and 116 apartment units on property fronting on Wall Avenue. As proposed, the RDA would
transfer the land at no cost to the developer and would enter into a reimbursement agreement for tax increment from the
Continental Community Reinvestment Area for up to $2.6 million over a period of 15 years. The agreement is with Salt Lake City
based Highline Partners, LLC. Highline Partners, LLC approached the Agency with an unsolicited proposal for the development.
He presented the site plan to orient the Council to the location of the subject property and the layout of the buildings, after which
he briefly reviewed the terms of the agreements that will govern the project.

Discussion then centered briefly on the ongoing consideration of a developer selection process, and the manner in which
developers would have been aware that the City was open to considering this type of project and agreement for development of
the subject property. Council Executive Director Eller-Smith noted that the City administered a very extensive public noticing
process when the Kiesel Community Development Area was created, which should have served as notification to the development
community that the City was looking for development partners for properties within the project area.

Ms. Eller-Smith then noted that the property is currently being used as a detention pond and she asked what will happen
with the water that will be displaced by this project. Mr. Cooper stated that one of the conditions of the agreement is for the
developer to design a retention system that will accommodate the same amount of water, plus the additional water to be created by
the development.

Closing discussion centered briefly on historical use of the land and the manner in which the City acquired it, which Mr.
Cooper stating the best indicator of future potential private investment in the property is what has occurred in the past; the
property has sat vacant for 17 years with private investment occurring on surrounding properties. The City does not want to pursue
development that will otherwise be handled by the private market and the City has only chosen to participate in development of
the subject property due to a lack of private interest for nearly two decades. The Council then concluded they are comfortable with
proceeding to consideration of final action on the agreements in future business meetings.

The meeting adjourned at 4:38 p.m.
