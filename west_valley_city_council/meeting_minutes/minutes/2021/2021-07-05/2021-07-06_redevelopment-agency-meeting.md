RESOLUTION 21-07: APPROVE AMENDMENT NO. 4 TO THE AGREEMENT
FOR DISPOSITION OF LAND FOR PRIVATE DEVELOPMENT WITH HEXCEL
CORPORATION
Wayne Pyle, Chief Executive Officer, presented proposed Resolution 21-07 that would
approve Amendment No. 4 to the Agreement for Disposition of Land for Private Development
with Hexcel Corporation.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       In October of 2020, the RDA became aware that Hexcel was considering moving
       its HQ & R&D facility from California to another location. During a quick
       evaluation of Hexcel’s WVC location, the RDA determined the site has enough
       additional acreage to accommodate this new facility. The RDA saw an opportunity
       to attract the new project which consists of $25M of capital investment along with
       115-150 jobs with salaries between $130-180,000 per year.

       Hexcel currently has a tax increment finance (TIF) agreement in place to capture
       up to $18.7M of over $350M of capital investment. The RDA proposes to simply
       raise the ceiling from $18.7M to $20M and give Hexcel the opportunity to try and
       capture an additional $1.3M of post-performance tax increment. Up to date, Hexcel
       has captured $10M of its potential $18.7M max.

       Additional TIF captured by Hexcel will serve to offset additional underground
       infrastructure costs needed for the research & development portion of its facility
       along with interior access improvements to the site in order accommodate the
       structure to be built. This is an important detail to make sure the West Valley City
       site is competitive with the other proposed sites.

       The opportunity for Hexcel to capture an additional $1.3M represents less than a
       50% pass through to Hexcel.

Upon inquiry by Chairperson Lang there were no further questions from members of the
Agency, and she called for a motion.

       Mr. Buhler moved to approve Resolution 21-01.

       Mr. Christensen seconded the motion.
