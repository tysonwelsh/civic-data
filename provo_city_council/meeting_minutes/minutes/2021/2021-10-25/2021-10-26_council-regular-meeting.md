235   Cliff Strachan, Council Executive Director, presented. The council and administration received a
236   request by a towing company operator to look at towing rates that had not changed since January
237   2014. After council review, they recommended setting the rates at eighty percent (80%) of the
238   amount allowed under Utah Code Section 41-6a-1409(4)(a). That way, we would not have to
239   change our ordinance every time the state made a change to their rates.
