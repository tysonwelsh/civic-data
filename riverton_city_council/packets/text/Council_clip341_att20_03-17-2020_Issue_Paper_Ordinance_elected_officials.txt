           RIVERTON CITY WORK SESSION / COUNCIL MEETING



FROM:         Ryan Carter
DATE:         March 17, 2020
SUBJECT:      Ordinance amending the compensation schedule for elected officials.




SUMMARY: This item follows an amendment to the City budget wherein the Council has
considered approving the budget-related aspects of updating the City’s compensation for elected
officials. If the amended budget is approved as planned, staff recommends adoption of an
amendment to Riverton City Code § 2.105.010(1), which speaks to the compensation of elected
officials. The amendment will base compensation on a fixed percentage of the mid-range for a grade
20 City employee.

to amend the compensation schedule of city elected officials.”
