                                          RECITALS:

   A. The parties entered into interlocal agreement (County # AE08498C) (“Agreement”) as of
      February 24, 2009, for the construction and operation of the Riverton Senior Center.

   B. The parties executed Amendment No. 1 to the Agreement, on or about December 12, 2011,
      which modified Sections 9(A) and 10(A) of the Agreement as to the duties and
      responsibilities of the parties for the maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center through the
      adoption of Exhibit A attached to and incorporated into Amendment No. 1.

   C. The parties desire to revise the duties and responsibilities of the parties as to the
      maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center, and to provide the minimum time and hourly fee
      that will be charged for the specific maintenance items handled by the County, which
      supersedes any earlier price list and is attached hereto and incorporated herein as Exhibit A.

   D. The parties further desire to again revise the duties and responsibilities of the parties as to
      the custodial cleaning schedule of the Riverton Senior Center as set forth in Exhibit B which
      is attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference.




                                                 1
                                     AGREEMENT AMENDMENT

       1. Section (10)(A) of the Interlocal Agreement is amended to include the duties and
responsibilities set forth in Exhibit A, which is attached hereto and incorporated by reference.
COUNTY, by and through its Facilities Management Division, shall perform the responsibilities
contained in Exhibit A, according to the rates and estimated times contained therein. These
responsibilities include certain HVAC system preventative maintenance, plumbing system
preventative maintenance, and electrical and security system preventative maintenance.

        2.     Section (9)(A) of the Interlocal Agreement is amended to include the preventative
maintenance duties and responsibilities set forth in Exhibit A. The CITY shall continue to fulfill
the responsibilities contained in Section (9)(A) as set forth in the underlying agreement except
for those responsibilities transferred to COUNTY pursuant to Exhibit A.

        3.    In the event of an emergency, the City shall perform the emergency services
within four hours of being notified by COUNTY. In the event of normal repairs and maintenance
the CITY shall perform said repairs within two (2) business days.

        4.     If the CITY is unable to perform the emergency services or maintenance within
the period specified above, County shall have the right to have said services performed by
County Facilities and bill the City for said services.


        5.    The City shall be responsible and perform the custodial cleaning services as set
forth in Exhibit B.


      6. The CITY shall use properly licensed and certified vendors to perform the emergency
services, preventative maintenance and/or custodial cleaning services.

          7. All other provisions of the underlying Interlocal Agreement remain in full force and
effect.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this First Amendment on the day and
year first above written.
