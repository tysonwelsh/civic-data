Jason Binks said the applicant, Jacob Weber, requests approval of a preliminary :

subdivision plat to reconfigure three adjacent parcels at 7039-7045 S 300 E into a 6-lot

subdivision with the purpose of building 3 twin homes. The twin homes labeled units 1 &

2 and units 3 & 4 will be on either side of a single driveway entrance from 300 E, and the

flag lot will be in the back for units 5 & 6. A single shared driveway will be used for all 6

units.

The 3 existing parcels have a total of 0.71 acres with 155 feet of frontage on 300 East.

Two existing single family homes with detached accessory structures were recently

demolished at this location. The proposed parcels meet lot standards and, according to

Title 17-7-1.14, can be subdivided to allow for individual ownership of each side of a single

family attached structure. The resultant 6 lots will be between 0.094 — 0.133 acres each.

The subject property is zoned Single Family Residential with Duplex Overlay (SF-1/DO).

All surrounding properties are in the same zoning district and there are adjacent single

family homes to the north, south, and east. There is a master planned development

across 300 East with 5 twin homes, a total of 10 residential units. All new development is

required to comply with the standards of the SF-1/DO zone. The proposed subdivision is

subject to Midvale City Municipal Code Title 16 (Subdivisions) and Chapter 17-7-1 (SF-

1/DO). The subdivision is subject to the large subdivision process in Title 16.04.050.

The proposed Mid 6 Twin Homes Subdivision includes 6 lots with a common 20’ driveway

shared by all residential units. There are 2 structures on frontage lots and 1 structure in

the rear. Per Title 16.04.040(A)(6), residential flag lots “may share an access drive for up

Jason Binks said the applicant John Thornton and Dale Bennet requests a rezone of 5
adjacent parcels at 532, 544 and 562 E Fort Union Blvd from Single Family Residential
with Duplex and 7200 S Overlays (SF-1/DO & 7200 S) to Mixed Use with 7200 S Overlay
(MU/7200 S). The rezoned parcels would total 0.82 acres. If the rezone is approved, the
applicant proposes a 17-unit master planned development on 1.1 acres called Palais 550
Townhomes. A master planned development is a conditionally permitted use in the
proposed MU zone. On March 24, 2021, the Planning Commission approved the 17-unit
development contingent on the Council’s approval of the rezone to MU.
The proposed project is a master planned development containing 4 buildings with 17
residential units. The proposed development will include the 5 rezoned parcels and 2
adjacent parcels with the same MU zoning. The plat will consolidate the 7 parcels into
one lot before subdividing into 17 residential lots for individual sale. The overall
development will comprise 285 feet of frontage on Fort Union, which is roughly 80% of
the city block between 525 E and 585 E. There is 90 feet of frontage on 525 E.
The property is in a SF-1/DO zone with a 7200 S Overlay. Across Fort Union to the north
is a continuation of the SF-1/DO zone with single family homes. To the south is a SF-
1/DO zone with several duplexes. There are MU zones to the east and west. All parcels
along Fort Union from State St to 700 East are in the 7200 S Overlay zone. Adjacent
neighborhood development includes a variety of single-family homes, duplexes,
restaurants, offices, and retail and commercial. The subject site has 4 existing single-
family homes with detached accessory structures that will be demolished for this project.
An existing retail store occupies the northeastern corner of the block.
GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS:
The 2016 Midvale City General Plan locates the subject property in the 7200 S
Opportunity Area. In Opportunity Areas, the General Plan states that
“[M]inor-to-major changes in current land uses are likely to occur due to market forces,
increasing land values and opportunities to optimize land uses to take advantage of transit
and other public investments.
Projects in Opportunity Areas are anticipated to be at higher levels of density than current
land uses and should be carefully planned and designed to integrate into the fabric of the
area and minimize impacts on adjacent and nearby existing land uses. Future proposed
uses in the Opportunity Areas may or may not be consistent with current zoning, and the
desirability of the proposed use and the need for re-zoning should be evaluated on a case
by case basis.”
1 The General Plan identifies these land use goals for the 7200 S Opportunity Area:
1. Support new retail and office uses located west of 700 East

A rezone to MU is consistent with the General Plan because it furthers goal #4 by

ORDINANCE CONSIDERATIONS:

Section 17-3-1 of the Midvale City Municipal Code requires the Planning Commission

hold a public hearing and adopt a written recommendation to the City Council, advising

the Council to approve, disapprove, or modify a request on all zoning map amendments.

The Planning Commission may only recommend approval of a rezone application if it

determines, in written findings, that the proposed rezoning is consistent with the policies

and goals of the general plan and that the applicant has demonstrated the following:

1. The proposed rezoning is necessary either to comply with the general plan
proposed land use map or to provide land for a community need that was not
anticipated at the time of adoption of the general plan;

2. The existing zoning was either the result of a clerical error or a mistake of fact, or
that it failed to take into account the constraints on development created by the
natural characteristics of the land including, but not limited to, steep slopes,
floodplain, unstable soils, and inadequate drainage; or

a The land or its surrounding environs has changed or is changing to such a degree
that it is in the public interest to encourage redevelopment of the area or to
recognize the changed character of the area.

In addition to meeting goals of the General Plan, the proposed rezone also satisfies

requirements of Section 17-3-1 by meeting #3 above. The area around the subject site

has changed rapidly and extensively. The area has a diverse mix of business and

residential opportunities. The subject site is on a busy Primary Corridor and provides an

ideal residential density buffer to the single-family/duplex zone to the south. A stated

purpose of the 7200 S Overlay Zone is to facilitate transition of the corridor from single

family residential neighborhoods to a mix of commercial and residential uses. As |

justification for the rezone, the applicant states that a MU zone would allow the “highest

and best use of these properties” along a main thoroughfare. The proposed rezone to MU

PUBLIC COMMENTS

Any person wishing to comment on any item not otherwise scheduled for public hearing on the
Agenda may address the City Council at this point by stepping to the microphone and giving his
or her name for the record. Comments should be limited to not more than three (3) minutes,
unless additional time is authorized by the Governing Body. Citizen groups will be asked to
appoint a spokesperson. This is the time and place for any person who wishes to comment on
non-hearing, non-Agenda items. Items brought forward to the attention of the City Council will
be turned over to staff to provide a response outside of the City Council meeting.
