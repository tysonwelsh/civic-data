Sponsors: Sharkey

Council Member Sharkey introduced the presenter and discussion on the bulk waste

Tracy Cowdell presented the bulk waste ordinance for discussion and possible council
adoption.
Council discussion and questions on the presentation and ordinance took place following
the presentation. City staff participated in council discussion.
The Council took public comment on this item:
Steve Van Maren commented on the draft ordinance.
A motion was made by Cyndi Sharkey, seconded by Zach Robinson, to adopt
Ordinance 22-01, an ordinance of Sandy City amending City Code Chapter 19-4 to
include section 19-4-11: Sandy City Bulk Waste Clean-up Regulations amending
section E. a. iv. to change the diameter from 12 inches to 18 inches, amending
section E. a. v. to increase the maximum weight from 20 to 50 pounds, and
amending section E. a. vi. To remove the limitation on diameter... The motion
carried by the following vote:

City Council Meeting Minutes February 1, 2022
Council Member Business
Council Member Robinson updated the council giving thanks to various departments, on
the senior center advisory board and the senior center. He provided an update on the Alta
Canyon Advisory Board application process. He would like for Council Member Ear! to be
on the committee. Dustin Fratto noted that the application process for Alta Canyon
Advisory Committee will open soon.
Council Member Mecham updated the council on the District 1 Town Hall that was held
on 1-31-22. He thanked Administrative staff for their efforts. He mentioned that the Utah
RCV has published their survey and expressed an interest in further Council discussion
on the methodology used for the survey.
Council member Houseman updated the Council on the South Valley Chamber.
Mayor's Report
Deputy Mayor Everton gave the council an update on the current legislative session.
CAO Report
Kim Bell updated the council on the council/admin retreat and upcoming city events.
Legislative Report
Legislative update provided during the Mayor's report.
