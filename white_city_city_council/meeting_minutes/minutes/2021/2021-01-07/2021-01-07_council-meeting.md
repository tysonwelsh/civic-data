Reports from other Municipal Providers

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

              Captain Ken Aldridge stated approximately 170 employees have had the COVID-
19 vaccine. The 2021-2023 goal initiatives are being reviewed and are expected to be approved
during the January meeting. The divisions will work on its goals which will drive the budget
process. Recruit camp begins February 1, 2021.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Officer Jeff Fenton stated vehicle burglaries are the highest right now and there
is an upward trend of stolen vehicles. If there is a crime happening, please call the police, do not
post it on Facebook.

                Chief Randy Thomas stated the Midvale precinct is 100 percent COVID fee. A
new program called SafeCam is being started where residents can sign up to share or provide
access to their video surveillance systems if a crime occurs in their area.

Council Member Reports

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

               Council Member Perry stated a market wage adjustment will be given to
employees to bring them up to the top three salary range. CARES Act funds received by UFA are
expected to be $89,000. The rebuild of the Olympus Fire Station is moving forward. The 2021
final budget was adopted by UFSA. The annual wild land participation agreement was approved.

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                 Mayor Flint stated a resolution was passed to create a Major Incident Review
Board, it will include officers and citizens. The 2021 budget was approved. Dave Olsen is new to
the board replacing previous Council Member Pazell from Copperton. A one-time reimbursement
from PEHP of approximately $500 per person was given to UPD employees.

                                    −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

              Council Member Price stated the annual public hearing to approve the 2021
budget with an increase of $42,000 was held. The staff is doing winter maintenance and setting
up goals. Someone from the District usually does presentations at the schools; however, since
COVID, they can not go to the schools so, they have been providing educational information
online.

                                    −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

              Mayor Flint stated there are a lot of technical programs for land use that are being
used. The Board will be presented an end of year report. Grants are coming in and being used.
The Salt Lake County Council Member that will represent the unincorporated county has not been
announced.

                                    −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

               Nothing to report.

                                    −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Salt Lake County Animal Services

               Council Member Little stated there will be an animal food pantry donation event
on January 23, from 9:00 – 11:00. A drive-thru licensing and chipping will be held on January 14,
from 2:00 – 4:00. If the animal is properly licensed, it will chip the animal for free.
