BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.46
acres located on the north end of Salz Way, at approximately 12926 S Salz Way (Exhibit B).
The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be
approved to reduce the storm drain easement along the rear property line from 20-feet to
10-feet.

The Salz Cove Subdivision was recorded on September 12, 2014 and created five (5) single
family Lots. Salz Way is a public street that ends in a cul-de-sac.

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by
        signing an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use
        Authority finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no
        public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
