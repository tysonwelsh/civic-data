De Wood Ranch Plat 3; 7400 South SR-111; Preliminary Subdivision Plat (104 lots on 24.023
acres) and Sub-area Plan; PCH Zone; Third Cadence/Gary Langston (applicant) [#30555
(PZ-00010); parcel 20-27-100-005; 101-001; 176-001]
Gary Langston, applicant, said plat 3 of Wood Ranch contains the balance of the first takedown of
property purchased from the Wood family. The plat contains 104 units with the same product mix as
plats 1 and 2 that the Commission previously approved. They have selected five different home
building groups, and they are actively working on mass grading for the first 100+ acres. Infrastructure
installation is beginning for the first plat. They hope to start building homes in late winter and into
spring. They could have a grand opening early next summer or fall.
Ray McCandless showed the location of Plat 3 and how it fits in with the other two plats approved so
far. The two areas shown at the north are open space areas that will be dedicated to West Jordan. The
plat complies with the master development plan and agreement that were approved in 2021. Tonight’s
approval also includes the sub-area plan, which is the document that addresses maintenance of open
space, general examples of building types, and supports and defines what will be built. Staff
recommended that the Planning Commission approve the application with the requirements of
approval listed:

Ammon Allen opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

There was a brief explanation regarding the overall Wood Ranch master planned project, which

contains approximately 634 acres. The property has been used for agriculture and there is also a major

gravel extraction operation to the north that will be developed with later phases. The hillsides will be

left as Transfer of Development Open Space. With each plat there is a portion of TDOS as well as

active open space areas.

MOTION: Jay Thomas moved to approve the Wood Ranch Plat 3 Preliminary Subdivision

Plat (104 lots on 24.023 acres) and Preliminary Sub-Area plan located at 7400
South SR-111 with the requirements of approval listed in the report. The motion
was seconded by Matt Quinney and passed 6-0 in favor. Kent Shelton was absent.
