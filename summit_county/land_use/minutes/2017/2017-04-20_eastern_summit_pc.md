Planner Strader shared the background. The applicant is requesting to amend a 
Conditional Use Permit that was previously approved in 2008 to add an additional 
storage unit building. The current zone is AP, which requires storage units to have a 
Conditional Use Permit. In 2008, the ESCPC approved a Conditional Use Permit for the

the Conditional Use Permit based on the Findings of Fact and Conclusions of Law that 
are found on page 4 of the Staff Report (and in the minutes below).

Findings of Fact  
1. On October 15, 2008, the ESCPC approved a Conditional Use Permit for the construction 
of eight (8) buildings, containing two hundred fifty (250) commercial storage units on 
Parcel NS-216.  
2. On March 10, 2017, Clay Vernon, on behalf of Vernon Storage LLC submitted an 
amendment to the 2008 Conditional Use Permit to add a ninth building.  
3. Parcel NS-216 is zoned Agricultural Protection (AP).  
4. Parcel NS-216 is owned by Vernon Storage LLC.  
5. During development of the site, the location of an existing power line easement required 
some of the buildings to be moved further east, closer to the Weber River.  
6. Section 11-3-2-C-1 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code requires a one 
hundred (100) foot setback for structures from the high-water mark of a year round 
stream.  
7. Due to the stream setback, there is not enough space on the east side of Building E to 
access the units located on the east side of that building.  
Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
April 20, 2017 
Page 6 of 31 
 
 
8. The additional building will account for the units removed from the east side of Building 
E.  
9. The number of storage units will not exceed two hundred fifty (250).  
10. The proposed building complies with the stream setback.  
11. A fire hydrant is located on-site.  
12. The parcel does not contain steep slopes, wetlands, or visually sensitive areas.  
13. Service providers have reviewed the proposal and did not request any conditions of 
approval.  
14. The proposed building complies with the setback requirements in the AP zone district 
and is smaller than the approved storage buildings.  
Conclusions of Law  
1. Commercial storage is an appropriate use on Parcel NS-216.  
2. The amendment to the originally approved 2008 Conditional Use Permit complies with 
Chapter 2 of the Code.  
3. The proposed building will not violate any county, state, or federal laws.  
4. Approval of the landowner has been granted.  
5. The proposed building will not adversely affect the public health, safety, or welfare.  
6. The length and size of the proposed building is compatible with the approved storage 
buildings and complies with the zone required setbacks.

not enforced or managed in any way by Summit County, other than being recorded at the 
Recorder’s Office. What the County has is the Development Code. The Development Code

represents a nursery and a nursery is allowed in the AG100 Zone, in this location, with a 
Conditional Use Permit, meaning there may be concerns or items that need to be mitigated 
so it does not cause detrimental effects to the neighbors. There have been people saying 
there cannot be a business because of the CC&Rs but that is something that will need to be 
mitigated with the HOA and is not a concern to this body.

Planner Lewis stated that a couple of meetings ago the Planning Commission was 
presented with a draft of the County Resource Management Plan (CRMP). It is required by 
State Code that all twenty-nine counties in the State of Utah draft this plan as a chapter or 
appendage to the General Plan. Since the last presentation, several edits have taken place, 
with edits from the previous draft being shown in the Staff Report, Exhibit B. Whitney May 
also handed out a list of edits to the Commission of things that have been changed since the 
Staff Report went out. The CRMP is to address the management of twenty-eight different 
resources as it applies to public lands, which in State Code is defined as everything that is

to lands that are owned by the State, lands that are owned by the County, parks, trails or 
anything like that. This is specifically for Federal lands, Forest Service and BLM.
