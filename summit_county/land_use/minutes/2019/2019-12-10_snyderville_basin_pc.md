Planner Northrup-Robinson reminded the Commission of the background of this 
project.  She said when the 1998 Canyons Specially Planned Area and Development 
Agreement were approved, there was an obligation to provide employee housing when 
the resort reached a certain level of build-out.  When the project reached 25% of build-
out, it was required for the housing study to commence.  At 33% build-out, the 
construction of the workforce housing would be required.

Planner Northrup-Robinson said changes were made to the timeline and to the 
minimum number of employee housing units as part of the 2017 amendment.  The 
minimum number of employee housing became 1,107 pillows or “equivalent occupant 
credits.”  These amendments were approved.
