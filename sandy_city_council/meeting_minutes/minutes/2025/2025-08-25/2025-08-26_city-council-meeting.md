General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                      Council Chair D'Sousa invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                      Mr. Doug Wismer spoke in support of the gondola and thought it would be a good thing
                      for Sandy City. He hoped the Mayor and Council would consider supporting the gondala
                      project.

                      Ms. Blair Roxbury spoke on behalf of her family and neighbors. She shared a
                      neighborhood concern about a home in their area that has fallen into disrepair. She
                      provided details about the current condition of the home and yard and asked about the
                      city's code enforcement options

                      General Citizen comment closed.

                      Council moved to Standing Reports.
                      Council Member Alison Stroud was excused at approximately 5:55 pm.

Council Business

Special Recognition


1.         25-258     Installation of the 2025-26 Sandy Youth Council and Sandy Youth Court

                      Chair D'Sousa welcomed the Sandy Youth Council and the Sandy Youth Court and their
                      parents to the evening's Special Recognition and joint installation. Judge Paul Farr
                      conducted the Oath and Swearing In Ceremony for both the Sandy Youth Council and the
                      Sandy Youth Court. On behalf of the City Council and Administration, the Chair
                      congratulated the youth for their commitment to serve their communities through these
                      programs.

Informational Items


2.         25-267     City Council interviewing Mark M. Taylor to serve as a regular member of
                      the Sandy City Historic Preservation Committee
                      Alison Stroud introduced Mark Taylor, who she was nominating for a position on the
                      Sandy Historic Committee. Mr. Taylor spoke to the Council about his interest in
                      becoming a member on the Historic Committee and provided background information.
                      Council questions and comments followed. This was an information item and will come
                      back to the Council for consideration and action at a future Council meeting.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Marci Houseman provided an update from Wasatch Front Waste and
                    Recycling Board meeting. The current director is retiring and the new director has been
                    named. She also provided a clarification regarding a lawsuit with UDOT.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson expressed appreciation to the staff at Alta Canyon
                    Recreation Center for holding their final events at the old facility. He thanked Chief
                    Severson and McConaghie for golfing with the Council at the Chamber's event. He
                    enjoyed the day.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked the Police and Fired departments for
                    sponsoring the community autism event last week. It was an amazing event and
                    important for the entire community. My daughter looks forward to this event every year.
                    She attended the Trans Jordan event on Sunday and toured the new transfer facility. It is
                    a great asset to our city.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey attended the recent meeting with the Utah League of
                    Cities and Towns (ULCT) to prepare for the next legislative session. Accessory Dwelling
                    Units and Detached Accessory Dwelling Units will be big issues during the next session.
                    She also spoke about a presentation she attended by the Utah Division of Drinking
                    Water. There is a possibility of water rate increases.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Monica Zoltanski extended her condolences to the families of the fallen officers in
                    Tremonton. Many of our city's officers participated in the procession for the officers. The
                    community is invited to the Trans Jordan Transfer Station Open House. The Autism
                    community event went well.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, provided updates to the council on retiring employees.

                    Ryan Kump, Public Works Director, spoke to the Council about reallocating funds to
                    purchase a new paver truck.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:33 pm.
