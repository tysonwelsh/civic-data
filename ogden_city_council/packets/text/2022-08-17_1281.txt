                                                     AGENDA
                                                  August 17, 2022

                                                   Traveling Tour
                                                     5:30 p.m.



TO ALL MEMBERS OF THE
OGDEN CITY PLANNING COMMISSION

   The Ogden City Planning Commission will hold a traveling tour on Wednesday, August 17,
2022. The tour will depart from the parking lot at the Ogden Municipal Building, 2549
Washington Boulevard, at 5:30 pm.

Tour Items

    1. Residential Developments. The commission will tour residential developments in
       Ogden and surrounding areas to observe residential development standards.

    2. Motel conversions to multiple family. The commission will visit motels
       considered for conversion to multiple family development.



In compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, persons needing auxiliary communicative aids and services for this
meeting should contact the Management Services Department at 629-8701 (TTY/TDD: 711 or 888-735-5906) or by email:
accessibility@ogdencity.com at least 48 hours in advance of the meeting. Any member of the public wishing to participate
in the tour should contact the Ogden Planning Division at 801-629-8922 at least 48 hours in advance of the tour.
