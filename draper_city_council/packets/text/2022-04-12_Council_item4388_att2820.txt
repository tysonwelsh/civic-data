RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 22-17 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Memorial Benches.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs.

The proposed fee for the Memorial Benches are new. The application fee for $2,300 is to cover costs
associated with the ordering/purchasing the bench including shipping, engraving the plaque ($1,885), site prep
for the bench which includes a 4’x8’ concrete pad ($100), hardware to install bench ($20), staff time
approximately 6 hours ($240), and miscellaneous expenses ($55).
