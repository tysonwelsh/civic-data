1. Welcome and Opening Comment
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, except
   for Council Condie who will join the meeting in progress. Councilor Hancock offered the
   opening comment.

2. Consideration of Resolution #2021-51, appointing Emily Britton as an Alternate
   Commissioner to the Lehi City Planning Commission

   Motion: Councilor Koivisto moved to approve Resolution #2021-51, appointing Emily Britton
   as an Alternate Commissioner to the Lehi City Planning Commission.

   Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes; Councilor Koivisto, Yes;
   and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

3. Discussion Items
    3.a. PARC tax discussion
    Cameron Boyle, Assistant City Administrator, gave the Council on update on the PARC tax.
    He said that the City has received the “for” and “against” arguments for the Voter Information
    Pamphlet. He also provided an update to the Engage Lehi website where people can go for
    information. There was a survey on the site that was used to help the city determine what
    residents feel is most important for the community. The average rank in order of preference
    was parks, recreation, arts and then culture. He said that the expected tax revenue from the
    bond would be about 1.2 million each year. There are strict guidelines on how the funds can
    be used. Based on the survey responses he suggested allocating 70% for parks and recreation,

    2b. Underground Containers Policy

    2c. Discussion of TSSD Letter of Support for an ARPA Funding Application
   This item was heard first on the agenda. Richard Mickelsen, Timpanogos Special Service
   District Manager, asked for the Council’s support and the District applies for the ARPA grant.
   He said that the grant money could be used to expand the facility, engineer wetlands for
   mitigation, and improve their processing system. The Council was in support of the District
   submitting an application.
