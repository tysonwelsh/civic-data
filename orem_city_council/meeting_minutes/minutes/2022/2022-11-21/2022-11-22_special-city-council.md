CITY OF OREM
SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL MEETING
56 North State Street Orem, Utah
November 22, 2022

4:00 P.M. SPECIAL SESSION - COUNCIL CHAMBERS

CONDUCTING                          Mayor David A. Young

ELECTED OFFICIALS            David Young, LaNae Millett, Jeff Lambson, Debby Lauret, and
Tom Macdonald.

APPOINTED STAFF JoD’Ann Bates, City Recorder and Tess Jones, Deputy City Recorder


CALL TO ORDER

Mayor Young called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m. and welcomed those in attendance. He
then turned the time over to the City Recorder for the Canvass of the 2022 Utah County General
Elections in relation to Proposition #2.


SCHEDULED ITEMS

RESOLUTION - Acceptance and Certification of the 2022 Utah County General Election;
Proposition #2

Mrs. Bates stated as the City Council they would be acting as the Board of Canvassers and will
be asked to approve and certify the 2022 Utah County General Election in relation to
Proposition #2. She continued by reviewing the final election statistics which included the
following:
•       Number of Eligible Registered Voters: 46,518
•       Number of Ballots Counted: 30,514
•       Voter Turnout: 65.60%
•       Votes For: 8,639
•       Votes Against: 21,423
Mrs. Bates concluded by indicating this is the highest voter turnout they have had in Orem and
turned the item back to the City Council for discussion and approval.


Mr. Macdonald moved the City Council sitting as a Board of Canvassers for the purpose of
canvassing the returns of the Utah County General Election results pertaining to City of Orem
Proposition #2 held in the City of Orem on November 8, 2022 and approve by Resolution the
results of the 2022 Election Canvass pertaining to Proposition #2; Orem School District with the
following results:
Proposition #2 – Orem School District
For             8,595
Against         21,337
and declare Proposition #2; Orem School District failed.
Mr. Lambson seconded the motion. Those voting aye: David A. Young, Tom Macdonald, Debby
Lauret, Jeff Lambson and LaNae Millett. The motion passed.


ADJOURN
Mrs. Lauret moved to adjourn the Special City Council meeting; Mrs. Millett seconded the
motion. Those voting aye: David A. Young, Tom Macdonald, Debby Lauret, Jeff Lambson and
LaNae Millett. The motion passed.

Meeting adjourned at 4:05 p.m.
