Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Dinner

2) Call to Order
Commissioner Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner Newall.

3) Stack/Gardner presentation of updates to Thanksgiving Point Area Plan Amendment
Ryan and Dave with Stack and Gardner presented updated information on the Thanksgiving
Point Area Plan.

4) Moderate income housing goals review
Brittney Harris reviewed feedback from the State regarding information they would like the
Planning Department to update.

5) Discussion of work session topics for 2023


Jacob Curtis and the Commissioners discussed issues, topics and standards to hold and discuss
for WS meetings.

Commissioner Jackson would like to see training on infrastructure proceedings, and a process
that can be explained to citizens that can be understood better.

Commissioner Everett would like to coordinate with neighboring cities with roads and traffic. He
suggested having Alan Matheson come and talk to us, or having Saratoga Springs, Eagle
Mountain, and Draper join us for a joint meeting.

Commissioner Ellis would like to go beyond what the state requires for affordable housing
efforts.

Commissioner Britton would like to do site visits with big developments with Staff.

Commissioner Eyre would like updates on Council decisions after they vote on Planning
Commission items.

Commissioner Carlson would like to see parking and the UTA addressed.

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Bunker moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
