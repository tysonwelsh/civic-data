ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-3-2022  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting an ordinance amendment to change the Zoning
           Ordinance (Title 7) and the Condominium Ordinance (Title 9). The purpose of these changes
           is to prohibit private streets, allow parking spaces for townhomes and condominiums attached
           to the side of each other to back onto a street, and create two new street classifications for use
           in planned unit developments. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)
