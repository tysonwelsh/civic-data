Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Russ Fox, Assistant City Manager
CC:  Date: February 18, 2020
Re:  Comments: On October 15, 2019, staff presented to the City Council an option to provide improved cell service for AT&T customers in SunCrest where new antennas would be installed on the legs of the existing water tank cell tower.  This item is the lease agreement with New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC (AT&T).  AT&T will lease a 400 square foot area, northeast of of water tank #3, for an equipment shelter and infrastructure.  Based upon the lease area and comparing the lease rate with Verizon Wireless, the city will receive $800.00/month with a 3% yearly increase. The findings are as follows: Entering into the Lease Agreement with New Cingular Wireless PCS, LLC complies with applicable law and is in the best interest of the health, safety and welfare of Draper residents by providing improved cell phone call and data service.
