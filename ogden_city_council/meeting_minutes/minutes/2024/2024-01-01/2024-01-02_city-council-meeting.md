of Allegiance.

         A moment of silence was observed.

Comments from outgoing Council members Louis Lopez and Benjamin K.
Nadolski
          Council member Lopez expressed his gratitude to City employees for their hard work, which contributes to Ogden being
‘still untamed’ and coolest City in Utah. He thanked his fellow Council members and Mayor Caldwell for their service to the
Ogden community. He also thanked the citizens of Ogden, his employers, and the electorate for the trust they have placed in him;
during his tenure, all decisions were made with them in mind and serving the most vulnerable in the community. He closed by
offering his remarks in Spanish for the benefit of the Spanish speaking members of the community.
          Council member Nadolski thanked Council member Lopez for his service and for making him a better man. He thanked
his family, other members of the Council for their support and friendship, and the community for the trust they have placed in
him.

Administration of the Oath of Office for newly elected Council members
Dave Graf, Richard A. Hyer, and Shaun Myers; and newly elected Mayor
Benjamin K. Nadolski
        City Recorder Hansen administered the Oath of Office to newly elected/re-elected Council members Dave Graf, Richard
A. Hyer, and Shaun Myers and to newly elected Mayor Benjamin K. Nadolski.
Comments from newly elected Council members Dave Graf, Richard A.
Hyer, and Shaun Myers, and Mayor Benjamin K. Nadolski
         Council member Graf stated he is honored to represent District Four and the City of Ogden; he is grateful for the trust his
supporters have placed in him and he looks forward to this opportunity to continue to embrace Ogden and help make this a City
that everyone enjoys living in. He believes Ogden is ‘standing in the doorway’ of the next great phase of its storied history and he
encouraged all residents to be engaged in the matters that are shaping Ogden. He concluded he is truly honored to have this
responsibility and ability to serve Ogden City in this way.
         Council member Hyer stated today is such a special day and he always becomes emotional when taking the Oath of
Office; he takes his oath very seriously and he is excited to work alongside the new Council members and new Mayor of Ogden
City. He thanked his wife for her support and taking care of so many other things in their life that has allowed him to serve in this
capacity. His children have also been very supportive of him in his endeavors as a Planning Commissioner and City Council
member. He expressed his appreciation for Council staff for the great support they provide to the City Council; they care very
deeply about the Ogden community and aiding the Council in its decision-making process. He looks forward to continuing to
serve the community and discussing important issues with the citizens.
         Council member Myers stated it is a great honor for him to be among the Council members, Mayor, City staff, and so
many other representatives and residents of Ogden City. Since he was a young boy, he has enjoyed City Council meetings and he
has always followed what is happening in this City. It is his privilege to now stand on the shoulders of those who have come
before him and be part of a team representing Ogden City. He shared a quick story to relay the moral that in the least expected
places, people can stand up and be counted; he looks forward to doing that on behalf of Ogden City. He wishes to achieve great
things for Ogden with a bit more vision and hope. He also addressed Council member Lopez in Spanish to thank him for his
service. He also thanked his wife, Christina, for her support and concluded by wishing all a Happy New Year.
         Mayor Nadolski stated he is thankful for this opportunity to serve the Ogden community; he also thanked his family for
their support that has enabled him to be a public servant. He encouraged everyone to look around the room and take note of the
faces of the other people in the room; they have come from every corner of this community and beyond. He specifically
highlighted the young faces in the crowd, their energy and smiles, and the potential they bring to the future of the community. The
children of this community are why he ran for City Council eight years ago and why he ran for Mayor this year. He asked the
Council to remember these young faces as they deliberate in these chambers and as they serve and decide on behalf of the people
who placed them in their seat. He had the opportunity to coach many youth in the community and he fell in love with helping
them. He then stated that change can sometimes be uncomfortable, and he acknowledged the 650 employees that are dedicated to
serving Ogden City and invited them to embrace this change and grow together. This is an opportunity to embrace the things that
work, build on the things that do not, and to be better as a City. He then used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide his
blueprint for his first hundred days in office; he will maintain high ethical standards for himself and staff in all City operations and
be led by the following shared values: integrity, trust, transparency, growth mindset, service, collaboration, and humility. He will
focus on innovation and data driven decision-making, health and wellness, the physical environment, economic environment,
social environment, cultural environment, and political environment. He again stressed his appreciation for his family and those in
the community who have supported him; today is a day to celebrate and he looks forward to the future.

Comments from other Council members
          Council member White stated it was an amazing opportunity to serve with Council member Lopez; she thanked him for
caring about all populations of Ogden City and representing the diversity of the community. She also congratulated Council
member Hyer on his re-election; his dedication and unwavering commitment to the community is commendable and his re-
election is a testament to the trust and confidence the constituents have placed in him and his ability to advocate for the betterment
of the City. She stated she looks forward to getting to know Council member Myers better and congratulated him on his successful
campaign for election; his commitment to making a positive impact on the lives of Ogden residents will be great. She stated she is
also excited about working with Council member Graf; his vision for fostering economic development and supporting local
businesses, and also addressing community needs are inspiring to her. She concluded by addressing Mayor Nadolski; she is
hopeful he will build upon the foundation that was built for him and also incorporate his vision into his public service. She is
excited to see how he will transition from the office of Council member to Mayor and to work with him to improve the
community. She read a quote from Henry Davis Thoreau “what lies behind us and what lies before use are tiny matters compared
to what lies within is”. She encouraged all elected officials to follow their internal compass and stated she looks forward to
working with them.
          Council member Blair stated one thing that he loves about Ogden is that it has always embraced diversity; Council
member Lopez’s election to the City Council was evidence of that and he has loved serving with him. He stated Council member
Lopez made the City better and stronger. He echoed Council member White’s comments about Council member Hyer’s re-
election and service to the City; he has been a great friend and mentor and he is pleased to be able to continue to serve with him.
He stated he is also very excited to serve with Council members Graf and Myers; he is excited for their new perspectives and
visions, and he looks forward to learning from them and growing together. He addressed Mayor Nadolski and stated that he is
very deserving of his new title; he noted he has always been drawn to leaders and it has been an honor to serve with Mayor
Nadolski and to watch him lead. He is excited for the Council to be able to work with the Mayor to continue to improve it. He
believes Ogden City is special and he knows Mayor Nadolski does too; he expressed his full support for him and thanked him for
his friendship and leadership.
          Vice Chair Richey also thanked Council member Lopez; he has served as a mentor to him over the past two years and
they have formed a great friendship. He also wished Council member Graf and Myers well as new Council members; he is
looking forward to serving with them. He also thanked Council member Hyer for his mentorship and congratulated him on his re-
election. He concluded by acknowledging Mayor Nadolski and stated he hopes he will continue to be who he is and represent the
City in that way. He stated he is excited to continue to work with him and support him in any way he can as a colleague and
friend; there are tough decisions to be made in the future, but he believes the right decisions will be made as the Council and
Mayor work together collectively.

Closing remarks from Council Chair Angela Choberka
         Chair Choberka congratulated everyone on their new roles; she is excited to serve with new and old Council members
and to work with Mayor Nadolski. She stated it was impossible to know what service as a Council member would be like, but she
considers it to be one of the great pleasures of her life. It has given her the opportunity to interact with people she never would
have otherwise and to build wonderful working relationships and friendships. Her eyes have been opened to many new
perspectives, all of which have informed her decision-making process. She is very optimistic about the future of the community
and thanked everyone who has attended today’s meeting for their support of all elected leaders of Ogden City.


     There being no further business to come before the Council, COUNCIL MEMBER MEYERS MOVED THE
MEETING ADJOURN AT 12:52 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER GRAF, ALL VOTING
AYE.
