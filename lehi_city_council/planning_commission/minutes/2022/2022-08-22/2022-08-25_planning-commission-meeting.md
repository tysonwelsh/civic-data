3.4) Public hearing and consideration of Clark Heringer’s request for conditional use
approval of the Utah Pool Builder site plan for pool sales and outdoor storage of materials
located at approximately 4300 North Digital Drive in a Light Industrial zone.

Planner Jacob Curtis presented on item 3.4.

Commissioner Bunker asked about landscaping plans since there was water isn’t available on
site.

Applicant Clark Heringer's explained the reason for the request for storing pools. They are
working to clean up the area. Not opposed to putting up rocks or items to help make the property
more appealing.

Commissioners asked clarifying questions on fencing, pool placement, and weed control.

Mr. Heringer's stated that he didn't need fencing and felt that it would make it harder with the
crane and a semi. He didn't feel that they would have more than 30 pools, but he wanted to make
sure that they had some flexibility with shipping and dig dates.

Commissioner Ellis opened the public hearing.
Commissioner Ellis closed the public hearing.

Commissioners expressed their concerns with the appeal, organization, weeds in the area, and
compliance. There wasn't a concern on the impact of pools, but that the property wasn't cleaned
up.

Craig Chambers guided that they could do reasonable conditions and what they could do for
enforcement and that there are options for a time limit.

Mr. Heringer's explained the Styrofoam was for the pools and they were used and had a function,
but that it could potentially be stored somewhere else.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve of Clark Heringer’s request for
        conditional use approval of the Utah Pool Builder site plan for pool sales and
        outdoor storage of materials located at approximately 4300 North Digital Drive in a
        Light Industrial zone finding that the property needs to be cleaned up and organized,
        landscaping needs to be included, the weeds need to be kept under control including a
        fenced in area to store stuff that can be blown around or just to keep the place organized;
        add the condition that they need to come back in one year; add that there be a limit of no
        more than 30 pools; the enclosure could be a chain link fence or some enclosure adequate
        for windblown debris to be stored; include DRC comments also finding that the proposed
        use, at the proposed location will not be detrimental or injurious to property or
        improvements in the vicinity; it will be located and conducted in a manner in compliance
        with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the purposes of this Code;
        the property on which the use, building, or other structure is proposed to be established is
        of adequate size and dimensions to permit construction of the facilities and the conduct of
        the use in such a manner that it will not be detrimental to adjoining properties and the
        area. Commissioner Newell seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Carlson; yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.5) Public hearing and consideration of Lehi City’s request for conditional use approval of
the Strata Network Shelter located at 945 North 200 East
Planner Jacob Curtis presented on item 3.5.

Commissioner Ellis asked a few clarify questions to staff.

Shaye Ruitenbeek explained that they will have four of these shelters. Ms. Ruitenbeek described
what the use of these shelters were for and expounded on landscaping- which is being done by
the Parks Department.

The Commissioners asked a few clarifying questions to Ms. Ruitenbeek.

Commissioner Ellis opened the public hearing.

Jerroleese Smith is worried about the fence and the size of the building and just had general
questions about the building.

Nick Bender- was concerned about if the generator running and if there was equipment in the
building. Concerned about the noise and transmission equipment.

Wayne Olds- was concerned that the park has no parking to attend the park but will this create
traffic and why put this item in a park with children.

Sonreesa Hasselback- wanted to know if the city was aware of the park condition as it is now.
Feels that it is currently undermanaged. She has a petition of 175 families that have signed to
approve that park. So, to see something industrial come in is concerning when they are trying to
improve that area.

James Jacob- what is it going to look like, and do we have a say on how it will be placed and
make sure the Ms. Smith was able to get her voice herd.

Commissioner Ellis closed the public hearing.


Ms. Ruitenbeek clarified noise, traffic, and other resident concerns. She stated that because they
are in parks, they wanted to make sure they look more residential then industrial so they can
blend in more.

Shad Sagers with STRATA answered questions about the equipment and that the buildings were
insulated, and residents shouldn't be able to hear the items. The generator is only there for power
outages and emergencies.

Kim Struthers discussed that this will be a public utility so to put it context this is a new form of
public infrastructure. If we are going to have fiber, then we need the infrastructure.

Ms. Ruitenbeek discussed the timeline on when building and landscaping.

Commissioners discussed what they liked and concerns.

Commissioner Ellis reopened the public hearing.

James Jacob – wanted to go on record stating that he does not want this on the property.

Jerroleese Smith- asked if that if it would be possible to have a 6ft fence next to the property.

Nick Bender- felt that there wasn't enough information. Felt that they are arguing without all the
facts.

Commissioner Ellis closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Ellis stated that the city is like the any other property owner that is allowed to
develop their property. Commissioner Newell reiterated this and that it really is about making
sure they are focusing on the code and does it meet the code.

    Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to approve Lehi City’s request for conditional use
    approval of the Strata Network Shelter located at 945 North 200 East with the following
    findings: the proposed use will help provide an essential service to Lehi City residents; the
    proposes use will not cause significant negative impact to the surrounding neighborhood
    providing reasonable conditions are complied with; the proposed use, at the proposed
    location, will not be detrimental or injurious to property or improvements in the vicinity; the
    proposed use will be located and conducted in a manner in compliance with the goals and
    policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the purposes of this Code; the property on which
    the use, building, or other structure is proposed to be established is of adequate size and
    dimensions to permit construction of the facilities and the conduct of the use in such a
    manner that it will not be detrimental to adjoining properties and the area; and we require
    compliance with all the DRC comments and that the facia on the building will be hardy-
    board siding; that a quality fence, 6’ high be provided for property screening of the building;
    and that a water-wise landscaping plan be developed and installed within 12 months; and that
    they comply with the City Noise Ordinance. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Carlson; yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
