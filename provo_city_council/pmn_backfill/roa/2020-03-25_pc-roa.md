    project will still need to apply for final project plan and subdivision applications and that owner-occupancy could
    not be enforced by zoning code. They also verified with the applicant that trees and shrubs will try to be
    protected/provided.

 *ITEM 6     Daniel LaFontaine requests a Zone Change from R1.10 to Low Density Residential for 1.07 acres for a

             aardmore@provo.org PLRZ20190265

                                                 CONTINUED

LEGAL DESCRIPTION FOR PROPERTY TO BE REZONED
The property to be rezoned to the LDR Zone is described in the attached Exhibit A.

RELATED ACTIONS
The Planning Commission also continued the related concept plan at this hearing, file #PLCP20190266.

STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations. Key points addressed in the Staff's presentation to the Planning Commission included the
following: Staff gave an overview of the zone request and concept plan, illustrating specific design and code issues that
staff had concerns with.

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING DATE
• A neighborhood meeting was held on 02/25/2020.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
• Neighbors or other interested parties were present or addressed the Planning Commission.
• Staff received 48 emails for this item, with 46 of the emails stating opposition to the proposal for the following
   reasons:
    •   the plan is not in line with Provo City General Plan
    •   the concept is different than what exists in the neighborhood
    •   there are already townhomes nearby
    •   the plan does not provide enough open space
    •   the applicant will not listen or compromise with the neighbors
    •   traffic and parking issues will spill into the neighborhood
    •   too much on a small site
    •   dumpster placement on/off the site


CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following: Staff forwarded the 48 emails referenced above to the Planning Commission
prior to the hearing, which were read and referred to in the hearing.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
   • The applicant stated he was not aware of all the concerns before the meeting, and feels like most of them can be
       addressed in the final project plan process.
   • Mr. LaFontaine stated that the neighborhood meeting didn’t work and that the neighbors don’t want to work out
       issues with the plans.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Lisa Jensen stated that the project should be a lower density and serve as a buffer in to the neighborhood.
   • Dave Anderson and Deborah Jensen had questions answered regarding the size of the lot (1.07 acres) and whether
       it falls into the North University Design Corridor (it does not).
   • Maria confirmed with the applicant that it was his choice not to have access onto 70 East.
   • Dave Anderson offered his views on difficulty the applicant has had, but is not impressed with updated elevations.
   • Deborah Jensen observed that both streets are dead zones with the designs shown, and that the applicant should
       work with staff to address elevations at the street.
   • Lisa Jensen stated density was too high, open space could be improved, and that the neighbor concerns are
       validated with what was shown.
   • Laurie Urquiaga feels that there is not much safety with south units facing a wall, the zone may work, but that
       design needs to fit the neighborhood better.
   • Planning Commission had issues with design and was not ready to hear from public until the applicant revised
       his plans and came back.
FINDINGS / BASIS OF PLANNING COMMISSION DETERMINATION
The Planning Commission identified the following findings as the basis of this decision or recommendation: The Planning
Commission feels that the LDR may work for the site, but the applicant needs to work with staff on design to meet code
and satisfy concerns.

6.10   Electronic Meetings

       This bylaw is adopted to satisfy UCA 52-4-207: Electronic Communications. Planning
       Commission Members may participate in Planning Commission meetings by electronic
       communication if the required technology is available and the meeting is legally noticed in
       accordance with the Utah State Law. The Planning Commission must provide space and facilities
       at a location where the public may attend, monitor and participate in the open portions of the
       meetings as specified in UCA 52-4-207.
