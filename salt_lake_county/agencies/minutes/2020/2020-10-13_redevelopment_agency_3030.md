4.2 Information on the Nomination of Historic Magna Main 
Street to the National Register of Historic Districts
20-1015
Ms. Dina Blaes , Director, Regional Transportation, Housing and Economic 
Development Department, stated following the March 18, 2020, earthquake, 
staff from the Utah Division of State History did an assessment of the 
damage sustained by Magna buildings.  There is a great collection of historic 
structures on Magna Main Street.  They found the funding necessary to 
nominate the Main Street area to the National Register of Historic Places .  
Such a designation would mean tremendous resources available for building 
owners and the business community.
Mr. Cory Jensen , Senior Preservation Program Specialist, Utah Division of 
State History, stated the nomination will go before the State History board on 
October 29, 2020.  If the area meets the qualifications, the nomination will be 
sent to the National Register Office.  Results would probably be known in 
early January, 2021.
