3.     Wasatch Electric; 9000 South 6800 West; Preliminary Site Plan and Preliminary
       Subdivision Plat; SWQ-MU (proposed) Zone; ARCO Design Build and Gardner Group
       (applicant) [#33811, 33809; parcel 26-03-326-001; 26-03-300-008]

Cole Peterson, Gardner Company, said the proposed 160,000 square-foot multi-level building and
subdivision will be located along the newly constructed 9000 South. There will be approximately
40,000 square feet of office and the rest will be for the truck shop and industrial space. The entire staff
of Wasatch Electric will be moving to this location. He expressed appreciation for the city staff who
have helped to get it to this point and said that the tenant is excited to get started on the project.

Tayler Jensen indicated that there are between 400 and 500 employees, which is one of the reasons that
this has been an economic development priority. The elevations and layout are unchanged from what
was shown at the recent rezoning hearing and what was presented to the Design Review Committee.

Based on the analysis contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning Commission
approve the Preliminary Major Subdivision and Preliminary Site Plan for Wasatch Electric located on
approximately 54.35 Acres at 9000 South 6800 West with the conditions and requirements of approval
listed in this staff report.

Requirements of Approval:
1.     Address and correct all redline comments generated by the City during Final Subdivision and
       Site Plan reviews.

4.     Oquirrh Highlands; approximately 5900 West to 6400 West and 6600 South to 7000
       South, Annexation of approximately 160.501 acres assigning a Future Land Use Map
       designation from Salt Lake County designation of Residential to West Jordan land use
       designation of Medium Density Residential, High Density Residential, and Parks and
       Open Land and from a Salt Lake County Zoning Designation of RM/ZC (Multiple
       Family/Zoning Conditions) and R-1-6/ZC (Residential Single Family Dwelling/Zoning
       Conditions) to West Jordan zoning map designation of P-C (Planned Community) Zone;
       City of West Jordan (applicant) [#34346)

Larry Gardner said the City Council accepted the annexation petition for the Oquirrh Highlands area.
When some of the property for Wood Ranch was annexed, the city agreed that if a petition came in for
this area that we would annex it. This area is entirely built-out with single-family, multi-family, and
attached residential products, parks, and a church. This annexation will create a clean border with West
Valley City. He reviewed the current density with the corresponding West Jordan land use
designations. The medium density residential and high density residential ranges meet those in the
general plan. The parks and open space should be designated as such to help guarantee that they
remain as parks. Oquirrh Highlands was built as a planned residential development in Salt Lake
County and the adjacent properties were as well, so the recommended zoning classification is P-C
(Planned Community). He felt that the P-C zone would lessen the number of non-conformities that the
annexation might create.

Based on the information and Criteria set forth in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to assign a land use designation
of Medium Density Residential, High Density Residential, and Parks and Open Land and zoning
designation of P-C (Planned Community) for the Oquirrh Highlands annexation area.

Jay Thomas opened the public hearing.

Brandi Frandsen, one of the original petitioners and part of the petitioning group, stated that she has
lived in the Oquirrh Highlands area for 21 years and she has been working on this annexation for 20
years. When they got the approval from West Jordan to begin collecting signatures, they were able to
