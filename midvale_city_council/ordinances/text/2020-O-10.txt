ORDINANCE NO. 2020-0-10
AN ORDINANCE VACATING THE UNIMPROVED RIGHTS-OF-WAY
KNOWN AS BINARY WAY AND IVY DRIVE LOCATED WEST OF BINGHAM
JUNCTION BOULEVARD, MIDVALE, UTAH.

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated Section 10-8-8 and Chapter 12.16
of the Midvale City Municipal Code, the City has authority to vacate and dispose of city-
owned rights-of-way; and

WHEREAS, a request has been made to vacate the Binary Way and Ivy Drive
rights-of-way described in Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, each City Department has reviewed the request to determine impact
to the City and made a recommendation to approve the request to vacate; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing
on June 10, 2020, which meeting was preceded by notice of publication in the Salt Lake
Tribune and Deseret News on May 27, 2020, to review the request to vacate, and, after
considering all the information received, made a recommendation to approve the vacation
request to the City Council: and

WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing on July
7, 2020, which meeting was preceded by notice of publication in the Salt Lake Tribune and
Deseret News on June 23, 2020; and

WHEREAS, after taking into consideration citizen testimony and_ the
recommendations from City Staff and the Planning Commission, the City Council finds
that the vacation of Binary Way and Ivy Drive will allow for better use of the adjacent
properties and the public interest will not be materially injured by the proposed vacations.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
MIDVALE CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. The foregoing recitals are true and correct;

Section 2. The portions of Binary Way and Ivy Drive shown on the attached
Exhibit A are hereby vacated;

Section 3. Ownership of the rights-of-way being vacated shall revert to the
adjacent property owner(s);

Section 4. The Midvale City Council does hereby authorize the Mayor to sign
any documents needed to complete this vacation; and
