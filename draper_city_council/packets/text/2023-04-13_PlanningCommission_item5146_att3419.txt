BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan and Plat Amendment for
approximately 2.44 acres located at approximately 650 East 12225 South (Exhibit B & C).
The property is currently zoned RM2 . The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan and Plat
Amendment be approved to allow for the development 21 townhomes.

The property was originally part of the Midas Creek development which included three
office buildings and four residential buildings. Of the overall development, one office
building and the four residential buildings were approved and built between 2008 and
2014. After the remaining area planned for office use sat vacant for a decade, a previous
property owner applied to place residential uses on the remaining land. Victoria Woods
Phase 2 was approved in 2019 for 17 townhomes, the maximum density allowed within the
then zone OR (Office Residential). The subdivision plat for Victoria Woods Phase 2 was
recorded, but the project never built.

Map Amendment to place the property in the RM2 zone, along with a Land Use Map
Amendment and a Development Agreement. The Development Agreement set the
maximum number of dwelling units allowed on the property at 21 and approved a concept
plan.

        E. Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a

               D103.6.1 roads 20 to 26 feet in width. Fire lane signs as specified in Section
               D103.6 shall be posted on both sides of fire apparatus access roads that are
               20 to 26 feet wide (6096to 7925 mm).

                       This section requires that parking be prohibited on both sides of
                        narrower fire apparatus access roads. Twenty feet (6096 mm) is the
                        appropriate width needed for two average-size fire trucks to pass one
                        another. If that width is reduced by parking even on one side, it will be
                        potentially difficult for a fire department to undertake emergency
                        operations in that area.

               D103.6.2 roads more than 26 feet in width. Fire lane signs as specified in
               Section D103.6 shall be posted on one side of fire apparatus access roads
               more than 26 feet wide (7925 mm) and less than 32 feet wide (9754 mm).

                       Because this width is more than sufficient for maneuvering at least
                        two fire department vehicles by one another, parking would be
                        allowed on one side.
