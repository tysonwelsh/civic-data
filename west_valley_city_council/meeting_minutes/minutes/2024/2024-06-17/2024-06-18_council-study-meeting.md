C.   RESOLUTION 24-108: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A
     REAL ESTATE PURCHASE CONTRACT AND ACCEPT A QUITCLAIM
     DEED AND A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT WITH AND
     FROM SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2880
     WEST 3650 SOUTH
     Mayor Pro Tem Christensen discussed proposed Resolution 24-108 that would
     authorize the City to Enter Into a Real Estate Purchase Contract and Accept a Quitclaim
     Deed and a Temporary Construction Easement With and From Salt Lake County for
     Property Located at 2880 West 3650 South.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            The Salt Lake County (library) parcel located at 2880 West 3650 South is
            one of several parcels affected by the Lancer Way Reconstruction Project;
            2700 West to 3200 West. This project will widen Lancer Way and provide
            curb, gutter and sidewalk from 2700 West to 3200 West where it does not
            currently exist. The project will also install textured, colored concrete park
            strip, street trees, streetlights and new ramps at street intersections on
            Lancer Way. Compensation for the purchase of the Quit Claim Deed,
            Temporary Construction Easement and improvements is $30,300.00 and
            was based upon an appraisal prepared by Integra Realty Resources.

            The project is being funded through the Surface Transportation Program
            (STP). The city is responsible for 6.77% of all project costs, including right
            of way, making the West Valley City share of the acquisition $2,051.31.

NEW BUSINESS
A.   POTENTIAL FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS
     Councilmember Whetstone stated that he would like to discuss Stanton Drive to
     see if the Council would like to direct staff to install speed bumps on this road. He
     noted that the speed study fell short but many residents are in favor of installing

      speed bumps regardless. Councilmember Harmon stated that he also hears a lot of
      concern and would like to discuss adjusting the point system again. He noted that
      he would be in favor of doing more rather than less.

      Councilmember Fitisemanu stated that he receives complaints regarding noise on
      the Mountain View Corridor. He indicated that he would like communication or
      information on what restrictions are in place as well as stats regarding
      speeding/crashes/casualties. Steve Pastorik indicated that UDOT maintains the
      traffic statistics, but staff should be able to provide those as well as information on
      what noise restrictions are in place.

      Councilmember Harmon stated that he provided a video of a crash that occurred on
      3500 South due to street racing. He asked if there are increased penalties that can
      be implemented for this type of accident. Councilmember Harmon expressed
      concern regarding on-street parking and boarding homes. He noted that his only
      idea so far has been to ban overnight parking. He requested a communication to
      discuss this issue.

B.    COUNCIL REPORTS
      COUNCILMEMBER WHETSTONE- CHILL WITH THE CHIEF,
      CROSSING GUARD BANQUET
      Councilmember Whetstone stated that the latest Chill with the Chief event was
      great and well attended. He added that he also attended the Crossing Guard Banquet
      which was a great event as well.

      COUNCILMEMBER HARMON- WESTFEST
      Councilmember Harmon noted that he attended the WestFest parade and there were
      difficulties with his antique car but noted that it was a great event. Councilmember
      Huynh requested clarification at future parades on whether candy can/can’t be
      thrown from vehicles. Jamie Young, Parks and Recreation Director, stated that it is
      a safety concern so staff asks that candy not be thrown from vehicles and just
      handed out by walkers along side them. She added that this is difficult to enforce.
