PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
Meeting of March 23, 2023

Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session
Thursday, March 23, 2023, Commissioner Heare called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Ken, Heare, Jordy Guth, Jessica Lucero, David Lewis, Eldon Peterson,
Sara Doutre

Commissioners Excused: Roylan Croshaw
Staff Present: Russ Holley, Tanya Rice, Mike DeSimone, Darren Farar, Mohammed Abdullahi

Commissioner Lucero made a motion to approve the minutes from the March 09, 2023 meeting as
submitted. Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 23-018 Sugar Creek [Zone Change & Subdivision Permit] Ben Steele/Rivergate LLC, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a rezone of approximately 35 acres from Suburban Neighborhood
Residential (NR-4) to Mixed Residential Transitional (MR-9) to develop a 208 lot mixed residential
subdivision that will include 82 single family building lots and 126 townhome lots located at
approximately 1095 West 600 South; TIN 02-066-0030; -0033; -0019; -0020; -0021 (Woodruff
Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Senior Planner reviewed the request for a rezone and subdivision permit for
approximately 35 acres located at 1100 West 600 South. The current zone is single-family at 4
homes per acre with a 10,000 square foot minimum lot size (NR-4) Future Land Use Plan designates
this area as Detached Residential (DR) which is single-family homes. The applicant is asking for a
rezone to Mixed Residential Transitional (MR-9) which allows for nine units per acre and different
types of attached housing. The subdivision plan is for a total of 208 lots that include 82 single-family
home building lots and 126 townhomes. The developer will have to complete the frontage along 600
South Street with asphalt that ties into the existing asphalt, curb and gutter, sidewalk, and park strip.

PROPONENT: Ben Steele said they are asking for a density of 6 units per acre. He spoke about
the extra land they are donating towards the 600 South street right of way. Mr. Steele spoke about
the need for more affordable, attainable housing in Logan.

PUBLIC: Cole Checketts spoke about his concern with the traffic at the intersection of 1000 West
and 600 South.

Keegan Garrity spoke about the traffic issues and the existing affordable high-density housing in the
woodruff neighborhood.

Melissa Dahle spoke about townhomes in the Woodruff neighborhood that are too narrow, trucks

cannot fit in the garages, and how the landscaping is not being maintained along 600 South on the
other side of the fence.


Kathleen Olsen asked where the water will run when the flowing wells that are on this property are
capped.

Ross Draper spoke about the standing water on this property and the townhomes having emergency
access.

Kay Lynn Beecher spoke about this area being zoned NR-4 to provide stability.

Paul Beecher spoke about the water issues with this property and how the water drains to the
northwest onto his property.

Shawn Milne said that density is not the problem, housing is needed in the area. There are just as
many problems with weeds and snow removal at single-family homes. Mr. Milne also spoke about
single-parent households and the elderly that need this type of housing.

Victor Landen spoke about his concern for the safety of the children and how there are no parks
nearby for them to play.

Stacy Bingham spoke about families outgrowing smaller homes on smaller lots. If lots are bigger
families can add on to their homes as their family grows and stay in the home.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Peterson stated that he cannot support a rezone, the city council
downzoned this area for a reason.

Commissioner Guth said that high-density housing is important and that many large properties in
Logan offer high-density housing.

Commissioner Lucero asked if there could be a restriction placed on the property with a rezone that
would only allow 6 units per acre. This property has good connectivity and it is designed well with
the single-family homes around the outside and the high density in the center.

Commissioner Lewis said this project has been put together well but this is not the right location for
MR-9.

Commissioner Doutre said she likes the design and this is the type of development the commission
would like to see in other areas where the zoning allows for it. Maybe there needs to be an MR-4
zone that would allow for this type of mixed housing.

Commissioner Heare said that the Planning Commission does not have the discretion to add a deed
restriction on the property that will have to be left to the Municipal Council to decide. Commissioner
Heare also agreed that this type of development is needed in the city.

MOTION: Commissioner Doutre moved to forward a recommendation of denial to the Municipal
Council on a Zone Change for PC 23-063 with the recommended findings for denial listed below.
Commissioner Peterson seconded the motion.

RECOMMENDED FINDINGS FOR REZONE DENIAL

1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as Detached Residential (DR).

2. The Woodruff Neighborhood Plan, a subset of the Logan General Plan adopted in 2021, plans
for this area as Detached Residential (DR) and recommends that zoning remain single family
detached in this area.

3. The nearest Multi-Family Zone (MR) is approximately 2,500 linear feet away.


4. The surrounding developed neighborhoods to the east, north, and south contain existing single-
family homes and are zoned NR-4 and NR-6.

Moved: S. Doutre Seconded: E. Peterson Approved: 5-1
Yea: Heare, Guth, Lewis, Peterson, Doutre Nay: Lucero Abstain:

MOTION: Commissioner Doutre moved to continue a subdivision permit for PC 21-063 to the May
11, 2023 meeting. Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion was approved
unanimously.

Meeting adjourned at 6:45 p.m.

Minutes approved as written and digitally recorded for the Logan City Planning Commission
meeting on March 23, 2023.
