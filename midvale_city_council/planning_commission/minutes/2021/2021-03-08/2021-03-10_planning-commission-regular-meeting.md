GENERAL SESSION
Chair Tippetts said this meeting will be an electronic meeting, without a physical location pursuant
to Utah Code 52-4-207, as amended by HB 5002. I, Candy Tippetts as Chair of Midvale City
Planning Commission, hereby make the determination that conducting the meeting with an anchor
location presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of those who may be present at the
anchor location. This determination is based upon the fact that the City is unable to ensure
compliance with the guidelines and public health protocols mandated by the state's current risk
phase.
