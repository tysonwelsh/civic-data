Citizen Public Input

               Adriana Caro read a letter, which requested the township to address a traffic issue
by her parent’s house located at 5416 South Sarah Jane Drive. In the 14 years that her parents
have lived on this corner there has been several accidents with one fatality. This is a busy
intersection and she asked that a wall be built along 5400 South. If this is not possible, then the
speed limit should be reduced.

                Madeline Franciso-Galang, Transportation Engineering Manager, Salt Lake
County, asked that Ms. Caro forward the letter to her so that it can be documented and forwarded
to the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) because 5400 South is a state road. Her office
will work with UDOT to address this concern. She asked that contact information be included in
the letter.

Coronavirus, Aid, Relief, & Economic Security (CARES) Act Funds

                Mayor Kelly Bush stated Kearns Metro Township was given the opportunity to
receive a second tranche of $1.15 million in CARES Act Funds. The Council is having a difficult
time trying to get this money out into the community in a way that would help businesses and the
community. The deadline to use these funds is December 3, 2020. Currently, the United States
Congress is the only ones that can extend the deadline and with everything going on in
Washington relating to the election, she did not see where it would be extending the deadline.
After talking with all Council Members, it was decided to return these funds to the County with the
stipulation that if the deadline was extended by six months to a year then Kearns would like the
opportunity to use the funds.

              Council Member Schaeffer, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to ratify
the decision not to accept the second tranche of CARES Act Funds. The motion passed
unanimously.

Process to Set Up Development Agency

              Adam Long, Attorney, Smith Hartvigsen, delivered a PowerPoint presentation on
Redevelopment Agencies. He highlighted what a Redevelopment Agency is, how it works, and
locations where it has been used. He stated the Council might want to create a project area for
the Kearns Town Center.

              Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated one of the
recommendations in the Kearns General Plan is to create a redevelopment agency to help
develop the warehouse district particularly for the town center.

             Mr. Long stated if the Council would like, he could put together a proposal for a
redevelopment agency.

All Council Members indicated it would be a good idea.

Process Needed to Adopt Impact Fees

                Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, presented an overview
of what impact fees are, and the process the Council would need to go through in order to adopt
them. He stated impact fees are highly regulated and can only be used to maintain the current
level of service. If the Council decided it wanted to investigation this possibility, it should be done
in conjunction with the creation of a Redevelopment Agency (RDA) and code updates. He asked
the Council for its input if he should start work with the Municipal Services District (MSD) on this
possibility.

All Council Members thought it was a good idea to move forward with this.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Levi Hughes updated the Council on the following:

    Scale back of the less critical areas that his office covers such as traffic details due to the
     rise of COVID-19 cases within the Kearns precinct.
    Office is currently closed, but appointments are available.
    Parking enforcement was done this week with 185 cars tagged.
    Time to rebid shifts for patrol officers. He is looking for ideas to let his officers know they
     are appreciated.
    Calls have gone up. Last year at this time, there was just under 1,300 calls. This year
     there has been 1,600 calls. He has seen an increase in family related calls.
    In the month of October there were no drive-by shooting reported. This is the first time in
     a long time.

Kearns Community Council Budget

               Paula Larsen, Chair, Kearns Community Council, presented the budget. She
stated the proposed budget shows the actual expenditures of the Kearns Community Council to
the end of October and what is projected for November and December. COVID expenses are
listed separately. The Community Council is asking to be made whole from the reduction it took
due to COVID. The amount it is asking for is $22,762.58.

2020, budget hearing, unless the Council has any changes it would like to make. The COVID

Christmas joy to the residents of Kearns.

COVID-19 Update

               Mayor Kelly Bush stated she would like to use some of the CARES Act Funds to
issue grocery vouchers to the most vulnerable population in Kearns. The vouchers would be for
$50 and handed out by the two food banks in Kearns as well as the senior center. She asked
what the Council Members thought about this idea.

              Council Member Peterson stated he would like to give a few of the vouchers to
the American Legion to hand out to some of the veterans in Kearns who are in need. These
vouchers could be included in a bag with the hand sanitizer and a mask.

All Council Member agreed that this would be a good idea.

              Mayor Bush stated she was thinking of getting 600 of these vouchers.

            Council Member Snow suggested this amount be increase to 650 vouchers. She
asked how these would be distributed to ensure that only one voucher per household was
received.

              Mayor Bush stated the vouchers would be given out on one day. The vouchers
should also be made available to the HOME center in Kearns.

             Council Member Peterson suggested the number of vouchers be increased to
700 to make sure there was enough.
