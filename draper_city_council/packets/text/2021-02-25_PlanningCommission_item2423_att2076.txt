BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 0.38 acres located
on the east side of Fort St., at approximately 12587 South Fort St. (Exhibit B). The property
is currently zoned TC. The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for
the development of a portion of the property into an office building.

The property currently contains a single-family residential home. The home was built in
1994 and is located on the east half of the property. A Site Plan was obtained on April 19,
2007 in order to convert the home into an office building. The applicant installed the
parking lot and road way improvements associated with the site plan, but never converted
the home into an office use. It has remained a single-family home, and that site plan is
expired.

The applicant obtained a Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit to redevelop the entire
property as a mixed use building on September 14, 2017. That approval included ground

floor office space and nine upper level apartments. That site plan approval has since
expired and no improvements were done on the property.

The proposed application will retain the single-family home on the property and develop
the vacant western half of the property into a two-story office building. The area subject to
this application equals 0.17 acres. The TC zone classifies single-family homes that existed at
the time of its adoption as legal nonconforming and allows them to stay. In addition, the TC
zone allows a residential density on the property of 6 dwelling units per acre. At 0.38 acres,
the property is allowed two residential units and is in conformance with the allowed
density allotment.



ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Town Center
land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is characterized as
follows:

The property has been assigned the TC zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the TC zone is to
“provide a method for implementing special provisions found within the general plan and the
Draper City conservation area master plan regarding the establishment and promotion of a
historic core area of the community characterized by stringent design standards for buildings,
public spaces, site design and landscaping with a harmonious mix of commercial uses, limited
high density residential uses and civic areas.” The TC zoning abuts the subject property on the
north, west and east, and RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum)
zone abuts on the south.

Site Plan Layout. The applicant is proposing to build a two story office building at the
southwest corner of the property (Exhibit E). The building will be located 2-feet from the
Fort St. right-of-way and 4-feet from 12600 South right-of-way. The TC zone sets a
maximum front setback of 15-feet. The building is 10-feet from the north property, and 81-
feet from the existing home on the property. The TC zone requires buildings to be at least
10-feet from residential properties. The parking lot will be located behind the building, and
between the building and the existing home on the property. The TC zone prohibits parking
in front of the building, and specifically states parking has to be to the rear or side of a
building. The parking lot has 10-foot setback from the 12600 South right-of-way, is 13-feet
from the north property line, and is 13.5-feet from the existing home on the property.

Landscaping and Lot Coverage. The property contains 27.6% overall landscaping (Exhibit F).
The TC zone requires a minimum of 15% overall landscaping. The building will cover 23.7%
of the property. The TC zone allows a maximum of 50% lot coverage when it comes to
building square footage. Both the percentage of landscaping and building coverage is
calculated for the 0.17 acres containing the office building and does not count the area
occupied by the home on the property. The building will contain an inner courtyard on the
first floor. This courtyard will be 707 square feet in size and include direct access to both
12600 South and the inside of the building. The TC zone is unique in that it requires
existing trees that are 4-inches or greater in caliper to be preserved on the property, or
replaced on a caliper per caliper basis. There is one tree that fits that standard. That tree is
to be preserved and is located along the north property line.

Parking. According to DCMC Section 9-25-100 office uses are required to provide 4 parking
spaces per 1,000 square feet of gross building floor area. The building equals 2,866 square
feet, requiring 11 parking spaces. The DCMC allows for an applicant to provide parking in a
range that is 10% +/- that number. The allowed parking range is 10-13 spaces. The
applicant has provided 10 parking spaces on the property (Exhibit E).

Architecture. The TC zone is specific in the architectural standards. The DCMC and TC zone
guidelines require a building to have a turn of the century architectural style. It also
requires at least 80% of a building’s façade to be in brick. The building complies with both



Fort Street Office                                                    App. # SPR-836-2019
Site Plan Request                              3
of the standards (Exhibit G). The building is 100% brick and features a traditional
architectural design, including vertical brick transitions between stories and vertical
windows. The building is 28-feet tall. The maximum height allowed in the TC zone is 35-
feet. The indoor courtyard will match the same window pattern as the rest of the building,
but in place of the glass windows, the openings will contain 2.5-foot tall wrought iron
fencing in order to enclose the space. During the November 18, 2019 Historic Preservation
Commission review, they recommended that the brick be authentic, not variegated or
painted, and could be red or yellow or other natural color in order to ensure the building
meets a turn of the century style. A condition of approval has been included in this staff
report on the type of brick.

Lighting. The applicant has provided a lighting plan which shows lights around the building,
with a concentration at the building entrances. Exhibit A includes some outstanding
redlines on the plans that will have to be addressed prior to the Land Disturbance Permit
being issued. The modifications include alteration of the foot candle levels on the property,
providing lighting fixtures that are appropriate to the Town Center area, and adding some
notes to the drawings. A condition of approval has been included in this staff report
requiring compliance with the lighting standards.

Fencing. No fencing is proposed for the development, and no fencing is required. There is
an existing 6-foot masonry wall along the north property line which will not be impacted by
this development.

                 and utilities concerns.
