Presentation title
       Call Type Descriptions
  The nine call types shared in this report indicate the highest volume type for Riverton Police Department and are listed in
  order of call demand.
  Assault - Intentional act causing fear of imminent bodily harm; physical contact is not required.
  Damaged Property - Real or personal property damaged by criminal behavior.
  Fraud - Intentional harm on another person causing financial harm.
  Larceny - Intentional deprivation of belongings.
  Stolen Vehicle - A stolen vehicle.
  Drugs - Drug-related offenses.
  Family Offense - Criminal act upon a family or household member.
  Vehicle Burglary - Items stolen from a vehicle.
  Domestic Violence - Abusive behavior or acts on another former or current family or household relationships.

Presentation title
         Accomplishments

     The Police Department embarked on policing for the Riverton community in July 2019. In February
     2023, a new administration was hired. The accomplishments shared in this report are not all inclusive
     and are representative of only the FY 2023.
     •     Established Core Values – P.R.I.D.E.
           Professionalism, Respect, Integrity, Dedication, and Excellence
     •     Restructured the Department
           Reclassified commander positions to deputy chief and reassigned 5 Detectives to Patrol
     •     Released law enforcement policies
     •     Reinstated Motors Unit

FY 2023 Department Goals
• Achieve Department accreditation
• Increase community policing and policing services
• Further collaborate with neighboring agencies
• Evaluate staffing and identify improved officer recruitment and development
• Conduct salary survey and potentially propose regular competitive pay increases
• Manage areas of risk management
   • Use/Show-of-Force tracking
   • Complaint tracking
   • Increase training opportunities
• Resume Animal Services for the community
• Proactive code enforcement and positive community resolutions
