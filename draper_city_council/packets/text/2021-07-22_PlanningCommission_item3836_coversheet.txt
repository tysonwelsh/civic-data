From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: July 22, 2021
Re: TriView Apartments Phase II – Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 8.27 acres located on the west side of Pony Express Rd., at approximately 12308 South Pony Express Rd. The property is currently zoned CR . The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for the redevelopment of the property into three apartment buildings, including mixed use buildings. The findings are as follows: For conditioned approval:
1.  The applicant acknowledges it has not met the specific conditions of approval.
2.  The application will meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code, including Section 9-5-090(E), once the specific conditions of approval have been met.
3.  The application complies with the approved Smith/Miller Development Agreement.
4.  The Planning Commission has agreed to approve the application with conditions (as outlined in the staff report) subject to the applicant complying with the specific conditions of approval.
5.  The final effective date of the Planning Commission’s site plan approval will be when the Zoning Administrator confirms the applicant has complied with the specific conditions of approval.
