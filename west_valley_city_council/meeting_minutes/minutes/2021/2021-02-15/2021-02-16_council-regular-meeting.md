COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Jim Vesock stated that he appreciates hearing from the other citizen who has been
    speaking during public comment period. He indicated that he is sure the Police
    Department is monitoring concerns on 4100 S.

       Mike Rigdon stated that traffic on 4100 South has increased considerably. He
       indicated that he was able to discuss the issues with a member of staff and he felt
       assured. Mr. Rigdon stated that he saw something on the news that registers and
       reports speed limits and would encourage the City to look into this. Mr. Rigdon
       stated he is frustrated that the City has charged him full fees at the Fitness Center
       when a senior is designated as 55 and up by federal law.

B.     CITY MANAGER COMMENTS
       Nicole Cottle, Acting City Manager, had no comments.

C.     CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
       Upon inquiry, members of the City Council had no comments.


CONSENT AGENDA
A.  RESOLUTION 21-30: RATIFY THE CITY MANAGER'S APPOINTMENT
    OF JIM VESOCK AS CHAIR OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY CLEAN AND
    BEAUTIFUL COMMITTEE FOR A TERM COMMENCING JANUARY 27,
    2021 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2021
    Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-30 that would ratify the City
    Manager's appointment of Jim Vesock as Chair of the West Valley City Clean and
    Beautiful Committee for a term commencing January 27, 2021 and ending June 30,
    2021.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              The Chair of the West Valley City Clean and Beautiful Committee serves
              for a term of one year. Committee members have nominated Jim to fulfill
              the remainder of the term for the previous Chair, Darrell Kirby, as he no
              longer serves on the Committee. His term will commence January 27, 2021
              and end June 30, 2021.

     EASEMENT PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND ACCEPT A PERPETUAL
     EASEMENT AND A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT WITH
     AND FROM ERIC AND STACEY HUTCHINGS FOR PROPERTY
     LOCATED AT 4189 SOUTH 2735 WEST
     Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 21-31 that would authorize the City
     to enter into an Easement Purchase Agreement and accept a Perpetual Easement
     and a Temporary Construction Easement with and from Eric and Stacey Hutchings
     for property located at 4189 South 2735 West.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            This portion of the Eric Hutchings and Stacey Hutchings parcel located at
            4189 South 2735 West is being acquired as part of the 2700 West
            Improvement Project Phase 2, scheduled to be constructed in 2020. This
            project will include construction of sidewalk, textured colored concrete
            parkstrip, street trees, and decorative street lighting along the west side of
            Constitution Boulevard (2700 West) from 4100 South to 4700 South. The
            project will also include an eight-foot tall decorative concrete post and panel
            wall along those properties with backyards facing Constitution Boulevard.
            The acquisition from Eric Hutchings and Stacey Hutchings includes a
            Perpetual Easement for the wall and for a Temporary Construction
            Easement. Compensation in the amount of $1,600 is based upon the
            Appraisal Report prepared by the Fortis Group LLC.
