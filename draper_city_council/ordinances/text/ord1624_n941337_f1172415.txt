City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1624

On September 17, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1624
amending the text of the land use and development code of the Draper City Municipal
Code. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 19th day of September 2024. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
