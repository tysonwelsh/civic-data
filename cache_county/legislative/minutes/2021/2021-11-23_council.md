                                    RESOLUTION NO. 2021-27



  A RESOLUTION INCREASING THE BUDGET APPROPRIATIONS FOR CERTAIN COUNTY DEPARTMENTS.

The Cache County Council, in a duly convened meeting, pursuant to Sections 17-36-12 through 17-36-26,
Utah Code Annotated, 1953 as amended, finds that certain adjustments to the Cache County budget for
2021 are reasonable and necessary; that said budget has been reviewed by the County Executive with all
affected department heads; that a duly called hearing has been held and all interested parties have been
given an opportunity to be heard; that the County Council has given due consideration to matters
discussed at the public hearing and to any revised estimates of revenues; and that it is in the best
interest of the County that these adjustments be made.

NOW THEREFORE, it is hereby resolved that:

Section 1.

The following adjustments are made to the 2021 budget for Cache County:

                                              See attached

Section 2.

Other than as specifically set forth above, all other matters set forth in the 2021 budget shall remain in
full force and effect.

Section 3.

This resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption and the County Executive and other county
officials are authorized and directed to act accordingly.

This resolution was duly adopted by the Cache County Council on November 23, 2021.
