Requested Agenda Title:
Adoption of a Proposed Scope of Work for the 2021/2022 Sandy Hills General Plan
Requested Agenda Item Description: The applicant is requesting the Council’s adoption of a proposed Scope
of Work for the 2021/2022 Sandy Hills General Plan. The Plan is intended to synthesize data and public
feedback to describe current conditions, establish a vision for Sandy Hill’s future, and guide this area of Salt
Lake County from its present conditions toward its desired vision. The proposed Scope of Work suggests four
planning elements for the Unincorporated Area of Sandy Hills:
1. Introduction
2. Land Use
3. Transportation
4. Resilience and Infrastructure
A one-year timeline is proposed to complete this work.
