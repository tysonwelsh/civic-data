                           Services Director

SUMMARY:

Riverton City Code has six (6) multifamily residential zones with densities ranging from six units per
acre (6 du/a) up to eighteen units per acre (18 du/a). Each of the Multi-family zones has a minimum lot
size as shown in the table below. These minimum lot sizes were established to encourage parcel
consolidation into projects of sufficient size to create quality projects.

As Riverton has grown over the years, the city has seen a shift from developing large farm fields, to
smaller projects of 3-5 acres, and in recent years a focus on small infill projects. While there is still
some undeveloped or underutilized land in the city, there are fewer and the parcels are generally
smaller. Assembling multiple parcels has become a high hurdle for development even in areas that
Riverton City has designated for multi-family in the General Plan.

These proposed adjustments to multi-family project size would create more options for the Planning
Commission and City Council as they make land-use decisions particularly for infill properties and may
enable development in areas previously designated for multifamily zoning. The proposed changes will
also simplify and standardize the area requirements such that all four zones for RM-8 to RM-14 will have
a 2-acre minimum. The highest density zone will still require a larger project size with 5 acres. The RM-
6 zone will remain unchanged.
