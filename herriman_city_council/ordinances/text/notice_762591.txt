PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on June 8, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2022-28 An ordinance of Herriman city adopting a final budget; making appropriations for the support of Herriman city for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2022, and ending June 30, 2024; revising the 2021-2022 fiscal year budget; approving the master fee schedule and determining the rate of tax and levying taxes upon all real and personal property within Herriman city, Utah. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
