REGULAR MEETING

Chair Erickson called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. She
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS


   1. Eric Winters requests Large Scale Master Plan approval for the View 78
      Apartments located at 795 W Ivy Dr in the Jordan Bluffs Subareas 1-3 zone.

      Midvale Municipal Code 17-7-10.12.4 outlines the development review process for a
      large-scale master plan as follows (staff responses in bold):

      A. A written narrative and graphic exhibits explaining and showing the nature and
         character of the development. This information shall include: total acreage broken
         down into the approximate acreage of any phases, parcels, and specific land uses;
         scale/intensity of each land use expressed in numbers (i.e., residential units/density,
         square footage of commercial uses).

         Staff response: The applicant provided the written narrative (letter on p.1 of
         submittal) and the project acreage for phases and land uses (all residential) (see
         C-100, C-101). The density for Phase 1, which consists of 260 units, is 20.8 units/
         acre, for Phase 2, which consists of 96 units, it is 14.5 units/acre. There are no
         commercial uses.

      B. A map of the existing site showing approximate site boundaries and dimensions,
         topography, roads, public utilities, and other major infrastructure improvements, as
         well as existing adjacent land uses and development.

         Staff response: The applicant provided a site plan (see C-100) and a boundary
         description (see C-101) which show site boundaries, dimensions, roads,
         topography, and drainage (see C-200). An overall utility plan is shown on sheet
         C-300. Please note the sewer vac station located between the Midvale City Public
         Works Department and the project. The details of this facility will be worked out
         as part of a development agreement. Adjacent uses are (1) apartments to the
         north of Ivy Dr which are currently under construction by the applicant; (2)
         condos to the south (Holmes Homes project); (3) Midvale City Public Works
         Department and an office/warehouse development (Mechanical Products NSW)
         to the east; and (4) vacant land to the west that is also in the Jordan Bluffs
         Subareas 1-3 zone.

      C. A proposed site plan showing general location and size of all buildings, setbacks,
         streets, walkways, parking areas, general landscaping plans, plazas, gathering areas,
         and recreational amenities. Site plans for pods adjacent to the park shall also show
         public pedestrian access to the park and parking for the park as required by Section
         177-10.12.5(H).

         Staff response: The applicant provided a site plan (see sheet C-100) which shows
         general building locations, sizes, and designs (see sheet A-4.01), setbacks, streets,
         walkways, and parking areas. The planting plan and amenities plan (see sheets
         labeled “LOFTSSIXFOUR”) show landscaping, plazas, gathering areas, and
         recreational amenities. With the latest changes in the Subarea 1-3 Project Areas,
         which the Planning Commission recommended to the City Council on 1/8/2025,
         the new Pods A, B1, and B2 which represent Phases 1, 2, and 3 of the
         development, do not border the Jordan Bluffs park; therefore specific access and
         parking is not required.

      D. A preliminary utilities master plan (including general location, size, capacity),
         grading, and drainage plan.

         Staff response: Sheets C-200 and C-300 provide a preliminary utilities master
         plan which was approved by the city engineer.

      E. A transportation plan showing road, bicycle, and pedestrian networks to include a
          project circulation system and connections to adjacent developments and larger
          circulation networks in the city and region (including transit); and descriptions of
          internal streets, roads, alleys, and pedestrian and bicycle facilities.

         Staff response: The applicant provided a circulation plan that shows vehicular,
         bicycle, and pedestrian circulation patterns. The tertiary road that traverses the
         site is shown for vehicular circulation only but could also qualify for bicycle

         during the site plan stage.

      F. An analysis of the traffic impact of the project on existing and proposed streets
         (including projected traffic counts).

         Staff response: The View 78 Trip Generation Study Update included in your
         packet shows that this development has a lower trip generation than the
         previous proposal and the underlying traffic study from 2020.

      G. A master parking plan for each pod. If the developer chooses to use parking from
         other pods to offset the parking requirement on a subject pod, then the parking plan
         will include each pod in its overall calculation(s). The master parking plan shall be
         based on the minimum parking requirements found in Table 17-7-10.12.6 and may
         allow for reductions based on the developer’s ability to show shared parking
         opportunities for uses within and between pods.

         Staff response: The parking analysis shown on sheet C-100 depicts that each
         pod/phase parks independently of the other and fulfills the parking
         requirements for the 58 Plex structures or exceeds them in the townhome areas.

      H. Architectural elevations showing architectural theming for each type of building
         including building materials and architectural design precedents.

         Staff response: The applicant provided project elevations (see sheet A 4.01, A
         4.02, and sheets named “Townhome Design”) and precedents (included photos,
         e.g. “The Austin”, “Embold”).

      I. Thematic site design elements to be utilized. These elements include street trees,
         street lighting, pedestrian scaled lighting, parking lot lighting, public furniture
         (including benches, trash receptacles, bike racks, etc.), pedestrian crossings, and
         wayfinding signage.

         Staff response: The applicant provided typicals for trees, lighting, furniture, and
         park equipment, among others found in the photographs. These items will be
         refined in the site plan stage of the approval process.

      J. A master sign plan with proposed project sign locations, sign designs, building sign
         envelopes and other related features. The master sign plan may include wayfinding
         signs that provide direction to points of interest within Jordan Bluffs and adjacent
         areas. These signs shall be designed to reflect the thematic site and architectural
         design of the area with the specific sign details and locations approved by the
         community development director.

         Staff response: The applicant provided a Signage and Wayfinding plan and
         designs in their thematic images. These items will be refined in the site plan stage
         of the approval process.

       K. A preliminary development schedule and proposed phasing plan.

          Staff response: The letter details the development schedule which shows initial
          construction of Phase 1 (232 units in 58-plexes, 28 townhome units) for Summer
          of 2025 with a projected completion in Fall of 2027. Phase 2 (96 townhome units)
          follows in 2028 after the completion of Phase 1.
       L. Such other information as the community development director deems necessary to
          determine whether the application complies with the standards adopted in this
          chapter. (Ord. 2019-03 § 1 (Att. A (part)))

           Staff response: This application is based on an amended development agreement
           and code exhibits that are pending city council approval (see findings).

       Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject area. No
       written objections have been received as of the writing of this report.

       Staff recommends approval of the Large-Scale Master Plan for the View 78 Apartments
       with the following findings:
               1. The project complies with Midvale Municipal Code (MMC) 17-7-10.12.4
               2. The project must adhere to all approval steps required in MMC 17-7-10.12.2
               3. Midvale City Engineering, Midvale City Planning, and Unified Fire
                  Departments recommend approval.
