PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on June 12, 2024, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2024-12 amending the Herriman City official zoning map for 1.10 acres of property located at 7457 West Hi Country Road from A-1-43 (single family agricultural zone) to A-1-21 (single family agricultural zone). A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
