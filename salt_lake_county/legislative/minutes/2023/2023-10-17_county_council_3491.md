RECITALS
A. WHEREAS, on October 10, 2023, the Salt Lake County Council 
considered enactment of a civil parking enforcement ordinance (“parking 
ordinance”), which was patterned after a similar ordinance enacted by the 
Town of Brighton (“Brighton”), and includes a schedule of penalties for 
violation of the parking ordinance.
B. WHEREAS, the Council learned from Brighton ’s experience about the 
costs of processing appeals of parking penalties, and the desirability of 
reducing penalties in limited circumstances to save the expense of contested 
appeals.
C. WHEREAS, the Council is authorized to reduce fees set by ordinance 
pursuant to Salt Lake County Ordinance section 3.42.073(B)(1).
D. WHEREAS, the Council desires to authorize the Greater Salt Lake
