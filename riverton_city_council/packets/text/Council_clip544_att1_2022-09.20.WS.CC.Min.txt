32            Code Enforcement Ordinance Discussion
33
34   Mr. Brickey explained that he met with a group of directors and discussed the ongoing code
35   enforcement issues. He asked Ryan Carter, City Attorney, and Jason Lethbridge, Development
36   Services Director, to report to the Council.
37
38   Mr. Carter explained that staff and Council have had an ongoing discussion about what
39   ordinances could be added to improve code enforcement. He said in state code, it is illegal to
40   drive a vehicle that is unregistered, but legal to park an unregistered vehicle on the road. In
41   state law, parking a vehicle for more than 48 hours on the street is illegal. However, it is hard
42   for law enforcement to prove the vehicle was parked in the same location for more than 48
43   hours.
44
45   Mr. Carter explained that a draft ordinance was sent to Councilmembers in an email. The draft
46   ordinance would require a vehicle to be moved more than 400 feet every 48 hours. The draft
47   ordinance would also make parking an unregistered vehicle on the street illegal.
48
49   Mayor Staggs asked the Council how they would like to move forward.
50
51   Councilmember Wells asked for more time to review the changes.

 1   Mr. Lethbridge explained he would implement a policy that if a dog bite results in an injury, the
 2   dog will be impounded. The City will hold the dog until vaccination status can be obtained. The
 3   resident will be able to appeal this through a process.
 4
 5   Councilmember McDougal would like to move forward with the changes to the parking
 6   ordinances. He asked if the City has a policy for multiple offenders related to dog bites. In
 7   response, Mr. Carter said the current ordinance states the license will be revoked if a dog bites
 8   three times. Mr. Carter said that staff is discussing whether this should be reduced from three
 9   times to two times. Mr. Lethbridge said impounding the dog allows staff time to evaluate the
10   dog while the animal is in the care and custody of the City.
11
12   Councilmember Buroker asked if the ordinance would outline when the dog is returned to the
13   owner. Mr. Lethbridge said the ordinance will specify when the dog would be returned to the
14   owner.
15
16   Mayor Staggs asked staff to clarify the policy versus the ordinance. In response, Mr. Lethbridge
17   said the current ordinance allows the code enforcement officer to determine what to do with the
18   dog. In the future, the ordinance language will state that the dog will be impounded, however,
19   until the ordinance is adopted, he has directed staff to impound the dog in the event of a dog
20   bite that causes injury.
21
22   Councilmember McDougal asked about the City’s liability in releasing a dog that may bite again.
23   Mr. Carter said there is not a liability to the City.
24
25   Councilmember Wells asked about City code regarding cargo containers. In response, Mr.
26   Lethbridge said the City’s ordinance prohibits cargo containers which raises the question about
27   whether they should be prohibited. Councilmember Wells commented that he has noticed
28   containers in his district and he would like the Council to consider if these should be allowed.
29   Councilmember McCay agreed and said she would like to discuss this topic in a future work
30   session.
31
32   Councilmember Buroker expressed appreciation for staff’s work on the dog bite ordinance.
33
34   Councilmember McDougal said he would also like a discussion on storage containers and

34   City is proposing approval of a Development Agreement establishing requirements, standards,
35   and parameters for development of approximately 3.2-acres located at 13040 S Redwood
36   Road, including approval of an eight (8) lot subdivision of that property, with all lots accessing a
