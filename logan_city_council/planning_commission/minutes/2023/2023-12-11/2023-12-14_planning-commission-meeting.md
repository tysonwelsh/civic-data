PC 23-072 Dunn/Dean STR [Conditional Use Permit] David Dunn/David D & Sandra Dunn, Eric R
& Robin Dean, authorized agent/owners are requesting a conditional use permit to operate a four-
bedroom short-term rental of the entire home located at 1369 North 1500 East in the Traditional
Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 07-111-0018 (Hillcrest Neighborhood).

PROPONENT: David Dunn said they purchased the property for a vacation home for use by the two
different families and intend to rent it as a short-term rental to help with the cost and to have the
home not sit vacant between family visits.

PUBLIC: Paul Larsen spoke about how short-term rentals allow overoccupancy and commercial use
in residential neighborhoods and how that does not follow Logan City’s General Plan. Mr. Larsen
also stated that the short-term rental ordinance needs to be revisited by the City Council.

Miriam Karbasi who lives near the proposed rental said she has young children and is worried for
their safety with strangers coming in and out of the short-term rental.

Bruce Cook who lives next to the proposed short-term rental asked the commission if they could
defer the approval until the City Council can revisit the ordinance. He spoke about a conversation
he had with a city council member regarding the short-term rental ordinance needing to be reviewed.

Ted Evans who lives near the proposed short-term rental asked the commission to please defer their
decision on this proposal.

Amber Jones spoke about deferring the decision on this project, her concern with the safety of the
children in the area, parking, snow removal, and this affecting home affordability.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Hear stated that the commission can continue a project, however,
because the applicant has already applied, the decision is based on the current code regardless of
any code changes in the future. Commissioner Hear also explained that the Land Development
Code allows different occupancy limits for short-term rentals.

Commissioner Doutre said that the Planning Commission's role is to determine whether or not the
proposed project meets the current code, they are not able to change the requirements. The public
needs to contact the City Council to change any of the requirements.

Commissioner Guth stated that if there are any short-term rental issues they need to be reported to
the City to look into and enforce upon if needed. !f any licensed short-term rental receives three
violations from the City, their business license will be revoked.

PC 23-077 Logan Secure Storage [Zone Change, Conditional Use Permit, Design Review Permit]
Briell Huddleston/Skyler Hartman, authorized agent/owner are requesting a zone change of a 4.43-

acre parcel from Industrial Park (IP) to Airport (AP) to build a new 70,000 square foot self-storage
facility located at 1950 North 1000 West; TIN 04-076-0015 (Bridger Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Planner reviewed the request for a rezone of a 4.43-acre parcel from Industrial
Park (IP) to Airport (AP) to build a new 70,275 square foot self-storage facility. The Airport zone is
the only zone that permits self-storage land uses. In October 2021 a conditional use permit was
approved for Infinite Storage (previous project name) that expired in October 2022. in March 2022
the Logan City Council adopted a code amendment prohibiting new storage units in all zones except
for the Airport zone. In May 2023 the applicant submitted for a building permit which was not
accepted by the City because their entitlements had expired. In July 2023 the applicant submitted
an appeal and the City’s Land Use Appeals Board upheld staffs denial of the project and determined
the project was expired. The applicant is now requesting a rezone to the Airport zone to acquire a
new permit and entitlement for the self-storage project that is now named Logan Secure Storage.

R. Holley reviewed the proposed storage shed designs and said that the Planning Commission can
make a decision on the design review and conditional use permit now or they can wait for the City
Council's final decision on the zone change. R. Holley also said that this area is zoned Industrial in
the future land use plan and it does not make sense to spot zone the property to Airport. Staff is
recommending denial of the rezone.

Commissioner Heare asked about the status of the appeal.
Mohammed Abdullahi, City attorney said the appeal was dismissed at the District Court.

Commissioner Lucero asked about the decision to not allow storage units. R. Holley said that
storage sheds take up a lot of land area and have very little employment benefit.

Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director said the city is running out of industrial land that

is suitable for future development. The City needs to ensure that there are adequate employment
opportunities for future residents and storage sheds do not provide that.


PROPONENT: Skyler Hartman spoke about not submitting for their building permit on time causing
their original permit to expire. He believes storage sheds are in high demand and that their proposed
project is congruent with the surrounding uses.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Lucero asked what the differences are between the Airport and
Industrial zones.

R. Holley explained that the airport zone is designed to support airport-type uses, storage, shipping
and receiving associated with air freight, and hotels. From a development standpoint, the zones are
similar with generous setbacks and higher landscaping requirements. The maximum building height
in the industrial zone is higher to anticipate for manufacturing allowing exceptions for towers and
batch plants. The airport zone has height limits associated with the airport overlay areas.

Commissioner Guth said the commission doesn't typically like to recommend spot zoning. If storage
sheds are needed and in demand in this area then the code may need to be amended.

Commissioner Doutre said she agreed with Commissioner Guth and stated that the zoning was
intentionally changed to not allow storage uses in this area.

Commissioner Heare said this is not the best use of land in this area.
Commissioner Peterson said this project would make more sense along Airport road.
MOTION: (Zone Change) Commissioner Peterson made a motion to forward a recommendation

of denial to the Municipal Council on a zone change for PC 23-077. Commissioner Guth seconded
the motion

FINDINGS FOR DENIAL-Zone Change

1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as Industrial Park (IP).

2. The surrounding zoning is IP.

3. The Logan City Municipal Council adopted a code amendment in 2022 prohibiting commercial
storage units in the IP zone.

4. Land in the IP zone should be reserved for uses and businesses that support better
employment opportunities.
