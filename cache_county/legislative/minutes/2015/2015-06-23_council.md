e Ordinance No. 2015-10- Adoption of Mid-year Adjustments to Salaries for Certain
Elected Officials — Chairman Robison asked Executive Buttars to explain the reasons

for the proposed salary adjustments. Executive Buttars reminded the Council they had a
discussion last year about adjusting the salaries of the Assessor and Clerk/Auditor.
Reasons for the adjustment to the Assessor are the level of training that is required and
the number of employees under the Assessor’s supervision. Comparison with other
assessors in the state justifies a bump in salary. The adjustment to the Clerk/Auditor
position is warranted because of additional responsibilities and an increased workload.
The recommendation is to increase the Assessor's salary for the remainder of 2015 by
$3,000.00 and the Clerk/Auditor’s salary for the remainder of 2015 by $2,000.00 AND to
increase the Assessor's salary by $6,000.00 for 2016 forward and the Clerk/Auditor’s
salary by $4,000.00 for 2016 forward.

Council member Yeates asked Finance Director Jensen if the salary adjustments can be
taken from Assessing and Collecting. Jensen replied it could be; however, the budget
opening later on the agenda tonight lists it in the General Fund.

4

06-23-2015

Council member White asked why the salary adjustments aren't the same amount.

Buttars said it was the opinion of the Compensation Committee that the level of training
required by the Assessor and the number of employees supervised were the deciding

factors. Council member Zilles confirmed that.

Chairman Robison asked Clerk/Auditor Zollinger how many additional hours of training

she has had since January 1, 2015. Zollinger responded she has been to three different
trainings by the State Tax Commission and also some through UAC and will be

attending more training tomorrow. Robison said a specific number of hours would be
helpful and asked Zollinger how much her workload has increased. Zollinger responded
her workload has increased by 50%. Robison asked for documentation of her

distribution of hours and training.

Council member Yeates observed that Zollinger has been the Fair Manager for many

years which requires additional hours and perhaps it’s time to shift that responsibility
elsewhere. Executive Buttars commented they will be looking at plans for possible
changes in the fair management after the 2015 Fair.

Attorney Lee Edwards pointed out that state code says when offices are combined the

individual's salary can be increased up to one-half of the second office’s salary.

Vice Chair Merrill noted the county combined the offices to save money and the
proposed salary increase is less than 10% of the salary of the previous Auditor.

Council member White said he would rather see the salary adjustments be the same.

Zollinger is now required to learn two jobs, not one job.

(Attachment 3)

ACTION: Motion by Council member Erickson to waive the rules and approve Ordinance

EMPATHY FAIRNESS INTEGRITY PROFESSIONALISM RESPECT

June 23, 2015

The Merit Commission, as established under the provisions of 17-30, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as
amended, members are appointed by the County Council at the recommendation of the Sheriff. Members
are appointed to six year terms and terms are staggered every two years. Members may be recommended
to serve additional terms. Not more than two members of the commission shall be affiliated with or
members of the same political party.

The primary duties of the Commission are to formulate rules and regulations covering the examinations
and ratings of applicants for appointment and promotion in the Merit System, to compile and maintain
eligible registers for this purpose, and to hear appeals from any Deputy Sheriff in the Merit System from
any disciplinary action.

As the Sheriff, and in accordance with Deputy Sheriff's Merit System Act of the Utah Code, I present the

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR CHANGES OF SALARIES FOR CERTAIN
CACHE COUNTY OFFICERS FOR 2015 AND SUPERSEDING PRIOR RESOLUTIONS
AND ORDINANCES.

The County Council of Cache County, Utah, in a regular meeting, lawful notice of which has been
given, finds that a public hearing regarding the county budget for 2015 including the change of
salaries for certain Cache County Officers for the remainder of 2015 was conducted on June 23,
2015, in accordance with Section 17-16-14, Utah Code Annotated, upon lawful notice: that the
Organic Act for the Government of Cache County, Utah, as approved on November 6, 1984,
authorizes the modification of the salaries for all elected county officers by ordinance; that the
budget for Cache County officers has been adopted by the Cache County Council in accordance
with the applicable law; and that an ordinance providing for a salary change for certain elected
county officers is appropriate for carrying into effect the approved salary changes.

Now therefore, the Cache County Council ORDAINS as follows:

A. The full time salaries for county officers for the period January 1, 2015 through December
31, 2015, shall be as follows:

County Attorney $ 111,395

County Executive-Surveyor 8 OB 1S7

County Sheriff S$ 86,109
County Treasurer § 15,753)

SECTION 2: REPEALER

ordinance, are hereby repealed and superseded by this ordinance to the extent of such conflict.
