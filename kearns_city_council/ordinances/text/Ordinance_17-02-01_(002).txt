WITNESS

WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature, during the 2015 General Session, passed Senate Bill 199 (SB199) creating the Metro Township form of municipal government and setting an election for the voters of Kearns on November 3, 2015 to choose between incorporating as a Metro Township or incorporating as a City; and
WHEREAS, SD199 also asked the voters to choose whether Kearns would join the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as members of the District; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns voters, in the November 3, 2015 election, voted to incorporate as a Metro Township and to join the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) as members of the District; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Voters elected the five residents to serve as their representatives on the Magna Metro Township Council on November 8, 2016; and
WHEREAS, to complete the incorporation process, per State Law, the Kearns Metro Township Council was required to file a notice of incorporation letter, a notice of boundary action, and a Final Local Entity Plat with the Lieutenant Governor's Office lo achieve the final certification of the Kearns Metro Township as a municipal entity; and

WIIEREAS, this work had to be performed prior to the Council's official terms office
commencing; and

WHEREAS, the elected members of the Kearns Metro Township Council approved the creation and filing of this work and authorized their chair to sign the required documents; and
WHEREAS, the required documents were filed with the Lieutenant Governor's Office on December 16, 2016; and
WHEREAS, the Lieutenant Governor certified the creation of the Kearns Metro Township on December 27, 2016; and
WHEREAS, the Certificate and Final Local Entity Plat were recorded on December 30, 2016 with the Salt Lake County Recorder, completing the incorporation process and officially creating the Kearns Metro Township.
RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Kearns Metro Township Council does hereby ratify and approve the finished work attached to this ordinance, creating the Kearns Metro Township and accepting the date of January 2, 2017 as the official creation date of the Kearns Metro Township.
