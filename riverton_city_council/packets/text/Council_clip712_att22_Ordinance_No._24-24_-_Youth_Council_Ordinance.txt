                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 24-24

       AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING RIVERTON CITY CODE TITLE 2, CHAPTER 130,
                            SECTIONS 010 TO 070
                      [RIVERTON CITY YOUTH COUNCIL]

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is charged with the duty to protect the health, safety and welfare of its
community; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds a need exists to improve citizen participation in
governmental decision making and implementation of public programs; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that one way to encourage the
participation in government is by providing programs which educate the City’s youth about how local
government functions, and the programs it implements which benefit the public; and

        WHEREAS, to this end, Riverton City has sponsored a youth council program which assembles
students from local high schools for attending conferences and undertaking projects which educate youth
about local government; and

        WHEREAS, recognizing the youth council program as a success, the Riverton City Council finds
and determines that it should be granted status of an equal dignity to the City’s other volunteer boards and
commissions, by recognizing it with an Ordnance which formally creates and implements the Riverton City
Youth Council program; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the following action is in the best
interest of the City’s health, safety and general welfare.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.       Amendment. The following Code Section is hereby adopted:

                 Title 2, Chapter 130, Sections 010 to 070, creating the Riverton City Youth
                 Council as provided in the attached Exhibit A.
