Public Comment (10:48)

honor our previous first lady.

Redevelopment Agency of Provo.

2.        A resolution authorizing the Chief Executive Officer to sign an extension to the Exclusive
          Right to Negotiate agreement with McWhinney Real Estate Development for the
          redevelopment of the existing City Hall property downtown. (22-025) (18:53)

     Motion:     An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-RDA-03-01-2, as currently constituted,

David Walter provided background information.

Chair Ellsworth opened public comment, with none provided, she closed public comment.

Chair Ellsworth called for a vote.

     Vote:     The motion was approved 6:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Mackay,
               Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Councilor Hoban excused.

reconvened as the Provo Municipal Council.

Action Agenda

3.        A resolution appointing individuals to the Civil Service Commission. (22-009) (21:05)

     Motion:     An implied motion on Resolution 2022-09, as currently constituted, had been made by
                 council rule.

Brian Jones, City Attorney, provided background of what the Civil Service Commission is responsible for.
He presented Rich Ferguson and Amy Jensen as the individuals being appointed.

Councilor Ellsworth asked which members were being replaced.

Chair Handley called for a vote.

     Vote:     The motion was approved 6:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Mackay,
               Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Councilor Hoban excused.
