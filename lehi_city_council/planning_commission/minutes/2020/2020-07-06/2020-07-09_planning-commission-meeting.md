2. Regular Agenda

2.1 Recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code Amendment
to Chapters 6, 16, 17, and 37, amending the single-family residential design standards for
(PC) Planned Communities, (PRD) Planned Residential Development, and (PUD) Planned
Unit Development. (Tabled from the June 25, 2020 meeting)

Commissioner Everett asked about cost to developers and concerned that it won't help promote
affordable housing. Kim Struthers said that it seemed that the developers stated that the


materials were the primary concern. Mr. Struthers discussed about balance of good neighbors
and the cost pushing it out of affordability.

Commissioner Nielsen asked if they could require instead of encouraged for the sections on
garages. Craig Chambers said they can require if the code allows it.

Commissioner Nielsen made a motion moved to forward a positive recommendation noting that
they would like to change wording on the garage section to be required as opposed to
encouraged.


Motion: Commissioner Nielsen made a motion moved to forward a positive recommendation
noting that they would like to change wording on the garage section to be required as opposed to
encouraged. Commissioner Peterson seconded it.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Danny Gutierrez & Mike Dubois’s request for
review of a General Plan Amendment on approximately 23.9-acres located at approximately
1402 West 900 North, changing the land use designation from VLDRA (very low-density
residential agriculture) to VLDR (very low density residential).

Commissioner Hemmert asked if it aligned with the current or future plan.

The applicants Danny Gutierrez and Mike Dubois presented some pictures and discussed why they
thought they could fit in the area to the commissioners

Commissioner Hemmert mentioned that he didn't think that people wanted smaller lots.
Commissioner Ellis asked about PRD’s. The applicant said they have been researching a lot and
felt that the PRD wasn't an option for them.

Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Hemmert noted that there were three public comments.

Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Peterson asked questions to the staff. If of the lots hit the animal requirements with
density bonuses. He pointed out properties to the south which were not mentioned in the
presentation that had R-1-22. His opinion is just because that's what we want and that the
applicants don't think people can take care of areas, but he believes you can so he doesn't feel like
there is enough justification to change.

Commissioner Nielsen said lot size doesn't make a good neighbor. The bigger issue if we approve
this what's stopping from someone else and that it will open a can of worms and make it hard to
enforce the vision and goals of the city for the future.
Applicant Danny Gutierrez asked a question to the Commissioners about the General Plan.
Commissioner Hemmert discussed the general plan and the amendments because they fit the future
plan.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson motioned to recommend a negative recommendation of Danny
Gutierrez & Mike Dubois’s request for review of a General Plan Amendment on approximately
23.9-acres located at approximately 1402 West 900 North, changing the land use designation from
VLDRA (very low-density residential agriculture) to VLDR (very low density residential).;
including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report stating that The proposed general
plan amendment is not in conformance with the general plan the intent and the vision in its various
elements Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed.
