                              AMENDING THE 2017 BUDGET
                                      OFTHE
                             COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP

BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council
("Council") held a regular public meeting following a public hearing on September 20, 2017.

        WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township ("Copperton") is a municipality and
political subdivision of the State of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Metropolitan Services District approved an increase of
$30,000 to Copperton's 2017 budget; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton desires to apply this amount to the Legal Services line item in the
2017 Budget to bring this fund more in line with the amounts allocated to the other metro
townships for legal services, bringing the total amendment for Legal Services in the 2017 Budget
to $50,000;

       WHEREAS, this increase requires an amendment to Copperton's 2017 Budget;

       WHEREAS, Copperton made said amendment available for public inspection for a
period of at least seven days prior to the public hearing in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-
5-107; and

       WHEREAS, on September 20, 2017 at 6:30 p.m., Copperton held said public hearing on
said amendment in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-5-108 at the Bingham Lions Club,
8725 West Hillcrest Street, Copperton, Utah.

        WHEREAS, the Council has determined that it is in Copperton's best interests to make
these revenue adjustments in Copperton's 2017 Budget.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council, that, in accordance with
U.C.A. §IOB-5-108, Copperton hereby adjusts Copperton's 2017 Budget by increasing the fund
available for legal expenses by $30,000 to $50,000.

       ADOPTED AND APPROVED by majority vote at a duly called meeting of the Board of
Trustees on this 20 th day of September, 2017.
