Pre Council, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present except for
Council Condie who is excused. Councilor Southwick gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Report
2.1 Discussion of Cell Towers on City Property
Mr. Ryan Wood, City Attorney, stated that they are receiving more requests for cell tower
construction on city property, and wanted to know if the Council was still willing to entertain more
cell tower applications. He explained that the city could enter into lease agreements that would
allow these companies to construct the towers on city property. The Council reached consensus
that they are willing to entertain these applications on a case-by-case basis.

2.2 Holbrook Farms Park Discussion
Steve Marchbanks, Parks Manager, gave an update on the latest concept design for the Holbrook
Farms Park. He stated that native grass is not aesthetically the best, but it’s the responsible thing
to plant for conservation. He stated that there have been requests from residents to modify the
current approve concept plan; however, that would require changes to the grading plan and
irrigation system and those plans have already been drawn up. He stated that they could ask the
developer if they’d be willing to switch the turf grass and native grass areas. There was
discussion of changing the code so that concept plans for large parks are given a public hearing
or possibly brought to the Council for final approval.

2.3 Master Transportation Plan Discussion
Ryan Hales and Josh Gibbons, with Hales Engineering, gave on update on the City’s new Master
Transportation Plan. Mr. Hales stated that they have added a new connector that will help with
