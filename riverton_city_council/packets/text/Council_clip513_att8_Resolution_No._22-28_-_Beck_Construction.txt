                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 22-28

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT BE ISSUED TO
 BECK CONSTRUCTION TO COMPLETE THE 11800 SOUTH (3807 W) FRONTAGE
                     IMPROVEMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to complete the 11800 South (3807
West) Frontage Improvement Project.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order Contract be issued to Beck
         Construction to complete the 11800 South (3807 W) Frontage Improvement Project.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 5th day of
April 2022 by the following vote:
