City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 5, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1502, amending
the official zoning map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Larson Zone Change.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 6th day of October 2021. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
