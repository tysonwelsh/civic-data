         AN ORDINANCE TO SUPPORT AND ENCOURAGE THE APPROVAL OF THE USE,
               REGULATION, AND STUDY OF CANNABIS FOR MEDICAL USES


       BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council met in
       a regular public meeting on November 15, 2017.
                                                 WITNESS
              A.    WHEREAS, studies have demonstrated medical cannabis eases the nausea
       associated with chemotherapy in cancer patients, thus decreasing the discomfort of
       chemotherapy and making frequent chemotherapy treatments more tolerable; and
              B.      WHEREAS, cancer patients often lose their appetite or eating becomes painful for
       them, and medical cannabis has been reported to increase one's appetite, thus enabling terminal
       cancer patients to take in sustenance longer; and
              C.      WHEREAS, the oil from the cannabis plant has been demonstrated to stop and/or
       prevent seizures in people with epilepsy and other seizure disorders; and
              D.      WHEREAS, some studies report that medical cannabis may slow or prevent, and
       in some cases reverse the growth and/or spread of some cancer cells; and
              E.      WHEREAS, some studies have shown that medical cannabis slows the
       progression of Alzheimer's in some patients; and
             F.      WHEREAS, many forms of cannabis for medical use do not contain THC, the
       psychoactive cannabinoid found in cannabis; and
              G.     WHEREAS, there are many prescription painkillers currently legal which contain
       codeine, morphine, and other opiates and narcotics which cause physical addiction and other
       mental and physical deterioration far worse than that caused by recreational cannabis; and
              H.      WHEREAS, twenty-nine states and the District of Columbia have legalized the
       medical use of cannabis.
                                               RESOLUTION
               THEREFORE, it is the resolution of this Council on behalf of Copperton Metro
       Township to show support for and encourage approval of the use, regulation, and study of
       cannabis for medical uses for the purpose of ensuring longer and better quality of life for the
       residents of Copperton Metro Township and other municipalities of Salt Lake County and the
       State of Utah.
