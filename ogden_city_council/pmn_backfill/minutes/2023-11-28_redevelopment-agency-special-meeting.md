         A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department came before the Board to consider a resolution
authorizing a Land Transfer and Development Agreement (LTDA) with Parkway Group, LLC. The memo stated a multi-family
residential and commercial development located at approximately 18th Street and Washington Boulevard is being developed which
would contain up to 115 units in two five-story residential buildings and a small commercial building where the Pioneer Heritage
building (Piccadilly’s building) is located. The overall project approvals include a rezone to C3/CO, a street vacation of River
Drive east of Washington Boulevard, both of which were approved by the Council on September 26, 2023, property sale and/or
transfer from the City, Redevelopment Agency (RDA), and the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT), and an incentive
agreement for public improvements from the RDA. The Council has approved the rezone and street vacation and has held the
public hearing for the sale of several properties at or near 1810 Washington Boulevard as significant parcels. The Administration
is in the process of finalizing the sale of property at 1810 Washington Boulevard to the petitioner (no further Council action
required for the sale). As part of the overall development, the next step is for the City to approve the transfer of property from
Ogden City ownership to Redevelopment Agency ownership. Once that transfer is completed, the property is then proposed to be
conveyed to the petitioner in a separate RDA action and a participation and incentive agreement is proposed to be approved for the
development of the project in another separate RDA action. This action is the proposed transfer of parcels from the
Redevelopment Agency to Parkway Group, LLC. The three properties involved in this transfer are the same three that are
proposed to be transferred from Ogden City to the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency in a separate action. The 1810 Washington
Boulevard property and the UDOT properties are being addressed with separate actions. The 1810 Washington Boulevard
property is being sold to the Parkway Group for $250,000. The UDOT properties are anticipated to be acquired by the RDA and
transferred in a separate action once those acquisitions are completed.
          An additional memo from the Community and Economic Development Department came before the Board to consider a
resolution approving a Participation and Incentive Agreement with Parkway Group, LLC. The memo stated a multi-family
residential and commercial development located at approximately 18th Street and Washington Boulevard is being developed which
would contain up to 115 units in two five-story residential buildings and a small commercial building where the Pioneer Heritage
building (Piccadilly’s building) is located. The overall project approvals include a rezone to C3/CO, a street vacation of River
Drive east of Washington Boulevard, both of which were approved by the Council on September 26, 2023, property sale and/or
transfer from the City, RDA, and UDOT, and incentive agreement for public improvements from the RDA. The Council has
approved the rezone and street vacation and has held the public hearing for the sale of several properties at or near 1810
Washington Boulevard as significant parcels. The Administration is in the process of finalizing the sale of property at 1810
Washington Boulevard to the petitioner (no further Council action required for the sale). As part of the overall development, the
next step is for the City to approve the transfer of property from Ogden City ownership to Redevelopment Agency ownership.
Once that transfer is completed, the property is then proposed to be conveyed to the petitioner in a separate RDA action and a
participation and incentive agreement is proposed to be approved for the development of the project in another separate RDA
action. This action is the proposed Participation and Incentive Agreement with Parkway Group, LLC. The terms of the agreement
include payment of $370,000 of tax increment incentive for the Parkway Plaza development paid from the Ogden River
Redevelopment Area (2002). The payment will be made over a three-year period beginning in 2027.
          Community and Economic Development Director Cooper summarized the memo and presented conceptual plans for the
proposed development of the subject property and the other parcels owned by the developer. Development of the property will
improve the property along the Ogden River and the area that is publicly accessible as part of the Ogden Parkway Trail system. He
also presented an estimate of the hard and soft costs to be expended to improve the public amenities in the area, which total over
$1 million; the $370,000 incentive will help to offset the developer’s investment related to public improvements in the area.
