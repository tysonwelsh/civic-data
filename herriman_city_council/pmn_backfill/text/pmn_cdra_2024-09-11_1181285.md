    Community Development and Renewal Agency of
               Herriman City Minutes

                           Wednesday, September 11, 2024
                            Approved October 9, 2024

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency meeting.
The meeting was held on Wednesday, September 11, 2024, at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City
Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this
meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to
members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Lorin Palmer

Directors Present: Jared Henderson, Sherrie Ohrn, and Steve Shields

Directors Excused: Teddy Hodges

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas,
City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, Communications Manager
Jonathan LaFollette, City Planner Michael Maloy, Public Works Director Justun Edwards,
Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, Public Utilities
Engineering Manager Jonathan Bowers.

     3. Discussion and Action Items
         3.1. Consideration of an Amendment to the Interlocal Cooperative Agreement with
              Salt Lake County for the Herriman Business Center Community Development
              Project Area – Blake Thomas, Community Development Director
     Community Development Director Blake Thomas offered a background of the Herriman
     Business Center Community Development Area that was established in 2016 to promote
     mixed-use development and generate revenue for infrastructure projects through tax
     increment funding. He noted the original agreement allowed for 27 acres of residential
     development and a 20-year tax increment collection period, which had been amended several
     times. The most recent amendment conducted in March 2022 increased the residential area
     limit to 32 acres with added density restrictions.

     Director Thomas indicated the current proposed amendment would seek to further expand
     the residential development area to 78.5 acres and add 708 new housing units. The
     amendment aligned with the proposed Panorama development plan and was expected to
     accelerate development in the area. Director Thomas recommended approval of the
     amendment and, if approved, would work to update the project area plan and budget to
     reflect the changes. The next step in the process would be to get the Interlocal agreement
     approved with Salt Lake County.

     Director Jared Henderson moved to approve Resolution R2024-04 approving an amendment
     to the Interlocal Agreement with Salt Lake County for the Herriman Business Center
     Community Development Project Area. Director Ohrn seconded the motion.

     The vote is recorded as follows:

     Director Sherrie Ohrn                  Aye
     Director Teddy Hodges                  Absent
     Chair Lorin Palmer                     Aye
