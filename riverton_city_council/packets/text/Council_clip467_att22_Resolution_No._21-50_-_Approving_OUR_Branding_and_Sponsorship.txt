                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-50

                 A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY
       COUNCIL APPROVING THE ACCEPTANCE OF BRANDING FOR THE
      OPERATION UNDERGROUND RAILROAD ON THE RIVERTON POLICE
                     DEPARTMENT COMMAND POST


      WHEREAS, The Riverton City Police Department (the “RPD”) has obtained a vehicle
from which it desires to operate a Police Mobile Lab/Command Post (“Command Post”); and

       WHEREAS, one modification, among others, required to improve the vehicle into a
Command Post is the need to outfit the exterior of the vehicle with appropriate markings that
enable it to be identified as a law enforcement vehicle; and

        WHEREAS, the Operation Underground Railroad (“OUR”) is a non-profit organization
dedicated to the protection of children from exploitation, in part by collaborating with law
enforcement agencies and providing them with tools, training, and technology to enhance the
abilities of law enforcement agencies who combat child exploitation; and

        WHEREAS, OUR has proposed to assist Riverton City with the cost to improve the
exterior of the Command Post in exchange for the City’s willingness to allow for OUR to
include OUR branding to be placed on the Command Post, and to coordinate with OUR in
media/press releases whenever RPD determines to make announcements about cases involving
child exploitation; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that OUR’s mission
statement is consistent with key objectives of RPD in protecting the health, safety and welfare of
the public, and would welcome appropriate OUR-related branding to be fixed on the Command
Post; and

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       1. The City Council approves the placement of OUR-related branding on a City asset,
          known as the Command Post. The Riverton City Mayor is authorized and directed to
          negotiate and execute agreement with OUR which permits OUR to place its branding
          on the Command Post at the Mayor’s discretion, in exchange for OUR’s donation of
          not less than $10,504.40 to Riverton City, for use in renovating the Command Post.
          Said Agreement must be approved as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney
          prior to execution.
      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of
