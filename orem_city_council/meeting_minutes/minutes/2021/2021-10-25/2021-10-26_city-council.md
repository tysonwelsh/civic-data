       PRESENTATION – Central Utah Water Conservancy District – Alpine Aqueduct Project

Ms. Sutherland introduced those presenting to the council. Mr. Whimpey gave background on
the Central Utah Water Conservancy District and showed the geographic area they service,
including the water facilities and pipelines near Orem City.

Mr. Brunst asked Mr. Whimpey the diameter of the Alpine and Salt Lake Aqueduct. Mr.
Whimpey stated the Alpine pipeline varies between 90 and102 inches and the Salt Lake pipeline
is 60 inches in diameter.

General discussion ensued regarding pipeline construction, lifespan, past issues, and possible
problems such as fault lines and earthquakes.

Mr. Whimpey showed the council a map containing fault line data and expressed how various
pipelines could be affected if a seismic event happened. He stated because of this, the

       UPDATE – American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) Funding

Mr. Downs gave the council general background on ARPA and stated the City of Orem received
just over 16 million dollars in ARPA funding. Mr. Downs then went over expenses the city has
used this funding on so far, as well as currently planned expenses.

General discussion ensued regarding planned expenses, mainly relating to how much of the
Hillcrest Park project this funding could cover.

Mayor Brunst spoke about different options of how the 2.5 million dollars of funding set aside
for “Emergency Operations Center for City Hall” could be used.

Mr. Downs concluded by stating there would be $992,778 left of the ARPA funding after these
planned projects, and went over possible areas where this could be used.

General discussion ensued regarding this unallocated funding.

then went over differences between citizen input and staff input on Park access.

General discussion ensued regarding park access, lighting, parking, and park fencing. The

       DISCUSSION – Code Amendment for Massage Establishments

Mr. Earl presented the background on the issues that caused this code amendment to be
considered. He stated one of the main effects of this amendment would be a new state licensing
requirement for massage companies and workers. Mr. Earl stated this amendment would make it
easier for law enforcement to identify and deal with “bad actor establishments.”

Mayor Brunst asked if this could help invite and protect proper massage parlors within the city.
Mr. Earl responded this was one of the main goals for this code amendment.

Mr. Peterson asked if the city could limit the number of massage establishments within the city.
Mr. Earl stated the council could do this as they have done to inherently negative establishments,
but he believes this wouldn’t be beneficial because massage parlors are not inherently
undesirable, and it could limit legitimate businesses from coming into the city.

       PUBLIC HEARING / ORDINANCE – Amending portions of Section 22-11-56 PD-43
       (Auburn Meadows) 2000 South Geneva pertaining to the approved uses in the PD43 zone
       for Area B. Also amending Appendix LL of the Orem City Code with updated concept
       plans and elevations pertaining to Area B.

different section of the property and constructing a grocery store on the property.

Mr. Macdonald asked if residents would be able to park in the grocery store parking lot. Mr.
Mensell stated it could be worked out, but he hopes it would not be necessary.

the proposal.

as parking costs and permit programs in the area.

compared to now. Mr. Mensell stated this project would have more parking and also cut down on
parking issues. He also stated there are programs in place to help deal with people who cause
