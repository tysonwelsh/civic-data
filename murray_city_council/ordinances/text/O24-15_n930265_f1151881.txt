ORDINANCE NO. 24-15

AN ORDINANCE RELATING TO LAND USE; AMENDS THE ZONING
MAP FROM R-1-8 (LOW DENSITY SINGLE FAMILY) TO R-2-10
(MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL) FOR THE PROPERTY LOCATED
AT 1151 EAST 6600 SOUTH, MURRAY CITY

BE IT ENACTED BY THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

WHEREAS, the owner of the real property located at 1151 East 6600 South,
Murray, Utah, has requested a proposed amendment to the Zoning Map to designate
the property in an R-2-10 (Medium Density Residential!) zone district; and

WHEREAS, it appearing that said matter has been given full and complete
consideration by the City Planning and Zoning Commission; and

WHEREAS, it appearing to be in the best interest of the City and the inhabitants
thereof that the proposed amendment of the Zoning Map be approved.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ENACTED:

Section 1. That the Zoning Map and the zone district designation for the
described property located at 1151 East 6600 South, Murray, Utah be amended from
the R-1-8 (Low Density Single Family) zone district to the R-2-10 (Medium Density
Residential) zone district:
