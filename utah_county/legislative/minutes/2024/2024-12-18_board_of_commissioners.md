1. RECOGNITION OF UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEE OF THE YEAR 2024

-P] Maughan, Human Resources

COMMISSIONER GORDON ANNOUNCED THAT CRYSTAL THORNE, FROM THE
CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER, WAS SELECTED AS THE UTAH COUNTY EMPLOYEE
OF THE YEAR 2024. HE READ THE FOLLOWING NOMINATION.

BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF UTAH COUNTY, UTAH
Public Meeting Notice

December 18, 2024

2|Page

Crystal Thorne of the Utah County Criminal Justice Center is our Employee of the
Year for 2024. Crystal stands out for her compassion, exceptional communication
and collaboration skills, ability to work with team members from different
agencies, and overall contribution to the well-being of child victims and families
in our community.

Crystal's remarkable communication skills are especially crucial as she works
with child victims. Her balance of empathy and understanding while ensuring
appropriate boundaries has profoundly impacted the children we serve. Crystal
has an unwavering commitment to providing support that is sensitive to each
child's unique needs and creating a safe and trusting environment. She can
perform exceptionally at this challenging work while maintaining her composure
and providing a calming influence on the job.

Crystal is an invaluable mentor to their multidisciplinary team members. Her
willingness to share knowledge, provide guidance, and offer constructive
feedback has dramatically enhanced the professional development of her
colleagues. Crystal's mentorship style encourages a collaborative and learning-
oriented atmosphere, fostering growth and cohesion within the team.

Crystal exemplifies the Criminal Justice Center's mission and goals.

Congratulations, and thank you for your hard work, Crystal!
