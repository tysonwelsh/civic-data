CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3350, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS RECOMMENDATIONS

Public Works Director Todd Edwards stated the Council of Governments (COG) serves as a
recommending body to Washington County and oversees the expenditures of local transportation
preservation funds. There are four (4) projects being recommended for funding which are:

e The de-obligation of funds totaling $626,330.73 back to the Preservation Fund for funds
that were not spent when the property was acquired, or for studies that were not completed.

e The de-obligation of funds totaling $121,423.86 back to the Construction Grant Fund for
funds that were not spent when the property was acquired, or for the studies that were not
completed.

e The approval of a transfer of $1,000,000 from the Revolving Loan Fund to the Corridor
Preservation Fund because excess funding has been accumulating in the Revolving Loan
Fund that could be better utilized for roadway acquisition.

e The approval of revisions to the COG Policies and their adoption, and authorizing the
updated Policies with the changes shown in the attached draft, including holding a

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3351, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LEASE OF OFFICE SPACE TO THE STATE OF UTAH FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF
HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw stated there are approximately 3,000 square feet of
available space in the Old Administration building at 197 East Tabernacle, and the Department of
Health of Human Services has reached out requesting to lease the space. This lease would be
approved subject to legal review by the county attorney’s office..

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3351, A
Resolution Approving a Lease of Office Space to the State of Utah for the
Department of Health and Human Services Subject to Attorney Review and
Allowing the Chair Pro-Tem to Sign in the Absence of the Chair. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3352, A RESOLUTION ANNEXING
THE PROPERTY WITHIN THE MUNICIPAL LIMITS OF THE TOWN OF VIRGIN
INTO ASH CREEK SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated Washington County received a joint resolution with
the Town of Virgin and Ash Creek Special Service District requesting that Virgin be annexed into
Ash Creek Special Service District. On April 16, 2024, the Washington County Commission
adopted a Resolution proposing the annexation, and a public hearing was held on the annexation
on June 4, 2024.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3352, A
Resolution Annexing the Property Within the Municipal Limits of the Town
of Virgin into Ash Creek Special Service District. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.
