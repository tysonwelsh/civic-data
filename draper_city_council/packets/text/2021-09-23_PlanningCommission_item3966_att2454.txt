SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a plat amendment application for
approximately 0.93 acres located on the south side of LeAmbra Lane (Exhibit B). The
property is currently zoned A5 and subject to the standards found in the South Mountain
Agreement. In addition, a Development Agreement was approved for the subdivision in
2012 and that agreement specifies that all lots shall conform to the standards of the RA2
(Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) zone. For this application, the
applicant is requesting to relocate the common lot line between Lots 2 and 3.

ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Low/Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This
category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the A5 zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020(A)(1) “the purpose of the A5 zone is to
maintain the status of large tracts of agricultural land by allowing most commercial
agricultural uses. Typical uses include farming, dairy, and cattle production.” However, per
the Development Agreement approved in 2012, these properties are subject the RA2
zoning district. DCMC Section 9-8-020(B)(1) states ”the purpose of the RA1 and RA2 zones
is to foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal
services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to promote

and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of limited
numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be
detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and
industrial uses.” The subject properties are surrounded by A5 zoning.

Subdivision Layout. The properties are part of the Cinnamon Ridge at Draper which was
recorded in July, 2013 (see Exhibit E). This request will adjust the common lot line between
the two lots. Currently there are two line segments between the two properties. The one
at the south end of the properties will remain at 89.35 feet. The segment between the
northern portions of the properties will be adjusted from its current 125.16 feet to 108.43
feet. With this adjustment, Lot 2 will be reduced by approximately 319 square feet and all
approximately 319 square feet will be added to Lot 3. The before and after lot sizes are as
follows:

                  Lot             Existing             Proposed              Difference
                   2               20,545              20,226.38             318.62 less
                   3               20,000              20,318.52            318.52 more

Both of the lots will comply with the minimum standards of the RA2 zoning district and will
not result in the creation of any non-conformities.

Plat Amendment.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for
such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by
        signing an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use
        Authority finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no
        public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
         Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
         and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.       Once final plat has been approved, a Land Disturbance Permit is required prior to
         construction activities onsite. Permit is obtained through the Engineering Division.
3.       Any work in a public right-of-way will require an Encroachment Permit obtained
         through the Engineering Division.
4.       If the plat amendment is not recorded within one year from the date of approval or
         construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced,
         such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be extended by the
         Planning Commission for up to one additional six month period for good cause
         shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the
         expiration of the original one year.

The findings for approval as are follows:

1.       There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
         way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
