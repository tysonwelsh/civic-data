DAYTIME IRRIGATION RESTRICTIONS:
     Consider approval of daytime irrigation restrictions for those that irrigate
     with culinary water.

      Water and Energy Conservationist Rene Fleming explained generally, this request is
      made annually in May. However, given the drought conditions the state is in, the
      levels of the local reservoirs, the Water Service Department is requesting daytime
      water restrictions for those that irrigate with culinary water. Staff is requesting that
      irrigation using culinary water be done between the hours of 8:00 pm - 8:00 am. It
      is recommended that customers complete watering before 4:00 am - 6:00 am as
      this is the time of day the system sees the highest demand. She noted that City and
      School District facilities irrigate with irrigation quality water; they will continue to
      water as the resources are available.

      Mayor Randall reiterated if the sprinklers at those facilities are on during the day, it
      is because irrigation quality water is being used. She encouraged residents to kindly
      let their neighbors know about the restrictions when they are watering during the
      day.

      Ms. Fleming added that parks and golf course staff have done a lot to improve their
      irrigation efficiency. She noted that residents use four times the amount of water
      outdoors as they do indoors. She then mentioned the Water District offers several
      rebate programs and provides suggestions on water conservation.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember McArthur to approve the
                    daytime irrigation restrictions for those that irrigate with culinary
                    water.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Curtis.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

      former Burt Burgess property.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman presented the Petition for Annexation for
      approximately 174 acres located west of Dixie Drive on the former Burt Burgess
      property. This property is west of the recent annexations at approximately 1600
      South Dixie Drive. From the beginning, staff has insisted on the condition that the
      right of way for Gap Canyon Drive be dedicated; some of the roadway is on this
St. George City Council Minutes

      property. This item is the beginning of the annexation process. One of the
      conditions of approval that the full width of Gap Canyon Drive be dedicated to the
      City as well as a power easement. Staff has met with Matt Ence who represents the
      applicants. He recommended approval on the condition pertaining to the roadway
      and utility easement.
