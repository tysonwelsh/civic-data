OPENING:
     Mayor Pike called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance.

DISCUSSION REGARDING PROPOSED REVISIONS TO THE CITY’S BANNER POLICY:
     City Attorney Shawn Guzman noted staff has been looking at and wanting to update
     the streetlight banner policy. The Council asked staff to draft a policy; which he
     provided to the Mayor and Council. He outlined the proposed changes as follows:
     Definitions: City Event, City Sponsored Event, Community Message, Other
     Government Entity Event, and Sponsored; Banner Display Criteria: Eligibility
     participants, and Priority for Banner shall be in the following order; Banner
     Application and Design; Banner Installation, Maintenance and Removal; Time
     Displayed; Liability; and Denial. Because this is a policy, the City Manager will
     approve the it.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted there may be a change to calendar basis rather
      than fiscal year.

      Mayor Pike asked the Council to provide feedback in the next week or so.

      Councilmember Randall commented that the Doctors Volunteer Clinic and the St.
      George Musical Theater will not hold galas this year.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard stated staff has an end of July deadline for a decision on
      the marathon.

      Mayor Pike noted that Ironman and Senior Games have not decided yet.

                session to discuss property.
