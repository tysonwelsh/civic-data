Washington County Planning Commission Meeting
                            July 09, 2019
The Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting was held on Tuesday, July 09, 2019 in the
Council Chambers of the Washington County Administration Building at 197 E. Tabernacle, St.
George, UT. Chairman Slade Hughes opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of
Allegiance and then explained meeting protocol.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Shayne Durrant, Kevin Jones, Mark Owens, Reed
Scow (Alternate), Doug Solstad, and Jon Walter.

Excused: Dan Briggs (Alternate).

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Natalie Nelson, Deputy County
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Todd
Edwards, County Engineer; Kim Hafen, County Clerk.


1.      CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT SITE PLAN REVIEW. Review of a conditional use
permit request for a staging area on Parcel 2004-E-N-NS in association with the UDOT I-15
Extension Project. The property is located at approximately 70 South 3900 East in New Harmony.
The parcel is 1.65 acres in size and zone RE-40 (Residential Estate 40,000 sq. ft. minimum lot
size). The applicant is Harward & Rees Construction .

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation with all documents related to the proposal. He
explained that the applicant, Harward & Rees Construction, has submitted application for
conditional use approval for a temporary staging area in association with UDOT’s I-15 project. The
project includes adding a climbing lane from Exit 36 at Black Ridge to the rest area at mile post 44.
The parcel is owned by Clair Hall; who has negotiated some improvements to the property in
exchange for the temporary use of the property. They will be cleaning up the parcel and removing
items that are a nuisance and installing the storm drain pipe to help with drainage of the property.
The subject parcel is adjacent to I-15 and the Highway 144 interchange. The duration of the project
is approximately 1 year (Please see Attachment 3 for more in-depth description). The applicants are
proposing to have temporary RV’s on the property for the workers to live in while they are working
on the project. They are working with Southwest Public Health Department on the septic. They are
asking to have permanent power run to the site. Although having power on site would alleviate the
need for generators, it is not the practice of the County to allow permanent power without a building
permit for a permanent dwelling or structure. It is staff’s recommendation that only temporary power
to the property be allowed, in association with, and for the duration of the I-15 project.

Recommendation:

The Planning Commission may approve the conditional use permit for the temporary staging area
with RV’s based on the following findings and conditions:

 1. The staging area is necessary for the construction of the I-15 improvements.
 2. That temporary power is allowed, but must be removed once the I-15 project is completed.

 3. The storage and staging of equipment, vehicles and materials are not permitted on the
    property past the completion of the I-15 project.
 4. No RV’s, campers, or other temporary living quarters is permitted on this property that is not
    in association with this project or past the completion of this project, without receiving the
    proper county approval.
 5. All such temporary living quarters must be removed from the property no later than 30 days
    following the completion of the project.
 6. That the temporary waste facility/septic holding tank must be approved by the Southwest
    Utah Public Health Department.
 7. Notice of this conditional use application was properly noticed; which includes posting the
    notice on the subject parcel.
 8. That the applicant repair any damage made to the County roads.
 9. That measures be taken to limit the amount of dirt and mud tracked from the site onto adjacent
    roads.

Commissioner Anderson asked what assurances the county had once the project is completed and
that the RV’s and power will be removed, the road is not damaged, and the property is cleaned up.

Todd Edwards, County Engineer informed the commission that Randy Cronk, the Public Works
Inspector, will go up there periodically and check on things. Once the project is completed, he will
inspect that all items are removed and the road meets the requirements. This project is a joint
project with UDOT. The county owns the road that goes underneath the freeway and due to the
construction of the overpass, the road will need to be redone. UDOT and the County will work
together on getting the road completed.

Commissioner Solstad confirmed the location of the property. A short discussion in regards to the
property north of the subject property occurred.

Commissioner Scow asked if consideration had been given to the heavy equipment storage with
hydraulic fluid and oil leakage that may occur.

Val Rees, representative, addressed the commission. He stated no large equipment will be stored
on the site. There will be drainage pipe materials, the fuel trailer, some construction products, and
a water truck. The site is mostly for the workers RV’s for them to stay in while the project is
ongoing. The heavy equipment will be on the road construction site. It is a 7 mile stretch of road
that is being built.

Commissioner Walter stated his concern was for the surrounding homeowners. He asked if there
was something to mitigate any impact to the homeowners. Mr. Messel answered that it was
discussed and the major issue was the noise, which is one reason why the power to the property
was requested. That would help eliminate noise from the generators. The dust will need to be
controlled which, the applicant has agreed to do. For fencing or screening property it is up to the
planning commission if that should be a condition of approval. This is a temporary use and if it
was a permanent facility then fencing would make sense.

Commissioner Solstad commented that we’re establishing a temporary residence for workers to
be driven out to the job site and then go back at the end of the day. They are not storing equipment

Mr. Messel stated it would be good to have good neighbor policies in place and not have wild
parties and noise at all hours. He would hope that the workers would keep things under control. If
there were complaints, then that is something we could bring back to the planning commission to
review if the use needs to be revoked.

Commissioner Jones asked if there would be any construction after working hours. Mr. Rees
answered there is night work on the Freeway but it did not involve them. If they do work at night
it would not be on this site. We work from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.m. which is when they would go
back to the proposed site. There would not be a lot of activity on this site. The workers who would
be there are good respectful people.

Mr. Messel stated there had been comments submitted from the neighbors who believed this was
going to be an RV park. We let them know that it was not a permanent use and it was only
temporary while the work on the freeway was being done. Some voiced opposition to the project.

Commissioner Solstad commented that they were all ok with the project but wanted to ensure that
there would not be an impact to the neighbors.
