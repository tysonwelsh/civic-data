WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
MARCH 19, 2019

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Cox at 2:00 p.m. on March 19, 2019, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commission Gil
Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, HCP Director Cameron Rognan and
Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance: DNR Representatives Melinda Bennion and Rhett Boswell
REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION AND PERIODIC REVIEW __OF_ _COUNTY__ RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included Predator Control; Threatened and Endangered
Species; and Wildlife. Links will be added throughout each of the sections which will direct
interested persons to specific websites for the most current information.

Suggestions were made from the DNR Representatives to update the charts in the
Threatened and Endangered species section to remove species not found within
Washington County.

The RMP will be updated based on the suggestions from those in attendance and will be
brought before the Commission for final adoption.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
3:30 p.m.

CHE E BENTLEY EXN COX

DEPUTY CLERK CHAIRMAN
