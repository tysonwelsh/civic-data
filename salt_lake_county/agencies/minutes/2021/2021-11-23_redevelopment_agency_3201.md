4.2 Discussion of 2022 RDA Budget 21-1353
Attachments: RDA Budget 2022
Mr. Jevon Gibb , Economic Development Division, stated the initial 
estimated budget this summer was for $ 1.36 million. Since then, many 
investment opportunities have been brought to the RDA. Rather than coming 
back for a budget adjustment in January, he is requesting the full $ 1.83 
million as part of the approval. The biggest difference between the 2021 and 
the 2022 budget is substantially increasing activity on Magna Main Street, 
which will include revitalizing the area.
