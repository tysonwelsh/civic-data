28   2. Presentations and Reports
29   2.1 Discussion of HTRZ funding with Thanksgiving Point Development
30   This item has been postponed and will be heard at a future meeting.
31
32   2.2 Discussion with Waste Management on the renewal of their option for trash removal.
33   Dean Lundell, Finance Director, explained that the City signed a renewal option with Waste
34   Management that expires at the end of June. The recycling market performed well for the city, but
35   costs have now gone up about 14%. They negotiated a 9.9% rate increase for the next 3 1/2 years.
36   Mr. Lundell isn’t proposing any rate increases to residents right now. He believes the city can
37   absorb the cost due to some reserve funds, but they may need to increase resident’s rates next year.
38   Blake Leonelli with Waste Management was available to answer questions. He stated that the costs
39   for steal, plastic, vehicles, labor, and fuel have all increased.
40
41   2.3 Review of the Lehi City Facilities Master Plan
42   Jason Walker, City Administrator, gave an update on the long-term city facilities master plan. He
43   said that most of the square footage for the new City Hall building would be for the library. The
44   new building would house the library and additional city staff along with a new Council Chambers
45   that can also be used as public space.
