Kimball Junction Presentation

Attachment: Kimball Junction Presentation.pdf
Council Member Armstrong introduced the discussion.
Margaret Olson, Attorney, spoke about the limitations on Council discussion about this issue with the public. (3:08 PM)
Carl Miller, Transportation Planning Director, summarized the transportation studies the County has related to the proposed development. (3:13 PM) Council Members asked questions. Director Miller and Brandon Brady, Transportation Planning Director, responded.
Marc Stanworth, Dakota Pacific Real Estate (DPRE) Chief Executive Officer, responded to an issue raised by Council Member Armstrong. Steve Borup, DPRE Director of Commercial Development, supported his response. (3:28 PM)
Robert Stewart, Utah Department Of Transportation (UDOT) Region 2 Director, introduced himself and his staff and explained the status of the Environment Impact Statement for Kimball Junction at UDOT. (3:39 PM)
Rebecka Stromness, UDOT Project Director, introduced Heidi Spore. (3:46 PM)
Heidi Spore, HDR Engineering, is consulting with UDOT on this project and introduced the Consultant’s role. (3:46 PM)
Charles Allen, HDR Engineering, gave more details about the current traffic planning that UDOT is doing and the information it has, including detail on the options under consideration. (4:00 PM)
Council Members asked questions. Heidi Spore, Charles Allen, and Robert Stewart responded. (4:08 PM)
Director Miller responded to some of Council’s questions. (4:52 PM)
CEO Stanworth made additional remarks. (4:59 PM)
Council Commented (5:02 PM)
