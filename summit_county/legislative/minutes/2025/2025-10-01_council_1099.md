Ice breaker activity (9:14 AM)
Dan Compton, Library Director, announced that the Summit County Library has received the Quality Library Award. He also led the icebreaker activities, which included the "Bring Your Own Book" (BYOB) icebreaker and a game of Giant Jenga. (9:14 AM)
3.
Discussion regarding housing and affordable housing targets; Jeff Jones (9:59 AM)
Attachment: Housing Retreat Presentation Revised 10-1-25
Economic Development and Housing Director Jeff Jones led a discussion about the targets for affordable housing in the County. Council members provided their comments and asked questions. In response, Director Jones, along with Madlyn McDonough, Economic Development and Housing Manager, and Peter Barnes, Community Development Director, responded. (10:00 AM)
Council member Roger Armstrong joined the meeting. (10:53 AM)

Elected Officials open discussion and working lunch; All (11:23 AM)
Elected officials--Assessor Stephanie Poll, Auditor Cindy Keyes, Clerk Eve Furse, Recorder Gregory Wolbach, Interim Sheriff Kacey Bates, Treasurer Corrie Forsling--Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Community Development Director Peter Barnes, and Economic Development and Housing Manager Madlyn McDonough discussed various topics with the Council, including support for teams and employees, needed building improvements, concerns about employee retention, employee childcare subsidies, and affordable housing issues. (11:24 AM)

(12:15 PM)

Public Works report; John Angell (1:57 PM)
Attachment: Solid Waste Update and 2026 Plan - Council Retreat 10.1.25
Attachment: September 2025 Report_PCCF_ZeroFoodWastePartnership
Public Works Director John Angell and Solid Waste Superintendent Tim Loveday provided updates on the construction of Landfill Cell 2, the Chipping Program, recycling efforts, and diversion strategies for 2026. Deputy Manager Janna Young and Sustainability Director Emily Quinton contributed additional information about these programs. Council members offered comments and asked questions, to which the presenters responded. (1:58 PM)
