                     FIRST AMENDMENT TO AGREEMENT
                BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY

                                        Water Service
                                    Riverbend Golf Course

        This First Amendment to Water Service Agreement (“Amendment”) is entered into
between Riverton City, a municipal corporation of the state of Utah, hereinafter referred to as
CITY, and SALT LAKE COUNTY, a body corporate and politic of the state of Utah, hereinafter
referred to as COUNTY.


                                          RECITALS


       A.     COUNTY and CITY entered into that certain Water Service Agreement
              (“Agreement”) on the date of August 2, 2022.

       B.     The Agreement secured culinary water utility service from CITY, for a golf
              course owned by COUNTY and within the territorial jurisdiction of the CITY,
              known as the Riverbend Golf Course.

       C.     EXHIBIT A to the Agreement set rates for culinary water utility service,
              beginning in the year 2022 and running to the end of the year 2041.

       D.     The rates provided by CITY under the Agreement were dependent upon operation
              of a culinary water well, which required improvements funded by COUNTY
              pursuant to other agreements.

       E.     Due to unforeseen circumstances the culinary water well experienced delays in
              improving the same before it could be operated, which resulted in a delay in
              furnishing culinary water service to the COUNTY at rates established under the
              Agreement.

       F.     COUNTY and CITY have accordingly renegotiated the rates for culinary water
              service as described in EXHIBIT A-1 to this Amendment.

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the covenants, representations, releases, and
obligations contained herein, the sufficiency of which is expressly acknowledged by City and
County, the Parties agree as follows:
