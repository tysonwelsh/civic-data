Business Meeting

1.0    Call to Order: Board Chair Troy K. Walker

       Board Chair Troy K. Walker called the meeting to order at 8:31 p.m.

2.0    Motion: Approval of November 19, 2019 Redevelopment Agency of Draper City
       Meeting Minutes

2.1    Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey moved to approve the November 19, 2019
       Redevelopment Agency Meeting Minutes. Boardmember Tasha Lowery seconded
       the motion.

2.2    A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
       Vawdrey, voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0    Public Hearing: Approval of Resolution RDA #20-01, amending the RDA Fiscal Year
       2020 Budget
3.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, said that there were two items on the RDA budget tonight.
      The first item was the 14000 South Bangerter signal (RDA FY20-02). He said that the
      project with cost $250,000 and would be funded by the B&C road money. After review of
      the project, it was determined that the project is in the East Bangerter project are of the
      Draper City Redevelopment Agency. Therefore, it was requested that funding for the
      project come from the East Bangerter Project Area. If the request is granted the original
      funding would be transferred back to the B&C fund.

      The second item was the Bangerter Parkway resurfacing project (RDA FY20-03). Staff
      requested funding to resurface the roadway from 13800 South up Bangerter Parkway to the
      southern border of the East Bangerter Project Area. The project was designed to increase
      the roadway’s capacity by resurfacing and then re-striping the road to have four lanes as
      opposed to two. Funding for this project in the amount of $170,000 would come from the
      RDA Fund.

3.2   Board Chair Troy K. Walker opened the public hearing. No one came forward so
      Board Chair Troy K. Walker closed the public hearing.

3.3   Boardmember Fred Lowry moved to approve Resolution RDA # 20-01, amending the
      RDA Fiscal Year 2020 Budget. Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey seconded the motion.

3.4   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
      Vawdrey, voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

4.0   Adjourn to a City Council Meeting

4.1   Boardmember Mike Green moved to adjourn to a City Council meeting.
      Boardmember Tasha Lowery seconded the motion.

4.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts, and
      Vawdrey, voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

4.3   The meeting adjourned at 8:36 p.m.
