Please be advised that on December 18, 2025, the City Council of the City of St
George approved the following ordinance(s):


Ordinance No. 2025-109 to add the name 'Tech Ridge Parkway' to the existing numbered
street '250 West Street' from 1190 South to 1300 South.

City Standard and Specifications to address an overall update to Section 4.

Ordinance No. 2025-111 approving a development agreement amendment for Kachina Cliffs
Phase 1 Lot 24 and Kachina Cliffs Phase 2 Lot 38.

Ordinance No. 2025-112 amending the City's General Plan to adopt an Annexation Policy
Plan as an Element of the General Plan.
