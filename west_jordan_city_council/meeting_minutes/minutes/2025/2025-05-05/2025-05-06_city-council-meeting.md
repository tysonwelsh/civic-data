 2. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Lamb opened the public comment period at 6:03 pm.
     Comments:
     Kristy Spencer contacted the City Council and Police Department on numerous occasions
     regarding parking issues. She said a series of dump trucks had been stored in a neighbor’s
     backyard for more than two years. Ms. Spencer said parking issues were a problem in many
     neighborhoods, and it seemed there had been no follow-through from Code Enforcement. She
     asked how the community could help, and emphasized that some of the parking that occurred
     in her neighborhood would block emergency response vehicle access, posing a safety hazard.
     Rebecca Thomas, West Jordan resident, said she had lived in the same home for 27 years, with
     a backyard against Old Bingham Highway. She listed businesses on Old Bingham Highway, and
     said semi-trucks had been parking and running engines right behind the homes on her street
     all day and sometimes overnight. Ms. Thomas said it was like living at a truck stop. She said her
     family typically enjoyed spending time in the backyard. She mentioned there were “no parking”
     signs all along Old Bingham Highway, especially at Boeing and Amazon, and asked for a few “no
     truck parking” signs to be posted along the area of her neighborhood.
     Chair Lamb closed public comments at 6:06 pm.
