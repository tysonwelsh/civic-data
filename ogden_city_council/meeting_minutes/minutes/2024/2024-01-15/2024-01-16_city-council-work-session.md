         The purpose of the work session was to review the agenda for the City Council meeting scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.;
receive a presentation by students from Harvard’s Kennedy School of Government regarding the Mayoral transition; discuss
Council business; and hear Council comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair Choberka and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Presentation by Students from Harvard’s Kennedy School of Government –
Mayoral Transition Report
          Cindy Weloth, Executive Assistant to Mayor Nadolski, noted that the Mayor’s office has partnered with graduate
students from the Transition Term program of Harvard Kennedy School’s Taubman Center for State and Local Government to
prepare a report for the upcoming State of the City Address. Ms. Weloth introduced Caren Yap, Hadar Sachs, and Christian Hess
and discussed their academic and personal backgrounds; the group then used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to discuss their
goals and focus; data collected to inform their project; the physical, social, cultural, and economic environment of Ogden; and key
takeaways. For a copy of the PowerPoint presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Discussion among the Council and the students centered on the sources of the data that was relied upon to form the key
takeaways; and regional housing challenges and the manner in which actions taken by other communities will impact housing
issues in the Ogden community.
          Mayor Nadolski thanked the Council for their support of this project; he also thanked Ms. Weloth for her efforts to
support and work with the group of students in this valuable endeavor. He thanked the students for their study of Ogden and the
surrounding communities and for the information they provided to his office and to the Council.

         The meeting adjourned at 5:06 p.m.
