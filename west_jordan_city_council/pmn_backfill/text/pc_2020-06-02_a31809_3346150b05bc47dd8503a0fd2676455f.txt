2.    B Jackson/Carmco II; 7022 South Airport Road; Review of screening for street-facing
      bay doors; M-1 Zone; CIR Engineering/Scott Thorsen (applicant) [#20923; parcel 21-30-
      126-002]

Kent Page said that the street-facing loading doors can be approved by the Planning Commission, after
a recommendation from the Design Review Committee, upon a finding that the loading area is
adequately screened from public view by landscaping. The screen wall plan shows a wall fronting
Airport Road and 7000 South. The wall is attractive and trees on the north side will look good
depending on how long it takes for them to grow and if they remain healthy.

Larry Gardner felt that the proposal meets the intent of the ordinance. Trees are designed to get larger
and a negative finding shouldn't be made based on the size of the trees.

Clarification was made by Gary Tyler, representing the applicant, that the wall is a 6-foot solid, pre-
manufactured concrete around the entire site. It is sealed and can be easily cleaned of graffiti.

Ammon Allen said the screen wall shows that the landscaping is approximate and there isn’t a grading
plan that would show how much higher in elevation the building is compared to the wall.

Gary Tyler indicated that the drawings were submitted. There is a gradual slope from west to east. By
the time they get to the building they will be about level with Airport Road, maybe a foot above curb
and gutter in order to get positive drainage flow. He said the code requires extensive landscaping and
the owner has gone above that with a cost of over $300,000 on screening and beautifying the view
from the road.

Ammon Allen didn't see any issues with the site plan. There is no residential development across the
street. He asked about the comment from the DRC regarding a sidewalk on 7000 South, and he also
asked if the landscaping is water-wise.

3.    Bingham (Barnes) Condo Plat; 6076 West 9860 South; Preliminary/Final Minor
      Subdivision/Condo Conversion Plat; M-1 Zone; Steven A. Barnes (applicant) [#20691;
      parcel 26-11-327-009]

Matt Quinney clarified that the approval request is for preliminary and final condominium plat.

Steven Barnes, applicant, said this is a 10-year old duplex building that he built with another party and
has been jointly owned. They want to formally divide the ownership with a condominium plat.

Kent Page reviewed the condominium plat process that addresses landscaping, common open space,
maintenance, and easements. This is an existing two-space building they want to formally convert
into individual condominiums. The outside is common area. The proposed plat specifies that parking
needs to be adequate. Staff suggested that more narrative be included as to the current condition of the
property and how the common space will be maintained.

Based on the analysis and findings contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission approve the Preliminary and Final Condominium Conversion Plat for Bingham (Barnes)
Condominium located at 6076 West 9860 South with the conditions listed below:

1.     All lots must comply with the applicable requirements of Title 13, Zoning Regulations and
       Title 14, Subdivision Regulations including, but not limited to lot area, width and frontage
       requirements.
2.     All applicable city departmental requirements must be met prior to recordation of the final plat.
3.     All buildings constructed within the Bingham Condo Plat shall comply with the requirements
       of the zoning ordinance.
4.     More specifically address the requirements of Ordinances 14-4-1 thru 14-4-4.
5.     An approved, unrecorded final subdivision plat shall remain valid for two (2) years. One 6-
       month extension may be granted by the zoning administrator if, upon written request by the
       owner/developer, the zoning administrator finds that the extension will not adversely affect the
       public health, safety or welfare of the city.

Jay Thomas asked if there will be a Condominium Owners Association established to incur the costs of
the mutual sewer and water lines and for upkeep and maintenance of the exterior.
