                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                     Tuesday, June 1st 2023 – 10:30 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Gage Froerer, James “Jim” H. Harvey (excused), and Sharon Bolos
STAFF PRESENT: Lynn D. Taylor, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes; Brian Baron, of the
County Attorney’s Office.

A. WELCOME: Chair Froerer
B. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. EXTENSION OF DECLARATION OF LOCAL EMERGENCY DUE TO IMMINENT FLOODING
      IN WEBER COUNTY.
            Chair Froerer: The reason for the proclamation is to gain access to state and federal funds if needed.
            Due to remaining snowpack and potential flooding, we are extending the proclamation for an additional 30
            days.
            Commissioner Bolos moved to approve the extension of Declaration of Local Emergency due to imminent
            flooding in Weber County.; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos - aye
C. ADJOURN
      Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn at 10:31:42 a.m.; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
      Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos - aye
