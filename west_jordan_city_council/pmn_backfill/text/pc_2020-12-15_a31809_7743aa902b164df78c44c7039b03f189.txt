      MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING (ELECTRONIC) OF THE WEST JORDAN
     PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION HELD REMOTELY ON DECEMBER 15, 2020

PRESENT:      Matt Quinney, Corbin England, Kent Shelton, Jay Thomas, Trish Hatch, Ammon Allen,
              and Pamela Bloom.

Matt Quinney read the statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-207(4).

training.

**********************************************************************************
The regular meeting was called to order at 6:00 p.m.

Matt Quinney read the statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-207(4).

1.     Approve Minutes from December 1, 2020

MOTION:       Corbin England moved to approve the minutes from December 1, 2020. The
              motion was seconded by Pamela Bloom and passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
2.    Text Amendment – Mining Extraction Overlay; Amend the 2009 City Code Section 13-6
      adding Article L Surface Mining Extraction Overlay Zone; City-wide applicability; City
      of West Jordan (applicant) [#22824]

MOTION:       Corbin England moved to continue Item #2 Text Amendment regarding Mining
              Extraction Overlay to January 5, 2021. The motion was seconded by Jay Thomas
              and passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
3.    Text Amendment – PCFB Zone; Amend the 2009 City Code adding 13-5 Article L
      “Planned Community Form Base (PCFB) Zone”; City-wide applicability; City of West
      Jordan (applicant) [#19432]


MOTION:       Corbin England moved to continue Item #3 Text Amendment regarding the PCFB
              Zone to January 5, 2021. The motion was seconded by Pamela Bloom and passed
              7-0 in favor.

MOTION:       Trish Hatch moved to adjourn.

The meeting adjourned at 6:05 p.m.
