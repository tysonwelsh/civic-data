2. Administrative Items

Administrative items are reviewed based on standards outlined in the ordinance. Public comment may be taken
on relevant and credible evidence regarding the application compliance with the ordinance.

2.1 Request for approval of a Special Exception for the rear yard setback for an accessory
structure on an irregularly shaped lot located at 7223 W Ansel Avenue in the R-1-10
Residential Zone
Applicant: Jeffery Cluff (property owner)

Acres: +0.46
File No: P20243-077
Planner ll Howa presented the request for a special exception for the rear yard setback. The applicant
requested to place an accessory structure on an irregularly shaped lot at 7224 W Ansel Avenue. He
identified the location and property background information. The structure would contain 1,336 total
square feet and be 24’-11” high at the tallest point. Notices were sent, no feedback has been received
from nearby property owners. The applicant requested a reduced rear property setback from twelve
to six feet, to allow for vehicular access and maintain valley views. Utility easement locations were
identified. The property elevations would be similar in color and materials to the primary structure,
and a tree lined buffer was recommended along the property line. Staff recommended approval with
the following conditions:
1. Final plans shall comply with City Code 10-34 and all recommended conditions of approval from
City departments.
2. The applicant shall submit an affidavit, which will be recorded with Salt Lake County, stating
that the structure will not be utilized or converted into an accessory dwelling unit (ADU).
3. The accessory structure’s rear elevation (South side) shall be screened from the adjacent
property with a fast-growing tree species suitable for the location.
4. The applicant shall secure public utility easement (PUE) waivers (where necessary) before
submitting a building permit application.
Concerns were raised by the Planning Commission regarding the setback reduction's impact on
neighboring properties and the potential for obstructed views and the impact on neighboring and
future homes.
Applicant Jeff Cluff explained their reasons for the setback reduction, taking into consideration their
neighbors’ views and the need for better vehicular access. Questions were also raised about the
location of the garage doors and the turning radius. The applicant stated the eastern part of the
structure not be used for parking, it would be a patio. Staff suggested that landscaping and
architectural elements might mitigate the impact of the reduced setback.
Commissioners expressed concerns about the setback reduction and the potential impact on
neighboring properties. The applicant was given the opportunity to provide more insight into their
plans and address these concerns. The applicant acknowledged the concerns and explained their
intentions to be considerate neighbors while also optimizing their own property. Planning Director
Maloy reviewed the standards for approval and requested the Commissioners state reasons for denial
or continuation when making a motion.
