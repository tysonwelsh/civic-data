    1. RECOGNITION OF NOVEMBER 2023 EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH
        -PJ Maughan, Human Resources

COMMISSIONER AMERLIA POWER GARDNER ANNOUNCED THE NOVEMBER 2023
EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH – KRIS SWENSEN.
I would like to nominate Kristen Swensen for employee of the month. Kris is my supervisor, and
she is truly my favorite supervisor that I have had in my long working career. She has the
attributes of Mother Earth for people and all creatures. She is like the calm in the midst of all
storms. Kris looks for the best in everyone. I even had an experience once with her and we were
discussing a few difficult issues. Kris told me not to worry and that she has my back. She has
stood by this, and she truly looks after her team. Kris does not have to micromanage people to
get results and she is able to lead with a gentle hand. We thrive as a team under her leadership,
and we would never want to disappoint her. Our team looks more like a work family instead of
just a work team.

~ I remember Kris saying a few months ago that her vision is to help her employees to rise to the
occasion and be the best version of themselves. ~

Sincerely, Lessie Overall

Nominating Kris again for the same reasons as last time. Very supportive, kind, and caring.
She's good at stating her opinion in a respectful way even when it's contrary to others' opinions.
She has great ideas, takes a real interest in others, and without a doubt goes above and beyond
that which is expected of her. Riley Zapata

I nominate Kris Swenson as employee of the month.

Kris is a dedicated employee in the elections division. She has significant health issues but comes
and works even when she is struggling. She loves her job and her people she supervises. Kris is
very organized and knowledgeable. She has an answer for almost every question we have. She
has worked with both Utah County and Salt Lake County elections and has knowledge of all
aspects of elections.

Kris always has a good attitude and a smile. She encourages those around her and loves to plan
events to make work more enjoyable. She knows each of us as a person, not just an employee.
She asks about our lives and our struggles. She does what she can to ease others’ burdens. She
has the heart of a giver, is kind and appreciative. She encourages growth in others and is never
condescending when questions are asked more than once. She is a great mentor and teacher.
She is attentive to details and encourages things done correctly. When mistakes are made, she
doesn’t make one feel insignificant or stupid, instead she kindly corrects and guides to learn
what should be done instead.

Kris works many hours overtime to get things done but doesn’t count it on her work hours. We
love working with Kris in our office. She radiates goodness and love to all around her. She
makes our office a good and safe place to work.

I believe that Kris Swenson definitely deserves to receive the employee of the month award.
