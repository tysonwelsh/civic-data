Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Doug Kofford to request the Planning Commission approve the conditional use
permit of a UVU/AT&T wireless telecommunications facility located at 1261 West 800 South in the M2 zone.

                                           Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to install a new wireless
                                           telecommunications facility. The request is made for a 100’ tower so the
                                           antennas may have better signal propagation into the campus and over the
                                           freeway. The 100’ tower will also allow for others to co-locate on it. This
                                           monopole shall be a steel, galvanized gray color. The pole is to be
                                           constructed to allow for two additional carriers.

The site will be 2,500 square feet of lease space area and will be enclosed by a six foot tall chain link fence with
barbed wire. There will be a typical layout of antennas.

The proposed facility is designed to provide additional coverage and capacity along I-15, into the campus of Utah
Valley University and the surrounding business areas. This site will close the gap in coverage and offer residents,
businesses and visitors 4G LTE wireless service. This site will also be prepared to handle the 5G LTE network that
will soon be implemented.

AT&T and its consultants examined numerous different locations for this site, with the primary goal to provide
coverage into Utah Valley University. For various reasons, none of those other sites were acceptable.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for the
conditional use permit of a UVU/AT&T wireless telecommunications facility located at 1261 West 800 South in the
M2 zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Vargas.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to come forward. Vinny Rowe introduced himself. Mr. Cook asked how close the
fence will be to the existing canal. Mr. Rowe said the equipment will be surrounded by a fence and all the equipment
has to be a certain distance from the fence so the equipment can be accessed. Mr. Cook said he is not questioning the
interference with the equipment. The canal company has access along that canal for maintenance purposes. The
question would be if the fence will interfere with that. Mr. Rowe said they are outside of any easement. Mr. Cook
said the canal is a prescriptive easement, it has been there since 1873. His concern is that there is enough room so the
canal company can do their maintenance. Mr. Rowe said he is not sure how much room they would need so that is a
tough question to answer. Mr. Cochran asked if Mr. Cook had any suggestions on how to work with this. Mr. Bench
said he could suggest that staff could work with the applicant so that by the time this goes before the City Council on
March 10th, that this is resolved and will be presented to the Council at that point. Mr. Rowe said they could keep the
tower in the same place and slide the compound away from the canal, so there should not be any issue with encroaching
on the canal or its easement.

Mr. Spencer asked how high the tower would be in relation to billboards in the area. Mr. Rowe said one company has
a market capitalization on the University and they are the only company that can put anything on University property.
It makes it difficult for any other company to cover the University, the surrounding housing and businesses. Anything
shorter eliminates the possibility of any other carrier co-locating on that tower and then there are multiple towers in
the area. At one hundred feet, they are at the top of the tower giving two additional carriers space below and the
ability to cover the area. This is a great spot with great coverage for the area.

Chair Cochran opened the public hearing and invited those from the audience who had come to speak to this item to
come forward to the microphone.

When no one came forward, Chair Cochran closed the public hearing and asked if the Planning Commission had any
more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Spencer moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for
the conditional use permit of a UVU/AT&T wireless telecommunications facility located at 1261 West 800 South in
the M2 zone subject to the easement difficulty with canal being cleared up. Ms. Jensen seconded the motion. Those
voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer and Mike
Staker. The motion passed unanimously.
