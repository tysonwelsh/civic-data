WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
JULY 12, 2022

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on July, 2022, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow via Zoom, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

INVOCATION BY: Jenny Jackson, the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Washington County Sheriff Deputy Jace
Wittwer

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:
CONSIDERATION OF ELECTION CANVASS OF JUNE 22, 2022

Washington County Clerk/Auditor Susan Lewis read aloud the results from the June 28, 2022
election. She reported that there were 66,158 active Republican registered voters and 11,131 other
party or UNA active registered voters for Local School 5 and 6 for a total of 77,289 voters. Those
numbers indicate a forty-four point nine four percent (44.94%) voter turnout.

(See attached election summary report for the primary election)
MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Accept the Results of the June 28, 2022

Primary Election. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS REPORT

Commissioner Almquist thanked all candidates for their participation as well as the
Clerk/Auditor’s staff for their diligent work during the election process.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:15 p.m.
