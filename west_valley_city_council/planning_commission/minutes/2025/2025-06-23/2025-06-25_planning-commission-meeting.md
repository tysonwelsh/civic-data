GP-3-2025
West Valley City
Adopt an update to the Fairbourne Station Vision

City staff is proposing an update to the Fairbourne Station Vision, which is part of the City’s General
Plan. The current version of this plan was adopted in 2012 and addresses the area between 3500 South
and Lancer Way (3650 South) and between 2700 West and 3200 West. This plan was originally called
the City Center Vision, which was adopted in 2004 in effort to steer development that helps create a
downtown for the City.

Staff is proposing this update for several reasons listed below:

   •    Much progress has been made in accomplishing the goals stated in the current plan. Examples
        include the construction of Weigh Station Road, Three Mill Lane, 3030 West, Granger Medical,
        the Fairbourne Office Tower, the parking structure, police headquarters, and two ICO apartment
        buildings. Given this progress, the future land use map was amended to reflect development that
        has already occurred or been approved. The specific goals that have already been accomplished
        were removed from the text of the plan.
   •    Reference data such as population and traffic counts were updated.
   •    The property between 2700 West and I-215 and between 3500 South and 3800 South was added
       to the plan with a future land use designation of commercial along the 2700 West frontage and
       Costco and mixed use on the balance. This area includes the Valley Fair Mall, Costco, The Opal
       Apartments, and the shopping center west of The Opal Apartments. This area was included in the
       original City Center Vision in 2004; however, it was removed in 2012 because the mall had just
       seen significant reinvestment. This area is being added back to the plan for two reasons. First, the
       mall once again needs reinvestment. Second, the State requires that station area plans for the
       areas surrounding light rail stations be certified by Wasatch Front Regional Council (WFRC) by
       the end of this year. Staff requested a courtesy review of the current Fairbourne Station Vision
       by WFRC staff before formally submitting the plan for certification. The feedback received was
       that the plan should include the mall area. As the Commission may recall, Wasatch Guaranty
       Development, LLC recently requested a mixed use designation on the mall site, which the
       Planning Commission approved. This change would have effectively added the mall site to the
       Fairbourne Station Vision. However, Wasatch has since tabled their application with no
       indication as to whether they will proceed with the application to City Council. Hence, to meet
       the State’s certification deadline, staff is now proposing to move forward with the update.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. The Fairbourne Station Vision update should be approved.
   2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the public
      hearing.
   3. Denial.

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. There were no questions or concerns.

Vote: Commissioner Porter motioned to approve GP-3-2025. Commissioner Durfee seconded. A voice
vote was polled, and all were in favor of the motion.

                              UNANIMOUS—GP-3-2025—APPROVED



Z-2-2025
Carl and Jennifer Newland
5173 West 3500 South
Zone change from A to R-1-7
0.86 acres (0.16 acres for the zone change)

Carl and Jennifer Newland have requested a zone change on 0.16 acres of a 0.86 acre parcel at 5173
West 3500 South from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-7 (Single Unit Dwelling
Residential, minimum lot size 7,000 square feet). Surrounding zones include R-1-8 (Single Unit
Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) and A to the north, RM (Multiple Unit
Dwelling Residential) to the east, A to the south, and A and RB (Residential Business) to the west.
There are single family homes to the north, west, and south and the property to the east was recently
platted as the Kiowa Court PUD for additional single-family homes. The subject property is designated
as Mixed Use in the General Plan.

Development Proposal

The subject parcel includes a home that, according to the Salt Lake County Assessor, was built in 1929.
The applicants would like to retain the existing home on a new lot that would be zoned R-1-7 and be 65
feet wide and 110 feet deep. On the remainder of the property, the applicants want to keep the existing A
zone and build a new home. Assuming the plat map from the Salt Lake County Recorder is correct, the
entire property is 148.5 feet wide (the applicant has indicated that the property is 150 feet wide), leaving
83.5 feet of width for the A zoned lot. The applicants have submitted a concept plan showing how they
would like to subdivide the property and where approximately the new home would be built.

There is an existing barn on the property. Section 7-6-205 of the Zoning Ordinance requires all
structures for the keeping of animals to be at least 40 feet from any dwelling. The existing home is
approximately 70 feet from the barn; however, based on the concept plan from the applicants, the new
home would be less than 40 feet from the barn. No animals can be in the barn if the new home is not at
least 40 feet from the barn.

Development Agreement

A development agreement is required for this project. Section 7-11-213 of the Zoning Ordinance states:
“All new Subdivisions involving a rezone of property, or a PUD, shall participate in a Development
Agreement that addresses housing size, quality, exterior finish materials, Streetscapes, Landscaping,
etc.” Development agreements may also be used to grant exceptions from zoning ordinance
requirements. For the development agreement, the applicants are proposing the following:

   •   The new home would be a 2,000 sq. ft. rambler with a 2-car garage that, in the words of the
       applicants, meets “all city codes as to building materials and criteria”. Normally a 3-car garage is
       required; however, Section 7-11-214 allows a 2-car garage for: “A home built on a legal and
       developed Single Unit Dwelling Lot under one acre in existence prior to April 15, 2015, that is
       large enough to be subdivided to create no more than one new Lot.”
   •   The minimum lot width is 70 feet in the R-1-7 zone and 100 feet in the A zone (Section 7-6-
       202). The applicants are proposing exceptions to these standards to reduce the lot width of the R-
       1-7 zoned lot to 65 feet and the A zoned lot to 83.5 feet.
   •   Section 7-2-111 of the Zoning Ordinance requires the front yard setback to be measured from the
       proposed right-of-way line where widening is anticipated in the Major Street Plan. The recently
       updated Major Street Plan shows the future right-of-way width of this portion of 3500 South at
       106 feet. The current right-of-way width of this portion of 3500 South is 80 feet, meaning an
       additional 13 feet (half of the difference between 106 and 80) of setback is needed on the south
       side of the street. The applicants have indicated that they plan to increase the setback by 13 feet.
   •   The applicant’s included the following commitments in their letter: “We would like to make
       some repairs to our existing home as well by replacing the roof with new architectural shingles,
       replacing the railings and fencing in the front yard, and improve the landscaping by using
       environmentally friendly plants and trees that will beautify the area and increase the overall
       aesthetic appeal of the neighborhood.”

Tyler Jones
3930 West Parkway Blvd
Zone change from BRP to C-2
11.25 acres

Tyler Jones has submitted a zone change application for an 11.25-acre parcel located at 3930 West
Parkway Blvd within the Lake Park Corporate Centre. The property is currently zoned BRP
(Business/Research Park) and the requested zone is C-2 (General Commercial). The General Plan
designation on the subject property is Business Park, which is described in the General Plan as “districts
where very large office uses are grouped together and surrounded by open space, which provides a
better transition to nearby residential neighborhoods.”

The zoning on all the surrounding property is BRP. Surrounding land uses the Stonebridge Golf Club to
the east and south, the Home2 Suites hotel to the west, and a vacant office building to the north. The
north portion of the property, which is approximately 4.6 acres in area, is undeveloped. The remaining
south portion includes a two-story office building built in 2000 that is 74,904 sq. ft. in area according to
the Salt Lake County Assessor.

If this application is approved, Salt Lake Behavioral Health intends to purchase the property for use as a
mental health hospital. Included with this report is a letter from the applicant that describes the services
that Salt Lake Behavioral Health’s current facility provides, their plans for the subject property, how
patients will arrive at the facility, and why they are looking to move to the proposed location.

For reference, a copy of the commercial and manufacturing zones use table is included with this report.
All uses that are regulated differently in the BRP zone from the C-2 zone are highlighted. In summary,
the C-2 zone allows hospitals, assisted living, nursing homes, and variety of retail uses that are not

Z-4-2025
Evy Collin
4048 South 4800 West (0.19 acres)
Zone change from C-1 and R-1-8 to R-1-7

Evy Collin has requested a zone change on two parcels totaling 0.19 acres at 4048 South 4800 West
from C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial) and R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size
8,000 square feet) to R-1-7 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 7,000 square feet).
Surrounding zones include C-1 to the north and R-1-8 to the east, south, west and a portion of the north.
The property is surrounded by single unit dwellings. The property is designated as Residential Office in
the General Plan.

Development Proposal
The subject property includes two parcels that total 7,972 square feet. Most of the property is paved and
there is a metal carport structure on the front parcel. The parcel lines between the two properties and
adjacent parcels have changed a few times over the years without any City approval. Staff has received
phone calls regarding potential commercial uses on the property from time to time, but due to the
property’s size, it would be difficult for a commercial development to work.

Since the combined subject parcels are below the minimum lot size requirement of 8,000 square feet for
the R-1-8 Zone, the applicant is requesting to rezone both parcels to the R-1-7 Zone. This zone does
have a minimum lot width requirement of 70’, so an exception to this code requirement will need to be
handled through a development agreement.

If this application is approved, the applicant intends to build a new home. The applicant has submitted a
house plan for the type of home that will be built on the property. The home will be at least 2,700 sq. ft.
above grade, and will include a basement, if possible. The exterior of the house will be a combination of
fiber cement siding, stone, and stucco. The applicant is also proposing to include a 2-car garage with the
home. After meeting the required side yard setbacks and other code requirements, there is not enough
space for a three-car garage. The applicant can still meet the zoning ordinance parking requirement for
the home by providing a paved parking area to the side of the garage. The new house will meet all other
requirements from the City’s
current design standards.

Development Agreement
Since the property will not meet the frontage requirement for the R-1-7 Zone, and the required 3- car
garage cannot be built due to the narrowness of the lot, these exceptions can be handled through a
development agreement. Other commitments from the applicant for the home will also be addressed in
the agreement, and all of these items are outlined below:
     • The lot width can be narrower than the required 70 feet.
     • A two-car garage (20’ x 20’ minimum interior) is allowed. An additional paved parking space
         will be provided on the side of the garage.
     • The minimum, above grade, square footage of the home will be 2,700 sq. ft.
     • The home’s exterior materials will be comprised of fiber cement siding, stone and stucco.
In staff’s view, the applicant’s proposal is a good use for two challenging, remnant parcels.

Staff Alternatives:
1. Approval. The application should be approved as it complies with the General Plan.
2. Continuance.
3. Denial.

too narrow for a commercial development and that a house would be an improvement to the property.

was polled, and all were in favor of the motion.

SMI-5-2025
Vantrust WVC Industrial Subdivision
6051 West Beagley Road
M Zone
2 Lots & 1 Parcel (40 Acres)

BACKGROUND
VTRE Development is requesting preliminary plat approval for the Vantrust WVC Industrial Subdivision.
The subject property is located at approximately 6051 West Beagley Road. The property was rezoned in
2024 from the Agriculture zone to the Manufacturing zone. The property is bordered on the north by
Beagley Road, and the east by a future road to be known as 5900 West. Property to the south is the
Wallace Stegner Academy Charter School, private property and Magna Water all zoned A. Property to
the west is also privately owned and zoned Agriculture as well.

ISSUES:
The proposed subdivision is approximately 40 acres in size and will consist of 2 lots and 1 parcel. The 2
lots comprise 18 acres with the remainder parcel being 22 acres in size. The remainder property is going
to be platted as a future phase. Part of the reason for developing the east side at a later date is due to the
realignment of the Riter Canal.


                        Minutes of WVC Planning Commission Public Hearing June 25, 2025
                                                 Page 8 of 11
During the rezoning of this property, a development agreement was approved by the City Council.
Condition #5 of the agreement stipulates that the developer agrees to perform both the design and
construction work for the box culvert for the realigned canal that will flow under the 5900 West Extension
beginning at the Eastern line of the property and continuing through the confluence of the realigned portion
of the Riter Canal where it merges with the existing Riter Canal facilities.

Access to the subdivision will be gained from Beagley Road. At the present time, curb and gutter along
with streetlights exist along the south portion of said street. Per the development agreement, the developer
will install a parkstrip with a minimum width of 5 feet. The Engineering Division is recommending that
the parkstrip be stamped concrete to match the City’s specification. The developer will also install a 10-
foot wide concrete trail within the existing right-of-way along Beagley Road.

At such time that the future phase develops, the developer shall design, dedicate and install a knuckle
intersection at the north end of Stegner Way. The road connection will then run along the north line of
the Stegner Academy eastward out to the future 5900 West. The section of road will be required to have
curb, gutter, sidewalk and streetlights.

The subdivision plat does contain areas designated as wetlands. Future studies will need to be done with
the Army Corps of Engineers to determine if these wetlands are jurisdictional. While these identified
areas do not affect the subdivision plat, they may affect future building locations. This item will be better
addressed during the conditional use process.

STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

1.     Approve the Vantrust WVC Industrial Subdivision subject to a resolution of staff and agency
       comments mentioned in the analysis.

2.     Continue the application for reasons determined in the Planning Commission meeting.

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented. Commissioner Durfee asked when the canal will be relocated on
lot 2. The applicant, Colby Anderson, was present and added information regarding the wetlands and
Ritter Canal. Mr. Anderson said canal work will begin as soon as they can obtain a permit with Salt Lake
County Flood and they are working with Rio Tinto as well as Kearn River Gas Company to relocate the
gas lines on the east side.

Vote: Commissioner Durfee motioned to approve SMI-5-2025. Commissioner Drozdek seconded. A
voice vote was taken, and all were in favor of the motion.
