PUBLIC HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Applicant 
Name: Justin Taylor 
Address: 8335 South 1000 East, Sandy 
Comments: Mr. Taylor said they live in the house in the front and will live in one of the units in the back 
and add a garage. Drive aisle on the southside. Met with hydrology to go through storm drain, all public 
utilities and what utilities are needed. Trying to minimize the footprint and keep separation from existing 
homes. 
 
Commissioner Collard motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Watkins seconded that motion. 
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Sue Phillips 
Address: 8284 South Fallbrook Circle. 
Comments: Ms. Phillips said the buildings discussed are in her backyard. Concerns with buildings starting 
to be built and never heard of anything. She asked what was happening and learned that there are townhomes 
being built, with a garage in between them. She was under the impression nothing could be built because 
the lot is so narrow and because of safety issues.  She has gone around to the neighboring homes, and no 
one received notification. She brought this up because her friend wanted to buy the property and it wasn’t 
buildable. The project affects 9 properties, and her petition shows that every person she spoke to is not in 
favor; had they been notified they might have been lenient. They want to know how applicant got away 
with building, and they don’t want the property built 2 stories because the new homes will look into all their 
properties. She proposes extending an olive branch-- put a halt to find a resolution (foundation and footings 
are in).  
 
Speaker # 3: Applicant 
Name: Justin Taylor 
Address: 8335 South 1000 East, Sandy 
Comments: Mr. Taylor said Ken Jorgenson is the owner of 8325 South, and Ms. Phillips is speaking about 
a different project. Taylor’s lot is two lots to the south, and he is essentially trying to do the same thing as 
Jorgenson, but the projects are separate and independent from each other . Jorgensen’s project has started 
construction.  
 
Commissioner Watkins asked if a two-story home. Mr. Taylor said the front unit is one story with basement. 
One of the proposed homes will be a two story with a basement and the home nearest the lot line will be a 
single story with a basement , which  fits with the area. Currently redesigning the one -story home . 
Townhomes to the east are two stories. 
 
Speaker # 4: Citizen 
Name: Lena Pham 
Address: 8331 South 1000 East 
Comments: Ms. Pham said she is right in the middle of both projects. Two story homes will impact her. 
There will be privacy issues. Sand y will require annexation to the properties in exchange for water. Her

Townhomes. Acreage: 4.43 acres. Location: 6807 South Mount Meek Drive. Zone: C-2/zc. Planner: Shad 
Cook (Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Shad Cook provided an analysis of the staff report. 
 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding sidewalks, grade, and rear load walkways.

PUBLIC HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Applicant Woodside Homes 
Name: Jim Clark 
Address: 460 West 50 North, Salt Lake City 
Comments: Mr. Clark said the purpose for the exception is topographical--80 feet of drop from one side to 
the other-- and for emergency access. Customers and homeowners need access to the front, have rear load 
access and walkway through there.  
 
Commissioner Collard asked about the sidewalk.  
 
Speaker # 2: Applicant Woodside Homes 
Name: Burke Staker 
Address: 460 West 50 North, Salt Lake City 
Comments: Mr. Staker said they are not trying to eliminate the sidewalk, but relocating to the side where 
residents will be using it. Answer to grade issue, if located at top wouldn’t be used as much and will end up 
with larger retaining walls and grade changes. Only eliminating sidewalk on one side.  
 
Commissioner Hiatt confirmed information about the retaining wall. Applicants confirmed the retaining 
wall is on the side, essentially the front. Built into landscape and nice and flat between the garages of rear 
load product. Commissioner Collard asked about retaining walls throughout the project, will they cut off 
access. Applicants advised the neighborhood is designed to move throughout and flowing in front of homes, 
streetscape and better product for the customer and not walking through alleys.  
 
PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To recommend approval of  application #WVR2023-000942 Woodside Homes requesting an 
exception to sidewalk standards for Sky Ranch Townhomes  to the County Mayor with staff 
recommendations. 
Motion by: Commissioner Watkins 
2nd by: Commissioner Vance 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the staff report.

PUBLIC HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Applicant 
Name: Jose Ramirez 
Address: 11825 South Ruby Court, Herriman

Comments: Mr. Ramirez said he is looking for roadway improvement. Reached out to Salt Lake County 
when he went through Herriman City process, and Herriman wanted a formal letter granting access. 
 
Commissioner Collard motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Watkins seconded that motion. 
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Leanne Peterson 
Address: 14540 South Majestic Oaks Lane 
Comments: Ms. Peterson said this will impact the entire road. The county maintained the road up to two 
homes. Opening the end of the road will open to all and will need a gate for through traffic except everyone 
living here. 
 
Speaker # 3: Citizen 
Name: Sue Scallion 
Address: 14775 South Majestic Oaks Lane 
Comments: Ms. Scallion asked why he wants a change in the grade and the benefit. Concerns about all the 
equipment coming up to extend and widen the road. What happens if the equipment sparks a fire ; grass is 
three feet tall, one spark and mountain will be on fire. There was a fire a few years ago, 15 structures burned 
down and eight of them were hers. There is no way to protect from a fire.  
 
Ms. Gurr read the emails and text messages received from Ms. Scallion and provided two pictures taken by 
Ms. Scallion (attached).  
 
Speaker # 4: Applicant 
Name: Jose Ramirez 
Address: 11825 South Ruby Court, Herriman 
Comments: Mr. Ramirez said extending the road will not increase traffic; he can’t control kids on 
motorcycles or quads. Change of grade is only two percent, which isn’t a huge difference. Since he is at the 
end of the street not too many cars coming up. Since he is at the end of the street, he is required to have a 
hydrant. He will speak with engineer at Herriman for standards and will be compliant to their requirements. 
 
Commissioners Watkins asked about drainage and runoff control. Mr. Ramirez  provided the site and 
grading plan and turn around; cross sectional design is for drainage and meets standards. 
 
Commissioner Watkins motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Collard seconded that motion.
