                                  RESOLUTION #2020-11-01

       A RESOLUTION OF APPRECIATION FOR APOLLO PAZELL FOR HIS
       DISTINGUISHED SERVICE TO THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP

       WHEREAS, Apollo Pazell, served on the Copperton Metro Township Council (“Council)
from January 1, 2018 to October 20, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, Councilmember Pazell served as Copperton’s Deputy Mayor, led the
Council’s annexation and legislative research committees, and represented Copperton on the Salt
Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area Board of Trustees and the Unified Police Department
of Greater Salt Lake Board of Directors; and

        WHEREAS, throughout his service, Councilmember Pazell endeavored to be a voice for
fiscal discipline, commitment to civil rights, and accountability, often developing and pursuing
creative and novel initiatives to further the interests of Copperton and its residents.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED effective immediately that the Copperton Metro
Township Council expresses its sincere appreciation to Councilmember Apollo Pazell for his
dedicated service; recognizes his time, energy, and devotion to serving Copperton and its residents;
and wishes him the best in his future endeavors.

                                                 COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
