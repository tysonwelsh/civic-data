3. Subdivision Preliminary Plat, Canyon Flats, 1518 Canyon Rd
Petitioner Craig North via Zoom was excited for the project to provide primary residence with
individual ownership, which includes 29 townhome units.
Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson displayed slides stating that the project meets the
12" Street Corridor Overlay standards and design requirements and that with some conditions
such as unit addressing requirements, corrected legal descriptions and signature blocks, and
required Plat verbiage changes, staff recommends approval.

4. Discussion of Multi-Family Housing
Planning Director Barton Brierley led discussion on multifamily housing trends, noting a 9.7%
vacancy rate and a goal to increase owner-occupied units to 70%. The zoning code update,
including the R-1-2.5 zone for small single-family homes, was highlighted.

Review of correspondence (if any)
None.

Reports
Landmarks Commission-Mandy Shale said they approved Certificates of Historic Appropriateness.

They also discussed that the Union Station sign is going to be repaired and possibly replaced, a new
sign on 25" St is going up for the National Historic Registry, and they reviewed updates to the code and
approved process changes.

Citizen Advisory Committee-Jeremy Shinoda reported they hadn’t met again since their last meeting
‘he reported on at the last Planning Commission meeting, but that they will meet again on March 18".
Ogden Trails Network-Michelle Wiliams said there has been a lot of work happening on the trails and
there is talk of conjoining some in the future.

Review of Meeting-James Tanner commented that the meeting went well.

As there was no additional business to come before the Commission, COMMISSIONER
SOUTHWICK MOVED THE MEETING ADJOURN. COMMISSIONER SHALE SECONDED THE MOTION. ALL
VOTING AYE.
