Council Work Session

Budget Discussion

              Mayor Clayton reviewed the elements of the 2019 Copperton Metro Township
budget.

              The Council Members each took different aspects of the budget (such as sales
tax, UPD, fees, etc.) to more closely examine the origins of all revenues and the reasons behind
each projected expense.

                It was noted that the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) will
be switching to a new computer system within 30 days. That will allow for more accurate line
items specific to Copperton. Currently, items such as mobile phones, contract printing, etc. were
Salt Lake County codes not used by the metro townships.
