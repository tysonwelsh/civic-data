GPZ-8-2023
Lieu Tran
7007 West Apaloosa Drive
General plan change from Parks and Open Space Existing to Light Manufacturing and zone
change from A-1 to M
5.91 acres


Lieu Tran has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for a 5.91-acre parcel located at
7007 West Apaloosa Drive. The property is currently zoned A-1 (Agriculture, minimum lot size 1
acre) acre) and the current General Plan designation is Parks and Open Space Existing. The current
General Plan designation is in place because this property used to be part of the mobile home park
to the west and was used as recreation space for the mobile home park residents. The proposed
zone is M (Manufacturing) and the proposed General Plan designation is Light Manufacturing. The
property includes a manufactured home along the west side that is accessed from Apaloosa Drive.

Surrounding zones include A-1 to the east and most of the south, RMH (Residential Mobile Home)
to the west and remaining south portion, and M to the north. Surrounding land uses include largely
vacant property owned by Magna Water to the south and east; the Western Mobile Estates mobile
home park to the south; and the Riter Canal, Beagley Lane, and the ARA Industrial Park to the
north. The property includes a manufactured home along the west side that is accessed from
Apaloosa Drive.

Development Proposal

The intended development for this property is a mix of 35,000 square feet of self-storage space,
30,000 square feet of flex space, and 4,000 square feet of office. Access would be gained primarily
from Beagley Road by crossing the Riter Canal. Apaloosa Drive would provide secondary access.

Self-storage facilities, warehouses, and light industrial require a conditional use permit within the
M zone whenever these uses are adjacent to a residential use, residential zone, or agricultural zone.
If this application is approved, a conditional use permit will be required given the neighboring A-1
zoning.

The applicant provided the following justification for the proposed changes:

       “There is M Zoning to the north with several large distributions buildings. Manufacturing
       would be a logical use for the property. Other surrounding land uses include a mobile home
       park, a mobile homes subdivision and a single family detached subdivision to the northwest
       across Beagley Road. Magna Water Company owns the wetlands to the south and east. The
       property owner wishes to build a self storage facility and ﬂex space for manufacturing uses.
       The residential areas near the property would beneﬁt from the self storage units, especially
       those residents in mobile homes. Beagley Road, a busy street that serves both industry and
       residential, provides good access to all planned manufacturing uses. Flexspace provides
       new or small manufacturing businesses with a space to grow. Home startup businesses
       often expand through renting ﬂexspace. Flexspace has the ability to grow or shrink with a
       businesses need and ability to pay. It has created the opportunity for many entrepreneurs to
       start a business with minimal initial capital investment. There is water and sewer available
       in Beagley Road.”
Issues to Consider

Since the adoption of the City’s first General Plan in the 80’s, the Riter Canal has been the dividing
line between industrial uses to the north and residential uses to the south in the northwest part of
the City. However, much has changed in the northwest part of the City since the 80’s. The
following list of current conditions may warrant consideration of M zoning on the subject property:

        The Northwest Economic Development Area (EDA), which was created in 2012, includes
         the subject property. The Northwest EDA covers approximately 1,000 acres in the
         northwest part of the City and makes available incentives for commercial development.
        The Magna Water property to the south and east includes wells and a water treatment
         facility. These uses will likely remain in perpetuity with no chance of having connecting
         roads to the subject parcel.
        The Western Mobile Estates mobile home park to the west has only one access, Apaloosa
         Drive, to 7200 West. Given that there are already 144 mobile homes with only one access,
         additional homes on the subject parcel would not be allowed without another access.
        There are large, distribution warehouses that are part of the ARA Industrial Park on the
         north side of Beagley Road.

Access

If the Commission believes M zoning is appropriate here, the primary access would need to be off
of Beagley Road. This will require the applicant to obtain permission to build a bridge over the
Riter Canal. If this application is approved, access to the site will need to be addressed as part of
the conditional use permit.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. This application should be approved.

   2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the public
      hearing.

   3. Denial. Industrial uses should not be developed on the south side of the Riter Canal.

Z-2-2023
Jackie Carlson
2256 and 2260 South 5700 West
Zone change from LI to M
2.2 acres

Jackie Carlson has submitted a zone change on two parcels totaling 2.2 acres at 2256 and 2260
South 5700 West from LI (Light Industrial) to M (Manufacturing). The subject property is
designated as Light Manufacturing in the West Valley City General Plan.

The property is surrounded by LI zoning. Neighboring uses include the Mountain View Corridor to
the west, vacant property owned by UDOT to the north and south, and vacant property and a
vehicle storage lot to the east. The larger of the two parcels includes a 7,150 square foot metal
building built in 1990 that was originally constructed for storage. Staff anticipates UDOT selling
their vacant parcels that were acquired as part of the Mountain View Corridor project. The property
is not currently being used for a business and is not licensed.

Up until earlier this year, a tiny home manufacturer called Modules Factory was operating without
a license or use approval on the property and had built an addition onto the building without a
permit. The previous owner submitted an application in an attempt to come into compliance;
however, the use eventually vacated the property before receiving approval.

The City’s Commercial and Manufacturing Zones use table is included with this report for
reference so the Commission can see the difference between uses allowed in both the LI and M
zones.

Development Proposal

The applicant acquired the property in March 2023 to serve as a truck service center to maintain
Carlson Distributing’s own fleet of trucks for their distribution business at 2449 South 6755 West.
Penske Truck, another West Valley City business, would operate the facility. The outside area
would be used for employee parking and truck staging while they are in-between servicing.

Within the LI zone, automobile service is only allowed as a use that is incidental to a permitted or
conditional use such as truck sales. In this situation, the truck service is the primary use proposed.
In addition, the LI zone does not allow outside storage and the trucks awaiting service would be
considered outside service. Given the LI zone limitations on truck service and outside storage, the
applicant is requesting the M zone.

The applicant is proposing a number of improvements to the property including the installation of
asphalt on the east, west, and south sides of the property; new metal siding for the west portion of
the building; new overhead doors on the building; interior remodeling of the building to include
restrooms and an office; landscaping along the east and west sides of the property; placing power
underground; storm water improvements; and repaired or replaced fencing.

If this rezone is approved, a conditional use permit will be required for the outside storage of trucks
awaiting service. The ordinance requires outside storage uses to be screened from public view
along 5700 West through fencing, landscaping, building placement, topography, berming, or other
methods as determined by the Planning Commission. For screening along Mountain View Corridor
and other high-image arterial streets, the ordinance was recently amended to require screening of
outside storage by means of a building or a 6’ tall, precast concrete wall meeting certain
specifications. However, the Planning Commission can waive screening requirements when, due to
changes in topography, screening techniques would not be effective. In this situation, solid
screening along the west side would not be effective for motorists on the Mountain View Corridor
as it is elevated. However, solid screening would be effective for users of the trail along the east
side of Mountain View Corridor.

Area History

A brief history of the surrounding area between 5800 West and 5600 West and from 2200 South to
2400 South (about 46 acres), which has been referred to as the salvage yards, is helpful background
information.
     Early 1960’s: Development began within the area with some unpaved roads and salvage
        yards.
     1980: By the time of the City’s incorporation in 1980, the area was occupied mostly by
        salvage yard businesses. The zoning in the area at the time was M-2, which was a zone for
        heavy industrial uses. The City also had a M-1 zone in 1980 for light industrial uses.
     1984: The City’s first General Plan designated the area as light industrial.
     1986: The City consolidated the M-1 and M-2 zones into the M zone.
     1999: The City’s General Plan included the goal of relocating or replacing the salvage yards
        from this gateway location.
     2001: The City created the 5600 W Gateway Redevelopment Area to provide incentives for
        redevelopment within the area.
     2004: The City formed a special improvement district (SID) to install utilities and road
        improvements in the area.
     2015: UDOT began to acquire land for the Mountain View Corridor which ultimately
        consumed about 22 acres of the 46 acres. UDOT will eventually sell about 5.5 acres of the
        22 acres acquired that were not needed for the Mountain View Corridor project.
     2015: The City passed a moratorium or temporary land use regulation on all land use
        applications to reevaluate zoning in the area.
     2016: The City created the LI (Light Industrial) zone and rezoned the area to the new LI
       zone.
      2021: Mountain View Corridor was completed in this area.

Development Agreement

As described in the history above, significant progress has been made in transitioning this area
from mostly salvage yards to either vacant property awaiting development or light industrial uses.
If the Planning Commission is supportive of a zone change to the M zone here to accommodate a
truck service center, staff is concerned about the possibility of a future use that would be counter to
the transitions occurring in the area. In addition, staff is also concerned about the outstanding issues
on the site including the illegal building addition, trailer storage that is already occurring on-site,
and the poor condition of the site.

Given these concerns, staff is recommending that the following items be addressed in a
development agreement:

      The following uses shall be prohibited:
           o Agricultural Business or Industry
           o Commercial Raising, Rental, Stabling, Training and Grazing of Animals
           o Detention Facility/Jail
           o Incinerator
           o Heavy Industrial
           o Outdoor Kennel
           o Towing and Impound Yard
           o Vehicle Recycling Facility
      Prior to any use of the property, a conditional use permit shall be obtained and all required
       improvements shall be installed.
      The right-of-way between the east property line and the curb and gutter along 5700 West
       shall be maintained weed free by the property owner.
      The sign frame in the upper lefthand corner of the east building façade shall either be
       removed or utilized for a new wall sign after obtaining a sign permit.
      A building permit shall be obtained for the addition on the west side of the original building
       and all building code deficiencies shall be corrected. A building permit shall also be
       obtained for any interior remodeling.
      As proposed by the applicant, the exterior of the addition shall be sided with metal siding
       that matches the existing building.
      The entire building exterior, including the addition, shall be painted consistently on all sides
       of the building.
      All weeds on the site shall be removed and replaced with either paving or landscaping
       according to a plan approved by the City.
      Damaged fencing, including razor wire, shall be repaired, removed, or replaced.
      All fence components shall be free of rust.
      The applicant shall dedicate 6-inches of right-of-way to the City along 5700 West. The
       existing right-of-way width is 65-feet and the standard right for a commercial street is 66-
       feet.

 Staff Alternatives:

      Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development agreement that

      Denial. The LI zone should remain.

The applicant, Samuel Wright, is requesting conditional use approval for two warehouse
buildings at 7091 and 7103 W Gates Ave. The property is zoned Manufacturing (M). The
properties to the north and east are zoned M, and the properties to the west and south are zoned
Agriculture (A). The West Valley City General Plan designates all properties along Gates
Avenue as light manufacturing, and all properties to the south as large lot residential. There is
industrial development to the north and east, single unit dwellings to the south, and an
undeveloped parcel to the west.

The subject property is currently two separate parcels. The 7091 West parcel has been zoned
manufacturing since 2017 and has remained vacant. The applicant originally developed the parcel
to the east, 7079 W Gates Ave, but has since sold that property. The 7103 West parcel was
recently rezoned from A to M (ZZ 20230001). That site was home to Backyard Auto Wrecking,
which began operating in 1978 (ZC 19780460). Mr. Wright is proposing to utilize both parcels
and build two warehousing/light industrial buildings. Each building will be 8,400 sq. ft. in size,
like the building on the property to the east. Earlier this year when the property was rezoned, the
applicant entered into a Development Agreement with the City, which applies to future
development.

Access to the development will be from Gates Avenue and will need to be reviewed and approved
by the Public Works Department. The proposed layout will likely need to change somewhat to
meet access spacing requirements outlined in 7-9-107(3). There was a delay agreement for the
right-of-way (ROW) improvements for the parcel to the east. As this property develops all ROW

property line. All landscaped areas shall include coverage of at least 50% live plant material.

It's anticipated that the buildings will be utilized for warehousing and light industrial uses. All
uses on the site shall comply with the Manufacturing Zone allowed uses (7-6-301), as well as the
list of prohibited uses listed in the Development Agreement. Any work done on the site shall be
done inside the buildings. No outside storage is being proposed. A 6’ tall masonry wall will be
installed along the south boundary of the site. A provision in the development agreement waived

the masonry wall requirement along the east property line, and the applicant can instead install a
6’ chain link fence with slats.

No signage is being proposed on the site, but any new signs shall meet the requirements of Title
11 and shall require a building permit.

C-21-2023
Royal Solutions Storage Units – Caretakers Dwelling 3489
South 1400 West
M Zone/Jordan River Overlay Zone (.76 Acres)


The applicant, Addison Hicken representing Royal Solutions, LLC, is requesting a conditional
use for a caretakers dwelling at the Royal Solutions Storage Units at 3489 South 1400 West.
This property is zoned M, Manufacturing. The West Valley City General Plan designates this
area as General Commercial. The surrounding zone is M, Manufacturing on all sides. The
surrounding uses include light industrial warehouse to the north and east, Dura Crete to the west,
and additional self storage space to the south.

This property is located within the Jordan River Overlay Zone (JROZ). The current use is a Self-
Storage facility which is prohibited in this overlay zone. However, per 7-14-203, existing
businesses may continue and can expand with approval by the Planning Commission.

This property was originally developed in 1971 as a warehouse flex storage use (C-101- 1971).
The adjacent self storage facility will also be supported by this Caretakers Dwelling and was built
in 1969. This property has been in the Hicken family for about 20 years and is in the process of
being cleaned up and renovated so it can be leased out solely as a self storage facility. The spaces
will not be used as tenant work spaces or shops and there will be no outside storage of any kind.
The caretakers dwelling is approximately 700 sqft and will be used to manage and support this
storage use as well as maintain some on site security. It will not be leased out as a stand-alone
apartment and the tenant will be employed by the storage units.

The self storage facility has 120 units, which is approximately 40,000 square feet of rentable
space. Per 7-7-122 a self storage facility under 60,000 sqft is allowed one Caretakers
Dwelling. There are existing sleeping, cooking and sanitation facilities but those will be re-
inspected and permitted through the Building Department upon use approval.

The site itself is in the process of being cleaned up including the building exterior, fencing
and landscaping. This is all part of the new business license review that is currently being
conducted and must be completed before the license can be issued.


 Staff Alternatives:


 Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
 following:

C-22-2023
Hunt Electric Outside Storage 2421
S. Progress Dr.
M Zone (1.6 acres)

The applicant, Carrie Stoehr representing Hunt Electric, is requesting a conditional use for
outside storage. The zoning for this area is M, Manufacturing. The West Valley City General
Plan anticipates light manufacturing uses for this area. The surrounding zone is manufacturing
on all sides. The surrounding uses include Desert Tech (gun/ammunition manufacturing) to the
north, Lindquist Sales (heating equipment supplier) to the south, UPS to the west and additional
Hunt Electric yards/uses to the east.

The primary building for Hunt Electric was approved as a permitted use in 1993 (PR-22- 93).
Business license records indicate that Hunt Electric located their facility here in 2001. Since that
time they have grown and have expanded their campus to adjacent properties. Part of this
expansion includes outside storage yards. These storage areas are located directly west of the
main Hunt Electric building (1935 and 1897 lot) as well as on the north side of Alexander Street
(1940 lot). A majority of the outside storage yards were approved in 2017 under C-35-2017. This
particular lot, which has been used by Hunt Electric for 13 years as indicated in the applicant
letter, was not under Hunt Electric ownership until this year and was not included as part of any
prior use reviews or plan approvals.

The primary purpose of these yards is for outside storage of specialized equipment and electrical
related materials. Tracked equipment is also used throughout these areas so the applicant is
requesting that the surfacing for this yard be gravel [§7-9-114(2)]. There is no direct access from
this parcel to Progress Drive so if it is properly maintained (weed free) and graded accordingly
Staff feels like this is an appropriate surface for this request. However, the Commission may
want to reinforce that vehicle and trailer parking shall only be permitted on paved surfaces.

Outside storage areas must also be screened from public view. The City Code suggests using
fencing, landscaping, building placement, topography, berming, etc. (§7-9-119). As outlined on
the site plan, this storage area is set back behind landscaping (50% live plant material and a
minimum of 1 tree planted every 30’) and a berm so the applicant believes this provides adequate
screening. Hunt Electric is concerned with security and feels a solid barrier fence would increase
the security issues for the yard. The landscaping that has been proposed does include trees
however the shrubs and perennials are all very low growing. Staff recommends at a minimum,
additional plantings be added that grow approximately 3-4’ tall in height adjacent to the fencing.

Also, as discussed in the study session, the sides of the lot are also visible from the right- of-
way. Currently no screening or landscaping has been provided for these areas. If the
Commission feels that there is not adequate screening a condition could be added that requires
slats in the fencing. These slats could be spaced to allow some visibility into the
site for security but also provide some screening as well. So not 100% covered but at least
50% could easily be achieved with slat spacing for example.

There are no structures proposed for this yard however, there are existing cargo/storage
containers on site. These must be removed or screened and located on the appropriate surface
to meet the standards in City Code (§7-7-106).

Public Works is also currently doing a review of the plans. They have indicated that curb, gutter
and sidewalk is required along this frontage. That will not impact the 20’ landscape setback or
fencing location. Also, an overall grading and drainage plan, including any required access and
drainage easements, will be required for this parcel as well as the parcels from C-35-2017 that
have not been completed.

 Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well as the
following conditions:

        1. The site improvements must be constructed per the approved plans and meet all
           applicable West Valley City standards including Public Works requirements.

        2. The storage area may be surfaced with gravel as outlined in 7-9-114(2). All

        3. The lot frontage must be landscaped per the standards set forth in the West Valley
           City Municipal Code including one tree for every 30’ of frontage and 50% live
           plant material.

        4. The landscape setback shall include a minimum 3’ berm and landscape
           plantings along the fence that are between 3’-4’ to provide screening.
