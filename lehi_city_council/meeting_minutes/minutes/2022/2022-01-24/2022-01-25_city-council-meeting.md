2. Presentations and Report

2.2) Property Excavation Discussion- Hutchings Museum
Daniela Larsen with Hutchings Museum gave a presentation.

    Councilor Koivisto joined the meeting at 5:38 p.m.

Daniela Larsen and the Hutchings Museum are requesting permission from the City to have access
to the Indian Caves to collect more artifacts for the Museum. Daniela stated they are expecting to
find flint that is used to make bow and arrows and would like a chance to go in to preserve the
artifacts before they get ruined. Elizabeth Hora with the States Artifact Office expressed no other
searches have been recorded in this area. The standard surfacing timeframe would probably take a
few hours, and then a few days to make a final report. Daniela said they don't need money for the
search, just permission, since they are just surfacing and not digging. The museum would be
partnering with other entities to help with cost. Lorin Powell clarified when developing, they were
very adamant that these nulls stay untouched.

The City Council gave permission to the Museum and the State Artifact Office to search the Indian
Caves.

2. 20 Minute Citizen Input
Mayor Johnson noted that item 6 was pulled from the agenda item at the applicant’s request.

Douglas Gordan with Utah Grand Finale Fireworks requested that the City require all Vendors
that sell fireworks be required to have lability insurance. He expressed his reasoning is because
his fireworks are distinct with the Utah Grand Finale logo and does not want his being
misconstrued as being uninsured. Mayor Johnson asked about the Fire Department and
regulation from the City. Ryan Wood stated that would be regulated by State Law, but he would
look into it. Mr. Gordan stated other cities require liability insurance from the vendor. Ryan
Wood stated he would check with the other cities and report back to the Council.

Kailynne Osborne requested request clarification in the development code. Her neighbors were
able to get an ADU permit due to the lack of code. She would like to see a minimum and
maximum in the code for a DADU. Mrs. Osborne suggested adding a maximum for a DADU. In
addition to the flag lot code, she would like to see definitions for hard surfaced roadway and
fencing clarification, as well as clarification for distance from a fire hydrant. Mrs. Osborne
would not like to allow flag lots near other homes to keep concentration for families and feels it
would keep in compliance with the general plan guidelines and asked the Council to address and
assess her concerns.

9. Consideration Ordinance #07-2022, approval of a comprehensive update to the Land Use
   Element of the General Plan including the land use map and text.
   The applicant, Lehi City, presented information on the General Plan Land Use.
   A separate application can be filed for an amendment for specific requests.

    Mike West presented on the changes that have been made to the Land Use Map and the Density
    Map.

    Mayor Johnson opened the public hearing for comment.

    Liz Anderson asked about a hotel and Kim Struthers stated it would be considered commercial
    use. She was concerned about the 100 unit restriction. Kim stated there are limits and they are
    structured in a different way. Each use is structured different with a cap. She stated the City is
    supposed to help the citizens not make it harder.

    The Aults asked if they bring in plans if it will be reconsidered for zoning. Mayor Johnson
    stated the General Plan was changed so property or zones could be revised. They said good
    ground is being wasted where they border main roads and light industrial. They would like the
    opportunity to re-zone. VLR next to half acre lots does not make sense with the location of
    their property. The Planning Commission suggested to lower the lot sizes. Staff suggested
    filing an application now or when they are ready to develop.

    Robert Cook stated he is in the triangle near 300 East, 700 South and Pioneer Crossing. He
    heard Planning Commission was reviewing the General Plan and got together with the
    landowners in that triangle that wasn't developed. All the landowners made an appointment
    with the Commissioners and all the landowners were represented in that meeting. The
    landowners were all unanimous in making the request that the area be high density or
    commercial as a buffer. He stated he has not been informed about the land use meetings or any
    of the changes. His concern is wanting the best value for the property.

    Mr. Merchant wanted to present on the commercial property on the property north of Adobe
    where the property is zoned for two different types of uses. There are two applications that
    have been submitted for the property and he requested that the plans be looked at in a fair
    manner. He is concerned about losing money and/or the property. They are willing to help with
    infrastructure.

    Megan Butler stated they weren't wanting to sell to apartments but with putting 100 units on
    the property it is hard to get people to be interested in the property for the highest and best use.
    She would like to see it be fair, maybe by a first come first serve basis. She doesn't feel like it
    is fair and feels like a salvage title with the zone that they are able to use their property for.

    Mayor Johnson closed the public hearing for comment.
