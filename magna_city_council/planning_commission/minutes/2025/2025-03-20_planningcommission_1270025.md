REZ2025-001339 - Jeffrey Copeland is requesting a rezone from A-1/zc to R-1-8. Acres: 0.5.
Location: 7563 West 2820 South. Planner: Justin Smith (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding R-1-10 lot size and surrounding property zones.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: CEO – Habitat for Humanity
Name: Carin Crowe
Address: 1276 South 500 West
Comments: Ms. Crowe said in an effort to build two homes with basements. The neighbors and community
member concerns with height, parking, density, and fencing and have addressed them. Partnering with the
Scott family for a land swap, and still in discussions. Not a large volume builder, they build safe, affordable
homes. Cannot be rental property, owner must remain in the home. Partner with Magna for over 20 years
and have built 22 homes. Believe in the power of home ownership.

Commissioner Cripps asked if this would stay within their vision. Ms. Crowe said within the financials to
keep affordable.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Cripps seconded that motion.

Speaker # 2: Scott Family
Name: Jeff Scott
Address: 7516 West 2820 South
Comments: Mr. Scott said he appreciates the conversations and meetings and finds the new plans
acceptable. Owns the road going up to 2820 and will welcome two families with two homes. Hope to make
the land swap and appreciates habitat for humanity.

Commissioner Cripps asked if it would open it up for the fire lane.

Speaker # 3: CEO – Habitat for Humanity
Name: Carin Crowe
Address: 1276 South 500 West
Comments: Ms. Crowe said hopeful that will be the main road to the homes and turn into a private lane
and to be respectful would like it to be a through road.

Speaker # 4: Citizen
Name: Jessica Platt
Address: 2748 South Dundalk Circle
Comments: Ms. Platt said she appreciates Habitat.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Cripps seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend application #REZ2025-001339 Jeffrey Copeland is requesting a rezone from A-
1/zc to R-1-8 for approval with staff recommendations to the Magna City Council.
        Motion by: Commissioner Richards
