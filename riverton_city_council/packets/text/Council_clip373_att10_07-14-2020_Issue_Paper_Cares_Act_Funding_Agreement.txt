SUMMARY:

In April of this year, Salt Lake County received federal funds under section 601(a) of the Social Security
Act as amended by section 5001 of the Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act (Public Law
116-136) (the “CARES Act”) to combat and address the effects of the novel Coronavirus Disease 2019
(“COVID-19”) within Salt Lake County. Riverton City has incurred significant expenses related to the
City’s response to the COVID-19 pandemic. Riverton City, therefore, is eligible to receive funding
provided under the CARES Act to Salt Lake County, which distributes said funding to the Cities.

Salt Lake County, for its part, requires that cities interested in reimbursement provided under the CARES
Act execute a “Cares Act Funding Agreement for Governmental Entities,” enclosed with this Issue Paper.
Stated generally, the agreement requires the city to provide monthly reports to the County describing how
funds are used for which the City seeks reimbursement under the CARES Act. The Agreement also
requires that we keep records of expenditures for six years following termination of the Agreement.
Cities may also be required to reimburse Salt Lake County, and by extension, the U.S. Dept. of the
Treasury if we are found to have improperly claimed or used funds received under the CARES Act.
