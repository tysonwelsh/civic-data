                                                                           MINUTES
                                                              WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                            Tuesday, February 10th, 2026 – 10:00 a.m.
                                                       at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Gage Froerer, James “Jim” H. Harvey (excused), and Sharon Bolos
STAFF PRESENT: Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Liam Keogh, of the County Attorney’s office; and Craig
D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME: Chair Froerer
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: All in attendance were invited to recite the Pledge.
C. INVOCATION/MOMENT OF SILENCE: Craig Brandt
D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. February’s Stall of Fame Recipient is Aubrey Vigil from HR.
      Chair Froerer: Aubrey Vigil is our Stall of Fame recipient. The process to hire employees at Weber County
      runs through Human Resources. Emily and her group do a fantastic job of making sure that we hire the best
      of the best and Aubrey is a great example of that.

            Commissioner Bolos: Aubrey is a really great asset to the County. She always has answers for my hiring
            questions. She is part of the team that puts together all of the extra employee parties and gatherings and is
            key to making those happen. I appreciate her willingness to be there for me and for all she adds to the
            County.

            Emily Wilde (HR Director): I have a really great team and one of those amazing members is Aubrey. She
            does a really great job planning and coordinating the employee events. She works great with all of our hiring
            managers and gets all of our new hires on boarded. I constantly get comments on how great and easy she is
            to work with. She earned her SHRM certification a year ago, which has a hard test and is a great honor to
            achieve. Every time I give her a task, I know it will get done. She is a problem solver and takes initiative. I
            am very glad to have Aubrey on my team.
