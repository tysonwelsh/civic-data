BUSINESS MEETING
Meeting began at – 8:34 a.m.
1) Approval of the October 13, 2022 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes. (Motion/Voting)
Motion: To approve the October 13, 2022 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes.
Motion by: Commissioner Wallace
2nd by: Commissioner Karkut
Vote: Commissioner Harpst abstained, all other commissioners voted in favor (of
commissioners present). Motion passed.
2) Discussion on a potential Night Sky Ordinance, including progress updates and feedback. Planners:
Matthew Starley, Gordon Bennett.
Mr. Starley provided a PowerPoint presentation on the potential Dark Sky Ordinance.
Commissioners and Staff had a brief discussion regarding parks and recreation, Return of
Investments, WFRC, Trails Masterplan, motorists safety assessment, general plan, wildlife, indoor
lighting, and light trespass, providing information and education, best practices, and
nonconforming uses.

Remove Change of Ownership and language under Scope and Applicability,

Remove Accent lighting compatibility under Conditions and Standards, Allowable

Change Amortization conformance from no later than ten years to no later than seven years
from the adoption of ordinance under Violations, Enforcement, and Penalties, Violations.
