Minutes of the Regular Session of the Lehi Local Building Authority held Tuesday, May 27,
2025, at 7:52 p.m. at the Lehi City Administration Building, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah.

Members Present:        Mark Johnson, Chairman
                        Paige Albrecht, Member
                        Chris Condie, Member
                        Paul Hancock, Member
                        Heather Newall, Member
                        Michelle Stallings, Member


Others Present: Jason Walker, City Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney; Dean Lundell,
Finance Director; Teisha Wilson, City Recorder.

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Mr. Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all members were present.

2. Public Hearing and Budget
2.a) Public Hearing

Mr. Johnson asked for public comment. There were no comments. Mr. Johnson closed the public
hearing.

2.a.) Consideration of Resolution #L2025-02 adopting a Lehi Local Building Authority Budget for
Fiscal Year 2026.


      Motion: Mr. Hancock moved to approve Resolution #L2025-02 adopting a Lehi Local
      Building Authority Budget for Fiscal Year 2026. Ms. Albrecht seconded the motion.

      Vote: Ms. Albrecht, Yes; Mr. Condie, Yes; Mr. Hancock, Yes; Ms. Newall, Yes; and Ms.
      Stallings, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the Local Building Authority at this time, Mr.
   Hancock moved to adjourn the meeting. Mr Condie seconded the motion. The motion passed
   unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:55 p.m.
