Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Travis VanEkelenburg
CC:  Date: January 14, 2021
Re: Siempre Event Center Development Agreement Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately 1.36 acres located on the north side of Mike Weir Dr., at approximately 1283 E Mike Weir Dr. The property is currently zoned C2 and RM . The applicant is requesting that an Agreement be approved to allow for:
1. A reduction in the number of spaces required in the 1990 Draper City Municipal Code; and
2. Twenty (20) of the proposed parking spaces to be constructed of geo-grass pavers to accommodate over flow parking for the site. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1.  The proposed development plans outlined in the Agreement meets the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan.
2.  The Parking Study adequately demonstrates that sufficient parking will be provided.
For Denial:
1.  The proposed development plans outlined in the Agreement do not meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan.
2.  The Parking Study does not adequately demonstrate that sufficient parking will be provided. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 1/11/2021 Cover Memo
