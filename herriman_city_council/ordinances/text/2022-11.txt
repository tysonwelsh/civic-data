HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-11

Z2021-151 -ORDINANCE TO AMEND TITLE 10 OF HERRIMAN CITY CODE TO
PERMIT MEDICAL SERVICES IN THE C-2 ZONE, MODIFY THE PARKING
SCHEDULE, AND AMEND THE DEFINITION OF MEDICAL SERVICES

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to state law, may adopt an ordinance to
establish land development regulations, which includes the adoption or amendment of zoning
regulations; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning regulation amendment to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior to
the said public hearing to recommend any zoning regulation amendment to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, on December 27, 2021 the required Planning Commission public hearing
notice was posted in public locations as required by State noticing provisions for a meeting to be
held on January 6, 2022, at 7:00; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission made a recommendation for the zoning regulation
amendment in a public meeting held on January 6, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chamber;
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council to
consider the zoning regulation amendment and Planning Commission recommendation in a public
meeting; and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on March 23, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. to
discuss the zoning regulation amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of Herriman
City to adopt the zoning regulation amendment, which was recommended by the Planning
Commission and documented in City File number Z2021-151;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:
A. Title 10, Chapter 16, Sections | of the Herriman City Code be amended as set forth in

the attached Exhibit A. All other sections and provisions of Title 10 shall be unaffected by this
amendment,

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 2022-11, shall take immediate effect as soon as it shall be published
or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s recorder.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 23 day of March 2022.

10-3-6: DEFINITIONS, LAND USE CATEGORIES:

MEDICAL SERVICE: An establishment providing therapeutic, preventive, or corrective personal
