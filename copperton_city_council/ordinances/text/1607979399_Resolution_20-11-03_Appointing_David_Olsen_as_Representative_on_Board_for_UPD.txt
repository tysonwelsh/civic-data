                         COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

                                     RESOLUTION #2020-11-03

                                         November 18, 2020

   A RESOLUTION APPOINTING DAVID OLSEN AS THE REPRESENTATIVE
   APPOINTEE OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP TO THE BOARD OF
   DIRECTORS OF THE UNIFED POLICE DEPARTMENT OF GREATER SALT LAKE

         WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township is a member entity of the Unified Police
   Department (the “UPD”) of Greater Salt Lake; and

         WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council (the “Council”) may appoint a
   Councilmember to the Board of Directors of UPD (“UPD Board”); and

          WHEREAS, Councilmember and UPD Board member Apollo Pazell resigned from the
    Council on October 20, 2020 (the “Resignation”); and

           WHEREAS, the Council desires to fill the vacancy on the UPD Board created by the
    Resignation.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE COPPERTON
   METRO TOWNSHIP EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

          1. The Council appoints David Olsen as the Copperton Metro Township’s representative on
   the UPD Board and to serve out the remainder of Councilmember’s Pazell’s term of office, which
   will expire on December 31, 2021.

          2. Mayor Clayton is authorized to provide UPD with this resolution, a copy of David Olsen
   oath of office, and David Olsen contact information.
