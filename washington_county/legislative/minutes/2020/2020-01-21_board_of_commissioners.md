PRESENTATION: 2020 PRESENTATION OF PILT PAYMENT FROM THE
DIVISION OF WILDLIFE SERVICES

Wildlife Program Chief of the Department of Natural Resources (DNR) Kevin Bunnell
explained that the payment presented today is for the 2019 contractual in-lieu tax payment
(PILT) on land the DNR owns in Washington County. The money used to pay the in-lieu
tax is provided by hunters and anglers in Washington County and across the state; the
amount being paid is $117.92.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2560, A RESOLUTION
SELECTING ARCHITECTURE AND CONTRACTING FIRMS FOR THE NEW
WASHINGTON COUNTY ADMINISTRATION BUILDING

Washington County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that the County is in need
of a new Administration Building as the County has outgrown the current facilities. The
new Administration Building will combine county departments that are currently in several
locations.

The County advertised for Request for Qualifications (RFQ), specifically for a design-build
model of contracting and many qualified companies submitted their qualifications. The
field was narrowed down to two (2) applicants after a selection process, and then those
companies participated in final interviews.

Ms. Felshaw stated that Watts Construction Inc. and Simpson Coulter Studio are being
recommended as the design-build team for the new Administration Building.

The planning and design process will take approximately eleven (11) months, with the
ground breaking to take place in December 2020 and construction lasting twelve (12) to
eighteen (18) months.

Commissioner Almquist further explained that current and past department heads and
elected officials should be commended for their saving strategies to prepare for the future.

Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen explained that the County is in good financial standing and will
not have to bond in order to pay for the construction of the new building. Mr. Hafen also
explained that the Justice Court will remain in the newly remodeled building moving
forward.

Commissioner Cox further explained the current building was constructed in 1966 when
the population of Washington County was only 12,000. Today the population is over
170,000 and the needs of that population require a larger and more practical workspace for
the County’s offices.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-

the New Washington County Administration Building Which Are
Watts Construction Inc. and Simpson Coulter Studio. Motion seconded

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2561, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING DIXIE STATE UNIVERSITY AND GREATER ZION MARKETING
AGREEMENT

Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that tourism tax revenues may only be spent to
promote and establish tourism and recreation in the County. There are marketing
opportunities, including certain naming rights to enhance tourism in the area and the
Tourism Tax Advisory Board (TAB) have recommended to enter into an agreement with
Dixie State University (DSU) for those marketing opportunities.

Mr. Lewis further explained that the agreement will be $500,000 annually, over twenty
(20) years with DSU. As part of the agreement, DSU’s outdoor stadium will be renamed
the Greater Zion Stadium. Funds for the marketing agreement come from visitors that pay
Transient Room Tax (TRT) while recreating in the area.

President Biff Williams mentioned that there is a great partnership between the University
and the community. The University has grown sixteen percent (16%) from spring 2019 to
spring 2020 and will move to be a National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA)
Division I school beginning in July 2020.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2561, A
Resolution Approving Dixie State University and Greater Zion
Marketing Agreement. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson,
Almquist, and Cox voting aye.
