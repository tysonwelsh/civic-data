DISCUSSION ITEMS
General Plan Discussion – Southwest Region
Steve presented. In Area 4 there are two distinct alternatives: all industrial or single-family development with
new buffering existing in (can’t see) activity to the north at carbon fiber manufacturer, Hexcel. Industrial
development in Area 4 would benefit from the city’s transportation access (can’t see) and employment
strengths in manufacturing and transportation, trade and utilities. Alternatively, Option B envisions a unique
opportunity for the city to add to its single-family housing inventory within a land-constrained region. The
preference among the commissioners is Option B given the city can provide police and fire in that area.
