201   connections would be to the existing stub roads and at Grays Drive. There was a current MAG
202   project to widen 2300 West to five lanes between Main Street and 2100 North, projected to be
203   finished by August 2025. South of Main Street, 2300 West would require a traffic signal at
204   Gray's Drive and 700 South. Many issues stemmed from regional traffic coming from Eagle
205   Mountain and Saratoga Springs. UDOT had recently received federal funding and would start the
206   2100 North corridor, with construction beginning in 2026. Pioneer Crossing would be installing
207   flex lanes starting in 2026 as well. These changes should alleviate traffic. He stated relief was
208   coming, though it was not immediate. The project would not add significant traffic to the area.
209   Kim Struthers applied to finish improvements on 700 South. MAG had provided funding for
210   pedestrian and bicycle activity, but this would all take time, with funding expected in two years.

212   Commissioner Everett stated they couldn't start construction until water dedication was resolved.
213   There was no sidewalk on the 700 South Island. Kim Struthers mentioned a ribbon road would
214   be connected, and Richard Chu owned this property. Commissioner Everett asked if there was an
215   ingress and egress on Main Street. Luke stated he was not sure what the developer had planned
216   for the site, but a traffic study would be conducted. Mr. Maddox stated the City did not want the
217   road stubbed out by limiting access to Main Street. He mentioned that traffic was a concern and
218   wait times were an issue. Mr. Maddox stated their traffic study presented a global solution. Luke
219   stated signal spacing would not allow another light. Commissioner Eyre asked if the City was
220   requesting an exception for the road access. The only access to the City would be from Main

225   if STRATA had a limited time in Lehi or if this was long-term. Kim stated they did not solely
226   operate in Lehi and were not exclusively for Lehi City, indicating it would be a long-term site.
