MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
Date: 2025-03-27
Re: Public Hearing: AT&T Galena Grove Conditional Use Permit Request (Administrative
Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use permit for approximately
5.77 acres located on the east side of Vista Blvd, at approximately 12383 S. Vista Station
Blvd. The property is currently zoned OS (Open Space) and RA1 (Residential Agricultural).
The applicant is requesting that a Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for
construction of a new wireless telecommunications facility (monopole).



Findings for Approval:

  1. The application complies with the standards for approval in Subsections 9-5-080(E)
     and 9-41-070(H) of the DCMC, and the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of
     the proposed use are substantially mitigated through the imposition of reasonable
     conditions.



Findings for Denial:

  1. The application does not comply with one or more of the standards for approval
     listed in Subsections 9-5-080(E) or 9-41-070(H) of the DCMC, and the reasonably
     anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use are not able to be substantially
     mitigated through the imposition of reasonable conditions.
