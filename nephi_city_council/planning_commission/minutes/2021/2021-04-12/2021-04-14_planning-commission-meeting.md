Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
April 14, 2021

Chairman Greenhalgh welcomed those in attendance and opened the meeting at 7:00 p.m.
Opening prayer offered by Glenn Greenhalgh.
Minutes of March 10th meeting were reviewed. Karl Brough motioned approval of minutes and Cory Thomson seconded. Motion passed by unanimous vote.
Commission discussed Paul McPherson’s request for zone change from R1H to CU. Ann Peterson motioned for public hearing to be held next month. Motion was seconded by Karl Brough. Motion passed by unanimous vote.
Commission discussed John Osburn’s request for zone change of 5 acres near 500 N, west of tracks. Currently zoned R1-8, John would like a zoning which would allow for the development/operation of a vacation rental such as an Airbnb. Cory Thomson feels this could bring up a bigger issue and more than one location should be looked at. Chairman Greenhalgh explained provisions could be made within existing districts and will put information together for the Commission to review next month. This request is tabled until then.
Ludlow zone change request of 42 acres along sheep lane was reviewed by Commission. Recently annexed into the City, Ludlow would like to change the zone from 8C Highway Commercial to Combined Use. Cory Thomson motioned a public hearing be held next month. Motion was seconded by Ann Peterson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.
LC Development vicinity plat and DRC notes were reviewed by Commission. Property is 7.3 acres located at 1300 N Main Street. LC Development is asking for opportunity to demonstrate to City Council this area is good for higher density in the CU zone. They would like to have 16 units per acre. Presently, 8 units per acre is allowed. Rick Carlton asked for exception to move ahead without access permit and geotechnical study needed for preliminary. It was recommended he allow extra time for the geotechnical report. Karl Brough motioned the approval of LC Development vicinity plat along with recommendations from DRC. Motion was seconded by Ann Peterson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote. 
Winn Ridge subdivision preliminary plat and DRC notes were reviewed by Commission. Updated plat shows 71 lots with lot #24 becoming a retention pond. 1250 North will be extended east to join with the subdivision. Karl Brough motioned approval of Winn Ridge subdivision preliminary plat and recommended DRC notes. Motion was seconded by Ann Peterson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.
Commission reviewed Red Cliffs Estates preliminary plat and DRC notes. Upon recommendation from DRC Patrick Painter has changed the name to Jamestowne. Karl Brough asked if a geotechnical report had been done and Painter explained he should be receiving the report any day. Cory Thomson motioned approval of Jamestowne preliminary plat and DRC recommendations. Motion was seconded by Karl Brough. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.
Chairman Greenhalgh updated the Commission he has been working with Casey Wright, City Attorney, on language for ADU’s (Accessory Dwelling Units), though nothing yet for the Commission to review.
Regarding a Blight ordinance, Glenn is working with City staff to put together areas possible of blight designation and incentives for developers. 
Noting no additional items of business, Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 8:13pm.
