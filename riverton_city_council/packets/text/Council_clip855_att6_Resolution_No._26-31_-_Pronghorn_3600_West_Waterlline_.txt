                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-31

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A CONSTRUCTION
    CONTRACT WITH PRONGHORN CONSTRUCTION FOR THE 3600 WEST
    WATERLINE REPLACEMENT PROJECT (11800 SOUTH TO 12350 SOUTH)

        WHEREAS, Riverton City solicited bids for the 3600 West Waterline Replacement
Project, consisting of the replacement of waterline infrastructure along 3600 West from 11800
South to 12350 South; and

       WHEREAS, the procurement was conducted under Bid No. CC26-468 in accordance
with applicable procurement requirements; and

       WHEREAS, Pronghorn Construction submitted the successful bid for the Project and
has agreed to perform the work in accordance with the contract documents; and

      WHEREAS, the contract amount for the Project is One Million Six Hundred Eight
Thousand Eight Hundred Eighty-Five Dollars and Eighty Cents ($1,608,885.80); and

       WHEREAS, the Project is required to be completed within 120 days after the agreed-
upon start date and no later than May 15, 2027; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that entering into the
contract with Pronghorn Construction is in the best interest of Riverton City and its residents and
is necessary to maintain and improve the City's water distribution infrastructure.

    NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

   1. The City Council hereby approves the Contract with Pronghorn Construction for the 3600
      West Waterline Replacement Project (11800 South to 12350 South), attached hereto as
      Exhibit “A.”

   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute the Contract and any non-substantive revisions
      necessary for execution, subject to approval as to form by the City Attorney.

   3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
