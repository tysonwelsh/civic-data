                        SUMMARY:     During the 2022 State legislative session, the Utah State Legislature adopted HB462
                                     that requires all local governments in the state with transit stations on a " fixed
                                     guideway" to adopt station area plans ( SAP) that address, among other things,
                                     housing, housing affordability, environmental practices, access to opportunity, and
                                     connectivity in the vicinity of the stations.
                                     Based on the provisions of HB462, three stations within Taylorsville ( 1300 West
                                     Station, West Atherton Station, and River Boat Road Station) on the Mid- Valley
                                     Express bus rapid transit ( BRT) system are required to complete SAPs for an area
                                     within' A mile of the applicable station platforms.
                                     This ordinance approves a general plan amendment to adopt the Taylorsville
                                     Expressway BRT station area plans.


     STAFF RECOMMENDATION:           Staff     recommends    approval    of   Ordinance #   25- 05   adopting   the   Taylorsville
                                     Expressway Station Area Plans as an addendum to the Taylorsville General Plan.

       WHEREAS,       the Taylorsville     City   Council ( the " Council")
                                                                   met during its regularly
scheduled meeting on May 7, 2025, to consider, among other things, approving a general plan
amendment adopting the Taylorsville Expressway BRT Station Area Plans; and

       WHEREAS,       pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE § 13. 35. 050, notice of a public
hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the City' s official website on
March 12, 2025; and


       WHEREAS, notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was also
published on the Utah Public Notice Website on March 12, 2025; and

        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on March 25, 2025; and

       WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission met in a regularly scheduled public meeting
on the proposed amendment on April 8, 2025, during which the Planning Commission voted
unanimously to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council regarding this matter; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on May 7,
2025; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to adopt the proposed
Station Area Plan.


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed Taylorsville Expressway BRT Station Area Plans are hereby adopted.
