Council Business
Eminent Domain – Golden Springs LLC
David Church stated eminent domain is the process by which government
acquires property that cannot be obtained through negotiations with property owner. A written
notification was sent to the property owners inviting them to attend the Council meeting.
Crystal Hulbert, Salt Lake County Engineering and Flood Control, stated she has
received complaints about a section of the canyon where rocks are sliding down a slope onto the
road causing accidents. To mitigate the problem a metal net needs to be installed on the slope
with anchors being installed a few feet back. The area that the anchors will be placed is not a
buildable area.

Eminent Domain – Vince & Karen Rodgers
David Church stated Vince and Karen Rodgers own a piece of property that is
incumbered by the old road. A construction easement is needed to do work on the property to
develop a hammer head turnaround and trailhead. The Salt Lake County Real Estate Division
has negotiated with Mr. Rodgers, offering $9,000 as compensation for the easement. Mr. Rodgers
response to Derrick Sorensen, Salt Lake County Real Estate Division, was to buy the entire piece
of property for $350,000. Negotiations were stalled and a notice was sent to Mr. Rodgers
regarding the opportunity to speak at the Council meeting. The property owners are not here and
there is no one to represent them. He recommends approving the resolution so if continued
negotiations fail, eminent domain can proceed.
The Council reviewed the following Resolution authorizing the acquisition of real
property through the use of power of eminent domain (condemnation):

The Council accepted the following Resolution expressing intent to study and

RESOLUTION NO. 19-12-04

Date: December 12, 2019

A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP’S
INTENT TO STUDY AND CONSIDER PARTICIPATION IN THE COMMUNITY
RENEWABLE ENERGY ACT OF THE STATE OF UTAH
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council ("Council'') met in regular
meeting on December 12, 2019, to consider, among other things, approving a resolution
expressing Emigration Canyon Metro Township’s intent to study and consider participation in the
Community Renewable Energy Act of the State of Utah; and
WHEREAS, during the 2019 Regular Legislative Session, the State Legislature adopted
House Bill 411, codifying the Community Renewable Energy Act, which act provides for the
opportunity for municipalities to commit to the development of renewable energy sources and the
utilization of renewable energy sources; and
WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds and declares that the adoption of renewable energy
technologies and the utilization of renewable energy sources is in the public interest and will
benefit the public welfare.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council as follows:
Section 1. Statement of Goal. Emigration Canyon Metro Township hereby states a goal of
achieving an amount equivalent to 100% of the annual electric energy supply for participating
customers from renewable energy resources by 2030. This goal is stated in accordance with the
provisions of the Community Renewable Energy Act codified at Utah Code Ann. § 54-17-903, and
is intended to express the Council’s decision to participate in the study and review of the costs
and other requirements related to the development of renewable energy sources for Emigration
Canyon Metro Township and for use by participating customers within the city limits of Emigration
Canyon Metro Township.
Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
approval by the City Council.
