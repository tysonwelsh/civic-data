3.2 Public hearing and consideration of David Doty’s request for approval of a concept for
the Lehi Bakery Expansion located at 172 West Main Street.
    Brittney presented information on the agenda item. The on street parking is currently used by
    employees and it won't increase because of time of use from bakery and eatery. Mixed use
    does not have requirements. The applicant provided parking solutions. DRC has 8 comments
    on this concept plan.

    The applicant, Dave Doty, addressed administrative purposes for the building and for
    parking. No plans for it to be a rented space.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing for comment.

    Dave Hoyt stated he interviewed the business of Main Street that would be most affected by
    the concept. He asked that final approval is not granted until the Commission has read the
    rebuttal and redlines for David. The main issue he addresses is parking. He feels it is a
    serious issue to developers and the City.

    John Thorton is a neighbor and his building has been listed as abandoned, although it is not
    abandoned. He uses it for storage and hobbies. He feels that the real issues are he must
    demand his existing egress and must be accepted as a legal right of way and wants to be
    guaranteed that is the only entrance and exit. The major issue is the historic materials. He is
    concerned about his bricks and limestone being preserved. He hopes the City will be
    responsible and feels there are other resolutions. His comment was received through email.
    He stated Dave Doty approached him and wanted to make a trade on his building.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

    The applicant wished he could've discussed this a little more with Dave and talked about the
    future parking on Main Street and the developers plans. A pizza shop and bakery are busy at
    different times and have different time demands. He presented information on his business
    hours.

    Commissioner Hemmert reminded the public about a concept phase looked like for the future
    and resolve problems and concepts in the future and take it into consideration and worked
    through with the process. He wants to make sure things are done thoroughly so there aren't
    issues in the future and will be talking points as we move forward.

    Commissioner Eyre wanted to discuss the staircase where it is on the roof of the existing
    bakery and wondered if there were other options. Commissioner Peterson stated he is ok with
    the expansion, but it seems there is a lot that didn't make it in not the packet with the concept
    plan and would like more information to review. Kim stated concept plans should have
    elevation, but it is a tricky balance before spending money making sure that it is an actual
    viable option. The concept plan is a chance to give input early on, but it is a valid take to
    request more information. Commissioner Hemmert stated it does help to be able to visualize
    the plan. Commissioner Ellis stated he is descriptive in the narrative. The applicant stated
    they would like to be a model property. Commissioner Hemmert stated they want it to
    happen but that it needs to happen the best way possible. Commissioner Everett stated he
    likes the idea, but it needs clarification and feels it would be a welcome addition.
    Commissioner Nielsen stated the concept plan is to test the viability regardless of what
    rendering are available and felt he provided that information to test the viability and felt that
    it could work and that it isn't easy to get people to invest on Main Street vision that the City
    has. He doesn't feel that parking is an issue and also stated if residents went on the third floor
    that would be great for mixed use downtown. Commissioner Ellis stated there are things the
    City can do to alleviate parking for the public and employees. Commissioner Everett stated
    this is a speedbump not a roadblock to get issues resolved. Commissioner Bunker stated he
    works for another City and projects that have investment are cared for and can echo the
    history while providing a facility that people want to visit and feels the applicant has
    provided enough to move forward with the concept plan.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to forward a positive recommendation of David
        Doty’s request for approval of a concept for the Lehi Bakery Expansion located at
        172 West Main Street; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Ellis seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, no; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed 5 – 2.

3.5 Public hearing and consideration of Holiday Oil’s request for approval of a concept for
a Holiday Oil to be located at 1225 West Sandalwood Drive in a Commercial zone.
    Commissioner Everett disclosed he works for the State Agency that regulates underground
    storage but has no vested interest. Will defer to the rest of the Commission.

    Brittney presented clarifying information on agenda item. Concept looks at the layout of the
    site. The building will be on the SE corner of the property and meets design standards. With
    1200 West being a busier street, it may warrant an exception that most pedestrian traffic will
    come from there. DRC had 9 comments including comments about looking into having
    shared access with UDOT on the southern side of the site. Ross has been working with
    UDOT and they don't really want to do that. Ross is updating his DRC comment to address
    an access point.

    The applicant was present and agreed that UDOT didn't want to share an access point.

    Commissioner Peterson stated the business could still be successful and that the exception
    can still be granted. Ross stated a landscape architect could be hired.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing for comment.

    Ron Harris with Young Living Essential Oils wanted to thank Lehi for their partnership. Ron
    expressed his concern for the project is the traffic. He is requesting at some point in time a
    traffic study be conducted or something similar along those lines and being involved so it
    doesn't become a real pinch point.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

    Ross Dinsdale addressed the traffic concerns and stated Ron is right that it is a difficult spot.
    They widen 1200 West and preformed a traffic analysis. The difference today from 1200
    West is the Frontage Road creates and inability to drive North to South and the City is
    looking for a way to connect that putting traffic on sandalwood. UDOT is concerned with
    sharing due to big trucks and accessibility. Commissioner Eyre asked which access would be
    preferred. Ross stated either one would be helpful as long as it is shared and said that site
    access would have to be permitted.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker motioned to send a positive recommendation of Holiday
        Oil’s request for approval of a concept for a Holiday Oil to be located at 1225 West
        Sandalwood Drive in a Commercial zone; including all DRC comments and findings
        stated in the report. Commissioner Hemmert seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
