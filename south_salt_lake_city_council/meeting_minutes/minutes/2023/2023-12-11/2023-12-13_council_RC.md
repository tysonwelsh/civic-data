New Business

    1. A Resolution of the City Council of South Salt Lake City, Utah Appointing a New
       Trustee to the Board Of Downtown East Streetcar Sewer Public Infrastructure District.
       City Attorney, Josh Collins, informed the Council that the board reached out to the City
       to inform them of a vacancy due to one of its members leaving. He reminded the
       Council that the public infrastructure district was established in April of 2022 and that
       the City Council appointed the Board of Trustees to act as the board until the time that
       the significant number of residents and voters live in the area.

        Director of Real Estate Development for Dakota Pacific, Steven Borup, is the potential
        replacement for the vacancy.

        Mr. Collins explained that there is no Resolution at this time but that the item was
        added for any needed discussion so that it can be ready for action at the next Council
        meeting. He also said that Mr. Borup should be present at the next meeting for any final
        questions.
