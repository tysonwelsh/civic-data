                                  PLANNING COMMISSION MINUTES
                                              Meeting of May 12, 2022
                            Logan City Council Chambers (290 N 100 W Logan, UT 84321) www.loganutah.org

Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session
Thursday, May 12, 2022, Commissioner Lucero, called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Roylan Croshaw, Jordy Guth, Ken Heare, David Lewis, Eldon Peterson,
Sara Doutre, Jessica Lucero

Commissioners Excused:

Staff Present: Russ Holley, Tanya Rice, Mike DeSimone, Mohammed Abdullahi, Ben Anderson, Bill
Young, Chief Brad Hannig

Commissioner Guth moved to approve the minutes from the April 28, 2022 meeting as submitted.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Heare. The motion was approved unanimously.

PUBLIC HEARING

 PC 22-025 Logan City Fire Station #70 [Design Review & Conditional Use Permit] Alex
 Oliphant, Chief Brad Hannig/Logan City, authorized agent/owner, request to construct a new
 22,750 Square Foot, 3-story Fire Station that will stand 45’-9” tall. The new Fire Station will sit on
 the northwest corner of the intersection at 200 North 100 East in the Town Center One (TC-1)
 zone; TIN 06-017-0001; -0002; -0004; -0026; -0024 (Adams Neighborhood).


STAFF: R. Holley reviewed the request for a Design Review and Conditional Use Permit for a new
22,750 SF 3 story Fire Station #70 located at 200 North 95 East. The three existing structures on
the site are proposed to be demolished. The new fire station has been designed with living
quarters above the garage area, and administrative offices on the east side of the building. There
is a drive aisle with parking stalls proposed along the west side of the building and a parking area
to the north of the building. The exterior of the building is proposed with red-colored brick and a
dark-colored standing seam vertical siding.

The Land Development Code conditionally permits public safety services in the TC-1 Zone. The
conditional use permit allows the planning commission an opportunity to mitigate any unique or
adverse impacts created by the land use proposed. Typically, the planning commission is looking
for any modification that can be made to help the proposal be more compatible with the
surrounding neighborhood.

As proposed, the project does not meet the 5-foot landscape border in the northwest corner of the
site. The Land Development Code does allow for exceptions when you have two abutting parking
lots that have joint access between them. The idea is to have a buffer between the neighbor,
better water retention, and help mitigate the impacts of asphalt parking lots.

The commission will need to determine if the proposed building design is compatible with
downtown, if they want to accept the alternate parking plan that includes 20-22 stalls, and if a
landscape buffer setback is needed in the northwest corner of the site.


Commissioner Peterson asked about the entrance on the south side of the building as the designs
do not account for the 6 ft slope along that side. R. Holley said that they are currently working with
a civil engineer to accommodate the grade. There will be slight adjustments to the position of the
building and possibly some retaining walls and fill added to account for the 6-foot variation, this
should not change the building design.

Commissioner Croshaw said he would like to see more details in the design, he is not sure if the
proposed design is compatible with the surrounding buildings. He also asked if bike stalls should
be required. R. Holley responded that the project could be conditioned to include bike stalls.

Commissioner Heare said that the design is very similar to the existing fire station and is
compatible with the neighborhood.

PROPONENT: Brad Hannig, Logan City Fire Chief, said they are working to balance the 6-foot
difference in grade by shifting the building slightly to the west and having the east side of the
building lower than the west. He talked about trying to match the design of surrounding buildings
while keeping the design aesthetically pleasing and the costs down.

Commissioner Guth asked about the property owner to the north and if they are okay with the lack
of a landscape buffer between the properties. Chief Hannig said that there is an existing retaining
wall on the property to the north due to the 8-foot change in elevation between the two properties.
They are planning on putting up a fence along the north of the property.

Commissioner Lucero asked what kind of brick material they will be using. Chief Hannig said it will
have a real red brick exterior.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Doutre said she is fine without the landscape buffer in the northwest
corner of the site. Commissioner Lucero said she would like to see bike parking added to the
alternate parking plan. Commissioner Lewis agreed with Commissioners Doutre and Lucero. He
said it is a good functional design. Commissioner Guth spoke about small details in the design not
being included on all sides of the building. Commissioner Peterson said that it would be preferred
to see renderings that reflect the slope of the site. R. Holley said if there is a drastic change that is
noticeable the design will have to come back to the commission for review. The commission
agreed to amend condition number three to require 20 parking stalls and a bike rack.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson moved to conditionally approve a Design Review and
Conditional Use Permit for PC 22-025 with the amended conditions of approval and findings for
approval as listed below. Commissioner Heare seconded the motion.

Moved: E. Peterson                 Seconded: K. Heare                        Approved: 7-0

Yea: Lucero, Croshaw, Guth, Lewis, Heare, Doutre, Petersen                Nay:            Abstain:


Meeting adjourned at 6:09 p.m.
