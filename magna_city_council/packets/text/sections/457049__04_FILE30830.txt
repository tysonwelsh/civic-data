                                          PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
19.14.030 - Conditional uses.
Conditional uses in the R-1 zones are as follows:

All R-1 zones - Accessory uses and buildings customarily incidental to a conditional use. Any accessory building or buildings
where the total square footage exceeds eight hundred square feet on lots under one half-acre or one thousand two hundred
square feet on lots one-half acre or larger;


Staff has no issues with the proposed garage, it is similar to neighboring accessory structures on adjacent lots and will not
cause any undue or significant impacts on neighboring properties.



                                 PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Planning Staff recommends that the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission approve the Conditional Use Request to
construct a detached accessory building in excess of 800 square feet in the R-1-6 zone in accordance with the proposed site
plan and the following conditions:
    1. Obtain a building permit for construction of the Garage.
    2. Obtain final technical review approval for the plans from the listed reviewing entities and agencies and construct
        in compliance with their stamped and approved plans.
