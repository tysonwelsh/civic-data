PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN THAT THE
BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF UTAH COUNTY, UTAH
WILL HOLD A BUDGET WORK SESSON
IN THE COMMISSIONER CONFERENCE ROOM

51S UNIVERSITY AVE STE 319, PROVO, UT 84601
November 17, 2025 - 10:00 AM

When necessary, board members may participate electronically with the anchor location as stated above.

WORK SESSION

1. WORK SESSION REGARDING THE 2026 UTAH COUNTY BUDGET FOR THE GENERAL
FUND AND OTHER BUDGETARY FUNDS OF UTAH COUNTY, UTAH.

-Aileen Conder, Commission
(Meeting called to order: 10:06 am)

GINA TANNER (Utah County Budget Manager) EXPLAINED THAT THEY WOULD BE
REVIEWING SEVERAL THINGS THEY WERE ASKED TO FOLLOW UP ON AND MAKE
SURE THERE’S NO CONCERNS OR QUESTIONS.

(Timestamp: 3:30)

HEALTH DEPARTMENT BUDGET DISCUSSION, LED BY COMMISSIONER POWERS
GARDNER. THEY SHOULD NOT HAVE A DEFICIT.

(Timestamp: 8:00 )

CLERK DEPARTMENT ASKED IF THEY COULD UPGRADE ON THEIR ELECTIONS
SPECIALIST TO AN ELECTION DATA SPECIALIST INSTEAD OF BEING GIVEN MONEY
FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AND UPGRADING ELECTIONS SPECIALIST ONE.

(Timestamp: 12:00)

HUMAN RESOURCES: DISCUSSION ON 4% RESTRICTED, WITH THE INTENTION OF
2% FOR SALARY AND 2% FOR TARGETED COMPS.

(Timestamp: 18:00)

PROPERTY TAX DISCUSSION BETWEEN COMMISSIONERS AND ROD MANN (Utah
County Auditor)

(Timestamp: 23:45)

RECORDER DISCUSSION - NEW RECORD STORAGE BUILDING, ISF, BUDGETING $2
MILLION. ANDREA ALLEN (Utah County Recorder) DISCUSSED THEIR USAGE OF
RECORDS MANAGEMENT AND LOOKS FORWARD TO WORKING WITH THE
AUDITOR'S DEPARTMENT.

(Timestamp: 33:26)

CIP - RUDY LIVINGTON (Chief Deputy Auditor) AND RICHARD NIELSON (Public Works
Director) TALKED ABOUT UPCOMING PROJECTS. A SUGGESTION TO MOVE THE
CODE BLUE BUILDING REMODEL TO 2027. TALKED ABOUT THE EMISSIONS
BUILDING REMODEL AND MAJOR UPCOMING BUILDING PROJECTS. DISCUSSION
ON BONDS.

(Timestamp: 57:12)

DISCUSSION ON 9.38 MILLION IN SURPLUS FUND. SUGGESTION FROM
COMMISSIONER GARDNER TO PUT $6,363,866 INTO CIP FUND AND $3 MILLION
TO RESTRICTED COMMISSION FUND.

COMMISSIONER GORDON THANKED THE AUDITOR DEPARTMENT FOR ALL OF
THEIR HELP AND SAID THAT THESE BUDGET MEETING ARE VERY HELPFUL.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER: MOTION TO ADJOURN
COMMISSIONER BELTRAN: SECOND

ALL IN FAVOR: AYE

PASSED: 3/0

MEETING ADJOURNED

(Meeting adjourned: 11:38am)
