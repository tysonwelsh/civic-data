 2. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Resolution No. 113 adopting the Annual Budget for the Municipal Building
          Authority of the City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2025.
          Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck oriented the Board regarding the
          resolution to adopt the final budget for FY2025. The Municipal Building Authority
          (MBA) issued debt for the public works building and receives rent from various
          departments to pay off the debt. The debt spans 20 years, with annual payments of $1.8
          million until 2039. Currently the MBA is in the 7th year of principal payments.

           Chairperson Jacob opened the public hearing at 6:03 pm
           Comments:
           None
           Chairperson Jacob closed the public hearing at 6:03 pm
