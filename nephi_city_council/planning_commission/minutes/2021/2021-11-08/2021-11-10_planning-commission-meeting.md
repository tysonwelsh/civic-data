NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION/PUBLIC HEARING
                                    MEETING MINUTES
                                       November 10, 2021
                                          21 E. 100 N.
                                        Nephi, UT 84648
                                           7:00 p.m.
Planning Commission Chairman Ann Petersen called the meeting to order and welcomed all in
attendance.

PRAYER: Commissioner Fran Petersen

PUBLIC HEARING - ZONE CHANGE REQUEST FOR THE MANGELSON PROPERTY:

  Mr. Atkinson explained the purpose of the hearing was to accept public comment on the zone change
request from the Mangelsons for property located approximately 700 north and between first and
second west. The property is zoned R3, and the request is for a zero lot-line overlay that allows twin
homes. Commission Chairman Ann Peterson opened the hearing for public comment and there was no
public comment.

Hearing closed at 7:05 p.m.

PUBLIC HEARING – ZONE CHANGE REQUEST FOR POWELL YARDS:

  Mr. Atkinson explained the purpose of the hearing was to accept public comment on the zone change
request from Jordan Jones on a piece of property located between the Sun Ridge Ranches and Phase
A&B of the Red Cliffs subdivision. The property is zoned R1-H, and the request is to change to R1 zone
which allows for quarter acre minimum lot size. Commission Chairman Ann Peterson opened the
hearing for public comment.
  Mr. Kent Allred introduced himself as an owner of a lot that is adjacent to this proposed subdivision.
He stated that it was his understanding that the lots would be made available for purchase to
homeowners along the street as an extension of the homeowners’ lots. He asked about the one-acre lot
at the north end of the subdivision.
  Property owner Jordan Jones explained to the commission that he plans on keeping the one lot at the
north end to be used by one of his children or himself to build a home someday.

Hearing closed at 7:11 p.m.

MINUTES APPROVED:

  Commissioner Ann Peterson moved to approve the minutes from the planning commission meeting
held on October 13, 2021. Commissioner Karl Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

  Commissioner Thomson moved to approve the minutes from the planning commission meeting held
on October 27, 2021, with an amendment to include Mr. Thomson having made the second to adjourn
the meeting. Commissioner Heather Robertson seconded the motion. The motion carried on a
unanimous vote.

POWELL YARD SUBDIVISION ZONE CHANGE AND PRELIMINARY PLAT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed the unique history of the Powell Yard Subdivision property. The commission
reviewed the development agreement that would be necessary for the development to move forward.
Commissioner Thomson noted that the agreement stated that no primary dwelling could be constructed
and yet there were plans for a primary dwelling to be constructed in lot 1 of the subdivision. Mr.
Atkinson stated that Mr. Thomson was correct and that he would make that change in the agreement.
Mr. Atkinson explained that the development would not comply with the city’s code, but by treating the
development agreement as if it were a separate land use item that goes through the legislative process
as would any other land use change, the subdivision could move forward. A discussion ensued about
many issues with this unique subdivision. Mr. Jordan Jones and Mr. Atkinson answered many questions
that the commissioner voiced.
   Commissioner Brough moved to approve the zone change request from R1-H to the R1 zone.
Commissioner Hancock seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
  Commissioner Thomson moved to approve the preliminary plat for the Powell Yards Subdivision.
Councilor Robertson seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MANGELSON ZONE CHANGE APPROVED:

  Commissioner Hancock moved to approve the zero lot-line overlay on the R3 zoned Mangelson
property. Commissioner Brough seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

VARIANCE PROCESS DISCUSSED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the commission the variance process and discussed the five criteria for a
variance to be granted. He also presented the commission with a summary document of the city zones.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

Commissioner Robertson moved to adjourn the meeting.
Commissioner Thomson seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 8:00 p.m.
