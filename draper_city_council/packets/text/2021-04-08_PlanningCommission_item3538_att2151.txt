BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately
0.99 acres located on the east side of 300 E, at approximately 13785 S 300 E (Exhibit C). The
property is currently zoned RA1. The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map
Amendment be approved to change the zoning of the property from RA1 to RA2
(Residential Agricultural, 20,000 ft² lot minimum size) for the purpose of a future
subdivision (Exhibit F).

The subject property is 0.99 acres and was developed as a single-family residence in
approximately 1985, and is not part of a subdivision.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Low/Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit D). This
category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RA1 zoning classification, supporting approximately
one dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit E). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA1 zone is to “foster low density development with little
impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the
city's semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these
zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by
commercial and industrial uses.” The RA1 zoning abuts the subject property on the north
and west, and RA2 abuts on the east. RM1 (Multi-family Residential, up to 8 dwelling units
per acre) zoning abuts the property on the south.

Zoning Map Amendment. The applicant is proposing to modify the zoning designation to
RA2. The RA2 zone allows for single-family detached units on lots of at least 20,000 square
feet. The properties current land use designation of Residential Low/Medium Density
supports up to two dwelling units per acre, as allowed in the RA2 zone. According to the
DCMC Section 9-8-020, the purpose of the RA2 zone is the same as the RA1 zone, and can
be found above in the General Plan and Zoning section of the report. In addition, the RA2
zone allows for horse and animal rights, similar to the RA1 zone.

Criteria For Approval. A Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion
of the City Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a
recommendation to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the
following factors in Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

        1. Map Amendments:
            a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and
               policies of the city's general plan;
            b. Whether the proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character
               of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property;
            c. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with the standards of any
               applicable overlay zone;
            d. The extent to which the proposed amendment may adversely affect adjacent
               property; and
            e. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject
               property, including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation
               facilities, police and fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems,
               water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection.

Planning Division Review.

   1. No comments.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.

   1. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including but not
      limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm
      water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water and refuse collection;

       Other than noted below, we are not aware of any inadequacies of the facilities intended
       to serve this property.

           a. The increase in density from one residential unit per acre zoning to two residential
              unit per acre zoning constitutes increase in the amount of traffic to and from the
              subject area. One single family residential structure and associated agricultural
              uses constitutes up to nine daily trips and two peak hour trips, based on industry
              standard vehicle trip tables. Based on use tables, This proposed zone change
              would double that traffic on 300 East. However, this increase is not a significant
              increased based on the overall capacity of the major collector, 300 East.

           b. Connectivity with the subject parcel to a public right-of-way is on 300 East. Specific
              access locations will be evaluated with any site plan or subdivision submittal.
              Typically, access location(s) would be required to meet Draper City Standards as
              outlined in our Master Transportation Plan. However, this request would add a
              single driveway to 300 East that would not comply with the spacing requirements.
              This access would be considered low-impact and can include mitigation to reduce
              street impacts. A Traffic Impact Study would not be required with the site plan or
              subdivision application because the traffic impact from the proposed use and
              projected traffic volume are so low.

           c. There are existing storm drainage facilities available fronting the subject area.
              Provisions for onsite storm drainage will need to be addressed with any
              subdivision or site plan application, and shall comply with the provisions of the
              development requirements within the Draper City Municipal Code.
           d. Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by South Valley Sewer District. Any
              subdivision application will require a commitment to serve from the Sewer District
              that facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.

           e. Drinking water facilities will be provided by WaterPro. Any subdivision application
              will require a commitment to serve from the WaterPro that facilities are adequate
              to provide service for the proposed uses.

           Accordingly, we will provide comments relative to the potential construction activities
           when the appropriate application is received. Thank you for the opportunity to review
           the Zone Amendment Request.
