Mayor and Council Informal Meeting

COUNCIL MEETING – 7:00 PM

Call to Order

Mayor Pro Tem Stewart called the Riverton City Council Meeting to order at 7:00 PM and
conducted a roll call. Councilmembers Buroker, McCay, McDougal, and Wells were present.

Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance

Kevin Hicks offered the invocation and Chief Watkins led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Citizen Comment

Mayor Pro Tem Stewart called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public
Comment period.

Consent Items

Mayor Staggs presented the following Consent Agenda:

    a. Minute Approval: October 18, 2022;
    b. Resolution No. 22-70 – Amending Resolution No. 22-68 Establishing Annual Meeting
       Schedules for Riverton City for the Year of 2023;
    c. Resolution No. 22-71 – Declaring the Identified equipment as surplus, authorizing the
       City Manager to dispose of the items as he deems appropriate;
    d. Resolution No. 22-72 – Warranty Bond Release for the Creek at Lover’s Lane (68);
    e. Resolution No. 22-73 – Performance Bond release for Parkside Phase 3 (Plat
       5)(9916); and
    f. Resolution No. 22-74 – Performance Bond release for Parkside Phase 3 (Plat
       6)(10234).

Councilmember McDougal MOVED that the City Council approve the Consent Agenda.
Councilmember Wells SECONDED the motion. Mayor Pro Tem Stewart called for discussion on
the motion. Mayor Pro Tem Stewart called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows:
Buroker-yes, McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, Stewart and Wells-yes.       The motion passed
unanimously.

Public Hearings – N/A

Discussion/Action Items

    Animal Control Discussion

Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, presented language for proposed changes to
the animal bite code. The proposed changes are included with the packet.

Councilmembers and staff discussed various language for the animal bite code. Mr. Lethbridge
will bring this ordinance back at the next City Council meeting for approval.

Upcoming Meetings

    b.   December 13, 2022 - Work Session & City Council Meeting
    c.   January 3, 2023 - Work Session & City Council Meeting
    d.   January 17, 2023 – Work Session & City Council Meeting

Adjournment

Councilmember McCay MOVED to adjourn the City Council Meeting. Councilmember Buroker
SECONDED the motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously. The meeting
adjourned at 7:20 PM.
