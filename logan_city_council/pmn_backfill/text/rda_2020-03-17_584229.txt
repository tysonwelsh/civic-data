254   Presentation: Homelessness in Cache Valley – Jess Lucero, PhD, MSW Local
255   Homeless Coordinating Committee (LHCC) Representative
256
257   Jess Lucero addressed the Council and gave a presentation on Homelessness in Cache
258   Valley. She stated that 1 in 5 renters in our area pay more than 50% of their monthly
259   income on housing.
260
261   2020 Bear River Association of Governments Point in Time Data
262      • 43 heads of households; 16 minor children
263      • Heads of households 62% were male and 38% female
264      • Most common shelter places: cars in big box parking lots, cars in work parking
265         lots, storage units, cars at park-n-rides, 24-hour establishments
266      • For 42% of sample, this was their first time being homeless
267      • On average, the sample had been homeless for approximately 7.5 months during
268         their current spell of homelessness, throughout the median was 3 months

269   Dr. Lucero stated the following comes next:
270       • Regional Homeless Plan is in final stages of development
271       • Local Homeless Coordinating Committee is reorganizing
272       • Need to advocate for decision-making seat at the State Homeless Coordinating
273          Committee
274       • There is a need for state elected officials and bureaucrats to better understand
275          nuances of rural and semi-rural homelessness
276       • Need for local elected officials to advocate for our region

277   Dr. Lucero stated that homelessness does occur in our community and is often times
278   invisible to most of us.
