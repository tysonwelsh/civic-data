                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   ORDINANCE NO. 20-30

ORDINANCE ENACTING A RIVERTON CITY FIRE IMPACT FEE, FIRE FACILITY
                 PLAN, AND RELATED ANALYSIS

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council met in regular session on December 1, 2020, to
consider, among other things, adopting an Impact Fee, Impact Fee Facility Plan, Impact Fee
Facility Analysis, fire suppression, emergency response, and other related matters; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is a political subdivision of the State of Utah, authorized and
organized under the provisions of Utah law and is authorized pursuant to the Impact Fees Act,
Utah Code Ann. § 11-36a-301 et seq.; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has caused a Fire Impact Fee Facilities Plan and Analysis,
prepared by a consultant, a copy of which is attached hereto as Exhibit "A"; and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that Fire Impact Fee
Analysis was prepared in compliance with the Impact Fees Act, and a copy of the Impact Fee
Analysis, together with a summary designed to be understood by a lay person, was made
available to the public, by placing a copy in the Riverton City Public Library in compliance with
the Impact Fees Act; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City, published notice of such Public Hearing to consider the
Impact Fee Facility Plan in the Salt Lake Tribune, Deseret News, the Public Notice Website and
Riverton City’s Website in compliance with the Impact Fees Act; and

       WHEREAS, on December 1, 2020, the Riverton City Council held a Public Hearing
regarding the Impact Fee Facility Plan; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration and review of the comments at the Public
Hearing, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the inhabitants of Riverton City to adopt the findings and recommendations
of the Fire Impact Fee Facilities Plan, accept the conclusions of the Fire Impact Fee Analysis,
and adopt an Ordinance to enact an appropriate impact fee,

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Riverton City Council
as follows:

       Section 1.     The Riverton City Council finds and determines that all required notices
                      have been given and made and public hearings conducted as requested by
                      the Impact Fee Act with respect to the Impact Fee Facilities Plan and this
                      impact fee enactment (this "Ordinance").
Section 2.   The Riverton City Council hereby accepts and adopts the Impact Facilities
             Plan, and related Impact Fee Analysis attached hereto as Exhibit A.

Section 3.   Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 11-36a-402 (1), this ordinance is required to
             contain a provision establishing one or more service areas within the
             political subdivision of Riverton City. The Riverton City Council hereby
             creates one service area within Riverton City which equates to the border
             of Riverton City’s municipal territory.

Section 4.   Pursuant to Utah Code § 11-36a-402 (1), this ordinance is required to
             contain a provision which allows the amount of an impact fee to be
             adjusted to respond to “unusual circumstances in specific cases,” “based
             on studies and data submitted by the developer,” and to respond to a
             “prompt individualized impact fee review for the development activity of
             an agency of the state of Utah, a school district, or a charter school.” The
             Riverton City Council hereby affirms provisions found in Riverton City
             Code Section 18.205.100 comply with the requirements in Utah Code Ann
             § 11-36a-402 (1).
Section 5.   Pursuant to Utah Code § 11-36a-402 (3), this ordinance is required to
             contain a provision which requires a credit against impact fees for “any
             dedication of land for, improvement to, or new construction of system
             improvements” which are ultimately dedicated to the City. The Riverton
             City Council hereby affirms provisions found in Riverton City Code
             Section 18.205.060 comply with the requirements in Utah Code § 11-
             36a402 (3).
Section 6.   Pursuant to Utah Code § 11-36a-402 (1), this ordinance is required to
             contain a provision which allows the amount of an impact fee to be
             adjusted to respond to “unusual circumstances in specific cases,” and to
             respond to a “prompt individualized impact fee review for the
             development activity of an agency of the state of Utah, a school district, or
             a charter school. The Riverton City Council hereby affirms provisions
             found in Riverton City Code Section 18.205.100 comply with the
             requirements in Utah Code Ann Utah Code § 11-36a-402 (1).

Section 7.   Pursuant to Utah Code § 11-36a-402, this ordinance is required to contain
             a provision which includes a schedule of impact fees for each type of
             development activity, or the formula that the political subdivision will use
             to calculate the fee. The Riverton City Council hereby amends Riverton
             City Code Section 18.205.080 by adding thereto the following table and
             related formula:
      FIRE IMPACT FEES
                                          Calls per Unit/SF            Fee per Unit/SF
      Residential – per unit              0.08946                      $355.38
      Nonresidential – per building SF    0.0000562                    $0.22

                    The City Recorder is authorized to incorporate this formula into Riverton
                    City Code Section 18.205080 in a manner consistent with other impact fee
                    schedules/ formulae found in the same, as the City Recorder deems
                    appropriate.

      Section 8. This Ordinance shall be posted no later than December 2, 2020.
