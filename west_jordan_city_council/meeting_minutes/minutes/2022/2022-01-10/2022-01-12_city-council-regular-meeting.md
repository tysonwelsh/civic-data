   CALL TO ORDER
   Chair Jacob called the work session to order at 6:00 p.m. and read a Notice of Declaration regarding
   meetings held electronically due to COVID-19, signed January 7, 2022 and valid for 30 days.

    A.     FY2022 Budget Amendment
   Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck oriented the Council with a proposed amendment to
   the FY2022 Budget to maintain a General Fund balance of approximately $15,000,000. The proposed
   amendment would transfer approximately $7,000,000 for recalculation of allocated services, Police
   Department overtime, transfer of duties, and transfer to Highlands Special Improvement District
   (SID) Fund and Capital Improvement Project (CIP) Fund.

   Related to the proposed transfer to the Highlands SID Fund, Ms. Steck explained the request for an
   additional full-time position in the Parks Department to provide the desired level of service in the
   Highlands SID. She explained residential and commercial assessments going forward.

   Ms. Steck explained proposed amendments to the FY2022 Budget for the CIP Fund and explained a
   budget neutral transfer in and out of the CDBG Fund. The Grants, Water, Sewer, Solid Waste, and
   Storm Water Funds and associated projects were reviewed, and Ms. Steck answered questions from
   the Council. She presented proposed changes to the Consolidated Fee Schedule.

    B.     City Hall Remodel
   Public Services Director Isaac Astill presented proposed changes to the west entrance of City Hall and
   Council Chambers. Responding to a request for feedback regarding the Council Chambers wall
   covering, Councilmember Whitelock encouraged using the least expensive durable option.
   Councilmembers Worthen, McConnehey, and Green expressed a preference for the lower chair-
   height wall covering. The Council provided feedback regarding options for the atrium, and a majority
   of the Council said they liked the proposed redesign of the staircase.

   Council Chair Jacob moved to adjourn the work session at 6:58 pm
   Council Member Bloom seconded the motion, which passed by unanimous vote (7-0).

 6.    REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. Airport Advisory Board Report
Airport Advisory Board Member Greg Baser reported on current initiatives of the Airport Advisory
Board and spoke of the Master Plan for the South Valley Regional Airport. Mr. Baser talked about
the desire of the Board to establish a museum to highlight historical significance of South Valley
Regional Airport and Utah in events such as World War I and II; Korean War; Viet Nam; and the Cold
War. He explained that items for exhibits would primarily be obtained from donations and
commented that a museum could provide educational opportunities and attract people to Jordan
Landing.

Mr. Baser asked for Council input regarding moving forward with obtaining cost estimates and
determining if a museum would truly benefit the community. Vice Chair Green said he liked the
concept and would want to see numbers and an overall proposal for a museum. He commented that
City Council Meeting Minutes – January 12, 2022                                               Page 4

the history of flight in West Jordan started six years after the Wright Brothers’ historic flight with
LR Culver.

Council Member McConnehey said he liked the idea and agreed there was a lot of history with flight
in West Jordan. He encouraged the Council to meet with Richard Myer for more information. Council
Member McConnehey said he would want more information regarding what would be requested
from the City, what would be asked of the City Museum, and information about staffing and
volunteer efforts. Vice Chair Green said he agreed. He encouraged Mr. Baser to contact the Utah
National Guard as a potential partner.
