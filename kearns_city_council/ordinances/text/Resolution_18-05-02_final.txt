A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL SUPPORTING SALT
LAKE COUNTY’S IMPOSITION OF THE .25 PERCENT LOCAL OPTION SALES TAX
DEDICATED TO TRANSPORTATION PER SB 136 (2018).
RECITALS

A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns Township) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 ef seg.

B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a county pursuant to UCA Title 17 - COUNTIES.

D. In 2018, the State Legislature passed SB136 authorizing counties to adopt a .25 percent local
option sales tax dedicated to transportation.

E. On April 24, 2018 Salt Lake County imposed the .25 percent local option sales tax dedicated
to transportation, subject to the requirement municipalities and their councils, representing 67
percent of the County's population, pass resolutions of support for imposing the tax before
June 30, 2018.

F. Given the benefits to Kearns for possible funding to build the identified Kearns Bicycle
Transportation Plan, improving transit operations, and performing timely road repairs,
maintenance, and construction, it is in the best interest of the residents of Kearns to pass a
resolution of support for Salt Lake County imposing the .25 percent local option sales tax
prior to the June 30 deadline.

RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Kearns Metro Township Council approves
this resolution of support for Salt Lake County’s imposing of the .25 percent local option sales
tax dedicated to transportation.
