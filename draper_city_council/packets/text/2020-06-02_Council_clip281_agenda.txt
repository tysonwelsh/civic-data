CITY COUNCIL AGENDA Notice is hereby given that the Draper City Council will hold a Business Meeting on Tuesday, June 2, 2020, at 6:00 PM,  in the City Council Chambers at 1020 East Pioneer Road, Draper, Utah.   The Agenda will be as follows: Electronic Meeting This meeting will be held electronically. This meeting will be an electronic meeting according to Draper City Resolution 14-54. As restrictions are in place across the State limiting public gatherings, we want to be sure everyone interested in participating  in City Council meetings can do so in a manner they feel most comfortable.
Listen through the Draper City website: https://www.draper.ut.us/95/Agendas-Minutes
Email your comments to public.comment@draper.ut.us by 5 PM on the day of the meeting. These will become part of the public record.
If you wish to speak during the Public Hearing portion of the meeting, please send a request for the Zoom meeting ID to public.comment@draper.ut.us by noon, June 2, 2020.
