3. BUSINESS ITEMS
   3.1     DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Waters Edge Subordination Agreement
       City Manager Jacob McHargue will present a subordination agreement for the 2021
       bonds. The RDA Board will take appropriate action.

Mr. McHargue stated that in 2014 the RDA entered into a reimbursement agreement with Waters
Edge and that they had installed most of the infrastructure for their development. He explained
the need for the subordination agreement and how it worked.

David Robertson with Lewis Young, Robertson and Burningham commented that this put the city
in a good position to move forward and to continue to improve the financial position of the city.

Mr. McHargue added that the RDA Board would sign the agreement today and then it would go
on the consent agenda for City Council approval and final signature.

Chair Fullmer called for a motion.
