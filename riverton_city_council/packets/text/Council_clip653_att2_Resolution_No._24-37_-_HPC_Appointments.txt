                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-37

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBER TO THE HISTORIC PRESERVATION
                          COMMISSION

        WHEREAS, on June 14, 2016, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 16-11
which provides for the preservation of historic structures and other assets located within Riverton
City; and

       WHEREAS, Administration of Ordinance No. 16-11 requires appointment of a Historic
Preservation Commission; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the following volunteer
appointment of Alex Johnson; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Alex Johnson is a
resident of Riverton City, and is willing and capable of serving on the Historic Preservation
Commission;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of Alex
          Johnson to serve on the Historic Preservation Commission, which term shall end on
          January 31, 2028.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
April 2024 by the following vote:
