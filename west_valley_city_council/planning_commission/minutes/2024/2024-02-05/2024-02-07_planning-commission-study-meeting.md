ZT-1-2024     Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting amendments to Sections 7-1-109, 7-2-
              117, 7-10-103, 7-13-517, and 7-14-102 of the zoning ordinance to increase the
              construction threshold that triggers certain improvements and to allow the option of
              payment in lieu of installation of off-site improvements. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
              963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal would make two changes to the zoning
              ordinance. He noted that since the 1980s, the ordinance states that if a property
              owner improves their site or an existing structure and costs over $50,000, it
              triggers specific street improvements to be added. Steve pointed out that if we
              were to count for inflation, that $50,000 value would be closer to $150,000 today.
              So, staff is suggesting changing the trigger amount to $150,000.
              Steve said the second change is regarding alternatives for improvement
              installation. There are two options if improvements are triggered: bonding or a
              delay agreement. Steve noted the delay agreement would state that the
              improvements do not need to be added now, but when the City calls in the
              agreement, they would need to be added. Steve said there are a couple of issues
              with the approach of a delay agreement. First, the Council has been reluctant to
              approve delay agreements over the past few years. Second, it can become
              complicated when our Public Works Department calls in an agreement, and there
              have been multiple owners since the development agreement was written. Steve
              said that what is being proposed would be a third option, payment instead of the
              improvements, giving the developer the opportunity to pay the City the cost of the
              improvements, which would be held in reserve for improvements and put toward
              a future City project. Steve noted that this option would be at the discretion of the
              City.
              Commissioner Lovato asked how many property owners would be affected by the
              increase in the trigger amount. Steve said this comes up often when improving the
              inside of a building, adding an addition, or adding a new structure. He pointed out
              that we want to ensure the improvements required by the City are proportional to
              the impact on the City, and raising the threshold is more likely to impact the City.
              Commissioner Lovato asked if there are times when a property owner states that
              the needed improvement costs $30,000 and pays more because their
              improvements are now part of a larger project.
              Steve explained that we have situations where landscaping isn’t maintained or
              some has been removed, and the City would enforce those problems. He said if
              you are looking at an older site and they are not meeting current standards, that is
              when the threshold would come into play.

Z-1-2024      Petition by GUY WILLIAMS requesting a zone change from LI (Light Industrial) to M
              (Manufacturing). The property is located at 2272 and 2296 South 5600 West on 3.65
              acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that this zone change request, from LI to M, involves
              two parcels that are located within the 5600 west overlay zone. He noted the
              existing structure is occupied by four different businesses. Steve mentioned that
              there is Apex Wraps which does vehicle wrapping. You have commercial truck
              and van equipment, sales and installation, and then a distributed van upfitting
              part. Steve stated that the applicant would like to expand the building to the south
              and incorporate outside storage.
              Steve noted the reasons they are asking for the change. First, outside storage is not
              permitted in the light industrial zone and they want storage on the north and the
              south parcel, which is only permitted in the manufacturing zone. Second, they are
              wanting to offer Auto services and the manufacturing zone is a better zone for that
              use.
              Steve presented a map and pointed out what properties have an active business
              license, and which do not. He noted that some of the property depicted is vacant.
              He presented the concept plan and pointed out that the smaller buildings on the
              south portion of the property would be demolished and the larger building would
              be expanded. Steve stated that staff is recommending the existing access close to
              the intersection be closed. He said there is landscaping and streetlights that need
              to be installed. Steve explained that there are several issues needing to be
              addressed as they exist on the site that need to be cleaned up as they exist on the
              site and those will be outlined in the staff report. He stated that the applicant is
              asking for an exception on the setback for the building on the southern parcel.
              Steve said that they are not moving the location of the structure they would be
              expanding the existing structure. However, they would like the building to follow
              the existing south wall line.
              Steve pointed out that that there is a possibility Clifford Street will be vacated, if
              it is vacated some improvement would need to be installed. He explained that if
              the vacation doesn’t happen full improvements would need to be added.
              Commissioner McEwen asked if it would be appropriate to continue this
              application until after the ruling from the City Council on the property to the
              West? Steve said the Commission could vote to continue next week. I'll leave that
              up to the Commission's discretion.
              Commissioner Lovato asked if they would be open to adding additional screening
              for the outside storage. Steve said if the Commission is supportive of the zone
              change, staff is suggesting there be a development agreement that covers things
              like, fixing any deficiencies, putting in the improvements, screening the outside
              storage. So, yes, the outside storage existing as well as new, would be screened
              with a solid type screening.
