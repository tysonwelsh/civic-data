I would like to nominate Jeff Steiner for employee of the month. Jeff has worked at the
Sheriff’s Office for 17 years. Over that time Jeff has work in Corrections,
Transportation, and Judicial. Jeff is currently assigned as a Bailiff. Jeff has never been
the type to just get by. He constantly goes out of his way to find things to do and help his
teammates. Jeff is always the first one to volunteer for any transport, anywhere in the
state. He actively works in his courtroom assisting Attorneys with exhibits and other
court procedures. After watching Jeff Working in his courtroom it is clear a desk and
chair are wasted on him as he never takes a moment to sit down. Jeff continually
monitors his judges schedule and often changes his personal days off to better cover the
needs of the court and to prevent extra workload on teammates. Jeff often plans and
presents trainings on building security, handcuffing and restraining inmates, and any
other topic he can dream up, including the proper way to eat an orange. Any time an
extra assignment is offered Jeff is the first to jump at it. He is truly on of the hardest
workers at the Sheriff's Office.

On top of his exemplary work ethic, Jeff is a pleasure to interact with. Moring shift
briefings are often turned to him and banter between he and coworkers. Jeff is not one
to shy away from banter and often builds upon it at his own expense to lighten the
mood. Jeff is one person you would be hard pressed to hear say a harsh word towards
anyone even if it is observed.

For those reasons I would like to nominate Jeff for employee of the month. ~Mat
Farnsworth~

JEFF STEINER - ‘MOST PEOPLE WHERE I WORK HAVE ACHIEVED THAT GOAL AS
EASILYAS I DID WITHOUT A DOUBT. IT’S AN HONOR AND A PRIVILEGE TO BE
ABLE TO WORK I THE SHERIFF’S DEPARTMENT IN THE COUNTY AND I'VE
ENJOYED IT A LOT AND I HOPE TO ENJOY IT A LONG LONGER.’
