Council Member Business

                     Council Member Robinson thanked the Police Department staff for their help with a recent
                     community meeting.

                     Council Member Houseman acknowledged the Police Officers and residents who helped
                     a young boy find his way home. She also spoke about the mental health and wellness
                     resources sponsored by Canyons School District.

                     Council Member Zoltanski recognized the Sandy legislators and staff for their
                     commitment and service to Sandy City. She asked Administration to conduct a survey to
                     gather resident feedback regarding the Mountain Transportation Plan options.

                     Council Member Stroud thanked the Police, Fire and Community Development staff for
                     their help recently. She also thanked Dustin Fratto for his help with a recent community
                     meeting.

Mayor's Report

                     Mayor Bradburn spoke about HB 284. He updated the Council on current state-wide
                     Covid restrictions and he provided information on the metrics used by the state as a guide
                     in their decision-making to ease restrictions.

CAO Report

                     Matt Huish, CAO, spoke about the Emergency Communication test this Friday. Parks
                     and Recreation department staff is preparing for spring and summer season. He updated
                     the Council on the Kuwahara rezone and the Farnsworth Farms and Thackeray
                     developments.

Legislative Report

                     Evelyn Everton, Deputy Mayor, provided a summary of the legislation passed this year.
                     She thanked the Council Members for their help during the Legislative session. Nick
                     Duerksen provided additional information and updates. Lynn Pace acknowledged Ms.
                     Everton for her work this legislative session.

           5975(CC)    #ZONE-01-21-5975, Coppercreek Rezone) submitted by Utah
                       Development Group, requesting that 0.62 acres of a 1.02 acre parcel
                       located at 1368 E. Coppercreek Road be rezoned to the RM(10) Zone.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Neighborhood Meeting Summary
                                  Planning Commission Staff Report
                                  Planning Commission Minutes-draft (2/18/21)

                                  Jake Warner, Long Range Planning Manager, presented a rezone application submitted
                                  by Utah Development Group for a parcel located at approximately 1368 Coppercreek
                                  Road. The Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation, by a vote of 4 - 2,
                                  to the Council, for review and approval. Ben Smith, with Utah Development Group spoke
                                  to Council about the proposed development.

                                  Council questions and comments occurred throughout the presentation.

                                  Public Comment:
                                  Chair Sharkey invited the public to comment. Dustin Fratto provided instruction.

                                  Ms. Julie Hepworth addressed her concerns with the proposed density of the
                                  development and the increased traffic associated with the project. She does not support
                                  the rezone.

                                  Mr. Tom Lewis expressed concerns with the removal of no parking signs in the area and
                                  the increase in traffic cutting through the neighborhood. He would like to see floor plans
                                  and more development details. He requested the Council to deny the rezone application.

                                  Ms. Karen Conder asked the Council to oppose the rezone application. She expressed
                                  concerns with traffic, guest parking and waste removal.

                                  Ms. Heather Meier and Mr. Dennis Haitt think the parcel is too small for the proposed
                                  development. They are concerned with the increased traffic and the density of the
                                  proposed development. They are opposed to the rezone and proposed development and
                                  asked the Council to vote against the rezone.

                                  Mr. Cody Kofoed is opposed to the project and would like to see single family homes on
                                  this parcel which is consistent with the rest of the neighborhood.

                                  Ms. Debra Stoker is opposed to the development because of the high density and
                                  concerns with increased traffic. She would like single family homes developed on this
                                  parcel.

                                  Public Comment Closed.

                                  Council discussion and questions followed.

                                  Following the vote on the motion, the Council moved to Item 5 on the Agenda: Time
