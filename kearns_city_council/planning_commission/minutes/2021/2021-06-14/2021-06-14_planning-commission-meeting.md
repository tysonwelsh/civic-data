                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                        Hearings began at – 5:34 p.m.

OAM2021-000342 - Proposed Land Use Ordinance Amendment for permitted and conditional uses in the
C-2, Commercial Zone to conform with the Kearns General Plan. Presenter: Travis Hair and Nathan
Bracken (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed Land Use Ordinance change after taking comments
from the public during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning
commission’s rules of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be
limited to three minutes per person.

Mr. Hair provided the changes. Mr. Bracken said the general plan is a lens to update the code and the
commercial zone is consistent.

Commissioners and Staff had a brief discussion regarding the effective date of the ordinance and Councils
discretion and authority.

Commissioner Nelson motioned to open the public hearing.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Kearns Community Council
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
Comments: Mr. Snow asked if there is ground base floor commercial, is there a limit to amount of stories
above. General plan has four story possibilities in the industrial area. Kearns Community Council gives
favorable recommendation.

Mr. Hair said currently not, this is an immediate fix, anticipate a fixed standard in the future. Mr. Bracken
said there is maximum density in the general plan and hard time attracting commercial only and need
robust residential.

Commissioner Koester motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Taylor seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend approval of file #OAM2021-000342 to the Kearns Metro Township Council for
adopting the Proposed Land Use Ordinance Amendment for permitted and conditional uses in the C-2,
Commercial Zone to conform with the Kearns General Plan. With provision for the Metro Township to
review the resolution # 3 to keep, change to immediately or expedite to 7 days.
       Motion by: Commissioner Taylor
       2nd by: Commissioner Koester
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
