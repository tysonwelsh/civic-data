EMIGRATION CANYON
ORDINANCE # 2025-0-19
AN ORDINANCE BUDGETING BONUS TO MAYOR SMOLKA

WHEREAS, Joe Smolka has dedicated nearly a decade of distinguished service to the
residents of Emigration Canyon, beginning with his election to the Metro Township Council in
2016 and his selection as Council Chair and Mayor in 2017; and

WHEREAS, under Mayor Smolka's steadfast leadership, Emigration Canyon successfully
navigated its transition from a Metro Township to a full city pursuant to Utah House Bill 35
(effective May 1, 2024), enhancing local governance powers while preserving the community's
unique rural and historic character; and

WHEREAS, Mayor Smolka has prioritized public safety and wildfire mitigation in this
fire-prone canyon, championing Firewise programs, community chipping events, fire restrictions,
and collaborations with the Unified Fire Authority and Unified Police Department to protect lives
and property; and

WHEREAS, he has advanced critical infrastructure improvements, including road
resurfacing and safety upgrades on Emigration Canyon Road, pavement maintenance projects, and
participation in regional transportation planning through the Wasatch Front Regional Council; and

WHEREAS, Mayor Smolka has guided thoughtful land use reforms, including
modernization of zoning ordinances and commercial development codes in 2025 to streamline
processes while safeguarding environmental and agricultural resources; and

WHEREAS, as a Trustee on the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Board, he
has effectively represented Emigration Canyon's interests in regional services, fostering
cooperation on water, emergency response, and community needs; and

WHEREAS, throughout his tenure, Mayor Smolka has promoted community engagement,
environmental stewardship, and resident-focused governance, earning the respect and gratitude of
the Emigration Canyon community.

WHEREAS, Utah law 10-3-818 provides that the governing body may fix compensation
for elected officials for their services through budgeted bonuses or incentive pay pursuant to
ordinance enacted by the legislative body following a public hearing;

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED, that the Emigration Canyon Council hereby
expresses its profound appreciation to Mayor Joe Smolka for his longstanding dedication, visionary
leadership, and outstanding contributions to the well-being and preservation of Emigration Canyon
with the following:
