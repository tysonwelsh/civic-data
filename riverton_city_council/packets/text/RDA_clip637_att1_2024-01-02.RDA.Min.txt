24   Call to Order
25
26   Chair Staggs called the Riverton Redevelopment Agency Board Meeting to order at 6:38 PM
27   and conducted a roll call. Board members Buroker, Haymond, McCay, McDougal, and Pierucci
28   were present.
29
30   Discussion/Action
31
32          Minute Approval: December 6, 2023
33
34   Board Member McCay MOVED that the City Council approve the minutes from December
35   6, 2023. Board Member McDougal SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion
36   on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows: Board
37   Members Buroker-Yes, Haymond-yes, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, and Pierucci-Yes. The
38   motion passed unanimously.
39
40   Closed Session
41
42      Discussion of the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real property, including any
43      form of a water rights or water share.
44
45   At 6:39 PM, Board Member McCay MOVED to suspend the Redevelopment Agency Meeting and
46   convene in a Closed Session to discuss the sale, purchase, exchange or lease of real property,
47   including any form of a water rights or water share. Board Member McDougal SECONDED the
48   motion. Chair Staggs called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for a roll-
49   call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, Haymond-yes, McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, and
50   Pierucci-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
