2. WRITTEN DETERMINATION 2021-24 Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. 52-4-207(4)
   I, Andrew Adams, do hereby determine conducting an electronic meeting of the Draper City
   Planning Commission with an anchor location presents a substantial risk to the health and
   safety of those who may be present at the anchor location and hereby authorize the Draper City
   Planning Commission to conduct electronic meetings without an anchor location.
B.     The foregoing determination is based on the following facts:
   • Federal, state and local leaders, including the Draper City Mayor and City Council, have
       all recognized a global pandemic caused by the spread of the COVID-19 virus.
   • While COVID-19 cases and hospitalizations in Draper show an encouraging downward
       trend and vaccines are becoming available to a larger segment of the Draper population,
       vigilance is required so that there isn’t a rebound in cases and hospitalizations.

       Travis Van Ekelenburg presented. He stated that the applicant has requested a conditional
       use permit. He stated that it is in a residential medium density designation as well as in the
       RA2 zoning. He stated that the applicant will be using small room as a florest and a

6.     Public Hearing:           Michel Fence – Conditional Use Permit Request
       (Administrative Action)
       A request by Ben Michel for approval to increase fence height to eight feet for a portion of
       the rear lot lines on the east and west sides of the property. The property is located at
       approximately 1257 E. Manfield Way in the RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square
       foot minimum Zone). Application: USE-048-2021, Staff Contact: Maryann Pickering at
       801-576-6391 or maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

       Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager and Zoning Administrator, presented. She stated
       that the property is located within the RA1 zone just sound of Draper Park. She stated that
       the applicant looking to install fencing around the property. She stated that it complies with
       the code.

       Chairperson Adams stated that shouldn’t change anything for the request, but Staff will fix
       that detail. He asked if there were any questions for the applicant.

7.     Public Hearing: Salz Cove Lot 3 Plat Amendment (Administrative Action)
       On request by Eric and Kimberly Rea approval of a Plat Amendment in the RA2 zone
       located at 12926 S. Salz Way, regarding decreasing the storm drain easement from 20 feet
       to 10 feet for Lot 3 of the Salz Cove Subdivision. Application SUBD-0032-2021. Staff
       contact: Travis Van Ekelenburg, 801-576-6522, travis.vanekelenburg@draperutah.gov.

       Travis Van Ekelenburg, City Planner, presented. He stated that the property is low to
       medium density and it’s in the RA2 zoning designation. He stated that the original plat had
       the easement at 20 feet and the proposed plat would only change the rear property
       alignment where the easement is from 20 feet to 10 feet.

       Commissioner Fowler asked who the easement is granted to.

       Brien Maxfield, City Engineer, stated that the developer put in some very wide swells and
       that required they be in drainage easements. He stated that the owner had a pipe put in
       rather than the swell, which allowed the easement to be reduced from 20 feet to 10 feet. He
       stated that staff agrees that it can be reduced.

       Chairperson Adams asked if there were any more questions.

       Eric Rea, applicant, stated that he doesn’t have anything to add.
