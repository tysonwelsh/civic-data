) Consideration of certifying the September 5, 2023, Municipal Primary
Election Results
A memo from the City Recorder came before the Board to consider certifying the September 5, 2023, Municipal Primary
Election results. The memo stated Section 20A, Chapter 4 of the Utah State Code sets forth the election review process, designates
the legislative body — the City Council — and the Mayor as the Board of Canvassers and outlines the Board’s duties for municipal
elections. The City Recorder acts as the election official. The Board of Canvassers is responsible for certifying the official election
results of municipal elections. Canvassing of retums for a Primary Election must occur no sooner than seven (7) days after the
election and no later than fourteen (14) days after the election. After reviewing the returns, the Board must certify the results of
the election and file a report that includes the following information:
1. The total number of votes cast in the Board’s jurisdiction; ,
2. The name of each candidate whose name appeared on the ballot; |
3. Each office that appears on the ballot;
4. The number of votes for each candidate;
5. The total number of votes given in the Board’s jurisdiction to each candidate;
6. The number of ballots that were rejected; and
7. A statement certifying that the information contained in the report is accurate.
City Recorder Hansen summarized the memo and reviewed the report of unofficial election results dated September 13,
2023. The seats to be filled in the 2023 Municipal Election are Ogden City Mayor, At Large Seat C, and City Council District 4.
Candidates for the position of Mayor are Taylor Knuth, Ben Nadolski, Bart E. Blair, Angel Castillo, Jon J. Greiner, Oscar Landon
Mata, and Chris Barragan. Taylor Knuth and Ben Nadolski received the highest number of votes, 2,073 and 1,887 respectively,
and will advance to the General Election. Candidates for the position of At-Large Seat C were Shaun Myers, Lara Gale, J. Levi
Andersen, and John H. Thompson; however, Lara Gale withdrew from the election. Shaun Myers and J. Levi Andersen received
the highest number of votes,4,426 and 1,595 respectively, and will advance to the General Election. Candidates for City Council
District 4 were Steven Van Wagoner, Dave Graf, Sebastian Benitez, Alexander Castagno, and Austin Raymond. Steven Van
Wagoner and Dave Graf received the highest number of votes, 1,461 and 1,384 respectively, and will advance to the General
_ Election. She then noted the total number of ballots cast was 10,391 and the City has 32,083 registered voters; this equates to a
) 32.39 percent voter turnout, She reviewed the report regarding the number of ballots that were disqualified and cited the reasons
~-“ for disqualification, noting that voters are notified of the potential for their ballot to be disqualified and given time to rectify the
issue from which the disqualification would result. She concluded that it is her recommendation that the Board certify the results
of the September 5, 2023 Municipal Primary Election.
