RDA CONSENT ITEMS
Approval of May 14, 2024 RDA Minutes
Mr. Macdonald moved to approve the May 14, 2024 RDA minutes, seconded by Mr. Killpack.
Those voting yes: David Young, LaNae Millett, David Spencer, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald,
Jenn Gale and Chris Killpack. The motion passed.
RDA SCHEDULED ITEMS
Public Hearing - Resolution - Adopt the RDA of the City of Orem FY 2024-2025 Budget
and Amend the FY 2023-2024 Budget Presenter: Brandon Nelson, CPA, Finance Director

Mr. Nelson explained the Redevelopment Agency (RDA) is a separate legal entity. He
presented the RDA budget for 2024-2025 which includes the following budget items: University
Place CDA incentive for $1,192,500, future projects - CDA $66,250, contributions to other funds
- CDA $66,250, and project area totals $65,000. This total is $1,390,000.
PUBLIC HEARING OPENS 7:48 PM

No comment
PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 7:48 PM
Ms. Gale moved as the board of directors of the RDA to approve and adopt by resolution the
RDA FY 2024-2025 budget and amend the FY 2023-2024 budget. seconded by Mr. Macdonald.
Those voting yes: David Young, LaNae Millett, David Spencer, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald,
and Jenn Gale. The motion passed.
