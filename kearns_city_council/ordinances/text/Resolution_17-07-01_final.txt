A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
AUTHORIZING ACCEPTANCE OF APPROXIMATELY 1.5 MILES OF ROADS TO
BE CONSTRUCTED IN THE CAMP KEARNS INDUSTRIAL PARK
RECITALS
A. The Kearns Metro Township (the "Metro Township") is a Metro Township pursuant to
Utah Code Ann. §§10-2a-401 et seq.
B, Salt Lake County (the "County") is a county existing pursuant to Article XI, Section 1 of
the Utah Constitution.
C. The Metro Township assumed ownership of all roads in its jurisdiction on or about
January 1, 2017.
ry. The County has been working for several years to clarify the right of way for roads in the
Camp Kearns area as shown on the attached map (“Attachment A”) and intends to construct the
toads shown thereon (the “Project” and “Project Roads”) during the construction season of 2017-
2018.
E. The Metro Township desires, upon completion of the Project, to take over ownership of
the Project Roads and the County desires to dedicate over the completed Project Roads to the
Metro Township upon completion of the Project.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council
that it will accept ownership of the Project Roads, after completion of the Project, upon the
dedication of the same by the County to the Metro Township.
