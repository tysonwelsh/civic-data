SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 0.32 acres located on the west side of Maple Park Court, at approximately
14684 S. Maple Park Court (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RM and is
part of the development agreement for the South Mountain Planned Unit Development, as
amended. The applicant is requesting to amend the common boundary line between two
adjoining properties.

The Ridgeway subdivision was originally recorded in March 2004. This application is the
first amendment to the recorded plat. The two lots affected by this amendment are lots
102 and 104.

The property is assigned the RM zoning classification, supporting approximately twelve (12)
dwelling units per acre (Exhibit D). The RM zoning classification is not found within the
current zoning ordinance. The South Mountain Development Agreement ties this property
to the 1999 Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC). According to the 1999 DCMC Section 9-4-
030 the purpose of the RM zone is to “to provide areas for low-to-medium residential
density with the opportunity for varied housing styles and character, providing for a
maximum density of up to twelve (12) units per acre for medium to high density residential
unit project subject to conditional-use permit procedures and conditions of this type of use
and based on minimum development guidelines adopted by the City”.

Subdivision Plat. The applicant is requesting to adjust the lot line between the two
properties in the existing subdivision (Exhibit F). The lot line will be adjusted angularly with
no change to the shared lot corner on the north, and a southwest shift of the shared lot
corner on the south by approximately 30 feet. Both lots will continue to meet the
minimum lot size requirements for the South Mountain Development Agreement
Guidelines. Lot 136 (formally Lot 102) will be approximately 14,893 square feet or 0.34
acres. Lot 137 (formally Lot 104) will be approximately 21,157 square feet or 0.48 acres.
The proposed plat amendment (Exhibit G) will add approximately 1,169 square feet to Lot
136. The public utility easements (PUE) between the two lots will also shift with the
updated lot line.
