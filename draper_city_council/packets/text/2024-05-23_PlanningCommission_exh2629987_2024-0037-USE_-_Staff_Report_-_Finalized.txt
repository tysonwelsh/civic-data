BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for
approximately 0.09 acres located on the west side of Rockwell View Ln., at approximately
13948 S Rockwell View Ln. (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned RM2. The
applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to
allow for a home-based Personal Care Service business that provides teeth whitening
services.

The property is part of the Rockwell Square Subdivision and a detached single-family
dwelling was constructed on the property in 2020.

The home occupation is proposed to operate from 4:00 pm to 8:00 pm Monday through
Saturday, with one Saturday per month between 8:00 am and 6:00 pm. The business will be
in the basement of the home with access to the business provided by a basement entry on
the side of the home. The applicant has provided a detailed overview of the businesses
operations which is attached in Exhibit G.

The applicant alluded in their responses to a potential negative that having customers
come to the home could create with regards to parking. Their proposed mitigation is to
limit customers to one at a time and to require that the customers park on their driveway.
Staff supports this approach as an effective mitigation. As the roadways are narrow and
privately owned, on-street parking is significantly limited in the neighborhood. Additionally,
given the small lot size, expansion of the driveway area to accommodate additional on site-
parking is not an available option. Staff also has identified the potential for additional
negative impact to parking if a non-resident employee was permitted as part of the
business, and has added to the suggested conditions section of this report that non-
resident employees not be permitted as an additional mitigation measure. In addition to
these mitigations, staff has included other suggested conditions to help ensure that the
business operations continue to be within the limitations of the standards in ordinance
that were agreed to in the applicant’s statements.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a home occupation conditional use permit
request is found in Section 9-5-080(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of
review for such requests as:

        E. Approval Standards: The standards of this subsection shall apply to the issuance of a
            conditional use permit:
         1. A conditional use permit may be issued for a use to be located within a zone where
            the particular conditional use is allowed by the use regulations of the zone.
         2. Reasonable conditions may be imposed as necessary to substantially mitigate
            reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. These conditions may
            include conditions concerning use, construction, character, location, landscaping,
            screening, parking, hours of operation, and other matters relating to the purposes
            and objectives of this section. Such conditions shall be expressly set forth in the
            motion authorizing the conditional use permit.
         3. If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use
            cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to
            achieve compliance with applicable standards, the conditional use may be denied.

teeth whiter, improving the teeth appearance. It produces brilliant results that can transform
smile and personality.
