Members, and requested a motion to adjust the agenda to move the public comment period to
the beginning.

2. Public Comments

Misty Riley, residing at 1250 East Forget Me Not Avenue, expressed concerns about
two dogs that had been roaming at large in the neighborhood for approximately two years. She
identified the dogs as a Rottweiler and a German Shepherd belonging to her neighbors located
two doors east of her residence. Ms. Riley stated that she had filed multiple complaints both
online and by phone and had interacted with seven different animal control officers regarding
the issue. She emphasized her concern for the safety of neighborhood children, noting that the

dogs were frequently observed in the area during times when children were getting off the
school bus.

2. Financial Report

Dave Sanderson, Financial Manager, presented the financial report for September and
October 2024.

Council Member Shelton, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to approve
the financial report. The motion passed unanimously.

3. Unified Fire Authority Report

Battalion Chief Kenneth Aldridge provided an update on Recruit Camp, a report on the
recent UFA awards ceremony, and an update on the Utah Task Force 1 deployment. Chief
Aldridge announced that UFA would be hosting a Battalion Chief Academy and reported on
recent promotions.

3.1. Sandy Fire Quarterly Report

Sandy Fire Chief Ryan McConaghie presented the quarterly fire and medical
incident report, provided a summary of the recent Sandy Fire annual banquet, and
extended an invitation to the Council for an upcoming event. Chief McConaghie
reported that Fire Station 31 was scheduled for completion in October 2025, with a
public walk-through planned for September 2025.

4. Unified Police Department Report

Detective Josh Smith reported on the call volume for October 2024. Chief April Morse
provided an update on the Drug Enforcement Unit (DEU), noting its active presence in White
City and commending its performance. Chief Morse informed the Council that retired K-9 Nitro
had passed away in October. Mayor Flint reported that streetlights on Sego Lily Drive were

now operational. Chief Morse also stated that the Unified Police Department (UPD) had
applied for a Utah Highway Safety Grant to fund traffic control devices.

5. Public Comments
The public comment period was moved to an earlier point in the meeting.

6.2 Resolution No. 2024-11-01 Establishing a Time and Place for holding
Regular City Council Meetings for the 2025 Calendar Year

Council Member Cardenaz, seconded by Council Member Huish, moved to
approve Resolution No. 2024-11-01, changing the July 3, 2025, meeting date to
July 10, 2025. The motion passed by unanimous vote.

6.3. Resolution No. 2024-11-02 Approving a 2025 Tax Rate in Excess of the
Certified Tax Rate by the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area
(SLVLESA)

Paul Ashton, Legal Counsel, stated that a proposed SLVLESA tax rate increase
was explained at a previous Council meeting. Mr. Ashton answered questions from the
Council. Council Member Cardenaz expressed the opinion that implementing the
proposed increase through the district seemed like the best way to get the money
needed to help the most people.

Council Member Cardenaz, seconded by Council Member Shelton,
motioned to approve Resolution No. 2024-11-02 approving a 2025 Tax Rate in
Excess of the Certified Tax Rate by the SLVLESA. The motion passed by
unanimous vote.

6.4 Discussion and Possible Action regarding Animal Control Ordinance and
Resident Complaints

Mr. Ashton said existing ordinances were not adequate to meet the needs of the
city with respect to animal control. He said staff could work on rewriting Title 12 and
Title 8, as well as adding an administrative law judge (ALJ) process to more effectively
enforce ordinances and maintain public safety.

Council Member Shelton, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to
direct staff to proceed drafting amendments to Title 12 and Title 8 as discussed.
The motion passed by unanimous vote.

7. Administrator Report

City Administrator Rori Andreason said she emailed information to the Council
regarding Streamline, a company specializing in websites for special districts. She said she
liked the Streamline website and was impressed with the accessibility. The monthly price with
Streamline would be $800, which was under the threshold for requiring a Request for Proposal
(RFP). If the Council liked what Streamline offered, staff could proceed with Streamline,
otherwise, an RFP could be prepared. The Council discussed inviting representatives of
Streamline to White City for discussions. Council Member Shelton commented that customer
service was a big, important part of the service.

8.1. Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District/Council of Governments

Daniel Torres, the Economic Development Manager, reported that a survey
about accessory dwelling units (ADUs) had been distributed, receiving a higher-than-
anticipated response from the community. Torres also noted that the Request for
Qualifications (RFQ) for the civic center was scheduled to close on November 15, 2024.

Chad Anderson, Engineer, provided an update on the Sego Lily Project. Mayor
Flint remarked that a storm drain on Sego Lily Drive had sunk. Anderson stated that he
would investigate the issue immediately following the meeting.

Mayor Flint provided an update on the new location for MSD. She said White City
needed to have preliminary budget information to MSD by February 28, 2025. Mayor
Flint emphasized that White City received 100% of sales tax revenue from online orders
with point of delivery in White City.

8.2 Unified Fire Authority/Unified Fire Service Area

Council Member Huish provided an update regarding UFA/UFSA budget and tax
discussions.

8.3 Mosquito Abatement/Salt Lake County Animal Control

Council Member Price reported on recent conventions regarding mosquito
abatement. She said no human cases of West Nile Virus were found in Salt Lake
County in 2024. Council Member Price provided an update on Salt Lake County Animal
Control and spoke of a new sign design for parks. She shared a suggestion for all
communities partnering with Salt Lake County Animal Control to have the same animal
control rules.

8.4 Unified Police Department/SLVLESA

Council Member Cardenaz spoke of the passing of K9 dog Nitro. He reported a
UPD hiring incentive bonus had been extended with the hope to be fully staffed by the
end of February 2025. Council Member Cardenaz provided an update on the proposed
SLVLESA tax increase.

8.5 Wasatch Front Waste & Recycling District (WFWRD)

Council Member Shelton spoke of a proposed $6.50 per month increase for trash
collection to be able to maintain current service level. He said a public hearing was
scheduled for November 18, 2024. Council Member Shelton said WFWRD had also
discussed adjustments to other services and rates.
