OAM2024-001175 - The City of Magna is considering the adoption of the Magna Historic District Area
Plan. The plan area encompasses nearly two square miles of the northwest corner of urban Magna, bounded
by the H201 corridor to the north and U111 8400 w. corridor to the east. This plan will provide guidance to
the municipality on updates to district open space and streetscape environments as well as provide a
framework for accomplishing context appropriate development and renovation in and around Magna’s
historic core. Staff will give a brief presentation to the Commission prior to opening the public hearing.
Following the public hearing, the Commission may make a recommendation to the Council regarding
adoption of the plan document. Planner: Matthew Starley (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Matt Starley provided a presentation
of the plan.

Commissioners and the public had a brief discussion regarding liquor permits and denials, transportation
on 3500 south, and 8400 west widening.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.
