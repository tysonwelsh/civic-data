NO ACTION COMMENTS
      1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
         meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—February 25th @ 7pm.

              City Council vacancy applications for District 1 & District 5 are due on February 18th
              by 5 p.m. More information can be found on the Elections page of the City’s
              website.

              The Utah Division of Drinking Water is offering a free, online DIY series on
              Waterwise Landscaping. More information can be found on the City’s social media
              pages or on the Utah Division of Drinking Water’s website.

              The City is partnering with the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District to host a
              ‘Localscapes Workshop’ on March 14th at the Granite Library

              The South Salt Lake & Millcreek City Youth Councils are teaming up to gather
              donations for teens experiencing homelessness.

              More information about all City events and projects can be found at sslc.gov

              City Hall and offices will be closed on Monday, February 16th in observance of
              President’s Day.

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          South Salt Laked resident, Susan Bowlden, asked questions regarding the City
          Council vacancies and inquired about the City’s follow up to the police department
          audit.

              South Salt Lake resident, Hazel Stout, shared his concerns regarding recent events
              involving Immigration and Customs Enforcement activities across communities.

              Former employee and South Salt Lake resident, Joe Anderson, shared his concerns
              about the police department morale.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood shared that the City’s strategic plan regarding the next steps for the
          police department’s direction was posted on the City’s website.

              She addressed a culinary water issue that happened on Sunday, February 8th and
              encouraged residents to call the non-emergency dispatch for recurring issues.

        4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
           None.

        5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
           Council Member Williams encouraged residents to be respectful of city employees,
           and to refrain from harassing anyone or spreading misinformation.

             Council Member Bynum shared her positive sentiments on the recent national
             sporting event and halftime show that provided a bridge for the appreciation of
             another culture through music and diversity.

        6. INFORMATION.
              a. Update on the Public Works Campus
                 Public Works Director, Craig Giles, went over the progress of the public
                 works campus. Phase 1 is set to be completed by June 2026, Phase II is set
                 to start in July of 2026, and project completion is set for June of 2027.

                  b. City Communication Efforts
                     Communications Manager, Joseph Dane, reviewed the Communications
                     Team responsibilities, audience, primary channels, new tools, and overall
                     accomplishments throughout the last year.
