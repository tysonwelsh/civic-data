ORDINANCE NO. 2022-44

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN,
UTAH, AMENDING TITLE 10-15A (ACTION LEVELS IN THE
ENVIRONMENTAL CONTAMINATION OVERLAY ZONE) OF HERRIMAN
CITY CODE

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the Herriman City authority to enact
ordinances that the City Council considers necessary or appropriate for the use and
development of land within the City; and

WHEREAS, City Staff, in consultation with the City’s environmental consultant, has
had several meetings with the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and the Utah State
Division of Environmental Remediation and Response (DERR); and

WHEREAS, the EPA and DERR have recommended the City amend action levels in
the environmental contamination overlay zone by reducing the action level for lead
contamination in the nonresidential uses from 5,000 mg/kg to 4,000 mg/kg; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission held a public hearing, reviewed the subject text
amendment, and agreed that the City should reduce the action level for lead contamination
in nonresidential uses from 5,000 mg/kg to 4,000 mg/kg, but the Planning Commission also
recommends that the City reduce the action level for arsenic in nonresidential uses from 850
mg/kg to 300 mg/kg; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing and reviewed the subject text
amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the subject text amendment will enhance the
public health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. Herriman City Code Section 10-51A-4 is now
amended by:

a. Reducing the action level for lead contamination in nonresidential uses from
5,000 mg/kg to 4,000 mg/kg;

b. Reducing the action level for arsenic in nonresidential uses from 850 mg/kg to
300 mg/kg; and

c. Reducing the action level for lead contamination in residential property from
1,600 mg/kg to 1,200 mg/kg;

SECTION 2. Severability. Should any part or provision of this Ordinance be declared
by the courts to be unconstitutional or invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of
