Re: Approval of the following Assessment's-in-Lieu


Comments:


Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) 10-3-020(C) requires that any residential
parcel not within a subdivision shall construct frontage improvements and allows
the owner to comply with DCMC 9-27-110(C) where an owner may pay an
assessment in lieu of public infrastructure construction.



In order to comply with the code, the owners requested they pay the assessment
in lieu of construction. The city staff determined it would best serve the owner
and the city to proceed with an assessment-in-lieu of construction of public
infrastructure rather than having the owner construct the improvements.



Staff recommends approval of the assessment in lieu for the following locations.
In the future, having the city construct the frontage improvements will ensure that
the seamless design and construction of the improvements in the area and
potentially save the city costs associated with modifying the city’s design by
incorporating infrastructure not presently constructed.
