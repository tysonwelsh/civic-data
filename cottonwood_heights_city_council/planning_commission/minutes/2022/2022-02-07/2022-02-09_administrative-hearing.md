                          ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING MINUTES

The Cottonwood Heights Community and Economic Development Director, Michael Johnson
held an Administrative Hearing Meeting on Wednesday, February 9th, 2022, which began at
12:00 p.m. in the Cottonwood Heights City Council Chambers located at 2277 E. Bengal Blvd.,
Cottonwood Heights, Utah 84121.

Staff Present:         Michael Johnson, CED Director and Administrative Hearing Officer;
                       Maverick Yeh, Assistant Planner; Heather Sundquist, Deputy City
                       Recorder; Samantha DeSeelhorst, Associate Planner

The Administrative Hearing Officer, Mr. Michael Johnson, called the meeting to order and
welcomed those present.

       1.0 BUSINESS ITEMS
           1.1 (Project CUP-22-001) Request from Kaela Lunt for conditional use
       approval of a short-term rental at 9015 S. 3605 E.
Mr. Maverick Yeh, Assistant Planner provided an overview of the proposed project. Staff
recommends approval of the project with conditions as stated.
Mr. Johnson opened the meeting to Public Comment
Mr. Isaac Etter, a resident in attendance, asked if the applicant was looking for Airbnb
opportunities for the property and if that is why it required the short-term rental application. Mr.
Johnson explained the short-term rental ordinance.
The application was approved by Administrative Hearing Officer Michael Johnson, pursuant to
the fact and findings in the staff report.
       2.0 Consent Agenda
                 2.1 Approval of Minutes for February 9th, 2021
Mr. Johnson stated that the meeting minutes will be approved following the below procedure.
The Director will move to approve the above-mentioned minutes after the following process is
met. The recorder will prepare the minutes and email them to the Director and present staff
members. The Director and members will have five days to review the minutes and provide any
changes to the recorder. If, after five days there are no changes, the minutes will stand
approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until the changes are made and
the Director and members agree, at which time the minutes shall be deemed approved.
The meeting was adjourned at 12:05 pm
