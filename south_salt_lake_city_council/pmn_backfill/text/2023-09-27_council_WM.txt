       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood let the Council know that Council Member Siwik had informed the City
          of his resignation. As a result, there is now a vacancy on the Council for District 5.
          Per Utah Code, Title 20A-1-510, if any vacancy opens in a City Council, they must do
          the following:
              a. Within 30 days from the date of vacancy, appoint a registered voter who
                   meets the qualifications to hold municipal office.
              b. Within 14 days prior to the meeting to fill the vacancy, give public notice of
                   the vacancy.
              c. The notice must identify the date, time, and place of the meeting, to whom
                   submissions should be made, and a submission deadline.

              She said that she would like to propose the October 25th Work Meeting for the
              Council to interview candidates that submit resumes and take a vote in the Regular
              Meeting on who will fill the vacancy for the rest of the year.

              Mayor Wood highlighted some upcoming City events:

              September 28th – There will be a ribbon cutting at 10 am to celebrate the 900 West

    Unfinished Business


    1. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Amending Chapter 17.06 of the South
       Salt Lake City Municipal Code to Correct Technical Errors and Amend Certain General
       Landscaping Design Standards.

        Community Development Deputy Director, Eliza Ungricht, provided an overview on this
        item. Staff is making changes to Title 17 for the Landscape Ordinance. There were items
        that needed to be updated so that residents can get the full water-wise rebate when
        they participate in the program.

        She made a request to the Council to table this item for another meeting since they
        received information today on some of the red lines from Jordan Valley. Staff now needs
        some additional time to include those in this update.
