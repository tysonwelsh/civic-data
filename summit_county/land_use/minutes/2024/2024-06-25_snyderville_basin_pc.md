Madlyn McDonough,
Planner,
presented a Conditional Use Permit in the Hillside Stewardship zone for a Major Accessory Building located in Silver Creek Estates Unit B subdivision.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:39 P.M.
Kelly McMullin was called to speak at 6:39 p.m.
Siegfried Reich was called to speak at 6:40 p.m.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:41 p.m.
Commissioners commented on the application, raised concerns, and responded to questions asked by members of the public.
Ryan Koemans with RM Koemans Construction, Planner McDonough, Director Barnes, and Attorney Viti responded to concerns and questions raised by the Commissioners.
John Kavas,
Owner,
addressed the commission and responded to questions about his desired use of the building.
Bruce Carmichael made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit for a Major Accessory Building located at 603 Aspen Lane pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report with the addition of two conditions. The conditions be that commercial use of the structure is prohibited and no cars associated with the property be parked on Aspen Lane. Also, noting the change of language of "does not comply" to "does comply" in the staff report. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: DJ Hubler, Matthew Nagie.
