       6.        Ordinance 2021-08 amending Provo City Code to remove the Design Review Committee
                 requirement for the Critical Hillside (CH) Overlay Zone. Citywide application.
                 (PLOTA20210014) (2:07:18)

             Motion:             An implied motion to adopt Ordinance 2021-08, as currently constituted, has been made
                                 by council rule.

189   Brandon Larsen, Planner, presented. In June 2020, the Critical Hillside Overlay Zone was created.
190   Subsection 14.33A.040(3) of the Code established design review requirements for certain uses
191   conducted within the Critical Hillside (CH) Overlay Zone. This subsection read as follows:
192   “Notwithstanding any other provision in Title 14 or 15, Provo City Code, all proposals for residential and
193   nonresidential developments in the CH Zone, as well as all proposals for main buildings, except for one-
194   family detached dwellings, shall obtain a recommendation from the Design Review Committee.” This
195   subsection required, among other things, design review for all residential developments. Design review
196   for a development of single-family homes would not be very useful considering there would be no
197   landscaping or architecture to review.
198
199   Additionally, design review of residential developments, which did not require project plan approval,
200   may lie outside the scope of the Design Review Committee's (DRC) powers and duties established in
201   Chapter 14.04A. Section 14.04.020 of the Code identifies the duties of the DRC, as follows: “The Design
202   Review Committee shall review and make design recommendations regarding the external design of
203   buildings and site plans for all proposed new buildings, structures, and uses which are subject to project
204   plan approval and design review.” The elimination of the subject requirement would allow the Powers
205   and Duties section of Chapter 14.04A to control in the question of when a proposal in the CH Zone must
206   go to the DRC for their recommendation. Other sections of the Code, such as Subsection 15.03.310(5),
207   help to give further direction regarding when a proposal must have design review.
