 . Consent Agenda, 2:05pm
1
 Council Chair, Mayor Dawn Ramsey, welcomed the Wasatch Front Regional Council members and guests.
 Introductions were made around the table, and Mayor Ramsey welcomed a new Council member,
 Representative Cal Mussellman, who is taking over for Representative Mike Schultz. Andrea Pearson,
 WFRC, read the names of those attending online.
 a. [00:06:39] ACTION : Mayor Jeff Silvestrini made a motion to approve the Minutes of the WFRC
 meeting held May 25, 2023 and Financial Statements for April, May & June 2023 and current
 Budget/Expenditure Report. Mayor Mark Shepherd seconded the motion. There was no discussion,
 the minutes, financial statements for April, May, and June 2023, and the Budget/Expenditure Report
 were approved unanimously.
 2. Chair Report
 a. [00:07:35] Mayor Dawn Ramsey introduced the Refreshed Wasatch Choice Vision which is the plan
 for Utah’s growth. Mayor Ramsey touched on the four key strategies of the vision. The Wasatch
 Choice Video was played. Ted Knowlton, Deputy Director, & Julie Bjornstad, Senior Planner of
 WFRC, gave an overview of the Wasatch Choice Vision map. They also touched on some of the
 other resources that can be found on the website. There was discussion related to the vision, and
 the partners involved.
 [00:35:55] Commissioner Bob Stevenson made a motion to move public comment to 5c. After the
 presentation on the 2024-2029 TIP, and before the vote. Mayor Joy Petro seconded. There was no
 discussion, approved unanimously.
 [00:40:16] Laura Hanson, GOPB gave a review of the results from the Guiding our Growth Survey
 that was put out by the Governor's office of Planning and Budget to residents of Utah. Andrew
 Gruber, Executive Director at WFRC, points out that the alignment of the survey results and the
 Wasatch Choice Vision are very consistent.
 b. [00:49:14] Andrew Gruber, Executive Director of Wasatch Front Regional Council gave an overview
 of the WFRC FY23 Activities and Accomplishments Report , and how it aligned with the goals put
 forth by the council.
 3 . Public Comment - Moved to Item 5c. 
 . Regional Growth Committee (RGC) [01:09:25]
4
 Mayor Bob Dandoy, Regional Growth Committee Chair introduces Megan Townsend, WFRC Community and
 Economic Development Director to discuss the station area plans that are up for certification in Roy and
 South Jordan. Ms. Townsend gave a brief overview of the work, requirements, and technical assistance
 involved with Station Area Planning.
 a. [01:14:47] Ms. Townsend and Mayor Dandoy gave a summary of the Roy City Station Area Plan.
 Mayor Dandoy, as Mayor of Roy City, abstained from the vote. [01:17:24] ACTION Mayor Mike
 Caldwell, Ogden City, made a motion to approve the Certification of the Roy City Station Area Plan.
 Commissioner Jim Harvey, Weber County, seconded the motion. There was no discussion- 
 approved unanimously with one abstention.
 b. [01:18:10] Ms. Townsend and Mayor Ramsey gave a summary of the South Jordan Station Area
 Plan and explained some of the impracticalities involved with this particular plan. Mayor Ramsey, as
 Mayor of South Jordan, abstained from the vote. [01:23:00] ACTION Mayor Jeff Silvestrini,
 Millcreek City, made a motion to approve the Certification of the South Jordan City Station Area
 Plan. Salt Lake County Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton, seconded the motion. There was no
 discussion- approved unanimously with one abstention.
 [01:24:00] Chair, Mayor Dawn Ramsey, explained that she needed to leave early, and would be turning to 
 ice Chair, Commissioner Bob Stevenson to chair the remainder of the meeting.
