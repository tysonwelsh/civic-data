Budget Public Hearing

            Mayor Kelly Bush asked for a roll call vote to start the public hearing. All Council
Members present voted “Aye.”

               Mayor Bush referred to the temporary conduct of electronic meetings of the
Kearns Metro Township Council that is consistent with the Utah Open and Public Meeting Act.
This allows the Council to hold electronic meetings without a physical location.

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider the 2021
recommended budget for the Kearns Metro Township.

               Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
open the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              Mayor Bush presented the 2021 proposed budget.

              No one appeared in favor of or in opposition to the budget.

               Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
close the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

             Council Member Schaeffer, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
adopt 2021 Kearns Metro Township budget. Roll was called showing the vote to be “Council
Member Butterfield “Aye,” Council Member Schaeffer “Aye,” Council Member Peterson “Aye,” and
Mayor Bush “Aye.”
