5.1 Discussion and consideration of a technical update to the Moderate-Income Housing
Element of the General Plan as required by Utah State Code.
Applicant: Herriman City
File Number: G2022-118
City Planner Maloy introduced Susie Petheram to explain the requirements of HB462. Utah State Code
required municipalities to adopt a Moderate-Income Housing Plan (MIHP) as an element of the General
Plan and to identify strategies and implementation measures. Herriman adopted a MIHP on November
13, 2019, which complied with these requirements.
During the 2022 Utah State Legislative session, the State revised the requirements for the MIHP and to
comply with the amended requirements, staff recommended Herriman City should adopt the following
technical amendments:
* Replace the 2019 MIHP strategies with the 2022 strategies adopted by the State of Utah
* For consistency with the adopted language in Utah Code, replace references to “action”
with “implementation”
* Update statistical information and references with current data
* Complete minor updates to selected implementation measures and identify benchmarks
Commissioner Rypien asked who managed programs for housing assistance within the city. City Planner
Maloy responded Mayor Palmer was exploring options. Commissioner Rypien encouraged spreading the
locations through the city and not concentrating them in one area. Commissioner Bradford asked the
median income for the area. Per City Planner Maloy the current data had not been reported by the State,
and it would be based on median income for Salt Lake County. Per 2020 the median income for a three-
person household was around eighty thousand. At the same time, Herriman City median income was
around ninety-five thousand, per Susie Petheram.
Chair Berbert opened the public hearing.
No comments were offered.
Chair Berbert closed the public hearing.
Commissioner Rypien moved to recommend approval to City Council of item 5.1. Discussion and
consideration of technical update to the Moderate-Income Housing Element of the General Plan as
required by Utah State Code. Commissioner Sickles seconded the motion.
