CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
CA-3-2020 Petition by NICHOLS NAYLOR ARCHITECTS requesting conditional use
          amendment for a tunnel carwash. The property is located at 3360 South Redwood
          Rd. on 3.53 acres and is zoned C-3 (Transitional Commercial). (Staff – Jody
          Knapp at 801-963-3497)

Discussion:       Jody explained the owner of the Westerner, Jason Hook, would like to construct a
tunnel carwash facility on the southeast corner of the property. Jody stated there had been back and
forth conversation about parking. The Westerner is 16,000 sq. ft., the requirement is one parking space
for every 50 sq. ft. The need for the Westerner is 320 parking spaces, which is what they have right
now. The addition of the carwash would take away 100 of those parking spaces. The Westerner only
uses 11,000 sq. ft. as a club; this would require 220 parking spaces instead of the 320. Therefore, they
can fulfill the parking requirement. Jody noted there would not be much overlap of business hours
between the carwash and the Westerner.
Commissioner Winters asked if there have been many noise complaints about the carwash businesses in
West Valley City. He also wondered if there are restrictions for the noise level. Jody has not received
any complaints. She explained there recently was an addition of another tunnel carwash along
Redwood Road, and the noise from the traffic is louder than that of the carwash. She also stated the
carwash would be closed at 8:00 pm, so she doesn’t foresee any noise level issue.




There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:45 p.m.
