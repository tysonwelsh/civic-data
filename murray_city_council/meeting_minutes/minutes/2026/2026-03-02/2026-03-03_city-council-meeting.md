  2. Consider an ordinance vacating a Municipal Utility Easement located at approximately 4998 South
     Galleria Drive and 4950 South Galleria Drive, Murray City, Salt Lake County State of Utah. Power Director
     Greg Bellon displayed a Galleria property site map to point out the existing power easement that no longer
     served a purpose. He explained that property developers requested that the City vacate the easement,
     and in exchange, a new utility easement would be granted to the City. Mr. Bellon believed the new
     easement would meet the needs of Murray Power, serve the new development well and be of great value
     to the City.

      The public hearing was open for public comments. No comments were given, and the public hearing was
      closed.

Business Item:
 1. Consider a resolution of the Municipal Council (the “Council”) of Murray City, Utah (the “City”) declaring
     the City’s intention to reimburse itself for expenditures incurred in connection with financing all or a
     portion of capital improvements to the water system of the City and related improvements, with
     proceed of bonds that the City intends to issue; and related matters. Finance Director Brenda Moore
     explained that the purpose of the resolution was to allow the City to reimburse itself for expenses
     associated with drilling a well or other water system improvements if the Water Fund later decides to
     borrow funds for such projects.

     Reimbursements could cover costs that occurred 60 days prior to the approval date of the resolution,
     which would include the current test drilling of the well on Winchester Street. Ms. Moore stated that the
     resolution did not mean the Water Fund would be issuing a bond, but if a future bond was needed the
     bond may not exceed $5 million.
       MOTION: Mr. Pickett moved to approve the resolution. Mr. Bullen SECONDED the motion.
           Council Roll Call Vote:
           Mr. Pickett       Aye
           Ms. Cotter        Aye
           Mr. Bullen        Aye
           Ms. Turner        Aye
           Mr. Hock          Aye
           Motion Passed: 5-0
