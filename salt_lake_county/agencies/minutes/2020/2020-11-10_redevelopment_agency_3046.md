4. Discussion Items
4.1 Discussion and Possible Approval of an Amendment to the 
Reimbursement Agreement between the Salt Lake County 
Redevelopment Agency and Arbor Park Associates L.C.
20-1097
Attachments: First Amendment - RDA Reimbursement Agreement - Arbor 
Park - 10-31-20
Resolution -- Arbor Park Reimbursement Agreement -- 
Amendment 1
Director Bradshaw  stated these are technical adjustments to the agreement 
approved at the October 13, 2020, RDA meeting.
Mr. Ruediger Matthes , Program Coordinator, Regional Transportation, 
Housing & Economic Development Department, responded to Mr. Van 
Maren’s question.  He did not have an answer and the developer was not 
available to attend today ’s meeting.  He will find an answer and get back to 
Mr. Van Maren.  
Director Bradshaw  stated because this line item was in the exhibits, it does 
not materially affect the Council’s vote on the resolution.
Mr. Matthes reviewed the amendments to the reimbursement agreement, 
which include a new exhibit for the post performance section, a change in the 
process for the post award project area funds, and it allows Arbor Park 
Associates to include other non -demolition costs in its reimbursement 
requests for the first phase.
