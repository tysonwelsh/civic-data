5.0   Report: Proposed property tax increase by Jordan Valley Water
      Conservancy District (JVWCD) for water service purposes

5.1   Reed Gibby, Draper representative on the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy
      District (JVWCD) Board of Trustees, reported that reservoirs were filled
      following the winter snowpack, but rainfall had been lower than average in
      June and July. Mr. Gibby spoke of population growth and aging infrastructure
      and said the JVWCD Board was proposing a property tax increase for FY 2024
      to fund needed capital projects.

5.2   Alan Packard, JVWCD CEO, and General Manager, applauded the Council for
      adopting water efficiency standards. He said property tax made up 25% - 30%
      of JVWCD revenue, and explained that property tax revenue affected JVWCD’s
      bonding ability. Mr. Packard spoke of needed capital projects and said the
      Board proposed a 9.3% property tax increase for FY 2024. Responding to a
      question from the Council, Mr. Packard confirmed the JVWCD Board raised the
      property tax rate in FY 2023. A JVWCD public hearing was scheduled to occur
      on August 9, 2023, at 6:00 p.m. at 8215 South 1300 West in West Jordan.

5.3   Mayor Walker opened the meeting for public comment, but seeing no one
      come forward he closed the public comment. Mayor Walker noted that
      no Council action was required.
