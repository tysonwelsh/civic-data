ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATION
ZT-1-2020  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY, requesting an ordinance change to Section 7-6-203 to
           add height limitations for attached private garages. (Staff - Steve Pastorik at 801-963-
           3545)

                Discussion:

                Steve Pastorik explained the origin of this application. He explained a current home
                where the garage being built next to it, is much larger and taller than the house itself.
                There were complaints about the size of the garage from the public and if they had a
                building permit. Steve stated the garage will be attached to the house and is permitted
                under the current ordinance. This proposal will limit the height and the area of attached
                private garages. Commissioner Lovato asked Steve if this proposal will prevent an
                apartment over a detached garage. Steve explained the current code does not permit
                living space over a detached garage
GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
GPZ-1-2020 Petition by FETZERS' INC. requesting a General Plan change from Mixed Use to Light
           Manufacturing and a zone change from C-2 (General Commercial) to M
           (Manufacturing). The property is located at 4850 W 3500 S on 7.73 acres. (Staff - Steve
           Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

                Discussion:

                Steve Pastorik presented this application. Fetzers’ Inc. is looking to purchase the old
                Shopko building to be used as a warehouse. Fetzers’ would eventually like to have a
                showroom for the public to view merchandise and are willing to enter into a development
                agreement.

                Steve explained there is a large parking lot associated with the building that Fetzers’ Inc.
                will not need to use, and they will look to parcel out that property. Steve also explained
                that Fetzers’ will disassemble furniture, however, this will take place inside the
                warehouse. Steve stated that for Fetzers’ to have the storage area they require they would
                need to change to an M (manufacturing) zone.

                Steve explained that there is land behind the building without landscaping. This was
                intended to be a road, however that did not happen. Steve explained that there has been
                discussion about the need to landscape that area. Fetzers’ plans to keep the footprint of the
                original building the same and paint the outside. Commissioner Lovato stated that he
                would like to see more of a change to the façade of the building than just fresh paint.
                Commissioner Wood agreed and stated the building currently looks like a big box. Steve
                explained that Fetzers’ might be adding more landscaping to the front of the building.

                Commissioner Woodruff asked Steve P. if Fetzers’ will have any outside storage. Steve
                stated that they are not proposing outside storage and this should be low impact use.

                Commissioner Winters asked Steve if the development agreement would inhibit large
                manufacturing. Steve explained that this proposal would allow indoor manufacturing,
                however not outside. Steve also stated that normally the City would not allow industrial
                next to a residential zone, however this would be low impact.

                 Commissioner Wood does not feel having an M (manufacturing zone) next to a school
                would be a good fit. Commissioner Fuller agreed with her and feels there could be a
                possible noise issue. Commissioner Fuller asked if it is possible to have just the section of
                the building that Fetzers’ is using zoned M and leave the rest of the lot a C2 zone. Steve
                stated that it is an option however Fetzers’ would need to provide a legal discription.
                Steve stated that Fetzers’ was wanting to see if acquiring this building is even possible and
                then look at what to do with the rest of the property that they will not be using.

                Commissioner Winters stated he likes the idea of using this building as a warehouse and
                only zoning the section that Fetzers’ will be using M. Steve then presented another option,
              the C3 zone. This is a transitional zone between the commercial and the manufacturing
              zones. However, later stated that the C3 zone cannot be next to a residential zone so it
              would not work in this situation. Steve then restated the key points of the discussion one
              concern of the Planning Commission is about only painting the building without other
              façade changes. Another concern is having industrial next to residential. Chairperson
              Meaders asked Steve if he has received many phone calls about this application. Steve
              stated he has not had any calls yet.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
 CA-1-2020 Petition by DUSTIN LATIMER WITH IG SIGN requesting a conditional use
           amendment for a pole sign at Kenworth Sales. The property is located at 2125 S
           Constitution Blvd on 27.8 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff - Brock
           Anderson at 801-963-3361)

               Discussion:

               Jody presented this application for Brock. Kenworth would like to move and raise their
               sign due to new development to the south of the building that impedes the view of the
               sign from the road. They will need to add landscaping at the base of the sign to comply
               with the code.


C-1-2020       Petition by DECKER LAKE BUSINESS PARK requesting a conditional use approval
               for outside storage. The property is located at 2340 South Redwood Road on 3.15 acres
               and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497)


               Discussion:

               Jody presented the application. She explained that Decker Lake is requesting conditional
               use for outside storage. Jody explained that this application started as an enforcement
               case, due to the business already having outside storage. Jody did visit the site and
               noticed the need to have the outside storage picked up and organized. She spoke to the
               tenant about the cleanup and they have made improvements. Jody explained this property
               is not to be used as a salvage yard but to be used for building materials that the business
               is actively using. Jody stated that there is an existing enforcement case, and the City is
               working with the tenant to clean up the property.



               There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:21 p.m.
