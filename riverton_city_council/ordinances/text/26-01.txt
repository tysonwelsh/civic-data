                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    ORDINANCE NO. 26- 01


       PROPOSED ADDITION OF SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENT DESIGNATIONS
   STIPULATING SETBACKS AND ARCHITECTURAL REQUIREMENTS FOR
  AGRICULTURAL BUILDINGS ON PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2673 WEST 11800
       SOUTH, REZONING THE PROPERTY FROM RR-22 TO RR-22/ SD
                             APPLICANT — BENJAMIN       AND SARAH MILAR.


       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,


       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map from RR- 22 to RR-22/ SD for the property
located at approximately 2673 West 11800 South.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
              Section   1.            The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is,
                                      amended to reflect the described changes as shown in
                                      Exhibit " A"



              Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
January 2026 by the following vote:
