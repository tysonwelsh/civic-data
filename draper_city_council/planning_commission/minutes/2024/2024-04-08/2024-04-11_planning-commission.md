6:30 PM Business Meeting

Chair Andrew Adams called the meeting to order at 6:30 PM and welcomed those present.

Community Development Director, Jennifer Jastremsky announced that tonight would be City
Planner, Tevai Haunga’s last Planning Commission Meeting as she is moving out of state. She
was wished all the best.

1.     Items for Commission Consideration.

       A.     Public Hearing: Bench Internal Accessory Dwelling Unit (I-ADU) Deviation
              Request (Administrative Action).
              On the request of Troy and Kim Bench, a Deviation Request to allow for an
              Internal Accessory Dwelling Unit (I-ADU) on approximately 0.54 acres located at
              approximately 968 E. 13200 S. also known as application 20240176-2024. Staff
              Contact: Tevai Haunga, tevai.haunga@draperutah.gov, 801576-6522.

Ms. Haunga presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is for an Internal Accessory
Dwelling Unit (“I-ADU”) deviation. A Vicinity Map was displayed showing the property on the
south side of the 13200 South. An Aerial Map was also displayed showing the property in the
Bench Cove cul-de-sac. The current land use designation is Residential Low-Medium Density.
The current zoning is RA-2 and the property is in the I-ADU prohibited area. There is an existing
home on the property with an addition proposed in the rear. The existing home will become the I-

ADU. Elevation drawings were shown of the site as well as site photos. Ms. Haunga stated that
no public comments were received.

A question was raised as to why this area has been excluded from having I-ADUs. Planning
Manager, Todd Draper, reported that Bench Cove is a private street and the City does not control
the parking there. That was one of the criteria when selecting which properties would be in the
mapped area where I-ADUs would not be allowed. The applicants are applying for a deviation in
that the lot is in excess of 20,000 square feet in size and they will provide the additional parking
required in the garage and driveway. A Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit (“D/ADU”) cannot be
built in front of the primary home so the two units were to be connected as one.

Chair Adams recalled a similar situation on Fort Street and 13200 South with a smaller dwelling
in front. Ms. Jastremsky stated that that situation predates the current Code.

Commissioner Nixon liked the idea of having the option for the I-ADUs. She was a proponent of
ADUs as they benefit the citizens who want to have family members live with them. She
considered it a great opportunity.

Commissioner Fowler asked if the I-ADU meets all of the Code requirements. Mr. Draper
confirmed that it will. Staff was still working through minor issues with the application but they
were nothing that would preclude deviation.

Commissioner Ogden commented that this is different than what is normally seen, which is a large
house in front with an ADU in the back. Ms. Jastremsky stated that they are reorienting the home.
It is a corner property that has historically fronted 13200 South. The ADU will be on the north
side of the home by the road. All access to the new home will be off Bench Cove.

The applicant, Kim Bench, identified herself and stated that they developed Bench Cove and the
subdivision. The home is laid out well and what is proposed will be a nice addition.

In response to a question raised by Commissioner Fowler, Ms. Bench identified a driveway from
Bench Cove that accesses the garage. The ADU will be attached physically to the house. The
orientation of the proposed ADU was described.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing
was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Squire moved to APPROVE the Deviation Request as requested by
Troy and Kim Bench for the Bench I-ADU Application 2024-0176-VAR based on the
following:

               Building, Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the

       B.     Public Hearing: Hastings Meadow Lot 9 Plat Amendment
              (Administrative Action).
              On the request of Richard Cunningham, for a Plat Amendment to Lot 9 of the
              Hastings Meadow Subdivision, covering 0.49 acres of land located at 12432 South
              Hastings Meadow Lane. Application 2024-0024-SUB. Staff Contact: Tevai
              Haunga, tevai.haunga@draperutah.gov, 801-576-6522.

Ms. Haunga presented the Staff Report and Vicinity and Aerial Maps were displayed. The land
use designation is Residential Low-Medium Density and the current zoning is RA2. The original
plats for the Hastings Meadows Subdivision were presented showing Lot 5 and Parcel A in the
corner. It is an amended plat and the applicant is merging the two properties into one. Staff did
not receive any public comment.

Commissioner Nixon asked about condition number two regarding the expiration of the approval.
The subdivision needs to be recorded within one year as required with the landscaping and
infrastructure. Ms. Jastremsky suggested that that be clarified in the motion. The Code specifies
that the expiration of a plat amendment is one year to record or one year to acquire a Land
Disturbance Permit and begin installing improvements. In this case, it will involve recording the
plat because there are no improvements to be physically done. She recommended that the Code
section be referenced in the motion.

       C.      Public Hearing: Banasky Rentals Zoning Map Amendment
               (Legislative Action).
               On the request of Banasky Rentals for a Zoning Map Amendment from the DC
               (Destination Commercial) zone to CBP (Business/ Manufacturing Park) Zone,
               located at approximately 132 West 13490 South. Application 2024-0044-MA.
               Staff contact is Tevai Haunga, 801-576-6522, tevai.haunga@draperutah.gov

Ms. Haunga presented the Staff Report and a Vicinity Map was displayed with the property located
on the west side of I-15. An aerial view of the 5.27-acre property was also displayed. The current
land use designation is Destination Commercial. The applicant is requesting a rezone from DC to
CBP to allow for a heavy equipment dealership, which is a similar use to what was previously
approved in the area. The use is classified as a commercial vehicle and equipment rental sales,
which would be an allowed use in the CPB zone but not in the DC zone. No public comment was
received on the matter.

Ms. Jastremsky reported that the previous use of the property was Cazier Excavating and the
property has been vacant for at least a few years. The proposed use matches how the property has
historically been used. It has, however, been vacant long enough that there is no legal non-
conforming use status remaining on the property. As a result, any new use must conform to the
Code. The applicant is requesting to rezone the property to allow for a use that is appropriate for
the existing physical improvements on the property. Staff did not ask that the land use map be
updated. She noted that the DC land use is still appropriate for when the property is eventually
redeveloped.

The applicant, Ryan Banasky was present with Tanner Pulsipher from Newmark. Mr. Pulsipher
stated that he is a licensed real estate agent representing Mr. Banasky on the potential purchase.
They are under contract with Stephen Wade and are confident what is proposed is the best possible
reuse of the property because it has a historical use for heavy equipment. It will be a new
dealership with the anticipated goal being $15 million in gross sales in the first year. It will
generate huge tax revenue for the City. There will be an ancillary service associated with it as
well. They believe it will be a great value add for the neighborhood.

Mr. Banasky reported that the business is called ArmUp Equipment Sales and will have a rental
component as well. They will carry strictly new high-end equipment.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There was no public comment. The public hearing was
closed.

Commissioner Fowler did not consider the change to be overly dramatic from one zone to the
other. She also felt that it matched what is across the street.
