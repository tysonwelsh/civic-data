OAM2025-001330 - An ordinance amending sections 19.14.070 Use Definitions and 19.42.350 Vehicle
and Equipment Repair and Table 19.34.030 Table of Uses for M-1 and M-2 zones to include and regulate
the rental of commercial vehicles and equipment. Planner: Curtis Woodward (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Senior Planner Curtis Woodward provided an analysis of
portions of Title 19 amendments.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding whether it was written specific to vehicles or
equipment tracked or wheeled. The change should be broadened to not only vehicles, but other industrial
equipment. Definitions cover other uses and can equipment be clarified as other equipment repair or rental
to include industrial and commercial grade equipment. Future of the area in Camp Kearns and alignment
with the general plan, sanitation equipment cleaned, used, and disposal, inoperable vehicles and adding
hardscape for storage,

Commissioner Thomas motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Citizen
Name: Roger Snow
Address: 5977 South Parkwood Drive
Comments: Mr. Snow said he agrees with Nathan’s changes to the ordinance numbering. and Line 8 should
be removed affecting residential areas. Mainly the company in the area and the oilers if they plan to change
that. The company doesn’t plan to rent out their own port-a-potties and mainly rent industrial machinery
and vehicles.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding access roads going through residential roads.
Restricting access distance from a residential street. It is easy to change to add industrial and commercial
equipment, rather than heavy industrial equipment.

Commissioner Thomas motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Commissioners had a brief discussion regarding removing heavy equipment commercial, collector
distributor roads, principal arterial road, amending 19.42.350.B route avoiding residential roads,

Motion: To recommend file #OAM2025-001330 An ordinance amending sections 19.14.070 Use
Definitions and 19.42.350 Vehicle and Equipment Repair and Table 19.34.030 Table of Uses for M-1 and
M-2 zones to include and regulate the rental of commercial vehicles and equipment to the Kearns Council
for approval with amendments to remove similar heavy commercial equipment, adding “and other
industrial, commercial, and construction equipment” to 19.04.070. A. Exclude number 8 in 19.42.350.B,
and control flow minimizing impact to traffic in residential neighborhoods.
        Motion by: Commissioner Thomas
        2nd by: Commissioner Nelson
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor
Commissioner Thomes motioned to adjourn, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.
