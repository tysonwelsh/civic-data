Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: September 6, 2022
Re: Action Item: Resolution 22-50 Comments: In association with a requested municipal boundary adjustment, DR Horton has proposed a 100.98 acre conservation easement along the north portion of the 1,035.49 acres that are proposed to be annexed into Lehi City. This conservation easement is to protect open space. The conservation easement will also act as a buffer to existing adjacent residential developments within Draper City. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. The conservation easement will protect 100.98 Acres of open space.
2. The conservation easement will provide an open space buffer between existing residences in Draper City and the proposed development to the south. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Resolution 22-50 9/2/2022 Cover Memo
