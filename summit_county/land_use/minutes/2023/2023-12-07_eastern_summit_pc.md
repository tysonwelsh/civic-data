2)
Continued Discussion and Recommendation, two (2) lot subdivision known as Western Sky Subdivision, located on parcel NS-227-H containing 40 acres at 170 Elk Hollow Drive, Wanship, Summit County, Utah . Applicant: Michael Watts. Project #22-040. Administrative Review. Tiffanie Robinson, Senior Planner.
(6:00 P.M.)
Tiffanie Robinson,
Senior Planner,
presented to the Commission and provided a brief history and background information on the Western Sky Subdivision for administrative review.
Commissioner Sargent asked a clarifying question to which Planner Robinson responded.
Bill Wilde made a motion to approve to approve the Western Sky Subdivision based on the findings of fact and conclusions of law. Clinton Benson seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
