                                                                MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                    Tuesday, May 13th, 2025 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Sharon Bolos, Gage Froerer, and James “Jim” H. Harvey
STAFF PRESENT: Jason Horne, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office; Chris Crockett, Deputy County Attorney;
and Craig D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.

B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Shelly Halacy
C. INVOCATION: Commissioner Harvey
D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   Commissioner Harvey: Under the direction Marty Smith our Culture, Parks, and Recreation department thrives
   and does very well. I do not believe people truly understand tourism in Weber County. In the facilities the County
   runs, nearly $30M of taxable income is generated by people visiting Weber County offsetting property tax
   payments for citizens by $1887 per year. There are wonderful things happening at Ice Sheet. Renovation
   preparations are being made for the 2026 Men’s World Cup of Curling happening next April funded through some
   Olympic legacy funding. A new Learn to Skate coordinator will be coming on board soon. Can-Am youth hockey
   is coming. May Mania demolition derby is happening at Golden Spike Event Center this weekend. They are
   migrating to a new ticketing system called ETIX. You have to put money away for HVAC, roofs, parking lots,
   and other routine building maintenance items. The Ogden Marathon Expo is on Friday followed by the Dwarfinator
   on Saturday. Excellence in the Community had a free concert this past Monday with the Crosstown Big Band. On
   Tuesday the 20th, there is a silent movie with the theater organ in the Peery’s Egyptian Theater. It is one of five
   theater organs left in the United States. The dedication of the OECC remodel is on November 4th. The chlorinator
   has been installed at North Fork Park remedying a decade old situation. A lot of great things to report from our
   Culture, Parks, and Recreation team.
     Commissioner Froerer: The Ogden Marathon starts Saturday morning in South Fork Canyon and I look forward
     to my fellow Commissioners joining me in the relay with Commissioner Harvey bringing it across the finish line
     for us.
     Stall of Fame: Stetson Almond (IT Department)
     Quinn Fowers (IT Director): Stetson has been here seven years, going on eight. When we hired him, he didn’t
     own or know how to use a razor. And that is still true today. We are happy to present this to him.
     Stetson Almond: Thank you!
     Chair Bolos: Stetson is one of the first names I learned when I got into office. He is always the guy there that
     troubleshoots and gets things done.
     Commissioner Froerer: He fixes problems in 30 seconds I have been working on over ten minutes trying to figure
     out. He does a great job.
     Commissioner Harvey: Stetson, you do a great job. Here’s to another 30 more years.

G. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH JOHN C. ROGERS FOR REPRESENTATION OF
       INDIGENT PERSONS IN THE SECOND DISTRICT COURT.

         Jim Retallick (Public Defender Director): John Rogers was hired to replace Martin Gravis who passed away
         on April 25th; his departure is greatly felt. I worked with Martin for over 25 years; he was a strong advocate
         and great pursuer of justice for his clients. Mr. Rogers worked for the LA District Attorney’s office, came to
         work for Weber County, went back to LA on a good offer, and has now returned after realizing life is better in
         Weber County. Everyone from our office to the judges are very satisfied with his performance as he has hit
         the ground running.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with John C. Rogers for representation of indigent persons
         in the Second District Court; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
