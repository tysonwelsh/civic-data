St. George General Plan
The following document presents the visions, goals, and strategies developed for the General Plan Update for St. George City. This document is informed by the 2002 General Plan, updated to reflect the community’s current needs, and reflects the direction and goals of the community. The community voice is echoed throughout this document and incorporates feedback received from over 5,000 residents through the Online survey from early summer 2021, interviews with experts and stakeholders throughout the community, and feedback from nearly 2,000 responses to the draft plan from summer 2022. The planning team also met with the Planning Commission and City Council throughout the process.
Water Element
Water Use and Preservation Element of the General Plan
Located in the extreme southwestern region of Utah, the City of St. George has a
current estimated population of over 105,000 residents and is one of the fastest growing
municipalities in the State. Due to the attractive nature of the City as a tourist and
vacation destination, temporary occupancy of the City can be significantly higher.
St. George operates a culinary water system that serves all of its service area, and an
expanding secondary irrigation system that serves smaller portions of the area. The
total service area encompasses over 49,000 acres of land and borders the State of
Arizona to the south. In addition to permanent residents, the City provides water service
to a number of golf courses, resorts, and many other commercial, industrial, and
institutional entities.
GENERAL PLAN SECTIONS
The General Plan has been formatted to reflect the City’s varied goals and provide clear and concise guidance for City Leadership as policy and land use evolutions occur within the City. The eight sections presented below offer distinct goals with supporting strategies to achieve the vision for the City. These sections are interdependent and complementary, reflecting the complex systems that work hand-in-hand to make St. George great.
