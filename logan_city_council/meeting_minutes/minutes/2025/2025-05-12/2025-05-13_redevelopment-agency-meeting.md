The Logan Redevelopment Agency convened in a budget workshop session on Tuesday,
May 13, 2025, in the Logan Municipal Council Chambers located at 290 North 100 West,
Logan, Utah 84321 at 6:45 pm. Logan Municipal Council Meetings are televised live as a
public service on Channel 17 and the City of Logan YouTube channel at:
https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCFLPAOK5eawKS_RDBU0stRQ
Council Members present at the beginning of the meeting: Chair Jeannie F. Simmonds,
Vice Chair Mike Johnson, Councilmember Amy Z. Anderson, Councilmember Mark
Anderson and Councilmember Ernesto López. Administration present: Mayor Holly H.
Daines, Finance Director Richard Anderson and City Recorder Teresa Harris.

Chair Simmonds welcomed those present. There were approximately 2 in attendance at
the beginning of the meeting.
F/Y 2025-2026 Budget Workshop Department Presentation:
Economic Development – Kirk Jensen, Director (1:15:09)
Economic Development Director Kirk Jensen addressed the Council regarding his
proposed budget.
Plaza 45 & The Jump to the Moon building sold this year and the funds returned to the

RDA reserves are $2.2 million as well.

Chair Simmonds requested clarification that the building next to plaza 45 has been sold.
Kirk Jensen, Economic Development Director clarified that both buildings sit on the
same parcel and have been sold.
Councilmember A. Anderson inquired if the real estate holdings are located in the
vicinity of 300 N Main St.
Mr. Jensen confirmed that the corner of the northwest intersection of 300 N Main St is
owned by the RDA, along with smaller parcels at the southwest intersection of 300 S 100
E.
Chair Simmonds asked if some low-income housing had been used for the purchases.
Mr. Jensen answered that only a small portion had been used for that purpose.
Chair Simmonds requested confirmation that the active project area will be the mall
project.
Mr. Jensen confirmed that the active project area commencing in 2026 will be the mall
project.
Chair Simmonds inquired if there are new opportunities for RDAs or other types of
structures that allow the City to create development in certain areas.
Mr. Jensen said that 300 North Main Street is currently being observed.
Chair Simmonds requested further clarification if an RDA 300 North Main Street is
created, will the RDA cover all the way to Smiths or will it only be that corner.
Mr. Jensen explained that it depends on development momentum and whether there is
any interest. Back in the day, RDAs cast a larger geographical area to increase revenue in
the area. Tax entities now would rather focus on a smaller area.
Chair Simmonds asked if there is any potential for development in a residential area.
