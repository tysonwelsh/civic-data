MAGNA
RESOLUTION 20-04-03
FIRST RESPONDER RECOGNITION
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
RECOGNIZING AND HONORING THE SERVICE OF THE FIREFIGHTERS AND
POLICE OFFICERS OF THE UFA AND UPD DURING THE COVID-19 AND
MARCH 18, 2020 MAGNA EARTHQUAKE EMERGENCIES
WHEREAS, the Unified Fire Authority ("UFA") is an Interlocal Cooperation A gency
that provides fire protection services to Magna; and
WHEREAS, the Unified Police Department ("UPD") is an Interlocal Cooperation
Agency that provides police services to Magna; and
WHEREAS, on March 6, 2020 the Salt Lake County Mayor signed a "State of
Emergency" declaration due to the COVID-19 pandemic, thereby activating both UFA and
UPD to conduct event response to the emergency; and
WHEREAS, on March 16, 2020 the Mayor signed a "State of Emergency" declaration
as a result of the declarations made by the County, State and Federal Government; and
WHEREAS, on March 18, 2020 at 7:09 AM Magna experienced an earthquake
measuring 5.7 on the Richter scale; and
WHEREAS, immediately following the earthquake, a number of the officers of the
UPD deployed to Magna Main Street and began work to protect citizens from the falling debris
and unsafe conditions created by the earthquake; and
WHEREAS, shortly behind the UPD, UFA's Heavy Rescue and other emergency
teams arrived on site to work alongside the UPD to protect the impacted residents who lived in
the Magna Main Street Area; and
WHEREAS, due to a toxic spill event that was happening near the Kennecott Smelter
due to the earthquake, both agencies had the added task of planning for a possible mass
evacuation of Magna that fortunately never came to pass; and
WHEREAS, for the next three days, to protect the people and properties along Magna
Main Street, UPD maintained a 24-Hour a Day physical presence; and
WHEREAS, during the heightened response to Magna's time of need, the entities
served by the UFA and UPD saw no decrease in service response; and
WHEREAS, though the earthquake event need has subsided, the COVID-19 event
continues to require a heightened response from both agencies; and
