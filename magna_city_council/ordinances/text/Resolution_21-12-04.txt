MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 21-12-04 DATE: December 14, 2021
A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CONDEMNATION OF
REAL PROPERTY OWNED BY ANDERSON PROPERTIES, INC
FOR EXTENSION OF 4100 SOUTH
WHEREAS, Magna Metro Township desires to facilitate the extension of 4100 South
beyond 8400 West; and
WHEREAS, it is necessary for this project to acquire a fee simple interest in a portion of
the real property located at 4100 South 8400 West from Anderson Properties, Inc., and described
in Exhibit A of this resolution (hereafter the ““Property”); and
WHEREAS, Township officials or their agents have as yet been unable to acquire the
Property from the owner through reasonable negotiation for the estimated market value; and
WHEREAS, although negotiations are ongoing and may continue, it is necessary to
commence condemnation action on the Property in order to proceed with the project without
further delay,
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township Council, Utah,
as follows:

Use of the Property for the extension of 4100 South, for access to adjacent
properties including a high school, is hereby declared to be a public purpose and necessary for the
best interests of Magna Metro Township and its residents.

Action by the Council and its attorneys to acquire immediate occupancy and
ownership of the Property by condemnation is hereby approved.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Magna Metro Township Council this 14" day of
December, 2021.
FOR MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP:
DAN W. PEAY, MAYOR
