General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

1.         25-165                 Public Works Department recommending the Council receive a
                                  presentation on Household Hazardous Waste
                   Attachments:   2025 Presentation

                                  Ryan Kump, Public Works, introduced Jose Carreon-Andrade with the Salt Lake County
                                  Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Collection Facility which opened in Sandy City in
                                  July of 2024. Household hazardous waste includes items that are corrosive, toxic, or
                                  flammable that when mixed with other substances can explode. The goal of this program
                                  is to reduce the amount of HHW is homes and the environment. He spoke about the
                                  operations of the facility located in Sandy at approximately 9000 South and 700 West
                                  and the disposal rates processed at the facility. Council thanked Mr. Carreon-Andrade for
                                  his presentation.

2.         25-228                 Central Wasatch Commission recommending the City Council approve the
                                  admission of the City of Holladay to the Commission
                   Attachments:   Draft Resolution 25-38C

                                  Mayor Zoltanski introduced Lindsey Nielsen, Executive Director of the Central Wasatch
                                  Commission. Ms. Nielsen presented Resolution 25-38C, a resolution allowing for the City
                                  of Holladay to be admitted as a member of the Central Wasatch Commission. The
                                  legislative bodies of all current CWC member cities must approve Holladay's admission to
                                  the CWC. This is an information item this evening and will come back to the Council at a
                                  future City Council meeting for consideration and action.

3.         25-231                 Presentation of three resolutions permitting the interdepartmental transfer

                                  Public Works Department, Facilities Division, and Police IT.
                   Attachments:   25-35C Resolution Interdepartmental Transfer Facilities

7.         25-234                 City Administration requesting Council approval of the updated cost
                                  estimates for the rebuild of the Alta Canyon Sports Center
                   Attachments:   Alta Canyon Funding Options

                                  Martin Jensen, Deputy CAO, provided an overview of the funding sources and options to
                                  pay for the construction cost increases associated with the Alta Canyon rebuild. The
                                  estimated cost increase was approximately $4.1M. Mr. Jensen provided details of the
                                  funding sources that could be utilized to cover the funding gap. Staff would make
                                  recommendations regarding the funding sources utilized. Council would approve the final
                                  amounts. Council questions followed.

                                  Public comment opened.

                                  Ms. Dawn Sidwell was not supportive of funding the increased construction costs. She
                                  did not think ACSC served the residents of District 1. She would like to see a city-wide
                                  vote.

                                  Ms. Cathy Spuck spoke about a discussion the Council had in 2022 concerning their top
                                  priorities. ACSC was not at the top of the list for Council Members Stroud and Mecham.
                                  She was not supportive of the ACSC rebuild for a variety of reasons.

                                  Ms. Kim Oleary expressed support for the rebuild of ACSC and appreciated the Council's
                                  vision for the new facility and was willing to fund the upgraded center. People come from
                                  all over to use the ACSC. She wanted her voice of support heard.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones has asked for a city-wide vote on the ACSC. There is a portion of the city
                                  who does not use the center. She was not supportive of using city funds to fund the
                                  construction costs increases.

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Brian Kelley, Administrative Services, responded to Council Member Stroud's questions
                                  regarding the process to get the Alta Canyons Sports Center on the ballot. He explained
                                  the process and timeline. He also explained that construction costs continue to increase
                                  the longer we wait. Shane Pace, CAO, mentioned that the discussion of ACSC has been
                                  going on for twenty years. The location of the amenity was not important. He pointed out
                                  that River Oaks Golf Course is in District 1. He also discussed the process needed to
                                  expand a special services district. Council Member D'Sousa understood why residents
                                  were concerned. This will not be the last capital project considered by the Council.

                                  Council made a motion approving the updated cost estimates of the project of $23.5 M.
                                  Discussion followed. Council Members spoke about the city's accomplishment for the
                                  many capital projects undertaken by the city including Fire Station 31, the Public Works
                                  Fleet building and the ACSC. The ACSC has been discussed for many years. The city
                                  started a multi-year process to set aside funding for the project without raising taxes.
                                  This facility will generate revenue. The ACSC will serve the entire city and we are building
                                  something great for the city. A majority of the Council Members expressed support of the
                                  funding options presented this evening and the use of these funds when needed.

                                  Following the vote on the motion, Council moved to General Citizen Comment.
                                  A motion was made by Aaron Dekeyzer, seconded by Zach Robinson, directing
                                  staff to proceed with the $23.5 million design option for the Alta Canyon Sports
