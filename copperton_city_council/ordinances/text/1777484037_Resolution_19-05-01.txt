A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
REQUESTING THE ANNEXATION OF CERTAIN PROPERTY WITHIN THE
TOWNSHIP TO THE SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT FOR THE
PURPOSES OF PROVIDING SANITARY SEWER SERVICES TO THE AREA
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council met in
a public meeting on May 15, 2019.
WITNESS

WHEREAS, the majority of the land area within the Copperton Metro Township
receives sanitary sewer services from the South Valley Sewer District; and

WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council desires that the all of the land
within the Township receive sanitary sewer services from the South Valley Sewer District; and

WHEREAS, state law authorizes the Copperton Metro Township Council to request
annexation of the property not currently served by the South Valley Sewer District to the Sewer
District by resolution; and

WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council desires to respect the wishes of the
property owners of the noted land proposed for annexation; and

WHEREAS, barring any protests that may be filed by property owners of the noted land
subject to the proposed annexation, the Copperton Metro Township Council hereby finds that the
public interest will be served by annexation of the noted area to the South Valley Sewer District.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Request for Annexation. The Copperton Metro Township Council hereby
requests the annexation of that certain area more particularly described on Exhibit A, attached
hereto and incorporated herein by reference, to the South Valley Sewer District, for the specific
purpose of the District providing sanitary sewer services to the described area.

Section 2. Declaration. The Copperton Metro Township Council hereby finds and
declares that should the District annex the area requested within Exhibit A, the Copperton Metro
Township will be wholly located within the boundaries of the South Valley Sewer District.

Section 3. Severability. If any clause, provision, sentence, paragraph or term of this
Resolution or the application thereof, is deemed to be invalid as to any person, entity,
establishment or circumstance, such invalidity shall not affect the other provisions of the
Resolution which shall remain in effect.

Section 4. Protests. The Metro Township Council will rescind this petition if any
property owners of the noted land subject to the proposed annexation file a protest.
Section 5. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective on the Ist day of
June, 2019. :
