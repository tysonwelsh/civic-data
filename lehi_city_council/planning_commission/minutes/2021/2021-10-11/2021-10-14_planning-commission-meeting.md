3.2) Public hearing and consideration of Jacob Hansen’s request for conditional use approval
of a flag lot located at 2029 West 900 North.

3.3) Consideration of Jacob Hansen’s request for approval of the JJR Tennis Club 2-lot
subdivision with an existing home located at 2029 West 900 North.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

3.2 and 3.3 were heard simultaneously.

The applicant, Jacob Hansen, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for item 3.2

Bart Thomas stated he has a few concerns because he isn't sure of the development plan. He is
zoned agricultural and is worried about lights and traffic. He has spent a lot of money on fences to
stop vandalism on his property. His property has been vandalized with the current commercial
property that is built. He is concerned with the safety for the children and for the welfare of his
property and animals. He would like a little more insight with the road and fencing from the
developer.

Commissioner Nielsen also acknowledged the online comments where the public comment was
opposed.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for item 3.2

The applicant stated there are fence requirements per City code that he will adhere to. As far as
lighting is concerned, there will be no courts running along Mr. Thomas property, so there should
be minimal affects to his property with lighting. The applicant stated they have to be closed by
10:00 pm per operation code, every day of the week. They are allowed to open for operation as
early as 6:00 am. He stated they don't anticipate much outdoor use during the winter season.
Commissioner Newall asked about the parking lot lighting. The applicant stated the parking will
be away from Mr. Thomas property, but there will be minimal lighting required. Commissioner
Bunker asked about the number of patrons and vehicles should be expected each day. The applicant
stated the maximum number of people would be approximately 60 people, and if they all drove
separately there would be 60-65 stalls to accommodate the patrons.

Commissioner Nielsen agreed with the public comment with misuse over general development
agreements. He stated regardless, it is commercial use and is a private facility for profit, but the
flag lot meets the code and requirements. Commissioner Bunker stated the area is mostly
residential that is surrounding the request.

3.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Mark Hampton’s request for review of the Lehi
Brownstones zone change located at 396 North 500 West, changing the zoning from R-2
(medium density residential) to R-3 (high density residential) on .42 acres.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Mark Hampton, was present. He clarified that he is proposing five units. He stated
his land was recently surveyed and found he has more acreage than they thought and was hoping
to have six units. He stated they would like to tie the two neighborhoods together with a walking
path and would like to make that park a public park.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Mark Hampton’s request
    for review of the Lehi Brownstones zone change located at 396 North 500 West, changing
    the zoning from R-2 (medium density residential) to R-3 (high density residential) on .42
    acres; that the proposed use will not be detrimental or injurious to property in the
    vicinity, will be in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and
    the purposes of this Code, that the property on which the use, building, or other structure is
    proposed to be established is of adequate size and dimensions to permit construction of the
    facilities and the conduct of the use in such a manner that it will not be detrimental to
    adjoining properties and the area; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the
    report, based on the total acreage of the new survey that Mr. Hampton will provide to the
    Council. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre; yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.7) Public hearing and recommendation of Edge Homes’ request for review of the River
Point Concept, a 529-unit residential development located at 1100 North 3600 West.
Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Brandon with Edge Homes, was present. He gave a brief power point presentation
on the Edge Homes concept. He stated they are close to twenty-five acres of open space with
trails and open space and a plaza area.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Michelle Stallings stated her concerns were the multifamily development amenity requirements.
She stated she feels that the developers should be responsible for providing the park space that is

required and provided information that the City requires per City code.

Lindsay Isles stated she would like to see the trees that are abutted to the wetlands to stay. She
also had concerns with 1500 North and 2300 West with traffic. She stated the Exchange has
parked cars and does not have sufficient parking and wanted to know if this is the direction the
City was going for and if the new development that is being heard tonight has enough parking.

Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged the public comments that were submitted electronically.

Ross Dinsdale stated the developer will be conducting a traffic study and the key point they will
be looking at is 1500 North and 3600 West. Ross stated the Peak Hour traffic times are when the
traffic studies are required to be counted. The City traffic engineer and the developers traffic
engineer will work cohesively to make sure the traffic study is done correctly.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

The applicant stated they would meet the ordinance when it comes to amenities. He will also
make sure a traffic study is conducted and comply with on street parking. He will look at the
trees to see if they are a buffer and do the best they can to try and preserve the trees.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Edge Homes’ request for
    review of the River Point Concept, a 529-unit residential development located at 1100
    North 3600 West; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report, and
    additional density request in lieu of widening of 3600 West and additional open space and
    trails for usable amenities as required by code. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre; yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Britton, no. The motion was forwarded with a
    negative recommendation 3 – 2.
