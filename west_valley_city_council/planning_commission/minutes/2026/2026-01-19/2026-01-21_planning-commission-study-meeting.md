PRESENTATIONS:

Z-10-2025
Petition by Jose and Lorena Jaramillo requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum
lot size ½ acre) to R-1-20 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 20,000 square
feet). The property is located at 7020 West 3100 South on 0.98 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at
801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented a zone change request for a property at 7020 West 3100
South, which is currently zoned A and seeks to be rezoned to R-1-20. The property owners
want to split the nearly one-acre land into two lots, each with a home, and would meet the
20,000 square foot Lot requirement. The presentation included detailed maps and images,
highlighting the property's location surrounded by homes in the Sugar Plum Subdivision and
condos. The discussion concluded with no questions or further comments on the proposal.


ZT-7-2025
Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to amend Section 7-6-202 of the zoning ordinance to modify
some of the RE Zone requirements for accessory buildings. (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-
963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).
Discussion: Brock Anderson presented a proposal to amend Section 7-6-202 of the Zoning
Ordinance, which deals with setback requirements for the Residential Estate Zone. The
change would reduce the setback requirement for accessory buildings from 10 feet to 1 foot,
aligning it with other single-family zones. The amendment would also include changing the
maximum building stories from 1.5 to 1 story.


C-1-2026
Petition by JOE SALISBURY requesting conditional use approval for a 26-unit rental townhome
development. The property is located at 2996 S Redwood Road on 2.48
acres, and is zoned RM, Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential. (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-
963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Brock Anderson presented a request for a rental townhome development off
Redwood Road. The units would feature basements, two-car garages, a mix of 3-bedroom/2-
bath units, 20’x20 foot garages, 18 feet driveways, and exceed required parking spaces. Brock
mentioned the developer would need to obtain a groundwater test and soils report to include
basements. Elevation materials include fiber cement siding and brick facades. Amenities
include a pickleball court and a fire pit. The backyards are proposed to be fenced and include a
cement pad surrounded by rock mulch. The project would require modifications to the
Redwood Road streetscape and need a new sidewalk.

Commissioner Durfee raised concerns about the maintenance of the rock mulch areas by
renters. The Fire Department is concerned there may be on-street parking, so they would work
with the developer and the builder to stripe curbs where there is no parking, and put up
signage.


GPZ-1-2026
Petition by CHRIS TORONTO requesting a General Plan change from Medium Density
Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) to High Density Residential (12 to 20 units/acre) and a zone
change from R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) to
MXD (Mixed Use). The property is located at 5035 W Lake Park Blvd. and 2736 S Corporate
Park Dr. on 26.34 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-
ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented a request from Wasatch Acquisitions to change the
zoning of a 26-acre property from Medium Density Residential to High Density. The same
request was denied two years ago and the applicant did not pursue the application further with
City Counsel.

The current proposal includes a mix of 368 units across 36 owner-occupied homes and
townhomes, 80 rental townhomes, and 236 apartments, with a reduced density of 14 units per
acre compared to the previous 16.5 units per acre. Some amenities include a large outdoor
pool for whole development, a private outdoor pool for the owner-occupied residents, hot tub, a
clubhouse, fitness room, tot lot, and covered pavilions.

Concerns were raised about the lack of for-sale housing options on the project, with some
Commissioners expressing worry that recent developments in West Valley have primarily been
rental properties, potentially limiting home ownership opportunities for residents.

The discussion then focused on the development’s access points and use of the land, with
Commissioner Lovato expressing concerns about unnecessary drive-thru gates to the owner-
occupied townhomes and the unusually large pool, questioning its practicality. Steve explained
the City's stance on no private streets and the challenges of HOA maintenance.

The property is located near Neil Armstrong Academy and Monticello Academy which both
have a lot of traffic during pick-ups and drop-offs. Commissioner Winters, who is also the
Board Chair of Monticello Charter School, mentioned there may be improvements to the
parking lot to address traffic issues near the school.



There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting
was adjourned at 4:30 PM.
