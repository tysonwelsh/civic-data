WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
FEBRUARY 5, 2020

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 10:00 a.m. on February 5, 2020, in the Washington
County Upstairs Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Chairman Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner
Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw,
Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Undersheriff James Standley

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION _OF_ APPLICANTS TO SERVE AS ACTING COUNTY
ATTORNEY TO TAKE EFFECT ON FEBRUARY 18, 2020

Chairman Iverson explained that County Attorney Brock Belnap submitted his letter of
resignation last Thursday in order to take a position with the Washington County Water
Conservancy District.

Commissioner Almquist stated that Mr. Belnap has done a great job leading the Attorney’s
Office for the past seventeen (17) years.

Chairman Iverson explained that State Code allows for any Deputy County Attorney to
submit their name for consideration to fill the vacancy until the next election cycle. There
was only one applicant for the vacancy which was Deputy County Attorney Eric Clarke. It
is the Commissioners recommendation for Mr. Clarke to fill that role as he has done an
exemplary job as Lead Deputy County Attorney as well as having a great working
relationship with all County departments. He has been a Deputy County attorney for the
past nine (9) years.

Commissioner Cox read aloud Mr. Clarke’s letter of intent.
(see attached letter)

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Eric Clarke as Acting
County Attorney to Take Effect on February 18, 2020. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

Acting County Attorney Clarke stated that he is humbled by the support from all the offices
and elected officials throughout this process. As Acting County Attorney, it is his intention
to continue the course of the current County Attorney’s Office with his areas of focus on
advocating for the use of Washington County public lands in a responsible and meaningful
way; support local law enforcement; and responsible criminal justice reform through the
