City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1674

On September 2, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1674 amending
the official land use map of Draper City for approximately 0.27 acres of property from
open space/parks to community commercial, located at approximately 687 West
126300 South within Draper City, otherwise known as the City Initiated Concord
Properties Land Use Map Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
