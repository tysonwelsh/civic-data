2. Text Amendment — Streetlights; Amend the 2009 West Jordan Municipal Code Section 8-

6-2: Streetlight Installation Required; City-wide Applicability; City of West Jordan
(applicant)

Cory Fralick, Public Service Director, said the proposed text amendment came about from a

recommendation of City Council. There are lapses in crosswalk lighting and the new code in Section

8-6-2 would require a streetlight on each side of a marked crosswalk with new development. After

installation, the lights would be dedicated to the city and maintained by city staff.

George Winn said this ordinance will help to alleviate problem areas where accidents may be

occurring and to help prevent future accidents.

Cory Fralick said he was aware of some of those situations. His department is in full support of the

amendment, which will take steps in preventing new accidents. The city engineering team created a

map that shows dark spots in the city where they can check for needed maintenance, but right now the

code is for new development. The goal is to one day be able to install lights as the budget allows.

Matt Quinney returned to the room at 6:05 p.m.

Trish Hatch was concerned that the older areas would not be able to have improved lighting at

crosswalks since they are not part of new development.

Cory Fralick said Section 8-6-2B allows residents in those areas to install lights at their own expense

that would then be dedicated and maintained by the city.

Trish Hatch said many people could not afford that.

Duncan Murray explained that the council could fund a program similar to the sidewalk replacement
program that appropriates money each year to improve areas in need. The purpose of this text
amendment is to establish a rule that developers will install streetlights for new construction going
forward. The city will then commit to maintaining them.
Mayor Dirk Burton explained that the streetlight fund on the utility bill is partly in place to install
streetlighting in needed locations as complaints or recommendations are made. The newly adopted
budget increased that amount by $1 per resident to help eliminate the dark areas.
Duncan Murray said Section 8-6-3 is regarding that streetlight fund.
Cory Fralick said the city already follows UDOT and national highway guidelines with regards to
required lighting. Crosswalks are considered an enhancement, and this amendment will take care of the
issues proactively.
Ammon Allen opened the public hearing.
Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.
MOTION: George Winn moved, based on the information and findings of the required
criteria set forth in the staff report and upon the evidence and explanations
received today, to forward a recommendation of approval to the City Council for
the Text Amendment regarding Streetlighting, finding that an affirmative
determination has been made for the criteria found in 13-7D-6B; 1 through 4. The
motion was seconded by Matt Quinney and passed 6-0 in favor. Catherine
Richardson was absent.
