DISCUSSION - Water Conservation Plan Update
Mr. Price introduced the background of the Utah State Water Code and the proposed update of
the Water Conservation Plan.

· Utah State Code 73-10-32 requires the City of Orem to prepare and adopt a Water
Conservation Plan.

·   Plan should be updated every five years.

· Submit a draft to the State Division of Water Resources for review for compliance with state
code.

He showed a map of the city and then explained the sources of the water for Orem.

·   Surface water (Deer Creek Reservoir/Jordanelle)

·   Springs (Alta springs/Canyon springs)

·   Wells

He then showed a graph and explained the historic and future water needs as well as a graph of
Orem Water Consumption. He explained that there has been a dramatic shift in water use
throughout the years. He then explained the types of water meters the city uses and what that
entails.



Mr. Macdonald asked for clarification on water consumption and water use.

Mr. Price showed a graph of the water production throughout the seasons of the years 2020 and
2021 and explained the difference between those two years.



Mr. Davidson asked why there is a spike at the end of the year of 2021.



General discussion ensued regarding the water consumption during the holiday season, the
fluctuation of weather/rainfall, business and home growth, and indoor water use.



Mr. Price explained Utah Regional M&I Water Conservation Goals. He stated that those goals
are made based on the City of Orem Water Consumption Summary from 2015-2021.

Water Challenges and Goals:
·   Reduce the City’s water use to reflect the Regional Water Conservation Gals for 2030.

·   Maintain an effective conservation-oriented water pricing structure each year.

·   Complete the meter replacement project by the end of 2022.

· Construct a tertiary treatment process at the Water Reclamation Facility to produce water
for irrigation by 2025.

·   Continue education efforts to the public regarding water conservation.

· Replace automatic sprinkler controllers on all City-owned properties to newer technology by
2025.




Mayor Young asked for clarification on the last goal if it was similar to the sprinklers that adjust
with the weather.



General discussion ensued regarding the types of sprinklers and areas that the city will be
working to improve.



Mr. Price explained some current and ongoing conservation practices that are in place and what
they feel are the next steps.



General discussion ensued regarding the replacement of meters, replacing automatic sprinklers,
concerns of Orem residents with water pricing structure each year, Deer creek Reservoir levels,
timing of water level intake project, watering proportions on certain sizes of land, and rebates on
water utility bill.

       ORDINANCE – Amending Section 14-1-4 Sign Zones and the Sign Zone map of the
       Orem City Code extending Sign Zone E to include a portion of the CenterPoint Church
       property located at 1550 S Sandhill Road.



Mr. Bench stated the Centerpointe Church would like to install a double sided, pole mounted
digital sign on their property. This sign would replace the historic use of hanging 10’ x 10’
banners on the building to advertise for events, such as a Christmas Eve Service. The pole sign
would be installed near the southwest corner of the building oriented towards the freeway.

1550 S Sandhill Road does not currently have a sign zone, so it is considered to be in Sign Zone
“D”. Sign Zone “D” allows free standing monument signs, however it does not allow any free
standing pole signs (Section 14-3-3). The applicant is requesting that sign zone “E,” which
allows pole signs, be extended to include 1480 S Sandhill Road and a portion of 1550 S Sandhill
Road property. The Planning Commission recommends the City Council, by ordinance, amend
Section 14-1-4 Sign Zones and the Sign Zone map of the Orem City Code extending Sign Zone E
to include a portion of the CenterPoint Church property to within 5 feet of the south face of the
existing structure located at 1550 S Sandhill Road



City council members asked for clarification of the height and the type of sign that will be
displayed. Mr. Bench responded stating that it will be a 6 feet high and 12 feet wide sign.



Mr. Spencer moved to approve the Ordinance amending Section 14-1-4 Sign Zones and the
Sign Zone map of the Orem City Code extending Sign Zone E to include a portion of the
CenterPoint Church property located at 1550 S Sandhill Road. Jeff Lambson seconded the
motion. Those voting aye: David A. Young, David Spencer, Tom Macdonald, Jeff Lambson, and
LaNae Millett. The motion passed.
