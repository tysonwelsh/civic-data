Planning

Consideration of Planning Commission’s recommendation to adopt Titles 18 & 19 of the Municipal
Code with revisions as outlined in presentation.

               Kayla Mauldin, Senior Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (MSD), reviewed Titles 18 and 19 of the Municipal Code along with revisions.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
approve Ordinance 2023-04-01, amending Titles 19 & 19 of the municipal code, provisions on
zoning and subdivisions. The motion passed unanimously.

                                           RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the five Metro
Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of Brighton; and

WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township adopted each county ordinance in effect at the time of its
incorporation pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-2a-414(3) and has authority to amend
or repeal the county ordinance when it determines it is necessary; and

WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate zoning and
subdivisions in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103(2); and

WHEREAS, the Council deems it necessary to amend its ordinances in order to ensure
compliance with State Statute, encourage conformity with the Kearns General Plan, promote ease
of use for residents and staff, and preserve the unique character of the Kearns community; and
for the protection and preservation of the public health, safety and general welfare.

BE IT ORDAINED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL as follows:

1.     Chapter 9.60, Section 14.12.050, and Section 14.12.060 (Subsection E) of Kearns
Municipal Code are repealed.

2.     Title 18 is repealed and replaced to read as follows:

Subdivision Ordinance: The following Title 18 is hereby repealed and replaced in its entirety with
the revised Title 18 attached hereto as Attachment 1.

3. Title 19, excluding Chapter 19.70, is repealed and replaced to read as follows:

Zoning Ordinance: The following Title 19, excluding Chapter 19.70, is hereby repealed and
replaced in its entirety with the revised Title 19 attached hereto as Attachment 2.

4.     Severability. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any part of these
Ordinances is unconstitutional or invalid, then such portion(s) of these Ordinances, or specific
application of these Ordinances, shall be severed from the remainder, which shall continue in full
force and effect.

5.     Implementation. Kearns staff are instructed to take any administrative steps needed to
prepare and finalize Attachments 1 and 2 for publication to Municode, including but not limited to
making any formatting, grammatical, or other non-substantive changes to the Ordinances that
may be needed.

Other Business

               Council Member Peterson asked if there were any sinkholes in Kearns.

                Mayor Kelly Bush stated she attended a meeting with contractors working on
4700 S. and she insisted they place steel plates over every large pothole. When the contractors
claimed to not have asphalt, she told them to take the asphalt that had been removed and get it
milled into hot patch.

               Council Member Peterson asked about the guidelines for using artificial turf in
one’s yard.

              Kayla Mauldin said it needed to be permeable and there were washing specific
requirements in the works.

             Council Member Peterson stated he would be meeting with Animal Services next
week and asked if anybody had anything to report. He wanted to know if there was going to be
a dog park.

               Mayor Bush said to call Patrick Leary.

District (MSD) announced Kearns Metro Township was selected for a program called Thriving
Communities Technical Assistance Program sponsored by the US Department of Transportation
to receive two years of technical assistance in preparing all of its transportation grants. Kearns
