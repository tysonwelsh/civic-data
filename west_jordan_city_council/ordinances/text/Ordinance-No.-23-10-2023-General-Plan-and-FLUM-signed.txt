                                 THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN, UTAH

                                      ORDINANCE NO. 23-10
        AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE 2023 GENERAL PLAN AND
     AMENDED FUTURE LAND USE MAP AS THE COMPREHENSIVE AND
          LONG-RANGE PLAN FOR THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
      WHEREAS, it is proposed that the City of West Jordan (“City”) General Plan and Future Land Use
Map be completely amended, revised, and updated; and
       WHEREAS, after having held a public hearing, the City’s Planning Commission (“Planning
Commission”), on February 7, 2023, recommended by a 6-0 vote that the draft 2023 General Plan and
amended Future Land Use Map be recommended to the City Council with various updates and amendments,
including an amendment that a Southwest Quadrant designation be placed on the amended Future Land Use
Map (“2023 General Plan and amended Future Land Use Map”); and
       WHEREAS, the City’s Council (“City Council”) held public hearings on March 8, 2023 and June
14, 2023 to receive comment on the proposed 2023 General Plan and amended Future Land Use Map; and
       WHEREAS, the City Council finds it to be in the best interest of the public health, safety, and
welfare of the residents of the City to adopt the 2023 General Plan and amended Future Land Use Map.
     NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WEST
JORDAN, UTAH AS FOLLOWS:
        Section 1. The 2023 General Plan and amended Future Land Use Map are hereby adopted as set
forth in the attached 2023 General Plan (Attachment 1) and in the attached amended Future Land Use Map
(Attachment 2).
       Section 2. Severability. If any provision of this 2023 General Plan and amended Future Land Use
Map is declared to be invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remainder shall not be affected
thereby.

         Section 3. Effective Date. This City of West Jordan General Plan and Future Land Use Map shall
become effective immediately upon posting or publication as provided by law and upon (i) the Mayor
signing the Ordinance, (ii) the City Council duly overriding the veto of the Mayor as provided by law, or
(iii) the Mayor failing to sign or veto the Ordinance within fifteen (15) days after the City Council presents
the Ordinance to the Mayor.
     PASSED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN, UTAH, THIS 28 DAY
OF JUNE 2023.
