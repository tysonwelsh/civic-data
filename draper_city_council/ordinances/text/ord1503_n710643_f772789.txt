City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 19, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1503, amending
the official land use and development code of the Draper City Municipal Code
otherwise known as the Day Dairy CSD TA. The complete ordinance is on file at the
Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 20th
day of October 2021. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
