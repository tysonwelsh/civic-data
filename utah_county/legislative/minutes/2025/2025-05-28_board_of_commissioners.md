Utah State University Extension - Utah County 2025 Summary

Utah State University Extension has been a trusted partner in Utah County for over a
century. As part of the land-grant university system, we are committed to delivering
research-based information that enhances the well-being of individuals in terms of
agriculture, finance, physical health, and mental well-being.

Extension plays a vital role in shaping American agriculture. We are a leading resource for
home food preservation, food safety, nutrition, and financial education, especially for
individuals facing economic challenges.

Locally, USU Extension supports commercial agriculture, youth empowerment, opioid
abuse prevention, and financial education, particularly for women and underserved

groups.

We provide services that other governmental agencies do not duplicate. Extension receives
funding from federal, state, and county governments, as well as private and competitive
grants. Utah County provides critical support through office space, staff, and some
programming. This support allows us to operate efficiently and deliver impactful services.

We estimate a return on investment (ROI) of at least 5:1 for Utah County residents and
businesses. This is based on:
