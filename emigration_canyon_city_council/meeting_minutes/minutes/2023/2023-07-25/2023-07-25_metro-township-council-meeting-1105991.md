Reports from other Municipal Providers
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Chief Bryan Case stated the strategic plan is ongoing. Council Member Harris
sits on the committee to assist with the plan. Station visits will be conducted to get stakeholder
input. UFA has a new safety trailer that can be reserved for community events. It is an educational
tool for kids and adults. Wildland crews have been busy in the canyon providing chipping services.
There have not been any big issues related to fireworks this year. There were five calls for service
in June.

Emigration Canyon Community Council (ECCC)
Bill Tobey stated chipping services have concluded with 110 households
participating. Volunteer hours and costs are being gathered to use to recertify for Firewise. A
newsletter was emailed to residents that included information on the dark sky ordinance, a link to
the open space survey, and an article on the Beaver Dam Analogs that are planned for Freeze
Creek. Salt Lake County Animal Services will be in the canyon hosting a clinic on Saturday. They
will provide pet licensing and microchip services. The house sign program is up and running again.
All delayed orders have been processed and are available for pickup. The ECCC is short
members, there are currently 9 members, and of those, 7 are actively participating. Any referrals
to fill the vacant seats would be appreciated.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)
Detective Dawn Larsen stated in June there were 49 calls for service, 14 written
reports, and 18 citations issued. DWR has an app where the public can report dead deer and it
will pick up the animal. Motors have been in the canyon several times doing speed enforcement.
The Deseret News Marathon went well; however, there was one issue with runners being rerouted
from Pinecrest onto UT-65. UPD had to shut down the road for people coming through I-80 to UT65, allowing cars through intermittently. The race may need to be planned differently. Bullet bikes
are a problem and UPD cannot chase them in the canyon. She is working on ideas to deal with
those on bullet bikes.

Polly McLean stated she is drafting a legal document to make sure there is public
access to the restrooms and the ability to maintain it. She has reached out to Utah Open Lands

Deputy Mayor Hawkes stated Utah Open Lands will own the property and

June 30 to obtain the grant from DWQ and Salt Lake County of which both have been invoiced.
She is working on getting a maintenance schedule and bids on the cost to maintain it. Utah Open
Lands wants to hold a ribbon cutting.

Legislation Converting Townships to Cities and Towns
Polly McLean stated this item will be discussed at the interim meeting on August
8 . There is not a draft bill to convert the metro townships to cities or towns. Since Emigration
Canyon has over 1,000 residents it would convert to a city. The metro townships would convert
to a five-member form of government with an elected mayor.
