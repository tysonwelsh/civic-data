December 14, 2021

The Public Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by
Chairman Anderson at 1:30 p.m. on December 14, 2021, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers and via Zoom, at 197 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. Commissioners
in attendance were Brandon Anderson, Kevin Jones, Todd Jones, Slade Hughes, Frank Leuck,
Reed Scow, and Mark Owens. County Staff in attendance were Scott Messel, Community

APPEAL, An appeal by Leon Rogers of Staff decision and interpretation of Washington
County Zoning Code denying a Short Term Rental License on Parcel SVES-1-18-HV
located at 601 South 250 West near Leeds. The appellant and applicant is Leon Rogers.

Mr. Messel proceeded to explain the appeal process and went on to state that the appellant
owns the property in Silver Valley Estates and wishes to appeal interpretation of code for
a denial of a Short Term Rental License by Staff. The building permit for the home was
issued March 17, 2021. Mr. Messel displayed supplemental building documents that were
submitted at the time of the building permit application, one of which was the signed Owner
Builder Certification and Agreement and explained what the form’s purpose is in regards
to the building permit. The Owner Builder form states that the property owner will serve
as the contractor of the dwelling, and that the homeowner agrees that the dwelling will only
be used for personal and none commercial uses.

Mr. Messel explained that later the owner attempted to apply for a business license to
operate a short term rental on the property and the applicant wishes to be “grandfathered”
under the previous STR code. The appellant contacted Community Development on or
around October 12, 2021 and was told he did not qualify to rent short term as outlined in
state and county code. The appellant felt that because his home was under construction and
that it was his intent to rent the home as a STR, that he should be “grandfathered” under
the old code. Mr. Messel explained that the county cannot regulate intent and had no
indication of the intent of the appellant to rent the home as a short term rental at the building
permit stage given the owner’s certification that it was a single family residence and only
