                           Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                                 April, 2022


  The total calls for the month of March were the highest we have recorded during the last year,
  although our cases were relatively low. This is largely due to the fact every traffic stop we make
  is categorized as a “call”, which was a change that was implemented last year to create
  consistency amongst all the agencies in the valley for billing purposes at VECC. We conducted
  well over 400 traffic stops during the month to remind the drivers on our roadways to reduce
  their speed and obey traffic laws to keep our streets safe as we transition from winter to spring.

  The investigative highlight of the month was a case which received a lot of media attention and
  was a case where a 29-year-old male suspect sexually assaulted a 14-year-old female victim.
  The suspect represented himself to be a 20-year-old and started an on-line relationship with the
  victim on social media. After developing the relationship for a period of time, he eventually
  picked the young victim up from school and engaged in sexual acts with her in exchange for
  drugs. Fortunately, the victim’s mother tracked her phone and found her in an unconscious state
  with the suspect. She was immediately taken to the hospital and received medical care.

  Our investigators teamed up with the Internet Crimes Against Children Task Force and a search
  warrant was served on the suspect’s home in Riverton and the suspect was arrested on multiple
  felony counts which included Rape of a Child, Sodomy of a Child, Sexual Exploitation, Human
  Trafficking, Enticing a Minor, and Dealing Harmful Materials to a Minor. Great job by our
  investigative team to put this predator behind bars.
