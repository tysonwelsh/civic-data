FLEET BY HOLDING OVER VEHICLES TO BE USED IN THE SHERIFF'S OFFICE AS PER

COMMISSIONER LEE — SINCE WE ARE GOING THROUGH A PROCESS, ISN’T THIS
SOMETHING WE SHOULDN’T HOLD UNTIL WE GO THROUGH THAT PROCESS?

RICHARD NIELSON/HEAD OF PUBLIC WORKS - THIS WAS ON THE AGENDA A
COUPLE MONTHS AGO AND IT WAS CONTINUED FOR A WEEK OR TWO. THE
SHERIFF’S OFFICE ASKED LAST WEEK WHAT THE STATUS WAS, AND WE SAW
THAT IT WASN’T ADDED BACK ON THE AGENDA, SO WE ADDED IT TO FIND OUT
WHAT IS THE NEXT STEP.

COMMISSIONER IVIE — WOULD LIKE TO HEAR FROM THE SHERIFF.

SHERIFF JEFFREY JONES/JAIL LIEUTENANT — TRUCKS IN THE KITCHEN/GARDEN
AREA. HAS SAVED UP THOUSANDS OF DOLLARS IN OPPORTUNITY BUYS AS
WELL AS FULFILLING OUR CONTRACT WITH MEALS ON WHEELS.

WE DO NOT HAVE THE CAPACITY TO FEED FROM OUTSIDE OF THE KITCHEN IN
A DISASTER AND WE’RE IN THE PROCESS OF FIGURING THAT OUT AND USING
SOME OF THE EQUIPMENT TO BE ABLE TO FEED OUTSIDE OF THE KITCHEN
CAUSE IF THE KITCHEN GOES DOWN WE WILL NOT HAVE THE MEANS TO FEED
AND WATER, BUT WITH THE TRUCK AND EMERGENCY TRAILERS WE WILL BE
ABLE TO ASSIST WITH THAT.

COMMISSIONER IVIE - IF WE SHIFT OUR FLEET, WHAT WOULD YOU DO WITH
THIS VEHICLE. WHAT DO YOU SEE AS THE LONG-TERM OBJECTIVE?

SHERIFF JONES — WE WILL MAKE IT WORK UNTIL WE FIGURE OUT SOMETHING

COMMISSIONER AINGE — THERE ARE SEVERAL VEHICLES ON THIS? THIS IS AN
INTERESTING TIME FOR A HOLDOVER. WE ARE NOT JUST TALKING ABOUT THE
LEASING VS. OWN, BUT WE HAVE VEHICLES IN OUR FLEET THAT MAY NOT BE
NECESSARY RIGHT NOW. NEED TO CUT BACK OR MAKING SURE THROUGH AN
AUDIT TO MAKE SURE THEY ARE ENTIRELY NECESSARY.

LIEUTENANT NANCY KILLIAN/SHERIFF’S OFFICE (JAIL) - OVER THE TRAINING
OF THE NEW CADETS THAT COME INTO THE ACADEMY. CURRENTLY HAVE
SEVERAL ACADEMIES GOING ON AT ONE TIME. OVER THE LAST FEW YEARS,
WE HAVE GONE INTO THE NEGATIVE BALANCE ON OUR MILEAGE BUDGET. WE
HAVE TO PROVIDE MILEAGE OR A CAR FOR OUR CADETS TO GO TO THE
ACADEMY. THIS IS ONE OF THE REASONS FOR THE REQUEST FOR THE
ADDITIONAL CARS TO HELP REDUCE THE NUMBER OF MILES WE USE. WE HAD 6
PEOPLE JUST GRADUATE AND 4 PEOPLE STILL IN THE ACADEMY AND TWO
MORE STARTING IN NOVEMBER. THEY HAVE DIFFERENT LOCATIONS,
DIFFERENT TIMES TO BE AT THE ACADEMY. DID SOME QUICK CALCULATION ON
WHAT WE WOULD PAY IN MILEAGE FOR ONE ACADEMY CAR, FIVE DAYS A
WEEK FOR 52 WEEKS AS WE’VE HAD, IT’D BE $23,400 IN MILEAGE
REIMBURSEMENT. THAT’S FOR ONLY 1 ACADEMY.

COMMISSIONER AINGE ASKED IF SHE HAD DONE THE ANALYSIS ON THE COST
OF MILEAGE VS. OWNING, DEPRECIATING, FUELING THE VEHICLE?

KILLIAN - HAVE NOT. WE CURRENTLY WE ARE NEGATIVE ALMOST $6,000 IN
OUR MILEAGE BUDGET.

COMMISSIONER AINGE — THAT’S NOT A BIG DEAL. THE QUESTION IS WHAT IS
THE MORE COST-EFFECTIVE WAY TO TRANSPORT?

MEMBER OF VICTIM’S ADVOCATES GROUP -CAN GUARANTEE YOU THESE
VEHICLES ARE AS BUSY AS THE SHERIFF’S, CONSTANTLY GOING FROM ONE END
OF THE COUNTY TO THE OTHER. USES ABOUT 18-20,000 MILES A YEAR
(APPROXIMATELY THE SAME AS A DETECTIVE).

RICHARD NIELSEN - THESE VEHICLES ARE THE ONES THAT WERE ALREADY
SCHEDULED TO BE ROTATED AND NEW ONES HAVE BEEN PURCHASED. THE
PROCEDURE IS THAT THESE COME TO THE COMMISSION AT THE REQUEST OF
THE DEPARTMENT WANTING TO HOLD THEM OVER, THEN YOU MAKE A
DECISION WHETHER WE INCREASE THE FLEET. THEY’VE ALREADY BEEN
RECAPPED AND REPLACED SO WE DO NOT HAVE THAT COMPONENT OF THE
COST OTHER THAN THE MAINTENANCE.
