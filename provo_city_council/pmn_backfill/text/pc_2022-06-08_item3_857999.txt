STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations. Key points addressed in the Staff's presentation to the Planning Commission included the
following:
   • The subject area is functions as a park and a vacant field with a power transformer upon it.
   • The OSPR zone is only for publicly owned parcels and the PF Zone is for public and private enterprises operating
       for the public convenience.
   • 12 parcels are privately owned by Utah Power and Light Company and 1 parcel is owned by Provo City
   • The City has an agreement with Utah Power and Light Company to use their parcels as a park.
   • This proposal aligns with the Park and Recreation Master Plan and the Southwest Future Land Use Map

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING DATE

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
