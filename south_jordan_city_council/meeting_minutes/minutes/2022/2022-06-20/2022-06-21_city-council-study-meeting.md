Present:        Mayor Dawn Ramsey, Council Member Tamara Zander, Council Member Patrick
                Harris, Council Member Brad Marlor, Council Member Jason McGuire, City
                Manager Gary Whatcott, Assistant City Manager Dustin Lewis, City Attorney
                Ryan Loose, Senior IT Technician Phill Brown, IT Director Jon Day, City

Absent:         Council Member Don Shelton

4:47 PM
STUDY MEETING

       A. Welcome, Roll Call and Introduction: By Mayor Dawn R. Ramsey

Mayor Ramsey welcomed everyone present and introduced the meeting. She excused Council
Member Don Shelton who was unable to attend tonight.

       B. Invocation: By Council Member Patrick Harris

Council Member Harris offered the invocation.

       C. Mayor and Council Coordination

Mayor Ramsey said she is not aware of the city having a “tagline.” She would love to have a
conversation about coming up with one.

Assistant City Manager Dustin Lewis noted that other cities have them, for example,
Centerville’s is “Friendly city beside the Great Salt Lake.” He will add this to their discussion
during strategic planning.

Council Member Harris noted he drove by one earlier that said “Malad, where Idaho begins.”

Council Member Zander asked if they wanted to get some brainstorming from people outside the
council.

Mayor Ramsey said she would be okay with council members asking others for suggestions.
They could open it up citywide as a contest with a prize, but they can figure that out later.

       D. Discussion/Review of Regular Council Meeting

          - Presentation: Music Man cast performing musical number.
          - Resolution R2022-29, City Policies.

          - Resolution R2022-30, Tier 2 Public Safety and Firefighter pickup election.

Assistant City Manager Lewis said the MOU was for the Bentley Dealership, allowing them to
work out some things now before deadlines change.

Mayor Ramsey noted the City Policy Resolution is one that is done every year on ethics. She
asked if the Tier 2 Public Safety and Firefighter pick up election is the difference we have to
cover.

Assistant City Manager Lewis said that’s correct, we pick up just like the other cities.

Mayor Ramsey noted they have gone over the final budget quite a bit.

Council Member Brad Marlor asked if we have had any comments from residents regarding the
budget.

Council Member Tamara Zander said there was a heckler in the parade.

Mayor Ramsey said she knows the gentleman, and actually invited him to discuss the budget
with him.

Assistant City Manager Lewis said he doesn’t recall any comments specifically about budget line
items, the comments were mostly “don’t raise my taxes” types of comments. He will check with
CFO Sunil Naidu to make sure nothing was missed.

Mayor Ramsey said she received an email from the planning commission, specifically Chair
Michele Hollist that the commission was very supportive of the text amendment and agreed that
the changes will make for a stronger Planning Commission with the inclusion of the mayor’s
appointee. Chair Hollist did mention that with district changes, each council member’s
commission member might not live in their district anymore, and that person can be
grandfathered in for the rest of their term, or changed. They proposed the idea to her potentially
having her appointment be that person, however she has already spoken to someone else about it
before the districting situation came up.

Council Member Marlor believes the current commissioners should be grandfathered in for the
current terms.

Council Member Jason McGuire said that his intention for his appointee was to have her
grandfathered in.
