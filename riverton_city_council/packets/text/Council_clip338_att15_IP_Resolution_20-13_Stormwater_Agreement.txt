                                       City Council Meeting Agenda
                                             February 18, 2020


PRESENTER:                Trace Robinson

                          Resolution No 20-13 Authorizing the Mayor to Execute a Storm Water
ITEM:                     Drainage Impact Fee Reimbursement Agreement Between Riverton City
                          and Hidden Pines Subdivision - City Engineer, Trace Robinson

SUMMARY:

Background: The developer of the Hidden Pines Subdivision located at approximately 3870 West
13800 South, was required to construct storm drainage system infrastructure in excess of the
developments storm water impact fee obligation. The storm drain system is identified in the Capital
Facilities Plan and will benefit other developments in the basin. The agreement is set forth to provide
a means of reimbursing the developer for excess fees.

FISCAL IMPACT:            $27,759.30

FUNDING SOURCE:           Storm Water Impact Fees

ATTACHMENTS:

Resolution No 20-13 Hidden Pines Stormwater.docx Hidden
Pines StormWater Impact Fee Reimbursement.pdf

RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

Approve Resolution authorizing the Mayor to execute a storm water drainage impact fee
reimbursement agreement between Riverton City and Cazco Enterprises, LLC.



Motion: I move the City Council approve Resolution No. 20-13, authorizing the Mayor to execute
a storm water drainage impact fee reimbursement agreement between Riverton City and Cazco
Enterprises, LLC
