                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 23-67

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A MEMORANDUM OF
       UNDERSTANDING WITH THE STATE OF UTAH, DEPARTMENT OF
                        TRANSPORTATION

        WHEREAS, due to an increase in traffic congestion and use of Bangerter Highway, the
State of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) has a need for additional space to
improve the interchange at 13400 South and Bangerter Highway; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT desires to expand Bangerter Highway, in part by encroaching upon
the western edge of the Subject Property, and has offered to purchase a portion of said property
from Riverton; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton has expressed a desire for the reconstructed interchange at 13400
South and Bangerer Highway be a grade separated interchange, resulting in a differential cost of
approximately $20,000,000.00; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that some contribution to
the costs for installing a grade separated interchange is warranted; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT has expressed a desire to receive two small parcels of real property
from Riverton City to enable the realignment of Rose Creek and to accommodate off ramps
adjacent to CR Hamilton Sports Park; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT is further willing to provide Riverton City with a pedestrian bridge
which originally spanned Bangerter Highway at approximately 12700 South for possible use at
13400 South and at a location to be determined to the west of the intersection at Bangerter
Highway; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that conveyance of said
right of way to UDOT will not interfere with the ordinary flow of traffic on city streets, will not
interfere with the operation of City utility systems in the vicinity of the Subject Property, and
will further advance the health and safety of Riverton City by improving safe traffic circulation
in the vicinity of Bangerter Highway and 13400 South; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the value of property
offered by UDOT and further consideration provided in the MOU is supported the exchange
value of Riverton City’s receipt of a grade separated interchange at Bangerter Highway and the
Mountain View Corridor;
       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

      1.     The Riverton City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute a Memorandum of
             Understanding with the State of Utah, Department of Transportation, in
             substantially similar form as provided in Exhibit A, attached hereto, upon
             verification the same is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

      2.     Resolution Nos. 23-29 and 23-30 are hereby repealed.

      3.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
August 2023 by the following vote:
