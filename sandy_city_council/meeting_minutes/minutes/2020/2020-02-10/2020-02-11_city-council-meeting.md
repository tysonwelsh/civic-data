                                  The meeting reconvened for the 6:00 p.m Citizen Comments after a brief break.



3.         20-057                 February 18, 2020 agenda preview.

                                  Kris Nicholl reviewed next weeks City Council agenda.

6:00 Citizen Comments
                                  Mike Applegarth read a citizen comment into the record Jodi Monico expressed support
                                  for the increase of household dogs in Sandy, live stream for public meetings, decorum of
                                  the City Council members and patience with residents; and the tone of Council's
                                  responses encouraging or discouraging resident participation.

                                  Aaron DeKiser, Living Wasatch, expressed concern and reasons why is opposes the new
                                  LED street lights based on light pollution, health and environment issues. He will be
                                  working with the Administration to see if the lights can be exchanged.

                                  Tom Ward discussed carefully reviewed data on the new LED lights along with safety
                                  concerns on dropping the color temperature of a light source.(Kelvin)

                                  Monica Zoltanski asked that a town hall meeting be held to address residents questions
                                  on last year's water issue. A discussion followed.
