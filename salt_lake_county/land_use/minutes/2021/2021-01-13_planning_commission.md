CUP2020-000004 - Laurice Lake is requesting Conditional Use approval to allow for nine (9) horses on 
the subject property. Acreage: 5.6 acres.  Location: 7841 West Canyon Road.  Zone: FR-5. Planner: Jim

Nakamura (Motion/Voting) 
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting 
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.  
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jim Nakamura provided an analysis of the Staff 
Report. 
 
Commissioner Cohen asked if the letters received have been addressed. Mr. Nakamura confirmed he did 
respond to the questions and there were no further questions from the citizens. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public meeting, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion.  
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Applicant 
Name: Laurice Lake 
Address: 7841 West Canyon Road 
Comments: Ms. Lake said this is for a conditional use permit. Mr. Nakamura was sufficient  in his staff 
report. 
 
Commissioner Cohen confirmed she was applying for nine horses. Ms. Lake said she has  seven but is 
applying for nine just in case her horses have another colt. Commissioner Cohen asked if she already has 
a conditional use permit. Ms. Lake said no and that horses were for her own personal uses. Commissioner 
Cohen confirmed she can comply with the conditions in the staff report. Ms. Lake said they have a dumpster 
to haul off manure and the whole property is fenced. 
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Glenn Trendler 
Address: 7858 West Canyon Road 
Comments: Mr. Trendler said he just wanted to support the recommendation to approve. 
 
Commissioner Vance motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner O’Meara seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the protected watershed, waste removal, and 
conditions of approval. 
 
Motion: To approve application # CUP2020-000004 for nine horses subject to conditions 1 -3 in the staff 
report. 
Motion by: Commissioner Cohen 
2nd by: Commissioner Vance 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
