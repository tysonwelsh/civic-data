OVERVIEW OF REQUEST

The applicant, Dr. Kirk Moore, owner of the property and his contractor, Mr. Mark Richards of
Intermountain Wind and Solar, are requesting the Planning Commission to review a modified site plan
for the addition to the site of two carport structures which will host solar panels on the roofs. The
existing zoning is Special Development-Harada (SD-Harada). Sandy City recently amended the
development code (case # CODE-04-20-5845) to allow accessory structure carports in the front, side
and rear yards of buildings in this particular zoning district. The Planning Commission is the land use
approval body for this action, as designated in the Sandy City Development Code.

As mentioned above in the case history section, the original site plan review and subdivision
amendment to create this lot at the rear of the existing building (7525 S. Union Park Ave.) was
reviewed and approved by the Planning Commission back in 2015. Finalization of the original site

plan was slow and construction was slow. The new building was not completed until 2020.

The carport request was initiated by a building permit application submitted by the solar panel
contractor in January of 2020. Staff was not able to approve the building permit, because the proposed
carport structures would have been located over existing surface parking stalls, approved by the 2015
site plan review, because the Sandy City Development Code did not allow "Accessory Structures" (the
carports) in the building setback for this new lot. The building setbacks are 10 feet on the side yard
(Northwest) and 30 feet on the rear yard (Northeast).

Planning staff worked with the applicant to craft an amendment to the Development Code provision
which prevented the "Accessory Structure" to be approved, within this specific zone only, by a site
plan approval by the Planning Commission. Hence, this application which was filed in March 2020 in
anticipation of the City approving the Development Code amendment which allows the proposed
addition of carports within the required building setback yards via approval by the Planning
Commission.

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING
A neighborhood/community meeting is not required for this application.

ANALYSIS

PROPOSED SITE PLAN AND BUILDINGS

Building Siting and Massing. This proposal for two carport structures to be located over existing
surface parking stalls within the required building yard setbacks, does not negatively affect the
building location or the site plan of the prior review and approval by the City. It simply adds carport
structures upon which solar panels will be mounted to support the electrical needs of the surgical
office building, to be located above existing surface parking stalls. There are no buildings on abutting
lots that will be impacted by the carport structures, since the properties on the Northwest and
Southeast are surface parking lots, and a steep, heavily vegetated hillside is located in the rear.

Parking. The change does not reduce the number of parking stalls on the site.

Landscaping. The change does not reduce the landscaping on the site.

City Department Review. All other City Departments and Divisions, except the Police Department,
have reviewed the project plans and support the preliminary site plan review approval by the Planning
Commission, subject to their usual and standard comments and conditions, to be perfected with the
final site plan review by staff. The Police Department will require that photo-sensing controlled
lighting be located under the carport roof structures, and that a new photometric plan showing this be
prepared and submitted for staff review and approval as part of the finalization of this site plan review.

Building Architectural Design, Materials & Colors. The building architectural design, materials
and colors meet the requirements of the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards and are not
affected by the proposed change, except to minimally impair the off-site view of the building from
neighboring properties.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that preliminary site plan review is complete
and that the proposed carport structures are approved, based on the staff report, and the two findings
listed below and subject to the following five conditions:

FINDINGS:

    A. That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer and the
       Transportation Engineer, have preliminarily approved the proposed site plan.

    B. That the Police Department will approve the carports provided they are lighted under the roof to
       discourage negative behavior and car burglaries.


CONDITIONS:

    1. That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior to the
       start of any construction (including payment of development fees and posting of an appropriate
       bond to guarantee completion of all required improvements on and off the site), according to
       the Site Plan Review Procedures Handout. The final site plan shall be in compliance with all
       Development Code requirements and those modifications required/allowed by the Planning
       Commission.

    2. That the development complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes
       applicable to this type of use.

    3. That the applicant complies with the Sandy City Noise Ordinance, including working hours
       during construction.

    4. That the applicant provides City Staff with a new photometric plan that incorporates lighting
       under the carport roofs of a sufficient level to help deter negative behavior and increase safety.

    5. That the applicant complies with all department requirements as noted in all preliminary
       review letters prior to submittal for final site plan review.
