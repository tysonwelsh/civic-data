                          RESOLUTION NO. 23-19

RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY GRANTING
CONSENT TO THE COUNTY OF SALT LAKE TO FORM THE SALT LAKE
COUNTY CONVENTION AND TOURISM ASSESSMENT AREA

      WHEREAS, the Convention and Tourism Business Assessment Area, Act,
Utah Code Title 11, Chapter 42b, (the “Act”), Section 11-42b-103 authorizes the
County to form a convention and tourism assessment area for the purposes
of promoting tourism; and

       WHEREAS, the County Council of the County of Salt Lake has requested
consent to form the Salt Lake County Convention and Tourism Assessment
Area (the “SLCo CTAA”) in the City of Draper with adoption of Salt Lake County
Council Resolution No.6083, dated April 4, 2023;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City of
Draper, that:

      Section 1: The above recitals are true and correct.

      Section 2: The County Council of the County of Salt Lake is hereby
granted consent to include the City of Draper in the SLCo CTAA, for the
formation of the SLCo CTAA and future renewals.

        Section 3: The Draper city recorder is hereby directed to transmit a
certified copy of this Resolution to the Clerk of the Salt Lake County Council.

      Section 4: This Resolution is effective upon its adoption.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 16TH DAY OF MAY, 2023.
