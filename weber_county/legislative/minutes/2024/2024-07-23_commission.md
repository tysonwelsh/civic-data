                                                                 MINUTES
                                                    WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                    Tuesday, July 23rd, 2024 – 10:00 a.m.
                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.


WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: James “Jim” H. Harvey, Sharon Bolos, and Gage Froerer (excused)
STAFF PRESENT Jason Horne, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office; Liam Keogh, of the Deputy County Attorney’s
office; and Craig D. Brandt, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME: Chair Harvey
B. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Chad Averett
C. INVOCATION: Shelly Halacy
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: We had an update from the Sheriff’s Department on a recent
   traumatic case. Chief Gard passed along to us how professional and graciously his staff have handled this case.
   I just want to express my gratitude to those who see and do those things we don’t see and do, so we can have
   justice.
E. PUBLIC COMMENT: NONE.
F. RECOGNITION:
   1. Recognition of the Patrick Davis Estate for the generous donation to the Weber County Animal Shelter.
      John Bond (County Treasurer): I was contacted a few weeks ago from an investment firm saying a client had
      a large donation for our animal shelter. I would like to introduce ... I want to thank Rena Kirkman and Jacob
      Brown of First Western Advisors and their affiliation with Raymond James Financial for assisting with this
      donation. Patrick G Davis grew up in Wyoming as an avid outdoorsman. His family moved to Ogden in the
      1940s where he attended and graduated from St. Joseph High School in the 1950s. He worked for the United
      States Postal Service where he took a medical retirement after being diagnosed with cerebral palsy. Patrick
      had a love for all animals especially cats. He was a kind of gentle man that wanted a best chance for all
      animals especially those in shelters. He donated $119,464.81 to our shelter.

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Chair Harvey: If you go the end of Highway 89 near the Box Elder County border near Rocky Point, there is
      a monument to trapper John C Weber, one of the first trappers in the area. Another monument at 40th and
      Washington for Peter Skeen Ogden. They encouraged early Mormon settlers to go west and there would be
      great land out here. We still have the most coveted things in the state, such as the Pioneer Day celebration. ...
         We encourage all to celebrate this wonderful history of those who were the first settlers in this area by attending
         either the rodeo or any of the many festivities put on the by the Pioneer Heritage Foundation or the parade on
         Wednesday morning which is free.
