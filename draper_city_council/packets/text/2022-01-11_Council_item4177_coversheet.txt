Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: David Dobbins, City Manager
CC:  Date: January 11, 2022
Re: Ordinance 1523 Comments: This ordinance amends subsections 3-5-020, 3-5-030 and 3-5-050 of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding public comment in committee and commission meetings.  The amended code directs public comment on issues within the purview of a committee or commission to the City Council and not the committee or commission.
