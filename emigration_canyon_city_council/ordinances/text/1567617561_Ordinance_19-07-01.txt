EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 19-07-01, July 25, 2019
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE CURRENT APPLICABLE CODE CONCERNING
THE NUMBER OF MEMBERS ON THE TOWNSHIP PLANNING COMMISSION
RECITALS
A. Whereas, the Township has previously adopted an ordinance creating a planning

commission which commission consists of seven (7) persons; and

B. The Council wishes to amend the ordinance to provide for a Planning
Commission of five (5) persons.

ORDINANCE

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:
I. The current code Chapter 19.05.010 is amended to read as follows:
19.05.010 - Appointment—Term.

The planning commission for the Emigration Canyon Metro Township shall consist of five
members and up to two alternate members appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of
the metro township council. Commission Members shall serve three-year terms or longer until
successors are appointed. Terms shall commence on March ‘st of each year. In the event a term
of a member shall expire before a successor is appointed, the member shall continue to serve
until a successor is appointed. Terms of at least one member, and not more than two, shall expire
each year. The members and alternate members of the planning commission shall be registered
voters residing in any area of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township.

II. Nothing in this Ordinance shall be construed to affect the eligibility or qualifications to serve
of any of the present members of the planning commission whose terms have not expired or to
affect their eligibility for reappointment.
