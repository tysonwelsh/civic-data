                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                                May 2020
May continued many of the same trends we have seen during the COVID pandemic, with a
reduced total call volume and a reduced number of citations as we attempt to slow the spread of
the disease. That said, although we have reduced the volume of our traffic stops, we have
focused our efforts on impaired driving enforcement to ensure the safety of our citizens.

As a result of these targeted efforts, we made 16 arrests for Driving Under the Influence of
Alcohol or Drugs during the month, which is our third highest month since our inception. This
occurred during a month when our total volume of traffic stops was significantly less than other
months.

I attribute this to our officers developing their skills to identify the signs of an impaired driver
based on the driving pattern they are observing on our roadways. It is a testament to our
officer’s commitment to discourage impaired driving in our city and save lives on our streets.
