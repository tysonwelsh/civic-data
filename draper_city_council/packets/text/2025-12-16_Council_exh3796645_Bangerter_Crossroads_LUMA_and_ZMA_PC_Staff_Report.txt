BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Land Use Map Amendment and a Zoning
Map Amendment for approximately 17.88 acres located on the west side of Bangerter
Highway, north of 13800 South, and west of 300 East, at approximately 13782 S. 300 E.
(Exhibit C & D). The property is currently zoned RA1. The applicant is requesting that the
Land Use Map and Zoning Map Amendments be approved to allow for the future
development of the overall site as a commercial center.

The overall subject property consists of seven (7) parcels ranging in size from
approximately 0.16 acres to 12.82 acres. Four (4) of the parcels previously had single family

According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA1
zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to
promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of
limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to
be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and
industrial uses.”

The purpose of the CR zone is to, “foster low density development with little impact on its
surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's
semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in
these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from
encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.”

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.

      1. We have reviewed the subject zone map amendment application, proposing
         approximately 18 acres to be zoned from Residential/Agricultural one acre (RA1)
         to Regional Commercial (CR) in accordance with the provisions of Section 9-5-
         060(E)(1)(e) of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC). We speak primarily to the
         adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property. In
         making an amendment, the City Council should consider the following factors.
         Accordingly, the following comments are recommended for your consideration:

      2. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property,
         including but not limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and
         fire protection, schools, storm water drainage systems, water supplies, and
         waste water and refuse collection; Other than noted below, we are not aware of
         any inadequacies of the facilities intended to serve this property.

          A. The change in zone from residential / agricultural one acre (RA1) to a
             commercial zoning, Regional Commercial (CR), represents a significant
             increase in traffic based on the density potential of the proposed zone. The
             proposed zone would increase traffic based on any of the potential proposed
             uses allowed in the zone and the intensity of those uses within the subject
             area. That being said, the fronting streets are 300 East, 13800 South, and
             Bangerter Hwy which are classified as major collector or arterial streets with
             significant traffic capacity. There are peak-time commuter and school
             congestion in the area, along 150 East and at each of the intersections
             surrounding the subject parcel. The size of the subject parcel and the
             potential number of trips generated from the subject area require mitigation
             measures, where mitigation is typically contemplated around 100 peak hour
             trips. A traffic impact study (TIS) has been completed, analyzing the intensity
             for traffic generation of the subject area based on the probable land uses
             and development density. The TIS report outlines both city deficiencies,
              which the city is addressing, and project mitigation requirements. The project
              mitigation improvements will be a requirement of any site development or
              subdivision approval by the city. Those improvements will be completed as
              part of the development construction. There are incomplete public frontage
              improvements existing on the subject site and the missing improvements,
              park strips and sidewalks, will be required of any project.

          B. Connectivity with the subject area to public rights-of-way are established
             based the city’s access management plan, as analyzed in a traffic impact
             study (TIS), and an existing agreement between the applicant and UDOT, for
             an access on Bangerter Hwy. Access onto 300 East, 13800 South, and
             Bangerter Hwy are controlled in order to limit impacts from the commercial
             development to the existing and future traffic on the surrounding streets.
             Development within the subject area will be required to share accesses and
             have cross-access agreements in place for each site due to the limited access
             locations available. Accesses are be required to meet Draper City standards
             as outlined in our Master Transportation Plan. Access is evaluated at the time
             of a site plan or subdivision application and will follow the requirements of
             the TIS.

          C. There are existing storm drainage facilities fronting the subject parcel that
             are feasible to connect to from the existing site. Any future site drainage will
             be required to be addressed with any subdivision or site plan application and
             shall comply with the rules and regulations pertaining to site drainage and
             the provisions of the development requirements within the Draper City
             Municipal Code.

          D. Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by Jordan Basin Improvement
             District, formally South Valley Sewer District. Any subdivision or site plan
             application will require a commitment to serve from the sewer district that
             facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.

          E. Drinking water is provided by WaterPro to the subject parcel. Any subdivision
             or site plan application will require a commitment to serve from WaterPro
             that facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.

   3. Accordingly, we will provide comments relative to the potential construction
      activities when the appropriate application is received.

We would like to express our appreciation to Draper City for considering our request for
rezoning and land use amendments for approximately 18 acres located at the intersection of
13800 South and Bangerter Highway. The property is situated across from a shopping center on
the west side of Bangerter Highway, which includes a Harmons grocery store and various retail
outlets. We look forward to engaging with our neighbors, the Planning Commission, and the City
Council to discuss this project further.

We are requesting a rezone to CR (Regional Commercial) for the property, as well as the same
designation for the land use amendment. This zoning request is appropriate given the property’s
strategic location within the City of Draper. It is positioned at the intersection of two major
traffic corridors—Bangerter Highway and 13800 South. The proposed development will serve as
a shopping district, capitalizing on "pass-by" trips from people already traveling on these roads
for other purposes. The developer, in collaboration with the city, has worked to secure funding
for essential roadway improvements from the Utah Department of Transportation. A traffic study
was commissioned to evaluate current conditions and analyze the additional traffic generated by
the proposed development. The improvements outlined in the study are not only necessary for
the development but will also improve traffic flow in the area immediately.

From a community development perspective, this site represents one of the last remaining large
parcels within Draper City that can accommodate an anchor tenant. This will provide local
residents with greater shopping options nearby, while stimulating economic growth within the
City.

Currently, most of the property is zoned RA-1, and the City’s Master Land Use Plan designates it
for medium-density residential, low/medium-density residential, and office/service uses on a
small parcel fronting 13800 South. We are requesting a land use designation of CR (Regional
Commercial), which aligns with the property’s unique location and potential. This rezoning
offers an opportunity for Draper City to enhance its retail offerings, expand its commercial base,
and bring additional convenience to residents without introducing multi-story buildings. Given
its position along a major arterial road and across from an existing shopping center, this
development will serve as a valuable asset to the area.
