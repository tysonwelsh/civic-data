White City Community Council Report

             Council Member Dickerson stated the Association Community Councils
Together (ACCT) leadership is staying the same as last year.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority

                Captain Marc West stated Unified Fire Authority (UFA) is working on a one on
one concept, where a paramedic and EMT will respond to emergencies and will be able to provide
life saving services. Last year the paramedic school was shut down because only two or three
people would go through the school and it was costing $100,000 to run. If an employee has
minimum qualification and want to go through paramedic school, UFA will pay for it. The fire
stations in the canyons will be reduced to a three-person crew and the other stations will be a
four-person crew. There is a peak ambulance that is on duty during peak times. He reminded the
community to keep fire hydrants clear of snow.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Officer Paula Stinson stated calls went down for the month of January with 75
calls for service. Another home in White City has been condemned because of meth
contamination. The home itself is in poor condition. A building inspector is working on the case.
The other house that was condemned has been cleaned up and is under contract. She is working
on the parking problems in the area.

               Chief Randy Thomas recognized Officer Stinson for the month of December for
her unique way in dealing with the White City Metro Township community, incorporated areas,
and the Jr. High. The regular police officer at the Jr. High had to take a leave of absence and

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Perry stated Chief Peterson has spoken to Sandy City and they
are working on finalizing the details. Member voting will be changed to one vote for per member;
however, if the item has a budget impact the vote will be weighted. Mayor Jeff Silvestrini is Chair
and Council Member Eric Ferguson is Vice-Chair of the board. UFSA is discussing upgrades for
fire stations. UFA votes for officers every year; Mayor Rob Dahle will continue as Chair and he
was nominated and selected for Vice-Chair for 2019.

                                    −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Price stated she was reelected to serve as Chair. There are
concerns about increased mosquito numbers because of the increase in moisture. The board
reviewed the bylaws and changed the requirement for elected officers to serve a maximum of
five-years. A new treasurer was elected, and district goals were set. The board is changing the
mangers, planning new routes, and creating a comprehensive plan for new employees. There is
a beaver problem which creates an issue with the mosquito population. Seasonal employees will
start in March and there is no tax increase.
