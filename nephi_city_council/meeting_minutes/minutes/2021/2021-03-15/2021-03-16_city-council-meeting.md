PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by five members of the Juab High School state championship
wrestling team. The young men recently helped win Juab’s fifth straight team title in the 3A
championship. Channing Warner took a few minutes to talk about the wrestling program under Coach
Joel Holman.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting held on 3-2-21, work session held on 3-2-21, work session held
on 3-9-21, sale of burial rights to Brittney Christensen, Troy and Ronda Lange, and a list of claims on the
warrant register dated 3-16-21. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to approve the business license applications of Skipp and Co, and Starrco
Trading LLC. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

LIBRARY DIRECTOR RUTH BONZO REPORTS ON LIBRARY GRANTS:

  Library Director Ruth Bonzo reported to the council that she has received another $5,000 COVID relief
grant that she plans on using to purchase washable keyboards and mouses and some audio-visual
equipment to help with story time. She also reported that she has received a Thrive grant of $2,000 to
purchase Utah authors’ titles. She has plans to create a story kit about Utah and hold an event featuring
Utah authors and will be promoting Utah history books for the summer reading program. The council
expressed their appreciation for the good work she is doing as the Library Director.

CANYON HILL SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT “A” APPROVED:

   Councilor Ostler moved to approve the final plat “A” for the Canyon Hill Subdivision. This was a one
lot subdivision where the Baptist church is located. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NEBO SOUTH COMMERCIAL SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to approve the final plat for Nebo South Commercial Subdivision. This
subdivision is a seven-lot subdivision with current plans for lot 1 to be retail sales of campers and
sporting equipment. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

ADDITIONS TO THE ANNEXATION AREA MAP APPROVED:

    Councilor Worwood moved to add some property on State Road 132, west of the city to the
annexation area map at a Level 3. The property owners are proposing developing a slaughterhouse on
the property. The property is not contiguous to the city boundaries and would be an island annexation
if the owners petition for annexation and if approved by the council. Councilor Seely seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
    Councilor Memmott moved to move the same property to a Level 2 on the annexation area map,
noting that the council does not normally move property to a Level 2 in the same meeting that it is
added to the annexation area map, but in this instance the council has had much discussion in several
open meetings about the issue. An in-depth analysis can be done on the feasibility of the proposed
development. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
    Councilor Worwood moved to add some property located at the mouth of the canyon on State Road
132 to the annexation area map at a Level 3. The property is owned by Nephi City and is contiguous to
the city boundaries. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 03-16-2021-A ADOPTED-ESTABLISHING AN INDUSTRIAL ZONE 2:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council that because of recent requests regarding
potential slaughterhouses being located in the city limits, the council had given direction to the Planning
Commission to develop a zoning district that governed these businesses. Establishing a new zone would
provide more control over location and distance from existing residential areas. The Planning
Commission recommended adopting a new zone and then fine tuning it to include animal limitations
and other provisions. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 03-16-2021-A that includes a new
definition for slaughterhouse, and the conditions under which a slaughterhouse may be constructed,
and establishes the Industrial Zone 2 (ID-2) that identifies slaughterhouses as the sole use of the zone
and specifically excludes slaughterhouses from any other zone. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CEDAR RIDGE AMENDED PLAT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson explained to the council the need for the Cedar Ridge Development to amend their
approved final plat for Phase 1 of the project. The amended plat reduces the amount of property
assigned to Phase 1. The two phases have different corporate owners and the city would not record
Phase 2 or Phase 3 until for improvements in these phases are complete or are secured by a bond.
Councilor Memmott moved to approve the amended Cedar Ridge Plat with the conditions that before
Phase 2 or Phase 3 are recorded that the owners bond for necessary improvements and fence along the
north side of Phase 1. Councilor Seely seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

MAXWELL ASPHALT AWARDED BID FOR AIRPORT PAVEMENT MAINTENANCE PROJECT:

   Councilor Ostler moved to award the bid to Maxwell Asphalt for Phase I and Phase III of the airport
pavement maintenance project and when monies become available to then award Maxwell Asphalt the
bid for Phase II. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

TWO PIECES OF EQUIPMENT DECLARED SURPLUS PROPERTY:

  Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that the Golf Course and the Parks and Cemeteries departments
each have pieces of equipment that they would like declared surplus. The Golf Course has an old John

OUT-OF-STATE TRAVEL TO THE APPA NATIONAL CONFERENCE APPROVED:

  Electric Department Superintendent Rust Finlinson and Mayor Glade Nielson requested to attend the
American Public Power Association (APPA) national conference in Orlando, Florida on June 20-23rd.
Councilor Ostler moved to approve the out-of-state travel. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

REAL ESTATE PURCHASE CONTRACT EXTENSION APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the need to extend by 45 days a real estate contract with a
developer the city is working with to purchase some city owned property on the south end of the city.
Councilor Memmott moved to approve the extension of the contract. Councilor Seely seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR AND CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reported that an apron extension on the south end of the airport will be another large
project that will be beginning soon at the airport. He stated that the new improvements to the pilot’s
lounge are very nice and were done at a minimal cost to the city.
   Councilor Ostler reported that he Miss Nephi pageant will be held on Friday at 6:30 p.m. He also

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:
