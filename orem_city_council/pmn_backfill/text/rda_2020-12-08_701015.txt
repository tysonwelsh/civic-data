SCHEDULED ITEMS

    RESOLUTION - Authorizing the transfer of $1,292,485.00 from Redevelopment Project Area
    #85-03-A to the City of Orem to assist with the purchase of the Hillcrest Elementary property

Mr. Davidson presented a recommendation that the RDA, by resolution, authorize the transfer of
$1.292,485.00 from Redevelopment Project Area #85-03-A to the City of Orem to assist with the
City of Orem's purchase of the Hillcrest Elementary property from Alpine School District.

The former Hillcrest Elementary School property was located within Redevelopment Project Area
#85-03-A. The City of Orem had contracted to purchase the Hillcrest Elementary property from
Alpine School District. Because the City's purchase and development of the Hillcrest Elementary
property would benefit Redevelopment Project Area #85-03-A, it was proposed that the
Redevelopment Agency of the City of Orem ("RDA") transfer $1,292,485.00 from Redevelopment
Project Area #85-03-A to assist with the City's purchase of the Hillcrest Elementary property. In
the proposed resolution, the RDA (1) finds that the City of Orem's proposed purchase and
development of the Hillcrest Elementary property benefits Redevelopment Project Area #85-03-
