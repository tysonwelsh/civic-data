                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-95

          A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING RIVERTON CITY TO GRANT A
             COMMUNICATIONS NETWORK EASEMENT TO VERIZON
          WIRELESS LLC, d/b/a VERIZON WIRELESS TO ALLOW FOR THE
           INSTALLATION OF FIBER OPTIC LINE CONNECTING THEIR
         FACILITIES IN RIVERTON CITY WATER SHOP TO A TRUNK LINE
               LOCATED UNDER SANBORN DRIVE AT 3335 WEST

       WHEREAS, Verizon Wireless LLC, d/b/a Verizon Wireless currently leases space in
Riverton City’s Water Shop Facility main park to operate cellular communications equipment; and

        WHEREAS, Verizon Wireless desires to install a fiber optic line connecting their facilities
in the Water Shop Facility to a trunk line located under Sanborn Drive;

        WHEREAS, Verizon Wireless currently does not have permission under the terms of the
existing lease agreement to connect said line; and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is willing to grant Verizon Wireless a Communications Network
Easement to allow for the installation of said line, upon receipt of appropriate value paid;

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute said Communications Network Easement
          and the City Recorder is authorized to record the easement for and on behalf of Riverton
          City, once it is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney, and upon the City’s
          receipt of payment in the amount of $7,500.00 from Verizon Wireless.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 15th day of
 October 2024 by the following vote:
