42   3.1) Public hearing and consideration of Steve Kelly’s request for approval of the Ember
43   Hotel Concept with an exception for a reduction in the landscape buffer located at 26 East
44   Main Street.
45   Brittney Harris presented information on the item. She noted that with the current plan, the south
46   and west parking do not have the required 10 foot buffer.
47


     Lehi City Planning Commission                   1                                  October 10, 2024
48   The applicant, Steven Kelly, was present. He petitioned for a buffer distance of 0 feet on the west
49   side of the parking lot to widen the space on the east side of the lot, which would provide a park
50   use area. This area would ideally keep noise away from residents that are on the west side of the
51   property. He also asked for a waiver of the 10 foot distance on the south side, as there will be a
52   six-foot privacy fence.
53
54   Acting Chair Everett opened public hearing.
55
56   Michelle Hopkinson (also representing her husband Dana) neighbors the property. She expressed
57   concerns over privacy and safety if the parking buffer is allowed to be 0 feet. She also expressed
58   concerns for the privacy of hotel clients.
59
60   Larry Webb is a neighbor of the property to the west. He pointed out that the driveway doesn’t
61   have the regulation 20 foot setback, and expressed worries over movability in the parking lot and
62   the possibility of cars blocking the sidewalk. He also expressed concern over shade from the
63   hotel in the winter preventing snow from melting, and concern that the sewage system isn’t
64   prepared for what the hotel will produce.
65
66   Ron Wall, a Lehi resident, neighbors the property to the West. He expressed worries about
67   increased traffic along Main Street.
68
69   Brada Mayor neighbors the property. She expressed concerns for privacy in her backyard.
70   Additionally, she mentioned that there would likely be increased concerns about the nearby fire
71   station and their ability to leave and return with additional traffic.
72
73   Tona Wall, a Lehi resident, expressed worries about RVs potentially parking on the street.
74
75   Janys Hutchings was not able to make it to the meeting, but submitted comments that expressed
76   worries about traffic, offsite parking, and garbage. She suggested a right in, right out system for
77   the parking lot.
78
79   Ben Matthews was not able to make it to the meeting but submitted comments expressing that he
80   supports it as a business endeavor.
81
82   Acting Chair Everett closed the public hearing.
83
84   Commissioner Eyre clarified that the issue at hand was the parking buffer, and that other
85   complaints were beyond the scope of the current meeting. Commissioner Lockhart asked for a
86   clarification of what Kelly was requesting. She also expressed frustration that properties that
87   were originally zoned residentially no longer have access to residential zoning law now that they
88   have been rezoned to be mixed-use. Kelly clarified that his main goal was to be granted
89   permission to have less than a 10 foot buffer for his parking lot, and that he would like concrete
90   numbers from the Commission as to how much of a buffer he is required to have, so that he can
91   work them into his next set of plans for presentation.
92
93                   Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to grant approval for the concept of the
94                   Ember Hotel located at 26 East Main Street and deny the exception for reduction
95                   in the landscape buffer on the south and west sides of the property, with the
     Lehi City Planning Commission                     2                                  October 10, 2024
 96                   parameters that an exception will be granted for a 0 foot buffer on the east side of
 97                   the property. There will need to be a minimum of 10 feet buffer per code on all
 98                   other sides of the property. Commissioner Lockhart seconded the motion.
 99
100                   Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Lockhart, yes, Commissioner
101                   Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes, Commissioner Roberts, yes. The motion
102                   passed 4-1.

104   3.2) Public hearing and consideration of Jeff Ackley’s request for conditional use approval
105   for the conversion of an accessory building into a detached ADU located at 1088 North
106   Trinnaman Lane.
107   Brittney Harris presented information on the item. She explained that this type of property would
108   usually be allowed, but since the house was built after 2021, it is a conditional use situation.
109
110   The applicant, Jeff Ackley, was present. He clarified that no special exceptions are being
111   requested, he just wanted to make sure there won’t be any zoning issues.
112
113   Acting Chair Everett opened and closed the public hearing.
114
115   Commissioner Roberts asked if the ADU will pass along to the next resident in the case that
116   Ackley chooses not to remain on the property. Harris explained that if there is a new owner, they
117   will need to fill out the paperwork again for possession of the ADU.
118
119                   Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to approve the conditional use for the
120                   conversion of an accessory building into a detached ADU located at 1088 North
121                   Trinnaman Lane. Commissioner Eyre seconded the motion. Commissioner
122                   Jackson seconded the motion as well.
123
124                   Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner
125                   Eyre, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes. The motion
126                   passed unanimously.
