Proposed Resolution 2024-2 adopting the budget for the Municipal Building
Authority for Fiscal Year 2024-2025
         A memo from Board staff regarding the proposed Fiscal Year 2024-2025 Municipal Building Authority budget came
before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the proposed budget totals $3,678,500 and the current proposal is to accept
the budget for review and set a public hearing for June 11, 2024 to consider adoption of the final budget.
Comptroller Sorensen used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to summarize the proposed Fiscal Year 2024-2025 budget. The
reason for the increase in the Municipal Building Authority budget when compared to last year’s $5,000 budget is that the
WonderBlock parking structure bond was issued by the Municipal Building Authority. The bond is $60,129,000 and the proposed
budget of $3.6 million represents the debt service payment on that bond.
         Chair Richey introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2024-2, entitled:
                  “A resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Ogden City Municipal Building Authority adopting the annual
                  budget for Fiscal Year 2024-2025.”
          TRUSTEE HYER MOVED TO ACCEPT THE PROPOSED FISCAL YEAR 2024-2025 MUNICIPAL
BUILDING AUTHORITY BUDGET FOR REVIEW AND SET A PUBLIC HEARING FOR JUNE 11, 2024 TO
CONSIDER ADOPTION FO THE FINAL BUDGET. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE BLAIR, ALL
VOTING AYE. Trustee Myers was not present when this vote was taken.
