1.         25-127                 Public Hearing to consider the tentative adoption of the Mayor's FY
                                  2025-26 Tentative Budget for Sandy City and the Alta Canyon Recreation
                                  District
                   Attachments:   Presentation
                                  Resolution 25-17C
                                  FY 2026 Tentative Budget

                                  Public Hearing: Administration presented the City's Tentative Budget for FY 2026 to the
                                  Council.

                                  Mayor Zoltanski presented the Tentative Budget for FY 2026 to the City Council. Our FY
                                  2026 tentative budget is a very disciplined budget, using resources we have and living
                                  within our means. This year's tentative budget includes $1 million in budget cuts from the
                                  prior year's budget. To achieve this, the city has made cuts to operations and a reduction
                                  in workforce and personnel. This year's tentative budget does not include any increases
                                  to property or sales tax. She spoke about the optimistic outlook, future investment and
                                  economic opportunities in Sandy. Moderate revenue growth does not cover the
                                  inflationary impact of rising costs and the city has had to make some tough budget cuts.
                                  New projects in Sandy include the rebuild of Alta Canyon Sports Center, the SEG NHL
                                  hockey training facility, and Fire Station 31. The city will continue to provide excellent city
                                  services ensuring our city is a great place to raise your family.

                                  Brian Kelly, Administrative Services, provided a presentation of the Tentative Budget for
                                  FY 2026 to the City Council. He reviewed the city's vision statement: Connecting an
                                  active, vibrant, and prosperous community, where people flourish, and you belong ; and
                                  the city's mission statement: Sandy City strives to be responsible stewards dedicated to
                                  providing high-value quality services, with an emphasis on safe neighborhoods, smart
                                  planning, preservation, and economic diversity. Mr. Kelley spoke about the importance of
                                  continuing to provide high quality services to our residents and reviewed the guiding
                                  principles and revenue policies of the Sandy City budget preparation and process. He
                                  reviewed key challenges, demographic trends, and the city sales tax growth year over
                                  year. Mr. Kelley provided an overview of the projected funding gap between revenues and
                                  requested needs for FY 2026 for both the general fund and capital & equipment fund.
                                  Major themes of this year's budget include the following: a balanced budget without a
                                  property tax increase, continuing to provide a high level of services, a proposed funding
                                  plan for the Alta Canyon Sports Center rebuild, and investment in fleet replacement and
                                  existing infrastructure. He reviewed revenue projections and expenditure prioritization,
                                  employee retention, a property tax comparison with other cities, and budget cuts. He
                                  encouraged all to utilize the interactive budget tool found on the city's website and
                                  reviewed the FY 2026 budget calendar and timeline. Council questions and comments
                                  followed. Council thanked the budget team and the administration for their presentation.

                                  Katrina Frederick, Human Resources, provided a presentation of the FY 2026
                                  compensation plan proposal. She reviewed the plan goals, key takeaways, COLA
                                  increases, step and grade adjustments, and fixed and variable benefits increases. She
                                  presented the FY 2026 compensation plan and proposal summary. Staff responded to
                                  Council questions and provided additional details regarding the elimination of city
                                  positions.

                                  Public Hearing comment period opened:
                                  Steve Van Maren commented on the elimination of city positions and suggested a typo
                                  correction to the document.
