                                       BUSINESS MEETING

Unified Fire Authority Report

                 Captain Ken Aldridge, Unified Fire Authority, stated 779 individuals have taken
the initial hiring exam, 84 advanced to the written interviews and physical testing, and then
background checks will be completed for those top candidates. Several fire stations are
undergoing either construction or demolition.

Construction has started for the Midvale fire station and the Magna fire station has been closed
for demolition. Light the Night event for fallen firefighters was held on October 2, 2021.

              Chief Bruce Cline, Sandy City Fire, reviewed calls for service for the month of
September and last quarter. October is fire prevention month with the focus on being smoke
alarms and detectors and teaching children in school how to react differently to different situations.
The Chili Cook Off will be held at the South Towne Mall and all proceeds will be given to the U of
U burn camp.

Unified Police Department Report

                Officer Jeff Fenton, Unified Police Department, reviewed calls for service for
October. He has been getting reports of a man walking around Big Bear Park, but it is not being
called in at the time of the incident. The zip line and swing have been broken, and the shed was
broken into. He encourages residents to report these incidents to UPD.

Public Comment

                Leonard Peterson stated since Alta View Elementary was redone, he has signs
in the front of his home that he would like removed. He has talked to several Council Members
regarding this issue and would like something to be done.

                Mayor Flint stated she has forwarded the request to have the signs removed to
Salt Lake County Engineering and they will come out and review the situation. She suggested
talking to the other neighbors to see if they want the signs down as well and then e-mail her with
the information.

White City Financial Report

               David Sanderson, Financial Manager, stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (MSD) will have the 2022 administrative budget available for review and will hold
a budget hearing. White City has submitted its preliminary administrative budget to the MSD and
will approve the budget in December with a public hearing. The Council will need to review and
possibly approve a budget adjustment for 2021 to include the CARES Act and ARPA fuding.

                Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to accept the
financial report. The motion passed unanimously.

Resolution 21-10-01 – Issuance of Bonds to Pay for Infrastructure

               Paul Ashton stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) is
working on obtaining bonds to pay for infrastructure within the townships, unincorporated areas
of Salt Lake County, and Brighton. The agreement has been drafted and is being reviewed by the
Salt Lake County District Attorney’s Office for amendments. The Council can either approve the
resolution with the caveat that the mayor will be authorized to sign the agreement with the
amendments when it is finalized, or the decision can be tabled until the final agreement is
completed for the Council to review.

              Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to defer the
resolution and agreement to a future meeting to allow time for the Council to review the final
agreement. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Google Fiber Non-Disclosure Agreement & Franchise Agreement

               Paul Ashton stated Google Fiber is requesting approval of a non-disclosure
agreement with White City Metro Township to protect confidential proprietary information and
trade secrets. The non-disclosure agreement must be signed before it will enter into negotiations
for the actual franchise agreement for the installation of Google Fiber. The finalized franchise
agreement will be available to the public for review.

              Council Member Cardenaz, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to
authorize the mayor to sign the Google Fiber non-disclosure statement. The motion passed
unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
White City Metro Township Draft General Plan

               Paul Ashton stated the White City Metro Township General Plan has been
reviewed by the Planning Commission and it has made its recommendation and forwarded it to
the Council for its decision.
DATE           THURSDAY                              OCTOBER                           7, 2021

                Erin O’Kelley, Planner, MSD, introduced herself as the new White City Metro
Township Planner, she will help finalize the General Plan and amend ordinances. The Planning
Commission reviewed the General Plan in March and requested a workshop that was held in July.
It received feedback from public hearings and those comments have been addressed in the plan.
There have been discussions on Airbnb’s, short-term rentals, and rezoning the park.

              Council Member Little stated the bulk of what is being reviewed should be the
removal of zones that are not applicable and zoning the park as a park.

              Council Member Price asked if each zone change must be a separate ordinance.

               Ms. O’Kelley stated all zoning changes can be made in one document. She asked
the Council to send her any technical changes it has so she can update the plan before the next
meeting.

              Mr. Ashton asked if the Council would like to hold a public hearing to receive
feedback from residents and have a workshop.

               Mr. Little stated he would like to have a workshop to discuss the Councils
feedback, have a public hearing to get the residents input, and then meet as a Council to make
final changes.

              Mr. Ashton stated suggested that the Council hold a workshop on Thursday,
October 21, 2021, at 6:00 pm and the public hearing on Thursday, November 18, 2021, at 5:00
pm.

                                   −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Public Hearing to Consider the White City Metro Township Draft General Plan

              Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Little, moved to hold the
White City Metro Township General Plan workshop on October 21, 2021, at 6:00 pm, at the White
City Water Improvement District located at 999 E Galena Drive; the White City Metro Township
General Plan public hearing on November 18, 2021, at 7:00 pm, at the Eastmont Middle School
located at 10100 S 1300 E. The motion passed unanimously.
