Proposed Ordinance 2023-70 vacating a portion of 3rd Street right-of-way
between Porter Avenue and Jefferson Avenue; quit claiming the property
within the vacated portion of said street to the abutting property owners: and
reserving a right-of-way for all utilities
A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding a proposed street vacation came before
the Council for consideration. The memo stated Steve Diamond has submitted a petition to vacate 25 feet of the northern right-of-
way of 3™ Street between Porter Avenue and Jefferson Avenue.
Planning Manager Brierley summarized the memo and stated this petition is connected to a rezone of 2.18 acres of
adjacent property from R-1-6 to R-1-5 that was approved by the Council on May 23, 2023. The petitioner is anticipating
. developing 16 single-family homes on the site of a former church and needs the additional right-of-way to accommodate the
development. Third Street jogs to the south at this location going from west to east and the street vacation, along with the
dedication of the 25 feet of existing right-of-way on the south side of 3“ Street, will help realign and straighten the road. The area
proposed for vacation was used for on-street parking for the church. The Planning Commission’s recommendation of approval of
the vacation comes with several requirements including improvements to the curb and gutter, other roadway improvements, the (
relocation of an irrigation headwall structure owned by Lynne Irrigation Company, and the combination of the vacated property
with the lots to the north. He concluded the proposed vacation was recommended for approval with the following conditions that
have been incorporated into the ordinance for the Council’s consideration:

The Planning Commission reviewed the proposed amendments at the September 6, 2023 meeting and forwarded a
recommendation of approval to the Council. The recommendation was made with the finding that there is good cause for the street ;
vacation, that neither the public interest nor any person will be materially injured by the street vacation, and that the vacation will
be consistent with the General Plan. He added that Lynne Irrigation will inspect the improvements, and City staff will also
perform an inspection to determine that the improvements are in line with City standards.

Council member Hyer referenced the conditions relating to the Lynne Irrigation Company waterway; he asked if the
petitioner will be required to relocate the entire waterway. Mr. Brierley answered no; the petitioner can reconstruct the headwall
and move it further to the south.

Chair Choberka invited input from the petitioner.

Mr. Diamond presented an aerial image of the property upon which he is pursuing a development. He believes that the
manner in which the 3" Street right of way was recorded was an error when the area was platted 60 years ago. The former church

-_-was using the area as an extension of their parking lot. If the vacation is approved, the right-of-way will still be 66 feet wide, and
. ) the travel patterns will not change whatsoever. He also presented a letter from Lynne Irrigation Company approving the relocation
of the headwall 14 feet to the south. There are six active water users on that section of the ditch and Lynne Irrigation has indicated

that eventually the waterway will no longer be needed.

The Chair then called for a public hearing on proposed Ordinance 2023-70, entitled:

“An ordinance of Ogden City Utah vacating a portion of the 3rd Street right-of-way between porter avenue and
Jefferson avenue; quit claiming the property within the vacated portion of said street to the abutting property
owners as their interests may appear; reserving a right-of-way for all utilities that may now exist in said portion
of such street; directing the City Recorder to record this ordinance in the office of the County Recorder; and
providing that this ordinance shall become effective immediately upon posting after final passage.”

The City Recorder presented written notice of said hearing and affidavit of the publication on the 22nd day of November :

public hearing and be considered for final passage.
