                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 20-13

     AN ORDINANCE REZONING LOCATED AT 11845 SOUTH REDWOOD RD,
    PROPOSED REZONE TO RM-6-SD, RESIDENTIAL MULTI-FAMILY WITH A
      MAXIMUM OF 6 UNITS PER ACRE, WITH SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENT
         DESIGNATIONS LIMITING DENSITY AND CONFIGURATION

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

     WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said zoning
amendment; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map by rezoning approximately 12.8 acres located at
11845 South Redwood Road from C-N. RR-22, and R-3 to RM-6-SD, as described herein.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended to reflect the
                  following changes, including Specific Development Designations, as shown in
                  Exhibit “A”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 19th day of

Exhibit A


    1. Area 1, as designated below, shall be limited to 14 lots, with a minimum lot
       size of 10,000 square feet, and shall comply with setback and lot
       requirements of the R-4 Zone.
    2. Area 2, as designated below, shall have a minimum lot size of 8,000 square feet,
       and shall otherwise comply with the setback and lot requirements of the R-4
       zone.
    3. Area 3 may include a maximum of 10 units consistent with the requirements of
       the RM-6 zone, with the exception that up to five (5) units may attached.
    4. Laurel Chase Drive shall connect to 11800 South at Redwood Road as a
       through public street.
    5. Area 3 shall not include a direct connection to Redwood Road.
