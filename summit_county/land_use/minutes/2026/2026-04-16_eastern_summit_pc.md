4)
Discussion and preparation of topics for the May 6, 2026 joint meeting with the Summit County Council. Peter Barnes, Community Development Director.
(6:24 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Community Development Director Peter Barnes led the discussion of potential topics for the May 6, 2026, joint meeting with the Summit County Council (6:24 PM)
Commissioners commented on the process and suggested topics to be included in the discussion at the joint meeting with the County Council. Several topics were raised during the discussion. The topics of interested suggested were maintaining the rural small-town character, code enforcement, boundaries surrounding the Overlay Zone and defining what a village is, open space versus affordable housing, water issues, coordination and integration of general plans and land use maps, the council’s vision of the master land use map, the ability to put guardrails on total development based on limited resources such as water, various affordable housing processes and associated costs, bleed over impacts to Summit County resulting from Wasatch County developments, and the ability to encourage development within incorporated zones (6:27 PM)
Mr. Barnes provided final thoughts to the commissions regarding preparing for the joint meeting.

Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner,
introduced amendments to Chapter 8: Community Health and Recreation of the Eastern Summit County General Plan and then turned the time over to Nathan Brooks, Environmental Health Director. (7:04 PM)
Nathan Brooks,
Environmental Health Director,
addressed the Commissioners and presented proposed amendments to Chapter 8: Community Health and Recreation of the Eastern Summit County General Plan. Director Brooks explained the Community Health Assessment (CHA) survey which formulated the Community Health Improvement Plan (CHIP) with the goal of creating a land use policy and program that promotes positive health outcomes throughout Summit County. (7:05 PM)
Commissioners commented on the Chapter 8 amendments and provided feedback to Planners Milliner and Osman. Topics discussed included the South Summit Recreation District and recreational opportunities, mental health services, RAP funding, affordable housing, radon, diverse cultures and maintaining heritage. Finally, the encouragement of community gardens and the addition of green belt language was suggested. (7:11 PM)
