### CANVASS - Acceptance and Certification of the 2023 City of Orem General Election
*Presenter: Teresa McKitrick, City Recorder*

Ms. McKitrick reported the following information: the County is currently running the most up to date statistics, the statistics given tonight are from Monday, December 4, 2023. The final results for the election are Jeff Lambson 9,098, Jenn Gale 8,606, Chris Killpack 8,457, Crystal Muhlestein 7,994, Matt McKell 7,334 and Spencer Rands 5,883. The total number of votes is 47,372 votes. Proposition #6 received 10,528 yes votes and 5,819 no votes for a total of 16,347 votes.

The number of Orem City registered voters was 45,432. The number of ballots cas was 16,347. This gives our voter turnout percentage to be 36.92%. For comparison in the 2021 general election, we had 35.42%. In the 2023 primaries we had 28%. That is an increase of 9% since the primaries.

Other statistics as of Monday, December 4th, there were 262 ballots that were not counted: 36 were not signed, 129 had a signature discrepancy, 97 were returned after the deadline and zero empty envelopes. The enhanced voting is for those voting that are out of state or country. There was one ballot mailed and one ballot returned, no ballots were emailed, 45 were sent enhanced voting and 20 of those were voted. There were 114 voters who voted in person and there were 2 provisional ballots that both counted.

**Ms. Millett moved** to accept and certify the canvass of the 2023 City of Orem General Election. Mr. Spencer **seconded** the motion. Those voting yes: Debby Lauret, Jeff Lambson, David Spencer, Terry Peterson, Tom Macdonald, and LaNae Millett. The motion **passed.**
