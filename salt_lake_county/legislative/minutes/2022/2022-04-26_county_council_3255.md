2. CITIZEN PUBLIC INPUT
Mr. Steven Winters  reported on a Harvard University study on water fluoridation and 
pregnant women that found when the mother ’s fluoride level was increased during 
pregnancy, the child ’s IQ dropped.  Eight years ago, Harvard classified fluoride with 
arsenic and lead as a development neurotoxin, and studies have shown brain injuries due 
to fluoride.  He was not opposed to using fluoride on his teeth, but he would not want to 
swallow it.  
Mr. Lawrence Horman  stated what happens in one section of the homeless community 
is happening within each segment, whether that is the need for housing, a safe place to go, 
care, pet companionship, etc.  All of these issues need to be connected to each other to 
make it easier for an individual to go from being homeless to be housed and being more 
stable once they get into housing.

A PROCLAMATION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL
DESIGNATING APRIL 30 AS NATIONAL THERAPY ANIMAL DAY
WHEREAS, therapy animals provide physical, psychological, and emotional 
benefits to those they interact with; and
WHEREAS, therapy animals are most frequently dogs, but can also include 
other domesticated species such as cats, equines, and rabbits, to name a 
few; and
WHEREAS, there are thousands of Pet Partners therapy animal teams 
serving in communities across the Unites States; and 
WHEREAS, April 30 has been designated as National Therapy Animal Day; 
and
WHEREAS, therapy animal teams in Salt Lake County play an essential role 
in improving human health and well -being through the human -animal bond by 
making millions of visits per year in settings such as hospitals, nursing

RESOLUTION NO. 5967
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
AUTHORIZING EXECUTION AND DELIVERY OF A REAL ESTATE 
PURCHASE CONTRACT WITH MILLCREEK CITY AND 
APPROVING CONVEYANCE OF TWO PARCELS OF REAL 
PROPERTY AND A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT
RECITALS
A. Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns a parcel of real property 
located at approximately 4189 South 900 East, Millcreek, Utah, also 
identified as Parcel No. 22-05-128-020 ( “Parcel 020”), which is part of 
Hillview Basin.
B. The County owns another parcel of real property that is part of 
Hillview Basin, identified as Parcel No. 16-32-378-011 (“Parcel 011”).
C. Millcreek (the “City”) is working with the Utah Department of 
Transportation (“UDOT”) on a road widening project along 900 East (the 
“Project”).  
D. As part of the Project, the City would like to acquire a 2004 
square-foot portion of Parcel 011 and a 142 square -foot portion of Parcel 
Page 8 of 17
County Council Meeting Minutes April 26, 2022
020.  In addition, the City is seeking a temporary construction easement for 
UDOT over a 315 square-foot portion of Parcel 020.
E. The City has submitted a Real Estate Purchase Contract (the “REPC”) 
to the County for the acquisition of the portion of Parcel 011 and the portion 
of Parcel 020 via two separate quit claim deeds (the “Deeds”) and for the 
temporary construction easement (the “Easement”).  The total amount 
offered by the City for acquisition of the Deeds and the Easement is 
$11,900.00, which amount has been reviewed and approved by the Salt Lake 
County Real Estate Section as full and adequate consideration.
F. It has been determined that the best interests of the County and the 
general public will be served by executing the REPC, the Deeds, and the 
Easements attached to this Resolution.  These actions will be in compliance 
with all applicable state statutes and county ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the grant and conveyance of the Deeds to the City and 
the Easement to UDOT pursuant to the REPC is hereby approved, and the 
Mayor is authorized and directed to execute the REPC, attached hereto as 
Exhibit A, and any other related document necessary to complete this 
transaction.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County Council that the 
Mayor and County Clerk are hereby authorized, consistent with the terms of 
the REPC to execute the Deeds and the Easement attached to the REPC and 
to deliver the fully executed documents to the Salt Lake County Real Estate 
Section for delivery to the City upon payment of the agreed upon purchase 
amount.

RECITALS
A. The Joy Bradshaw Woolley Living Trust, Et Al, a Utah Trust 
(“Owner”), owns real property in Salt Lake County, Utah, more particularly 
described as Parcel No. 4062, Park Place Phase 2 - Lots 201, 202, and 203 
of the Park Place Phase 2 Subdivision recorded as Entry No. 13198078 in 
the Office of the Salt Lake County Recorder, with an approximate address of 
11065 South 1055 West, South Jordan, Utah (the “Trust Property”).
B. On October 29th, 2008, B .D. Williams, LLC recorded a culvert, ditch, 
and pipeline perpetual easement (the “Easement”), which gave Salt Lake 
County (the “County”) “the right of ingress and egress, for maintenance, 
replacement and enlargement of a culvert, ditch and pipeline on, over and 
through” the Trust Property.
C. The flow of the Midas Creek has been diverted, and the Easement as it 
Page 10 of 17

exists does not provide the County with the necessary access to the culvert, 
ditch, and pipeline to provide the necessary maintenance.
D. Owner and the County would like to move the Easement.
E. County has agreed to release the Easement and quit -claim any interest 
it may have in the Easement to the Owner in consideration of the Owner 
entering into a new easement agreement and moving the Easement to another 
location (the “New Easement”) more suitable to the County.
F. It has been determined that the best interests of the County and the 
general public will be served by releasing the Easement and accepting the 
New Easement in its new location.   
G. The release of the Easement will be in compliance with all applicable 
state statutes and county ordinances
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the Easement be declared surplus property.

attached hereto as Exhibit A (“Release”), which are hereby approved.  The

AUTHORIZING EXECUTION OF AN AMENDMENT TO THE 
INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT LAKE 
COUNTY FOR ITS DIVISION OF PARKS AND RECREATION AND 
SALT LAKE CITY FOR IMPROVEMENTS TO THE CITY ’S OAK 
HILLS TENNIS CENTER
A. To fund the acquisition, construction, renovation, and equipping of 
parks and recreation facilities and related improvements, Salt Lake County 
(“County”) sought approval by voters in the November 8, 2016 general 
election to issue general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed Ninety 
Million Dollars ($90,000,000) (the “Recreation Bonds”).
B. Salt Lake City (“City”) submitted a Recreation Bonds Application 
proposing to replace an accessory building at its recreation facility known as 
Oak Hills Tennis Center with a new clubhouse (“Facility”) for which it will 
receive a contribution of County Recreation Bonds funds in the amount of 
$1,750,000.00.
C. County and City entered into an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement, 
Salt Lake County Contract No. 0000001949 ( “the Agreement ”) whereby the 
County contributed the Recreation Bonds funds for City to complete the 
desired improvements to its Facility.
Page 12 of 17
County Council Meeting Minutes April 26, 2022
D. The adoption of the Agreement was made pursuant to the Utah 
Interlocal Cooperation Act, U.C.A. § 11-13-101 et seq. (the “Act”).
E. The Agreement requires the City to construct an improvement to real 
property thereby requiring approval of the County ’s legislative body 
pursuant to the Act.
F. The Agreement will expire on May 17, 2021, but the work will not be 
complete.
G. The City and the County wish to amend the Agreement to extend the 
termination date.
H. The amendment of the Agreement is in the best interests of the parties 
and in furtherance of the public interest.
