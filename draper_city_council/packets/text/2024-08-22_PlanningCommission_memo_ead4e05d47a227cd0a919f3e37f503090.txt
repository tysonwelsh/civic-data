Comments:

This application is a request for approval to amend the Moderate Income Housing Plan (MIHP) in
the Draper City General Plan. In 2019, Senate Bill (SB) 34 required cities to adopt specific strategies
into their general plans to encourage the creation of moderate-income housing. In 2022, House Bill
(HB) 462 made revisions to the list of specific strategies cities could choose from. Draper City
complied with the requirement to adopt a MIHP in 2019, and now proposes to replace Strategy L,
regarding impact fees, with Strategy E, regarding accessory dwelling units.

Findings for approval:

  1. A Public Hearing before the Planning Commission was held in accordance with Utah State
     Code Section 10-9a-404.
  2. The General Plan Amendment is consistent with the requirements of Utah State Code and is
     appropriate.

Findings for denial:


  1. The General Plan Amendment is not consistent with the requirements of Utah State Code and
      is not appropriate.
