BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 2.4 acres
located on the west side of Jerema Court, at approximately 12194 S. Jerema Court (Exhibit
B). The property is currently zoned R3. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment
be approved to allow for the subdivision of the larger lot in the subdivision. The lot
proposed to be subdivided is approximately 1.4 acres or 60,984 square feet.

The subdivision was initially approved as a four lot subdivision and the plat was recorded
on January 8, 2013 (Exhibit E). The plat consisted of one larger lot and three smaller lots.




River Park Hill 2nd Plat Amendment                                    App. # SUB-232-2018
Plat Amendment Request                             1
The first amendment (Exhibit F) to the subdivision was recorded later in 2013 and
reconfigured the three smaller lots into two lots. Those two lots are on the west side of the
subdivision and have frontage on Joseph View Lane.

The access for the two lots on the west side of the subdivision will continue to be from
Joseph View Lane and the two proposed on the east side will be accessed from Jerema
Court. The overall boundaries of the subdivision will not be modified as a result of this
application.

Medium Density and Sensitive River Overlay land use designations for the subject
properties (Exhibit C). These categories are characterized as follows:

three dwelling units per acre. The purpose of the R3 zone is to “permit medium density
residential development without special mitigation requirements. This zone is intended to
provide incentives to foster residential development with little impact on its surroundings and on
municipal services, and to generally preserve the semirural character called for in the density

The existing subdivision is completely surrounded by the RA2 (Residential Agricultural,
20,000 square foot minimum) zoning designation.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the Draper City Municipal Code
(DCMC). This section depicts the standard of review for such requests as:

         “The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
         an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
         that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
         way, or easement has been vacated or amended.”

         structure on the newly created Lot 104.
