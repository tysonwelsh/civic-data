City Council Meeting Minutes May 3, 2022

Council Member Business
Council Member Brooke D'Sousa announced that the Sandy Youth Council application
process is now open. Please see the City website for more information. She thanked
Administration for coordinating the recent employee luncheon.
Council Member Scott Earl provided an update from the CDBG Committee and the Jordan
River Commission meeting.
Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged the efforts of the first responders for
their response to an tragic incident that occurred in Sandy City today. She knows is was
a difficult and sad day.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Monica Zoltanski expressed her thoughts and prayers to all impacted by today's
tragic incident. She thanked all involved with the coordination of the employee luncheon.
She invited the Council to join in the City's 5K race this weekend. Mayor Z thanked the
Explorers for their efforts and service to the city and was grateful to them for restoring and
installing a park bench in Dimple Dell Park.

CAO Report
Cliff Strachan, CAO, updated the council on the following: newly sworn in officers. Police
Citizens Academy, and the May 16th Public Works Town Hall event. He mentioned that
River Oaks is hosting state High School golf tournament on May 10th.

Adjournment
Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the City Council meeting at 7:13 pm.
