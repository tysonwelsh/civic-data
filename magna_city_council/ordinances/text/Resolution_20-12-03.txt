MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 20-12-03 DATE: December 8, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING A SPECIAL EVENT FEE WAIVER FOR THE SALT
LAKE TRACK CLUB IN SUPPORT OF THE 2021 WINTER RACE
SERIES AT SALTAIR
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seg.; and
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council’) is the municipal
legislative body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake City Track Club (the “Track Club”) is a nonprofit community
organization that promotes physical activity through running, jogging, and walking events.
WHEREAS, for over 40 years, the track club has managed an annual Winter Race Series
at Saltair where local runners and recreationalist can compete in a series of race events from
January to March; and
WHEREAS, the Winter Race Series brings in up to 500 people per event to Magna to
participate and/or support the races; and
WHEREAS, on November | 1, 2020, Magna received a request for a fee waiver from the
track club for the 2021 Winter Race Series at Saltair; and
WHEREAS, granting the waiver, given the legacy history of the event and past support
for it, continues the legacy for local support, now therefore be it
RESOLUTION

RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the waiver of the Special Event Permit Fees
for the Salt Lake Track Club in support of their 2021 Winter Race Series at Saltair, and be it
further

RESOLVED, the Council hereby directs staff to communicate the fee waiver decision to
Salt Lake County Public Works Operations so the track club can get their permits issued in a
timely manner.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this fi day of December,
2020.
