Director Ward explained that the application was for an amendment to the Deer Valley Resort 
Master  Planned  Development  (“MPD”)  Conditional  Use  Permit  (“CUP”)  for  Phase  I  of  that 
development.  The applicant proposed Phase I of the Base Area project that would include a 
three-level parking garage, an underground Transit Hub adjacent to the Snow Park Lodge, and a 
conceptual Site and Circulation Plan with road and utility improvements.  The application was 
submitted in December 2020 and has been pending for a number of years.  This application was 
last before the Planning Commission on January 18, 2023.  As part of that review, Director Ward 
recalled that the Commission did not take a formal vote on a positive or negative recommendation 
on the proposed vacation of the right-of-way but reached a consensus that the question should 
be moved to the City Council for review and action.  The Commission forwarded their consensus 
with detailed qualifiers, concerns, conditions, and caveats for the City Council to consider. 
 
Director Ward reported that the City Council reviewed the applicant’s request for a vacation of 
right-of-way over 12 meetings in 2023.  On December 14, 2023, the City Council approved the 
vacationApproved of right-of-way and also approved a  Resolution for a public/private partnership and a 
Letter of Intent for a shared parking facility in the area of Highway 40 and State Route (“SR”) 248.  
Since that time, the applicant has been working with Staff as they worked to revise and update 
the plans.  She also reported that the applicant held a series of community meetings for input on 
the revisions.

Ms. Tyler addressed  the  day  skier  drop-off  component.   She  explained  that  if  someone  were 
looking to drop off a day skier in the multi-modal hub, they would take a right at the Y, navigate 
through the new roundabout at the intersection of Doe Pass road and Deer Valley Drive West,

to cross eight lanes of traffic to get to the resort and would have direct vertical access to the plaza 
and the lift infrastructure.  She added that could be through escalators, stairs, or an elevator.  
Once a driver dropped off their passenger and wanted to park, they would exit the Transit Center 
on the east side and then either go into the parking garage on Doe Pass Road or use another 
entrance on the east side to current lots 5 and 6.  The other option would be for drivers to exit the 
area by turning right at the Y and exiting Deer Valley. 
 
Ms. Tyler noted that they worked out with the City that Doe Pass Road would be a public right-of-
way maintained by Deer Valley.  For ski school or dropping off a child at the year-round daycare 
at Deer Valley Academy, the drop-off would be on the east side next to the lodge. They chose to 
separate that drop-off from the normal drop-off because10.09.2024 they recognized that the behaviors of 
those  people  were  different  that  someone  looking  to  drop-off  a  regular  guest.   They 
accommodated the ski school and daycare drop-offs so that the driver could park temporarily, 
leave the car, and get their student or day-skier guest into the ski school.  She mentioned that 
they  did  not  want  to  clog  up  the  underground  Transit  Center,  so  they pulled  it  out  and 
accommodated it as close as they could to those different options in the lodge.  
 
Ms. Tyler commented that if someone just wanted to park and not drop anyone off, they could go 
either direction at the Y and enter one of the parking garages either off of Doe Pass Road or on
