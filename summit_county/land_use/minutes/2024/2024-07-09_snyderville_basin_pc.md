Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:23 p.m.

Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:23 p.m.
Commissioners provided comments and asked follow-up questions.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the Moore Accessory Building located at 415 East Earl Street, based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval with the addition that the nightly rental of the ADU be prohibited. Matthew Nagie seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
