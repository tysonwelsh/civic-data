23   Administrative Hearing Officer, Michael Johnson called the meeting to order at noon and
24   welcomed those present.
25          1.1 (Project CUP-24-017) Request by Jin-Xiang Yu, to operate a home business
26          (Music Educator/Performer – Beyond 2135, LLC.) at 7485 S. 2135 E.
27   City Planner, Maverick Yeh, presented the Staff Report and stated that the applicant is seeking
28   approval to operate a home-based business within their residence, offering private music
29   instruction to clients of all ages by appointment. According to the applicant’s narrative, lessons
30   will be scheduled between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM, primarily on weekdays, Monday through
31   Friday. Weekend classes may occasionally be scheduled, though this will be limited and is not
32   intended to be a regular occurrence.
33   Music instruction will take place in the applicant’s basement. The business will not produce
34   excessive odors; all activities will be contained within a designated area. During lessons,

1    windows will remain closed, and sound levels will be maintained at the lowest possible volume
2    to minimize any disturbance. The property's front yard includes two parking spaces available for
3    clients. Parking areas shall consist of a paved surface, covered by asphalt, concrete or other hard
4    surface materials.
5    City Planner Yeh outlined the zoning designation of the property is R-1-8 (Residential Single-
6    Family). Home occupations with clients are listed as a conditional use in the R-1-8 zone, as
7    referenced in §19.26.030. C. Home occupations are allowed only if the proposed business is
8    clearly secondary and incidental to the primary use of the property as the applicant’s permanent
9    and full-time place of residence. Home Occupations are regulated in §19.76.040
10   Applicant Jian—Xian Yu gave comments and added that the lessons are primarily private
11   lessons. Hearing Officer Johnson asked for more information on how many students does she see
12   on average per day. Ms. Yu stated that she sees between 2 and 5 students per day. Hearing Officer
13   Johnson also asked for more information on the length of lessons. Ms. Yu responded that they are
14   between 45 minutes and 1 hour.
15   Hearing Officer Johnson noted that there were two public comments received by the city, both of
16   which were in support of the applicant.
17   Hearing Officer Johnson opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public
18   hearing was closed.
19   Hearing Officer Johnson stated that this is the type of business that belongs in the residential area
20   and that it brings value to the neighborhood and city.
21   Hearing Officer Johnson moved to APPROVE Project CUP-24-017 pursuant to the
22   Conditions of Approval
23   1. The applicant shall obtain and maintain an approved business license with Cottonwood
24   Heights.
25   2. Customers must use provided parking. Customers must be made aware of these parking
26   restrictions.
27   3. The business hours shall adhere to the applicant’s narrative.
28   4. Approval of this home occupation is subject to review upon complaint.
29   5. No more than one non-resident employee is permitted at this business.
30   6. Any business activity conducted outside the dwelling shall not violate the rule of the use
31   being clearly incidental and secondary to the use of the dwelling for dwelling purposes.
32   7. The parking area in the front yard must be paved before next year's business license
33   renewal, and it needs to meet the city code required 11.04 R, 11.20.030 D.
34   Hearing Officer Johnson recommended Ms. Yu follow up with Mr. Yeh and the Business License
35   Department regarding next steps.
