3.2) Public hearing and consideration of Trademark Visual’s request for conditional use
approval of two pylon signs for The Collective commercial development located at 1050
West Sunrise Way and requesting an exception for greater height and sign area.

Commissioner Kunze passed leadership of the meeting to Commissioner Eyre, who arrived
during Item 3.1.

The Commission discussed their discomfort with the current sign code and the upcoming
amendments that the planning department is working on. They discussed the size and height of
nearby and surrounding signs.

The applicant, Justin Grub, was present. He expounded on the visibility problems the developer
was facing.

Commissioner Eyre opened public comment.

There were three online comments against signs being placed in neighborhoods. The comments
recommended placing signage along the highway instead.

Commissioner Eyre closed public comment.

The Commission discussed lighting, sign placement in relation to neighborhoods, the number
and design of the signs, the location of the sign on the property, and the potential effectiveness of
signs.

3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Deborah Walker’s request for review of the
Walker Zone Change on 2.58-acres located at 915 West State Street, changing the zoning
from R-1-8 to C-H (Heavy Commercial).

The Commission discussed connectivity.

Deborah Walker, the applicant, was present.

Lehi City Planning Commission                   3                                     June 11, 2026
Commissioner Eyre opened public comment.

Sheena Welch, Cathy Sunderland, Kyle Welch, Andrea Hansen, and several online comments
were against the item.

Commissioner Eyre closed public comment.

The Commission discussed vested rights and the General Plan. They discussed how zoning
changes operate and how property rights function in shifting zones.

                Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to recommend denial to the Lehi City
                Council with the findings that there are no invested rights to the zone change at
                this moment, that this zone change may make sense down the road, as it is called
                for on the General Plan, but in weighing concerns of adjoining properties and
                residents, it is not currently in the general welfare of Lehi City to make the zone
                change. He included all DRC comments. Commissioner Jones seconded.

                Vote: Commissioner Jones, yes. Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner
                Eyre, no. Commissioner Kunze, no. Commissioner Roberts, yes.

                Motion passed with three in favor, two against.


3.5) Public hearing and consideration of Thanksgiving Point’s request for approval of the
Thanksgiving Point Science Center site plan located at 2356 West Wheatley Lane,
requesting exceptions to the EIFS, glass, and setback requirements.

The applicants, McKay Christensen and Brad Walker were present.

The Commission discussed the design plans and glazing options.

Commissioner Eyre opened public comment.

John Petersen is in favor of the item.

Commissioner Eyre closed public comment.

The Commission discussed the need to maintain equity across projects, and the potentially
special nature of this project.

                public health, safety, general welfare, or convenience, but in fact does the
                opposite, that the proposed use will be located and conducted in a manner in
                compliance with the goals and policies of Lehi City General Plan and the
                purposes of this code, that the property on which the use building or other
                structure is proposed to be established is of adequate size and dimensions to

3.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to both Table 05.030-B and Section 12.130, updating the
regulations for Automobile Sales businesses.

The Commission discussed the purpose behind the passages being updated.

Commissioner Eyre opened public comment.

John Petersen is generally in favor of the item.

Commissioner Eyre closed public comment.


                Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to recommend approval to the Lehi City
                Council with the findings that the proposed Development Code amendments
