                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     RESOLUTION NO. 26-03

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING COUNCILMEMBER SHANNON SMITH TO SERVE AS A
        BOARD TRUSTEE FOR THE SOUTH SALT LAKE VALLEY MOSQUITO
                          ABATEMENT DISTRICT

       WHEREAS, the South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District (the "District") is
governed by a Board of Trustees, whose members represent the area in which the District provides
mosquito abatement service; and

         WHEREAS, members of the Board of Trustees are appointed to serve in office for a fixed term
of four years; and

        WHEREAS, on February 6, 2024, the Riverton City Council approved Resolution 24-15, thereby
appointing Councilmember Tish Buroker to serve as a board member for the District; and

       WHEREAS, Councilmember Buroker has faithfully served Riverton City in her capacity as a
Board Trustee two years but will now serve as Mayor for Riverton City and would like to appoint
Councilmember Shannon Smith to the Board of Trustees for the remainder of the term.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

       Section 1.      The Riverton City Council hereby appoints Councilmember Shannon Smith to
                       serve on the South Valley Sewer District’s Board of Trustees for the remainder
                       of the four-year term commencing January 20, 2026 until January 1, 2028.

       Section 2.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
