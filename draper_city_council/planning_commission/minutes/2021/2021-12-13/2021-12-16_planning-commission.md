3.    Public Hearing: Kum & Go #2500 Plat Amendment and Site Plan Requests
      (Administrative Action)
      A request by Nathan Abbott, representing Galloway & Co., Inc. for approval of a site plan
      for the purpose of constructing a gasoline service station and convenience store and a plat
      amendment to adjust lot lines with a recorded subdivision. The property is located at
      approximately 13639 and 13691 S. 200 West. Staff Contact: Maryann Pickering at 801-
      576-6391 or maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

      Maryann Pickering, City Planner III presented that the zoning is CSD-136. The gasoline
      service station will be at the corner. She showed the access off 200 West and another that
      will connect to the 136 Center. The Hampton Inn is on the other side of the road. The plan
      does comply with the CSD at 18% for landscaping. The proposed elevations were shown
      on the presentation slide. She noted all uses need to have a corporate design and the
      materials comply with the code. The plat amendment moves the property line 106 feet to
      the South. The text amendment was approved by the City Council and included a change,
      which was a reduction to 200 square feet for signs and the height remained the same.

      Commissioner Van Hoff asked has there been any identification of traffic accessing the
      property South bound.

      Brien Maxfield, Senior City Engineer stated that there was a traffic study on 200 West. He
      noted that within the 136 Center they can come either direction on 200 West. He noted it
      does meet the city requirements. He noted UDOT did not have any concerns about the
      traffic.

      Chairman Adams commented that the building materials were standard.

      Ms. Pickering stated it did comply with the CSD.

      Christian Michaelson of the Galloway & Co the applicant on behalf of Kum & Go. He
      stated he did not have much to add. This will be one of their first stores in Utah.

      Commissioner Hawker asked why it took so long for Kum & Go to come to Utah.

      Christian Michaelson stated that he did not know. He mentioned Kum & Go is
      environmentally friendly, and they are considering EV stations and PVRA’s on the
      canopies.

      Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no speakers. The public hearing was
      closed.

4.    Public Hearing: Vista 600 West Master Area Plan Amendment (Legislative Action)

      On the request of Howard Cooke, representing Gardner Company, for a Master Area Plan
      Amendment on 15.24 acres of land located at 13645 South 600 West in the TSD zone and
      a Text Amendment to DCMC Section 9-14-110. Application TEXTMAP-0109-2021, Staff
      Contact is Jennifer Jastremsky, (801) 576-6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draperutah.gov.

      Jennifer Jastremsky, Planning Manager mentioned the site is located off 600 West and
      Bangerter Highway south of the Vista Front Runner Station. The property has a land use
      designation of Transit Station and zoned TSD Transit Station District. In 2018 the applicant
      obtained a master area plan approval for the property. The approval included a mix of
      office, retail, and residential uses. The applicant is requesting to amend this plan and the
      zoning text. The TSD zone has sub zone categories: TSD-1, TSD-2, and TSD-3. Each area
      master plan requires an exhibit showing which sub-zone is being proposed. The sub-zones
      established where specific uses may locate within the project. The property is all within
      the TSD-3 Sub-Zone and their proposal seeks to switch out the exhibit with their new
      concept. The TSD-3 Sub-Zone is created to have a high level of walkability but allows for
      cars.

      She presented the approved concept plans in the adopted Master Area Plan. Concept Plan
      A includes 250 residential units, 10,000 sf of retail. A 120,000-sf office. Concept Plan B
      would add a second office building of 250,000 sf and a parking garage to accommodate
      the extra building. The proposed concept plan eliminates the office buildings and increases
      the retail to 20,000 sf and increases the residential units to 305. She highlighted the changes
      in her presentation. She showed the proposed open space and mentioned 15% is required
      under the TSD zone plus a trail that circulates the perimeter of the site. A cross access
