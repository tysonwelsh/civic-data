2022 Emigration Canyon Metro Township Budget Amendments
THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider the 2022

the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

The motion passed unanimously.

High-Density Zoning
Mayor Smolka stated a developer has submitted a request to Salt Lake City to
rezone property located at 3052 Emigration Canyon Road to high density multi-family residential.
The request includes 500 plus units in an 8-story building. He wants the Councils feedback on
how it would like to voice its opinion on the project.
Council Member Brems stated any opinion from the Council needs to be
presented to the Salt Lake City Planning Commission and it should be formulated in writing.
Council Member Pinon stated residents should join the effort as well.
Mayor Smolka stated this type of development could add 800 more vehicles to
the canyon. Issues could arise with the well protection zone for the Emigration tunnel that supplies
water. The Donner Trail has historic value and if it is built on, then it is lost forever.
Council Member Brems stated he would like Gordon Bennett to research the
developers request and provide the Council with an update on where Salt Lake City is in the
process.

American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) Funds
Council Member Pinon stated there are still shadow areas in the canyon where
residents are unable to connect to the internet. A possible use for ARPA funds would be wired
infrastructure where there is not effective broadband. Conversations with Utah Broadband are
ongoing and as more information if obtained, there may be an opportunity for the Council to
participate.
Mayor Smolka stated there is around $90,000 available.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes stated if the Council is considering using funds for
broadband, a better option may be developing a broadband infrastructure plan.
Mayor Smolka stated ARPA funds do not need to be used on for the Unified Fire
Authority invoice that was submitted and placed on the agenda.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Update on Broadband
Council Member Pinon stated the Got Teeth Lane repeater is working. There are
some areas in the canyon that are not receiving service because they are in shadow areas. He
has forwarded this information to Utah Broadband to follow-up on.
