                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-17

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE HISTORIC PRESERVATION
                           COMMISSION

        WHEREAS, on June 14, 2016, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 16-11
which provides for the preservation of historic structures and other assets located within Riverton
City; and

       WHEREAS, Administration of Ordinance No. 16-11 requires appointment of a Historic
Preservation Commission; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms her nomination for the following volunteer
appointments of Rick Bills, Bill Applegarth, Jen Preece, Brenda Newman and Cheri Colligan;
and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Rick Bills, Bill
Applegarth, Jen Preece, Brenda Newman and Cheri Colligan are residents of Riverton City, and
are willing and capable of serving on the Historic Preservation Commission;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointments of Rick Bills,
          Bill Applegarth, Jen Preece, Brenda Newman and Cheri Colligan to serve on the
          Historic Preservation Commission, which term shall end on January 31, 2030.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 5th day of
May 2026 by the following vote:
