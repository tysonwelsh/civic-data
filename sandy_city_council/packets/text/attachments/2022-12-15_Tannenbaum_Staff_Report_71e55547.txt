Request
 The applicant, Alec Moffitt, is requesting preliminary subdivision
 review to amend lots 102 and 103 of the Tannenbaum Cove
 Subdivision located at 9668 and 9682 S. Tannenbaum Cove
 respectively. The amended plat (see the attached Exhibit “A”) adjusts
 the property line between the two lots to allow for the installation of a
 fence that would be in keeping with the requirements of the Tree
 Preservation Easement.

Background
 The subject property is zoned R-1-10. All surrounding properties are
 similarly zoned. No special overlay zones are applicable to the properties.

 The original subdivision was reviewed by the city and recorded in 2020.
 During this review, it was determined that Tree Preservation Easements
 would be established to protect the existing trees where possible. The
 Urban Forester assisted in reviewing existing conditions and
 documenting the existing tree locations. Easements were then
 designated on the recorded plat and further described within the
 CC&R’s. Between lots 102 and 103, a 35-foot-wide Tree Preservation
 Easement was established. The property line within this easement did
 not take into consideration the location of existing mature trees.

 The owners have installed fencing south of the existing property line. They would like to relocate the property line to this
 same location in order to preserve the mature trees on-site.

Public Notice and Outreach
 This item has been noticed to property owners within 500 feet of the subject area. No neighborhood meeting was conducted
 as there are no new impacts created by this proposal.

Analysis
 The initial subdivision approval included the following condition:

 The applicant and their arborist shall work in coordination with the City’s Urban Forester to come up with a plan to preserve
 as many trees as possible, after considering proposed drainage designs and possibly location of accessory structures. This plan
 should be enforced through CC&R’s.




 An inventory of existing trees and their current condition was performed and used to determine the location of the Tree
 Preservation Easements. One of these was a 35-foot-wide easement between lots 102 and 103. The property line location
 was unfortunately shown directly over the location of several mature trees.

  The owners have installed a fence between their lots, but instead of
  following the property line, they angled the fence to the south in
  order to avoid damaging any existing trees.

  This amendment is to formally change the property line to match
  the location of the installed fencing. This change will result in lots
  that will continue to meet the requirements of the underlying
  zoning district including lot size, setbacks, and street frontage.

  Staff Concerns
  Staff has no concerns with the proposed subdivision Amendment.

Recommendation
 Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine the
 Tannenbaum Cove Amended Plat review is complete as described
 in the staff report is for the properties located at 9668 and 9682 S.
 Tannenbaum Cove based on the following findings and subject to
 the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. That the various city departments and divisions have
       preliminarily approved the proposed subdivision plat.

    2.    That the proposed subdivision will be finalized with city staff and recorded with the County Recorder.

Conditions:
   1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout the final review process and
       that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be recorded.

    2.    That all city provisions, codes, and ordinances are adhered to during the review process of this project.

    3. That all lots comply with the requirements of the R-1-10 Zone.

    4. That all previous conditions of approval imposed upon the original subdivision will remain in full for force and affect.
