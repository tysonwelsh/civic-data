                         KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

         RESOLUTION NO. 2023-11-01                            DATE: November 13, 2023

        A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP’S 2024
                          TENTATIVE BUDGET


       WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Kearns is required to adopt a tentative budget for the upcoming fiscal year
pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-111; and

      WHEREAS, Kearns has prepared a budget for 2024 and desires to adopt it as its tentative
2024 Budget.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED effectively immediately:

        1.     The attached budget is hereby adopted as Kearns’ tentative budget for the 2024
fiscal year.

        2.     The Kearns Council will hold a public hearing on December 6, 2023, at 6:00 p.m.
at the Kearns Library located at 4275 W. 5345 S.to receive public comments on the tentative
2023 Kearns budget.

        3.     Notice of the public hearing shall be provided pursuant to Utah Code Ann § 10-6-
113.

       4.       A copy of this tentative budget shall be made available for public inspection for a
period of at least ten days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-112.

        5.     This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
