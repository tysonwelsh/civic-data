   1. Factory Powersports Addition Planned Development Amendment and Development Agreement –
      Russell Key, representing General Properties LLC, is requesting approval of an amendment to the Factory
      Powersports PD (Planned Development) and adopt a development agreement. The amendment and
      development agreement would allow the applicant to add a 6,900 square foot addition to an existing
      building. The property is approximately 2.23 acres and is located at 1685 East Red Hills Parkway. The
      applicant is General Properties LLC. (Staff – Carol Winner)

       Carol Winner presented the following:

       Carol Winner – I will go over this briefly, we are returning because we have a development agreement to
       go with this planned development amendment. The addition is going to be two story, but it will look like
       a one story except from the back. We are doing a development agreement because 15 ft of landscape is
       required along Red Hills Pkwy, the applicants don’t quite meet that. They will be dedicating land for a 10
       ft trail and landscape 5 ft on each side of the trail. If you add the two 5 ft landscaped areas along the trail
       then it would equal 15 ft along Red Hills Pkwy. One of the issues they are having is on they are trying to
       get the deliveries away from the residential areas and move them to the commercial areas.

       Commissioner Andrus – Basically the development agreement is about the landscape and the trail?

       Carol Winner – Yes.

       Russell Key – Carol presented it really well. Essentially we want to expand the business and bring the
       delivery trucks away from the residential.

   3. Tech Ridge Southeast Slope/Ridgeline Mitigation Hillside Development Permit – Mike Bradshaw,
      representing Alliance Consulting, is requesting approval of a Hillside Development Permit to make
      modifications to the ridgeline along the southeast slope of the Tech Ridge Development. The applicant is
      Isaac Barlow. Case No. 2023-HS-002 (Staff – Carol Winner)

      Carol Winner presented the following:

      Carol Winner – Both this item and the next item are dealing with the east side, the southeast area. The
      applicant is trying to develop Tech Ridge, they noticed along this side we have a prominent ridgeline and
      then it peters out to nothing then it extends then peters out. There isn’t a consistent ridgeline that goes
      around the edge. They want to add some retaining walls to make it more stable so they can use the area. I
      believe you are all familiar with the trail that goes around the ridgeline of Tech Ridge, they want to bring
      the trail above the road so that it continues all the way around Tech Ridge. The Hillside Committee
      recommended approval. They wanted to make sure it looks like they way they did Cloud Drive, that was
      their recommendation. Carol showed a video of the ridgeline, you can see where it’s nonexistent and they
      are just trying to build it up a bit.

      Mike Bradshaw – The steepest portion is I believe 12% and then it flattens out dramatically after that in
      order to mitigate the road as much as possible, we had to steepen that up.

      Commissioner Andrus – Do you know what Cloud Drive is?

      Mike Bradshaw – Cloud Drive, the steepest on Cloud Drive I believe is like 8.5%. The 12 is really just
      the short segment as you come around this curb and come up.

      Commissioner Fisher – If you didn’t cut down and create a bridge there it would actually be steeper, right?

      Mike Bradshaw – What it does is, it creates this ridgeline. What it does is that when you put a building up

      will be utilized.

      trying to figure out where the ridgeline is.

      Commissioner Fisher – And they are following the ordinance as far as the setback, there is no deviation
      from that.

      Mike Bradshaw – Yes as soon as we build this then we will set back the buildings to the standard ordinance
      distances. On top of that the trail will be in there so everyone will be able to appreciate and walk that trail
      and participate with the scenery on the exterior.
