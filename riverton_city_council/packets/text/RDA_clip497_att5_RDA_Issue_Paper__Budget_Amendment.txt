Background:

Contract 17-1040-04, approved February 3, 2017, allowed for a sales tax sharing
agreement and the RDA. The specific details of this agreement were to have the first
$1,000,000 in annual point of sale tax dollars stay with the City, but any annual amounts
up to $88,204, which is also the maximum incentive, be remitted to the RDA. For
calendar year 2021, the City will transfer just under $59,000 to the RDA.


A separate agreement, 17-104-03, was also approved on February 3, 2017, requiring
that the RDA remit this same payment to CenterCal Properties LLC.

Also included in this amendment is budget to account for the land agreement between
the RDA and LNG LLC for property next to Riverton Hardware.



Recommended Motion:

“I move the board adopt RDA Resolution No. 22-01 - approving amendments to the
2021-2022 RDA fiscal year budget.”
