2.   PRESENTATIONS/RECOGNITIONS/AWARDS/PROCLAMATIONS

3.   WORK SESSION
     3.1. 47G/UDOT

     Paul Damron with the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) gave a presentation on the
work being done by the department to address the future use of Advance Air Mobility aircraft in
the state.

    Kori Ann Edwards with 47G gave a presentation on the current partnerships they had and the
benefits those relationships have for the city and state as a whole.

     Chris Motts with 47G presented on future projects planned in conjunction with the Air

Logistics and Transportation Alliance (Project ALTA).

    Ian Robertson with 47G presented current and future focused projects that their organization
planned to work on that included direct involvement with the city of Vineyard and the
opportunities those projects would provide to the city and its residents.

   Mayor Fullmer commented on future cyber security needs and the work that is being done by
47G in that field. Mr. Robertson contributed to the discussion and added clarification.

      3.2. Vineyard City's Pavement Preservation Program Update

    Streets and Stormwater Manager Chris Thomas presented the importance of pavement
preservation and the current state of the city’s roads.

    Aaron Eppley with Holbrook Asphalt gave a presentation on the work they had been
contracted to do and what options had been chosen for specific road types throughout the city.

      3.3. Discussion on Code of Conduct Ordinance

     Councilmember Clawson led a discussion on the proposed changes to the previously proposed
code of conduct and gave a general report of the work that had been done by both him and
Councilmember Holdaway. He invited members of the public to contact him if they were interested
in providing any feedback.


 4.     PUBLIC COMMENTS

      Mayor Fullmer opened for public comment.

   David Lauret, living on Holdaway Road, had questions regarding pavement preservation and
how the work done was warrantied.

    Daria Evans, living in The Villas subdivision, expressed concerns with the current state of
several roundabouts.

     Karen Cornelius, living in The Villas subdivision, shared concerns regarding a news article from
2024 which she felt seemed to indicate advanced knowledge by the mayor of some of the projects
presented by 47G. She also encouraged the council to find a more creative solution to building a new
city hall.

     Jane Pearce, living in the Cascade subdivision, expressed concern over the size of the proposed
library that had been allocated in the designs for the future Vineyard City Center.

    Mayor Fullmer closed public comments and answered questions that had been raised during
public comment.

5.    MAYOR AND COUNCILMEMBERS' REPORTS/DISCLOSURES/RECUSALS

     Councilmember Clawson expressed his appreciation for Orem fire, having recently gone to the
Orem Fire Awards Gala. He also thanked staff and the council for their patience and understanding as
the subcommittee worked through the code of conduct. Lastly, he thanked Mayor Fullmer and City
Manager Eric Ellis for including him on a working session with Lewis Robertson and Burningham
(LRB) so that he was able to gain a better understanding of the Redevelopment Agency (RDA).

     Councilmember Sifuentes expressed her excitement over the groundbreaking of the new grocery
store. She also gave an update on issues concerning the new school district as well as let the public
know that there would be townhalls in the future as the Active Transportation Commission worked
on their master plan.

    Councilmember Cameron reported on proposed legislation and her time spent with the Utah
League of Cities and Town’s legislative policy committee reviewing bills as they made their way
through the legislative process. She also expressed gratitude for the experience as it allowed her to
gain a better understanding of the systems of checks and balances that were in place. She contrasted
what she had seen with what had been portrayed on social media and in the news and said that the
experience gave her increased trust and confidence in those systems.
