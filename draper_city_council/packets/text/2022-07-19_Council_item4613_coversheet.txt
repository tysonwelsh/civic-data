Re: Exeter (Summit Commerce Center) Amended Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an Amended Development Agreement for approximately 26.94 acres located at the southwest corner of the intersection of Pony Express Rd. and Golden Harvest Rd., at approximately 13004 S. Pony Express Rd. The property is currently zoned M1. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Development Agreement be approved to allow for a Fast Food Restaurant as a Permitted Use under the agreement.
The Planning Commission heard this item at their June 23, 2022 meeting and forwarded a positive recommendation on the application with a 5-0 vote. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. The proposed Amended Development Agreement complies with the requirements of DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D).
For Denial:
