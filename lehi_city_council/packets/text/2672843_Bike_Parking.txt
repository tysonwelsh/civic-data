  REPORT ANALYSIS

Staff is proposing amendments to the bike parking requirements for three uses listed in Table 37.100 of the Lehi City
Development Code. Specifically, staff is requesting amendments to the bike parking requirements for Clubhouses,
Churches, and Manufacturing, Industrial, and Wholesale Establishments, and adding additional notes at the end of the
table. These amendments reduce the amount of bike parking required to a more realistic level while still providing room
to expand if the bike culture in Lehi continues to grow. These three uses were proposed to be amended first because of
three recent projects that have moved through the development process in Lehi that showed the bike parking requirements
were unreasonable. For example, a recent church in Lehi would be required to have 121 bike parking spaces based on the
current requirements. The proposed requirements would reduce that to 16 bike parking spaces. Staff has reviewed the
requirements of the other uses listed in the table and realized some of them were also unreasonable for today’s standards.
Staff will continue to gather data on more realistic requirements for those uses and propose another development code
amendment at a later date.

The notes added at the end of the table are to add additional flexibility to the bike parking requirements. Staff has realized
there is rarely a simple solution to bike parking and is highly dependent on a variety of factors. Therefore, note one was
added to allow the Zoning Administrator to consider other options to the required bike parking if deemed necessary. Note
two was added to be more reasonable for higher square-footage buildings. Notes four and five were added to help centralize
bike parking in more high-traffic pedestrian areas rather than requiring bike parking for every building.

There were no comments from the DRC.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Code Amendments are legislative items and also subject to public hearings. The public can share their
opinions on the amendments and recommend any changes. Ultimately, they would need the Planning Commission to
include the proposed changes in their motion.

The Planning Commission may move to recommend approval or denial of the proposed amendments to the Lehi
City Development Code. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

    1.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provi-
         sions of the General Plan and its various elements.
    2.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.

Narrative – Bike Parking Table Development Code
Amendment

The Planning Division is proposing a Development Code Amendment to Table 37.100, Table of
Bike Parking Requirements. This was in response to several projects moving through the city
currently that are being required to provide an exorbitant amount of bike parking. For example, a
stake center would have been required to provide 112 bike parking spaces. After reviewing the
current bike parking table it was determined several of the uses are overparked for bike parking
to an unnecessary degree. Staff will continue to work on reducing the other uses to be more in-
line with current demand. In the meantime, however, to address the projects currently moving
through the city, we have proposed amendments to three of the uses: Clubhouses, Churches, and
Manufacturing, Industrial, Wholesale establishments. Staff will continue to work on the other
uses as we gather more data and conduct surveys.
