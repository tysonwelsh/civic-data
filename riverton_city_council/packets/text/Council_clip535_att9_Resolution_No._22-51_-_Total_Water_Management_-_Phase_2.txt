                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 22-51

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
 WITH TOTAL WATER MANAGEMENT FOR THE COMPLETION PHASE 2, THE
             GREEN WELL WATER TREATMENT SYSTEM

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council required that the project must come back for final
approval before proceeding; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director and Water Manager would like to proceed with
the Green Well Treatment Project.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an Agreement with Total Water
            Management for the completion of Phase 2, the Green Well Water Treatment System.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
August 2022 by the following vote:
