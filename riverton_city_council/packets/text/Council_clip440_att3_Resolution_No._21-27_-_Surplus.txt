                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     RESOLUTION NO. 21-27

    A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE THE IDENTIFIED POLICE VEHICLES AS SURPLUS,
   AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF THESE ITEMS AS HE DEEMS
                              APPROPRIATE

       WHEREAS, the identified equipment has already been replaced and/or is no longer needed; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no longer
needed as surplus in a public meeting.

follows:
