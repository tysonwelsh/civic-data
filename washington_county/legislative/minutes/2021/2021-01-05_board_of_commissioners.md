WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JANUARY 5, 2021

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on January 5, 2021, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah.
Those in attendance were Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Dean Cox,
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, Commission
Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

INVOCATION BY: Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Undersheriff James Standley

Swearing in of Official: Dean Cox (Commissioner), Eric Clarke (Attorney), Tom
Durrant (Assessor), David Whitehead (Treasurer), Gary Christensen (Recorder)

At the commencement of the Commission Meeting Agenda, newly elected officials Dean
J. Cox, Eric Clarke, Tom Durrant, David Whitehead, Gary Christensen were sworn into
office by Washington County Clerk/ Auditor Kim Hafen.

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are
placed at the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a
motion to approve the items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Commissioner Cox stated that the groundbreaking of the new administration took place
today, January 5, 2021. The building is anticipated to be completed in September 2022 and
will be a much needed addition to better serve the public as Washington County continues

to grow in population.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:34 p.m.
