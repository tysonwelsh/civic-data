     Companies, LLC, requesting Final Plat Approval for the Decker Lake Subdivision

       Proposed Ordinance 21-08 related to the proposal to be considered by the City

       ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 21-08, APPROVE THE AMENDMENT OF
       LOTS 2E, 2F, AND A PORTION OF LOT 2G E-CENTER RETAIL
       SUBDIVISION LOT 2 SECOND AMENDED
       Steve Lehman, CED, discussed proposed Ordinance 21-08 that would approve the
       amendment of lots 2E, 2F, and a portion of lot 2G E-Center Retail Subdivision Lot 2
       Second Amended

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

               Rockworth Companies LLC, is requesting final plat approval for the Decker
               Station Subdivision. The subdivision will amend all of lots 2E and 2F and
               a portion of lot 2G, E-Center Retail Subdivision Lot 2 Second Amended.
               The purpose for the subdivision is to consolidate existing lots, vacate the
               public utility easement and dedicate right-of-way necessary for a new
               residential housing project

               The E-Center Retail Subdivision, Lot 2 second amended was recorded with

              the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office in July 2015. That subdivision plat
              consisted of 3 lots on 5 acres. This application will consolidate lots 2E and
              2F and a portion of 2G into 1 lot approximately 2.8 acres in size. The

RESOLUTION 21-34: APPROVE A PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
WITH A TO Z LANDSCAPING, INC. FOR MAINTENANCE WORK ON CITY
PROPERTIES
Jason Erekson, Parks and Recreation, presented proposed Resolution 21-34 that would
approve a Professional Services Agreement with A to Z Landscaping, Inc. for maintenance
work on City properties.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       was placed and ten bids were received. The lowest qualified bid was $123,245.93.
       The funding will come from the Park Maintenance budget and from a previously
       eliminated full-time position and two seasonal positions, which began happening
       in 2004. Of this, $57,351.51 was previously approved from the CARES Act funding
       for completion of existing projects and maintenance.

COMMUNICATIONS
A.  VETERAN’S HALL DISCUSSION
    Wayne Pyle, City Manager, stated he has had many conversations with the Mayor
    and staff and is need of Council direction at this time. Wayne detailed conversations
    regarding programming, building design, park planning, etc. Wayne provided a
    map of the proposed location and detailed Phase 1 of the project which would
    include a memorial wall, landscaping, minimal parking, etc. Wayne stated that
    Phase 2 would consist of a building, water features, and landscaping while Phase 3
    would complete the project with landscaping and other needs. Wayne noted other
    goals the City would like to work on in conjunction with the County to use river
    space and other property to the east as part of the project. Wayne provided concept
    plans of the Veterans Memorial, memorial wall, park space, etc. Wayne discussed
    funding options and budgetary constraints. Councilmember Buhler stated that there
    is a large park area that was formerly the Jordan River Marketplace and questioned
    how this would develop. Wayne replied that is part of Phase 3 and funding has not
    been provided at this time. Councilmember Buhler noted that the Council just
    purchased property from the Jones Trust and asked if this is included. Wayne
    replied that is part of the proposal as an option to be used. Councilmember
    Christensen asked where the building would be located. Wayne noted the proposed
    location on the map. Councilmember Lang asked where parking would be provided.
    Wayne indicated the location of parking options in Phase 1. Councilmember Lang
    stated she would like more detailed pictures of phases. Wayne stated that he could
    get a closer rendition of how the building would sit. Councilmember Buhler asked
    where funding will come from. Wayne replied that it would come from the General
    Fund. Councilmember Buhler asked how the City is doing with the Fund Balance.
    Wayne replied the City is still doing well percentage wise. Councilmember Buhler
    stated that he would like to go forward with this now. Mayor Bigelow stated that
    he was concerned with the proposal at first but feels much more comfortable with
    the suggestion of phases as outlined. Mayor Bigelow stated that there are other
    funding options that he is pursuing, including those with Salt Lake County. He
    noted the difficulty that has come from funding related to COVID. Wayne
    discussed the need for further design related to the park space. Councilmember
    Lang expressed concern about this area becoming a hotspot for homeless camps.
    Wayne replied that this is something that has been discussed and noted that there
    has been a strong effort to keep this area free of those problems. Councilmember
    Lang asked if there are bathroom facilities being proposed. Wayne replied no but
    noted that there are facilities several hundred yards away on the South Salt Lake
    side of the street as well as at the Cultural Center. Mayor Bigelow stated that he
    was concerned about the wall being long and straight, so staff is looking into
