WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
                         WORK MEETING MINUTES
                             MARCH 25, 2026

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Commissioner Iverson on March 25, 2026, in the Washington County
Commission Conference Room 421 in the Washington County Administration Building,
111 E Tabernacle Street, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Adam
Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County
Attorney Steven Scott, Dimon McFerson, Glenn Webb, Ginger Nelson, Michael Lee,
Trevor Sanders, Tony Allen, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County
Clerk Jayanne Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION WITH WASHINGTON COUNTY RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS
BOARD ON FUNDING GUIDELINES

Commissioner Iverson welcomed all in attendance and thanked them for coming, also for
being willing to serve on the Recreation, Arts, and Parks Board. Commissioner Iverson
shared the history of the Recreation, Arts, and Parks Tax. (RAP Tax)

There was a discussion on how the board looks at applications and determines eligibility
each year. It was reviewed with Deputy County Attorney Stephen Scott on how public
entities could qualify for RAP Tax funding. The board and Commissioners discussed
creating a tier system for applications. No details were decided upon. It was also
recommended that the Grant reporting date be changed to earlier in the year. This would
allow Board Members additional time to review how the grant funds awarded the previous
year were used. Allowing for more informed decisions on current-year applications.

Several board members shared appreciation for how efficiently the St. George City
Recreation, Arts, and Parks Board scores applications. It was recommended that everyone
in attendance review the St. George City Recreation, Arts, and Parks Board scoring matrix.

An additional meeting will be held at a future date.
