     LAND PLANNING AND DESIGN WORKSHOP FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT
     APPROXIMATELY 1300 WEST 300 SOUTH

     Jonathan Springmeyer introduced Ben Levenger, Nathan Davis, and Ryan Wallace with
     Downtown Redevelopment Services who explained the following:

           -   The purpose of this exercise is to explore potential land use patterns in a big-
               picture scale, focusing on overall form and not parcel-level analysis
           -   How the game works:
                   o Each square of grid represents roughly one square acre
                   o Place stickers on grid squares
                   o Colors represent different land use
                   o Stickers are symbolic, to indicate land use type within the acre
           -   Purpose of Exercise
                   o Each grid square will contain 1 sticker
                   o Future planning will allow for subdividing uses within acres
                   o Focus on general patterns, not restricting consideration to building
                       footprints or lot boundaries
                   o Testing the relationship between uses to shape the character of
                       downtown
           -   Takeaways
                   o Finished land use map will illustrate participants broad scale vision for
                       downtown
                   o Provides a discussion tool for future opportunities, tradeoffs, and
                       priorities for redevelopment


Each participant worked independently, placing stickers on their map to represent preferred
types of land use.

Mr. Levenger explained that the next steps will involve Downtown Redevelopment
Services compiling the land use preferences gathered from the sticker game exercise. These
preferences will be used to develop preliminary 3D scenario models, which will be
presented to the Council at a future meeting. Following that presentation, the scenarios will
be refined, and a final preferred scenario will be created and presented.

MOTION TO ADJOURN
Upon motion by Councilmember Tom Huynh all voted in favor to adjourn.
