RESOLUTION NO. 5861
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
STRONGLY ENCOURAGING AND SUPPORTING EFFORTS FOR 
THE MAYOR ’S OFFICE TO FULLY OPEN RECREATION AND 
COUNTY FACILITIES
WHEREAS, the Novel Coronavirus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) has been 
characterized by the World Health Organization as a worldwide pandemic 
caused by Severed Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus 2 
(SARS-CoV-2), a virus that spreads easily from person to person and can 
cause serious illness or death in certain individuals;
WHEREAS, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention 
(CDC), heart disease is the leading cause of death in America with about 
655,000 deaths a year;
WHEREAS, 88 million people 18 years of age and older have prediabetes 
(34.5 percent of the adult US population) and 24.2 million people 65 years of 
age or older have prediabetes;
WHEREAS, the number of people screening with moderate to severe 
symptoms of depression and anxiety has increased since the start of the 
COVID-19 pandemic and hit rates higher than prior to COVID-19;
WHEREAS, the CDC states that exercise helps prevent and improve a 
number of health problems, including high blood pressure, diabetes, and 
arthritis;
WHEREAS, the CDC states that research on depression, anxiety, and 
exercise shows that the psychological and physical benefits of exercise can 
help improve mood and reduce anxiety;
WHEREAS, the State of Utah established health guidance levels based off of 
a COVID -19 transmission index, which transmission index clarified the 
public health metrics used to determine which counties are placed in which 
Page 3 of 12
County Council Meeting Minutes May 18, 2021
transmission level and counties will be placed in one of three transmission 
levels: High, Moderate, or Low; 
WHEREAS, the transmission levels corresponded directly to case rates, 
positivity rates, and ICU utilization;
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County started in a high transmission level, moved 
into a moderate transmission level, and is nearing a low transmission level;
WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature passed HB 294 that eliminated mask 
requirements under the state health guidance levels and eliminated the 
established health guidance levels once three metrics are reached: ( 1) the 
14-day case rate reached a level at or below 191 per 100,000 people; (2) the 
7 day average COVID -19 ICU utilization was less than 15 percent; and (3) at 
least 1.63 million prime (first dose) COVID-19 vaccine doses have been 
allocated to the State of Utah;
WHEREAS, as of May 4, 2021, the three metrics have been reached and all 
state established health guidance levels have been eliminated;
WHEREAS, vaccines have been developed to prevent and reduce the impact 
of COVID -19 and are available to the general public ages 16 and older at 
no-cost;
WHEREAS, on Monday, May 17, 2021, vaccines became available to 
children between the ages of 12-15 at no-cost;
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council desires to create a reasonably 
safe environment so its customers and the general public may return to 
County facilities;
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council values its employees and desires 
to protect its employees and maintain a safe environment for them to conduct 
County business in;
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council feels strongly that the vaccine 
would be the best way to benefit its employees and protect them from the 
COVID-19 virus and wishes to support incentive options to encourage them 
to receive the vaccine;
Page 4 of 12
County Council Meeting Minutes May 18, 2021
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council desires that all employees that 
would like to be vaccinated to have work accommodations to do so;
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council believes a public health strategy 
should be taking into account all the health concerns and issues posed to a 
community;
WHEREAS, due to the lowering current case rates, ICU utilization rate, 
transmission rates; the increasing availability of vaccines; and the ongoing 
concerns about the other physical and mental health risks to an individual; the 
Salt Lake County Council would like County arts and recreational facilities to 
open and operate under normal protocols;
WHEREAS, the Mayor of Salt Lake County is the county executive officer 
of the county and Utah Code Title 17, Chapter 53, Part 3, authorizes her to 
perform all executive functions of the county and to direct and organize the 
management of the county in a manner consistent with state law, county 
ordinance, and county’s optional plan of county government;
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County owns and operates arts and recreational 
facilities for the public benefit and enjoyment;
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council desires that the mayor direct Sal 
Lake County arts and recreational facilities and programs to take additional, 
immediate actions to increase capacity, reduce restrictions, and resume all 
pre-COVID-19 programs.
NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Salt Lake County Council that;
1) The Council requests that the Mayor develop and execute a plan 
forthwith for re -opening Salt Lake County ’s arts and recreational facilities 
and programs at a potential capacity that is consistent with their 
pre-pandemic operations and present said plan to the Salt Lake County 
Council;
2) The Council requests that the Mayor create incentive options to 
encourage employees to receive the vaccine;
3) The Council requests that such incentive options also be available to 
employees who have already received the vaccine; and

4) The Council requests that the Mayor take all steps necessary to ensure 
that all Salt Lake County employees who desire a COVID -19 vaccine timely 
receive all necessary doses of a COVID-19 vaccine.

RESOLUTION NO. 5862
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
APPROVING A REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND THE 
CONVEYANCE OF SALT LAKE COUNTY ’S INTEREST IN CERTAIN 
REAL PROPERTY BY QUIT-CLAIM DEED TO HOTEL 5600, LLC
RECITALS
1. Salt Lake County (the “County”) claims to own a parcel of real property 
located at approximately 2880 South 5600 West, West Valley City, Utah (the 
“Property”) based upon a Quit -Claim Deed that was recorded on July 9, 
1970 (the “1970 Deed”).
2. A parcel with a unique parcel number was never created for the Property, 
and instead the Property appears on the Salt Lake County Assessor ’s 
records to be part of a much larger parcel identified as Parcel No. 14-26-229-
004 (the “Larger Parcel”), which is owned by Hotel 5600 LLC (“Buyer”).
3. The Property is subject to a Public Sidewalk, Lighting and Utility 
Easement granted to West Valley City by Buyer ’s predecessor -in-interest, 
recorded as Entry No. 12609390 on September 1, 2017, (the “Public 
Easement”).
4. During its efforts to develop the Larger Parcel, Buyer identified the 1970 
Deed as a cloud on its title.
Page 7 of 12
County Council Meeting Minutes May 18, 2021
5. To resolve all questions of title, Buyer has offered in writing to purchase 
the Property from the County for a nominal amount, which amount has been 
approved by the Salt Lake County Real Estate Section as adequate value, 
considering the Public Easement and the uncertainty regarding title 
undermining the strength of the county ’s claim of interest in the Property .  
This offer is in the form of a Real Estate Purchase Agreement (the 
“Agreement”) attached hereto as Exhibit A.
6. It has been determined that the best interest of the County and the general 
public will be served by the sale and conveyance of the Property to the 
Buyer.  The sale and conveyance will be in compliance with all applicable 
state statutes and county ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council the sale and conveyance of the Property by quit -claim deed 
to the Buyer as provided in the Agreement for the agreed nominal payment of 
One Hundred Fifty Dollars and Zero Cents ($150.00) is hereby approved; 
and the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute the Agreement and the Mayor 
and County Clerk are hereby authorized to execute the Quit Claim Deed, 
attached to the Agreement as Exhibit 2, and to deliver the fully executed 
documents to the County Real Estate Section for delivery to the Buyer as 
provided in the Agreement.

RESOLUTION NO. 5863
A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF SALT LAKE 
COUNTY APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING EXECUTION OF AN 
INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE 
UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT AND SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR 
THE PROVISION AND COST OF SERVICES PERTAINING TO FLEET 
MANAGEMENT SERVICES, INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY, 
COUNTY SURVEY SERVICES, RECORD MANAGEMENT AND 
ARCHIVE SERVICES, FACILITIES MANAGEMENT SERVICES, AND 
LEGAL AND RISK MANAGEMENT SERVICES.
RECITALS
A.  Salt Lake County (the “County”) and the United Police Department 
(“UPD”) are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation 
Act, Utah Code Ann. §§ 11-13-101 et seq . (the “Interlocal Agreement ”), and, 
as such, are authorized by the Interlocal Act to enter into this Agreement to 
act jointly and cooperatively on the basis of mutual advantage in order to 
provide facilities in a manner that will accord best with geographic, 
economic, population and other factors influencing the needs and 
development of local communities.
B. The County and UPD desire to enter into the agreement for services 
attached hereto (the “Agreement”) wherein the Parties agree to provisions 
(and cost) of services pertaining to Fleet Management services, Information 
Technology, County Survey services, Record Management and Archive 
services, Facilities Management services, and Legal and Risk Management 
services. 
C. The County Council believes that the arrangement under the Agreement 
will contribute to the prosperity, welfare, peace and comfort of residents 
within Salt Lake County.
