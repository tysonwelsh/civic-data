Dear Lehi City,
We are actively looking to simply change the plan from. 50 acres to .30 acres. We respect and
understand the want to preserve. 50 acres. However, it isn't so far off to ask for .30. We want
what's highest and best use for the consumer and the city.
Our thought process is with .30 right off the main street it would allow for a softer more aesthetic
look where we could have some soft landscape/trees/sign and would be more appealing over all
than just large homes right on the street. Respectfully, College Farms is walking distance
southwest at .33 acres. I understand they were major land owners and able to offer open land in
negotiations for .33. I don’t think that smaller land owners should be discriminated against
because they don’t have that land for that option. We are willing to work with you as best as
possible. The next point to highly consider, is that the real estate market and the community in
general is demanding more affordable housing. The .50 acres would have us building 1.7-2mill
homes vs the .30 could lower that to hopefully right under 1 million. With the surrounding
elementary school etc., that seems to be what the community needs. There are several half
acre lots sitting on the market I'm sure because of affordability. Lastly this would create more
tax id's for the city which is a bonus to the city and the community. We appreciate you taking a
practical look at this and working with us amicably.


Best Regards,
