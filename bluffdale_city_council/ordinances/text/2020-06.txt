                                CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UT AH

                                   ORDINANCE NO. 2020-06

AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A TEMPORARY LAND USE REGULATION
PROHIBITING CERTAIN APPLICATIONS OR APPROVALS OF A COMMERCIAL
SIGN PURSUANT TO TITLE 11, CHAPTER 22, SIGNS AND OUTDOOR
ADVERTISING.

WHEREAS Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-504 allows municipal legislative bodies, without prior
consideration of or recommendation from the planning commission, to enact an ordinance
establishing a temporary land use regulation for any part or all of the area within the municipality
if the legislative body makes a finding of compelling, countervailing public interest;

WHEREAS such a temporary land use regulation may prohibit or regulate the erection,
construction, reconstruction, or alteration of any building or structure or any subdivision
approval;

WHEREAS the City finds it necessary to establish such a temporary land use regulation for
certain portions of the City regarding commercial signs while an amendment is written to the
current Bluffdale Land Use Ordinance;

WHEREAS currently, the City has one completed application for a residential development
monument sign that has been approved and ready to be picked up at the Building Department
and there are no pending applications for any commercial sign; and

WHEREAS the reason for the temporary regulation is that the City desires to reconsider its
regulations to avoid adverse impacts on the public health, safety, and welfare of its residents due
to the existence of commercial signs in their current form;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.      Findings. The City Council hereby finds a compelling, countervailing
public interest in protecting residential property interests and in preserving the health, safety, and
welfare of its residents as they are affected by certain commercial signs. The City Council further
finds that such interests may not be properly protected under existing provisions of the Bluffdale
Land Use Ordinance.

        Section 2.      Temporary Land Use Regulation. Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-
504, a temporary land use regulation not to exceed six months is hereby established for the
entirety of the City of Bluffdale except properties located within the GW-R Gateway Redwood
Zone, the SD-C Gateway Hospitality Zone, and the SD-X Bringhurst Station Zone pertaining to
signs in commercial zones. By this temporary regulation, all applications and other development
activities involving signs in commercial zones not otherwise exempted herein are hereby
prohibited.
