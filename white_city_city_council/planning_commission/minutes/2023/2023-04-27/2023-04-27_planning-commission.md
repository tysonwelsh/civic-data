                                         Hearing began at – 6:25 p.m.

OAM2023-000785 – Public Hearing on Drafted Titles 18 and 19 (Zoning & Subdivisions). The Council
and Planning Commission have been working with staff to draft amendments to Title 18 (Subdivisions) and
Title 19 (Zoning) of White City Municipal Code. Participants will have the opportunity to provide feedback
on the Titles as drafted. Prior to opening the public hearing, the Planning Commission will hear a
presentation from planning staff regarding the drafted Titles. This item affects all areas within White City
Metro Township. The drafted sections of Title 18 and Title 19 will be proposed as a repeal and replacement
of existing Titles 18 and 19 of Municipal Code. This item covers ordinance text amendments ONLY. There
are no map amendments proposed at this time. Planner: Erin O’Kelley (Motion/Voting).

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Erin O’Kelley provided an analysis of
the code amendments.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding grandfathering, use standards, lot coverage,

Commissioner Seiger-Webster motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Millen seconded that
motion.
