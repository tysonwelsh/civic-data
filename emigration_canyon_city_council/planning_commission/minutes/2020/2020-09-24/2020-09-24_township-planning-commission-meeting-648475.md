Meeting began at – 8:32 a.m.
Commissioner Pinon read the electronic meeting and opening statement.
1) General Plan Land Use Chapter Discussion and Review. Planner: Kate Davies
Kate provided an update and review of the Land use chapter. Commissioners discussed the
Emigration Canyon Character Areas regarding main canyon road character area and recent
development character area. Went through the demographic projections through the canyon, recent
developments. No changes made to character area descriptions. Work Plan goals. Commissioners

No other business items to discuss.
