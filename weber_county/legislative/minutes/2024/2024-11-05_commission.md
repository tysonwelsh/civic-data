D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: In the process eight years ago of my first election process, my
   wise and dear friend, John Bond, told me, “Have you ever studied hurricanes? They blow things around and
   they’re windy. Where is the calmest part of a hurricane? In the center, in the eye. “No matter where you or
   what you are participating where you feel or hear the winds blowing, the center is calm.” The challenge is the
   storm keeps moving so you have to adjust to stay in the eye. His council is good and right. I want to tell him
   that after 30 plus years of service to Weber County it is a better place because you have chosen to be a part of it.
   You did that not only for your county but for your community in Roy and in the walls of your own home. Thank
   you so much for being who you are.

    2. Public Hearing for reconsideration of the development agreement related to a rezone of phase 2 of the
       Winston Park development, approximately 40 acres from the A-1 (Agriculture) zone to the R1-15
       (Residential) zone. The proposal is located at approximately 3300 South 3900 West.

         Charlie Ewert (Planning): This proposed ordinance will rezone around 40 acres of Winston Park Phase 2
         from A-1 to R1-15. This was recommended by the Western Weber Planning Commission on February 13th,
         2024.

    3. Public hearing and possible action on a request to amend the residential zones to create an R1-5 single family
       zone and to amend the R3 zone to create R3-A and R3-S zones to provide for a multifamily attached zone
       and a multifamily stacked zone, and to amend the site development standards for all R1, R2, and R3 zones,
       and related clerical and administrative amendments

         Charlie Ewert (Planning): This proposed change is trying to bring our ordinance into the 21st century and
         make it useful for existing development patterns as opposed to the 1950s style development patterns that our
         ordinances were previously based on. This is coming at the request of an applicant who is pursuing a rezone
         you guys will be talking about in a couple of months.

     1. Stall of Fame: Christie Ingram from Assessor’s Office.
        John Bond (County Treasurer): In 2007 July Christie Ingram provided an application to come work for
        us. A lot of Christie's work currently involves helping the county pay all the bills. She would pump out
        10k to 12k checks a year. They would cover her desk and she'd get everything paid for the county. That
        was a lot of a lot of movement and keeping track of all that stuff, getting all those records put together.
        That's changed a little bit because we've moved a lot to electronic format where we currently are doing
        7.5k to 8k checks a year. Last year we collected over $60M on our website electronically and about 14k
        payments. In the last four weeks, 63% of the County's bills were paid electronically. She keeps track of
        all of that in our office. She's there to take care of our retirement and payroll taxes. She helps us
        wherever we need help in our office; she jumps in there and we appreciate that floatiness because we
        always have little pressure points. She's anxious to help us where we have gaps, problems, and stuff like
        that. Where she sits in the front counter is in direct line to my office and the thing I like just closing my
        ears and closing my eyes and listening to her answer the phone. It's just a pleasant moment every day of
        my life. I love it and that's one of the joys of my life. She also has a life away from the office. You may
        not know she loves the farm life and is just adapted to that whole world with the chickens and the goats
        and the cats and all that goes with all those things. She gets up in the morning early at 3:30 4:00 in the
        morning to do her routines and then she's off running on the road. She runs like a half marathon every
        week. I don't know how on earth that happens but she is the most fit person in my universe. And she
        now has three granddaughters that she tends to every Wednesday and it's fun to watch her involvement
        with her grandkids and beam at her boys and their families growing up. We see different parts of
        Chrissy's life because of our family that we have in the Treasurer's office. What a treat it is for us to ask
        you to provide her the Stall of Fame for the county this month.
