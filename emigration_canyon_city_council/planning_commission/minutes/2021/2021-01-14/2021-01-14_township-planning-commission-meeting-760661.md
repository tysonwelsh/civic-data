PUBLIC HEARINGS
Hearings began at – 8:39 a.m.
(Continued from 11/19 and 12/17/2020) - Emigration Canyon Metro Township is considering adopting
the proposed 2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township General Plan, which plans for the areas within
Emigration Canyon Metro Township. The 2020 General Plan is Emigration Canyon’s first comprehensive
plan since incorporation as a metro township in 2017. The Plan synthesizes data and public feedback to
describe current conditions, envision Emigration Canyon’s future, and guide Emigration Canyon from
present conditions toward that desired vision. This General Plan meets the required State laws for general
planning, including elements on Land Use and Transportation. It also includes the following supplemental
elements: Introduction, Economic Development, Environment, Resilience and Infrastructure, and
Community Work Program. Planner: Kate Davies (Motion/Voting)
Please visit: https://ec-lrp-hub-gslmsd.hub.arcgis.com/ to view a copy of the 2020 Emigration Canyon
Metro Township General Plan.
The Planning Commission will act on the proposed general plan.
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Kate Davies provided a presentation
of the updates and overview of the General Plan.
Commissioners and Staff had a brief discussion and correction regarding page 74 on the bike lanes,
correction from 40 feet wide to four feet wide and include symbols for footage, repaired bike lanes and
shoulders, Ms. Davies will confirm with engineering, and Lochner report and progress. Dropping a foot
from car lanes and added a foot to the bike lanes, with consistent 11-foot car lanes and with the shoulder a
five-foot bike lane where road width allowed.
Replacing the word budget with funding. Removing the second sentence from 3.4.2. Add Lochner report to
the Appendix E so it’s clear it isn’t just identified as the Emigration Canyon Transportation Study.
Ms. Davies explained the council wants a postcard sent to the canyon citizens and will be heard at their
February 25th meeting.

Planning Commissioners gave thanks the Ms. Davies, MSD, GPSC, and Emigration Canyon Community

Motion: To recommend the proposed 2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township General Plan, which plans
for the areas within Emigration Canyon Metro Township. The 2020 General Plan is Emigration Canyon’s
first comprehensive plan since incorporation as a metro township in 2017. The Plan synthesizes data and
public feedback to describe current conditions, envision Emigration Canyon’s future, and guide Emigration
Canyon from present conditions toward that desired vision. This General Plan meets the required State laws
for general planning, including elements on Land Use and Transportation. It also includes the following
supplemental elements: Introduction, Economic Development, Environment, Resilience and Infrastructure,
and Community Work Program as written and modified pursuant to discussion points today and when
passed for public review, clear the draft general plan document is the one for approval to the Emigration
Canyon Council for adoption.
Motion by: Commissioner Karkut
2nd by: Commissioner Harpst
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
