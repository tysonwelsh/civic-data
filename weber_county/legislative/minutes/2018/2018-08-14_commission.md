F. PRESENTATION: UPDATE FROM YOUR COMMUNITY CONNECTION (YCC) AND REQUEST FOR FUNDING.
   Julie Smith, of the YCC, introduced staff members and outlined their indispensable work with the YCC, which has
   been in existence for 73 years and is a breakaway from the YWCA. They have five centers that address homelessness,
   rape recovery, domestic violence, Senior Life Care, etc. The number one cause of homicides in Utah is domestic
   violence. Lana Dean spoke as a teacher, principal, and now as a volunteer where she has seen the lasting insidious
   imprints and negative impacts of domestic violence and homelessness, which affects every age and facet of the
   community. Taxpayers absorb most of the negative economic impacts of homelessness due to domestic violence.
   The YCC is being proactive to reduce those expenses and Ms. Dean asked the Commission to aid them in this effort.
   Zen Uriarte said that the Commission’s support is crucial to the community and its contribution helped serve over
   235 individuals. She asked for their consideration of funding stating that the YCC programs serve over 60 families
   every day, that their five housing programs are crucial to our community and gave patrons’ successful examples. Ms.
   Smith referred to the great investment created with the county’s funding support and asked for their continued
   investment in the people of Weber County. In the past year they served over 1,400 people related to domestic
   violence. She addressed the commissioners’ questions including that their shelter has increased from 26 to 62 beds,
   noting that they’ve had to turn away many people. The commissioners expressed support for this invaluable resource.

    7. REAL PROPERTY LEASE AGREEMENT WITH STAKER & PARSON COMPANIES.
         Bill Ross, of County Property Management, presented this lease of the county’s 3.5 acres of the lot where the
         County Road Shops are adjacent to Staker for $2,000/month for three years. Commissioner Ebert expressed
         concern with the county leasing the property when it has a huge need to build a storage facility and does not own
         much suitable land as this; it was tagged for that facility. Initial conversation was that Staker would buy the
         entire property and the county would construct a storage facility along with the new shops. Mr. Ross responded
         that there is ongoing discussion but no firm plans to build anything, and there is a contract termination clause.
         Staker has provided various property options to the county, and the subject property is just a junk pile and will be
         used for their outdoor storage. Chair Harvey stated that the county leases indoor storage space. He also said that
         the county owns acreage by the Sheriff’s Office. Commissioner Jenkins noted that currently the property is not
         being used and thus favors this lease. He recommended having discussions on constructing the storage building.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Real Property Lease Agreement with Staker & Parson Companies;
         Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – abstained; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
