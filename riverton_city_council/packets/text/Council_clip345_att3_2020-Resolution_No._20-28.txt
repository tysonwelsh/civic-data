                              RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                              RESOLUTION NO. 20-28

   A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL RENEWING AND
          EXTENDING LOCAL EMERGENCY DECLARATION

       WHEREAS, on the date of March 17, 2020, Mayor Staggs of Riverton City,
Utah identified the COVID-19 virus as an imminent threat to the health and safety of
Utah residents, and accordingly declared a local emergency pursuant to Utah Code §53-
2a-208; and

        WHEREAS, specifically, the Mayor identified the nature of the local emergency
is the “potential for widespread transmission of the COVID-19 virus and all associated
risks to health, welfare, and safety of Riverton residents and subsequent negative
economic impact to individuals, families and businesses;” and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code§53-2a-208 (b) states “a local emergency shall not be
continued or renewed for a period in excess of 30 days except by or with the consent of
the governing body of the municipality;” and

        WHEREAS, On March 27, 2020, Governor Gary Herbert issued a statewide
directive encouraging all Utah citizen to stay safe by staying home, and seeking to make
clear what all individuals and businesses should do to slow the spread of COVID-19 in
the State and local communities; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the presence of
COVID-19 virus remains a threat to the health and safety of Riverton residents, as does
the possibility of widespread transmission of the same;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton
City as follows:

   1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to, supports and adopts Mayor Staggs’
      Declaration of an Emergency, issued on the date of March 17, 2020 and
      Emergency Executive Order on the date of April 1, 2020.
   2. The Riverton City Council hereby extends Mayor Staggs’ emergency declaration
      until such time as the Riverton City Council repeals this Emergency Declaration
      by adoption of a Resolution.

   PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
April, 2020 by the following vote:
