GENERAL SESSION

Chair Snow called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:03 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

  1. Boyd Brown requests a Conditional Use Permit approval for two duplexes located at
     6871 South 185 East and 6875 South 190 East.

     Boyd Brown requests a Conditional Use Permit approval of two duplexes located at 6871 South 185
     East and 6875 South 190 East in the Single Family Residential/Duplex Overlay (SF-1/DO) zone.
     These structures will be placed on the front lot and the associated flag lot of the Carlsen Subdivision
     which received City Council approval on March 1, 2022, and Planning Commission approval on
     February 9, 2022.

     Public notice was sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel; additionally, a sign
     was placed on the property on 7/28/2022 and will remain until city council approval. At the time of
     this writing no public comment has been received.

     Staff recommends approval.

                                                    ***

APPLICANT:

Mr. Brown introduces himself and explains the work that went into making sure the back lot was
within ordinances of the code. Mr. Brown takes great pride and is happy with the turn out. He
explains that the foundation for the old structure was not sound, and they decided to move
forward with demolition.

The carport is the best, residential looking solution to the parking for the property. The intention
was to make sure that the side of a building wasn’t facing the street as the Planning Commission
had previously asked for.

2. Tyler Robbins requests a Final Plat approval for Prestige Estates #2 to convert a duplex
   into a twin home located at 481-483 Acoma Road.
