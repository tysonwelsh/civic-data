include power washing driveways, sidewalks, homes, businesses, and vehicles.

       C.     FUNARO PRIVATE LANE, PLZ-23-2014, A CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT
              FOR A PRIVATE LANE LOCATED AT 1794 WEST MYERS LANE.
              APPLICANT - PETER FUNARO.

Ms. Halversen presented the Staff Report and stated that the subject parcel is .5 acres in
size. It was recently rezoned to R-4 so that it could be subdivided. The Planning Commission
previously recommended approval of the Funaro rezone request and the City Council
subsequently granted final approval to change from RR-22 to R-4 zoning. The neighboring
parcels are a mix of R-3, R-4, and RR-22. The lot has 125 feet of frontage onto 11800 South
along the south side of the property. The applicant is proposing to run a 20-foot-wide private
lane from the southeast corner of the lot to the north. This will result in two buildable lots
with the south lot retaining the existing home with access to 11800 South. The new lot will
be accessed on the private lane. Ms. Halverson stated that the subdivision application will
be required before any construction on the lane begins and any new lot will ultimately be
reviewed by the Planning Commission.

City Code states that the first preference is for development to use public streets but
recognizes that public streets may be “impossible or impractical” on some properties. Staff
believes this property should be considered a private lane for several reasons including:

      There are no City roads planned in this area on the Master Transportation Plan.
      No local streets are stubbed to this property or pointed toward this property.
      A private lane will not affect access for neighboring properties.
      There is already an existing drive approach on this lot to 11800 South in the area of
       the proposed private lane.
      City Code further states that private lanes serving 1 to 2 lots must have 20 feet of
       paved surface width and have an approved fire turnaround.

Staff recommended approval with the conditions listed in the Staff Report.

Commissioner Cluff clarified that the private lane will consolidate the drive approaches.
Access will be through the private lane and adjacent road.

Commissioner Cannon stated that 11800 South is not a State Road with the property to the
north being in the South Jordan boundary. It was confirmed that Midas Creek runs adjacent
to the property. Commissioner Matheson requested a diagram of the fire access and
turnaround. Commissioner Park believed the one-quarter acre lot will be taken up by the
required turnaround access.

The applicant, Peter Funaro, stated that they are taking the project step by step and learning
as they go. Turnaround, safety, and sewer issues will all be addressed at the appropriate
time. He confirmed that the second driveway will be moved to the end of the project.

       D.     SMART PRIVATE LANE, PLZ-23-2015, A CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT
              FOR A PRIVATE LANE LOCATED AT 13595 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD.
              APPLICANT - DAVID SMART.

Ms. Halversen presented the Staff Report and displayed a rendering of the property, which
is located on the east side of Redwood Road at approximately 1602 West 13595 South. The
large 3.3-acre property has one existing home. The applicant has worked out an agreement
with the adjoining property owner to the west to adjust the existing parcel lines to make the
existing vacant parcel larger and accessible by a private lane from Redwood Road. The
Planning Commission previously approved a private lane for this property; however, the final
condition required the Utah Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) approval of the private
lane access onto Redwood Road. UDOT did not give their approval, which resulted in the
applicant having to relocate the proposed access to the private lane. The applicant reached
an agreement with a neighboring property owner to grant an easement for their access to
Redwood Road.

Previously, the Planning Commission did not approve a specific plan but a general concept
that determined that a private lane is appropriate for this property in the location shown,
however, UDOT did not approve the access in that location. As a result, the applicant
returned with a new private lane alignment with a connection point on Redwood Road, which
was approved by UDOT.

In reviewing the configuration of the properties and the application, staff believes it qualifies
for a private lane as it is a parcel of record with no frontage and will not impede any road on
the Transportation Plan or possible connections to adjoining properties. Staff recommended
approval with conditions listed in the Staff Report.

Commissioner Cannon appreciated the applicant’s effort in returning after having completed
the Planning Commission’s recommendations.

Commissioner Rushton commented that the applicant has a 3.3-acre parcel but they are
only discussing a single lot access with this lane. He asked if there are suitable options to
access the rest of the parcel at a future date. Mr. Carter stated that the Code prohibits
subdividing in such a manner that would result in landlocking. One way to address the
access would be to amend the Conditional Use for the private lane and stretch or redesign
the lane.

City Planner, Tim Prestwich, clarified that there is a 25-foot access with a 20-foot paved
surface. The existing property is served off an adjacent private lane and either would return
to the Commission for approval. The applicant is proposing one lot on the most buildable
spot. The easement runs with the land and is recorded against the title of the property.

The applicant, David Smart stated that he intends to build one additional structure. There is
an executed Easement Agreement that has been completed and notarized. In reviewing
the process, it was determined that based on the lane width, there is the capability of an
additional structure down the road.
