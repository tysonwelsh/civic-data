G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. PRESENTATION AND REQUEST FOR APPROVAL OF VISIT OGDEN’S 2021 MARKETING PLAN
      Sara Toliver, of Visit Ogden, said that they started the year very strong until the pandemic (March). It is estimated
      that the Utah ski industry lost $234M in revenue due to the early closure. She outlined the myriad of things that
      they have been engaged in during this pandemic including that they pivoted from the marketing campaign and
      sales efforts and became a community resource for the latest information, Governor recommendations and travel
      sanctions. They began providing education, training, support services and CARES Act grant information for the
      industry, have been constantly monitoring industry trends, created new content to ensure the community and
      partners knew where to find the information they needed, ran the first campaign encouraging the local increase
      of Pineview visitors to pack-in/pack-out, combined the ski and summer campaign into a year-round leisure
      campaign and applied for grants. She noted that Salt Lake International Airport’s phase 1 completion and opening
      last month should help our State significantly as we move forward. We received great coverage including in the
      Travel and Leisure and Readers Digest. Last December they secured a $20,000 grant from the Utah Office of
      Tourism to create a tourism master plan and this work began in August with various groups. Transient room tax
      collection is down about 13% year over year, restaurant tax collections are down 3% year over year, leased vehicle
      taxes are the most impacted tourism tax, and short term rentals experienced a dramatic rebound this summer.
      Industry experts predict that occupancies will rebound to 2019 levels in 2022, but the rate won’t return until
      2023+. She referred to the Destination Development Plan that they are ready to begin implementing that includes
      a list of destination imperatives. The commissioners thanked Ms. Toliver for the great job of adapting and staying
      within the guidelines in these trying times.
      Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve Visit Ogden’s 2021 marketing plan; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
      Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
