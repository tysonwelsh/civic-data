SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND

This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for approximately 1.47 acres
located on the north side of Kennington Court, at approximately 13648 S Kennington Court (Exhibit B).
The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment be
approved to amend lot 8 and create a new lot within the subdivision.

The original Kennington Estates Subdivision was created in 2007, and amended in 2010. The original
subdivision has 14 Lots and 3 parcels. An amendment in 2010 changed lot lines and adjusted parcels that
were switched over to Home Owner Association (HOA) control to allow a more functional configuration
for existing structures (Exhibit F). The subject application affects Lot 8 within this subdivision. The
applicant is seeking to purchase property located behind their lot and incorporate it into their property. In
order to do this, the boundary of the subdivision will need to be extended to include the entire property
behind Lot 8.

The property that will be newly incorporated into the subdivision is located on Shadow Mountain Lane.
This lane was created around 1980 and none of the homes on the lane are located within a subdivision.
The home on the property was built in 1980. The applicant will purchase the back half of the property and
include it in the existing Lot 8 of Kennington Estates. The remainder of the property will become a new
lot within the Kennington Estates development.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential Low/Medium

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately two dwelling
units per acre (Exhibit C). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 “The
purpose of the RA1 and RA2 zones is to foster low density development with little impact on its
surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas;
and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of limited
numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family
dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.” The RA1 zoning abuts the
subject properties on the east, and RA2 abuts on the north, south, and west.

Subdivision Layout. The proposed amendment will be adding one lot to the subdivision and allow Lot 8
to adjust the rear boundary line, adding approximately one half acre from the proposed new Lot 102.

DCMC Section 17-4-030 requires lots within a plat amendment to be renumbered. Lot 8 is referenced as
Lot 101 on the proposed plat. Lot 101 will have access to the property from Kennington Court. An access
easement will be provided on Lot 102 in favor of Lot 101 in order to allow for access to a detached
garage via Shadow Mountain Lane (a private lane). Lot 102 will continue obtaining access from Shadow
Mountain Lane (Exhibit E). Since there will not be a new lot added to Shadow Mountain Lane in regard
to access, no lane improvements will be required with this amendment. Additionally, Kennington Court
is a public road and no improvements are required for the roadway. Each lot will meet minimum area
requirements for the zone (20,000 ft² min lot size).

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment
request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

          The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing an
          amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds that there is
          good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has
          been vacated or amended.
