BUSINESS ITEM(S)

CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT(S) – ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION

More Than Mysteries - Project # 25-014 - 635 West 5300 South - Review and Approval of an
Escape/Puzzle Room Business in the C-D Zone

Hannah Tucker was present to represent the request. Ruth Ruach presented the application for
Conditional Use Permit approval to allow an entertainment business that provides escape rooms in
the C-D zone. Ms. Ruach showed the site plan for the business. She described the unit, located in
the Riverview Plaza, and showed the floor plan. She provided details regarding the parking access.
Public notices were sent to surrounding residents, with no comments being received. Staff
recommends that the Planning Commission approve a conditional use permit to allow an escape
room entertainment business at this property, subject to the conditions.

Ms. Tucker approached the podium. Chair Richards asked if she had read and could comply with
the conditions. Ms. Tucker said yes, she could.

Chair Richards opened the agenda item for public comment. Seeing none, the public comment
period was closed.

Commissioner Klinge and Ms. Tucker had a discussion about how the escape room works.

Vice Chair Hristou made a motion to allow an escape/puzzle room entertainment business at the
property addressed 635 West 5300 South, #301, subject to the following conditions:

1. The applicant shall obtain a Murray City Business License prior to beginning operations at this
   location.
2. The project shall comply with all applicable building and fire code standards.
3. The applicant shall obtain permits for any new attached or detached signs proposed for the
   business

Seconded by Commissioner Klinge. Roll call vote:

SUBDIVISION REVIEW(S) – ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION

Berger Station Subdivision & Berger Place Vacation - Project # 24-010 - 171, 173, 175,177, 179,
237, 273, 277 West Berger Lane – One-year time Extension for Subdivision Vacation and
Preliminary/Final Subdivision Approval

Jared Shupe was present to represent the request. Zachary Smallwood presented the application
requesting a one-year extension to the approval to vacate the Berger Place Condominiums and
Final subdivision approval for the three-lot Berger Station Subdivision, located in the MCMU zone.
Mr. Smallwood showed the parcels on the site plan, pointing out the parcel with the condo
subdivision. He reminded the Planning Commission that the property was approved in March 2024
for residential and small commercial use. Staff recommends that the Planning Commission grant
the one-year time extension, subject to the initial conditions.

Vice Chair Hristou asked about the indentation into the parcel. Mr. Smallwood said that piece is
privately owned and is not included in this application.

Mr. Shupe approached the podium. Chair Richards asked if he had read and could comply with the
conditions. Mr. Shupe said yes, he could.

Commissioner Hacker asked how many units will be developed. Mr. Shupe said there will be a total
of 423 units.

Commissioner Klinge asked Mr. Shupe what the layouts of the units will be. Mr. Shupe said there
will be studios, as well as one-bedroom and two-bedroom layouts.

Chair Richards opened the agenda item for public comment. Seeing none, the public comment
period was closed.

Commissioner Hacker made a motion that the Planning Commission grant a one-year time
extension to vacate Berger Place Condominiums and grant preliminary & final subdivision approval
for the Berger Station Subdivision on the properties located 171, 173, 175,177, 179, 237, 273,
277 West Berger Lane, subject to the original final subdivision approval as outlined below:

1. The project shall meet Murray City Engineering requirements including the following:
   a. Meet City Subdivision requirements and standards – City Code Title 16.
2. Meet all Wastewater Division requirements including the following:
   a. All sewer laterals for existing buildings must be properly capped and abandoned according
        to Murray Wastewater Specifications.
3. Meet all Water Division requirements.
4. Meet all Power Department requirements.
5. Meet all Fire Department requirements.
6. The project shall meet all applicable requirements of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
7. The applicant shall prepare a Final Subdivision plat which complies with all requirements of
   Title 16, Murray City Subdivision Ordinance.

LAND USE TEXT AMENDMENT(S) – LEGISLATIVE ACTION

Furever in Style - Project # 25-009 - Land Use Text Amendment to Add Dog Grooming LU 8224 as a
Permitted Use in the Residential Neighborhood Business (R-N-B) Zoning District

Jorge Cumpa was present to represent the request. David Rodgers presented the application
requesting to add Land Use Code “8224 – Pet Grooming” as a permitted use in the R-N-B Zoning
District. Mr. Rodgers showed the site plan and described the request. He said that a dog grooming
business would be appropriate in the requested zone. He discussed how the language for the
current code would be changed. Staff feel that the requested change meets the goals of the
General Plan. Notices were sent to affected entities, with no comments being received. Staff
recommends that the Planning Commission forward a recommendation of approval to the City
Council for the proposed amendment.

Jorge Cumpa approached the podium for questions.

The commissioners and Mr. Cumpa had a brief discussion about location of the business, the type
of building, the amount of parking and the number of clients. Mr. Cumpa indicated that the
business is in an office building with ample parking. He said they have three mobile vans that are
parked at groomers’ homes. They want to give clients the option to have a brick-and-mortar
location.

Chair Richards opened the agenda item for public comment. Seeing none, the public comment
period was closed.

Commissioner Hacker asked if they’ll need to redo their business license with the addition of the
brick-and-mortar location. Mr. Smallwood said yes.

Commissioner Rogers made a motion for the Planning Commission to forward a recommendation of
approval to the City Council for the proposed amendment to Section 17.140.020 adding Land Use
Code #8224 as reviewed in the Staff Report.
