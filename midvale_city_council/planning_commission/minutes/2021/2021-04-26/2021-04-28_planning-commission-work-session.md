DISCUSSION
2 PLANNING COMMISSION WORKSHOP ON LEGISLATIVE UPDATES
Mr. Alex Murphy presented a legislative update from the 2021 Utah Legislative Session to the
Planning Commission.
The presentation highlighted passed bills that require action. These include:
e SB 201 - Public Notice Amendments
e HB 107 - Subdivision Plat Amendments
e HB 82 - Single Family Housing Modifications
e HB 980 - Local Government Building Regulations
e HB 409 — Municipal land County and Use Development Revisions.
Other topics covered included passed bills not requiring response, failed bills, and interim topics.

DISCUSSION:
In regards to required training, Vice Chair Anderson suggested that a joint LUDMA training be
held for all Commissioners at one time, except for new commissioners who would need to have
it done separately, if needed.
Mr. Murphy suggested that trainings be held quarterly.
Commissioner Snow asked about square foot minimum requirements with regards to HB 98.
Mr. Murphy stated that assuming HB 98 passes in the near future, the City would not be able to
regulate the size of a house.
Commissioner Snow also expressed concern over HB 82 and wanted to know how it would
override HOA’s.
Mr. Garrett Wilcox, Midvale City Deputy Attorney stated that HOA’s were created by State
statute, therefore the State can overrule HOA’s by statute.
