NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—May 22nd @ 7pm.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          None

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood highlighted some previous and upcoming City events.

              She talked about Mural Fest and another year of its success and turnout.

              She also talked about the Flood Prep training that was done on April 4th and noted
              that City staff was on alert for warmer weather over the next two weeks.

              May 18th – ‘Stop the Bleed’ training will be hosted by the University of Utah at the
              South Salt Lake Community Center from 10-11 am. Residents can learn how to use a
              tourniquet, stop mass bleeding, and apply life-saving practices. There is no cost, but
              registration is required. More information may be found at: sslc.gov

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          The Council Members spoke about Mural Fest and expressed their appreciation for
          the event and all the people who worked to make it possible.

              Council Member Sanchez read an email that he received from a resident that
              expressed concern over construction in the city and how it is affecting their
              commute.
