3. Review and Discussion of Budget Request Items
   The council discussed various budget requests and priorities for different city departments. The
   Council discussed using caution about committing to new programs without considering long-
   term costs. The Council also discussed strategies to improve law enforcement recruitment,
   community engagement, and employee retention.

   Highlights and Action Items:

       •   It was suggested that the Council discuss plans and funding for Dry Creek Park at an
           upcoming meeting and investigate funding opportunities for joint maintenance
           agreements for Dry Creek Park to reduce the financial burden.
       •   The Council would like a report on unredeemed tree vouchers in the Olympic Park area.
       •   The Council discussed expenditures for storing and moving books from the Library
           during construction of the civic center. Jason Walker, City Administrator, explained that
           the cost to keep the library open during construction was substantial. Moving the Library
           was also a safer option for patrons.
       •   Fees were proposed and discussed for the Literacy Center.
       •   Recreation Center fees, memberships, and a proposal for a larger ice rink were
           discussed.
       •   Councilor Stallings expressed concerns about committing to costly programs without
           considering long-term implications.
       •   Changes were proposed for employee benefits to offset the cost of medical insurance.
           Different options to discussed to help reduce the renewal costs.
       •   The Council discussed the complexity of managing information systems and the need
           to be proactive with protecting data.
       •   Engineering presented and discussed various infrastructure projects including 600 East
           and SR92, and the Dominion gas relocation of pipeline near Family Park.
       •   Discussions are happening with the State to improve pedestrian crossing on Timpanogos
           Hwy, and the city is encouraging a grade separated crossing.
       •   There was discussion on employee promotions, furniture upgrades, and various budget
           allocations.
       •   There was discussion regarding the public works campus addons and remodel. Dean
           Lundell, Finance Director, suggesting bonding the entire project to avoid patchwork and
           save money in the long run because of the high rates of construction inflation and low
           borrowing rates for local government.
