 Action Agenda

 1.        A resolution of the Mayor and the Municipal Council sitting as the Board of Canvassers
           accepting the election returns and declaring and certifying the results of the vote for Primary
           Elections held on Tuesday, August 10, 2021. (21-092) (0:00)

Amanda Ercanbrack, Provo City Recorder, presented the Primary Election canvass report and resolution.
The report indicated the following election results and statistics:

Provo City had 45,441 active registered voters for the Mayor and Council Member City-Wide I positions.
A total of 11,299 ballots were cast, for a voter turnout of 24.87%.

The number of registered voters in Council District 5 was 7,297 with a total of 1,242 ballots cast, for a
voter turnout of 17.02%.

There were 183 mail ballots that were not accepted. Of the ballots that were not accepted, 40 ballots
were returned unsigned, 21 signatures did not match the voter’s record and could not be verified, 1
envelope was returned without a ballot, and 121 ballots were returned after the deadline.

Candidates nominated for office and whose names will appear on the ballot for November 2, 2021, are
as follows:

Chair Sewell opened public comment, there was no response.

Councilor Harding noted that Provo City was under contract with Utah County to conduct this election,
he wanted Ms. Ercanbrack to speak about the process and security of the election. Ms. Ercanbrack
explained the responsibilities of the City and Utah County. The County was responsible for printing,
mailing, and tabulating the ballots. They followed all applicable election laws to ensure a secure
election. She added that Josh Daniels, Utah County Clerk, was willing to address any specific questions
regarding their process or the integrity of the election.

       Motion:            Mayor Kaufusi moved to accept the election returns and declare and certify the results of
                          the vote for Primary Elections held on Tuesday, August 10, 2021. Councilor Harding
                          seconded the motion.

Councilor Harding thanked those who had participated in the election and spoke about the importance
of having good candidates.

       Vote:              The motion was approved 6:0 with Mayor Kaufusi, Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Harding,
                          Sewell, and Shipley in favor. Councilors Handley and Hoban were excused.
Adjournment
