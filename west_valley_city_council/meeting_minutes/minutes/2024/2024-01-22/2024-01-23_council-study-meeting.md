          MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – JANUARY 23, 2024
                                 -1-


THE WEST VALLEY CITY COUNCIL MET IN ELECTRONIC STUDY SESSION ON
TUESDAY, JANUARY 23, 2024 AT 4:30 P.M. AT WEST VALLEY CITY HALL,
MULTIPURPOSE ROOM, 3600 CONSTITUTION BOULEVARD, WEST VALLEY CITY,
UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY MAYOR LANG.

THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:

            Karen Lang, Mayor
            Lars Nordfelt, Councilmember At-Large
            Don Christensen, Councilmember At-Large
            Tom Huynh, Councilmember District 1
            William Whetstone, Councilmember District 3
            Jake Fitisemanu, Councilmember District 4

ABSENT:
            Scott Harmon, Councilmember District 2



STAFF PRESENT:

            John Flores, Interim Assistant City Manager / HR Director
            Nichole Camac, City Recorder (electronically)
            Eric Bunderson, City Attorney
            John Evans, Interim City Manager/ Fire Chief


     APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF STUDY MEETING HELD JANUARY 16, 2024
     The Council considered the Minutes of the Study Meeting held January 16, 2024. There
     were no changes, corrections or deletions.

     Councilmember Huynh moved to approve the Minutes of the Study Meeting held January
     16, 2024. Councilmember Whetstone seconded the motion.

     A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

     REVIEW AGENDA FOR REGULAR CITY COUNCIL MEETING OF JANUARY
     23, 2024
     Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang, members of the Council had no further questions or concerns
     regarding items listed on the Agenda for the Regular City Council meeting scheduled later
     this night.

     MOTION FOR CLOSED SESSION
     Councilmember Nordfelt moved to adjourn and reconvene in a Closed Session for
