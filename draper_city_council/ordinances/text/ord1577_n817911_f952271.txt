City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On March 7, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1577, approving the
second amendment to the Master Development Agreement for EPG Pony Express.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 8th day of March 2023. Laura Oscarson, Draper
City Recorder.
