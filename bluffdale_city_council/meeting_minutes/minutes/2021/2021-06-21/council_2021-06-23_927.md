was to replace the traffic signal with a freeway interchange. The intent was to reduce the number
and severity of crashes, reduce travel time, and improve mobility in all directions. A pedestrian
bridge was planned. Meetings with the school district and community were plaimed to relocate
the bridge. Mr. Allen explained that the plans align with UDOT's vision for improving the quality

A slide showing the draft schedule for the Environmental Study was presented. Mr. Allen
described the different activities and stated that the environmental process is flexible. They were
addressing multiple interchanges at one time. He provided a flyer that addressed how the State
Environmental Study process works .

Mr. Allen reported that they are in the early phase of the project and have met with the City on
utility locations. They have several different interchange configurations to decide on because of
the low volumes on 2700 West. Other interchanges on Bangerter are single-point urban
interchanges. They plan to evaluate the costs, benefits, and environmental impact of shifting
Bangerter Highway to the east or west. Mr. A llen explained that usually a highway is widened to
one side or the other or both. Mr. Allen explained that UDOT does not pump water but relies on
gravity float systems. Mayor Timothy explained it needs to be looked at when they address 13400
South.

Mr. Allen showed a slide with several public outreach coordination effo11s UDOT is pursuing to
involve the public including social media, a website for Bangerter Highway, and public meetings
including neighborhood specific meetings. Dates were to be finalized as the study progresses.
They are meeting with staff monthl y and presenting study milestones to the City Council.
Mr. Allen described the various ways to communicate with the enviro1m1ental project and
construction teams.

Mr. Allen addressed Mayor Timothy's questions regarding Bangerter going "over and under". He
explained that the team will calculate the cost of the traditional approach and the "under" approach.
The City will work with UDOT on design. The intersection will be closed for six to eight months
for the construction of Bangerter to go under 2700 West to help reduce costs and address safety
issues. Mr. Allen explained that past projects have cost $2 to $6 million. The Bange11er 3 Project
included two "under" interchanges and was $40 million over the estimate, which was mainly due

stated that it was discussed previously and additional authority was given to the Fire Chief. Fire
Chief, Wanen James explained that he and Mr. Sheeran met with the State Attorney General and
State Fire Marshall. Utah State Law has restrictions on what can be restricted. There are small
areas of land that were not included in any other restrictions including the south end of
Independence and the park above Spring View Farms. He explained that the City cannot restrict
it any further. He stated that if in the future the City wants more restriction options they should
write to the State Legislature.

In response to a question raised regarding three cities that restricted all fireworks, Chief James
stated that the Salt Lake City Fire Chief indicated that the Mayor reasoned that they can restrict
what they wish because they are the Capital City. South Salt Lake has an ordinance allowing the
Fire Chief to impose restrictions for any fire hazard any time of the year. The Attorney General,
however, has indicated that they are acting outside of their legal purview. Midvale City chose to
enact an ordinance against the advice of their legal counsel. It was noted that Draper will expand
their restrictions as well. On Red Flag Days the City has the ability to restrict at will.

Mayor Timothy reported that the C ity Council gave the Fire Chief authority and felt it was the
right deci sion based on how knowledgeable the Fire Chief is on the issues. Chief James stated that
they will be enforcing their restrictions on July 3rd and 4th. An investigator from the Fire
Department will work each night to help with citations and enforcement. It was reported that the
door-to-door campaign in Bringhurst Station was underway . Firefighters were working on a film
for the City website focusing on fireworks safety.

        the City of Bluffdale, Staff Presenter, Todd Sheeran.

Mr. Sheeran reported that he was asked to make changes to the City's volunteer policy and
discovered that there was none. He looked at what other cities had done and designed a policy
that would work for the City and volunteers. He stressed that this is the first draft and changes
wi ll be needed. It was sent to staff recently and had not yet received much feedback. Mayor
Timothy praised the volunteers and stated that the subject resolution will prevent him from
volunteering for the City. Several Council Members agreed.

Mayor Timothy expressed appreciation to Ms. Garner and all City volunteers. He stated that their
input is valuable.

Ms. Garner stated that she received notification of the proposed resolution earlier in the evening.
There was discussion as to whether Ms. Garner was consulted on the matter. She indicated that
had not been. Ms. Garner asked for clarification on who is considered a volunteer. Council
Member Hales explained that it is anyone who is not paid. Ms. Garner listed people who would
be considered volunteers including the Arts Advisory Board, parents, and teenagers. She gave
examples of projects where she had 65 teens and youth working as volunteers. The Council
determined that they would be considered volunteers. Ms. Graner explained that for the upcoming
production of Annie many are sewing, making props, and organizing events.

There was discussion regarding the roles of volunteers including some who only work for one day
and parents who help with sets or costumes. Ms. Garner explained that she would not volunteer if
it requires fingerprints and interviews. She stated that the ordinance would essentially prohibit
Arts Advisory Board productions. Ms. Garner if it would be difficult to conduct background
checks and collect fingerprints from all of the volunteers. Mayor Timothy agreed with
Ms. Garner's sentiments.

Ms. Garner stated that if the reso lution were to pass, she would be forced to look for work because
it would shut down theater productions. She read a quote regarding the importance of community
theater. Mayor Timothy stated that if the resolution passes he will stop being Mayor.

Council Member Aston agreed with Ms. Garner and stated that the resolution should have been
broader. There was discussion about the reasoning behind the resolution and some of the important
parts of the resolution that could be included later. Issues of liability with volunteers working
around children were addressed. It was agreed that there need to be some provisions in place.
There was discussion about enacting a specific rule for two adults with minor volunteers at all
times. Mayor Timothy suggested having the City Attorney draft something less restrictive than
the current resoluti on.
