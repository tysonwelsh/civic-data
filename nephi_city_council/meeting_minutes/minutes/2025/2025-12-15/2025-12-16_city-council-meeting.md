PRESENTATION TO COUNCIL MEMBER SKIP WORWOOD
      Skip Worwood was honored for his 8 years of service as a Nephi City Council Member. The
      Mayor presented him a challenge coin and a plaque with a hammer engraved with the phrase
      “Hammer Down,” a phrase Skip used often in council meetings when something needed to be
      decided or done. Skip was praised for his dedication, wisdom, and community service. During
      Skip’s 8 years of service, he was involved as the council member liaison on the library board,
      planning and zoning commission. He was also the event and parade coordinator for a time.
      Priorities during his tenure on the council included water rights, code enforcement, and planning
      for the city’s growth. Councilor Worwood served as mayor Pro Tem the last two years of his
      terms.
      Councilor Worwood expressed his pleasure and honor to serve Nephi city.
      Council Members and staff expressed their admiration and gratitude for Skip’s service.

DATA PRIVACY POLICY
       Seth Atkinson presented a draft of the city’s data privacy policy. The privacy policy draft complies
       with state law and mirrors a template that many cities are using. He described other steps
        needed to comply with the state’s requirements: 1) identify areas of non-compliance or
        improvement that can be instituted. The city will need to update certain forms to refer
        customers to the policy. 2) The state also wants local governments to identify areas where the
        city shares, sells, or purchases personal identifiable information. There are no instances where
        the city is engaging in those activities at present. 3) The city will also need to identify where the
        city is engaging in high-risk processing activities. The only area that may meet the criteria is the
        use of Flock cameras for law enforcement purposes.
        Councilor Skip Worwood made a motion to approve the Data Privacy Policy. Councilor Shari
        Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

CONTRACT AMENDMENT FOR BOWEN & COLLINS—NEBO HEIGHTS PROJECT
      Seth Atkinson described the need to amend the contract with Bowen and Collins. The Nebo
      Heights Sewer Lining Project is currently in the design process. Bowen & Collins and Associates
      contracted with Twin D to collect the necessary video inspections of the sewer to assess the
      status of the sewer and to provide the needed information to rehab and line the sewer lines.
      During the video inspection process several obstacles were encountered that had to be
      remedied to complete the video process: removing tree roots and protruding sewer services
      from sewer main line. This does not impact the overall cost of the project, but it does change the
      timeline of when this cost is paid.
      Councilor Jeramie Callaway made a motion to approve the amendment to the Bowen and Collins
      contract. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
