City Council Meeting Minutes April 23, 2024
1. 24-157 City Council Missing Middle Housing Tour of Daybreak
Attachments: Tour Itinerary.pdf
For a complete audio recording of the April 23, 2024 Council Tour, please go to
sandyutah.legistar.com.
On April 23, 2024, The Council, Mayor Zoltanski, Administration, and Staff participated in
a Housing Tour of the Daybreak Master Planned Community. The bus left Sandy City Hall
at 5:00 pm and the first stop of the tour was at the Glass House located at 4563 West
Kestrel Ridge Road, South Jordan, UT 84009.
The Council tour was led by executives with the Larry H. Miller Group: Kelsey Berg, Vice
President of Government Relations and Cameron Jackson, Senior Vice President of
Operations. They provided an overview and background of the Daybreak development and
master planned community. Also joining the tour were members of the South Jordan City
Council: Tamara Zander and Kathie Johnson.
The tour began at the Glass House located at 4563 West Kestrel Ridge Road, South
Jordan, UT 84009. Following the brief meeting at this location, the tour continued on to
the following locations:
1. Location 1: Lake Bridge Drive from Oquirrh Lake Road to Oquirrh Mountain LDS
Temple
Purpose: Participants will see a mix of housing types at single family scale integrated
on the same street.
2. Location 2: Corner of Topview Road and Talquin Lane
Purpose: Integration of single family, stacked condo, paired homes, and paseo (shared
front yard) all at similar scale working together.
3 Location 3: 11102 South Kestrel Rise Road
Purpose: Example of a “transect” from small single_story cottages to multi story
townhomes, to office and 3 story harbor townhomes.
4. Location 4: Daybreak Parkway to Lake Run Road to Split Rock Drive
Purpose: Mix of 3 different styles of townhomes with single family included in one
block. Fine grain mix of architecture and mixing housing types on the block makes
density less noticeable.
5. Location 5: Kestrel Rise Road and Split Rock Drive
Purpose: Modern paired homes
The tour ended at approximately 7:00 pm. The Council, Administration, and department
staff returned to Sandy City Hall at approximately 7:30 pm. The Council unanimously
agreed to adjourn the meeting at approximately 7:30 pm.
