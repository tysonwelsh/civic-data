Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
    Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2) Election of Chair and Vice Chair

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to re-elect Commission Chair Nielsen as Chair
        for 2022. Motion was seconded by Commissioner Jackson.

        The motion passed unanimously.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to elect Commissioner Jackson as Vice Chair for
        2022. Commissioner Newall seconded.

        The motion passed unanimously.


3) Review of Planning Commission meeting schedule for 2022
    Spring Break Work Session will be canceled.
    Thursday October 6th Work Session and October 15th Regular Session will be combined
    on the October 6th Work Session meeting.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis made a motion to ratify the calendar with the exception to
        change the October 6th Work Session and October 13th Regular Session to a hybrid
        session, canceling the October 13th Regular Session. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4) Review and acceptance of the Planning Commission Bylaws
    Kim Struthers and the Commissioners reviewed the By-Laws for the 2022 year.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to accept the By-Laws as written, with the exception
        to renumber the missing section and the consistency with 7.4. Commissioner Everett
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
        Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

5) Introduction of book reading “A Time to Build”
    Brittney Harris introduced a book and the idea for a book club. She introduced the book "A
    Time to Build". Brittney assigned Part one of the book to discuss at the next Work Session.

6) Overview of Planning Commission Teams Channel
    Briefly discussed a Teams channel. Kim stated they will discuss more at the next Work
    Session.

7) Discussion of Micron Area Plan Land Use Section
    Kim Struthers introduced Scott Bishop with DR Horton. They discussed the land use area
    plan and what their future plan is for their development.

8) Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Ellis moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:35 p.m.
