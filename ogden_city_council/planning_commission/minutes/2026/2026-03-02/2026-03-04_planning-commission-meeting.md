. Preliminary Subdivision, the Vibe, 10 townhouse lots, 2546 Adams Ave

Petitioner Mike Vowles proposed a townhouse project with 10 units, addressing fire marshal
concerns about access and suppression systems.

Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson showed slides and said staff recommended
approval to subdivide for individual ownership with some conditions such as a defined site plan
and some minor corrections with utilities access and plat format and verbiage put in place by
legal staff.

. Encroachment Permit, Balcony, 2560 Lincoln Ave

Petitioner Mitch Vance discussed the Wonder block project and adapting the balconies
encroachment in the right of way by extending the sidewalk out and putting landscaping
directly under the balconies.

Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson showed aerial example slides of past balconies
that were too large and expressed support of the applicants’ proposal to mitigate size and
encroachment with added strategic landscaping and having drainage with industry standard
materials supported by city engineering while at the same time adding visual interest.

COMISSIONER SANDAU MOVED TO APPROVE THE ENCROACHMENT PERMIT

6. Change of Nonconforming Use, Reuse for coffee shop with exterior revisions, 810 Canyon Rd
Petitioners Chase Burch and Adam Stevenson proposed converting the project into a coffee
shop with a walk-up window and the parking configuration recommending one ADA stall and
access aisle, taking up a temporary parking space.

Planner Olivia Thomas advised the applicant to use 296-gallon carts instead of a dumpster for
waste management and recommended approval of the coffee shop use with conditions,
including compliance with engineering standards, landscaping, removal of some concrete and
potential future consideration of a walk-up window.

COMISSIONER SOUTHWICK MOVED TO APPROVE THE CHANGE OF NONCONFORMING
