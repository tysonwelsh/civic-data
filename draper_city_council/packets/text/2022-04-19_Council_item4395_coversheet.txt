Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Brien Maxfield
CC:  Date: April 19, 2022
Re:  Comments: In the 1990’s, the city pursued obtaining an easement for the old South Field Ditch Channel through Draper to use as an official storm drainage facility.  This was outlined in the city’s 1993 Storm Drain Master Plan and pursued by the city to obtain easements on several parcels in order to continue to use the ditch when development transitioned its use from agricultural irrigation to storm runoff conveyance.  The old South Ditch ran from the northeastern part of the city, from a diversion on the Draper Canal near Willow Creek, then southward toward Corner Canyon Creek.  This old ditch used to cross the UTA corridor and its alignment would bisect many parcels.  Based on information available to city staff today, it appears the city was not successful in obtaining the entire South Field Ditch Channel alignment and the channel itself became segmented.  Beginning in the early 2000s, there are easement vacations recorded on several parcels.  Those documents indicate the city was vacating the entire alignment as a storm drainage facility.  However, we are finding that the vacation documents were not recorded on every parcel where the city was successful in obtaining the easement.  The city currently does not use the ditch and it is not included on the city’s storm drainage master plan as a current or future facility.  The ditch no longer exists in most areas of its historic alignment.
