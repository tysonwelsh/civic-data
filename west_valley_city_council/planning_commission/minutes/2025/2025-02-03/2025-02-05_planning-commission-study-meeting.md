         MINUTES OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                     STUDY MEETING FEBRUARY 5, 2025

THE PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON WEDNESDAY,
FEBRUARY 5, 2025 AT 3:35 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600 CONSTITUTION
BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED TO ORDER
AND CONDUCTED BY CHAIR MATTHEW LOVATO.
THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:
     Mathew Lovato, Chair
     Darrick Porter, Commissioner
     Harold Woodruff, Commissioner
     Nancy Drozdek, Commissioner
     Rob Durfee, Commissioner

PLANNING DIVISION STAFF PRESENT:
     Steve Pastorik, CED Director
     Brock Anderson, P&Z Senior Planner
     Jody Knapp, P&Z Zoning Administrator
     Steve Lehman, P&Z Planning Manager
     Julie Davis, P&Z Administrative Assistant

LEGAL DEPARTMENT PRESENT:
     Brandon Hill, Assistant City Attorney

PRESENTATIONS:

1) SA-1-2025 Petition by Kristen Floyd with Ability Inclusion Services is requesting minor
   subdivision approval and a plat amendment for lots 25-27 of the Lake Park Subdivision
   No. 1. The subdivision will consolidate 3 existing lots into one lot totaling .62 acres. The
   subdivision is located at 2574 South Redwood Road and is zoned RM. Staff-Steve
   Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov.

   Steve Lehman presented an application from AIS for a property in the Lake Park subdivision,
   which would consolidate three subdivision lots into one large lot .62 acres in size. In
   addition, the public utility easement in the middle of the lots will be vacated and the current
   easement will remain as a right-of-way access, and curb and gutter improvements to be
   added. The subdivision plot will be one. The property is currently vacant. There were no
   questions or concerns raised.

2) SMI-1-2025 Petition by Adis Alagic is requesting minor subdivision approval for the
   Alagic-Mahir Subdivision. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on .46 acres. The
   proposed subdivision is located at 3753 South 4400 West and is zoned R-1-8. Staff-Steve
   Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov.
