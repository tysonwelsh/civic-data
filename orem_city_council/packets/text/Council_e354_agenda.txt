    10.1 RESOLUTION - FY 2025 Independent Financial Audit
         Presenter: Brandon C. Nelson, CPA, Finance Director & Jon Haderlie, Partner with
         Larson & Company, PC

          performed by an independent auditing firm on an annual basis and whereas, the Orem

          Company, PC regarding the City's financial position for the Fiscal Year ended June 30,
