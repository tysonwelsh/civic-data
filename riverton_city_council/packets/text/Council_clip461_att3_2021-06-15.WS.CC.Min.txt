16         Ordinance No. 21-09 – Amending the Stormwater Fees – Ryan Carter, City Attorney
17
18   Ryan Carter explained that he circulated a memorandum on April 20, 2021 and a preliminary
19   draft of the amendments to adjust the stormwater fees. He said that the Jordan River is
20   deemed to be regulated by the Clean Water Act and the City must regulate the stormwater
21   discharge. He said that years ago the City started the Stormwater Utility program. He said
22   there was a uniform rate of measurement called an ERU with a fee attached to that unit.
23
24   Mr. Carter said that the City had been studying what the costs associated with the Stormwater
25   program are as it compares to residential and commercial properties. He said there is also a
26   roadway use component. He said that in studying this further, regular residential use is very
27   low. He said as a general rule, commercial use is going up and residential use is going down.
28   He says this is a much more scientific approach to determining stormwater use.
29
30   Councilmember Stewart MOVED that the City Council adopt Ordinance No. 21-09 –
31   Amending the Stormwater Fees. Councilmember Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor
32   Staggs called for discussion on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and
33   the vote was as follows: Councilmembers Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-
34   Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
