WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 30, 2023

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Snow at 9:06 a.m. on August 30, 2023, in Conference room 301, 111
East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Chairman Adam Snow,
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney
Steven Scott, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle
Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Jayanne Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION ON PROPOSED COUNTY SALES AND USE TAX FOR EMERGENCY
MEDICAL SERVICES.

Commissioner Iverson started the discussion with updates gathered since the last meeting.

There was in-depth dialog about modifying the EMS tax at the legislative level to clearly
define options available to meet needs in particular EMS areas.

Due to concerns with the current bill, the commissioners would like to work cooperatively
with the municipalities and the fire district on a legislative change to the EMS tax. The
EMS tax will not be on the commission agenda next week, so this will not be on the ballot
this year.

Another Work Meeting will be held to continue evaluations on the matter.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
09:46 a.m.

JAY ANNE\LEWIS ADAM SNOW
DEPUTY CLERK CHAIRMAN
