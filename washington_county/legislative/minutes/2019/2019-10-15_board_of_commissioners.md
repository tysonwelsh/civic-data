PRESENTATION: MAKERSPACE: LIBRARY DIRECTOR JOEL TUCKER

Washington County Library Director Joel Tucker explained that a makerspace is a
community-operated workspace where people with common interests, such as computers,
machining, technology, science, digital art, or electronic art can meet, socialize, and
collaborate. Library patrons have access to technology they might not have access to
otherwise. Some examples of the available technology include: 3D printing, laser cutting,
virtual and augmented reality, and various craft projects. Each library branch has different
resources which are available to all County residents.

The Commissioners encouraged citizens to explore the various makerspaces at the different
libraries as well as to bring children to experience the fun in learning.

Mr. Tucker concluded by mentioning that there will be a ‘Virtual Reality Party’ held at the
Hurricane Library branch on Monday, October 21, 2019 at 3:00 PM for anyone who may
be interested.

CONSIDERATION OF PERSONAL PROPERTY PENALTY REDUCTION
REQUEST

Division Manager Adel Murphy explained that Rocky Vista University was eighteen (18)
business days past the statutory due date of May 15, 2019 for payment of personal property
tax. However, they since have sent a letter asking for a reduction of the penalty. The penalty
was $3,987.05 and was paid to the Assessor’s Office on August 5, 2019. The only condition
to be considered is whether the Commission considers there to have been an
extraordinary/unanticipated circumstance which resulted in the overdue payment.

Previous decisions made by the Commission were to reduce penalties to ¥% of one percent
(0.05%) per day the payment was late. In this particular case, the payment was eighteen
(18) days late which would equal a nine percent (9%) penalty. The Commissioners
concluded that the conditions allowing for a penalty waiver were not met in this situation,
therefore, the penalty would not be reduced.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Deny the Personal Property
Penalty Reduction Request for Rocky Vista University as the
Conditions for Reducing the Penalty Were Not Met. Motion seconded
by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Cox, Iverson and Almquist voting aye.
