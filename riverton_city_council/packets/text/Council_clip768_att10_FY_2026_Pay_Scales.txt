The above pay scale has been adjusted from the previous year based on a
benchmark analysis of the surrounding cities.

The Council voted not to take a pay increase for fiscal year 2026.

The Mayor’s salary increase of 9.98% aligns with a city code provision that sets the Mayor's salary at 25% of
the average (midpoint) of Grade 20.

Starting in fiscal year 2027, the Mayor and Council’s salary increases will be the same as City staff.
