   b. Discussion and possible action regarding Aligned Energy – Estoppel Certificate
Senior Management Analyst Jeremy Olsen explained the proposed Aligned Energy Estoppel Certificate.
Responding to a question from Board Member Whitelock, Mr. Olsen explained that the agreement
required Aligned Energy to meet income and employment thresholds to qualify for a full payment.
Aligned Energy had not met employment thresholds in past years and had only qualified for half
payment. He said the agreement did provide payment accommodations for less than full employment.
Chair McConnehey clarified that the Estoppel Certificate did not change the agreement requirements.
