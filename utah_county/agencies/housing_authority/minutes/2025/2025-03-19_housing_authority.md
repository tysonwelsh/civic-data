The Board previously approved partnering with a developer from out of state. The 
development agreement was approved during the February 2025 Board Meeting. HAUC legal 
counsel reviewed the development agreement.  It is presented to the board for approval. Legal

development agreement incorporates these recommendations. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-03-03 Component Unit 2024 Audits & Tax Returns 
Sara made a motion to approve the Component Units 2024 Audits & Tax Returns.  
Suzanne seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
This resolution seeks approval for both the 2024 audit and tax return of Pacific Drive and 
Cedar View. These component units are required to undergo a separate audit, which is 
conducted by a different audit firm. The results from these component unit audits are then 
submitted and incorporated into the HAUC agency audit. Alongside these audits, each 
component unit's 2024 tax return is also prepared.
