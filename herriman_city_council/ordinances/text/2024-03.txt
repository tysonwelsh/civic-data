ORDINANCE NO. 2024-03

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
IMPOSING A NEW PARKING VIOLATION FEE SCHEDULE.

WHEREAS, the City met in a regular session on January 24, 2024 to discuss the
current city parking issues and potential increase of parking violation fines.

WHEREAS, the City continues to experience problems with illegal vehicle
parking throughout the City on public roads.

WHEREAS, the Council believes utilizing an increase to the current fee will deter
illegal parking and enhance vehicle, pedestrian, and the overall safety of the community.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Parking Fee Amendment. The City Council hereby incorporates the
text amendment shown in Exhibit A.

SECTION 2. Fee Schedule. The City Council authorizes the parking fee described
in Section 1 to be added to the City’s Fee Schedule in Title 6-5A-6.

SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon passage.

APPROVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH, ON
THIS 13 DAY OF MARCH 2024

EXHIBIT A

CHAPTER 6-5 STOPPING, STANDING, AND PARKING

ARTICLE A: CIVIL INFRACTION

6-5A-6: Fees

B. At the City’s discretion, parking violations on City streets or roadways shall be
punishable either as a criminal infraction or a municipal code infraction. The Fee for
violating a municipal code parking infraction under Title 6 shall be $100.00, with a
maximum fee of up to $200.00.

6-6 PARKING PERMIT PROGRAM

6-6-18 Unlawful Activities; Penalty

C. It is unlawful and a violation of this chapter for a person holding an area regular
permit-to permit the use or display of such permit on a motor vehicle other than that
for which the permit is issued. Such conduct shall constitute an unlawful act and
violation of this chapter, both by the person holding the valid parking permit and the
person who so uses or displays the permit on a motor vehicle other than that for which
it is issued. Such violation shall be punishable by a fine not to exceed ene two hundred
dollars ($4200.00).
