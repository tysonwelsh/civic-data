            RESOLUTION NO. 2023-02-01                                        FEBRUARY 13, 2023


    A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL EXPRESSING
               APPRECIATION FOR SENATOR KAREN MAYNE

      WHEREAS, since 2008, Senator Mayne has represented the 5th District, which includes Kearns, in
the Utah Senate; and

       WHEREAS, Senator Mayne sponsored S.B. 199, Local Government Revisions, in the 2015
General Session of the Utah Legislature, which allowed Kearns and other previously unincorporated
communities within Salt Lake County to incorporate as metro townships; and

        WHEREAS, following the incorporation of the metro townships, Senator Mayne vigorously
supported and defended the interests of Kearns and its residents; and

       WHEREAS, during the 2020 General Session of the Utah Legislature, Senator Mayne
championed an effort to develop and pass S.B. 214, Gambling Machine and Sweepstakes Amendments,
which addressed the criminal and public nuisance challenges that Kearns and other communities
throughout Utah experienced because of fringe gambling machines; and

      WHEREAS, during the 2021 General Session of the Utah Legislature, Senator Mayne was
instrumental in developing and passing S.B. 58, Metro Township Amendments, which authorized metro
townships like Kearns to enact municipal energy and telecommunications taxes, placing Kearns and the
other metro townships along with their residents on equal footing with other municipalities; and

      WHEREAS, Senator Mayne’s tireless efforts and leadership in passing S.B. 199, S.B. 214, and
S.B. 58 are only three examples of the many actions she has taken to support and assist Kearns.

     NOW, THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, effective immediately that:

        1.    The Kearns Metro Township Council expresses its sincere appreciation, gratitude, and
praise to Senator Mayne for her dedicated and exemplary leadership; recognizes her time, energy,
devotion, and commitment in successfully supporting Kearns and its citizens.

       2.         The Kearns Metro Township Council wishes Senator Mayne a very happy retirement.

     PASSED and ADOPTED this 13th day of February, 2023.
