(Track A, 06:00)
Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson outlined the main purpose of amending the 2018 Budget is to address
some medical billing within the Utah County Sheriff’s Office; additional items include a budget transfer of $40,000
for an incentive program, funding for the upcoming election, and rising fuel and maintenance costs within the Public
Works Department. Commissioner Graves mentioned a request for $500,000 from the Public Works Department, and
suggested the Sheriff’s Office increase be updated from $600,000 to a restricted $1,000,000 to compensate for
expenditures of $400,000 related to the medical bills. He also referenced a newspaper article (found here) and
suggested $1,500,000 be set aside for county employee back-pay once internal systems are capable of issuing those
monies. Commissioner Graves and Mr. Thompson discussed statutory fund balance requirements and the capital
expense account. Darin Durfey of the Utah County Sheriff’s Office supported the request for additional monies to the
Sheriff’s Office, and reviewed the dollar amounts of outstanding bills with Mr. Thompson.

Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson reviewed the 2018 Utah Bounty Budget amendments as discussed
during the Public Hearing earlier on the agenda: $1,000,000 increase to the Utah County Sheriff’s Office as restricted
funds; $40,000 transferred to the Utah County Public Works and Utah County Sheriff’s Offices for the 2018 Trust
and Accountability Program; $650,000 for election expenses; and $500,000 towards Public Works, $400,000 of which
to cover additional fuel costs through year end and the remaining $100,000 for Motor Pool parts. Mr. Thompson
mentioned staying within the parameters of the initial $500,000 estimate for vote-by-mail elections is possible because
the county is likely to receive a grant in the amount of $160,000 for equipment. The commissioners discussed amounts
to be restricted and prioritized projects with Utah County Public Works Director Richard Nielson and Mr. Thompson.
Utah County Human Resources Director Lana Jensen talked about the complications of addressing back-pay through
the WorkDay system and Commissioner Ivie emphasized the Board is actively seeking a solution.

                                          PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 29:25)
Darin Durfey of the Utah County Sheriff’s Office referenced his upcoming retirement to mention this is his last
‘official’ Commission meeting, and read a prepared statement (a copy of which can be found attached to these
minutes). He commented on the retirement of Utah County Sheriff James O. Tracy the week prior, noting the lack of
appearance of Commissioners at his ceremony – with the exception of Greg Graves – seems symbolic of the discord
between the Board and other County Elected Officials. Chief Deputy Durfey thanked the commissioners for the recent
pay increase for county employees, but pointed out most employees are still not earning the same as their counterparts
in comparable cities and counties. He acknowledged the difficult decisions facing the Board and implored them to
make the right choices in providing for their employees to prevent further hardship and long-term complications for
both the workers and all County residents.
