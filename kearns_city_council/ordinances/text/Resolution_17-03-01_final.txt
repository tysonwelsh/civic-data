KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A MODIFIED FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION
PROCESS IN LIEU OF ORDINANCE CHANGE TO ALLOW FOR THE NECESSARY
PROCESSING OF INVOICES AND PAYMENTS OF COSTS FOR THE
METRO TOWNSHIP
RECITALS

A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 ef seg.

B. The Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Kearns pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. On January 5, 2017 the Council adopted Kearns Metro Township Code (“KMTC”) Chapter 3
— REVENUE AND FINANCE pursuant to UCA §10-6-158 ef al.

D. To improve fiscal transparency for Kearns, and to provide for the timely processing of
invoicesfor services provided, the attached process (ATTACHMENT “A”) was created in
lieu of amendments to KMTC Chapter 3 with the understanding this process will be formally
incorporated into the amendments to Chapter 3.

RESOLUTION

BE IT THEREFORE RESOLVED, the Council adopts ATTACHMENT “A” to their fiscal

procedures to allow for the timely processing and payment of the invoices for services of Kearns

in lieu of amendments to KMTC Chapter 3 - REVENUE AND FINANCE,

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Kearns, Salt Lake County, Utah this 2 ta day of March,
2017.

ATTACHMENT “A”
STEPS FOR ‘THE PROCESSING OF INVOICES OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
1. Unless otherwise stated in this process, all other rules regarding purchasing and the
procurement of services shall be regulated by KMTC Chapter 3 - REVENUE AND
FINANCE.
2. Processing of Invoices for Goods and Services

a. All Council budgeted and approved contracts for goods and services shall submit
their invoices for goods and services to the council chair for review and signature
approval.

b. Ifthe council chair finds the invoice to be in order with the contract, and funding
in the budget is available for expenditure, the chair may approve the expenditure
by signing the invoice and presenting it to SLCO/GSLMSD Fiscal Staff for final
processing and payment.

c. Once SLCO/GSLMSD Fiscal Staff has confirmed the findings of the Chair, staff
shall, unless otherwise directed by the Chair, produce and make final payment
directly to the vendor or service provider and give notice of the same to the Chair.
