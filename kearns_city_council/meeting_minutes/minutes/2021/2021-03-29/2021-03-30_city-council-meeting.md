Crime Deterrent Equipment

           Detective Peter Jin, Unified Police Department (UPD) introduced Jason Silva and
Ron Teachman from ShotSpotter.

                Ron Teachman, ShotSpotter, presented a video and presentation on ShotSpotter
Inc. and its gunshot detection technology ShotSpotter Respond. Acoustic sensors are used to
detect, locate, and alert police to nearly all gunshot incidents in less than 60 seconds.

                Mayor Bush asked if the technology could differentiate between gunfire and bottle
rockets or firecrackers.

              Mr. Teachman stated the technology is good at determining the difference
between gunfire and other similar noises.

               Council Member Snow asked what the pricing is.

               Jason Silva, ShotSpotter, stated the cost is $49,500 per square mile.

               Mr. Teachman stated there are typically 20-25 sensors per square mile.

               Detective Jin stated it was determined that Kearns has 4.63 square miles to cover.

               Council Member Snow asked how many shootings there are a month.

               Detective Jin stated there are at least 15 shootings a month and that does not
include shootings that are not called in.

               Council Member Snow stated $200,000 a year is high and questioned how
Kearns will pay for it every year.

                Detective Jin stated after the first year ShotSpotter will help the Township apply
for grants to cover some of the cost.

                Mr. Teachman stated the Council can sign up for a one-year subscription. There
is not obligation to continue the service if it feels the value is not there. The Council can also start
with a smaller footprint to help lower the cost.

               Chief Levi Hughes, Unified Police Department (UPD), stated it costs $120,000
for each police officer to patrol in Kearns. Bringing in two additional officers will cost more than
$200,000. Nobody has been able to get a handle on the shootings in Kearns and he does not
believe there is an accurate number of actual gunfire. There is an opportunity to try something
new and make a difference in the community.

               Council Member Snow asked about maintenance of the sensors.

              Mr. Teachman stated ShotSpotter is responsible for all the maintenance and
replacement of the infrastructure.

               Mayor Bush stated if any of the Council Members have additional questions, they
can reach out to Chief Hughes or Detective Jin.
