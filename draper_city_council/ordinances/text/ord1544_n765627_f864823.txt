City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 21, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1544, vacating a portion
of a Right-of-Way on 700 West. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 27th day of June
