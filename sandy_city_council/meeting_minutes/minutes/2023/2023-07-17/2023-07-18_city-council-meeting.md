Council Member Business

                    Ryan Mecham provided an update from the Sandy Historic Committee and Heritage
                    Festival. The community is very excited for the fall festival.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski represented the City at a recent event with the Governor. She provided
                    an update on the Shelter the Homeless shelter for the medically vulnerable homeless
                    population. She reminded the residents to observe the speed limits in Sandy City and
                    she invited the public to attend the events at the Sandy Amphitheater. She provided
                    updates from recent meetings with the Salt Lake County and the ULCT caucus meeting.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO reminded the Council that July 24th is a holiday - the city offices will
                    be closed. He provided some city updates.

                    Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:04 pm.
