                                           Nephi City
                                     Work Session Minutes
                                         May 25, 2021
                                            7:15 p.m.
                                  Nephi City Council Chambers



   1.    Planning Commission members Ann Peterson, Karl Brough, and Glenn Greenhalgh,
        discussed with the council the blight ordinance and developments that are ongoing.

   2.    The council discussed the Bullwinkle Project and directed staff to come back with a
        resolution of support of a CRA.

   3. The council discussed the feedback the city is receiving from residents and non-
      residents about charging to use the gun range starting in July. The council did not want
      to add to the fee schedule a daily use fee for non-residents and to keep with the annual
      pass fee schedule adopted previously.

   4. The council discussed a piece of property to be used for a park near the Jamestowne
      Subdivision.

   5. The council reviewed a draft of the Justice Court Interlocal Agreement with Juab County.

   6. City Attorney reported on the naming of the field house. The “Hive” was chosen through
      a survey of the players involved in the field house project.

   7. Mr. Atkinson gave a summary of the US Treasury guidance of the American Recovery
      Plan funds.

   8. The councilors gave various updates.

   9. Adjournment at 10:05 p.m.




Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielsen, Councilor Larry Ostler, Councilor Justin Seely, Councilor
