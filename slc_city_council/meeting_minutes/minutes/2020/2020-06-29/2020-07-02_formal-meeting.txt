MINUTES OF THE SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL LIMITED FORMAL MEETING
 THURSDAY, JULY 2 , 2020
 The City Council of Salt Lake City, Utah, met in an electronic Special
 Limited Formal Session on Thursday, July 2, 2020 pursuant to Salt Lake
 City Emergency Proclamation No. 2 of 2020 (2) (b) .
 The following Council Members participated electronically:
 Chris Wharton Analia Valdemoros James Rogers
 Andrew Johnston Daniel Dugan Darin Mano
 The following Councilmember was absent:
 Amy Fowler
 Cindy Gust-Jenson, Council Executive Director; Jennifer Bruno,
 Council Executive Deputy Director; Erin Mendenhall, Mayor; Rachel Otto,
 Mayor' s Chief of Staff; Katherine Lewis, City Attorney; and Scott
 Crandall, Deputy City Recorder participated electronically.
 Councilmember Wharton presided at and conducted the meeting.
 The meeting was called to order at 2 : 08 p.m.
 OPENING CEREMONY
 #1. 2:08:45 PM Welcome and Review of Standards of Conduct.
 NEW BUSINESS
 #1 . 2:10:54 PM Adopting a resolution extending the Mayor' s March
 18 proclamation declaring a local emergency relating to a magnitude 5. 7
 earthquake. The Council's most recent extension of the local emergency
 relating to COVID-19 terminates June 6, 2020 and the proposed extension
 would be until July 10, 2020. View Attachment
 Councilmember Johnston moved and Councilmember Rogers seconded to
 adopt Resolution 20 of 2020, extending the proclamation declaring a local
 emergency relating to a magnitude 5. 7 earthquake until October 9, 2020,
 which motion carried, all members present voted aye (roll call) .
 (R 20-6)
 Councilmember Johnston asked for clarification regarding the
 October 9th date. Ms . Gust-Jenson said this related to opportunities for
 federal benefits and a date was needed for the Council to preserve
 options . She said the date could be changed or withdrawn at the Council' s
 discretion.
 The meeting adjourned at 2 : 15 p .m.
