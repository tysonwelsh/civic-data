The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on September 6, 2022, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance
were Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2989, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A CONSERVATION EASEMENT THAT IS BEING CONVEYED AS PART OF THE
PINE VALLEY PRESERVATION PROJECT

Deputy Attorney Devin Snow explained that preservation and conservation measures are ongoing
in the Pine Valley area and the property owner is ready to grant a conservation easement. The
County will be a joint holder of the conservation easement of the Pine Valley Meadows
Preservation Project along with the Virgin River Land Preservation Association.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2989, A

Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2990, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND THE

STATE OF UTAH DEPARTMENT OF NATURAL RESOURCES, DIVISION OF STATE
PARKS FOR TRT FUNDING TO IMPROVE AND EXPAND A PORTION OF ROAD

INSIDE SAND HOLLOW STATE PARKS

Chairman Iverson explained that road improvements are being made in Sand Hollow State Park to
allow for course modifications for the annual IRONMAN events. Improvements to the Sand
Hollow Road will alleviate traffic congestion in the various areas of the County affected by the
IRONMAN. Tourism Tax Revenue Tax (TRT) will pay for a portion of the project with the
remaining balance being paid for by the State of Utah Facilities Construction and Management

(DFCM).

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2990, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement Between Washington County
and the State of Utah Department of Natural Resources, Division of State
Parks for TRT Funding to Improve and Expand a Portion of Road Inside Sand
Hollow State Parks. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

07/18/2022 @ 1850, Stranded Vehicle — Babylon — Man Hours — 4

SAR and Deputies responded to a stranded vehicle in Lower Babylon. The vehicle was
within feet of the Virgin River. The vehicle was pulled out and the subjects escorted back
to the main roadway. No medical was needed.

07/23/2022 @ 1130, UTV Accident — Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 24

SAR responded to a UTV rollover on Sand Mountain. A 43 year old male rolled the
vehicle and was thrown out. He was in and out of consciousness and had possible internal
injuries. Life Flight was called to respond. SAR and EMS responded up the dunes. Life
Flight arrived on scene and assessed the subject. They loaded him in the helicopter and
flew him to St George Regional hospital with serious injuries.
07/29/2022 @ 1500, UTV Accident — Sand Mountain - Man Hours — 30

SAR responded to a 54 year old male that rolled a UTV. The male was complaining of a

08/04/2022 @ 1100, Ill Subjects - Upper Babylon — Man Hours — 45

SAR responded to three subject complaining of Heat Exhaustion. A 49 year old female
and two males 18 and 15. The subjects had hiked in to get their motorcycle that was
stuck in the sand from the previous day. SAR and EMS went in and located the subjects.
They were all treated for heat exhaustion and brought out to the Incident Command.
SAR also recovered their motorcycle.

08/05/2022 @ 1800, Unknown Suspicious — Sand Hollow Res. — Man Hours — 18

SAR responded to a suspicious circumstance and Sand Hollow State Park. A male subject
said he was swimming at the Jumping Rocks and said he saw what looked like a foot and
possibly a leg in a cove. The SAR Dive Team deployed and located the suspicious object.
It was the bottom sole of a Barefoot water shoe.

08/09/2022 @ 1330, Jeep Accident — Sand Mountain — Man Hours — 42

SAR responded to a Jeep that rolled over on Sand Mountain. It was reported the Jeep
was laying on a 78 year old male and a second person, a 73 year old male, was trapped
inside the vehicle. Life Flight was launched. EMS and SAR went in on UTV’s. SAR
arrived and got both subjects free from the vehicle. Life Flight flew one male to SGRH.
The second male was brought back to the IC in a SAR vehicle. He was taken to SGRH by

ambulance.

08/10/2022 @ 0900, Mountain Bike Accident — Bear-Claw Poppy — Man Hours — 22

SAR was dispatch to a Mountain Bike crash on the Bear-Claw Poppy Trail. A 25 year old
female wrecked and went unconscious. She regained consciousness, but had an altered
mental status. SAR located her and stabilized her in a UTV. She was brought back to the
IC and turned over to EMS. She was taken to SGRH by ambulance.
