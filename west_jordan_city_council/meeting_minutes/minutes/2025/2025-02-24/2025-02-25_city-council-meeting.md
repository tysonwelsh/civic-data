 3. SPECIAL RECOGNITION
       a. Recognizing Hope Bevilhymer
          Mayor Burton introduced Hope Bevilhymer, West Jordan native and goalie for the U.S.
          Women’s Development Sled Hockey Team. The U.S. Women’s Development Sled Hockey
          Team won gold for the third consecutive year at the 2024 Women’s World Challenge.
          Mayor Burton recognized Ms. Bevilhymer for her work off the rink with the Limbs of Hope
          Foundation. Ms. Bevilhymer was presented with a West Jordan pin and certificate of
          recognition. The Council took the opportunity for photographs.

       b. Recognizing Kathy Saupe
          Rob Durfee, Vice President of the Utah Chapter of the International Code Council (ICC),
          recognized Kathy Saupe, Senior Plans Examiner/Building and Safety for nine years of
          service on the ICC Executive Board. Mr. Durfee said Ms. Saupe was a mentor to everyone
          on the Board and spoke of her many contributions. Ms. Saupe said her next goal was to
          become a Certified Building Official, which Mr. Durfee said would be of huge value to West
          Jordan. Mr. Durfee showed a video highlighting moments of a Field of Dreams event.

 5. PUBLIC COMMENT
     Chair Lamb opened the public comment period at 7:20 pm.
     Comments:

 6. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Ordinance No. 25-03 approving the Oquirrh Highlands Annexation Petition for an
          Annexation Area of approximately 160.501 acres at approximately 5900 West to
          6400 West and 6600 South to 7000 South
          City Recorder Tangee Sloan presented a formal request for Council approval of Ordinance
          No. 25-03, to formalize annexation of the Oquirrh Highlands area into the City. She
          outlined the timeline of events that led to the request, and reported all statutory
          requirements had been met and no formal protests filed. Approval of the proposed
          ordinance would allow staff to move forward with the final legal filings prior to issuance
          of an annexation certificate. Ms. Sloan stated all steps must be completed by April 30,
          2025.

           Responding to a question from Council Member Jacob, Ms. Sloan said approval of
           Ordinance No. 25-03 represented the final Council action in the process. Vice Chair
           Whitelock commented that Attachment A in the packet differed from the map shown on
           the screen and asked which the Council would be approving. Ms. Sloan confirmed the
           Council would be approving the map shown on the screen. Responding to a question from
           Chair Lamb, City Administrator Korban Lee said garbage collection in the subject area
           would continue to be performed by Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District.

           Chair Lamb opened a public hearing at 7:31 pm
           Comments:
           Nicole Combs, petitioner for the annexation, said the process had been an example of how
           things could be run efficiently. She said the process had been an education for her and her

       b. Ordinance No. 25-04 Oquirrh Highlands Annexation (160.5 acres) approximately
          5900 W to 6400 W and 6600 S to 7000 S, Future Land Use Map designation of Medium
          Density Residential, High Density Residential, and Parks and Open Land and a Zoning
          map designation of P-C Zone (Planned Community)
          City Planner/Zoning Administrator Larry Gardner presented the proposed Future Land
          Use Map (FLUM) and Zoning Map designations for the annexation area. He said the P-C
          Zone was proposed because Oquirrh Highlands was developed as a Salt Lake County
          planned community, and West Jordan neighborhoods to the east, south, and west were in
          the P-C Zone. Mr. Gardner recommended the Council adopt Ordinance No. 25-04.

           Council Member Green commented that the annexation area would not meet West Jordan
           P-C Zone open space requirements. Mr. Gardner agreed, and said the area would be
           known as nonconforming reminding of the Council's discretion in legislative matters.

       a. City Council Reports

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson demonstrated options available with the Swagit

       c. Mayor’s Report
