LICENCE        FOR   PIPELINE    AGREEMENT        FOR    A    NONEXCLUSIVE         WIRELESS
TELECOMMUNICATIONS INFRASTRUCTURE FRANCHISE BETWEEN TELEPORT
COMMUNICATIONS AMERICA LLC AND WHITE CITY


                                         RECITALS


A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
   Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.


B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
   for White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. Teleport Communications America, LLC (the “COMPANY’) is a Delaware limited liability
   company that conducts business under Utah Law.

D. The Company has contacted White City to seek a nonexclusive wireless telecommunications
   infrastructure franchise license agreement (the “AGREEMENT”) from White City.

E. The agreement would allow the Company to install wireless telecommunications pipelines in
   White City to improve the efficiency of wireless telecommunications for the users of such
   services.

F. Improved wireless telecommunications services are generally desired by the residents of
   White City.

G. Entering into this agreement is to the mutual benefit of the Company and White City.
                                         RESOLUTION

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council does
hereby approve the attached Agreement, hereinafter known as Attachment A to include its
exhibits, between Teleport Communications, LLC and White City for the work referenced in the
Agreement attached hereto, marked Attachment "A" including attachments thereto, and
authorizes the Mayor to sign the Agreement for White City.
