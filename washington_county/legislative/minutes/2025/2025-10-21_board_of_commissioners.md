CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3610, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LEASE OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY PIONEER COURTHOUSE TO UNITED
WE PLEDGE

Commissioner Iverson stated recently St. George C ity deeded the historic Pioneer Courthouse to
Washington County. This agreement would allow the non-profit United We Pledge to take on the
day to day management of the courthouse. CEO of United We Pledge Dennis Leavitt explained
that United We Pledge will care for the building in a way the public can enjoy it.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3610, A
Resolution Approving a Lease of the Washington County Pioneer Courthouse
to United We Pledge. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Snow voting aye.
