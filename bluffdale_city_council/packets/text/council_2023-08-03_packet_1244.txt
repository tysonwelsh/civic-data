Notice is hereby given that the Bluffdale City Council will hold a meeting on Thursday, August 3, 2023, at Bluffdale City Hall,
2222 West 14400 South, Bluffdale, Utah. The meeting will begin at 6:00 PM or as soon thereafter as possible. This meeting
will also be broadcast live to the public at: www.bluffdale.com. The public may comment at the meeting or by emailing
comments to councilmeetingcomment@bluffdale.com by 4:00 PM the day of the meeting. Emailed comments will be
submitted to the City Council but will not be read at the meeting. Notice is further given that access to this meeting by the
City Council may be by electronic means. Comments should be limited to not more than three (3) minutes, unless additional
time is authorized by the Chair. Groups wishing to comment will be asked to appoint a spokesperson. Items brought forward
to the attention of the City Council will be turned over to staff to provide a response outside of the City Council meeting.
In the event the meeting is disrupted in any way that the City in its sole discretion deems inappropriate, the City reserves the
right to immediately remove the individual(s) from the meeting and, if needed, end virtual access to the meeting. Reasons
for removing an individual or ending virtual access to the meeting include but are not limited to the posting of offensive
pictures, remarks, or making offensive statements, disrespectful statements or actions, and any other action deemed
inappropriate.

                                    ORDINANCE NO. 2023-11

AN ORDINANCE OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL TO LEVY A PUBLIC
SAFETY SERVICE PROPERTY TAX IN SALT LAKE COUNTY

         WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-133 requires the governing body of each City to
set the real and personal property tax levy for various municipal purposes before June 22, or
September 1 in the case of a property tax rate increase under Sections 59-2-919 through 59-2-923;

         WHEREAS, as required by Utah Code Ann. Section 59-2-913 prior to June 22 the City
provided to the Salt Lake County Auditor a statement containing the amount and purpose of each
levy fixed by the Bluffdale City Council (the “Council”);

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-133.4 states that a City may account separately
for the revenues derived from a property tax that is lawfully levied for a specific purpose in
accordance with that section;

        WHEREAS, a city council is required to indicate through ordinance that the City levies the
tax under Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-133.4 and must indicate the specific service for which the
City levies the tax;

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-133.4 further requires a City to create a special
revenue fund to hold the revenues collected under that section and that the City may only expend
revenues from that special revenue fund for a purpose that is solely related to the provision of the
service for which the fund is established;

        WHEREAS, on June 14, 2023, the Council adopted a proposed tax rate of .000260 in Salt
Lake County for public safety services and adopted the tentative budget as the interim budget, which
established and included the Fire and Police Protection Fund which is a public safety services special
revenue fund to hold revenues collected through the public safety services levy;

        WHEREAS, the City Council held a Truth in Taxation public on August 3, 2023, to provide
any interested party the ability to come and provide oral testimony to the Council regarding the
proposed tax rate;

        WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the important and lifesaving work provided by the
public safety personnel of the City of Bluffdale;

        WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the importance of a dedicated funding source to
provide competitive wages to the City’s public safety personnel in order to ensure that the City is
able to employ highly qualified and competent public safety personnel;

         WHEREAS, the Council met in regular session on June 14, 2023, to consider, among other
things, levying a tax for public safety services, adopting a final tax rate of .000260 for that public
safety service levy, and adopting the final budget establishing and including the Fire and Police
Protection Fund to hold revenues collected through the public safety service levy;




                                                                                               Page 1 of 3
        WHEREAS, the Council finds it is necessary and in the best interests of the City of
Bluffdale to levy a property tax for public safety services; and

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Council of the City of Bluffdale as
follows:

SECTION I: The City of Bluffdale levies a property tax pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-
133.4 for public safety services. The rate of the public safety service property tax shall be .000260.
All revenues collected through the public safety services levy shall be deposited and held in the
Public Safety Service Special Revenue Fund Budget adopted as part of the final budget for the fiscal
year July 1, 2023, to June 30, 2024.

SECTION II: A copy of this ordinance shall be delivered to the Salt Lake County Auditor.

SECTION III: Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of this
ordinance is, for any reason, held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction,
such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision, and such holding
shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this ordinance.

SECTION IV: Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon Mayor’s signature and
publication, or after fifteen days of transmission to the office of the Mayor, if neither approved nor
disapproved by the Mayor, and thereafter, publication.

                                   RESOLUTION NO. 2023-38

A RESOLUTION OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL TO ADOPT THE FINAL
FIRE AND POLICE PROTECTION FUND BUDGET FOR THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE
FISCAL YEAR JULY 1, 2023, THROUGH JUNE 30, 2024

        WHEREAS, on April 26, 2023, the City Manager of the City of Bluffdale (the “City”)
submitted to the Bluffdale City Council (the “Council”) a tentative budget (the “Tentative Budget”)
including the Tentative Fire and Police Protection Fund budget, which was prepared and made
available to the public in accordance with state law;

        WHEREAS, on April 26, 2023, the Council approved the Tentative Budget, including the
Tentative Fire and Police Protection Fund budget, which was officially placed in the City Recorder’s
office for inspection by the general public; and

        WHEREAS, a duly noticed public hearing on the adoption of the budget for Fiscal Year July
1, 2023, to June 30, 2024, was held on June 14, 2023, as prescribed by The Uniform Fiscal
Procedures Act for Utah Cities;

        WHEREAS, the Council met in regular session on June 14, 2023, and adopted the Tentative
Fire and Police Protection Fund budget as the Interim Fire and Police Protection Fund budget for
the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2023, and ending June 30, 2024;

         WHEREAS, due to the City’s proposed adoption of a final tax rate that exceeds the certified
tax rate, the City held a truth in taxation public hearing on August 3, 2023, as required by law;

         WHEREAS, on August 3, 2023, the City Council met in regular session and adopted a final
tax rate for the Fire and Police Protection Fund of .000260, which funds shall be deposited and held
in the Fire and Police Protection Fund adopted by this resolution.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City of Bluffdale as
follows:

SECTION I: The Fire and Police Protection Fund Budget attached hereto, incorporated herein by
this reference, is hereby appropriated for the corporate purposes and objects of the City for the
fiscal year commencing July 1, 2023, and ending June 30, 2024, and is hereby adopted as the Budget
for the Fire and Police Protection Fund of the City of Bluffdale, Utah for the Fiscal Year 2023-
2024” (the “Budget”).

SECTION II: Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. 10-6-118, a copy of the Budget for each fund within
the Budget, including the Fire and Police Protection Fund Budget, shall be certified by the Budget
Officer and it is hereby directed that the Budget be filed with the State Auditor within 30 days after
adoption and with the Office of the City Recorder and shall be available to the public in accordance
with the law.

SECTION III: Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of this
ordinance is, for any reason, held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction,

disapproved by the Mayor, and thereafter, publication.
