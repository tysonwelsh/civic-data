 VARIANCES
 B-1-2020                     DAVID BAKER is requesting variances from Section 7-11-204, 7-11-208, 7-11-203(1) and 7-
                              11-212(Table 1) of the West Valley City Code. These sections require that for new homes, the
                              minimum roof pitch be 6:12, a 2-car garage be constructed with the home, the home must have
                              a minimum 25% brick or stone and 250 architectural points be achieved respectively. The
                              applicant is requesting a variance for the roof pitch to 4:12, that he be allowed to keep an
                              existing single car garage, that he be allowed to use less than 25% brick or stone and that he be
                              allowed to build a new home with less than the required 250 points. The property is located at
                              1515 West Russett Avenue and is zoned R-1-6 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum
                              lot size 6,000 square feet). (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311)

 B-2-2020                     DRH DEVELOPMENT, representing the property owner is requesting a variance from
                              Section 7-6-302 of the West Valley City Code. This section requires that the minimum front
                              setback adjacent to a street in the C-2 (General Commercial) zone be 20 feet. The applicant is
                              seeking a variance of 10 feet in order to construct an awning on the front of a proposed new
                              building. The property is located at 3257 West 3500 South and is zoned C-2(General
                              Commercial). (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311)
