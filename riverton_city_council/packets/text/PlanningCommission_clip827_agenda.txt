                                                Public Comment Procedure
This is an open meeting, which the public may attend. Prior to the meeting, the staff reports will be available for public review
through the City website. Audio and video of the meeting will be broadcast live. Following the meeting, audio and written
minutes will be available. Public comments may be submitted via email to planning@rivertonutah.gov or by phone to 801-208-
3138. All comments submitted before 5:00 pm on the date of the hearing will be relayed to the Planning Commission.

Riverton City Meeting information: https://www.rivertonutah.gov/meetings/index.php. This link will take you to the applicable
meeting date and to the meeting packet, where you can review all items scheduled for review by the Planning Commission.
