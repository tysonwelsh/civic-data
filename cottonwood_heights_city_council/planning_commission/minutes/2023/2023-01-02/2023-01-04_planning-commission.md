33   Community and Economic Development Director, Michael Johnson presented the Staff Report
34   and stated that the request is for an existing use or structure violation deemed to be legal non-
35   conforming at 7128 South 2985 East in the R-1-8 Single-Family Zone. The request involves taking
36   a structure use that is illegal or not approved under Code and having it deemed to be legal non-
37   conforming. The Staff recommendation pertains to both existing structures although the applicant
38   only submitted an application for one of the structures. A Notice of Violation was issued by Code
39   Enforcement in November 2022. As a result, the applicant found a provision in the Ordinance and
40   made the application based on the Notice of Violation as a potential remedy. Staff recommended
41   temporary approval.
42
43   Mr. Johnson described the Ordinance provision located in the Non-Conforming Structure section
44   of the City Code. The three main criteria were as follows:
45

 1          1.      The use exists on the property and has been in continuous violation for at least five
 2                  years.
 3
 4          2.      No complaint has been made to the Department or the City regarding the violation
 5                  for at least five years.
 6
 7          3.      Continuation of the use of the structure will not have a detrimental effect on the
 8                  health, safety, or welfare of persons or property in the vicinity.
 9
10   The Planning Commission may set conditions deemed necessary for the protection of adjacent
11   properties or the public welfare including provisions limiting the period of time the use may
12   continue. Commission decisions may be appealed to the Appeals Hearing Officer.
13
14   Mr. Johnson reported that the height of the existing structure complies with accessory building
15   standards, however, it is located in the side yard 26 inches from the side property line. The
16   Ordinance requires buildings be located in the rear yard of the home and separated by at least six
17   feet from the primary structure and be at least three feet from property lines. The applicant’s
18   narrative states that the structure is already considered a legal non-conforming structure per the
19   State Code definition. He reported that no evidence has been provided to the City to demonstrate
20   that claim or that the structures are property fire rated.
21
22   Code Enforcement is done primarily on a reported or complaint basis. Once the City is made
23   aware, an inspection will be conducted to determine if there is a violation. In this case, the
24   violation was noticed by a Code Enforcement Officer who was dealing with another issue related
25   to the property. He emphasized that the City has standards in place and exceptions should be very
26   difficult to obtain, and not simply because a violation has not been reported within the five-year
27   timeframe.
28
29   Staff recommended temporary approval of the structures as non-conforming for a time period not
30   to exceed six months with the findings and conditions listed in the Staff report. Mr. Johnson
31   remarked that at the end of the temporary approval period, both structures should become
32   compliant with the current accessory structure regulations. If the structures are allowed to remain
33   as-is, they represent a potential risk to the health, safety, or welfare of persons or property in the
34   vicinity, which is the reason Staff did not recommend they be allowed to remain in perpetuity.
35
36   Commissioner Anderson agreed with the enforcement of violations and understood that this type
37   of issue is only before the City due to the applicant’s submittal. Mr. Johnson explained that the
38   matter is before the Commission as a result of the application being submitted based on the notice
39   of violation received from the City.
40
41   Commissioner Mills expressed appreciation for the City’s Code Enforcement. He commented that
42   if the Code is not enforced, they will have created a situation where sheds can be placed anywhere.
43   Safety issues were discussed. Commissioner Mills stressed the need to be proactive should they
44   wish to see these types of issues trend positively.
