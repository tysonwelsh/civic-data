MEMO
To: City Council
From: Spencer DuShane
Date: 2025-10-07
Re: Resolution #25-54


Comments:

Citizens of Draper in the neighborhood where Stampede Drive is located have petitioned
the city to rename the street in honor of Bailey Quinn Mallard, a Draper citizen and child
in the neighborhood who was taken from this earth too soon. The requested new name
would be Bailey Quinn Lane. The proposal complies with Salt Lake County Ordinance
2.49.090 which establishes a process for renaming streets.
