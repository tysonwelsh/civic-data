                            RIVERTON REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                                 RDA RESOLUTION NO. 21-05


A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING DEVELOPMENT TO PROCEED ON PROPERTY
OWNED BY SUBURBAN LAND RESERVE WITHOUT COMPLETING A ROADWAY
          CONNECTION TO SUNDAY DRIVE, RIVERTON, UTAH.


      WHEREAS, Riverton City (hereinafter “City”) has entered into an agreement with the
Utah Department of Transportation (hereinafter “UDOT”) to transfer property from the City to
UDOT in consideration of development services provided to the City by UDOT; and

      WHEREAS, the Utah Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City, Utah (hereinafter
“Agency”) desires to facilitate said transfer of property as noted above; and

        WHEREAS, the Agency possesses title to property located within City boundaries
specifically held to facilitate development in Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, the Agency desires to transfer title of property to City by Quit Claim Deed
and City wishes to accept title to said property, for valuable consideration, the sufficiency of
which is stipulated to by both the City and the Agency; and

       WHEREAS, the Agency board has carefully considered whether the transfer by Quit
Claim Deed of said property promotes the development of City after receiving information from
representatives of City; and

         WHEREAS, the Agency board finds and determines that the transfer of property from
Agency to City by Quit Claim Deed does promote development with the boundaries of City and
is in the public’s interest;

      NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of the Utah
Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City, Utah as follows:


   1. The transfer of title as set forth in the Governing Body of the Utah Redevelopment
      Agency of Riverton City, Utah grants its consent and authorizes the transfer of the
      property as described in the attached proposed Quit Claim Deed from the Agency to the
      City.

   2. This Resolution shall be construed to require the City upon receipt of title to the above
      noted property to transfer title to said property to UDOT in a reasonable time frame.
