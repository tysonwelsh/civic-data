AIRPORT GRANT ENTITLEMENT TRANSFER APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reported that the Nephi Municipal Airport is mainly funded through grants from the
Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). A certain amount is dedicated to the Nephi Airport each year
called the airport’s “entitlement”. This amount is generally not enough to accomplish some of the
projects needed at the airport. In order to do the main projects needed by airports, the FAA uses the
entitlements from other airports to fund a project and then, uses future entitlements from that airport
to fund projects at other airports.
   Last year the Nephi Municipal Airport had an apron expansion and a runway/taxiway/apron
rehabilitation. Entitlement funds for the Nephi Airport as well as other airports were used to fund these
projects. Now the FAA is requesting that $150,000 of entitlement funds dedicated for the Nephi
Municipal Airport be transferred to the Logan-Cache Airport.
  Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the airport entitlement transfer
agreement. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 06-07-2022 ID-3 LIGHT INDUSTRIAL ZONE ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed the proposed ID-3 Zone ordinance. The height requirement had been a big
discussion issue and the proposed ordinance height requirement is 62 feet. Councilor Memmott asked
about the 25% “park-like” landscaping requirement. He wanted to make sure that was not requiring
and forcing businesses to put in lawn. Mr. Atkinson said that “park-like” landscaping could mean any
type of plants or greenery (i.e. trees, shrubs) and didn’t necessarily mean they needed to put in lawn.
   Councilor Parady moved to adopt Ordinance 06-07-2022: AN ORDINANCE OF NEPHI CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ADDING TITLE 10 CHAPTER 9 ARTICLE C OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE. Councilor Ostler seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

RODERICK NEPHI BUSINESS PARK ANNEXATION AGREEMENT APPROVED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Roderick Nephi Business Park
Annexation Agreement. The agreement was with property owner Sandra Worthington. Councilor
Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 06-07-2022-A RODERICK NEPHI BUSINESS PARK ANNEXATION ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson presented Ordinance 06-07-2022-A that would annex approximately 44.92 acres located
southwest of Elevator Road and Main Street, Juab County, parcel no. XB2592-1112. The annexation
property would be zoned as ID-3 and is owned by Sandra Worthington. Councilor Memmott moved to
adopt Ordinance 6-07-2022-A contingent on the owner of the property signing the annexation
agreement. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 06-07-2022-B AMENDMENT TO SECTION 3-1-4 OF THE BUSINESS AND LICENSE
REGULATION TITLE ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council an earlier discussion where the council wanted the approval
of business license applications to be done administratively instead of legislatively. Ordinance 06-07-
2022-B changed the Nephi City Code section 3.1.4 to allow for this. Councilor Parady moved to adopt
Ordinance 06-07-2022-B. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

WORK SESSION CONVENED AT 9:10 P.M.

  1.   Golf Director Mike Booth discussed with the council constructing a practice area and parking lot
       on the north side of the golf course. He stated that the cost would be around $25,000 and that
       he had enough funds in his capital improvement budget to complete the project. The council
       directed Mr. Booth to move forward with the project.

  2.    Mr. Atkinson informed the council that during the recent Utah State legislative session, Utah
       Code Title 49 was amended to require employers to provide benefit protection coverage for both
       their Tier 1 Public Safety Service Employees and their Tier 2 Public Safety Service Employees. This
       protects the employee who may be on long-term disability or receiving workers compensation
       benefits to still receive retirement service credit or 401(k) contributions.

  3. Recreation Director Craig Oswald explained to the council about the shortage of lifeguards this
     summer. He requested that the rate of pay be increased to attract more lifeguards. The council
     agreed to an increased rate of pay for lifeguards.

  4. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the Jenson Refrigeration impact fee agreement needed
     some revisions that were going to be made and initialed by both parties. A new agreement
     would not be drawn up.

  5. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the price of natural gas had increased significantly and
     that some tough decision would need to be made before October when the contract of the
     current prices would be ending.

  6.   Meeting adjourned at 10:00 p.m.
