17-2-8 “H” definitions.
“Historic district” means a geographically definable area possessing a significant concentration,
linkage, or continuity of buildings, structures, sites, or objects united by past events, plan or
physical development. A “historic district” may comprise an individual site or individual
elements separated geographically but linked by association, plan, design, or history.
“Home-based microschool” means an individual or association of individuals that registers as a
business entity in accordance with state and local laws and provides kindergarten through
grade 12 education services for compensation to 16 or fewer students from an individual's
dwelling unit, accessory dwelling unit, or residential property. A home-based microschool does
not include a child care center or a child care facility.
“Home occupation” means a business carried on entirely within a dwelling by persons residing
within the dwelling, which business is clearly incidental and secondary to the use of the
dwelling for residential purposes,

“Micro-education entity” means a person or association of persons that registers as a business

not include a child care center, child care facility, home-based microschool, private school, or a

“Minor public utilities” means local utility structures that are necessary for a specific

17-6-6 Home-based microschool standards.

A home-based microschool shall meet the following requirements:

A. Conducted from a dwelling unit or accessory dwelling unit.

B. Maximum of 16 students. This includes the licensee's and any employees’ children if they
are a student of the home-based microschool.

C. Minimum of 35 net square feet per student.

D. A traffic plan must be reviewed and approved by the City Engineer. The traffic plan must
include: traffic flow, drop off, turn-around areas, and no more than 32 vehicular stops per
day, excluding regular traffic generated by residents of the dwelling.

E. Shall not include identifying signage in excess of a two square foot name plate attached to
the building.

F. Shall be enclosed within a structure in complete conformity with current building, fire,
electrical, and plumbing codes.

G. Required parking.

a. 1 space for each staff. If a staff member resides within the home-based microschool,
no additional parking space shall be required for the staff member

b. 1 visitor parking space.

c. 1 on-site drop off area space.

17-6-7 Micro-education entity standards.

A micro-education entity shall meet the following requirements:

A. Newly constructed micro-education entities shall comply with the development standards
of the zone in which they are located. For the zones listed below, micro-education entities
shall comply with the development standards in the following sections:

a. Transit-Oriented Development: Section 17-7-8.4
b. Riverwalk: 17-7-9.12.1.5

c. The Junction at Midvale: 17-7-9.12.2.5

d. Silver Refinery Overlay: 17-7-9.12.3.6

B. The number of students that a micro-education entity may have in a facility is determined
by the facility's occupancy classification and occupant capacity under the state construction
codes, as incorporated by Section 15A-2-103. Notwithstanding the facility's occupant
capacity, a micro-education entity may not have more than 100 students.

C. Minimum of 20 net square feet per student.

D. Access must be provided and restricted to a minor arterial or larger as designated by the
City’s Master Transportation Plan.

E. A traffic plan must be reviewed and approved by the City Engineer. The traffic plan must
include: traffic flow, drop off, turn-around areas.
