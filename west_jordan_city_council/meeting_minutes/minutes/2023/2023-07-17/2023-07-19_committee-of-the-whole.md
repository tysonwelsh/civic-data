 1. CALL TO ORDER
     COUNCIL: Chair Chris McConnehey, Vice Chair Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, David Pack,
               Kayleen Whitelock, Melissa Worthen
     STAFF:    Assistant City Attorney Chase Ames, Council Office Director Alan Anderson, Mayor Dirk

Chair McConnehey called the Committee of the Whole to order at 6:00 pm and noted that all were
present except Council Member Pack, who would join soon.

Chair McConnehey was aware there may be citizens in attendance hoping to address the Council.

At 6:01 pm, Council Member Whitelock made a motion to allow public comment at the
beginning of the meeting. Vice Chair Bloom seconded the motion, which passed by unanimous
vote (6-0).

Comments:
Laurie Taylor, West Jordan resident, said she was concerned about a Kum & Go project approved by
the West Jordan Planning Commission, and asked that Council Members treat the situation as if their
own homes were involved. Having a gas station behind her home was concerning to her and she
asked that the Council consider requiring a higher masonry wall as a buffer to existing residential.
Ms. Taylor asked what would happen if Kum & Go were to go out of business.

Jeremy Draford, West Jordan resident, was concerned about safety in relation to the Kum & Go
project. He expressed concern that the site would become a place where students would congregate
during the school day. Mr. Draford agreed with Ms. Taylor’s concerns regarding what would happen
if the Kum & Go were to go out of business. He pointed out that there were already several gas
stations within a mile radius of the proposed site. He mentioned lighting, crime, and traffic as
concerns.

Council Member Pack joined online at 6:06 pm

Chair McConnehey closed public comments at 6:11 pm

       b. Introduction of a development at 6170 West 7800 South (previously proposed as
          Bowman’s Arrow)
          Mayor Burton introduced Kyle Brostrom with Strategy & Business Development. Mr.
          Brostrom said Strategy & Business Development purchased property at 6170 West 7800
          South (86 acres) with a desire to develop a healthcare campus (approximately half of the
          acreage) with some retail and residential. Mr. Brostrom did not yet have a definitive
          timeline, but the goal would be to begin with construction of a medical office and
          outpatient facilities, followed by a hospital, probably in the next three or four years.
          Responding to a question from Council Member Whitelock, Mr. Brostrom did not know
          the anticipated water use of the project. He stated that the healthcare campus would be
          on the southern portion of the property. Mr. Brostrom noted that Strategy & Business
          Development was not interested in master planning all 86 acres. He said there would be
          interest in selling the remaining acreage if an interested buyer came forward. Council
          Member Jacob commented that the school district would need a new high school in the
          area and suggested the subject location as a possibility.

          Council Member Green believed a hospital would be a less intense use with less impact
          on the neighborhood than previous proposals for the property. He looked forward to
          seeing the project move forward. Council Member Worthen believed the proposed project
          would be a great addition to the community.

          Chair McConnehey suggested reordering the agenda to take item (d) regarding Kearns
          Improvement District before (c). There was objection from the Council.

       d. Kearns Improvement District Emergency Connection Agreement
          Capital Projects Manager David Murphy presented an Emergency Connection Agreement
          with Kearns Improvement District for emergency planning preparedness for mutual aid
          during a disaster affecting the water system. He said the use connections would be in the
          7000 South 5600 West area, and would only be used in times of emergency. The Kearns
          Improvement District would build one connection, and West Jordan City would build the
          other connection.

          Council Member Whitelock asked how potential future annexation would affect the

        c. Discussion on Proposed Amendments to Business License Code (Title 4 Chapter 1)
           Council Office Director Alan Anderson said an ad hoc committee (Council Members Green,
           Whitelock, and Bloom) met three times with representatives of the Legal Department to
           discuss proposed amendments to the Business License Code. Council Members Whitelock
           and Green thanked Mr. Chandler and Mr. Ames from the Legal Department. Council
           Member Green said the Business License Code was not yet where he wanted it however,
           it was about 80%, which he believed was a good starting place. Vice Chair Bloom believed
           the proposed amendments represented a great move in the right direction. Council
           Members Worthen and McConnehey expressed appreciation to the committee and
           everyone who worked on the proposed amendments.

           Mr. Anderson said the proposed amendments could be brought back for final review and
           acceptance on August 9, 2023.
