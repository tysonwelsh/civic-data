3.5 Update on Salt Lake County ’s Coordinated Response to 
COVID-19
21-0911
Attachments: Staff Report
County Council Briefing -- 08102021
Presenters: Dr. Angela Dunn, Executive Director, Health Department and 
COVID David Schuld, COVID Coordinating Officer (Approx. 30 mins). 
Informational
Dr. Angela Dunn , Director, Salt Lake County Health Department, stated it 
was her goal to give the Council the best data, and health and science 
recommendations.  The latest focus has been on school, and the goal is to 
have kids get in -person learning with as little disruption as possible.  From a 
public health perspective, having students mask indoors will limit the spread 
of COVID -19 and decrease that disruption whether it is from kids actually 
getting COVID -19 and having to isolate or being exposed to it and having to 
quarantine.  
She delivered a PowerPoint presentation on the COVID -19 weekly update 
reviewing the 7-day case rate that is 64 percent higher than last year; data 
showing 62 percent of Salt Lake County residents 12+ are fully vaccinated; 
cases of vaccinated and unvaccinated showing that without herd immunity or 
preventative measures, everyone ’s risk will increase with Delta; case rates 
and vaccination coverage by zip code; data showing that without preventative 
measures, severe illness will continue to increase; upcoming school 
vaccination clinics; and what is happening in other states.
Page 6 of 14
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes August 10, 2021
Council Member DeBry  asked how many children 12 and under were in 
intensive care units (ICU).   
Mr. David Schuld , Intelligence Section Chief for COVID -19, stated three 
children ages 0-4 were currently hospitalized, as were four children ages 
12-17, and 17 people ages 18-29 and 30-39.  The highest group hospitalized 
now are 40-49-year-olds.  Those are all Salt Lake County residents.
Mayor Wilson  stated if another variant takes over, or if the County does not 
get back to declining cases, everyone could be at risk into the future.  The 
County has to  use every tool it has to stop the spread of Covid.
