HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2021-13

AN ORDINANCE OF THE HERRIMAN CITY COUNCIL APPROVING AN
AMENDMENT TO TITLE 7 OF THE HERRIMAN CITY CODE AND ADOPTING,
THEREWITH, CHAPTER 8 SECTION 14 REGARDING RULES AND REGULATIONS
FOR DOG PARKS.

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Council (“Council’’) met in regular meeting on June 9,
2021 to consider, among other things, approving an amendment to Title 7 of the Herriman City
Code; and

WHEREAS, the Council is authorized to enact ordinances, codify such ordinances, and
make amendments to such codifications as set forth in Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-707; and

WHEREAS, the Council has considered and discussed certain changes to Title 7,
Chapter 8 of the Herriman City Code to add a Section 14 regarding Dog Park Rules and
Regulations; and

WHEREAS, Council has reviewed the proposed changes to Title 7 and hereby finds that
it is in the best interests of the public to adopt the proposed changes to Title 7, Chapter 8, Section
of the Herriman City Code.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Herriman City Council as follows:

1. Section 14 is hereby added to Title 7, Chapter 8 of the Herriman City Code, a copy
of which is attached hereto as Exhibit A.

2. The City Recorder is authorized to publish the amendment to Title 7, Chapter 8 upon
passage and publication of this Ordinance.

This ordinance assigned no. 2021-13 shall take effect immediately upon passage and
acceptance as provided herein.

PASSED AND APPROVED by the Council of Herriman, Utah, this 9th day of June,
2021.

HERRIMAN CITY CODE 7-8-14: Dog Park — Rules and Regulations.

A. Definitions. As used in this section:

“Dog Park” or “Park” means the Herriman City Dog Park.
“Dog” means any Canis familiaris over four months of age.

“Park User” means any dog owner, handler, or observer.

B. Entry into and use of the Dog Park is at the risk of individual Park Users. Any person who brings a dog
into the park assumes the legal responsibility for any damage, disease, or injury to persons, other dogs,
or property which is caused by the dog. Utilizing the Dog Park constitutes acceptance of the following
rules as well as each Park User’s willingness to assume personal responsibility for any and all injuries
arising from the use of the Dog Park.

Aggressive dogs, including dogs who jump on other Park Users, are prohibited.

Dog defecation must be immediately cleaned up by the person or Park User who is handling,

At all times while in the Dog Park, Children under the age of 12 must be supervised by

Bicycles, skateboards, rollerblades, scooters, and motorized vehicles are prohibited in the Dog

Only Dogs and their handlers are permitted in the Dog Park. All other animals are prohibited.

All dogs must be licensed with Herriman City or another city or county agency.

14. Hours of use for the Dog Park are from 7:30 AM to dusk.

15. Dogs must be in sight and under voice control of their owners at all times.

16. No food of any sort, including for dogs, is allowed in the Dog Park.

17. Smoking is prohibited in the Dog Park.

18. Park Users bringing dogs into the Dog Park must be at least 18 years old.

19. Glass bottles and containers are prohibited.

19-20. The sale, transfer of ownership. or exchange of animals at the Dog Park is prohibited.

D. These rules shall be posted in a conspicuous place in and around the Dog Park including at each
entrance.

KE. __The City’s animal control service or any law enforcement officer is hereby authorized and
empowered to enforce the provision of this chapter and to apprehend, transport, and impound any
animals and issue criminal citations to any Park Users found to be in violation of this section.

GF. ___Any violation of the rules and regulations set forth in subsection (C) shall be an infraction subject

to a $50.00 penalty for the first offense, and a $100.00 penalty for each subsequent offense.
