            1. DISCUSSION ON PROPOSED CUTS TO THE COUNTY COMMISSION BUDGET, AS WELL AS POTENTIAL
            CUTS IN OTHER DEPARTMENTS OF UTAH COUNTY, AMID THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.
            -Nathan Ivie

            COMMISSIONER IVIE – EXPRESSED THAT DUE TO REVENUE DECLINING AS A RESULT OF COVID-19, IT
            WOULD BE PRUDENT FOR THE COMMISSION TO START HAVING PRELIMINARY DISCUSSIONS ON
            WHERE CUTS ARE GOING TO NEED TO BE MADE WITHIN THE COUNTY BUDGET TO ENSURE THE
            BUDGET STAYS BALANCED THROUGHOUT THE COURSE OF THE YEAR AND CONTINUE TO MEET THE
            NEEDS OF THE CITIZENS IN DELIVERING ESSENTIAL SERVICES.

            LOOKED TO HIMSELF FIRST IN THE AREAS THAT CAN CREATE SAVINGS. THE COMMISSION HAS GONE
            THROUGH THE COMMISSIONER’S BUDGET TO LOOK FOR AREAS OF POTENTIAL SAVINGS. MEETING
            TODAY IS TO SHARE SOME OF THOSE AREAS AND ALSO HAVE SOME PRELIMINARY DISCUSSIONS OF
            OTHER AREAS IN THE COUNTY TO LOOK FOR POTENTIAL SAVINGS.

            HAS WORKED WITH RALF BARNES (HUMAN RESOURCES) ON THE COMMISSION BUDGET REGARDING
            BENEFITS. ABLE TO FIND SIGNIFICANT SAVINGS THERE AS WELL AS SAVINGS THROUGH CUTTING

                                                        PUBLIC COMMENTS

            DEBORAH HORRICK, RESIDENT - WHY NOT USE THE DATA AND MAKE A PERCENT DECISION.

            COMMISSIONER IVIE – COMMENTED THAT WE DON’T HAVE ALL THE DATA YET AND THIS IS A
            BEGINNING DISCUSSION WHERE THE COMMISSION IS TRYING TO FIND THE BEST PATH FORWARD
            WORKING TOGETHER IN SHARING IDEAS TO BE ABLE TO COME UP WITH THE RIGHT SOLUTION
            RATHER THAN HARDLINING WITHOUT ALL THE FACTS AND DATA.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – WHEN IT COMES TO LEADERSHIP, THERE ARE SOME SITUATIONS WHERE
            USING THE 10% ACROSS THE BOARD MIGHT MAKE SENSE. WE ARE NOT SURE WHERE THE DECLINE IS
            AT THIS POINT AND SO WE HAVE A RESPONSIBILITY TO SET A RESPONSIBLE BUDGET. PART OF THAT
            IS MAKING SURE THAT WE HAVE COVERED THE MOST ESSENTIAL SERVICES.

            JULIE BLANEY – STATED THAT SHE REPRESENTS 23,000 PEOPLE IN UTAH COUNTY THAT SIGNED A
            REFERENDUM TO PUT THE QUESTION ON THE BALLOT CONCERNING THE 67% TAX INCREASE.
            BUSINESSES ARE OPERATING AT ABOUT 30-40%. CONCERNED FOR PROPERTY TAXES IN THE SPRING.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – WE ALL IN ONE WAY OR ANOTHER, EXPERIENCING THE PAIN. THE COUNTY
            DOES HAVE A NUMBER OF DEPARTMENTS THAT HAVE SEVERE CUTS WITH HEAVY LAYOFFS AND
            FURLOUGHS, SO THE COUNTY IS EXPERIENCING THE LOSS.

            COMMISSIONER IVIE – FUTURE TAXES IS A CONCERN FOR ALL. WORKING CLOSELY WITH THE
            CLERK/AUDITOR’S OFFICE SO THAT THOSE IN NEED AT THAT TIME CAN APPLY FOR THE TAX RELIEF
            THAT IS AVAILABLE TO THEM.

            DANENE JACKSON, CLERK AUDITOR’S OFFICE - COLLECTION RATE HAS DROPPED ABOUT 9%.

            TOM SAKIEVICH, SPANISH FORK – TALKED ABOUT OPINIONS ON LAYOFF AND SUGGESTIONS.

            COMMISSIONERS AND TOM SAKEIVICH DISCUSSED TAX DETAILS.

            CAMERON WAGA, RESIDENT - INQUIRED AS TO WHAT THE COMMISSIONERS FEEL THE FUTURE PLANS
            ARE FOR TAXES.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – STATED HE WANTS TO RUN A SURPLUS NEXT YEAR WHILE REDUCING THE
            PROPERTY TAX RATE. WE ARE IN A UNIQUE ENVIRONMENT NOW – WE HAVE NO IDEA WHAT HOME
            VALUES ARE GOING TO DO THIS YEAR. IN AN HISTORICALLY NORMAL ENVIRONMENT, IF THERE WAS A
            SLIGHT INCREASE IN OVERALL PROPERTY VALUES IN THE COUNTY, THE TAX RATE WOULD
            AUTOMATICALLY GO DOWN. BASICALLY, THE SAME THING AS SAYING WE’RE NOT GOING TO GO
            THROUGH TRUTH IN TAXATION. WHEN WE BUILD THE RESERVE CAPACITY AND WE BUILD THAT
            SURPLUS THEN WE HAVE THE ABILITY TO NOT NEED INCREASES FUTURE. WE CAN LET THAT RATE
            CONTINUES TO DECREASE. DOES NOT THINK IT IS A SUSTAINABLE TAX POLICY TO LET THE PROPERTY
            TAX RATE DECLINE YEAR OVER YEAR. WE LET IT HAPPEN FOR 23 YEARS WHICH IS KIND OF UNHEARD
            OF EVEN IN THE STATE OF UTAH. PERIODICALLY YOU HAVE TO DO A MINOR ADJUSTMENT BUT FEELS
            THAT FOR THE NEXT YEAR AND YEAR AFTER, CAN GET BY WITHOUT DOING A TRUTH IN TAXATION
            PROCESS.

            COMMISSIONER IVIE – FEELS THIS IS SOMETHING THAT ALL 3 COMMISSIONERS ARE VERY
            COMMITTED TO – DEVELOPING MORE MULTI-YEAR BUDGETING PRACTICES WITHIN THE COUNTY.
            HAVE WORKED WITH AMELIA GARDNER POWERS (COUNTY CLERK AUDITOR) WHO IS A STRONG
            PROPONENT OF THIS ALSO. OBVIOUSLY ON PROJECTIONS, THE FARTHER YOU GET OUT, IT’S GOING TO
            BE A LITTLE LESS ACCURATE BUT IT GIVES YOU A BETTER DIRECTION ON WHERE YOU’RE GOING
            RATHER THAN LIVING PAYCHECK TO PAYCHECK SO TO SPEAK. EXPLAINED THE STATE LAW
            CONCERNING PROPERTY TAX. TRUTH IN TAXATION IS A STATE LAW.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – AGREED WITH COMMISSIONER IVIE ON THE EXPLANATION, BUT CLARIFIED
            THAT WHEN NEW HOMES ARE BUILT, WE DO GET A GROWTH FACTOR ON THE NEW HOMES, SO
            THERE IS SOME ADDITIONAL REVENUE THAT COMES FROM THAT, BUT WITH EXISTING PROPERTIES,
            THE RATE CONTINUES TO GO DOWN.

            COMMISSIONER LEE – BEFORE A CERTAIN TIME (NOT SURE OF THE DATES), PRIMARILY THE COUNTY
            RELIED ON PROPERTY TAX SO THE BURDEN THAT WAS MET WAS MET PRIMARILY ON PROPERTY TAX.
            WHEN THE STATE SHIFTED AND WE SHIFTED TO SALES TAX AS WELL, THE BURDEN ON PROPERTY TAX
            WAS DECREASED BECAUSE SALES TAX TOOK UP PART OF THE BURDEN THAT PROPERTY TAX WAS
            HOLDING UP ALL ON ITS OWN. IF WE’RE RELYING ON THE BURDEN OF BEFORE AND NOW, WE NEED
            TO HAVE, NOT JUST A CONVERSATION ON PROPERTY TAX, BUT SALES TAX AS WELL.

            DANENE JACKSON – THERE IS NOT A STANDARD FORMAT/FORMULA FOR SALES TAX PROJECTIONS.
            THE COUNTY TAKES INTO CONSIDERATIONS WHAT IS HAPPENING WITH THE ENVIRONMENT. GOING
            INTO 2020 THERE WERE SOME PREDICTIONS THAT THERE WAS GOING TO BE A SCALE BACK IN THE
            ECONOMY. THIS WAS BEFORE ANYTHING WAS KNOWN ABOUT THE CORONAVIRUS.

            WHEN ESTIMATES WERE DONE FOR 2020, TOOK A VERY CONSERVATIVE APPROACH WHICH IS A
            GENERAL GOOD BUDGETING TECHNIQUE. ONCE WE RECEIVED OUR FINAL 2019 SALES TAX
            DISTRIBUTIONS, THE 2020 SALES TAX REVENUE ENDED UP AT ABOUT 2.2% INCREASE FOR THE
            COUNTY OPTION TAX. IN TIMES WHEN THERE HASN’T BEEN THAT EXPECTATION, WE’D PROBABLY DO
            A 4-5% INCREASE IN SALES TAXES.

            KATHERINE HIGHMARSH, RESIDENT – STATED SHE WAS PART OF THE REFERENDUM. STATED THAT
            6,000 NAMES WERE CUT FROM THE REFERENDUM STATING THAT THEY WERE NOT VALID. HAD
            ISSUES BACK IN 2016 WHEN HE HUSBAND WHO HAD PASSED AWAY 15 YEARS PRIOR, WAS STILL
            LISTED ON THE VOTING RECORDS FOR YEARS. WHAT LIST IS USED TO VALIDATE THE SIGNATURES?

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – STATED THAT THIS HAS BEEN CHALLENGE IN THE PAST. IN OUR
            CLERK/AUDITOR’S DEPARTMENT, AMELIA POWERS HAS REALLY SHOWN A LOT OF LEADERSHIP AND
            HAS FIXED OF ISSUES THAT WE HAVE HAD IN THE PAST. IT HAS TAKEN SOME INVESTMENT IN
            TECHNOLOGY AND PEOPLE TO BE ABLE TO GO THROUGH THESE PROCESSES IN UPDATING VOTER
            ROLLS. VERIFICATION IS EITHER A MANUAL PROCESS OR A PROCESS THAT RELIES ON TECHNOLOGY. IT
            REQUIRES SOME INVESTMENT.

            AMELIA POWERS GARDNER, UTAH COUNTY CLERK/AUDITOR – CONCERNING:
              • HUSBAND WHO HAS PASSED AWAY 15 YEARS AGO: WHEN SHE CAME INTO OFFICE IN
                  JANUARY OF 2019, THE VOTER LISTS IN UTAH COUNTY HAD NOT BEEN ACTIVELY UPDATED
                  SINCE 2012. ONE OF THE VERY FIRST THINGS DONE WAS UPDATE THOSE LISTS AND THEY ARE
                  NOW UPDATED WEEKLY THROUGH DEATH CERTIFICATES RECEIVED THROUGH THE HEALTH
                  DEPARTMENT.
              • THE 6,000 SIGNATURES THAT DIDN’T GET VERIFIED – THIS WAS EXPLAINED IN THE LETTER
                  THAT WAS SENT TO JULIE BLANEY. THOSE SIGNATURES DID NOT FOLLOW THE PROCESS THAT
                  WAS SET FORTH BY THE LEGISLATURE. SHE AGREES THAT THE PROCESS IS INCREDIBLY
                  ONEROUS AND IS COMMITTED TO WORKING WITH THE LEGISLATURE TO FIX THE PROCESS
                  MOVING FORWARD. HAS REACHED OUT TO THE BILL SPONSOR AND IS HOPING THAT IN THE
                  NEXT LEGISLATIVE SESSION THAT CAN WORK WITH THE PEOPLE THAT WORKED ON THE
                  REFERENDUM AND HOPEFULLY CHANGE THAT PROCESS TO MAKE IT BETTER.

                     EVEN IF 100% OF THE SIGNATURES WERE VERIFIED AS VALID, THE REFERENDUM STILL WOULD
                     NOT HAVE MADE THE BALLOT. DIDN’T THINK IT WAS A GOOD USE OF TAXPAYER DOLLARS TO
                     SPEND THE TIME TO CONTINUE TO VERIFY THOSE SIGNATURES THAT CAME OUT OF PROCESS
                     BECAUSE EVEN IF 100% OF THEM HAD BEEN VALID, THE REFERENDUM STILL WOULD NOT
                     HAVE MADE THE BALLOT. AS A FIDUCIARY OF THE TAXPAYER DOLLARS, COULD NOT SPEND
                     ABOUT $10,000 OF ADDITIONAL TAXPAYER DOLLARS TO VERIFY SIGNATURES THAT
                     WOULDN’T HAVE HAD GONE TO COURT TO HAVE A JUDGE OVERRULE THE STATE
                     LEGISLATURE TO HAVE COUNTED.

                     THERE WERE ALSO APPROXIMATELY 1,800 SIGNATURES THAT WERE HAD BEEN INVALIDATED
                     BECAUSE THE SIGNATURES EITHER DIDN’T MATCH, OR THEY WERE NOT A REGISTERED VOTER
                     OR THEY DIDN’T LIVE IN UTAH COUNTY. THAT BROUGHT THE NUMBER OF SIGNATURES
                     BELOW THE THRESHOLD.
