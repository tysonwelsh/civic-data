Date: 2024-09-17
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1623


Comments:

This application is a request for approval to amend the Moderate Income Housing Plan
(MIHP) in the Draper City General Plan. In 2019, Senate Bill (SB) 34 required cities to adopt
specific strategies into their general plans to encourage the creation of moderate-income
housing. In 2022, House Bill (HB) 462 made revisions to the list of specific strategies cities
could choose from. Draper City complied with the requirement to adopt a MIHP in 2019,
and now proposes to replace Strategy L, regarding impact fees, with Strategy E, regarding
accessory dwelling units.

The Planning Commission reviewed this request at their August 22, 2024 meeting and
forwarded a positive recommendation on a 3-0 vote.

Findings for approval:
  1. A Public Hearing before the Planning Commission was held in accordance with Utah
     State Code Section 10-9a-404.
  2. The General Plan Amendment is consistent with the requirements of Utah State
     Code and is appropriate.

Findings for denial:
  1. The General Plan Amendment is not consistent with the requirements of Utah State
     Code and is not appropriate.
