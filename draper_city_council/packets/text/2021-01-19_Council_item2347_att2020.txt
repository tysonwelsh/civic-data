         A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, UTAH
         (THE “ISSUER”), AUTHORIZING THE DEFEASANCE OF CERTAIN
         OF ITS OUTSTANDING SALES TAX REVENUE BONDS, SERIES
         2012B AND CERTAIN OF THE OUTSTANDING LEASE REVENUE
         REFUNDING BONDS, SERIES 2016 ISSUED BY THE MUNICIPAL
         BUILDING AUTHORITY OF THE CITY OF DRAPER, UTAH FROM
         LEGALLY AVAILABLE MONIES OF THE ISSUER; APPROVING
         THE EXECUTION OF AN ESCROW AGREEMENT; AND RELATED
         MATTERS.

        WHEREAS, the City Council (the “Council”) of the Issuer has previously issued
its Sales Tax Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series 2012B (the “Series 2012B Bonds”) and
the Issuer desires to retire and defease/discharge all or any portion of the Series 2012B
Bonds with legally available funds on hand; and
       WHEREAS, the Municipal Building Authority of the City of Draper, Utah (the
“Authority”) has previously issued its Lease Revenue Refunding Bonds, Series 2016 (the
“Series 2016 Bonds”) and the Issuer desires to retire and defease/discharge all or any
portion of the Series 2016 Bonds with legally available funds on hand; and

        WHEREAS, to accomplish the purposes set forth in the preceding recitals, and
subject to the limitations set forth herein, the Council desires to approve and authorize one
or more Escrow Agreements to be entered into between the Issuer and an escrow agent
(collectively, the “Escrow Agreement”) in compliance with and effecting a defeasance or
discharge of bonds pursuant to the bond documents related to the Series 2012B Bonds and
the Series 2016 Bonds;

       NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby resolved by the City Council of Draper City,
Utah, as follows:

       Section 1.    The defeasance, discharge and retirement of all or any portion of the
Series 2012B Bonds and Series 2016 Bonds (collectively, the “Defeasance”) is hereby
authorized and approved.

        Section 2.     The Escrow Agreement is hereby authorized, approved, and
confirmed. The Mayor or any acting Mayor Pro Tem (the “Mayor”) and the City Recorder
or designee (the “City Recorder”) are hereby authorized to execute and deliver the Escrow
Agreement for and on behalf of the Issuer and the Authority, with final terms as may be
established in consultation with Lewis Young Robertson & Burningham, Inc., as municipal
advisor.

       Section 3.     The Mayor, City Recorder, and other appropriate officials of the
Issuer and the Authority are authorized to approve and execute and deliver any other
document (including but not limited to releases of pledged security) which may be
necessary to conform the same to the final terms of the Defeasance. The execution thereof
by the Mayor and the City Recorder on behalf of the Issuer and the Authority of the
documents approved hereby shall conclusively establish such necessity, appropriateness,

        Section 4.     The Mayor, City Recorder, and other appropriate officials of the
