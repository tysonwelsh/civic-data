       C.      Public Hearing: City initiated Variance and Land Use Appeal Update Text
               Amendment Request.
               (Legislative Item)
               On the request of Draper City, a Text Amendment to portions of Draper City
               Municipal Code (DCMC) Titles 2, 3, 6, 8, 9, and 17 to address recent changes to
               Utah State Code related to variances and land use appeals. as Application No.
               2026-0003-TA.          Staff    Contact:    Todd     Taylor,     801-576-6510,
               todd.taylor@draperutah.gov.

City Planner, Todd Taylor, reported that the proposed text amendment was in response to House
Bill 368, adopted in 2025, which prohibits the City from requiring a public hearing for a variance
or land use appeal.

The following changes had been made:

   •   References to “hearing” were changed to “meeting”.
   •   The Appeals and Variance Hearing Officer title was changed to Appeals and Variance
       Officer.
   •   The noticing section was updated to clarify the noticing area and remove language that no
       longer applies.
   •   A section was added specifying how public meetings will be noticed.

Mr. Taylor reported that Commissioner Nixon had brought a USC reference error to his attention
that will be corrected prior to presenting the item to the City Council.

Commissioner Nixon asked why the State made the change. Assistant City Attorney Spencer
DuShane reported that variances must meet five legal criteria: they must be questions of law, not
questions of public concern. At a public hearing, members of the public can comment. However,
comments do not affect whether the applicant can prove they meet all five criteria required for a
variance. It makes sense not to call it a public hearing or to give members of the public an
opportunity to comment, because their comments have no bearing on the decision.

In response to a follow-up question from Commissioner Nixon, Mr. DuShane stated that he was
uncertain if the same would apply to appeals. The Appeals and Variance Officer stands in place
of the Planning Commission and can uphold, overturn, or impose new conditions on the Planning
Commission's decisions. In that scenario, a public hearing may be useful.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

       D.      Public Hearing: Cityinitiated Public Access Amenity Zoning Text Amendment
               Request.
               (Legislative Item)
               On the request of Draper City, a Zoning Text Amendment to portions of Draper
               City Municipal Code (DCMC) Title 9 to address recent changes to Utah State Code
               related to public access amenities. Known as Application No. 2026-0004-TA.
               Staff Contact: Todd Taylor, 801-576-6510, todd.taylor@draperutah.gov.

Mr. Taylor reported that the proposed text amendment was in response to House Bill 368, adopted
in 2025, which prohibits the City from requiring private maintenance of public access amenities,
except for park strips and sidewalks, or amenities agreed upon through an agreement. The bill
defines a public access amenity as “a feature like a trail or recreation areas that a municipality
designates for public access and use.”

The following changes had been made:
   • All instances of the words “public” and “public use” were removed.
   • The definition of “public use space” was deleted.

Commissioner Fowler asked if the space is designated for public use or just green space. Mr.
Taylor clarified that it would depend on the specific development. Open space can be public or
private, and State law no longer allows the City to require that it be public. Chair Adams stated
that it is sensible to allow private land to be held for private use.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Motion: Commissioner Ogden moved to forward a POSITIVE recommendation to the City
Council for the City initiated Public Access Amenity Zoning Text Amendment, as requested
by Draper City, Application No. 2026-0004-TA, based on the following Findings and the
Criteria for Approval listed in the Staff Report dated January 5, 2026.

Findings for Approval:

              (Legislative Item)
              On the request of Draper City, a Zoning Text Amendment to portions of Draper
              City Municipal Code (DCMC) Title 9 to address recent changes to Utah State Code
              related to residential parking requirements. Known as Application No. 2026-0005-

Mr. Taylor reported that the proposed text amendment was in response to Senate Bill 181, adopted
in 2025, which modified provisions on the size and configuration of parking for one- and two-
family single-family homes and townhomes, and restricts cities from requiring a garage for owner-
occupied affordable housing. Other changes were made for clarity and to add visitor, tandem, and
accessory dwelling unit parking standards.
