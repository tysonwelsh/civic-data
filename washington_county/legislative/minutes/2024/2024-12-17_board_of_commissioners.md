CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3414, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LETTER OF SUPPORT FOR THE ZION/SR-9 REGIONAL SHUTTLE SERVICE

Regional Tourism Regulatory Affairs Director Heath Hansen informed the Commissioners of a
Zion transportation study that started in April of 2024. The study researched the feasibility of a
shuttle service between the Zion visitor center and the east side of Zion. As the study concludes,
it is proposed that a three-year pilot program take place of running a shuttle between those
locations. The letter also serves as a financial commitment from Washington County to provide
$100,000 in annual funding for three years beginning in 2025. The County will be seeking grant
funding to assist in covering that commitment.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3414, A
Resolution Approving a Letter of Support for the Zion/SR-9 Regional Shuttle
Service. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

Mr. Sullivan relayed the budget was initially presented last month and since then it has been
updated to reflect all anticipated activities in 2025. Key updates include salaries and wages
increasing due to a 2.5% cost of living adjustment and merit-based increases; the elections
department lowered its budget by $300,000, postponing the purchase of new equipment to a future
year; the Sheriff's office budget increased for additional expenses, and updated insurance and
rebate information required nearly $1.4 million in adjustments to the payroll fund. With these
updates, the 2025 budget is again balanced and ready for approval.
