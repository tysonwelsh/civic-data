MEMO
To: City Council
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2025-05-06
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinances #1649 and #1650


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a land use map amendment, zoning map
amendment and development agreement amendment for approximately 0.05 acres
located on the west side of 1300 East, at approximately 13040 S. 1300 East. The property
is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that the applications be approved for
the purpose of allowing for a boundary line adjustment between neighboring property.
The future boundary line adjustment will transfer approximately 1,970 square feet of
property from the Englands to the Lingos.

The Planning Commission reviewed these items at the April 10, 2025 meeting and
forwarded a positive recommendation on all applications on a votes of 4-0.

Land Use Map Amendment
Findings for Approval:
  1. The proposed land use map amendment is consistent with goals, objectives, and
     policies of the city's general plan.
  2. The proposed land use map amendment is harmonious with the overall character
     of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
  3. The proposed land use map amendment will not adversely affect adjacent property.
  4. There are adequate facilities and services intended to serve the subject property,
     including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire
     protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater
     and refuse collection.
  5. The proposed land use map amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F).
  6. The legislative body may adopt any amendment to the general plan land use map
     that the legislative body considers appropriate.

Findings for Denial:
  1. The proposed land use map amendment is not consistent with goals, objectives,
     and policies of the city's general plan.
  2. The proposed land use map amendment is not harmonious with the overall
     character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
  3. The proposed land use map amendment will adversely affect adjacent property.
  4. There are not adequate facilities and services intended to serve the subject
     property, including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities,
     police and fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies,
     and wastewater and refuse collection.
  5. The legislative body may reject any amendment to the general plan land use map
     that the legislative body considers appropriate.
