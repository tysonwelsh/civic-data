3. Reports
            3.1 Mayor's           Report
            Mayor         Silvestrini      reported       the Promise         Program      Advisory       Council        met. The Promise
            Program         had been having            a lot of success         and had a lot of projects               in the works.           He said
            the Millcreek          Common           ice ribbon       had been successful             and had generated                 over $170,000
            in revenue         to the city in the month              of December           2022. The goal was to cover costs, not
            make money.            He noted Millcreek               Comi'non       had won two local               awards.       The mayor           was
            excited       for the upcoming            legislative      session.     He felt there would               be meaningful            progress
            with     addressing          homelessness.         The average         intake     for Millcreek's           overflow          homeless
            shelter       was 90 people         a night.      Law     enforcement         had been getting             calls for service           within
            the shelter        but not from         outside     the shelter.       He credited        law enforcement               for their work.


            3.2 City        Councn         Member         Reports
            Council        Member         Catten      said the Point in Time              Count,    which        counted       homeless
            individuals          in the county,        would       be taking     place on January           26Ih. She noted she would                         be
            participating.         She reported           she was the new chair for the South                       Salt Lake Valley
            Mosquito          Abatement        District       and unofficially          the chair of the Jordan                River     Commission.
            Council        Member         DeSirant        noted he would          also be attending          the legislative             session in his
            professional          capacity.


            3.3 Staff       Reports
            Mike       Winder      reported      he met with          Stan Penfold         of Green Bikes            to discuss          potentially
            having        a station      in Millcreek's        new Jordan         River     trailhead.     There       may also be a future                   for
            it at Millcreek           Common.         Winder        said every     three years the city has the opportunity                            to be
            a Community            Development             Block     Grant     (CDBG)        city with      the federal          government.            The
            city currently         contracts       with    the county         through     the Urban       County        Program           and staff
            concluded          that the city should           stay with       the county      and not be a CDBG                  city.     The mayor
            explained         all of the work         associated       with     a city doing       it on its own,         and agreed it was
            wise to stay with             the county.
