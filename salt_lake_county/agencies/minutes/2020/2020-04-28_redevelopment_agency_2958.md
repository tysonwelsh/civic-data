Mr. Blake Thomas , Executive Director, Salt Lake County Redevelopment 
Agency, stated the request is to seed a Magna Main Street Revolving Loan 
Fund by transferring $ 569,835 from the Magna Main Street RDA area to 
Preservation Utah to administrator the fund.  This would zero out the 
dormant fund. He delivered a PowerPoint presentation showing the location 
of the Magna West Main RDA Project Area, locations of damaged buildings, 
due to the March 18, 2020, earthquake, the purpose of the Revolving Loan 
Fund, what the funds can be used for,  who is eligible to apply for the funds, 
the history of Preservation Utah, and the benefits of this Revolving Loan 
Fund.   
Director Bradley asked what entity would determine if an applicant was 
eligible to apply for a loan.  
Ms. Dina Blaes , Director, Regional Development, stated Preservation Utah 
will determine who is eligible for the loan.  It will utilize the Secretary of 
Interior Standards for rehabilitation, which are national standards that are 
accepted by most historical preservation organizations in the nation.
