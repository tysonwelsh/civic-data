     Francis        Lilly     said this ordinance               deals with           residential       facilities      for persons         with    a disability
     and reasonable              accommodation                 to exceed           occupancy         limits     that are established              in the zone.
     Disability        includes         anybody           who is actively             recovering        from        a substance         use disorder.
     Residences             housing         disabled       people       have a right          to live in neighborhoods.                  These residences
     are subject            to extensive         licensing.        If they wish to exceed the occupancy                            limit     in the zone,
     which      for single        family         is four unrelated                adults,    they must apply            for a reasonable
     accommodation.               Reasonable               accommodations                   are the result      of decades of case law and
     guidance         from      the Department               of Justice           and the Department                of Housing          and Urban
    Development.               Cities        have to on a case-by-case                      basis consider           reasonable         accornrnodations.
    The city can impose                      reasonable        limits      as long      as those limits             are not discriminatory


    The revised              ordinance         is an attempt            to balance          the interests      of persons         with     a disability      with
    the city's        prerogative             to impose        reasonable            land use regulations.              The new ordinance
    accomplishes              the following:
       1) New         language          that states that a reasonable                       accommodation              does not require            structural        or
       landscaping             alterations           that would         change the structure's                 residential       character        or
       appearance.
       2) Reorganizes             the "reasonable                 accommodation"                   language      into a new section.              Includes
       language             that states that a reasonable                   accommodation                cannot       create a nonconforming                 use if
       that reasonable             accommodation                   were to be terminated                 (such as a reasonable
       accornrnodation                that could          permit      a duplex         in a single-family             zone).
       3) Creates            approval         criteria     for reasonable             accommodation                 determinations.
       4) Significantly               adds to the submittal                 requirements             for reasonable          accommodation
       determinations,                which      are currently            lacking.
       5) Adds to termination                     and transferability                requirements           for reasonable          accommodation
       permits.
       6) Adds         a definition           for "Reasonable               Accommodation".
