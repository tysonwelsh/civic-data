3.   PUBLIC COMMENTS

    Jessica Rassmussen, living in The Garden neighborhood, expressed frustration with the
impact over occupancy has had on the bus route in her neighborhood. It has prevented the bus
from being able to pick up her daughter because of the difficulty in navigating the congested
road.

    Brandon Peterson, living in The Garden neighborhood, expressed his support for Operation
Neighborhood Shield but also shared his concern with it potentially pitting neighbors against
each other.

     Dana Blake, living in the Bridgeport neighborhood, asked for clarification on the process
moving forward and what the city’s expectation would be from those that were selected to lead
their neighborhoods.

    Emily McClean, living in The Maples neighborhood, also expressed concern over the
potential to have neighbors pitted against one another. She asked the council provide someone to
help mediate any discussions so that the resolution wasn’t coming exclusively from residents
who may not know the legality of their proposed solutions.

    Marcus Jessup, living in The Gardens neighborhood, was concerned about the potential
expansion of any neighborhood created committee onto areas other than parking or over
occupancy concerns. He was worried that it would become a type of HOA.
