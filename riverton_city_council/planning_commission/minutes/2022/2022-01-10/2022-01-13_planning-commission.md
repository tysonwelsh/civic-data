             BAMBERGER DRIVE. APPLICANT – TRACIE MAYNARD.

Chair Rushton opened the public hearing.

City Planner, Tim Prestwich presented the staff report stated that the above item was on the
agenda in December but was tabled to the January meeting due to lack of a quorum. The
application was renoticed for tonight’s hearing. Mr. Prestwich described the proposed
business as an in-home teeth whitening business located at 11800 South and 2078 West in
a newer neighborhood. The zoning is R-4 but the lots are nearly one-third acre in size. The
proposed hours of operation will be 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. in an office on the main floor of
the two-story home. It was anticipated that there will be one to five clients per day by
appointment and no more than two clients per hour. The applicant was proposing to have
two part-time employees and one resident. It was noted that the proposed employees are
family members.

The ordinance allows for one full-time or equivalent on-site employee with no action by the
Planning Commission. The Planning Commission may grant permission for more
employees based on criteria in the ordinance, including off-street parking, employee vehicle
screening, less than 25% of main floor area used for the business, employees between the
hours of 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m., and no construction vehicles. Customer visits may not
exceed two per hour or eight per day and no signage is allowed.

Mr. Prestwich presented a layout of the home showing the whitening room and parking
availability for employees and customers. Staff recommended approval of the application

       C.     CONDITIONAL USE, “MY STORAGE RIVERTON” FOR STORAGE UNITS
              IN A C-G ZONE AT 13487 SOUTH ROSECREST ROAD. APPLICANT –
              JEFFREY BERNSON REPRESENTING MY STORAGE RIVERTON, LLC.

Chair Rushton opened the public hearing.

Mr. Prestwich presented the staff report and stated that the application was on the December
agenda and re-noticed for tonight’s meeting. About one year ago, this application came to
the City under the name “MY STORAGE HERRIMAN”. The application expired and the
applicant changed the name of the facility and provided an updated site plan from the
previous meeting. Numerous comments were received regarding the application that were
provided in the meeting packet.

The site is located at 13400 South and 5600 West on the border of Riverton and Herriman.
The property is zoned RR-22. There is an IHC clinic on the property and a credit union on
the corner. The Commission approved a commercial subdivision on the property that will
split the remainder of the property into three parcels. There is a new street that runs through
the property, an eight-foot masonry wall along one boundary, and a six-foot masonry wall
along the boundary next to the park.

The site is 2.11 acres in size. The zoning of the property to the north is C-G, to the south is
RR-22, to the east is Parks and Open Space, and to the west is R-1-21 in Herriman City,
which allows for one-half acre lots. There was a question as to whether the proposed
Conditional Use is allowed in the zone. It was noted that permitted uses are listed in two
places in the ordinance; in the front of each section of each zone, and on the table of
commercial uses. Storage Units are listed as an allowed Conditional Use except on
properties adjacent to 12600 South, east of Bangerter Highway. This property is outside of
that area and is within the permitted boundaries.

Mr. Prestwich displayed a rendering of the site area and described the proposed location in
relation to the surrounding properties. The previous plan included substantial grading
Riverton City Planning Commission Meeting                                                      6
January 13, 2022


issues, however, the applicant has since revised the plan and resolved the issues.
Mr. Prestwich explained that the building will be set back 35 feet from the south property line
and will include three stories with a basement. The front building will be a single level and
the applicant is requesting a zero-lot-line. This is allowed in a C-G Zone when it borders a
similar zone and similar use property. The property was determined to meet these criteria.
The building will be designed so that all of the drainage will remain on the site.

The setback to the park is 20 feet and 40 feet. That building will have two levels with a
basement. The neighbors expressed concern regarding the height of the buildings. There
are currently no elevations for the site, which makes it difficult to fully evaluate the project.
The applicant has indicated that they intend to drop the level down by excavating.
Mr. Prestwich diverted that discussion to the applicant. Staff recommended approval of the
application with specific conditions.

Commissioner Russell asked if the zone allows for a three-story structure. Mr. Prestwich
confirmed that the structure can be up to 35 feet in height. Mechanical equipment is allowed
to exceed that height but must be screened. Commissioner Russell stated that there will be
approximately 8.5 feet net clearance per story.

Commissioner Brown asked for clarification on the zero-lot-line. Mr. Prestwich explained
that it means that the building wall is the property line. She asked if there are any other
locations in the City where there is residential property bordering a commercial property with
has 35-foot buildings. The Neighborhood Walmart was identified as an example. City
Attorney, Ryan Carter, described the setback requirements and how they relate to the
neighboring properties. He explained that at this point with no elevations, it is difficult to fully
assess the impact the business will have on the residential neighbors. This Conditional Use
is generally allowed with reasonable conditions to mitigate potential issues. There are
additional concerns with the application regarding building height, character, and human
activity on the site.

The applicant, Jeff Berson, asked that the Commission disregard the previous site plans as
they have since been revised. The new plan includes bringing the level down to the six-foot
level of the existing wall, which will create an eight to 10-foot screen for the property.
According to the engineers, the two levels of the building will be just slightly higher than the
first level shown in the previous plan. The three-story building will be commensurate with
the previous two-story building. No fill will be brought in. There should be no interference
from car lights based on the proposed setback. The site is secured with gate access
between 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m., which also limits traffic and lights issues. There will be
surveillance on the property by virtue of the storage unit use. Screening along the roadway
will be trees along Rosecrest Road. Eventually, there will be no view of the buildings from
the road. The only restriction is an electrical transformer at the front of the property.

Mr. Berson acknowledged that the first step is to obtain Conditional Use Permit approval to
proceed with the new site plan. They would also like to involve the neighbors to ensure that
their voices are heard. He stated that traffic is substantially minimized with the security gate
and surveillance.

Chair Rushton opened the public hearing.

Mr. Prestwich presented the staff report and stated that the property to the north is zoned
RR-22, however, the General Plan is R-3. The subject property was granted a rezone to
R-3 last September or October. He described the SLR property where The Park is under
development.      A question was raised regarding the road and the Council ultimately
determined to close it. The applicant owns the adjacent odd-shaped property. He is
proposing to do develop lots on his property and is requesting permission for a private lane
for access. The parcel is approximately two acres in size and is narrow on the west side
with about 58 feet of frontage on 4570 and 128 feet of frontage on Sunday Drive. The
proposed lots will be 95 to 100 feet deep after a 30-foot lane is added.

Mr. Prestwich reminded the Commissioners of the requirements for private lanes. The
Commission should consider (1) whether the property qualifies for a private lane according
to the Code, and (2) what are the necessary conditions for the private lane to function on
the property, such as the maximum number of lots, lane width, fire turnaround, etc.

The Conceptual Site Plan shows that it could potentially work based on the preliminary
dimensions and requirements. Mr. Prestwich discussed the requirements for signage,
parking limitations, frontage improvements, and the width of the lane. The Planning
Commission will determine if the lane is necessary. The Commission also needs to ensure
that it will not impede a public road. Mr. Prestwich stated that it will not. Finally, the
Commission needs to determine if the lane needs to be extended for any future use. The
site plan shows that it would be possible to extend it to the west. The applicant asked if the
City would want to put a public street through the property. Mr. Prestwich stated that it is
not on the Master Transportation Plan and would not be feasible. A private lane was
determined to work in this situation.

Staff believes that the proposal meets the criteria for a private lane and recommended
approval based on the conditions set forth in the staff report.

Commissioner Brown asked for clarification on the lane and how far it extends. It was noted
that the proposed site plan shows that it ends before going through the entire property.

third was to deny the motion and keep the zoning as is. The exterior structure would be
similar to what currently surrounds the property but the different zoning would allow the
applicant to achieve the desired outcome.

comments or questions.

              WEST, PHASE 18”, LOCATED NEAR 4701 WEST 13400 SOUTH.
              APPLICANT – EDGE HOMES.

Mr. Prestwich presented the information for the Mountain Ridge West Subdivision and
reminded the Commission that previously they were presented with the Community Scale
Plan and the Block Scale Plan. What is before them now is the Detail Plan. Phase 17 was
done previously and Phase 18 is on tonight’s agenda. Staff was in the process of reviewing
the engineering plans and recommended approval of the Detail Plan. Mr. Prestwich
displayed slides of the plan and identified the streets and various amenities.

Commissioner Brown asked about connecting a specific road but stated that it actually ends
at the pond, which would not be possible.

The applicant, Brandon Watson, identified the clubhouse and pool area as part of this phase.
He was pleased to provide the residents with those amenities.

Commissioner Lefgren asked if this is a for sale or for rent subdivision. It was noted that
Edge Homes is the applicant, and they only offer for sale units.

Chair Rushton asked if it is possible to complete the entire roadway system at once or do it
in phases. Mr. Carter stated that in order to do it all at once they would need to stub out all
the utilities and they may not be ready to do all of that. Construction access and traffic were
of concern but the hope was that the developer manages it well. To this point, the developer
has phased the project appropriately. They were also willing to construct temporary walking
paths for children walking to and from school. The City cannot require the developer to
complete the entire roadway system as they have already approved the phased option.
Additionally, once the roads are in, they will be dedicated to the City. They will then become
the City’s responsibility to maintain.
