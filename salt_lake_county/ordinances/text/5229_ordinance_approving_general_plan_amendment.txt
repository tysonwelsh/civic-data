SALT LAKE COUNTY 
ORDINANCE 
 
ORDINANCE NO.                                                            , 2020 
 
AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING WASATCH CANYONS GENERAL PLAN UPDATE  
 
 The County legislative body of Salt Lake County, State of Utah, ordains as follows: 
 
 
 Section 1:  The 1989 Wasatch Canyons Master Plan is hereby replaced by the Wasatch Canyons 
General Plan Update, which is attached hereto as Exhibit 1 and incorporated herein as if fully set forth.  
The Moderate-Income Housing Element for Salt Lake County Unincorporated General Plans and the 
Salt Lake County Resource Management Plan, which the County Council previously approved as part 
of the general plans for the unincorporated Salt Lake County, shall remain in effect.    
 Section 2:  This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at least 
one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Salt Lake County, and if 
not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately upon its first publication. 
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Salt Lake County Council has approved, passed and adopted this 
ordinance this                 day of                                         , 2020.
