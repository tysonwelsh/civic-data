O COVID-19 Update — Executive Buttars indicated the Governor announced there will be a
lessening of COVID-19 restrictions as of Friday, May 1, 2020, from a category red (high)
to category orange (moderate). Cache County will open some restaurants with revised
seating of tables ten feet apart; the maximum number of individuals allowed in a group
will be twenty (20) instead of ten (10) and the Health Department is tasked with further
defining any changes on a focal level. Monitoring will continue and this may move back
to a category red if cases increase or to a category yellow if Cache County maintains or
reduces the current level of COVID-19 cases. Executive Buttars will hold a Department
Heads meeting tomorrow to determine when Cache County will open the building to the
public. Masks will be provided for county employees.

O New operations center for the Road Department — Construction is progressing and
Executive Buttars asked if the Council is interested in a tour within the next week or two.

Council members responded they would like a tour. Buttars will schedule it.

THE COUNCIL CONVENED AS A BOARD OF EQUALIZATION

» Property Tax Exemption Requests — Findings and Determination:

o IHC Health Services, Inc. for Logan Regional Hospital - Chairman Ward has
reservations about the charitable donations claimed by IHC. Council member Erickson
asked if the donated portion is IHC’s billed amount or the allowed billing by the average
insurance company. Council member White said it is billable services allowed by
Medicare. Vice Chair Worthen and Council member Borup said IHC’s donation is within
the rules for allowing of a tax exemption.

o Community Nursing Services

ACTION: Motion by Council member Zilles to approve the property tax exemption
requests for IHC Health Services, Inc. for Logan Regional Hospital and Community
Nursing Services. Borup seconded the motion. The motion passed, 6 aye — Borup,
Erickson, Tidwell, White, Worthen & Zilles and 1 nay — Ward.

o Corporation of the Episcopal Church — Chairman Ward noted the recommendation is to
approve the exemption for the church (06-0018-0037) and deny the exemption for the
Champ House (06-0018-0001).

Vice Chair Worthen concurred with the recommendation and stated this is not based on
whether the Council thinks these are good people doing good things or not. It is based
on the fact it is rentable property and should be taxed and doesn’t qualify under what
the law says.

Attorney Luthy stated he reviewed last year’s denial based on the fact that Summerfest
does not give the requisite gift to the community. It is a good organization, but does not
meet the standard. That denial has been appealed and is awaiting a decision. He

request for the Episcopal Church building, and deny the property tax exemption request

Council Member Reports

Cache County Council
04-28-2020

Barbara Tidwell said the RAPZ/Restaurant Committee has had its first meeting with another
scheduled for Friday, May 1, 2020. They are cognizant of the fact that revenue will probably be
much less next year because of closures and the canceling of so many events.

Jon White hopes the County Fair and Rodeo won’t be canceled. He asked whether there will
be a summer county employee party. Buttars said that has not been discussed. It may depend
on whether the Aquatics Center is open or not.

Gina Worthen has serious concerns over the economic consequences of COVID-19.

Work has begun on 3100 North and should be done by August or before from 1200 East to 400
East streets.

The state Republican convention used a voting app she is impressed with.

Dave Erickson commented he liked the state Republican convention and felt more people were
voting than in previous years.

He asked if Executive Buttars has heard any more about the Mayors getting together on the
CCCOG funding distribution. Executive Buttars replied they have decided to put the money

towards the county’s Richmond project. Providence will be reapplying for their project in the
coming funding cycle.

Erickson said Director Jensen's sales tax report was excellent, but he is not excited to see the
next one because of COVID-19’s effect on businesses. Executive Buttars observed it may be
better than we think because every time he drives by Lowe’s their parking lot is full.

Erickson hopes the Fair will not be canceled because the citizens will need a celebration by that
time after all the COVID-19 restrictions.

Paul Borup thanked Director Jensen for the sales tax report and said the sales tax will likely
drop and the county will need to be more conservative next year.

ADJOURNMENT

The Council meeting adjourned at 5:56 p.m.
