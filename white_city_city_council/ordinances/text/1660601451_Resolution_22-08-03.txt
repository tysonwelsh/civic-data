WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO: 22-08-03 DATE: August 4, 2022
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPOINTING PHILLIP CARDENAZ AS THE WHITE CITY’S MEMBER OF THE
BOARDS OF TRUSTEES OF THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT AND SALT
LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA
RECITALS

A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Section 10-2a-401 ef seq.

B. White City is part of the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Services Area (“SLVLESA”), a
local district, and Unified Police Department (“UPD”), an interlocal entity consisting of a
various municipalities and Salt Lake County.

C. White City has the right and legal responsibility to appoint a member of the Board of
Trustees for SLVLESA and to the governing body of UPD.

D. On this date, the White City Metro Township Council, the legislative body of White City
elected by the Voters of White City, has determined it is in the best interest of White City to
appoint Phillip Cardenaz, as White City’s member of the Board of Trustees of SLVLESA
and the governing body of UPD.

RESOLUTION

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the
Council formally appoints Phillip Cardenaz to serve as White City’s member of the Board of
Trustees of the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area and governing body of the
Unified Police Department; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the Council hereby directs staff to provide a copy of this
Resolution to the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area and Unified Police
Department to inform them of the appointment.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County,
UT this 4" day of August 2022.
