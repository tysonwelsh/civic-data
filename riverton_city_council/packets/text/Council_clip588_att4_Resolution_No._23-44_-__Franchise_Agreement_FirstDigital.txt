                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-44

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
           WITH FIRSTDIGITAL COMMUNICATIONS, LLC

       WHEREAS, FirstDigital Communications, LLC (“FirstDigital”) desires to install,
maintain, operate and/or control a fiber optic infrastructure network in Riverton City (“City”)
owned right of ways to provide internet service to City residents; and
       WHEREAS, it is both reasonable and necessary for City to allow the installation of
FirstDigital optic infrastructure network in the City owned public right of ways within the City
and thereafter to maintain, operate and/or control said network; and
       WHEREAS, the City finds and determines that the public interest in health, safety and
welfare will be enhanced by the provision of the proposed service; and
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
   1. The City Council concludes that entering into the above-described agreement with
      FirstDigital will enhance the public health, safety and welfare of the public, and
      authorizes the City to enter into and sign the same.
   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute a Franchise Agreement with FirstDigital in
      substantially similar form to the Agreement attached as Exhibit A, once it is approved as
      to legal form by the City Attorney.
   3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 6th day of
June, 2022 by the following vote:
