1. DISCUSS A PROPOSED ORDINANCE AMENDING UTAH COUNTY CODE 10-8 REGARDING

UTAH COUNTY DRINKING WATER SOURCE PROTECTION PROVISIONS.
- BENJAMIN C. VAN NOY, DEPUTY ATTORNEY —

UTAH COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS
MINUTES — January 29, 2019
Page 9

BENJAMIN VAN NOY, DEPUTY ATTORNEY, INFORMS THE COMMISSION THAT IN 2010
THERE HAS BEEN A REQUIREMENT FOR WATER SOURCE PROTECTIONS. SINCE 2010,
UTAH COUNTY HAS NEEDED TO MAKE IMPROVEMENTS. BEN EXPLAINS THAT THIS
ORDINANCE CLARIFIES DEFINITIONS, REMOVED REQUIREMENTS FROM THE COUNTY
TO COLLECT AND MAINTAIN SOURCE PROTECTION MAPS BECAUSE THE STATE KEEPS
A METICULOUS UPDATED MAP THAT THE COUNTY HAS ACCESS TO. CITIES ARE
REQUIRED TO GIVE THE COUNTY NOTICE FOR ANY WATER SOURCE PROTECTION
UPDATES.

COMMISSIONER LEE EXPRESSES CONCERNS ABOUT RENOVATIONS THAT RESIDENTS
WISHED TO MAKE, THE CURRENT ORDINANCES PREVENTED CITIZENS FROM
UPDATING THEIR SYSTEMS.

COMMISSIONER IVIE EXPRESSES HIS CONCERNS OVER THE STATE’S MAPPING IF THE
COUNTY DISAGREES WITH THE STATE MAP. HE WANTS TO KNOW WHAT MECHANISMS
EXIST TO ALTER THE STATE MAP IF IT IS INCORRECT. BEN VAN NOY STATES THAT THE
ORDINANCE ALLOWS CITIZENS TO APPEAL THE STATE IF THE MAP JIS INCORRECTLY
DRAWN. COMMISSIONER IVIE ASKS WHAT MECHANISMS ARE IN PLACE THAT DICTATE
WHAT ZONES ARE DESIGNATED THE WAY THEY ARE. BEN VAN NOY STATES THAT THE
CONVENTIONAL SYSTEM CURRENTLY USED IS SCIENTIFIC BUT CAN HOLD ERRORS.
JASON GARRETT, COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT, ANSWERS COMMISSIONER’S IVIE
QUESTION BY STATING THAT THE CONTROL SYSTEM ALLOWS PROTECTIONS AGAINST
WATER POLLUTANTS.

DAVE JACKSON: EXPRESSES CONCERN OVER FISCAL IMPACT WITH THE
INSTALLATION OF THE NEW REQUIRED SYSTEM. THE COST WOULD BE PASSED ONTO
THE NEW HOME OWNER WHICH WOULD BE APPX $70,000.

JOEL TAYLOR: RETIRED ATTORNEY WHO HAS SERVED ON THE PLANNING
COMMISSION OF UTAH COUNTY WHEN GOVERNOR GARY HERBERT WAS A
COMMISSIONER. SHE STATES THEAT THE PROBLEM IS NOT THE ORDINANCE RATHER
IS THE CURRENT INTERPRETATION OF THE ORDINANCE.

COMMISSIONER LEE ASKS CIVIL ATTORNEY BEN VAN NOY WHETHER OR NOT THE
INTERPRETATION IS CORRECT THAT THE COUNTY IS TAKING. BEN VAN NOY STATES
THAT THE INTERPRETATION IS CORRECT ON THE DEFINITION OF “NEW USE”. THE
INTERPRETATION OF THE STATUTE HAS REMAINED THE SAME FOR THE LAST 5 YEARS.

TRACEY TANNER: EXPRESSES CONCERNS THAT THE NEW PROPOSED ORDINANCE
WILL IMPOSE MORE RESTRICTIONS ON HOME OWNERS WHO LIVE WITHIN ZONE 2.

UTAH COUNTY RESIDENT: CONCERNS OVER PAYING TAXES FOR YEARS AND BEING
UNABLE TO USE THEIR OWN PROPERTY, PROPOSED ORDINANCE WOULD PREVENT
IMPACTED HOME OWNERS FROM UTILIZING A TRADITIONAL WATER SYSTEM WHICH
RESIDENTS OUTSIDE OF ZONE 2 CAN UTILIZE.

ROBERT BEERS, UTAH DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY, DIVISION OF
WATER QUALITY: MENTIONS THAT NO REFERENCE TO THE DIVISION OF WATER
QUALITY IS IN THE PROPOSED ORDINANCE. BELIEVES THAT THE DIVISION OF WATER
QUALITY SHOULD BE INCLUDED IN THE ORDINANCE.

DIEDRE BECK, DIVISION OF DRINKING WATER, LICENSED GEOLOGIST: STATES THAT
THE ORDINANCES WILL STILL NEED APPROVAL FROM APPLICABLE COUNTY AND
STATE GOVERNING AUTHORITIES.

2. DISCUSS THE FORMATION OF THE GOOD GOVERNANCE ADVISORY BOARD. THIS
INDEPENDENT BOARD COULD BE TASKED WITH CONDUCTING A THOROUGH RESEARCH AND
REVIEW PROCESS, ULTIMATELY RESULTING IN A FINAL RECOMMENDATION TO THE COUNTY OF
THE OPTIMAL FORM OF GOVERNMENT FOR UTAH COUNTY’S LONG-TERM FUTURE.

- Tanner Ainge, Utah County Commissioner —

COMMISSIONER AINGE INTRODUCES THE REASON FOR THIS WORK SESSION. UNDER
STATE STATUTE, COUNTIES ARE CAPABLE OF RESEARCHING FORMS OF
GOVERNMENT TO CHANGE INTO. IN JANUARY, A GROUP OF PETITIONS ARE GOING TO
BEGIN CIRCULATING A PETITION TO CHANGE THE FORM OF GOVERNMENT.
COMMISSIONER AINGE PREFERS TO RESEARCH THE OPTIONS OF A NEW FORM OF
GOVERNMENT OPPOSED TO CIRCULATING A PETITION WITHOUT THE PROPER
RESEARCH.

COMMISSIONER AINGE WANTS TO CREATE A COMMITTEE/INDEPENDENT BOARD TO
PERFORM THE RESEARCH AND PRODUCE AN ANALYSIS TO SUBMIT TO THE BOARD OF
COMMISSIONERS. STATES THAT THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS HAVE A CONFLICT
OF INTEREST WITH CHANGING THE FORM OF GOVERNMENT AND BELIEVES THAT AN
INDEPENDENT BOARD WILL ADDRESS ANY POTENTIAL CONFLICTS OF INTEREST.

COMMISSIONER IVIE ACKNOWLEDGES THAT HE IS A SIGNER ON THE PETITION AND
BELIEVES THAT THE PETITION THAT IS BEING CIRCULATED HAS BEEN THOROUGHLY
STUDIED AND FALLS IN LINE WITH THE INTENT OF THE FOUNDERS OF THE UNITED
STATES.

COMMISSIONER LEE EXPRESSES SUPPORT OF CHANGING THE FORM OF
GOVERNMENT. STATES THAT THE STUDY PROCESS IS NECESSARY BECAUSE WE NEED
TO BETTER UNDERSTAND THE PRO’S AND CON’S OF EACH FORM OF GOVERNMENT.

PAUL JONES, COUNTY ATTORNEY’S OFFICE: STATES THE RECOMMENDATION BY THE
COMMISSION COULD BE SOUGHT AFTER IF THE PETITION THAT IS BEING CIRCULATED
FAILES TO BE PLACED ON THE BALLOT DUE TO LACK OF NECESSARY SIGNATURES.
PAUL INFORMS THE COMMISSION THAT AN AMENDMENT TO THE PLAN SUBMITTED
WITH THE PETITIONS CAN BE SUBMITTED. BUT THERE ARE QUESTIONS WAS TO
WHETHER OR NOT THE DEPTH OF ANY POTENTIAL AMENDMENTS.

COMMISSIONER AINGE ASKS WHETHER OR NOT A COMMITTEE CAN BE FORMED
WHILE THE PETITION IS BEING CIRCULATED. PAUL JONES, CONFIRMS THAT NO
COMMITTEE CAN BE FORMED WHILE THE PETITION IS BEING CIRCULATED.

AMELIA POWERS, UTAH COUNTY CLERK/AUDITOR: STATES THAT THE PETITION
PACKETS HAVE NOT BEEN DISTRIBUTED TO THE PETITION GATHERERS AS OF TODAY
DUE TO INCONSISTENCIES SUBMITTED TO THE CLERK/AUDITOR’S OFFICE ON THE
PETITION PLAN.

BRIAN CHAPMAN, CONTACT FOR PETITION SPONSORS: INFORMS THE COMMISSION
THAT THEY HAVE BEEN WAITING FOR DIRECTION FROM THE CLERK/AUDITOR
