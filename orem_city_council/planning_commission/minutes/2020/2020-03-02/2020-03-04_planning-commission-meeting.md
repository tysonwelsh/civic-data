Easement for Lots 11 and 12 Legacy Ridge, Plat A and approving Legacy Ridge, Plat F located generally at 68 West
2070 South in the R8 zone.

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by Development Services to request the City amend Section 14-3-3 Freestanding
Signs: Pole & Monument (7) by clarifying language and Wall Signs of the Orem City Code by adding the PO zone
as a permitted zone and amending the Sign Zone Map of the City of Orem by applying Sign Zone D to properties
located generally at 825 East, 950 East, 1020 East, 1040 East, 1068 East, and 1145 East 800 North.

                                          Staff Presentation: The first change would amend Section 14-3-3
                                          Freestanding Signs (Pole & Monument) to specify that both commercial
                                          and residential parcels located outside of the established sign zones adhere
                                          to sign zone D standards subject to the specific criteria outlined in the
                                          amendment.

                                             The second change would update the language within the sign code
                                             allowing wall signs in the PO zone. Current sign code does not allow wall
signs in the Professional Office (PO) zone and was found while researching for a previous zone (CM zone) that did
not allow wall signs. In the past, the City has approved wall signs within the PO zone.

The third change would amend the Sign Zone Map of the City of Orem by applying Sign Zone D to properties
located generally at 825 East, 950 East, 1020 East, 1040 East, 1068 East, and 1145 East 800 North. These properties
are zoned from PD 4 to PO and adding them to sign zone D would standardize commercial and office properties on
the east end of 800 North.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council by
amending Section 14-3-3 Freestanding Signs (Pole & Monument) by clarifying language and Wall Signs of the
Orem City Code by adding the PO zone as a permitted zone and amending the Sign Zone Map of the City of Orem
by applying Sign Zone D to properties located generally at 825 East, 950 East, 1020 East, 1040 East, 1068 East, and
1145 East 800 North.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Mr. Willardson.

Chair Cochran opened the public hearing and invited those from the audience who had come to speak to this item to
come forward to the microphone.

When no one came forward, Chair Cochran closed the public hearing and asked if the Planning Commission had any
more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did, he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Cook moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council by
amending Section 14-3-3 Freestanding Signs (Pole & Monument) by clarifying language and Wall Signs of the
Orem City Code by adding the PO zone as a permitted zone and amending the Sign Zone Map of the City of Orem
by applying Sign Zone D to properties located generally at 825 East, 950 East, 1020 East, 1040 East, 1068 East, and
1145 East 800 North. Mr. Sakar seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille
Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar and Ross Spencer. The motion passed unanimously.
