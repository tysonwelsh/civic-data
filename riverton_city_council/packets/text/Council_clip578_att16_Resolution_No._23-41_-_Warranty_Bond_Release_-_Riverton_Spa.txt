                                   RIVERTON, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-41

      RESOLUTION APPROVING RIVERTON CITY’S RELEASE OF BOND TO
       GUARANTEE PUBLIC IMPROVEMENTS WITHIN THE Riverton Spa and
                  Salon (9839) DEVELOPMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Marc Brimhall (“Developer”) has engaged in the development of a
subdivision in the vicinity of 3703 West Market Center Drive Riverton, Utah, known as the
Riverton Spa and Salon (9839) Subdivision

       WHEREAS, Developer is required by Riverton City Code § 17.10.030 to post a bond to
guarantee construction of dedicated improvements in a manner satisfactory to Riverton City
standards and specifications;

        WHEREAS, upon completion of the project, 90% of Developer’s bond value is required
to be released, with the remaining 10% retained to warrant the workmanship of the dedicated
improvements for a period of one year following acceptance of same, pursuant to a bond
agreement with developer and Riverton City Code § 17.10.030; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council accepted the Riverton Spa and Salon (9839)
Subdivision improvements on the date of 2022Apr19, which triggered the aforementioned 90%
bond release immediately thereafter; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to inspection reports provided by City staff, the Riverton City
Council finds and determines that the dedicated improvements within the Riverton Spa and
Salon (9839 Subdivision have remained in a state of good condition which comports with
Riverton City standards and specifications for one year following acceptance; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council further finds and determines that releasing the
remaining 10% of Developer’s bond held to warrant the subdivision improvements is proper;


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:


   1. The City Manager is authorized to direct staff to release the remaining 10% of
      Developer’s bond held by Riverton to warrant the aforesaid improvements for one year
      following acceptance of same, in accordance with Riverton City standards and
      specifications.
   2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
