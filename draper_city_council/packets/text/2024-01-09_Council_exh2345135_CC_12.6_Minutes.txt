7.h   Action Item: Ordinance #1597, an Ordinance of the Draper City Council
      amending regulations for winter street parking
      City Manager David Dobbins explained that the current ordinance prohibited parking
      on streets when snow was on the ground. He shared the concerns of both residents
      and snow plow drivers and asked if Councilmembers were interested in amending
      regulations to prohibit overnight parking on streets during winter months.

      Councilmember T. Lowery said she liked the flexibility provided by language drafted
      by staff, particularly the prohibition of on-street parking between 12:00 am and 6:00
      am. Councilmember Green said he was uncomfortable with the use of the word
      “highway” in the document to refer to any street. It was suggested that “or street” be
      added after “highway” to add clarity.

      Councilmember Vawdrey moved to approve Ordinance #1597 with the addition
      of the words “or street”. Councilmember Green seconded the motion.
