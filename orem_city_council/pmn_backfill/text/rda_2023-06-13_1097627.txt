RDA CONSENT ITEMS
Approval of Meeting Minutes for May 9, 2023
Mr. Lambson moved to approve the May 9, 2023 minutes. Mr. Peterson seconded the

motion. Those voting yes: David Young, David Spencer, Debby Lauret, LaNae Millett, Tom
Macdonald, Terry Peterson and Jeff Lambson. The motion passed.

RDA SCHEDULED ITEMS

Public Hearing: Approving and Adopting the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Budget for the

Redevelopment Agency (RDA) of the City of Orem Presenter: Brandon Nelson, Finance

Director

Mr. Nelson presented the RDA Budget for FY 2023-2024. The $1,665,000 for FY
2023-2024 is all University Place RDA/CDA. There are no longer any money coming from the
old RDAs.

Public Hearing opened at 7:39 pm
No Comments

Mr. Macdonald recommended that the board of directors of the Redevelopment Agency
approve and adopt the fiscal year 2023-2024 budget and amend the fiscal year 2023-2024
budget. Mr.Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting yes: David Young, David Spencer,
Debby Lauret, LaNae Millett, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson and Jeff Lambson. The motion
passed.

ADJOURN TO A MEETING OF THE OREM SPECIAL SERVICE LIGHTING
DISTRICT (SSLD)

District (SSLD), seconded by Mr. Spencer.Those voting yes: David Young, David Spencer,
Debby Lauret, LaNae Millett, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson and Jeff Lambson. The motion

passed.
