Nephi City
 Work Session Minutes
October 26, 2021
 7:00 p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers
	


	•	Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they would be in favor of offering long-term leases at the airport.  Scot Noll the FOB  of the airport stated that 20-year leases are more common at airports.  The council agreed to support longer term  airport leases.

	•	 Mike Sperry addressed the council about a development up the canyon.  He is developing seven building lots that will be in the county.  He will be drilling a well for water and according to the experts the best place to dig the well is on the property line city owned property and the Sperry property.  He requested to purchase of be issued an easement on the city owned property for source protection requirements of the well.  He needs about 100 feet for a well zone protection easement.  Mr. Sperry also asked if the city could put a sign up in the area to discourage dumping of trash.

	•	Nephi City Chamber of Commerce President Abby Ivory addressed the council about requesting their support of hiring a part-time executive director for the Chamber to make the Chamber more successful.  She stated that leadership in the Chamber gets burned out and the membership is decreasing.  She stated that the City benefits from local spending and an active Chamber and business community could bring in more sales tax revenue.  There are many new businesses that do not have a web presence.  She thought that maybe the Chamber could partner with the school to have student help businesses develop a web presence.  The Chamber needs to be a voice for the business community to the council in legislation that affects them.  Travis Worwood stated that maybe the Travel Council could partner with the Chamber and share office space. 

	•	The council discussed the possibility of changing the Humphrey property on Highway 132 to a level 1 on the annexation area map.   

	•	The council discussed a UDOT mill and fill project along Highway 132 from the fairgrounds to the middle freeway exit.  The project provides an opportunity to refurbish several drainage ways that meet with 100 North.  UDOT has provide a cost for these improvements and the City could pay for this project out of the Street Improvements line in the Capital  Projects Fund.  The council agreed to get back with UDOT in favor of joining the project and to bring back the agreement in the November 2, 2021, council meeting. 

	•	The council gave various updates.

	•	Meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
