Marisol Rodriguez, Insignia Partners, stated the MSD hired her to help develop a strategic
plan that will help drive the future of the organization. MSD Board Members and leadership have been
interviewed and work groups have been created to work on strategies. A postcard will be sent to residents
that the MSD serves, to get their feedback. She will also be conducting focus groups with each Metro
Township to get its perspective.
The Council had discussions on the following questions:

What would you say are the biggest concerns for your community?
What are some opportunities you see for your community right now?
What does your community need the most help with from the MSD within the near term?
What matters the most to you when it comes to long term success?
Are there other services, programs, activities, that you would like to see the MSD take on to better
meet your community needs?
Do you have anything else you want to see from the MSD?
What do you think the MSD is doing really well to achieve the goals of the mission statement in your
community?
What other weaknesses would you name with the MSD or how things are working?
How do you feel the MSD is doing at engaging your residents and what additionally would you like
them to do to better to engage Emigration Canyon residents?
What would be one piece of advice that you would offer to the group that is creating the strategic
plan?
