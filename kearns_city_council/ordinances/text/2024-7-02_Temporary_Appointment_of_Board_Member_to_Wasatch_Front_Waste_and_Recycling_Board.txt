KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
RESOLUTION NO. 2024-7-02 DATE: July 8, 2024
A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL APPROVING A TEMPORARY
APPOINTMENT OF BOARD MEMBER TO WASATCH FRONT WASTE AND
RECYCLING BOARD

WHEREAS, the City of Kearns (“Kearns”) is a municipality within the service area of
Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (“‘“WFWRD”); and

WHEREAS, Kearns has one elected official from the Kearns City Council appointed to
represent Kearns on the WF WRD Board; and

WHEREAS, the current Kearns WFWRD Board member is Patrick Schaeffer; and

WHEREAS, Patrick Schaeffer is taking an extended leave of absence effective
immediately; and

WHEREAS, Kearns City Council seeks to appoint Kelly Bush as a temporary WF WRD
Board Member in Patrick Schaeffer’s place until such time as he returns from his leave of
absence or his term ends, whichever is sooner.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that by the Kearns City Council that effective
immediately:

1. Kelly Bush will act as a temporary replacement for Patrick Schaeffer on the
WFEWRD Board.

2 The replacement will take effect immediately after the approval and adoption
of this Resolution.

3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately after adoption.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 8th day of July, 2024.

KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
