ADMINISTRATIVE LAND USE APPLICATION(S) 
Meeting began at – 8:38 a.m. 
EXP2022-000676 - Scott Mortensen is requesting a non-conforming use determination for a detached guest 
house located in the rear of his property. Location: 870 East Vics Road. Zone: R-1-8 (Single Family 
Residential). Planner: Morgan Julian Motion/Voting 
 
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting 
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.  
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services D istrict Planner Morgan Julian provided an analysis of the staff 
report. 
 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the remodel and remodel permit. Mr. Shaw said 
the application is for a special exception  to declare legal , not a non-conforming use. Guest house is a 
different use than a dwelling unit and subject to different ordinances. Mr. Shaw read the special exception 
criteria and evidence submitted is continuous use for 10 years as a guest house without complaint and not 
detrimental to health, safety, or welfare. 
 
Speaker # 1: Applicant 
Name: Scott Mortensen 
Address: 8423 Town View Circle, West Jordan 
Comments: Mr. Mortensen said when they did renovation, it was just cosmetics-- replaced toilet, water 
heater, and two gas meters. Door was put back up and flooring and paint  done. The swimming pool was 
buried and is not there any longer. He has owned the property two years. 
 
Speaker # 2: Neighbor 
Name: Joanna Wuertz 
Address: 860 East Vics Road 
Comments: Ms. Wuertz said she doesn’t have anything to add. She is a neighbor and only attended the 
meeting but has no comments. 
 
Motion: To approve application #EXP2022-000676 for Scott Mortensen requesting a special exception 
determination for a detached guest house located in the rear of his property with staff recommendations. 
Motion by: Commissioner Watkins 
2nd by: Commissioner Vance 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
