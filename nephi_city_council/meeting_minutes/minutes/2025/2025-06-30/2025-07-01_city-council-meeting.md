PUBLIC COMMENT
   • Jacque Thorn, marketing manager for the Nephi Farmer’s market, discussed the importance of
       the market and its impact on the community. With the Ute Stampede next week, the farmer’s
       market was going to move to the Old Mill Park. There were concerns from the Ute Stampede
       committee mentioned at city council meeting last week. Belle Brough, the craft fair organizer,
       was concerned about the farmer’s market taking away from the craft fair. Jacque said the
       purpose was not to compete, but to compliment and was working hard to make the Farmer’s
       Market a consistent and regular Saturday event.
   • Morgan Clark, supporting Nephi farmer’s market, stated that he is a small business owner and
       the Farmer’s market has allowed him to get out and interact with people. Allowing the farmer’s
       market the week of the stampede would allow vendors trying to be entrepreneurs to see people
       outside of our community to help grow their business.
   • Natalie Jacobson declared her support for the Farmer’s market and the opportunity the rodeo
       will give them to meet new people and engage in the community.
   • Monica Izatt stated that she participates in the Farmer’s Market as a small business. Being able
       to participate during the stampede would allow them to add to the festivities of the week and
       get more out in the community.
   • Brette Hawks praised Jacque for her hard work with the Farmer’s Market. She mentioned that
       those who come seem to have a good time and the vendors put in a lot of work. She stated
       when the health inspector came he was impressed with the permitting and licensing and
       following the rules. She expressed a desire for the opportunity to be part of the Ute Stampede.
   • Seth Atkinson stated he had received a call from Meadow Perides earlier that day expressing her
       support for the Farmer’s market.

UTAH PATRIOT PARTNER PRESENTATION—
       Lisa Duckworth introduced the Utah Patriot Partnership program which was started by Governor
       Herbert in 2011. She works with the Department of Workforce Services and this program is
       designed to support veterans and military families in employment. The program offers priority
       employment consideration for veterans, military members, and their spouses. Those who
       partner with the Utah Patriot Program have a network for marketing, the ability to earn awards
       and agree to help the program do more to help veterans find employment following service.
       Mayor Seely mentioned several individuals at Nephi city who are veterans: Seth Atkinson (City
       Administrator), Army; Paul (police department), Marine Corps; Howard Bonzo (spouse to library
        director), Army; Mike Morgan (Chief of Police), Air Force; Ryan Worwood, Army; and Skip
        Worwood (Councilman), Navy. Both the Mayor and Council members are in support of becoming
        a partner with the Utah Patriot Program.



NO PLANNING ITEMS

CERTIFICATION OF PROPERTY TAX RATE
       Seth Atkinson explained that the property tax rate decreased to .000504 due to increased
       assessed value.
       Councilor JD Parady made a motion to accept the property rate of .000504. Councilor Jeramie
       Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 07-01-2025 FIREWORKS RESTRICTIONS
      Seth Atkinson presented the resolution 07-01-2025 encouraging the prohibition of the discharge
      of fireworks on the east side of Interstate 15 to reduce the potential for damage costly forest and
      range fires.
      Councilor Travis Worwood made the motion to approve Resolution 07-01-2025. Councilor Shari
      Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP CHANGE
      Mr. Atkinson stated that following the addition of area being added to the annexation map last
      week, it was requested that the area where the Agri Park is planning to go be changed from a
      level 3 to a level 2 to undergo analysis. This is an extensive development, and the analysis may
      take a month or more so they can be very thorough in what they present. Mr. Atkinson
      announced that an Agri park representative will be speaking at the Chamber luncheon this
      month.
      Councilor JD Parady motioned to approve the annexation are change from a level three to a level
      two. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.
