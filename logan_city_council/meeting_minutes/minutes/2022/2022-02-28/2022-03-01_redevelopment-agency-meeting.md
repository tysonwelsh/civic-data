The Logan Redevelopment Agency convened on Tuesday, March 1, 2022, at 7:23 pm, in
the Logan Municipal Council Chambers located at 290 North 100 West, Logan, Utah
84321. Logan Municipal Council Meetings are televised live as a public service on
Channel 17 and the City of Logan YouTube channel at:
https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCFLPAOK5eawKS_RDBU0stRQ

Council Members present at the beginning of the meeting: Chair Jeannie F. Simmonds,
Vice Chair Ernesto López, Councilmember Amy Z. Anderson, Councilmember Amy Z.
Anderson, Councilmember Tom Jensen, and Councilmember Mark A. Anderson.
Administration present: Mayor Holly H. Daines, Assistant City Attorney Craig Carlston,
Finance Director Richard Anderson, Economic Development Director Kirk Jensen, City
Excused: City Attorney Kymber Housley.
Chair Jeannie F. Simmonds welcomed those present. There were approximately 7 in
attendance at the beginning of the meeting.

WORKSHOP ITEM:

Budget Adjustment FY 2021-2022 appropriating: $65,000 funds for the construction
of a new Ellen Eccles Theatre cooling system canal diversion at 50 West 100 South;
$2,150,000 donations received for the Center Block Plaza Project – Resolution 22-08
RDA – Kirk Jensen, Economic Development Director

              Logan Municipal Council Minutes ~ Logan, Utah ~ March 1, 2022
Economic Development Director Kirk Jensen addressed the Council regarding the
proposed budget adjustments. The reason and need for the canal diversion are that the
Mill Creek project Phase 1 has ended and will enter Phase 2. Part of the construction will
impact the existing diversion per a 1992 Easement Agreement that the city entered. It is
the City's obligation to fund the expense in the instance there is an impact on the existing
diversion which is occurring at present.

Vice Chair Simmonds was uncertain as to the rationale or reason for the city to enter into
such an agreement.

Mr. Jensen replied that the rationale behind the decision is that the ground was
undeveloped at that time and that the canal directly benefitted the city.

Chair Simmonds clarified for the public that all donations received for the Center Block
Plaza Project will be used for the project. However, any remaining funds from Center
Block will go back into the RDA funds to be used for other projects.

Councilmember M. Anderson asked how much of a percentage is the donation for the
plaza project.

Finance Director Rich Anderson responded that roughly 50% of the donations make up
the plaza's budget.

The proposed resolution will be an action item and public hearing at the March 15, 2022,
Council meeting.

ADJOURNED. There being no further business, the Logan Redevelopment Agency
adjourned at 7:28 pm.
