      A.     LIFFERTH SHORT-TERM RENTAL, PLZ-23-2017, A CONDITIONAL USE
             PERMIT FOR A SHORT-TERM RENTAL HOME OCCUPATION LOCATED
             AT 13259 SOUTH FOREST MEADOW DRIVE. APPLICANT - MONICA
             LIFFERTH.

Chair Matheson opened the public hearing.

City Planner Lisa Halversen presented the Staff Report and stated that the above item is a
request for a short-term rental within a detached garage located at 13259 South Forest
Meadow Drive. The property is zoned RR-22 and measures .50-acre. The home has an
attached three-car garage with a detached garage and a fenced backyard.

Ms. Halversen reported that the home has two drive approaches and driveways. The access
onto South Forest Meadow Drive leads to the attached garage and driveway, while the
access onto Viola Lane leads to parking in front of a detached garage. A property rendering
was displayed. The detached garage is approximately 1,200 square feet in size on the main
floor with a finished loft area of approximately 500 square feet. Parking will be located in
the driveway outside the detached garage. Staff received one public comment expressing
concern about the short-term rental ordinance in general.

Commissioner Rushton’s understanding was that the State put out Housing Enabling
Legislation and asked if requiring cities to allow these types of requests fall under Accessory
Dwelling Units (“ADU”). Ms. Halversen explained that ADUs are allowed in any residential
area but if the intent is to rent short-term, a Conditional Use Permit is required.

Commissioner Park was concerned with a short-term renter being unaware of the property
address in the event of an emergency. He asked if the rental would be required to have an
address that is separate from the primary dwelling. City Planner, Tim Prestwich confirmed
that the addressing concern could be included as a condition that the property be addressed
following the ADU guidelines.

Chair Matheson pointed out that all residents within 300 feet are required to have the
property owner’s contact information who should be available if and when issues arise.

City Attorney, Ryan Carter stated that contact information should be circulated to allow the
neighbors to call the owner if there are any concerns regarding behavior. He encouraged
concerned residents to first call the Police Department in these situations, which would
initiate a record of a nuisance and give the City what it needs to enforce the Ordinance by
revoking the Conditional Use Permit.

The applicant, Monica Lifferth, reported that she moved into the home one year ago and
believed that a short-term rental would help her family with finances. She works as a
schoolteacher and is a 20-year retired veteran. They want to be good neighbors and keep
their children safe. She stated that there is an option on the Airbnb platform where settings
allow the owner to vet each person who is requesting to rent the property.

Commissioner Park asked the applicant what she has found to be the likely demand for the
property. Ms. Lifferth stated that there is a Riverton family with a short-term rental that is

Jack Clark identified himself as the closest neighbor to the property. He previously shared

      C.     "SYCAMORE GLEN TOWNHOMES," PLZ-22-8011, A SITE PLAN FOR TEN
             NEW TOWNHOMES TO BE LOCATED ON .87-ACRES AT 11833 SOUTH
             REDWOOD ROAD. APPLICANT - BROOKE ANDERSON.

Chair Matheson opened the public hearing.

Mr. Prestwich asked the Commission to continue the public hearing for the above item to
the next Regular Planning Commission Meeting in order for the applicant to be present. The
request is a Site Plan review of 10 new townhomes on .87-acres located at 11833 South
Redwood Road. The Suburban Land Reserve (“SLR”) Specific Development District
(“SDD”) was adopted by the City in January 2016 and includes a unique three-step approval
process for development. The Master Site Plan was approved in 2022 with Parcel A being
a future phase. A property rendering was displayed. The delay in the item returning to the
Commission was due to the applicant trying to work with the Utah Department of
Transportation (“UDOT”), which was unsuccessful. They intend to proceed with the
permitted townhomes. The property details were reviewed. Each unit includes a two-car
garage plus two-car driveway parking. The sidewalk is located on one side. Mr. Prestwich
reported that the matter will return with a response from the applicant and a motion for
approval at that time.

In response to a question raised by Commissioner Rushton, Mr. Prestwich confirmed that
notices were sent to the residents of Melba Lane.

With regard to drainage, the pipe that runs across the canal is an underdrain to the homes
to the east. A project is in place to replace the pipe with a public system to mitigate any
flooding or water beneath the homes.

Bill Ellingson reported that he has lived on Melba Lane for 57 years and expressed concern
with the entrance into the proposed townhomes. He also had safety concerns with the road
divider to the north and south with a divider in the middle. He stated that there are times
when they exit Melba Lane onto Redwood Road and were nearly hit by motorists making a
U-Turn.

There were no further public comments. Chair Matheson closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Park moved the Planning Commission CONTINUE PLZ-22-8011
“Sycamore Glen Townhomes,” Final Site Plan to the next regularly scheduled
Planning Commission Meeting. Commissioner Rushton seconded the motion. The
motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Commission.

Ms. Halversen presented the Staff Report and stated that the business will be co-located

with the Tropical Smoothie that is currently under construction. It was the intent of the
developer to have both on one parcel. A property rendering was displayed. The overall
parcel is 0.89 acres in size and zoned Commercial Regional (“C-R”) as are the three
properties to the west. All other surrounding properties are in the SLR Specific Plan.

The building is a small 604-square-foot building with no customer seating. It has a single
drive-thru window and features a lower course of stone with an upper wall of stucco and
metal access panels. The roofline is varied in height with a maximum height of 19 feet with
exterior awnings. The drive-thru has double stacking at the start leading to the pre-order
menu boards for at least eight cars and then stacking for at least eight more cars to the order

comply with all City Ordinances. Landscaping details were reviewed.

Commissioner Park referenced employee parking to the north and asked if the turf area is
intended for patrons to congregate. Staff confirmed that the coffee shop is a drive-thru only.
The area has two-way access with potential stacking for 20 cars or more with a right-in and
right-out on 13400 South. The Tropical Smoothie Project overpacked parking in anticipation
that stalls would be used in conjunction with the coffee shop.

Allison Albert from Benchmark Engineering stated that they were contracted to design both
pads and confirmed that all of the concrete area is Americans with Disabilities Act (“ADA”)
compliant.

The applicant, Gregory Ford, stated that this is his first commercial business and shared
excitement with the proposal. He recently returned from Cost Rica and stated that the coffee
is excellent and responsibly sourced. The shop has drive-thru access with a walk-up window
with no customers inside the facility. Picnic tables will be placed in the landscape area with
a targeted consumer of moms on the go.

Cody Saar reported that he owns the project and the Tropical Smoothie Café. It was his
original intent to have a coffee shop as they are busier in the mornings with the Tropical
Smoothie business being busier in the afternoons. He hoped the project to the east would
carry through and provide cross-access to mitigate traffic concerns.

       B.     "THE VILLAGE AT SOUTH MOUNTAIN WEST," PAD A, LOT 1, PLZ-23-
              8002, DETAIL PLAN FOR COMMERCIAL MULTI-TENANT DEVELOPMENT
              LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 4158 WEST 13400 SOUTH. APPLICANT
              - AWA ENGINEERING.

Mr. Prestwich presented the Staff Report and stated that the above item is a Final Plan for
Phase 2 of a Commercial Multi-Tenant Development in an SLR/SDD Zone located at 4158
West 13400 South. The Community-Scale Plan/Block Scale Plan (“CSP/BSP”) was
approved by the Planning Commission in June of 2022 with the Detailed Plan for the
subdivision, main roads, and backbone infrastructure being approved in March 2023. The
building is 9,844 square feet in size on 1.552 acres with right-in and right-out access onto
13400 South and full access to the shared private driveway to the north. The building is a
single-story with a drive-thru planned for the western side tenant space.

The site provides 75 parking stalls including four accessible stalls in front. There will be
stacking for four cars before the food window. The site features a waterwise Landscaping
Plan with 30 trees and plantings in a variety of ground covers with no sod. A Questar Gas
facility is located to the west with a six to eight-foot redbrick wall surrounding the site. The
existing fencing will remain in place with no other fencing required.
