Background

This is a public hearing to adjust the lot line between Lot 11 in the existing River Haven Estates
Phase 1 subdivision and Lot 19 in the River Haven Estates Phase 2 subdivision. River Haven
Estates Subdivision Phase 1 was approved by the city in 1979, with 18 residential building lots.
Phase 2 was recorded in 1984 and added 39 new lots. The subdivision is in the R-4 zone with
single-family homes on a minimum lot size of 10,000-sf.

The applicant, Ms. Cartaino, wishes to add on to the back of her home. In the process she
discovered that the long-established fence line was not the actual property line, and her addition
cannot meet the required setbacks without that section of property. The proposed plat
amendment would change the shared property line between Lot 11 and Lot 19 to follow the long
existing fence. No new lots will be created. Both resulting lots meet the minimum city
standards for lots in the R-4 Zone.

The applicant and her neighbor have been working together on this for some time, and they
have already signed and recorded a cross Quit Claim with Salt Lake County as the first step to
adjusting their common boundary to match the existing fence line. They will continue to work
together to get the plat signed and recorded.
