City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1642

On April 1, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1642 amending the text
of Title 7 and Title 9 of the Draper City Municipal Code Relating to Tree Maintenance.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available
online at https://www.draperutah.gov/.

Published this 2nd day of April 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
