           3.2 DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Final Fiscal Year 2023-2024 RDA Budget (Resolution
               U2023-05)
               Finance Director David Mortensen will present the Final Fiscal Year 2023-2024 RDA
               budget. The RDA Board will act to adopt (or deny) this request by resolution. (A public
               hearing was held on the tentative budget during the June 14, 2023 RDA meeting.)

            Chair Fullmer turned the time over to Finance Director David Mortensen.

          Mr. Mortensen reviewed the final budget. He gave an overview of the capital projects being
      contributed by the RDA.

          Chair Fullmer asked for an overview of the Mill Road design. Mr. Ghandour explained that
      these were for the pedestrian enhancement designs for Mill Road. A discussion ensued. Mr. Nair
      recommended to change it to say $75,000 for roadway design improvements. The discussion
      continued.
