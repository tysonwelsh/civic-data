                                     ORDINANCE NO. 1587

         AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING THE TEXT OF
         TITLE 6 CHAPTER 9 OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO
         SPECIAL EVENTS BUSINESS LICENSING


         WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to revise certain terms of the Draper City
Municipal Code to address provisions that become diminished in appropriateness, applicability,
or clarity;

      WHEREAS, Chapter 6-9 “Events Charging Admission” was adopted in 2016 and has not
been substantially revised since then;

        WHEREAS, the business license official and city staff have reviewed Chapter 6-9 with the
goal of updating it; and

        WHEREAS, the proposed amendments to Chapter 6-9 are in the best interest of the
health, safety and welfare of Draper City and its residents and businesses.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:


       Section 1.   Amendment. Chapter 9 of Title 6 of the Draper City Municipal Code is
hereby amended to read as set forth in Exhibit A.

        Section 2.    Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to correct
any punctuation, spelling, formatting, or similar errors prior to submitting this Ordinance to the
codifier.

        Section 3.    Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of
this Ordinance, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

       Section 4.     Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final
passage.

Chapter 9: Events Charging Admission or With Vendors Making Taxable Sales

6-9-010: EVENTS CHARGING ADMISSION:
Sporting, musical, or other spectator or participation events, shall furnish the city with
names, addresses, state tax collection numbers and city business licenses number of
all concessions and vendors making taxable sales at the event. Sporting, musical or
other spectator or participation events charging admissions shall be required to obtain a
city temporary use permit or special event permit and temporary business license and
shall pay the required license fee and any additional fee as set forth in the city's
consolidated fee schedule unless exempted by ordinance or other applicable local, state
or federal law. (Ord. 1187, 2-2-2016)

6-9-010: DEFINITIONS

SPECIAL EVENT BUSINESS LICENSE: Business license issued to the event organizer
which authorizes (1) the event organizer to hold the event; and (2) all vendors making
taxable sales to participate in the event.

EVENT: Any athletic event, City event, entertainment event, filming event, large scale
event, block party, political event, or outdoor sales event, whether held for profit,
nonprofit or charitable purposes held on private or public property within the city.
EVENT ORGANIZER: Individual or business in charge of administering the event.

6-9-020: EVENTS CHARGING ADMISSION OR HOSTING VENDORS:

A. A special event business license must be obtained before any event is held which
charges admission or where taxable sales occur. The event organizer shall apply for the
special event business license at least two weeks prior to the event on a form provided
by the city. The event organizer shall fill out the application in full and sign it as
verification under penalty of law that all information contained therein is true.

B. The event organizer shall pay the required license fee and any additional fee as set
forth in the city’s consolidated fee schedule unless exempted by ordinance or other
applicable local, state or federal law. No fee shall be assessed for a City event as
defined in section 15-4-030.

C. The special event business license may only be issued upon the submission of a
complete application, payment of any applicable license fee, the issuance of temporary
sales tax licenses by the Utah State Tax Commission to all applicable vendors, and
issuance of a special event permit to the event organizer unless exempted by section
15-4-040.

D. The certificate of license shall be signed by the license official and the mayor.
