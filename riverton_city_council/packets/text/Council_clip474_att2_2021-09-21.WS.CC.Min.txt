46          Discuss COVID-19 Employee Related Programs – Councilmember McCay
47
48   Councilmember McCay said that many women have been affected by the COVID-19 pandemic
49   and especially in their employment. She said that she would like to know what Riverton City is
50   doing to support women in the workplace. She said she had talked to Trish Dixon, Human

 1   Resource Director, and wanted to find ways to allow more flexibility for women and possibly
 2   allow paid maternity leave.
 3
 4   Councilmember Stewart said that short term disability covers maternity leave in his workplace.
 5   Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director, said that the City currently has short term and
 6   long term disability. He said that short term disability starts after 1 week of being gone from
 7   work and pays at 60% and the other 40% can be supplemented by the employee. He said the
 8   disability paychecks are tax free.

10   Mayor Staggs said that the Councilmembers could propose a change to these policies. Mr.
11   Hicks suggested that the Councilmembers implement maternity or paternity leave. Mr. Hicks
12   said that estimated cost for these benefits is very hard.

14   Councilmember Buroker said the City should examine the current policies and decide what
15   benefits to offer so the City will be able to compete to have the best employees in Riverton.
16   Councilmember McDougal said the City needs to consider that employees are wearing multiple
17   hats and doing multiple jobs.
18
19   Mr. Hicks said that from a financial standpoint it is easy to say that we should offer these
20   benefits. Mayor Staggs said that it is hard to compare the benefits of working in the private
21   sector versus working in the public sector.
22
23   Mayor Staggs said that he believes the Councilmembers are directing staff to look at benefits
24   and make recommendations on changes that might make the City more attractive and then
25   bring it back to the Council for consideration. Councilmember McCay said that she would like
26   them to present a couple different options. She said that she wanted to add in a job sharing
27   option where two part time employees could be hired to perform one job.
28
29   Mr. Hicks asked the Councilmembers when they would like to see the options. Councilmember
30   McCay said she would like it sooner than later. Councilmember McDougal said that he would
31   like to have a bigger, broader discussion in January. Councilmember Stewart said he believes
32   this is a strategic discussion and it should be discussed in January. Mayor Staggs said that the
33   majority would like it brought back in January.

 4            Signage Discussion (12600 S. Redwood Road) – Councilmember McCay
 5
 6   Councilmember McCay said that she would like to see the Silverwolf sign on Spirit Corner stay.
 7   She said that she has enjoyed that Riverton City supports Riverton High School and she
 8   believes there will be push back from residents if the sign is removed.
 9
10   Councilmember Wells said that when he originally heard that the City wanted to move the
11   Silverwolf sign, he was against it but that was from a logistic standpoint. He said that he walked
12   the area with Councilmember Buroker and said that this was not maintained as well as it
13   should’ve been. He asked why the high school has not been maintaining this corner.
14
15   Councilmember McDougal explained the history of Spirit Corner. He said that this corner was
16   owned by UDOT but not maintained. He said that Mayor Applegarth decided that this should be
17   a Spirit Corner and the senior class at Riverton High School donated the sign. He said that
18   there is no parking by Spirit Corner and because it is not located on school grounds, it is hard
19   for the students or parents to maintain it. He said that he is supportive of it being moved to
20   school grounds where it can be utilized more.
21
22   Councilmember Stewart said this sign is on private property and he is concerned that this is not
23   part of the school. He said that the City needs to support all of the high schools in the City. He
24   said the Hope Walk should include all high schools and bring the schools together on a common
25   theme.
26
27   Mayor Staggs discussed the history of Spirit Corner. He said that he would like this corner to be
28   another way to communicate with the residents through a digital sign. He asked if there would
29   be an opportunity to get a grant to improve the corner. He said he does not believe this corner
30   needs to be part of the first phase of the signage installations.
31
32   Councilmember McDougal said he was concerned about spending money on this corner when
33   the City does not own this land. Ryan Carter, City Attorney, said he does not believe UDOT will
34   ever sale this land to the City because they know that someday the road will need to be
35   expanded and this is part of their long term traffic plan.
36
37   Mayor Staggs said that he asked Casey Saxton, Communications Director, to remove the
38   placement of a major monument on this corner from the plans for now. Councilmember McCay
39   asked what the cost would be to move the sign to the high school. David Brickey, City
40   Manager, said he did not have a dollar amount for moving the sign. Mayor Staggs said that he
41   has asked Mr. Saxton to come back with another plan for the corner that will be minimal.
42

28            Riverton Police Department Presentation – Chief Don Hutson
29
30   Chief Don Hutson said that Councilmember McCay delivered cards and notes to the police
31   department and he thanked her for coordinating this.       He said that the Emergency
32   Preparedness Coordinator, Jason Jones, has been presenting neighborhood watch and
33   emergency preparedness to various neighborhoods around the City.
34
35   Chief Hutson said that the South Jordan, Riverton and Herriman SWAT team competed in a
36   SWAT competition and were able to place 3rd out of 16 other teams. He said that Riverton
37   SWAT team has been called out to other jurisdictions and been able to help with various
38   incidents.
39
40   Chief Hutson said that he created a heat map which shows the location of all the vehicle
41   burglaries that occurred in Riverton City. He said this month was the most burglaries he has
42   ever seen. He said they are analyzing evidence in these cases and looking for ways to stop the
43   burglaries from happening.
44
45   Chief Hutson said that VECC has redone their phone system and is constantly in a battle to
46   keep good employees. He said that VECC has improved on emergency calls but non-
47   emergency calls have been neglected. Councilmember Stewart agreed with Chief Hutson’s
48   analysis of the data from VECC. He said he would like to have a work session focused on
49   VECC. Mayor Staggs said that VECC should be full service and believes the City is paying a lot
50   of money and not receiving quality service.
