Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Special Meeting
From: Russ Fox, Assistant City Manager
CC:  Date: March 19, 2020
Re:  Comments: In an effort to assist Draper City businesses and residents in Draper during the declared emergency due to COVID-19, Ordinance No. 1430 is being proposed to suspend Subsections 9-26-080(D)(3) and (4) of the Draper City Municipal Code. Currently, businesses can obtain a temporary sign permit for banner signs four (4) times a year for 14 days or once for 30 days with a new business license. This temporary land use regulation will allow Draper City businesses to obtain a temporary sign permit for banner signs without any time limits for either 6 months or until the ordinance is rescinded by the City Council.    The findings are as follows: 1. A local emergency is declared related to the spread of COVID-19 which is having a major impact on Draper City businesses and residents.
2. The City Council finds there is a compelling, countervailing public interest to preserve the economic base of Draper City. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ordinance 1430 3/18/2020 Cover Memo
