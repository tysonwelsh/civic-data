Council Business
Title 2, Chapter 2.56 – Community Districts & Community Councils Ordinance
The Council reviewed Title 2 – Administration and Personnel, Chapter 2.56 –
Community Districts and Community Councils. Further review and amendments need to be made
to the ordinance and then discussed at a future meeting.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Fireworks Restrictions Resolution
The Council reviewed its firework restriction resolution and decided to keep the
same restrictions in place.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission Members
The Council discussed changing the number of commissioners from seven to five
to serve on the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission. This item will be
discussed further at a future meeting.

Item was not discussed.
