                             WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING

A Public Hearing of the West Valley City Planning Commission will be held on Wednesday, November
   12, 2025, at 4:00 PM, in West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah. A
premeeting will be held at 3:30 PM. Members of the press and public are invited to attend in person
           or view this meeting live on Zoom at https://us06web.zoom.us/j/83896786911.

  Preliminary application information will be available by 6:00 PM on Tuesday, November 4, 2025 at
 www.wvc-ut.gov/PCAgendas. Staff analyses, as well as any final application materials, will be updated
                             by 12:00 PM on Monday, November 10, 2025.

 Testimonies for or against the proposed applications can be provided to the Planning Commission
 during the public hearing or in writing prior to the hearing. If you wish to submit written comments,
 be advised that this should be provided to the Planning Staff member assigned to the application at
least seven (7) days prior to the meeting to enable the Planning Commission to properly consider the
                                                comments.
