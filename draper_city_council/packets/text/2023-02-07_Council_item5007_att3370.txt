                         RESOLUTION NO. 23-08

 A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING THREE
 MEMBERS TO THE ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD FOR THE
 TRAVERSE RIDGE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. (UCA) Section 17-D-1-301 et seq.
Draper City has, by Ordinance No. 1124 and Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 3-1-290, created an administrative control board and delegated certain
powers thereto to administer the responsibilities of the Traverse Ridge Special
Service District; and

       WHEREAS, DCMC Section 3-1-290(e) requires the Administrative Control
Board be comprised of five members appointed by the Mayor with the advice
and consent of the City Council, and that each member shall be a registered
voter within the special service district or be an officer or employee of Draper
City; and

WHEREAS, the terms for Benjamin Frederick and David Augeson appointed
January 22, 2019, and Daryl Acumen, appointed June 16, 2020, as members of
the Administrative Control Board, has expired; and

      WHEREAS, pursuant to UCA Section 17B-1-304(2)(b) the City prepared a
notice of vacancy for these vacant board member positions, posted the notice
in four public places within the local district at least one month before the
deadline for accepting nominees for appointment, post the notice of vacancy
on the Utah Public Notice Website, and otherwise complied with all
requirements of law for filling vacancies; and

      WHEREAS, pursuant to UCA Section 17B-1-304 et seq. the City now
desires to appoint three members of the Administrative Control Board.

 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

 The following registered voters of the Traverse Ridge Special Service District,
are hereby appointed to serve on the Traverse Ridge Special Service District
Administrative Control Board for the term specified herein.
