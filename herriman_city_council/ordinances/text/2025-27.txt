HERRIMAN CITY
ORDINANCE NO. 2025-27

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING HERRIMAN CITY CODE § 1-6-8 TO REMOVE
OUTDATED REFERENCES TO THE CITY ORGANIZATIONAL CHART

WHEREAS, the Herriman City Code previously included the City’s organizational chart
within § 1-6-8.

WHEREAS, the City has transitioned to maintaining its organizational structure through
administrative processes rather than codified ordinance, providing greater flexibility to adapt to
staffing, reporting, and departmental changes.

WHEREAS, City Code § 1-6-8 no longer reflects the City’s current practices and may
create confusion for employees, elected officials, and the public.

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that removing this outdated section will promote
clarity and ensure the code accurately reflects present administrative procedures.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. Herriman City Code § 1-6-8 is hereby repealed in its
entirety. The section shall be reserved for future use. ©

SECTION 2. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
passage.

SECTION 3, Severability. If any part of this ordinance is held invalid by a court of
competent jurisdiction, the remainder shall continue in full force and effect.

SECTION 4. Repealer. All provisions of the Herriman City Code that are inconsistent
with this ordinance are hereby repealed to the extent of the inconsistency.

APPROVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH, ON THIS
10% DAY OF DECEMBER 2025.
