FASHION PLACE MALL — 6191 South State Street - Project #20-005

Mimi Olson was present to represent this request. Jared Hall reviewed the location and request
for Conditional Use Permit approval for to allow new project signage throughout the Fashion
Place Mall property, including Electronic Message Centers and multiple directional and branded
identification signs within the C-D Zone on the property located at 6191 South State. The
owners of the Fashion Place Mall have proposed a complete overhaul of the project signage
including 11 vehicular direction signs, new wall mounted letters for building entries, 6 new
monument signs, and two new pedestal signs (which are large, free-standing project signs.) The
proposed signage is unified by common themes that have been chosen to accentuate the brand
at Fashion Place, and to include common elements. The Fashion Place Mall is greater than 20-
acres which would allow the proposed changes to the Pedestal Signs according to the Murray
Land Use Ordinance, Section 17.48.140(H). The proposed is, for two large pedestal signs which
will serve as project signage to advertise key tenants and contain an EMC component, several
project-branded monument signs throughout the property for individual property owners and

tenants, as well as many project-branded directional signs. The EMC Component is allowed per
Section 17.48.280 of the Murray City Land Use code. The Murray City Land Use Ordinance

The Planning Commission is charged to consider four criteria in their review of Planned Center
Signs. The four criteria are:

e Signs must be integrated into the development with a common theme or integrated
architecturally with the buildings.

e Location, number, and size of all signs must receive approval by the Planning Commission
and meet the provisions within the Sign Code.

e Additional standards for pedestal signs are located within Section 17.48.140 of the Sign
Code.

Based on the analysis of the application materials and a survey of the surrounding area, staff
concludes that the proposed Planned Center signage with EMC components is allowed in the
C-D Zoning District subject to Planning Commission review and Conditional Use Permit
approval, that with conditions as outlined in the staff report, the proposed signage will comply
with the standards of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance and that the proposed use is in
harmony with the goals and objectives of the Murray City General Plan in this area and will
enhance an important community and regional destination. Staff recommends approval for
Condional Use Permit for the proposed signs as outlined in the application and staff report
subject to conditions.

Mimi Olson,106 Basin Park, Philadelphia, PA, stated she is representing this request for the
Applicant and wishes to comply completely with the sign code. Ms. Olson stated she has
reviewed the staff recommendations and will be able to meet the conditions of approval.

Ms. Milkavich asked if there are any dimming requirements for lighted signs besides the EMC
sign lighting. Mr. Hall replied that the language in code about dimming applies specifically to
EMC’s for changeable copy and would not necessarily apply to the lighting around the signs.

The meeting was open for public comment. No comments were made, and the public comment
portion was closed.

In lieu of Ms. Milkavich’s inquiry Mr. Woodbury asked if the lighting around the sign was
considered when the Sign Ordinance was written. Ms. Milkavich wondered if that lighting around
the sign did not need to be considered because it is considered in the lighting standards. Mr.
Hall explained that the EMC lighting that has to be controlled is the changeable copy but the
lighting on the outside is the same as any kind of lighted sign and there are often standards of
brightening, not necessarily dimming. Mr. Woodbury stated that he is glad the lighting is subject
to operate within the regulations of the Sign Code and conditions as well. Mr. Hall stated that
the closest residential structure to the EMC signs are about 385 feet away across State Street
and over 1,000 feet away on the east side.

A motion was made by Maren Patterson that the Planning Commission Approve the Planned
Center Signs for the Fashion Place Mall, including Conditional Use Permits to allow the

SMITH FAMILY DENTAL — 152 West Winchester Street - Project #20-011

Morgan Smith was present to represent this request. Jared Hall reviewed the location and
request for Conditional Use Permit approval to allow the construction and operation of a new
dental office on the subject property within the R-N-B Zone on the property located at 6271
South Sunset Links Drive. This proposal is to build a new dental office on the 4,859 sq. ft.
vacant lot. The I-215 corridor is located direct to the rear of this property and the applicant
proposes to locate the parking to the rear of the property. The site plan will be adjusted to
eliminate a parking stall in order to accommodate an ADA parking stall, however; the proposed
25 parking stalls are still over the minimum requirement of 19-20 parking stalls. The building will
be placed closer to the Winchester Street frontage which creates more of a residential feel and
complies with the standards of the R-N-B Zone. The main entrance to the building will be
located to the rear of the building, near to the parking area. Staff recommends that the sidewalk
be extended from the Winchester Street frontage to the main entrance at the rear of the building
to accommodate any pedestrian traffic. An additional adjustment to the site plan will be the
widening of the drive access from Winchester Street from 20 feet wide to 25 feet wide. After a
distance from Winchester Street the access could narrow to the minimum 20-foot wide drive
access required for emergency services. Both the north and south facades of the building

b. Replace any damaged curb, gutter, and sidewalk along the property frontage.

c. Develop and implement a site Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) prior to

d. Obtain a City Excavation Permit for work in city rights-of-way.

including structural calculations and soils reports upon application for building permits.

the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.

5. The applicant shall work with Planning Division Staff to modify the Site Plan to include the

entrance at the north end of the building.
