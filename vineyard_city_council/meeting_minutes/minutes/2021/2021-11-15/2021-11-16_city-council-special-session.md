3. WORK SESSION
   3.1      UDOT Betterments for the Vineyard Connector
       City Engineer Naseem Ghandour will lead a discussion about desired betterments for the
       Vineyard Connector expansion.

Andrew Gwynn with the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) explained that they would
be completing the Vineyard Connector from Main Street to 1600 North to help with the
completion of the town center. He added that the Connector would go from Geneva Road in
Orem and tie into the American Fork/Lehi area. It would be one lane in each direction with turn
lanes and a grade-separated pedestrian crossing. He reviewed the timeline for completion of the
expansion and three options for the betterments of the Vineyard Connector.

Baseline
• 16-foot opening
• Roadway going over
• Four-sided box
• Cost – $1 million (estimate)

Option 1
• 42-foot opening
• Roadway goes over
• Pre-cast arch
• Cost - $3.1 million (estimate)

Option 2
• 42-foot opening
• Roadway goes under
• Cost – $3.4 million (estimate)
• Can accommodate future widening at $100,000 per foot (estimate)

Option 2A
• 42-foot promenade
• Roadway goes under
• Cost $3 million (estimate)
• Can accommodate future widening at $80,000 per foot (estimate)

     There was a discussion about the options. One item of note was that the promenade would
be vehicle rated. The council agreed to have the promenade go over the road. Ryan Wride with
Horrocks Engineers explained that they needed to take into consideration the width of the
promenade and how they would get pedestrians back down to grade.

There was as discussion about the design. The council wanted the promenade to be 100 feet
wide. There was also a discussion about the difference between the arches and a girder structure
and the pros and cons of both.

      There was discussion about the phasing of the road. Geoff Dupaix with UDOT explained
that there would be some flexibility with the phasing, but not the design. The discussion
continued.

Consensus was:
   • Going over with the promenade
   • Start with 50-foot construction width and expand to the 100-foot in the future
   • Preferred with the arch, with the flexibility to change to the girder if necessary
   • Right-of-way discussion with the developer
   • Road built this next year with the bridge if possible

Mr. Ghandour mentioned that he would work with UDOT to work out a contract. There was as a
discussion about further betterments.
