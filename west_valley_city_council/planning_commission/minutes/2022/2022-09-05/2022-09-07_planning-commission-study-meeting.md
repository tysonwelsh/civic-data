CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-16-2022 Petition by GOLDEN STAR STUCCO and PLASTER, LLC requesting a
          conditional use for a larger commercial vehicle in conjunction with a home
          occupation. The property is located at 3751 W. 4400 S. on .18 acres and is zoned
          R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square
          feet). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that this is a conditional use request to store a larger
              commercial vehicle on the property for a major home occupation. She explained
              that the applicant was applying for a business license, and at the time, they had
              different types of outside storage, a box truck, and a large work truck in the yard.
              Jody noted that the applicant is going through the enforcement process and have
              resolved all violations prompted by applying for a business license. She stated
              that the subject property is a corner lot, making it challenging to meet the setback
              requirements. She explained that the large box truck had been relocated; however,
              they need a conditional use for the other truck because it is too large to be stored
              onsite. Jody explained that the work truck could be kept on the owner’s property
              if screened. However, the gate opens into the street, so the required screening
              would not allow the applicant to meet the clear view requirement. Jody stated they
              are proposing a vinyl fence along the north side of the driveway. She explained
              that the gate would also require screening which should blend in with the main
              building and meet the fence requirements. Jody stated they have two options for
              the gate, add screening and have it open towards the house or shift the gate to the
              north.

              Commissioner McEwen said that he isn’t sure why this needs to be a conditional
              use. He stated that the subject property looks excellent and well maintained, and
              he has seen many RVs parked in driveways, which are much bigger than this
              truck. Jody explained that the car in question does not meet the requirement to be
              stored onsite, so they would need to keep it off-site.

              Commissioner Winters feels having the gate swing in, and the rest of the
              screening added is a reasonable solution. Jody said she would try to get some
              pictures of the open gate for next week’s meeting.


CA-4-2022     Petition by VALLEY COLLISSION requesting conditional use approval for
              outside storage. The property is located at 2738 S Constitution Blvd on 1.74 acres
              and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
              brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that a collision repair business was previously on the
              subject property, and they have moved out. He said the applicant would be
              operating a similar business, but would like to store cars outside overnight, which
              would be considered outside storage. This use is permitted within the M zone with
              a conditional use permit. Brock pointed out that the chain-linked fence along the
              frontage contains slats, and they are proposing to use this to screen the outside
              storage area. Brock explained there are some concerns regarding the parking.
              Brock said with the proposed eighteen bays and office and storage space in the
              building, they would need sixty- four customer and employee stalls according to
              code, and they have twenty-two currently. He explained that the applicant states
              this business would be mostly storage, and there are currently 10 employees
              working on vehicles, but they would ideally have 15 employees, and no more than
              three customers parking at one time. Brock noted that they would have enough
              parking. Brock noted that they would have enough parking, according to the
              applicant. He asked for the Commission's feedback regarding the parking.

              Commissioner Fuller said he had noticed many cars parked on the street in front
              of the property and asked if the parking overflow was from this business. Brock
              said it could be however, he thinks most of it is from the adjacent mobile home
              park.

              Commissioner Woodruff feels twenty-two stalls are insufficient, especially with
              the amount of on-street parking.

              Commissioner Fuller feels that requiring three stalls per service bay is more than
              what would be needed. Brock said he did address the parking with the applicant,
              and if the outside storage is approved, they will pack the cars tightly in that area.
              He explained that without the approval of the outside storage, they would need to
              move the vehicles inside at night, and parking that tight would not be an option.

              Commissioner Winters asked what the options the applicant has to meet the code
              for parking stalls. Brock said they could reduce some of the storage areas to make
              room for additional parking.


C-19-2022     Petition by GALLOWAY & COMPANY requesting conditional use approval for
              a convenience store and fuel sales. The property is located at 6383 W 3500 S on
              1.25 acres and is zoned C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial). (Staff – Brock
              Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson said this proposal would combine two parcels into one lot for a
              convenience store and fuel sales. He noted that the Public Works Department
              would require a high back curb and gutter along Meander Ave. Brock pointed out
              the existing cinder block wall, which would remain. He said he would find out if
              it meets our current code requirements.

              Brock presented the submitted site plan and noted that the applicant would need
              to make some modifications to the plan. He explained currently, they are not
              meeting the setback requirements and would require additional landscaping.
              Brock said the building would front onto 6400 West. He stated that the Public
              Works Department has reviewed the site plan and stated the applicant would only
              be allowed one access off 6400 West. Brock pointed out that they are proposing
              another access off 3500 South, and would review that access. He said they are
              short a couple of parking stalls based on the square footage of the building.

              Brock stated that the applicant must meet all commercial design standards. He
              noted that they would need additional design treatments and to add detail to the
              columns of the canopy, so it ties in with the main building.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if the applicant might turn the building to the north,
              where they will only have one access. Brock said that is an option. He explained
              that all comments had been given to the applicant, and we await their reply.

C-20-2022     Petition by GREY GARZA requesting conditional use approval for 18 townhome
              units. The property is located at 1854 W 4100 S on 1.54 acres and is zoned RM
              (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361
              or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that the applicant requests approval for eighteen
              townhome units. He noted that the townhomes would front the common area
              where the amenities would be located. Brock stated that the parking lot for the
              apartment building is currently paved and striped.

              Brock discussed a couple of outstanding issues. Brock explained how proposed
              elevations are different from the original development agreement. He stated that
              the end units would have rooftop decks. Brock said that the applicant is proposing
              smaller units. He noted that the driveways are relatively small, approximately six
              feet deep. Brock pointed out that they are missing a window treatment on the
              primary façade. Brock asked the Commission if they had any thoughts regarding
              the window treatments.

              Commissioner Lovato said the windows look good as proposed. He stated the
              windows above the garages need more variation. Brock noted that staff had
              provided the applicant with the suggestion of adding a band to break up that block
              of windows. Brock explained that he has talked to the applicant about the
              placement of the rooftop decks being able to see over into the adjacent residential
              buildings. He stated that staff suggests the addition of a railing.

              Commissioner Winters asked if they lacked one space per unit. Brock said the
              Development Agreement for this project was approved prior to the code changes
              for townhome parking.

              Commissioner Winters asked if the applicant proposed meeting the full garage
              space. Brock said they are.

MISCELLANEIOUS APPLICATIONS
M-6-2022 Petition by CHRISTINE SCHMIDT requesting an exception to the requirements
         of 7-10-103 due to landscaping impacts from right-of-way widening at 3497
         South 5600 West. (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
         brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock explained that this is a request for a landscape exception due to UDOT
              widening 3500 South and 5600 West. He said there is a McDonald’s on the
              subject property, and two to three years ago, they modified the drive-thru, which
              triggered the 7-10-103 requirements. Brock noted that UDOT was still working
              on the street at that time and was unaware of how much property they would
              need. He explained that the applicant could hold off on requesting the landscape
              exception until they knew how much property they would be losing. Brock stated
              that when he visited the site, they were in the process of adding trees, and they
              have now added the twelve required trees. He noted that the applicant is close to
              meeting the required twenty feet of landscaping along 5600 West, but they are a
              few feet shy along 3500 South. Brock pointed out that they currently have forty-
              six parking stalls, and they should have seventy-four according to code, so they
              request this exception so they will not lose any parking.


M-7-2022      Petition by SCOTT HUNTSMAN requesting an exception to the requirements of
              7-10-103 due to landscaping impacts from right-of-way widening at 3549 South
              5600 West. (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-

              stated that the site needs to be cleaned-up and additional landscaping. He pointed
              out that we could request enhanced landscaping or add specific tree requirements
              in exchange for granting the exception.
              Commissioner Winters said he is in favor of the exchange. He said moving the
              parking stalls and landscaping might not leave enough room to access the bay
              doors and could be costly.


DISCUSSION ITEM
Land Use Training – Steve Pastorik

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained that this training would review topics often discussed
              during Planning Commission meetings and could count toward their three hours
              of training required yearly. He discussed the history of housing standards. Steve
              noted that a project could come to the Commission with a development agreement
              vesting them with previous standards. He reviewed the process for approving a
              development agreement and said if there are changes to a development agreement,
              it must be approved by both the developer and City Council. Steve gave examples
              of topics covered via a development agreement and what is required for a zone
              change application.

              Brandon Hill explained the definition of legislative and administrative decisions.
              He reviewed Section 7-8-103, which covers the reasonable conditions the
              Planning Commission can apply to a conditional use. Brandon said the
              Commission can always ask him if they have questions regarding any issue. He
              reviewed the Kilgore vs. Utah County case and four issues involving the
              conditional use of that case.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:33 p.m.
