Approve February 28, 2022 Meeting Minutes (1:10) Chris Trusty moved to approve the February 28, 2022 TAC meeting minutes. Richard Nielson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Public Comment (1:55) None 2022 TIP Project Selection – Bob Allen, MAG (2:20) MAG selects projects every two years to be funded using certain Federal, State, and County funds. There are approximately $100m in funds available this cycle. Most of these funds are available in 2025 and 2026. Projects are scored and ranked through an adopted selection process. TAC visited each project via a bus and virtual tour in April. MAG staff has scored each project based on approved criteria. TIP projects were briefly reviewed and opened for discussion. Each entity designated a representative to score during the meeting using a Google form. UDOT elected to not vote on the projects. The scores were combined with the MAG staff score to create an initial prioritized list. The TAC score and staff score will each constitute 50% of the final score. The prioritized list will be presented to RPC to be considered and adopted in June. Funding and final adoption into the TIP will occur in August. MPO Technical Advisory Committee April 25, 2022 Page 3 MAG staff recommends that the ranked project list be approved as presented in the meeting. Andy Spencer moved that TAC recommend that RPC approve the 2022 Project Prioritization List as presented. Rebecca Andrus seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
