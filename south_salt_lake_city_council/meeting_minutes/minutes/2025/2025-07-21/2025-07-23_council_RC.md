NO ACTION COMMENTS
      1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
         meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—August 13th @ 7pm.

              July 24th – All City offices and community centers will be closed in observance of
              Pioneer’s Day.

              August 1st – ‘Movies in the Park’ will show Kung Fu Panda at the Central Park
              Community Center at 7 p.m.

              August 2nd – The Jordan River Commission will host a ‘Puncturevine Pull’ along the
              Jordan River Trail.

              August 7th – The South Salt Lake Police Department will host their first annual
              ‘Badges and Brats’ at their headquarters from 4:30-6:30 p.m.

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          None

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood spoke about a few City highlights:

              City Employee, Deidre Rioux, was recognized as the first recipient of the 'South Salt
              Lake Kindness in Action' award, acknowledging her outstanding contributions to the
              City.

              New City-wide watering restrictions are in effect, prohibiting lawn watering
              between 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. Residents can view the KSL highlight for further
              details.

              Residents can now monitor their household water consumption using the 'Eye on
              Water' application.

              Mayor Wood shared that she was recently invited to present at Harvard University,
