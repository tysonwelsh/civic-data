NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                    WORK SESSION MEETING MINUTES
                        August 3, 2022 @ 6:30 p.m.
                       21 E. 100 N. Nephi, UT 84648

Attendance:
Staff: Shauna Talbot
City Council Member: Skip Worwood
Commissioners: Ann Peterson, Karl Brough, Alan Hancock, Fran Petersen, Heather
Robertson
Excused: Cory Thomson, Kent Heap, Seth Atkinson
Scribe: Shari Cowan
Public: Donald Ball

Ann Peterson, Planning Commission Chair, opened the meeting and offered
the prayer.

1.    Discussion regarding:

      a.     Title 10 Updating required minimum lot size, frontage, density, building
             height, and setbacks in all zones

      b.     Title 1 Updating some uses in zones

      c.     10.11 Planned Residential Development (PRD)- Eliminate code

      Shauna Talbot presented a document with some discussion items for proposed changes
      to Title 10. She said the purpose of this discussion is to update the document.

      A)     Items 1-12

             The commission agreed to the changes and/or updates to items 1-8, and 10-11.
             The vote for item 9 was to leave it as is. The commission decided to not place a
             minimum building size requirement in any zone except CU-1, as outlined in item
             12.

             Item 4: Karl Brough said he thinks there are good points to the PRD. He asked if
             we will revisit the PRD? Shauna confirmed and said she would also like to focus
             on training.

             Item 8: Fran asked if we can require how many units are for rent vs how many
             are for sale. Shauna stated that there is a condominium code that requires
             CCNR and HOA. She also stated that we need to follow the state code, since

     the city does have its own code. Shauna said that we will eventually need to
     develop our own code, and that it will most likely be for the R3 zone since it is the
     densest. She said that she and Seth Atkinson are both in favor of promoting
     different types of living and making sure there is something for everyone.
     Shauna thanked Skip Worwood for defending the concept plan during last night’s
     City Council meeting. He said that there has been plenty of opportunities for
     public comment and input regarding this subject.

     Item 9: Shauna stated that some positives to this item are that there wouldn’t be
     as much code to keep track of, and it would be easy to see multi-family zones. A
     negative would be that the commission may end up “spot-zoning.” Fran asked if
     combining zones would still allow for a single-family dwelling to be next to a
     duplex. Shauna confirmed that it would still be possible. Ann wondered what
     problems would arise from this allowance. Alan Hancock stated that developers
     could potentially take advantage of it. Fran commented that she saw a mixed
     dwelling subdivision in Springville that seemed very nice. She felt that there was
     less traffic because of the varied dwellings. Ann stated that she doesn’t see a
     problem with duplexes dispersed throughout the city. Skip, Fran, & Heather all
     agreed that this would be more of a concern for new developments, rather than
     existing neighborhoods. Ann feels like combining zones makes the issue more
     ambiguous. Shauna decided that this item would be left alone at this time. Ann
     asked how it fits in with our pod concept. Alan stated that builders can always
     ask for a rezone. Skip stated that the city doesn’t have a lot of ways to control
     things, except for zoning. Heather loves the idea of spotty duplexes but doesn’t
     want it as an option for new developers. Donald Ball asked why don’t we want
     diversity? Fran said that the new code allows for more leniency and
     opportunities for location. Shauna explained the difference between density and
     number of units and gave some examples. Skip pointed out that it is not our job
     to make money for developers, it is our job to protect what we want for the city.
     Alan stated that it is also not our job to prevent it and that we should be neutral.

     Item 12: Alan provided positive and negative examples of tiny home communities
     in Sanpete County. Fran asked if we would need to change zoning to allow for
     something like a tiny home community. How would we accommodate individuals
     interested in that? Shauna said that she and Seth agree that there could
     potentially be a place in our city for tiny homes. Shauna also stated that this is a
     new subject for her, and Skip agreed. He said that the city council hasn’t even
     approached it yet. Ann suggested that tiny homes should be on a foundation,
     rather than mobile. Karl said that we shouldn’t put a minimum limit on what
     someone wants to build. Skip said that we can address the issue when it
     becomes a problem, but right now it isn’t a problem.

B)   Items 1, 2, & 3 (i-xvi)

     The commission agreed on items 1 & 2. A few suggestions were made for item 3.
     Ann suggested a change in verbiage, replacing “all but” with “except for” in sub-item vii.
     Karl requested that any time a decimal is used, a zero should be placed before the
     decimal so that it doesn’t get lost. Heather asked what CU-3 would offer that highway
     residential doesn’t offer? The decision was made to strike sub-item x, excluding the
     potential CU-3 zone. Shauna stated that we don’t currently have any guidelines for
     sub-items xi-xvi. Fran feels like it would be unrealistic to have the minimum
     requirements any smaller than what is listed.

     C)     Item 1 (i-xvi)

     Heather requested a visual in order to better discuss the frontage requirements. Fran
     thinks that you can better utilize your space without so many requirements. Skip stated
     that these requirements are already in place, so let’s leave them alone. Karl wants to
     add an extra 10 feet and revisit the item if necessary. Shauna said she wants minimums
     and that there is not a current minimum. Alan told Shauna that the commissioners trust
     her and her judgment regarding the minimums. He said that if she and Kyle put
     something together, we’ll all probably be OK with it.

     D)     Item 1

     Commissioners agree to re-wording.

     E)     Items 1-2

     Much discussion was had concerning item 1. The decision was ultimately made to
     require an eve height of no more than 15 feet, with a 1-foot setback. If a building is 16
     feet or taller, an 8 foot setback would be required. It was suggested that item 2 be kept
     at 10 feet due to concerns for fire equipment and personnel safety.

     F)     Items 1-2

     Karl asked if we can consolidate any of these codes? Alan explained how he is
     conflicted between how he feels as a commissioner versus how he feels as a builder.
     Fran would like to see all codes in one place instead of spreading out like a treasure
     hunt. Heather said she would like the ability to print everything from one place, rather
     than having to follow a bunch of links. Shauna stated that the commission will still need
     to revisit these items before bringing it to a regular commission meeting.

2.   Misc. Topics

     Ann asked the commissioners if they received an email from her regarding the code
     about planning and zoning. She asked that everyone review at least the first 15 pages
     before the next work meeting. The code is outdated, and she is concerned that the

     commission is not performing according to the current code. She has given a copy of it
     to Kasey Wright, Nephi City Attorney, for review. She would like each commissioner to
     be ready for the issue when it comes up.

     Ann asked if anything can be done about General Dollar’s parking lot? Is there anything
     in the nuisance ordinance that would require them to clean it up and make it look nice?

3.   Adjourn: 8:37 p.m.
