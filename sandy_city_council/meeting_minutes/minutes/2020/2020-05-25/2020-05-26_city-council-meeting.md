5.         20-131                 Community Development Department presenting, and recommending
                                  approval of, the 2020-2024 Consolidated Plan and First Year Annual
                                  Action Plan for the 2020 Program Year, including the 2020-2021 CDBG
                                  annual budget as prepared by the Citizen Advisory Committee, for the use
                                  of Community Development Block Grant funds.
                   Attachments:   Revised Funding Recommendation Summary (CARES not incl.)
                                  Original Funding Recommendation Summary (CARES incl.)
                                  Goals, Projects, and Activities Summary (2020)
                                  Resolution 20-15c
                                  2020-2024 Consolidated Plan-draft (5.20.20)
                                  CDBG Public Hearing Notice
                                  Comments from Utah Community Action

                                  Jake Warner from the Community Development Department presented the Community
                                  Development Block Grant consolidated plan budget and annual action plan. Council
                                  questions and comments followed.

                                  The Council Chair opened the public hearing. Senior Analyst Dustin Fratto gave
                                  instruction on how to participate in the public hearing.

                                  Council Executive Director Mike Applegarth read an e-mail from John Gold, Executive
                                  Director of The Sharing Place about how CDBG funding helps provide grief support
                                  services.

                                  Peggy Daniel from South Valley Services thanked the CDBG Committee, Council and
                                  staff for support in helping provide domestic violence services.

                                  Jason Wheeler, Executive Director of Assist Community Design Center noted how many
                                  people his organization is able to help with the assistance of CDBG funding.

                                  Kim Correa with the Inn Between expressed gratefulness for partnership with Sandy City
                                  in providing hospice care.
                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Zach Robinson, that
                                  Resolution #20-15c be approved, including the following:

                                             1. Approval of the 2020-2024 Consolidated Plan and First Year
                                  Annual Action Plan for the 2020 Program Year; and

                                              2. Authorization for the Mayor to submit the 2020-2024 Consolidated
                                  Plan and First Year Annual Action Plan for the 2020 Program Year to the U.S.
                                  Department of Housing and Urban Development and to sign associated
                                  documents... The motion carried by the following vote:
