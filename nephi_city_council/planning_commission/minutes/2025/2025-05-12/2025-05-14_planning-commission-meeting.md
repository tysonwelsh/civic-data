   3. Public Hearing Zone Change: request by Steven M. Anderson, represented by Taylor
      Warburton, for property located at approximately 800 N to 860 N on 300 W Parcel #:
      XA00-0814, for approximately 3.04 gross and 2.77 net acres from R1-H (Residential 1
      acre holding) zone to the R1-½ (Residential ½ acre) zone with associated animal rights
      for the purpose of a 3-lot, single-family, residential development with minimum of ½
      acre lots, and animal rights.

          a. Staff Presentation
                    Mrs. Shauna Talbot, City Planner, presented that some areas are zoned for
                    animal rights and agricultural rights. This rezone application is for
                    property located from approximately 800 N to 860 N on 300 W for
                    approximately 2.77 net acres from R1-H (Residential 1 acre holding) zone
                    to the R1-½ Residential ½ acre zone with associated animal rights
                    (Agricultural Overlay) for the purpose of a 3-lot, single-family, residential
                    development with minimum lot sizes of ½ acre lots, and animal rights.

                     The width of 300 West Street is limited by large power poles on both sides
                     of the right-of-way owned by both Nephi City and Rocky Mountain
                     Power. This road also abuts up against the railroad tracks with limited
                     crossings, and a less-than-ideal intersection crossing onto 300 W due to
                     the jogging street. Because of this the R.O.W. will necessarily be more
                     narrow than normal on this street. Limiting traffic on this thoroughfare
                     would be a recommendation staff hold for this area for this reason,
                     particularly to large vehicles. Also, limiting the amount of traffic in
                     general is recommended, so less density is desirable.

                     This area already has some established neighborhoods with larger
                     properties, and historical animal rights usage. Staff feel this zone would
                     be in keeping with the surrounding uses currently occurring in this area.
                     Also, the Nephi City General Plan Concept Zoning Map lists the area west
                     of this location as being future planned to be low or mixed density
