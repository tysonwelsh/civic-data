BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Deviation for approximately 0.54 acres
located on the south side of 13200 S, at approximately 968 E 13200 S. (Exhibit B and C). The
property is currently zoned RA2 and is located within a mapped area where I-ADU’s are
prohibited without approval of a deviation. The applicant is requesting that a Deviation be
approved to allow for the creation of an I-ADU within a proposed Primary Dwelling on the
subject property.

The primary dwelling is set to be added onto, with the existing home becoming the I-ADU.
There will be a connection from the main dwelling to the addition, with access to the
property off of Bench Cove. Homes in this neighborhood are within the areas on the map
referenced in Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-31-090 that are prohibited
from containing an I-ADU. Deviations from this prohibition are permitted when the
Planning Commission finds that the property meets the deviation standards of DCMC

According to DCMC Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density
development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve
the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to
large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The
predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected
from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.”

Site Plan Layout. The site plan, elevation drawings, and floor plans that have been provided
with the ADU application (Exhibits F, G and H) show the existing single-story home,
with the addition being added to the rear yard. The property is a corner lot on 13200 S and
Bench Cove. The applicant is proposing to covert the existing home into the I-ADU and built
a large addition with access to Bench Cove. The proposed addition includes a full dwelling
unit and has a large (4) car garage just east of the main dwelling.

Parking. The proposed site plan and floor plans show a (4) car garage. The garage and
driveway should be accessed off of Bench Cove, though not reflected on current plans. The
applicant has not indicated on the site plan where they intend to designate the I-ADU
parking as stated in their request (Exhibit I), however as a condition of approval the
applicant can indicate on a revised plan were these will be located. Parking for ADU’s may
not impede use of the required parking spaces for the Primary Dwelling.

Architecture. The elevation drawings (Exhibit G) depict the proposed addition to be built to
the south of the main dwelling. The proposed elevation drawings are in compliance with
the intent of DCMC Section 9-31-045(B)(5) that the exterior appearance of the primary
dwelling is not altered in a manner that would appear other than as a single-family
dwelling. The floor plans depict the layout of said addition and I-ADU (Exhibit H). Two
possible floor plan options have been provided. The final layout will be determined during
the ADU permit and building permit stage.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a Deviation request for an I-ADU is found
in Section 9-31-055 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:
    A. Deviation Authorized: A deviation from strict compliance with the prohibition of I-ADUs
        on properties located within the areas identified on the I-ADU map in Exhibit A of this
        chapter may be approved by the planning commission if all of the following criteria are
        met and the planning commission finds that:
        1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-thousand
            square feet (20,000 ft²);
        2. No D-ADU exists on the property;

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission approves the request, staff recommends they include the
following conditions of approval:

    1. That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
       Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
       and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
    2. That the applicant submit a revised site plan to staff indicating where the designated
       parking for the I-ADU will be located.

The findings for approval as are follows:

    1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-
       thousand square feet (20,000 ft²);
    2. No D-ADU exists on the property;

    4. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are
