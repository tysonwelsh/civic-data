   1. WEBER COUNTY BOARD OF CANVASS - OF JUNE 30, 2020 PRIMARY ELECTION
      Ryan Cowley, County Elections Director, noted that this was an election like none before; the COVID-19
      pandemic altered the process significantly. For the first time there was drive through voting (at the County
      Fairgrounds), to keep everyone safe. There was record-breaking participation for any county Primary Election
      of 62%, with less than 0.9% voting in person. He outlined the Legislature’s bill during the special session that
      eliminated in person voting, except if the county wished to have mobile voting, and eliminated same day
      registration and early voting. Highlights included that 20,000 ballots were quarantined for safety but were
      processed in two days—a major feat. Republican turnout was 66.4% and 47.7% Democrat, 99.1% of ballots
      were by mail/drop box, and 0.8% were undeliverable, an incredibly small number. Leann Kilts, the incumbent
      County Recorder/Surveyor received 65.34% of the vote and James Couts 34.66%. The commissioners expressed
      great confidence on our county’s processes.
      Commissioner Harvey moved to certify the June 30, 2020 Weber County Board of Canvass for the Primary
      Election; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
      Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
