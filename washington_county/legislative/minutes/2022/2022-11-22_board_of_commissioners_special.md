WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
NOVEMBER 22, 2022

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on November 22, 2022, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance
were Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk
Sarah Lloyd.

INVOCATION BY: Tim Martin, Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Commissioner Gil Almquist

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-3029, A RESOLUTION APPOINTING
JUSTIN _SIP_TO THE ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD OF HURRICANE

VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

Deputy Attorney Devin Snow explained that Keen Ellsworth of Toquerville recently stepped down
from the Hurricane Valley Fire District. Mr. Ellsworth was Toquerville City’s nominee on the
board, and Toquerville City has nominated Mayor Justin Sip and asked that he be appointed to fill
the vacancy.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-3029, A
Resolution Appointing Justin Sip to the Administrative Control Board of
Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-3030, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_AGREEMENT_TO_ INITIATE A LAND EXCHANGE INVOLVING WARNER

VALLEY

Mr. Snow stated that when the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve was created, large portions were privately
owned. The federal government agreed to acquire privately owned property inside the reserve, and
the county has agreed to facilitate those acquisitions. The BLM and the Washington County Water
Conservancy District would like to initiate an exchange that would result in the BLM’s acquisition
of certain private property remaining in the reserve. This is an agreement that would initiate the
exchange, and the county agrees to facilitate the exchange and pay certain costs relating to NEPA
compliance. The contract would be approved subject to legal review.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-3030, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement to Initiate a Land Exchange Involving
Warner Valley. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

BOARD OF CANVASSERS MEETING:

CONSIDERATION OF ELECTION CANVASS OF NOVEMBER 8, 2022

Washington County Clerk-Auditor Susan Lewis read aloud the results from the November 8, 2022
general election. She reported that there are 105,450 registered voters, and there were 70,003
ballots that were cast in the election. Those numbers indicate a 66.38% voter turnout.

(See attached election summary report for the general election)
MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve and Certify the Election
Results of the November 8, 2022 General Election. Motion seconded by

Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Ms. Lewis explained the process of verifying voter registration rolls and the processes involved in
maintaining and updating voter registration rolls.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:43 p.m.
