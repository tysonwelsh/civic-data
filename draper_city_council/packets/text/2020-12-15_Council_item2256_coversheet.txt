Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Tom Draper
CC:  Date: December 15, 2020
Re: Public Hearing: Approval of Ordinance #1470, Vacations of Wheatfield Way & Wheatfield Circle Comments: This application is a request for approval to vacate public rights-of-way known as Wheatfield Way (40 West) and Wheatfield Circle (13015 South) in the Greenfield Farms Subdivision. The rights-of-way area is approximately 91,036 square feet . The property was recently rezoned M1. The Greenfield Farm Subdivision has been purchased by Exeter Draper Land LLC with the intent to develop it into a commercial development. The public rights-of-way must be vacated in order to consolidate the land into a new single lot and move forward with the redevelopment process. The findings are as follows: Neither the public interest nor any person will be materially injured by the vacations, the rights-of-way no longer serves a public purpose, and there is good cause for the vacations.
