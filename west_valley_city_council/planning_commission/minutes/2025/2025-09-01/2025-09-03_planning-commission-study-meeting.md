     MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING
                            SEPTEMBER 3, 2025

THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON
WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 3, 2025 AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600
CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED
TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY CHAIR MATTHEW LOVATO.

     Mathew Lovato, Chair
     Martell Winters, Vice Chair
     Harold Woodruff, Commissioner
     Darrick Porter, Commissioner
     Nancy Drozdek, Commissioner
     Rob Durfee, Commissioner
     Pauline Matagi, Commissioner

     Brandon Hill, Assistant City Attorney

PRESENTATIONS:

1) SMI-7-2025 Petition by Jake Hone is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Mann Way
Subdivision. The subdivision is located at 4030 West Mann Way and will consist of 4 lots on .50
acres, and is zoned R-1-4 (Residential Single- Unit Dwellings, Min. lot size 4,000 Sq. Ft.) Staff-
Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented a request from Jake Hone to develop a 4-lot subdivision on a
corner of Mann Way and 4000 West, which would require removing existing structures and trees while
installing new sidewalk, gutter and parkstrip. Each of the lots are 50 feet wide, apart from the corner lot
that would have a 60-foot frontage and, and over 5,000 square feet. There were no questions or concerns
from the Commission.

2) PUD-7-2025 Petition by Ken Milne is requesting a plat amendment for the first phase of the
Heather on the Parkway to adjust square footage of certain units within the PUD and increase the
percentage of open space within the community. The subdivision is located at 2875 South 6105
West and is zoned RM. Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented an application from Ken Milne requesting a reduction in the
square footage of senior living project units to 1,500 square feet, instead of the originally planned 2,000
square feet, which would modify the plat and require a plat amendment The new proposal will increase
the open space for the project. The Planning Commission had previously approved a 3% reduction in the
open space based on an amenity package, including a clubhouse and a perimeter walking trail. There
could be an opportunity in the future for Mr. Milne to purchase from West Valley City the sliver of
property to the east of his lot, which would allow a south extension to Parkway Boulevard and to the
Meadowlands Park and Wetland Park.

3) C-12-2025 Petition by Scott Godfrey requesting conditional use approval for the Godfrey
Industrial Park No. 3 with Flex Industrial, Warehousing, Heavy Truck and Trailer Service, and
Outside Storage. The property is located at 2349 South 5900 West on 13.5 acres, and is zoned M,
Manufacturing. Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented the lot layout of six flex-space buildings, a trucking maintenance
building, landscaping, and parking surface options. Jody explained that while trucking yards typically
require 100% paving, there might be flexibility for some gravel areas in the north lot, but not along the
frontage road or adjacent to the right-of-way. The group discussed whether to require paved or gravel
surfaces for the outside storage on the southern portion of the site, with Commissioner Durfee
suggesting that the type of surface should be determined once the building uses are known through
business licenses. They also discussed the possibility of using tracking materials to prevent gravel from
being tracked onto public roads. The staff report will include options for surfacing requirements, and the
Commission will address the issue further next week.

4) C-13-2025 Petition by Right Side Up RV requesting conditional use approval for Outside
Display of Merchandise. The property is located at 6028 West 4700 South on 2.8 acres and is
zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented a request for an RV sales and service business seeking approval for
an outside display of merchandise, requesting an exception to the standard 20-foot setback requirement
by placing displays 10 feet from the property line. She explained the site's history, the specific area for
the display, and the requirements for an updated landscape plan to enhance the encroachment area. A
Commissioner inquired about potential disadvantages and Jody clarified that the exception is limited to
specific targeted areas and does not allow the entire lot to have reduced setbacks. Commissioner
Drozdek asked for an example of what the display would look like under the proposed exception to
better understand the impact. Jody will provide an enlarged picture of the corner at the hearing meeting.

5) C-14-2025 Petition by Johnson Controls Inc., requesting conditional use approval for Outside
Storage. The property is located at 2255 S. Technology Park Way on 3.13 acres total/.21 for
Outside Storage and is zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or
jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented a request from Johnson Controls seeking approval for outside storage
in CONEX containers at the rear of the building. She presented the storage layout and surfacing options,
noting that the CONEX containers would be screened from view by the warehouse, the neighboring
building, and the curve of the road. The Commission will need to decide if gravel is an acceptable
surface for the equipment yard for the 25’ trailers that would be stored on it. There were no questions or
concerns from the Commission.

6) C-15-2025 Petition by Aisza Wilde requesting conditional use approval for a commissary
kitchen with outside storage. The property is located at 1802 W. Alexander Street on .71 acres and
is zoned M (Manufacturing). Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented a proposal for a 24-hour commissary kitchen on Alexander Street
designed to accommodate 19 mobile food units and 15 workstations. The kitchen would meet Salt Lake
County Health Department requirements for equipment storage and cleaning, including gray water
disposal facilities, interior prep areas, walk-in coolers, and sinks. The site would require fencing,
screening, updates to landscaping, and building maintenance. The Commissioners raised concerns about
parking, which Jody confirmed the 7 designated parking stalls would be sufficient for personal vehicles,
and that food trucks/trailers would never be allowed to park on the street.


There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting was
adjourned at 4:29 PM.
