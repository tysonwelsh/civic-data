                      COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

                              NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-113 that the Copperton Metro
Township Council will hold a public hearings on December 5, 2023, at 6:30 PM to receive public
comment on the Copperton Metro Township’s proposed budget for 2024 (“2024 Tentative
Budget”). The meeting will be held at the Bingham Lion’s Club, 8725 W. Hillcrest Street,
Copperton, Utah 84006.
The Council will consider the adoption of the 2024 Tentative Budget after taking comments from
the public during the hearing. The public is also invited to review and inspect the proposed budget
on the Utah Public Notice Website (https://www.utah.gov/pmn/) and on the Copperton Metro
Township’s official webpage (https://coppertonutah.org/).
You may also obtain copies of the proposed budget and submit written comments prior to the
public hearing by contacting Nichole Watt at nwatt@slco.org or (385) 468-7400. Individuals
needing special accommodations (including ancillary communicative aids and services) during
this public hearing should contact Ms. Watt three (3) days before the public hearing.
