Wednesday, July 03, 2024, at 6:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West

Commission, media, and interested citizens.

   4.2. Consideration of a Preliminary Plat for two (2) residential lots in the Oaklie
        Estates Subdivision located generally at 6221 W 13900 South in an A-1-43
        Agricultural Single-Family Zone.
        Applicant: Cade Cunningham, Western Flats LLC (property owner)
        Acres: ±5.0 acres
        File No: S2020-016
https://www.herriman.org/uploads/files/4772/Item-4.2-Staff-Report.pdf
Planning Director Maloy counseled the public hearing was closed in the previous meeting
with the item being continued for additional review. This project was long in the making.
Back in 2020 a 5-lot subdivision with a rezone request was denied and last month a 3-lot
subdivision (minus existing home) was continued. The applicant has resubmitted a new plat
with 2 lots not including the existing home. The new plat map was shown. The dedication
of the street will need to be approved. The recommendations are in the staff report. They
have 2 years to record the preliminary plat. Staff recommends approval.
Chair Powell pointed out the plat typo on the flag lot labeled part of lot 3 is actually lot 1.
Applicant Travis Cunningham approached the podium. Commissioner Rypien inquired if lot
1 has a cross easement to access the existing home. Applicant Cunningham conveyed it has
an easement even though it’s shown as green on the plot map it is there. Commissioner
Oberg raised the question whether the easement is included in the one-acre measurement?
Applicant Cunningham revealed it is included because it’s an existing home that doesn’t
need a dedicated access road.
Chair Powell informed the Commission has already had a public hearing at the last meeting
so no public discussion will occur at this time. Commissioner Oberg aired he remembered
July 3, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes                                   Page 5 of 7


the main concern being the flag lot portion on lot 1 which remains unchanged in this plan.
Commissioner Rypien countered with the main concern was 1 acre minimum after the
dedication of the street. Chair Powell disclosed the 30-foot street dedication is no longer
included in the acreage.
Applicant Cunningham pointed out the street dedication is a ¼ acre, so they lose a lot of
land. Commissioner Oberg sought if the existing trees would need to be removed.
Applicant Cunningham replied they are trying to keep the trees, but they might lose one for
the easement. The existing house only has power. He would like to add the rest of the
utilities. Commissioner Fenn sought whether they would be asking the city to add the
utilities to the existing house. Applicant Cunningham commented he will add the utilities
himself as he is also a developer. Commissioner Fenn commented for him that the hang up
was the dedication of 13900 south and expressed his appreciation of the new design.
Commissioner Fenn moved to approve item 4.2 Consideration of a Preliminary Plat for
two (2) residential lots in the Oaklie Estates Subdivision located generally at 6221 W
13900 South in an A-1-43 Agricultural Single-Family Zone with staff’s recommendations
1. The final subdivision plat shall comply with all applicable Herriman City regulations and
engineering standards. The final civil engineering plans for the subdivision shall contain a
landscape plan for the 13900 South storm drain swale that contains “low impact design”
(LID) features acceptable to the City and stamped by a licensed landscape architect as
required by City Code 10-23-5. 3. The final subdivision plat shall be recorded within two
years of preliminary approval.
Commissioner Oberg seconded the motion.

https://www.herriman.org/uploads/files/4773/Item-4.3-Staff-Report.pdf
Planner Hoadley showed the vicinity map in the Anthem Commercial area. This is an
amendment for the outdoor area. Staff have requested that storage, seating and a restroom
be added. The proposal includes 9 picnic tables (6 under shade structure), 2 restrooms, and
3 outdoor pickleball courts. The new landscaped area was shown. Zoning has no concerns.
Engineering’s only concern was a stop sign. She spoke with the applicant and the stop sign
was already installed. Staff recommends approval with one recommendation.
Applicant Jamie McQueen voiced after going out to the area they agreed adding 3 outdoor
pickleball courts would add to the environment. He had a lot of feedback requesting
outdoor reservable courts.
Commissioner Rypien moved to approve item 4. Consideration of a Conditional Use
Permit Amendment for Premier Pickleball to add three (3) courts and a practice court
at a recreational facility in the Anthem Commercial Development located at 5163 W
Denali Park Drive in the C-2 Commercial Zone with staff’s recommendation to receive
and agree to all recommendations from other agencies.
