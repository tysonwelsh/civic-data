SCHEDULED ITEMS

        PUBLIC HEARING / RESOLUTION - Adopting the Redevelopment Agency of the
        City of Orem (RDA) Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Tentative Budget

Mr. Nelson presented the Council with the RDA Tentative Budget for the 2022-2023 fiscal year.
Items in this budget included a University Place CDA incentive, future CDA projects. The total
RDA expenditures for fiscal year 2023 added up to 1,874,909 dollars.

General discussion ensued regarding the University Place CDA incentive, parks projects, as well
as water usage and fees. Mr. Peterson asked when the CDA incentive with University Place was
set to expire. Mr. Clark responded and expressed it is slated to end in 2040.

Chair Young opened public hearing at 7:29pm. Hearing no comments, Chair Young closed the
public hearing and brought the item back to the Board for further discussion.

Mr. Peterson moved to approve by Resolution the adoption of the Redevelopment Agency of
the City of Orem (RDA) Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Tentative Budget. Mrs. Millett seconded the
motion. Those voting aye: David A. Young, Jeff Lambson, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson,
David Spencer, and LaNae Millett. The motion passed.
