BUSINESS MEETING
Meeting began at – 8:34 a.m.
1) Approval of the September 14, 2023 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes. (Motion/Voting)
Motion: To approve the September 14, 2023 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes.
Motion by: Commissioner Harpst
2nd by: Commissioner Karkut
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
2) MSD Planning Staff will be working the Planning Commission to review Title 19 Zoning
recommendations including Forestry and Recreation Zones, and Commercial Zones. Planners:
Brian Tucker and Matthew Starley (Discussion)
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding Emigration Canyon maps and layers in
Commercial and Forestry and Recreation zones and omitting commercial and changing designation
to preserved open space. Discussed process and noticing for changing zoning. Reduce the number
of uses in Commercial Zones and create a set of use standards. Visited the use table to eliminate
uses not wanted in the canyon and went through comments received from the commissioners.
