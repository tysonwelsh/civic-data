Reports from Community Stakeholders

Magna Chamber of Commerce

              Sara Van Roosendaal stated the Chamber’s November luncheon is Thursday,
November 15th at noon at the Webster Center. It will be the first opportunity to get a passport for
the Shop Small event Saturday, November 24th. Vendors have donated items for the event, and
passport holders can get stamps in their passports from them at the event. There will also be a
passport drawing, valued at $545.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Magna in Motion

                Keri Duckworth, Magna in Motion, stated Magna in Motion wrapped up its 30-day
weight loss challenge. Two successful people met their goals and split the prize. Most people
who participated lost weight. Magna in Motion will be doing its Santa Stroll at 3:00 p.m. at the
library in conjunction with the Shop Small event. The first 50 people to participate will receive a
tee-shirt. Jazzercise will also be doing its annual warmup.

Reports from Other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department

                Chief Jason Ackerman stated the Kearns/Magna Precinct is separating into two
separate precincts. Officers will start bidding for the Magna Precinct early December and will be
here for a year at a time. That will enable them to get to learn the community better. Since all
agencies are down police officers, resources will still be shared when public safety is at risk. Also,
the Veteran’s Parade was a tremendous success. The officers really enjoy doing it every year,
and it is a great tribute to veterans.

               Max Johnson, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Division,
asked if the parking issue by Brockbank Campus got resolved.

               Chief Ackerman stated that is still a work in progress.

            Greg Schulz stated UPD detectives are working with Salt Lake County’s Public
Works and Municipal Services Department on that.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Council Member Ferguson stated he had nothing to report.

             Council Member Prokopis stated the Unified Fire Authority will hold its budget
hearing December 13, 2018.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Wasatch Front Waste & Recycling District

                Council Member Peel stated the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District
Administrative Control Board is holding its budget hearing Monday, December 19, 2018, at 6:00
p.m. It will likely approve the budget after the public hearing. The District will not have a tax
increase this year, but the cost of a private dumpster is going up to $145. In comparison, most
cities throughout the County charge $200 for that. Unfortunately, the District is losing its financial
officer to the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District. Lastly, Herriman City has backed off
its requests for independent billing, as it had no legal stance to do that.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District

              Council Member Peay stated the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
meets tomorrow night and is in the middle of its budget. While it is getting new sales tax from
online companies, the Legislature reduced taxes that manufacturers within the valley would
have paid, and that was a greater loss than the online sales tax gain.

Planning and Zoning Updates (Not on the agenda – maybe we can leave out?)

                Max Johnson stated there is an application to build apartments and demolish the
existing Kennecott buildings on the campus where Café Rio is. The developer also wants a
couple retail pads on U-111. That will have to go to the planning commission.

              Council Member Prokopis asked about the development on 7800 West and 3100
South now that D.R. Horton has taken over.

               Mr. Johnson stated D.R. Horton has submitted a formal application to amend the
conditional use. The change is to build ramblers – 47 rambler homes and the open space plans.
Some people are upset about that proposal. D.R. Horton is still planning this as a homeowner’s
association (HOA) and putting in all the amenities previously promised. Everyone who lives within
300 feet of the property line will be notified of the hearing for the amendment.

              Council Member Peel stated he has had constituents from that area approach
him with concerns.
