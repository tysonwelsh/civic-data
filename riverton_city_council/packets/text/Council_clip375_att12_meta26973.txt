FROM:          Mayor Staggs

ITEM:          Establishing Annual Meeting Schedules for Riverton City for the Year 2020


SUMMARY:

Pursuant to UCA 52-4-202. Public notice of meetings -- Emergency meetings.
   (1) A public body shall give not less than 24 hours public notice of each meeting including
the meeting:
   (a) agenda;
   (b) date;
   (c) time; and
   (d) place.
   (2) (a) In addition to the requirements under Subsection (1), a public body which holds
regular meetings that are scheduled in advance over the course of a year shall give public
notice at least once each year of its annual meeting schedule as provided in this section.

                                2020 MEETING SCHEDULES

Pursuant to § 52-4-202(2) Utah Code Annotated, Riverton City hereby gives notice of the
following meeting schedules for the Year 2020:
    • City Council Meetings are generally held on the first and third Tuesday of each month
        with Work Sessions beginning at 5:00 p.m. and City Council Meetings beginning at 7:00
        p.m., at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah.
    • Planning Commission Meetings are held on the second and fourth Thursdays of each
        month beginning at 6:30 p.m. at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood
        Road, Riverton, Utah.
    • Riverton Law Enforcement Service Area Meetings are held on a regular basis on the
        third Tuesday of each month following City Council Meetings.
    • Board of Adjustment Hearings may be held as circumstances require; best practicable
        notice will be given for such meetings.
    • Redevelopment Agency Meetings, Special and/or Emergency Meetings, in addition
        to those specified, may be held as circumstances require; best practicable notice will be
        given for such meetings.
