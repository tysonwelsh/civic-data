Chair Handley asked Brian Jones if there needs to be a process of re-approval.

Brian Jones stated that the council is authorized to change the data inside the plan.

Bill Peperone clarified the process it takes to amend if there are any new developments.

Chair Handley asked about commercial nodes. He questioned the need for commercial development.

Brandon Larsen stated that the documents call for a grocery store. He also stated that there are areas
where they will be developed commercial.

Chair Handley asked if they could prioritize the commercial nodes to be like Dentist offices, etc.

Mr. Larsen responded that the document does not get into that much of detail to what type of commercial
can be built.

Councilor Whipple questioned Brandon Larsen about certain areas shown on the map and asked about
areas that are wanting to be for industrial development.

Mr. Larsen answered her questions.

Councilor Whipple asked about commercial opportunities near the regional sports park. She asked if it
would be accommodated in this plan.

Mr. Larsen agreed that the area surrounding the regional sports park can be changed to food
establishments, hotels, etc.

Chair Handley opened it up to public comment.

Becky Bogdin of Provo stated she is excited about this document.

Chair Handley closed public comment and opened it up for council discussion.

Chair Handley asked Brian Jones about vote total concerns as there are three excused council members.

Brian Jones responded to Chair Handley.

Chair Handley called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 4-0 with Councilors Handley, Hoban, Shipley, and Whipple
                      in favor. Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore and MacKay are excused.

Councilor Hoban asked Aaron Ardmore about parking and single-family development needs.

Steve Sobey of American Fork stated he is the engineer of this project. He stated that the commission and
staff are in favor of this and gave facts of why this should be approved.

Diane Christensen of Provo stated that the vote that was done in planning commission was not
unanimous. She is concerned about the outcome of the planning commission vote.

area. He stated the report shows it would be a small increase of two hours of traffic versus one for a
single-family home.

Kristy Charles of Provo spoke on the irregular voting practice of the planning commission and feels that
the council should keep the plan as it stands.

Tama Lawson of Provo spoke on the neighborhood and the parking issue. She is pleading with council to
vote no for this change.

Mike Lawson of Provo is asking the council to vote no for this change.

Kylie Pearson of Provo says she is opposed to the amendment. She stated that there is a demand for a
single-family home and they will not have any issues finding a buyer. She also spoke on safety of the
children in this area.

Trevor Sharpe of Pleasant Grove is the owner of the lot. He gave documents showing photos of the area
at what he stated was a peak time for school children to be walking home. He also provided photos of
cars parked in front of his property.

Randalyn Macedoni of Provo stated her concerns with changing the amendment.

Becky Bogdan of Provo spoke on concerns about disturbances it can cause from changing an ordinance.

John Heinrod, builder of this lot spoke on the purchase and stated it was listed and approved on the
master plan.

Councilor Shipley has concerns about the remaining council members who are not present for vote. He
stated he feels there is not a stark change from a single-family to twin homes.
