Amir Caus, AICP Senior Planner, and Michael Anderson, Principle Architect, CORE Architecture, presented a low-impact permit application at Quarry Junction. (6:15 p.m.)
Commissioner Makena Hawley asked about the possibility of potential businesses promoting livability and community. Senior Planner Caus responded. (6:16 p.m.)
Commissioner Young asked a question about parking and green space. Senior Planner Caus and Principle Architect Michael Anderson responded. (6:18 p.m.)
Commissioner Tim Jeffrey asked a question about Architectural design and how that aligns with the development code. Senior Planner Caus and Principle Architect Michael Anderson responded. (6:21 p.m.)
Commissioner Matthew Nagie asked for clarification on "and that the commission make a recommendation on architecture land scape plans." What does that look like? Senior Planner Caus and Principle Architect Michael Anderson responded. (6:27 p.m.)
Commissioner Heather Peteroy wondered what the intent was for the buildings being added to the community to assimilate with the community in terms of shape and color, with a mountain feel. Senior Planner Caus and Principle Architect Michael Anderson responded. (6:28 p.m.)
Commission Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:37 p.m.
No one appeared.
Commission Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:37 p.m.
Commissioner Young asked how this project could support Jeremy Ranch and Pinebrook. What types of offices and retail would work well there? Principle Architect Michael Anderson responded. (6:37 p.m.)
Commissioner Tyann Mooney commented on parking and recommended adding more mountain stone and materials to provide a mountain feel. Senior Planner Caus and Principle Architect Michael Anderson responded. (6:31 p.m.)
Tim Jeffrey made a motion to make a recommendation to the Community Development Director that the architecture follow the neighborhood this is going into, as opposed to a box look. (6:41 p.m.). Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Eric Sagerman. Abstain: Matthew Nagie.
