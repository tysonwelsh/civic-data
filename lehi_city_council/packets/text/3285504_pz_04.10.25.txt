129   3.4) Public hearing and consideration of Mint Architecture’s request for approval of a Plat
130   Amendment for Gardner Point Phase 1, creating two commercial parcels at approximately
131   1500 North 3600 West.
132
133   Jacob Curtis presented the item. The Plat Amendment creates a new lot within the current lot and
134   facilitates the first stage of construction for development.
135
136   Brooke Moore, the applicant, was present. She thanked city staff for their help with the project.
137
138   Commissioner Jackson opened public comment.
139
140   Scott Larsen lives nearby the property. The development will be happening in an alleyway
141   adjacent to his property. He has concerns over two private roads being used for access. He
142   doesn’t want commercial and construction traffic driving through his neighborhood. He would
      Lehi City Planning Commission                      3                                      April 10, 2025
143   prefer the bulk of traffic to come through 1500 North and suggested that the Commission add an
144   easement to allow that.
145
146   Commissioner Jackson closed public comment.
147
148   Gary Ellis thought that the project has the right to bring traffic in through private roads. The
149   property is too close to the intersection to have an access point added for this particular section
150   of the property. Craig Chambers said that the construction company would need to work with the
151   private owners of the road, and that the city has no role in determining whether they can be used
152   or not.
153
154   Commissioner Lockhart asked to see the previously decided upon access points. Kim Struthers
155   pulled up the site plan. Moore showed her phased plan. She clarified that only the plat
156   amendment was up for approval at the moment, and not construction. The plat amendment
157   images show only the property around the building and cut off other access points that are shown
158   in the full plan.
159
160   Commissioner Jackson asked how much traffic there would be in the interim between
161   construction beginning and the other access points being installed. Moore did not have estimated
162   numbers. She is planning on continuing to the next building as soon as she has tenants in the
163   first. Commissioner Jackson clarified that Moore is still in the paperwork phase.
164
165   Commissioner Everett clarified that there would only be ingress from the private neighborhood.
166   He asked when the utilities for Phase 2 would be put in. Moore explained that there is not a set
167   date yet. Commissioner Everett asked if a crash gate could be a requirement.
168
169   Commissioner Jackson asked if Moore has a right to use the roads if they are private. Kim
170   Struthers informed the Commission that there will eventually be an ingress off 1500.
171
172   Commissioner Kunze asked about the property to the South. Struthers and Moore said that it is
173   residential. Moore explained that they are trying to make the neighborhood commercial buildings
174   accessible and walkable for adjacent neighborhoods.
175
176   Commissioner Lockhart said she doesn’t think development can happen without public access.
177   Moore explained that construction will have access from the other plats in the development plan,
178   those points are just cut off from the plat map being discussed. She said that she can use the
179   private access, but that the main access will be from 1500. Ellis clarified that the access was too
180   close to the intersection, but there may have been an exception made. Struthers explained that the
181   site plan had already been approved. Commissioner Eyre asked why the site plan had access
182   points that were against code. Struthers believed that Luke Seegmiller granted an exception to
183   the project.
184
185   Commissioner Kunze asked if construction would be coming through the private roads. Moore
186   said it would be accessed from the South until access from 1500 was finished. Commissioner
187   Kunze asked if there would be permanent access from the South. Moore confirmed that there
188   would be.
189

228   3.6) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s requests for preliminary
229   subdivision review of Inverness Plat D, including 278 single family homes and 126
230   townhomes located at approximately Horton Way and Edinburgh Avenue.
231
232   Jacob Curtis presented the item. This is the northernmost area of the Skye Area Plan and has the
233   steepest slopes. The development doesn’t currently meet city requirements, but the steep slopes
234   justify an exception, in his opinion as a planner.
235
236   Commissioner Everett asked about the connectivity of the suburb in regard to emergency vehicle
237   access. Curtis explained that the city has to balance the needs of the community with what it is
      Lehi City Planning Commission                     5                                      April 10, 2025
238   possible to create in a development. Gary Ellis explained that the development meets the code
239   requirements as far as access points go.
240
241   Scott Bishop was present representing DR Horton. The company is taking the advice of DRC
242   comments and are planning on installing pedestrian pads on longer block lengths. They are also
243   adding trails and pathways in every place that makes sense, which will increase walkability and
244   shorten block lengths for pedestrians.
245
246   Commissioner Eyre asked if staircases were being avoided as they aren’t accessible to those with
247   mobility aids like wheelchairs. Bishop explained that there will be staircases. He also clarified
248   that some of the trails would be asphalt and some would be dirt trails.
249
250   Commissioner Jackson opened and closed public comment.
251
252   Commissioner Eyre asked city staff if the two access points would become a problem once the
253   neighborhood is built out. Ellis and Bishop explained that there are three access points to the
254   neighborhood. Ellis also stated that while it is a valid concern, it hasn’t come up until this point
255   in planning. He asked for the opinion of the Planning Department. Kim Struthers explained that
256   the development is not abnormal or unusual. Ellis explained that the wide roads will help with
257   traffic flow. Curtis also mentioned that the fire department already suggested having turnarounds
258   on dead end streets.
259
260                   Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to give a positive recommendation to the
261                   Lehi City Council with the findings that the proposed subdivision has significant
262                   grade that justifies an exception to the connectivity standards, the proposed
263                   subdivision meets the requirements of the Skye area plan, and that the proposed
264                   subdivision meets the requirements of the Lehi City Development Code in regard
265                   to subdivision approvals. She included all DRC comments. Commissioner
266                   Lockhart seconded.
267
268                   Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner Lockhart, yes. Commissioner
269                   Eyre, yes. Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes.
270
271                   Motion passed unanimously.
