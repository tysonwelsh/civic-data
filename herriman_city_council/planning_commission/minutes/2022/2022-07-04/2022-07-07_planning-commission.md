Planning Manager Spencer reviewed the request for the Commission to re-consider whether a condition
of approval made at the June 2, 2022, Planning Commission meeting was ordinance based and warranted
modification or removal to resolve legal concerns with the original decision.
After the Commission meeting on June 2, 2022, the developer appealed the 1th condition of approval,
which stated that the developer shall “provide a sewer easement to the Doxey property”—the triangle
piece. While the developer was entitled to go through the appeal process, staff wanted to give the
Planning Commission an opportunity to discuss the condition and either modify or remove it to
comply with State Code and legal precedence.
Accordingly, staff recommend either the removal of condition number 11, or the Commission amend the
condition with the following language:
11. The applicant work with the Doxey property owners on the possibility of providing a sewer
easement to said property.
Ben Duzet from focus engineering stated they would work with the property owner to work something
out but did not think the requirement was necessary.
