# WFRC Publications & Document Series Inventory

Compiled 2026-07-22 from live fetches of wfrc.utah.gov (the site migrated from wfrc.org, which now 301-redirects; deep `wfrc.org/...` document URLs still resolve), wasatchchoice.org, unifiedplan.org, data.wfrc.utah.gov, and targeted searches. Organized by category; one entry per series.

---

## 1. Long-Range Planning

**Regional Transportation Plan (RTP)** | quadrennial (4-yr federal cycle) | vintages: 2007–2030, 2011–2040 ("Charting Our Course"), 2015–2040, 2019–2050, **2023–2050 (adopted May 2023)**, 2027–2055 (in progress, preferred-scenario comment closed May 2026) | PDF main doc + lettered PDF appendices + Google Sheets project list + ArcGIS web map | hub: https://wfrc.utah.gov/regional-plans/regional-transportation-plan/ ; adopted: .../adopted-rtp/ ; archive: https://wfrc.utah.gov/regional-plans/past-regional-transportation-plans/ | **The richest structured artifact set WFRC produces.** 2023–2050 has 13 appendices A–M, including: **Appendix H Phased Project Lists by Mode — a live Google Sheets workbook of all road/transit/AT projects with phase (1/2/3), cost, mode** (docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1eiiMX_ZeI-_6f8gLD3QxOBnqXlLYv9y6A7EWQLYIvjc); Appendix F Revenue & Cost Assumptions and G Revenue Sources (financial plan dollar tables); E Project Selection Criteria + I Needs-Based Phasing Criteria (scoring rubrics/weights); J System Performance Report (federal PM baselines/targets); K Congestion Management Process; M System Resiliency. Archive depth: full docs to 2007 (2015 plan: 17 appendices; 2011: 24; 2007: 15 + Financial Plan "Technical Report 47"); page references Technical Reports back to the 1960s–80s.

**RTP Amendments** | ~annual between plans (2023–2050 has had ≥4; 2019–2050 had 4; 2015–2040 had 6) | PDF resolutions + revised project tables | https://wfrc.utah.gov/regional-plans/regional-transportation-plan/adopted-rtp/amendments/ | Project-level adds/deletes/phase changes with costs.

**Wasatch Choice Vision (2050 → "Wasatch Choice for 2054")** | updated with each RTP cycle | interactive web map + embedded dashboards + PDF toolbox guides; **no single standalone vision PDF currently linked** (wasatchchoice.org/vision/ is a 404 — flag) | https://wasatchchoice.org/ ; vision map: https://wfrc.org/wasatch-choice-map/ ; land-use map update May 2025 vs 2023 comparison: https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/11da5f7919534d61b609fb28ec15c488 | Map layers: transportation, land use, economic development, recreation, Community Development Areas/centers.

**Wasatch Choice Progress Indicators** | recurring dashboard (update cadence not stated) | embedded interactive dashboard | https://wasatchchoice.org/resources/progress-indicators/ | 5 quantified indicators on homes/jobs alignment with transportation and amenities — accountability metrics for the Vision.

**Wasatch Choice Toolbox guides** | one-off/evergreen (mostly 2023 file dates) | PDFs | https://wasatchchoice.org/resources/ | Series of technical guides: *Creating Communities: Guide to Walkable Centers*, *Utah Street Connectivity Guide* (+appendix), *Utah AT Plan Standards* (5-13-2023), *Public Engagement Guidebook*, plus resource pages for Complete Streets, Form-based Code, Envisioning/Implementing Centers, Parking Modernization, Housing & Opportunity Assessment, Great Streets, Envision Tomorrow Plus. Mostly qualitative guidance; AT Plan Standards contains standard metrics/level-of-service tables.

**Utah's Unified Transportation Plan** | quadrennial, joint with UDOT/UTA/all 4 MPOs | website + statewide interactive map | https://unifiedplan.org/ ; statewide map: https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/e2b10d6141cc4d91900f783dbdeefed5/ | Aggregates all member-agency project lists statewide; hosts the shared tools below (traffic volume map, HTS, housing inventory explorer).

**Morgan County–Ogden Valley RPO Long-Range Plan (2019–2050)** and **Tooele Valley RPO 2050 plan elements** | one-off per RPO cycle | PDFs (transit element summary, bicycle route types & phasing map, highway phasing map, comments/responses) | https://wfrc.utah.gov/committees/tooele-and-morgan-county-rural-planning-organizations/ | Rural project lists with phasing — WFRC staffs both RPOs.

## 2. Short-Range Programming (TIP)

**Transportation Improvement Program (TIP)** | annual (6-year rolling window) | PDF project tables + ArcGIS interactive map per vintage | hub: https://wfrc.utah.gov/regional-plans/transportation-improvement-program/ | **Core structured series: per-vintage "TIP Project Table Listing" PDF — hundreds of projects with sponsor, county, funding program (STP/CMAQ/TAP/CRP/state), phase, and year-by-year dollar amounts** (~$50M/yr federal administered; "billions" over 6 years). Verified vintages on-site: 2026–2031 adopted (TIPProjectTableListing2026-2031.pdf), 2025–2030, draft 2027–2032 (comment map open to Aug 2, 2026). Older vintages exist at predictable URLs though delisted from the hub page: `.../Programs/TransportationImprovementProgram/2024_2029TIP/Draft2024_2029TIPProjectTables.pdf`, `2023_2028TIP/...`, `2022_2027TIP/...` (flag: pre-2025 archive only via direct URL or TIP coordinator Ben Wuthrich). Each vintage also carries: public comments list + summary, approval resolution, FHWA TIP/STIP conformity concurrence letter.

**TIP Amendments / Board Modifications** | multiple per year | PDF resolutions (e.g. RESOL 2025-2030 Amendment One/Two) + Trans Com packet memos | linked from the TIP hub and Trans Com meeting packets | Project-level funding changes with dollars; the Trans Com packets contain the amendment detail tables.

**Annual Federal Obligation Report** ("annual listing of obligated projects," federally required) | annual | PDF | https://wfrc.utah.gov/Programs/TransportationImprovementProgram/FederalObligationReports/2024FederalObligationReport.pdf and ...2023... | Table of every project with federal funds obligated that FY: project, sponsor, program, obligated $. **Flag: only 2023 and 2024 currently linked; earlier years not found on the new site.**

**Federal Funds Project Submissions (annual call for projects)** | annual | Google Drive PDFs, one per urbanized area (Salt Lake; Ogden–Layton) | linked from TIP hub | Candidate project lists with requested $ by program.

**Process documents** | evergreen | PDFs | *IIJA Competitive Programs guide*, *Process for Approving and Revising the RTP and TIP*, RTP/TIP flow chart, under `.../TransportationImprovementProgram/HelpfulLinksDownloads/`.

## 3. Air Quality

**Air Quality Conformity Memoranda** | one per RTP/TIP action (~1–2/yr) | numbered PDF series ("AQ Memo #") | archive: https://wfrc.utah.gov/resources/air-quality/ with files under `/Programs/AirQuality/AirQualityMemoArchive/` | **Numbered series runs Memo 27 → 43a (21 archived docs), covering 2015–2040, 2019–2050, 2023–2050 RTPs and each TIP (suffixed a/b, e.g. 42A for 2025–2030 TIP, 42B for 2026–2031, 43a for draft 2027–2032)**. Structured content: modeled emissions tables (tons/day of precursors) vs. SIP motor-vehicle emissions budgets by pollutant, analysis year, and nonattainment/maintenance area. Plus FHWA conformity approval letters and a 2019 Air Quality Report.
