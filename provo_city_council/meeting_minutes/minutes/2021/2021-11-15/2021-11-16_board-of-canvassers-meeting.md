                                                       CANVASS REPORT
                                                          2021 General
                                                     Election Provo, Utah

Provo City held a vote-by-mail General Election in conjunction with Utah County on November 2, 2021. A
general election was held for the office of Mayor, Council City-Wide I, Council District 2, and Council District
5.

Mayor
There were 43,986 active registered voters for the Mayor position with 14,426 ballots cast for a voter
turnout of 32.8%

Council City-Wide I
There were 43,986 active registered voters for the Council City-Wide I position with 14,369 ballots cast for a
voter turnout of 32.7%

Council District 2
There were 5,072 registered voters for the Council District 2 position with 1,153 ballots cast for a voter
turnout of 22.7%

Council District 5
There were 6,783 registered voters for the Council District 5 position with 1,593 ballots cast for a voter
turnout of 23.5%

After review by the County, 14,597 ballots were counted, 319 were not counted. Of those 319 ballots, 54
were returned unsigned, 56 signatures did not match, 64 ballots returned from past election, 1 ballot was
received without an envelope or affidavit, and 144 had invalid postmarks. There were 24 provisional ballots
issued and 1 was not counted because they had already voted by absentee ballot.

Individual results of the election can be found in Resolution 2021-37. Candidates elected for office to serve
terms of four years, beginning January 3, 2022, are as follows:

        Mayor:                     Michelle Kaufusi
        Council City-Wide I:       Katrice MacKay
        Council District 2:        George Handley
        Council District 5:        Rachel Whipple


Information by precinct will be provided by the County and made available on the voteprovo.com website
following the official certification of the Canvass Report.

I certify the information reported in this Canvass Report and accompanying resolution is accurate.
