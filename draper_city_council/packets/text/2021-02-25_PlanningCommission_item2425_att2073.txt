BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 3.442
acres located on the west side of 1700 E, at approximately 1661 E 12435 S (Exhibit B). The
property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be
approved to allow for the amendment of the Godfrey Subdivision for the creation of 5 lots.

The Godfrey Subdivision was originally recorded in 2012, and the applicant has changed
the Lot design to increase the subdivision from four to five Lots. Lot 1 is not included in the
boundary of the amendment and no changes to the lot are proposed.

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately
one dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit E). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little
impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the
city's semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these
zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by
commercial and industrial uses.” The subdivision is surrounded by the RA2 zone.

Subdivision Layout. The proposed Plat Amendment will add an additional lot to the Godfrey
Subdivision. Additionally, the access to the lots will change from a private road with a
standard hammerhead fire turn around to a private road that ends in a cul-de-sac (Exhibit
E). The private lane will be 30-feet wide with a 100-foot wide diameter cul-de-sac. DCMC

Section 9-27-150 requires a minimum 24-foot wide private lane for subdivisions serving up
to five units. The private lane will be owned and maintained by private individuals in the
subdivision through recorded CC&R’s. All lots will comply with the minimum lot size
standard of 20,000 square feet and range in size from 20,002 square feet to 66,751 square
feet.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        ‘The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended’.
