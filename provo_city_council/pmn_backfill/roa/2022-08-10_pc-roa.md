 ITEM #1        Matt W. Brown requests Concept Plan approval for a 6-unit townhome development in a proposed LDR
                (Low Density Residential) zone, located at 193 West 4800 North. Riverbottoms Neighborhood. Aaron
                Ardmore (801) 852-6404 aardmore@provo.org PLCP20210401



 The following action was taken by the Planning Commission on the above described item at its regular meeting of
 August 10, 2022:


                                                   APPROVED
 On a vote of 4:0, the Planning Commission approved the above noted application.


 Motion By: Miles Miller
 Second By: Robert Knudsen
 Votes in Favor of Motion: Miles Miller, Robert Knudsen, Melissa Kendall, Lisa Jensen
 Lisa Jensen was present as Chair.

 The Planning Commission gave a positive recommendation on the associated Zone Map Amendment in PLRZ20210400
 (item #2 at the August 10th hearing).

*6 Total Units

*Standard Land Use Code 1112

*18 Total parking stalls required
*24 Total parking stalls provided
*3 Required parking stalls per unit

STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations. David Day (Provo City Engineering) also answered commissioner questions regarding the estimated
trips of the proposal, the traffic flow from the site, and confirmed that the current design works for the proposed project.

CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Matt Brown noted that the owner may keep one or two of the units, that the open space adjacent to the river will
       remain common area, and that access to the front doors and changes in architecture will be shown at the Project
       Plan approval stage.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Robert Knudsen inquired about the access to 4800 North from the project.
   • Melissa Kendall confirmed that the occupancy would be a family occupancy for the townhomes.
   • Lisa Jensen asked staff what would be allowed on the property without a zone change, staff confirmed only a
       single-family home would be permitted.
   • The commission discussed needed improvements to the street-side elevation, access to the front doors of the units,
       and their overall appreciation of the concept plan.
