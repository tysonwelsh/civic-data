Resolution Amending Policy #2 – Purchasing, Contracting, and Procurement

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution amending Policy #2 – Purchasing,
Contracting, and Procurement:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-09-01                                                 Date: September 5, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       AMENDING POLICY #2 – PURCHASING, CONTRACTING, AND
       PROCUREMENT ADDING A SECTION TO THE POLICY TO INCLUDE THE USE
       OF APPROVED VENDORS FOR THE PURCHASE OF PRODUCTS AND
       SERVICES

                                           RECITALS


                                                3
DATE           THURSDAY                            SEPTEMBER                            5, 2019

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for White
      City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. On January 4, 2018 the Council adopted Ordinance 18-01-01 Creating Policy #2 –
      Purchasing, Contracting and Procurement (“Policy #2”) to provide a process for White City
      to procure goods and services for the operation of the municipality.

   D. During the Council meeting of August 1, 2019 it was directed by the Council that Policy #2
      be amended to add a section to allow the Purchasing Agent and Council to procure goods
      and services using the Approved Vendor Lists maintained by the State of Utah and Salt
      Lake County, respectively.

   E. During the development of the Policy #2 amendments, it was discovered the White City
      Water Improvement District Approved Vendors List also provided a valuable resource for
      the Municipality to procure services.

   F. Using Approved Vendors can make White City’s procurement of goods and services
      effective and efficient; and save the municipality money.

                                        RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby
approves the attached amendment to Policy #2, hereinafter to be known as Exhibit A, to allow the
use of Approved Vendors for the provision of goods and services for the municipality.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 5th day of
SEPTEMBER, 2019.
