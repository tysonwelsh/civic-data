Work Session
(4:31 P.M.)
1)
Work Session, one year review and discussion of possible amendment to increase the number of residents from 16 to 24 of a Conditional Use Permit for a Rehabilitation Facility at 241 South Highland Drive. Jim Huffman, Applicant.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner. (4:32 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
June 11, 2024 Report Final.pdf
Principal Planner, Ray Milliner, introduced for discussion the yearly review of the rehabilitation facility and the request to increase the number of residents from 16 to 24.
Jim Huffman,
Applicant
, provided a review of the past year to the Commission and expressed his appreciation for the Commission Members.
Commissioner Hawley joined the meeting at 4:41 P.M.
Commissioners asked questions of the applicant to which Mr. Huffman and Planner Milliner replied.
Planner Milliner asked for clarification on next steps. Commissioners responded.
Commissioners expressed their comfort level with increasing the number of residents and provided final thoughts on what they would like to hear more about before moving to a public hearing.

2)
Public Hearing and Action regarding a Conditional Use Permit/Master Planned Development for a 26,431 SF expansion of an existing Public Facility, the Summit County Justice Center, located at 6300 Justice Center Road. GSBS Architects, Applicants. Administrative Process. Project #24-028.
Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner. (6:02 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Staff Report SBPC 6.11.24.pdf
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner
, presented a Conditional Use Permit/Master Planned Development for an expansion of the Summit County Justice Center for public hearing and action.
Jeff Bolinger,
GSBS Architects
, presented to the Commission providing site plans, elevations, and signage.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions to which Mr. Bolinger, Planner Strader, and John Angell,
Public Works Director,
responded.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:22 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:22 p.m.
Commissioners provided additional comments to the discussion.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the CUP and MPD for the Summit County Justice Center expansion pursuant to the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Bruce Carmichael seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0).
3)
Public Hearing and Recommendation for an amendment to chapter 6.1, Moderate Income Housing, of the Snyderville Basin General Plan.
Elyse Kats, Economic Development and Housing Manager & Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner. (6:28 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner
, and Elyse Kats,
Economic Development and Housing Manager
, presented to the Commission the annual review of the Moderate Income Housing plan in order to be in compliance with State requirements.
Jeff Jones,
Economic Development Director
, provided additional information to the discussion.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions to which Ms. Kats responded.
Commission Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:44 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:45 p.m.
Commissioners provided additional comments.
Makena Hawley made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council regarding amendments to Chapter 6.1 of the General Plan pursuant to the findings of fact, and conclusion of law. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0).
