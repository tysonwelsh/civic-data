                                        PROCLAMATION
                                  National Clean Energy Week


      WHEREAS, Draper, Utah utilizes readily abundant forms of diverse energy resources that
are powering homes, businesses, and schools across our community; and

      WHEREAS, Utah’s efficient baseload resources, including fossil, geothermal, biomass, and
hydro, support the development of Utah’s rapidly growing intermittent solar and wind
resources;

      WHEREAS, the effectiveness of Utah’s ‘all-of-the-above’ market-based approach has been
demonstrated with significant environmental improvements, including a thirteen percent
carbon-emission reduction state-wide from 2007 to 2019;

      WHEREAS, Utah is one of seven states with utility-scale electricity generation from
geothermal sources, and has some of the best geothermal potentials in the nation; and

       WHEREAS, the renewable energy sector has created thousands of new direct jobs
throughout the past several years, with nearly 8,000 jobs in Utah supported by the industry
today;

       WHEREAS, we encourage individuals, local governments, corporations, and other
organizations in Draper to champion common sense solutions that address the United States’
evolving economic and energy needs; and
      WHEREAS, we must cultivate the power of Utah’s entrepreneurs and small businesses and
ensure government collaboration to assure Utah’s and the United States continued energy
leadership in the global marketplace and assure low-cost, reliable energy here at home;
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Troy K. Walker, Mayor of Draper City, State of Utah, along with members of
the Draper City Council do hereby proclaim September 26-September 30, 2022 as National
Clean Energy Week. We urge all citizens to recognize this observance.
SIGNED THIS 4TH DAY OF OCTOBER, 2022.
