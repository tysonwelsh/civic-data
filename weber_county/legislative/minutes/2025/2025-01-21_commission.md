G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVAL OF FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 16-2-9 SOLID
      WASTE FEES OF THE WEBER COUNTY CODE.
            Sean Wilkinson (Community Development Director): This adjusts the fee schedule for residential solid waste
            collection and disposal services for unincorporated Weber County and Uintah Highlands to account for
            contractual increases. This ordinance also reduces the County’s monthly administrative fee from $2 to $1.50.
            Residents in western Weber County will see a decrease of $0.79 per quarter and residents in Uintah Highlands
            will see a decrease of $0.85 per quarter.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Ordinance 2024-23 amending certain fees to the Peery’s Egyptian
            Theater and Ogden Eccles Conference Center; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
     2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 06-2025 ADOPTING THE PRE-DISASTER MITIGATION PLAN.
            Lisa Gosling (Economic Development Director): We have been working on our pre-disaster mitigation plan
            that includes our cities, special districts, and unincorporated Weber County. It is a required 5 year plan that
            qualifies us for FEMA grants and assistance. This plan includes goals, strategies, and actions to reduce long
         term risk. We are submitting our final plan to FEMA, who has concurred we can go ahead and ask for adoption
         with any changes being added later.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 06-2025 adopting the Pre-Disaster Mitigation Plan;
         Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    3. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH ALTA EXCAVATION FOR THE 2200 SOUTH
       IMPROVEMENT PROJECT.
         Ashley Thoman (Engineering): This contract was bid out to general contractors with over 10 bids received.
         Alta Excavation had the lowest bid. The project will widen 2200 South from 4300 West to the west side of
         the new high school, creating a middle turn lane allowing students to turn into the parking lot. This is funded
         from Lower Valley Roads impact fees, Lowe Valley Storm impact fees, and some road maintenance money to
         mill and overlay the existing asphalt. Work will be done by August 1st before the new school year.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with Alta Excavation for the 2200 South Improvement
         Project; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    4. APPROVAL OF A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH
       DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC SAFETY TO ACCEPT ONE-TIME GRANT FUNDING FROM THE
       DPS TO FUND MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES.
         Commissioner Harvey: This is an agreement with the State to receive one-time grant money.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a Memorandum of Agreement with the State of Utah Department of
         Public Safety to accept one-time grant funding from the DPS to fund mental Health services; Commissioner
         Froerer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    5. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH ROY CITY FIRE AND RESCUE TO PROVIDE COUNTY
       WIDE PARAMEDIC SERVICES.
         Eli Johnson (Sheriff’s Department, Homeland Security): We contract with local fire districts and city fire
         rescue departments to provide paramedic services. This contract is a renewal of a contract with Roy City Fire
         and Rescue. It is a five year contract based on increases to the salary offset negotiated by Roy Fire and Rescue
         during budget hearings earlier this year.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with Roy City Fire and Rescue to provide county wide
         paramedic services; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    6. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH WEBER FIRE DISTRICT TO PROVIDE COUNTY WIDE
       PARAMEDIC SERVICES.
         Eli Johnson (Sheriff’s Department, Homeland Security): Same as (Item G5) above but with the Weber Fire
         District.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with Weber Fire District to provide county wide paramedic
         services; Commissioner Freorer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
