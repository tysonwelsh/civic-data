WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 12, 2021

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Almquist at 10:30 a.m. on August 12, 2021, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Gil Almquist, Commissioner Adam Snow, County Attorney
Eric Clarke, Clerk/Auditor Susan Lewis, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance included: Tom Kuhlmann, Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service
District

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PRESENTATION ON PROPOSED HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL
SERVICE DISTRICT 2022 BUDGET AND BUDGETING PROCESS

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlmann explained that the coverage area has grown
immensely in the Hurricane Valley area since 2013. Due to this growth, the response time
to calls has dropped as staff shortages have been insufficient to cover the needs. Other
concerns for the area include event growth, overlapping calls, insufficient growth formula
for taxes, impact fee restrictions, station location, and regional partners. The Hurricane
Valley Fire response is also county wide when assisting with other wildland fires and
mutual aid needs.

The 2022 budget is significantly behind where it should be as there is a need for six (6)
additional fire stations. There should be four (4) full time staff at each fire station 24/7,
however when calls come in, staff is pulled away due to the shortage of staffing and fire
stations. Due to the construction of a new hospital in Hurricane City, HVF will lose a
substantial amount of funding, as transportation will no longer be needed to St. George.

The last truth in taxation hearing for District was held in 2017. The funding proposal is to
hold a truth in taxation hearing by September 20, 2021 and raise the taxes within the district
by approximately 18% - 25% taking effect in 2022. In order for an increase to take place
the County must make the contribution and hold the truth in taxation hearing as it is the
taxing authority for the district.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
