APPROVAL OF A REVISED FIRST READING OF FEE ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS RELATING
TO THE WEBER COUNTY TRANSFER STATION AND RESIDENTIAL SOLID WASTE
COLLECTION AND DISPOSAL.

Sean Wilkinson (Director, Community Development): Last week was the First Reading for this Ordinance.
Since that time, we received the annual increase from Waste Management for the residential service they
provide in Ogden Valley and we wanted to include this with this Ordinance. After meeting with the Solid
Waste Subcommittee, they recommended to WACOG, with WACOG agreeing last night, to implement the
increase on April 1* in lieu of March 1* as originally proposed. Everything else remains unchanged from last
week, except the Ogden Valley adjustment. That adjustment follows the CP] index which is about 4.5%. Plus,
last year Waste Management had exceptional costs with fuel and other rising costs; they asked us for a large
increase and we asked them to spread it over two years. Part of that increase happened in 2022 with the
remaining 3% being added to the 4.5% CPI increase for a total of about 7.5% increase this year. The County’s
administration fee in Ogden Valley will remain at $2.61.

Commissioner Bolos moved for approval of a revised first reading of Fee Ordinance amendments relating to
the Weber County Transfer Station and Residential Solid Waste Collection and Disposal; Commissioner
Harvey seconded.

Commissioner Harvey — aye; Commissioner Bolos — aye; Chair Froerer — aye

2. Public hearing to consider and take action on an ordinance and development agreement to rezone 5
acres of property from A-2 to C-3. Property located at 1941 S 5100 W, Ogden. Applicant is Randy
Ropelato, (ZMA 2021-07).

Steve Burton (Planning): This is an applicant driven rezoning request to change zoning of five acres

from A-2 to C-3. This property falls within an approximately 20 acre area deemed by the recently

mechanisms in our Ordinances that will provide for a good looking site with decent architecture with
good landscaping. Not all uses allowed in C3 zones fit with the intent of Business-Tech; they are not

agricultural projects. One item the applicant would like is a contractor shop onsite which is

improvement costs for contributing to 5100 West construction, of which the portion is not yet
defined by the County, and a ten year window for completing the project. On September 17", 2022,
the Planning Commission forwarded a unanimous approval with intent there would be a
development agreement, which is now complete. There are no changes to the project since that
approval.
