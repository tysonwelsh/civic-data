MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-05-09
Re: Public Hearing: Big Willow Phase 9 Subdivision Plat Amendment (Administrative
Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 6.09 acres located on the south side of 11400 S and west of Engelmann
Drive, at approximately 11492 S. Engelmann Drive. The property is currently zoned R3
and R4. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment be approved to
allow for the development of the currently vacant site with 32 single-family lots.



This property is part of the overall Big Willow Creek development. The Big Willow Creek
Development agreement was approved in January 2017 for 115 dwelling units on 49.75
acres. The approval included a Development Agreement limiting the density to a
maximum of 2.3 dwelling units per acre (du/ac), and required a variety of lot sizes
between 10,000 square feet and 20,000 square feet in size. Preliminary plat approval for
all 115 lots was approved by the Planning Commission on July 18, 2017. Phases 1A and 1B
obtained final plat approval on September 18, 2018, with an amended plat approved for
these phases on November 21, 2019.



In April 2019 the applicant amended the development agreement (1st Amendment
Development Agreement) in order to add an additional 18.1 acres of property into the Big
Willow Creek Development. The 1st Amendment of the Development Agreement rezoned
some of the additional 18.1 acres of property to the RM2 zone, but limited any
development to a maximum of 9 du/ac. The remainder of the property was rezoned to
R4.



On December 10, 2019 the applicant obtained a rezone and land use amendment for an
additional 4.12 acres of land located adjacent to the development. This area was not
included in the development agreements at the time of rezone. On May 5, 2020, the
Development Agreement was amended a second time (Second Amendment
Development Agreement) and added the 4.12 acres of property to the agreement, set
density standards for development on the additional properties added to the agreement,
and set standards for the approval and development of Phase 2.



Phase 1A was vacated by the City Council on April 6, 2020 to allow for the reconfiguration
of the access road. The applicant obtained final subdivision plat approval for Phase 2 on
August 27, 2020. Big Willow Creek Phase 6 was approved on April 7, 2021, as a minor
subdivision, and included reconfiguration of the access road from 11400 South. Phase 1B
obtained approval to reconfigure the existing roadway into a cul-de-sac on April 22, 2021.



The Phase 3 final plat was approved by the Planning Commission on August 26, 2021
after another amendment to the development agreement (Third Amendment) was
approved on April 20, 2021. The Third Amendment to the development agreement which
set the standards for approval for the Phase 3 plat.



Findings for Approval:

1. There is good cause for the amendment, and no public street or municipal utility
easement will been vacated or amended.



Findings for Denial:

1.   Good cause does not exist for the amendment.
