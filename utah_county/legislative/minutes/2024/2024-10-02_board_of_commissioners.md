NOMINATION.

I would like to nominate Deputy Wyatt Topham for the Utah County Employee of
the Month. Deputy Topham has worked at the Sheriff's office for over seven years.

He is dedicated to his position in the jail and always goes out of his way to teach
all the newer deputies to help set them up for success in their careers.

During Labor Day weekend Deputy Topham took the initiative to power wash the
recreation yards for the inmates. During his night shift rotation, he brought his
own pressure washer and gas and took the time to go to each recreation yard and
make sure the floor and all windows were cleaned. He was assisted by Deputy
Sorensen.

Deputy Topham always makes each shift enjoyable with his humor and his
computer skills (if you know, you know). He organizes potluck dinners with a
different theme each month, which has been a great asset in changing up the
menus and showcases the other team members' skills and talents in cooking.

Deputy Topham has a great attitude and excellent work ethic. Since becoming my
Deputy in Charge, he has become a true leader with his roll and can handle every
situation that has been thrown his way no matter how difficult the situation.
