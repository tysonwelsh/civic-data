Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present, except for
Councilor Hancock who is excused. Councilor Albrecht gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Fiber Network Update - Shaye Ruitenbeek
Shaye Ruitenbeek, with the Fiber Department, gave an update on the project. She stated that they
have all the signed ISP agreements. They are finalizing pricing and can release that information
next month. Once they begin construction, they’ll push for more pre-registrations. They are also
finalizing the backbone design and progress reports are available to the public.

2.2 The Point Project Update - Alan Matheson
Scott Cuthbertson gave an update on the Point Project. The goals of the project are to maximize
job creation, promote qualify of life, less car dependence and more environmentally consciences.
He stated that they are soliciting feedback from residents and encouraging public engagement.
Demolition of the site will begin soon, and they are in the process of selecting an initial
development partner. They anticipate that it will take 25 years to build out the entire project.

2.3 Finance Update
Dean Lundell, Finance Director, stated that generally things are still positive. The city saw a 10%
increase in July for sales tax revenue. He stated that the utility fund balances are where they
expect them to be. He also gave an update on the budget amendment that will be considered. He
suggested that they re-evaluate nonresident fees for recreation.

2.4 Discussion of Revisions Needed for Wisper Hollow Estates to meet UDOT Railroad
Crossing Standards- Planning Department.

7. Consideration of Ordinance #68-2022 the Good and the Beautiful General Plan
   amendment on 9-acres of property located at 700 South Mill Pond Road changing the
   land use designation from Mixed Use to Heavy Commercial.
   Daniel and Jenny Phillips were representing the applicant. Mrs. Phillips stated that they are an
   educational publishing company that caters to home schooling curriculum. She stated that the
   back of the building will be for supplies and the front will be for retail and also a recital hall
   that they will be affordable for public use.

    Tony Trane was representing the applicant. He stated that there were discussions with Planning
    on what might be the best approach for this concept. He said that a Development Agreement
    was discussed and they are open to that option.

    The Council liked the concept but had concerns with unintended future consequences if they
    approved the proposed land use. The Council agreed that they would prefer to maintain the
    General Plan Land Use for this location, but they would be open to a development agreement
    that would allow this use.
