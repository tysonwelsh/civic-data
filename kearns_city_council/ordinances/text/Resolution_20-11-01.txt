                         KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

                                  RESOLUTION #2020-11-01

                                       November 19, 2020

  A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP’S TENTATIVE
                          2021 BUDGET


       WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Kearns is required to adopt a tentative budget for the upcoming fiscal year
pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-111; and

        WHEREAS, Kearns prepared a budget for the upcoming 2021 fiscal year and desires to
adopt it as its Tentative 2021 Budget.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

        1.     The attached budget is hereby adopted as Kearns’ tentative budget for the 2021
fiscal year.

       2.      A public hearing shall be held on November 25, 2020, at 6:00 p.m. via WebEx and
telephone, to receive public comments thereon, with notice thereof being duly provided pursuant
to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-113.

       3.       A copy of this tentative budget shall be made available for public inspection for a
period of at least ten days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-112.

        4.     This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
