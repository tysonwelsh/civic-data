NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg—March 27th @ 7pm.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          South Salt Lake resident, Lorrina Heisley, thanked the Council for their censure of
          and the sanctions placed on Council Member Sanchez at last week’s Special
          Meeting.

              Council Member Williams read a comment that she received via email from South
              Salt Lake resident, Courtney McBride, which stated that as a resident of District 5,
              they are happy with the service of the Police Department, the majority female
              Council, and are proud of the hard work that the City does. They addressed Council
              Member Sanchez’s claims of corruption and said that they find it disturbing that he
              has made condescending remarks towards the female Council Members. Due to his
              focus on calling people out for his own personal dislikes and ignoring the needs of
              his district, they would like to call for Council Member Sanchez’s resignation.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood talked about some upcoming City events:

              March 18th – The Lions Park off-leash dog area will be closed after this date for
              repairs until June 2024. Residents may take their dogs to the off-leash dog park near
              City Hall at 150 East Oakland Avenue.

              March 30th – The annual Easter Egg Hunt & Tiny Tots Fair will be held at the Central
              Park Community Center from 9-10:30 a.m. The Egg Hunt will start promptly at 10
              a.m., and there will be a butterfly release as well.

              April 4th – The City will offer ‘Flood Preparedness Training’ for residents at the South
              Salt Lake Community Center from 6-7:30 p.m. For more information, residents can
              contact the Emergency Management Coordinator, Yasmin, at 801-464-6713.

              May 4th – The annual Mural Fest will have 14 artists featured this year and will focus
              around the S-Line area. For more information, residents can go to themuralfest.com
