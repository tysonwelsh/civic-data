3.         21-395                 Administrative Services Department recommending that the Council Adopt
                                  Resolution 21-38C authorizing the execution and delivery of a bond
                                  purchase agreement, a supplemental indenture of trust, and a sales tax
                                  interlocal pledge and loan agreement between the City and the
                                  Redevelopment Agency of Sandy City, Utah
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Resolution 21-38C Approving Interlocal Agreement - Sandy RDA
                                  STRR 2021.pdf
                                  Brett Neuman, Finance Department, introduced Jason Burningham with Lewis, Young
                                  who provided an overview of the Resolutions under consideration by the Council. The
                                  purpose of Resolution 21-38C and RD 21-06 is to give authorization to issue bonds and
                                  outline terms of that transaction, for the purpose of refunding (refinancing) our 2007
                                  Soccer Stadium Bonds to achieve savings on the debt payments. Resolution 21-38C is
                                  the City's side of that transaction. Specifically, it formalizes agreements associated with
                                  the City's pledge of sales tax revenues to cover new bond payments, should other
                                  revenues be insufficient. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Public comment opened.
                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren suggested the Council keep the maximum interest rate allowable in
                                  the parameters resolution as low as possible.
                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Following the vote on the motion, Council moved to Standing Reports.


                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Monica Zoltanski, to
                                  adopt Resolution 21-38C, a Resolution authorizing the execution and delivery of
                                  a bond purchase agreement, a supplemental indenture of trust, and a sales tax
                                  interlocal pledge and loan agreement between the City and the Redevelopment
                                  Agency of Sandy City, Utah (The "Agency") in connection with the issuance by
                                  the Agency of its transient room tax and sales tax revenue refunding bonds,
                                  series 2021, in the aggregate principal amount of not to exceed $18,000,000 in
                                  one or more series, and with such additional or alternate designations as the
                                  Agency may determine; and related matters... The motion carried by the
                                  following vote:

                          Yes:    7-    Alison Stroud
                                        Kris Nicholl
                                        Zach Robinson
                                        Monica Zoltanski
                                        Marci Houseman
                                        Cyndi Sharkey
                                        Brooke Christensen

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto updated the council on upcoming Agenda items. There is a special
                      recognition and social media presentation next week. He also reminded the Council that
                      the Nov 2nd meeting is cancelled due to the election.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Brooke Christensen mentioned that the CDBG committee meeting is
                      tomorrow evening at 5:00 pm and can be accessed by a Zoom link.

                      Council Member Marci Houseman thanked the residents who came out to the community
                      connectedness workshop last week. We will hold future workshops. She also thanked
                      James Sorenson and his staff for their help with a resident's concerns and acknowledged
                      Dan Medina and the Parks staff for coordinating the upcoming Trunk or Treat community
                      event.

                      Mayor Bradburn had no report.
