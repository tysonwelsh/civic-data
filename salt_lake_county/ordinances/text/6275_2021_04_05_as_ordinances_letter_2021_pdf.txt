FROM: Talia Butler, Division Director 
Animal Services Division 
 
RE: Updated Animal Ordinance 
 
 
 
The Animal Services Division has updated aspects of our 
ordinance that affect the manner in which we enforce 
animal laws. 
 
The majority of these suggested revisions are cosmetic or 
style-related and do not affect the substance or essence of 
the ordinance. Many of the changes provide for increased 
clarity and eliminate outdated practices. 
 
A summary of these changes follows, as well as a redlined 
copy of the ordinance in which revisions are proposed. 
 
Feel free to contact me directly regarding any comments or 
suggestions you may have regarding these proposed 
changes. We appreciate your review and feedback. 
Summary of Ordinance Title 8: Animals 
 
The attached ordinance contains several proposed revisions and deletions. The majority of 
suggested modifications do not change how we conduct business but update the ordinance 
to reflect current practices and provide an easier, more organized ordinance for the public 
to utilize. Below is a summary of all substantive changes: 
 
8.01.10 - Definitions 
Clarified the definitions of these terms: 
 
• Abandonment – includes intentionally depositing, leaving, or dropping off an animal with 
no intent to return, veterinary, clinic, commercial establishment and abandoned animal 
will be considered unclaimed stray animals subject to the statutory stray wait period for 
the purpose of this Title. 
• Adequate space – changes, the term dog to animal and removes “fixed to a tether, 
fastener, shelter, chain, tie, or other restraint”. 
• Animal Exhibition – Means any display of, event, spectacle, act, or contest in which 
animals are exhibited or used to provide a performance, whether a fee is charged or 
not. 
• Animal control officer – “or by Salt Lake County, through the division of animal services” 
has been removed. 
• Animal at large – defines owner restraint of an at-large animal and removes stipulation 
that unvaccinated and unlicensed animals are considered at-large. 
• Animal under physical restraint – clarifies that animals are not under restraint if left 
unattended and a passerby may come into contact with the animal. 
• Animal capable of being immediately controlled – removed. 
• Cat - means any feline of the domesticated type. 
• Dangerous Animal – elaborates on the characteristics of a dangerous animal. 
• Director - means any director or head of any municipal animal services agency within 
Salt Lake County 
• Division - means any municipal animal services agency within Salt Lake County. 
• Dog - means any canis familiaris. 
• Domestic fowl – Clarifies any variety of ducks or chickens regardless of age. 
• Exotic animal - means any animal whose native habitat is not indigenous to North 
American Continent. 
• Guard dog - Clarifies the duty to warn the handler of an intruder and to protect a 
commercial area that is being secured. 
• Handler – Clarifies the age and responsibilities. 
• Impound – clarifies that animals are considered “impounded” when in the control or 
custody of an officer, even if they are not at an animal shelter. 
• Microchip – Clarifies a secondary form of identification. 
• Nuisance – detailed definition of “nuisance animal” has been moved to Nuisance Animal 
section (8.06.010) 
• Owner – ownership previously referenced “harboring,” which has been moved to a 
separate definition. 
• Pet Shop – includes stores that sell birds and other pets, in addition to dogs and cats 
• Wild Animal – Clarifies the species of animals that fall under the jurisdiction of DWR. 
Added definitions of these terms: 
• Adequate fencing for Livestock 
• Animal Husbandry 
• Attack 
• Commercial Dog Walker 
• Community Cat 
• Custody 
• Harboring 
• Humane Treatment 
• Identification 
• Interference

Removed Definitions:

Chapter 8.02 – Administration 
 
8.02.030 –Enforcement Authority: Clarifies who is authorized to retain an animal if the 
public and/or animal is at serious risk and what type of penalty will be issued. 
 
8.02.040 – Powers and Duties: Removes “Volunteers” as an employee with enforcement 
powers. 
 
8.02.060 – Interference: Removes stipulations that would otherwise cause a person(s) to be 
in violation of Interfering with and employee or authorized representative of the division. 
 
Chapter 8.03 - Commercial Permits

8.03.020 –Permit fees–Expiration-Renewal: Identifies how long a permit is good and how a 
permit will be renewed. 
 
8.03.030 –Procedures (moved to 8.03.010)

businesses and facilities to be exempt from the permit requirements sections. 
 
8.03.035 –Sale of dogs, cats and rabbit at a commercial animal establishment - Combined

8.03.160 – Permits: (moved to 8.03.050) 
 
8.03.170 – Permit Fee-Expiration-Renewal: (moved to 8.03.020)

Chapter 8.04 Animals Requiring a License 
 
8.04.010 –  License Required: Identifies what animals need to license, when animals 
need to be licensed, and what is needed to get animals licensed. 
 
8.04.020 – License-Application)-Procedure: Stipulates how an owner will apply for 
an animal license and what is needed to obtain an animal license. 
 
8.04.030 – License-Application)-Procedure: (moved to 8.04.020) 
 
8.04.030 – Sterilization Verification: Stipulates that no animal will be licensed as 
sterilized  without verification of sterilization that such surgery has been performed. 
 
8.04.040 – Additional Requirements for Licensing of Ferrets: removed stipulation t hat 
first- time applicants for ferret licens es need to take a class and added that they will be        
provided with educational information regarding ferret ownership. 
 
8.04.040 – License-Fees: Identifies how license fees are established and who 
maintains  the fee schedule. 
 
8.04.050 – Sterilization Verification: (moved to 8.04.030) 
 
8.04.050 – License-Term and Renewal: Identifies how long a license is good for. 
 
8.04.060 – License—Fees—Senior Citizens: Removes senior citizen license for 
unsterilized  animals. Senior citizen licenses will be offered for sterilized animals only. 
Senior Citizens with unsterilized animals may purchase a standard pet license. (moved to 
8.04.040) 
 
8.04.060 – License Tag Requirements: Identifies how animal tags will be received, 
penalty  for failure to attach license and replacement tags. 
 
8.04.070 – License—Term and Renewal: Removes 2 and 3 year licenses that are no 
longer in the fee schedule (annual licenses only) and adds that late fee will be 
charged after a 30-day grace period. (moved to 8.04.050) 
 
8.04.070 – License Exemptions: Identifies which Dogs, Cats or Ferrets may be 
exempt  from being licensed. 
 
8.04.080 – Penalties for non-compliance: Identifies penalties for license violation(s). 
 
8.04.090 –(D) Microchip in Lieu of Tags – Clarifies which animals must wear tags. 
This  inadvertently exempted dogs from wearing identification tags if they were 
microchipped (moved to 8.04.060)

8.04.200 – Community Cats: Explains how animal control agencies will sterilize, rabies

Chapter 8.05 – Rabies Control 
 
8.05.010– Dog, Cat and Ferret rabies vaccination requirements: State that divisions will 
follow the National Compendium protocols. 
 
8.05.020 –Impoundment of animals without a valid vaccination tag: Establishes the 
procedure to reclaim an animal prior to disposition and establishes the authority  to 
quarantine any animal that may have been exposed to or may have exposed a person to 
rabies. 
 
8.05.030 –Rabid Animal Report: Establishes a procedure if an animal is suspected of rabies 
exposure and clarifies reporting and outlines methods of reporting to the appropriate agencies. 
 
8.05.040 –Impoundment of animals without a valid vaccination tag: (moved to 8.05.020) 
 
8.05.040 –Animals exposed to rabies: Establishes any animal potentially exposed to 
rabies, but is not available for testing, shall be regarded as having rabies. 
 
8.05.050 –Rabid Animal Report: (moved to 8.05.030) 
 
8.05.050 –Penalties for non-compliance: Establishes criteria for penalty to be issued. 
 
8.05.060 –Animals exposed to rabies: (moved to 8.05.040) 
 
Chapter 8.06 – Animal Violations 
 
8.06.010 – Nuisance: Identifies who is in violation of a nuisance animal and defines the 
types of nuisances listed in sections A thru D. 
 
8.06.020 – Animals-Running at Large: Defines what running at large is and what penalty 
will be issued. 
 
8.06.030 – Animal Trespass: Defines what animal trespass is and what penalty will 
be  issued. 
 
8.06.040 – Animals Prohibited or restricted in designated areas: Defines the violations as 
outlined in A thru D. 
 
8.06.050 – Harboring Animals: Identifies the type of violation and penalty. 
 
8.06.060 – Staking Animals Improperly – added that animals may not be tethered in a 
public  place unless supervised and properly restrained. 
 
8.06.070 – Staking and Tethering-Exemptions: As defined in sections A thru F. 
 
8.06.080 – Control and Fencing of Livestock: As defined in sections A thru D. 
 
8.06.090 – (Moved to 8.06.060) 
 
8.06.100 – Dangerous Animals: Identifies all animals designated as “dangerous” will 
require a dangerous animal permit. 
 
8.06.110 – Prohibitions Relating to Wild Animals-Exemptions: Identifies which agencies 
are exempt from keeping a wild animal. 
 
8.06.200 – Attacks by Animals: Added that pet owners are liable for damages to persons 
or   animals injured in an attack. 
 
8.06.210 – Animal Biters-Reporting Requirements: Identifies the time frame in which a bite 
needs to be reported to an agency and when an agency needs to report the bite to the 
appropriate animal control agency. 
 
Chapter 8.07 – Animal Cruelty 
 
8.07.010 – Care and Maintenance Responsibility: Defines the owners responsibilities 
required for the care of an animal and added that pets’ owners must provide 
veterinary care and grooming in addition to other basic care. 
 
8.07.020 – Cruelty Violations – Penalties: Identifies what type of penalty will be issued if 
an owner or handler is found in violation of this title. 
 
8.07.030 – Injury to Animal by Motorist: Duty to stop and assist: Adds that a motor vehicle 
operator shall report an incident in the absence of the animal’s owner and may  transport 
a hit animal for treatment in the absence of the animal’s owner. 
 
Chapter 8.08 – Impoundment 
 
8.08.010 – Animal Shelter and Facilities: Identifies the responsibility the divisions to provide 
suitable premises and facilities as outline in sections A thru D. 
 
8.08.020 – Impoundment Authorized-When: Establishes the authority to impound as outline 
in sections A thru C. 
 
8.08.030 – Terms of Impoundment: Clarifies the minimum holding period, voluntary 
relinquishment, medical attention and determination to destroy as outline in sections 
A thru D. 
 
8.08.040 – Redemption of Animals: Clarifies what is needed by the animal owner in order 
to redeem their animal. 
 
8.08.140 (C) - Tethering of dogs—Exemptions: Removes process for applying for extension    
to tethering hours. This extension is not offered. 
 
Chapter 8.09 - Dangerous or Vicious Dogs 
 
Creation of process to classify dogs as potentially dangerous, dangerous, or vicious. The 
section identifies requirements for insurance, confinement and restraint, and outlines an 
appeal process. 
 
Chapter 8.10 - Enforcement and Penalties 
 
8.10.020 –Issuance of Criminal Citations—Notice of Violations and Stipulation: Rewritten 
for clarity and includes Notice of Violation language repealed in Chapter 11. 
 
8.10.050 Repeated Dangerous or Vicious Animal Violations--Owner Debarment: Enacts  
process for owner debarment of repeat offenders. 
 
8.10.060 Seizure and Disposition—Written Evaluation Criteria: Enacts process for division  
to establish evaluation criteria for classification of dangerous or vicious dogs. 
 
8.10.070 Administrative Hearing Process—Animal Disposition or Destruction: Enacts  
hearing and appeal process for seizures of dangerous or vicious dogs. 
 
Chapter 8.11 - Notice of Violation and Stipulation Procedures 
 
Language moved to Chapter 10. 
 
Chapter 8.11 – Domestic Fowl 
 
8.11.050 – Number of Domestic Fowl – The total number of fowl kept was previously 
separated by adult fowl and baby chicks (e.g. three adults and three baby chicks). 
Because it is hard to define at what age a “baby” becomes an adult, and as all chicks grow 
into adults, the division proposes to create a total number of fowl allowed and not 
delineate by age. 
 
Chapter 8.12 – Feral Cats 
 
8.12.010 – Feral cats—Impoundment and Disposition: 
 Removes five-day holding period for feral and free-roaming cats in accordance with the  
Community Cat Act. Cats may be immediately released, in accordance with state law, as 
long as they are sterilized, vaccinated against rabies, ear-tipped, and returned to the 
location where they congregate. Allows division to track feral and unowned community 
cats. 
 
Chapter 8.14 – Dog Breeders 
 
Dog Breeders section removed and handled in animal care requirements.
