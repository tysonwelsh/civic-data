                              RESOLUTION NO. 23-43

             A RESOLUTION APPOINTING SILVIA ARAOZ TO THE DRAPER
             CITY DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION ADVISORY COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies with the city
as deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has established the Draper City Diversity
and Inclusion Advisory Committee to identify and advise on issues faced by diverse
groups and to create opportunities for an inclusive community within the City; and

       WHEREAS, the Council recognizes the importance of diversity and inclusion
within the City; and

      WHEREAS, Committee member Gary Thompson is unable to complete his
term as a member of the committee; and

      WHEREAS, Silvia Araoz has expressed a willingness to serve as a member of
the Diversity and Inclusion Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint and the Council hereby consents to
the appointment of Silvia Araoz to serve as a regular member on the Diversity and
Inclusion Advisory Committee to complete Gary Thompson’s term.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Appointment. The following individual is hereby appointed to
serve on the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Committee according to
the laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the committee.

      Regular Member:
           Silvia Araoz          August 15, 2023 through October 31, 2026



       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution
is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.
     Section 3. Effective Date.       This Resolution shall become effective
immediately its passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 15TH DAY OF AUGUST, 2023.
