#4 GGUSA Hyrum LLC Conditional Use Permit (CUP) 
Smith reviewed the staff report for the GGUSA Hyrum LLC CUP.

Staff and Commissioners discussed the wildfire hazard zone designation on the property. 
Casey Meracomie (SP) spoke as the applicant regarding the project. 
Watterson asked if it was a filtration and pressurization process. 
Casey responded it is actually splitting the gas and explained the process. 
Sands asked what it is being used for. 
Casey responded some will go to the boilers but most will go into the pipeline. 
Daugs motioned to approve the GGUSA Hyrum LLC Conditional Use Permit with the 19 conditions and 
3 conclusions; Sands seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
Ayes: Brady Christensen, Chris Sands, Jason Watterson, Nate Daugs 
Nays: 0 
05:56:00 
#5 Discussion: Honey Solar Code Amendment 
Smith reviewed the changes that came out of the joint planning commission/county council meeting. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the overlay zone, the permitted/conditional uses table, locations for 
small scale solar projects, location of commercial solar projects, rezoning vs conditional use permits, and 
questions on bonds. 
06:26:00 
#6 Discussion: Short Term Rentals 
Zetterquist reviewed the information for short term rentals. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed occupancy, if there is a short-term rental issue or an event issue, 
guest houses vs short term rental, recreational vs commercial use, and making the Agri-tourism, ADUs, 
and short-term rental codes congruent.
