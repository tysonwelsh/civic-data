MPO Technical Advisory Committee February 28, 2022 Page 3 Regional Transportation Plan Kickoff – Shawn Eliot, MAG (19:13) MAG’s TransPlan50 is updated every four years in an effort to remain relevant to emerging trends and political realities. It incorporates the latest travel demand models for the area including socioeconomic and land-use data. The objective of the TransPlan50 is to address local transportation needs and educate local communities on the regional needs to address growth in the regional transportation system. By working together, communities can find sound solutions and create local plans that reflect regional needs. To create the plan, MPO staff have tools to predict where future improvements are needed and start with local and regional land use plans. These inform MAG’s land use and travel models which then predict future traffic conditions. Work on the next update to the TransPlan50 has already begun and will be completed next year. New state 2050 population projections for Utah County are down from the last projection series (-112k). It is anticipated that Utah County will grow from 685k people today to 1.2 million by 2050 This growth will present major challenges for our transportation system. In late spring, MAG will conduct a series of Transportation Summits in the north, central, and south areas of the county and will invite a large group of decision makers and technical staff to these meetings for their respective areas. Invitees include mayors, county commissioners, city managers, city councils, planning commissions, city staff, and representatives from UDOT and UTA. Goals for theTransPlan50 update, including a timeline was presented. The goal of the meetings is to Educate, Engage and Inform on all things regional. Northwest Utah County Transit Study – Chad Eccles, MAG (30:17) The Northwest Utah County Transit Study looked at developing a transit system to better meet the needs of the communities of Eagle Mountain and Saratoga Springs as their population continues to grow. This study outlines near term steps for transit improvement and long-term steps for planning. Results from the study and possible solutions to resolve the transit gaps were discussed. MPO Technical Advisory Committee February 28, 2022 Page 4 South County Transit Study – Mary De La Mare-Schaeffer (44:12) The county and cities of southern Utah County partnered with UTA, UDOT, and MAG to perform the South County Transit Study. The purpose of the study is to analyze the implementation of a fixed guideway transit system. UTA purchased a railroad right-of-way with the intent of developing a commuter rail or fixed guideway transit service. This study was the first step of the process for developing this service. Results from the study and possible solutions were discussed. Other Business (55:45) MAG: The 2022 Moves Utah Summit will be held on March 16th and 17th.
