       3.   Update on City Council Discussions with UDOT on SR224 Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)
            Project
            Park City officials are actively collaborating with the Utah Department of Transportation
            (UDOT), Summit County, and High Valley Transit on the SR-224 Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)
            project, aiming to enhance public transit between Kimball Junction and Old Town. While
            the initiative seeks to alleviate traffic congestion and improve transit efficiency, it has
            prompted significant discussions regarding its design, cost, environmental, and community
            impacts. The City Council will provide an update on their discussions to date.
