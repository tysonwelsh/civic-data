2)
Public Hearing and Possible approval, Fox Run North Lot 1 Amended plat amendment, located at 1824 Fox Run North on parcel FRN-1, Wanship, Utah. Applicant: Troy Anderson. Project #23-172.
Administrative Review.
Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior County Planner. (6:02 PM)
Tiffanie N. Robinson,
Senior County Planner
, provided background information for a plat amendment for the Fox Run lot 1. Commission members asked brief clarifying questions and provided additional information. Troy Anderson, the
Applicant
, responded to questions from the Commission.
Commission Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:11 p.m.
Danielle Montague-Judd was called to speak at 6:13 p.m.
Commissioner Alex Peterson arrived at 6:13 p.m.
Curtis Judd was called to speak at 6:18 p.m.
Joan North was called to speak at 6:20 p.m.
Mark Pearl was called to speak at 6:22 p.m.
Commission Chair Sonntag closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:22 p.m.
Commission members responded to comments made during the public hearing and continued the discussion with the Applicant. The applicant responded to questions asked by the Commission.
Don Sargent made a motion to deny the proposed plat amendment on lot 1 Fox Run North based on the following findings: building pads in the subdivision are located to preserve view sheds and provide separation and privacy of residential homes; buyers are under the due diligence of purchasing the lot in the subdivision and acknowledge the location of the building pads; undue precedent that could be set if the building pad was amended going forward and essentially eliminating any cause for having a building pad in the subdivision. David Darcey seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Clinton Benson. Abstain: Rich Sonntag, Alex Peterson.
