meeting was held on Thursday, April 2, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

City Planner Malay reviewed the single family dwelling elevations. The Jackson Village subdivision received

City planner Maloy reviewed the required design elements and stated which elevations did not meet the guidelines
for approval. They were preparing to build and have submitted proposed elevations for review and approval.

Staff recommended approval of the single family dwelling elevations for Jackson Village as submitted, as long as they
comply with the Teton Ranch Design Guidelines, with the following exceptions:
NOT APPROVED:
   Anika A
   Annette A
   Annette B
   Avalon A
   Avril A

Applicant Thomas Calton Spoke. Exterior elements would be chosen by the purchaser and the elevations provided
intended to show possible variations for some models.

Commissioners discussed elements for front and rear elevations and made recommendations for elevations that
were not in compliance. Architectural elements were discussed including elements to increase the character of the
homes. Commissioners expressed reservations with approving elevations that did not meet City standards.
Commissioners requested confirmation that side elevation elements would be met. They discussed only approving
the elevations that met the standards and identified models that did not meet the design standards. They also
stated that some were missing from the packet. Staff stated that elevations for all sides of the homes would be
reviewed when building applications were submitted. City Planner Maloy expressed that due to volume of work
the staff had relied on architectural design committee review process. At this time he asked that staff review all
applications.

Applicant Calton recognized that all submitted elevations did not meet the requirements. Unfortunately, they
don’t have the ability to cater guidelines for each proposed development. He mentioned an HOA letter which
stated all homes must meet the City requirements and proposed that the Commission approve all models with the
stipulation that the HOA letter would ensure that all homes would meet the requirements. He stated there were
many options for each elevation.

Commissioner Rypien moved to approve with staff recommendations item 2.2 file number P2018-004-02 Review and approval of single
family dwelling elevations for Jackson Village, with staff recommendations to not approve the elevations named Anika A, Annette A,
Annette B, Avalon A, and Avril A. Commissioner Palmer seconded the motion.

City Planner Maloy informed the item was conditional use which was an administrative decision and the Planning
Commission would make a decision based on compliance with the approval standards. Notices and public
hearings were not required for conditional use applications. This property is lot 403 in the Anthem Commercial
Center and the entire Anthem Commercial property received preliminary site plan approval. Each site in the
commercial center comes back to the PC for final site plan approval. The applicant requested conditional use for
a fast food restaurant known as Slim Chickens.

Applicant Robert Poirier conveyed they are excited to come to Utah and have worked hard with staff. This will
be the second Utah location.

Commissioners offered suggestions for integral pigment with anti-graffiti spray on the garbage enclosure and
asked if signage was painted on or neon. The applicant replied that it was a combination of neon and paint. The
Commissioners reviewed the elevations and were confused by two that were labeled the same. The applicant
identified which was on the site plan.

City Planner Maloy stated a separate review would take place for sign review and acceptance.

Requirements

2.    Submit final landscaping plans showing types, sizes and placement of plant material to
      the Staff for review and final approval as part of the engineering review.

Assistant City Planner MacDonald stated this was a discussion item only and the applicant had requested feedback

City Planner Maloy reviewed the pods and development phases, trail access and locations. Open space and amenities
were discussed. The phasing plan for road construction was reviewed and most streets were to be maintained by the

Commissioners expressed concern with variety in the master pod plan. They requested more variety in lot sizes and

City Planner Maloy informed the Commission that this was a discussion item.

Applicants Tim Soffe and Tyler Kirk presented that the development was changed to have 73 fewer stacked units and
larger courtyard units were added. They offered that walkability had been increased. Building footprints were
decreased and parking areas were moved. Site plan evolution changes were discussed. Staff stated that phase three
had been modified after the packet was sent to the Commission.

Commissioners requested parking and garage locations. They requested for staff to confirm correct information be
included in the packet when the item comes back for Commission review.

Commissioners requested for smaller units to be near the SLCC side, as opposed to the residential side. They
requested more detailed elevations and parking garage locations and offered recommendations for the commercial
locations. Commissioners requested to see the cartography of the area and for the building heights to be staggered.
They offered that the previous plan included more hidden parking and garages. Commissioners requested to know
total number of units with garages, and recommended adding courtyard garage transitions. Commissioners requested
to see the new elevations.

The applicant reviewed challenges they had faced due to grading and the area not being flat. Conditions of the site
don’t allow for some elements, and some garages were used for retaining.

Staff and the applicant reviewed the Commission recommendations. The applicant expressed appreciation to staff.

Discussion item only.


Chair Powell expressed appreciation to the first responders and essential workers for their hard work during the
Covid-19 pandemic.

the motion, and all voted aye.
