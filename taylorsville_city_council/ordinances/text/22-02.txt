               SUMMARY:    The City of Taylorsville is proposing to amend the Land Development Code to
                           eliminate the minimum land area requirements to establish a nonresidential or

                           mixed- use zoning district. The current Land Development Code requires a lot or
                           parcel be a certain size to qualify to be rezoned to a particular zoning district.

                           This approach is common with older zoning codes and is used as a regulatory
                           mechanism to ensure that a property is a particular size so it can accommodate
                           certain types of development. As Taylorsville has developed and evolved, there are

                           very few lots and parcels that meet these minimum area thresholds that are being
                           considered for development and/ or redevelopment. In some cases, a zone change is
                           appropriate to align the property' s zoning designation with the City' s General
                           Plan; however, these minimum area thresholds act as a barrier to infill and

                           redevelopment potential in the City. For example, if a property owner desired to
                           build a small- scale mixed-use project on a lot less than 2 acres in size, and a rezone
                           was necessary, the property would not be eligible for a rezone to the Mixed-Use
                            MU) district because it is does not meet the established minimum 2- acre
                           threshold.



                           To resolve this matter, planning staff recommends that the minimum area
                           thresholds established in current Section 13. 04.090 be eliminated to allow the City
                           Council to determine on a case- by- case basis whether or not a property should be
                           rezoned to a particular district, without being constrained to only considering
                           properties with a particular land area. All other development standards still apply

        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council( the" Council") met in regular session on April
6, 2022, to consider, among other things, approving a text amendment to the Taylorsville
Municipal Code Section 13. 04. 090, related to the minimum land area required to establish a zoning
district; and


        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 040, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the city' s official website
on February 9, 2022; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on February 9, 2022; and

        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on February 22, 2022, during which the Planning Commission voted 6-
0 to recommend that the Council approve the proposed text amendment; and


       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on March
16, 2022; and


        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed text amendments to the Taylorsville Municipal Code Section 13. 04. 090 are hereby
approved, as articulated in Exhibit" A."


        This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 22- 02, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
