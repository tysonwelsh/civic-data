GOVENOR GARY HERBERT ARRIVED AT 5:25 p.m.
Chairman Merrill welcomed Governor Herbert and invited him to speak.

Governor Herbert thanked the Council for their service noting that local governments play a
significant role in citizens’ lives.

Herbert just returned from the Western Governors Association meeting which includes North
Dakota, South Dakota, Texas and all points west. Utah is always praised as doing well and
he is asked how they can copy Utah’s success, e.g., economy, education system
improvements, improved test scores and graduation rates, health care is the lowest cost in
America and rated fifth in quality — those ratios are remarkable. Utah is becoming the “gold
standard” of good state government and great quality of life. After spending an hour and a
half with the Pew Research Center people, Governor Herbert was told Utah’s secret is good
people.

The U.S. Chamber of Commerce placed Utah as first in the nation as best for business in
America. Their analysis credited Utah's spirit of collaboration and cooperation as being
better than any state in the nation.

Herbert believes the challenge of elected officials is to bring people together and unite them
in common efforts and causes.

Herbert thanked Cache County for the good work done to make Utah the best place in
America to live, to raise families and to do business.

Herbert was introduced to and shook hands with the Cache County Rodeo Royalty and
reminded them they are role models for others.

e Reconsideration of Property Tax Relief Request — Executive Buttars explained
the Council voted, at their last meeting, to abate one year of penalty and interest on
this request and asked Deputy Auditor Schaeffer to give the Council some
background on the issue. Schaffer said the property, which is a hangar at the airport
that is leased from the airport, was sold; however, the County was not informed and
continued to send the tax notices to the previous owner who did not notify the new
owner. When the previous owner was threatened with a tax sale, he gave the
County the contact information for the new owner. When the new owner was
contacted, he immediately came in to pay the taxes, but disagreed with paying
penalties and interest because he never received a tax notice. He assumed the
airport was paying the taxes as part of the lease agreement. Because the request
was already acted on at the previous meeting, there must be a motion to reconsider
by one of the members of the prevailing vote. (Details are on file in the office of the
Cache County Clerk/Auditor.)

ACTION: Motion by Vice Chair Erickson to reconsider the Property Tax Relief
Request for #0170. Potter seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 6-0.
Yeates absent.

ACTION: Motion by Council member Potter to waive penalty and interest on years
2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015 for #0170. The motion passed, 4 aye — Erickson, Merrill,
Potter & Robison and 2 abstentions — White & Zilles. Yeates absent.

After some discussion, it was determined there is probably a disconnect between the
airport manager and the county on similar properties which needs to be corrected.
