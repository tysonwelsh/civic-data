  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant is requesting approval of the Gardner Point Phase 1 plat amendment. The plat would create a new lot
within Lot 2. This is to facilitate the construction of the first phase of the Exchange Business Center. The applicant
has stated they intend to come back and amend the plat in a series of phases to create one additional commercial lot
for the second building. There is no minimum lot size requirement for the Neighborhood Commercial zone, and the
plat still maintains 60-feet of frontage.

There was one comment from the DRC, which was to include a note that parking will be shared between all of the
lots. Please consider this in your motion.


 POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS
