           AN ORDINANCE AMENDING PUBLIC NOTICE STANDARDS IN
          CHAPTERS 17-2 (DEFINITIONS) AND l7-3 (ADMINTSTRATION AND
           ENFORCEMENT) OF THE MIDVALE CITY MUNICIPAL CODE.

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated Sections 10-8-84 and l0-9a-501 through
10-9a-503, Midvale City ("the City") has authority to make and amend any regulation of or
within zoning districts or any other provision of the land use ordinance to promote the prosperity,
improve the morals, peace and good order, comlbrt, convenience, and aesthetics ofthe
municipality; and

        WHEREAS, on January 2,2002, the Midvale City Zoning Ordinance, Title 17 of the
Midvale City Municipal Code (the "Code"). became effective and is subject to amendments liom
time to time pursuant to Section l7-3-1 the Code; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 17-1-1 of the Code, the City desires to protect private
property rights; support the goals of the Midvale general plan; and secure economy in
govemmental expenditures; and

        WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature amended public notice requirements in Chapter 10-9a
of the Utah Code Annotated in the 202 I Legislative Session by adopting HB 107 and 38201 ; and

       WHEREAS, the City desires to amend the public notice standards in the Code to
substantially follow the adopted language in Chapter l0-9a ofthe Utah Code Annotated; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission held a public hearing on June 23, 2021, which
meeting was preceded by notice ofpublication in the Salt Lake Tribune on June 6,2021, to
review the request for a text amendment and, after considering all the information received,
made a recommendation to approve the text amendment request to the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Midvale City, Utah held a public hearing on July 20,
2021, which meeting was preceded by notice by publication in the Sall Lake Tribune on July 3,
2021; and

       WHEREAS, after taking into consideration citizen testimony, planning analysis, and the
Planning Commission's recommendation as part of its deliberations, the City Council finds it is
appropriate and within the best interest ofthe City to make changes to the Code.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council olMidvale City, Utah
as follows:

      Section 1. The following sections of the Midvale City Municipal Code are hereby
amended as included in the lollowing attachments to this document:
