Council heard presentations regarding the traffic impacts from the proposed Dakota Pacific Development at the Park City Tech Center. Council Members asked questions during and after the presentations and provided their thoughts following the presentations.

Consideration of Approval (5:04 P.M.)
a.
Discussion and approval regarding National Association of Counties (NACo) Public Lands request; Janna Young (5:05 P.M.)
Janna Young, Deputy Manager, provided information on the National Association of Counties (NACo) Public Lands request for funding contributions from public lands counties to establish a National Center for Public Lands Counties.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve contributing 1% of the County's $850,776 in federal recovery funds received as a public lands county through the Local Assistance and Tribal Consistency Fund (LATCF) to help the National Association of Counties (NACo) set up a National Center for Public Lands Counties. Chris Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0).
