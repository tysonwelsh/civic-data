PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Lisa Jensen confirmed language in some of the amendments with Staff, and asked if new zones needed to be
       added to Section 14.34.340.
   • The Planning Commission generally felt that the proposed changes made sense and had no problem with
       recommending approval.

FINDINGS / BASIS OF PLANNING COMMISSION DETERMINATION
   • Though the motion included suggested additions of the LDR and VLDR to Section 14.34.340, staff realized after
      the hearing that this Section refers to conditional uses of twin homes in the zones, which does not apply to the
      VLDR and LDR zones, so no changes are needed.
