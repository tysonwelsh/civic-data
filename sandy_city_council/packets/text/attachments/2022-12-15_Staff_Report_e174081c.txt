Request
 With the permission from the property owner (Sandy
 Retail Center, LLC), the applicants, Brian and
 Savannah Suitter, are requesting a conditional use
 permit for indoor recreation for a property located at
 10701 S. State Street. The request is to convert a
 portion of an existing salon building to an indoor
 mixed-martial arts gym within the Limited
 Commercial (LC) zone (see applicant letter, Exhibit
 “A”).

Background
 The subject property is located on the east side of State
 Street, a major arterial state highway. The site was
 developed as a restaurant in 1993, then later changed
 uses as a salon and creating two separate units inside
 the building. The entire property area is 40,122 square
 feet, with the office building compromising 8,002
 square feet of net retail space.

 Properties directly east of the property are residential
 homes zoned R-1-10. Directly north and south are
 businesses in the Limited Commercial (LC) zone and
 to the west across state street is the Automall zone with
 more commercial developments.

 This item has been noticed to property owners within 500 feet of
 the subject area by mail. A neighborhood meeting was held on
 November 28, 2022, and no residents attended the meeting.

Analysis
 The applicant is proposing to utilize the space for a mixed-martial
 arts gym, which is considered to be an indoor recreation land use.
 That is a conditional use within the LC Zone. The proposed gym
 operates a variety of classes including kickboxing, boxing, personal training, and youth martial arts for people of all ages
 inside of the building. The applicant is not proposing to make any exterior changes to the site.




 Parking
 As they are changing use from commercial retail sales and services to indoor recreation, the parking requirements are different
 for these uses. The Sandy City Land Development Code requires that parking for a recreation center be determined by the
 Planning Commission. This property has a shared parking lot for the entire building and the current parking lot has 43
 stalls. The lease for the building allocates four assigned parking stalls with signs at the front entrance of the building and the
 rest for overflow parking. The adjoining salon requires five parking stalls per one thousand square feet. The salon is
 approximately 4,000 square feet which would require 20 parking stalls. That would leave 23 parking stalls for the use of the
 proposed gym.

    Conditional Use Standards
    The City may impose conditions on a conditional use based on certain standards of review found in section 21-33-4 of
    Sandy City Land Development Code. The following items from that list are applicable to this particular request and merit
    discussion or additional consideration by the Planning Commission (staff analysis is found below in italics):

  Sec. 21-33-4. Conditions.
       In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions that address:
       (1) Size, configuration and location of the site and the proposed site plan layout.
    That the size and configuration of the site meets the intent of the proposed use.
       (2) Proposed site ingress and egress to existing and proposed roads and streets.
    That the site already has an ingress and egress that is cohesive for a drop off and pick up use for the recreation use and adjoining
    salon.
       (4)    Design, location and amount of off-street parking, loading areas and solid waste disposal and collection areas.
    The Planning Commission will determine whether the current parking stalls is satisfactory for the use of an indoor recreation
    facility. See analysis of this item above.
       (6)    The mass, size, number, location, design, exterior features, materials, and colors of buildings and other facilities.
    The applicant is not proposing to make any changes to the existing exterior of the building and will remain matching with the
    adjoining salon.
       (7)    The location and design of all site features, including proposed signage, lighting, and refuse collection.
    The applicant will have an approved sign on the building and will maintain existing exterior lighting.
       (9)    Fencing, screening and landscape treatments, and other features designed to increase the attractiveness and safety of
              the site and protect adjoining property owners from noise, visual and other impacts.
    The applicant intends to only operate during allowed business hours and mitigate any other impacts.
       (15) Such other conditions determined reasonable and necessary by the City to allow the operation of the proposed
            conditional use, at the proposed location in compliance with the requirements of this title.
    That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.
    Staff Concerns
    Staff has no other concerns with the proposed use.

  Recommendation
#1 Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for indoor recreation in the Limit
   Commercial (LC) zone for the property located at 10701 S. State Street based on the following findings and subject to the
   following conditions:

    Findings:
      1. The proposed use meets the intent of the LC zone.

      2.     The proposed use should have minimal to no impact on the surrounding uses.

    Conditions:
     1. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fide & Life Codes.
