Council Business

Adoption of the 2019 White City Metro Township Tentative Budget

             The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting the 2019 White City Metro
Township Tentative Budget:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-12-01                                               Date: December 13, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING THE 2019 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP TENTATIVE BUDGET

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Council approved the 2019 White City Metro Township Tentative Budget on
      November 15, 2018, and set the public hearing for the 2019 Tentative budget for
      December 13, 2018 at 7:00 PM at the White City Water Improvement District Boardroom
      located at 999 East Galena Drive, White City, UT 84094.

   D. Pursuant to fiscal policy for funding the 2019 White City Budget, the Greater Salt Lake
      Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) adopted the 2019 Budget for the MSD on
      December 5, 2018, releasing the funding for the 2019 White City Budget.

   E. The noticing for the public hearing to consider and copies of the 2019 White City Tentative
      Budget, (Attachment “A” to this resolution) were made available for public consideration
      at least ten (10) days prior to the date of the public hearing for the Budget.

   F. Adoption of the 2019 Budget will allow White City to comply with State Law and the
      Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act for Cities.

Tentative Budget, Attachment “A.”

Adoption of the 2019 White City Consolidated Fee Schedule

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting the 2019 White City
consolidated fee schedule:

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING THE 2019 WHITE CITY CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE

                                        RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

                                             5
DATE          THURSDAY                             DECEMBER                         13, 2018


   C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a local district that
      provides certain municipal-type services to White City pursuant to UCA §§ 17B-2a-1103
      et al.

   D. The Council adopted the 2018 Consolidated Fee Schedule of February 27, 2018.

   E. In October 2018, after staff review, changes were recommended to the Fee Schedule for
      2019 (“Proposed Fee Schedule”).

   F. The 2019 White City Tentative Budget, and recently adopted Greater Salt Lake Municipal
      Services District Budget both used revenue projections based on the Proposed Fee
      Schedule.

   G. Pursuant to State Law, White City must annually adopt a consolidated fee schedule.

   H. Adoption of the 2019 Consolidated Fee allows White City to comply with State Law.

                                       RESOLUTION

      THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the proposed changes
and adopts the 2019 White City Consolidated Fee Schedule, hereinafter known as Exhibit 1.

Unified Fire Authority & Unified Fire Service Area

                Council Member Perry stated Unified Fire Authority (UFA) is looking into four
different options for board member votes. Once a decision is made a new interlocal agreement
will be drafted and adopted. Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA) will start assessing its fire stations
and may go out for a bond in 2019 to get fire stations up to date or replaced. UFA and UFSA has
decided not to pursue legal action against the form administration. The UFA is reviewing
ambulance staffing, employee turnover, and having full-time fire fighters on trucks and
ambulances.
