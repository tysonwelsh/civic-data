4. Public Hearing Zone Change, R-4 to R-5, 211 Patterson St
Petitioner Tim Price with the Ogden Housing Authority stated they were seeking the rezone to
add a housing unit for foster care and homeless youth needs. With approval for 25 units, they
will qualify to get help with tax credits.
Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson displayed slides and said the single lot plan
supports the General Plan and that staff recommended approval of the proposed rezone.

COMMISSIONER SOUTHWICK MADE A MOTION TO CLOSE THE PUBLIC HEARING.
COMMISSIONER AABERG SECONDED THE MOTION. ALL IN FAVOR — AYE

COMISSIONER SHALE MOVED TO APPROVE THE ZONE CHANGE SUBJECT TO STAFFS
RECOMMENDED CONDITIONS BASED ON FINDING THE APPLICABLE CRITERIA ARE MET.
MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER SANDAU AND PASSED UPON BY THE
FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE — COMMISSIONERS AABERG, ROSS, SANDAU,
SHALE, SOUTHWICK, WILLIAMS, AKHMEDOV, AND CHAIR SHINODA. VOTING NO — NONE.

5. Public Hearing General Plan Amendment, General Plan Water Element
Delaney Sillman with Logan Simpson gave a presentation on recommendations for water
consolidation, considering water demand and water infrastructure methods and reducing water

COMMISSIONER SANDAU MADE A MOTION TO CLOSE THE PUBLIC HEARING.
