                                                                 MINUTES
                                                     WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                    Tuesday, February 26, 2019 - 10:00 a.m.
                                           Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who appear and speak at a
County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy
or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: Scott K. Jenkins, James “Jim” H. Harvey, and Gage Froerer.
OTHER STAFF PRESENT: Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; Lynn Taylor, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office;
and Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME – Chair Jenkins
B. INVOCATION – Christopher Crockett
C. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Quinn Fowers
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY – Commissioner Jenkins
E. PUBLIC COMMENTS - All pertained to H.1:
   --Toby Mileski, of Pleasant View, expressed that this is a bad policy, that it should have been dealt with last
   October, that the Commission should completely remove it and there should not be a public service payout. Chair
   Jenkins noted that item H.1. was being held today.
   --Phil Swanson, of North Ogden, stated that the initial purpose of the policy was to provide 5-year healthcare
   coverage after retirement, without a cash payout for elected officials. He urged the Commission to support a no-
   cash payout but to simply keep those individuals on the county’s 5-year health insurance program.
   --Kerry Wayne, of Ogden, stated that he had asked the Commission last August to change the policy, which is a
   burden to taxpayers, particularly due to the property tax increase and for those on fixed incomes, especially having
   to pay for such programs. He believed nothing was done because the commissioners in office from August until
   December reaped that benefit but it needs to be done away with. He received information today that former
   commissioner Ebert’s payout had been made, and today’s item was merely to cover the paper trail.

    4. CONTRACT WITH PERPETUAL STORAGE FOR BACKUP MEDIA STORAGE.
         Quinn Fowers, County Information Technology Director, stated that this 3-year contract for about $1,700/year

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Perpetual Storage for backup media storage;
         Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    5. INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH HARRISVILLE CITY RELATING TO OBLIGATIONS REQUIRED FOR SMALL
       MUNICIPAL SEPARATE STORM SEWER SYSTEMS UNDER UPDES PERMIT NO. UTR090000 – RESOLUTION
       13-2019.
         Blane Frandsen, of County Engineering, stated that this is part of a series of interlocal agreements with MS4
         cities of the county and some from Box Elder County to support/fund the Golden Spike Stormwater Advisory
         Coalition, which is a stormwater advisory committee to Utah’s Department of Water Quality. This will allow
         funding for the local coalition to consider hiring a professional manager to assist in the administering/directing
         of that coalition.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to adopt Resolution 13-2019 approving the Interlocal Agreement with Harrisville
         City relating to obligations required for small municipal separate storm sewer systems under UPDES Permit
         No. UTR090000; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
I. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS: Chair Jenkins invited John Bond, County Treasurer, to present Lynnelle Jensen as
   the recipient of the Stall of Fame for the Month of March. Mr. Bond said that during the very competitive hiring
   process Ms. Jensen had risen above the others because she exemplified problem-solving, a high degree of
   responsibility and superior attitude. She has continued to exhibit these qualities over the past 10 years in a very
   sophisticated, high-level job, including reconciling all the Weber County funds. The commissioners congratulated
   Ms. Jensen. Commissioner Froerer recently met with the Treasurer’s Office and can say without hesitation that
   Weber County is in great hands. Commissioner Harvey noted that Lynn Taylor, of the County Clerk/Auditor’s
   Office, was in a recent Standard Examiner’s editorial about the county’s bond rating. The county was on the top
   3% in the U.S. and was again upgraded because of all the county departments working together and making good
   sound financial decisions. He appreciates being a part of this team.
