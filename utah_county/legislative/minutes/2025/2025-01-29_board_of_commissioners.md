I would like to nominate Jody Carly for employee of the month. Jody has worked in the
Welcome Baby program for many years and previously worked in WIC. Because of her
background working in both programs, she is amazing at collaborating with other
departments to provide great service to the public. Most recently, she assisted with a
baby that was having difficulty with feeding. Jody was able to help one of our staff make
a connection with WIC before their office was officially open for the day. The baby was
able to get connected to the needed resource extremely fast! Jody is a hard worker along
with being very thoughtful and caring. She assists our newer employees with answering
questions and uses her strong problem-solving skills to help our clients that have
numerous risk factors and needs, by searching until she finds the resources that will
best help them. One of her clients is a mom with very limited literacy abilities and Jody
worked diligently to ensure this client received the information in a format that made
sense and helped this mom increase her knowledge in parenting and developmental
milestones for her child. Jody is an asset to our Welcome Baby program!
