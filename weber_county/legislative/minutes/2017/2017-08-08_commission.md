F. ACTION ITEMS:

   1. RELEASE OF COVENANT BY/AMONG WEBER COUNTY, ROY CITY & ROY CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY.

        Bryan Baron, Deputy County Attorney, stated that in 2014 the county entered into an agreement with Roy
        City and the Roy City Redevelopment Agency, which included the county’s agreement to remove a
        reversion on the North Park property that would prevent Roy from using that property for anything other
        than a park. Roy agreed to transfer that reversion to an amended park property which will be directly
        west of that North Park property. Roy recently contacted the county stating that they are ready to move
        forward. They prepared this Release of Covenant to remove the reversion of the North Park property.
        They also prepared another document that their city council will be approving granting a reversion or
        restriction on the amended park property.
        Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Release of Covenant by and among Weber County, Roy
        City and the Roy City Redevelopment Agency; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
        Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

   2. APPROVE THE WESTERN WEBER COUNTY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN. RESOLUTION 31-2017

        Commissioner Gibson stated that he is comfortable with the Plan. Charles Ewert, of County Planning,
        noted the minor changes made that had been discussed in last week’s meeting and referred to the final
        plan before the commissioners. Commissioner Harvey expressed thanks to Mr. Ewert for his tremendous
        amount of work on this item.
        Commissioner Gibson moved to adopt Resolution 31-2017 approving the Western Weber County
        Resource Management Plan; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
        Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

   3. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE WEST WEBER-TAYLOR CEMETERY DISTRICT.

        Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
    4. CONTRACT WITH MERIDIAN ENGINEERING FOR SURVEYING AND DESIGN SERVICES ON 3300 SOUTH.
         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that these services are related to the road widening project.
         Requests for Proposals were issued, the interview process ensued and Meridian rose to the top.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the contract with Meridian Engineering for surveying and
         design services on 3300 S.; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    5. CONTRACT WITH AAA EXCAVATION INC. TO INSTALL SEWER LINE FOR A FUTURE RV PARK AT GSEC.

         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that Requests for Proposals were issued and AAA Excavation
         was the low bidder. Commissioner Gibson noted that there was quite a difference in bid submission
         amounts and asked Mr. Andersen to request AAA to keep change orders to a minimum in order to better
         manage costs. Commissioner Harvey noted that Ogden City has agreed to allow the county to do capital
         improvements on the additional parcel in conjunction with the revised lease.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the contract with AAA Excavation Inc. to install sewer line for a
         future RV Park at the GSEC; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    6. CONTRACT WITH AEROGRAPHICS GEOSPATIAL SERVICES FOR PHOTOGRAMMETRIC MAPPING
       SERVICES FOR 2550 S. AND 3300 S.

         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, stated that an RFP process was followed for the aerial photography
         services. The county will save money by doing the photogrammetric mapping for both road projects.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the contract with Aerographics Geospatial Services for
         photogrammetric mapping services for 2550 S. and 3300 S.; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    7. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH PLEASANT VIEW CITY FOR THE SKYLINE
       DRIVE 350 WEST TO 500 WEST PROJECT.

         Brooke Stewart, of the County Economic Development Partnership Office, stated that this is for phase 2
         which expands phase 1 and to construct the intersections for 350 W. and 500 W. The transportation
         subcommittee recommended to WACOG to fund this project in November and WACOG recommended
         $182,638 from quarter preservation funds for right-of-way acquisition and $375,417 from sales tax funds
         for the construction. The project was on the 2016 priority list approved by the Commission on 12/2016.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Local Transportation Funding Agreement with Pleasant
         View City for the Skyline Drive 350 West to 500 West project; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    8. CONTRACT WITH KRISTEN HADEED, LLC, FOR THE SERVICES OF KEYNOTE SPEAKER AT THE
       NORTHERN UTAH WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT (NUWDA) EVENT.

         Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, stated that NUWDA is a
         partnership in economic and workforce development that spans many Utah counties. The event will
         occur on October at Peery’s Egyptian Theater and the OECC. The contract amount is $10,000.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the contract with Kristen Hadeed, LLC, the keynote speaker at
         the Northern Utah Workforce Development Event; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    9. INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR INMATE HOUSING.

         Kevin Burton, of the County Sheriff’s Office, stated that Salt Lake County has been overcrowded for a
         number of years and unable to house misdemeanor inmates at its County Jail. The Legislature has
         allowed some funding for them to contract some current inmates with other facilities. Weber County
         currently has room for about 40 female inmates.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Interlocal Agreement with Salt Lake County for housing
         inmates; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye

    2. PUBLIC HEARING ON PROPOSED AGRICULTURE PROTECTION AREA (APA) - KARMEN SANONE.

         The APA application had been submitted to the Commission previously for review. Fátima Fernelius, of
         the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, stated that State statute was followed on this item, including for publications,
         postings, mailings and noticing requirements. Ms. Fernelius explained the purpose of APAs. The public
         had a 15-day period to submit written objections or requests to modify the proposal and none were
         received. The application was submitted to the Western Weber Planning Commission and the APA
         Advisory Board and the recommendation was for approval. She stated that today the Commission would
         take oral and written comments from the public and they could approve, modify and approve, or reject the
         proposal. Subsequent to approval, a document will be recorded with the County Recorder’s Office, and
         notice filed with the Planning Commission and the Commissioner of Agriculture and Food. The
         applicant, Karmen Sanone, was present. Ms. Sanone thanked the Commission for entertaining this
         proposal and she thanked Ms. Fernelius for walking her through this process. This property has been in
         her family’s name since 1865. They are very pleased that they can maintain that life style and wish to
         continue it.

    3. Public comments: Commissioner Harvey invited public comments and none were offered.
    4.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to adjourn the public hearing and reconvene the public meeting;

         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye
