WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 19-03-01 DATE: March 7, 2019
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
AMENDING THE 2019 CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE TO
INCLUDE FEES FOR SPECIAL EVENTS AND PARK RENTALS
RECITALS
A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.
B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council” is the municipal legislative body for
White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.
C. On December 13, 2018 as a part of the 2019 budget approval process, the Council approved
the 2019 White City Consolidated Fee Schedule (the “Fee Schedule”).
D. In a review of the fee schedule in late January, it was found the fees for park rentals and
special events had not been posted to the fee schedule that had been adopted.
E. The attached exhibit (Exhibit A) reflects the special events and park rental fees proposed for
addition to the fee schedule.
F. Both fees allow White City to offset some of the regulatory and use impact costs of special
events and certain park activities.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the proposed fees for special
events and park rental, hereinafter known as Exhibit A, to the 2019 White City Consolidated Fee
schedule; and be it further

RESOLVED, the effective date for these fees be as of January 1, 2019. .
