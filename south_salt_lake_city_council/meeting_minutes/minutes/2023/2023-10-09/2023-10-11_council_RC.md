       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Pinkney talked about the heart shattering news and expressed her
          sentiment for the families impacted by terrorism on both sides of the Israel and
          Gaza conflict and said that she denounces all acts of terrorism.

              Council Member Huff said that the news has been terrible to watch unfold and
              expressed her sentiments for the people impacted as well.

              Council Member Thomas said that she shares the same sentiments on the current
              events.

              Council Member Williams wanted to thank the City and public safety staff for
              making many City events happen over the last two weeks. She said that she has
              received positive comments from people saying how great the events have been
              and wanted to recognize the staff that she knows make it possible.
