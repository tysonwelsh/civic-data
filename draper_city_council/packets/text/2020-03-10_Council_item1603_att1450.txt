                  DRAPER CITY BOARD-UP ROTATION CONTRACT
        THIS CONTRACT (“Contract”) is made effective as of March 10, 2020 (“Effective
Date”) by and between Draper City (the “City”), and Utah Disaster Kleenup (UDK), Alpine
Restoration, Paul Davis Restoration, ServPro (Precision), Liberty Restoration, and ServPro (Six
Gems), (“Contractor”), collectively the “Parties,” for the purpose of establishing the terms and
conditions under which Contractor will participate in the Draper City Fire Department Rotating
List for Board-Up Services (the “Rotating List”).
                                           RECITALS
        WHEREAS, the City has created the Rotating List to provide a prompt and efficient
means for the Draper City Fire Department (“DCFD”) to request board-up and related services
from providers of emergency board-up services after hours and at other times when there is no
immediate means known for contacting an affected property owner, the affected property owner
is unable or unwilling to respond, or at the request of the affected property owner; and
        WHEREAS, the City has created the Rotating List for the additional purpose of
establishing a list of approved contractors who may be contacted by DCFD on a rotating basis
for the purpose of emergency board-up services; and
       WHEREAS, the City and Metro Fire Agency issued the Request for Proposal to Provide
Emergency Clean up Services (“RFP”), dated November 2019, attached hereto as Exhibit A, for
the purpose of identifying companies for placement on the Rotating List; and
      WHEREAS, Contractor submitted a bid in response to the RFP, attached hereto as
Exhibit B; and
       WHEREAS, Contractor is experienced and skilled in providing emergency board-up
services; and
       WHEREAS, the City has promulgated rules and regulations for the provision of
emergency board-up and other services identified in the RFP and described in the Draper City
Fire Department Emergency Board-up Services Policy and Procedures (the “Policy”), which has
been provided to and reviewed by Contractor; and
        WHEREAS, Contractor has been chosen by the City for participation in the Rotating
List,
        NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the
Parties hereto agree as follows:
                                         COVENANTS
1.      REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP): A copy of the RFP (Exhibit A) and Contractor’s
bid (Exhibit B) are attached hereto and by reference are incorporated as a part of this Contract
with all terms and conditions contained therein being applicable to this Contract. In the event of
a conflict in the terms of this Contract and the exhibits, the language in this Contract shall be

controlling. In the event of a conflict in the terms of Exhibits A and B, the language of Exhibit A
shall be controlling.
2.     INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR: The Parties agree that this Contract creates an
independent contractor relationship between Contractor and the City. Contractor shall not
represent itself as an agent of DCFD or the City.
3.       CARE AND SKILL: Contractor agrees to follow all applicable federal, state, and local
laws in providing services under this Contract. Contractor agrees to use the degree of care and
skill ordinarily exercised by professionals under these circumstances and conditions and shall be
solely responsible for all means, methods, techniques, sequences, and procedures and for
coordinating and providing all services under this Contract. Contractor, its agents,
representatives and employees entering any fire-damaged scene for repairs or other services at
the request of DCFD shall wear proper protective equipment and comply with all OSHA or other
applicable safety and health regulations for construction workers in such circumstances.


4.     Rotating List:
       A.      Contractor will be placed on the Rotating List. Placement on the Rotating List
       does not guarantee service calls. DCFD dispatch will call parties on the Rotating List in
       order, unless a party has been suspended, terminated, or for some other reason is unable
       or unwilling to provide services in compliance with the Policy. DCFD shall call only
       parties on the Rotating List, except in emergencies determined by DCFD or when an
       affected property owner requests that services be provided by an individual or entity not
       on the Rotating List. DCFD may, in its reasonable discretion, require that such other
       contractor requested by a property owner be expected to arrive at the scene within 60
       minutes from the call for service. DCFD may call Contractors out of order when, in its
       sole discretion, it determines that doing so would favor public health, safety, or welfare.
       Contractor shall have no claim against and shall hold harmless the City, its officials,
       employees or agents, Valley Emergency Communication Center (VECC), its officials,
       employees, or agents for out-of-order calls for any reason except willful violations of the
       Policy by such persons.
       B.      The Fire Chief, or designee, may add additional names to the Rotating List from
       those parties that submitted qualified proposals at the time of the last RFP by the City, if
       the existing Contractors are unable to provide the services as needed or if including
       additional parties would be in the best interests of the City. The solicitation and inclusion
       of additional Contractors may also occur at any time through the issuance of an RFP by
       the City based upon qualifications as determined by the City.
5.     Response:
       A.      Contractor must respond to the scene of an event within 60 minutes of a request
       by DCFD or VECC. Inability to respond within the required time limit may, depending
       on the circumstances, result in Contractor being placed at the bottom of the Rotating List

                                                 2
       and may result in temporary suspension or removal from the Rotating List at the Fire
       Chief’s discretion.
       B.      Contractor must possess and maintain at least one vehicle containing an inventory
       of equipment and supplies normally sufficient to perform services as requested by DCFD
       or the City.
6.      Services: Contractor shall provide only those services that are reasonably necessary or
requested by DCFD or an affected property owner. These services may include, but are not
limited to, any of the following:
       A.      Board-Up: 5/8 inch or thicker exterior grade plywood cover-up of all damaged or
       unsecured openings such as doors, windows, vent holes and fire openings to protect and
       secure the property.
       B.     Roof Coverings: plastic and tarp cover-up of roof, ceiling, or other openings to
       prevent weather damage.
       C.      Door Coverings: a hasp and loop or staple, and combination padlock or other lock
       to secure entry to the property, with provision of the combination or key to DCFD or its
       designee.
       D.     Debris Removal: the cleanup, removal, and disposal according to law of debris as
       requested by DCFD, and the removal of debris from nearby properties, streets, and
       sidewalks.
       E.      Fencing: the erection of temporary fencing or caution tape as requested by DCFD.
       F.      Equipment: provide light and heavy equipment as need for the investigation of a
       fire.
       G.      Shoring: provide shoring as needed to prevent collapse of a structure.
7.     Billing:
       A.       Contractor agrees to neither bill, charge, affix or seek fees to or from DCFD, the
       City, its officers, agents, employees, or representatives for services rendered under the
       Policy. Any remuneration shall come solely from the affected property owner or
       companies insuring the affected property, even when services are performed at the
       direction of DCFD.
       B.     Contractor will charge no more than industry-standard rates for all work
       performed and materials provided, consistent with the sample rate sheet provided in
       response to the RFP issued by the City.
       C.     Each invoice to an affected property owner or insurance company shall contain a
       reasonably detailed statement of services and materials provided by Contractor and the
       charge for each service and material provided.
       D.      Contractor will keep invoices or statements of charges for services and other
                                                 3
       records related to this policy for not less than three years from the date of the service.
       Contractor shall keep electronic copies of all invoices, and upon request by the City shall
       quickly provide electronic invoices or statements of charges for services by computer
       search. Contractor shall allow the Fire Chief or designee to inspect or review all such
       records upon request during normal business hours.
8.     Licensing and Insurance:
       A.      Contractor must obtain and maintain proper licensing as may be required by the
       State of Utah to provide any services to be performed by Contractor.
       B.     Contractor must maintain in force at all times, and on file with the Fire Chief or a
       designee, during participation in the program, a certificate of insurance covering
       Contractor’s operation(s) and naming the City, its members, employees, and agents as
       additionally named insureds.
       C.      Contractor must maintain in force at all times, comprehensive auto insurance,
       general liability insurance, and workers’ compensation insurance, as may be required by
       the State of Utah to provide any services to be provided by Contractor or otherwise
       required by the terms of the RFP issued by the City, attached as Exhibit A. Contractor
       shall immediately notify the Fire Chief if auto, general liability, or worker’s
       compensation insurance lapses or is terminated for any reason. Contractor shall not
       provide services if any required insurance is not in force.
9.      Reporting: Contractor agrees to provide an annual report to DCFD reporting the
following information: number and type of calls for service; summary of charges per call; report
of customer complaints; billing or cost disputes; and any other information that Contractor may
deem helpful to DCFD or requested specifically by DCFD.
10.    Additional Requirements: Contractor agrees to the following in providing any services:
       A.     Contractor will comply with all applicable Federal, State, County, and City laws
       and regulations;
       B.    Contractor shall require that its employees and agents be knowledgeable about
       and comply with all such laws and the Policy;
       C.      Contractor will ensure that all employees and agents have sufficient experience or
       training and are capable of and perform their duties in a lawful, safe, proper, courteous
       and effective manner;
       D.     Contractor may not assign or subcontract its services under this Contract;
       E.      Contractor will not employ an employee or agent who has been convicted of a
       felony or who has a history of theft, dishonesty, or violence that would reasonably be
       considered to cause such employee or agent to be a risk to life, safety, or property in
       providing services under this Contract or the Policy. The Fire Chief may, in his or her
       discretion and in a signed writing, waive a violation under this Section where he or she

                                                4
       reasonably deems the individual not to be a threat to public safety or property;
       F.     Contractor will maintain drug screening and background check policies and
       procedures for all employees;
       G.      Contractor will promptly resolve claims for damage or theft that are related to the
       services provided under this policy in a timely, courteous, and fair manner. Contractor
       shall be liable to property owners for theft, loss or damage to property; and
       H.      Contractor will expeditiously, courteously, and safely perform all services at the
       scene. Contractor and its employees or agents will follow all lawful commands of DCFD
       at the scene. If Contractor believes that the commands of DCFD are unlawful, or are
       inconsistent with City or DCFD policy, they shall promptly so report to the Fire Chief.
11.     “No Chase” policy: Contractor agrees to abide by a “no chase” policy. Contractor will
only respond when requested by DCFD, VECC, an affected property owner, or insurance
company on behalf of an affected property owner. Contractor may be ordered to leave by the
DCFD incident commander or proper designee and may be subject to suspension or removal
from the Rotating List if it is found by DCFD to have “jumped a call” or otherwise arrive on the
scene of an emergency without prior request from DCFD, the City, an affected property owner,
or insurance company on behalf of an affected property owner, begin work without
authorization, or is uncooperative or disruptive to the services performed by DCFD.
12.    Communication:
       A.     Contractor will provide up-to-date contact information to DCFD for use on the
       Rotating List, including: telephone number(s), office address(es), mailing address(es),
       and e-mail address(es).
       B.     Contractor will identify and be available through a single-point-of-contact
       telephone number 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Availability through a paging
       system will not satisfy this requirement.
       C.     Contractor’s phone number will be answered in the name of Contractor.
       D.      Contractor will maintain an active e-mail address, which Contractor’s employees
       or officers shall review regularly. E-mail is an acceptable means of communication
       between DCFD and Contractor.
       E.     Contractor will notify DCFD in writing within one business day of any changes in
       telephone number(s), office address(es), mailing address(es), or e-mail address(es).
13.    Removal from Rotating List:
       A.     The Fire Chief may temporarily suspend or permanently remove Contractor from
       the Rotating List if:
              1. Contractor fails to respond within the maximum allotted sixty (60) minutes;
              2. Contractor fails to maintain the requisite Utah State Contractor license and
                                                5
              tools/equipment to operate a Board-Up business;
              3. Contractor fails to maintain a City business license;
              4. Contractor fails to maintain or cannot obtain the minimum required insurance;
              5. Contractor, its employee, or agent violates any City ordinance, State or Federal
              law related to Contractor’s services, except minor traffic offenses, in performing
              services under this policy;
              6. Contractor interferes with or does not allow inspection of Contractor’s invoices
              or other records that Contractor is required to maintain;
              7. Contractor engages in practices detrimental to the efficient operation of the
              Rotating List, the operations of DCFD, or the relationship of DCFD with the
              public; or
              8. Other violations of the Policy breach of this Contract, or such other causes that
              may exist which, in the discretion of the Fire Chief, show Contractor’s
              unwillingness or inability to perform according to the Policy and removal from
              the Rotating List would not be in the interests of the public.
       B.      The Fire Chief must notify Contractor in writing (“Notice”) when removing a
       Contractor from the Rotating List. Notice shall set forth the cause(s) for removal. Notice
       shall be sent, certified mail/return receipt, to the mailing address provided by Contractor
       for use on the Rotating List. Notice must include an explanation of the process for
       appealing the decision to remove Contractor from the Rotating List.
       C.      Contractor shall not institute any action or other legal proceedings for damages
       against the Hearing Officer, the Fire Chief, DCFD, the City, or any of their officers,
       agents, or employees for any act done in good faith and in the intended performance of
       any power, or for any neglect or default in the performance or exercise in good faith of
       any such duty or power, under DCFD or City policy relating to the Rotating List.
