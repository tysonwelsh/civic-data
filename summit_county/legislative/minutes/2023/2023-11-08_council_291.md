General budget discussions; Shayne Scott and Matt Leavitt (3:35 PM)
Attachment: 2024 Truth In Taxation Discussion

2)
Discussion regarding Republic Services Winter service operations; Tim Loveday, John Angell, Gordon Raymond, and Mark Sherwood (4:49 PM)
Attachment: Republic_Services_Winter_Service_Operations
John Angell, Public Works Director, introduced the discussion about Republic Services’ winter operations. Mark Sherwood, Republic Services Municipal Services and Government Affairs Manager, introduced the team: Gordon Raymond, Republic Services General Manager, Jeff Wheeler, Republic Services Operations Manager, Ashely Cowleigh, Republic Services Community Administrator, Sam Rex, Republic Services Commercial Account Manager. Council Members asked questions, and the panelists responded. Bridget Conway, Communications and Public Engagement Deputy Director, added information about the Summit County website’s role in communications regarding Republic Services.

Discussion and approval of the 2023 RAP Tax Recreation Committee's recommendations; Amy Yost (5:22 PM)
Attachment: 2023 RAP Tax Recreation Committee Recommendations
Malena Stevens made a motion to approve the 2023 RAP Tax Recreation Committee's recommendations. (5:28 PM). Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Amy Yost, Recreation Arts and Parks Tax Recreation Committee Chair, presented the Committee’s recommendations.
2)
Criminal Justice Coordinating Council strategic plan discussion and approval, Margaret Olson, Frank Smith, and Malena Stevens (5:29 PM)
Attachment: Staff Report-Summit County Criminal Justice Strategic Plan
Canice Harte made a motion to acknowledge the County Attorney's representation that the work of the CJCC, as contained in the packet, satisfies the state law requirements concerning the CJCC and their work, with no budget impact on the Council unless separately approved during any kind of budget hearings. (5:54 PM) Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Council Member Stevens introduced the proposed strategic plan for the Criminal Justice Coordinating Council (CJCC). Attorney Margaret Olson and Sheriff Frank Smith contributed to the presentation. Council Members asked questions and commented. The presenters responded.
