                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 23-16

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
 WITH GBS BENEFITS, INC. TO PROVIDE BENEFITS CONSULTING SERVICES

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Administrative Services Director would like to contract with a benefits
consulting firm; and

       WHEREAS, GBS Benefits, Inc was selected through a competitive proposal process.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an Agreement with GBS Benefits,
            Inc. to provide Benefits Consulting Services.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
February 2023 by the following vote:
