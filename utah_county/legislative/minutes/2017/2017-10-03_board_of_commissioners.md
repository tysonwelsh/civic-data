1.     APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO SIGN AN AGREEMENT WITH
       ARTHUR GALLAGHER TO PERFORM BROKERAGE AND CONSULTING SERVICE FOR THE
       COUNTY’S GROUP EMPLOYEE BENEFITS PROGRAM
(Track A, 06:35)
Utah County Deputy Attorney Robert J. Moore reviewed the most recent draft of the proposed agreement with
Gallagher Benefit Services, Inc. Mr. Moore noted American Benefits Group provides COBRA administrative
services. The current county provider, NFP, has been paying those fees at approximately $700 monthly or $8,400
annually. This cost was not included in the original Request for Proposals (RFP) disseminated by the county.
Commissioner Graves noted it is not the fault of Gallagher that their estimate did not include this service; since
Gallagher has already reduced their original contract price by $10,000 for the county, paying the additional fees is still
a savings. Utah County Human Resources Director Cammie Taylor pointed out of the top three responses to the RFP,
Gallagher was an outlier – the other two companies were both willing to negotiate these extra expenses for less money.
Commissioners Lee and Graves discussed deadlines and upcoming employee meetings with Mr. Moore and Ms.
Taylor. Commissioner Lee noted Commissioner Ivie may not be aware of this issue, and suggested discussion be
continued for one week so all three Board members could be present.

            Commissioner Graves made the motion to continue Regular Agenda Item No. 1,
            regarding an agreement with Arthur Gallagher to perform brokerage and
            consulting service for the County’s Group Employee Benefits Program, for one
            week to the October 10, 2017 meeting of the Utah County Board of
            Commissioners. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Lee and carried
            with the following vote:
                                                                          AYE: William C. Lee
                                                                                  Greg Graves
                                                                          NAY: None

of their property. He talked about the difference of land values when declared as building lots by the county. Mr.
