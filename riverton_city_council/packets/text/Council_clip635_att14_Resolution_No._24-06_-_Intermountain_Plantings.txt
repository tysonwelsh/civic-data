                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-06

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT BE ISSUED TO
    INTERMOUNTAIN PLANTINGS FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE SPIRIT
         CORNER WATER CONSERVATION LANDSCAPE PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to complete the Spirit Corner Water
       Conservation Landscape; and
       WHEREAS, the City has received a Water Wise Grant to help fund the project.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order Contract be issued to Intermountain
            Plantings for the completion of the Spirit Corner Water Conservation Landscape
            Project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
January, 2024 by the following vote:
