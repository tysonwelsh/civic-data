HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a hillside development permit to allow for development
     of PA-9 area of the Divario development to be known as Divario PA-9 or
     Salarno Hills at Divario on approximately 19.87 acres located southeast of
     Varano Vistas and Cecita Crest at Divario subdivisions on Divario Canyon
     Drive.
St. George City Council Minutes
February 25, 2021
Page Five

      Assistant Public Works Director Wes Jenkins presented the request for a hillside
      permit to allow for development of PA-9 area of the Divario development to be
      known as Divario PA-9 or Salarno Hills at Divario on approximately 19.87 acres
      located at south-east of Varano Vistas and Cecita Crest at Divario subdivisions on
      Divario Canyon Drive. He provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the following
      topics: Overall slope analysis; Preliminary plat. One of the concerns of the Hillside
      Review Board was people falling due to the steep hill. The developer will need to
      install a fence between this property and the BLM property. Another concern was
      with aesthetics. The cut is approximately 15’-20’

      Councilmember Larkin inquired if there were any considerations of not cutting into
      the hillside. Mr. Jenkins replied, there were no discussions regarding this.

      Councilmember McArthur commented the City has the hillside ordinance and it
      should be adhered to. There are too many concessions made.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman clarified any requests that come before the Council
      first go before the Hillside Board for review and recommendation and is something
      the ordinance allows to be considered. Staff denies any requests that are greater
      than what the ordinance allows to be considered by the Board; the item would not
      even go before the Board. The Council can amend the ordinance if they would like to
      tighten it up. He asked the Council to provide staff with direction on what they
      would like to focus on.

      Councilmember Larkin commented that it is disconcerting for her that this is rock, it
      isn’t a normal slope that can be re-vegetated.

      Councilmember Curtis noted one of the realizations he picked up on is the amount of
      work that goes into the report. That gives him a lot more confidence; however, he
      agrees that the Council may need to reevaluate the ordinance.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Curtis to approve the hillside
                    development permit to allow for development of PA-9 area of the
                    Divario development to be known as Divario PA-9 or Salarno Hills at
                    Divario.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Hughes.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – nay
                    Councilmember Larkin – nay
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    Mayor Randall – aye

                    The motion carried.
