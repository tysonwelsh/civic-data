Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: August 25, 2022
Re: Cinnamon Ridge Lots 2 & 3 Plat Amendment Approval Extension Comments: On September 23, 2021, the Planning Commission granted approval of a plat amendment for Cinnamon Ridge Lots 2 & 3, located at approximately 1106 and 1124 E. LeAmbra Lane.  The application number is SUBD-110-2021.  Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 17-9-070 states:
If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced, such approval shall be null and void.  This time period may be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause shown.  The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.
To date, the property owner has not been able to record the plat due to the need to reprint a new mylar and get new signatures on the mylar.  This has happened to the applicant twice and the applicant does not anticipate it needing to be done again.  As such, the applicant is seeking a six-month extension to finalize the signatures and get the plat recorded.  Attached to this memo is the applicant’s written request for approval extension, the original approval letter, and Planning Commission staff report.  Staff received the request for extension on August 7, 2022, prior to the expiration of the plat amendment.
If approved, the site plan approval will be valid through March 23, 2022. The findings are as follows: Sample Motion for Approval – I move that we approve the plat amendment extension, as requested by Broch Lassig, property owner for the Cinnamon Ridge Lots 2 & 3 Plat Amendment Request, application SUBD-110-2021, based on the findings listed in the memo dated August 25, 2022.
1.   That the applicant has shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has occurred towards acquiring the signatures to record the mylar.
Sample Motion for Denial – I move that we deny the site plan extension, as requested by Broch Lassig, property owner for the Cinnamon Ridge Lots 2 & 3 Plat Amendment Request, application SUBD-110-2021, based on the findings listed in the memo dated August 25, 2022.
1.   That the applicant has not shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has not occurred towards acquiring the signatures to record the mylar.
