PUBLIC HEARING/AMEND BOUNDARY LINE/ORDINANCE:
     Public hearing and consideration of an ordinance approving the
     adjustment of a common municipal boundary line with Washington City.

      Assistant Public Works Director Wes Jenkins presented the request to adjust a
      common boundary line with Washington City in the vicinity of 2450 South and
      Crimson Way. The purpose of the adjustment is to align boundaries of adjoining
      properties with the new boundary which alignment will more easily facilitate
      development and reconstruction of future roads and utility infrastructure without
      creating islands of land within the boundaries of the City. He showed the area on
      a map.

      Mayor Randall opened the public hearing. There being no comment, she closed
      the public hearing.

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the zone from OS (Open
     Space) and R-3 (Multi-family) to PD-R (Planned Development
     Residential) on approximately 6.82 acres located at north of Gateway
     Drive, east of Cottonwood Springs Road, and west of the Twin Lakes
     Resort subdivision known as Twin Lakes. Case No. 2021-ZC-008
City Council Meeting Minutes
September 23, 2021
Page Five

      Planner Mike Hadley presented the request to change the zone from OS and R-3
      to PD-R on approximately 6.82 acres located at north of Gateway Drive, east of
      Cottonwood Springs Road, and west of the Twin Lakes Resort subdivision known
      as Twin Lakes. He presented a PowerPoint presentation covering the following
      topics: Vicinity map; Zoning map; Zone change exhibit; Site plan; Landscape
      plan; and Elevations. The Planning Commission recommended approval with the
      following conditions: 1) The applicant will work with staff to address any concerns
      on drainage; 2) The applicant add ten (10) more guest parking stalls; and 3)The
      applicant dress up the backs and sides of the buildings.

      A brief discussion took place regarding the rock fall hazards. The Council
      suggested a berm be required to address the rock fall hazards concerns. A
      discussion also took place regarding the disturbed and undisturbed boundaries of
      the hillside. Assistant Public Works Director Wes Jenkins presented some photos
      of the area in question to better identify the area discussed.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Larkin to approve the
                    ordinance changing the zone from OS and R-3 to PD-R on
                    approximately 6.82 acres located at north of Gateway Drive, east of
                    Cottonwood Springs Road, and west of the Twin Lakes Resort
                    subdivision known as Twin Lakes, including the conditions approved
                    by the Planning Commission: 1) The applicant will work with staff to
                    address any concerns on drainage; 2) The applicant add ten (10)
                    more guest parking stalls; and 3)The applicant dress up the backs
                    and sides of the buildings.
      .
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Smethurst.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a roll call vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
