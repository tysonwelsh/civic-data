Independent QC of the newly built `kearns_city_council/` repo, run with the
`audit-city-data` method (baseline anomaly screen · random ground-truthing ·
reconciliation of redundant representations · completeness vs external reality).
Read-only: no canonical CSV, minutes file, extractor, or `.db` was modified.
`db/build_db.py` was NOT re-run (READ `db/civic.db` only).

## Headline verdict: **FIX-FIRST**

The data that is present is faithful and reconciles exactly. **But the council
dataset is materially incomplete, and the gap is mis-described as unrecoverable.**
The `2017-2023` metro-township council minutes the build logs as "genuinely absent
(only agendas + MP3 audio on PMN)" **do in fact exist on Utah PMN as written
"Meeting Minutes" attachments** and were simply not harvested. This must be fixed
(harvest + re-carve + correct the gap record) before the council dataset can be
called complete. Everything else ships.

## Finding 1 (BLOCKER / data loss) — 2017-2023 township council minutes exist on PMN and were not harvested

**Claim under test** (from `meeting_minutes/minutes_unrecovered.csv`, `SOURCES.md`,
and the build facts): *"the 2017-2023 metro-township council posted only agendas +
MP3 audio to PMN — NO written minutes were ever published; text council minutes
begin 2024-01."* 111 meetings are logged in `minutes_unrecovered.csv` on this basis
with the reason *"minutes genuinely absent (not fabricated)."*

**Test:** fetched the PMN notice pages for 6 logged-unrecovered township-council
dates spread across the range and read the literal PMN attachment **Category**
labels. Result — **6 / 6 carry a written "Meeting Minutes" attachment:**

| Logged date | PMN notice | Attachment labeled "Meeting Minutes" |
|---|---|---|
| 2017-01-18 | notice/371805 | `011817.docx` |
| 2017-02-15 | notice/377737 | `021517.pdf` |
| 2018-12-17 | notice/504553 | `12-17-18.pdf` |
| 2020-10-12 | notice/634025 | `10-12-20.pdf` |
| 2021-10-11 | notice/708213 | `10-11-21.pdf` |
| 2023-10-12 | notice/863927 | `10-12-23.pdf` |

Each notice also has the MP3 audio and a `…Agenda.docx` (category "Other"). So the
minutes are **present on PMN, categorized as minutes, across the whole 2017-2023
span** — not audio-only. The "genuinely absent" characterization is **incorrect**;
this is a recoverable **acquisition miss**, not an honest gap.

**Scope:** `minutes_unrecovered.csv` council rows by year — 2017:14, 2018:16,
2019:14, 2020:17, 2021:16, 2022:11, 2023:14 (≈102 township-era rows), plus a few
2024-2026 not-yet-posted. On this sample, the great majority of those ~102 are
recoverable. Effect: the council record currently begins ~2024-01 when **seven
additional years of primary-source minutes were available all along.**

**Not a fabrication.** No fake data was created; the failure is (a) under-collection
and (b) a false "unrecoverable" label. Cardinal-rule-1 corollary: a recoverable gap
must not be documented as absent. The `README`/`CLAUDE`/`VERIFICATION` written in
this pass describe the ACTUAL state (recoverable back-catalog / acquisition backlog).

**Fix:** harvest PMN body 5823's full notice back-catalog 2017-2023 → pull each
"Meeting Minutes" attachment → carve votes → rebuild `db/`, `weeks/`. Then correct
`minutes_unrecovered.csv` (drop the recovered dates; keep only genuine no-minutes
meetings), regenerate `SOURCES.md`, and update the build-fact note. `.docx` minutes
(2017-era) need a docx→text path in addition to `pdftotext`.

**Parity check (PC gap is genuine):** the same test on 2 PC 2017-2018
logged-unrecovered notices (2017-04-10 notice/387205; 2018-04-09 notice/455189)
found **Agenda + Packet only, NO "Meeting Minutes"** — 2/2. So the PC claim
("approved minutes begin 2019-03") is HONEST and holds; the council miss is
specific to council body 5823, not a blanket pre-2019 PMN emptiness.

  member+vote CSV rows = **5** = `vote` rows (1 council abstain: Colby; 4 PC
  abstains). 0 dropped, 0 orphan FKs (`vote→motion`, `motion→meeting`). PASS.
- **Meeting count** db 74 = council 31 + PC 43. Council 31 (not 32) because the
  `2025-08-26` Board of Canvassers minutes carry **0 motions** (`votes:[]`), so
  `build_db` creates no meeting row — explained, benign; the file is present +
  indexed.
- **weeks/** 32 bundles; weekly votes sum **178 = flat council total**; weeks not
  older than the canonical CSVs (not stale). PASS.
- **Tally invariant:** validator `f.tally` reports 0/1 named tallies "match" — this
  is the **expected** narrative-tally artifact (the 4-0 motion names only the
  abstainer; the 4 unnamed ayes are honestly unrecorded). Not a defect.
- **Vote ceilings:** no motion exceeds roster size; no member votes twice on one
  motion; every named voter resolves to roster.
