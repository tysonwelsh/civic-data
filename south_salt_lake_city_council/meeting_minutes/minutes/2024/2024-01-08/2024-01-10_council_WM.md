South Salt Lake – City Council Work Meeting                                   January 10, 2024


Matters for Discussion

   1. Elect Chair and Vice Chair for 2024

       Council Chair:
       Council Member Huff made a motion to nominate Council Member Bynum as the Council Chair
       for 2024. Council Member Williams seconded the motion.

       MOTION: LeAnne Huff
       SECOND: Clarissa Williams


       Council Member Sanchez made a motion to nominate Council Member Mitchell as the Council
       Chair for 2024. Per no second motion made, the motion was rejected.

       City Recorder, Ariel Andrus, affirmed that there was no need for a vote with only one standing
       nomination.

       Council Member Bynum was selected as the 2024 Council Chair.


       Vice Chair:
       Council Member Williams made a motion to nominate Council Member Thomas as the Council
       Vice Chair for 2024. Council Member Huff seconded the motion.

       MOTION: Clarissa Williams
       SECOND: LeAnne Huff


       Council Member Sanchez made a motion to nominate himself as the Council Vice Chair for 2024.
       Per no second motion made, the motion was rejected.

       City Recorder, Ariel Andrus, affirmed that there was no need for a vote with only one standing
       nomination.

       Council Member Thomas was selected as the 2024 Council Vice Chair.


   2. Council Rules Training
      City Recorder, Ariel Andrus, went over the City Council Rules of Procedures on: Rule 12 -
      Decorum, and explained the State Law approved reasons for calling for Closed Door Meetings,
      as well as the 2/3 majority vote needed for them to take place if called for during a public
      meeting. Typically, Closed Door Meetings are planned for in advance with the reason being
      noticed on the agenda.

   3. Appointment by the Mayor: Danielle Croyle – Police Chief
      Mayor Wood presented Danielle Croyle to the Council. This item was added to the Work
      Meeting agenda for initial discussion per Council rules.

       Questions that were submitted by the Council prior to the meeting were asked by the Council
       Chair.

       The appointee, Danielle Croyle, will be presented again for the Council’s consent and approval in
       the Regular Meeting. The appointee’s resume, a copy of which is attached and incorporated by
       this reference, has also been provided to the Council.




       The meeting adjourned at 6:56 p.m.
