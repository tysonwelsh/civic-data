              Herriman City Fire Service Area Minutes
                              Wednesday, March 25, 2026
                               Approved April 6, 2026

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Fire Safety Area meeting. The meeting was
held on Wednesday, March 25, 2025, at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355
West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law,
was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media,
and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Lorin Palmer

Trustees Present: Terrah Anderson, Matt Basham, Jared Henderson, Teddy Hodges

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, City
Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran,
Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, Interim Police Cody Stromberg, Assistant to the
City Manager Trevor Ram, Community Development Director Blake Thomas, City Planner
Michael Maloy, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, UFA Assistant Chief Anthony Widdison,
Operations Director Monte Johnson, City Engineer Bryce Terry, HPD Commander Brent
Adamson, HPD Commander Zach Adams, Operations Director Monte Johnson.

1. Call to Order - 7:00 p.m. (or as soon as possible thereafter)
Chair Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 7:09 p.m.

2. Public Hearing
   2.1. Public Hearing and Consideration of a Resolution Amending the Fiscal year 2026
        Herriman City Fire Safety Area budget – Kyle Maurer, Director of Finance and
        Administrative Services
March 25, 2026 Herriman City Fire Service Area Minutes                                      Page 2 of 3


      Finance Director Kyle Maurer presented the budget amendment request to the Board. He explained
      that the City was looking at purchasing fire station land in the southeast corner of the city, but
      initial appraisals had come back significantly higher than what had been budgeted in the impact
      fee fund. The budget amendment would authorize the use of $900,000 from the fund balance,
      which would be combined with the $1 million already allocated in impact fee funding to
      accomplish the land purchase if the city council ultimately approved the transaction.

      Director Maurer outlined the reimbursement plan, explaining that as the city collected impact fees
      in the future, those funds would be transferred back to the Fire Service Area to reimburse the
      service area for this expense.

      Chair Palmer opened the public hearing.

      No comments were offered.

      Trustee Teddy Hodges moved to close the public hearing. Trustee Basham seconded the motion
      and all voted aye.

      Trustee Matt Basham moved to approve Resolution R01-26 approving an amendment to the Fiscal
      Year 2026 Herriman City Fire Service Area budget. Trustee Terrah Anderson seconded the
      motion.

      The vote was recorded as follows:
      Trustee Terrah Anderson              Aye
      Trustee Matt Basham                  Aye
      Trustee Jared Henderson              Aye
      Trustee Teddy Hodges                 Aye
      Chair Lorin Palmer                   Aye
      The motion passed unanimously.

      3. Adjournment
      Trustee Jared Henderson moved to adjourn the Herriman City Fire Service Area meeting at 7:11
      p.m. Trustee Hodges seconded the motion, and all voted aye.
