           4.2 Discussion            and Consideration              of Ordinance              21-27,       Regulating,            Preventing,        and
           Banning        the Discharge             of Fireworks         Within           Areas     of the City        that       are East      of 900
           East    Street
           Mayor       Silvestrini      said he and others lobbied                  the Legislature           several       years ago to shorten
           the numbers           of days fireworks         were able to be lawfully                    discharged          and to enable
           governments           to have more local            control      over the discharge               of fireworks          based upon
           conditions         of dryness.      He said the Legislature                intended        to prohibit        cities     from     an outright
           ban of fireworks,            but cities    could     go through          a process         to restrict     areas that met certain
           conditions.        The restrictions          can only be enacted               upon the recommendation                      of the fire
           marshal.      The fire marshal            recornrnended           the designated            restriction       line be 900 E. in
           Millcreek.       He said the City           could not ban fireworks                    citywide     per code. The Mayor
           recommended             avoiding        the use of personal            fireworks         this season due to the heat and
           drought.


           Council      Member          Marchant       said the owners            of the firework            companies            were threatening
