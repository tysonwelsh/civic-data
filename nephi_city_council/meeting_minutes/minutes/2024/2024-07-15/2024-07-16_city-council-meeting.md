AMENDMENT TO THE DIVISION OF NATURAL RESOURCES LAND EXCHANGE AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council that earlier they had authorized the mayor to
sign a new land exchange agreement with the Utah State Division of Natural Resources (DNR). This
agreement was to facilitate the exchange of land owned by the city and by DNR to expand the golf
course. An amendment to that agreement is now being proposed because an updated appraisal had
been completed on both pieces of property. The appraisal update shows that the city’s property is
valued at $60,000 while the portion of the parcel from the DNR that will be swapped is valued at
$35,000. In the original exchange the DNR parcel was valued higher and the city needed to pay the
difference at closing. In this case the request from DNR is that the city would donate the difference in

ROLLIN’ HOME SUBDIVISION AGREEMENT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the current owner of the Rollin’ Home RV Park is looking to sell
the park and stay on as the manager. The current buyer would like to purchase the land immediately
occupied by the park and not the undeveloped land around the park. The undeveloped land would
remain in the ownership of the current owner of the RV Park. Because the land will be subdivided the
new owner will need to go forward with the city’s subdivision process. The subdivision process usually
will last 2-3 months based on the scheduled meetings for Planning Commission and City Council. The
buyer would like to purchase the property sooner than a subdivision process would be completed. To
accomplish this, a separate agreement has been drafted that would require the new owner to comply
with the subdivision process. If the buyer would agree to comply with the city’s subdivision process,
then the purchase/sale could be accomplished sooner. The proposed agreement had been prepared
by the city attorney and the agreement with Rollin’ Home RV Park would allow for the sale of the
property.
   Councilor JD Parady moved to authorize the mayor to sign the agreement with Rollin’ Home RV Park.
Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

Work Session 8:35 p.m.

    1.    Mr. Atkinson presented a Central School Bell Monument design that the Daughters of the Utah
         Pioneers have presented and approved. City Arborist Gerratt Bethers is heading up this project
         and wanted the design approved by the council before proceeding. The council liked the design
         and directed Mr. Atkinson to proceed with the design presented.
    2.   Councilor Parady said that he and Councilor Travis Worwood had been researching the inland
         port. They had invited Danny Stewart, Utah Inland Port Authority Associate Vice President of
         Regional Project Areas and Business Development and Brent Boswell, Juab County Economic
         Development Director to present the idea to the city council. Mr. Stewart explained that the
         city would need to designate certain areas in the community as an inland port and the areas
         would not need to be contiguous with the city boundaries. Councilor Parady sad the city would
         have complete control of how the areas would be zoned and developed. Mr. Steward explained
         how the new tax revenues generated by the project areas would be reinvested into the area.
         Mr. Stewart said that the first step would be for the council to adopt a resolution identifying the
         project areas by September 1st. Mr. Stewart answered many questions and outlined the
         procedure if the city wanted to participate in the inland port this year.
    3.   Mr. Atkinson related a request from a property owner that wants to develop a large lot
         subdivision in the county. The property owner would like Nephi City Power to provide electricity
         to the subdivision if Rocky Mountain Power (RMP) grants permission for the city to serve the
         subdivision. The developer would pay all the costs associated if the city was allowed to service
         the area. The council was open to the idea of seeing if RMP would be willing to give permission
         to the developer. Mr. Atkinson warned the council about future potential problems of a
         residential development being built near a meat processing plant.
    4.   Councilor Cowan moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the
