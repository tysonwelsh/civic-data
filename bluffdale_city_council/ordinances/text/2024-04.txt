                                    ORDINANCE NO.2024-04


AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH, AMENDING
BLUFFDALE CITY CODE TITLE 7.20.40


       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the City of Bluffdale (the "City") authority
to enact ordinances that the Bluffdale City Council(the "City Council") considers necessary
or appropriate for the health,safety, and welfare of the City; and

         WHEREAS,the City has enacted Title 7.20.40 of the Bluffdale City Code;and

         WHEREAS,the City Council finds it is in the best interest of the City to enact
amendments to Title 7.20.40.


NOW,THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BYTHE CITY COUNCIL OFTHE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH:

         SECTION 1. Amendment. Bluffdale City Code is hereby amended as shown on Exhibit A.

         SECTION 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable,such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance shall be
severable.


       SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting as required by law.

7.20.040 Parking
   A. No parking during a "snowstorm",as defined In section 7.20.020 of this chapter,and for
      a period of twenty four(24) hours after a snowstorm. It shall be unlawful for any person
      causing or responsible for vehicles,trailers, dumpsters,or other obstructions to be left
      on city streets during a snowstorm or for a period of twenty four (24) hours after a
      snowstorm, as specified herein. Any vehicle or obstruction in violation of this chapter
      may be towed or removed at owner's expense.
   B. Exceptions:
      1. Vehicles may park for not more than three(3) minutes on the street when loading or
         unloading passengers.
      2. Vehicles may park for not more than thirty(30) minutes on the street when loading,
          unloading,or delivering property or supplies.
      3^Medical, fire, police, city maintenance, or other emergency vehicles during
          emergency response shall not be limited by the parking restrictions of this section.
      ^T4.Vehic[es mav park during and after a snowstorm in areas designated by City
         signage.
   C. Penalty: Any person in violation of this section shall be guilty of an infraction,subject to
      penalty as provided in section 1.40.010 of this code.
