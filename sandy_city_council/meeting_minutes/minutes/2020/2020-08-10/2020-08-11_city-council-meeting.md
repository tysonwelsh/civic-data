Non-voting Items

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto will send out an updated agenda planning calendar tomorrow.

                      He gave an update to the Council regarding the open position in the Council Office.

Council Member Business


                      Council Member Sharkey gave an update from the ULCT meeting.

                      Council Member Zoltanski spoke about a closure at Dimple Dell Monument Trail Head.
                      She gave feedback from the Salt Lake County Recorder's Office - the Recorder's Office
                      received many Property Watch registrations from Sandy residents following last week's
                      Council presentation.

                      Council Member Houseman acknowledged the loss of a fire fighter from the Unified Fire
                      Department. The Sandy Arts Guild is preparing for the 35 year Anniversary and she
                      spoke about upcoming events related to the anniversary.

                      Council Member Stroud also gave an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. She mentioned
                      that all events at the Sandy Amphitheater have been cancelled and she spoke about the
                      fall event that has been planned. She thanked the Parks and Recreation Department and
                      Scott Earl for their help with a resident's concerns.

                      Council Member Robinson thanked everyone who helped with the Canal Trail.


Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Bradburn updated the Council on upcoming events. He thanked the Police
                      Department for their help with protests that occurred in Sandy over the past weekend.
                      The Police Department has implemented reforms. He asked Chief Severson to speak to
                      the Council.

                      Chief Greg Severson further updated the Council regarding the protests that occurred over
                      the past weekend. He thanked Council Members Stroud and Houseman for their support.

CAO Report

                      Matt Huish, CAO, had no report.

1.         20-265                 The South Valley Chamber of Commerce providing the Council with an
                                  update on the status of small businesses in Sandy City
                                  Council Member Sharkey introduced Greg Summerhays, President of the South Valley
                                  Chamber.

                                  Greg Summerhays spoke about the impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on the small
                                  businesses in Sandy City. He introduced Rowena Montoya, founder of JulieAnn
                                  Caramels.

                                  Rowena Montoya spoke about the growth of her business and how COVID-19 has
                                  impacted her business.

                                  Greg Summerhays introduced Dr. Brown.

                                  Dr. Michael Brown and his wife, Tiffany Brown, Office Manager, opened their business in
                                  Sandy City on March 2nd. He spoke about his new business and the impacts of COVID
                                  on their business.

                                  Greg Summerhays further briefed the Council about the impacts on Sandy Business
                                  owners and stressed creativity and long-term strategies to help Sandy businesses. He
                                  also expressed his gratitude to the Sandy Community and introduced Jay Francis who
                                  will be the new President of the South Valley Chamber.

                                  Jay Francis introduced himself and spoke about his new role as President of the South
                                  Valley Chamber. He thanked Greg Summerhays for all his work with the Chamber.

                                  Council Members thanked Greg Summerhays and welcomed Jay Francis.

                                  presentation.

                                  Ryan Kump introduced Darien Alcorn, Senior Civil Attorney, who provided details
                                  regarding the street vacation and the legal process associated with the vacation of 9270

                                  additional questions and comments for Ms. Alcorn and Mr. Kump and Mr. Gladbach.

                                  Public Comment:

                                  Dustin Fratto gave instructions on how the public may participate in the public comment.

                                  Brian Chamberlin, is a property owner in the area and is a strong supporter of this project
                                  and thought it would be a great asset to have a signal at that intersection.

                                  Public Comment Closed
                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Monica Zoltanski, to
                                  adopt the proposed ordinance #20-07 to vacate a portion of 9270 South Street as
                                  shown in the attached exhibits, based on the following findings and subject to
                                  the following conditions of approval:

                                  FINDINGS
                                  1.             The partial street vacation action by the City will transfer the
                                  ownership of the subject property to the abutting property owners, who will use
                                  the area in future development of the property, or agree to own and maintain the
                                  property. Therefore, good cause for the action exists.
                                  2.             That there is neither material injury to either the public interest
                                  nor any person by the requested vacation.
                                  3.             Staff has not received any objection from the abutting property
                                  owners.

                                  CONDITIONS
                                  1.            That the approval is conditioned upon the City proceeding
                                  through the vacation process through action by the City Council.
