Procedures:

The election of an individual to fill a midterm vacancy shall comply with Utah Code § 20A-1-510 and
the following procedures:

               (a) The Council shall conduct interviews of all individuals who applied for the
                   midterm vacancy in an open meeting. The City Council shall conduct interviews
                   in the order in which applications were received, beginning with the individual
                   who applied earliest.

               (b) The Council shall submit interview questions to the Council Chair in advance of
                   the meeting. The Council Chair shall then review those questions with the City
                   Attorney to ensure compliance with applicable law. The Council Chair shall then
                   decide upon an appropriate number of questions considering factors including,
                   but not limited to:

                      a. The number of applicants

                      b. The length of the midterm vacancy

                      c. The position to be filled

                      d. The amount of time allotted for the City Council meeting during which
                         the interviews will take place

               (c) The Council Chair, or the Council Chair’s designee, shall ask each candidate the
                   decided upon questions on behalf of the Council.

               (d) The Council Chair shall determine the length of time for each interview based on
                   the number of candidates and the allotted time for the meeting at which the
                   interviews will take place. However, each applicant interviewed shall be given
                   the same maximum amount of time.

               (e) Voting shall be by written ballot which identifies the Council member casting the
                   vote. Council Members shall cast a vote for a single candidate. The City
                   Recorder shall collect all written votes and announce the vote cast, as well as
                   the council member who cast the vote.

               (f) As provided in Utah Code § 20A-1-510, the candidate receiving the majority vote
                   of the City Council in the initial vote shall fill the vacancy.
(g) If no candidate receives a majority vote of the City Council in the initial vote
    described above, the two candidates receiving the most votes in the initial vote
    shall be placed before the City Council for a second vote.

(h) Tie breaking procedures established in state law shall be followed. When a coin
    toss is utilized as part of the tie breaking process, the applicant who applied
    earlier will be assigned heads. The City Recorder shall conduct the coin toss.

AN ORDINANCE OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL ENACTING CHAPTER 12.01 OF
THE SOUTH SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL CODE PROHIBITING CAMPING IN ALL PUBLIC PLACES
IN THE CITY OF SOUTH SALT LAKE.

       WHEREAS, the South Salt Lake City Council (the “Council”) is authorized to enact and amend
ordinances governing the City of South Salt Lake (the “City”); and
       WHEREAS, the City has three promises to which it strives to adhere: 1) Every child has the
opportunity to attend and to graduate from college, 2) Every resident has a safe, clean home and
neighborhood, and 3) Everyone has the opportunity to be healthy and to prosper; and
        WHEREAS, enactment of a more robust ordinance prohibiting camping in public places supports
an enhanced quality of life and is consistent with the City’s three promises, specifically that every
resident has a safe, clean home and neighborhood, and that everyone, including those engaging in public

         WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that enacting Chapter 12.01 prohibiting camping in
all public places in the City of South Salt Lake is in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of
the residents of the City and all who work, learn, worship, or play in the City;
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED, by the City Council of the City of South Salt Lake as
follows:
SECTION I: Enactment. Chapter 12.01is hereby enacted, as attached hereto and incorporated by
reference in “Exhibit A.”
SECTION II: Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of this
ordinance is, for any reason, held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction, such
provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision, and such holding shall not
affect the validity of the remaining portions of this ordinance.
SECTION III: Conflict with Existing Ordinances, Resolutions, or Policies. To the extent that any
ordinances, resolutions, or policies of the City of South Salt Lake conflict with the provisions of this
ordinance, this ordinance shall prevail.
SECTION IV. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon Mayor’s signature and
publication, or after fifteen days of transmission to the office of the Mayor if neither approved nor
disapproved by the Mayor, and thereafter, publication.

                      City Council Staff Report
                        January 8, 2025 City Council Meeting


                                         Summary
Currently, the South Salt Lake Municipal Code prohibits camping in the following ways: 1)
camping is prohibited in parks; and 2) camping is prohibited as a land use in every land use
district because it is not specifically permitted in the City’s land use matrix. The proposed
language would enact a camping prohibition on all public property in the City boundaries.
Quality of life for South Salt Lake residents and any individuals engaging in camping in the
City is decreased when public camping is allowed to persist. Additionally, the state
legislature has strongly expressed its desire that public camping in municipalities be
abated and has gone as far as tying available funding to the enforcement of camping
statutes. Expansion of the camping prohibition from parks to all public property in the City
is an effort to improve the ability of the City to abate camping and seek to maintain or
improve quality of life for all individuals who live, learn, work, worship, or play in the City.

                      Current Applicable Rules and Laws
1. South Salt Lake Municipal Code 12.38.070
       No person shall camp, lodge, or pitch a tent, fly, lean-to, tarpaulin, or any other type
       of camping equipment in any park except in conjunction with a city-sponsored
       event.
2. South Salt Lake Municipal Code 12.38.010(5) Definitions
       "Park" means real property owned by the city or other government entity and
       designated for public use and enjoyment for recreation purposes.
3. South Salt Lake Municipal Code 12.44.060
       No person shall camp, lodge or pitch a tent, fly, lean-to, tarpaulin or any other type
       of camping equipment in any park or playground except in conjunction with a city-
       sponsored camp or except in cases of local emergency as declared by the mayor of
       South Salt Lake City.
4. South Salt Lake Municipal Code 17.03.010(A)
       Any Use not specifically permitted or conditionally permitted in this Land Use Matrix
       is prohibited.

                        January 8, 2025 City Council Meeting


                                          Analysis
The City of South Salt Lake has Three Promises to which it strives to adhere:
   1. Every child has the opportunity to attend and to graduate from college
   2. Every resident has a safe, clean home and neighborhood; and
   3. Everyone has the opportunity to be healthy and to prosper.
Implementation of the proposed language assists the City in its commitment to ensure that
every resident has a safe, clean home and neighborhood and everyone has the opportunity
to be healthy and to prosper, as public camping reduces quality of life for everyone
working, playing, learning, worshipping, or living in South Salt Lake, including those
engaged in public camping.
Currently the City is limited in its ability to abate public camping. As noted above, the City
can only address camping if it occurs in parks. While this is not limited to City owned parks,
it still limits the enforcement ability of City enforcement officials when public camping
occurs. The updated language includes a more robust definition of camping and an
expanded definition of where it is prohibited. Enacting the proposed language expands the
City’s ability to abate public camping on all publicly owned property, regardless of which
governmental entity owns the property.

                                     Council Action
As this is the first reading of the proposed language and as an ordinance is not yet finalized,
further review by staff and City Council is warranted. Therefore, staff recommends that the
Council move this item to a date certain for further discussion and consideration.
At a future meeting, the Council, consistent with its rules, will have the ability to: 1) pass an
ordinance enacting the language; 2) move the language to a future meeting for further
consideration; or 3) table the proposed language for an indefinite period of time.

GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS:

Land Use & Neighborhood Strategy 6: Regulate land uses based on compatibility with surrounding uses, the health of
residential areas, and economic feasibility.

         Analysis: Currently, the properties are restricted to the types of allowed uses in the TOD District. However, with
         the proposed zoning change, the Applicants and property owners of 3302 S., 3330 S., and 3340 S 300 West will
         gain the flexibility to lease to tenants whose business activities are better aligned with and more compatible
         with the surrounding land uses, fostering a cohesive Commercial General District immediately south of the same
         zone across 3300 South.
