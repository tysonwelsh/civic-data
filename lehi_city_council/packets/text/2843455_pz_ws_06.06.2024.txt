25   Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.
26
27   1) Call to Order
28
29   Commission Chair Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting.
30
31   2) Discussion Items
32
33   2.1) Discussion of a 200-foot buffer between hotels/motels and residential.

34   Jacob Curtis discussed code amendments, while Annie Reid presented on buffers between
35   commercial and residential zones. Both addressed issues related to privacy and the lack of
36   implemented buffers. The Planning Department noted that this topic would likely continue to
37   arise until it is properly addressed.

38   Commissioner Eyre arrived at the meeting at 5:56 p.m.

39   Commissioner Everett expressed that there is merit in exploring buffers between zones, and
40   Commissioner Jackson agreed. Commissioner Jackson stated that respecting property rights does

     Lehi City Planning Commission                  1                                      June 6, 2024
41   not mean disregarding someone else's property and raised questions about potential negatives,
42   such as the challenges of repurposing a property like a car wash if it shuts down. He also
43   inquired about existing regulations, whether the matter is legislative for the Council, and whether
44   it conflicts with other ordinances. Mike West reminded the Commissioners that they are citizen
45   planners whose role is to provide recommendations and advice.

46   2.2) Discussion of car wash uses
47
48   Jacob Curtis gave a brief presentation on the permitted uses for car washes and the related code
49   for Lehi City.
50
51   2.3) Training and discussion on Lehi City water rights requirements for development.
52
53   Mike West presented information on water rights, which include underground water, surface
54   water, spring water, and deep wells. He discussed Chapter 37, which outlines the process for
55   obtaining water rights and the associated requirements. He emphasized the importance of
56   infrastructure to ensure that water resources and rights are secured before development occurs.
57
58   2.4) Discussion of coordinating with neighboring cities notices, projects, etc.
59
60   Mike West had a brief discussion on noticing and neighboring infrastructure. The
61   Commissioners expressed interest in seeing impact fees and were curious whether neighboring
62   cities have different criteria than Lehi, making them more attractive for development. Mike
63   noted that much of it is market-driven and inquired about the working relationship with other
64   cities.
65
66   3) Adjournment
67
68   With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
69   Everett moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Eyre seconded the motion. The motion
70   passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:18 p.m.
