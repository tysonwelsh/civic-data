 STAFF/APPLICANT PRESENTATION
 The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
 and recommendations. Key points addressed in the Staff's presentation to the Planning Commission included the
 following:
    • A General Plan amendment to approximately 10 acres in the Southwest Area Future Land Use map from low
        density residential (LDR) to Commercial.
    • The proposed amendment is aligned with the neighborhood desire to have no low density residential on this parcel.
    • The new zooming will allow for a desired distanced setback from the neighborhood to the north of the parcel.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
  • Comments from Becky Bogden, the Lakewood neighborhood chair: The neighborhood appreciates the efforts of
     staff to be mindful of the neighborhood and neighborhood desires while crafting these zone changes. She also
     expressed her desire that the City and staff be mindful of the 65-foot set-back requirements abutting the
     neighborhood and not change those requirements in the future.


 PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
 Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   •   Commissioner Jensen expressed support for the amendment as this still meets the goals of the initial Future Land
       Use map in providing a buffer to the neighborhood.
