Background

This is a public hearing for a Short-term Rental for Stephanie Winkel to be located in an
existing home at 13752 South 4170 West in the Deer Mountain Estates Phase 7 Subdivision.
The neighborhood is zoned RR-22, which is a Single-family Zone with ½ acre minimum lots.
The subject property is 0.52-acre, the existing house built in 1998 is a rambler with 2,700-sf
above grade and 2,600-sf in the basement. The house has a three-car garage with an extra-
wide driveway and a fenced back yard.

Ordinances and Issues
Short term rentals are considered Home Occupations under Riverton City Code and are allowable
as conditional uses in all residential zones. The code gives a list (see 18.190.051 items 1 thru 13)
of requirements for licensing and operating a short-term rental including:
     1. The applicant must own the home and live there.
     2. Rentals are limited to 150 nights per year.
     3. Only one rental agreement at a time, and no more than 8 people.
