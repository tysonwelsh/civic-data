                                 Emigration Canyon Resolution



RESOLUTION NO. 2024-06-01                                                   Date: June 25, 2024

          A RESOLUTION OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL ADOPTING THE
          ANNUAL BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2024 – 2025 FOR THE OPERATION OF
          THE CITY

         WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Council has previously adopted a tentative budget
for the fiscal year 2024-2025 budget, and

       WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon has made the tentative budget available to the public for
review and consideration as required by law; and

        WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Council has held a public hearing on the proposed
Fiscal Year 2024-2025 budget for the City;

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL FOR EMIGRATION
CANYON:

        1.      That the attached exhibit A is hereby adopted as the Fiscal Year 2024-2025 budget
for the operation of Emigration Canyon for the fiscal year 2024-2025 or until such time as it may
be amended.

          2.   This resolution shall take effect upon passing.
