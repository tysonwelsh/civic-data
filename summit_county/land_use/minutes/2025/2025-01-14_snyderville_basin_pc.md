Public hearing and recommendation to the County Council for amendments to Chapter 11, Definitions. The purpose of the amendments is to amend the definition of Recreation Vehicle. Legislative Process. Project #24-164. Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planner.
(6:03 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Development Code Amendment 10-11-1 RV Definition Staff Report and Exhibits 1.14.25.pdf
Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner,
presented an application for a proposed Code Amendment to Chapter 10-11 (Definitions). The proposal was to amend the existing definition of Recreation Vehicle by removing the language, "less than 9 feet (9') wide and thirty-five feet (35') long" from the definition.
Commission Chair Pro-Tem Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:08 p.m.
No members of the public commented.
Commission Chair Pro-Tem Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:08 p.m.
Commissioners commented and asked brief clarifying questions. Planner Kuhrmeyer responded to their concerns.
Makena Hawley made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council per the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the application. Megan Recher seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Tyann Mooney.

Director Items
(6:17 P.M.)
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner,
requested a current member of the Snyderville Basin Planning Commission volunteer to replace Thomas Cooke on the General Plan Advisory Board. Commissioner Recher agreed to serve on the Board.
Planner Milliner stated the next meeting scheduled for the 28th would be cancelled.
Planner Milliner reviewed the Development Code regarding the specific set of rules relating to the Chair and Vice Chair of the Commission. He stated the first meeting in March is generally when the Chair and Vice Chair are elected to serve for one year. If the Chair withdraws the Vice Chair is elevated to the Chair position and a new Vice Chair is elected to serve out the remainder of the term. Commissioner Mooney was elevated to the Chair position. The Vice Chair election will be held in February.
Planner Milliner added that the Planning Department would be coming back to the Commissioners to discuss DRC appointments and that the County Council has received five or six applications to replace Commissioners Conabee and Cooke and will be holding interviews and announcing the appointments in the coming weeks.
Commission Chair Pro-Tem Carmichael thanked Thomas Cooke for his eight years of service on the Snyderville Basin Planning Commission.
