1.1 Public Hearing on Consideration of the Draft Project Area Plan and Budget for

Millcreek Center Community Reinvestment Area

Mike Winder said in the original timeline, the Agency would have had the continued
hearing on and would have adopted the plan and budget for the Millcreek Center
Community Reinvestment Area (CRA). He said the Agency voted to abandon the
pursuit of blight in this area in December, which legally set the CRA down another
path. State law requires the City to go through the interlocal agreement path now
instead of the Taxing Entity Committee path for the CRA approval. He said the
boundaries have been revised in the Millcreek Center CRA due to public comment
received. He said there will not be any action required of the Agency yet, but when
the interlocal agreements are signed off by the other agencies, they will come to the
Agency for approval.

Vice Chair Uipi opened the public hearing. There was no comment provided.

Board Member Jackson moved to close the public hearing. Board Member Marchant
seconded. Vice Chair Uipi called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The motion
passed unanimously.

Winder said the map that was passed out today to the audience is the map the City intends to use
for the project area. Vice Chair Uipi pointed out that removing the blight study from the project
area made the project okay with the public. Winder highlighted the revised map, which was
found on the Millcreek website. He said it no longer included the Home Depot, Granite Mill
Court, and other areas that were not intended for redevelopment. Winder said staff was looking
for opportunities to meet with property owners to craft win-win situations. Vice Chair Uipi asked
how the property owners were being contacted. Winder said staff meets one-on-one with them.
He announced that at the City’s 24 Anniversary Party on January 24" that the city center
consultants would be engaging with the public on the city center master plan. Vice Chair Uipi
invited the public to contact Winder for those individual meetings.
