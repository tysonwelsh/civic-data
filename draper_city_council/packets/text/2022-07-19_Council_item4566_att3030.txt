                               ORDINANCE NO. 1546



AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 14 OF THE
DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING SPEED LIMITS AND RESTRICTIONS ON
USE OF ROADS.



      WHEREAS, Title 14 regulates vehicle traffic; and

     WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to revise certain terms of the
Draper City Municipal Code to address provisions to provide for the health, safety,
and welfare of the citizens of Draper; and

        WHEREAS, the Draper City Traffic Committee has met and discussed the
traffic problems on and near Traverse Ridge and Rambling Roads; and

     WHEREAS, The Draper City Traffic Committee has found good cause to
propose the amendments to Title 14 based on the findings below; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text amendment and
finds good cause to revise the terms and provisions of Title 14.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings to amend Title 14 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) Traverse Ridge
Road and Rambling Road are roads in mountainous areas of the city with steep
grades; 2) the nature of Traverse Ridge and Rambling Roads creates dangerous
situations; 3) a large contingency of fatal accidents that occur on or involve Traverse
Ridge and Rambling roads also involve restricted vehicles as defined in DCMC 14-7-
020 or trucks with three axles or more; 4) the city has a compelling interest in
maintaining the roads and other public infrastructure on Traverse Ridge and
Rambling Roads; 5) the city has a compelling interest in ensuring efficient routes for
emergency vehicles, public transportation vehicles, and school busses; 6) the
proposed amendment is in compliance with the Utah Code Annotated; and 7) the
proposed amendment is in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the
citizens of Draper.
      Section 2. Revision. Sections 14-7-030 and 14-7-040 of the Draper City
Municipal Code are hereby amended to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached
hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

      Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to
correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to
submitting the Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

      Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS THE 19THDAY OF JULY 2022.
