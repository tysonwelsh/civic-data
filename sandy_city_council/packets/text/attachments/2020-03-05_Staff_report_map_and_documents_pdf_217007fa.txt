DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Cody Wright, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to allow for a 2,250
square foot accessory structure, nearly 25 feet high (24' 9") (See Exhibit #1 – Application
Materials) located at 88 E. 11000 S. The applicant is also requesting a setback waiver to
build approximately eight feet into the required east side setback.

The proposed structure will be 50 feet wide and 45 feet long (2,250 square feet) and
located on the southeast side of the existing home. The proposed use of the structure is
for a trailer, boat, and passenger vehicles. The proposed steel panel accessory structure
will have ash gray walls, a charcoal gray roof, and white doors and trim. (See Exhibit #2–
Site Plan and Construction Drawings)

The property is zoned R-1-40A and is not listed as part of a subdivision. The property is
located on the south side of 11000 S. between State St. and 115 E. The property is
currently calculated at approximately .4 acres (17,424) square feet. A survey adjustment
of the property is waiting to be recorded at the county that will put the lot at .54 acres
(22,400 square feet). The property is surrounded by single family homes zoned R-1-40A
on the west and south, and R-1-10 on the east. The property to the north is a church zoned

ANALYSIS
Section 21-11-2-(a)(2)(a)(2) of the Sandy City Development Code states that no single
accessory structure shall exceed 1,500 square feet, unless the Planning Commission
approves a larger size through the Conditional Use Permit process. The proposed
accessory structure is 2,250 square feet requiring Planning Commission approval.

Section 21-11-2-2(a)(2)(d) of the Sandy City Development Code states the total
maximum square footage of all accessory structures on the property may be increased up
to 25% larger than the permitted size upon receipt of a Conditional Use Permit from the
Sandy City Planning Commission. For any properties over 40,000 square feet, or with an
"A" designation, the total maximum square footage of all accessory structures on the
property may be increased up to 50% larger than the permitted size through a Conditional
Use Permit. The Planning Commission shall consider the scale of the buildings in relation
to the immediate surroundings, the nature of the zone and land uses in the immediate
vicinity, architectural design, landscaping, access, proposed use, impact upon adjacent
properties, in addition to other criteria normally considered during the Conditional Use
Permit process. The Planning Commission may require additional setback from side and
rear property lines as a condition of approval.

The permitted size of an accessory structure is determined by the square footage of the
property. The property in question is 22,400 square feet which is permitted to construct
an accessory structure up to 1,500 square feet according to the Sandy City Development
Code (Section 21-11-2(a)(2)(a)). Because this property is within the R-1-40A zoning
district, the applicant can request up to 50% larger (maximum of 2,250 square feet).

The 2,250 square footage is the maximum square footage allowable for all accessory
structures on the lot. There is currently a detached garage (approximately 625 square
foot) on the lot. The applicant is aware that this structure must be removed prior to
completion of the proposed accessory structure.

Section 21-11-2-(a)(3)(c) of the Sandy City Development Code states that an accessory
structure may be built taller, up to the maximum building height for a permitted dwelling
within the zone in which it is located, upon receipt of a Conditional Use Permit from the
Sandy City Planning Commission. The Planning Commission shall consider the scale of
the building in relation to the immediate surroundings, the nature of the zone and land
uses in the immediate vicinity, architectural design, landscaping access, proposed use,
impact upon adjacent properties, in addition to other criteria normally considered during
the Conditional Use Permit process. The Planning Commission may require additional

setback from side and rear property lines as a condition of approval. The proposed
structure is in an R-1-40A zone allowing the structure to be up to 20 feet high in the rear
yard (Section 21-11-2-(a)(3)(a)). The applicant is requesting that the Planning
Commission approve the accessory structure to be 24' 9" feet in height.

Additional Setback Requirement. Detached structures exceeding 15 feet in height shall
increase the minimum setback one foot for each foot of additional height up to the
minimum setback for the primary dwelling (Section 21-11-2-(a)(3)(b)). A setback of 12
feet from both the side and rear yard property lines would be required for the requested
height. The applicant is proposing a 20 foot setback in the rear yard and is requesting a
setback waiver from the Planning Commission to allow for a four foot setback from the
east property line.

Ancillary to Main Dwelling. Accessory buildings are only allowed on properties where a
main dwelling or building exists. According to the Sandy City Land Development Code
an ancillary use is defined as a use conducted on the same lot as the primary use of the
structure to which it is related; a use which is clearly incidental to and customarily found
in connection with such primary use (Section 21-37-2(20)). The main dwelling unit is
approximately 2,073 square feet while the proposed structure is 2,250 square feet. The
proposed structure sits well within the rear yard but is larger than the existing main
dwelling unit.

Architectural Guidelines Generally, accessory structures and buildings shall be designed
and constructed as to be compatible with the architectural components of the main
dwelling or building.

The proposed garage is constructed out of gray metal with white trim steel panels. The
main dwelling unit is constructed from red brick with black shingles. The applicant is
aware of the architectural differences but plans on replacing the main dwelling unit with
one that is more architecturally compatible with the proposed garage in the future.

Section 21-24-4 of the Sandy City Development Code states that all areas utilized for the
parking of vehicles shall be paved with a hard surface (e.g. concrete, asphalt, brick, or other
water impenetrable surface). This includes the front yard, side yard, and rear yard of the
home. The exceptions are as follows:

    1). A six-inch gravel base complying with City specifications may be used for the
    storage of recreational vehicles. This area must be kept weed free.

    2.) Single-family residential properties with animal rights will be allowed to store
    animal transport trailers. These trailers are not required to be parked on a gravel or
    hard surface, but must be parked within the rear setback of the home.

    3). Passenger vehicles used for daily transportation located at a residence that existed
    prior to hard surface parking requirements (concrete or asphalt) are exempt from this
    requirement.

CONCERNS
Staffs concerns about the request are related to the proposed square footage, architectural
material, setback waiver, removal of existing garage and property size.

    2. The proposed material for the accessory structure is steel panels. Of the structures
    that are a similar size to the proposed structure only one is metal. Staff is
    concerned that the architecture of the proposed structure may not match that of
    the surrounding area. Typically, metal buildings have been allowed in an "A"
    designated zone when used for an outdoor animal domicile.

    3. Staff is concerned about the proposed setback waiver. Setbacks increase with
    height to provide a buffer between the accessory structure and neighboring
    properties. Staff is concerned that waiving the setback will increase the visual
    impact of the structure to neighboring properties.

    4. According to Salt Lake County data the property is 17,400 square feet. It is Staffs
    understanding that the applicant has taken steps to have the property recorded at
    22,400 but it is not currently reflected in the county data.

    5. Staff would like to ensure that the applicant remove the existing garage on the
    property. Staff is recommending that the structure be removed within one year
    after possible issuance of the CUP. Staff has prepared an affidavit for the
    applicant to sign to ensure compliance with Section 21-11-02 of the Sandy City
    Land Development Code (See Exhibit #4 – Affidavit). The affidavit sets the time
    frame for removal of the structures to be on or before March 5, 2021. If the
    Planning Commission is not comfortable with this time frame, a different time
    period can be stipulated in the affidavit. This affidavit will be recorded at the Salt
    Lake County Recorder's office.

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for
Cody Wright for the property located at 88 E. 11000 S. to allow for an accessory structure
as described in the application materials based on the finding and subject to the following
conditions.
