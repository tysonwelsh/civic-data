4.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for the Traverse Mountain
Area Plan Amendment, changing the land use designation from Open Space to Public
Facility on approximately 5.2 acres located at approximately 5700 North Flight Park Road.
    Commissioner Hemmert excused himself from the meeting for the evening and
    Commissioner Nielsen took over. Commissioner Eyre voted in place of Commissioner
    Hemmert.
Jacob presented info on the agenda item.

The applicant was present. Jared Morgan with George Washington Capital was present
representing Geneva Rock.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

Rob Ludlow was present and respectfully asked for the item to be tabled so the City can get a
proposal and financial aspects of the project.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale, Brad Kenison and Joel Eves provided information on the item.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker motioned to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
    for the Traverse Mountain Area Plan Amendment, changing the land use designation
    from Open Space to Public Facility on approximately 5.2 acres located at
    approximately 5700 North Flight Park Road; including all DRC comments and findings
    stated in the report, encouraging that negotiations continue on financial aspects and the
    applicant be prepared for City Council. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Eyre, no; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson,
    no; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
    Commissioner Hereth, no. The motion passed 4 – 3.

4.3 Public hearing and recommendation of Fieldstone Homes’ request for the Traverse
Mountain Area Plan Amendment eliminating a maximum block length.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Mr. Loveland, was present.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

Rob Ludlow discussed the Master Plan changes and Amendments since 2012. He stated that he
is not in favor of the general plan amendment and it is the developer’s responsibility to adhere to
the Master Plan. He discussed park and open space and the location where it will meet at the
Traverse Mountain Trails and that the developer needs to follow through with their
commitments. He stated he feels this is an economically driven decision.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Mr. Loveland responded to the public comment that was given. The Commission and Staff
discussed the item.

    Motion: Commissioner Peterson motioned to recommend approval of Fieldstone Homes’
    request for the Traverse Mountain Area Plan Amendment eliminating a maximum

    block length; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
    Bunker seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Eyre, no; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson,
    yes; Commissioner Ellis, no; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
    Commissioner Hereth, no. The motion passed 4 – 3.
