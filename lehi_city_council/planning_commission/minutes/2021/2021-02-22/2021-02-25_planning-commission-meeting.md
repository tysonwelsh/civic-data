2.3) Public hearing and consideration of Thomas Pannell’s request for approval of the
Caliber Collision Concept Plan, an automobile service and repair business located at 1746
North Boston Street.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Trisha Fielding, was present and gave a short presentation. Commissioner
Hemmert asked about vehicle repairs and where they would take place. The applicant stated they
would take place inside of the building. Commissioner Peterson asked about the exception and
where the building was located. The applicant stated the grade had a steep slope and that is why
the exception was asked. Commissioner Nielsen asked about the fence and the applicant said
they are planning on the standard vinyl fence.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

Wesley Gurney had concerns for Boston Street. His property is North and across from Boston
Street by Boyd Browns project. There is a four foot drop, and he has no access to his property
now. Mike West stated all grading must be done on the applicant property and they will make
sure that the adjacent property owner still has access. Commissioner Nielsen stated he wanted to
make sure the buffering requirements were taken into consideration as well.

An online comment was also submitted by Mr. Reynolds where is addressed previous concerns.

Commissioner Hemmert asked if they were looking at a conditional use or a site plan. Staff
clarified that it was a conditional use and then the site plan. Ross Dinsdale stated the road on the
west side of the development, Boston Street, has not yet been designed and the final
development they will need to be reviewed for final plans and that Staff will look at Boston
Street for final review before approval.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

Commissioner Peterson stated the access is something he will be looking for and the buffering
between the development and residential. Commissioner Nielsen asked if the development was
required to install sidewalks. Staff stated that would be required with the subdivision application.

    Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to send a positive recommendation of Thomas
    Pannell’s request for approval of the Caliber Collision Concept Plan, an automobile
    service and repair business located at 1746 North Boston Street; noting that Boston
    Street needs to be designed to have access and the internal use of the property will be
    designed in a way that will limit traffic in the residential neighborhood, as well as the
    buffering requirements; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
    Commissioner Hemmert seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Peterson, Yes; Commissioner Hemmert, Yes; Commissioner Nielsen,
    Yes; Commissioner Ellis, Yes; Commissioner Bunker, Yes; Commissioner Everett, Yes;
    Commissioner Hereth, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.4) Public hearing and consideration of Yesco’s request for approval of a conditional use
permit for a pylon sign for Ken Garff located at 2302 North Thanksgiving Way.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant is requesting several exceptions to the sign.

Commissioner Hemmert asked if there was a light trespass study that has been done. Staff stated
that there had not been one done since it does not abut next to residential. Commissioner Everett
asked if the other pylon signs that have been approved remain. Staff clarified the amount of
measurable lighting that is allowed. Commissioner Nielsen clarified they are compiling the three
properties signs into one.

The applicant, Brent Godfrey with Ken Garff, was present. He clarified on the amount of signage
they were asking for. They would like to continue with directional signage. Commissioner
Everett asked about the height of the signs and if it will do any good. The applicant stated the
overpass restricts the view of the sign.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing for comment.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing for comment.

The Commission agree that the overpass creates a unique barrier of sight for the sign.

    Motion: Commissioner Hemmert moved to send a positive recommendation of Yesco’s
    request for approval of a conditional use permit for a pylon sign for Ken Garff located
    at 2302 North Thanksgiving Way; noting as a finding of fact that this is a unique parcel
    before UDOT buried the developer, on the condition that no other pylon signs be approved
    on all three parcels, that a light test be completed to show that it meets the guidelines within
    the code; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
    Peterson seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Peterson, Yes; Commissioner Hemmert, Yes; Commissioner Nielsen,
    No; Commissioner Ellis, Yes; Commissioner Bunker, Yes; Commissioner Everett, No;
    Commissioner Hereth, No. The motion passed 4 – 3.
