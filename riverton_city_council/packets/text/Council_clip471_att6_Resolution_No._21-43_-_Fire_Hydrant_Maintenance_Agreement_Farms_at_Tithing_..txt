                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 21-43

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A FIRE HYDRANT
      TESTING AGREEMENT WITH THE TITHING HILL HOMEOWNERS
                         ASSOCIATION.


       WHEREAS, The Tithing Hill Homeowners Association (“Association”) is responsible for
maintaining private infrastructure and common areas located within the Farms at Tithing Hill
Subdivisions, Phases I, II, and III; and

       WHEREAS, The Association has recently identified a need for assistance in exercising and
maintaining its fire hydrants which provide fire protection within the aforementioned Farms at
Tithing Hill Subdivisions; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it has the resources and
skills among the City’s workforce to provide maintenance to the aforementioned fire hydrants, in the
form of exercising the same and reporting any issues to the Association; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Attorney has negotiated an agreement with the Association to
establish the terms and conditions in which the City may provide these services to the Association,


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the governing body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The attached Fire Hydrant Testing Agreement with the Association to provide fire
          hydrant testing services to the Association is hereby approved.

       2. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, the proposed Agreement
          with the Association, upon verification that the Agreement has been approved as to legal
          form by the City Attorney.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 21st day of
September, 2021 by the following vote:
