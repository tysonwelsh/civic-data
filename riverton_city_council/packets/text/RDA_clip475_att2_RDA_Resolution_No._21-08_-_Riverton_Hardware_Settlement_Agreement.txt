                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RDA RESOLUTION NO. 21-08


        A RESOLUTION APPROVING A SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT FOR A
        CONTRACT DISPUTE BETWEEN THE REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
              OF RIVERTON CITY AND RIVERTON HARDWARE.
       WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code § 17C-1-202 (1), the Redevelopment Agency of
Riverton City (“RDA”) may:

       (a) sue and be sued;
       (b) enter into contracts generally;
       (c) buy, obtain an option upon, acquire by gift, or otherwise acquire any interest in
       real or personal property;
       (d) hold, sell, convey, grant, gift, or otherwise dispose of any interest in real or
       personal property;
       (e) own, hold, maintain, utilize, manage, or operate real or personal property, which
       may include the use of agency funds or the collection of revenue;
       …
       (i) if disposing of or leasing land, retain controls or establish restrictions and
       covenants running with the land consistent with the project area plan;


      WHEREAS, on or about May 6, 2014, Riverton Hardware, through its owners, Ross and
Neal Griffiths, entered into an Agreement for Development of Land (“ADL”) with the RDA; and
        WHEREAS, the ADL provided, among other things, that Riverton Hardware make
improvements to the site location for its hardware store, in consideration for receipt of remnant
parcels of land owned by RDA (Identified by Parcel Nos. 27-34-201-049 and 27-34-201-029); and
      WHEREAS, after making some improvements to said site location, a dispute arose
between RDA and Riverton Hardware over what remained to be completed to comply with the
ADL; and
        WHEREAS, the RDA Board finds and determines that retaining residual title to parcels
in response to holding a dispute with the Hardware Store has the unintended effect of prohibiting
redevelopment within the RDA Project Area; and
       WHEREAS, the parties have engaged in extensive negotiations to resolve this matter; and
        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 63-30d-602, the RDA Board of Directors is
empowered to “compromise and settle any action” against the City, after conferring with the City’s
legal counsel; and
        WHEREAS, RDA and its attorney have thoroughly considered the ramifications of
maintaining a dispute with Riverton Hardware, and balanced the same against the merits of
resolving said dispute by agreeing to the Proposed Settlement Agreement attached hereto and
incorporated herein as Exhibit “A”;
       NOW THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Redevelopment Agency of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The RDA Board Chair is hereby authorized to take steps necessary to resolve the dispute
between RDA and Riverton Hardware so as to avoid the costs and risk associated with litigating
this matter. Accordingly, a Settlement Agreement by and between RDA and Riverton Hardware
attached hereto as Exhibit “A” is hereby approved. The Mayor is hereby authorized to execute the
Settlement Agreement upon verification that same has been approved as to legal form.
        2. RDA staff and legal counsel are further authorized to take and complete all necessary
actions to carry out the directives contained in this resolution.
      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
Agency this 5th day of October, 2021 by the following vote:
