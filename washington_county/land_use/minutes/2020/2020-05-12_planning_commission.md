Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                                 May 12, 2020

The regular meeting of the Washington County Planning Commission was conducted via
electronic means through Zoom, on Tuesday, May 12, 2020 from the Commission Chambers in
the Washington County Administration Building, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah.
Chairman Kevin Jones attending by Zoom, opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. Vice Chairman
Brandon Anderson led the Pledge of Allegiance. He stated he understood there were several people
via Zoom and they may not be able to see the video, nor would they be heard until they have the
floor. He then turned the meeting back over to Chairman Jones.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate), Shayne Durrant, Reed Scow
(Alternate acting as a voting member), Doug Solstad, and Jon Walter. Chairman Jones and
Commissioner Slade Hughes attended the meeting via Zoom.

Excused: Mark Owen.

County Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Natalie Nelson, Deputy
Attorney; Eric Clark, County Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant;
Cheyenne Bentley, Deputy Clerk at host location. Todd Edwards, Engineer; Ron Whitehead,
Public Works Director; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Kyle Layton, Building Inspector.

Chairman Jones thanked Commissioner Anderson and then explained how the meeting would be
conducted. He reminded the participants that although this is a public meeting, it would only be
open for public comments when declared so. There will not be a public hearing for the conditional
use permit application, which was Item 1. The agenda items that are public hearings will allow for
public comments. He explained the protocol for the public hearing portions.

(1) (a) A county may adopt a land use ordinance that includes conditional uses and provisions

The applicant has met the public noticing requirements, which he explained.

It is no secret that Washington County has been discovered. Over the last decade, the number of
people moving to the region as well as the number of visitors from all over the world coming to the
region has skyrocketed. With this growth, has come a demand for diverse outdoor experiences.
Those coming to Washington County seek a variety of sleeping accommodations. The
unprecedented number of hotel rooms that came online with a boom in hotel construction in the last
several years has helped meet the demand for such accommodations. As Washington County
representatives followed national trends of campgrounds, tiny cabins, and glamping facilities, it was
determined that the county should proactively address such facilities in county code. Among the
code amendments, was the creation of a term “rural recreation grounds and facilities”.

Washington County Code: 10-4-1: Terms Defined:
RURAL RECREATION GROUNDS AND FACILITIES: Facilities for use by owner, operating
organization, members and/or paying or nonpaying guest. Facilities may include amenities such as
cabins, lodges, reception centers, social halls, campgrounds, swimming pools, tennis and pickle ball
courts, golf courses, petting zoos, fishing ponds, recreational vehicle pads, and/or temporary
structures or enclosures such as tents, park models, yurts or other facilities designed for short-term
stays. Facilities may include single-family dwellings if the dwellings are under the same ownership
or operating organization.
