JOB INFORMATION

TITLE:                   City Manager                               FLSA: E
DEPARTMENT:
REPORTS TO:              Governing Body
EFFECTIVE DATE:          12/2017

JOB PURPOSE: Under the direction of the governing body of the City, performs professional
managerial duties; directs and coordinates administration of government operations in accordance with
policies determined by the governing body. Appoints department heads and staff as provided by state
laws or local ordinances. Supervises activities, such as collection and disbursement of taxes, law
enforcement, maintenance of public health, construction of public works and purchases of supplies and
equipment. Oversees and may direct community and economic development programs. Assists with
preparation of annual budget and submits estimates to authorized elected officials for approval.

ESSENTIAL JOB FUNCTIONS:
List the job's essential or most important functions and responsibilities.

1. In coordination with Finance Director, prepare an annual budget using criteria of strategic plan to
    present to Mayor to start budgeting process.
2. Exercise general control and supervision of administrative activities of the City.
3. Prepare plans and programs concerning development, operation, and needs of the City.
4. Set performance standards and controls to ensure efficient and effective operations.
5. Provide general oversight of all financial decisions of the City.
6. In coordination with Finance Director, responsible for financial statements.
7. Oversee hiring and discharging of all employees, except those appointed by statute.
8. Review all proposed contracts and make recommendations to the Governing Body.
9. Ensure appropriate procurement practices are established and followed.
10. Exercise all powers and duties assigned by ordinance or resolution.
11. Develop, maintain, and manage a team of professionals to achieve goals and objectives as outlined
    in the strategic plan.
12. Other responsibilities, as assigned.
