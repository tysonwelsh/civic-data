2)
Discussion and recommendation for the Lower Village Development Area Master Plat LV1B and LV5 Amended Final Subdivision Plat, located on parcels PP-102-C-2-C, PP-102-B-12, PP-102-B-3-A, PP-102-B-3 and PP-PW-1-610-A - 11.42 acres, Canyons Village. Applicant, Spencer White. Administrative Review. Project # 24-001.
Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior Planner. (6:01 P.M.)
Tiffanie N. Robinson,
Senior Planner,
presented a plat amendment for the Lower Village Subdivision and recommended forwarding a favorable recommendation to the County Manager.
Commissioners provided comments for discussion and asked clarifying questions to which Planner Robinson and Applicant, Spencer White, responded.
Christopher Conabee made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Manager for the Lower Village Development Area Master Plat Amendment LV1B and LV5 Final Subdivision Plat based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval as mentioned in the staff report. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).

3)
Public hearing and action regarding a Plat Amendment proposing to combine Lots 22 and 23 of the Park City Business Center Subdivision, located at 4462 & 4428 Forestdale Drive, Parcel #PCBC-22 & PCBC-23 (0.78 Acres and 1.18 Acres); Applicant, Connor Roberts Administrative Review. Project #23-139.
Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planner. (6:42 P.M.)
Laura Kuhrmeyer,
County Planner
, presented a proposal to combine Lots 22 and 23 in the Park City Business Center into one lot with shared parking.
Commissioners asked brief clarifying questions.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:47 p.m.
Steve Stanton was called to speak at 6:47 p.m.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:55 p.m.
Commissioners provided comments and asked additional questions to which Planner Kuhrmeyer and Director Putt responded.
John Kucera made a motion to approve the Park City Business Center Subdivision Plat A Lots 22 and 23 amended based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Tyann Mooney seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
