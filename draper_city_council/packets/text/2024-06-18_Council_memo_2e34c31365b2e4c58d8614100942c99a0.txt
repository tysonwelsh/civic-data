MEMO
To: City Council
From: David Dobbins, City Manager
Date: 2024-06-18
Re: Approval of Resolution #24-28


Comments:

The City Council created the city manager position and delegated powers, duties,
responsibilities and authority to the person serving in that position. David Dobbins has
submitted his resignation effective June 28, 2024. This resolution appoints Mike Barker as
the Acting City Manager starting June 28, 2024, and continuing until the City Council
appoints a permanent city manager. Mr. Barker will exercise the authority and fulfill the
duties of the position according to state law and Draper ordinances.
