Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson expressed appreciation to the Public Works staff. The
                    spring bulk waste clean up went very well. He acknowledged the passing of Bruce
                    Blanchard. We had a great deal of respect for him.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham acknowledged Bruce Blanchard for his untiring work and
                    ability to help people understand the City budget.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman also spoke of Bruce Blanchard. He was always so
                    engaged and collaborative and we appreciated his analysis and input. She thanked
                    department staff for their help with residents' concerns. She also expressed a desire to
                    move forward with a code of conduct.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud spoke about her interactions with Bruce Blanchard. She
                    always appreciated his input and recommendations. He will be missed. She attended the
                    public utilities spring fair and provided an update.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa spoke about Bruce Blanchard. He was a great teacher
                    and helped her to understand the city budget. She also provided an update from an
                    Envision Utah meeting she attended.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey spoke of Bruce Blanchard and said that he will be
                    missed. People like Bruce make us better and ensure that we are prepared. She spoke
                    about upcoming ribbon cuttings and acknowledged the South Valley Chamber for their
                    work on behalf of Sandy City.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski spoke about Bruce Blanchard. She valued his input. He was recently
                    awarded the One Sandy Award for Empowerment and Belonging. We all benefited from
                    his involvement. She attended the Fair Housing Summit and provided an update. She
                    wished the Director of the Sandy Senior Center well in his retirement and invited the
                    community to the first movies in the park event.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, mentioned that the city will be implementing Workday financial
                    software in the new fiscal year and provided some updates.

Adjournment


                    Council unanimously adjourned the meeting at 7:15 pm.
