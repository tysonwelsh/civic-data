46       a. Ordinance No. 20-25 - Amending Section 18.90.010 Table of Commercial Uses, and
47          18.85.030(12) Permitted Uses (in the C-R Zone), Regarding Distance Limitation for
48          Certain Uses
49
50   Council member McCay recused herself from the following discussion and vote:
51
     Page | 5 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     October 16, 2020



 1   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that Riverton City’s Table of
 2   Commercial Uses Zone, and the Commercial Downtown, Gateway, and Regional zones,
 3   currently included language limiting convenience stores with gas stations and standalone gas
 4   stations to a maximum of two within 1,000 feet. He said that Staff has brought up the question of
 5   whether this restriction is one the City wishes to maintain in its Code, as development patterns
 6   with gas station uses in the City has not brought this issue to light for many years. He said the
 7   language was apparently originally adopted in part to address concerns with 12600 South and
 8   1300 West, where for a time there was the potential for three service stations at that
 9   intersection. He said that, as gasoline service stations are much more difficult to convert to other
10   uses or retrofit, one likely concern at the time this language was originally adopted was avoiding
11   what may become unstainable competition resulting in vacant and difficult to redevelop
12   properties.
13
14   Mr. Lethbridge said that the Planning Commission, in reviewing the existing language, voted to
15   recommend maintaining the limitation as it currently stands. However, he said Staff included an
16   alternative amendment that would keep the maximum of two within 1,000 feet unless stations
17   were separated by a major roadway, one of at least seven lanes. He said this would limit
18   situations where more than two stations could exist within 1,000 feet to areas where traffic
19   volumes could make multiple stations more sustainable, and also where the separation across
20   such a large right-of-way could make the uses less impactful to one another.
21
22   Mayor Staggs opened a Public Hearing and called for public comment; there being none, he
23   declared the Public Hearing closed.
24
25   Council Member Stewart said the proposed ordinance affected his district and there was more
26   need than he originally thought, especially traveling west. He said his concern was regarding
27   preservation of the downtown area; however, he supported the recommended motion.
28
29   Brief comments were given by City Attorney Ryan Carter and other Councilmembers in support
30   of the following motion:
31
32   Councilmember Stewart MOVED that the City Council adopt Ordinance No. 20-25 -
33   Amending Section 18.90.010, and 18.85.030(12) Distance Regulations on Gasoline
34   Service Stations, a max of two within 1,000 feet unless separated by a roadway of at least
35   seven lanes. Councilmember Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for
36   discussion on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as
37   follows: Councilmembers Buroker-Yes, McCay-Recused, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and
38   Wells-Yes. The motion passed.
