                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-69

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SURPLUS AND PURCHASE OF TWO POLICE
                           VEHICLES

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, the police chief would like to keep two Chevy Traverse police vehicles that
are going to be rotated out now and use those vehicles to replace two older police vehicles that
was received from UPD; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no
longer needed as surplus in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the keeping of these two vehicles is the most cost-effective way of
purchasing line cars; and

       WHEREAS, by approving this agreement, Council will need to approve $44,364.00 as
an expense and $24,000.00 as an incoming revenue in a future budget amendment.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the surplus of two police vehicles, as identified and the
            purchase of two police vehicles that are scheduled to be rotated out.
            Surplus:
            2017 Toyota Camry, VIN #4T1BF1FKXHU382642
            2016 Ford Explorer, VIN #1FM5K8D88GGC08414

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 5th day of
September 2023 by the following vote:
