7. Department or Committee Reports
a. Children’s Justice Center
Discussion: Terryl Warner presented on the new Children’s Justice Center and what new services the center now can
provide and the new artwork that is presented there.

b.  Clerk/Auditor’s Office
Discussion: County Clerk/Auditor Jess Bradfield presented on the new improvements being made in his office, which includes a
mass digitizing of all records for greater public accessibility. Bradfield also explained that the county is assisting municipal
elections within Cache County. Bradfield presented on the completions of two performance audits. Bradfield explained the
audits of the County Recorder's Office and Executive's Office were a “general overview” and looked at compliance with state
code. Two attorneys went in and asked if the offices were following each item required by state law. The audit reports highlight

1


what changes were recommended, and do not include the spreadsheet checklist of statute compliance. Council members
questioned the finding which recommended moving the office of Surveyor from the office of County Executive. Currently the
Surveyor work is contracted out. Auditor Bradfield said the recommendation was based on the potential for waste, fraud and
abuse. Council members asked if any waste, fraud or abuse had been found. Bradfield said no. Council member Gunnell
commented that waste, fraud and abuse isn’t limited to contractors, but can also be committed by elected officials and that
separating out the office would not necessarily prevent waste, fraud and abuse. Council members asked if any cost comparisons
had been done between in-house and contracting the survey work. Bradfield said no comparisons had been done.

The Council also questioned why a recommendation was included in the audit to change the name of the County Executive to
County Mayor. Bradfield explained that it was his office's opinion and based on a soft poll, not a scientific poll, that no one knew
what a County Executive was.

Council members Karl Ward and Gina H. Worthen emphasized that the County Executive is different from a Mayor. The
Executive does not have the same functions and oversight as a mayor.

Council member Erickson expressed concern that it appeared the results of the audit were predetermined. Auditor Bradfield
assured Erickson that the two attorneys conducting the audit went in open minded.

The Council also expressed concern that audits are black and white, and not opinion-based. Council member Ward also said if
something is broken, then it needs to be pointed out specifically why and what the consequences have been when making
recommendations for a fix.

Request for Chief Deputy Recorder Position DENIED

Discussion: County Recorder Devron Andersen presented on his need of a highly qualified Chief Deputy to assist with a 66%
increase in workload. Andersen stated that the county has advertised the position three times and only 1 highly qualified
candidate applied. That individual declined the job offer, citing a low wage for an experienced employee. Andersen asked for
approval to raise the starting pay above the currently allowable .95 compensation ratio. Councilmembers Erickson, Gunnell and
Borup expressed concern about raising the pay and the possibility of creating a precedent. They desired the office to contract the
work instead of hiring a long-term Chief Deputy at an increased wage.

Action: Motion made by Karl Ward to approve the request to waive the normal hiring procedures and raise the offer by $14,000
and to allow 40 PT front loading under the condition. Also contract immediate qualified help, not overlooking the former county
recorder, and get help from GIS; Seconded Barbara Tidwell

Action: Motion made by David Erickson to amend the main motion and strike “waive the normal hiring procedures and raise the
offer by $14,000 and to allow 40 PT front loading,” leaving only “contract immediately or get temporary qualified help, not
overlooking the former county recorder, and get help from GIS,” seconded by Paul Borup.

Motion to amend fails

Aye: 3 Nolan Gunnell, David Erickson, Paul R. Borup
Nay: 3 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward

Absent: 1 Gordon A. Zilles

The Council then considered the original motion.
