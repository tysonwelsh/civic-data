                                         Nephi City
                                   Work Session Minutes
                                       June 14, 2022
                                          7:00 p.m.
                                Nephi City Council Chambers

1. Several members of the Ute Stamped Committee discussed with the council the details of
   the Mammoth Parade. They asked if Nephi City obtains UDOT permits for both the
   Heritage and the Mammoth Parade. Mr. Atkinson replied that she applies for permits for
   both parades. Committee member Chris Memmott expressed the committee’s
   appreciation to the Nephi Power Department for their help with the celebration. Nephi
   City has agreed to sponsor two banners in the rodeo arena, one for Nephi Power and one
   for Nephi Natural Gas.

2. Devan Shields and Jaison Hardman representing Sunrise Engineering presented a draft of
   the Wastewater Master Plan. The plan outlines a series of short- and long-term projects to
   upgrade and improve the city’s sewer infrastructure. The plan assumed a growth rate of
   3.1% through 2041. The projects will inform a rate plan that will be presented to the
   council later. Mr. Atkinson explained that some projects may have alternatives that could
   be explored. Also, some pipes may not need to be replaced but we will not be able to
   determine this until the lines have been observed through a video line feed. In addition,
   the hypersonic line testing that was completed a couple of years ago can be done
   periodically to determine where there are problem areas, and these can be put on an
   enhanced maintenance schedule. One of the main projects will be to add a new primary
   cell to the existing sewer ponds. It will give added capacity to the sewer system and allow
   for new growth. . Councilor Worwood stated that he would like the possibility of a sewer
   lagoon being built on the south end of the city examined.

3. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the Hive agreement between Nephi City and Juab School ?District.
   One of the main negotiating points was the facility use schedule. Councilor Ostler asked if
   there would be fees charge to use the facility. The fee schedule has yet to be determined.
   The summer use schedule has not been worked out yet and will be added as an addendum
   later in the year. Councilor Memmott and Mayor Seely have reviewed the agreement with
   the leadership of the Juab School District Board and have ironed out many issues.
   Councilor Worwood stated that he felt confident with the parties that have reviewed the
   agreement. A question of allowing the facility to be rented out on Sundays had been
   asked. No definite answer to that question has been established. Mayor Pro Tempore
   Memmott suggested that the council review in detail the agreement over the next few
   weeks before final approval is voted on.

4. Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that some property boundary lines need to be
   cleaned up on the Greenhalgh Property. The council directed staff to bring back a Quit
   Claim Deed for council approval to clean up the boundary line issues.
5. Mr. Atkinson presented a Concept Zoning Map for the growth area around Nephi City. City
   Planner Shauna Talbot, Public Works Director Kyle Marchant, along with himself and the
   Planning Commission had been working on the map. The finalized map will be included in
   the updated general plan. The council discussed maybe adding a zone that only allows ½
   acre lots.

6. Mr. Atkinson reviewed a draft of an ordinance that would amends Title 11, Chapter 3,
   Section 2 of the Nephi code that would allow some subdivision exceptions.

7. Mr. Atkinson reviewed a zone change request for the Northridge development. The
   developer would like to rezone to highway commercial along the frontage of Highway 132
   (Main Street) for a couple of commercial lots he wants to subdivide off, one on the north of
   1500 N. and one on the south and rezone a strip to the East of that as R-3 (Residential) with
   a possible PUD overlay zone; and a strip along the south of 1500 North to the R-3
   (Residential) with a possible PUD overlay zone. A discussion ensued about the zone
   request. The Council agreed to asking the developer if he would enter into a development
   agreement stating that he would not divert from the conceptual plan he has presented that
   shows five banks of 8 condominiums style units like Hunter Village. The council asked if
   the Planning Commission could begin working on a new zone that would only allow
   condominiums and townhomes within a zone.

8. Mr. Atkinson reminded the council that they had directed the Planning Commission to
   examine the possibility of allowing chickens in the non-animal rights residential areas. Staff
   had looked at ordinances from other cities and settled on a ordinance from Springville City
   as a template. He presented a draft ordinance allowing chickens in the single-family
   residential zone based on a lot size. It prohibited roosters and commercial sales of eggs. It
   required hens to be in an enclosure but also an enclosed fenced area of the back or side
   yard. Council Worwood stated firmly that he was against the ordinance. Councilor
   Memmott and Councilor Oster both agreed with Councilor Worwood. After some
   discussion the council directed staff to not bring the chicken ordinance back for a vote.

9. Mr. Atkinson reviewed some budget amendments that would be brought before the
   council at next regular council meeting.

10. Mr. Atkinson presented the draft of a real estate exchange with the Uta Division of Wildlife
    Resources. The council directed staff to continue working through the agreement for the
    land swap for expansion of the golf course.

11. Mr. Atkinson reported that Mr. Bob Garrett had approached the City about helping pay for
    a gate and fence to protect the highway from grazing cows. Mr. Garrett stated that Juab
    County was willing to donate a cattle guard and UDOT was willing to help with some
    expenses. Mr. Atkinson recommended that the city allow this project but to not help fund
    the project. The council agreed.
12. The council directed Mr. Atkinson to make a job offer to the applicant that the hiring
    committee recommends after conducting interviews. Golf Director Mike Booth is wanting
    to speed up the process because he needs the help as soon as possible.

13. Councilor Larry Ostler agreed to be on the hiring committee for the Recreation/Pool
    Coordinator position. The job closing will be June 22, 2022.

14. Meeting Adjourned at 9:44 pm. No executive session was convened.
