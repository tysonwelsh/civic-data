D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: My thoughts go back to our economy and where it sits today.
We are in uncertain territory with the stock market and interest rates. I looked at my friend Warren Buffet to see what
he has to say about these times. He has done a great job on investing and good job on philosophy on investment. “The
business schools reward difficult, complex behavior more than simple behavior but simple behavior is most effective.
There seems to be some perverse human characteristic that likes to make things difficult.” You don’t have to be a
genius to achieve what you want to do. Let’s not panic. We have seen tough times before and we will get through it.
