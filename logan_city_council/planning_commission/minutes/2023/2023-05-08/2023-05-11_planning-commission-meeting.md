PC 23-027 Old Mann Lofts [Design Review Permit] Tony Johnson/Old Mann Holdings LLC is
requesting a design review permit to convert the existing event center into twenty-two residential
units on the second floor with the lower floor remaining commercial, located at 185 Cache Valley
Blvd. in the Commercial (COM) zone; TIN 05-014-0077 (Adams Neighborhood).

STAFF: Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director reviewed the request for a design review
permit to convert the existing event center located at the corner of 200 E and Cache Valley Blvd, in
the Commercial! zone to residential uses. The existing building contains retail spaces on the main
floor and event space on the second floor. The proposal is to add 22 units that are a mix of studio
and one-bedroom. The applicant is also proposing a rooftop patio with pickleball courts. There are
no changes proposed to the exterior of the building.

Commissioner Peterson asked if the open space percentage includes the rooftop patio area because
the proposal refers to the rooftop patio as an intention and not part of the plan.

PROPONENT: Tony Johnson said that all of the second-floor units will be a mix between micro units
and flex space. Each unit will include a Murphy Bed so they can be used as a bedroom and/or office
space. The garden area on the east side of the building was added to meet the open space
requirement for the building.

Commissioner Peterson asked if the building will ail be under one utility. T. Johnson said they are
working through the utilities to determine if the building will need to be metered or sub-metered.

PUBLIC: Jeannie Simmonds asked what a micro-unit is, if there is a minimum square footage
requirement for residential units, and if the open space requirement is met without the rooftop patio.
M. DeSimone said that the minimum size for a residential unit in the Building Code is 110 square
feet which the proposed units exceed. These smaller housing choices will provide more housing
supply that will meet a wider variety of housing demand.

COMMISSION: M. DeSimone said that the building has already met open space requirements when
it was originally built, reconfiguring the inside does not change the open space requirement.
Commissioner Lucero asked if short-term rentals would be allowed in these units. M. DeSimone
said that short-term rentals are allowed in the commercial zone.

Commissioner Doutre said this is a good multi-use project at a good location.
