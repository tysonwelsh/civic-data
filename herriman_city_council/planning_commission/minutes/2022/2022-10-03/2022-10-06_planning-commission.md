The following are the minutes of the Herriman Planning Commission meeting held on Thursday,
October 6, 2022, at 6:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City
Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Planning Commission, media, and
interested citizens.
Presiding: Chair Chris Berbert
Commissioners Present: Andy Powell, Heather Garcia, Brody Rypien, Jackson Ferguson, Alternate
Forest Sickles, and Alternate Darryl Fenn
Commissioners Excused: Adam Jacobson, Andrea Bradford
Staff Present: Communications Specialist Destiny Skinner, Planning Manager Clint Spencer, Assistant
City Attorney Matt Brooks, City Attorney Todd Sheeran, Deputy Recorder Wendy Thorpe, Planner |
Talia Wright, Planner ll Sheldon Howa, Engineer Josh Petersen, Community Development Director Blake
Thomas, and HPD Operations Commander Brent Adamson.
1. 6:00 PM - Work Meeting (Fort Herriman Conference Room)
Chair Berbert called the meeting to order at 6:01 p.m.
1.1. Review of City Council Decisions
Planning Manager Spencer reported no land use decisions were approved. The Teton commercial
development was reviewed in the work meeting and would be up for consideration in October. The
Council discussed concerns with the Auto Mall Special District (AMSD) in the work meeting and issues
regarding uses still required more discussion.
1.2. Review of Agenda Items — Michael Maloy, City Planner
Planner Howa reported no one attended the community meeting for item 4.2. Commissioner Garcia
expressed concern about the reduction of trees for item 4.3, and Commissioner Fenn agreed. Planning
Manager Spencer responded the development contained one hundred percent live growth and the
terrain was not suited to trees. Landscape Architect Shegrud approved the tree reduction.
Commissioners debated ideal landscaping options for the area, tree sizes and number of trees. Staff
