Principal Planner Ray Milliner and Planner Mustapha Osman led a Work session on Chapter 5 (Economic Development and Agriculture) and Chapter 6 (Natural and Cultural Resources) of the Eastern Summit County General Plan. (6:42 PM)
Commissioners raised questions and comments seeking clarification on key objectives. They questioned whether development responsibility falls on the County, the developer, or both, and what that means for smaller local entities. The group emphasized the importance of providing support at all levels and agreed on the need to clearly define the scope of the discussion. Commissioners also explored what tools are available to deliver sidewalks, curbs, and gutters, noting that cities typically provide infrastructure that the County cannot, and that the County’s rural identity shapes what is feasible. On economic development, discussion centered on the tension between promoting a livable wage and supporting small businesses, how businesses operate in a rural setting, and the challenges posed by mixed-use businesses, tech, and restaurants all within an economy heavily dependent on construction. Regarding zoning, Commissioners noted that progress would require updates to the use table and the creation of new business zones to accommodate mixed-use development. For Chapter 6, the focus was on revising Section 6.1’s Process Objective and clarifying strategy and process, along with a review of migration patterns. Finally, the group discussed using the natural environment to coordinate trails and bike paths, and agreed on the need to add language protecting public land, aquifers, and open space, and more, with a focus on quality of life. Planner Milliner and Planner Osman responded. (6:47 PM)
