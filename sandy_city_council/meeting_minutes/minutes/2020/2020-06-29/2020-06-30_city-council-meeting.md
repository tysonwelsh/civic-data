Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Zoltanski asked about pool hours at Alta Canyon. She asked the
                                  Council Meetings be broadcast on the City Facebook page.

                                  Council Member Robinson noted that he is participating with a weak internet connection.

Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn spoke about the pool hours at Alta Canyon and the difficulty of
                                  expanding hours during pandemic uncertainty. Parks Director Scott Earl provided
                                  additional detail.

                                  Mayor Bradburn spoke about a "mask event" tomorrow at Rio Tinto Stadium.

CAO Report

                                  Chief Administrative Officer Matt Huish asked that staff be allowed to brief the Council as
                                  follows:

                                  Park Director Scott Earl spoke about strong revenue at River Oaks Golf Course. He also
                                  noted efforts to create virtual recreation opportunities. Lastly, the Bell Canyon
                                  Preservation Trailhead was bid out but additional funding will be required. A presentation
                                  to the Council will be forthcoming.

                                  Fire Chief Bruce Cline updated the Council on the Lehi and Saratoga fires. He also spoke
                                  about fireworks signage and extra patrols.

                                  Mearle Marsh reported on the Youth Showcase that was just completed. There were 54
                                  participants.
