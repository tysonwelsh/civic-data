2.  Public hearing and possible action regarding a 30-lot Master Planned 
Development (MPD) with a remainder parcel outside of the platted subdivision; 
located off of Cherry Canyon Road; Parcel NS-227-230-G; Brett Hollberg representing 
Trail Ridge Partners LLC and LRH Inc., Applicant. – Amir Caus, ACIP, County Planner 
 
Planner Caus shared the background for this application. The applicant has requested to 
subdivide a 337-acre parcel and a ten-acre parcel. All properties constitute one conforming 
parcel. The proposal is to have 248 acres as permanently deed restricted open space. The 
property has historically been used for cattle grazing and recreation. The applicant is also

Findings of Fact:  
1. The Promontory Development Agreement was approved on January 2, 2001.  
2. The Promontory Development Agreement provided for 885 estate lots as part of a base 
density, 716 incentive density lots, two hotels/300 resort units, equestrian facility, up to five golf 
club houses, and other accessory uses on approximately 6500 acres with no less than 3900 acres 
remaining as passive open space.  
3. Of the 1901 total units in the density pool, 1,339 units have been approved.  
4. Promontory Investments LLC is the owner of record of Parcel SS-25 (410.70 acres).  
5. The applicant is proposing a Final Subdivision Plat for 18 units and is legally described as 
Promontory Clubhouse Villas at Painted Valley Subdivision.  
6. The density is established by the Promontory Development Agreement pool of density.  
7. The applicant is proposing to use density from Resort Unit pool of entitlements for the 
Promontory Clubhouse Villas at Painted Valley Subdivision.  
8. Staff has received approvals from the Promontory Conservancy and Architectural Review 
Committee for the proposal.  
9. Public notice of the public hearing was published in The Summit County News.  
10. Postcard notices announcing the public hearing were mailed to property owners within 1,000 
feet of the Parcel SS-25.  
11. Service providers have reviewed the plats for compliance with applicable standards and no 
project issues have been identified that could not be mitigated.  
12. Staff has reviewed the proposed plats for compliance with applicable Development Code 
standards.  
13. Staff has reviewed the proposed plats for compliance with the Promontory Development 
Agreement standards.

4.  Discussion/Possible action on potential amendments to Section 11-6 of the 
Eastern Summit County Development Code. The purpose of the amendments is to 
provide permitting regulation for both commercial and private campgrounds in 
Eastern Summit County. – Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this discussion. Over the past few months 
there have been different versions of Section 11-6 discussed. It has become clear that 
having a Commercial Campground is not getting to the concerns being expressed, so the 
Commercial Campground has been removed from this section of Code and will be included 
in the RC (Recreation Commercial) Zone. Private campground definition has been 
streamlined to be consistent with the Health Department. Anything pertaining to 
Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
February 4, 2021 
Page 9 of 11 
 
commercial activity has been removed. There are two exemptions to the seasonal use of an 
RV. One exemption allows a person to park their RV and leave it for a season and the other 
allows multiple vehicles for no more than one week. 
Definitions were discussed. There are a lot of people permanently living in RVs on a lot but 
are not hooked up properly in many cases. Manufactured homes have now replaced mobile 
homes on the Use Table and RVs will no longer be allowed to be used as a permanent home. 
Those already in use will be grandfathered in. 
Commissioner Clyde stated that there needs to be a cap on the number of trailers that can 
be stored on a lot. Do we need to note the difference in the size of lots? Should more 
hookups be allowed for this use? Commissioner Clyde stated he is troubled with 
manufactured housing being an allowed use in any zone. They belong in a mobile home 
park. Dumping requirements should be included for RVs. Commissioner Sonntag stated 
that if a neighborhood wants to prevent manufactured home use, then it should be included 
in their CC&Rs. Planner Milliner stated that manufactured homes are a source of 
affordable housing. 
Commissioner Wheaton stated a lot of people spoke during the Public Hearing saying that 
Samak Hills is a platted subdivision. How will enforcement work in that area? Planner 
Milliner answered that enforcement will be done on a complaint basis. Seasonal RV 
parking would be allowed. Storage would be addressed through a Junk Ordinance with one 
unregistered vehicle being allowed on the property and being stored out of sight. 
Chair Sargent stated the Planning Commission would like to see another draft before any 
action is taken.
