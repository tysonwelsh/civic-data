5, TO APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF A RESOLUTION HONORING THE
SACRIFICES OF UTAHNS AND FIRST RESPONDERS ON THE TWENTIETH
ANNIVERSARY OF THE SEPTEMBER 11TH TERRORIST ATTACKS AND INVITING LOCAL
FIRE AND POLICE DEPARTMENTS TO JOIN IN A SOLEMN DISPLAY OF SOLIDARITY

-THOMAS V. SAKIEVICH, Commission

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH - THIS RESOLUTION HONORS THE SACRIFICES OF
UTAHNS AND FIRST RESPONDERS ON THE 207 ANNIVERSARY OF THE
SEPTEMBER 11° TERRORIST ATTACKS AND INVITING LOCAL FIRE AND POLICE
DEPARTMENTS TO JOIN A DAY OF SOLEMN DISPLAY OF SOLIDARITY MEMORIZING
THE TRAGIC EVENTS OF THAT DAY. IT WAS A DAY THAT THAT WORLD STOPPED.
NOTHING LIKE THIS HAD HAPPENED SINCE THE ATTACK ON PEARL HARBOR IN
DECEMBER OF 1941 AND IT’S THE DAY WHEN POLITICAL PARTY AFFILIATION
SEEMED TO HAVE DISAPPEARED AND THE COUNTRY WAS UNUSUALLY UNITED.

THIS RESOLUTION THAT WE HAVE BEFORE US TODAY IS A MEMORY TO THEM TO
THAT EVENT BUT PRINCIPALLY TO OUR FIRST RESPONDERS WHICH ARE THE
POLICE, SHERIFFS, FIREFIGHTERS, EMERGENCY MEDICAL TECHNICIANS AND
MANY UNSUNG PEOPLE THAT WE DON’T SEE ON A DAILY BASIS.

VIOLENT TERRORIST ATTACKS LEADING TO THE TRAGIC DEATHS OF 2,977 AND

FROM MORE THAN 90 DIFFERENT NATIONS AND TERRITORIES;

WHEREAS, IN EXTRAORDINARY RESPONSE TO THE ATTACKS IN NEW YORK CITY,
WASHINGTON, DC, AND SHANKSVILLE, PENNSYLVANIA, COUNTLESS
