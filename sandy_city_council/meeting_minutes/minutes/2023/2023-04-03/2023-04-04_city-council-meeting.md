Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledeged Leslie in Animal Services for her efforts 
                    to help a Sandy resident. He is working on coordinating a town hall event for Alta Canyon 
                    Recreation Center for May 8th. All are invited.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey acknowledged the public works staff and all they do for 
                    our city - snow plowing, bulk waste clean up. They are doing an awesome job!

                    Council Member Alison Stroud updated the Council on the flag design committee. More 
                    feedback will be gathered from residents on design option A. She also provided an update 
                    from the recent National League of Cities and Towns conference she attended in 
                    Washington DC. She and Council Member D'Sosa met with our lobbyist, Bill Simmons 
                    and Representative Curtis and Owens regarding issues our city is facing. It was an 
                    extremely beneficial meeting.

                    Council Member D'Sousa echo'd Ms. Stroud's comments. The conference was a great 
                    way to interact and connect with our federal representatives. Sandy Youth Council 
                    applications are available on the website. The SYC is open to all high school age youth in 
                    9th through 12th grades.


Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski thanked Council Member D'Sousa and Stroud for meeting with our 
                    Federal Representatives in Washington DC while they were at the conference. She 
                    acknowledged the Public Works crews for all their hard work over this past winter and 
                    she thanked the police officers for their work at Little Cottonwood Canyon. On May 1st 
                    the City will hold a town hall event to discuss flood mitigation and preparation. All are 
                    encouraged to attend.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO spoke about the city's response and flood preparation. He spoke 
                    about the communication and outreach to residents. He also provided an update on the 
                    city's efforts to repair the potholes on city streets. 

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:23 pm.
