40.4 acres

Connor Sheppard with Wasatch Acquisitions & Capital, Inc. has submitted a General Plan/zone
change application for three parcels totaling 40.4 acres that are located at 2736 and 2856 S
Corporate Park Dr and 5035 W Lake Park Blvd. The property is currently zoned R-1-8 (Single
Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) and A-2 (Agriculture, minimum
lot size 2 acres) with a General Plan designation of Medium Density Residential (7 to 12
units/acre) and Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre). The proposed zone is MXD (Mixed
Use) and the proposed General Plan designation is Mixed Use.

Surrounding zones include A-2 (Agriculture, minimum lot size 2 acres) to the north, BRP
(Business/Research Park) to the east, R-1-8 to the south, and M (Manufacturing) and R-1-8 to
the west. Surrounding land uses include vacant land to the north, Lake Park Corporate Center
and Monticello Academy to the east, single family homes to the south; and Neil Armstrong
Academy and single family homes to the west. The property is currently vacant.

Development Proposal

The applicant is proposing a mixed use development that would include:
     a deep-water wave generating pool or surf pool with associated amenities open to the
       public for a fee;
     32 single family detached homes;
     a 7-story, 55-unit for-sale condo/hotel building to accommodate overnight stays for
       customers at the surf pool;
     approximately 40,000 square feet of commercial space within the hotel building to
       accommodate surf pool customers;
     32 for-sale townhomes in a gated community;
     56 for-rent townhomes;
     280 stacked flat apartments;
     a community clubhouse and community pool for those living on-site; and
     competition style pickleball courts, a dog park, and playground available to the public.

The proposed density of the 14-acre single family detached area is 2.3 units/acre. The density of
the remaining 26.4 acres is 13.9 units/acre without the condo/hotel building and 16 units/acre
with the condo/hotel building included.

Property History

A development agreement was approved with Ivory Development on the north 26.4 acres of this
property as well as other properties back in 2008. The development agreement, which is included
for reference, called for 69 townhomes and 89 parkside homes on the subject property. The
parkside homes were intended to be alley-loaded homes on small lots. The approved density in
the development agreement was 6 units/acre. Ivory Development completed development on all
of the properties addressed in the development agreement except the subject property.

Issues to Consider

In reviewing this application, staff suggests that there are two primary questions to answer:

   1. Is this the right location for a mixed use development?

   2. Assuming the location is appropriate, does the quality of the project further City goals?

These two questions are explored below.

Is this the right location for a mixed use development?

Since the MXD zone has only been used once in the City, staff believes a brief explanation of the
MXD zone is warranted here. Unlike other zones, the MXD zone has very few standards spelled
out in the ordinance. For example, there are no setbacks, height restrictions, or maximum
building coverages. The lack of standards was intentional to allow for design flexibility for
applicants who could essentially propose their own standards through a development agreement.
Another unique aspect of the MXD zone is that all allowed uses within the zone, except for
public utility installations, are reviewed as permitted uses and not conditional uses. That means
that the site plan approval process will be handled only by staff. These unique aspects of the
MXD zone make detailed development agreements for developments within the MXD zone
essential in staff’s view.

CA-2-2023
Ken Garff Truck Center Phase II
4770 S 5600 W
M Zone (12.37 acres)

The applicant, Daniel Goodwin with Anderson Wahlen & Associates, on behalf of Ken Garff
Automotive Group, is requesting a conditional use amendment for a truck processing center.
The overall property is 36.53 acres and is zoned M, Manufacturing. The West Valley City
General Plan designates this area as light manufacturing. The surrounding zoning is M to the
north, west, and south. The properties to the east of 5600 West are in Kearns and include an
industrial property and single unit dwellings. Other surrounding land uses include The Ridge
Golf Course to the south and west, a power substation to the west and self-storage and a
convenience store to the north.

The property was originally approved for an office and production facility for the Newspaper
Agency Corporation (C-6-2004), which includes a 205,444 sq. ft. building. The Ken Garff
Automotive group now operates a truck processing center from the property. Trucks will come
to the property unfinished, and they will be customized to meet buyers’ needs. The installation
of lift kits, tires, wheels, and accessories will also be done. All work will be performed in the
building.

In June of 2021 Ken Garff received conditional use approval to operate the truck center from
the existing building and parking lot on the north end of the site (C-12-2021). Then, in August
of 2021, the Planning Commission granted a conditional use amendment for the second phase
of the project, which was to create a large outside storage area for trucks on the south and west
sides of the building (CA-6-2021). The project did not move forward, and the approval expired
last August. The applicant is now ready to start the second phase of the project and is
requesting the amendment for outside storage again.

The expansion into the undeveloped areas of the site will impact 12.37 acres. The new truck
storage areas will be paved with asphalt and will accommodate over 1,000 trucks. The area will
only be used for the storage of truck inventory. There shall be no inoperable or junk vehicles
stored on the property. The code requires that outside storage be screened from public view.
There was also an update to the City Code that happened since the original approval, which
requires that outside storage areas adjacent to high image arterial streets be screened with a 6’
tall masonry wall (7-7-119(4)). The code change does, however, give the Planning Commission
the option to waive the screening requirement when changes in topography or distance of
storage from rights-of-way would make the screening not effective. Both 5600 West and 4700
South are high image arterial streets. In the prior approval, due to the existing topography and
distance from the street, the Planning Commission did not require any additional screening for
the storage area. The Commission can waive the masonry wall requirement in this instance for
the same reasons.

The applicant is proposing to add an area behind the asphalt that will function as a drainage
collection area and will also be landscaped with trees, shrubs, and ornamental grasses. The
areas surrounding the new pavement and landscaping will be left as existing, native vegetation.
All other formal landscaped areas on the property will remain, which meets the 5% minimum
landscaping requirement for the M Zone. There is an area in the south-east corner of the
property that is overgrown. The section within the 20’ setback from the sidewalk shall be
cleaned out and formally landscaped with 50% live plant material that does not exceed 3’ in
height. The native/natural vegetation beyond the 20’ setback, in all other areas of the site, shall
be mowed and maintained. The applicant intends to install a berm along the edges of the site to
create a buffer from the golf course. Any related issues shall be coordinated with the Parks
Department.

Additional signage is not proposed on the property with this application. Any future signage
shall comply with Title 11 of the West Valley City Code.

C-13-2023
High Mountain Forest Products Storage Yard
2061 W 2300 S (Printers Row)
M Zone (2.51 acres)

The applicant, Porter McDonough with Sage Construction Group, is requesting conditional use
approval for outside storage. The overall property is 2.51 acres and is zoned M, Manufacturing.
The West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light manufacturing. The
surrounding zoning is M also. Manufacturing and light industrial land uses surround the
property.

The site was originally developed in 1971 as a machine shop and manufacturing facility (ZC
19710187). The current business supplies hardwood lumber to commercial millwork shops
around the valley. They are requesting an outside storage space to store the company’s excess
truck trailers. The property owner recently acquired a 0.25-acre parcel south of their existing
property that used to be part of the Brighton Canal, before it was abandoned. The applicant
requested to use this area behind the building for the outside storage space. The long-term goal
is to do a building addition that will occupy a large portion of the area, but in the meantime the
business would use the area for storage. The plan calls for clearing the vegetation and putting
down and compacting road base. The area will be fenced. The applicant is also working with
the Public Works Department, as there is a City storm drain project being planned through the
area.

Per 7-7-119, the Planning Commission evaluates screening and surfacing for outside storage
uses. The proposed storage area is over 400 feet from the street. Besides the distance from the
road, it is tucked behind structures and a masonry wall that is up-front. Since the area will not
be used for vehicle storage, and given the distance from the public street, the proposed road
base should satisfy the surfacing requirement.

The total property area for the business with the additional 0.25 acre parcel is 109,336 sq. ft.
Manufacturing zones require 5% landscaping. The existing landscaping along the front of the
property meets the minimum landscaping requirement. There is a small area on the north-west
corner of the building that needs to be cleaned up, but other than that, the landscaping is well-
maintained. Since the applicant will not be adding any landscaping around the storage area,
staff recommends that trees be added along the street, providing 1 tree for every 30 feet of
frontage.

Additional signage is not proposed on the property with this application. Any future signage
shall comply with Title 11 of the West Valley City Code.
