 BACKGROUND

Mr. Mark Garza is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Crewe 2 Lot Subdivision. The
subject property currently has an existing dwelling that will be removed in lieu of two new single-
family dwellings. The property is bordered on the north, east and south by existing residential
uses along 5450 West and the Erin Hills PUD to the west.

Fire Department:

Granger Hunter Improvement District:

               Project will need to run availability for water, sewer and fire protection.

               Subject to design and review inspections.

Utility Agencies:

               Subject to all standard easement locations.

Public Works:

               Authorization required of ditch/water users for any abandonment, relocation,
                piping or any other modifications to existing ditches or irrigation structures.

               Will require a delay agreement for improvements along 5450 West. A discussion
                will be had with the City Council to determine the vision and improvements for this
                street. The delay agreement is subject to change.
               Revisions to the plat required.

               Will need to coordinate subdivision name with County Auditor's Office.

               Soils report is required.
Building Inspections:

             Follow recommendations outlined in the soils report.


ISSUES:

      The applicant is the developer of the Erin Hills PUD to the west and the owner of the lots
       proposed in this application. The subdivision will consist of 2 lots on .42 acres. Although
       zoned R-1-8, both lots are approximately 9,250 square feet. During the rezoning process
       of the Erin Hills PUD to the west, the developer requested an exception to the frontage for
       these two lots. An exception was granted by the Planning Commission that each lot would
       be approved with 66 feet of frontage.

      In addition to the frontage allowance, the developer is able to build homes in this
       subdivision in accordance with Section 7-11-214 of the City Code. This section allows for
       new homes to be built with a minimum square footage of 1400 square feet for a ramble and
       1600 square feet for a two story. In addition, each home is allowed to be built with a 2-car
       garage in accordance with this provision.

      Access to the subdivision will be gained from 5450 West. At the present time, this street
       is dedicated and improved with asphalt only. Although there are a few sections with curb,
       gutter and sidewalk, the majority of this street is void of those improvements. Staff has
       discussed the long-term vision of this right-of-way with the City Engineering Division.
       The Public Works Department is planning on a discussion with the City Council to
       determine if formal improvements should be required along this road. For the present time,
       the City will require a delay agreement for such improvements, but that could change based
       on the discussion with the City Council. For this application, we are moving forward with
       a delay agreement.

      As no new single-family development has occurred near this property in quite some time,
       staff is unsure of any groundwater impacts. As part of the subdivision process, a soils
       report will need to be submitted. The report will need to address soil characteristics and
       ground water if any. This report will be reviewed by the City Engineering Division and
       Building Official.

      Property to the north and south is zoned R-1-8 and will not require any special fencing.
       Property to the west, as already mentioned, is part of the Erin Hills PUD. They will be
       installing a 6-foot pre-cast masonry wall along their east boundary - west side of this
       subdivision.

      As mentioned earlier, an existing single-family dwelling is currently located on the
       property.
       A staff understands this home was used for a training exercise by the West Valley City
       Police Department. The home has broken windows and could be seen as an attraction for
       unwelcome activity. It is staff’s opinion that this home be demolished as soon as possible
      to prevent problems that occur with abandoned buildings.

6765 West 3500 South

BACKGROUND

Mr. Greg Larsen is requesting preliminary approval for a residential community to be known as
the Cottages at Pearce Farm. The subject property was rezoned in May 2021 from the A zone to
the RM zone. The subject property is bordered on the east by existing single-family zoning, the
south by property zoned agriculture, the west by 6800 West and the north by 3500 South


STAFF/AGENCY COMMENTS:

Public Works Department:

      Authorization required of ditch/water users for any abandonment, relocation, piping or any
       other modifications to existing ditches or irrigation structures.

      Contact Salt Lake County Auditor’s Office for approval of street names and subdivision
       names.

      Dedication and improvements required along 3500 South and 6800 West as described in
       the development agreement.

      Ensure that grade difference at property lines are minimalized.

      Revisions to plat required.
      Flood control, drainage, and other issues associated with 6800 West be coordinated with
       Public Works.

Utility Companies:

      Standard Utility Easements required.

Granger Hunter Improvement District:

      Project is subject to all requirements and design standards of the water district.

Fire Department:

      Proposed fire hydrant locations need to be shown on subdivision plat.

      Project shall meet all provisions of Fire Code relating to this type of development.

Building Division:

      Soils report will be required.


ISSUES:

Project Overview:

       The Cottages at Pearce Farm is a residential community for senior living which will consist
       of 65 units on 10 acres. This equates to an overall density of 6.5 units per acre. A club
       house is also proposed for the site and will be located toward the center of the project.

       Residential units will be one story in height. Based on the development agreement, 25%
       of all units will have a bonus room above the garage. The developer is proposing units
       with a minimum size of 1500 square feet. All units will have a two-car garage of
       approximately 400 square feet and a drive space in front of the garage for parking.

       Building architecture is virtually the same that was shown to the Planning Commission
       during the rezone application but has been refined. Materials will be stone/masonry
       products and fiber cement. All units will have a private patio that is accessible from the
       dining/living room area within the dwelling. The developer is proposing
       craftsman/bungalow architecture with each having multiple elevations. The developer has
       also proposed multiple colors schemes to be used throughout the project to add variety.

       As mentioned previously, all units will have a 2-car garage with parking in front of the
       garage. In addition to the garage and driveway spaces, the developer is providing guest
       parking in and through the project as well. At the present time, the site plan is showing a
       total of 273 parking spaces which equates to 4.2 spaces per unit.

Other topics related to this project:

Access:
       Access to the subdivision will be gained from 3500 South and from 6800 West. The
       developer will be coordinating access and improvements along 3500 South with UDOT.
       A main entrance will be gained from this access and will have a landscaped median as you
       pull into the development. In addition, signage identifying the project will also be located
       at this point.
      A second access point will be gained from 6800 West. According to the development
      agreement, the developer will be piping the center running ditch, burying the power lines,
      installing curb, gutter and sidewalk along the east side of 6800 West and installing 24-feet
      of pavement on the east side of the street. The improvements along this right-of-way will
      extend out to 3500 South.

       The project itself has a looped street system which will satisfy the fire department for
       access and maneuverability. The right-of-way width for the internal street will be 26 feet
       of asphalt. A sidewalk is located along the interior portion of the private road with various
       crossings. Per City policy, gutters are not allowed to be counted towards the asphalt width.
       Staff will coordinate this with the City Engineering Division and developer.

Building Setbacks:

       Setbacks for this project are illustrated on the site plan. According to the RM zoning
       standards, the perimeter setbacks are required to be 20 feet. According to the site plan,
       setbacks along the perimeter of the development range in size from 20-30 feet. As the site
       continues to be reviewed and refined, setbacks may change, but a minimum of 20 feet is
       required. For those units that may have a loft and are taller than 25 feet, which is unlikely,
       but possible, an additional setback would be required for those buildings if located along
       the perimeter boundary of the subdivision.

Site Amenities:

       The developer is proposing a number of amenities for this project. At the present time, the
       applicant is proposing a club house of approximately 2,800 square feet with a kitchen,
       fitness center and gathering area, and either a pool or pickleball court will be constructed.
       A walking path around the perimeter of the development is also proposed. Although not
       an amenity listed in the development agreement, each unit will have a private courtyard or
       patio space with access from the dwelling as well.
Fencing:

      During the rezoning process, the developer agreed to install a 6-foot masonry wall as you
      enter the project and vinyl siding around the remaining sides. The design of the wall and
      fence has yet to be selected but will be evaluated during the final plat application.

Grading:

      The site slopes downward from south to north a vertical distance of 25 feet. Although the
      site looks relatively flat, there is a good amount of slope over a distance of 700 feet.
      Although the slope is gradual, staff does have some concern that adjacent properties are
      not impacted by any grade differential at property line. To help lessen this problem, the
      developer will be installing a retaining wall along the south boundary of the subdivision.
      The engineering division will review the existing grade and proposed grading plan to
      ensure that water runoff will not be a problem for adjacent properties. Staff understands
      that some elevation change is to be expected for drainage and sewer requirements, but
      staff’s expectation is that the site will have a similar grade as it does today when finished.
      Measures shall be taken to eliminate significant grade differences at any property line and
      fill shall be limited to help with this potential problem.

Landscaping:

      While landscaping is generally addressed at the final plat stage, staff would like to mention
      a few things at this point in the review process. The overall open space requirement is
      50%. During the rezone, the developer received a reduction in the open space requirement
      to 48%. Staff will work with the developer to define what is needed for the project
      landscaping as this moves through the subdivision process. A landscaping and irrigation
      plan will need to be submitted prior to final plat review.

      As part of this project, a main entry will be gained from 3500 South. The developer plans
      on landscaping this area with a median and landscaping on both sides of the entry. A
      monument sign will be located in this area. Staff will work with the applicant to provide a
      landscape plan for all areas of the project including the entrance to the subdivision.

Lighting:

      The project site will require streetlights along 3500 South and 6800 West consistent with
      current City lighting standards. Internal lighting will also be required. The developer can
      choose to use similar lighting, but on a smaller scale, or choose a different light to fit the
      project. The developer is still deciding how to light the sidewalk areas and open space
      areas within the project. An overall lighting plan will be provided during the final plat
      application.
Garbage Collection:

      The development has been designed with a good street pattern. The developer will need
      to coordinate with a waste management company for the collection of garbage. Due to the
      length of some drive courts, residents may need to place their garbage cans along the
      private street for this purpose.

CA-7-21
Malone Powersports Expansion
1331 West 3300 South
M Zone (2.14 Acres total/Exp .86 Acres)

The applicant, Malone Powersports, is requesting a conditional use for expansion of a
recreational vehicle facility at 1331 West 3300 South. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing.
The West Valley City General Plan designates this area as General Commercial. The
surrounding zone is C-2 to the north and east and M to the west and south. The surrounding uses
include Jones Heating to the north, Questar Gas and GHID facilities to the east and south, and an
additional Malone Powersports facility to the west.

This property is located within the Jordan River Overlay Zone (JROZ). The use is considered
“Motorized Outdoor Recreation Equipment” and is currently prohibited in this overlay zone.
However, per 7-14-203, existing businesses may continue and can expand with approval by the
Planning Commission.

This property was originally approved for redevelopment and expansion for Highland Golf in
2019 (C-15-19). This approval included demolition of an existing building on site and
construction of a new building that is currently being built. The 3500 South Streetscape is also
being updated to comply with City Code. The rear portion of the site was to remain gravel for
golf cart storage. At this time Malone Powersports would like to expand further and improve the
south portion of the lot as well. This area will be hard surfaced and used for inventory storage.

Upon approval of the use expansion there will also be some updated plan submittals required.
There will be some modifications to the overall grading and drainage for the site. The internal
design of the building was also modified to remove the second story mezzanine so updated
building plans will be required. The site landscaping has been increased from 9% to 14% (5%
min is required) so updated landscaping plans will also be reviewed.

There is currently a multi-tenant pole sign on the property that was used for Highland Golf and
Plaza Cycle. Both of these uses no longer occupy the site and this is now a single tenant user so
the pole sign will be removed.

There is a canal on the west side of this site that will remain, and no internal connection shall be
provided between this lot and the adjacent Malone Powersports lots. Any unimproved areas shall
be maintained free of weeds.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:
           1. The existing business, Malone Powersports, may expand per the approved plans.

           2. An updated landscaping plan must be submitted with a minimum of 5% of
              landscaping on site. This includes the 3500 South streetscape improvements set
              forth in 7-10.

           3. The back storage area must be hard surfaced and maintained free of weeds.

           4. There shall be no storage or use on unimproved areas.

           5. All customer and employee parking and maneuvering areas must be hard
              surfaced.

           6. All fencing shall be maintained, and only approved fencing materials shall be
              used.

           7. Signage shall meet the standards set forth in Title 11 of the WVC Municipal
              Code.

           8. Any dumpsters provided on site must meet the requirements set forth in 7-2-118.

           9. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

           10. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

C-18-2021
Kim Plaza
1778-1816 West 3600 South
C-2 Zone (1.23 Acres)

The applicant, Greg Steffensen Architect, is requesting conditional use approval for a retail
shopping center building at 1778-1816 West 3600 South. This property is zoned C-2, General
Commercial, and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as General Commercial.
The adjacent property to the north is zoned A, Agricultural and C2, General Commercial with
the remaining areas C-2. The surrounding uses include auto service and single unit residential
development to the north with similar office/retail uses to the west (Kim Heang Market), a Sonic
restaurant to the east, State Trailer Supply and Ray Hour Chiropractor to the south.

This site is Lots 14-16 of the Plaza 3600 Business Park which was approved by the Planning
Commission in 1998 (C-26-98). Access to the site is gained off of 3600 South, which is a private
road. The original approval specified that each development must be reviewed as a separate
conditional use before the Planning Commission since the tenants were not known at the time of
the overall approval, furthermore this particular use is also listed as a conditional use in the C-2
zone.

The proposed development for the site is a 7-unit multi-tenant building with a mix of retail and
restaurant uses. The proposed building will be approximately 47,465 sq. ft. There shall be no
outside storage. The parking for the site has been calculated for the proposed mix of uses,
including two potential restaurant tenants. The tenant mix and parking counts shall be monitored
as the units are developed to maintain the required parking.

The building will be constructed out of masonry block with a mixture of metal panels, wood
veneer, concrete panels, stucco and metal architectural awnings. The building design must
comply with the Commercial Design Standards. The north side of the building will be partially
visible to the public due to the location of the adjacent residential buildings and a stub street
(Shafer Lane) so staff recommends that the visible portions also comply with the secondary
façade requirements in the Commercial Design Standards.

The landscaping on site must meet the 15% minimum area as required in the C-2 Zone. The
landscape plan indicates 15% has been provided. The landscaping/setbacks along the frontage of
this parcel were approved as part of the original overall shopping center landscaping concept.
This includes a minimum setback for the building of 40’ from the property line, which is the
center of the private road, as well as a 7-foot landscaped setback for the parking to provide an
area for street trees that were originally planted when the roadway was improved. The site will
also have a 5’ strip of landscaping adjacent to the parking lot on the east and west sides and a 10’
wide landscaped area to the north. These areas must all include 50% live plant material and trees.

Since the property is partially adjacent to residential a 6’ tall solid masonry wall is required.
There is an existing unfinished masonry block wall that will be painted/refinished to match the
building. A similar masonry enclosure will also be constructed around the dumpster located on
the site.

A lighting plan has been provided. Staff will work with the applicant to ensure that the site
complies with the standards set forth in the West Valley City Code including the light levels
adjacent to residential uses. The applicant has indicated that the wall mounted lighting on the
north side of the building are security lights so they will not be on at all times and there will be
shields installed to direct the light away from the adjacent property.

Only wall signage is proposed for this site. All signage shall comply with the West Valley City
Sign Ordinance, to include no more than 15% signage on the front building face, and 5% on the
remaining sides, and no more than 50% of the window area shall be covered in signs. A building
permit must be issued for all signage. Ground signage has not been proposed at this time.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval of the conditional use for a shopping center subject to compliance with all of the
City’s zoning ordinances and the following:


   1. The landscaping must be completed per the approved site plan and in accordance with
      applicable standards in the West Valley City Municipal Code including but not limited to
      a minimum 40’ front building setback measured from the front property line and a 7’
      wide landscaped area along 3600 West with trees per C-26-98.

   2. The building must be designed in accordance with the Commercial Design Standards
      which shall include secondary façade treatments on the visible portions of the north side
      of the building.

   3. There must be adequate parking on site for the proposed tenant mix.

   4. A 6’ masonry wall shall be required along all residential zone boundaries. The wall shall
      not be unfinished concrete block and must be painted or finished to match the adjacent
      building.

   5. The dumpster location shall be at least 20’ from any residential boundary and completely
      secured with a 6’ tall masonry enclosure.

   6. All lighting shall be designed per Title 7 and not negatively impact adjacent residential
      uses.

   7. All signage must comply with the West Valley City Sign Ordinance.

   8. Must meet requirements of all affected departments and agencies and is subject to review
      upon valid complaint.
