RECOMMENDATION:

Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 20-31 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Electric Vehicle Charging Stations and GRAMA requests.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs. The fees being proposed for the Electric Vehicle Charging Stations is a new user fee that is being
adopted. The installation of the new charging stations are complete and ready to use. The new proposed fees
for GRAMA Requests will align the current Draper City fees schedule with the state code. Only the items or
sections that are proposed for changes are listed in Exhibit A. Items highlighted in blue are the new proposed
fees or description.
