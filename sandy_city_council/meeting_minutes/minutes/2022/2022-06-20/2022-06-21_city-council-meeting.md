2.         22-231                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Marci Houseman invited the public to participate in General Citizen 
                                  Comment.

                                  Ms. Nicole Lewis expressed her support for Alta Canyon Recreation Center. She grew up 
                                  going to Alta Canyon Recreation Center and thinks the center provides a safe space for 
                                  the community,  and jobs for the staff. The center provides a sense of community. 

                                  Ms. Pat Jones expressed the need for the council to reach consensus on potential 
                                  property tax increases. She mentioned that many campaigned as fiscal conservatives 
                                  and she was against increasing property taxes. She expressed concerns with the 
                                  reasoning to rebuild Alta Canyon Recreation Center and thinks the funds should be 
                                  allocated to the Public Works facility and the Fire Station.

                                  Ms. Alison Holley is a member of the Alta Canyon community and expressed support for 
                                  the facility. It is one of the things that Sandy does best. She thinks funding the ACRC is 
                                  money well spent.

                                  Mr. Dave Ross was happy to be here for the Flag Ceremony and swearing in. He felt he 
                                  was in the presence of real heroes. He expressed concerns over the deer problem in 
                                  Sandy. He has called Animal Control on the issue and was told that nothing can be done. 
                                  One deer jumped his fence and almost killed their dog and then advanced towards him. 
                                  He has spoken to the Division of Wildlife Services and would like the City to become 
                                  more involved in handling the deer issues in Sandy.

                                  Mr. Anders Reber spoke in support of Alta Canyon Recreation Center and offered an 
                                  option for the pool area.

                                  Ms. Shauna Rohbock has been a resident of Sandy City for 37 years and was a member 
                                  of the ACRC Board of Directors. She expressed a desire for the Council to come together 
                                  on this issue and make a decision. People love it there and the price has doubled since 
                                  2008 and will continue to go higher. She urged the Council to come together on this and 
                                  acknowledged their service to the City.

                                  Ms. Lynnette Hunt been in Sandy for 30 years and has always loved going to Alta 
                                  Canyon Recreation Center. She is supportive of moving forward with one of the options for 
                                  ACRC. She expressed the importance of physical exercise and the benefits it has on 
                                  overall health. She thinks it would be nice to have an new facility for the city.

                                  Ms. April Barton ask that the Council be conservative. Many family budgets are tightening 
                                  and any tax increase would be hard on the residents on a fixed income. Not everyone 
                                  benefits or uses the facility and she asked the Council to put needs before the wants.

                                  Mr. Larry Graft expressed concerns with the rising inflation and spoke about the 
                                  similarities to the Jimmy Carter era. He asked the Council to cut back expenses as 
                                  much as possible. 

                                  Ms. Susan Foster watched the current Alta Canyon Recreation center being built. She 
                                  lives in the special district and uses the center. She thinks it is a wonderful facility that is 
                                  worth saving and keeping and she spoke about the great staff that work and teach at the 
                                  center.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson gave a shout out to the Information & Technology team 
                    for their efforts. They protect the city and do a great job. He also acknowledged the 
                    Community Arts staff - the show at the Sandy Amphitheater was amazing and he was 
                    proud of all of their hard work. He spoke about the loss of Ms. Homer, an educator at 
                    Parklane elementary school who taught for 30 years. He thanked her family for sharing 
                    her with the community. She will be  missed.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked all those for making the summer barbecue in 
                    Historic Sandy a huge success. Thank you to the Mayor's office, Parks and Rec, Tracy 
                    Cowdell, and all who helped.  made this event such a great event.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey also thought the Historic Sandy event was a great 
                    success.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud spoke about the food truck night at the Sandy 
                    Amphitheater which featured the Youth Entrepeneurs. They will be back at the July 18th 
                    event. A special thanks to the Administration for setting that up.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman thanked everyone for supporting the Sandy Club 
                    Carnival. She thanked Fire and Police for helping the Sandy Club community, and the 
                    Rotary Club for making the carnival possible. She spoke about Juneteenth and the 
                    symbolism associated with the flag.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski thanked the Council for moving the discussion on the capital projects 
                    forward. Our job is to be responsible and she is looking forward to future conversations 
                    with the Council and addressing residents' questions. The Mayor also spoke about the 
                    outstanding city events that have recently taken place: HCT Cultural Series, Historic 
                    Sandy Picnic, Sandy Club Carnival. The Parks & Rec Department staff have done an 
                    outstanding job organizing these events. She is proud of the city's employees. Last 
                    Friday, there was a press conference kicking off the Slow Down in Your Town initiative 
                    that was attended by valley Mayors and Police Chiefs. You will get ticketed if you are 
                    speeding in Sandy. We want safe neighborhoods. She showed a video with the 
                    messaging which is supported by all valley Mayors and Police Chiefs. She spoke about 
                    Juneteenth and stressed the need for fireworks safety and legal discharge days. 



CAO Report

                    Cliff Strachan, CAO, mentioned that the SCOPE Clinic is celebrating its 10 year 
                    anniversary. There will be an open house from 3 to 5 pm. Dr. Hurlburt has been with us for 
                    the entire 10 years.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:35 pm
