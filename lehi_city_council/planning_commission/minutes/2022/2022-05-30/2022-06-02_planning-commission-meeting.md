Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner
Bunker and Commissioner Jackson.

2) Training on Planning Commission order of item discussion.
Noreen Edwards presented information on planning meetings and bylaws. Noreen discussed
addressing the Chair, public comments, and addressing the Commissioners. She discussed Robert's
Rules and limiting the time of public comments.

3) Discussion of Moderate Income Housing Element update.
Brittney Harris asked if there was anything the Commissioners wanted to discuss on Housing
Affordability from the last presentation.

4) Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Nielsen moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
