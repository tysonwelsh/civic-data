                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 ORDINANCE NO. 24-10

 AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE “PAIGE RETAIL PLAZA
 DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT” APPROXIMATELY 3.9 ACRES LOCATED AT
 THE SE CORNER OF 12800 S & REDWOOD ROAD CURRENTLY ZONED C-D,
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT INCLUDES PEDESTRIAN ORIENTED OVERLAY
 (PO-OV), ARCHITECTURAL & COMMERCIAL SITE PLAN REQUIREMENTS,
   AND CONSTITUTES ZONING AND SITE PLAN APPROVALS, RIVERTON
                 CITY/JARED OSMOND, APPLICANTS

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that under Utah Code §10-9a-532, the City
Council may lawfully adopt development agreements that have different standards set forth in
existing land use regulations if it does so in accordance with the same procedures for enacting
a land use regulation, including review and recommendation from the Planning Commission
and a public hearing; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed development agreement; and,

      WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said development
agreement; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of
the public to adopt the Master Development Agreement for the Paige Retail Plaza, located at
the southeast corner of 12800 S and Redwood Road as described herein and shown as Exhibit
“A.”

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City,
Utah as follows:
       Section 1. The Paige Retail Plaza Development Agreement, Exhibit “B”, and all
                  associated design documents as approved, is hereby approved and
                  adopted. Execution of the development agreement is subject to the
                  following conditions:

                         Transfer of title to parcels 2734251035, 2734251006, 734251036,
                          2734251027, and 2734251033 from the Riverton Redevelopment
                          Agency to the Developer.
                         Any required approvals by UDOT of modifications to the detention
                          pond.
                         Approval of construction plans for detention pond and storm water
                          designs.
                         Approval of grading, lighting, fencing, and landscaping plans.

       Section 2.     This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
