                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-61


    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF A COMPLAINT TO TAKE
                PROPERTY THROUGH EMINENT DOMAIN

  (Affecting Parcel No. 27291020120000 - 3807 West 11800 South Riverton, Utah 84065)


       WHEREAS, Riverton City has identified a number of safety concerns related to
pedestrian travel along and in the area of 3807 West 11800 South, Riverton, Utah (the “Subject
Property”); and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has determined that in the interest of public safety it is both
reasonable and necessary to design, construct, maintain, use, repair, replace and improve a
sidewalk system where the Subject Property fronts 11800 South street and terminates on either
end of the Subject Property located at the above described address; and

        WHEREAS, The public need for the completion of a sidewalk system necessitates that
Riverton must acquire in fee simple a portion of the Subject Property, from owners of the same,
fronting 3807 West 11800 South Riverton, Utah, specifically Thom Belchak and Does 1-10; and,

        WHEREAS, Riverton City entered into extensive negotiations with Defendant Thom
Belchak to establish the fair market value of the Property and acquire, purchase or otherwise
obtain fee simple ownership of the Property; and,

      WHEREAS, Riverton City has the statutory authority to exercise the power of eminent
domain pursuant to Utah Code Section 78-34-1, et seq.; and,

         WHEREAS, The acquisition of the above described Property is for an authorized public
use, is necessary to accomplish the purposes for which it is being acquired and will further the
health, safety and welfare of many residents of Riverton City; and,

       WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code Section 72-5-103, Riverton City is entitled to
condemn the Property in fee simple and to have a court of competent jurisdiction determine and
adjudicate the amount to be paid as just compensation on the same.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the public interest and necessity
          require the acquisition and the immediate occupancy of part of the parcel of real
          estate located at the address of 3807 West 11800 South, Riverton, Utah, and more
          particularly described as parcel number 27-29-102-012 0000, for construction of a
          public sidewalk and related infrastructure, and for other lawful and legitimate public
          purposes.
      2. The property to be affected by the action taken in Section 1, above, is owned by
         Thomas A Belchak.

      3. The proposed location of the public improvements is planned and located in a manner
         which will be the most compatible with the Greatest Public Good and the least private
         injury.

      4. The Riverton City Council concluding that the condemnation of the subject property
         is necessary to promote the public health, safety and welfare authorizes the exercise
         of the power of eminent domain in order to acquire the Subject Property in fee and to
         initiate such legal proceedings as may be necessary to do so.

      5. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
