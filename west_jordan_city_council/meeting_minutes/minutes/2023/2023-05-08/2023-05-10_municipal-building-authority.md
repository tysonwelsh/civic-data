 2. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Public hearing and consideration of MBA Resolution No. 111 adopting the Annual
          Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West Jordan for Fiscal
          Year 2024.
          Administrative Service Director Danyce Steck explained that the Municipal Building
          Authority (MBA) was in place to hold debt for the public works building. The MBA
          issued the debt, and the city rents it back until the debt is paid in full.

           Chair McConnehey opened the public hearing at 6:02 pm
           Comments:
           None

           Chair McConnehey closed public the public hearing at 6:03 pm

           MOTION: Board Member Green moved to APPROVE MBA Resolution No. 111 adopting
                   the Annual Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West
                   Jordan for Fiscal Year 2024.
                    Board Member Worthen seconded the motion.
