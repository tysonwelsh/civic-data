of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. APPROVAL OF FINAL READING OF ORDINANCE 2022-26 AMENDING CERTAIN FEES TO
        WEBER COUNTY PARKS AND RECREATION DEPARTMENT FACILITIES.
            Todd Ferrario (Parks, Culture, & Recreation): Final reading with no changes. As you know, costs have
            increased and the proposed increases are needed.
            Commissioner Harvey moved for final approval of Ordinance 2022-26 amending certain fees to Weber County
            Parks and Recreation department facilities; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

     2. APPROVE A LOCAL TRANSPORTATION FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH OGDEN CITY FOR
        THE OGDEN 20TH STREET (PHASE 3) PROJECT.
            Brooke Stewart (Community Development): The Local Transportation funding agreement is for the 3rd and
            final phase of the Ogden City 20th Street project. With this project Ogden City intends to widen and improve
            just under half a mile from Quincy Avenue to a couple hundred feet east of the Harrison Blvd
            intersection. The project will be completed in part with local matching funds from Ogden City, a Surface
            Transportation Fund grant and the 3rd quarter cent sales tax funds. The project was recommended from
            funding by WACOG and approved by the County Commission as part of the 2021 Transportation priority
            list. The agreement outlines the terms related to the sales tax funds that are programmed for 2024. All
            concerns raised earlier have been addressed.

            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a local transportation funding agreement with Ogden City for the
            Ogdne 20th Street (Phase 3) Project; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
