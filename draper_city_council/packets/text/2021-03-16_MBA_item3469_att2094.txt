Business Meeting

1.0      Call to Order by Board Chair Troy Walker

This meeting was held as an electronic meeting pursuant to Draper City Resolution #14-54. As restrictions
take place across the State for limiting public gatherings, we want to make sure everyone interested in
participating in City Council meetings can do so in a manner that they feel most comfortable. Individuals
could participate by:

      1. Listening through the Draper City Website: https://www.draper.ut.us/95/Agendas-Minutes
      2. Emailing comments to public.comment@draper.ut.us by 5 p.m. on the day of the meeting.
         These will become part of the public record.
      3. Requesting the Zoom Meeting ID from public.comment@draper.ut.us by noon, June 16, 2020,
         to be able to speak during the Public Hearing portions of the meeting.
All of the Board members were present except for Boardmember Fred Lowry who was excused.
2.0      Motion: Approval of May 19, 2020, and June 2, 2020, Municipal Board Authority
         Meeting Minutes.

2.1      Boardmember Mike Green moved to accept May 19, 2020, and June 2, 2020,
         Municipal Board Authority Meeting Minutes. Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey
         seconded the motion.

2.2      A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Roberts, Vawdrey,
         and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0      Action Item: Resolution #MBA 20-02 Adopting the Fiscal Year 2021 Annual Budget
3.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, said that the board was voting on adoption of the same
      tentative budget as was presented at the May 19, 2020 meeting. There were no changes to
      the budget.

3.2   Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey moved to adopt the Fiscal Year 2021 Annual
      Budget. Boardmember Mike Green seconded the motion.

3.3   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Roberts, Vawdrey,
      and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

4.0   Adjourn to a Draper City Redevelopment Agency Meeting.

4.1   Boardmember Mike Green moved to adjourn the meeting. Boardmember Marsha
      Vawdrey seconded the motion.

4.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Roberts, Vawdrey,
      and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

4.3   The meeting adjourned at 7:48 p.m.
