                                        Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 1-12-21
                                            7:00 p.m.
                                   Nephi City Council Chambers


1. City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the goals and outcomes the council
   set at the beginning of the fiscal year. He stated as the city moves to a 2-year budgeting model,
   this type of review will occur several times within the two-year time frame.

2. Mr. Atkinson presented an overview of the General Fund and what departments are funded by
   the General Fund. He stated that any surplus from General Fund will be transferred to the
   Capital Projects Fund.

3. The council listened to presentations from the following departments:

        a.   Police
        b.   Golf
        c.   Parks & Cemeteries
        d.   Library
        e.   Recreation
        f.   Justice Court
        g.   Non-Departmental
        h.   Planning and Zoning
        i.   Airport

4.   The council reviewed the updated financial reports.

5.    The council reviewed the Red Cliffs Commercial Annexation and the Four Mile Annexation
     certification letters from the Nephi City Recorder.

6.    Mayor Nielson called to order a regular council meeting. Councilor Seely moved to adjourn and
     convene into executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual and
     real property. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

7. Adjournment 10:00 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Kent
Jones, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Kasey Wright, Craig Oswald, Michael Morgan, Michael Booth,
Gerratt Bethers.
