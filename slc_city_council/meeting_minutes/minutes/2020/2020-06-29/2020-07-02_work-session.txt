MINUTES OF THE SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL WORK SESSION
 MEETING
 THURSDAY, JULY 2 , 2020
 The City Council met in a Special Work Session on Thursday, July
 2, 2020, in an Electronic Meeting, pursuant to Salt Lake City
 Emergency Proclamation No. 2 of 2020 (2) (b) .
 In Attendance: Council Members Andrew Johnston, Amy Fowler, Chris
 Wharton, Daniel Dugan, Darin Mano, James Rogers, and Analia
 Valdemoros .
 Staff in Attendance: Cindy Gust-Jenson, Council Executive
 Director; Jennifer Bruno, Council Executive Deputy Director; Erin
 Mendenhall, Mayor; Rachel Otto, Mayor' s Chief of Staff; Lehua
 Weaver, Associate Council Deputy Director; Samantha Slark, Senior
 City Attorney; Lisa Shaffer, Mayor' s Chief Administrative Officer;
 Amanda Lau, Council Public Engagement & Communications Specialist;
 Benjamin Luedtke, Council Policy Analyst; Katherine Lewis, City
 Attorney; Allison Rowland, Council Policy Analyst; Kira Luke,
 Council Policy Analyst/Public Engagement; Robert Nutzman, Council
 Administrative Assistant; and Scott Crandall, Deputy City
 Recorder.
 Councilmember Wharton presided at and conducted the meeting.
 The meeting was called to order at 2 : 15 p .m.
 AGENDA ITEMS
 #1 . 2:15:47PM CONSIDER A MOTION TO ENTER INTO CLOSED SESSION,
 IN KEEPING WITH UTAH CODE §52-4-205 FOR ANY ALLOWED PURPOSE.
 Councilmember Mano moved and Councilmember Valdemoros
 seconded to enter into Closed Session to discuss the character,
 professional competence, or physical or mental health of an
 individual; deployment of security personnel, devices, or systems
 pursuant to Utah Code §52-4-205 (1) (a) & (f) ; and Advice of Counsel,
 Utah Open and Public Meetings Law. A roll call vote was taken.
 Council Members Johnston, Wharton, Mano, Valdemoros, Dugan,
 Fowler, and Rogers voted aye . See File M 20-1 for Sworn Statement .
 In Attendance: Council Members Johnston, Valdemoros, Wharton,
 Fowler, Rogers, Dugan, and Mano.
 Others in Attendance: Cindy Gust-Jenson, Jennifer Bruno, Erin
 Mendenhall, Rachel Otto, Lehua Weaver, Samantha Slark, Lisa
