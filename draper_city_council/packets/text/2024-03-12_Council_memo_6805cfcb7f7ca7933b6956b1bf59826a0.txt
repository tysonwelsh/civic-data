MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2024-03-12
Re: Action Item: Ordinance # 1607


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of an amendment to the Draper School
Development (DSD) Development Agreement for approximately 0.13 acres located on the
north side of 12200 South, at approximately 352 E. Mistilake Ln. The property is currently
zoned RM2 (Residential Multiple-Family). The applicant is requesting that an amendment
to the original Development Agreement be approved to clarify the setbacks applicable to
Lot 20 of the Rasmussen Family Subdivision First Amendment Subdivision Plat.



The findings for approval as are follows:


  1. The proposed clarifications to setbacks are consistent with the original development
     agreement.
  2. The proposed amendment does not conflict with any of the limitations of DCMC
     Section 9-5-200(D).


The findings for denial as are follows:


  1. The proposed clarifications to setbacks are not consistent with the original
     development agreement.
