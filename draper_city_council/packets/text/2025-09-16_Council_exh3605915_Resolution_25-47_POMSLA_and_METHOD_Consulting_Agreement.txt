                               RESOLUTION NO. 25-47

    A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING A
  CONSULTANT AGREEMENT WITH POINT OF THE MOUNTAIN STATE
        LAND USE AUTHORITY AND METHODS CONSULTING

        WHEREAS, Draper City has approved an interlocal agreement (“the Interlocal”)
with Point of the Mountain State Land Use Authority (“POMSLA”) that addresses the
design, construction, ownership and maintenance of certain public infrastructure, as well
as the delivery of certain public services to the point of the mountain state land (“The
Point”); and

        WHEREAS, the Interlocal provides for a development consultant, chosen by
Draper City and POMSLA, to provide design review, construction monitoring and
similar services; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City and POMSLA have gone through a rigorous
procurement process and have selected METHODS Consulting (“METHODS”) to
provide development consultant services outlined in the Interlocal; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City, POMSLA and METHODS have negotiated a
consultant agreement; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council finds entering into the consultant agreement with
POMSLA and METHODS promotes the public health, welfare and safety of Draper
residents and businesses.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Approval of Development Agreement. The Development
Consultant Agreement between POMSLA, METHODS and Draper City, attached hereto
as Exhibit A, is hereby approved by the Council, and the Mayor is hereby authorized to
execute the same on behalf of Draper City.

        Section 2. De Minimus Changes. The City Manager is authorized to attach the
final exhibits to the Agreement and correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical,
or de minimis errors or omissions consistent with the City Council’s approval of this
Resolution prior to submitting the Interlocal for the Mayor’s signature.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 16th DAY OF SEPTEMBER 2025.
