Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on August 16, 2022, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin
Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

CONSIDERATION OF PROCLAMATION NO. 2022-24, A PROCLAMATION TO
DECLARE THE UPCOMING SCHOOL YEAR AS AN ALCOHOL-FREE YEAR FOR

YOUTH

The Youth Coalition explained that their mission is to prevent substance abuse among their peers.
The municipalities along with the County is expected to adopt the proclamation declaring the
upcoming school year as an alcohol-free year for youth.

The Commissioners thanked the youth for their commitment to their fellow students and to the
community.

The proclamation was read aloud.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Proclamation No. 2022-24, A
Proclamation to declare the Upcoming School Year as an Alcohol-Free Year
for Youth. Motion seconded by Chairman Iverson and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1216-O, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING,

MODIFYING, RENUMBERING WASHINGTON COUNTY ORDINANCE, TITLE 8
CHAPTER 7 REGARDING INDIGENT AND UNCLAIMED CREMATION

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the County has an obligation to provide the
burial for unclaimed bodies upon death. The updates to the Ordinance provide a requirement
threshold for indigence in circumstances where the deceased person is not unclaimed but family
members are indigent and are unable to pay for burial.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2986, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_AGREEMENT_ BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY _ AND LEGACY PARK

EQUINE TRAINING CENTER

Chairman Iverson explained that the County owns and operates Legacy Park, its fairgrounds and
equine facility. The equine facility rents stalls for trainers to house and train their race horses on a
regulation track. The current trainers have formed a non-profit organization and desire to enter into
an agreement with the County in order to continue stall rental and training for their horses.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2986, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington County and
Legacy Park Equine Training Center. Motion seconded by Chairman Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2987, A RESOLUTION AFFIRMING
THE CREATION AND LEGAL EXISTENCE OF THE NEW HARMONY VALLEY

SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

Commissioner Almquist explained that the New Harmony Valley Special Service District still
operates as a District as outlined in the original creation documents. This resolution affirms its
creation and legal existence.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2987, A
Resolution Affirming the Creation and Legal Existence of the New Harmony
Valley Special Service District. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist
voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT

Public Works Director explained that due to the recent storms, some of the roads are not in passable
conditions. He cautioned the public to be aware and cautious while the road crew continues to chip
seal and make the necessary repairs to damaged roads in the coming weeks.

Commissioner Almquist stated that the elected officials work together in their various capacities
and encouraged citizens to attend the town hall meetings in order to educate themselves on the
processes of government.
