MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-06-13
Re: Public Hearing: Jenson Farms Lots 208 and 209 Subdivision Plat Amendment
(Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.62
acres located on the north side of Rocky Bluff Cove in the Jenson Farms Phase 2
Subdivision. The property is currently zoned R4. The applicant is requesting to amend the
existing plat to consolidate two lots owned by the applicant into one larger lot. A single-
family residence is proposed to be constructed on the new consolidated lot.



The subject property was platted on March 5, 2019 as part of the Jenson Farms Phase 2
subdivision. The rezoning application and Development Agreement for the original
Jenson Farms Subdivision project was approved by the City Council in October 2017. The
Development Agreement outlined a process for dedication of land to the city for a new
park, road stubbing or street connections to the planned subdivision to the north and a
chart showing the minimum number of different sized lots required in the subdivision.
There was no other modification of development standards as part of that Agreement.



Finding for Approval:

1. There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
way, or easement has been vacated or amended.


Finding for Denial:

1.   There is not good cause for the vacation or amendment.
