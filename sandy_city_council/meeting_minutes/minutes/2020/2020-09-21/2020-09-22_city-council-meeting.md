5.         ZONE-07-20- Community Development Department presenting a rezone application (File
           5877(CC)    #ZONE-07-20-5877, Copper Creek Rezone) on behalf of Utah
                       Development Group, requesting that 1.02 acres located at 1368 E. Copper
                       Creek Road be rezoned from the CN and R-1-8 Zone to the RM(12) Zone.
                   Attachments:   Planning Commission Report-Copper Creek (9/3/20)
                                  Concept Plan (7/20/20)
                                  Neighborhood Meeting Summary (8/13/20)
                                  Planning Commission Minutes-draft (9/3/20)
                                  Resident Email (Miner, 9/4/20)
                                  Ordinance #20-11 (Copper Creek Rezone)
                                  Public Comment Email
                                  Additional Public Comment Email

                                  Jack Warner, Long Range Planning Manager, presented on the item and provided
                                  information and background regarding a rezone application for a property located at 1368
                                  E. Copper Creek Road. The applicant is Utah Development Group. The Planning
                                  Commission did not forward a favorable recommendation.

                                  Marco Diaz spoke on behalf of the applicant. His presentation included pictures of the
                                  existing structures located on the parcel and also of the surrounding area. He provided
                                  additional information about the revised, proposed development and addressed concerns
                                  expressed from area residents and the planning commissioners.

                                  Council member questions and comments followed.

                                  Public Comment:

                                  Dustin Fratto invited the pubic to comment.

                                  Mr. Jim Edwards felt the development was too dense for the area and enjoys the mature
                                  trees on the property. He was not in support of the project and asked the council to vote
                                  against the rezone application.

                                  Mr. Tom Lewis asked the developers to change the development density of the project.

                                  Mr. Dennis Hiatt and Ms.Heather Myer felt the proposed development was too dense and
                                  asked the Council to deny the rezone. Mr. Hiatt also expressed concern with the
                                  proposed development.

                                  There were no written comments.

                                  Public Comment closed.

                                  Council discussion followed public comment. The Council Members discussed the
                                  current zoning in the area and the impact of the proposed rezone and development in the
                                  neighborhood. They discussed the importance of having the Planning Commission review
                                  the revised site plan prior to bringing it to the Council for consideration.
