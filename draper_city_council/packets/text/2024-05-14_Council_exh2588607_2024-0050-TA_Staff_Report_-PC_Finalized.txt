BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Text Amendment to the CSD-SWF zone.
The CSD-SWF zone includes only the Ashley Furniture site, is approximately 3.36 acres in size,
and is located at approximately 13177 S Lone Peak Parkway (Exhibits B & C). The applicant is
requesting to add stucco, exterior insulation and finish systems (EIFS), and exposed concrete
as allowed primary materials in the CSD-SWF zone in addition to those primary materials
found in Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-22-040(F)(3).

The CSD-SWF was adopted by the City in May 2009 to facilitate commercial development in
the West Bangerter area which had large vacant parcels near the freeway. The CSD-SWF was
amended in in 2013 to add banner sign standards and in 2020 to allow medical cannabis
pharmacies, as required by state law.

Criteria For Approval.

A Text Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City Council and
is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation to the City
Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in Section 9-5-
060(E)(2) of the DCMC:

    2. Text Amendments:
       a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies of
           the city's general plan;
       b. Whether a proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of the
           zoning ordinance;
       c. Whether the proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request and
           there is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning ordinance;
       d. The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or part of
           this title or the general plan;
       e. Whether the potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated and
           determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare and represents an
           overall community benefit; and
       f. The extent to which a proposed text amendment implements best current, professional
           practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices

1. How is the proposed amendment consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies
of the City’s General Plan?

The proposed amendment is consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies of the city’s
general plan in that it provides a revitalization to the Ashley store. By modernizing the Ashley
store with a new exterior look, we are projecting stronger economic well-being for the store,
which in turn, provides greater employment opportunities for the public, an increase in
Ashley’s taxable income, and a greater opportunity for Ashley to continue to support the
community.

2. How is the proposed amendment appropriate given the context of the request and
what is the justification for the modification?

The proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request, as this updated
exterior for Ashley will provide long lasting materials contributing to the long-term aesthetics
of the building. The justification for the modification is that the amendment allows for a revised
exterior giving a fresh appearance to the Ashley store to better compete in a retail furniture
area, in which there have been new or recently renovated furniture stores. Additionally, as
mentioned in the beginning, the exterior materials listed in the proposed amendment will
provide a longer lasting look than the existing stone material.

3. How might the amendment impact the community?

The amendment will impact the community in a positive way, as this will assist Ashley in
maintaining a consistent economic presence in the City of Draper. Also, by revitalizing the
exterior of the store, the view is that not only will Ashley receive additional traffic flow from
consumers, but surrounding businesses and retail stores will benefit as well.
