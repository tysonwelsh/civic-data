BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.50
acres located on the a private lane east of Boulter St., at approximately 1543 E Conrads
View Ln (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a
Plat Amendment be approved to allow for a modification in the size of a private storm
retention easement.

Dana Subdivision Phase 1 was approved on January 7, 2014. Phase 2 was approved on June
1, 2016. This amendment is to Lot 1 of Phase 2.

ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Low/Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This
category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately
two dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)
Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little
impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the
City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life,
including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. This zone is intended to promote
a combination of retail and service facilities which, in character and scale, meet day to day
needs of nearby residents.” The RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot lot
minimum) zoning abuts the subject property on the north and south, and RA2 zone abuts



Dana                                                                 App. # SUBD-0143-2021
Plat Amendment Request                           2
on the east and west.

Subdivision Layout. The Dana Phase 2 Subdivision included a 15-foot wide by 120-foot long
private storm retention easement along the north boundary of Lot 1 (Exhibit E). Per a note
on the plat, the owner of Lot 1 is responsible for the maintenance of the easement. The
current owner of Lot 1 would like to amend the easement so that it is only 10-feet wide by
146-feet long (Exhibit F). This means that the easement will extend along the entire rear
property line, but not extend as far into the rear yard area as before. The applicant is
proposing to regrade the area to ensure the retention pond continues to function. The new
design has been reviewed and approved by the Engineering Division. No other changes to
the property or subdivision are proposed.

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

        1.      That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
        2.      The applicant work with the Engineering Division to ensure the
                improvements comply with the approved plat, including obtaining a Land
                Disturbance Permit, if applicable.
        3.      Per DCMC Section 17-9-070, if the plat amendment is not recorded within
                one (1) year from the date of approval or construction of the required
                landscaping and infrastructure has not commenced, such approval shall be
                null and void. This time period may be extended by the Planning Commission
                for up to one additional six (6) month period for good cause shown. The
                subdivider must petition in writing for an extension prior to the expiration of
                the original one (1) year.

The findings for approval as are follows:

        1.      There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street,
                right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
