Proposal
The applicant Todd Hansen, on behalf of Park City School District, is proposing a ~105,000 sq. ft.
expansion of the Ecker Hill Middle School, located at 2465 Kilby Rd, Park City, UT 84098,
Summit County, UT. The proposed expansion would accommodate the incorporation of the 8th
grade into the school that would result in 400 additional students. The proposal includes, but is
not limited to: new classrooms, restrooms, remodel of cafeteria, addition to the gymnasium,
career and technical education, and other associated school facilities.

Background
Ecker Hill Middle School was constructed in 1996 at ~180,000 sq. ft. in area. The school became
operational in 1997 and consisted of 24 classrooms. The school originally housed grades 6-8,
but currently houses grades 6 and 7.  The ~20,000 sq. ft. auditorium was constructed in 2003.
The maximum height of the existing school is 42 feet.
As part of their preparation to increase the capacity by 400 students, the Park City School
District designed a ~105,000 sq. ft. expansion which includes two levels and associated rooftop
mechanical equipment which causes it to exceed the maximum zone height of 32 feet.
2
Ecker Hill Middle School Expansion MPD-CUP -December 13, 2022

The overall process for the proposed expansion involves the Special Exception (height) and an
MPD (Rezone-CUP) review.
On September 13, 2022, Staff introduced the project to the Snyderville Basin Planning
Commission. The Planning Commission directed the applicant to finalize the Special Exception
process first before they review the MPD process and make the subsequent recommendation
to the County Council.
On September 28, 2022, Summit County Council reviewed the Special Exception proposal.
During the meeting the Council found that the southern addition was acceptable due to the
existing gymnasium layout. The Council expressed concern regarding the northern additions
mechanical rooms and questioned whether they needed to be as tall as proposed. At the
conclusion of the meeting the Council decided to schedule a site visit and continued the public
hearing to the October 5, 2022 meeting. The Council also directed the applicant to return with
additional analysis to support their request, including a design that matches the existing
maximum height of the school.
Following the September 28, 2022 meeting, the applicant provided an additional analysis which
resulted in a 4.5-foot decrease in height. The proposed height was reduced from 47 feet to 4.5
feet. Subsequently on October 5, 2022, Summit County Council granted a Special Exception to
increase the maximum height from 32 feet to 42.5 feet for specific portions of the expansion.

EXHIBIT A.1
Ecker Hill Middle School
Ecker Hill Middle School is located in Snyderville Basin's Hillside Stewardship (HS)
Zone and is not a charter school.  Summit County Code, Section 10-2-10 Use Table,
allows "Churches, schools, institutional uses" in the Rural Residential (RR) Zone
through review and approval of a Conditional Use Permit. The Hillside Stewardship
Zone prohibits that use category. The existing School was established prior to the
current Hillside Stewardship zone. The existing school is a lawiully created non-
conforming use and as such can be expanded in area through an LIP per 10-3-4.D.3 so
long as it is less than 15% but no greater than 10,000 SF. The proposed expansion will
be more than 25,000 SF. This is inconsistent with current land use regulations.
Consequently, for the County to approve a Conditional Use Permit for this project the
School District will need to apply for a rezone from HS to RR (10-7-4).  The rezone
needs to be done in conjunction with a Conditional Use Permit (10-3-5) and a Master
planned Development process (10-2-18).
The 104,500 SF addition has been sited to minimize the disturbance area on site.
Several locations were studied, but the proposed location allowed the addition to be
directly attached to the existing school with minimal displacement of green space and
maximizing the benefits of daylight. Proposed design reflects a balance of site materials
and there is no proposed large-scale site grading. There is no proposed impact existing
wildlife corridors or existing waterways. Existing green space will remain as is.
The Project includes the design and construction of new Ecker Hill Middle School
additions which will include a new CTE Community, two new Learning Centers, an
expanded gymnasium and cafeteria to accommodate the incorporation of the 6th, 7th,
and 8th grades. The estimated gross square footage is 104,500 gross sq. ft. of floor
area.
The design will focus on building expansion along the north and west side of the
existing building as well as remodeling the existing cafeteria. New CTE and classroom
spaces will be added. The additions will connect and supplement the existing classroom
space that currently wraps around the perimeter of the building. This intends to offer an
opportunity for improving the capacity and quality of the space. The CTE spaces should
provide better access to technology and maker spaces.
PCSD desires to design and construct new spaces that are as energy efficient as
possible and incorporate green materials in the design while working within the allotted
budget limitations and would like the new spaces to meet the minimum requirements for
a LEED Silver certification. PCSD may pursue the formal LEED certification.

Section 10-2-8 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code states that the purpose of the SC
zone is:
"The purpose of the service commercial/light industrial (SC) zone district is to provide appropriate
locations within the Snyderville Basin for service commercial and  light industrial  uses,  recognizing that
such uses provide vital support functions for the basin's resort and  residential economy."
The purpose of the zone is not mixed use residential. The zone was originally created as a place
for '`back of house" uses such as plumbers, auto shops, and light manufacturing uses. Generally,
these types of zones do not allow residential uses. Nonetheless, over the years, the Commission
has approved condominium plats within the SC zone that allow for an accessory apartment
within a commercial unit (Park East 11 Park East 11 and Synergy Commons). These developments
brought a residential component into an area that previously had none. The question before
Code Amendments

the Planning Commission is whether it is appropriate to take the next step by introducing multi-
family development into the area.
HEIGHT
The applicant would like to increase the maximum height in the SC zone from 32 feet to 45 feet.
This increase in height would give him the ability to build a mixed-use building with
commercial/office on the bottom and 2-3 stories of residential above. If approved, the SC and
NMU zones would be the only zones within the Snyderville Basin with a height limit above 32
feet (many buildings have been approved above 32 feet through the SPA/Development
Agreement process).
SETBACK REDUCTION
The applicant is requesting that the front setback be reduced from 30 feet to 20 feet measured
from the front property line. This would increase the buildable area of the lot and move the
building closer to the street. Reduced front yard setbacks are a good thing, they have the effect
of making an area feel more walkable and pedestrian friendly. Setback areas for commercial
structures usually end up being large unused landscape areas that require a lot of water to
maintain or are ignored and go to seed.   Reducing the front setback to 20 feet will still give the
property owner enough area for snow storage, provide adequate site distances for cars pulling
in and out of the property and preserve the look of the existing streetscape.
