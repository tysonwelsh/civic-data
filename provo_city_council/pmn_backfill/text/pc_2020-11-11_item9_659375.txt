CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following:
• The HOA president of the parking garage had concerns about how many stalls are available based on others also
    using the garage.
• Living in the downtown is not a quiet place and those with sensitivity may find it challenging.
• One neighbor didn’t know that there was a neighborhood meeting held for this item.
• The parking garage has been over-sold.
• Entering and exiting the parking garage can be challenging, and if more users are allowed, it will become even more
    difficult.
• There is a home across the street that will now have a very tall building across the street and face even more
    challenges with parking and having the driveway to the home blocked.
• Residents of the new building will not have a good access to parking garage.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
• Some of the units will be used for seniors over 62 year of age and the other will be for individuals with autism.
   These are tax credit affordable housing units and the Provo City Housing Authority (PCHA) is required to own
   them for 15 years and then they are required to be used another 15 years as affordable units. The PCHA does not
   plan to ever sell the project.
• The PCHA owns other similar projects with senior housing for 55 and older residents and all their parking lots are
   over parked. They use less than half of a space per unit. With this project being for 62 and older it can be expected
   that even less parking will be needed.
• Changes have been made to the design to match the DRC request to have vertical integration of materials and
   colors.
• There will be a three foot parkstrip with pavers and trees and eight feet will be sidewalk and sidewalk extended to
   the building.
• There will be a designated postal delivery space.
• The alley will have lighting and cameras to provide added safety and security.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
• There are approximately 504 stalls in the parking garage. The applicant has control of 120 stalls and is working with
   the HOA to determine how these spaces can be designated.
• It would be nice if there was a drive-up area for pick-up and delivery.
• ADA stalls were discussed. There will be ADA stalls in the on-street angled parking and stalls in the parking
   garage. The building has elevators as does the parking garage.
• The amenity space includes a sensory room for the resident with autism to have a controlled environment for light
   and sound.
• Visitors will be able to park in the garage and on-street angled parking spaces near the building.
• With the proposed type of housing there will be less need for parking and a reduction makes sense.
