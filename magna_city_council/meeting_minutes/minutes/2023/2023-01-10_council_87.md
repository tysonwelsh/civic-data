                                          WORKSHOP

Form of Government Change & Feasibility Study

                  Greg Schulz stated he was asked to research the different forms of government
that Magna could potentially change to. Regardless of what form of government Magna becomes,
how services are obtained is at the Council’s discretion. If the Council wants to provide its own
services, it will want to do a study to determine what the cost will be. Why consider changing the
form of government? Residents and other government entities do not know what a metro township
is. They do not know how it works, functions or who is in charge. Based on discussions with
citizens and municipal peers, it may be a good idea to change the form of government.

               Council Member Hull stated it is frustrating that Salt Lake County employees do
not understand what the metro township is. They do not recognize Magna as having its own
authority as a municipality.

              Mr. Schulz stated the public is confused and it will not change unless the form of
government is changed.

               The Council will continue the presentation on the form of government change later
in the meeting. It amended its agenda to allow for citizen public input at this time.

              Council Member Barney, seconded by Council Member Pierce, moved to amend
the agenda to allow for citizen public input. The motion passed unanimously.

               After a motion to return to the normal agenda, the Council continued discussions
on the form of government change after taking citizen public input.

               Mr. Schulz reviewed the following types of government that can be considered:

   -   Full-time mayor-council form of government consists of a full-time mayor elected at large
       and an elected five-member council.
   -   Five-member form of government consists of a part-time mayor elected at large and an
       elected four-member council.
   -   Six-member form of government consists of a part-time mayor elected at large and an
       elected five-member council.

After looking into each type of government, he recommends the six-member form of government.
The Council can move forward with the change with the least amount of disruption. The five
council districts will be retained, it will just add a mayor. The mayor is currently appointed by its
peers, with the change of government, the citizens will elect the mayor. Since the Mayor would
be elected by the citizens, a legislative change would need to be made to allow for a concurrent
election of the mayor.

               Council Member Prokopis asked if each of the options require a legislative
change.

                Mr. Schulz stated yes, if the Council wants the mayor elected at that same time
as the transition.

               Council Member Barney stated if a legislative change is not made, then it would
be two years before citizens could elect a mayor. If the legislative change is made, there will be
two items on the ballot; the form of government change and the mayor election.

              Mr. Schulz stated the timeline will include adoption of a resolution officially starting
the process, hold two public hearings within 45 days, work with the Salt Lake County Clerk and
Lieutenant Governors Office to set up the election process, and finally the election.

               Mr. Ashton recommended putting this on the January 24, 2023, Council agenda
for consideration.

Dog Park Fencing

                Council Member Hull stated Magna received a grant for $4,000 to install fencing
for a dog park. The fencing was supposed to be installed by the end of December 2022; however,
it was not completed. She contacted Healthy Utah and got the grant extended. There are
decisions that need to be made before construction can move forward.

              Council Member Barney stated at a previous meeting, it was determined that the
mayor had the discretion to move forward, and Mayor Peay was going to obtain a contractor.

               Mayor Peay stated the Council wanted a clear title before it entered into a contract.

               Council Member Prokopis stated the land was given to Magna to use.

needed to be completed.

Council would need to contribute additional funds to finish the fencing.

                Mayor Peay stated he will contact fencing companies and get a few quotes. The
land will need to be graded and the weeds will need to be removed.

Council Rules and Procedure

                Rori Andreason stated a few changes were made to the Councils Rules of
Procedure and Order. Workshop meetings were added to the language, and she will add
amendments that were discussed tonight. Language was added to rule number 2, stating items
can be placed on the agenda by “staff or by any council member”. Rule number 3 eliminates the
language stating “no formal motions or seconds are required or necessary. The Council will need
to make motions during its meetings. Rule number 4 adds a word. Rule number 5 was added to
include “to the extent not covered by preceding rule. Roberts Rules of Order shall apply”. She will
bring these changes back to the next business meeting for final adoption.
