1.0   Presentation: Visual Arts Foundation

1.1   Jean Hendricksen shared the history of art education, collection, and
      conservation in Draper City, and spoke of forming the Draper Visual Arts
      Foundation. She emphasized that Draper City needed a place where art could
      be taught, shown, and celebrated.

1.2   Leslie Johnson spoke of the opportunity to build a cultural arts center in the
      center of Draper City to support the arts and enrich the community. She spoke
      of the many groups such a facility could support.

1.3   Sherrie Jensen, founder and Artistic Director of the Draper Philharmonic &
      Choral Society, spoke of the need for a cultural arts center in Draper City.

1.4   Darrell Smith spoke of the history of the arts in Draper. He expressed the
      opinion that a cultural center needed to be included in the City Center project.

1.5   Lowell Baum requested to schedule a time to sit down with the Council and
      discuss the possibility of a center for cultural arts in Draper.

8.0   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1581, an Ordinance of Draper City amending
      the text of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City
      Municipal Code relating to the IKEA Parking and Sign Standards and
      Concept Plan

8.1   Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky said IKEA desired an
      addition that would allow for home delivery services. Floor area for customers
      would not increase, and amount of parking needed would not increase. Ms.
      Jastremsky said IKEA requested a reduction in the required amount of parking
      per thousand square feet of gross building floor area, currently 4 stalls per
      1,000 square feet in the CSD. The proposed text amendment would reduce the
      requirement to 2.5 parking stalls per thousand square feet, which would align
      with City Code for regular retail uses. Ms. Jastremsky said the proposed
      amendment would also allow IKEA to have electronic message center (EMC)
      signs, within established City standards. She said she had not seen proposed
      concepts for the sign.

8.2   Councilmember Green asked if the sign would comply with state and federal
      standards for electronic signs near the freeway.

8.3   Adam Bortz, representing IKEA, said he believed the CSD restrictions regarding
      EMCs were more strict than federal requirements. He said IKEA would
      absolutely comply with the CSD restrictions. Mr. Bortz said the proposed
      expansion area was currently largely used for storage containers.

9.0   Public Hearing: Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Tentative Budget

9.1   Mayor Pro Tempore Roberts opened a public hearing.

9.2   Stacy Jenson, Draper resident, said her home backed up to what would be
      Jensen Farm Park, and said she would love the property to be developed as
      the planned park.

9.3   Melina Itinose, Draper resident, said she lived in Jensen Farm Cove, and said it
      would be nice to have the Jensen Farm Park developed.

9.4   Mayor Pro Tempore Roberts closed the public hearing.

9.5   Parks and Recreation Director Rhett Ogden said public comments and
      suggestions were incorporated in the Jensen Farm Park Master Plan where
      possible. He said final designs were completed.

9.6   Staff explained that funds to develop Jensen Farm Park were not included in
      the FY 2024 Tentative Budget. Funds were not available to develop or maintain
      the park at that time.
