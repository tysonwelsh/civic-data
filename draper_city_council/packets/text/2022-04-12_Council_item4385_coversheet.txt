Re: Providing Local Consent for a Full Service Restaurant alcohol license for Spark Group Restaurants Comments: Spark Group Restaurants LLC dba Paradise Biryani Pointe located at 280 E 12300 S Suite 110 is seeking Local Consent for a Full Service Restaurant alcohol license. They have provided the following items:
-A current copy of their Draper City business license, license number ACC-000989-2020.
-A sales tax license
-A background check completed by the Bureau of Criminal Identification
-A floor map showing where alcohol is to be stored, sold, and consumed.
-Proximity maps showing distances from community locations.
-A bond payment to the DABC
-Liquor liability insurance. The findings are as follows: DCMC states in 6-4-040, B, b.:
A beer-only restaurant, full-service restaurant, or limited- service restaurant license shall not be granted to any applicant for any location situated within three hundred feet (300') of any community location as measured from the nearest patron entrance of the proposed restaurant by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the property boundary of the community location or within two hundred feet (200') of the proposed restaurant measured in a straight line from the nearest patron entrance of the proposed restaurant to the nearest property boundary of the community location.
Paradise Group Restaurants LLC currently meets both of these standards.
