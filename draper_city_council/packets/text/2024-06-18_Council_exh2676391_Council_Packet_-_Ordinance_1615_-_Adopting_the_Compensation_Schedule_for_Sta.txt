                                          ORDINANCE NO. 1615

              AN ORDINANCE COMPENSATION SCHEDULES FOR STATUTORY
                      OFFICERS FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2024-2025.

        WHEREAS, Utah Code §10-3-818 provides that elective and statutory officers in municipalities
may only receive compensation for services pursuant to ordinance enacted by the legislative body following
a public hearing; and

      WHEREAS, the compensation schedules for elective officers will not change while the
compensation schedules for statutory officer are proposed to be changed for FY 2024-2025; and

       WHEREAS, a public hearing to consider the compensation schedules for statutory officers has
been noticed and held and all interested persons were heard; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has considered the compensation schedule for statutory officers.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

         Section 1.       The City Council adopts the following compensation schedules for Draper City’s
statutory officer:


 Title                 Grade                      Min. Range             Mid. Point            Max. Range
 City Recorder         18                           34.77                  45.18                 55.58
 City Treasurer        18                           34.77                  45.18                 55.58


         Section 2.        Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid, or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Ordinance, and
all sections, parts, and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 4.       Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final passage.
