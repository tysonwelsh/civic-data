                                         Proclamation

WHEREAS, John Cahill has been an avid outdoorsman and began skiing in Alta in 1960; and

WHEREAS, John Cahill has owned the Alta Peruvian Lodge since 1970; and

WHEREAS, John Cahill has maintained the Alta Peruvian Lodge in the tradition, atmosphere, and
mountain spirit of Alta; and

WHEREAS, John Cahill has been a strong supporter and champion of Alta for many years
resulting in the betterment and advancement of Alta; and

WHEREAS, John Cahill has been a very colorful, lively, spirited, strong, interesting, and unique
individual; and

WHEREAS, John Cahill was born on April 11, 1924 and has attained the age of 100 years old.

Now therefore in consideration of the foregoing and the Town of Alta’s recognition and deep
appreciation of John Cahill’s contribution to Alta, the Town of Alta is proud to proclaim April 11,
2024 as “John Cahill Day”.

In witness whereof, we, the undersigned, Mayor and Members of the Alta Town Council, have
hereunto set our signature and caused the official seal of the Town of Alta to be affixed this ___
day of March, in the year 2024.
