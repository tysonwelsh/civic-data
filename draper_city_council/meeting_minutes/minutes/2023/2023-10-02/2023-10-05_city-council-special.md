Business Session

1.        Call to Order by Mayor Troy K. Walker


2.        Items for Council Consideration
2.a       Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Full-Service Restaurant License
          for SF Hash Kitchen, LLC.
          Seth Hoyt with Savory representing Hash Kitchen, LLC introduced himself and
          shared the concept for Hash Restaurant.
          Mayor Walker opened the public hearing. No one came forward so Mayor

          Walker closed the public hearing.

          Travis DeJong reviewed the requirements for a Local Consent. The restaurant

          complies with all requirements.

          Councilmember Green moved to approve providing Local Consent for a Full-

          Service Restaurant License for SF Hash Kitchen, LLC. Councilmember

          Vawdrey seconded the motion.

          A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
