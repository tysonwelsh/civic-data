From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: March 10, 2022
Re: Kum and Go – Site Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Site Plan for approximately 2.3 acres located on the south side of 12300 South, at approximately 137 West 12300 South. The property is currently zoned CR . The applicant is requesting that a Site Plan be approved to allow for the development of a vacant piece of property into a gasoline service station and convenience store . The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   The site will be a phased development, with the proposed site plan occupying half of the property.
2.   The site plan conforms to applicable standards set forth in this title, including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points, parking, landscaping, and building materials.
3.   The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is located.
4.   The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the subject development, as required by engineering standards and specifications.
5.   The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm water drainage, and utilities concerns. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 3/3/2022 Cover Memo
