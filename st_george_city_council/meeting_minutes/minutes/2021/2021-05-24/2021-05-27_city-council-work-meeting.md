                         ST. GEORGE CITY COUNCIL MINUTES
                                  WORK MEETING
                               MAY 27, 2021 4:00 P.M.
                        ADMINISTRATIVE CONFERENCE ROOM

PRESENT:
     Mayor Michele Randall
     Councilmember Jimmie Hughes
     Councilmember Bryan Smethurst
     Councilmember Gregg McArthur
     Councilmember Dannielle Larkin
     Councilmember Vardell Curtis
     City Manager Adam Lenhard
     Deputy City Attorney Paula Houston
     City Recorder Christina Fernandez

OTHERS PRESENT:
     Several staff members from various departments

OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was offered by Deanna Martin and The Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag
     was led by City Manager Adam Lenhard.

DISCUSSION REGARDING THE FISCAL YEAR 2021-2022 BUDGET:
     Human Resources Director Sharon Hokanson thanked the Council for their support.
     She spoke about the need for additional training of employees as well as the need for
     a Citywide wellness program for employees. A new timekeeping system is proposed
     to be purchased in the new fiscal year. She thanked the Council for approving pay
     increases. After being asked by Councilmember Larkin, Ms. Hokanson spoke about
     the City’s participation with Blomquist Hale, the Employee Assistance Program.

      Councilmember Curtis noted he is appreciative of opening positions internally to give
      current employees the opportunity to apply.

      Financial Planning and Budget Manager Deanna Brklacich thanked Human Resources
      for their help with the budget.

      Energy Services Director Laurie Mangum mentioned the 2021 Summer Reliability
      Assessment. Her department is purchasing energy and solar; their portfolio is
      looking good. She would like the Council to be prepared for what may happen in the
      summer. She then outlined the personnel she requested in the 2021-22 budget.
      After being asked by Councilmember Larkin, Ms. Mangum spoke about ways to
      conserve energy.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted energy is among one of the most expensive
      services the City provides; however, the City’s rates are one of the lowest in the
      nation.

      Ms. Mangum spoke about a slight proposed rate increase. After being asked by
      Councilmember McArthur explained why the City is not buying into the nuclear
      program at this time. She then spoke about education opportunities for residents.

      Community Development Director John Willis noted they are hitting records with
      building permits. Most of the projects in the 2021-22 budget are carryover projects
      from the current fiscal year. He spoke about the 2040 survey that recently took
      place. The proposed budget is similar to that of the current fiscal year’s budget.

      Mayor Randall noted she is concerned that the City only has one Code Enforcement
      Officer.

      The Council discussed what resources they would like to provide to code
      enforcement. The cases the officer is working on are complaint driven, nothing is
      being done proactively. The consensus of the Council is to add a Code Enforcement
      Officer position in the budget.

      Public Works Director Cameron Cutler outlined his proposed budgets for Fleet
      Services; Airport including proposed fee increases; Streets; SunTran; and Capital
      Projects.

      Leisure Services Director Shane Moore noted his department has 18 budgets. He
      spoke about personnel requests; park projects; and capital outlay projects. After
      being asked by Councilmember Curtis, Mr. Moore provided an update on naming a
      field after Bill and Mona Given. He then spoke about proposed fee increases.

      Economic Development and Housing Director Shirlayne Quayle explained the Airport
      is moving under her direction and the name of her Department is going to be
      renamed Economic Vitality and Housing. She outlined the proposed budget for her
      divisions.

      Communications and Marketing Director David Cordero stated he requested a full-
      time graphic arts designer and the social media designer from Leisure Services will
      be moving to his department. Additionally, he has recently hired an intern. He
      outlined projects his staff will implement in the 2021-22 fiscal year.

REPORTS FROM MAYOR, COUNCILMEMBERS, AND CITY MANAGER
Councilmember McArthur reported on the recent Habitat Conservation meeting he recently
attended.

      Councilmember Smethurst provided an update on the Gold Star Memorial.

      Councilmember Larkin spoke about the Arts Commission meeting she recently
      attended.
