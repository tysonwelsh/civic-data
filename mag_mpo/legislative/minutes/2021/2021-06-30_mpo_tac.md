Mountainland MPO Technical Advisory Committee Electronic via Zoom Live Stream: mountainland.org/live June 30, 2021 10:00 a.m. Minutes Attending: Representing: Adam Olsen American Fork Alma Haskell UTA Brad Stapley Springville Eric Rasband UDOT Jared Johnson Spanish Fork Jason Bond Santaquin Jed Muhlestein Alpine Jill Spencer Payson Kelly Lund FHWA Marty Beaumont Pleasant Grove Mary De La Mare-Schaeffer UTA Matt Parker UDOT Naseem Ghandour Vineyard Noah Gordon Lindon Richard Nielsen Utah County Shane Winters Provo MAG Staff: Shawn Seager Melanie Haws Chad Eccles Bob Allen Jill Spencer called the meeting to order at 10:01 a.m. MPO Technical Advisory Committee June 30, 2021 Page 2 Public Comment None MAG TIP Amendment – Bob Allen, MAG State Legislature directed the Transportation Commission to fund certain transportation projects using funds from HB433, SB2, and COVID Relief Funds. The following projects are considered critical to be approved which will allow UDOT to begin construction immediately: 1. Vineyard Connector; Main St to 1600 N, Vineyard $16.5M 2. Geneva Road; University Pkwy to 1800 S, Orem $20M 3. Mountain View Corridor; Porter Rockwell to 2100 North $350.6M 4. US-189; University Avenue viaduct replacement $49M The projects listed are state and federally funded, therefore MAG does not determine how they are used. Marty Beaumont moved that the TIP be amended to include the list of UDOT projects presented. Richard Nielsen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Other Business UTA: None UDOT: None The next TAC meeting is scheduled for Monday, July 26, 2021. Location TBD Mary De La Mare-Schaeffer moved to adjourn the meeting at 10:16 a.m. Jason Bond seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
