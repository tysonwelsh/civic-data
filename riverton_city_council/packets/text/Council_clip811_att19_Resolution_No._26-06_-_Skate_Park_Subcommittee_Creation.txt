                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       RESOLUTION NO. 26-06

   A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE CREATION OF A SKATE PARK SUBCOMMITTEE

         WHEREAS, the Section 3.10 of the Riverton City Council Rules of Order and procedure provide
for the creation of subcommittees, comprised of up to three Council Members, to “facilitate discussion,
draft proposed policies and make recommendations to the full Council for its consideration;” and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines the City Council should create a
subcommittee to evaluate the level of service it should continue to provide for skate boarding, use of
scooters, and the like, and to return to the Council with a recommendation as to what the City’s level of
service should be for skating-related activities in City parks, and what facilities should be installed in
support of the recommended level of service;

         NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as follows:

         1. The Riverton City Council hereby establishes a subcommittee, pursuant to Section 3.10 of the
            Riverton City Council Rules of Order and Procedure, which shall consist of three members of
            the Council, namely Councilmember _____________, _____________, and
            __________________. The Subcommittee shall consider the question of what level of
            service Riverton City should provide to skating-related activities in City Parks, and what
            skate facilities should be constructed in City Parks to meet this level of service. Said
            recommendations should include cost estimates and recommendations as to the appropriate
            location(s) for skating-related park facilities.

    2.       The Riverton City Council hereby directs the City Manager to provide support to the Council
             Subcommittee by supplying, without limitation, documents such as supportive studies, and
             cost estimates, and any other supportive information required for the subcommittee to
             consider in formulating recommendations described under this Resolution.

         3. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
