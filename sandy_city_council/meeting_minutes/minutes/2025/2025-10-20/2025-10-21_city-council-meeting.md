City Council                                      Meeting Minutes                                        October 21, 2025


Tour of the Metropolitan Water District of Salt Lake and Sandy (3430 Danish Rd, Cottonwood
Heights, UT 84093)

The Tour will include:
- A presentation from MWDSLS staff regarding the background of the District's facilities and
projects.
- Tour of the Little Cottonwood Water Treatment Plant.
                        Annalee Munsey, Metropolitan Water General Manager, welcomed all in attendance. She
                        thanked the Council for coming and she introduced the MWDSLS Board and staff. Tom
                        Ward, Sandy Public Utilities, spoke about the relationship between Sandy and MWDSLS
                        board and staff. We are part of the "Metro Family" and the Council can reach out to the
                        staff any time they have questions or need information.

                        Ms. Munsey provided background and history of the Metropolitan Water District of Salt
                        Lake and Sandy. The Metropolitan Water District of Salt Lake City was created by a
                        special election held in August of 1935. The district was created to adequately provide for
                        future water supply of Salt Lake City. Ms. Munsey spoke about the major projects
                        completed by the water district. Projects include the Deer Creek Project, the
                        construction of the Salt Lake Aqueduct, construction of Deer Creek Dam and Reservoir,
                        construction of Terminal Reservoir, and the Little Cottonwood Water Treatment Plant.
                        Sandy City applied to the Board of Directors of the Metropolitan Water District of Salt
                        Lake City for annexation into the District in May of 1988 and was approved by the Metro
                        Board in January 1989. Ms. Munsey reviewed the financial impact to Sandy resulting from
                        the annexation into Metro Water, the capacity and non-capacity capital projects
                        completed, water sources and usage, and provided a ten-year look-back of water use in
                        Sandy from 2015 to 2024. She also reviewed with the Council the status of current and
                        future commitments of Sandy, proposed property tax increases in FY28 and FY31 and
                        projected water rate increases in the upcoming years. Council questions and comments
                        occurred throughout the presentation.

                        At the conclusion of the presentation, the Council toured the Metro Water Facility. The
                        Council meeting concluded at approximately 7:15 pm.
