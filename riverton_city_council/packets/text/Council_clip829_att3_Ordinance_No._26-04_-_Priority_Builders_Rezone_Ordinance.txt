                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 26-04

   AN ORDINANCE AMENDMENT TO THE GENERAL PLAN FOR AND REZONE OF
   APPROXIMATELY 0.71-ACRES LOCATED AT 1758 AND 1742 WEST 11800 SOUTH.
CHANGING THE GENERAL PLAN DESIGNATION TO HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL AND
THE ZONING TO RM-8-SD, A MULTI-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL ZONE UP TO 8 DWELLING
     UNITS PER ACRE. APPLICANT- SCOTT YERMISH OF PRIORITY BUILDERS

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map to Medium Density Residential and the Riverton
City Zoning Map to R-4 for the approximately .71-acre parcels located at approximately 1758 & 1742
West 11800 South.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map and Zoning Map
                              shall be, and hereby is, amended to reflect the described changes
                              as shown in Exhibit “A”

               Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 3rd day of March
