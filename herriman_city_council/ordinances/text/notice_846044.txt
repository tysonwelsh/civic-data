PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on July 12, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2023-13 An ordinance of the City Council of the City of Herriman, Utah amending Herriman City Code §6-1-2 (definitions), 6-5-1 (Parking prohibited in specific areas), 6-5-5 (parking prohibited when), and 7-6-6 (snow removal) relating to rules and regulations for parking and snow removal. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
