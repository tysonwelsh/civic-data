Request
 The applicant, Abi Rushing with SGA Design Group, P.C.
 representing the Walmart Real Estate Business Trust, with
 permission from the Boyer Group, is requesting a sign
 theme amendment for the signs at approximately 1000 E.
 9200 S. This request is to amend the previously approved
 sign theme to add changeable copy in the form of digital fuel
 price signs on the existing multi-tenant development signs.
 (see Exhibit A for the application materials).

Background
 The subject property is Quarry Bend Retail Development at
 approximately 1000 E. 9200 S. The original sign theme was
 approved in 2005 as part of the Quarry Bend development
 site plan review.

 Walmart is located in the SD(X) zone. To the south and west
 is commercial business in the SD(X). To the north is
 apartments in the SD(X) zone. To the east is the
 Pebblebrook Golf Course in the A-1 zone and residential
 homes in the R-1-8 zone.

 This item has been noticed to property owners within 500 feet (Figure 1). A

 calls regarding the project.

Analysis
 Section 21-26-10(b) of the Sandy City land development code states that upon Planning
 Commission approval, the design and placement of on-premises signs (including any
 proposed advertising statuary signs) for developments of seven acres or larger and
 having at least 300 lineal feet of frontage may vary from the regulations set forth herein.
 The Planning Commission must determine that:
       a. The proposed sign exceptions are not in conflict with the purpose and
            intent of this chapter;
       b. The proposed signs are in architectural harmony with the development; and
       c. The proposed signs appropriately utilize those elements listed in the design                        Figure 1.
            criteria of this chapter

 Sign Design. Each sign submitted for approval shall incorporate the following elements:
      1. Architectural compatibility
      2. Size, scale, proportion (balance)
      3. Illumination
      4. Color and style
      5. Location
      6. Landscaping

 The Quarry Bend Retail Development sign theme was originally approved in 2005. The Walmart
 sign theme was also approved in 2005 and went through an amendment in 2022 for a new color
 scheme and updating signage. This sign theme amendment is to allow for changeable copy type
 signs to the retail development pylon signs. Walmart intends to use this change to allow for signs
 for the proposed gas station addition to their site.

 Proposed Signs
 The applicant is proposing to add changeable copy to the two development pylon signs on the
 northern and southern ends of the Quarry Bend development in the form of digital fuel pricing
 on Walmart’s portion of sign. The changeable copy portion of the sign will be 6% of the total
 tenant portion of the sign. Walmart’s allotted portion of the development sign will not change.
 Sign #1 – Northern Pylon
 The proposed northern pylon sign is 5’ 3” x 10’ 2” or 53.3 square feet. The Walmart logo will be
 24.2 square feet and the digital gas pricing will be 28 square feet. The gas prices are the only portion of the sign that is
 changeable copy. The proposed changeable copy on the northern sign is 20% of the total sign.

  Sign #2 – Southern Pylon
  The proposed southern pylon sign is 5’ 3” x 10’ 2” or 53.3 square feet. The Walmart logo will be 22.7 square feet and the
  digital gas pricing will be 27.5 square feet. The gas prices are the only portion of the sign that is changeable copy. The
  proposed changeable copy on the southern sign is 20% of the total sign.

  By allowing for this change, it will open the door to allow for other tenants to add changeable copy to their portion of the
  pylon signs.

  Staff Concerns
  Staff has no concerns. The proposed alterations to the sign theme are in keeping with the current and proposed changes for
  this master planned development.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the sign theme amendment for the Quarry Bend Retail
  Development located at 1000 E. 9200 S. based on the following findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
  1. Staff finds that the proposed sign theme meets the intent of Section 21-26-10 of the Sandy City Development Code
     regarding approval by the Planning Commission for developments of seven or more acres and having more than 300
     linear feet of frontage.

  2. The proposed sign design meets the sign design elements regarding architectural compatibility, size, scale, proportion,
     illumination, color and style, and location as approved by the Planning Commission.

Conditions:
 1. That all sign installations have the proper Sandy City sign permits.

  2. That the signs installed meet all requirements set forth in the Sandy City Land Development Code and sign code.

  3. Any additions or alterations to the Sign Theme be approved by the Planning Commission prior to being installed.

  4. That no more than 50% of each tenant sign and the overall sign face area of these pylon signs be utilized for changeable
     copy type signs.
