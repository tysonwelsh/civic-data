   b. Consider approving Resolution No. 227 adopting the Final Budget for the City of West
       Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2022
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck explained that changes to the budget were a slight
decrease to low income housing and increase to adjust the Paypal Payment Performance
Incentive. Otherwise the final budget stands the same.

MOTION:       Vice Chair Green moved to approve Resolution No. 227 adopting the Final
              Budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2022

    c. Consider approving Resolution No. 229 regarding amendments to the City of West
       Jordan Redevelopment Agency Budget for Fiscal Year 2021
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck reminded council a public hearing was held on June
9, 2021 reviewing the budget amendment in detail. She reminded them that they adjusted the tax
increment to actuals, increased expenditures to record debt service for the 2019 land purchase,
and adjusted participation agreements based on their performance.

MOTION:         Board Member McConnehey moved to approve Resolution No. 229 amending
                the Annual Budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency Budget
                for Fiscal Year 2021
                Vice Chair Green seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Chair Zach Jacob                                Yes
Board Vice Chair Kelvin Green                   Yes
Board Member Chad Lamb                          Yes
Board Member Chris McConnehey                   Yes
Board Member David Pack                         Yes
Board Member Kayleen Whitelock                  Yes
Board Member Melissa Worthen                    Yes
The motion passed 7-0

   d. Consider approving Resolution No. 226 amending the bylaws of the Redevelopment
       Agency, designating a location for agency meetings, and authorizing electronic agency
       meetings
Board Member McConnehey reported that the amendment addressed a request for the Chair and
Vice Chair term expiration to be the end of a calendar year and not the end of the first year of service.

MOTION:         Board Member McConnehey moved to approve Resolution No. 226 amending
                the bylaws of the Redevelopment Agency, designating a location for agency
                meetings, and authorizing electronic agency meetings
                Board Member Green seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
