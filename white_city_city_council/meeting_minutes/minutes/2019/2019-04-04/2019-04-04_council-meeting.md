Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Officer Paula Stinson stated calls for service went up last month; however,
reports and cases have decreased. Transients have been spotted in the gully. She is trying to
involve Sandy City Police in patrolling the area and gain access to the gully. She is investigating
an incident in the gully where deer parts and a bucket full of fresh blood was located. The owners
of the house that was condemned due to meth contamination are trying to sell it. Be aware of
scams, package thefts and leaving your doors unlocked in your house and vehicles.

White City Metro Township Council Member Reports

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Council Member Cutler stated UPD has hired an outside law firm to help with the
discussions between UPD and Herriman City. Frank Nakamura is the new administer for
SLVLESA and would like to introduce himself at the Council’s next meeting. The incident involving
a police officer who hit a female in a parking lot is still being investigated. Copperton Metro
Township and Magna Metro Township now have separate precincts. Magna Metro Township’s
new building needs improvements. The use of body cameras on officers is an ongoing discussion.
The cost is high to equip each officer with a camera and storing the data. There will be a public
hearing at the Sheriff’s Office building to hear public input about body cameras.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Perry stated UFA is working on interlocal agreements with the
various jurisdictions for services. Fire school for elected officials will be held on May 24, 2019.
UFSA hired a new bond counsel and financial officers. There are a few buildings that need to be
replaced because they do not meet seismic requirements.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

              Council Member Price stated mosquitos will be worse this year because of all the
water Utah has received. She would like to include on the website, information about standing
water and other mosquito related items.
