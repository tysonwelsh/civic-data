     2. APPROVE RESOLUTION 41-2023 FOR AN INTERFUND LOAN FROM THE WEBER COUNTY
        CAPITAL PROJECTS FUND TO CULTURE PARKS AND RECREATION FOR RENOVATIONS
        OF THE OGDEN ECCLES CONFERENCE CENTER.
            Scott Parke (Comptroller): Some people may wonder why the county is lending money to itself. Counties
            have funds and each come from a different source of revenue. The Tourism, Recreation, Culture, and
            Convention (TRCC) Fund supports our Ogden Eccles Conference Center, the Ice Sheet, Golden Spike Event
            Center, and Parks and Recreation. That fund has taxes levied on restaurants, hotels, and car rentals that support
            maintenance and operation of convention facilities such as Ogden Eccles Conference Center. The
         approximately $2M leftover each year is usually not enough to cover capital expenses. Waiting 8 or 9 years
         will only see increases in final cost and we may never catch up to do these repairs. Money in the Capital
         Projects fund that is earmarked for future projects a few years off can be used now to renovate the OECC and
         have the loan paid back before the other projects begin. The line of credit would go up to $11M but we will
         only lend what they actually need. Tourism Fund will pay off with interest to Capital Fund at cheaper rate
         than an outside bank. The proposed Resolution will authorize the loan from the Capital Fund to the Parks and
         Recreation Fund with repayment pledged from the TRCC Fund at a term no longer than 10 years.
         Commissioner Harvey: Recently Standard & Poors and Moodys reflected us having two of highest bond
         ratings in the US. Does this action help us maintain these high ratings?
         Mr. Parke: Yes, the more we take care of our own financial needs internally that helps maintain our
         strongcredit rating.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 41-2023 for an Interfund Loan from the Weber County
         Capital Projects Fund to Culture Parks and Recreation for renovations of the Ogden Eccles Conference Center;
         Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
