CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2025-19

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 3.60 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE ALCOHOLIC
BEVERAGE LICENSE PROVISIONS TO CREATE A DEFINITION FOR AND ALLOW FOR
LICENSING OF ON-PREMISE BEER RECREATIONAL FACILITIES AS ALLOWED BY STATE
CODE

WHEREAS, Chapter 3.60 of the Bluffdale City Code governs the licensing and
regulation of events and businesses offering alcohol; and

WHEREAS, the City desires to amend Chapter 3.60 to establish definitions for and
allow new business uses offering on premise beer sales associated with recreational
activities in accordance with rules established by the Utah Division of Alcoholic Beverage
Services (“DABS”).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

Section1. Adoption. Ordinance 2025-19 is hereby adopted, amending Chapter
3.60 of the Bluffdale City Code as shown in Exhibit A.

Section2. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED: August 13, 2025
