     from January 2025 through January 2026.

Mayor’s Report and Questions: Mayor Hales announced that the Murray Police Department now has an online
option for filing a non-emergency police report. Instead of calling in by phone, citizens can access a link found
on the police department page of the Murray City website. He also announced an upcoming event that would
be held to recognize individuals for serving on various boards and commissions.
