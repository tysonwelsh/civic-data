A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION ZT-10-2020,
     FILED BY WEST VALLEY CITY, REQUESTING A ZONE TEXT
     CHANGE TO ALLOW OUTDOOR EVENT CENTERS IN CONJUNCTION
     WITH A REGIONAL SHOPPING MALL AS A CONDITIONAL USE IN
     THE C-2 ZONE
     Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Council Meeting scheduled February 9, 2021, in order for the City Council to hear
     and consider public comments regarding Application ZT-10-2020, filed by West
     Valley City, requesting a Zone Text Change to allow outdoor event centers in
     conjunction with a regional shopping mall as a Conditional Use in the C-2 Zone.


       Proposed Ordinance 21-04 related to the proposal to be considered by the City
       Council subsequent to the public hearing, was discussed as follows:

       ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 21-04, AMEND SECTION 7-7-110 OF THE
       WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO AMEND CERTAIN
       REQUIREMENTS APPLICABLE TO OUTDOOR EVENT CENTERS
       Steve Pastorik, CED Director, discussed proposed Ordinance 21-04 that would
       amend Section 7-7-110 of the West Valley City Municipal Code to amend certain
       requirements applicable to outdoor event centers.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

           The Valley Fair Mall has held or is planning on holding outdoor events that fall
           into one of the two following categories:

                  Third Party Vendor Events - these events are primarily hosted,
                   organized, and operated by third party vendors. Examples of these
                   events include car shows, breast cancer awareness, Bubble Bash, and
                   friends for sight.
          MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – FEBRUARY 2, 2021
                                 -3-


                       Valley Fair Mall Sponsored Community Events - these events are
                        concert series, outdoor movie nights, festivals, fairs, and multiple
                        vendor booths. These are typically between 200-500 people.

                 The mall is in the process of remodeling the outdoor plaza west of the main
                 mall building. In addition to making the plaza a more inviting place for patrons
                 to linger, the remodel would also facilitate small, outdoor events.

                 The proposed code change would allow the mall to apply for a conditional use
                 permit to receive approval to hold the outdoor, sponsored, community events
                 like concerts. Through the conditional use process, the Planning Commission
                 can impose conditions on items like hours of operation and the place of sound
                 emitting equipment to minimize impacts on adjoining properties.

             Councilmember Buhler stated that he has seen car shows and other events at the
             mall and asked if this proposal is an expansion of an existing use. Steve replied that
             those types of events are considered “Temporary Use” which would limit the
             frequency. Councilmember Buhler asked if events like the car show would need to
             apply for a conditional use now. Steve replied that those events would still apply
             for Temporary Use Permits. Councilmember Lang asked if concerts or other events
             can be held as frequently as wanted. Steve replied that the number of events, type,
             sound, etc. can be controlled at the Conditional Use phase. Councilmember Buhler
             stated that he agrees this seems really expansive, but he doesn’t see a significant
             noise problem. Wayne stated that previous events have gotten too loud during the
             week days so the City would want the ability to restrict it in some manner.
             Councilmember Buhler asked if noise can be controlled by the proposed ordinance.
             Steve replied that the ordinance would allow concerts and events would be
             restricted at the Conditional Use level. Councilmember Lang asked how many
             events the mall wants to do in a year. Steve replied that he does not have a firm
             number. Councilmember Lang expressed concern about large crowds.
             Councilmember Huynh expressed concern about a conflict with amphitheater
             events and Maverik Center events. Mayor Bigelow stated that this would not impact
             or compare with the Maverik Center or USANA but may impact Fairbourne Station
             or events at the Cultural Center.

Councilmember Christensen arrived at 4:49 PM.

             Mayor Bigelow stated that he doesn’t know that the same types of people would go
             to the same events either. Councilmember Huynh expressed concern about the size
             of events and security. Mayor Bigelow stated that security and those details would
             be worked out at the conditional use level.

ORDINANCE 21-07: AMEND SECTION 16-6-103 OF THE WEST VALLEY
MUNICIPAL CODE TO AMEND CERTAIN REQUIREMENTS APPLICABLE
TO ALARM SYSTEMS
John Evans, Fire Chief, presented proposed Ordinance 21-07 that would amend Section 16-
6-103 of the West Valley Municipal Code to amend certain requirements applicable to alarm
systems.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The City enacted this amendment prior to the promulgation of NFPA 72. The
       2019 edition of NFPA 72 establishes comprehensive code requirements for alarm
       systems and related infrastructure, which addresses the concerns that prompted
       the amendment. Therefore, the amendment is no longer necessary and alarm
       systems shall be required to meet NFPA 72 standards.
