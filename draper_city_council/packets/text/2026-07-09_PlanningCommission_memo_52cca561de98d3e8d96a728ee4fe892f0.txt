MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann D. Pickering, AICP
Date: 2026-07-09
Re: Public Meeting: Vista 11 South Subdivision Plat Amendment Request (Administrative
Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 2.36 acres located on the east side of 600 West at the Bangerter Highway
exit. The property is currently zoned TSD. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision
Plat Amendment be approved to create three new commercial lots within an existing
commercial subdivision.



The property is part of the original Vista Station Master Area Plan that was originally
approved in 2013. The site has been part of the TSD zone since 2010, and was zoned DC
(Destination Commercial) before that. In 2022, the Vista 600 West Plat Subdivision was
approved representing the initial subdivision of the property. Lot 1 of that subdivision
has been developed with Multiple-Family Housing. In early 2024, the four lot Vista 11
South Plat Subdivision was approved amending Lots 2 thru 4 of the Vista 600 West Plat.
Development is complete on Lots 5, 7 and 8 of the Vista 11 South Plat, and the applicant
now desires to subdivide Lot 5 to create individual lots for each of the three retail
buildings located in the development.


Finding for approval:
  1. There is good cause for the amendment, and no public street or municipal utility
     easement will been vacated or amended.

Finding for denial:
  1. Good cause does not exist for the amendment.
