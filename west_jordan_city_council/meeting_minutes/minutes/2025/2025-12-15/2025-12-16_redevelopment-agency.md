 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Quarterly Update and Discussion Regarding Future Use of RDA-Owned Property at
          5600 West 9000 South
          Economic Development Director David Dobbins informed the Council that the RDA owns
          27 acres at 5600 W 9000 S, which has not been actively marketed. While the City has
          received inquiries for uses such as gas stations and car washes, staff has declined those
          proposals in favor of pursuing the highest and best use, ideally through a master-
          planned development. He noted the property is zoned SC-3 and reviewed allowable
          uses, shared that Costco has been contacted but has not expressed interest in the site,
          and mentioned there has also been interest from car dealerships, which would require
          hiring a broker. Mr. Dobbins requested Council direction on whether to begin marketing
          the property now or wait until the expansion of MVC is completed to assess its impact
          on the site.

           Board Members discussed potential future uses for the property. Board Member Lamb
           asked about discussions with Peterson, noting they have expressed interest but would
           need a mater plan for the entire site. He suggested an apartment complex as a good
           option. Acting Chair Bedore suggested creative uses such as a container park or
           amusement-style attraction. Board Member Shelton favored retail uses, including a
           possible car dealership, while acknowledging Costco would be ideal but unlikely; staff
           noted RV or boat dealerships as potential options. Board Member Green supported a
           master-planned retail and entertainment district, ideally walkable and centered on arts
           and entertainment, similar to an early Jordan Landing concept. Chair Whitelock
           emphasized importance for a unique, master-developed project that would draw
           visitors to West Jordan, expressed interest in entertainment-focused uses such as a
           container park and farmers market, and noted she was not in a rush to sell the property,
