REQUEST APPROVAL FOR A FENCE HEIGHT EXEEDING 6 FEET. Applicant is
requesting a 10- foot high fence pursuant to county ordinance 10-15-9. The property will be used
for an apple tree orchard. The 10-foot fence is to prevent deer or other animals from eating the
crops. The Parcel, 3028-NS, is near New Harmony. The applicant is New Harmony Farm, LLC,
Perry Myers.

Mr. Messel explained that the applicant has started to construct and is wanting to continue to
construct a 10 foot high chain link vinyl coated fence around his property. There will be an orchard
and the reason for the fence height is to deter the deer from destroying the orchard. The applicant
has done some earth work and poured a few pads without obtaining permits from the county. Any
fence above 6 feet requires approval from the Planning Commission and is not a conditional use.

Ms. Hales stated that in order for the Planning Commission to approve the fence, the applicant must
show that the fence height does not impair the intent or purpose of the title of code. Mr. Messel
explained that the parcel is zoned as agricultural and the use fits with the zone.

Commissioner Owens inquired as to how much fence has been installed. Mr. Myers stated that %
of the fence has been installed. Commissioner Owens asked if the applicant has had any feedback
from the neighbors and the applicant stated 3 are excited and there has been 1 complaint from an
unknown source. Mr. Myers stated that there have been rumors that the fence will have sight
obscuring slats, and that he does not have any intention to install slats in the fence. Commissioner
Owens asked if the county had received any complaints and Mr. Messel stated that the county had
received complaints and have been working with the applicant to come into compliance. A “stop
work order” was given to the applicant and the county has also received letters both for and against
the fence.

Recommendation:

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request and approve the height of the
fence as proposed.

MOTION: Commissioner Hughes made a motion to APPROVE the fence height exemption.
Commissioner Owens stated he would like to see a condition that no modifications be made
to the fence and no slats. The motion did NOT PASS.

MOTION: Commissioner Todd Jones made a motion to APPROVE the height exemption as
long as the fence does not obstruct the view through the fence with slats, and with the finding
that the fence height does not impair the intent or purpose of the title of code. Commissioner
Owens SECONDED the motion. The motion PASSED unanimously.

AMENDED PLAT. Review a request for an Amended Plat for Parcels GMSR-5C-HU242-HV
through GMSR-5C-HU292-HV to provide hanger access and allow access to HOA land. The
parcels are located in the Grassy Meadows Sky Ranch Development. The applicants are Michael
Wright, Pong Leong, Andy Deeds, Roger Mielink, and Koby Jackson.

Mr. Messel explained the way the subdivision was set up and approved with a group of hangers
that could be individually purchased by property owners. When the original plat was approved, the
hangers were intended to be on the property line of the adjacent building lot. The subdivision plat
has been previously amended to allow for setback footage to build homes on the adjacent parcels.
There was an agreement made with the property owners that they would not build within 50 feet,
being that a 4 hour fire wall separation is required by building code between the hangers and the
homes. The currently proposed amendment would allow increased setbacks to build homes. Mr.
Gardner explained that even after moving the parcel lines back, the required setback will still need
to be met unless the building is built to a 4 hour fire wall. Commissioner Hughes asked how wide
the newly created strip of land would be and Mr. Messel stated that it will 8 feet which allows the
property owners newly created setback footage.

Scott explained that there is a group of 4 property owners that are requesting this amendment.
Victoria Hales explained that all the owners and the HOA need to sign the final mylar before
recording.

PDC CONCEPT PLAN. Review a request for a Planned Development for Parcels 1135-6-HV,
1135-7-HV, 1135-8-HV, 1135-9-HV, 1135-10-HV,1135-11-HV. The parcels are currently zoned
OSC-20 (Open Space Conservation 20 acre minimum lot size) and located near Kolob Terrace
Road. The applicant is Palo Holdings, LLC.

Mr. Messel gave an overview of the concept plan process and proceeded to explain the current plan
being reviewed. The parcels are located along Kolob Terrace Road across from the Under Canvas
development and access for the proposed concept plan would come through the Under Canvas
project. The proposed individual units are permanent structures.

The applicant explained that they are looking to do a 50 room boutique hotel focused on design and
natural environment with the purpose of keeping to a minimal impact to the surrounding area. The
structures proposed are single story buildings minus one common building. The property will be
accessible through an easement that goes through Under Canvas. The property is flanked by BLM
on the western side and the Moyles ranch on the other side. A fire suppression plan is currently
being planned by the applicants. A holding tank for quarter million gallons of water on site is being
proposed. The applicant explained that they are hoping to build an extension from the WCWCD,
to be on their culinary line. He also stated that the proposal is not going to be built in phases.
