                                     ORDINANCE NO. 1689

        AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER CITY FOR
        APPROXIMATELY 17.88 ACRES LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 13782 SOUTH
        300 EAST (BANGERTER CROSSROADS) FROM RA1 (RESIDENTIAL
        AGRICULTURAL) TO CR (REGIONAL COMMERCIAL), OTHERWISE KNOWN AS
        THE BANGERTER CROSSROADS ZONING MAP AMENDMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and
Zoning Map to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

      WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Zoning Map to
keep pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of
economic uses; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council finds that rezoning the subject property to CR (Regional
Commercial) is consistent with the General Plan and the concurrently adopted amendment
to the Land Use Map only if appropriate infrastructure improvements, traffic mitigation
measures, funding commitments, and other public benefits are secured through a
development agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council expects the applicant to submit and obtain approval of a
development agreement that addresses traffic impacts, roadway improvements,
infrastructure funding and construction, phasing, and other conditions prior to or
concurrently with any development on the property; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change set forth herein has been reviewed by the
Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been
held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to
the Zoning Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper
City, and the City Council has found the proposed zone change, subject to the contingency
and effectiveness provisions herein, to be in the best interest of the health, safety, and
welfare of the citizens of Draper City and consistent with the City’s General Plan.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

        Section 1.      Findings and Contingent Approval. The City Council finds that the
amendment of the official Zoning Map as described in Section 2 below is in the best interest
of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper City and is consistent with the City’s

       Section 3.     Contingent Effectiveness; Expiration if Conditions Not Met. This
ordinance shall not take effect unless and until a development agreement between Draper
City and the applicant that adequately addresses traffic mitigation, roadway improvements,
infrastructure funding and construction, phasing, and other conditions deemed necessary
by the City Council has been fully executed by all parties and recorded with the Salt Lake
County Recorder. The City Recorder is hereby instructed not to publish this ordinance until
the development agreement has been fully executed and recorded. Publication shall occur
within ten (10) calendar days after recordation.

       All activities undertaken by the applicant prior to the effective date of this ordinance
are at the sole risk of the applicant, and no vested rights shall accrue until this ordinance
becomes effective. If the development agreement has not been fully executed by all parties
and recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder on or before May 7, 2026, this ordinance
shall automatically become null and void on May 8, 2026, without further action by the City
Council, and the prior zoning designation (RA1) shall remain in full force and effect. In such
event, the City Recorder is instructed not to publish the ordinance.

      Section 4.      Expectation of Development Agreement. The City Council expects the
applicant to submit a complete development agreement application no later than December
29, 2025, to allow sufficient time for processing, any required public hearings, and City
Council consideration and approval prior to May 7, 2026.

       Section 5.    Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.
