         7 December 2023               Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 2 of 2 
Present:  Angie Zetterquist, Stephen Nelson, Kurt Bankhead, Brady Christensen, Jason Watterson, Chris 
Sands, Nate Daugs, Lane Parker, Val Jay Rigby, Nolan Gunnell, Taylor Sorensen 
5:00:00 Pm 
Light refreshments served in the Cache County Conference Room. 
Start Time: 05:30:00 
Daugs called the meeting to order and Bankhead gave the opening remarks. 
05:30:00 
Agenda and Minutes 
Parker motioned to approve the agenda and minutes from November 2, 2023; Rigby seconded; Passed 7, 
0. 
Consent Items 
#1 Otto Ranch Subdivision 1st Amendment 
Parker motioned to approve the consent agenda; Watterson seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Regular Action Items 
#2 2024 Meeting Dates and Application Deadlines 
Commissioners reviewed the meeting dates and application deadlines. 
Watterson motioned to accept the 2024 meeting dates and application deadlines; Sands seconded; 
Passed 7, 0. 
#3 Elections for 2024 Chair and Vice Chair 
Parker motioned to nominate Nathan Daugs for Chair; Rigby seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Christensen motioned to nominate Kurt Bankhead for Vice Chair; Parker seconded; Passed 7, 0. 
Gunnell thanked the Commissioners for their service. 
Nelson reviewed how the Planning Commission will be affected by the new subdivision ordinance. There 
is a possibility that the subdivision code will be tweaked again during the next legislative session. 
Adjourned.
