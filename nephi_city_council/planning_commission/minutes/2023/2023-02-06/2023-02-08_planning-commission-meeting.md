2.    Tesla Site Plan Approval

      a. Staff Presentation
      City Planner Shauna Talbot reviewed with the Commission the Tesla charging stations
      expansion site plan on the south interchange area of Nephi. The address is
      approximately 2100 S. Main, and the new stations would be on the South side of the
      existing building previously the Ideal Dairy, or JC Mickelson’s. The project will require
      an upgrade from a 500 KVA transformer to a 1500 KVA one. Also, a new 3 Phase
      sectionalizer. They are meeting the city’s requirement for a 25 feet drive-path, and to do
      so will be re-configuring an existing curbing with an electrical pole on the south side of
      the property.
      Chris Hedlund representing Tesla joined the meeting via zoom and added more
      information about the intent of the project. He stated that they do not own the building
      the charging station will be next to, but they do lease the property next to the building.
      Commissioner Brough asked Mr. Hedlund if they had considered going further west of
      the existing charging station so that the people charging their cars could use the
      bathrooms at the Texaco located near there.
      Commissioner Fran Petersen asked about upkeeping and landscaping around the
      building. Mr. Hedlund said that typically the landlord upkeeps the landscape. Ms. Talbot
      asked for a copy of the lease that Tesla has with the landlord.
      Action Item:
              Motion: To approve the Tesla site plan with the accompanying
              Development Review Committee notes.

      Motion: Cory Thomson Second: Heather Robertson Outcome: Passed Unanimously

      Chairman Ann Peterson arrived 7:20 p.m.


3.   Mountain Country Equipment Site Plan Review

       a. Staff Presentation and Commission Discussion
        Ms. Talbot presented the Mountain Country Equipment site plan submitted by Joe
        Humphrey who has requested to build an agribusiness, which is a permitted use by right
        in the ID-3 Light Industrial zone, for the relocation of his Mountain Country Equipment
        from Main Street which is a livestock equipment business model. The project will
        eventually have three buildings, including an office/sales building, a storage building,
        and a production building. Mr. Humphreys has been working with UDOT to get the
        required access approval, and with the city to get power to the location. Mr. Humphrey
        will have to bore under the highway for access to all utility infrastructure.
        Mr. Humphrey is going to have to work with the city to ensure no unacceptable level of
        chemical contaminants will be released into the sewer system. This is one item that is
        very important that is missing at this time, and will need to be addressed before approval
        should be granted.
        Ms. Talbot reviewed many items on the DRC notes. A photo metric plan still needs to be
        submitted. The DRC has calculated that he will need 22 parking spaces. Mr. Humphreys
        would prefer to install chain link fencing, but the code requires opaque fencing for the
        outside storage area. He needs to verify if there is a fire hydrant in the area and he
        needs to list the water meter size needed. Mr. Humphrey needs to clarify that he will
        have curb and gutter around the building.
        Mr. Atkinson and Commissioner Hancock felt like there were many items that still
        needed to be resolved. Commissioner Thomson was concerned about the idea if Mr.
        Humphrey decides to do powder coating in the future and what implications this would
        cause in the future.
       b. Action Item
             Motion: Table the approval of Mountain Country Equipment site plan for
             further review

        Motion: Alan Hancock Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Passed Unanimously

4.      Request by developer Ben Wheat to modify Nephi City Code

       a. Staff Presentation and Commission Discussion

       Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the Commission the background of the Roderick Enterprise
       development and the code change request by the developer Ben Wheat. This
       development has been working toward installing their infrastructure to record the
       subdivision and receive a building permit. As our current city code stands, it allows a
       developer to bond for certain components of a development to pull a building permit, but
       other components need to be installed. The code currently does allow bonding for all
       improvements (including road base) under the escrow section of the guarantee of
       improvements but still does not allow for a building permit.

        Mr. Wheat’s request was the following:
    Road base is a non-bondable item and has to be installed prior to the issuance of a building
     permit except under the following conditions:
            i. Any future roadway that does not have road base installed prior to a building
                permit issued, will not be allowed to be used for any access until road base is
                installed and signed off by the city of Nephi. In this case the developer would be
                required to block off access to the road and then be able to bond for the road
                base.

           ii. If the existing roadway is being currently used in its “AS-IS condition”, and the
               underground utilities were installed in a trench that did not disturb the existing
               infrastructure, the developer would be able to bond for that portion of the road
               base in the trench, and future road base requirements over the existing
               roadway. If access would be required over the trench to the site, road base
               would have to be installed over that portion of the trench.

   Mr. Wheat’s second request was to clarify that “electrical service” be known as the electrical
    conduit to the site.” He is referring to 11.5.1 (A)(1)(b). As numerous developments have
    been initiated over a short period of time, staff has allowed electric conduit to be put into
    place and allowed for a building permit to be issued while the actual electric service is
    waiting to be installed. This has been a flexible option that has been allowed while we are
    receiving excessive lead times and experiencing high demand for electrical services to be
    installed.

    A long discussion ensued about the road base issue.

      b. Action Item
          Motion: To deny the code change request

Motion: Heather Robertson Second: Alan Hanock Outcome: Passed Unanimously

       The Commission and Mr. Wheat discussed the idea of amending the current
       development agreement he has with the City in order to work out the road base problem.
       The Commission agreed to allow Mr. Wheat to put the conduit in to meet the obligation
       of installing a “electrical service”. The Commission directed staff to work on clarifying
       the code on what “electrical service” means.
