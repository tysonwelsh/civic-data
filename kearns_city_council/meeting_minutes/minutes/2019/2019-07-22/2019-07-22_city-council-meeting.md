Public Hearing

Ordinance – Title 12 Chapter 19.94

               THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider adopting
Title 12 and amending Chapter 19.94 of the Kearns Metro Township Municipal Code, to enact a
“Code Enforcement and Community Enhancement Program” and to update the civil penalties for
code violations.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                 A citizen asked for an explanation of the ordinance.

                Nathan Bracken stated the metro townships adopted Salt Lake County’s
ordinances upon creation. The Council is going through the ordinances to amend them to make
them more Kearns specific. The Salt Lake County code enforcement process is based on a
provision of Utah law that is more specific to counties rather than municipalities. Salt Lake County
staff sites individuals for a violation and then turns it over to the Salt Lake County District
Attorney’s (DA) Office. The DA’s Office has to obtain a court order to clean-up a property. This
process can take years and in the meantime the property owner is accruing fines every day. Some
of the violations are serious safety issues that have gone on for years. There is a provision that
authorizes every municipality to give a property owner a fair notice indicating what the problem

is, the violation code, and a timeframe to correct the violation. The amendments to the ordinance
states the property owner can fix the violation, appeal it, or the metro township fixes the violation
and bills the property owner. The Kearns community wanted an ordinance that is stronger and
more effective to fix the long-standing code violations. A new administrative abatement process
will be implemented along with a new fine schedule. The Kearns Metro Township Planning
Commission has reviewed the ordinance and made some edits, that are incorporated within the
document. The process will be more transparent and gives the Council more authority to clean
properties up without having to wait and gives the violator’s the option to file an administrative
appeal instead of going through the court process.

ORDINANCE NO. 19-07-01                                                          Date: July 22, 2019

       AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING TITLE 12 AND AMENDING CHAPTER 19.94 OF
       THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP MUNICIPAL CODE, TO ENACT A “CODE
       ENFORCEMENT AND COMMUNITY ENHANCEMENT PROGRAM” AND TO
       UPDATE THE CIVIL PENALTIES FOR CODE VIOLATIONS

      WHEREAS, the current remedies available for ordinance enforcement rely heavily on fines
and court action to resolve violations of the Metro Township Code; and

         WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-2a-414(3), the Metro Township’s code
enforcement provisions are based on Salt Lake County’s ordinances and were enacted pursuant
to Title 17, which pertains to counties, as opposed to Title 10, which governs municipalities; and

        WHEREAS, resolving code enforcement actions through the current enforcement process
is often costly, can take years to resolve, and can allow violations to exist unbated for significant
periods to time, forcing citizens to endure an unsightly and unhealthy living environment; and

        WHEREAS, fines and penalties can accrue on a daily basis while violations of the Metro
Township Code await resolution in court, and can result in substantial, cumulative fines for
relatively minor violations; and

        WHEREAS, other municipalities in Utah utilize a combination of administrative, civil, and
criminal remedies to bring about compliance with municipal ordinances, and such an approach
can bring about compliance in a reasonable amount of time and in a more cost-effective manner;
and

        WHEREAS, the Metro Township Planning and Zoning Commission (“Commission”) held
a public hearing on June 10, 2019, to review and take public comment on: (1) the proposed
adoption of a new, more comprehensive code enforcement program (the “Program”) that is based
on Title 10 of the Utah Code and would be set forth in Title 12 of the Metro Township Code; and
(2) amendments to Chapter 19.94 of the Kearns Metro Township Code to update and revise the
