                            MEMORANDUM
To:             Honorable Mayor and City Council
From:           Planning Department
Date:           September 20, 2022
Subject:        Ordinance 22-21, AFCU Rezone, 7.93 acres at 12653 South 3600 West.
                Proposed Rezone from C-PO to C-G, Rick Magness with AFCU, applicant.


On August 25, 2022, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this rezone
request. The Planning Commission voted to recommend APPROVAL of the rezone. Minutes from
that meeting are included below. The Planning Commission recommended the following motion:

I move the City Council adopt Ordinance #22-21, rezoning 7.93 acres of land located at 12653
South 3600 West from Commercial Professional Office to to C-G (Gateway Commercial).

Background

This is a public hearing for an application to Rezone 7.93-acres of land located at 12653 South
3600 West. The property is the southeast corner lot and has frontage on 12600 South and
3600 West. The rezone includes all, or portions of, parcels 27-32-200-072, as shown on the
attached exhibit.

The property is rectangular and roughly 466-ft wide and 750-ft deep. The applicant proposes to
put an America First Credit Union branch on the northwest corner, and the rest of the property
will be left undeveloped to be subdivided and sold at a future date.

The property is currently zoned C-PO, which is Professional Office Commercial. Several
surrounding properties are zoned RR-22. There is also a hospital, church, and school in the
near vicinity. The applicant is proposing rezone to Commercial Gateway, to expand the allowed
uses to include additional retail options. A copy of the Table of Commercial Uses chart is
included below for comparison of the zones.

The current General Plan Designation for the property and area is Professional Office
Commercia land Business Park. The proposed rezone to Commercial Gateway is consistent
with that designation.

This application was noticed on the city web-page and public noticing websites. Direct mailers
were sent to property owners within 1,000 feet. At the time of this report no written comments
had been submitted.
