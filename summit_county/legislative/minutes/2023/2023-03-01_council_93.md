# Summit County Council — County Council
**2023-03-01**  ·  clip 93  ·  source: Granicus MinutesViewer

SUMMIT COUNTY

ECKER HILL MIDDLE SCHOOL AUDITORIUM

WEDNESDAY, MARCH 1, 2023
Meeting also conducted via Zoom.
The Council was called to order at the Richins Building for closed session. (2:27 P.M.)
Tonja Hanson made a motion to enter closed session . Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Closed Session (2:27 P.M.)
a.
Property Acquisition
The Summit County Council met in closed session from 2:27 P.M. to 3:29 P.M. to discuss property acquisition. The following people were present: Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Robinson, Hanson, and Harte; Attorney Margaret Olson, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Executive Secretary Annette Singleton, Open Space Manager Jess Kirby, Summit Land Conservancy Vice President of Conservation and Counsel Kate Sattelmeier.
Chris Robinson made a motion to leave closed session to discuss property acquisition and enter closed session to discuss litigation Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
b.
Litigation
The Summit County Council met in closed session from 3:29 P.M. to 4:17 P.M. to discuss litigation. The following people were present: Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Robinson, Hanson, and Harte; Attorney Margaret Olson, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas, Manager Shayne Scott, Deputy Manager Janna Young, Executive Secretary Annette Singleton.
Malena Stevens made a motion to leave closed session to discuss litigation and convene in open session Chris Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Council traveled to Ecker Hill Middle School to continue the meeting.
