 2. DISCUSSION TOPICS
       a. Discussion of proposed Title 2 amendments “Executive Branch Committees”
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson reminded the Council of direction given at the
          meeting on November 8, 2023 for proposed amendments regarding Executive Branch
          Committees. He outlined prepared amendments to West Jordan City Code Title 2
          Executive Branch Committees.

          Council Member Green suggested section 2-1-3 Definitions should be moved to 2-1-1 of
          Title 2, and suggested many lines of code in section 2-1-1, 2-1-2, and 2-1-4 could be
          eliminated if terms were defined correctly. He requested amending the following sections
          of codes:
              • 2-1-4 Term Limits; suggesting the Mayor could define terms in an executive order
                  and eliminate 28-35.
                     o Statutory Committee terms can remain three years and add ‘or when they
                         no longer reside in the city’.
              • Line 75 should say ‘Mayor shall fill vacancies with advice and consent of city
                  council for the unexpired term.
              • 2-1-6 Qualifications; Residency Requirements – suggesting it say Statutory
                  Committees must meet the following requirements.
              • 2-1-7 Committee Size – suggesting the section could be eliminated by letting the
                  Mayor decide the size of the committee.

          Council Member Green proposed changes to the title that were not part of the packet
          noting he would prefer to amend the entire title. Council Members Whitelock, Bedore, and
          Jacob agreed. Council Member Green continued with proposed changes as follows:

          Council Member Green referenced lines 167-170 regarding Planning Commission term
          limits. He preferred maintaining a limit of two terms. Council Member Shelton believed
          there were extremely capable individuals on the Planning Commission and felt it would
          be sad to require them to leave. Council Member Bloom commented that the City
          continued to evolve, as should the Planning Commission. Council Member Whitelock
          believed others should have an opportunity to serve. Chair Jacob preferred to defer the
          decision to the Mayor.

          The Council discussed Planning Commission terms. Chair Jacob said he was comfortable
          with no term limits for the Planning Commission. Council Member Bedore suggested a
          waiver was the same as not having a limit. Mayor Burton expressed the opinion that if
          someone was serving well, he would like the option to extend the time of service. Chair
          Jacob suggested a 2-term limit, plus a provision to waive the term limit with some type of
          standard or review. A majority of the Council expressed support.

          Council Member Green believed a statutory majority should be required for Board of
          Adjustment decisions. At least four Council Members expressed agreement. Council
          Member Green suggested Title 10 needed to be cleaned up, with the Board of Building
          Appeals moved to Title 2. A majority of the Council expressed agreement.

          City Administrator Korban Lee asked for confirmation regarding approval of broad
          elimination of references to executive committees in Code, and retention of references to
          statutory committees. A majority of the Council indicated support.

          Council Member Whitelock suggested changing “Council Office Director” to “Council
          Office” on Line 541. Council Member Green pointed out an additional location for the
          same change. Council Member Whitelock expressed the opinion that the Council should
          receive an annual report from groups who annually receive money from the City. Council
          Member Green agreed with requiring an annual report from groups who received cash,
          but suggested removing “shall”, and adding language that such a requirement would be
          done by resolution or contract. The Council discussed preferred language, with staff
          feedback, and requested a review of directed changes at a future meeting.


       b. Ordinance No. 24-11 amending West Jordan City Code Title 1, Chapters 4, 6, 9 and 15
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson said the Council asked at the January 10, 2024
          meeting to schedule further discussion of Title 1. Mr. Anderson presented proposed
          amendments to Title 1 that primarily dealt with the City Council, including compensation,
          duties, leadership, and staff, and received feedback from the Council. The Council asked
          Mr. Anderson to make the changes discussed and include the ordinance on a future
          Council meeting agenda for review and approval.

       d. Discussion of amendments to Title 13 Chapter 6H Senior Housing Overlay (SHO) Zone
          Council Member Bloom referred to a recent request considered by the Council, and said
          she anticipated more developers would approach the City Council about removing the
          Senior Housing Overlay (SHO) Zone. She suggested the Council establish a clear
          procedure. Council Member Whitelock suggested the zoning should revert back to the
          previous zone if an SHO were removed. Council Member Green responded that her
          suggestion would involve complications. Council Member Lamb said the situation had
          happened before, and a procedure was already in place. Council Member Bloom said she
          did not want the City to be taken advantage of.

          Community Development Director Scott Langford advised the Council not to be too
          focused on the SHO, and pointed out that discretion for development plans was still with
          the Council. Council Member Whitelock said she wanted to establish more guidelines, so
          developers knew what to expect. The Council discussed possible development scenarios,
          as well as development situations with which they had experience. Council Member
          Whitelock said she believed the current process was a problem, and suggested discussion
          of possible zoning changes should be brought to the Council as a whole for discussion.
          Council Members Bedore and Bloom expressed agreement. Council Member Whitelock
          suggested more collaboration at the front end.

          Mr. Langford said staff were working on language that would require Land Use Map
          amendments to come to the Committee of the Whole before going through the rest of the
          process. He asked if the Council would like major zoning variation requests to follow the
          same process. Council Members expressed support. Council Member Green said
          developers should come up with a completed application and a rough draft of what they
          would be willing to give up in exchange for the requested zoning change.

          Council Member Green suggested that denial of a major zoning change could be done
          “with prejudice” or “without prejudice”. A denial with prejudice would require a specified
          waiting period before reapplication (perhaps 12 months); denial without prejudice
          would allow reapplication within a shorter time period. Council Members Bedore, Bloom,
          and Jacob expressed support. Chair Jacob pointed out the Council had already done
          something similar. City Attorney Josh Chandler advised the Council to make sure policy
          was applied consistently.

          The Council discussed the new room for work sessions in City Hall.
