1. Discussion regarding a proposed Plat Amendment involving Lots 24 and 25 of the 
Park City Business Center – Sean Lewis, County Planner  
 
Planner Sean Lewis said Soren Urry and his team are present to discuss Lots 24 and 25 
of the Park City Business Center.  His team included Jill Jones and Justin Heppler who 
are the architects.  In January 2017, a work session was held on a plat amendment 
involving these lots.   
 
Planner Lewis said the Commission had concerns about the potential condominium 
units and accessory dwellings in the units.  The worry was centered on what would 
happen if the plat were approved and the units were not filled.  He said the applicant 
has decided not to pursue the accessory dwelling units within the structure.  The CC&Rs 
would state no accessory dwellings would be allowed within the development.  The 
number of units has been reduced from 19 to 16 units.   
 
Planner Lewis said in order to show good-cause, the applicant submitted a possible site 
plan of what the two parcels may look like if not combined.  Commissioner 
Klingenstein asked if they would condominiumize if they went with something like 
this.  Would the units be for sale or rent?  Mr. Heppler said the size of the space is larger, 
so it would not be conducive to a small business owner.

Planner Lewis showed a slide demonstrating what the site plan might look like.  The 
traffic flow was shown, along with the elevation of the structures. Planner Lewis added 
these are not relevant at this time because this meeting is about the plat amendment.   
Planner Lewis said that he received a phone call from Commissioner DeFord who was 
unable to attend this meeting.  He said they may want to consider what might make the 
site plan better.  This may lead to a finding of good-cause for the plat amendment.  As a 
result of this request, the applicant submitted a possible plan for illustrative purposes 
on how the site may work without the plat amendment.  There would be a shared 
driveway and parking in the front of the structures.  The square footage of the buildings 
would be very similar to the proposed plan. 
 
Planner Lewis ended by reminding the Commission that tonight’s application is for a 
plat amendment that would allow a lot combination.  If approved, a structure could be 
built across the property line.  With the plat amendment, what they are looking for is 
good cause and no increase of density.  Commissioner DeFord suggested earlier that 
the good cause might be the better site circulation.   
 
Ms. Jones clarified that the density has been reduced by 24% from their original 
proposal.  They have also reduced the number of units.  The biggest of several reasons 
to combine the lots is to reduce the scale.  Instead of two large, bulky buildings, they 
will be able to break it down into a more reasonable scale.  A third building will help 
break up the parking area.   
 
Commissioner Klingenstein asked what happened to the previously proposed 
accessory dwelling units.  Mr. Urry said at the last meeting the Commission expressed 
concerns with the accessory dwelling units.  They chose to respond and take the 
direction given.  Planner Lewis said the discussion on accessory dwellings at the last 
meeting seemed to overshadow the discussion on the plat amendment.
