This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are
based on reasonably debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a
recommendation to the City Council which has final decision authority for rezone applications.


Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-22-4004, for
changing the General Plan Designation and Zoning of 3.25-acres located near 11944 South
3600 West, thus changing the General Plan Designation to Medium-high Density Residential
and changing the zoning to RM-6.

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-22-4004, for
changing the General Plan Designation and Zoning of 3.25-acres located near 11944 South
3600 West, thus keeping the property designated as “Low Density Residential in the General
Plan and Zoned R-3.



Background
This is a public hearing for a General Plan Amendment and Rezone application brought by the
applicant for three adjoining parcels totaling 3.25-acres located near 11944 South 3600 West.

The subject property sits on the west side of 3600 West on the north bank of Midas Creek. To
the north of this property is the recently approved Ivory development “Harvest Gardens”. Ivory
Homes rezoned the property that makes up Harvest Gardens to RM-6 SD. During the rezone
hearing it was mentioned that the Kunz property may also want to develop in the near future.
Part of Ivory’s approval was to include a stub road south to Kunz property, but the Kunz
property was not a part of the Ivory rezone, or the Harvest Gardens development.

The subject property is currently zoned R-3, which is a single-family residential zone, with
14,000-sf lots. The same R-3 zoning applies to the subdivision to the west, and the next few
parcels to the south. To the north is the RM-6 SD mentioned above. To the east across 3600
West Street the Zoning is RR-22 and R-2 both single-family residential zones with large lots
over 20,000-sf.

The General Plan has three designations in the area. For properties on the east side of 3600
West the designation is “Estate Density Residential”. On the west side of 3600 West most of
the properties, including the subject properties, are designated “Low Density Residential”. The
exception is the Ivory Harvest Gardens property which is designated “Medium-High Density
Residential”.

The requested General Plan Designation of “Medium-High Density Residential” and rezone to
RM-6 would place the Kunz property in the same zoning as Harvest Gardens. Since the
Harvest Gardens property was already rezoned we can say the Council determined the RM-6
Zone is appropriate for the area. With this application the Commission and Council will
determine if the same zoning can be extended south to the north bank of Midas Creek.



Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
web-page. At the time of this report no comments had been received.

When considering rezone applications, the Planning Commission makes a recommendation to
City Council. The City Council will make a final decision on this application in a future City Council
Meeting. The City will send additional notices to property owners within 1,000 feet to announce
the City Council meeting where a final decision will be made.
