                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director presented to the Council for consideration the FY 2023-24 
                                  Tentative Budget for Sandy City and Alta Canyon Recreation District. Mayor Zoltanski 
                                  spoke about the budget process and expressed pride in the Finance and Administration 
                                  team who worked diligently on the tentative budget over the past year. She provided an 
                                  overview of the FY 2023-24 goals which includes an investment in the Fire Department 
                                  staffing and capital projects, among other goals. Mayor Zoltanski is requesting a property 
                                  tax increase to cover additional costs of the city and to fund the FY 2023-24 budget. The 
                                  average financial impact of the tax would be approximately $9 per month per household 
                                  for an averaged valued home. 

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director provided an overview of the FY 2023-24 Tentative Budget, 
                                  budget process and timeline, mission and vision statement. He reviewed the high 
                                  standard of services Sandy City offers and the Guiding Financial Principals. The City 
                                  exists to serve the needs of its citizens and strives for economy, efficiency, and 
                                  effectiveness. Mr. Kelley thanked the budget committee and finance team for their time 
                                  and dedication and work on this year's fiscal budget. Council questions and a lengthy 
                                  Council discussion followed.



                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Ryan Mecham to adopt 
                                  Resoution 23-15C, a resolution adopting Tentative Budgets for Sandy City and 
                                  Alta Canyon Recreation District for Fiscal Year 2023-2024; scheduling a public 
                                  hearing; and providing for public access to tentative budgets and schedules.

                                  A lengthy discussion followed the motion.

                                  At 7:45 pm a Call to Question was motioned to end the discussion on the current motion 
                                  on the floor and immediately take a vote on the motion.

                   Public Hearing comments opened:

                   Mr. Bruce Cline does not want to see elected officials interrupted when they are 
                   speaking. He expressed support for the new fire station and is willing to pay $9 per month 
                   for all of the upgrades. He asked that all unite and work together to make this a great city

                   Mr. Paul is supportive of paying $9 per month for the privilege of getting the services this 
                   city needs. He expressed support for taking care of our competent city staff.

                   Mr. Steve Van Maren thought the presentation was good. He is not supportive of a tax 
                   increase city-wide for the recreation center. 

                   Ms. Pat Jones thanked Brian and Administrative staff for their work on the tentative 
                   budget. She is supportive of passing the tentative budget and making changes or 
                   amendments in the upcoming meetings. She thanked the Council for all of their service. 

                   Mayor Zoltanski spoke in favor of building a new fire station 31 and it is her top priority. 
                   She is not supportive of putting an essential city function on a GO Bond. She spoke 
                   about the need for open and uninterrupted communication among the Council and 
                   Administration.

                   Ms. Sandra Haak thanked staff for their work on the tentative budget. She understands 
                   the need for a property tax increase but is not happy about a 32% property tax increase. 

                   Ms. Cathy Spuck is supportive of passing the tentative budget. This is an election year, 
                   and the citizens need to understand what the elected officials support. She agreed with 
                   earlier comments made by Bruce Cline.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      The Council meeting on May 16, 2023 is cancelled for the Police banquet.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Zach Robinson thanked Public Utilities for holding the recent water 
                      conservation event. He thanked the Police Department for coordinating the Council Tour 
                      at the police driving range. He invited the public to attend the Alta Canyon Town Hall on 
                      May 8th at the recreation center.

                      Council Member Brooke D'Sousa mentioned that 3 Sandy Youth Council members had 
                      attended this evening's meeting to participate in general citizen comment, but had to 
                      leave before citizen comment was heard by the Council. She suggested the Council take 
                      another look at the policy regarding when public comment is heard at meetings.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey thanked Fire Chief Bassett for taking them on a tour of 
                      the Fire Academy. It was a very educational day.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud thanked Chief Severson and Police for the tour last week 
                      and thanked Chief Bassett for the tour of the fire academy. The Wildland event organized 
                      by the Fire Department was very good and she thanked the Parks staff for coordinating 
                      the 5K race.

                      Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked the Fire and Police staff for helping a visitor to 
                      Sandy City last week.

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski's report was given by Shane Pace, CAO.

CAO Report

                      Shane Pace, CAO, provided an update to the flood preparations and monitoring the city 
                      crews are doing. He also spoke about moving forward with the RFP for the design of the 
                      Alta Canyon Recreation Center.

Adjournment
                      Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 10:10 pm.
