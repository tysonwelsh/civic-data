Planning Commission Public Hearings – Sept. 7

August 30, 2021

The Planning Commission will hold two public hearings on Tuesday, September 7 beginning at 6:00 pm.   The purpose of the first hearing is to review and recommend updates on associated technical amendments to Title 13, of Holladay City Cod as they relate flood and geologic hazard mapping and related study revisions by the Federal Emergency Management Agency and Utah Geologic Survey. (Ord. 13.74 and 13.75)

The purpose of the second hearing is to make recommendation on proposed amendments to Chapter 13.77, of the City Code regarding consideration of parking lot shade tree requirements and standards.  

  • Emailed comments: must be received by 5:00 pm on September 6, 2021 to the Community and Economic Development Department-jteerlink@cityofholladay.com
  • Interested parties are encouraged to watch the video stream of the meeting on the City of Holladay webpage
