Subject:                    Resolution 21-46 Amending the Draper City Police Pay Plan
Staff Presentation:         David Dobbins, City Manager

RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Resolution 21-46 amending the Draper City Police Pay Plan
Recently the Salt Lake City Police Department announced a new pay plan which had a ripple effect
throughout the state. SLCPD have 60 open positions and are actively hiring from other cities. In
response, West Valley City, West Jordan, South Jordan, South Salt Lake and other cities have already
adopted new pay plans that are equal to, and in some cases are more than, the pay plan Salt Lake City
adopted.

To be consistent with other police departments in Salt Lake County, we propose updating the police
pay plan. We have reviewed the salary ranges and step-systems of other police agencies within Salt
Lake county, and feel confident that Draper City can provide a competitive step plan to assist in
retaining our current staff and recruiting qualified and seasoned staff.

Key points
    Shortens steps in patrol from 11 to 10
    Decreases the yearly step increase from 5% to 4%
    Decreases the steps for sergeant and lieutenant

This change would be effective the pay period beginning September 19, 2021.
