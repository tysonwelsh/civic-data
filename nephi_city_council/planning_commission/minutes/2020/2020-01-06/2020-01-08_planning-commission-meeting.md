Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
                           January 8, 2020

Chairman Greenhalgh opened the meeting at 7:20 p.m.

Discussion opened regarding both public hearings held earlier in the evening involving home
occupation permits for a dog grooming business located at 763 East 500 North and a hair salon
located at 1132 South 175 East. Brent Arns motioned to approve both home occupation permits.
Karl Brough seconded. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

New members of Planning Commission Cory Thomson, Kent Heap, and Ann Peterson as an
alternate member introduced themselves and gave a small background. Chairman Greenhalgh
offered an overview of Commission job/duties.

Home Occupation Ordinance amendment has been recommended by City staff. Karl Brough called
for Public Hearing to be held February 12th, 2020. Motion was seconded by Brent Arns. Motion
passed with a unanimous vote.

Commission discussed Kallie Bowers’ RV Park and her request to remove the requirement of hard
surfacing within the park. Karl Brough would like to have a definition of ‘hard surfacing’ before a
decision is made.

An updated and current Zoning Map was viewed by the Commission. Motion was made to
recommend Nephi City Council approval of new zoning map by Karl Brough. Motion was seconded
by Kent Heap. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Commission reviewed minutes of the December 11th, 2019 meeting. Minutes were accepted on a
motion by Brent Arns and a second by Cory Thomson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Project update was given by Chairman Greenhalgh on what was previously Jones’ Subdivision.
Property has now been purchased by developer and is known as North Ridge Estates. A new final
plat is needed.

Noting no additional items of business, Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 8:14p.m.
