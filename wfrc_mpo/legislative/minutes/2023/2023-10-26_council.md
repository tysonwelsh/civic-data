 1. W
 elcome and Consent Agenda [00:00:05]
 At 2:06pm, Council Chair Mayor Dawn Ramsey welcomed the Wasatch Front Regional Council
 members and guests. Introductions were made around the table, and online via roll call. [00:04:08]
 ACTION : Mayor Ramsey brought attention to the first item on the agenda - the minutes of the August
 24, 2023 meeting and Financial statements and check registers for July and August 2023 and
 Budget/Expenditure Report to date. Mayor Bob Dandoy made a motion to approve all the documents
 as written and UTA Trustee Carlton Christensen seconded the motion. There was no discussion and
 the minutes and financial statements were approved unanimously.
 2. C
 hair Report [00:05:00]
 a. Update and preview - growth policy and legislation
 Mayor Ramsey informed the Council that with the 2024 legislative session approaching, policy
 discussions are underway, within groups such as the Unified Economic Opportunity Commission and
 various interim committees. [00:07:30] Mayor Ramsey invited Utah House Representative Calvin
 Mussleman, who also serves on WFRC Council, to provide an update on some of the
 recommendations and priorities coming out of those discussions that relate to WFRC’s work.
 [00:16:40] Mayor Ramsey then introduced Miranda Jones Cox, Government Affairs Manager at
 WFRC, who shared more information about additional policy proposals that are currently under
 consideration.
 b. ACTION: Approve WFRC’s Title VI Plan, Public Participation Plan (PPP), and Limited English
 Proficiency (LEP) Plan [00:29:28]
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Andrew Gruber, Executive Director at WFRC, to present the
 updates to the 2023 Title VI Plan and related documents. [00:30:30] Mr. Gruber introduced Lauren
 Victor, WFRC Transportation Planner, who presented a brief outline of the updates. [00:32:40]
 ACTION: Mayor Ramsey entertained a motion to approve WFRC’s updated 2023 Title VI Plan,
 updated Public Participation Plan, and updated Limited English Proficiency Plan as presented.
 Commissioner Bob Stevenson made a motion, which was seconded by Mayor Mike Caldwell. There
 was no further discussion and the vote to approve was unanimous.

 a. ACTION: Concur on the adoption of the 2023-2028 Regional Comprehensive Economic
 Development Strategy (CEDS)
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Commissioner Gage Froerer who provided an update on the
 current CEDS document.The WFEDD recently completed the 2023-2028 Regional Comprehensive
 Economic Development Strategy or CEDS. This process took seven months to complete with
 extensive outreach to stakeholders including government officials, private-sector business and
 economic development organizations, local and state government economic development staff, and
 public input. The work by WFRC and the WFEDD is meant to complement and support the work done
 by the cities and counties and other economic development organizations. [00:54:15] Commissioner

 5. R
 egional Growth Committee (RGC) [01:06:23]
 a. Utah’s Unified Transportation Plan 2023-2029
 Mayor Ramsey directed the meeting to Mayor Bob Dandoy, Chair of RGC, for an update on RGC.
 Mayor Dandoy encouraged the group to attend the upcoming Wasatch Choice Vision Workshops that
 will be held throughout the valley over the next few weeks. These meetings are hosted jointly by
 WFRC, UDOT, and UTA, and will focus on implementation of the Wasatch Choice Vision. There will
 be discussion on parks and public spaces, water, housing and active transportation, particularly
 feedback to inform UDOT’s development of the Utah Trail Network, along with the specific results for
 each workshop area that have come from the Guiding our Growth public process. [01:09:25] Mayor
 Dandoy then introduced Jory Johner, WFRC, who provided an overview of the progress and process
 of Utah’s Unified Transportation Plan.

 7. B
 udget Committee [01:37:35]
 Mayor Ramsey then brought the group’s attention to Mayor Mike Caldwell, WFRC Budget Committee
 Chair, for the WFRC Budget Committee report.
 a. ACTION: Accept the FY23 WFRC Audit Report
 Mayor Caldwell reported that the Council’s financial statements for FY23 received an unqualified
 opinion with no findings or questioned costs. A copy of the audit report was included in the meeting
 material previously provided. A summary of the audit results can be found on page 47 of the report.
 The WFRC Budget Committee met to review the audit. Marian Florence, WFRC, and our independent
 auditors from Larson Certified Public Accountants, are here, to answer questions. [01:38:55]
 ACTION: Mayor Caldwell made a motion that the Council accept the FY23 audit report.
 Commissioner Lee Perry seconded the motion and the affirmative vote was unanimous.
 b. PUBLIC HEARING and ACTION: Approve Fiscal Year 24 WFRC Budget amendments
 [01:39:55] Mayor Caldwell made a motion to open a public hearing regarding approving amendments
 to the WFRC FY24 Budget. Commissioner Bob Stevenson seconded the motion. The affirmative vote
 was unanimous. [01:40:20] Mayor Caldwell stated that each year in October WFRC amends its
 budget to reflect the final financials from the previous year, and to include projects that were budgeted
 in the previous year but were not completed prior to the end of that year and are carried forward to
 the current fiscal year. This is a routine action, which reflects that many of WFRC’s projects are
 multi-year in nature. [01:41:14] Mayor Ramsey asked if there were any comments from the public in
 attendance. Steve Van Maren, Sandy City resident, provided a comment/question about the projects
 where the funding was underspent. [01:42:07] Mayor Ramsey invited Marian Florence, WFRC Chief
 Financial Officer, to review the FY24 WFRC Budget amendments. Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive
 Director, acknowledged that WFRC staff is available to answer questions and be transparent
