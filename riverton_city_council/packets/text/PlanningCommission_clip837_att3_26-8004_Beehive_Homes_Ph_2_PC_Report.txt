                             MEMORANDUM
To:              Planning Commission
From:            Planning Department
Date:            March 26, 2026
Subject:         Beehive Homes Phase 2, PLZ-26-8004, a final commercial site plan application
                 for an assisted living center to be located at 12550 South Doreen Drive.
                 Applicant – Tim Rush


This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application, the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motions
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-26-8004 “Beehive Homes Phase 2,” approving
the site plan of Phase 2 of the commercial building located at 12550 South Doreen Drive with
the following conditions:
    1. Site Improvements and the Building, including the exterior finish, dumpster enclosures,
         and landscaping, and all other improvements shall be consistent with the plans
         presented to the Planning Commission except where otherwise noted.
    2. Applicant shall correct or resolve the remaining staff redlines.
    3. A brick wainscot shall be added to the building façade on the elevations labeled “South”
         and “West”.
    4. Site Landscaping shall include 20 trees per acre counted for the entire 1.29-acresite.
    5. Building and Site lighting shall be installed and screened according to Riverton City
         Standards.
    6. The roof top mechanical shall be screened from view.
    7. Wall mounted service panels shall be painted to match the building.
    8. The site and structures shall comply with any and all applicable Riverton City Standards
         and ordinances, including the International Building and Fire Codes.
    9. Storm drainage systems and accommodations shall comply with Riverton City Standards
         and ordinances, and with the recommendations of the Riverton City Engineering
         Division. An offsite Storm Drain Pond must be built by others prior to this site getting
         occupancy from Riverton City.
    10. The Applicant must create and agree to follow a Long-Term Storm Water Management
         Plan according to Riverton City standards and ordinances prior to receiving building
         occupancy.
    11. A Land Disturbance Permit must be issued by the City prior to any construction grading
         on the site.
    12. The applicant must obtain final approval from utility providers.

Background
This application is for a final commercial site plan for an assisted living center proposed on an
existing 1.29 acre commercial site that already has an assisted living center. This site was
originally approved in 2016 with two assisted living facilities, with the idea that buildings would
be built separately. At the time it appears that staff, the commission and the applicant all
expected both buildings to be built in roughly the same time era. The west building, sometimes
called Phase 1, was completed in 2017 and has been operating since. However, the second
building was not constructed, and the space has sat as a grass field.

Recently the applicant approached the City about constructing the east building. Because of
the length of time and a few site changes proposed by the applicant this application is coming
back to the Planning Commission for approval.

The property is located north of 12600 South on Dorren Drive. It is bounded on the north and
west by residential zoning. The line shared with the residential property is fenced with an 8-ft
masonry wall.

To the south and east is Commercial Downtown Zoning with IFA Country Store sitting east
across Doreen Drive. To the south is Nuttall’s Sewing which shares an access and parking lot
with Beehive homes. All the cross access easements and agreements were executed with the
original Beehive and/or Nuttall’s site plans.

Site improvements were installed with Phase 1, including the parking lot, fencing, utilities and
stormwater. This application proposes minor changes to the site to connect the new building
with the existing building with a breezeway.

The proposed site and building footprint meets the C-D zone for lot coverage, setbacks, parking
stalls and landscaping area. The landscape plans shown to the Commission do not show the
required amount of trees, staff has alerted the applicant in the redlines, and added a condition
that the required number of trees be met.

Fencing is in place and no additional fencing is required.

Site lighting is shown compliant with city code, and condition #3 states that lighting must be
installed per city code which includes positioning the light fixtures or using shielding to prevent
direct lighting into the adjoining residential properties.

Building design, color and materials will match the existing building. This match is important to
the applicant as the breezeway will connect the two buildings with the desired effect of making it
appear as one continuous building. The building will have a lower course of brick wainscot
and brick accent to match the existing building. It meets the following architectural standards:
        1- 2 major elements of cohesion with existing building
        2- 4-sided architecture.
        3- Uses approved materials.
        4- No more than 75% of any wall is made of the same material.
        5- Brick is included as a building material.
