PUBLIC HEARING:

  Mayor Seely called the hearing to order and stated that the purpose of the meeting was to accept
public comment on the city declaring a parcel of property known as the Greenhalgh Property surplus.
There was no public comment and Mayor Seely closed the hearing at 7:06 p.m.

Mayor Justin Seely opened the regular council meeting and welcomed all present.

PRAYER: Mayor Justin Seely

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

   Juab County Commissioners Marvin Kenison, Richard Hansen, and Clinton Painter led the pledge of
allegiance. Commissioner Painter informed the council about services that the Six County of
Governments offers our community. Commissioner Hansen informed the council about the mental
health outreach resources within the community. Commissioner Kenison stated his concern about
water conservation and said that he has been appointed to the Central Utah Water Conservancy District
Board.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Worwood moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting 4-5-22, work session held on 4-12-22, a list of claims on the
warrant register dated 4-19-22 and the sale of burial rights to Ondi and Tina Williamson, and Kyle Bell.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

  Councilor Parady reviewed business license applications for General Ops Construction, LLC and
Summit Foot and Ankle and moved to approve the licenses. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion.
The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Mr. Blaine Malquist addressed the council about how he had waited fifty years for water promised by
the Central Utah Water Conservancy District. He stated the water will never come to this valley. He
knew of other communities that had pulled out of the district and wished that Juab County had done so
year ago.

LOVELESS ESTATES SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

   City Planner Shauna Talbot presented the final plat for Loveless Estates Subdivision which is a
Planned Unit Development with seventy-five lots and 3 lots of common open space. Councilor
Worwood moved to approve the final plat for Loveless Estates Subdivision contingent on the developers
securing any necessary right-of-way, and utility easements along 200 West, the property boundary lines
cleared up, and mandatory CC&Rs and HOA set up and recorded with the subdivision. Councilor Parady
seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

BARBAROSA SUBDIVISION FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

   Ms. Talbot presented the final plat for Barbarosa Subdivision which is a two-lot subdivision. The east
lot has a proposed Wendy’s restaurant and an associated site plan submitted. The site plan was
approved at Planning Commission contingent on them providing their photometric plan, and there being
a cross-over easement between Lots 1 and 2. A cross-over easement would be needed from Lot 1 to Lot
2 since there is no direct access onto the highway for Lot 2. The cross-over easements have since been
updated on the final plat by the developer and the photometric plan had been submitted.
   Councilor Memmott moved to approve the final plat for the two-lot Barbarosa Subdivision. Councilor
Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MAYOR SEELY READ A TEXT MESSAGE ABOUT THE SURPLUS PROPERTY:

  Mayor Seely read a text message from his phone of Donni Pay stating that she was against the city
declaring the Greenhalgh Property surplus. She could not understand why the City would want to sell
property that they have control over.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Memmott reported that he and Councilor Parady and the County Commissioners had
attended a Sheep Discovery event on the west bench.
  Mayor Seely reported on the successful easter egg hunt sponsored by Nephi Recreation. He said that
over 5000 filled easter eggs were gathered. Miss Nephi royalty and the Library Teen Council had helped
the recreation department staff and wives fill the eggs.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Parady moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session to
discuss the character and competence of an individual, real property, and water shares. Councilor
Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 7:50 p.m.
