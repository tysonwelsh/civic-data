MEMO
To: City Council
From: Nicole Smedley, City Recorder
Date: 2025-12-16
Re: Approve Resolution #25-76


Comments:


Pursuant to Utah Code § 63A-19, a governmental entity is required to initiate a data
privacy program before December 31, 2025. Resolution No. 25-76 formally adopts the
Data Privacy Policy as the official policy governing the City’s handling of personal data.
The policy applies to all departments, employees, officials, and contractors.
Implementation will occur through existing departmental procedures, required training,
and coordination with IT, Records, and Legal functions. No direct fiscal impact is
anticipated.
