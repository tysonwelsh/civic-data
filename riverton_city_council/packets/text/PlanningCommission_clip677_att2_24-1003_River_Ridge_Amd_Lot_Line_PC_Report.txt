This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.

decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motion
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-24-1003 “River Ridge Lane Preliminary
Subdivision Amended”, located at 13214 and 13198 South Ariat Cove with the following
conditions:

      1. Address any outstanding Riverton City staff redline comments on the plat and plans.
      2. The applicant must obtain any necessary approvals or permits from applicable outside
         agencies or jurisdictions, including utility companies that have an interest in any
         PUE’s on the subject properties.
      3. Include a temporary turnaround on the property to meet City and Fire Department
         requirements.
      4. Final Plan and Plat to be reviewed by City Staff.
      5. Final Plat shall be approved and recorded with Salt Lake County.

 Background

This is a public hearing to amend River Ridge Lane Subdivision by combining lots 104 and 105.
This application would only adjust the common boundaries, no new lots are created and no new
improvements are required. The resulting lot and parcel meets the minimum requirements for
size and setbacks in the zone (RR-22).

River Ridge Lane is a 16-lot subdivision which was approved by the City in 2019. The
subdivision is zoned RR-22 (estate density, ½-acre lots) and the lots in the subdivision are
required to have a minimum of 21,000-sf. The properties surrounding the subdivision are also
RR-22.

The applicant, David Freiss, desires a one-acre lot. He intends to combine lots 104 and 105
(each are half-acre lots) to reach the desired size lot.

Staff from Engineering and Planning, have reviewed this proposed plat amendment and are
recommending approval with the conditions listed above in the example motion.
