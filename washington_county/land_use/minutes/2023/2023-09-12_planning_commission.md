                Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                            September 12, 2023
The Regular Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by Chairman Kevin
Jones at 1:30 p.m. on September 12, 2023, in the Washington County Commission Chambers and via Zoom, at
111 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah. In attendance; Planning Commissioners Kevin Jones, Mark
Owens, Frank Leuck, Bradley Gaston, Brandon Anderson, Todd Jones, and Brian McMullin. County Staff in
attendance were Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy Attorney; Ron
Whitehead, Public Works Director, Todd Edwards, County Engineer, Leslie Fonger, Destination Development
Manager, Emerson Rivera, Zoning & Code Compliance Specialist, and Reid Walkenhorst, Planning and Permits
Specialist.

2. PLANNED DEVELOPMENT CONCEPT PLAN (Public Meeting - Administrative). Review a
submission for a concept plan to develop parcel 3151-A-1-HV (approximately 200 acres) located
approximately at 2000 W 1200 N, Leeds, Utah 84746. The proposed concept plan puts forth a
commercial space and residential areas of varying densities. The current zone is A-20 (Agricultural 20
acre minimum lot size), proposed zone is PDC/PDR (Planned Development Commercial/Planned
Development Residential). The applicant is SmartFi Toquerville, LLC.

Mr. Messel explained the location of the parcel just North of Leeds. Mr. Messel further explained the
process for Planned Development applications starts with submitting and presenting a Concept Plan.
Mr. Messel introduced and turned the time over to Mr. Bob Hermandson from Bush and Gudgell.

Mr. Hermandson approached the podium and presented information regarding aspects of their project
and proposed Concept Plan. Discussion was had between Planning Commission members and
questions were answered as relevant to the project. A Zonda study was completed to look at the
absorption rate of different types of housing. The Zonda study showed a strong demand for attainable
housing. The applicant will move into the next phase of the approval process which is the Project Plan
and Zone Change.

3. PLANNED DEVELOPMENT CONCEPT PLAN (Public Meeting - Administrative). Review a
submission for a concept plan to develop parcels 1157-B-1-HV, 1157-C-HV, and 1156-C-HV totaling
approximately 89 acres. The proposed development will consist of two existing homes which will be
used for nightly rental, and two additional homes developed in a manner consistent with that of the
site and phasing plan, also to be single family nightly rentals. The current zone is A-20 (Agricultural
20 acre minimum lot size), proposed zone is PDC (Planned Development Commercial). The applicant
is Millstream Properties LLC.

Mr. Messel explained the location of the property and how the intended use has been revised from
their last Concept Plan review. The lodge style properties will be rented nightly as a single rental unit.
This type of rental is targeted toward family reunions or corporate gatherings where the client would
rent the entire home for the duration of the stay.

Mr. Robert Muir approached the podium and provided additional details on the property and proposed
operations. There was discussion around needing to expand turn around radius in parking areas to
accommodate fire apparatus. Occupancy is expected to 35-45 per structure, 7-9 bedrooms. The
applicant will move into the next phase of the approval process which is the Project Plan and Zone
Change.
