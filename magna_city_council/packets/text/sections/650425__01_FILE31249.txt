                                         PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant, Scott Braithwaite, is requesting a waiver from roadway improvements for curb, gutter and
sidewalk along this property frontage at 2350 South 7200 West.


                           SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The subject property is located on 7200 West, just off Highway U201. The property across 7200 West and to the
east is in West Valley City’s jurisdiction.


                                     PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
Although this property is on the north side of Highway U201 in the industrial area of Magna, Staff believes that
these required roadway improvements should be installed to start the process of getting appropriate roadway
improvements in this area.

This area is part of the proposed Utah Inland Port Authority dry port; therefore, Staff believes that roadways
improvements will be a necessity for this area as the inland port is developed.

The County Engineer’s recommendation has been attached to this Staff Report for your review and
consideration.

                             PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
The County Engineering Department is recommending that a 10-year delay agreement be granted for the
installation of curb, gutter, and sidewalk. See attached recommendation.

The MSD Planning staff recommends to the Magna Metro Mayor (or his designee) that the Exception Request
for Roadway Improvements be denied, and that the roadway improvements be installed as required.




                        PLANNING COMMISSION RECOMMENDATION

The Planning Commission recommends to the Mayor (or Mayor Designee) that the approval for the request a
waiver of roadway improvements be approved as a ten-year delay agreement (per the County Engineer’s
recommendation), and that the applicant comply with the County Engineer’s three conditions listed below:
            1. The dedication of the previously identified right-of-way area.
            2. The installation of roadway meeting the half-width requirements previously identified.
            3. The regrading of the entire right-of-way are beyond the new roadway and installation of 4”
               (min) deep gravel in anticipation of the future improvements (curb, gutter, and sidewalk, etc.)
