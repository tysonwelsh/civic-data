EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION 2021-08-01
A RESOLUTION. PURSUANT TO UTAH CODE 20A-1-206, CANCELLING
EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP'S NOVEMBER 2. 2021 LOCAL
ELECTION AND DETERMINING THE UNOPPOSED DECLARED CANDIDATES
TO BEELECTED TO THE TOWNSHIP COUNCIL.

WHEREAS the Emigration Canyon Metro Township (hereafter "ECMT”) is a metro
township under Utah Code Ann. § 10- 2a-401 et seq., and
WHEREAS ECMT is governed by a five-member elected Council, each of whom is elected at-
large under Utah Code Ann. § 10- 3-205.5, and
WHEREAS the terms of two members of the Council are set to expire at the end of 2021, and
WHEREAS, because of the expiration of terms, an election was called for November 2, 2021,
pursuant to applicable sections of the Utah Code, to fill those two positions on the Council, and
WHEREAS notice of the election was duly noticed to the public, and
WHEREAS only two candidates filed declarations of candidacy to run for the two open
positions on the Council, said candidates being ROBERT PINON and JOSEPH SMOLKA, and
WHEREAS, as of August 24, 2021, no other individual gave notice of intent to run as a write-in
candidate, pursuant to Utah Code §20A-9-601, and
WHEREAS, the deadline for write-in candidates to declare is August 27, 2021, and
WHEREAS, due to ballot printing deadline requirements for mail in ballots the determination
of whether to print ballots is needed prior to September 17, 2021, and
WHEREAS, the next regular meeting for ECMT is scheduled for September 28, 2021, and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 20A-1-206, a municipal legislative body can choose
to cancel an election where the number of declared candidates, including declared write in
candidates, do not exceed the number of open council positions subject to election, and may
certify the unopposed declared candidates to be elected to the council and

WHEREAS, a significant amount of money will be saved by ECMT by not holding a
formal election as allowed by the Utah Code, and
WHEREAS. the Emigration Canyon Metro Council wishes to inform the clerk of its
intention to cancel the election upon the expiration of the deadlines for others to declare for
|
candidacy, so ballots could be finalized, and committed to ratify the same at its next regularly
scheduled meeting. |
NOW THEREFORE, IT WAS HEREBY RESOLVED. APPROVED AND
CERTIFIED, by the Council of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township that:
1. The scheduled election for members of the Council, set for November 2, 2021 (the
"Election”) shall be cancelled if no write in candidates are submitted by August 27, 2021,
and due to the herein certified fact that in such case only two declared candidates are
running, pursuant to statutory requirements, for the two open positions on the Council that
were to be chosen by the Election
2. Effective January 1, 2021, the two unopposed candidates for the two open positions
on the Council, are hereby deemed to be elected to those positions, which unopposed
candidates are: ROBERT PINON and JOSEPH SMOLKA.
3. Council's clerk is instructed to take whatever action is required to give notice of the
cancellation of the election pursuant to Utah Code Section 20A-1-206 after August 27,
2021 if it is determined that there are no write in candidates.
APPROVED this 24"" day of August 2021.
