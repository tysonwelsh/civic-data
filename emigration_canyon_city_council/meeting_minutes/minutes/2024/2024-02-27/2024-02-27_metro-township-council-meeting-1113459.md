Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

legislative bills. Recruit camp 57 started with 34 recruits, and camp is now down to 27 recruits.

communities and stakeholders to get the plan where it is today. He reviewed recent retirements

Cameron Platt stated H.B. 35 – Metro Township Modifications, has passed.
Emigration Canyon will officially convert to a city on May 1, 2024. All ordinances and resolutions
will convert and business will continue as usual.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes asked what would happen to the clerk services.
Mr. Platt stated the city will need to hire a clerk/recorder.
Mayor Smolka stated there is a short amount of time to come up with a new city
logo and name. Does the Council have any suggestions?
Council Member Harris recommended sending a survey out to residents to get
their input on city names. A postcard will be sent to residents explaining the conversion to a city,
it would be good if it could include name options.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes suggested upgrading the logo to make it more modern. It
can be tweaked a little bit but does not need to be redone.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
H.B. 374 – County Sheriff Amendments
Cameron Platt stated separation of the Unified Police Department (UPD) and the
Sheriff’s Office is scheduled to take place on July 1, 2024. The Council should decide which law
enforcement agency it will go with in March or April.
