ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
Z-3-2020       Petition by RICHARD DEFA requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture,
               minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-8 (Single unit dwelling residential, minimum lot
               size 8,000 sq. ft.). The property is located at 3793 South 5725 West on 1.5 acres.
               (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that the proposed zone change is from A (agriculture,
               minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-8 (single unit dwelling residential, minimum lot
               size 8,000 sq. ft.). Steve stated the applicant can apply for the zone change where
               the property is less than 2 acres. Steve explained the property to the north and
               west is zoned R-1-7 and the property to the east is zoned A. The property to the
               south is currently zoned A pending approval from City Council to be rezoned RE.
               Steve presented the concept plan showing the subject property divided into 5 lots
               all above the 8,000 sq. ft. requirement. Steve stated there is a home still on the
               subject property that will be demolished. Steve noted that the applicant is aware of
               the housing standards and is willing to follow them.
GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATION
GPZ-3-2020    Petition by JOE SALISBURY requesting a General Plan change from
              Commercial/Light Manufacturing to High Density Residential (12 to 20
              units/acre) and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) and
              M (Manufacturing) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The property is
              located at 2892 S Decker Lake Dr. on 16.05 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
              963-3545)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik explained the applicant, DAI is proposing a total of 320 housing
              units made up of apartments and two different types of townhomes (front load
              and rear load). The housing units will be 2-4 bedrooms with a 2-car garage.
              Steve explained the apartments will have either tuck under garages or detached
              garages. Steve stated the applicant is not certain if townhomes will be rentals or
              for sale, however they will know by the public hearing. Steve stated DAI is also
              looking at two different apartment designs and will have a better idea of what
              design they are leaning toward prior to the public hearing.

              Steve gave a background explaining that DAI had proposed a General Plan/Zone
              change in 2013 that was denied by the Planning Commission and the City
              Council. DAI submitted a second proposal last year just prior to the City
              initiating a moratorium on all zone changes to the RM zone. DAI chose to
              withdraw that application and submitted this application.

              Steve explained the two questions that should be asked when evaluating a multi-
              home development. The first question is; Is this the correct location for an
              apartment development? Second; Is the quality in line with the City’s standards?
              Steve explained the recent change to the code allows one to apply if the property
              is ½ mile away from a rail station. The subject property is within ½ mile of two
              rail stations. Steve explained a traffic impact study is required for any
              multifamily of 100 units or more. Steve stated DAI will have this impact study
              done soon. Steve also stated the application should coincide with the overall
              goals of the City.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION
C-9-2020      Petition by Joe Wixom with FETZERS’ INC. requesting conditional use
              approval for warehouse and light industrial uses. The property is located at 4850
              W 3500 S on 3.89 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff - Brock
              Anderson at 801-963-3361)

Discussion:   Brock explained that this parcel is 7.73 acres and 3.89 acres of this parcel was
              rezoned from C-2 to M. Brock stated that Fetzers’ Inc is looking for additional
              storage space to house-built wood products for shipment. Brock stated that
              Fetzers’ will make landscaping enhancements. They will be replacing dead grass,
              adding trees to the east of the parcel, and redoing the parking lot islands with 50%
              live plant material. Brock stated that the front of the building will be a 3-tone paint
                 and the rest of the building will be 2 tone. This does comply with the original
                 development agreement.


 SUBDIVISION APPLICATION
 SA-3-2020       Petition by the Utah Department of Facilities Construction and Management
                 requesting an amendment to lot 3 in the Highbury Centre Subdivision. The
