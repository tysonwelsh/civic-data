REQUEST
Mr. Kelly Lund, owner of K. Lund Mechanical is requesting Conditional Use approval as a
"Heavy Commercial" land use (Contractor's Office and Equipment Yard) and expansion
of the building to an existing "Legal, Non-Conforming Use" located in the Research and
Development (RD) Zoning District.  The property is located at 415 W. 9800 S. Street and is the
current location of K. Lund Mechanical Contractors.  A 4,913 square foot warehouse addition is
proposed to be added to the existing office building and small warehouse. The existing use of the
property as a contractor's office, warehouse and equipment yard is currently a "Legal, Non-
Conforming Use".  A Conditional Use Permit is required for the expanded Warehouse
building and to bring the existing non-conforming use into zoning compliance.  The current

BACKGROUND
The use of the subject property as a "Heavy Commercial" land use began in 1997 when Allstate
Construction received approval from the City to build a small office and storage/warehouse
building.  The subject property was subsequently re-zoned by Sandy City to a Research and
Development (RD) zoning district (72 Acres) located along the west side of the Interstate
Highway I-15. This action of re-zoning made the Allstate Construction operation a "Legal, Non-
Conforming" land use.  As such, "Legal, Non-Conforming uses are allowed to continue, but not
allowed to expand.  The applicant in this matter purchased the property in 2016 for his mechanical
contractor business, thereby continuing the legal non-conforming use of the property.  In 2020,
the applicant approached the City for a building permit to add a warehouse addition to the existing
buildings, which was denied due to the fact that it would result in an expansion of the non-
conforming use.  Staff then worked with Mr. Lund to amend the zoning ordinance to allow the
"Heavy Commercial" land use as a Conditional Use in the RD zone, which was approved by the
City.  This was in preparation for the site plan and this conditional use review now before the
Planning Commission. (See related Site Plan Review staff report for SPR-09-20-5906 on this
same agenda).

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting.  A neighborhood meeting was not held, nor was it
required for this project, due to the project's  low visiblity location in a non-residential  area.

CONDITIONAL USE ANALYSIS
Conditional Use consideration for a "Heavy Commercial" land use in the Research and
Development (RD) Zoning District for the property located at 415 W. 9800 S. Street.

Under the Sandy City Development Code, the Planning Commission, through the Conditional
Use process, can: approve Conditional Uses if no mitigation of reasonably anticipated
detrimental impacts is necessary; approve Conditional Uses, subject to reasonable conditions
designed to mitigate or eliminate reasonably anticipated detrimental impacts; or deny
Conditional Uses, if it is determined that reasonably anticipated detrimental impacts cannot be
adequately mitigated.  The burden of mitigating these impacts or effects is the responsibility of
the applicant and not of the municipality or adjacent property owners (Section 21-33-04).

Compliance with Section 21-33-04 Conditional Use Permit:
In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions
that address these standards:

1.    Size, configuration and location of the site and the proposed site plan layout.

The layout is properly designed and located.  The site is adequately sized for the proposed new
buildings.

FINDINGS:

A.  That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer and the

    appropriate and adequate mitigation measures will be implemented or will be imposed as
    deemed necessary to minimize impacts to the surrounding business community, subject to
    the following conditions:

    Sandy City Development Code.

2.  That the Conditional Use Permit for "Heavy Commercial" land use be reviewed upon

    conditions.

3.  That the applicant be responsible to meet all provisions of the Sandy City Development

    imposed by the Planning Commission to mitigate the reasonably anticipated detrimental
