3-5-120 Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Community Engagement
Committee:

A. Creation: There is hereby created the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion
Advisory Community Engagement Committee to act as an advisory welcoming
committee on identifying issues faced by diverse groups and focused on creating
opportunities for an inclusive unified community within the city.

 B. Duties: It shall be the duty of the committee to act in a voluntary and advisory
capacity to the city regarding diversity, equality and inclusivity activities that foster
unity and belonging within the city, including, but not limited to:
    1. Act as a liaison to inform the city of issues faced by diverse our varied
communities and groups.
    2. Advocate and voice support for initiatives that cultivate and foster unity
while an inclusive culture and stimulateing socioeconomic equality for the greater
community.
    3. Create educational opportunities through guest lectures at local schools and
community workshops to raise awareness of diversity, equality and inclusivity
issues.
    4. Host events for the greater community to celebrate and educate inform the
public about various cultures and diverse groups traditions.
    5. Assist with developing resource materials related to topics of diversity,
equality, and inclusivity for the city.

   5. Host community events focusing on neighbors getting to know each other
and creating a supportive and welcoming community.

   6. Attend ribbon cuttings for new businesses in the community and help new
business owners and new residents to Draper feel invited and welcomed.
