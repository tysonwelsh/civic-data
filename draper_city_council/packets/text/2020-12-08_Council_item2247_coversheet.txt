From: Christina Oliver
CC:  Date: December 8, 2020
Re: Public Hearing - Local Consent for a Limited-Service Alcohol License for Spark Group Restaurants LLC Comments: The applicant, Sagar Srungarakavi, is requesting Local Consent for a Limited-Service Alcohol License for Spark Group Restaurants LLC DBA Paradise Biryani Pointe, located 280 E 12300 S Suite 110, Draper, Utah. The findings are as follows: The Local Consent for the Limited-Service Alcohol License meets the pedestrian pathway distance requirement (300+ feet) from a community location, the boundary distance requirement (200+ feet) from a community location, and  the provisions of Chapter 6-4 of the Draper City Municipal Code.
Draper City Municipal Code does not limit the number of Limited-Service Alcohol Licenses within City boundaries.
