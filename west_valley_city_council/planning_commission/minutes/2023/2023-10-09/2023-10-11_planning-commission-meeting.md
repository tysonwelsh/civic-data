C-24-2023
Rocky Mountain Power Lay Down Yard
5875 West 4700 South
M Zone (6.90 Acres)

The applicant, Rocky Mountain Power (RMP), is requesting conditional use approval for a
laydown storage yard as part of their public utility installation facility. This property is zoned M,
Manufacturing and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as Utility. The
surrounding zone is M, Manufacturing on all sides and the surrounding uses include Ken Garff
(NAC) to the east and south and an existing RMP substation to the west.

The following item was continued from the September 27, 2023 hearing to allow time for the
applicant to address landscaping and screening for the site. In particular, the setback and design
for the east side.

This property is located adjacent to an existing RMP substation and will be utilized as a storage
yard for utility infrastructure equipment and materials. Due to the nature of the yard the area will
be surfaced with gravel, which is typical for the utility yards and substations. The storage area
will also be screened. Since this is adjacent to 4700 South and is a high image arterial the
screening must be masonry to comply with City Code section 7-7-119. The wall will be 10’ tall
and is located behind the landscaped area on the north side. On the east side the wall will be on
the property line and continue south for 150’. The remaining 249’ of fencing along the laydown
yard will be chain link with slats.

Landscaping will be completed along the north frontage that complies with the 7-10 standards of
the City Code. There are utilities and easements throughout this area so the setback has been
increased to 41’ and therefore also satisfies the 5% minimum requirement for landscaping on the
entire site. (Approx 5.5% proposed)

The grading and drainage plans, as well as the design of the right-of-way improvements (curb,
gutter, sidewalk and streetlights, etc.), will be coordinated through West Valley City Public
Works.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well as the
following conditions:

       1. The site shall be improved and landscaped per the approved plans including
          landscaping along that frontage to comply with the 7-10 standards of the WVC
          Municipal Code and the 5% site minimum.

       2. The entire north frontage and a 150’ section along the east side of the site shall be
          screened with a masonry wall to comply with section 7-7-119 of the WVC Municipal
          Code. The remaining east portion of fencing (249’) shall be chain link with slats.
       3. All requirements of affected departments and agencies must be met, including but not
          limited to WVC Public Works and section 7-2-117.

       4. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing or to allow
for a revised site/landscape plans to be submitted.
