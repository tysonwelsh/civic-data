ORDINANCE - Reauthorizing a Cultural Arts and Recreation Enrichment (CARE) Tax,
settling an effective date for the tax, and authorizing the Mayor and/or City Manager to
provide any notice and to sign any documents required to impose the tax Presenter: Steve
Earl, City Attorney

Mr. Earl explained that this meeting is to pass an ordinance to reauthorize the CARE tax
that was voted on and approved by the Orem City voters in November 2023. This CARE tax
will be extended 10 years. This ordinance is the final step in the process of extending the CARE
tax. The City Council will need to pass this ordinance to enact the tax.

Ms. Lauret asked what the vote was for the CARE tax in 2023. Mr. Spencer and Mr.Earl
confirmed it was about 65 % yes to 35% no.

Mt. Peterson does not like taxes but he does approve this tax. He likes that those from
other cities help pay this tax when they shop in Orem. Personally, he appreciates that this money

helps arts and recreation in Orem. This helps make Orem unique.
Ms. Lauret echoed her support for the CARE tax. She believes that the arts and recreation
support Family City USA.

Mayor Young wanted to remind the citizens that in the last 2 years no new taxes were
added by the City Council. This tax is a renewal of the current CARE tax. This tax is very
effective and the funds are accountable.

Ms. Millett spoke of when she served on the CARE Commission, It was a great
opportunity for her to see where these funds go. She is excited about what these funds will be
used for going forward.

Mr. Lambson affirmed his support as well. It is a great blessing and benefit to our
citizens.

Mr. Spencer believes that the CARE tax helps to make Orem a destination city. He knows
how the CARE tax benefits the SCERA and he thinks it gives Orem a sense of community.

Ms. Spencer moved to adopt an ordinance reauthorizing a Cultural Arts and Recreation
Enrichment (CARE) Tax, settling an effective date for the tax, and authorizing the Mayor and/or
City Manager to provide any notice and to sign any documents required to impose the tax,
seconded by Mr. Peterson. Those voting yes: Debby Lauret, Jeff Lambson, David Spencer, Terry
Peterson, David Young and LaNae Millett. The motion passed.

ADJOURN MEETING

Ms. Millett moved to adjourn, seconded by Mr. Spencer. Those voting yes: David
Spencer, Debby Lauret, Jeff Lambson, Terry Peterson, David Young, and LaNae Millett.. The
motion passed.
