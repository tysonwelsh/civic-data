IN CONSIDERATION of the foregoing hereinafter set forth, it is mutually agreed by the parties as follows:

1. Riverton City will be purchasing twenty feet of land along the street frontage of 12600 South (3,330 Sf) to be
   included into the City’s Right-of-Way (ROW). Riverton City will pay the grantor $36,630 for said property.
2. The City will grant the owner up to two driveway accesses on 12600 South. The City Public Works Department
   will work with owner regarding location of driveways to insure they meet City access management
   requirements.
3. Upon execution of this agreement, Riverton City will hire a land surveyor to set survey hubs at locations of the
   new property line on owners’ property.
4. This Agreement is a complete agreement and final understanding between the parties. Other prior discussions
   representations, agreements or other considerations expressed between the parties but not made a part of this
   Agreement are not binding upon any party to this Agreement. Grantor understands this agreement is an option
   until approved by Riverton City.
