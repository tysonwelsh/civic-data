CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-5-2023 Petition by PRO DYNAMIC COLLISION CENTER requesting conditional use
         approval for Outside Storage. The storage area is located at 3270 W 2200 S on
         0.25 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-
         3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson stated that this application is a request for outside storage. He
              pointed out that this is a 0.25-acre piece of the lot, and there is outside storage to
              the north and south of the subject property. Brock explained that the Commission
              may remember an application for outside storage from a couple of years ago just
              to the south of the property. At the time, he spoke with the property owner and
              suggested that he make one request for outside storage for the entire lot. Brock
              noted that the property owner was not interested in doing that, so now individual
              tenants will continue to apply separately for outside storage on the site.
              Brock explained that the subject property has an existing chain link fence, and the
              easiest way to screen the area would be to add slats. He explained that they would
              be storing vehicles, so they would need to use pavement for surfacing. Brock
              pointed out that they would need to clean up the trash in front of the building.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
S-1-2023  Petition by ELIZABETH HERRERA is requesting approval for the Elizabeth
          Herrera Subdivision. This subdivision consists of 2 lots on 2.52 acres and will
          amend all of lots 1, 2 and 3, Block 23 Chesterfield Plat A. The subdivision is
          located at 1580 West Whitlock Ave and is zoned A (Agriculture). (Staff – Steve
          Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Lehman stated that the subject property is located on the corner of Whitlock
              Ave and Chatham St. in Chesterfield. He explained that this was a request to
              subdivide the property into two lots. Steve pointed out an existing home and barn
              on the lot, and the applicant is currently working on the barn. He said he would
              add a note to the staff report stating that they continue their work and finish the
              barn.

              Steve stated that there is some history regarding the subject property that he wants
              to review with the Commission. He explained that the previous owner, Mr.
              Graham, was interested in subdividing the subject property into four or five lots.
              As part of the subdivision, the City wanted to acquire part of the right-of-way, and
              Mr. Graham was not interested in doing that. Steve said that Mr. Graham then
              sold the property.

              Steve stated that he had attended a meeting with the Public Works Department
              recently, and the City again mentioned wanting to attain part of the right-of-way
              to make the island more of a permanent structure. He said that if that land is
              dedicated to the City, it should not interfere with the subdivision. Steve explained
              that there would be enough room to proceed with this request.

              Steve mentioned receiving a phone call from a resident wanting to know if the
              zone would change on the subject property. He told the caller that it would not
              change.

              Commissioner Woodruff said that if the applicant builds a new home on the
              second lot, it must face the east. Steve said that there is enough room for the
              property to face either to the east or to the south. Steve noted that he would talk to
              Mrs. Herrera if they had that in mind or if they were creating the lot intending to
              sell it.

              Commissioner Lovato said that the residents of Chesterfield have requested to
              keep the area more rural. He asked Steve if improvements would be part of the
              subdivision. Steve noted this right-of-way would require improvements so the
               curb, gutter, and sidewalk would be installed.


SA-1-2023      Petition by GEORGE TOMADAKIS is requesting approval for the Tomadakis
               Subdivision. This subdivision will consist of 2 lots on .45 acres and will amend
               lot 1 of the Pheasant Park Subdivision and lot 214 of the Hunter Hills No. 2
               Subdivision. The subdivision is located at 5203 Holder Drive and is zoned R-1-7
               (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 7,000 square feet) and R-1-
               8(Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff –
               Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman explained that in years past, this request would have been handled
               as a lot line adjustment and would not come before the Planning Commission.
               However, in 2021 there was a legislative change, and this application is now
               considered a plat amendment; therefore, coming before you today.

               Steve said this application involves two subdivisions: the Pheasant Park and the
               Hunter Hills #2 Subdivision. He stated that the applicant owns the two lots in
               question. Steve explained that Mr. Tomadakis is requesting to move the common
               property line seven feet to the east and possibly build a detached structure in the
               rear of lot 1.

               Commissioner Wood asked Steve to clarify the location of the subject property.
               Steve presented an aerial photo and reviewed the area with her.



There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:46 p.m.
