2.2) Public hearing and consideration of Coldwater Capital’s request for approval of the
Shiny Shell Car Wash & Grease Monkey Concept Plan located at 1053 South 1100 West
    Commissioner Ellis asked a question about traffic on 975 South.

    Applicant Mitch Morris discussed the playground and keeping kids safe in an area while
    parents vacuum their car.

    Chairman Nielsen was concerned about the safety because the site is on the opposite side. He
    was wanting to see if he could get it a little closer. Mr. Morris clarified that the vacuum stalls
    will be designated “family” next to the playground. Commissioner Newell asked a clarifying
    question about the “blow-out” lane.

    Chairman Nelson opened public hearing.

    Candice Jones- Is concerned about this commercial being built in her neighborhood.

    Wade Christiansen- He is concerned about the noise and the traffic.

    Carl Loveless- He address the noise and that the streets aren’t large and doesn’t feel there is a
    need for another carwash.

    Chairman Nelson closed public hearing.

    Mr. Morris addressed some of the concerns of the residents. He discussed the hours, reducing
    traffic, and wants to be good neighbors. He let everyone know that he had large stack so that
    they don’t feel like there will be many cars waiting on the street. He stated that the cars will
    be louder by Pioneer Crossing then by the vacuums.

    Commissioner asked clarifying questions to staff about 975. Mr. Jacob Curtis reminded that
    car wash is allowed in this zone, but that they can add stipulations.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson motioned to approve Coldwater Capital’s request for
        approval of the Shiny Shell Car Wash & Grease Monkey Concept Plan located at
        1053 South 1100 West including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Newall seconded.


        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Newell, yes. The
        motion passed unanimously.

2.7) Public hearing and recommendation of Symphony Home’s request for review of a
General Plan Amendment on 8.18-acres from Neighborhood Commercial and Very Low
Density Residential to Medium Density Residential located at approximately 2900 North
and 1200 West.
    The Applicant showed a PowerPoint presentation.

    Chairman Nelson opened public hearing.
    There was two e-comments.

    Lauren Shriner- felt that residential was a better fit here then neighborhood residential and
    would like to see this amended back to residential.

    Ryan Hill- seconded Lauren Shriner’s comments.

    Nolan Johnson- was wanting a turn around so people don’t come in his property and was
    concerned his property would be unloaded in his property.

    Tyler Anderson- they would prefer low density. Asked for a contingency for single family
    homes if a project fell through and then it’s a completely different starting point.

    Mike Shriner- agreed with Laura Shriner concerned about the problem of others taking
    advantage of medium size

    Whitney Wells wanted a contingency plan.

    Rebecca Wells- Didn’t like neighborhood and wanted contingency

    Mary wants a contingency also

    Chairman Nelson closed public hearing.

    Mr. Curtis clarified about the leeway they can place contingencies. Mr. Dinsdale stated the
    only way to reduce traffic is allowing neighborhood commercial.

    Chairman Nelson felt like there was a good argument either way. Commissioner Bunker felt
    that he would support the medium density. Commissioner Newell had concern about
    increasing density because of the financial liability. Commissioner Jackson felt like he it
    would reduce traffic. Commissioner Ellis and Hereth felt that it was rare to have a developer
    work with the neighbors.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to send a positive recommendation of Symphony
        Home’s request for review of a General Plan Amendment on 8.18-acres from
        Neighborhood Commercial and Very Low Density Residential to Medium Density
        Residential located at approximately 2900 North and 1200 West including all DRC
        comments and findings stated in the report. Stating that he felt that it met the needs of the
        area. The propose general plan amendment is consistent and that it won’t have an
        adverse effect on existing properties. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Ellis; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Newell, no. The
        motion passed.

2.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Symphony Home’s request for review of the
Lehi Terrace Concept Plan a 39-lot single family subdivision located at approximately 2900
North 1200 West.
        Chairman Nelson opened public hearing.

        Mike Shriner- felt that the developers have been very good to work with, but they were hoping
        some contingencies attached to it. Precast concrete to be a must and green space for a buffer
        from the neighborhood. He was hoping for a lot or two less by the Johnson property.

        Ryan Hill- He appreciate Symphony Homes time and effort. He was concerned that it would be
        like Ivory Homes where the driveways were too small for two cars to park so parking is more on
        the street. He also wanted a fence to deter parking.

        Ceri Curtis- She also appreciates Symphony. The one thing is that the stub road concerns. She
        wants to move 35 and 34 down so it looks more like a driveway, so it doesn’t look like a
        turnaround into Nolan Johnson’s property. Also was hoping for a few more lots for a transition.

        Whitney Wells- Wanted to agree everything that was just said.

        Chairman Nelson closed public hearing.

        Applicant address reducing numbers and the wall. He felt they are fine to include the wall as a
        condition since they are already do that. They will also make sure that the driveways can get to
        cars in the garage. As far as density goes, he wasn’t sure the best way to answer that since
        they’ve gone through several variations. They aren’t opposed to moving the properties if the city
        is good with moving the stub street.

        Mr. Dinsdale discussed the stub road, and the 2900 North does need a stub. There are a handful
        options to discourage people from going in there.

        Commissioners asked Mr. Struthers clarifying questions. Mr. Dinsdale let them know that this
        was more to help utilize the space.

        Chairman Nelson reopened public hearing.

        Nolan Johnson- He doesn’t want it to be further north. He is good with the placement. Feels that
        modify lot 34 to make it more open.

        Applicant thinks the can extend wall. As far as putting a turnaround will do what they can to
        make people aware it’s not a turnaround or driveway.

        Chairman Nelson closed public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Hereth moved to approve Symphony Home’s request for review of the
        Lehi Terrace Concept Plan a 39-lot single family subdivision located at approximately 2900
        North 1200 West. He stated that it was based on the finding that the concept meets the intent of
        the PUD overlay and adding diversity and is consistent on the general plan and is contingent on
        the general plan amendment. Also, holding the developer for the fence and wider driveways for
        two vehicles. That the city staff work with the developer to discourage people to entering Mr.

        Johnson’s property including all DRC comments, with a note that all applicable codes need to be
        adhered to with the exception of the drive aisle. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Ellis; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Newall no. The
        motion passed.
