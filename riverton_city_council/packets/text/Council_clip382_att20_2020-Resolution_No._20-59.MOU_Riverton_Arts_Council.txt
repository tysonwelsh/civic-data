                                     RIVERTON CITY
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-59

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE
    MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH RIVERTON ARTS COUNCIL


       WHEREAS, Riverton Arts Council has served the residents of Riverton City for many
years and would like to continue to grow and expand; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City plans to support the Riverton Arts Council by working with
the Riverton Arts Council to improve the facilities in which it stages performances; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it needs to secure
privately donated funds to assist with the cost to construct new performing arts facilities; and

       WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that executing a
Memorandum of Understanding with the Riverton Arts Council will enable it to obtain private
funding to upgrade and expand the facilities used by the Riverton Arts Council; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City agrees through the Memorandum of Understanding to
contribute $500,000 toward the costs of the project paid in annual installments of $100,000 per
year for the first five years, with Riverton Arts Council being responsible for the remaining
costs; and

      WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Memorandum of
Understanding is in the best interests of the citizens of Riverton City;


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:


   1. The Memorandum of Understanding, attached to this Resolution as Exhibit A is hereby
      approved. The Mayor is authorized and directed to sign a copy of the Memorandum,
      once the same has been approved as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney.

   2. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
