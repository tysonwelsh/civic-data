As a distinct property under the tax credit guidelines, Rosewood Place is required to have a 
separate annual audit completed and approved annually by the HAUC Board of 
Commissioners. The audit and tax return are presented for approval. Rosewood Place is a 
high-performing property.  The audit provided an unqualified opinion and presented no 
findings.
