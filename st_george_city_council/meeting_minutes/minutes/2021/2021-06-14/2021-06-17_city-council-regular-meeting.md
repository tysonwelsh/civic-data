PUBLIC HEARING/ADOPT FISCAL YEAR 2021-2022 BUDGET/RESOLUTION:
     Public hearing to receive input and consideration of a resolution to formally
     adopt the final Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget.

      Budget and Financial Planning Manager Deanna Brklacich noted State Law requires
      the City to have at least one public hearing to receive citizen input and a public
      hearing was held June 3, 2021, therefore this will be the second public hearing. The
      City Council also had two open work meetings to review the Preliminary budget and
      discuss changes thereto. The total budget for Fiscal Year 2021-2022 is
      $415,321,142 which is a $110 million increase from Fiscal Year 2021; the General
      Fund is $91,442,597, which is a $21 million increase from Fiscal Year 2021, most of
      which is due to capital projects and some additional personnel. Property taxes are
      not proposed to increase. She outlined the revenues the City receives from property
      taxes. She then outlined the recommended adjustments to the preliminary budget
      which will be incorporated into the final Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget if the Council
      approves of them. The Preliminary Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget was presented
      May 6, 2021 and has been available on the City's website and in the City Recorder's
      Office.

      Mayor Randall opened the public hearing. There being no comment, she closed the
      public hearing.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to approve the
                    resolution adopting the final Fiscal Year 2021-2022 Budget with the
                    adjustments as outlined.

          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Hughes.

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the zone from M-2
     (Manufacturing) to MC (Manufacturing/Commercial) on approximately
     12.52 acres located at 3287 East Desert Drive known as Quality
     Park/Millcreek Industrial Park. Case No. 2021-ZC-039

      Planner Mike Hadley presented the request to change the zone from M-2 to MC on
      approximately 12.52 acres located at 3287 East Desert Drive known as Quality
      Park/Millcreek Industrial Park. He provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the
      following topics: Aerial map; Area map; General plan map; Existing zoning map; and
      street view of the property. The Planning Commission and staff recommended
      approval. After being asked by Councilmember Larkin, Mr. Hadley noted the
      property owners can drop off the RVs, the business will take them in and store them.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted there is a lack of pedestrian infrastructure in this
      area. Staff discussed whether or not this applicant would warrant the installation of
      sidewalks; it does not. The City will look into adding pedestrian infrastructure.
      When this area was originally developed, this area was a dead-end as Riverside Drive
      was not connected.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Hughes to approve the
                    ordinance changing the zone from M-2 to MC on approximately 12.52
                    acres located at 3287 East Desert Drive known as Quality
                    Park/Millcreek Industrial Park.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.

          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye

                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye
