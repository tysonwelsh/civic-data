138   3.4) Public hearing and recommendation of the Hospital and 2100 North Station Area Plan
139   covering both future transit stations and the land with ½ mile of each station.
140
141   Brittney Harris presented the item. The locations were selected partially due to the fact that UTA
142   owns the existing tracks in the area. Two transportation studies were conducted, which
143   determined that the South Triumph Station would benefit most from a light rail system and that
144   the 2100 Station would be best implemented as a BRT (Bus Rapid Transit) station. The stations
      Lehi City Planning Commission                     3                                     March 27, 2025
145   may be connected to the Frontrunner line. As of 2020, UTA has taken public comment and
146   feedback on the stations.
147
148   The TOD (transit oriented development) zone was added to Lehi’s city code in 2012. The
149   stations are coming before the Commission now because UTA wants to ensure there will be
150   density before building the transit, while the city would prefer the lines be built before risking
151   heavy density in the area. The plan is a way to show that the city is serious about incorporating
152   public transit. The city had a lot of input in the plan and gathered its own feedback from the
153   public. They hope to bring an industrial feel to the aesthetics of the development and encourage
154   building around the hospital. They aim to create a mixed-use neighborhood with an emphasis on
155   health and wellness. The plan presented today is a visionary plan and thus has no regulatory
156   implications. It’s important to address the plan now so that the necessary zoning and regulatory
157   changes can be made in the code, but it is a guideline, not actual legislation.
158
159   Commissioner Everett asked if the reason for having one station be a light rail and the other a
160   BRT was to maintain 1200 West as a five lane arterial road. Harris explained that it was likely
161   due to the fact that the stations were determined based on two different studies, but that a third
162   study also recommended a BRT line to Provo.
