                                   KEARNS CITY COUNCIL

           RESOLUTION NO. 2024-5-3                                DATE: May 13, 2024

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE KEARNS CITY MAYOR AND CITY STAFF
 TO PREPARE RECOMMENDATIONS ON REVISED VOTING DISTRICTS AND TO
         TAKE OTHER ACTIONS NEEDED TO IMPLEMENT H.B. 35

                                            RECITALS

       WHEREAS, On January 1, 2017, Kearns incorporated as a metro township; and

      WHEREAS, during the 2024 legislative session, the Utah Legislature passed H.B. 35,
which converted the metro townships of Copperton, Emigration Canyon, Kearns, Magna, and
White City into cities and towns based on their populations effective May 1, 2024; and

        WHEREAS, among other things, H.B. 35 requires: (1) Kearns and the other former metro
townships to operate as a five-member council form of government until such time as they elect
(if they elect) to convert to a different form of municipal government; and (2) for the Mayor to
stand for election at-large at the next municipal election in 2025; and

       WHEREAS, making these changes will require the Kearns City Council (“Council”) to
reduce and revise its voting districts from five to four districts in accordance with Utah Code § 10-
3-205.5; and

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to coordinate with the Lieutenant Governor’s Office and
the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office on these changes and to authorize the Mayor and City staff
to take any other actions that may be needed to implement H.B. 35.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS CITY COUNCIL
EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

   1. The Mayor is authorized and directed to execute and send the attached letter to the
      Lieutenant Governor and the Salt Lake County Clerk.

   2. The Mayor is authorized and directed to work with City staff to prepare recommended
      revised voting districts that comply with all applicable laws, including Utah Code §
      10-3-205.5, in consultation with the Council and to present the recommended
      revisions to the Council for approval as soon as reasonably practicable.

   3. The Mayor and City staff are authorized and directed to take such other actions as
      may be needed to implement H.B. 35 in accordance with all applicable laws and
      consultation with the Council.

                               Although the metro townships were municipalities and had essentially the same
                               rights and obligations as cities and towns, their councils differed from a typical city
                               council. For a community of Kearns’s size, all five of the metro township council
                               members were elected by district and the position of mayor was appointed by the
                               other members of the Kearns Council instead of being elected by voters.

                               Under H.B. 35, Kearns now operates under a five-member form of municipal
                               government. H.B. 35 also requires the mayors of the former metro townships to
                               stand for election during the 2025 municipal election. See Utah Code § 10-1-201.5.
                               To implement to H.B. 35, Kearns will revise and reduce its voting districts from five to
                               four prior to the 2025 municipal election in accordance with Utah Code § 10-3-205.5
                               to allow the position of mayor to be elected at-large by the voters of Kearns.

                               In the meantime, please update your offices’ records to reflect Kearns’s conversion
                               to a city. Please let me know if there are any other actions that Kearns needs to take
                               in advance of the 2025 municipal election. Thank you for your assistance.
