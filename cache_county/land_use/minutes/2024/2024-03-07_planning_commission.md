#4 Pre-Application Meeting 5 
Nelson explained that this is a pre-application meeting and no decision will be made tonight. 6 
Dallin Riggs explained that he would like to build a single family home on a currently zoned RU5 parcel 7 
and that he is looking at a one lot subdivision with an agricultural remainder. He explained his concerns 8 
with the road improvement requirements and paving the road in front of the agricultural remainder. 9 
Daugs asked about where the asphalt ends. 10 
Mr. Riggs shared a picture of where the asphalt ends. 11 
Watterson asked about the length of the frontage. 12 
Mr. Riggs responded a little over 750 feet. 13 
Staff and Commission discussed the requirement to pave the whole frontage to meet fire code. 14 
Mr. Riggs explained his question is, is an agricultural remainder part of a development or not. 15 
Sands asked who would be responsible to pave the rest of the road if a property owner to the north would 16 
like to develop. 17 
Mr. Riggs responded that currently, if the property to the north developed, the developer of the north 18 
would pay for that. 19 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the road improvement requirements from the updated road manual 20 
and a possible fee in lieu for the agricultural remainder. 21 
Mr. Riggs pointed out the term development in the ordinance. 22 
Nelson reviewed the definition of development from the code.  23 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the definition of development and expanded paving allowing for 24 
more development. 25 
Mr. Riggs explained the turnaround situation that is out there currently and that the construction of an 26 
actual turnaround would be built. 27 
Commissioners discussed building in the unincorporated area and the benefit all taxpayers receive from 28 
roads built in the County. 29
