                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)

                                        Hearings began at – 6:35 p.m.
OAM2024-001179 – An application to amend Magna Code to allow drive thru windows in the
Downtown Historic District (DH) Mixed Use Zone. This proposed ordinance would also create specific
use standards and conditions for drive thru’s on Magna Main Street. Applicant: Steve Andrews and Jeff
Fisher. Planner: Brian Tucker (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the
amendments to the Downtown Historic District.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding location, the drive-thru and drive-up should be
conditional, not permitted use in historic Magna, and parking requirements.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Vanroosendaal seconded that
motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Architect works with Steve Andrews
Name: Jeff Fisher
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Fisher said currently We Witches is over 800 square feet and single apartment 800 square
feet. The proposed new facility is twice that, about 1600 square feet and two apartments above that. The
distance between the two buildings is about 6 feet and gated for privacy and private entrances with good
lighting. Insists on applicant obtaining a survey and will fit in as much parking as possible. Has a proposed
layout for the coffee shop. Stairs will be enclosed on the east side.

No one from the public was present to speak.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner VanRoosendaal seconded that
motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend file #OAM2024-001179 An application to amend Magna Code to allow drive thru
windows in the Downtown Historic District (DH) Mixed Use Zone. This proposed ordinance would also
create specific use standards and conditions for drive thru’s on Magna Main Street for approval of the
entirety of the attached withing the historic district considering amendments to the DH Zone with them as
a conditional use to the Magna City Council.
        Motion by: Commissioner VanRoosendaal
        2nd by: Commissioner Richards
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
