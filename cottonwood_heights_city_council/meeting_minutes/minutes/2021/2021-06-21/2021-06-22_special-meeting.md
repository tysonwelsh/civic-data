MINUTES OF THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL WORK
SESSION HELD TUESDAY, JUNE 22, 2022, AT 5:30 P.M. IN THE COTTONWOOD
HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL WORK ROOM LOCATED AT 2277 EAST BENGAL
BOULEVARD


Members Present: Mayor Mike Weichers, Council Member Douglas Petersen, Council
Member Scott Bracken (via Zoom), Council Member Shawn E. Newell, Council Member Ellen
Birrell

Staff Present: City Manager Tim Tingey, City Attorney Shane Topham, Records Culture and
Human Resources Director Paula Melgar, Police Chief Robby Russo, Police Lieutenant Dan
Bartlett, IT Manager Matt Ervin.


1.      WELCOME – Mayor Weichers.

Mayor Mike Weichers called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m. and welcomed those present.

2.   CLOSED MEETING TO DISCUSS LITIGATION, PROPERTY ACQUISITION
AND/OR THE CHARACTER AND PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCE OR PHYSICAL
OR MENTAL HEALTH OF AN INDIVIDUAL.

At 5:31 p.m. Council Member Shawn Newell made a motion to move to a closed meeting to
discuss litigation. The motion was seconded by Council Member Petersen. The work session was
closed to the public.

3.      ADJOURN CITY COUNCIL WORK SESSION

At 9:21 p.m. Council Member Scott Bracken made a motion to close the closed meeting, move
to the open meeting and adjourn. The motion was seconded by Council Member Doug Petersen.

The City Council Special Work Session adjourned at 9:21 p.m.
