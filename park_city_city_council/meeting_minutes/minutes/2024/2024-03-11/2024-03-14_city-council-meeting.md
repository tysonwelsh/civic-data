26    Discuss Recreation, Ice, and Golf Fee Recommendations: 
27    Ken Fisher, Recreation Manager, noted the Park City Recreation Sled Derby won the 
28    Outstanding Special Event of the Year Award from the Utah Recreation and Parks 
29   Association. He related that until 2003 there was a fee differential for those living within 
30   the City limits. When Basin Rec built their building, the fee differential was terminated. 
31   He stated having the same fees no matter which facility was used helped build 
32   community. The interlocal agreement confirmed the entities would not have a fee 
33   differential. He hoped for Council direction as a new agreement was being discussed. 
34

36   users lived in 84060. He defined “resident” as anyone living in the Park City School 
37   District boundaries and indicated some services had discounts for residents. He 
38   discussed options for resident discounts, including the addition of a new visitor rate for 
39   MARC monthly passes, a nonresident fee increase higher than the resident fee 
40    increase, expanding early access for popular programs to those residing in 84060, and

35    Discuss IT Infrastructure Initiatives: 
36    Scott Robertson, Mindy Finlinson, and Sarah Mangano were present for this item. 
37    Robertson stated Future Core would replace the current aging ERP and financial 
38    system. It would affect business processes and included extracting data from the old 
39    system, testing, verifying, training, and a lot of coordination. The process would repeat 
40    each year for the next three years as each module came online. This would ensure a 
41    better customer service experience and improve transparency. 
42     
43    Robertson stated there was a request for email accounts for part time and seasonal 
44    employees who didn’t normally get an email account when they were hired. This 
45    required licensing and an automation component that would make the City compliant.

 9    Adriane Juarez and Becca Lael, Library, and Seth Beal and Patricia Stokes, Library
