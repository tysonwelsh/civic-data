                           RESOLUTION NO. 25-67

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ACTING AS THE
       MUNICIPAL BOARD OF CANVASSERS DECLARING THE RESULTS
        OF THE NOVEMBER 4, 2025, GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

       WHEREAS, Draper City held a General Municipal Election on November
4, 2025, for the position of Mayor and one City Council positions (two-year
term); and

     WHEREAS, Utah Code Title 20A-4-301 states the mayor and the
municipal legislative body are the board of municipal canvassers for the
municipality.

      WHEREAS, Utah Code Title 20A-4-304 requires that the board of
municipal canvassers shall declare as “elected” those persons who had the
highest number of votes and shall certify the results of the election.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MUNICIPAL BOARD OF
CANVASSERS OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. The election results shown in Exhibit A are accepted,
declared, and certified to be the official canvass results for the November 4,
2025 general election for Draper City.

     Section 2. Effective Date.      This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.
