TIP Modification Vineyard Rail Realignment – Bob Allen, MAG The Vineyard Rail Consolidation Project will improve freight rail operations in Vineyard and Orem, Utah, by streamlining routes, consolidating switching, increasing safety by reducing the number of at-grade crossings, and improving overall freight operations through a fast- MPO Technical Advisory Committee February 24, 2020 Page 3 growing urban area. This project will abandon 2 miles of the Provo Industrial Lead track that currently runs along Geneva Road in Vineyard from approximately Orem Center Street to 1600 North and construct new leads from the Provo Subdivision tract that runs parallel to the Provo Industrial Lead to the west. Vineyard Local Funds 10,000,000 CRISI Federal Grant 6,839,292 Total Funds $16,839,292 Chris Trusty moved that the TIP be modified to add the Vineyard Rail Consolidation Project at $16,839,292 to the TIP. Todd Trane seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
