                                                                       MINUTES
                                                           WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                          Tuesday, November 5, 2019 - 10:00 a.m.
                                                           2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who appear and speak at a
County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy
or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

 and Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.

 B. INVOCATION - Commissioner Froerer

 D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY - Chair Jenkins

 F. PRESENTATIONS:
    1. PRESENTATION OF CHECKS FROM THE ANNUAL COMMISSIONERS’ FALL CHARITY GOLF CLASSIC-BRIDGING
       THE GAP & CYSTIC FIBROSIS

            Chair Jenkins presented a check for $7,000 to Bridging the Gap and $3,000 to Cystic Fibrosis.
            Commissioner Harvey noted that all the money was raised by private groups and individuals and does not
            come from taxpayer dollars.
       2. KICK-OFF OF ANNUAL THANKSGIVING FOOD DRIVE-TURKEYS, HAMS & ALL FOOD ITEMS ACCEPTED IN
          THE COMMISSION OFFICE BY NOVEMBER 21ST @ 10 A.M.

            Chair Jenkins announced the annual Thanksgiving food drive, noting that the community and staff can
            bring their food items to the Commission Office, which will then be given to the foodbank.

 H. ACTION ITEMS:

       1. RATIFY EXTENSION OF A LEASE AGREEMENT BY & AMONG WEBER COUNTY, SUMMERS & SUMMERS,
          LLC, & WESTERN LANDSCAPING, CO. FROM NOVEMBER 1, 2018 TO OCTOBER 31, 2019 AND EXTEND THE
          LEASE TERM FROM NOVEMBER 1, 2019 TO OCTOBER 31, 2020.

            Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Director, noted that the county leases a small piece of
            the property that it owns up at the Roads Shop in Ogden Valley on a one-year basis to Summers & Summers
            & Western Landscaping. There is a letter of agreement that is required for renewal. It was missed last
            year and is hereby being ratified. The lessee paid in full for last year. There is also a letter of extension
            ending on 10/31/2020.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to ratify the extension of a Lease Agreement by and among Weber County,
            Summers & Summers, LLC, and Western Landscaping, Co. from November 1, 2018 to October 31, 2019
            and extend the lease term from November 1, 2019 to October 31, 2020; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    2. CONTRACT WITH LIBERTY BROADBAND, LLC FOR A WIRELESS COMMUNICATIONS TOWER LEASE.

         Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Director, stated that the county currently owns a tower
         in Ogden Valley and Liberty Broadband is requesting to co-locate on that tower to place equipment to help
         with internet service in the Valley. They will pay $400/month to the county for its use, and as part of the
         revenue sharing agreement, 35% of that will go to Eden Heights, LLC, which deeded the property in 2017.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Liberty Broadband, LLC for a wireless
         communications tower lease; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

    3. FINAL SUBDIVISION APPROVAL OF THE RIDGE TOWNHOMES PRUD PHASE 4, CONSISTING OF 8 UNITS.

         Tammy Aydelotte, of the County Planning Division, stated that the original PRUD was approved in
         12/2013 with a total of 48 units in five phases. This phase consists of approximately 1½ acres in the FR-3
         Zone. There is no public road dedication; the roadways are private. This item received a final approval
         recommendation from the Planning Commission on 9/24/2019.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to grant final subdivision approval of The Ridge Townhomes PRUD Phase
         4, consisting of 8 units; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

I. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS: Commissioner Froerer noted that he and Commissioner Harvey attended the
   Ogden Valley Library open house on Saturday, stating that it is a magnificent building and brings the Valley up
   to speed with the rest of the county. He stated that Lynnda Wangsgard, County Library Director, and her staff
   deserve to be commended for bringing the project on time and on budget. Commissioner Harvey also noted
   that the commissioners attended the Monarch grand opening on Friday, which rents small spaces to local artists.

J. ADJOURN

      Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
