22          1.1     (Project CUP-22-019) Request from Logan Dance, on Behalf of Wasatch
23                  Uptracks, LLC for Conditional Use Approval of Short-Term Rental at 7430
24                  South Wasatch Boulevard #C1.
25
26   Associate City Planner, Maverick Yeh presented the Staff Report and stated that the above item is a
27   request for Conditional Use approval of a Short-Term Rental located at 7430 South Wasatch
28   Boulevard #C1. The subject property is located in an R-2-8 Multi-family Residential Zone where
29   short-term rentals may be approved as a Conditional Use provided the property is part of a Planned
30   Unit Development (“PDD”) or a condominium project that contains a minimum of eight units and
31   fronts on a private street. The subject property is part of the Canyons Racquet Club project containing
32   40 units. Staff determined that the request meets the standard for the Short-Term Rental requirements
33   as outlined in the City Code. Staff recommended approval with the conditions set forth in the Staff
34   Report.
35
36   Mr. Johnson opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing was
37   closed.

15          1.2     (Project CUP-22-020) Request from Peter Hughes, on Behalf of Talon Real Estate
16                  for Conditional Use Approval of Short-Term Rental at 3596 East 9050 South.
17
18   Mr. Yeh presented the Staff Report and stated that the above item is a request for Conditional Use
19   approval of a short-term rental located at 3596 East 9050 South. The property is located in the R-2-
20   8 Multi-Family Residential Zone where short-term rentals may be approved as a Conditional Use
21   provided the property is part of a Planned Unit Development (“PDD”) or a condominium project that
22   contains a minimum of eight units and fronts on a private street. The subject property is part of the
23   Canyons Place Planned Unit Development (“PUD”) that contains 24 units and fronts along a private
24   street. Staff found this request to meet the standard for the Short-Term Rental requirements as
25   outlined in the City Code. Staff recommended approval with conditions listed in the Staff Report.
