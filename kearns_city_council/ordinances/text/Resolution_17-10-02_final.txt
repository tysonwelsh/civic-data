KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AUTHORIZING THE
FEE INCREASE PROPOSED BY THE WASATCH FRONT WASTE AND RECYCLING
DISTRICT TO COVER THE ONGOING COSTS OF OPERATION AND PROVISION OF
GARBAGE COLLECTION, DISPOSAL, AND RECYCLING SERVICES
RECITALS
A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah Code
Ann. (““UCA”) §§10-2a-401 ef seq.
B. The Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (“WFWRD”) is a special
service district established to provide garbage collection and disposal pursuant to
UCA § 17D-1-201 ef al.
C. WEWRD has proposed the following fee increase for implementation as a part of the FY
2018 to cover the ongoing operation and provision of garbage collection and disposal, and
recycling services:
Increasing the Basic Residential Service fee from $14.75 a month to $17.00 a
month;
Increasing the extra can fee from 15.00 a month to $17.00 a month for more
than one garbage can; and
Adding a new fee of $3.00 a month for each additional recycling can.
D. The Kearns Metro Township Council recognizes the benefits of the services
receive from WFWRD and wishes those services to continue at the current levels
going forward.
E. The Kearns Metro Township Council believes approving this fee increase for
WEWRD in in the best interest of the Kearns residents.

RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township
Council:

The Kearns Metro Township Council approves the proposed FY 2018 WFWRD fee
increase and directs its Trustee to vote in favor of the increase to maintain the current levels of
service being provided to Kearns by WFWRD.
