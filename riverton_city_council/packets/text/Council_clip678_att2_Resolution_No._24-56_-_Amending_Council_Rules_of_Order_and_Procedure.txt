                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-56

      A RESOLUTION APPROVING AMENDMENTS TO THE RIVERTON CITY
               COUNCIL RULES OF ORDER AND PROCEDURE

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-606, the Riverton City Council requires that
it maintain Rules of Order and Procedure to govern the method by which the Council conducts
public meetings, with particular focus upon parliamentary procedure, ethical behavior, and civil
discourse; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council Rules of Order and Procedure, adopted in
compliance with Utah Code §10-3-606, is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in
law or in best practices of administering governmental operations may arise; and

        WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the Rules of Order and Procedure and determined
some terms need to be amended to: 1) match changes in statutory law for public noticing
requirements; 2) update budgeting procedures for training; 3) update submittal requirements for
the council meeting agenda; 4) update requirements for keeping minutes of meetings; 5) set
limits in amount of time devoted to general citizen comment; and make related changes; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the amendments
offered to the Council by City staff will improve efficiency in government while maintaining
transparency in the conduct of its meetings; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City to adopt
amendments to the Riverton City Rules of Order and Procedure as provided by City staff.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council Rules of Order and Procedure, attached to this Resolution
          is hereby amended as provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
