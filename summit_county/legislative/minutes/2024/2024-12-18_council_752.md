Council Chair Stevens opened the meeting for discussion and approval of Ordinance No. 987.
Dave Thomas, Civil Chief Deputy Attorney, addressed the Council and provided an update on the amended development agreement.
Marc Stanworth, Dakota Pacific Real Estate CEO, expressed his appreciation to the County and staff for the time and detail spent on the agreement.
Steve Borup, Dakota Pacific Real Estate Commercial Development Director, added brief additional remarks and thanks.
Council Member Robinson provided his thoughts on the amended development agreement.
Council Member Hanson provided her thoughts on the amended development agreement.
Council Member Harte provided his thoughts on the amended development agreement.
Council Member Armstrong provided his thoughts on the amended development agreement.
Council Chair Stevens provided her thoughts on the amended development agreement.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to approve Ordinance No. 987, an Ordinance Approving and Adopting the Amended and Restated Development Agreement for the Park City Junction formerly known as Summit Research Park with the changes that Dave Thomas indicated at the beginning of the meeting.
