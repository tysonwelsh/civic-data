Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Project Selection Process – 2020 Project List Approval - Bob Allen, MAG The MPO funds projects in the TIP every two years. The Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) and Regional Planning Committee (RPC) created a final prioritized project list September 3, 2020. Approximately $85M in funds are primarily available in 2023-2024. The scenario includes an additional $20M to be added to the contingency fund due to rising construction costs. RPC must approve the funding scenario for the final prioritized project list and add those projects to the TIP. MPO staff have created a funding scenario and presented it to the Committee. TAC recommended the final funding scenario be approved as presented and the projects be added to the TIP Mayor Mann moved that the final funding scenario be approved as presented and the funded projects be added to the TIP. Mayor Staheli seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MAG TIP Modification Elk Ridge Drive/SR164 Intersection Improvement – Bob Allen, MAG Elk Ridge Drive is an existing TIP project that connects SR-198 and SR-164. UDOT has participated in the project by funding traffic signals on both ends of the project. It was determined that an additional northbound left turn lane is needed at the intersection of Elk Ridge Drive and SR-164. This will require an additional westbound receiving lane on SR-164 between the intersection and an additional northbound left turn lane. UDOT Region 3 proposes MPO Regional Planning Committee October 1, 2020 Page 3 to add $700,000 of funding to install the additional turning and receiving lanes. The additional funding is UDOT Transportation Solutions funds that MAG does not control. Current Project Value $17,269,050 New UDOT Transportation Solutions Funds $700,000 New Project Value $17,969,050 Mayor Fugal moved the TIP be modified to add $700,000 UDOT funds to the Elk Ridge Drive project. Mayor Johnson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Major Investments Studies Update – Shawn Eliot, MAG The Utah County area population is projected to reach one million in population by 2040. To better understand the growing needs of the area, UDOT, UTA and Local Governments have acquired the services of various consulting agencies to conduct Major Investment Studies. A brief update was given on the following studies: a. Provo to Santaquin High Capacity Transit Analysis – Claire Woodman, Parametrix Consultant b. Central Corridor Transit Study – Claire Woodman, Parametrix Consultant c. Point of the Mountain Transit Study – Claire Woodman, Parametrix Consultant d. North Lakeshore Study - Kim Clark, VIA Consultant e. Eagle Mountain & Saratoga Springs Transit Service Study – A.T. Stoddard, LSC Transportation Consultants, Inc. Updates Federal Highway Administration: FHA received a continuing resolution for one more year of Fast Act funding. Funding will be at the same levels as the previous year. Utah Department of Transportation: The I-15 Tech Corridor project is in the final stages and expected to be substantially completed by Thanksgiving. A virtual tour will be given at the Transportation Commission meeting on October 9, 2020. A ribbon cutting event is set to take place end of October. Utah Transit Authority: An emphasis was put on the importance of the transit studies being done. Transit services being proposed from the point of the mountain to Santaquin are anticipated to run the same length as the current FrontRunner system. Additional input is encouraged in the North Lakeshore Study. Ridership on all modes of transit continues to increase.
