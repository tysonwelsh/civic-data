Chapter 2.56 - Community Councils
2.56.010 - Purpose.
The purpose of Salt Lake County recognizing certain community councils in 
the unincorporated area of the county is to provide a mechanism by which 
residents living in the unincorporated area of the county who have chosen to 
associate together in a community council may formulate and present 
advisory recommendations on certain actions within the authority of the 
county as specified in this chapter and to outline the requirements a 
community council must maintain in order to fill that role.
2.56.020 - Definitions.
The following terms used in this chapter as defined as follows:
“Community council ”: a private, nonprofit entity that is entitled to tax exempt 
status under the laws of either the United States or the State of Utah that has 
been established by private individuals living in the unincorporated county to 
represent the collective interests of residents living in a particular geographic 
area of the unincorporated Salt Lake County.
“Unincorporated area” or “Unincorporated county ”: the geographical area of 
Salt Lake County outside of the incorporated area of a municipality.
2.56.030 - Recognition of Community Councils - Requirements.
Salt Lake County will formally recognize community Councils that comply 
with all the following requirements:
A. The community council is a nonprofit entity organized under la and 
entitled to tax -exempt status under the laws of either the United States or the 
State of Utah.  A community council seeking recognition shall submit to the 
County its articles of incorporation as a Utah nonprofit entity along with a 
determination letter or similar proof demonstrating its tax-exempt status.
B. The boundaries of the community council ’s geographical area are 
exclusively within unincorporated Salt Lake County and do not overlap with 
another recognized community council.  A community council seeking 
recognition shall submit to the County a map of its geographic area, which 
Page 5 of 17
County Council Meeting Minutes March 21, 2023
map shall be subject to the review and approval of the County.
C. The community council has adopted bylaws or other governing 
documents which include the following minimum standards:
a. Requirements that the community council ’s meetings and 
elections are held regularly in public places and with adequate notice, 
and that a recording or written minutes of the meetings are maintained;
b. Requirements that the community council ’s officers or leaders 
are selected for fixed terms of service through open and transparent 
elections that are held at least every four years;
c. Requirements that the community council ’s voting members and 
elected offices or leaders reside within the approved geographical area 
of the community council;
d. Requirements that the community council prepare and approve 
an annual budget, which budget includes a disbursement of funds 
report for any funding received from Salt Lake County.
A community council seeking recognition shall submit a copy of its 
bylaws or other governing documents to the County.  Such materials 
shall be delivered to the County at the time the application for 
recognition is submitted, and any future amendments to such materials 
shall be delivered to the County promptly following the adoption of 
such amendments.
2.56.040 - Recognition of Community Councils - Process.
A. A community council may seek recognition from Salt Lake County by 
requesting recognition in writing to the Salt Lake County Council.  The Salt 
Lake County Council shall review requests for community council 
recognition and grant recognition to community councils that have complied 
with the requirements of this chapter, subject to the legal review and advice 
of the District Attorney ’s office.  Recognition shall be approved in the form 
of a resolution of the Council, with notice provided to the community council 
and to the Salt Lake County Mayor.
B. Salt Lake County may withdraw county recognition of any community 
Page 6 of 17
County Council Meeting Minutes March 21, 2023
council that fails to comply with the requirements of this chapter, dissolves 
as a legal entity, fails to hold any official meetings during any period of 
twelve consecutive months or longer, or represents any geographical areas 
outside of the unincorporated county.  Withdrawal of recognition shall be 
approved in the form of a resolution of the Council, with notice provided to 
the community council and to the Salt Lake County Mayor.
2.56.050 - Recognition of Associations of Community Councils.
Associations of two or more community councils that represent portions of 
the unincorporated county may be recognized by Salt Lake County but shall 
not be eligible for funding requests from Salt Lake County.
2.56.060 - Functions of Recognized Community Councils Resecting 
Municipal Services Planning and Zoning in Unincorporated Salt Lake 
County.
A. Each recognized community council is encouraged to develop 
priorities regarding capital improvement projects within the community 
council’s geographical area using the funds provided to Salt Lake County by 
the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District.  These recommendations 
should be communicated in writing to the Mayor and County Council on an 
annual basis as part of the budget process outlined in 2.56.070.  Community 
councils are encouraged to arrive at such recommendations pursuant to duly 
held and appropriately publicized public hearings in their respective 
community council district.
B. Each recognized community council may make advisory 
recommendations to the appropriate county planning commission on 
applications for text changes to Salt Lake County ’s zoning ordinances, and 
on applications for conditional uses occurring within the geographic area of 
the community council.  County staff shall provide each appropriate 
community council with a copy of any applicable zoning or conditional use 
application at the same time that it is referred to the appropriate planning 
commission.  Community councils are encouraged to make 
recommendations concerning such applications to the appropriate planning 
commission.
2.56.070 - County Council participation.

A recognize community council may request the County Council schedule at 
least one meeting annually during the month of September for the purpose of 
receiving funding requests and discussing priorities from each community 
council.  During such a meeting, a recognized community council should 
explain how funds received from the County in the prior year were spent and 
should also provide information demonstrating that it has complied with the 
requirements of this chapter.  The Council may designate Council staff to 
serve as a liaison to recognized community councils.
2.56.080 - Salt Lake County Contributions and In -Kind Assistance for 
Community Councils.
A. Funding requests.  A recognized community council may request 
contributions from Salt Lake County, and the Salt Lake County Council may 
contribute funds to a recognized community council consistent with Utah law 
and County ordinance and policy.  Such contributions are made at the 
legislative discretion of the County Council as a part of the County ’s budget 
process.  Funds that the County Council contributes to such requests shall 
only come from funds provided by the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services 
District, and only to the extent that the District chooses to budget moneys to 
the county for the purpose of supporting community councils.  County 
contributions shall be spent by the community council in a manner consist 
with Utah law and County ordinance and policy for administrative costs such 
as insurance premiums, legal notices, elections, newsletters, computers and 
software, other office equipment, dues to associations of community 
councils, county -sponsored community events and community council 
outreach activities or events, postage, stationary, and duplication costs .  
Each community council that receives contributions from Salt Lake County 
agrees to indemnify Salt Lake County for any misuse of public funds and 
agrees to immediately return any inappropriately spent funds to Salt Lake 
County.
B. In-kind assistance requests.  A recognized community council or 
association of community councils may request in -kind assistance, including 
use of county facilities, and account for such assistance, in accordance with 
the processes outlined in Salt Lake County policies.
C. Additional requirements.  The Salt Lake County Council may establish 
additional requirements and guidelines in countywide policy for processing

funding requests from recognized community councils.
