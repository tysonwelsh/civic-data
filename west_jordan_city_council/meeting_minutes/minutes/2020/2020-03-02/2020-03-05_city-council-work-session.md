            DISCUSSION             OF       ISSUES        RELATED           TO     WOOD       RANCH
            DEVELOPMENT

Korban Lee introduced Jodi Hoffman, Contract Land- Use Counsel, and listed seven topics
for discussion: assignability; PIDs; lanes; density; dispersion and phasing; contract issues;
annexation; and timing and schedule.

        ASSIGNABILITY
Rob Wall said Daybreak Communities had asked that the assignment for Wood Ranch be
unqualified. Rich Sonntag with Daybreak Communities explained the request for
assignment to be with either Daybreak Communities or one of their partners, and
emphasized that Daybreak Communities would manage the entire project. Mr. Wall stated
that unqualified assignment would limit the amount of input the City Council would have
in the project.

Jodi Hoffman explained that assignability had a lot to do with the amount of confidence
the City Council had in the particular developer, and said she believed there was generally
a lot of confidence in Daybreak Communities. She advised that all the discretion the
Council intended to exert on the Wood Ranch project needed to be pinned down.

Mr. Wall commented that this type of development came with some risk to the City, but
also came with many potential benefits to the City. Responding to a question from Chair
McConnehey, Ms. Hoffman explained that whether or not the set criteria were met would
be evaluated        at an administrative           level.   Councilmember     Green   emphasized     that the
objective criteria set needed to be specific to ensure a quality project.

A majority of the Council indicated they did not agree with an unrestricted assignability
clause. A majority of the Council indicated agreement with an assignability clause that had
objective criteria regarding the financial wherewithal and experience of the party assigned,
and an MDA with more specific restrictions regarding to whom the project could be
assigned.

             Response: 5- 0 in favor


             DENSITY/ DISPERSION &                  PHASING

Staff explained that Daybreak Communities considered townhomes to be single- family,
while West Jordan considered townhomes to be multi- family by definition. Mr. Sonntag
explained that Daybreak Communities did not typically allow rental of units within the first
year of ownership. Staff suggested stipulating in the Master Development Plan that, with
the exception of apartments, homes must be owner-occupied for the first year of ownership.
Mr. Sonntag confirmed that Daybreak Communities was conscious of the issue. Ms.
Hoffman suggested short- term rental should be addressed in the Master Development Plan.


Ms. Hoffman asked the Council for feedback regarding placement of parks, roads,
amenities,  commercial, and residential density within the Wood Park development.

Councilmember Worthen said she believed it was more important to ensure that plans for
a park in a specific space at the beginning of development carry through to completion,
than  to define where    different types of housing should be placed. Councilmember

Whitelock said she believed it did matter where the different types of housing were located,
and        said   she   would       prefer   greater     parameters.    Staff   commented       that   the   Master

Development Plan could place a limit on the total number of possible housing units.
Councilmember            Green stated there needed to be a cap on the number of apartments
allowed. Councilmembers Whitelock and Green said they were more concerned about
density than design.

             Response:

             5- 0 in favor of defining placement of parks, roads, amenities, and commercial.
             5- 0 willing to allow Ms. Hoffman to work with Daybreak Communities regarding
             a cap on total number of apartments.
             4- 1 willing to allow Wood Park residential density to float.
             4- 1 willing to allow Ms. Hoffman to work with Daybreak Communities regarding
             ratios of housing product type.
             5- 0 willing to allow Ms. Hoffman to work with Daybreak Communities regarding
             phasing of amenities.

             CONTRACT ISSUES
Staff suggested tying the proposed design book to the MDA as design guidelines. Daybreak
Communities    requested some flexibility and ability to evolve the design plans. Ms.

Hoffman said language would be drafted to ensure the end product would meet quality
expectations.




             ANNEXATION

To fully complete the Wood Ranch project as proposed, annexation of property from the
County would be required. Staff reported the County agreed to the annexation, with the
expectation          that West Jordan would               also   annex   an
                                                                              adjoining    area   in the " near future" (   one

or   two years). Councilmember Whitelock asked if it would be possible to require that
streets, parks, and infrastructure be in good condition at time of annexation.

            ADJOURNMENT

The Council and Staff discussed the process going forward and timing of future meetings.

Chair McConnehey moved to adjourn the work session at approximately 9: 00 p. m.
Councilmember Green seconded the motion, which passed by unanimous vote (5- 0).
