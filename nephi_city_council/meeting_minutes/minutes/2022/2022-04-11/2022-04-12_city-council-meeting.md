Mayor Justin Seely opened the meeting and welcomed all present.

Work Session:

1.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council and asked for feedback on a proposed solar project at the
     airport. He stated that recently UMPA completed a community solar project on an old landfill in
     Spanish Fork. This same company would like to investigate a project to utilize some of the open
     ground on the west side of the airport, and potentially other city-owned property to the west of the
     airport for a solar array with a battery. As part of the project, they would need to build a power line
     to convey the generated power to the city’s substation #3.
     This could be beneficial to the city in many ways, including: 1) allowing the city to serve the airport’s
     electrical needs; 2) assist the city financially with the construction of the northern transmission line;
     3) could provide a way to electrify the city’s wastewater ponds; 4) provide lease revenue for the use
     of lands owned by the city; 5) provide a power backup system with the inclusion of a battery.
     There will be some drawbacks and a glare study will need to be conducted to determine the effect
     it may have on aircraft. In addition, this would use up areas of land that could be used for sewer
     and airport expansion. Long term effects of the solar project on the airport would need to be
     studied. Initial inquiries have shown that not many airports allow these types of projects.
     The solar company would operate the project for the first 15 years and then UMPA has expressed
     interest in purchasing the project afterward.
     The council directed staff to let the company find other land for their project and that they were
     not interested in leasing the land for the solar project.

2.    Mr. Atkinson updated the council about a water agreement we have had with Mr. James Ockey
     that involved water use from Bradley Spring. Previously there had not been a way to measure the
     water use. The agreement stated that the city would sell the Ockeys up to 3,040,000 gallons of raw
     water from the spring and that the Ockeys would be responsible for all cost associated with
     installing and maintaining the meter. After a year of metering the water, the meter indicated that
     the Ockeys had used over 4 million gallons. One of the variables in the water usage was how much
     water was being used by the campground. The city will measure how much the homes are using
     this next month to five a better idea of how much water the Ockeys are using. Mr. Atkinson asked
     the council if they would be in favor of amending the agreement to add some penalties if the water
     usage exceeds the amount allowed. The council directed staff to bring back an agreement
     amendment.

Mayor Seely opened up regular council meeting for public comment.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Mr. Joel Cowan stated that he was against the solar project that had been previously discussed. He
said solar farms are ugly and they use up valuable land. He said he is in favor of looking into nuclear and
gas power plants instead of solar power.
  Mr. Gerratt Bethers stated he was against the solar project. He knows that it is a good idea to have an
array of different power sources, but solar power is the least effective source of energy in his opinion.
He also said that disposing of the solar panels is also a problem.
  Ken Jenson from Draper, Ut representing Jenson refrigeration spoke to the council about his land
purchase agreement that he has with the city. He would like to see some adjustments to paragraphs 6
and 9 of the agreement. He would like a new trigger date to be when the building permit is purled
rather than when the land was purchased. He stated that he has a huge financial risk and millions of
dollars of investment into the project right now. He said on the buy-back details of the agreement there
are no provisions to account for the investment he has into the project right now. The council agreed to
investigate his concerns.

WRIGHT DIRECTION, LLC DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented a development agreement with Wright Direction, LLC for the building of a
slaughterhouse. Councilor Memmott moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the agreement. Councilor
Parady seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 04-12-2022 AMENDING THE ZONE MAP FOR THE BAR W PROPERTY APPROVED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 04-12-2022 changing the zone for property being
developed west of Main Street on Hwy. 132 for purposes of building a slaughterhouse from the HC zone
to the ID-2 zone. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Callaway moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session to
discuss the character and competence of an individual, real property, and water shares. Councilor
Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 9:15 p.m.
