SYNOPSIS:
On June 06, 2023, Daily’s Premium Meats LLC
represented by Chris Ninow submitted a Plat
Application to plat and consolidate six
parcels. The structure located at 3535 S 500
W is used mainly for “Food Processing”. The
proposed consolidated lot will be 6.985 acres.
The current land uses of the six parcels are
“Food Processing”, “Warehouse”, “Parking”
and vacant. The current land use district of all
the parcels is Flex. This plat application is to
consolidate all the different parcels that the
existing food processing structure spans
across and to provide parking for the existing
structure. Industrial use surrounds the
proposed plat and the project parcel abuts
the I-15 freeway. The subject parcels include
an existing non-exclusive easement, and gas easement in favor of Mountain Fuel Supply Company,
extending across the north edge of the consolidated parcel. There is also a right-of-way agreement for Salt
Lake City along the north edge of the consolidated parcel. On the south edge of the parcel, there are the
following easements: construction easements granted to South Salt Lake City; a waterline easement in favor
of Salt Lake County Water Conservancy District; a pipeline easement granted to Salt Lake City Suburban
Sanitary District; and a private road right of way easement. On the west edge, 100 feet well protection zone
and easements for surface, access, and future well connection granted to Daily Foods, Inc.

The Planning Commission is the land use authority over all Plat Applications.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:

Based upon the information submitted in the application, supporting documentation, and analysis of the
relevant code sections and general plan goals, Staff recommends the Planning Commission move to
approve the preliminary subdivision plat amendment to consolidate the six (6) parcels into one lot located
at 3535 S 500 W, based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval for the
subdivision in the staff report.


Findings of Fact:

     1. On June 06, 2023, Christopher Ninow submitted a Plat Application on behalf of Daily’s Premium
        Meats LLC to plat and consolidate parcels 15-36-127-0210, 15-36-127-0280, 15-36-127-0200, 15-
        36-127-0130, 15-36-127-0320, 15-36-127-0250

     2. The purpose of the proposed subdivision plat amendment to consolidate the parcels is to
        consolidate the parcels for a new parking area.

     3. The subject location 3535 S 500 W is in the Flex Land Use District.

     4. 3595 S 500 W is where the existing vacant lot for the new parking lot is located.

     5. The total development area of all six (6) lots is 6.985 acres.

     6. The proposed parking lot provides approximately 51 extra parking stalls for the existing business.
        The existing Food Processing Development has a non-conforming parking need, the new parking
        lot, will provide sufficient parking stalls.

     7. The previous land-locked parcels will now gain access to 500 W and 3600 S.

     8. Section 17.07.010 of the South Salt Lake Municipal Code states that a change of use in the building
        requires the building to comply with all IBC, IFC, development, and design standards.

     9. No impact is expected as part of the business on environmentally sensitive lands. There is no
        environmentally sensitive land on site.

     10. No specific hazards or adverse impacts are expected due to the proposed use.


                                                       8
    11. “Food Processing (Large-Scale)” land use is permitted in the Flex land use district.

    12. The proposed development will comply with all requirements of the South Salt Lake Municipal
        Code.

    13. Staff has reviewed this subdivision application for compliance with South Salt Lake Municipal Code
        Sections 17.10.120, 17.10.130, 17.10.140, 17.10.150, 17.10.160, 17.10.170, 17.10.180, 17.10.190
        and 17.10.200, and found that this subdivision application is compliant with this section, as
        outlined above.

Conclusions of Law:

    1.   The use, as specifically conditioned below, is consistent with the South Salt Lake City General Plan.

    2.   The use, as specifically conditioned below, is compatible in use, scale, and design with allowed
         uses in the Flex district.

    3.   The use, as specifically conditioned below, does not compromise the health, safety, or welfare of:
         Persons employed within or using the proposed development; Those residing or working in the
         vicinity of the proposed use or development; or Property or improvements in the vicinity of the
         proposed use or development.

    4.   The use, as specifically conditioned below, does not impose disproportionate burdens on the
         citizens of the city.

Conditions of Approval:

    1.   The applicant will continue to work with City staff to make all technical corrections necessary prior
         to recording;

    2.   Prior to plat recordation and any additional development of the subsequent lot, the applicant must
         provide city staff the final plat mylar to include notarized signatures of owners’ consent to
         dedication; obtain signatures of all entities indicated on the subdivision plat attached hereto;

    3.   The applicant shall complete (or post an adequate improvement completion assurance), warrant
         and post required warranty assurance for all required landscaping and public infrastructure
         improvements including street lights;

    4.   The applicant shall ensure that all site improvements are installed consistent with the requirements
         of the South Salt Lake City Municipal Code;

    5.   The lighting plan shall show where they are connecting to the power source. This must be approved
         by Rocky Mountain Power.

    6.   The lighting fixtures and pole height shall comply with the SSL Lighting Master Plan;

    7.   The applicant shall dedicate an easement to the City of South Salt Lake for purposes of storm water
         management practices and ensure the plat includes language of the dedication of such easement,
         in a form as approved by the City Attorney’s Office;

    8.   The proposed development shall meet all requirements of the South Salt Lake Community
         Development Department, South Salt Lake Fire Marshal, the South Salt Lake Building Official, and
         the South Salt Lake Engineering department;

                               PLANNING COMMISSION STAFF REPORT
Meeting Date: September 19, 2024
Request:      A petition to the Planning Commission to forward a positive recommendation to the South
              Salt Lake City Council to allow Off-Premise Beer sales in the ground floor retail space of
              multi-family developments and to amend the Park and Ride definition.
Applicants:   South Salt Lake City
Type of item: Legislative – Code Amendment

 SYNOPSIS:
 City staff has received feedback that developers are having a hard time leasing out ground floor retail spaces
 to small grocers because alcohol sales are not permitted. The shortage of small grocers contributes to a food
 desert in some areas of the city along with empty retail spaces. This code amendment would allow more
 bodegas in the retail space of multi-family developments in South Salt Lake, which would benefit the residents
 within the development and the businesses surrounding the multi-family development.

 Additionally, the City has pending plat application for the Millcreek TRAX station. As part of the review
 process, staff noticed that the Park and Ride definition is not as compressive as it could be. Staff has proposed
 some language, which would include Accessory Structures that are necessary to the operation of public
 transit.

 The Planning Commission is the recommending body for amendments to land use ordinances and the City
 Council is the land use authority for amendments to land use ordinances.

 Staff has provided a summary of proposed amendments below. For the full amendment to Title 17, please
 see the attachments provided herein that include a full-redlined ordinance.

 RECOMMENDATIONS:
 Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a recommendation of approval to the City Council for
 an ordinance amending Section 1 of Title 17 of the South Salt Lake City Municipal Code (“Title 17”) to the
 allow off-premise beer sales in the ground floor retail space of multi-family developments and amend the
 Park and Ride definition.
