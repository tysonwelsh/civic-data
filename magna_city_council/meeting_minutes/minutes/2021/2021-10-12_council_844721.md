Financial Report

                Dave Sanderson reviewed the financial report stating in the next coming months,
the Council will need to ratify its administrative budget with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (MSD) and adjust the current year budget in various areas, such as COVID-19
expenses. In November, it will get a tentative budget from the MSD; and in December, a public
hearing will be held on the current budget and next year’s budget.

                Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to accept the
financial report. The motion passed unanimously.

Pleasant Green Cemetery

                Rori Andreason, Administrator, stated the Council has had some inquiries about
a discount for cemetery plots when purchasing a large number of them. A group of Cambodians
are asking to purchase 72 plots. In their culture and religion, they buy more than one plot for

every burial, wherein bodies are buried on angles in relationship to nature and their worthiness.
The individuals are non-resident, and non-resident prices are $950 plot. She suggested
discounting them $50 a plot, which is a difference of $3,600. The cemetery has over 5,300 plots
still available, so it is not going to run out of plots if it sells that many.

               Paul Ashton stated the Council has imposed a fee schedule and no one can waive
fees other than the Council. The Council can waive the fees, or it can authorize the Mayor to do
so.

              Council Member Pierce stated the Council needed to do the imaging to know
where plots were.

                 Ms. Nichols stated there could be unmarked, unknown burials where the family
wants to purchase the plots. There is a disclaimer when purchasing plots that says if another
burial or vault is found while digging, the plots will have to be moved somewhere else. She has
explained that to the family.

                Council Member Pierce asked if this would be a standard discount that met
criteria or more discretionary than that.

               Mr. Ashton stated it would be more discretionary.

               Council Member Pierce stated this is going to set a precedent. She was not
against doing a package deal, but she wanted to have some metrics in place.

             Council Member Peel stated in the past, a discount could be applied if someone
was buying 20 or more spaces. If the Council put that policy in place, he had no problem letting
the Mayor make the decision

                 Ms. Nichols stated she would go into the 30s to prevent people wanting 16 or so
to try to get the discount.

               Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Pierce, moved to give the
Mayor the ability to approve a discount on the purchase of multiple lots of graves at the Pleasant
Green Cemetery at 25 plots or above. The motion passed unanimously. Council Member Peel
recused himself.

to 2:00 PM, in the Webster Center parking lot, and Trick or Treating is from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM

Council Reports

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

               Mayor Peay stated the MSD is going to vote on the bond tomorrow.

               Paul Ashton stated the MSD is in the process of negotiating an interlocal
agreement between the metro townships, the town of Brighton, and Salt Lake County to allow the
MSD to go out for bonding. All of the metro townships and the MSD have agreed on a form for
the contract. Then, the MSD just received the redlines from Salt Lake County today and will
discuss those at its meeting tomorrow. Once those are agreed on, every Council will have to
approve the interlocal agreement.

                Mayor Peay stated his biggest concern was if the Magna Metro Township signed
onto this, it would be tying itself to the MSD for another 10 years.

                  Mr. Ashton stated if the Magna Metro Township eventually becomes a city, it can
withdraw from the MSD. It will just have to pay its proportional share. This deals with B & C road
funds, so it will not require additional taxes. The MSD is just trying to take advantage of the funds
while they are there. He is focused on making sure the money the MSD bonds for regarding
Magna’s stormwater projects stays within Magna’s boundaries regardless of what happens.
