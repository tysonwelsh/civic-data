MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Connor Mangelson
Date: 2023-10-26
Re: Public Hearing: Smythe I-ADU Deviation Request (Administrative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Deviation for approximately 1 acre located on the
south side of 12500 S, at approximately 1602 E 12500 S. The property is currently zoned RA2 and is
located within a mapped area where I-ADU’s are prohibited without approval of a deviation. The
applicant is requesting that a Deviation be approved to allow for the creation of an I-ADU within a
proposed Primary Dwelling on the subject property.


The findings for approval as are follows:




   1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-thousand square
      feet (20,000 ft²);
   2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
   3. The proposed I-ADU meets all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-045,
      including other applicable requirements of this chapter; and
   4. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are provided for use
      by occupants of the I-ADU.
