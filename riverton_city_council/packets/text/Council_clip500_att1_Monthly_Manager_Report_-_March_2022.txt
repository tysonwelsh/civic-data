                      Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                         March, 2022

Historically, February has yielded lower numbers of dispatched calls than the other months of
the year, and this year is no exception. Burglaries and vehicle burglaries were down
significantly compared to previous months, which is a trend we like to see as those types of
crimes are often described as “quality of life” indicators in a low-crime community.

Additionally, reduced calls for service allowed our officers to commit more time to pro-active
traffic enforcement and those efforts resulted in more citations and significantly more DUI
arrests.

Our policy dictates we conduct a yearly analysis of our uses of force and we have recently
completed our report for 2021. The chart below is a description of the types of force utilized and
their relative frequency.

It is important to note the police department generated 18,282 cases during the calendar year
2021 and force was utilized only 51 times, which means the department used force on less than
1% of our calls. We take pride in our commitment to utilize only the amount of force necessary
to address a situation and we also focus significant training resources on de-escalation
techniques. I believe these numbers are an illustration of that commitment.
