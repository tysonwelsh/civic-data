                                General Plan/Zone Change Analysis

                                          March 11, 2020

Application #:          GPZ-2-2020
Applicant:              Scott Helm
Address:                4619 West 4100 South
Request:                General plan change from large lot residential to low density residential
                        and a zone change from A to R-1-10
Property Size:          1.985 acres
Scott Helm has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for a 1.985-acre property
located at 4619 West 4100 South. The property is currently zoned A (agriculture, minimum lot
size ½ acre) with a General Plan designation of large lot residential (2 to 3 units/acre). The
proposed zone is R-1-10 (single unit dwelling residential, minimum lot size 10,000 sq. ft.) and the
proposed General Plan designation is low density residential (3 to 4 units/acre).

The property is surrounded by R-1-8 (single unit dwelling residential, minimum lot size 8,000 sq.
ft.) zoning and single unit dwellings. The subject property is vacant. This property was reviewed
in 2003 as part of the Condie Estates Subdivision; however, the property was not included in a
final plat that was recorded.

The applicant is proposing to combine the subject property with a vacant 0.39-acre parcel to the
north to create a 7-lot subdivision as shown in the attached concept plan. Included with this report
is a letter from the applicant providing reasons for his request.

Back in 2015 when the City created the Residential Estate (RE) zone, this parcel along with other
vacant parcels of 2 acres or more were rezoned by the City (application GPZ-1-2015) to one of
the Agricultural zones as a type of holding zone with the anticipation of eventual development
under the RE zone. At the same time, the City updated its housing standards. The reason for these
changes was to encourage larger homes and larger lots on the relatively little remaining vacant
land within the City. Prior to these changes, the subject property was zoned R-1-8. Along with the
City initiated zone changes described above, the City also changed the ordinance to prohibit zone
change applications to the R-1-7, R-1-8, R-1-10 or R-1-12 zones unless the property was less than
2 acres.

The reason this property was included in the City initiated rezone is that the parcel is over 2 acres.
However, upon closer inspection, the property includes a private driveway extending to 4100
South that is just over 27’ wide by 601’ long. When the private driveway, which is unusable other
than for a driveway, is excluded from the overall 2.35 acres, the remaining land is 1.985 acres.
For this reason, staff believed the application warranted consideration.

The Zoning Ordinance requires that “All new Subdivisions involving a rezone of property, or a
PUD, shall participate in a Development Agreement that addresses housing size, quality, exterior
finish materials, Streetscapes, Landscaping, etc.” The applicant has committed to build homes
that meet the City’s design standards.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. The approval is made subject to a development agreement that includes the
      standards proposed by the applicant.

   2. Continuance.

   3. Denial

 C-3-2020
Albarran Storage in Accessory Building for Home Occupation
3790 S 3600 W
R-1-8 Zone (0.42 acres)

The applicant, Pedro Albarran, is requesting conditional use approval for storage within an
accessory building associated with a home occupation at 3790 S 3600 W. The zoning for the
property is R-1-8, Single Unit Dwelling Residential. The zoning for adjacent parcels is R-1-8 to
the east and west, A to the north, and C-2 to the south. The West Valley City General Plan
designation is low density residential for the area.

The applicant operates a landscaping business and will need to obtain a business license for a
home occupation on the property. The code specifies that there can be one vehicle associated with
the home occupation, up to the equivalent of a one-ton pickup truck, and one trailer. Heavy trucks
and similar equipment are not allowed in a residential area. Typically, garages cannot be used for
a home-based business, except for parking of the one business vehicle and trailer. The code
allows for other business-related storage to occur in a garage only with conditional use approval,
so long as the home retains two parking spaces for residents, and the garage is still usable for
parking.

The only equipment that can be stored on the property within a garage is something that can be
carried or moved by the pickup truck or loaded onto the permitted trailer. A new detached garage
will be built on the property to accommodate additional storage. The proposed structure shall
meet all provisions in the code for accessory buildings, and a building permit shall be obtained.
The rest of the site shall be maintained, and not be used for storage.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing, as well as the
following conditions:

   1. A business license shall be obtained.
   2. The garage shall meet all applicable requirements for accessory buildings inresidential
       zones.
   3. Stucco shall be used on the garage exterior to match the house.
   4. The garage shall not be used for living space.
   5. A building permit shall be obtained for the garage.
   6. No outside storage of equipment or supplies shall be allowed.
   7. The vehicle associated with the business on the property shall be no more than
       theequivalent of a one-ton pickup truck.
   8. Only one trailer as defined in 7-7-115(2)(e) shall be allowed for the business on the
       property.
   9. No heavy trucks shall be stored inside or outside of the garage.
   10. Driveway surfacing shall be in accordance with 7-9-114 of the City Code.
   11. The property shall be maintained, and the site and business shall meet all applicable
       requirements of Title 7 of the West Valley City Municipal Code.
   12. All requirements of affected departments and agencies shall be met.
   13. This approval is subject to review upon a valid complaint.
