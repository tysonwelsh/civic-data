To: Sandy City Council Members
From: Brooke D’Sousa – Council Member At-Large
Date: May 29, 2026
Subject: FY2027 Budget Amendment Proposal – Senior Center Equipment Fund


Recommendation
I propose the creation of a $5,000 annual Equipment Contingency line item specifically
dedicated to the Senior Center, effective for the upcoming fiscal year.
Purpose & Importance
Our Senior Center provides vital daily services, recreational activities, and nutritional programs
for our community's aging population. To maintain a safe, welcoming, and operational
environment, the center relies heavily on various appliances, electronics, and specialized facility
equipment (e.g., commercial kitchen appliances, fitness equipment, and audio/visual systems).
Currently, when essential equipment breaks down unexpectedly, the center must either delay
repairs or scramble to reallocate funds from active programming budgets. This contingency
fund would provide:
   •   Operational Continuity: Immediate access to funds ensures that critical services (like
       meal programs or mobility fitness classes) suffer zero to minimal disruption.
   •   Budget Integrity: Safeguards regular programming budgets from being depleted by
       sudden, unavoidable equipment costs.
Fiscal Year-End Carryover Provision
To ensure fiscal responsibility and encourage prudent spending, this fund is not designed as a
"use it or lose it" budget item. If the $5,000 contingency is not fully utilized by the end of the
fiscal year, the remaining balance should be carried over into the Senior Center’s equipment
management fund or an active capital project that they are saving up to complete.
Example: If the center only spends $1,000 on needed equipment this year, the remaining
$4,000 will be carried over to help fund their next major planned upgrade, such as a shuttle van
replacement or other significant equipment/capital need.
