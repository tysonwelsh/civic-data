RICHARD NIELSON, DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC WORKS - STATED THAT THE COST ESTIMATE HAS
BEEN EMAILED TO EACH COMMISSIONER REGARDING PAVING THE ROAD OUT TO THE
WASATCH WING AND CLAY SITE. THE FIRST % MILE IS INSIDE EAGLE MOUNTAIN CITY AND
REMAINING 1% MILES IS IN THE COUNTY. THE COST ESTIMATE REFLECTS THAT.

THIS WAS BROUGHT UP ABOUT 1 % AGO WITH CONCERNS FROM WASATCH WING. DURING
SOME OF THEIR LARGE NATIONAL SHOOTS THE ROADS GET VERY WET AND SOFT IN THE
SPRING AND HAVE HAD SOME MOTORHOMES HAVE DIFFICULTY GETTING TO THE SITE.
PUBLIC WORKS HAS TRIED TO WORK WITH THEM SINCE THAT TIME IN GRADING THE ROAD
PRIOR TO THESE SHOOTS.

WASATCH WING THERE IS AN AREA, WHEN CROSSING THE TICKVILLE WASH, WHERE

LANES WITH 3’ PAVED SHOULDERS ON EACH SIDE. WE WOULD ASSUME THAT THE
LANDOWNERS WOULD BE FAVORABLE TO THIS AND POSSIBLY DONATE THE RIGHT-OF-WAY
WITH THE COUNTY DOING THE IMPROVEMENTS.

2. DISCUSS A PROPOSAL FOR A LIFE JACKET LOANER STATION TO BE PLACE ON UTAH
COUNTY PROPERTY AT THE KNOLLS AREA OF UTAH LAKE.

~ Richard Nielson, Public Works Director -

RICHARD NIELSON, DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC WORKS — STATED THAT BACK IN MAY,
A COUPLE OF YOUNG LADIES FROM THE SARATOGA SPRINGS AREA WERE OUT
ON UTAH LAKE AND GOT CAUGHT IN A WINDSTORM AND UNFORTUNATELY
LOST THEIR LIVES. THE FAMILIES OF THESE YOUNG LADIES HAVE PUT FORTH
A PROPOSAL FOR A LIFE JACK LOANER STATION THAT WOULD BE PLACED ON
COUNTY PROPERTY AT THE KNOLLS AREA OF THE LAKE. DISCUSSED THE
PROPOSAL THAT WAS SENT TO EACH COMMISSIONER.

THE PROPOSAL IS FOR A KIOSK WITH SIGNS ABOUT WATER SAFETY THAT WILL
ALSO HAVE A PLACE FOR LIFE JACKETS TO BE HUNG FOR LOANER USE.

FEELS AS FAR AS PUTTING THIS ON THE PROPERTY, DOES NOT SEE ANY ISSUES.
JUST WOULD WANT TO COORDINATE THE LOCATION AND HAVE THE
ATTORNEY’S OFFICE LOOK INTO ANY POSSIBLE LIABILITIES.

COMMISSIONER LEE - EXPRESSED CONDOLENCES FOR THE TRAGIC INCIDENT
THAT HAPPENED AND RECOGNIZED THAT THIS IS AN EFFORT TO SEE IF YOU
CAN HELP STEM SOMETHING LIKE THIS FROM HAPPENING AGAIN.

STEPHANIE COWLES, SISTER OF PRISCILLA BIENKOWSKI — STATED THAT
DURING THE EIGHT DAYS THEY WERE SEARCHING FOR THE GIRLS, THEY MET A
LOT OF PEOPLE THAT HAVE HAD INCIDENTS WHERE SOMETHING SIMILAR HAD
HAPPENED. THE KIOSK WOULD BE A WAY OF GETTING EDUCATION OUT THERE
AND ALSO HOPEFULLY HELP SAVE THE LIFE OF A PERSON WHO IS NOT REALLY
AWARE OF THE DANGERS OF THE LAKE,

STATED THAT THEY WOULD TAKE CARE OF THE MAINTENANCE OF THIS KIOSK
AND RESTOCKING OF LIFE JACKETS,

COMMISSIONER AINGE —- STATED WE MAY WANT TO GET WITH PUBLIC WORKS
AND THE UTAH LAKE COMMISSION TO SEE IF WE COULD GO BROADER WITH
SIGNAGE AND INFORMATION ON HOW WINDY THE LAKE CAN GET,
