G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. APPROVAL OF FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 2 OF THE
        WEBER COUNTY FEE ORDINANCE, SEC. 16-2-2 CLERK/AUDITOR FEES.
            Mona Wald (Clerk/Auditors Office): We are proposing to begin offering marriage services on a trial period
            for $10 for the officiating and $25 if witnesses from our office are needed. We do about 4k marriage licenses
            per year.

            Chair Froerer: If this a trial basis, we may want to designate an area in the building renovation plans for
            these marriages.

            Commissioner Bolos moved to approve First Reading of an Ordinance amending Chapter 2 of the Weber
            County Fee Ordinance, Sec. 16-2-2 Clerk/Auditor Fees; Chair Froerer seconded.
            Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos – aye
     2. APPROVAL OF FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE ALLOWING APPOINTMENT OF
        ADDITIONAL ALTERNATE MEMBERS TO THE GRAMA APPEALS BOARD.
            Courtlan Erickson (Attorney’s Office): Ernie Gonzalez, our GRAMA Appeals Board Chair, is requesting
            flexibility to having additional alternate Board members to support GRAMA Appeals Board scheduling.
         There have been occasions where a Board member had to cancel and alternate members were not available,
         leading to last minute cancellations. The Ordinance proposes changing wording from “appoint one alternate
         as a County employee and one alternate as a member of the public” to “at least one”, giving the Commission
         flexibility to appoint one or more alternates.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve First Reading of an Ordinance allowing appointment of additional
         alternate members to the GRAMA Appeals Board; Chair Froerer seconded.
         Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos – aye

    3. APPROVAL OF FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN FEES TO THE
       WEBER COUNTY CULTURE, PARKS, AND RECREATION DEPARTMENT.
         Brad Cragun (Parks and Recreation Director): Our department has a diverse range of services and facilities
         that we constantly monitor. We do this to make sure our fees are where they need to be and to adjust or
         eliminate fees structures to accurately reflect what we offer. Proposed changes reflect adding campsites to
         our campgrounds in North Fork and Memorial and removing classes that are no longer offered.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve First Reading of an Ordinance amending certain fees to the Weber
         County Culture, Parks, and Recreation Department; Chair Froerer seconded.
         Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos – aye
    4. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 08-2026 CREATING THE WEBER COUNTY PARAMEDIC AND
       TECHNICAL RESCUE COMMITTEE.
         Lisa Schwartz (Emergency Management and Homeland Security Program Director): This is a Resolution to
         create the Paramedic and Technical Rescue Committee. This Committee will help enhance coordination and
         cooperation between Weber County and the fire agencies and enable us to do a better job with expenditures
         on program funding for supplies, equipment, and training.

         Chief Britt Clark (Weber Fire District Chief): The County funds a few programs that are distributed between
         departments. This is a way for all agencies to have input on those programs and a way for them to help guide
         the Commission on those financial decisions. The Committee will be comprised of every Fire Chief in the
         County or their designees. The Committee will fall between the existing subcommittees.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve Resolution 08-2026 creating the Weber County Paramedic and
         Technical Rescue Committee; Chair Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos – aye
    5. APPROVE A MASTER SERVICES AND PURCHASING AGREEMENT WITH AXON FOR THREE
       BODY CAMERAS FOR WEBER COUNTY ANIMAL CONTROL OFFICERS.
         Siri Main (Animal Shelter Director): Animal Control lacks accountability and transparency for the
         community. Though Animal Control officers are not sworn officers, we propose to provide them with body
         cameras, similar to other local animal control agencies. Funding will be pulled from the quartermaster and ...
         budgets. Long term funding will have to be addressed through a budget increase. There are future plans to
         purchase bullet proof vests with name tags.

         Commissioner Bolos: This is a wise idea for full transparency and to protect both our animal control officers
         and the public. I really appreciate Siri and her diligence watching every dollar.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve a Master Services and Purchasing Agreement with Axon for three body
         cameras for Weber County Animal Control Officers; Chair Froerer seconded with comment: the more
         transparent we can be with the public, the better.
         Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos – aye
