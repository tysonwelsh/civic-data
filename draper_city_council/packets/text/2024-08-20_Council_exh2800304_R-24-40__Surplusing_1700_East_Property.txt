                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-40

A RESOLUTION DECLARING AS SURPLUS PARCELS 28-33-203-007-0000
AND 28-33-203-010-0000

      WHEREAS, Draper City owns approximately 10 acres of property at 12695
South 1700 East consisting of Parcel No. 28-33-203-007-0000 and Parcel No. 28-33-
203-010-0000 (the “Property”); and

      WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code Section 3-3-160 describes the
procedures to be followed to surplus a significant parcel of city-owned property;
and

       WHEREAS, the City has complied with Section 3-3-160 to properly authorize
the disposal of such property; and

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council desires to declare the Property as surplus
as the sale of the Property will realize significant financial benefits to the City and is
otherwise in the public interest.

      NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the City Council of Draper City:

       Section 1. Declaration of Surplus Property. The Property described in
Exhibit A is no longer needed by the City for a public purpose and is declared
surplus and its disposal is authorized.

       Section 2. Severability Clause. If any section, part, or provision of this
Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall
affect any other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of
this Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 20th DAY OF AUGUST, 2024.
