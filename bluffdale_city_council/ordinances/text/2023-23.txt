                                  ORDINANCE NO.2023-23


AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH, AMENDING
BLUFFDALE CITY CODE SECTIONS 11.90 AND 11.100 (COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL
ZONES) RELATING TO MINIMUM LOT AREA, FRONTAGE, WIDTH, AND SETBACK
REQUIREMENTS IN THE HEAVY COMMERCIAL AND LIGHT INDUSTRIAL ZONES

       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants Bluffdale City (the "City") authority to enact
ordinances that the Bluffdale City Council (the "City Council") considered necessary or
appropriate for the use and development of land within the City; and

       WHEREAS,the subject text amendment modifies the regulations for minimum lot area,
frontage,width,and setbacks in the heavy commercial and light industrial zones which remove
unnecessary restrictions on innovative subdivision and site design; and

        WHEREAS,the Planning Commission held a public hearing and gave the City Council its
recommendation;and

       WHEREAS, after holding a public hearing and reviewing the subject text amendment,
the City Council finds that the subject text amendment will enhance public health,safety, and
welfare.

NOW,THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH:


      SECTION 1. Amendment. Sections 11.90 and 11.100 of the Bluffdale City Code are
hereby amended as shown in Exhibit A:

severable.

       SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
