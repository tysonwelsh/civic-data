Pledge (11:03 A.M.)
Discussion regarding Dakota Pacific traffic/SR224; Marc Stanworth (11:04 A.M.)
Council Chair Stevens welcomed everyone to the meeting and provided a brief background on the traffic study that had been conducted.
Carl Miller, Transportation Director, and Brandon Brady, Engineer, added background information and addressed items raised in the study.
Council Members asked brief questions and provided feedback on the study to which Director Miller, Steve Borup, Dakota Pacific Real Estate, and Preston Stinger, Fehr and Peers responded.
Council Chair Stevens directed the conversation to the issue with SR224 and traffic impacts at 11:13 A.M.
Marc Stanworth, Dakota Pacific Real Estate, stated their alignment with the County to work toward solutions for SR224.
Director Miller provided information regarding potential modifications to SR224 and presented options A, B, and C for discussion. Council Members asked clarifying questions and provided feedback on the options presented.
Council Chair Stevens brought up a scheduling change regarding the public hearing. The new public hearing would be held on February 15th at 6 p.m. at Ecker Hills Middle School in order to avoid Winter Break when many people would be out of town. The decision would be held on February 20th at 6 p.m.
