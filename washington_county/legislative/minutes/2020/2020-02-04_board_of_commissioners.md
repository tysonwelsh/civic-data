CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2563, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING TWO _INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENTS TO
PROVIDE SUPPORT TO WASHINGTON COUNTY IN HOSTING FOR
IRONMAN RACES

Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that these agreements are between Washington
County and St. George City; and Washington County and the Utah Sports Commission in
regards to the upcoming IRONMAN races scheduled for the next five (5) years in
Washington County. Those races include the IRONMAN 70.3 World Championship
(2021), the IRONMAN North American Championship (2020 and 2023), and the
IRONMAN 70.3 North American Championship 2021, 2022, and 2024).

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-
2563, A Resolution Approving Two Interlocal Cooperation Agreements
to Provide Support to Washington County in Hosting for IRONMAN
Races. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2564, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING FUNDING FOR THE ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STATEMENT
FOR_ THE NORTHERN CORRIDOR PROJECT AND RENEWAL OF THE
WASHINGTON COUNTY DESERT TORTOISE INCIDENTAL TAKE PERMIT

Deputy Washington County Attorney Eric Clarke explained that the renewal of the
incidental take permit will allow for development of private property in the county without
interfering with the regulatory provisions of the Endangered Species Act. The estimated
remainder of the cost of the County’s responsibilities for the completion of the Habitat
Conservation Plan (HCP) renewal contract is approximately $620,000. The Utah
Dperatment of Transportation (UDOT) will continue to pay the costs for the Northern
Corridor right-of-way application and accompanying EIS. The County is only paying the
portion of work that is directly related to the HCP.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2564, A
Resolution Approving Funding for the Environmental Impact
Statement for the Northern Corridor Project and Renewal of the
Washington County Desert Tortoise Incidental Take Permit. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2569, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE HABITAT CONSERVATION ADVISORY

COMMITTEE

Habitat Conservation Planning (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan explained that there were
two vacancies created by the expiration of terms. It was recommended for Gregg McArthur
to serve as the citizen-at-large and Mayor Chris Hart to serve as the local development

representative.

Mr. Rognan went on to say that Larry Crist retired from his position at the U.S. Fish and
Wildlife Service (USFWS). The USFWS has recommended Laura Romin to be appointed
to fill his vacancy on the HCAC.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2569, A
Resolution Appointing Gregg McArthur and Chris Hart to the Habitat
Conservation Advisory Committee with Terms to Expire February 28,
2023; along with the Amendment of Appointing Laura Romin as the
Representative of the US Fish and Wildlife Service for an Ongoing
Term. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2570, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING THE CONSTRUCTION OF A _PRE-ENGINEERED STEEL
BUILDING TO STORE WASHINGTON COUNTY SEARCH AND RESCUE

EQUIPMENT

Commissioner Almquist explained that the Sheriff's Office has a need for additional
storage space for search and rescue equipment. The County has determined to purchase a
pre engineered steel building from the General Steel Corporation (GSC) and sought bids
for a contractor to complete the groundwork and erection of the new building. GSC
Independent Authorized Builder submitted the lowest bid and are being recommended to
provide the needed services.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-
2570, A Resolution Approving the Construction of a Pre-Engineered
Steel Building to Store Washington County Search And Rescue
Equipment. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.
