Eric Chase, Neighborhood Chair of North Park of Provo spoke on the fatal auto-pedestrian accident that
happened a few weeks ago. He is concerned that widening the road at 800-820 North would only cause
more concern and safety issues for the neighbors in this area.

Kat Linford of Provo spoke on the general plan versus the transportation plan. She stated that it does
not align with the Welcome Home slogan of Provo.

Rebecca Leavitt of Provo agrees with the concerns already given about the general and transportation
plans.

Dylan Singleridge of Provo spoke on cost and coverage of the plan to widen the 800-820 North road. He
commented that it is using taxpayer funds to fund a road that in the end will not serve Provo.

Chris Erickson of Provo has concerns of the 800-820 north widening of the road and the traffic that it will
bring. He also has concerns about the schools it would affect and the pollution it will cause.

Matthew Sanders of Provo spoke on walkable areas and the residence not having added pollution. He is
asking the council to consider the plan and how it will affect the surrounding neighborhoods.

Tyler Sanders of Provo is against the plan of widening of the 800-820 North. Spoke on walking around
the city versus using transportation.

Nick O’Connor of Provo urges the council to amend this general plan. As a cyclist he states he has
concern he has for the speed in the area and the safety for cyclists and pedestrians would be affected.

Johnathan Hill of Provo spoke on the pros and cons of having this road widened. Recommends doing a
study on the economic impact.

Chair Handley closed public comments and opened it up for council discussion.

Councilor Hoban asked what the general plan has regarding the 800-820 north.

Mr. Larsen stated the general plan does not reference that, it is the transportation plan that has this as
an option.

Councilor Hoban asked if there was the plan to make changes to the transportation plan for the 800-820
North Road.

Mr. Larsen stated there is no plan to make any changes to 800-820 north at this time.

Chair Handley asked when the code requirement expires.

Mr. Larsen stated that it is in five years.

Chair Handley spoke to the public and stated the council made some revisions to the plan. He stated it
was discussed in work session and that the proposed changes and recommendations will be presented
at the next council meeting, which would be on January 17th.

 12.        An ordinance amending the General Plan Map classification of approximately 24 acres
            generally located at 3450 W. Center St. from Residential (R) to Airport-Related (AR). Fort
            Utah Neighborhood. (PLGPA20220334) (02:36:25)

       Motion:      An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-55, as currently constituted, has been
                    made by council rule.

Aaron Ardmore, City Planner presented. He answered questions brought up in the work study meeting.
He has confirmed with Parks & Recreation that there is no equestrian parkway planned for this area. He
further explained that there will be equestrian parking available for them to use the trails, but no
parkway is in the plan.

Aaron answered Chair Handley’s question on how much acres are around the airport and is designated
for airport industrial zone. He stated that there are roughly 650 acres and majority of it is airport
related. Nine of those acres has been approved for Airport Industrial zone and 28 of those acres are N1.

Chair Handley had a follow-up question about the southside of the airport and asked what the status of
that area is.

Mr. Ardmore stated that the area on the Southeast is zoned N1 and the Southwest is zoned for Airport
Industrial.

Councilor Ellsworth asked if the applicant was present and said she would like to hear from them what
their preference for the zone would be.

Councilor Fillmore asked if there was a better location for this type of development.

Councilor Hoban asked Mr. Ardmore what the thoughts of the Planning Commission and Development
Services are concerning this amendment.

Mr. Ardmore stated that there is a need for both industrial and residential in this area.

Developer spoke on what use would be an asset to the airport because the land is so close to it. He
stated that residential homes would block the river and would create an alleyway. He spoke with staff,
Council, the Neighborhood Chair and Mayor Kaufusi and the proposal was formed to be a commercial
property and to design and allow public access to this area and the river trail.

Councilor MacKay spoke on the design and asked about the percentage of commercial.

Developer stated that he does not have a number to give at this time.

Councilor Whipple asked about the number of acres that would be designated for green access space.

Developer stated that he does not have a definite number, but the plan requires for a 100-foot buffer
from the river, so it can be designed to have plenty of green space and stated he would love input from
the city for the design.

Councilor Whipple commented on her concerns with the residential being a safety concern for kids and
families.

Chair Handley has a question about the protection of the southside. He stated the council will be
drafting a river and lake plan and wants to make sure it aligns with that process.

Councilor Ellsworth asked if it is creating a relationship with the river and park. She stated she hopes
that the plan will include solid landscaping and natural looking area.

Councilor Fillmore asked Bill Peperone if this is approved how it would affect the river development
plan.

Bill Peperone spoke on the steps it requires. He also stated that the river plan would respect this plan
and it should not affect this area.

Councilor Fillmore asked if this would precede the river plan.

Mr. Peperone agreed that it would precede the plan.

Councilor Hoban questioned Bill Peperone about the plan along the river trail for residential.

Mr. Peperone stated that the land is not yet zoned for residential at this time.

Councilor Hoban stated that he would like to see that happen.
