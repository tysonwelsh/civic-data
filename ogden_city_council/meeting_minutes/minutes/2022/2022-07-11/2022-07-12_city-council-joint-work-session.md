The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agendas for the Special City Council and Redevelopment
Agency (RDA) meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.; participate in a discussion with the Mayor and Police Department
relating to homeless outreach efforts; discuss the Fiscal year (FY) 2022-2023 Financial Principles relating to use of unused
salaries and benefits dollars; discuss FY 2022-2023 Budget Goals and Guidelines relating to use of unused salaries and benefits
dollars; discuss Council/Board business relating to Council Rules and Norms and RDA Developer Selection Process; and hear
Council/Board comments.

Agenda Review
Chair Nadolski and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the Special City Council

and Regular RDA business meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Council/Mayor/Police Discussion — Homeless Outreach Efforts

Police Chief Young introduced the Police Departments full-time Homeless Outreach Advocates, Anna Davidson and
Nancy Griggs. He invited them to provide the Council with information about ongoing efforts in the community. Ms. Davidson
and Ms. Griggs used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to discuss the root causes of homelessness; opportunities for building
trust with the homeless population; methods of support that are provided to homeless individuals in the community, including
health/mental care, housing support, obtaining legal documents needed to acquire housing, navigating the justice system following
receipt of a citation or being arrested; and the public costs associated with the impacts of homelessness. They also relayed a few
stories about successes they have realized with a few homeless individuals in the community, after which they engaged in
discussion with Ms. Davidson and Ms. Griggs regarding the resources they need to continue to facilitate improvement for the
homeless population in the community and the need to pursue State-wide legislation or partners with other government entities to
share the load of providing services to the homeless individuals throughout the State of Utah. The Council thanked Ms. Davidson,
Ms. Griggs, and Chief Young for the information they provided tonight and for the valuable service they provide to the entire
community. They added they would like to hear from the Homeless Advocates on a regular basis to stay apprised of
improvements in the community and efforts to work with the State of Utah regarding homelessness programming.

Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Financial Principles — Use of Unused Salaries
and Benefits; and
Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Budget Goals and Guidelines — Use of Unused
Salaries and Benefits
Council Executive Director Eller-Smith explained the proposal is to amend the Fiscal Year (FY) 2023 Financial
Principles to include language governing the use of excess salary and benefit funds resulting from vacancies in full-time, funded
positions as follows:
Employee Salaries. The City recognizes the importance of competitive compensation and will make funding for
the 2023 Compensation and Benefits Plan a budget priority. Excess compensation and benefit funds resulting
from vacancies in authorized full-time positions shall not be transferred to other departmental operating
accounts without Council approval.
Relative to the budget and goals guidelines, the proposal is to adopt an amendment related to excess compensation and
benefit funds as follows:
Salaries and Benefits. In keeping with the intent of maintaining adequate levels of service and the pay-for
performance system, the Council desires that all salaries and benefits are spent for this purpose. Per joint
agreement between the Council and Mayor in the Financial Principles, excess compensation and benefit funds
resulting from vacancies in authorized full-time positions shall not be transferred to other operating accounts
without Council approval.

Council/Board Business

Council Rules and Norms: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to

summarize the three proposed changes to the Council’s Rules and Norms as follows:
e Changing Electronic Stipend Amount
e Establishing Threshold for Special Day by Proclamation
e Clarifying Language — Public Comment

The proposal is to increase the annual electronic stipend amount from $500 to $1000; this is based upon
recommendations from the Evergreen Compensation Study. The proposal relating to public comments would be to change the
language in the Rules and Norms document as follows:

The public comment portion of the City Council meeting is set aside for members of the public to address the City

Council on any issue. Each person has up to three (3) minutes to provide comment. Individuals may speak for

themselves or on the behalf of another person or organization. However, the three (3) minute time limitation applies

regardless of whether individuals are speaking for themselves or for others. The time is not cumulative.

Finally, the change to the language dealing with declaring a special day by proclamation would be to require informal
agreement by five Council members in order to proceed with the declaration. She then facilitated discussion about the Council
initiative process and requests to be on the agenda to solicit feedback and suggestions from the Council regarding potential
additional adjustments to the Rules and Norms document regarding those two issues. She indicated Council staff will use the
feedback provided to amend the document before it is presented to the Council for adoption.

Redevelopment Agency Developer Selection Process: Ms. Eller-Smith stated that Council staff and the Community and
Economic Development Department have discussed important elements of a developer selection process for the RDA; first would
be to create a data base that would include information about developers interested in and qualified to be involved in RDA
projects in the community. Public solicitation would be the preferred formal solicitation process, but other selection options are
available under certain circumstances. She noted that she will include an item on a future work session agenda to provide
additional time for the Board to discuss the important components of a policy with Administration.
