                                         MEMORANDUM

                                        October 26, 2021

To:             City Council Members

CC:             Kurt Bradburn, Mayor
                Mike Applegarth, Council Executive Director

From:           Zach Robinson, Council At-large

Subject:        Implementing the Wattsmart Communities Energy Action Plan




Over the past few months Administration has provided the Council with an exceptional plan for
Sandy sustainability in the Energy Action Plan. I've been nothing but happy with
implementation, up to this point, and have been very pleased with the regular Council reports
that we have received. A new Administration will take the reins in just over 2 months from
today and I would like to guarantee that this sustainability initiative continues, no matter who is
elected Mayor. Tonight, I'd ask the Council to give me the go ahead in finalizing the attached
draft resolution which directs administration (both current and future) to continue implementing
this important initiative.
