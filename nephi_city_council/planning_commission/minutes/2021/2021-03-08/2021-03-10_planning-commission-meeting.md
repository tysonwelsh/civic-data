Nephi City Planning Commission Public Hearings
                               March 10, 2021

Chairman Greenhalgh welcomed those in attendance and opened the hearing at 7 p.m. He explained the
purpose of the hearing was to receive public comment on proposed changes to the Nephi Land Use Code
concerning commercial and industrial performance standards, minor changes to the makeup of the
Development Review Committee and changes to the Subdivision section. Also, Commission members were
given a rough draft of the blight ordinance. Seth has suggested waiving landfill fees as a possible incentive.
Some funding opportunities are available to developers once an area has been designated as ‘blight’.
Definition of blight will be reviewed in more detail.
Kent Heap motioned the approval of minutes of February 10th meeting. Motion seconded by Cory Thomson.
Motion passed with a unanimous vote.
Canyon Hills final plat and DRC notes were reviewed by Commission. Cory Tomson motioned approval of
Canyon Hills final plat along with DRC notes. Kent Heap seconded the motion. Motion passed with a
unanimous vote.
Chairman Greenhalgh closed the hearing at 7:10 p.m.

Chairman Greenhalgh opened second public hearing of the evening at 7:10 p.m. He explained the purpose of
the hearing was to receive public comment on proposed changes to the Nephi Land Use Code creating an ID-2
district. Ken Jensen asked if there are specific areas being considered for an ID-2 designation. Glenn replied
that nothing has been settled on yet, but possibly around Airport Road and south end of town away from
anything residential. Paul Terry wanted to know if annexation will go around his property. Legislation of
island annexations was then explained by Glenn. Skip Worwood offered the City Council’s goal is to create a
specific district for slaughter houses.
Chairman Greenhalgh closed the hearing at 7:20 p.m.

Chairman Greenhalgh opened third public hearing of the evening at 7:20 p.m. He explained the purpose of
the hearing was to receive public comment on a zone change request for property owned by Craig Sperry
located at approximately 850 North 200 East, from R1-H to R2-H. This would allow for duplexes. Ken Jensen
commented there is not adequate housing in Nephi for the job market he would like to bring in.
Chairman Greenhalgh closed the hearing at 7:30 p.m.
