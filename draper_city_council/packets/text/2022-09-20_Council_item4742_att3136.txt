                                   • Reporting requirements were changed as part of HB 462.
                                   • For 2022 the submittal requirement consists of submitting a copy of the
                                     updated and adopted Moderate Income Housing element from the General
                                     Plan together with information about the plan entered into an online google

                                       • General Plan update must include a description of each moderate

                                   • For 2023 and beyond the report will need to include:

                                       • A description of each action taken by the jurisdiction in the previous
                                         fiscal year to implement selected strategies.
