                               Council Budget Workshop Meeting

FY2022 Proposed Budget

               Dave Sanderson, Financial Manager, stated Magna Metro Township is more self-
supporting than other townships. The Council may want to consider completing a feasibility study
to become its own city. The cost of the feasibility study will need to be put into your budget. The
council also needs to consider what it wants to use its CARES and ARPA money for.

                  Council Member Peel asked if the CARES and ARPA funding can be used to pay
for a feasibility study.

               Paul Ashton stated the CARES money could pay for it.

                Council Member Peel stated there is $1.5 million in the CARES fund and $1.5
million in the ARPA fund. How can those funds be legally spent? What does the Council need to
do to start the process of hiring a firm to do the feasibility study?

              Mr. Sanderson stated the Council needs to budget for it first. The CARES funds
need to be budgeted and then it can be spent. The ARPA funds are restricted for capital projects.

              Council Member Prokopis asked if CARES and ARPA money could be used to
purchase land.

               Council Member Brint stated the Council should consider buying land for future
use.

                   Council Member Hull suggested the purchase of the Cyprus High School
property. A feasibility study would need to be done to see if the Metro Township can afford to buy
it, fix, or rebuild it.

               Council Member Peel stated to purchase a piece of property that large, it would
need a bond.

                Mr. Sanderson stated the Council will need to consider a budget adjustment in
December and the Council can allocate funds for a feasibility study in that adjustment. He
recommended the Council do some research and talk to entities that have purchased an old
building like the school to get more of an idea on how that has worked out.

              Council Member Peel stated he would like to meet with Marla Howard, Greater
Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD), and Mr. Sanderson to discuss finances for the
cemetery and the turn around time it takes to issue a check.

               Council Member Hull stated the problem with the MSD is that they are slow
paying out invoices.

                Council Member Peel stated he would also like to see cemetery bylaws presented
to the Council for approval.

                 Mr. Sanderson stated ARPA funds need to be spent by 2026, CARES funds do
not have a time limit. He recommended that the Council focus on what projects are a priority and
create a list of 10 items.

              Council Member Peel stated he would like to use some funds to hire a person to
be at the Webster Center to answer the phones or help people in person, for a few hours a day.
Residents need someone locally to be able to answer questions.

             Mr. Ashton stated that Rori Andreason is working on hiring someone that can
handle communications and set up the electronic system for the cemetery. He would like to see
how she wants to proceed.

              Council Member Peel stated as a Trustee of the Webster Center, space would
be made available to accommodate an office for Ms. Andreason and other employees.

            Council Member Hull asked if CARES money can be used to upgrade technology
to accommodate virtual meetings.

              Mr. Sanderson stated each Council Member should come up with ten priorities on
their own and then come together as a Council to discuss them and decide where the funding
should go.

               Council Member Hull stated Crystal Hulbert, Salt Lake County Engineering and
Flood Control, has a list of projects to be completed in Magna, can some of the money be spent
to fund those projects.

               Mr. Ashton stated after the Council decides on its priority projects, Mr. Sanderson
and himself will figure out how to fund them.

                 Mr. Sanderson stated the equipment and upgrades to technology should be put
on your list of priorities.

             Council Member Peel asked how to clean-up the pass-through money that is
given to Magna Town Council, 4th of July, and other events and groups.

              Mr. Sanderson stated the entities should present an annual budget to show where
the money is going and how much is being spent.

               Council Member Peel stated he was criticized for requesting a budget and
accounting.

                Mr. Sanderson stated these entities should have to show the Council its budget
for approval to get funding. They must follow the Council rules to get the contributions and if they
do not, the Council has the right to deny the funding request.

              Council Member Peel stated some of the organizations like Magna in Motion, get
funding through the Magna Town Council. Magna in Motion pays for functions out of their own
pockets and then request reimbursement from the Town Council. The Town Council takes a while
to refund them. Can entities like Magna in Motion come to the Council individually and request
funding and then the funds would go directly to them?

               Mr. Sanderson stated the Council can require that the Magna Town Council finds
a way to give funds to these groups so they do not have to pay out of pocket, or they will not be
funded.

               Mr. Ashton stated the Council can have Magna in Motion and other groups
present their own budget to get funds from the Council.

             Council Member Prokopis stated the Magna Town Council would go away and
a sub-committee could be created under the Council to help these groups.

              Council Member Hull stated the ordinance that covers contributions needs to be
reviewed and amended.

               Council Member Peel asked that the ordinance be revised and put on the agenda.

                Mr. Sanderson stated the documents that were distributed to the Council, include
the administrative budget, CARES and ARPA funds; however, CARES and ARPA are the
Councils priority.

              Mr. Ashton stated Mr. Sanderson will bring back a preliminary budget for the
Council to consider and then it will need to set a public hearing.
