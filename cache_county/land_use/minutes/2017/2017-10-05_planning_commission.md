#2 Public Hearing (5:40 p.m.): Rosehill Farms I Rezone 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for Rosehill Farms I Rezone. 
 
05:42:00 
 
Olsen motioned to open the public hearing; Smith seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
Tim Wilkinson commented that the rezone is for financial reasons. The dairy processing plant will remain and 
continue to operate.  
 
Parker asked if Mr. Wilkinson owned cows still or was it just the processing plant. 
 
Mr. Wilkinson responded that he doesn’t own cows and just has the processing plant. 
 
White asked if Hyrum City had commented. 
 
Zetterquist responded no comment had been received. 
 
Wayne Jewkes commented against the proposal because of trash from development, impacts on surrounding 
agricultural uses, people not wanting to live next to the sight and smells of a farm,  
Parker asked about the location of Mr. Jewkes land and if it was part of the Agricultural Protection Area. 
5 October 2017                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 4 
Mr. Jewkes showed the commission where his land was located and responded that his land is part of the 
Agricultural Protection Area. 
 
Cody Matthews commented against the proposal because of the negative impacts the development could have 
on his mink, people not wanting to live next to the sights and smell of agriculture, and concerns for 6400 south. 
 
Cliff Abbott  commented against the proposal because they want to live in the country and enjoy the current 
setting of the land. 
 
Marcia Acevedo commented she is against the proposal because they don’t want that many neighbors, concerns 
for her well, and the size of the lots for the subdivision. 
 
Glenn Matthews commented against the proposal because of the possible negative impacts to his mink ranch, 
specifically the lighting a subdivision brings. 
 
Tyce Matthews commented against the proposal because of the negative impacts to agricultural businesses 
around this development. 
 
Kyle Richardson commented against the proposal because of the closeness and number of neighbors that would 
be added. 
 
Cole Matthews commented against the proposal and woul d like to see the area remain agricultural and continue 
to sustain the families that make a living through agricultural. 
 
Chet Olsen commented against the proposal because of the possible disturbance to his irrigation water, possible 
flooding, high water t able area, lights disturbing the mink ranches, traffic increasing and road issues, and the 
canal on the west side of the road. 
 
06:03:00 
 
Gunnell motioned to close the public hearing; Smith seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the agricultural protection area and how the residential area affects the 
agricultural protection area. Concerns for State Road 165 were brought up also. State Road 165 isn’t wide 
enough for the traffic with all the agricultural equipment that uses the road now. Hyrum City has sent an email of 
inquiry for possible annexation of this area. 
 
Smith motioned to recommend denial  to the County Council based on it not being a moderately dense pattern 
and impeding adjacent agricultural uses; Gunnell seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
06:11:00 
 
#3 Public Hearing (5:50 p.m.): Rosehill Farms II Rezone 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for Rosehill Farms II Rezone. 
 
06:16:00 
 
Smith motioned to open the public hearing; Christensen seconded; Passed 6, 0.

Ted Wilkinson commented that he does have the water rights for 15 homes currently and they will have to be 
wells because Hyrum City won’t bring the water out. There has been discussion for possible annexation with 
Hyrum City. 
 
Mr. Jewkes commented on the area being very wet ground. The road also has issues, especially with the amount 
of farm equipment that goes down the road. 
 
Mr. Cody Matthews commented on concerns for the surrounding agricultural interests, and wells. 
 
06:21:00 
 
Gunnell motioned to close the hearing; Olsen seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the proposal. Hyrum does have an annexation plan for the area. There is no 
application for annexation at this time but there has been an inquiry. The density of th e proposed zone was 
discussed.  
 
Gunnell motioned to recommend denial to the County Council based on it not being a moderately dense pattern 
area and impediments to agricultural interests in the area; Smith seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
06:29:00 
 
#4 Public Hearing (6:05 p.m.): Cache County Trails and Active Transportation Master Plan 
 
Runhaar introduced Dayton Crites to the commission. 
 
Dayton Crites reviewed the Cache County Trails and Active Transportation Master Plan.  
 
Staff and Commission discussed the Trails Master Plan. 
 
07:13:00 
 
Smith motioned to open the public hearing; Christensen seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
Joel Fuhriman commended the commission on their decision on the previous items. Initially Mr. Fuhriman was 
opposed to this project, but is no longer as he now understands  private land owners cannot be forced to give 
property for trails.  
 
07:15:00 
 
Olsen motioned to close the public hearing; Gunnell seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the Trails Master Plan. The map that was handed out  is available online and 
there was an open house held on August 24, 2017  
 
Smith motioned to continue the item until November’s meeting for additional information to be given to the 
public and for the commission to have another month to look at the given information; Christensen; Passed 6, 0.
