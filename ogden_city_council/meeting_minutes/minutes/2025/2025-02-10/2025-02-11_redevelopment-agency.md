Report; the presentation touched on the following:
              • The Mission and Value Statements for the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency (RDA);
              • Areas of focus for the RDA:
                       o Redevelopment
                       o Economic Development
                       o Housing Development
              • The role of the RDA in Ogden’s City’s financial health;
              • Tax Increment Financing (TIF);
              • The current RDA project areas and their expiration years;
              • Project area outcomes for expired areas;
              • Key activities in 2024;
             • Upcoming activities; and
             • Profiles for current project areas;
         For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
         Discussion among the Board and Mr. Johnson centered on topics such as opportunities for accepting public input

Public Comments
          Linda Sanchez stated that in a prior Council meeting, there was an action prohibiting multi-family development on a
main road or on a commercial property; however, there is an action on tonight’s City Council agenda to allow just that. She then
stated her 90-year old mother-in-law lives with her currently and she wanted to share what she currently sees in the City. She
noticed there are way too many stores and other buildings that are not open to the public or are boarded up and vacant. These
buildings are targets for homelessness and could result in fires or other damage.
          Travis Pate stated he recently met with a member of City staff to express the dynamic of his neighborhood and his ideas
about its future; many residents are investing in the area, and it is important to recognize that investment and pursue
redevelopment projects that will be the catalyst for continued private investment in the community. He is opposed to wiping out
historic buildings and properties in favor of new development.
          Debra Darrington stated she has opened several businesses in Ogden and would like to open more, but one of her
concerns about being a resident of Ogden is that she does not have access to a local store that offers quality clothing for men,
women, and children. Most residents are shopping in Riverdale because the retail offers in Ogden are lacking. She opened a pizza
restaurant in Ogden, and it was shut down two weeks after it opened because the counter was 38 inches high rather than 36 and it
was not compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). These are the types of barriers that are keeping people from
investing in Ogden. She relayed a story of another potential business that was never started because the City did not have a
mechanism to allow them to sell soap out of a cart. The City needs to pave the way for improving the economic base of Ogden
other than beer and tattoos.
          Heath Satow thanked Council members Choberka and Graf for the great questions they asked during tonight’s work
session meeting; he agreed that some processes and projects do not feel transparent at this time. He is grateful there was a
discussion about the housing data that is used to populate certain reports and agreements coming from the Ogden-Clearfield area,
rather than just Ogden. This is important because it impacts housing inventory and the average income of the population of that
area.

Executive Director Comments
          Executive Director Nadolski stated he is not sure what else to do or how much harder to try to be transparent; he is tired
of the claims of a lack of transparency and is not sure how much more he can do. He asked for suggestions for improving
transparency. He then addressed Ms. Darrington’s comments about her pizza restaurant and stated that is the first he has heard of
that issue; there is work underway to perform a ‘deep-dive’ of the Business Services division to try to address those types of
issues. He stated that he truly believes the City staff ‘hits the mark’ 95 percent of the time but only hears about the few instances
that do not go perfectly and that is what he is committed to fixing and changing.
