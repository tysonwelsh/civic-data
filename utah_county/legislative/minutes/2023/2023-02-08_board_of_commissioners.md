1. DISCUSS THE PROPOSED OPIOID SETTLEMENT FUND BUDGET
        -Ben VanNoy, Attorney

(Timestamp: 6:13)

BEN VAN NOY, DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY – STATED THAT THE 3 MAIN
COMPONENTS OF THIS ARE WASATCH BEHAVIORAL HEALTH, THE SHERIFF’S
OFFICE AND THE HEALTH DEPARTMENT THAT WOULD BE PROVIDING OPIOID
REMEDIAL SERVICES. IN NOVEMBER 2022, A 2-YEAR PROPOSED BUDGET WAS
PRESENTED BUT THE COMMISSION REQUESTED THAT WE BROADEN THE SCOPE
TO A 5-YEAR BUDGET. BEFORE THE COUNTY GOT ANY OF THE MONEY, THERE
WAS A 20% OF THE FUND THAT WAS PUT INTO AN ATTORNEY’S FEE FUND TO
MAKE SURE THAT OUR ATTORNEY’S GET PAID. FOR THE SETTLEMENT
AGREEMENT HAD TO GO THROUGH THE NATIONAL FUND FIRST.

THE COUNTY IS MORE ½ WAY THROUGH THE FIRST YEAR’S SETTLEMENT.
STATED THAT WE HAVE A HEALTH FUND BALANCE OF AROUND $2 MILLION BUT
AS WE GET TO YEARS 3 ON WE WOULD BE ASKING THE COUNTY ESSENTIALLY TO
SUPPLEMENT THIS BUDGET BY $600,000 FOR THE 4TH YEAR AND $1.6 MILLION
THE 5TH YEAR. ALSO STATED THAT THERE ARE AT LEAST 3 OTHER LAWSUITS
THAT ARE SOON TO SETTLE THIS SPRING THAT SHOULD GIVE US ANOTHER $17
MILLION OVER THE COURSE OF A DECADE. THIS IS TOTALLY A PROJECTION.

COMMISSIONERS, BEN VAN NOY, JEREMY WALKER (DIRECTOR OF FINANCIAL
SERVICES) DISCUSS ITEMS SUCH AS INFLATION GROWTH PROJECTIONS, RISK
FACTORS, ETC.

       -Bryce Armstrong, Community Development

(Timestamp: 17:58)

BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATE DIRECTOR –
STATED THAT THIS A ZONE MAP AMENDMENT FOR PROPERTY LOCATED ON
WEST MOUNTAIN THAT IS CURRENTLY IN THE GRAZING ZONE G-1 ZONE. THE
APPLICANT IS ASKING TO REZONE THE PROPERTY TO OUR RESIDENTIAL
AGRICULTURE DESIGNATION WHICH IS ONE OF THE COUNTY’S MAIN
AGRICULTURAL ZONES WHICH IS INTENDED TO BE AREAS THAT ARE SUITABLE
FOR CROP PRODUCTION. THE LAND USE ORDINANCE SPELLS OUT CRITERIA TO
BE LOOK AT THROUGH THESE TYPES OF REQUESTS SUCH AS: MUST BE
CONSISTENT WITH THE GENERAL PLAN, THAT THE REQUEST IS IN THE BEST
INTEREST OF THE HEALTH, SAFETY AND WELFARE OF THE PUBLIC AND SHOULD

COUNTY THAT HAVE BEEN HISTORICALLY IRRIGATED AND UTILIZED FOR THE
GROWING OF CROPS AND RAISING OF LIVESTOCK.

10% OF THE PROPERTY. ALSO DISCUSSED THE CORRECT BERM FOR DEBRIS
