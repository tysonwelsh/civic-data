Public Comment (3:50) No public comment was received. Approve October 5, 2023 Meeting Minutes (4:14) Mayor Frost moved to approve the October 5, 2023 meeting minutes as presented. Commissioner Sakievich seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Approve Vetting Criteria: 2024 Legislative Project Prioritization LaNiece Davenport, MAG (5:07) LaNiece Davenport explained that the Project Prioritization-2024 Legislative Strategy outlines a new process that MPO Board members could use to create a list of countywide prioritized transportation projects for any one-time funding from the 2024 legislative session. The list is meant to be a decision tool lens to understand better the projects based on criteria and was requested by legislators. Shawn Eliot provided detailed information on the process MAG used to determine the ranking criteria for both roads and transit. The Board members discussed their concerns and identified projects that may or may not have political support, and were given the opportunity to remove projects that are not ready to be considered for funding. MPO Board November 2, 2023 Page 3 Mary DeLaMare-Schaefer stated that UTA has planning money to move projects forward and recommended MAG staff remove all the transit projects from the priority list except for the Sharp-Tintic Railroad Realignment in Springville. Mayor Kaufusi said the 820 North Interchange Project isn’t ready to move forward and requested that it be removed from the list, and Ryan Clark said to remove the I-15/Orem 800 S/Campus Drive project as well. All MPO Board voting members and legislators will have the opportunity to vote on the Project List at the MPO Board meeting in December. Further discussion took place to finalize the vetting criteria and process. Vetting Criteria and Additional Considerations Approved by the MPO Board ● Approved the Vetting Criteria ● Top 10 projects ○ With a balance of high and low cost projects ○ With at least 1 north and 1 south county project ● Each MPO Board member and legislator has 5 votes ○ Each vote must be used for a different/separate project, i.e. cannot vote for any one project more than once ● MPO Board members and legislators (or their proxy) to vote in person or virtually during the December meeting. Votes can be accepted prior to the meeting on an as needed basis. ● Votes will be anonymous. ● All but 1 transit project will be removed from the list. UTA requested that the studies happening now for transit projects conclude before the county prioritize them. Transit Project #T8 Sharp/Tintic Railroad Alignment will be the only transit project on the list due to its impact on nearby highway projects at I-15/Springville 1600 S Interchange & Springville 1600 S/Spanish Fork 2700 N. Commissioner Gordon moved to approve the Vetting Criteria and any changes based on today’s discussion. Mayor Miller seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Other Business and Adjournment (1:16:06) Commissioner Gordon announced that an email would be sent to the mayors inviting them to a meeting to discuss the possibility of having a major league baseball team in Utah and what that means for the cities. Dinner will be provided at the meeting on November 30th at 6 pm at the County Building. MPO Board November 2, 2023 Page 4 Dave Stewart encouraged MPO Board members to contact their legislators and senators and encourage them to attend and vote at the December MPO Board meeting. Shauna Mecham announced that MAG recently purchased a foldable e-bike that is available for mayors and cities to use. The next meeting is scheduled for December 7, 2023. Mayor Packard moved to adjourn the meeting at 6:57 pm. Mayor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. *Meeting minutes are recorded.
