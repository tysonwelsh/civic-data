ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-2-2021 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone text change to add
          regulations for internal accessory dwelling units. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-
          963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-2-2021  Petition by NORTHROP GRUMMAN INNOVATION SYSTEMS LLC
          requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size 1/2 acre) to RE
          (Residential Estate) and R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot
          size 8,000 sq. ft.). The property is located at 6865 W 4100 S on 7.6 acres. (Staff –
          Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained the subject property is a portion of a parcel. Steve
               pointed out the L-shaped land to the east of the subject property, being part of the
               same parcel and already zoned R-1-8. Steve stated that the existing homes to the
               west had installed a fence that is two feet onto the neighboring property. Instead
               of the applicant going to each property owner and asking for them to either
               remove or relocate the fence, they would instead shift everything to the west. So,
               the applicant is requesting the zone change to R-1-8 for that portion of the subject
               property. Steve then pointed out another fence that impacts lots 109, 110, and
               230 by encroaching onto the subject property, reducing the depth of the lots. The
               applicant will need to address this with the neighboring homeowners for removal
               or relocation. Steve explained this also being the reason for the setback exception
               request.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if it is only the two feet they are looking to rezone R-
               1-8. Steve said that is correct. He said the code allows for this request if it is
               under two acres and this is under two acres.

GENERAL PLAN/ZONE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-4-2021 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting amendments to the General Plan
           and rezoning of certain properties in West Valley City. A full list of affected
           properties and proposed General Plan and zone changes is available at
           (http://bit.ly/3eLPDOw). (Planning and Zoning at 801-963-3282)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that this proposal is also a result of a bill passed by the
               Utah Legislature in the 2021 General Session, HB 98. This bill restricts the City’s
               design standards for single-family, duplexes, and townhome developments.

               Steve said most of the properties within the City that have not been developed are
               covered by a development agreement or zoned A (agriculture). If someone is
               looking to build within the A zone, a development agreement could contain the
               design standards.

               Steve explained that some properties within the City zoned R-1-6, R-1-8, or RM
               that could decide to build either single family or multi-family housing, and the
               City would only be able to apply the restricted design standards outlined in HB
               98. He said this proposal is for these properties to be rezoned to either a
               commercial or agriculture zone. The commercial zone does not allow single-
               family homes, and the agriculture zone can have a development agreement with
               design standards. Steve said eight properties are being proposed. He stated there
               were nine properties, but one was removed because the owner agreed to enter into
               a development agreement. Steve presented each of the eight properties explaining
               the requested zone change and General plan change (if needed).

               Commissioner Woodruff said if someone is looking to develop a multi-family
               project after this application passes, it will be challenging to get the original
               zoning back. Steve said that is a possibility. However, some parcels can still
               request a rezone for multi-family development.

GPZ-6-2021 Petition by GREG LARSEN requesting a General Plan change from Low Density
           Residential (3 to 4 units/acre) and Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to
           Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) and a zone change from A
           (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling
           Residential). The property is located at 3525 S 6800 W on 9.9 acres. (Staff –
           Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik explained that the subject property is a combination of five parcels.
               He pointed out a few agricultural buildings on the property, but most of the land is
               vacant. Steve said the plan is for a senior community development with sixty-five
               units. The main (right-in, right-out) access is on 3500 South and the second access
               is onto 6800 West. Steve presented a second concept plan that does not have
               access off of 6800 West. The concept plan with access to 6800 W has 48.5% open
               space, and the one without access to 6800 W has 49.2%, and the required open
               space is 50%. Steve explained that staff prefers access to 6800 West and the
               amenities offered to warrant a reduction in landscaping.

               Steve explained that 6800 West is a complicated street with a mix of right-of-way
               widths and a lack of improvements. He said the development agreement contains
               staff improvements from the south end of the property to 3500 South.

               Commissioner Winters asked what the zoning is for the residential property
               adjacent to the subject property. Steve said the zoning is R-1-8 to the east and
               west of the subject location.
