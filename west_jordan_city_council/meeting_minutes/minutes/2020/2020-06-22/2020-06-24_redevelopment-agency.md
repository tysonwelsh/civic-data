                         APPROVED MINUTES OF THE CITY OF WEST JORDAN
                            REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY BOARD MEETING

                                        Wednesday, June 10, 2020 - 5:30 pm
                                          Approved September 9, 2020

                 This meeting was held electronically via Zoom and streamed on West Jordan’s YouTube channel
                           Base location at City Hall 8000 S Redwood Road • West Jordan, UT 84088


BOARD:        Chris McConnehey, Chair; Board Members Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob (joined at
              5:37 pm), Chad Lamb, David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, and Melissa Worthen

STAFF:        Interim Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson, Community Engagement
              Director Tauni Barker, Mayor Dirk Burton, Senior Engineer Todd Johnson,
              Development Services Director Scott Langford, Chief Administrative Officer
              Korban Lee, Fire Chief Derek Maxfield, IT Administrative Assistant Rachel
              MacKay, Assistant City Attorney Duncan Murray, Capital Projects Manager David
              Murphy, Parks Division Manager Dave Naylor, IT Manager Michael Oliver, City
              Recorder Cindy Quick, Finance Director Danyce Steck, City Attorney Robert Wall,
              Police Chief Ken Wallentine


1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair McConnehey called the RDA meeting to order at 5:41 p.m.

2. BUSINESS ITEMS
   a. Consider approving the minutes of May 27, 2020
The minutes of the May 27, 2020 RDA meeting were presented.

MOTION:       Board Member Green moved to approve the minutes of the May 27, 2020 RDA
              meeting. Board Member Jacob seconded the motion, which passed by
              unanimous vote (7-0).

  b. Consider approving Resolution No. 223 adopting the Budget for the City of West
      Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2020-2021.
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck presented the FY 2021 Budget for the RDA.

MOTION:       Board Member Whitelock moved to approve Resolution No. 223 adopting the
              budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year
              2020-2021. Board Member Jacob seconded the motion.

Board Member Whitelock expressed the opinion that hiring an Economic Development Director
should be a priority for City Administration. She disagreed with statements that West Jordan had
plenty of retail and commercial.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Board Member Green           Yes
Board Member Jacob           Yes
Board Member Lamb            Yes
Board Member Pack            Yes

       3. PUBLIC HEARING
          a. Public Hearing and consideration of Resolution No. 222 regarding amendments to the
              Budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2019-2020.
       Ms. Steck presented amendments to the FY 2020 RDA Budget, and answered questions from the
       Board. The RDA Fund would have an ending amended reserve balance of $5,972,474.

       Chair Chris McConnehey opened the public hearing at 5:52 PM

       Citizen Comments:
       None

       Chair Chris McConnehey closed the public hearing at 5:53 PM

       MOTION:         Board Member Green moved to approve Resolution No. 222 amending the
                       budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year
                       2019-2020. Board Member Worthen seconded the motion.

       The vote was recorded as follows:
       Board Member Green           Yes
       Board Member Jacob           Yes
       Board Member Lamb            Yes
       Board Member Pack            Yes
       Board Member Whitelock Yes
       Board Member Worthen Yes
       Chair McConnehey             Yes
       The motion passed 7-0.

       4. ADJOURN

       MOTION:         Chair McConnehey moved to adjourn the RDA meeting and return to City
                       Council work session at 5:54 p.m. Board Member Green seconded the motion,
                       which passed by unanimous vote (7-0).
