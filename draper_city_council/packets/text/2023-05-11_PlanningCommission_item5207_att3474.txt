BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 12.99
acres located on the east side of Lone Peak Parkway, at approximately 12047 S Lone Peak
Parkway (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned CSD-LPA. The applicant is
requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for property lines within the
subdivision to be adjusted.

The Loveland Living Planet Aquarium was originally approved in 2012 with the creation of
the CSD-LPA zone and approval of a site plan. The Aquarium expanded in 2019 with an
outdoor plaza and stage referred to as the Claw. On November 10, 2022 the Planning
Commission approved an Amended Site Plan to allow for a new Science Learning Center
expansion. This expansion will feature event space, classrooms, exhibit areas, and
administrative offices.



Living Planet Aquarium Science Learning Center                    App. # SUDB-045-2023
Plat Amendment Request                                 1
Shortly after review of the building permit for the Science Learning Center the Building
Division determined that the proposed Science Learning Center did not meet building code
standards as the new indoor breezeway and entrance that will connect the Science
Learning Center and the existing Aquarium crosses property lines. The International
Building Code (IBC) does not allow buildings to cross property lines without strict fire
separations. In addition, the properties are owned by different entities, Draper City and The
Living Planet, Inc. The City owns the land and parking lot that the current Aquarium
building is on and has a ground lease with the Aquarium.

In order to comply with the IBC, the property lines will need to be adjusted so that the
buildings are all on one property, with a property line set 10-feet away from the existing
building footprint. In addition, the Draper City Council would need to surplus the property
owned by the City that the Aquarium building sits on and amend the ground lease with the
Aquarium. Conditions of approval have been included that state the plat cannot be
recorded until after the City surpluses the ground and amended the ground lease.

         The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request based on the findings
listed below and the criteria for approval, or denial as listed within the staff report. The
Planning Commission should also review the request, hear from the applicant, and receive
public comment prior to making a decision.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

         1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                  Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                  site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
         2.       That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
                  development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
         3.       That if the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date
                  of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure
                  has not commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period
                  may be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6)
                  month period for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing
                  for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.
         4.       Address all outstanding redlines listed in Exhibit A of the staff report prior to
                  plat recordation.
         5.       The Draper City Council will need to vote to approve a surplus of the City
                  owned property prior to the recordation of the Plat Amendment. If the City
                  does not approve a surplus of the City owned property, then the plat cannot
                  be recorded.
