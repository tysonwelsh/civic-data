MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Todd Taylor
Date: 2024-05-09
Re: Public Hearing: Power Stitch, LLC Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
(Administrative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
for approximately 1.07 acres located at 690 E. 12100 S. The property is primarily zoned
RA2, with a small portion zoned RA1 at the rear. The applicant is requesting that a Home
Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow a cottage business that makes
canvas and nylon bags.

The findings for approval as are follows:
  1. Reasonable mitigation measures are placed on the conditional use to alleviate
      anticipated detrimental effects.

The findings for denial as are follows:
  1. Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to
      sufficiently alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
