Work Session to discuss and receive direction on proposed amendments to Chapter 7: Open Space and Chapter 8: Community Health and Recreation of the Snyderville Basin General Plan. This item is for discussion and direction only; no recommendation or action will be taken by the Planning Commission. Ray Milliner, Principal Planner, Mustapha Osman County Planner.
(4:34 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Work Session Report April 15, 2026.pdf
Ray Milliner,
Principal Planner
, Mustapha Osman,
County Planner
, and Deputy Health Director Kendra Babitz led a discussion on Chapters 7 and 8 of the Snyderville Basin General Plan. (4:35 PM)
Commissioners praised the document’s thoroughness and discussed expanding indoor recreation, addressing the Event Center in the Code, joint use of public or private spaces, school and nonprofit partnerships, defining affordable food and housing, and linking initiatives to health outcomes. They emphasized mapping community assets, supporting accessibility, updating general plan language, and considering needs for elder care, broadband, deed restrictions, and demographic balance. Discussion then focused on open space. Commissioners noted that most of the land was already conserved, with little left for acquisition. They called for updated, consistent open-space codes and ongoing maintenance. Topics included expanding public access to event spaces, aligning with state biking and trail initiatives, improving wildlife crossings, clarifying use of open space bonds and easements, managing multi-use trails, and coordinating with neighboring general plans. Support for wildlife corridors and clear signage was also emphasized. Planner Milliner, Planner Osman, and Director Babitz responded. (4:46 PM)
