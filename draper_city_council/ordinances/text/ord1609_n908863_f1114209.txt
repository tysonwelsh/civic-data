City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On April 23, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1609, amending the
official Zoning map of Draper City, otherwise known as the Newell Zoning Map
Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office
and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 24th day of April 2024. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
