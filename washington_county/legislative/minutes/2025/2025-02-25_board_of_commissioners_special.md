Deputy County Attorney Steven Scott, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3468, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE “FEDERAL AID AGREEMENT FOR LOCAL AGENCY PROJECT” BETWEEN

UDOT AND WASHINGTON COUNTY REGARDING THE COST OF IMPROVEMENTS

TO SHEEP BRIDGE ROAD (SR-59 TO SR-9)

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales stated that the County would like to enter into a federal
aid agreement for improvements to Sheep Bridge Road. The agreement would authorize a
maximum cost of $4,875,960 with Washington County contributing $975,960.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3468, A
Resolution Approving the “Federal Aid Agreement for Local Agency Project”
Between UDOT and Washington County Regarding the Cost of Improvements
to Sheep Bridge Road (SR-59 to SR-9). Motion seconded by Commissioner

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3469, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_TRADEMARK LICENSE AGREEMENT WITH THE STATE OF UTAH’S
DEPARTMENT OF CULTURAL AND COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT FOR
WASHINGTON COUNTY’S AMERICA250 LOGO

Deputy County Attorney Steven Scott explained that in preparation of America’s 250" anniversary
in 2026, the County has been working on a committee to celebrate this anniversary. The County
would like to establish a logo for the celebrations planned, and has worked with the State of Utah’s
Department of Cultural and Community Engagement to develop a logo and negotiate a trademark
license agreement.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3469, A
Resolution Approving a Trademark License Agreement with the State of
Utah’s Department of Cultural and Community Engagement for Washington
County’s America250 Logo. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist

voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 3:41 p.m.
