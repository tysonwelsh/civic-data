TransPlan50 Amendment 1-2021 - Shawn Eliot, MAG Every four years, MAG is required to prepare and adopt a Regional Transportation Plan (RTP). The current plan was approved in June 2019. An amendment process allows for changes to be made. Amendment 1-21 proposes two changes: widening Pioneer Crossing and reconfiguring the I- 15/Santaquin Main ST interchange. #20 | Pioneer Crossing; Widen to 6-lanes Over 40,000 trips a day travel this corridor (Bangerter HWY levels of traffic). The current 4-lane cross section needs to be widened to a 6-lanes to handle current volumes. Phase one of the RTP has widening this corridor to 6 lanes between Redwood RD and Lehi 2300 W. This amendment would MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 25, 2021 Page 3 extend these limits to the east ending at Lehi 300 E (east of 300 E to I-15 currently has 6 lanes). The amendment includes: • Change limits of project to Redwood RD to Lehi 300 E. • Change cost from $5.9m to $13.6m. • RTP has enough funding to cover the new cost. • Modeling shows air quality conformity is met. #81 | I-15/Santaquin Main ST Interchange Traffic on Santaquin Main ST, under I-15, is nearing failure. With many new businesses and housing proposed around the interchange, additional capacity is needed. The RTP proposes to reconstruct the interchange in phase two. UDOT has concluded that a retrofit of the current interchange is all that is needed in the next 30 years. It is proposed to remove the phase two reconstruction project and place a add a reconfiguration project in phase one. UDOT will shore up the current sloped embankments under I-15 to create two more travel lanes under the freeway. Main ST will also be widened around the interchange. The amendment includes: • Move project from phase 2 to phase 1 in the RTP. • Change the description from reconstruction to reconfiguration. • Change the cost from $40m to $25m. • RTP has enough funding to cover the new cost. • Modeling shows air quality conformity is met. The proposed amendments allow congested corridors to be addressed UDOT in the near term. Staff recommends approval of the proposed amendment 1-21 to TransPlan50 and the associated Air Quality Conformity Determination. Richard Nielsen moved that MPO TAC recommends to MPO RPC approval of TransPlan50 amendment #1-21 and the associated Air Quality Conformity Determination to allow for UDOT to address congestion along Pioneer Crossing and the I-15/Santaquin Main ST Interchange. Norm Beagley seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
