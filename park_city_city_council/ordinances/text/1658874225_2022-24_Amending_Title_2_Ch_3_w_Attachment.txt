            Ordinance No. 2022-24

                           AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2, CHAPTER 3, SECTIONS 1-6
                                    OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF PARK CITY

                  WHEREAS, in 2009, Title 2, Chapter 3 of the Municipal Code of Park City was
            overhauled to conform to changes in state law and the Six-Member Council Form of
            Municipal Government;

                      WHEREAS, the Sections 1-6 mostly duplicated state law from 2009;

                 WHEREAS, these amendments help avoid conflicts with state law and simplify
            and modernize the City Code.

            NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Park City as follows:

            Sections 2-3-1 through 6 of the Municipal Code of Park City are amended and adopted
            to read as outlined in Exhibit A. This Ordinance shall become effective upon publication.

                      PASSED AND ADOPTED this 14th day of July 2022.
