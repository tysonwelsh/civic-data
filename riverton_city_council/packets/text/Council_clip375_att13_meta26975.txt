FROM:          Ryan Carter, City Attorney

ITEM:          Reappointment of Mayor Staggs to serve on the Board of Trustees for the South
               Valley Sewer District

SUMMARY:

The South Valley Sewer District (“District”) provides sewer service to Riverton City and other cities
in the southwest region of Salt Lake County. The District is governed by a Board of Trustees,
whose members are selected from the governing bodies of the cities that receive sewer service
from the District. The term in office for a Board of Trustees member is 4 years. Mayor Staggs
has served as a board member for the past four years and his term in office is about to expire at
the end of this year. Mayor Staggs is both willing and eligible to be reappointed.

                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       RESOLUTION NO. 19-82

  A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING MAYOR STAGGS TO SERVE AS A BOARD TRUSTEE
                 FOR THE SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT

        WHEREAS, the residents of Riverton City receive sewer service from a local district known as
the South Valley Sewer District (hereinafter referred to as the “District”); and

      WHEREAS, the District is governed by a Board of Trustees, which generally consists of officials
who were elected to serve as officials for the municipalities that receive service from the District; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17b-1-304 vests the Riverton City Council, as an appointing
authority, with the power to appoint a member of the City’s governing body to serve on the District board
of Trustees; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-b-301, Riverton City is governed by a Six
Council Member form of Government, one of which is the Mayor.

         WHEREAS, Mayor Staggs has served faithfully on the District’s Board of Trustees for the
statutory term of four years, and the Mayor’s present term shall expire on December 31, 2019; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Mayor Staggs’ experience
acquired from years of faithful service on the District’s Board of Trustees benefits the residents who
receive sewer service, and therefore Mayor Staggs should be reappointed to the Board of Trustees.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:

        1. The Riverton City Council hereby reappoints Mayor Trent Staggs to serve on the Board of
           Trustees for the South Valley Sewer District for a four-year term commencing January 1,
           2020 until January 1, 2024.

        2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
