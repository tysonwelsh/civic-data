1. 2       Mayor' s Report


 MI Mayor Overson attended a ribbon cutting at Impeccable Barbers on 6200 South. She enjoyed
hearing the proprietors' story as well as learning about their experience working with the city.
The legislative session would end Friday at midnight. She loved the political process and thanked
Representatives Dunnigan, Mauga, Dominguez, and Matthews and Senators Harper and Kwan.
She said they had been extremely attentive and quick to respond to inquiries from Taylorsville,
which she greatly appreciated.

ChamberWest had a professional development series, and she recently attended a seminar on
artificial intelligence with various members of city administration. She was happy to see Council
Members Burgess and Barbieri in attendance as well.

Along with members of the City Council, Mayor Overson enjoyed helping Cub Scout Troop# 4996
celebrate the birthday of scouting. Layla Beckstrand had invited them, and she was happy to report
that scouting was alive and thriving.

The Taylorsville Arts Council had been very busy. The Mayor attended the Menken Musical Revue
which was a very fun evening. The Taylorsville Symphony recently presented( along with the Salt
 Lake Community Symphony) a Wizard- of-Oz- themed concert.

 Mayor Overson always enjoyed visiting with the city' s youngest residents and recently visited
 Mrs. Harker' s 3rd grade class at Calvin Smith Elementary School. The class had used artificial
 intelligence to design a park, and she was invited to watch them make their final presentations. It
 was delightful to see what they came up with and what they were capable of.

 She and Det. Christensen participated in Read Across America with 5th grade students at Arcadia
 Elementary. She had so much fun interacting with the kids in this way and she hoped to do it again
 later in the week.


 Council Member Barbieri had touched briefly on recycling during the briefing session, and both
 had attended a community council meeting the previous night where there was a more in-depth
 recycling presentation. She appreciated all the people in the community who attended the event.
