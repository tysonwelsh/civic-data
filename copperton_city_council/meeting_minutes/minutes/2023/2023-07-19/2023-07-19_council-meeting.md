Law Enforcement Update

                 Mayor Clayton stated progress on law enforcement has been slow due to the
July 4th holiday.

                 Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated Mayor
Silvestrini of Millcreek called him this last week to try to understand where the metro townships
stood on this issue. Mr. Bracken told him it was mostly dependent on cost. According to Mayor
Silvestrini, the cities are united on the idea that the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service
Area (SLVLESA) should remain intact and unchanged. The cities also believe revising Unified
Police Department (UPD) would be the most cost effective option. Mr. Bracken has also had
phone calls with some of the County attorneys and attorneys from other metro townships.

              Council Member Olsen stated he met with Chief Jason Mazuran, who gave him
some ballpark projections. He forwarded these to the Council. Labor increases will be roughly
11.5 percent. Without SLVLESA, it would be closer to 31.5 percent.

HB 374 – Interim Legislative Update

              Mayor Clayton asked Mr. Bracken if the draft bill had been started.

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated it had been
drafted and someone from his office, who used to work with the Office of Legislative Research,
is going to look at it tomorrow to ensure the form is correct. He hopes to send it to the other
metro township attorneys and Representative Jordan Teuscher tomorrow. The interim hearing
will be on August 9th. Bob Reese, the drafting attorney for the political subdivisions committee,
is out of the office until July 31st. The bill would make metro townships cities/towns effective
May 3rd, 2024. The elected officials will remain the same and the mayors will become mayors
“at large” and will be elected by the citizens instead of the council members. This will not
change much where Copperton is concerned. There is also language ensuring that ordinances
and contracts remain in effect.

                Mayor Clayton stated he received a quote from West Jordan City, which he
would like to discuss later in closed session.

              Mr. Bracken stated this closed session would pertain to “deployment of security
personnel”.

               Mayor Clayton stated there are some unincorporated areas in Northern Utah
that are being pushed into becoming cities/towns. He wants to ensure that the metro townships
do not become involved in this because they are two separate items.

              Mr. Bracken stated the bill addresses this.

Rezone REZ2023-000840

                Curtis Woodward, Senior Planner, Municipal Services District, stated Charles
Adderley who resides at 8580 W. State Highway requested his property be rezoned from a C-2
to an (Neighborhood Mixed Zone) NMU. There is a pending ordinance called an NMU that
would allow for the construction of the home but leave the door open for commercial uses. The
planning commission voted to approve this back in June but needed to wait to bring it before the
Council. Mr. Adderley has applied to be allowed to replace the existing storage building with a
new one. The application has already been processed under a C-2 zone. Mr. Adderley was
present to answer any questions.

             Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Copperton Metro Township, stated an
ordinance summary needed to be posted.

                        COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

ORDINANCE NO. 2023-07-01                                                  DATE: July 19, 2023

   AN ORDINANCE OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AMENDING
   THE ZONING MAP TO CHANGE THE ZONING OF THE PROPERTY AT 8580 WEST
   STATE HIGHWAY FROM C-2 (COMMERCIAL) ZONE TO THE NMU
   (NEIGHBORHOOD MIXED-USE) ZONE.

                                          RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District provides services to the five Metro
Townships in the Salt Lake Valley, unincorporated areas, and the Town of Brighton; and

WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township is a municipality and has authority to regulate
Zoning in general pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Subsection 10-3c-103 (2); and



                                               4
DATE            WEDNESDAY                                 JULY                            19, 2023

WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township has authority to adopt zoning ordinances, including a
zoning map pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-501 in accordance with the Municipal Land
Use, Development, and Management Act, (“MLUDMA”), Title 10, Section 9a, Utah Code, to
establish zones within the metro township; and

WHEREAS, the Council deems it necessary to amend its zoning map in order to accommodate
the rezone of the property located at: 8580 West State Highway; and for the protection and
preservation of the public health, safety and general welfare.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL as follows:

1.      Section 19.14.020, the Zoning Map of Copperton Metro Township is hereby amended as
follows:

The property described in Application #_REZ2023-000840 filed by Charles Adderley, and
located at 8580 West State Highway, within the Copperton Metro Township, is hereby
reclassified from the C-2 (Commercial) zone to the NMU (Neighborhood Mixed-Use) zone, said
property being described as follows:

PARCEL #: 26-08-376-001-0000

LEGAL DESCRIPTION: Commencing 1396 feet east from the southwest corner of Section 8,
Township 4 South, Range 2 West, Salt Lake Basin Meridian; then proceeding east 100 feet,
north 400 feet, west 100 feet, and south 400 feet to the point of beginning. The property is
measured to be approximately 0.92 acres.

2.      The official zoning map kept on file with the Planning and Development Services
Division of the Municipal Services District of Greater Salt Lake shall be changed to reflect this
ordinance.

3.     Effective Date. This ordinance will take effect immediately upon posting and publication
as required by law.

PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS 19th day of July, 2023.
