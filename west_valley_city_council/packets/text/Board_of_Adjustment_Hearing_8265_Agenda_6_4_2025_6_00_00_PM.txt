                                                                                 AGENDA

 VARIANCE
 B-1-2025               Peter Corroon, representing Salt Lake Brewing Company is requesting a variance from Section
                        11-5-109 of the West Valley Municipal Code. This title requires that the area limitation for
                        wall signs on the face of a building or structure in the C-2 zone be 10 percent of the first story
                        building face for the front face of the building and five percent of the first story building face
                        on all other faces of the building. The applicant is requesting a variance to allow the east and
                        north sides of their building to have a 1.1% and 3.2% increase respectively over what the
                        ordinance allows. The overall signage for the building will still comply with City ordinances.
                        The property is located at 3555 South Constitution Boulevard and is zoned C-2.
