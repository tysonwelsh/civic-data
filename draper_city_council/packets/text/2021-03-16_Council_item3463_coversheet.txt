Re: Shipp Barn (Stokes) – Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately 2.52 acres located on the south side of Stokes Ave, at approximately 570 East Stokes Ave. The property is currently zoned RA2 . The applicant is requesting that a Development Agreement be approved to allow for the development of the property for a future residential subdivision.
On February 11, 2021 the Planning Commission reviewed the request and forwarded a positive recommendation on a 5-0 vote. The recommendation included that all items still being reviewed by staff be satisfactorily agreed upon. The Development Agreement has been updated to address the concerns discussed during the Planning Commission meeting.  The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The Development Agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200.
2.  The proposed deviations appear to be reasonable and potential detrimental effects are off-set by the proposal to preserve the existing home and retain farm land as prescribed in the agreement.
For denial:
1.  The proposed deviations do not appear to be reasonable and potential detrimental effects are not off-set by the proposal to preserve the existing home and retain farm land. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Ord 1481 3/9/2021 Cover Memo Concept Plan 3/9/2021 Cover Memo
