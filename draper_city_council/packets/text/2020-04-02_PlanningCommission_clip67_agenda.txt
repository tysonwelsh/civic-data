6:30 PM Business Meeting

1.    Action Item: Approve Planning Commission Meeting Minutes for February 13,
      2020 (Administrative Action)
2.    Action Item: Approve Planning Commission Meeting Minutes for February 27,
      2020 (Administrative Action)
3.    Public Hearing: Seidel Residence Plat Amendment (Administrative Action)
      A request by Stephen & Diana Seidel for approval of a Plat Amendment in the RA2
      (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) Zone to vacate a portion of an
      access easement to allow for a new home construction. The property is located at 1888 E
      12200 S. Application SUBD-890-2020. Staff contact: Pete Kane, 801-576-6335,
      pete.kane@draper.ut.us.
4.    Public Hearing: EPG Pony Express Development Agreement, Land Use Map
      Amendment, & Zoning Map Amendment (Legislative Action)
      A request by C. Ryan Dickson, representing Exeter Draper PSA, LLC, for approval of a
      Development Agreement, Land Use Map Amendment from Residential Low/Medium
      Density and Community Commercial to Business/Light Manufacturing, and a Zoning Map
      Amendment from the RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot minimum lot)
      Zone to M1 (Light Manufacturing) for the redevelopment of the Greenfield Farms
      Subdivision into a warehouse distribution development. The project is located on multiple
      properties - the Greenfield Farms Subdivision (all of Wheatfield Circle; all of Wheatfield
        Way; 79, 101, & 123 W Golden Harvest Road). Applications DA-955-2020, TEXTMAP-
        948-2020, & TEXTMAP-949-2020. Staff contact: Pete Kane, 801-576-6335,
        pete.kane@draper.ut.us.
5.      Public Hearing: Restaurant Sign Text Amendment Request (Legislative Action)
        On the request of Justin Grubb with YESCO LLC to amend Chapter 9-26-070(7)(e) of
        the Draper City Municipal Code to accommodate electronic message menu boards for
        restaurant drive-throughs. Staff Contact is Travis Van Ekelenburg, 801-576-6522 or
        travis.vanekelenburg@draper.ut.us. Application number: TEXTMAP-952-2020
6.      Public Hearing: Highline Signage Text Amendment Request (Legislative Action)
        On the request of Justin Grubb with YESCO LLC to amend Draper City Municipal Code
        (DCMC) Section 9-18K-070 Signage. The applicant is requesting that a Text Amendment
        be approved to increase the allowed sign area in the CSD Highline Zone. Staff Contact is
        Travis Van Ekelenburg, 801-576-6522 or travis.vanekelenburg@draper.ut.us. Application
        number: TEXTMAP-952-2020
7.      Public Hearing: Draper Plaza Plat Amendment (Administrative Item)
        A request by David Hamilton, representing Mountain High Real Estate Advisors, for a plat
        amendment of the Draper Plaza Subdivision. The applicant is proposing to internally
        reconfigure the existing four lots into six new lots. The exterior boundary of the subdivision
        will not be affected. The property is located at approximately 11479 S. State Street in the
        CR (Regional Commercial) zoning district. Staff contact: Maryann Pickering at (801) 576-
        6391 or maryann.pickering@draper.ut.us.
8.      Public Hearing: Burt Brothers Tire Remodel Plat Amendment & Site Plan Review
        (Administrative Action)
        A request by Sam Gowans, representing Legendary Construction, for the approval of a
        Site Plan and Plat Amendment in the CR (Regional Commercial) Zone for the remodel
        and addition of a previous automotive service center and construction of an additional
        parking lot. The property is located at 263 E & 267 E 12300 S. Applications SPR-898-
        2019 & SUBD-899-2019. Staff contact: Pete Kane, 801-576-6335,
        pete.kane@draper.ut.us.
9.      Public Hearing: Big Willow Creek 2nd Amendment Development Agreement
        Request (Legislative Action)
        On the request of Bryon Prince, representing Ivory Development, LLC, for a development
        agreement on 20.9 acres of property located approximately at 491 West 11400 South. The
        agreement will authorize various deviations to the Draper City Municipal Code Title 9 Land
        Use and Development standards in regards to density allocation and minimum lot size.
        Application number DA-931-2019. Staff contact is Jennifer Jastremsky, 801-576-6328,
        jennifer.jastremsky@draper.ut.us.
10.     Adjournment
Any person adversely affected by a decision of the Planning Commission regarding the transfer,
issuance or denial of a conditional use permit may appeal such decision to the City Council by filing
written notice of appeal stating the grounds therefore within fourteen (14) days from the date of such
final determination.
