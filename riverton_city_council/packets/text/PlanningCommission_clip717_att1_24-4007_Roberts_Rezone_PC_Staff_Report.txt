                                 MEMORANDUM
To:              Planning Commission
From:            Planning Department
Date:            November 14, 2024
Subject:         “Roberts Rezone,” PLZ-24-4007, Rezone of approximately 0.91-acres
                 located at 4191 West 13400 South from R-1 (Residential 1-acre min) to C-R
                 (Regional Commercial). Applicant- Jay and Shelly Roberts


This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-24-4007,
“Roberts Rezone,” by rezoning a 0.91-acre parcel located at 4191 West 13400 South to C-R
(Regional Commercial).

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-24-4007, for
rezoning 0.91- acres located at 4191 West 13400 South, thus keeping the current zoning of
R-1 (1-acre minimum lots).


Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicants, Jay and Shelly
Roberts, for about 0.91-acres located at 4191 West 13400 South. The property is currently
zoned R-1 which is a single-family residential zone with minimum lot size of 1-acre, the
requested zone is C-R which is Regional Commercial. The General Plan designation for this
property is Professional Office.

The subject parcel is a rectangularly-shaped property just under an acre in size, located on the
south side of 13400 South. Rose Creek runs along the south of the property and SLR property
borders it on the west.

The Roberts Property is the last of several similar properties to request a rezone. Most of the
other properties were rezoned in 2008, and one more was rezoned in 2023. This rezone
request matches what the city has done on all the similarly situated properties and will make the
area have consistent zoning.
Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
webpage. No public comments have been received.

When considering rezone applications, the Planning Commission makes a recommendation to
City Council. The City Council will make a final decision on this application in a future City Council
Meeting. The City will send additional notices to property owners within 1,000 feet to announce
the City Council meeting where a final decision will be made.
