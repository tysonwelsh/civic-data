Re: Public Hearing: Jim Duncan Fence Conditional Use Permit Request (Administrative

Comments:


A request for approval of a Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.50 acres located
on the west side of 800 East at approximately 12136 S. 800 E. The property is currently
zoned RA2 (Residential, Agriculture). The applicant is requesting that a Conditional Use
Permit be approved to allow an eight-foot (8’) tall fence to be installed. The purpose of the
fence is to provide a barrier around a sports court.




Findings for approval:

  1. Reasonable mitigation measures are placed on the conditional use to alleviate
     anticipated detrimental effects.


Findings for denial:


  1. Reasonable mitigation measures are not able to be placed on the conditional use to
     sufficiently alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
