                         KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

         RESOLUTION NO. 2022-11-04                                    DATE: 11-28-2022

                       A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE
               KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP’S AMENDED 2022 BUDGET

                                           RECITALS

       WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, on November 14, 2022, Kearns adopted a tentative amended budget for
the 2022 fiscal year; and

        WHEREAS, Kearns provided notice pursuant to Utah Code Ann § 10-6-113 concerning
the tentative amended budget and of the date, time, and place of a public hearing on the same; and

        WHEREAS, Kearns made the tentative amended budget available for public inspection for
a period of at least ten (10) days prior to the public hearing in accordance with Utah Code Ann. §
10-6-112; and

      WHEREAS, on November 28, 2022, Kearns held the public hearing on the tentative
amended budget in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-114.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

        1.     That the tentative amended budget adopted on November 14, 2022, and attached
hereto, be and hereby is adopted as the Amended Budget of Kearns for the 2022 fiscal year.

       2.      The Kearns budget officer is directed and authorized to file the Amended budget
with the Utah State Auditor within thirty (30) days of the date of this resolution pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-6-118(2).

       3.      This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
