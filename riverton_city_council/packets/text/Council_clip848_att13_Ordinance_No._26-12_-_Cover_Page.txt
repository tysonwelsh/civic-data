                                       City Council Meeting Agenda
                                               June 2, 2026



PRESENTER:             Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director


ITEM:
Ordinance No. 26-12 - Amending Section 9.20.010(1), Adopting Standards for the Discharge of a Firearm
as Part of the Firearm Manufacturing Process, Amendments Proposed by Zev Technologies. - Jason
Lethbridge, Development Services Director
“I move the City Council adopt Ordinance 26-12, amending Section 9.20.010, adopting standards for the
discharge of a firearm as part of the firearm manufacturing process, as shown in Exhibit “A.”


SUMMARY:


Riverton City Code Section 9.20.010 currently prohibits the discharge of a firearm within Riverton City
limits. Zev Technologies has submitted this application to amend that section to allow the discharge of a
firearm for testing purposes during the manufacturing process. Testing of firearms and associated
equipment during the manufacturing process is typical, and staff has reviewed the proposed language with
the City Attorney’s Office and the Riverton Police Department. Both made amendments to the proposed
language, and the code before the Council for consideration reflects the input from those offices. It should
be noted that this amendment only allows the discharge of a firearm during manufacturing under control and
permitted circumstances, and does not allow for outside or external discharge, nor for any type of
recreational, “shooting range”, or other firearm discharge.
