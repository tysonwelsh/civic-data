ZT-5-2021
Petersen Staggs Architects, LLP

The applicant, Petersen Staggs Architects, LLP, has requested a proposed amendment to Section
7-14-404 of the West Valley City Municipal Code, Use Limitations in the 5600 West Overlay
Zone.

Fast food establishments with drive-through windows are currently allowed in the C-2, C-3, M
and MXD zones. The following overlay zones restrict fast food establishments with drive-up
windows:
           • Decker Lake Station Overlay Zone - Established in 1996 - Prohibited
           • Jordan River Overlay Zone - Established in 1998 - Prohibited
           • 5600 West Overlay Zone – Established in 2004, amended in 2012 – Allowed
               under certain conditions

The 5600 West Overlay limits the amount of fast food establishments allowed in a building with
a drive-through as follows:

7-14-404 USE LIMITATIONS

       (3) Fast Food Establishments with drive-up windows unless the following conditions are
       met:
              a. The Fast Food Establishment shall be part of a multi-tenant Building with only
              one drive-through.
              b. The Building square footage used by or accessible to Fast Food Establishments
              shall be less than or equal to the Building square footage used by or accessible to
              other tenants within the Building.
              c. The view of the drive-through area from the Street shall be minimized through
              Landscaping, berming, and/or other screening.
              d. The drive-up window shall be placed on the side or rear of the multi-tenant
              Building.
              e. The drive-up window shall comply with the Drive-Through Window Standards
              in Section 7-11-314.

The applicant would like to remove the square footage requirement, item (b.), from above to
allow more flexibility of tenants in the 5600 West Overlay as indicated in the attached request.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval of the proposed ordinance amendment.

   2. Continuance, to allow for the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing or to
      allow time for the applicant to revise the proposed regulations in the ordinance
      amendment.

   3. Denial, the current Ordinance requirements are adequate, and this amendment is not
      appropriate at this time.


Discussion:   Jody Knapp presented the application. During the study meeting last week, Jody
              stated that there were questions about what defines a fast-food establishment, so
              she included the definition in her presentation. She explained that the applicant is
              proposing a text change regarding the 5600 West Overlay Zone requirements and
              not the definition of a fast-food establishment.

              Greg Goins, representing WinCo, said that Petersen Staggs Architects, LLP is
              actually representing WinCo in this proposal. Mr. Goins explained that since the
              COVID pandemic, the food user had become the predominant small shop user. He
              said that these three tenants would be a benefit to West Valley City and WinCo.


Motion:       Commissioner Wood motioned to approve ZT-5-2021 subject to the staff report.
              Commissioner Lovato seconded the motion.

CA-8-2021
T-Mobile (Declan Murphy)
2305 S. 1070 W.
B/RP Zone (6.71 Acres)

T-Mobile is requesting a conditional use amendment for an expansion of roof mounted antennas
at 2305 South 1070 West. This property is zoned business research park (B/RP) and the West
Valley City General Plan designates this area as a Business Park. The surrounding zone is B/RP
on all sides with the Jordan River as the eastern most boundary of the property.

This site was originally approved in March 2015 under C-18-15 for three antennas on separate
tri-pod mounts. In 2016 (C-52-16), the site was expanded to 3 ballast mounts with 2 antennas per
sector for a total of 6 antennas. T-Mobile would like to expand the facility again and proposes
two ballast mount sectors with 3 antennas each but the third sector will be split into 3 separate
tri-pod mounts like the original design, for a total of 9 antennas. This design modification is due
to structural issues with the roof at this location. These antennas will be located in the same spot
as the previous mounts and set back 10’ from the edge of the building and will be 10’ tall, which
complies with the location standards set forth is section 7-7-400p of the WVC Municipal Code.
As previously required, the antennas will be painted brown to match the building. T-Mobile has
not proposed to add any screen type structure around the antennas due to the flat design of the
roof and the spacing of the different sectors.

Because of the poor coverage in the area, the previous tenant in the building initially utilized an
interior T-Mobile network to boost their service until this external macro-site was installed. The
new antennas will update the technology at the site and broadcast a different radio frequency
than what is currently at the site which enables for better cell service and faster download speeds
for this area.

Any equipment changes necessary will be located in the existing screened fenced equipment area
located adjacent to the building. A generator is not proposed for this site.

Staff Alternatives:

      Approval, subject to the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing and the
       following conditions:

           1. The antennas shall be installed per the approved plans and in accordance with
              Chapter 7-7-400p of the WVC Municipal Code.

           2. The antennas shall be painted to match the colors of the existing building.

           3. The supporting equipment shall be located in a screened enclosed area per the
              approved plans.

           4. Must comply with all other relevant requirements set forth from applicable
              department and agencies.

      Continuance, to allow for the resolution of any issues raised at the public hearing.


Discussion:    Jody Knapp presented the application. She explained that one requirement would
               be for the antennas to be painted brown, and the applicant will be reminded of that
               requirement.
