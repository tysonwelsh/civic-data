1. Welcome – Mayor Dahle
Mayor Dahle opened the meeting at 4:13 p.m. and welcomed those in attendance.

2. Introductory Discussion – Gina Chamness
Gina Chamness provided an overview of the purpose of the retreat, highlighting the City’s
opportunity to look beyond past challenges and define a vision of the future. Each member of the
City Council in turn shared why they ran for Council and the underlying values that guide their
decision-making. Ms. Chamness and Holly Smith also provided information on their
professional backgrounds and why they enjoy working for local government.

3. Review of Demographic Data for the City – Gina Chamness
Gina Chamness reviewed data resources featuring key characteristics of the community,
including the report titled “Utah Informed”, produced the Kem Gardner Institute, as a digital data
book designed for policymakers and the Salt Lake County Health Department’s Healthy Salt
Lake website.

Council members had a general discussion on the matters of density, affordable housing,
providing housing choices, etc. and the related interplay with current State legislature initiatives
and laws.

4. Retreat Questions
Council members were asked to individually answer three key questions: (1) What do you value
about our current community, (2) What is your 50-year vision for our community? and (3) In
order to achieve that 50-year vision, what are important areas of focus for the next 10 years?
Council members provided their feedback on post-it notes that were then collected on a large
whiteboard for each corresponding question; responses are summarized below.

Gina Chamness then lead a facilitated discussion highlighting each comment and providing
opportunity for additional dialogue and exchange of ideas. Ms. Chamness also reviewed staff
priorities identified from a Fall 2022 staff retreat.

5.What do we want to focus on for FY 2023-24?
Gina Chamness shared her recommended staffing plan for next 10 years, indicating her top
priorities for new positions for the upcoming FY 2023-24 fiscal year as a
communications/community engagement manager and GIS manager. The need to immediately
hire additional parks works was also discussed. Ms. Chamness reviewed the sales tax budget
from 2020 through 2023. The Council Members expressed their next fiscal year priorities to
include sustainability initiatives, the further development of active transportation facilities and
the need to renovate and seismically upgrade City Hall.

12. Adjourn
Council Member Fotheringham moved to adjourn. Council Member Durham seconded the
motion. The Council voted in the affirmative and the meeting adjourned at 8:59 p.m.
