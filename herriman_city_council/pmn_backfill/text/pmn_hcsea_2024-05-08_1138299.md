                                HERRIMAN CITY SAFETY
                              ENFORCEMENT AREA MINUTES
                                     Wednesday, May 08, 2024
                                      Approved June 20, 2024

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The meeting was held on
Wednesday, May 8, 2024 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main
Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the
City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

       1. Call to Order – 7:00 p.m. (or as soon as possible thereafter)
Chair Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 7:11 p.m.

       2. Approval of Minutes
                 2.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes
                      of May 8, 2024
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn moved to have the minutes of May 8, 2024 be prepared and distributed to each
member of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Board. The Board will have ten days to review the
minutes and submit any changes to the secretary, if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will
stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes are made and the
board is in agreement. Trustee Teddy Hodges seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

       3. Discussion and Action Items
                  3.1. Discussion and consideration of a Resolution of the Herriman City Safety
                       Enforcement Area to adopt the biennial tentative budget for the fiscal years
                       beginning July 1, 2024, and ending June 30, 2026, and schedule a public hearing on
                       June 12, 2024 – Kyle Maurer, Finance Director
Finance Director Kyle Maurer informed the audience the Safety Enforcement Area served as the primary
funding source for the police department. He emphasized the future discussion on the police department's
budget during the subsequent work session. Transitioning to the high-level budget overview, Director
Maurer delved into the HCSEA budget, indicating an anticipated 3-percent increase in property tax. He
underscored the uncertainty surrounding final property tax figures until early June, aligning closely with
the final budget adoption timeline. He elucidated on transfers out, particularly the main transfer to the
General Fund for police services, foreseeing a 4-percent increase, approximately $320,000, attributed
partly to interest and other income earned by the fund. He Highlighted the restricted fund balance, and
noted it stood at approximately $978,000, reminding the Board of the previous year's decision to restrict
funds following an approved property tax increase, until specific triggers were met for their utilization.

Director Maurer looked ahead to fiscal year 2026, and projected a conservative 3-percent increase in
property tax revenue and a corresponding 4-percent increase in transfers out for police services. He
reiterated the expected growth in fund balance and the fund itself, pushed by interest earnings and the
restricted revenue.

Trustee Henderson moved to approve Resolution No R24-01 adopting the tentative budget for fiscal years
beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2026 and to schedule a public hearing on June 12, 2024. Trustee
Ohrn seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Trustee Jared Henderson                     Aye
Trustee Teddy Hodges                        Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                        Aye
Trustee Steve Shields                       Aye
Chair Lorin Palmer                          Aye
The motion passed unanimously.
