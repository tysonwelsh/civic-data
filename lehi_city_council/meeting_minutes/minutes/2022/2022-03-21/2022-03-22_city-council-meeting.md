29   2.1 Ranked Choice Voting Presentation
30   Mayor Johnson stated that Kelleen Potter wanted to present on some statistics and survey results
31   from the election. Mayor Johnson stated that they are not interested in debating or discussing
32   Ranked Choice Voting at this time, but that he would be open to hearing the survey results. She
33   stated that they conducted this survey because RCV is a pilot program and the State is interested
34   in knowing if residents liked it. Y2 analytics conducted the survey which uses the gold standard
35   for survey research methodology. They surveyed about 1500 people that used RCV and 500
36   respondent in a non-RCV election. The overall satisfaction was nearly the same for people voting
37   with RCV and those that voted traditionally. She said that some of the technical issues were
38   updated in a recent bill.
39
40   2.2 Power Rate Study Presentation
41   Joel Eves, Power Director, introduced David Berg to the Council. He was there to discuss the
42   rate study and future options. He said they don’t have any proposed rates at this point, so they are
43   hoping that based on the discussion tonight they can get some guidance relative to rates. He said
44   that they looked at operating results at existing rates. He noted that this is a high growth city and
45   they looked at operating costs that are increasing. Wholesale power costs have increased due to
46   market exposure and natural gas cost increases. They are guessing a 4% wholesale power
47   increase, so over time they have a declining bottom line. The City is strong with cash reserves, so
48   that’s positive. He showed and discussed the current rate design. He stated that the commercial

     Lehi City Council Meeting                        1                                     March 22, 2022
49   customers are paying more on a per dollar bases compared to cost of service. They are also
50   paying a demand charge rate. There was discussion regarding customer charges for residential
51   use. He said that there are cost to read a meter and create a bill, but there are fixed costs. He said
52   that they are well below Rocky Mountain Power with residential rates. He compared monthly
53   customer charges in the area. The most was $17.50. Lehi is currently charging $0.
54   Rates haven’t been changed in over 20 years, partly because wholesale power costs didn’t
55   change; however, that is no longer the case and those costs are increasing. They could implement
56   a wholesale power cost adjustment which would be a formula that becomes part of your rate that
57   automatically adjusts bills based on changes to wholesale power costs. If wholesale power goes
58   up then bills increase, but if wholesale power decreases then the bill would decrease. Most
59   utilities use this variable because whole power costs are so unpredictable. The Complexity of the
60   system has changed dramatically from what it was 20 years ago, because there’s more volatility
61   in the market.
62
63   Mr. Berg said that he will be recommending a residential customer charge with some overall
64   increases in rates with more on a percentage basis in the residential class then the other class due
65   to cost of service. They will also try to fix some of the rate design issues and will suggest the
66   wholesale power cost adjustment.

68   2.3 Discussion of Lehi City’s Green Waste Program
69   Todd Munger gave an update on the Green Waste Program. Dean Lundell, Finance Director,
70   stated that North Pointe is no longer processing the green waste, so the containers this year will
71   be processed the same as the regular containers. The issue is that the green waste customers are
72   charged less for their container. Mr. Lundell recommended that at the end of the year they tell
73   residents that they no longer have this program so residents can keep their green waste container
74   and use it as a second container and be charge $10.65 or someone can pick it up. Mayor Johnson
75   would like to find an alternate program, possibly something with TSSD.
