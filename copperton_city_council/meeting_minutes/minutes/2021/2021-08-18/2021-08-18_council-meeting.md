Copperton Code Rewrite Title 1

                Nathan Bracken stated as the various titles of the Copperton Metro Township

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

               Ed Walden, UFA, stated July 4th and 24th went well with not major firework reports.
The rain has reduced fire risks locally; however, fires from other states are bringing smoke into
the valley. UFA is working on a uniform patch that represents each community that UFA serves.

restricted and employees must wear masks while they are staying at the station.

                Mayor Clayton stated he researched different devices for the Council to use for
council business and decided on a MacBook Air or iPad Pro. Microsoft 356 can be purchased
under the family plan and installed on the devices. He reviewed the IRS regulations on how to
provide the device to Council Members as compensation. If the Council Member wants to take
their device at the end of their service, they would pay whatever the fair-market value is. There
will need to be an extra computer dedicated to running the audio, video, and presentations for the
meetings. A dedicated MacBook with Apple Care will be $1,507.99. The Metro Township received
$23,000 in COVID funding that can be used to purchase the devices. The State has an approved
contract that can be used for the purchase.

              Council Member Bailey, seconded by Council Member Stitzer, moved to approve
the purchase of one device in the amount of $1507.00. The motion passed unanimously.

Change to Copperton Code – R-1 ADU Size Update

                Mayor Clayton stated the Planning Commission will review the amendments and
forward it to the Council for review for September’s meeting. The language will be drafted to
include 25 percent of the backyard and no maximum square footage. There are utility easements
that are not in use, which means approval will be needed from the easement holders.

                 Nathan Bracken stated HB82 created changes to the improvement district and
the Copperton code concerning ADU’s. The improvement district will require people to update
their strip to free up enough water and add water efficient fixtures in the ADU. The Metro
Township code will require a letter before a building permit for an ADU is issued.

UPD Proposed Budget Presentation

               Lisa Dudley, Chief Financial Officer, UPD, provided a summary of property tax
valuations since 2022. She reviewed the certified tax rate, how the tax rate is calculated, Truth in
Taxation, SLVLESA sustainability plan, taxable value without new growth, percentage of centrally
assessed to total adjusted value, and proforma statements. The SLVLESA Board will be voting
tomorrow on whether to implement a tax increase.

              Chief Jason Ackerman, UPD, presented information on UPD compensation. He
reviewed current compensation challenges, market ratings, compensation comparison between
agencies and ranks, Copperton’s expenses, and member assessment.

               Mayor Clayton stated if the SLVLESA Board votes for an increase he would like
an additional presentation with information specific to Copperton and how the increases would
apply.

               Council Member Bailey stated she would like the report to be provided in advance
of their next meeting.
