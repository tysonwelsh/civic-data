2. Tour of Adobe Phase 2
   The Council and Commission participated in a tour of Adobe.

3. Training on Legal Issues
   Mayor Johnson discussed the concepts of takings and exactions as defined by constitutional
   law. He emphasized that takings, as per the Fifth Amendment, are legal when accompanied by
   just compensation. The Fourteenth Amendment also applies to takings, ensuring applicants
   have the right to be heard, which is part of their due process. Legislative bodies create specific
   laws for zoning and nuisance.

   In Lehi, density bonuses are used to negotiate just compensation, guided by a legitimate interest.
   He noted that a reasonable calculation must be used.

   Craig Chambers, Assistant City Attorney, emphasized the importance of considering general
   health, safety, and welfare in every project, and whether there is sufficient evidence to support
   a denial. Mitigation options were discussed, and he noted that a decision should be based on
   valid and reasonable evidence, including traffic studies, air quality assessments, and other
