                                    TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                    ORDINANCE NO. 23- 04


     AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A TEXT
    AMENDMENT TO TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE SECTIONS 13. 20. 030,
 RELATED TO MINIMUM LOT WIDTH AND LOT AREA FOR THE RESIDENTIAL
                           R-2- 8 AND R- 2- 10 ZONING DISTRICTS.


       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville      City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on
March 15, 2023, to consider, among other things, approving a text amendment to the Taylorsville
Municipal Code Sections 13. 20. 030, related to minimum lot width and lot area for the Residential
R-2- 8 and R-2- 10 zoning districts.; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 040, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the city' s official website
on February 17, 2023; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on February 17, 2023; and

        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on February 28, 2023, during which the Planning Commission voted 5-
1 to recommend that the Council approve the proposed text amendment; and


       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on March
15, 2023; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed text amendments to the Taylorsville Municipal Code Sections 13. 20. 030, related to
minimum lot width and lot area for the Residential R-2- 8 and R-2- 10 zoning districts, are hereby
approved, as articulated in Exhibit" A."


       This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 23- 04, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
