                                    TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 24- 07

      AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE ADDING THE NAME
        CONSTITUTION BOULEVARD" TO 2700 WEST FROM 4700 SOUTH TO
                                    LONGMEADOW DRIVE.


       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville           City Council ( the " Council") met during its regularly
scheduled   meeting   on   August 21,    2024, to consider, among other things, adding the name
 Constitution Boulevard" to 2700 West from 4700 South to Longmeadow Drive; and


       WHEREAS, TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 14. 60 allows the City to name, or
change the name of, a public place including, but not limited to, City streets; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE § 14.60.030, the City formed a
Naming Committee to recommend to the City Council a name of a public place or to change the
name thereof; and



       WHEREAS, the Naming Committee has recommended to the City Council that the name
 Constitution Boulevard" be added to 2700 West from 4700 South to Longmeadow Drive; and


       WHERAS, after careful consideration, the City Council has determined that it would be
in the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of the City to add the name
 Constitution Boulevard" to 2700 West through the City.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
recommended name" Constitution Boulevard" is hereby approved and shall be added to 2700 West
from 4700 South to Longmeadow Drive.

       This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 24- 07, shall take effect on September 17, 2024.



       PASSED AND APPROVED this 4th day of September 2024.
