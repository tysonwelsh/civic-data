1.         22-296                 General Citizen Comments

2.         22-290                 2022 Redevelopment Agency of Sandy City Annual Report

                   Attachments:   2022 Sandy Annual Report

                                  Mr. Jason Burningham, with Lewis Young, presented the 2022 Redevelopment Agency of 
                                  Sandy City Annual Report. He spoke about the Agency Tax Increment for FY 2022. Mr. 
                                  Burningham provided an overview of the RDA projects in Sandy: Civic Center South, Civic 
                                  Center North, Union Heights CDA, 9400 South CDA, 11400 South CDA, and Sandy TOD 
                                  CDA. Council questions followed. Council thanked Mr. Burningham for his presentation.

3.         22-292                 Council discussion on how best to involve the Alta Canyon Advisory 
                                  Committee in decisions surrounding Alta Canyon Sports Center
                                  Council Members Robinson and Earl are the Council liaisons to the Alta Canyon Advisory 
                                  Committee and requested feedback from the Council related to the ACAC. The Council 
                                  discussion included reaching out to the Advisory Committee members to get their input 
                                  and recommendations on the Alta Canyon design options and amenities, the city-wide 
                                  impact of the recreation center, the funding options for the project, and additional benefits 
                                  the committee would like to see offered at the center. Please send Council Members 
                                  Robinson and Earl any additional thoughts or recommendations.

4.         22-294                 Administration providing the council with an update on the 
                                  #SlowDownSandy campaign
                   Attachments:   Slow Down Sandy Memo

                                  Amy Bryant with the Police Department  provided an overview on the #SlowDownSandy 
                                  initiative and educational program that launched on the city website on August 1st. She 
                                  acknowledged the Communications Team and the City Traffic Engineer for their help. The 
                                  campaign runs from August through December. Each month, new information will be 
                                  pushed out. She provided an overview of the #SlowDownSandy webpage and showed 
                                  several one minute videos that will be featured each month on the website.. Council 
                                  questions and comments followed. Council thanked Ms. Bryant for her presentation and 
                                  acknowledged the staff for their great work on the webpage.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham recently met with some residents who also expressed 
                    concerns related to the intersection at 500 W 9400 S - as stated by a resident in the 
                    General Citizen Comment period this evening.

                    Council Member Houseman took her grandson to the Splash Pad and acknowledged the 
                    Parks Departments for creating such a great park. The Sandy Arts Production, Crazy for 
                    You runs through August 13th and she encouraged the community to see the production. 
                    The Night Out Against Crime was a great event. She also spoke about the Canyons 
                    School District Red Carpet event to welcome new teachers to the school district.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey thanked the police department staff and officers for their 
                    efforts coordinating the Night Out Against Crime - she was on Route 6. It was a great 
                    event.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson thanked the police department for organizing the Night 
                    Out Against Crime event. They did a fantastic job. Anybody who is interested in serving 
                    as a crossing guard - please apply. Council Member Robinson mentioned that he just 
                    completed the Flip your Strip and he encouraged the community to consider participating 
                    in the program and help conserve water. He thanked the Public Utilities staff for their help. 

                    Council Member Scott Earl thanked the Police Chief and all those who participated in the 
                    Night Out Against Crime event - it was a special evening.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud encouraged the community to see the Sandy Arts Guild 
                    production, Crazy For You. School starts on Monday so please watch out for kids 
                    walking to school and #SlowdownSandy. She thanked the police department for 
                    coordinating the Night Out Against Crime and for organizing the Police Citizens' 
                    Academy.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski invited the community to come out to the Sandy City Balloon Festival 
                    this weekend. The event information can be found on the city website. New this year is a 
                    drone light show taking place on Saturday evening at 9:45 pm.She thanked the Police 
                    Department and residents for participating in the Night Out Against Crime event. She 
                    spoke about the amazing participation at an event on Saturday to honor a little girl and 
                    her family. Chiefs Cline and Severson invited the Council to attend the annual softball 
                    game this Saturday at 1:00 pm at Quarry Bend Park.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, mentioned that there will be a discussion on capital facilities at next 
                    week's Council meeting. He also updated the Council on road construction projects 
                    taking place in the city. Please call him with any questions or concerns.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:09 pm.
