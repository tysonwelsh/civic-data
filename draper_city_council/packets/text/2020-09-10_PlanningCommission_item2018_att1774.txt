RE:    Notice of Approval for The Annex Plat Amendment Request


Dear Mr. Morgan,


On September 12, 2019, the Draper City Planning Commission approved your request for a Plat
Amendment on the properties at approximately 690 East 12200 South, application SUBD-733-
2019, with the following conditions:

       1.     That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
              Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
              and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
       2.     That the Zoning Administrator’s signature block be changed to a Planning
              Commission signature block prior to obtaining City signatures on the mylar.
       3.     That the dedication language be amended to comply with DCMC Section 17-4-
              030(N).
       4.     That the mylar be amended to remove all grading and existing site improvements
              prior to obtaining City signatures on the mylar.
       5.     That the mylar be amended to show public utility easements are required by
              DCMC Section 17-5-050 prior to obtaining City signatures to the mylar.
       6.     That the mylar shall include a note stating that there is a perpetual cross-access
              and shared parking easement between the different uses.
       7.     That if there are any minor changes required by the County Recorder, that those
              changes may be made without further approval by the Planning Commission.

Please be aware that the approval of a Plat Amendment by Draper City in no way exempts the
property from complying with other requirements that may be in effect on the property such as
CC&R’s, zoning, subdivision, site plan, land disturbance, and building permit regulations, as
applicable. It is the responsibility of the property owner to ensure compliance with these
regulations.
