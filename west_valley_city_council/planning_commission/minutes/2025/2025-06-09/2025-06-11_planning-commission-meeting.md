PRESENTATION:

SMI-4-2025
Quik Trip 7497 Subdivision
2155 South 3270 West
M Zone/2 Lots/5 Acres

BACKGROUND
Spencer Hymas, representing the property owner is requesting subdivision approval for the Quik Trip
7497 Subdivision. The subject property has been owned by Zions Bank and was used as a processing
center. The proposed subdivision is intended to create two new lots from the existing parcel.

ISSUES:
The Quik Trip 7497 Subdivision is being proposed to create 2 new lots, one of which will be for a new
Quik Trip fueling station and store. The second lot does not have a planned use at this time. The existing
parcel is situated between 3200 West and 3270 West.

Access will be gained from both 3200 West and 3270 West as noted above. According to the site plan
submitted as part of the permitted use application, access along 3200 West is proposed to be shared
between the two lots. A 35-foot shared access easement is indicated and runs parallel to 3200 West.
While these lots may have been able to have their own drive access, the plan for the present time is to
have the shared access. If for whatever reason that proposal goes away, staff would recommend that the
Engineering Division evaluate spacing requirements along the right-of-way.

Public improvements are existing along both 3200 and 3270 West. Landscaping along these rights-of-
way exists and has been kept in good shape. Any proposed alterations or enhancements to the landscaping
will be addressed by staff. Any damage to existing public improvements will be addressed with the
permitted use application and will be coordinated with the City Engineering Division.

As part of the subdivision, the developer will be dedicating a portion of property along 3270 West as part
of the right-of-way. According to the plat, the width of property to be dedicated is 8 feet, which matches
the dedication the City acquired with the development to the north. The developer will need to coordinate
this dedication along with new drive approaches and modifications to existing drive approaches with the
Engineering Division.

STAFF ALTERNATIVES:
1. Approve the Quik Trip 7497 Subdivision subject to a resolution of staff and agency comments.

2. Continue the application to address concerns raised during the Planning Commission hearing.

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented. The applicant, Spencer Hymas, was available to the Commission.
There were no questions or concerns.

Vote: Commissioner Porter motioned to approve SMI-4-2025. Commissioner Durfee seconded. A voice
vote was taken and all were in favor of the motion.

                                UNANIMOUS—SMI-4-2025—APPROVED


PLANNING COMMISSION BUSINESS:
The Commission considered the Minutes of the Study Meeting held May 21, 2025, and the Public
Hearing held May 28, 2025. There were no changes. Commissioner Durfee motioned to approve the
Minutes. Chair Winters seconded the motion. A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of
the motion.

Commission, the meeting was adjourned at 4:09 PM.
