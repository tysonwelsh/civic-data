CONSIDERATION __OF _ RESOLUTION NO. __R-2026-3669, _A _RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING THE EXPENDITURES OF WASHINGTON COUNTY’S SHARE OF
THE RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw stated that this item refers to 15% of the 1/10th of 1%
sales and use tax collected, with the intent of funding cultural organizations within Washington
County. Several cultural organizations submitted applications, and the Recreation, Arts and Parks
Board (RAP Board) met to review funding recommendations, which were then sent to the
Commissioners for review. Slight changes were made based on goals and objectives on how RAP
tax funds should be used, including: yearly grant reports were turned in, whether funds were used
appropriately, what is the value given to the community, and how the funds will be used.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3669, A
Resolution Appropriating the Expenditures of Washington County’s Share of
the Recreation, Arts, and Parks Sales Tax. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow,
Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3670, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGRICULTURE LEASE AGREEMENT TO RESTORE AGRICULTURE USE AT
THE CONFLUENCE PARK

Habitat Conservation Plan Director Cameron Rognan explained the HCP has been working with
Casey Lofthouse, a farmer in the community, for several years to restore agriculture in Confluence
Park. This a renewal of a lease agreement from five years ago, and it is recommended to renew the
lease for another three years.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3670,
A Resolution Approving an Agriculture Lease Agreement to Restore
Agriculture Use at the Confluence Park. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist
and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3672, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A MARKETING SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT WITH BLACK DESERT EVENTS

Greater Zion Sports and Adventure Sales Manager Chad McWilliams stated the Tourism Tax
Advisory Board (TAB Board) recently voted to approve funding from the Transient Room Tax
(TRT). He further clarified that per state law, a certain portion of TRT funding must be used for
marketing and promotion of Washington County, with Washington County helping build
infrastructure, not buildings or golf courses.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3672,
A Resolution Approving a Marketing Sponsorship Agreement with Black
Desert Events. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3673, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A SPONSORSHIP AND ADVERTISING AGREEMENT WITH SPARTAN RACE, INC.

Mr. McWilliams explained that these events have been in the works for many years, with events
starting in March of 2027. It is anticipated that the first event will bring in 8,000 — 10,000 visitors,
with 90% being non-County residents, to Sand Hollow Reservoir with little to no impact on local
traffic. This agreement is for three years.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3673, A
Resolution Approving a Sponsorship Agreement with Spartan Race, Inc.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2026-3674, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE NORTHERN CORRIDOR HIGHWAY PROPERTY EXCHANGE AGREEMENT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated the BLM has not yet decided on the Northern
Corridor; however, in the event of a favorable result, this is an exchange agreement that would
facilitate the Northern Corridor right-of-way. Washington County will convey its 450-acre
property to the Utah Division of Wildlife Resources (UDWR), and UDWR will convey its 46-acre
property to the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT).
