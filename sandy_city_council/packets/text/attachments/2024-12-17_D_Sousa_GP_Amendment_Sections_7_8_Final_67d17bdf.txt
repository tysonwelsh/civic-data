Subject:           Proposed Amendment to Section 7 & 8 of the Draft General Plan



This past Tuesday, the final sections of the Draft General Plan were presented to the Council. Following
this presentation, I took the opportunity to re-review Section 8, which outlines the Implementation Plan
and provides a clear and actionable path forward for executing the General Plan's objectives.

Reflecting on the process of developing this document over the past two years, it has become evident
that creating and updating the General Plan requires a significant investment of time, effort, and
resources from city staff, consultants, residents, and elected officials alike. Given the depth of this effort,
it is critical to ensure the plan remains a relevant and effective tool for guiding the city’s growth and
development.

To achieve this, I recommend that the city commit to a more frequent review of the General Plan.
Specifically, incorporating a structured review process at least every 10 years into Section 8 of the
Implementation Plan would provide several key benefits:

      •   Adaptability: Regular reviews allow the plan to respond to evolving community needs,
          economic trends, environmental challenges and new legislation.
      •   Transparency and Accountability: Revisiting the plan periodically ensures that progress is
          monitored, goals are reassessed, and adjustments are made as needed.
      •   Efficient Resource Use: A systematic review process can prevent the need for extensive
          overhauls by addressing changes incrementally over time.

Historically, the city’s current General Plan has not been reviewed in the decades since its initial
adoption. This prolonged period underscores the importance of establishing a consistent and
predictable review schedule moving forward. A 10-year review cycle strikes a balance between
maintaining the plan’s long-term vision and ensuring it stays current with contemporary circumstances.

To help achieve this, I propose amendments to Sections 7 and 8 of the Draft General Plan as identified
in Exhibit “A”, below. This amendment adds two additional objectives to Section 7.1. Each of those
objectives will also be reflected in Section 8, the Implementation Plan.
