                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-17

 A RESOLUTION TO APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH CAVORTEX TECHNOLOGY
INTERNATIONAL TO COMPLETE A CULINARY WATER FEASIBILITY STUDY ON
         TREATING AND USING WATER FROM EXISTING WELLS

       WHEREAS, the Mayor has determined that this is needed; and

      WHEREAS, because funding for this has not been included in the 19/20 Fiscal Year
Budget. By approving this contract council will need to approve $40,000.00 in a future 19/20
FY Budget Amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

        1. The Riverton City Council approves entering into a contract with Cavortex
Technology International to complete a Culinary Water Feasibility Study on treating and using
water from existing wells.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 3rd day of
March 2020 by the following vote:
