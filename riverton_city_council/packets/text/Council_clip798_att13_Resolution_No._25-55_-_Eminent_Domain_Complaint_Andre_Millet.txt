                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       RESOLUTION NO. 25-55

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF A COMPLAINT TO TAKE PROPERTY
                      THROUGH EMINENT DOMAIN

        (Affecting Parcel No. 27-22-379-004 – 1776 West 11800 South, Riverton, Utah 84065)


        WHEREAS, Riverton City has identified a number of safety concerns related to pedestrian travel
along and in the area of 1776 West 11800 South, Riverton, Utah (the “Subject Property”); and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has determined that in the interest of public safety it is both
reasonable and necessary to design, construct, maintain, use, repair, replace and improve a sidewalk
system where the Subject Property fronts 11800 South street and terminates on either end of the Subject
Property; and

        WHEREAS, the public need for the completion of a sidewalk system necessitates that Riverton
must acquire in fee simple a portion of the Subject Property, from owners of the same, fronting 11800
South, specifically Andre Millet; and,

         WHEREAS, Riverton City entered into negotiations with Defendant Andre Millet to establish
the fair market value of the Property and acquire, purchase or otherwise obtain fee simple ownership of
the Property; and,

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has the statutory authority to exercise the power of eminent domain
pursuant to Utah Code Section 78-34-1, et seq.; and,

        WHEREAS, the acquisition of the above described Property is for an authorized public use, is
necessary to accomplish the purposes for which it is being acquired and will further the health, safety and
welfare of many residents of Riverton City; and,

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Section 72-5-103 Riverton City is entitled to condemn the
Property in fee simple and to have a court of competent jurisdiction determine and adjudicate the amount
to be paid as just compensation on the same.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as follows:

        1.      The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the public interest and necessity
                require the acquisition and the immediate occupancy of part of the parcel of real estate
                located at the address of 1776 West 11800 South, Riverton, Utah, and more particularly
                described as parcel number 27-22-379-004, for construction of a public sidewalk and
                related infrastructure, and for other lawful and legitimate public purposes.

        2.      The property to be affected by the action taken in Section 1, above, is owned by Andre
                Millet.
       3.     The proposed location of the public improvements is planned and located in a manner
              which will be the most compatible with the greatest public good and the least private
              injury.

       4.     The Riverton City Council, concluding that the condemnation of the subject property is
              for the public health, safety and welfare, authorizes the exercise of the power of eminent
              domain in order to acquire the Subject Property in fee and to initiate such legal
              proceedings as may be necessary so to do.

       5.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of
November, 2025 by the following vote:
