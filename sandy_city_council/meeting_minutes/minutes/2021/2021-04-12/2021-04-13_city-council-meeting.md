Council Member Business

                             Council Member Houseman reported on the reopening on the Sandy Senior. She also
                             noted a constituent call regarding rapid response of the Police Department. She thanked
                             Dan Medina and the Park team for some upcoming outdoor events.

                             Council Member Christensen noted that the Historic Committee and CDBG committees
                             will be meeting next week.

                             Council Member Sharkey also spoke to the reopening of the Senior Center. She
                             expressed concerns for Sandy businesses in light of the expiration of the mask mandate.

                             Council Member Zoltanski welcomed back the Food Truck Night at the Sand
                             Amphitheater on Monday nights.



Mayor's Report

CAO Report

                             CAO Matt Huish noted that the Mayor continued the City facility mask policy for
                             employees and guests. He noted that 5,000 of 8,300 street lights have been converted to
                             LEDs. He noted that the City will be promoting water conservation this year. 10 of 20 bulk
                             waste areas have been completed so far for the spring cleanup. There have been 225
                             violations so far. Public Works, Waste Management (WM), and Communications have
                             been working together on a recycling outreach plan. Mike Gladbach added details about
                             the outreach schedule, an FAQ sheet, newsletter updates, a letter from WM to residents,
                             and social media links. Todd Asay reported on a safe, scaled-down observance of the
                             Fourth of July.
