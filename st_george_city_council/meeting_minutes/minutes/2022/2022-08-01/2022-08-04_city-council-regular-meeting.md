         BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This request is to award the bid to
         Western Utility Telecom Inc for the purchase of steel transmission line poles. The
         line will be constructed by SGESD crews from Aliente Drive the new Canyon View
         Substation Site. Staff recommends approval of awarding the bid to Western
         Utility Telecom Inc.

     b. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products for the Virgin
        River South Trail from Bloomington Park to Brigham Road in the amount
        of $545,943.25.

         BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This is a multi-use trail that will run
         along the south side of the Virgin River between Bloomington Park and Brigham
         Rd. This trail is part of the master-planned trail system and will eventually
         connect to the Web Hill trail. This project was broken into phases because of
         property acquisition issues. The original bid for both phases by Interstate Rock
         was $2,200,260.15. Staff recommends approval to award the bid to Interstate
         Rock Products.

     c. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Rosenberg
        Associates for design and engineering of the proposed George
        Washington Boulevard from Boulder Creek Crossing commercial center
        near River Road to the east side of the proposed Virgin River bridge
        structure east of 270 East (Crosby Way) Project.

         BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This agreement is for civil engineering
         and land surveying services including roadway, drainage, and utility construction
         plans, geotechnical investigation, contract documents, bidding assistance, and a
         FEMA LOMR for the proposed George Washington Boulevard from Boulder Creek
         Crossing commercial center near River Road to the east side of the proposed
         Virgin River bridge structure east of 270 East (Crosby Way) Project. Staff
         recommends approval.

     d. Consider approval of the Warner Draw Watershed Supplemental
        Watershed Work Plan Agreement #9 between Washington County, City
        of Hurricane, Washington City, City of St. George, Washington County
        Water Conservancy District, The Nature Conservancy, and the Natural
        Resources Conservation Service (NRCS).

         BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This agreement covers five sites within
         the Warner Draw Watershed, two of which are in St. George. The two sites in St.
         George covered by this agreement include Seegmiller Marsh and Warner Valley
         Disposal System. Detailed information concerning this project can be found on
         the NRCS website at https://bit.ly/3DTnKNk. Staff recommends approval of the
         agreement.

     e. Consider approval of a renewal of a Transportation Network Company
        Operating Agreement between the City of St. George and Raiser, LLC.
        (Uber).

INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT/RESOLUTION:
     Consider approval of a resolution entering into an Interlocal Agreement
     with the County for the 2022 Byrne Justice Assistance Grant (JAG) Program
     Award.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The St. George Police Department is
      applying to receive the 2022 Department of Justice, Edward Byrne Memorial Justice
      Assistance Grant, (JAG) award. The $28,217 award will be used to supplement
      funding to purchase a 3-dimensional scanning machine for use by the Accident
      Reconstruction Team and Investigations Division to more efficiently process and
      diagram extensive collision and crime scenes. Funding will also be used to acquire
      individual first aid kits for officers. The interlocal agreement is part of our application
      process and has been signed by Washington County and St. George City each year
      we have applied for the grant. Staff recommends approval of the resolution.

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the zone from Central Business
     District (C-4) to Planned Development Mixed Use (PD-MU) on approximately
     1.3 acres to allow for a new mixed-use building for a project to be known as
     300 West Mixed Use. Case No. 2022-ZC-027

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The southwest corner of St. George
      Boulevard and 300 West currently has a used car lot, the Sun City Glass building,
      and an older mechanic shop that is being used as a part-time hobby shop. Behind
      the mechanic shop there is an unfinished dirt area that houses a few vehicles. The
      applicant is proposing to remove the car lot and mechanic shop buildings that wrap
      around the Sun City Glass building for the purpose of adding in a new mixed-use
      building that will house commercial on the main floor. At their meeting held on June
      28, 2022, the Planning Commission held a public hearing and recommended
      approval.
