                                  ORDINANCE NO.2024-29


  AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH,ADOPTING
                         TITLE 1.60 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE


       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the City Council of the City of Biuffdale
authority to enact ordinances considered necessary or appropriate for the health,safety,
and welfare of the City; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code§ 52-5-207(2)(a) of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act
states,"A public body may not hold an electronic meeting unless the public body has
adopted a resolution, rule, or ordinance governing the use of electronic meetings."

       WHEREAS,the City Council has determined it is in the best interests of the City, its
residents and citizens,to adopt an ordinance authorizing the City's public bodies to hold
electronic meetings and detailing procedures related thereto.

NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH:


       SECTION 1. Enactment. The City Council hereby enacts and adopts Title 1.60 of the
Biuffdale City Code as shown on Exhibit A and incorporated by this reference.

       SECTION 2. Amendment of Conflicting Ordinances. If any ordinances, resolutions,
policies, or zoning maps of the City heretofore adopted are inconsistent herewith they are
hereby amended to comply with the provisions hereof. If they cannot be amended to comply
with the provisions hereof,they are hereby repealed.

        SECTION 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable,such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and
independent provision, and such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance shall
be severable.


      SECTION 4. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

1.160            ELECTRONIC MEETINGS


1.160.010        Electronic Meetings Authorized

   1. Any public body of the City of Biuffdaie may, by following the procedures and
      requirements of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act and this chapter,convene and
      conduct an electronic meeting.
   2. Electronic meetings may be conducted by means of telephone, video,or other
      computerized,electronic, or other teleconferencing means,as designated by the
        chair.
   3. A member of the public body participating remotely through telephonic or electronic
      means is included in the calculation of a quorum.
   4. No proxy votes shall be allowed.


1.160.020        Procedures for Electronic Meetings

   1. The procedures to be followed at any electronic meeting shall be the same as those
      followed by the public body in a non-electronic meeting of the public body.
   2. The public body shall follow the requirements ofthe Utah Open and Public Meetings
      Act related to notice, agendas, minutes,anchor location, etc.
   3. Comments made by members of the public body participating remotely will be
        broadcast at the anchor location.
   4. To ensure full participation of each member present, and those participating
      electronically, members will be given a specific opportunity to make inquiries and
      participate in the discussion through a roll call method.
   5. Votes taken in electronic meetings shall be by roll call method,with each member
      audibly verbalizing their vote.
