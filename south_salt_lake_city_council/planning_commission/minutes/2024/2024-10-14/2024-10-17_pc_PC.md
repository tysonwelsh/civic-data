1.       PUBLIC HEARING
         An Application for Preliminary Subdivision Plat Amendment to Consolidate Three
         Parcels into One Lot, Located at 3200 South 700 East.

         ACTION ITEM
         Applicant: St John’s Community Child Development Center SSL

City Planner, Jed Shum, presented the Staff Report and explained that the item is the Spring View Park
Subdivision Amendment. The subject property is located at 3200 South 700 East. It is an application
for a Preliminary Subdivision Plat Amendment to consolidate three parcels into one lot. Mr. Shum
shared an image of the three parcels and explained that the current zoning is Commercial Corridor
(“CC”) and the purpose of combining the lots is for the existing child care center. The use is conditional
on the site and is already existing. The proposed plat was reviewed, which showed the combined three
parcels. Existing conditions images were shared. Mr. Shum noted that no changes to the existing
structure on the site are proposed. He presented the Landscape and Parking Plan for reference.

Mr. Shum reported that the application meets two General Plan considerations, which are as follows:

     •   Land Use and Neighborhood Strategy #8:
            o Appropriately seek the redevelopment of legal non-confirming uses, properties, and
               structures to be more conforming over the long term.
            o Analysis: This plat application allows St. John’s Community Child Development Center
               SSL to fix up their land and bring their current non-conforming multiple parcel spanning
               use closer into conformity with the South Salt Lake City Municipal Code.
     •   Economic Development Goal #3:
            o Continue to support existing and future businesses through the City’s partnership with
               the South Salt Lake Chamber of Commerce and comprehensive business retention and
               recruitment program.
            o Analysis: This plat application allows St. John’s Community Child Development Center
               SSL to improve their business’s current conditions.

Based on the information submitted in the application, supporting documentation, and analysis of the
relevant code sections and General Plan goals, Staff recommends that the Planning Commission move
to approve the Subdivision Plat Amendment to consolidate the three parcels into one lot located at 3200
South 700 East, based on the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval for
the subdivision as written in the Staff Report. The Commission can approve, deny, or continue.

purpose of the plat is so it is possible to issue a Building Permit to expand the existing structure. Mr.

3.       PUBLIC HEARING
         An Application for Preliminary Subdivision Plat for a One-Lot Plat, located at 170 West
         Stratford Avenue.

         ACTION ITEM
         Applicant: Brett Christiansen

Mr. Shum presented the Staff Report and explained that this is the Whitney Subdivision Plat. This is a
petition for approval of a Preliminary Subdivision Plat to plat one parcel at 170 West Stratford Avenue.
The current zoning is TOD and the proposed use is a new office building, which is a permitted use in
the TOD Zone. He shared the proposed Preliminary Plat and pointed out that on the left, there is an
easement that is directly south of the Public Works building. Since the project is on a dead end next to
the TRAX line, there will be a cul-de-sac installed that meets engineering and fire standards.

Images of the existing conditions were reviewed. Mr. Shum shared the Site Plan for the proposed office
building as well as the proposed elevation. The General Plan considerations were shared:

     •   Economic Development Goal #3:
            o Continue to support existing and future businesses through the City’s partnership with
               the South Salt Lake Chamber of Commerce and comprehensive business retention and
               recruitment program.
            o Analysis: This plat application allows for new office development at 170 West Stratford
               Avenue, providing more opportunities.

Based on the information submitted in the application, supporting documentation, and analysis of the
relevant code sections and General Plan goals, Staff recommends the Planning Commission move to
approve the Preliminary Subdivision Plat to plat the one parcel, located at 170 West Stratford Avenue,
based on the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval for the subdivision in
the Staff Report. Mr. Shum noted that the Commission can approve, deny, or continue the application.

Community and Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, reported that the intention
is for there to be an office building. The administrative efforts will be consolidated into the building.

Chair Carter opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.

Chair Carter noted that it is nice to see dirt lots be filled with something that will generate a little more
revenue. Commissioner Southey pointed out that it is also nice to see new office space coming in when
many have told the Commission that office space is not needed as much as it was. Commissioner Ewell
stated that this will utilize an underutilized parcel and expressed support for what is proposed.

Commissioner Dahlberg understands that this is one parcel and it is surrounded by parcels that the
applicant has no control over, but she has questions about connectivity and walkability. Mr.
Weidenhamer confirmed that this abuts the rail line and is also on a dead end. With the Public Works
project, the intention is to build a plaza or park under the water tower. As part of that process, there

The City is also studying where the best places are for bicycle lanes. Ultimately, there is a desire to

Ms. Ungricht reported that the applicant will put in a full cul-de-sac in addition to the sidewalk and
