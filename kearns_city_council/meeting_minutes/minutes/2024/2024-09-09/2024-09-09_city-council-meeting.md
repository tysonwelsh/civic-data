G. Unified Police Department (UPD) — Chief Hughes
Chief Hughes shared that within 24 hours they had a suspect apprehended who had been
identified the night of the incident. Officers walking the surrounding area were able to locate
both weapons used in the crime, along with the video that led law enforcement to identify the
suspect. In addition, Sheriff Services had special detectives on call to come out, with a special
set of skills. The Gang Unit was involved, however he pointed out that all the evidence
collected that led to the identification and apprehension was from the UPD. It was not a drive-
by, this was a confrontation between two individuals, not a gang related hit. He addressed the
use of mini-bikes in the community, and noted they are working on that. Most of the parents
contacted have been unaware those bikes are illegal to ride in the streets, and UPD is doing
their best to use education at this time to reach those parents. They did put out a bonus for
hiring of lateral officers, which allows for much faster officer implementation over new hires that
still have to go through the academy and additional training. This resulted in lots of lateral
officer applications, which they are thrilled about.
A member of the audience asked why they don’t pursue the kids on bikes, and Chief Hughes
responded that it’s a safety issue. They don’t want any kids or members of the public getting
hurt as a result of a chase.
Council Member Schaeffer asked about side by sides, noting that in his recent training he was
told they were not allowed on the pavement. However, he sees them all over the city like a car.
