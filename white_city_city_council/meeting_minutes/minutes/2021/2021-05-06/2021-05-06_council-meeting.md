Electronic Council Meeting Determination

                Mayor Flint stated I would like to call this meeting of the White City Metro
Township Council to order for Thursday, May 6, 2021, at 999 Galena Drive. I know I have a habit
of doing that all of the time. We do have a quorum. At this point we will have to read this electronic
Council meeting determination.

              Consistent with Section 52-4-207(4) of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act,
and acting as Mayor of the White City Metro Township, I, hereby make the following written
determinations in support of my decision to hold and convene electronic meetings of the White
City Metro Township without a physical anchor location:

   1. Conducting Board meetings with an anchor location that is physically accessible for
      members of the public to attend in person presents a substantial risk to the health and
      safety of individuals present at the anchor location.

   2. This determination is based upon the fact that the White City Metro Township remains
      under a state of public health emergency related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic,
      and significant, continued person to person transmission of the SARS-CoV-2 virus
      continues to occur in our county, with institutions and persons being required to take
      precautions, including limiting ‘in-person’ interactions and providing physical distancing
      guidelines. The health and safety of Council Members, officials, staff, contract service
      providers and members of the public who are in the high-risk category for COVID-19 due

      the meetings and, as appropriate, make comments electronically.

Reports from other Municipal Providers

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)


                                                  3
DATE            THURSDAY                        MAY                                       6, 2021

                Chief Ken Aldridge stated recruit camp is in its 13th week and will graduate at the
end of May. UFA Captains are going through the Battalion Chief testing process. The 2021-2022
preliminary budget was presented by the UFA Finance Committee to the board in April and the
divisions will present its individual budgets to the board for tentative approval. Three wildland
bosses were promoted, and the crew continues to work toward obtaining hotshot status. Hotshot
crews are a highly trained, qualified crew of 20 that plays a critical role in wildland fire
management.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Officer Jeff Fenton stated there have been an increase of stolen landscaping and
construction trailers in White City and surrounding areas, domestics, and petty yard thefts. He is
working on getting the speed trailer running again. UPD is excited to help with the 5K on June 5.

                Chief Randy Thomas stated police departments and dispatch centers have
worked together to develop Project Safeguard, a program where residents can provide
information about someone in the household that has a special need like autism or dementia with
situations that may negatively affect them. When the resident needs help, the first responders will
receive the information about the individual prior to contact and know what to do to effectively
help and prevent a crisis.

White City Metro Township General Plan

             Paul Ashton stated the Resolution in your packet is to receive the Steering
Committee report regarding the White City Metro Township General Plan and referral to the
Planning Commission. He suggested one amendment to the language in the resolve section
based on Council Member Little’s suggestion in the business meeting.

               Council Member Little stated he would like the Council to consider several
amendments to the resolution that will clarify what role the Steering Committee played in drafting
the White City Metro Township General Plan.

               Mayor Flint stated with that many changes, the resolution would need to be
rewritten.

Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Mayor Flint stated Sheriff Rosie Rivera gave an update on the deputies that were
shot at the Sheriff’s Office building. The deputies will have a long recovery before they can go
back to work. The Granite School District crossing guard contract is being negotiated. Kearns and
Magna Metro Townships have requested additional officers due to Taylorsville City leaving, which
eliminates the sharing of officers. The new chief in Magna is Dale Craig. Every month we accept
donations from various organizations, communities, cities, and businesses. A gift in the amount
of $3,000 from the Salt Lake Honorary Colonels to the cadet program was received. There were
discussions regarding the fund balance and raising the cap for taxing.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                 Council Member Price stated mosquitos are out early this year. All the seasonal
employees have been hired and started the beginning of May. The Board continues to go through
all the district manuals and some legal questions were brought up regarding the employee
manual. Service requests have started coming in, if residents have an issue, they can call the
district. There have been no accidents.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

              Council Member Price stated the postcards for the yearly cleanup were sent to
residents. Dumpsters will be available May 26 – 28 on a first come first serve basis. WFWRD will
put the dumpster in the driveway, not on the street. There are not enough dumpsters for every

Salt Lake County Animal Services

                 Council Member Little stated Animal Services is working on a new Salt Lake
County Animal Services ordinance and would like feedback from the White City Council. The
Council can adopt the ordinance with its own amendments. Animal Services is willing to come to
different events; however, they have expressed concerns about lack of advertising. When they
go to different events throughout the county, sometimes entities have not advertised their services
well and there is a low turnout. They would like us to make residents aware of when they will be
in White City so it can utilize its time effectively. They would also like the Council to encourage
residents to join its Facebook page.
