   2. Public Hearing – Accept Public Comment on changes to Title 10 & 11 to:
         1. Create a new Industrial Zone in Title 10 the: “Industrial 4 (ID4) Zone with
             uses primarily pertaining to large Data Centers, and their support
             structures.
         2. Modify Title 11 to only require prescribed landscaping buffering of
             industrial use from other adjoining uses in certain circumstances.
         3. Modify Title 10 & 11 to allow for xeriscape landscaping, and a certain
             percentage of native landscaping if well maintained.
         4. Modify Title 11 to require certain “dark sky” lighting requirements in the
             ID4 zone.
         5. Modify other areas to Title 10 & 11 as they concern data center uses,
             including the definition section of Title 10.

      a. Staff Presentation
                    Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented to the commission on Title 10 & 11 zoning
            changes that are coming forward due to the interest in the Nephi City area for
            Data Centers.
                    Items for the October meeting that need to be addressed are the definition
            of Accessory Buildings that may be support structures (i.e. guard stations);
            Fencing ordinance; Sound Ordinances; Lot size Ordinance; Frontage, Setbacks,
            and Height Ordinances; Waste Ordinance; Lighting Ordinance; General Lighting
            Ordinance; Landscape Ordinance; Dark Sky Ordinance; Public Works Definition
            section of the ordinance; Signage Ordinance; and miscellaneous items.

Nephi City Planning Commission                                                 October 8, 2025

                     Diving into the presentation, Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that after
             discussing with the DRC the following has been recommended for consideration
             in Title 10 & 11:
                         1. Accessory Buildings will include locations for a guard tower
                             station, backup generator housing, and buildings that are not
                             considered part of the main data center site.
                         2. Security Fence will be adjusted to provide additional security
                             measures, including height adjustments, razor wire, and similar
                             measures.
                         3. Sound Pollution will follow a set of ordinances that include
                             setback adjustments, sound-level checks, and consideration of
                             backup generator use.
                         4. Lot size and area will require at least 20 acres; they can be on one
                             side of the parcel with many structures.
                         5. Frontage, Setback, and height ordinances to be adjusted for this
                             commercial zone will be as follows:
                                 a. Frontage 140’ minimum
                                 b. Setbacks of 50.’
                                 c. Setbacks for accessory structures to be determined.
                                 d. The height ordinance will set the maximum building height
                                     at 100’.
                                 e. Accessory building height maximum of 35’.
                         6. The Waste Ordinance will require that waste be disposed of
                             outside Nephi City.
                         7. General Lighting must be followed by Title 11, with the exception
                             of the Dark Sky Ordinance.
                         8. The Landscaping Ordinance for ID4 will be specific to this zoning
                             area. Frontage will require landscaping, visible from the street, will
                             also apply here; however adjustment will be made to exclude rear
                             landscaping.
                                 a. Frontage and Side Landscaping will require 25% to be
                                     completed, and can be used with xeriscape or nativescape.
                                     This will additionally require an ordinance to remain
                                     maintained, and watered for plants to remain living.
                         9. Dark Sky ordinance will need continued research on lighting
                             requirements, consideration with the FAA for beacons of lights. It
                             has been proposed that it will not follow the subdivision standard
                             ordinance, but will follow commercial. The Dark Sky ordinance
                             will be brought forward in a future planning commission meeting.
                         10. Public Works will use Title 37 of the state code for all public
                             works definitions for the proposed changes to the ordinance.
                         11. The signage ordinance will also be addressed and limited to all ID4
                             in Section 10.18.4.
                         12. Miscellaneous items and adjustments to Titles 10 & 11 also
                             included the need for a redundant Natural Gas Subline, ordinances
                             that may be needed, and Back-up Generator requirements.

      b. Public Hearing
            TIME: 7:02 p.m.

                            Mr. Donald W. Ball commented to the building height and the
                    proposed fence ordinance change. He wondered if it would matter if the
                    height of the building was 100’, and what fence size changes would need
                    to be made. Mr. Ball then also commented on the Dark Sky Ordinance and
                    the use of emergency lights, and that it would seem pertinent that, in an
                    emergency, the Data Centers have them. Mrs. Shauna Talbot clarified that
                    security was important and that as for the emergency lighting, that was
                    correct and a valid concern that would be addressed in the ordinance.

      c. Commission Discussion
                          Chairman Cory Thomson inquired about the site and road
                  improvements. Mrs. Talbot responded that some items still need attention.
                  One concern is that if the Data Centers are allowed to do interior roads
                  that aren’t up to city standards, and they leave the area, how will that work
                  for the city in the future? It will need to be addressed.

                            Commissioner Fran Petersen asked what the answer was to the
                    Public Works items that were not listed and how they would be serviced.
                    Mrs. Talbot reiterated that there isn’t an answer on that yet, but it does
                    need to be addressed, hence the need for the ordinance changes and why
                    Title 10 & 11 are being adjusted.

                             Commissioner Jim Nelson asked for clarification on the Public
                    Works ordinance portion. The city will supply their normal use needs, but
                    what about the entire system when it is completed? Will the Public Works
                    be able to support that need? Mrs. Talbot responded that it depends on
                    their needs, in addition to what agreement is placed between Nephi City
                    and whoever comes in. City Administrator, Seth Atkinson also added that
                    at the time Public Works has been heavily involved and their current needs
                    are being addressed and looked into for future use.

                            Commissioner Meadow Perides raised a concern and question
                    about the private water line and wells in that area, and what that will mean
                    for those users in the future. Would it be possible to give those users? Mrs.
                    Shauna Talbot responded that there is only one well in Nephi City that is
                    in the canyon, as far as the utilities go that will be up to what happens
                    when services adjust for their needs.

                            Commissioner Meadow Perides raised concerns about the water
                    use. It was discussed that the Data Centers would not have a great need for
                    water, and that it is coming back up and that they have a greater need. She
                    emphasized that the city put in place a limit to the amount of commercial
                    use due to the water concerns.

                            Commissioner Fran Petersen stated that recently an article had
                    come out that Data Centers and the substations that are needed, transfer
                    cost to all users. Is that correct? Mrs. Shauna Talbot stated that the entire
                    DRC and staff are already looking to the future. Upgrades to the
                    substations have not been done quickly, and Rust Finlinson planned ahead
                    for future need when cost was low. Costs up to this point have not been
                    transferred. Additional utilities that will be needed to operate the data
                    centers will be part of agreements, and upgrade costs directed at those
                    businesses.

                            Commissioner Fran Petersen commented that by limiting and
                    doing phases is that there is some control of how many can come in at a
                    time, and that all utilities and roads are accounted for. Mrs. Talbot stated
                    that was correct, currently an example of this was the Messersmith
                    property.

                           Commissioner Heather Robertson, voiced concerna about the
                    acreage being developed at a time, 40 acres is a lot for one project. Mrs.
                    Shauna Talbot stated that the utilities are a huge benefit to the community,
                    and the property tax value is significant. Where these are located,
                    according to the zoning, this area was already earmarked for industrial.

                            Commissioner Fran Petersen would prefer at this time to not see
                    the ID4 zone put in the General Plan. She felt that this will give the city to
                    have the opportunity to say yes or no to projects, and will not be held to a
                    General Plan outline. Mrs. Shauna Talbot agreed and stated that by putting
                    ares in a holding zone, that gives opportunity to have a say in what
                    happens. Currently, the state of Utah is being pressured to have cities
                    uphold the General Plans that they have set down, and that is causing
                    issues in communities. She agrees that the ID4 would be better suited for a
                    holding zone on a concept map for the time being.

                           Chairman Cory Thomson reiterated that this goes against
                    everything that the Planning Commission has set forward for the General
                    Plan and keeping Nephi City feeling like a rural community, and that his

                    coming, and do we want the county to have control of it or the city?

                            Commissioner Meadow Perides raised concern to the commission
                    that there didn’t seem to be enough information about this project, and
                    planning ahead for this didn’t seem advisable. Mrs. Shauna Talbot and
                    Mrs. Shari Cowan agreed that the information has been minimal due to
                    agreements. Mrs. Shauna Talbot did state that the company that has come

                    companies such as google, amazon, and the like.

   3. Discussion Regarding “Modular Data Centers.”
         a. Staff Presentation
                      Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that Mike Alland had shown interest in the
             Messersmith property for a Modular Data Center, which will use a lot of power
             but would use a smaller piece of property. They are good for cloud computing and
             smaller data centers for hospitals and the like.
                      One of the upsides to this approach would be the latent use of utilities,
             utilities that are already sitting there and could be used. In addition, they are not
             required to be manned, though the manager may live close by to monitor them.

          b. Commission Comment
