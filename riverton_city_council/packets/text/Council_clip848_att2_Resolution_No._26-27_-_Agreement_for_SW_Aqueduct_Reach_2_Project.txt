                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-27

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE AGREEMENT
 FOR CONSTRUCTION AND COST-SHARING OF THE SOUTHWEST AQUEDUCT
                        REACH 2 PROJECT

        WHEREAS, the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District has prepared plans and
specifications for the construction of the Southwest Aqueduct Reach 2 Project in Riverton City,
Utah; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to include within the Project certain improvements
consisting of (1) the 3200 West Roadway Improvements and (2) the relocation of Riverton City’s
pipeline, as more particularly described in Exhibit “1” to the Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District and Riverton City desire to
enter into an Agreement for Construction and Cost-Sharing of the Southwest Aqueduct Reach 2
Project setting forth the terms and conditions under which the improvements will be constructed
and the associated costs shared; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it is in the best interest
of the City to approve and authorize execution of the Agreement for Construction and Cost-
Sharing of the Southwest Aqueduct Reach 2 Project.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute the Agreement for Construction
          and Cost-Sharing of the Southwest Aqueduct Reach 2 Project in substantially the
          same form as attached hereto as Exhibit “A,” and upon approval as to legal form by
          the City Attorney.
       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
