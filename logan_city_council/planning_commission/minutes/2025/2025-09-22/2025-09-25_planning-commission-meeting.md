Minutes of the meeting for the Logan City Planning Commission convened in regular session on
Thursday, September 25, 2025. Commissioner Peterson called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m.

Commissioners Present: Eldon Peterson, Sara Doutre, Jessica Lucero, Sarah McNamara, Jennifer

PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 25-044 Vinyl Fencing Storage -Withdrawn by Applicant. [Conditional Use Permit] Garyn
Perrett, property owner, is requesting a conditional use permit for an outdoor storage area used by
Mtn West Vinyl to store fencing bundles and runners located at approximately 495 North 1000 West
in the Commercial Services (CS) zone; TIN 05-062-0026 (Ellis Neighborhood).

PC 25-051 Seed School [Conditional Use Permit] Madison Jones/Royal Oaks Legacy LLC,
authorized agent/property owner is requesting a conditional use permit to operate a new hybrid
microschool that focuses on academics taught through mindfulness, nature, and play, as an
alternative place to receive education located at 80 West Golf Course Road, Suite B, in the
Commercial (COM) Zone; TIN 02-064-0008 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

Staff: Aimee Egbert, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a Microschool located at 80 West
Golf Course Road, Suite B, serving approximately 35 children, operating Monday through
Wednesday from 10:00 A.M to 3:00 P.M. The proposed parking plan shows 6 stalls in front of the
Microschool Suite designated for drop-off and pick-up circulation, and 7 stalls in the southwest corner
of the parking lot for school staff.

A. Egbert explained that Microschools are not specifically listed in the Land Development Code
(LDC), but staff has determined that the use is most comparable to daycare/preschool facilities, and
because the proposed school exceeds 17 students, a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) is required in
the Commercial zone.

Commissioner Peterson asked if there would be any differences in a CUP for preschools vs private
schools. A. Egbert said the CUP would be the same in regards to mitigating potential impacts to the
surrounding properties.

Proponent: Maddie Jones, owner of the Seed School said the ages of kids attending will be
Kindergarten through 5" grade.


Public: No public comments were made.

MOTION: Commissioner Duncan made a motion to conditionally approve a Conditional Use Permit
for PC 25-051 to operate a microschool with the conditions of approval and findings for approval as
listed below. Commissioner McNamara seconded the motion. The motion was approved
unanimously.

PC 25-050 Cache Valley Marketplace PDO Amendment [Design Review & Code Amendment]
Brady Morris/Woodsonia Cache Valley LLC, authorized agent/owner is requesting an amendment to

change commercial property layout and final design of multi-family structures, located at 115 & 155
East Cache Valley Blvd and 1295 North 200 East in the Commercial (COM) Zone; TIN 05-140-0003;
-0004; -0005 (Adams Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Logan City Planner, reviewed the original 2023 Cache Valley Marketplace
Planned Development Overlay (PDO) project that consisted of a Target, 156 room hotel/retail
building, and 346 multi-family residential units. The amended proposal is for the south portion of the
property while the north portion of the project remains the same. The amendment eliminates the
156-room hotel and replaces it with 62,000 square feet of single-story retail space. The new retail
space includes two store fronts that align with the storefront of the Target building to the north. The
new multi-family residential buildings proposed east of the new retail space are both 4-story buildings,
one with 41 dwelling units, and one with 74 dwelling units. Both residential buildings have rear-entry
ground floor garage parking proposed. Both multi-family buildings comply with the Land
Development Code (LDC) design standards as submitted, but the south fagade of the retail building
adjacent to Cache Valley Blvd. has a blank wall that does not meet the LDC minimum design
standards.

R. Holley reviewed the proposed changes to the Code for the PDO, which include updating the
number of dwelling units per building and the square footage of the proposed retail space. R. Holley
said that the layout and pedestrian circulation has not changed from the original project.

Commissioner Lucero asked about the blank wall space on the west facade of the retail building,
between the two tenant spaces, and what the minimum transparency requirement is in this zone. R.
Holley said that the Commercial zone requires 30% transparency.

Commissioner Peterson asked if the bus stop is still required along Main Street. R. Holley said that
a bus stop with additional enhancements is still planned along Main Street in front of the Arby's.

Proponent: Brady Morris said they are excited to get this project started and are in the process of
getting the building permits for the northern multi-family buildings that are not included in this
amendment.

Public: No public comments were made.

Commission: Commissioner Doutre asked about the 1200 North 200 East intersection improvement
timeline. Darren Farar, Logan City Engineer, stated that the street improvements outside of the right
of way will start this week, and the roadway improvements will start next spring and should be
completed before Target is complete.

Commissioner Doutre asked if the proposed changes to the development warrant a new traffic study.
D. Farar said that there isn’t a big enough change in use to generate a new study.

Commissioner Lucero said she would like to see some enhanced landscaping along the west and
south side of the commercial building, where the blank walls are.


Commissioner Peterson asked what Target was required to do for their blank wall space. R. Holley
said that Target had extra enhancements around their entry ways.

The Commission discussed having additional enhancements in front of the retail space whether it be
landscaping, trellises, or pedestrian seating areas.

B. Morris said there is a possibility that there could be three tenants in the retail building creating a
third entrance on the west facade.

The Commission determined that enhanced landscaping or additional architectural features were
needed along the south and west facade where there are blank wall sections, or on the west facade,
there could be a third entrance added.
