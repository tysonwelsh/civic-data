                                                              FY26 Budget Amendment




Honorable Mayor and Council,

Background:
The City’s Skate Park is over 20 years old and has reached the end of its useful life. Based on professional
assessments, its condition has deteriorated to the point where replacement is recommended as the
most viable option.
Proposal:
City staff propose engaging a qualified and experienced vendor to develop conceptual design
alternatives for the replacement of the existing Skate Park. The selected consultant will provide options
in order to facilitate a community engagement process to gather input from residents regarding desired
features, design elements, and amenities to be included.
Fiscal Impact:
The estimated cost for the conceptual design phase (including public outreach and concept
development) is $50,000. Staff recommends amending the Fiscal Year 2026 budget to allocate $50,000
from the General Fund, specifically from fund balance. These funds would be transferred to the General
Capital Improvement Fund (450) to support this project’s design phase. The City has submitted the
project to be awarded TRCC funds, if approved, the budget will be adjusted as part of our year end
budget amendment process to reflect the new funding source.
Recommendation:
It is recommended that the City Council authorize staff to amend the FY2026 budget and approve the
transfer of $50,000 from the General Fund to the General CIP Fund (450) to fund design services for the
Skate Park replacement project.
