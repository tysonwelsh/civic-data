Public Hearings (6:00 PM)
1)
Public hearing and possible adoption of Ordinance No. 988, an Ordinance Amending the Snyderville Basin Development Code Sections 10-4-9: Parking Requirements, 10-8 General Regulations, 10-2-10 Use Table and 1011-1 Terms Defined. The purpose of the amendments is to create regulations for Electric Vehicle Parking, Bicycle Parking, and Solar Energy Systems, and to amend the existing gas station regulations. Project #24-179. Ray Milliner, County Planner (6:01 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Council Staff Report January 29, 2024 with ordinance.pdf
Ray Milliner, Senior Planner, presented Ordinance No. 988 amending the Snyderville Basin Development Code to create regulations for Election Vehicle Parking, Bicycle Parking, and Solar Energy Systems and to amend existing gas station regulations.
Emily Quintin, Sustainability Director, addressed the Council.
Senta Beyer, Active Transportation Planning Manager, briefly addressed the members of the Council.
Council Members asked brief clarifying questions to which Planner Milliner responded.
Council Chair Hanson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:17 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Council Chair Hanson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:18 p.m.
Council Members asked follow up questions and provided feedback on the proposed amendments.
Council directed staff to go into the community to gather additional information and bring the item back to the Council for possible adoption.
