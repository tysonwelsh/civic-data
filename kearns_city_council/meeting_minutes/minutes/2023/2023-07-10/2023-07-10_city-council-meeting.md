Citizen Public Input

               There was no public input.

Presentation by Ben Horsley of the Granite School District

              Ben Horsley, Chief of Staff, Granite School District, stated the district is working
on a study that is looking into boundary changes, consolidations, and possible school closures.
These studies are available online at graniteschools.org. He will share the recommendations in
tomorrow’s meeting with the public and receive feedback.

Wasatch Front Waste & Recycling District (WFWRD)

               Renee Plant, Administrative Manager, WFWRD, stated WFWRD placed a glass
recycling container at Oquirrh Park. However, the County Parks & Recreation Division has asked
that it be removed. She is uncertain of the reason. Some other ideas for placement are Oquirrh
Hills Elementary or the Kearns Shopping Center. WFWRD recently placed a chatbot on the
website to assist customers after hours and it has received good feedback. It is close to having
a QR code for the landfill vouchers.

                Mayor Bush suggested that Ms. Plant reach out to the Parks & Recreation
Division for clarification.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Chief Wade Russell, Unified Fire Authority, stated there was a kitchen fire that
afternoon. It was not a working incident, but there was about $50,000 worth of damage. The July
4th holiday kept UFA busy. There were a few dumpster/yard fires but no working incidents. UFA
staffed an extra engine and will do so again on the 23rd and 24th. UFA will be formulating its
strategic plan, which is redone every three years. This will likely be done during August. Chief
Mecham retired after 25 years and moved to Florida to accept a position as an emergency
manager.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−

Unified Police Department (UPD)

                 Chief Levi Hughes, Unified Police Department, stated he did not have statistics
with him, but he would send them out electronically later. He reported that a recent survey
revealed citizens are happy with dispatch, response times, hold times, and with the officers in
general. In three years, there have been fewer than ten officer complaints, which is good. He
will continue to report to the Council on a quarterly basis.

He presented the Officer of the Month award to Paul Lufkin, who was able to locate $12,000 in
tools that were stolen from a local resident’s business. The victim was very grateful, as the
insurance company was about to deny the claim. Detective Lufkin has been in investigations for
three years. He also presented an award to Detective Travis Clark, who was able to identify two
suspects and recover $105,000 in stolen property that was stolen from a storage unit. He shared
that recently, Representative Burgess Owens presented four officers with Certificates of Special
Congressional Recognition and their names were read aloud during the recent session. Those

Kearns Community Council

               Paula Larsen, Chair, Kearns Community Council, reported on Family Fun
Hometown Game Night, which will be on August 4th at 6:00 pm between the Kearns Library and
Oquirrh Hills Elementary School. This idea was a collaboration with the Community Council and

Hoses” obstacle course and to choose teams of 5 from their stations.

Legal Updates/Information

Update on HB 374

                Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated he will continue
to work on legislation to convert the metro townships to cities and towns. His firm is drafting
legislation on this and working with other councils and Representative Jordan Teuscher. There
is a hearing scheduled for August, which is well within the time frame. Kearns needs to either
come up with a contract with the County Sheriff or revamp Unified Police Department (UPD).

Update on Kearns Art Council
