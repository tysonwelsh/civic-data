D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Chair Froerer: Commissioner Harvey, welcome back from your excursion to the Olympics. We very much
      appreciate your efforts representing Weber County. And it was a great Olympics. I'm going to admit
      publicly I'm a Olympic addict. I watched just about every event I could. I've had ties to Olympics for a
      number of years. My boys and I got to skate in the opening and closing ceremonies in 2002 Winter
      Olympics, which was a very special event. I want to recognize our USA hockey teams, both the men’s and
      the women’s teams. They both played and won in overtime, anybody that watched those games that wasn't
      standing on their feet might have had a problem because they were great games. Both of those were great
      games and it really brings us together as a country, in my opinion, to see that happen. Not unlike in 1980
      when we last won a hockey gold medal at Lake Placid with a bunch of amateur college kids going against the
      Soviet Union. Even though pro players were not allowed in the Olympics at that time, the Soviet hockey
      players were by all accounts pro players. They had jobs in the military but they were paid by the Soviet
      government and their whole job was to skate. The USA team in 1980 Lake Placid ended up winning the
      gold. That was impressive to all of us in 1980 but just as impressive was the wins both women men's and
      women's had this past week. It's a great event. We appreciate you being there for us, Commissioner Harvey.

F. RECOGNITION:
   1. Recognition of the Ogden Eccles Conference Center along with Hogan Construction and VCBO Architects for
      receiving two awards for the Conference Center remodel project:
                          Associated General Contractors of Utah – Interior/Tenant Improvement Project of the Year Award.
                          Utah Construction & Design Magazine – Outstanding Project Awards Publishers Pick
            Kassi Bybee (Ogden Eccles Conference Center & Peery’s Egyptian Theater General Manager):
            (Presentation) Ogden Eccles Conference Center was built in 1997 and due for a technology upgrade and a
            facelift. Through an RFP process VCBO Architecture was chosen to design the project with Phil Haderlie as
            Principal, Marilee Vicencio the associate principal and lead designer, and Corine Crouch as Principal. And
            Hogan construction was selected as the Construction Management General Contractor with Rob Birch as
            Principal, Carissa Miller as Project Manager, Kelli Munoz as Superintendent, and Jose Garcia as Foreman.
            Together they managed all the sub-contractors, with Britany Punjabi as Sr Marketing Manager. There were
            18 phases in the project with the balcony being first. It was really a unique project as events were being held
            during the renovation, which included a new RGB lighting system, a new audio/video system, the terrace, the
            courtyard, the socializing spaces in the hallway, the custom skylights in the Garand, all along with the
            HVAC, carpet, tile, and paint. Over the period of the remodel, over 500 events were held. I am grateful to
            the community that decided to still hold their events with us during the remodel. Flexibility, problem
            solving, creativity, work ethic, accountability, trust, and friendship are what earned these awards. We
            worked together to solve the problems that arose.
            Phil Haderlie (Principal & Architect, VCBO): VCBO has been involved with the County on this project since
            our selection in 2022. It’s been a fantastic project and we really loved working with your people in the County.
            Remodels are messy and complicated. We are grateful for the partnership, the Commissioners, and for being
            recognized for some nice awards. Our goal is to create spaces for people to thrive. This is an amazing amenity
            you already had in the County. We added some shine so it can continue to sparkle and function for many more
            years. We are grateful to have been part of the project.
            Rob Birch (VP Hogan Construction): The best way to describe this project is a big dance. If you can imagine,
            we had... Our biggest concern was keeping the public and our crew safe. There is a lot you can’t anticipate
            when remodeling an old building and this one had its setbacks and challenges. The thing I am most proud of
         are the relationships we built. I feel like our team got stronger with each setback and challenge. Thank you
         for trusting us with this project.
         Commissioner Bolos: A couple of impressive things about this project. First, the fact you were able to stay
         open during the project. And I love the tie-ins to the theater and its architecture. I appreciate the work by
         everybody.
         Commissioner Harvey: Why do we do this? Why would put $20M into this facility? The answer is tourism.
         With all the Weber County facilities, operationally, tourism pays for all of the parks and facilities. Every man,
         woman, and child would have to pay $2,200 a year to make up the difference tourism brings to this County.
         Only Weber County can say it breaks even on its parks. I want to thank Jason Horne from Purchasing, and
         Sean and his team, and Kassi especially you and your team for making this work. You brought economic
         impact during the project. You still made a difference in tourism and it’s absolutely phenomenal. I have been
         in the event space for over three decades now and you did a wonderful job. To Rob, Carissa, Britney, and
         especially Kelly and Jose...holy cow! I’d come over and stop by during the project and never did I see people
         misbehave, ever. They represented you really well. There a million more things I can say. But great job! The
         only thing I could say is we would hire you again.
         Chair Froerer: I have to admit when Kassi proposed this idea years ago I was concerned how they would be
         able to stay open during the renovation. Kassi looked at us and said, “We will get it done.” And they did.
         This is a facility that entertains people from around the world. It is a facility we can be proud of. If we had
         gold medals, I would give one to each member of the team today.
