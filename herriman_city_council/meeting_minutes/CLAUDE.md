# meeting_minutes/ — Herriman City Council vote pipeline

## What's here
- `minutes/<year>/<week-monday>/<date>_<slug>.md` — **180** City Council minutes,
  2020-01-08 → 2026-05-27. Council meets **Wednesday** (2nd & 4th; work + general captured in
  one combined minutes doc); the folder is keyed on that week's Monday. Indexed in
  `minutes_index.csv` (8-col standard). `source` ∈ `primegov` (PrimeGov CompiledDocument) /
  **`s3-legacy` (the 2020 backfill from the city's legacy `herriman-agendas` AWS S3 bucket**
  — PrimeGov only serves 2021-01+). **2026-07-16 repairs:** two 2021-10-13 index rows were
  agenda-compilation WRONG DOCS (templateIds 547/553 — 553 embedded the full 2021-08-11
  minutes and had produced 18 wrong-dated motions) → deindexed, .md removed, raw PDFs
  retained; the 2021-01-13 doc is the CDRA minutes portal-mistitled "City Council Meeting"
  → front-matter body corrected Council→CDRA (see the file's **Note:** header).
- **+55 PMN-promoted council-family docs** (21 council incl. the real 2021-01-13 RCCM,
  5 joint CC/PC merged as `body=Council` per the audited joint-doc convention, 13 CDRA,
  10 HCSEA, 6 HCFSA) living in `../pmn_backfill/text/` and merged into `all_votes.csv` by
  **`extract_backfill_votes.py`** with **`provenance=pmn_minutes`** (audited rows =
  `minutes`). Their `source` paths point into `pmn_backfill/`.
- `minutes_unrecovered.csv` — the one remaining true gap: 2020-07-29 joint CC/PC work
  meeting (minutes never posted; PMN holds the audio).
- `raw/<date>_<id>.pdf` — the retained source PDFs (never modified).
- `extract_votes.py` — the deterministic parser (no LLM, no network; resumable).
- `votes/<year>/<week>/<date>_*.json` — one structured JSON per meeting (the resumable
  intermediate; `all_votes.csv` is rebuilt from these).
- `votes/_validation_report.txt` — integrity checks, per-year roster, tally mismatches,
  contested-vote list (regenerated by `validate_votes.py`).
- `all_votes.csv` — long format, **one row per member-vote** (or one placeholder row per
  tally-only motion), 13-col schema:
  `date,year,title,body,motion_no,motion,motion_type,result,mover,seconder,member,vote,source`.
- `motions_std.csv` — the normalization layer (normalized outcome/tallies/motion_type_std),
  joinable on `(source, motion_no)`; **cross-city comparison goes through this**, never the
  raw `result`/`motion_type`.
- ⚠ The 2020 interior date gaps were NOT all COVID cancellations — 9 proven cancellations
  + 12 real meetings whose minutes were PMN-recovered and promoted 2026-07-16 (see above).

## ⚠ The MAYOR VOTES — max council roll = 5
Herriman uses Utah's **six-member council form in which the Mayor is a full voting member**
(the Millcreek model). Four district members (**D1–D4**) **plus the Mayor** cast roll-call
votes → a full roll = **5** (never 4). The extractor treats `Mayor <Name>` in a roll as a
real voting member. Verified at source:
```
Councilmember Jared Henderson         Aye
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn            Aye
Councilmember Steven Shields          Aye
Councilmember Clint Smith             Nay
Mayor David Watts                     Nay          ← the Mayor's Nay is counted
The motion passed with a vote 3:2                  (2020-01-08 motion 5)
```
Mayor **David Watts** (2020–2021, 167 vote rows) and Mayor **Lorin Palmer** (2022+, 478 vote
rows) are both first-class voters. The Mayor even appears in absence tracking (`"...passed
unanimously with Mayor Watts being absent"`). ⚠ This **corrects `../recon.md` §4**, which
assumed the Mayor was non-voting (max tally 4) — follow the verified data, not the recon.

## Vote grammar the parser handles
- **Full named roll (Mayor + members)** under a `"The vote was recorded as follows:"` header,
  one member per line (`Councilmember <Name> Aye|Nay|Yes|No`, `Mayor <Name> Aye`), then a
  narrative outcome — the common substantive form (`Yes`/`No` normalize to `Aye`/`Nay`).
- **Tally-only / unanimous-unnamed** — `"passed (all voted aye)"` (454 motions) → one
  placeholder row, member blank, `names_recorded:false`. Honest, not a miss.
- **Named-absence unanimous** — `"The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Ohrn being
  absent"` → the absentee gets an explicit `Absent` row.
- **Contested tallies** — `"...passed with a vote 4:1"` / `"3:2"` with the named dissenter(s).

`motion_type` uses the city-native taxonomy (top: Procedural/Administrative 618 · Land-Use/
Zoning 125 · Public Hearing Action 82 · Ordinance 69 · Resolution 65); `result` and the
numeric tally are **verbatim as printed**. Normalized values live **alongside** in
`motions_std.csv` (verbatim is never overwritten).

## `body` column — agencies (CDRA / HCSEA / HCFSA)
Default `Council`. Three district agencies convene around council meetings; their minutes
are **separate documents** (each with its own `**Body:**` front matter), tagged `body`:
- **CDRA** — Community Development & Renewal Agency (152 rows / 64 motions)
- **HCSEA** — Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area (95 rows / 39 motions)
- **HCFSA** — Herriman City Fire Service Area (87 rows / 31 motions)
Officials sit as "Trustee/Board Member/Director" (the mayor as Chair; the CDRA docs print
present-tense "The vote is recorded as follows"). ⚠ The pre-2026-07-16 claim that "the
in-meeting captures are the complete published record" was WRONG — standalone agency
minutes exist on Utah PMN and their content is absent from the combined council docs;
they are now promoted (`provenance=pmn_minutes`). One retained source anomaly: the
2020-05-27 HCSEA roll prints "Trustee Nicole Martin" (a 2019-era trustee) though the
attendance line lists Shields — kept verbatim.
