WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
MAY 13, 2025

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 1:00 p.m. on May 13, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist (via telephone), Deputy County
Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Adam Snow was excused.
CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that LaVerkin City has received a proposal by
Interstate Rock Products to create a critical infrastructure materials protection area. Under state
law, the County Commission appoints an Advisory Board. It is recommended the Advisory Board
consisting of the following individuals: Kirk Thornick, Victor Iverson, Todd Edwards, Phil
Schmidt and Mark Owens.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3512, A
Resolution Appointing Board Members to the Washington County Advisory
Board Regarding a Proposal for a Critical Infrastructure Materials Protection
Area. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3513, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AMENDMENT TO THE LEASE AGREEMENT FOR SPACE IN THE OLD ADMIN

BUILDING

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated a lease is currently in place in the Old Administration
Building, and this lease agreement would amend the square footage, amend the rental schedule,
and extend the lease term.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3513, A
Resolution Approving an Amendment to the Lease Agreement for Space in the
Old Administration Building. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Snow voting

aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3514, A RESOLUTION TO ISSUE A
FORMAL COMMENT ON THE GUNLOCK STATE PARK WEST ACCESS ROAD ROW

Mr. Snow explained that Gunlock State Park would like to create a west access road, which is
currently in the comment period. This letter would submit a comment in support of the action.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3514, A
Resolution to Issue a Formal Comment on the Gunlock State Park West
Access Road ROW. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 1:05 p.m.
