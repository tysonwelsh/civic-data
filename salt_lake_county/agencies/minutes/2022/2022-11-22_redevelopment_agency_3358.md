Presenter: Jevon Gibb, Executive Director, Salt Lake County RDA 
Informational
Mr. Jevon Gibb , Director, Economic Development Division, reviewed a 
briefing memorandum describing the proposed Redevelopment Agency 
(RDA) budget for next year.  The big activity will be Magna Main Street .  
Some of the real estate transactions he was hoping to close in 2022 are being 
pushed back to early 2023.  Then, the RDA is going to continue the fa çade 
improvement grant and is looking at doing murals and other beautification 
projects there to try to activate the neighborhood.  Most of the activities on 
Magna Main Street are due to the reimbursement agreement from the Magna 
Arbor Park project, which was discussed a couple weeks ago.  The project 
area will just be administered until it closes out in 2030.  
A motion was made by Director Granato to approve this agenda item.
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated a motion was not 
needed.  The budget attached to this agenda item can now be put on display 
for the public, and Council staff has already noticed the Redevelopment 
Agency meeting in conjunction with the other budget hearings that will be 
held December 6, 2022.
