ORDINANCE NO. 26-03

AN ORDINANCE VACATING A MUNICIPAL UTILITY EASEMENT
LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 4998 SOUTH GALLERIA DRIVE AND
4950 SOUTH GALLERIA DRIVE, MURRAY CITY, SALT LAKE COUNTY,
STATE OF UTAH.

WHEREAS, the Murray City Municipal Council received a petition to vacate a
i municipal utility easement from Murray City Power; and

WHEREAS, the petition requested that a municipal utility easement located at
approximately 4998 South Galleria Drive and 4950 South Galleria Drive, Murray, Salt
Lake County, State of Utah be vacated; and

WHEREAS, the petition meets the requirements of U.C.A. §1 0-20-813, as
amended; and

WHEREAS, the request was made in order to facilitate the proposed construction
and operation of a new building at 4998 South Galleria Drive; and

WHEREAS, the developer of at 4998 So Galleria Drive has provided to the City
with a new power easement at of equal or greater value than the easement proposed
for vacatur; and

WHEREAS, the Murray City Municipal Council finds good cause to vacate the
municipal utility easement located at approximately 4998 South Galleria Drive-and 4950
South Galleria Drive, Murray, Salt Lake County, State of Utah; and neither the public
interest nor any person will be materially injured by the vacation; and

WHEREAS, the Murray City Municipal Council finds that Affected Entities and
Record Owners of Land have been given notice and have been consulted and that


notice has been published and a public hearing has been held on March 3, 2026
pursuant thereto, all as required by law.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL:

Section1. That the municipal utility easement located at approximately 4998
South Galleria Drive and 4950 South Galleria Drive, Murray, Salt Lake County, State of
Utah, is vacated and that the City releases any and all title, right or interest it may have
in the described municipal utility easement. The vacated municipal utility easement is
particularly described as follows:

COMMENCING West 367.29 feet and South 1192.02 feet, from the Northeast
Corner of the Northwest quarter corner of Section 12, Township 2 South, Range
1 West, Salt Lake Base and Meridian. Said point of commencement being an
existing power pole; thence five feet on each side of the following three courses:
Thence N 77°54’44” W, 77.62 feet; thence N 21°37'34” W, 153.98 feet; thence N
02°33’53”E, 135.02 feet to the terminus of said 10 feet wide easement, which is
also an Overhead Sign Location.

Section 2. This Ordinance shall take effect upon the first publication and filing
of a copy thereof in the office of the City Recorder.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Murray City Municipal Council on
this 3 day of March 2026.
