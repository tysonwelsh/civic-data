RESOLUTION NO. 142
RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE 
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY APPROVING 
AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH THE 
GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT TO HELP 
FUND STREETLIGHT POLES AND BANNER ARMS IN MAGNA
R E C I T A L S
WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “RDA”) 
and the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “District”) are 
“public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah 
Code §§ 11-13-101 to -608, and as such, are authorized to enter into 
agreements to act jointly and cooperatively in a manner that will enable them 
to make the most efficient use of their resources and powers;  
WHEREAS, the District has decided to replace several streetlight poles and 
banner arms along Main Street in the Magna Metro Township, Utah; 
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township is within the boundaries of the 
RDA’s Magna Main Street Community Development Project Area;
WHEREAS, in order to help provide for the development of the Magna Main 
Street Community Development Project Area, the RDA desires to make a 
financial contribution toward the cost of the streetlight poles and banner 
arms;
AND WHEREAS, the agreement will provide for the RDA to share $ 11,774 
of its revenue with the District as provided therein.
R E S O L U T I O N
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Board of 
Directors of the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County:

DISSOLVING THE MAGNA WEST MAIN STREET NEIGHBORHOOD

DEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREA
R E C I T A L S
WHEREAS, in 1988, the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the 
“Agency”) created the Magna West Main Street Neighborhood Development 
Project Area (the “Project Area ”) and adopted a project area plan (the “Area 
Plan”);
WHEREAS, the Area Plan limits the allocation and use of tax increment from 
the Project Area to a 32-year period beginning on the date that the plan was 
approved by the Agency;
WHEREAS, the time period whereby tax increment from the Project Area 
may be collected, allocated or used has ended; and
WHEREAS, the Agency now desires to dissolve the Project Area.
R E S O L U T I O N
NOW, THEREFORE, the Governing Board of the Redevelopment Agency 
of Salt Lake County, Utah, resolves as follows:
A. That the Magna West Main Street Neighborhood Development Project 
Area is hereby DISSOLVED.
B. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval and 
adoption by the Agency and will be filed and recorded in the official minutes 
and records of the Agency for this meeting.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 6th day of February, 2024.
