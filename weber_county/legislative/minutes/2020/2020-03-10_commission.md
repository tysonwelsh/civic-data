    1. LEASE AGREEMENT BY/BETWEEN THE WEBER COUNTY LIBRARY BOARD OF TRUSTEES & THE WEBER
       COUNTY LIBRARY DEVELOPMENT FUND FOR LEASE OF PROPERTY AT THE OGDEN VALLEY BRANCH.
    2. LEASE AGREEMENT BY/BETWEEN THE WEBER COUNTY LIBRARY BOARD OF TRUSTEES AND THE WEBER
       COUNTY LIBRARY DEVELOPMENT FUND FOR LEASE OF PROPERTY AT THE PLEASANT VALLEY BRANCH.
       Chair Froerer recognized the Library Board members present. Lynnda Wangsgard, County Library
       Director, presented these two related items. This Development Fund was established in 1976 as a 501(c)(3)
       corporation to promote, develop, and expand the Weber County Library and its educational, scientific, and
       charitable objectives. The Fund may receive, hold, own, manage, use, purchase, pledge and dispose of
       property of all kinds for the Library’s benefit. During 2007, Doug and Elaine Stephens donated property
       to this Fund to provide a building site for the Pleasant Valley Branch Library and Washington Terrace City
       provided an easement on city-owned property for expansion of green space and parking. In 2013 a small
       piece of property (not large enough for a home) on the west side of the Ogden Valley Branch was put on
       the market and county officials did not want the Library Board to purchase it using fund balance savings
       or available capital funding. To secure the future of that property to provide enhanced services for the
       Ogden Valley Branch, the lot was purchased through the Fund. This Branch’s waste water disposal system
       was recently located on this lot as well as the outdoor education center. It recently came to their attention,
       that because the county’s liability insurance is procured through the Utah County Insurance Pool (UCIP),
       that UCIP may not honor a claim if the property is titled in the Fund’s name, a not for profit corporation.
       Marsha Harris, Library Development Fund Chair, had approached the Library Board with a proposal to
       offer a 50-year lease at $1/year for each property to resolve that issue. The Library Board voted to approve
       this at its public meeting on February 4. Utah law includes granting the library board of directors, with the
       approval of the county executive, and in accordance with county ordinances and policies, responsibility for the
       expenditure of the library fund, construction, lease, or sale of library buildings and land, and the operation and
       care of the library. She outlined the county’s ordinance which also grants certain related responsibilities to the
       Library Board of Directors for the benefit of the library. Ms. Wangsgard requested approval of the two
       agreements on behalf of the Development Fund. Commissioner Jenkins does not feel that this is wrong or
       a huge mistake but would handle it differently because he believes that the county should have possession
       of the deed, rather than the Library Development Fund. Chair Froerer said that a detailed discussion on
       this occurred on 2/24 at the Commission’s work session, and that he is comfortable with it. Commissioner
       Harvey, sits on the Library Board and supports this 100%. He has the utmost confidence that the board
       members are not self-serving, that they have the Library’s best interest at heart, and that it represents good
       government and efficiency. He appreciates their efforts to positively perpetuate the libraries.
         Chair Froerer reiterated that this is a matter of resolving the liability issue and making sure there is coverage
         under UCIP and Ms. Wangsgard concurred that liability is the major concern.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the lease agreement by/between the Weber County Library Board
         of Trustees and the Weber County Library Development Fund for lease of property at the Ogden Valley
         Branch; Chair Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – nay; Chair Froerer – aye
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the lease agreement by/between the Weber County Library Board

    3. CONTRACT WITH NORTH OGDEN CITY FOR THE PURCHASE OF A RIGHT-OF-WAY THAT WILL FACILITATE
       ADA SIDEWALK ACCESS AT STREET CROSSING.
       Bryan Baron, Deputy County Attorney, stated that North Ogden is working to expand 2600 North east of
       Washington Blvd. The City is currently trying to acquire the rights to the land along 2600 North. The
       North Library Branch sits on 2600 N. and some of the county’s property will be needed for that expansion
       project. North Ogden made an offer of $3,365 to the Library Board for 214 square feet of the far northwest
       corner of the property, but the Library Board felt it was a low offer. However, the City is offering an
       incentive of $3,000, if this contract is signed and returned today, which Mr. Baron feels is more than the
       property is worth. The Library Board agreed. The City approved this last week.
       Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contract with North Ogden City for the purchase of a right-
       of-way for $6,365 to facilitate ADA sidewalk access at a street crossing; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
       Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    4. CONTRACT WITH DAVIS COUNTY FOR TEMPORARY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY NURSING SERVICES.
       County Sheriff Chief Aaron Perry stated that currently the Weber County Jail is experiencing a shortage
       of nurses. This contract allows Davis County jail nurses that perform similar services to pickup shifts
       temporarily at Weber County’s jail. The initial term is for 30 days but can be extended 30 days at a time
       as needed. He addressed Chair Froerer’s question stating that there are currently six vacancies out of
       fourteen nurse positions, that they are in the process of hiring one, that there are three more in the interview
       process, and other steps are also being taken to handle the issue, but this would just give an extra option if
       needed.
       Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the contract with Davis County for temporary correctional
       facility nursing services; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
       Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
