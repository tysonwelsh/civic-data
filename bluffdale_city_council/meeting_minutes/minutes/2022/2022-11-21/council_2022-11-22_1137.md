                                         BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL
                                   BOARD OF CANVASSERS MEETING AGENDA
                                        Tuesday, November 22, 2022

Notice is hereby given that the Bluffdale City Council Board of Canvassers will hold a special meeting on Tuesday, November 22,
2022, at the Bluffdale City Hall, 2222 West 14400 South, Bluffdale, Utah, scheduled to begin promptly at 5:45 p.m. or as soon
thereafter as possible. This meeting will also be broadcast live to the public at: www.bluffdale.com. Notice is further given that
access to this meeting by the Mayor and/or City Council may be by electronic means via telephonic conference call.

BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING 5:45 P.M.

 1. Roll Call

 3. Adjournment

Mayor Natalie Hall called the meeting to order at 5:45 p.m.

BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING

1.       Roll Call.

All members of the City Council were present, with the exception of Dave Kallas who was
excused.

2.       Consideration and Vote on Resolution No. 2022-50, Certifying the Canvass of the
         Official Election Results for the 2022 City of Bluffdale Tax Referendum, Conducted
         by Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office, Elections Division.

A copy of the Resolution containing the election results was distributed to the Council Members
for their review.

Council Member Gaston moved to approve Resolution No. 2022-50, Certifying the Canvass
of the Official Election Results for the Bluffdale Tax Referendum. Council Member Aston
seconded the motion. Vote on motion: Council Member Aston-Aye, Council Member Hales-
Aye, Council Member Gaston-Aye, Council Member Crockett-Aye. The motion passed
unanimously.

3.       Adjourn.

Council Member Crockett moved to ADJOURN. Council Member Aston seconded the
motion. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

The Work Session adjourned at 5:50 p.m.
