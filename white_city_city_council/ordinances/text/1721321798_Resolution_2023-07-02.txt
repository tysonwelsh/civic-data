A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
| COUNCIL TO RECONSIDER RESOLUTION 23-06-01
REGARDING PLANNING COMMISSIONS APPOINTMENTS AND,
IN THE ALTERNATIVE, APPROVING THE APPOINTMENT OF
ROBERT FRAILEY AS A FULL-TIME PLANNING COMMISSION
MEMBER FOR A THREE-YEAR TERM ENDING FEBRUARY 28,
2026

Whereas, the White City Metro Township ("White City") is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seg.; and

Whereas, pursuant to Ordinance 17-06-02, Planning Commission Members are appointed by the
Metro Township Council to serve three-year terms; and

Whereas, on June 6, 2023, Resolution 23-06-02 was approved by the White City Metro
Township Council, which Resolution needs to be reconsidered due to an applicant withdrawing an
application for a Planning Commission appointment; and

Whereas, the White City Metro Township Council now desires reconsider it prior resolution 23-
06-02 and desires to adopt a new resolution to appoint Robert Frailey as a Full-Time Planning
Commission Member with a term ending February 28, 2026,

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council does hereby
reconsider its prior adoption of Resolution 23-06-02 and, instead does adopt this Resolution approving
the appointment of Robert Frailey as a Full-Time Planning Commission Member with a term ending
February 28, 2026.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
6" day of July 2023.
