 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
      a. Accept the proposed Tentative Budget for the Redevelopment Agency of West Jordan
         for Fiscal Year 2024
         Budget/Management Analyst Becky Condie oriented the Council regarding the Fiscal
         Year 2024 Tentative budget. She noted several infrastructure projects including:
         cemtery tank, street lights, and street scapes on Redwood Road, along with signage in
         various areas. Some of these projects had been budgeted for FY 2023 but were still in
         the works, therefore they were being budgeted again for this fiscal year. She reminded
         them April 26, 2023 was scheduled for adopting the tentative budget and a public
         hearing was scheduled for May 10, 2023. The budget will be posted on the City website
         for public access.

          Board Member Kelvin Green pointed out the expense of $739,000 on a participation
          agreement and $1,000,000 for incentive agreements. He asked when the last time those
          agreements were reviewed and were they in compliance.

          In response, Mayor Burton reported that the agreement and contract are monitored
          closely. They met with them on an annual basis to make sure they meet their
          requirements.

          Chief Administrator Korban Lee reported that at times money had actually been
          withheld until they performed within the year. The agreement and contracts are
          reviewed regularly to be sure they are meeting the terms of the agreement and capital
          investment required.
