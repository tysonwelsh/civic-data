CITY COUNCIL AGENDA Notice is hereby given that the Draper City Council will hold a Business Meeting on Tuesday, April 21, 2020, at 5:30 PM,  in the City Council Chambers at 1020 East Pioneer Road, Draper, Utah.   The Agenda will be as follows: ELECTRONIC MEETING This meeting will be held electronically. This meeting will be an electronic meeting pursuant to Draper City Resolution #14-54. As restrictions take place across the State for limiting public gatherings, we want to make sure everyone interested in participating in City Council meetings can do so in a manner that they feel comfortable.
Listen through the City website:  https://www.draper.ut.us/95/Agendas-Minutes
Email your comments to public.comment@draper.ut.us by 5 p.m. on April 21, 2020. These comments will become part of the public record.
If you wish to speak during the Public Hearing portions of the meeting, please send a request for the Zoom Meeting ID to public.comment@draper.ut.us by 12 noon, April 21, 2020.
