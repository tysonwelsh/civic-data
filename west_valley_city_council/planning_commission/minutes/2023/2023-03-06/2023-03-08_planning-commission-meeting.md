C-5-2023
Pro Dynamic Collision Center Outside
Storage 3270 W 2200 S
M Zone (0.25 acres)

Javier Nieto with Pro Dynamic Collision Center is requesting conditional use approval for
outside storage. The property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley City General
Plan designates this area as light manufacturing. The surrounding zoning is also M. Adjacent
land uses include light industrial businesses to the east, west and south, and largely
undeveloped land to the north.

The subject property is located just west of a light industrial complex. The 1.9 acre overall parcel
is largely undeveloped, except for the far northern end and another 0.25 acre area south of the
subject property, which have outside storage uses approved. This proposal will occupy 0.25 acres
of the parcel. The applicant operates an auto collision repair business out of the adjacent building
to the east. The requested area will be for the storage of vehicles that are waiting to be repaired.

Per 7-7-119, outside storage areas are to be screened from public view. There is an existing 6’
tall chain link fence around the area. Slats should be added to the east side of the fence for
screening. There is a storage yard on the west side of the site, and other storage areas on the
north and south, so slats are not required on those sides. Surfacing for outside storage is also to
be evaluated by the Planning Commission. The applicant proposed to finish the site with
crushed limestone. The City has always required automobile storage to be on asphalt or
concrete. Staff recommends the same for this proposal as well.

The area directly adjacent to the west side of the building is not approved for the storage of
vehicles for more than 24 hours. It should be used for employee and customer parking and can
also be used temporarily to move cars in and out of the shop area. The area shall also be free of
solid waste.

The adjacent parcel already meets the required 5% landscaping for the site, so no additional
landscaping is needed. Because the proposed storage area is undeveloped, a grading and
drainage plan is required for review by the Public Works Department.

Staff Alternatives:
Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

             1. Slats shall be required in the chain link fence on the east for screening.
             2. Outside storage is only permitted within the fenced area, which shall be
                hard surfaced with asphalt or concrete.
             3. The area adjacent to the building shall be for customer and employee parking
                and shall be free of solid waste.
             4. A grading and drainage plan shall be submitted for review by the
                Public Works Department.
             5. Requirements of all affected departments and agencies shall be met.
              6. Subject to review upon valid complaint.


Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.


Discussion:     Brock Anderson presented the application. He pointed out that the applicant has
                requested crushed limestone for surfacing. Brock said he spoke with the applicant

                that he had added this as a condition in the staff report.

                Luis Javier Nieto, the applicant, explained that he is willing to comply with all the
                conditions to keep his business in operation.

S-1-2023
Elizabeth Herrera Subdivision – Amending lots 1, 2, and 3 Block 23 Chesterfield Plat A.
1580 West Whitlock Ave.
A Zone
2 Lots
2.5 Acres

BACKGROUND

Elizabeth Herrera is requesting subdivision approval for the Elizabeth Herrera Subdivision. The
proposed subdivision will also amend lots 1, 2, and 3 Block 23 Chesterfield Plat A. The property
is zoned manufacturing and is occupied by an existing single family dwelling. The proposed
subdivision is located on the northwest corner of Whitlock Avenue and Chatham Street.

STAFF/AGENCY CONCERNS:
Fire Department:

               Fire hydrants to be installed in accordance with the Uniform Fire Code if not
                already existing.

Granger Hunter Improvement District:

               Project will need to run availability for water, sewer and fire protection.
               Subject to design and review inspections.

Utility Agencies:

               Subject to all standard easement locations.

Public Works:

               Authorization required of ditch/water users for any abandonment, relocation,
                piping or any other modifications to existing ditches or irrigation structures.

               Revisions to plat are required.

               Will need to coordinate flood plain information at the time of a new building permit.

               Recommendation to increase right-of-way along Chatham Street to improve
                intersection movements. Engineering will coordinate this effort with the property
                owner.

               Dedication and public improvements will be required along Whitlock Avenue and
                Chatham Street.

Building Division:

               Due to ground water and flood plain requirements, a basement is unlikely for the
                new dwelling.


ISSUES:

      The proposed subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 2.5 acres. Lot 1 will be approximately
       2 acres in size with lot 2 comprising a minimum lot size of a half-acre. An existing
       dwelling located on lot 1 will remain.

      Access to the subdivision will be gained from both Whitlock Avenue and Chatham Street.
       Dedication will be required along both rights-of-way, and public improvements including
       curb, gutter, sidewalk and asphalt will be required by the property owner.
    The property is located northwest of the T intersection at Whitlock and Chatham. Years
     ago, the City initiated a relatively small street improvement project at this intersection to
     better move traffic congestion at certain points of the week generated from the Islamic
     Society of Greater Salt Lake. The previous landowner did not want to participate in the
     project and subsequently sold the property. The City installed temporary channelization
     devices which has worked in the interim. Essentially, these devices allow traffic going
     south on Chatham Street to have a free right at the intersection (with a yield sign) to better
     move traffic out to Redwood Road.

    After reviewing the proposed subdivision, the Engineering Division is recommending that
     the same dedication and improvements proposed a few years ago, be applied for this project
     as well. The City would acquire and pay for the additional right-of-way to make the
     intersection work. The applicant would install these improvements as they would normally
     have done for the subdivision anyway. The City would install a permanent island at a
     future point in time.

    In order to make all of this work, an area of 1,281 square feet would need to be dedicated
     for the wider intersection. So that the area requirement of lot 2 still meets with the A zone
     requirements, the north property line would need to be shifted approximately 9 feet to the
     north. According to the subdivision plat, there is sufficient room for this to occur.

    The location of the proposed subdivision sits in a flood plain area as denoted on FEMA
     flood plain maps. While new homes are allowed to be constructed, basements will not be
     permitted. The contractor of the new dwelling will need to verify that the lowest habitable
     floor is a minimum of 1 foot above the flood plain elevation in this area.

    As mentioned in the study session, the property owner has constructed an outbuilding for
     agricultural use. The building is exempted from building permits, and is not quite yet
     finished. Staff is recommending that the structure be completed as it was proposed as soon
     as possible.


STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

A.   Grant preliminary subdivision approval for the Elizabeth Herrera Subdivision subject to
     the following conditions:

     1.     That compliance be made with Granger Hunter Improvement District regarding
            water line extensions, sewer connections and fire protection.

     2.     That the subdivision name be approved by Salt Lake County.

     3.     That the applicant revise the plat to accommodate the additional dedication needed
            for the intersection improvements at Whitlock Avenue and Chatham Street.
      4.      That all matters pertaining to any existing irrigation system be addressed with the
              City Engineering Division and water users.

      5.      That the developer resolve all staff and agency concerns.

      6.      That the property owner’s complete the agricultural building as soon as possible.

      7.      That an elevation certificate be provided at such time the building permit is issued
              for the new lot.

B.    Continuation to address issues raised during the public hearing.


Discussion:   Steve Lehman presented the application. He noted that there was a meeting with
              the Engineering Department and the property owner this past Monday. Steve said
              during the conference, it was decided that the City would be willing to purchase
              an additional 1,280 square feet of right-of-way for intersection improvements at
              Whitlock and Chatham Streets. He noted that the lot line to the north would need
              to move about 9 feet to still make the 21,780 square feet. Steve stated that the
              subject property is within a FEMA flood plan, and most likely, basements would
              not be an option.
              Miguel Carrillo, the property owner, said he had initially been told that the City
              would reimburse him for installing the curb and gutter. He stated that he
              discovered he would be paying for the installation during the meeting last
              Monday. Mr. Carrillo noted that he was okay with the City buying the additional
              property if they were paying for the curb and gutter, and he needs clarification
              about who is responsible. Steve pointed out that those subdividing their property
              are responsible for those improvements. The City would buy the land and the
              improvements would be installed as they would otherwise.
              Mr. Carrillo pointed out the canal located adjacent to his property. He said he
              would be interested in purchasing the canal to offset the property the City would
              buy for the right-of-way. Steve explained there was discussion about the City
              taking ownership of the canal; however, he would need to see if that happened.
              Elizabeth Carrillo, Miguel’s spouse, said she called the canal company, and they
              told her the canal had been deeded to the City last fall. Steve said he would look
              into it and pass along the applicant’s interest in purchasing the canal.
              Steve explained that the City would acquire the additional property for the right-
              of-way in the street. Therefore, the subject property would remain 2.5 acres in
              size as the square footage of that property did not look like it was included in the
              lot square footages. Mr. Carrillo said okay, that is what he was wondering.
             Commissioner Porter asked Steve what would happen with installing the curb,
             gutter, and retaining wall. Steve said he would need to discuss whether or not the
             retaining wall would be required with the Engineering Department. He explained
             that the property owner is responsible for the curb and gutter as part of a
             subdivision.
             Mr. Carrillo stated that he had received a letter from the City stating they would
             add a curb and gutter to his neighborhood. He said it would be unfair for him to
             pay for something the City plans to do anyway.
             Steve explained that an overlay zone was added to the Chesterfield area, and
             certain streets would be required to have improvements installed. Whitlock and
             Chatham Streets are mentioned in the overlay zone and would be required for this
             property.
             Steve pointed out that if the Commission approves this application, it would be
             forwarded on to the City Council. But Planning Commission approval alone
             would not lock the applicant into doing anything if they decided to hold off on the
             subdivision.

SA-1-2023
Tomadakis Subdivision – Amending Lot 1 Pheasant Park & Lot 214 Hunter Hills No.2
5203 West Holder Drive
R-1-8 & R-1-7 Zones
2 Lots


BACKGROUND
Mr. Chris Tomadakis, is requesting preliminary plat approval and an amendment to lot 1 Pheasant
Park and lot 214 Hunter Hills No. 2 Subdivisions. The purpose for the plat amendment is to adjust
the common property line between the two lots. No new lots will be created from this application.


ISSUES:

Mr. Tomadakis is proposing to adjust the common property line between his two properties. This
type of request would previously have been allowed by a lot line adjustment reviewed by staff
only. However, in 2021, State Law changed requiring a plat amendment.

Mr. Tomadakis who owns both properties would like to adjust the existing property line to provide
more useable space for the dwelling at 5203 West Holder Drive. This application is unique in that
it will amend lots in two different subdivisions. The Pheasant Park Subdivision is zoned R-1-8,
and the Hunter Hills No. 2 Subdivision is zoned R-1-7. Both lots meet and exceed frontage and
area requirements of their respective zones. As a result of the subdivision amendment, lot 1 in
Pheasant Park will increase in size to 12,835 square feet, and lot 2 in Hunter Hills will be reduced
to 7,100 square feet in size.

There is an existing 10-foot utility easement on the east side of lot 1 Pheasant Park and a 15-foot
utility and drainage easement on lot 214 Hunter Hills No. 2. Both easements will remain in place.
Any new structures and/or fencing will need to be coordinated with the respective utility and or
drainage companies. The easements are not being vacated or realigned with this application.


STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

1.     Approval of the Tomadakis Subdivision amending lot 1 Pheasant Park and lot 214 Hunter
       Hills No. 2 subject to a resolution of staff and agency comments.

2.     Continuation to address issues raised during the public hearing.
