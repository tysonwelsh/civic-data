NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Council Meeting—September 10th @ 7pm.

              September is National Preparedness Month. Residents can visit the City’s website to
              learn the steps to prepare for natural disasters and emergency situations.

              September 5th – ‘Paint the Parkway’ will take place at the Tracy Aviary’s Nature
              Center at Pia Okwai from 4-8 p.m.

              September 19th - ‘Celebrate South Salt Lake’ will take place at the Central Park
              Community Center from 5-9 p.m.

              September 26th - ‘Paddle with the Mayor’ will take place on the Jordan River.

              October 4th – ‘Craftoberfest’ will take place between on Oakland Avenue between
              Main and West Temple from 12-5 p.m.

              All City offices will be closed on Monday, September 4th in observance of Labor Day

              More information about City events and highlights can be found at sslc.gov

       2. PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          South Salt Lake resident, Hazel Stout, asked some questions regarding the upcoming
          City events.

              South Salt Lake resident, James Brown, shared some comments regarding a recent
              event at Tracy Aviary that hosted over 300 people.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood shared some highlights about City employees who displayed ‘Kindness
          in Action’ as a part of the City’s initiative to partner with One Kind Act A Day
          foundation.
