OPENING:
     Mayor Pike called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was offered by Reverend Brian Mallette with the Springs Church and the
     Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag was led by Planning Commission Member Vardell
     Curtis.

      Washington City Mayor Kenny Nielson thanked the Mayor and Council for their
      partnership. He then invited the Mayor, Council, City Manager and Department
      Heads April 14th to the Community Center for a thank you.

UPDATE FROM THE HOUSING ACTION COALITION:
     Economic Development and Housing Director Shirlayne, PowerPoint presented a
     PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: “HAC”ing the Status Quo;
     What is HAC?; Who is HAC?; What is “Attainable Housing”?; What is the link between
     Housing and Economic Vitality?; Future growth; Household AMI; How AMI translates
     to affordability; Housing availability gap; Washington County rentals; Assessed value
     of housing stock; Collaborate.Educate.CELEBRATE; HAC in 2019 and HAC in 2020;
     and Parting thoughts.

      A discussion took place regarding second homes, attainable housing, short term
      rentals, and HB273.

DISCUSSION AND UPDATE REGARDING DESERT COLOR:
     Mitch Dandy with Desert Color provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the
     following topics: Mission and Vision; Agenda; Project overview; Residential;
     Commercia district; The Shores Resort; Active adult; and Sustainable. The time
     frame is estimated at approximately 20 years to complete the full project; phase 1 is
     expected to be complete in five years or less.

DISCUSSION AND UPDATE REGARDING TECH RIDGE:
     Isaac Barlow with Tech Ridge provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the
     following topics: Why create a Tech Park in St. George?; Economic Development;
     How do we create a tech center?; First illustration presented for Tech Ridge; Updated
     plan for Tech Ridge; Project report; Tech Ridge works like a start up; Trail
     development; Black Ridge Cove; Elevations; Printer Logic building; Future building;
     Skyview building; Tech Ridge Skyview and Pavilion floor plans; and Renderings.

      Clint with Zonos introduced himself and spoke about the differences between tech
      companies and traditional companies. He explained what his company does.

      Mr. Barlow continued with his PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics:
      Pavilion; Slope of pavilion; Amphitheater; Hotel and apartments; View Park; Zipline;
      Adventure park; Viewing platform; Stadium Trail; Stadium Monument; Mid station
      platform; Return terminal; and Asks.

      Mayor Pike called for a short recess. The Planning Commission Members …

DISCUSSION REGARDING DOWNTOWN IRRIGATION:
     Water Services Director Scott Taylor presented a PowerPoint presentation covering
     the following topics: Open Ditch Irrigation Users; Cottonwood Irrigation System –
     500 E to 700 E; East City Springs Irrigation System – Main Street to 200 E; Open
     Ditch Irrigation Users; Inefficiency; Costly; Proposed action; and Annual water use –
     5 random irrigation users vs. neighbors.

      The consensus of the Council is to move forward with Mr. Taylor’s proposal.

      The Council moved to the Administrative Conference Room for the remainder of the
      meeting.

      Mayor Pike provided an update on the Legislative Session.

UPDATE REGARDING THE CITY CAMPUS REMODEL:
     Support Services Director Marc Mortensen provided an update on the City campus
     remodel.

      Chris and Jim Childs with JRCA showed updated renderings and floor plans for the
      City campus remodel.

      The meeting adjourned following the City campus remodel update.
