                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 23-09

      AN ORDINANCE DISSOLVING THE “REDWOOD ROAD SOUTH NEIGHBORHOOD
                       REDEVELOPMENT PROJECT AREA”

            WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of Title 17C of the Utah Code Annotated (“UCA”) by
   resolution, the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency (the “Agency”) adopted the Redwood Road
   South Neighborhood Redevelopment Plan (“Project Area Plan”) on September 2, 1997, thereby
   creating the Redwood Road South Neighborhood Redevelopment Project Area (the “Project Area”);
   and

          WHEREAS, the Agency has adopted a resolution dissolving the Project Area; and

            WHEREAS, UCA § 17C-1-702(1)(b) mandates that the Project Area remains in existence
   until the community legislative body adopts an ordinance dissolving the Project Area; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council, pursuant to UCA § 17C-1-702, desires to dissolve the Project Area.

   NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as follows:

          1.    Riverton City hereby dissolves the Redwood Road South Neighborhood
                Redevelopment Agency Project Area, for which a map and legal description are
                attached as Exhibit A.

          2.    Riverton City staff and legal counsel are hereby authorized and directed to take
                all such actions as necessary to effectuate the purposes and aims of this
                resolution.

          3.    This ordinance shall take effect upon its adoption.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of April 2023
