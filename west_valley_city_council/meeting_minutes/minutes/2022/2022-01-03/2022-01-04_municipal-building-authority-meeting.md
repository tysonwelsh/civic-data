     APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING HELD JUNE 1, 2021
     The Board considered Minutes of the Regular Meeting held June 1, 2021. There were no
     changes, corrections or deletions.

     A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

    ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2022
    Chairman Fitisemanu stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2022 had
    been scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been
    used in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board
    and not mandatory.

    Chairman Fitisemanu called for a motion.

    Mr. Nordfelt moved to elect Don Christensen as Chair of the Municipal Building Authority
    for 2021 and the new Councilmember for District 3 (who will be appointed January 18,
    2022) as Vice Chair of the Municipal Building Authority for 2022. Mr. Huynh seconded
    the motion.

    A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

    MOTION TO ADJOURN
    Mr. Huynh moved to adjourn at 7:03 PM.


    THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE MUNICIPAL BUILDING
    AUTHORITY OF WEST VALLEY CITY, THE ANNUAL MEETING OF TUESDAY,
    JANUARY 4, 2022, WAS ADJOURNED AT 7:03 PM BY CHAIRMAN FITISEMANU.
