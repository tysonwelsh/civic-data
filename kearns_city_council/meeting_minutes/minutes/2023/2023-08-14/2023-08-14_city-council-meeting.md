Planning/Engineering

              Jeffrey Miller, Planner, Municipal Services District, reviewed the request for
approval. This item was previously reviewed and approved by the Planning Commission on July
10th. He explained there are a lot of recording errors with the Camp Kearns area that will
eventually need to be corrected and those corrections will require Council approval.

               Kent Withers stated he was not the landowner; he worked for the surveying firm
that is doing the subdivision plat. The proposal to correct things was to vacate a portion on
Liberator Drive and have a dedication on Thunderbolt Drive.

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated this would
essentially be a true-up to make the physical boundary match what was recorded. There are
multiple recording errors on the plat that were made by the County 50 years ago. This will not be
the last time the Council has this type of discussion. He advised that before approving the
changes, the Council would need to hold a public hearing.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to open
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              There were no comments in favor of or opposition to the approval.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to close
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                Council Member Peterson, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to approve
the ordinance as written subject to correcting the misnumbered paragraphs and inserting the
standard summary language, and to direct planning staff to record the ordinance with the
recorder’s office. The motion passed unanimously. (The following day, it was determined through
an email conversation that the ordinance did not need to be recorded.)

       AN ORDINANCE VACATING PUBLIC INTEREST IN A PORTION OF PLATTED
       STREETS WITHIN THE CAMP KEARNS SUBDIVISION PLAT LOCATED
       WITHIN KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP.

The legislative body of Kearns Metro Township, State of Utah, ordains as follows:

Section I:

(1) A portion of the platted streets, which are more fully described in Exhibit A and shown on
Exhibit B attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, are hereby vacated.

(2) The purpose of the vacation is to allow the applicant to record a plat, which corrects known
surveying errors within the Camp Kearns area.

(3) This ordinance is based upon a determination by the Kearns Metro Township Legislative Body
following a public hearing on August 14, 2023 that due and proper notice of the hearing to vacate
the platted street segments were duly given according to law; that no objection was made to the
proposed vacation; that good cause exists for the vacation; and that neither the public interest nor
any person will be materially injured by the vacation; that fair and adequate consideration shall
be provided; and that Kearns has no present or foreseeable future public need for the portion of
the streets being vacated, or for any other public purpose.

(4) Pursuant to Section 14.48.030 of the Kearns Township Code of Ordinances, the vacation of
these streets are adequate compensation for Kearns’s interest.

(5) All right, title, and interest in and to the specified portions of the street being vacated on
Liberator Drive, more fully described in Exhibit A, are to revert or otherwise be conveyed, by
operation of state law & Kearns’ ordinances, to the abutting property owners.

(6) This ordinance shall have no force or effect to impair any other existing easements or rights-
of-way for public utilities, public franchises, irrigation, storm drainage, or other such easements
as presently exist under, over, or upon the vacated portions of said streets, or as are or may be
shown on the official plats and records of the County.

(7) Planning Staff is hereby directed to take any actions needed to record in the Salt Lake County
Recorder’s Office the official plat reflecting the approved road vacation.

SECTION II: This ordinance is effective upon posting as required by law.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Kearns Council has approved, passed and adopted this ordinance
this 14th day of August, 2023.

Legal Updates/Information

                 Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stated the draft
legislation that would automatically convert the metro townships into cities/towns was sent to the
Office of Legislative Research. The drafting attorney assigned to the bill was out of the office for
the entire month of July and was not able to get to it before the August 9th interim committee
hearing. He hopes to have the bill back before the September 10th hearing.

             Mayor Kelly Bush stated she met with the attorneys for Holladay, Millcreek,
Midvale, and Magna. She felt it was a productive meeting and there were discussions about
moving forward with the UPD model. She was adamant that there not be a replication of County
services.
