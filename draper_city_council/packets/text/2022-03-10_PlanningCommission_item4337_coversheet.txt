Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: March 10, 2022
Re: Edson – Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 1.83 acres located on the east side of Pioneer Rd., at approximately 12258 South Grizzly Hollow Cv. The property is currently zoned RA2 . The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for the modification of easements and no-built zones . The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended .
2.   No significant harm will result.
3.   The proposed modification will result in a more functional and improved plan.
4.   The builder agrees to comply with any conditions or requirements imposed by the Planning Commission to mitigate any adverse effects which may result from the proposed modification.
5.  The City’s geo-technical and geo-hazards consultants have found that CMT Engineering Laboratories has adequately addressed slope stability and geologic concerns for the proposed project.
For denial:
1.   There is not good cause for the vacation or amendment; and a public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended .
2.   Significant harm will result.
3.   The proposed modification will not result in a more functional and improved plan.
4.   The builder has not agreed to comply with conditions or requirements imposed by the Planning Commission to mitigate any adverse effects which may result from the proposed modification.
5.   The City’s geo-technical and geo-hazards consultants have not found that CMT Engineering Laboratories have adequately addressed slope stability and geologic concerns for the proposed project. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 3/4/2022 Cover Memo
