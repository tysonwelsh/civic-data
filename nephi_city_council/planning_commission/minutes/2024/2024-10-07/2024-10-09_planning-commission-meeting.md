2. Considering a preliminary/final plat for the Rollin’ Home Subdivision parcel #XA00-
   2588-12, located at approximately 2888 S Four Mile RD (575 W). This is a phased
   subdivision with one lot being created containing an existing RV Park business, and two
   remainder parcels that will be deed restricted prohibiting development until future
   subdivision occurs.
                 a. Staff Presentation
                  Mrs. Shauna Talbot gave the staff presentation. Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented
              that the developer and landowner have entered into a development agreement to
              allow them to split the property into 3 parcels, that are deed restricted, until the
              full subdivision process has been completed for each future respective
              phase. Phase 1 must happen within an agreed upon time frame. This has allowed
              the new landowner (Chad Ledy) to purchase the property, and work towards
              finalizing the subdivision process. The agreement includes the developer paying
              either a fee in lieu or supplying a non-expiring bond to cover any missing
              infrastructure, particularly along Four-mile Road.
              The remainder parcels, and the future Lot 1 parcel have been deed restricted in
              accordance with USC § 10-9a-103(68)(c)(v) to state that the division:
                a) is in anticipation of future land use approvals on the parcel or parcels;
                b) does not confer any land use approvals; and
                c) has not been approved by the land use authority.

   6. Public Hearing: Considering an ordinance updating Title 11 Land Use, Subdivision
      and Development Regulations (Currently Subdivisions), and Title 10 Zoning
      (Currently Land Use): updating time frame requirements for city to meet regarding
      approvals per state code; creating a land use authority; updating standards and
      specifications; consolidating development code; and other modifications to both
      titles.

              a. Open Public Hearing
                 TIME: 7:36 p.m.

              b. Staff Presentation
                 Mrs. Shauna Talbot and Mr. Seth Atkinson gave the presentation. Mrs.
                 Shauna Talbot presented that the State of Utah has passed LAMLA that cities
                 do certain things to meet the building requirements, especially when it
                 pertains to single family homes and townhome structures. Assisting builders
                 with what costs will be for permits up front, responding to permits in a timely
                 manner, ensure that the codes are available and easily accessible. In addition,
                 the State of Utah is requiring that an administrative land use authority be put
                 in place. Mr. Seth Atkinson also added that Legislative Body of a city cannot

                  be on that land authority board, for the purpose of protecting the community
                  of opposing items when the general plan and requirements are met and
                  therefore there is no reason to oppose the item. Which in the past has confused
                  the public, because they may not want an item but it has already met the
                  requirements, codes, and been through the process and therefore there is no
                  reason to prevent that subdivision, project, home to be denied because where
                  the change should have happened was during the Legislative side of things.
