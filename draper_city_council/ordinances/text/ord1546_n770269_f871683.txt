City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On July 19, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1546, amending Title 14
of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding speed limits and restrictions on the use
of roads. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 20th day of July 2022. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
