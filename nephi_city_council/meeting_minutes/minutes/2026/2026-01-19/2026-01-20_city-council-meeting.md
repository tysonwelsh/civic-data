RESOLUTION 01-20-2026 – PARAMETERS RESOLUTION
      Seth Atkinson introduced the resolution. The City Council has considered options for funding two
      projects in the city. One of the projects is the purchase of available land in the city for parks and
      recreation purposes. The second project is the golf course expansion. The city has looked at the
      option for a sales tax bond that would be paid for with future golf revenues. The purpose of a
      parameters resolution is to set an upper limit to a bond and to allow for any current expenses
      associated with either project to be reimbursed with the upcoming bond.
      The parameters resolution was created by the city’s bond counsel and has been reviewed by
      staff and the city’s financial advisor (Marcus Keller). The bond amount listed does not require the
      city to borrow that much, it just sets an upper limit. There are still a couple of months before a
      bond will be issued and the Council will decide that later.
      Councilor Tate Douglas moved to approve Resolution 01-20-2026 – Parameters Resolution.
      Councilor JD Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed with the following vote:

Work Session 7:20 pm
   1. JustServe City
           a. Rhett and Ann Peterson, members of Mt. Nebo Communication Council, introduced the
               council to the JustServe initiative and how Nephi can become a JustServe City.
           b. The initiative aims to connect volunteers with local service opportunities, enhancing
               community service efforts.
           c. It is free to individuals of all ages, persuasions, societies, and faiths. It is a program fully
               funded by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints as a humanitarian offering.
           d. JustServe can be accessed via the web or mobile app.
           e. The Petersons outlined requirements to become a JustServe City.
                     i. Create a Volunteerism proclamation.
                    ii. Email the signed proclamation to Awards@JustServe.org
                   iii. Work to meet criteria outlined for a Global JustServe City Award
                             1. Be an active JustServe City.
                             2. List service project opportunities of JustServe.org.
                             3. Encourage unity and city values by supporting diverse groups in service.
                             4. Formally recognize individuals for serving in the community.
           f. The council agreed to proceed with the initiative and plans to present a proclamation at
               the next meeting on February 3.
