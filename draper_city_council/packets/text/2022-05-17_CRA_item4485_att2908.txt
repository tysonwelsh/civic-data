3.0   Action Item: Resolution #CRA 22-01, approval of the Fiscal Year 2022-2023
      Tentative Budget and setting the public hearing date of May 17, 2022, to
      receive comment on the Tentative Budget.

3.1   Finance Director Bob Wylie presented the proposed budget for the fiscal year
      2022. He noted that the budget was balanced, and there was an overall
      budget of $9.8 million for the agency. He stated there were four active project
      areas, and listed their respective budgets. He said that Sandhill’s budget was
      $1.7 million, and the Crescent project area had a budget of $2.2 million. He
      said that FrontRunner was their largest project, with a budget of $4.6 million,
      and their most recent project, South Mountain CRA, had a budget of $1.2
      million.

3.2   Boardmember Tasha Lowery moved to approve Resolution #CRA 22-01
      accepting the Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Tentative Budget and setting the public
      hearing date for May 17, 2022. Boardmember Marsha Vawdrey seconded the
      motion.

3.3   A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
