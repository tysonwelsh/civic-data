1. Preliminary Subdivision Approval, for Lincoln Square, 184 20th Street. (Attachment 1)

     Mr. Chaz Schlang stated this item had been tabled by the Commission at the previous meeting at which time
     the existing owner had requested a joint access easement be eliminated. This has now been accomplished. He
     stated the request is to split the existing property into two lots, while allowing Walker Cellular to remain on their
     existing property and creating a separate lot to allow for new development. The proposal would be to create a
     multi-family project which would be in compliance with the Ogden Bend Master Plan.

     Mr. Brierley stated this request is to create two lots from what is a parcel of .99 square feet,. A commercial
     building is situated on what will become Lot 1, and will continue to operate as a legal nonconforming use. The
     second lot is intended to be rezoned to MU and accommodate a townhome project to be consistent with the
     Ogden Bend Master Plan. Access will be from 20th Street, and the previous joint-access easement has been
     eliminated. The owner of Lot 2 will be required to work with City engineering in order to obtain access until the
     existing moratorium for access onto 20th Street expires in August 2023. Staff recommendation is for approval,
     subject to the following conditions;
      1. The applicant must post a financial guarantee for all right-of-way improvements and utility work.
      2. Dedication of 10-foot wide municipal utility easements along the exterior of the property.
      3. Submission of stamped and signed drawings.
      4. Replacement of damaged sidewalk and curb/gutter along the frontage.
      5. City Engineer approval if 20th Street is cut into prior to August 15, 2023, and if approved, asphalt
         patching of the full width of the road 50 feet in each direction.
      6. Right-of-way dedication along 20th Street and Lincoln Avenue.
      7. Relocation of the proposed utility trench to avoid a lamp post.
      8. Making technical corrections to the plat dedication, legal description, and signature blocks.
     Commission asked if the previous concerns of the existing property owner had been satisfied. Mr.
     Brierley noted the only opposition voiced at the previous meeting was the access easement which
     has now been removed. Commissioner Schade asked if the turn lane on Lincoln Avenue would be
     affected. Mr. Brierley responded as it is not adjacent to the proposed project it is not in the scope of
     this project.

     Mr. Josh Walker, existing property owner, stated purchase deadlines had not been met by the
     potential buyer and that he no longer desires to sell a portion of the property to be subdivided.
     Attorney James Tanner stated if the existing owner is not willing to sell the property, its subdivision is moot and
     no action is required of the Planning Commission.

2.   Consideration of CIP Plan Amendment, to AR014 for Airport Terminal.

     Mr. Frank ____ stated while the existing CIP Plan had slated $2 million, the recent infrastructure bill has
     allocated an additional $5 million, and now the request is to increase the scope of the project, adding 14,000
     square feet of floor space. This will accommodate four TSA lines, two banks of restrooms and a new ticket
     office. In addition, fire sprinklers will be added and an upper level will accommodate business offices. The
     expansion will allow larger planes to use the airport. He reviewed the conditions of receiving and using the
     funding, stating it is anticipated to be a 3-4 year project.

     Mr. Montgomery stated this is a request to amend the Capital Improvement Plan by increasing funding from
     $525,000 to $1 million per year for 5 years, adjusting the scope of work as described. Consideration by the
     Planning Commission is to determine the project as proposed is consistent with the General Plan and the
     Airport Master Plan. The proposed addition will allow the airport to accommodate more flights and improve
     passenger service as directed in both plans. Staff recommends approval of the amendment as requested.

     Commissioner Safsten asked if the footprint of the existing terminal building will be altered. Mr. Montgomery
     stated the current plan accommodates expansion for fire fighting equipment while the new proposal will allow
     full-time fire trucks on site. While the long range Airport Plan would relocate the terminal to the west, it also
     acknowledges a short-term expansion of the existing terminal to increase passenger traffic.

     MOTION:      A motion was made by Commissioner Sandau to recommend approval of the amendment to the
                  Capital Improvement Plan to accommodate the increased funding and scope of work as proposed
                  based on the findings it is consistent with the General Plan and the Airport Master Plan. Motion
                  was seconded by Commissioner Safsten and passed unanimously with Commissioners Blaisdell,
                  Safsten, Sandau, Schade and Graf voting aye.
