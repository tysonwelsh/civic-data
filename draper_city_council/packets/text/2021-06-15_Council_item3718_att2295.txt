                                  RESOLUTION NO. 21-24

       A RESOLUTION APPOINTING GARRETT VANAMEN AS A REGULAR MEMBER OF
       THE DRAPER CITY SPECIAL EVENT ARENA COMMITTEE

     WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies with the city as
deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

       WHEREAS, Garrett VanAmen has expressed a willingness to serve as a member of
the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor recommended Garrett VanAmen to serve as a member of the
Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s appointment
of Garrett VanAmen to serve as a member of the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee.

      Section 1. Appointment. Garrett VanAmen is hereby appointed to serve as a
member of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee according to the laws,
ordinances, and regulations governing the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee and
its members for the term specified herein.

       Regular Member:
       Garrett VanAmen                            June 15, 2021 through June 30, 2025

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be
severable.

       Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective upon the effective
date of Ordinance #1493.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THIS THE 15TH DAY OF JUNE 2021.
