Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Marc West, Unified Fire Authority (UFA), stated UFA is finishing the budget
process and a public hearing will be held on June 19, 2018. Wild fire season is anticipated to be
average. UFA is working on getting four crew members at each fire station. The safety message
this month is the danger of leaving pets and kids in the car during the warmer months. There have
been a lot of drownings this year. He asked everyone to be aware of any body of water, small or
large.

                                −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)

                Officer Paula Stinson stated burglaries increase when the weather is nicer. He
recommended keeping all valuables put away and out of sight. The speed trailer was setup on
Tulip Drive to educate drivers of their speed.

                The Council reviewed the following resolution developing the Moderate Income
Housing Plan:

       A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING AND AUTHORIZING ALISON WEYHER TO
       DEVELOP THE MODERATE INCOME HOUSING PLAN FOR THE WHITE CITY
       METRO TOWNSHIP IN ACCORDANCE WITH SECOND SUBSTITUTE HB 259
       (2018)

                                          RECITALS

   1. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   3. During the 2018 Legislative session, the legislature passed Second Substitute HB 259 –

Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) seeking modification of the Master Interlocal
Agreement:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-06-03                                                         Date: June 7, 2018

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE OFFERING OF A LETTER TO THE
       GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT (MSD) SEEKING A
       MODIFICATION OF THE MASTER INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
       MSD, THE METRO TOWNSHIPS, AND SALT LAKE COUNTY

                                            RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.




                                                  4
DATE           THURSDAY                                JUNE                             7, 2018

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
      White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District pursuant
      to UCA §§ 17B-2a-1101 et al.

   D. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a county pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Title 17 Counties,

   E. White City receives services from Salt Lake County, through the MSD, pursuant to a
      Master Interlocal Agreement (the “Agreement”), which is subject to amendment to insure
      the services being provided are of a satisfactory quality and consistent with the needs of

      the MSD will greatly benefit White City and the MSD and improves effectiveness,
      efficiency, overall quality, and better control costs of services.

the offering of a letter to the MSD seeking a modification of the Master Interlocal Agreement
between the MSD, the Metro Townships, and Salt Lake County, and

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the letter be composed by Council Staff to present to the
MSD at its Planning Meeting to be held on Friday, June 8, 2018 at 9:00am as a possible agenda
item for their upcoming meeting on June 13, 2018.

RESOLUTION NO. 18-06-04                                                     DATE: June 7, 2018

       A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING THE BID AND AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO
       SIGN A CONTRACT BETWEEN THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP AND
       MUNICIPAL CODE CORPORATION FOR THE CREATION, OPERATION AND
       MAINTENANCE OF A WEBSITE FOR THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of

Compensation

              The Council reviewed the following resolution establishing compensation amounts
for Council Members in accordance with the FY 2018 budget:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-06-06                                                    DATE: June 7, 2018

       A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE COMPENSATION
       AMOUNTS TO COUNCILMEMBERS PURSUANT TO THE FY2018 WHITE CITY
       METRO TOWNSHIP ADOPTED BUDGET

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body of
      White City pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a local district that
      provides certain municipal-type services to White City pursuant to UCA §§17B-2a-1101 et
      seq.

   D. During the creation of the MSD’s and White City’s FY2018 Budgets, it was determined
      funding should be made available to compensate the Council for their service.

   E. As a result of the MSD’s and White City’s budget creation, it was determined a budgeted
      amount up to $66,000 would be set to fund the compensation of councilmembers.

   F. On November 9, 2017 the Council accepted the FY2018 tentative budget and set the date
      for the public hearing for consideration and possible final adoption of the budget for
      December 14, 2017 at 7:00pm at the Board Room of the White City Water Improvement
      District Office located at 999 E Galena Dr White City, Utah.

   G. On December 14, 2017 the Council adopted the FY2018 White City Metro Township
      Budget.

                                              8
DATE           THURSDAY                                  JUNE                             7, 2018


   H. Based on the monthly amount of time councilmembers attend council meetings, boards of
      trustees meetings with the local districts serving White City, staff meetings, and most
      importantly resident/constituent meetings to fulfill their service commitments, some
      amount of compensation to offset time and income lost due to the public service is justified.

   I.   Council compensation opens the doors of opportunity for others who may consider a
        candidacy who cannot afford the economic costs of performing such service
        uncompensated.

                                         RESOLUTION

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, pursuant to the FY 2018 White City Metro
Township Adopted Budget, the White City Metro Township Council hereby establishes the
following compensation amounts:

   a. The Council approves the amount of $450.00 per month for the Chair/Mayor of the
      Council’s compensation for FY2018.

   b. The Council approves the amount of $450.00 per month, per member, for the rest of the
      Councilmember’s compensation for FY2018.

        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the effective date for the commencement of compensation
shall be January 1, 2018.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 7th day of June, 2018.
