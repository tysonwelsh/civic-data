                                    RESOLUTION NO. 20-46


       A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND ADOPTING A LITIGATION
       SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND THE
       DEFENDANT IN A CIVIL ACTION FILED BY RIVERTON CITY AND
       HERRIMAN CITY AGAINST THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT IN
       THE THIRD JUDICIAL DISTRICT COURT, STATE OF UTAH, CASE NO.
       190904012.


        WHEREAS, in the year 2009, Riverton City entered into an interlocal agreement with
the City of Holliday, the City of Bluffdale, the City of Herriman, and Salt Lake County to create
the Unified Police Department of Greater Salt Lake (“UPD”); and
        WHEREAS, paragraph 20 of the Interlocal Agreement provides that Riverton City, as a
member of the UPD, may withdraw from the UPD upon tendering one full year advanced written
notice to the UPD and its members; and

        WHEREAS, paragraph 20 of the Interlocal Agreement also granted members a vested
interest in a member’s proportionate share of the UPD’s assets and liabilities after an entity held
the status of a member of the UPD for 5 years; and

       WHEREAS, the Interlocal Agreement provides that within one year after the effective
operational date (which date was July 1, 2010) of the UPD’s creation, the UPD Board of
Directors shall establish a formula for the allocation of assets and liabilities that shall be applied
upon the withdrawal of any fully vested member from the UPD, but it failed to do so; and

         WHEREAS, on July 19, 2018, the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No. 18-63,
to commence the withdrawal of Riverton City from its membership in the UPD, because it
discovered that an allocation formula was under consideration by the UPD to mete out assets and
liabilities of a withdrawing member, which would cause material harm to Riverton City’s
investment in the UPD; and

        WHEREAS, after tendering a notice of withdrawal in July of 2018, the parties could not
agree to an amount owed to Riverton City to compensate it for the value of its investment in the
UPD; and
       WHERAS, on May 17, 2019, Riverton City filed suit in the Third Judicial District, State
of Utah (Case No. 190904012), challenging the UPD’s refusal to tender appropriate payment to
Riverton City Riverton as required by the interlocal agreement; and
        WHEREAS, after lengthy negotiations and mediation with the UPD, legal counsel for
the parties have drafted a proposed settlement package as described in the enclosed Settlement
Agreement and Release with the UPD; and
        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 63G-7-602, the Riverton City Council is
empowered to “compromise and settle any action” against the City, after conferring with the
City’s legal counsel; and
        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, has thoroughly considered the Settlement
Agreement and Release in light of the continued expense of ongoing litigation against an entity
with a questionable future;


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:
       1. A Settlement Agreement and Release by and between Riverton City and the Unified
Police Department of Greater Salt Lake attached hereto as Exhibit “A” is hereby approved. The
Mayor is hereby authorized to execute the Settlement Agreement.
       2. Riverton City authorizes the City Manager, through counsel, to take all steps
necessary to carry out the directives contained in this resolution.
       3. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.
