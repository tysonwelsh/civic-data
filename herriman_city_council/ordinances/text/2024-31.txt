ORDINANCE NO. 2024-31

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
TO AMEND HERRIMAN CITY CODE § 11-4-3 (REVIEW AUTHORITY).

WHEREAS, Almost two years ago, the City enacted Title 11 — Residential
Treatment Facilities. Title 11 gives a process and parameters for disabled persons seeking
a reasonable accommodation from some provision in City Code.

WHEREAS, When a request is made, the City contracts with a neutral third-party
hearing officer to determine whether the requested accommodation should be legally
granted.

WHEREAS, The subject text amendment adds a provision that will allow the City
Manager to act as the hearing officer.

WHEREAS, The City Council finds that passing the proposed text amendment is
in the best interest of public health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. The City Council hereby adopts the text
amendments shown in Exhibit A.

SECTION 2. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon passage.

APPROVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH, ON
THIS 13 DAY OF November, 2024.
