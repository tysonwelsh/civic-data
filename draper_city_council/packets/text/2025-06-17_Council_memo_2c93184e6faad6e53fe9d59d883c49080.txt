MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2025-06-17
Re: Public Hearing: Approval of Ordinance #1655 and #1656


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment, Land Use Map
Amendment, and Development Agreement for approximately 20 acres located on the
south side of Kimballs Ln (11800 S), at approximately 600 E Kimballs Ln. The property is
currently zoned RA1 and RA2. The applicant is seeking approval to allow the property to
be developed at a future date with multi-family housing in density range of 25-27
dwelling units per acre.


The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their May 22, 2025 meeting and
forwarded a positive recommendation on all three application with a vote of 5-0. The
recommendation for the Development Agreement includes the following:

The Planning Commission requests the City Council do their best and negotiate in good
faith with the applicant to achieve the following:
   1. Negotiate appropriate height restrictions by considering neighboring properties.
   2. Consider allocating more units beyond 30 to workforce housing.
   3. Consider larger landscaping buffers and fencing then required by City Code.
   4. As long as property does not change, we ask that 11900 S remain emergency only
      access. That means if there are no other properties acquired by the applicant on
      11900 S.
   5. Consideration for more parking than the code requires.

The findings are as follows:


For approval:

  1. The zoning map amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the
     city's general plan.
  2. The zoning map amendment is consistent with the standards of any applicable
     overlay zone.
  3. Adequate facilities and services will be available to serve the subject property,
     including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire
     protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater
     and refuse collection.
  4. The zoning map amendment will not materially affect the existing Station Area
     Planning efforts underway with respect to the Utah State Code requirements of 10-
     9a-403.1.
  5. The land use map amendment is appropriate and complies with the requirements
     of DCMC Section 9-2-020(F) and Utah State Code 10-9a-404.
  6. The development agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D) and
     provides for a density range on the property while still allowing the City and
     applicant to work together to create a site plan that best meets the interests of the
     community and applicant.


For denial:

  1. The proposed zoning map amendment is not consistent with goals, objectives and
     policies of the city's general plan, particularly as the Kimballs Station Area Plan is not
     complete and still going through the master planning process outlined in Utah State
     Code 10-9a-403.1.
  2. The zoning map amendment is not harmonious with the overall character of
     existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
  3. The proposed zoning map amendment will adversely affect adjacent property.
  4. The land use map amendment does not comply with DCMC Section 9-2-020(F) and
     Utah State Code 10-9a-4.
  5. Approval of the development agreement will materially affect the existing Station
     Area Planning efforts underway. The master planning process as outlined in Utah
     State Code 10-9a-4.403.1 provides for additional public and stakeholder
     involvement and is the best way to determine appropriate densities for the
     property.
