   •   Funds for the White City Community Council
   •   $8,000 for administration for the White City Community Council
   •   $20,000 for Southeast Township Days

               Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Cutler, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

Resolution – 2020 White City Metro Township Tentative Budget

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting the 2020 White City Metro
Township tentative budget:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-12-01                                                 Date: December 5, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING THE 2020 WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP TENTATIVE BUDGET

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
      10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body
      for White City pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. The Council approved the 2020 White City Metro Township Tentative Budget on
      November 14, 2019 and set the public hearing for the 2020 Tentative Budget for
      December 5, 2020 7:00 PM at the White City Water Improvement District Boardroom
      located at 999 East Galena Drive, White City, UT 84094.

   D. Pursuant to fiscal policy for funding the 2020 White City Budget, the Greater Salt Lake
      Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) adopted the 2020 Budget for the MSD on October
      23, 2019, releasing the funding for the 2020 White City Budget.

   E. The noticing for the public hearing to consider and copies of the 2020 White City Tentative
      Budget, (Attachment “A” to this resolution) were made available for public consideration
      at least ten (10) days prior to the date of the public hearing for the Budget.

   F. Adoption of the 2020 Budget will allow White City to comply with State Law and the
      Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act for Cities.

                                        RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby adopts the 2020 White City
Tentative Budget, Attachment “A.”

         APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this 5th day of December, 2019.

Resolution – Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with the White City Water Improvement District

            The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the shared use of the
“CodeRED” communications platform:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-12-03                                                Date: December 5, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING AND ADOPTING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT
       BY AND BETWEEN THE WHITE CITY WATER IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT AND
       THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL FOR THE USE OF THE
       “CODERED” EMERGENCY COMMUNICATIONS PLATFORM

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (“White City” or the “Municipality”) is a municipality
      pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The White City Water Improvement District (the “District”) is a Local District Pursuant to
      Utah code §§ 17B-1-101 et seq.

   C. The District has subscribed to use the CodeRED emergency alert system and
      communications platform (the “Platform”) to notify water users in the event of an
      emergency or delivery issue regarding their water service.

   D. The platform would also allow the municipality to communicate with residents regarding
      municipal emergencies and other community issues.

   E. The Municipality and the District can share the use and costs of the platform via an
      interlocal cooperation agreement pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-203 et al.

                                        RESOLUTION

       NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the White City Metro Township
Council:

       APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE REVISED AND RESTATED INTERLOCAL

       GOVERN THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY

                                          RECITALS

   A. The White City Metro Township (the “White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
      Ann. §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Unified Fire Authority (the “UFA”) is an Interlocal Agency created to provide fire
      protection services pursuant to UCA §§ 11-13-203, et al.

   C. The UFA has proposed a Revised and Restated Agreement by Public Agencies to provide
      certain changes to its creation, rules of governance, and to clarify entity voting processes
      on certain budgetary issues of the UFA.
