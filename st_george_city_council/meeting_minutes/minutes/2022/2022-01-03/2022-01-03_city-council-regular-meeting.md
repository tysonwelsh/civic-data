                  ST. GEORGE CITY COUNCIL CORRECTED MINUTES
                               REGULAR MEETING
                           JANUARY 3, 2022, 12:00 P.M.
                            CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS

PRESENT:
     Mayor Michele Randall
     Councilmember Jimmie Hughes
     Councilmember Gregg McArthur
     Councilmember Dannielle Larkin
     Councilmember Michelle Tanner
     Councilmember Natalie Larsen
     City Manager Adam Lenhard
     City Attorney Tani Pack Downing
     City Recorder Christina Fernandez
     Deputy City Recorder Annette Hansen

OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was offered by Tim Martin and the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag was led
     by Police Chief Kyle Whitehead.

SWEARING IN OF ELECTED OFFICIALS:
    Michelle Tanner, Natalie Larsen, and Michele Randall were sworn in by Deputy City
    Recorder Annette Hansen.

      Mayor Randall, Councilmembers Hughes, McArthur, and Larkin took turns welcoming
      the new Councilmembers.

APPOINT CITY OFFICER/RESOLUTION:
     Consider approval of a resolution appointing City Officers.

      Mayor Randall outlined the appointments as follows:

      City Attorney – Tani Pack Downing
      City Recorder – Christina Fernandez
      City Treasurer – Laura Woolsey
      Chief of Police – Kyle Whitehead
      Fire Chief – Robert Stoker
      Director of Leisure Services – Shane Moore
      Director of Public Works – Cameron Cutler
      Director of Water Services – Scott Taylor
      Director of Energy Services – Laurie Mangum
      Director of Administrative Services – Trevor Coombs
      City Manager – Adam Lenhard

ADJOURN:
     MOTION:        A motion was made by Councilmember Hughes to appoint the City
                    officers as outlined.
      SECOND:       The motion was seconded by Councilmember McArthur.

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye

     SECOND:        The motion was seconded by Councilmember Hughes.
     VOTE:          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye

                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
