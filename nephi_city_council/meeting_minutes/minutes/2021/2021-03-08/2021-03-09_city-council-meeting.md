                                             Meeting Minutes
                                          Work Session 03-09-21
                                                 7:00 p.m.
                                        Nephi City Council Chambers

    1.    Electric Department Superintendent Rust Finlinson reviewed with the council the employee
         residency 84648 policy. He requested if the council would consider changing the policy to allow full-
         time employees to be able to live in east Juab County. The council directed staff to draft some
         possible policy changes.

    2.   City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the list of budget priorities.

    3. The council discussed the proposed new Industrial 2 zone that will be the subject of a public hearing
       at the Planning Commission meeting on March 10, 2021.

    4. City Recorder Lisa Brough directed the council to go the Utah State Auditor’s website and complete
       the Introductory Training for Municipal Officials 2021 in order to meet the fraud risk assessment
       requirements put out by the State Auditors office.

    5. The council watched a training video on the Utah Open Public Meetings Act.

    6. Each council member signed an Ethical Behavior Pledge Form.

    7. The council reviewed the financial statements.

    8.   The City Administrator and City Councilors gave various updates.

    9. Councilor Memmott moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
       competence of an individual, litigations, and real property. Councilor Seely seconded the motion.
       The motion passed on a unanimous vote.



Present: Mayor Glade Nielson, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Justin Seely,
Kasey Wright (Absent), Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Kyle Marchant, JD Parady, Rust Finlinson, Travis
Worwood.
