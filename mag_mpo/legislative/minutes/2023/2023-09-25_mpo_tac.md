TIP Modification American Fork 700N Connection – Bob Allen, MAG American Fork 700 N Connection (16:38) In 2016, American Fork was awarded $1,100,000 in MAG Exchange funds to connect 700 N from 100 E to 200 E. The project includes a 3-lane cross-section and a new signal at 100 E. The project is intended to begin construction as soon as possible, and costs have risen considerably since the project was initially selected due to inflation. Additionally, due to grades in the area, a traditional intersection at 200 E is not feasible. American Fork is requesting a scope change to add a roundabout at the 200 E intersection and additional funds to cover the costs of the project. Original Funds $1,100,000 10% Contingency $ 110,000 Additional Funds $1,389,503 New MAG Total $2,599,503 MPO Technical Advisory Committee September 25, 2023 Page 4 Brad Kenison moved to recommend the scope change and addition of $1,389,503 to the American Fork 700 North project to be approved. Drat ideas are due by October 5th. Safe Street for All Update – Charles Allen, Parametrix (21:43) SS4A is a new federal program developed through the newest transportation bill. It is a specific grant directed at improving safety on the streets in America. US DOT SS4A grant program receives $1B annually for 5 years. There are two grant types: 1) planning grants, and 2) implementation grants. MAG was awarded a planning grant in 2022 to complete a safety action plan. Parametrix was hired to do the safety action plan for the MAG area. The primary objective of the grant is to position for future implementation grants and elevate safety in the MPO/RPO project planning and prioritization process. The intent is to develop projects that are data driven. Local government coordination will consist of monthly stakeholder committee meetings and quarterly update to MAG staff. Public outreach will be conducted via pop-up community events, websites, online survey, pedestrian intercept surveys, and in-person and virtual open houses. Next steps and timeline Ongoing Crash analysis and public engagement Early October Stakeholder committee meetings begin Late 2023 Early project identification Spring 2024 Plan adoption June 2024 First package of project concepts complete July 2024 Anticipated Implementation Grant deadline Counties and cities can apply individually or collectively. MAG will be in contact with staff to collect information needed to begin the grant process. Other Business and Adjournment (39:59) Next Meeting scheduled for October 23, 2023.
