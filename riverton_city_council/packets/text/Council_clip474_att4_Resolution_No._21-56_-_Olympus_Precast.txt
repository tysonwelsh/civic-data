                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 21-56

   A RESOLUTION APPROVING A PO CONTRACT TO OLYMPUS PRECAST TO
   INSTALL A PRECAST CONCRETE WALL ALONG A SECTION OF THE ROSE
                 CREEK DRAINAGE IN CHAMONIX PARK

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the Risk Manager and the City Attorney deems this project necessary; and


       WHEREAS, by approving the PO Contract to Olympus Precast, Council will need to
approve $35,680.00 in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The Riverton City Council approves a PO Contract be issued to Olympus Precast to
         install a precast concrete wall along a section of the Rose Creek Drainage in
         Chamonix Park.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 5th day of
October 2021 by the following vote:
