18            Roberts Rules
19
20   Mayor Trent Staggs explained that he had four points he wanted to address in regards to
21   following Roberts Rules during City Council meetings.
22
23       1. He asked Councilmembers not to talk to or direct questions to the audience from the
24          Dias.
25       2. He reiterated that Councilmembers must be recognized by the Chair in order to speak.
26          Each Councilmember has a limit and must not speak more than once on a topic until
27          every other Councilmember has had a chance to speak.
28       3. He explained that when a motion is presented, he will wait until there is a second to the
29          motion. If there is no second to the motion then the motion dies. Councilmembers
30          discussed how much time would be appropriate to wait for a second. The Council
31          agreed that 15 seconds would be appropriate to wait for a second to a motion.
32       4. Mayor Staggs explained that he had spoken to both David Brickey and Ryan Carter
33          about substitute motions. Some councils do not allow substitute motions. He said that
34          he would like to allow substitute motions as long as the substitute motion has a second.
35          The substitute motion would be considered first and then if it fails the Council would
36          revert back to the original motion.
37
38   Councilmember Stewart asked if a failure to vote is a no vote. Mr. Carter explained that a
39   failure to vote would be the same as voting no to a motion. Councilmember Stewart asked what
40   would happen if nobody made a motion on a particular item. Mr. Carter said that the Board
41   Chair could call for the next item on the agenda. The Board Chair can also decide not to allow
42   substitute motions.
43
44   Councilmember McDougal explained that instead of a substitute motion, the Council could just
45   let the motion fail, have a conversation and then do another motion. Mayor Staggs said that a
46   substitute motion may be valuable in some instances.
47
48   Councilmember Stewart said that he supports substitute motions. In response to
49   Councilmember Wells concern about a failure to vote, he said that he has seen councilmembers
50   walk off the Dias if there is a conflict and the chair can excuse them from voting.

 7            Ordinance No. 22-06 – Scooters Rezone, Proposed Rezone of 0.51-acres located at
 8            3479 West 12600 South, from C-N to C-G, Kim Williford, representing Scooters
 9            Coffee, Applicant
10
11   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that this was a public hearing for
12   an application to Rezone 0.51-acres of land located at 3479 West 12600 South. Kim Williford,
13   representing Scooters Coffee, is requesting the rezone to accommodate a potential drive-
14   through coffee shop on the property. The property is currently zoned C-N, Commercial
15   Neighborhood. The applicant had initially requested a rezone to C-PO, Commercial
16   Professional Office. However, during the Planning Commission hearing, the Commission
17   discussed the appropriateness of that zone, and recommended that the Commercial Gateway
18   zone is also consistent with the General Plan and more compatible with the proposed use of the
19   property. The Commission made that formal recommendation to the City Council, and the
20   applicant made the change in their requested zoning, as well.
21
22   Kim Williford, applicant for the rezone, from Scooters Coffee commented that her coffee brand
23   is really awesome. She said she is a big proponent of Riverton and appreciates the support
24   Riverton City gives for mental health.
25
26   Councilmember McCay explained that she has not received any communication from residents
27   and asked if the City had received any comments. Mr. Lethbridge said there were no comments
28   for this public hearing but did receive a couple of clarifying phone call, not objections. Ryan
29   Carter said there were no comments at the planning commission meeting.
30
31   Mayor Staggs called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public Hearing.
