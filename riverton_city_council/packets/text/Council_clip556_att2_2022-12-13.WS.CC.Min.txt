38          Bangerter Highway Update
39
40   Marwan Farah, Utah Department of Transportation Project Manager, presented the Bangerter
41   Highway update. This presentation is included in the packet.
42
43   Councilmember Buroker asked if the acquisitions had begun on 2700 West. Mr. Farah said the
44   residents are aware and have been contacted. Councilmember Buroker asked about closures
45   on 13400 South. Mr. Farah said the moving of the utilities will take place before any closures
46   will happen. As a result of these utilities being moved, there will be lane closures. Residents
47   will see construction from Winter 2023 to the Fall of 2025.
48
49   Councilmember Wells asked about the Rose Creek relocation and if there would be acquisitions
50   of properties. Mr. Farah said they are widening and putting in curb and gutter and this requires
51   some acquisitions. Councilmember Wells asked Mr. Farah to estimate when the actual closure

 1   would occur at 13400 South. Mr. Farah said it would be late 2024 through 2025.
 2
 3   Councilmember McDougal asked if they could maintain access for businesses and schools near
 4   the 13400 South intersection. Mr. Farah said there would be interference but they would
 5   maintain access to these businesses and the school during the construction.
 6
 7   Councilmember McCay asked for a copy of the slides to share with the residents. She would
 8   also like to know how the residents would be detoured around the construction. Mr. Farah said
 9   that north and south bound to continue to flow through the intersection. They are likely to utilize
10   4150 West. However, there is a possibility of roads being closed for 6-9 months.
11
12   Councilmember McCay asked if they would direct traffic traveling east to detour north onto
13   12600 South instead of through the neighborhood to the south of Walmart. These residents are
14   concerned with traffic being a problem especially since there will be construction on 2700 West
15   and Bangerter. Mr. Marah said that before they decide traffic detours, they will consult with
16   local leaders of the City. Mayor Staggs asked them to communicate as far in advance as
17   possible for these detours.
18
19   Councilmember Stewart asked if they have considered punching in at 13200 South and creating
20   temporary signals to maintain the left turn on the northbound movement to the westbound
21   movement. This would give business access and moves it away from the construction zone.
22   Mr. Farah said they have not considered this option but if they could obtain permission from the
23   City and from Suburban Land Development, they might consider it.
24
25   Mayor Staggs asked if the construction on Bangerter includes 9800 South. Mr. Farah said it
26   does include 9800 South.

 3            Community Center Updates
 4
 5   Mayor Staggs explained that the budget for the Community Center auditorium was $369,000
 6   and has cost the City $381,000. The City has secured the TRCC grant for the first-floor
 7   renovation, which will begin in 2023. He and Councilmember Buroker discussed the City
 8   spending a little more to complete the esthetics for the auditorium to make it all come together.
 9
10   Councilmember Buroker consulted an interior designer for some recommendations. She
11   presented the report from the interior designer and this is included in the packet.
12
13   Mayor Staggs asked the Council to weigh in on these suggestions. He has estimated the
14   expense to be about $10,000 to $20,000. The Council agreed to spend money to paint areas
15   to improve the aesthetics.
16
17   Sheril Garn, Director of Events and Operations, said the railing would need to be replaced if the
18   Council wanted it to be a different color. It should not be painted because it will chip. Mayor
19   Staggs directed staff to find out about the cost of replacing this railing and get back to the
20   Council.

14   Councilmember Stewart reported that UFA had a meeting today. Mayor Wickers will be taking

19   Councilmember McCay reported that she attended the Utah League of Cities and Towns
20   meeting. They talked about HB 462 which is the moderate-income housing bill. She said that
21   most cities would be in compliance within the next few weeks, however, there are 7-10 cities
22   that will not comply. She believes they will add a consequence for these cities.
23
24   Councilmember McCay said the governor is planning to provide free fares for 2023. She
25   believes this would only benefit areas that have access to transit. She said they updated the
26   free fares to include Via and UTA on demand. The southwest valley needs to get ridership up
27   to justify transit being budgeted for this area.

35   Mayor Staggs said he has been meeting regularly with the Southwest mayors since 2018. They
36   have worked together to show housing statistics in the county’s southwest part. Bluffdale and
37   Herriman both have about 50% attached housing, Riverton has about 25% attached housing.
38   He believes the southwest cities are doing their fair share with the housing situation in the
39   county.
40
41   Mayor Staggs said the southwest cities have been successful at moving up the time frame for
42   completion of Bangerter interchanges. The southwest cities are now trying to advance the
43   shared priorities of getting funding for Mountain View Corridor and U-111 to become freeway-
44   type roads. They are looking at ways to improve east/west traffic, looking at improving trails that
45   run through all of the communities and looking to secure TRCC funds and any other available
46   funding.
