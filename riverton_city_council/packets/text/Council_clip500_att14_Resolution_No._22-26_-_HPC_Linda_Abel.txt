                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-26

         A RESOLUTION RE-APPOINTING LINDA ABEL TO THE HISTORIC
                       PRESERVATION COMMISSION

        WHEREAS, on June 14, 2016, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 16-11
which provides for the preservation of historic structures and other assets located within Riverton
City; and

       WHEREAS, Administration of Ordinance No. 16-11 requires appointment of a Historic
Preservation Commission; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the following volunteer re-
appointment of Linda Abel; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Linda Abel is a
resident of Riverton City, and is willing and capable of serving on the Historic Preservation
Commission;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the re-appointment of Linda
          Abel to serve on the Historic Preservation Commission.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
April, 2021 by the following vote:
