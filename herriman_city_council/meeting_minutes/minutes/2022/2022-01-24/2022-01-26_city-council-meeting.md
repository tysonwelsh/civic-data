     2.1. Current State of Local Economy with Inflation and Cost of Living Increases - Travis Dunn,
          Human Resources Manager
Human Resources Manager Travis Dunn – ties into the budget, pinpoints, crazy economic market for employee
increases, informational presentation for planning. Offered information about national inflation market. Cost
of living has increased, 4.5 million workers quit their jobs in November 2021. Utah unemployment is at 2.1%
Hard to predict in the next year, economic employment arena – retain good employees. Need institutional
knowledge, hard to find good employees in the market.

Low employment – driven out people to work in the physically demanding positions. Travis – entry level wages
competing.

market adjustments, strategically align ourselves with the market, and provide a recommendation on an annual
increase percentage. Have shared ideas internally, . Steve- noted wage was not the top thing employees look for,
wanted to be competitive, and look at flexible work schedule, quality of life, other enhancements being

ensuring employees impact, diversity, ideas being shared, teddy – entry level, live within city boundaries. Travis
suggested it was 50/50 or 60/40. Successful to draw talent in. Nathan – market does not impact all positions
the same.
