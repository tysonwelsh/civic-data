              SUMMARY:     Paul Randstrom, Brad Reynolds Construction, is requesting a Zoning Map
                           amendment to rezone the properties identified in Exhibit A from Single-Family
                           Residential ( R- 1- 10) to Single- Family Residential ( R- 1- 8) and Single-Family
                           Residential( R- 1- 6).


                           The Taylorsville zoning map applies land use and ' intensity classifications
                           established in the Land Development Code Title 13 to every property in the City.
                           The City Council, at their discretion, may make amendments the zoning map that it
                           considers appropriate and that are in the best interest of the City and its residents in
                           accordance with Section 13. 05. 010: Purpose.


                           The applicant is seeking this zoning map amendment with the intention of
                           subdividing the property into 31 single- family residential lots. A conceptual
                           development plan is included in this report( Exhibit E). Amending the zoning map
                           does not approve the concept development plan; rather, a zoning amendment would
                           establish the legal mechanism for the applicant to pursue a subdivision application
                           based on the underlying zoning regulations. Future development of the property
                           would be subject to review and compliance with adopted zoning, subdivision,
                           engineering, and other applicable standards.


                           The subject property is currently designated as " Low-Density Residential" in the
                           Taylorsville General Plan. The net density of the proposed zoning designation and
                           concept development plan is approximately 4 dwelling units per acre. This is
                           consistent with General Plan for this area of less than 6 dwelling units per acre. The




                                                    1
                               R- 1- 6 and R- 1- 8 zoning classifications allow for the construction of single- family
                               detached homes— also consistent with the long-term policy established for this area.

                               The Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation to the City
                               Council for the zoning map amendment request on June 22, 2021.

     STAFF RECOMMENDATION:     Staff recommends approval of Ordinance No. 21- 12 based on the findings outlined

        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the" Council")         met in regular session on July
7, 2021, to consider, among other things, approving a Zoning Map amendment from Single-Family
Residential ( R- 1- 10) to Single-Family Residential ( R- 1- 8) and Single-Family Residential ( R- 1- 6)

        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 030, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published in the Deseret News on May
14, 2021; and


       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on May 12, 2021; and

        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on June 22, 2021, during which the Planning Commission voted 6- 0 to
recommend that the Council approve the proposed zoning map amendment; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on July 7,
2021; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed Zoning Map amendment is hereby approved for the property described below and
illustrated in Exhibit A.

            Summary:
            Paul Randstrom, Brad Reynolds Construction, is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to
            rezone the properties identified in Exhibit E from Single- Family Residential ( R- 1- 10) to
            Single- Family Residential ( R- 1- 8) and Single- Family Residential ( R- 1- 6).

            The original zoning map amendment application requesting to rezone the property from R-
            1- 10 to RM- 10 and R- 1- 6 was filed with the City on May 7, 2021 and was presented to the
            Planning Commission on May 25, 2021. The Planning Commission forwarded a negative
            recommendation to the City Council based on public comment and concerns related to
            amending the General Plan map. The applicant has amended the application to address
            neighborhood concern and Planning Commission comments and is requesting the revised
            application be re- considered by the Planning Commission, pursuant to the Taylorsville
            Planning Commission Policies and Procedures ( Section 4, page 7):

                     Reconsideration ofApplications: An application which has been denied by the
                    Planning Commission may be reconsidered by the Commission if an applicant
                    presents new evidence which the Chair and Community Development Director
                    determine is sufficient to merit reconsideration of the application. A requestfor
                    reconsideration shall be made in writing andfiled within ten daysfrom the date
                    of the original decision. If a request for reconsideration is granted, the
                    Commission shall reconsider the application in the same manner as an original
                    application was considered."



            The remainder of this report includes an analysis of the revised zoning map amendment
            application.




            The applicant is seeking this Zoning Map amendment with the intention of subdividing the
            property into 17 single- family lots with a minimum lot area 6, 000 square feet( R- 1- 6) and
            14 single- family lots with a minimum lot area 8,000 square feet( R- 1- 8). A conceptual
            development plan is included in this report( Exhibit E) illustrating a concept development
            plan if this zoning map amendment request is approved by the City Council.

            Any action or recommendation regarding this zoning map amendment does not approve the
            concept development plan illustrated in Exhibit E; rather, a zoning change would establish
            the legal mechanism for the applicant to pursue a subdivision application based on the
            underlying zoning. Any future development application( s) would be subject to compliance
            with adopted zoning, subdivision, engineering, and other applicable standards.


            Site Description
            According to the Salt Lake County Assessor data, the subject property is made up of nine ( 9)
            individual parcels with a cumulative area of 371, 871. 72 square feet( 8. 54 acres). The
            parcels are owned by the same individual and currently function as one property. There is
            an existing single- family dwelling and several accessory buildings on the site, as shown in
            Exhibit C. The property has vehicle access via Turnberry Lane and 4800 South. Surrounding
            zoning designations are as follows:

            General Plan Analysis
            The Taylorsville General Plan is intended to guide land use and development decisions that
            address the present and future needs of the City and to accomplish coordinated, efficient, and
            harmonious growth and development within the City.
            The subject property is currently designated as" Low- Density Residential" on the Proposed
            Land Use Map. The following statement describes the intent of the Low- Density Residential
            designation ( pg 3- 4):

                      Low Density Residential ( LDR) districts are residential areas that contain
                     existing or proposed residential developments less than 6 dwelling units per
                     acre( excluding ER districts). Properties that are assigned the LDR classification
                     aregenerally( but not necessarily limited to) neighborhoods consisting ofsingle
                     family dwellings."

            The applicant' s proposal for a single- family residential development is consistent with the
            adopted policies and goals in the City' s General Plan. The net density( excluding the right-of-
            way) of the concept development plan is 4.48 dwelling units per acre; which is consistent
            with the Low-Density Residential threshold of less than 6 dwelling units per acre. The R- 1- 6
            and R- 1- 8 zoning classifications allow for the construction of single- family detached homes
             also consistent with the long-term policy established for this area.

            Zoning Map Analysis
            The City Council may consider a proposed Zoning Map amendment and may adopt or reject
            the amendment as it deems appropriate ( Section 13. 05. 030( B)( 2)). Additionally, the City

            Council may amend the Zoning Map consistent with the purposes set forth in the
            Taylorsville Land Development Code, the General Plan, and the Land Use Development and
            Management Act( Section 13. 05. 010).


            The current zoning designation of the subject property is Single- Family Residential ( R- 1-
            10). This designation allows for the construction of single- family detached dwellings on lots
            at least 10, 000 square feet in size. As depicted in Exhibit F, the proposed amendment would
            rezone a portion of the property to Single- Family Residential ( R- 1- 8) and a portion of the
            property to Single- Family Residential ( R- 1- 6). The applicant has provided a legal
            description delineating the boundaries for each respective zoning district.

            Public Comment

            and posted on the State Website May 12, 2021.

            As of June 17, 2021, two public comments in opposition of the amended application were
            received by staff( see Exhibit H).
