2.    SIGN ORDINANCE, DISCUSSION ABOUT POSSIBLE AMENDMENTS TO THE
      SIGN ORDINANCE IN THE PCC ZONE.

Mr. Lethbridge reported on the history and purpose of PCC Zone. Currently, the City does
not have a table of uses or sign regulations. An applicant is in the process of preparing to
come before the Commission with a master site plan and will need requirements.

Mr. Lethbridge requested that for signage requirements, the RM-6 zone is recommended to
match residential zones and the PCC Zone is recommended to match the regular
commercial zones. Currently, a building is allowed 15% of the primary façade in signs to
determine how to display their signs. Building owners, developers, tenants, etc., have
complained that they are only allowed two secondary signs, which results in the majority of
the tenants not receiving a sign for advertising. Mr. Lethbridge brought the matter up with
