     1. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH UTAH CUTTING HORSE ASSOCIATION TO HOLD A UCHA
        FALL CUTTING COMPETITION AT THE GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER.
            Duncan Olsen (GSEC Director): This contract is one of our biggest annual shows with 10-12 days
            depending on the number of participants. We have traded some sponsorship and discounted some setup and
            tear down days.

            Commissioner Harvey: Cutting horses is the second most lucrative equine competition behind thoroughbred
            racing. Cutting horses are well trained and expensive; all for two minutes and thirty seconds for the horse to
            cut a cow out of the herd.

            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a contract with Utah cutting Horse Association to hold a UCHA Fall
            Cutting Competition at the Golden Spike Event Center; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     2. APPROVE CONSIDERATION AND ACTION ON A REQUEST FOR PRELIMINARY APPROVAL
        OF THE ORCHARDS AT JDC RANCH PHASE 1, AND VILLAS AT JDC RANCH PHASE 1
        LOCATED AT 2850 W 2600 N, PLAIN CITY.
            Steve Burton (Planning): Normally you don’t see preliminary subdivisions applications. The JDC Ranch
            development agreement was written to conform to changes being made by the state legislature. Currently the
            zoning is R1-10 and R3. The Villas at JDC have 108 attached patio homes and the Orchards at JDC have 64
            clustered single family homes both as called out by the development agreement. Includes a two to three acre
            park. The Planning Commission recommended approval on September 19th and staff recommends approval.
            Water and sewer preliminary approval letters are in hand. Final plats will be approved during an
            administrative approval meeting with the Planning director.

            Commissioner Bolos moved to approve consideration and action on a request for preliminary approval of the
            Orchards at JDC Ranch Phase 1, and Villas at JDC Ranch Phase 1 located at 2850 W 2600 N, Plain City;
            Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
