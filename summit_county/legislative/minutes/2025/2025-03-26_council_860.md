Public hearing and adoption of Ordinance No. 991 amending the Snyderville Basin Development Code Sections 10-3-20, Building Permit and Certificate of Occupancy; 10-4-3, Critical Lands; 10-4-22, Height Regulations; 10-8-18, Wireless Communications; and 10-11-1, Definitions.
