WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
SEPTEMBER 9, 2019

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Dean Cox at 11:00 a.m. on September 9, 2019, in the Washington
County Upstairs Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County
Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen,
Internal Auditor Susan Lewis, Public Works Director Ron Whitehead, Deputy Clerk
Cheyenne Bentley, and GRO Promotions representative Cimarron Chacon.

Commissioner Almquist was excused as he was out of town on County Business.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2019-2512, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT FOR TRAIL CONSTRUCTION IN THE
WASHINGTON COUNTY CONFLUENCE PARK

Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan explained that Washington
County was awarded a Utah Division of Parks and Recreation and Federal Highway
Administration Recreational Trail Program Grant for the expansion of non-motorized trails
within Washington County.

The Drop Trail has been identified as a trail that could benefit from this grant and is located
in La Verkin at the Confluence Park. The trail will allow safe access to the bottom of the
hillside from the staircase platform and is approximately 1,400 feet in length. Bids were
received for the project and GRO Promotions was selected as the contractor for the
beginning stages of the project. Due to the steep and narrow terrain of the area all trail
improvements will be completed via manual labor.
