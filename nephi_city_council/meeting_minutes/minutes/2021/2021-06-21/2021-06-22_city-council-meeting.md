                                          Nephi City
                                    Work Session Minutes
                                        June 22, 2021
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                 Nephi City Council Chambers

   1. Juab County Commissioner Clinton Painter addressed the council about having their
      support and for the police department to help with a parade for the Xtreme Racing event.
      He stated that the county had obtained the necessary parade permit from UDOT and
      that safety precautions had been discussed with the sheriff’s office and the police
      department. County Attorney Wright warned that there is a lot of liability without shutting
      down the traffic. Chief Morgan said that they would have to ask for volunteer law
      enforcement to assist with the parade. There would be a cost to the city because the
      extra police would earn compensation time. The council agreed to allow the parade.

   2.    The council discussed with Code Enforcement Officer Andrea Holm the fees that will be
        charged at the gun range effective July 1, 2021.

   3.    The council discussed an education assistance request from Chief of Police Michael
        Morgan. Chief Morgan was informed that the cost of the education would come out of
        his budget. The council decided that the education assistance policy needed further
        discussion and possibly some revisions.

   4. City Administrator Seth Atkinson reported to the council the latest sales tax numbers.
      Sales tax revenue has increased from last years numbers.

   5. Councilor Seely reported on a meeting with the Economic Development Team and the
      city’s legislative representatives. He stated that both state representatives felt that the
      inland port would be a great thing in this area. They also met with Snow College officials
      and the Snow College nursing program staff and talked about educational opportunities.
      There was an interest in partnering with CVMC and Snow College for vocational type
      training opportunities.

   6. Adjournment at 9:07 p.m.
