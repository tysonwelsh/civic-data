                                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                                ORDINANCE NO. 25- 07


   AN ORDINANCE                    AMENDING       SECTION       10. 05. 090 ( 2) OF RIVERTON   CITY CODE
        REGARDING                  TRUCK ROUTES ON SPECIFIED                RIVERTON   CITY STREETS


         WHEREAS, Title 10 of Riverton Municipal Code regulates the operation and parking of
 vehicles within the territorial jurisdiction Riverton City; and

          WHEREAS, Section 10. 05. 090 ( 2) of the Riverton Municipal Code further identifies city
streets, which may be used as routes for commercial vehicles; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that with the further
development of residential property in the southwest areas of Riverton City, there is a need to
revise the list of designated truck routes found in Section 10. 05. 090 ( 2) in order to protect the
safety of residents in these areas;

            NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:


       Section       1.            The Riverton City Code sections 10. 10. 010 ( 2) is hereby amended as
                                   provided in Exhibit A of this ordinance.

       Section 2.                  This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication and posting as provided
                                   by law.
