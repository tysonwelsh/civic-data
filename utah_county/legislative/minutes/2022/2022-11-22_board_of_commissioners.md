-Ben VanNoy, Attorney

(Timestamp: 9:36)

2)

BEN VAN NOY, COUNTY DEPUTY ATTORNEY - STATED THAT STATE LAW
REQUIRES THAT WE ESTABLISH THIS CRIMINAL JUSTICE COUNCIL BEFORE THE
END OF THE YEAR. DISCUSSED SECTIONS ON THE ORDINANCE INCLUDING:

e ESTABLISHMENT PART B THAT DISCUSSES THE REPRESENTATIVES FROM
DIFFERENT ORGANIZATIONS/ENTITIES THROUGHOUT THE COUNTY
WHICH INCLUDES A CHIEF OF POLICE, DISTRICT COURT JUDGE, JUSTICE
COURT JUDGE, AP&P (ADULT PROBATION AND PAROLE) REPRESENTATIVE
AND A CRIME VICTIM ADVOCATE. COMMISSION NEEDS TO DECIDE HOW
THESE POSITIONS ARE APPOINTED.

COMMISSION AND BEN VAN NOY DISCUSS AND DECIDE FOR SELF-SELECT PER
ORGANIZATION EXCEPT FOR THE CRIME VICTIM ADVOCATE. THE CRIME VICTIM
ADVOCATE TO BE DECIDED BY THE VICTIM RIGHTS COMMITTEE WITH A TERM
LENGTH OF SERVICE OF 3 YEARS BUT CAN SERVE MULTIPLE TERMS.

e MULTI-COUNTY COUNCIL - ALLOWED UNDER THE STATUTE.

COMMISSION, SHERIFF MIKE SMITH AND BEN VAN NOY DISCUSS AND DECIDE
THAT COMMISSION CAN INVITE OTHER COUNTIES AND THOSE COUNTIES HAVE
TO FORMALLY JOIN VIA A RESOLUTION.
