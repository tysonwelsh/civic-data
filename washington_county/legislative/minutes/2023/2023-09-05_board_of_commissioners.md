Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan updated the Commissioners on the municipal election. As of
September 4, 2023, over 23,000 registered voters in Washington County had voted. Approximately
18,500 registered Republicans voted during the Primary thus far, which would equal
approximately twenty-eight percent (28%) turn out for the Congressional District 2 race.

Mr. Sullivan addressed the Resolution put out by the Republican National Committee. He
explained that Washington County has tried and true voting methods that were implemented many
years ago. Mr. Sullivan stated that Washington County uses paper ballots that are marked by hand
that have anti-counterfit properties, including high tech envelopes that ensure that one person, one
vote along with signature verification procedures. He stated that Washington County will do some
hand count audits at a precinct level, based on random selection.
