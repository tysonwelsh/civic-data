                                ORDINANCE NO. 1620

AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING SECTION 3-5-120 OF
THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE RENAMING THE DIVERSITY AND
INCLUSION ADVISORY COMMITTEE TO THE COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT
COMMITTEE.

WHEREAS Draper City established the Draper City Diversity and Inclusion Advisory
Committee to identify and advise on issues faced by diverse groups and to create
opportunities for an inclusive community within the City; and

WHEREAS the Council desires to focus on engaging the community and fostering
unity and belonging to all Draper City residents; and

WHEREAS due to the desire to focus on unity and engaging all members of the
community, the Diversity and Inclusion Advisory Committee needs to be renamed
to the Community Engagement Committee.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Section 3-5-120 of the Draper City Municipal Code is hereby amended to
read as shown in Exhibit " A" attached hereto and incorporated herein by
reference.

Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

Section 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date
of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 3RD DAY OF SEPTEMBER, 2024.
