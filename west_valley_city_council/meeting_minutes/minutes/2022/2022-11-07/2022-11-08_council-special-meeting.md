       MINUTES OF COUNCIL SPECIAL MEETING – NOVEMBER 8, 2022

THE WEST VALLEY CITY COUNCIL MET IN ELECTRONIC SPECIAL SESSION ON
TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 2022 AT 4:30 P.M. AT WEST VALLEY CITY HALL,
MULTIPURPOSE ROOM, 3600 CONSTITUTION BOULEVARD, WEST VALLEY CITY,
UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY MAYOR LANG.
THE MEETING WAS ALSO HELD ELECTRONICALLY VIA ZOOM.

       MOTION FOR CLOSED SESSION
       Councilmember Nordfelt moved to adjourn and reconvene in a Closed Session for pending
       litigation and discussion of a person’s character, competency, and/or health and
       Councilmember Huynh seconded the motion.

       A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.




THE WEST VALLEY CITY COUNCIL RECONVENED IN CLOSED SESSION ON
TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 8, 2022 AT 4:35 P.M., IN THE MULTI-PURPOSE ROOM, WEST
VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600 CONSTITUTION BOULEVARD, WEST VALLEY CITY,
UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED TO ORDER BY MAYOR LANG.

THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:

              Karen Lang, Mayor
              Lars Nordfelt, Councilmember At-Large
              Don Christensen, Councilmember At-Large
              Tom Huynh, Councilmember District 1
              Scott Harmon, Councilmember District 2
              William Whetstone, Councilmember District 3 (electronically)
              Jake Fitisemanu, Councilmember District 4


STAFF PRESENT PARTIALLY:

              Eric Bunderson, City Attorney


The City Council met in Closed Session and discussed pending litigation and discussion of a
person’s character, competency, and/or health.


THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY COUNCIL, THE
