                                 Work Session Minutes
                              Tuesday, November 14, 2023
                                       7:00 p.m.
                                   21 East 100 North
                                    Nephi, UT 84648

1.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson explained to the council that an individual was
     interested in putting an EV charging station up by Arby’s. The impact fees are based on
     panel size. The individual has asked if he could enter into an impact fee agreement with
     the city to defer some impact fees to see if the charging station would use the capacity of
     the panel size. The Council agreed that they would not enter into such an agreement
     and directed staff to communicate this to the developer. The council agreed that they
     needed to be consistent with the way the city charges impact fees.

2.    City Recorder Lisa Brough reviewed with the council the 2023 fiscal year fraud risk
     assessment that is required by law to complete annually. Ms. Brough said that the City’s
     score had increased this year because we added a link to a fraud reporting hotline on
     the city website. The assessment showed that Nephi City has a low risk level of fraud.

3. City Administrator and City Council Updates:
    Mr. Atkinson and Mayor Seely reported about the progress of the land swap for the
       golf course expansion.
    Mr. Atkinson reviewed the overstated sales tax numbers.
    Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the design work by Storm Engineering on the
       gas gate station and said that soon the city will be going out for bid on the project.
    Mr. Atkinson reported that they had rebid the Worwood Well. He said that they are
       looking at the sand issue with the well to make sure the issue is resolve, therefore
       the project may take longer than anticipated.
    Councilor Memmott asked about the road closure in Hunter Meadows and when it
       will be finished up. Mr. Atkinson said that the finishing requirements for the road
       closure are being worked out with Terry Messersmith.
    Ms. Brough reported that the Hive awning is being built by Clark Wood. She stated
       that Recreation Director Craig Oswald reported at management meeting that the
       Hive is booked every weekend but two or three through March of 2024. Travis Story
       is running a pickleball tournament next weekend and Randy Cuff is running a
       basketball tournament this coming weekend.
    Kasey Wright reported that National Vinyl has committed to the $150,000
       contribution to the Hive to be paid in $30,000 increments over five years. He
       recommended that we investigate purchasing floor coverings and air conditioning.
    Ms. Brough updated the council that an individual that has recently purchased some
       property from Patrick Painter has indicated that he may be calling the council
       members to get their feelings about a possible annexation of the area. Mr. Atkinson
       is preparing an analysis that is necessary to move the defined area from a level 2 to
       a level 1 on the annexation area map.
          Councilor Memmott reported on his communication that he had with County
           Commissioner Palmer about city and county mutual partnerships. He said that the
           commissioners do want to meet with the city about common issues. The council
           agreed with the idea of each party putting down in writing topics of discussion.
          Councilor Parady and Councilor Ostler thanked the Streets Department Head Ed
           Park for fixing the water drainage problem that was discussed at the last meeting.
          Councilor Ostler said he had a complaint about a dumpster that was out in the road
           easement east of the Verizon Store.

10.    Mayor Seely opened a regular council meeting. Councilor Ostler moved to executive
session to discuss litigation and the character and competence of an individual. Councilor
Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

11. Meeting adjourned at 8:45 p.m.
