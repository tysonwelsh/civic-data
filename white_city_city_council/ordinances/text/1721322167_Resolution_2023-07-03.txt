White City Metro Township
RESOLUTION NO.: 2 3-07-03 DATE: July 6, 2023
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL SUPPORTING THE GREATER SALT LAKE
MUNICIPAL SERVICES DISTRICT'S EFFORTS FOR ECONOMIC
DEVELOPMENT WITHIN THE METRO TOWNSHIPS
WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township ("White City”) is a Municipality pursuant to Utah
| Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.
| WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for White City pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-501; and
WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council has determined that it’s in the best interest
of the residents of White City to support the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District's efforts
for Economic Development within the Metro Townships, including White City.

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
hereby supports the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District's efforts for Economic
Development within the Metro Townships, including White City.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
6™ day of July 2023.
