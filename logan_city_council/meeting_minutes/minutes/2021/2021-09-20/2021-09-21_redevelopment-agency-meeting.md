ACTION ITEM:

PUBLIC HEARING – Budget Adjustment FY 2021-2022 appropriating: $3,000,000
funds for Library project completion – Resolution 21-48 RDA – Richard Anderson,
Finance Director

Finance Director Richard Anderson addressed the Council regarding the proposed budget
adjustment. He explained this completes the accumulation of funds for the construction
of the new Library. There is $3 million dollars coming from the RDA and was approved
earlier by the Council and the request is now to appropriate those funds. He will move
those funds to the capital projects fund where they will be maintained for construction
and the design of the Library. The total for the construction of the new Library is $14.1
million dollars plus design. Total with everything including design is $16 million dollars.

Chair M. Anderson opened the meeting to a public hearing.

Logan resident Gail Yost addressed the Council and said the community needs a new
Library and asked the Council to approve the appropriation of these funds.

There were no further comments and Chair M. Anderson closed the public hearing.

ACTION. Motion by Vice Chair Simmonds seconded by Councilmember Jensen to
approve Resolution 21-48 RDA as presented. Motion carried unanimously.

WORKSHOP ITEM:

Consideration of a proposed resolution approving Agency Assistance to Triiio
Group, LLC for its redevelopment project in the Downtown Redevelopment Project
Area (RPA) – Resolution 21-49 RDA – Kirk Jensen, Economic Development
Director

Economic Development Director Kirk Jensen addressed the Redevelopment Agency
regarding the proposed resolution. He explained that the Triiio Group, LLC, referred to as
Renewal - Mixed Use Project (“Project”), located at the northeast corner of 300 South
and 100 West in the Downtown Redevelopment Project Area (“RPA”) is proposed to

consist of 69 residential units and will be approximately 13,400 square feet of
commercial space and will have various other amenities. It will also have a 1,200 sq. ft.
corner bistro, commercial office space and retail space. This project will contribute
significantly to the achievement of the City’s economic goals, including: the fiscal

in the downtown area; the creation of new jobs; the improvement of the appearance of a

proposed investment and recommends its approval. They also approved the following
incentive package:

Triiio Group, LLC will receive $250,000 from the Agency’s Affordable Housing Fund
(“Fund”) upon completion of its project as conditionally approved by the City of Logan
Planning Commission in Design Review Permit PC#21-039. Monies from the Fund can
be used for market-rate housing projects located in redevelopment project areas where a
blight finding exists. All construction plans are to be approved by the City of Logan
Planning Commission. Additionally, the Redevelopment Agency encourages Triiio
Group, LLC to utilize local contractors and vendors whenever possible.

Councilmember A. Anderson said the Council received a letter asking a question about
the funds being used for moderate income housing and the fact that this housing is all
market rate housing.

Mr. Jensen responded and said the RDA State Code states that when they created the
redevelopment project areas, it does allow for affordable housing funds which are
earmarked by the RDA for those funds to be used for market rate housing in situations
where you have a project area where blight exists. When the Downtown RDA was
created, there was a blight study and a determination of blight so with that criteria it does
allow for affordable housing funds to be used for market rate housing. This project will
bring some housing inventory that is more affordable to our area.

Proponent Michael Jewell addressed the Council and said they looked into affordable
housing and its low income housing tax, called Litech and its surprisingly competitive to
qualify for Litech in Utah. It’s especially difficult to break into because of the way the
State of Utah works is essentially, the more often you’ve done a Litech project, the more
likely you are to award another Litech project. His company is very interested in pursuing
a Litech project in the future. He said the pricing is just a function of scarcity and the
more projects there are especially in the downtown and in areas where we are looking for
density the more affordable a market rate will become.

Kirk Jensen said regarding the use of RDA funds in the context of affordable housing or
market rate housing. There is an assertion that the RDA is not using some of these
already reserved affordable housing funds for those projects which are in there up to 80%
