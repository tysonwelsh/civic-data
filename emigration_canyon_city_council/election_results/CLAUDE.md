# Emigration Canyon — election_results

Audited slice of the **canonical Salt Lake County Clerk SOVC** canvass
(`../../salt_lake_county/elections/slco_municipal_results_long.csv`), filtered to the
**genuine Emigration Canyon at-large council contests** and rebuilt idempotently by
`build_emigration_elections.py`. **DERIVED — regenerate, never hand-edit.**

Emigration Canyon incorporated as a **Metro Township** (voters approved Nov 2015,
effective **Jan 1 2017**) and converted to a **CITY** effective **2024-05-01** (H.B. 35).
**Same 5-member council, ALL elected AT-LARGE, throughout** (no districts, no separately
elected mayor — the council selects one of the five as mayor, who presides and votes).

## Files
```
emigration_canyon_races.csv                 25-col audited race summary (header == south_jordan)
emigration_canyon_results_by_candidate.csv  one row per contest x candidate (rank, is_winner)
emigration_canyon_results_by_precinct.csv   one row per contest x precinct x candidate
build_emigration_elections.py               regenerates all three from the county SOVC
```
`is_winner = rank <= n_seats` (multi-seat at-large convention). Candidate names are kept
**verbatim UPPER-CASE** from the SOVC — normalize (`ROBERTO PINON`->Robert Pinon;
`DAVID PAUL BREMS`->David Brems) only at join time to votes/roster.

## 2026-07-17 — 2019 general At-Large now REGENERATED by the build (was hand-appended)
The **2019 general At-Large** contest is now reproduced by `build_emigration_elections.py`
itself — the earlier hand-appended row (and its "regen would drop this" caveat) is retired.

  a primary source, not read from either data file (the sheet-code era leaves `vote_for` blank).
