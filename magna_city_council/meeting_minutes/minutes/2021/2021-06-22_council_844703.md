Citizen Public Input

               Kristy Buchanan, We Witches 3, stated on Saturday from 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
We Witches 3, located at 9068 W Magna Main Street, is having an event. Fifteen vendors from
around the state are setting up booths in the parking lot and showing their stuff. The vendors are
new artists, and do not have a place to show their things, other than on Facebook. We Witches
3 has received 6,000 RSVPs. If this event goes well, another one will be held in July and one in
August.

               Mr. Schulz suggested getting the local artists in contact with the Magna Chamber
of Commerce.

               C.J. Withers, We Witches 3, stated We Witches 3 had some problems with an
establishment across the street when it asked it not to use its parking lot for two days. People
blew the request way out of portion and have even made death threats. She asked that the
situation be kept quiet to prevent anything further from happening.

               Council Member Hull stated the police are working on this.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
                 Audrey Pierce, speaking as a citizen, stated Code Enforcement is making it
difficult to maintain her family business, Magna’s Automotive Center, located at 8040 Magna Main
Street. Since January of last year, the business has made huge strides to come into compliance
with the conditional use requirements. However, every time the business did what was asked of
it, Code Enforcement found other issues, and most of those findings were petty and picky. Now,
the GLSMSD’s Planning Division has scheduled an upcoming hearing to consider pulling the
conditional use permit that allows Magna’s Automotive Center to operate. She felt Code
Enforcement was trying to categorize her family business as a nuisance in the community, rather
than getting it to come into compliance. The notice is for a virtual hearing, and the only way to
have an in-person hearing is to make special arrangements. There was a person’s name and
phone number on the notice, and family members have tried multiple times to contact that person,
but they have not received a response back. Magna’s Automotive Center has reached a point
that the only thing it can do is start a petition in the community and ask people to support whether
or not it should stay in this community.

                Dan Stanton stated Donnie Sweazey, Magna’s Automotive Center, has done so
much for this community, and at times, never asking for a penny for his work. He needs a little
bit of time to take care of the issues, as he has been through a lot this past year. He is not a
problem to Magna.

               Donnie Sweazey, Magna’s Automotive Center, stated he has been trying to take
care of business, the properties, and his health, but has to keep dealing with Code Enforcement.
He recently had a massive heart attack and lost over 40 pounds, and he is now going to lose
everything his family has worked for. The GSLMSD’s Planning Division is having a special
meeting to fine him $80,000. He got the notice on the 14th, and called Wendy Gurr, Planning
Coordinator, GSLMSD, leaving six messages on the 16th and 17th, but he still has not heard from
her.

Reports from Community Stakeholders

Magna in Motion

Magna Chamber of Commerce

               Sara VanRoosendaal stated the Magna Chamber of Commerce had its first
luncheon last Thursday, without providing lunch. It was the 2021 State of Magna, and 18 people
attended. A slide show is on the chamber’s website. The website did have some malware on it,
but that has been remedied. However, the chamber needs a new digital sign. The one it has on
the corner of 8400 and 2700 cannot be fixed. The technology has not been maintained in a
decade and the equipment is not available anywhere within the United States. The chamber is
looking at two options to replace it.

Magna United / Magna Community that Cares

recommended looking at the coalition’s website, as it has released a few press releases. Also,

Reports from other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Chief Del Craig stated the UPD has hired a new receptionist for the substation
who will be a great addition there. The previous receptionist took a job in Florida. Currently, the
Magna Precinct is ramping up for the upcoming celebration. Of note, there were two homicides
in Magna, and they have both been solved. The people involved in those have been taken into
custody. The one on 3600 South and 8000 West arose out of a domestic situation, whereby the
altercation resulted in the death of an individual.

               Mayor Peay asked if there was anything on the robbery at the 7-11.

                Chief Craig stated as far as he knew, a person was taken into custody. A shot
was fired in the ceiling there, and that case is still being worked on.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Council Member Prokopis stated Council Member Prokopis stated the UFA had
asked for the 1948 Mack for the parade, but that has already been reserved by Herriman. So, it
will need to find other transportation for the parade or walk in it. He also reported he helped with
a fire at 6400 West about 4000 South for an hour-and-a-half. No one was hurt in that.

               Craig Schulz stated he has been given an account for the software that allows
data to be viewed in real time. In the past, that data would have taken three to six months to get.

              Council Member Prokopis stated he has access to that program as well. The
UFA is going to put out quarterly statistics using that software because it is accurate and up to
the minute. The UFA has approved its budget, but it has not committed to not having ongoing
Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security (CARES) expenses.

Code Enforcement ~ Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District

                 Tom Zumbado, Code Enforcement Officer, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District, stated since the last meeting, there have been 40 new cases started and 21 cases closed.
That means, there are 88 open cases, 53 of which are because of weeds and the rest zoning
issues.

               Greg Schulz stated one of those weeds cases is the Magna Metro Township’s
park property at the corner of 2700 South and 8000 West. The property is a mess with Russian
Thistles growing.
                                               4
DATE           TUESDAY                                  JUNE                             22, 2021


               Mr. Zumbado stated the greatest concern is the thistles growing on the park strip,
as they are starting to affect pedestrians. He continued with the report, stating Code Enforcement
conducted its second inspection at the intersection on California Avenue and 2200 South – the
homeless encampment. The recreation vehicles and trailers that had been there are gone now.
However, the footprint is considerable, and it is going to be a large cleanup. The people attending
the inspection with him were Detective Harry Holt, Unified Police Department; Zachary Torres,
Salt Lake County Health Department; and Colton Norman and Ryan Perry, Kennecott. They
wanted to see what the Magna Metro Township Council’s reaction was before committing
resources to the cleanup. Mr. Torres mentioned that sometimes funds were available for
homeless abatements through the Health Department, and he would look into whether resources
could be made available for this one. As soon as he has that information, he will forward it to
Kennecott, so represents there can start working on a plan.

               Council Member Prokopis asked how many dumpsters the cleanup would take.

                Mr. Zumbado stated it will take three to four large dump trucks. There is another
extensive encampment of about six or seven vehicles, and eight or nine people north of the
intersection along 7200 West on the Salt Lake City side. Kennecott is interested in bringing the
landfill administration to the table on that.

               Paul Ashton asked if Kennecott owned all that land.

               Mr. Zumbado stated no, the northern parcel belongs to Kennecott, and the
southern parcel is listed in the land use record as belonging to Salt Lake County/Salt Lake City.
He thought it might be expansion area for the landfill.

               Mr. Schulz recommended waiting for an answer from the Health Department
before looking at other avenues.

Resolution

                 Paul Ashton reviewed the following resolution stating this is the formal resolution
that the Council talked about at its last meeting. The Magna Metro Township has received UFA’s
invoice, but it cannot be forced to pay it. It will come through in a bill later:

RESOLUTION NO. 21-06-01                                                      DATE: June 22, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       THE PAYMENT OF $58,840.00 FROM THE SALT LAKE COUNTY INTERLOCAL
       GRANT FUNDS TO THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY FOR POST CARES ACT
       COVID-RELATED EXPENSES

       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna” or the “Municipality”) recognizes our
need for municipal-types services; and

       WHEREAS, the Unified Fire Authority (“UFA”) is an Interlocal Cooperation Agency that
provides fire protection services to Magna; and

      WHEREAS, on September 22, 2020 Magna and UFA signed an Interlocal Cooperation
Agreement (“subgrant agreement”) and Magna paid UFA $40,954.00 for Magna’s share of UFA’s
COVID-related expenses (Attachment A); and

       WHEREAS, the monies Magna used to pay UFA’s COVID-related expenses were
received from Salt Lake County pursuant to the CARES Act for use in covering unbudgeted
COVID-related costs for the delivery of municipal-type services; and

       WHEREAS, on December 1, 2020, Magna received and paid an additional invoice for
$9,751.00, from UFA for additional COVID-related costs incurred by UFA providing fire protection
and EMS service to Magna since September 22, 2020; and

                                                 6
DATE          TUESDAY                                JUNE                          22, 2021

       WHEREAS, in late December 2020, Magna was required to return over $1.5 million in
unused CARES Act funds to the County pursuant to the CARES Act Agreement between Magna
and the County; and

        WHEREAS, on March 23, 2021 the Council approved an Interlocal Agreement with Salt
Lake County to receive $1,523,436.16 for Magna to use for unrestricted municipal costs; and
WHEREAS, on May 31, 2021 Magna received a third invoice from UFA for $58,840.00 (Exhibit
A) for COVID-related costs incurred by the UFA since the December 2020 payment; and

       WHEREAS, using funds from the County Interlocal Grant to Magna to assist UFA would
be consistent with Magna’s support of UFA throughout the pandemic; therefore be it

       RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council approves, the payment of $58,840.00
to UFA to assist them in covering their unbudgeted COVID-related costs.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 22ND DAY OF JUNE, 2021 in the Magna Metro
Township, Salt Lake County, Utah.
