             Rules of Conduct for Planning Commission Meetings
PROCEDURE FOR PUBLIC COMMENT

1.   Any person or entity may appear in person or be represented by an authorized agent at any meeting of the
     Commission.

2.   Unless altered by the Chair, the order of the procedure on an application shall be:

     a. The supporting agency staff will introduce the application, including staff’s recommendations and a
        summary of pertinent written comments and reports concerning the application

     b. The applicant will be allowed up to 15 minutes to make their presentation.

     c. The Community Council representative can present their comments as applicable.

     d. Where applicable, persons in favor of, or not opposed to, the application will be invited to speak.

     e. Where applicable, persons opposing the application, in whole or in part will be invited to speak.

     f. Where applicable, the applicant will be allowed 5 minutes to provide concluding statements.

     g. Surrebutals may be allowed at the discretion of the Chair.

CONDUCT FOR APPLICANTS AND THE PUBLIC

1.   Speakers will be called to the podium by the Chair.

2.   Each speaker, before talking, shall give his or her name and address.

3.   All comments should be directed to the Commissioners, not to the staff or to members of the audience.

4.   For items where there are several people wishing to speak, the Chair may impose a time limit, usually 3
     minutes per person, or 5 minutes for a group spokesperson. If a time limit is imposed on any member or
     spokesperson of the public, then the same time limit is imposed on other members or spokespersons of the
     public, respectively.

5.   Unless otherwise allowed by the Chair, no questions shall be asked by the speaker or Commission Members.

6.   Only one speaker is permitted before the Commission at a time.

7.   The discussion must be confined to essential points stated in the application bearing on the desirability or
     undesirability of the application.

8.   The Chair may cease any presentation or information that has already been presented and acknowledge that it
     has been noted in the public record.

9.   No personal attacks shall be indulged in by either side, and such action shall be sufficient cause for stopping the
     speaker from proceeding.

10. No applause or public outbursts shall be permitted.

11. The Chair or supporting agency staff may request police support to remove offending individuals who refuse to
    abide by these rules.

12. After the public comment portion of a meeting or hearing has concluded, the discussion will be limited to the
    Planning Commission and Staff.

Property Address: Approximately 4700 South 5200 West (Camp Kearns Area)
Request: Roadway Dedication Plat for Camp Kearns Area Roads
Applicant Name: Salt Lake County

MSD Planner: Diana Martinez
MSD Planning Staff Recommendation: Approval




                                       PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Salt Lake County requests approval of a Roadway Dedication Plat for the Camp Kearns Area Roads.

Camp Kearns is considered the “industrial and warehouse district” of Kearns. Prior to 2017, the roadways within
Camp Kearns were unpaved and there were no roadway improvements installed.

In 2017, a $1.9 million project to install public roadway improvements began. To get the amount of land to
install the public roadway improvements at the required right-of-way width, most of the property owners in the
Camp Kearns area were required to dedicate portions of their property. The project was completed in August of
2018; however, collecting the signatures for the plat has been ongoing since that time. All signatures have now
been collected and the MSD has been tasked with moving the project forward to completion.




                                   SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION
                                               (see attached map)

Camp Kearns is located in the northwest corner of Kearns. The subject property is located along the corner of
5600 West and 4700 South. The public road dedication area encompasses all roadways within “Camp Kearns”.
These roads include: Camp Kearns Road (5200 West), Warehouse Road, Cable Ridge Road, Liberator Drive, 4850
South and 4985 South.
        Request: Dedication Plat Summary and Recommendation                  File #: RWD2021-000289




                                  GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The Kearns General Plan was recently adopted in October 2020. The subject property is located in a Warehouse
District and is located along the northern border of Kearns. The site is bounded to the west by 5600 West and the
railroad, and to the east by Shadow Ridge Estates. This area currently service as the primary manufacturing and
industrial center of Kearns. Of the Warehouse District’s 120.6 acres, 73.2 acres (or 60%) are dedicated to industrial
uses.




                         ISSUES OF CONCERN/PROPOSED MITIGATION
There are no issues of concern with this application.



                                      PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
This is the last step in the process to complete the public roadway improvement project. Now that all property
owners that dedicated portions of their property have signed the plat (or an affidavit), the roadway dedication
plat can be recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office.



                              PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
The MSD Planning Staff recommends that the Kearns Planning Commission forward a recommendation of
approval for the Roadway Dedication Plat for the Camp Kearns Area Roads to the Kearns Council.
