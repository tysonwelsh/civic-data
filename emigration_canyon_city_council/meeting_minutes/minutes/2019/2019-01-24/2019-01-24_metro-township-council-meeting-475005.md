Community Input
Citizen Comments
Michael Hughes, Chair, Emigration Improvement District, stated for the last five
or six years the Emigration Improvement District (EID) has been involved with an ongoing lawsuit.
The EID has tried to release what information it could without compromising the lawsuit. Because
of the limited information that has been released; the story has become something that it is not.
The EID wants to make a conscience effort to inform the public of its side of the argument. The
EID is in the process of hiring a public relations professional to help get correct information out
and help with transparency. They want the public to understand what the EID is. The EID Board
wants to include the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council, Planning Commission, and the
Community Council, in educating the public on what each entity does so the public and the various
entities are on the same page.

Council Business
Beer Tax
Mayor Smolka stated beer tax is money that is collected on the sale of beer. The
tax is split up by population and distributed to the Salt Lake County Jail to fund programs for
individuals incarcerated due to alcohol and substance abuse. Emigration Canyon Metro
Township’s portion can be moved to another specified area of service.
Council Member Paine, seconded by Council Member Hawkes, moved to leave
the funds as currently allocated. The motion passed unanimously.

2019 Meeting Schedule
The Council reviewed the 2019 Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council
meeting schedule.
Council Member Paine, seconded by Council Member Brems, moved to adopt
the 2019 meeting schedule for the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council. Meetings will
be held on the fourth Thursday of the month, 7:00 p.m. at the Emigration Canyon Fire Station
119, 5025 E Emigration Canyon Road, Salt Lake City. The motion passed unanimously.

Council Meeting Rules and Procedures
David Church reviewed rules and procedures for the Emigration Canyon Metro
Township Council meetings.
Council Member Paine, seconded by Council Member Bowen, moved to adopt the

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) Hired Staff for the Metro Townships
Mayor Smolka stated the GSLMSD discussed hiring an employee that would
support the metro townships. It makes sense to have someone that will attend meetings and
report back to the councils and other various duties. He would eventually like approval to allocate
$24,000 of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township budget to assist in the hiring of a full-time
GSLMSD employee.
