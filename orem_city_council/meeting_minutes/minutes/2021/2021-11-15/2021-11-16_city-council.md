       PRESENTATION – Acceptance and Certification of the 2021 City of Orem General
       Election

Mrs. Bates presented the City Ccouncil with the final results of the 2021 City of Orem General
Election as provided by the Utah County Elections Office for the Board of Canvassers
acceptance and certification.

Mayor Brunst moved the City Council, acting as the Board of Canvassers certify the 2021
City of Orem General Election results as presented by the City Recorder Mr. Lambson the
motion. Those voting aye: Richard F. Brunst, Tom Macdonald, Terry Peterson, Debby Lauret,
David Spencer, and Brent Sumner. The motion passed.
