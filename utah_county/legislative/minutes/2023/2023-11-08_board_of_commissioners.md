A RESOLUTION HONORING UTAH COUNTY'S VETERANS

WHEREAS. Veterans Day is a U.S. holiday, established in 1938, and is dedicated
to American veterans of all wars and occurs every November 11". Memorial Day. held
on the last Monday of May, was established to remember Service personne! who died
in combat related action.

WHEREAS, in 1918, on the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month, an
Armistice, or temporary cessation of hostilities, was declared between the Allied
nations and Germany in World War I, then known as the Great War;

WHEREAS, in November 1919, U.S. President Woodrow Wilson signed into
law Armistice Day to be commemorated on November | |"; with the end of the Korean
War, Armistice Day was renamed Veterans Day on June |, 1954;

WHEREAS, nine million Allied soldiers died during World War | and 21 million
wounded, with Germany, Russia, Austria-Hungary, France and Great Britain each losing
nearly a million or more lives; of that number 116,516 American soldiers were killed in
action, with at least five million European civilians who died from disease, starvation,
or exposure;

WHEREAS, since World War | another 625,000 American service personnel died in all
wars in defense of the United States, and for people in distant lands;

WHEREAS, Utah's Soldiers, Sailors, Marines, Air Force, Coast Guard, and Space Force
personnel have served in many foreign lands during World War II, the Korean War, Viet Nam, the
wars in Iraq and Afghanistan, and numerous other conflicts, to repel and subdue international
horrors:

WHEREAS, American veterans of foreign wars continue to serve in their private lives.

They serve bettering their homes, churches, communities, schools, business. all fields of law
enforcement, state and national defense, and in the medical professions; most of them prefer living

out of the spotlight.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the LEGISLATIVE BODY OF UTAH

COUNTY, UTAH:
(1) Commemorates the week of November 6th — | Ith, 2023, with displays of the

United States flag and military service banners in and around the county complex,
and with booths highlighting Veterans services for the sharing of military
experiences, Constitutional liberties and principles in the Utah County Historic
Courthouse November 8th — 10th;

(2) Commends and honors the selfless dedication of fellow Utah County citizens
displayed through their honorable military service, both in war and in peace:

(3) Calls upon elected officials and employees of Utah County, and all Utah County
residents to extend expressions of thanks to military Veterans:

and engage in charitable activities in honor of Veterans.
