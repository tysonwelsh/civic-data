Chair Kucera opened the Retreat at 4:35 P.M. 
 
 
1. Planning Department projects update (4:37 P.M.) 
 
Director Putt introduced his goals for the meeting and provided an update of projects including 
the community visioning process. Director Barnes provided his perspective on the community 
visioning process. Director Putt received Dakota Pacific’s new proposal that morning. 
Commission Members asked questions and gave input. 
 
2. Review of Snyderville Basin Development Code Use Table (5:17 P.M.) 
 
Chair Kucera opened discussion. 
 
a. Residential Zones—Commercial Uses 
 
Commissioners provided their thoughts on the issue of having commercial uses in residential 
neighborhoods. Director Barnes added his thoughts. Planner Ray Milliner responded to 
questions and requests from the Commissioners. 
 
2 
 
b. Service Commercial Uses 
 
Those present touched briefly on uses in the Service Commercial zone. 
 
c. CC Zone Uses 
 
Further mention was made about Community Commercial zone uses. 
 
 
3.  Parking requirements (5:39 P.M.) 
Chair Kucera opened discussion on parking, and particularly parking in the service commercial 
zone. Commissioners raised their concerns with respect to parking requirements. Director 
Barnes explained some theories behind parking. Planner Strader provided her perspective on 
parking. Director Putt weighed in as well. Planner Milliner responded to questions.  
4. Workforce Housing (6:03 P.M.) 
 
Commissioner Conabee provided information on what Park City is doing to try to attract 
workforce housing. The Commissioners and staff moved the discussion into parking at the 
Canyons Base of the Park City Mountain Resort. Chair Kucera and Director Putt refocused the 
discussion on future intentions regarding workforce housing. Commissioners asked questions 
and provided their perspectives. Director Putt made some suggestions that the Commission 
agreed with on a process to move forward. 
 
5. Open Forum (6:27 P.M.) 
 
Commissioners brought up other issues that they would like considered in the year to come. 
Peter Barnes responded to questions. Chair Kucera asked for staff suggestions for 
improvement. 
 
6. Future joint meeting with Park City Planning Commission (6:57 P.M.) 
 
Director Putt provided a general overview of the thoughts around having a joint meeting.  
 
The meeting adjourned at 6:59 P.M.
