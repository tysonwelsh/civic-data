 6. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Ordinance No. 25-16 Amending the Good Landlord Program and Short-Term Rentals
          and Corresponding Amendments to the Consolidated Fee Schedule
          Senior Assistant City Attorney Patrick Boice introduced the item explaining that the
          Council had discussed the Good Landlord Program and Short-Term Rentals in multiple
          previous meetings. Responding to a question from Council Member Green, he confirmed
          the contract between the City and landlords for participation in the Good Landlord
          Program would include interior inspections to provide flexibility to Code Enforcement
          officers.

           Chair Lamb opened a public hearing at 7:17 pm.

           Comments:
           None

           Chair Lamb closed the public hearing at 7:18 pm.

           Council Member Green believed including the option for interior inspections would help
           ensure properties did not become rundown. Council Member Shelton struggled with the
           idea of interior inspections and believed the City needed to be careful with how interior
           inspections were approached. Council Member Green believed interior inspections
           included in the contract would be done by the property owners or property managers as
           part of participation in the Good Landlord Program. Council Member Jacob also struggled
           with the idea of interior inspections. Council Member Green said the interior inspections
           of which he spoke would be different from an interior inspection by the government.
           Council Member Jacob noted the language in the ordinance addressed interior
           inspections by City government. Mr. Boice said the document mirrored language
           provided by the State.

           Council Member Jacob expressed concern that what was meant by interior inspection was
           not specified. Mr. Boice said some buildings routinely require fire inspections or County
           Health inspections. Council Member Jacob said he would prefer to remove the language
           and replace Section 4-2r-5 with “reserved” (to maintain existing numbering). Mr. Boice
           confirmed the State Code would continue to apply if the language were removed. Mr.
           Boice suggested referencing the State Code to which inspections would be subject.

           City Attorney Josh Chandler noted it was his understanding that State language and the
           City language were different, with State Code providing prohibition on performing an
           inspection in certain circumstances, and City Code providing permissive language making
           it clear what the City could do. Mr. Chandler suggested the language in City Code and State

           Code would operate differently, and said he did not believe the language to be of concern.
           Mr. Chandler expressed the opinion that the City Code provision would be a valuable tool
           in ensuring people complied with provisions of the Good Landlord Program. Council
           Member Jacob said he would be more comfortable if the word “consent” were added.

           MOTION:      Council Member Jacob moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 25-16
                        Amending the Good Landlord Program and Short-Term Rentals and
                        Corresponding Amendments to the Consolidated Fee Schedule striking
                        the second sentence remaining in 4-2R-5 so that it simply reads “All
                        licensed premises are subject to inspection.” And then it can be
                        governed by state code after that.
                        Chair Lamb seconded the motion.

           Vice Chair Whitelock asked Mr. Boice if there had been a situation in which the City
           needed to enter without a warrant and if it was a tool the City needed. Mr. Boice said the
           City had not had the personnel needed for the type of oversight desirable for the Good
           Landlord Program. He said he could think of situations in which an internal inspection
           may be warranted, and said he believed the City would have that ability with existing
           Code and the contract to participate in the Good Landlord Program. Council Member
           Green said Code Enforcement had the ability to literally seize property if needed. He said
           he could see the value in having a line in the Code stating units could be inspected but
           said he would be comfortable if the line were struck.

           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Chad Lamb, Kayleen Whitelock, Bob Bedore, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob,
                      Kent Shelton
           NO:
           ABSENT: Pamela Bloom
           The motion passed 6-0.
