MEMO
To: City Council
From: Mike Barker, City Manager
Date: 2025-09-16
Re: Approve Resolution #25-47


Comments:

The City Council has approved an interlocal agreement (“the Interlocal”) with Point of the
Mountain State Land Use Authority (“POMSLA”) that addresses the design, construction,
ownership and maintenance of certain public infrastructure, as well as the delivery of
certain public services to The Point.

The Interlocal provides for a development consultant, chosen by Draper City and
POMSLA, to provide design review, construction monitoring and similar services. Draper
City and POMSLA have gone through a rigorous procurement process and have selected
METHODS Consulting to provide development consultant services outlined in the
Interlocal.
