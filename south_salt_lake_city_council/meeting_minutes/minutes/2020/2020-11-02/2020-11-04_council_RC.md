NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of
          upcoming events, meetings, activities, etc.

       2. CITIZEN’S COMMENTS. Ashley Gray, 922 W. Teal Run Way. She has
          lived in South Salt Lake for 11 years and is an HOA vice-president in the
          Riverfront community. She brought to the attention of the Council that the
          street lights near the new elementary school have not yet been turned on and it
          is very dark in the area. Secondly, she worries about the safety of the
          Riverfront neighborhood as there are a lot of RV campers in the neighborhood
          and this is a new development since the opening of the Men’s Resource
          Center.

          Council Chair Bynum said as far as the RV parking the City Council passed
          an ordinance last year which doesn’t allow for RVs to park in front of
          residences without a permit. She encouraged Ms. Gray continue to call
          dispatch to report these RVs

          Mayor Wood said the City has been in contact with Rocky Mountain Power
          regarding the lights at the elementary and they have informed the City that
          this lighting project is on the list. She also added that the City holds a monthly
          Men’s Resource Center meeting and said they would reach out to Ms. Gray to
          give her more details on that.

       3. MAYOR’S COMMENTS. Mayor Wood gave an update on the Citizen
          Review board selection process. The process is on the City’s website and they
          are sending out invites to professionals in the civic, social and equity
          professions to serve as members of the board selection committee member. As
          soon as committee members are selected the Council will be updated with that
          information.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS. None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS. None.

       6. INFORMATION – COVID-19 Update. Fire Chief, Terry Addison, gave the
          Council an update on the current COVID-19 pandemic.

       2. Discussion of the Citizen Review Board Ordinance. Police Chief, Jack
          Carruth said with the creation of a Citizen Review Board City staff knew there
          would need to be staffing implications. He went over the staffing needs and
          the budget that would go along with those needs. A copy is attached and
          incorporated by this reference. The budget costs presented weren’t exact
          numbers. The positions needed to help with a Citizen Review Board would be
          a crime analyst, a police lieutenant and an assistant city attorney. These
          positions could be used to assist with other needs in the City as needed but
          their main focus would be the Citizen Review Board.

          There was a brief discussion on the where the budget for the Citizen Review
          Board.

          Council Chair Bynum said these are initial numbers and will be fine-tuned
          further as the Council continue their discussions.

          Chief Carruth discussed why adding these positions would help improve the
          internal affairs process. He went over the organizational chart of the Police
          Department currently and how the structure would look if these positions were
          added.

          There was some confusion with the organizational chart example that was
          shown and a lengthy discussion was had as to what role the Citizen Review
          Board would play in this structure. Chief Carruth will make clarifying changes
          to the organizational chart.

          City Attorney, Hannah Vickery, clarified that the Citizen Review Board will
          be a separate body that doesn’t report to anyone in the City. This board will
          require help from City staff and will have some required training.

          Chief Carruth continued the presentation and gave an overview on additional
          costs the Board would possibly require.

          Ms. Vickery said the budget presented shows the City staff implications as
          well as other budgetary obligations that may arise. Adding this budget will
          require a budget amendment that will then require Council action so any final
          approval on the board budget will come from the Council.

          Council Member Bynum added that this budget is a very rough draft and there
          will be more discussion in the coming weeks.

          Council Member Thomas asked what the overall cost would be for the
          creation of this board.

          Ms. Vickery said there are some parts of this budget are still up in the air so
          there isn’t any final budget numbers yet.

          Council Chair Bynum said at a future meeting the Council could hear from the
          Finance Director for further details.

          Council Member deWolfe said his estimate from what was shown tonight is
          about $500,000.

          The Council discussed what the stipend for this Board should be.

          Council Member deWolfe made a motion to move this item to Unfinished
          Business at a future meeting.
