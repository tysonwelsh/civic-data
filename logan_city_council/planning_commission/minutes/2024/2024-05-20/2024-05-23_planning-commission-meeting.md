Approval of the Minutes from the May 9, 2024 meeting.

PC 24-022 The Rock House Part 2 STR [Conditional Use Permit] John A. & Janene Petersen,
authorized agent/owner are requesting a conditional use permit to operate a 3-bedroom short-term
rental using the entire home located at 317 East 300 North #A in the Traditional Neighborhood
Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 06-046-0008 (Adams Neighborhood).

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
available in the Community Development Department.

2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a three (3) bedroom short-term rental occupying the
entire home.

3. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes two (2) people per bedroom with two (2) additional
individuals for a maximum of eight (8) occupants in the STR.

4. A short-term rental may not be the subject of multiple rental contracts by separate individuals,
groups, or parties for the same night or nights.

5. Occupancy for the single-family home portion of the structure shail comply with LDC
regulations.

6. A minimum of three (3) parking stalls are required for the STR. One stall in the garage and two
along the shared driveway. Stalls must be 9’x18’ in size and paved with an approved hard
surface.

7. Parking of recreational vehicles, trailers, boats, ATVs, or similar vehicles associated with the
STR shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on-street.

8. Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.

9. Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two
consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records
available for review and inspection by Logan City.

10. Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8 am.


11. A Logan City building/fire inspection is required which may result in building remodeling and
building permits prior to the issuance of a business license.

12. A Logan City business license is required before a rental contract can be issued.

13. The STR shall comply with local and State Building, Health, Fire, Safety, Maintenance, and
Nuisance Codes prior to operating.

14. Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast and reviewed and approved prior to the
issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.

15. No signs are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All signage shall be approved and
permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

16. No fences are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All fences shall be approved and
permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

17. All points of use of water down stream of this properties water meter should meet Utah’s current
plumbing code and its amendments along with Utah’s Administrative Drinking Water rules. This
would help minimize the risk of a contamination of the water that would affect the homes
occupants or lodger and Logan Cities water system. This also would help minimize liabilities to
the owner.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. The proposed project conforms to the requirements of the Land Development Code and is listed
as a conditional use in the Use Table.

2. This STR meets the City’s STR density and location parameters as specified in Chapter
17.37.130.

3. As conditioned the use is compatible with the surrounding land uses and will not interfere with
the use and enjoyment of adjoining or area properties.

4. The streets providing access and other infrastructure to the subject property have adequate
capacities and level of service for the conditional use.

5. The Planning Commission has substantiated the findings for a Conditional Use Permit in Chapter
17.42.050.

PUBLIC HEARING:

PC 24-021 Downtown Blue Cottage STR [Conditional Use Permit] Bo Johnson/North Park Holdings
LLC, authorized agent/owner is requesting a conditional use permit to operate a 2-bedroom short-
term rental within the main floor of the home located at 25 South 200 West in the Traditional
Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 06-032-0023 (Ellis Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Planner reviewed the request for a two-bedroom short-term rental (STR) on the
main floor of the duplex located at 25 South 200 West in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential
(NR-6) zone. The home was grandfathered as a legally existing nonconforming duplex in 2001
establishing a second unit in the basement of the home. The basement apartment will continue to
operate as a long-term one-bedroom rental.

The parking is proposed in the driveway to the south of the home, two stalls for the STR and two
stalls for the long-term rental (four stacked stalls). The Land Development Code requires parking


stalls to be 9x18 and located at least two feet from the side property line. The total area on the south
side of the home is 17.5 feet in width from the house to the property line. There is not enough width
to fit the proposed stacked stalls. Three tandem parking stalls would fit on the south side of the
home, but that would not meet the required four stalls.

There was also a cement pad that was recently poured in the front yard that is connected to the
driveway. The proponent has stated that it is a patio space for the basement apartment. Staff is
concerned that it will end up as parking in the future if it remains as is. If it is a patio space it needs
to be modified and delineated as a separate patio space to prevent it from being used as parking.

The proponent indicated that they were considering purchasing some property from the neighbor to
the south to get more parking width. R. Holley pointed out that the adjacent property to the south is
already out of compliance with the home being under the required 8-foot side setback.

Commissioner Peterson stated that even if the commission allowed a 10 percent reduction in the
minimum parking area setbacks, the project would still be short on the space needed for parking.

Commissioner Croshaw asked if the grandfathered basement apartment affects the parking. R.
Holley said when this apartment was built in the 1950’s there were no parking standards. The
nonconforming grandfathered status only applies to the building, it does not apply to today's parking
standards.

Proponent: Tony Johnson spoke about the front yard patio and said they are planning to install some
decorative items along the east and south sides of the patio to separate it from the driveway. T.
Johnson said he has spoken to the church across the street about using one of their parking stalls
for the downstairs tenant. T. Johnson said he also spoke to the neighbor to the south about getting
an easement in place that would allow them to use some of the neighbor's property for parking.

Public: No public comment was made.

Commission: The Commission discussed the illegal parking pad. Commissioner Peterson said he
would like to see a permanent delineator installed to separate the driveway from the concrete patio.
Commission Lewis is in favor of the proposed patio being removed. Commissioner Croshaw
mentioned separating the patio area with a gabion wall. Commissioner Doutre agreed that a gabion
wall would be a permanent separation that would not allow parking on the proposed patio.

The Commission discussed the minimum parking requirements and whether the compact stall
allowances in the parking standards could be applied here. R. Holley indicated that the compact stalls
are not permitted in this type of application. There was also discussion about off-site parking. R.
Holley said the basement apartment could have offsite parking with an alternate parking plan and a
written agreement, but the short-term rental parking must be located on-site.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson made a motion to deny a conditional use permit for PC 24-021 with
the findings for denial as listed below. Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

FINDINGS FOR DENIAL

1. The proposed project is unable to meet the minimum dimensional requirements for parking stall
areas, setbacks, and locations as per the Parking and Short-term Rental chapters in the Land
Development Code.

2. The recently unpermitted paved driveway extension does not meet LDC Code standard setbacks
and location and did not receive an approved driveway permit prior to installation.


3. The Planning Commission thoroughly debated and considered numerous possible mitigations to
the project's dimensional shortcomings, but after deliberation voted to deny because the property
is physically unable to accommodate the proposal as configured and with the neighboring
property to the south already having a nonconforming side yard setback, additional property
acquisition is not possible.

Moved: E. Peterson Seconded: R. Croshaw Approved: 4-0

Yea: Doutre, Croshaw, Lewis, Peterson Nay: Abstain:
