C-12-2025
Godfrey Industrial Park No. 3
2349 South 5900 West
M Zone (13.5 Acres)

The following application was continued from the September 10, 2025 Planning Commission
hearing to allow time for the applicant to address the following:

   •   Provide an elevation of Building G
   •   Update the site plan to show proposed surfacing as discussed by the Commission
   •   Update the plans to show the berming/screening and fencing locations

The applicant, Scott Godfrey representing Godfrey Industrial Park No. 3, is requesting
conditional approval for a flex industrial, office/warehouse, heavy trailer service and outside
storage at 2349 South 5900 West. This property is zoned M, Manufacturing, and the West Valley
City General Plan designates this area as Light Manufacturing. The surrounding zone on all sides
is Manufacturing (M). The surrounding uses include large office/warehouse complexes and
similar trucking facilities.
The overall development will consist of seven (7) buildings. There are also two wetland type
areas that are being coordinated with the Army Corps of Engineers. These areas will currently
remain undeveloped with natural vegetation and will be required to be maintained free of weeds
and debris. If these areas are modified or developed in the future those plans will be reviewed at
that time.

The southern portion of the development is proposed to have potentially have 12 units that are
3,500 SF in size. These units are laid out to have an office, restroom and warehouse or light
industrial area with overhead doors on the front and rear of the buildings for pass through access.
Each tenant space has been allocated three parking stalls so the uses/tenant mix will be
monitored as building permits and business licenses are submitted and the parking will be
evaluated periodically to ensure that the site is in compliance. These buildings will be required
to meet the Commercial Design Standards along the front primary facades.

These south units will also have outside storage yards of approximately .2 acres with one larger
yard of .40 acres for Unit F-1. The exterior access to the yards will all be gated and paved. As
discussed in the prior meetings the Commission recommends that the outside storage areas also
be hard surfaced so with this resubmittal the applicant is now showing the entire area all paved.

The north development is for heavy trailer service. They will not be providing any service for
heavy trucks at this location. There will be a 5,200 SF maintenance shop building. The exterior
elevations will match buildings A-F although this use is not required to meet the commercial
design standards.
For surfacing of this area there have been several different versions of the plan submitted and
reviewed and at this time the plan shows the following:

   •   The northeast corner is left undeveloped due to possible wetland locations as well
       as maintaining an existing drainage channel for this phase of development.
   •   The main access off 5900 West is paved as well as the area adjacent to and around the
       maintenance facility building. The northwest and west area behind the building is gravel.

As discussed in the previous meetings, historically trucking uses are required to pave the entire
lot as outlined in the surfacing standards (§7-9-114). Gravel surfaces are an exception for
tracked equipment or areas with limited travel and limited use of rubber tire vehicles and trailers.

Based off the discussion with the Commission, Staff recommends the following options:

   •   Option A: The entire lot area that will be occupied for this use must be paved.
   •   Option B: The entire lot area that will be occupied for this use must be paved, including
       all vehicle aisles, except the truck pads in the northwest and west area, behind the
       maintenance building, may be surfaced with gravel.
   •   Option C: The entire lot area that will be occupied for this use shall be paved, except the
       truck pads and aisles in the northwest and west area, behind the maintenance building,
       may be surfaced with gravel as outlined on the current site plan being presented.

The required landscaping for this site is a minimum of 5%. Landscaping must also be provided
along the interior of the site with a minimum of 15 SF of landscaping per parking stall. There are
two street frontages so there will be a 20’ wide landscaped setback required, measured from the

of the property and with the latest update, and that section of the site not being developed, Staff
recommends deferring those requirements until that portion of the site is further developed.

along the property boundary, similar to the site to the north, to provide a more finished edge to
this development at this time.

The details of signage have not been discussed at this stage in the development. All signage shall
comply with the requirements set forth in the Sign Ordinance and require a building permit prior

located in masonry enclosures designed to match the buildings or within the screened yards as
outlined in §7-2-118.

C-16-2025
Woodward and Moody LTD 8-9 (Outside Storage)
6746 and 6764 W. Moody Circle (2230 S)
M Zone (.7 acres)

The applicant, Woodward and Moody LTD, is requesting conditional use approval for
outside storage located at 6746 and 6764 W. Moody Circle (2230 S), on lots 8 and 9 of the
Moody Subdivision (ZSMI-12-2022). This property is zoned M, Manufacturing and the West
Valley City General Plan designates this area as Light Manufacturing. The surrounding zone is
Manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses include outside storage, warehouse, office
and light manufacturing.

The lots are located on a private street that serves this development. The outside storage areas are
set back off the private roadway and behind the parking area and building which satisfies the
outside storage screening requirement. These are speculative buildings, and the future tenants are
not yet known. Therefore, the storage areas will be hard-surfaced with asphalt or concrete to
maintain flexibility for potential users.

The building exteriors are constructed of architectural metal panels with a block wainscot.
There are metal like columns and horizontal elements provided in the panels for some
building and roof line relief. The exterior elevations will comply with the Commercial Design
Standards based off the possible tenant uses for this site.

There is a 10’ utility easement along the frontage of this private road which also serves as the
landscape setback for this development. There are also additional landscaped areas adjacent to
the buildings. A minimum of 5% landscaping is required for each site, and the landscaping must

GP-5-2025
West Valley City
Adopt an update to the General Plan Map and the Land Use Element of the General Plan

City staff is proposing an update to the General Plan Map and the Land Use Element of the
General Plan. While the General Plan Map has had periodic changes since its last comprehensive
update in 2015, the Land Use Element has not changed since 2015. A summary of the changes
and the reasons behind them is provided below in two parts – Part 1: General Plan Map and Part
2: Land Use Element.

Part 1: General Plan Map

The changes to the General Plan Map fall into one of the following five categories:

   •   The General Plan Map was amended to align with the recently adopted update to the
       Fairbourne Station Vision (the Station Area Plan for the West Valley Central Station) and
       the adopted new Station Area Plans for the River Trail, Redwood Junction, and Decker
       Lake Stations. Instead of users having to refer to the Station Area Plan documents to
       learn the City’s desire for future land use, the General Plan Map has been synced with the
       future land use reflected in these documents.
   •   The General Plan Map was updated to reflect current land uses and/or zoning that is
       unlikely to change in the future. Examples include having existing charter schools be
       designated as Community uses and having existing smaller lot (less than 8,000 square
       feet) subdivisions be designated as Small Lot Residential.
   •   The City retained Leland Consulting to identify potential development and
       redevelopment sites along 3500 South and Redwood Road and to suggest possible future
       development scenarios for each site. Leland’s development scenarios were presented to
       both the Planning Commission and City Council for their input over a series of meetings.
       The proposed General Plan Map incorporates the feedback from the Commission and
       Council where it differed from the current General Plan Map.
   •   There were several instances where staff anticipates new uses that were incorporated into
       the proposed General Plan Map. These instances are described below:
           o The Rocky Mountain Power transmission corridor between Parkway Blvd. and I-
               215 was changed from Utility to Parks and Open Spaces. The City has had
               conversations with Rocky Mountain Power about utilizing this portion of the
               power corridor as a future park.
           o The City-owned property just west of Mountain View Corridor and south of
               Parkway Blvd was changed from Large Lot Residential to mostly Parks and Open
               Space with Medium Density Residential along the west side of the property. Most
               of the property is impacted by a UDOT held easement that prevents buildings
               given the storm water detention use, hence the Parks and Open Space designation.
               The Medium Density Residential designation on the west side matches the
               designation to the west and could support the expansion of the senior condo
               project to the west.
           o The north portion of the properties west of Mountain View Corridor and on the
               north side of 3500 South were changed from Large Lot Residential to Low

              Low Density Residential and rezoned to R-1-8 and the remainder of the properties

Part 2: Land Use Element

The current Land Use Element addresses opportunity areas or areas of the City that, in 2015,
were deemed most likely to experience change in the time horizon of the General Plan. Those
opportunity areas are Redwood Road, Northwest, Metro Business Park, Decker Lake, 5600
West, 3500 South, and 4100 South.

The proposed Land Use Element addresses a shortened list of opportunity areas – Southwest and
the major arterials of 3500 South and Redwood Road. Northwest, 5600 West, and 4100 South
were removed as many of the anticipated changes in the current Land Use Element have already
occurred. Metro Business Park and Decker Lake were removed as these areas are now addressed
in the recently adopted Station Area Plans. The new Southwest opportunity area was added
primarily due to the property swap that occurred between Northrop Grumman and Kennecott
where Kennecott now owns approximately 1,000 acres of developable property.

The proposed Land Use Element also addresses a State code requirement (subsection 2aiB of
Section 10-9a-403) where cities must include a statement of the projections on population given
the various land use categories covered by the plan.

By way of information, the City recently solicited proposals from consulting firms to undertake a

   1. Approval. The update to the General Plan Map and the Land Use Element of the General
