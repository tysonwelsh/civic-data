5.         25-364                 Interlocal Agreement between Sandy City and the Governor's Office of
                                  Economic Opportunity
                   Attachments:   Staff Report
                                  GOEO Sandy Interlocal Agreement
                                  Memorandum of Understanding LHM

                                  Kasey Dunlavy, Economic Development Director, presented an Interlocal Cooperation
                                  Agreement between Sandy City and the Governor's Office of Economic Opportunity
                                  ("GOEO"). This agreement allows the City to receive and utilize transient room
                                  tax-related revenues pursuant to Utah Code. These funds function as a pass-through to
                                  the Larry H. Miller Company for future stadium and infrastructure improvements at
                                  America First Field. The funds will go towards the design, renovation and construction of
                                  necessary upgrades of the 20 year old stadium located in Sandy City. Council questions
                                  followed. This is an information item and will come back to the Council at a future
                                  meeting for consideration and action.

6.         25-368                 Police Department providing the Council with a presentation about policing
                                  in the Historic Sandy neighborhood
                   Attachments:   Historic Sandy Crime Statistics

                                  Jon Arnold, Interim Police Chief and Deputy Police Chief Cory Hess presented and
                                  reviewed crime statistics in Historic Sandy over the past five years. Crime in this area is
                                  dropping. Community involvement has been a key factor in the drop in theft crimes
                                  committed in this area. Police involvement and engagement, a greater outreach through
                                  social media and neighborhood watch groups have all contributed to lower crime in the
                                  area. They also spoke about the police involvement, specifically Detective Worwood, in
                                  the metro gang taskforce and the significant role he played in breaking up gangs in
                                  Historic Sandy area. Moving forward, the Police Department will continue to increase
                                  communication with the residents of the Historic Sandy area, have added camera
                                  surveillance in the area and will continue to work with the residents to address crime in
                                  the area. Council thanked the Police Department for their ongoing efforts on behalf of
                                  Sandy and Historic Sandy residents.

7.         25-369                 Public Hearing Regarding the Sale of Certain Real Property (Old Sandy
                                  City Fire Station, Hale Centre Theatre and Plaza, 500 W Industrial Corner,
                                  and Monroe Street Lot 3)
                   Attachments:   Property Disposition Details
                                  Draft Plaza Resolution

                                  Public Hearing: Dan Nelson, Real Property Manager for Sandy City, presented this item
                                  to the Council in a Public Hearing. No action from the Council is required. Mr. Nelson
                                  provided information and details on the proposed disposition of five Sandy City properties:

                                  1. Old Sandy City Fire Station located at 9010 South 150 East
                                  2. Hale Centre Theatre (HCT) and Plaza located at 9900 Monroe Street (2 parcels)
                                  3. Parcel located at 9392 South 500 West
                                  4. Lot 3 located at 9326 S. Monroe Plaza Way

                                  Mr. Nelson provided additional details regarding the disposition of the Hale Centre Theatre
                                  Plaza and resolution to close the Plaza for Public Use. Hale Centre Theatre is in the
                                  process of purchasing the Theatre, which includes two parcels: the theatre parcel and the
                                  adjacent plaza parcel. The City has determined the Plaza parcel is surplus, and must
                                  vacate the dedication of the Plaza for public use in order to complete the sale. HCT is
                                  paying off the bond obligation early and accelerating their purchase of the theatre and
                                  plaza. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Public Hearing comment period opened.

                                  Brooke Christensen expressed support for HCT. This is a perfect example of how a
                                  private-public partnership should work.

                                  Steve Van Maren expressed concern regarding the $2 million in funds the City used to
                                  build the Plaza and spoke about the sale of the Plaza parcel.

                                  Public hearing comment period closed.

                    most charitable state in the country. He also spoke about the need to address campaign
                    finance reporting criteria and oversight. He made a motion to add this item to the council
                    agenda for next week. There was not second to the motion and the motion failed. Many
                    Council Members expressed a desire to review and address this topic. Given that only
                    one Council meeting remained for the year, they felt this topic should be addressed by
                    the incoming 2026 council members. The Chair said the item was already placed on the
                    agenda planning calendar and is planned to be discussed in January at the meeting on
                    the 27th.
