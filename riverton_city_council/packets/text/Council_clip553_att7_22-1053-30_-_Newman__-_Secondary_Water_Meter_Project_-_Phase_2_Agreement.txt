1.0   GENERAL

1.1   The work to be performed to be performed under this project shall consist of furnishing all labor,
      materials, and equipment necessary or required to complete the work in all respects as shown in
      the City’s Standard Details and Specifications and as herein specified. All work, materials, and
      services not expressly shown or called for in the Contract Documents which may be necessary to
      complete the construction of the work in good faith shall be performed, furnished, and installed by
      CONTRACTOR as though originally so specified or shown, at no increase to OWNER. Davis-
      Bacon Act Compliance is required, see attached.

1.2   CONTRACT DESCRIPTION
      A. The project will consist of the installation of the indicated number of OWNER furnished 1”, 1
         ½” & 2” Neptune Mach-10 meters with R900i Pit Register and Antenna in the Project Areas of
         the City. The Project Areas are indicated and shown on the attached map included in this
         package. Additional detail for each Project Area (Project Areas 1 through 8) are also shown
         at a larger scale in the additional attachments to this package. Installation of meters will only
         be required at locations where stop-and-waste valves exist. The CITY will provide mapping
         for the location of each stop-and-waste valve, and will mark with purple paint on the ground of
         each residence the location of the existing stop-and-waste valves to be replaced with this
         new meter assembly and installation. The installation will require removal and replacement of
         the existing stop-and-waste valve, furnishing and installing a new stop valve, a compression
         by meter swivel, CTS inserts (minimum of 2), a 21-inch diameter plastic meter box with ring
         and lid, CTS purple poly pipe as needed, and other miscellaneous items to complete the
         work. Restoration of landscaping and/or surface conditions to as good, if not better,
         conditions than the existing conditions, is also a requirement of this project.
      B. Only with the OWNER’S approval shall the CONTRACTOR relocate the new meter to a
         location more than 2 feet from the location of the existing stop-and-waste valve.
      C. CONTRACTOR is responsible to put a message board in the area a week before
         onsite work is done.
      D. CONTRACTOR is responsible to provide a flyer/door hanger to all impacted residents. The
         door hanger shall be provided to the residences 24 hours before work may commence at the
         site. Flyer/door hanger shall also contain the CONTRACTOR’s name and phone number
         and will ask the check the sprinkler system for debris in the line. The flyer/door hanger shall
         be submitted and pre-approved by the CITY before commencing work. Contractor must
         document proof of door hanger being hung.

1.3   CONTRACTOR’S USE OF SITE
      A. CONTRACTOR’s use of each residential project site shall be limited to its construction
         operations, including on-site storage of materials and on-site fabrication facilities.
         CONTRACTOR shall clean up each day and not keep a residence/property owner out of
         water for more than a 24 hour period. All landscaping shall be completed within 48 hours.
         CONTRACTOR’s employees are required to wear ID Badges while on-site and are obligated
         to wear orange vests.
      B. Storm water BMP’s traffic and pedestrian control signage will be required if materials are
         stored in the CITY road right-of-way.

1.4   OWNER FURNISHED MATERIALS
      A. The OWNER shall furnish to CONTRACTOR all meters and RFs for installation.
         TheCONTRACTOR, after acceptance of the meter and RF, will be responsible for
         safeguarding, storing, and otherwise keeping meters free from damage or theft.
         Meters and RFs will both have matching labels that will be required to be installed
         at the same location. Should a meter be damaged, stolen or lost, CONTRACTOR
         shall be responsible for the replacement of the meter and/or RF.
      B. The OWNER shall provide to the CONTRACTOR the meters in bulk packaging by pallet (96
         meters per pallet) on a weekly basis. The CONTRACTOR shall determine each week how
         many meters they anticipate to install for the coming week(s) and shall pick up the meters
         from the Riverton City Water Department located at 3323 Sanborn Drive (13590 South),
         Riverton, Utah, between the hours of 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, each Tuesday morning. The
         entire pallet of meters shall be assigned and become the responsibility of the
         CONTRACTOR.

1.5   WORK SEQUENCE
      A. The contract time for substantial and final completion is as indicated in the Terms &
         Conditions, and is on a set date basis commencing from the date of the Notice to Proceed.
      B. Specific work sequence is provided below:
         1. OWNER shall provide a description and figure that identifies location of stop-and-waste
            valves, where meters are to be installed, within a given PROJECT AREA.
         2. OWNER shall then provide for CONTRACTOR to take possession of the meters
            (provided by the OWNER) for installation. CONTRACTOR will sign acceptance form for
            the furnished meters.
         3. CONTRACTOR shall install meters per specifications, including documenting the meter
            identification (ID #) and radio frequency (RF #) to an address.
         4. CONTRACTOR is to make sure the meter stop valve is in the off position after
            installing the meter. When water main is ready to be re-loaded the City will operate all
            main line valves, flush pipe to clean and remove all air. After City Operator determines
            that all air is out and water is clean for operation they will pressurize the pipe. At that
            time the CONTRACTOR will go and turn on all meter stop valves to check for leaks
            and to provide the residents with secondary water service.
         5. If the meter installation requires more work than described in Base Bid, CONTRACTOR
            shall request the City Representative who will document issues and direct/approve
            CONTRACTOR’S additional work.
         6. Any meter location that requires disturbance of the surroundings shall be photographed
            by the CONTRACTOR prior to any work to document existing conditions at the site. The
            photographs shall be identified and provided to the CITY.
         7. CONTRACTOR shall restore landscaping and/or surface conditions, to as good, if
            not better conditions from the existing conditions.
         8. Upon completion of each meter installation, CONTRACTOR shall request inspection by a
            CITY Representative. CITY Representative shall inspect and approve installation for the
            meter installation and any landscaping repairs. If deficiencies are found, CONTRACTOR
            shall correct items within 48 hours.
         9. Upon acceptance of meter installation by CITY, CONTRACTOR shall progress to the
            next installation.

1.6   WORK RESTRICTIONS
      A. Work hours shall be Monday through Saturday 7 AM to 7 PM. Work outside of these times
         shall be pre-approved prior to any work being done.

The following Table lists the 8 Project Areas that have been defined with a close approximation
of the total number of meters to be installed in each of the areas. The “Base Bid” for each of

categories depending on the depth the meters are to be installed. For purposes of this Bid, the
quantity of meters at each of these three depths was “assumed” so when it comes to actual
construction, the number of meters installed at a specific depth may vary significantly, However,
the total number of meters to be installed per Project Area will not vary by more than 5 or 10
percent.

With this in mind, the three depth categories are defined as: 1.) from zero to 24-inches deep are
considered “Shallow depth Meters”, and are the base bid amount; 2.) from 24-inches through 36-
inches are considered “Medium depth Meters”, are priced as an “adder price” for the additional
12-inches; and 3.) from 36-inches deep and deeper are considered “Deep depth Meters”, are
priced as an “adder price” for the additional 24-inches plus. A Standard Drawing and Detail for
each of these defined depths is attached with this Bid Package and shows the details of
installation with required equipment, parts, and labor requirements that will be required for the
installation of the meters. The Detail Drawings for depths are titled M-1, M-2, and M-3 (all
showing a 1” meter) and Detail Drawings for Meter Sizes are titled SW6, SW-7 & SW-8.
