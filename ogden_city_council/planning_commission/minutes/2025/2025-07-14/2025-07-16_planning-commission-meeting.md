1. Harrison Boulevard Station Area, Plan Discussion

Page 1

Presenters McKayla Dunley, Delaney Sillman and Jake Young, all via Zoom, presented the
Harrison Boulevard Station area plan displaying slides focusing on implementation, vision and
framework, state requirements and future land use maps with a variety of discussion on improving
current crossings and pedestrian access, parking, integrating efficiency and tree lines, etc.

As there was no additional business to come before the Commission, COMMISSIONER
