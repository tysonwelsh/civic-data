                           AGREEMENT FOR EMPLOYMENT
        THIS AGREEMENT (“Agreement") is entered between Riverton City, a Utah municipal
corporation ("City"), and Nicholas Geer, an individual ("Employee"). The City and Employee
are jointly referred to in this Agreement as the "Parties".


                                           RECITALS
      A.     The City desires to employ the services of Employee as the Administrative
             Services Director, as described by a Job Description published by Riverton City.
             Employee desires to be employed by City as Administrative Services Director.
      B.     The City believes the appointment of Employee will improve and enhance the
             effectiveness and efficiency of administrative and finance operations for the City.
      C.     It is the desire of the City to:
             1.      Secure and retain the services of Employee and to provide inducement for
                     him to accept a position with the City, and remain in such employment,
             2.      To make possible full work productivity by assuring to Employee high
                     morale and peace of mind with respect to future employment security,
             3.      To establish the employment expectations of the City for Employee,
             4.      To establish a just means of compensation and benefits for the
                     employment of Employee; and
             5.      To provide a just means of compensation for the separation of Employee
                     from the City's employment.


                                                TERMS


SECTION 1. APPOINTMENT AND DUTIES.


1.1   This Agreement shall become effective ("Effective Date") June ____, 2024.
1.2   Employee shall be employed as the Administrative Services Director, reporting directly
      to the City Manager. He shall carry out such duties and functions as set forth by the City
      Manager and as required in State and Federal Law, City Ordinances, City Job Description
      and City Policy. Employee shall perform his function and duties on a full-time basis,
      shall use his best efforts to lawfully implement executive orders of the Mayor and City
      Manager; the policy decisions of the City; and shall comply with provisions of the
      Riverton City Employee Policies and Procedures Manual ("Employee Manual"); and
      other policies of the Riverton Police Department.

                                                  1
SECTION 2. DURATION AND AMENDMENT
2.1   This Agreement may be amended at any time only upon mutual written agreement of the
      Parties.
2.2   Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent, limit, or otherwise interfere with the right of the
      City to terminate the services of Employee upon Employee’s receipt of written notice
      from the Riverton City Mayor.
2.3   Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent, limit, or otherwise interfere with the right of
      Employee to resign his position with the City upon twenty-one (21) days' notice to the
      City, subject to the provisions of this Agreement.


SECTION 3. EMPLOYMENT STATUS AND INDEMNIFICATION.
3.1   Employee shall be considered an at will employee of the City, subject to Section 2.2 of
      this Agreement, and not an independent contractor. Employee acknowledges and agrees
      that the position of Administrative Services Director equates to a head of a municipal
      department or division, and is therefore considered an at will employee pursuant to Utah
      Code §10-3-1105. It is understood that such status entitles Employee to statutory benefits
      such as worker's compensation and unemployment compensation coverage as well as all
      other benefits available to all City employees generally, as further described in the in this
      Agreement or in the Employee Manual.
3.2   The City shall defend, hold harmless, and indemnify Employee against any action
      brought against him arising from an act or omission occurring during the performance of
      Employee's duties, within the scope of employee's employment, and under color of
      authority in accordance with the Utah Governmental Immunity Act and other applicable
      law (“City's Duty”). To the extent the City's Duty is invoked per this provision, the City
      shall have the exclusive right to manage any litigation associated with any action brought
      against Employee; and the City shall have the exclusive right to compromise or settle any
      claim or suit. Employee shall not have veto authority over such settlement or
      compromise. The City shall keep Employee advised of the progress of the claim or suit
      and shall advise Employee in advance of any agreed settlement.
3.3   Employee may engage in outside employment or other commercial or professional
      activities in addition to his employment with the City, based upon the following
      conditions:
      A.     Any outside employment or other commercial or professional activities in
             addition to Employee’s employment with the City shall be conducted in full
             compliance with Riverton City Code. In addition, any employment or other
             commercial or professional activities in addition to Employee’s employment with
             the City cannot be undertaken without the prior written approval by the City
             Manager or designee, whose approval shall be given or denied in accordance with

                                                2
             Riverton City Code. Employee agrees to remain apprised of Riverton City Code,
             particularly Section 2.120.060 or its successor, when considering whether to seek
             outside employment.
      B.     Employee (i) is available to perform the full extent of Employee’s responsibilities
             as required by the job description as currently approved or as amended subsequent
             to the Effective Date, and applicable laws, rules, and policies; (ii) does not engage
             in any activity that would have a negative or adverse impact on the City; (iii) does
             not engage in any activity that would conflict with employee's duties as described
             in Employee’s job description; and (iv) does not engage in any activity that would
             violate any policy, procedure, or applicable City, state, or federal law as relates to
             employee's duties as an employee of Riverton City.
      C.     Employee files and maintains a current, approved outside employment form as
             promulgated by the City.


SECTION 4. COMPENSATION.
4.1   All compensation and payments described in this paragraph are referred to as "Salary”.
      The City shall compensate Employee for his services, a salary as adopted annually in the
      City's fiscal year Budget, and beginning at the rate of One Hundred Forty-five Thousand
      Dollars and No Cents ($145,000.00) per year, which equates to $69.71 per hour. In
      addition. Employee shall also be eligible for salary increases, whether annual or
      otherwise, not less than the minimum provided to any other full-time employee of the
      City, subject to the conditions for such increases applicable to other full-time employees.
      Following commencement of employment, Employee will be eligible for his first salary
      increase on July 1, 2025.


SECTION 5. BENEFITS.
5.1   In addition to Salary, employment benefits (“Benefits”) provided to Employee by the
      City shall include but not be limited to the following:
      A.     Insurance coverage available to all City employees generally including Disability,
             Life, Medical, and Dental,
      B.     Vacation Leave available to all City employees generally, as described under the
             Employee Manual,
      C.     Sick Leave available to all City employees generally as described under the
             Employee Manual,
      D.     Personal leave available to all City employees generally as described under the
             Employee Manual, and


                                               3
      E.     Cellular phone allowance, or City provided cell phone in accordance with existing
             City policy described in the Employee Manual.
5.2   City will Provide Employee with 80 hours of vacation leave and 40 hours of sick leave
      upon Employee’s arrival to his first day of work for the City. Execution of this
      Agreement. Leave provided to Employee under this subsection shall not be deducted
      from leave accruals owing to employee as described under the Employee Manual.
      Vacation Leave, Sick Leave, and Personal Leave, are jointly referred to as “Paid Leave”.
      Paid Leave shall continue to accrue to Employee as provided under the Employee
      Manual. Except as otherwise provided in Section 7 of this Agreement, any accrued Paid
      Leave shall be used or paid to employee in a cash equivalent as provided under the
      Employee Manual. Employee is not entitled to payment in cash equivalent any accrued
      leave under this Agreement, unless said payment is otherwise allowed under the
      Employee Manual.
5.3   Should there be a conflict in provisions, decisions, or actions regarding benefits provided
      for in: (i) the Employee Manual; (ii) other documents in existence on the Effective Date;
      (iii) documents or actions promulgated by the City for all employees generally on or after
      the Effective Date; or (iv) this Agreement, this Agreement shall govern and be considered
      a contractual right of Employee and a contractual obligation of the City.


SECTION 6. PROFESSIONAL PARTICIPATION AND EXPENSES
6.1   The Parties agree that to effectively perform the duties and expectations of his
      employment position, Employee must be able to access appropriate job-related
      information and be reasonably available and accessible to the City when issues and
      events occur or arise either outside regular City business hours or when Employee is not
      present on City premises. The Parties acknowledge that to accomplish such, the City shall
      provide appropriate technology resources, including a smartphone allowance and a tablet
      for Employee's use as set forth in the Employee Manual.
6.2   The City recognizes that "out-of-pocket" expenses of a general job affiliated nature will
      be incurred by Employee, and the City agrees to reimburse or to pay said expenses to
      Employee. Said reimbursement shall be upon receipt by the City of executed expense or
      petty cash vouchers, receipts, statements, or personal affidavits as required by applicable
      City Code and/or City Policy.


SECTION 7. TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE.
7.1   Termination of this Agreement by the City (“City Termination”) occurs upon any of the
      following:
      A.     The City terminates Employee's employment with the City for any reason other
             than Good Cause. For purposes of this Agreement, “Good Cause” shall be

                                               4
             defined as and be limited to: 1) refusal to carry out a city-related assignment or
             order of the Mayor or City Manager which does not violate Utah statutory law,
             the Riverton Municipal Code, or any resolutions or policies adopted by the
             Riverton City Council; 2) commission of a public offense which is punishable as
             a class B misdemeanor and is not related to traffic, except for reckless driving or
             driving under the influence of alcohol, a controlled substance or a controlled
             substance metabolite; or 3) commission of any public offence, whether related to
             traffic regulations or not, which is punishable as a class A misdemeanor or a
             felony; and 4) willful violation of a policy or ordinance Riverton City. Further,
             termination for cause may only occur after Employer has notified Employee in
             writing of the basis of the proposed termination for Good Cause and allowed
             Employee seven business days to cure such basis. In the event Employee is
             charged with a public offense which if proven to be true constitutes “Good
             Cause” as defined herein, Employee may be placed on administrative leave by the
             City Manager as the matter is adjudicated.
      B.     The City is in default of any of the provisions of this Agreement and fails to cure
             the default within ten (10) days from receipt of written notice of such default,
      C.     Employee resigns from employment with the City following a request from the
             City Manager that Employee resign his position unless such request is based upon
             Good Cause,
      D.     The City reduces the Salary or Benefits of Employee in a greater percentage than
             an applicable across-the-board reduction for all employees of the City.
7.2   In the event of a City termination, other than for Good Cause, the City shall provide the
      following:
      A.     A payment to Employee in an amount equal to: (i) Employee’s regularly earned
             unpaid Salary accrued to the date of City Termination; and (ii) the balance of
             Employee's regularly accrued unused Vacation Leave: and
      B.     A payment to Employee equal to the total value of six (6) months of: (i) Salary;
             (ii) family health insurance premium available through the City's health insurance
             benefit program; and (iii) family dental insurance premium available through the
             City's dental insurance benefit program, all as then valued at the date of a City
             Termination. The payment described in this subsection purposefully excludes a
             contribution to Employee’s retirement benefits. Employee’s payment shall not be
             construed to toll employee’s date of termination by an additional six month
             employment period. The use of six months equivalent salary, medical and dental,
             is used solely for the purpose of calculating the cash value of the termination
             payment described in this subsection.
7.3   In the event Employee at any time either: (i) is terminated for Good Cause; or (ii)
      voluntarily resigns or retires from his employment with the City and the resignation is not

                                               5
      the result of a City Termination; then Employee shall only be entitled to the
      compensation outlined in section 7.2A of this Agreement.

SECTION 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS.
9.1   The terms herein shall constitute the entire Agreement between the parties and any
      changes shall be reduced to writing and agreed upon by the Parties.
9.2   This Agreement shall be binding upon the Parties and inure to the benefit of the heirs at
      law and executors of Employee.
9.3   This Agreement shall become effective as noted above.
9.4   The Parties presume and intend that this Agreement is consistent with and complimentary
      to provisions of the Riverton City Code, the Employee Manual, and other policies
      applicable to management employees of the City, as of the date the Effective Date and
      intend that the provisions of such documents be interpreted as consistent and
      complimentary, where lawfully possible.
9.5   If any provision, or any portion thereof contained in this Agreement is held
      unconstitutional, invalid, or unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement or portion
      thereof shall be deemed severable, shall not be affected, and shall remain in full force and
      effect.
9.6   In the event of any controversy, claim or dispute between the parties hereto, arising out of
      or in any manner relating to this Agreement, including an attempt to rescind or set aside
