BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Text Amendment to the CSD-Highline zoning
district located on the southeast corner of Future Way and Highland Dr., at approximately
14761 South Future Way (Exhibit B). The Text Amendment will specially affect the listed
uses on 6.5 acres of property (Subject Property) within the CSD-Highline zoned area (Exhibit
C). The applicant is requesting that a Text Amendment be approved to allow for an office
development on the property.

In September 2016, the City Council approved Ordinance No. 1215 creating the CSD-
Highline zone. The creation of this zoning district was so that a mix of uses could be
located on the 70.76 acre site including retail, office and multifamily. So far one office
building, Pluralsight, and one multi-family residential development, Point of View
Apartments, has been constructed within the zone.

According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of a CSD
zone is to “permit a compatible, master planned mix of day and evening uses consisting of
office, light manufacturing, retail, residential, recreation and open space components. Although
development size and use mix will vary from location to location, each development is intended
to consist of well designed, architecturally integrated structures which are appropriately

Manufacturing) zone and R3 (Single-family Residential, 13,000 square foot lot) zone to the
north, the RM2 (Multi-family Residential, up to 12 dwelling units per acre) zone and the CR
(Regional Commercial) zone to the east. To the South is the CR zone, and to the west is the
M1 zone.

Text Amendment. The applicant is proposing to update the Exhibit A to the CSD-Highline text
(Exhibit F). This will impact DCMC Section 9-18K-090. The update will include new site plan,
pedestrian connection, and amenities plans showing a new layout for the Subject Property.
It will also include an updated zoning plan, which calls out what types of uses are allowed
on the property. The table below outlines the use category changes being proposed.

   Property ID           Existing Land Use Description   Proposed Land Use Description
   B                     Office/ Retail                  Office/ Retail
   C                     Residential/ Retail             Residential/ Retail/ Office/ Hotel

There are no proposed changes to the allowed building heights or standards within the
zone. The zoning district allows for office buildings between 4 stories and 15 stories, and
residential uses with a maximum of 10 stories. The applicant is proposing a five story
office building on the property.

Criteria For Approval.
A Text and Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City
Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation
to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in
Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

        2. Text Amendments:
             a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies
                 of the city's general plan;
             b. Whether a proposed amendment furthers the specific purpose statements of the
                 zoning ordinance;
             c. Whether the proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request
                 and there is sufficient justification for a modification to the zoning ordinance;
             d. The proposed amendment will not create a conflict with any other section or part
                 of this title or the general plan;
             e. Whether the potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated
                 and determined not to be detrimental to public health, safety, or welfare and
                 represents an overall community benefit; and
             f. The extent to which a proposed text amendment implements best current,
                 professional practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices.
