Tentative Budget

             Council Member Clayton explained the proposed budget but stated there is not
enough information available now. However, in the future there should be more information

available to make a tighter budget. Next year he would like to introduce a non-capital
projects/rainy day fund. He is working to separate Copperton and cemetery insurance.
Copperton’s insurance is $8,000 per year because it includes the cemetery. Additional savings

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) Budget

       Property Tax

               Council Member Clayton stated there will be no property tax this year from the
MSD but there will likely be a push next year. He will oppose increases until more information is
provided to justify the proposed increases. The GSLMSD also needs to discuss potential
increases earlier than October and have public meetings and other outreach.

       Storm Water Fee

                Council Member Clayton stated the stormwater fee pared down to $8.00 per
residential unit and pushed back to July 1, 2019. The GSLMSD is having a problem finding
someone to put it on their bill. That will be voted on at the metro township level rather than at the
GSLMSD level.

               Council Member Bailey asked about the Council budget covering the fee if it was
not charged to residents.

               Council Member Clayton stated it would come out of Copperton’s budget. There
is room in the budget now.

Planning Commission

              Residential Compatibility Design Standards Ordinance

                Curtis Woodward stated he has revised the prior proposal to focus on
construction of new homes within the historic overlay. He started with the Salt Lake County’s
Residential Compatibility Overlay Zone that had been used in Millcreek and then modified it. It
has been written to apply to all of Copperton Metro Township with a few basic elements to cover
architectural compatibility. He also kept the three-tiered approach.

             Council Member Clayton stated the Council will discuss this at the next meeting
and have a public hearing in January. The Council will review the draft and ask questions of Mr.
Woodward.
