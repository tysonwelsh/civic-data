3. Conditional Use water tank addition, 4826 Old Post Rd
Petitioner Moreno stated that they found a number of voids under the current 4.3-million-
gallon tank, and that it had also reached the end of its design life. So, they feel it is time to
replace it with a 5-million-gallon tank to accommodate the increased population.
Planner Damian Rodriguez noted that with all the parcels that comprise the property it is 7.2
acres. Staff finds that the five criteria to consider with a conditional use permit amendment
are being met and recommends approval of the proposed conditional use permit amendment,
subject to the following two conditions; the various parcels that comprise the water treatment
plant be combined via an unplanted parcel combination review with the city, and appropriate
SWPP measures be implemented during future site work and any necessary SWPP permits be
obtained per state and local regulations.

COMISSIONER AABERG MOVED TO APPROVE THE CONDITIONAL USE AMENDMENT
SUBJECT TO THE TWO RECOMMENDED CONDITIONS. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY
COMMISSIONER SANDAU AND PASSED UPON BY THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE:
VOTING AYE — COMMISSIONERS AABERG, AKHMEDOV, ROSS, SANDAU, SHALE, SHINODA,
SOUTHWICK, AND WILLIAMS. VOTING NO — NONE

5. Preliminary Group Dwelling CUP, The Village at the Gardens, 81townhome units, 397 & 399
Harrisville Rd
Petitioner Bryan Smith proposed an 81-unit townhome community. They agree with Plannings
recommendations and plan on implementing great amenities with the goal of a nice
community.
Planner Damian Rodriguez said that the subject property is located on two different parcels
right now, 397 and also 399 Harrisville Rd with a cumulative lot size of 4.9 acres (property lines
and plat map shown in slides). The elevations and floor plans do lack features and
architectural variety that is required by code, and because of which, among other things, Staff
recommends approval upon what was enumerated on the slides and the provided staff report,
and the following conditions; that proposed building elevations and floor plans are amended to
provide the amenities and design features that are required by code, that the site plan and
landscape plan are amended to comply with the development and landscaping standards of
the ordinance, an irrigation plan and tree preservation plan are provided for staff and
commission review prior to final approval, and lastly, that the project is subdivided to allow for
individual ownership of each unit.

110 units and a trail side restaurant. They have been working on this project for quite a while

subdivision phase.
Assistant Planning Director Joe Simpson led with the project having already been approved by
the City Council, and at this time they were focusing on the subdivision and combining parcels.
The project is proposing to split the property up into three lots, so each building would be on
its own lot. In review of the corrections report, basically there are some verbiage and

7. Public Hearing, Zoning Ordinance Amendment, maximum turf requirements of new single-
family dwellings
Planning Director Barton Brierley informed of the text amendment for new single-family
dwelling water-wise landscaping requirements to accommodate the moderate drought
conditions and address our water infrastructure. Ogden City has worked with the State and
came up with a plan for a grant agreement for $10 million from the Division of Water
Resources with conditions that the water we're saving will go back to Willard Spur and the
Great Salt Lake to provide improved habitat in those areas, and to adopt a water efficient
landscape ordinance of no more than 35% of the landscaped area in the front and side yard be
natural grass.
