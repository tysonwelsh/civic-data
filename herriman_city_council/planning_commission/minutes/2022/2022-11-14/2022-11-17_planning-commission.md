and interested citizens.

4.3. Consideration of a Preliminary Plat for the Axia Business Park Subdivision at 15880 S
Redwood Road in the M-1 (Manufacturing) Zone. (Public Hearing)
Applicant: Chase Hudson (authorized agent)
File No: $2022-123
Acres: 21.12
Planning Manager Spencer explained this request was for preliminary approval of a four-lot
subdivision. Due to the topography and the location of the towers, it had been difficult to design the
right-of-way. Based on this hardship, a deviation to the standard had been approved. As part of the
deviation, the applicant proposed a five-foot wide landscaped buffer on the west side of the trail
adjacent to lot three and four. Maintenance would be required by the property owner to avoid
nuisances and maintain the landscaping.
Applicant Chase Hudson expressed appreciation to the Commission for hearing the request and noted
he was available to answer questions. The Commission asked if combining the parcels was considered.
Applicant Hudson responded in the affirmative.
Vice Chair Powell opened the public hearing.
There were no comments offered.
Vice Chair Powell closed the public hearing.
Commissioner Darryl Fenn observed the steep grade of the site and questioned if there were any sort
of erosion protection being put in place to keep downhill neighbors and roadways safe. Applicant
Hudson indicated they had been working closely with GSH Engineering, City staff, and the landscape
architect for mitigation efforts. He indicated the development would look more to natural vegetation
to accommodate those efforts.
Commissioner Adam Jacobson moved to approve item 4.3. approving the preliminary plat for the Axia
Business Park Subdivision located at 15880 South Redwood Road with the following conditions:
1. Receive and agree to the recommendation from other City departments
2. Maintain all lots nuisance free until time of development
Commissioner Jackson Ferguson seconded the motion.
The vote was recorded as follows:

the M-1 (Manufacturing) Zone.
Applicant: | Chase Hudson (authorized agent)
Acres: +18.08
File Number: C2022-124
Planning Manager Spencer reviewed the request to approve a conditional use permit to construct a
manufacturing and industrial development. The request was made with the following specifications:
e 242,768 square-foot building with dimensions being approximately 960’x260’
e 20,100 square-feet of the building would be designated for office use
e 212,668 square feet of the building would be designed for warehouse space
e 244 parking stalls would be installed
Planning Manager Spencer observed adverse effects relating to the development including traffic
patterns, connectivity for pedestrians and bicyclists, and environmental impacts such as noise and
odors. He spoke on the landscaping aspects of the development which would meet the design and
water efficiency standards. Planning Manager Spencer recommended approval with the following
conditions:
1. Engineering:
a. Complete the Floodplain Development Permit process with the City Engineering
department in order to comply with FEMA and NFIP regulation requirements
2. No signs are approved with this request
3. Verify landscaping numbers across plans
Commissioner Jacobson opined it appeared the drainage channel was being filled in off-site and
questioned if this was permissible. Discussion ensued between the Commission and staff debating
landscaping and fencing being incorporated into the project. Applicant Hudson indicated the team
had been working on landscaping with staff to come up with a visually appealing design which would
also complement the topography on the site. He touched on the drainage canal grading and explained
he had been working with the property owners to get a temporary easement access to install a
drainage pipe which would be beneficial to both properties. The Commission and applicant discussed
the anticipated landscaping plans for the development.
Commissioner Adam Jacobson moved to continue item 4.4 considering the conditional use permit for
Axia Business Park to a future meeting so the applicant and staff can address the drainage
improvements, landscaping, and fencing. Commissioner Darryl Fenn seconded the motion.

5.1. Consideration of amendments in Title 10 of the Herriman City Code to permit the
construction, maintenance, and use of trails on slopes equal to or greater than 30% in all
zoning districts and overlays in the City.

Applicant: Herriman City

File No: Z2022-168
City Attorney Todd Sheeran presented the public hearing to permit the construction, maintenance,
and use of trails. He observed the inconsistency found in the FR zone which do not permit the
construction of such trails on slopes with a grade of 30-percent or more. He looked to have this be
permissible in City Code.
Vice Chair Powell opened the public hearing.
There were no comments offered.
Vice Chair Powell closed the public hearing.
Commissioner Adam Jacobson moved to recommend approval to the City Council to amend Title 10 to
permit the construction, maintenance, and use of trails on slopes equal to or greater than 30-percent
in all zoning districts and overlays in the City. Commissioner Forrest Sickles seconded the motion.

File No: Z2022-169
