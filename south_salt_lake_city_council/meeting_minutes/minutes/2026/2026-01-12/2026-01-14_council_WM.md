   2. Affordable Housing Presentation
      Senior Project & Housing Manager, Steve Lyon, gave a presentation that went over the review of
      the City’s existing policy, progress updates, and next steps.

       Some of the strategic objectives include:
          a. Ensuring that the City can continue to be a welcoming and inclusive community for
              residents to put down roots and thrive.
          b. Provide opportunities for residents to remain in the community throughout their
              housing lifecycle.
          c. Enhancing the stability of neighborhoods by increasing high quality, attainable owner
              occupancy focused housing.

       A copy of the presentation is attached and incorporated by this reference.

   3. Aqui2194 Presentation & Code Amendment Review
      City Planner, Teresa Bagdasarova, along with dbURBAN co-founders, Dustin Holt and Bryce
      Baker, spoke about the upcoming development project that would bring 48 studio sized units to
      the City.

       A copy of the presentation is attached and incorporated by this reference.

   4. Fire Department Annual Report
      Chief Addison presented the annual report that showcases data collected from July 2024-June
      2025. The Fire Department received a total of 7,804 calls for service. 6,474 of those were
      medical related calls, and 1,330 were fire related calls.

       A copy of the presentation is attached and incorporated by this reference.

   5. Urban Forest Inventory & Canopy Study Presentation
      Neighborhoods Director, Sharen Hauri, gave a presentation that highlighted the City’s goal to
      increase its urban tree canopy, prevent hazards and problems, treat trees like infrastructure,
      and involve the community. These goals fall in alignment with the department’s strategic plan
      goals, ‘Improve Quality of Life’, and ‘Build Stronger Neighborhoods’.

       A copy of the presentation is attached and incorporated by this reference.
