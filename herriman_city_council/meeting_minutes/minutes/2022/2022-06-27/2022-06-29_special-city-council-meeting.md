                                SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL
                                      MINUTES
                                 Wednesday, June 29, 2022
                                 Approved August 10, 2022

The following are the minutes of the special City Council Meeting of the Herriman City Council.
The meeting was held on Wednesday, June 29, 2022, at 4:30 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Fort
Herriman Conference Room, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice
of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and
delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Lorin Palmer

Councilmembers Present: Teddy Hodges, Sherrie Ohrn, and Steve Shields

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski and City Recorder Jackie Nostrom

Councilmembers Excused: Jared Henderson

4:30 PM:
   1. Call to Order
Mayor Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 4:33 P.M. and noted Councilmember Steven
Shields would be late for the meeting and excused Councilmember Jared Henderson.

    2. Closed Session
          2.1. The Herriman City Council may temporarily recess the City Council meeting to
               convene in a closed session to discuss the character, professional competence, or
               physical or mental health of an individual, as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-
               4-205
Councilmember Ohrn moved temporarily recess the City Council meeting to convene in a closed
session to discuss the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an
individual, as provided by Utah Code Annotated 52-4-205 at 8:21 p.m. Councilmember Hodges
 June 29, 2022 – City Council Minutes                                            Page 2 of 3



seconded the motion.

 The vote was recorded as follows:
 Councilmember Jared Henderson Absent
 Councilmember Teddy Hodges        Aye
 Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn        Aye
 Councilmember Steven Shields      Absent
 Mayor Lorin Palmer                Aye

 The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Henderson and Councilmember Shields
 being absent.

 Councilmember Steve Shields arrived at 4:38 p.m.

 The Council reconvened the City Council work meeting at 5:58 p.m.

    3. Adjournment
 Councilmember Ohrn moved to adjourn the work meeting at 5:58 p.m. Councilmember Shields
 seconded the motion, and all voted aye.



I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a
true, accurate and complete record of the meeting held on June 29, 2022. This document constitutes the
official minutes for the special Herriman City Council Meeting.
