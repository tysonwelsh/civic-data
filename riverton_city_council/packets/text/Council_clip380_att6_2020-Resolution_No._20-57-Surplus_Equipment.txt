                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-57

   A RESOLUTION TO DECLARE RSG-1, RSD-3, PWT-2 AND PWB AS SURPLUS,
   AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO DISPOSE OF THESE ITEMS AS HE
                       DEEMS APPROPRIATE

       WHEREAS, the identified equipment has already been replaced and/or is no longer
needed; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to declare fixed assets that are no
longer needed as surplus in a public meeting.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council declares RSG-1, RSD-3, PWT-2 and PWB as surplus,
          authorizing the City Manager to dispose of these items as he deems appropriate.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 1st day of
September 2020 by the following vote:
