4.    SCHEDULED ITEMS
      4.1     ORDINANCE – Enacting a temporary land use regulation (moratorium), not to
              exceed six months in duration, pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-9a-504
              prohibiting the erection, construction, reconstruction, or alteration of any
              building or structure or any subdivision approval in the City Center District
              zones, the Hub District zones, the Canyon Crossing District zones, the Arts
              District zones and the North Village District zones, or in the alternative, enacting
              a temporary land use regulation establishing maximum building height limits,
              increasing parking requirements, increasing building setback requirements,
              prohibiting residential uses and/or enacting other development standards in the
              City Center District zones, Hub District zones, the Canyon Crossing District
              zones, the Arts District zones and the North Village District zones
