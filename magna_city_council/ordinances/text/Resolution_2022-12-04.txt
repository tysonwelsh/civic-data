                                MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO.: 22-12-04                                            DATE: December 13, 2022


            A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
               APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE 2023 MAGNA METRO
               TOWNSHIP COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING SCHEDULE


                                              RECITALS


      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and
       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (“Council”) is the municipal governing
body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-3b-501 et al; and
       WHEREAS, pursuant to the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act (the “Act”), the Council
must adopt and post an annual regular meeting schedule of the Council; and
       WHEREAS, the 2023 Magna Metro Township Council Regular Meeting Schedule,
hereinafter known as Attachment “A” fulfills the requirements of the Act; therefore be it

                                        RESOLUTION


       RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council, the Council approves and adopts
the attached 2023 Magna Metro Township Regular Meeting Schedule hereinafter to be known as
Attachment “A” to this resolution, for the benefit of Magna.



       APPROVED and ADOPTED in the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, State of
Utah on this 13th Day of December 2022.

                                                      MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
