Re: NCS Residential Development Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a plat amendment application for approximately 9.8 acres located at approximately 12197 S. 300 East. The property is currently zoned RM2.  The applicant is requesting a plat amendment to allow for a new 54 lot single-family residential subdivision.
The subject property has historically been utilized as agricultural or farming land since at least 1978 when the City incorporated.  There have been previous applications to rezone the property and the most recent one was in earlier this year.  An application in the fall 2020 was for a commercial zoning, and there were concerns expressed about that request from the public.  The applicant submitted a subsequent application earlier this year and the site was rezoned to RM2.  Based on concerns raised at the Planning Commission hearing for that rezone, a Development Agreement was proposed and adopted by the City Council. The findings are as follows: For approval:
