TITLE 10 AMENDMENTS TO THE ZONING CODE
WHEREAS, Nephi City Finds it is necessary and prudent to update it's Title 10 Zoning Code;
and
WHEREAS, Nephi City wishes to have minimum standards for lot size, frontage, setbacks,
height, and size regulations in all zones; and
WHEREAS, Nephi City wishes to update selected uses within certain zones; and
WHEREAS, Nephi City wishes to add a new residential zone with a minimum lot size of a halfacre that would afford animal rights;
NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained by the City Council of Nephi City, UT as follows:
SECTION 1: Repealer. If any provisions of the municipal code previously adopted are
inconsistent herewith they are hereby repealed.

In order that it may recommend approval of a planned residential development, the planning
commission shall have authority to require that the following conditions be met by the applicant:
A. Financial Ability Of Applicants: The proponents of the planned residential development have
demonstrated to the satisfaction of the planning commission their financial ability to comply with
regulations imposed on the proposed project pursuant to this title.
B. Time Limit: The proponents intend to start construction within one year of the approval of
the project, and any necessary zoning district change, and intend to complete said construction of all
phases within four (4) years from the date construction begins.
C. Conformity To General Plan: The proposed planned residential development conforms to the

b. Provide for proper entrance and exits and for internal traffic circulation and

SECTION 147: Amendment. Section "10.11.6 Reserved" is hereby amended as follows.
10.11.6 Scope Of Planning Commission ActionReserved
In carrying out the intent of this chapter, the planning commission shall consider the following
principles:
A. It is the intent of this chapter that site and building plans for a planned residential
development shall be prepared by a designer or team of designers having professional competence in
urban planning. The final development plan shall be prepared by a design team composed of an
architect, a landscape architect and an engineer or land surveyor, all licensed to practice in the state.
B. The planning commission, in carrying out this chapter, shall also observe such of the
principles stated under chapter 17, "Site Plan Review", of this title, as are appropriate to the review of
a planned residential development.
C. The planning commission may recommend to the legislative body the approval or disapproval
of an application for a planned residential development. In recommending approval of an application,
the planning commission may attach such conditions as it may deem necessary to secure compliance
with the purposes set forth in this title.

SECTION 148: Amendment. Section "10.11.7 Reserved" is hereby amended as follows.
10.11.7 Development RequirementsReserved
A PRD established under the provisions of this chapter shall conform to the following standards and
requirements:
A. Land Area: The minimum land area required for development of a PRD is four (4) acres.

Page 75, Ordinance 07-11-2023

B. Ownership: The development shall be in single or corporate ownership at the time of
application or the subject of an application filed jointly by all owners of the property.
C. Density: Density (dwelling units per net acre) within a PRD may exceed that permitted by the
underlying zone with which the PRD is combined up to a maximum of twenty percent (20%) at the
discretion of the planning commission and subject to concurrence of the city council. The city must
find, however, that any increase in density and/or decrease in setback requirements will be
compensated by increased amenity and improved design which, in its opinion, are proportional to the
density increase which it authorizes. (Net acreage is calculated using the total acreage less public and
private streets and parking lots.)
D. Building Location And Arrangement: The planning commission shall require that the
locations and arrangements of buildings on the lot should be accomplished in a manner that will best
utilize the lot area and create an attractive living environment and that ensures that adjacent
properties will not be adversely affected. The following exceptions shall be considered as minimum
requirements as they apply:
1. Permanent, landscaped setbacks at least twenty feet (20') in width shall be maintained
along the peripheral property lines of the PRD.
2. In those instances where a proposed PRD will front upon a public street, a permanent
landscaped setback, at least twenty five feet (25') in width, shall be required along the frontage of the
public street.
E. Single Ownership Or Control: The area proposed for the PRD shall be in one ownership
during development to provide for full supervision and control of said development and to ensure
conformance with these provisions and all conditions imposed by the development review committee,
planning commission and city council upon the preliminary and final development plans. However,
lots for single-family dwelling units may be sold; provided, that building begins within one year from
the date of sale, or if building does not begin within one year, said lot shall be landscaped and
maintained by the owner and/or homeowners' association.
F. Open Space: Not less than fifteen percent (15%) of the gross area of the PRD shall be retained
in permanent open space, parks and playgrounds for the use of the occupants of the PRD. Land
proposed to be devoted to vehicular streets or roads, parking, driveways, required setbacks and slopes
greater than twenty five percent (25%) shall not be included in computations of open space, park or
playground areas. The open space to be reserved shall be protected against building development by
conveying to the city an open space easement over such open areas, restricting the area against any
future building or use, except as consistent with approved or amended development plans. Buildings
or uses for noncommercial, recreational or cultural purposes, compatible with the open space
objectives and the underlying zone, may be permitted only with the express approval of the city
council, following review of building site and operational plans by the planning commission.
G. Landscaping Of Front And Side Yards: The required front and side yards which face upon a
public or a private street shall not be used for vehicular parking, but shall be landscaped with lawn
and appropriate plants and shrubs as indicated on approved final development plans.
H. Sewer And Water Supply; Underground Utilities: Public sewer and a public water supply shall
serve all dwelling units. All utilities within the PRD shall be placed underground, including telephone,

Page 76, Ordinance 07-11-2023

electrical and television cables. Dwelling units under separate ownership shall have separate utility
metering. Meters shall be located as specified by the city.
I. Off Street Parking: All off street parking shall comply with the provisions of chapter 16 of this
title. Any guest parking shall be located within reasonable walking distance (approximately 300 feet)
of the dwelling to be served.
J. Street Rights Of Way And Improvements: Dedication of street rights of way and improvement
of streets shall be made in accordance with the adopted street plan of the city, and further as may be
determined by the city to be necessary to serve the vehicular and pedestrian needs of the proposed
development of the city. Said streets shall conform to the standards set forth for various classes of
streets by the city. Streets within the development which are not specified in the adopted street plan
may, at the option of the city, be maintained in private ownership at a standard approved by the city
engineer, provided a system of perpetual maintenance is assured.
K. Landscaping: All areas not covered by buildings or by off street car parking, driveways and
alleyways shall be planted into lawn, trees and shrubs, and shall otherwise be landscaped and
maintained in accordance with good landscape practice. This is to be done within one year from the
date of final approval of the PRD.
L. Entrances: There shall be at least one hundred feet (100') between an entrance to the
development and a street intersection, and entrances shall not be closer than one hundred feet (100')
from each other.
M. Storage And Solid Waste: All storage and solid waste receptacles outside of the dwelling units
must be housed in a closed structure compatible with the design of the development.
N. Preservation Of Open Space: Preservation, maintenance and ownership of required open
space within the development shall be accomplished by:
1. Dedication of the land as a public park or parkway system if accepted by the city; or
2. Granting to the city a permanent open space easement on and over the said private open
spaces to guarantee that the open space remains perpetually in recreational use, with ownership and
maintenance being the responsibility of a homeowners' association established with articles of
association and bylaws which are satisfactory to the city; or
3. Creation of a separate corporation for homeowners by which common areas shall be
owned and maintained; or
4. Complying with the provisions of the condominium ownership act of 1963, Utah Code
Annotated title 57, chapter 8, as amended, which provides for the payment of common expenses for
the upkeep of common areas and facilities within condominium portions of a development.
