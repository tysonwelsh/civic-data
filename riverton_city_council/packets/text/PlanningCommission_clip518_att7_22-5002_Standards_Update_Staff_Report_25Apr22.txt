Subject:         Zone Text Change -- Update to the City Engineering Standard Specifications and

This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on
reasonably debatable evidence. Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council
which has final decision authority for changing the Zoning Ordinance.

Example Motion
I move the Planning Commission recommend APPROVAL of the Zone Text Change updating the City
Standard Specifications and Plans Manual, as shown on the attached document.

Background

This is a public hearing for a Zoning Text Change proposed by Riverton City which is a comprehensive
update to the City Engineering Standard Specifications and Plans Manual.

This proposed update has been reviewed by the Public Works Department, the Development Services
Department, and the City Attorney. All comments received were addressed.

Staff is recommending approval of the update, with any requested changes or additional items from the
Commission.
