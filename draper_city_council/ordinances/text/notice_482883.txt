RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only — the full ordinance text is not attached on PMN)

To be published on Friday, August 24, 2018, in the Salt Lake Tribune and Deseret News
On August 7, 2018, the Draper City Council adopted the Ordinance No. 1344 which adopted the Community Reinvestment Project Area entitle 'Project Area Plan for the Draper South Mountain Community Reinvestment Project Area' as the official plan of the community reinvestment area pursuant to Utah Code 17C-5. The City Council determined that the Redevelopment Agency of Draper City had held a public hearing on the draft Project Area Plan and Budget and at that hearing (a) allowed public comment on the draft Project Area Plan and Budget on whether the draft Project Area Plan and Budget should be revised, approved or rejected, and (b) received all written and heard all oral objections to the draft Project Area Plan and Budget, and after holding the Plan and Budget hearing the Agency considered the oral and written objections to the draft Project Area Plan and Budget, and whether to revise, approve or reject the draft Project Area Plan and Budget and approved the Plan and Budget.
The City Council determined that it has become necessary and desirable to adopt the Project Area Plan entitled, 'Project Area Plan for the Draper South Mountain Reinvestment Project Area' and the Project Reinvestment Area Budget. The Project Area Plan is hereby designated as the official Project Area Plan for the Draper South Mountain Reinvestment Project Area. 
Pursuant to Utah Code 17C-5-110, the community legislative body gives notice of the adoption of the Plan and Budget. Copies of the Project Area Plan and Budget shall be filed and maintained in the office of the Agency and the City Recorder for public inspection at the Draper City Recorder's Office from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday through Friday for 30 days.
