6.     Public Hearing: Chandler Pointe at South Mountain Amended Site Plan and
       Conditional Use Permit (Administrative Action)

On the request of Mary O’Donnell, representing Chandler Pointe at South Mountain for a
Conditional Use Permit and Amended Site Plan in order to install an emergency access gate at the
east entrance into the Chandler Pointe at South Mountain development. The property is located at
approximately 14874 South Quarry Pointe Ln and is in the RM2 zone. Applications SPR-1083-
2020 and USE-1098-2020. Staff Contact: Jennifer Jastremsky, (801) 576-6328 or
jennifer.jastremsky@draper.ut.us.

Jennifer Jastremsky presented an aerial map of Chandler Pointe and The Seasons. Currently, a road
connection goes through Chandler Pointe and The Seasons. The applicant proposes making an
emergency access gate at the junction between these properties. This would be an emergency gate
and not be for everyday use. A conditional use permit is needed in order to have a gate on the
property. The amended site plan application is for the amendment of adding the gate.

Jennifer Jastremsky requested that additional condition of approval be include that gate is for both
egress and ingress.

Commissioner Player stated that the plan would include a requirement for the gate to be
operational.

Fire Marshal Don Buckley explained that is correct. Currently, cones are placed between the
properties which poses some problem when emergency personnel need to enter the area and the
cones must be removed.

Commissioner Fowler inquired if the lighting at the gate was currently sufficient, or if more
lighting is needed.

Jennifer Jastremsky observed that the existing lighting should be good enough. Both properties
have lights in close proximity to the gate; however, they can add more lights if they deem it
necessary.

Commissioner Van Haff inquired if only the fire department would be able to access the knox box.

Fire Marshal Don Buckley confirmed that is the case. It is only set up for the fire department in
the event of the need for manual operation.

Commissioner Squire inquired if this particular location was considered an access point.

Fire Marshal Don Buckley stated that when Chandler Pointe was built it was required to have two
access points that went all the way to Traverse Ridge; however, the code allows for delayed
construction when there is knowledge of a second development, which was the case. When the
Seasons was built, their plans also required a second access point. The second access point is by
Chandler Pointe so both locations need this connection.

Commissioner Squire asked if the requirement for a second access point was for emergency access
or for regular traffic.

Fire Marshal Buckley stated the fire code reads that locations with more than 30 single family
homes or more than 100 multi-family units are required to have two separate and approved access
points.

Jennifer Jastremsky noted that the City Council recently approved code changes to clarify what
type of secondary access is required and at what point they are required for developments.

Commissioner Squire asked if they approved the gate if it violated the code.

Jennifer Jastremsky stated it would not.

Commissioner Fowler asked if the primary access for The Seasons is sufficient, or if the residents
wanted the egress from their unit.

Jennifer Jastremsky referenced the site plan for the Seasons stating that the main answer to the
question is that there are two separate private roads through The Seasons and Chandler Pointe.
Residents from The Seasons are not legally allowed to exit through Chandler Pointe since it is a
private community. The intent is to add an additional access point at The Seasons during future
developments.

Commissioner Fowler observed the current “right in, right out” access point is probably awkward,
and another access point seems needed.

Commissioner Player inquired if there would be reflectors on the gate.

Jennifer Jastremsky recommended they ask the applicant as the request did not show them.

Chairman Adams noted that he only sees three conditions but believed there were four.

Jennifer Jastremsky noted that for the conditional use permit there are four conditions, and the site
plan has three conditions listed on page seven in the report.

Michael Miller stated he is the attorney for Mary O’Donnell, the applicant. He stated that currently
there is no gate that allows parties to trespass into the neighboring subdivision. That will not
change if the gate is installed with the requirement for the gate to be shared as an ingress and egress
with The Seasons. It does solve the issue of people trespassing. The report lists seven factors that
allow the Commission to approve the petition for the conditional use permit. None of those factors
state a requirement for them to share the gate with The Seasons, nor does any of the conditions
prepared by staff suggest that requirement. The report complies with all the codes. He stated this
is a very important decision for Chandler Pointe and they ask the Planning Commission to give
this serious consideration.

Mr. Miller stated the residents at Chandler Pointe love where they live as well as the safety and
the quiet. The roads are a significant expense to his client. They are private roads that are common
property under HOA, and they are obligated to maintain the roads. If the roads are utilized by more
than the residents, it shortens the life of the road which requires more financial contributions. He
asked that the Planning Commission grant the conditional use permit without the condition that it
be shared with The Seasons hotel. This allows both parties to prevent trespassers. It does not
preclude other parties from negotiating for additional access. He stated The Seasons are in a better
position to negotiate a second access points imposed on them since they are still developing.
Chandler Pointe has been developed for more than 17 years. He asked the request for the gate be
granted without the condition of egress and ingress. To the extent that the client is required to
negotiation cross access easement with The Seasons, that should be predicated to The Seasons
willingness to contribute to the usefulness cost.
