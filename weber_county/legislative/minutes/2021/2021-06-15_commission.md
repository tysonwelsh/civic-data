REQUEST FOR APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY AND
GRAVITON CONSULTING FOR IMPLEMENTATION OF THE ORACLE HUMAN RESOURCES
INFORMATION SYSTEM.

Emily Wilde, of Human Resources, requested approval of new HR information system. This was put out to
bid in July last year. A thorough evaluation process culminated in selection of the Oracle system with Graviton
as the implementer.

Commissioner Froerer added it was a long process to find a system that is more professional and efficient with
11 vendors initially considered. Consulted with current users of the system. Fairly expensive investment, but
looking at the efficiency savings, it will pay for itself over time.

Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with Graviton Consulting to implement the Oracle Human
Resources Information System; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.

Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

CONSIDERATION AND ACTION ON A REQUEST FOR FINAL APPROVAL OF VISTA VIEW
SUBDIVISION, CONSISTING OF FOUR RESIDENTIAL LOTS, (UVV021621).

Felix Lleverino, of the Weber County Planning Division, offered brief background on the request. This is a
little neighborhood east of Huntsville. The request went through Planning Commission a couple of times. It
meets all planning and plat requirements.

Commissioner Jenkins remarked it is a simple, small subdivision.

Commissioner Froerer discussed the process followed. There are three steps: preliminary proposal by the

developer, Planning approval, and Commission approval. Any public concerns can be aired at each stage of
these public meetings. No concerns have been raised.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Vista View subdivision with four residential lots; Commissioner
Jenkins seconded.

Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

CONSIDERATION AND ACTION ON A CONDITIONAL USE REQUEST FOR THE VAL
SANDERS PRUD, LOCATED AT 2900 S 3500 W, OGDEN, (CUP 2021-01).

Steve Burton, of the Weber County Planning Division, presented the first step in this PRUD approval. The
process was recently changed to be a legislative item. This Conditional Use Request was submitted before the
legislative change. It is in the Western Weber A2 Zone, about 15.75 acres and includes a large agricultural
parcel (11 acres) preserving farmland. Development plan includes the owner’s residence. Planned
layout/design was reviewed. On April 13, the Western Weber Planning Commission recommended approval
with the following conditions:

Vinyl fence

All water access is thoroughly investigated

Screen dumpster from view

Storage units have same architectural appearance as town homes

Submit docs showing exact tree species & streetlight design

HOA covenants and bylaws re: private park

Commissioner Jenkins asked if the sewer will hook into the new station. Steve Burton replied that he does not
know, but it will be part of Central Weber Sewer.

Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Conditional Use Request for the Val sanders PRUD, located at
2900 S 3500 W, Ogden; Commissioner Froerer seconded.

Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye

REQUEST FOR APPROVAL OF AN ORDINANCE OF THE COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER COUNTY
AMENDING FEES FOR THE PARKS & RECREATION DEPARTMENT.

Todd Ferrario, County Ice Sheet and Parks & Recreation Division Director, presented the second reading of
the ordinance that was presented at the last meeting. There have been no requested changes. This ordinance
updates some fees. The most significant is that camp fees are increasing from $20 to $25. Capital improvement
prices are up, and they are trying to capture the value we give customers.

Commissioner Froerer asked if we are still on the lower end of our competition for pricing. Mr. Ferrario
confirmed and added that further amenities are slated to be introduced.

Commissioner Jenkins stated that he is anxious to see what Culture Parks & Recreation does with $1M extra
they were provided. Mr. Ferrario indicated that only $90k went to the Parks & Recreation division. Marty
Smith, Culture Parks and Recreation Department Director, will provide an accounting of how the money was
applied in a future work meeting.

Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the Ordinance of the County Commissioners of Weber County
amending fees for the Parks & Recreation Department; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.

Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer — aye; Commissioner Jenkins — aye; Chair Harvey — aye
