Citizen Public Input

               Joshua Nielsen, Executive Pastor, Community Church, stated he had a concern
relating to the Ezzy Mart Convenient Store located in the Kearns Plaza. Ezzy Mart sells
vaporizers, bombs, pipes, and ways to hide drugs. This store replaced two long standing
businesses and church that was used as a food bank serving the Hispanic community in Kearns.
What this convenient store sells do not represent the standards of the Kearns Community.

               Mayor Bush asked Levi Hughes, Chief, Unified Police Department, to check this
store out to make sure it was legal.

              Mr. Hughes stated as far as he is aware all these items and how they are sold are
permitted under the law. The business license is the only thing he would have the ability to check
to make sure the store is in compliance with that.

             Mayor Bush stated under new legislation, the Salt Lake County Health
Department can investigate this making sure it is located the correct distance from schools and
neighborhoods. She will contract the health department tomorrow.

                Kathy Wiley stated her neighbor is running a business directly across the street
from her house, located at 4795 West 5135 South. There are always cars lined up along the
street as well as at least three cars that are parked along the street that do not run. This has got
to be taken care off.

               Mayor Bush stated code enforcement has an open case relating to this property
and she will check to see what is happening with this case.

Other Business

Utah League of City and Towns Membership

               Mayor Bush stated it would cost Kearns Metro Township $18,141.62 to join the
Utah League of City and Towns. This amount is based on population and sales tax revenue. She
would recommend the Kearns Metro Township join this organization. The League does a lot at
the Legislation and has supported Kearns in several bills

             Council Member Peterson, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to join the
Utah League of City and Towns. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Municipal Service District Audited Financials

                Mayor Bush stated the Municipal Service District (MSD) hired an external auditor
to do a full audit for all the financials for the five metro townships as well as the MSD. Everything
came back looking really good. All five metro townships need to review the audit and accept it.

               Council Member Peterson, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to accept
the Municipal Service District audited financials. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Emergency Communications Plan

            Mayor Bush stated she reviewed the changes that she made to the Emergency
Communications Plan. These changes were made with the assistance of Chief Hughes:

    Correct information for emergency contacts
    Added Kearns Improvement District to the list
    Corrected hospitals and fire stations phone numbers
    Removed the number of citizens that could be reached through the Kearns Metro
     Township website and Facebook page. She did not think this was necessary.
    Listed phone number of the Public Information Officer.
    Kearns logo was corrected
    Non-emergency phone number was corrected

                Council Member Peterson asked if Animal Services’ phone number should be
added to the list of contracts.

              Council Member Peterson, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
adopt the emergency communication plan adding the animal services phone number. The motion
passed unanimously.
