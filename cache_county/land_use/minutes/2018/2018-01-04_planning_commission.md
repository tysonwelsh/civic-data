#2 Public Hearing (5:35 p.m.): West Acres Rezone 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the West Acres Rezone 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the comments received from Smithfield regarding annexation. 
Smithfield has annexed up to the road and if the applicant would like the subdivision to be 
accessed from 800 West, Smithfield City will have to approve that. Otherwise access from SR-
218 will have to have UDOT approval. 
 
05:46:00 
 
Gunnell motioned to open the public hearing for the West Acres Rezone; Watterson seconded; 
Passed 4, 0. 
 
Jeff West commented regarding annexation into Smithfield; the applicant would like to build 1 
home and would hopefully use the existing well.

Cheryl Burgess commented regarding annexation into Smithfield, water concerns with the city 
well and how it affects the homes to the west of Forrester Acres. 
 
Duane Williams commented in support of the rezone and annexation into Smithfield. Smithfield 
currently cannot provide sewer services in this area. He also commented that his engineering 
reports state that wells west of 800 West should not affect the City’s wells. 
 
Ms. Burgess commented that they aren’t against the rezone. 
 
Mr. Williams commented that the city manager, Craig, is not opposed to annexation but that the 
city engineer, Clay, is opposed and would like to see the area stay in the County. 
 
05:58:00 
 
Watterson motioned to close the public hearing for the West Acres Rezone; Gunnell seconded;

Gunnell motioned to recommend approval to the County Council for the West Acres Rezone with 
the stated conclusions and finding of facts; Olsen seconded; Passed 4, 0.

#3 Public Hearing (5:50 p.m.): Pisgah Stone Products Rezone 
 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the Pisgah Stone Products Rezone. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the areas needing to be rezoned and have the ME (Mineral 
Extraction) overlay.  
 
06:09:00 
 
Olsen motioned to open the public hearing; Watterson seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
 
Lance Anderson commented regarding the parcels being stockpiled on and the location of the 
scales.  
 
Gunnell asked about the rolling 10 acres and how that affects traffic increase. 
 
Zetterquist responded that as part of the original master plan, the truck traffic was estimated. 
When the applicant submitted the amendment in November of 2017, the truck traffic had 
significantly increased. 
 
Mr. Anderson commented regarding the truck traffic and the traffic study. 
  
Gunnell asked how the oversight works for the working 5 to 10 acres. 
4 January 2018                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 4 of 5 
Harrild stated the applicant is currently out of compliance. The applicant is trying to update the 
CUP and come into compliance with that update so that the gravel pit can be expanded. 
 
Mr. Anderson stated with the stock piling the working area is at about 7 ½ acres. The demand 
has only happened in the past year and the applicant needs to move at a faster rate to meet the 
demand. 
 
Gunnell asked if there need to be a limit on the area worked before reclamation happens. 
 
Harrild stated that would be addressed with the CUP. 
 
White asked where the extraction was actually happening. 
 
Mr. Anderson responded that most of the extraction is in the same area, the material 
composition, and what it is being used in. The area that is being stockpiled on is currently out of 
compliance but is needed to keep running so needs to be brought into compliance. 
 
Watterson expressed concerns for the parcel next to the road and how that affects the aesthetics 
of the canyon.  
 
Mr. Anderson stated that screening could be put in place and there are things that can be done 
 
Gunnell asked about the footprint down the road.  
 
Mr. Anderson stated the operation is going down to extract. The plan for now is to have the 
extraction stay where it currently is. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the reasoning behind the rezone. 
 
Mr. Anderson stated that some areas were brought in to be rezoned so that scales and stock 
piling can be located on that area. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed reclamation which is over seen by the State and will be 
discussed more in depth with the CUP. 
 
Gunnell asked about the large footprint. 
 
Mr. Anderson stated that the rezone area could be smaller. 
 
Staff and Commission discussed the intent of the ME overlay area.  
 
Mr. Anderson redrew the map to reflect the areas needed for the operation to work for the 
Commission to see.
