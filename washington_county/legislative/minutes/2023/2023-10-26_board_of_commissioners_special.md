WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 26, 2023

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 10:48 a.m. on October 26, 2023, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Adam
Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Victor Iverson was excused.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3192, A RESOLUTION _IN
CONNECTION _WITH_ 1992, REFUNDING REVENUE CAPITAL APPRECIATION

BONDS

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that in 1992, the Dixie Center Special Service
District issued Refunding Revenue Capital Appreciation Bonds, which have since matured. An
escrow account was created to pay the principal and interest. The bonds have now matured, and
this resolution would direct a payment to CIB in the amount received from the escrow account.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3192,
A Resolution in Connection with 1992 Refunding Revenue Capital
Appreciation Bonds. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 10:52 a.m.
