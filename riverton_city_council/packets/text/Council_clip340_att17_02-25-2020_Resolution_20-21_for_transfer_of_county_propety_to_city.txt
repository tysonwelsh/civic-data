                                 RIVERTON CITY
                               RESOLUTION NO. 20-21


A RESOLUTION TO ENTER INTO AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH SALT
 LAKE COUNTY WHEREBY SALT LAKE COUNTY SHALL CONVEY TO AND
  RIVERTON CITY SHALL ACCEPT CERTAIN REAL PROPERTY LOCATED
      APPROXIMATELY 2262 WEST 11800 SOUTH, RIVERTON, UTAH

           WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. §11-13-202 and other provisions of the Interlocal
   Cooperation Act (codified as UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-101, et seq.) (the “Act”) provides
   that any two or more public agencies may enter into an agreement with one another for joint
   or cooperative actions.

          WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. §11-13-214 provides that any public agency may
   convey property to or acquire property from any other public agencies for consideration as
   may be agreed upon.

          WHEREAS, the County and the City are public agencies for purposes of the Act.

         WHEREAS, the County acquired title certain real property located at approximately
   2262 West 11800 South, Riverton, Utah, identified as Parcel No. 27-21-478-018, (the
   “County Property”), in 1983 when it was acquired by the County after the annual tax sale.

           WHEREAS, the County Property is also known as 2260 West Street and used to
   access several residential properties. The City maintains the County Property as part of its
   public road system.

           WHEREAS, the County heretofore has transferred similar parcels used for public
   right-of-way purposes to other incorporated municipalities.

          WHEREAS, back taxes are owed on the County Property in the amount of $400.84.

          WHEREAS, the County is willing to convey the County Property to the City on the
   condition that the County Property shall be maintained by the City as part of the public right-
   of-way.

         WHEREAS, the parties, wishing to memorialize their arrangement, enter into this
   Agreement and

          WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has considered this resolution in a public
   meeting, following public notice required by law,


   NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF RIVERTON
CITY AS FOLLOWS:


      1. Riverton City shall enter into an interlocal agreement, attached hereto as Exhibit A, with
         Salt Lake County whereby Salt Lake County shall convey and Riverton City shall accept
         certain real property located approximately 2262 west 11800 south, Riverton, Utah
      2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
