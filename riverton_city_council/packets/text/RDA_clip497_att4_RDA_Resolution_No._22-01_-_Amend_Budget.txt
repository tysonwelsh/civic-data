                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                RDA RESOLUTION NO. 22-01

        A RESOLUTION APPROVING AMENDMENTS TO THE RIVERTON CITY
            REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY FISCAL YEAR 2021-2022 BUDGET

       WHEREAS, State law requires the adoption of the Fiscal Year 2021-2022 budget for
each Redevelopment Agency located within the State of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, the amended budget complies with the requirements set out in U.C.A. § 10-
6-128; and

        WHEREAS, the budget has been reviewed, considered, and adopted by the governing
body.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
OF RIVERTON CITY, STATE OF UTAH, as follows:

        1.   The Riverton City Redevelopment Agency adopts amendments to the Fiscal Year
             2021-2022 Budget.

        2.   This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the Board of Directors of Riverton City Redevelopment
Agency this 1st day of March, 2022 by the following vote:
