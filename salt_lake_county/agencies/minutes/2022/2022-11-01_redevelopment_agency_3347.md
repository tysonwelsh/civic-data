4.1 Review of Request for Public Assistance and Guidance on 
Potential Developer Reimbursement Contract
22-1073
Attachments: Staff Report
20221021 NDC Magna Apartments TIF Analysis
Presenter: Jevon Gibb, Executive Director, Salt Lake County RDA; Eric 
Barney, Magna Councilmember
Discussion/Direction
Mr. Jevon Gibb , Director, Economic Development, Office of Regional 
Development, gave an outline of a proposal as submitted to the Regional 
Development Agency (RDA) and asked for guidance on a developer 
reimbursement contract.  The agreement was for Magna Arbor Park to Arbor 
Park Associates and was submitted to the RDA in April and the official 
proposal was received in May.  ( Mr. Gibb previously submitted a report 
from the National Development Council that was included in the packet .)  
The agreement is expected to pay itself out some time in 2025 or 2026.  It 
has been determined by the RDA that this proposal will not necessarily

benefit the County as much as the Magna Metro Township.  Mr. Gibb stated 
he would not have necessarily recommended participation as the estimated 
returns would have been about 16 percent and the market rate is somewhere 
between 12 and 14 percent.  However, interest rates and rent rates have 
created a gap that should be considered.  The project area will last through 
2029 and the developer group is proposing that whatever it generates 
between 2025 and 2029, up to $ 5.9 million, would go to the development 
group.  This received a positive recommendation from the Magna Metro 
Township Council.    
Eric Barney , Magna Metro Township Council, thanked the RDA Board for 
offering Magna Metro Township deference in this matter.  He also thanked 
Mr. Gibb and his department as they have been very helpful in providing 
data.  The Magna Metro Township Council unanimously supports this 
proposal, and it wishes to provide up to $ 5.9 million in tax increment, but 
that the Magna Metro Township and the RDA be held harmless if the project 
generated less than that in its lifetime.  
Mr. Gibb asked the Board to proceed with the proposed contract.  He did 
not need specific terms; he would work with the District Attorney ’s Office to 
sufficiently craft the wording.  He would then bring contract to the Board for 
final approval.  
A motion was made by Director Snelgrove, seconded by Director Granato, to 
provide direction to the RDA to draft an agreement for approval of the project . 
The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
