    5) Discussion of Planning Commission annual training requirements – Nathan Bracken, Kearns Legal
       Counsel.

        Mr. Bracken advised HB 409 imposes new training requirements. Empower PC being more
        informed with land use and required 4 hours, 1 on LUDMA and can be taken online, other 3 hours
        on wide variety, put together large training session for the remaining 3 hours. Maybe wait until
        after legislative session in the meantime take LUDMA. In addition, open and public meetings act
        training and should complete beginning of the year, annually.

    6) Review of Rules of Order and Procedure – Nathan Bracken, Kearns Legal Counsel

        Mr. Bracken advised may need to update to include training requirements. Discretion in holding
        the meeting, if holding public hearing, general plan, ordinance, and zoning, public is entitled to
        speak. Dealing with public comment on land use applications, public can feel ignored, thought about
        limiting public comment, unless controversial, take case by case approach.

        Limit speak time to three minutes per person or five minutes for group spokesperson. Rules of ethics-
        conflicts of interest, just disclose publicly. Ex-parte communication, and conflicts of interest.

    7) Discussion of development of possible amendments to Chapter 19.77 regarding water efficient
       landscape design and development standards – Nathan Bracken, Kearns Legal Counsel and Greg
       Anderson, Kearns Improvement District General Manager.

        Mr. Bracken advised Greg Anderson with Kearns Improvement District to provide a presentation
        and answer questions.

    8) Other Business Items. (As Needed)

        No other business items to discuss.
