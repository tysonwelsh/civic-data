                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 24-08

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A GROUND
 LEASE AGREEMENT WITH ALL WEST/UTAH, INC. WHICH ALLOWS FOR THE
     INSTALLATON OF A UTILITY CABINET ON CITY-OWNED PROPERTY

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-8-2, a municipal legislative body may
“purchase, receive, hold, sell, lease, convey, and dispose of real and personal property for the
benefit of the municipality, whether the property is within or without the municipality’s
corporate boundaries, if the action is in the public interest and complies with other law;” and

       WHEREAS, All West/Utah Inc, (the “Tenant”) desires to provide communications
services, and other data transmission services (“Services”) within the southwest region of Salt
Lake County; and

        WHEREAS, Tenant has a need to install nodes known as “utility cabinets” which
require a footprint of approximately 3’ by 7’ in surface area size, to serve a network of voice and
data transmission lines installed by Tenant; and

        WHEREAS, the City is the fee title owner of property located at 4742 West Highfield
Circle, Herriman, Utah ( the “Subject Property”) which is suitable for the installation of a utility
cabinet for Tenant’s network; and

        WHEREAS, City staff has negotiated the terms of a Lease Agreement with Tenant to
locate a utility cabinet on the Subject Property, as specified in Exhibit A to this Resolution; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council finds and determines that entry into the attached Lease
Agreement with Tenant is for fair market value, enables the City to improve the Subject Property
where the utility cabinet will be located, and further serves the public with enhanced
telecommunications services.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Mayor is authorized to sign and execute a Ground Lease Agreement with All
          West/Utah, Inc. in substantially similar form as provided in Exhibit A attached to this
          Resolution, after the same is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney. Exhibits
          shall be completed in a manner consistent with terms and conditions of approval.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
