ORDINANCE 24-04: AMEND SECTION 2-1-106 OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY
MUNICIPAL CODE TO AMEND CERTAIN REQUIREMENTS CONCERNING
THE ELECTION OF A MAYOR PRO TEMPORE
John Flores, Interim Assistant City Manager, presented proposed Ordinance 24-04 that
would amend Section 2-1-106 of the West Valley City Municipal Code to Amend Certain
Requirements Concerning the Election of a Mayor Pro Tempore.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Section 2-1-106 of the West Valley City Municipal Code establishes requirements
       for the Council to elect a Mayor Pro Tempore. The current requirement is that a
       Mayor Pro Tempore shall be elected at the City Council meeting following the
       expiration of the Mayor’s term of office. This ordinance would amend Section 2-
       1-106 to require the election of a Mayor Pro Tempore every two years.

RESOLUTION 24-17: APPROVE MODIFICATION NO. 2 TO A FEDERAL AID
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF
TRANSPORTATION FOR THE CROSS TOWNE TRAIL
John Flores, Interim Assistant City Manager, presented proposed Resolution 24-17 that
would approve Modification No. 2 to a Federal Aid Agreement Between the City and the Utah
Department of Transportation for the Cross Towne Trail.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The West Valley City Engineering Division has finalized the design for a segment
       of the Cross Towne Trail along Parkway Boulevard. This project will close gaps in
       the trail between Bangerter Highway and 2700 West. Improvements include the
       addition of sidewalk where needed along with minor roadway widening to
       accommodate new bike lanes in Parkway Blvd. In addition, a pavement
       preservation treatment will be applied to the entire roadway within the project
       limits.

       The final project estimate ($2,764,200) exceeds the authorized amount of the
       previously executed federal aid agreement ($2,384,200) resulting in the need to
       modify the agreement.

      The City previously applied for and received $806,130 of federal Transportation
      Alternative Program (TAP) funds and $1,350,000 of County Transportation funds
      for use on this project.
      TAP funds require a local government match of 6.77% ($54,575) and the City is
      responsible for the difference between the available funding and the project total
      cost. The additional $380,000 required with this modification will be paid with
      local transportation funds.

      The right of way for this project has been cleared. It is anticipated that this project
      will be advertised for bids in February and that construction will begin in the spring
      of 2024.

B.     RESOLUTION 24-19: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A REAL
       ESTATE PURCHASE CONTRACT AND ACCEPT A TEMPORARY
       CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT AND A PUBLIC UTILITY EASEMENT
       WITH AND FROM NICHOLE DAVIS FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT
       2835 WEST 3650 SOUTH
       Mayor Lang discussed proposed Resolution 24-19 that would authorize the City to
       Enter Into a Real Estate Purchase Contract and Accept a Temporary Construction
       Easement And a Public Utility Easement With and From Nichole Davis for
       Property Located at 2835 West 3650 South.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              The Nichole Davis parcel located at 2835 West 3650 South is one of several
              parcels affected by the Lancer Way Reconstruction Project; 2700 West to
              3200 West. This project will widen 3650 South and provide for curb, gutter
              and sidewalk on Lancer Way from 2700 West to 3200 West where it does
              not currently exist. The project will also install textured, colored concrete
              park strip, street trees, streetlights and new ramps at street intersections on
              Lancer Way. Compensation for the purchase of the Public Utility
              Easement, Temporary Construction Easement and improvements is
              $4,800.00 and was based upon an administrative compensation estimate
              prepared by HDR Engineering, Inc.

              The project is being funded through the Surface Transportation Program
              (STP). The city is responsible for 6.77% of all project costs, including right
              of way, making the West Valley City share of the acquisition $324.96.
