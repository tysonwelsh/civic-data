                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1642

       AN ORDINANCE OF DRAPER CITY AMENDING THE TEXT OF TITLE 7 AND TITLE 9
       OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO TREE MAINTENANCE.

      WHEREAS, Utah State law grants to Draper City the authority to regulate uses of
property by enacting land use regulations, among other methods; and

       WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to amend certain terms of the Draper City
Municipal Code to address provisions that become diminished in appropriateness,
applicability, or clarity; and

      WHEREAS, Chapter 7-13 of the Draper City Municipal Code has been established to
provide requirements for upkeep and maintenance of real property; and

       WHEREAS, Title 9, the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal
Code has been established to provide regulations concerning general developments within
the City boundaries; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City finds good cause to amend the terms and
provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding the maintenance of trees on private
property and within park strips; and

      WHEREAS, notice has been issued according to the requirements of the Utah Code
Annotated and Draper City Municipal Code for public hearings before the Planning
Commission and City Council to receive public input regarding the proposed changes to the
Draper City Municipal Code; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission and City Council have each held a public hearing
to receive public input regarding the proposed changes to the Draper City Municipal Code.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.    Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings that the proposed text amendments to the Draper City Municipal Code relating to
the maintenance of trees on private property and within park strips: 1. The proposed
amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the city's general plan. 2. The
proposed amendment is appropriate given the context of the request and there is sufficient
justification for a modification to the Development Codes. 3. The proposed amendment will
not create a conflict with any other section or part of the development codes or the general
plan. 4. The potential effects of the proposed amendment have been evaluated and
determined to be beneficial to public health, safety, and welfare and represent an overall
community benefit. 5. The proposed text amendment implements best current, professional
practices of urban planning, design, and engineering practices.

        Section 2.  Amendment. Section 7-13-030, and Sections 9-27-080 of the Land Use
and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code are hereby revised to read as set
forth in Exhibit A.

      Section 3.    Correction of Editing Errors. The city attorney is authorized to correct
any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors in Exhibit A prior to
submitting the ordinance for publishing.

        Section 4.   Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 5.   Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of
final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 1st
DAY OF APRIL, 2025.
