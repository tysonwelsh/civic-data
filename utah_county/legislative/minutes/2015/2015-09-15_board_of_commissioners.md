                                                 WORK SESSION
1.     PRESENTATION FROM MARK NELSON OF THE HEBER VALLEY HISTORIC RAILROAD
       (Continued from the September 8, 2015 Commission meeting)
Mark Nelson of the Heber Valley Historic Railroad presented a slide show detailing the history, statistics, and upcoming
projects of the railroad (a copy of which can be found attached to these minutes). Though the owning entity was created
by the state legislature in 1992 after acquiring private property, the state does not fund the Historic Railroad; the 15 miles
of track beginning at ‘The Depot’ in Park City and ending at Vivian Park in Utah County remains in business year-round
primarily through ticket sales.

Mark Nelson addressed anticipated ridership - 80,000 to 85,000 this year - and how more than fifty percent of the
customers come from the Wasatch Front. He reviewed the types of Heber Valley trains, and talked long-term goals of
Vivian Park becoming a destination point for passengers. This would include a train station turntable, and hopefully
involvement with Utah County Government. He and the Board discussed increasing tourism, potential traffic and parking
concerns, and benefits to the community.
