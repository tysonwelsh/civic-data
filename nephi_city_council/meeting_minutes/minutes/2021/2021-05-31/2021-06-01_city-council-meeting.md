Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the regular meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Attorney Kasey L. Wright offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by the Juab High School Basketball State Championship team captains
and coach. Coach Kamron Wright expressed his appreciation for the great leadership of the team
captains Jakob Bailey and Ty Allred.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
for the regular council meeting held on 5-18-21, work session held on 5-18-21, work session held on 5-
25-21, public hearing minutes 5-25-21 and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 6-1-21.
Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPLICATIONS APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones reviewed and moved to approve a business license application for Blue Diamond
Remodeling LLC . Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

FISCAL YEAR 2021 AUDIT ENGAGEMENT DISCUSSED:

   The council directed staff to continue using Larsen & Company as the city auditors. The council said
they would take official action and authorize the Mayor to sign an engagement letter with Larsen &
Company at the next regular council meeting.

ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER/PACIFICORP EASEMENTS APPROVED:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the alignment of a Rocky Mountain
Power/Pacificorp proposed power line that intersects city property on Reese’s Flat as well as property
owned jointly by the city and Juab County. Rocky Mountain Power had requested easement across the
properties. Rocky Mountain Power has offered a market value of $184,996.00 for the easements. Mr.
Atkinson stated that this matches closely with assessed and appraised properties in the area. Councilor
Jones moved to authorize the Mayor to sign over the easements to Rocky Mountain Power. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

INDUSTRIAL PARK DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT AND ADDENDUM #2 WITH JENSON COMPANIES
APPROVED:

    Mr. Atkinson reported that for the past several months, the City staff had been working with
Jenson Companies (JRC Investments, LLC) to finalize the sale of land in an area designated for an
industrial park. The Mayor signed a real estate purchase contract with the approval of the City Council
in February contingent upon a development agreement being accepted by both parties. After a
thorough round of negotiations, a final draft of the development agreement had been completed.
   The development agreement sets forth the conditions of development on the city’s parcel designated
as an industrial park. These conditions include:
             1. Development must conform to the Industrial (ID) zone of the city.
             2. The developer is required to install all infrastructure associated with the development.
             3. A reimbursement agreement could be created for any upsizing in utilities.
             4. Impact fees are required.
             5. The city has an option of a buyback if there is not a facility constructed, and a certificate
                 of occupancy issued, within 5 years.
             6. A first right of refusal is given to the city if the property is sold or transferred to an entity
                 other than a subsidiary company, family member, or family trust.
             7. Reimbursement agreements will be recorded with the Juab County Recorder’s Office

  Mr. Atkinson added that in order assist Jenson Companies with closing, an additional extension of
time was needed. Addendum #2 to the real estate purchase contract extends the contract another 45
days. Councilor Memmott moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Industrial Park Development
Agreement and the Addendum #2 or the original real estate purchase agreement. Councilor Seely
seconded the motion.

FY 2021 AMENDED BUDGET FY 2022-23 BIENNIAL BUDGET ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson presented the FY 2021 Amended Budget and the FY 2022-23 Biennial Budget for
approval. Councilor Memmott moved to approve the FY 2021 Amended Budget. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
   Councilor Jones moved to approve the biennial budget for FY 2022-23. Councilor Seely seconded the
motion. The motion passed with the Mayor breaking the following tie vote:
                Nay: Larry Ostler
                     Nathan Memmott
                Yes: Kent Jones
                     Justin Seely

AIRPORT HANGAR LEASE AGREEMENT WITH HAMMOND HELICOPTERS APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented an airport hangar lease agreement with Hammond Helicopters. The lease
agreement is for 80X80 ft hangar at the southernmost end of the current hangars, space #8. Councilor
Ostler moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the lease agreement. Councilor Memmott seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR AND CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reported on a meeting with Johansen Construction Inc. (JC I) on some final wrap up
items for the water project. He said that JCI officials reported that this was one of the best projects they
have ever worked on and that the city crews were very cooperative.
   Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about the Field House open house, Tuesday, June 8th at 5:30 p.m.
at the Junior High. He said that the field house has been named “The Hive” and will be swarming with
activity.
   Councilor Jones reported that the tenants renting the home at the airport have dogs running at large.
   Councilor Seely reported that Wednesday, June 2nd the Library is hosting an event at the park from
10:00 a.m. to noon.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Seely moved to adjourn into executive session to discuss the character and competence of
an individual and litigation. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.
