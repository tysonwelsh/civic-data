Year End School Safety Presentation: Presenter: Josh Adams, Police Chief
Chief Adams is recapping some of the programs the police department was involved in.

They had a reading program at Cherry Hill, Bonneville, Westmore and Windsor. They also had
28 visits to 325 3rd graders, as well as brought those students school supplies. They participated
in Cookies with Cops at Cherry Hill. Cookies were donated by Crumbl. The officers also
helped with the mobile school pantry program with the Utah Food Bank. In the secondary
schools, the officers participated in Donuts with Police at Orem Jr High and the donuts were
donated by Macey’s Grocery Store.

Chief Adams also discussed the significant Police interventions at the schools in Orem
this school year. He also went over accomplishments of the police department. Chief Adams
reported all officers are NASRO certified and this year another SRO officer was assigned to the
schools. Chief Adams also met with each of the administrators at all Orem schools. Rapid
Response Plans were executed at each of the schools. The police have also partnered with many
groups such as ASD, United Way, Boys and Girls Club, and various other public and private
businesses and philanthropic organizations.

Amending Article 2-34 of Orem City Code- Orem Neighborhood Commission: Presenter: Kena
Mathews, Community Service Manager and Steve Earl, City Attorney

Ms. Mathews explained that she has been working with Mr. Earl, Ms. Millett, and Mr.
Lambson on changing the Orem Neighborhood Commission to allow for 2 representatives for
each district. This would require a change to the city code. This will increase the commission to
18 members. Mayor Young asked for a description of the responsibilities of the commission.
Ms.Mathews explained that they meet about quarterly when an issue arises. For example NIT
(Neighborhood Improvement Team) was discussed with this commission before it was started
and recently the new grant program (Neighborhood Refresh and Business Safety and Security
grant) was also taken to the commission prior to being presented to City Council. The city has 9
neighborhood districts. Mr. Bybee explained this will be presented in the next City Council
meeting on June 27th.

Public Hearing: Enterprise Funds for Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Presenter: Brandon Nelson,

Finance Director

Mr. Nelson addressed the Enterprise transfer of funds back to the General Fund. A notice
was sent to the citizens of Orem as required by the State. The City provides basic services of
water, sewer, storm drainage, street lighting to all residents, businesses and City owned
properties. The State Auditor requires Cities to charge themselves the retail rate of these services
and pay for these services from the general fund. To pay from the general fund the City has three
choices 1) raise taxes, 2) cut services or 3) transfer funds. The City has opted to transfer funds
from the utilities since the cost of servicing City owned property is already encompassed in the
utility rates. The General Fund pays the utility funds for the services provided as follows:
$900,000 for the Water used for parks, cemetery, and internal building usage. $16,000 for Water
Reclamation/Sewer, $141,000 for Storm Water and $15,000 for Street lighting fees.

Public Hearing opened at 7:00 pm

Ms. Mitchell is worried about the price of water in the City of Orem, she had questions

Public Hearing: Approving and Adopting the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Budget Presenter:
Brandon Nelson, Finance Director

Mr. Nelson is presenting the City Council with the final budget that has been presented to
the City Council over the past few months. The Proposed budget amount for the Fiscal Year
2023-2024 is $152,344,861. Some budget highlights include projected FY 2023 revenues over
FY 2022-2023, the funding of the city’s compensation program for benefited employees, the
funding of market adjustments for employees based on a market analysis, funding for health
insurance increases, funding for fleet replacement, funding for capital projects and creating a
new Development Fees fund. Mr. Nelson continued to speak on revenues and expenditures for
the City of Orem FY 2023-2024. The slides are available at https://orem.org/city-finances/. Mr.
Peterson is interested in the city doing a study to compare departments to other cities, in order to
make sure our departments are running efficiently. Mr. Nelson replied that there is a study for
Management Efficiency that the city plans to do but it will be difficult to compare our city
departments to other cities because cities do not have the same fees or function the same way.

Public Hearing opened at 7:28 pm

Ms. Heather Fry asked for the budget slide show to be made available to the public. Ms.
Fry is also wondering about the problems with recycling. Mr. Nelson said that the presentation
will be on the City Website on the Finance Department page. Mr. Bybee spoke to the higher
standards that have been put in place for recycling and the upgrades made by WM in their
recycling program to meet these standards.

Public Hearing closed at 7:32 pm

Mr. Spencer moved to recommends to the City Council by ordinance to approve and
