AN ORDINANCE, AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED "ZONING" OF
THE MAGNA MUNICIPAL CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2017, BY
RECLASSIFYING CERTAIN PROPERTY LOCATED IN MAGNA
FROM THE R-2-6.5 (MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL) TO R-M/zc
(HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL) ZONE
The Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, State of Utah, ordains as follows:

Section 1: Section, 19.06.020, The Zoning Map of the Magna Metro Township,
Magna Municipal Code of Ordinances 2017, is hereby amended, as follows:

The property described in Application #31117 filed by Miguel Munevar and
located at 2943 S Dora St, within Magna Metro Township, is hereby reclassified from the
R-2-6.5 zone to R-M/zc zone, said property being described as follows:

PARCEL #:14-29-176-001, 14-29-176-002, 14-29-176-003 and 14-29-176-004
LEGAL DESCRIPTION:

Lots 5,6,7 and 8 of FLANGAS SUBDIVISION. according to the Official Plat on file in the
Office of the Salt Lake County Recorder.

Net Area approximately: 0.64 Acres

Pursuant to Section 19.90.060 of the Magna Metro Township, Code of
Ordinances, 2017, development of property is subject to the following conditions:

1. The following permitted uses are prohibited from being placed on the property:
1. Agriculture

2. The following conditional uses are prohibited from being placed on the property:
1. Airport;
2. Apartments;
3. Apartments for elderly persons;
4. Banks;
5. Bed and breakfast homestay (provided it is located on a lot which has a

minimum area of ten thousand square feet);

6. Bed and breakfast inn, which may include conference meeting rooms;
7. Boardinghouse;
8. Cemetery, mortuary, etc.;

9. Day care/preschool center;

10. Electrolysis of hair;

11. Golf course;

12. Gymnastics, dance, dramatic, cosmetic, modeling and art studios for
instructional purposes only;

13. Hospital;

14. Hotel;

15. Lodging house;

16. Massage (every massage technician shall be licensed by the state);

17. Medical, optical and dental laboratories, but not to include the
manufacture of pharmaceutical or other products for general sale or
distribution, and also not to include the use of animals;

18. Mobile home park;

19. Nursery and greenhouse, excluding retail sales;

20. Nursing home;

21. Office, business and/or professional;

22. Parking lot;

23. Private educational institutions having an academic curriculum similar to
that ordinarily given in public schools;

24. Private nonprofit recreational grounds and facilities;

25. Public and quasi-public uses;

26. Sportsman's kennel;

27. Tanning studio;

28. Veterinary

Section 2: The map showing such change shall be filed with the Magna Metro
Township Planning Commission in accordance with Section 19.06.020 of the Magna
Municipal Code of Ordinances, 2017.

Section 3: This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and
upon at least one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in
the Magna Metro Township, and if not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall
take effect immediately upon its first publication. |
