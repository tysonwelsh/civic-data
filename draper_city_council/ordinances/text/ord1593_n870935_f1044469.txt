City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On November 7, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1593, amending a
master area plan design per the land use and development code of Draper City
Municipal Code relating to the Vista 600 West Master Area Plan. The complete
ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 8th day of November 2023. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
