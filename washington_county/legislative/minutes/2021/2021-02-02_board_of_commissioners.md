WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
FEBRUARY 2, 2021

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on February 2, 2021, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah.
Those in attendance were Commission Chair Gil Almquist, Commissioner Dean Cox,
Commissioner Victor Iverson, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, Deputy County
Attorney Brian Graf, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

INVOCATION BY: Laura Fairchild, Baha'i Faith

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Jasmine Monrreal, Dixie High School
Jr. ROTC

Commissioner Almquist invited Stephen Wade and Emilie Neilson to speak about the item
on the Agenda regarding a zone change in Pine Valley and the Pine Valley Community.
Emilie Neilson spoke representing a committee opposed to rural recreational zoning and
commercial zoning in the area. Ms. Neilson urged the Commissioners to consider the
desires of the residents of Pine Valley in regards to proposed zone change.

PRESENTATION: 2020 PRESENTATION OF PILT PAYMENT FROM_ THE
DIVISION OF WILDLIFE SERVICES

explained that the payment presented today is for the 2020 contractual in-lieu tax payment
(PILT) on land the DNR owns in Washington County. The money used to pay the in-lieu
tax is provided by hunters and anglers in Washington County and across the state; the

Mr. Bunnell thanked the Commissioners and the County Attorney for their efforts in
securing the northern corridor right-of-way.

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE _NO._2021-1168-O, AN ORDINANCE
REZONING A CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON _COUNTY_AT
APPROXIMATELY 1200 EAST MAIN STREET IN PINE VALLEY FROM A-20

(AGRICULTURAL 20 ACRE MINIMUM_LOT SIZE) ZONE TO _ THE _ A-5

(AGRICULTURAL 5 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE AND THE FR-13.5

(FOREST RESIDENTIAL 13,500 SQUARE FOOT MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE

HEREINAFTER FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS ORDINANCE

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the applicants adjusted
their initial plans in order to build a six standard subdivision located at approximately 1200
East Main Street in Pine Valley.

The Planning Commission reviewed the request and recommended that the Commission
approve the zone change.

The applicant was invited to speak, but declined.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1168-O,
An Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County at
Approximately 1200 East Main Street in Pine Valley from A-20
(Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone to the A-5 (Agricultural
5 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone and the FR-13.5 (Forest Residential
13,500 Square Foot Minimum Lot Size) Zone Hereinafter Fully
Described in this Ordinance. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist,
Cox, and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. _2021-1169-O, AN ORDINANCE
REZONING A CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON _COUNTY_AT
APPROXIMATELY _ 180 NORTH PINION HILLS IN DAMMERON_ VALLEY

FROM _ THE OST-20 (OPEN SPACE TRANSITION 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT
SIZE) ZONE TO THE RE-40 (RESIDENTIAL ESTATE 40,000 SQUARE FOOT
MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE HEREINAFTER FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS

ORDINANCE

Mr. Messel explained that the applicant is requesting to change the zone change
designation of a parcel located at approximately 180 North Pinion Hills Drive in
Dammeron Valley. The property appears to have been split from the surrounding larger
parcel which is why the applicant is requesting the zone change in order to bring it into
compliance for a possible future subdivision development.

The Planning Commission reviewed the request and recommended that the County
Commission approve the zone change.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO._R-2021-2717, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
WASHINGTON COUNTY SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT NO. 1 TO UTILIZE
NON-PUBLIC ROADS

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that there is a non-public roadway owned
by the Solid Waste Special Service District that runs along the south boundary of the
landfill to the north boundary, where it abuts a roadway running across county land into
the purgatory fairgrounds and industrial park area. The County and the District desire to
occasionally utilize the roadway that crosses the other’s property.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-
2717, A Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington
County and Washington County Special Service District No. 1 to Utilize
Non-Public Roads. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson

voting aye.

Amendment. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2021-2724, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY _AND

SANTA CLARA FOR FUNDING A PARKING LOT AT CANYONVIEW PARK
TO SERVICE THE LITTLE LEAGUE FIELD AND BMX TRACK

Deputy Tourism Director Brittany McMichael explained that Santa Clara is preparing to
improve its Canyonview Park parking lot which will serve both the little league field and
BMxX track. The Tourism Advisory Board recommended matching Santa Clara’s cash
contribution of $130,000 and provide an additional $30,000 in engineering costs for a total
of $160,000.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2724, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington County and
Santa Clara for Funding a Parking Lot at Canyonview Park to Service
the Little League Field and BMX Track. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson voting aye.

Mr. Graf explained that the Attorney’s Office utilizes case management software to assist
