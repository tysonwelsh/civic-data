1.         25-227                 City Administration providing the Council with an update on the Alta
                                  Canyon Sports Center
                   Attachments:   Presentation

                                  Martin Jensen, Deputy CAO, provided an update to the Council on the Alta Canyon
                                  Sports Center (ACSC) rebuild. VCBO Architecture and Layton Construction have been
                                  hired for the design and construction of the ACSC. Our goal is to provide updates
                                  throughout the design and construction process. Mr. Martin introduced Nathan Leavitt
                                  with VCBO Architecture who provided an overview of the ACSC design renderings and
                                  site plan. The ground breaking for the construction is planned for August 25th and the
                                  planned opening of the new center is January 2027. Council questions and comments
                                  followed.

                                  Jared Adamson and Jacob Zufelt with Layton Construction spoke to the Council
                                  regarding the rising costs and the impact this will have on the construction costs of the
                                  ACSC. They estimated the rising construction costs may impact the total project costs
                                  by approximately $4 million. Council comments and questions followed.

                                  Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director, outlined a strategy to fund the gap in the
                                  projected construction costs. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Council moved to General Citizen Comments.

2.         25-229                 Brewpub Analysis to Consider Streamlining Processes, Lowering Costs,
                                  and Expanding Locations for Brewpubs in Sandy City
                   Attachments:   Brewpub Memo 7-15-2025
                                  Business License Fee Comparison
                                  Map of Buffer Areas
                                  Map of Brewpub Areas
                                  Brewpub Presentation Slides

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, reviewed the City Council objectives
                                  regarding the streamlining of the permitting process for brewpub establishments beyond
                                  the Cairns area. She reviewed her findings with the Council and outlined the licensing
                                  process. Her recommendations included locating these establishments near ski hubs
                                  and neighborhood activity centers, allowing brewpubs in areas that currently allow
                                  restaurants that serve alcohol such as commercial, mixed use, office, industrial and
                                  special development commercial districts. Council questions and comments followed.
                                  Lesley Casaril spoke about the licensing fees process and the impact to the city and
                                  employees' workload. Council thanked Ms. Anderson and the staff for their presentation
                                  and research.

                                  Council made a motion to direct staff to move the process forward.
                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa to direct
                                  staff to move the recommendations presented this evening through the land use
                                  process and forward to the Planning Commission for feedback, and then bring
                                  the results back to the Council for consideration and action...The motion carried
                                  by the following roll call vote:

9.         CA06062025 Amendments to Title 21 of the Land Development Code related to
           -0006979   Residential Parking Standards and Minimum Dwelling Size
           (CC)
                   Attachments:   Signed_Ordinance_25-16
                                  Ordinance 25-16
                                  Presentation Slides
                                  Planning Commission Minutes (DRAFT) 06.19.2025
                                  Staff Report with Exhibits

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, presented amendments to Title 21, Land
                                  Development Code, Chapter 20, Residential Development Standards, Chapter 24,
                                  Parking, Access and Circulation Requirements. The proposed amendments will bring the
                                  city code up to date with the Utah State Code by revising parking standards for single-
                                  and two-family residential dwellings, removing garage requirements for owner-occupied
                                  affordable housing, and by adjusting the requirements for the minimum dwelling size to no
                                  more than 1,000 square feet. The Planning Commission forwarded a positive
                                  recommendation. This was presented as an information item at a prior City Council
                                  meeting is before the Council for consideration and action.

                                  Public Comment opened.
                                  Public Comment closed.
                                  A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Alison Stroud, to adopt
                                  Ordinance 25-16, an ordinance amending the Sandy City Municipal Code title 21,
                                  "Land Development Code", Chapter 20, "Residential Development Standards",
                                  and Chapter 24 "Parking, Access and Circulation Requirements"...The motion
                                  carried by the following roll call vote:

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked the city for coordinating the July 4th Parade and
                    event. It was a great event.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud thanked the staff for all their work on the July 4th event.
                    She provided an update from the Historic Committee. In their service to the community,
                    the Sandy Youth Council, along with the Draper Youth Council worked together to
                    compile 2,500 swag bags for the recent educators wellness conference sponsored by
                    Utah's First Lady, Abby Cox.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey was looking forward to the upcoming ribbon cutting for
                    the Public Works Fleet building. She appreciated all the work the staff has done. She
                    was also looking forward to the upcoming opening of Fire Station 31.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa will not be at the ribbon cutting tomorrow but she
                    acknowledged the Council for all their work and support of the Public Works fleet building
                    and project.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski was looking forward to the Public Works ribbon cutting. She thanked the
                    Sandy Youth Council and Sandy Explorers for their volunteer service at the July 4th
                    Parade.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, invited the Council to attend Dave Poulsen's retirement farewell. We
                    appreciate his service to the city and community.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:15 pm.
