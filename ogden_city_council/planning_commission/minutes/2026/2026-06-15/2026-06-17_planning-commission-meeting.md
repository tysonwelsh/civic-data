1. Zone changes related to the General Plan

and

2. Annexation Policy Plan Updates
The meeting discussed proposed zone changes to align with the future land use map, affecting
42 properties, including a school, an Institute building, and a rehabilitation center. The changes
aim to match existing land uses, such as rezoning residential properties to commercial or
community facilities. The annexation policy plan was reviewed, focusing on areas along the
foothills and industrial zones. The commissioners also addressed the status of the ice facility,
noting ongoing investigations and the need for evidence to revoke a conditional use permit.
Upcoming events include a General Plan open house and a demonstration regarding the ice

facility.

As there was no additional business to come before the Commission, COMMISSIONER
SHALE MOVED THE MEETING BE ADJOURNED. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COMMISSIONER
