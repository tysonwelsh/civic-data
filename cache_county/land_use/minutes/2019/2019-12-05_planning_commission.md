#5 Whisper Ridge Conditional Use Permit Revocation Review 
Harrild reviewed the staff report for the Whisper Ridge Conditional Use Permit Revocation Review. 
Staff and Commission discussed the Whisper Ridge Conditional Use Permit (CUP) and what violations 
have occured to cause the revocation review. If the permit is revoked both amendments to the CUP are no 
longer valid. 
Dayson Johnson commented that he works for Magleby Construction and is a partner with Whisper 
Ridge. He also commented on the removal of the yurts and non-compliance with the heli-biking. 
Christensen asked about what part Magleby Construction plays in the Whisper Ridge operation. 
5 December 2019                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 3 of 6 
Mr. Johnson stated the owner of the Magleby Construction is an equity partner and was not aware of 
any of these issues and is willing to come into compliance.  
Jason Rickards commented that compliance for the permits and conditional use permit is no longer with 
Mr. Lockwood but will be with Mr. Johnson. 
Mr. Johnson stated that Whisper Ridge will be in compliance within 30 days. 
Christensen asked about the yurts. 
Mr. Rickards stated that the yurts are being taken down and will not be put back up for this season. 
Mr. Johnson commented on the removal of the yurts not being able to happen due to there being 60 
inches of snow in April. 
Gunnell asked about the disturbed water. 
Mr. Johnson stated commented on the existing beaver ponds creating erosion issues and that no fish 
were stocked. 
Gunnell asked who did the work. 
Mr. Johnson stated that a construction company will come in to check the work. 
Gunnell asked about the biking trails. 
Mr. Johnson stated that he has retained an engineer to double check all the work and make sure there is a 
plan that will have an NOI and SWPPP. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the water disturbance and what permits would be needed. 
Mr. Johnson stated they are taking responsibility and trying to rectify the problems. 
Gunnell asked questions about the helicopter use. 
Mr. Johnson stated the use for the helicopter was too specific and they are hoping to rectify that. 
White stated there is a pattern of non-compliance and that is an issue. 
Mr. Johnson stated Magleby’s is going to be around for the long haul and there has been a failure of 
communication with the operations part of the company that will not happen again. 
Sands asked about conditions 7 and 8 and when compliance would happen. 
Mr. Johnson commented on conditions b & c, and how those will be brought into compliance. 
Sands asked if the mountain biking use would be part of a future amendment #3. 
Mr. Rickards stated yes. 
5 December 2019                       Cache County Planning Commission Minutes                        Page 4 of 6 
Sands asked about 8a and if it would be part of the future amendment #3. 
Mr. Johnson stated they are operating the helicopter intermittently. 
Sands asked if there was any use of the helicopter that would need to be permitted under amendment 3. 
Mr. Johnson stated no. 
Sands commented on the resources and the Greater Sage grouse and how they may have been impacted 
by the use. 
Mr. Johnson stated that as far as he knows that greater sage grouse have not been impacted and they 
have been working with the DWR on the Weber County side and will continue to work with them on the 
Cache County side. 
Sands asked when compliance with the original CUP could happen. 
Mr. Johnson stated that compliance with the original CUP will happen within 30 days. The second 
amendment they are planning to record soon. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed ceasing operations until Whisper Ridge is in compliance. 
Everything under the second amendment can cease without revoking the first CUP. 
White stated there could be no biking or activities related to the pond until the required applications, 
reviews, approvals, and permits are completed. 
Mr. Johnson responded yes. 
Christensen asked about the operations under amendment 2. 
Mr. Rickards stated that no activities listed under amendment 2 will happen until it is recorded. 
Staff and Commission discussed what damage has possibly been done to water ways with the 
unauthorized earth movement. 
White asked about what money comes back to Cache County why the office is in Eden. 
Mr. Johnson stated that is not for this meeting but he understands the point being raised. 
Mr. Rickards stated there is other development coming that will hopefully alleviate some of those 
concerns. 
White commented on the water issues. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed whether or not to revoke the permit and on what grounds revocation 
could be founded. Questions regarding the use of the helicopter were discussed. The original properties in 
in Avon area were part of the original CUP and 1
st amendment with the  La Plata properties included in 
the 2nd amendment. Heli-skiing is allowed under the 1st amendment on the originally properties; the La 
Plata properties cannot be used until the 2nd amendment is recorded.

Christensen asked if the applicant has met with the state regarding the remediation of the scarring from 
the bike trails and pond and stream adjustments. 
Mr. Johnson stated he has not, but he has met with an engineer. 
Mr. Rickards stated they are working towards remediation and the trails to be properly permitted to start 
in the spring. 
Gunnell asked the mileage for the trails is going to be. 
Mr. Johnson stated he can send that plan to staff and give what details are needed. 
Commissioners discussed giving a 30 day time frame to bring the CUP into compliance with the 1st 
amendment and what would happen if that does not happen. 
Mr. Rickards stated they want to prove to the Planning Commission that they are following the CUP and 
trying to come into compliance and bring everything else into compliance. 
Gunnell asked about what equipment was moved for earth movement for the bike trails. 
Mr. Johnson stated he is not sure how the trails were done but the vegetation was still there and the trail 
was a single track. 
Mr. Rickards stated the bike trails are not meant to be drivable. 
Luthy stated that equipment much larger than a bobcat had been used on the pond. 
Sands motioned to continue the Whisper Ridge Conditional Use Permit Revocation Review for 30 days; 
Gunnell seconded; Passed 5, 0. 
Harrild asked for some clarification on the timeline the Planning Commission is wanting for the 
applicant. 
Commissioners discussed the compliance with the pond and the bike trials and how to bring that into 
compliance. Compliance for those issues and a third amendment cannot happen until the damage to the 
area can be reviewed in the spring.
