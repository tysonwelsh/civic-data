  information as follows:

         Corbin Bennion representing Natomas Meadows LLC, is requesting final
         plat approval for the 3rd phase of the ARA Industrial Center Subdivision.
         This application will also amend lot 1 of the Freeport West – WV Phase 1
         Subdivision. The subject property is located immediately to the east of 6755
         West and north of 2540 South. The SR 201 South Frontage road borders

         western portion of the old Rocky Mountain Raceway and consists of 76
         acres.

         was constructed under the Freeport West Subdivision. Lot 301 will be

         in size.

         boundary of the subdivision. This road will be known as 6480 West Street.
         In addition, portions of the SR 201 South Frontage Road will be dedicated
         as part of this application. Improvements along both streets will be
         addressed as part of the conditional use application. Both lots will gain
         access from the frontage road, and 2540 South. In addition to these points
         of access, lot 301 will gain access from 6755 West and lot 302 will gain
         access from 6480 West.

         In summary, the subdivision plat will amend lot 1 of the Freeport West –
         WV Phase 1 Subdivision and will add and additional lot. The subdivision
         plat will also dedicate a new street to be known as 6480 West.

       AMEND SECTION 7-7-406 OF THE WEST VALLEY MUNICIPAL
       CODE, PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO MONOPOLE TOWERS
       Mayor Bigelow informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
       Council Meeting scheduled February 18, 2020, in order for the City Council to
       hear and consider public comments regarding application ZT-16-2019, filed by
       APC Towers, requesting a Zone Text Change to amend section 7-7-406 of the West
       Valley Municipal Code, provisions applicable to monopole towers.

       Proposed Ordinance 20-09 related to the proposal to be considered by the City
       Council subsequent to the public hearing, was discussed as follows:

       ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 20-09, AMEND SECTION 7-7-406 OF THE
       WEST VALLEY CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO AMEND STANDARDS
       APPLICABLE TO CERTAIN COMMUNICATION FACILITIES
       Jody Knapp, CED, discussed proposed Ordinance 20-09 that would amend section
       7-7-406 of the West Valley City Municipal Code to amend standards applicable to
       certain communication facilities.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              Currently the maximum height for a monopole tower in any zone is 60’ and
              the maximum width, including the antennas, is 24 inches. If the tower is
              stealth, there is some flexibility in some of the standard requirements.
              Stealth is defined as follows:

                     7-7-402 Stealth. A communications antenna, facility and/or
                     Structure that is completely disguised as another object or otherwise
                     concealed from view thereby concealing the intended Use and
                     appearance of the facility and where the dimensions of the facility
                     reasonably approximate the dimensions of the object they are being
                     disguised as and the location of the Stealth facility is in concert with
                     its surroundings. Examples of Stealth facilities include, but are not
                     limited to, trees, synthetic rocks, flag poles, light pole standards, or
                     architectural elements such as dormers, steeples, and chimneys.
                     Final determination regarding Stealth poles shall be made by the
                     Community and Economic Development Staff based on these
     MINUTES OF COUNCIL STUDY MEETING – FEBRUARY 11, 2020
                             -5-


                      standards. All antennas and associated equipment not camouflaged
                      in a manner to be completely disguised as another object or
                      otherwise be concealed from view by concealing the intended Use
                      and appearance of the facility are not Stealth.

              However, APC Towers feels that in some instances a stealth application
              may be difficult to achieve and would look more out of place than just the
              tower itself. They also stated that if the allowable tower height and width
              were increased it would provide more opportunities for colocation which
              could reduce the number of tower sites needed. Therefore, they are
              proposing to amend the monopole tower standards to allow for a 100’ tower
              with a full antenna array (36” width) for properties located within the M
              Zone and are also designated as Heavy Manufacturing in the General Plan.

       Councilmember Lang stated that this property may change uses in the future and
       may not always be manufacturing. Councilmember Buhler agreed and stated that
       all property is vacant at some point but it’s difficult to see how it will transition
       over time. The Mayor and the Council discussed the proposed ordinance and
       expressed concerns about allowing a larger, non-stealth monopole in an area that
       could potentially change zones in the future. They discussed possible stealth
       solutions for the area. There were no changes to the proposed ordinance suggested
       by the Mayor or City Council.

       The City Council will consider Ordinance 20-09 at the Regular Council Meeting
       scheduled February 18, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

           - The Commission on Accreditation for Law Enforcement Agencies
               (CALEA) Purpose
                  o Establishes a body of professional standards and an accreditation
                     process that:
                  o Requires an agency to develop a comprehensive set of written
                     directives
                  o Provides the necessary reports and analyses a CEO needs to
                     make fact-based, informed management decisions
                  o Required a preparedness program be put in place
                  o Is a means for developing or improving upon relationship with
                     the community
                  o Strengthens an agency’s accountability, within the agency and
                     the community

              o Facilitates an agency’s pursuit of professional excellence
       -   Standards
              o The standards address nine major law enforcement areas:
              o Roles, responsibilities, and relationships

              o Personnel structure
              o Personnel process
              o Operations

              o Traffic operations
              o Detainee and court-related activities

B.     NEIGHBORHOOD PILOT PROJECT
       Nicole Cottle provided a brief summary of the Neighborhood Pilot Project that was
       discussed two weeks ago. Councilmember Buhler stated that he is concerned about
       having a spotlight on this neighborhood and not knowing a timeframe for its
       completion, moving from this neighborhood to another and abandoning this initial
       project, residents moving out of the neighborhood once they have gained success,
       etc. Mayor Bigelow stated that he feels frequent updates to Councilmembers or to
       the Council as a whole may be beneficial. Councilmember Buhler agreed and
       indicated that he believes the Council would like to be involved in neighborhood
       meetings. Councilmember Huynh stated that he feels people of all races care about
       their families and he is thankful for this project. He indicated that members of
       District 1 have done something similar and there are many ways of creating a
       successful program. Councilmember Christensen asked how impacts in the trailer
       park community will be tracked. Nicole detailed statistics in the trailer park
       community and discussed the different ways of tracking changes. Mayor Bigelow
       noted that this is a pilot project and staff will learn what does and does not work.
       He indicated that the concept is fantastic because it focuses on strengthening
       communities. Councilmember Lang suggested wider sidewalks being put in the
       neighborhood instead of adding park-strips. Nicole discussed funding, priorities,
