     5. REMARKS - Newly Elected Officials
         Mayor Dirk Burton acknowledged taking the oath of office four years prior and expressed
         gratitude to the staff for their assistance during his tenure. He emphasized that the residents
         are his priority and thanked them for their support, expressing eagerness to continue working
         side by side in his coming term.

         Council Member Chad Lamb expressed gratitude to Chris McConnehey for his twelve years of
         service in District 1, acknowledging his excellent work. Lamb became involved in civic matters
         after observing issues in his neighborhood. While transitioning from the at-large council
         position to serving specifically in District 1, he pledged to attentively listen to the needs and
         desires of all residents.

         Council Member Bob Bedore expressed his honor to serve in District 2, particularly in light of
         the closely contested race. His love for West Jordan and happiness in serving alongside his
         colleagues were evident. He shared the aspiration to contribute to making West Jordan the best
         it can be, aligning with Mayor Burton's vision to make West Jordan ‘Best Jordan’.

         Council Member Zach Jacob acknowledged the positive progress the city had made,
         highlighting votes he contributed to with pride. He expressed anticipation for the upcoming
         four years, emphasizing his commitment to represent and work for the betterment of West
         Jordan alongside the council.

         Council Member Kent Shelton expressed gratitude to his supporters during his campaign. He
         voiced commitment to prioritize the city and its residents, with a focus on setting responsible
         budgets. His goal was to represent the residents effectively and ensure the city's well-being.


     6. ADJOURN
         Acting Chair Pamela Bloom extended congratulations to the Council, acknowledging collective
         responsibilities, collaboration, and aspirations each member had pledged with the oath of
         office. She expressed anticipation for a respectful and cooperative environment within the
         council, emphasizing collaboration with residents to achieve a shared vision of being united,
         thriving, and resilient for the community.

         Seeing no further business, Chair Bloom adjourned the meeting.

         The meeting adjourned at 6:25 pm
