Due to the ongoing public health emergency related to the COVID -19 pandemic and response, 
public gatherings have been limited to less than 20 persons and public access to the Salt Lake 
County Government Center has been limited on the orders of the Salt Lake County Mayor. 
Consistent with Salt Lake County Council Resolution 5714, approved March 24, 2020, the Salt 
Lake County Council has made preparations to conduct its Council Meetings electronically. In 
light of current public health orders and social distancing requirements, the Council cannot 
provide physical space or facilities for members of the public to attend its meetings for the 
duration of the public health emergency.
Members of the public are invited to participate in the Council ’s Meetings electronically . 
Agendas, audio live -streams and recordings, and meeting minutes can be accessed at 
slco.legistar.com. The Council will continue to broadcast a live -stream of its meetings on 
Facebook Live, which can be accessed at http://www.facebook.com/slcocouncil/.
