LEGISLATIVE LAND USE APPLICATION(S) 
                                                  Hearing began at – 3:21 p.m. 
OAM2021-000493 - An Ordinance of the Salt Lake County Council Amending Section 19.04.462 of the 
Salt Lake County Code to include Wedding and Reception Uses in the definition of Restaurant, Liquor 
License; and to Enact Related Regulations. Presenter: Melissa Anderson (Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commission will act on the proposed ordinance amendment after taking comments from the 
public during their respective public hearings. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning 
commission’s rules of conduct. Public comments will be limited to three minutes per person. The public is 
also invited to review and inspect the proposed ordinances at https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html under 
the Planning Commission’s agenda. 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District  Planning Manager Melissa Anderson provided an analysis 
of the staff report. 
 
Commissioner Cohen said a restaurant with a liquor license is not the only facility that has weddings and 
outdoor weddings that can hold functions like this. Doesn’t know if adding to restaurants is sufficient. Ms. 
Anderson said this pertains to gatherings at the facil ity, reception at the restaurant , and that an outdoor 
patio is included. Commissioner Palmer said this is extending what a restaurant allows and other zones 
allow wedding and reception uses . Ms. Anderson said these uses may be ongoing now at restaurants, but 
ordinance doesn’t explicitly say these uses are allowed at the restaurant; this ordinance change  clarifies 
they are allowed. 
 
Commissioner Reid motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Hutchings seconded that motion. 
Commissioners voted unanimous in favor.
