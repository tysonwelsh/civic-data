CUP2021-000329 - David George is requesting a Conditional Use Permit for an Office/ Warehouse.
Acreage: 5.00 acres. Location: 2172 South 7200 West. Zone: M-2. Planner: Shad Cook (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Shad Cook provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding parking stalls, setback, dedicated public right-
of-way, and current view of the property and current use.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: David George
Address: 7550 West 2100 South
Comments: Mr. George said rubber mechanical, new facility process rubber and tire retread and tire
recycling. Everything stored indoors. Will not keep much on site, everything will be processed in one to
two days and send out. Nothing on the site, just a pile of dirt and fencing. Been cleaning up the property
over the last month. Parking would meet the code. Anticipate ten to fifteen employees between the two
buildings at once with three shifts a day.

Commissioner Richards asked if they could have a minimum twenty-five stalls. Mr. George said parking
will change between the two buildings. Mr. George said height is twenty-eight feet. Fencing in the front
will be masonry and the rest of non-visionary. Commissioner Weight asked if there are any smells and
asked if this is all done on the site, since they are only using half the property. Mr. George said they don’t
anticipate smells and plan to do other types of things on the site later, but haven’t planned for anything,
except thought of manufacturing.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public meeting, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.

No one from the public present to speak.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public meeting, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Commissioners had a brief discussion regarding minimum parking stalls.

Motion: To approve application # CUP2021-000329 requesting a Conditional Use Permit for an Office/
Warehouse with staff recommendation and a condition of no less than twenty-five parking stalls or
ordinance minimum.
       Motion by: Commissioner VanRoosendaal
       2nd by: Commissioner Elieson
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)

OAM2021-000273 - A proposed ordinance creating Chapter 19.15 of the Magna Municipal Code allowing
for and regulating Interior Accessory Dwelling Units (IADUs) in single-family detached
dwellings. Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed public hearing item after taking comments from the
public during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s

Magna Planning Commission – September 9, 2021 – Meeting Summary                                Page 4 of 5
rules of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three
minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Travis Hair provided an analysis of the proposed
ordinance creating Chapter 19.15.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding required building permits and metered, business
licensing for monitoring, health and safety, short term rentals, and recordation.

Commissioner Weight questioned 19.15.030. Mr. Hair said proposed by developers above garage style
ADU’s show how that will be done and development agreement signed off by Magna. Commissioner Weight
asked to define family. Mr. Hair said the language the county changed to the definition of family because
the state keeps changing. HB82 allows to define as four adults. Commissioner Weight asked about
19.15.080 lot coverage of 25%. Mr. Hair said comes down to existing conditions. Commissioner Weight
asked if they could apply for an exception. Mr. Hair said variances are hard to get, one can’t be self-
imposed, and another can’t be financial. An IADU can be added on to the existing house and call it internal.
Commissioner Weight asked about 19.15.100, one IADU or reversed. Mr. Hair said can’t have an IADU if
you already have one, counsel said they are confident you can say you get one.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public present to speak.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend approval of file #OAM2021-000273 on a proposed ordinance creating Chapter
19.15 of the Magna Municipal Code allowing for and regulating Interior Accessory Dwelling Units
(IADUs) in single-family detached dwellings to the Magna Metro Township Council with changes to
19.15.040 to limit the number of residents to four persons or two adults and dependents, and 19.15.100 to
limit one IADU or ADU.
        Motion by: Commissioner Weight
        2nd by: Commissioner VanRoosendaal
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
