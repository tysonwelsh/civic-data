                                                                   Proposed Fee Schedule
                                                                       Amendment


March 18, 2025



Honorable Mayor and Council,



The City’s Administrative Services department submits the following proposed amendments to the
2024-2025 fee schedule for your consideration:

During the City Council meeting on March 4, 2025, the Council approved a contract with Herriman City
to assume responsibility for shelter services for dogs and cats in Riverton City. This decision follows
Stoneridge, the City's current provider, notifying the City of its intent to discontinue services at the end
of March 2025. The agreement with Herriman City is intended as a temporary solution while the City
constructs its own animal shelter. The contract with Herriman includes a different pricing structure for
boarding services compared to the rates previously agreed upon with Stoneridge. Staff recommends
that the Council update the fee schedule for boarding services to align with the pricing outlined in the
Herriman contract.

Additionally, the City will enter into a contract with a veterinary provider to handle necessary intake
services. Staff also recommends amending the fee schedule to reflect the actual costs incurred by the
City for any intake services the City deems necessary.
