OATH OF OFFICE CEREMONY:

    Out-going Mayor Glade R. Nielson gave some parting remarks. City Recorder Lisa E. Brough
administered the Oath of Office to the new Mayor, Justin D. Seely and the two newly elected council
members, Skip F. Worwood and Jeramie Callaway. Mayor Seely offered some remarks about how him
and Glade had run a clean campaign and that Glade had taught him many lessons even after the
election. He complimented his parents for teaching him how to serve and to be charitable. He thanked
his extended family and in-laws. Mayor Seely expressed his desire to preserve the ability to live across
the street from your family.
   Councilor Callaway said he would like to echo what Mayor Seely had said and he mentioned that the
first person to call and congratulate him on election night was Glade Nielson.
   Councilor Worwood thanked his wife for being his campaign manager and thanked everyone that had
supported him in the election and was grateful that we live in a unique situation where our local
elections are unlike the unfriendly national elections.
   Mayor Seely announced that he had chosen Councilor Nathan Memmott to act as Mayor Pro Tem and
that other councilor assignments were coming.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

RACOON EY ANNEXATION PETITION ACCEPTED:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson presented the Raccoon Eye Annexation petition. The area of
annexation is located approximately ½ mile west of Main Street on the northside of Highway 132 and
contains 10.98 acres. Councilor Memmott moved to accept the annexation petition so that it can move
forward in the annexation process. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

FOOTHILL INDUSTRIAL LAND PURCHASE AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the city has been approached by a company to purchase some
land adjacent to land already owned by the city. This is on the south end of the city near the Sheriff’s
office. The land being offered for sale is part of a parcel owned by Foothill Industrial Park LLC. The
portion being offered for purchase is 5.251 acres. If the agreement is approved by the Council, a portion
of the property would be owned by the city’s industrial park development fund and the natural gas
fund.
  If the property is purchased, the city can then do a boundary line adjustment to conform to the
boundaries owned by the city. A company called Roderick Enterprises is planning on purchasing the
property in February and would like to subsequently sell a portion to the city.
   Mr. Atkinson said that the city has an interest in the property for several reasons, it is adjacent to a
piece already owned by the city, this could also be developed into a south industrial park, and a portion
of the property could potentially be used for natural gas infrastructure. It will also “square off” the
piece already owned by the city and provide additional frontage along Main Street. The greater area is
seeing quite a bit of development activity, and this would provide an option for the city to continue
encouraging industrial park development.
     Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the real estate purchase agreement with
Roderick Enterprises. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

WORK SESSION:

    1.    The council discussed the process of interviewing the applicants for the vacant city council seat
         that Mayor Seely vacated because of being elected Mayor. The council decided to have each
         applicant take 7 minutes to discuss the following three topics:
              Thoughts on annexation and growth
              What does a rural community mean to you?
              What is the appropriate role of city government?
         The order of applicant would randomly be determined, and the interviews would be held on
         January, 11, 2022 at 6:00 p.m. in the Senior Citizens Center in the Juab County Building.
