Appointment of Summit County Sheriff; Margaret Olson
Attachment: Oath of Office--Smith
Attorney Margaret Olson presented the appointment of Frank Smith as Summit County Sheriff for the Council’s approval.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve the Appointment of Frank Smith as Summit County Sheriff. Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson.
Council Chair Armstrong noted that Sheriff Smith’s salary will remain at the current level.
Attorney Margaret Olson swore in Frank Smith as Sheriff. Sheriff Smith thanked those present for their confidence and set forth his intentions to continue to protect and serve the public.
