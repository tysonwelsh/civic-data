PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Spencer Strange
      Spencer was named the Jr. Jazz referee of the year for Utah, Idaho, and Wyoming. He was
      honored at a special event. Craig Oswald and TJ Sutherland from the recreation department
      praised Spencer for his dedication, work ethic, and growth over seven years, highlighting his
      continuous improvement and willingness to learn. Councilor Travis Worwood discussed the
      challenges that youth referees face touted him on his achievements and future opportunities he
      has. Mayor Seely congratulated Spencer and was appreciative of the efforts of the recreation
      department in promoting sportsmanship and recognizing youth referees.

PRESENTATION TO DONALD BALL
      Lisa Verzella and Darren Van Cleave from the National Weather Service presented Donald W.
      Ball with the John Campanius Holm Award for two decades of exemplary weather observations
      from Nephi, Utah. They stated that Donald is consistently the first to submit his data every
      month and his observations are the go-to for quality control of all other data in the area. Mayor
      Seely noted that Donald shares the information he collects with the city and this data is helpful
      for water planning and storm water mitigation. Mr. Ball has spent many years volunteering in
        the community in other respects as well. The mayor and council shared their appreciation for
        his contributions to the community and congratulated him for this well-deserved award.

ARBOR DAY PROCLAMATION
      Lisa Brough presented the Arbor Day Proclamation. Each year Nephi desires to promote Arbor
      Day in the community. Trees are an important part of our community providing many benefits:
      reduce erosion of topsoil, lower heating and cooling costs, moderate the temperature, clean the
      air, produce oxygen, increase property values, enhance economic vitality of business areas, and
      beautify the community. Nephi City’s arborist is Garrett Bethers. Mayor Justin Seely designated
      April 26, 2025 as Arbor Day in Nephi City.

        Councilor Skip Worwood made the motion to authorize the mayor to sign the proclamation.
        Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

NOTAM SYSTEMS MANAGER MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
     Kyle Marchant, Public Works Director, presented the NOTAM systems MOU. The airport has
     historically issued Notices to Airmen (NOTAMs) through an online system created by the FAA. A
     new system to issue NOTAMs has been developed along with an accompanying Memorandum
     of Understanding (MOU). The MOU has been reviewed by staff and had some initial concerns
     about the original NOTAM system being removed but this system will eventually be phased out.
     The Administration recommended approving the memorandum.

        Councilor Travis Worwood made the motion to authorize Seth Atkinson to sign the MOU.
        Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

UDOT AGREEMENT FOR LIGHT POLES
      UDOT decided to replace the traffic light poles and add street lights to the fixtures. Through a
      miscommunication with staff, the poles arrived not powder coated to resemble the street
      lights. The staff was able to have UDOT contact their contractor and transport them to a
      manufacturer to powder coat the poles. Although this ended up with additional costs, it could
       have been many years before these poles would be replaced again and the time to have it done
       was now.
       The agreement was the costs for the powder coating and transport costs. The funds can come
       from the Community Projects line in the Capital Projects fund. The poles are now set to be
       installed on Wednesday, April 16th. The agreement is simply an acknowledgement to pay the
       costs associated with the powder coating and transport.

       Councilor Shari Cowan made the motion to authorize Mayor Seely to sign the agreement with
       UDOT. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

WORK SESSION 8:20 pm
  1. Farmers Market
          a. UDOT did not want to allow food trucks on Main Street. The mayor would like to
             understand more about why and will have a meeting with UDOT on Thursday to discuss.
             He has attended farmers markets in Springville and Spanish Fork that have food trucks
             on UDOT roads so just wanted more understanding.
          b. Fee of $35 per Saturday to use the park was proposed. The mayor and council found
             that reasonable.
          c. The mayor feels that the Farmers Market can promote healthy lifestyles and brings a
             sense of community. He also appreciated that Mrs. Thorn was trying to promote local
             vendors and helping our community.
          d. Mrs. Brough asked the city if the council would be okay with other vendors wanting to
             use our park because if we allow the farmers market then others will want to.
  2. Non-Disclosure Agreement
          a. The city has been approached by a company who prepares sites for data centers and
             would like to enter into a non-disclosure agreement. This agreement has been reviewed
             by Craig Smith, our contracted attorney, and he has made appropriate revisions to
             reflect the requirements of a municipality. Our city attorney has also reviewed the
             agreement and concurs with Mr. Smith on these revisions.
               The non-disclosure agreement will allow the company to be more forthright with the
               information about where they are looking to locate. They have also stated that a goal of
               theirs is to remove the risk for existing ratepayers by bringing such a large load to the
               grid system. This is important for Nephi City as well as the UMPA organization who
               provides power to Nephi City and other member cities.
          b. The council requested time to look at the agreement more closely before making any
               decisions.
    3. Miscellaneous
          a. Councilor Cowan reported that a Planning and Zoning Meeting ‘flex homes’ were
               presented. She asked if the council would be interested in having something like that in
               Nephi. If so, there would need to be some areas rezoned. The council talked about the
               possibility of bringing affordable housing to Nephi, but Councilor Cowan informed them
               that the flex homes were quite expensive for the size. The council was not interested in
               rezoning.
          b. Councilor Cowan also asked about having the council look and update the CU1 and CU2
               zones along main street. She said Shauna Talbot, Planning and Zoning Department, has
               had to turn several businesses away because they didn’t meet the code. The council
               suggested the Planning and Zoning committee discuss it.
    4. Councilor Shari Cowan moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor JD Parady seconded the
       motion. The motion passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at 9:25 pm.
