AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CITY CODE, SECTION 18. 90. 010, " TABLE
      OF COMMERCIAL USES" TO ALLOW FOR A " RECEPTION CENTER" AS A
       CONDITIONAL                 USE IN THE C- N ZONE.          APPLICANT- JOSEPH WHITE.


        WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said text amendment;
and


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend Riverton City Code Section 18. 90. 010 as described in Exhibit " A".

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:


        Section       1.    Riverton City Code Section 18. 90. 010, Table of Commercial Uses shall be,
                            and hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit " A.", allowing " Reception
                            Centers"   as a Conditional Use in the Commercial   Neighborhood   Zone.


        Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4tn day of
