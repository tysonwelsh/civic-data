39   3.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Uncommon Architecture’s request for review of
40   the Willow Park Church zone change, 10.92-acres located at approximately 1500 North 2600
41   West, changing the zoning from TH-5 to R-1-22
42   Chairman Jackson- opened the public hearing.
43
44   Lonnie Bates-Mr. Bates raised concerns about the proposed development plan, including the
45   construction of two churches and the placement of garbage facilities. He sought clarification on
46   whether his comments had been considered by the Commission and requested updates on the status
47   of the development plans. Mr. Bates emphasized the unique impact on his neighborhood and
48   expressed willingness to engage in the process to address his concerns.

50   Chairman Jackson- closed the public hearing.
51
52   Darren Mono, representing Uncommon Architects for the LDS Church, addressed the
53   commissioners. He clarified that only the first building is currently planned, with the second being
54   conceptual. They're not seeking site plan approval for the second building yet. There's flexibility
55   regarding the placement of trash facilities, pending coordination with the project manager. Darren
56   welcomed questions from the commission.
57
58       Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to send a positive recommendation for Uncommon
59       Architecture’s request for review of the Willow Park Church zone change, 10.92-acres located
60       at approximately 1500 North 2600 West, changing the zoning from TH-5 (transitional holding)
61       to R-1-22 (residential); including the DRC comments; with the findings as listed in the staff
62       report. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Eyre.
63
64       Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
65       Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
66
67   3.2.) Public hearing and consideration of Uncommon Architecture’s request for review of
68   the Willow Park Church concept, 10.92-acres located at approximately 1500 North 2600
69   West, requesting exceptions to the architectural and site orientation requirements of
70   Chapter 37, Nonresidential Design Standards.
71   Commissioners and staff discussed the construction scope of 2550 West and confirmed access
72   points on 1500 North and 2550 West. The density of churches in the area was noted, alongside
73   concerns about parking and seating capacity. Traffic and access issues were addressed, with
74   assurances provided. Phase two of the project was briefly mentioned without immediate concerns.
75
76   Chairman Jackson opened public hearing.
77
78   Lonnie Bates was representing the house on the corner between the two churches, expressed
79   concerns about the density of having two churches in close proximity. He noted that nowhere in
80   Lehi had a house with one church on each side, although he acknowledged the existence of parcels
81   with two churches. Mr. Bates found such arrangements odd from his perspective.
82
83   Chad Watkins (online comment) - criticized the oversized parking lot for the proposed LDS
84   churches, suggesting it could be better used for landscaping or to encourage walking accessibility.
85   He expressed concern that the large parking lot encourages driving and negatively impacts the
86   community and environment.
87
88   Lonnie Bates (Online Comment)- expressed concerns that the proposed plan for two LDS churches
89   does not align with Lehi city's general plan, citing issues with excessive parking, lighting, and
90   trash enclosures. He emphasized that the proposal would diminish the neighborhood's character,
91   value, and quality of life, and set a negative precedent for church and residential density in Lehi.
92
93   Chairman Jackson closed public hearing.
94
95   During the meeting, Mr. Mono from Uncommon Architects removed a second access point due to
96   proximity concerns, eliminating the need for an exception. Seating capacity was discussed,
     Lehi City Planning Commission                    2                                   January 25, 2024
 97   requiring 225 parking stalls. Discussions covered glazing exceptions, dumpster locations, and
 98   parking issues, prompting considerations for code standards and adjustments. Concerns about
 99   neighboring properties led to discussions on landscaping buffers and road connectivity.
100
101         Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve Uncommon Architecture’s request for review
102         of the Willow Park Church concept, 10.92-acres located at approximately 1500 North 2600
103         West, requesting exceptions to the architectural and site orientation requirements of Chapter
104         37, Nonresidential Design Standards in regards to the glazing on the front while denying the
105         exception for the location that it still needs to adhere to the code and push the building to the
106         corner; including the DRC comments that there needs to be a 12ft landscaping buffer for the
107         neighboring properties and the parking facilities in general; including the findings listed in the
108         staff report and recommendation to move the garbage location. Also, Commissioner Eyre
109         recommended that the city council consider a requirement for both roads, 1400 North and 2550
110         West, being completed. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Everett.
111
112         Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
113         Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
