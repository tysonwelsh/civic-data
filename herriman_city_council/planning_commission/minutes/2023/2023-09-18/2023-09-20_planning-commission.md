    4.1. Consideration of a Conditional Use Permit for a commercial site plan at 5143 W
         Miller Crossing in the C-2 Commercial Zone and subject to the Mountain View Plaza
         Development Agreement.
         Applicant: Aaron Osmond (property owner)
         Acres: ±6.00
         File Number: C2023-081

Planning Manager Clint Spencer begins presentation for the conditional use permit for
Mountain View Plaza. The subdivision already approved this is for the site plan. The project will
be happening in two phases, the first phase includes 4 buildings but only 2 buildings are being
considered tonight. Tonight’s proposal includes two game areas, four plazas, lots of sidewalk
connections, 141 parking stalls, and 18 % landscaping. The MDA defined the spaces in between
the buildings should be usable, outside seating and dining. The corner plaza will be used as a
destination to visit. The MDA states no two buildings should be the same but, look similar like
brother/sister. Each building is 4950 sq feet, one will be a restaurant, the other is a multi-
tenant. Staff considerations include adding a turn-around area for parking and adding bicycle
parking. Staff is also recommending the utilities be screened and made obscure.

The corner plaza proposal will provide shade, safety, lighting, paving, seating, water feature,
and signage. Staff met with the developer after the packet was sent out. The developer
presented a new site plan that provides a lot of the elements in the proposal. Signage was not
provided and would need to be considered later. Staff recommends providing landscaping in
front of the street signage. Staff concerns are adding lighting elements, secondary seating,
music, sun/shade study, plant size, sight triangle, building heights and structures in easement,
sign heights and types. Staff recommends approval with recommendations listed.

Developer Aaron Osmond confirmed he has enjoyed working with city staff. He communicated
wanting to make himself available for questions on the project. Commissioner Jackson
questioned if he could name the restaurant yet? Developer Osmond conveyed they don’t have
a named tenant for building 1 yet, but Red Fuego (Mexican restaurant) has signed for building
2. They are looking for more local restaurants that offer breakfast, lunch, and dinner. They
want to make an entertainment, restaurant, retail destination where people come and stay.
They are committed to the long-term vision of this project. Commissioner Ferguson asked
where dumpsters are located. Developer Osmond cited he would allow the architect to
respond to the locations. They are also in talks with D-bat to create a 2-floor indoor softball
batting cage environment for the office building, and possibly pursuing a 3rd floor with
reception center.

Commissioner Sickles presented working with city on the northwest corner retention park to
make it into a usable park, as it will be dry 90% of the time. Developer Osmond revealed they
had one idea to create an outdoor rope course in the retention.

Architect Chris Layton appreciates all the interaction and feedback with the city staff and
commission. Two of the dumpsters are located in the south corner at each end of the parking
lot, two are behind buildings 5 and 6 near the drive-thru side of the building, and one at corner
of building 7. The dumpsters are all recessed by berms and masonry material. The water
feature with the help of the planners has evolved into more of an organic splash pad protected
by raised planters with seating attached. Having the plaza open to the businesses is another
important feature. The line of site will need to be reviewed for when the road expands.

Commissioner Bradford suggested providing less access to the road in the plaza by the water
feature. She also furnished if there was room for a stage(temporary) you could bring in music
at the gaming area. Planning Director Maloy points out we still want to invite people into the
plaza center, if you block full street access, you’ll block people from coming into this area, there
needs to be a balance. Commissioner Jacobson felt the need to consider differing all the
landscaping and detention pond but move forward on the two structures.

         Commissioner Jacobson moved to approve item 4.1 Consideration of a Conditional Use
         Permit for a commercial site plan at 5143 W Miller Crossing in the C-2 Commercial
         Zone and subject to the Mountain View Plaza Development Agreement. With
         conditions and an addition 11th condition that all landscape plans will come back for
         approval. Commissioner Sickles seconded the motion.

    4.2. Consideration of a Special Exception to reduce the minimum side yard setback of an
         accessory structure to four feet (4’) at 6576 W Sky River Circle in the A-1-10
         Agricultural Zone.
         Applicant: Blake Krahenbuhl (property owner)
         Acres: ±0.524
September 20, 2023 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes                                 Page 5 of 6


         File Number: P2023-117
   Planner Sheldon Howa presented the consideration for special exception for a corner lot
   accessory building proposal of 2100 square feet. The exception is based on irregular lot
   shape. The building is a garage, moving it elsewhere would not give vehicular access. The
   lot has almost no rear yard. The finished material will be cementitious fiber board, stucco,
   main doors will have windows with remaining portions being steel construction. The garage
   placement is typical in this subdivision. Staff recommends approval with conditions.

   Applicant Blake Krahenbul expressed his gratitude for all of planner Howa’s help. The main
   goal is to be able to back in the RVs with the irregular shaped lot.

   Commissioner Jackson appreciated the building seems to encroach the least amount
   possible, since the backyard neighbor is established and set back a long way.
   Commissioner Fenn quired if the elevation on the roof pitch of 12% is city approved. That
   pitch will cause snow to fall off the roof. Planner Howa confirmed pitch is approved,
   adding the pitch to match the house would create a much larger building. Commissioner
   Sickles verified the structures east elevation is the front, and it match the house structure.


        Commissioner Jacobson moved to approve item 4.2 Consideration of a Special
        Exception to reduce the minimum side yard setback of an accessory structure to four
        feet (4’) at 6576 W Sky River Circle in the A-1-10 with staff’s recommendations.
        Commissioner Fenn seconded the motion.
