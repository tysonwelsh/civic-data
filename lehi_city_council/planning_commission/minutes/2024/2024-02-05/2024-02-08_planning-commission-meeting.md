Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chair Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner Eyre
from the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Amy Sylvester's request for conditional use
approval to board two horses on 0.59-acres at 196 West 2875 North in an R-1-15 zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Lockhart asked why it was a conditional use. Jacob clarified this is a zone that it
is allowed use by State Law and is permitted.

The applicant, Nick Sylvester, was present. He stated he does not plan on using this for
commercial use. The horses will be boarded and will be exercised regularly at an equestrian
park. The main use is for therapy and mental and emotional support. He would also like his
children to get involved in 4H. The coral will be fenced in with livestock fence panels. They will
be building a loafing shed with durable materials. He addressed the smell of the horses and the
flies.

Commissioner Everett asked about the placement of the loafing shed and whether it meets the
code.

Commission Chair opened the public hearing.

Jana Rampton stated she was opposed to the item. She spoke with the applicant this morning and
asked them if they would consider moving the horses to another property. She stated nobody in
the neighborhood wanted the horses on the property. The neighborhood recently got rid of the
mink farms that brought in the flies. She was concerned with the 60-foot setback. She was
worried about the value of the homes.

Jared Rhyme stated he was opposed to the request. The mink farm that was near the homes
brought in a lot of flies. He went through a lot of work to get rid of that. He would like to make
sure the home value stays. He said he is concerned about not being able to resale his home,
although he bought his home with the purpose of staying. He appreciated the applicants are
looking at putting in a closed space.

Ted Rampton asked if he could request some conditions. He was wondering if Sylvester’s could
mitigate flies, rodents, animal noises, etc. He asked how they would be enforced. He stated they
wanted to remain cordial neighbors. He asked if the applicants would be willing to create a view
buffer. Mr. Rampton asked if the equestrian activities could be limited to 8:00 am to 10:00 pm.

Jacob Curtis read the online comment from Lauren Simper regarding where the horse trailer will
be parked and that it be prohibited from parking on the road keep the sidewalk cleared and the
drainage be contained to the owner's property.

Jennell Palmer stated she is concerned about the flies and the rodents. She stated the corrals are
always the furthest away from their own home, but always closest to the neighbors.

Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.

Jacob Curtis wanted to clarify that noticing was done according to code and state requirements.

Commissioner Everett stated his neighbor has horses and he loves them and considers them his
best neighbors. He understands the concerns and the impacts, and he can only speak to his
experience, but he enjoys having this in his neighborhood. He is close to a mink farm, he likes to
think he can relate, and he appreciates the thoughts that have gone into the mitigation of the
request.

Commissioner Lockhart stated she understands the concerns, but they are bound to the code. She
stated the amount of care and concern the applicant brought to the meeting was thoughtful. The
60-foot requirement will need to be met if that is part of the code.

Commission Chair Jackson stated developments will impose on what your views are. He also
wanted to take the opportunity to remind the public to engage with state and local leaders
concerning the noticing requirements. He does not see how this conditional use can be granted if
it needs to be measured from a certain setback.
Commissioner Roberts echoed Commissioner Jackson's sentiments regarding the setback.

Commissioner Everett stated that it would be the applicant's responsibility to meet the
parameters of the code.

Commissioner Lockhart stated the time to say something was when there was a zone change,
because we have to respect property rights, and it is the applicant's responsibility to meet the
code.

Kim Struthers stated there was a reasonable condition to allow the request.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to grant the conditional use of Amy Sylvester’s
        request for conditional use approval to board two horses on 0.59-acres at 196 West
        2875 North in an R-1-15 zone; including all findings stated in the report and all DRC
        comments, noting the property itself meets the requirements. Commissioner Roberts
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Roberts,
        yes; Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and consideration of Brent Ventura's request for approval of the
updated Skyridge Subdivision Concept Plan, a 5-lot residential development located at
approximately 260 East 2750 North.).
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Brent Ventura, Reno Mahe, and Jason Mandaleras, were present. Skyridge High
School does not want access points to the school, and they will be working with the City. Reno
stated the exceptions they are asking for an integral sidewalk and to eliminate the park strip.
Commissioner Kunze asked about the extra lot. Reno stated he plans on building in the future.
Commissioner Everett asked about the design. The applicants stated they chose to have more
open space.

Commission Chair opened the public hearing.

Chad Watkins left an online comment stating he would like to keep the sidewalk, asserting they
increase walkability.

Commission Chair closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Roberts moved to recommend approval of Brent Ventura? S
        request for approval of the updated Skyridge Subdivision Concept Plan, a 5-lot
        residential development located at approximately 260 East 2750 North; including all
        DRC comments and findings stated in the report; noting the commission is only
        approving the exception for the park strip since it is consistent with the adjacent
        neighborhoods. Commissioner Roberts recommends the layout of the owners desired
        exceptions that was presented. Commissioner Lockhart seconded.
        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Roberts,
        yes; Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City's request for review of a
Development Code amendment to Table 05-030-B Nonresidential Zones, removing the
Trades Shop use to be consistent with Chapter 39, Definitions.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the item.

Commission Chair Jackson opened and closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Lehi City's request
        for review of a Development Code amendment to Table 05-030-B Nonresidential
        Zones, removing the Trades Shop use to be consistent with Chapter 39, Definitions;
        including the findings stated in the report. Commissioner Lockhart seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Roberts,
        yes; Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
