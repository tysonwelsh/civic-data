ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of Ordinance No. 2025-003 amending the city zoning map
     by changing the zone from A-1 (Agricultural, 40,000 sq ft minimum lot size)
     to OS (Open Space) on approximately 0.56 acres generally located near the
     St. George/Washington border near the Paseo Development. (Case no.
     2024-ZC-015 - City Property Next to Paseo Zone Change)

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The purpose of the amendment is to
      designate the City-owned property as open space. The City of St. George acquired
      the property under review in anticipation of a future road. When it was determined
      that a road would not be placed in that location, a trail was indicated on the
      property. From research, it appears that the property is zoned A-1 (Agricultural). It
      is proposed that the property be rezoned to open space to keep consistent with the
      intent to landscape and construct a trail. At their meeting held on December 10,
      2024, the Planning Commission held a public hearing on the matter and
      recommended approval of the zone change with a 6-0 vote, with no conditions.

      Link to introduction from City Manager John Willis and presentation from Planner Dan
      Boles, including discussion between the City Council, City Manager John Willis, and
      Mr. Boles: 00:10:59

AMEND DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT/ORDINANCE:
    Consider approval of Ordinance No. 2025-004 amending a Development
    agreement affecting approximately 5.03 acres located south of the existing
    southern terminus of Gap Canyon Parkway at the southern boundary of PA-
    2 in the Divario development. (Case No. 2024-DA-001)

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The purpose of the amendment is to allow
      the applicant to disturb unimproved open space in order to construct a sewer line
      and channelize Gap Canyon Wash to be known as Divario Development Agreement
      Second Amendment with no conditions from Planning Commission. An updated
      development agreement for the project formerly known as The Lakes, now
      commonly known as Divario, was approved in 2014. As part of the development
      agreement, certain areas were designated “unimproved open space," or open space
      that was not to be disturbed. This second amendment would allow for development
      of a small area of unimproved open space in the southern part of Divario known as
      PA-2. The disturbance would allow for channelization of Gap Canyon wash and for a
      sewer to be constructed in order to serve other portions of the Divario Development.
      The Planning Commission held a public hearing on the request and forwarded a
      positive recommendation with a 6-0 vote and no further conditions.

      Link to introduction from City Manager John Willis and presentation from Planner Dan
      Boles, including discussion between the City Council, Assistant Public Works Director
      Wes Jenkins, City Manager John Willis, and Mr. Boles: 00:16:18

HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a Hillside Development Permit establishing the
     ridgeline on lot 44 of the Foremaster Ridge subdivision located at 1856 Joe
     Circle. (Case number 2024-HS-010)

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Foremaster Ridge plat was recorded in
      March of 1995. The plat designated a ridgeline but did not give an exact location of
      the ridge. The applicant wishes to establish the ridgeline on lot 44. The Hillside
      Review Board (HSRB) has met several times on site and forwarded a
      recommendation to the Planning Commission on this proposal that the red line
      shown on Exhibit 'E' of the staff report be adopted as the ridgeline location. The
      Planning Commission held a meeting on December 10, 2024, and failed to forward a
      recommendation as the motion died with a 3-2 vote (the motion recommended that
      the City Council adopt the blue line as depicted in exhibit 'E' of the staff report).
