## Pillar 4 — completeness vs external reality (the decisive pillar here)

**A. Council 2017-02 → 2018-06 gap — GENUINE (verified against live PMN).** PMN body 5831 lists
28 (2017) + 17 (2018) notices, so the meetings happened, but **40+ of the referenced 2017-2018
attachment PDF file-IDs all return HTTP 404** (retention purge; audio 404s too), while three
recovered control files (459667 / 459671 / 522659) return HTTP 200. The purge boundary is
~mid-2018 (earliest survivor 459667 = 2018-07-18). Correctly logged in `minutes_unrecovered.csv`,
never stubbed. **This is NOT a Kearns-style false gap** — there, minutes were live on PMN; here
they are purged. (Full method in ../VERIFICATION.md §Gap.)

**B. PC thin-by-design — VERIFIED (weighted heavily because the probe surfaces ~100 PMN dates vs
18 indexed).** Downloaded and classified **23 non-indexed PC candidate dates** spanning 2019–2026:
**22 are ~150-word CANCELLATION agendas** (e.g. "WE WILL CANCEL THE JULY 13, 2021 MEETING"), 1
empty, multi-doc dates carried staff-report/plan packets — **0 standalone minutes**. Copperton's
PC cancels most scheduled meetings; the 18 captured docs are the held meetings. No missed minutes
found. *(Caveat: sampled, not exhaustively enumerated — deferred.)*

**C. Council cadence.** ~11–12 meetings/yr from 2018-07 onward with no unexplained multi-month gap
beyond the documented 2017–2018 purge and the logged Sep/Dec-2025 + June-2026 not-yet-posted
minutes. Consistent with the 3rd-Wednesday monthly cadence.

**D. Elections vs external reality.** 6 council races (2017 vote-for-2; 2021 D/E; 2023 A/B/C) match
the Salt Lake County SOVC; the 2019 (archive drop) and 2025 (unopposed, untabulated) absences are
documented gaps, not silent losses. Mayor Clayton cross-checked against the town's own council page.
