MINUTES OF THE DRAPER CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY MEETING HELD ON
TUESDAY, JUNE 15, 2021, IN THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS, 1020 EAST
PIONEER ROAD, DRAPER, UTAH

PRESENT:              Mayor Troy K. Walker, and Board members Mike Green, Tasha
                      Lowery, Fred Lowry, Cal Roberts, and Marsha Vawdrey
EXCUSED:

STAFF PRESENT:        David Dobbins, City Manager; Mike Barker, City Attorney; Laura
                      Oscarson, City Recorder; Scott Cooley, City Engineer; Hazel
                      Dunsmore, Human Resource Director; John Eining, Police Chief;
                      Rhett Ogden, Recreation Director; Clint Smith, Fire Chief; and Bob
                      Wylie, Finance Director

Business Meeting

1.0   Call to Order

2.0   Motion: Approval of June 1, 2021, Draper City Redevelopment Agency Meeting
      Minutes

2.1   Boardmember F. Lowery moved to accept the June 1, 2021, Redevelopment
      Agency Meeting Minutes. Boardmember T. Lowery seconded the motion.

2.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0   Public Hearing: Resolution #RDA R-21-03, approve Resolution #RDA R-21-03
      amending the current Fiscal Year 2021 budget.

3.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, had one budget amendment to present for the
      opened FY2021 RDA budget:

                 1. RDA FY-21-04 (Draper South Mountain CRA): This was a payment
                    of $45,300 to Salt Lake County for the South Mountain CRA
                    project area. This was the amount due for their tax increment
                    administration fee for the project area that Draper received tax
                    increment money for.

3.2   Boardmember Walker opened the public hearing. No one came forward so
      Boardmember Walker closed the public hearing.

3.    Boardmember F. Lowery moved to approve Resolution #RDA-R-21-03,
      amending the current Fiscal Year 2021 budget. Boardmember T. Lowery
      seconded the motion.

3.    A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

4.0   Action Item: Resolution #RDA R-21-04, approve Resolution #RDA R-21-04
      adopting the Fiscal Year Budget for FY2022.

4.    Mr. Wylie said that the final RDA budget for FY2022 was exactly as presented
      for the tentative budget back in May. There were no changes to the RDA
      budget for the final FY2022. It was a $10,977,500 budget.

4.    Boardmember T. Lowery moved to approve Resolution #RDA-R-21-04,
      adopting the Fiscal Year 2022 budget. Boardmember Roberts seconded the
      motion.

4.    A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously

5.0   Adjourn to a City Council Meeting

5.    Boardmember Vawdrey moved to adjourn to a City Council meeting.
      Boardmember Tasha Lowery seconded the motion.

5.    A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Lowry, Roberts,
      Vawdrey, and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

5.    The meeting adjourned at 8:05 p.m.
