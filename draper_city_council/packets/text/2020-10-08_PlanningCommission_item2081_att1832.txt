SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.468 acres located at
13187 S. Grayson View Cove (see Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RA2 (Single Family
Residential, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) and is part of the Grayson Cove subdivision.

The subject property was platted on January 20, 2017 as part of the Grayson Cove subdivision. The
original Grayson Cove subdivision application was approved by the City Council on July 5, 2016. The
approved subdivision was for seven lots all located on a single cul-de-sac. This application is a request to
modify Lot 1 of that subdivision.


ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential Low-Medium
Density land use designation for the subject property (see Exhibit C). This category is characterized as
follows:

The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately two dwelling
units per acre (see Exhibit D). The purpose of the RA2 zone can be found in Draper City Municipal Code
(DCMC) Section 9-8-020(B)(1) and states: “the purpose of the RA1 and RA2 zones is to foster low
density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve
the character of the city's semirural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot
family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these
zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial
and industrial uses.”

The RA2 zoning designation is identified by the General Plan as a preferred zoning classification for the
Residential Low-Medium Density land use designation. The subject property is surrounded by RA2
zoning on the west, north and east. To the south, across 13200 South, the zoning is RA1 (Single Family
Residential, 40,0000 square foot lot minium).

Subdivision Layout. The subject property is a corner lot located at the northwest corner of 13200 South
and Graysonview Cove (see Exhibit E). The applicant is the homeowner who is currently constructing a


Grayson Cove                                                                           App. # SUBD-1008-2020

home on the property. The original plat approval included a grading plan that showed the retention area
for the subdivision at the southwest corner of Lot 1 (see Exhibit F). The applicant is asking that the
retention area be located to the northwest corner of his lot in order to accommodate the construction of the
residence. The new retention area will be clearly shown on the recorded plat for the subdivision as an
easement, similar to the original plat. There are no changes to the size or dimensions of the lot. The
resulting lot will comply with DCMC and will not result in the creation of any non-conformities.

Criteria For Approval. The criteria for review and potential approval of a Plat Amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such requests as:

         “The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing an
         amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds that there is
         good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has
         been vacated or amended.”

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning Commission
review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application based on the findings listed
below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include the following
conditions of approval:

1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building, Planning, and Fire
         Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site and the construction of all buildings
         on the site, including permitting.
2.       That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the development of the
         site and the construction of all buildings on the site.

The findings for approval as are follows:

1.       There is good cause for the amendment and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has been
         vacated or amended.
