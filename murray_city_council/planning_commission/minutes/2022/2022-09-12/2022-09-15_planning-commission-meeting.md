BD PARRIS, LLC – 4373 South Main Street – Project #22-134

Seth Rios presented this request for Conditional Use Permit approval to tear down the cannery
building and build a parking lot for RVs to sell and display. The property is in the CD zone next
to the TOD zone, and because of this, the code calls for TOD sidewalks and improvements in
this area. Per the images in the Staff Report, additional landscaping and required sidewalks
have been planned for. One requirement of the CUP is the applicant will need to combine the
two existing parcels. Mr. Rios displayed an image showing the required combination of
properties as well as additional images of the current area. The site plan submitted does not

on Edison will also need to be updated, as there is nothing there currently. Staff is

as discussed, updates the landscape on Edison, and gets any permits for signage if applicable.
Dave Frampton stated he is representing the owners. He acknowledged that he has reviewed

Ms. Milkavich motioned to approve the Conditional Use Permit to allow RV sales and storage on
the property addressed 4373 South Main Street, subject to the following conditions:
1. The applicant shall obtain a demolition permit before demolishing the existing building.
2. The applicant shall provide the TOD sidewalk improvements along Main Street, as outlined
   in Chapter 17.168 of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
3. The applicant shall consolidate the two lots addressed 4373 South Main Street and 23 East
   Edison Avenue before beginning any work on the property.
4. The applicant shall provide landscaping along all public frontages in accordance with
   Chapter 17.68 of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
5. The applicant shall obtain any necessary building permits prior to installing any signage on
   the property.

WILLOW WOOD PROFESSIONAL PLAZA / YESCO – 622 East 4500 South – Project #22-137
Seth Rios reviewed the request for Conditional Use Permit approval for an electronic message
center sign. The property is located in the G-O Zone, which requires any type of electronic sign
to come before the planning commission for approval, as well as meet all other requirements in
the sign code. Mr. Rios showed images of the area and the site, which were included in the
staff report. As proposed, the sign does comply with all the city requirements. The sign will be
facing 4500 South Street, which is a large road with six lanes and a median. Staff does not
believe this will be an issue. An email was received regarding potential impacts on nearby
residential areas. Mr. Rios visited the site and surrounding area. He presented photos that are
included in the staff report. Those images show the homes facing the subject property have six-
foot high fences, and not be affected from the sign. They will have to reduce the brightness on

the sign after a certain time, and that will be checked during inspections. Staff is recommending
approval based on the seven conditions listed in the staff report.
Ben Olsen, applicant for YESCO, stated that he and the property owner have both reviewed the

Ms. Milkavich asked if the applicant has read the code in regard to what is required on the sign.
Mr. Olsen responded that yes, they have read the code, and the requirements are stated pretty
clearly in the CUP section.
Mr. Pehrson opened the hearing for public comment. There was one comment received prior to
the meeting, which was included in the staff report. There were no comments in-person or
electronically and the hearing was closed.
Mr. Hacker motioned to approve Conditional Use Permit for an electronic sign at the property
address 622 East 4500 South, Subject to the following conditions:

1. The applicant shall follow all height and size restrictions listed for signs in the G-O zone in
   Chapter 17.48.210.
2. The sign shall be set to dim and reduce sign intensity after dark.
3. The sign shall be oriented in a way that is not a traffic hazard or a nuisance to the
   surrounding properties.
4. The person installing the sign will demonstrate that the sign is not emitting light brighter than
   0.3 foot candles before passing inspection.
5. Any display on the electronic sign must remain lighted for at least 2 seconds.
6. The sign may not operate between 10:00 p.m. and 6 a.m. unless it holds a static image that
   does not cause illuminance in excess of the amount listed in the formula found in Chapter
   17.48.280(5) of the Murray City Land Use Ordinance.
7. A minimum of five percent (5%) of the time that the sign is in use shall be devoted to public
   service messages.

Seconded by Mr. Richards.
