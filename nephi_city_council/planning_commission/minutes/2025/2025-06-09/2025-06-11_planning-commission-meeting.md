   2. Public Hearing - Annexation Area Map Change Request: Additions to the Annexation Area
      Map are proposed.
             a.      Staff Presentation

                     Mr. Seth Atkinson presented that there are some things that are afoot in the
                     community. The map was presented to the commissioners for the current area
                     that are annexed and then presented that the west and north benches are being
                     considered for large businesses. Businesses will need power, and the request has
                     come in the form of needed power at the Clover Creek Power Plant. The Agri-
                     park project has come forward, due to the R6 Regional Council. All six of the
                     counties are supportive of the Agri-park. An Agri-park would be similar to
                     industrial areas, as they cater to process products that are raised, grown, and then
                     processed locally.

                     As Nephi City is central to the R6 area, the draw is that infrastructure is
                     available. Power is a main concern, PacificCorp has been hard to get power
                     available, but because Nephi City has already begun the process of the 138
                     KVline it makes Nephi City more attractive for this project. The Agri-park has
                     already purchased land and is currently highlighted in the yellow on the map
                     (Map 1 Attached). This project has some positives and negatives to coming to the
                     area. If they require a lot of jobs it will push housing requests up, further
                     stressing the market. As of right now these companies that would participate in
                     the Agri-park are sending their products out of state, however by doing this
                     project it will make shipment and processing of the items easier to accommodate
                     and in return make farmland more valuable. Currently there are only a few ways
                     to make AG Land more valuable, one is by making it either touristy or bringing
                     the processing closer to home. Nephi City has had many requests from the

                     citizens that the west fields be preserved, this is one of the ways that this can
                     happen and make that farmland more valuable.

                     Another couple of projects that are interested in this area are data centers, they
                     have a lower worker population required, some less than one hundred employees.
                     However, they are large users of utilities, and Nephi City is in the business of
                     selling utilities. In order for the city to do that, Nephi City will need to annex
                     them in due to state regulations. The city airport, and city sewer ponds are also in
                     the area considered for zoning as the city would like to provide it with city
                     power, but due to the cost the city has not been able to due that. Thus, these areas
                     are also included for future annexation. Additionally, utilities will also be
                     required for this to happen in the form of substations and a large gas line.
                     Connection to this is substantial because they are huge power consumers, and
                     data centers are consistent loads as they don’t fluctuate in usage enabling easier
                     supply from the power grid. The more you decrease peaking in the power supply,
                     the cheaper the power is to supply, and Nephi City has the cheapest rates of all
                     UMPA cities.

                     One of the most important things to remember for this is that the data centers do
                     use alot of acreage in development, which is why Nephi City staff are presenting
                     a zoning change request.
