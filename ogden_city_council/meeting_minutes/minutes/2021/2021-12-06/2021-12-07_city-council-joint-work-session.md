FY22 Salary Schedule Amendments- Council Salaries
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported that during the FY2021-2022 budget discussions related to the salaries
of elected officials, the Council indicated that it would consider moving council salaries to benchmark beginning January 1, 2022.
Council staff is proposing an amendment to the FY2022 Salary Schedules to implement the direction given by the Council in June
2021 as shown below:
               •    City Council Chair- $25,200
               •    City Council Vice Chair- $23,850
               •    City Council Member - $22,500
         If approved, the proposed salary increase will increase the budget by $18,000. Ms. Eller-Smith concluded that as part of
the FY2021-2022 budget process, there were discussions about hiring a consultant to perform in-depth benchmark surveys for all
elected positions and the Chief Administrative Officer position, as these positions were not benchmarked in the most recent study.
Evergreen has been hired and will begin that work in January of2022.
         The Council briefly discussed the matter and concluded they are supportive of proceeding with the salary increases and
they are supportive of the work to be performed by Evergreen that will inform the next budget cycle.

Synchrony Bank Line of Credit Renewal
           Community Development Manager Ogden reported that as a part of the Annual Action Plan and the City's administration
of Community Development Block Grant funding, program guidelines are developed for specific programs targeting specific
needs. The program guidelines serve as general parameters for how the City will use funding for that program. These guidelines
are refened to in the Annual Action Plan but are entirely developed and adopted by the City. Amending the program guidelines is
done through a resolution adopted by the City Council. The cUITent proposal is not an amendment to any program guideline;
rather, it is the renewal of a line of credit the City uses to supplement the funding of community development programs. The terms
for the Synchrony Bank line of credit of $1.4 million remain the same as previously adopted. Mr. Ogden then reported on the
projects that have been completed with the assistance of the line of credit in the past and asked for Council support of the renewal.
           Discussion among the Council and Mr. Ogden centered on the terms of the line of credit, the manner in which the City
acquires the homes that are rehabilitated using line of credit proceeds, and efforts to include waterwise landscaping in rehab
projects.

FY22 RDA Budget Amendment- DL Evans Loan, Quality Neighborhood
Program Income
         Comptroller Stout explained this proposed budget amendment recognizes additional revenues in the Continental Project
Area from loan proceeds ($1,887,425) and tax increments ($17,075) and appropriates those revenues to the loan fees ($20,925)
and development costs for Wonder Block ($1,883,575). The budget amendment also recognizes revenues in the Housing Fund
from lease revenue ($150,000) and the sale of property ($331,850) and appropriates those funds to rental property expenditures
($150,000) and Quality Neighborhood housing projects ($331,850). The proposed amendments would increase the FY2022
Redevelopment Agency (RDA) budget by $2,386,350.

Council Business
          Neighbor-up Grant Program Amendments: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported on proposed amendments
to the program guidelines, the most significant being to reduce the total grant amount possible from $1,000 to $57 5. This will help
to resolve conflicts with guidelines for other City programs. An additional amendment is to encourage grant funded events to
occur on private property rather than public properties. She added that Council staff would like to translate the program
documents into Spanish to make it easier for the City's Diversity Commission to promote it.
          Council discussion of the grant program centered on past grant awards and subsequent projects/events completed with the
proceeds, the process for applying for a grant, and the overarching goals for the program.
          Rules/Norms Amendments: Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported the proposed amendments to the Council
Rules and Norms are as follows:
              1. ModifY the Regular Meetings section to update meeting schedule.

              3. ModifY the Civility and Decorum section to clarifY the reason and purpose for limiting outward expressions of
                   support or disagreement with a given position or statement.
