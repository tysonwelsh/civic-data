Re: Public Hearing: Approve Resolution # 20-56; A Resolution adopting the 2020 Water Conservation Plan U Comments: Since 1998, Utah State legislature has required each water system provide a five-year water conservation plan and update that plan every five years.  Draper City completed the conservation plan and with this resolution is presenting the 2020 Water Conservation Plan Update for adoption.  This update meets the requirements of the Utah State "Water Conservation Plan Act"  (§73-10-32 UCA).
The 2020 Water Conservation Plan outlines the progress in which water consumption, based on the measured standard of the number of gallons used per capita per day (gpcd), is trending toward the conservation goal the city adopted in 2014 of 183 gpcd of usage.  In 2019, within the city's water franchise area, the usage was 186 gpcd.
The 2020 Water Conservation Plan Update provides four water conservation activities to facilitate reaching the city's conservation goal.  These four programs are expanding and cooperating with partners and other agencies in wastewater reuse, continuing public education programs, evaluating and strengthening conservation ordinances, and installing advanced metering infrastructure and enhancing leak detection. The findings are as follows: The City Council finds that the 2020 Water Conservation Plan Update satisfies the requirements of  §73-10-32 Utah Code Ann. and is in the best interests of the city to achieve its water conservation goal.
