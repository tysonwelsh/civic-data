2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Consideration of T-Mobile's request for approval of the North Slopes Stealth Cell Tower
located on top of an existing office building at 4100 North Chapel Ridge Road in the Traverse
Mountain Planned Community.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant was not present, and Commissioner Nielsen clarified this is not a public hearing
item.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve T-Mobile's request for approval of
        the North Slopes Stealth Cell Tower located on top of an existing office building at
        4100 North Chapel Ridge Road in the Traverse Mountain Planned Community;

        with the findings that the proposed cell facility meets the requirements of a roof mounted
        antenna, has a reasonable height in the context of the existing neighborhood, will be
        located and conducted in a manner in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi
        City General Plan and the purposes of this Code, and the conduct of the use in such a
        manner that it will not be detrimental to adjoining properties and the area. Including all
        DRC comments. Commissioner Eyre seconded the motion.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Lee and Mary Kelly’s request for review of the
Kelly General Plan Amendment on 1.08-acres of property located at 3055 North 1200 West
changing the land use designation from VLDR (very low density residential) to NC
(neighborhood commercial).
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicants, Lee and Mary Kelly, were present. They feel the amendment meets the
establishment for what the City would like to happen. They do not have a current concept in
mind right now.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Troy Sheffield, a Lehi resident, inquired what the plan would be and what input he would have
in the future where his property borders the applicants and would lose the buffer. He asked if the
concept could be pushed closer to the road than his property. Mr. Sheffield also asked about
buffering and fencing requirements.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker motioned to recommend approval of Lee and Mary
        Kelly’s request for review of the Kelly General Plan Amendment on 1.08-acres of
        property located at 3055 North 1200 West changing the land use designation from
        VLDR (very low density residential) to NC (neighborhood commercial; with the
        findings that the proposed amendment to the Lehi City General Plan is compatible with
        the existing surrounding uses, provides a better use next to an arterial road, is in
        conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the General Plan and its various
        elements, is consistent with the land uses and zoning of nearby and adjoining properties,
        and will not have a substantial adverse effect on surrounding property. Including all DRC
        comments. Commissioner Newall seconded the motion.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

2.6) Public hearing and consideration of Dennis Schoonover’s request for conditional use
approval of the Wagner Drive Thru Site Plan located at 600 West State Street.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Dennis Schooner, was present. He discussed landscaping and parking. Dennis
stated he has been working with the City to straighten out 600 West to help with safety concerns.
Commissioner Everett asked about continuous directional arrows in the parking lot, which the
applicant stated they will be doing. Rick Bennion was the other applicant. They stated they
would look into the perimeter of the inside drive through.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Cory Gurney, a Lehi resident, stated the overflow is usually only affected twice a week. He
addressed sound, buffer, littering, lights from vehicles and the building, landscaping, light
pollution and operating hours, and traffic/stacking concerns. Mr. Gurney would like to make sure

there are consequences if the uses that are approved are not adhered to. He would like to make
sure the developer is held accountable and assurances. He would like these concerns to be passed
along to City Council with those recommendations.

Melissa Knokes, a Lehi resident, stated Cory Gurney covered most of the issues. She is
concerned with her driveway being affected by the development where it is directly across from
the entrance/exit. She also had questions about widening of 600 West. She requested a landscape
buffer.

Dave Barnhart, a Lehi resident, agree with Cory Gurney and Melissa Knokes. He would like to
request a landscape buffer and is really concerned about stacking.

Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged an online comment and closed the public hearing.

The applicant stated they have done some landscaping, but the City has limited the to some
landscaping due to line of site for traffic. 600 West will be widened, and the telephone poles will
be moved, with a park strip, but will not cut into the residents parking lot. Luke Seegmiller stated
they will have to look into how wide the road will be widened to clarify. There would be a
microphone, but the trajectory of the sound will not be directed at the residents. The lighting will
follow code and the DRC comments. Luke stated the item did not warrant a traffic study. Trash
pickup and time had not been determined.

Commissioner Everett liked the idea of revisiting the approval in a year. He would like to see the
stacking and lighting mitigated. He asked if it would be reasonable to put a condition on when
garbage could be emptied due to the noise. Commissioner Bunker liked the different businesses
and peak hours are different times. Commissioner Newall felt the minimal menu items with
separate things will help mitigate congestion. Commissioner Nielsen asked the applicant to look
at the park strip on state street to make sure it would meet standards and not cause any issues.
Craig Chambers discussed recourse and conditions under the nuisance ordinance that needed to
be met. He explained permitted uses and what conditions would need to be met in the code and
the conditions must be reasonable and that the Commissions responsibility is to regulate the
operations and then the developer to fix it. Commissioner Ellis wanted to make sure the trash
was maintained on their property.

Commissioner Nielsen re-opened the public hearing.

Melissa Knokes had a question on the wall and landscaping. She suggested landscaping to help
with buffering.

Commissioner Nielsen re-closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Dennis Schoonover’s request for
    conditional use approval of the Wagner Drive Thru Site Plan located at 600 West State
    Street; with the findings that the proposed use will not be detrimental or injurious to
    property or improvements in the vicinity, will be located and conducted in a manner in

    compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the purposes of this
    Code, and the property on which the use, building, or other structure is proposed to be
    established is of adequate size and dimensions to permit construction of the facilities and the
    conduct of the use in such a manner that it will not be detrimental to adjoining properties and
    the area. Noting the conditions for hours of operation be from 6:00 am – 10:00 pm,
    recommending an eight foot solid fence in length of approximately 40 feet including the turn
    for consistency to keep light, sound and trash contained, that the lighting turns off after
    business hours and meets the code requirements to prevent light pollution, that there will be
    proper striping to limit traffic issues and the possibility of three lanes for a queuing lane to
    avoid stacking, with the recommendation to revisit in one year from. Including all DRC
    comments. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
