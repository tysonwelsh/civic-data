17   Keith Morey, Development Services Assistant Director, presented. In early 2018, the owners of
18   Day's Market were planning an extensive remodel of their aging store and requested that Provo
19   City assist in reducing the construction costs by entering into a ten-year sales tax
20   reimbursement agreement on a post-performance basis. Days Market would spend
21   approximately $1.3 million dollars on the interior remodel of the store. Similar to other sales
22   tax agreements, Day's would be able to earn back some of their costs if they produced sales
23   above an established base line -- which in this case was set at $39,800. They qualified for sales
24   tax reimbursement of $8,772.09 for fiscal year 2021.
25
26   Chair Sewell invited public comment. There was no response. With no council discussion, Chair
27   Sewell called for a vote on the implied motion.
28
            Vote:        The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley,
                         Harding, Hoban, Sewell, and Shipley in favor.
