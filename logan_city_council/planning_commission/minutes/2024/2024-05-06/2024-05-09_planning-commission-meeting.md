PC 24-019 Two Rein STR [Conditional Use Permit] Jared Adams/Two Rein Properties LLC,
authorized agent/owner is requesting a conditional use permit to operate a 3-bedroom short term
rental in the entire home located at 741 South 650 West in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential
(NR-6) zone; TIN 02-213-0011 (Woodruff Neighborhood).

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
available in the Community Development Department.

2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a three (3) bedroom short term rental occupying the full
single-family home.

3. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes two (2) people per bedroom with two (2) additional
individuals for a maximum of eight (8) occupants in the STR.

4. A short-term rental may not be the subject of multiple rental contracts by separate individuals,
groups, or parties for the same night or nights.

5. Occupancy for the single-family home portion of the structure shall comply with LDC regulations.

6. A minimum of three (3) parking stalls are required for the STR. Two stalls in the garage and one
stall, 9x18 feet in size, located at least 25 feet away from the curb on 650 West Street located to
the south of the garage. All parking areas shall be paved with an approved hard surface.

7. Parking of recreational vehicles, trailers, boats, ATVs, or similar vehicles associated with the STR
shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on-street.

8. Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.

9. Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two
consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records
available for review and inspection by Logan City.

10. Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8 am.

11.A Logan City building/fire inspection is required which may result in building remodeling and
building permits prior to the issuance of a business license.


12. A Logan City business license is required before a rental contract can be issued.

13. The STR shall comply with local and state Building, Health, Fire, Safety, Maintenance, and
Nuisance Codes prior to operating.

14. Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast and reviewed and approved prior to the
issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.

15. No signs are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All signage shall be approved and
permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

16. No fences are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All fences shall be approved and
permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

17. All points of use of water downstream of this property's water meter should meet Utah’s current
plumbing code and its amendments along with Utah’s Administrative Drinking Water rules. This
would help minimize the risk of a contamination of the water that would affect the home's
occupants or lodger and Logan Cities water system. This also would help minimize liabilities to
the owner.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. The proposed project conforms to the requirements of the Land Development Code and is listed
as a conditional use in the Use Table.

2. This STR meets the City’s STR density and location parameters as specified in Chapter
17.37.130.

3. As conditioned the use is compatible with the surrounding land uses and will not interfere with
the use and enjoyment of adjoining or area properties.

4. The streets providing access and other infrastructure to the subject property have adequate
capacities and level of service for the conditional use.

5. The Planning Commission has substantiated the findings for a Conditional Use Permit in Chapter
17.42.050.

PC _ 24-020 BSR Roofing Warehouse [Design Review Permit] Jace Skabelund/BSR Property
Holdings LLC, owner/authorized agent/owner is requesting a design review permit for a new 6,000
square foot warehouse located at 1097 West 200 South in the Commercial Services (CS) zone; TIN
05-107-0012 (Ellis Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Planner reviewed the request for a new 6,000-square-foot warehouse building
located at 1097 West 200 South in the Commercial Service (CS) zone. As proposed the project
meets all access, circulation, parking, building setbacks, and open space requirements in the Land
Development Code for the CS zone. The building design is required to have 30% fenestration on
street-facing facades. The east side of the building, facing 1080 West, is being proposed at 15%
fenestration with windows on the upper floor and enhanced landscaping along the building to make
up the difference. The Planning Commission will need to determine if 15% fenestration is acceptable
and if additional design alternatives such as landscaping, or trellis structures are appropriate to offset
the fenestration requirement.


Commissioner Croshaw asked where the proposed access is.

Darren Farar, City Engineer stated that as per the note on the original subdivision plat, this property
shall not gain access from 200 South. The access will have to be from 1080 West because the
proposed access from 200 South would be too close to the intersection.

Proponent: Jace Skabelund said he would like to use additional landscaping along the east side of
the building to meet the fenestration requirement and to help with heating and cooling the building.

Public: No public comments were made.

Commission: Commissioner Peterson said where 200 South will be a main corridor to the future
park planned to the west, there should be something on the east side of the building to meet the
fenestration requirement.

The Commission discussed using landscaping to meet the fenestration requirement and if they
should require a specific percentage of landscaping and/or other elements to make up the additional
15% needed.

Mike DeSimone suggested the Commission could also require two columnar trees per building
section with shrubs between to screen the bottom half of the building along the east side.

Commissioner Lewis agreed with M. DeSimone’s suggestion to screen the lower level with trees and
shrubs and said it would also help with the heating & cooling.

Commissioner Heare said he would rather see additional landscaping, not trellis, windows, or
spandrel glass.

Commissioner Dourte said that an additional 15% of landscaping could be a small hedge along the
building and would prefer requiring a specific number of trees and shrubs to span the length of the
building.

Commissioner Guth said there are other inexpensive ways to screen the building that could be used
with trees, it doesn’t have to be one or the other.

Commissioner Croshaw agreed that there should be several trees required, not just a percentage.

The Commission discussed striking condition number 11 (it does not apply to this project), rewording
the Light & Power conditions, and amending condition number 3 to allow 15% fenestration on the
east side fagade with the addition of 12 columnar trees (2 per panel as shown in the submitted plans)
planted along the east side of the building, and which are in addition to the minimum landscaping
requirements.

WORKSHOP: R. Holley reviewed the existing Land Development Code for Wireless
Telecommunications Facilities, including purpose, required permits, placement and design
requirements, and which zones towers are permitted in.

The Commission discussed several different elements of regulating Wireless Telecommunication
Facilities (WTF) including the following: limit/eliminate cell towers in certain zones: limit the numbers
of cell towers on public property & within parks or other recreational areas; utilize existing towers,
structures, signs, buildings or light poles whenever feasible to locate WTF's; enhanced/reduced
setbacks from different types of elements or features; consider the viewscape when locating a WTF;
screening of WTF’s; requiring stealth design; and tower heights (both shorter and taller) versus the
number of cell towers throughout the City.

The Commission also discussed a broader City policy of prohibiting new WTF’s in public parks or on
publicly owned property and having the City consider conducting a masterplan for locating and
managing WTF’s citywide.

it was decided that staff will provide recommended code revisions for the Commissian’s consideration
in an upcoming meeting.
