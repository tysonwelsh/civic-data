2.    City of West Jordan Zone 3 North 3 MG Reservoir; Continued from 2-4-20; 6050 West
      7800 South; Preliminary/Final Site Plan and Conditional Use Approval for Utility, Major;
      Preliminary/Final Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit; P-C Zone; City of West Jordan
      (applicant) [#20340, parcel 20-26-326-012]

Ray McCandless said the property is within the Copper Rim development and is two acres in size. The
lot is adjacent to Mountain View Corridor. The water tank will be within the commercial portion of the
overall development. He reviewed current and future access points. Because this is in a planned
community zone, staff would like to see more screening with additional trees on the perimeter and low
shrubs on the sloped berm against the tank. Staff did not think that the chain link fence with barbed
wire shown on the site plan fit with the development, so public works staff agreed to provide a
wrought iron fence with masonry columns like the one at the Jordan Landing tank site.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the preliminary/final site plan and
conditional use for the City of West Jordan Zone 3 North, 3 Million Gallon Water Tank located at
6050 West 7800 South subject to the approval conditions listed below.

Site Plan
1.      The proposed development shall meet all applicable 2009 City Code requirements.
2.      All changes to the site shall be consistent with the Site Plan application.

       masonry pillars or as approved by the Zoning Administrator.

3.    Oakridge Park Phase 5 Rezone; 8200 South 6700 West; Rezone 9.98 acres from P-F
      (Public Facilities) to R-1-8E (Single-family residential 8,000 square foot minimum lots)
      Zone; Ivory Development LLC/Skylar Tolbert (applicant) [#20400; parcel 20-34-252-002]

Skylar Tolbert, Ivory Development, provided information about Ivory’s commitment last February to
plant 30,000 trees in Utah. Last year they planted 7,000 of those trees with over 300 in Oakridge Park
phases 1 through. Phase 5 will have another 60-70 trees. He encouraged the City to tell them of any
tree planting needs/projects so they can help out in the community. Regarding the proposed zone
