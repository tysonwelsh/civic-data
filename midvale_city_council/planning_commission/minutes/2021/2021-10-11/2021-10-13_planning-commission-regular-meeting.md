GENERAL SESSION

Chair Tippetts called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. She
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

DISCUSSION

6.     Consent Agenda


PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION OCTOBER 13, 2021
Mr. Olsen stated that Staff wanted to gather the Commissioner’s feedback on including a consent
agenda in the Planning Commission Meeting. He added that a consent agenda is present when
projects are very straight forward and are addressed with one motion. If there is an item associated
with a public hearing on the consent agenda, it will be opened and then closed, and the motion will
be made with the complete consent agenda. This is a more efficient way to have meetings
especially if there are controversial items that call for more time to have discussion.

Commissioner Snow asked what type of items would be considered to go on a consent agenda?

Mr. Olsen stated that it would be those items that have not received comment and staff feels like
fits on the consent agenda. If at any time the commissioners feel differently, they can request to
pull it from the consent agenda.

Commissioner Erickson asked what the timeline is to pull an item from a consent agenda?

Mr. Olsen said that it has seen it take place at a meeting. It could be a situation where a staff may
have thought it was a simple subdivision request and a member of the public has concerns
pertaining to the request and the Commission can pull the item from the consent agenda and
addressed on its own.

Commissioner Erickson stated that the Commission would just like to ensure that public comments
are still heard.
