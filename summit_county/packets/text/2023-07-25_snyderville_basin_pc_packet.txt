Type of Item:  Condominium Plat Amendment 
Process:  Administrative Review 
 
RECOMMENDATION:  Staff has reviewed the application for compliance with all standards in the 
Snyderville Basin Development Code and has found that it meets the requirements for approval. 
Staff recommends that the Snyderville Basin  Planning Commission review the proposed  
amendment to the Summit Center Condominium plat , conduct a public hearing , and vote to 
forward a positive recommendation  to the County Manager pursuant  to the findings of fact, 
conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in this Staff Report.   
 
Project Description 
Project Number:  23-088 
Project Name:   Summit Center Condominiums Second Amended 
Applicant(s):   David Nadler 
Property Owner(s):  Summit Center Property Owners LLC 
Zone District:   Rural Residential 
Parcel Number and Size: SUMCC-101-K; 0.646 acres 
Type of Process:  Administrative 
Final Land Use Authority: County Manager 
  
Proposal 
The applicant proposes to combine suite 101-K with suites 101-J and 101-I of the Summit 
Center Condominiums, which totals 751 square feet. Additionally, the applicant proposes to 
add 529 square feet of common area to the suite for a total of 1,280 square feet. The amended 
condominium unit will be used for a leasing office. 
 
The proposed plat, The Summit Center Condominiums Second Amended, is attached as Exhibit 
A.

Background 
Summit Center is a 140-unit non-residential condominium located within the Summit Center 
Commerce Park Subdivision.  The development agreement for Summit Center was entered into 
on November 17, 2004 after the approval of a Specially Planned Area (SPA) by the Board of 
County Commissioners.    
 
The Summit Center Condominiums plat was recorded on February 29, 2008 (Exhibit B).  There is 
no maximum square footage listed in the recorded development agreement or the 
condominium covenants, conditions, and restrictions. 
 
The applicant has held a board meeting with all condominium owners and received unanimous 
approval of the proposal. 
 
Section 10-3-8 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code states that “a detailed condominium 
plat is required in all cases which comply with the definition of "condominium" contained in 
chapter 11 of this title. A condominium plat shall contain the information required for a final site 
plan as identified in section 10 -3-15 of this chapter. CCRs for the development shall also be 
submitted for review by the Summit County attorney's office prior to recordation of the plat.”   A 
condominium is defined as: 
Any structure which has been submitted to condominium ownership under the provisions 
of the Utah condominium ownership act, whether for residential, nonresidential, and any 
other use.

3 
 
 
 
Analysis and Findings 
The Snyderville Basin Development Code does not contain standards for approval of a 
condominium plat; however Utah State Code does require that Condominium Plats be recorded 
with the associated Covenants, Conditions, and Restrictions for the development. These 
documents have been provided to Staff.  
 
The process for approval of a condominium plat follows the same as the process as a Final Site 
Plan. A Final Site Plan is required to be recommended by the SBPC, after a public hearing, to the 
County Manager. 
 
Recommendation 
Staff has reviewed the application for compliance with all standards in the Snyderville Basin  
Development Code and has found that it meets the requirements for approval.  Staff 
recommends that the Snyderville Basin Planning Commission review the proposed amendment 
to the Summit Center Condominiums , conduct a public hearing , and vote to forward a positive 
recommendation to the County Manager pursuant  to the following findings of fact, conclusions 
of law, and conditions of approval:  
 
Findings of Fact: 
1. On June 2, 2023, an application to amend the Summit Center Condominium plat 
(SUMCC-101-K) was submitted to the Community Development Department.   
2. SUMCC-101-K (2750 Rasmussen Road) is 0.646 acres. 
3. SUMCC-101-K is zoned Rural Residential.   
4. The applicant is David Nadler. 
5. The property owner is Summit Center Property Owners LLC. 
6. The final site plan for the Summit Center Commercial Park Subdivision was recorded 
February 29, 2008. 
7. The Summit Center Condominium Plat was recorded February 29, 2008. 
8. The applicant proposes to combine suite 101-K with suites 101-J and 101-I, which totals 
751 square feet. Additionally, the applicant proposes to add 529 square feet of common 
area to the suite for a total of 1,280 square feet. 
9. The application has been routed to applicable service providers and all service provider 
comments have been addressed.    
 
Conclusions of Law: 
1. The amendment to the condominium plat is consistent with all prior approval for the 
project.  
2. The amendment to the condominium plat will not increase the maximum allowed 
square footage for the project.  
3. Approval of the condominium plat will not adversely affect the public health, safety, and 
general welfare. 
 
Conditions of Approval: 
1. All service provider requirements shall be met.
