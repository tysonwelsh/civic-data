STUDY SESSION

At 3:30 p.m.     Canceled

REGULAR MEETING

NOTICE OF ALL-ELECTRONIC MEETING
To minimize the potential spread of COVID-19, and as authorized by Utah Governor Gary Herbert’s March 18,
2020 Executive Order, the Orem City Planning Commission meeting on May 20, 2020 will be an all-electronic
meeting. There will be no public access to the Orem City Council chambers. Those desiring to view or participate in
the meeting must do so electronically. You can view the meeting live at orem.org/meetings. If you want to make a
comment, please use: orem.org/comments or PlanningCommissionPublicComments@orem.org

Comments will be accepted before and during the meeting. All comments received will be read into the record
during the live meeting. Once an item has been voted on, that item will be closed to additional comments. Thank
you for your cooperation during these challenging times.

Place:           Orem City Council Chambers

At 4:30 p.m.     Chair Cochran called the Planning Commission meeting to order and offered the invocation.

Those present: Mickey Cochran, Planning Commission Chair; Ryan L. Clark, Development Services Director;
               Jason W. Bench, Planning Division Manager; Cheryl Vargas and Jake Harding, Associate Planners
               and Steve Earl, Legal Counsel

                 Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Mike Staker, and Ross Spencer, Planning
                 Commission members; Sam Kelly, City Engineer; and Brady Hale, Transportation Engineer (via
                 telephone)

Those excused: Grant Allen, Long Range Planner; Jenna Albers, Long Range Planner; Kendall Willardson,
               Planning Intern; and Terry Peterson, City Council Liaison

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Richard & Diane Taylor for the Planning Commission to vacate Lot 3, Hoyal
Subdivision, Plat B, vacate Lot 4, Hoyal Subdivision, Plat C and approve the final plat of Hoyal Subdivision, Plat E
located at 342 North 1160 East in the R8 zone.

                                                                         Staff Presentation: The applicant owns
                                                                         both properties. The applicants intend to
                                                                         construct an addition on their home in the
                                                                         rear yard. They are hoping to move the
                                                                         property line to align with the properties to
                                                                         the north. This will give them enough space
                                                                         in the rear yard to meet the rear setback
                                                                         requirement for this addition. They are
                                                                         aware that the existing easements will
                                                                         remain and understand they cannot infringe
                                                                         upon them.


Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission vacate Lot 3, Hoyal Subdivision, Plat B, vacate Lot 4, Hoyal
Subdivision, Plat C and approve the final plat of Hoyal Subdivision, Plat E located at 342 North 1160 East in the R8
zone.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Vargas. When there were none, Chair
Cochran invited the applicant to speak up via telephone. Mr. Taylor spoke about how it made more sense to align his
property with the north property.

Chair Cochran then opened the public hearing and invited those who wanted to make a comment on this item to send
in their comments via email or chat.

When there were no comments/chats, Chair Cochran closed the public hearing and called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Sakar moved to vacate Lot 3, Hoyal Subdivision, Plat B, vacate Lot 4, Hoyal
Subdivision, Plat C and approve the final plat of Hoyal Subdivision, Plat E located at 342 North 1160 East in the R8
zone. Mr. Spencer seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille
Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer, and Mike Staker. The motion passed unanimously.

Chair Cochran asked Mr. Earl to present Agenda Item 4.1:

Mr. Earl then presented the annual URMA Planning Commission Training.

Minutes: The Planning Commission reviewed the minutes from the previous meeting. Chair Cochran then called for
a motion to approve the minutes of May 20, 2020. Mr. Cook moved to approve the meeting minutes for May 20,
2020. Mr. Cochran seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Those voting aye: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille
Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer, and Mike Staker. The motion passed unanimously.

Adjourn: Chair Cochran called for a motion to adjourn. Mr. Spencer moved to adjourn. Mr. Roberts seconded the
motion. Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Haysam Sakar, Ross Spencer, and Mike
Staker. The motion passed unanimously.

Adjourn: 5:01 p.m.
