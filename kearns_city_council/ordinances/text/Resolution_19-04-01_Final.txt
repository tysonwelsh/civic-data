| A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF AN EASEMENT FROM THE TOWNSHIP TO
SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE BENEFIT OF THE COUNTY LIBRARY.
| A. The Kearns Metro Township Council has previously closed a public right of way
to traffic for purposes of accommodating the building of a public library.
| B. Salt Lake County now desires an easement over the right of way so that it can
| allow pedestrian access across the right of way as part of the library plaza design.
| NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Council of the Kearns
Metro Township:
| 1. The Mayor of the Township is hereby authorized to sign the Easement in the form
| attached hereto as Exhibit A.
2. This resolution will be effective upon signing.
