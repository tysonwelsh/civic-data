Study Session

1.0   Update: Draper Days

1.1   Kellie Challburg and David Wilks provided an update on plans for Draper
      Days.

2.0   Report: Traffic

2.1   Police Chief Rich Ferguson provided a traffic report for the month of May
      2023.

3.0   Council/Manager Reports

3.1   City Manager David Dobbins reported the number of motorized scooters on
      public trails and sidewalks had increased since the end of the school year and
      asked the Council for direction regarding enforcement. Chief Ferguson spoke
      of difficulties or obstacles to enforcement and suggested beginning by
      educating parents. The Council discussed the possibility of prohibiting the use
      of most motorized scooters and bicycles on City trails and sidewalks. Mr.
      Dobbins agreed to bring back a recommendation.

3.2   Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky and Mr. Dobbins
      explained changes to state law regarding bonding for landscaping
      improvements and answered questions from the Council. A majority of the
      Council appeared to support requiring landscaping to be in place within 180
      days of a Certificate of Occupancy.

3.3   Public Works Director Scott Cooley updated the Council on the bid process for
      a planned roundabout project.

3.4   Councilmember Green spoke of the possibility of a conditional use permit for
      a prospective apartment/ADU project. The Council discussed different
      scenarios and possible criteria. A majority of the Council appeared to be in
      favor of actively enforcing existing regulations.

11.0   Council/Manager Reports continued from the Study Session

11.1   Mayor Walker said the Parks and Trails Committee desired to put a temporary
       pedestrian bridge in place where Corner Canyon Road washed out to facilitate
       continued use. Public Works Director Scott Cooley said staff were working with
       a geotechnical engineer to find a permanent solution, and said he could ask
       the engineer about the possibility of a temporary solution. A majority of the
       Council appeared to support looking at temporary solutions.

11.2   Councilmember T. Lowery said she had noticed an increase in graffiti. Staff
       said they were working on finding the best approach to communicate with the
       public.

12.0   Adjournment

12.1   Councilmember Green moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilmember T.
       Lowery seconded the motion.

12.2   A voice vote was taken, and the motion passed unanimously (5-0).

12.3   The meeting adjourned at 7:51 p.m.
