G. ACTION ITEMS:
    1. CONTRACT WITH CRS ENGINEERS TO PROVIDE FOR THE DESIGNERS TO PARTICIPATE AS A DESIGN SUPPORT
       DURING THE CONSTRUCTION PHASE.
          Gary Myers, of County Engineering, presented this contract modification with CRS, which assisted with the
          design for the 12th Street reconstruction project, segment 3b, and is not to exceed $98,640. Because of the many
          issues that may arise on this construction project—because there are multiple private irrigation companies within
          the county’s right-of-way, etc.—this allows the designers to be available during the construction phase on an as-
          needed basis. Commissioner Froerer asked if this could have been put into the original bid packet to eliminate
          the need for a modification. Mr. Myers said that possibly a retainer could have been placed on the contract but
          the county used a more innovative approach with this contract to see if CRS could come up with a cost savings.
          Additionally, at times all the money is not spent on these projects. Chair Jenkins said that he didn’t understand
          why Engineering could not do this work themselves. Mr. Myers said that this would help him on the field because
          these projects are complex and because his department is short manned by two employees and this work would
          be challenging at this time. Commissioner Harvey asked for a completion date and Mr. Myers said that it is
          planned for next fall. He said that the biggest hurdle is getting the amount of asphalt needed to be placed per the
          county’s outlined specifications, and that as crews are moving their utilities, complication have arisen.
          Commissioner Harvey spoke of the need to have a concrete completion date. Chair Jenkins asked that Mr. Myers
          speak with him personally about these projects in the future. Mr. Myers said that they are trending $30,000-
          $40,000 under budget on the construction phase that ended in July.
          Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with CRS Engineers to provide for the designers to
          participate as a design support during the construction phase and that the $98,640 will be on an as-needed basis
          with Mr. Myers determining whether the service is needed. He expressed concern with the ambiguous completion
          date and encouraged a more certain date. Mr. Myers had confused the date with another contract earlier and
          stated that a firm completion date is in the contract and believes it is December 1. Chair Jenkins seconded.
          Commissioner Harvey – nay; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

    6. CONTRACT WITH THE U.S. DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE, OFFICE OF CIVIL RIGHTS, FOR IMPROVING REENTRY
       FOR ADULTS WITH CO-OCCURRING SUBSTANCE ABUSE AND MENTAL ILLNESS.
         Brian Baggs, of the County Sheriff’s Office, stated that earlier this year the Sheriff’s Office, in conjunction with
         Alpha Counseling, started the Weber Addictions and Reentry Program (WARP) designed to provide case
         management and cognitive behavioral re-entry services to offenders in the county. In conjunction with Alpha
         Counseling, application was made for several Bureau of Justice assistance grants to expand and test the program.
         Notification was received on 9/26/2019 of a program grant award for $999,999. Alpha Counseling and the
         University of Cincinnati (the latter will run the randomized control trial) are sub-award grantees. Community
         support of this program has been given by several entities including Adult Probation and Parole, Vocational
         Rehabilitation, and the Utah Bureau of Criminal Identification. Sharlott Sutherland of the County Sheriff’s Office
         addressed general questions.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with the U.S. Department of Justice, Office of Civil Rights,
         for improving reentry for adults with co-occurring substance abuse and mental illness; Commissioner Froerer
         seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
