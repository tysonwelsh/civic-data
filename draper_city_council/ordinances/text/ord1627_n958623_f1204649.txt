City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1627

On December 3, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1627 amending
the text Title 7 of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding the Draper City Cemetery.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 5th day of December 2024. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
