                                         ALTA, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 2021-R-7

                A RESOLUTION REPEAL AND REPLACING THE PURCHASING POLICY
            FOR PROCUREMENT OF SERVICES, SUPPLIES AND EQUIPMENT FOR THE
                                  TOWN OF ALTA

                WHEREAS, on April 14, 2020, the Alta Town Council (the "Council") of the Town of
        Alta (the "Town") met in regular meeting to consider, among other things, repealing and
        replacing the policy regarding procurement of services, supplies and equipment for the Town; and

                 WHEREAS, the Purchasing Policy was last amended on June 16, 2011; and

                 WHEREAS, it would benefit the Town to replace the Purchasing Policy to clarify for
        staff the administrating purchasing; and

               WHEREAS, the Council desires to maintain the most efficient process possible for
        procurement of services, supplies and equipment; and

                 WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
        interest of the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of the Town to repeal and replace the
        policy regarding procurement of services, supplies and equipment.

               NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council of the Town of Alta that
        Purchasing Policy for the Town of Alta be amended to read as attached in Exhibit A
        (PURCHASING/PROCUREMENT POLICY FOR THE TOWN OF ALTA)


This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


                 PASSED AND APPROVED this 14th day of April, 2021.

                                                                TOWN OF ALTA
