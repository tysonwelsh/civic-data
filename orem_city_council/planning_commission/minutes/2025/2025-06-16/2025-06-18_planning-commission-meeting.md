    1. General Plan Update – Land Use Element: Redevelopment

         Matt Taylor presented a review of the Redevelopment Element for the General Plan. The presentation covered the
         distinctions between redevelopment, rehabilitation, and conservation, and highlighted various tools the City could use
         to revitalize aging areas. These tools included adaptive reuse, infill development, infrastructure upgrades, and
         placemaking initiatives. The Commission discussed areas of concern such as declining commercial corridors, aging
         housing stock, mobile home communities, and sites with frequent police activity. They also examined zoning barriers
         that hinder rehabilitation efforts, such as limitations on updating legal nonconforming duplexes or multifamily units.

         Commissioners expressed interest in exploring new zoning tools or overlays to encourage investment, especially in
         areas with absentee landlords or code enforcement issues. The discussion also addressed the need for neighborhood-
         scale retail, better lot size variety, and preservation of housing diversity. Commissioners emphasized the importance
         of establishing guiding values for redevelopment, including support for affordability, public safety, owner occupancy,
         and a mix of housing types. The Commission agreed that the Redevelopment chapter of the General Plan should
         reflect long-term goals for neighborhood character, revitalization incentives, and infrastructure sustainability.

Agenda Item 3.3 – Rand Subdivision Preliminary Plat – Located generally at 470 North 300 West

Commissioners identified an error in the public notice, which listed the incorrect address for the subdivision. Due to inadequate
notice to surrounding property owners, the item was continued.

Motion: Commissioner Erickson moved to continue Item 3.3 to the July 2, 2025 Planning Commission meeting. Commissioner
Carpenter seconded. Motion passed unanimously.

Agenda Item 4.1 – Canyon Park Preliminary Plat – Located generally at 1200 North 400 East

Staff requested that the item be continued until engineering concerns could be resolved and the Development Review
Committee (DRC) completes its review.

Motion: Commissioner Carpenter moved to continue Item 4.1 to the next available Planning Commission meeting following DRC
review. Commissioner Crismon seconded. Motion passed unanimously.

Agenda Item 4.2 – Ken Garff Site Plan – Located at 134 East 1200 South

Staff presented the site plan for an expansion of the Ken Garff dealership parking area. The project includes demolition of an
existing home and outbuildings, new parking stalls, and installation of landscaping and screening walls per the associated
development agreement.

Commissioners inquired about long-term maintenance of landscaping in front of the wall. Staff clarified that the Ken Garff
organization is responsible.

Motion: Commissioner Erickson moved to approve the site plan for the expansion of the Ken Garff dealership. Commissioner
Carpenter seconded. Motion passed unanimously.

Adjournment: Motion to adjourn by Commissioner Erickson, seconded by Commissioner Carpenter. Motion passed
unanimously. The Regular Session adjourned at 6:12 p.m.
