                                  PUBLIC NOTICE

Please be advised that on August 7, 2025, the City Council of the City of St George
approved the following ordinance(s):


Ordinance No. 2025-059 amending Title 10-17A-3 of City Code and modifying the
specific standards for accessory dwelling units.

Ordinance No. 2025-057 amending the City Zoning Map by changing the zone plan
from C-4 (Central Business District Commercial) to PD-MU (Planned Development
Mixed-Use) on approximately 3.24 acres located at the northwest corner of St.
George Boulevard and Temple Street.

Ordinance No. 2025-056 vacating a municipal utility easement located between Lots
9 and 10 of the Banded Ridge Subdivision.

Ordinance No. 2025-058 amending the Hidden Valley Master Plan PD-R (Planned
Development Residential) zone for construction of a new 51-unit townhome
development on 7.38 acres.

Ordinance No. 2025-060 amending portions of Title 10, Chapters 2 and 19 of City
Code as it pertains to parking regulations in PD-MU zone and other minor changes.

Ordinance No. 2025-061 amending Title 5 Chapter 3 Section 7 of City Code to
amend the Utah Code section defining the term "negligent discharge."
