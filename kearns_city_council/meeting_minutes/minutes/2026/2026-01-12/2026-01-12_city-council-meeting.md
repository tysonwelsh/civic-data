     B. Monthly Financial Report (Acknowledgment of Report)

 required the City Council to review the city's financials on a quarterly basis. He stated that best
practice allowed council members to review financial inflows and outflows on a monthly basis,
even if a formal presentation was not given each month. He clarified that the financial

financials at every meeting.

the meeting packets.

preparing monthly presentations. They reiterated that the decision rested with the council

Nathan Bracken explained that at the beginning of each year, city councils typically review and
renew committee and board assignments. He clarified that these assignments referred to
various boards and special districts that provided services to Kearns, noting that Kearns, like
several other Utah cities, relied heavily on special districts for municipal services. He stated
that while many appointments would be addressed at a later meeting, there were two
appointments that required formal action through resolutions, including appointments to
SLVLESA and the UPD Board. Before addressing those, he introduced the need to elect a
Mayor Pro Tem. He explained that under Utah law, the council had the option to elect a Mayor
Pro Tem, who would assume mayoral duties when the mayor was unavailable. He described
typical situations in which a Mayor Pro Tem might be needed, such as presiding over
meetings, covering during absences, or responding to emergencies when the mayor was out
of town. Mr. Bracken stated that although it was ultimately the council's decision, best practice
was to elect a Mayor Pro Tem in advance rather than addressing the issue during an
emergency. He also noted that some councils rotated the position annually due to the
additional workload. He then turned the discussion back to the mayor and council.
