29
30   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained this was a public hearing for a
31   proposed Code Text Amendment to adjust Riverton City’s Commercial Uses in Title 18 – Land
32   Development Code. This proposal does not make substantial changes to current policy or
33   practice. The goal of this proposal is to make it easier for customers, city officials, and city staff
34   to identify land uses in the city’s commercial zones. To achieve this goal staff identified these
35   objectives:
36
37             1- Place all commercial zones in one Table of Uses, and
38             2- Eliminate references to commercial uses in each commercial zone description
39             (18.65, 18.70, 18.75, 18.80, 18.85, and 18.100).
40

42   commissioners recommended that City Council consolidate two table of uses by adding unique
43   uses from the PPC Table of Uses to the existing “Table of Commercial Uses”. City Council
44   approved this change on January 17, 2023. Commercial zones are not consistently described,
45   and some discrepancies have been found between zone descriptions and the Table of
46   Commercial Uses. Staff is proposing to eliminate references to commercial uses in each
47   commercial zone description and thus eliminate any discrepancies in the code by deferring to
48   the updated Table of Commercial Uses. This change would affect commercial zone descriptions
49   18.65, 18.70, 18.80, 18.85, and 18.100 by removing references to approved, conditional, and
50   prohibited uses. Thus, the Table of Commercial Uses would become the controlling legal
