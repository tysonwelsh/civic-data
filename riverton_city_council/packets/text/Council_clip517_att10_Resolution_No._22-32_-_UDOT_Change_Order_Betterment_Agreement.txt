                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 22-32

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A CHANGE ORDER TO CONTRACT 22-1053-01,
UDOT BETTERMENT AGREEMENT, UDOT PROJECT NUMBER S-0154(84)14, PIN
14415, BANGERTER THREE INTERCHANGE PROJECT, TO CHANGE THE SCOPE
                 OF WORK ON THE MICRO-SURFACING

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would like to change the Micro-surfacing scope of
       work in order to have the road surface finished instead of just patched.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes a Change Order to Contract 22-1053-01, UDOT
            Betterment Agreement, UDOT Project Number S-0154(84)14, Pin 14415, Bangerter
            Three Interchange Project, To Change the Scope of Work on the Micro-surfacing.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 19th day of
April 2022 by the following vote:
