BACKGROUND
The site is currently occupied by an existing self-service commercial car wash, and the Jim's
Diner restaurant, which will both be demolished in preparation for the new development. The
property is located in the Auto Mall (AM) (Commercial) zoning district.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was not held for this project
due to the lack of residential zoning in the area.

ANALYSIS

Zoning Review.  Discussions on this project began in August of 2020.  The site plan has been
revised several times after input from City staff. The building and site development elements now
meet the City's requirements as specified in the Sandy City Development Code for the AM
(Commercail) zoning district, with exception as to the number of vehicle parking stalls on site.
The site is larger than is actually required for the size of the building.  Marverik would like to have
more parking stalls than the Sandy Development Code allows.  However, after consultation with
City staff, the number of on-site customer parking stalls has been reduced to 44 on-site parking
stalls.  This has been done by the following:  (1) 5,637 square feet of building area divided by 200
sq. ft. equals 29 required parking stalls. (2) The applicant is seeking from the Planning
Commission, the maximum possible bonus of 25 percent of the required stall count which equals
7 additional stalls.   (3) The applicant is making long term lease arangements with the abutting
hotel to allow shared parking for 8 stalls on this site as overflow parking for the hotel use.

Due to the daily multiple (A.M., P.M. and Noon) peak volumes of customers visiting the Maverik
gas stations and food intensive convenenice stores, this excess parking is justified by the
experience of Maverik and by observation by the general public.  Staff supports these requests for
the parking bonus and the shared parking for overflow use by the hotel next door to the south.

However, in order for the Planning Commission to grant the 25 percent parking stall bonus and
approve the shared parking agreement, certain justification information is required  by the Sandy
City Development Code, to be submitted by the applicant for consideration by the Planning
Commission of these requests.  The proposed site plan has been revised by the applicant in
anticipation that their requests will be granted.  The justification information (either a parking
demand study or market demand analysis) has not yet been provided by the applicant in order to
be considered in this staff report.

Therefore, in an effort to allow this project to move forward, planning staff is requesting that the
Planning Commission consider all other aspects of this project on May 6, 2021, and when the
required information on the parking bonus and shared parking is available from the applicant,
decisions on the parking bonus and the shared parking agreement will (at the Planning Commission
discretion) be either brought back before the Planning Commission for consideration and decision,
or considered by the planning staff. If staff determines that the justifications and details are
sufficient, staff would approve the parking numbers and the shared parking as part of final site
plan review.

Architectural Design Review.  The building's design, materials and colors have been reviewed
by staff and will meet the City's Architectural Design Standards for commercial development.
This is a "Branded" building unique to Maverik, and is the largest prototype building they build,
and will be sited on a very large property.

Vehicle Access.  Access to the site will be basically the same as on the existing site, with one
existing driveway approach located to the north of the site center along Holiday Park Dr. and a
second driveway relocated further to the south end of the site to better align with existing
driveways across Holiday Park Drive. Required reciprocol driveway accesss is also provided with
two connections along the south property boundary with the existing hotel use.to improve multi-
site circulation.  Access and on-site circulation will be adequate for this development.

Pedestrian Access.  Public siewalks are provided along both 10600 South Street and Holiday Park
Drive, abutting this site.  The use of the property is very vehicle intensive.  However, an ADA
accessible walkway from the public sidewalk to the building will need to be designated and
approved duing final site plan review with staff.

City Departmental Reviews.  All of the reviewing City departments and agencies have reviewed
the proposed new site plan development.  All departments and agency redlines and conditions will
be incorporated into the final site plan for approval by City staff.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is
complete for the proposed Maverik Convenience Store and Self-service Gasoline Station, located
at 10640 S. Holiday Park Drive, and also approve the building architectural design, materials and
colors as presented.  Staff further recommends that the Planning Commission either delay a
decision on the requested 25 percent bonus for additional on-site parking stalls and the long term
lease for shared parking with the abutting hotel use until that required information is submitted
and reviewed by City staff, or delegate the review and approval consideration to the Community
Development Director as a part of final site plan review, based upon the following two findings
and the following eight conditions:

FINDINGS.
     A. That the proposed project will meet all the applicable requirements of the Sandy City
        Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as to building
        architectural design, materials and colors.
     B. The new site plan will provide much needed improvement to this existing property
        and provide improved building design, materials, location, site circulation and
        streetscape improvements to this gateway commercial corner.

CONDITIONS.

  1. That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior to
     the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all Development Code
     requirements and those modifications required or allowed by the Planning Commission.

  2. That the development complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes
     applicable to this type of use.

  3. That an acceptable accessible pedestrian walkway be added to the site plan and approved
     by City staff.

  4. That the details of the requested 25 percent bonus for additional on-site parking stalls and
     the long term lease agreement for the shared parking be reviewed and approved by City
     staff as part of the final site plan review, unless the Planning Commission elects to have
     this come back for a separate approval consideration.

  5. All utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall be shown
     on the site plan and shall be placed underground or moved behind the front setback of the
     buildings and screened from view.  Each box shall be shown in its exact location and
     shall be noted with its exact height, width and length. Building utility meters shall be
     mounted to the side or rear elevation or screened.

  6. That the developer be responsible for the placement of a temporary 6 foot high chain
     link fence around the perimeter of the project during the construction phase of the project
     for security. Said fence shall also be required to include fabric to prohibit blowing dust
     problems, if it becomes necessary or if it is required by the Community Development
     Department during Site Plan Review.

  7. That the applicant complies with all department requirements as noted in all
     Preliminary Review letters prior to submittal for final site plan review with staff.

  8. That all signage be reviewed and approved under separate permit and be in conformance
     with City Code.
