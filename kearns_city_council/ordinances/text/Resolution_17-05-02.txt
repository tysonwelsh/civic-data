RECITALS

A. The Kearns Metro Township (the "Metro Township") is a Metro
Township pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§10-2a-401 ef seq.

B. The Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission (the “Planning
Commission,” or "Commission") is a quasi-judicial body appointed by the Kearns
Metro Township Council to hear, recommend, and/or decide on land use issues for
Kearns pursuant to UCA J 0-9a-3 —-PLANNING COMMISSION, e/ al.

C. The State of Utah, under the statutes of the Open Meetings Act,
allows local governments to establish electronic meeting policies pursuant to
UCA § 52-4-407.

D. The Metro Township Council recognizes the benefits of establishing an
electronic meetings policy to assist in the facilitation of business in the Metro
Township.

E. The Metro Township Council also recognizes the benefits of establishing
an electronic meetings policy to assist in the facilitation of business of the Planning
Commission.

F. | The Metro Township believes the electronic meetings policy is in the best
interests of the Council and the Metro Township's residents.

RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council:
The Kearns Metro Township Council Adopts Attachment "A" — Electronic Meetings Policy
for use in the conduct of public meetings to allow the Metro Township Council and Planning
Commission to conduct electronic meetings.

Attachment A - Electronic Meeting Policy

KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
POLICY ON
ELECTRONIC MEETINGS
Purpose —
The purpose of this policy is to establish the means and procedures by which the Kearns Metro Township
Council (“Council”) and the Kearns Metro Township Planning Commission (“Commission”) may
conduct electronic meetings in accordance with the provisions of the Public Meetings Act ("Act"), and
particularly § 52-4-207 (UCA, as amended).
1.0 Application of the Act ~ definitions.
Ll The Council, and for benefit and possible use of the Commission, hereby adopts those
definitions of specific terms which appear in the Act at § 52-4-103 for application in this policy.
2.0 Electronic Meetings
2.1 The Council or Commission hereby determines that it may, from time to time as needed,
convene and conduct Council or Commission meetings in which one or more members attend and
participate in the meeting through electronic means.
2.2 Council or Commission electronic meetings may include meetings conducted by means
of telephone, telecommunications, electronic mail, or by other computerized, electronic, or
teleconferencing means and media.
2.3 Members of the Council or Commission who participate in a meeting through electronic
means shall be counted as present for all purposes, including the determination that a quorum is
present.
3.0 Notice
3.1 Prior to conducting an electronic meeting, the Council or Commission shall, through its
staff, provide advance written and electronic notice of the meeting, including agenda items, 24
hours in advance.
3.2 Notice shall be provided to all Council or Commission members, as wel] as to members
of the public and the news media in accordance with the provisions of the Act.
3.3 Each notice shal] describe the means of communication and the procedures by which
members of the public will be able to monitor and, when appropriate, participate in the electronic
meetings.
3.3.1 The notice shall designate which anchor location will be available for public
monitoring and participation. Council or Commission electronic meeting anchor locations
may include the following: the Kearns Library, Conference Room, 5350 South 4220

West, the Salt Lake County Council Conference room (N2-800), the Salt Lake County
Council Chambers (N1-110), , or the Utah Olympic Oval, World Record Lounge, 5662
South Cougar Lane. (The anchor locations at Salt Lake County are located at the Salt
Lake County Government Center, 2001 South State Street, Salt Lake City, Utah.)
3.3.2. The Council or Commission may establish other anchor locations for electronic
meetings by majority vote.
4.0 Public Attendance
4.] Council or Commission staff shall provide sufficient and necessary space, equipment and
other means as required by the Act, to allow members of the public and the news media to attend,
monitor and, where appropriate, participate in the public portion of any electronic meeting
conducted by the Council or Commission.
