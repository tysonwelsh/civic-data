            Ordinance No. 2023-42

                            AN ORDINANCE AMENDING PARK CITY CODE SECTION 13-1-25 AND
                             ESTABLISHING THE CITY'S DESIGNATED WATER SERVICE AREA

                    WHEREAS, in 2020, the Utah State Legislature passed a joint resolution proposal for an
            Amendment to the Utah Constitution allowing municipalities to designate water service areas
            beyond municipal boundaries, and the Amendment was subsequently approved by Utah's
            voters;

                  WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature passed House Bill 31 in 2019 to amend Utah
            Code sections 10-7-14 and 10-8-22, which became effective on January 1, 2021;

                    WHEREAS, Utah Code section 10-7-14 now requires municipalities to establish by
            ordinance a Designated Water Service Area by publishing a map that includes areas where
            water is provided to customers at locations outside of the municipality's boundaries;

                    WHEREAS, Park City Public Utilities has determined that its current water service area
            should be used to identify its Designated Water Service Area and has prepared a map that
            reflects the boundaries of the area;

                    WHEREAS, Utah Code section 10-8-22 now prohibits charging different retail water
            rates to customers “based solely on the fact that a particular classification of retail customers is
            located either inside or outside of the municipality's corporate boundary”;

                     WHEREAS, Park City Code section 13-1-25 requires individuals and entities outside of
            the corporate limits of Park City to pay double the applicable rate for water provided inside City
            limits, and this provision conflicts with state law;

            NOW, THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Park City as follows:

            SECTION 1. PURPOSE. The purpose of this ordinance is to amend Title 13, Chapter 1, Section
            25 of the Park City Code to conform with Utah Code section 10-8-22 and to establish the Park
            City Designated Water Service Area in accordance with Utah Code section 10-7-14.

            SECTION 2. AMEND PARK CITY CODE SECTION 13-1-25. Title 13, Chapter 1, Section 25 of
            the Park City Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

                     13-1-25 Sale of Water Outside of Park City

                     It is the policy of the City to provide culinary water within the corporate limits of Park City.
                     Those individuals or entities desiring connection to the Park City water system must
                     petition the Park City Council for Annexation as a condition of water service. If
                     annexation is denied or otherwise unsuccessful, City Council may choose to waive the
                     annexation requirement and provide the individuals or entities with culinary water outside
                     the corporate limits of Park City. Those individuals and entities outside the corporate
                     limits of Park City currently connected to the water system and receiving water shall
                     agree to abide by the terms and conditions of this Title[ and shall pay double the
