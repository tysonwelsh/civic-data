                                     PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant, Holmes Homes (Trish Smith), is requesting Preliminary Approval for a proposed Planned
Unit Development (PUD) which includes 74 townhome dwelling units. The proposed density for the
project is approximately 11.20 units per acre. The townhome buildings would be in groups of 3-family,
4-family, 5-family and 6-family dwellings. The proposed townhome units have three bedrooms. All the
dwelling units will have an attached 2-car garage, there is also a 20’ long driveway for each unit, which
would allow for guest parking.

The proposed number of guest parking spaces for the community is 18, according to the PUD ordinance,
the number of spaces should be 20. Most of these spaces are in the southwest area of the project. The
proposed recreational amenities are located in the southwest area of the project and include a
playground, picnic area, and a pickleball court.

The proposed PUD would use the Truong property as an access to 8400 West, while using Arbor Park
Commercial area as an access to 3500 South. The proposed access to the site would be via a private
street system which will connect to the private street proposed for this project.
                        SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located within the commercial area, on the corner of 8400 West and 3500 South, and
known as Arbor Park. This is a large commercial site with retail stores (like Walmart and smaller strip
mall shops), banks, and restaurants.

Currently, there is an office building (Alorica) on the subject property. This building will be removed and
replaced with the proposed PUD project.

To the east, there is an apartment complex and to the southeast, there are single-family homes in the
subdivision along Arbor Park Drive.

To the southwest of the property is the Truong property (previously under the name B. Logue). This
property has preliminary approval for a 192 multi-family unit project.



                                         LAND USE CONSIDERATIONS
The purpose of a planned unit development (PUD) is:

    1. To provide a high-quality living environment, and to utilize and incorporate natural features in the
       land development design.
    2. To provide a more efficient use of the land and the preservation of greater proportions of open
       space for recreation and visual use than is otherwise provided for in the zoning regulations.
    3. To provide good and compatible neighborhood and housing design by utilizing a variety of
       dwelling types and site arrangement plans to allow for greater flexibility and diversity in the
       physical pattern of the development.
    4. To provide developments compatible with existing residential uses while maintaining a
       harmonious environment within the community.
    5. To create mixed use areas designed to be beneficial to the neighborhood.
    6. To ensure substantial compliance with the intent of this chapter related to the public health,
       safety and general welfare, while securing the efficient use of the land for residential, or a
       combination of commercial and residential development.


                              GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The General Plan for Magna Metro Township was adopted September 2012. The property is located on
a mapped General Plan corridor which is labeled as an area of “moderate change”. Therefore, the
development of this land is encouraged. It should be noted that the General Plan for the Magna Metro
Township is currently being updated by the MSD Planning Staff and GSBS Consultants.

                           PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
The MSD Planning Staff recommends that the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission grant
Preliminary Approval for a Conditional Use/PUD (Planned Unit Development) to allow for 74
townhomes on the subject property, and amending Arbor Park Commercial Subdivision Lot 1, with the
following conditions:
     1. That the applicant be required to and comply with the recommendations from all reviewing
        agencies.
     2. That the applicant be required to install a 6’ high solid visual barrier wood, masonry or other
        material designated by the Planning Commission, along the property line around the residential
        apartment buildings.
     3. All individual private yards be fenced entirely for privacy.
     4. That the applicant complies with the Development Standards for Recreation Facilities and Open
        Space for the PUD project.
     5. That the applicant be required to submit a Final Landscape Plan to staff for approval.
     6. That the applicant be required to comply with the parking requirements for guest parking.
     7. That the applicant be required to meet all County Engineering Standards for the private roadway
        within the project.
     8. Application to come back to the Planning Commission for final approval.
