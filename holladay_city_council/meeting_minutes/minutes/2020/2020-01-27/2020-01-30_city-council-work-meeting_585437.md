1. Welcome and Call to Order – Mayor Dahle
Mayor Dahle called the meeting to order at 6:05 pm. Introductions were made and the Mayor
thanked the Planning Commission for all their time and hard work.
2. Land Use Training – Meg Ryan, ULCT
Meg Ryan went over a power point presentation that review State land use and how it applies to
the city as well as how the Planning Commission and Council work together. She discussed
LUDMA and the eight themes with the last one being that the land use authority shall apply plain
language of land use regulations. She also noted that the Planning Commission is advisory.
There are two land use tools – the general plan which is a vision for the future and the
zoning/land use code is the detailed specifics.

The types of decisions and applicable authorities were reviewed as well as how much authority
the Council and Planning Commission have. Finally Ms. Ryan discussed what the future holds
and issues cities will be dealing with such as growth, density, quality of life, and accessory
dwelling units.
3. Questions & Answers
It was noted that Matt Durham was selected to serve on the Utah League of Cities and Towns
Board of Directors.
Mayor Dahle thanked Ms. Ryan for her time and for all the League does for the cities.
