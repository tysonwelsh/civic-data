1.         25-277                 Building our Future: A Community Housing Workshop Series, Workshop
                                  Three - Identifying and Prioritizing Key Issues
                   Attachments:   September 2, 2025 Memo_Housing Workshop Series
                                  Issues Reference Sheet
                                  Housing Questionnaire Memo and Graphs
                                  Housing Questionnaire Data

                                  Lynn Pace, Workshop Facilitator, reviewed the the workshop series. The first two
                                  workshops were informational and educational. Tonight's workshop was participatory
                                  allowing for Council Members discussion. The purpose of Workshop 3 was to prioritize
                                  which issues the City intends to focus on addressing first. Mr. Pace turned the time over
                                  to Dustin Fratto, Council Director who reviewed the survey results from the questionnaire
                                  sent out to the Council.

                                  Mr. Fratto reviewed the data compiled by staff and provided an overview of the graphs and
                                  survey results. The first graph details the survey results by issue. The second graph
                                  provided a weighted ranking of results by issue. Graph 3 combines the first two graphs
                                  and provides a side-by-side comparison. The fourth through sixth graphs provides an
                                  overview of issues that received 3 or more council responses and those issues ranked
                                  with a higher priority.

                                  Lynn Pace led the Council discussion of the housing priorities and issues. The goal at
                                  tonight's workshop was to narrow down the list of priorities and narrow the list to three or
                                  four big-picture items for the Council to focus on which will help direct staff on council
                                  priorities. In reviewing the survey results, available housing options and obstacles to
                                  middle housing, appearance and neighborhood integration, and housing for seniors
                                  ranked as top concerns for the Council. Council participated in a robust discussion
                                  regarding housing needs in Sandy City. Council discussed a wide range of housing
                                  related topics including tools for enforcing owner occupancy, greater variety of housing
                                  options, higher volume of housing opportunities, home ownership and the impact of rental
                                  properties. A lengthy Council discussion occurred. Council discussed the goal of creating
                                  a broad statement. Staff provided additional insight and information. Key priorities for the
                                  Mayor included home ownership, new housing options, smaller lots, higher density,
                                  senior housing, redevelopment of older homes and housing that is adjacent to transit.
                                  Council also discussed densification trends in housing and private property rights.
                                  Council worked towards the goal of creating a direction to move forward with areas of
                                  exploration: ensuring sustainability and character of the city, housing affordability and
                                  options, owner occupied housing, and more variety of housing options. The next housing
                                  workshop is planned for October.

                                  Following the workshop, Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Marci Houseman recently played golf at River Oaks Golf Club. The River
                    Oaks Golf Club is a gem for our city and she thanked the staff at the golf course. She
                    attended a concert at the Sandy Amphitheater and had an opportunity to review the city
                    venue and staffing. She thanked Community Development staff for their responsiveness to
                    a resident's concern.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey also attended a concert at the Sandy Amphitheater. It
                    provides the Council with a great opportunity to see first-hand the inner workings of the
                    venue, by both observing the guests as well as the volunteers and staff. The Arts Guild
                    production of Shrek wrapped up another successful show. She volunteered at the
                    concessions.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson shared a resident's comments with the Council regarding
                    the changes to the STR code. The issues this resident had with a short term rental in his
                    area have been resolved as a result of the amendments to the code. He acknowledged
                    the work done by the Council and staff and the impact they have had for residents. What
                    we have done was working for our residents.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud attended the Police-Fire softball game. It was a great
                    event.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Monica Zoltanski attended the Open House for the new Trans Jordan Transfer
                    Station and she spoke about the facility's impact on residents. She spent time recently
                    with the students at Altara Elementary School. She congratulated a resident for being
                    named a super senior. She also attended a great concert on the Plaza at RSL Stadium.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, invited the Council to attend a first responders ceremony and service
                    event.

                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:36 pm.
