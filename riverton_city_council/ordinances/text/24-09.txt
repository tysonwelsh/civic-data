                                     RIVERTON     CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 24- 09


AN ORDINANCE OF RIVERTON CITY ADOPTING A COMPENSATION                             SCHEDULE
                  FOR EXECUTIVE         OFFICERS       AND CITY ATTORNEY


       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10- 3- 818, a Public Hearing was held on June
4, 2024 for the purpose of receiving public comment to consider the compensation schedule for
executive officers and city attorney for Fiscal Year 2024- 2025; and,

        WHEREAS, a notice of the Public Hearing regarding the compensation schedule for
executive officers and city attorney was published and posted as required by law; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to adopt the compensation schedule for executive officers and city attorney.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
that the compensation schedule for executive officers and city attorney for Fiscal Year 2024-
2025, as attached hereto, is hereby adopted.

       This ordinance, assigned No. 24- 09 shall take effect immediately upon passage and
acceptance as provided herein.

ITEM:
                           Ordinance No. 24- 09 - Adopting the Compensation for Executive Officers for
                           Fiscal Year 2024- 2025 - Kevin Hicks, City Manager

SUMMARY:



Under State Code 10- 3- 818, before a governing body may adopt a final budget or a final amended
budget that includes a compensation increase for an executive municipal officer, the body must hold a
public hearing. For the purposes of this code section, " executive municipal officer" means all department
heads and their chief assistants   or deputies.   As a fulfillment   of the requirements   of this code, Riverton
City will be treating all executive municipal officers will receive the same percentage increase as all other
employees, namely a 4% COLA and 3% merit.
