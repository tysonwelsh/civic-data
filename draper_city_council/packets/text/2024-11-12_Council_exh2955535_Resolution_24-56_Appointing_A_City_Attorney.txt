                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-56

               A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL
                     APPOINTING A CITY ATTORNEY

       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has created the position of City Attorney to
administer the day-to-day legal affairs of the City; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Draper City Municipal Code the City Attorney shall be
appointed by the Mayor upon recommendation by the City Manager with the advice and
consent of the City Council; and

        WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Traci Gundersen as the City Attorney
to exercise the powers, duties, responsibilities, and authority assigned to the City
Attorney under statute, ordinance, and policy; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has determined it is in the best interest of the
health, safety and welfare of the citizens of Draper to provide its advice and consent to
the appointment of Traci Gundersen as Draper City Attorney based on her abilities and
prior experience relating to the duties of the office.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its advice and consent
to the Mayor’s appointment of Traci Gundersen as Draper City Attorney, and Traci
Gundersen is hereby appointed Draper City Attorney with all the powers, duties,
responsibilities, and authority specified by statute, ordinance, and policy.

       Section 2. Employment Agreement. A written employment contract has been
prepared and negotiated setting forth Traci Gundersen’s terms of employment. That
contract is hereby approved.

        Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution
shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.
     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 12th DAY OF NOVEMBER, 2024.
