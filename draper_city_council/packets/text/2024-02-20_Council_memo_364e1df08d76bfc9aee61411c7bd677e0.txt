MEMO
To: City Council
From: Todd Taylor
Date: 2024-02-20
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinance #1602


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately 0.803
acres of a 1.68 acre property located south of Bangerter Highway, at approximately 13673 S. 100 E.
The property is currently zoned CO2 and CR. The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map
Amendment be approved to place the entire property in the CR zone.

The Planning Commission reviewed this item at their February 8, 2024 meeting and forwarded a
positive recommendation on a 5-0 vote.
