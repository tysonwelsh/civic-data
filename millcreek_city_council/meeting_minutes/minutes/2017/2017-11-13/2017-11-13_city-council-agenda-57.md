The City raised donations for purchasing a conservation easement, 25.5 acres, above
the water tanks on Crestwood Dr. for the continuation of the Bonneville Shoreline
Trail. The land was purchased at auction by Salt Lake County from its open space
fund. The City raised about $16,000 in private donations. The Mayor asked the
Council to give direction to planning staff to put together a report to propose to the
Planning Commission that the City downzone the zoning of the purchased parcel
from its current Forest and Recreation Zone FR-5 to either FR-10 or FR-20. The
Council expressed favor of this recommendation, so it was directed to city staff.
