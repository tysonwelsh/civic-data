GENERAL SESSION

Chair Snow called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS


Jeff Beck requests Final Plat approval for the West Main Subdivision located at 7598
South Main Street in the Main Street Form-Based Code (MS-FBC) zone.

Mr. Knobloch stated that the applicant requests approval of the West Main Subdivision which
merges five existing parcels surrounding 7598 South Main Street, between Main, Depot and
Stagg Streets, into “Lot 1” and provides right-of-way dedication in anticipation of future
development.


APPLICANT:

Mr. Beck did not have any additional comments to provide.

PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Snow opened the public hearing.

There were no comments.


MOTION:       Commissioner Erickson MOVED to close the public hearing. The motion
              was SECONDED by Commissioner Benson. Chair Snow called for a roll call
              vote. The vote was as follows:

                     Commissioner Erickson          Yes
                     Commissioner Liedtke           Yes
                     Commissioner Benson            Yes

STAFF UPDATE

1.     Upcoming Meetings and Projects.

Mr. Olsen stated that the next Planning Commission meeting will be held on March 9th. At the
moment, there is a code amendment on the agenda with the possibility of a few other items. Mr.
Olsen added that staff is currently working with MHTN, a consulting group, to conduct a State
Street Corridor Study and it will have an outreach component that the Planning Commission will
be involved with. He stated that staff is still planning on rescheduling the Open House and will
keep the Commission updated on a date and time.

ADJOURN

A motion was made to adjourn at 6:10 p.m. No one opposed. The meeting adjourned 6:10 p.m.
