F. ACTION ITEMS:
    1. FINAL READING OF ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN PROVISIONS OF THE BILATERAL CONTRACT
       ORDINANCE – ORDINANCE 2015-17
         David Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, noted that the first reading occurred last week and minor
         word changes had been made. This allows certain division directors—for Eccles Conference Center,
         Peery’s Egyptian Theater, Golden Spike Event Center, Ice Sheet, Parks & Recreation, and Weber
         County Fair—authority to approve standard contracts that have been pre-approved by the County
         Commission without such contracts having to be approved at a County Commission meeting.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Ordinance 2015-17 amending certain provisions of the Bilateral
         Contract Ordinance; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye

    2. RESOLUTION APPOINTING A COUNTY REPRESENTATIVE AND ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIVE FOR THE
       UTAH COUNTIES INDEMNITY POOL (UCIP) ANNUAL MEMBERSHIP MEETING – RESOLUTION 39-2015
         David Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, noted that UCIP’s by-laws require the county to appoint a
         representative and an alternate representative to this meeting.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 39-2015 appointing Kerry W. Gibson as the Weber
         County representative and Brad Dee as the alternate representative for the Utah Counties Indemnity
         Pool annual membership meeting; Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye

    3. CONTRACT WITH WEBER STATE UNIVERSITY–D2 HOCKEY FOR THE 2015-2016 ICE RENTAL
       AGREEMENT – CONTRACT C2015-361
         Todd Ferrario, County Ice Sheet Manager, presented the yearly agreement stating that there were no
         changes from the previous year.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-361 with Weber State University–D2
       Hockey for the 2015-2016 Ice Rental Agreement; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye

    4. CONTRACT WITH IMPACT DEMOLITION FOR THE DEMOLITION OF PROPERTY AT 701 W. 12TH
       STREET – CONTRACT C2015-362
       Commissioner Bell stated that currently a left hand turn is not allowed into the County Sheriff’s
       Complex off of 12th Street. The county purchased property and the building on it was subsequently
       demolished so that a new road to the Complex can be constructed at the traffic light at BDO.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-362 with Impact Demolition for the
       demolition of property at 701 W. 12th Street; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye

    5. CONTRACT WITH FRIENDS OF THE FAIRGROUNDS FOR THE BRIGGS MADSEN BENEFIT BEING HELD
       ON OCTOBER 8, 2015 – CONTRACT C2015-363

       James Harvey, with Golden Spike Event Center, stated that Briggs was a promising 16-year-old
       freshman athlete who competed on national and international levels of rough stock rider. He was
       injured recently and fractured his back. Friends of the Fairgrounds wish to hold a benefit on
       10/8/2015 to help Briggs and his family. Commissioner Ebert expressed appreciation to Mr. Harvey
       as someone who reaches out to the community, is determined to resolve issues and is a problem
       solver.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-363 with Friends of the Fairgrounds for the
       Briggs Madsen benefit on 10/8/2015; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye

    6. CONTRACT WITH EA ENGINEERING SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY (EA) FOR ACCESS TO THE C/D
       LANDFILL ROAD FOR HAFB – CONTRACT C2015-364
       Gary Laird, with County Solid Waste, stated that there is a Hill Air Force Base (HAFB) facility west
       of the county’s C&D landfill on 900 S. and EA, HAFB’s contractor, must access the county’s
       property to perform some contract work for HAFB—which is to carry low level radioactive material,
       that to Mr. Laird’s understanding is legal on public roads. However, just as a precaution, EA will
       conduct baseline and post-remedial gamma activity scans at the entrance to the county landfill
       property and where they leave the site, and then a similar test on the site to ensure nothing was left at
       the landfill.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-364 with EA Engineering Science &
       Technology for access to the C/D Landfill road for HAFB; Commissioner Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye
