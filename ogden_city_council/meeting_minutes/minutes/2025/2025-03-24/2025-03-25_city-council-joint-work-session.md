        The purpose of the joint work session was to discuss New Markets Tax Credits opportunity and process; discuss
Council/Trustee business; and hear Council/Trustee comments.

New Markets Tax Credits opportunity and process
           Council Executive Director Eller-Smith said the Administration is proposing that the City take advantage of the federal
New Markets Tax Credit (NMTC) Program to receive approximately $2.1 Million in investor funding. This funding will be used
to complete elements of the Marshall White Community Center (MWCC) project that were removed from the scope of work due
to budgetary constraints. Use of the NMTCs for the MWCC project requires a series of complex business transactions among
multiple parties. Actions by the City Council include authorizing a lease of the MWCC to a Special Purpose Entity created to
facilitate the NMTC transactions. The Council, acting as the Municipal Building Authority Board, would also need to authorize a
FY 2024-2025 Budget Amendment to fund a leverage loan in the amount of $6.88 Million. The loan will be repaid to the MBA at
the end of the seven-year NMTC compliance period. Ms. Eller-Smith introduced Cody Rogers, the attorney from Stinson LLP,
who has been working on this issue for the City.
           Mr. Rogers used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to expound on NMTC financing opportunities for the MWCC
project. He presented a diagram to provide a very high-level overview of the structure of the NMTC Program, which highlighted
the role of private investors in the Program. Private investors are incentivized to make investments in struggling communities in
exchange for tax credits that reduce their tax liability. This improves their return on investment (ROI). He then discussed the
amount of funding that historically been allocated annually for the NMTC Program, the process an entity must follow to secure
NMTC funding, NMTC compliance requirements, and the anticipated net benefit to Ogden City as a result of participation in the
Program. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
           Discussion among the group and Mr. Rogers centered on the role of a financial institution in the NMTC Program; the
definition of the term ‘leverage loan’; the risk the City would assume, if any, by participating in the Program; any instability in the
Program based upon the current status of funding programs managed by the Federal government; interest rates for NMTC loans
and any prepayment penalties associated with the loans; creation and role of the Special Purpose Entity (SPE) Board; separation of
the loan proceeds/repayment sources from the City’s general funds; the role and responsibilities of the City Council in approval of
SPE actions; and loan terms and conditions. Mr. Rogers left the meeting at 4:48 p.m.
           The Council and staff then discussed the terms of the agreement between the City and Stinson LLP., details regarding
how the money generated through the NMTC program will be used at the MWCC, and access to the signing documents for the
NMTC loan transaction.
Council/Trustee Business
         Naming of the Marshall White Community Center (MWCC) Field House: The Council engaged in discussion with
representatives of the Public Services Department regarding special events surrounding the opening and naming of the Field
House at the MWCC. The Council also expressed concerns regarding the lack of their involvement in the process to arrive at the
proposal to name the Field House. They concluded they would like to hear input from the Marshall White Center Advisory
Committee as well as the public before taking action to approve the proposed name of the Field House.
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith stated an action item to approve the proposal to name the Field House after fallen
Police Officer Lyday will be included on the next City Council meeting agenda.


         The meeting adjourned at 5:26 p.m.
