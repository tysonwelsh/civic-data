Appointment of New Council Member

              Council Members asked the following questions of Doug Green and David Olsen,
two candidates applying to fill the vacant Council seat.

Mayor Clayton:

   •   Tell us about yourself.
   •   What qualities make an effective Council Member?
   •   What qualities do you have that would help you be an effective Council Member?
   •   Why would you like to participate in Copperton’s local government by being a member of
       its Council?

Council Member Stitzer:

DATE           WEDNESDAY                               NOVEMBER                           18, 2020

   •   The Copperton Metro Council must make decisions regarding the implementation of
       policies and/or programs that may be somewhat difficult. How will you go about making
       decisions that are best for not only our residents, but the future of Copperton, our services
       and our budget?

Council Member Severson:

   •   How would you feel serving on a board with people who may have different views from
       your own or different views of those of your constituents you will be representing?
   •   Give an example of a situation where you have had to work with others who have different
       views?
   •   Describe the situation, what the differing view was, and what you did to effectively work
       together?

Council Member Bailey:

   •   Imagine you have been appointed to represent Copperton as a Trustee on a service
       providers Board of Trustees. Your fellow Trustees (who are Mayor, Council Members,
       etc.) want to change the voting format of the entity from one vote per Trustee to a weighted
       vote by population. This decision will make Copperton’s vote less than 1%.

              1. How would you approach Trustees you disagree with?
              2. How do you defend Copperton’s vote in this scenario?

                             Copperton Metro Township Council

                                  RESOLUTION #2020-11-02

                                       November 18, 2020

A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPOINTING DAVID
OLSEN TO THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

       WHEREAS, Councilmember Pazell resigned from the Copperton Metro Township
Council on October 20, 2020 (the “Resignation”); and

        WHEREAS, a midterm vacancy on the Council for Council Seat D, the term for which
will expire on December 31, 2021 (“Vacancy”), currently exists because of the Resignation;
and

      WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township Code Section 2.04.050 requires that a
vacancy on the Council must be filled as provided in state law; and


                                                4
DATE           WEDNESDAY                              NOVEMBER                          18, 2020

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 20A-1-510(1)(a) requires the Council to appoint a
registered voter in Copperton Metro Township who meets the qualifications for office
described in Section 10-3-301 to fill the unexpired term of the vacated office when a vacancy
occurs; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-301, qualified applicants must: (1)
be a United States Citizen: (2) be a registered voter of Copperton; and (3) maintain their
principal place of residence within Copperton, meaning that they have resided in Copperton
for 12 consecutive months before the date of the appointment; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 20A-1-510(1)(b) requires the Council to give public
notice of the vacancy at least two weeks before the Council meets to fill the vacancy,
identifying the date, time, and place of the meeting where the vacancy will be filled; the
person to whom an individual interested in being appointed to fill the vacancy may submit
the interested individual’s name for consideration; and the deadline for submitting an
interested individual’s name (the “Vacancy Notice”); and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 20A-1-510(1)(b) further requires the Council to
interview each individual whose name is submitted for consideration, and who meets the
qualifications for office, regarding the individual’s qualifications (the “Interviews”); and

      WHEREAS, the Council gave the Vacancy Notice on October 27, 2020 in
accordance with the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act and applicable Utah law; and

       WHEREAS, the Council conducted the Interviews on November 18, 2020; and

        WHEREAS, the Council now desires to fill the Vacancy by appointing David Olsen
to the Council for Council Seat D.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED effectively immediately that David Olsen is
appointed to the Copperton Metro Township Council for Council Seat D with a term ending
on December 1, 2021.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Salt Lake County, Utah, this 18th day of November 2020.
