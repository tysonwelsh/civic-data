5:15 Council Meeting
                       Council Chair Cyndi Sharkey welcomed those in attendance.

                       Chair Sharkey read a statement regarding the continuation of virtual City Council
                       meetings without an anchor location.

Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance

                       Council Member Houseman offered the Prayer.

                       Matt Huish, CAO, led the Pledge.

Non-voting Items

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                       Mike Applegarth, Executive Director, briefed the Council on upcoming agenda items.

Council Member Business

                       Council Member Nicholl updated the Council on a recent meeting with the Wasatch Front
                       Waste and Recycling District..

                       Council Member Christensen inquired about the scheduling of the bulk waste program
                       presentation to the Council.

                       Council Member Zoltanski thanked the Parks staff for their work on the Bell Canyon
                       Preservation Trail. She acknowledged the sad milestone of reaching 500,000 deaths due
                       to COVID-19. She also asked for a discussion on the billing detail provided by the Council
                       attorney. Comments and discussion followed.

                       Council Member Houseman thanked residents for their service and help to their neighbors
                       and community during the recent snow storm. She spoke about a recent KSL segment
                       that featured the Sandy City Fire Department. She congratulated the staff and students at
                       Alta Canyon High School on their recent theater performance and she thanked the Sandy
                       Rotary Club for their service to Sandy City.

Mayor's Report

                       Mayor Kurt Bradburn gave an update on the Covid-19 vaccination process. The County is
                       looking for volunteers to help at vaccination centers. This Thursday is the Sandy City
                       Business Awards program.

CAO Report

                       Mr. Huish acknowledged the Police Department staff for their recent efforts. He thanked
                       Lynn Pace, City Attorney, for drafting an Election FAQ which will be distributed to
                       employees and council members. Kim Bell spoke about a Salt Lake County media
                       campaign "This is our shot. Let's take it." Mike Gladbach briefed the Council on the city's
                       response during the recent snow storm.

Legislative Report

                                  Evelyn Everton, Deputy Mayor, reviewed legislative bills currently under review and spoke
                                  about the potential impact on the City.

                                  Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Council moved to Time Certain Items and Public Hearings on the Agenda and heard
                                  General citizens comments.

Information Items

Information Items



1.         21-076                 Administrative Services Department recommending the Council receive an
                                  update on sales tax revenue
                   Attachments:   FY 2021 YTD Sales Tax

                                  Brett Neumann, Budget Services Director, presented on the sales tax revenues collected
                                  this fiscal year from July 2020 through January 2021. The sales tax revenues collected to
                                  date have exceeded the finance department projections.

                                  Council thanked the finance staff for their conservative approach and their excellent fiscal
                                  management on behalf of the City.

2.         21-078                 Discussion on Council budget priorities for fiscal year 2022

                   Attachments:   FY22 Zach Priorites

                                  Council Member Sharkey asked the Council to share their top two budget priorities. The
                                  Council member discussion included the following budget priorities: funding the
                                  compensation plan, Alta Canyon Recreation Center, the Parks and Recreation building,
                                  long-term capitol projects and sidewalk concrete repairs. A lengthy discussion among the
                                  members followed.

Approval of the Consent Calendar

                                  approve the Consent Calendar. The motion carried by a unanimous voice vote:
