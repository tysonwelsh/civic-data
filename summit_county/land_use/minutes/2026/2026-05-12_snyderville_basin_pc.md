Work Session
(4:30 P.M.)
1)
Discussion, Canyons Development Agreement Amendment to modify Section 3.15 regarding Automobile Access to Red Pine Village Development Area, located in the Red Pine Village, Tombstone and Red Pine Lake Development Areas of the Canyons Resort. Legislative Review.
Applicant: Dave Smith, authorized representative. Project #23-064. Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior Planner. (4:30 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Red Pine Dev Area access DA Amendment  SBPC WS 5.12.2026 #23-064
Spencer White,
TCFC Authorized Representative
, and Dave Smith,
Applicant
, addressed the Commission and provided a brief history of TCFC as the master developer of the Canyons Resort Specially Planned Area.  Mr. Smith then presented the amendment to the Canyons Development Agreement to modify Automobile Access to the Red Pine Village Development Area. The amendment proposed gated access for Red Pine members and guests. (4:33 P.M.)
Mike Owens,
Park City Fire District Fire Marshal
, briefly addressed the Commission and made himself available for questions. (4:55 P.M.)
Commissioners asked clarifying questions of the applicants. Questions raised by the commissioners included environmental and wildlife impacts, alternate means of transportation, unit numbers and daily trip calculations, parking, and event venues. The applicants and Fire Marshal Owens addressed their concerns. (4:57 P.M.)

Discussion and Recommendation for Vintage on the Strand Phase 3 Final Site Plan and Condominium Plat on parcels VOS-1-2A and VOS-2-2B (Friedman2/F2) located at 2673 West High Mountain Road at Canyons Resort. Administrative Review.  Applicant: Spencer White, authorized representative. Owner: Sugarbowl Developers, LLC. Project #25-204.
Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior Planner. (6:03 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
VINTAGE Phase  3 Final Site Plan and Condo SBPC 5.12.2026 - #25-204
Tiffany N. Robinson,
Senior Planner
, presented the Vintage on the Strand Phase 3 Final Site Plan and Condominium Plat proposal in the Canyons Resort. She provided historical context for the proposal. Staff recommended sending a positive recommendation to the county manager. (6:03 P.M.)
Commissioners asked clarifying questions to which Planner Robinson responded. (6:11 P.M.)
Tyann Mooney made a motion to make a positive recommendation to the county manager for the Vintage on the Strand Phase 3 Ten Units in accordance with the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report.. Spencer Young seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0).
