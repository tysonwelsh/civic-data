                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       RESOLUTION NO. 25-20

 A RESOLUTION SETTING THE STAFFING STANDARDS STATIONED AT FIRE STATION
         121 FOR FIREFIGHTERS RESPONDING TO CALLS FOR SERVICE

         WHEREAS, The National Fire Protection Association is a nonprofit corporation which
recommends standards by which public safety services, such as fire protection and ambulance service, are
staffed and equipped in a given community; and

        WHEREAS, NFPA standards generally call for fire apparatus to be staffed with four firefighters
when responding to calls for fire service, however it is possible to staff an apparatus with a three-person
team; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, faced with balancing levels of firefighting services
against rising costs, has considered the question of whether to adjust the staffing for firefighters
dispatched with an apparatus from four persons to three; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has received public input from the Unified Fire Authority
regarding the implications of adjusting staffing levels at its fire stations and carefully considered the
information provided by the Authority; and


        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

        1. The Riverton City Council hereby approves a staffing level for Fire Station 124 to equal _____
           person teams when dispatched with an apparatus.

        2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 18th day of
