                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 20-67

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING A PO CONTRACT TO PERFORMANCE AUDIO TO
   FURNISH AND INSTALL AUDIOVISUAL EQUIPMENT IN THE POLICE AND
 PUBLIC WORKS CONFERENCE ROOMS TO ALLOW VIDEO CONFERENCING

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the IT Manager, the Public Works Director and the Police Chief would like
to make the conference rooms Zoom ready rooms.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

      1. The City Council approves a PO Contract to Performance Audio to furnish and install
         audiovisual equipment in the Police and Public Works conference rooms to allow
         video conferencing

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
November 2020 by the following vote:
