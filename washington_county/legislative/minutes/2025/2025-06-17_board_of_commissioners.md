Hales, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

REPORT AND CONSIDERATION OF THE 2025 WASHINGTON COUNTY TAX SALE

Clerk-Auditor Ryan Sullivan reported the tax sale started out with 33 properties, 13 went on the
sale but were put on payment plans by sale date. Only one property was put in the tax sale, which
did not sell and Mr. Sullivan suggested be striken to the County.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Strike Property H-3-2-3-432 to
Washington County. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF THE 2025 CERTIFIED TAX RATES FOR WASHINGTON
COUNTY

Mr. Sullivan explained the certified tax rate for Washington County will be as follows: General
Operations rate is 0.000361; the Interest and Sinking Fund/Bond is Sufficient; the Library Rate is
0.000125 for a total budgeted revenue of $19,567,850. The County Assessing & Collecting Rate
is 0.000223 for a total budget of $9,059,082.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve 2025 Certified Tax Rates.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3539, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE EXECUTION OF A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN
WASHINGTON COUNTY AND DOVE CENTER FOR IMPROVING THE CRIMINAL
JUSTICE RESPONSE TO DOMESTIC AND SEXUAL CRIMES

Dove Center Grants Manager Trudy Despain and Director Jillian Penhale explained this
memorandum would allow the Dove Center to apply for federal funding to further their mission in
assisting victims of domestic abuse and sexual violence. Washington City Councilman Kurt Ivie
explained the cut in funding from the legislature and thanked the Commissioners for their support
of the Dove Center.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3539, A
Resolution Approving the Execution of a Memorandum of Understanding
Between Washington County and Dove Center for Improving the Criminal
Justice Response to Domestic and Sexual Crimes. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson and Almquist voting aye.
