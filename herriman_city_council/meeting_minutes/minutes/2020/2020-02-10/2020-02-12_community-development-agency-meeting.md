meeting was held on Wednesday, February 12, 2020 at 8:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355
West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in
City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

8:00 PM: (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER)
   1. Call to Order
   Vice Chair Henderson called the meeting to order at 9:12 p.m.

                  1.1. Motion for review and outline of the finalization process to approve the minutes of
                        February 12, 2020
Director Ohrn moved to have the minutes of February 12, 2020 be prepared and distributed to each member of the Community
Development and Renewal Agency Board. The Board will have ten days to review the minutes and submit any changes to the secretary,
if after ten days, there are no changes, the minutes will stand approved. If there are changes, the process will be followed until all changes
are made and the board is in agreement. Director Shields seconded the motion, and all voted aye.

    2. Discussion and Action Items
            2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution approving a Participation Agreement with
                 Game Point Properties LLC in the Herriman North Community Reinvestment Area –
                 Heather Upshaw, Economic Development Manager
Economic Development Manager Upshaw explained the participation agreement would be between the
Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City and Game Point Properties. She notated the
project has been slated to commence construction in the spring. The agreement would provide for an incentive of
50-percent sales tax rebate with a cap of $100,000. Vice Chair Henderson asked if the sales tax incentive would
come solely from what Game Point would produce. Manager Upshaw confirmed and notated it had a sunset clause
of five years.

Director Smith moved to approve Resolution No. R2020-01 approving a Participation Agreement with Game Point Properties LLC.
Director Shields seconded the motion.

The vote is recorded as follows:
Director Jared Henderson                         Aye
Director Steven Shields                          Aye
Director Sherrie Ohrn                            Aye
Director Clint Smith                             Aye
Chair David Watts                                Aye
The motion passed unanimously.

    3. Adjournment
Director Smith moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 9:16 p.m. Director Shields seconded
the motion, and all present voted aye.

Community Development and Renewal Agency Meeting.
