D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: I want to talk about one of our great assets in Weber County.
Our Peery’s Egyptian Theater recently received a $1M donation that went toward a digital lighting upgrade. The seats
have been replaced and floors upgraded. It’s a beautiful theater that we are very fortunate to have. It even has a theater
organ still; one of four or five such organs in the nation. But our greatest asset in the theater is the staff.

E. PUBLIC COMMENT:
   1. Maris Bischoff, Ogden, UT: A Concern that has bothered me for 12 years. In 2005-2006, the AAA bond
      rating for Weber County was received through the work of (Commission names). Since that time, I have seen
      campaign materials saying they achieved the AAA bonding. It’s not honest, it’s not ethical, it’s not true.
      Response: Ricky Hatch (Clerk/Auditor): Bond ratings are achieved for each individual bond issuance. While
      the first AAA bond in 2005-2006 was great and we certainly have built off the processes and benefits of prior
      elected county officials, since then, the county has over the past 6 to 7 years on its own obtained AAA ratings
      for other bonds. The bond rating is for each specific bond.
   2. Sylvia Salsbury, Ogden, UT: Item number F8 has caught my attention due to 2000 Mules. I was wondering
      why Ogden was not included on the list of cities?
      Response: Mr. Hatch: Cities listed do not have surveillance in place. Ogden City will most likely be on in
      the future as we could not get to all the cities on this round of interlocals.

    4. APPROVE A MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH TONAQUINT FOR A VIRTUAL SERVER
       PROVIDER THAT HOSTS CASE MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE FOR THE WEBER COUNTY
       ATTORNEY’S OFFICE.
         Jamie Pitt (Attorney): Servers are moving to new virtual server provider to host the case management
         system. It’s easier to have them host all of our data, system updates, and service. Current case management
         system provider is doing away with the system. We have to pay to move away from their current contract.
         The new company, Pine Tech, will actually host the current program for three to five years while they write a
         new case management program.

         seconded.
