                                                       PUBLIC NOTICE
                                        PUBLIC HEARING – ALTA PLANNING COMMISSION
                                                WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 28, 2024
                                                       3:00 – 3:05 PM
                                                  ALTA COMMUNITY CENTER
                                                      10351 E Hwy 210
                                                         ALTA, UTAH

          The Town of Alta Planning Commission will hold a public hearing to receive input on a proposed
ordinance that makes amendments and additions to Municipal Code Title 10, Chapter 11 Subdivisions. A draft
  ordinance is available for review on the Town’s website, www.townofalta.com, and in the Town Office at
  10201 East Highway 210 during normal business hours. This notice shall formally initiate proceedings to
                                 amend the Town of Alta land use regulations.
Following the public hearing, the planning commission will hold a regular business meeting where it may take
     action to approve the draft ordinance and recommend it to the Alta Town Council for final adoption.

                                Questions regarding the draft ordinance may be directed to:
                               Chris Cawley, Town Manager, P.O. Box 8016, Alta, Utah 84092,
                              by calling 801-742-6010 or by email to Ccawley@townofalta.com
  Individuals making public comment may speak for up to three minutes during the public hearing. To make a
    comment during the public hearing virtually please contact (maustin@townofalta.com) in advance of the
                                                   meeting.
  If you are interested in watching the meeting virtually without making public comment, please refer to the
                             instructions on the Alta Planning Commission website:
               https://townofalta.com/town-government/planning-commission/agendas-minutes/

   Written public input can be submitted here: https://townofalta.com/town-government/public-comment/
