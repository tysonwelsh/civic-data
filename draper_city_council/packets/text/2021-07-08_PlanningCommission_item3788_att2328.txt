SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a site plan application for approximately 0.94
acres located on the south side of 13200 South, at approximately 106 E. 13200 South
(Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned CG. The applicant is requesting that a site plan
be approved to allow for the development of a new office/warehouse building on the
eastern half of the property. The existing building on the western half of the property will
remain and will not be altered as part of this application.

The existing building was built around 2000 according to the Salt Lake County Assessor’s
site. In 2002, a plat was recorded that created the property under review along with two
other lots. This plat is known as the Sherm Margetts Subdivision. The subject property is
Lot 3 of that subdivision.


ANALYSIS
General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Community
Commercial land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is
characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the CG zoning classification (Exhibit D). Draper City
Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020(H)(5) states: “The purpose of the CG zone is to
provide areas to accommodate commercial uses which are not within shopping centers of
integrated design. This zone is intended to apply to existing strip commercial
developments. This zone should not be used for new commercial development unless
integrated shopping center development is not practical or desirable because of difficult



Draper Warehouse                                                    App. # SPR-1127-2021
Site Plan Review                             2
size, shape, topography, or similar problems related to land otherwise deemed to be
appropriate for commercial use. Typical uses in this zone include offices, retail stores,
personal services, heavy commercial and institutional uses.” The property is completely
surrounded by the CR (Regional Commercial) zoning designation.

Site Plan Layout. The subject site is located on the south side of 13200 South. It is
generally square in shape and currently has a building located on the western side of the
property. The remainder of the property is currently undeveloped but has been used as
storage in the past. This request is to build a building on the eastern side of the property
with parking for both buildings located between the two buildings. The current driveway
access from 13200 South will not be changed with this application. A trash enclosure and
landscaping will also be added as part of the new building. The existing building will not be
modified as part of this application. The proposed building complies with all setbacks and
height of the CG zoning district.

Landscaping and Lot Coverage. The buildings on the site will cover approximately 19,389
square feet of the 0.94 acre site. This provides a lot coverage of 47.4% for the site. The
maximum lot coverage allowed by the zone is 50%. The site also contains 8,202 square
feet of landscaping on the property, providing a site landscaping calculation of
approximately 20% which meets the code minimum of 20%. The proposed amount of
landscaping for the parking lot area is approximately 7.5% and meets the minimum
requirement of 7%. This site meets the minimum standard.

Parking. According to DCMC Table 9-25-1, there shall be a minimum of four parking spaces
for every 1,000 square feet of office space and a minimum of 0.5 spaces per 1,000 square
feet for warehousing uses. The parking for both buildings will be located between the two
buildings and shared by both buildings. The existing building requires a minimum of 14
parking stalls and the proposed building requires 15 parking stalls for a total of 29 stalls
required. The applicant has provided 29 stalls and meets the minimum for the zone. The
proposed project combined with the existing building meet the minimum standards.

Architecture. As noted previously, the existing building will not have any exterior
modifications as part of this application. The discussion in this section will relate to the
new building. The proposed building will have warehousing space on the ground floor with
associated reception, breakroom, and restrooms on the ground floor. A partial floor or
mezzanine is proposed in the building which will contain the offices for the building. The
building is proposed to be 35 feet tall which is the maximum height allowed in the CG zone.

The building is proposed to have its primary material as split face CMU blocks in a light gray
color. Accent materials include stucco, stacked ledgestone, metal panels and smooth color
CMU. The building has a minimum of three colors as required by the code. Each exterior
elevation meets the minimum requirement of 75% for primary materials and is as follows:

Lighting. The site plan calls for the installation of wall mounted lights on the new building.
The applicant has provided a spec sheet for the proposed lighting fixture and it is proposed
to be a full cutoff fixture, meaning all light will be directed downwards. The applicant has
provided photometric plans that show the proposed lighting meets the illumination
standards in both Tables 9-20-1 and 9-20-2.

Fencing. There is an existing chain link fence along the east and south property lines which
will remain in place. An existing fence runs along the front or north property line and the
applicant has indicated that this existing fence will be removed and a new six foot fence will
be installed in the future. Six foot tall fences do not require any special approvals and that
fence will be reviewed administratively in the future.

Criteria For Approval. The criteria for review and potential approval of a site plan request is
found in Section 9-5-090(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for
such requests as:

E.      Standards For Approval: The following standards shall apply to the approval of a site
        plan:
        1.    The entire site shall be developed at one time unless a phased development
              plan is approved.
        2.    A site plan shall conform to applicable standards set forth in this title,
              including but not limited to, building heights, setbacks, access points,
              parking, landscaping, and building materials.
        3.    The proposed development plans meet the intent, goals, and objectives of
              the general plan and the purpose of the zone district in which the site is
              located.
        4.    The public facilities and services in the area are adequate to support the
              subject development, as required by engineering standards and
              specifications.
        5.    The proposed development plans comply with the engineering standards
              found in Titles 7, 8, 11, 12, 16, and 18 of this code, including traffic, storm
              water drainage, and utilities concerns.

Fire Division Review.
1.     Fire Department Access is required. An unobstructed minimum road width of
       26 feet exclusive of the shoulders and a minimum height of 13 feet, 6 inches
       shall be required. The road must be designed and maintained to support the
       imposed loads of emergency apparatus. The surface shall be able to provide all
       weather driving capabilities. The road shall have an inside turning radius of 28
       feet. There shall be a maximum grade of 10%. Grades may be checked prior to
       building permits being issued.
2.     Fire Sprinklers Required. A deferred submittal for a NFPA 13 fire sprinkler
       shop drawings are to be sent via email to: Don Buckley at
       don.buckley@draper.ut.us. A complete set of plans, with manufacturer cut
       sheets, and hydraulic calculations. Plans must be stamped by a NICET level III
       or better in Auto Sprinkler Layout. There needs to be a hydrant within 100 feet
       of the FDC. FDC is required to have KNOX Locking Caps. ALL FIRE PROTECTION
       PLANS REQUIRE 3rd PARTY REVIEW PRIOR TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE DRAPER
       FIRE DEPARTMENT.
3.     Post Indicator Valve with Tamper May Be Required. If there is no designated
       fire riser room with a direct access door from the outside. There shall be
       either a wall mounted P.I.V (OS&Y) or a typical P.I.V placed a minimum distance
       of 40 feet from the building with a tamper switch.
4.     Fire Alarm Required. A deferred submittal for fire alarm shop drawings are to
       be sent via email to the following to: Don Buckley at don.buckley@draper.ut.us.
       A complete set of plans, with manufacturer cut sheets, and battery
       calculations. Plans must be stamped by a NICET level III or better in Fire Alarm
       Systems. ALL FIRE ALARM PLANS REQUIRE 3RD PARTY REVIEW PRIOR TO BE
       SUBMITTED TO THE DRAPER FIRE DEPARTMENT.
5.     Knox Boxes Required. Fire Department “Knox Brand” lock box to be mounted to
       exterior walls, near the main entrance and/or nearest the door serving the exterior
       access to the fire sprinkler riser room,at a height of 5 feet to the top of the box.
       Lock box purchase can be arranged by the General Contractor. See attached
       information form. The current gate will need to have a Knoxbox installed for fire
       access.
6.     2A-10BC Fire Extinguishers required. The extinguisher needs to be a serviceable
       type meaning metal head and metal neck. Extinguishers need to be located in a
       conspicuous location where they will be readily accessible and immediately
       available for use and placed every 75 feet of travel. If in cabinet or not the
       extinguisher or cabinet needs to be mounted so that the top is not more than five
       feet above the floor.
7.     Visible Addressing Required. New and existing buildings shall have approved
       address numbers plainly legible and visible from the street fronting the property.
       These numbers shall contrast with their background. The current building does
       not have a clear visible address that can be seen from the road since the fencing
       has gone up. This will need to be fixed.
