A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL EXPRESSING APPRECIATION FOR KAYLA
MAULDIN
WHEREAS, the community of Kearns (“Kearns”) incorporated as a metro township on
January 1, 2017, and later became a city on May 1, 2024; and
WHEREAS, since its incorporation, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(“MSD”) has provided the city’s administrative, planning and development, business licensing,
and code enforcement services; and
WHEREAS, Kayla Mauldin began working for the MSD as a long-range planner on May
26, 2020, and subsequently became the MSD’s senior long-range planner with supervisory
responsibilities for other long-range planners within the MSD; and
WHEREAS, during her tenure with the MSD, Kayla has worked closely with Kearns to
develop and implement several instrumental projects that have significantly furthered Kearns’s
interests and prerogatives; and
WHEREAS, among other important projects, Kayla played a key role in facilitating the
development and implementation of the current Kearns General Plan, the restatement of Kearns’s
land use ordinances in Titles 18 and 19 of the Kearns Municipal Code, and numerous other land
use ordinances and actions that have significantly furthered the interests of Kearns and its citizens.
NOW, THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, effective immediately that:
1. The Kearns Council expresses its sincere appreciation for Kayla’s years of faithful
service, valuable advice, and significant contributions.
2. The Kearns Council expresses its gratitude for the opportunity to have been
associated with Kayla as a friend and colleague and wishes her the best in her future endeavors.
ADOPTED AND APPROVED this 10" day of June 2024.
