SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval to declare property that was formally a public right-of-way at
690 East 12100 South in the Draperville Plat as surplus, and authorize the sale of the property. The area
was vacated as public right-of-way on January 14, 2020. The site is approximately 2,392 square feet
located on the south side of 12100 South, at approximately 690 East 12100 South (Exhibits C & D). The
property is currently zoned RA2. At the time the property was vacated as public right-of-way, the
inclusion of the property surplus was not included in the City Council action. Therefore it is required to
be declared surplus now.


BACKGROUND
The section of property was determined to be not needed by the City as public right-of-way and was
vacated by Ordinance #1416 on January 14th, 2020 (Exhibit B). Due to its location within the Draperville
Plat and state laws that existed at the time of the plat recording results in the property being owned fee
simple by the City also needs to be surplussed as well as vacated. During the time that the property was
vacated, the requirement to surplus it was not included in the City Council action.
