The following are the minutes of the Planning Commission Meeting held on Thursday, August 6, 2020 at 6:00
p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 W. Herriman Main Street (12090 South), Herriman, Utah.
Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered
to members of the Commission and media.

Ferguson, Adam Jacobson, Alternate Joy Kaseke, Alternate Heather Garcia was present, but did not vote.

Item 3.4 – City Planner Maloy reviewed the request for an exception to the single-family dwelling requirement.
If approved it would grant a masonry exception to accommodate the proposed farmhouse style.
Commissioners discussed the standard of quality and the need for compensating architectural features to allow
an exception.

Item 3.5 – City Planner Maloy stated the applicant requested a two-year extension.

Item 3.6 – City Planner Maloy explained the end units needed additional architectural definition per the MDA.
An extra image was submitted after the packet was created.


City Manager Maloy requested feedback from the Commission regarding dwelling standard updates. Some
styles were not allowed due to the current masonry requirements. Minimum square footage considerations
could also be recommended. Chair Berbert mentioned more townhome standards would be beneficial and he
was not opposed to allowing smaller home sizes. Possibly the City could have different standards for attached
and detached dwellings. Commissioner Jacobson requested language to expand options for exterior
architectural designs. Commissioners agreed exceptions should be reserved for products of equal or greater
value. Durable products were essential for attached dwellings. Commissioner Jacobson recommended
designating standards for different design styles, such as modern or farmhouse. Current standards don’t allow
for creativity. Exterior material options should be available for different types of architectural designs.
Commissioners stated multi-family dwellings needed high standards. It is hard to quantify good design.

City Planner Maloy talked about visual preference surveys. Images would be reviewed by Commissioners and
August 6, 2020 – Planning Commission Minutes                                        Page 3 of 10


they would provide feedback with instant polls. Pattern books were another tool that could identify desirable
features. Pictures could also be added to City standards. Commissioners added more needed to be done to
address the sides of buildings. The longevity of products was critically important and smaller, single-family
homes sizes were desirable. City Planner Maloy requested the Commissioners email him suggestions.

City Planner Maloy reviewed the request for a Farmhouse style home. The exception was to waive the
masonry requirement for 40% brick or stone. Farmhouse style home examples were displayed and featured
prominent porches, no visible garages and other design elements. The proposed elevation and site plan were
displayed.

Commissioners discussed the front porch size, and materials. Commissioners expressed the opinion that the
proposed home did not look like the provided examples. The proposed product has a prominent garage, the
examples did not. Commissioners expressed the opinion that the home did not have enough design elements
and compensating features to warrant an exception. The Commissioners identified additional elements that
would be helpful for an exception to be granted, if the applicant chose to submit another request.

The applicant was not present.
