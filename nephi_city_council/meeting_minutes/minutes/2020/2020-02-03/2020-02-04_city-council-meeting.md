                                                   NEPHI CITY

Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (press), Donald W. Ball, Shanna Memmott, Dave Nielsen, Jennifer Nielsen, J.D.
Parady, Susan Cowan, Bailey Wright, Levi Reber, Bert Wright, Jami Wright, Terry Messersmith

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Administrator Seth Atkinson offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by and National Anthem was sung by Bailey Wright.

COUNCILOR JONES PRESENTATION:

   Councilor Kent Jones introduced David and Jennifer Nielsen. Mr. Nielsen is a train enthusiast and has
great interest in the historic Union Pacific Big Boy steam engine. Councilor Jones presented Mr. Nielson
with some Big Boy paraphernalia. Councilor Jones also recognized Susan Cowan representing the
Daughters of the Utah Pioneers and Shanna Memmott representing the Travel Council who had helped
with a school program and with the Big Boy celebration held in Nephi.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 1-21-20, work session minutes 1-21-20, work session minutes 1-28-
20, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 2-4-20, and the sale of burial rights to Elton Castleman,
Lori Jackson, Steven and Patricia Ingram. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on
a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to approve the business licenses for Horsepower Fleet Leasing LLC and
Horsepower Fleet Management, LLC. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on
a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Shanna Memmott representing the Travel Council asked the council if they would be willing to write a
letter of support for the Utah State University Extension who is applying for a grant to get funds for the
Jenkins Flat rehabilitation project. The project would include dredging of the pond and installing picnic
tables and other amenities.

  J.D. Parady addressed the council and expressed his concern about the condition of the roads on 200
South. He indicated that he was aware that the roads were ripped up because of the culinary water
project and that the snow plows had created even more damage to the roads. Mr. Parady was given a
book that he wrote his complaint down and the complaint will be given to the construction company
doing the work on the water project.

ORDINANCE 2-4-2020 NEW ZONE MAP ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson presented an ordinance and a copy of a new revised zone map. Councilor Memmott
questioned some of the changes on the map. Councilor Memmott suggested that whenever the council
adopts a change in the zoning of the city that a new zone map be prepared and attached to the
ordinance. Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Ordinance 2-4-2020 a revised Nephi City Zone Map.
Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:
                                Nay: Kent Jones
                                Yes: Nathan Memmott, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Justin Seely

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

    Mr. Atkinson reported that the Community Awareness Survey will be going out tomorrow.
    Mr. Atkinson reported that he had attended a meeting acting as Chairman of the Board of Central
Utah 911 in Pleasant Grove. The topic of discussion was the possibility of Pleasant Grove transitioning
to utilizing Central Utah 911 as their dispatch provider. He explained as more cities are added to the
dispatch service Nephi City’s costs would decrease.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

   Councilor Memmott informed the council that he would be attending a meeting with Pacific Corp on
Thursday to discuss the high voltage transmission line path.
   Councilor Jones reported that he had attended Clean Air Coalition meeting and he recited some
statistics about the consequences of using fossil fuels as an energy source.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competence of an individual, litigation, and real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion,
which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:09 p.m.
