  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 15.010. Intent.
The intent of this Chapter is to provide for the efficient and orderly implementation of the Lehi City General Plan by
allowing for the formulation of Area and Land Use Plans and providing a development review process for multi-phased
development proposals to prevent the waste of public and private resources. This Chapter provides a framework for the
processing of preliminary and final subdivision plats, and preliminary and final site plans pursuant to this Code and allows
the City to make necessary refinements to the General Plan Land Use Map.


  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation of a proposed amendment to the Traverse Mountain Area Plan
allowing as a permitted use the “manufacture, processing, or packaging of food and beverage products” in the High-
way Commercial Planning District. The Area Plan currently does not allow it at all.

The one DRC comment was to consider it as a conditional use instead of permitted. The description in the Area Plan
already includes a lot of requirements.

         Manufacture, processing or packaging of food and beverage products,
         personal hygiene products and cosmetics, drugs and pharmaceuticals and
         which are not obnoxious or offensive by reason of emission of odor, dust,
         smoke, noxious gases, noise, vibration, glare, heat or other impacts, nor
         hazardous by way of materials process, product, or waste and where all
         activities are conducted within a completely enclosed building and where all
         equipment, compressors, and other ancillary equipment is enclosed.

The applicant has expressed they do not have any concerns if the proposal was changed to a conditional use instead
of permitted.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL
Area Plan Amendments are legislative items which give the Planning Commission a lot of discretion. They can

will be made by the City Council.

The public can also express support or concern with the proposed changes. Comments focused on the potential im-
pacts and how any negative impacts can be addressed are the most helpful.


 POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may provide a positive or negative recommendation to City Council on the proposed
Traverse Mountain Area Plan Amendment. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

    1.   The proposed area plan amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the
         General Plan and its various elements.
    2.   The proposed area plan amendment is/is not consistent with the land uses and zoning of nearby and adjoin-
         ing properties.
    3.   The proposed area plan amendment will/will not have a substantial adverse effect on surrounding property.
    4.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.
