       e. Public hearing to receive comments regarding the utility funds transfer to the
          General Fund
          Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck oriented the Council with the utility funds
          transfer included in the FY 2024 Budget, and explained that without the utility funds
          transfer, an additional 13.5% property tax increase would be needed to sustain a balanced
          budget. Ms. Steck provided accounting data for the utility funds involved and explained
          the difference between transfers and administrative costs.

           Chair McConnehey opened a public hearing at 7:05 pm

           Comments:
           Chad Lamb, West Jordan resident, said the Council was asked to raise water rates five
           years ago or the fund would be in trouble, yet the proposal was now to transfer money
           from the Water Fund to the General Plan. Mr. Lamb asked what had changed. He
           commented that General Fund balance was $28 million and suggested putting the
           transferred funds back into the utilities. Mr. Lamb asked the Mayor and Council to look
           for a way to stop relying on the utility fund transfer.

           Chair McConnehey closed the public hearing at 7:07 pm

           Council Member Jacob was opposed to the utility fund transfer in 2020 when it was first
           proposed, and believed it was a policy issue. He was still not a fan of the utility fund
           transfer, and wanted a discussion regarding the policy in January before the next budget

           was prepared. Council Member Pack believed the transfer was proposed as a crisis fix in
           2020, and agreed with the suggestion to address a possible change at the beginning of the
           calendar year.

           Chair McConnehey understood the rationale and benefit of the utility fund transfer but

       c. FY 2024 Annual Budget discussion of changes to date


City Council Meeting Minutes                 Wednesday, June 14, 2023                            Page 6
           Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck said FY 2024 property tax numbers were
           received from the County and reported that a 5% property tax increase would equal
           $918,251 in revenue. She presented changes to the FY 2024 Budget since the last Council
           review and discussion. The County set August 22nd as the date for a truth in taxation public
           hearing. No changes could be made to the budget after August 12th.

           Vice Chair Bloom joined the meeting 7:56 pm

           Council Member Green spoke of the dedication of the budget staff. He knew they had been
           putting things together at 2:30 in the morning and expressed his support for the
           requested full-time budget analyst position. Council Member Pack believed it was better
           to retain great staff and expressed support for the full-time budget analyst position. Staff
           answered questions regarding the increased allowance for tree replacement. Council
           Member Pack was not a fan of reducing the Healthy West Jordan Committee budget by
           $5,000 but understood budget constraints.

           Chair McConnehey expressed support for modifying the budget analyst position to full-
           time. Chair McConnehey said he liked the $50,000 for tree replacement coming from one-
           time money. City Administrator Korban Lee explained that the City invoiced responsible
           parties for any damage done to City property and trees in the course of an accident.

           Council Member Whitelock struggled to include the full 5% property tax increase in the
           budget. She worried that some of the things in the budget were wants and not needs. She
           believed her job was to make sure residents’ money was used wisely and thought the
           budget had too much in it for her appetite. Council Member Worthen agreed and said that
           she would not agree to a tax increase until wants were removed, or at least reduced, in
           the budget.

           Council Member Green felt most of the wants were coming out of one-time reserve money
           and were not contributing to a tax increase. He commented that revenue from new growth
           in the FY 2024 Budget would not cover the increase in staffing costs (but it was close). He
           expressed the opinion that new growth should cover the cost of new growth. He
           expressed a desire for better statistics to help indicate when a new employee or
           equipment was needed. Council Member Green said he supported the West Jordan Fire
           Department but was having a hard time with adding $500,000 to ongoing revenues
           without increasing services. He said a long-term mistake had been made and would be
           compounded. Council Member Green expressed support for using one-time money to
           keep the Fire Department whole and letting a market adjustment fix the situation.

           Chair McConnehey appreciated that Council Members were able to disagree without
           becoming disagreeable.

           Council Members Jacob, Pack, McConnehey, Green, and Worthen indicated support for
           moving the budget analyst position to full-time in the FY 2024 Budget. Council Member
           Whitelock was interested in hearing more details about Council Member Green’s idea for
           the Fire Department.

        c. Council discussion of communication distribution from staff to Council Members
           Chair McConnehey asked if the Council wanted to provide direction regarding
           distribution of information from staff to Council Members when a Council Member made
           a specific request. Council Member Jacob expressed a desire to remove ambiguity and said
           the entire Council should be looped in if an agenda item was involved. Council Member
           Whitelock said she often wrote and asked staff questions because she had asked the
           questions on the dais and been made to feel that she should not have. She said she did not
           want to codify that all Council Members would receive all communications. She
           commented that the Council might look significantly different in a few months.

           Council Member Pack said in general he believed that more communication was better.
           He expressed the opinion that something of major substance should be made known to
           the entire Council and said he would rather be more informed. Council Member Pack said
           he did not think communicating more needed to be codified. Council Member Whitelock
           said she would bring all her questions to Council meetings to be asked from the dais.
