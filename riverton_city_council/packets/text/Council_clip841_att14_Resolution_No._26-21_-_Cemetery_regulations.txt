                                      RIVERTON, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 26-21

  A RESOLUTION AMENDING RULES AND REGULATIONS NECESSARY FOR THE
     OPERATION, MAINTENANCE, APPEARANCE AND PROTECTION OF THE
                      RIVERTON CITY CEMETERY

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Code Title 11, Chapter 15 provides general regulations
pertaining to burial rights in the Riverton City Cemetery; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Code, section 11.15.080 (6) states the City Council “may adopt
other rules and regulations necessary for the operation, maintenance, appearance and protection” of
the Riverton City Cemetery; and

        WHEREAS, in the year 2010, the Riverton City Council adopted Resolution No. 10-32
establishing a set of rules and regulations “necessary for the operation, maintenance, appearance,
and protection” of the Riverton City Cemetery (hereinafter referred to as the “Cemetery
Regulations”); and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the regulations need to be
updated to provide more options for placement of headstones in the cemetery; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the proposed amendment
to the Cemetery Regulations will enhance the appearance of the Riverton City Cemetery while
enabling efficient maintenance of Cemetery space within close proximity to headstones; and

     NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.      Riverton City, in accordance with City Ordinance, adopts by resolution an
               amendment to cemetery regulations found Exhibit A, attached hereto and by this
               reference made a part hereof.

       2.      This resolution shall supersede any prior rules and regulations adopted by prior
               Resolution.

       3.      This resolution shall become effective upon passing.
