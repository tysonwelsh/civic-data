CONSIDERATION _OF _ ORDINANCE NO. __2022-1211-O, _AN _ORDINANCE
REQUESTING A ZONE CHANGE FROM A-20 (AGRICULTURAL 20 ACRE MINIMUM
LOT SIZE) ZONE TO FR-13.5 (FOREST RESIDENTIAL 13,500 SQUARE FOOT
MINIMUM _ LOT SIZE) ZONE AND RA-2.5 (RESIDENTIAL AGRICULTURAL 2.5

ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE (JENNINGS FAMILY TRUST)

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the applicants are requesting to
change the zoning designation of a parcel located in Pine Valley. The purpose of the zone change
is to bring the property into a suitable zone for a future subdivision. Initially the applicant was
seeking a denser request, but later modified the request to RA-1 zoning on the northern portion of
the property and FR-13.5 zoning on the southern portion.

The Planning Commission reviewed the request and County staff recommendations and findings
are as follows:

1. The zone change and public hearing were properly noticed.

2. The public hearing was held on April 12, 2022, in the regularly scheduled Planning
Commission Meeting.

3s A zone change to the FR-13.5 (Forest Residential 13,500 sq. ft. minimum lot size) zone
and the A-5 (Agricultural 5 acre minimum lot size) zones will allow the applicants to utilize
their property in a beneficial way and still preserving open space.

4. The zoning designation of A-5 for the northern portion of the property would match what
has been recently approved on the adjacent parcel. A zoning designation of FR-13.5 for the
southern portion of the property also would match what has been recently approved on the
adjacent parcel.

5. The zone change represented in Attachment 5 is appropriate for the Pine Valley

community, Washington County, and in harmony with surrounding uses and purposes of

land in the area.

That proof of utilities is required prior to development of the property.

hie The zone change represented in Attachment 5 is consistent with the Washington County
General Plan.

a

Mr. Jennings explained that a pipeline was discovered underneath the southern portion of the
property which would disallow for any building on that site unless the pipeline was relocated. He
proposed several alternatives that were not previously reviewed or presented to the Planning
Commission.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-2974, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
WASHINGTON ___COUNTY’S_ _§ WITHDRAWAL __ FROM ___ THE _ INTERLOCAL
COOPERATION AGREEMENT FOR THE OPERATIONS AND MANAGEMENT OF
CONFLUENCE PARK

Habitat Conservation Plan Director Cameron Rognan explained that an Interlocal Entity was
intended to be created at the execution of the agreement with Hurricane, LaVerkin, and Toquerville
and has not yet been done yet. Washington County desires to preserve Confluence Park and
withdraw from the original agreement while still managing, funding, and overseeing the land.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-2973, A
Resolution Approving Washington County’s Withdrawal from the Interlocal
Cooperation Agreement for the Operations and Management of Confluence
Park. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.
