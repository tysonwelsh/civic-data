            AMENDMENTS PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed amendment to the Riverton City Code; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public amend the Riverton City Code Section 18.25.110, as described in Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Code shall be, and hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit
       “A”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

Exhibit “A”

18.25.110 Farm animals.
(1) Minimum Requirements. The minimum lot size to keep and own farm animals shall be one-half
acre (21,780 square feet). Residential lots zoned R-1 and R-2, which are not at least one-half acre,
are excluded from owning or keeping farm animals. Notwithstanding the foregoing, lots zoned RR-
22 which are smaller than one half acre but are large enough to be eligible for a certificate of
occupancy may keep up to six chickens and one rooster. This exception shall not be construed to
allow ducks, geese, or other bird or fowl.
