       Consider a request for a hillside development permit to determine the ridgeline on lot 29 of South Rim at

       The applicant is Ryan Andrews and Tonya Shepard. Case No. 2022-HS-019. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      B. Consider a request for a one (1) lot and (1) Parcel commercial subdivision known as Delta by
         Marriott located at the intersection of 250 W and Blackridge Dr. The property is 14.63 acres and is
         zoned PD-C. The applicant is Ensign Engineering, representative Josh Nowell. Case No. 2023-PP-
         002 (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

         Wes Jenkins presented the following:

         Wes Jenkins – This was approved at least a year ago as far as the zoning goes. They will be
         subdividing into a lot and a parcel. Lot 1 will be developed. The other parcel will be developed at
         another time. You won’t see it, but they will further divide the building into condos.

         Chair Kemp – If I recall there was a pile of asbestos on this site.

         Wes Jenkins – That is correct, we had analysis done and the engineer said that they wouldn’t get into
         that with the grading.

         Commissioner Chapman – Why did you not make them take out the asbestos?

         Wes Jenkins – You could make them haul it off but we got the State involved and they decided it
         was better for it to stay there than to disturb it as the grading would not disturb it.

         Discussion continued on asbestos.

         MOTION: Commissioner Anderson made a motion to recommend positive approval of Item 2B to
         City Council.
