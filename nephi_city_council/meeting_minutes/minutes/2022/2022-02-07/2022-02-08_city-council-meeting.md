                                        Nephi City
                                  Work Session Minutes
                                    February 8, 2022
                                         7:00 p.m.
                               Nephi City Council Chambers
                                        21 E 100 N
                                     Nephi, UT 84648

1. Callie Newell the manager of the Motel 6 addressed the council about the issue of
   not allowing long-term stays at motels. She said that there is a demand for
   construction companies to house workers for longer than 30 days. City Attorney
   Kasey Wright explained that Nephi City Code allows only short-term stays not longer
   than 30 days for motels. Ms. Newell said that the current enforcement of limiting the
   long-term stays would ruin her business. She admitted that there had been drug
   problems at the Motel 6 and she claimed that she had been harassed by the police
   when she had asked for their help. She asked the council to consider changing the
   code to allow long-term stays at motels for companies that will be doing work in the
   area for a longer period than 30 days.

2. Brett and Garrison Ludlow representing Priority Homes asked the council for their
   feedback on two different vicinity plans for a new subdivision development named
   Loveless Estates. The property is currently zoned R2-Z and would require a zone
   change to R3-PUD to accommodate the proposed development. Mr. Atkinson
   explained that there were some concerns about parking both for visitors and for
   RV/ATV/OHV/trailer parking. In addition, the city’s Parks and Recreation Master
   Plan shows a regional park being in the area. Since this development would use up
   much of the dwindling open land in the quadrant of the city, Mr. Atkinson pointed out
   that the council may want to consider negotiating for some land for a park using
   parks and recreation impact fees. One of the plans showed a park with more units
   and the other plan did not include a park but had less units with more space per unit.
   After some discussion, the council seemed more in favor of the plan with less units
   and no park. The council directed the Ludlow’s to move forward with the planning
   commission process of a zone change request and vicinity plan approval.

3. Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they wanted to participate again this year in the
   Well-Being survey. The council directed staff to participate again, and they will be
   thinking about some different questions that could be asked on this year’s survey.

4. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the city had just passed a quarter of the way
   through the 2-year budget cycle. He showed a list of general service and enterprise
   funds outcome priorities. Many priorities had been completed some were in
   progress and some were forthcoming.
5. Mr. Atkinson reported to the council that the city had been approached about the
   possibility of erecting a Mid-Utah Radio tower on the site of the south water tank.
   Mid-Utah Radio already has one tower in the area and the city has equipment for the
   water system on that tower. Having a second tower could allow us to place SCADA
   equipment for the water system on the tower. He stated that other utilities may also
   benefit from the tower. The council directed staff to negotiate with Mid-Utah Radio
   for the placement of a 100 ft tower on the site of the south water tank.

6. Mr. Atkinson asked the council what projects they would like to see on the county’s
   comprehensive list for the Community Impact Board for funding consideration. The
   council directed Mr. Atkinson to remove the recreation center from the list and to
   move bridge improvements up on the list and to add street improvement to the list.

7. City Administration Updates:

      a. Parking in the PUD code revision got mixed up in the adoption of the
         ordinance to what was intended by the council and planning commission.
      b. Mr. Atkinson advised the council to keep an eye on HB-151 that eliminates
         retail incentives from local control.
      c. Planner Shauna Talbot asked the council for their feedback on how many
         residential units they would prefer per acre.

8. City Council Updates:
      a. Councilor Memmott wanted to clarify how the council felt about long-term
         stays at the motels. The council directed Attorney Kasey Wright to research
         some different ideas for accommodating corporations for longer stays and
         maybe some specification on the type of rooms that could be rented out for
         longer stays. Councilor Worwood noted that he was opposed to any open-
         ended long-term stays at motels.
      b. Mayor Seely reminded the council about a meeting on
         February 16th with the legislative representatives. Mayor Pro tempore
         Memmott will represent the council at the meeting.
      c. An individual approached the Mayor about annexing some property in the
         canyon. The council directed the Mayor to tell this individual they are not
         interested in annexing land up the canyon.



9. Meeting adjourned at 9:55 p.m.
