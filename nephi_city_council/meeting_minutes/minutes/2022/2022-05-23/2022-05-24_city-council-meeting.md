                                          Nephi City
                                    Work Session Minutes
                                        May 24, 2022
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                 Nephi City Council Chambers

1. Ken Tuttle representing Sunrise Engineering presented the phase I draft of the 40-year
   water right projection need plan. He stated that the projected water right need is created
   from actual usage values coupled with estimated growth rates created for Nephi City. The
   plan is prepared to be used by water right management and uses the Utah Division of Water
   Rights duty of 4.0-acre foot per acre ( which includes diversion and depletion) and the .45
   acre foot per equivalent residential connections (ERC) which are both normal duty
   quantities used by the Utah /Division of Water Rights as water usage factors for this area of
   the state. Phase II of the study should be completed within the year, and it will cover how
   Nephi City will implement the water right exactions.

2. Mr. Chet Linton and Mr. Tyson Jex representing AEGIX presented a product used for
   communication within a facility when there is an active shooter or other threat. The Juab
   County Commissioners, the Juab School District Superintendent, the Juab County Sheriff
   and Nephi City Chief Morgan were in attendance to see the presentation and to discuss
   whether these entities would like to partner to obtain a grant to purchase the system.

3. Recreation Director Craig Oswald discussed with the council the adaptive sports program.
   The council advised Mr. Oswald to provide shirts for the participants and to subsidize the
   program and to not charge the participants more than the current fee of $10.00 per
   program.

4. Mayor Seely reported on the meeting held to finalize the Hive agreement. The agreement
   is near completion and will be brought to the council for authorization soon.

5. Council members gave various updates concerning junk on center street, Orgill fields need
   some more care, the Chamber of Commerce has 70+members, people are still parking in
   driveways at the soccer fields, and the Sub-for-Santa money from the raffle has not been
   received by the Sub-for-Santa organization.

6. Councilor Parady moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
   competence of an individual. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
   passed on a unanimous vote.

7. Meeting adjourned at 9:45
