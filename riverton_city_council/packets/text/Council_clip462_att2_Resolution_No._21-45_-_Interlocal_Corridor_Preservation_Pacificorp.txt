                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 21-45

INTERLOCAL COOPERATION BETWEEN THE CITY OF RIVERTON AND SALT
LAKE COUNTY FOR AN AGREEMENT PROVIDING FOR THE TRANSFER OF
SEVENTY-EIGHT THOUS DOLLARS ($78,000) OF COUNTY CORRIDOR
PRESERVATION FUNDS TO THE CITY OF RIVERTON TO BE USED BY THE CITY
TO ACQUIRE CERTAIN PROPERTY FOR TRANSPORTATION PURPOSES.

       WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-202 provides that any two or more public
agencies may enter into an agreement with one another for joint or cooperative actions; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City (the “City”) and Salt Lake County (“the County”) are public
agencies as contemplated in UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-101; and

       WHEREAS, the City has the need to widen the south side of 12600 S across the property
located at 4515 W and 12600 S which is owned by Pacificorp; and

      WHEREAS, Pacificorp agreed to sale the north10 ft of said property described as
4515 W 2600 S to the City for construction of a future widening project; and

        WHERAS, the City of Riverton sought County Corridor Preservation funds to purchase
said property; and

      WHEREAS, on January 24, 2019 the Salt Lake County Council of Governments (COG)
awarded to the City $78,000 for said purchase; and

      WHEREAS, Corridor Preservation funds are transferred by execution of an Interlocal
Cooperation Agreement;

       WHEREAS, the County has prepared an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement for the City
to execute; and

        WHEREAS, approval of this agreement is beneficial for the health, welfare, and safety
of the citizens of Riverton City; and

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

        1.      The Interlocal Cooperation Agreement by and between Riverton City and Salt
Lake County for the transfer of County Corridor Preservation funds for the purchase of property
owned by Pacificorp to widen 12600 S street be approved, and the Mayor is hereby authorized to
sign the Agreement. Prior to signing the agreement, the City Attorney is authorized to make any
minor revisions deemed necessary to accomplish the intent of the agreement but do not result in
a material alteration to the terms of the Agreement.
       2.     The Interlocal Agreement is made effective on the date the Agreement is signed
by the Mayor.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 3rd day of
