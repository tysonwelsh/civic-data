                              RESOLUTION NO. 24-60

         A RESOLUTION APPOINTING __________________________TO
         FILL THE VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF DRAPER CITY
         COUNCILMEMBER CREATED BY THE RESIGNATION OF CAL
         ROBERTS

      WHEREAS, Cal Roberts was duly elected as a City Councilmember for Draper

City in November of 2019 and took office in January 2020; and

      WHEREAS, Cal Roberts was elected as a Representative in the Utah State

Legislature on November 5, 2024, and resigned his seat on the Draper City Council on

November 12, 2024, thereby creating a vacancy in the office of City Councilmember;

and

      WHEREAS, the City Council is required by Utah Code Section 20A-1-510 to fill

the vacancy within thirty days of the vacancy being created; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council provided a public notice inviting interested and

qualified individuals in the city to submit their names to the City Council for

consideration as candidates to fill the position of City Councilmember; and

      WHEREAS, the City Council, in an open and public meeting held on November

19, 2024, received presentations by the candidates present at the meeting and

considered the qualifications and background of each of the candidates.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,

STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. The         Draper      City     Council    hereby     appoints
____________________________ to serve as a City Councilmember for Draper City until
January 5, 2026;

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.
PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 19TH DAY OF NOVEMBER, 2024.
