   In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
  appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinions or purported facts.

b. Public Hearing – Resolution 2022-10 33:21
A resolution approving the appointments of representatives to the Avon, Cornish, Hyde Park, Millville/Nibley, Newton, Paradise,
and Richmond Cemetery Districts Boards of Trustees
Discussion: Members of the public seeking appointment to Cemetery Districts Boards of Trustees were invited to stand and
briefly present to the County Council (previously serving members on the Boards seeking unopposed reappointment did not
speak); from Hyde Park/North Logan District: Rachael Willis, Alma Brown, Jolene Kirby, Maureen Herns, Mark Lind, Cody Johnson;
from Millville/Nibley District: Randy Feezer, Doug Pearson, Chad Davis, Dan Ames, Thayne Mickelson; from Cornish District: Dale
Buxton ; from Newton District, Justin Cooley; from Paradise District: David Curtis, Robert Lee.
County Attorney John Luthy read the appointment procedures and applicant qualifications from county code.
Members of the public were invited to stand and comment on the appointments to Cemetery Districts Boards of Trustees and
other related issues.
Action: Motion made by Councilmember Karl Ward to close the public hearing; seconded by Councilmember Gordon Zilles
1:02:16
Motion passes.
Aye: 7 Barbara Tidwell, Paul R. Borup, David Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Karl B. Ward, Gina H. Worthen, Gordon A. Zilles
Nay: 0

c. Public Hearing – Ordinance 2022-14 1:03:44
An ordinance to vacate any public right-of-way along 10940 South, lying north and within the boundary lines of the One Sixty Five
subdivision in Avon
Discussion: Matt Phillps gave the Council information regarding the vacation of public right-of-way along 10940 South. The
Council asked clarifying questions.
Members of the public spoke on the matter and commented on the history and use of the roadway and surrounding properties,
including: Garrie Maughan, Dirk Lewis, Roger Pulsipher, David Best, Owen and Brooke Canter, and County Attorney John Luthy.
Action: Motion made by Councilmember Gina Worthen to close the public hearing; seconded by Councilmember Paul Borup
1:44:44
Motion passes.
Aye: 7 Barbara Tidwell, Paul R. Borup, David Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Karl B. Ward, Gina H. Worthen, Gordon A. Zilles
Nay: 0

d. Public Hearing – Resolution 2022-14 –Open 2022 Cache County Budget 1:44:59
Discussion: Cameron Jenson presented Resolution 2022-14, a resolution increasing the budget appropriations for certain County
departments. Council members asked questions about specific items on the proposed budget and ARPA funds, including funding
for new software for the Development Services Department. Cameron Jenson summarized some items for the Council and the
Council asked clarifying questions.
Terryl Warner spoke concerning funding for Victims Services. Council members asked questions and made comments.
County Attorney John Luthy commented on budget amendment procedure according to County code. The Council discussed the
procedure for amending the budget and adjusting Victim Services funds.
Action: Motion made by Councilmember Gordon Zilles to close the public hearing; seconded by Councilmember David Erickson
2:23:30
