Council and Administration Retreat

Saturday March 19, 2022

know that they are there to attend the Sandy City Retreat.
There will be a quorum of Council Members present during the retreat, however no final decisions will be made on
any Sandy City business.

8:00 Breakfast
Breakfast was available to participants from 8:00 am - 8:30 am
8:30 Welcome Remarks from Mayor Zoltanski and Introductions
Mayor Zoltanski welcomed those in attendance and thanked Council Members and staff
for attending the Saturday retreat.
8:45 Leading at the Speed of Trust: The Case for Trust
Mr. Shawn Moon, the retreat facilitator, began his presentation on the Speed of Trust. He
spoke about trust, leadership, character, and integrity.
10:00 Break
10:15 Leading at the Speed of Trust: Self-Trust
Mr. Moon discussed the 4 Cores of Trust. Following his presentation, the group was
divided into breakdown groups for small group discussion.
12:00 Lunch Break

City Council Meeting Minutes March 19, 2022
12:30 Leading at the Speed of Trust: Relationship Trust
Mr. Moon presented on the 13 Behaviors to Develop Trust and also reviewed the 13
Counterfeit Behaviors that erode trust. Small group activities followed the presentation
which included role playing the behaviors and small group discussion related to the
development, restoration, and implementation of the trust behaviors. Smart Trust was
also discussed.
2:00 Break
2:25 Review and discussion surrounding the Sandy Mission/Vision Statements
Mr. Moon discussed the development and importance of a city-wide mission and vision
statement. In this workshop, each breakdown group discussed and developed a vision
statement for the city and presented to the whole group.
3:45 Closing Remarks from Council Chair Houseman
Council Chair Marci Houseman gave closing remarks. She thanked Mr. Moon for his
presentation and thanked all who attended the retreat. She spoke about future
collaboration with Administration, Department Heads, City Council Members and office
staff.
The Retreat concluded at 4:00 pm. All participants were invited to tour the aquarium
following the retreat.
