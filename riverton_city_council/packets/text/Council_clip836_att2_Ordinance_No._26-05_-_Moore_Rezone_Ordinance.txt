                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 26-05

       AN ORDINANCE GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT AND REZONE OF
   APPROXIMATELY 1.45 ACRES LOCATED AT THE NE CORNER OF 13200 S &
     REDWOOD ROAD AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN TO MEDIUM-HIGH
    DENSITY RESIDENTIAL AND REZONING FROM RR-22 TO RM-6, TAYLOR
              MOORE, TMOORE REAL ESTATE- APPLICANT

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment;
and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map to Medium-High Density
Residential and the Riverton City Zoning Map to RM-6 for the approximately 1.45-acre parcel(s)
located at the northeast corner of 13200 South and Redwood Road .

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City General Plan Land Use Map and Zoning
                           Map shall be, and hereby is, amended to reflect the
                           described changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.            This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of
