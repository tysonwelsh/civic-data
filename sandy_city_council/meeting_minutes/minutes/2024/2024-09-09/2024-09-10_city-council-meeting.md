2. 24-311 City Council receiving a presentation on a possible amendment to an
existing interlocal agreement for delivery of fire protection services
between Sandy City and the Unified Fire Service Area Special District

Attachments: Resolution 24-39C
Sandy City Interlocal Agreement Fire Protection Services 2024 -
amendment
Chief Ryan McConaghie presented Resolution 24-39C to the Council. The amendment to
the existing interlocal agreement for the for the delivery of fire protection services between
Sandy City and Unified Fire Service Area Special District. The amendment was needed to
service a zone that had been previously miscoded and the amendment changed the zone
to incorporate Sandy fire response service. Council questions followed.

City Council Meeting Minutes September 10, 2024

Council Member Business
Council Member Marci Houseman thanked Dustin Fratto for all of his planning and
organization in coordinating the Friday trip to Seattle for the Council. She thanked the
Community Development staff for all their hard work and dedication related to the Pace of
Progress and General Plan. This has been an outstanding process and the community
engagement and feedback has been great.
Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer encouraged everyone to watch the Presidential debate
this evening. He provided an update from the ULCT Annual conference that he recently
attended. They had excellent speakers and presentations and it was a great experience.
Council Member Zach Robinson gave a shout out to the Public Works Department for the
bulk waste program. It went so smoothly and not one of his neighbors put any piles in the
gutters so the communications from the city and the Public Works Department was
working and effective. He attended the Blue Mass at St. Johns Church this past weekend
with the Sandy Fire and Police department staff. It was a wonderful mass and event. This
Thursday is the 25th Anniversary of the Sandy Amphitheater. All are welcome. The
Healing Fields honoring those we lost on 9/11 is occurring this week and he invited the
community to come. He also extended appreciation to all involved with the Healing Fields
event.
Council Member Alison Stroud spoke about helping at the police department's event, The
Bradley Taylor Memorial K9 Trial this past weekend. It was a great event and our K9
team won many awards. She helped with the set up of the Healing Fields which was set
up in 58 minutes. Pilots from across the country came to Sandy to help with the set up
and breakdown of the over 3,300 flags. She participated in the Sandy City/Canyons
School District awards ceremony for the Freedom contest essay winners. She thanked
the Administration for organizing the event.
Council Member Brooke D'Sousa attended the ULCT Annual Conference. Membership in
the League is important for Sandy City and the conference was a great opportunity to
work with other city leaders. The Council invited the Board of Adjustment committee
members to join them for a Hale Centre Theatre production which she attended. She
thanked the committee members for their service to the city. The Sandy Arts Guild
production of Mama Mia was very successful and their best attended show. Their next
show is Little Women. She attended a recent show at the Sandy Amphitheater. It was a
great show. She also mentioned that guests sitting near her were discussing that there
were no bike racks at the amphitheater and they had ridden their bikes to the show. She
wanted to mention that to the Administration.
Council Member Cyndi Sharkey attended the UCLT Conference. It was a both an
educational and informative conference on issues impacting cities. Last week's Council
meeting was held at the Police Training facility. All Council meetings are open to the
public and she thanked Tim Gibson and Steve Van Maren for attending the off site council
meeting last week and for their involvement with the city.

City Council Meeting Minutes September 10, 2024
Mayor's Report
Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor thanked Katie Atkinson, the Parks and Recreation staff, and
volunteers who helped with the Healing Fields event. The Freedom Essay contest
ceremony was held just prior to the Council meeting this evening and acknowledged the
winners and thanked the Canyons School District and city staff for coordinating the
ceremony. The Healthy Sandy Committee provided gift cards to the Sandy Club to give to
their members.
CAO Report
Shane Pace, CAO invited the community to attend the Sandy Amphitheater's 25th
Anniversary event this Thursday evening and he acknowledged the director of the
Community Arts Department. He also wanted the Council to be awared of trainings that
would be conducted at Hidden Valley Park this week.
Following the CAO report, Council moved to General Citizen Comment.
Adjournment
Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:11 pm.
