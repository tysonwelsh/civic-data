CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2021-1172-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING TITLE 10 CHAPTER 4 DEFINITIONS: REMOVING THE TERM
AND DEFINITION FOR RURAL RECREATIONAL _GROUNDS__AND
FACILITIES. ALSO AMENDING CHAPTER 9 PD PLANNED DEVELOPMENT
ZONES: REMOVING PLANNED DEVELOPMENT RURAL RECREATIONAL
GROUNDS AND FACILITIES ZONE

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the land use ordinance is
being recommended to remove the term and definition for Rural Recreation Grounds and
Facilities, and remove the Planned Development Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities
zone from all of its zoning ordinances, including amendments to Washington County Code
Title 10, Chapters 4 and 9.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1172-
O, An Ordinance Amending Title 10 Chapter 4 Definitions: Removing
The Term And Definition For Rural Recreational Grounds And
Facilities. Also Amending Chapter 9 Pd Planned Development Zones:
Removing Planned Development Rural Recreational Grounds And
Facilities Zone. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO._R-2021-2742, A RESOLUTION
PROVIDING CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDS TO PURCHASE EQUIPMENT
FOR THE EMERGENCY SERVICES OFFICE

Tiffany Martineau explained that the Emergency Operations Center (EOC) is seeking to
purchase equipment including water-filled barricades to be used as anchors for field
medical tents, golf carts for transportation of people and supplies at the event/incident
venue, and interoperable gateway radio systems and other medical equipment. These will
be purchase using Coronavirus Relief Funds.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2742, A
Resolution Providing Coronavirus Relief Funds to Purchase
Equipment for the Emergency Services Office. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO._R-2021-2746, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENTS FOR SCHOOL-
BASED THERAPY FOR VICTIMS OF CRIMES

Children’s Justice Center (CJC) Director Kristy Pike explained that the CJC receives grant
funding to provide mental health services in are schools to juvenile victims of crimes who
are approved by the Washington County CJC. The CJC is accepting applications to fill the
role and it is recommended to approve the form agreement until a therapist is selected.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2747, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND
TRANSCON_ENVIRONMENTAL, INC. FOR ENVIRONMENTAL SURVEYS
AND BIOLOGICAL REPORTS

Washington County Habitat Conservation Plan (HCP) Director Cameron Rognan
explained that Washington County is responsible for completing required reports on the
biological inventories of the parcels, specifically for environmental surveys and subsequent
biological reports to be done by a qualified biologist and botanist for the Mojave Desert
tortoise and three rare plant species.

It is recommend for Transcon Environmental, Inc. to provide the needed services to the
County.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-
2747, A Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington
County and Transcon Environmental, Inc. for Environmental Surveys
and Biological Reports. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and
Iverson voting aye.
