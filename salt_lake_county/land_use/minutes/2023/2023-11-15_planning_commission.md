OAM2023-001008 - An ordinance repealing and replacing Title 18 of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances, 2001, entitled “Subdivi sions,” in order to update the title to reflect state legislative changes, 
changes to the planning and development service provider for Salt Lake County, and to otherwise update 
Salt Lake County policy on subdivisions to reflect better principles of plannin g, efficiency, and 
affordability; and making other related changes. Presenter: Brian Tucker and Zach Shaw (Motion/Voting) 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the 
ordinance amendment. 
 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding who this ordinance amendment comes before for 
consideration and approval and about the requirement for masonry or durable wall s for fencing  as 
recommended by the mayor. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Citizen 
Name: Katie Jelitto 
Address: 15152 South Rose Canyon Road 
Comments: Ms. Jelitto said the example given by staff was a forty-acre parcel subdivided, which seemed 
like a major subdivision. She asked why they would want to push through this type of subdivision without 
review. She also asked about what a solid visual fence means. 
 
Mr. Tucker said a forty -acre lot would be a major subdivision and go through phases. Noticing would be 
required at that time and the review process would be followed . Mr. Tucker read from 18.14.160B, 
regarding a solid visual fence. The proposed language doesn’t say you have to have a masonry wall, just a 
solid visual barrier that is six feet tall. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To continue Application #OAM2023-001008 - An ordinance repealing and replacing Title 18 of 
the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001, entitled “Subdivisions,” in order to update the title to 
reflect state legislative changes, changes to the planning and development service provider for Salt Lake  
County, and to otherwise update Salt Lake County policy on subdivisions to reflect better principles of 
planning, efficiency, and affordability; and making other related changes - to the December 13th Salt Lake 
County Planning Commission Meeting,  
Motion by: Commissioner Collard 
2nd by: Commissioner Cohen 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
