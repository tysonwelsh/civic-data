                          “ We Show Up” Week Proclamation



WHEREAS, National Volunteer Week is celebrated April 22 to April 26 in the year
2024; and

WHEREAS, HCA Healthcare and Lone Peak Hospital is committed to showing up for
their patients, colleagues, and community; and

WHEREAS, volunteering and community service are essential pillars in fostering
unity, compassion, and positive change within the city of Draper; and

WHEREAS, "We Show Up" week aims to inspire and encourage Draper citizens to
actively participate in volunteer activities that will continue to enrich the lives of
those in need and strengthen the Draper community; and

WHEREAS, the strength and resilience of the Draper community are demonstrated
by the dedication of its residents to support and uplift one another; and

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT KNOWN, I, Troy Walker, Mayor of Draper, the City Council
concurring, do hereby proclaim April 22 to April 26, 2024, as Draper’s “We Show Up”
week. I encourage all residents to embrace the spirit of service by dedicating their
time, talents, and resources to support local organizations, initiatives, and
individuals in need.

IN WITNESS THEREOF, I have set my hand and caused to be affixed the Great Seal
of Draper this 9th day of April 2024.

                                                     Troy K. Walker, Mayor
