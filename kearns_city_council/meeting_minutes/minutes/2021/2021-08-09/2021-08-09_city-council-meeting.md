Agreement for Canal Crossing

                Madeline Francisco-Galang, Salt Lake County Engineering, stated the 4700
South project has been split into two phases; 5400 W to 4015 W (Phase 1) and 5600 W to 5400
W (Phase 2). The canal portion will be built first because it is empty from October to April. The
railroad is the other piece that is being completed on its own as it requires an agreement with
Union Pacific. She and Nathan Bracken are working on the agreement.

             Mayor Bush stated the Council needs to direct Mr. Bracken to move forward with
the agreement. Once the agreement is completed, it will need to be signed off on by herself.

               Travis Hair, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District, reviewed the
following Ordinance adding used car dealership as a permitted use to the C-2 zone in the Kearns
Metro Township Code Section 19.62.020:

ORDINANCE NO. 2021-08-02                                                    Date: August 9, 2021

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADDING
       USED CAR DEALERSHIP AS A PERMITTED USE TO THE C-2 ZONE IN THE
       KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP CODE SECTION 19.62.020

                                           RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Ann. § 10-2a-401, et seq; and

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (“Council”) is required to approve
changes to the Kearns Municipal Code of Ordinances; and

        WHEREAS, a request has been made to add “Used Car Dealership” to the Permitted Use
table in the C-2 zone; and

       WHEREAS, staff and legal counsel believe this use is appropriate in the zone and
consistent with the other uses allowed within the C-2 zone; and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Planning Commission has recommended that the Council amend
the C-2 Zone to include used car dealerships after holding a duly noticed public hearing on August
9, 2021, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-502, during which time it took public comment on
the proposed change.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED: by the Kearns Metro Township Council the
following ordinance amendments, repeals, and additions to the Kearns Municipal Code of
Ordinances:

   A. “Used Car Dealership” is added to section 19.62.020.

   B. This Ordinance will become effective immediately upon its passage and its publication on
      the Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1).

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 9th day of August, 2021.

Amendment to the Existing Floodplain Ordinance

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following request:

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to open
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Travis Hair, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District , reviewed the
following Ordinance repealing Chapter 19.72 of the Kearns Metro Township Code and enacting
a new Chapter 19.72 to establish floodplain development standards and flood damage prevention:

ORDINANCE NO. 2021-08-01                                                     Date: August 9, 2021

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL REPEALING
       CHAPTER 19.72 OF THE KEARNS METRO CODE AND ENACTING A NEW
       CHAPTER 19.72 TO ESTABLISH FLOODPLAIN DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS
       AND FLOOD DAMAGE PREVENTION

                                           RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Ann. § 10-2a-401, et seq; and

       WHEREAS, one of the natural disaster risks that exist in Kearns is the risk of flooding; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-3-701 and 010-9a-101 et seq. the Utah
Legislature delegated the responsibility of local governmental units to adopt regulations designed
to minimize flood losses; and

                                                4
DATE          MONDAY                       AUGUST                                     9, 2021


       WHEREAS, Kearns inherited Chapter 19.74 – Flood Damage Prevention Ordinance of
the Salt Lake County Code in Chapter 19.74 pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-2a-414, which is
now codified as Chapter 19.74 of the Kearns Metro Code (“KMC”); and

        WHEREAS, the Federal Emergency Management Agency (“FEMA”) mandates the
municipality adopt a flood control development ordinance that adequately covers and addresses
the flood control for risks that exist in Kearns to be a member entity of the National Flood
Insurance Program (“NFIP”); and

       WHEREAS, the current ordinance that was inherited by Kearns, namely Chapter 19.74 of
the KMC incorporating Chapter 19.74 of the Salt Lake County Code, has been deemed
unsatisfactory by the State and FEMA for covering and addressing Kearns’ flood damage
prevention measures due to development; and

       WHEREAS, adopting a new ordinance to conform with the regulations of FEMA and the
State will ensure Kearns ability to maintain its membership in the National Flood Insurance
Program.

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Planning Commission has recommended that the Council adopt
the proposed revisions to Chapter 19.74 after holding a duly notice public hearing on August 9,
2021, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-502, during which time it took public comment on the
proposed change.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED: by the Kearns Metro Township Council the
following ordinance amendments, repeals, and additions to the Kearns Municipal Code of
Ordinances:

   A. Chapter 19.74 is repealed in its entirety and replaced with the form attached hereto as
      Exhibit A.

   B. This Ordinance will become effective immediately upon its passage and its publication on
      the Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-711(1).

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Kearns, Salt Lake County, Utah this 9th day of August,
2021.

Stakeholders

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Lieutenant Torin Chambers reviewed the monthly statistics that show burglaries
and property damage are trending downward. Narcotic cases have risen; however, that is a good
thing, it shows that UPD is getting more drugs off the streets. Officers have been asked to be
more present in the neighborhoods and to be proactive in determining what problems there are
and come up with a solution. There was a shift change in August which changed which Sergeants
are assigned to the council districts. The new Sergeants will be reaching out to the Council
Members to identify problems and find solution.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

                Chief Wade Russell stated the month of July has been busy. UFA had some
EMAC deployments to various states. EMAC is a system in which states can request assistance
from other states in the event of emergencies. There was a major push from fire chiefs, mayors,
and municipalities asking citizens to refrain from lighting fireworks this year. Over the July 4th
weekend there were 33 fire responses with 14 of those being firework related. July 24th was also
successful in terms of firework responses. Because of the rise in COVID numbers, masks are
required in the stations and when they are out in the community. There is an 83 percent
vaccination rate within UFA, those that have not been vaccinated are required to wear masks all
the time. He is working on a pancake breakfast and hopes to get that organized shortly.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

                  Pam Roberts, General Manager, WFWRD, stated on August 3, she and the board
met with the Salt Lake County Council to discuss the reorganization to a local district. Moving to
a local district would allow the board to have total control and independence. The leaf bag program
is changing, there are usually dumpsters provided; however, the area that has been used in the
past is unavailable. She is trying to get a site confirmed and get information out to the residents.
She would like to come back on October 11 to provide a quarterly report and update on the 2022
budget.
