11.    APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE EXECUTION OF RETENTION AGREEMENT FOR OPIOID
       MANUFACTURERS AND DISTRIBUTORS INVESTIGATION AND LITIGATION
Commissioner Lee provided statistics regarding the current national opioid epidemic, pointing out residents in the
small town of Kermit, West Virginia, in Mingo County were prescribed 9,000,000 pills in 2014 – with a total
population of 406 residents. He talked about doctors and hospitals being part of the solution; this contract is a step
forward in continuing dialogue and education. Commissioners Ivie and Graves echoed their support of moving in the
right direction, with Commissioner Graves mentioning Utah County is approaching crisis level and he is hopeful to
find the right way to help citizens.

           Commissioner Graves made the motion to approve the Retention Agreement for
           Opioid Manufacturers and Distributors Investigation and Litigation as detailed
           in Regular Agenda Item No. 11. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Lee
           and carried with the following vote:
