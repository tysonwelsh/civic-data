CEDAR RIDGE TOWNHOME PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT AMENDED APPLICATION APPROVED:

    City Administrator Seth Atkinson updated the council that the developers of Cedar Ridge Townhome
Planned Unit Development (PUD) had submitted an amended application requesting a conditional
building permit for the first five units before the infrastructure is completed. The developer will be able
to access the property from the back side. The developer and the owner are the same person;
therefore, they have interest in the infrastructure being installed correctly. The developer would be
required to bond for 125% of the improvements. Mayor Nielson asked why the developer wanted to
begin construction before the infrastructure was in place. Mr. Atkinson answered that time is money
and the developer would like to build a model home so that he can begin selling units as soon as
possible. Councilor Worwood moved to approve the amended PUD application. Councilor Seely
seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:
                                   Nay: Jones
                                   Yes: Ostler, Seely, Memmott, and Worwood

City Attorney Kasey Wright arrived 8:00 p.m.

ANNEXATION LEVEL CHANGE REQUEST APPROVED TO LEVEL 2.

   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that two landowners had approached the city to evaluate the
possibility of changing the level of certain properties in the annexation area map from a level 3 to a level
1. The property was on the southern edge of the city to the west of the freeway. Using the definitions
in the current annexation policy plan and due to the installation of the city’s south substation and also
the extension of water lines to the south as part of the city’s comprehensive water project, utilities in
the area were much more accessible than they have been in the past. Natural gas and water were
found along the eastern boundaries and electric lines ran in the middle and along the northern
boundary of the property. Sewer lines are less accessible but could be found along the eastern
boundary. Septic systems or lift stations could be installed to service any development.
   A discussion ensued about the sewer questions among the council. Councilor Memmott felt that until
the sewer question could be answered more fully, he proposed that the area be changed to a level 2
instead of a level 1. Councilor Seely moved to change the proposed area
 to a level 2 on the annexation area map. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion
passed on the following vote:
                                           Nay: Ostler
                                           Yes: Seely, Memmott, Worwood, and Jones

ELECTRIC FUND LONG-TERM RATE PLAN ORDINANCE 05-19-2020 ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the need to capitalize on low revenue bond rates to fund the
north side substation project. He said that the electric fund is in a good position to meet the
requirements by lenders but there is a long-term concern about meeting the debt coverage ratio desired
by lenders. Lenders like to see net income large enough to cover 125% of an annual debt service
payment. He further explained in order to demonstrate the council’s commitment to having an
attractive debt coverage ratio, the staff recommended adopting Ordinance 05-19-2020 a long-term rate
plan. While rate revenue was being examined, it was determined that a change in the power charges
rates for commercial, large commercial, high voltage, and irrigation was warranted.
   Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordinance 05-19-2020: AN ORDINANCE OF NEPHI CITY FOR THE
PURPOSE OF EXTABLISHING A SCHEDULE OF THE FEES CHARGED BY THE CITY FOR ELECTRIC SERVICE.
Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

JUAB COUNTY FAILITY LEASE AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained that the fireworks for the 4th of July celebration need to be moved to the large
arena at the Juab County fairgrounds because of residential construction near the field where the
fireworks have been lit in the past. Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign a facility
lease agreement with Juab County. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

THREE- YEAR RECYCLOPS AGREEMENT APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to authorize the Mayor to sign a three-year agreement with Recyclops.

motion passed on a unanimous vote.
