General Plan Moratorium

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution lifting the moratorium on the work
of the White City Metro Township Planning Commission on the White City Metro Township
General Plan:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-10-01                                                  Date: October 4, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL LIFTING
       THE MORATORIUM ON THE WORK OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
       PLANNING COMMISSION ON THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
       GENERAL PLAN

                                          RECITALS

A.    The White City Metro Township (“White City’) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B.    The White City Metro Township Council (“Council”) is a Metro Township Council pursuant
to UCA §§ 10-3b-501 et al.

C.     The White City Metro Township Planning Commission (“Planning Commission”) is the
Planning Commission for White City pursuant to UCA §§ 10-9a-301 et al.

D.     On April 5, 2018 the Council passed Resolution 18-04-03 ceasing the work of the Planning
Commission on the White City General Plan until the Planning Commission and Planning Staff
had received proper land use training and the State of Utah had clarified the substance to be
contained in a Moderate Income Housing Plan.

E.     At the time of this Resolution, it has been determined the concerns that stopped the work
on the General Plan have been satisfactorily answered and/or addressed.

F.     White City needs a General Plan to fulfill its land use statutory requirements as a
municipality under State Law.

G.     White City needs to commence work on the White City General Plan to validate its grant
requests for trails funding and other grant funding.

                                        RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
hereby lifts the suspension of the work of the Planning Commission on the White City General
