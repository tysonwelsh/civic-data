City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1662

On July 1, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1662 amending Sections
9-3-040, 9-3-060 and 9-8-050 of the Draper City Municipal Code relating to new and
unlisted land uses, otherwise known as the City Initiated New and Unlisted Land Uses
Zoning Text Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
