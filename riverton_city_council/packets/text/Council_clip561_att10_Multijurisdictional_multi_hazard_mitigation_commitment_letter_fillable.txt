 RE:   Letter of Commitment as Participating Jurisdiction in Salt Lake County Multi-

Dear Hazard Mitigation Officer:

As the Federal Emergency Management Agency’s (FEMA) Local Mitigation Plan requirements
under 44 CFR §201.6 specifically identify criteria that allow for multi-jurisdictional mitigation
plans and that many issues are better resolved by evaluating hazards more comprehensively by
coordinating at the county, regional, or watershed level, the Riverton City                       is
submitting this letter of commitment to confirm that Riverton City                         has
agreed to participate in the Salt Lake County Multi-jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Planning.

Furthermore, as a condition of participation in the mitigation planning, Riverton City
agrees to meet the requirements for mitigation plans identified in 44 CFR
§201.6 and to provide such cooperation as is necessary and in a timely manner to the Salt Lake
County Emergency Management to complete the plan in conformance with FEMA
requirements.

Riverton City                   understands that it must engage in the following planning
process, as more fully described in FEMA’s Local Mitigation Planning Handbook, including, but
not limited to:

   •   Identification of hazards unique to the jurisdiction and not addressed in the master planning document.
   •   The conduct of vulnerability analysis and identification of risks, where they differ from the general
       planning area.
   •   The formulation of mitigation goals responsive to public input and development of mitigation actions
       complementary to those goals. A range of actions must be identified specifically for each jurisdiction;
   •   Demonstration that there has been proactively offered an opportunity for participation in the planning
       process by all community stakeholders (examples of participation include relevant involvement in any
       planning process, attending meetings, contributing research, data, or other information, commenting on
       drafts of the plan, etc.); and
   •   Documentation of an effective process to maintain and implement the plan; and,
   •   Formal adoption of the Multi-jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Plan by the jurisdiction’s governing body
       (each jurisdiction must officially adopt the plan).

Therefore, with a full understanding of the obligations incurred by participating in the FEMA
hazard mitigation planning process as a participant in a multi-jurisdictional plan; I
Mayor, Trent Staggs                   , commit Riverton City                      to the Salt
Lake County Multi-jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Planning effort.
