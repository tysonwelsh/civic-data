II)        NEW BUSINESS

1. Police Complaint Review Committee (PCRC) Interviews:
Police Chief Wade Carpenter requested that the Council determine from the responses
diversity in beliefs, background, etc. and to look for impartiality from applicants during
the interviews. He would like committee members to be able to give from their life
experiences and add their input into the process. Michelle Downard, Resident Advocate
and Chairperson of the PCRC, stated the committee was currently comprised of two
staff members and three community members. Two of the community member terms
were ending. She asked if Council wanted to change the makeup of the committee
including appointing a Council liaison and more community representation.

Mayor Beerman asked for some background about the committee makeup to see if it
made sense to have a Council liaison. Downard indicated Council involvement would be
a benefit for understanding what the committee focused on and could be a bridge
between Council and policy direction and the committee’s role.

Council Member Joyce asked if having a Council liaison could create a risk if there was
ever an appeal where the Council would be the appeal authority. Margaret Plane
indicated the committee was advisory only and the Council would not sit as the
appellate authority.

Council Member Worel asked why staff was considered part of this board since staff
was normally not part of other boards and committees. Downard did not have
information why this committee was set up as such, and noted Council could discuss
changing staff membership. Chief Carpenter stated Downard should stay on the
committee as staff liaison representing the City Manager's office, but not have a voting
role. Council Member Doilney favored adding two committee member seats plus having
a staff member or Council liaison.

Chief Carpenter reviewed the history of the committee and stated that in 2008, the City
considered terminating the board because there were so few complaints. When the
board was originally formed, the City Manager chose to put two staff members on the
board. Margaret Plane stated there was a time when many municipalities were creating
boards that had staff as members. Now the trend was to reduce staff involvement.

Matt Dias indicated there had been good public outreach for these open seats, and
Council had a good pool of applicants to interview. Council agreed to add two
community member seats for a total selection of four applicants to be appointed.
Downard indicated she would bring back a resolution for Council to amend the PCRC
policy. Council agreed the committee should be comprised of one staff liaison that was
non-voting and five voting community member positions. Downard asked if the terms
should be staggered from the four selected. Council Member Doilney asked when the
remaining committee member’s term ended, to which Downard indicated in two years.
Council approved that those appointed would have staggered terms: three three-year
terms and one two-year term.

Mayor Beerman and the City Council interviewed 13 applicants: Amir Vonsover, James
Bedell, Michael Collins, Nicole Nelmark, David Greenholtz, Rich Weinman, Cassidy
Schindler, Beth Armstrong, Stanley Rodman, Andrew Hecht, Austin Lau, and Mary
Christa Smith. Kara Bard submitted an application, but did not call in for the interview.
The candidates were informed the selections would be made within two weeks. Council
Member Henney recused himself from interviewing Mary Christa Smith.

III) CLOSED SESSION

Council Member Doilney moved to close the meeting to discuss property, personnel,
and litigation at 6:00 p.m. Council Member Worel seconded the motion.
