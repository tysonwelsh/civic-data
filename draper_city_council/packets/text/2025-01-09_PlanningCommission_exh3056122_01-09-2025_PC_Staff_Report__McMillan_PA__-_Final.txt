SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 1.89 acres located along Draper Peak Cove, along the north side of 13400
South, at approximately 13367 S. Draper Peak Cove (Exhibits B and C). The property is
currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting to modify access easements for all three
lots within an approved subdivision. The purpose of the amendment is to accommodate a
proposed single family residence on the property at 13367 S. Draper Peak Cove.

The three-lot McMillan subdivision was approved in 2020 and was recorded in December of
2021. Lots 1 and 3 have been developed with single family homes. Lot 2 is currently
vacant and development is pending approval of this Subdivision Plat Amendment.

According to DCMC Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zoning classification is to “foster
low density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to
generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve
conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals
and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family
dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.”

Subdivision Plat. The applicant is requesting that the fire turnaround and access easement
on Lot 2 be modified to accommodate construction of a new residence on the lot (Exhibit
F). The existing turnaround protrudes into the center of the lot and creates an odd setback
from the access easement and designated turn around area. The proposed alternative
turn around area has been reviewed and accepted by the Fire Marshal and is compliant
with Fire Code standards. The new layout will allow for the desired single-family residence
that meets zoning setback specifications (Exhibit G).

At some point after the original subdivision was recorded, Lots 2 and 3 were each divided
into two parcels in violation of the subdivision ordinance. The ownership of both parcels
that comprise each lot are retained by the same owners. The effect of the proposed plat
amendment will also serve to reverse these illegal divisions of the property and restore the
lots in the subdivision to their original approved shapes and sizes.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request are found in Section 17-9-050 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat if the
        Planning Commission finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment, and
        no public street or municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request and approve the application based on the findings listed
below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff notes the applicant is
required to comply with the following requirements pertaining to the plat and
development of the subdivision:

1.      That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
        Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
        and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.      That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
        development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
3.      If the subdivision plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date
        of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not
        commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be
        extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period
        for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension
        prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year. See DCMC Section 17-9-070.
