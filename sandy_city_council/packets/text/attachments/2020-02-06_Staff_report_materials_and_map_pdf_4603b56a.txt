DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST

The applicant, Mr. Adam Nash, of Adam Nash Land Development LLC, representing property
owners Nordic Alliance, LLC; Nupetco 106, LLC; and The Trustees of The IGP Trust, is
requesting the Planning Commission to review a three lot commercial subdivision, located at
984 E. 10600 S. in Sandy City.  The existing zoning is Special Development-Carnation (SD-
Carnation).  The Planning Commission is the land use approval body for this action, as
designated in the Sandy City Development Code.

BACKGROUND
This 2.17-acre site has been assembled from seven individual property parcels beginning
approximately 1999, including property obtained from Sandy City and Salt Lake County from
the vacation of a portion of 1000 East Street.  1000 East Street was relocated to the east, by Salt
Lake County, sometime before 2002, to align with the intersection of 10600 South Street and
Carnation Drive in the White City Township.  The applicant has purchased the eastern five of
the seven existing property parcels from the original commercial property owner, and has

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING

A neighborhood meeting was not required and was not held for this subdivision request.

ANALYSIS
City staff and departments have reviewed and recommend preliminary subdivision plat approval,
subject to their normal requirements and specifications.  The subdivision meets all the zoning
requirements.  All needed subdivision improvements for this site will be required and completed
through the site plan application and approval.   Some utility easements and reciprocal access
driveway easements will be granted through the recording of the subdivision plat.  Staff has no
concerns about this proposed subdivision.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that preliminary review is complete
for the Jolley Pharmacy three-lot commercial subdivision, by making the following three findings
and subject to the following two conditions:

FINDINGS:
A.  That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer and the
    Transportation Engineer, have preliminarily approved the proposed subdivision plat.

B.  That the proposed subdivision will be finalized with City Staff, through recording with the
    County Recorder.

C.  That the proposed subdivision will dedicate required street right-of-way areas to Sandy City
    on both 10600 S. and 1000 E. streets, and clean up the existing multiple property parcel
    configuration to compose the three-lot subdivision.

CONDITIONS:
1.  That the developer proceed through the final subdivision review process with staff prior
    to the start of any construction (including payment of development fees and posting of an
    appropriate bond to guarantee completion of all required improvements on and off the
    site), according to the Subdivision Procedures Handout.

2.  That the applicant comply with all department requirements as noted in all Preliminary
    Review letters prior to submittal for final subdivision review with planning staff.
