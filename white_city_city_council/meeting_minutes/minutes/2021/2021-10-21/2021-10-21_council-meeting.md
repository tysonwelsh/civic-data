                                       WORKSHOP

General Review of 2021 White City Draft General Plan

              The Council reviewed the 2021 White City Metro Township General Plan and
made the following recommendations:

Acknowledgement

       Page 2 – Acknowledgement

          •   Add Council Member Phillip Cardenaz under the Council list.

Executive Summary

       Page 5 – Executive Summary

          •   Under the “Key Strengths and Challenges” paragraph; remove the last statement
              “and fear of government and planning efforts”.

       Page 6 – Key Recommendations and Goals

          •   Remove the language in paragraph 5, section b, “to allow small, quiet, local
              businesses in future, mixed-use areas”.
DATE          THURSDAY                               OCTOBER                          21, 2021

Chapter 2: Community Engagement

      Page 12 – The Importance of Community Engagement, Meetings

          •   Clarify the number of Steering Committee Members that were appointed (10 vs.
              13).

Chapter 3: Community Background

      Page 21 – “Share Your Thoughts”: Online Comment Boxes, Results

          •   Change the language from “passes” to “transmitted” in the sentence “This
              comment box is accessible throughout the formal public comment process that
              happens after the Steering Committee passes the fully drafted plan to the Planning
              Commission and before the Planning Commission hosts their public hearing to
              consider recommending the plan.”

      Page 30 – Population

          •   The number of households may need to be updated to be consistent throughout
              the document and to conform to the current census data.

          •   Consider changing the term “household” to “parcel” to remain consistent.

Chapter 4: Land Use

      Throughout Chapter 4

          •   Throughout the chapter where it references that the property owner can make
              desired changes, indicate that they must go through the proper administrative and
              legal channels to seek approval. Clarify that property owners need to go through
              the proper procedures.

      Page 38 – Parcel Based Land Use

          •   The parcel count of 1,889 needs to be updated to be consistent throughout the
              document and to conform with current census data.

          •   Consider changing the term “household” to “parcel” to remain consistent.

      Page 40 – What is Zoning?

          •   Remove the last two paragraph starting with “Euclidean zoning is meant to
              promote the public’s health, safety, and welfare. For instance,…” through the last
              sentence, “Many communities that choose form-based or hybrid form-
              based/Euclidean zoning hope to maintain a certain design character.”
