                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 25-54

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER CONTRACT BE ISSUED TO
CORE INDUSTRIAL GROUP FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE 4800 PUMP HOUSE
                MANIFOLD REPLACEMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, a pipe that is part of the 4800-pump house manifold rusted out and
ruptured; and
       WHEREAS, the Water Department Manager assumes that all the existing pipes are in
the same condition; and

        WHEREAS, the Water Department Manager would like to replace all of the manifold
instead of just the ruptured pipe; and

        WHEREAS, they will use existing funds already approved in the FY26 budget, called
out for Canal Crossing Pipe Repairs to pay for this project.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order Contract be issued to Core Industrial
            Group for the completion of the 4800 Pump House Manifold Replacement Project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 18th day of
November, 2025 by the following vote:
