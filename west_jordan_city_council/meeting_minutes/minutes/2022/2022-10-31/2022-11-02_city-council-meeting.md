 1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Whitelock called the meeting to order at 7:00 pm. She noted that Council Member Worthen
would attend remotely.

 2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Deputy Chief Robertson led participants in the Pledge of Allegiance.


 3. PUBLIC COMMENT
Chair Whitelock opened the public comment period at 7:01 pm

Citizen Comments:
None

Chair Whitelock closed public comments at 7:01 pm


 4. SPECIAL PRESENTATION
         a. West Jordan Police Department Badge Pinning

            Kanges, Sgt. Scott Elison, Mike Jones, Gary Christiansen, Nick Melani, Kendall Holt,
            Benjamin Crisp, Taylor Griffith, Austin Larsen, Alia Mason, Caleb Money, Trevor Newan,
            Geron Shultz, Caden Strassers, Jordan Whitwer, and Eric Wood. The Council
            congratulated the officers and took the opportunity for a photo.

         b. Accreditation Presentation
            Val Shupe, Executive Director of Utah Chiefs of Police Association, presented Chief
            Wallentine and the West Jordan Police Department with accreditation sponsored by the

 5. EXECUTIVE REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Dirk Burton reported on events attended in the community and spoke of upcoming
          events and meetings.

       b. City Administrator’s Report
          Public Services Director Isaac Astill updated the Council regarding temporary office space
          for the remainder of the City Hall renovation project. The Council and Mr. Astill discussed
          signage to clearly direct citizens to the location.

           Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck provided an update on the planned West
           Jordan Wellness Clinic at City Hall. She spoke of touring two similar clinics in Arizona and
           answered questions from the Council. The contract for the West Jordan Wellness Clinic
           was awarded to OnSite Care.

           City Administrator Korban Lee praised and thanked Mr. Astill and Ms. Steck for their
           contributions and dedication.

 6. PUBLIC HEARINGS
       a. Ordinance No. 22-47 a petition by Ryan Peterson/Kick Creek LLC requesting a
          rezone of approximately 1.276 acres from HFR Zone (High Density, Multi-Family
          Residential) to SC-2 Zone (Community Shopping Center) for Promontory at the
          Highlands located at approximately 5701 West 7800 South.
           Senior Planner Tayler Jensen presented the proposed rezone of a small piece of land from
           HFR Zone to SC-2 Zone for a Home Depot project and answered questions from the
           Council.

           Chair Whitelock opened a public hearing at 7:38 pm

           Comments:
           None

           Chair Whitelock closed the public hearing at 7:38 pm

           MOTION: Council Member McConnehey moved to APPROVE Ordinance No. 22-47 a
           petition by Ryan Peterson/Kick Creek LLC requesting a rezone of approximately
           1.276 acres from HFR Zone (High Density, Multi-Family Residential) to SC-2 Zone
           (Community Shopping Center) for Promontory at the Highlands located at
           approximately 5701 West 7800 South. Chair Whitelock seconded the motion.

           The vote was recorded as follows:

 7. CITY COUNCIL REPORTS/REMARKS
       a. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan R. Anderson updated the Council regarding Jordan Valley
          Water Conservancy Board nominations, enhancements to PrimeGov, and the General Plan
          update.

       b.   City Council Reports / Remarks
            Council Member Jacob –
                • Expressed gratitude and thanked staff for Maple Hills Park, he noted it was well
                   loved and being used regularly.
            Vice Chair Green –
                • Thanked staff for working to implement the Wellness Clinic and for putting
                   together a temporary workspace.
            Council Member Bloom –
               • Echoed appreciation to staff for their work.
               • Spoke enthusiastically of a recent ride-along with the Fire Department.
            Chair Whitelock –
               • Shared a positive citizen comment regarding the Police Department and thanks
                   for their presence in neighborhoods on Halloween.
               • She reported that participation with Western Growth Coalition had been a more
                   useful in the past and asked for Council feedback regarding continued
                   participation.

       c.   Council Chair Housekeeping Items
            None

 8. ADJOURN

Vice Chair Green moved to adjourn the meeting. Council Member McConnehey seconded the
motion, which passed by unanimous vote (7-0). The meeting adjourned at 7:49 pm
