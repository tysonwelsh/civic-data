               THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set to hold a hearing to review Los Coyotes,
LLC and Los Coyotes Centro Botanero, LLC (Collectively “Los Coyotes”) business license and
take action on the business license including, but not limited to, suspending, revoking, or taking
other action.

               Mayor Bush swore in the following witnesses for the city:

Deborah Garner, Chief Levi Hughes, Detective Cordova, Officer’s Curley, Casada, Lufkin,
Chambers, Patino, Henderson, Martinez, Lopez, Santoro, Decker, and Virgin.

               Mayor Bush also swore in the following witnesses for Los Coyotes:

Guadalupe Sanchez and Hector Sanchez

                Clayton Preece, Legal Counsel, Municipal Services District and Kearns Metro
Township, stated for the record he will be presenting evidence regarding past and recent incidents
to show that Los Coyotes is a general threat. The evidence will be done through proffer (witness
statements through attorney.) The evidence will be for the address of 5415 South, which is a
nightclub that can serve alcoholic beverages. The hours of operation are between 8 pm to 4 am
on the weekends. The Unified Police Department (UPD) has compiled a list showing the history
of criminal activity in Los Coyotes and the parking lot serving the nightclub.

              Shawn Smith, Attorney, Los Coyotes, stated he did not have a problem offering
evidence through proffer. He intends to do the same.

              Mayor Bush instructed council members to interrupt the proceedings if they had
any questions about what was being presented.

                Mr. Preece stated the primary reason for the request to suspend and/or revoke
the license for Los Coyotes is for the safety of the public at large and the safety of the police
officers that serve Kearns. During the past four years there has been approximately 180 calls, 60
of which have been for violent crimes. This call volume far exceeds the amount of call for similar
establishments. He will present evidence of various incidents, which has given the staff and police
department concerns about the operation of Los Coyotes. He then reviewed the exhibits that have
been prepared by staff, which showed the various criminal activities including stabbings, fights,
selling of narcotics, and assault. In addition crimes have been investigated that occurred in the
surrounding parking lot, which included multiple incidents of public urination, activities consistent
with prostitution, exhibition, driving under the influence, open containers of alcoholic beverages
in vehicles, intoxication, selling of narcotics, shootings, stabbings, and domestic abuse.

Officers also observed employees of Los Coyotes not checking identification to make sure patrons
are over 21, serving of more than two beers at a time, and serving patrons that were extremely
intoxicated. Officers of UPD are having a difficult time getting employees of Los Coyotes to
corporate with them when investigating criminal activities.

He then highlighted some of the assaults on officers that go back to 2012 including assault and
being dragged by a vehicle. Because of the number and severity of these criminal activities, Chief
Levi Hughes made the determination that it is no longer safe for one officer to respond to calls
that are coming from this location. Currently, there is a standing order within the Kearns precinct
that if there is a call for service at this location, officers must reach out to their sergeant for
approval before responding to the scene. The sergeant must decide how many additional
members of the UPD force are necessary in order to respond. There is a required minimum of
five officers; however, up to 10 officers may be needed. This takes away from officers being able
to respond to other calls.

Mr. Preece then reviewed the sections of Utah Code and Kearns Ordinances that relate to the
crimes being committed and when a license can be revoked or suspended. The incidents
described in the exhibits raise to the level of being a menace to the health, peace and safety, and
general welfare of the city or its residents. In addition, the events raise to a level of being a public
nuisance and interferes with the comfort, enjoyment of live and property of residents and is a
danger to the public.

Kearns Metro Township Council needed to weight the evidence as a whole and to look at what is
going on in the business and decide if there are concerns, and as long as those concerns are
supported by evidence in the record the Council is able to suspend and/or revoke the business
license. The suspension and/or revocation of a business license is a very serious matter, it is not
something that staff would recommend lightly; however, when there are numerous crimes of this
gravity, and with the standing police order the recommendation is to suspend or revoke the
business license in order to protect the public safety.

He then questioned Deborah Gardner, Business, License Official, Kearns Metro Township, and,
and asked her if there was any corrections that needed to make to the information presented and
what the recommendation of staff was concerning the suspension and/or revocation of the
business license for Los Coyotes.

                Deborah Gardner, Business License Official, Kearns Metro Township, stated
there was no corrections to the information presented that she would like to make. Staff
recommends revocation of the business license because it felt Los Coyotes was a public nuisance
and a menace to the health and safety of the residents of Kearns. She not aware of anything
specifically that could be done to help mitigate or remedy the public safety concerns.

               Mr. Preece then asked the same questions to Chief Hughes.

             Chief Hughes stated he was not aware of any corrections that needed to be made
to what was presented. He is recommending that the business license for Los Coyotes be
revoked based on the number of calls made to this location and the seriousness of those calls.

                Mr. Preece then asked all officers, which are on the call if there were any
corrections to the information presented.

               No officers responded.

               Mayor Bush stated she would like to hear from Officer Cordova because he was
involved with a lot of these investigations.

                 Officer Cordova stated he is a patrol sergeant in the Kearns area working the
afternoon or graveyard shift on weekends. It is a common occurrence to respond to this business
for all types of calls from petty to violent crimes. He has had officers that have been assaulted.
The crimes occurring at this location are out of control and he did not think there was anything
that could be done to eliminate the problem.

               Mr. Preece stated he did not have any additional information to present at this
time.

              Mayor Bush asked Shawn Smith, Legal Counsel, Los Coyotes, if he would like to
present any evidence or questioning.

                Shawn Smith, Legal Counsel, Los Coyotes, stated he had additional exhibits that
he would like included in the record and he will forward those to Ms. Gardner to be distributed.
The information that he is proffering tonight is that his clients would like the Council to understand
the different mitigating factors that have taken place over the years. Los Coyotes has installed
26 security cameras inside of its business and 4 additional ones outside, as well as hiring 12 to
15 additional security personnel through Premier Security to work both inside and outside of the
business. Employees are trained on a weekly basis regarding the law and what their
responsibilities are. Policies are in place that discourage patrons from leaving and reentering the
premises.

Nuisance is very subjective and with this type of business there are certain activities that will
occur. That is why there are a limited number of licenses issued. Narcotics are brought and sold
in many different locations. A large number of these calls are for incidents that occurred outside
of the night club. Los Coyotes shares a parking lot with several businesses including another night
club.

               Mayor Bush asked if Mr. Preece had a rebuttal or any additional information to
present.

                Mr. Preece presented the State code, which defines a public nuisance as doing
anything that annoys, injury’s, or endangers the comfort, health or safety of persons. Mr. Smith
does raise a good point, that there are certain activities that come with a nightclub. What is
different here, is when someone comes to this night club, they are not bargaining for the level of
criminal activity that occurs. Just because it is a night club does not mean that you have
stabbings, shootings, drunken people leaving the business, and assault on police officers. There
are multiple businesses within this location and some of the calls could be attributed to other
businesses; however, when you look at the police reports they are specific to Los Coyotes.

Mr. Smith raised a number of things the owners have done to try and mitigate these crimes, but
they are not working and that is why Chief Hughes has issued the standing order regarding the
number of offices that must respond to a call at this location. He would respectfully submit to the
Council that both staff and UPD believes that Los Coyotes is both a public nuisance and a menace
to the health and peace of the public.

               Mayor Bush asked if there were any questions from the Council.

               No one responded.

                Mayor Bush stated she would like the Council to go into closed session in order
to obtain legal advice from its counsel.

               Mayor Bush, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to go into closed
session to discuss pending litigation. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
             Council Member Butterfield, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to reopen
the Kearns Metro Township Council meeting. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
               Mayor Bush stated she would like to give Mr. Smith an opportunity if there is
anything that he would like to add.

               Mr. Smith stated his clients wanted him to point out that they are actively involved
in the community and would like to stay in the community. No matter the outcome, the owners
would like to meet with Chief Hughes and work with him and his department.

              Mr. Preece stated he wanted to clarify that security tapes from Los Coyotes is only
shared once a warrant is obtained.

                Mr. Smith stated his client has explained to him specifically on the allocation where
there was a stabbing within the restaurant, the surveillance video was given to the investigator
without the need for a warrant. However, later police obtained a warrant for the video. Emails are
available to prove this is what happened.

               Mayor Bush read the following statement:

The Council is persuaded by the evidence and the notice of testimony provided tonight, that the
business license should be revoked on the grounds provided for in the notice. The Council is also
concerned that the current situation cannot be mitigated in part because the business owners
report that they already have a number of safety measures in place that proved inadequate and
because of the large number of police needed to response to incidents at the place of business.
Legal Counsel for Kearns is instructed to provide a written order with Findings of Fact and
Conclusions of Law that is based on the notice and the Council’s findings tonight. Legal Counsel
for Kearns is instructed to provide a copy of the proposed written order with Findings of Fact and
Conclusion of Law to the legal counsel of the business. The Council will meet again on December
2, 2020, at 6:30 pm.

             Mr. Smith stated that date would not work for him. He would be available on
December 3, 2020.

               Mayor Bush continued:

The Council will meet again on December 3, 2020, at 6:30 pm to determine whether to approve
and execute the written order with Findings of Fact and Conclusion of Law. Notwithstanding the
Council’s preliminary decision tonight, the Council would like to review the additional information
that legal counsel for the business has offered to provide for executing the written order with the
Findings of Fact and Conclusion of Law. Legal counsel for the business if therefore instructed to
provide any additional information by 3 pm on December 3. The Council’s decision tonight is only
preliminary and will not go into effect until the Council has executed a written order with Findings
of Fact and Conclusion of Law.

               Mayor Bush, seconded by Council Member Snow, moved to approve the
statement. Roll was call, showing the vote to be: Council Member Snow “Aye,” Council Member
Butterfield “Aye,” Council Member Schaeffer “Aye,” Council Member Peterson “Aye,” and Mayor
Bush “Aye.”
