The Council will accept public comment and consider adopting an ordinance that would amend the zoning map for the properties at 1950 South West Temple and a portion of the property at 1948 South West Temple, changing them from RMF-35 (Moderate Density Multi-Family Residential) to CG (General Commercial). The requests are part of an effort to expand the existing Intermountain Wood Products operation into a new office building with uniform zoning on their properties.
