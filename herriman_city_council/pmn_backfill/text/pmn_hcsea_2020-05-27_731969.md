                                    SPECIAL HERRIMAN CITY SAFETY
                                     ENFORCEMENT AREA MINUTES
                                             Wednesday, May 27, 2020
                                              Approved June 9, 2021

The following are the minutes of the Special Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The meeting was held on
Wednesday, May 27, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street,
Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website,
and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding:        Vice Chair David Watts

Trustees Present:          Jared Henderson (via phone), Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Clint Smith

Staff Present: City Manager Brett geo. Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, Community Development
               Director Blake Thomas, and Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette.

7:00 PM - SPECIAL MEETING (OR AS SOON AS POSSIBLE THEREAFTER):
       1. Call to Order
Vice Chair David Watts called the meeting to order at 7:03 p.m. and welcomed all in attendance. He noted Trustee
Henderson was participating electronically.

         2. Consent Agenda
                 2.1. Approval of the September 11, 2019 HCSEA meeting minutes
                 2.2. Approval of the September 25, 2019 HCSEA meeting minutes
                 2.3. Approval of the October 10, 2019 HCSEA meeting minutes
Trustee Clint Smith moved to approve the Consent Agenda as written. Trustee Ohrn seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Chair Jared Henderson                              Aye
Trustee Nicole Martin                              Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                               Aye
Trustee Clint Smith                                Aye
Trustee David Watts                                Aye

The motion passed unanimously.

       3. Public Hearing
                   Public Hearing for the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area to discuss the
                   adoption of the annual budget for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2020 and ending
                   June 30, 2021 – Alan Rae, Finance Director
Finance Director Alan Rae reviewed the timeline of the budget approval and referenced the Herriman City Safety
Enforcement Area budget document outlining revenue and expenditures.

Vice Chair Watts opened the public hearing.

There were no comments offered.

Trustee Ohrn moved to close the Public Hearing. Trustee Smith seconded the motion and all voted aye.

         4. Adjournment
Trustee Shields moved to adjourn the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area meeting at 7:07 p.m. Trustee Smith seconded the motion,
and all present voted aye.

I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on May 27, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes for the Herriman
City Safety Enforcement Service Area Meeting.
