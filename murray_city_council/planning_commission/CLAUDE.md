# Murray City — Planning Commission subtree

Parallel dataset for Murray's **Planning Commission**, sibling of `meeting_minutes/`, built to
the same schemas (SCHEMA_SPEC.md applies in full). Every `all_votes.csv` row is
`body=PlanningCommission`. Data floor **2020**.

## Files
- `minutes/<year>/<week>/<date>_planning-commission-meeting.md` — **120** PC minutes,
  2020-01 → **2026-05**, indexed in `minutes_index.csv`. PC meets **Thursday**. `source` =
  `civicplus` (61 files, CivicPlus ArchiveCenter, `Archive.aspx?AMID=33` — which ends
  2022-11-17) or **`pmn`** (59 files 2023-01-05 → 2026-05-07, promoted 2026-07-16 from
  `../pmn_backfill/`; born-digital; `source_url` = PMN `files/<id>.pdf`). `format` =
  `pdf-text` except **`ocr` for 4 files recovered from image-only scans** (2020-01-02,
  2020-01-16, 2020-02-06, 2020-02-20 — Tesseract, 2026-07-11). Raw PDFs in `raw/` (never
  modified; the promoted files keep their `pc_<date>_<pmn_file_id>.pdf` basenames).
- `all_votes.csv` — long format, one row per member-vote (or one placeholder per tally-only
  motion), the standard 13 columns. **678 motions across 120 meetings; 2,708 data rows.**
- `minutes_unrecovered.csv` — the ONLY minute-less PC meetings: **2025-04-17** (no minutes
  ever published) and **2025-07-17** (PMN's "Meeting Minutes" attachment is actually the
  agenda). Other no-minutes dates 2023–2025 are officially-noticed cancellations
  (`../pmn_backfill/coverage.md`); 4 recent 2026 dates (02-05, 05-21, 06-18, 07-02) were
  agenda-only as of 2026-07-13 (minutes post after approval — refresh should re-probe PMN).
- `votes/<year>/<week>/<date>_*.json` — the resumable per-meeting intermediate. `all_votes.csv`
  is rebuilt from these.
- `votes/_validation_report.txt` — the sanity report (totals, per-year roster, tally-vs-named
  consistency, contested list), regenerated by `validate_votes.py`.
- `roster.csv` — 15 commissioners observed (from named votes): `member, first_seen, last_seen,
  n_votes`.
- `extract_votes.py` — PURE deterministic parser (no LLM, no network; resumable).

Run: `python3 extract_votes.py` then `python3 validate_votes.py`. `extract_votes.py` skips
meetings whose JSON already exists; pass `--force` (or delete the stale JSON) to reprocess a
rebuilt/recovered markdown file.

## Vote grammar — NAMED roll calls + tally-only voice votes
Murray's PC prints per-name roll calls on land-use actions; routine items pass by voice. The
deterministic parser handles:
- **A) TALLY-ONLY voice vote (minutes approval, Findings of Fact, adjournment):**
  `"Scot Woodbury made a motion … Seconded by Lisa Milkavich. A voice vote was made, motion
   passed 7-0."` → **one placeholder row, blank member/vote** (`names_recorded:false`). The X
  Ayes are never guessed (154 such motions — a source style, not a missing extraction).
- **B) NAMED roll call (`"Call vote recorded by Mr. Hall."`):** a per-commissioner roll block,
  each row a vote code + name → `"__A__ Lisa Milkavich"` style (code `A`=Aye, `N`=Nay,
  `AB`=Abstain, …). A named abstention appears as its own row alongside the printed tally
  (e.g. 2020-01-16: 5 Ayes + `"Sue Wilson abstained from voting."` under `"Motion passed 5-0"`
  — the abstain is captured, the verbatim `5-0` retained).
- **Passive mover form** `"A motion was made by <Name> to …"` and active `"<Name> made a
  motion / moved …"` are both captured; page-break footers interrupting a roll block are
  flattened before parsing.
- **2023–2026 grammar additions (2026-07-16 promotion):** the bare `"A  Hacker"`-style roll
  under `"Roll call vote:"` is the dominant 2023+ named form; new result phrasings
  `"A voice vote was made/taken, with all in favor"` and `"Motion fails: 0-6"`; pre-form
  seconders (`"Mr. Hacker seconded."`, `"Seconded from …"`); clerk-typo intro `"<Name> move
  to …"`; `"made a recommendation that …"` intros; a mid-sentence pronoun motion (`"…, she
  moves that …"`, mover = the roster name earlier in the sentence); and a headerless roll
  block (2025-08-07) that self-opens on two consecutive code+surname rows. A roll block with
  NO printed result (clerk omission, e.g. 2023-10-19 Fairborn CUP) synthesizes its numeric
  tally from the roll and never steals the next motion's result line.

`result` and the numeric tally are **verbatim as printed**. `motion_type` (fixed 12-cat, land-
use checked first) and normalized outcome/tallies live **alongside** in `motions_std.csv`.

## Commissioner roster (appointed, not elected — roll tops out at 7)
The PC is a **separate 7-member appointed body**; a motion with 7 named voters is correct, not
an over-count. Observed commissioners (2020–2022): Phil Markham (Chair 2020–21), Scot Woodbury
(Vice Chair), Travis Nay, Maren Patterson, Lisa Milkavich, Sue Wilson, Ned Hacker, Jeremy Lowry
(Chair 2022), Jake Pehrson, Michael Richards; (2023–2026, added at the 2026-07-16 promotion):
**Pete Hristou, Michael Henrie, Aaron Hildreth, Peter Klinge, Katie Rogers** (Chairs: Patterson
2024, Richards 2025, Hacker 2026). No mayor; the Chair/Vice-Chair vote like any member.
Surnames resolve through a fixed canon in `extract_votes.py` (folds OCR/first-name variants).
**NOTES:** Commissioner "Travis **Nay**" — surname collides with the vote word "Nay"; he is
only ever resolved in a NAME position (mover/seconder/roll-call name), never as a value.
Planning staff (Jared Hall, Zac Smallwood, Susan Nixon, Chad Wilkinson, Mustafa Al Janabi,
Ruth Ruach, …) and applicants never map to a vote — including **"Phil Markham, CED Director"
(staff from 2023**, the former Chair) and **"David Rodgers, Senior Planner" (STAFF here;
a COUNCILMEMBER in the sibling dataset — and a near-collision with Commissioner Katie
Rogers)**. "Commissioner Michaels" (2024-03-07 adjournment) is a clerk typo ambiguous between
Michael Henrie and Michael Richards — honestly left unresolved (blank mover). Do not conflate
rosters across the two datasets.

are officially-noticed cancellations.

## OCR-recovered files (faithful capture)
The four early-2020 PC minutes above were scanned image-only PDFs that `pdftotext` reduced to
header-only stubs (dropping their votes). They were re-OCR'd (Tesseract, 300 dpi) 2026-07-11;
the raw PDFs are retained, the markdown bodies are the OCR text (roll-call rows transcribed
into the parser's `__A__ Name` grammar), `format=ocr` in `minutes_index.csv`. Recovered:
**2020-01-02** 5 motions (3 named 4-0), **2020-01-16** 5 motions (3 named incl. the Sue Wilson
abstain), **2020-02-06** 4 motions (2 named 7-0), **2020-02-20** 9 motions (6 named 4-0). Every
named tally matches its printed result. See `../VERIFICATION.md` §(a).

## Don't
- Don't Present-fill a blank-member tally-only row (voice votes are unnamed by design).
- Don't read a >7 PC roll as an over-count (7 is the full body).
- Don't add stub rows for 2025-04-17 / 2025-07-17 — those minutes genuinely don't exist
  (`minutes_unrecovered.csv`); cancelled dates are non-meetings, not gaps.
- Don't map "David Rodgers" (Senior Planner, staff) or post-2022 "Phil Markham" (CED
  Director) to a PC vote.
