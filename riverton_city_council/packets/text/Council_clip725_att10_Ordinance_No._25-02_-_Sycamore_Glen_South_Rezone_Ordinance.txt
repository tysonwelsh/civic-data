                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 25-02

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN TO HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL
AND REZONING APPROXIMATELY 1.04-ACRES LOCATED AT 11851 SOUTH REDWOOD
     ROAD FROM RR-22 (RESIDENTIAL ½ ACRE) TO RM-14-SD. APPLICANT- RD
                       DEVELOPMENT HOLDING, LLC

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made A
recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the General Plan designation to High Density Residential and the Riverton City Zoning Map
from RR-22 to RM-14-SD for the approximately 1.04 acre parcel located at 11851 S Redwood Road.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City General Plan and Zoning Map shall be, and
                              hereby is, amended to reflect the described changes as shown in
                              Exhibit “A”

               Section 2.              The zoning shall include the following Specific Development
                                       Designations:
                                              1. Minimum lot size of 1-acre.
                                              2. Up to 10 units total, no more than 5 contiguous units.
                                              3. Other zoning regulations to be consistent with the
                                                 standards applied to the Sycamore Glen Townhomes.

               Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of January
2025 by the following vote:
