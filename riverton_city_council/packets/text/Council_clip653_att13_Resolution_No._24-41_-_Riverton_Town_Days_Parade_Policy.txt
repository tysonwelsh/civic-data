                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     RESOLUTION NO. 24-41

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING OPERATIONAL STANDARDS FOR ENTRY INTO THE
                   RIVERTON TOWN DAYS PARADE

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City annually conducts the Riverton Town Days festival; and

        WHEREAS, a component of the Riverton Town Days festival is the Riverton Town Days
Parade, which features a variety of entrants from community organizations, candidates for office,
private businesses, and other municipalities; and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is insured against third party liability through the Utah Local
Governments Trust; and

       WHEREAS, the Utah Local Governments Trust has issued standard guidelines by which
municipal parades should be conducted; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, finds and determines that the arrival of
parade guidelines from the Trust, coupled with a recent and tragic event in another parade event in
the state of Utah, call for the City to update and improve the standards it requires of parade entrants;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

        1. The Riverton City council hereby adopts the operational standards of the Riverton City
           Town Days Parade, as found in the attached Exhibit A.

        2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
April 2024 by the following vote:
