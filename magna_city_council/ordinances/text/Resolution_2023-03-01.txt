                               MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 2023-03-01                                      DATE: March 28, 2023

          A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL A
          RESOLUTION APPROVING THE RANKED CHOICE VOTING
          ELECTION METHOD FOR THE 2023 MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
          ELECTION AND AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN
          INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN SALT LAKE
          COUNTY ELECTION DIVISION AND MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
          FOR ELECTION SERVICES

     WHEREAS, during 2023, Municipal Elections will be conducted within Magna Metro
Township for Council Districts 1, 2, and 3 municipal council seats; and

      WHEREAS, the Council has made the decision to have the municipal election conducted by
Ranked Choice Voting by election officials of Salt Lake County (“the County”); and

       WHEREAS, the County has the expertise and election equipment to provide such services at a
cost equal to or less than the City may be able to conduct the election; and

        WHEREAS, the County has proposed an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement to provide said
election services at an estimated cost of $16,937;

       WHEREAS, the parties desire to continue by contract said services under the terms and
conditions set forth in the attached Interlocal Cooperation Agreement;

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it resolved by the Magna Metro Township Council that the Mayor is
authorized to execute the Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with Salt Lake County Election Division
providing for the conducting of the 2023 Municipal Election within the City attached hereto.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Magna Metro Township Council, in Magna, Salt Lake
County, Utah this 28th day of March 2023.
