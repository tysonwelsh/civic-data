                                WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING

 A Public Hearing of the West Valley City Planning Commission will be held on Wednesday, July 8, 2026, at
4:00 PM, in West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah. A premeeting will be held
at 3:30 PM. Members of the press and public are invited to attend in person or view this meeting live on Zoom
                                 at https://us06web.zoom.us/j/83896786911.

         Preliminary application information will be available by 6:00 PM on Tuesday, June 30, 2026 at
https://www.wvc-ut.gov/669/Agendas-Minutes. Staff analyses, as well as any final application materials, will be
                                updated by 12:00 PM on Monday, July 6, 2026.

  Testimonies for or against the proposed applications can be provided to the Planning Commission during the
  public hearing or in writing prior to the hearing. If you wish to submit written comments, be advised that this
 should be provided to the Planning Staff member assigned to the application at least seven (7) days prior to the
                 meeting to enable the Planning Commission to properly consider the comments.
