3. REPORT OF ELECTED OFFICIALS:
3.1. Council Members
Council Member Theodore  stated she attended the Salt Lake Valley Law 
Enforcement Service District (SLVLESD) meeting where the board discussed 
proposed legislation relating to modifying the tax levy.  The Utah Association of 
Counties (UAC) did not support this bill so it probably will not pass.

She was not able to attend the Unified Fire Authority (UFA) meeting; however, Sam 
Klemm, attended for her.  He reported that the fund balance for the UFA is healthy.  
Council Member Stringham stated she attended the Municipal Services District 
(MSD) meeting where she was brought up to date on what is happening.  The 
Metro Townships have a process in place to get projects for engineering approved; 
however, the unincorporated area does not.  She is working with the Engineering 
Division to come up with a process.
Council Member Newton stated she attended the Utah Association of Counties 
(UAC) meeting. UAC meets weekly to discuss bills at the Legislature.  Any of the 
Council Members are invited to attend these meetings. 
Council Member Granato stated she was appointed to the Transportation 
Committee for Wasatch Front Regional Council. 
UAC accepted Council Member Snelgrove as the replacement member for Michael 
Jensen.  It also accepted Council Member Newton as the alternate member for Salt 
Lake County.  Council Member Newton was selected as a member of the governing 
body for the Wasatch Front Regional Council. Council Member Snelgrove was 
appointed to the Regional Growth Committee.
