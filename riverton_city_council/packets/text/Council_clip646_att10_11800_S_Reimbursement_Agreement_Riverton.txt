                            REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENT

        This Reimbursement Agreement is entered into this _______day of ______________,
2024 (the “Effective Date”), between South Valley Sewer District (the “District”) and Riverton
City, a municipal corporation (the “City”).


                                          RECITALS:

A.     The District intends to construct sewer improvements as shown in the 11800 South Sewer
Replacement Plan Sheets included in the 11800 South Reconstruction Project.

B.     City requested the District to provide for the installation of certain City water system
improvement, included in the construction designs made a part of that certain Agreement dated
February 21, 2024 by and between the South Valley Sewer District and Red Pine Construction
(the “Agreement”) the attached as Exhibit A and incorporated herein by reference.
Improvements pertaining to City water system shall hereinafter be identified as “City
Improvements”, and the District is willing to complete such City Improvements and have City
reimburse the District for the City Improvements.

C.     The District has agreed to bear a portion of the cost related to the City Improvements in
order to complete the Project in conjunction with the reconstruction of 11800 South Street,
thereby avoiding the need to reroute the contemplated Replacement Sewer around the City
Improvments.

       NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, and
other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby
acknowledged, the parties hereby agree as follows:

1.    Incorporation of Recitals. The foregoing Recitals are hereby incorporated into this
Agreement.

2.     City Improvements Made by the District.

        a.     District shall cause its contractor to construct the City Improvements in
accordance with the attached Agreement and specifications and plans incorporated therein.
District will use its best efforts to assure construction is completed in accordance with the
attached plans and specifications and in a good and workmanlike manner.

3.     City Obligations.

       a.      City shall reimburse the District for the cost of the City Improvements in the
amount of, and not to exceed, Two Million, Four Hundred Thousand Dollars and No Cents
($2,400,000.00) upon completion of the work contemplated in the Agreement, and within 30
days of receiving the District invoice.
4.      Notices. All notices and other communications required or allowed by this Agreement
shall be in writing and shall be personally delivered or sent by Certified or Registered Mail,
return receipt requested, and addressed to the respective Party at the address set forth below or at
such other address as a Party may hereafter designate by written notice to the other.

If to District. to:

South Valley Sewer District
Attn: Joel Thompson, General Manager

Bluffdale, Utah 84065

If to the City, to:

Notice under this paragraph shall be effective on the date it is sent or delivery is refused.

5.      Fees and Costs. In the event of a dispute over or relating to the terms of this Agreement,
or over any party's performance or default under this Agreement, the prevailing party in any
proceeding brought in connection with the dispute shall be entitled to recover from the other
party its costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees, whether incurred in litigation or otherwise.

6.      Assignment. No party may assign this Agreement.

7.       Amendment. This Agreement may be amended only by written instrument executed by
all parties.

8.    Successors. All of the grants, covenants, terms, provisions, and conditions in this
Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the successors of the parties.

9.      Integration. This Agreement, including exhibits and addenda, constitutes the entire
agreement of the parties and supersedes all prior undertakings, representations, or agreements of
the parties regarding the subject matter in this Agreement.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement by and
through their respective, duly authorized representatives as of the day and year first hereinabove
written.
