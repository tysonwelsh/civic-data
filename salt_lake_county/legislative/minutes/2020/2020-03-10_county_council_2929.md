4.2 Proclamation in Celebration of Wasatch Mountain Club ’s 
100th Anniversary
20-0289
Attachments: Resolution in Celebration of Wasatch Mountain Club 100th 
Anniversary
Staff Report
Council Member Granato  read the following proclamation celebrating 
Wasatch Mountain Club’s Centennial Anniversary:  
CELEBRATING WASATCH MOUNTAIN CLUB ’S CENTENNIAL 
ANNIVERSARY
Be it resolved by the Salt Lake County Council, the Mayor concurring 
therein:
WHEREAS, in 1920  the Wasatch Mountain Club was formed by a 
close-knit group of dedicated hikers with a deep appreciation for nature and 
outdoor exploration; and
WHEREAS, the Wasatch Mountain Club was created to promote the 
physical and  spiritual well -being of its members and unite the energy, 
interests and knowledge of students, explorers and lovers of the mountains, 
deserts and rivers of Utah through activities that promote awareness of our 
scenic beauties and encourage preservation of our natural areas; and
WHEREAS, the Wasatch Mountain Club strives to collect and disseminate 
information regarding the Rocky Mountains in behalf of science, literature 
and art; and
WHEREAS, with activities that include hiking, biking, swimming, climbing,

camping, kayaking, rafting, and canoeing, the Wasatch Mountain Club has 
not only fostered appreciation for our natural world, but has also 
championed the benefits of health and wellness through outdoor activity and 
exploration; and
WHEREAS, the Wasatch Mountain Club fosters social leadership, 
development, and organizational skills through its wholly volunteer -based 
program; and
WHEREAS, the Wasatch Mountain Club has sponsored the Wasatch 
Mountain Club Scholarship Fund to support education through the Parks, 
Recreation, and Tourism Program at the University of Utah, encouraging 
students to promote sustainability, advocate for community health, and 
inform policy; and
WHEREAS, the Wasatch Mountain Club has been serving the Utah 
community for 100 years, gathering members from across the state; and
WHEREAS, the activities of the Wasatch Mountain Club support and 
validate Salt Lake County ’s efforts to preserve natural lands for the 
enjoyment of county residents and the public at large.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that Salt Lake County Council 
members, the Mayor concurring therein, recognize and celebrate the 100th 
anniversary of the Wasatch Mountain Club.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the County and the Mayor acknowledge 
and give thanks to the members of the Wasatch Mountain Club who willingly 
volunteer their time to provide opportunities for residents to appreciate, 
enjoy, and protect the natural world around us.
Signed this 10th day of March 2020.

A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
APPROVING EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION 
AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE CITY CORPORATION PROVIDING 
FOR THE COUNTY ’S OPERATION CERTAIN RECREATION 
PROGRAMING AND MANAGEMENT.
W I T N E S S E T H
WHEREAS, Salt Lake City Corporation (the “City”) and Salt Lake County 
(the “County”) entered into an interlocal cooperation agreement MA 10405C 
dated May 21, 2010, amended June 20, 2012 (the “Prior Agreement ”) under 
which the City transferred to the County and the County accepted recreation 
programming and management responsibilities for the programs described in 
the Prior Agreement.
WHEREAS, the term of the Prior Agreement ended on January 1, 2020, and 
was inadvertently not extended by the parties for the additional ten -year 
period authorized by the Prior Agreement.
WHEREAS, the parties desire to enter into a new Agreement in order to 
continue operating under the terms of the Prior Agreement.
WHEREAS, as public agencies, the parties are authorized under the Utah 
Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code § 11-13-101, et seq. (the “Interlocal 
Act”), to make the most efficient use of their powers by acting cooperatively 
to provide needed services and facilities so that the parties benefit from 
economy of scale and shared resources.
R E S O L U T I O N
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the County Council 
of Salt Lake County:
1. That the attached Interlocal Agreement between Salt Lake County and 
the Salt Lake City Corporation is approved, in substantially the form attached 
hereto as ADDENDUM A, and that the Salt Lake County Mayor is 
authorized to execute the same.
2. That the Interlocal Agreement will become effective as set forth in the
