PUBLIC HEARINGS
A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2020-
     2021 BUDGET
     Chairwoman Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Redevelopment Agency Meeting scheduled June 1, 2021, in order for the Agency
     to hear and consider public comments regarding re-opening the FY 2020-2021
     Budget.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
       information as follows:

              State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
              allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
              West Valley City Redevelopment Agency holds, as needed on a quarterly
              basis, public hearings to amend the annual budget.

              A Public Notice was posted May 20, 2021 in public view at City Hall, the
              Family Fitness Center and UCCC; also, posted on the Public Notice
              Website and West Valley City’s Website. Notice was given that a public
              hearing is to be held June 1, 2021 at 6:30 p.m.

       Chairwoman Lang opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
       or opposition, Chairwoman Lang closed the Public Hearing.

       ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 21-05, AMEND THE BUDGET OF
       WEST VALLEY CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE FISCAL
       YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2020 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2021 TO
       REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED REVENUES
       AND AUTHORIZE THE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
       The Redevelopment Agency previously held a public hearing regarding proposed
       Resolution 21-05 that would amend the budget of West Valley City Redevelopment
       Agency for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021 to
       reflect changes in the budget from increased revenues and authorize the
       disbursement of funds.

       Upon inquiry by Chairwoman Lang there were no further questions from members
       of the Agency, and she called for a motion.

       Mr. Huynh moved to approve Resolution 21-05.

B.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2021-2022 BUDGET
     Chairwoman Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular
     Redevelopment Agency Meeting scheduled June 1, 2021, in order for the Agency
     to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2021-2022 Budget.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            This resolution adopts the final budget for the Redevelopment Agency of
            West Valley City for the 2021-2022 fiscal year after receiving public
            comment at a hearing on June 1, 2021.

            The Redevelopment Agency adopted a tentative budget that was made
            available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
            Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
            before adopting this final budget for FY 2021-2022.

     Chairwoman Lang opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor
     or opposition, Chairwoman Lang closed the Public Hearing.

     ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 21-06, ADOPT THE ANNUAL
