                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 20-42

    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A LEASE
AGREEMENT BETWEEN RIVERTON CITY AND FRIENDS OF TRACY AVIARY TO
   LEASE CITY OWNED PROPERTY FOR THE PURPOSE OF CREATING A
                       NATURE CENTER

       WHEREAS, the City owns real property around Roi Hardy Park; and

      WHEREAS, Friends of Tracy Aviary would like to lease the identified property for the
purpose of running a small nature center to educate and connect visitors with the natural
environment; and

       WHEREAS, this small nature center is intended to be an interim use in anticipation of
the development of a larger nature center; and

       WHEREAS, City desires to lease to Friends of Tracy Aviary and Friends of Tracy
Aviary desires to lease from the City the identified property.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute a lease agreement
          between Riverton City and Friends of Tracy Aviary to lease city owned property for
          the purpose of creating a nature center.
       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of June
2020 by the following vote:
                                           YES    NO
