          1. 2    Mayor' s Report


         Mayor Kristie Overson reported that there were twenty or more new trees that had been
planted at Vista Park the previous weekend.


Taylorsville-Bennion Improvement District had recently extended an invitation to elected
officials and city staff to tour the Central Valley Water Reclamation Facility. Congressman
Burgess Owens and Senator Mitt Romney were present for the tour, and it was a great
opportunity for everyone present to learn more about what the facility did for Taylorsville and
surrounding communities.

Utah' s    Lt.
             Governor Diedre Henderson had recently visited city hall to meet with the
administration. It was a great, interactive meeting and Mayor Overson mentioned how nice it
was to see the Lt. Governor' s interest in Taylorsville since she once lived there herself.


A community partners lunch had been held in Herriman City. It was yet another opportunity to
foster relationships with other elected officials and have a chance to collaborate for the good of
everyone in the Salt Lake Valley.

Mayor Overson extended a huge thank you to Congressman Burgess Owens for acknowledging
an award Chief Cottam had recently received by sending him a video message.

The mayor reminded the council that it had recently approved a vehicle lease for the police
department. The new vehicles looked different from the current ones, and one had been parked in
front of city hall so that the councilmembers could take a look at it if they wished to.

City administration had met with newly appointed Unified Fire Authority Chief Dominic
Burchett and received an in-depth report of the agency' s fire and EMS coverage of Taylorsville.
She appreciated the great partnership with both UFA and the Unified Fire Service Area ( UFSA)
which was the UFA' s funding mechanism.

Regarding Youth Council, Mayor Overson expressed her appreciation to City Council and Youth
Council Coordinator Kris Heineman who had recently become a member of the Utah
Association of Youth Councils. It would be exciting to see here take the expertise she had honed
at Taylorsville and apply it at the state level. Taylorsville' s 2022- 2023 Youth Councilmembers
had been chosen and would be sworn in later in the meeting.
