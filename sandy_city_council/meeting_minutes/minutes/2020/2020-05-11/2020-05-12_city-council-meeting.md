Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Nicholl reported that Council Member Christensen was officially voted to
                                  the Hale Centre Theatre Board of Trustees.

                                  Council Member Christensen noted the Historic Committee would be meeting soon again.

                                  Council Member Sharkey reported on the Jordan River Commission. An anonymous
                                  donor from Sandy donated 1,000 trees.

                                  Council Member Robinson noted a thank you to the Police Department from a constituent
                                  to Sergeant Webb and other Police officers.

                                  Council Member Houseman updated the Council about the Central Wasatch
                                  Commission.

Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn reported that there was not much appetite on the federal level for another
                                  round of stimulus funds which would include money for local government. Passports are
                                  available by appointment only starting next Monday. Preparations are being made to
                                  move from Orange to Yellow. Council Member Zoltanski asked about plans for the 4th of
                                  July.

CAO Report

                                  Kim Bell reported that the County Health Department will not be issuing mass gathering
                                  permits until the reopening of services is in the Green phase.

Information Items


1.         20-142                 Public Works Department introducing a possible ground lease with Salt
                                  Lake County for a household hazardous waste facility
                   Attachments:   HHW Sandy facility_2020

                                  Public Works Director Mike Gladbach spoke about a partnership with Salt Lake County
                                  (SLCo) for the installation of a household hazardous waste (HHW) facility at the Public
                                  Works Facility in Sandy. He introduced Dorothy Adams, SLCo Public Health Deputy
                                  Director and Eric Michaels, Environmental Supervisor who briefed the Council on the
                                  nature and history of HHW collection and the benefits of a facility in Sandy. Council
                                  questions and comments followed.
