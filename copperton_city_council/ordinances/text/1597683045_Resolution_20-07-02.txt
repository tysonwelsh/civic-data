                      COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP RESOLTUION

  RESOLUTION NO.: 20-07-02________                                      DATE: July 15, 2020

  A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
  APPROVING AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN COPPERTON AND SALT LAKE
  COUNTY TO RECEIVE $23,364.68 FROM THE SALT LAKE COUNTY CARES
  ACT FUND FOR CERTAIN COVID RELIEF ACTIVITIES

                                        RECITALS

A. The Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
   Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.;

B. The Copperton Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body for
   Copperton pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501;

C. Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17: COUNTIES;

D. The CARES Act is a law passed by the Federal Government that provided for an appropriation
   of funds to the States and certain Counties for certain governmental emergency costs and
   economic relief efforts related to the COVID-19 pandemic;

E. In May 2020, the State of Utah and Salt Lake County received their CARES Act appropriations
   from the Federal Government;

F. In late May 2020, the County, taking a cue from the State regarding the management of CARES
   Act funds, and not compelled by the CARES Act to do so, decided it would send amounts of
   their appropriation to the municipalities of the County to fund and manage local COVID-19
   efforts;

G. Pursuant to the County’s desire to promote local engagement in the COVID-19 relief effort,
   the Council and Copperton’s legal counsel have received and reviewed the attached agreement
   for consideration and possible approval of Copperton; and

H. In addition to providing funding to municipalities, the County will retain a portion of the
   CARES Act funding it has received to operate a program that will assist businesses affected
   by COVID19, including businesses within Copperton.

                                      RESOLUTION

   THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Copperton Metro Township Council effective
immediately:

       1. The attached agreement authorizing Copperton to receive $23,364.68 of the County’s
          appropriation of the CARES Act funding is approved and Copperton assumes all of the

                                              1
   Federal regulatory requirements, reporting rules, advertising costs, and possible future
   liabilities of the use of these funds as set forth in the Agreement;

2. The Mayor and Council will coordinate with the County to educate Copperton
   businesses regarding the County’s CARES Act program and will assist businesses, as
   needed, in applying for assistance from the County;

3. All requested expenditures of CARES Act funding that Copperton receives directly
   shall require approval of the Council prior to the distribution of the funds; and

4. The Mayor and Council will expend the CARES Act funds that Copperton receives as
   follows:

       a. Reimbursement of Act-Qualified expenses incurred by the Unified Fire
          Authority and Unified Police Department as Copperton’s first-responder
          agencies;

       b. Reimbursement of Act-Qualified expenses incurred by the Greater Salt Lake
          Municipal Services District as Copperton’s municipal-type services provider;
          and

       c. Reimbursement of any other Act-Qualified expenses that Copperton incurs
          directly responding to the COVID-19 pandemic.
