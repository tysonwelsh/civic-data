BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council met in
a regular public meeting on May 17,2017.


                                         WITNESS
        A.     WHEREAS, the Jordan School District ("JSD") acquired the Bingham Cemetery
(the "Cemetery") pursuant to Utah statute on or about November, 1972 in connection with the
dissolution of Bingham Canyon City (the "City"); and

        B.    Contemporaneously with acquisition of the Cemetery and also resulting from the
dissolution of the City, JSD received an escrow fund to be used solely for the ongoing
maintenance and operation of the Cemetery (the "Escrow Account"); and

      C.      The Copperton Metro Township ("Copperton") desires to acqnire title to the
Cemetery and to operate the Cemetery for the benefit of the community; and

       D.     As a Metro Township, Copperton is unable to acquire the Escrow Account, which
must be held by the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District ("MSD"); and

       E.    JSD desires to transfer the Cemetery to Copperton and to transfer the funds from
the Escrow Account to the MSD for use by Copperton for Cemetery purposes; and

      F.      Copperton has adopted Ordinance #2017-05 authorizing the creation of a trustee
form of management, consisting of three Copperton residents, to operate and manage the
Cemetery, subject to the supervision of the Copperton Metro Township Council (the "Council");
and

       G.      The MSD has agreed to receive the funds from the Escrow Account and place
them in a special revenue fund reserved solely for the purposes of reimbursing Copperton for
the costs and expenses it incurs in operating and maintaining the Cemetery and/or of paying
those costs directly, with the Council's approval; and
