COMMISSIONER AINGE - OUR EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH IS LORI Barber (Read
Nomination)

Lori Barber may just be the ultimate ‘perfect’ employee. She is responsible for approximately
50% of the staff at Utah County Health Department. In her role as Division Director of Family &
Personal Health Lori is motivated, dedicated, dependable, and committed. These qualities are
necessary when it comes to overseeing diverse organizations such as the immunization clinic, the
wellness clinic, the school nursing program, the epidemiology program, the home visitation
programs, and the tuberculosis clinic.

Lori began at the Health department back in 1985. She began her health department career as a
school nurse then moved to Bureau Director of Immunizations and then went on to be the Division
Director for Family & Personal Health.

In 1996, Lori left the health department and pursued other nursing channels and they have
provided a comprehensive and impressive background. Other extraordinary positions Lori has held
outside the health department have been Legal Nurse Consultant, Professor of Nursing at UVU,
Assistant Dean and Associate Department Chair at UVU, Associate Professor of Nursing and
Director of Clinical Resources for Roseman University of Health Sciences, Associate Nursing
Program Director at Provo College, and a RN Care Manager at IHC.

We are very happy that Lori returned to the health department in 2018 as Division Director of
Family and Personal Health.

“We are very fortunate to have Lori here at the Health Department. She has brought with her
not only a wealth of knowledge, but also a lot of energy and enthusiasm. She works diligently in
closing any gaps we have in policies or the manner in which our nursing services are provided. She
takes on challenges head-on and seeks for innovative methods to solve these issues. During this
time of the pandemic we have had many challenges within the department, many of which affect
the nursing division and Lori has looked for ways to tackle all of these challenges in a way that will
protect and promote the health of our citizens.

l appreciate so very much the talents she shares and the leadership she brings to our Nursing
Division.” Ralph Clegg

“Lori is the glue that sticks us all together. Without Lori during this Pandemic I really think
situations might not have gone as well as she has done. Lori is here every night till at least 8 pm
working on projects to just keep us afloat and never complains. Lori has put her whole heart and
soul into this job.

Lori is always there to support each of our programs and if something goes wrong or is

positions.

Lori is firm, will stand up for what she believes and follows all the rules, so we are all in
