CUP2021-000321 – Jose’ Rodriquez is requesting approval to modify his original Conditional Use permit
to allow a 3,200 square foot carport to be placed on the used car lot site. Acreage: approximately 1.35 acres.
Location: 2646 South 7200 West. Zone: C-3/zc (Commercial with zoning conditions). Planner: Diana
Martinez (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Richards asked if the type of carport would be temporary or permanent. Ms. Martinez said
permanent. Used car lot will be asphalted. Commissioner Richards asked if fence would remain. Ms.
Martinez said nothing in concerns with the actual approval of the car lot will be changed. Commissioner
Cripps confirmed the carport is in the northwest corner. Ms. Martinez said will be using a screening fence
and front part was dedicated of nine feet. Commissioner Richards said along the frontage is a lot of cars to
pick up the kids. Ms. Martinez said that is the fenced in area 25 feet deep. Commissioner VanRoosendaal
asked if confirmed no electricity wired to the carport and purpose. Commissioner Cripps said we make sure
we put restrictions to activities and what can be done, maintain third condition no auto repair or
dismantling in that area. Commissioner Taylor said in wintertime for equipment, they need power to plug
them in. Commissioner Weight said already has zoning conditions we are worried about and doubling up
to staff recommendations. Ms. Martinez said is putting on displayed area.
