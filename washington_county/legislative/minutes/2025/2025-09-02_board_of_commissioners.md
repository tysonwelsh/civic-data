WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
SEPTEMBER 2, 2025

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on September 2, 2025, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance
were Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin
Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

Commissioner Victor Iverson was excused.

Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan stated recently a new ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) Software
was purchased by the Clerk/Auditor’s Office which included a budget module. A newer module
is now available and Oracle is offering that new software at no cost to the County.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3584, A
Resolution Ratifying the Approval of an Ordering Document Securing the
County a No-Cost Upgrade to the County’s Enterprise Resource Planning
Software by Oracle. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3585, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN ADJUSTMENT OF 2025 PROPERTY TAXES FOR CERTAIN PARCELS OWNED
BY RURAL HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION DBA SELF-HELP HOMES

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated this item was previously approved, however, Exhibit
A had dated tax information and this Resolution will update that tax information.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3585,
A Resolution Approving an Adjustment of 2025 Property Taxes for Certain
Parcels Owned by Rural Housing Development Corporation DBA Self-Help
Homes. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3586, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND HORROCKS LLC FOR
THE TOPAZ WASH FEASIBILITY STUDY

Public Works Director Todd Edwards explained this is a feasibility study for a probable retention
basin in Diamond Valley to handle stormwater solutions.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3586, A
Resolution Approving an Agreement Between Washington County and
Horrocks LLC for the Topaz Wash Feasibility Study. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. _R-2025-3587, A RESOLUTION _IN
OPPOSITION TO ZION NATIONAL _PARK’S PROPOSED VISITOR USE

MANAGEMENT PLAN

Regional Tourism Regulatory Affairs Director Heath Hansen stated Zion National Park has been
involved in a Visitor Use Management Process since 2017, which Washington County and the
State of Utah have opposed regarding restrictive reservation systems. This Resolution would
affirm Washington County’s opposition to the use of a reservation system for Zion National Park.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3587, A
Resolution in Opposition to Zion National Park’s Proposed Visitor Use
Management Plan. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3588, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE EXECUTION OF A FEDERAL AID AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE UTAH
DEPARTMENT_OF_ TRANSPORTATION AND WASHINGTON COUNTY FOR A
FEASIBILITY STUDY OF THE VIRGIN RIVER PATH FROM SUNRISE VALLEY
PARK TO GRANDPA’S POND PROJECT

Greater Zion Destination Development Manager Leslie Fonger explained these funds were
awarded through the Metropolitan Planning Organization for a feasibility study of the Virgin River
multi-use path from Sunrise Valley Park in Washington City to Grandpa’s Pond in Hurricane City.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve Resolution No. R-2025-3588,
A Resolution Approving the Execution of a Federal Aid Agreement Between
the Utah Department of Transportation and Washington County for a
Feasibility Study of the Virgin River Path from Sunrise Valley Park to
Grandpa’s Pond Project. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow and Almquist voting

aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:36 p.m.
