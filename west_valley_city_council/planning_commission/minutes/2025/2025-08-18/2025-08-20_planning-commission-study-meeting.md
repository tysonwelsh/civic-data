    MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING
                             AUGUST 20, 2025

THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON
WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 20, 2025 AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600
CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED
TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY VICE CHAIR MARTELL WINTERS.
THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:
     Martell Winters, Vice Chair
     Harold Woodruff, Commissioner
     Darrick Porter, Commissioner
     Nancy Drozdek, Commissioner
     Pauline Matagi, Commissioner

PLANNING DIVISION STAFF PRESENT:
     Steve Pastorik, Community Development Director
     Steve Lehman, P&Z Current Planning Manager
     Jody Knapp, Zoning Administrator
     Brock Anderson, P&Z Senior Planner
     Julie Davis, P&Z Administrative Assistant

LEGAL DEPARTMENT PRESENT:
     Brandon Hill, Assistant City Attorney

PRESENTATIONS:

   Z-6-2025 Petition by Scott Boettcher requesting a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot
   size ½ acre) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 7005 West Gates Avenue on 0.71
   acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).

   Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented a zone change for a property on Gates Avenue, an area that has
   been transitioning from residential to industrial uses. The applicant plans to build a 6,000 square foot
   metal welding shop with an outdoor storage yard for materials and equipment. Staff is considering
   implementing a Development Agreement with requirements similar to previous rezones in the area
   which would include installing curb, gutter, and sidewalk; enhancing the building's appearance;
   limiting hours of operation; and restricting certain uses due to nearby residential homes. The
   discussion then led to a City-owned strip of land that provides access to a cell tower, with concerns
   about maintenance and potential changes. Steve explained that the land is not a public right-of-way,
   but a City-owned parcel that requires ongoing maintenance, which has been neglected. The City
   considered transferring ownership of the strip but determined it wouldn't be beneficial due to
   existing easements for the cell tower access and Magna Water. Commissioner Woodruff asked if a
   cul-de-sac could be installed at the end of Gates Avenue and Steve answered that it would be
   difficult to require since the property isn't being subdivided.


   SA-4-2025 West Valley City is requesting minor subdivision approval for the West Valley
   Promenade Subdivision. The subdivision will also amend and extend lots 34-44 and a portion of lot
   45 of the Lehman Subdivision. The subdivision is located at 2875 West Lehman Avenue and will
   consist of 1 lot on 2.5 acres. The property within the subdivision boundary is zoned CC (City Center
   Zone) and R-2-8 zone. (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov).

   Discussion: Steve Lehman presented a request from the City to transfer RDA properties into City
   ownership through a subdivision plat. The properties are part of the Lehman Subdivision and the
   Fairbourne Station Promenade, which are already developed. The plan involves consolidating
   multiple platted lots into one lot and vacating a 5-foot public utility easement along the back of the
   lots to simplify management.

   SA-5-2025 West Valley Land Holdings, LLC is requesting minor subdivision approval for the
   Wasatch West Valley Retail Subdivision. The proposed subdivision will also amend lots 2 and 3 of
   the West Valley Land Holding Subdivision. The subdivision is located at 3750 South 2700 West,
   consists of 3 lots and 1 parcel and is zoned CC. (Staff-Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
   steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

   Discussion: Steve Lehman presented a minor subdivision request from West Valley Land Holdings
   for property adjacent to City Hall, which will create three lots for future development and dedicate a
   sliver of land for right-of-way along 2700 South. Wasatch Group owns these properties and has
   potential users for Lot 2, while Lots 1 and 3 are being created for future marketing purposes.


There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting was
adjourned at 4:00 PM.
