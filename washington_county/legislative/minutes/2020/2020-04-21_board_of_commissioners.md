CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE NO. 2020-1155-O, AN ORDINANCE
REZONING A_ CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY LOCATED
AT 1383 WEST DIAMOND VALLEY DRIVE IN DIAMOND VALLEY FROM RE-
40 (RESIDENTIAL ESTATE 40,000 SQUARE FOOT MINIMUM LOT SIZE)
ZONE _ TO THE RE-20 (RESIDENTIAL ESTATE 20,000 SQUARE FOOT
MINIMUM _ LOT SIZE) ZONE, HEREINAFTER FULLY DESCRIBED IN THIS
ORDINANCE

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that a private property owner is
requesting a three (3) lot subdivision. There is an existing single dwelling on lot 83, which
is approximately one (1) acre in size. The proposed amended and extended subdivision will
divide parcel 7236-A-6-DA into two (2) flag lots. County code permits two (2) flag lots on
one flag stem system.

Mr. Messel further explained that the public hearing was held on April 14, 2020 at the
regularly scheduled Planning Commission meeting and was unanimously recommended
by the Planning Commission.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Ordinance No. 2020-1155-O,
An Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County
Located at 1383 West Diamond Valley Drive in Diamond Valley from
RE-40 (Residential Estate 40,000 Square Foot Minimum Lot Size) Zone
to the RE-20 (Residential Estate 20,000 Square Foot Minimum Lot
Size) Zone. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2592, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING THE EXECUTION OF AN AGREEMENT _BETWEEN
WASHINGTON COUNTY AND THE CITY OF WASHINGTON REGARDING
TRANSFERRING OWNERSHIP OF A PARCEL OF REAL PROPERTY AND
APPROVING THE EXECUTION OF A QUIT CLAIM DEED TRANSFERRING
OWNERSHIP OF THE PARCEL

Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen explained that this small parcel of property located in the Indian
Oaks subdivision of Washington City was acquired in a tax sale. Washington City
maintains the roadway within and abutting the property and is requesting that the County
transfer the property, as it is only useful as a public right-of-way.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2592, A
Resolution Approving the Execution of an Agreement Between
Washington County and the City of Washington Regarding

Parcel. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2593, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING TOURISM ADVISORY BOARD RECOMMENDATIONS

Deputy Tourism Director Brittany McMichael explained that the Tourism Advisory Board
(TAB) receives applications for events and marketing opportunities to assist with

promoting and establishing tourism and recreation in the area. The TAB is recommending
funding for the following non-profit organizations:

$4,000 to the Utah Museums Association for its annual conference in September; $11,000

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2596, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE DAMMERON VALLEY SPECIAL SERVICE
DISTRICT ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD

Commissioner Cox explained that the makeup of the Dammeron Valley Fire Speical
Service District’s Administrative Control Board was modified so that each member shall
be appointed by the Washington County Commission rather than through the election
process.

It is recommended for Brant Smith and Ryan Anderson to serve terms to expire December
31, 2021; and Rick Johnson, Barbara Hjelle, and Mack Sorensen to serve terms to expire
December 31, 2023.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2596, A
Resolution Appointing Members to the Dammeron Valley Special
Service District Administrative Control Board as Outlined. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2597, A RESOLUTION
ADOPTING THE _WASHINGTON COUNTY _AMENDED _ RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN

Acting Chairman Almquist explained that many parties were involved in creating the
County’s Resource Management Plan (RMP). The County continues to revise and update
the RMP to ensure that the information for each section is accurate.

Commission Cox read aloud Resolution No. R-2020-2597.

Commissioner Iverson stated that it has been important for the plan to be reviewed and
updated periodically in order for it to remain current and also thanked the staff and
participating agencies for their efforts in continuing to do so.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2597, A
Resolution Adopting the Washington County Amended Resource
Management Plan. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist,
and Cox voting aye.
