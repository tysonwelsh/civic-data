In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who appear
and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The
County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. AGREEMENT WITH CACHE VALLEY BANK ALLOWING FOR TEMPORARY WEBER CENTER PARKING ON BANK PROPERTY
         Scott Mendoza, of County Community Development, said that this contract is related to the imminent parking
         structure demolition project. It allows county employees and Weber Center tenants to use about 70 parking spaces
         across the street at the Bank’s property through March 31, 2021. The bank is not requesting any remuneration
         only that the county provide regular minor routine maintenance. Commissioner Harvey would like to leave that
         area in even better condition; Commissioner Jenkins agreed noting that the Bank has been very accommodating.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the agreement with Cache Valley Bank allowing for temporary Weber
         Center parking on Bank property; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    2. FINAL APPROVAL OF PARKSIDE PRUD PHASE 2A, CONSISTING OF 12 LOTS
         Steve Burton, of the County Planning Division, stated that this is part of the Wolf Creek development in the
         Bridges PRUD. The Ogden Valley Planning Commission and County Planning staff recommended approval.
         This item includes approval of a subdivision improvement agreement for $31,146.00.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to grant final approval of Parkside PRUD Phase 2A, consisting of 12 lots;
         Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    3. CONTRACT WITH EPIC ENGINEERING, P.C., FOR SWPPP INSPECTION SERVICES ENSURING COMPLIANCE TO
         THE STATE REGULATIONS

         Ashley Thoman, of County Engineering, stated that this contract is for inspection services to meet State
         regulations for erosion prevention. Proposals were received and Epic Engineering was selected. These
         inspections are funded through the permit fees collected. He addressed Commissioner Jenkins’ question stating
         that it would require additional manpower for staff to perform these services and the engineering firm has the
         expertise and efficiency in performing these services.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Epic Engineering, P.C., for SWPPP Inspection
         Services ensuring compliance to the state regulations; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye

H. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS: Commissioner Jenkins said that Melissa Freigang, Prosperity Center of Excellence Director, had
just emailed him stating that she is working with the State for teams to go directly to shelters to help people with taxes, to get stimulus
checks to those eligible, and that they are working with Youth Futures and the street outreach to deliver mail; they are making good
strides. Chair Froerer noted the need to be cognizant of water issues now that we’re moving into hot temperatures after a couple of
dry springs. Commissioner Jenkins agreed and referred to Sevier County’s plight because they over allocated the aquifer water, which
can cause serious issues. The 50-year old Weber Center parking structure had been slated for demolition for some time and the issues
are now more serious after the recent earthquake. It has been closed since then, RFPs were issued and a firm has been selected.
