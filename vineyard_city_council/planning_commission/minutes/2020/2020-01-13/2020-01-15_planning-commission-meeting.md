37   5. BUSINESS ITEMS:
38       5.1 Mill Point Site Plan Amendment
39           Mr. Brim introduced the project. The applicant, Ralph Moffat, requested approval of a
40           site plan amendment to their initial landscape and trails plan, which was approved by
41           the Planning Commission. The site is located on the west side of Mill Road and south of
42           the Utah Valley University soccer fields. This development is within the Regional Mixed
43           Use (RMU). Their proposal will not affect any of their buildings. The amendments are
44           specifically in regards to the trail and landscaping bordering 400 North. They propose to
45           remove the trail and just have the sidewalk (which has been installed at this point). The
46           sidewalk is six feet; the proposed trail was ten feet. The code does not require a trail.
47           They did curve the sidewalk they installed so that it does have more of a trail feel to it.
48           There is a powerbox from Rocky Mountain Power that impeded the trail. There is a
49           slope that comes down from the sidewalk to the parking garages. David Church, City
50           Attorney, told Mr. Brim that the city could only require what is in the code, even if the
51           applicant proposed something and then decides to change it.
52
53            Commissioner Jenkins clarified that it is a site plan amendment, so even though it had
54            already been installed, it was not approved by the city or commission.
55
56            Mr. Brim showed the proposed landscaping plan. The amendment does not propose to
57            lose any open space, just to move more of the landscaping closer to the road to be
58            more visible. Ms. Hart also indicated that it is conforming to landscaping code. 400 N is
59            a private road, so they don't have to conform to the Vineyard Tree Manual, but their
60            species do comply with the manual anyway. The trail was supposed to connect to the
61            trail by the railroad, so we should preserve that connection. Staff recommended
62            approval.
63
64            John Moffat, applicant, stated their intention is to continue the path. The green
65            powerbox sits on top of a vault that is eight feet wide by thirteen feet long. There are
66            three of those power boxes along the north of the property. It is the backbone of UVU
67            and was not anticipated by the applicant. By the time you have the eight-foot vault and
68            then the six-foot sidewalk, it only leaves about three feet for the plantings. In an ideal
69            world, the site plan would be the delivery, but there is a significant elevation drop. The
70            applicant tried to maintain the visibility of the plants by moving them up closer to the
71            road. He then asked for questions.
72
73            Chair Jenkins asked to clarify if they had already built what the proposed changes were.
74
75            Mr. Moffat stated he was correct.

130           Vineyard to get ahead of the curb to make sure that what is changed will benefit the
