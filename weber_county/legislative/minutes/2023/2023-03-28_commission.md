G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. APPROVE AN AMENDMENT TO THE CONTINENTAL CRA INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT.
            Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): In 2022, we created an Economic Development Finance
            Committee to administer our two financial incentives of Tax Increment Finance and Public Infrastructure
            Districts. The committee tracks performance of the County’s participation in other CRAs, and other
            incentives as well as record our participation from unincorporated areas. In September 2022, the Legislative
            Auditor General published a performance audit of the Tax Increment Finance in Utah. There was legislation
            in the past session that did not pass but this is an issue we will continue to hear more about. Some of the
            recommendations in the proposed legislation included guidance on managing unexpended TIF funds once a
            collection period expires. They also looked at requiring local governments to make financial information such
            as receipts, expenditures, accounts balances, and fund transfers publicly available for each project area. It also
         recommended requiring local governments conduct a robust justification study called a “But For” analysis that
         justifies the use of Tax Increment Finance. Taking those recommendations into consideration, our Economic
         Finance Committee has been working diligently on policies and procedures for our two incentives we
         administer and track. As a result, we have created a process that takes our participation with any developer or
         municipalities through a rigorous process to make sure the projects the county participates in funded tax payer
         finances will provide public benefits and long term economic sustainability and growth in the area.
         The City of Ogden is asking for an extension of the Continental CRA established in 2019 for beginning their
         Wonder Block project. The TIF funding for this project will be used for infrastructure purposes to prepare the
         site and development purposes for project going on that site. Ogden is asking for an extension of the current
         agreement. We propose the following: for the tax increment period of 28 years the agency, Ogden City, shall
         collect from Weber County 90% of the county’s tax increment generated from the county’s local levy within
         the project area for the first 22 years of the tax increment period and 75% for the remaining 6 years. The
         County consents to the agency’s use for administration purposes 10% of the county’s tax increment for the
         first 5 years and 5% thereafter. The Economic Development Finance Committee recommends this extension
         of the interlocal agreement and the support of the Continental CRA.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve an amendment to the Continental CRA Inter-local Agreement;
         Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
