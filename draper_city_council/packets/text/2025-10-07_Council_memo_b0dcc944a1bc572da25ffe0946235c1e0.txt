MEMO
To: City Council
From: Scott Cooley
Date: 2025-10-07
Re: Ordinance #1680 - Solid Waste Text Amendment


Comments:

Draper City provides solid waste and recyclable material collection services (“Collection
Services”) to promote public health, safety, and welfare. Providing such services on
private streets increases liability and maintenance costs due to the city’s lack of control
over private road conditions.

Draper City seeks to allocate limited resources efficiently by amending Draper City Code
Section 7-11-10 (Definitions) and Section 7-11-030 to clarify the City will not directly
provide Collection Services to residences on private streets but will contract with a
qualified third-party provider to provide Collection Services to residences currently
serviced by the City.

The proposed amendments would allow residents on private streets currently serviced by
the City to either use a city-contracted third-party provider or contract independently for
solid waste and recyclable material collection services.
