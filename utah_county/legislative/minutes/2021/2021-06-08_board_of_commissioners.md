Funding Proposal Moving Forward:

Simplify the funding formula & improve accounting accuracy of overall ®

costs for the School Nurse Program to determine fair share of costs.

Accomplish this by:

* Divide billing codes into separate categories by each separate District

* School Nurses bill non-school health nurse efforts to other billing codes
(i.e.,flu).

* Find more accurate measure of administrative costs for billing

* After several months of refined tracking, utilize updated data to better
plenior the Utah County budget beginning January of 2022 (Calendar
Year).

* Allow 1 full year of tracking to assist the School Districts in budgetary
preparation for the fiscal cycle beginning July of 2022

* Credit either partner for budgetary corrections (i.e., savings due to
attrition)

Draft Timeline:

June Set billing codes for accurate accounting with Utah County Clerk/Auditors office.

July Keep current contract levels for July 1, 2021 + 7% and new positions School districts requesting. School
Health Nurses begin billing new codes by district/county efforts, etc.

October Analyze collected billing data to assist with preparing the Jan 1 County budget. Share data with both
County and Districts.

January Utah County SNP budget begins,

Feb - March Utilize shared finance to negotiate District Contribution for their upcoming budgetary cycle.

April - May Meet and finalize contracts.

duly 1, 2022 Separate District SNP budget begins.
