                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 23-84

A RESOLUTION APPROVING COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT NO. 2 BETWEEN THE
 UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND RIVERTON CITY FOR THE
RELOCATION OF A PEDESTRIAN BRIDGE IN CONJUNCTION WITH IMPROVING
 AN INTERSECTION AT THE LOCATION OF BANGERTER HIGHWAY AND 13400
                      SOUTH, RIVERTON UTAH

       WHEREAS, The State of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) is commencing
with construction to improve an intersection located at 13400 South and Bangerter Highway
(hereinafter referred to as the “Intersection Project”); and

       WHEREAS, The Intersection Project affects a broad variety of municipal facilities, such
as roads, pedestrian facilities, utility lines, and park space; and

        WHEREAS, as UDOT proceeds with the Intersection Project, the parties need to
establish by agreement the process by which UDOT’s construction will be reviewed and
coordinated with Riverton City officials; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Council finds that the enclosed Cooperative Agreement
establishes an arrangement by which UDOT will donate a bridge to Riverton City, which
presently spans Bangerter Highway at approximately 13400 South; and

        WHEREAS, a Cooperative Agreement has been prepared for approval and execution by
and between UDOT and Riverton City, which states the purposes thereof, and the extent of the
required participation of the parties and the rights, duties, responsibilities, and obligations of the
parties described in this Resolution.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.      The Riverton City Council hereby approves the Cooperative Agreement with UDOT,
               attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.
       2.      The Mayor is authorized to sign the approved Cooperative Agreement with UDOT in
               substantially similar form to that provided in Exhibit “A,” once the Cooperative
               Agreement is approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.
       3.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
