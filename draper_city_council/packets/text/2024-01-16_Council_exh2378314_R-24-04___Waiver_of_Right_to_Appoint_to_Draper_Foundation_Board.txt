                           RESOLUTION NO. 24-04

A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPROVING A WAIVER
AND CONSENT TO REMOVAL OF RI GHTS TO APPOINT BOARD
MEMBERS TO THE DRAPER COMMUNI TY FOUNDATION, I NC.

      WHEREAS, Draper Community Foundation, Inc. (the “Corporation”) a
Utah nonprofit corporation, is organized and operated for charitable and
educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal
Revenue Code of 1986; and

       WHEREAS, the Corporation is in the process of amending and
restating its Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, and other charter documents,
and is generally reorganizing its Board of Trustees and Officers; and

       WHEREAS, Article VI of the Corporation’s Bylaws, presently grants Draper
City (“Draper”) the right to appoint two (2) members of Draper’s City Council as
voting board members of the Corporation’s Board of Trustees (the
“Appointment Right”); and

       WHEREAS, the Corporation desires to remove Draper’s Appointment
Right from its Bylaws and desires Draper to waive the Appointment Right and
consent to the removal of the Appointment Right; and

       WHEREAS, this waiver and consent agreement reflects the relationship
between Draper and the Corporation and will be beneficial to the health,
safety and welfare of Draper City’s businesses and residents; and

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

     Section 1. Approval of Agreement. The Waiver and Consent to
Removal of Rights to Appoint Board Members of the Draper Community
Foundation, Inc., attached hereto as Exhibit 1, is hereby approved and the
Mayor is directed to sign the same.

      Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this
Resolution is held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or
unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and all
sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, ON THE 16TH DAY OF JANUARY 2024.
