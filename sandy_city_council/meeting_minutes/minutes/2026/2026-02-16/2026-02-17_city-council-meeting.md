General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                                  Council Chair Cyndi Sharkey invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                                  Sandra Haak suggested rotating the council chair position to ensure that each member
                                  had a chance to serve as the council chair.

                                  Jacob Shamy spoke about living in Sandy. It is where he got his first job, first home, first
                                  speeding ticket. Sandy is very dear to him. He spoke about the proposed closure of
                                  Parklane Elementary School. He is opposed to the school closure and thinks it will
                                  weaken the community. He also cited lack of transparency from the Canyons School
                                  District.

                                  Scott Cowdell spoke about the Kane Creek development and the impact of SB258 which
                                  allows landowners to incorporate a preliminary municipality for the purpose of developing
                                  land. The State has chipped away at your ability to govern. He also spoke about another
                                  development in Echo Canyon. He was opposed to this.

                                  Brayden Miller spoke about fence issue in the New Castle neighborhood and reviewed the
                                  budget allocation for this project from the FY2026 budget. Two sections of the fence have
                                  fallen down. Children and residents walk in this area every day. We want the fence
                                  replaced.

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Council moved to Item 3 on the Agenda.
                                  Council Member Aaron Deleyzer was excused from the meeting at 7:04 pm.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Marci Houseman thanked the community for supporting the Sandy Arts
                      Guild production. She joined Representative Okerlund on the Utah House Floor and
                      thanked him for the opportunity.

                      Council Member Brooke Christensen provided an update from the Historic Preservation
                      Committee. She gave an update on the committee's goal to educate the community on
                      historic preservation. She attended the SYC meeting last week and really enjoyed the
                      meeting at Fire Station 31.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey mentioned that the naming survey for the recreation
                      center went out. She attended the South Valley Chamber luncheon. She provided an
                      update from the Legislative Policy Committee. The Senate President and House Speaker
                      spoke to LPC at their last meeting.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud joined the Sandy Youth Council meeting with the Sandy
                      Fire Department. We had a great meeting with Fire. The SYC learned all about careers
                      and roles in the fire department, took a tour of the new facility, dressed in the gear and
                      used the equipment and fire hose. We had a great turnout. The Historic Committee is
                      planning on having an informational table at the Historic Sandy barbecue.



Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the naming survey that went out to residents. She invited
                      the Council to attend a Police Department swearing in ceremony this Thursday.

CAO Report

                      The Police Department are holding an Internet Safety Class on February 23rd. The
                      community is welcome to attend.

Recreation Center Construction Report

                      Jetta Marrott, Parks & Recreation, mentioned to the Council that the naming survey had
                      gone out to the residents. They will bring back the information to the Council when the
                      survey closes. She provided an update on the construction of the new facility.

Legislative Report

                      Ryan Mecham, Senior Policy Advisor, provided a legislative update to the Council on the
                      current session. He spoke about SB258 and the impact on municipalities. SB258 allows
                      landowners to incorporate a preliminary municipality, making land use decisions for the
                      purpose of developing land without an election. HB236 related to property taxes. SB97
                      related to required fund balances. Other bills he reviewed with the Council include HB238,
                      SB206, HB507 and HB48. He provided details and impact of the proposed bills to the
                      city, if the bills are passed by the legislature.

Adjournment
                      Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:47 pm.
