                                         PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 25:10)
Utah County Sheriff James O. Tracy explained there is currently a deficit of between $800,000 and $1,000,000 in the
jail medical budget, largely due to an incarcerated individual with extraordinary medical expenses. Sheriff Tracy
stated he has been requesting money or advice on how to proceed and received no response from the commissioners;
he is interpreting this as meaning the issue must be solved within the department’s existing budget. His
recommendation is a reduction of force by 15 or 20 deputies and use the saved money to pay bills. Sheriff Tracy also
considers the lack of communication from the Commission as a vote of “no confidence,” and therefore resigns
effective August 1, 2018. Sheriff Tracy noted he is also working on a system to evaluate 128 individuals for release
back into the community to eliminate those beds from the jail. Commissioner Ivie pointed out they are currently
working through the budgetary process to identify funds. Sheriff Tracy replied, “We’re out of time… I will move
forward with the internal solution(s) today.” He and Commissioner Lee briefly discussed the potential to reallocate
funds and plans to meet today to continue discussion.
