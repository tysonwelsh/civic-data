Example Motion
This item was noticed for public hearing and discussion only.

Background

Riverton City has contracted with a company, Tuvas, to update the City’s network connectivity
between municipal facilities. The primary locations are City Hall, the Public Works Building, and
the Water Shops. Underground fiber-optic connections have been installed to these facilities as
part of this project, providing both network connection between the facilities as well as
connection to the internet. Tuvas has proposed in their design that three eighty (80) foot towers
be installed at the primary locations with two purposes to the towers. The first is to provide a
backup wireless connection between facilities should the fiber-optic connection fail, and second
to provide wireless connection through receiver antennas to be installed at secondary locations
including the Main City Park and the C.R. Hamilton Sports Park. Final detail has not been
provided on the secondary location antenna size and placement. This hearing is to review the
Conditional Use Permit for the three proposed towers. Public notices were sent to the
surrounding notification area for each site individually, but for the purposes of this meeting the
staff report covers all three towers.
Towers similar to these in height have generally in Riverton City been associated with mobile
communications (“cell towers”), which are regulated by Ordinance 18.175, Wireless
Communications Towers. However, as these towers do not provide wireless communication
services, they do not fall under that section of Riverton City’s Code. The towers are limited to
Riverton City network connection, and will not provide any residential or commercial services.
Therefore, the towers are being more generally reviewed through a Conditional Use Permit.

Technical Information
The towers are proposed to be located at three sites in the City, all of which are owned by
Riverton City. The sites are as follows, and as shown on the included maps:
         •       12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton City Hall Campus, directly west of the Sandra
                 Lloyd Community Center.
         •       12526 South 4150 West, Riverton City Public Works Building, adjacent to the north
                 side of the building.
         •       13680 South 3160 West, Riverton City Water Shops, adjacent to the east side of the
                 building.
The Public Works and Water Shop sites have secured fencing and gates surrounding the
property. The Riverton City Hall campus site does not.
The towers are each eighty (80) feet in height, and are proposed as lattice
structures, consisting of a braced metal framework, as shown in Figure 1.
The engineering provided with the towers show that given the height and
proposed load, “guy wires”, or cables extending at angles from the towers to
stabilize the tower, are not required. Panels would be placed on the bottom
sections of the towers to prevent climbing. The antenna equipment mounted
on the towers is shown in the included information and will consist of
relatively small dish-type antennas. The towers will be linked by line-of-sight
wireless connection.

Tuvas has provided the City with general information about the proposed
location of the towers and the towers themselves. The height is specific at
eighty (80) feet. Staff has included in the material below the information
provided by Tuvas for each location. To help visually convey the height of
the tower, staff has also prepared a photo of the City Hall location, with the
tower height approximated relative the height of the adjacent building.
Additional information will be presented at the public hearing.

                                                                  Tower Input Data

The main tower is a 3x free standing tower with an overall height of 80.00 ft above the ground line.
The base of the tower is set at an elevation of 0.00 ft above the ground line.
The face width of the tower is 1.50 ft at the top and 1.50 ft at the base.
This tower is designed using the TIA-222-G standard.
The following design criteria apply:
              Basic wind speed of 90 mph.
              Structure Class II.
              Exposure Category C.
              Topographic Category 1.
              Crest Height 0.00 ft.
              Nominal ice thickness of 0.5000 in.
              Ice thickness is considered to increase with height.
              Ice density of 56 pcf.
              A wind speed of 60 mph is used in combination with ice.
              Temperature drop of 50 °F.
              Deflections calculated using a wind speed of 60 mph.
              Weld together tower sections have flange connections..
              Connections use galvanized A325 bolts, nuts and locking devices. Installation per TIA/EIA-222 and AISC
              Specifications..
              Tower members are ''hot dipped'' galvanized in accordance with ASTM A123 and ASTM A153 Standards..
              Welds are fabricated with ER-70S-6 electrodes..
              A non-linear (P-delta) analysis was used.
              Pressures are calculated at each section.
              Stress ratio used in tower member design is 1.
              Local bending stresses due to climbing loads, feed line supports, and appurtenance mounts are not considered.
