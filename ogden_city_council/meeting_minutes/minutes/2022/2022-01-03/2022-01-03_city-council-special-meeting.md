Comments from outgoing Council member Doug Stephens

Chair Blair introduced a video tribute to Council member Stephens, who was participating in his last meeting as a City
Council member after having served for 16 years.

Council member Stephens wished everyone a very happy New Year and stated he believes that there are many great
things to come in 2022. He began a very exciting journey 16 years ago and he has very strong emotions about the leaving the City
Council. He recited a quote from President John F. Kennedy, “ask not what your country can do for you, but what you can do for
your country” and he implored the City Council to consider what they can do for Ogden. He referenced a media article discussing
his decision to not seek re-election to the City Council; when being interviewed he indicated he had no regrets associated with his
time as a Council member, but that is not entirely true. He does regret that he will no longer be able to associate with so many,
including the City Council’s staff and his peers on the Council. He regrets that there is still some unfinished business, but he has
greatly enjoyed this journey. Ogden is his hometown, and he has a great love for it. He is proud of the accomplishments of past
and present City Councils but indicated there is still much work to be done in Ogden City and he looks forward to being a witness
to great things to come. When he first became a member of the City Council, the City’s slogan was “Ogden, its all within reach”;
the same is still true today. He thanked his family, especially his wife, for their support over the years. He thanked City
Administration as well and spoke to the quality of City employees. He concluded that the great things that have been done in
Ogden are not due to his efforts, but to the collective efforts of all Council members, Mayors, and City employees working
together over the years. He thanked everyone for the memories, love, and support over the past 16 years and indicated he will
continue to be involved in the community that he loves.

Chair Blair thanked Council member Stephens for his comments and for the service he has provided to the community.
He invited Council member Stephens to step down from the dais and sit with his wife as a member of the audience.

Minutes of Special Meeting of Council of Ogden City, Utah, January 3, 2022

Comments from newly elected Council members

Vice Chair White first commented on Council member Stephens’ service as a member of the City Council; she cited
some of the actions Council member Stephens has taken over the years but wished him well and noted she hopes he will enjoy his
time with his family.

Council member Richey stated that he has known Council member Stephens for years and is aware that he ‘has big shoes
to fill’ by stepping into this role. He looks forward to working with his fellow Council members and serving the community. He is
grateful for this opportunity and for the support he has received from his family, the community, and his fellow Council members.

Comments from other Council members

Council member Hyer stated the Council gets to witness the oath of office administered to newly elected members of the
Council every two years; it is a sacred oath, and he appreciates the opportunity to witness it. He commented on his years of service
and friendship with Council member Stephens and stated he will miss him dearly. He looks forward to serving with Council
member Richey and expressed his appreciation for his willingness to run for the office of City Council member.

Council member Nadolski also indicated he will miss Council member Stephens; his heart is pure and is always in the
right place. He also looks forward to working with Council member Richey and also congratulated Chair Blair, Vice Chair White,
and Council member Choberka on their re-election. There are some difficult issues on the horizon that the Council will need to
tackle, but he believes everyone who will participate in those decisions is a good person and truly has a desire to make Ogden
better.

Council member Lopez echoed the comments that have been made about Council member Stephens; he thanked him for
always being very kind and being willing to help anyone in need. He has already met with Council member Richey and noted that
he believes the newest people are very important; he thanked him for serving and offered his support and willingness to help him
be successful.

Closing remarks from Council Chair Bart Blair

Chair Blair stated that years ago when a fellow member of the Council was leaving their position, another Council
member had a super doll action figure made for them. He wishes he could have found an action figure to represent Council
member Stephens because he truly does think he has been powerful as a Council member. He stated that the Council may be
losing Council member Stephens as a fellow member, but they are not losing him as a mentor and a friend; he anticipates Council
member Stephens will still be available to any member of the Council when needed. He added Council member Stephens is a
tremendous painter and he is excited that he will have time to pursue that passion. He then stated that he looks forward to working
with Council member Richey and stated he strongly believes he will do great things. He is excited for the experience he brings to
this position and knows that he comes from a great family. He stated one important piece of advice he has received in the past that
he will now pass on to Council member Richey relates to the importance of being willing to seek out information from others
about important issues in the community.

Mayor Caldwell added that he will also miss Council member Stephens. Additionally, he congratulated Council member
Richey and indicated he looks forward to working with him. He hopes that a new tone will be set in Ogden City in 2022 and he
looks forward to working closely with the City Council to find common ground on important community issues.

Council member Lopez acknowledged Weber State University President Mortensen’s attendance this evening.
