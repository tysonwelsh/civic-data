Fee Schedule for Municipal Services District Services

              Mayor Clayton discussed the fee schedule, particularly the fees for developers.
A developer used to pay one fee and get any number of iterations of the plan as he wanted. That
has changed to only two or three revisions. The Council will table this matter. He asked Council
Members to review the fees and send any questions to Curtis Woodward, Salt Lake County
Planning & Development Services Division.

               Nathan Bracken recommended that the fees stay the same until the Council
adopts its own planning and zoning codes.

Single Member District

and sent to Council Members.

Council Members would run in single district at-large seats. Every district would cover the entire
town.

               Mayor Clayton asked for the Council’s opinion.

               Council Member Patrick stated he was indifferent about it.

               Council Member Bailey stated she was good with it.

               Council Member Severson stated he had some concerns about accountability.

               Mayor Clayton stated this does lead to some more transparency.

               Council Member Pazell stated he has spoken to the Salt Lake County Clerk and

         Annexation Committee

               Council Member Pazell stated he has met with the Annexation Committee a
couple of times. They came up with three proposed plans that are now under review by Nathan
Bracken. The goal would be to maintain the historical nature of Copperton while still being self-
sustaining.

                Nathan Bracken stated the Council must come up with something that works for
Rio Tinto. Rio Tinto would like to continue operating its business around Copperton and not be
subject to the whims of every new Council. The way to handle this is to have a policy that zoning
can only be changed by a percentage of the majority of the citizens living in that area.

              A representative from Rio Tinto stated the company pays taxes on the land it owns
in Copperton. He would like to be part of any discussion about annexations.

               Council Member Pazell stated once the legal aspect of the proposals is settled,
he would like to invite Rio Tinto and whoever else may want to come to the table.

               Mr. Bracken recommended a meeting be scheduled with Rio Tinto to hammer out
the details.

             Council Member Pazell will schedule an upcoming meeting with no more than
two Council Members, the annexation committee, and Rio Tinto. He discussed available dates
and times.

              Vern Winkler stated he has spoken to Curtis Woodward of Salt Lake County’s
Planning & Development Services Division. Mr. Woodward indicated that any policy set by this
Council can be changed by a future Council.

              Mr. Bracken stated the real issue comes up when someone wants a variance to
the zoning. Rio Tinto needs assurance that if it joins Copperton, it can continue to operate its
business.
