                                       NOTICE OF RECOUNT
Public notice is given that at 9:00 am on Thursday, December 14, 2023, the Salt Lake County Clerk's
Election Division will conduct a recount of the election results for the Bluffdale City Council At-Large
race. In conjunction with the recount, an audit will be conducted on a random sampling of 1% of the
voting machines and paper vote-by-mail ballots cast in that jurisdiction.

The recount will be conducted at the Salt Lake County Government Center, 2001 South State Street,
South Building, Room Sl-200, Salt Lake City, Utah. This recount is open to public observation.
