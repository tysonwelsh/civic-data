       B.     CONSIDER AMENDING CHAPTER 17-7.1.13.8 AND TABLE 17-7-1.2
              OF THE MIDVALE CITY MUNICIPAL CODE TO ADDRESS THE
              DUPLEX APPROVAL PROCESS
Adam Olsen said staff proposes an amendment to the above listed chapter and table of
the Municipal Code, permitting duplexes in the SF-1(DO) as an allowed use rather than
a conditional use. Duplex proposals currently go to the Planning Commission for
approval, through the conditional use process. Staff proposes moving them from
conditional use approval to staff level approval. All code requirements related to
placement, height, etc. will remain the same. This change will allow the Planning
Commission to focus on larger, city-wide planning issues; given that duplex approvals are
administrative in nature (if all the boxes are checked, they must be approved), while
expediting the approval process with staff level approval.
Public notice has been sent to affected entities as required in 17-3-9.8 of the Municipal
code. No comments have been received as of the writing of this report.
The Planning Commission reviewed the request at their October 12, 2022 meeting and
recommended approval to the City Council.

              CODE REGARDING WASTEWATER DISPOSAL
Glen Kennedy said the South Valley Water Reclamation Facility (SVWRF) recently
updated their rules and regs for its pre{reatment program. The last update was in 2011
and the City officially adopted those rules by resolution. SVWRF requested that the City
complete that process for the latest update. After some research it was found that two
chapters of code currently address wastewater regulations and the relationship with the
City and SVWRF. Chapters 13.08 and 8.09.
Chapter 8.09 has not been updated to reflect the current regulating agencies and any rule
updates that have occurred since 1967. The proposed amendments correct this and any
conflict with Chapter 13.08.
Wastewater Regulation Code Adjustments

Background
South Valley Water Reclamation Facility (SVWRF) recently updated their rules and regs
for its pre-treatment program and requested that we officially adopt them by resolution.
Two chapters of code currently address wastewater regulations and SVWRF. Chapters
13.08 and 8.09.

Current Code
   . Chapter '13.08 Sewer System - Details the relationship between the City and
      SVWRF, and "adopts the rules and regs that are formally approved by SVWRF's
      Board", which Midvale City is represented on.
   . Chapter 8.09 Wastewater Disposal - Contains three sections.
         . The first tvvo sections 8.09.010 and 8.09.020, in abbreviated terms, state
            that the City will follow the regulations adopted by the Utah Division of
            Health and the Utah State Committee on Water Pollution pertaining to
            "lndividual wastewater sewage disposal" (septic tank/systems) adopted in
            1965 and revised in 1967.
                . The current government agency enforcing the regulations for septic
                    systems is the County Health Department which are guided by the
                       EPA and State DEQ.
            .   The thilrd section 8.09.030, states that the City adopts and enacts the
                SVWRF rules and regs as revised and amended to April 1Sth, 1992.
                   . This probably should have been restructured with the
                       adoption/resolution associated with an update of these rules in 201 1.

Proposed Code Changes
   . Chapter 8.09 Wastewater Disposal -
            .   The first two sections 8.09.0'10 and 8.09.020, would be combined and read

8.09.010 - Adoption of septic system regulations.
Midvale City adopts all federal, state, and county laws and regulations applicable to septic
systems.
          . Section 8.09.030 would be changed to read as
