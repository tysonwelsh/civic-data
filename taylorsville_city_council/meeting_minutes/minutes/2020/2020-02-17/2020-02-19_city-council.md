        1.2      Mayor’s Report

      Mayor Kristie Overson spoke on the following topics:
         • She and Councilmember Christopherson had recently watched the SWAT team in
             action. She found it fascinating and expressed appreciation to Chief Wyant and
             his department for the work they did.
         • She mentioned the importance of scouting in the community and recognized
             Troop #91, which was the first-ever scout troop in Taylorville and was present at
             the meeting.
         • Taylorsville High School student Mohammed Jabbar, originally from Iraq, was
             recognized earlier in the day by the Taylorsville Exchange Club with the ACE
             Award (Accepting the Challenge of Excellence). Mohammed received the award
             for making a dramatic change in attitude and performance in school while also
             working ten-hour shifts at the airport.
         • Legislators had been very responsive to the City’s needs and were in
             communication with Taylorsville’s intergovernmental team throughout the
             legislative session
         • She recently joined Utah’s other female mayors in celebrating Utahn Seraph
             Young who, in 1870, became the first woman to cast a ballot in the modern
             United States.
         • She and members of the City Council had been given a tour of Momentum
             Recycling the previous day. The Mayor was very excited to have glass recycling
             in the City.
         • Fresh Market was being converted to a Macey’s grocery store and would open in
             the Spring.

7.      OTHER MATTERS

        7.1      Taylorsville Signage Discussion – Mayor Overson

 Mayor Overson reminded the Council that there had been several robust discussions regarding
gateway signage in Taylorsville, specifically 5400 South Redwood Road.            She had given
the matter a great deal of thought and had spent some time at the intersection in question. She
had come to believe that placing new tall and expensive signage there was not the best use of
taxpayer funds. Instead, she felt that perhaps the current walls could remain but that the
hardware and outdated logo could be removed and replaced with improved landscaping to
beautify the area.

Councilmember Christopherson still wished to have something specifically identifying the City
of Taylorsville at that location.
Taylorsville City Council Minutes

Council and staff discussed various possibilities and recommendations for 5400 South Redwood
Road which was the busiest intersection in the state.

Mayor Overson asked the Council to give the matter some thought. She also suggested keeping
the utility boxes in mind and possibly dressing them up in some way, perhaps with art. She liked
the idea of using the funds originally earmarked for that intersection for much-needed signage
elsewhere in the City.

The Council agreed that it no longer wished to spend a great deal of money on signage at 5400 S
and Redwood Road. Chair Harker asked the Mayor for a proposed timeline. Mayor Overson
and Mr. Taylor wished to have the project completed within the fiscal year. If the Council were
to give her a green light, Mayor Overson would return to the Council in a month with some
specific recommendations.

Councilmember Burgess recommended that any left-over funds allocated to signage be added to
the City Center. Councilmember Cochran agreed.
