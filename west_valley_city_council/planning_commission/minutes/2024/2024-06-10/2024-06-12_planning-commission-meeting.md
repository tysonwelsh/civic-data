ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS

Application #: ZT-6-2024
Applicant:     West Valley City
Request:       An ordinance text change to amend Sections 11-2-109, 11-4-110, and 11-5-103 of
               the sign ordinance to remove the regulation exemption for noncommercial banner
               signs, to prohibit signs containing content which is not protected by the U.S.
               Constitution or the Utah Constitution, and to modify several standards for
               temporary on-premises signs.

City staff, as directed by the City Council, is proposing an ordinance text change to amend
Sections 11-2-109 and 11-4-110 of the sign ordinance to remove the regulation exemption for
noncommercial banner signs and to prohibit signs containing content which is not protected by
the U.S. Constitution or the Utah Constitution. The staff is also proposing to modify several
standards for temporary on-premises signs in Section 11-5-103.




Planning Commission Public Hearing Minutes   June 12, 2024                      Page 1 of 29
The first change relates to noncommercial banner signs. The current code in Section 11-2-109
exempts noncommercial banner signs from meeting any signage regulations. The proposed
change will remove this exemption so that noncommercial banner signs must meet the same sign
regulations that commercial banner signs are required to meet. As a point of information, Section
11-1-103 already allows for any noncommercial message to be substituted for a commercial
message on any sign type permitted by the code.
The second change adds another item to the list of prohibited signs in 11-4-110. Signage
containing content which is not protected by the United State Constitution, or the Utah
Constitution will be prohibited. Such content would have to be quite sexually explicit in nature
or be something that incites violence or makes specific threats against an individual to not be
protected under either constitution.
The first two changes are being proposed at the request of the City Council. The other proposed
changes to Section 11-5-103, dealing with temporary on-premises signs, are being suggested by
City staff. These changes will remove formatting inconsistencies between different temporary
sign types in the code and will also modify several standards for temporary signage.
The following summarizes the changes proposed to Section 11-5-103 for temporary on-premises
signs:
  • Currently the code specifies that only one temporary sign type is allowed for a given
  establishment. This language will be removed since it is regulated by individual temporary sign
  types.
  • Formatting for the number of road widening construction signs will be changed to be
  consistent with other sign types.
  • An establishment will only be allowed to have one banner sign on the property, whether that
  sign is acting as the primary wall sign or not. Language will also be removed about sign
  placement on the building. Finally, the requirement to allow for tenant spaces larger than 150
  linear feet to have a banner sign equal to 10 percent of the building façade was removed.
  • A line will be added to allow for an establishment to have one other temporary sign, in
  addition to an inflatable sign.
  • A size restriction of 16 sq. ft. for temporary noncommercial signs will apply to this sign type
  in all zones. Currently there is no size restriction in non-residential zones.
  • Formatting for the number of property signs will be changed to be consistent with other sign
  types.
  • A line will be added to allow for an establishment to have one other temporary sign, in
  addition to a vehicle sign.



Planning Commission Public Hearing Minutes   June 12, 2024                          Page 2 of 29
  • The current code allows for 50% coverage of all windows of the building on a given
  elevation, regardless of the number of stories in a building. The proposed change will modify
  this regulation so that window signage can only be calculated based on 50 percent coverage of
  the first story window area and specifies that the signage shall be located on the first story of
  the building as well.

Application #: ZT-7-2024
Applicant:     West Valley City
Request:       A zone text change to amend Section 7-5-101 of the zoning ordinance to allow
               zone change applications to the R-1-7, R-1-8, R-1-10, and R-1-12 zones for
               properties that meet certain conditions.

City staff is proposing a zone text change to amend Section 7-5-101 of the zoning ordinance to
allow zone change applications to the R-1-7, R-1-8, R-1-10, and R-1-12 zones for properties that
meet the following conditions:
   • the property is developed as residential with a density of at least two dwelling units/acre,
   • the property has access and frontage on a street with a planned right-of-way width of at least
     66 feet as indicated on the Major Street Plan, and
   • the property does not have the off-site improvements of curb, gutter, and sidewalk along the
     street frontage.

Back in 2015, the City passed Ordinance 15-34 that included the following language: “Property
within City limits may be amended or rezoned to the R-1-7, R-1-8, R-1-10, or R-1-12 zones if

Planning Commission Public Hearing Minutes   June 12, 2024                          Page 3 of 29
the property is less than two acres in area as of the effective date of this ordinance.” At the time,
the intent was, given the limited amount of remaining vacant land, to steer single family
residential development to the RE (Residential Estate) zone which requires an average lot size of
15,000 square feet and a minimum lot size of 12,000 square feet. To this end, the City
downzoned vacant properties of two acres or more to one of the A (Agricultural) zones.

Staff believes that the City did not anticipate the potential need to rezone developed property to
the R-1-7, R-1-8, R-1-10, or R-1-12 zones. Two examples are provided below.

First, the property at 4342 South 4000 West, which is the subject property of Z-4-2024, is
developed as one single family home and a mobile home park without curb, gutter, and sidewalk
along 4000 West and is zoned R-4 (Four Unit Dwelling Residential). Staff were recently
contacted about this property and its redevelopment potential. While the R-4 zone does not allow
mobile home parks (the current mobile home park is nonconforming), this zone does allow
duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes, and apartments. Since this property is surrounded by R-1-8
zoning and mostly single-family homes, staff is recommending a rezone to R-1-8 that would:
  • keep the mobile home park as a nonconforming use,
  • comply with the General Plan designation of Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre),
  • allow the property to redevelop as single-family homes and get improvements along 4000
     West, and
  • prohibit duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes, and apartments.

Second, staff has had several conversations with the family that owns the properties at 3814 and
3876 South 6000 West that are zoned A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre). Between the
two properties which have no curb, gutter, and sidewalk along 6000 West, there are 15 single
family rental homes and 2 duplexes, totaling 19 rental dwelling units. These units are aging and
in need of repairs and the family is exploring options, including redevelopment, for the
properties. Given the rental income the family receives from the existing units, redevelopment of
the property under the current zone or the RE zone is unlikely. The subject ordinance change
would, in the staff’s view, provide a path for the redevelopment of this property to single-family
homes that are much nicer than what is on the property now.

Staff submits that the proposed change will:
  • maintain the Council’s original intent to steer vacant property planned for residential use to
     the RE zone,
  • restrict, through the proposed conditions, the possibility of zone changes to zones that allow
     lots smaller than 15,000 square feet,
  • prohibit uses that, in staff’s view, would not be appropriate given the surrounding uses,
  • facilitate the construction of off-site improvements through redevelopment, and



Planning Commission Public Hearing Minutes   June 12, 2024                           Page 4 of 29
  • allow developed, aging, residential properties to redevelop into something better than what
    exists.

As a reminder, if this proposed change is approved, all zone change requests are still legislative
decisions. In other words, even if a property a property meets the conditions proposed by this
change, the City is not obligated to approve a zone change on such property.

Application #: C-9-2024
Applicant:     Midwest Motor Express (MME)
Address:       5175 West 2100 South
Request:       M Zone (51.81 Acres Total/Project Area 7.60 Acres)

The applicant, Midwest Motor Express (MME), is requesting conditional use approval for a
trucking facility located at 5175 West 2100 South. The zoning for this area is M, manufacturing.
The West Valley City General Plan designates this area as light manufacturing. The surrounding
zone is manufacturing on all sides. The surrounding uses include similar freight/trucking uses
with Wheeler Machinery to the east.

The applicant is requesting approval to construct a new facility on the existing Swift Trucking
property. The entire site is currently paved, and the building will be a single-story, pre-
engineered metal building that is approximately 31,000 SF. There will be approximately 8,000
SF of office with 56 dock high truck doors.

An expansion to this south area was completed in 2014 (C-22-14) and there was also the addition
of a fuel station in 2015 (C-30-15). At that time 2400 South did not extend all the way to the east
connecting to 4800 West. Based on the updated improvements in this areas and current City
Code additional right-of-way requirements will be required at this time (§7-2-117). That includes
sidewalk and streetlights. The landscaping along this frontage has also deteriorated and will need
to be redone to meet City Code, as well as setting back the 6’ tall fencing out of the 20’ setback.
The entire north frontage along the 2100 S Frontage Road is required to comply with the 7-10
landscaping standards which have been completed and maintained but will also be reviewed for
the 7-2-117 right-of-way requirements.

STAFF ALTERNATIVES:
1. Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following conditions:
      a) The property discussed in this application shall be improved per the approved site plan
          including hard surfacing and landscaping.
      b) The site landscaping shall be maintained and free of weeds.
      c) All parking and maneuvering areas must have the required lighting as outlined in 7-9-
          113.
      d) The overall minimum required landscaping for the site is 5%. The north frontage shall
          be designed in accordance with the Landscaping Standards for High Image Arterials
          (7-10).

Application #: CA-4-2024
Applicant:     CDS Development (LongHorn Steakhouse)
Address:       3031 South 5600 West
Request:       C-2 zone, 1.43 acres

The applicant, CDS Development, is requesting conditional use amendment approval for a
LongHorn Steakhouse restaurant at 3031 South 5600 West. The zoning for this property is C-2,
General Commercial and the General Plan designation is General Commercial. This property is
also located within the 5600 West Overlay Zone. The surrounding zone is C-2, general
commercial on all sides. The surrounding uses include the Kohls shopping center to the east,
Chilis restaurant to the south and Starbucks/retail uses to the north.

The site is located within the existing Kohls shopping center parking lot, currently Lot 4 of the
Shoppes at Lake Park Subdivision. This center has been developed under several phases
including applications C-35-2003, C-5-2006 and C-15-2016 and C-16-2023.

The building is proposed to be a 5,780 SF restaurant with sit down dine-in service of 182 seats.
The facade will be constructed of a variety of products including stucco and stone and must
comply with the Commercial Design Standards. There is also a dumpster enclosure and
housekeeping type area on the northeast corner of this building. This area must be enclosed
behind a masonry screen wall that matches the building per 7-2-118.
