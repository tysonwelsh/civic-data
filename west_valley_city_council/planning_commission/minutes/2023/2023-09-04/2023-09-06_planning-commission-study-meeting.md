GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-8-2023 Petition by LIEU TRAN requesting a change to the General Plan from Parks and Open
           Space Existing to Light Manufacturing and a zone change from A-1 (Agriculture,
           minimum lot size 1 acre) to M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 7007 West
           Apaloosa Drive on 5.91 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Steve Pastorik stated that there is an industrial use to the north of the subject property and
              mobile homes to the east. He pointed out the wetlands to the south and west and pointed
              out the property the City owns and that owned by Magna Water. Steve noted an access
              easement running through the mobile home park to the subject property. He pointed out
              that they are proposing a second access above the canal, which would require the
              applicant to work with Salt Lake County’s Flood Control and, potentially, Kennecott.
              Steve said the Fire Department may not consider this a sufficient secondary access. He
              explained that there isn’t frontage because the Ritter Canal runs between the property in
              question and Beagley Road. Steve said the applicant intends to use the existing home as
              an office building.

              Steve mentioned that staff feels the proposed use would work in this location. He noted
              that access would be problematic for any development on this lot.

              Commissioner Woodruff asked how this relates to the City not wanting private streets.
              Steve said that when the Council voted to prohibit private streets, they thought of those
              owned by a separate association, such as an HOA. He stated that these would not be
              considered private streets where there is one owner.

              Commissioner Woodruff asked if there would need to be a discussion about who would
              maintain the roads if they were to sell the property or start a partnership. Steve said the
              Council did make an exception that permits private streets within the M zone.

              Chair Fuller asked if the two proposed access points would be permitted where they are
              close in proximity. Steve said this would be coming back to the Commission as a
              conditional use, and the access points would be addressed further at that point.

              Vice Chair Lovato said he had not heard cars racing on Beagley Road recently and asked
              if Steve had heard anything about why the racing has decreased. Steve stated that the
              enforcement has increased on Beagley Road. However, the street is long, straight, and
              primarily industrial, so racing will likely be a continuous issue.

              Vice Chair Lovato asked if the storage units would be locked and only those leasing a
              storage unit would have access or if someone onsite would manage the property.

              Steve said he isn’t sure if there will be an onsite manager, but he will find out and follow
              up with the Commission.
ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-2-2023  Petition by JACKIE CARLSON requesting a zone change from LI (Light Industrial) to
          M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 2256 and 2260 S 5700 W on 2.2 acres.
          (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:   Steve Pastorik reviewed the history of the subject property to help the Commission
              understand how it became zoned LI and why the applicant is requesting the M. He said
              the applicant would use the property to repair their fleet trucks. Steve said that trucks
              would be stored for over twenty-four hours while waiting to be repaired, so they would
              be considered outside storage. He noted that the LI zone only permits auto repair as a
              secondary use and does not permit outdoor storage. Therefore, the applicant is requesting
              the zone be changed to the manufacturing zone.

              Steve pointed out that the existing metal storage building was developed in 1990. He
              mentioned that recently, there was a business that manufactured tiny homes, operating on
              the subject property without any licensing. However, that business is no longer in
              operation at this location. He said that Carlson, the new owner, intends to bring the site
              into compliance. Steve pointed out the particle board that had been added to the existing
              structure and said the applicant would attain the needed building permits and add the
              required landscaping. Steve stated that the site would require cleanup and maintenance,
              and the parking lot would need to be paved. He explained that since the property is on a
              high-image arterial street, it would be the addition of a precast wall is required. However,
              the Commission can waive that requirement in certain circumstances. Steve pointed out
              that this would come back to the Commission as a conditional use, and proper screening
              could be discussed then.

              Steve explained that if the Commission is leaning toward approval, there are still specific
              uses that the City would not want on the property, such as a salvage yard. He then
              reviewed the other staff suggestions listed within the staff report.

              Commissioner Winters said this would be a great addition to the neighborhood. He asked
              if there would be a reason the City would not favor this proposal. Steve said this would
              be an improvement where it would clean up the site and add pavement. He noted that it is
              a challenging property to develop, but this would help improve the area.

              Commissioner Winters would be okay with not adding the precast wall; however, he
              would like to see the existing fence replaced.

              Commissioner Lovato pointed out that the chain-linked fence has razor wire running
              along the top and asked if we could replace it with a different type of fence. Steve said
              that most of the surrounding area is vacant, so they could have added the razor wire for
              extra security. He believes that as the area develops the safety concerns will decrease.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-19-2023 Petition by SAMUEL WRIGHT requesting conditional use approval for warehouse
          buildings. The property is located at 7091 W and 7103 W Gates Ave on 1.2 acres and is
          zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
          brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)
Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that this request is for the addition of two warehouse
              buildings. He noted that the property is next to residential, so it is coming before the
              Commission as a conditional use. Brock stated that the biggest issue with this proposal is
              the access points. He noted that the applicant is proposing to remove the access for the
              property to the east, which he would need to work out with that property owner and put in
              a shared access. Brock said that he is proposing a second access point to the west. In
              speaking with the Public Works Department, Brock noted that they mentioned the two
              proposed access points would be too close to each other. Therefore, the applicant will
              continue to work on this issue with Public Works.

              Brock stated that the existing development agreement states a ten-foot setback and chain-
              linked fence with slats are allowed on the west side of the property. He reviewed the
              proposed landscaping and pointed out where they would be required to put in additional
              landscaping. Brock presented pictures of the site and pointed out that it would need to be
              cleaned up from the previous auto business.

              Chair Fuller asked how far apart the access points need to be. Brock said he wasn’t sure
              but would find out and add the distance to the staff report.


C-20-2023     Petition by JACOB WEBER requesting conditional use approval for a parking lot
              expansion located within the Decker Lake Overlay Zone. The property is located at
              3422 S Decker Lake Dr. on 1.45 acres and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff –
              Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock stated that this property owner also owns the Country Inn & Suites, the adjacent
              property to the east. He said there had been enforcement issues with semi-trucks parking
              on the subject property to stay at the hotel. Brock noted that the applicant is requesting to
              extend the parking lot to accommodate the guests needing to park these larger trucks.
              Brock did inform the applicant that they could not have people sleeping overnight in their
              vehicles or anywhere in the parking lot. He explained that they would only need a slight
              adjustment to the proposed landscaping to meet those requirements. Brock pointed out
              where they have striping for parking along one side of the property. He said he needs to
              find out if they plan to line the rest of the lot.


C-21-2023     Petition by ROYAL SOLUTIONS, LLC requesting conditional use approval for a
              caretaker’s dwelling at the Royal Solutions Storage Units. The property is located at
              3489 S. 1400 W. on .76 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing) and is located within the
              Jordan River Overlay Zone (JROZ). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or
              jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp stated that the subject is outlined in red on the aerial photo; however, the
              units on the lots to the south are also involved in this application. She explained that the
              Higgens family had owned the property for over twenty years. Since then, Mr. Higgens
              Clark has passed away, and his son, Addison, wants to clean up and reopen. She said the
              u-shaped building on the subject property had commercial and warehouse uses. Jody
              noted that the units to the south have not been utilized for quite some time. She pointed
              out that the zoning is tricky because the property is located within the Jordan River
              Overlay Zone, which does not permit commercial, or warehouse uses. Jody stated that
              Addison had applied for a business license. As part of the review, it was noticed that the
                site needed cleanup and maintenance, and a caretaker's unit was discovered within the U-
                shaped building. She said that where the caretaker's unit is permitted, it makes sense to
                request approval at this time. Jody noted that the applicant is proposing self-storage units
                and does not intend to lease any of the units out. She explained that the building
                inspections still would need to do their evaluation. However, complying with building
                codes, maintenance, and site cleanup are the most significant issues.


C-22-2023       Petition by HUNT ELECTRIC requesting conditional use approval for Outside Storage.
                The property is located at 2421 S. Progress Dr. on 1.6 acres and is zoned M
                (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)


Discussion:     Jody Knapp explained that this is a conditional use request for outside storage. She
                pointed out that the applicant, Hunt Electric, also owns the property adjacent to the
                subject property at 1863 W. Alexander Street. Jody stated that they have only submitted
                plans for the parking lot off Alexander Street, and the City is waiting for plans on the rest
                of the property. She explained that a conditional use application was approved for the
                property at 1863 W. Alexander Street in 2017. However, the applicant did not own the
                subject property then, and they are returning for conditional use approval on this
                property. Jody stated that the grading and drainage can be determined once the plans are
                submitted, and hopefully, this will not become an enforcement case.

                Jody noted that Mr. Hunt has had issues with theft and does not want to put up a fence
                screening his property; instead, he is requesting to use berming and landscaping. She
                pointed out that they may need to add a sidewalk along the east side of the manufacturing
                street. Currently, they are showing landscaping where the sidewalk would be placed.
                Jody said that the Public Works Department had commented that a sidewalk would be
                required, so it would be part of the red-line comments.

                Commissioner Winters asked if the existing chain-linked fence should have slats or if it is
                okay as it is. Jody stated that she is struggling a bit with the screening. She noted that the
                outside storage is right next to the street and quite visible. Jody said it would be up to the
                Commission if you feel a solid fence should be added or if you are okay with the
                applicant’s request to use landscaping and berming.
