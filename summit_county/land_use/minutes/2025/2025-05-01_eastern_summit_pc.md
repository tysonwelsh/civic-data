3)
Continued discussion and possible action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for a Utility structure and Underground transmission line for a secondary irrigation system for Henefer Town, located southwest of the Henefer Cemetery, Summit County, UT; Parcel NS-836-X (38.5 acres). Project #25-047. Jennifer Leslie, County Planner.
(6:05 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Staff Report from April 17 ESCPC Meeting
Attachment:
Memo Regarding Henefer Secondary Irrigation CUP
Jennifer Leslie, County Planner, stated that there was insufficient documentation to show the potential risks to County infrastructure and residences if the dam broke, overflowed, or drained. Also, one outstanding question about ownership related to the engineering standards for grading across property lines. The ownership question had been resolved within the last half hour. County Engineer Brandon Brady stated that he just received an update; however, he needed to review the information to present a formal review. (6:06 PM)
Henefer Attorney Mason Kjar and Henefer Town Engineer Chris Thomsen provided additional updates for project #25-047. (6:09 PM)
Commissioner Hayes asked about the water flow and what would happen if there was a dam breach. Engineer Thomsen responded. (6:11 PM)
Commissioner Peterson asked if there was sufficient infrastructure in place downstream to control runoff and prevent flooding should there be a dam breach. Engineer Thomsen, Engineer Brady, Deputy County Attorney Helen Strachan, and Attorney Mason Kjar responded. (6:19 PM)
Bill Wilde made a motion to table until May 15, 2025, any possible action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for a Utility structure and Underground transmission line for a secondary irrigation system for Henefer Town, located southwest of the Henefer Cemetery, Summit County, UT; Parcel NS-836-X (38.5 acres). Project #25-047. (6:39 PM). Seth Bowen seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: David Darcey, Don Sargent, Marion Wheaton.
