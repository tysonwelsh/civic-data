                            Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                     April 23, 2024
                                       7:00 p.m.
                                      21 E. 100 N.
                                   Nephi, UT 84648

1.    Mayor Seely stated that the Ute Stampede Committee was unable to attend the
     meeting. The Committee had asked him in advance if the city would sponsor $2000
     through the electric and gas departments again this year for the rodeo. The council
     agreed to continue the sponsorship.

2.     Mr. Atkinson said that the City had received a request from Priority Homes for a code
     to be written to allow for short-term rentals. He listed some pros and cons of having
     short-term rentals. Mr. Atkinson said we have a housing crisis in Nephi.
     The question is if investors would come in and buy up the supply of homes and rent
     them out short term which restricts long-term rentals and home ownership.
        Garrison Ludlow and Hollie Holman addressed the council to ask them if they would
     consider allowing short-term rental within the city. Ms. Holman said she has owned
     some short-term rentals in other cities. She said that Nephi is different from a city that is
     located by a National Park like Moab. Homeowners and investors could benefit from
     having short-term rentals. Holman didn’t think it would affect the housing market. She
     stated that at the super-state wrestling event she asked many of her friends where they
     stayed and almost all of them stayed in Utah County. Holman said that the city could
     look at Richfield as a model for allowing short-term rentals.
         Councilor Travis Worwood said that Washington County requires a conditional use
     permit so that the county can know where the short-term rentals are located. Councilor
     Cowan asked if the city could restrict the amount of short-term rentals and where they
     could be located. r
         The council agreed to allow short-term rentals and directed staff to look at other cities'
     laws governing short-term rentals and to draft an ordinance that would allow them.

3. The council reviewed a list of project priorities. Councilor Travis Worwood suggested
   having a deadline on each project in a spreadsheet or app. Councilor Parady asked
   about the master transportation plan. Mr. Atkinson answered that Juab County had
   completed that transportation plan. Mr. Atkinson said that the City may need to add
   interior roads to the county’s plan. Councilor Callaway said that several of the projects
   when completed would create a domino effect for other projects. Councilor Travis
   Worwood asked for a link on the agenda with the project priorities so that they could
   always have access to the updated list.

4.    The council discussed participating in the Rural Utah Delegated Representative to the
     Utah League of Cities and Towns. The idea was initiated by Hilldale City Mayor Donia
     Jessop. The purpose of the representative would be to represent the collective interests
        of rural Utah communities to the ULCT Board of Directors. The council agreed to
        participate in the program.

   5.    The council discussed the natural gas rate for power plant. The rate would be a unique
        rate that follows the market natural gas rate. The proposed rate would be the northwest
        pipeline rate plus .40 cents. The council agreed with the rate and directed staff to bring
        back a fee resolution for official approval.

   6. Finance Director/City Recorder Lisa E. Brough reviewed with the council the new
      legislation that increased URS Tier II hybrid public employee contribution rate by .7%
      and the public safety Tier II employees rate by 2.14% beginning July 1, 2024. The
      council directed staff to pick up the extra URS costs through a salary enhancement for
      the Tier II public employees and to pick up the extra contribution for the public safety
      Tier II employees.
