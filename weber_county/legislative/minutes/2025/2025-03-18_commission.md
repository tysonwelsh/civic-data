    7. APPROVE A MULTI-JURISDICTIONAL IT MUTUAL AID AGREEMENT BY AND AMONG
       BOUNTIFUL, CENTERVILLE, CENTRAL UTAH 911, COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS, DAVIS
       COUNTY, DRAPER, DUCHESNE COUNTY, NORTH OGDEN, OREM, PROVO, RIVERTON,
       SALT LAKE CITY, SALT LAKE COUNTY, SANDY, SARATOGA SPRINGS, SOUTH SALT LAKE,
       TOOELE, UTAH COUNTY, WASATCH COUNTY, WEBER AREA 911, WEBER COUNTY, WEST
       JORDAN, WEST VALLEY.
         Quinn Fowers (IT Director): This is a mutual aid agreement that has grown to 25 cities and counties, with a
         pending total of 35. This allows these entities to help each other during natural disasters or cyber security
         events. It allows cost savings through shared training and resource and facility sharing. If you have the
         resources available, support is no-cost for up to 40 hours to send your staff to help someone get back on their
         feet. This came from one of the larger cities that was ‘ransomed’ and down for four months. Salt Lake City
         and Provo City put this together and felt it would be good to have a formal agreement.

    8. APPROVE A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BY AND BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY
       AND WEBER HUMAN SERVICES FOR A GRANT FUNDED MAT (MEDICATION ASSISTED
       TREATMENT) PROGRAM PROVIDING NURSING AND CASE MANAGEMENT/PEER SUPPORT
       SERVICES WITHIN THE COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY UTILIZING ONE FULL TIME
       REGISTERED NURSE TO MANAGE THE MAT SERVICES AND ONE CASE MANAGER/PEER
       SUPPORT SPECIALIST TO PROVIDE EDUCATION FOR MAT AND LINK INDIVIDUALS TO
       SERVICES UPON REENTRY.
         Chief Phillip Reese (Sheriff’s Department - Corrections): Some endeavors that are worthwhile take time to
         put together. This has taken 18 months to pull together. This agreement pulls opioid funds from the state
         and County to use toward putting negative past consequences from our history into really good working
         solutions for our vulnerable population. My list of gratitude to those who helped accomplish this is way too
         long and I am fearful I would miss someone. I will simply say my heart is full and I am grateful to be
         offering this Memorandum of Understanding to you.

         Commissioner Harvey: Chief Reese, you have done a phenomenal job and this shows your level of care.
         We appreciate that.

         Commissioner Froerer: Chief Reese we appreciate your effort in this. This is a great use of these funds.
         Thank you very much for what you have done.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve a Memorandum of Understanding by and between Weber County and
         Weber Human Services for a Grant Funded MAT (Medication Assisted Treatment) Program providing nursing
         and case management/peer support services within the county correctional facility utilizing one Full Time
         Registered Nurse to manage the MAT services and one case manager/peer support specialist to provide
         education for MAT and link individuals to services upon reentry; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
