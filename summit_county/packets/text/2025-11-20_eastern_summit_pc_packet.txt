Background 
On September 25, 2025, the Community Development Department received an application 
from Upland Development Inc., acting as the applicant on behalf of the property owner, Upland 
Capital LLC. The application requests approval of a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) to allow the 
construction of an accessory building (detached garage) on the property located at 1586 
Stewart Lane in Kamas, Utah. The subject parcel is approximately 5.70 acres in size and is 
located within the AG-10 zoning district.  
 
The subject property is currently being developed with a primary residence. The proposed 
accessory building is a detached garage with a total area of approximately 4,292 square feet. It 
is intended for personal, non -commercial use in association with the primary residence. Under 
Section 11-3-16 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code, accessory buildings and uses 
that exceed 2,000 square feet require a Conditional Use Permit. This provision exists because 
larger accessory buildings, depending on their use and design, can have impacts on the 
surrounding neighborhood. The CUP process ensures that such proposals receive special review 
and that potential impacts are considered before approval.  
 
The Community Development staff, in coordination with service providers, has reviewed the 
application and determined that the project, as conditioned, complies with all applicable 
requirements of the Eastern Summit County Development Code.

Background 
The Green Hill Subdivision was recorded on March 21, 2008 and consists of five lots and one 
ancillary/agricultural parcel. The subdivision was approved as a 2 -lot Minor Subdivision, with 
the incorporation of two (2) additional Lots of Record, and a transfer of one unit of density into 
the subdivision, creating a total of five lots. Development envelopes and plat notes were 
established in order to mitigate potential negative viewshed impacts. The subdivision was 
approved under the previous Eastern Summit County Development Code and Zoning. In 2021, 
the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission approved a plat amendment for Lots 2 and 3 
which removed the building pads and added notes for height restrictions due to previous 
grading. In 2025, the Planning Commission approved another plat amendment updating plat 
notes that regulate building height throughout the Green Hill Subdivision. 
 
The subject parcel GRNHL -2-2AM is approximately 1.00 acres in size and is located within the 
AG-10 zoning district. Parcel GRNHL-2-2AM is currently in the process of being developed with 
a primary residence. The proposed accessory building is a detached garage with a total area of 
approximately 3,000 square feet. It is intended for personal, non -commercial use in association 
with the residence.
 The building includes two levels, a lower level designated for storage and a 
main level to function as the garage. Under Section 11 -3-16 of the Eastern Summit County 
Development Code, accessory buildings and uses that exceed 2,000 square feet require a 
Conditional Use Permit. This provision exists because larger accessory buildings, depending on 
their use and design, can have impacts on the surrounding neighborhood. The CUP process 
ensures that such proposals receive special review and that potential impacts are considered 
before approval.  
 
The Community Development staff, in coordination with service providers, has reviewed the 
application and determined that the project, as conditioned, complies with all applicable 
requirements of the Eastern Summit County Development Code.

ee the graphic illustration of the history of these subdivisions and plat amendments. 
Re

does not contain natural slopes exceeding 30%, and research has not revealed any other  
reason for the inclusion of building pads.    
Analysis and Findings 
Section 11-4-5(E.10) of the Eastern Summit County Development Code sets the following 
grounds for vacating or amending a subdivision plat not involving a private or public road: 
1. I
f the Land Use Authority is satisfied that the public interest will not be materially
injured by the proposed vacation, alteration, or amendment, and there is good cause
for the vacation, alteration, or amendment, the Land Use Authority may vacate, alter,
or amend the final subdivision plat, or any portion of the final subdivision plat.
A
nalysis: The adjustment of the exterior property lines is minimal and does 
not significantly alter the overall layout of the lot.  No part of Richins Ranch 
Road, a private road, is vacated with this plat amendment.  Because the 
adjacent lots have been relocated and staff have verified that no natural 
slopes exceeding 30% exist on the site, the removal of the building pad would 
not impact any adjoining  property owners or allow for development in 
sensitive lands.
