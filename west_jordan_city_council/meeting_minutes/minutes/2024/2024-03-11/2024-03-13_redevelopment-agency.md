REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY MEETING 7:09 pm
 1. CALL TO ORDER
      BOARD:     Chairperson Zach Jacob, Vice Chairperson Chad Lamb, Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Kelvin
                 Green, Kent Shelton, Kayleen Whitelock
      STAFF:     Council Office Director Alan Anderson, Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck,
                 Council Office Clerk Cindy Quick

      Chairperson Jacob called the meeting to order at 7:09 pm


 2. PUBLIC HEARINGS
      a. RDA Resolution No. 242 amending the budget for fiscal year ending June 30, 2024.
         Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck proposed a budget amendment to
         purchase property at 8701 S. Redwood Road. The proposed amount was slightly higher
         than the purchase price in order to capture anything else that may arise regarding the
         property after its been purchased. She noted the revised version on Exhibit A to correct
         a math error on a previous version provided to the Council. She asked that the board
         adopt the revised version.

          Chairperson Jacob opened a public hearing at 7:10 pm
          Comments:
          None
          Chairperson Jacob closed the public hearing at 7:11 pm

          Board Vice Chair Lamb asked if the cost to remove the home was included in the
          proposed amount which was later confirmed. Ms. Steck reported that if needed a budget
          amendment was scheduled for May 8, 2024 and any necessary changes would be
          brought back if necessary.

          MOTION: Chair Jacob moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 242 amending the budget for
                  fiscal year ending June 30, 2024.
                  Board Member Whitelock seconded the motion.
          The vote was recorded as follows:
          YES:        Bob Bedore, Pamela Bloom, Zach Jacob, Chad Lamb, Kent Shelton, Kayleen
                      Whitelock
