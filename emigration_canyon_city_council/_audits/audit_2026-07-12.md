## Pillar 4 — Completeness vs external reality

**(a) THE KEY CHECK — pre-2018-10 404 gap is GENUINE (not a Kearns-style missed harvest).**
Tested live 2026-07-12 (browser UA) against PMN notice pages **and** the file store:

| file (date, body) | notice link says | `GET /pmn/files/<id>` |
|---|---|---|
| 406463.pdf (2018-01-15 Council) | labelled **"Meeting Minutes"** | **404** (315 B) |
| 391513.pdf (2018-05-17 PC) | cancellation notice | **404** (315 B) |
| 269375.pdf (2017-01-12 PC) | cancellation notice | **404** (315 B) |
| 362997.mp3 (2018-01-15 audio) | audio | **404** (315 B) |
| **459655.pdf (2018-10-25 Council, earliest kept)** | — | **200** (68,960 B pdf) |
| **511437.pdf (2018-11-15 PC, earliest kept)** | — | **200** (289,998 B pdf) |
| 1456089.pdf (2026-05-19 current) | — | **200** (461,467 B) |

The notice **metadata** survives (2018-01-15 still lists a "Meeting Minutes" link), but the
underlying blob is purged for **every** pre-2018-10 file, while 2018-10+ files download. The
surviving early files carry **higher, later-issued ids** (459655/511437 > the purged
406463/391513) → an upstream re-post/purge of the store around file-id ≈ 450000 (~July 2018).
**This is a collection-wide PMN file-rot trait — independently documented for Kearns**
(pre-~July-2018 `file_id` < ~450000 now 404). Emigration's honest-gap logging (council 14 / PC
74 in `minutes_unrecovered.csv`) is **correct**; MSD AgendaCenter is the only backfill avenue.
**Opposite of the Kearns false gap** (there the files actually downloaded). ✅ **gap genuine.**
