DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT:
     Consider approval of a development agreement with Toll Southwest LLC for
     the Regency at Desert Color – Model Home Center, Toll Brothers.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: The preliminary plat for Desert Color Plat A
      was approved by the City Council on August 19, 2021. The approval was subject to
      a development agreement for the model home center. Staff recommends approval.

      Link to presentation from City Manager Adam Lenhard: 00:01:38 [Recording 1]

      Agenda Packet [Page 4]

      Link to motion: 00:02:39 [Recording 1]

      MOTION:
            A motion was made by Councilmember Tanner to approve the development
            agreement with Toll Southwest LLC for the Regency at Desert Color – Model
            Home Center, Toll Brothers, subject to the requirement to switch the outdoor
            landscaping to a secondary water source.
      SECOND:
            The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.
      VOTE:
            Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

             Councilmember Hughes – aye
             Councilmember McArthur – aye
             Councilmember Larkin – aye
             Councilmember Larsen – did not vote
             Councilmember Tanner – aye

             The motion carried.
