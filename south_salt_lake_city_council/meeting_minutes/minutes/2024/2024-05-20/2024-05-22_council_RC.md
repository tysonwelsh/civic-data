Unfinished Business

    1. Update on the Water Well Bond
       Finance Director, Crystal Makin, along with Zions Bank Vice President, Alex Buxton, and
       bond counsel, Randy Larsen, went over the status of the Water Well bond.

        Back in 2022, the City went to the Department of Water Resources and applied for a
        bond to help pay for building a new well. At the time, it was approved for an amount up
        to 8.5 million dollars. The bond covers up to 85% of the cost, and the City is covering the
        remaining 15% of the cost.

        The project has accepted its first bid from a contractor to begin work on the project site.
        As a result, the City will be moving to close on the bond. Due to the type of action that
        was taken in 2022 there is no need to re-authorize anything at this time. This was meant
        to be an informational update for the Council as they begin the work on the project.
