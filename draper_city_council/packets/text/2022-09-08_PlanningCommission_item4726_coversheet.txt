Re: City Initiated 2022 General Plan Amendment - Moderate Income Housing Plan Request Comments: This application is a request for approval and adoption of changes to the Draper City General Plan with respects to requirements of the Moderate Income Housing Plan element(s) within the plan.  In 2019 the Utah State Legislature passed Senate Bill 34 (SB 34) which required Cities to adopt specific strategies to encourage the creation of Moderate Income Housing into their General Plan from a specific list provided within SB 34. Draper City complied in 2019 and adopted four (4) Moderate Income Housing strategies from the pre-approved list into the General Plan. House Bill  462 (HB 462) passed during the 2022 Utah State Legislative session, now requires that the City update the General Plan and select from a revised list of specific strategies that were contained within HB 462. The findings are as follows: For a positive recommendation:
1. A Public Hearing before the Planning Commission was held in accordance with Utah Code Section 10-9a-404.
2. The General Plan Amendment is consistent with the requirements of Utah Code and is appropriate.
For a negative recommendation:
1. The General Plan Amendment is not consistent with the requirements of Utah Code and is not appropriate. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type P.C. Staff Report 9/2/2022 Cover Memo
