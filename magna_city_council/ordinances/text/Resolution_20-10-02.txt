A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ADOPTING AN AMENDMENT TO THE = 2020 AMENDED
CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE TO UPDATE AND AMEND THE
CEMETERY FEE SCHEDULE FOR THE PLEASANT GREEN CEMETERY

RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (““UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body

, for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. On November 26, 2019, pursuant to state law, the Council approved and adopted the
2020 Consolidated Fee Schedule for Magna.

D. On May 13, 2020 ownership and operational control of the Pleasant Green Cemetery
(the “Cemetery”) was transferred to the municipality.

E. The Cemetery does have certain costs for services that require the payment of fees to
pay for those services, the municipality is required to adopt a fee schedule so fees can
be imposed and collected.

1

F. On June 9, 2020, the Council tentatively approved the Cemetery Fee Schedule
(Exhibit A) and set the public hearing to be held on June 23, 2020 at 7:00 PM to
accept input from the public regarding the Cemetery Fee Schedule.

G. On June 23, 2020 the Council Adopted the Cemetery Fee Schedule (the “Schedule”)
as a part of the 2020 Magna Consolidated Fee Schedule.

H. After further research by the Cemetery Advisory Board, it was determined the
adopted schedule did not cover the costs of the services provided and requested the
Council consider an amendment to the schedule so the fees charged covered the costs
of the services.

I. On September 22, 2020, the Council tentatively approved the Amended Cemetery Fee
Schedule (Attachment A) and set the public hearing to be held on June 23, 2020 at
7:00 PM to accept input from the public regarding the amendments to the Cemetery
Fee Schedule.

J. On September 30, 2020, a notice of the public hearing and a copy of the proposed
amendments to the Cemetery Fee Schedule were posted on the municipality’s website
(www.magnametrotownship.org), the Utah Public Meeting Notice website
(www.utah.gov/pmn), and posted at the Webster Center (8952 W Magna Main St
Magna, UT, 84044) which fulfills the noticing requirements under State Law.

K. The Cemetery Fees are necessary for the conduct of the business of the Cemetery.

RESOLUTION
2

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and
Council hereby approves and adopts the amendment to the 2020 Magna Consolidated Fee
Schedule amending the Cemetery Fee Schedule, attached to this resolution and listed as
