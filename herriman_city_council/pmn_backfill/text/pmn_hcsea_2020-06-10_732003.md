                                 SPECIAL HERRIMAN CITY SAFETY
                                  ENFORCEMENT AREA MINUTES
                                            Wednesday, June 10, 2020
                                             Approved June 9, 2021

The following are the minutes of the Special Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. The meeting was held on
Wednesday, June 10, 2020 at 7:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street,
Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website,
and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding:      Chair Jared Henderson

Trustees Present:         Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, Clint Smith and David Watts

Staff Present: City Manager Brett geo. Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, Community Development
               Director Blake Thomas, Chief of Police Troy Carr, City Planner Michael Maloy, and Communications
               Manager Jonathan LaFollette. .

7:00 PM - SPECIAL MEETING:
        1. Call to Order
Chair Jared Henderson called the meeting to order at 7:32 p.m. and welcomed all in attendance.

               1.1. Approval of the May 27, 2020 HCSEA meeting minutes
Trustee David Watts moved to approve the May 27, 2020 minutes as written. Trustee Clint Smith seconded the motion and all voted
aye.

        2. Discussion and Action Items
              2.1. Discussion and Consideration of a Resolution Adopting a Final Budget; Making
                   Appropriations for Support of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area for the
                   Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2020 and Ending June 30, 2021, and Determining the
                   Rate of Tax and Levying Taxes Upon All Real and Personal Property within Herriman
                   City – Alan Rae, Finance Director
June 10, 2020 – Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area Minutes                          Page 2 of 2
Finance Director Alan Rae explained this Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area budget needed to be approved
independently and had been incorporated into the Herriman City budget. Total budgeted amount was $6.7 million
dollars.

Chief Carr offered an overview of the Herriman Police Department five-year plan including adding an additional
lieutenant as the City looked to becoming a second-class city, and offered a brief synopsis of the divisions growing.
The expenditures for the Police Department coincided with the anticipated property tax revenue.

Trustee David Watts moved to approve Resolution R20-01 adopting a final budget and making appropriations for the support of the
Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area for the period beginning July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021, and determining the rate of
tax to be set at .0019 for levying taxes upon all real and personal property within the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area. Trustee
Smith seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Chair Jared Henderson                                Aye
Trustee Steven Shields                               Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                                 Aye
Trustee Clint Smith                                  Aye
Trustee David Watts                                  Aye
The motion passed unanimously.

         3. Adjournment
Trustee Smith moved to adjourn the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area meeting at 7:44 p.m. Trustee Shields seconded the motion,
and all present voted aye.

I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on June 10, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes for the Herriman
City Safety Enforcement Service Area Meeting.
