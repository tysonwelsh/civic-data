       d.     ZONE TEXT CHANGE: RIVERTON CITY IS PROPOSING AMENDMENTS
              TO SECTION 18.225.050, AGRICULTURAL STRUCTURES, OF THE
              ACCESSORY STRUCTURES ORDINANCE.

Chair Rushton opened the public hearing.

Development Services Director, Jason Lethbridge, reported that the Accessory Structures
Ordinance contains a section for agricultural structures. The standards require the materials
on the exterior to be those that exist on the home. There is an exception for agricultural
structures and the ordinance defines it exclusively for farm animals or feed. He explained
that in looking to allow for larger greenhouses in appropriate settings, the proposed
amendment adds to the agricultural structures section expanding the definition to include
structures used exclusively for the growing of plants. A greenhouse would be allowed on a
.50-acre lot or larger where the building was used exclusively for growing plants. Height is
restricted to 20 feet. He indicated that the ordinance specifies that agricultural structures
cannot include an accessory dwelling unit or an upper story. Staff recommended approval.
A single-level requirement was discussed.

Chair Rushton stated that an accessory dwelling or unit is that is larger than 200 square feet
is allowed on a .25-acre lot. He questioned why a larger greenhouse is not allowed on a
smaller lot. Mr. Lethbridge’s response was inaudible.

There were no public comments. Chair Rushton closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Cluff moved to recommend APPROVAL of the Zone Text Change to
Section 18.225.050 Agricultural Structures, including structures used exclusively for
plants, as described in the staff report. Commissioner Brown seconded the motion.
The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Commission.
