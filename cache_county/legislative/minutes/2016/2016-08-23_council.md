SUMMARY

The Phoebe Meadows Subdivision is a request to create four residential lots out of the existing 20.50-
acre parcel 01-087-0005, which is considered a 1970 parcel. The existing home and agricultural
buildings will remain on Lot 1, and the applicant has proposed reconfiguring the driveway to access
from 400 West instead of 7900 South. Lots 2 and 3 will front 400 West while Lot 4 will front 7900
South. 7900 South does not meet the minimum county standards for road surface width and must be
improved to meet the standards.

This subdivision was originally scheduled for the 7 July 2016 Planning Commission meeting but was
rescheduled for the 4 August 2016 meeting to get more accurate information on the maintenance of
7900 South.
