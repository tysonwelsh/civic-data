EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. \4-02,70 | February 2D, 2019
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 2 OF THE CURRENT APPLICABLE CODE
CONCERNING THE CONDUCT OF MEETINGS OF THE TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
RECITALS
A. Utah State Code section 10-2a-414 provides that when a metro township is
incorporated that each county ordinance that is in effect on the day that metro township is
incorporated will remain in effect as a metro township ordinance until the metro township
council amends or repeals the ordinance.
B. The current code applicable in the Township establishes procedures for the
conduct of the meetings of the council.
C. The Township Council desires to amend the provisions to fit the actual practice of the
Township Council.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY ORDAINED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:
I. The current code section 2.04.180 is amended to read as follows:
2.04.180 - Rules of order—General.
A. Procedural rules not specifically provided herein or by state law, or Township
ordinance, shall be regulated, interpreted and construed in accordance with the
Township Council Meeting Rules of Procedure as may be adopted by resolution
from time to time by the Council.
B. As circumstances may reasonably require, the council may, upon a two-thirds
vote of the members present, temporarily suspend the rules with respect to the
specific matter before it.
II. This ordinance shall take effect upon publication and posting as required in Utah Code 10-3-
711.
