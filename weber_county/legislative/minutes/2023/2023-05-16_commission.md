         5.       Public hearing to discuss and/or take action on an ordinance that will amend the Weber County Code
                  to provide zones, regulations, permissions, and restrictions that will help implement the Western
                  Weber General Plan as it generally relates to residential zoning and residential development and
                  provide related clerical edits and policy adjustments deemed necessary by the Western Weber
                  Planning Commission and the Ogden Valley Planning Commission

                  Charlie Ewert (Planning): Part of the Western Weber General Plan talks of rezoning properties to
                  15k sq foot lot sizes or an average of 15k square foot sizes as long as smart growth strategies have
                  been implemented. This Ordinance creates a 15k square foot zone that provides for less livestock
                  than the RE15. It also sets up street and pathway connectivity standards that run with the zone. We
                  tried to consolidate and clarify code a little bit to make it easier to read and get through, including a
                  use table for zones.

                  Chair Froerer: This gives the ability to make some type of affordable housing out there and have the
                  infrastructure in place. Someone said good infrastructure makes good development.

                  Mr. Ewert: Two items that may need to be tweaked a little bit. One of them is in the very last
                  paragraphs regarding the Dark Sky Committee and reads that “someone from Visit Ogden or other
                  similar tourism bureau.” Visit Ogden mentioned deleting “other similar tourism bureau”. The other
                  item was the 300 foot river buffer. We can change that if you want to.

                  Chair Froerer: No, that is exactly what we expected.
