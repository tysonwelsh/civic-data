to the public at: www.bluffdale.gov. Notice is further given that access to this meeting by the Mayor
and/or City Council may be by electronic means via telephonic conference call.

BLUFFDALE CITY COUNCIL BOARD OF CANVASSERS MEETING

1. Roll Call.

In the absence of Mayor Hall, Acting Mayor Pro Tempore Aston called the meeting to order at

4:30 PM.

All members of the City Council were present with the exception of Mayor Hall and Council

Member Crockett, who were excused..

2. Consideration and Vote on Resolution 2025-91 certifying the Canvass of the Official
Election Results for the 2025 City of Bluffdale Municipal General Election,
Conducted by Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office, Elections Division.

Council Member Wilding moved to APPROVE Resolution 2025-91, certifying the Canvass

of the Official Election Results for the 2025 City of Bluffdale Municipal General Election.

Council Member Austin seconded the motion. Vote on motion: Mayor Pro Tempore Aston-

Aye, Council Member Austin- Aye, Council Member Lord- Aye, Council Member Wilding-

Aye. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

3. Adjournment.

Council Member Aston moved to ADJOURN. Council Member Wilding seconded the

motion. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

The Board of Canvassers Meeting adjourned at 4:32 PM.
