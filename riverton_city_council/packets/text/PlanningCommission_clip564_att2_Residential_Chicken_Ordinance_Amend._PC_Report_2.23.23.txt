Example Motions
   1. I move the Planning Commission recommend adoption of the “Residential Chicken
      Ordinance”, as shown, amending and updating the previous ordinance.

Background
Riverton City ordinance currently allows up to 6 residential chickens to be kept by residents
whose lot size is 10,000-sf or larger. Requests by residents resulted in an inquiry by a city
council member suggesting that fewer chickens may be appropriately kept on smaller lots.
Planning commissioners are asked to review adoption by the city of a sliding scale of permitted
numbers of residential chickens for smaller lot sizes.

The neighboring city of Herriman uses a sliding scale for keeping residential chickens on lots of
different sizes. Their zoning results in residents of a 5,000-7,999 sf lot being allowed to keep
four (4) chickens, and residents of an 8,000-10,000 sf lot are allowed to keep six (6). Planning
commissioners are being asked to review and give their recommendations on the following
questions:
     1. Whether Riverton City should allow residential chickens to be kept on lots smaller than
        10,000-sf, and if so,
     2. How many chickens should be allowed on lots between 5,000-7,999 sf ?
     3. How many chickens should be allowed on lots between 8,000- 10,000 sf?
