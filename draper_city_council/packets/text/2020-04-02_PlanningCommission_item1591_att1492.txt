BACKGROUND & SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a development agreement (Agreement), land use map
amendment (LUM), and zoning map amendment (ZM) for approximately 27.4 acres located south of
Golden Harvest Road, west of Pony Express Road, north of 13100 S (does not include any properties on
13100 S), and east of the Jordan and Salt Lake City Canal. The project area includes the entirety of the
Greenfield Farms subdivision as recorded in 1976. The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential
Agricultural, 40,000 square foot lot minimum) with a land use designation of Residential Low/Medium
Density and Community Commercial. The applicant is seeking a zoning change to M1 (Light
Manufacturing) and land use designation of Business/Light Manufacturing. The applicant is also seeking
a development agreement to amend the permitted uses within the subject property. The applicant is

requesting that an Agreement, LUM, and ZM be approved to allow for the redevelopment of the
residential subdivision to two (2) tilt-wall warehouse distribution buildings.

The subject property is a 22-lot single-family residential subdivision (Greenfield Farms) and includes two
public rights-of-way (Wheatfield Circle and Wheatfield Way) to be vacated with a future request. The
property was developed in the late 1970s. All lots are developed except for 12966 S Wheatfield Way
which previously had a single-family dwelling but is currently vacant.

If approved, the applicant would like to redevelop the entire site into a commercial/industrial use. This
would include two large warehouse distribution building with associated access, circulation, parking, and
stormwater detention – to be sought for approval as a separate site plan application and request to vacate
the residential subdivision at a later date.

Zoning Map Amendment. The applicant also seeks to change the zoning district from RA1 to M1. The
purpose of the M1 zone “is to provide areas for uses involving processing and assembly of manufactured
goods, warehousing, and material storage. Uses which generate excessive noise, vibration, odor, dust, and
fumes are excluded from this zone.” There is another M1 zone (the Coca-Cola facility) less than a quarter
mile to the northwest of the subject property.

The rezone of the subject property to M1 will mean that four properties directly to the south of the subject
property will remain RA1 and will be the only residentially-zoned properties within the immediate area.
Those properties (totaling approximately 1.23 acres) include the addresses of 66 W 13100 S (three lots
make up this one address) and 13096 S Pony Express Road. The properties are currently used for
residential purposes.

While there is an RA1 zoning district to the west of the subject property, this land is actually the Rocky
Mountain Power utility corridor. Residential development on that abutting land will not be possible due to
the utility corridor.

contain a list of items that are required as part of an Agreement, as found in DCMC Section 9-5-200(C).

         C.      Procedure: All development agreements, upon proper execution, shall be recorded with the
                 respective County Recorder's Office, shall run with the land, and shall be binding on all
                 successors in the ownership of the affected property. A development agreement shall
                 contain, at a minimum, the following:
                  1. A legal description of the land subject to the development agreement.
                  2. The restrictions or conditions to be attached to the property including development
                      standards and the provision of public facilities.
                  3. The configuration of the project as shown on a project master plan.
                  4. A statement of the benefits and value the development agreement will have for the

                  5. The time frames for performance by the parties.
                  6. A description of the various City approvals required before the commencement of

                      development agreement.
                  7. Provisions for enforcement of the terms and conditions of the development

Planning Division Review.
   1. Development Agreement - The Development Agreement alters the permitted and conditional
       uses typically granted to the M1 zoning district. Those changes add a higher level of
       restriction/allowance typically afforded the M1 zone aside from “Retail, general” which it sets as
       a permitted use rather than the M1 standard of conditional use. The changes are consistent with
       the general area and add a greater level of predictability regarding how it will be used and
       redeveloped. All other zoning regulations and standards remain consistent with DCMC and the
       M1 zone.

    2. Land Use Map Amendment - The current land use designation of Community Commercial (on
       all put two properties within the project) is not consistent with the current zoning – RA1
       (Residential Agricultural). The proposed land use designation of Business/Light Manufacturing is
       consistent with the proposed zoning designation of M1 (Light Manufacturing) and is compatible
       with the surrounding land use designations of Destination Commercial, Community Commercial,
       and Cultural/Institutional. While the land to the west has a land use designation of Residential
       Low/Medium Density, the subject property and this area are separated by Lone Peak Parkway,
       the Rocky Mountain Power utility corridor, and the Jordan and Salt Lake City Canal. The
       Business/Light Manufacturing should be located along collector and arterial streets; the subject
       property is surrounded by minor collectors and a minor arterial.

    3. Zoning Map Amendment - The current zoning designation of RA1 (Residential Agricultural)
       may not be the most appropriate designation given the zoning districts to the north and south
       (commercial and institutional) and U.S. Interstate 15 directly to the east. This is also the only
       remaining area abutting I-15 that is zoned residential. The proposed M1 (Light Manufacturing) is
       more compatible with the current surrounding designations and the highway proximity. Although
       the land to the west is zoned RA2 (Residential Agricultural), the subject property and the RA2-
       zoned neighborhood are separated by Lone Peak Parkway, the Rocky Mountain Power utility
       corridor, and the Jordan and Salt Lake City Canal.
