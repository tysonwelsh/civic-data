                AUTHOR:    Karyn Kerdolff, GIS Planner

             PRESENTER:    Wayne Harper, Economic &        Community Development Director
               SUMMARY:    An applicant is requesting to amend the zoning map to change the zones
                           of the properties located at 3399 W and 3397 W 4700 S from R- 1- 8 and
                           LC to RM- 8. The applicant has expressed interest in pursuing a twenty-
                           lot townhome style subdivision on the properties if the amendment is
                           approved.


           BACKGROUND:     Robert Thompson, applicant, is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to
                           rezone the properties located at 3399 W and 3397 W 4700 S from R- 1-
                           8 and LC, respectively, to RM- 8. The R- 1- 8 zoning district is
                           characterized as a residential district allowing one dwelling unit on a
                           minimum 8, 000 square foot lot. The LC zoning district characterized as
                           commercial zone. The RM-8 zoning district allows eight dwelling units per
                           acre. The applicant has expressed interest in pursuing a twenty- lot
                           subdivision for this property if the map amendment is approved. Review and
                           consideration of a potential subdivision is not being considered at this time.
 STAFF RECOMMENDATION:     Staff recommends approval of Ordinance No. 20- 16 based on the
                           findings stated in the staff report.

                                   ORDINANCE NO. 20- 16

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING MAP
 AMENDMENT FROM RESIDENTIAL SINGLE- FAMILY MINIMUM 8,000 SQUARE
    FEET (R-1- 8) AND LIMITED COMMERICAL (LC) TO RESIDENTIAL MULTI-
FAMILY DISTRICT; 8 UNITS PER ACRE (RM-8) FOR THE PROPERTIES LOCATED
                           AT 3399 W 4700 S AND 3397 W 4700 S.

       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville        City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on
August 5, 2020, to consider, among other things, approving a zoning map amendment from
Residential Single-Family Minimum 8, 000 Square Feet( R- 1- 8) and Limited Commercial ( LC) to
Residential Multi-family district; 8 units per acre ( RM-8) for the Properties Located at 3399 W
4700 S and 3397 W 4700 S; and


       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 05. 030( A)(2),
notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published in the Salt Lake
Tribune on July 1, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the State Public Notice Website on July 1, 2020; and

       WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendment on July 14, 2020 and voted 6- 1 to recommend that the City Council
approve the proposed amendment; and



       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendment during its regularly scheduled meeting on August 5,
2020; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendment.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposal to amend the City' s zoning map from Residential Single- Family Minimum 8, 000 Square
Feet ( R- 1- 8) and Limited Commercial ( LC) to Residential Multi-family district; 8 units per acre
RM-8) for the Properties Located at 3399 W 4700 S and 3397 W 4700 S, a legal description of
which is attached as Exhibit" A' and incorporated by this reference, is hereby approved.

       This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 20- 16, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the city recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
