II)    BOARD OF CANVASSERS

1. Consideration to Adopt Resolution 27-2021, a Resolution of the Board of
Canvassers Certifying the Official Canvassers’ Report from the November 2,
2021, Municipal General Election for Park City, Utah:
Michelle Kellogg, Election Official, presented this item and indicated the official election
report was emailed to the board members along with the updated resolution. Nann
Worel was elected as mayor, and Tana Toly and Jeremy Rubell were elected as
Council members. In response to a question emailed prior to this meeting, Kellogg
explained the percentages next to the votes were based on the number of votes each
candidate received divided by the total number of votes cast. She also reviewed
reasons for ballots being rejected, including being received after the deadline, the
signature did not match the voter’s registration signature, or the ballot envelopes were
not signed.

Board Member Joyce asked if any attempt was made to contact voters who had
signature issues. Kellogg stated the County Clerk would call the voter as well as mail
them a letter, notifying them to come into the office within seven days of the election to
prove their identity. Council Member Worel asked if the voters had to go to the County
Clerk’s office in person, to which Kellogg affirmed.

Board Member Joyce moved to adopt Resolution 27-2021, a resolution of the Board of
Canvassers certifying the Official Canvassers’ Report from the November 2, 2021,
Municipal General Election for Park City, Utah. Board Member Gerber seconded the
motion.
RESULT: APPROVED
AYES: Board Members Doilney, Gerber, Henney, Joyce, and Worel

III) ADJOURNMENT

With no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
