Background

This application is an amendment to an approved site plan for Extra Space Storage (formerly
Towne Storage) located at 4425 West 12600 South, in the west center of the city. Zoning for the
site is C-G, Gateway Commercial. The applicant proposes to amend the site plan by adding
solar panels to the north building which faces 12600 South. All other elements of the previously
approved site plan would remain in place.

In reviewing the proposed site plan amendment, staff identified an area in need of clarification.
The City’s commercial development code requires screening roof-top mechanical, but solar
panels are not specifically mentioned. As solar panels will continue to be added to more
commercial buildings, planning commissioners should evaluate and decide what this
requirement means for solar panels. Residential solar is not required to be screened.

The applicant proposes to install solar panels on the furthest north building. This building is
separated from residential property to the north (across 12600 South) by 175-feet and is 125-
feet from the nearest residential property directly east across Totorica Circle. The building is
approximately 8-feet tall, and the solar panels will be flat on the top with a 4-foot clearance
around each edge of the building. The line of sight graphic illustrates applicant’s contention that
the panels will not be noticeable from the view of a person walking down the sidewalk.
