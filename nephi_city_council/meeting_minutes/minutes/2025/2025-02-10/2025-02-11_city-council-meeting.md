                           NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                    February 11, 2025
                                        6:00 P.M
                                       21 E 100 N
                                     Nephi, UT 84648


   1.​ The council met at the Public Works Building to discuss and prioritize the department
       budget requests. Due to bad weather Mayor Seely and Councilor Cowan did not arrive
       until 7:00 p.m. After they arrived Councilor Travis Worwood moved to convene into
       closed session to discuss the character and competence of an individual and real
       property. Councilor JD Parady seconded the motion. The motion passe unanimously.

   2.​ The work session began at 8:08 p.m. The meeting focused on the budget prioritization
       for Nephi City. City Administrator Seth Atkinson detailed requests estimating a surplus
       of revenues between $200,000-$300,000. Key discussions included funding for the
       chamber at $15,000 annually, reducing it to $12,000 in year two of the budget. The
       council discussed a property tax increase for public safety needs. The need for a new
       garbage truck was highlighted. The council also discussed the importance of
       maintaining quality over quantity in hiring and focusing on retaining talent. The meeting
       concluded with plans to prioritize funding for ongoing expenses. The preliminary list of
       budget priorities was scheduled to be reviewed next week.

   3.​ The meeting adjourned at 9:40 p.m.
