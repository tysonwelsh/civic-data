                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 23-01

       AN ORDINANCE AMENDING RIVERTON CODE SECTION 18.90.010, TABLE OF
          COMMERCIAL USES, AMENDMENTS PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY


       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendment; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
amend the Riverton City Code Section 18.90.010, Table of Commercial Uses, as described in Exhibit “A”.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Code Section 18.90.010, Table of Commercial Uses, shall be, and
       hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit “A.”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 17th day of January,
2023 by the following vote:
