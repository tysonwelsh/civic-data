                              RESOLUTION NO. 25-49

 A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CANCELING THE RACE FOR
THE 4-YEAR AT-LARGE CITY COUNCIL SEATS AND CERTIFYING TASHA LOWERY
                     AND MIKE GREEN AS ELECTED

WHEREAS, the Draper City Council (“Council”) met in regular meeting on
September 16, 2025, to consider, among other items of business, canceling
the race for the 4-year at-large city council seats for the 2025 municipal general
election and certifying Tasha Lowery and Mike Green as elected to the two
open 4-year at-large city council seats; and

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 20A-1-206(3) provides that a municipal legislative body
may cancel a race in a local election if the ballot for the race will not include
any contested races or ballot propositions; and

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 20A-1-206(3)(b) further requires that the municipal
legislative body adopt a resolution no later than 20 calendar days before the
scheduled election certifying that the ballot for the race would not include any
contested races or ballot propositions and that the candidate or candidates
are considered elected; and

WHEREAS, there are two open 4-year at-large City Council seats scheduled for
the November 4, 2025 Draper City General Election; and

WHEREAS, following the withdrawal of candidate Jared Turner, the number of
remaining candidates equals the number of open seats, leaving the race
uncontested and the ballot without any contested races or ballot propositions
for the two 4-year at-large seats; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to cancel the race for the 4-year at-large City
Council seats in accordance with Utah law and certify the candidates as
elected.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1. Cancellation of Race. The Draper City Council hereby cancels the
race for the two 4-year at-large City Council seats scheduled for November 4,
2025.
Section 2. Certification of Candidates. The Draper City Council hereby certifies
that the ballot for the race for the two 4-year at-large City Council seats would
not include any contested races or ballot propositions and that Tasha Lowery
and Mike Green are considered elected to the office of Draper City
Councilmember for a 4-year at-large term.

Section 3. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution
shall be severable.

Section 4. Effective Date.
This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.
