53   Councilor Harding mentioned the version shared with the Planning Commission was an older version,
54   not the most recent draft. The second draft clarified that there was no recommended change on the

59   Hanna Salzl, Provo City Planner, presented. The west side of Provo was growing, and residents and city
60   staff had collaborated over the last five years to establish design guidelines to direct future
61   development. Councilor Harding had requested that the Council adopt an intent statement that would
62   (1) reaffirm their commitment to the Westside Development Policies (WDP) and Future Land Use map,
63   (2) request assistance from the Planning Commission in drafting ordinances to accomplish WPD 3.a, (3)
64   change the density of four units per acre from net to gross, and (4) clarify where WDP 3.a-d apply and
65   give examples of desired development patterns.
66
67   Ms. Salzl reviewed the feedback that had been received. There was opposition to increasing density and
68   integration. Landowners wanted the flexibility to decide how to use their land. Feedback from Open City
69   Hall was somewhat supportive. The Planning Commission recommended denial of the statement and
70   recommended the Council encourage housing integration city-wide, not just in southwest Provo. It was
71   universally agreed that Provo needed more housing at a variety of price points. They recommended
72   incentivizing, rather than mandating.
