Welcome and Consent Agenda [00:00:08]
At 2:08pm, Council Chair Mayor Jeff Silvestrini welcomed the Wasatch Front Regional Council
members and guests and introductions were made around the table and online via roll call.
[00:04:16] Mayor Silvestrini noted the gathering of public attendees and their interest in Item 5 on
the agenda, and clarified the action recommended for today’s meeting - releasing the DRAFT
2023-2050 Regional Transportation Plan for public comment. He advised the group that there will
be time for public comment later in the meeting. [00:08:20] ACTION: In order to move the
meeting forward more efficiently, Mayor Silvestrini requested a motion to address the agenda
items out of order. Commissioner Jim Harvey motioned to approve re-ordering the agenda as
necessary, which was seconded by Mayor Mark Shepherd. The affirmative vote was unanimous.
3.
Public Comment [00:09:38]
Mayor Silvestrini exercised the Chair’s prerogative and extended the meeting’s public comment
period for 30 minutes. Attendance sheets listing in-person attendees, denoting those who
provided comments, will be attached to this document of record. The names of the public
attendees who joined the meeting virtually are listed below.
2.
Chair Report [00:10:55]
2a. ACTION: Appointments to WFRC Council and Committees
The WFRC Bylaws require that a Chair and Vice Chair be elected bi-annually at the first regular
meeting in odd number years. At the October 2022 WFRC meeting, WFRC Chair Mayor Jeff
Silvestrini appointed a six-member Nominating Committee to propose a slate of officers for
consideration. The Nominating Committee met in December 2022 and now presents
recommendations for the following: South Jordan City Mayor Dawn Ramsey as Council Chair
and Commissioner Bob Stevenson as Council Vice Chair for 2023-2024. [00:12:30] ACTION: A
motion was made to accept these appointments by Mayor Mark Shepherd and was seconded by
Mayor Troy Walker. Voting in the affirmative was unanimous. At Mayor Ramsey’s request, Mayor
Silvestrini continued to chair this meeting, rather than turning it over to her, as she was attending
remotely. [00:15:47] The WFRC Chair has made appointments of WFRC Members to the
Regional Growth Committee (RGC), Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com), WFRC
Budget Committee, Active Transportation Committee (ATC), and Joint Policy Advisory Committee
(JPAC). Appointments to WFRC and its Committees have also been made by the County
Councils of Governments (COGs) and other appointing authorities that partner with WFRC.
Updated WFRC Council and Committee membership lists were distributed at the meeting and will
be posted on WFRC’s website. [00:17:20] ACTION: Commissioner Bob Stevenson made a
motion that the members of the Council endorse the appointments to WFRC Council and other
Committees as listed. Mayor Dirk Burton seconded the motion and the vote was unanimous. The
newly appointed members briefly introduced themselves.
3.

Public Comment continued [00:20:13]

Chair Report continued [00:59:05]
Mayor Silvestrini reported that several members of WFRC leadership and staff, along with some
partners at Mountainland Association of Governments, attended the National Association of
Regional Councils in Washington DC to learn best practices about working regionally and how to

2b. ACTION: Legislative session report and endorse positions on legislation [01:02:48]
Mayor Silvestrini introduced Miranda Jones Cox, WFRC, who briefed the Council on several
pertinent bills, focusing on key transportation, housing, and growth items, that are being tracked
during this Legislative Session. [01:04:21] Mayor Silvestrini asked Ms. Jones Cox to pause her
presentation for a brief statement from Mayor Erin Mendenhall. [01:07:53] Mayor Silvestrini
turned the time back to Ms. Jones Cox to complete her presentation. She, along with Carlos

recommended positions as discussed. Mayor Troy Walker made the motion and Mayor Dirk
Burton seconded. The position approvals were accepted unanimously.
5.
Regional Growth Committee (RGC) [01:19:35]
5a. ACTION: Release DRAFT 2023-2050 Regional Transportation Plan for public comment
Mayor Silvestrini brought attention to Item 5 and invited Mayor Dawn Ramsey, RGC Chair, to give
a report. Mayor Ramsey briefly reviewed the process for updating the Regional Transportation
Plan (RTP) and then introduced Jory Johner, WFRC, who reported more in-depth on the progress
of the RTP, in preparation for releasing it for public comment. [01:33:15] Mayor Ramsey noted
that there was a good discussion on the RTP during the RGC meeting held on January 19 and
turned the time to Andrew Gruber, WFRC, to summarize that information. [01:37:40] ACTION:
Mayor Ramsey noted that there will be more opportunity for discussion among the Council
members and alternates, and then made a motion to release the draft phased and financially
constrained 2023-2050 Regional Transportation Plan and draft Air Quality Memorandum #41 for
public comment from January 27, 2023 to February 26, 2023. Salt Lake County Councilmember
Aimee Winder Newton seconded the motion. Since UDOT is responsible for the Little Cottonwood
Canyon Environmental Impact Study (EIS), Mayor Silvestrini invited Carlos Braceras, UDOT, to
make comments regarding the RTP. After a request from Commissioner Bob Stevenson, how to
participate in the 30-day public comment process was outlined by Mr. Johner. [01:43:55] Mayor
Monica Zoltanski made a substitute motion to approve the RTP as presented, but remove the
language anticipating the gondola phase of Little Cottonwood Canyon funding. Mayor Jenny
Wilson suggested an amendment to the substitute motion to include removing all additional
funding line items associated with the gondola/Little Cottonwood Canyon, (the entirety of the
transportation-related projects that were identified in the Little Cottonwood Canyon Environmental
Impact Study (EIS) Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3), which Mayor Zoltanski accepted. Mayor
Wilson seconded the substitute motion. There was more discussion among the Council members.
[02:07:45] ACTION: Mayor Silvestrini called for a roll call vote from the 19 members and
designated alternates present, on the substitute motion. There were five affirmative votes and 14
votes against the substitute motion. With no majority, the substitute motion did not carry.
[02:11:18] ACTION: Mayor Silvestrini then called for a roll call vote from the 19 members and
designated alternates present, on the original motion. There were 15 affirmative votes and four
votes against the original motion. With a majority vote, the motion to release the draft phased and
financially constrained 2023-2050 Regional Transportation Plan and draft Air Quality
Memorandum #41 for public comment from January 27, 2023 to February 26, 2023 was
approved.

Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) [02:26:35]
Mayor Mark Shepherd, Trans Com Chair, informed the Council of the items that were discussed in
the Trans Com meeting held on February 16, 2023.
4a. Board Modifications to 2023-2028 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP)
Mayor Shepherd noted that since the last meeting of the Council, WFRC staff received requests
to modify the current 2023-2028 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). The modification
required action from Trans Com (since WFRC was not meeting) and the Utah Transportation
Commission, but did not require a new air quality conformity analysis or a 30-day public comment
period. The modification is included in the meeting materials, and Mayor Shepherd stated that if
there were any questions, Ben Wuthrich, WFRC, would be available to answer them.
4b. ACTION: Board Modifications to 2023-2028 TIP [02:27:05]
Mayor Shepherd then reported that WFRC staff has again received several requests to modify the
current 2023-2028 TIP and this list of projects is also included in the meeting materials. Mayor
Shepherd turned the time to Ben Wuthrich, WFRC, who briefly discussed each project. [02:38:21]
ACTION: Mayor Mark Shepherd made a motion to approve the resolution to modify the
2023-2028 TIP as requested. Mayor Mike Caldwell seconded the motion and the voting was
unanimous in the affirmative.
6.
WFRC Budget Committee [02:38:52]
6a. ACTION: Approve updates to WFRC Accounting and Administrative Policy
Mayor Silvestrini invited Mayor Mike Caldwell, Chair of the WFRC Budget Committee, to give a
report. Mayor Caldwell turned the time to Marian Florence, WFRC, who provided a review of the
updates made to WFRC’s Administrative and Accounting Policy.
WFRC has made the following updates to its financial processes, now reflected in the Policy:
● An Accounting Specialist has joined the WFRC staff, providing increased internal control
and capacity.
● WFRC has upgraded its accounting software to a system specifically designed for
municipalities and local governments.
● Payroll is now processed in house, not by a third party.
● Payments are made by ACH, not by physical check.
Other updates to the language further defining WFRC’s processes, as well as all the components
currently addressed in the existing Policy, have been incorporated into the proposed updated
Policy with updated language. [02:41:40] Mayor Caldwell made a motion to approve the updated
WFRC Accounting and Administrative Policy. Commissioner Bob Stevenson seconded the motion
and the affirmative vote was unanimous.
