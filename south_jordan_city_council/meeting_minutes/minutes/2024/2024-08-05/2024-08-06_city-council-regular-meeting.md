alerted to the lower speed sooner. We also think that people are not aware that this is a
vulnerable area, and similar to a school zone. We believe that if they knew they would be more
likely to honor the speed limit. In conclusion, we would like for the City of South Jordan to be
aware that this area remains a dangerous spot, especially for us seniors, to consider adding

road that borders our over 55 community and to consider alerting drivers to the special nature of
the area, perhaps with additional signs such as slow elderly crossing or something like that. Let’s
make drivers very aware that they need to slow down and why they need to slow down.
