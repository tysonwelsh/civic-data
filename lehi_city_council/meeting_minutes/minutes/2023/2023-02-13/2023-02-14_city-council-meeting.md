7. Consideration of Ordinance #12-2023, approving a Holbrook Farms Area Plan
   Amendment changing the land use map and changing the location of residential units
   located at approximately Hudson Way and Redwood Road.
   Brad Mackay was representing Ivory Development. He stated that this cleans up the Area
   Plan in a way that makes the most sense to develop. This will consolidate all residential to
   the east and keep the west side more industrial.



Lehi City Council Meeting                       2                                 February 14, 2023
    There was discussion regarding the Johnson mink farm deal when the density was granted to
    this area and that moving the density could violate the terms of that agreement. Mr. Mackay
    stated that this would add guaranteed commercial that the current plan does not have.

    There was discussion about the land that UDOT purchased from Ivory as part of the
    Mountain View Corridor and that Ivory was compensated that way for the density.

    The Council discussed the pros and cons of moving the residential units. The number of units
    isn’t increasing, but the location and possibly the intensity. The infrastructure can support it,
    but there was discussion to recommend a traffic study. Having the guarantee of Commercial
    in that area would be beneficial. If the residential units remained on the east side, those future
    residents may be frustrated by their location surrounding industrial and they would be
    disconnected from the amenities on the east side of Redwood. Concerns with transfer of
    included adding more units into an already dense area.

    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to deny this amendment and include the findings that
                it’s not in conformance with the General Plan, that it’s not consistent with the
                nearby area properties, and it will have an adverse effect on the surrounding
                property. Councilor Albrecht seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, No; Councilor Hancock, No;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, No. The motion failed with 2 in favor and
    3 opposed.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to approve Ordinance #12-2023, the Holbrook Farms
                Area Plan Amendment changing the land use map and changing the location of
                residential units located at approximately Hudson Way and Redwood Road;
                including the Development Review Committee and Planning Commission
                Comments; noting that the Planning Commission recommended approval and
                noting that a traffic study be required in the next step of the process. Councilor
                Southwick seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, No; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, No; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed with 3 in favor
    and 2 opposed.
