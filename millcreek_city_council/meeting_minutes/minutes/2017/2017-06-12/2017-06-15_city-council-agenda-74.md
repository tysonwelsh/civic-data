                                                                    APPROVED 6/26/17

                                                                Minutes of the
                                                             Millcreek City Council
                                                            Thursday, June 15, 2017



Councilmembers present

Jeff Silvestrini – Mayor
Silvia Catten – Council District 1
Dwight Marchant – Council District 2
Cheri Jackson – Council District 3
Bev Uipi – Council District 4

Other present

Leslie Van Frank – Acting Recorder

        1. CALL TO ORDER: Mayor Silvestrini called the meeting to order at 5:36 PM.
        2. OTHER MATTERS: The purpose of this meeting is to conduct interview(s) for the position of
           City Manager / Administrator.

        MOTION by Councilmember Catten to close the session to discuss the character, professional
competence, or physical or mental health of an individual, pending or reasonable imminent litigation, or
the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property, as provided by Utah Code Annotated §52-4-205,
seconded by Councilmember Marchant. Motion passed unanimously by roll call vote with members
voting as follows:
        Councilmember Uipi – Aye
        Councilmember Catten – Aye
        Mayor Silvestrini – Aye
        Councilmember Jackson – Aye
        Councilmember Marchant – Aye

        The meeting was closed to the public at 5:36 PM.

        The meeting was reopened at 8:10 PM.

        3. Adjournment

       Motion to adjourn was made at 8:10 PM by Councilmember Uipi, seconded by
Councilmember Catten. Motion passed unanimously.
