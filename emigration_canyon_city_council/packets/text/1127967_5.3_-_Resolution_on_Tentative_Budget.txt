                                RESOLUTION #2024 – 05 – 01
     A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR THE CITY OF
EMIGRATION CANYON, UTAH, FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2025 BEGINNING JULY 1, 2024
    AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2025 AND TO SET A TIME AND PLACE FOR A PUBLIC
  HEARING ON JUNE 25, 2024 AT 7:00 PM AT FIRE STATION 119, 5025 EMIGRATION
    CANYON RD, SALT LAKE CITY, UTAH, AND ELECTRONICALLY VIA ZOOM.
       WHEREAS, State law requires the filing of a tentative budget for each municipality
located within the State of Utah; and
      WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon City Council ("Council”) met in a regular session on
May 28, 2024, to consider, among other things, adopting a fiscal year 2025 tentative budget; and,
       WHEREAS, the tentative budget complies with the requirements set out in Utah Code
Ann. § 10-6-111; and
         WHEREAS, the budget shall be reviewed, considered, and tentatively adopted by the
governing body and may be amended or revised in such manner as is considered advisable prior
to final adoption.
       WHEREAS, the Council shall set a time and date of June 25, 2024 at 7:00 PM to hold a
public hearing concerning the fiscal year 2025 budget and FY 2024 amended budget and will
publish public notice of the hearing as the requirements set out in Utah Code Ann. 10-6 et seq; and
    NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE OF
EMIGRATION CANYON, STATE OF UTAH, as follows:
        SECTION 1. That Emigration Canyon, hereby adopts the following tentative budget for
the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025 attached here to as Exhibit 1.
       SECTION 2. That Emigration Canyon shall hold a public hearing on June 25, 2024 at 7:00
PM concerning the fiscal year 2025 budget and FY 2024 amended budget and will publish public
notice of the hearing at least seven days before the hearing as the requirements set out in Utah
Code Ann. 10-6 et seq.
        SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon the passage
thereof.
      PASSED AND APPROVED by the Emigration Canyon City Council, Utah, this 28th day
of May in the year 2024.


                                             EMIGRATION CANYON



                                             Joe Smolka, Mayor
