MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 20-02-01 DATE: February 25, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING A LETTER TO THE UNITED STATES ARMY SPECIAL
OPERATIONS COMMAND INVITING THEM TO PERFORM TRAINING
EXERCISES IN MAGNA FROM MAY 11, 2020 TO MAY 15, 2020
RECITALS
A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.
B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.
| C. The United State Army Special Operation Command (“USASOC”) is an operational
command group of the United States Army tasked to defend the United States
pursuant to the US Constitution.
D. USASOC desires to conduct a training exercise in Magna from May 11th to May 15th
2020.
E. USASOC policy requires a letter from the jurisdiction in which they desire to train
inviting them to conduct training.
F. The proposed training exercise allows USASOC personnel to gain tactical and
technical efficiencies that improve operational readiness and safety.
G. Magna desires to support USASOC’s training effort and views such training exercises
as necessary to supporting the defense of our nation.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council approves this resolution, and
authorizes the Mayor to sign the attached letter, hereinafter known as Attachment “A,”
inviting the United States Army Special Operation Command to conduct their training
exercises from May 11" to May 15" 2020 in Magna.

SUBJECT: Proposed Training Exercise in Magna
Dear Colonel Jeffers:

On behalf of the Magna Metro Township, we invite you to conduct urban military
training in our municipality from May 11-15, 2020.

I understand that Staff Sergeant Aaron Del Valle of your command has met with Steve
Ball of the Unified Fire Authority (UFA) and Sergeant Mike Ikemiyashiro of the Unified Police
Department (UPD). These gentlemen have been advised that the proposed training will be
coordinated with and approved by the UPD and UFA. We are confident that this detailed
coordination with the UPD, UFA and other appropriate agencies will result in a safe and
successful training. We understand that all liability for your personnel and their actions during
training rest with the U.S. Army.

Magna supports the soldiers of our armed forces as they prepare for the challenges ahead.
We look forward to your group having a safe and productive training exercise in our
Municipality.

If you require further assistance, please do not hesitate to contact Steve Ball at (801) 381-
9217 or Sergeant Mike Ikemiyashiro at (801) 330-7986.
