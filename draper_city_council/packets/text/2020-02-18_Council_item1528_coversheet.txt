From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: February 18, 2020
Re: Personnel Policy 7070 -- Fitness Facility Comments: The fitness facility in the police wing of city hall is nearing completion.  Policy 7070 -- Fitness Facility addresses the following issues:
1. The facility is for the use of Draper City full and part-time employees and elected officials only.
2.  Users need to tour the facility and receive some training before using the facility.
3. Users will need to sign a form acknowledging they have received a copy of this policy and agree to assume the risk of using the facility.
4. Use of the facility, except by on-duty patrol officers and fire personnel, is not considered "time worked" and will not be compensated by the city or covered by workers compensation. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Res. 20-11, Fitness Facility 2/13/2020 Cover Memo
