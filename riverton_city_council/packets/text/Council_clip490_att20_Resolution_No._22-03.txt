                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-03

             A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL
      APPROVING AMENDMENTS TO THE 2021-2022 FISCAL YEAR BUDGET

       WHEREAS, the Budget for the 2021-2022 Fiscal Year was adopted on June 15, 2021, by
Resolution No. 21-38; and

       WHEREAS, after careful consideration and scrutiny it has been determined by the
Administration that amendments are required to more accurately reflect actual collections and
newly projected trends; and

        WHEREAS, the Administration has also determined that several expenditure lines in the
budget corresponding to the changes in revenue should also be adjusted reflecting operational and
capital needs.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

      1.   The Riverton City Budget for the 2021-2022 Fiscal Year be amended according to the
           attached detail identified as “Exhibit A”

      2.   This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of
January, 2022 by the following vote:
