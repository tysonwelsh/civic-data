Meeting Minutes August 31, 2021
A. The Salt Lake County (the “County”) recently acquired a parcel of real 
property in South Salt Lake City, Utah, where the Municipal Building 
Authority of Salt Lake County (the “MBA”) plans to build a new library (the 
“Granite Property”).
B. The Salt Lake County Municipal Building Authority (the “MBA”) 
intends to finance the construction of library facilities at the Granite Property 
through the issuance of municipal bonds.
C. Therefore, the County has agreed to transfer title of the Granite 
Property to the MBA.
D. The Salt Lake County Library Board has approved transfer of the 
Granite Property to the MBA.
E. In furtherance of the above stated public purpose, the County and the 
MBA have negotiated an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement that contains the 
terms and conditions governing the transfer.
F. It has been determined that the best interests of the MBA and the 
general public will be served by entering into the Interlocal Cooperation 
Agreement.  The execution of said Interlocal Cooperation Agreement will 
comply with all applicable state statutes and County ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, the Governing Board of the Municipal Building 
Authority of Salt Lake County resolves that the Interlocal Cooperation 
Agreement between the County and the MBA is hereby approved, and that 
the President is authorized to execute said Interlocal Cooperation Agreement 
and any additional documents necessary to effectuate the transfer of the 
Granite Property to the MBA.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 31st day of August, 2021.
