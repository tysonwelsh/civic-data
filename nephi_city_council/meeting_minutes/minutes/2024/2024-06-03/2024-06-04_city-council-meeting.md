PUBLC HEARING:

PRAYER: Councilor Skip Worwood

Ashton told those present that Sandstone provides rural mental health services. They offer individual
and group counseling and medication management. He stated that the Nephi community had given a
lot to him, and he is now wanting to give back to the community.

motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

Agreement. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous

affecting parcels XB002129-1121 and XB00-212-1122. The annexed property was classified in the R1-

       Yes: Jeramie Callaway, JD Parady, Skip Worwood, Shari Cowan
       Absent: Travis Worwood

ELECTRIC REVENUE BOND RESOLUTION 06-04-2024 ADOPTED:

   Marcus Keller representing Crews & Associates reported to the Council that through the bidding
process for the electric revenue bonds that will be used to fund the 138 KV power line, the city received
five bids. City staff and Crews & Associates’ staff members reviewed the proposals and chose Bank of
Utah for the bonds. Bank of Utah was chose for their interest rate of 4.79% and for the most flexible call
features. Calling a bond allows the city to refinance the bond within a certain time frame. Mr. Keller
stated that if interest rates decrease in the future, then refinancing the bonds would be an option. He
stated that it is highly anticipated that the city will receive a federal grant for the project and may want
to use the grant to refund some of the bond principal.
   Councilor Parady moved to adopt Resolution 06-04-2024-$8,500,000 Electric Revenue Bonds, Series
2024. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion carried on the following vote.
         Yes: Jeramie Callaway, JD Parady, Skip Worwood, Shari Cowan
         Absent: Travis Worwood

USED BUCKET TRUCK PURCHASE APPROVED:

      City Arborist Gerratt Bethers reported to the Council that he and the Power Superintendent Rust
Finlinson have been searching for a replacement bucket truck to be used by him for tree care and by the
electric department as a backup truck. Mr. Bethers recommended to the council the purchase of a 1998
Freightliner FL80 bucket truck out of Twin Falls, Idaho. The recommended truck is very similar to the
truck he has been using. Many of the parts would be interchangeable. The truck only has 44,000 miles
showing and the hour meter only reads 10,348. He had personally checked out the truck and felt it was
in excellent condition. He had negotiated a price with the seller of $38,900.
   Councilor Skip Worwood moved to approve the purchase of the 1998 Freightliner bucket truck for
$38,900. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

 CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES:

       Mr. Atkinson and Mayor Seely reported on the UMPA trip to Glen Canyon Dam that they had
        recently gone on. Seely reported that UMPA receives about 23% of their power from the dam.
        He stated that the small mouth bass and other issues have decreased the amount power being
        generated at the dam. He said that there is a fine balance between managing recreational
        needs and power generation.
       Mr. Atkinson reported on an individual who has built a garage onto his home without obtaining
        a building permit. The individual was issued a red tag notice from the building inspector to
        cease work on the garage. The red tag was ignored. Mr. Atkinson said that there is a provision
        in the code to allows on a non-conforming use a rare and unique exemption for the council to
        approve the non-conforming use. Attorney Kasey Wright stated that this will not be a trend
        that these types of issues will be coming before the council, but this one is a unique situation.
        Mr. Atkinson said that at next week’s meeting the individual will be presenting his case before
        the council.
       Councilor Cowan complimented Mr. Atkinson on his presentation at the R6 regional summit.
       Mayor Seely invited Trent Brown to introduce himself. Mr. Brown manages the Nephi and
        Fillmore civil engineers’ team for Sunrise Engineering. He stated that Sunrise is excited to have a
        new office in Nephi City and plan on being more visible at council meetings in the future.
       Councilor Skip Worwood said that the Community Night Out event was very successful.
       Councilor Parady suggested that they end the Community Night Out event earlier at 7:30 and
        that they have more food trucks.
       Councilor Callaway said that he had had a medical emergency last week and he wanted to
        express publicly his appreciation to Central Valley Medical Center. He only had to drive two
        blocks to a wonderful hospital that serves our community so well.
       Councilor Skip Worwood suggested cancelling the regular council meeting scheduled for August
        6th because of some fair activities happening that evening that the council should attend and
        support.

    1.   Mr. Atkinson suggested to the council that he draft a policy that delegates sewer connection
         determinations to staff instead of the council. The council directed him to draft such a policy
         and to bring it back to the council for approval.

    2.    Mr. Atkinson asked the council if they would like to engage Larson & Company for auditing
         services for the fiscal year 2024 audit. The council directed staff to allow the mayor to sign the
         engagement letter with Larson & Company.

    3. Mayor Seely expressed frustration with Wall Consultant Group who the city has contracted to
       design and engineer the Crimson Flats development. Mr. Seely said they continue to fail in
       meeting deadlines. Mr. Atkinson said that they had finally sent the design late in the afternoon.
       Mr. Atkinson said that he will put pressure on them to perform better than they have
       demonstrated so far.

    4. Mr. Atkinson said that he has sent bid documents out for the land behind McDonalds for a hotel
       site. He reported that he thinks that he has three viable bidders.

    5. The council discussed an individual that is doing business in an accessory building. Mr. Atkinson
       said that per our code, doing business is not allowed in an accessory building.

    6. Councilor Callaway questioned whether the builder who is building a structure on 200 East has
       been told that twin homes cannot have internal accessory dwelling units. He wanted to make
       sure that staff communicates clearly upfront when builders pull their building permits of
       allowed uses.

    7. Mayor Seely asked what we are requiring on geotechnical purposes. The council answered that
       we only are suggesting to the builders that they follow what the geotechnical report suggests.
