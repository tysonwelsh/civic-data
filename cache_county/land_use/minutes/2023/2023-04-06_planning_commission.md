#4 Cherry Peak Ski Area Conditional Use Permit Amendment 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the Cherry Peak Ski Area Conditional Use Permit Amendment. 
Anderson commented that staff is still willing to work with Cherry Peak Ski Area to achieve their goals 
while following their current CUP conditions and meet County standards. 
Commissioners discussed possible denial of the amendment due to the applicant not being able to 
answer questions raised at the previous meeting in October. 
Parker motioned to deny the Cherry Peak Ski Area Conditional Use Permit Amendment based on the six 
findings of facts; Watterson seconded; Passed 4, 0.

#6 UT1 Beaver Mountain Tower Conditional Use Permit 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for the UT1 Beaver Mountain Tower Conditional Use Permit. 
Commissioners discussed the location and the height of the tower. 
Troy Benson commented as the representative for Verizon Wireless and needing 60 feet for line of sight 
needs and possible co-location of other carriers. 
Daugs asked about the coverage area. 
Mr. Benson commented that in more rural areas, like this, possible 2 miles. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed adding more towers in the canyon. 
Sands motioned to approve the UT1 Beaver Mountain Tower Conditional Use Permit based on the 8 
conditions and 4 conclusions; Watterson seconded; Passed 4, 0. 
05:54:00 
#7 Dog Boarding Project Conditional Use Permit 
Zetterquist reviewed the staff report for a Dog Board Project Conditional Use Permit. 
Watkins reviewed the site map of the property. 
Ekaterina Brusko commented on wanting to run a small boarding and training kennel without a lot of 
noise and disturbance to the surrounding area, the location of the building from the property line, and 
asked about using recycled asphalt for the entrance of the property. 
Watkins reviewed the road requirement. 
Ms. Brusko asked about the detailed site plan versus a survey. 
Zetterquist reviewed what a site plan is. 
Ms. Brusko asked about the location of the building and the setback requirement. 
Sands suggested she look into the variance request for the setback requirement. 
Watterson motioned to approve the Dog Boarding Project Conditional Use Permit with the 10 conditions 
and 2 conclusions; Sands seconded; Passed 4, 0.
