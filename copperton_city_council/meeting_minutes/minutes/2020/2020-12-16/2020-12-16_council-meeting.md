                                  RESOLUTION #2020-12-01

                                      December 16, 2020

A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S FINAL 2021 BUDGET

       WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, on October 21, 2020, Copperton adopted a tentative budget for the 2021 fiscal
year; and

      WHEREAS, Copperton has provided notice pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-113
concerning the tentative budget and of the date, time, and place of a public hearing on the same;
and

       WHEREAS, Copperton has made said tentative budget available for public inspection for
a period of at least ten days prior to the public hearing in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-
6-112; and

        WHEREAS, on December 16, 2020 at 6:30 pm, Copperton held said public hearing on said
tentative budget in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-114.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

       1.        That the tentative budget adopted on October 21, 2020, as amended (if
amended) and attached hereto, be and hereby is adopted as the Final Budget of Copperton for
the 2021 fiscal year.

       2.       This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.

ADOPTED AND APPROVED by majority vote at a duly called meeting of the Metro Township
Council on this 16th day of December, 2020.

Draft Arborist Contract

             Nathan Bracken stated he prepared a draft contract for the arborist but had two
outstanding matters:

   1. He would like to include the RFP in the contract, but the original RFP anticipated a park
      master plan being done by August, 2020. Since that date has passed, a new deadline
      needs to be included.
   2. He would also like a copy of the fee schedule from the arborist to include as an exhibit in
      the contract.

The contract also needs to be reviewed by the arborist for his approval.

     Mayor Clayton stated the arborist is looking to start his work in February. Council
Member Bailey could work out the timeline with the arborist.

      Mr. Bracken stated the Council could include a tentative date subject to change. He
asked when the Council would like to have a draft contract ready for their approval.

COVID Update

                 Mayor Clayton stated Copperton received 27 mask orders and he has sent out
approximately 240 masks. They still have a healthy supply in all colors and sizes. He will submit
a final report to the County on the use of funds.

              Council Member Stitzer asked about agencies that previously asked for funding,
and also about requests for hand sanitizer.

               Mayor Clayton reviewed the entities that received Copperton funding and noted
that they did not have the funding for hand sanitizer.

              Nathan Bracken stated he has heard rumor of additional funds being available
from the County after the first of the year.

              Mayor Clayton stated if additional funds are available, it would likely be about the
same $23,000. He has also heard rumor that Washington may extend the deadline for use of the
funds. Everything is up in the air.

               Mr. Bracken stated if additional funding came without liability concerns for the
township, it might be able to help more small businesses.

        Legislative Research Committee

                Nathan Bracken reviewed the efforts to charge a Municipal Energy
Telecommunication Tax. Currently the metros are not able to charge such a tax. The Utah
League of Cities and Towns voted to approve this legislation. Senator Karen Mayne will sponsor
the legislation. The League has been asked to change its bylaws so the metros can be members.
