City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On July 20, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1496, amending the
official zoning map of Draper City. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper
City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 21st day of
July 2021. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
