Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order

    Motion made to elect Commissioner Everett to chair the meeting. Commissioner Ellis
    seconded. All in favor.

    Commission Everett welcomed everyone to the meeting. (Commissioner Nielsen came in late
    and took over the meeting on item 3).

2) Discussion of Thanksgiving Point Land Use Concept Plan
John Bankhead with Gardner, Ryan Thomas with Stack updated the Commissioners on the
Thanksgiving Point Land Use Concept. .

-The City Council was not interested in 5,000 units, but and would consider 2,000 units.
Working with Staff.
-Better understanding with infrastructure costs that would be brought in off site.
-Disconnect with development team and what Staff was comfortable with and have met with

Staff and UTA weekly. Staff wants to densify for the benefit of Thanksgiving Point to keep them
financially viable.

3) Discussion of “A Time to Build” chapters 3 & 4
       Commissioner Nielsen joined the meeting when item 3 started.

        Commissioner Eyre made a motion to have Commissioner Nielsen chair the
        remainder of the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded. All in favor.

Commissioner Eyre asked about the last item. Staff and the Commissioners discussed the item.

The Commissioners and Staff discussed the pros and cons of the assigned reading.

Chapter 5 and 6 assigned for April WS.

4) Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Everett moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Ellis seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:35 p.m.
