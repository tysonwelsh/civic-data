PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Juab School District Superintendent Rick Robins. Mr. Robins told
the council that he has taken the new position as Canyons School District Superintendent. He expressed
his appreciation to Nephi City for partnering with the school district on several different projects.
Councilor Kent Jones presented Mr. Robins with a T-shirt and a letterman’s jacket. Mayor Nielson
expressed his appreciation and wished him well in his new job. Councilor Seely also expressed his
appreciation for the influence he had on the youth of the community and especially his own children.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes of four public hearings held on 05-19-20, the regular council meeting held on 5-19-20, work
session minutes 5-19-20, work session minutes 5-26-20, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 6-
02-20 and the sale of four burial rights to George and Jill Jackson. Councilor Worwood seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSES APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones reviewed and moved to approve the business license application of Steele the
Moment Videography. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

  Mayor Nielson opened the meeting up for public comment. Alex Johnson asked the council if the
curb, gutter and sidewalk requirement for new construction could be waived for him and his son Jared
who have built homes on the west side of town where there is an irrigation ditch in front of the
property. City Attorney Kasey Wright suggested that this issue be put on a work session agenda for
further study and discussion.

commercial and personal storage units. The development property is on the southern edge of the city
to the west of the freeway and the landowners are requesting that the annexation area map be changed
to a level 1. Councilor Ostler said that the council had discussed the issue last week in a work session
and the sewer issue was better understood. Councilor Memmott said that he better understands the
issues with the utilities on the property and he was thankful to have first changed it to a level 2 in order
to take the necessary time and effort to understand the issue better. Councilor Ostler moved to change

UDOT STATEWIDE UTILITY LICENSE AGREEMENT APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a Statewide Utility License Agreement that assists in expediting
the approval of UDOT permits for operating, constructing, and maintaining utility lines within state
highway rights-of-way. Councilor Worwood moved to ratify the Mayor signing the agreement.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 06-02-2020- PUBLIC SAFETY TIER II RETIREMENT EMPLOYER PICK-UP:

  Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that in the legislative session this year, police retirement
funding for Tier II employees was increased. Local governments have the option of funding this increase
or passing the increase on to employees. Up to this point, Nephi City has covered police retirement
100%. The city currently has four police officers who are Tier II members. If the council chooses to pick-
up the employee portion of the increase, the total contribution increase would be a 4.27%. The FY21
annual dollar amount increase would total $9,487.
   Councilor Jones said he was originally against paying for the increase but after studying the issue
further he better understood the change and agreed with the resolution. Councilor Jones moved to
adopt Resolution 06-02-2020: A RESOLUTION OF NEPHI CITY FOR THE PURPOSE OF PICKING UP AND
PAYING THE REQUIRED EMPLOYEE CONTRIBUTION FOR MEMBERS OF THE NEW PUBLIC SAFETY TIER II
CONTRIBUTORY UTAH RETIREMENT SYSTEM. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.

C-PACE AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that last year the city designated the parcel with the CRA hotel
project as a C-PACE District. A state law then allows this parcel to be eligible for financing from a third-
party lender that helps to install energy efficient appliances, equipment, and fixtures. The savings from
these energy efficient items is then used to pay back the financing from a third-party lender. Once a
local district was established, an agreement was made with the state Office of Economic Development
(OED).
   Since that time, there has been a change with OED and for the local district to be effective, a new
agreement must be made with the company who had previously contracted with OED to help find
financing for eligible projects. This company is Sustainable Real Estate Solutions, Inc. or SRS. SRS would
now like to enter into an agreement directly with Nephi City to assist the local hotel project with finding
financing for energy efficient items. Councilor Jones moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the C-PACE
agreement. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

jurisdictions in the policing of protests be held because of the killing of George Floyd by Minneapolis
policeman.
