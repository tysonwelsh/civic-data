              BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, UTAH COUNTY, UTAH
                          MINUTES OF PUBLIC MEETING
                      COMMISSION CONFERENCE ROOM 2300
                 OF THE UTAH COUNTY ADMINISTRATION BUILDING
                              June 9, 2017 – 9:30 A.M.
BOARD MEMBERS PRESENT:                                      ALSO PRESENT:
COMMISSIONER WILLIAM C. LEE, CHAIR                          Jeffrey R. Buhman, Utah County Attorney
COMMISSIONER GREG GRAVES, VICE-CHAIR                        David Shawcroft, Civil Div. Chief Deputy Attorney
ELECTRONICALLY PRESENT:                                     Don Nay, Utah County Public Works Assoc. Director
COMMISSIONER NATHAN IVIE                                    Renée Caron, Clerk/Auditor Administrative Assistant

Commissioner Lee called the meeting to order at 9:34 A.M. and welcomed those present. The following matters were
discussed:
                                                 AGENDA
1.    APPROVE AND SET A DATE, TIME AND LOCATION FOR A CLOSED MEETING FOR A
      STRATEGY SESSION TO DISCUSS PENDING OR REASONABLY IMMINENT LITIGATION
      (Suggested for today’s date, at this location, to be held immediately) (Announce the vote of each
      commissioner)

                 Commissioner Graves made the motion to set a date, time, and location for a
                 closed meeting for a strategy session to discuss pending or reasonably imminent
                 litigation to take place immediately in Room 2300 of the County Administration
                 Building. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Ivie and carried with the
                 following vote:
                                                                                   AYE: William C. Lee
                                                                                          Greg Graves
                                                                                          Nathan Ivie
                                                                                   NAY: None

The meeting recessed at 9:35 A.M. to go into a closed session, and the public meeting reconvened at 10:35 A.M.
Commissioner Lee excused Commissioner Ivie from the meeting, and noted Robert’s Rules of Order would be waived
for the purpose of making motions.
                  Commissioner Graves made the motion to adjourn the June 9, 2017 meeting of
                  the Utah County Board of Commissioners. The motion was seconded by
                  Commissioner Lee and carried with the following vote:
                                                                                    AYE: William C. Lee
                                                                                            Greg Graves
                                                                                    NAY: None

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:35 A.M. The minutes of the June 9, 2017 special
Commission meeting were approved as transcribed on June 20, 2017.
