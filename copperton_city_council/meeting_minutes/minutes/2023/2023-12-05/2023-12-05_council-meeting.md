RESOLUTION NO. 2023-12-01                                             Date: December 5, 2021

        A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP CERTIFYING THE
        2023 MUNICIPAL GENERAL ELECTION RESULTS

                                          RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) 2023 municipal general election was
held on November 21, 2023; and

WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council (“Council”) serves as the Board of
Canvassers for Copperton (“Board”) pursuant to Utah Code § 20A-4-301(2)(a); and

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 20A-4-301(2)(b) requires the Board to meet and canvas the election
returns no sooner than seven days and no later than fourteen days after the election; and

WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Clerk’s Office, Election Division, received and counted the
election returns for each voting precinct; and

WHEREAS, the Board met on December 5, 2023, to canvass the returns of the 2023 municipal
general election; and

WHEREAS, the Board publicly canvassed the returns and determined from the returns the vote
of each voting precinct for each candidate.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE RESOLVED by the Council effective immediately:

1.    The Copperton Metro Township Council, as the official Board of Canvassers, accepts and
     approves the attached election report for the 2023 Municipal General Election for the
     Copperton Metro Township.

2.   The following candidates are declared to have received the number of votes indicated.

                      Councilmember At-Large Seat A – Four Year Term

               Kathleen Ray Bailey                                       118

                        Councilmember At-Large Seat B – Four Year Term

               Sean Clayton                                               98

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-12-02                                              Date: December 5, 2021

       A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S FINAL
       2024 BUDGET

                                           RECITALS

WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political subdivision
of the state of Utah; and

WHEREAS, on November 15, 2023, Copperton adopted a tentative budget for the 2024 fiscal
year; and

WHEREAS, Copperton provided notice pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-3c-202 and 10-6-113
concerning the tentative budget and of the date, time, and place of a public hearing on the same;
and

WHEREAS, Copperton made the tentative budget available for public inspection for a period of
at least ten (10) days prior to the public hearing in accordance with Utah Code §§ 10-3c-202 and
10-6-112; and

WHEREAS, on December 5, 2023, Copperton held the public hearing on the 2024 budget in
accordance with Utah Code § 10-6-114.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

1. That the tentative budget adopted on November 15, 2023, and attached to this resolution, is
   adopted as the final budget of Copperton for the 2024 fiscal year.

2. The Copperton budget officer is directed and authorized to file the final budget with the Utah
   State Auditor within thirty (30) days of the date of this resolution pursuant to Utah Code §
   10-6-118.

3. This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 5th day of December 2023.

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-12-03                                             Date: December 5, 2021

       AN ORDINANCE OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING THE FEE 2024 FEE SECHDULE

                                           RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) contracts with the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (“MSD”) to provide planning and development, business licensing, and
code enforcement services (the “Services”); and
WHEREAS, Section 3.42.060 of the Copperton Metro Code states that all “fees or charges for
services provided … shall be set by the council;” and
WHEREAS, the MSD board approved the attached 2024 fee schedule for the Services the MSD
provides; and
WHEREAS, the Copperton Council desires to adopt the attached 2024 fee schedule.
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIPCOUNCIL
that effectively immediately:

1. The attached 2024 fee schedule is adopted: and

2. Staff are instructed to file this resolution and the attached fee schedule with the Copperton
