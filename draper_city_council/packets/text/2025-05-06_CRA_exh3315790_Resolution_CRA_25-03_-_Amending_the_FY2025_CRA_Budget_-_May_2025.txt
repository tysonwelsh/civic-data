                            RESOLUTION NO. CRA 25-03

      A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE ADOPTED BUDGET OF THE
      COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AGENCY OF DRAPER CITY FOR THE
      FISCAL YEAR 2024-2025.

        WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City has adopted
Resolution No. CRA 24-02 which adopted the final budget for the fiscal year beginning
July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025, in accordance with the requirements of the
Utah Code Annotated (UCA 10-6-128); and

      WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency Board of Draper City wishes
to amend the fiscal year 2024-2025 budget; and

     WHEREAS, a public hearing to consider the appropriations has been noticed
and held and all interested persons were heard, for or against the appropriations;
and

       WHEREAS, the Community Reinvestment Agency Board of Draper City hereby
finds this action in the best interest of the public’s health, safety and general welfare.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT
AGENCY OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Purpose. The purpose of this Resolution is to amend the budget
of the Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City, as approved and finalized by
Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City Resolution No. RDA 24-02.

       Section 2. Adoption of Amendments. The budget amendments attached
hereto and made a part of this Resolution shall be, and the same hereby are adopted
and incorporated into the budget of Community Reinvestment Agency of Draper City,
Utah for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2023 and ending June 30, 2024, in accordance
with the requirements of the Utah Code Annotated (UCA 10-6-128).

       Section 3. Filing of copies of the Budget Amendments. The Budget Officer is
authorized and directed to certify and file a copy of said budget amendments in the
office of the City Recorder which amendments shall be available for public inspection.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this

      Section 5. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to

immediately upon its passage.
