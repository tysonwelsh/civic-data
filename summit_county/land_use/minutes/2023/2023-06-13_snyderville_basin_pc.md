2.
Public hearing and action regarding a Conditional Use Permit for expansion of a non-conforming structure, 5672 N. Kingsford Avenue, Summit County, Utah; Parcel SMT-A-100 (0.15acres). Applicant: Stefan Brutsch. Owner: Christopher Lee. Project #23-031. Administrative review. Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior Planner.
(6:00 P.M.)
Tiffanie N. Robinson, Senior Planner, presented to the Commission background information, process, and a recommendation regarding a Conditional Use Permit for the expansion of a non-conforming structure.
Commission Vice Chair Conabee opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:08 P.M.
Theron Miller was called to speak.
Commission Vice Chair Conabee closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:09 P.M.
John Kucera made a motion to approve the Conditional Use Permit according to the findings of fact, conclusion of law and conditions of approval as found in the staff report. Makena Hawley seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Tyann Mooney, Bruce Carmichael.
