 Public Comment

Chair MacKay read the public comment preamble and opened the public comment period.

Joshua Johns, a Provo resident, addressed the Council regarding his two daughters, Amelia and Sophia,
who he stated have been missing since July 2025 following an alleged abduction by their mother. He
explained that he is the sole custodial parent and has expended significant personal resources, including
hiring private investigators and attorneys, and attempting to work with federal authorities to locate
them. Mr. Johns expressed concern with his interactions with the Provo Police Department, stating that
he felt the responses he received were dismissive and that efforts to locate his daughters had been
insufficient. He further stated that, despite obtaining a court-issued writ of assistance directing law
enforcement to aid in locating the children, he was informed the matter was civil in nature and no
action would be taken. Mr. Johns initially requested that the Council encourage local law enforcement
to take additional action and follow established procedures for missing children cases but indicated that
his confidence in that approach had diminished. He concluded by asking the Council and the public to
help raise awareness of his daughters’ situation and to share information through community networks
and social media in hopes of assisting in their safe return.

Glen Johns, a Springville resident and grandfather of Amelia and Sophia, addressed the Council
regarding the ongoing absence of his granddaughters, who he stated have been missing for
approximately nine months. He shared concerns about the responses he and his family have received
from local and federal officials, noting they had been told the situation was a civil matter, that no laws
had been broken, and that the children were believed to be with their mother. He expressed frustration
that, despite these responses, the children have not been seen or heard from during that time. Mr.

 Action Agenda

 1           A resolution approving the appropriation of $96,547 to pay for three new firefighters and
             their equipment between May and June for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026 (26-024)
             00:07:14

       Motion:        An implied motion to approve Resolution 2026-7, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

Fire Chief Headman presented a request to the Council for a one-time appropriation of $96,547.60 to
hire three seasonal firefighters for the months of June and July. He explained that the funding would
cover both personnel costs and necessary equipment to prepare the new hires for service. Chief
Headman stated that adding one firefighter per shift would help address current staffing needs and
improve overall operational readiness. He further explained that hiring the firefighters at this time
would allow them to be trained together through the department’s eight-week training program,
avoiding the need to run multiple training sessions later in the year. Additionally, he noted that the
department has recently completed a hiring process and currently has qualified candidates available but
expressed concern that delays could result in losing additional candidates to other fire departments, as
has already occurred with two individuals.

Chair MacKay opened public comment. with none, she closed public comment and invited a council
discussion.

Councilor Whitlock clarified for the benefit of the public that the proposed hiring of additional
firefighters would not increase ongoing costs. He explained that the positions would be funded through
reductions in overtime expenses, resulting in a budget-neutral impact over time.

Chair MacKay called for a vote.

       Vote:          The motion passed 7:0 with Councilors Bogdin, Christensen, Garrett, Hoban, MacKay,
                      Whipple, and Whitlock in favor.
