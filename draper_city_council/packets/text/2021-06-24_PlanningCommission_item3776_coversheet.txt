From: Travis Van Ekelenburg
CC:  Date: June 24, 2021
Re: Olson Residence Zoning Map Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately 1.84 acres located on the west side of Fort Street, at approximately 13764 S & 13770 S Fort Street. The properties are currently zoned RA1 . The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map Amendment be approved to rezone the 13764 S property from the RA1 zoning designation to the RA2 zoning designation as the applicant would like to create a 20,000 ft² lot for the existing home. The findings are as follows: Positive Recommendation:
1.     That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the zoning text and maps of the DCMC.
2.     That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
Negative Recommendation:
1.     That this map amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1). ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 6/17/2021 Cover Memo
