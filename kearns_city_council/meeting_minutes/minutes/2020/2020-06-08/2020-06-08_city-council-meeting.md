CARES Act Update

               Mayor Kelly Bush stated Salt Lake County received CARES Act Funding from
the federal government and is distributing it to municipalities and townships within the County. In
order for Kearns to receive this funding it needs to sign an agreement. The agreement is currently
being reviewed by Paul Ashton, Kearns Metro Township Legal Counsel. She has heard that
some townships have decided not to participate.

This money is destinated to help with the impact of the COVID-19 virus. It is not a loan, but a
grant. The money can only be used for certain things. If the funds are not used as directed by
the federal government, then they will need to be paid back. The Municipal Service District (MSD)
will be the holder of the funds. The funds will be appropriated in destinated accounts for each
township.

               Council Member Snow stated what the funds can be used for is very limited.

              Mayor Bush stated the Council will be responsible to distribute these funds and
need to make sure the funds are only distributed to people who have not already received funding.
She then went through the requirements for the use of these funds.

              Council Member Schaeffer stated the Kearns Metro Township needed to take
advantage of these funds in order to help the small businesses in the Kearns area.

               Council Member Peterson asked if there was an application form for people to
fill out.

               Mayor Bush stated she has talk with Mr. Ashton about an application and was
informed that he has someone in his office who should be able to help create an application form.

              Council Member Snow stated the application form should also include a list of
documents that will need to be submitted to verify the loss of revenue. Some businesses, like day
care, would be hard to provide the documentation for loss of revenue. The Council might need
to review each application on a case-by-case situation.

                Mayor Bush stated she will need to ask what documentation a small business
would need to submit as far as showing their losses. Kearns has a lot of small businesses that
are ran out of the home, that are very lucrative.

              Council Member Snow stated the Council will to get guidelines to follow and
probably set up a committee to review each application.

              Council Member Butterfield stated the committee will need to make sure that the
losses are due to the COVID-19 virus and not prior to the shutdown.

               Mayor Bush stated that should not be a problem because the Council knows the
exact date of the shutdown.

               Council Member Peterson asked if the County could administrator this program.

                Mayor Bush stated the County has indicated it would not administrator this

to help review the application. The volunteer would need to have a financial background.

               Council Member Snow stated the Council could ask someone from one of its
local banks to help.

               Mayor Bush stated that would be a good idea.

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Council Member Peterson updated the Council on the following:

    On June 20, 2020, a public hearing will be held prior to adoption of the budget. The
     proposed budget includes a 2.75 percent merit increase and a 2 percent market increase
     in wages. He asked what position the Council would like him to take on the approval of
     the budget.

All Council Members indicated the budget looked good to them.

             Council Member Snow stated she wanted it on record that the Unified Police
Department was ranked second to the bottom in the state as far as pay.

               Council Member Peterson stated he did not think it was that low, but it was near
the bottom; however, it depended on the rank. The higher the rank the further up the scale UPD
was as far as pay. He indicated that he would go ahead and vote in favor of the proposed budget.

    Weighted voting was discussed at the last UPD meeting. Three options were presented,
     but there was not a consensus reached. A fourth option was then discussed, which uses
     the weighted voting formula (contributions and population) but increased the motion to
     pass from over 50 percent to 60 percent. This option was to be taken back to all Councils
     for input.

All Council Members stated they were opposed to any kind of weighted voting.

              Council Member Peterson stated he will vote against weighted voting.

                Mayor Bush stated there are some issues in the Kearns precinct relating to
rumors. She asked that there be no communication (texting or calling) from Council Members to
officers or the chief. Chief Levi Hughes is trying to quell the gossip and it is not helpful when
Council Members are in contact with the officers. The Kearns Metro Township Council supports
the precinct 100 percent.
