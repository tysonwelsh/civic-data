MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 23-06-02 DATE: June 27, 2022

A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

AUTHORIZING COUNCIL MEMBER TRISH HULL TO SIGN AN

INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN MAGNA METRO

TOWNSHIP FOR ITS MAGNA UNITED COMMUNITIES THAT

CARE COALITION AND CHOOSE GANG FREE, OVERSEEN BY

THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Municipality pursuant to

Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seg; and

WHEREAS, Magna is governed by a five-member elected Council ("Magna
Council") under Utah Code Ann.§ 10-3-205.5, which Council elects from its members a
Mayor, and

WHEREAS, Choose Gang Free is a program owned and operated by the Salt Lake Area
Gang Project overseen by the Unified Police Department of Greater Salt Lake ("UPD"); and

WHEREAS, Magna on behalf of the Magna United Communities that Care Coalition
("Magna CTC") desire to enter into an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with the Salt Lake
Gang Project to provide funding so that Choose Gang Free may provide programming in Magna
that will help build community capacity to address youth incidences of violence through an
integrated community, health, and public safety approach; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Council desires to authorizes Council Member Trish Hull to
sign all documents dealing with Magna CTC, including the Choose Gang Free project; and

WHEREAS, the Magna Council finds it is in the best interest of the citizens of Magna
Metro township on behalf of the Magna CTC to enter into an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement
with Choose Gang Free,

NOW THEREFORE IT IS RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township Council,
Magna, Utah:

Section 1. The Magna Council hereby authorizes the Council Member Trish Hull to
sign on behalf of Magna and Magna CTC an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Magna
on behalf of Magna CTC and Choose Gang Free, a draft of which is set forth in Attachment A.

Section 2. The Magna Metro Township Council hereby authorizes Council
Member Trish Hull to execute any additional documents related to Choose Gang Free,
including documents dealing with the Safety and Success Workgroup, provided any

documents authorizing the expenditure of funds beyond the Choose Gang Free authorization
as set forth in Attachment "A" must first be reported to the Magna Council.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Magna Metro Township Council, in Magna, Salt
Lake County, Utah this 27" day of June 2023.
