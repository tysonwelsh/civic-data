6. Public Hearing, Zone Change, R-1-6 to R-2/ 12". St Corridor, 1172 Porter & 1135 Jefferson
Applicant Jamie Ponce de Leon was seeking approval for a zone change so he could combine
multiple parcels and build townhomes.

Planner Haylie Hale showed slides and stated that the zone change with a conditional overlay,
that would create a Development Agreement so that when all the parcels were combined into
one, it would require some design elements and for the townhome units to be owner occupied,
among other conditions. With that, the project would support the General Plan, therefore, staff
recommended approval.

Some public concerns were that the entrance onto Jefferson from the townhome project would
crowd the road as it is already narrow and on street parking makes it basically a one lane road.

7. General Plan Amendment, Harrison Blvd OGX Station Area Plan
Presenters Jake Young and Megan Moore with Logan Simpson and Senior Planner Brandon
Rypien displayed slides and presented the Harrison Boulevard OGX Station Area Plan, focusing
on mixed-use development, transit support, and community integration. Key points included
higher density residential zones around stations, potential student housing near Weber State,
and pedestrian safety improvements like mid-block crossings and bike lanes. The plan aims to
balance development with transit needs, emphasizing public spaces and sustainable
infrastructure. The five-year implementation plan includes zoning updates and transportation
enhancements, with a focus on maintaining community character and addressing public
feedback.
Local residents Meredith Hobbs, Gary Bell, Carson Campbell, Blaine Richey, Nick Milford, Colby
Kendell, Joe Butcher, Marianne Bell, and Sara Russell, among others, shared concerns about
