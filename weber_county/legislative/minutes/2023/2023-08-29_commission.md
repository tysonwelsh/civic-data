    7. APPROVAL OF AN AGREEMENT WITH COLE CANYON WATER COMPANY TO PIPE A
       PORTION OF SECONDARY WATER DITCH.
         Todd Ferrario (Parks, & Recreation Director): Cole Canyon serves the south end of North Fork Park and have
         been working with the state to try to pipe some secondary water ditches in that whole valley. There is 62%
         water loss in this portion of the ditch that goes from the upper pond to the lower storage pond creating a swamp.
         We asked them to go down one of the campsite roads on our loop. If the work is done in the original area
         where the ditch goes, scarring would be 50-60 feet wide and restoration would take 10-20 years. Instead, if
         they dig along the road, it’s easier to repair and future maintenance. They are doing all the work and the
         contract states they are to return everything to as-is or better status.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approval of an agreement with Cole Canyon Water Company to pipe a portion
         of secondary water ditch; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    8. APPROVAL OF AN AGREEMENT WITH COLE CANYON WATER COMPANY FOR
       INSTALLATION OF CHLORINATOR IN NORTH FORK PARK.
         Todd Ferrario (Parks, & Recreation Director): This water system over the last decade has had cleanliness
         issues. The state has now required chlorination. The selected location is near the south entrance with easy
         access that won’t impede access to our campground. This is their chlorinator that serves all the homes below
         in their district.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approval of an agreement with Cole Canyon Water Company for installation
