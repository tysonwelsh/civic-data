CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2022-1219-O, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
AND _ EXPANDING WASHINGTON COUNTY ORDINANCE, TITLE 8 CHAPTER 6
REGARDING TOURISM, RECREATION, CULTURAL AND CONVENTION TAX

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that a seven percent (7%) tax is currently imposed
on rental vehicles. This ordinance allows for that tax to be applied to rental Off Highway Vehicles
(OHV) and recreational trailers as well.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2022-3002, A RESOLUTION SELECTING
A CONTRACTOR FOR REMODELING AND CONSTRUCTION PROJECTS AT THE
WASHINGTON COUNTY PURGATORY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY

Mr. Snow explained that the County’s jail was constructed in 2007 and is in need of several
expansion and remodeling projects. A Request for Proposal (RFP) was advertised and two
construction companies submitted their proposals. The projects include a medical housing unit to
provide thirty-two (32) additional cells and an opportunity for quarantining inmates in order to
Washington County Commission

lessen the chances of any facility-wide outbreak as well as an addition to the southeast side of the
existing jail and a remodel of the booking/release area. An evaluation committee reviewed the
proposals and interviewed both offerors. The evaluation committee found that the proposal that
was most advantageous to the county was the proposal submitted by Sletten Corrections Company.
The evaluation committee recommended that Sletten Construction Company be awarded the
contract as they have experience with constructing jails.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to select Sletten Corrections Company and
Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-3002, A Resolution Selecting a Contractor for
Remodeling and Construction Projects at the Washington County Purgatory
Correctional Facility. Motion seconded by Chairman Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION __OF _RESOLUTION NO. ___R-2022-3003, A | RESOLUTION
PROCLAIMING OCTOBER 24-30, 2022 AS IRONMAN VOLUNTEER WEEK_IN

GREATER ZION

Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that the County will hold the Ironman 70.3 World
Championship on October 28-29, 2022. In order to help facilitate and support the World
Championship activities, the Washington County School District has declared October 28" as a
no school day. Mr. Lewis went on the mention that a large number of volunteers are needed to
help coordinate the World Championship event and said that anyone willing to help should contact
the Greater Zion tourism office.

Commissioner Almquist read aloud the Resolution and proclaimed October 24-30, 2022 as
Ironman Volunteer week.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2022-3003, A
Resolution Proclaiming October 24-30, 2022 as Ironman Volunteer Week in
Greater Zion. Motion seconded by Chairman Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson and Almquist voting aye.
