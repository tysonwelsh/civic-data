1)
Work Session regarding a Master Planned Development application for the Latitudes project, a 5-building mixed use project located at 6417 N Pace Frontage Rd, Silver Summit, Summit County, UT; Parcel SCBP-10-2AM; Applicant: Clive Bridgwater representing Fisher Creek, LLC (Roger Arsht and Ron Sharp); Administrative Process. Project #25-001, 25-002, and 25-003.
Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner. (4:30 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Latitudes MPD-NMU Staff Report - October 14, 2025 SBPC Work Session.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner
, briefly introduced the project and turned the time over to the applicant, Clive Bridgewater, representing Fisher Creek, LLC, for the work session discussion. (4:30 P.M.)
Clive Bridgwater,
Applicant
, addressed the Commission regarding a Master Planned Development application for the Latitudes project with a focus on the NMU rezone option. Mr. Bridgwater sought feedback and support from the Commission. He specifically asked them to consider whether or not the Silver Summit Business Park was an appropriate place and solution for the NMU rezone. (4:31 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy began the discussion by asking questions about the requirements for a transit center, clarification on the restrictions for a 55+ community and nightly rentals, increased traffic in the area, reduced commercial space, and increased housing units. (4:42 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley requested clarification on the amenities being open to the public. She inquired about the potential for a tree wall between the property and the freeway, and a follow-up regarding the intention behind the active aging population. (4:59 P.M.)
Commissioner Sagerman circled back to the issue of transportation and sought clarification on the idea of a private shuttle being available through the HOA. He asked for information on the proposed bike paths and the potential for the commercial space including a restaurant, and additional clarity on parking spaces. (5:11 P.M.)
Commissioner Nagie provided general comments on items that had already been addressed. He raised concerns regarding the number of commercial amenities being proposed, provided feedback on the transit center discussion, and pondered how the Commission could best give an applicant the ability to do more with their 8 acres if they don’t own the whole community. (5:20 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney inquired about the stormwater management system and a recycling center. She commented on the availability of the bike path and the proposed sustainability features. She raised concerns about the lack of commercial things to keep people in the community in order to reduce driving. (5:26 P.M.)
Commissioners provided final comments on the project. (5:36 P.M.)
Mr. Bridgwater expressed his appreciation to the Commissioners. (5:54 P.M.)
Commissioner Young joined the meeting at 6:00 P.M.
