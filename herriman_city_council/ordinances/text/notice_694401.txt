PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on August 4, 2021 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2021-16 adopting a final budget; making appropriations for the support of Herriman City for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2021, and ending June 30, 2022; approving the master fee schedule and determining the rate of tax and levying taxes upon all real property within Herriman City. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
