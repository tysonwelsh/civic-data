Walkara Way Trail Tour: Presenter: Matt Taylor, Senior Planner, Briam Amaya and Matt Neeley

with Consor

Those who attended the tour are Mayor Dave Young, Brenn Bybee, Debbie Lauret, Dave
Spencer, Jeff Lambson, Jason Bench, Keri Rugg, Ryan Clark, Grant Allen, Matt Taylor, Bryce
Merrill, Chris Tschirki, Reed Price and Teresa McKitrick.

Mr. Taylor explained that the Walkara Way Trail is funded by the state and it will connect
trails from Vineyard to Utah Lake. The project is requesting annexation to Orem to tie into our
water and electrical services.

Mr. Amaya and Neeley explained that the trail head is 1.5 acres and will have 65 parking
spaces. The trail head is part of a 1,000 acre conservation site in the area. The project is led by
the Holdway family. They also explained that the annexation is for water, sanitation and
electricity and Orem is the closest city This project has gone through the Development Review
Committee (DRC) in Orem City.

City of Orem’s Mental Health Program & Activities and Introduction of the “Communities That
Care Program” Presenter: Kamryn Wilson, Mental Health Advocate, Dave Hallam, Police

Detective; Kena Mathews, Community Services Manager; and Jessica Maxwell, Utah County
Health Department

Ms. Wilson and Mr. Hallam are from the Mental Health Response Team. This team
responds to active police calls for service involving mental health crisis, conducts follow up with
individuals who experienced a mental health crisis, trains other officers on current tactics for
mental health crises, interacts and build relationships with community resources, provides
resource and assistance to our homeless population, and plans and executes community events to
build social relationships and trust with Orem citizen. Mental crisis can include suicide
threats/attempts, psychotic behavior, individuals who are developmentally delayed and transient
populations.

The Mental Health Response Team has special projects they are working on. They have
started a mental health response plan. Families can complete online submissions for their loved
ones who may wander or experience significant difficulties due to mental health/medical
diagnosis. They also have an autism picnic with the police and they will source items for
officers to provide to the homeless population.

The CDBG funding is used for hotel stays and supplies for the homeless as well as a sub
for Santa program. This year they provided an entire Christmas for 3 specific families who are
all experiencing some sort of long term mental health and financial crisis.

Ms. Mathews is presenting on the United Way of Utah County Program, Everyday
Strong. Everyday strong is a new approach to anxiety and depression in children through
building resilience. United Way EveryDay Strong has the following resources: social media,
podcast & text program, training, handbooks & videos, and events & activities. The City of
Orem was the first EveryDay Strong City. City employees have gone through the EveryDay
Strong program. The City of Orem does training and hands out information in the schools and
community.

Ms. Maxwell is from the Utah County Health Department, she is introducing the
“Communities That Care Program”. This CTC program is organizing a coalition that includes
key leaders, community board, facilitators, work groups and an executive board. The foundations
of the CTC are prevention science (risk and protective factors), social development strategies and
tested & effective programs, policies, practices.

Ms. Maxwell explained the following risk factors: risk factors are found in every domain
of a child’s life, they present across development, they predict multiple problems, they operate
similarly across racial groups and regions, they vary across communities, they are buffered by
protective factors and they can be measured. Protective factors are influences that buffer
individuals from the risk factors present in their environments. Some of these protective factors
are opportunities for prosocial involvement, family attachment, and belief in the moral order.

Ideas for a coalition's involvement are providing information, providing support, and
enhancing skills. If a city wants to start a coalition then Utah County will cover the first year's

311 Help Center Recognition: Presenter: Kathy Lehrer, City Treasurer

Ms. Lehrer read a statement from Brandon Nelson, Finance Director. Five years ago the
311 Help Center was created. This group was created by combining the utility billing group with
employees from other departments. The purpose of this group is to give the citizens of the City
of Orem a one stop place for most of their city needs including fingerprinting, utility billing,
passports, landlord licensing, and business licensing.

Ms. Lehrer gave the following stats for the last 5 years. 25,655 work orders, 228,617
incoming calls, 267,386 processes mailed payments, 107,689 in person payments, 6,764
fingerprints, 9,223 photos taken, 16,702 passports processed, 23,601 phone payments, 1,700,770
bills created, 34,401 checks printed, as well as thousands of letters sent, new business licenses
and renewals processed, daily till reconciliation, garbage request, chats, emails and tickets to
departments within the city.

PERSONAL APPEARANCES

Andrew Jackson, a resident of Orem, addressed the mayor and council. A few weeks ago,
he brought up the matter of a retaining wall in easements and mentioned that there seemed to be
no specific section in Orem City's code addressing improvements in easements. He believed that
such matters would be governed by the state code. During that meeting, Mr. Bench assisted him
by guiding him to the appropriate section of the city code. Andrew Jackson expressed his
gratitude towards the City of Orem. He commended their exceptional cooperation and assistance
throughout the permitting process.

Jim Lauret reported on what is happening in scouting in the City of Orem. Currently,
Orem is home to two Cub Packs and four Scout Troops. Among the troops, three are for boys
and one is specifically for girls. I am proud to serve as the Cub Master of Pack 1776, which is
sponsored by The Arches Academy. Additionally, there are other troops in our area. To support
their activities, scouts participate in a fundraiser selling popcorn. For those interested in learning
more about scouting and the units available in Orem, I encourage you to visit the council website
at Utahscouts.org.
