Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commissioner Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commissioner Vice
Chair Jackson and Commissioner Bunker.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1 Public hearing and recommendation of Capital Real Estates request for review of a
General Plan Amendment on 1.74 acres located at approximately 1800 North 2300 West
changing the land use designation from LDR (low density residential) to Neighborhood
Commercial.

2.2 Public hearing and consideration of Capital Real Estates request for approval of the
Everbrook Academy Child Care Facility Concept located at approximately 1800 North
2300 West with exceptions requested.
*Items 2.1 and 2.2 were heard simultaneously.

Katie Bussell presented agenda item 2.1


Kim Struthers clarified the GPA and the current land use. The land was purchased from Alpine
School District for a Fire Station, but it was ultimately decided that the Fire State would be at a
different location. A developer bought the property and is developing residential homes.

Katie Bussell presented agenda item 2.2

The applicant, Allison Morgan, with Capital Real Estate, was present to represent the childcare
provider. She stated she understands they are aware of the traffic study that needs to be done, and
the landscape buffer that is required will be met. Allison specified the childcare operation hours
and programs that will be provided. The elevations with the architecture will blend in, and she is
aware that the materials do not meet requirements but will meet the staff recommendations.

They have been in discussion with Alpine School District regarding the parcel that is not being
used and they have agreed to sell it to the applicant. She discussed options for the road stub that
XDevelopment had previously worked with.

Commission Chair Newall opened public comment for 2.1

Angie Lethbridge is opposed to the development. She left an online comment that I will include
in the minutes that summarizes her comments. North Pointe Elementary is directly across the
street from where the driveway will be located. She stated this is not the place for this type of
development. The road will be five lanes and it will not be safe adding more children and homes.

Brian McQuiston is opposed to the development. He stated the only thing that changed in the
application is the amount of money that went towards property. The General Plan Amendment
was approved with LDR on the corner. The property was analyzed very closely. The only
difference is a new application. The XDevelopment proposal had a comment made by Mayor
Johnson that often brings up Development Agreements but feels uncomfortable afterwards. He
would like to see the Planning Commission forward a negative recommendation on the request.

Russ Franklin was opposed to the development. He would like to echo the comments that have
already been said. He timed the traffic going down 2300 west to go through 2100 North and it
takes 4 minutes longer, even though it is a shorter distance. Putting more pressure on the road
will add congestion and effects the quality of life. It has been analyzed and looked at, and there is
zoning for a reason. A day care would be great to have where it is already approved.

Jason Lethbridge is opposed to the development. He also wants to echo the comments. The
traffic to the corner area, specifically with a day care, will have peak times that will be
challenging. The city created a commercial node on the corner. There is a need for day cares, but
he feels this is the wrong decision. In this case the sell is more of a matter for the City Council
and should treat this property no differently to any other. He would like to maintain the general
plan as it currently stands.

Jenny Lee Furness and she is opposed to the development. She leaves her house at three and
stated the school is already full and the streets are already lined with cars to pick up their
children. When cars are trying to exit the school, it is nearly impossible to exit. When people are
leaving work, there is a lot of congestion, especially if there is going to be construction. Her
mother used to run a day care, so she is not opposed to day cares. But the traffic and buses, and
all the children that already attend the school, seems a bit excessive. Morning and afternoon drop
off will be bad, as well as evening pickup.

Katie Bussell summarized the online comments that will be included in the minutes.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing on 2.1

Commission chair Newall opened the public hearing on 2.2

Commission chair Newall closed the public hearing on 2.2

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to offer a negative recommendation of Capital Real
        Estates request for review of a General Plan Amendment on 1.74 acres located at
        approximately 1800 North 2300 West changing the land use designation from LDR
        (low density residential) to Neighborhood Commercial, finding that doing so would
        create a zoning island and the uses could become problematic; including all DRC
        comments. Commissioner Carlson seconded. Amended that the proposed changes are
        inconsistent with the land around it. The second still stands.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Britton, no;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner, Carlson, yes. The motion passed 4 – 1.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to deny Capital Real Estates request for
        approval of the Everbrook Academy Child Care Facility Concept located at
        approximately 1800 North 2300 West with exceptions requested, based on
        connectivity standards, the size of building is not in conformance, and that the
        health, safety and welfare standards are not met. Including all DRC comments.
        Commissioner Carlson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner, Carlson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.3) Consideration of Smartlink’s request for approval of revisions to the AT&T
Boardwalk Cell Tower site plan located at approximately 2800 West and 1800 North.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant was present to represent the item. AT&T is working to improve their sites in Lehi.
This purpose is to improve the service, this particular one is near the hospital. Staff will have the
final say on what the tower will look like, and AT&T is fine with that.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson move to table Smartlink’s request for approval of
        revisions to the AT&T Boardwalk Cell Tower site plan located at approximately
        2800 West and 1800 North until that applicant has the opportunity to provide monopine
        of the proposed rendering that would be installed; finding that the monopine shown does
        not have a stealth appearance and the applicant would like to have it tabled to provide a
        different rendering. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner, Carlson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.4 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of the Lehi Fire
Station 84 zone change on 4.1 acres located at approximately 1500 North 3600 West from
TH-5 (transitional holding) to Public Facility.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Chief Craft, was present to represent the item. The call volume and west Lehi is
expanding. Her explained that trying to serve the community is becoming increasingly difficult
and they would like to see a reduction in response time.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request
        for review of the Lehi Fire Station 84 zone change on 4.1 acres located at
        approximately 1500 North 3600 West from TH-5 (transitional holding) to Public
        Facility; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Noting that the
        Fire Department is trying to serve the community with an expanding community and
        would like to reduce response times. Commissioner Carlson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passes
        unanimously.

2.6 Recommendation of Chad Jones’ request for review of the Vantage concept plan and
Land Use Plan, a mixed-use development including 80 apartments and flex workspace
located at 77 East State Street.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.


The applicant, Chad Jones, was present to represent the item. Listen to the audio.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

The commission discussed the concept plan needing to meet the parking requirement. There are
122 total units. The applicants are proposing 102 total parking stalls, that includes on street
parking.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to approve Chad Jones’ request for review of
        the Vantage concept plan and Land Use Plan, a mixed-use development including 80
        apartments and flex workspace located at 77 East State Street, subject to meeting all
        of the DRC comments with the findings that the proposed concept plan is not yet
        consistent with the development code, but might be with the final site. Regarding
        discussion about meeting parking requirements, adding conditions that the applicant
        determine proper parking requirements and demonstrate to staff the proposed parking
        will be adequate and will maintain all of the residents vehicles on the property, and that
        there will be no misunderstanding to meet access requirements with a single point on
        access with more than 50 units that a city code change will be require with a prior
        approval to the site plan. Commissioner Eyre seconded.


        Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Britton, no;
        Commissioner Newall, no; Commissioner, Carlson, yes. The motion passed unanimously.
