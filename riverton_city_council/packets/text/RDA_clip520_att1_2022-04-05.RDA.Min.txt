46          RDA Resolution No. 22-02 – 12600 South Community Reinvestment Project Area
47
48   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that the Riverton Redevelopment
49   Agency (the “Agency”), by resolution, authorized the preparation of a Project Area Plan and
50   Project Area Budget (the “Plan & Budget”) for the Downtown 12600 South Community
51   Reinvestment Area (the “CRA”). The Plan and Budget provides, among other things, an
     Page | 2 Minutes – Redevlopment Agency Meeting
     April 5, 2022


 1   evaluation of appropriate land uses and economic and community reinvestment forecasts for
 2   the land encompassed by the CRA. The Plan and Budget also sets forth the aims and
 3   objectives of the anticipated new development, including its scope, its mechanism, and its value
 4   to the residents of Riverton City (the “City”) and other taxing districts. The Agency has
 5   requested $18,246,541 in property tax incremental revenues that will be generated by
 6   development within the CRA to fund a portion of the project development costs within the CRA,
 7   as outlined in the Project Area Plan. These property tax revenues are a result from an increase
 8   in valuation of property within the Project Area and will be paid to the Agency rather than to the
 9   taxing entity to which the tax revenue would otherwise have been paid if one or more of the
10   taxing entities below agree to share the property tax increase under an interlocal
11   agreement. All of the property tax increment to be paid to the Agency for the development in
12   the CRA are taxes that will be generated only if the CRA is developed.
13
14   Randy Sant, said that a notice was sent to property owners 30 days before the public hearing
15   along with the taxing entities. Mr. Sant asked Jamie Larsen, City Recorder if she had received
16   any comment or questions about the notice. Ms. Larsen said no.
17
18   Mr. Sant asked Mr. Lethbridge if he had received any comments or questions about the notice.
19   Mr. Lethbridge said he spoke with several property owners who had general questions and
20   property owners who asked for additional information.
21
22   Mr. Sant explained that the Redevelopment Agency Board does not have the power to condemn
23   any properties. He said there are no defined projects in the Community Reinvestment Project
24   Areas. If a development meets the requirements of the plan and the RDA Board decides to
25   participate in this development, then tax increment will be given.
26
27   Chair Staggs called for public comments;
28
29   Jay Silcox commented that he owns property on the southeast corner and his main concern is
30   traffic. He believes that traffic should not go through his property and he does not want his
31   property taxes increased.
32
33   Mr. Lethbridge commented that he spoke to Salt Lake County and they were worried about the
34   commercial pads on 12600 South being in the project area. Councilmember Stewart asked
35   what division of the county was concerned. Mr. Lethbridge said it was the Auditors office.
36
37   Chair Staggs said he understands Mr. Silcox’s concern about traffic and said the City would like
38   to see this area be commercial. He reiterated that the Board does not have the power for
39   imminent domain or the power to change the zoning.
40
41   Chair Staggs called for additional public comments; there being none, he closed the Public
42   Comment period.
43
44   Board Member Buroker MOVED that the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency approve
45   RDA Resolution No. 22-02 – Approving and adopting the 12600 South Community
46   Reinvestment Project Area. Councilmember McDougal SECONDED the motion. Chair
47   Staggs called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call vote. The
48   vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, McCay-yes, Stewart-yes and Wells-yes. The motion passed
49   unanimously.

22             RDA Resolution No. 22-04 – Redwood Road Community Reinvestment Project
23             Area
24
25   Mr. Lethbridge said that the proposed Plan and Budget provides, among other things, an
26   evaluation of appropriate land uses and economic and community reinvestment forecasts for
27   the land encompassed by the CRA. The Draft Plan and Budget also sets forth the aims and
28   objectives of the anticipated new development, including its scope, its mechanism, and its value
29   to the residents of Riverton City (the “City”) and other taxing districts. The Agency has
30   requested $6,329,656 in property tax incremental revenues that will be generated by
31   development within the CRA to fund a portion of the project development costs within the CRA,
32   as outlined in the Project Area Plan.
33
34   Mr. Lethbridge said this proposed Project Area Plan does not include any power of
35   condemnation or eminent domain. There has been concerns expressed in public comment that
36   establishment of the CRA will result in homes being taken, residents being forced to relocate, or
37   other such actions. This is neither the intent nor within the legal scope of the Project Area
38   Plan. The Plan also does not include any proposed development layout or plan, or any specific
39   land use approvals.
40
41
42   Board Member Wells MOVED that the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency approve RDA
43   Resolution No. 22-04 – Approving and adopting an official plan for the Redwood Road
44   Community Reinvestment Project Area. Councilmember Buroker SECONDED the motion.
45   Chair Staggs called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call
46   vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, McCay-yes, Stewart-yes and Wells-yes. The
47   motion passed unanimously.
