To the City Council members,

I recently built a home on Laurel Chase Drive, just south of the proposed
development. I have four young kids and my biggest concern with the proposed
development is the road alignment. I, and many of my neighbors, do not want the
road to connect from 118th South to Laurel Chase Drive, because of the increased
traffic that would come with it. To me, connecting this development from 118th to
Laurel Chase Drive would be a major disappointment after just recently building a
home in a quiet neighborhood.                 It seems that there are a few alternatives that should
be seriously considered. Those alternatives are ( in order of my preference):

1. Don' t connect this new development to any roads except Redwood Road, as is
shown in the developers conceptual road design Alt-A
2. Connect the road to Stone Ridge Lane, which is stubbed to connect. Stone Ridge
is a wider road than Laurel Chase and is a straight shot to access 1300 West. There
are multiple places where roads go over the canal, including in my neighborhood.
This seems more practical and like a better fit.
3. Connect only the cul de sac in Area 1 to Laurel Chase, as is shown in the
developers conceptual road design Alt- B


The developer submitted multiple options for road alignment and will get the same
amount of doors regardless of the alignment, so it seems to me that the developer
would be satisfied with any of the three options I mentioned above. If the developer
approves of road alignment alternatives I provided, and the home owners on Laurel
Chase drive would approve, then why doesn' t it seem like any of these options are
being seriously    considered          by the planning     commission         or the city council? I' m
guessing   even   home        owners on         Stone Ridge    Lane would        approve of the option # 1 or

 3 that I mentioned.


Thanks for your service and for your time.
