A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE FINAL BUDGET FOR THE 2020 FISCAL YEAR FOR
THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP

BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Kearns Metro Township Council
(the “Council’”’) held a public hearing on December 20, 2019, to take public comment regarding
the adoption of a final budget for the 2020 fiscal year beginning on January 1, 2020, and ending
on December 31, 2020.

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the State of Utah; and

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-3c-202(2) specifies that the fiscal year for metro townships
within a municipal services district such as Kearns is the calendar year;

WHEREAS, on December 20, 2019, the Council approved a tentative budget for Kearns
for the 2020 fiscal year and scheduled a public hearing for December 20, 2019 to receive public
comment regarding the adoption of the tentative budget pursuant to Utah Code § 10-5-108; and

WHEREAS, the Council provided notice of the date, time, and place of the public hearing
in accordance with Utah Code § 10-5-108; and

WHEREAS, the Council made the tentative budget available for public inspection pursuant
to Utah Code Ann. § 10-5-107 for a period of at least seven days prior to holding a public hearing
in accordance with; and

WHEREAS, on December 20, 2019 at 6:00 p.m., the Council held a public hearing on the
tentative budget in accordance with Utah Code § 10-5-108 at 5658 South Cougar Lane
Kearns, Utah 84118.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

1. The tentative budget, as amended (if amended) and attached hereto, is adopted as
Kearns’s final budget for the 202.0 fiscal year.

2. The Mayor will file the final budget with the State Auditor no later than thirty (30)
days after the date of this resolution.

2. The effective date of this resolution is December 20, 2019.

ADOPTED AND APPROVED by majority vote at a duly called public hearing of the
Kearns Metro Township Council on this 20" day of December 2019.

Kearns, the largest township in the District, is located in the North west quadrant of the
District.
Kearns offers a many amenities including the Legacy Center, a facility built during the 2002

Kearns also lies adjacent to the USANA Amphitheater, a 20,000-seat outdoor amphitheater.
While providing an exclusive entertainment venue, the amphitheater brings with it unique and
difficult transportation issues for Kearns.
Transportation challenges are also created in that the major transportation routes for
surrounding entities bifurcates Kearns and adds additional traffic to routes that are already at
capacity.
