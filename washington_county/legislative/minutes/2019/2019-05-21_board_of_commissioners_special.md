WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
MAY 21, 2019

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Cox at 2:15 p.m. on May 21, 2019, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil
Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, Community Development Director Scott
Messel, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance: Tyce Palmer, Dixie Conservation District and Cory Cram, Water
Conservancy District

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION __AND__ PERIODIC _REVIEW__OF_ COUNTY _RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included: Agriculture; Livestock and Grazing; and
Irrigation.

The agriculture section will be updated to include language which states that Washington
County supports Utah’s policy and water law, that all water is owned by the State.

Several formatting and grammatical suggestions were made to the Livestock and Grazing
as well as the Irrigation sections.

The RMP will be updated based on the suggestions from those in attendance and will be
brought before the Commission for final adoption.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
3:20 p.m.
