Closed Session - Litigation (1:49 PM)
Christopher Robinson made a motion to enter closed session to discuss litigation. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Council Members Armstrong, Stevens, Hanson, Harte, and Robinson met in closed session to discuss litigation along with Shayne Scott, Manager, Janna Young, Deputy Manager, Dave Thomas, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney, Annette Singleton, Executive Assistant, Frank Pignanelli, Renae Cowley, and Steve Styler from Foxley and Pignanelli.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to leave closed session to discuss litigation and convene in open session. (3:15 PM). Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
3.
Work Session (3:20 PM)
1)
Pledge of Allegiance (3:20 PM)
2)
Presentation by Weber Basin Water Conservancy District regarding Truth in Taxation; Scott Paxman (3:20 PM)
Attachment: Weber Basin Water Conservancy District Presentation.pdf
Scott Paxman, Weber Basin Water Conservancy District Assistant General Manager, presented Council with the District’s need to go through the Truth in Taxation process. Council Members asked questions. Mr. Paxman responded.
3)
Presentation of "Let's Talk", a leadership training program focused on facilitating more civil discourse in our community; Scott van Hartesvelt and Virginia Solomon (3:43 PM)
Attachment: Lets Talk Presentation.pdf
Attachment: Lets Talk Summary.pdf
Park City Leadership Class 29--Virginia Solomon, Scott van Hartesvelt, Shari Noteware, Bridget Hayes--presented their class project, "Let’s Talk", to the Council. Council Members asked questions to which the presenters responded.
4)
Discussion regarding 2023 Summit County Fair events; Tyler Orgill (4:03 PM)
Attachment: 2023 Fair Presentation.pdf
Tyler Orgill, Events Manager, and Kate McChesney, Fair Coordinator, briefed Council on plans for the upcoming fair. Council Members asked questions. Manager Orgill and Coordinator McChesney responded.
5)
Discussion and overview of the 2023 tax roll and demonstration of the Assessor's office property valuation heat maps; Stephanie Poll (4:16 PM)
Attachment: 2023 Tax Roll Overview Presentation.pdf
Stephanie Poll, Assessor, provided an overview of the property taxes for the coming year. Council Members commented and asked questions. Assessor Poll responded. Matt Boyer, Data Analyst, demonstrated the property valuation heat maps.
4.
Convene as the Governing Board of Mountain Regional Water Special Service District (3:55 PM)
Malena Stevens made a motion to convene as the Governing Board of Mountain Regional Water Special Service District. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
1)
Approval of Resolution MRW-2023-10, a Budget Resolution of the Mountain Regional Water Special Service District 2023 Budget Amendments; Dave Thomas (3:55 PM)
Attachment: Staff Report-MRW 2023 Capital Budget Amendment.pdf
Chief Civil Deputy Attorney Dave Thomas introduced the proposed budget resolution.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve Resolution MRW-2023-10 amending Mountain Regional Water Special Service District's 2023 Budget. (3:58 PM). Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Resolution MRW 2023-10 executed
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to dismiss as the Governing Board of the Mountain Regional Water Special Service District and reconvene as the County Council. (3:58 PM). Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
5.
Consideration of Approval (4:53 PM)
1)
Appeals regarding Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension; 1662 S West Hoytsville Rd., Wanship, Summit County, UT; Parcels NS-125-A, S-415, and NS-124 (4:53 PM)
Attachment: Appeal Record regarding Rees’s Gravel Pit Powerline Extension.pdf
Helen Strachan, Civil Deputy Attorney, presented the basis for the Community Development Director’s interpretation.
Bradley Vernon, executor of the Dean Lamar Vernon Estate, waived the appellant’s right to seek appeal in front of the Board of Adjustment.
Amir Caus, Senior Planner, provided background on the Appeals in front of Council. Helen Strachan, Civil Deputy Attorney, representing the Community Development Director and the East Side Planning Commission, provided an outline of procedural issues and sought Council input.
a.
Discussion and action regarding an appeal of the Community Development Director's determination on whether a power pole constitutes a structure as defined in the Eastern Summit County Development Code; Bradley R. Vernon executor of the Dean Lamar Vernon Estate, Appellant
Bradley Vernon, appellant representative, set forth his basis for appealing this issue. (5:03 P.M.)
Larry Vernon added further comment.
Council members commented and asked questions. The presenters responded. Dave Thomas, Civil Chief Deputy Attorney, provided additional thoughts.
Bill Wilde, Rees’s Enterprises representative, provided additional factual background information.
Robert Wadman, General Manager for Rees’s Enterprises, provided factual information.
Pat Putt, Community Development Director, provided further context on how the parties arrived at the situation in front of Council.
Manager Wadman provided further information.
Harold Dudley, Rocky Mountain Power’s Right of Way Director, clarified Rocky Mountain Power’s position and the factual information.
6.
Public Input (6:18 PM)
Council Chair Armstrong opened the meeting for public input.
No one appearing for public input, the Council Chair closed the meeting for public input.
7.
Consideration of Approval (cont'd)
1)
Appeals regarding Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension; 1662 S West Hoytsville Rd., Wanship, Summit County, UT; Parcels NS-125-A, S-415, and NS-124 cont'd. (4:53 PM)
b.
Discussion and action regarding an appeal of the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission's approval of the Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension CUP Amendment (Project #23-015); Bradley R. Vernon executor of the Dean Lamar Vernon Estate, Appellant
Andy Badger, Rocky Mountain Power Regional Business Manager, provided further background on providing power.
Larry Vernon explained his basis for appealing the Planning Commission’s approval of the Powerline Extension.
Council Members commented on the Conditional Use Permit (CUP).
Manager Andy Badger and Harold Dudley, Rocky Mountain Power Right of Way Agent, responded to questions from Council.
Tonja B Hanson made a motion to approve the Community Development Director's determination that a power pole does not constitute a structure as defined in the Eastern Summit County Development Code and the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission's issuance of the CUP with the conditions stated in the easement that the power poles be buried within the easement subject to findings of fact and conclusion of law to be drafted by the County Attorney's Office consistent with the discussions today and authorizing the Council Chair to sign. Canice Harte seconded, and the motion carried, (4-1).
Roger Armstrong voted AYE
Malena Stevens voted AYE
Tonja B Hanson voted AYE
Canice Harte voted AYE
Christopher Robinson voted NAY
Attachment: Rees Gravel Pit CUP Appeal F&C (Final 8.28.2023)
c.
Discussion and action regarding an appeal of the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission's condition of approval (portion of Condition #1) where the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission requested that the powerline extension for the Rees's Gravel Pit Powerline Extension CUP Amendment (Project #23-015) be located on the centerline of the utility easement or be buried; Applicant/Appellant (Rees's Gravel Pit); Robert Wadman representing Rees's Gravel Pit; Andrew Badger representing Rocky Mountain Power (PacifiCorp)
See discussion and vote above.
8.
Public Hearing (7:05 PM)
1)
Public hearing and approval of Ordinance No. 962, an Ordinance Amending the Snyderville Basin and Eastern Summit County Moderate Income Housing Elements of the General Plans; Jennifer Strader
Attachment: Staff Report and Ordinance No. 962 Moderate Income Housing.pdf
Jennifer Strader, Planner, provided background and presented the proposed Ordinance. Council Members suggested changes to the East side portion of the Ordinance and the Recitals.
Council Chair Armstrong opened the public hearing.
No comment was made.
Council Chair Armstrong closed the public hearing.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to adopt Ordinance No. 962 and amending the 5th recital by deleting strategy B from the East Side Plan. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Ordinance 962 Executed
