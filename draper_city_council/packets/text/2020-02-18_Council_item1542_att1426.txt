                                        ORDINANCE NO. 1422

        AN ORDINANCE THE NIELSEN’S FROZEN CUSTARD DEVELOPMENT
        AGREEMENT FOR APPROXIMATELY 0.70 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED
        AT APPROXIMATELY 1462 EAST DRAPER PARKWAY WITHIN DRAPER
        CITY.

        WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the City to
allow development of the subject property in accordance with the Development Agreement; and

      WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow modifications to site design standards for the
CC (Community Commercial) zone; and

      WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow modifications to parking standards for the
CC (Community Commercial) zone; and

        WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow for additional sign area and modifications to
sign design standards than would otherwise be allowed for the CC (Community Commercial) zone; and

        WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow for deviations to architectural standards from
what is required for the CC (Community Commercial) zone; and

         WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will allow for deviations to lighting standards from what
is required for the CC (Community Commercial) zone; and

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1.     Development Agreement. The City of Draper approves the Development
Agreement provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as the Nielsen’s Frozen Custard Development
Agreement.

        Section 2.        Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Ordinance and
all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 3.       Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon
publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THE 18TH DAY OF FEBRUARY, 2020.
