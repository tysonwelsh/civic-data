                        ORDINANCE NO. 1582
   AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL PROHIBITING THE
DISCHARGE OF FIREWORKS IN SPECIFIED AREAS

WHEREAS, on May 16, 2023, the Draper City Council adopted Ordinance 1580
regarding the discharge of fireworks; and

WHEREAS, the Draper City Recorder identified an error in the verbal description of
the area subject to fireworks restrictions such that the verbal description did not
match the map attached thereto as Exhibit A; and

WHEREAS, this Ordinance enacts the correct verbal description of areas in which
the use of fireworks is prohibited; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the minimal restrictions on public
convenience which would occur through a restriction on the use of personal
fireworks in designated areas is significantly outweighed by the need to protect the
general health, safety and welfare of the public;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION I. Restrictions on Personal Fireworks. Pursuant to the provisions of
Utah Code Ann. 15A-5-202.5 and based on the findings as noted above, the use of
fireworks is prohibited east of 1300 East Street, south of 13800 South Street, west
of Interstate 15, and Starting at the intersection of 11400 South and Interstate 15,
follow the city boundary to the east (continue past 700 East, continue past 1000
East) all the way to 1300 East, follow 1300 East to the south to the intersection of
1300 East and UTA TRAX line, follow the UTA TRAX line to the west to the
intersection of Kimballs Lane and UTA TRAX line, follow Kimballs Lane to the west to
State Street and continue west to Interstate 15, follow Interstate 15 back to the
intersection of 11400 South and Interstate 15, also included as allowed in Utah
Code Title 15A-5-202.5 Section 1.1.2, is the East Jordan Canal waterway with a 200’
buffer on either side through the remainder of the city, all as shown on the
following map:
      [The map is attached as Exhibit A]
      Any discrepancy between the written description and the map, the map will
      take precedence.



SECTION II. Duration and Scope. This Ordinance shall remain in effect until
October 15, 2023, unless terminated earlier by action of the City Council. This
Ordinance shall not apply to permitted public fireworks displays authorized by the
City and approved by the City Fire Marshal.

SECTION III. No Repealer. This Ordinance temporarily supersedes Draper City
Municipal Code 6-6-020(D) and does not repeal the same.

SECTION IV. Repealer. Ordinance 1580 is repealed.



SECTION V. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

SECTION VI. Penalty. In addition to any other fines, penalties, or liability
permitted under Utah law, in accordance with Utah Code Ann. 53-7-225, a person is
guilty of an infraction, punishable by a fine of up to $1000, if a person discharges a
Class C common state approved explosive in violation of this Ordinance.

SECTION VII. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 6th DAY OF JUNE, 2023.
