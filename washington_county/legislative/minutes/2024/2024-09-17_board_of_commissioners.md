CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2024-1270-O, AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING
VARIOUS AMENDMENTS TO TITLE 10 CHAPTER 30 ZONING REGULATIONS
REGARDING THE SCENIC BYWAY OVERLAY ZONE (SBOZ)

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that the Scenic Byway Overlay Zone was
passed in December 2023. The County desires to improve provisions in its Title 10 Zoning
Regulations to support residential and business development and harmonious land uses by
providing more opportunities for transient and non-transient lodging. The Washington County
Planning Commission held a public hearing in conjunction with the amended provisions on
September 10, 2024, and unanimously recommended approval of the various amendments.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1270-O, an
Ordinance Adopting Various Amendments to Title 10 Chapter 30 Zoning
Regulations Regarding the Scenic Byway Overlay Zone (SBOZ) with the
Change that an Accessory Dwelling Unit May be Up to 3,500 Square Foot If it
Complies with County Code. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow
voting aye.

Community Development Director Scott Messel related that as the County code is updated,
different overlay zones have been created which requires provisions in Title 10 Zoning Regulations
to be amended and consolidated. The recommended amendments adds provisions regarding walls,
fences, landscaping and buffering, outdoor lighting, and other miscellaneous provisions. The
Planning Commission held a public meeting and unanimously recommended approval of
Washington County Code Title 10, Chapters 15A, B, and C. Commissioner Iverson requested that
Chapter 15-B-9: Barbed Wire be changed from being prohibited in all zones except agricultural
zones to being prohibited when not being used in the keeping of livestock.

Ms. Hales stated that after conversations with the Commissioners, there are no proposed changes
to 15-A, Retaining Walls. Proposed changes include fences, trees, topiaries, and lighting
provisions.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Ordinance No. 2024-1271-O, An
Ordinance Adopting and Amending Zoning Regulations to Create Title 10,
Chapter 15A, B & C Regarding Walls, Fences, Landscaping and Buffering,
and Outdoor Lighting with the Changes as Outlined by Deputy County
Attorney Hales. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Amend the Agenda to Move Items 10
and 11 Up in the Agenda. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3357, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE SECOND AMENDMENT OF THE RAP TAX INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the Recreation, Arts, and Parks (RAP) Tax
was imposed in 2014. This agreement would continue the current distribution arrangement except
for an administrative fee of 1.5% to be added, with the date extending to 2035. This would not
increase the tax, but it would allow for the administrative fee to be collected by the county before
distributing RAP funding.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3357, A

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3358, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN ADJUSTMENT OF 2024 PROPERTY TAXES FOR CERTAIN PARCELS OWNED
BY RURAL HOUSING DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION DBA SELF-HELP HOMES

Deputy Attorney Snow explained that the Rural Housing Development Corporation DBA Self-
Help Homes provides affordable housing to low-income families through federally funded
programs and sweat equity of the participants. Under Utah State Code, the county Commission
may adjust or defer property taxes if is in the best interest of the State and County.

County Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan explained that this corporation does not qualify under
traditional tax exemption. The commissioners would like some more information from the property
owner before taking action on this request.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Table Resolution No. R-2024-3358, A
Resolution Approving an Adjustment of 2024 Property Taxes for Certain
Parcels Owned by Rural Housing Development Corporation DBA Self-Help
Homes. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting to Table.
