City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On January 18, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1512 amending the official
Zoning Map of Draper City and approving a development agreement for the Concord
Lane Zoning Map Amendment and Development Agreement. The complete
ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 19th day of January 2022. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
