Citizen Public Comment
Willy Stockman, Emigration Canyon Sustainability Alliance, stated the watershed
has issues and it can be addressed in a variety of ways; a lot of things can be done from the
bottom up. The Emigration Canyon Sustainability Alliance wants to be part of the community
educational and outreach piece.

grant, what role will the Emigration Canyon Sustainability Alliance take.
Ms. Stockman stated community education and outreach. The Council will do the
administrative and ordinance piece. Involvement from the community to help protect the
watershed is important.
Council Member Brems asked if there is a concern about the direction the
Council will not accommodate the plan.
Ms. Stockman stated the plan needs to address the entire watershed problem.
Council Member Brems stated a study needs to identify what is going to be
studied, what already exists, and how to implement it. It is a logical process.
Ms. Stockman stated she would like to keep the study broad in terms of defining
the different problems there are in the canyon and then pick and choose. If all the problems are
well defined in the plan, then it is a better watershed plan.

Utah Conservation Corps Invoice
The Council reviewed a request for payment in the amount of $3,200 from the Utah
Conservation Corps for deadfall cleanup in 2016. A payment was never made for the cleanup
because an invoice was never sent to the Emigration Canyon Community Council. Steve Borst,
Chair, and Paul Brown, Treasurer, Emigration Canyon Community Council have repeatedly
requested an invoice. They believe work was done in the canyon but they do not know how much.
David Church stated the Metro Township Council can include $3,200 in the
budget for the Community Council.
Deputy Mayor Hawkes stated per Emigration Canyon Metro Township’s
ordinance, the Metro Township Council needs to have a financial statement from the Community
Council before any funds are approved.
Council Member Brems stated he does not see a bill.
Steve Borst stated that has been the issue for 2 ½ years. Utah Conservation
Corps has not been able to produce any type of invoice or proof or work. He recognizes that they
did do work for about four or five days.
Mayor Smolka, seconded by Deputy Mayor Hawkes, moved to approve, upon
receipt of Emigration Canyon Community Council’s financial statement, $4,500 to the Emigration
Canyon Community Council for its annual funding and an additional $3,200 to pay Utah
Conservation Corps; for a total of $7,700. The motion passed unanimously.

Resolution – Unified Fire Authority
The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving entry into a revised and
restated interlocal agreement to create and govern the Unified Fire Authority.
RESOLUTION NO. 20-01-01

Date: January 23, 2020

A RESOLUTION OF EMIGRATION METRO TOWNSHIP APPROVING ENTRY
INTO A REVISED AND RESTATED lNTERLOCAL AGREEMENT TO CREATE
AND GOVERN THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY
A. The Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Ann. §11-13-101 et seq. (the “Interlocal
Cooperation Act”), provides that any two or more public agencies may enter into
agreements with one another for joint or cooperative action following the adoption of an
appropriate resolution by the governing body of each participating public agency.
B. The multiple parties to the proposed Revised and Restated Interlocal Agreement to Create
and Govern the Unified Fire Authority (“Revised Interlocal Agreement”) are all public
agencies for purposes of the Interlocal Cooperation Act.
C. The Unified Fire Authority (“Authority”) was initially created via Interlocal Cooperation Act
in 2004 to provide fire protection and emergency medical services to public agencies for
a fee. As described in the Revised Interlocal Agreement, some members of the Authority
directly pay this fee to the Authority while others do so as members of the Unified Fire
Service Area, a special district for fire protection and emergency medical services.
D. To better reflect the current status and governance of the Authority, the members of the
Authority, including those members of the Unified Fire Service Area, wish to revise and
restate the original 2004 interlocal agreement by entering into the Revised Interlocal

Agreement in the form attached hereto as Exhibit “A,” so that the Authority may continue
to provide fire suppression, rescue services, emergency medical services, emergency
protection, and related services to the parties.
E. Each legislative body of the Authority and its members has met or will meet in regular
open session in compliance with the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act to approve the
respective entity’s entry into the Revised Interlocal Agreement.
F. The Chief Legal Officer of the Authority has reviewed and approved the form of the
Revised Interlocal Agreement as required by Utah Code Ann. §11-13-202.5(3). Each
member entity’s respective legal counsel must similarly approve the Revised Interlocal
Agreement.
G. After careful consideration, the Council of Emigration Metro Township has reviewed the
form of the Revised Interlocal Agreement and determined that it is in the best interests of
Emigration Metro Township to approve the entry into the Revised Interlocal Agreement as
proposed.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council of Emigration Metro Township:
SECTION 1. PURPOSE. The purpose of this Resolution is to approve the entry of
Emigration Metro Township into an Interlocal Agreement.
SECTION 2. APPROVAL OF INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT. The Council hereby
authorizes and directs the execution and delivery of the Revised Interlocal Agreement, in the form
attached as Exhibit “A” hereto, on behalf of Emigration Metro Township.
SECTION 3. RECORDS. Emigration Metro Township’s keeper of records is authorized
and instructed to keep and maintain an executed copy of the Revised Interlocal Agreement
available for inspection.
SECTION 4. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Resolution will take effect upon approval. The
Revised Interlocal Agreement will be effective when the following Interlocal Cooperation Act
requirements have been satisfied: (i) the Revised Interlocal Agreement has been approved by all
parties as required by Section 11-13-202(2); (ii) each party to the Revised Interlocal Agreement
has submitted the Revised Interlocal Agreement to an attorney authorized to represent the said
party for review as to proper form and compliance with applicable law as required by Section 1113-202.5(3); and (iii) the approved, signed Revised Interlocal Agreement has been filed with the
keeper of records of each of the parties as required by Section 11-13-209.
