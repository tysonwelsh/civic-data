D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: Last week we had some out of state and out of country visitors
   and we also celebrated the 30 year anniversary of one of your prime assets in your portfolio Commissioner
   Harvey, the Ice Sheet. We had some Team USA Utah Olympic people come along with some IOC members to
   inspect and look at our Ice Sheet for a possible Olympic venue for curling 10 years from now. We met with a
   gentleman out of Scotland that's actually the Federation president; he was quite impressed with our facility along
   with his staff member as well. But it was a proud moment for all of us to show the Ice Sheet. What we stressed
   to the IOC and the Utah Olympic Committee is that facility has been well used for over 30 years for basically 20
   hours a week from three to four year old skaters up to eighty year old skaters and older. It's used by all
   community members for a lot of different events from ice skating, hockey, and curling. I think that's an asset that
   continues to show Weber County is very cognizant of the fact we provide excellent recreation opportunities for
   our citizens. The next event that Commissioner Bolos and I were happy to attend was previewing Snowbasin,
   the site of the 2002 Downhill and Super G events, with some additional members of the IOC. I was informed
   that part of the group we met with was part of the group from IOC that concentrated on Summer Games while
   others concentrated on Winter Games. We had people from France and all over Europe. Interesting IOC
   member I had a chance to talk with was from Afghanistan, which was quite unusual. As we rode to the top of the
   John Paul lift and look at the backside of Mount Ogden, you could see the look of the awe in their face of that
   great venue. It really brought to mind the significance of what we have to offer here for our Olympic venues.
   But just as important as our physical asset was what they stressed was the generosity, the welcoming attitude that
   Utahans in Weber County have for our out of state guests. I think we made it pretty clear during our opening
   remarks to that group that, “we welcomed you here in 2002 and we're going to welcome you in 2034.” We left
   that with a commitment from the Utah Olympic Committee that Snowbasin would be the site of all the Alpine
   events for the 2034 Olympics. Now we're still 10 years away but that's a pretty good commitment to have. I just
   want to thank you two for being involved with that. I think sometimes the public doesn't understand the process
   we goes through for this Olympics. I think we understand that this is going to be another showcase for Utah, for
   Weber County, and we're going to see probably some substantial growth. People will become interested in
   locating here from outside the state. So we're very proud to showcase our assets and it was it was great to hear
   the positive comments from everybody that was involved with those two delegations.

     Chair Harvey: I am proud to say my grandfather was on the first Utah Olympic Committee in 1964.

    9. APPROVE AN INTER-LOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH WEBER-MORGAN
       HEALTH DEPARTMENT FOR HEALTHY HOMES HUD GRANT.
         Brian Cowan (Weber-Morgan Health Department Directors): We have been awarded a $2M grant from the
         US Department of Housing and Urban Development that is to be used between now and October 2027 to
         distribute out to the community for reduction in living hazards in low income housing. Living hazards may
         be high lead levels from lead based paint, issues related to mold, or just proper ventilation and heating and air
         conditioning in the homes. To qualify, people need to be below 80% income median for our area for families
         with children or elderly over 62 years old. This also allows landlords to hold rent levels on low income
         tenants.

         Commissioner Bolos: This is a great opportunity for us to accept this money and help those intended
         individuals and families.

         Commissioner Froerer: This money is accessible to all cities in Weber County and not just unincorporated
         Weber County?

         Mr. Cowan: Correct.
