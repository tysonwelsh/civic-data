                                             Staff Meeting

Electronic Board Meeting Determination

                 Greg Schulz read the following statement regarding the electronic meeting
determination:

               Consistent with provisions of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, specifically
Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(4), and acting in my capacity as the Mayor of the Magna Metro
Township (“Magna”), I hereby make the following written determinations in support of my decision
to hold and convene electronic meetings of the Magna Metro Township Council without a physical
anchor location:

1.     Conducting Council Meetings with an anchor location that is physically accessible for
members of the public to attend in person presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of
those who may be present at the anchor location.

2.     This determination is based upon the following:

       a.     Pursuant to the Public Health Orders issued by the State of Utah and Salt Lake
       County, the Magna Metro Township remains under a state of public health emergency
       related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, and significant person-to-person
       transmission of the virus continues to occur in Magna; and

       b.        All other facts noted in the August 7, 2020 Letter of Determination are still in effect.

                                                    1
DATE             TUESDAY                            OCTOBER                              27, 2020

               These written determinations shall be read into the minutes at the beginning of the
Council meeting held in Magna on September 8, 2020, and future Council meetings as
appropriate. Council staff are instructed to include in the public notice of each Council meeting
the information on how a member of the public may view and make comments during the Council’s
Electronic meetings.

              This written determination is effective until midnight on October 10, 2020 and may
be re-issued by future written determinations of the Mayor at that or any other appropriate time.

Dated this 8th day of September, 2020.

Reports from other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Chief Jason Ackerman stated he and three other officers in the Magna Precinct
contracted COVID last month. In light of that, the precinct is closed to walk-in customers, although
people can still make appointments over the phone to pick up reports, etc. Officers are
encouraged to minimize face to face contact as much as possible. The Health Department has
been helping flag COVID-positive addresses, so that when an officer is dispatched to an address,
they know whether there are COVID-positive individuals in the home. Then, detectives are
working from home, but could be brought in to handle patrol calls if necessary.

               Council Member Hull asked if the Magna Precinct had enough personal
protective equipment for its officers

              Chief Ackerman stated the precinct got a stockpile of personal protective
equipment early on and is continually adding to that. All of the officers have N95 masks, and
wear masks when transporting suspects in vehicles. Suspects are also given masks to wear to
minimize contact.

Resolution No. 20-10-05

             Paul Ashton reviewed the following resolution adopting the Magna Emergency
Response Guidelines Section of the Magna-GSLMSD Emergency Operations Plan. He stated
Salt Lake County has received additional Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security
(CARES) funding from the Federal Government, and it wants to distribute more to Magna. This
amendment to the original agreement allows the funds to be put in a fund at the GSLMSD. All
terms and conditions of the original agreement are the same except Magna is accepting more
money.

RESOLUTION NO. 20-10-05                                                      Date: October 27, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AMENDMENT #1 TO THE CARES ACT SUBGRANT AGREEMENT BETWEEN
       MAGNA AND SALT LAKE COUNTY

                                             RECITALS

      A.     The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

       B.    The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing
body for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

       C.      Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to UCA Title 17: COUNTIES.
                                                5
DATE           TUESDAY                             OCTOBER                             27, 2020


       D.      The CARES Act is a law passed by the Federal Government that provided for an
appropriation of funds to the States and certain Counties for certain governmental emergency
costs and economic relief efforts related to the COVID-19 pandemic.

      E.       In May 2020, the State of Utah and Salt Lake County received their CARES Act
appropriations from the Federal Government.

       F.    In late May 2020, the County, taking a cue from the State regarding the
management of CARES Act funds, and not compelled by the CARES Act to do so, decided it
would send amounts of their appropriation to the municipalities of the county to fund and manage
local COVID-19 efforts.

        G.      Pursuant to the County’s desire to promote local engagement in the COVID-19
relief effort, Magna received an agreement on June 8, 2020 for consideration and possible
approval of Magna.

       H.    On June 23, 2020, pursuant to actions approved by the Council in Resolution 20-
0606, the Mayor signed the agreement with Salt Lake County and accepted $794,779.38 for
CARES Act Relief for Magna’s municipal-service providing entities.

       I.     On October 23, 2020, Magna received an amended agreement for Salt Lake
County that increases Magna’s distributed portion of CARES Act funding from $794,779.38 to
$1,589,558.38.

        J.     Due to the Federal Government updates in uses for CARES Act funding, Magna’s
public safety services providers would benefit from the additional revenues to cover their costs
caused by the pandemic.

                                        RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and Council
hereby approves the attached amended subgrant agreement Exhibit A, to increasing Magna’s
CARES Act funding from $794,779.19 of the County’s appropriation of the CARES Act funding to
$1,589,558.38.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
27 day of October, 2020.

Historical Preservation

                Greg Schulz stated the State Historic Preservation Office is looking to nominate

to try to maintain the historic integrity of the buildings and other structures there.

2550 South Road

                Greg Schulz stated the GSLMSD has enough money to build the road along 2550
South, but not enough to do a full buildout. It had a meeting with the Inland Port Authority to try
to work together to find the approximately $5 million needed to get the road built to its intended
specification. If the GSLMSD cannot find the money, it will have to retrofit the road later after it is
built.
