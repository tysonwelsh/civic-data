I hereby nominate Aileen Conder, a legal assist in the Civil Division of the Utah County
Attorney’s Office as employee of the month for January 2023. Aileen has now worked more
than 15 years in the Civil Division. She has worked for most of those years with one of the more
demanding attorneys in the Civil Division. This year has been excessively challenging because
that demanding attorney became the Civil Division Chief and assumed many responsibilities
which meant that Aileen had to learn many new areas, including but not limited to the
processing of claims against the County. In addition to her new areas of responsibility, Aileen
was assigned a new attorney to the Civil Division which put her in a quasi-training role. Aileen
also helped train two new legal assistants to the Civil Division in 2022. Despite all her enormous
responsibilities, if you have an emergency (whether in the Civil Division or from another
County Department), Aileen will drop what she is doing to help resolve the emergency. The
County sure has a lot of emergencies and thanks in part to Aileen many are solved without her
getting any credit for role in the resolution.

Aileen has a role with many items that make it on the County Commission Agenda and
works very closely with Alice Black and the Commissioners’ Senior Policy Advisors. This role
includes putting items on the Commission Agenda; drafting ordinances, resolutions, and
contracts; locating and uploading back up documents; and following up with approved items to
make sure they get signed and sent to the right parties. Aileen is one of the most consistent
employees in the Civil Division and can be seen in the office most days with a great willingness
to help. Many of the aforementioned items are in her job description and some are not, but to
Aileen she helps no matter what. Also, her job description does not necessarily indicate the level
of helpfulness and kindness needed, but she maxes those out. For these reasons and more,
Aileen should receive the recognition that she so rightfully deserves and be made the employee
of the month for January 2023. ---- Rob Moore

Aileen is diligent, thorough, and always positive. She has been training a new legal assistant
that was recently hired, and therefore I have had opportunities to see what a mastery Aileen has
over all the needs of attorneys in the division. She can anticipate exactly what we might want in
bankruptcy filings, drafting complex resolutions, preparing first versions of interlocal and other
agreements, adjusting presentations, keeping records organized, and more. And on top of it all,
she keeps the office running smoothly with event planning and countless other tasks that most
of us never think about, like organizing donations for important causes in the office.

I have really valued her professionalism, attention to detail, and insights. She is a problem
solver who often proposes courses of action that are exactly what various departments need to
resolve issues efficiently and effectively. She has handled difficult people and situations with
skill and a sincere desire to assist the public and address needs, and I would be happy to see
Aileen recognized and honored in this way. _--- Ben Stanley

Aileen is an exemplary employee. She goes above any beyond her own assignments to help
anyone who comes to her. Whenever I have a question, I know I can ask her, and she will
always point me in the right direction or pause what she is doing to help me herself. Aileen has
recently taken on new assignments herself and has helped train two new legal assistants in our
office. She has done an excellent job with all the changes and training. Aside from her
proficiency in her job and willingness to help, Aileen is one of the nicest people you will meet in
the county and cares about making things better for everyone. ---- Zachary Zundel

List for why Aileen should be employee of the month:
- Aileen is a superstar.
- She is incredibly kind and welcoming.
- She seems to know how to help with any dilemma that the office faces.
- She is willing to take on the tough assignments and has a positive attitude.
- She has a PH.D. level understanding of Microsoft Word. She can take documents with
atrocious formatting issues and cure any defects within a short amount of time.
I would strenuously recommend Aileen for employee of the month. — ---- Adam Beck

I’d like to nominate Aileen Conder for employee of the month. There are many cogs and
gears in the wheels that turn the government. Some are better known or appear to do more than
others. But everyone knows that deep inside the walls where there is little fanfare, a work horse
quietly and diligently pulls more than their own weight, day after day, year after year. Aileen is
that work horse of the civil division. She’s always friendly, always doing more than she’s asked,
and always a positive force for good in the government. We are lucky to have her, and the
people of Utah county, though they don’t know it, are extremely fortunate to have someone like
her working for them. Aileen is more than deserving of this this honor. ---- Ben VanNoy

our team. Her organizational ability and her institutional knowledge of county operations have

19. ADOPT AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE UTAH COUNTY GENERAL PLAN TO
UPDATE THE RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN FOR PUBLIC LANDS RELATED TO THE
PRESERVATON OF PUBLIC ROADS

-Bryce Armstrong, Community Development

(Timestamp: 11:01)

BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT ASSOCIATE DIRECTOR -

| STATED THAT IN 2016 THE UTAH STATE LEGISLATURE PASSED A BILL THAT

REQUIRED EVERY COUNTY IN THE STATE TO ADOPT A RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

| PLAN FOR ALL PUBLIC LANDS WITHIN THEIR COUNTY AND THAT PUBLIC LANDS

IS A DEFINED TERM WHICH 40% OF UTAH COUNTY IS UNDER THAT CATEGORY.

IN 2016 THERE WERE 28 RESOURCES THAT WERE TO BE INCLUDED AS PART OF

| THIS RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN SO COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT WENT
THROUGH THIS PROCESS EARLIER THIS YEAR FOR RENEWABLE ENERGY
RESOURCES, CRITICAL MINERAL RESOURCES, PIPELINES AND UTILITY
CORRIDORS. THE PROCESS TODAY REVOLVES AROUND LANGUAGE THAT PLIPCO
(UTAH PUBLIC LANDS POLICY COORDINATION OFFICE) HAS ASKED EACH COUNTY
TO INTEGRATE INTO THEIR RESOURCE MANAGEMENT PLAN AS IT RELATES TO
PRESERVING PUBLIC ROAD ACCESS RELATED TO PRESERVING THE STATUS OF
CLASS D ROADS AND ROADS THAT MAY BE RECOGNIZED AS PUBLIC THROUGH A
FEDERAL LAWN KNOW AS REVISED STATUTE 2477 WHICH IS ASSOCIATED WITH

THE MINING ACT.
