Salt Lake County Urban County Community Development Block Grant Program

                 Paul Ashton stated White City Metro Township has previously opted into the Salt
Lake County Urban County Community Development Block Grant Program (CDBG). Every three
years the Metro Township has to authorize its participation. If the Council wants to continue to
participate in the program, it will need to authorize the Mayor to sign the interlocal agreement.

             Council Member Shelton, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to authorize
the Mayor to sign the interlocal agreement to participate in the CDBG Program. The motion
passed unanimously.

Form of Government Legislation

                Paul Ashton stated attorneys for the metro townships have been meeting on a
weekly basis to discuss various issues. The attorneys are recommending that the metro
townships approach the legislature, specifically the Political Subdivision Interim Committee, to
pass a statute that would automatically reclassify the metro townships as a city or town, based on
population. A letter is being drafted that each mayor will sign. There have been discussions with
Representative Jordan Teuscher, and he has agreed to sponsor a bill. If White City Metro
Township becomes a formal city, it will increase its ability to obtain funding. A provision will be
written in the bill to allow metro townships to remain members of the districts it currently belongs
to.

Title 18 (Subdivision) & 19 (Zoning) Ordinance Amendment

               Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(MSD), stated draft codes were presented to the planning commission, and a public hearing was
held at the end of April. She reviewed comments from the public hearing as well as emails she
received. She reviewed the following items with the Council:

   -   Allow pavers to be an exception from the impervious surface calculation for parking
       standards for residential lots in the off-street parking.
   -   Individuals using a private road and blocking access. There is nothing in the code that can
       regulate access to the road.
   -   How standards are set for the intensity of use for agricultural uses.
   -   Increase the maximum allowed lot coverage to 40% for all buildings on a residential lot.
   -   Allow Reiki as a permitted use in zones where commercial uses are allowed.
   -   Remove duplex as a permitted use in the A-2 zone.
   -   Allow IADU in the A-2 zone.
   -   Miniature horses.
   -   Guest or caretaker house in the A-2 zone.
   -   Add horse stables within 20 ft of a dwelling.
   -   Landscaping business and related traffic.
   -   Add standards for the parking of commercial vehicles in the A-2 zone.
   -   Add language in the landscaping, screening, and water efficiency chapter to be fully
       compliant with the State of Utah requirements.
