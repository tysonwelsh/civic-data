32   2.1 Presentation on CharityVision’s SightBuddies program.
33   Melanie Davey stated that she’s an intern with CharityVision and explained the SightBuddies
34   program to the Council. She said that they provide free glasses for kids that need them. She said
35   that they are looking for community partners and local eye doctors and asked for
36   recommendations.
37
38   2.2. Mountainland Association of Governments presentation on Pre-Disaster Mitigation Plan
39   Shauna Mecham with MAG presented the updated mitigation plan to the Council. She said that
40   the plan identified things that can be done before a major event occurs to mitigate the impact.
41   She also presented a map that showed various events, such as earthquake or flood, and what
42   areas of the city could be affected.
43
44   2.3 Waste Management Recycling Diversion Report
45   Todd Munger, with Lehi City, and Blake Leonelli, with Waste Management, gave on update to
46   the Council on the success of the City’s recycling program. He stated that they were able to save
47   $251,408 with recycling efforts.

89   5. Amending the compensation schedule for the Mayor in Lehi City Code Chapter 1-6-4.
90      Councilor Albrecht stated that the Mayor should be fairly compensated for their time. She
91      supports the increase but would like more discussion on the amount.
92
93       Councilor Koivisto stated that she doesn’t feel that they need to increase the salary. She also
94       wanted to make it clear that the salary increase was not initiated by Mayor Johnson. She felt
95       that the increase wasn’t justified because Lehi is structured with a part time Mayor, and usually
96       elected officials aren’t in office for the money, it’s because they want to serve. She would like
     Lehi City Council Meeting                         2                                      July 13, 2021
 97       to see where the Council plans to take the money from in the budget that will be used to cover
 98       the increase.
 99
100       Paul stated that he was in support of some type of increase. He felt that it was important for
101       this increase to not take affect until January 1, 2022.
102
103       Councilor Condie stated that when a Mayor signs up for office, they don’t often realize the
104       time commitment that comes with that office. He stated that they don’t want to make the
105       increase so attractive that they get people that are in it just for the money. He said that great
106       Mayors want to serve regardless of the pay.
107
108       a. Public Hearing
109       Rob Ludlow stated that there is a lack in compensation for the job that’s being done. He
110       would like to see the salary increased to 85,000-95,000 per year. He said it’s a matter of
111       fairness and they need to increase this to bring in and attract a strong leader.
112
113       Jason Harris stated that he supports an increase and believes the Mayor should be well
114       compensated.
115
116       Michelle Stallings stated that she is against an increase. This is a part time position, and it
117       should be paid as such. If the City wants to look at this as more then a part time job then they
118       should change the form of government.
119
120       Heather Newall stated that Lehi deserves to have the best Mayors and there’s nothing wrong
121       with correctly valuing the position.
122
123       Dave Scoville stated that he was in support of an increase.
124
125       Councilor Koivisto stated that she would like to table this item to a time that Mayor Johnson
126       can be here so they know where he stands and can have him speak to the matter.
127
128       Councilor Hancock stated that he doesn’t believe it’s necessary for the Mayor to be here
129       because this is a Council decision.
