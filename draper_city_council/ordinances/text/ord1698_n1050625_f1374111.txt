Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1698

On January 6, 2026, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1698 amending Title
3, Chapter 5 of the Draper City Municipal Code to consolidate the Community
Engagement Committee and the Special Event Arena Committee into the Community
Engagement and Events Committee. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper
City Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/

Published this 8th day of January, 2026.
