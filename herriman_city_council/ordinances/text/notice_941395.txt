PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on August 28, 2024, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2024-23 amending the Herriman City Zoning Map for .81 acres of property located at 13333 South Rose Canyon Road from A-1-43 (Agricultural single-family) to R-1-10 (Residential single-family). A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
