## Pillar 1 — Baseline statistical screen (per-year, OCR-weighted)
`screen_corpus.py` (skill screener) on both minutes corpora:

- **Council (166):** dict_ratio median **0.768** (min 0.687), **0** dict_ratio
  outliers; weird_char median 0.0003, **0** outliers; **0 stubs / 0 read errors**.
  split_word_rate median 0.52/1k (max 16.21), 38 relative outliers. Per-year dict
  medians 0.754–0.780 (flat); the only year that breaks is **2021** with a raised
  split median (**3.69/1k** vs ~0.4–0.75 neighbors).
- **PC (91):** dict_ratio median **0.735** (min 0.689), **0** outliers; weird_char
  **0** outliers; **0 stubs**. split_word median 0.49/1k (max 12.42), 16 relative
  outliers; per-year dict medians 0.730–0.744 (flat — no year breaks).

**Interpretation:** the split_word flags are **benign** — dict_ratio stays high
(~0.74–0.78) and weird_char is ~0 wherever they fire, i.e. these are pdftotext
line-wrap artifacts in born-digital layouts, not corruption (contrast the Ogden
failure: ~25/1k split *with* a 0 baseline and depressed dict_ratio). The 2021
council split bump was spot-checked against source (see Pillar 2 #3) and is clean
line-wrapping. `ends_mid` (131/166) is the advisory page-break-truncation signal,
benign for minutes. **No corruption outlier in either corpus.**

## Findings (all advisory — none blocking)
1. **LBA stage cross-tag (cosmetic).** `db/civic.db`'s 22 LBA motions carry
   `stage='mba_vote'`. The `body` split is correct; only the stage string reuses
   the MBA bucket. Documented in `db/SCHEMA.md`. *Fix (optional):* add an `lba_vote`
   stage in a future `build_db.py` pass.
2. **Referral layer is large** (269 links; 189 high / 69 med / 11 low) relative to
   peer cities (Taylorsville 28, South Jordan 13). Not verified link-by-link here.
   *Recommend:* a targeted spot-check of the `high` tier and a precision-tune pass
   (the skill's "dump-mediums-weakest-first" step) at next refresh; treat `medium`/
   `low` as flag-only until then. Do **not** rebuild casually — `build_db.py` drops
   the referral table.
3. **Build-note wording drift (doc-only).** The build note referenced "an OCR'd
   2020–21 council doc"; the council OCR set is actually **2023–2026** (2020–21 is
   born-digital text). Corrected in the docs; immaterial to any count.
