I.    Call to Order – Vice Chair Brewer.
Vice-Chair Brewer called the RDA Meeting to order at approximately 7:06 p.m.

II.    Public Hearing on Proposed 2022-23 Fiscal Year Budget
Vice-Chair Brewer opened the public hearing at 7:07 pm. There were no comments and the hearing
was closed.

III.   Other Business.
There was no additional business.

IV.    Adjourn RDA Meeting.
Board Member Fotheringham moved to adjourn the RDA Board Meeting and reconvene in a
City Council Meeting. Board Member Dahle seconded the motion. The motion passed with the
unanimous consent of the Board

The RDA meeting adjourned at 7:09 pm.
