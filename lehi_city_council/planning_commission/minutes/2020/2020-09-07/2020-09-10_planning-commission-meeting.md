3.3 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code amendment to Chapter 20, Home Occupations, clarifying the storage of vehicles as a
prohibited home occupation.
    Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

    Hemmert clarified rental charge for storage use. The Commission asked about enforcing.
    Sierra stated they are trying to avoid future use for storage. Hemmert stated he is not in favor
    of "reprimanding" when something hasn't taken place. Ownership and money changing
    hands. Mike stated the intent was to regulate storage. Ross asked if there was a way to
    differentiate from res to commercial and personal property. Greg asked if this was a real
    issue or if it was something that could be visited later. Sierra stated there have been a few
    inquiries about rental space. Mike stated he agrees with Commission that the City doesn't
    want to regulate if it isn't an issue but would like it addressed. Nielsen sated the nuisance was
    the unsightliness and if it was being regulated for the commercial that everyone should be
    regulated other than people who are just making money. Rather the sight than the money.
    Hemmert worries about regulation just to regulate if there isn't a public’s health safety and
    welfare issue. Hemmert doesn't want to see it turn into storage space. Everett stated this is
    specific to home occupation.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing for comment.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

    Everett stated the right to enjoy property for all, not just for one. The goodness of the
    neighborhood by allowing limits. Discussion ensued. Hemmert would like to see some
    tailoring of the item.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson motioned to forward a negative recommendation of
        Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 20,
        Home Occupations, clarifying the storage of vehicles as a prohibited home
        occupation. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Hemmert,
        no; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, no; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, no. The vote failed.

    Commissioner Hemmert stated he would entertain another motion.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson motioned to table the item at the request of the applicant.
        of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development Code amendment to Chapter 20,
        Home Occupations, clarifying the storage of vehicles as a prohibited home
        occupation. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
        Commissioner Hereth, yes. The passed unanimously.
