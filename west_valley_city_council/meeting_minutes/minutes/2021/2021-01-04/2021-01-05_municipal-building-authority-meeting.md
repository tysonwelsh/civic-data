    ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2021
    Chairman Nordfelt stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2021 had
    been scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been
    used in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board
    and not mandatory.

    Chairman Nordfelt called for a motion.

    Ms. Lang moved to elect Jake Fitisemanu as Chair of the Municipal Building Authority for
    2021 and Don Christensen as Vice Chair of the Municipal Building Authority for 2021.
    Mr. Buhler seconded the motion.

    A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

    MOTION TO RECOVENE IN CLOSED SESSION
    Upon motion by Mr. Christensen, the Agency/Council reconvened in Closed Session to
    discuss Pending Litigation and Professional Competency.


    THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE MUNICIPAL BUILDING
