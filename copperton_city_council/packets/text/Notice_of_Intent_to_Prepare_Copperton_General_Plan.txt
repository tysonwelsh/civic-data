                               Notice of Intent to Prepare
                     Copperton Metro Township General Plan Update

This notice is given pursuant to Utah Code Ann. 10-9a-203 and is being sent to those entitled to such
notice under Utah Code Ann. 10-9a-203(1).
PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that Copperton Metro Township intends during the remainder of the 2019 calendar
year and during the 2020 calendar year to update the Copperton Metro Township General Plan. The
update is being prepared in compliance with Utah Code Ann. 10-9a-401 through -407. The update
addresses long range planning for the entire community, within the adopted boundaries and will contain
the State required elements as well as additional important planning subjects. The intent of Copperton
Metro Township is to complete this amendment effort by March 2020.
The public and each of the affected entities are invited to provide information for Copperton Metro
Township to consider in the process of preparing, adopting, and implementing the plan update
concerning:
      (a) impacts the use of land proposed in the plan update may have on such affected entity; and
      (b) uses of land within the plan area that such affected entity is planning or considering that may
      conflict with the plan update.
Interested parties are encouraged to subscribe to future notices through the State Public Notice website
(http://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html) or the project website for further information, including detailed
meeting agendas and other issues related to the planning process.
