WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 12, 2020

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 11:00 a.m. on August 12, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Commissioner Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Natalie Nelson, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION __OF__§_ COOPERATIVE || AGREEMENT ___ BETWEEN
WASHINGTON COUNTY AND ZION NATIONAL PARK FOR RESTROOM
CLEANING SERVICES AT THE KOLOB FINGERS PARK ENTRANCE

Commissioner Almquist explained that the West Zion/Kolob Fingers area in Zion National
Park has not been open during the COVID-19 pandemic due to lack of staffing. It is
important that all areas of the Park remain open in order to disperse visitors throughout the
Washington County area.

Commissioner Cox stated that the agreement will be between Washington County and a
private company to provide restroom cleaning services at the Kolob Fingers Park entrance.

Chairman Iverson clarified that the Visitor’s Center will remain closed.

Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that there were two (2) bidders for the contract
to provide the necessary services. The South Campground has also remained closed,
however, could potentially open up with the restroom facilities being taken care of by the
selected contractor.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Approve the Cooperative
Agreement Between Washington County and Zion National Park for
Restroom Cleaning Services at the Kolob Fingers Entrance, as
Resolution No, R-2020-2636, Subject to Legal Review with the Potential
Addition of the South Campground. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
11:14a.m.
