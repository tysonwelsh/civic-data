Discussed 19.38.060 Slope Protection - Additional bonding related to slope protection. An urban
hydrology bond will not be issued for retaining walls without the grading permit, look at current
bonding language and invite Alex Rudowski, grading and Tolin Hessel, engineering to explain.
Discussed 19.38.070 Grading Standards - Allow and follow rules for retaining walls, foundation walls
and egress, foundation wall versus basement. Replace subject “to” with “shall” comply with.
Discussed 19.38.080 Site Access – Private access roads, private roadways, and driveways defined.
Roadway Standards considers as a driveway if less than 150 feet. *Revisit next month to discuss with
engineering.
Discussed 19.38.090 Trails – Trails master plan not implemented, trails and open space plan followup. Trail threshold for public access and dedication, cluster developments and public versus private
access, and find the standard defining proportional access. *Revisit Section E next month.
Discussed 19.38.100 Fences – Height and setback.
Discussed 19.38.110 Tree and Vegetation Protection – Confirm application of International Wildland
Urban Interface Code and confirm with the County which version, and which version the Emigration
Canyon City prefers. Check on the version adopted by the County plan approval process. Confirm FR
zone removal of silviculture and agriculture. Commercial Apiaries and allowed uses. Confirm time
limits of expiration and activity on land use and building permits. Spell out UFA with Unified Fire
Authority.
4) Other Business Items. (As Needed)
No other business items to discuss.
