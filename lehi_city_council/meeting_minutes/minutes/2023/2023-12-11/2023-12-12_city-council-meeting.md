Pre Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Albrecht gave the opening comment.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Finance Update
Dean Lundell presented an update to the Council. He reported that the sales tax is trending well
and the general fund balance is healthy. He also reported on the utility fund revenues and
expenses. He also stated that there is a budget amendment on the agenda which includes
adjustments for capital project carryovers, grants, fiber allocations, Local Building Authority,
miscellaneous capital projects, and Station #84.

2.2 Fiber Network Update
Shaye Ruitenbeek provided an update on the project. She said that total subscriptions are at 583.
She also noted that all cabinet areas are designed and have been submitted to the Development
Review Committee. She also noted that the overall take rate is at 24.5%.

2.3 Discussion of Subdivision Review Process
Kim Stuthers informed the Council that changes will need to be made to city ordinances to
comply with SB 174, which states that the City Council can no longer be the Land Use Authority
for preliminary or final plats. There was a discussion about preliminary plat approval process and
public hearings. The Council agreed that they would prefer to have a different process for
PUD/PRD/PC subdivisions. Mr. Struthers stated that he would draft an ordinance for proposed
changes that would be presented to the Council.
