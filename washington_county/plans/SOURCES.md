# plans/ — sources

All plans retrieved 2026-07-20 from Washington County Community Development. **All born-digital**
(pdftotext, no OCR needed). Raw PDFs retained in `raw/` when <50MB; text sidecars in `text/`;
`index.csv` is the catalog.

- **General Plan (2010)** — `washco.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/cdev/pdf/gp/washco-general-plan.pdf`
  (406 pp; Public Lands element adopted 2010-11-16, later element amendments ~2012).
- **Attainable Housing Plan (2021)** — `.../wp-content/uploads/2021/09/washco-attainable-housing-plan.pdf`
  (county MIH-equivalent; amended June 2025 by Ord. 2025-1295-O, see `../ordinances/`).
- **Transportation Master Plan (2024)** — 63 MB, **link-only** per >50MB rule (text extracted).
- **Southern Utah Regional Trail Standards (2024)**.
- **Community general plans** (13, 2010–2011) + 2 legacy 1990s — `.../cdev/pdf/cgp/community-*.pdf`.
- **Overlay zones** (3) — Pine Valley / Scenic Byway / New Harmony — `.../uploads/2024/04/comdev-general-plan-*-overlay-zone.pdf`.
