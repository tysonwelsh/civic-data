MEMO
To: City Council
From: Traci Gundersen, City Attorney
Date: 2025-08-19
Re: Approval of Ordinance #1668 or Ordinance #1669


Comments:

The City Council will consider amending Draper City Municipal Code Title 19 – Ethics,
either by limiting the prohibition on employing relatives to only those of elected city
officers (Ordinance 1668) or by allowing the employment of relatives of city officers under
specified conditions with City Manager approval (Ordinance 1669).
