BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately
1.43 acres located on the north side of Richins Rd. (12700 South), at approximately 1595
East 12700 South (Exhibit C). The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is
requesting that a Zoning Map Amendment be approved for the purpose of rezoning the
properties from the RA2 zone to the R4 zone.

This area of the City is an older part of town with many parcels and homes that pre-date
the City incorporation in 1978. There are four properties subject to this request, three of
which contain homes built in 1913, 1971, and 1986. In March of 2021 the property owners
adjusted the parcel boundaries in order to make the property lines match historic fence
boundaries. None of the parcels complied with the minimum lot size in the RA2 zone as

application is to place the existing properties into a zone with a smaller minimum lot size
so that the existing properties comply with minimum lot size requirements. No new lots or
parcels are proposed. One of the four parcels is vacant and the homeowner wishes to build
a single-family home on the parcel.

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Residential
Low/Medium Density land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit D). This
category is characterized as follows:




The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately
two dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit E). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC)

Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with
little impact on its surroundings and municipal services; to generally preserve the character
of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and preserve conditions favorable to large-
lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl. This zone is
intended to promote a combination of retail and service facilities which, in character and
scale, meet day to day needs of nearby residents.” The RA2 zoning abuts the subject
properties on all four sides.

Zoning Map Amendment. There are 21 properties that front onto Richins Rd, or 12700 South.
All of those properties are zoned RA2. Most of those properties pre-date the City
incorporation, and only two comply with the minimum RA2 lot size standards. The subject
properties did not conform to the minimum lot size before the boundary adjustment in
2021. Three of the properties do not conform to the minimum lot size today. The fourth
property, at 0.50 acres, complies, but has substantial encumbrances due to a canal
easement and potential roadway dedication needs that reduces its buildable and

The proposed rezone to R4 would allow the properties to be considered legal conforming
on parcel size. The R4 zone has a minimum lot size of 10,000 square feet instead of the
20,000 square feet required by the RA2 zone. Per DCMC Section 9-8-020, the purpose of
the R4 zone is to “permit medium to medium-high density residential developments with
single-family homes. These zones are intended to foster thriving and well maintained
neighborhoods that allow upscale single-family homes on smaller lots that require less
maintenance and provide a balanced lifestyle.”

Criteria For Approval.
A Text and Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City
Council and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation
to the City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in
Section 9-5-060(E) of the DCMC:

        1. Map Amendments:
            a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies
               of the city's general plan;
            b. Whether the proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character of
               existing development in the vicinity of the subject property;

Planning Division Review.

   1. The proposed rezone would allow properties that have historically been small to
      become legal conforming to the code.
   2. The properties are not large enough to subdivide in the future.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.

   1. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including but not
      limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm
      water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water and refuse collection;
      Other than noted below, we are not aware of any inadequacies of the facilities intended
      to serve this property.
          a. The increase in density from three residential units per 1.38 acres to
              approximately four units per 1.38 acres constitutes a minor increase in the
              amount of traffic to and from the subject area based on the subject parcel. One
              single family residential structure and associated agricultural uses constitutes up
              to nine daily trips and two peak hour trips, based on industry standard vehicle trip
              tables. In the future three or so vehicles could be added to the surrounding streets
              if the parcel were to be subdivided. The anticipated increase in traffic is well below
              the 100 peak hour trips that would require a Traffic Impact Study.
          b. Connectivity with the subject parcel to a public right-of-way is on Richins Road
              (12700 S), a local residential street. Access locations will be required to meet
              Draper City Standards as outlined in our Master Transportation Plan.
          c. There are no existing storm drain facilities available to the subject area. Provisions
              for onsite storm drainage will need to be addressed with any subdivision or site
              plan application, and shall comply with the provisions of the development
              requirements within the Draper City Municipal Code.
          d. Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by South Valley Sewer District. Any
              subdivision application will require a commitment to serve from the Sewer District
              that facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.
          e. Drinking water facilities will be provided by WaterPro. Any subdivision application
              will require a commitment to serve from the WaterPro that facilities are adequate
              to provide service for the proposed uses.

APPLICANT QUESTIONAIRE
                                   Zone map explanation and request



The request is being submitted to allow for a building permit to be built at 1595 East Richins Road
(12700South)

1-Zoning designation



2-Reason

The Family that owned the property recently re-aligned the property with the permission of the
landowners on both the East and West sides of the property. By doing so, this created a more uniform
plot that was, and has been the assumption of adjacent land owner for many years. The lot was plotted
and engineered by a private engineering firm and presented to me as a legal buildable lot.

Request

We have lived in the Draper community for more than 30 years and would like to downsize. The request
is to allow the owner to petition the city to allow either a re-zone, or to allow the property to be
accepted as a one third acre Non-conforming lot.

Justification

To allow the owner to move forward with the build of a home, with the blessing of the city and the
neighbors to complete a newly built home on a piece of property that has been vacant since 1913. This
would add a nice finish to the street as this is the last remaining unattended lot.

3-How is the requested lot zoning designation harmonious with the overall character.

The lot gives proper dimension to match adjacent lots. If the lot remains at current boundary
designations. The owner plans to build a small home that matches to surrounding homes and beautify
the neighborhood and street. There are also homes along the same street with equal size that will
maintain a consistent appearance. A larger lot may encourage a larger home that could look “out of
place”

4-Facilities and Service

The proposed home will be service by existing culinary lines location 20 feet from the property line.
Permits and fees have been paid to the city pending approval by the zoning commission. Additionally,
the sewer lines run along the same side of the street as the current lot, within 8 feet of the property
lines (estimated). There is also Power available at the location.

5-Consistent with City Goals

We consider this to be in line with city objectives to carefully allow the build of property and home that
reflect a careful consideration in preserving the culture, historic value, and aesthetic values of the
community.
