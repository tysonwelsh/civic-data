PRESENTATION: DIXIE HIGH SCHOOL FUTURE FARMERS OF AMERICA
(FFA)

Students of the Dixie High School Future Farmers of America (FFA) Ag Issues Team
presented pro and con arguments of the Lake Powell Pipeline (LPP). The title of their
presentation is: “The Lake Powell Pipeline: Hope or Hype?” Stakeholders within the
Washington County area are worried that current water sources will become limited in the

CONSIDERATION OF FINAL PLAT AND VARIATION FROM TITLE 11
SUBDIVISION STANDARDS

Washington County Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the
Planning Commission reviewed and approved the preliminary plat of a parcel located in
Brookside on which, the owner desires to construct a home. The parcel needs to be brought
into compliance by going through the subdivision process and approval. However, the
applicant is unable to acquire the necessary land needed for the right-of-way width as
required by county standards. There are special circumstances attached to the applicant’s
property that do not generally apply to other properties in the same zone. It is recommended
to grant the variation from the current twenty-five (25) foot wide right-of-way requirement
to a twenty (20) foot width.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2019-2442, A RESOLUTION
AMENDING THE MAKEUP OF THE HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE DISTRICT'S
ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROL BOARD

Chairman Cox explained that the current Hurricane Valley Fire District Administrative
Control Board is made up of elected or former elected officials nominated by each
municipality within the District, namely Hurricane, LaVerkin, Leeds, Togerville, and
Virgin and one appointee selected by Washington County. It is proposed to add two (2)
additional representatives to the board; one (1) from Rockville and one (1) Springdale.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-
2442, A Resolution Amending the Makeup of the Hurricane Valley Fire
District’s Administrative Control Board. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.

Commissioner Iverson reported that the Legislature is currently in session and he has been
attending those sessions to represent Washington County. Several impacts to the County
