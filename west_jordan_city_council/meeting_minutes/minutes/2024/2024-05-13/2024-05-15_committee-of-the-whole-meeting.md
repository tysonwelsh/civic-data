 2. DISCUSSION TOPICS
       a. Fiscal Year 2025 Tentative Budget Discussion
          Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck oriented the Council with the following FY
          2025 Tentative Budgets:
            • The General Fund

             •   Proposed Property Tax Increase of 3.5%
                 Council Member Green moved to direct staff to prepare the appropriate
                 resolution to notify the County of truth in taxation at a rate of 3.5%.
                 Chair Jacob seconded the motion, which passed by unanimous vote (7-0).

             •   Sales Tax Revenue
                 The Council discussed establishing a policy that would set reserves at a specific
                 percentage of the annual budget, with any additional revenues going to the CIP
                 Fund. Council Member Whitelock expressed concern that the number of City
                 employees was growing much too fast.

             •   Council Office Budget
                 Council Office Director Alan Anderson demonstrated modules available with
                 PrimeGov software for additional cost. Council Member Whitelock commented that
                 most features of the Committee Manager Module applied to general administration,
                 and suggested the cost would be more appropriate in the Mayor’s Budget. The
                 Council and staff discussed convenience versus cost.
                 Council Member Bloom did not believe the City needed to pay extra for live closed
                 captioning with the Swagit EASE Module considering the many third-party
                 captioning options available. Council Member Green suggested the transcription
                 feature could be used to prepare meeting minutes and spoke in favor of the live

             •   Justice Court

             •   City Administration Budget
                 Council Member Whitelock said she was not in favor of adding a full-time
                 Communications Specialist, a CAO Intern, and $17,500 for site visits for economic
                 development as proposed in the Tentative Budget. City Administrator Korban Lee
                 described management analyst studies and tasks that would be assigned to a CAO
                 Intern. Council Member Green said he would want to see metrics of expectations
                 and output for the proposed Communications Specialist position. Community
                 Engagement & Government Affairs Manager Tauni Barker spoke of requests from
                 residents for various forms of communication, and said her staff did a great job with
                 the resources available to them but could not increase production without an
                 increase in staffing. Council Member Bloom believed the proposed Communications
                 Specialist position could help the City meet established goals and felt it was a wise
                 choice. Vice Chair Lamb believed staff would not propose adding a Communication
                 Specialist position to the budget without strongly believing the position was
                 needed. Three Council Members expressed support for a Communications
                 Specialist, CAO Intern, and funds for site visits for economic development as
                 proposed in the Tentative Budget.

             •   Police Department
                 Police Chief Wallentine answered questions regarding police staffing requests, and
                 listed police staffing request goals: maintain ability to respond to increasing
                 demands for service; meet the challenge of high priority call increases; proactively
                 serve the business community and address economic impact crimes; and provide
                 an additional School Resource Officer with funding partnership from a charter
                 school. Chief Wallentine said he would be happy to discuss the possibility of
                 changing the Code to allow non-sworn officers to issue parking tickets if the Council
                 desired. The Council and staff discussed the benefits and challenges of employing
                 active military personnel in the Police and Fire Departments.

             •   Fire Department
                 Council Members expressed interest in discussing the possible addition of a mental
                 health officer in the Fire Department. Fire Chief Maxfield spoke of the importance
                 of cancer screenings for firefighters.
                 Council Member Green suggested development-based triggers should be
                 established to indicate needed Fire Department expansion. Chief Maxfield
                 answered questions from the Council about emergency vehicles navigating through
                 traffic, and the possibility of providing evening firefighter training programs. A
                 majority of the Council expressed a desire to allocate funds to the budget for
                 additional turnout gear for firefighters as a one-time CIP Fund expense.

              •   Public Services Department
                  Responding to a question from Council Member Green about the cemetery, staff
                  noted that the Tentative Budget included funds for the addition of a columbarium,
                  which was a service currently not offered by the cemetery. The Council and staff
                  discussed possible improvements to the rodeo arena, and Council Member Green
                  asked staff to bring back cost estimates for possible inclusion in the FY 2025 Budget.
                  Staff answered questions about funds allocated for the Western Stampede in the FY
                  2025 Tentative Budget. The Council discussed adding a multi-use events stage to
                  Veterans Memorial Park. Mr. Lee spoke of challenges in planning the Western
                  Stampede each year from the beginning of July when the budget was approved by
                  the Council near the end of June. He suggested the Council review and approve the
                  Western Stampede budgets earlier in the year going forward.


 3. ADJOURN
     Chair Jacob adjourned the meeting at 9:24 pm.
