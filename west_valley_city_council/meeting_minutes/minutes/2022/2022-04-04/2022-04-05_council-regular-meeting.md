COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Mike Markham stated that he has lived in West Valley City for many years. He
    indicated he had a conversation with an employee several weeks ago that left him
    very disappointed. Mr. Markham stated that he believes employees who present to
    Council should attend in person. He indicated that the network issues in the Study
    Meeting made that evident tonight.

       Jim Vesock stated that he believes the highest paid employees should be present at
       every Council meeting and should be aware of what is going on. Mr. Vesock
       thanked the Council for holding staff accountable for Strategic Planning items. He
       indicated that he spoke with a resident who had a negative experience with an
       Animal Control employee. Mr. Vesock stated that Code Enforcement and Parking
       Compliance should work on weekends. He indicated that his neighbor’s husband
       died and the COP officers were a great help to her.

       Alan Naumann encouraged the City to opt in to HB 411. He indicated that the City
       can still opt out if they are not happy with how the program progresses.

B.     CITY MANAGER COMMENTS
       Wayne Pyle, City Manager, had no comments.

       Upon inquiry, members of the City Council had no comments.

 Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
 Meeting scheduled April 5, 2022, in order for the City Council to hear and consider
 public comments regarding Re-Opening the FY 2021-2022 Budget.

 Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
 information as follows:

         State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
         allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
         West Valley City holds public hearings on budget amendments on a
         quarterly basis each fiscal year.

         A Public Notice was posted March 17, 2022, in public view at City Hall,
         the Family Fitness Center and UCCC; also, posted on the Public Notice
         Website and West Valley City’s Website. Notice was given that a public
         hearing is to be held April 5, 2022, at 6:30 p.m., West Valley City Hall,
         3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah.

 Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing.

 Jim Vesock stated that he would like to know where the funds for the landscaping
 at the Veteran’s Memorial Park are coming from. He stated that he is also concerned
 about the trailer being purchased for My Hometown and whether it was necessary
 or needing to be designated specifically for that program.

 Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

B.    ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING APPLICATION Z-4-2022, FILED
      BY JAKE LARSEN, REQUESTING A ZONE CHANGE FROM R-1-10
      (SINGLE UNIT DWELLING RESIDENTIAL, MINIMUM LOT SIZE 10,000
      SQUARE FEET) TO C-2 (GENERAL COMMERCIAL) FOR PROPERTY
      LOCATED AT 2858 SOUTH 5600 WEST
      Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
      Meeting scheduled April 5, 2022, in order for the City Council to hear and consider
      public comments regarding application Z-4-2022, Filed by Jake Larsen, Requesting a
      Zone Change From R-1-10 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, Minimum Lot Size
      10,000 Square Feet) to C-2 (General Commercial) for Property Located at 2858 South
      5600 West.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             The owner of the subject property also owns the two parcels to the west and
             south. These parcels were rezoned in October 2020. An access easement
             will be conveyed to West Valley City that will allow for the construction of
             a road to connect to the existing roads to the north and south. The property
             east of the proposed access easement and road was rezoned to C-2 for a
             hotel development. The property to the west was rezoned to RM for
             townhomes. The subject parcel was not included in the 2020 rezone request
   MINUTES OF COUNCIL REGULAR MEETING APRIL 5, 2022

           because it was in different ownership. Since that time, the hotel developer
           has acquired the property and would like to incorporate this parcel into the
           hotel development, which will be included as part of Lot 1 of the Hotel 5600
           Subdivision.

           Assuming this zone change is approved, the owner plans to use the area for
           the hotel’s trash enclosure and the remainder would be landscaped.

    Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor or
    opposition, Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

    Wayne Pyle discussed proposed Resolution 22-57 that would accept a Public Access
    Easement and a Public Sidewalk Easement from JTK Brothers Corporation for
    Property Located at 2912 South Glen Eagles Drive.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             The proposed Hotel 5600 Subdivision is located north of the JTK Brothers
             Corporation parcel and the unfinished Glen Eagles Drive (2900 South). The
             Hotel 5600 Subdivision is proposing a public access to extend from the
             5655 West Public Alley on the northerly boundary to Glen Eagles Drive
             (2900 South) on the southerly boundary. As this public access will connect
             to a public alley and a public street, a Public Access Easement and a Public
             Sidewalk Easement was required through the proposed subdivision. As
             portions of the public sidewalk and public access fall outside the boundaries
             of the subdivision onto Lot 4 of the Colt Plaza III Subdivision, the developer

B.    RESOLUTION 22-58: AUTHORIZE THE CITY TO ENTER INTO A
      RIGHT OF WAY PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND ACCEPT A
      WARRANTY DEED AND A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION
      EASEMENT WITH AND FROM CHARLOTTE AND CALEB OTTLEY
      FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT 4441 SOUTH 4625 WEST
      Wayne Pyle discussed proposed Resolution 22-58 that would authorize the City to
      Enter into a Right of Way Purchase Agreement and Accept a Warranty Deed and a
      Temporary Construction Easement With and From Charlotte and Caleb Ottley for
      Property Located at 4441 South 4625 West.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             The Charlotte C. Ottley and Caleb D. Ottley property is one of several
             properties affected by the required removal and replacement of pedestrian
             ramps to meet current ADA standards as part of the 2022 Pedestrian Ramp
             Project. As part of this project pedestrian ramps will be constructed at
             various locations throughout the City in locations where they do not
             currently exist and existing pedestrian ramps which do not comply with
             current ADA standards will be removed and replaced.

             The Warranty Deed and the Temporary Construction Easement will
             facilitate construction of the pedestrian ramps. Compensation in the amount
             of $425.00 was negotiated based upon land values for properties in the area.
