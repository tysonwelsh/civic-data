Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Executive Director, invited Liz Theriault to the lectern to brief the Council on 
                      the new web page created for the Alta Canyon Sports Center. Ms. Theriault provided an 
                      overview of the new web page. Mr. Fratto also briefed the Council on upcoming Agenda 
                      items.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Brooke D'Sousa invited all to attend the Sandy Club Annual Family 
                      Carnival on June 17th.

                      Council Member Zach Robinson gave an update from the Alta Canyon Advisory 
                      Committee meeting. The committee is engaged and functioning.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Interim ULCT Legislative 
                      Policy Committee meeting that she and Council Member Brooke D'Sousa attended. She 
                      spoke about the presentation by Robert Spendlove at the LPC meeting and asked if the 
                      Council would like to have this presentation at a future Council meeting. Council 
                      expressed an interest to schedule this presentation at a future meeting.

                      Council Member Ryan Mecham invited all to attend the Summer Kickoff Barbecue in 
                      Historic Sandy on June 15th. He thanked Administration, Dawn Sidwell and Tracy 
                      Cowdell for their efforts and help to organize this event.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the Historic Committee. The 
                      Heritage Festival will take place this September and is a city-wide event.

                      Council Member Scott Earl spoke about the speeding in Sandy City and asked for 
                      additional information from Administration regarding the new Slow Down Sandy initiative.

                      Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged all those who supported the military this 
                      past Memorial Day weekend. She appreciated all the events that took place across the 
                      state. And she shared a personal story - she took her father to Layton City to visit a 
                      replica of the Vietnam War Memorial. She recommended to the community to visit this 
                      memorial if you are able to.


Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski thanked the Council Members for joining the employee picnic last week. 
                      This Friday is the launch of the movies in the park at the Rio Tinto stadium. All are 
                      welcome. She spoke about the traffic and speeding concerns and is committed to 
                      enforcing traffic laws in Sandy. A week from Friday, the Mayors and Police Chiefs from 
                      across the valley are meeting at the Amphitheater for a press conference to send the 
                      message to "slow down" and to let the community know that our police will enforce traffic 
                      and speed laws. "Slow Down Sandy" - you will hear that message a lot this summer. 
                      Mayor Z recently attended the Utah Peace Officers event. The Governor's 20 Award was 
                      given to our Police Chief, Greg Severson and she congratulated him on this achievement. 
                      June marks Pride Month and mentioned that all are welcome in Sandy. The new 
                      pickleball courts opened in Sandy City. The Central Wasatch Commission launched an 
                      environmental dashboard on the website.
