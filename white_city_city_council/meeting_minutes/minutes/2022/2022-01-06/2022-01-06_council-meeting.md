    Engineering Projects update

               Shane Ellis, Salt Lake County Engineering, stated the Walk White City Committee
had a meeting in December, a website has been created, and a mailer will go out to the
community. There will also be a community open house that is scheduled for January 27, 2022,
from 5:00 – 7:00 pm at Eastmont Middle School. A survey was finished for the sidewalk master
plan. The White City Canal Trail is in the hands of Salt Lake County Parks and Recreation.
Madeline Galang-Fransico, Salt Lake County Parks and Recreation, will contact Mayor Flint to
discuss the progress of the canal trail.

               Mayor Flint stated there is $800,000 in ZAP tax that is dedicated to the canal trail.
As the project moves forward, she asked Mr. Ellis to make sure that money remains dedicated.

               Mr. Ellis stated he did not think that was up to him to make that decision.

                 Mayor Flint stated Salt Lake County Parks and Recreation made the decision to
dedicate those funds to go along with the work. The $800,000 needs to be preserved for the trail.
Mr. Ellis is part of the engineering of this project.

                 Mr. Ellis stated he does not have any control over those funds.

             Paul Ashton stated what Mr. Ellis does have control over is when someone asks
what the money should be used for, he can inform those individuals that it is dedicated to the

                 Mr. Ashton stated do not let the other engineers have that funding.

                 Mr. Ellis stated he told Ms. Galang- Francisco that the Council wanted to continue

and the information has been mapped out. There are a couple of areas that have also been
surveyed. The preliminary design has started and that will help determine what the costs will be
so funding can be obtained. The preliminary design phase will continue over the next few months
to a year.

                 Council Member Little asked if the Walk White City study is part of the sidewalk
inventory.

grant purposes, it enables them to go after the funds.

                 Council Member Price asked if the study also includes the new school.

               Council Member Perry stated there will be an open house to identify public
perception of walkability or where they want to see walkability.

                 Council Member Price stated the walkability meeting had a good turnout.

             Mr. Ashton stated the mailing should be arriving soon. There is a website with an
unusual URL address, it is safe to click on.

                 Mayor Flint stated this website has strictly committee work.

               Council Member Perry stated the goal of the website is to allow anyone to indicate

                Mr. Ellis stated the light on 700 east and Larkspur is scheduled to be installed in

already been done.

               Mayor Flint suggested holding the public hearing for the General Plan in March or
April.

Unified Fire Authority

               Chief Cline reviewed the November and December call for service statistics. Last
month was the fifth highest in COVID cases since the pandemic started. Due to the increase in
COVID cases, the fire stations will be closed to the public; however, CPR classes will be held with
social distancing and mask requirements. There has been an 18 percent increase in COVID
related calls since June 2021. Fire crews are tested before the beginning of their shifts to
determine if they can continue to work. Crews will be in Dimple Dell Park doing wildfire mitigation
work. Sandy City has a new Mayor, and she has a lot of things she is working on, and public
safety is one of her objectives. There have been two positions that were vacant but have since
been filled.

                 Captain Ken Aldridge stated the 2022-2023 strategic plan has been completed.
There will be an upcoming change in command when Chief Dan Peterson retires in March. The
internal hiring process for the Chief is open and there will be a meet and greet with the candidates.
The following day, interviews and presentations will begin. After the interviews a recommendation
will be made to the UFA Board. Recruit camp will begin in February; there have been 26 job offers
made and accepted and six pending. Station 102 in Magna was demolished, and construction of
the new building has begun.

Unified Police Department

                Detective Jeff Fenton reviewed the December call for service statistics. Domestic
violence and civil custody disputes were high. He will send a crime map when it comes out
tomorrow. He is working with the principal at Altaview Elementary School to get a flashing stop
sign. There will be a safety check and the principal is asking for the curbs to be painted red. The
crossing guard is having to cross kids in two different directions in-between parked cars that are
waiting for children.

               Mayor Flint stated the school needs to educate the parents on the rules for picking
up children. Parents should have to sign a document at the time of registration confirming that
they understand the pick-up guidelines. The PTA and school community groups could do training
for the parents.

                Council Member Little stated there is a lot of traffic for a short period of time, that
will not be eliminated. If you paint the curb red, the families that live there cannot have visitors
parking in front of their homes.

               Paul Ashton asked if there can be a timeframe in which they cannot park.

                Council Member Little asked if the design of the new school that is currently being
built needs to be looked at for the same type of problems.

               Chief Randy Thomas stated UPD has met with Canyons School District regarding
the construction of the new schools. The aesthetics of the new schools are terrible for different

Council Reports

Unified Fire Authority (UFA) & Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                 Council Member Perry stated because of staffing shortages, UFA has had to
move to mandatory staffing. Many of the stations have a four-man crew, if shortages continue,
crews will be reduced to a three-man crew. The Government Finance Officers Association
awarded UFA with the budget award for the third year. The software company that UFA uses for
payroll was hit with ransomware and it lost access to payroll. Sandy City Fire invited White City
to attend its strategic planning session to discuss any concerns White City has. He will attend the
session with Captain Ken Aldridge on February 23, 2022.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

               Mayor Flint stated the budget was passed with one amendment. PEHP provided
a $164,000 rebate to be distributed to civilian employees. Sheriff Rivera presented the board with
a budget adjustment to be able to distribute those funds. SLVLESA is working on future legislation
and putting together a committee to seriously look at policies and finances. The way UPD is being
funded needs to be restructured.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                 Council Member Price stated on December 13, 2021, the board passed the
budget without any amendments and with the same funding as prior years. The fraud committee
finalized its risk assessment, and it came back low. The board discussed whether to hold in-
person meetings, the board was split. It was decided that there will be four in-person meetings
with an option to join virtually. Staff is working on preparations for the upcoming season.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services Provider (MSD)

                Mayor Flint stated Marla Howard is the new General Manager for the MSD.
Interviews for the top three candidates for the Director of Finance position will begin. It is hoping
to have someone hired for that position by the end of the month. The bond will be finished on
February 2, 2022. The MSD is doing very well and it is very active and proactive in getting White
City’s needs met.
