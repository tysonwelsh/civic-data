WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 20, 2024

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on August 20, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.

the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3340, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
COUNTY _ ASSISTANCE FOR THE LA VERKIN RIM TO RIVER TRAIL IN
CONFLUENCE PARK

Commissioner Iverson stated this is a collaboration with the City of La Verkin near River Rock
Roasting in La Verkin. The City received a recreation grant to pay for the majority of the trail, and
this will pay for the additional items that were not covered by the grant in the amount of $43,500.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3340, A
Resolution Approving County Assistance for the La Verkin Rim to River Trail
in Confluence Park. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3343, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AN_INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITIES OF ST. GEORGE,
HURRICANE, WASHINGTON, SANTA CLARA, ENTERPRISE, AND WASHINGTON
COUNTY FOR BUILDING INSPECTION SERVICES

Mr. Snow explained that allows Washington County to cooperate with municipalities within the
County for building inspection services to meet state law deadlines.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3343, A
Resolution Approving an Interlocal Agreement Between the Cities of St.
George, Hurricane, Washington, Santa Clara, Enterprise, and Washington
County for Building Inspection Services. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson,
and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. __R-2024-3344, A || RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING FUNDS _ COLLECTED AS — WASHINGTON _ COUNTY’S
RECREATION, ARTS, AND PARKS SALES TAX FOR RECREATIONAL FACILITIES

Mr. Snow explained that two projects are being presented for Recreation, Arts, and Parks (RAP)
funding. Enterprise City is requesting $35,000 to resurface outdoor tennis, basketball, and
pickleball courts; and Washington City is requesting $30,000 to go towards a new park on George
Washington Boulevard.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3344, A
Resolution Appropriating Funds Collected as Washington County’s
Recreation, Arts, and Parks Sales Tax for Recreational Facilities. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 5:06 p.m.
