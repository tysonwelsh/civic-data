MINUTES OF THE DRAPER CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING HELD ON
THURSDAY, OCTOBER 27, 2022 IN THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS

PARTICIPATING:        Chairman Andrew Adams, Commissioners Craig Hawker, Lisa Fowler,
                      Gary Ogden, Alternate Commissioners Susan Nixon, and Kendra Shirey

ABSENT:               Commissioners Mary Squire, Tab Bingham, and DeLaina Tonks

STAFF PRESENT: Fred Aegerter, Jennifer Jastremsky, Angela Fixmer, Brien Maxfield,
Spencer DuShane, and Mike Barker.

6:30 PM Business Meeting
Chairman Andrew Adams welcomed everyone present to the meeting. Chairman Adams
informed the public that a change to the agenda needed to be made Agenda item number four
needed to be advertised longer. The item will take place at a different date.

1. Action Item: Approve Planning Commission Meeting Minutes for May 26, 2022
(Administrative Action)
Commissioner Susan Nixon began to make a motion to approve the minutes with some
changes. Commissioner Nixon informed the Commissioners and staff that she had emailed
them, and would like to go over them at the end of the meeting. It was asked if the changes
could be shown. The staff was unable to locate the email. Commissioner Nixon stated she
would send the email again.

Motion: Commissioner Gary Ogden motioned to postpone the approval of the meeting
minutes from May 26, 2022, until they can be reviewed and revised from the staff and
commissioners.

Second: Commissioner Susan Nixon.

Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 5-0 in favor of the motion. Nixon,
Ogden, Fowler, Hawker, and Shirey voted, “Aye”.

2. Public Hearing: Kathryn's Catering Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
(Administrative Action)
On the request of Kathryn Myers, for a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for a
catering business, located at 1119 E. Lone Peak Lane. The property is 0.74 acres in size and
located in the RA1 zone. Application USE-165-2022. Staff contact is Jonathan Concidine, 801-
576-6522, j.concidine@draperutah.gov. Staff report by Jennifer Jastremsky.

Ms. Jennifer Jastremsky, presented the project. Applicant Ms. Kathryn Myers is requesting a
Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for a catering business. Ms. Kathryn Myers does
have a commercial kitchen in her home, which is required. There will be no customers coming
to pick up orders, all orders will be delivered.

Chairman Andrew Adams invited the applicant to speak. Kathryn Myers asked if they had any

3. Public Hearing: Jenson Farms Phase 1A & Phase 6 Plat Amendment
(Administrative Action)
On the request of Tim Gough, representing Gough Homes, LLC, for a Plat
Amendment for a lot line adjustment, located at approximately 623 W. Rolling Hills
Way. The property is 1.72 acres in size and located in the R4 zone. Application
SUBD-161-2022. Staff contact is Jonathan Concidine, 801-576-6522,
j.concidine@draperutah.gov. Staff report by Jennifer Jastremsky.

Ms. Jennifer Jastremsky presented the project. The application includes two
subdivision plots. Phase 1A and phase 6. The plat amendment is to move the square
footage about ten feet. Even with the change, the lots will comply with the
development agreement requirement of 13,000 square feet and 20,000 square feet.

Commissioner Lisa Fowler asked why the corner lot would be an irregular shape if
this took place.

Applicant Tim Gough informed the commissioners for the reasoning of the change. A
home that is being built needs a little more square feet, the property line will be
moved to the right, not the left. This clarification answered the question stated before
by Commissioner Fowler.

Chairman Adams opened the Public Hearing
No Comments were made.
The Public Hearing was closed

Motion: Commissioner Craig Hawker motioned to approve Jenson Farms Phase 1A &
Phase 6 Plat Amendment as requested by Tim Gough representing Gough Homes, LLC.
