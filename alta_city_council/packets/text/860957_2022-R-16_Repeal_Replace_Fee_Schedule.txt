                                 TOWN OF ALTA

                           RESOLUTION No. 2022-R-16

     A RESOLUTION REPEALING AND REPLACING RESOLUTION 2022-R-6
           TO UPDATE THE TOWN OF ALTA FEE SCHEDULE


       WHEREAS, the Town of Alta adopted Resolution 2022-R-14 updating its
water rates on June 15, 2022;

       WHEREAS, the Town of Alta adopted Resolution 2022-R-15 updating its
sewer rates on June 15, 2022;


     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE TOWN COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. The Town of Alta Fee Schedule which was adopted by Resolution
2022-R-6 on March 9, 2022 is hereby repealed and replaced with Exhibit A attached.

       Section 2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

APPROVED by the Town Council on the 15th day of June, 2022.

                                           By:




                                           MAYOR ROGER BOURKE
ATTEST:




JEN CLANCY, TOWN CLERK
