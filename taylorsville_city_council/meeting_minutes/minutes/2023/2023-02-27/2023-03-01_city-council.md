1.      WELCOME, INTRODUCTION AND PRELIMINARY MATTERS

        1.2      Mayor’s Report
       Mayor Kristie Overson reported that she had been invited by Salt Lake County Mayor Jenny Wilson
to attend a meeting during the NBA All-Star Week. The NBA was involved in giving back to communities
throughout the valley and as such, they had shared in the opening of a new STEM (science, technology,
engineering and mathematics ) center and gymnasium at Lincoln Elementary School in South Salt Lake.
Additionally, they made donations for cancer research, the Big Brothers & Sisters of Utah program, the
Utah Food Bank, and the Bishop’s Central Storehouse. The week-long All-Star event filled hotel rooms
throughout Salt Lake County and spilled over to both Davis and Utah Counties as well. This brought in

Convention program.

There were two days left in the current legislative session and there had been a great deal of dialogue with
the legislators that represented Taylorsville residents and businesses. As always, the Mayor was very
grateful to the legislators and also for John Hiskey’s hard work.

Regarding the snow storm the previous week, a record-breaking amount of snow had fallen throughout the
valley, including in Taylorsville. Many city employees worked from home one day, but TVPD officers had
assisted with 200 vehicle slide-offs and a 4-hour closure of Bangerter Highway due to ice. The officers
were very grateful to be equipped with 4WD trucks that allowed them to assist in situations they might not
otherwise be able to. Mayor Overson expressed her appreciation to Scott Harrington and Scott Lloyd who
handled the fleet contract.

The facilities crew also worked tirelessly and did an amazing job. She wished to give a big thank you to
residents who not only shoveled their driveways and sidewalks but who helped neighbors whenever
possible. She reminded those gathered that it would help the fire department immensely if residents and
business owners would keep fire hydrants clear of snow. Doing so would save precious seconds in an
emergency.

A quarterly staff meeting had been held the previous week and Jay Ziolkowski had presented information
regarding the city’s emergency operations plan.

The new wall on 2700 West would soon be complete. Additionally, construction was taking place on
Redwood Road between Smiley Drive and Taylorsville High School. The beltway project just north of the
state campus was underway and she invited the councilmembers to take a look next time they drove by.
She reminded everyone that orange cones meant good things were coming to the city.

Mayor Overson had the opportunity to read to school children at both Arcadia and Freemont Elementary
Schools in honor of Read Across America Day. She mentioned what a joy it was to spend that time with
the children.

3.      REPORTS

       3.1       Green Committee Report – Mark Wendel

      Green Committee Chair Mark Wendel explained that it had been about six months since he had
presented a report to the City Council. The committee met each month, and the annual clean-up was
scheduled for June 10, 2023 at Taylorsville High School from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Most of the vendors
and volunteers had already been lined up.

Another Localscapes presentation was scheduled for April 13th in the council chambers at Taylorsville City
Hall. There was more grant money available for city residents who made water-wise changes to their yards
and park strips.

Several committee members were taking a field trip the following day to the Waste Management transfer
facility to give committee members more insight into how the waste stream worked. They hoped to have
more interaction with residents and provide more educational opportunities.

A new committee member had recently identified an opportunity to increase recycling at Vista Park. The
plan was in the very early stages, but Mr. Wendel and the rest of the committee were excited about the
project.

There was room for more members on the Green Committee and they could certainly use more help for
Taylorsville Dayzz.

Councilmember Barbieri asked when the Green Committee met. Mr. Wendell responded that they met at
Fire Station No. 118 on the first Tuesday of each month at 5:30 p.m.

Chair Knudsen expressed his appreciation to Mr. Wendell for the time and effort he and the committee put
forward on behalf of the entire community.
