The purpose of the joint work session was to review the agendas for the Redevelopment Agency (RDA) and City Council
meetings scheduled to begin at 4:30 p.m.; discuss a proposed rezone of 171 Franklin Street; discuss the proposed street vacation of
a portion of Franklin Street (approximately 171 Franklin Street); discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board
comments.

Agenda Review

Chair Nadolski and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agendas for the Special Redevelopment
Agency and City Council meetings scheduled to begin at 4:30 p.m.

Council/Board Business

Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported she invited Fire Chief Mathieu to provide the Council with information
about fireworks events/restrictions.

Chief Mathieu indicated that the State of Utah provided documents listing the number of fireworks complaints and actual
fireworks violations; just one citation was written by the Police Department. He then provided a report of fireworks related fires in
2022 and he reviewed with the Council the map illustrating the areas in which fireworks will continue to be restricted in
compliance with State Law.

Proposed Rezone — 171 Franklin Street; and
Proposed Street Vacation —A portion of Franklin Street (approximately 171
Franklin Street)
Deputy Planning Manager Simpson reported Mr. Curtis Groft, representing the Young Automotive Group, has filed a
petition to rezone three properties to the east of the Young Ford dealership on Wall Avenue. The properties, which include 171
Franklin Street and 162 and 170 35th Street, are currently owned by the Young Automotive Group and are zoned R-1-5 (Low
Density Residential). The petitioner is requesting these properties be rezoned to CP-3 (Regional Commercial) to allow for the
expansion of the dealership. The Planning Commission reviewed the proposal at its February 2, 2022 meeting and forwarded a
recommendation of approval to the Council. The recommendation was made with the following findings:
e [The proposal] will provide a definite edge to development and buffering between types of uses by:
© restricting vehicle access on Franklin Street (as a requirements of the street vacation permit)
o installing the most intense screening and landscaping along the east property line
o completing all development processes before expanding.
e Will reflect the prevailing character of the overall district by expanding the rezone to the vacated Franklin
Street right-of-way.
e Will not cut across lots or developments by completing the vacation of Franklin Street adjacent to the property
and combining all of the parcels associated with the car dealership.
e Is supported in the Jefferson Community Plan and the Wall Avenue Corridor Plan.

Minutes of Joint Session of Council of Ogden City, Utah, July 5, 2022

Several comments were received by Planning staff and the Planning Commission. Residents of the area expressed
concerns with the potential impacts of having the car dealership expanded to the east including safety and noise concerns.
Additional comments centered on the existing home at 171 Franklin Street and the potential of rehabilitating and preserving the
home.

Mr. Simpson also reported that Mr. Groft has filed an additional petition to vacate approximately 82 feet of Franklin
Street west of Lincoln Avenue adjacent to 171 Franklin Street. The petition to vacate the right-of-way is associated with the
rezone. The vacation will allow for the expansion of the Young Ford dealership. The portion of right-of-way proposed for
vacation is located directly east of the existing dealership property and would allow the expansion to line up with the property to
the north. Currently, Franklin Street west of Lincoln Avenue is not a through street and has no other traffic circulation other than
to provide access to the adjoining residential properties.

Brief discussion among Mr. Simpson and the Council centered on any impact the zone change could have on historic
properties in the area. Mr. Simpson stated that the home to be reserved is not historic in nature. The Council communicated they
are comfortable moving the petitions forward to a meeting agenda for action.

The meeting adjourned at 4:30 p.m.
