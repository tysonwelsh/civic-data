Minutes of the Regular Meeting of the Lehi City Council held Thursday, August 31, 2023, at
8:45 a.m. Lehi City Hall, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah. The meeting began at approximately
4:00 PM.

Present:     Mark Johnson, Mayor
             Paige Albrecht, Council Member
             Chris Condie, Council Member
             Paul Hancock, Council Member
             Katie Koivisto, Council Member
             Mike Southwick, Council Member

Others Present: Jason Walker, City Administrator; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Jeremy
Craft, Fire Chief; Andy Gowans, Management Analyst; and Teisha Wilson, City Recorder

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present.
   Councilors Hancock and Albrecht joined virtually.

2. Regular Agenda
   2.a. Approve Purchase Order for Used Fire Truck
   Chief Craft explained that this opportunity became available to purchase for a reasonable cost,
   but they needed to act quickly.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to approve the purchase order for used fire truck.
                Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time the meeting adjourned
   at approximately 9:00 a.m.
