D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: We have a couple of important holidays in February. I’m
   talking about Valentine’s Day today as we do not have a meeting next week. I thought it would be appropriate to
   remind the gentlemen in the room that it’s next week. Who created this holiday? It’s a feast celebrated annually
   and it started in the third century honoring the martyr Saint Valentine; later through folk traditions it became a
   cultural, religious, and now commercial celebration of romance and love. When I think of St. Valentine, I think
   of our Commissioner Harvey. St. Valentine was a lover of people; he went out and did things for people, some
   of which got him in trouble. But he liked to wave at people and associate with people. He was imprisoned for
   marrying two Christians who were not allowed to be married under Roman law at that time and was later
   executed. Later on in the 14th and 15th century, it became noted for courtly love and it flourished associated with
   love birds that came out in the spring. When you think of St. Valentine, think of our Chair who is kindly, waving
   at people; we appreciate him very much.

    3. PRELIMINARY SUBDIVISION APPROVAL OF ORCHARDS AT JDC RANCH PHASES 2 AND 3
       CONSISTING OF 115 UNITS, FILE NO. LVO112223.
         Tammy Aydelotte (Planning): This part of an area rezoned to R3. These do not usually come before you for
         preliminary approval but the recorded development agreement includes this as part of the process. This is
         part of a 13 acre project area located near 2850 West and 2600 North between Plain City and Farr West.
         There are 115 units proposed, 100 of which are townhomes; these are the only townhomes to be proposed as
         this is the maximum allowed on the JDC Ranch project area. There are also 15 clustered single family units.
         There is a 3 acre park already located in Phase 1 with common area maintained by the HOA. Previous
         approval have been received for 148 of the 725 total units leaving 462 residential units left to be approved.
         The applicant is proposing 61 parking spaces. The Planning Commission’s recommendation for preliminary
         approval mirrors that of Planning Staff. In the Planning Commission’s preliminary approval, they removed
         the need for an egress to 2700 North as they felt the platting of Phase 1 would address that need. The
         Planning Commission recommends preliminary approval of JDC Ranch Phases 2 & 3 subject to all agency
         review requirements and based on the following conditions:
                    Final letters of approval should be submitted from The Culinary and secondary water providers
                      prior to recording the final plat.
                    Open spaces will need to be properly labeled as common area on each final plat, and
                    Street cross sections will be verified for compliance with the development agreement once final
                      Improvement drawings are submitted for each phase

         Commissioner Froerer moved for preliminary subdivision approval of Orchards at JDC Ranch Phases 2 and 3
         consisting of 115 units, File No. LVO112223; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye

                improvements, installation and maintenance of street trees, and the adoption of a species list of
                approved street trees/vegetation, and Section 108-7-7.040, Public Tree Care, providing for the long-
                term maintenance and care of street trees.

                Bill Cobabe (Planning): There is a concerned citizen up in the Valley concerned about street trees.
                The street trees that may be of concern are not going to go in every location. They will go on streets
                required to be normal subdivision streets and not country area. In the effort to provide street trees in
                those areas, we are proposing this update. This was approved by both Planning Commissions with the
                proviso that the permit required is a ‘no fee’ permit. We are not going to require this for every
                subdivision.
