Resolution

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving a letter to the United
States Army Special Operations Command inviting them to perform training exercises in Magna
from May 11, 2020 to May 15, 2020:

RESOLUTION NO. 20-02-01                                                 Date: February 25, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       A LETTER TO THE UNITED STATES ARMY SPECIAL OPERATIONS
       COMMAND INVITING THEM TO PERFORM TRAINING EXCERCISES IN
       MAGNA FROM MAY 11, 2020 TO MAY 15, 2020

                                           RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code Annotated
      (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501

   C. The United State Army Special Operation Command (“USASOC”) is an operational
      command group of the United States Army tasked to defend the United States pursuant
      to the US Constitution.

   D. USASOC desires to conduct a training exercise in Magna from May 11th to May 15th, 2020.

   E. USASOC policy requires a letter from the jurisdiction in which they desire to train inviting
      them to conduct training.

   F. The proposed training exercise allows USASOC personnel to gain tactical and technical
      efficiencies that improve operational readiness and safety.

   G. Magna desires to support USASOC’s training effort and views such training exercises as
      necessary to supporting the defense of our nation.

                                         RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Council approves this resolution and authorizes the
Mayor to sign the attached letter, hereinafter known as Attachment “A,” inviting the United States
Army Special Operation Command to conduct their training exercises from May 11th to May 15th,
2020 in Magna.

       APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
25th day of February 2020.
