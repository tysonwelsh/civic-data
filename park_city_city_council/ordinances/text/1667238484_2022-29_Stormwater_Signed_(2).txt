              Ordinance No. 2022-29

               AN ORDINANCE AMENDING STORMWATER ILLICIT DISCHARGE DETECTION
               AND ELIMINATION AUTHORITY FOR PARK CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION.

                    WHEREAS, the Municipal Separate Storm Sewer Permit requires that the city
              has authority to enter properties to investigate and eliminate illicit discharges; and

                   WHEREAS, a public hearing was held on September 1, 2022, at the City
              Council's regularly scheduled meetings.

                      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Park City, Utah
              that:

                   Amendment of SECTION 13-3-1 of the Municipal code, is hereby amended as
              shown below:

            13-3-1 Illicit Discharges
            …

                 E. Inspections.

                         1. Visual inspections: visual inspections by the City of discharges to natural
                            water bodies, spills and stormwater related controls on private property
                            within the City limits are permitted by the City at any time. Reasonable
                            attempts to contact the property owner for the inspection shall be made prior
                            to accessing private property.
                         2. Other Inspections: When a visual inspection is not adequate to determine
                            the extent of discharges to natural water bodies, spills or to determine the
                            status of stormwater related controls on private property, real or suspect,
                            the City will give twenty four (24) hours' notice of the inspection to take place
                            and the extent of the inspection. Equipment and manpower necessary to
                            perform the inspection will be allowed to access and work as necessary to
                            determine the state of the situation.
                         3. Emergency Inspections: During times of emergency including discharges
                            to natural water bodies, spills or potential damage to life or property, real or
                            suspect, the City may access the location of concern on private property as
                            necessary and with the equipment required to determine the status of the
                            situation. Reasonable attempts to contact the property owner prior to the
                            inspection shall be made prior to accessing private property.

            …
                 SECTION 2. EFFECTIVE DATE. The Stormwater Illicit Discharge Code
            Amendment shall take effect upon publication.
