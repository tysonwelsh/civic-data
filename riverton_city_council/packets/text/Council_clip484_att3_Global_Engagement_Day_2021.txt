                                Global Engagement Day 2021

WHEREAS, Riverton is a globally connected city maintaining a wide variety of global
relationships; and

WHEREAS, Exports provided by local businesses in Riverton, contribute to America’s efforts to
fight hunger and poverty around the world; and

WHEREAS, Riverton’s economic success has directly supported over 410,000 jobs throughout
the state and Utah’s $17.7 billion in goods exported to foreign markets; and

WHEREAS, Riverton City strongly supports Utah’s higher educational system which enrolls
over 8,400 international students per year - contributing nearly $240 million to the Utah
economy; and

WHEREAS, Since 1961, over 1,200 individuals representing Riverton and the State of Utah
have served overseas in the Peace Corps; and

WHEREAS, Global Partnerships and trust are key to ending the spread and impact of COVID-19
here at home and throughout the world; and

NOW, THEREFORE, I Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton, Utah, do hereby proclaim December 14,
2021, as Global Engagement Day in Riverton and ask the residents of our community to
support the U.S. Global Leadership Coalition’s efforts to strengthen diplomacy and America’s
international affairs programs.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 9th day of December 2021.
