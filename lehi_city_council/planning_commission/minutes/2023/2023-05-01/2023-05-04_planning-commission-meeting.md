Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
Commission Chair Newall welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2.1) Discussion of the Vistas Station Area Planning Process.
Christine Richman with GBS gave a presentation on the item.

Commissioner Everett really encouraged planning to think regionally about this. How do we make
it work for people more than those in the region? Look at the sideways connections and make those
work, not just north and south.

2.2) Discussion of how regional draw of TODs works for everyone, not just people who live
at the transit station (ie driving to the frontrunner station from Saratoga or Eagle
Mountain to get to work in Salt Lake).
Mike West talked about this being a phased approach. He stated we need to look at east-west
connections. There will be a big push for parking right now, with redevelopment potential in the
future. He touched on bike infrastructure and that in our lifetimes probably won’t become a
major mode of transportation.

2.3) Discussion of the concept plan approval process.
Kim Struthers presented on the approval process. He stated some feedback that is given is
different from the concept. Some ideas to improve the approval process could be to improve
DRC process to send concepts forward with fewer comments, and possibly remove parking from
TOD until transit is in place. The Commissioners don’t like General Plan Amendments, with a
concept plan they can approve it with a Development Agreement. They would like to see what
other cities do with general plan amendments, especially with growth like ours.

3) Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Eyre moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:45 p.m.
