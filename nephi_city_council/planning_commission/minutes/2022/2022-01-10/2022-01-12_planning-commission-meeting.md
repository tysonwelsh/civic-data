NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION/PUBLIC HEARING
                                      MEETING MINUTES
                                         January 12, 2022
                                           21 E. 100 N.
                                         Nephi, UT 84648
                                            7:00 p.m.
Planning Commission Chairman Ann Peterson called the meeting to order and welcomed all in
attendance.

PRAYER: City Planning Shauna Talbot

PUBLIC HEARING - HOME OCCUPATION LICENSE - BRITE LITE TEETH WHITENING:

   Ms. Carolyn Ford came to the meeting to discuss her home-based business. It will be teeth
whitening. They will have minimal traffic impact and only one person a time with ample time between
people. Commission Chairman Ann Peterson opened the meeting up for public comment.

   Commissioner Fran Petersen asked about OSHA requirements and any other state requirements that
will need to be adhered to. Ms. Ford gave further information about procedures.

Hearing closed at 7:05 p.m.

PUBLIC HEARING – HOME OCCUPATION LICENSE- STORMY’S CREATIVITEES:

  License applicant for Stormy’s Creativitees was absent. Public hearing was postponed until the
applicant can be present.

PUBLIC HEARING – ZONE CHANGE REQUEST - PRIORITY HOMES:

   Ms. Talbot informed the commission that the request would need to be postponed until next month
because of a change in the request. They will be requesting R2-Z with a PUD overlay similar to Gardner
Village. Because of the changes, a new public hearing will be noticed and scheduled for next month.

PUBLIC HEARING – ZONE CHANGE REQUEST - ALEX JOHNSON:

   Mr. Atkinson gave some background information about the request to change the zone from
Industrial to Highway Commercial. Mr. Alex Johnson explained that there will be nice lots there for any
business interested that fits the zone. City Council Member Skip Worwood stated that would be a good
continuation to the development happening to the east of that property. Commission Chairman Ann
Peterson opened the meeting up for public comment and there was no public comment.

Hearing closed at 7:14 p.m.

MINUTES APPROVED:

  Commissioner Cory Thomson moved to approve the minutes from the planning commission meeting
held on December 8, 2021. Commissioner Heather Robertson seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.

CEDAR RIDGE PHASE 2 AND 3 FINAL PLAT APPROVED

  Mr. Riley Park was present to discuss his development and the amenities phases 2 and 3 will have.
Mr. Atkinson explained that there was a gap between the preliminary approval and final approval. All
improvements were installed in phase 1 and a development agreement was in place to complete the
improvements for Phase 2 and 3. Commissioner Alan Hancock made the motion for final approval of
phases 2 and 3 of the Cedar Ridge Subdivision. Commissioner Karl Brough seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BOUNDARIES FOR THE ANIMAL RIGHTS OVERLAY ZONE DISCUSSION

   Mr. Atkinson gave background information on a request for an individual animal rights zone change
request. This led to a broader discussion about the boundaries of animal rights. Commission Chairman
Ann Peterson said that there are more pressing issues for them to focus on right now when preparing
for the changes the city will be facing. There was a consensus among the planning commission on this
matter. Commissioner Alan Hancock pointed out that it is a slippery slope to allow on certain blocks and
not others if the commission starts making new changes.

  Commissioner Fran Petersen said that residents have also purchased their lot knowing that they did
have animal rights or didn’t have animal rights. It wouldn’t be fair to change those with a planning
commission decision without public input. There will need to be a larger discussion in a public hearing to
consider any changes like that.

PRD/PUD ZONE DISCUSSION

   Mr. Atkinson explained the difference between PRD and PUD. Following the discussion about PRD,
Commission Chairman Ann Peterson made a motion to make a recommendation to the city council to
repeal PRD and remove it from city code. Commissioner Fran Petersen seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Commissioner Heather Robertson moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Cory Thomson
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

Meeting adjourned at 8:39 p.m.
