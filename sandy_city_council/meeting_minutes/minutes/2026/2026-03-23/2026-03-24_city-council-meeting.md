4.         26-189                 First Reading: Council Member Christensen proposing Recreational
                                  Vehicle parking amendments to Title 14
                     Sponsors: Christensen

                   Attachments:   Council Member Christensen Proposal

                                  First Reading: Council Member Brooke Christensen proposed an amendment to Title 14
                                  of the Sandy City Municipal Code to include recreational vehicles (RV) to Section 14-7-5.
                                  She further explained that residents brought this to her attention. By amending Title 14
                                  adding "recreation vehicles" to the referenced section, we allow for an additional
                                  enforcement mechanism for our police officers. By amending our traffic code, our officers
                                  can cite the vehicle owner of the recreation vehicle if the vehicle is illegally parked in the
                                  street. Currently, the City's recreational vehicle parking restrictions are codified in Title 21
                                  (Land Development Code) and is enforced through the City's code enforcement process,
                                  which is directed at property owners. This amendment incorporates the same RV parking
                                  restriction into Title 14 which allows for enforcement through both code enforcement and
                                  law enforcement and allows for a mechanism to cite the RV owner, rather than solely the
                                  property owner. Council questions and discussion followed. Council feedback included
                                  ensuring that an owner could not be cited twice for the same infraction. Council was
                                  supportive of bringing this item back for a second reading.
