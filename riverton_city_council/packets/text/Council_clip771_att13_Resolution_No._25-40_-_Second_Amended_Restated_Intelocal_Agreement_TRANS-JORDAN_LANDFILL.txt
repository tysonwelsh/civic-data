                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 25-40

    A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A SECOND
       AMENDED AND RESTATED TRANS-JORDAN CITIES INTERLOCAL
                     COOPERATION AGREEMENT

        WHEREAS, the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Ann. § 11-13-101 et seq., permits
two or more public agencies to enter into an agreement with one another for joint or cooperative
action, to provide services they are each authorized by Statute to provide, to make the most efficient
use of their powers by enabling them to cooperate with other public agencies on a basis of mutual
advantage, and to do anything they are each authorized by Statute to do; and

      WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §19-6-503(1)(d)(i) provides that the Riverton City Council
may “enter into a short- or long-term interlocal agreement to provide for or operate a solid waste
management facility [such as a landfill] with another public entity”;

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §19-6-505(1)(a) permits two or more public entities, including
any political subdivision of the state, to enter into long-term agreement with one another pursuant to
Title 11, Chapter 13, Interlocal Cooperation Act, “related to the acquisition, construction, ownership,
operation, maintenance, and improvement of solid waste management facilities . . . pursuant to the
terms, conditions, and consideration provided in the [Interlocal] agreement[s];

         WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §19-6-505(1)(c) provides that cities may, in the Interlocal
agreement, “agree to make available to other public entities a specified portion of the capacity of the
solid waste management facilities, without regard to its future needs of the specified capacity for its
own use and may in the agreement agree to increase the capacity of its solid waste management
facilities from time to time, as necessary, in order to take care of its own needs and to perform its
obligations to the other parties to the agreement;

       WHEREAS, the current members of the Trans-Jordan Landfill include Draper City, Midvale
City, Murray City, Riverton City, Sandy City, South Jordan City and West Jordan City (collectively
the “current members”); and

        WHEREAS, the current members desire to enter into an amended Interlocal Agreement with
the intent to add Herriman City as a member of the Trans-Jordan Landfill with all the same rights,
duties and obligations, as proscribed by the Interlocal Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that entering into the Second
Amended and Restated Trans-Jordan Interlocal Cooperation Agreement with the intent to add and
include Herriman City as a member of the landfill is in the best interest of the City’s health, safety
and general welfare; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has appointed the Riverton City Attorney to review and approve
as to form the amended and restated agreement.
       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

      1. The attached Second Amended and Restated Trans-Jordan Cities Interlocal Cooperation
         Agreement is hereby approved.

      2. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, the proposed Second
         Amended and Restated Trans-Jordan Cities Interlocal Cooperation Agreement. upon
         verification that the Agreement has been approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
June, 2025 by the following vote:
