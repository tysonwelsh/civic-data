Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Travis DeJong
CC:  Date: August 15, 2023
Re: Providing Local Consent for an On Premises Banquet alcohol license for Boondocks Food & Fun Comments: Boondocks Food & Fun - Draper LLC located at 75 E Southfork Dr is seeking Local Consent for an On Premises Banquet License. They have provided the following:
- A floor plan showing where the alcohol is to be stored, served, and consumed.
- A bond payable to the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services.
- The results of a background check performed on the food and beverage manager.
- Copy of sales tax license.
- Liquor liability insurance.
Boondocks Food & Fun - Draper LLC possesses a valid, issued business license. The findings are as follows: 6-4-040 (B) (2) (a) of the Draper City Municipal Code states the following:
An on-premises banquet license , reception center, on- premises beer retailer license, or bar establishment license shall not be granted to a business located within six hundred feet (600') of any community location as measured from the nearest patron entrance of the proposed outlet by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the property boundary of the community location or within two hundred feet (200') of the proposed outlet measured in a straight line from the nearest patron entrance of the proposed outlet to the nearest property boundary of the community location.
Boondocks Food & Fun - Draper LLC has met both the 600' and 200' distance requirements.
Per Draper City Municipal Code 6-4-050 (B) (4) (d), there is no limitation on the amount of on-premises banquet licenses licenses granted by the city.
