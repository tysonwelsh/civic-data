Proposal
The applicant proposes to construct an Accessory Building that will contain an indoor swimming 
pool and exercise room on their property on Lot 4 of the Lazy Acres Ranch Estates North Minor 
Subdivision, located at 268 W Lazy Acres Ln. The proposed pool and workout room will not be 
open to the public and will be for the private use of the property owners. The proposed 
building will be 26.5’ tall and 3,613 square feet.

Background 
The Lazy Acres Ranch Estates Minor Subdivision was recorded on August 8, 2006 (Entry No. 
786738) and contains 5 residential lots. Each lot contains a single-family dwelling, and most 
have associated accessory buildings. There are also two agricultural parcels within the 
subdivision. 
 
A single-family dwelling with an attached garage was constructed on Lot 4 of the subdivision 
(the property) in 2016. There is also an existing barn on the property. The Code does not 
include restrictions on the number of Accessory Buildings on a property.  
 
The Eastern Summit County Development Code (Code) defines Accessory Buildings as: “A 
building upon the same lot (or in a contiguous lot under the same ownership) as the principal 
building and is: a) clearly incidental to, and customarily found in connection with, such principal 
building or use; and b) is operated and maintained for the benefit or convenience of the 
owners, occupants, employees, customers or visitors of the lot with the principal use.” 
 
The proposed building will be 26.5’ tall and 3,613 square feet. The footprint will be 4,193 
square feet. A site plan and building plans can be seen in Exhibit A. In the AG-10 Zone, 
Accessory Buildings that exceed 2,000 square feet are conditional uses. According to Section 
11-4-6 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code, all conditional uses require a Final 
Site Plan for recordation. The Final Site Plan has been provided in Exhibit B.  
 
Staff and service providers have reviewed the Conditional Use Permit and Final Site Plan and 
have found that as conditioned, the project complies with all applicable standards.

Recommendation 
Staff recommends that the ESCPC review the proposed Conditional Use Permit and Final Site 
Plan, conduct a public hearing, and approve the application, pursuant to the following Findings 
of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Conditions of Approval. 
 
Findings of Fact: 
1. On April 3, 2025, the Summit County Community Development Department received a 
complete application for a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) and Final Site Plan at 268 W 
Lazy Acres Ln (the property).  
2. The property is owned by Loren Chandler Trustee. Loren Chandler signed the CUP 
application form.  
3. The property is identified as Parcel LAREN-4 (1.28 acres).  
4. The property is within the Lazy Acres Ranch Estates North Minor Subdivision, recorded 
on August 8, 2006 (Entry No. 786738). 
5. The property is a Conforming Parcel. The property is within the AG-10 Zone District. 
6. The purpose of this CUP and Final Site Plan is to allow an Accessory Building to be 
located on the property. The Accessory Building will contain an indoor pool for the 
private use of the property owners. 
7. The proposed building will be 3,613 square feet and the footprint will be 4,193 square 
feet. The building will be 26.5’ in height as measured from Natural Grade.  
8. The proposed Accessory Building will be incidental to the existing residence on the 
property, which was built in 2016. There is also an existing barn. This accessory use is 
appropriately positioned on the lot in relation to the primary use.  
9. The massing of the structure is consistent with detached garages, barns, and other 
Accessory Buildings found in residential subdivisions within the AG-10 zone.  
10. The proposed structure is on a flat portion of the land and is not encroaching into any 
sensitive lands or natural resources as defined in Section 11-2-4: Natural Resources of 
the Eastern Summit County Development Code. 
11. The Marion Waterworks Company is the water service provider for this property. The 
Secretary of the Mairon Waterworks Company issued a will-serve letter for this proposal 
on April 4, 2025.  
12. The proposal will require a septic system. 
13. The project location is not a geographic hazard area or anticipated to accelerate the 
erosion. There are no slopes in excess of 30%.  
9
8 
 
14. The proposal is not on a hillside, ridgeline, 100-year floodplain, or near identified 
wetlands.  
15. The proposed development will not affect wildlife birthing areas, critical winter range or 
migration corridors.  
16. The proposal will not intensify run-off characteristics or significantly degrade air quality.  
17. The proposal will not increase traffic to the neighborhood as it is a private amenity for 
the property owners. The proposal does not include any new roads. 
18. The proposal does not present an unusual fire hazard.  
19. The project location has access to emergency services. The South Summit Fire District 
services the area.  
20. Proposed outdoor lighting includes full cut-off fixtures that direct light downward onto 
the property.  
21. The existing single-family dwelling on the property has light fixtures that do not meet 
the requirements of 11-6-20. As stated in Condition of Approval 6, the noncompliant 
lighting shall be immediately abated.  
22. There are reasonable conditions that can be imposed to mitigate the anticipated 
negative impacts of the proposed use.  
23. Staff and service providers have reviewed the project for compliance with all applicable 
standards.  
 
Conclusions of Law:  
1. The proposed use, as conditioned, is appropriate in the particular locations, taking into 
 account the nature of the use, its relationship to surrounding land uses, and its impact 
 on the natural environment. 
2. The proposed use, as conditioned, is in compliance with the development evaluations 
 standards in chapter 2 of the Code. 
3. The applicant is the property owner (or, the applicant has submitted evidence to show 
 approval of the landowner for the particular use. 
4. There are reasonable conditions that have been imposed to mitigate with reasonably 
 anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. 
 
Conditions of Approval: 
1. The proposed Accessory Building shall be constructed in accordance with Exhibits A of 
this Staff Report.  
2. The Final Site Plan included in Exhibit B shall be amended in accordance with service 
provider comments included in Exhibit C. The amended Final Site Plan shall be recorded 
in the Office of the Summit County Recorder prior to approval of a Building Permit 
application.  
3. The proposed Accessory Building shall be for the private use of the property owners. 
Any proposed commercial uses of the Accessory Building shall be subject to subsequent 
review in accordance with the Eastern Summit County Development Code.   
4. The Accessory Building shall not be sold separately from the single-family dwelling on 
the property.

Background 
The applicant proposes to vacate Lot 11 of the Pine Meadow Ranch Plat F Subdivision and 
combine the vacated lot with Lots 1 and 2 of the Pine Meadow Ranch Plat D Subdivision. The 
proposal would result in one conforming parcel that is 7.51-acres within the Pine Meadow Plat

recorded simultaneously. Both plats are reviewed as part of this application. 
 
Currently, Lot 11 of Plat F and Lot 1 of Plat D are both undeveloped. Lot 2 of Plat F contains a 
single family dwelling. Access to property is from W Alexander Canyon Rd. An existing driveway 
begins on a neighboring property and provides access to the dwelling. The driveway easement 
is recorded as Entry #01233124. The proposed vacation and lot combination will not affect 
access to the property or any public or private roads.  
 
Section 11-4-5-E-3 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code states that the Final Land 
Use Authority for any subdivision plat amendment that involves vacating a portion of a 
subdivision plat and that does not involve a public or private road is the Eastern Summit County 
Planning Commission (ESCPC). Although the project also involves a lot combination, for which 
the Community Development Director is the Final Land Use Authority as described in 11-4-5-E-
2, the more restrictive process applies. Therefore, the ESCPC is the Final Land Use Authority for 
this application.

the subdivision have a single-family dwelling unit and an accessory structure. 
  
The Eastern Summit County Development Code  defines Accessory Buildings as: “A building 
upon the same lot (or in a contiguous lot under the same ownership) as the principal building 
and is: a) clearly incidental to, and customarily found in connection with, such principal building 
or use; and b) is operated and maintained for the benefit or convenience of the owners, 
occupants, employees, customers or visitors of the lot with the principal use.”  
 
Section 11 -3-16 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code considers Accessory 
buildings and uses exceeding 2,000 square feet to the principal residential dwelling unit or 
subdivision to be conditional uses in the AG-10 zone district. The proposed accessory structure 
is approximately 3,698 square feet total; 2,700 square feet of building footprint and 998 square 
foot accessory dwelling unit on the upper mezzanine area, and a 510 square foot covered deck. 
 
A Conditional Use Permit application for a new accessory building exceeding 2,000 square feet 
was submitted on February 27, 2025. 
 
Staff and service providers have reviewed the Conditional Use Permit and found that, as 
conditioned, the project complies with all applicable standards. According to Section 11 -4-6 of 
the Eastern Summit County Development Code, all conditional uses require a Final Site Plan for 
recordation. A Final Site Plan, consistent with the Conditional Use Permit, will need to be 
recorded prior to the issuance of a building permit.
