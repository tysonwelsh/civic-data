                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                                 July,2021


July was highlighted by our participation in the Riverton Town Days celebrations in the city. We
provided security for the rodeo, the city sponsored road races, and the myriad other events which
occurred throughout the week at the city park. Additionally, we managed traffic control for the
parade and utilized our crossing guards to augment our sworn officer resources to control traffic and
provide a safe environment for the entries and the thousands of residents who attended the parade.
We also threw lots of candy as our command staff represented our department as an entry.

We also hired three new officers to fill our vacancies and bring us to full staffing during the month.
We will spend the next several weeks acclimating our new additions to the intricacies of Riverton
and ensuring they are properly trained and ready to work on their own. All three of them have
previous law enforcement experience and we are excited to have them on board.

There were no significant statistical anomalies related to our calls for service for the month, which
is good news. Vehicle burglaries and drug arrests trended down from the previous month as we
focused our patrol efforts in our neighborhoods, especially during the overnight hours, which is an
encouraging sign.
