WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 1, 2024

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 6:00 p.m. on October 1, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, and County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PRESENTATION: FOR AND AGAINST RAP TAX ARGUMENTS

Commissioner Almquist stated the purpose of this meeting was for the presentation of the “for”
and “against” arguments as it relates to the renewal of the RAP Tax.

Michelle Adams read the submitted argument for the RAP Tax. Those present were given an
opportunity to present an argument against the RAP Tax, but none came forward.

PUBLIC HEARING: COMMENTS ON RAP TAX

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Open the Public Hearing. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

Glenn Webb advocated for the renewal of the RAP Tax.

Alice Stevens Gardner endorsed the renewal of the RAP Tax.

Commissioner Iverson explained the background regarding the initial issuance of the RAP tax and
the County’s role in assisting the administration of the RAP tax.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Close the Public Hearing. Motion seconded

by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 6:15 p.m.
