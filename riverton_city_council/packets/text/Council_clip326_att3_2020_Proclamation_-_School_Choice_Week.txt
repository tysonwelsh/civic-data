                         Riverton City Mayoral Proclamation
                                 School Choice Week
       WHEREAS, all children in Riverton should have access to the highest-quality education
possible; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton recognizes the important role that an effective education plays in
preparing all students to be successful adults; and

       WHEREAS, quality education is critically important to the economic vitality of
Riverton; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton is home to a multitude of excellent education options from which
parents can choose for their children; and

      WHEREAS, educational variety not only helps to diversify our economy, but also
enhances the vibrancy of our community; and

       WHEREAS, our area has many high-quality teaching professionals who are committed
to educating our children; and

        WHEREAS, School Choice Week is celebrated across the country by millions of
students, parents, educators, schools and organizations to raise awareness of the need for
effective educational options.

        NOW, THEREFORE, I Mayor Trent Staggs do hereby proclaim January 26 – February
1, 2020 as RIVERTON SCHOOL CHOICE WEEK, and I call this observance to the attention of
all of our citizens.


Proclaimed on this the 21st day of January 2020 in Riverton City, Utah.
