alcoholism and drug treatment center, or a work-release facility for convicts.   
 
The site plan was shown and the tennis courts were pointed out.  The applicant is 
proposing to put the home entirely within the footprint of the tennis courts, as well as 
the majority of the parking.  The floor plan of the home was shown.   
 
Planner Milliner s aid the General Plan allows for single occupancy group homes.  Staff

Chair Harte invited the applicant, Todd Hood, to describe the project and how it will

up early enough to get to work on time.  They will have staff there 24 hours a day; 
however, they won’t be living there.  Typically, the Staff to client ratio is approximately 
1 in 8.  Right now, his group plans on housing four autistic adults with 1 staff member.
