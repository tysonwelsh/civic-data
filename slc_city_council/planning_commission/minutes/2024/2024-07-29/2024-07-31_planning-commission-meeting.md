BRIEFINGS

1. 300 West Corridor & Central Pointe Station Area Plan (PUBLIC HEARING) - The Planning
Commission will receive a second briefing from Design Workshop, the project consultant, and City
Planning Staff, on the Salt Lake City Planning Division's 300 West Corridor & Central Pointe Station

Area Plan draft. Draft plan content was previously presented to the Commission in March. The
consultant has prepared a complete draft plan based on Planning Commission and public input. The
Commission will hold a public hearing, but no recommendation will be made at this meeting. An
additional public hearing and recommendation will follow at a later date. The plan will guide the future
development and use of property in the area extending from 1700 South to 2100 South and from I-
15 to West Temple in the Central Community planning area of Salt Lake City. The project area is
located in Council District 5, represented by Darin Mano. (Staff contact: Daniel Echeverria at
daniel.echeverria@slcgov.com)) Case Number: PLNPCM2023-00397

Senior Planner Daniel Echeverria reviewed complete draft plans. Marianne Stock from Design
Workshop discussed the draft plans. Commission and staff discussed different parts of the draft
plan.

The Chair opened the public hearing.

PUBLIC HEARING

Ballpark Community Council Chair, Amy J Hawkins, discussed the trail proposed along the TRAX
line.

Seeing that no one else wished to speak the chair ended the public hearing but left it open to
reconsider this at a future meeting.

Commissioner Barry requested the plan identify some alternate green space to augment the area.

Commissioner Scheer asked if there is any publicly owned land within this space.

Marianne Stock addressed the issue of greenspace to augment the area.

Commissioner Kraczek clarified properties giving space for right away.

Commissioner Gayle stated language needs to be clear when addressing properties giving right of
way and not promising what we don’t have.

Commissioner Christensen agreed allowing UTA to work with property owners to create a trail or a
linear park.

Commissioner Kraczek stated the plan needs to be clear and clarify the language with a linear park
or green space and property right of way.

2. Zoning Consolidation Project - The Planning Commission will be briefed on the proposal to
consolidate the existing mixed use and commercial zones into new "MU" zones. This briefing will
include reviewing how the current parking regulations will be addressed in the proposed
consolidation. (Staff Contact: Nick Norris at 801-535-6173 or nick.norris@slcgov.com) Case
Number: PLNPCM2024-00707

Planning Manager Amy Thompson reviewed proposed changes to the parking chapter in relation to
the MU zoning consolidation. Staff discussed several options that were explored to address off street
parking, the pros and cons of each option, and the recommended option moving forward.

The commission indicated that some additional information on how the parking chapter will be
implemented is needed before they can provide constructive feedback on the proposed changes.
Planning staff agreed to follow up with a subsequent briefing to provide a parking chapter overview
and information sheet at a future PC briefing.

The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:55 PM.
