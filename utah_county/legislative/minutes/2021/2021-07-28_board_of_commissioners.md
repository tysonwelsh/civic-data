ADAM BECK, COUNTY ATTORNEY - THE REQUEST FOR REINSTATEMENT OF
EXEMPTION STATUS FOR 2021 MAY NOT BE GRANTED HOWEVER, IF THE BOARD
OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF UTAH COUNTY FINDS THAT THE BEST HUMAN
INTEREST AND THE INTEREST OF THE STATE AND COUNTY ARE SERVED, IT MAY
ABATE THE 2021 TAX PENALTIES AND ALL APPLICABLE INTEREST.

COMMISSIONER SAKIEVICH - WERE THERE ANY OTHER CONGREGATIONS
AFFECTED BY THIS TIME LAPSE OR CHURCHES THAT MISSED THE DEADLINE?

ADAM BECK - I DON’T BELIEVE SO.

BRAD WALTON, FIRST ELDER AT THE PROVO SEVENTH-DAY ADVENTIST CHURCH
- AS LIKE MANY OTHER ORGANIZATIONS, WE WERE NOT MEETING DUE TO THE
PANDEMIC. WE HAD JUST STARTED TO MEET IN PERSON AGAIN IN JANUARY AND
OUR CHURCH SECRETARIES WERE GOING THROUGH ALL OF THE MAIL THAT HAD
ACCUMULATED AND THE NOTIFICATION FOR NEEDING TO FILL OUT THE FORM
FOR PROPERTY TAX EXEMPTION WAS MISSED. I RECEIVED IT AROUND THE 2?
WEEK OF FEBRUARY AND IT WAS DUE THE END OF JANUARY. I IMMEDIATELY
FILLED IT OUT AND SENT IT IN TO THE BOE AND WAS INFORMED THAT IT WAS
LATE. WE ARE ALL VERY CONCERNED THAT THIS HAPPENED AND CERTAINLY
DON’T WANT TO HAVE IT HAPPEN AGAIN.

DISCUSSED WITH COMMISSION AND CLERK/AUDITOR ABOUT DEADLINES,
STATUTES AND REQUIREMENTS.
