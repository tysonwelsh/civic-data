        West from R-1-10 to R-1-7. The R-1-10 zoning district is characterized as a residential district allowing one
        dwelling unit on a minimum 10,000 square foot lot. The R-1-7 zoning district allows one dwelling unit on a
        minimum 7,000 square foot lot. The applicant has expressed interest in pursuing a two-lot subdivision for
        this property if the map amendment is approved. Review and consideration of a potential subdivision is
        not being addressed at this time; however, a conceptual subdivision plan was included in the Staff Report
        for reference only.

        3.1.1    On February 26, 2020, the property owner applied for a zoning map amendment (File #6Z20) to
                 rezone the property from R-1-10 to R-1-5. On June 9, 2020, the Planning Commission forwarded
                 a negative recommendation to the City Council (7-0 vote). On June 17, 2020, the application was
                 presented to the City Council as Ordinance 20-14 and not approved. Rather than pursuing the R-
                 1-5 zoning district, the applicant is now requesting consideration to rezone the property to R-1-7.

        3.1.2    The subject property is approximately 17,860 square feet (.041 acres) and currently includes a
                 single-family dwelling originally constructed in 1970 with a building footprint of approximately
                 1,250 square feet.

        3.1.3    Surrounding zoning designations: North (R-1-10), South (R-1-10), East (R-1-5) and West (R-1-20).

        3.1.4    The Proposed Land Use Map in the General Plan sets the foundation for all land uses within the
                 City of Taylorsville. The land use plan identifies general land use classifications and in some cases
                 the intensity within the classification. Each land use classification on the proposed Land Use Map
                 corresponds with compatible classifications on the official Taylorsville Zoning Map.

        3.1.5    The applicant’s Zoning Map Amendment Request is consistent with the City’s General Plan and
                 would not allow for the creation of residential densities in excess of six dwelling units per acre. A
                 7,000 square foot minimum lot size equates to a density of approximately six dwelling units per
                 acre The property is approximately 0.41 acres and with the approval of a flag lot subdivision could
                 accommodate two dwelling units, resulting in a density of 55 units per acre.

        3.1.6    The City recognizes that this area will eventually involve in change with the installation of the Bus
                 Rapid Transit (BRT) line just north on the Meadowbrook Expressway as well as the new subdivision
                 that is under construction just south and east of this property that has R-1-6, R-1-8 and R-1-10
                 designations for property sites.

3.2     FINDINGS:
        3.2.1  This application was initiated by Randy Mitchell, owner of property at 783 West Bayport Way (Lot
               2)
        3.2.2  The applicant is requesting to amend the plat notes associated with the Bayport Way subdivision
               plat.
        3.2.3  775; 783; and 793 West Bayport Way are in the Single-Family Residential (R-1-10) zoning district.
        3.2.4  Parcels A and B are in the Single-Family Residential (R-1-40) zoning district
        3.2.5  The Future Land Use Map Designation for these properties on the City’s General Plan is “Low
               Density Residential.”
        3.2.6  The proposed plat amendment meets all subdivision and zoning standards in the City Code.
        3.2.7  Property owners are responsible for securing the appropriate development permits prior to any
               construction activity.
        3.2.8  The Taylorsville Planning Commission is the decision-making body for subdivision amendment
               requests.

        outstanding review comments prior to recording the plat.
