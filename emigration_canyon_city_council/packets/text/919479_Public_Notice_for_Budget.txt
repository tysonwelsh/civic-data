                  EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
                           NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-113 that the Emigration Canyon
Metro Township Council will hold public hearings on December 20, 2022, at 7:00 PM to receive
public comment on amendments to Emigration Canyon Metro Township’s fiscal year 2022 budget
and Emigration Canyon Metro Township’s proposed budget for 2023. The meeting will be held at
the Emigration Canyon Fire Station, 5025 Emigration Canyon Road, Emigration Canyon, Utah
84108, and via Zoom.com, https://us02web.zoom.us/j/83328451408.

The Council will consider the adoption of the Amended 2022 Budget and the 2023 Budget after
taking comments from the public during the hearing. The public can review and inspect the
proposed budget on the Utah Public Notice Website (https://www.utah.gov/pmn/). You may also
obtain copies of the documents and submit written comments prior to the public hearing by
contacting Nichole Watt at nwatt@slco.org or (385) 468-7400.
