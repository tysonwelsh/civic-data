RESOLUTION NO.: 20-02-01 DATE: February 6, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL RATIFYING THE
APPOINTMENT OF PAULINA F. FLINT AS WHITE CITY’S MEMBER ON THE BOARDS
OF TRUSTEES OF THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT AND SALT LAKE VALLEY
LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA
RECITALS

A. The White City Metro Township (“White City”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code § 10-2a-401

| et seq.

B. White City is part of the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area ("SLVLESA"), a local
district, and the Unified Police Department ("UPD"), an interlocal entity consisting of various
municipalities and Salt Lake County.

C. White City has the right and legal responsibility to appoint a member to the Board of Trustees for
SLVLESA and to the governing body of UPD.

D. At its January 2, 2020 meeting the White City Metro Township appointed D Scott Little as White
City's member of the Board of Trustees of SLVLESA and the governing body of UPD.

E. Subsequently, it was determined Mr. Little could not accept the appointment and it became
necessary to find a replacement.

F, Paulina F. Flint, Mayor of White City Metro Township, accepted the appointment, and confirmed,
through legal counsel polling the remainder of the White City Metro Council, that no one else on the
Counsel was ready, willing and able to accept the appointment.

G. The appointment was communicated to UPD and SLVLESA and Mayor Flint has already started the
appointment.

H. Based on the foregoing, the White City Metro Township Council, as the legislative body of White
City elected by the voters of White City, believes it is in the best interest of White City to formally
ratify by resolution the appointment of Paulina F. Flint as White City's member of the Board of
Trustees of SLVLESA and the governing body of UPD.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the White City Metro Township Council, the Council
formally ratifies the appointment of Mayor Paulina F. Flint to serve as White City's member of the
Board of Trustees of the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area and governing body of the
Unified Police Department; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED the Council will provide a copy of this Resolution to the Salt
Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area and Unified Police Department to inform them of the
Council action.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
