Public Comment (0:15) None. Approve November 3, 2022 Meeting Minutes (0:57) Mayor Kaufusi moved to approve the November 3, 2022 meeting minutes. Mayor Packard seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Board Officers - Appoint Chair and Vice Chair – LaNiece Davenport, MAG (0:39) The Mountainland MPO Bylaws govern MPO Board officers. A chair and vice chair are appointed annually as stated in Section 2.4 – Officers. The responsibilities of the chair were discussed. MAG Staff recommend that the MPO Board seek nomination(s) for the chair and the vice chair and then appoint, by simple majority, one elected official to serve a one-year term as chair and one elected official to serve a one-year term as vice chair. MPO Board February 2, 2023 Page 3 Commissioner Gordon moved that Mayor Kaufusi is appointed as MPO Board chair for a period of one-year. Mayor Wright seconded that motion. The motion passed unanimously. Mayor Johnson moved that Mayor Wright is appointed as MPO Board vice chair for a period of one-year. Mayor Andersen seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO Board Officers - Appoint Ex-officio Member – Shawn Eliot, MAG (5:37) The Mountainland MPO Bylaws govern MPO Board officers. Ex-officio, non-voting members currently on the board include positions for FHWA and FTA. With Camp Williams proximity to the high-growth areas surrounding the Point of the Mountain, they desire to play a more collaborative role in coordinating and planning in the area. Camp Williams has requested a board position, with BG Joseph Green appointed as an ex-official, non-voting member. MAG Staff recommends that due to the impact that Camp Williams has on the area and the high growth occurring in the region abutting Camp Williams, the camp should have representation, as an ex-official member, on the MPO Board to broaden the discussion of regional issues and decisions. Mayor Merrill moved that as per MPO Bylaws, an ex-official position be made for Camp Williams on the MPO Board, and that General Joseph Green be appointed to serve in that position. Mayor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. TIP Modifications – Bob Allen, MAG (8:52) Pleasant Grove 2600 North Additional Funding Pleasant Grove was awarded two projects to widen 2600 North to three lanes, including shoulders and a trail. The two projects were combined for design purposes and are ready to go out to bid. Project Selected Funds Type Scope Phase I 2018 $2,693,450 Exchange Widening, shoulders and trail Phase II 2020 $1,732,300 Exchange Widening, shoulders and trail Estimates show that an additional $3,113,174 is needed to complete the project due to unforeseen slope mitigation and construction and ROW inflation. Pleasant Grove requests the following funding modification. Original Funds $4,425,750 10% Contingency $442,575 Additional Funds $2,670,599 New project Total $7,538,924 MPO Board February 2, 2023 Page 4 MAG Staff recommends that the additional funds be awarded to the project. Mayor Andersen moved that $2,670,599 be added to the combined Pleasant Grove 2600 North widening project. Commissioner Gordon seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Spanish Fork Center Street Scope Modification Spanish Fork was awarded $6.5mto widen Center St to 5 lanes from US6 to 900 East. Several properties were acquired to build the project and the remnants are ready to be sold with an estimated value of $131,000. Spanish Fork would like to use the proceeds of the sale to construct a trail to the school in this corridor. The trail was originally to be built by a different project that was removed. The estimated cost would be $116,160. MAG Staff recommends that the trail be added to the scope of the project and be built with the proceeds from the sale of unused properties. Mayor Ostler moved that a trail be added to the scope of the Spanish Fork Center Street project. Mayor Olson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. UDOT 800 North Trail; 400 West to 900 West The TIP Manager approved $375,000 UDOT funds be added to the UDOT Orem 800 North Trail; 400 W to 900 W project.
