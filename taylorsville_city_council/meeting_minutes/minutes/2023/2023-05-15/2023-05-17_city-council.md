         5. 1     Overview of the Taylorsville Land Development Code Update Project– Jim
         Spung

          a Senior Planner Jim Spung explained what the land development code was and why
an update was important. He noted that a comprehensive update specifically for Taylorsville has
never been done. He explained that the goals for the update were:

         To streamline development review procedures and increase predictability of land use
         decisions;
         To consider both current and future needs of the City;
         To align the LDC with the General Plan and Utah Code
         To enable great design and a diverse mix of land uses; and
         To improve user- friendliness through logical structure, graphics, tables and illustrations


Mr. Spung presented the timeline for the project and the workflow priorities. Jim stated there are
three different stages for preparing the draft LDC— drafting the zone districts and land uses,

addressing the development and design standards and finally, addressing procedures and
administration.




IR Mr. Spung stated each portion would be brought to the Council and public for review and
comment.   He stated the current LDC was laid out in forty chapters where it could be quite
challenging to find needed information. The new code would be consolidated into just seven
chapters. He gave reference to the dates the drafts would be presented to the City Council.

Mr. Spung asked for feedback from the Council regarding how detailed they wished the various
presentations to be. He explained that each draft would include a summary memo highlighting key
changes. Jim gave a brief explanation on the commentary and footnotes that would aid the council
in understanding what changes were made and why.

     Councilmember Burgess indicated that he preferred summaries as opposed to in-depth detail.
Mr. Spung added that the planning commission had been going through the drafts in greater detail.
Chair Knudsen and Councilmember Cochran agreed that highlighting the documents for the
Council would be most beneficial. Mr. Spung stated that both summaries and drafts were available
online for their reference and that the final draft would be presented for the council' s formal
adoption in November 2023.


 IR Mayor Overson commented on the huge task that Jim and the entire planning team had
undertaken. She felt they were to be commended for their hard work. Chair Knudsen also thanked
Mr. Spung for his continued efforts.
