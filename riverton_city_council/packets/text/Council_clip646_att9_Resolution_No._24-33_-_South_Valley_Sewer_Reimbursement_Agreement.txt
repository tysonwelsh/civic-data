                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-33

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A REIMBURSEMENT
          AGREEMENT WITH SOUTH VALLEY SEWER DISTRICT


      WHEREAS, the South Valley Sewer District (“Distric”) intends to construct sewer
improvements on 11800 South Street, Riverton, Utah; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City (“City”) has requested that the District provide the
installation of a certain City water system improvement while constructing the sewer
improvements and have the City reimburse the District for said improvements; and

       WHEREAS, the District has agreed to bear a portion of the cost related to the city
improvements in order to complete the project in conjunction with the reconstruction of 11800
South Street thereby avoiding the need to reroute the contemplated replacement sewer around the
City improvements; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that execution of the
enclosed Reimbursement Agreement will enable the City to complete the aforesaid City
improvements;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

   1.      The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to execute the
           Reimbursement Agreement with South Valley Sewer District, in substantially similar
           form to the agreement attached hereto, upon verification that the agreement has been
           approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

   2.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 19th day of
