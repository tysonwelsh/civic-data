2. Public hearing and possible action regarding the Cove at Sun Peak Lot 9 Plat 
Amendment to modify the existing building pad; located at 5460 Cove Hollow 
Land; Parcel CSP-9; John Randle, Applicant- Tiffanie Northrup-Robinson, County 
Planner     
 
Planner Robinson said this item is for a plat amendment for the Cove at Sun Peak Lot 9.  
The parcel is 4.58 acres.  A slide with an aerial map was shared and the major 
landmarks were pointed out.  The subdivision was created in 1996.  It consisted of 
townhome units and nine estate lots.  Lot 9 is an estate lot.  The estate lots range in lot 
and building pad size.  Lot 9 has a building pad of 7,653 square feet.

The applicants are Jamie and John Randall.  There was a home built in 2001.  From that 
home, there is remaining square footage that can be developed.  The applicants are 
proposing to amend the building pad and place the remaining square footage at the rear 
of the lot.  The building pad square footage will remain the same.  They are not asking 
for any kind of an increase.  They ask to take what is remaining and place it in the back.  
They desire to relocate it where they will be able to build a more suitable deck.  The 
applicants have the ability to build a deck, but it would be higher and more visible.   
 
Planner Robinson summarized the two standard of review for a plat amendment.  
Standard 1: Neither the public nor any person will be materially injured.  
Standard 2: No plat amendment shall be approved which results in an increase of 
density. 
 
Planner Robinson said Staff reviewed the application and found this request results in a 
more suitable location for the building pad.  There is no increase of density.  Staff 
recommends that following a public hearing, the Commission take into consideration all 
comments received and vote to approve the proposed amendment as outlined in the 
Staff Report.  She added that proper noticing of the agenda item took place.   
The public hearing was opened.  There were no comments made and the public hearing 
was closed.
