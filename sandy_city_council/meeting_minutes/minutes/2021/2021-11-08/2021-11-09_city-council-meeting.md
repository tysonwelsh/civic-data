2.         21-428                 Parks and Recreation Department seeking input regarding the Council's
                                  vision for the future of the Parks and Recreation Building
                                  Dan Medina, Parks and Recreation Director, introduced Brian Parker with AEComm who
                                  is the consultant hired by Sandy City to provide an analysis on the Parks and Recreation
                                  building located in Historic Sandy. Mr. Parker asked the Council for their feedback and
                                  ideas regarding options for the building. The goal is for the parks administration staff to
                                  move their offices to the Alta Canyon Recreation Center as part of the facility redesign.
                                  Council Members offered ideas and feedback to Mr. Parker: community arts center,
                                  sports and wellness center, hub utilizing the gym for sports programs and community
                                  center. Council was supportive of keeping the existing Parks and Recreation office
                                  building and re-purposing the facility. Mr. Parker thanked the Council for their comments
                                  and ideas.



3.         21-430                 Public Utilities Department presenting the City Council with a report on the
                                  draft Water Conservation Plan
                   Attachments:   Memo - WCP to City Council.pdf
                                  DRAFT - Sandy City 2021 Water Conservation Plan.pdf

                                  Abi Holt with Public Utilities, presented the Water Conservation draft to the Council. The
                                  plan is a required submittal by the state, every five years. The plan has been presented to
                                  the Public Utility Advisory Board and is currently posted on the city website. The plan
                                  meets the conservation goals set by the state. The plan needs to be adopted by
                                  December 31, 2021. The public hearing will be held at next week's City Council meeting.
                                  Keith Larson with Bowen, Collins addressed questions and comments from the Council.
                                  Council questions included how the declining snow pack will impact the goals set in the
                                  conservation plan. Questions also included the education efforts directed to the youth.
                                  Tom Ward, Public Utilities Director, thanked the Council for their comments.

4.         21-427                 Parks and Recreation Department briefing the Council on a proposed
                                  amendment to the prohibited activities section of Chapter 13-11 of the
                                  Sandy City Municipal Code
                   Attachments:   Draft Ordinance 21-31_Redline.pdf

                                  Dan Medina, Parks and Recreation Director, presented on a proposed amendment to the
                                  prohibited activities section of Chapter 13-11 of the Sandy City Municipal Code. Council
                                  questions and comments followed. Mr. Medina will bring the proposed amendment back
                                  to the council next week for consideration and action. Council is supportive.

8.         21-431                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                                  Mr. Paul Pratt spoke about the proposed remodel of Alta Canyon Recreation Center. He
                                  expressed some concern with the remodel costs and thinks those funds could be utilized
                                  for other projects that have a more imminent need to the City. He congratulated those
                                  who won their elections and he thanked all for their service to the city.

                                  Ms. Jaci Renyolds is a professional pickleball player and mentioned that there are many
                                  professional players living in Utah. She spoke about the need to provide indoor pickleball
                                  courts in the area. It is one of the fastest growing sports in the country and is a sport for
                                  all age groups.

                                  Mr. Zachary Frankel, with Utah Rivers Association, spoke about the water conservation
                                  plan that will be presented later this evening. He expressed concern that the plan does
                                  not go far enough and the conservation goals should be set higher.

                                  Ms. Lori Sperry spoke about revenue that can be generated from indoor tennis and
                                  pickleball tournaments and the need for indoor courts in the area. She mentioned that
                                  over 600 people have signed a petition in support of indoor facilities and also spoke about
                                  a possible donation to the center.

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke about the proposed expansion of the Sandy Senior Center.
                                  He expressed concern that the Parks and Recreation building would not withstand an
                                  earthquake and thinks the building needs to be torn down.

                                  Public Comment Closed.

                                  Council moved to Item 3 on the Agenda.

Adjournment
                                  Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 8:13 pm.
