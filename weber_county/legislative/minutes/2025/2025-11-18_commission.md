D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Commissioner Froerer: I wanted to give a brief history of MIDA (Military Installation Development
      Authority). MIDA was created through the state legislature in 2007 and was intended to generate tax dollars
      to be passed on to Federal installations. Falcon Hill employs about 7k people who all make above the average
      AMI (Area Median Income). A 1.5M square foot manufacturing and office space is 100% occupied with about
      20% of the total project completed. Snow Basin had a lodge used by the military. When the Olympics came
      in, an agreement was made for that lodge to go private with the contingency that another location would be set
      aside for a new lodge with land eventually secured in Deer Valley. Last year, the Grand Hyatt opened there
      and offers military personnel substantially reduced rates. It created a $6.5B investment and $2.5B increase in
      property values on a project that is 20% complete. MIDA donated $50k to Weber County and to each of the
      four surrounding cities and $100k to Hill AFB Museum with money from the tax base MIDA created at Falcon
      Hill.

       APPROVING THE DONATION OF FUNDS TO LOCAL FOOD BANKS.
         Commissioner Froerer: Three years ago we received a substantial amount of money from the federal
         government through ARPA. Because we were so conservative with those funds and held those groups who
         received funds accountable we ended up with some left over. That money has to be spent by the end of 2026.
         Due to the delay in funding for SNAP funds, I feel it would be appropriate to share up to $50k of those leftover
         funds with the Catholic Community and TriCity food banks.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 46-2025 of the County Commissioners of Weber County
         approving the donation of funds to local food banks; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
