      1320 N (Lot 6 of Bryce’s Landing Plat A)

   4. Public Hearing: Considering changes to Title 10.16.5 Parking Standards and
      Required Maintenance for Parking Lots, Areas, and Accesses to:
               • Consider requiring an ingress/egress drive aisle (lane) adjacent to a
                  drive through aisle (lane).
               • Consider allowing a narrower drive aisle (lane) when a drive aisle
                  (lane) is exclusively one-way.
               • Adding some associated definitions.

             a. Open public hearing
                TIME: 7:13 PM

             b. Staff Presentation
                 Mr. Seth Atkinson presented that this came up as parking standards were
                 being discussed. This concerns drive lanes for egress. There is a twenty-five
                 (25) foot drive path required for all parking, and if there is only a one-way
                 parking that may not be needed. So, on the forty-five (45) degree angle it
                 would be a 16-foot drive aisle, a sixty-foot degree angle would require 18-foot
                 angle, and a dual drive would still require the twenty-five (25) drive aisle. The
                 definitions would define this, and it is proposed that we clarify the terms.

   5. Discussion regarding concept zoning along Highways 28 and 132 regarding
      the CU1 and CU2 zones.
            a. Staff Presentation
               Mr. Seth Atkinson discussed that this was spoken about in the General
               Plan. One of the things the city heard back from the community was that
               the public wanted to keep this area rural. One of the concerns was that
               the public did not want to see Main Street a traffic area, and very
               commercial. Once of the concerns is the traffic loads on that Main
               Street. One of the ways to mitigate that is that we would propose areas
               that would be dedicated to commercial pods, and the freeway to provide
               access to it. Combined use areas
               In this way, the city can avoid using Main Street as the sole
               transportation corridor to access commercial services. The city would
               like a long-term plan to help with this area and mix combined use and
               areas in the proposed areas on the map. (see attachment 1)
               Proposed areas are the following:
                   • Purple areas on map be turned to Commercial Use 2 (CU2)
                   • North side starting at 700 North to 400 North (Wasp Way) to
                       Commercial Use 2 (CU2).
                   • Alternate after between Commercial Use 1 (CU1) and
                       Commercial Use 2 (CU2) out.
                   • 100 South to 200 South as Commercial Use 1 (CU1)
                   • 200 South to 700 South as Commercial Use 2 (CU2)
                   • 700 South and to the end of town would be Highway
                       Commercial Use Zones.

             b. Commission discussion

                 Acting Chairwoman Heather Robertson asked for clarification about
                 homes and zoning on Main Street. If a home were destroyed or needed
                 to be scrapped if it could remain only for a home, or could a business
                 build there. Mr. Seth Atkinson answered that it would need to be zoned
                 strictly for residential, if combined use is there then a smaller business
                 could build in that location. Commissioner Robertson thinks that
                 Central Commercial needs to be removed and Commercial Use 2 (CU2)
                 needs to go the length of Main Street.

                 Commissioner Alan Hancock requested clarification for areas, because
                 he was under the impression that from Harmon Tires to Lisa’s Country
                 Kitchen and the idea was that there was a limited size to the buildings
                 and the zoning. It would limit businesses and homes to a certain size,
                 parking is clarified. Mr. Seth Atkinson clarified that because the Main
                 Street is currently zoned for mixed use it could potentially all be turned
                 into commercial which could turn Main Street to higher traffic. Politics
                 will change how things are done here, and Commissioner Hancock
                 didn’t see the need to change this right now.

                 Commissioner Fran Petersen stated that she thought more traffic lights
                 would be needed, and that homes will be sold to businesses when they
                 are tired of trying to get on the main roads. Mr. Seth Atkinson stated that
                 is correct that is why the city is trying to get traffic patterns that aren’t
                 congested, and zoning will help this.

                 Mr. Seth Atkinson expressed to the committee that the first approach
                 was to mitigate the zoning, and to prevent high traffic on Main Street
                 and keep the rural feeling of Nephi City. It was expressed that once the
                 pressure comes to Nephi City from businesses it will be harder for
                 future Planning Commissions and City Councils to turn them away. The
                 best approach would be to start now and help future Planning
                 Commissions to plan and assist the community in their desire for a rural
                 feel.

                 Commissioner Cory Thomson had a comment on high density housing
                 along main street, there are some issues with fourplexes around town
                 that aren’t as nice as they were. Commissioner Alan Hancock responded
                 that happens when the owners change, commissioners change, and it
                 seems when things get older it gets harder to take care of. Looking
                 forward though he would like to see “track street” stay residential.
