                                  sale of not more than $10,000,000 aggregate principal amount of Sales 
                                  Tax Revenue Bonds, Series 2023; and related matters for the Monroe

                                  Parameters Resolution 23-44C
                                  Bond Purchase Contract
                                  Supplemental Indenture

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director, presented Resolution 23-44C for Council consideration 
                                  and action. The resolution authorizes the issuance and sale of Sales Tax Revenue Bonds 
                                  in the aggregate principal amount of not to exceed $10,000,000. The Series 2023 Bonds 
                                  shall mature in not more than fifteen years and shall bear interest at a rate of not to 
                                  exceed five and one-half percent. The funds will be used for the Monroe Street extension. 
                                  Engineering has begun the design process. Ryan Kump with Public Works mentioned 
                                  the possibility of a funding shortfall of approximately $700,000. The department is working 
                                  to mitigate the shortfall through design engineering. Council questions and discussion 
                                  followed.

                                  Public comment opened.
                                  Public comment closed.



                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey, to adopt 
                                  Resolution #23-44C, a resolution of the City Council of Sandy City, Utah (the 
                                  "City), authorizing the issuance and sale of not more than $10,000,000 aggregate 
                                  principal amount of sales tax revenue bonds, series 2023; fixing the maximum 
                                  aggregate principal amount of the series 2023 bonds, the maximum number of 
                                  years over which the series 2023 bonds may mature, the maximum interest rate 
                                  which the series 2023 bonds may bear, and the maximum discount from par at 
                                  which the series 2023 bonds may be sold; delegating to certain officers of the city 
                                  the authority to approve the final terms and provisions of the series 2023 bonds 
                                  within the parameters set forth herein; authorizing the posting of a notice of 
                                  public hearing and bonds to be issued; authorizing and approving a 
                                  supplemental indenture a bond purchase contract and other documents required

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson thanked Shane Pace, CAO and Mayor Zoltanski and the 
                    department heads for their responsiveness and for coordinating all the public 
                    engagement. He appreciated everyone's efforts.

                    Council Member Scott Earl thanked Parks & Recreation for installing a bench on the 
                    canal trail in response to a resident's request. 

                    Council Member Alison Stroud thanked the Public Utilities teams for their quick response 
                    to help a resident with their issue. She also expressed appreciation to James Sorenson 
                    for his responsiveness.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked Dustin Fratto and Administration for their 
                    collaboration and problem solving. Working together is extremely beneficial. He 
                    expressed his gratitude for their work on the RFP. 

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey said there was a good turnout for food truck night in 
                    conjunction with Healthy Sandy. She provided an update from the Wasatch Front Waste 
                    and Recycling District Board meeting and mentioned the high costs related to the 
                    delivery of recyclables. She shared with residents the information presented at a recent 
                    news conference in the Willow Creek area, where Senator Mitt Romney announced the 
                    replacement of the Willow Green Circle Bridge over Little Cottonwood Creek as part of the 
                    Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act. This was a great day for the residents of Willow 
                    Creek.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski encouraged residents to vote in the Primary Election. The polls for early 
                    voting are open. She acknowledged the opening of the Waterford School science wing. 
                    She invited the public to the Healing Fields event and the Heritage Festival. At the 
                    festival, the City will be unveiling the time capsule which will be buried at Main Street 
                    Park - to be opened in 100 years. The city will also unveil the new city flag and present 
                    the Noal Bateman Lifetime service award.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, had no report.

Adjournment
                    The Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:38 pm.
