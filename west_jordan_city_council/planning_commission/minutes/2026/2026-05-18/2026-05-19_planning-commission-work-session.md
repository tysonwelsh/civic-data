5. Public Hearing (with Recommendation to City Council for Final Action)
     a.   Adopt, as a set of Land Use Regulations, a Completely Revised Version of the
          Public Improvement Standards, Specifications and Plans, including chapters
          for Plan Preparation, Transportation, Storm Water, Sanitary Sewer, Culinary
          Water, Construction and Construction Inspections, and Appendices for
          Standard Drawings.
          •   Utilities Director Greg Davenport stated that the standards are typically updated
              every 4–5 years, but West Jordan’s have not been updated in almost 19 years.
              He explained that the purpose of the update is to make the standards easier to
              understand for individuals who may not have an engineering background. This
              revision is primarily intended to simplify the standards. No comments, no
              questions.
     b.   7800 South Commercial; Rezone from C-M (Heavy Commercial) to C-G (General
          Commercial) and Future Land Use Map amendment from Business and research
          Park to Community Commercial, 4606, 4614, & 4622 West 7800 South.
          •   Senior planner Ray McCandles gave an overview of the item.
          No comments, no questions.
     c.   Sugar Factory on the Creek Townhomes; 1790 West Sugar Factory Road;
          Rezone 1.78 acres of property from a CC-F (City Center – Frame) Zone to a CC-R
          (City Center – Residential) Zone, a Preliminary Development Plan, and a Master
          Development Agreement.
          •   Associate Planner Forsythe gave an overview of the item.
          No comments, no questions.
     d.   Diaz Lot; 8305 South 2200 West; Rezone 0.43 acres from P-F (Public Facilities)
          Zone to R-1-8 (Single-family residential 8,000 square foot lots of minimum) Zone;
          •   Associate Planner Forsythe stated that the project area includes the canal.
              Research determined that the county maps are incorrect, and the applicant has
              been working with the South Jordan Canal Company to purchase the land. Mark
              stated the home proposed for the site will be very close to the canal bank.
              Commissioner Roberts indicated that he is a long-shot potential buyer for the
              property. Commissioners asked clarifying questions regarding the engineering
              needed along the canal bank. Staff noted that special engineering would be
              required, which would be handled through the Building Permit process.
          •   Commissioner Roberts asked Assistant City Attorney Duncan Murray for
              guidance, stating that he is a long-shot potential buyer for the property. He
              clarified that he has not engaged with the seller but is aware the property is for
              sale and has considered purchasing it and asked whether he should recuse
              himself from the item.
          •   Assistant City Attorney Murray stated that the primary requirement under state
              law is that such disclosures be made at the beginning of the meeting. He added
