                              Copperton Metro Township, Utah
                            RESOLUTION NO.   20-07-03

A RESOLUTION OF Copperton Metro Township ADOPTING THE 2019 SALT LAKE COUNTY HAZARD
MITIGATION PLAN


      WHEREAS Copperton Metro Township recognizes the threat that natural hazards
pose to people and property within Copperton Metro Township; and

      WHEREAS the Copperton Metro Township has participated in the creation of a
multi-hazard mitigation plan, hereby known as the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard
Mitigation Plan in accordance with the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000; and

      WHEREAS the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan identifies
mitigation goals and actions to reduce or eliminate long-term risk to people and
property in Copperton Metro Township from the impacts of future hazards and
disasters; and

      WHEREAS adoption by Copperton Metro Township demonstrates their commitment to
hazard mitigation and achieving the goals outlined in the 2019 Salt Lake County
Hazard Mitigation Plan

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY Copperton Metro Township, OTAH, THAT:

Copperton Metro Township adopts the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan

This resolution shall be effective on the date it is adopted.
