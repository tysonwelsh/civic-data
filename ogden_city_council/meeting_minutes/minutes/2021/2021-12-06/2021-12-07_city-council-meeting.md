Farr's Ice Cream
         A memo from Council staff regarding recognition of Farr' s Ice Cream for celebrating over 100 years of business came
before the Council for consideration. Council member Stephens read the certificate of recognition issued by the Ogden City
Council and Mayor Caldwell and presented Michael Farr and other members of the Farr family with the certificate.
         Mr. Farr thanked the Mayor and Council for this recognition and stated his family has brought ice cream for all in
attendance. He stated the Farr family is honored to be part of Ogden City. His father ran his business on his own for 40 years and
his children are now working together to keep it operating. This recognition is very meaningful to the Farr family, and he
summarized the history of Farr's operation in Ogden; there has been multi-generational support for the business, which has
allowed it to become a legacy family business and landmark in Ogden City. The Farr family is pleased to have been part of the
Ogden community for 101 years.
         Individual Council members related their own personal Farr's Ice Cream stories and expressed their love for the business
and the product they sell in the community.

        the whole block under the Mixed Use (MU) zone and would allow for multiple types of land uses within the same zone (i.e.,
        residential, and commercial). This zone text amendment is specific to the Capitol Square site and will not apply to other MU zones
        in the city. The ordinance outlines specific land uses and design standards that will apply to the site. The purpose of the zone text

        located.

        simultaneously review and approve a project master plan. Reviewing the master plan ensures that the developer follows design

     Proposed Ordinance 2021-61 amending sections 15-15-2.8, 15-35-5.8, and

               A memo from the Community and Economic Development Deparhnent regarding an ordinance prohibiting painted brick
     in certain East Central Zones came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated zoning code restricts brick homes in
     the East Central Community from being painted or covered with another material. This regulation is based off the community
     plan's emphasis on maintaining historic elements in the area. The applicant is requesting to remove this restriction so that brick
     homes can be painted in the East Central Community. The East Central Community is part of the Central Bench National Historic
     District, which was established in 2005. Certain zoning regulations, such as this, have been placed in this area to help preserve the
     historic nature of the community. The Planning Commission reviewed the request in July 2021, then in a work session in August,
     and a fmal vote took place on October 5, 2021. The Planning Commission ultimately decided that the painting restriction should
     remain, but with some minor amendments that would allow homes to be painted in exceptional circumstances where paint or a
     clear seal coat would be necessary to preserve deteriorating brick.
               Plarming Manager Montgomery summarized the memo and stated some general questions for Council consideration
     including the following:
                   •     Does the Council feel that restricting the painting or covering of brick is an important historic preservation tool
                         to retain historic character of buildings in the East Central?
                   •     How important is retaining the character of the buildings as a public good?
                   •     How does the fact buildings were painted prior to the regulation affect future considerations?
               Mr. Montgomery then engaged in discussion with the Council regarding whether the painted brick restriction should
     apply to new construction as well as existing homes. There was a heavy focus on whether the City can fairly enforce the
     regulations included in the proposed ordinances, with Mr. Montgomery noting that it may become necessary to adjust code
     enforcement regulations to address any unintended consequences of the adjusted ordinance.

Mayor Comments
          Mayor Caldwell thanked the City's Public Services Department for their work at the City's Christmas Village. The
opening weekend was very successful, and he thinks that is due in large part to the quality of the venue and the desire of residents
to get out and participate in festive activities. He added today is the 80 111 anniversary of the attack on Pearl Harbor and he thanked
the "Greatest Generation" for the sacrifices they made for this nation following that attack.

Council member Comments
          Council member Stephens stated it was great to honor Farr' s Ice Cream and the contribution that they have made to the
Ogden community. He added there are many successful companies throughout the world that got their start in Ogden. He echoed
the comments made by Mayor Caldwell about the opening weekend of the Christmas Village; the parade and fireworks show were
great. He encouraged residents to do their holiday shopping in Ogden this year. He then thanked Ms. Bennett for her comments
and suggestions for increasing communication from the City to its residents.
          Council member Choberka thanked all residents for their participation tonight. She then thanked the City Council and
City Administration for their support of the Dumke Arts Plaza. It will make a wonderful contribution to the community and is an
example of a meaningful community partnership that will benefit the entire City. She feels that something similar can occur with
the Marshall White Center and she looks forward to continued discussion of that project. She thanked the community members
who organized the meeting over the past weekend and indicated she was grateful to have had the chance to participate.
          Council member Nadolski stated he shares the frustration of many in the community. There are many times that the City
leaders are the cause of frustration, and they match that frustration with their own frustration, but it is necessary for everyone to
get to the point where they come together and agree on something or 'the community is going to explode'. He hopes everyone can
move in a positive direction that is collaborative and communicative regarding the MWC project. He encouraged everyone to lead
with love when working on this project.
          Vice Chair White thanked Mr. Hutchinson for his comments about the speeding problems in his neighborhood. She
agrees that a response is needed before someone is hurt or killed in the area.
          Chair Blair announced that the special Redevelopment Agency meeting scheduled to follow this meeting has been
cancelled.


     There being no further business to come before the Council, VICE CHAIR WIDTE MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 8:22P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER STEPHENS, ALL VOTING AYE.
