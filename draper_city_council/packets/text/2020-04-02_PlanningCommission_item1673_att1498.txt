SUMMARY and BACKGROUND
The applicant is requesting approval of a Plat Amendment of the Draper Plaza Subdivision to reconfigure
the existing four lots into six new lots. The exterior boundary of the subdivision will not be affected and
none of the buildings within the subdivision will be affected. The original subdivision was recorded in
April, 2000. All of the properties within the original subdivision are developed. A majority of the
properties were built in the early 2000’s. The corner parcel (currently Key Bank) was constructed around
2013.

Exhibit C). The purpose of the CR zone is to “provide areas where a combination of destination-
oriented business, retail commercial, entertainment, and related uses may be established, maintained and
protected to serve both residents and non-residents of the City.” Typical uses in this zone include large-
scale, master-planned commercial centers with outlying commercial pads, big-box stores, offices, and
various types of high density residential uses.” The CR zoning designation is not identified by the
General Plan as a preferred zoning classification for the Community Commercial land use designation.
The property is surrounded by R3 (Single Family Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum) to the
east and southeast. The properties to the south are zoned CR. The properties to the west are zoned CI
(Interchange Commercial) and CC (Community Commercial). To the north of the site is the City
boundary with Sandy City. The properties located to the north in Sandy City are zoned CBD (Central
Business District).

All of the lots include the buildings and parking associated with the lot or building. According to Table
9-11-2 of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC), commercial lots within the City do not have a
minimum lot size, lot frontage or lot width. Therefore all of the current and proposed lots meet this
requirement. Any newly created vacant lot that is developed in future will need to meet all requirements
for lot coverage, setbacks and building height.

Criteria for Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision Plat Amendment
request are found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

         “The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing an
         amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds that there is
         good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has
         been vacated or amended.”
