ROBERT FILLERUP - I'M AN ATTORNEY FROM OREM AND I HAVE
REPRESENTED THE HOLDAWAY’S ON THIS PROPERTY SINCE THE EARLY
1980'S. WE SETTLED WITH THE STATE OF UTAH IN THE FEDERAL
LITIGATION BACK IN 2012 WHICH STIPULATED THAT THE STATE OF UTAH
GAVE UP ANY CLAIM TO THAT PROPERTY. COMMISSIONERS, ADAM BECK,
AND CLARISSA REEVE (DATA ANALYST) DISCUSS ISSUES SUCH AS ISSUE
WITH THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT REGARDING NORTH OF THE PROVO
RIVER, GREENBELT REDUCTION, WHETHER THE PROPERTY IS GREENBELT,
ETC. COMMISSIONERS DECIDE TO STRIKE AND LET THEM GO THROUGH
APPLICATION PROCESS FOR GREENBELT APPLICATION SO RECOMMEND
CONTINUING 3 MONTHS (SEPTEMBER 14, 2022)

e BEZZANT: COMMISSIONERS, JEANNE BOWEN AND ADAM BECK DISCUSS
SITUATION INCLUDING DEATH IN THE FAMILY, OUT OF THE COUNTRY FOR
