                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)

                                        Meeting began at – 5:32 p.m.

OAM2022-000724: Following their public hearing on April 3rd, 2023, the Planning Commission forwarded
a recommendation of adoption of Titles 18 and 19 (Subdivisions and Zoning) to the Council. After review,
the staff and Council identified an issue with the Multi-Family Residential standards in Chapter 19.30,
Medium and High-Density Residential Zones. Revisions to the original language have been drafted and
include added standards for single-family attached homes and new allowances for Residential
Neighborhood PUDs in RM and R-4-8.5 Zones. Staff will discuss these recommended revisions with the
Planning Commission before proceeding to the Council for final review and adoption. Planner: Kayla
Mauldin (Discussion / Possible Motion)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Kayla Mauldin provided an analysis of
the identified issues with the Multi-Family Residential standards.

Commissioners, staff, and Counsel had a brief discussion regarding less houses per dwelling, maximum
density, and standards limiting the density.

Commissioner Thomas motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Taylor seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public was present to speak.

Commissioner Taylor motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Thomas seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend approval of file #OAM2022-000724 regarding issues with the Multi-Family
Residential standards in Chapter 19.30, Medium and High-Density Residential Zones. Revisions to the
original language have been drafted and include added standards for single-family attached homes and new
allowances for Residential Neighborhood PUDs in RM and R-4-8.5 Zones to the Kearns Council.
        Motion by: Commissioner Thomas
        2nd by: Commissioner Taylor
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)

Commissioner Hatch advised of training on June 15th. Mr. Bracken advised of the 4-hour training
requirement passed by the legislature. Ms. Mauldin reminded the Commission of Night out against crime.

Commissioner Taylor motioned to adjourn, Commissioner Thomas seconded that motion.
