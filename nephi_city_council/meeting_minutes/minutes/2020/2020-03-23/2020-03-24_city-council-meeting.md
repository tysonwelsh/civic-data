                                         Meeting Minutes
                                       Work Session 3-24-20
                                             7:00 p.m.
                                    Nephi City Council Chambers


1.   Mr. Neal Cook, candidate for Juab County Commissioner, visited with the council about issues and
     partnerships between Nephi City and Juab County.

2.    Finance Director/City Recorder Lisa Brough discussed a draft of a sick-leave donation policy with the
     council.

3. The council discussed the possible location sites for Substation #3.

4. The council discussed the possibility of adopting an electronic meeting ordinance. They talked about
   only holding electronic meetings in times of declared emergencies and to eliminate the public
   comment portion of the meeting during electronic meetings.

5. The council discussed holding off on a property tax increase for the police department building and
   new school resource officer because of the economic impact of the COVID-10 pandemic. The council
   also discussed moving up the communication coordinator on the budget priority list.

6. The council reviewed the up-to-date financial reports.

7. The council discussed modifying the city’s shut-off Policy for lack of payment during these difficult
   economic times.

8. Councilor Worwood volunteered to be on the hiring committee for the solid-waste collector.

9. The council gave various updates.

10. The meeting adjourned at 8:20 p.m.



Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan Memmott, Skip
Worwood, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, J.D. Parady, Neil Cook.
