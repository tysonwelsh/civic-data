40            Ordinance No. 21-21 – Removal of Park Restrictions – Ryan Carter, City Attorney
41
42   Mayor Staggs said that Ryan Carter, City Attorney had been working on changing the ordinance
43   for the main park.
44
45   Ryan Carter, City Attorney, said that the change to the ordinance came because it has been a
46   unique year. He said that the flood at Midas Creek Park pushed leagues that were using that
47   park out. He said the school district has also put a moratorium on the use of their fields due to
48   water restrictions. He said that leagues have looked elsewhere for space to practice and play
49   sports. He said that the main park was not built for league use and he does not recommend
50   long term league use. He said that that current ordinance prohibits league sports and believes
51   that removing the park restrictions would allow the main park to be used for situations like this
     Page | 5 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     August 3, 2021




 1   year. He said that it will be up to the City Manager to decide if the main park will be used for
 2   leagues and what the fees and costs associated with this use will be.
 3
 4   Councilmember Stewart said that the City should try to educate leagues that the facilities are
 5   not just for the sport using the park; the facilities are for everyone.
 6
 7   Councilmember Buroker said she is not supportive of this change to the ordinance. She said
 8   that she would like to discuss and not make an action tonight. She asked how many parks the
 9   City has, how many parks are active and passive, how do they make the decision as to whether
10   they are active or passive parks, what’s the value of a passive park and how will the new
11   regional park help. She said she is not supportive of this change.
12
13   Councilmember McDougal said he is supportive of this change but wants to know what the
14   broader strategy is for the use of the parks. He said he would like to preserve the main park
15   until after the 4th of July. He said that he would like to evaluate the park policy and believes this
16   document will change over time.
17
18   Councilmember Wells said that he would like to have a discussion about park usage and
19   restroom facilities.
20
21   Councilmember McCay said that she would like to move forward with the change to the
22   ordinance and believes the City still has the ability to decide what will happen with the main
23   park.
24
25   Councilmember Stewart MOVED that the Riverton City Council adopt Ordinance No. 21-21
26   removing prohibitions against use of Riverton City Main Park by sports leagues. He
27   would like to have a broader discussion about Councilmember Buroker’s questions.
28   Councilmember McDougal SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the
29   motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-no,
30   McCay-yes, Stewart-yes, McDougal-yes, and Wells-yes. The motion passed unanimously.

33            Riverton City Sign Ordinance, Initial Discussion of Current Ordinance to Review
34            Potential Revisions and Updates
35
36   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, said it was time to update the sign ordinance
37   for various reasons including simplifying the code and to address current issues. He said that
38   one area he wanted to change is digital signs which are currently limited to a 50% max of the
39   sign. He said that the technology is more cost effective and more businesses are wanting to
40   have a larger percentage of their signs be digital.
41
42   Councilmember Stewart said that some of the concern in the past is about pixilation. He said
43   that the ordinance could include language about the pixilation being at a certain level to be able
44   to have a larger percentage of digital signage.
45
46   Councilmember Wells said there was an issue about a sign on Bangerter which was too bright.
47   He said that this should be taken into consideration. Councilmember Wells asked if Paul
48   Wayman was trying to put a sign on his fence.
49
50   Mr. Lethbridge said that the code for civic signs are exempt but must meet the same standard
51   as a commercial sign. He said that the needs for a City getting out information is different than
     Page | 6 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     August 3, 2021




 1   for a business. He said a lot of cities have a separate category for civic signs and he would like
 2   this included in the ordinance. He said that Paul Wayman wanted to attach a sign to one of the
 3   panels in the fence. He said the current code says that the monument sign has to be free
 4   standing and the language precludes that. He said there are ways the code could be written to
 5   accommodate this.
 6
 7   Mayor Staggs said he would like to address the 50% digital signage language. He said that
 8   Casey Saxton and Brook Bowen are working on some mockups for the monument signage that
 9   has been budgeted $150,000 out of the RDA. He said he is not opposed to changing the
10   ordinance so that Paul Wayman could have signage along his fence. He said that this
11   ordinance will need to go before the Planning Commission.
