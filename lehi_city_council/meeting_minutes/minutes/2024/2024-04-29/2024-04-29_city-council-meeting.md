Regular Session, 2:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Opening Comment
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Hancock gave the opening comment.

2. Discussion and Presentation Items

2.1a) Consideration of a Resolution approving an interlocal agreement with other entities within
the current boundaries of the Alpine School District for the creation of a new school district.

Mayor Johnson clarified that the meeting’s purpose was to authorize the preparation for a filing,
not to make final decisions. This filing would allow the new district proposal to potentially appear
on the November ballot. The chair reassured attendees that there would be ample opportunities for
public discussion and input as the process progressed.

Councilor Condie inquired about the City’s future obligations if this passed, roles regarding
advocacy, and requirements for the ballot proposition. Jason Walker, City Administrator
responded by stating that this is uncharted territory, and that further research is needed to provide
answers.

Mayor Johnson suggested that if a motion were made, it should recognize the coordination with
the west interlocal, ensuring that there is no overlap or competition, but rather mutual support.

Mayor Johnson asked for public comments. There were none.

    Motion: Councilor Newall moved to approve Resolution #2024-21 approving an interlocal
    agreement with other entities within the current boundaries of the Alpine School District for

    interlocal and that those proposed boundaries do not overlap.

    Ryan Wood, City Attorney, suggested adding to the motion that the Mayor is authorized to
    execute the agreement and make changes that aren’t substantially different.

    Councilor Hancock requested adding to the motion that Lehi City supports the west side
    interlocal.

    Councilor Stallings suggested that findings be added to the motion such as why this is being
    done by interlocal agreement. Also, include that the individual cities are choosing this option,
    rather than having the option chosen for them by the school district.

    Mayor Johnson stated that it recently became clear that in order to secure the best option for
    Lehi residents it was necessary to form the interlocal.

    Amended Motion: Council Newall amended her motion to include all suggestions with the
    findings that the establishing an interlocal allows Lehi voters to have a the say without
    having voters from outside of our interlocal that impact the decision of whether or not they
    are able to form a new school district. It’s a power that the cities have that the district does
    not. If the district were to put an option on the ballot it would require the majority from
    everyone in the school district to pass. The interlocal allows residents to control their own
    destiny and future without the impact from other cities outside of the interlocal. Councilor
    Condie seconded the amended motion.


    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Newall, Yes; and Councilor Stallings, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the City Council at this time, Councilor Hancock
   moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Albrecht seconded the motion. The motion passed
   unanimously. The meeting was adjourned at approximately 2:20 p.m.
