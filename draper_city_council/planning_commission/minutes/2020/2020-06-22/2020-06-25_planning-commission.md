3.    Action Item: Hidden Canyon Estates Phase 6 Final Subdivision Plat (Administrative
      Action)
      On the request of Brandon Watson, representing Edge Land 16, LLC, for a Final
      Subdivision Plat for the Hidden Canyon Estates Phase 6 Subdivision on 6.32 acres located
      at approximately 14755 South Springtime Rd. Application SUBD-843-2019. Staff Contact
      is Jennifer Jastremsky at 801-576-6328, jennifer.jastremsky@draper.ut.us.

      Jennifer Jastremsky gave an overview of the item. Hidden Canyon Estates was a larger,
      300 lot subdivision at the top of the mountain. Phase 6 was the final phase. Phase 6 was
      within the residential medium density land use. The zoning was split between RM and C3.
      These zoning designations were older and were kept on the property with the Suncrest
      Development Agreement and the Traverse Ridge Development Agreement. The
      development agreement allowed for single family homes on at least 7,500 square foot lots.
      The proposed plat would add 22 additional lots into Hidden Canyon Estates. The lots varied
      from approximately 7,500 square feet to 34,000 square feet. The development would have
      a 50-foot-wide public road. The road and the subdivision all followed Draper City code as
      well as followed the development agreement. The one condition of approval was that they
      bring forth to the Council an annexation into the TRSSD service district prior to plot
      recordation.

      Mr. Adams asked if there were any questions.

      Mr. Hawker asked if the developer had to disclose the sensitive overlay prior to someone
      purchasing a lot.

      Ms. Jastremsky replied the shaded areas were easements to protect the retention walls. The
      grading of the slopes would be improved prior to a citizen purchasing the lot.

      Mr. Adams asked the applicant if he had anything to add.

      Brandon Watson, the applicant, stated he had worked on the project since 2016 and had
      enjoyed his time on the project. It was bittersweet that the project was coming close to
      ending. He mentioned the easements were there to protect the retaining walls and asked if
      anyone had any questions for him.

4.    Public Hearing: Jenson Farms Lot 109 Plat Amendment (Administrative Action)
      A request by Tim Gough, representing Gough Homes, for a plat amendment in the R4
      (Single Family Residential, 10,000 square foot lot minimum) Zone in order to allow the
      placement of a 20-foot-wide Draper City access and maintenance easement on Lot 109
      Jenson Farms. The subject property is located at approximately 11837 S. Ridge Oak Lane.
      Application SUBD-1003-2020. Staff contact: Maryann Pickering at (801) 576-6391 or
      maryann.pickering@draper.ut.us.

      Maryann Pickering gave an overview of the item. This was lot 109 of the Jenson Farms.
      The land use was residential medium density and the zoning was R4. This project had been
      approved in 2017 and was called the Jenson Farms Development. When this had been
      approved, there was the subdivision rezone and the development agreement. The
      development agreement was only there to designate who would be completing the
      improvements for the proposed park. In August 2018, the agreement was modified to limit
      who could use the crossing for phase 1A and 2 of the subdivision until the bridge was
      constructed. This was so the City could maintain the retaining walls until the bridge was
      constructed. She asked the Planning Commission if they had questions for her.

      Mr. Adams asked if the applicant was in the meeting he was not.

      Mr. Adams opened the public hearing.

      Tamil Thorn commented the map the Planning Commission had was different from the
      map she had. She had thought it was on the back of lot 108, but on the map the Planning
      Commission presented it was across the street from lot 108. She had no concerns if the lot
      was across the street.

      Ms. Squire asked if the need for the easement was to complete the bridge.

5.     Public Hearing: Fox Landing Phase 3 Addition Land Use Map and Zoning Map
       Amendment Request (Administrative Action)
       On the request of Derek Wright, representing Wright Homes, for a Land Use Map
       Amendment from Residential Low/Medium Density to Residential Medium Density, and
       a Zoning Map Amendment from RA1 to R3 on 0.461 acres of property located generally
       at 11720 South 700 West. Application TEXTMAP-998-2020 and TEXTMAP-999-2020.
       Staff contact is Jennifer Jastremsky at 801-576-6328 or jennifer.jastremsky@draper.ut.us.

       Ms. Jastremsky stated the piece of property was part of a larger parcel that equaled 1.38
       acres. There was a home that fronted 7th West. The applicant wanted to purchase the rear
       0.461 acres of the property highlighted on the screen in order to incorporate that property
       into the future phase 3 of Fox Landing Subdivision and retaining an existing 40,000 square
       foot lot for the existing home on 7th West. The property was in the residential low medium
       density land use and the applicant was requesting change the land use designation to the
       residential medium density land use. This land use change would allow for third of an acre
       lots and fourth of an acre lots. The property was currently in the RA1 zone, where the
       minimum lot size was one acre, and the applicant was requesting to be in the R3 zone where
       the minimum lot size was 13,000 square feet. The existing home would retain the RA1
       zoning. She showed site photos. Fox Landing had a development agreement that limited
       the number of lots and the density. The request complied with the development agreement.
       The maximum number of units allowed was 90 and the addition requested would make
       them have 89 units. The maximum density allowed was 2.2 units per acre and with the
       requested addition they would have 2.01 units per acre. The minimum average lot size was
       14,500 square feet and with the requested addition the average lot size would be 16,963
       square feet. She asked if there were any questions.

       Ms. Fowler asked if the addition of this property would need the development agreement
       to be amended.
