PRIMARY ELECTION CANVASS
      The canvassing of election results was brought before the council again. According to state law
      the results cannot be canvassed until an audit has been completed. Lisa Brough, City Recorder,
      participated in that audit at the County Clerk’s office this past week. The audit consisted of
      taking random batches of ballots and physically tallying them and comparing them against the
      machine’s totals to see if they matched. Councilor Skip Worwood made a motion to accept the
      results from the primary election. Councilor JD Parady seconded the motion. The motion
      passed unanimously. A recorder’s certificate was signed by the Nephi City Board of Canvassers
      including the Mayor and City Council Members.
        Councilor Cowan expressed concern that there was only 30% voter participation. She
        questioned what could be done to get more voters out. City administrator, Seth Atkinson,
        mentioned challenges of informing public. Lisa Brough discussed the role of the chambers in
        promoting candidate meetings and the potential benefits of recording and posting such
        meetings. The council liked the idea of having a meet the candidate night prior to the general
        election.

RESOLUTION 08-26-2025 – NON-CONFORMING USE EXCEPTION
      The request by James Sinkovic to split a parcel that contains a non-conforming structure on the
      property was brought before the council last week (8/19/25). The council wanted to review it a
      little more before making a decision. Mr. Atkinson presented the request to the council again.
      He explained the non-conforming use due to insufficient setback and the proposed adjustment
      to meet zone requirements so the west parcel could become a buildable lot. The staff
      recommended the adjustment as it does not worsen the non-conforming use and benefits the
      adjacent lot. Councilor Shari Cowan made a motion to adopt Resolution 08-26-2025 – Non-
      conforming Use Exception. Councilor JD Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed
      unanimously approving the parcel line adjustment.

ADMINISTRATIVE AND COUNCIL UPDATES
   • Mr. Atkinson reminded everyone of the upcoming Employee Appreciation Dinner on September
      4 at the City Park Large Bowery. The council will arrive early to help cook.
   • Mr. Atkinson reported on the new gas filters installed by the natural gas department at various
      gate stations to help protect the system from any dirt and debris. The filter at the main gate
      station weighed 500 lbs.
   • Mr. Atkinson stated that there was a data center that was looking to upgrade our line up to the
      north. They talked to him last week and are still interested. They need to pay for the feasibility
      study cost and then we can make plans to move forward.
   • Mr. Atkinson reminded the council to plan for the tour of the Nortonville substation the second
      meeting in September.
   • Councilor Cowan mentioned citizen comments about the flooding in town from the heavy rains.
      Donald Ball reported there was over an inch in an hour. Mr. Atkinson explained the stormwater
      drains were built to handle a 25-year stormwater event, but an inch of water in an hour exceeds
      that amount. It would be very expensive to upgrade the stormwater system. A stormwater fee
      utility was recently adopted to fund maintenance and improvements including cleaning sumps.
