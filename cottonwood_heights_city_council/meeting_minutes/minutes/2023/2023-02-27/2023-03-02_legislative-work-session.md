MINUTES OF THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL LEGISLATIVE
WORK SESSION HELD THURSDAY, MARCH 2, 2023, AT 9:00 A.M. VIA ZOOM WITH
AN ANCHOR LOATION AT THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL WORK
ROOM LOCATED AT 2277 EAST BENGAL BOULEVARD, COTTONWOOD
HEIGHTS, UTAH

Members Present:         Mayor Mike Weichers, Council Member Scott Bracken, Council Member
                         Douglas Petersen, Council Member Shawn E. Newell, Council Member
                         Ellen Birrell

Staff Present:           City Manager Tim Tingey, Records Culture and Human Resources Director
                         Paula Melgar, Community and Economic Development Director Michael
                         Johnson, Administrative and Fiscal Services Director Scott Jurges, Systems
                         Administrator Alex Earl

City Lobbyists:          Greg Curtis, Brian Allen, Chantel Nate

1.      Welcome and Determination – Mayor Weichers.

Mayor Mike Weichers called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. and welcomed those present.

2.      Legislative Update and Discussion.

City Lobbyist, Brian Allen, presented the Legislative update and stated that there are two days
remaining in the 2023 Session.

SB-295 had been circled and remains as such until the end of the session.

HB-499 pertaining to Homelessness made it through the House of Representatives and is expected
to pass out of the Senate.

HB-364 and HB-406 pertaining to Housing Affordability, was presented by Representative White.
They are addressing land development issues and working through changes.

HB-291 pertaining to Short-Term Rentals was intended to create a pilot program and was sent to
the Intern Study.

HB-527 pertaining to Mining Operations did not leave rules and will not be heard.

SB-260 pertaining to Transportation Funding continued to go back and forth and was currently in
suspended rules.

HB-329 pertaining to Event Permits requires the City rank content for events. There may be an
attempt to amend with language that would allow the City to put all permitting events on a website
with QR Codes to notify residents of events. Mr. Allen was uncertain of the outcome.

Council Member Birrell raised a question regarding SB-2 and the $150 million designated to
transportation in Cottonwood Canyons.

City Lobbyist, Greg Curtis, reported that SB-185 pertaining to the Creation of Trail Programs, will
include ongoing annual funding of $45 million with an additional jumpstart of $45 million the first
year. The project includes paved trails connecting communities while allowing transportation
without having to interact with traffic. The Utah Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) will
have latitude when building up the program with an implemented ranking process. He noted that
it is a new program that is part of the Governor’s initiatives.

Council Member Birrell expressed enthusiasm for SB-185 and felt it was important to connect to
neighboring cities. She believed that continuing to fund more lanes and fast arterials and collectors
throughout the communities will create fractures between cities. Mr. Curtis stated that the use of
funds is not yet available as it will be administered by the Transportation Committee and
appropriated through UDOT. There are restrictions within the Code with regard to imminent
domain for trails. Details on how the prohibitions will shape out have not yet been provided. He
understood that a number of trails will be dedicated areas adjacent to highways or roads. Locations
were discussed.

Mr. Curtis responded to Council Member Birrell’s concern regarding SB-2 and stated that it would
include $100 million in State money with $50 million going to the Cottonwood Canyons. Potential
projects in the City were discussed.

Council Member Birrell requested an update on HJR-17, a proposal to amend the Utah
Constitution to require 60% votes on initiatives. Mr. Allen believed HJR-17 passed but would
confirm the status.

HB-527, a Mining Operations Amendment, received significant pushback and died.

City Manager, Tim Tingey reported that after the close of the 2023 Legislative Session, the City
Lobbyists will present a Legislative Summary during a future Work Session.

Mayor Weichers thanked Messrs. Allen and Curtis and Ms. Nate for their efforts representing
Cottonwood Heights.

3.      Adjourn.

MOTION: Council Member Birrell moved to adjourn. The motion was seconded by Council
Member Newell. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the Council.

The Work Session adjourned at 9:43 a.m.
