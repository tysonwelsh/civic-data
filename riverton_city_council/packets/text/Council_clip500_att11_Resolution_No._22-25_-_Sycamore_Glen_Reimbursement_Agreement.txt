                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-25

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A REIMBURSEMENT
              AGREEMENT WITH TOLL SOUTHWEST LLC


      WHEREAS, Riverton City has engaged in a project to create a regional detention pond
and make related improvements to the same at 11800 South and Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah;
and

        WHEREAS, said project requires the installation of improvements which are system
improvements as that term is understood under the Utah Impact Fees Act, Utah Code § 11-36-
101 et seq.; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has determined that the installation of these system
improvements are included in a real estate development project known as the Sycamore Glen
Subdivison Project, undertaken by Toll Southwest LLC; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that execution of the
enclosed Reimbursement Agreement will enable the City to complete the aforesaid stormwater
detention improvement project;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

   1.      The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to execute the
           Reimbursement Agreement with Toll Southwest LLC, in substantially similar form to
           the agreement attached hereto, upon verification that the agreement has been
           approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

   2.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately.


       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 15th day of
March, 2022 by the following vote:
