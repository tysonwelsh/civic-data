1. WORK SESSION TO DISCUSS VISION, PARTNERSHIPS, AND FUNDING THE
LEGISLATURE APPROPRIATED FOR THE NEWLY DESIGNATED BRIDAL VEIL FALLS

STATE MONUMENT
-BEN STANLEY, Attorney

(Discussed prior to Public Hearing)
(Timestamp: 7:54)

BEN STANLEY, DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY - STATED THAT THE COUNTY HAS
EXPECTATIONS FOR WHAT THEY WANT SEEN IN THE MONUMENT AND THE
STATE HAS EXPECTATIONS OF HOW THEY’D LIKE IT TO BE CONSISTENT WITH
OTHER STATE MONUMENTS. PUBLIC WORKS HAS ALREADY DONE A LOT OF
WORK IN THE AREA AND MORE TO BE DONE WITH THE TRAILS. THE STATE HAS
APPROPRIATED OVER $1.2 MILLION FOR IMPROVEMENTS. REFERRED TO THE
DRAFTED DOCUMENT THAT REINFORCES THE FACT THAT THE COUNTY WANTS
TO MAKE SURE WE HAVE CONTROL AND THAT WE HAVE CONTINUING
APPROPRIATIONS THAT PROVIDE ADDITIONAL MONEY FOR THE MAINTENANCE.

JEFF RASMUSSEN, STATE DIVISION OF PARKS DIRECTOR - STATED THAT HE HAS
BEEN WORKING ON THIS PROJECT FOR THE LAST COUPLE OF YEARS. EXPLAINED
THAT THE STATE MONUMENTS ACT REQUIRES THAT THE SITE BE SELF-
SUFFICIENT OR COME WITH A PARTNER OR FUNDING SOURCE TO BE ABLE TO
COVER ONGOING COSTS. TURNS OUT THAT WE’RE GOING TO HAVE A PARTNER TO
BE ABLE TO CO-MANAGE BRIDAL VEIL FALLS AND THIS WAS THE KEY TO BEING
ABLE TO MAKE THIS WORK.

(Showed the initial idea for the signage for the Falls)
