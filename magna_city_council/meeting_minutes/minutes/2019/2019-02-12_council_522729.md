Application #30802 – Mary Taylor requesting approval of a zone change from R-1-6 (single-family
residential) to RM (residential multi-family) to allow for office use of the property, located at 8446
West 3500 South.

                Max Johnson, Salt Lake County Planning and Development Services Division,
reviewed the application stating there were some things to consider: the property would abut
residential property to the north, there would be setbacks, and the wall may need to be more
substantial depending on the number of units. The draft General Plan reflects that this property
area could be more than just single-family home. Planning and Development staff recommended
support, and the Magna Planning Commission unanimously recommended approval with a
condition to limit the height to 35 feet. The RM allows for a higher height, so that would have to
be done as a zoning condition.

               Council Member Hull asked if the property owner planned to put an office in there.

                Mr. Johnson stated an RM zone does allow for a home-based office. That
requires a certain amount of parking, so there would have to be provisions in the back of the home
to accommodate that. He did not know what the applicant’s intent was right now, but she did talk
to him about moving it into a commercial zone down the road. Staff would probably be supportive
of that, although the impact on neighbors would have to be evaluated at all phases of that process.

               Council Member Hull asked if there was a residence there now.

              Mr. Johnson stated yes, there a home directly west of the old Department of
Children and Family Services (DCFS) building.

               Council Member Prokopis asked the applicant what her intent was.

             Mary Taylor stated she was not sure, but she may decide to sell it. She has been
approached by people asking to put an insurance agency in there.

               Council Prokopis asked if a business was being run out of there now.

               Ms. Taylor stated no.

              Mr. Johnson stated if the Council granted the RM rezone now, and the applicant
decided to rezone to commercial, she would have to reapply.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Ferguson, moved to open
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

                Todd Richards, Magna Planning Commission stated the planning commission did
hear this for an RM zone with the height restriction. If the applicant wanted a commercial zone,
she would have to go before the planning commission again.

               Mr. Johnson stated the staff report does have to be amended to include the zoning
condition.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Prokopis, moved to close the

Reports from Other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Ken Malone, Executive Officer, Magna Precinct, Unified Police Department
(UPD), stated he is new to the precinct, but excited to be here and help achieve things that need
to be done.

              Chief Jason Ackerman, Magna Precinct, UPD, stated snow and parking issues
have been a problem. Dead ends and circles are congested and neglected when storms hit.
Residents and vehicle owners need to make room for snowplows and anticipate the weather. The
UPD has ramped up education, warnings, and actually towed cars. Information is on its Facebook
page. Then, on Super Bowl Sunday, there was an aggravated kidnapping in which a woman was
held against her will. Several patrol officers recognized early warning signs and were able to
apprehend the suspect and rescue the woman, although a couple officers were injured during the
