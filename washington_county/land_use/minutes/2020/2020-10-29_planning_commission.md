Washington County Land Use Authority Special Meeting
                              October 29, 2020

The specially scheduled meeting of the Washington County Planning Commission was conducted
on Tuesday, October 29, 2020 from the Commission Chambers in the Washington County
Administration Building, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Also, via electronic means
through Zoom. Chairman Kevin Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of
Allegiance.

Chairman Jones explained how the meeting would be conducted. He reminded the participants that
although this is a public meeting, it would only be open for public comments when so declared.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate acting as a regular voting
member), Shayne Durrant, Slade Hughes, Mark Owens, and Doug Solstad.

Excused: Reed Scow (Alternate) and Jon Walter.

County staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Steven Scott, Deputy
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner, Building Official.


1.   PUBLIC HEARING/SUBDIVISION PRELIMINARY AND FINAL PLAT. Review
and hold a public hearing for a minor 2-lot subdivision preliminary and final plat request for Parcel
8183-E-MW located at approximately 31 South 300 West in Veyo and located in the RA-1
(Residential Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot size) zone. The applicants are Pam and Jessie Hunt.

Chairman Jones gave a short synopsis of the project pointing out that it was reviewed and approved
in the October 13 meeting. The item was back for re-review and public hearing due to a clerical
error regarding the time of the meeting in the public notice that was mailed out to the surrounding
residents.

Commissioner Solstad was not at the last meeting and indicated he had some questions. He asked
if there was a reason to not extend 100 South. He commented on the access to the property at 150
West.

Commissioner Hughes explained why they were not expanding 100 South pointing out that it
would be a flag lot.

A brief discussion regarding clarification for the flag lot ensued.

Recommendation:
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission review and approve the preliminary and final
plat subject and send a recommendation to the County Commission to approve the requested
variation from Title 11 Subdivision Regulations based on the following findings and conditions:

   1. Literal enforcement of the ordinance would cause an unreasonable hardship for the
      applicant that is not necessary to carry out the general purpose of the land use ordinance.

MOTION:            Commissioner Hughes MOVED to approve the preliminary and final plat
                   request for a minor 2-lot subdivision with the flag lot for Parcel 8183-E-MW
                   located at approximately 31 South 300 West in Veyo and further recommended
                   that the county commission approve a variation for the road improvements.
                   The parcel is located in the RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot
                   size) zone. The applicants are Pam and Jesse Hunt, based on the following
                   findings:

                       1. Subject to the County Commission approving the requested variation
                          from Title 11 Subdivision Regulations.
                       2. The public hearing and zone change request was properly noticed.
                       3. The public hearing was held on October 29, 2020 in a specially scheduled
                          Planning Commission Meeting.
                       4. The proposal is consistent with the General Plan.
                       5. The proposed subdivision should not affect the surrounding area.
                       6. Literal enforcement of the ordinance would cause an unreasonable
                          hardship for the applicant that is not necessary to carry out the general
                          purpose of the lane use ordinance.
                       7. There are special circumstances attached to the applicant’s property that
                          does not generally apply to other properties in the same zone.
                       8. Granting the variation is essential to the enjoyment of a substantial
                          property right possessed by other properties in the same zone.

                   Commissioner Durrant SECONDED. Chairman Jones asked if there were any
                   additional comments. Commissioner Durrant clarified that the people who
                   called for this public meeting were not in attendance of the meeting. He was
                   informed they were not but that the noticing requirement had now been met.
                   Chairman Jones called for vote. The motion carried unanimously.

5.   MINUTES. Consider approval of the minutes of the regular planning commission meeting
held October 13, 2020.

MOTION:            Commissioner Owens MOVED to approve the meeting minutes of the planning
                   commission meeting held on October 13, 2020 as presented. Commissioner
                   Hughes SECONDED. Chairman Jones called for a vote. The motion carried
                   unanimously.


6. COUNTY COMMISSION ACTION REVIEW. Review of action taken by the County
Commission on planning items.

Mr. Messel reported that the county commission reviewed and approved the zone change request
the Troy Pearson project in Veyo as recommended by the planning commission. The variation for
the road improvements for both Mr. Pearson’s and the Hunt’s project, will be on the county
commission meeting agenda for November 17, 2020.


7.     COMMISSION & STAFF REPORTS. General reporting on various topics.

Mr. Messel stated that with the governor’s orders and recommendations, we are not approving any
events at this time. Staff will update the planning commission as necessary. A clarification and
discussion on the types of events the county would review transpired.


Chairman Jones thanked everyone who participated in the meeting then called for a motion
to adjourn. Commissioner Anderson MOVED to adjourn the meeting at 1:50 p.m.
Commissioner Durrant SECONDED. The motion carried unanimously and the meeting then
adjourned.


People had the opportunity speak during the public hearing phase of the meeting. County staff
monitored the methods when the public hearing phase was opened and closed after everyone
who so desired had the opportunity to make public comments. Comments were taken by several
methods that included by Zoom by using the action icon of clapping or thumbs-up or the chat
feature, by phone participation, and by in-person participation.



Doreen Bowers-Irons, Planning Secretary
