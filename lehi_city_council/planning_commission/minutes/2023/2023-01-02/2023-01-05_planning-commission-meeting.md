Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1) Call to Order
    Commission Vice Chairman Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2) Review of Planning Commission meeting schedule for 2023

Noreen Edwards reviewed the Planning Commission schedule for the 2023 year. She stated we
can always amend the schedule, but the schedule needed to be posted with a vote.

Heather Newall stated May 25th is graduation. Noreen will strike the May 25th. It was
recommended that the June 1st, along with December 7th, be a combined Work Session/Regular
Meeting.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve the 2023 Planning Commission schedule,
        with the modifications that the May 25, 2023 meeting be removed, and the June 1st and
        December 7th meeting be combined Work Session/Regular Meetings.


        Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Bunker,
        yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Carlson, yes;
        Commissioner Britton, yes.

3) Review and acceptance of the Planning Commission Bylaws

Kim Struthers gave a presentation on the Planning Commission Bylaws for 2023. The
Commissioners asked clarifying questions regarding the Bylaws.

4) Review of 2022 Planning Commission meeting lengths and number of items

Jacob Curtis gave an update of the 2022 Planning Commission meetings. 2022 was the lowest
year with development items in the meetings.

5) City Business

Kim Struthers, Mike West, Ross Dinsdale and the Commissioners discussed City Code and what
needed to be revised. If it isn't required, suggestions were made to remove the voting option so
there can be open, candid conversation. Having a concept process to present to the
Commissioners to see if the item/development is even something that can be approved. Might be
helpful to discuss if the Commissioners like the idea of the concept and talk about options that
need to be fixed.

6) Adjournment

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Eyre moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:05 p.m.
