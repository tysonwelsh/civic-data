The following are the minutes of the Planning Commission Meeting of the Herriman Planning Commission.
The meeting was held on Thursday, February 18, 2021 at 6:00 p.m. in the Fort Herriman Conference Room
and the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice
of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members
of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Chris Berbert

Commissioners Present In Person: Brody Rypien, Jackson Ferguson, and Heather Garcia

Commissioners Present Electronically: Andrea Bradford, Andy Powell, and Adam Jacobson

Commissioners Excused: Lorin Palmer and Joy Kaseke

Stromberg, Deputy City Recorder Wendy Thorpe, Communications Specialist Destiny Skinner, City Attorney

Development Director Blake Thomas

City Planner Michael Maloy explained this was a review of building elevations for compliance with commercial
design standards. Site plan, parking and landscaping were approved by the Planning Commission on September
3, 2020. The signage would not be reviewed tonight, it would be submitted later with a different request. The
applicant has met with the staff to meet compliance. Owner Steve Pruitt was present with material boards.

Owner Steve Pruitt approached the podium and stated exterior materials were significantly upgraded. He
presented building elevations and materials, including Pella stone from Ogden with an articulated pattern. It
would contrast with a blue EIFS stucco type material, plus black metal wall caps and canopies. The building
would have a flat roof.

Commissioner Garcia requested more diverse plants be placed along the parking lot. She did not think the same
Feather Reed Grass should be all the way around.

City Planner Maloy stated drought tolerant landscaping had been recommended and grants may be available for
the applicant.

Commissioner Rypien offered the opinion the windows needed more trim to add dimension. Owner Steve Pruitt
stated the intention was for the inner space to be visible from outside as the interior would be a very active
space. It was stated the exterior design met the City requirements. Commissioner Jackson Ferguson requested
window glazing materials. Owner Steve Pruitt stated he thought the windows would be anodized. Commissioner
Bradford asked if there would be a patio. Owner Steve Pruitt said there was limited space on the east side for
seasonal outdoor seating.

Commissioner Jackson Ferguson MOVED to approve item 3.3 file number C2020-013 Review of building elevations and
exterior materials for Garage Grill . Commissioner Adam Jacobson SECONDED the motion.
