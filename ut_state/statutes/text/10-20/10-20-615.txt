10-20-615. Specified public utility located in a municipal utility easement.

[History: Renumbered and Amended by Chapter 15, 2025 Special Session 1]

A specified public utility may exercise each power of a public utility under Section
(1) with the consent of a municipality; and
(2) that is located within a municipal utility easement described in Subsections 10-20-102(50)(a) through (e).
