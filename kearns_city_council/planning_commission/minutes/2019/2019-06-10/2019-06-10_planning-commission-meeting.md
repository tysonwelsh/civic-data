30939 – Ordinance Amendments - Revisions to the code enforcement process and the associated civil
penalties for code violations within the Kearns Metro Township, including amendments to Chapter 19.94
of the zoning ordinance and the enactment of a new Title (12) to the Code.

Nathan Bracken provided the state municipalities regarding code enforcement provisions and zoning
amendments. Mr. Woodward added amending the penalties, idea government pays it back and zero-sum
solution. Mr. Bracken said have been moving quickly before receiving comments. Commissioner Nelson
asked why it’s better to change warning period. Mr. Bracken advised would be at directors discretion. If
there is a conflict between Chapter 19.94 and Title 12, Title 12 would control. Commissioner Robertson
asked who the hearing officer is. Mr. Bracken said the county states would require being an attorney.
Commissioner Wellman had some corrections and questions. Commissioner Robertson asked if the appeal
authority is the same as a land use hearing officer.

Roger Snow said Kearns Community Council gave a favorable recommendation.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

No one from the public present to speak.

Mr. Bracken said he sent out the draft to title 12 and went over their changes made.

Commissioner Wellman motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Hatch seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend file #30939 to the Kearns Metro Township Council for approval, Chapter 19.94
changes and adoption of Title 12 with the changes discussed in the tracked version and brought forth.
      Motion by: Commissioner Wellman
      2nd by: Commissioner Nelson
      Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
