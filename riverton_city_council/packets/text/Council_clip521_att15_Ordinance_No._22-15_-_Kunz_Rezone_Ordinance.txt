                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 22-15

AN ORDINANCE REZONING APPROXIMATELY 3.25- ACRES LOCATED AT 11944
   SOUTH 3600 WEST FROM THE GENERAL PLAN DESIGNATION OF “LOW
 DENSITY RESIDENTIAL” TO “MEDIUM-HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL”, AND
    ZONING FROM R-3 TO RM-6, NATHAN AND LAURA KUNZ, APPLICANT

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

     WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said zoning
amendment; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City General Plan and Zoning Map for the 3.25 acres located at
11944 South 3600 West to Medium High Density in the General Plan and zoning from RR-22 to
RM-6.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is,
                           amended to reflect the following changes as shown in
                           Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.             This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 17th day of
