                 NOTICE OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS’ MEETING OF THE
                     CITY OF HOLLADAY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
                                THURSDAY, JUNE 4, 2026

                                 Holladay Council Chambers - 3330 S 1300 E


PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Holladay Redevelopment Agency will hold a
meeting on Thursday, June 4, 2026 as close to 6:00 pm as possible. A member of the Board may
participate by electronic means. The Council Chambers shall serve as the anchor location. NOTE: State
Law requires that the City Council members constitute the Board of the Redevelopment Agency.

All documents that are available to the RDA Board are also available on the City’s website or are linked in this
agenda. Interested parties are encouraged to watch the Live video stream of the meeting –
http://cityofholladay.com/government/elected-officials/meetings-and-agendas/

Persons desiring to make public comments or to make comments during any public hearing may provide such
comments as follows:
1.      In-person attendance: at Holladay City Hall
2.      Email your comments by 5:00 pm on the date of the meeting to scarlson@holladayut.gov



                                                          AGENDA

1.        Call to Order –Chairman Gray

2.        Pledge of Allegiance

3.        Public Hearing on Proposed 2026-2027 Tentative Budget

4.        Adjourn RDA Meeting
