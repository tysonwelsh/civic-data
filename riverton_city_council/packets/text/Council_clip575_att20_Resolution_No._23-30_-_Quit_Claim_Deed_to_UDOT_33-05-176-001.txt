                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO 23-30

     A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE TRANSFER OF APPROXIMATELY 0.151
     ACRES OF REAL PROPERTY LOCATED AT 3620 WEST 13800 SOUTH TO THE
             STATE OF UTAH, DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION

       WHEREAS, in Riverton City holds a parcel of real property identified by sidewell No.
33-05-176-001 and located at 3620 West 13800 South, Riverton Utah (the “Subject Property”);
and

        WHEREAS, due to an increase in traffic congestion and use of Bangerter Highway, the
State of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) has a need for additional space to
improve the interchange at 13400 South and Bangerter Highway; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT desires to expand Bangerter Highway, in part by encroaching upon
the Subject Property, and has offered to purchase a portion of said property from Riverton; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that conveyance of said
right of way to UDOT will not interfere with the ordinary flow of traffic on city streets, will not
interfere with the operation of City utility systems in the vicinity of the Subject Property, and
will further advance the health and safety of Riverton City by improving safe traffic circulation
in the vicinity of Bangerter Highway and 13400 South; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the value of property
offered by UTOT is supported by a certified appraisal, issued by a qualified appraiser, and
provides Riverton City with just compensation under Utah law;


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1.      The Riverton City Council authorizes and Mayor to transfer title to the Subject Property
        as described in the Quit Claim Deed instrument attached to this Resolution, to the State of
        Utah, Department of Transportation, upon verification by the City Attorney that deed
        instruments used to convey the Subject Property shall take the proper management of
        City stormwater into account, and following receipt of title to Parcel No. 33-06-300-016.

2.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
