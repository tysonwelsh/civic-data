REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY
FISCAL YEAR 2021 BUDGET
RESOLUTION NO. 126
A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE 
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY, UTAH, 
ADOPTING THE ANNUAL BUDGET REQUIRED FOR FISCAL YEAR 
2021; APPROVING AND PROVIDING FOR THE NECESSARY TAX 
REVENUES AND OTHER REVENUES TO FUND THE BUDGET; 
APPROVING AND PROVIDING FOR ADJUSTMENTS TO THE 2020 
BUDGET; AND RELATED MATTERS.
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT:
WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17C-1-601, 1953, as amended, requires the 
governing board of the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County 
(“Agency”) to adopt an annual budget of revenue and expenditures for the 
Agency before December 15 of each year; and
WHEREAS, the Agency staff, with the assistance of the County Mayor ’s 
Offices, has caused to be prepared proposed and final budgets for the 
Agency for fiscal year 2021 and for adjustments to the 2020 budget, which 
have been reviewed and discussed in public meetings, and which have met all 
the requirements imposed by Utah law; and
WHEREAS, the Agency held a public hearing on its proposed annual budget 
for fiscal year 2021 on December 1, 2020, at 4:00 p.m., and provided notice 
of the public hearing as required by law; and
WHEREAS, the adoption of this resolution has been duly noticed in 
accordance with the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, and Utah Code 
Ann. § 17C-1-601.5(4).

NOW, THEREFORE, the Governing Board of the Redevelopment Agency 
of Salt Lake County, Utah, resolves as follows:
1. The Agency hereby adopts an annual budget for fiscal year 2021, as more 
specifically described in Attachment A to this Resolution; and approves and 
adopts year-end adjustments to the 2020 budget as set forth in Attachment B.
2. The Agency further declares that this budget provides for payment of 
debt, bond debt service, if any, including transfers for appropriate fund 
balances, and that said debt service payments exclude payments from any 
proceeds of Tax Anticipation Notes or borrowing, consistent with Treasury 
Department regulations.
3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its approval and 
adoption by the Agency and will be filed and recorded in the official minutes 
and records of the Agency for this meeting.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 1st day of December, 2020.
