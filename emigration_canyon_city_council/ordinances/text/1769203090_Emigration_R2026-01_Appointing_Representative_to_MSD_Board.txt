WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon is a member entity of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (the "MSD"); and

WHEREAS, as a member municipality, Section 178-2a-1106(2)(a)(i) and I106(2Xb) of the Utah
Code requires the Emigration Canyon City Council (the "Council") to appoint a representative to
the MSD Board of Trustees; and

WHEREAS, former Mayor Joe Smolka, whose term ended on January 5, 2026, previously
represented Emigration canyon on the MSD Board of Trustees; and

WHEREAS, the Council desires to appoint a new representative to the MSD Board of Trustees.
