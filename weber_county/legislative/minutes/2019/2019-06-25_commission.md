G. ACTION ITEMS:
      1. INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH WEBER-MORGAN HEALTH DEPARTMENT AND WEBER HUMAN
         SERVICES (WHS) FOR THE PROSPERITY CENTER OF EXCELLENCE – RESOLUTION 39-2019.
            Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, read the agreement’s recitals that included that the parties have
            identified a need to address social, public and behavioral health problems by combining interdisciplinary
            expertise and forming cross-sector coalitions that will engage community stakeholders to identify root causes
            to those problems, remove systematic barriers, and work toward a common goal of collective well-being for all
            county residents. This new Center will be operated as a division of the Health Department and its Director,
            Melissa Freigang, will be a Health Department employee. An administrative board will be formed to govern
            this Center consisting of one Weber County commissioner and the directors for WHS and the Health
            Department. The contract term is five years and can be extended for 50 years. The commissioners are excited
            for this new direction and vision and are looking forward to having major positive impacts. Bryan Bennion,
            Health Department Executive Director, and Kevin Eastman, WHS Director, are also excited to be working on
            these issues that impact our community in trying to prevent the risk factors. Miss Freigang has been integrating
            with the Health Department and the WHS for the past month. She gave a brief update on the intergenerational
            poverty initiative (IGP), including that they have brought in two IGP grants, and that 16 households, consisting
            of 25 families, are receiving services. They average about 42 agencies around each family and are having great
            positive impacts.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 39-2019 approving the Interlocal Agreement with Weber-
            Morgan Health Department and Weber Human Services for the Prosperity Center of Excellence; Commissioner
            Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      2. RESOLUTION PROVIDING NOTICE OF ITS INTENT TO APPOINT A MEMBER TO THE WEBER HUMAN
         SERVICES GOVERNING BOARD – RESOLUTION 40-2019.
            Kevin Eastman, Weber Human Services Director, noted that apparently the county did not have the names for
            appointment last week. The governing board consists of the three Weber County commissioners, there is an
            interlocal agreement to provide services to Morgan County, which has two appointed representatives, and also
            three community at large positions. The latter is to fill this vacancy. Mr. Eastman recommended appointing
            Clint Thurgood. Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, noted that no less than two names must be
            presented to this Commission for consideration and the other two names were Kim Tyler and Timothy Yanni.
            Because the three names were recommended by the Advisory Council, the commissioners moved forward.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to adopt Resolution 40-2019 providing notice of this Commission’s intent to
            appoint Clint Thurgood to the Weber Human Services Governing Board as recommended by the Advisory
            Council (beginning 7/16/2019-12/31/2024); Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
