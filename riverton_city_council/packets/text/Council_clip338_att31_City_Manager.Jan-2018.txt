                                        CITY MANAGER

OPENING DATE: 12/29/2017
CLOSING DATE: 01/23/2018
SALARY: $122,279.00 - $191,257.00 Annually
GRADE: 20


GENERAL DESCRIPTION:

Riverton City is seeking an executive level candidate for a City Manager. This appointed position is under
the direction of the governing body of the City, performs professional managerial duties; directs and
coordinates administration of government operations in accordance with policies determined by the
governing body. Appoints department heads and staff as provided by state laws or local ordinances.
Supervises activities, such as collection and disbursement of taxes, law enforcement, maintenance of
public health, construction of public works and purchases of supplies and equipment. Oversees and may
direct community and economic development programs. Assists with preparation of annual budget and
submits estimates to authorized elected officials for approval.



EXAMPLES OF DUTIES:
(These examples are not all inclusive)
    • In coordination with Finance Director, prepares an annual budget using criteria of strategic plan to
       present to Mayor to start budgeting process.
    • Exercise general control and supervision of administrative activities of the City.
    • Prepare plans and programs concerning development, operation, and needs of the City.
    • Set performance standards and controls to ensure efficient and effective operations.
    • Provide general oversight of all financial decisions of the City.
    • In coordination with Finance Director, responsible for financial statements.
    • Oversee hiring and discharging of all employees, except those appointed by statute.
    • Review all proposed contracts and make recommendations to the Governing Body.
    • Ensure appropriate procurement practices are established and followed.
    • Exercise all powers and duties assigned by ordinance or resolution.
    • Develop, maintain, and manage a team of professionals to achieve goals and objectives as
       outlined in the strategic plan.

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & ABILITIES:
