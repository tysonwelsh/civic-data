City Attorney Harrington stated that the Open and Public Meetings Act is a State law

City Attorney Harrington stated that the main person of the Act is to make sure

ex parte rules that make the Commissioners pay attention to whether they are acting in
a legislative or administrative capacity. Mr. Harrington remarked that for legislative




                                                   3.
