will be needed in the future.

9. Consideration of Ordinance #56-2022, a Development Code Amendment to Table 05-030
   C, adding Portable Storage Containers as a permitted use in the Public Facility zone.
   Mr. Struthers stated that this is being done to accommodate for the temporary storage of the
   secondary water meters that are required to be installed. The Council discussed concerns that
   the storage containers may be used on other properties and that there was no time limit so they
   could become permanent.

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to approve Ordinance #56-2022, a Development Code
                Amendment to Table 05-030 C, adding Portable Storage Containers as a permitted
                use in the Public Facility zone; and modify the Ordinance to state that it is a
                Conditional Use, and that there is a two-year time limit with the option of an
                extension; and including any Development Review Committee and Planning
                Commission comments. Councilor Koivisto seconded the motion.
