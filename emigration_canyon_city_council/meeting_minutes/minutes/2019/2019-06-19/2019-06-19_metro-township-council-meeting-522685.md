Willy Stokman, Sustainability Alliance, stated Utah Geological did a preliminary
study proposal to develop hydrogeological maps. Part of the study will include stream flow, water
diversions and climate change. Data collection will help when making sustainable water
management decisions. The study is preliminary, so everyone has the option to provide input.
The Sustainability Alliance will pay for half of the study, Salt Lake Public Utilities said they are
interested in participating and would like the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council and the
Emigration Improvement District (EID) to help fund the study.
Mayor Smolka stated the Council is interested in water quality; however, it is not
interested in creating another district. The EID needs to be involved.
Council Member Hawkes stated the EID already has a process in place and they
have jurisdiction over septic. It does not make sense to start another group. The Council needs
to be on the same page and there is not authority to conduct these studies.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Captain Michael Conn stated UFA’s new website will go live on July 1, 2019. UFA
is trying to improve its data collection to get a better idea of the type of calls it is receiving.
Fireworks are banned in Emigration Canyon and there are signs for a potential algae bloom. UFA
has approved its budget for next year with a 4.5% salary increase, making wages for competitive.
Part-time positions are being phased out to allow the hiring of full-time employees to maintain
more consistency at each station.

Council Business
Stormwater Maintenance Agreement/Fee
Mayor Smolka stated the Council needs to approve the Stormwater Maintenance
Agreement form for Salt Lake County Public Works Engineering Division to use on behalf of
Emigration Canyon Metro Township.
David Church stated the Stormwater Maintenance Agreement form will be for new
developments. Regulations require that land owners or developers retain onsite stormwater; the
form is required to be signed indicating that they will maintain those stormwater facilities. Salt
Lake County wanted a uniformed agreement form across the metro townships.

Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commission
Council Member Brems stated the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning
Commission has always been a seven-member commission. It has been difficult to find willing
and qualified candidates. He believes a five-member commission with two alternates will work.
David Church stated he suggests a five-member commission because the size of
the community is small and there are several different boards that use up all the public volunteers.
Council Member Brems, seconded by Council Member Hawkes, moved to change
the number of Emigration Canyon Metro Township Planning Commissioners from seven to five
with two alternates. The motion passed unanimously. Council Member Bowen was absent from
the vote.

Council Member Reports
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)
Council Member Brems stated part of the reason Herriman and Riverton Cities
left was because of the lack of transparency. The new accountant for UPD has made the
budgeting process very transparent. Adoption of the budget will occur on June 20, 2019. Included
in the budget is a recommendation to increase salaries with the goal to be one of the top three
agencies for salaries. UPD finances are being looked at closely. Body cameras are being used
by 25 percent of officers. To supply cameras for all the officers will cost $500,000 a year. Weighted
voting is still being considered. Right now, every member has a full vote and Salt Lake County
continues to support all members having a full vote. SLVLESA is looking at a fee increase to be
able to continue its level of service. The cost to Emigration Canyon Metro Township is $293,000
if the four percent salary increase is adopted.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)
Council Member Paine stated National Garbage Person day is June 20, 2019.
WFWRD is working on employee satisfaction. Discussions with Salt Lake County regarding the
transfer station are ongoing. Taking trash to other areas instead of the one transfer station, would
be helpful and save money. China has changed what type of recycling it will accept, and this has
created recycling challenges around the nation. Recycling may cost a little bit more; however, the
board is working to keep the costs as low as possible.
