                                         PUBLIC COMMENTS
(Track A, 14:50)
Provo resident Robert Stevens reminded the Board the Lord has said, ‘Thou shalt not steal.’ Taxation is theft and an
indiscretion that needs to be addressed directly. Mr. Stevens expressed concern over the potential to lose property
due to mail issues, such as misplaced mail or a resident being ill for an extended period of time. He suggested the
county offer an email or text notification option for property tax notices.

There were no additional public comments and the meeting recessed at 9:30 A.M. to go into closed meetings. The
public meeting reconvened at 10:56 A.M.
