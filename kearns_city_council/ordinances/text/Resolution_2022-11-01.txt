                          KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

       RESOLUTION NO. 2022-11-01                                    DATE: November 14, 2022

        A RESOLUTION PROPOSING AMENDMENTS TO THE KEARNS METRO
                         TOWNSHIP’S 2022 BUDGET


       WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Kearns is considering amending its 2022 budget as set forth in the attached
and desires to hold a public hearing to receive public input on the same.

         NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED effective immediately:

        1.     The Kearns Council will hold a public hearing on November 28, 2022, at 6:00 pm
at the Kearns Library located at 4275 W. 5345 S. to receive public comment on the attached
proposed amendments to the 2022 Kearns budget.

         2.    Notice of the public hearing shall be provided pursuant to Utah Code Ann § 10-6-
113.

       3.      A copy of the proposed amendments to the 2022 Kearns budget shall be made
available for public inspection for a period of at least ten (10) days prior to the public hearing,
pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-112.

         4.    This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
