Re:     Hardman Way 3600 W Traffic Signal Warrant Analysis



The intersection of Hardman Way @ 3600 W has met Signal Warrant 2, the four-hour traffic volume
warrant per the standard in the Utah Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices.

We can use the 70% factor chart because the 85th percentile speed is above 40 mph at 43.5 mph. The
red dot on the graph below represents of the traffic volumes on the approaches to the intersection, the
sum of the major street approaches on the x axis and then the volume from the busier minor approach
on the y axis. The warrant is met because the dot falls above the 2 or more lanes (3600 W) and 1 lane
(Hardman Way) line.
