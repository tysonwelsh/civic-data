 2. Public Hearings
       a. Public Hearing to Receive Comment on the West Jordan Redevelopment Agency Fiscal Year
          2027 Budget
          Budget Analyst Becky Condie presented the proposed Fiscal Year 2027 Redevelopment
          Agency (RDA) Budget. She explained that the RDA supports revitalization and redevelopment
          efforts in designated project areas through property tax revenues, General Fund transfers,
          incentive agreements, and strategic land acquisitions.

          Ms. Condie reviewed the proposed revenues and expenditures, including funding for a
          redevelopment study, administrative costs, incentive agreements, and land acquisition
          opportunities. She noted the estimated FY 2026 ending fund balance of approximately $21
          million, primarily due to funds reserved for future property acquisitions and improvements
          that had not yet occurred.

          Ms. Condie also reviewed the status of the RDA project areas, noting that only three areas
          remain active and that two are scheduled to close following Tax Year 2026. She stated that
          the tentative budget had been adopted previously and that the public hearing provided an
          opportunity for public comment before final budget adoption.

          Council Member Jacob asked about the significant differences between budgeted and
          projected ending reserves in the Town Center area. Ms. Condie explained that funds
          budgeted for land purchases had not been spent because acquisition opportunities had not
          yet materialized, resulting in higher-than-anticipated reserves. She confirmed that the FY
