REQUEST FOR A PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request to amend the Atkinville Interchange Area
     Commercial PD-C for a 120-room hotel, a 23,000 sq ft medical office and an
     8,431 sq ft retail building. The applicant is TJN Development, and the
     representative is Tucker Nipko. The project will be known as Mojave
     Crossing Case No. 2024 – PDA-009 - PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: In December of 2021, a planned
      development amendment for the SunRiver Patio Furniture project was approved, that
      project failed to progress, and the property went back on the market. This proposal
      is to amend the Atkinville Interchange Area Commercial PD (Planned Development)
      for the purpose of adding a new 120-room hotel, a 23,000 sq ft medical office and an
      8,431 square foot retail building on approximately 5.06 acres. These uses are
      approved uses in the Atkinville Interchange Area Zone Plan. Case No. 2024-PDA-
      009 (Staff – Carol Winner).

REQUEST FOR A ZONING REGLUATION AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request to amend Title 10-2, definitions; 10-7B, Single
     Family Residential Zone, 10-7C Multiple Family Residential Zone, 10-7E,
     Residential Central City Zone; and 10-17A, Specific Standards; of the city code
     to add provisions for Live/Work Units to be allowed in the R-1-8, R-2, R-3, R-
     4, and RCC Zone with specific standards. The applicant is Lynn Potter. Case
     No. 2024-ZRA-002 – PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: City staff and the applicant have worked
      closely together on this code amendment. The applicant would like to propose adding
      Live/Work Units as a permitted use in the R-1-8, R-2, R-3, R-4, and RCC Zone. On
      June 25, 2024, The Planning Commission held a public hearing for this item and
      recommended approval with a 6-0 vote to allow Live/Work units in the RCC district
      with conditions. Since this time, staff has been working on making a few updates to
      this proposed amendment to increase the allowed locations for a live/work unit in the
      downtown area. Case No. 2024-ZRA-002. (Staff – Carol Winner).
