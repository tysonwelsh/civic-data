D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: I experienced a little bit of history at the Delta Center recently.
   It was a professional hockey game between the Utah Hockey Club and Boston Bruins. The Bruins started in
   1924 and have won the Stanley Cup. The Utah team, who are in their first season, won in OT. I have quoted
   Wayne Gretzky at times with high school teams I coached. He said the following:
                        “A great hockey player plays where the puck is going to be.”
                        “When you win, say nothing. When you lose, say less.”
                        “I skate where the puck is going to be, not where it has been.”
                        “You miss 100 % of the shots you don’t take.”
