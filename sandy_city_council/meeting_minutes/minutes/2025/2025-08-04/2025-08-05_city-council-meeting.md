Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions

                       Council Vice Chair Ryan Mecham welcomed those in attendance.

                       Council Member Marci Houseman offered the Prayer.
                       Council Member Alison Stroud led the Pledge.

                       Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.

General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                       Council Vice Chair Ryan Mecham invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                       Comment.

                       Mr. Doug Wismer expressed support for the gondola in Little Cottonwood Canyon (LCC).
                       The gondola is the only long term solution that will address the traffic in LCC. The
                       gondola provides year round access to the canyon and has a smaller footprint. Many
                       cities around the world use gondolas. Please stop opposing this project. Build something
                       bold and lasting for our city.

                       Mr. Will Shiflett expressed a public safety concern. He requested police patrols in his
                       neighborhood to address the speeding issues in the area between Midvale and Sandy
                       along 150 East. Please help us to resolve this public safety issue.

                       Mr. Randy Nielsen requested help from the city with an infestation of rabbits in his
                       neighborhood.

                       Mr. Mark Mason Taylor spoke about the impact on the community resulting from climate
                       change and wanted to discuss ways to offset this impact.

                       Ms. Shannon Ritzman spoke about traffic and public safety concerns in the area along
                       150 East by the Sandy-Midvale border. We need help from the City.

                       Mr. Dave Egelund thanked Sandy City for the Night Out Against Crime event. He
                       appreciated the great job the coordinator, Amy Bryant and the Police did.

                       Public comment closed.

                       Following public comment, Council moved to adjourn the meeting.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson thought the Night Out Against Crime event was a great
                    event and he thanked the Police Department for organizing the event.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman spoke about a resident's interest in adding a booster
                    tower in his neighborhood in the Willow Creek area and she requested a staff
                    presentation on the process to do this.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham provided an update from the Sandy Club and spoke about
                    their funding needs. He thanked the Police Department for organizing the Night Out
                    Against Crime event. It was a great event and appreciated by the community.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud attended the garden ceremony at the MVP shelter. She
                    also attended the recent ribbon cutting for the Quad. The Sandy Museum Car Show was
                    a great event in Historic Sandy. She enjoyed participating in the Canyons School District
                    New Teacher Welcome event and the Night Out Against Crime. Both events were very
                    well attended and she thanked all who organized the events.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer thought the Night Out Against Crime was a great event.
                    He will reach out to the Police Department and provide some feedback on the format of
                    the event.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided some additional information about the cell tower
                    issues in the Willow Creek area.

Mayor's Report

                    KIm Bell, Deputy Mayor, invited the community to attend the Balloon Festival at Storm
                    Mountain Park. All are welcome.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, provided a staff update.

                    Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:13 pm
