Proposed Ordinance 2023-9 reclassifying property located at 1123-1127
Wail Avenue from Community Commercial Zone (C-2) to Regional
Commercial Zone (C-3) to allow tire sales

A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding a zone change application came before
the Council for consideration. The memo stated Cassandra and Victor Galindo have submitted a petition to rezone property at
1123-1127 Wall Avenue from C-2 to C-3, which would allow a tire sales and service use; the Galindo’s own and operate various
Victor’s Tires locations in the region.

Deputy Planning Manager Simpson summarized the memo and stated the Galindo’s had purchased this property with the
hopes of developing a tire store, which would serve as a second business location in Ogden City. After applying for a business
license, they discovered that the property is located within a C-2 zone which only allows tire stores if they are located on a ten-
acre or larger shopping center property. Since the property is only 0.66-acres, the petitioners are requesting a rezoning to C-3
which does not have a minimum lot area requirement for tire stores. The property is located within the Lynn Community and
adjacent to the 12" Street Corridor. The adopted plans for these areas do not provide any specific zoning direction for this
property but do support C-3 zoning to the immediate north and south. The properties to the south are already zoned C-3 which
would provide continuity with the zone boundaries if the rezone were to be approved. He indicated the Planning Commission
considered the proposal and on a six to three vote, forwarded a recommendation of approval to the City Council. The motion was

based on the findings that the request:

e Will provide a definite edge to development and buffering between types of uses.

e Will reflect the prevailing character of the overall district.

e Will not cut across lots or developments; ( )
e And is supported in the Lynn Community Plan.

stated that he is opposed to the rezone and that a tire shop is not an appropriate use for the property. Mr. Merrill stated that the
business owner should have checked the zoning on the property before purchasing it like he had to when buying the neighboring

purposes. This led to a review of the aerial image for the property and surrounding properties in order to be oriented to the current
road layout in the trailer park.

Public Comments

Heath Satow referenced the agenda for tonight’s Redevelopment Agency (RDA) meeting; one of the topics to be
discussed is the creation of the Gibson Community Reinvestment Area (CRA) and he thought it would be a good idea to revisit
the topic selecting developers for RDA projects, which was discussed in a work session in July of 2021. The description of that
agenda item was that the Council has requested public discussion regarding the developer selection process and may request
changes to ensure the process is fair, open, and transparent. He cited a Standard-Examiner article regarding the Council’s
discussion of the issue, which indicated there has been a growing feeling that the selection process is unfair; specifically, Council \
member Lopez was quoted as saying there is a perception of favoritism. It was also noted that former Community and Economic
Development Director Christopulos indicated the Council often uses the term ‘transparency’ when discussing the developer
selection process, but that is a term that is vague and undefinable. Mr. Satow then noted there was no additional discussion of the
issue until July 2022 and Council staff noted at that time that the biggest complaint they hear is that it is very hard to do business
with the City regarding these types of projects. There was more talk of changes to policy, but he has not seen an agenda item
relating to that matter. He referenced Council member Hyer’s comments about property taxation and noted that given that tax
increment financing is used to fund RDA projects, the City does lose a great deal of property tax revenue to the County.

Steve Van Wagoner stated he recently visited Mesquite, Nevada for a soccer tournament; the soccer complex there is
beautiful, and it has also served as an economic driver for that community, resulting in a great deal of commercial development
surrounding the soccer complex. He stated that he understands that the City Council cannot force the purchase of the Grandview
