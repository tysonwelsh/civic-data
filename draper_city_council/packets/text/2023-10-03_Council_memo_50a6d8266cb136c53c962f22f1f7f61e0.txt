MEMO
To: City Council
From: Rhett Ogden, Parks and Recreation Director
Date: 2023-10-03
Re: Action Item: Ordinance #1588


Comments:

Staff recommends approving Ordinance #1588 to adopt the Draper City 2023 Parks, Trails and
Recreation Master Plan. For the past 3 years (with a year break during the Covid-19 pandemic) staff
has been working with our consultant as well as residents of Draper City to study, analyze and plan
to effectively craft a Master Plan to be used as a guiding document for Parks, Trails and Recreation.
Various focus groups were formed, and residents were listened to in order to understand needs,
issues and future developments. Residents had opportunity to attend Public Open houses to make
comment on current conditions and express their thoughts of what they feel would be good for
Draper City regarding parks, trails, recreation and community events.
