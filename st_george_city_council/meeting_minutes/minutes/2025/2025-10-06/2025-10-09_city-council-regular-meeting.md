PASSENGER FACILITY CHARGE APPLICATION/RESOLUTION:
     Consider approval of Resolution No. 2025-022R authorizing the Airport
     Director to submit to the FAA, a new Passenger Facility Charge (PFC)
     Application (Application #9) and an amendment to the current application
     (Application #8).

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: A PFC is authorized by Federal Law, and this
      user fee is imposed only on eligible enplaned passengers utilizing airports. The St
      George Regional Airport implemented its PFC Program in 1998 to provide funding for
      approved capital projects. The current application will be fully collected by January
      1, 2026, and the submission of a new application is necessary for the continuation of
      a PFC charge in the amount of $4.50 to accomplish capital projects designed to
      preserve and enhance capacity, safety, and security of the airport. Additionally, the
      previously approved PFC Approved Application #8 requires an amendment to
      reconcile the final costs, funding sources and PFC authority of the projects included
      in the application. A public notice was posted to the City of St George website on
      8/7/25 for public comment. The air carriers were notified via certified mail on
      8/7/25 for comment. No comments were received.
