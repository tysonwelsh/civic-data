Mayor Smolka read the following statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-202:
Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the
electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those
who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under an “Yellow” alert
stage for the COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does
not have sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing
to keep people safe from infection.

PUBLIC HEARING
THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to consider the 2021 Magna
Metro Township Budget
Council Member Harris, seconded by Deputy Mayor Brems, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.
No public comment.
Council Member Harris, seconded by Council Member Hawkes, moved to close
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.
Council Member Bowen, seconded by Deputy Mayor Harris, moved to approve the
budget adjustment and the following Resolution:
RESOLUTION NO. 20-12-01

Date: December 17, 2020

A RESOLUTION OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL OPENING AND AMENDING THE ANNUAL BUDGET FOR THE YEAR
2020 FOR THE OPERATION OF THE TOWNSHIP
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council has previously adopted a
budget for the 2020 budget year; and
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township needs to open and amend the
adopted budget because of changes that have occurred during the year; and
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council has held a public hearing on
the proposed amended 2020 amended annual budget for the Township;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE EMIGRATION
CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP:
1. That the attached exhibit A is hereby adopted as the 2020 annual budget for the operation
of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township for the 2020 calendar year.
2. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.

Community Input
Citizen Public Comment
Willy Stokman stated grants have been applied for to get better internet access.
The Sustainability Alliance and a neighborhood in Sky Crest, has been trying to purchase 87
acres of land but it has been hard to get everyone around the table with the owners. The land is
going to be difficult or impossible to develop on and the real estate agent does not have to
disclose. Signs have been placed around the property, but they have been damaged. Utah Open
Lands has a good offer, but the seller wants money, and someone will end up buying and won’t
be able to develop. Is there anything the Council can do?
David Church stated real estate agents have some disclosure requirements.
There is nothing by ordinance that can be done to require disclosure from the real estate agent.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Captain Michael Conn stated the strategic plan was completed; that will help
determine where the organization goes in the next three years and it helps drive the budget. There
are more COVID-19 cases within UFA; however, it can keep all stations up and running. CDC
recommendations are being followed and there is in-house rapid testing. The goal with Versaterm,
the new dispatch system, was to get the closest rig to the location in need quickly. Everyone
seems to have adjusted to it very well.
