                        UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY
              NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING FOR FEE INCREASE

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that pursuant to Utah Code Section 11-13-531, the
Board of Directors of the Unified Fire Authority will hold a public
hearing on a proposed increase in fees.
The UFA is proposing an increase to multiple fees for services as
detailed in the fee schedule document available at
https://unifiedfireut.gov/budget-and-financial-audits/.
All concerned citizens are invited to the Public Hearing on:
                           Date: June 16, 2026
                             Time: 6:00 P.M.

This meeting will be electronic, with a potential in-person opportunity
at the Unified Fire Authority Emergency Communications Center located at
3380 South 900 West, Salt Lake City, Utah 84119.

If you are interested in participating electronically during the
Public Hearing portion of the meeting, The Board of Directors
shall assemble in person and electronically for the meeting.
Information about how to access the electronic meeting will be
provided on the agenda which will be posted on the Utah Public
Notice Website at least 24 hours in advance of the meeting.

All persons interested and present will be given an opportunity to
be heard in this matter.

In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the
Unified Fire Authority will make reasonable accommodations to
participate in the hearing. Requests for assistance can be made
by calling 801-743-7213 at least 24 hours in advance of the
hearing to be attended.

      DATED this 26th day of MAY, 2026.

      PUBLISHED BY ORDER OF THE UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY
