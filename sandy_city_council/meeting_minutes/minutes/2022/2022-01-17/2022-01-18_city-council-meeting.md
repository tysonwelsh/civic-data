City Council Meeting Minutes January 18, 2022

1. 21-514 City council interviewing and selecting an appointee to fill a midterm
vacancy in council district four

Attachments: Memorandum District 4 Vacancy Interview and Selection Process.pdf

The City Council interviewed applicants for the District 4 City Council Member position to
fill the vacancy. Each applicant was allotted 3 minutes to speak to the Council. The
following candidates interviewed for the District 4 Council seat:
Mr. Scott Earl
Ms. Kristen Wray
Mr. Aaron Dekeyzer
Ms. Jessica Davies
Mr. Robin Saville
Mr. Chase Schaeffer
Mr. Burke Staker
Mr. Steve Van Maren
Mr. Patrick Casaday
Ms. Debra Hafner
Ms. Terri Tapp Hrechkosy
Council thanked all the candidates for their interest in serving on the City Council as the
District 4 Council Member. The applicants responded to Council Member questions. The
Council asked questions regarding the reason the applicants wanted to serve, the budget,
the priorities of the residents of District 4, their approach to making difficult decisions,
and the biggest challenges facing Sandy City.
Following the questions, the Council deliberated on their decision and selection of an
applicant to fill the District 4 vacancy.
The Council Clerk, Christine Edwards, took the first round of votes from the City Council
Members. The verbal vote was taken.
Ms. Stroud voted for Scott Earl.
Mr. Mecham voted for Pat Casaday.
Mr. Robinson voted for Scott Earl.
Ms. D'Sousa voted for Scott Earl.
Ms. Houseman voted for Scott Earl.
Ms. Sharkey voted for Scott Earl.
The Council Clerk stated the vote count: 5 votes for Scott Earl. 1 vote for Pat Casaday.
The vote count was also recorded and confirmed by Dustin Fratto, Interim Council
Director, and Tracy Cowdell, Council Attorney.
The Council moved to Item 2 on the agenda and made a motion to appoint Scott Earl to
fill the District 4 Council seat vacancy.

City Council Meeting Minutes January 18, 2022

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Interim Council Director, updated the Council on upcoming agenda items.
He acknowledged Scott Earl and is looking forward to working with our newest Council
Member.

Council Member Business
Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged Graham Tinius for his work in Kentucky
as a First Responder. He represented the city and department well and Mr. Robinson
thanked him for his service and wanted to publicly recognize him.
Council Member Marci Houseman spoke about Dr. Martin Luther King Jr - she has visited
the memorial in Washington DC. She welcomed Scott Earl to the Council and thanked all
candidates for their willingness to serve. She read a quote from Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Monica Zoltanski thinks it is a wonderful night for District 4 residents. She thanked
all who applied for the vacancy. She is very proud to be a resident of District 4 and knows
they will be in good hands with Mr. Earl representing them. We are a stronger community
because of your efforts. She is proud to serve as the Sandy City Mayor. She
congratulated the Beehive Academy - their new campus in Sandy recently opened. She
encouraged residents to participate in the Canyons Transportation survey which is open
until January 21st and the link can be found on the City website.

CAO Report
Kim Bell, Interim CAO, congratulated Scott Earl on his appointment to the District 4
Council seat and she spoke about Mr. Earl's service and commitment to Sandy City.
Brian Kelley, Finance Director, spoke about the refinancing of the Rio Tinto Stadium
bond, the city's Triple A rating and the net present value of the savings to the City over
the next six years. Mayor Zoltanski commented on the great work and efforts by the
finance department on behalf of the city.
