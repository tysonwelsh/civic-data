       b. Ordinance No. 26-20 Amending West Jordan City Code Section 13-5E-3, Permitted
          and Conditional Uses for SC-3 Zones
            City Planner Larry Gardner said the proposed ordinance would amend the Use Table for the
            SC-3 Zone to change eight uses from conditional to permitted: Daycare, General, Hotel or
            Motel; Motor Vehicle Sales and Service; Recreation and Entertainment, Indoor; Recreation
            and Entertainment, Outdoor; Restaurant Fast Food, General; and Restaurant Fast Food,
            Limited. Mr. Gardner said staff believed any necessary mitigation would be possible with the
            site plan and business license processes. He commented that all schools in the State of Utah
            were required to be permitted uses in any zone they were in, which was the reason for
            including School, K-12 use in the amendment. Mr. Gardner noted that the SC-3 Zone
            encompassed a large portion of Jordan Landing, and about 30 acres off of Mountain View
            Corridor between 9000 S and 8600 S.

            Council Member Whitelock asked why preschool and adult daycare uses had not been
            included. Mr. Gardner said staff felt it was generally better to establish conditions for pick up
            and drop off processes.

 7. Business Items
       a. Joint Resolution No. J26-001 with the City Council and Mayor of West Jordan
          Declaring a Stage 2 Severe Drought Condition
            Public Utilities Director Greg Davenport said the City Council received a report regarding
            severe drought conditions during the Committee of the Whole meeting earlier in the evening.
            Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District (JVWCD) had adopted a Stage 2 drought condition.
            He encouraged the Council to adopt a State 2 drought condition to ask residents to reduce
            water use by 10%. The 10% reduction would be mandatory for City properties, schools, and
            golf courses.

            Council Member Jacob said the JVWCD website included conservation information, and a
            Conservation Garden Park was located at 8275 South 1300 West with water saving ideas and
            tips. Mr. Davenport said the City would also provide resources for residents.

            MOTION:      Vice Chair Wignall moved to APPROVE Joint Resolution No. J26-001 with the
                         City Council and Mayor of West Jordan Declaring a Stage 2 Severe Drought
