 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING MAP
       AMENDMENT FROM SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL( R-1- 10) TO
 PROFESSIONAL OFFICE( PO) FOR 0.66 ACRES OF PROPERTY LOCATED AT 1633
                                            W. 6235 S.


        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council( the" Council")       met in regular session on May
19, 2021, to consider, among other things, approving a Zoning Map amendment from Single-
Family Residential( R- 1- 10) to Professional Office( PO) for 0.66 acres of property located at 1633
W. 6235 S.; and


        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 030, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published in the Deseret News on April
16, 2021; and


        WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on April 15, 2021; and


        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on April 27, 2021, during which the Planning Commission voted 6- 0 to
recommend that the Council approve the proposed zoning map amendment; and

        WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on May 19,
2021; and


        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed Zoning Map amendment is hereby approved for the property described below and
illustrated in Exhibit A.

            Summary:
            Jay Bollwinkel, owner's agent, is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to rezone the
            property at 1633 W. 6235 S. from Single-Family Residential ( R- 1- 10) to Professional Office
             P0). The applicant is seeking this Zone Map amendment with the intention of creating a
            new access for the properties at 6235 and 6243 S. Redwood Road and to allow for the
            expansion of the parking area for the existing office building at 6243 S. Redwood Road. A
            proposed concept development plan is included as Exhibit E illustrating the applicant's
            intent if the Zoning Map request is approved by the City Council. If the proposed Zoning
            Map amendment is approved by the City Council, the applicant will be required to complete
            a subdivision amendment or lot-line adjustment prior to any development or construction
            to relocate property boundaries and record necessary easements for access. They will also
            be required to complete the site plan review process prior to any construction to ensure
            compliance with all city development standards.

            Site Description
            The subject property is 28,750 square feet( 0.66 acres) and is currently a detached single-
            family residence in the R- 1- 10 zoning district. Exhibit F provides photos of the subject site.
            Surrounding zoning designations are as follows:

             North                   South                  East                 West
             Professional Office     Single- Family         Single- Family       Industrial( ID)
                                      R- 1- 10)              R- 1- 10)           Professional Office( P0)


        General Plan Analysis
        The General Plan designation for this property is" Professional Office." The General Plan
        includes the following statement describing the intent of the Professional Office designation
         pg 3- 4):

                    Professional Office ( P0) is a commercial classification focusing primarily on
                   professional office uses such as legal, financial, insurance, real estate,
                   marketing, medical and other healthcare- relatedfields."

        The applicant's proposal to rezone the subject property to Professional Office( P0) is
        consistent with the adopted policies and goals in the City's General Plan.

        Zoning Map Analysis
        The City Council may consider a proposed Zoning Map amendment and may adopt or reject
        the amendment as it deems appropriate( Section 13. 05. 030( B)( 2)).
                                                                        Additionally, the City
        Council may amend the Zoning Map consistent with the purposes set forth in the
        Taylorsville Land Development Code, the General Plan, and the Land Use Development and
        Management Act( Section 13. 05. 010).


        Staff recommends approval of the zoning amendment request as it is consistent with the
        City's General Plan, as described earlier in this report.

        Public Comment

            on April 16, 2021. Additionally, a notice was published in the Deseret News and on the State

            opposition of the request.



            Findings
                1.   This application was initiated by Jay Bollwinkel.
                2.   The applicant is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to rezone the property at
                     1633 W. 6235 S. ( 21- 22- 202- 065- 0000) from Residential ( R- 1- 10) to Professional
                     Office ( P0).
                3.   The subject property is within the" Professional Office" designation in the City's
                     General Plan Proposed Land Use Map.
                4.   The proposed concept development plan includes a new access for the properties at
                     6235 and 6243 S. Redwood Road and to allow for the expansion of the parking area
                     for the existing office building at 6243 S. Redwood Road.
                5.   If the proposed Zoning Map amendment is approved by the City Council, the
                     applicant will be required to complete a subdivision amendment or lot-line
                     adjustment prior to development to relocate property boundaries and record
                     necessary easements for access.
                6.   Site plan review is required prior to any construction or development to ensure
                     compliance with all city development standards.
                7.   The proposed Zoning Map amendment is consistent with the adopted General Plan.
                8.   Staff recommends approval of the zoning map amendment.
                9.   The City Council is the decision- making authority for a Zoning Map amendment
                     request and may adopt or reject the amendment as it deems appropriate pursuant to
                     Section 13. 05. 030 and other applicable sections of the Taylorsville City Code.

            Conditions
            Staff does not recommend that additional conditions be considered with this request.


            Staff Recommendation
            Staff recommends the Planning Commission send a positive recommendation to the City
            Council to amend the Zoning Map for the property at 1633 W. 6235 S. ( parcel i. d. 21- 22-
            202- 065- 0000) from Residential ( R- 1- 10) to Professional Office( P0), based on the Findings

Mr. Spung,

I received your letter regarding the request for the property owner at 1633 West 6235 South,
Taylorsville to change his zoning from single family residential to professional office and
would like to provide my comments relating to the request:

 1. I'm opposed to a change from residential to professional office in that this property abuts to
single family residential on 2 sides and if approved, we are aware that other properties along
6235 South will seek to change their zoning from single family to commercial which will
change the demographics of the neighborhood and affect property values.
2. Utah is experiencing a severe housing shortage at this time and changing the zoning on a
residential property to professional office, leaves one more family unable to find traditional
housing at a reasonable price. Perhaps a better use for this property would be multi-family
instead of an office building.
3. My final concern is the increase in traffic and the safety of multiple cars exiting on to 6235
South during peak business hours. The traffic in this area during peak business hours is quite
congested and as the property is close to Redwood Road, it will increase the congestion we
already experience when entering and exiting our neighborhoods.


Also, when I last spoke to the Planning Commission 2 or 3 years ago when my neighbor
sought to change its zoning, I was informed that the Planning Commission was not in favor of
 spot zoning" or changing the zoning in neighborhoods from residential to
commercial/professional offices; so I'm a little confused that we now have this issue and I
would welcome your response as to why the Planning Commission is considering this
request. There are a large number of vacant properties in this area that could accommodate
this business without changing the zoning in a residential neighborhood which would then
snowball as other properties in that general area will also seek to change their zoning.

Thank you for your consideration
