                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-48

    A RESOLUTION ADOPTING AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE USE POLICY


       WHEREAS, the advent of Artificial Intelligence (AI) is expected to revolutionize the
means by which the workforce of the United States will innovate, conduct ordinary tasks, and
perform research; and

        WHEREAS, as a local government entity, Riverton City is a highly regulated political
subdivision of the state of Utah, whose operations require careful compliance with an array of
statutes and judicial opinions; and

        WHEREAS, the prospect of implementing AI technology for use by Riverton City’s
staff requires careful consideration of how it must integrate within the legal framework in which
the City performs its chief duty to protect the health, safety, welfare, and morals of the City;

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that adoption of a policy
which governs the use of AI by City staff is necessary to maintain compliance with laws which
regulate it; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Councils finds that the AI use policy offered by City staff
will enable the City’s objective to deploy AI tools while remaining in compliance with Utah law;

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

   1. The Artificial Intelligence Use Policy attached hereto as Exhibit A is hereby approved
      and adopted.

   2. This resolution shall take effect immediately.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
