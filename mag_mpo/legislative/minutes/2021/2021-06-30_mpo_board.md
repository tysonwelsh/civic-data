- MOUNTAINLAND MPO REGIONAL PLANNING COMMITTEE Location: Zoom Livestream: mountainland.org/live Meeting Minutes June 30, 2021 4:00 p.m. Mayor Steve Leifson called the meeting to order. Public Comment None. Voting Members: Representing: Mayor Steve Leifson, Chair Spanish Fork Mayor Jim Miller, Vice Chair Saratoga Springs Mayor Julie Fullmer Vineyard Mayor Brad Frost American Fork Mayor Tom Westmoreland Eagle Mountain Mayor Rod Mann Highland Mayor Richard Brunst Orem Mayor Bill Wright Payson Mayor Richard Child Springville Eric Rasband UDOT Mary De La Mare-Schaeffer UTA Commissioner Bill Lee Utah County Commissioner Amelia Gardner Utah County Commissioner Tom Sakievich Utah County Attending: Representing: Ezra Nair Utah County MAG Staff: Shawn Seager Robert Allen Shawn Eliot Melanie Haws MPO Regional Planning Committee May 6, 2021 Page 2 MAG TIP Amendment – Bob Allen, MAG State Legislature directed the Transportation Commission to fund certain transportation projects using funds from HB433, SB2, and COVID Relief Funds. The following projects are considered critical to be approved which will allow UDOT to begin construction immediately: 1. Vineyard Connector; Main St to 1600 N, Vineyard $16.5M 2. Geneva Road; University Pkwy to 1800 S, Orem $20M 3. Mountain View Corridor; Porter Rockwell to 2100 North $350.6M 4. US-189; University Avenue viaduct replacement $49M The projects listed are state and federally funded, therefore MAG does not determine how they are used. Mayor Fullmer moved that the TIP be amended to include the list of UDOT projects as presented. Mayor Mann seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. The following individuals voted in favor via email prior to the meeting: Mayor Denise Andersen, Cedar Hills Mayor Jeff Acerson, Lindon Mayor Mark Johnson, Lehi Mayor Kirk Hunsaker, Santaquin Mayor Michelle Kaufusi, Provo Other Business Next Scheduled Meeting: August 5, 2021, Central Utah Water Conservancy District, 1426 East 750 North, Bldg. 2, Orem, UT. Mayor Brunst moved to adjourn the meeting at 4:24 p.m. Mayor Miller seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. *Meeting minutes are recorded. A summary and all motions made during the meeting will be approved at the next meeting and can be found at https://mountainland.org/rpc/
