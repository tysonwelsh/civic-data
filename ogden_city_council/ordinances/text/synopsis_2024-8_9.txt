                                           AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                     SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE



        I, Jason Gould, the duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber
County, State of Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its April 9, 2024, meeting adopted the
following:

        ORDINANCE NO. 2024-8: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE OGDEN
        MUNICIPAL CODE BY ADOPTING SUBSECTION 15-17-4.I TO REVISE PAINTING OF EXTERIOR
        BRICK REGULATIONS IN THE R-3 ZONE; ADOPTING SUBSECTION 15-18-4.I REVISING PAINTING
        OF EXTERIOR BRICK RESTRICTIONS IN THE R-4 ZONE; AMENDING TITLE 15, CHAPTER 32 BY
        ADOPTING A NEW SECTION 7 TO REVISE PAINTING OF EXTERIOR BRICK REGULATIONS IN THE
        R-MFV AND R-MFH ZONES; AMENDING SUBSECTION 15-33-5.A REVISING PAINTING OF
        EXTERIOR BRICK REGULATIONS IN THE PI ZONE; AMENDING TITLE 15, CHAPTER 34 BY
        ADOPTING A NEW SECTION 10 TO PROHIBIT PAINTING OR COVERING EXTERIOR BRICK IN
        DOWNTOWN COMMERCIAL ZONES; AMENDING SUBSECTION 15-37-5.A REVISING PAINTING OF
        EXTERIOR BRICK REGULATIONS IN THE R-9 ZONE; AMENDING SUBSECTION 15-41-5.F
        REVISING PAINTING OF EXTERIOR BRICK REGULATIONS IN THE NC ZONE; AND BY PROVIDING
        THAT THIS ORDINANCE SHALL BECOME EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY UPON POSTING AFTER
        FINAL PASSAGE.

        ORDINANCE NO. 2024-9: AN ORDINANCE OF OGDEN CITY, UTAH, AMENDING THE OGDEN
        MUNICIPAL CODE BY AMENDING CHAPTER 3-31 TO REVISE THE ORGANIZATION AND NAME OF
        THE OGDEN NATURAL RESOURCES AND SUSTAINABILITY STEWARDSHIP COMMITTEE; TO
        MAKE TECHNICAL CHANGES; AND BY PROVIDING THAT THIS ORDINANCE SHALL BECOME
        EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY UPON POSTING AFTER FINAL PASSAGE.

        Copies of all ordinances in their entirety are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office
located on the second floor of the Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard #210 during regular
business hours.

The undersigned, duly appointed Deputy City Recorder, does hereby certify that the above notice was
posted in accordance with Utah State law on this 26th day of April 2024.


                                                                    JASON GOULD
                                                                    DEPUTY CITY RECORDER
