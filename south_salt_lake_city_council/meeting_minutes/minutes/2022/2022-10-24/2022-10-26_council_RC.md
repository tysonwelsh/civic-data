NO ACTION COMMENTS

       1. SCHEDULING. The City Recorder informed those at the meeting of upcoming events,
          meetings, activities, etc. Next Mtg— Nov. 9th @ 7pm.

       2. CITIZEN COMMENTS/QUESTIONS.
          Lawrence Horman, a local homeless advocate, talked to the Council about a
          Facebook page he operates a Facebook page called the Desert Defiant Initiative
          that’s about the homeless and by the homeless. It provides an online space for
          discussions about what they see homelessness as and what they also see as
          solutions. He knows that there is no one solution but believes that it is multi layered
          and that it should be every City’s duty to contribute to the solution and cannot be
          done alone by cities such as Salt Lake City or South Salt Lake.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood gave an overview on the Capstone Project Presentation by the USU
          group. The project was presented to City staff in May and the Mayor wanted to
          extend her thanks for the professional level of work and thanked the staff that also
          worked on it.

              Mayor Wood informed the Council that the City had prepared a Flash Vote survey to
              go out after the Granite School District’s presentation this evening to get more
              information from the South Salt Lake population to share with Granite District.
              Originally, there were two elementary schools in South Salt Lake that were being
              considered for potential closure. Mayor Wood noted that Promise South Salt Lake
              staff had been working with students and families that are part of the voiceless and
              vulnerable population in the City to voice their opinion on the potential school
              closures and how that would affect these families.

              Mayor Wood also gave an update on past and upcoming City events:
              -Spooktacular & Howloween were both successful and fun filled events for
              residents.
              -Spooky City Hall- coming up on Monday 10/31 from 3:00-4:45 pm.

              Lastly, Mayor Wood informed the Council that Lieutenant Darren Carr has been
              promoted to Deputy Police Chief.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS


    1. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Amending Section 5.04.180 of the
       South Salt Lake City Municipal Code To Increase the Number of City Alcoholic
       Beverage Licenses In Excess of the Quota Available In the Downtown District.
       City Attorney, Josh Collins, presented the change that would be made in the City Code if
       this Ordinance is passed which would allow for two additional alcohol licenses for bars
       and taverns in the Downtown District and have a total number of five alcohol licenses in
       that District.

        Conducting Chair Siwik opened the floor for a motion.

    2. A Resolution In Support of Equipment Financing For The Lease Purchase of Three
       Ambulances.
       Chief Addison reviewed the need for this time sensitive Resolution that would aid in the
       financing of the lease purchase of three ambulances.

        Conducting Chair Siwik made a clarifying point that it says in Paragraph 3 that the City’s
        obligation is subject to annual appropriation, meaning that this is something that is not
        set in stone but is based on the budget process each year and this Resolution is
        something that would be done in support of the Fire Department’s needs at this specific
        time.
