                                         Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 10-27-20
                                             7:10 p.m.
                                    Nephi City Council Chambers


1. Mr. Todd Edington addressed the council about a ramp that he had built for his RV that is located at
   the Nebo View Rentals RV park. He said that the city’s code enforcement officer had visited his
   home and asked him to remove the ramp. He was asking the council if they would allow him to keep
   the ramp due to his disability. He presented pictures of the ramp to the council. The council agreed
   to discuss the issue further and get back to Mr. Edington with their decision. City Attorney Kasey
   Wright suggested that there might be something in the law that allows for a reasonable
   accommodation due to Mr. Edington’s disability.

2.    City Recorder Lisa Brough updated the council about the Juab High School art department mural
     progress. The mural is completed, and Custom Sign Works will install it as soon as the wall where it
     is being placed is painted.

3.   City Administrator Seth Atkinson presented the pros and cons of adopting a 2-year budget instead of
     an annual budget. A discussion ensued and the council directed staff to pursue preparing a 2 -year
     budget this next budget season.

4. Mr. Atkinson gave an update on the Juab Fieldhouse project.

5. Mr. Atkinson shared with the council a report prepared by Travis Worwood that summarized Nephi
   City’s systems that provide cyber security to the city’s records and data.

6. The council shared the following updates:
                 Councilor Seely reported that the JRDA has awarded the contract for the scale
                    house at the landfill.
                 The council discussed that the mulch pile needs to be cleared out.
                 Councilor Memmott reported that the Central Utah Water Conservancy
                    representative will be presenting at a future meeting.
                 Councilor Worwood reported that the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints
                    may be calling service missionaries that may be able to help with our cemetery
                    maintenance in the future.
                 The council requested that Mr. Atkinson come back with a progress report on the
                    hotel project.

7. Adjournment 8:25 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Kent
Jones, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Todd Edington, Bailey Hansen, Andrea Holm, Bret
