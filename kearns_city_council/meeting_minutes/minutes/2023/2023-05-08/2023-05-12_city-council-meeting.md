Planning

CTAA Resolution

               Mayor Kelly Bush reviewed the resolution.

              Council Member Snow asked if Kearns needed to have a hotel to participate in
the Convention and Tourism Assessment Area.

                Kayla Mauldin, Senior Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (MSD), stated Kearns is not required to have a hotel. But should it ever have a hotel in
the future, there would be an assessment that tourists would pay that would go into the program.

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaefer, moved to approve
Resolution No. 2023-05-01, granting consent to the County of Salt Lake to form the Salt Lake
County Convention and Tourism Assessment Area. The motion passed unanimously.

RESOLUTION NO. 2023-05-01                                                DATE: May 12, 2023

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL, GRANTING
       CONSENT TO THE COUNTY OF SALT LAKE TO FORM THE SALT LAKE
       COUNTY CONVENTION AND TOURISM ASSESSMENT AREA

                                          RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Convention and Tourism Business Assessment Area Act, Utah Code Title 11,
Chapter 42b, (the “Act”), Section 11-42b-103 authorizes the County to form a convention and
tourism assessment area for the purposes of promoting tourism; and

WHEREAS, the County Council of the County of Salt Lake has requested consent to form the
Salt Lake County Convention and Tourism Assessment Area (the “SLCo CTAA”) in Kearns Metro
Township; and

WHEREAS, Kearns desires to benefit from the programs offered by a future SLCo CTAA,
particularly in promoting its assets and making investments to capture spending dollars from
visitors to the Olympic Oval, Oquirrh Park, and other Kearns amenities and destinations.

                                         RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL that:

Section 1: The above recitals are true and correct.

Section 2: The County Council of the County of Salt Lake is hereby granted consent to include
Kearns Metro Township in the SLCo CTAA, for the formation of the SLCo CTAA and future
renewals.

Section 3: The Kearns Metro Township Clerk is hereby directed to transmit a certified copy of
this Resolution to the Clerk of the Salt Lake County Council.

Section 4: This Resolution is effective immediately upon its adoption.

PASSED and ADOPTED this 12th day of May, 2023.

Legal Updates/Information

Discussion regarding letter of intent to draft legislation.

                 Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, asked the Council
permission to draft a letter of intent to the Legislature stating the metro townships are developing
legislation to transition from townships into cities/towns. This letter would need to be completed
before the next Kearns Metro Township Council meeting.

                 Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
authorize legal counsel to work on a letter stating the metro townships are developing legislation
to transition from townships into cities and towns. The motion passed unanimously.
