Healthy Utah Community Designation: Presenter: Heather Cox, Grant Specialist and Carson
Hardy, Management Analyst

Ms. Cox explained that Get Healthy Utah is an initiative that was created by Lieutenant
Governor Greg Bill and the Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT). This effort is to help
cities across the state to improve the health of their citizens. There is a designation called “The
Healthy Utah Community.” The process to gain the Get Healthy Utah is four parts: sign a letter
of commitment, identify healthy strategies, establish a healthy coalition, and develop a
community health plan.

The current highlighted strategies for a Healthy Orem is active living, access to healthy
food, and mental health. Active Living strategy includes free or low cost community programs,
addressing active transportation connectivity gaps, and adopting an ordinance to require new
subdivisions to provide sidewalks. The strategy for Access to Healthy Food is to establish a
farmers market, promote enrollment in SNAP, WIC, school meal programs and other food access
programs, and implement one new worksite wellness strategy around healthy food among city
employees. The Mental Health Strategies are to train first responders on mental health crises,
host recurring community events that promote social connection, and implement a program,
recurring event or establish an area of the community that promotes connection.

Ms. Cox and Mr. Hardy continued to speak about establishing a community health plan.
This plan will highlight the need to access and promote Healthcare Services for healthy eating,
obesity and diabetes. Another highlight will be social health and community connectedness. Ms.
Cox and Mr. Hardy gave recommendations for the community plan which include partnering
with Hospitals to implement mobile screenings, expansion of recreational amenities and trails,
establish community gardens, start a seed library program, partner with Utah State University’s
Community Wellbeing project to conduct a survey of resident’s health, and build new and add to

existing trails.

Potential Campaign Financial Disclosure Requirements for Incumbents: Presenter: D. Jacob
Summers, Deputy City Attorney

Mr. Summers is here to discuss if the City of Orem wants to adopt an extra financial
disclosures for candidates. The goal would be to provide greater transparency to the residents of
Orem City. The proposed ordinance would require elected officials to file an annual reporting of
any funds received or expenditures since their last disclosure statement. This is the proposed
ordinance language. “Any elected official, who holds office in the City of Orem, on September
26, 2023, shall file an annual campaign financial disclosure statement with the City Recorder on,
or before, (TBA). This reconciliation filing must disclose all contributions received and
expenditures since the elected official’s last campaign financial disclosure statement filed with
the City Recorder through (TBA), which have not been previously disclosed.”

The City Council had discussions about this new ordinance and that the City of Orem
will follow state code with the financial disclosure calendar with the addition of this one extra
disclosure.

Fire Presentation Program: Presenter: Derek Spencer, Division Fire Chief; Brandon Valley,
Deputy Fire Marshall and Marc Sanderson Fire Chief

Chief Sanderson explained that the fire prevention part of the fire department needed to
be updated. Deputy Fire Marshal Valley explained the new program they are presenting. The
goal of the inspection program is to identify safety concerns, education and build relationships of

trust. The fire inspectors check alarm panels, sprinkler systems, fire extinguishers, exit lighting
and egress, and target hazards. The program is expected to be annual and requires physical
inspections of every home based (impact) and commercial business. The challenges are
subleased spaces, mobile business, scheduling, data entry, billing and credibility.

Some of the business concerns are: is this a scam, a money grab, why can’t I pay Orem,
how is the fee justified and this is too expensive. The fire department's solution has three main
changes; change the way we bill our inspections, add a digital review component and modify our
fee structure. The fee structure is based on the “boots on the ground” total compensation for our
entry level, Inspector II @ $46.80 per hour and the type of business. Home based business = |
hour and Commercial businesses = 2 hours. These fees cover the following associated costs of
community Risk Reduction, code review of the CRR report, and follow-up inspections, if
needed.

Deputy Fire Marshall Valley continued to explain the benefits for the business owner
which are: businesses have asked for this change, safety, comfortability, adds validity, and builds
stronger relationships of trust. The benefits for fire prevention by giving more time for
community outreach programs, public education, cadet programs and follow-up. The council
had questions and discussions about the program.

Transportation Master Plan Update: Presenter: Sam Kelly, City Engineer

Mr. Kelly presented the 2022 update from the 2015 Transportation Master Plan (TMP)
which include updating existing data to current conditions, base traffic counts, roadway level of
Service, road cross-sections, state street master plan elements removed, travel demand modeling
update from 2040 to 2050, incorporated undated MAG Transplan50 recommendations and
capital improvement plan (cost estimates), The recommendations from the Transportation
Advisory Commission is that the City of Orem can not build out of congestion, balance the needs
of all users, good planning and transportation encourages active transportation, and we must be
fiscally and socially responsible. The planning commission recommended the following
changes; (1) Remove the following projects: 800 East widening, 1600 North widening, 800
South overpass and 800 South widening, 1200 North overpass and 800 West widening; (2)
modify language concerning connectivity; (3) future discussion items such as bike lanes, UVU
connectivity and connectivity.
