RESOLUTION NO. 6244
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR THE PRESERVATION OF 
ABRAVANEL HALL
WHEREAS, Abravanel Hall is a Salt Lake County -owned and operated 
facility that has served as the permanent home of the Utah Symphony since 
1979; and
WHEREAS, Abravanel Hall has long served as an iconic cultural and 
architectural landmark in Salt Lake County, recognized for its exceptional 
acoustic design and architectural significance; and

WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council acknowledges the historical and 
cultural importance of Abravanel Hall to the residents of Salt Lake County, 
and its vital role as a performance venue for the Utah Symphony and other 
cultural events; and
WHEREAS, Abravanel Hall is located within the Sports, Entertainment, 
Culture, and Convention District recently established by the Utah Legislature 
and Salt Lake City, and plays a crucial role in the eastern part of this District, 
serving as an anchor for cultural activities; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council supports efforts to revitalize 
downtown Salt Lake City and recognizes that the integration of Abravanel 
Hall into any redevelopment plans related to the Sports, Entertainment, 
Culture, and Convention District must take into account the Hall ’s cultural 
significance and the need to maintain its architectural and acoustic integrity; 
and
WHEREAS, the public has demonstrated strong support for the preservation 
of Abravanel Hall, with petitions garnering over 50,000 signatures in 
opposition to any plans that would alter or demolish the hall; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council recognizes that while renovations 
may be necessary for the long -term maintenance of Abravanel Hall, such 
renovations must be 2 undertaken with care to ensure the Hall ’s historical, 
architectural, and acoustic integrity is preserved;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Salt Lake County 
Council states and affirms a commitment to preserve Abravanel Hall in its 
present form and on its current site, recognizing it as an essential cultural 
institution within Salt Lake County.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Salt Lake County Council intends 
that any renovations to Abravanel Hall shall prioritize the preservation of the 
Hall’s unique architectural and acoustic design while creatively integrating it 
into the broader revitalization of the Sports, Entertainment, Culture, and 
Convention District.
BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the Salt Lake County Council will work 
closely with the Smith Entertainment Group, the Utah Symphony, the State 
of Utah, Salt Lake City, and distinguished philanthropists who have

Council Member Bradley  stated it was a delight to work with Council 
Member Alvord on this resolution.  He held a mutual feeling of respect.  He 
recalled going to see the symphony as a boy before the hall was built.  Many 
distinguished philanthropists have supported the hall throughout the years, 
and he expressed hope that would continue.  This resolution would send the 
message that the members of the Council are all on the same page.  
Council Member Stringham  recognized Kiersten Thompson and Juan T . 
Becerra from the Government and Community Relations Department at the 
Church of Jesus Christ of Latter Day Saints.  She extended gratitude to the 
Church for its partnership in making Abravanel Hall possible.  The Church 
originally leased the land to Salt Lake County for only $ 1 a year.  Later, it 
allowed the County to purchase the land.  She noted arts and culture are very 
important to Utah residents. 
A motion was made by Council Member Alvord, seconded by Council Member 
Bradley, that this agenda item be approved. The motion carried by a unanimous 
vote.
Ms. Karen Wyatt , Violinist, Utah Symphony, stated she and her colleagues 
were thrilled that the Smith Entertainment Group, Mayor Jennifer Wilson, the 
Revitalization Zone Committee, and both the County and City Councils were 
united in their efforts to keep the hall where it is and as it is, with the 
expectation that informed and thoughtful renovations will allow it to continue 
to serve as a beacon of cultural excellence.
