                                                           AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                                     SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE




I, Lee Ann Peterson, the duly appointed Chief Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber County, State of
Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its February 20, 2024, meeting adopted the following:

         ORDINANCE 2024-3 – An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah, amending the zoning map of Ogden City, as adopted by
         Section 15-3-3 of the Ogden Municipal Code, to reclassify as two-family and small lot residential zone (R-2S)
         property heretofore classified as single-family residential zone (R-1-6), generally located at 218 Chester Street;
         and providing that this ordinance shall become effective immediately upon posting after final passage.

         ORDINANCE 2024-4 – An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah, amending the Ogden Municipal Code by amending
         Section 15-2-4, Section 15-2-5, Section 15-2-6, Section 15-2-8, and Section 15-2-20 to amend and adopt
         definitions relative to group dwelling units; by amending Chater 10 of Title 15 to address group dwelling units;
         amending 15-41-7 to revise uses relative to group dwellings units; and by providing that this ordinance shall
         become effective immediately upon posting after final passage.




Copies of ordinances are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office located on the second floor of the
Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard #210, Ogden, Utah during regular business hours.
