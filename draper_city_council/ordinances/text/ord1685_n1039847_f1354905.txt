City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1685

On November 18, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1685 amending
the official land use map of draper city for approximately 0.89 acres of property from
residential low/medium density to residential medium density, located at
approximately 1394 east 12900 south within draper city, otherwise known as the Fetzer
zoning map amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
