    9.1. Municipal Code Title 2 and 3 Amendments 
        DISCUSSION AND ACTION: Interim City Attorney Jesse Riddle, with Deer Valley 
        Law, will present proposed changes to municipal codes title 2 and 3. The City Council 
        will act to approve (or Deny) these changes via Ordinance. 
 
 
    Interim City Attorney Jesse Riddle presented the proposed changes and noted proposed 
amendments to the ordinance during the presentation. There was discussion amongst council, in 
conjunction with some staff, regarding changes to employment status, appointments and 
removal of appointed staff and other various changes that were in the proposed ordinance.   
 
 
    Motion: COUNCILMEMBER MCCUMBER MOVED TO ADOPT ORDINANCE 2026-
01 AS AMENDED. THE MOTION WAS TABLED TO ENTERTAIN AN AMENDED 
MOTION PROPOSED BY COUNCILMEMBER NAIR.  
 
    Counter Motion: COUNCILMEMBER NAIR MOVED TO CONTINUE THE ITEM TO 
THE NEXT MEETING.  
 
    The council discussed the motions. It was decided that the council would vote on the motion 
but revisit both title 2 and 3 at a future meeting to see if further revisions were necessary.
