 VARIANCES
 B-9-2020                     CHRIS FURSTENAU is requesting several variances from the West Valley City Code.
                              These Sections are as follows: Section 7-14-701 and 7-11-212. More specifically, Mr.
                              Furstenau is requesting that a new home to be built at 1495 Russett Ave that does not comply
                              with the garage, materials, or points sections needed for a new single-family dwelling. The
                              new home is being built to replace a home that was recently destroyed by fire. The property is
                              located at 1495 West Russett Avenue and is zoned R-1-6 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential,
                              minimum lot size 6,000 square feet). (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311)
