       6. INFORMATION.

               a. CRB Quarterly Report
                  Civilian Review Board Chair, Joy Glad, went over some brief information of
                  what the Civilian Review Board is, and isn’t, as well as what it oversees, and
                  doesn’t oversee. She then went over the highlights of the 1st quarter for
                  2024. From January to March, the CRB Board reviewed 33 cases, and found
                  zero to be in any violation of Federal, State, and City rules and policies.
                  There were four cases that required significant discussion, and a total
                  number of one ride along that is done as a part of education and outreach
                  to the community. There were zero citizen complaints, and one
                  recommendation that the CRB Board has forwarded to the Police
                  Department. Based on some feedback from residents, the desire for better
                  visibility for the sides of police vehicles has been made.

                   A copy of the report is attached and incorporated by this reference.

               b. Adopt a Storm Drain
                  Stormwater Division Manager, Corby Talbot, and Stormwater Tech and
                  Outreach Coordinator, Spencer Dunn, talked about Adopt a Drain, a national
                  clean water program, that works with cities to protect water and
                  waterways. The City is using this online portal to have residents sign up and
                  adopt a drain and join in the City’s efforts to keep its drains clear of debris.
                  Residents can go to adopt-a-drain.org for more information.

                   Council Member Sanchez asked what the cost of using this program was to
                   the City.

                   Mr. Talbot didn’t know at the moment and said that they would be happy to
                   provide the exact numbers to the Council.

                   A copy of the PowerPoint presentation is attached and incorporated by this
                   reference.
