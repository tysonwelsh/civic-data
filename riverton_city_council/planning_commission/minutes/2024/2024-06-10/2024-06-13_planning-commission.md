      A.     CHECKETTS PRIVATE LANE, PLZ-24-2021, A CONDITIONAL USE
             PERMIT APPLICATION FOR A PRIVATE LANE TO ACCOMMODATE TWO
             LOTS LOCATED AT 13341 SOUTH TRESTLE LANE. APPLICANT – KELLY
             CHECKETTS.

City Planner, Lisa Halversen presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is for a
1.25-acre property situated in the south-central area of the City, bordering Trestle Lane and
abutting the Utah Salt Lake Canal. The proposal involves subdividing the property, which
currently includes a house on the west side with access to Trestle Lane, to create an
additional lot in the eastern section.

The zoning is RR-22 as are the neighboring properties. The land meets current zoning
regulations for subdivision. An Owner's Affidavit was provided. No responses were received
from the public from the mailed notices. The original plan was presented with a proposed
configuration for the private lane during the Design Review Committee (“DRC”) Meeting, it
may be adjusted slightly. The lane, which would service a maximum of two lots, needs to
be at least 20 feet wide and extend 216 feet in depth. The turnaround is in compliance with
Fire Code regulations.

Ms. Halversen believed that the property meets the qualifications for a private lane due to
the absence of planned City roads in the area, the lack of existing local streets extending
towards the property, and the fact that access for neighboring properties would not be
impacted. Staff recommended approval with the conditions enumerated in the Staff Report.
Commissioner Rushton asked if Trestle Lane was designated as a public road, as opposed
to a private lane. It was confirmed that that was the case. He further sought clarification

agreement. A question was raised regarding the existing entrance point for Sandstone, LLC,

       D.     REDWOOD DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT, PLZ-24-6002, FOR 3.9 ACRES
              LOCATED AT THE SOUTHEAST CORNER OF 12800 SOUTH AND
              REDWOOD ROAD. APPLICANT - RIVERTON CITY, JARED OSMOND

Mr. Lethbridge presented the Staff Report and stated that the property is 3.9 acres in size
and located near Riverton City Hall. The application differs from standard applications due
to the property being situated within a Redevelopment Area (“RDA”). The Code grants cities
the authority to pursue redevelopment initiatives. These areas, typically aimed at addressing
blighted properties or incentivizing redevelopment, offer municipalities a broader range of
tools to achieve their goals. The Riverton RDA is essentially the City Council acting in a
different capacity and is responsible for administering and overseeing the placement and
management of these areas.

Mr. Lethbridge reported that this approach to redevelopment has been used frequently
throughout the City such as with the Surburban Land Reserve and the Mountain View Village
Project. Historically, an RDA has existed in the downtown since 1997 and has guided
significant development. The prior RDA has been dissolved and a new Community
Reinvestment Area (“CRA”) encompassing the current project area was established. The
initial designation identified the area for potential redevelopment and the current project
encompasses a slightly smaller area.

The location of the project area was identified. It is north of Riverton City Hall and bordered
by 12910 South. Three parcels were previously occupied by the Riverton Senior Center that
is now a detention pond that collects water runoff from Redwood Road. The zoning is
Commercial Downtown (“CD”) and has been in place for some time. The similarly zoned
Riverton Hardware Building is located to the north. The adjacent zoning is RR-22 and
applies to the existing homes along the eastern boundary and properties south of Redwood
Road. An R-4 zone designation encompasses the area, including Riverton City Hall. The
General Plan designation is Community Commercial. The eastern properties retained their
General Plan designation of Estate Density Residential. No changes were proposed to the
