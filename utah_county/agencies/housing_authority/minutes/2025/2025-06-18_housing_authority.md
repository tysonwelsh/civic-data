B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-06-02 2024 Agency Audit 
Suzanne made a motion to approve the Annual Agency Audit. April seconded the 
motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
The HAUC Board of Commissioners is required by HUD to approve annual audits for the 
agency and programs administered by HAUC. The 2024 HAUC audit has been completed and 
is ready for board approval. HAUC engaged outside independent auditors complete the 2024 
audit. The auditors presented the report to the Board of Commissioners.  The 2024 HAUC 
agency audit received an unqualified opinion and contained no material findings.  HAUC is 
considered a low audit risk. 
 
C. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-06-03 Addendums 6 & 7 Purchase Agreement 12-04-24. 
Steve made a motion to approve Addendums 6 & 7, Purchase Agreement 12-04-24. 
Suzanne seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
The Housing Authority of Utah County placed a property located in Orem, Utah, under contract 
by way of a purchase agreement dated 12-04-2024. Since this time, HAUC has completed 
many items of due diligence. Addendums 6 & 7 are the result of some of the information 
obtained from the reports generated by the property. These addendums reduce the purchase 
price by $450,000. Closing is scheduled to occur on or before July 31, 2025. 
 
D. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-06-04 Procurement Policy 
April made a motion to approve the updated Procurement Policy. Suzanne seconded 
the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC’s current procurement policy, amended January 1, 2022, contains dollar amounts which 
are below HUD, State of Utah, and Utah County thresholds. Staff is proposing to increase the 
threshold for small purchases from the current cap of $50,000 to $100,000.
