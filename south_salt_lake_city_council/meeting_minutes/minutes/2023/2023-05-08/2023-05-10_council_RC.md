Action Items

   New Business

   1. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Amending Section 3.11.110 of the
      South Salt Lake City Municipal Code to Modify Fees Relating to Parks and Community
      Centers in South Salt Lake City and to Update Names of Certain City Owned Facilities.
      Neighborhoods Director, Sharen Hauri, showed the Council a PowerPoint presentation,
      a copy of which is attached and incorporated by this reference, that explained the
      reasons for the proposed Fee Schedule update for Parks and Community Centers and
      showed the current and proposed fees.

       The Council Members all agreed that they would like to see more data on what other
       parks and community centers in other cities charge for deposits and reservations.

       Council Chair Bynum made a motion to move this as Unfinished Business to the next
       Council Meeting.

    3. The City Council Authorizing the Loan of Project Area Funds for the 3900 South
       Community Development Area.
       Community & Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, about the
       existing CDA that was created back in 2014 which created 134 single family homes,
       some industrial spaces, apartments, and an elementary school. The developer had
       believed that the process had been started so they could start to receive funds this fiscal
       year but found that it hadn’t happened. Mr. Weidenhamer said that both he and the
       Mayor are willing to start the process but would need Council approval to borrow
       money from another existing CDA. There is a cap on how much they would be able to
       and doing so wouldn’t affect other projects.

        Council Member Huff motioned to move New Business Items 2 and 3 as Unfinished
