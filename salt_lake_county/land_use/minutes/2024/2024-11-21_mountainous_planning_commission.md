Comments: Mr. Winn said he has been the manager of the HOA since 2005-2006. He managed for MACU 
and later Tavaci Holdings. In 2004, the road stub was made for secondary emergency access road, which is 
stubbed off to 600-foot cliff. Proposal is to incorporate stub road into lot 15 and vacate. Utility companies 
willing to vacate and secondary access will never be possible. HOA doesn’t have to maintain. Took out the 
paved road, deemed  to be dangerous and excavated to match the sloping contours , and doesn’t exist 
anymore.  
 
Commissioner Knoblock asked if the land would be incorporated into one of the two lots and administrative 
adjustment with respect to the property line. Mr. Winn says stubbed road has been incorporated into lot 15 
via a plat adjustment and no objections to the same; HOA doesn’t want responsibility of the common stub.  
 
No one from the public was present to speak.
