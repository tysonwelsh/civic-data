1. City Administrator Seth Atkinson described the MOU with Utah Cyber Tech. The State of Utah
   offers local governments training and services related to cyber security. Our information
   technology vendor, Tech Legion, is recommending that we engage with the Utah Cyber Center,
   through a memorandum of understanding, to use these resources.
       a. The training helps local government employees recognize suspicious emails, phishing
            attacks, and links. Through this training, employees can be more astute about the types
            of communications they receive and spot fraudulent messages. The Utah Cyber Center
            will test employees through fake attacks and then help employees become educated if
            they fall for these attacks. Ransomware and phishing attacks leave local governments
            vulnerable and can be very costly. Training employees to avoid these attacks can help
            our organization stay safe.
       b. The state also has additional services that can help monitor cyber attacks. Some of
            these services are redundant along with the services received through Tech Legion but it
            can all be helpful. The state has allocated funding so that none of these services will
            cost the city money at this point. In the future if the funding runs dry, the city can opt
            out of these services or choose to continue them by paying a fee.
       c. Mr. Atkinson recounted a phishing attack attempt on their Gmail system that was
            thwarted by spam filters.
       d. Councilor Travis Worwood recalled a phishing attack that targeted the city, using
            information from a council meeting.
       e. Mr. Atkinson emphasized the importance of ongoing training due to the evolving nature
            of phishing attacks and mentioned the success of initial training among staff and the
            need for continued vigilance.
       f. The MOU will be brought to city council for action next Tuesday

2. The City Administrator introduced a technical assistance agreement with UDOT for a
   transportation master plan.
       a. The agreement involves a grant of $62,000 from UDOT and a contribution of $4,800
           from the city.
       b. The funds required by the city are already saved in the planning funds.
       c. The scope of the master plan was discussed which included the west and south areas of
           the city. The ag park and data center area was mentioned. The potential need for
           private roads within the development were discussed and the need to coordinate with
           the county for the road from city limits to the development.

3. Administrative and Council Updates
      a. The capital improvement plan for the airport was completed last year and it had several
          projects. One was to use the bipartisan infrastructure law or Bill money to purchase
          snow removal equipment. They only received one bid for the project which was very
          high. They turned it down. Now looking for ways to rebid to avoid losing the bill funds:
                i. Swap federal funds with the state money
               ii. or use existing grant funds.
          Mayor Seely questioned what was wrong with the current equipment. Mr. Atkinson
          explained the we don’t receive enough snow for the large snow blower to work
          properly.
          Mr. Atkinson mentioned the draft agreement needed for the FAA to authorize the re-
          bidding process.
      b. Mayor Seely shared details from Governor Cox’s recent visit. The governor was
          impressed with the city’s planning and growth. Interest was expressed in the Ag Park
          and other community projects. Steve Lisonbee, the Senior Advisor for Rural Affairs; Mike
          Mower, The Senior Advisor for Community and Intergovernmental Relations; and the
          First Lady, Abby Cox was also present.
      c. Councilor Skip Worwood questioned whether we still require those annexing to provide
          their own water. The cooling system for the data center was discussed. Mayor Seely
          mentioned having a closed water-cooling system to conserve water. Mr. Atkinson
          recommended annexing on condition that the data center air cools to conserve water.
      d. Mayor Seely mentioned a request from Rural Utah Water to increase membership fees.
          Mr. Atkinson explained the benefit of membership, including consulting services and
          networking with other rural water users. For smaller cities the cost is about $300.
      e. Mayor Seely announced the soft opening for Elite Laser Tag on August 15 from 4 to 8
          pm. They invited the council to come help and work out the kinks of the equipment.
          Then on August 22 or 23 will be the Grand Opening. They will do a ribbon cutting.
          Mayor will let the council know on the specific date.
      f. Councilor Parady attended the ULTC privacy seminar today. He stressed the need for
          compliance with state regulations. Mr. Atkinson stated there are templates available
          from the state to help with compliance. Mr. Parady will send the information from the
          meeting to Mr. Atkinson when he receives it.
      g. Mr. Parady suggested adding a question to the community survey about the public’s
          demand for viewing public meetings. Mr. Atkinson emphasized the need for a policy
          decision on this matter. Also discussed were the potential need for an electronic
          meeting policy and attendance policy for city council members.
      h. Mayor Seely visited with Chief Morgan today. They discussed a proposal for holiday pay
          for the police department, covering major holidays like Thanksgiving and Christmas.
          Chief Morgan is willing to cover it under his current budget. Mr. Atkinson also
                recommended considering holiday pay for all employees who work or are called out on
                those days. Mayor Seely suggested looking into building it into the budget for future
                years for all city departments.
