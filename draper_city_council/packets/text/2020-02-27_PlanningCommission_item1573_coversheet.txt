Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Travis Van Ekelenburg
CC:  Date: February 27, 2020
Re: BL Smith Rezone Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately 1.13 acres located on the North side of 13200 South.  The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 sq. ft. minimum) .  The applicants are seeking a change in zoning designation from RA1 (Single-family Residential, 40,000 sq. ft. minimum) to RA2 (Single-family Residential, 20,000 sq. ft. minimum) . The subject properties are accessed to the south by an asphalt driveway that leads to a single family home with a detached garage, constructed in approximately 1980. The findings are as follows: For a positive recommendation:
1.  That Section 9-5-060 of the Draper City Municipal Code allows for the amendment of the city’s zoning map;
2.  That all findings for a zone map change, as contained in DCMC Section 9-5-060 (E), are satisfied;
3.  That adequate facilities and services exist to serve the subject property, including but not limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water and refuse collection;
4.  That the proposed zone map change is harmonious with the overall character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property; and the General Plan.
For a negative recommendation:
1.  The proposed amendment is not consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the Draper City General Plan.
2.  That the findings for a zone map change, as contained in DCMC Section 9-5-060 (E), are not satisfied.
3.  The RA2 zone is not appropriate for this parcel given the surrounding zoning on two sides.    ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 2/21/2020 Backup Material
