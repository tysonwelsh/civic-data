                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-15

  A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL PROVIDING ITS ADVICE
   AND CONSENT TO THE MAYOR’S APPOINTMENT OF BLAIR BARFUSS TO
     SERVE AS THE RIVERTON POLICE DEPARTMENT CHIEF OF POLICE

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council (“Council”) met in regular meeting on February
21, 2023 to consider, among other things, approving a resolution advising and consenting to the
Mayor’s appointment of a Chief of Police for the Riverton Police Department (“RPD”); and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor has determined to appoint Blair Barfuss as Chief of Police for
the RPD; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds and determines that Blair Barfuss has extensive
training and experience in providing public safety and law enforcement services to the public;
and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds that Blair Barfuss has a positive reputation in the
law enforcement community as well as a strong record of community service, reliability, and
respect for diverse groups of people; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby finds that Blair Barfuss is qualified to be the Chief of
Police for the RPD; and

       WHEREAS, the Council has given advice for such appointment; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the citizens of Riverton City to consent to and ratify such appointment.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Council hereby consents to the
appointment of Blair Barfuss as Chief of Police of the Riverton Police Department.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 21st day of
February 2023 by the following vote:
