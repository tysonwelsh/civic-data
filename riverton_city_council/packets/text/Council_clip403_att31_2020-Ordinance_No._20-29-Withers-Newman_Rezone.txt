                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 20-29

AN ORDINANCE REZONING 9.92 ACRES LOCATED AT 1300 WEST 12800 SOUTH,
PROPOSED REZONE FROM RR-22 TO RM-8D-SD, MULTI-FAMILY 8 UNITS/ACRE
 WITH SPECIFIC DEVELOPMENT DESIGNATIONS LIMITING UNIT TYPE AND
      CONFIGURATION, SCOTT YERMISH REPRESENTING NEWMAN
                    CONSTRUCTION, APPLICANT

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed zoning amendment; and,

     WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said zoning
amendment; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map by rezoning approximately 9.92 acres located at
1300 West 12800 South from RR-22 to RM-8D-SD, as described herein.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended to reflect the
                  following changes, including Specific Development Designations, as shown in
                  Exhibit “A”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.
