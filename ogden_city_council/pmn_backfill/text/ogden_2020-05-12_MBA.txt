Proposed Resolution 2020-1 adopting the annual budget of the Municipal
Building Authority for Fiscal Year 2020-2021
          A memo from the City Comptroller regarding the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2020-2021 annual Municipal Building
Authority (MBA) budget came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the current proposal is to accept for review
and set a public hearing on the proposed FY2020-2021 MBA budget, totaling $297,175, for June 9, 2020.
          Senior Analyst Sorensen summarized the memo and offered a comparison of the current FY budget with the proposed
FY 2021 budget. The total budget is decreasing by nearly half, from $537,425 to $297.175. Expenditures in the budget relate to
debt service for the 2007 Raptors Lindquist Stadium Bond and the 2006 Public Works Facility Bond; the Stadium Bond was
refunded in FY2020, and no debt service payment is required. The Public Works Facility Bond has a principal remaining of
$280,000 and the bond matures on June 15, 2021.
          Chair Choberka then introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2020-1, entitled:
                   “A resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Ogden City Municipal Building Authority adopting the annual
                   budget for Fiscal Year 2020-2021.”
          A copy of the proposed resolution was deposited with the Deputy City Recorder and ordered that the City Recorder have
at least one copy available for public inspection in her office during all business hours.
          The proposed resolution was then read by long title.
          TRUSTEE HYER MOVED TO ACCEPT FOR REVIEW AND TO SET A PUBLIC HEARING ON THE
PROPOSED RESOLUTION TO BE HELD IN THE CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS ON JUNE 9, 2020, DURING THE
CITY COUNCIL MEETING TO BE HELD AT 6:00 P.M. AND THAT THE CITY RECORDER BE DIRECTED TO
PROVIDE NOTICE AS REQUIRED BY LAW. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE WHITE, ALL VOTING
AYE.

     There being no further business to come before the Council, TRUSTEE NADOLSKI MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 8:01 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE HYER, ALL VOTING AYE.
