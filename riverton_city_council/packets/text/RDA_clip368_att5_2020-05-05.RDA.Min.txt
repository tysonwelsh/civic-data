22   Call to Order
23
24   Mayor Staggs called the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Meeting to order at 9:02
25   p.m. and conducted a Roll Call. Board Members Buroker, McCay, McDougal, Stewart, and
26   Wells were present.
27
28   Discussion/Action
29
30      a. RDA Resolution No. 20-01 – Adopting a Tentative Budget for the Riverton City
31         Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2020-2021
32
33   Mayor Staggs explained that the Board Members received the Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year
34   2020-2021, which was to be tentatively adopted by the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency.
35   He said the Tentative Budget would be reviewed and discussed and public hearings would be
36   held prior to its final adoption on June 16, 2020.
37
38   Board Member Stewart MOVED to adopt RDA Resolution No. 20-01 – Adopting a Tentative
39   Budget for the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year 2020-2021. Board
40   Wells SECONDED the motion. The vote was as follows: Board Member Buroker-Yes, McCay-
41   Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
