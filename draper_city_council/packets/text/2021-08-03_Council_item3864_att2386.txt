RECOMMENDATION:

Recommend approval of the Assessment-In-Lieu Agreement for Ruby Ballard Subdivision


BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) requires that any development or subdivision shall construct frontage
improvements and allows the owner to comply with DCMC 9-27-110(C) where an owner may pay an
assessment in lieu of public infrastructure construction.

The Ruby Ballard Subdivision located at 13065 South Fort Street was approved in 2021. A condition of
approval for that plat was that the owner either install the frontage improvements or execute an assessment
in lieu. The Subdivision is in an area where adjacent curb, gutter, and asphalt is not continuous. In order to
comply with the code, the owner decided he pay the Assessment in Lieu Agreement. The city staff
determined it would best serve the owner and the city to proceed with an assessment-in-lieu of construction
of public infrastructure rather than having the owner construct the improvements.

Staff recommends approval of the assessment in lieu of improvements for the parcel. Having the city
construct the frontage improvements will ensure that the seamless design and construction of the
improvements in the area and potentially save the city costs associated with modifying the city’s design by
incorporating infrastructure not presently constructed.
