Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Administrator Seth Atkinson offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Councilor Kent Jones.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 3-17-20, work session minutes 3-17-20, work session minutes 3-10-
20, work session minutes 3-24-20, and a list of claims on the warrant register dated 4-7-20. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 4-7-2020 EMPLOYEE SICK BANK DONATION POLICY ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council Resolution 4-7-2020 that adds to the personnel policy a sick-
leave donation section. The policy states that employees who have at least 500 hours of accrued sick
leave may donate up to 40 sick or vacation hours in any given year to a citywide pool for use by other
employees who have exhausted their sick leave. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Resolution 4-7-
2020. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 04-07-2020-A – EMERGENCY DELCARATION – CORONAVIRUS (COVID-19) ADOPTED:

   The council reviewed and discussed Ordinance 04-07-2020-A - Emergency Declaration. Councilor
Jones read a written statement of his opposition to the ordinance. (Copy Attached) Mayor Nielson said
he agreed with Councilor Jones and expressed his feeling of setting a date when the city should “draw a
line in the sand” to resume normalcy. He asked the council to consider the retaking control of our lives
here in Nephi and would like to discuss the issue further in next weeks work session. Councilor
Memmott offered the suggestion of extending the ordinance for one month at a time. Councilor
Worwood moved to amend the Ordinance 04-07-2020-A to read, “the state of emergency concerning
COVID-19 declared by Mayor Glade R. Nielson be extended until repealed by this body or until the
Wednesday after the first City Council meeting in May 2020, whichever occurs first.” Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:

                        Nay: Jones
                        Yes: Seely, Memmott, Worwood, and Ostler

   Councilor Ostler moved to adopt the amended Ordinance 04-07-2020-A. Councilor Memmott
seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:

                        Nay: Jones
                        Yes: Seely, Memmott, Worwood, and Ostler

ORDINANCE 04-07-2020 – ELECTRONIC MEETINGS ADOPTED:

   The council recognized the possible need to hold electronic meetings due to the current conditions of
social distancing requirements ordered by the State of Utah. State code requires municipalities to adopt
an electronic meeting ordinance before conducting such meetings. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt
Ordinance 04-07-2020 with amended language of Section 5 to read “This ordinance shall terminate
upon termination of Utah Governor Gary R. Herbert’s Executive Order 2020-1.
Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

EASEMENTS DONATED TO UDOT:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) had requested
two easements on property owned or partially owned by Nephi City along Highway 132 in the canyon.
The storm drain easements would provide improvements to the drainage system along the highway.
Mr. Atkinson reminded the council that UDOT had been helpful to Nephi City on several projects and the
easement areas requested were small. He indicated that one of the parcels is co-owned by the county
and the city. A donation on that parcel would need to be a joint venture from the county and city.
Councilor Memmott moved to authorize the mayor to sign the documents to donate the rights-of-way
easements for Parcel XB00-2177 owned solely by Nephi City and to donate the city’s portion on the
jointly owned parcel XB00-2685-1 to UDOT. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

  Mr. Atkinson reported that a new playground feature had been installed in the Town Square Park.
The feature was longer than anticipated and it became necessary to remove two trees.
  Mr. Atkinson reported that Central Utah Public Health has a link for updates on the COVID-19
pandemic.
  He also reported that the Equipment Shed Well has a newly installed system that will allow the well to
ramp up much slower and will use less power and have lower demand costs.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Ostler reported that the Miss Nephi Pageant is on hold until further notice.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competence of an individual, real property acquisition and disposal, and litigation. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:11 p.m.
