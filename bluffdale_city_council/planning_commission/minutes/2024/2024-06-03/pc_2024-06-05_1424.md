LEGISLATIVE ITEMS
5. PUBLIC HEARING, CONSIDERATION, AND RECOMMENDATION on Proposed

Amendments to the Bluffdale City Zoning Map (to RC Zone) and General Plan Land

Use Map (to Commercial Land Use). The WILL Group, Applicant (Application 2024-

01) — Staff Presenter, Ellen Oakman.
Associate City Planner, Ellen Oakman, presented the Staff Report and stated that the subject parcel
is 0.89 acres in size. The project is in the Inabi Data Center, which has an approved Site Plan. The
plat and mylar were soon to be finalized. There was a recent boundary adjustment to move a piece
of the property from Draper to Bluffdale to make it consistent with the project area. The issue before
the Planning Commission was the rezoning and Land Use Change for this small part of the project.
It is not a rezone from another zone but rather a rezone from no zone because of the boundary
adjustment. It will be added to the Regional Commercial Zone of the rest of the project. The
accompanying application is for a General Plan Land Use Amendment to designate the no Land Use
portion to Commercial Land Use to match the project area.
The applicant, David Driggs, reported that Bluffdale incorporated this parcel from Draper and they
need to zone it Regional Commercial to match the rest of the property. He assumed it was merely a
formality.
Chair Cragun opened the public hearing.
Scott Peterson, a Bluffdale resident who owns an adjoining parcel, asked if there were any pending
proposals to change the zoning on other properties in that area.
Chair Cragun asked for clarification on where the property is located. Community and Economic
Development Director, Grant Crowell, identified its location on the map. He stated that there are no
current rezoning applications for the area.
Mr. Crowell stated that there were no email comments.
There were no further public comments. Chair Cragun closed the public hearing.

DISCUSSION ITEMS
6. Planning Commission Business (Planning Session for Upcoming Items, Follow-Up, etc.)
Mr. Crowell introduced New Planning Manager, Caitlyn Tubbs. Ms. Tubbs was with the City of
Bluffdale for three years before leaving for four years. She was happy to have the opportunity to
return to Bluffdale.
The Love Where You Live event was to take place the following week.
A groundbreaking was scheduled for the Smith’s grocery store on June 13, 2024, at 1:00 p.m. at
Redwood Road and Bangerter Highway. The City has been working on this project for a long time
and was excited to see it move forward.
The 7-Eleven at Redwood Road and Bangerter Highway will also have a groundbreaking, as well as
the Arctic Circle and the Parkside Condominiums.
Chair Cragun reported that she has been asked what businesses will be in the Centrum at Bluffdale.
Mr. Crowell stated that the Tenant Finish Permit is in place for a Starbucks but there are no permits
in place for other tenants. He had heard there was to be a pizza restaurant. The developer has had a
lot of interest in the available space but they do not yet have tenants fully in place.
