Chair Curt Cochran called the Redevelopment Agency of Taylorsville City Board Meeting
to order at 8:25 PM and welcomed those in attendance. RDA Secretary Jean K. Ashby
conducted a Roll Call, wherein all Board Members were present.

1

Consideration of RDA Board Meeting Minutes of May 19, 2021

At 8:25 PM Chair Cochran explained that there was one correction to the minutes
they received in their packets, which corrects the chair and vice-chair designations.
Board Member Harker MOVED to approve the Minutes from the May 19, 2021, RDA
Board Meeting as amended. Board Member Burgess SECONDED the motion. Chair
Cochran called for a roll call vote. The vote was as follows: Harker-yes, Barbier/-yes,
Armstrong-yes, Burgess-yes, and Cochran-yes. All Board Members voted in favor
and the motion passed unanimously.

Resolution No. RDA 271-03 - A Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of Taylorsville City
Board Adopting a Final Balanced Budget for the Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2021, and
Ending June 30, 2022 - Wayne Harper, Economic & Community Development Director

At 8:26 PM, Mr. Harper explained that they have received the balanced RDA 2021-
2022 budget, which was discussed in the last board meeting. This includes the rental
income, and there are no transfers from the general fund. The main difference is

that this budget splits out the three project areas so it is more clear where the
revenues are coming from and being expended. It is easier to see where the Agency
is beginning to invest in the project areas with infrastructure, parks, and other
things of that nature.

Chair Cochran invited questions. There were none.

At 8:27 PM Board Member Armstrong MOVED to APPROVE Resolution No. RDA 21-
03. Board Member Barbieri seconded the motion and Chair Cochran called for a roll
call vote. The vote was as follows: Barbieri-yes, Harker-yes, Cochran-yes, Burgess-
yes, and Armstrong-yes. All Board Members voted in favor and the motion passed
unanimously.

3. Other Matters
There were no other matters.

4. Adjournment

At 8:28 PM Board Member Armstrong moved to adjourn the RDA meeting and move
to the noticed closed session. Board Member Burgess seconded the motion. City
Attorney Tracy Cowdell explained that a roll call vote is not normally needed for a
motion to adjourn. But since the motion that Board Member Armstrong made
included reconvening in a closed session, there does need to be a roll call vote. The
Open Meetings Act requires documenting who voted for a closed session and who
voted against it.

The vote was as follows: Armstrong-yes, Harker-yes, Burgess-yes, Barbieri-yes,
and Cochran-yes. All Board Members voted in favor and the motion passed
unanimously.

The meeting was adjourned at 8:29 PM.
