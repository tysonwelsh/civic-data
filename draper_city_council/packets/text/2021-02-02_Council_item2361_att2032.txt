BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map and Land Use Map Amendment for
approximately 0.29 acres located at approximately 11450 S 820 W (Exhibit B). The property is currently
zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that the property be rezoned from the RA2 zone to the RM1 zone
and the land use map be amended from Residential Low/Medium Density to Residential Medium/High
Density.

The property was subject to a rezone request on September 1, 2009. That rezone was for 18.56 acres
(Master Project), and included the Windsor Mill and Soccer City developments. Staff research has
uncovered an error on the legal descriptions used in the 2009 rezone. The legal descriptions provided in
the 2009 ordinance did not include the entire 18.56 acres, it left a small approximately 1.05 acre area in
the RA2 zone, including the 0.29 acres that is the subject of application. The 0.29 acres was supposed to
have been rezoned from RA2 to RM1 in 2009. The remainder of the 1.05 acres is either owned by UDOT
or was dedicated to the City as open space with the Windsor Mill Subdivision and has not been included
in this application.

On February 21, 2019 the Planning Commission approved a site plan on the property for 30 townhome
units and an office building. The Planning Commission then approved a preliminary subdivision plat on
July 25, 2019. An application for final plat was obtained on August 9, 2019. The applicant then obtained
an extension of approval on the site plan approval from the Planning Commission on April 9, 2020. This

Zoning. The property has been assigned the RA2 zoning classification, supporting approximately two
dwelling unit per acre (Exhibit D). According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020
the purpose of the RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings
and municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to promote
and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of
animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family dwellings,
protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.” The RM1 zoning abuts the subject
property on the east, and RA2 abuts on the south, and the A5 (Agricultural, 5 acre lot minimum) zone
abuts on the west.

The applicant is proposing to modify the properties zoning designation to the RM1 zone. According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RM1 zone is to “permit well
designed apartments, townhomes, twin homes, and condominiums at relatively high densities that are
appropriately buffered from and compatible with surrounding land uses.”

The 0.29 acres of property is part of the Windsor Mill Townhomes development. The final subdivision
plat for Windsor Mill Townhomes shows 6,200 square feet, or 0.142 acres, of the 0.29 acres of land being
dedicated to the City as public right-of-way. The remainder of the RA2 zoned area is a small strip of land
that runs along the west property line and encompasses a UDOT easement. This strip of land measures
10-feet to 23-feet wide and is utilized as common space within the townhome development.
