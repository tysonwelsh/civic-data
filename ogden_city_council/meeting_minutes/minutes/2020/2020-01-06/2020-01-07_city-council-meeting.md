    Administration of the Oath of Office for newly elected Mayor Michael P.
    Caldwell
            City Recorder Tracy Hansen administered the oath of office for newly elected Mayor Michael P. Caldwell.

    Comments from newly elected Mayor
              Mayor Caldwell thanked the great number of people that have been supportive of him over the past several years. In his
    time as Mayor, the City has received over 15 recognitions in national media sources. Most notable are being recognized as Most
    Charitable City in America, one of the Top Ten Boom Towns in America, one of the 25 Coolest Towns in America, One of the
    Best Places for Business and Careers (Forbes), and One of the Best Places in America to Raise a Family. He credited many City
    officials and community partners for making it possible for the City to receive these recognitions. He recalled the Ogden of his
    youth and cited significant changes over the years and when he thinks of all of the people who work for and in the City of Ogden,
    this recognizable change is a testament to them. He thanked the Council for being supportive of many of the efforts that led to
    recognizable change. One of the reasons that he chose to seek reelection to the office is Mayor is to continue to support the great
    employees that have done so much for this community and it is his great honor to be in a position to do so. Lastly, he thanked his
    friends and family for their support. He feels there are many great years ahead and he looks forward to continuing work on many
    significant projects that will further the City's success. He acknowledged that the Council and Administration do not always
    agree, but they work together constructively and respectively, which sets a good tone for the entire community.

Comments from newly elected Council members
          Council member Hyer stated it has been a pleasure to serve the citizens of Ogden for the last eight years; for 16 years
before that, he was a member of the Planning Commission and he has grown to love Ogden from the inside out. He was bom and
raised in Ogden and it is a place that he never wants to leave. The years of service have been very rewarding to him and he holds
them near and dear. Working with the Planning Commission, his fellow Council members, and City Administration has allowed
him to develop relationships that he treasures. He thanked his family, and especially his wife, for their support. Their effmts make
it possible for him to pursue public service opportunities. He looks forward to the next four years; working with City staff is very
rewarding and he is constantly in awe of their expe1tise; the City is fmtunate to have quality employees. He then stated that he
appreciates the constituency of the City. The many impromptu meetings and discussions he engages in with community members
are a prime example of democracy and he relishes those opportunities. He is open and willing to listen to any concerns any
resident may have and he is committed to be a positive influence to help correct any problem that is identified. He thanked
everyone for their support and he is grateful to have the oppmtunity to continue to serve.
          Council member Lopez stated that he also enjoys the encounters he has with community members on a regular basis. He
is an immigrant, having been born in Guadalajara, Mexico. He came to the United States when he was 19 years old and did not
speak any English. He also loves Ogden and it is a great joy to be here. He became a United States citizen in 2008 and his
extended family is going through the process of becoming a citizen now. Having been elected to the office of Council member is
one of the greatest honors of his life. He thanked everyone who supported him in his election to this position and he thanked the
employees of Ogden City; he has had many great interactions with many staff members and he is appreciative of the service they
provide to the community. He also thanked his fellow Council members and indicated he looks forward to another four years
serving with them.
          Chair Nadolski stated the decision to seek re-election was a very hard one to make; with the caustic tone of national
politics, he seeks to remind everyone that the same tone is not and should not be present in Ogden City. He stated the Mayor is
correct in his comments that the Council and Administration do not always agree on everything, but even public disagreements are
handled very professionally. He stated that he truly believes that the Council, Administration, and staff members do what they do
because they truly love what they are doing. The people serving this City love this City and that is why he chose to seek re-
election. He is proud to have been re-elected and he thanked the people of Ogden for having given him that opportunity. He looks
forward to four more years of growth, not only for him, but also for the City of Ogden.

Comments from other Council members
          Vice Chair Choberka thanked the Mayor and her fellow Council members for choosing to seek election to their
respective office; she has enjoyed working with them and looks forward to continuing to do so. She stated that in the time that she
has worked with them, she has leamed so much.
          Council member Stephens congratulated the Mayor and Council members on their re-election. During the December 17,
2019 meeting, Chief Administrative Officer Johnson noted that it was the last meeting of the decade and he has attended most of
the meetings for the past decade. Now, this is the start of anew decade of meetings and there are opportunities for new challenges
and perspectives; it is up to the Council and Administration to do what they can to benefit those who live here. This perspective
should permeate all work done by the Council and Mayor. He stated that he has known and worked with Council member Hyer
for a great number of years and they have worked to solve many of the problems present in Ogden City. Each Council member
adds a great deal of diversity to the City Council. He has enjoyed Chair Nadolski's leadership as Council Chair and he can feel the
compassion he has to serve his fellow man and community. He attended the inauguration of the Weber State University president
and he commented on the importance of not forgetting those who are downtrodden and he feels Council member Lopez emulates
that charge as a member of the City Council. He looks forward to continued work with the City Council and focusing on those
things that will benefit those who work, live, and play in the City.
          Council member White stated that people cannot chose their family and the same is true about being elected to a public
body. She likened the Council to a family and noted they cry, laugh, and get frustrated with one another. But, at the end of the day,
they all share a common goal of working to benefit the community. Each Council member brings something different to the
family and she is happy to be able to continue to work with them.
          Council member Blair stated the list of accolades read by the Mayor is very impressive and is the biproduct of the work
of a lot of people. Anyone with ties to the community had a hand in making Ogden City successful. He believes the fact that
people seek re-election to their respective office is an example of dedication to supporting the City's movement in a positive
direction. He is honored to be able to work alongside with his fellow Council members and the Mayor and he is in awe of the
individual talents they each bring to their position. They all work hard to find solutions to problems and make the most out of the
limited resources of the City. He is excited about the direction the City is moving in and the opportunities on the horizon.

Closing remarks from Council Chair
         Chair Nadolski thanked those in attendance who care so much about the community. He thanked them for their support
and for being concemed about the future of Ogden City. He appreciates Council member White's comparison of the City Council
to a family and indicated he feels that is very true.
