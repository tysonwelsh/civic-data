City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On September 3, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1620,
amending the text of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding renaming the Diversity
and Inclusion Advisory Committee to the Community Engagement Committee. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 9th day of September 2024. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
