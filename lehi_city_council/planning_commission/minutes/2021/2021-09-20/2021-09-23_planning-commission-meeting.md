2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Diane Myer’s request for Conditional Use approval
of the Myers Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit located at 2269 North 600 West
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Kailynne Osborne has concerns with the application and the flag lot with the property being
divided. Due to her concerns with dust noise and vehicles going back and forth daily, as well as
privacy, she asked that if it is approved that it would be with conditions, such as adding a hard


surface. She is concerned that if the surface of the driveway is gravel that it will create dust and
noise. She would also like to see some screening.

Robby Myers stated they want to make sure there isn't dust, and they want all the neighbors to be
happy. They can add gravel in needed or paved if required. He stated that it is difficult to make
soundproof fences.

Dianne Myers said they considered a barrier and doing some privacy things and they could
heighten the fence. They want to landscape to provide more privacy and will add some
improvements to make it look more like a home.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve the Myers Detached Accessory Dwelling
    Unit located at 2269 North 600 West; with the findings that the proposed use will be located
    and conducted in a manner in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General
    Plan and the purposes of this Code will not be detrimental or injurious to property or
    improvements in the vicinity, that they provide some form of privacy in regards to the fences,
    such as an increase to 6 feet, and improve the driveway to mitigate the dust concerns, and
    encourage the applicant to extend the driveway. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner
    Ellis seconded.

Suggest that it's too nebulas in suggesting a fence. It's subjective to say change to require the fence
to be 6 foot privacy fence.

Commissioner Nielsen re-opened the public hearing.

Doug Osborne, they built the house 150 feet off the road for privacy. If they need a fence it needs
to be another fence. The road is a nuisance. He does not want anything hung on the fence and
wants the applicant to install a second fence. He stated a fire truck would have a hard time turning
down the road.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve the Myers Detached Accessory Dwelling
    Unit located at 2269 North 600 West; with the findings that the proposed use will be located
    and conducted in a manner in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General
    Plan and the purposes of this Code will not be detrimental or injurious to property or
    improvements in the vicinity, that they provide some form of privacy in regards to the fences
    such as an increase to 6 feet and improve the driveway to mitigate the dust concerns, and
    encourage the applicant to extend the driveway, to install a double privacy fence to mitigate
    the dust and take into account the sound and problems that it might provide. Also verify with
    the Fire Department that there are no concerns with the width of the driveway. Including all
    DRC comments. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. Motion passed unanimously.

2.6) Public hearing and recommendation of X Development’s request for review of the 2100
North subdivision concept, a 66-lot development located at 2100 North 2300 West.
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item. There has been some adjustment to lots
due to the 2100 North connection.

Ross Dinsdale stated UDOT has verbally agreed that this can work and is supportive of the
applicant. There is a 10-foot trail on the West side of the road that will transition to the existing
sidewalk.

The applicant, Eric Towner with X Development, was present. They would have a dog park near
the train tracks so the open space can be utilized. The 2100 North right in-right out has been
widened and includes a 20 foot driveway requirement so there will be more line of site. The
location of open space shall be well planned and walkable and connected. He stated the dog park,
and the pocket park would be separated.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Angie Lethbridge would like to see traffic mitigation for the street that goes to 2100 North. She
would like to see another connection to 2300 West. She is concerned about speeding and heavy
traffic, especially with an elementary school directly across the street. She also stated the lots need
to be smaller. She was not in agreeance with the density bonus.


Brian McQuiston stated he agrees with Angie’s comments. He would did not want to see the
density bonus increased. Making sure the open space was incorporated in the plan. He asked that
the Commissioners keep in mind that there was an elementary school located directly across the
street from the proposed development. He recommended a traffic calming device for the area.

Commissioner Nielsen noted there were two comments made online that were not in favor of the
development.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of X Development’s request
    for review of the 2100 North subdivision concept, a 66-lot development located at 2100
    North 2300 West; with the findings that the proposed concept meets the requirements for a
    Planned Unit Development listed in the Lehi City Development Code, is consistent with the
    Lehi City Development Code and conforms to the goals and policies of the General Plan, that
    the proposed concept does not affect the health, safety, welfare, and morals of the City.
    Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Hereth seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. Motion passed unanimously.

2.10) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a
comprehensive update to the Land Use Element of the General Plan including the land use
map and text.
Sierra Pierson presented information on the agenda item.

The Commissioners discussed the areas and land use map. The color and text was discussed for
clarifying purposes.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing and acknowledged the online comments.

Virginia Ault and Julie Gillman. They stated they are requesting a zone change to 1/4 acre lots
where it is zoned at 1/2 acre lots. They would like to know why it can’t be re-zoned and feel like
they should be able to zone in the 1/4 acre.

Brian McQuiston likes the idea that the general plan amendment being done once a year. He
would like the Commissioners and Council to consider the criteria for that.

Eric Towner talked about their property along 2100 North and 2300 West and being consistent
with zoning. It may make sense for their property to be zoned commercial instead of
neighborhood commercial.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to recommend approval of Lehi City’s request for
    review of a comprehensive update to the Land Use Element of the General Plan
    including the land use map and text; with the recommendation that parcel properties that
    are divided on the land use map where owners can opt for either designation on the whole of
    their property, and that the Ault property north of Pioneer be altered to LDR and that the
    commercial next to the Ault property be extended to give greater continuity from one zone to
    the next. Including all DRC comments. Add a finding that there were two years of study put
    into the General Plan and the land uses are appropriate for the city and various people in the
    city as shown on the map. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. Motion passed unanimously.
