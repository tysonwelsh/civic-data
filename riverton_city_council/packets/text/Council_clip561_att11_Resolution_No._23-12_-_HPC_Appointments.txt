                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-12

  A RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE HISTORIC PRESERVATION
                           COMMISSION

        WHEREAS, on June 14, 2016, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 16-11
which provides for the preservation of historic structures and other assets located within Riverton
City; and

       WHEREAS, Administration of Ordinance No. 16-11 requires appointment of a Historic
Preservation Commission; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the following volunteer
appointments of Noah Crovo and Brooke Glaittli; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Noah Crovo and
Brooke Glaittli are residents of Riverton City, and are willing and capable of serving on the
Historic Preservation Commission;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointments of Noah Crovo
          and Brooke Glaittli to serve on the Historic Preservation Commission, which term shall
          end on January 31, 2026.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 7th day of
February 2023 by the following vote:
