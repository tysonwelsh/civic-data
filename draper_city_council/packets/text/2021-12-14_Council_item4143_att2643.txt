                                   Recognition
                     Draper City is a Bicycle Friendly Community

Draper City is now recognized by the League of American Bicyclists as a bronze level
Bicycle Friendly Community (BFC)!

Earlier this year, Draper City applied for the League of American Bicyclists’
prestigious Bicycle Friendly Community award. It has long been a goal of this city to
be a recognized leader for the work our city staff, elected officials and local citizens
have put in over the years to make our community more accessible by bike. Today,
we’re proud to be part of the nearly 500 communities across the United States that
are transforming America one Bicycle Friendly Community at a time.

“Biking has the power to transform our nation economically, environmentally and
socially and it’s so important that communities like Draper have laid the groundwork
over several years to make biking a safe, accessible option for all. With many more
Americans turning to biking as a fun way to get outdoors and as a necessary
transportation option, we must keep the momentum to build a Bicycle Friendly
America for everyone going,” said Bill Nesper, executive director of the League of
American Bicyclists.

“This Bicycle Friendly Community award is the culmination of years of work put in by
Draper City and its citizen advocates for better biking. This award round, Draper joins
57 new and renewing Bicycle Friendly Communities in the movement toward
healthier, more sustainable and connected places. As we gear up for what we hope
to be a transformational new year for biking investment, we’re grateful that Draper
City and communities like it are pedaling alongside us to power the better bicycling
movement.”
