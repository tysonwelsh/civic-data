                                       LOBBYING SERVICES


Riverton City will consider statements of qualifications from lobbyists for lobbying services.
Lobbyist will be required to represent Riverton City interests before the Utah Legislature for up
to five years, beginning immediately upon execution of an agreement for lobbying services.

Submit a resume outlining skills, qualifications, experience and areas of expertise. The City may
award a contract based solely upon the merits of the initial submittal, without an oral

statement of qualifications received and to waive any informality or technicality in any statement
