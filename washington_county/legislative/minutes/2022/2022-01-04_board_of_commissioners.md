OPEN AND PUBLIC MEETINGS TRAINING

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales conducted an annual training regarding the open and
public meeting requirements as well as other statutory obligations.

Ms. Hales explained that the purpose of the Open and Public Meetings Act (OPMA) is for the
State, its agencies, and its political subdivisions to take actions openly and conduct their
deliberations openly. Meetings are considered the convening of a public body for the purpose of
discussing, receiving comments from the public, or acting upon a matter over which the public
body has jurisdiction or advisory power.
