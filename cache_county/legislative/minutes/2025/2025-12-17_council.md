                  0:19 Everyone in attendance introduced themselves. 2:43 Joseph provided overview of Bill and the requirements.
                  Chair Sandi Goodlander asked if the fee would be set based on the square footage of the home or property. Joseph
                  answered the structure. He explained the cycle for the fees and how the costs are allocated after they are sent to
                  the state. Councilmember David Erickson commented the County will be blamed for the fees. 10:20
                  Councilmember Nolan Gunnell asked for clarification of insurance on property then elaborated his concern for
                  cabins near Hardware Ranch that are on private property and not accessible to get footage. Dallin said the fees
                  would apply to high risk and the FR40 would keep the structures far enough apart. Joseph added density was a
                  factor. 15:20 Chair Sandi Goodlander introduced Casey Snyder virtually and asked if the county had the map
                  already from the state. Chief Brady George said yes but there was some misalignment to be grandfathered and
                  identified still. Joseph explained there was a high risk map and a WUI map the county would enforce. Casey added
                  the only lots that would receive the fee were the high risk areas. Councilmember David Erickson asked about
                  commercial and where WUI would be. Casey answered the fees are structure based and Dallin answered the WUI
                  areas would be similar to Ant Flat, Scare Canyon, and Beaver Mountain. Casey elaborated the logistics behind
                  insurance. 24:38 Councilmember David Erickson clarified the assessments would be risk mitigation and said simply
                  being neighbors to the forest is a risk. Casey answered it would be up to the landowners to protect their property.
                  Chair Sandi Goodlander asked if the state would help with the assessments frontload. Casey responded the state
                  would be responsible for it all however the county could do the assessments for return in matched time and
                  resources. Chair Sandi Goodlander asked about the timeline for notice of fees. Casey answered collected taxes
                  would be on the tax bill for next year. 29:23 Joseph added they could help with the language in the notice.
                  Councilmember Nolan Gunnell asked for an example of a lot assessment. Joseph answered vegetation surrounding
                  the property and the home construction materials and briefly described the software used. Dallin added since Cache
                  county already adopted the WUI code there shouldn’t be any drastic changes to structures. Councilmember David
                  Erickson asked if grazing would be allowed to mitigate forage. Casey answered the state doesn’t specify that. 35:46
                  Chair Sandi Goodlander asked if the state had provided their map yet. Casey answered there was a URAP Map that
                  would be fairly close. Chair Sandi Goodlander asked what URAP meant. Joseph answered a public mapping system
                  with a layer that gave structure exposure scores. Chair Sandi Goodlander commented she felt more comfortable
                  and it appeared to set the homeowners in a better position if there was a fire. Councilmember David Erickson
                  voiced his favor for the discussion. Councilmember Mark Hurd asked what the next step would be when the map
                  was available and the role council would play. Joseph answered once the map was received the properties within
                  the boundary line would be identified with no requirement from council. Chief George said his concern would be
            structures built before the code. Councilmember Barbara Tidwell asked who would receive the map. Joseph
            answered it would be publicly available after the high risk areas are determined. 40:33 Councilmember Mark Hurd
            suggested a spot on the agenda in the future to provide an update. Chief George asked how often the map would
            be revised. Joseph answered annually. Chair Sandi Goodlander asked about the process to get the information to
            the Treasurer. Councilmember David Erickson said this would be its own line item. 43:24 Chair Goodlander opened
            for comments. None. Council discussed. Councilmember Nolan Gunnell asked if an area is assigned WUI high risk
            but the applicant does not provide any mitigation who does the risk fall under. Chief George answered language
            would be included for who would be responsible. 47:31 Councilmember Barbara Tidwell exited Chambers.
            Councilmember Nolan Gunnell commented of a past fire near duck creek and the fuel it received from sage brush.
            Dallin said projects were going on in the county to reduce that fuel near scare canyon and beaver mountain. Chief
            George added services for mitigation can be matched. Councilmember David Erickson said the forest needed to be

position on a board, committee, or similar body whose membership is appointed by the county
shall be by the County Executive, with the advice and consent of the county legislative body,
and;

WHEREAS, Pursuant to Cache County Organic Act, Article 4, Section 4.04(e), the County
Executive shall appoint persons to all offices which are to be filled by appointment with and
upon the advice and consent of the County Council, and;

WHEREAS, Pursuant to Cache County Code 2.08.030(E), the County Executive, as chief
executive of the county, shall have the power and it shall be his duty to appoint persons to all
offices which are to be filled by appointment with and upon the advice and consent of the
County Council, and;

WHEREAS, Pursuant to Cache County Code 2.08.060(A), the County Executive “shall appoint
all officers and heads of departments upon the advice and consent of the County Council, the
same being approved by the affirmative vote of four (4) council members,” and;

WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code §17-62-203(5)(b), references in any statute to the
“governing body” or the “board of county commissioners” of the county, in the county
executive-council form of county government, means the county executive, with respect to
executive functions, duties, and powers.
