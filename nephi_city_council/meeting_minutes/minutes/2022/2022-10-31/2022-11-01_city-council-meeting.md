JUAB COUNTY COMMISSIONERS/CITY COUNCIL PUBLIC SAFETY COMPLEX DISCUSSED:

    Juab County Commissioner Marvin Kenison and Commissioner Clinton Painter discussed with the
council about the county’s need to build a new jail. Mr. Kenison stated that the current county jail
facility does not pass the physical facility inspection and hasn’t for some time. He stated that he does
not know where the 50-million-dollar figure came from that has be thrown out there for the cost of the
proposed public safety facility. He said that the feasibility study will determine the cost.
  Councilor Memmott stated that we should work together because it would not be wise to pay twice
for similar facilities with duplicate amenities. The council and the commissioners agreed to look at the
feasibility study when it is completed and make more informed decisions.

EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR ABBY IVORY REPORTED ON THE GREATER NEPHI CHAMBER OF COMMERCE:

   Abby Ivory, Greater Nephi Chamber Executive Director, reported to the council about the past year’s
activities. Ms. Ivory stated that the chamber has rebranded and received the IRS 501(c) (6) non-profit
status. They held 12 luncheons, held an Excellence in Business Awards ceremony along with 10 grand
opening ribbon cutting ceremonies. She said that the chamber has changed their fee schedule with
different tiers of membership. The council thanked Ms. Ivory for all her hard work and expressed their
excitement for the new face of the chamber.

FIVE STAR INVESTMENT AGREEMENT APPROVED:

    City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council that Five Star Investments, the developer
for the Cedar Ridge Planned Unit Development whose plat B had previously been approved through the
subdivision process, the actual building footprint needs to conform to the city’s planning codes as well.
The design of the buildings they are currently constructing did not meet the city’s setback requirements
due to the configuration of the property boundaries. This meant that the developer would need to
either change the building design or acquire an appropriate amount of neighboring property to meet the
setback requirements. The developer chose the latter option and would now like to proceed forward
with an amended plat.
   The developer had confirmed that the property needed to meet setback requirements had been
acquired and desires to move forward with the construction of the buildings while the amended plat is
making its way through the planning process. The amendments are so minor, and the intent of the code
is being met, the city has agreed to allow the developer to move forward with the construction process.
The draft agreement ensures that the developer agrees to bear all risk, including the outcome of not
receiving a certificate of occupancy, as the amended plat is being reviewed and considered.
    Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Five Star Boundary Line
Indemnification Agreement. Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

NATURAL GAS MASTER PLAN ADOPTED:

    Mr. Atkinson updated the council that Sunrise Engineering had completed a Natural Gas Master Plan
for the city. The plan will help to know how to improve the gas system as Nephi City and east Juab
County grow. Nephi City is the agent for the JRDA to provide gas service to Nephi City distribution
system as well as county residents and businesses in east Juab County. The plan separates it s
recommendations into project for the distribution system and also for the transmission system.
  Gas Department Superintendent Drew McPherson and Public Works Director Kyle Marchant
answered questions on different potential projects that were included in the master plan.
   Councilor Ostler moved to adopt the Natural Gas Master Plan. Councilor Memmott seconded the
motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
