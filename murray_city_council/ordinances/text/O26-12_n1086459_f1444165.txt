ORDINANCE NO. 26-12

AN ORDINANCE RENUMBERING SECTIONS OF CHAPTER 2.32, AND
AMENDING SECTIONS 2.32.020, 2.32.040, 2.32.050, AND 2.32.070
AND ENACTING SECTION 2.32.060 OF THE MURRAY CITY
MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO THE PUBLIC WORKS
DEPARTMENT.

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MURRAY CITY MUNICIPAL COUNCIL:

Section 1. Purpose. The purpose of this Ordinance is to renumber sections of
Chapter 2.32 and amend sections 2.32.020, 2.32.040, 2,32,050, and 2.32.070 and
enact section 2.32.060 of the Murray City Municipal Code relating to the organization of
the Public Works Department.

Section 2. Renumbering sections of chapter 2.32 and amendment of sections
2.32.020, 2.32.040, 2.32.050, and 2.32.070 of the Murray City Municipal Code.
Renumbering chapter 2.32 and amendments to sections 2.32.020, 2.32.040, 2.32.050,
and 2.32.070 of the Murray City Municipal Code, related to amending the Public Works
Department, shall be amended to read as follows:

CHAPTER 2.32
PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT

SECTION:

2.32.010: Established; Role And Mission

2.32.020: Divisions Of The Public Works Department
2.32.030: Engineering Services; City Engineer
2.32.040: Streets, Stormwater And Solid Waste Division
2.32.050: Water Division

2.32.060: Wastewater Division

2.32.070: Fleet Services Division

2.32.020: DIVISIONS OF THE PUBLIC WORKS DEPARTMENT:

In addition to the Office of the Director of Public Works, the department shall also
consist of the Engineering Services Division, the Streets, Stormwater, and Solid Waste


Division, the Water Division, Wastewater Division and the Fleet Services Division, as
described in this chapter. (Ord. 18-06)

2.32.030: ENGINEERING SERVICES; CITY ENGINEER:

A. The Engineering Services Division of the Public Works Department is created.
The division shall be directed by the City Engineer, who reports to the Public Works
Director. The City Engineer shall be a registered professional engineer of the State of
Utah.

B. The City Engineering Division is responsible for:

1. The review, supervision and acceptance of all engineering and construction
work required by or for the City, except as otherwise provided in this Code;

2. Coordination and supervision of all construction work done within the public
rights-of-way of the City;

3. Keeping records of public improvements as prescribed by State Statute,
including, but not limited to, maps, plans, plats, profiles, drawings, final estimates,
specifications and contracts. These functions may be delegated to other departments or
divisions upon the approval of the City Engineer and the Public Works Director; and

4. Providing minimum standards and practical safeguards against threat to life and
limb. (Ord. 18-06)

2.32.040: STREETS, STORMWATER AND SOLID WASTE DIVISION:

A. The Streets, Stormwater, and Solid Waste Division of the Public Works
Department is created. The division shall be directed by the Streets and Stormwater
Superintendent, who reports to the Public Works Director. The division is responsible
for:

1. The construction, operation and maintenance of rights-of-way;

2. The maintenance and repair of all storm drains, ditches, flumes, and culverts
within the City rights-of-way; and

3. Supervision of solid waste collection and disposal within the service area. (Ord.
18-06)

2.32.050: WATER DIVISION:

A. The Water Division of the Public Works Department is created. The division shall
be directed by the Water Superintendent, who reports to the Public Works Director. The
division is responsible for:


1. The acquisition, transportation, storage, treatment and distribution of all raw and
potable water for the City and its designated service areas;

2. Reading utility meters. (Ord. 19-05)

2.32.070: FLEET SERVICES DIVISION:

A. The Fleet Services Division of the Public Works Department is created. The
division shall be directed by the Fleet Manager, who reports to the Public Works
Director. The division is responsible for:

1. The purchasing, registration, maintenance, repair, and disposal of City vehicles;
and

2. The supervision of fuel and fueling stations for City vehicles. (Ord. 18-06)

Section 3. Enactment of section 2.32.060 of the Murray City Municipal Code.
Section 2.32.060 of the Murray City Municipal Code, creating a Wastewater Division,
shall be enacted to read as follows:

2.32.060: WASTEWATER DIVISION:

A. The Wastewater Division of the Public Works Department is created. The division
shall be directed by the Wastewater Superintendent, who reports to the Public Works
Director. The division is responsible for:

1. The collection and disposal of all wastewater and sewage generated within the
City or its designated service areas; and

2. The maintenance and repair of all sewer collection system assets within the
designated service area.

Section 4. Effective date. This Ordinance shall take effect upon first
publication.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Murray City Municipal Council on
