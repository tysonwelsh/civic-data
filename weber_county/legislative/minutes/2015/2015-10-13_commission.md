                                                 MINUTES
                        OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WEBER COUNTY
                                    Tuesday, October 13, 2015 - 10:00 a.m.
                           Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-7(1)(d), the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all citizens
who appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or
purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

COMMISSIONERS: Kerry W. Gibson, Chair, and James Ebert. Matthew G Bell was excused.
OTHERS PRESENT: Ricky D. Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Chris Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and
Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
A. WELCOME – Chair Gibson
B. INVOCATION – Commissioner Ebert
C. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Ricky Hatch
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY – Chair Gibson

E. CONSENT ITEMS:
   1. Purchase orders for $152,048.65
   2. Warrants #325808-#325938 for $541,070.21
   3. Minutes for the meeting held on October 6, 2015
   4. Surplus three mobile stages and a 1963 12-ton asphalt roller from the Golden Spike Event Center
      Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the consent items; Chair Gibson seconded.
      Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

F. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. THREE RIGHT OF WAY CONTRACTS FOR THE 3500 WEST PROJECT:
        Wesley & Sharee Robison, Parcel 71 – Contract C2015-365
        Val Carver, Parcel 42 – Contract C2015-366
        Jared Prince, Parcel 52 – Contract C2015-367

         Dana Shuler, of County Engineering, presented these standard contracts.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the above listed right-of-way contracts, C2015-365 through
         367, for the 3500 West project; Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    2. TWO RIGHT OF WAY CONTRACTS FOR THE 12TH STREET PROJECT:
        Anthony & Eliza Lyons, Parcel 109 – Contract C2015-368
        Egan Farm Service, Parcel 166 – Contract C2015-369

         Dana Shuler, of County Engineering, presented these standard contracts.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve the above listed right-of-way contracts, C2015-368 through
         369, for the 12th Street project; Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    3. CONTRACT WITH GRANT MACKAY COMPANY FOR DEMOLITION OF A PROPERTY – CONTRACT
       C2015-370

         Dana Shuler, of County Engineering, stated that this is for demolition of a building on Combe Road
         on property that the county acquired for the Skyline Drive road project.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-370 with Grant Mackay Company for the
         demolition of a property for the Skyline Drive project; Chair Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
    5. COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE UTAH STATE UNIVERSITY EXTENSION SERVICE FOR A NEW
       CONTRACTED FINANCE EDUCATOR POSITION – CONTRACT C2015-371

       Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, presented this contract renewal outlining the
       significant change, which is that it will be a 4-year rather than a 1–year contract. It now includes a
       provision allowing the parties to terminate the contract upon 60 days notice. Commissioner Ebert
       noted that there is no change to the annual cost.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-371 with the Utah State University
       Extension Service for a new contracted finance educator position; Chair Gibson seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    4. INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH HOOPER WATER IMPROVEMENT DISTRICT FOR
       ELECTION SERVICES – CONTRACT C2015-372

       Ryan Cowley, County Elections Director, presented this standard contract. He noted that the District
       had a couple write-in candidates and the county will conduct the election services. Mr. Cowley
       addressed Commissioner Ebert’s question on the contractual billing.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve Contract C2015-372 with Hooper Water Improvement
       District for election services; Chair Gibson seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

G. PUBLIC COMMENTS: None

H. ASSIGN PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE & THOUGHT OF THE DAY FOR TUESDAY, OCTOBER 20, 2015, 6 P.M.

I. ADJOURN
      Commissioner Ebert moved to adjourn at 10:23 a.m.; Chair Gibson seconded.
      Commissioner Ebert – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
