   2. ZONE CHANGE (ZC) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      A. Consider a request to change the zoning from R-1-10 (residential single-family, 10,000 sq ft
         minimum lot size) to PDR (Planned Development Residential) on approximately 159.18 acres, R-1-7
         (Residential single-family 7,000 sq ft minimum lot size) on approximately 69.95 acres, R-1-10
         (residential single-family, 10,000 sq ft minimum lot size) on approximately 77.74 acres, PDC
         (Planned Development Commercial) on 61.94 acres, C2 (Highway Commercial) on approximately
         7.63 acres, and M-1 (Manufacturing) on approximately 65.04 acres. The applicant is also requesting
         the resort overlay on areas 3 and 4 (approximately 34 acres) to allow for nightly rentals. The

         property is located generally east of White Dome Nature Conservancy and Sage Canyon/White
         Sands subdivisions and north of Southern Parkway. The applicant is CRS Development and the
         representative is Brett Burgess. The project will be known as Southern Hills East. Case No. 2021-

         Dan Boles Presented the following:

         Dan Boles – You may remember this coming before you in part last year. The City Council had said
         they would like to see the entire area planned out. Dan described the general plan of the area. All of
         the zoning categories correspond with the general plan. The entire property is zoned R-1-10 today,
         which has traditionally been the holding zone. They are proposing 9 PD-R’s, they are proposing a
         portion to remain R-1-10, they are proposing 5 manufacturing zones, 3 R-1-7 zones and then several
         areas of commercial. Areas 1, 2 and 10 are asking for specific approval of the site, the rest is just for
         initial zoning. Area number 1 is 132 units, 9 per acre, they are all 4 plex. They do meet code for
         amenities and parking. Area number 2 is 145 dwelling units, they are meeting all the parking
         requirements, they are not asking for any waivers as far as parking goes, 8.79 dwelling units per
         acre, single family homes. This is number 10 it is 123 units, single family homes. They are asking
         for the resort overlay in areas 3 & 4. Our concern with that is that there isn’t a large amount of stock
         out there now for long term ownership and long-term rentals. We don’t feel that is what we need
         now as a City. There is a use table provided for the PD-C zones. They will have the uses that we
         allow in our City Code for both the C-2 and the M-1.

         Commissioner Fisher – Is the C-2 at their request or the request of the City?

         Brett Burgess – It took me 6 months to get this programmed in my mind. There is still a lot that
         needs to be brought in but part of that is partnerships that I don’t control. I didn’t bring area 21 in
         because it needs a general plan change. That is the old Sunroc gravel pit. There is standing water in
         there, it’s a pretty big hole. In trying to figure out reclamation out there, we have several wells that
         we use to water some grasses and trees. It makes a lot of sense to put in secondary water, we feel
         like we can put that in pretty quick. Water is one of our biggest concerns. The thought for that area,
         is something like the fire rock lake at Ivins, do something that is a native park. I think we could put
         neighborhood commercial in there by the lake. We want to keep our outdoor rec and enhance the
         area going into Little Valley. Brett described what he thought the R-1-10 area would be for the
         future zone change. We are requesting M-1 near the Ft. Pierce area. Brett described the areas that
         Dan talked about and why they chose the uses they chose. We feel like people will be able to buy
         their first homes in the detached Townhouse, then graduate up to the R-1-7 single family and if you
         are an empty nester then you can do it in the reverse. I got a call from Dan and John a couple weeks
         ago, there were some concerns with the nightly rental area I was proposing. At one point we had it
         zoned as high density. I couldn’t visualize that, so I came back in and general planned it to MDR.
         Discussed reasons to have short term rentals. I think we can get the commercial off the ground
         sooner than we thought. I want to do something different than Desert Color; they have a lot of
         businesses that need to be visible and highway commercial. I do feel like there is a little call for the
         highway commercial. But I want to do more sidewalk commercial. Continued to describe the feel
         of the projects. We think the short-term rentals will make that neighborhood commercial successful.
