Minutes of the Regular Session of the Lehi Local Building Authority held Tuesday, December
12, 2023, at 6:20 p.m. at the Lehi City Administration Building, 153 North 100 East, Lehi, Utah.

Members Present:        Mark Johnson, Chairman
                        Paige Albrecht, Member
                        Mike Southwick, Member
                        Chris Condie, Member
                        Paul Hancock, Member
                        Katie Koivisto, Member


Others Present: Jason Walker, City Administrator; Ryan Wood, City Attorney, Marlin Eldred,
Economic Development Director; Dean Lundell, Finance Director; Teisha Wilson, City Recorder.

1. Welcome and Roll Call
   Chairman Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all members were present.

2. Regular Agenda
   2.1) Budget Amendment for the Local Building Authority Budget for FY 2024.

   a. Public Hearing. Mayor Johnson opened the public hearing. There were no comments.
   b. Consideration of Resolution #L2023-02 amending the current FY2024 budget for the Local
      Building Authority of Lehi.

   Motion:     Mr. Southwick moved to approve Resolution #L2023-02, approval of the Lehi City
               Local Building Authority Budget for FY 2024. Ms. Koivisto seconded the motion.

   Roll Call Vote: Mr. Hancock, Yes; Mr. Southwick, Yes; Mr. Condie, Yes; Ms. Koivisto, Yes;
   Ms. Albrecht, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


3. Adjournment
   With no further business to come before the Local Building Authority at this time, Mr.
   Southwick moved to adjourn the meeting. Mr. Hancock seconded the motion. The motion
   passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:25 p.m.
