                                  ORDINANCE NO. 1604

         AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL LAND USE MAP OF DRAPER CITY
         BY REMOVING FROM THE MAP APPROXIMATELY 1,035.49 ACRES OF
         PROPERTY LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 1950 EAST 16365 SOUTH WITHIN
         DRAPER CITY, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE CITY INITIATED DRAPER-LEHI
         LAND USE MAP AMENDMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a General Plan and Land
Use Map to guide the future development within the City; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City and Lehi City adjusted their common municipal boundary line
to remove approximately 1,035.49 acres of property located at approximately 1950 East
16365 South as described in Section 1 below (the “Property”) from Draper City’s municipal
boundary and placed it in the municipal boundary of Lehi City; and

         WHEREAS, it is necessary to remove the Property from the Draper City Land Use Map
as it is no longer located within Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed land use change set forth herein has been reviewed by the
Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been
held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to
the Land Use Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Land Use Map of Draper
City, and the City Council has found the proposed land use change to be consistent with the
City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

       Section 1.   Land Use Amendment. The following described real property located
at approximately 1950 East 16365 South within Draper City, Utah County, State of Utah,
previously designated Community Commercial and Residential Hillside Low Density as
shown on the Draper City Land Use Map, as depicted in Exhibit “A” hereto, is hereby removed
from the Land Use Map:

Parcels: 11-015-0007, 11-015-0002, 11-015-0008, 11-030-0024, 11-030-0025, 11-030-0018
