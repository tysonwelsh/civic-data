RESOLUTION NO. 2024-09-01 DATE 09-09-2024

A RESOLUTION OF THE MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL OF THE

CITY OF KEARNS, UTAH APPROVING, SOLELY FOR THE

LIMITED PURPOSES OF SECTION 147(F) OF THE INTERNAL

REVENUE CODE AND SECTION 163.01, FLORIDA STATUTES,

THE ISSUANCE BY THE CAPITAL TRUST AUTHORITY OF ITS

REVENUE BONDS, IN ONE OR MORE SERIES OF TAX-EXEMPT

QUALIFIED 501(C)(3) BONDS OR TAXABLE BONDS, PURSUANT

TO A PLAN OF FINANCE, IN AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL

AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $275,000,000, FOR THE PURPOSE OF

FINANCING THE FACILITIES LOCATED IN THE CITY OF

KEARNS, UTAH

WHEREAS, the Mayor and City Council of the City of Kearns, Utah (the “City”),
has been informed that the Capital Trust Authority (“CTA”) (a legal entity duly created
and a public agency duly organized and validly existing under the laws of the State of
Florida, which is empowered under the Constitution and other laws of the State of Florida
to issue revenue bonds for the purpose of providing funds to finance or refinance, including
through reimbursement, projects such as the Project (defined below)) proposes to issue a
principal amount not exceeding $275,000,000 of one or more series of tax-exempt
“qualified 501(c)(3) bonds” (as defined in section 145 of the Internal Revenue Code of
1986 (the “Code’)) and/or taxable bonds (the “Bonds”) as part of a plan for financing the
Project (defined below); and

WHEREAS, the Bonds will be issued to provide funds to be loaned by the CTA to
Alpha Lifestyle Partners, Inc., a Delaware non-profit corporation and an organization
described in Section 501(c)(3) of the Code (“Alpha”), and/or one or more related and/or
affiliated entities of Alpha (each of which will be an entity whose separate existence from
its sole member, Alpha, is disregarded for tax purposes) (together with Alpha, the
“Borrowers”), for the purpose of financing, including through reimbursement, (i) the
acquisition, improvement, and/or equipping of the Senior Living Facilities (as defined
below); (ii) the renovation and improvement of the Senior Living Facilities, if deemed
necessary or desirable; (iii) the funding of a debt service reserve fund for the Bonds, if
deemed necessary or desirable; (iv) the funding of capitalized interest for the Bonds, if
deemed necessary or desirable; (v) the payment of certain costs of issuing the Bonds; and
(vi) the payment or funding of certain working capital expenditures relating to the Senior
Living Facilities and approved by the CTA (collectively, the “Project”).

WHEREAS, the Project includes acquisition, renovation, furnishing and equipping
of various existing senior living and memory care facilities in various jurisdictions in
multiple states (collectively, the “Senior Living Facilities”), including in the City (the “City
Facilities”); and

WHEREAS, the City Facilities consist of an existing senior living facility known
as Summit Senior Living, located at 5524 West 6200 South, Kearns, Salt Lake County,
Utah 84118, including related facilities, fixtures, furnishings and equipment (maximum
principal amount of Bonds: $41,250,000); and

WHEREAS, the initial owners of the Senior Living Facilities will be the Borrowers,

as applicable, or an affiliate thereof; and
WHEREAS, in order to finance the costs of the Project from the proceeds of the
Bonds on a tax-exempt basis, it is necessary that a public hearing be held and the issuance
of the Bonds by the CTA be approved for the purposes of Section 147(f) of the Code and
Section 163.01, Florida Statutes, as amended (the “Interlocal Act”); and

WHEREAS, the Borrowers and the CTA have requested the City provide limited
approval of the issuance of the Bonds by the CTA for purposes of Section 147(f) of the
Code and the Interlocal Act; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of the City is the elected legislative body of the City,
and the City has jurisdiction over the entire area in which the City Facilities are located;
and

WHEREAS, the City Council has determined, based solely upon representations

and information provided by the Borrowers and without any independent investigation or
research by the City, that the issuance of the Bonds by the CTA to finance or refinance the
Project: (i) is appropriate to the needs and circumstances of, and will make contributions
to the economic growth of the City, (ii) will provide or preserve gainful employment, (iii)
will promote commerce within the City, and (iv) will serve a public purpose by advancing
the economic prosperity, and the general welfare of the City and its people by providing
facilities performing the function of the Project; and

WHEREAS, based solely upon representations and information provided by the

Borrowers, the City will be able to cope satisfactorily with the impact of the financing of
the Project related to the City Facilities by the Borrowers and will be able to provide, or
cause to be provided when needed, the public facilities, including utilities and public
services, that will be necessary for the operation, repair, and maintenance of the City
Facilities and on account of any increases in population or other circumstances resulting
therefrom.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT,

1. Approval of Recitals. The matters set forth in the “WHEREAS” clauses above
are hereby ratified, adopted, and affirmed.

2. Approval for Purposes of Section 147(f) of the Code. For the purposes of
Section 147(f) of the Code, the Mayor and City Council of the City hereby
approve the issuance of the Bonds by the CTA in one or more series, in an
aggregate principal amount not exceeding $275,000,000 (no more than
$41,250,000 of which will be allocated to the City Facilities).

3. Approval for Purposes of the Interlocal Act. For the purposes of the
Interlocal Act, the City authorizes the CTA to issue the Bonds in a principal
amount not exceeding $275,000,000 (no more than $41,250,000 of which will
be allocated to the City Facilities) and to loan the proceeds thereof to the
