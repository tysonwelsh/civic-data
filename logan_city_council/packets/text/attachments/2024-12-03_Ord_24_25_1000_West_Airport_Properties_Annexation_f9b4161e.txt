BACKGROUND
Annexation petitions are referred to the Planning Commission for a recommendation to the Logan Municipal Council. According to Section 17.53.040.A.3, the Planning Commission shall hold a public hearing on the proposed annexation request and then make a recommendation to the Council.

ANNEXATION REVIEW PROCESS
Annexations are governed by Utah Municipal Code 10.2.4 and the Land Development Code. The following process and dates have been followed for the current annexation petition:

    • 9/16/24 - Applicant filed Petition with Cache County Recorder
    • 9/20/24 - Applicant filed Petition with City of Logan
    • 10/1/24 - City sent Notice of Intent to Affected Entities
    • 10/16/24 - City Council Accepted the Petition
    • 10/16/24 - City Recorder Certified the Petition
    • 10/19/24 - City Recorder published public notice & established the 30 day protest period.
    • 10/16/24 - CD sends Public Notice to owners within ½ mile of site and distributes Petition to governmental entities for review.
    • 11/14/24 - Planning Commission hearing & recommendation on Petition
    • 11/19/24 - Council Workshop on Petition
    • 11/19/24 - Protest Period Expires
    • 12/03/24 - Council Hearing on Petition
    • 120/4/24 – 2/2/24 – Applicant has 60 days to finalize the Annexation Plan, and once finalized, the Recorder sends the signed Annexation Plat & decision to Utah Lt. Governor's office for approval.

The red line is the current Logan city boundary while the hatched line shows the proposed city boundary post annexation. The property directly south was annexed into Logan in 2022.

The map below shows the presence of wetlands on the site. Wetland delineations and environmental reviews have either been conducted, or are under contract, for most of the properties and will be required with design review permits application.

The site is located south of the Logan Cache airport and is located within the Airport Overlay Zone. While the airport overlay zone does not affect the

The subject parcels are designated as Airport in the FLUP and surrounded by both Airport and Industrial zoning. Directly east and west of the site are large rock, gravel, sand, concrete and asphalt facilities while further south are several manufacturing and warehousing facilities.     • Anticipated demand for municipal services for the proposed annexation area;

The applicant indicated their preliminary development plans include both industrial and commercial uses. They are anticipating they will need water, sewer, access to public roads, and electrical service, all of which are available in 1200 West, 1000 West and 600 West. The parcels are located on two future gridded streets (2200 North and 800 West) and will be required to dedicate and improve those roadways at the time of development.

In regard to municipal services, the City's Department of Public Works responded with the following comments:

    1)   The are substantial areas of apparent wetlands in these parcels.  City will require Army Corps approved delineations prior to submitting for Development Permits.

    2)   The existing sewer line in 1000 West is currently not connected to the City's sewer system.  Additional improvements will need to be constructed to provide sewer services to the properties including extending a sewer line west to the 1200 W alignment along the 2200 North alignment.

    3)   An existing 12-inch water line exists in 1000 West and would be able to supply water for the developments, a fire flow analysis will be required during the development process for each lot to confirm the City's minimum requirements are met.

    4)   Access to the UDOT right of way will require UDOT approval.  Developments will be required to construct street improvements on 1000 West including travel lane, parking lane, curb and gutter, park strip, and sidewalk as identified in the City's Public Works Design Standards.
