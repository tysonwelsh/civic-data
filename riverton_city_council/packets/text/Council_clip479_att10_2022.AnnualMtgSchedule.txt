                                         RIVERTON CITY
                                    2022 MEETING SCHEDULES

Pursuant to § 52-4-202(2) Utah Code Annotated, Riverton City hereby gives notice of the following
meeting schedules for the Year 2021:
     City Council Meetings are generally held on the first and third Tuesday of each month with
       Work Sessions beginning at 5:00 p.m. and City Council Meetings beginning at 7:00 p.m., at
       Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah.
     Planning Commission Meetings are generally held on the second and fourth Thursdays of each
       month beginning at 6:30 p.m. at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood Road,
       Riverton, Utah.
     Riverton Fire Service Area (RFSA) Meetings are held quarterly and as needed for budgeting
       purposes on the first Tuesday of each month following City Council Meetings. This meeting is
       scheduled in the following months: March, June, September, November, and December.
     Riverton Law Enforcement Service Area (RLESA) Meetings are held quarterly and as needed
       for budgeting purposes on the third Tuesday of each month following City Council Meetings.
       This meeting is scheduled in the following months: March, May, June, September, and
       December.
     Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Meetings, Special and/or Emergency Meetings, in addition
       to those specified, may be held as circumstances require; best practicable notice will be given for
       such meetings.
     Board of Adjustment Hearings may be held as circumstances require; best practicable notice
       will be given for such meetings.

Meetings may be canceled; however, best practicable notice will be given for canceled meetings.
Meetings will not be held on a State or Federal Holiday or on an Election Day. All meetings are open to
the public unless closed, pursuant to the applicable provisions of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act.
Riverton City Hall is an accessible facility. Individuals needing special accommodations or assistance
during this meeting shall notify the City Recorder’s Office at 801-208-3128, at least 24 hours in advance
of the meeting. Accessible parking and entrance are located on the south end of the building with elevator
access to the City Council Chambers located on the second floor.
