3.         25-201                 Public Hearing to consider the adoption of the FY 2025-26 Sandy City
                                  Budget. This item includes:

                                  Resolutions 25-23C and 25-24C of Sandy City, adopting the annual budget
                                  for Sandy City and the Alta Canyon Recreation District for the fiscal year
                                  commencing July 1, 2025 and ending June 30, 2026; also establishing
                                  certain fees and charges in Sandy City for said fiscal year.
                   Attachments:   Signed_25-23C_Final_Adopted_Version
                                  Signed_25-24C_Final_Budget_Adoption_-_Alta_Canyon_(Amended)
                                  June 17 2025_Approved Amendments to the FY26 Tentative Budget
                                  June 17th Sharkey Budget Proposal Memo
                                  June 17th Dekeyzer Budget Proposal Memo
                                  25-23C Final Budget Adoption (Amended)
                                  25-24C Final Budget Adoption - Alta Canyon (Amended)

                                  Council discussed and considered proposed amendments to the FY 2026 Tentative
                                  Budget.

                                  Council Member Cyndi Sharkey presented her proposed amendment to the FY 26
                                  Tentative Budget. Ms. Sharkey proposed lengthening the period of time for the proposed
                                  storm water rate hike over 15 or 20 years rather than the 10 year period of time proposed
                                  in the tentative budget. Council discussed the proposal, expressing support for the rate
                                  increase but also some concerns. A friendly amendment was offered by Council Member
                                  Robinson to set the period at 12 years. The friendly amendment was declined. Tom
                                  Ward, Public Utilities and Shane Pace, CAO, offered additional insight regarding the
                                  proposed storm water rate increase and the 60-40 split of the funds between capital
                                  infrastructure and operations.

                                  A motion was made by Marci Houseman, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa to set the
                                  storm water rate at $7.67

                                  Council discussion on the motion followed. Council Member Sharkey discussed whether
                                  the 60-40 split between infrastructure and operations was needed or if that restriction
                                  could be changed.

                                  Dustin Fratto recommended adding clarifying language to the motion. His
                                  recommendation was accepted.

                                  A motion was made by Marci Houseman, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa to set the
                                  storm water rate at $7.67 and to direct Administrative staff to adjust the
                                  appropriation of storm water funds as staff deems appropriate to meet the
                                  projected revenues...The motion carried by the following roll call vote:

                                  Yes:   4   Zach Robinson
                                             Marci Houseman
                                             Cyndi Sharkey
                                             Brooke D'Sousa

                                  No:    3   Alison Stroud
