                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 25-08

  AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 18.90.010 (TABLE OF COMMERCIAL
USES) CLARIFYING ALLOWED COMMERCIAL USES, AMENDMENTS PROPOSED
                       BY RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission and City Council have held a public hearing to
consider said text amendment; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council on recommendation from the Commission has
determined that it is in the best interest of the public to amend Riverton City Code Section
18.90.010 as described in Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

       Section 1. Riverton City Code Section 18.90.010, Table of Commercial Uses shall be,
                  and hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit “A.”.

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
April, 2025 by the following vote:
