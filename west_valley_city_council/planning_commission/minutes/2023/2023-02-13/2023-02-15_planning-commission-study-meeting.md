ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-1-2023 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY to amend Section 7-2-115 of the zoning
          ordinance to clarify the measurement standards for a commercial vehicle from the
          “gross weight” to the “gross vehicle weight rating” and establish the allowed vehicle
          Class size for storage of vehicles in Agricultural and Residential zones. (Staff – Jody
          Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp said this proposal is a text amendment from West Valley City to clarify
               measurement standards for a commercial vehicle and permitted vehicle Class storage.
               She noted that this application came from discussions between the Police Department
               and Code Enforcement. Jody explained that currently, we are using a “gross weight”
               measurement, causing contradictions between Title 7 and Title 22 of the City Code.
               She explained that switching to the “gross vehicle weight rating” in Section 7-2-115
               of the zoning ordinance would keep the measurement the same throughout the code
               and help simplify enforcement. Jody presented a chart that our Legal Department had
               put together depicting the gross vehicle weight rating and parking, Classes one
               through eight. She explained that any vehicle in Classes one through three could be
              stored in Agricultural and Residential zones, and any vehicle in Class four and above
              would not be permitted.

              Commissioner Winters asked if Jody knew how our measurement standards compare
              to those of neighboring Cities. Jody said she would find out and let the Commission
              know at our next meeting.

              Commissioner Lovato asked Jody about a situation where a larger vehicle was being
              stored on a pad behind a home and if this would still be permitted. Jody said that was
              for a home occupation, in which storage of larger vehicles is allowed under certain
              conditions, and that will remain the same.

              Commissioner Lovato asked if we receive many issues regarding storing large
              commercial vehicles. Jody said that there had been problems with semi-trucks and
              larger box trucks. However, the biggest issue is with enforcement. She stated that the
              contradiction within our code currently needs to be clarified for Code Enforcement
              and the Police Department.

              Commissioner Porter asked if this would also apply to personal vehicles. Jody
              explained that the word “Commercial” applies to the DOT rating, not the type of
              vehicle. Steve Pastorik pointed out that Section 7-2-115 states what would not be
              considered a Commercial Vehicle.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-3-2023  Petition by MAPLELEAF CABINETS requesting a conditional use for Outside
          Storage. The property is located at 2554 South 3400 West on 2.46 acres and is zoned
          M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-
          ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that this applicant had requested a conditional use for an
              expansion in 2020, and that has been put on hold due to some violations regarding
              outside storage. She stated that approval of this request would help bring them into
              compliance. Jody noted that the applicant isn’t looking to store much outside. They
              are looking for some flex space to provide room for moving materials around onsite.
              She presented a slide highlighting the proposed storage areas and pointed out they
              would be on the existing paved area. Jody noted that the applicant owns the adjacent
              building, and the outside storage will be screened from view between the two
              buildings.

              Jody stated that the cargo container would remain in its current location as a
              permanent structure, so they would need to acquire a building permit. She pointed out
              that trees would be planted between the subject property and adjacent residential
              areas.


C-4-2023      Petition by JONES HEATING & AIR requesting conditional use approval for the
              expansion of an existing business within the Jordan River Overlay Zone. The
              property is located at 1352 West 3300 South on 0.54 acres and is zoned C-2 (General
              Commercial). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-
              ut.gov)

Discussion:   Brock Anderson explained that the applicant had applied for a building permit, and
              while reviewing the application, it was determined that the Planning Commission
              should review the requested expansion. He noted that the subject property is located
              within the Jordan River Overlay Zone and would be permitted to expand if approved
              by the Commission.

              Brock presented the floor plan for the existing building and what they have added. He
              explained that the applicant proposes to paint the addition the same color as the main
              building. Brock pointed out an existing cargo container on the subject property,
              which is prohibited within the Jordan River Overlay Zone. He said the applicant is
              proposing to enclose the container.

              Brock pointed out that there is one white garage door, and if the Commission wants it
              to be painted the same color as the other doors, he could add that as a condition of
              approval. He stated that he would add a condition to remove the extra signage on the
              building.

              Commissioner Winters would like the paint to match. Commissioner McEwen feels
              that the materials should match as well.

              Commissioner Winters asked Brock if keeping the ground clear and preventing
              contamination of groundwater were the only reasons for the restrictions of the Jordan
              River Overlay Zone. Brock explained that this area is the main entrance to the City.
              Therefore, we want it to look clean and aesthetically pleasing.

              Commissioner Lovato feels that screening might be appropriate between the property
              and the open field right next door due to the use of the subject property.

              Commissioner Winters asked if it would be possible to move the cargo container to a
              spot on the property that isn’t visible. Brock said that the container is not permitted
              within the Jordan River Overlay Zone, so the applicant proposes enclosing it.


MISCELLANIOUS APPLICATIONS
M-1-2023  Petition by READY MADE CONCRETE requesting an exception to the 7-10,
          Standards for Landscaping Along High Image Arterial Streets, for property located at
          2635 South Constitution Blvd. on 3.57 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff
          – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that the applicant had submitted a building permit application
              for a building remodel, which triggered a review of their file. She stated that this site
              was developed before Ready Made Concrete occupied the building. Jody noted that
              an exception was made to the landscaping requirements in 2007. She said after the
              exception was granted; Ready Made Concrete submitted a landscape plan showing
              that set-back exception on 2700 West. However, the landscaping on the rest of the
              property would meet the 7-10 requirements. Jody pointed out that the proposed
               landscaping was never fully added, and more and more landscaping has been
               removed over time.

               Jody explained that the City has been spraying the weeds along the sloped area
               running along 2660 South. She said the applicant would like to maintain and clean up
               that area and, in return, will add landscaping along 2660 South. Jody said that would
               be possible by amending the maintenance agreement between West Valley City and
               UDOT. She had discussed this with Facilities and UDOT, who are amenable to the
               amendment.

               Commissioner Winters asked Jody what landscaping would need to be added for the
               applicant to meet the landscaping requirements. Jody explained that there is an
               exception to the frontage. However, the applicant wants to settle everything regarding
               the maintenance agreement before adding the landscaping along the sloped area of
               2660 South.

               Commissioner Lovato pointed out the block-type masonry wall on the subject
               property. He feels it isn’t aesthetically pleasing and asked Jody if that wall is
               acceptable. Jody said that this situation is a bit different, and typically we would like
               to see another type of wall installed. She stated that this business makes concrete, so
               they use their product to build the wall.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if the City has plans to deal with the sloped areas, such
               as the one in this proposal. Steve explained that he is unaware of a plan; however, the
               City will usually work with the property owner to maintain it well.

               Commissioner McEwen pointed out the large amount of dirt on the property and how
               it runs into the street. He asked Jody what could be done to prevent this from
               happening. Jody said she isn’t sure how the applicant intends to deal with this
               situation. However, they are listening to this meeting and can respond during the
               public hearing next week.
