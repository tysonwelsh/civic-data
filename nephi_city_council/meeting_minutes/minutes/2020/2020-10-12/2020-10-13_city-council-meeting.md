                                        Meeting Minutes
                                     Work Session 10-13-20
                                            7:00 p.m.
                                   Nephi City Council Chambers


1. Representatives from the Juab Livestock Association met with the council to discuss the danger of
   cattle coming down from Reese’s Flat onto Highway 132. Representative Bob Garret said there is a
   need for fencing along the highway and the installation of cattle guards on the Reese’s Flat road to
   prevent the cattle from getting onto the highway. The council agreed to identifying the players who
   would have liability on this issue and coming up with a solution to help solve this problem.

2.    The council discussed a utility easement negotiation between Gordon McPherson and the City. The
     council directed staff to move along with the agreement with Mr. McPherson

3. The council discussed a level change in the annexation area map concerning the Jason Carlton
   property. The council directed staff to put the level change on the agenda for next weeks meeting.

4. The council reviewed a new draft of the Biglow Annexation Agreement that was acceptable to all
   parties involved. Council directed staff to draw up the agreement and to bring it back to the next
   regular meeting for approval.

5. City Attorney Kasey Wright gave an update of the bidding process of the Juab Fieldhouse. He stated
   that a contractor had been chosen and the details are currently being worked out.

6. City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the CARES Act funding and what
   expenses the funds can be used for.

7. Adjournment into executive session 8.50 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson (ABSENT), Mayor Pro Tem Justin Seely, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood,
Nathan Memmott, Kent Jones, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Robby Kelsey, Bob Garrett, Lynn
Bailey, Alex Johnson, Jason Carlton, Kyle Marchant.
