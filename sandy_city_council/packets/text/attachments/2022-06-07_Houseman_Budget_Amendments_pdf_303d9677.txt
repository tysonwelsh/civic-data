                                           MEMORANDUM

                                             June 7, 2022

To:              City Council Members

CC:              Monica Zoltanski, Mayor
                 Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director

From:            Marci Houseman, Council Member At-large

Subject:         Proposal to amend the fiscal year 2022-23 Tentative Budget to maintain a
                 $90,000 contribution to the Central Wasatch Commission




The FY 2022-23 Tentative budget (Tentative) includes a total contribution of $45,000 to the Central
Wasatch Commission (CWC). This number is half of the current fiscal year’s contribution ($90,000) and
less than half of previous fiscal years contributions ($100,000). I’ve consistently expressed my opinion
that the CWC does important work that Sandy City needs to fully support and be intimately involved in.
My proposal is to renew the $90,000 allocation to the CWC for FY 2022-23. I propose that 25% come
from the general ($22,500) fund and 75% ($67,500) come from the water fund.

General Fund

In the General Fund, the Tentative suggests allocating $22,500 from Department 1900 (Non-
Departmental) towards the city contribution to the CWC. I’d encourage the Council to maintain this
allocation.

Water Fund

In the Water Fund, the Tentative suggests allocating $22,500 (1/3 of my proposed allocation from the
water fund) towards the city contribution to the CWC. In looking for an additional $45,000 from the
water fund, I’m recommending that the council decrease the proposed allocation to Water Education
and Conservation by $45,000 and instead allocate that funding to Water Operations Special Programs
for contribution to the CWC. I’m also open to the Council deciding that the Water Operations Special
Programs line item will have a total allocation of $67,500 for contribution to the CWC, and then allowing
public utilities staff to determine where they’d ultimately like to see the additional $45,000 come from
within the water fund.
