                 APPROXIMATELY 3600 WEST 11820 SOUTH CONSISTING OF 0.02 ACRES (1,032
                 SF) TO THE ADJACENT PROPERTY OWNER, PROPOSED BY RIVERTON CITY


Proposed Motion:

I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance #22-08, vacating 0.02 acre portion of public right-of-
way located at approximately 3600 West 11820 South to the adjacent property owner, as shown
on Exhibit A

Background

This is the proposed vacation of an unused portion of right-of-way on the west side of 3600 West
just south of 11800 South. Ivory Homes is developing the property adjacent to the right-of way on
the west. The survey work for that subdivision identified a gap between the back of sidewalk and
the adjacent property, which continues only for a short stretch before the property line moves to
back of sidewalk. This is not uncommon when survey work is updated. Neither the sidewalk nor
the travel lanes are affected, and as part of a dedicated public right-of-way, the resolution of this
situation is vacation of the property to the adjacent land owner.

The attached document shows visually the area to be dedicated, which consists of 1,032 square
feet, or 0.02 acres. The ordinance includes a legal description of the area to be vacated. This
action did not require review and recommendation from the Planning Commission. A proposed
motion is outlined above.

Attachments

The following items are attached:
   • An aerial photo generally describing the area.
   • Documentation for vacation of ground and dedication of right-of-way.
