The motion passed unanimously. 2024 TIP Selection Process Adoptions – Bob Allen, MAG MAG conducts a TIP Project Selection Process every two years to fund new regionally significant projects within the MPO boundary using federal, exchange, and local transportation tax funds. Prior to the initiation of each selection cycle, the process is reviewed, and potential modifications are proposed and discussed. The following process modifications have been proposed. ● Environmental Guidance ● Design and Construction Standards ● Scope changes and concept cost estimates ● Field Review vs Meeting ● Sunset Clause for lingering projects ● Mode-specific concept reports and criteria See draft TIP policy and procedures and presentation slides for detailed information Brad Kenison moved to recommend the proposed 2024 TIP Selection Process and TIP Policies and Procedures be approved as presented. Vern Keesler seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. MPO TIP Selectin Process Kick-Off – Bob Allen, MAG The Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) lists regional transportation projects (MPO, UDOT, UTA, locals). The MPO selects projects every two years. The next selection cycle begins now. New projects will be funded in 2027 and 2028 at approximately $100m. Eligible projects and their process were discussed in detail. MAG projects use Federal, County, and Exchange Funds. Projects must use the UDOT cost estimate spreadsheet. Estimates are based on a federal funded project.
