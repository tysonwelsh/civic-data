GENERAL SESSION

Chair Tippetts called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. She
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

4.     SUB-21-25-479-021; 51 Alabama Plat “A’; 51 W. Alabama Dr.; 5-Lot Preliminary
       Subdivision Plat; TOD Zone; John Heiner/ Heiner Land Company
       (Applicant/Owner); Jason Binks, Senior Planner (Staff).

Mr. Binks stated this is a request for a recommendation for a 5-lot subdivision at 51 W Alabama
Dr. The subdivision proposes to create 5 lots that align with the 5 existing townhouse units at this
multifamily residential development. The subdivision would allow for private ownership of each
3-story townhome.

                                                ***

APPLICANT:

The applicant did not have any comments.

                                                ***

PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Tippetts opened the public hearing.

Mr. Thomas Winand, business owner located at 7704 Maple St., stated that he has concerns
regarding the lack of extra parking available for the project and the effect that will have on local
traffic and businesses. He added that the lack of additional parking for this project, in conjunction
with the large 240 unit development proposed just south of this project, will cause traffic
congestion on neighboring streets and a quagmire for residents. He stated that he sent an email to
the planning department describing the issues with traffic and some suggestions he had on how to
address them. Mr. Winand added that the reason he is bringing these issues to this meeting as
opposed to the prior Planning Commission meeting held on the 240 unit development project on
Center St. is because he was not notified of the public meeting on that project. He stated that he is
unsure why he wasn’t notified, and felt that he should have been.

PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION AUGUST 11, 2021
Mr. James Abraham, business owner located to the west to the subject property, stated that he
agrees with the prior statements made by Mr. Winand regarding traffic and stated that he also was
not notified of the large project. Mr. Abraham then asked the Planning Commission and staff if
the private utility lateral easement would be discussed as part of another hearing?

Commissioner Smallwood said most likely not as utility easements are already recorded against
the property.

Mr. Abraham stated that the easement shown on the current plat was not presented to him on the
original project plat. On the original plat it appeared that utilities were going to run through his
parking lot on his property, which he never agreed to.

Mr. Binks stated that this application does not deal with easements and only deals with separating
the units for private sale.

Mr. Abraham said that he wants to ensure that his parking spaces would be safe and would like to
know what the plan is for entering and exiting the property as he wants to make sure that it does
not affect his property’s access and his patients.

Commissioner Smallwood stated that the easement and access is not part of the subdivision. The
only item being considered is the subdivision of the 5 units for individual sale.

Mr. John Heiner, the applicant, stated the issues being brought forth are not part of this request
however to address some of the concerns he added that in terms of the visitor’s parking there are
two stalls in the common area which satisfies the city’s requirement. As for the easement he stated
that he decided to purchase an easement from the neighboring property to the north instead of
pursing the easement through Mr. Abraham’s property. In terms of traffic, he stated that in visiting
this project, he has not witnessed many issues with traffic in the neighborhood and feels that the
area is very quiet.

Chair Tippetts echoed prior statements by stating that this request is only for the subdivision of the
5 units for individual sale and all other prior approved aspects of the project are final.

Mr. Abraham stated that he is thankful that the applicant is no longer pursuing the easement on his
property, but he still has issues with the right of way. He stated that the applicant has already dug
with concrete cutters to run utilities.

Mr. Wilcox stated that this is a legal matter between two property owners and as the
Commissioners have already established this request is solely about the individual sale of the 5
units in this project. This is not to say that the city is not concerned about these issues, but this is
a civil issue, and this is not the appropriate forum to address these matters.


MOTION:        Commissioner Smallwood MOVED to close the public hearing. The motion
               was SECONDED by Commissioner Costello with all voting in favor.

CHAIR UPDATE

6.      Chair Comments; Chair Tippetts, Planning Commission Chair.

Chair Tippetts stated that she wanted to discuss and propose a change to the meeting procedures.
She stated that having staff provide presentations to the Commissioners on items that are detailed
in the staff report seems redundant and instead she purposes that Commissioners come to the
meeting prepared, having read the staff report, and Commissioners can instead spend meeting time,
asking questions, hearing from Mr. Wilcox, hearing from the applicant and listening to public
comment. All staff reports are available to the public in the packet which is posted online.

Commissioner Costello stated that she disagrees and thinks that the presentations are important
because it provides the public, who may not have access to the staff reports, with a complete


PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION AUGUST 11, 2021
understanding of the request being brought forth. She added that not having the presentation will
make residents more confused and lead to more questions.

Chair Tippetts stated that her concern is that residents have the perception that the Planning
Commission can take steps that they can’t as a body.

Commissioner Costello stated that it is very important that the Planning Commission’s powers and
abilities are communicated clearly to the public.

Commissioner Anderson stated that transparency is key to this issue.

Commissioner Costello added that the more information that is out there the better it is. She also
stated that Mr. Wilcox comments are very important in providing the public with information.

Commissioner Smallwood stated that as a planner the presentation should be for the public and
not the Commissioners. The Commissioners should be reading their staff report.

Chair Tippetts stated that she hears and receives what the Commissioners are saying and is happy
to move forward as usual, she was just trying to reduce the amount of redundant work staff is
having to do

Mr. Binks stated that staff can work on making the presentation briefer and to the point. He added
that it is also important that the Planning Commissioners continue to oversee the meeting and how
it operates.
