4.         21-364                 Police Department providing the Council with an update on the 
                                  Neighborhood Traffic Safety Program
                   Attachments:   NTSP Presentation.pptx

                                  Police Chief Severson and staff presented on the Neighborhood Traffic Safety Program. 
                                  The program focuses on daily traffic enforcement, over 2 to 4 hours shifts. The program 
                                  focuses on residential streets and secondary arterial roadways with an emphasis on 
                                  school zones, red light violations, speeding and DUI's. The locations patrolled are chosen 
                                  based in part by residents' complaints. He provided traffic statistics. Council questions 
                                  and comments followed.

                                  The Public Hearing was continued from the 9/21/21 City Council Meeting. Mike Wilcox, 
                                  with Community Development, presented on this item. He provided an overview on the 
                                  amendments related to Accessory Apartments (Internal Accessory Dwelling Units, 
                                  I-ADUs).  The Amendments to Title 21, Chapter 7, Permitted Land Uses in Residential 
                                  Districts; Mixed Use, Transit Corridor, and Research and Development Districts; Title 21,
