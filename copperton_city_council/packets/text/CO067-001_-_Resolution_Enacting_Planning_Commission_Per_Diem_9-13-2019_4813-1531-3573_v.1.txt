       A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ESTABLISHING PER DIEM COMPENSATION FOR MEMBERS OF THE
       PLANNING COMMISSION

        WHEREAS, Section 19.05.060 of the Copperton Metro Township Code authorizes the
Copperton Metro Township Council (“Council”) to fix per diem compensation for members
of the planning commission for meetings actually attended by resolution; and

        WHEREAS, the Council has determined that per diem compensation in the amount of
fifty dollars ($50.00) per planning commission member for each meeting attended is a
reasonable amount to compensate members of the planning commission for necessary
expenses associated with attendance at meetings.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Copperton Metro Township
Council that pursuant to Section 19.050.060 of the Copperton Metro Township Code, per diem
compensation for planning commission members in the amount of fifty dollars ($50.00) per
member for each meeting attended is hereby established effective January 1, 2020.

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah
this ______ day of ________________, 2019.
