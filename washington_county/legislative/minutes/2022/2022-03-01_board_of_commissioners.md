Attorney Eric Clarke, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne

DECLARING MARCH _ OF _ 2022, TO BE WASHINGTON COUNTY LAW

ENFORCEMENT AND FIRST RESPONDER MONTH

Commissioner Almquist explained that law enforcement and first responders are valued within
Washington County and read aloud a proclamation declaring March 2022 Washington County
Law Enforcement and First Responder month.

Washington County Sheriff Nate Brooksby, Hurricane Police Chief Lynn Excel, St. George Police
Chief Kyle Whitehead; Washington City Police Chief Jason Williams; and Fire Chief Robert
Stoker all expressed gratitude to the Commissioners and the community, stating that they all felt
supported and appreciated the partnerships with one another.

Proclamation Declaring March of 2022 to be Washington County Law
Enforcement and First Responder Month. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and

Almquist voting aye.
PRESENTATION: WASHINGTON COUNTY ART CONTEST WINNERS

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that Washington County had an Art Contest
seeking art for the parking structure currently under construction. Due to the overwhelming
response from local artists, several runners up were chosen and will have their artwork displayed
in the new administration building.

The runners up included: Mariellen Lopez’s painting, which was chosen to be displayed in the
lobby. Spencer Sullivan’s photo of Zion National Park to be displayed in the lobby and photo of
Sand Hollow to be displayed in the Chambers. Dewey Farmer’s photos of Zion National Park,
Gunlock overflow, and Snow Canyon State Park all of which will be displayed in the Chambers.

The winner and photo to be displayed on the parking structure also came from Mr. Farmer and
was a photo of Zion National Park.

Harmony Heights Subdivision. The cost of the improvements shall be paid by a special assessment

improvements.

wish to speak on the matter.
