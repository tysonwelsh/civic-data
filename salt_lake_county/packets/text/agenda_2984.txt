Members of the Board May Participate Electronically.  
Due to the Ongoing  Public  Health  Emergency  Related  to  the  COVID -19 Pandemic and  
Response,  public  gatherings  have  been  limited  to  less  than   50 persons  and  public  access  to 
the Salt Lake County Government Center has Been Limited on the Orders of the Salt  Lake  
County Mayor.
Consistent with Salt Lake County Council Resolution 5714, Approved March 24, 2020, The board 
has made preparations to conduct its meetings electronically.  In light of current public health 
orders and social distancing requirements, the Board cannot provide physical space or facilities 
for members of the public to attend its meetings for the duration of the public health emergency.
Members of the public are invited to participate in the Board ’s meetings electronically.  Agendas, 
audio live streams and recordings, and meeting minutes can be accessed at slco .legistar.com.  The 
Board will continue to broadcast a live stream of its meetings on  Facebook Live, which can be 
accessed at http://www.facebook.com/SLCoCouncil/
