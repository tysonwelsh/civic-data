DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST

The applicant, Mr. J.L. Llavina, owner of American Loans, is requesting the Planning
Commission to review a proposed two-lot commercial subdivision located at 8800 S.
Harrison Street in Sandy City.  The Planning Commission is the land use approval body
for this action, as designated in the Sandy City Development Code.


BACKGROUND
This 0.62-acre site has been assembled from four individual property parcels to
configure the sites for two new office buildings, one each on the two lots to be created
by this subdivision action.  The owner of both prior businesses, American Loans
business office and the propane gas distribution business, Mr. Llavina, was heavily
impacted by the widening of I-15 in that 50 feet of the west property boundary was
taken by UDOT for the freeway widening.  The prior office for American Loans was also
demolished to make room for a new ramp.  Existing zoning of the property is Regional

Commercial (RC).  Zoning to the north and south is also RC.  Property to the east,

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING

A neighborhood meeting was not required and was not held for this subdivision request.

ANALYSIS
City staff and departments have reviewed and recommend preliminary subdivision plat
approval, subject to their normal requirements and specifications.  The subdivision
meets all the zoning requirements.  All needed subdivision improvements for this site
will be required and completed through the site plan application and approval.   Some
utility easements and reciprocal access driveway easements will be granted through the
recording of the subdivision plat.  Staff has no concerns about this proposed
subdivision.

Street Right-of-Way Dedication.  Street right-of-way dedication is required on
Harrison Street and the subdivision plat will dedicate these areas to Sandy City as a
condition of subdivision approval.  A total of 2,050 square feet of the applicant's land
will be dedicated along the Harrison Street right-of-way.  This dedication will allow for
the currently required 5-foot wide sidewalk and 5-foot wide landscaped parking strip
streetscape design.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that preliminary review is
complete for the American Loans two-lot commercial subdivision, by making the following
three findings and subject to the following two conditions:

FINDINGS:
   A. That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer and
      the Transportation Engineer, have preliminarily approved the proposed subdivision
      plat.

   B. That the proposed subdivision will be finalized with City Staff, through recording with
      the County Recorder.

   C. That the proposed subdivision will dedicate required street right-of-way areas to
      Sandy City on Harrison Street, and clean up the existing multiple property parcel
      configuration of the site, to compose the two-lot subdivision.

CONDITIONS:
   1. That the developer proceeds through the final subdivision review process with
      staff prior to the start of any construction (including payment of development fees
      and posting of an appropriate bond to guarantee completion of all required
      improvements on and off the site), according to the Subdivision Procedures
      Handout.
