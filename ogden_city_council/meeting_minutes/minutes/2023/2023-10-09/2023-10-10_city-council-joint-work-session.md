The purpose of the Joint Work Session was to review the agendas for the Special Redevelopment Agency (RDA) and
Regular City Council meetings scheduled to begin at 4:30 p.m.; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board
comments.
Agenda Review

Acting Chair Richey and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agendas for the Special RDA and
Regular City Council meetings scheduled to begin at 4:30 p.m. There was a focus on the amended ordinances that have been
provided by Administration regarding the proposed zone text amendments for the M-1 and M-2 zones, which were tabled during
the October 3 City Council meeting and will be considered during tonight’s City Council meeting.

Council/Board Business

Capital Improvement Plan: Council Deputy Director Symes discussed the timeline for reviewing and prioritizing Capital
Improvement Plan (CIP) proposals provided by City Administration; Council staff has developed a survey that each Council
member can use to prioritize and rank CIP projects and the results of the survey will be provided to Administration for their
consideration as they develop the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2024-2025 budget.

Jack In The Box Update: Chief Administrative Officer Brown reported management of the Jack In The Box restaurant
on Harrison Boulevard has made some changes to their site and traffic flow responsive to the code enforcement action taken
against them; the access point from Harrison Boulevard has been closed and a storage unit on the site has been removed.

144 25" Street Update: Ms. Brown also provided a report on progress at 144 25" Street; the owner of the property has
until October 16 to submit revised plans in response to the City’s issuance of the notice of dangerous building. She will report
back to the Council once she receives the response. This led to discussion of the manner in which the City was notified of the
dangerous conditions at the building and City’s efforts to inspect other projects being pursued by the same developet/owner.

Misc. Council Business: The Council discussed with Ms. Brown the rumor of a contract for services with former Chief

Administrative Officer Johnson; Ms. Brown stated that there is no formal contract, but Mr. Johnson’s services will be utilized
until they are no longer needed. There was a brief focus on who made the decision to continue to utilize Mr. Johnson’s services
and the funding source for the arrangement.

Council member White asked for a report from Administration on any existing bonds associated with projects that are
planned for properties throughout the City for which the zoning of a property has been changed.
