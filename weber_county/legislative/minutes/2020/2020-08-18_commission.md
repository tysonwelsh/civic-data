 G. ACTION ITEMS:
     1. RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING ISSUANCE & SALE OF NOT MORE THAN $2,500,000 AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL AMOUNT
         OF SALES TAX REVENUE REFUNDING BONDS AND RELATED MATTERS - RESOLUTION 24-2020
         John Bond, County Treasurer, stated that this is to refinance the Animal Shelter bonds (outstanding amount of about
         $1.765 million) to save about $150,000. There were eight outstanding bonds 2-3 years ago and now there are six. The
         county’s aggressive strategy is to pay them off quickly or to refinance them when advantageous. Jonathon Ward, of
         Zions Public Finance, county’s financial advisor, stated that the existing interest rate on the outstanding bonds is 4.97%
         and based on the current interest rate environment he anticipates to get closer to a 1.4% average. Randy Larsen, of
         Gilmore & Bell, county bond counsel, briefly outlined the resolution and related contracts.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 24-2020 authorizing issuance and sale of not more than $2,500,000
         aggregate principal amount of Sales Tax Revenue Refunding Bonds and related matters; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     2. CONTRACT WITH UTAH YOUTH RODEO ASSOCIATION FOR 2020-2021 SEASON AT GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER
         Duncan Olsen, GSEC General Manager, presented this contract.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with the Utah Youth Rodeo Association for the 2020-2021 season
         at the Golden Spike Event Center; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
     3. MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH OGDEN CITY FOR THE PURPOSE OF DISTRIBUTING JAG GRANT FUNDS
         Chief Brandon Roundy, of the County Sheriff's Office, stated that the county anticipates receiving 10% of the $53,498.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the Memorandum of Understanding with Ogden City to distribute JAG Grant
         funds; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
