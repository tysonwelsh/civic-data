Council Member Business

                    Council Member Kris Nicholl thanked all who attended the Oath of Office Ceremony and
                    also thanked staff for coordinating the event. It was an awesome event. It felt like a family
                    event.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman expressed appreciation to all who attended and
                    organized the Oath of Office Ceremony for the incoming Council Members. She was
                    thrilled to work with the new council members. She hoped the Council would again
                    participate in a budget priorities workshop later this month or next. She spoke about the
                    One Word Challenge and asked the Council if they would like to pick a word to guide
                    them during 2026.

                    Council Member Brooke Christensen thanked all who supported her re-election to the
                    Council. She was excited to return to the Council and represent District 1.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa supported the One Word Challenge. She thanked staff
                    for organizing the Oath of Office Ceremony and appreciated all those who attended. She
                    was looking forward to working with her colleagues this year.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud attended the retirement farewell for Dan Median, P&R
                    Director. She also attending the Hale Centre Theatre signing ceremony signifying the
                    payoff of the bond, seventeen years early. She also toured the new HCT education center
                    and spoke about the excellent community partnership with Hale Centre Theatre which
                    has had a significant impact on Sandy City. People from 48 out of 50 states purchase
                    tickets for productions at HCT.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer welcomed the new Council Members and was looking
                    forward to working with them. He requested a recap of the Housing Workshop Series and
                    asked if more direction was needed from the Council. He wanted Sandy City to lead out
                    on housing.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey thanked the Council for electing her as the new chair.
                    She was looking forward to working with a veteran Council who have all served as a
                    council member in the past. She provided an update from the Salt Lake Convention and
                    Visitors Board and spoke about the A/V contract the board was reviewing.

                    Dustin Fratto, Council Director, told the Council that staff was in the process of compiling
                    the final report from the housing workshops and would send the report out to the Council
                    soon. He also thanked the Facilities staff for all their help with the Oath of Office
                    Ceremony set up.

Mayor's Report

                    Ryan Mecham, Senior Policy Advisor, provided the Mayor's report. He provided an
                    overview of the recent resident survey ranking the issues that is most important to them.
                    Housing costs and affordability ranked very high in importance, as did local land use
                    authority and water fees. He will forward the results of this survey to the Council.
