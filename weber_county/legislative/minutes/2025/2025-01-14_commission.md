D. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Commissioner Froerer: Last week I was able to attend a ribbon cutting at Deer Valley up in Wasatch
      County. Tt actually was the second project area for a semi-government private agency called MIDA,
      Military Installation Development Agency, put into statute in 2007 and was of my first bills I voted on in
      2007. This has ties to Weber County as there was a little seven room cabin located in Snow Basin owned by
      Hill Air Force Base. It was set up by the military’s Morale, Welfare, and Recreation (MWR) program for
      their military personnel. As you know the Olympics came and the state needed the cabin for the Olympics.
      MIDA became involved. Jim Hansen and I worked together on that project and we said, “Okay let's trade
      that piece but we need to have a replacement piece that would work out for the military.” At the end of the
      day this resulted in several hundred million dollars and a major 500 room hotel that backs on Deer Valley.
      The room rate if we were to go up there tonight would be $1600, obviously out of my price range. But
      there's a hundred rooms that are reserved for our military, retired disabled, and also National Guard that they
      get to pay $100 a night reserved for them. Again it's a private/public partnership that we did with Hill Air
      Force Base to honor our military and give them the ability to take their families up there. They can ski,
      there's an equestrian park, and there's two golf courses. If you have the opportunity, take time to go through
      that Grand Hyatt; it's an amazing complex. It all started right here in Weber County with a little seven room
      cabin.

    3. APPROVE RESOLUTION 04-2025 APPOINTING TRUSTEES TO THE BOARD OF THE OGDEN
       VALLEY PARK SERVICE AREA.
         Stacy Skeen (Commission Office): This park service area has amended their bylaws to allow their Board to
         make appointments in lieu of the Commission. Our attorneys are verifying whether this is allowed. Four
         positions are available.

         Chris Crockett (Lead Civil Attorney): We have been unable to locate any legal authority allowing the
         Service Area Board to make these appointments. Our opinion is the County is the appointing authority as in
         other Districts and Service Areas.

         Chair Bolos: I am comfortable with the research and result our attorneys have concluded. I am in favor of
         going forward with this Resolution.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 04-2025 approving Lon Child, Emily Phillips, Heather
         Lowe, and Marshall McGonegal as trustees to the board of the Ogden Valley Park Service Area; Commissioner
         Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey - aye
