3) Accessory Dwelling Unit Discussion 
 
Mr. Young provided a presentation and training on Accessory Dwelling Units.  Public process for 
the creation of an ordinance for the ADU’s. Mr. McNulty said he has had experience in Murray 
and Sandy and they both offer ADU’s, 800 square feet, two-bedroom, one bathroom, offer parking 
on site and not on the street. Salt Lake City  is different, ADU’s are working. In Murray in 2018 
and 2019, closer to 100 now. Commissioner Cohen  asked if they require  the owner live in the 
home. Mr. McNulty  said they are required to be owner occupied.  Primary dwelling is single 
family and is required to be have a business license and fees are nominal compared to business 
licenses. Commissioner Cohen asked if there are enforcement mechanisms. Mr. McNulty said yes 
two parts, land use and building dep artment. Restriction recorded with the county and stays with 
the property and new owner would need to come in and apply for conditional use.
