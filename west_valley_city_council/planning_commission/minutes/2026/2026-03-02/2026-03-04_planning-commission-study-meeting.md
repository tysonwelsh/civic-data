PRESENTATIONS:


GPZ-2-2026 Petition by VanTrust Real Estate requesting a General Plan change from
Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Light Manufacturing and a zone change from A
(Agricultural, minimum lot size ½ acre) and A-1 (Agricultural, minimum lot size 1 acre) to
M (Manufacturing). The property is located at 6290 West Parkway Blvd. on 51.8 acres.
(Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov).


Discussion: Steve presented the petition by VanTrust Real Estate requesting a General Plan
change from Large Lot Residential to Light Manufacturing and a zone change from A and A-1 to
M for the property located at 6290 West Parkway Boulevard. The property involves six parcels
owned by three. different entities, including one owned by the City. The intent is to do some
larger industrial buildings to the north and smaller flex industrial buildings to the south. Steve
mentioned concerns about the transition between residential and industrial areas. He presented
two potential options: maintaining the current residential boundary or implementing a buffer
using setbacks, elevation, or landscape. Commissioner Durfee asked about the height of the
smaller buildings. Steve will get more detail with VanTrust, though he does know they will all
be single level buildings. Commissioner Winters brought up the five-lane road being a good
transition, while also pointing out the General Plan focuses on residential housing.
Commissioner Durfee doesn’t mind the proposal but wants to shield the existing homes as much
as possible. A couple members had concerns about traffic counts on Parkway saying it might not
lend itself to residential. Per Steve, Public Works looks at that as part of the project so we can
look at recent traffic counts but also get a projection from Wasatch Front Regional Council.
Commissioner Winters also brought up that it might be nice to have a neighborhood in that area
instead of industrial buildings because it’s right next to a big, beautiful, new park.




SA-2-2026    Petition by Meridian Engineering, representing the Utah Department of
Transportation, requesting to amend Lots 302 & 309 and vacating Lot 301 of West Ridge
Commerce Park Phase 3 Subdivision and vacating a portion of Double Eagle Circle and a
portion of Westridge Boulevard. The property is located at 6046 W Double Eagle Cir and is
zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).


Discussion: Brock presented the request by Meridian Engineering requesting to amend lots 302
and 309 and vacating lot 301 of West Ridge Commerce Park Phase 3 Subdivision and vacating a
portion of Double Eagle Cir. It’s a clean-up of a number of lots that are part of Mountain View
Corridor. Brock informed the Commission that Steve Dale in Engineering learned that UDOT
has deeded Parcels B, C and D Parcel C to the City. No questions were asked regarding this
request.




SMI-1-2026 Petition by Jake Hone requesting minor subdivision approval for the Burke
Farm Subdivision. The subdivision is located at 3801 S 6400 W, consists of 7 lots on 1.84
acres, and is zoned R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square
feet). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).


Discussion: Brock presented the request by Jake Hone requesting minor subdivision approval for
the Burke Farm Subdivision located at 3801 S 6400 W consisting of 7 lots on 1.84 acres. The
original submittal showed 7 lots, but the home on the north end of the parcel will need to be a lot
in the subdivision as well, so there will be eight lots. Two of the lots have homes on them, so
there will be six new home lots. Public Works has reviewed the concept and has requested some
modifications. There is one lot that is quite narrow, so the applicant may need to extend the cul-
de-sac radius more to the west. No questions were asked.
