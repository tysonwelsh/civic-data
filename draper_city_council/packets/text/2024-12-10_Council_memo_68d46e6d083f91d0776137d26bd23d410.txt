MEMO
To: City Council
From: Travis DeJong
Date: 2024-12-10
Re: Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Single Event Permit for C.R.U.E. Group
LLC dba Utah Premier Events.



Comments:

C.R.U.E. Group LLC dba Utah Premier Events will hold an event serving alcoholic
beverages at 14658 S Bangerter Pkwy Suite 400 on December 31, 2024 from 4:00PM until
11:59PM. They are seeking Local Consent for a Single Event Permit.

They have provided the following:
- Floor map showing where alcohol is to be served, stored, and consumed.
- A background check conducted by the Bureau of Criminal Identification.

We are currently in communication with the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services
(DABS) regarding the bond made payable to the aforementioned Department. The
applicant has indicated the bond has been paid. Under Draper City Municipal Code and
DABS regulations, the bond is required.

The findings for Local Consent approval are as follows:

Per Utah State Code 32B-9-201(5), single event permits do not have any proximity
requirements to meet.

DCMC 6-4-050 states that organizations are limited to four (4) single event permits per
calendar year if at least one permit is a one hundred twenty (120) hours single event
permit. Organizations may apply for up to twelve (12) single event permits per calendar
year if they are all seventy-two (72) hour single event permits.

The applicant has applied for one (the current application) seventy-two hour permit in
calendar year 2024 and meets this requirement.

DCMC 6-4-050 places no restrictions on the number of single event permits that can be
granted by the City.
