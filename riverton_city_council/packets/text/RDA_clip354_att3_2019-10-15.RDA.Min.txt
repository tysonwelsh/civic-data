30   Discussion / Action Items
31
32      RDA Resolution No. 19-09 – Designating the Riverton Downtown East Community
33      Reinvestment Project Area be Created and Authorizing and Directing all Necessary
34      Action by the Agency, Staff and Consultants
35
36   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that the proposed resolution was
37   the first step in the process of establishing economic development tools for the City to utilize in
38   its efforts to revitalize the Downtown Area. He said the resolution created a project area in the
39   Downtown, defining the area in which the City can then create more specific redevelopment
40   projects and public/private partnerships. The project area itself does not have a financial impact,
41   nor does it commit the Redevelopment Agency or the City to any particular project or property.
42   He said it designates the area in which the City, through the Redevelopment Area, has
43   determined that future projects would be established.
44
45   Discussion was held regarding the expansion of the project area on the east side of Redwood
46   Road to include remnant parcels south to Bangerter Highway.
