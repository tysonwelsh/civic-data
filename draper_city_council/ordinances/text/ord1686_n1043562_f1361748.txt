City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1686

On December 2, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1686 Amending
the Official Zoning Map of Draper City for Approximately 19,621 Square Feet of
Property from Ra1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 Square Foot Lot Minimum) To R3
(Single Family Residential, 13,000 Square Foot Minimum) Zone for the Property Located
at Approximately 11730 S. 700 West within Draper City, otherwise Known as The Huber
Zoning Map Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City
Recorder’s Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/

Published this 5th day of December, 2025
