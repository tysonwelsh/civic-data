Proposed Ordinance 2022-6 amending the Ogden Municipal Code to
revise Council member qualifications; and provisions related to Council
meetings
A memo from Council staff regarding proposed amendments to Ogden Municipal Code Title Two came before the
Council for consideration. The memo stated the proposed amendments to Title Two would allow a Council member to retain their
municipal district seat if a change in the boundary lines of the municipal district results in the Council member residing outside the
new district boundaries. An additional change would reduce the number of regular Council meetings from three meetings per
month to two meetings per month. Redevelopment Agency meetings are not affected.
Council Executive Director Eller-Smith summarized the memo and stated there are three basic requirements that must be
followed when redrawing municipal districts:
e Each district must be substantially equal in population.
e ach district must be geographically compact.
e Areas within a district must be contiguous.

As staff members evaluated several potential options for new municipal district boundaries, it became apparent that the
changes at the state and county levels had the potential to impact municipal district boundaries in such a way as to leave one or
more Council members outside the boundaries of the new municipal districts. Ogden Municipal Code § 2-3-2 requires that each
member of the City Council shall be a registered voter and reside within the City for a period of twelve (12) consecutive months
prior to the date of election and remain a resident during the member’s term of office; and be a resident of the district from which
the Council member is elected at least sixty (60) days prior to the election. The Code further states that “if'a Council member shall

cease to possess any of these qualifications” the member’s office “shall immediately become vacant.” Therefore, in current form,
the Municipal Code requires any Council member elected to a municipal district to vacate their seat if a change in boundaries
results in the Council member living outside the boundary of the new municipal district to which the Council member was elected.
Ms. Eller-Smith then touched briefly on the Council meeting schedule; in July 2021 the Council started holding regular City
Council meetings on the first and third Tuesdays only, skipping the regular fourth Tuesday meeting. The new schedule was
implemented on a trial basis to determine if there would be any negative impacts on workload or efficiency or have other
unintended consequences. After the six-month trial period, the Council and staff had observed no negative impacts. The Council
requested that the changes to the meeting schedule be formalized, both in the Council Rules and Norm and by a change to the
Municipal Code.
Chair Nadolski introduced in writing proposed Ordinance 2022-6, entitled:
“An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah, amending the Ogden Municipal Code by amending Section 2-3-2 to revise
Council member qualifications; amending Section 2-3-4 to revise provisions related to Council meetings; and
by providing that this ordinance shall become effective immediately upon posting after final passage.”
A copy of the proposed ordinance was deposited with the City Recorder and ordered that the City Recorder have at least

Public Comments

Heath Satow referenced current drought conditions and asked that the Council reevaluate the City’s response and
recommendations for water conservation community wide. He then suggested the Council begin considering fireworks regulations
earlier than last year to be fair to fireworks vendors and customers; he suggested a ban be imposed that can be lifted later in the
season if conditions warrant.

Angel Castillo thanked the Council for their discussion of the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) program
and asked that they consider cultural events that should be eligible for CDBG grant funds. It is important to make
accommodations for all individuals and cultures represented in the City.

Mayor Comments
Mayor Caldwell stated his thoughts are with the country of Ukraine and its residents.
