ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the zone from R-1-10
     (Residential, Single Family, 10,000 sq ft minimum lot size) to PD-R (Planned
     Development Residential) on property generally located at 1050 East 600
     South.

      Planner Dan Boles presented the request to change the zone from R-1-10 to PD-R on
      property generally located at 1050 East 600 South. He provided a PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: Zoning map; General plan map; Site
      plan; Parking plan; and Amenities. The Planning Commission recommended
      approval.

      Councilmember Larkin commented that the proposed project is an impressive on just
      .86 acres.

      Councilmember Hughes asked for clarification regarding the fact that this property
      adjoins commercial, when the zoning is changed it can change the setbacks for the
      adjacent property.

      Mr. Boles explained this will not occur as the property is built out. If the adjacent
      property was vacant, it may occur.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to approve the
                    ordinance changing the zone from R-1-10 to PD-R on property
                    generally located at 1050 East 600 South.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a roll call vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the zone from A-20 (Agriculture
     20-acre minimum lot size) to RE-20 (Residential Estates 20,000 square foot
     minimum lot size) on approximately 9.824 acres generally located at Old
     Seegmiller Road and 3275 East.

      Planner Mike Hadley presented the request to change the zone from A-20 to RE-20
      on approximately 9.824 acres generally located at Old Seegmiller Road and 3275
      East. The applicant is asking for two (2) different parcels. The applicant is
      requesting to rezone parcel 1 to RE-20 and parcel 2 to A-1. He provided a
      PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: Vicinity map; Zone change
      map; Zoning map; and General plan map.

      Councilmember Curtis commented he remembers this item from the Planning
      Commission meeting. It has an unusual configuration; therefore, he asked Mr.
      Hadley to explain that piece.

      Mr. Hadley explained the applicant is providing the City property for a detention
      basin.

      Assistant Public Works Director Wes Jenkins noted staff is trying to work with the
      property owner and realign the road in this area so that it comes in at a correct
      angle with the developer. The proposed trail will run along the southside of the
      property.

      Mayor Randall asked staff to clarify the acreage.

AMEND AND ADOPT FEES FOR VARIOUS SERVICES AND RECREATION
ACTIVITIES/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance amending and adopting fees for various
     services and recreation activities.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained most of these changes have already been
      adopted, this request is just a cleanup item, putting all of the fees into a
      consolidated master fee schedule.

      Councilmember Larkin inquired about the Town Square rental fees. She asked what
      people are renting. She also asked about the time frames for Seegmiller Farm. She
      asked the times be looked at and removed if necessary.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained they are reserving space, typically under the
      clock tower.

      The Council discussed creating non-resident fees.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to approve the
                    ordinance amending and adopting fees for various services and
                    recreation activities.
      SECOND:
          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.
      VOTE:
          Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye
                    Councilmember Curtis – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
