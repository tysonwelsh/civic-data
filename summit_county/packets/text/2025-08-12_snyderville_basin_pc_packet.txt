Proposal 
The applicant, on behalf of the owner, is requesting Conditional Use approval for an Accessory 
Dwelling Unit (ADU) in Unit 6 of the Altitude Lofts Condominiums. The applicant states that the 
three-level unit will include a ground floor garage, second-floor offices and conference area, 
and third floor ADU.

Background 
On May 22, 2025, the applicant submitted a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) application to 
construct an Accessory Dwelling Unit within the third floor of Unit 6 of the Altitude Loft 
Condominiums, located at 4123 Forestdale Dr Unit #6 (Unit ALTLC-6).  
 
Mark A. Abbott is listed as the owner of Parcel ALTLC-6. The property is located at 4123 N 
Forestdale Dr #6. The subject Unit is located within a shell building (Building A).  Both buildings 
on the site were approved as an amendment to a Low Impact Permit on May 26, 2022. In 
September of 2022, Buildings A and B were condominiumized and recorded as the Altitude Loft 
Condominiums Plat. The Altitude Loft Condominiums are located within the Park City Business 
Center (PCBC) and are subject to the Park City Business Center Development Agreement as well 
as the Parking Management Plan (Exhibit C). 
 
The proposal for Unit 6 will include space for garage on the ground floor; office space and 
conference room on the second floor; and an ADU on the third floor.  
 
The original shell building approval created a third level that exceeds the allowable area for an 
Accessory Dwelling Unit (1,000 square feet). Due to this restriction, the applicant has proposed 
walling off a section of the third level in order to meet the size limitations for ADUs. 
 
The applicant proposes an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) consisting of approximately 880 
square feet, with an inaccessible/non-occupied space of approximately 230 square feet. The 
ADU will be accessed via a staircase from the second floor. Exhibit B includes the floor plans for 
this proposal.

3 
 
 
Section 10-2-10 of the Snyderville Basin Development Code lists Accessory Dwelling Units as a 
Conditional Use within the Service Commercial Zone. Only one (1) ADU may be permitted per 
commercial unit within the zone. Conditions of Approval have been included to mitigate the 
potential for two ADUs to be built within this unit. Staff and service providers have reviewed 
the project and found that it complies, as conditioned, with all requirements of the Code and 
the Park City Business Center Development Agreement.
