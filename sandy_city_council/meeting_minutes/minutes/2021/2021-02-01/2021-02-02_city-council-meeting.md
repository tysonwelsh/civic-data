Mayor's Report

                                  Mayor Bradburn addressed a personal issue - he sold his house and will continue to live
                                  in Sandy through his term. He thanked all those involved in the Citizen Awards
                                  Ceremony. The business awards will be Thursday, February 25th. He also gave an
                                  update on the Covid-19 vaccination process and new vaccination centers in set up in
                                  Utah.


CAO Report

                                  Matt Huish, CAO, gave an update on the City's efforts to implement the WellBeing Survey
                                  - the survey is planned to go live on Monday and will run for three weeks. He also
                                  encouraged all to attend the Community Arts Guild program playing at Mt. Jordan Middle
                                  School. He briefed the Council on the RFP process and timeline for the Alta Canyon
                                  Recreation Center project.

Legislative Report

                                  Evelyn Everton, Deputy Mayor, updated the Council on bills currently under review at the
                                  Legislature. She invited the Council and residents to participate in the February 6th
                                  mid-session legislative update hosted by the City. Council question and comments
                                  followed.

Information Items


1.         21-043                 Hale Centre Theatre providing annual update to the City Council.

                   Attachments:   2020 HCT Sandy City Council

                                  Council Member Christensen introduced Mark Dietlein, Quinn Dietlein and Michael Fox
                                  with the Hale Centre Theater who presented to the Council a progress report on the
                                  theatre. Mark Dietlein spoke about the Covid-19 pandemic and restrictions and the
                                  impact of those restrictions on the theater. Michael Fox presented on the state of the
                                  theatre and provided a two year comparison of revenues, expenses, ticket sales and
                                  productions from 2019 and 2020.

                                  Council questions and comments followed.

4.         21-042                 Council Member Christensen recommending the Council initiate a land use
                                  code amendment to create the Historic Sandy Neighborhood Farmer’s
                                  Market zone.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Memo from Council Member Christensen
                                  Draft Kuwahara Code Amendment - Staff Proposal

                                  Council Member Christensen introduced the process to create a new zone in Historic
                                  Sandy. The zone is specific to the Historic Sandy area and is a land use code
                                  amendment creating the Historic Sandy Neighborhood Farmer's Market zone. James
                                  Sorenson, Community Development Director, responded to Council questions. Ms.
                                  Christensen asked for feedback and recommended the staff continue the process to
                                  create this new zone. Council questions, comments and discussion occurred during the
                                  presentation.

                                  Public Comment:
                                  Chair Sharkey invited the public to comment. Dustin Fratto provided instruction.
                                  Ecomments were received on this item.

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke about the ecomment features and mentioned that he was
                                  redacting part of his submitted comment for the next item.

                                  Ms. Brooke D'Sousa commended the Council and staff for their efforts and creative work
                                  on the Kuwahara rezone in Historic Sandy.

                                  Mr. Alex and Sarah Kuwahara thanked the Council for their efforts and hard work. This is
                                  an answer to their prayers.

                                  Public Comment Closed.
                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Monica Zoltanski, to
                                  direct staff to take the draft zone through the standard public process, including
                                  Planning Commission review and recommendation, before coming back to
                                  Council for final adoption... The motion carried by the following vote:

                          Yes:    7-    Alison Stroud
                                        Kristin Coleman-Nicholl
                                        Zach Robinson
                                        Monica Zoltanski
