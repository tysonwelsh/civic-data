64   approval for an 8-foot fence at 1455 North Sierra Way.
65   Commissioner Beau Jones joined the meeting.
66
67   Jacob Curtis presented information on the item.
68
69   The applicant, Ryan Wilson, was present. He stated this would help cut down on the wind.
70
71   Commissioner Vice Chair Everett opened the public hearing.
72
73   Joe Caudell left an online comment which was submitted into the record.
74   “I am submitting this comment to oppose the requested conditional use permit. My home is
75   located at 1448 N Sierra Way, on the eastern side of Sierra Way, across the street from the home
76   in question and slightly to the southeast.
77
78   I would first note that I am in agreement with the DRC redline comments from Kim and Gary. I
79   can attest to the fact that the grading is very slight, nothing unusual at all. I can also attest to the
80   fact that the wind in this neighborhood is significantly less than anything I experienced during
81   the five years that I owned and lived in a home in the Canyon Hills neighborhood of northeast
82   Lehi - in that neighborhood I was unaware of any 8 foot privacy fencing, and certainly none on
83   any corner lots.
84
85   As support for opposition, I do believe that pursuant to Chapter 9, Section 09.050.A.2 of the Lehi
86   City Development Code, the proposed conditional use would be detrimental to the safety of
87   persons and properties within the vicinity of the proposed conditional use. The home in question
88   is on a corner lot right at the intersection of 4100 W, the main entrance to the community, a main
89   N-S arterial street 1470 N, as well as Sierra Way. As you can see in the first picture of the
90   application (Page 20), if an 8 foot fence were placed, it would significantly obstruct the lines of
     Lehi City Planning Commission                       2                                      March 28, 2024
 91   sight for vehicular and pedestrian traffic flowing to or away from 4100 W, 1470 N, and in
 92   particular Sierra Way.
 93
 94   I would also note that having lived in two other fairly large planned unit developments in North
 95   Salt Lake and Lehi, I don't believe it is common at all to permit 8 foot high privacy fences in
 96   PUDs per standard CC&Rs and ancillary rules and regulations issued pursuant to CC&Rs - as
 97   Edge Homes is still the sole declarant governing the CC&Rs, and the development is in its early
 98   stages, no uniform and consistent fencing standards have been published by Edge Homes. It is
 99   very likely that at some future date restrictions will be placed on fence height, and so allowing
100   this would create an inconsistent design standard in the PUD as the fence would likely be
101   grandfathered by the HOA.
102
103   Lastly, if this conditional use permit were issued, it is not unlikely that other neighbors tying in
104   to the 8 foot fence will want to maintain a consistent look - the result would be 8 foot fencing
105   through the homes bounded by the west side of Sierra Way, the south side of 1470 N between
106   Linen Way and Sierra Way, the east side of Linen Way, and the north side of a to-be-built street
107   between Linen Way and Sierra Way. If such a precedent were to be set, similar vehicular and
108   pedestrian obstruction previously mentioned would follow on each subsequent corner.
109
110   Taking into account that the rationales proffered by the applicant for a conditional use permit are
111   not justified, the detriment to safety previously described, the unusual nature of an 8 foot fence in
112   a PUD, and the concerning precedent this would create, compounding the expressed safety
113   concerns, I believe these factors make clear that the conditional use permit should be denied.
114
115   Thank you for your time and consideration. Joe Caudell”
