City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On April 12, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1534, amending Draper
City Municipal Code 9-6-080 relating to nonconformities created by public action.
The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 13th day of April 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper
City Recorder.
