WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
OCTOBER 28, 2020

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 11:00 a.m. on October 28, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist,
Commissioner Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE LED BY: Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf
REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2664, AN AMENDED
RESOLUTION PROVIDING CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDS TO PURCHASE

EQUIPMENT FOR THE EMERGENCY SERVICES OFFICE AND/OR

HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that the previous resolution was amended
to allow the Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District use of designated CARES Funds
to purchase necessary emergency operations equipment as the EOC and HVFSSD often

work together.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2671, AN AMENDED
RESOLUTION PROVIDING CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDS TO PURCHASE
EQUIPMENT FOR THE EMERGENCY SERVICES OFFICE _AND/OR
HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

See previous.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2664
and Resolution No. R-2020-2671, An Amended Resolution Providing
Coronavirus Relief Funds to Purchase Equipment for the Emergency
Services Office and/or Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2674, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A GRANT DONATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY AND WASHINGTON COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Washington County School District Accounting Director Brent Bills explained that the
School District has been impacted for COVID-19 and is in need of additional Personal
Protection Equipment (PPE) for the students and faculty. The PPE being requested consists
of: masks, nitrile gloves, thermometers, student COVID tests, and chrome books with a

cellular plan to be used by quarantined students.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2674, A
Resolution Approving a Grant Donation Agreement Between
Washington County and Washington County School District for
$726,650. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _ R-2020-2675, A RESOLUTION
PROVIDING CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDS TO PURCHASE EQUIPMENT
AND IMPROVEMENTS TO LEGACY PARK

Chairman Iverson explained that it would be more appropriate to purchase the requested
items from the regular budget rather than from CARES funds and recommended striking

this item from the agenda.

MOTION: Motion by Chairman Iverson to Strike Resolution No. R-2020-2675.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION _NO._R-2020-2676, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AN AMENDMENT TO THE TEMPORARY SHUTTLE SERVICES
AGREEMENTS BETWEEN _ ST. GEORGE SHUTTLE AND WASHINGTON
COUNTY FOR SHUTTLE SERVICES IN SPRINGDALE AND ZNP

Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that due to the reduced use of buses, the initial
allocated funding for the Zion shuttle system has not been fully utilized. It is recommended
to amend the shuttle services agreement to extend the agreement through December 31,
2020 as the original agreement was set to expire on October 31, 2020.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-
2676, A Resolution Approving an Amendment to the Temporary
Shuttle Services Agreements Between St. George Shuttle and
Washington County for Shuttle Services in Springdale and ZNP.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

COMMISSIONERS REPORT

Chairman Iverson stated that October 30, 2020 will be the last day that the Commission
will accept requests for CARES funding. Applications must be fully completed in order to
be considered.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
11:23 a.m.
