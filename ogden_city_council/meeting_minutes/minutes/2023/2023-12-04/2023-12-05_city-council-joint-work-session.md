Agenda Review

scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.
Affordable Housing and Home Ownership Discussion
The City Council is interested in ascertaining whether there are additional policies or programs that the City could
undertake to facilitate home ownership. The Council has invited developers, experts in commercial lending, non-profits, and
investment groups to share their expertise with the Council. The Council hopes that these discussions will lead to new
opportunities for home ownership in Ogden. The further discussion will focus on unique approaches to facilitating home
ownership, such as rent-to-own options, and other types of investment that improve housing stability.
Steve Waldrip and Bruce Jardine, representatives of the Rocky Mountain Home Fund (RMHF), used audio visual aids
and a PowerPoint presentation to discuss unique approaches to providing a pathway to home ownership. They discussed the
concept of equity sharing between the investment fund and the homeowner can help those who might not otherwise be able to
afford a home.
Discussion among the Council and Mr. Waldrip and Mr. Jardine centered on topics such as those eligible to apply for
funding through the RMHF; the income thresholds for those who will be able to secure lending; and the average home price for
the program.

Proposed Request for Recertification of Ogden City Justice Court |
Justice Court Administrator Richardson explained justice courts must be certified by the State Justice Courts Standards
Committee and the Judicial Council every four years. The Administrative Office of the Courts must receive a request for
certification no later than December 15, 2023. She reviewed the Utah State Code requirements for justice courts; daily operations
of the court; funding and resources available to those who are referred to the justice court. The City Attorney has provided an
opinion letter outlining the operational procedures of the Ogden City Justice Court and the feasibility of continuing operations as
required by law. Judge Clay Stucki has signed an affidavit affirming that the Court is in compliance with all statutory
requirements and minimum standards established by the Judicial Council.
There was discussion among the Council, Ms. Richardson, and Judge Stucki about the caseload and staffing levels of the
Court and revenues/expenses of the Justice Court.
