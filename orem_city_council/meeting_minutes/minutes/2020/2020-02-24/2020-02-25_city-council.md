     DISCUSSION – City of Orem Compensation Philosophy

Keri Rugg, Human Resource Director stated they have contracted with NFP, have created a
steering team and along with working with Executive Staff come up with a compensation
philosophy. She reviewed the proposed changes to the Guideline (section 3.4) and the Statement
of Compensation Philosophy (section3.4.1).

Discussion ensued regarding wage increase and compensation being based on both merit base
along with a performance base with a greater emphasis on communication. How they will address
each position and how often they review that information. Council discussed the level of review
provided from the City Manager during the budget process. Discussion of how to address any
positions that come back that are not competitive and if they are compared to only municipalities
and/or private sector.

Mr. Davidson stated the challenge they face is there is some degree and expectation that they can

presented. This will help to represent to the employees these changes are endorsed by the council.


       PRESENTATION – Pediatric Trauma Care Unit at Timpanogos Hospital

 Dr. Trotter addressed the council stating Utah County is one of the fastest growing
 counties in the nation and is the fastest growing county in Utah. The population of
 Utah County is approximately 622,000 and is projected to double by 2050. According
 to national standards, in areas with over 100 pediatric trauma cases, it is recommended
 to have at least a level 2 trauma system in place. Currently most of the pediatric trauma
 cases are being transferred to Primary Children's Hospital in Salt Lake City.
 Transportation costs, financial burden, inconvenience and logistical concerns for
 families are reasons to support the creation of a level 2 pediatric trauma care unit in
 Utah County. The benefits of having a pediatric trauma care unit in Utah County
 include improved outcomes, better survival rates, improved patient access, expanded
 medical services, education and outreach to more hospitals and reduction of costs and
 stresses to local communities. They are asking the State Trauma Committee to support
 efforts to expand trauma services in Utah County. His presentation included some of
 the following information:
State/National Trauma System
     Level l- Highest level of care. Typically, University system/research/unique subspecialty
     care
     Level 2- Clinically nearly the same as Level l, very capable facility with specialists
     Level 3-ER physician, surgeon
     Level 4-ER personnel (RN or midlevel), possibly surgeon
     Level 5-RN available

Benefits of having Pediatric Trauma Center in Utah County
    -Improved survival (mortality rate drops 25%, complication rate drops 10%)
    -Improved outcomes when compared to pediatric care at adult trauma Centers
    -Improved patient access to definitive care in timely manner (15 min OR availability)
    -Improved patient care throughout entire hospital ("Halo effect")

     PRESENTATION - Update to the Comprehensive Financial Sustainability Plan

Mr. Davidson stated they continue to monitor year to year where the city is going financially. They
feel it is important to look at the bigger broader long term picture. In 2014 they initiated the
development of a Long-Term Financial Sustainability Plan. About 6 months ago the felt it was
time to freshen up the plan to see where they were today and if they had achieved what they wanted.
Some of what the financial future was predication on was specific development issues. Now in
2020 they are looking at what the future holds and how can they appropriately plan for the future.
They contacted LYRB (Lewis Young Robertson and Burmingham) and asked then to help them
update this Financial Sustainability Plan. LYRB is here to give and overview as to the effort and
how things look today and where we are going in the future.

          SITE PLAN – Site plan approval of Stewart Retail Addition located at 21 West
          University Parkway in the PD12 zone.

Mr. Bench stated the PD12 zone requires the City Council to approve site plans in that zone. The
applicant requests approval of a site plan to build a new building at 21 West University Parkway
fronting on University Parkway. The applicant proposes to remove the front part of the existing
building located at 41 West University Parkway to create parking and an easement through the
properties.
The new building will be set back approximately ten feet from University Parkway which will
match the setback of the building directly to the east. The setback requirement in the PD12 zone
was recently modified by the City Council on November 19, 2019 to ten feet from University
Parkway and Main Street.
The proposed new building will potentially have five retail units. The new easement through the
property between the buildings will make access to the businesses easier. The proposed 24 foot
ingress and egress easement is part of the new Barber Bros Subdivision, Plat C, which was
approved by the Planning Commission on February 5, 2020.
The proposed elevations will match the style and colors of the existing structure currently located
on the site. The proposed structure will have an overall height of forty-one feet six inches.

Mayor Brunst inquired as to where they current businesses would be going. Mr. Bench stated the
rental business will be remaining and there will be additional spaces available.

Mrs. Lauret inquired of parking to ensure efficient parking. Mr. Bench indicated they had met the
requirement for parking.
