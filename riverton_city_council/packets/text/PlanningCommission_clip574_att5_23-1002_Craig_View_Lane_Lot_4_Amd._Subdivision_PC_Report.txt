 Background

This is a public hearing to amend and extend Lot 4 of Craig View Lane by adding 7,510-sf to the
existing lot. The additional area comes from the adjoining parcel which is outside of the
subdivision. This application would only adjust the common boundary and no new lots are
created, and no new improvements are required. The resulting lot and parcel meet the minimum
requirements for size and setbacks in the zone.

Craig View Lane is a 4-lot subdivision using a private lane which was approved by the City in
2021 with the subdivision plat recorded in September of 2021. The zoning in the subdivision is
R-3 (3 units per acre and 14,000-sf minimum lots) with lots ranging from 14,002-sf to 21,759 -sf.
Lot 4, the subject of this amendment is the smallest lot in the subdivision at 0.32-acre.

The developer of Craig View Lane owns both Lot 4 and Parcel # 27-27-101-084 which adjoins
Lot 4 on the east side. He would like to make Lot 4 larger by adjusting the subdivision boundary
east. The resulting Lot 4 would be 21,512-sf or 0.49-acre. The resulting Parcel # 27-27-101-084
would be over 22,000-sf or greater than 0.50-acres.

Staff from Engineering and Planning, have reviewed this proposed plat amendment and are
recommending approval with the conditions listed above in the example motion.
