                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-60

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH
THE JORDAN SCHOOL DISTRICT TO CONTINUE BUS SERVICE FOR STUDENTS
       RESIDING WITHIN THE WESTERN SPRINGS NEIGHBORHOOD

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council finds and determines that the safe transport of
children under the age of 12 years to their respective elementary school is vital to the
transportation safety of all Riverton residents; and

        WHEREAS, historically, the Jordan School District has funded school bussing services
for elementary school children to various elementary schools within Riverton City; and

       WHEREAS, some of the school bus routes transported children to school on roads which
do not offer sidewalks for pedestrians such as elementary school children, known as “hazardous
transportation routes”; and

        WHEREAS, under Utah Law, the State of Utah funds up to 85% of transportation needs
for elementary school children located more than 1 ½ miles away from their respective
elementary school; and

       WHEREAS, in July of 2010, the Jordan School District cancelled funding for all school
bussing services for elementary age children who live less than 1 ½ miles from the Jordan
School District because such services are not funded by the Utah Legislature; and

       WHEREAS, foregoing notwithstanding, the Jordan School District has continued to
provide bussing service to students within the Western Springs neighborhood, owing to unique
transportation issues affecting said neighborhood; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council hereby recognizes and appreciates the Jordan
School District for continuing to provide bussing services for elementary aged children residing
within the Western Springs neighborhood; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has worked to provide the safest possible pedestrian
pathways for elementary school students by hiring crossing guards, re-striping roadway surfaces,
and planning roadway widening projects to construct sidewalks; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City is working to provide improvements to 13400 South Street,
in the vicinity of the Mountain View Village development project, to help provide students with
safe crossing over 13400 South Street; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City needs school bussing services to be maintained by the Jordan
School District while Riverton City constructs roadway improvements on several streets to build
sidewalks for pedestrians;
        WHEREAS, the Jordan School District has expressed a willingness to continue to
provide bussing service for the benefit of Western Springs students, provided Riverton City share
in the cost of providing bussing service, in an amount not to exceed $40,000.00;

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby authorizes the Mayor to execute a Memorandum of
          Understanding with the Jordan School District to continue bussing service for students
          living in the Western Springs neighborhood, upon verification the same has been
          approved as to legal form by the Riverton City Attorney.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 18th day of
