2. Presentations and Reports

2.1 Environmental Sustainability Committee Annual Report- Todd Munger
The committee has just elected a new Chair. Todd Munger outlined some of the projects the
committee undertook in 2024. Highlights included the Radon Gas Meter Checkout at the Lehi
Public Library, the Traverse Mountain Conservation Easement, the Rain Harvest Utah initiative,
new Jordan River Interpretive Signage, and the Water Wise Landscape of the month program.
For 2025, the ESC is planning on continuing the Trex Plastic recycling Challenge and the Hefty
Renew Program, both of which collect single-use plastics which are then turned into patio and
decking material. Additionally, they would like to have a dedicated spot for hard to recycle
plastic and glass. They would also like to expand the Water Wise Landscape program to include
businesses and other sustainable practices beyond landscaping. The mayor expressed his
appreciation for the work the ESC has done.

2.2 Lehi Cares Coalition Report
The coalition recently began reaching out to people who work directly with youth (teachers,
coaches, doctors, etc.) to communicate the top four risk factors they see, namely low
commitment to school, depressive symptoms, and anti-social and apathetic behaviors on the part
of parents and students. This data, collected through the SHARP survey, will benefit the
coalition as it will support grant applications in the future. Lehi Cares focuses on an upstream

7. Consideration of Ordinance #06-2025 approval of a comprehensive Development Code
   amendment to Table 05.030.A, the residential table of uses.
   Kim Struthers presented the ordinance. While in the process of looking at the nonresidential
   table, the planning division took the opportunity to clarify and simplify the residential table.
   Most of the items being taken off are already covered in the accessory uses chapter. Animal
   rights will be located exclusively in chapter 12, rather than in the table as well.

    Councilor Stallings asked if private schools should remain conditional use, even if all other
    types of schools are permitted now. Struthers communicated the desire of the Planning
    Commission to eliminate as many conditional use situations as possible. Councilor Stallings
    clarified that requirements could still be placed on schools if they were permitted. She supports
    minimizing conditional use instances.

    Councilor Stallings asked if golf courses could be a conditional use situation instead of a
    permitted one. Ryan Wood stated that litigation when it comes to golf courses and damage to
    personal property is difficult to litigate because it is the individual golfers who are legally
    responsible, not the course itself. Councilor Albrecht asked if there was space anywhere in
    Lehi for another golf course to be constructed. The mayor could only think of one location. He
    shared an anecdote about people building intentionally by nuisances to highlight the fact that
    the council will never be able to prevent all complaints or problems.

    Councilor Hancock and Councilor Newall both agreed with minimizing conditional use
    situations, as they are frustrating for both commissioners and citizens. Councilor Newall
    worries that complete elimination will leave the planning commission with no alternatives once
    they approve something.

    Motion:     Councilor Albrecht moved to approve Ordinance #06-2025 approving a
                comprehensive Development Code amendment to Table 05.030.A, the residential
                table of uses. Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

Councilor Stallings asked that the approval be amended to permit all schools in residential areas.

    Amended Motion: Councilor Albrecht moved to approve Ordinance #06-2025 approving a
            comprehensive Development Code amendment to Table 05.030.A, the residential
            table of uses, with the amendment that all schools be permitted in residential areas.
            Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Absent; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Newall, Yes; and Councilor Stallings, Yes. The motion passed with 4 in favor and

8. Consideration of Resolution #2025-09 approving the amended Planning Commission
   Bylaws.
   Kim Struthers presented the two changes. The first adds a citation to the city code where the
   duties of the planning commission were outlined. The second makes the final action of the
   commission the point at which appeals are based off, rather than the approval of the minutes.
   This is now possible because of technical advances that give citizens access to meeting
   recordings.

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to approve Resolution #2025-09 approving the
                amended Planning Commission Bylaws. Councilor Newall seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Absent; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Newall, Yes; and Councilor Stallings, Yes. The motion passed with 4 in favor and
    1 absent.
