PC 23-060 Finley Short Term Rental [Conditional Use] Leland & Maisie Finley, property owners
are requesting a Conditional Use Permit to operate a two-bedroom short-term rental in the basement
of a home located at 1444 East 1220 North in the Suburban Neighborhood Residential (NR-4) zone;
TIN 07-095-0009 (Hillcrest Neighborhood).

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL
1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
    available in the Community Development Department.
2. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a two (2) bedroom short-term rental occupying a portion
    of the basement in the single-family.
3. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes one party of up to two (2) people per bedroom with two
    (2) additional individuals for a maximum of six (6) occupants in the STR.
4. A short-term rental may not be the subject of multiple rental contracts by separate individuals,
    groups, or parties for the same night or nights.
5. Occupancy for the single-family home portion of the structure shall comply with LDC regulations.
6. A minimum of two (2) parking stalls are required for the STR and two (2) are required for the
    residence. All parking areas shall be located off-street and outside the front yard setback. The
    four (4) total parking stalls shall meet minimum LDC dimensions (9x18) per stall. Parking shall
    be reviewed and approved prior to the issuance of a business license.
7. Parking of recreational vehicles, trailers, boats, ATVs or similar vehicles associated with the STR
    shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on-street.
8. Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.
9. Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two
    consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records
    available for review and inspection by Logan City.
10. Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8am.
11. A Logan City building/fire inspection is required which may result in building remodeling and
    building permits prior to the issuance of a business license.


12. A Logan City business license is required before a rental contract can be issued.
13. The STR shall comply with local and state Building, Health, Fire, Safety, Maintenance and
    Nuisance Codes prior to operating.
14. Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast and reviewed and approved prior to the
    issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.
15. No signs are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All signage shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.
16. No fences are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All fences shall be approved and
    permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.
17. Prior to issuance of a Building Permit, the Director of Community Development shall receive a
    written memorandum from each of the following departments or agencies indicating that their
    requirements have been satisfied:
    a. Water/Cross Connection - contact 716-9627
        1. All points of use of water down stream of this properties water meter should meet Utah’s
            current IPC plumbing code and amendments. This would help minimize the risk of
            contamination of the water for homes occupants and Logan Cities water system. This also
            would help minimize liabilities to the homeowner.
    b. Business Licensing - contact 716-9230
        1. Business license required.
    c. Building Safety - contact 716-9032
        1. Egress windows, smoke/co detectors per the 2021 IRC

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL
1. The proposed project conforms to the requirements of the Land Development Code and is listed
   as a conditional use in the Use Table.
2. This STR meets the City’s STR density and location parameters as specified in Chapter
   17.37.130.
3. As conditioned the use is compatible with the surrounding land uses and will not interfere with
   the use and enjoyment of adjoining or area properties.
4. The streets providing access and other infrastructure to the subject property have adequate
   capacities and level of service for the conditional use.
5. The Planning Commission has substantiated the findings for a Conditional Use Permit in Chapter
   17.42.050.

PC 23-061 K & B Apartments [Design Review] Justin Campbell/K & B 1 LLC, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a Design Review Permit for a new four-story, six-unit apartment building
with an eight-stall parking structure on the first floor, and 19 surface parking stalls located at
approximately 698 East 700 North in the Campus Residential (CR) zone; TIN 06-052-0020; -0021
(Adams Neighborhood).

STAFF: Russ Holley, Planner reviewed the request for a new apartment building located at
approximately 698 East 700 North in the Campus Residential (CR) zone. The proposed plan is for
a six-unit apartment building that is four stories tall with parking on the first floor. The proposed
design of the building meets all requirements listed in the Land Development Code. The applicant
is proposing vertical landscaping along the west façade to screen the concrete walls of the parking
garage. As proposed the project meets all parking, setback, frontage, coverage, and height
requirements in the Land Development Code. The outdoor space and landscaping will be reviewed
for compliance with the building permit application.

Commissioner Doutre asked if there are any concerns with the dumpster located so close to the
street. R. Holley said his concerns with the dumpster location is the screening and people parking in
front of the dumpster blocking the sidewalk.

PROPONENT: Justin Campbell asked for clarification on the boundary line adjustment in condition
number four. R. Holley said condition number four refers to the boundary line between the two
parcels included in this project, not the south property line.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Heare said he would like to see a screen or gate to conceal the
dumpster from the street. Commissioner Peterson said that even if a gate is required it does not
guarantee that they will keep the gate closed. Commissioner Doutre said that a closed gate could
encourage parking and an open gate could impede the sidewalk and encourage public dumping.
Commissioner Doutre pointed out that condition number nine requires the dumpster to be screened.
R. Holley said that staff can review the dumpster screening with the building permit, or the
commission can add specific requirements to screen the dumpster.

The commission decided to leave condition nine as is, strike conditions five and six, and add to
condition number 8, item d, that vertical landscaping is required along the west side of the building.
