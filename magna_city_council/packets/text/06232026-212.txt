                      NOTICE OF INTENT TO PREPARE AN IFFP

                                       MAGNA CITY

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, pursuant to Utah Code §11-36a-501, that the Magna City Council
(the “Council”) intends to prepare an Impact Fee Facilities Plan. The geographical area covered
by the proposed Impact Fee Facilities Plan will cover the entirety of the service area of Magna
City. The Council will hold a public meeting at 6:00 pm on June 23, 2026, during which time it
will discuss the possibility of approving and issuing a request for proposals from qualified
contractors to prepare the Impact Fee Facilities Plan.

For additional information or special accommodations, please contact Kelly Bush (801) 654-2123
or kbush@magna.utah.gov.

Dated: June 10               , 2026
