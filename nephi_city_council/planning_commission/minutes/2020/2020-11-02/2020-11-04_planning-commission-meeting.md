Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
                          November 4, 2020
Chairman Greenhalgh opened the meeting at 7:10 p.m.

Opening prayer offered by Cory Thomson.

Minutes from both July 8 and October 14 meetings were reviewed. Ann Peterson motioned
approval of minutes from both months. Karl Brough seconded the motion. Motion passed with a
unanimous vote.

Proposed modification to Planned Unit Development (PUD) ordinance was discussed by the
Commission. Public hearing was held earlier in the evening with no public comment received. Karl
Brough motioned recommendation to City Council the approval of proposed modification. Motion was
seconded by Cory Thomson. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Savage Bluffs zone change request from R1H to R1 was reviewed by Commission. Request involves
27 quarter acre lots. Karl Brough motioned a public hearing be held December 9th directly before
Commission’s regular meeting. Ann Peterson seconded the motion. Motion passed with a
unanimous vote.

Savage Bluffs vicinity plat along with notes from Development Review Committee (DRC) were
reviewed. Items such as utility location, easements, and irrigation were discussed. Skip Worwood
asked for clarification of 100 East and will it continue through. Chairman Greenhalgh responded
that it will not, although the walking path will be continued from 400 East through Covington Ridge
and on through Savage Bluffs. Cory Thomson motioned approval of Savage Bluffs vicinity plat which
Brent Arns seconded. Motion passed with a unanimous vote. Zone change, if approved, will not
occur until final plat approval.

Commercial and Industrial Performance Standards were discussed by Commission. Items such as
clarity of requirement for curb, gutter, and sidewalk were mentioned. Chairman Greenhalgh asked
Commission if they want to require entire frontage of lot be curbed and guttered. Karl Brough said
“yes, but it depends on location and what is around it”. Brent Arns also said ‘yes, it should be put in
as part of development. The City should be required to do it also on their own property”. Cory
Thomson agreed mentioning it helps direct the run off.

Commission discussed proposal from Kilgore. Current ordinance allows 8 units per acre. Proposal is
for 7.5 units per acre. No action is currently needed. Karl Brough mentioned the demand for
smaller/starter homes needs to be met.

Other business included Hwy 132 Bar W Angus Cattle Co. LLC has been added to the Annexation
Policy Plan. This is adjacent to current piece in the Annexation Policy Plan.

Noting no additional items of business, Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 7:54pm.
