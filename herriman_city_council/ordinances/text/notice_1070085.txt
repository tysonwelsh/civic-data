PUBLIC NOTICE

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on March 25, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2026-05 Ordinance amending the Herriman City's Official Zoning Map for .97 acres of property located at 6319 W 13400 South from OP (Office Professional) to C-1 (Commercial) with a Zoning Condition. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Of-fice during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
