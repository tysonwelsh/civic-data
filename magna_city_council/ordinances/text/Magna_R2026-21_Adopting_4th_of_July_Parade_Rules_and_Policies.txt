A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING 4"! OF JULY PARADE
RULES AND PARTICIPATION GUIDELINES POLICY

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-717 authorizes the Magna City Council (the
“Council”) to exercise all administrative powers by resolution;

WHEREAS, pursuant to its administrative powers, the Council desires to adopt a policy
for 4™ of July Parade Rules and Participation Guidelines;

WHEREAS, a policy for 4" of July Parade Rules and Participation Guidelines has been
prepared (the “Parade Policy”) and sets forth the criteria for acceptance of entry applications,
rules for participation, acknowledgment of risk, and waiver of liability; and

WHEREAS, the Council has determined that the Parade Policy attached to this
Resolution will serve the best interests of Magna City and the general public.

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Council that the Parade Policy
attached to this Resolution is hereby approved in substantially the form attached, subject only to
minor administrative errors or clarifications which may be modified by staff, and effective
immediately.

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 24th day of March, 2026.

2026 — USA 250 Years: Celebrating Freedom (1776-2026)

1. Parade Purpose & 2026 Theme
The Magna 4th of July Parade is a family-centered community celebration honoring America’s
Independence.
In 2026, we proudly celebrate 250 Years of American Freedom (1776-2026). Entries are
encouraged to reflect:

e Patriotism and national pride

e America’s founding and freedoms

e Military, first responders, and community service

e Magna’s heritage (Est. 1874)

e Unity, gratitude, and celebration
Entries should be uplifting, family-friendly, and aligned with the 250th Anniversary theme.

2. Application & Approval
e All floats, vehicles, bikes, walkers, and performers MUST submit a completed
application by the posted deadline.
e Entries must include:
o Description of float, vehicle, bikes, walkers, and performers ~
o Any music being played
o A primary contact person (must be present on parade day)
e Incomplete or inappropriate applications will not be accepted.
e The Parade Committee reserves the right to approve or deny any entry.
All decisions of the Parade Committee are final.
Parade Categories:
e Floats
e Classic Cars
e School & Local Sports Teams
e School & Local Bands
e Children’s Section
o Children in the parade under the age of 18 must be accompanied by a responsible
adult at all times.
o Bikes, strollers, and walkers are welcome
« Use of hover boards, electric bikes or motorized bikes/vehicles is
prohibited for children.
= Anyone under the age of 13 riding a bicycle, scooter, skateboard or similar
vehicle must wear a helmet at all times.
o Decorations on bikes, strollers, etc. are highly encouraged
e Trained animals
3. Safety Requirements
(Strictly Enforced & Non-Negotiable @ )
The safety of spectators and participants is the highest priority. Safety for all float riders and
participants within the parade is the responsibility of each float sponsor or organizer.
Parade Safety 10-foot Buffer Rule
* A minimum 10-foot safety buffer must be maintained and not compromised at any time between
o Vehicles/floats and spectators

o Vehicles and walkers
o Animals and spectators
Participants
e ALL participants must dress modestly and behave in a safe and appropriate manner.
e ALL children in the parade under the age of 18 must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all
times.
e Riders

o No one may ride on any section of a vehicle that is not designed for the safe transport of
passengers.

o Riders may not get on or off the vehicle at any time during the parade, except in the case
of emergency.

o There shall be no performances from a moving vehicle unless the performers are seated,
or there are side rails on the float to prevent someone from falling off.

e Standing Riders.

o Each standing float rider must be provided with a firm, adequate support (i.e. handhold,

rail, barrier, rope), that is away from the edge of the float.
e Performers/Walking Groups.

o Performers shall keep up with the pace of the parade, and shall not act or perform in any
manner that causes a temporary or permanent stopping or slowing of any vehicles in the
parade.

o All groups must ensure that everyone is capable of walking the entire distance of the
parade route in the heat of a July day, and provide sufficient water.

Animals
e Only animals that are properly trained and able to handle large crowds, loud/sudden noises and
the presence of other animals are permitted to participate in the parade.
e Animals shall be properly restrained at all times and remain under the constant control of a trainer
or experienced handler or rider at all times.
e Noanimal may be permitted to get within 10 feet of a spectator or any parade participant other
than a participant in the same entry.
e Applicant must ensure that waste is cleaned before the next parade entry arrives.
Vehicles
e Vehicles must move at walking speed only.
o Minimize abrupt starts/stops, and not perform unsafe maneuvers like racing, burnouts,
wheelies, or doughnuts.

e Drivers must be licensed, insured, sober, and capable of safely operating the vehicle.

« Riders must remain seated — no jumping on or off while moving.

e All decorations must be securely fastened (no dragging, falling, or sharp edges).

© Decorations shall not cover any exhaust pipe or be placed in a position that may interfere with the
exhaust system.

e Floats shall be designed to allow the driver 180 degrees of clear forward vision at all times, and
an unobstructed rear view of the float.

o If.an unobstructed view to the rear is not possible, a spotter must walk alongside the
operator to monitor float activity.

e No person shall discharge firearms, pyrotechnics, fireworks, silly string, or any other projectiles
during the parade.
¢ Generators must be properly secured and safely enclosed.
o No flammable decorations may be placed near the generator.
e Operators and passengers of a motorcycle, ATV, or similar vehicle must abide by the State of
Utah’s helmet and certification laws.
4. Large Entry Requirements
To assist with parade flow and safety:
* Large entries (15+ participants OR any motorized float/trailer entry) must provide two
(2) adult volunteers.
¢ Volunteers may be assigned to assist with spacing, safety monitoring, or parade
operations.
e Volunteers must check in at staging and follow the Parade Committee’s direction.
5. Insurance Requirement
¢ All motorized entries (cars, trucks, motorcycles, trailers, tractors, etc.) must carry valid
automobile liability insurance.
e Proof of insurance must be available upon request.
o The Parade Committee may request proof of insurance prior to parade day.
¢ The Parade Committee assumes no liability for accidents, injuries, or property damage
involving parade participants.
6. Candy, Water & Giveaways
Permitted:
¢ Candy, giveaways, and handouts may be handed out by walkers.
* Gentle tossing is permitted ONLY if it does not cause spectators to enter the street.
¢ Squirt guns/bottles ONLY containing clear water are permitted with respect and spectator
consent.
Not Permitted:
e Throwing items from moving vehicles into the street path.
¢ Any action that encourages spectators to approach vehicles, floats or animals.
¢ Squirt guns/bottles containing anything other than clear water.

e Spraying spectators without consent.
e Spraying near elderly spectators, strollers, electronics, volunteers, government
employees, or government vehicles.

If distribution becomes unsafe, Parade Officials may immediately stop distribution if deemed
unsafe.
7. Signage & Commercial Presence

e Sponsor logos are permitted but should not overpower the patriotic theme.

e All messaging must remain patriotic and family-friendly.

e Entry numbers must be clearly visible.

e Inappropriate, vulgar, or disruptive messaging or imagery is prohibited.
8. Anti-Demonstration & Parade Integrity Policy
The Magna 4th of July Parade is a permitted community event. Participation is by approval of
the Parade Committee. It is not intended as an open forum for demonstrations or advocacy.
The following are strictly prohibited:

¢ Demonstrations or protest activity (positive or negative)

e Messaging intended to disrupt, provoke, or divide

e Signs, audio, or materials unrelated to the parade’s patriotic theme

° Organized activity designed to redirect attention away from the parade celebration
Any entry or participant engaging in prohibited conduct may be:

e Immediately removed from the parade route

e Barred from future participation

e Referred to law enforcement if necessary
9. Lineup & Arrival

e Participants must arrive at their assigned arrival time.

e No day-of vehicle additions will be permitted.

e Follow volunteer instructions at all times.

e Clean your staging area before departure.

¢ No stopping at the end of the parade route — follow designated exit instructions.

Late arrivals may be denied participation.
10. End of Route
¢ No stopping at the end of the route.
e Follow designated exit instructions.
e Be aware of road closures and traffic direction.
11. Conduct Expectations
Participants must:
e Follow directions from parade officials and volunteers
e Maintain parade spacing and flow
e Avoid drawing spectators into the street
¢ Keep behavior respectful and family-appropriate
Entries may be removed for:
e Unsafe behavior
e Disorderly conduct
e Failure to follow volunteer or law enforcement direction
e Violating parade rules
Serious or repeated violations may result in denial of future participation.
12. Liability & Assumption of Risk
Participation in the Magna 4th of July Parade is voluntary. Participation may involve inherent
risks including but not limited to heat exposure, crowd interaction, and moving vehicles.
By participating, all entrants agree:
e They assume full responsibility for their vehicle, float, participants, and actions.
e They release and hold harmless the Magna 4th of July Parade Committee and affiliated
entities from liability for injury, damage, or loss.
e They understand parade officials may enforce rules at their discretion for the safety and
integrity of the event.
