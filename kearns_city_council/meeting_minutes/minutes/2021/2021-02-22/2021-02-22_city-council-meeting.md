Christmas on 54th Budget Increase

                 Mayor Bush stated the Council approved the funding of $8,000 to the Community
Council at its last meeting, to purchase a Christmas decoration for Christmas on 54th. The $8,000
did not cover the cost of the purchase and the Community Council has requested an additional
$7,000. There are some concerns regarding the Community Council purchasing these items for
Kearns Metro Township. Purchases made through the Metro Township are tracked and audited
all year long and, therefore, purchases should be made by the Metro Township.

Surrounding cities also purchase these types of items for their communities. Events like Christmas
on 54th help draw businesses and people to the area which will help Kearns. An individual was
unhappy with Salt Lake County Operations installing the Christmas decorations. Operations is
being paid regardless of the work they do, and she would rather pay them to do something than
to pay them to sit at home.

Funds are moved from the administrative budget to the contribution budget to pay for the
Christmas decorations. This has seemed to confuse some people and how the funding works.
Because of that she has requested that a new line item be added specifically for Christmas on
54th. There is $20,000 in the Christmas on 54th line item and the Council will determine how much
it wants to contribute.

               Council Member Schaeffer stated he has concerns with the ramifications of
spending this amount of money on Christmas decorations.

               Council Member Butterfield stated if this will draw businesses into Kearns then
she is okay with spending the money. There are businesses failing and they could use the traffic.

               Council Member Snow stated it is the last big decoration that is needed, and the
Council has the money. This event will draw businesses out here and people who come from
other areas to see the lights may stop and spend money in the Metro Township.

               Council Member Peterson stated he was ready for the vote.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Butterfield, moved to
approve an additional $7,000 for a total of $15,000 to the Kearns Community Council. The motion
passed 4 to 1, showing that Council Member Schaeffer voted in opposition.

Completion of Kearns Street Light Project

                Mayor Bush stated the goal for 2021 was to complete the light installation in
Kearns. Lighting on the east of the railroad tracks has been completed. She has spoken to Salt
Lake County Operations and they are getting ready to finish the lighting on the west side of the
railroad tracks and it will be completed this spring or summer. Once the lights are in they are not
coming out.

               Council Member Snow stated the light heads are being replaced, not the entire
pole.

               Mayor Bush stated the electricians will go back next year and see what poles can
be installed and how much the grid can handle. This year is not a good year because of the
amount of trenching that will take place in the area.

The council agreed with the plan.
