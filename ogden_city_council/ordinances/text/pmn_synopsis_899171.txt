                                                           AFFIDAVIT OF POSTING
                                                     SYNOPSIS OF OGDEN CITY ORDINANCE




I, Lee Ann Peterson, the duly appointed Chief Deputy City Recorder, in and for the City of Ogden, Weber County, State of
Utah, do hereby certify the Ogden City Council at its October 4, 2022 meeting adopted the following:

         ORDINANCE 2022-39 – An ordinance of Ogden City, Utah, amending the zoning map of Ogden City, as adopted by
         Section 15-3-3 of the Ogden Municipal Code, to reclassify as Multiple-family Residential Zone – East Central (R-
         3EC) property heretofore classified as both Multiple-family Residential Zone – East Central and Central Business
         District/Conditional Overlay Zone (R-3EC and CBD/CO), generally located at 539 22nd Street, and providing that
         this ordinance shall become effective immediately upon posting after final passage.




Copies of ordinances are available for review in the Ogden City Recorder’s Office located on the second floor of the
Municipal Building, 2549 Washington Boulevard #210, Ogden, Utah during regular business hours.
