                                CITY OF BLUFFDALE, UTAH

                                 ORDINANCE NO. 2025-17


AN ORDINANCE AMENDING BLUFFDALE CITY CODE 2.50.010 RELATED TO THE TERM OF
                     HEALTHY BLUFFDALE COALITION BOARD MEMBERS


       WHEREAS,the City Council previously adopted Title 2.50.010 related to Healthy
Bluffdale Coalition Board Members;

      WHEREAS,the term of Healthy Bluffdale Coalition Board Members in Bluffdale City
Code 2.50.010 currently conflicts with the term found in Bluffdale City Code 2.50.030;

         WHEREAS,the amendments in Exhibit A will eliminate the conflict in the code and
clarify that the term of HBC Board Members is intended to be three(3) years.

NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:


       Section 1.     Adoption. Ordinance 2025-17 is hereby adopted,amending Bluffdale
City Code 2.50.010 as shown in Exhibit A hereto.

       Section 2.      Amendment of Conflicting Ordinances. If any ordinances,
resolutions, policies, or zoning maps of the City heretofore adopted are inconsistent
herewith they are hereby amended to comply with the provisions hereof. If they cannot be
amended to comply with the provisions hereof,they are hereby repealed.

       Sections.      Severabllity. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable,such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and
independent provision,and such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance
shall beseverable.


      Section 4.     Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED: June 11,2025
