Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Hazel Dunsmore, Human Resource Director
CC:  Date: July 20, 2021
Re:  Comments:
This is a proposed amendment to Sections 6040, 1045 of Draper City Personnel Policy to update the following items:
Amend Section 5080 – Performance Appraisal
Clarify how the lump sum merit increase is awarded for employees who are at the maximum of the grade for their position.
Amend Section 5100 – Employment Separation Procedures
Update the policy to clarify when an employee who separates from the City will receive their final paycheck. Government agencies are not required to provide a final paycheck within two business days.
Amend Section 6040 – Computer Security
Add language stating that employees are prohibited from accessing security surveillance cameras for anything other than City business.
Amend Section 7030 – Overtime, Compensatory Time and On-Call Pay
Remove the requirement that comp time must be paid out at the end of the fiscal year.
Employees may still accrue a balance up to a maximum of 40 hours.
Adopt Section 10450 – Motor Vehicle Record Checks
This is a new policy explaining when the City will search an employee’s motor vehicle records and outlines what constitutes unacceptable driving.
