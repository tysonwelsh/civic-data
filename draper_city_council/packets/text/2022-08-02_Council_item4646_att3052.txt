ThepurposeofthisletteristosatisfytheDraperCityrequirementoutlinedintheMarch28,2022emailfrom
BryceMcConkie.Inthatemail,Mr.McConkiestatedthefollowingaboutthenorthsideofthehome,whichis
anexisting75’wideopenspaceanddrainageeasement(hereafterreferredtoas“theeasement”)perthe
recordedPheasantHillsPlat4:

        c.    The soils placed within the north open space easement require written HOA approval, and a letter
        shall be provided by Wilding Engineering verifying that the added soils will not impact the required
        drainage system located within the open space easement. Otherwise, the soils shall be removed and the
        area restored. 

IpersonallyvisitedthesiteonMay31,2022andatopographicsurveywasconductedonJune9tolocatethe
fillonthelot.Thefollowingobservationsweremade:
      1. Thebottomoftheexistingdrainagedoesnotshowanysignsofsignificantrecenthistoricalflow.
          Thereisnoobviousflowlineoranerodedstreambed.Wehaveconcludedthatthisdrainage
          primarilyhandlestheminimalflowsthatsurfaceflowfromthebackyardsofhomesthatbackonto
          thischannel.
      2. Wefoundan18”culvertinletwherethisdrainageterminatesatHighlandDrive.Thesizingofthis
          culvertindicatesthatthedesignflowsofthisdrainageareminimalandsupportsourfindingsin#1.
      3. Thefillslopefromyourhomeconstructionactivityiscurrentlywithintheeasement,butdoesnot
          blockthebottomoftheexistingdrainage.

Itisouropinionthatthefillslopedoesnotimpactthefunctionofthecurrentdrainagesystem.Thelocation
ofthecurrenttoeslopeofthecurrentfillshouldbeconsideredanorthernboundaryofanyfuture
disturbance.Allgradingorretainingwalldesignandconstructionshallbelocatedsouthofthisfillline.The
easementontheproposedamendedplathasbeenadjustedtoawidthof40’(from75’),withtheeastern
edgeclippedtofollowthecurrenttoeofthefillslope.
