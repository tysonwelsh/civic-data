             1. A DISCUSSION ON HOW TO ADDRESS MOSQUITO ABATEMENT IN UTAH COUNTY.
             - Bill Lee, Utah County Commissioner -

             DAN MILLER, MOSQUITO ABATEMENT MANAGER – LAST YEAR WERE ABLE TO TREAT SOME
             ACREAGE IN AN AREA UP IN THE NORTHERN PART OF THE COUNTY ALONG UTAH LAKE IN
             WHICH WE WERE ABLE TO REACH THROUGH A DRONE THROUGH A CONTRACT WE FOUND.
             THIS SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED THE NUMBER OF ACTUAL AIR SPRAYS THAT WE HAD TO DO.
             HAS ACTUALLY KEPT IT LOW. WE’RE FINDING MORE AND MORE ACREAGE IN WHICH WE
             COULD USE THE DRONE THAT WOULD GIVE US BETTER COVERAGE. USING A DRONE SAVES
             A SIGNIFICANT AMOUNT OF COSTS IN MAN HOURS AND CHEMICAL COSTS AS WELL AS THE
             COST OF A PLANE INSTEAD WHICH IS MUCH MORE. A VERY USEFUL TOOL. WOULD LIKE TO
             DO MORE ACREAGE THIS YEAR.

             BUDGETED $7,800 FOR THE DRONE. WOULD LIKE TO INCREASE IT TO $10,000 TO ENCOMPASS
             MORE ACREAGE. THEN WOULD NEED AN ADDITIONAL $18,000 FOR THE CHEMICAL,
             VECTOBAC.

             THERE IS NO OTHER PERSON WITHIN UTAH THAT IS SAYING THEY WILL DO THIS WORK
             WITH A DRONE. DOES WORK WITH DWR. IS ALWAYS LOOKING FOR AND TRYING THE FIND
             THE BEST TOOLS POSSIBLE.
