Request
 The property owners and applicants, Ann, and Joe Werner,
 are requesting review of an accessory structure for a property
 located at 2 Circle Oaks. The applicant is requesting a
 conditional use permit for an increased height of an accessory
 structure and a setback waiver. The applicant is proposing to
 have a detached accessory building up to 28 feet in height
 placed three feet (3’) from the property line. They feel the
 request will not have a detrimental impact on the
 neighborhood as there are structures with similar proximity
 to the property line and there are many mature trees that
 mitigate the visual height impacts (see applicant letter,
 Exhibit “A”). The proposed structure is to be used as storage
 for vehicles, and a golf simulator (see building plans, Exhibit
 “B”).

Background
 This property is located in the Pepperwood 2A subdivision
 on a 0.69-acre (28,771 square foot) lot. The home is zoned
 PUD(1.62) with properties on all sides being single family
 homes zoned PUD(1.62). This area was platted in 1978 and
 most homes in this area were built by the mid 1980’s. The
 property is subject to the sensitive overlay zone because the
 southwest corner of the property is clipped by the overlay
 zone map due to steep slopes in the nearby area.

                                                Analysis
                                               The applicant’s plans indicate that they intend to build a detached garage on
                                               the south side of their lot. The proposed structure would be approximately
 1,500 square feet. The applicant currently has a tennis court, underground trampoline and an underground pool. On the
 east end it would measure 20 feet in height but because of the change in grade the west side of the structure will reach 28
 feet in height. They are proposing to build the building as close to the side property lines as possible, proposing a side setback
 of three feet (3’). They are also proposing some interior remodeling of the home. are not part of this conditional use permit
 request.

 Building Height
 The applicant is requesting that the Planning Commission approve the accessory structure to be 28 feet in height on one
 side. Section 21-11-2(a)(3)(c) of the Sandy City Land Development Code states that an accessory structure may be built
 above the maximum height of (20’ in the PUD(1.62) Zone), up to the maximum building height for a permitted dwelling
 within the zone in which it is located (35’ in the PUD(1.62) Zone), upon receipt of a Conditional Use Permit from the
 Sandy City Planning Commission. The Planning Commission should consider the scale of the building in relation to the
 immediate surroundings, the nature of the zone and land uses in the immediate vicinity, architectural design, landscaping

 the increased height The Planning Commission may waive a portion or all the additional setback requirement through the
 conditional use review process. There are no other structures or main dwellings near the proposed detached structure
 location. There are several large mature trees nearby.

 Additional Setback
 The applicant is requesting a setback waiver of nine feet (9’) for the side property lines. Detached structures exceeding 15
 feet in height are required to increase the minimum setback one foot for each foot of additional height up to the minimum
 setback for the primary dwelling (Section 21-11-2(a)(3)(b)). A setback of twelve (12) feet from the side property lines would
 be required for the requested height. However, the Planning Commission may waive or reduce this requirement through
 this conditional use review.

 Per the submitted plot plan (see image below), the
 applicant is proposing to place the structure as close as
 three (3) feet to the property line. The applicant has
 spoken with the neighboring property owners and have
 all signed an agreement for the height and setbacks (see
 signed letter, Exhibit “D”).

 The additional setback is designed to mitigate the impact
 of a large imposing detached structure from being located
 close to the property line. If the Planning Commission
 finds that this setback is not warranted in this context and
 situation, they may waive it, reduce it, or substitute other
 mitigation measures. This area of the lot does not have
 any other detached buildings or dwellings nearby and has
 several large mature trees nearby.

 Conditional Use Standards
 The City may impose conditions on a conditional use based on certain standards of review found in section 21-33-04 of
 Sandy City Land Development Code. The following items from that list are applicable to this particular request and merit
 discussion or additional consideration by the Planning Commission (staff analysis is found below in italics):

Sec. 21-33-4. Conditions.
    In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions that address:
    (1)   Size, configuration and location of the site and the proposed site plan layout.
 The applicant is proposing to locate the 1,500 square foot accessory structure that is 28’ tall in the northwest side of the rear yard.
 The minimum setback must be at least two feet (3’) from the property line.

    (6) Mass, size, number, location, design, exterior features, materials, and colors of buildings, structures, and other facilities.

 The applicant is proposing to build the proposed structure to match and be cohesive with the existing home.

Staff Concerns
Staff feels the proposed application meets the intent of the Development Code and has no concerns.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for additional height of a detached
  structure as well as a setback waiver as described in the staff report for the property located at 2 Circle Oaks based on the
  following finding and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. The proposed structure meets the intent of section 21-11-2.

    2. There is at least one nearby lot that has a similar minimal setback of a detached structure to the property line.

Conditions:
   1. That the proposed structure complies with all applicable Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes

    2. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City Development Code, including adjusting
       the setback of the detached structure to a minimum of three feet (3’).

    3. That the structure not be used as an accessory dwelling unit.

    4. That this Conditional use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.
