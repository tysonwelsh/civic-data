Re: Public Meeting: Powers I-ADU Deviation Request (Administrative Action)


Comments:

This application is a request for approval of a Deviation from the prohibition against creation of an
I-ADU within the Primary Dwelling on the subject property for approximately 0.97 acres located
north of the Pristine View Cove cul-de-sac, at approximately 14653 S Pristine View Cv. and is located
in the R3 (Residential, 13,000 min. lot size) zone.

Findings for Approval:
   1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-thousand square
      feet (20,000 ft²);
   2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
   3. The proposed I-ADU meets all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-045,
      including other applicable
   4. requirements of this chapter; and
   5. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are provided for use
      by occupants of the I-ADU.
