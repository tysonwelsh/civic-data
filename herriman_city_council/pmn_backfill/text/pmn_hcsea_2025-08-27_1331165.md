     Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area Minutes
                            Wednesday, August 27, 2025
                             Approved October 1, 2025

The following are the minutes of the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area meeting. The
meeting was held on Wednesday, August 27, 2025, at 6:30 p.m. in the Herriman City
Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of
this meeting, as required by law, was posted in City Hall, on the City’s website, and
delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Chair Lorin Palmer

Trustees Present: Terrah Anderson, Jared Henderson, Teddy Hodges, and Sherrie Ohrn

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas,
City Recorder Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran,
Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, Community Development Director Blake
Thomas, Public Works Director Justun Edwards, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Engineer
Bryce Terry, Operations Director Monte Johnson, City Planner Michael Maloy, Building
Official Cathryn Nelson, UFA Assistant Chief Anthony Widdison, Assistant to the City
Manager Trevor Ram, and Public Utilities Engineering Manager Jonathan Bowers.

1. Call to Order - 6:30 p.m. (or as soon as possible thereafter)
Chair Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 6:47 p.m.

2. Public Hearing
   2.1. Public hearing and consideration of a Resolution amending the Herriman City
         Safety Enforcement Area (HCSEA) fiscal year 2026 budget – Kyle Maurer,
         Finance Director
Finance Director Kyle Maurer addressed the board with several budget amendment
requests. He began by explaining that, following the Truth in Taxation hearing held at the
August 27, 2025 –Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area Minutes                     Page 2 of 3


previous meeting, an amendment to the property tax was necessary to reflect the approved
increase. The HCSEA Board had adopted a final tax rate on August 13, 2025, resulting in a
$513,335 increase in forecasted property tax revenue. This was in addition to a $151,735
increase attributed to growth exceeding initial projections.

Director Maurer also requested an adjustment to the Motor Vehicle In Lieu budget, based
on actual revenue trends. He noted that with several years of historical data now available,
he was able to more accurately project these revenues. Staff proposed a decrease of
$80,250 to better align with current figures.

Lastly, Director Maurer addressed the fund’s interest income, noting that the existing cash
balance had generated more interest than originally anticipated. He recommended an
increase of $13,990 in the interest income budget to reflect actuals for fiscal year 2025.
Despite expectations of lower interest rates, he expressed confidence that the substantial
cash balance designated for future projects would continue to yield higher-than-projected
interest earnings.

Chair Palmer opened the public hearing.

No public comments were offered.

Trustee Anderson moved to close the public hearing. Trustee Ohrn seconded the motion
and all voted aye.

Trustee Anderson moved to approve Resolution R25-05 approving an amendment to the
Fiscal Year 2026 Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area budget. Trustee Ohrn seconded
the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Trustee Terrah Anderson                   Aye
Trustee Jared Henderson                   Aye
Trustee Teddy Hodges                      Aye
Trustee Sherrie Ohrn                      Aye
Chair Lorin Palmer                        Aye
The motion passed unanimously.

3. Adjournment
Trustee Hodges moved to adjourn the Herriman City Safety Enforcement Area meeting at
6:50 p.m. Trustee Henderson seconded the motion, and all present voted aye.
