Repealing the Stormwater Impact Fee from the White City Consolidated Fee Schedule

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution repealing the stormwater impact
fee from the White City consolidated fee schedule:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-11-01                                               Date: November 1, 2018




                                              2
DATE           THURSDAY                               NOVEMBER                            1, 2018

       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       REPEALING THE STORMWATER IMPACT FEE FROM THE WHITE CITY
       CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE

                                           RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“WHITE CITY”), as part of its adoption of the
2018 White City Budget also adopted the 20 8 White City Consolidated Fee Schedule (the “FEE
SCHEDULE”); and

       WHEREAS, during a cost-benefit review of all fees performed during the summer, it was
determined by staff the imposition of the Stormwater Impact Fee (the “FEE”) was a costly and
unnecessary fee due to charges in the state law regarding the reporting and use of the fee; and

        WHEREAS, it is to the benefit of White City, given the limitations the state has placed on
this fee to discontinue its imposition on development;

       THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby amends
the Fee Schedule to delete the Stormwater Impact Fee as a fee imposed by White City on property
development.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 1st day of November
2018.

Scope of Work for Professional Services Regarding Creation of White City Metro Township
General Plan

               The Council reviewed the following Resolution approving the scope of work for
professional services regarding the creation of White City Metro Township General Plan:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-11-02                                                 Date: November 1, 2018

                                                3
DATE           THURSDAY                               NOVEMBER                             1, 2018


       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING SCOPE OF WORK FOR PROFESSIONAL SERVICES
       REGARDING CREATION OF WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP GENERAL
       PLAN

                                           RECITALS

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township (“WHITE CITY”), is a municipality pursuant to
Utah Code §§ 10-2a-401 et seq; and

       WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council (“COUNCIL”) is a Metro Township
Council pursuant to UCA §§ 10-3b-501 et al; and

    WHEREAS; the White City Metro Township Planning Commission (“PLANNING
COMMISSION”) is the Planning Commission for White City pursuant to UCA §§ 10-9a-301 et al;
and

       WHEREAS, Planning and Development Services (“PDS”) provides White City with land-
use planning services, pursuant to the Master Interlocal Agreement by and between Salt Lake
County, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District and White City, which can include the
creation and ongoing administration of the White City Metro Township General Plan (the
“GENERAL PLAN”); and,

       WHEREAS, PDS has provided White City a scope of work for services for creating the
White City General Plan; and,

       WHEREAS, White City is required by State Law to have a General Plan; and,

          WHEREAS, agreeing with PDS as to the scope of work for services will not only provide
an effective and efficient platform for creating the General Plan, it will also allow White City to
fulfill other legislative obligations made during the SB199 (2015) process; now therefore be it

                                         RESOLUTION

        RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council hereby approves the Scope of Work
for Professional Services with PDS for the creation of the White City Metro General Plan, a true
and correct copy of which shall be attached hereto, marked A, upon its approval as to form by
White City’s legal counsel.

     APPROVED AND ADOPTED in White City, Salt Lake County, Utah this 1st day of
November 2018.
