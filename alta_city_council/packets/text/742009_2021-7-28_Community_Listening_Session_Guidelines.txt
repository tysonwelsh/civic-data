                                   GUIDELINES
COMMUNITY LISTENING SESSION FOR THE LITTLE COTTONWOOD CANYON ENVIRONMENTAL
               IMPACT STATEMENT FOR ALTA COMMUNITY MEMBERS
                    5:00-7:00 P.M. - Wednesday, July 28, 2021

Listening Session Purpose:
To provide an opportunity for Alta community members to share their thoughts related to the
Little Cottonwood Canyon Environmental Impact Statement (LCC EIS) with the Alta Town
Council members.

Please Note the Following:
    • Please familiarize yourself with LCC EIS here: https://littlecottonwoodeis.udot.utah.gov/
           o If you wish to submit a comment to UDOT you will need to follow the
               instructions on the project website
           o If you have questions about the LCC EIS please contact UDOT directly
    • The Town is offering this meeting to provide a safe and respectful space where
       community members can share their thoughts on the project in order to have a
       designated time where community members can listen and learn from one another
    • Comments are limited to 2 minutes
    • This is a listening session, not a question-and-answer session. This event is to provide
       an opportunity for Alta community members to share their thoughts on the project with
       the Alta town council members
    • Simply stating you like or don’t like a project is ineffective. For help on how to write a
       public comment review this article: https://townofalta.com/article/7460-2/
    • During the breakout sessions, a facilitator (either council member or town staff) will
       have a few questions to engage the group in discussion
    • Comments shared at this meeting WILL NOT be submitted as comment to the LCC EIS
       project. The Town encourages community members to submit official comments on the
       EIS to UDOT either in person at an official UDOT public comment event or through the
       project’s website by September 3, 2021
    • If you are unable to join this meeting, you may submit written comment to the town
       council by emailing jen@townofalta.com by July 27, 2021 at 3:00pm. Remember, by
       submitting a comment to the Town you are NOT submitting an official comment to
       UDOT
    • You must stay muted until it’s your turn to make a comment
    • Please be familiar with Zoom meeting controls. If you dial in, note that to unmute
       yourself you must use *6. Contact Jen in advance if you are unfamiliar with Zoom
    • Please follow the instructions for joining the meeting on the Listening Session Agenda
