This is regarding contract #20‐1065‐08. Because of COVID 19, the rescheduled town days event is
canceled; however, the city would still like to have a firework show. The City would like to change the
date of the fireworks show to September 11, 2020 and add two additional locations, so there is a total of
three (3) locations where the fireworks will be shot; Riverton City Park, Monarch Meadows Park and
Centennial Park. This event will be named “Riverton Remembers 911”.

Because the City will add locations and change the type of fireworks to a non‐gathering type show, the
cost of this event will increase. The Contractor will be paid $45,000.00 for the Riverton Remembers 911
Event.
