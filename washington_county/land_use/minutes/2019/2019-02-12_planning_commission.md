Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                              February 12, 2019
The Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting was held on Tuesday, February 12, 2019 in
the Council Chambers of the Washington County Administration Building at 197 E. Tabernacle,
St. George, UT. Chairman Mark Owens opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He explained meeting
protocol and then led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate), Shayne Durrant, Kevin
Jones, Slade Hughes, Reed Scow (Alternate acting as regular voting member on the conditional
use request only), Doug Solstad, and Jon Walter.

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Natalie Nelson, Deputy County
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Todd
Edwards, County Engineer; Kim Hafen, County Clerk, Dean Cox, County Commissioner; Gil
Almquist, County Commissioner; Nicole Felshaw, County Commission Administrator.

Chairman Owens asked for a motion to table agenda Items 2 and 3 to the end of the agenda to
accommodate the large number of citizens in attendance for the items on the agenda.
Commissioner Durrant so MOVED. Commissioner Anderson SECONDED. The motion carried
unanimously.

IV.     CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT. Review of a conditional use permit request for a rural
recreation grounds and facilities project on Parcel 7139-G-NW. the parcel is located at
approximately 800 North Cottom Ranch Road in Veyo. The approximate 43.46 acre parcel is
zoned OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 acre minimum lot size). The applicants are Pine Valley
Mountain, LLC/Slade Hughes.

Commissioner Slade Hughes recused himself from the discussion and voting of the agenda item.
He then stepped off the podium.

Mr. Messel stated there was a typo on the notice and the staff report that went out. The correct
parcel is 7139-G-NW. The notice posted was on the correct property.

Mr. Messel provided a map and site plan of the project. He reported the applicant is requesting
conditional use approval for Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities to establish a Recreational
Vehicle Park on Parcel 7139-G-NW. Such a use fits within the definition of Rural Recreation
Grounds and Facilities which, is a conditional use in the OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 acre
minimum lot size) zone. The plan is to have approximately 118 RV pads; approximately 10 tent
pads; and in the future 9 cabinettes. A gravel driving surface is proposed. Water will be provided
by the Veyo Culinary Water Association. The power will be provided by Rocky Mountain Power.
The applicant will need to work out the sewer service with the Southwest Utah Public Health
Department. The fire department has expressed concerns over the fire hydrant availability and
would request some be put on the property. The applicant has stated that they would be willing to
install whatever fire protection is needed for the project. Cottom Ranch Road is a county road and
maintained by the county.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the project and it meets the requirements of the conditional use for the zone. If
the Planning Commission is comfortable with the proposed conditional use request, they may
approve it based on the following findings and conditions:

 1. The conditional use application and Planning Commission meeting agenda where properly
    noticed.
 2. Rural Recreation Grounds and Facilities are a conditional use in the OST-20 (Open Space
    Transition 20 acre minimum lot size) zone.

VI.    PUBLIC HEARING/PRELIMINARY AND FINAL PLAT. Review of a preliminary
and final plat request for a 1 lot subdivision of Parcel 7131-B-NW. The property is located on
Lucky 7 Drive in Brookside. The parcel is approximately 3 acres in size and zoned RE-40
(Residential Estates 40,000 square foot minimum lot size). The applicant is Ty Bennett.

Mr. Messel provided a map of the area and the plat. He stated Ty Bennett owns Parcel 7131-B-NW
which, is located in Brookside on the north side of Wells Fargo Drive. The parcel was illegally
subdivided by a previous owner, from Parcel 7131-A-5-NW, which is located to the north of the
subject parcel. The approximately 3 acres in size parcel is zoned RE-40 (Residential Estates 40,000
square foot minimum lot size). Ty Bennett wants to construct a home on the subject parcel, but needs
to bring the property into compliance by going through the subdivision process approval. Utah Code
Annotated section 17-27a-601 and Washington County Title 11 require that any tract or parcel of
land that is divided into two or more parcels constitutes a subdivision, and must go through the
subdivision process. The preliminary and final plats have been submitted and reviewed by Staff. The
property is accessed from Wells Fargo Drive on an access road that has been historically called
Lucky 7 Drive that was the former access to the old Lucky 7 Ranch which, no longer exists. Lucky
7 Drive is approximately 20’ wide. Washington County Code 11-6-E states, “Each lot shall abut on
a street shown on the subdivision plat or on an existing publicly dedicated street or on a street which
has become public by right of use”. The street must be improved to county standards as stated in
Title 11-5-C Improvement Standards: “All streets within the County shall be improved in accordance
with the standards, rules and regulations adopted by the County Commission”. The applicant is
unable to acquire the necessary land needed for the right-of way width to meet County Standards. A
flag lot configuration is an option available for property that does not have the necessary street
frontage. A flag lot requires a minimum 25’ wide stem portion of a lot for drive access. Again, the
applicant is unable to obtain the necessary land from the neighbors to the south to meet the flag lot
standards.

Washington County Code 11-3-M states, “Where unusual topographic or other exceptional
conditions exist, the county commission may vary the requirements of this title after receiving the
recommendations of the planning commission; provided, that such variations will not substantially
impair the intent of this title.” The applicant is asking the planning commission recommend that
the County Commission wave the access requirements of Title 11.
