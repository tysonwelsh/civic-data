Community Reinvestment Agency Draft Minutes of the July 15, 2025 Meeting
3.0   Annual CRA Report 2025
      Jason Burningham with LRB Public Finance Advisors presented an overview of
      the 2025 Annual CRA Report. He said Draper currently had seven active project
      areas, four of which had expired collection periods and unexpended fund
      balances. The Council discussed the potential impact of the school district
      purchase of property in the FrontRunner CDA with Mr. Burningham.

      Mr. Burningham reported that Pluralsight had not met some conditions of the
      participation agreement in the South Mountain CRA, and further discussion
      would be needed regarding the future of the CRA. Board Member Green
      suggested scheduling a meeting with representatives of Pluralsight to discuss
      possible solutions. Assistant Finance Director Jared Zacharias said the City’s
      consultant, Mr. Dobbins, was actively looking at options.

      Board Member Green moved to accept CRA Resolution #25-05 adopting the
      2025 Annual CRA Report. Board Member F. Lowry seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
