         NOTICE OF OGDEN PLANNING COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING


The Ogden City Planning Commission will hold a public hearing on Wednesday, October
2, 2024, at 6:00 pm in the City Council Chambers, 3rd Floor, Ogden Municipal Building,
2549 Washington Boulevard, in Ogden, Utah to consider the following:

    •   Zone change at 2869 Grant Ave. from CP-3 (Regional Commercial) to R-4
        (Multiple-family residential)

Boulevard, during regular business hours, or on the city’s website

interested in these items may participate in person or electronically by following the
instructions at: https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88441486504 Meeting ID: #884 4148 6504

629-8922 or at planning@ogdencity.com.
