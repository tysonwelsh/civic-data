25   1. Call to Order
26
27   Mayor Staggs called the Riverton City Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Meeting to order at 8:47
28   p.m. and conducted a Roll Call. Board Members Buroker, McCay, McDougal, Stewart, and
29   Wells were present.
30
31   2. Public Hearing(s)
32
33      a. RDA Resolution No. 20-02 - Adopting a Riverton Redevelopment Agency Final
34         Amended Budget for Fiscal Year 2019-2020
35
36   Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director, explained that Budgets were estimates, and
37   therefore, from time to time it may be necessary to amend the City’s budget. He said it was
38   necessary periodically and at fiscal year-end, based on year-to-date budget to actual
39   comparisons to open and amend the fiscal budget as needed.
40
41   Mayor Staggs opened a Public Hearing and called for public comment; there being none, he
42   declared the Public Hearing closed.
43
44   Board Member Buroker MOVED to approve RDA Resolution No. 20-02 - Adopting a
45   Riverton Redevelopment Agency Final Amended Budget for Fiscal Year 2019-2020. Board
46   Member McDougal SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion;
47   there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows: Board Members
48   Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion passed
49   unanimously.
