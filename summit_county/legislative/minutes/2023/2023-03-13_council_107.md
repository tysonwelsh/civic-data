Continued discussion of design for the new Silver Summit County Services Building, and Council to provide budget direction for the new Silver Summit County Services building and remodel of the Summit County Justice Center; Shayne Scott, Janna Young, Matt Leavitt, Danny Page, and Scott Henriksen (10:15 A.M.)
Janna Young, Deputy Manager, led the Council in discussion of the budget and plans for the new Silver Summit County Services building. Council Members commented and asked questions. Shayne Scott, Manager, Matt Leavitt, Financial Officer, and Scott Henriksen, GSBS Architect, answered questions and contributed to the discussion.
Malena Stevens made a motion to convene in closed session to discuss property acquisition Christopher Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
The Summit County Council met in Closed Session from 11:44 A.M. to 12:10 P.M. to discuss property acquisition. The following people were present:
