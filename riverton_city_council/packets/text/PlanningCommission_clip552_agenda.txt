Riverton City Hall is an ADA compliant facility. Individuals needing special accommodations or assistance during this meeting
shall notify the City Recorder’s Office at 801-208-3126 at least 24 hours in advance of the meeting. Accessible parking and
entrance is located on the south end of the building with elevator access to the Council Chambers located on the second floor.

                                                   Public Comment Procedure
This is an open meeting, which the public may attend. However restrictions related to COVID-19 will be observed. Prior to the
meeting, the staff reports will be available for public review through the City website. Audio and video of the meeting will be
broadcast live. Following the meeting, audio and written minutes will be available. Public comments may be submitted via
email to planning@rivertonutah.gov or by phone to 801-208-3138. All comments submitted before 5:00 pm on the date of the
hearing will be relayed to the Planning Commission.

Riverton City Meeting information: https://www.rivertonutah.gov/meetings/index.php. This link will take you to the applicable
meeting date and to the meeting packet, where you can review all items scheduled for review by the Planning Commission.
