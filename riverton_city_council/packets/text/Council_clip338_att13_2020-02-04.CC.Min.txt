30       a. Proposed Ordinance No. 20-02 – Amending the Land Use Code Sections 18.190
31          and 18.135, Addressing Seasonal and Temporary Commercial Uses
32
33   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that Seasonal Temporary
34   Commercial Uses such as shaved ice stands, Christmas tree lots, and fireworks stands were
35   frequent among Utah communities. Riverton City’s ordinances have in the past only broadly
36   addressed how those uses were reviewed and approved. He said the proposed ordinance
37   addition clarified where they may be located within Riverton City and codified a process and
38   criteria for reviewing and approving temporary commercial uses, and included the information
39   that must be provided by applicants. He said the proposed amended clarified that such uses
40   would not be allowed on residential property.
41
42   Mr. Lethbridge said the Planning Commission, following a public hearing, voted to recommend
43   approval of the text amendment.
44

46   for public comment; there being none, he closed the Public Hearing.
