Re: 2023 Moderate Income Housing Report (Discussion and Work Session) Comments: Staff presentation and work session regarding the changes to annual reporting requirements associated with implementation of the City's Moderate Income Housing Plan. Current reporting period is for actions taken between November 22, 2022 and August 1, 2023. Reporting is done online through a google form and is due to the Department of Workforce Services, Housing and Community Development Division prior to August 1, 2023.
An outline of the elements that the city will report on and a copy of the currently adopted Moderate Income Housing Plan are attached. Discussion will focus on needed adjustments to the implementation timeframes, and actions that have been taken or will be taken to insure compliance with recent changes to State Code.
