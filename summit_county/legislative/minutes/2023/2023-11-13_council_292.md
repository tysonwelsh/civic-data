Discussion regarding childcare; Shayne Scott, Janna Young, David Warnock (1:43 P.M.)
Shayne Scott, Manger, introduced the topic of childcare for discussion with Council Members.
Janna Young, Deputy County Manager, provided additional information for the discussion.
David Warnock, Personnel Director , addressed the Council and presented the results of the childcare survey conducted with County employees. The survey was sent to all employees of the County. The results revealed the following:
41 employees currently pay for childcare
12 employees can't find but need assistance with childcare
53 employees need childcare with 58 children impacted by this need
72 kids need afterschool care or drop-in options
Employees have left County employment due to lack of childcare options
Employment offers were not accepted due to lack of childcare options
Ms. Young discussed the proposal to include $150,000 in the 2024 Manager's budget for matching contributions to the FSA dependent care plan.
Council Members asked clarifying questions to which David, Janna and Shayne responded. Matt Leavitt, Finance Officer, provided additional information to the discussion.
Additional meeting dates to address budget matters were discussed. The Council added meetings on December 4th, 11th and 27th to allow more time for budget conversations.
