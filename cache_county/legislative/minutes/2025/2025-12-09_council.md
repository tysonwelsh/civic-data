1:12:28 Council discussion about hiring a Board of Equalization Hearing Officer and other items to prepare for.
Councilmember David Erickson asked why with the $10,000 from 2025 Cache Water District Study is in the budget for
2026. Matt answered the cost would be rolled forward. Executive Daines asked if that was general practice. Matt
answered yes. Chair Sandi Goodlander asked Matt if they were in process of re-evaluating tax admin percentages.
Matt answered no however the tax Admin tax revenue would most likely be increased next year. Councilmember Keegan
Garrity requested $5,000 reduction for Professional and Technical for Fairgrounds. Chair Sandi Goodlander asked if the
Fairgrounds was aware and if there were specific needs for those excess funds. Matt added the Executive budget
committee felt there was too much budgeted for travel. Councilmember David Erickson cautioned the same rationale
could be made for other spikes. Matt said data would be received in February to show where the money in 2025 had
been spent and where to cut in 2026. Executive Daines echoed the budget process would be more fluid than in the past
and each department should only ask for what they need. Matt interjected that an expense was incorrectly placed and
the amount the fairgrounds had budgeted for was not in excess.

Action: Motion made by Councilmember Keegan Garrity to decrease Fairgrounds budget by $5,000; seconded by
Councilmember Nolan Gunnell.
Motion withdrawn.
