                                  General Obligation Bonds and (b) the potential economic impact that the 
                                  improvements, facilities, or properties to be financed in whole or in part 
                                  with proceeds of said Bonds will have on the private sector.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Argument for Posting - Sandy City GO Election 2023.pdf
                                  Timeline GO Election 2023.pdf
                                  Public Hearing Minutes Confirmation Form.docx

                                  Public Hearing: Brian Kelley, Administrative Services Director, presented the brochure 
                                  that will be mailed as part of the Voter Information Packet regarding the General 
                                  Obligation bond. The City Council held a Public Hearing regarding (a) the issuance of not 
                                  more than $21,000,000 of GO Bonds and (b) the potential economic impact that the 
                                  improvements, facilities, or properties to be financed with the proceeds of the said bonds. 
                                  Chief Jeffrey Bassett spoke to the Council on the need for a new fire station 31. He 
                                  provided an overview of the issues with the current station 31 facility. He spoke about the 
                                  proposed location for the new fire station 31. Mr. Kelley reviewed the term and details 
                                  associated with the bonds and the language that will be on the ballot this November. 

                                  Public Hearing comment period opened:

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren did not think the argument was appropriate. He also had questions 
                                  regarding the location of the new fire station.

                                  Ms. Danea Brunatti-Shaw requested for the city to consider installing a restroom at the 
                                  dog park in Sandy. There are many elderly and handicapped people that use the park and 
                                  so there is a need for a restroom or port-a-let in the park. 

                                  Public Hearing comment period closed.

                                  Council questions followed. The City Attorney's Office will look into the differences in the 
                                  language on the ballot and provide clarification at a future meeting.

8.         23-404                 A Resolution authorizing a Memorandum of Understanding between Sandy 
                                  City and Shelter the Homeless.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item.
                                  Resolution #23-49C
                                  MOU Between STH and Sandy City

                                  Tracy Cowdell, Council Attorney, presented the Memorandum of Understanding between 
                                  Shelter the Homeless and Sandy City. He provided the background information and 
                                  spoke about the collaborative process to create the MOU. This was a significant effort 
                                  and he expressed appreciation to the staff at Shelter the Homeless for their efforts.  

                                  Public comment opened:

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke about the restricted items, and suggested additional 
                                  restrictions on knives of a certain length. 

                                  Ms. Elise Taylor expressed concerns regarding the security at the shelter and suggested 
                                  that a minimum of two security guards should be on staff at all times. She is in favor of 
                                  the shelter, but would like the community in the area to be protected. This needs to be a 
                                  secure facility.

                                  Mr. Jacob Wiedrich is happy with the direction of MOU, but he thinks this is a huge 
                                  mistake for Sandy City. There is a lot of corruption in the homeless community. 

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Council comments followed. Council acknowledged the efforts of the Council, Council 
                                  Attorney, and Shelter the Homeless staff for the collaborative process used to create the 
                                  MOU. The Council expressed support of the partnership with Shelter the Homeless. The 
                                  Council worked to provide an MOU that has increased the comfort and confidence for the 
                                  Council and the community and addressed the concerns with security and safety brought 
                                  up by residents over the past few weeks. The Mayor expressed support and pride as the 
                                  city moves forward with bringing the shelter for the medically vulnerable population to 
                                  Sandy.  This is an important issue.
                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa, to adopt 
                                  Resolution 23-49C authorizing a Memorandum of Understanding between Sandy 
                                  City and Shelter the Homeless... The motion carried by the following vote:

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged Kasey Dunlavy for his department's 
                    support of the Canyons Education Foundation golf tournament. His foursome finished 
                    first. She acknowledged the efforts of staff organizing the upcoming Trunk or Treat event 
                    and will have her 1970 Chevelle at the event.  

                    Council Member Scott Earl provided an update of the general plan committee focusing on
