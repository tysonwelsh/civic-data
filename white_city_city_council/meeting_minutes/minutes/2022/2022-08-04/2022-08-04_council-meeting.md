Moderate Income Housing Plan Requirements

               Paul Ashton stated the planning commission held a public hearing for the
moderate income housing plan requirements that the legislation passed in HB 462, during the
2022 session. Erin O’Kelley, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD), has
taken the feedback from the planning commission and made various recommendations for the
council’s review. He recommended holding a workshop to discuss the moderate income housing
plan on either August 18 or 25, 2022.

               The Council agreed to hold a workshop on August 18, 2022 at 6:00 pm.

White City Financial Report

               Dave Sanderson, Financial Manager, reviewed the White City Metro Township
administrative budget stating that 50% of the year has gone by. The American Rescue Plan
(ARPA) funds need to be spent by 2024.

              Council Member Perry, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to accept the
White City Metro Township financial report. The motion passed unanimously.

Unified Fire Authority

                 Captain Ken Aldridge updated the Council on the progress of various fire stations
that are under construction. The wildland hand crew completed a 14-day assignment and were
then dispatched to Idaho. The fuels crew helped with chipping services and have provided
personnel for fire suppression. Retired fire fighter Alex Kluger was killed in a motorcycle accident
on August 3, 2022. Sandy City Fire sent its fire report, and they are committed to increasing its
staffing to a three-person crew.

                                  ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦
Unified Police Department

              Detective Jeff Fenton stated high crimes in White City are vehicle burglaries. On
Wednesday the motors unit is getting together to do traffic enforcement. The Community Police
Academy registration is opening soon. The academy is 12 weeks and is usually held on Tuesday’s
from 6:00 p.m. – 10:00 p.m.

                Chief Randy Thomas stated Unified Police Department (UPD) and Canyons
School District have been discussing school shootings, gang violence and other types of
incidents. Officers have gone through active shooter training. Once the new schools in Canyons
School District are up and running, officers will tour those schools to become familiar with the
layouts. Bus drivers and crossing guards are needed throughout the school district.

Utah League of Cities and Towns Membership

               Rori Andreason stated the Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT) has made
the determination that Metro Townships are allowed to join the organization. ULCT lobbies for
cities and has a lot of influence in the legislature. It offers trainings, resources, and represents its

Salt Lake County Justice Courts

                Paul Ashton stated the metro townships receive justice court services through an
interlocal agreement with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) and Salt Lake
County. The Salt Lake County Council notified the Justice Courts of its intent to dissolve the
courts. If the Council wants to look elsewhere for justice court services, it will need to notify the
MSD and Salt Lake County that it wants to modify the interlocal agreement.

              Council Member Little stated it may make sense to look into contracting with
Sandy City Justice Courts.

               Council Member Perry stated initiating the letter will open the discussion and start
the process of finding justice court services.

              Council Member Cardenaz, seconded by Council Member Price, moved to
approve the process of sending a letter to continue discussions on justice court services. The
motion passed unanimously.
