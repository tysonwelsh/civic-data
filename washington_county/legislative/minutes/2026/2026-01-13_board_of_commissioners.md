 1.     ZONE CHANGE (Public Hearing – Legislative): Review an application for zone change for a
        parcel in Veyo. The affected parcel is CB-5-NW located at approximately at 102 N Main, Veyo. The
        current zone is RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1-acre minimum lot size), the applicant is proposing
        parcel CB-5-NW be rezoned to R-3 (Multi-Family Residential 8,000 square feet minimum lot size)
        Zone. Rezoning will bring an existing triplex into zoning compliance. The applicant is Slade Hughes.

        Mr. Scott Messel provided a staff report below with staff recommendations:

        The applicant, Mr. Slade Hughes, is requesting a zone change for the property
        located at 102 N Main Street, Veyo, Utah 84782, from the RA-1 (Residential
        Agricultural, one-acre minimum lot size) zone to the R-3 (Multi-Family Residential,
        8,000-square-foot minimum lot size) zone. The subject property is located on Lot
        5 of the Chad Burns Subdivision, which was approved in 1983. The existing
        structure on the property was constructed approximately in 1997 and consists of
        a 2,688-square-foot building containing three separate residential units, two units
        on the ground floor and one unit on the second floor with no internal connections
        between units. At the time of construction, the building was constructed as three
        separate units. the property has not been zoned in a manner consistent with its
        existing use. Approval of the requested rezoning would bring the property into
zoning compliance with its established multi-family configuration. Concerns have
been raised regarding recreational vehicles (RVs) located on the property and their
use for residential occupancy. Under the proposed R-3 zoning designation, the
property owners RVs may remain on the property; however, they may not be used
as dwelling units. In support of the zone change request, the applicant has
demonstrated compliance with the requirements for rezoning to a higher-density
district, including documentation of adequate culinary water, septic feasibility, fire
protection planning, and legal access.

Staff Recommendations:

Staff recommends the Planning Commission hold the required public hearing and send a
recommendation to the County Commission to approve the zone change request from the
RA-1 (Residential Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot size) zone, to the R3 (Multi-Family
Residential 8,000 minimum lot size) zone for parcel CB-5-NW located in Veyo, based on
the following findings and conditions:
    1. The Public Hearing was properly noticed on December 16th, 2025.
    2. The public hearing is being held on January 13th, 2026, in the
       regularly scheduled Planning Commission Meeting.
    3. Approval of the requested rezoning would bring the property into
       compliance with the County’s zoning regulations relative to its existing
       multi-family use.
    4. The property is located immediately north of the main intersection in
       Veyo, an area where higher residential density is considered appropriate
       and compatible with surrounding development patterns.
    5. The zone change request would make incremental improvement within
       the county in regard to zoning compliance and legally existing lots.

         to encompass 8 acres, simultaneously expanding the MFG (Manufacturing) zoning designation. The zone

         which will formally eliminate several parcels which were created as a result of agricultural splits. The

will remain zoning compliant for the A-10 (Agricultural 10-acre minimum lot size) zone and will continue

Mr. Scott Messel provided the following staff report:

The applicant, John Leavitt, is requesting a zone change for the property located approximately at 300 N
Main St, Veyo, UT 84782, from the A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre minimum lot size) zone to the MFG
(Manufacturing) zone. The Leavitt family has owned this property for an extended period of time and,
over the years, portions of the land were divided into multiple parcels without completing the formal
subdivision process required by Washington County. The applicant is now working to correct this issue
by combining parcels to create a single, legally recorded parcel totaling approximately eight acres. The
creation of the eight-acre parcel allows the property to meet the applicable septic density requirements
for the Veyo area. As part of this request, the applicant is proposing to rezone approximately three acres
of the newly created eight-acre parcel from A-10 (Agricultural, 10-acre minimum lot size) zone to MFG
(Manufacturing) zone. The remaining acreage would retain the existing A-10 zoning designation. The
applicant has submitted a rough conceptual site plan illustrating the proposed use of the property. The
proposed use is storage rental units, which are a permitted use in the MFG (Manufacturing) zone. At this
time, the site plan is provided for informational purposes only and is not being reviewed as part of this
zone change application. Detailed site plan review would occur at a later date, should the zone change
from A-10 to MFG be approved. Generally, Washington County does not support rezoning portions of a
parcel and prefers that rezoning requests apply to an entire parcel. In this case, the applicant is
requesting rezoning of only three acres of the eight-acre parcel, which staff does not consider a best
practice. Access to the property is taken from State Route 18 (SR-18), which is under the jurisdiction of
the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT). Any new or modified access associated with future
development would require review and approval from UDOT. The applicant has demonstrated
compliance with the requirements for rezoning to a higher-density district, including documentation of
adequate culinary water, septic feasibility, fire protection planning, and legal access.

Staff recommends the Planning Commission hold the required public hearing and determine if the zone
change should be granted, revised, or denied, and then send its recommendation to the County
Commission for its consideration of the zone change request from the A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre
minimum lot size) zone, to the MFG (Manufacturing) zone for three acres of parcel 7170-A-1-A-1-F-NW
located in Veyo. The Planning Commission can consider the following findings and conditions:

        1. The Public Hearing was properly noticed on December 16th, 2025.

           Planning Commission Meeting.

           zone.
