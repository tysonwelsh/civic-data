5:15 Council Meeting
                                  Council Chair Cyndi Sharkey read a statement regarding the continuation of virtual
                                  meetings without an anchor location.

                                  Chair Sharkey welcomed those in attendance and explained to the community that this
                                  evening's meeting would consist of a hybrid format with the Council Members and staff
                                  participating virtually and in-person in the Council Chambers.

Prayer / Pledge of Allegiance

                                  Council Member Brooke Christensen offered the prayer.

                                  Council Member Monica Zoltanski led the Pledge.

Work Session Items

1.         21-203                 Administrative Services Department providing a monthly sales tax and
                                  budget update.
                   Attachments:   FY 2021 YTD Sales Tax 1-9.pdf

                                  Brett Neuman with the Finance Department, presented the sales tax report for the activity
                                  period ending in March 2021 and distributed in May. The current sales tax distribution
                                  once again exceeded budgeted expectations by $700,000. Council questions and
                                  comments followed. Council thanked the Finance Department staff.

2.         21-204                 Public Works, Public Utilities, and Parks and Recreation presenting their
                                  FY 2021-22 tentative department budgets.
                   Attachments:   Public Works
                                  Public Utilities
                                  Parks and Recreation

                                  Mike Gladbach, Paul Browning, Ryan Kump, and Nathan Schaffer with the Public Works
                                  Department presented the department budget. Mr. Gladbach provided an overview of the
                                  budget and detailed the Fiscal Year 2022 budget requests which includes new fleet
                                  purchases. Capital improvement projects for FY 22 include traffic signal upgrades, curb
                                  and gutter upgrades, Highland Drive EIS, electronic traffic control devices, intersection
                                  improvements and the reconstruction and widening of specific roads in Sandy.
                                  Replacement projects planned for the next fiscal year include street reconstruction and
                                  hazardous concrete repair. Council questions and comments occurred throughout the
                                  presentation.

                                  Council moved to Time Certain Items on the Agenda and heard General Citizen
                                  Comments.

                                  Tom Ward with Public Utilities provided an overview of the department budget, enterprise
                                  funds, and capital projects. He detailed fiscal year 2022 budget requests and savings
                                  resulting from smart fiscal management and the asset management program. Currently,
                                  there is no plan to raise water rates. Council questions and comments followed. The
                                  Council thanked the Public Utilities team and members of the Public Utilities Advisory
                                  Board for their hard work on behalf of Sandy City. Mr. Ward also addressed Sandy City's
                                  response to the current drought conditions and reviewed the water supply for Sandy. He
                                  stressed conscientious use of water by the City and residents is necessary during the
                                  current drought conditions and he reviewed the smart water saving tools available to
                                  residents on the City website.

                                  Dan Medina with Parks and Recreation reviewed the fiscal year 2022 budgets for Parks,
                                  Community Arts, Senior Center, Alta Canyon Recreation Center, and River Oaks Golf
                                  Course. Council comments and questions occurred throughout the presentation. Mr.
                                  Medina reviewed the proposed future capital projects and fiscal year 2022 budget
                                  requests. Council provided feedback and suggestions for the Parks Department.




3.         21-205                 Administrative Services Department providing the Council with an update
                                  on the American Rescue Plan
                   Attachments:   ARP Presentation.pdf

                                  Brian Kelley with the Finance Department presented an overview of the American Rescue
                                  Plan Act Funding (ARPA). Sandy City will receive an allocation of approximately $9.1
                                  million dollars from ARPA. The funds can be used for eligible expenses through
                                  December 31, 2024. He reviewed the goals of the program funds and eligible expenses
                                  covered by the ARPA funding. Council questions and comments occurred throughout the
                                  presentation. Council discussed capital projects that the ARPA funds could be utilized
                                  for. Staff provided recommendations for use of the funds for Council review and
                                  consideration.

                                  Council convened a 5 minute break at approximately 8:17 pm.
                                  Council reconvened the meeting at approximately 8:22 pm.




4.         21-207                 Administrative Services presenting facilities financing plan options.

                                  Brian Kelley with the Finance Department, presented an overview of the future proposed
                                  capital projects and the associated costs. Mr. Kelley reviewed three different scenarios
                                  with the Council detailing the possible funding sources for these projects and the
                                  associated costs. He provided an analysis of the assumptions used for each of the
                                  scenarios. Council questions and comments occurred throughout the presentation.

                                  Council Member Houseman requested for this Agenda item to be rescheduled for a future

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                                  Mike Applegarth, Council Executive Director, thanked the Council for their feedback and
                                  support as we move towards hybrid Council meetings in the future. Next week's Council
                                  meeting will take place in the Council Chambers. The community is invited to participate
                                  in person in the Chambers or virtually. Mr. Applegarth reviewed upcoming agenda items.
                                  The Council agenda planning calendar will be sent out tomorrow.

Council Member Business

                                  Council Member Houseman acknowledged the staff at the Sandy Senior Center for
                                  hosting a town hall event for Congressman John Curtis. Ms. Houseman gave an update
                                  from the Central Wasatch Commission meeting. The commission has drafted a Pillar
                                  document which if approved by the CWC at the June 7th meeting will be forwarded to
                                  UDOT for consideration. She thanked the City staff for their efforts and help with the draft
                                  document.

                                  Council Member Zoltanski discussed a Salt Lake Tribune article that provided details
                                  about the Salt Lake City budget. She found the article offered a great guide to
                                  understanding budgets and recommended it to the Council and residents. Ms. Zoltanski
                                  recently attended Les Miserables at the Hale Centre Theatre and thought it was a
                                  wonderful performance.

                                  Council Member Robinson reminded residents to follow the city's ordinance which
                                  recommends watering yards after 10:00 pm. He asked residents to adjust their watering
                                  schedule in an effort to conserve water during the current drought conditions.

Mayor's Report

                                  Evelyn Everton, Deputy Mayor, spoke about the 4th of July events planned for this year.
                                  There will be a fireworks show. Details can be found on the Sandy City website and the
                                  events will be listed in the June issue of the Sandy Journal. She also updated the Council
                                  on the recent special session and provided some details related to the American Rescue
                                  Fund and affordable housing project funds that the city may be eligible for.

CAO Report

                                  Matt Huish spoke about the city's response to a resident's concern from last week's
                                  meeting. He mentioned the city has a very comprehensive water conservation plan in
                                  place for this year.
