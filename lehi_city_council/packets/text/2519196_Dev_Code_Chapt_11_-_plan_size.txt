Section 04.060. Criteria for Approval.
No amendment to this Code, or the Zoning District Map(s), may be recommended for approval by the Commission
nor approved by the City Council unless such amendment or conditions thereto are consistent with the General Plan.
In considering a Code amendment, or Zoning District Map(s) amendment, the applicant shall identify, and the City
Staff, DRC, Commission, and the City Council may consider the following factors, among others:

    1.   The effect of the proposed amendment on the character of the surrounding area;
    2.   Consistency with the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    3.   Consistency and Compatibility of the proposed zone with the General Plan land uses of nearby and adjoining
         properties.
    4.   The suitability of the properties for the uses requested and their suitability for the existing uses identified by
         the General Plan;
    5.   Whether a change in the uses allowed for the affected properties will unduly affect the uses, or proposed uses
         for nearby and adjoining properties;
    6.   The gain to the public health, safety and welfare from the existing classification to the proposed amendment;
         and
    7.   The overall community benefit of the proposed amendment.


  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant is requesting review and recommendation of an amendment to Chapter 11 of the Development code to update
plan size requirements. The proposed amendment includes setting the minimum page size for site plans and subdivision
submittals to be 22 x 34 inches, unless otherwise approved by the Lehi City engineer, and requiring there to be no more
than two City standard details per sheet. The amendment to the page size requirement applies to site plans, preliminary
subdivisions, final subdivisions, and two-lot subdivisions. This amendment was initiated to make it easier for the plans to
be printed in the field and for legibility.

The DRC had two comments for this development code amendment. They are:
1. Provide option to scale to 11x17. All electronic submittals shall be full size PDF.
2. 22x34 inches unless otherwise approved by the Lehi City Engineer.

During the DRC it was determined that including the 22 x 34 inch requirement would allow the plans to be scaled to 11 x
17 if needed. The Planning Department has already updated the language to reflect this and is included in the packet.

  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development and Municipal Code Amendments are Legislative items with full discretion. This means that the Plan-
ning Commission may choose to provide a positive or negative recommendation. They also may request any changes
to the proposed amendment.

The public may also speak to the proposed amendment. They can bring up any concerns or support for the changes.
In order for changes to happen to the written text, they will need the Planning Commission to include it in their motion
and have City Council approve it.


  POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may move to recommend approval or denial of the proposed amendments to the Lehi
City Development Code. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

    1.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provi-
         sions of the General Plan and its various elements.
    2.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.
