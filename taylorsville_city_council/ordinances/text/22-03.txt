              SUMMARY:     Mark Stephenson, Rocky Mountain Builders, is requesting to change the Proposed
                           Land Use Map designation in the City' s General Plan for the property at 2720 W.
                           6200 S. from" Professional Office" to" Medium-Density Mixed-Use."


                           The applicant is seeking this General Plan Map amendment with the intention to
                           develop a vertical mixed-use building containing three( 3) retail/ commercial units
                           and three( 3) residential dwelling units. Conceptual development plans are
                           included in this report( Exhibit B) illustrating a proposed development scenario if
                           the General Plan Map amendment and subsequent rezone request are approved by
                           the City Council.

                           The Taylorsville General Plan is intended to guide land use and development
                           decisions that address the present and future needs of the City and to accomplish
                           coordinated, efficient, and harmonious growth and development within the City.
                           The City Council, at their discretion, may make revisions to the General Plan that
                           it considers appropriate and that are in the best interest of the City and its residents
                            per Section 13. 06. 030: General Plan Adoption and Amendments).



                           Page 3- 6 of the Taylorsville General Plan states the following," All amendments to
                           the Proposed Land Use Map should be required to consider and not be in conflict
                           with the following principles:
                                   Promote economic sustainability
                                   Promote efficient use of land and public infrastructure

                                   Provide community amenities and benefits




                                                1
                                       Protect environmentally sensitive lands
                                       Support alternate modes of transportation including pedestrian
                                       Create safe attractive neighborhoods and protect residential quality of life
                                       Minimize non- compatible adjacent land uses

                                Based on this information, staff supports the applicant' s request to amend the

                                General Plan map from Professional Office to Medium-Density Mixed-Use. The
                                following is a summary of staffs findings regarding the proposed General Plan
                                amendment:

                                       The proposed designation allows for residential and non- residential uses to

                                       expand the City' s tax base and economic sustainability.
                                       The new designation allows for the potential for additional housing in
                                       Taylorsville, helping to diversify the housing stock;
                                       The proposed designation promotes an efficient use of land, allowing
                                       multiple uses on a relatively small and undeveloped property;
                                       The proposed designation would allow for an attractive development that

                                       incorporates pedestrian- oriented design to enhance the quality of life of city
                                       residents;


                                       The proposed designation is consistent with the goal of minimizing potential
                                       non- compatible land uses.


                                The Planning Commission forwarded a positive recommendation to the City
                                Council for the zoning map amendment request on February 22, 2022.
                               Staff recommends approval of Ordinance No. 22- 03 based on the findings outlined

       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council( the" Council") met in regular session on April
6, 2022, to consider, among other things, approving a General Plan Map Amendment from
 Professional Office" to" Medium-Density Mixed-Use" for 0. 640 acres ofproperty located at 2720
W. 6200 S.; and


       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 030, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the city' s official website
on February 9, 2022; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on February 9, 2022; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also mailed to each affected entity, as defined in Utah Code § 10- 9a- 103( 3),
                                                                      on February 11, 2022;
and;




        WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on February 22, 2022, during which the Planning Commission voted 4-
2 to recommend that the Council approve the proposed General Plan Map amendment; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on April 6,
2022; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed General Plan Map amendment is hereby approved for the property described below and
illustrated in Exhibit A:

  RE:             Submittal Request for General Plan Map Amendment

           With the proposed Amendment, the proposed infill project will provide (3) New
           Retail Business and ( 3) New Residential Units that will not conflict with the listed
           criteria below:



  Amending the Proposed Land Use Map

  The Taylorsville City Proposed Land Use Map and accompanying land use goals, objectives, action
  statements, and best practice policy statements should direct future development and redevelopment
  decision-making and also strengthen, complement and reinforce other elements of the General Plan. From
  time to time, however, it may be appropriate to amend the Proposed Land Use Map based on changing
  technologies, market demands, or other unforeseen circum- stances. All amendments to the Proposed
  Land Use Map should be required to consider and not be in conflict with the following principles:

          Promote economic sustainability

          Promote efficient use of land and public infrastructure


          Provide community amenities and benefits

          Protect environmentally sensitive lands

          Support alternate modes of transportation including pedestrian

          Create safe attractive neighborhoods and protect residential quality of life

          Minimize non- compatible adjacent land uses




           The Project will promote economic sustainability and create an attractive
           addition to the neighborhood along with protecting the residential quality of life
           in this area.
