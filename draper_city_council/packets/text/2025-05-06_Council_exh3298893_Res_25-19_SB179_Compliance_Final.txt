                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-19

       A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING AN INTERIM POLICY FOR PROCESSING
       CLASSIFICATION REQUESTS FOR NEW AND UNLISTED BUSINESS USES PURSUANT TO
       SENATE BILL 179.

WHEREAS, the Utah State Legislature enacted Senate Bill 179 (SB 179) during the 2025
legislative session, codified as Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-507.5, effective May 7, 2025; and

WHEREAS, SB 179 requires municipalities, including Draper City, to adopt ordinances
establishing a process for reviewing and approving classification requests to determine
whether a proposed business use aligns with existing land uses or constitutes a new or
unlisted business use, and to designate appropriate zones for approved uses; and

WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) 9-8-050(B) authorizes the Zoning
Administrator to resolve ambiguities in use classifications by determining if a proposed use
is substantially similar to permitted uses, subject to appeal under DCMC 9-5-180; and

WHEREAS, DCMC 9-8-050(B) does not fully satisfy SB 179’s requirements, including
provisions for City Council review of new or unlisted business uses, designation of zones,
and a specific appeal process; and

WHEREAS, due to statutory public notice and hearing requirements for text amendments
under Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-205, it is not feasible for Draper City to adopt a fully
compliant code amendment by May 7, 2025; and

WHEREAS, Draper City is actively drafting amendments to the DCMC to achieve full
compliance with SB 179; and

WHEREAS, pending adoption of such amendments, Draper City intends to implement an
interim policy leveraging DCMC 9-8-050(B) and existing appeal procedures, supplemented
by City Council review for new or unlisted business uses, to process classification requests
in substantial compliance with SB 179; and

WHEREAS, this interim policy is necessary to ensure orderly land use administration,
protect the public interest, and minimize legal vulnerabilities while the City finalizes its code
amendments; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1. Interim Policy for Processing Classification Requests.

       A. Classification Requests: Until the DCMC is amended to fully comply with SB 179,
          the Zoning Administrator shall process classification requests to determine
          whether a proposed business use aligns with an existing use in the DCMC,
          pursuant to DCMC 9-8-050(B).

       B. Existing Use Determinations: If the Zoning Administrator determines the
          proposed use aligns with an existing use, the applicant may proceed under the
          applicable DCMC regulations.

       C. New or Unlisted Business Uses: If the Zoning Administrator determines the
          proposed use is a new or unlisted business use, the applicant may submit an
          application to the City Council for review. The City Council shall review the
          application in a public meeting, consider whether to approve or deny the use
          based on consistency with the General Plan, public health and safety, and zoning
          compatibility, and, if approved, designate appropriate zones.

       D. Denials and Appeals: If the Zoning Administrator’s classification or the City
          Council’s decision is disputed or a new/unlisted use is denied, the City shall
          provide written reasons for the decision. Applicants may appeal the Zoning
          Administrator’s classification or denial to the appeal authority designated under
          DCMC 9-5-180, within the timeframe specified in the DCMC.

       E. Good-Faith Compliance: This interim policy is intended to substantially comply
          with SB 179’s requirements pending adoption of a permanent text amendment,
          and all actions taken under this policy shall be deemed consistent with the City’s
          obligations under Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-507.5.

Section 2. Commitment to Code Amendment. Draper City shall diligently pursue
amendments to the DCMC to fully comply with SB 179, including adopting a permanent
process for reviewing classification requests, approving new or unlisted business uses,
designating zones, and referencing such process in all relevant use tables.

Section 3. Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this
Resolution is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such decision shall not affect the validity
or enforceability of the remaining portions of this Resolution.

Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its adoption
and shall remain in effect until the DCMC is amended to fully comply with SB 179.
