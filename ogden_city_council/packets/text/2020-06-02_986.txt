                                                  4:00 p.m. Work Session
                                                       June 2, 2020

The purpose of the work session includes presentations and discussions regarding:
    • Agenda review of the City Council meeting;
    • Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Budget:
        o Public Services, including:
            ▪ Public Services General Fund
            ▪ Water, Sanitary Sewer, Storm Sewer, Refuse Utility Funds
            ▪ Golf Course, Recreation Enterprise Funds
        o Capital Improvement Plan;
        o Fee Changes:
            ▪ Fire Burn Permit Fee
            ▪ Planning Review and Petition Fees
            ▪ Airport Land Lease Rates
        o Schedule A-1;
    • Council business; and
    • Council comments.

                            Any items not fully addressed prior to the City Council meeting,
                    which begins at 6:00 p.m., may be addressed immediately following that meeting.


                                              6:00 p.m. City Council Meeting
                                                      June 2, 2020

1. Roll Call.

2. Approval of Minutes: (Voice vote)
   a. Regular meeting of February 4, 2020 – Council member White

3. Public Hearing:
   a. Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Tentative Budget. Proposed Ordinance 2020-17 adopting the tentative budget of Ogden City
       for Fiscal Year 2020-2021. (Adopt/not adopt ordinance – roll call vote)

4. New Business:
   a. Enterprise Fund Transfer. Proposed Resolution 2020-13 scheduling a public hearing to allow public input regarding
      proposed transfer from the City’s Utility Enterprise Fund to the General Fund. (Adopt resolution to set public hearing
      for June 23, 2020 – voice vote)
