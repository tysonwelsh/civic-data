                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-24


         A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF RIVERTON CITY
           APPOINTING CLAUDE WELLS AS THE CITY’S ALTERNATE
          REPRESENTATIVE ON THE UNIFIED FIRE SERVICE AREA AND
                    UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY BOARDS.

WHEREAS, Riverton City (the “City”) is a part of the Unified Fire Service Area which utilizes
the Unified Fire Authority for first responder services; and

WHEREAS, Riverton City is also a member-city of the Unified Fire Authority, an interlocal
entity which provides first responder services to its members; and

WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint a representative to serve on the governing boards of
the Unified Fire Service Area and the Unified Fire Authority; and

WHEREAS, Councilmember Sheldon Stewart has serves as the City’s representative on said
boards but is in need of an alternate to serve when Councilmember Stewart is unavailable;


NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the governing body of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

     1. Councilmember Claude Wells is hereby appointed to serve as the City’s alternate
        representative on the governing boards of the Unified Fire Service Area and the Unified
        Fire Authority.
