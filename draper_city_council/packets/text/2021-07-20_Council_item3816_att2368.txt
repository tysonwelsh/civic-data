                                     9-31-020: SCOPE:
                                     The requirements of this chapter shall apply to any I-ADUs and D-ADUs within the
                                     city. I-ADUs shall be a permitted use in all primary residential zoning districts
                                     within the I-AUD overlay zone. D-ADUs shall be a permitted use on individual
                                     single-family lots or parcels in all primary residential zoning districts or when a
                                     including single lot or parcel unit development is approved in the RM1 and RM2
                                     zoning districts, but only when the minimum lot areas as required in section 9-10-
                                     090, table 9-10-3 of this title, is satisfied. D-ADUs are also permitted in residential
                                     special districts (RSD) if the minimum lot area of the single unit lot or parcel is
                                     twelve thousand (12,000) square feet or larger.

                                     9-31-045: I-ADU DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS:

                                     A. Existing Structure: An I-ADU must be contained within the existing footprint of
                                        primary dwelling structure….
                                     B. Conditions: An I-ADU may be allowed under the following conditions:
                                              a. Only one I-ADU shall be allowed per lot or parcel.
                                              b. At least one additional parking stall above that required for the primary
                                              dwelling unit shall be provided on site. The parking stall may not be
                                              tandem with parking serving the primary dwelling unit and shall be accessible
                                              from the street by a driveway constructed of concrete or asphalt. Driveways
                                              must conform to all requirements of Section 9-10-075 and other applicable
                                              requirements of this Title.

                                     9-31-030: DEFINITIONS:LIMITATIONS:

                                     A. D-ADUs may not be constructed on any property containing an I-ADU. As a
                                     condition of approval for any application for construction of a D-ADU the property
                                     owner shall agree to waive any right to construct an I-ADU on the same property
                                     unless the D-ADU is first removed from the property. This condition shall be
                                     included in the recorded notice of accessory dwelling unit required by Section 9-
                                     31-060 of this chapter.

                                     B. Only one I-ADU within the primary dwelling is permitted. I-ADUs are not
                                     permitted within a D-ADU or other dwelling structure.

                                     9-31-030: DEFINITIONS:LIMITATIONS:

                                     C. I-ADUs shall not be permitted in single-family residential dwellings located on
                                     lots or parcels that are six-thousand square feet (6,000 ft²) or less in size.

                                     D. I-ADUs shall not be approved in mobile homes, nor in a primary dwelling unit
                                     with a failing septic tank system.

                                     E. Separate utility meters may not be installed for any I-ADU or D-ADU.
