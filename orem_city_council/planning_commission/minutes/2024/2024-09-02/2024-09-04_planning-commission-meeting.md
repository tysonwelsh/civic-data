 3   Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Tres Miller, for the Planning Commission to vacate Lots 3 and 4 of The Concord,
 4   Plat A and approve The Concord, Plat B located generally at 507 North 400 West in the R6.5 zone.
                                    5
                                    6    Staff Presentation: The applicant is requesting to combine two lots that he
                                    7    owns into one lot. When the property had been rezoned to create four lots, two
                                    8    of them fronted 400 West. There is an existing house that was to be torn down
                                    9    and removed because it straddles the property line between the two new lots.
                                   10
                                   11    The applicant decided he no longer wanted to tear the existing house down, so
                                   12    he now needs to combine the two lots into one lot as structures are not allowed
                                   13    to straddle property lines. With this plat amendment, the house can remain and
                                   14    will no longer straddle the property line.
15
16   Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
17   Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission to vacate Lots 3 and 4 of The Concord, Plat A and approve The
18   Concord, Plat B located generally at 507 North 400 West in the R6.5 zone.
19
20   Chair Komen asked if the Planning Commission had any questions for Ms. Leon.
21
22   Chair Komen invited the applicant to come forward. Tres Miller introduced himself. Mr. Miller said it makes more
23   sense to keep the house than to separate the property.
24
25   Chair Komen asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did,
26   she called for a motion on this item.
27
28   Planning Commission Action: Mr. Runolfson moved to vacate Lots 3 and 4 of The Concord, Plat A and approve
29   The Concord, Plat B located generally at 507 North 400 West in the R6.5 zone. Mr. Hawkes seconded the motion.
30   Those voting aye: Mike Carpenter, Gerald Crismon, Rod Erickson, James Hawkes, Madeline Komen, Britton
31   Runolfson and Haysam Sakar. The motion passed unanimously.
