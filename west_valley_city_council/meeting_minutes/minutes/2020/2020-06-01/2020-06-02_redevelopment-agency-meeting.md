A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING RE-OPENING THE FY 2019-
     2020 BUDGET
     Chairman Christensen informed a public hearing had been advertised for the
     Regular Redevelopment Agency Meeting scheduled June 2, 2020, in order for the
     Agency to hear and consider public comments regarding re-opening the FY 2019-
     2020 Budget.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            State Statute Title 10, Chapter 6, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended,
            allows the City of West Valley to amend its budget during the year. The
            West Valley City Redevelopment Agency holds, as needed on a quarterly
            basis, public hearings to amend the annual budget.

            A Public Notice was posted May 21, 2020 in (the Salt Lake Tribune and
            the Deseret Morning News) general circulation to the general public.
            Notice was given that a public hearing is to be held June 2, 2020 at 6:30
            p.m., West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City,
            Utah.

     Chairman Christensen opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in
     favor or opposition, Chairman Christensen closed the Public Hearing.

     ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 20-07, AMEND THE BUDGET OF
     WEST VALLEY CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR THE FISCAL
     YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2019 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2020 TO
     REFLECT CHANGES IN THE BUDGET FROM INCREASED REVENUES
     AND AUTHORIZE THE DISBURSEMENT OF FUNDS
     The Redevelopment Agency previously held a public hearing regarding proposed
     Resolution 20-07 that would amend the budget of West Valley City Redevelopment
     Agency for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2019 and ending June 30, 2020 to
     reflect changes in the budget from increased revenues and authorize the
     disbursement of funds.

     Upon inquiry by Chairman Christensen there were no further questions from
     members of the Agency, and he called for a motion.

     Mr. Buhler moved to approve Resolution 20-07.

     Ms. Lang seconded the motion.

B.   ACCEPT PUBLIC INPUT REGARDING THE FY 2020-2021 BUDGET
     Chairman Christensen informed a public hearing had been advertised for the
     Regular Redevelopment Agency Meeting scheduled June 2, 2020, in order for the
     Agency to hear and consider public comments regarding the FY 2020-2021
     Budget.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            This resolution adopts the final budget for the Redevelopment Agency of
            West Valley City for the 2020-2021 fiscal year after receiving public
            comment at a hearing on June 2, 2020.

            The Redevelopment Agency adopted a tentative budget that was made
            available for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s
            Recorder’s office and gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment,
            before adopting this final budget for FY 2020-2021.

     Chairman Christensen opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in
     favor or opposition, Chairman Christensen closed the Public Hearing.

     ACTION: CONSIDER RESOLUTION 20-08, ADOPT THE ANNUAL
     BUDGET OF THE WEST VALLEY CITY REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY
     FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1, 2020 AND ENDING
     JUNE 30, 2021
     The Redevelopment Agency previously held a public hearing regarding proposed
     Resolution 20-08 that would adopt the annual budget of the West Valley City
     Redevelopment Agency for the fiscal year commencing July 1, 2020 and ending
     June 30, 2021.
