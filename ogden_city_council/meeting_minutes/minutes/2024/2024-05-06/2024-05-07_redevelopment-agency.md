Proposed Resolution 2024-1 regarding a budget amendment in the amount
of $66,064,650 to recognize and appropriate bond proceeds and additional
funds to the Redevelopment Agency General Fund, Housing Fund, Hinckley
Drive Redevelopment Area and Continental Redevelopment Area budgets
          A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding a proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2023-
2024 budget amendment pertaining to sales tax and tax increment bonds Series 2023 came before the Board for consideration.
The memo stated the proposed budget amendment, totaling $66,064,650, makes the following changes:
               • Redevelopment Agency (RDA) General Fund. Recognizes interfund transfers from the Continental Project
                    Area and Housing Fund for marketing costs that were originally budgeted as a General Fund expenditure.
               • Hinckley Drive Project Area. Appropriates remaining fund balance from the expired Hinckley Drive Project
                    Area for airport-related expenses.
               • Continental Project Area. Recognizes revenues from bond proceeds and premiums and the sale of surplus
                    property in the Continental Project Area and appropriates those funds for bond issuance costs, property
                    improvements, repayment of existing debt, and a transfer to the General Fund for marketing costs.
               • Housing Fund. Recognizes the sale of property in the Housing Fund and transfers those funds to the General
                    Fund for marketing costs.
          Comptroller Sorensen summarized the memo and used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to highlight the use
allocation of funds identified in the staff report and budget amendment resolution document. For a copy of the presentation in its
entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
           Chair Richey introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2024-1, entitled:
                    “A resolution of Ogden City Redevelopment Agency amending the budget for the Fiscal Year July 1, 2023 to
                    June 30, 2024 by increasing the anticipated revenues for a gross increase of $66,064,650 from sources as
                    detailed in the body of this resolution; and increasing the appropriations for a gross increase of $66,064,650 as
                    detailed in the body of this resolution.”
          Chair Richey then opened the public hearing.
          Linda Sanchez read a quote from Thomas Jefferson regarding mental attitude, and she asked that the Board keep that
quote in mind when acting on fiscal matters of the Agency.
          Angel Castillo asked the Board to consider the 2017 Tax Cuts Job Act, which will expire next year. This will have a
major impact on municipal bonds and if there is extra money in the budget, it should go back to the City’s General Fund rather
than the RDA fund.
          There being no further persons appearing to be heard, BOARD MEMBER HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC
HEARING BE CLOSED. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER MYERS, ALL VOTING AYE.
          Vice Chair White asked Mr. Sorensen to address Ms. Castillo’s comments. Mr. Sorensen stated the Agency is currently
setting funding aside in the General Fund to offset the impacts mentioned by Ms. Castillo.
          ON A MOTION BY BOARD MEMBER HYER AND SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER BLAIR,
RESOLUTION 2024-1 WAS ADOPTED UPON THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE – BOARD
MEMBERS BLAIR, CHOBERKA, GRAF, HYER, MYERS, VICE CHAIR WHITE, AND CHAIR RICHEY. VOTING
NO – NONE.


     There being no further business to come before the Agency, BOARD MEMBER MYERS MOVED THE MEETING
ADJOURN AT 8:21 P.M. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER CHOBERKA, ALL VOTING AYE.
