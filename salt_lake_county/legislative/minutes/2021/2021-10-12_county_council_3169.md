RESOLUTION NO.  5901
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
APPROVING A CONTRIBUTION IN THE AMOUNT OF TWO 
MILLION DOLLARS TO THE FRIENDS OF SWITCHPOINT INC ., A 
NONPROFIT ENTITY.
WITNESSETH
WHEREAS Salt Lake County (the “County”) would like to contribute to The 
Friends of Switchpoint Inc. ( “Switchpoint”), a nonprofit entity with 
experience in providing homeless services, for the purpose of purchasing 
and renovating the Airport Inn Hotel property, located at 2333 West North 
Temple, Salt Lake City, to house approximately 130 homeless individuals in 
studio-style apartment units, on a short -term rental basis, and to provide 
additional employment related services.  The project will be known in the 
future as “The Point”. 
WHEREAS, under Utah Code Annotated 17-50-303(3)(a) and Salt Lake 
Countywide Policy 1200, the County may appropriate money to or provide 
nonmonetary assistance to a nonprofit entity if, in the judgment of the county 
Page 5 of 10
County Council Meeting Minutes October 12, 2021
legislative body, the assistance contributes to the safety, health, prosperity, 
moral well -being, peace, order, comfort, or convenience of county residents; 
and 
WHEREAS the Council finds that the abovementioned contribution to 
Swithcpoint benefits the Salt Lake County residents by addressing an urgent 
need to provide housing for our unsheltered homeless community and by 
mitigating against the ongoing spread of the COVID-19 virus.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the County Council 
of Salt Lake County:
1. That the contribution to Switchpoint, a nonprofit entity, in the amount of 
Two Million dollars ($2,000,000) is hereby approved for the purposes set 
forth herein.
(a) Switchpoint will operate The Point property as a non -congregant 
housing facility for approximately 130 homeless individuals in an effort to 
provide a safe and healthy living space, while also providing residents 
with training and job placement opportunities.
(b) The Point, owned and operated by Switchpoint, will provide a 
transitional housing arrangement until a more permanent housing option 
becomes available.  
(c) The operation of The Point by Switchpoint will serve to reduce the 
negative impacts of the COVID -19 virus within Salt Lake County and will 
provide a stable housing situation for members of our unsheltered 
community.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 12th day of October, 2021.
