3)
Public hearing and action regarding an amendment to a Conditional Use Permit, Master Planned Development, and Development Agreement for a 5,621 SF addition to the previously approved Maintenance Building for High Valley Transit, located at 1608 Sego Lily Way, Summit County, Utah; Parcel FJGILL-3-X GSBS Architects, Applicant. Project #23-158 Administrative Process.
Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner.
(6:08 P.M.)
Jennifer Strader,
Senior Planner,
presented background information regarding a Conditional Use Permit, Master Planned Development and Development Agreement for an addition to a previously approved maintenance building at 1608 Sego Lily Way.
Commissioner Cooke asked a clarifying question about affordable housing units to which Planner Strader responded.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:16 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:17 p.m.
Commissioner Hubler asked a question about electric buses. Scott Henrickson, GSB Architects, responded to the question.
Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the requested amendments to the CUP and Master Planned Development Agreement based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law and conditions of approval found in the staff report with the addition that the language be cleaned up to indicate that the workforce housing units are actually being built as part of phase one. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
