Pre-Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Discussion of Potential Paintball Tournament for First Responders.
Sterling Bronson stated that he would like to organize a paintball tournament fundraiser to
benefit the Lehi Fire and Police Departments. All the proceeds would be donated to a cause
specified by the City’s first responders. He said that the departments can put together a team for
the event. Mr. Bronson is not asking for any funds from the city, but he would like their support
and assistance with retaining the rodeo grounds for the event. Lehi High School could also be an
option. The Council liked the idea of hosting the event at the rodeo grounds, and suggested that
Mr. Bronson work with the City’s Events Coordinator.

2.2 Pressurized Irrigation Water Update
Dave Norman, Public Works Director, gave an update on the potential water shortage for this
year. He stated that as of the first of the month, the mountains had 70% of normal snowpack. He
said they were lucky to have a record month for moisture in February, but the other months were
an issue. He said that a significant concern is the water saturation levels which are at 25% of
normal. This is due to the dry summer and late start of winter which left the soil very dry, so they
are expecting a lot of moisture to be absorbed into the soil before it becomes runoff.

Matt Dalton, Water Department, stated that in pervious dry years, they’ve been able to acquire
surplus water from the Salt Lake Metropolitan Water District, but Lehi was told that would be
unlikely this year.
