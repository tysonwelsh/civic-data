Proposed Resolution 2024-12 appropriating funds in the amount of
$600,000 for earnest money and maintenance costs associated with the
purchase of the Forest Service Building
         A memo from the City Comptroller regarding a proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2024-2025 budget amendment came before
the Board for consideration. The memo stated Administration is proposing the purchase of the Forest Service Building for $3.6
Million Dollars. The proposed budget amendment ($600,000) recognizes funds from the Adams Community Reinvestment Area
(CRA) fund balance and loan proceeds and appropriates those funds for the 10 percent earnest money payment and estimated
maintenance costs for two years.
         Finance Director Sorensen summarized the memo and stated an additional budget amendment will be required prior to
closing to fund the remaining purchase price. It is anticipated that proceeds from a private placement bond issue will reimburse the
Agency for the earnest money payment and purchase of the building.
          Chair Richey introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2024-12, entitled:
                   “A resolution of the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency amending the budget for the Fiscal Year July 1, 2024,
                   to June 30, 2025, by increasing the anticipated revenues for a gross increase of $600,000 from sources as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution; and increasing the appropriations for a gross increase of $600,000 as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution.”
         Chair Richey then opened the public hearing.
         Travis Pate stated this proposal is a ‘no brainer’; he was an intern for Ogden City at the time that the City purchased the
old Ogden City Mall property and there were many problems with that transaction. For this project, purchasing the Forest Service
building, the City has the opportunity to preserve history, and he believes the building can continue to be a great asset to Ogden
City. He has extensively researched the history of the building and the block that it is located on he is very excited about this
project.
         Chair Richey then noted that the Board has concluded to continue this public hearing to December 10, 2024; action will
be taken that evening rather than tonight.
         There being no further persons appearing to be heard, BOARD MEMBER HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC
HEARING BE CONTINUED TO DECEMBER 10, 2024. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY BOARD MEMBER BLAIR,
ALL VOTING AYE.
