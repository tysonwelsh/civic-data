4. Rail Yard Lofts, 195 W Rushton
Petitioner Craig Marashky shared as a National Affordable Housing developer they wanted to
help provide a quality community.
Engineer Chris Bick with Kimley-Horn working closely with the project showed slides to support
exceptions and amenities like a clubhouse, swimming pool and community gardens.
The Commission raised concerns about the oversupply of apartments affecting single-family
home rentals and addressed community concerns about safety and aesthetics while
emphasizing the importance of maintaining the project's gateway significance.
Assistant Planning Director Joseph Simpson recommended approval with exceptions for
building setbacks, distances, and balcony heights.

Reports

Landmarks Commission-Mandy Shale said they had a great month and approved a few staff
ratifications, two Certificates of Historic Appropriateness, and approved the Pearl Building National
Historic nomination to move forward.

Citizen Advisory Committee-Jeremy Shinoda reported they had a good meeting and reviewed the Five-
Year Consolidation Plan, the Annual Action Plan and agreed upon the future meeting schedule.

Ogden Trails Network-Michelle Williams shared that they did not meet in December, but they had been
working on the trails and focusing on securing the golf course.

Review of Meeting-James Tanner commented that the Commission handled things really well such as
the topic of exterior design materials and it being best to have staff work on those details as it should
be the developer/architect that comes forward with design elements, and that overall, it was a good
meeting.

As there was no additional business to come before the Commission, COMMISSIONER SHALE
MOVED THE MEETING ADJOURN. COMMISSIONER SANDAU SECONDED THE MOTION. ALL VOTING
AYE.
