B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-10-02 HAUC Annual Plan 
Claudia made a motion to approve the HAUC Annual Plan. April seconded the motion. 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
Each year, HUD requires an annual plan to be submitted on behalf of the agency. The Annual 
Plan must include updates on what the agency will be doing in the upcoming fiscal year. The 
updates are to include the addition or subtraction of any programs that are administered by the 
agency and are under HUD guidance and requirements. In addition, any administrative and 
operating functions that will be changing or have changed in the prior year. A narrative 
regarding the progress of the agency's 5-year plan is also required to be submitted as part of 
the Annual Plan. A public comment period was posted, and the plan was made available for 
anyone wishing to review the agency's annual plan or 5-year plan. Along with the availability of 
the annual and 5-year plans for public comment, the agency's administrative plan must also be 
made available for public comment. The plan will need to be submitted to HUD within 45 days 
of the end of the fiscal year for the agency.  This resolution grants signing authority to the 
Executive Director for any documents, forms, and the like related to the annual plan and 
submits the same.  
 
 
C. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-10-03 Audit Services 2025-2029  
Steve made a motion to approve the HAUC Audit Services 2025-2029. Suzanne 
seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
HAUC is required by HUD to complete annual audits for the agency and its programs.   The 
2024 HAUC Agency Audit was completed by HAUC’s contracted audit provider earlier this 
year.  The agency audit was approved by the HAUC Board of Commissioners in June 2025 
and the last audit of the existing contract.  As a result HAUC needs to secure another contract 
and vendor.    
HAUC commenced and issued a RFP in September/October 2025 for Audit Services covering 
all programs and three component units for years ending 2025, 2026 and 2027, with an option 
for HAUC to extend by way of two annual extensions at its sole discretion.   This resolution 
grants signing authorization to the Executive Director for any and all necessary documents 
relating to an engagement letter or equivalent.  
 
 
D. Discuss/ Approve Resolution 2025-10-04 Retirement Benefits 
April made a motion to approve the Retirement Benefits. Suzanne seconded the motion. 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
The Housing Authority of Utah County provides retirement accounts to all full-time employees, 
with both employees and HAUC making contributions to one or more individual accounts per 
company policy. These retirement benefits, offered by HAUC, serve as an important tool to 
attract and retain qualified staff.  
HAUC desires to review its retirement offering and is seeking assistance. HAUC desires to 
engage its existing benefits broker.  The broker will provide expertise and coordination 
throughout the evaluation and potential change over to another vendor should the evaluation 
and broker recommend a change.  
 
  
E. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2025-10-05 Asbestos Mitigation 
Suzanne made a motion to approve the Asbestos Mitigation. April seconded the motion. 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
  The Housing Authority of Utah County purchased a property in Orem, Utah, near the end of 
July 2025.  During the due diligence period before purchase, HAUC discovered that one of 
the three buildings had asbestos-containing materials on portions of the walls and ceilings.   
HAUC secured three sealed bids from qualified companies performing asbestos removal 
services.  This resolution authorizes the Executive Director to sign, complete, or provide any 
required documents to the recommended vendor, as well as Orem City, utility provider, or 
other entity necessary pertaining to the asbestos mitigation of the recently acquired property.
