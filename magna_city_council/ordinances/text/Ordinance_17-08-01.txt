AN ORDINANCE, AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED "ZONING" OF THE MAGNA
METRO TOWNSHIP CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2001, BY RECLASSIFYING CERTAIN
PROPERTY LOCATED IN MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP FROM THE A-1 z/c ZONE to the
R-1-4 z/c ZONE

        The Magna Metro Township Council of Salt Lake County, State of Utah, ordains as

follows:

        Section 1: Section, 19.06.020, The Zoning Map of Salt Lake County, Salt Lake County

Code of Ordinances 2001, is hereby amended, as follows:

        The property described in Application #30384 filed by Matthew Loveland and located at

approximately 7824 West 2820 South within Magna Metro Township of Salt Lake County, is

hereby reclassified from the A-1 z/c zone to the R-1-4 z/c zone, with z/c to no more than 6 lots

per acre, height limited to the lesser of 2 standard stories or 35 feet; and to include this property

as part of the existing development agreement with the adjacent Fieldstone project, said property

being described as follows:
