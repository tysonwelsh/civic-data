204   3.4) Public hearing and consideration of Roger Harding’s request for conditional use
205   approval of a 10-foot wall at 2693 North and 1200 East.
206
207   Katie Bussell presented the item. She clarified that an exception is being asked for, not a
208   conditional use. The wall will prevent noise and trespassing.
209
210   Craig Chambers explained the difference between a conditional use and an exception. Fences are
211   allowed to be six feet tall in city code, so the exception being asked for is an additional four feet
212   of height. The Planning Commission must decide for any fence above eight feet. Commissioners
213   Kunze and Everett asked staff if a fence was different than a sound barrier in code or if there is
214   specific code for sound barriers that should be applied. Kim Struthers explained that fences and
215   soundwalls are considered the same in code. Commissioner Jackson suggested that staff look
216   into putting sound barriers into code especially as the city continues to grow and get noiser.
217
218   Theresa Harding, the applicant, was present with the builder, Mike. She wants the wall to be
219   something beautiful that people can appreciate. The wall would keep out noise from cars
220   speeding through the ravine and would help keep lights from shining into the master bedroom
221   window. This is why the extra height is being requested. They are happy to pull the wall back
222   and add additional landscaping.
223
224   Commissioner Everett asked how often trespassers are an issue. Harding explained that people
225   come onto her property frequently and she worries about liability. Additionally, people
226   frequently dump garbage on the lot. Commissioner Everett asked why there were breaks in the
227   wall. Harding explained that there will be gates installed there. One is for the main driveway, and
228   the other is for a second drive. The wall and gates will be deep enough into the property that cars
229   will be able to safely turn around if they make a wrong turn.
230
231   Commissioner Jackson asked how far back from the curb the wall would be. It would be twenty
232   feet from the road. DRC asked for it to be twelve feet back from the street (five feet for the
233   sidewalk and seven feet for landscaping), but Harding preferred the wider landscaping strip that
234   was suggested by the Parks Department.
235
236   Commissioner Kunze asked if the wall would be sufficient to keep out light. Harding explained
237   that they would likely need to go with a twelve foot wall to block out light entirely, but she only
238   wants a ten foot wall for aesthetic purposes. She is planning on planting trees on both sides to
239   help block out the rest of the light. Commissioner Lockhart asked clarifying questions about the
      Lehi City Planning Commission                     5                                      June 12, 2025

246   Commissioner Lockhart asked about sidewalk installation. The Hardings are not developing the
247   land, so no sidewalk will be put in. Commissioner Everett asked if the applicants would be
