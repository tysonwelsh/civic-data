2. Highlands Landing Residential; 5490 West Highlands Loop Road; Preliminary
Subdivision Plat (46 lots on 7.26 acres); R-3-20 Zone; Peterson Development Co.,
LLC/Victor Barnes (applicant) [#29800; parcel 20-25-351-014]

Kaylen Nichols, Peterson Development, said the proposed layout is generally the same as the plan
shown to the Commission at the land use map amendment except that the access points were
redesigned to meet code for spacing. Lot sizes range from 4,000 square feet to 6,500 square feet. The
shared access, driveway, and the main road system are private. The plat has 46 single-family lots and
home sizes are between 1,500 to 2,400 square feet, which offers a more affordable product. She
clarified that the easement between lots 114 and 115 is for emergency access. The fire department will
have a key that unlocks the bollards to be placed close to the end of the drive so people will not use it
for parking.
Kent Shelton commented that he did not see any guest parking.
Kaylen Nicols said every home will have a 2-car garage and the plan is to push every driveway to the
22-foot mark to accommodate two additional parking spots. The majority of homes will also have
enough width for a 3“ car garage or RV pad. She clarified that the alley to the driveways will be
shared, but no parking will be allowed in the alley. Each driveway to the home will allow for two
vehicles outside of the garage. Street parking will be allowed.
Larry Gardner said the property previously had the Senior Housing Overlay to accommodate a senior
apartment building. However, the overlay was removed, and the current proposal is for 46 single-
family lots available to all age groups. All traffic into this subdivision will come from 7800 South or
Highlands Loop Road. The project is well under the 20 units per acre allowed with the R-3-20 zoning

The private driveways meet fire code for length and width requirements. Private roads must be

approved by the Planning Commission. The code requires two parking spaces, which are provided with

the garage, and the driveway allows for two additional parking spaces. Driveways will be at a right
angle to the alley. There is a mix of lot sizes, which at least meet the minimum of 4,000 square feet. He

detention basin. Garbage pickup on a private street is paid for by the HOA. He reviewed the proposed
landscaping on 5490 West, Highlands Loop Road, the private road, and the detention pond, which will
all be maintained by the HOA. A masonry wall is required along 5490 West and Highlands Loop

Road. Vinyl fencing will be along the private Street A. The Planning Commission could require a

different fencing, but he felt that the proposal meets the minimum standard of the ordinance. He

reviewed the conditions of approval. In the staff report under Criteria C, the utilities department has
determined that there is adequate water and sewer available and adequate storm drain capacity for this
project. The availability of water and sewer are generally determined by zoning, but there are many
reviews prior to this meeting where those capacities are reviewed in order to determine adequate
availability. Those issues were also addressed at the time of the zone change.

Based on the analysis and findings contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning

Commission approve the Preliminary Major Subdivision Plat for Highlands Landing Residential

Subdivision a 46-lot single family residential subdivision located in the R-3-20 Zone on approximately

7.26 acres at 5490 West Highlands Loop Road, with the conditions and requirements of approval

listed.
