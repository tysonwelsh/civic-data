windows. Staff answered that its location makes it so it does not have a glass requirement.
Commissioner Carlson asked a question about sharing an access with the southern parcel. Ross
answered that staff has reviewed the spacing of the accesses and that they work and that they
have cross access on the southeast side of the property.

    Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to approve WPA Architecture’s request for site plan
    approval of the F-Matic Warehouse remodel and addition of an existing building located at

2.6 Public hearing and recommendation of Matthew Jorgensen’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of Carla’s Cove, a 13-lot residential development located at 2402 West
Main Street.

Jacob Curtis presented information on this item.

Commissioner Everett asked about the DRC comment for a pedestrian connectivity on to Main
Street. Kim Struthers clarified it would just be a sidewalk connection and gate. Commissioner
Nielsen asked about the sidewalk and park strip dimensions. Kim Struthers clarified that this is a
private road, and they are proposing to not meet the public street standard for park strips and
sidewalk. Commissioner Eyre asked questions about the City Council approval of it. Kim
Struthers explained the compromise the City Council made to allow for shared driveways and
other things. Commissioner Carlson asked about parking. It was clarified that there are no on
street parking spots, but there are four guest parking spots provided. There will also be parking
on 70 north. “No Parking” signs will be located on the side of the road.

The applicant, Matthew Jorgensen, was present. Matthew explained in order help with parking
they provided 20 foot driveways. The lots are wide enough for a three car garage. They also
added additional parking spots. They will have no parking signs and an HOA tow-away zone if
people do park in the private shared driveway.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve to the City Council for Matthew
    Jorgensen’s request for preliminary subdivision review of Carla’s Cove, a 13-lot
    residential development located at 2402 West Main Street; with the findings that the
    proposed subdivision complies with the City Council’s approval of the Concept plan, meets
    the requirements of the Lehi City Development Code in regards to subdivision approvals.
    Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, no; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
    Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed 5 – 7.

2.14 Consideration of Atlas Towers request for approval of the Ignite Stealth Tower
located at 1650 West Traverse Terrace Drive with a proposed stealth clock cell tower.
Jacob Curtis presented information on this item.

The applicant, Mike Powers, was present. The applicant answered questions about a previous
tower needing to place a satellite on the outside of the stealth facility. The applicant stated it will
not happen again, and that this site will not have any gadgets visible outside of the stealth cell
tower facility. The applicant explained their justification for the cell tower height. Commissioner
Eyre asked if it will be built at ground level on or the hill. The applicant stated it will be at the
same elevation as the parking lot. Commissioner Ellis asked if other wireless companies would
locate on this cell tower. The applicant stated that yes, they are designing it for four different
antennas. Commissioner Carlson asked what color it will be painted to blend into the
surrounding area. The applicant stated that they do not really care what color, they are happy to
use whatever colors Staff and the Commission requires. Commissioner Carlson asked about the
height of the tower compared to the homes on the hill. The applicant stated that the tower will be
below the homes. Jacob measured the elevation on google earth. The elevation change was about
70 feet from the parking lot to the base of the homes. Commissioner Bunker asked about the
DRC comment regarding removing the storage container. Kim Struthers clarified staff
recommended including that in the motion.

    Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to approve Atlas Towers request for approval of the
    Ignite Stealth Cell Tower located at 1650 West Traverse Terrace Drive with a proposed
    stealth clock cell tower; including all DRC comments. With the findings that the
    proposed tower meets the requirements of a stealth facility and has reasonable height in
    the context of the existing neighborhood, the proposed use will be located and conducted
    in a manner in compliance with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and
    the purposes of this Code, the property on which the use, building, or other structure is
    proposed to be established is of adequate size and dimensions to permit construction of
    the facilities and the conduct of the use in such a manner that it will not be detrimental
    to adjoining properties and the area. Commissioner Newall seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
    Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
