                         Consideration - Jason Lethbridge, City Planning Manager

SUMMARY:

The Planning Commission, following a public hearing, voted to recommend DENIAL of this rezone
application. Minutes from that meeting are included below. Following the Commission’s action,
there was neighborhood and property owner input to staff and the Councilmember over the district
that indicated further consideration by the Commission may be warranted. This action is limited to a
decision to remand the application back to the Commission for review. No discussion, public input,
or action on the rezone itself will be taken. If the Council votes to remand the item back to the
Commission, it will be noticed for a public hearing with that body. If the Council choose not to
remand this item, it will be noticed for a future public hearing with the City Council.
