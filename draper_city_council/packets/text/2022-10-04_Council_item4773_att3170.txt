                                       RESOLUTION NO. 22-55

        A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF DRAPER CITY TO
        APPOINT LISA GARNER AS THE DRAPER CITY JUSTICE COURT JUDGE

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 78A-7-202 outlines the procedures for Draper City to follow
in appointing a justice court judge; and

        WHEREAS, Draper City has followed the procedures as outlined in the Utah Code; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 78A-7-202 directs the Mayor to appoint a justice court judge
upon ratification of the nominee by the City Council; and

        WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Lisa Garner as Justice Court Judge for the City of
Draper in accordance with the appointment procedures provided by law; and

       WHEREAS, Lisa Garner has the qualifications, credentials, experience, and professionalism
necessary to serve as the Draper City Justice Court Judge.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Ratification. The Draper City Council ratifies the appointment of Lisa Garner as
Draper City Justice Court Judge.

        Section 2. Appointment. The Mayor hereby appoints Lisa Garner as Draper City Justice Court
Judge in accordance with appointment procedures provided by law.

        Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution,
and all provisions, clauses and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 4th DAY OF OCTOBER 2022.
