                                   Single-family Housing
                                   Modifications (I-ADU’s) - HB 82
                                   • Allows internal accessory dwelling units (I-ADU’s) to be
                                     created in single-family dwellings within the existing
                                     footprint.
                     DRAPER CITY
C O MMUNI T Y DEVEL O PMENT




                                   • Allowed as a permitted use in any area zoned primarily
                                     for residential use
                                   • The intent of the State law is to create additional long-
                                     term rentals (longer than 30 days)
                                   • The primary dwelling is required to be owner occupied
                                   • Cities are limited in what regulations they may place on
                                     I-ADU’s
                                   • HOA’s are prohibited from restricting I-ADU’s.
                                   Draper City – Current Regulations
                                   • New I-ADU’s are not allowed
                                   • Detached accessory dwellings (D-ADU’s) are allowed
                     DRAPER CITY
C O MMUNI T Y DEVEL O PMENT




                                     • Only one (1) D-ADU allowed per lot
                                     • Same setbacks and height limits as single-family dwelling
                                     • Same architectural design, color and materials as single-family
                                       dwelling
                                     • Not greater than 50% of the single-family dwelling size
                                     • A minimum of one (1) additional parking space is required
                                   • Non-conforming I-ADU’s exist in some neighborhoods
                                     (i.e. South Mountain, Suncrest, Inauguration) due to
                                     allowance by Development Agreements and prior city
                                     code (1990 -1999).
                                   Possible Approaches
                                   HB 82 only requires that Draper City allow for I-ADU’s as a permitted
                                   use in residential zones, however the city is given some discretion in
                                   how it regulates or enforces the State mandate.
                     DRAPER CITY
C O MMUNI T Y DEVEL O PMENT




                                   Some possible approaches are:
                                   1.   Wait and see.
                                   2.   Provide minimum or basic regulations for I-ADU’s.
                                   3.   Enact moderate regulations for I-ADU’s, including some basic
                                        rental regulations.
                                   4.   Enact all allowed regulations for I-ADU’s, update standards for
                                        D-ADU’s, create rental regulations for both long and short term
                                        rentals.
                                   1. Wait and See
                                   • I-ADU’s would be limited to regulations within State Law
                                      • Owner occupancy of primary dwelling would be required

                                      • Required to comply with all building, health and fire codes
                                      • Allowed in all residential zones, with no lot size restrictions
                                      • Allowed in mobile homes
                                      • Allowed in dwellings with failing septic tanks
                                      • I-ADU’s could install separate utility meters and request separate addressing

                                   • Limits ability for enforcement now, and in the future
                                   • Possibility of some properties having both an I-ADU and
                                     a D-ADU at the same time.
