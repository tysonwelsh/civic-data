     3.       5Z22 - Public Hearing and Recommendation to the City Council for a Zoning Map Amendment for
              the Properties Located at 5465; 5433; and 5439 South 1300 West, Taylorsville, Utah, from Single-
              Family Residential R-1-20 to Single-Family Residential R-1-10. (Jim Spung/AICP/Senior Planner)

3.1       Mr. Spung presented this item.             Mr. Scott Urie, the property owner, is requesting a Zoning Map
          Amendment for his property at 5465 South 1300 West. This property, as well as the two properties
          immediately north (5433 and 5439 South 1300 West) are in the R-1-20 zoning district; however, each of the
          three lots is less than 20,000 square feet in area. This zone change is intended to correct this discrepancy
          between the existing zoning designation and the built condition.

          3.1.1     These lots were created under Salt Lake County’s jurisdiction under a prior zoning code which has
                    since been amended, resulting in the creation of legal nonconforming conditions. A “legal
                    nonconforming lot” is a lot that does not meet the minimum lot area and/or dimensional standards
                    in the zoning district in which it is located but complied with the standards at the time the lot was
                    created.

          3.1.2     He displayed a zoning map from 2008 and Taylorsville was incorporated in 1996 from former Salt
                    Lake County unincorporated land. The 2008 version was the only available one to him at the time.
                    On that map the three properties were zoned RM, which is residential multi-family and in 2012
                    there was a City-wide rezone with the adoption of the updated Land Development Code that is
                    currently in use in Taylorsville. As a result of that these three properties were rezoned from RM
                    to R-1-20, which is the current zoning designation. The properties are all single-family residences.
                    The General Plan designation for these properties is low density residential, which is in line with
                    the current built condition and well as the current zoning of R-1-20. The General Plan designation
                    limits residential developments to less than 6 dwelling units per acre or less.

          3.1.3     The requested zone change would essentially change some of the rules for development on those
                    three properties, i.e., minimum lot width and size. The minimum lot width would be reduced from
                    90’ to 80’ and the minimum lot size would be 20,000 square feet reduced down to 10,000 square
                    feet.       Presently all three lots are below the 20,000 square foot area threshold which makes
                    them legal nonconforming lots. Two of the properties currently comply with the allowable width
                    and one is within one foot of being so. The R-1-10 would insure all three lots are in compliance.
                    In addition, the setbacks would change for those three properties. The difference would be the
                    side yard setbacks would go from a minimum of 7’ to a minimum of 5’ and then the combined

                   setbacks for both side yards together would be reduced from 18’ to 16’. The rear setback would
                   be reduced from 30’ to 15’. The rear setback for structures attached to the house would be

3.2      FINDINGS:
         3.2.1  This application was initiated by Scott Urie and the City of Taylorsville.
         3.2.2  This application is requesting a Zoning Map amendment to rezone the properties at 5465, 5433,
                and 5439 South 1300 West (parcel IDs: 21152270030000; 21152270040000; and
                21152270050000) from R-1-20 to R-1-10.
         3.2.3  The subject properties are within the “Low Density Residential” designation on the City’s General
                Plan Proposed Land Use Map.
         3.2.4  The proposed zoning Map amendment is consistent with the adopted General Plan.
         3.2.5  The subject properties do not comply with the minimum lot area requirements of the R-1-20
                zoning district.
         3.2.6  The R-1-10 zoning district standards more closely align with the built condition of the subject
                properties than the R-1-20 zoning district.
         3.2.7  The proposed zoning change would result in reduced minimum lot area and side and rear yard
                setback requirements for the subject properties, in accordance with the R-1-10 zoning standards
                in the Taylorsville Land Development Code.
         3.2.8  Staff recommends the Planning Commission forwards a positive recommendation to the City
                Council for the proposed zone change.
         3.2.9  The City Council is the decision-making authority for a zoning Map amendment request and may
                adopt or reject the amendment as it deems appropriate pursuant to Section 13.05.030 and other
                applicable sections of the Taylorsville Land Development Code.

3.3      STAFF RECOMMENDATION: That the Planning Commission sends a positive recommendation to the City
         Council to amend the Zoning Map for the properties at 5465; 5433; and 5439 South 1300 West from R-1-
         20 to R-1-10, based on the Findings as outlined previously. Commissioner Wilkey asked Mr. Spung to again
         display the slide showing the square footage for the       R-1-10 versus the R-1-20. She asked when they
         look at the minimum width for the lot in square feet should they meet both area size and width or is it one
         or the other. Mr. Spung said it should be both.

3.4      APPLICANT ADDRESS: Scott Urie – 5465 South 1300 West. Mr. Urie said he is looking to add on to the
         existing residence by putting in a two-car garage but as it stands now there is not enough room for that to
         happen. That it would need to go on the south side of the lot, and the setback would need to be changed
         in order to do that.
