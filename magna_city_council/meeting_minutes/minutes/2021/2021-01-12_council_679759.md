                                             Staff Meeting

Electronic Board Meeting Determination

                 Greg Schulz read the following statement regarding the electronic meeting
determination:

               Consistent with provisions of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, specifically
Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(4), and acting in my capacity as the Mayor of the Magna Metro
Township (“Magna”), I hereby make the following written determinations in support of my decision
to hold and convene electronic meetings of the Magna Metro Township Council without a physical
anchor location:Dan

1.     Conducting Council Meetings with an anchor location that is physically accessible for
members of the public to attend in person presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of
those who may be present at the anchor location.

2.     This determination is based upon the following:

       a.     Pursuant to the Public Health Orders issued by the State of Utah and Salt Lake
       County, the Magna Metro Township remains under a state of public health emergency
       related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, and significant person-to-person
       transmission of the virus continues to occur in Magna; and

       b.        All other facts noted in the August 7, 2020 Letter of Determination are still in effect.

Council Business

Resolution No. 21-01-01

             Paul Ashton reviewed the following resolution authorizing an amendment to the
agreement with Government Consultants Inc., for Greg Schulz’s administrative services. The
amendment is to increase the amount for the services.

                 Council Member Peel stated he has not seen the resolution and was not prepared
to vote on it.

              Mr. Ashton stated the Council approved the increase when it adopted the budget.
It was a market-driven increase, based on what city managers were making.

             Council Member Peel stated he was absent for the budget meeting, so had not
known the Council approved a raise.

                 Council Member Prokopis asked what the amount of the increase was.

               Greg Schulz stated his services went from $5,100 a month to $6,000, a $900
increase. The $6,000 a month is still below market, but the idea is to increase it slowly and
incrementally over time to at least get to the bottom line of the market.

                 Council Member Prokopis asked what the term of the contract was.

               Mr. Ashton stated it is three years. The Council can wait until the next meeting to
vote on this. The raise would be retroactive anyway to the date of the contract.

               Council Member Peel stated if the rest of the Council is comfortable with this,
since it passed it in the budget meeting, then he was okay.

RESOLUTION NO. 21-01-01                                                Date: January 12, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       THE THIRD AMENDED CONTRACT BETWEEN MAGNA AND GOVERNMENT
       CONSULTANTS LLC TO PERFORM MUNICIPAL ADMINISTRATION WORK
       FOR MAGNA

                                         RECITALS

A.     The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
401 et seq.

B.    The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

C.    Government Consultants LLC (the COMPANY) is the contracted service provider to
Manga for Municipal Administration.

D.      During the creation of the 2020 Magna Budget, the Council decided an increase in the
monthly pay to Company was desired to reflect their appreciation for the company’s work and to
begin transitioning the company contract to market.

E.     To affect the new compensation rate requires amending the contact with the Company.

F.     The attached amended contract (Attachment “A”) reflects those changes.

                                       RESOLUTION

THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby approves the amended contract between
Magna and Government Consultants LLC, and authorizes the Mayor to sign the contract to affect
the same.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this 12 day of January, 2021.

Council Reports

Unified Police Department (UPD) and Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area
(SLVLESA)

                Council Member Prokopis stated the UPD Board met last month and approved
an approximate $600 bonus for all of its employees for their efforts with COVID. Other agencies
in public safety got a bigger incentive. It is also looking to increase salaries going forward to get
closer to market. The UPD is still trying to adjust to the separation of Taylorsville, which will
happen July 1, 2021. As a result, SLVLESA will have to ask for a tax increase so it has the funds
it needs. That will require the Legislature approving legislation that will allow SLVLESA to raise
the cap on what it can levy.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Inland Port Authority

                  Council Member Prokopis stated the Port Authority Board is meeting quarterly.
Last fall, its strategic business plan went out. Now, other counties outside of Salt Lake County
are trying to get in on the satellite ports, etc.

               Council Member Hull asked if protestors were still going to the meetings.

              Council Member Prokopis stated at the last meeting, 127 people commented.
Their number one concern is the environment and the pollution the Inland Port will generate.

              Council Member Peel asked if there were some options that would eliminate the
environmental effect.

               Council Member Prokopis stated there are options that would minimize the
environmental impact, such as Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design (LEED) certified
buildings and electric vehicles, but the Port Authority Board is not at the point where it can make
those kinds of decisions. It is working on a 20-30 year plan.
