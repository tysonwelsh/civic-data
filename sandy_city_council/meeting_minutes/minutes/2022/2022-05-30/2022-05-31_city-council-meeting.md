Citizen Comments


1.         22-189                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to ecomment on this item

                                  Council Chair Marci Houseman invited the public to participate in General Citizen 
                                  Comment.

                                  Mr. Paul Pratt asked that Citizen Comment time period be moved back to 6:00 pm. He 
                                  expressed concerns with the cost of the Alta Canyon Recreation Center options for 
                                  remodel or new build. He also spoke about the "wow" factor of Sandy City employees 
                                  and the excellent services provided and he wants to take care of what we have rather than 
                                  building a new recreation center. 

                                  Ms. Dawn Sidwell spoke about code enforcement issues concerning a resident in her 
                                  neighborhood who has junk vehicles on his property. She shared communications she 
                                  had with the city code enforcers. She wants more responsiveness. 

                                  Ms. Rebecca Colly spoke about those residents who will not use Alta Canyon Recreation 
                                  Center and the associated costs of options B and D with respect to the ACRC. She 
                                  spoke about inflation, supply chain issues, food shortages, a predicted recession and 
                                  other concerns. 

                                  Ms. Pat Jones thanked the Council and staff for providing a history of the ACRC. She 
                                  spoke about the costs to remodel or new build of the recreation center. She asked the 
                                  Council to consider the comments of those who do not support the new recreation center.

                                  Ms. Dea Theodore spoke about the new Bell Canyon Preservation Trailhead and 
                                  acknowledged and thanked those who worked on this project. She offered comments 
                                  regarding the ACRC and the Special Services District.

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren spoke very highly of the newly remodeled Council Chambers in 
                                  West Jordan.

                                  Public Comment closed.

2.         22-188                 Council Member Robinson requesting that the council continue discussing 
                                  the Alta Canyon Recreation Center, specifically the future of the Special 
                                  Service District and scenarios for funding a rebuild and/or remodel of the 
                                  Center
                     Sponsors: Robinson

                   Attachments:   Robinson Memorandum Alta Canyon 5-17-22.pdf

                                  Council Member Robinson introduced the item and requested the Council have a 
                                  thorough discussion on the Alta Canyon Recreation Center options, funding, and special 
                                  services district. The discussion included the goals for the Alta Canyon Recreation 
                                  Center, the impact on the community, and the financial impact and funding sources for 
                                  the proposed options. 

                                  The Council requested the following information from staff: a breakdown of the utilization 
                                  of the recreation center by residents and non-residents, plot map of survey responders, 
                                  day use of the center, modeling of predicted use, research information regarding special 
                                  revenue fund process and the special services district and city-wide impact for parks 
                                  funding. Council Member Earl requested information from a prior year demographic study 
                                  conducted in approximately 2018. Brian Kelly reviewed the process for creating a special 
                                  revenue fund. Cliff Strachan, CAO, mentioned that Administration will be providing the 
                                  Council information regarding the special revenue fund process. Council will hear 
                                  presentations from Administration at the June 14th meeting regarding the overall City 
                                  building replacement projects under consideration.

                                  Council convened a five minute recess at approximately 7:00 pm.
                                  Council meeting reconvened.

3.         22-178                 Council Office requesting direction regarding the proposal to remodel and 
                                  update the City Council Chambers, Overflow, and Conference Room
                   Attachments:   Proposal - Sandy City Council Chambers Updates 4.14.22.pdf
                                  Staff Priority Ranking May 24.pdf

                                  Council discussed the proposed remodel for the Council Chambers. Dustin Fratto 
                                  provided a summary of the remodel costs and requests and discussed the need to 
                                  update the technology interface. Council discussed the chamber and technology 
                                  upgrade. Staff requested direction on moving forward. 

                                  A motion was made by Marci Houseman, seconded by Zach Robinson to consider 
                                  the proposal as presented this evening and to not gather additional bids for the 
                                  chamber remodel project...The motion failed by the following vote:

                                  Yes:   3    Alison Stroud
                                                  Zach Robinson
                                                  Marci Houseman

                                  No:    4    Cyndi Sharkey
                                                  Ryan Mecham
                                                  Brooke D'Sousa
                                                  Scott Earl

                                  Council discussion followed the vote on the motion. Administration will provide an 
                                  overview to the Council of the Sandy City Building Replacement Study and 
                                  recommendations at the June 14th Council meeting. Council decided not to move forward 
                                  with the council chamber remodel at this point in time.




4.         22-173                 Council Member Houseman requesting that the council discuss council 
                                  attendance at town halls and other extra curricular events
                                  Council Member Marci Houseman requested a discussion regarding the council policy 
                                  attendance at town hall events and the impact on council staff when a quorum is present 
                                  at these town hall events. Council discussion included the importance of being present at 
                                  city events and town halls which help Council members gain a greater understanding of 
                                  the issues facing the city. The Council attorney provided some additional information 
                                  relative to the Open and Public Meetings Act. Council staff requested direction regarding 
                                  the Council attendance policy.
                                  A motion was made by Marci Houseman, seconded by Brooke D'Sousa directing 
                                  Council staff to bring back options for Council attendance at City meetings and 
                                  Town Hall events...The motion carried by the following vote:

                          Yes:     5 -  Alison Stroud
                                        Marci Houseman
                                        Cyndi Sharkey
                                        Brooke D'Sousa
                                        Scott Earl
                           No:     2 -  Zach Robinson
                                        Ryan Mecham

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Council Executive Director, briefed the Council on upcoming agenda items.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged and thanked the first responders in 
                      Sandy City. Our city has experienced some very difficult and traumatic events over the 
                      last few weeks and she spoke about mental health resources available for first 
                      responders.

Mayor's Report

                      The Mayor's report was given by Cliff Strachan, CAO.

CAO Report

                      Cliff Strachan, CAO, mentioned we are in the last week of bulk waste clean up. He thinks 
                      the program was a success. This Friday is an employee luncheon and barbecue. The 
                      Emergency Town Hall event originally scheduled for June is cancelled.

                      On behalf of Mayor Zoltanski, he acknowledged all city staff who worked on the Bell 
                      Canyon Preservation Trail Head project and he expressed how proud the Mayor was on 
                      this accomplishment. He also mentioned that a new task force has been created at the 
                      request of the Mayor, which will focus on the speeding issues in Sandy City -  Slow Down 
                      Sandy.

Adjournment
                      Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 9:14 pm.
