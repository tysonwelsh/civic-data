MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
Date: 2024-05-09
Re: Public Hearing: Urbana - Land Use Map Amendment, Zoning Map Amendment, and
Development Agreement Requests (Legislative Actions)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Land Use Map Amendment, Zoning Map
Amendment and Development Agreement for approximately 3.82 acres located on the
west side of Lone Peak Parkway, at approximately 285 W. River Chapel Road (Exhibits C
and D). The property is currently zoned A2. The applicant is requesting that the
applications be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site with
30 single-family lots.



The property has historically been used for agricultural, but has been vacant since the
mid-2000’s. In 2021, an application was submitted to rezone the property to RM2 for the
development of residential townhomes. That application was denied by the City Council.
A separate application was submitted in early 2023 for a different residential townhome
project, but that application was withdrawn before there was any action on the request.
