#5 Public Hearing (6:05 PM or soon thereafter) – Subdivision Code Amendment 
 
Crane reviewed the information for the proposed Subdivision Code Amendment. 
 
Commissioners and Staff discussed the type of subdivisions that this would apply to, requiring 
subdivisions to try and hook into municipal services from the closest municipality, requiring a drilled 
well for subdivisions. 
 
06:19:00 
 
Sands motioned to open the public hearing for the Subdivision Code Amendment; Watterson seconded; 
Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Jason Watterson, Brady Christensen, Kurt Bankhead, Nate Dauges, Chris Sands, Morris Poole 
Nays: 0 
 
Nate Player commented on how this would be limiting for big parcels, how most cities will not extend 
services before annexation, and concerns requiring wells before development. 
 
Gunnell commented on how the lot is more profitable with a pre-drilled well. 
 
Christensen commented on protecting the public. 
 
Mr. Player commented with concerns on drilling wells. 
 
06:29:00 
 
Christensen motioned to close the public hearing; Daugs seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Jason Watterson, Brady Christensen, Kurt Bankhead, Nate Daugs, Chris Sands, Morris Poole 
Nays: 0 
 
06:58:00 
 
Commissioners and Staff discussed concerns with the limiting of property due to urban wildland 
interface for fire. 
 
Goodlander explained on the increased fire costs are being handled. 
 
Commissioners and Staff discussed how public water systems would affect requiring a public sewer 
system on subdivision applications of more than 7 lots,  
 
Christensen motioned to recommend approval to the County Council with the suggested modifications to

be allowed based on the requirements of the Bear River Health Department, and Exhibit C4 to increase 
that number from 3 lots to 7 lots; Poole seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Jason Watteron, Brady Christensen, Kurt Bankhead, Nate Daugs, Chris Sands, Morris Poole 
Nays: 0

#6 Blacksmith Hyrum Fork Irrigation Company Conditional Use Permit 
 
Smith reviewed the staff report for the Blacksmith Hyrum Fork Irrigation Company Conditional Use 
Permit (CUP). 
 
Brandon Parker commented on wanting to pipe the existing canal. 
 
Sands asked if this was for water conservation. 
 
Mr. Parker responded yes. 
 
Poole asked if ownership of the land is defined well, 
 
Mr. Parker responded yes, it’s all recorded. 
 
Watterson motioned to approve the Black Smith Fork Irrigation Company Conditional Use Permit with 
the 10 conditions and 3 conclusions; Daugs seconded; Passed 6, 0. 
Ayes: Jason Watterson, Brady Christesen, Kurt Bankhead, Nate Daugs, Chris Sands, Morris Poole 
Nays: 0 
 
Kohn Smith commented against the CUP due to a study conducted by USU that stated piping the canal 
would cause water loss, and the negative impacts that would come from piping the canal. 
 
Gary Smith commented on concerns with setting a precedence with approval of this project. 
 
Mr. Players commented against this because it goes right through his mother’s property, the land being 
owned by the property owner and not the canal, the loss of trees due to the piping of the canal, the springs 
in the area, 
 
Mr. Parker responded there would be a French drain system. 
 
Floyd Burnett commented 2 years ago at the annual meeting there was an explanation of what was going 
to happen and there have been several others and the water users were invited to those meetings. 
 
Sands explained the legal precedence of approving CUPs.
