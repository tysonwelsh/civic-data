CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2613, A RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING FUNDING FOR A_ TRANSPORTATION STUDY FOR ZION
PUBLIC FINANCE

Commissioner Cox explained that there has been an increase in traffic traveling to visit
Zion National Park. Due to this increase, the County is considering the possibility of
providing public transportation from within the County to the Park. A public transit system
would greatly decrease the number of vehicles traveling to and through the park on a daily
basis, as well as decrease the parking demand. A transportation study is needed to
determine if a public transit option would be feasible. Several groups including Kane
County, the Utah Outdoor Recreation Department, and UDOT have agreed to contribute
funds towards the completion of research in the amount of $5,000 each. The County would
also like to contribute to the study for Zion public transit options.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2613, A
Resolution Authorizes Funding for a Transportation Study for Zion
Public Finance. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.

FIRE PROHIBITIONS FOR WASHINGTON COUNTY

Washington County Fire Warden Adam Heyder explained that increased fire restrictions
began on June 1, 2020, limiting fires to established campgrounds in designated fire pits in
the unincorporated areas of the County. All municipalities put together fire restriction
guidelines by that were due by June 1, 2020, specifically in regards to firework restrictions
as a result of increased human caused fires over the past several weeks.

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Tom Kuhlmann explained that local firefighters have
responded to an average of four (4) to five (5) fires per day within the last month.
