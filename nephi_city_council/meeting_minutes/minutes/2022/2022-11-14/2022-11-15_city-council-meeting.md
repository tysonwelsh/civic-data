FY 2022 AUDIT REPORT PRESENTED BY JON HADERLIE FROM LARSON AND COMPANY:

   Mr. Jon Haderlie, representing Larson and Company, joined the meeting through Zoom and
presented to the Council and Mayor the certified annual audit report for the fiscal year ending June 30,
2022. He told of the audit procedures and their conclusions. He stated that the auditors were offering a
“clean” opinion. A clean opinion stated that there were no internal control or state compliance findings.
 He also stated that Larson and Company had performed a single audit on federal funds received and the
City was found to be in compliance with requirements applicable to each major program. Mr. Haderlie
thanked City Recorder Lisa Brough, City Administrator Seth Atkinson and staff for the smooth audit
process.

FRAUD RISK ASSESSMENT PRESENTED:

  City Recorder Lisa Brough reviewed the Fraud Risk Assessment for fiscal year ending June 30, 2022.
The assessment is required to be uploaded to the State Auditor’s website. The assessment indicated
that Nephi City has a moderate fraud risk score. The city does not have a formal internal audit function
which accounts for several points in the scoring.

PLANNING COMMISSION MEMBER AND ALTERNATE RATIFIED BY COUNCIL:

   Councilor Skip Worwood moved to ratify the appointment of Heather Robertson as a voting member
of the Planning Commission and John Ford as an alternate member of the Planning Commission.
Councilor Parady seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Ms. Robertson has been an
alternate member of the commission for the past couple of years and is replacing Kent Heap who
resigned from the Planning Commission due to employment obligations.

CEDAR RIDGE PLAT B AMENDED FINAL PLAT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that the Cedar Ridge Plat B Subdivision plat needed to be
amended to include some additional property that the developer had obtained. This additional property
will allow the developer to have the size of residential buildings he desires and meet the setbacks the
city requires of thirty (30) feet on the north boundary since that is an exterior PUD boundary. Two of
the original six lots will be adjusted with additional ground being added to their north borders.
   Councilor Worwood moved to approve Cedar Ridge Plat B amended final plat. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

WARBURTON PROPERTY AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that several months ago the City Council approved an update to the
city code for exemptions to the subdivision process. This mainly affected corner lots in the original
platted section of the city. These corner lots were allowed to have 3 lots instead of 2 exempted from
the subdivision process after the code was updated. One of the lots affected was on the corner of 400 E
and 300 N, just northeast of Nebo View Elementary School
   The owner of this lot wanted to subdivide the lot, however, there were some storm drainage and
sidewalk issues that needed to be resolved that would normally be resolved in the subdivision process.
Since this lot is exempted, these issues can be resolved through mutual agreement. Mr. Warburton had
agreed to make the sidewalk improvements on the corner piece with the existing home, the other
pieces will install their sidewalks as they develop. The storm water issues will also be resolved based on
the agreement.
   Councilor Memmott moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Tompkins Street, Inc (Warburton)
agreement. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

SCHOOL RESOURCE OFFICER OUT-OF-STATE TRAINING TRAVEL REQUEST APPROVED:

MASTER FEE RESOLUTION 11-15-2022 APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented a Master Fee Resolution 11-15-2022 that defined the fee schedule for the use
of The Hive Recreational Facility. The council discussed changing the fee schedule to allow free use of
the track only. Mr. Atkinson noted that the fees would also need to be approved by the Juab School
Board at their meeting the following evening. Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Master Fee
Resolution 11-15-2022 outlining The Hive fee schedule with the change that there is no charge for track
use. Councilor Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

OPT OUT OPTION FOR EMPLOYEE MEDICAL COVERAGE DISCUSSED:

  Mr. Atkinson discussed with the council the idea of offering a cash in-lieu option for any benefited
employee wanting to opt out of medical insurance coverage. He recommended $250.00 cash in-lieu or
$300.00 employer contribution to the employee’s 401K plan. The council directed staff to move forward
with the idea.
