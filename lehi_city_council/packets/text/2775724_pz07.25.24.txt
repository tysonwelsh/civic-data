87   3.3) Public hearing and recommendation of CORE Architecture’s request for review of an
88   amendment to the Skye Area Plan adding design requirements for Civic Land Uses.
89
90   Applicant Curtis Miner did a presentation to show why they are requesting to amend some of the
91   aspects of the Skye area plans and requirements for Civic Land uses.
     Lehi City Planning Commission                    2                                      July 25, 2024
 92
 93   Commissioners asked the applicant clarifying questions.
 94
 95   Chairman Jackson opened public hearing.
 96   Chairmen Jackson closed public hearing.
 97
 98   Commissioners asked several clarifying questions including questions about property ownership
 99   and distinctions between a temple and a potential chapel. Mr. Miner clarified that the church
100   owns both properties. The commissioners expressed concerns about the lack of differentiation in
101   the document between the temple and chapel standards, especially regarding lighting and site
102   plans suggesting adding language to distinguish the two. Kim Struthers proposed defining
103   standards by building size. Mr. Miner agreed, stating that they could separate elements to ensure
104   clarity.
105
106   Further discussions involved practical considerations for site planning, including tree planting
107   requirements, bike stalls, and lighting standards. Some commissioners expressed concerns about
108   customizing standards too much, which could exclude other civic uses. They also discussed bike
109   parking, noting the importance of encouraging biking despite current habits.
110
111   In summary, the commissioners are working to ensure clear, distinct standards for the temple and
112   chapel, while considering practical site planning needs and maintaining flexibility for other civic
113   uses.
114
115           Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to table the of CORE Architecture’s request for
116           review of an amendment to the Skye Area Plan adding design requirements for Civic
117           Land Uses; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report with the
118           following suggestions including that they retool some of the items discussed- changes
119           being made of two classifications of the two buildings and what applies to each building
120           i.e. square footage. They also suggest looking at lighting, bike parking, tree
121           requirement/water-wise landscaping, parking, and to formally add any request and any
122           other clarification that could be useful. Commissioner Lockhart seconded.
123
124           Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
125           Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Kunze, yes. The motion passed
126           unanimously.
