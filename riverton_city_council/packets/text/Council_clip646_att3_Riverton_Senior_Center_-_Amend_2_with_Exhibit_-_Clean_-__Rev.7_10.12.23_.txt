        THIS AMENDMENT No. 2 (“Amendment”), is entered into as of the __ day of
September 2023, by and between SALT LAKE COUNTY, a body corporate and politic of the
state of Utah (the “County”), and RIVERTON CITY, a Utah municipal corporation (the “City”).
County and City may be referred to jointly as the “Parties” and individually as a ”Party.”



                                          RECITALS:

   A. The Parties entered into an interlocal agreement (County # AE08498C) (“Agreement”),
      as of February 24, 2009, for the construction and operation of the Riverton Senior Center.

   B. The Parties executed Amendment No. 1 to the Agreement, on or about December 12,
      2011, which modified Sections 9(A) and 10(A) of the Agreement as to the duties and
      responsibilities of the Parties for the maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center through
      the adoption of Exhibit A attached to and incorporated into Amendment No. 1.

   C. The Parties now desire to revise the duties and responsibilities of the Parties as to the
      maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center.

                                 AGREEMENT AMENDMENT

   1. The Recitals stated above are incorporated herein and made a material part of the
Agreement by this reference.

    2. Section (10)(A) of the Agreement is amended to remove the County’s duties and
responsibilities related to maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center, as set forth in Amendment
1. County, by and through its Facilities Management Division, shall perform maintenance
responsibilities at the Senior Center only upon request by City.

    3. Section (9)(A) of the Agreement is amended to remove the County’s maintenance duties
and responsibilities set forth in Amendment 1. City shall fulfill the responsibilities contained in
Section (9)(A) as set forth in the Agreement. City shall also be responsible for all preventative
maintenance of the Riverton Senior Center facility and equipment, including electrical, HVAC,
plumbing, and security systems and equipment, and other required maintenance or repair. County
will transfer HVAC system control to the City. County will be permitted access to the City
system in case of emergencies.

     4. If County becomes aware of a maintenance concern that poses an immediate threat to the
health and safety of the Riverton Senior Center participants or staff, County will notify City
immediately of the needed repair(s) and City will contact County within the same day to initiate
the repair(s). If City does not initiate the necessary repair(s) within the same day, County will
arrange for its Division of Facilities Management to complete the required repair(s) and bill City
at the current rate charged by County.

    5. If County becomes aware of a maintenance concern that does not pose an immediate
threat to the health and safety of the Riverton Senior Center participants or staff, County will
notify City of the needed repair(s) and City will contact County to initiate action for the
necessary repair(s) within five (5) business days. If City does not initiate action for the necessary
repair(s) within five (5) business days, County will arrange for its Division of Facilities
Management to complete the required repair(s) and bill City at the current rate charged by
County.

    6. City shall provide a custodial worker at the Riverton Senior Center. Custodial duties
include those specified in the Custodial Cleaning Checklist (Exhibit A), assistance with tear-
down and clean-up following special event programming, and on-call response to emergency
cleaning requests. City will ensure that the custodial duties specified in Exhibit A are completed
in a timely manner, and that the building is clean at the end of each operating business day and
ready for use the following operating business day. AAS staff will communicate the dates and
times of special event programming to City in advance so that custodial assistance can be
scheduled following each event.
