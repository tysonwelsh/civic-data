Public Comments

                Lt. Van Emmerik, UPD, stated UPD Officers responded to a noise complaint at
an individual’s home that UPD has been dealing with for a while. The individual was not aware
that the police were at his home and thought it was someone else on his property and did come
out with a gun. The officers approached the individual and gave him commands at which time the
individual complied. There was some miscommunication and procedural issues during the
incident that is being addressed with the officers. Because officers witnessed the disturbance a
citation was able to be issued and that will put him into the court system. Lt. Van Emmerik
contacted the mental health unit to see if there is anything it can do to help this individual as there
may be underlying health issues. He is hopeful that between court system and the mental health
unit, something can be done to rectify the situation.

               Council Member Peel stated he does not want this situation to see the situation
escalate.

              Council Member Prokopis stated he is worried about retaliation against the
complainant and wants to make sure the Council is being an advocate for him.

Ivory Homes Payment

                Paul Ashton stated when Ivory Homes developed the CW Farms subdivision, a
piece of property was dedicated to Magna to be developed into a park. Since then, Dominion
Energy has requested that a small portion of the property be deeded to them so they can expand
their property and move forward with natural gas improvements. In addition, they asked for a
construction easement. The Council agreed and received payment from Dominion Energy. The
Council then agreed to take part of those funds and give them to Ivory Homes to add amenities
to the park beyond what was originally planned. Ivory Homes has sent an invoice for the cost of
the additional features.

              Council Member Peel asked if $40,000 was the limit that was set.

              Mayor Peay stated yes.

               Council Member Peel moved to approve the distribution of up to $40,000 to Ivory
Homes for upgraded park amenities. Council Member Prokopis made a friendly amendment to
also include a pickle ball court in the upgrades, if possible. Council Member Peel, seconded by
Council Member Hull, accepted the amendment. The motion passed unanimously.
