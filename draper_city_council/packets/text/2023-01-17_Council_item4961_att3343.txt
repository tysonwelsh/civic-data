                                   • Move definitions of terms out of the individual chapters and into the
                                     definitions section of ordinance
                                   • Add definitions for new or undefined terms to the definitions section

                                   • Incorporate Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District water efficiency
                                     standards and model ordinance provisions
                                   • Move site and building design standards to DCMC chapter

                                   • Move landscaping requirements from other chapters into
