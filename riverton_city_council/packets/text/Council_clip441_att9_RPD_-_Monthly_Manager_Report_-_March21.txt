                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                                 March

The most statistically significant information contained in this month’s report is the fact we wrote
more general offense reports (486) than we have during any month since our inception. This is a
42% increase over February and is largely a result of a continued increase in traffic related offenses,
as well as public order and public peace cases. We are continuing to see traffic issues related to the
closure of 12600 South at Bangerter Highway and our traffic units conducted several enforcement
operations, which resulted in a relatively high number of traffic citations and warnings.

Additionally, we observed a dramatic increase in public order and public peace cases, which are a
somewhat generic category of miscellaneous calls which encompass suspicious circumstances,
noise complaints, juvenile issues, and other types of calls not specifically identified elsewhere.

I believe we are also generally seeing more demands on law enforcement services as we emerge
from the self-imposed restrictions of the pandemic and people are resuming more normal patterns
of activity.
