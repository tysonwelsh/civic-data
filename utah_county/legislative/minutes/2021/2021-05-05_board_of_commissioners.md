‘I would like to nominate Jay Sundberg for Employee of the Month.

On Saturday, January 23, the County experienced a serious database hardware failure. By
Sunday, this failure had brought down many of the County’s most important systems that rely
on those databases. Jay led the recovery team in IT and worked through the night barely
leaving his office for more than 30 hours, recovering data and restoring systems one by

one. Largely because of Jay’s herculean efforts, many county employees had no idea of the
failure. By Wednesday, most of the major systems were back online without hardly a hiccup.

This recent heroic episode is just one of many instances over his 38 years of service where Jay’s
immense technical knowledge and experience were directly responsible for making Utah County
a better place to live, work and do business. In just the last few years Jay has made key
contributions to allow recorder’s office to receive digital recording, automate election results so
that our website reflects results in near real-time, and modernizing the County tax notice and
online payment processes, and so much more.

Jay has been the sentry that protects one of Utah County Government’s most important and
vulnerable assets — its data. Jay deserves to be recognized for this unheralded yet essential
role. He deserves to be Utah County’s Employee of the Month.’ ~Patrick Wawro, 1S Director
