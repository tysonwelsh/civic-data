RECOMMENDATION:
Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 21-48 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Accessory Dwelling Units and Appeals and Variance Hearing Officers Fees.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs.

The proposed fees for the Accessory Dwelling Units are new. The application fee for $450 is to cover costs
associated with the initial review and processing. The annual renewal fee is to cover the costs associated with
verifying and processing that the applicant is still in compliance.

The proposed fee for the Deviation Requests is to cover the costs associated with reviewing and processing the
request.

Only the items or sections that are proposed for changes are listed in Exhibit A. Items highlighted in blue are
the new proposed fees or description.
