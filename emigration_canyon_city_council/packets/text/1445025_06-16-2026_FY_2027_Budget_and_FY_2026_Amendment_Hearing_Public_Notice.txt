        EMIGRATION CANYON CITY COUNCIL
                 NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
 Pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3-818, notice is hereby given that a Public Hearing will be
 held at Unified Fire Authority Station 119, 5025 E Emigration Canyon Road, Salt Lake City,
  Utah 84108 during the City Council Meeting on Tuesday, June 16, 2026, at 7:00 pm MST.
  The purpose of the hearing is to discuss the City of Emigration Canyon’s Fiscal Year 2027
  Budget AND an amendment to the City of Emigration Canyon’s Fiscal Year 2026 Current
                                            Budget.
At the date and time fixed for the public hearing all who appear will be given an opportunity to
 express their views on the estimates of revenues and expenditures or any item in the proposed
  City’s FY 2027 Budget, as well as the proposed amendment to the FY 2026 Current Budget.
  Those who wish to attend and speak at the hearing may do so. A copy of the budget and the
proposed amendment will be available for inspection at least 7 days before the public hearing at
 the front counter of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District office located at 860 W.
                          Levoy Dr., Suite 300, Taylorsville, UT 84123.
 In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, the City of Emigration Canyon will
make reasonable accommodations to participate in the hearing. Requests for assistance can be
made by calling the City Recorder at 385-377-9466 within three working days of the meeting.
                               Dated this 6th day of June, 2026
