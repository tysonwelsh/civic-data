1.       Welcome



Mayor Overson welcomed the two residents in attendance at 6: 00 p. m. and explained that the
meeting was intended to be for open discussion in an informal setting. She invited those present
to discuss any topics they were interested in.

the construction going        on   at   Taylorsville Park.   She   pointed   out   that it affected 60+ senior
