   Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present. Mayor Nielson
explained that the council meeting had been scheduled to consider the approval of a beer, business, and
special event application from the Ute Stampede Committee for the Extreme Bull Riding Event to be
held at the fairgrounds on September 19, 2020. The Ute Stamped Committee was applying for a Class A
Beer License that would allow for the sale of beer in original containers for consumption off premise.
   Mayor Nielson said that a conference call meeting had been conducted with Commissioner Painter,
Commissioner Woodland, Sheriff Anderson, Anita from the Utah Department of Alcoholic Beverage
Control, Chief Morgan, Seth Atkinson and the beer distributor. Mayor Nielson said that the original idea
of a “beer garden” was not allowed by the state because of the COVID-19 restrictions. He said that if
the beer license is granted that certified servers would be selling the beer.
   Commissioner Woodland addressed the council and said that the Ute Stampede Committee had
given a great presentation to the commissioners and the commissioners had voted to allow the sale of
beer at the event. He stated that now that he has had more time to think, he has decided that he is
against the beer sales. He believes that the event can still be successful without beer sales.
    Councilor Memmott questioned the committee about if they had signed a facility lease agreement
with the county. He interpreted the code to mean that since the committee had leased the entire
fairgrounds, the “premise” was the entire fairgrounds and that the consumption of the beer would not
be off premise but would be on premise therefore he would be against approving the beer license.
    The Ute Stampede Committee interpreted the code as the building where the beer is sold to be the
“premise” and that the arena where the beer would be consumed would be off premise and would
comply with the Nephi City code.
    City Attorney Kasey Wright read the Utah State code’s definition of “premises” and said that he
legally could defend both interpretations of the code but thought that the committee’s interpretation
more aligned with the state code’s definition. He stated that the council is making an administrative
decision and if the application meets all the requirements of our code, then the council could not deny
the license.
    Mr. Jim Welburn commented that he thought the public should be more involved with this decision
and that hurried decisions aren’t always the best decisions.
     Chris Memmott and Kyle Garrett from the Ute Stampede Committee indicated that their desire is to
provide a good show every year. The said that the money earned from beer sales would aide in them
being able to provide the kind of rodeo that people want.
    Committee member Jonathan Webster said that when they filled out the lease agreement from the
county, they had to check the boxes of what facilities they wanted to rent at the fairgrounds. He
concluded that since each facility was rented separately that the agreement implied that each building
or venue was a separate premise.
    Councilor Seely clarified with staff that this was an administrative decision and not based on the
merits of beer sales at a community event. Mr. Atkinson verified that this was an administrative
decision and that the staff felt that the application met the city code requirements.

   Councilor Ostler stated that alcohol is being consumed at the events at the fairgrounds whether the
committee sells the beer or not. Councilor Ostler moved to approve the Class A beer license. Councilor
Seely seconded the motion. The vote was as follows:




                        Kent Jones              Nay
                        Larry Ostler            Yes
                        Nathan Memmott          Nay
                        Justin Seely            Yes

Mayor Nielson broke the tie with a “Yes” vote. The motion carried.


Councilor Jones moved to approve the business license application for the Ute Stampede Committee.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion the motion passed on a unanimous vote.


Councilor Jones moved to approve the special events application for the Ute Stampede Committee.
Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Ostler moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session to
discuss litigation, collective bargaining, and real property. Councilor Jones seconded the motion, which
passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
