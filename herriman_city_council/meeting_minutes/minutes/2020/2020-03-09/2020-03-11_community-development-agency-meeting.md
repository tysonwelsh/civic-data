                      SPECIAL COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND
                       RENEWAL AGENCY OF HERRIMAN CITY
                             WORK MEETING MINUTES
                                      Wednesday, March 11, 2020
                                       Approved July 28, 2020

The following are the minutes of the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City. The
meeting was held on Wednesday, March 11, 2020 at 5:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Council Chambers, 5355
West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in
City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Clint Smith

Directors Present:   Sherrie Ohrn, Steve Shields, and Chair David Watts (via teleconference)

Staff Present: City Manager Brett Wood, Assistant City Manager Tami Moody, Assistant City Manager Wendy
               Thomas, Community Development Director Blake Thomas, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder
               Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Alan Rae, Director of Operations Monte Johnson, Public Works
               Director Justun Edwards, Building Official Cathryn Nelson, City Attorney John Brems,
               Communications Manager Jonathan LaFollette, City Planner Michael Maloy, Assistant City Planner
               Bryn MacDonald, Deputy Police Chief Chad Reyes, Lt. Cody Stromberg, Lt. Brian Weidmer,
               Victims Advocate Christie Morgan, Economic Development Manager Heather Upshaw, and
               Landscape Architect Heidi Shegrud.

Directors Excused: Jared Henderson

5:00 PM:
   1. Call to Order
Director Steve Shields nominated Director Clint Smith to conduct the Community Development and Renewal
Agency meeting, Director Watts seconded the motion, and all voted aye. Vice Chair Smith called the meeting to
order at 5:08 p.m.

     2. Administrative Reports
            2.1. Discussion of a proposed Participation Agreement for the Herriman Innovation District
                 Project Area – Heather Upshaw, Economic Development Manager
Economic Development Manager Heather Upshaw offered an overview of the proposed participation agreement.
The participant, Herriman Industrial #1, developed a new leisure recreation product and equipment manufacturing
facility. The participant also constructed several infrastructure component and requested to be reimbursed through
the tax increment generated through the Community Development and Renewal Agency of Herriman City in the
amount of $1,280,441. Vice Chair Smith asked if other entities had approved this amendment. Manager Upshaw
responded in the negative, and added they are continuing discussions with the various entities regarding this
proposal.

    3. Adjournment
Director Shields moved to adjourn the Community and Development Renewal Agency meeting at 5:16 p.m. Director Watts seconded
the motion, and all present voted aye.



I, Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder for Herriman City, hereby certify that the foregoing minutes represent a true, accurate
and complete record of the meeting held on March 11, 2020. This document constitutes the official minutes for the
Community Development and Renewal Agency Meeting.
