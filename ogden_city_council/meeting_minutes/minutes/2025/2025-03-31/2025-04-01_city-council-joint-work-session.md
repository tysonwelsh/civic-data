        The purpose of the Joint Work Session was to review the agenda for the City Council and Municipal Building Authority
meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.; receive an Administrative Update; discuss Council/Trustee business; and hear
Council/Trustee comments.

Agenda Review
        Chair White and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council and Municipal
Building Authority meetings scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m.

Administrative Update
         Mayor Nadolski and Parking and Mobility Operations Coordinator Emil Vargason provided the Council with an
update regarding the following topics:
             • Downtown parking program.
             • Safety near open waterways, specifically along the Weber River, over the next several weeks and months.
             • Status of the Ogden Canyon waterline project.

Council/Trustee Business
        There was no additional Council/Trustee business.

Council/Trustee Comments
        There were no additional Council/Trustee comments.


         The meeting adjourned at 5:59 p.m.
