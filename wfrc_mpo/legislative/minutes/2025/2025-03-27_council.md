 FRC Minutes 
W
 March 27, 2025 

 MINUTES 
 Wasatch Front Regional Council 
 March 27, 2025 
 WFRC Council meeting was held at WFRC Offices, 41 N Rio Grande Street, Salt Lake City and via Zoom. 
 1. Welcome & Consent Agenda [00:00:16] 
 At 1:37pm, WFRC Council Chair, Mayor Dawn Ramsey welcomed the Wasatch Front Regional Council members 
 and guests. Introductions were made around the table, and online via roll call. Time was taken to gather the 
 Council membership for a group photo. [00:11:04] ACTION : Mayor Ramsey brought attention to the minutes of the 
 January 23, 2025 meeting and Financial statements and check registers for December 2024 & January 2025 and 
 Budget/Expenditure Report to date. Commissioner Mike Newton made a motion to approve all the documents as 
 presented and Mayor Joy Petro seconded the motion. The minutes and financial statements were approved 
 unanimously. 
 2. Chair Report [00:11:42] 
 a . 2025 Utah Legislative Session Overview 
 The state legislative session concluded a few weeks ago, and WFRC’s Government Affairs Manager Miranda 
 Jones Cox provided information about session outcomes. WFRC has two legislators who serve as members of 
 WFRC: Senator Wayne Harper, Chair of the Senate Transportation Committee, and Representative Ariel Defay. 
 They each provided some thoughts on the session. 
 b . Federal Transportation Reauthorization Principles [00:31:38] 
 Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, joined Ms. Jones Cox in providing an update on federal reauthorization 
 and how Utah is working on shared principles that include input from all transportation partners. 
 3. Public Comment [00:47:57] 
 Mayor Ramsey opened the meeting up for public comment. There was one member of the public that provided a 
 comment. 
 4. Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) [00:52:56] 
 Mayor Ramsey turned the time to Commissioner Jim Harvey, Vice Chair of Trans Com, who introduced Ben 
 Wuthrich, Transportation Improvement Program Coordinator at WFRC. 
 a . Board Modifications to the 2025-2030 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Mr. Wuthrich stated that 
 since the last meeting of the Wasatch Front Regional Council (WFRC), requests were received to modify the 
 current 2025-2090 TIP. The modification required action from Trans Com (since WFRC was not meeting) and the 
 Utah Transportation Commission but did not require a new air quality conformity analysis or a 30-day public 
 comment period. The modification is attached to the approved resolution. 
 b . ACTION: Board Modifications to the 2025-2030 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) [00:55:22] 
 Mr. Wuthrich continued the report by briefly describing the modification requests to the current 2025-2030 
 Transportation Improvement Program (TIP). [00:57:20] ACTION: Commissioner Harvey made a motion to 
 approve the modifications as discussed. Commissioner Bob Stevenson seconded the motion and the affirmative 
 vote was unanimous in the affirmative. 
 c . Report on obligations of federal transportation funds during FY24 [00:57:48] Commissioner Harvey 
 turned the time back to Mr. Wuthrich, who presented information on the annual obligations of federal 
 transportation funds during FY24. 
 d . Adjustments to the TIP Amendment Process [01:04:30] Commissioner Harvey introduced Wayne Bennion, 
 WFRC Short Range Planning Director, who along with Mr. Wuthrich, reported on two adjustments that have been 
 made to the amendment approval process. 
 5. Regional Growth Committee (RGC) [01:14:05] 
 Mayor Ramsey called on Mayor Bob Dandoy, Chair of RGC, to discuss two items: the announcement of the 2025 
 projects awarded for the Transportation and Land Use Connection Program, and an action item to certify the 
 Station Area Plan for Sandy City.

 Mayor Dandoy introduced WFRC Community & Economic Development Director, Meg Padjen, who reminded the 
 group of the purpose of the TLC Program. She then announced this year’s awards from the Transportation and 
 Land Use Connection (or TLC) Program.

 Byron Head, Community Planner at WFRC, presented on the responsibility that WFRC has to certify that all 
 Station Area Plans satisfy the required statutory requirements. Cities around the region have been engaged in 
 Station Area Planning around transit stations, which are key areas to effectively accommodate growth. Mr. Head 
 then discussed the Sandy City SAP. [01:36:16] ACTION: Mayor Dandoy stated that the Regional Growth 
 Committee reviewed the SAP and recommendation at their last meeting on March 20, and unanimously 
 recommended presenting it for approval by the WFRC Council. Mayor Ramseyl entertained a motion to certify the 
 Sandy City Station Area Plan.The motion was made by Mayor Monica Zoltanski, and seconded by Mayor Tamara 
 Tran. The affirmative vote was unanimous. 
 6. WFRC Budget Committee [01:38:20] 
 Mayor Ramsey then brought the group’s attention to Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton, WFRC Budget 
 Committee Chair, for the WFRC Budget Committee report. 
 a . ACTION: Release the Draft FY26 WFRC Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) for 
 public comment Councilmember Winder Newton gave a brief introduction and then turned the time over to 
 Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, who provided an overview on the process for the FY26 Goals, Budget, 
 and Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP). Mr. Gruber provided a high-level summary of WFRC’s FY26 Goals 
 and the UPWP. [01:43:09] Marian Florence, Chief Financial Officer at WFRC, presented the FY26 Draft WFRC 
 Budget, discussing the two major changes to the budget: consulting, and staffing. [01:46:40] ACTION: Mayor Jeff 
 Silvestrini made a motion to release the draft FY26 WFRC Goals, Budget, and Unified Planning Work Program 
 (UPWP) for public comment. Mayor Dustin Gettel seconded the motion. With no further discussion, releasing the 
 three FY26 documents for public comment was approved unanimously. 
 b. ACTION: Updates to the Administrative and Accounting Policy and the Personnel Policy [01:47:20] 
 Councilmember Winder Newton, continued, stating that WFRC regularly reviews its policies to ensure that they 
 reflect best practices and any updates in State or Federal fiscal guidance. There are afew minor policy updates to 
 WFRC’s Administrative and Accounting Policy and Personnel Policy that the Budget Committee and WFRC staff 
 are recommending for approval. The updates fall into two basic categories: 
 Edits to reflect updates to the Uniform Guidance for federal funds : 
 - In our Accounting and Administrative Policy, the capitalization threshold for depreciating equipment needs to be 
 raised from $5,000 to $10,000. 
 - We’re required to add to our Personnel Policy a written whistleblower policy, to clarify the rights of any 
 employee who suspects illegal or fraudulent activity. This is in addition to the fraud hotline that we have had in 
 place for years. 
 Best practices updates: 
 - Clarifying internal control over banking deposits. 
 - Increased reimbursements for educational assistance in line with IRS allowable amounts. 
[ 01:48:40] ACTION: Mayor Dustin Gettel made a motion to adopt the amended WFRC Administrative and 
 Accounting Policy and Personnel Policy. Mayor Jeff Silvestrini seconded the motion. With no further discussion, 
 releasing the three FY26 documents for public comment was approved unanimously. 
 7. Reports [01:49:20] 
 a. Active Transportation Committee (ATC) update 
 Mayor Joy Petro, Vice Chair of ATC, provided this report. On Saturday, May 10, the Golden Spoke Bike Ride is 
 back for its eighth year of celebrating the eight trails that connect riders from Ogden all the way to Provo. There are 
 several different ride options available, including child-friendly routes. Please visit goldenspoke.net for more event 
 information. The Golden Spoke ride is supported by WFRC, MAG, Move Utah, UTA, the Jordan River Commission, 
 local health departments and other local partners. Beehive Bikeways : WFRC staff are continuing to evaluate 
 recommendations for Beehive Bikeways that were received at the Fall 2024 Wasatch Choice Vision Workshops. 
 Beehive Bikeways are high-quality, in-town trails that are suitable for users of all abilities to bike to more places 
 and specifically connect Wasatch Choice Centers. They are being incorporated into the 2027-2055 Regional 
 Transportation Plan. Utah Trail Network : The Trails Division at UDOT has finished their statewide workshops and 
 stakeholder outreach efforts. Feedback received from these efforts are being refined into a Vision for the Utah Trail 
 Network, which will include both new and existing trails across the state.

 asatch Front Economic Development District (WFEDD) update [01:53:53] 
 Commissioner Gage Froerer, Chair of WFEDD, began his report by discussing the topics that were on the agenda

 d. Executive Director’s report [01:58:58] 
 Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director, acknowledged three staff members for five years of service. Josh 
 Reynolds, Data Scientist in WFRC’s Analytics Group, Bill Hereth, Deputy Director of the Analytics Group, and 
 Rosie Hernandez, Executive Assistant and part of the Organizational Operations Team at WFRC. All three are 
 reliable, professional, and their dedication to their work greatly benefits WFRC and the communities served along 
 the Wasatch Front. Mr. Gruber also introduced a new Transportation Planner in the Long Range Planning Group, 
 Samantha DeSeelhorst. 
 8. Other Business and Adjournment [02:06:20] 
 The next WFRC Council meeting is set for Thursday May 22, 2025. The meeting adjourned at 3:42pm.
