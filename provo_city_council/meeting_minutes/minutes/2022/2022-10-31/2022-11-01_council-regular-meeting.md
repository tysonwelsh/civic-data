 3.           An ordinance amending the zone map classification of approx. .22 acres of property located
              at 1220 E 960 S, from One Family Residential (R1.8) & Residential Conservation (RC) to Low
              Density Residential (LDR). Provost South Neighborhood (PLRZ20220244) 00:21:46

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve an Ordinance, as currently constituted, has been made
                      by council rule.

Chair Handley opened for public comment.

Christy Charles of Provo discussed living near the plan. She has requested they vote against this ordinance
amending the zone map classification. She stated this home would be twice the size as the other homes
in this area.

Nick Blackburn of Provo is opposing the amendment. He stated he is a Provo Firefighter for 18 years. He
spoke on living and working in Provo and he is concerned with parking issues that this neighborhood is
faced with. He states the street is lined with cars and that the parking would invade the cul-de-sac. He
stated as a Firefighter he is concerned that is could potentially be a fire hazard to the neighborhood since
it could be a safety issue on getting to the emergencies.

Brad Bundy of Provo stated he prides himself on the community and feels that this is not a safe approval
for children. He spoke on parking concerns for this area.

John Pearson of Provo spoke on being new to the neighborhood and his opinions on this proposed
amendment.

Mark Geslison of Provo stated that this is zoned as R-1. He stated he represents the HOA for his
neighborhood. He stated the overall concern from his neighborhood is the parking and the spill-over from
the apartment complex that is in that neighborhood.

Trevor Sharp of Pleasant Grove stated he is the developer and he bought this lot for his son so that his
son could live on one side and rent out the other side to assist him in paying off his mortgage. He states
that their plan meets all city codes and parking is not an issue as they will have ample parking within their
property.

Randalynn Masadoni of Provo spoke on being a resident of 25 years. She feels like the area they live in is
a gem. She stated that changing the zoning would be detrimental to this neighborhood and states that
the developer can still build a home with an accessory dwelling unit that would be approved under the R1
zone it is now.

Angela Blackburn of Provo states she is a resident of 12 years. She stated that the plan was made for the
neighborhood for a reason and is against the amendment.

Chair Handley stated that this ordinance would be continued to the November 15th council meeting. He
thanked the public for their comments and concerns.

 4.           A resolution appropriating $245,000 in the General Fund to cover costs incurred during golf
              course construction and a transfer of those monies from the General Fund to the Golf Course
              Fund. (22-074) 00:41:25

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-53, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

Scott Henderson, Director of Parks and Recreation presented.

Brett Watson, Golf Pro presented and spoke on environmental sustainability. He stated there is no
culinary water usage at this golf course. He also spoke on going fully electric. Meaning using all electric
items such as electric carts and electric machinery and tools. He also reported that there was 100,000
rounds of golf last year and that revenue has improved over the past two years. He thanked the council
and administration for the support and guidance.

John Borget, Director of Administrative Services presented. He stated that the golf course will be self-
sustainable in the future.

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-53, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

With no public comment or council discussion Chair Handley called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                      MacKay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

 5.           A resolution appropriating $5,500,000 in the General Fund for a regional sports park and a
              transfer of those monies from the General Fund to the Parks and Recreation Capital
              Improvement Project (CIP) Fund. (22-017) 00:52:47

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-54, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

Scott Henderson, Director of Parks and Recreation presented. He stated he is hoping to have this project
completed by Spring of 2024. He spoke on the sports park being self-sustaining with no operation or
capital debt. He also mentioned that current parks and recreation staff will take on this area.

Chair Handley opened it up for public comment. With no public comment, he opened it up for council
discussion.

        Motion:       Councilor Shipley proposed a motion to amend this resolution to withhold $500,000.00
                      from the requested $5,500,000.00. Councilor Ellsworth seconded that motion.

Councilor Fillmore stated he is against this amendment. He stated he feels it’s not appropriate and the
funds would be needed based on the amount they requested. He feels that parks and recreation has done
a phenomenal job financing this development.

Councilor Hoban asked Mr. Henderson on his thoughts about this motion presented.

Mr. Henderson stated having the full amount of funding in place helps contracts be in place and project
management run smoothly. He mentioned it will help get bids and will set the scope of what you are
asking for.

Councilor Hoban stated that Mr. Henderson words were convincing.

John Borget spoke on his feelings of holding funds from the requested amount and stated he has concerns
doing so. He spoke on ARPA funds and the allotted time to use them.

Councilor Shipley gives his input on vetting this project. He would like to wait 60-90 days before releasing
the remaining amount if it is still needed.

Councilor MacKay feels like the discussion has been a long time coming. She agrees with Councilor
Fillmore as well as Parks and Recreation and feels there is no need to hold back funds.

Chair Handley agrees with the original motion. He stated that the Parks and Recreation department has
gone above and beyond to show that the funds are needed and will be spent correctly and accordingly.

Councilor Whipple stated she is torn about the motion. She feels convinced from Scott Henderson and his
vision and his past projects. She feels the right thing to do is to give the full amount to Parks and
Recreation.

Councilor Ellsworth stated we owe the citizens a thoughtful and thorough process.

Chair Handley called for a vote on the substitute motion.

        Vote:         The motion was defeated 5:2. Shipley & Ellsworth in favor. Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                      MacKay and Whipple were opposed.

With no public comment or council discussion Chair Handley called for a vote.

        Vote:         The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, Hoban,
                      MacKay, Shipley, and Whipple in favor.

Councilor Whipple asked what the Preliminary estimation for asbestos abatement is.

Mr. Parker responded with he believes it is at $1.8 Million and asked Scott Henderson to confirm.

Scott Henderson stated that amount is correct but that it is just a preliminary estimate and not a formal
estimate or bid.

Mr. Parker spoke on cost escalation since they received that preliminary estimate.

Councilor Whipple asked if this included demolition.

Mr. Parker stated it is solely for the abatement only.

Chair Handley opened it up to the public for discussion. With none, he opened it up for council discussion.

      Motion:       Councilor Shipley proposed a motion to amend this resolution to withhold $500,000
                    from the requested $3,000,000. Councilor Whipple seconded.

Councilor Fillmore asked Wayne Parker or Mayor Kaufusi to respond to this proposal.

Wayne Parker gave his input and concerns on the proposal.

Council Fillmore commented on Mr. Parkers concerns.

Councilor Hoban asked Councilor Shipley questions about his proposed motion.

Councilor Shipley answered Councilor Hoban with his concerns.

Councilor Whipple stated she has reservations about the incentive for developers and using the remaining
balance of ARPA funds for this project.

Councilor MacKay commented that she thought we were taking the building down ourselves as a city.

Keith Morey, Assistant Director of Development Services spoke on the need to get the building down so
that we can get the fire station built. He stated he feels this is something that we need to get done now
to due to safety concerns. He also spoke on the city not having the proper equipment to demolish the
building ourselves.

Councilor MacKay commented on Mr. Morey’s comments.

Mayor Kaufusi spoke on the time and cost of abatement and demolishment of the city building. She
expressed concerns on the cost and the chain supply demand.

Councilor Whipple thanked the Mayor for her thoughts. She stated she has reservations on the language
of the redevelopment incentives wording in this resolution.

Keith Morey made a comment to make a substitute motion to take out the wording of Redevelopment
Incentives.
