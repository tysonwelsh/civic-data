Jennifer Jastremsky, Senior Planner, introduced the Item and provided an overview of what had
previously been approved by the Planning Commission. She noted that the extension would
extend the site plan approval until November 23, 2020.

Vice Chairman Hawker inquired what would happen if construction was not started before the
end of the additional six months.

Ms. Jastremsky clarified the site plan approval would expire and they would need to start the
process over again. She noted that the new site plan may need to be configured differently to
meet changes that have been made to the City’s standards.

Clayton Morgan, Applicant, explained that there were some issues that took longer than they had
foreseen, but were almost finished with everything they need for the City to issue them a
building permit and to begin construction.

Motion: Commissioner Player motioned to approve the Site Plan Extension as requested by
Clayton Morgan for the Annex Site Plan, Application: SP-103-2018, based on the findings
listed in the memo dated May 14, 2020.

Second: Commissioner Van Hoff

Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 5-0 in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Player, Van Hoff, Squire, Gundersen, and Hawker voted, “Aye”.

2.     Public Hearing: Stevens Lot Rezone (Legislative Action)
       On the request of Bryan Flamm for approval of a zoning map amendment to change the
       zoning designation from RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 sq. ft. Lot minimum) to
       RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 sq. ft. lot minimum). This property is
       approximately 1.0 Acre in size. The address is approximately 13755 Shadow Mountain
       Lane. Staff Contact: Travis Van Ekelenburg, 801-576-6522,
       travis.vanekelenburg@draper.ut.us Application: TEXTMAP-964-2020.

Chairman Adams rejoined the meeting.

Travis Van Ekelenburg, City Planner, provided an overview of the Item including the current
status of the lot and the requested changes. He shared the applicant’s intent with the rezone was
to extend the size of their rear yard.

Vice Chairman Hawker asked if the rezone would allow for an additional home to go in on the
property on the private lane.

Mr. Van Ekelenburg stated that it would be possible. He reviewed some of the requirements that
would have to be meet for the private lane to service 2-5 homes.

Chairman Adams asked if Shadow Mountain Lane complied with the 20 feet width requirement
with curb and gutter since it currently serviced four homes.

Mr. Van Ekelenburg shared that it varied in width from 22-33 feet and noted there was space to
make the needed lane improvements if the rezone was approved and a fifth home was added.

Ms. Jastremsky commented that if the rezone was approved a subdivision application would also
need to come forward which is when a full analysis of what improvements to the lane would be
required and what approval would be needed.

Commissioner Squire inquired if the possible lot split would be compliant with the RA2 Zone.

 Mr. Van Ekelenburg clarified that they would verify all the lots met the necessary requirements
during the subdivision application if the applicant decided to divide the lot in the future.

Bryan Flamm, Applicant, stated he had reached an agreement with Mr. Stevens to buy the back
half of his property. He clarified for the record that his intent for the property was simply to add
the space to his back yard and not to build an additional home.

Commissioner Player asked if the property the applicant planned to purchase included the bare
ground and large garage in the image.

Mr. Flamm indicated it included the gravel parking area on the far side of the garage as well
comprising of about a half-acre in total.

Commissioner Squire stated some neighbors were concerned with additional people accessing
the private lane and asked if he would have rights to the lane if he purchased the property.

Mr. Flamm explained that the recorded easements that run with the land that indicated those
rights would be conveyable to him if he purchased the property. He added that he would
continue to use his primary garage on the other side for daily in and out-vehicle usage and not
Shadow Mountain Lane. He shared more detailed information and images of his plans for the
subject property.

Vice Chairman Hawker asked if the applicant’s neighbors the McPhersons were comfortable
with his plans.

Mr. Flamm responded that they were and noted he would be giving them access to his pickleball
court.

Chairman Adams opened the meeting up for Public Comments.

Julie Zimmerman, 13765 Shadow Mountain Lane, stated they preferred to limit the private lane
to the four current residence and suggested Mr. Flamm be restricted to accessing the land from
Kennington Court if he purchases the subject property.

Nancy Nichols indicated her concern was already addressed.

Ellen Thorpe, 13712 South Court Street, indicated that she agreed with Ms. Zimmerman that
access should not be given to Mr. Flamm if he purchased the property even though the garage
doors face Shadow Mountain Lane.

Diane Brown, 13735 Shadow Mountain Lane, commented that they were told only four
properties could have access to the Lane. She expressed concern with the potential for more
traffic and an additional home going in on the private lane.

Chairman Adams called for additional public comments. No further comments were offered, and
the public hearing closed.

Commissioner Squire asked if City Code only allowed for one driveway per property.

Ms. Jastremsky explained that the code allowed for two driveways if there were multiple street
frontages.

Commissioner Van Hoff asked if the transferable access that comes with existing property on
Shadow Mountain Lane would be in effect if the property was split adding a fifth property to the
lane. He suggested that even if a home was not built on the property it may require the fifth lot
owner on the lane to make improvements to the lane.

Ms. Jastremsky indicated that would be fully analyzed with a subdivision application.

Commissioner Squire pointed out that this was simply a zone change and lot lines were not being
changed. She added that it may also not be the City’s place to sort out who had access to the
private lane or not.

Chairman Adams remarked that there were additional homes that fronted the private lane that did
not have driveway access from the lane.

Commissioner Squire commented that she understands the neighbors’ concerns and believes they
are valid but acknowledged that the proposed rezone did not go against zoning in the area.

Motion: Commissioner Squire motioned to forward a positive recommendation to the City
Council for the Stevens Lot Zoning Map Amendment as requested by Bryan Flamm from
RA1 to RA2, Application TEXTMAP-964-2020, based on the findings listed in the Staff
Report dated May 8, 2020.

Second: Commissioner Hawker

Vote: A roll call was taken with the Commissioners voting 4-1 in favor of the motion.
Commissioners Hawker, Player, Squire, Gundersen voted, “Aye”. Commissioner Van Hoff
“Nay”.
