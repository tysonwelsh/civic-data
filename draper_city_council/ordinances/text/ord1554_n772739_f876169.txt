City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On August 2, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1554, amending Title 1
of the Draper City Municipal Code regarding the City Seal and other City symbols. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 3rd day of August 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper
City Recorder.
