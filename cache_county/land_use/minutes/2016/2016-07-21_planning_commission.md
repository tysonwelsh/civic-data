#1 Public Hearing (5:35 PM): Ordinance 2016-11: Agritourism 21 
 22 
Harrild reviewed the Amendments to sections 17.07 Definitions and 17.09 Schedule of Zoning Uses of 23 
the County Code regarding Agritourism uses.  24 
 25 
Staff and Commission discussed Agritourism. The main use of the farm would still be used for 26 
agriculture and the accessory use would be Agritourism. There will be an occupancy limit of two people 27 
per room (excluding children under 15); the parcel needs to be at least 10 acres and used more than 14 28 
days (consecutive or non-consecutive) to be considered Agritourism. Bed and Breakfasts are not 29 
considered part of the Agritourism. Currently staff knows of two landowners that would qualify under 30 
this new definition and are operating and have received notices to stop and cease their operations. If there 31 
are operations that currently have a conditional use permit (CUP) their permits are still valid under their 32 
existing conditions. Some Commissioners expressed that the consequences from this definition could be 33 
larger than intended. There will be some things that come up that will have to be addressed and the code 34 
can be amended as needed. In the use chart Agritourism is conditionally permitted in the A10 and FR40. 35 
Produce stands do not fall under this definition and are a zoning clearance done administratively. The 36 
main reason for this code amendment is to help with impacts on the surrounding area.  Many 37 
commissioners felt the 14 days was too restrictive and should be increased to twenty one (21) days. 38

#2 Public Hearing (6:20 PM): Ordinance 2016-12: Telecommunications Facilities 4 
 5 
Harrild reviewed the Amendments to the Telecommunications Facilities Ordinance. The FCC issued a 6 
statute stating coverage justification and economic feasibility cannot be considered or reviewed when 7 
making a decision concerning tower height. Some questions were asked regarding setbacks; currently the 8 
ordinance requires that the towers have enough land to equal the height of the tower plus 10 feet. No 9 
applications for a new telecommunications facility have been received since the current ordinance was 10 
passed. The new ruling from the FCC does allow for the Planning Commission to decide on aesthetics.  11

#3 Public Hearing (6:30 PM): Ordinance 2016-13: Various amendments to Title 17 31 
 32 
Harrild reviewed the various amendments. This includes amendments to sections 17.02 Administration, 33 
17.04 Enforcement, 17.06 Uses, 17.07 Definitions, 17.09 Schedule of Zoning Uses, 17.10 Development 34 
Standards, 17.13 Mineral Extraction and Excavation (ME) Overlay Zone, and 17.16 Group Living 35 
Facilities. 36 
 37 
06:27:00 38 
 39 
Olsen returned. 40 
 41 
Harrild reviewed 17.02 Administration. There were some redundant pieces in the ordinance and the 42 
redundant sections were deleted. The second item is to allow extensions. For 17.04 Enforcement, is a 43 
Class C misdemeanor not a Class B misdemeanor. For 17.06 Uses, it was updated to address a gap in the 44 
process for CUPs that cease operation but still have ongoing requirements to fulfill. This change is mostly 45 
for gravel pits that have exhausted their permit but still need to complete reclamation requirements. For 46 
17.07 Definitions, it was updated to more accurately specify appropriate definitions for any words or 47 
phrases not found in the county, state, or building code. Section 1130 was added to reflect the previous 48 
update to the use chart in 17.09 for accessory structures. These definitions were moved from general 49 
definitions section, amended, and placed here with the use related definitions. Some minor updates were 50 
made for the residential living facility to reference the code. Definition 5100 was updated to clarify and 51
