MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY
 1. CALL TO ORDER
     BOARD:     Chairperson Chris McConnehey, Vice Chairperson Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach
                Jacob, David Pack, Kayleen Whitelock, Member Melissa Worthen
     STAFF:     Council Office Director Alan Anderson, Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck,

                Office Clerk Cindy Quick

Chairperson McConnehey called the meeting to order at 8:57 pm and noted that Board Member
Worthen was participating via Zoom.

 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
   a. Resolution No. 110 adopting the Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building Authority
      of the City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2024 and setting May 10, 2023 as the date for a
      public hearing regarding the adoption of the final budget.
      Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck proposed the adoption of the Tentative Budget
      for the Municipal Building Authority (MBA) explaining that the budget was in the amount of
      the debt service to pay for the bond for the public works building. The general fund and utility
      fund pays to rent the building and the MBA owns the building. She then proposed setting the
      public hearing for the adoption of the final budget on May 10th and noted that the MBA has no
      taxing authority.

      MOTION:     Board Member Green moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 110 adopting the
                  Tentative Budget for the Municipal Building Authority of the City of West Jordan
                  for Fiscal Year 2024 and setting May 10, 2023 as the date for a public hearing
                  regarding the adoption of the final budget.
                  Vice Chairperson Bloom seconded the motion.

      The vote was recorded as follows:
      Yes: Chris McConnehey, Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, David Pack, Kayleen
      Whitelock, Melissa Worthen
      No:
      Absent:
      The motion passed 7-0.
