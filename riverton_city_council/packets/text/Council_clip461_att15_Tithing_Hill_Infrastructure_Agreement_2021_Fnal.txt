                                  FIRE HYDRANT TESTING AGREEMENT



This Agreement is entered into this ____ day of ____________, 2021, by and between Farm at Tithing
Hill Homeowners Association Management Company (herein after HOA) located at 12547 South Tithing
Hill Drive, Riverton, Utah 84065 and Riverton City, a Utah municipality organized and existing pursuant
to U.C.A. Section 10-3-101, et seq. (“Riverton”) to establish the rights and responsibilities associated
with the Fire Hydrants located within the operational boundaries of the HOA.



                                                RECITALS

WHEREAS, the parties wish to define and clarity the scope of City’s operational function in regards to the
fire hydrants located within the operational boundaries of the HOA.

NOW THEREFORE, in the light of consideration given and received by the parties both of whom
acknowledge the sufficiency of said consideration, and subject to the mutual covenants contained
herein, the Parties agree as follows:



1.      SERVICES. Riverton City shall as the date of this agreement conduct a yearly hydrant flow test of
        each fire hydrant located within the operational boundaries of the HOA and immediately notify
        the HOA of any problems observed/discovered by the City during the hydrant flow test. The
        HOA at their sole expense shall be responsible for the resolution/repair of any infrastructure or
        operational problems discovered by the City during the yearly hydrant flow test.

2.      COMPENSATION TO CITY. The HOA shall, within 30 days from the City’s performance of any
        hydrant flow test, pay to Riverton City the amount of $50.00 per metered fire hydrant in
        consideration of the services provided to the HOA by the City.

3.      TERM. The term of this Agreement shall commence on the date of latest parties signature on
        this Agreement and continue unless and until either party terminates of this agreement by
        giving the City 60 days written notice of termination to the other party.

4.      INTEGRATION. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties hereto
        and the final, complete and exclusive expression of the terms and conditions thereof. All prior
        agreements, representations, negotiations and understandings of the Parties hereto, oral or
        written, express or implied, are hereby superseded and merged herein.

5.      EFFECTIVE DATE. This Agreement shall become effective upon a resolution approving the
        Agreement being duly adopted by the respective governing bodies of the Parties and execution
        of this Agreement by their respective duly authorized representatives.

6.      DISPUTE RESOLUTION. In the event of a dispute between the Parties related to this Agreement,
        each Party shall be responsible for their own costs and shall split the cost of any pre-judicial
      arbitration, mediation of alternative dispute resolution undertaking by dividing the total pre-
      judicial costs by the number of Parties involved. Any and all litigation and disputes under,
      regarding or in relation to this Agreement shall be subject to Utah State Law.

7.    NOTICES. A notice or communication under this Agreement, by a Party to another Party, shall be
      sufficiently given or delivered, if given in writing by personal service, express mail, Federal
      Express, DHL or any other similar form of courier or delivery service, or mailing in the United
      States mail, postage prepaid, certified, return receipt requested and addressed to such Party as

8.    ATTORNEYS’ FEES. In the event that a judicial action is instituted to interpret and/or enforce the
      terms of this Agreement, the substantively prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover from the
      non-prevailing Party, in addition to all the sums that the non-prevailing Party may be called
      upon to pay, reasonable attorneys' fees and expenses, whether the dispute was resolved
      through litigation or otherwise.

9.    COUNTERPARTS. This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which
      shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one instrument and one
      instrument. Electronic scans of original signatures shall be treated as original signatures for all
      purposes of this Agreement.

10.   SEVERABILITY. If any provision of this Agreement shall be invalid or unenforceable to any extent
      the remainder of the Agreement shall not be affected thereby and shall be liberally construed
      and enforced to the greatest extent permitted by law.

11.   FILING OF AGREEMENT. Executed copies of this Agreement shall be deposited with and remain
      in the office of each of the Parties during the effective term hereof.
12.       MUTUAL DRAFTING. Each Party has participated in negotiating and drafting this Agreement and
          therefore no provision of this Agreement shall be construed for or against any Party based on
          which Party drafted any particular portion of this Agreement.

13.       ASSIGNMENT. Neither Party may assign this Agreement, nor any part thereof, without prior
          written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, delayed, or
          conditioned.

14.       THIRD PARTIES. There are no third-party beneficiaries to this Agreement, and no person or
          entity not a party hereto shall have any right or cause of action hereunder.

15.       AMENDMENTS. No modification of this Agreement shall be valid, binding, or enforceable unless
          made in writing and signed by the Parties.
