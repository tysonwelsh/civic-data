      1. RESOLUTION APPROVING AN AMENDED INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT OF THE WEBER-
         MORGAN HEALTH DEPARTMENT – RESOLUTION 19-2018.
           Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, noted that late last year a subcommittee was organized of the
           Weber-Morgan Board of Health to examine the structure of their Interlocal Agreement and the membership
           structure. Pursuant to those meetings, a recommendation was made to that Board to amend the Agreement and
           to modify the membership structure. That recommendation was approved by the Weber-Morgan Health
           Department and referred to the Weber County Commission, which approved it and sent it to the Morgan County
           Council, which approved it with one change to board representation—Morgan requested to be included in the
           local school district rotation representation. It is now before Weber County for its action.
           Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 19-2018 approving an amended Interlocal Cooperation
           Agreement of the Weber-Morgan Health Department; Chair Harvey seconded. Commissioner Gibson is
           concerned that this amendment takes away much of the individual taxpayer’s voice by eliminating two Weber
           County commissioners from the Board. Dave Holmstrom, Weber-Morgan Health Board Chair, said that the
           Board increases from 11 to 13 and includes representation from people that have the related expertise.
           Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – nay; Chair Harvey – aye
      2. APPROVE THE WEBER COUNTY TOURISM TAX ADVISORY BOARD (TTAB) BYLAWS.
           Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, stated that the TTAB is a statutory entity charged with advising
           the County Commission with what it considers the best use of revenues collected under the tourism, recreation,
           cultural, convention and airport facilities tax/transient room tax. He said that that these are the standard bylaws
           and also incorporates the statutory requirements that govern TTAB.
           Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the Weber County Tourism Tax Advisory Board bylaws;
           Commissioner Ebert seconded.
           Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

    2. PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING A PROPOSED ISSUANCE BY UTAH COUNTY, UTAH (THE “ISSUER”) OF ITS
       HOSPITAL REVENUE BONDS (THE “BONDS”) IN ONE OR MORE SERIES & IN AN AGGREGATE PRINCIPAL
       AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $34,000,000 WITH RESPECT TO CERTAIN HEALTH CARE FACILITIES OF IHC
       HEALTH SERVICES, INC. LOCATED IN WEBER COUNTY, FOR THE PURPOSE OF FINANCING, REFINANCING OR
       PROVIDING REIMBURSEMENT FOR THE ACQUISITION, IMPROVEMENT & EQUIPPING OF SUCH HEALTH CARE
       FACILITIES & CONSIDERING FOR ADOPTION A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE ISSUANCE OF THE BONDS FOR
       PURPOSES OF SECTION 147(F) OF THE INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1986, AS AMENDED.

         Matt Dugdale, of George K Baum & Company, stated that this is part of an interlocal agreement that IHC has
         entered into with various counties throughout the State where they have facilities. In order to access tax exempt
         interest rates, IHC has to issue bonds through a local conduit issuer and because some bond proceeds will be spent
         within Weber County, they are required per regulations to come before the county in a public hearing. There is
         no financial impact to Weber County. Commissioner Gibson said that this partnership is beneficial to both sides.
         John Bond, County Treasurer, said that the county as a conduit has very minimal risk and the county’s financial
         advisor has reassured of this as well. Chair Harvey spoke in favor of this item.
