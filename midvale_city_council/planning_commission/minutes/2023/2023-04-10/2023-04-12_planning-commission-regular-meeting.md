GENERAL SESSION

Chair Liedtke called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

  1.    Ezra Nuttall requests Rezone approval for a property located at 610 W 7th Ave
        from Single Family Residential-2 (SF-2) zone to Multifamily Residential – Medium
        Density (RM-12) zone.

        The rezone proposal is to change part of an existing lot from SF-2 to RM-12 zoning. The
        other portion of the lot will remain in CI zoning. Please see the Rezone Exhibit in the
        attachments.

        Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. Two
        written objections have been received as of the writing of this report and have been
        included in the attachments. This proposal has been reviewed by Planning Staff, the City
        Engineer, the Unified Fire Authority, and Public Works for compliance with the
        respective guidelines, policies, standards, and codes. Staff finds the proposed rezone
        complies with requirements outlined in Midvale City Code.

        Staff recommends approval.


                                             ***



PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION APRIL 12, 2023.
APPLICANT:

Ezra Nuttall, property owner, introduces himself and states that the property would still be
protected from becoming a large multi-family building due to the size of the lot. The largest
multi-family building allowed would be a duplex and that is the intended use. The owner will be
adding onto the existing structure.

Mr. Nuttall is not concerned about the one-way street because the parking entrance would be
located on the East side of the property, which is a two-way street.

The height of the proposed addition is not much taller than the existing structure so no concern to
be drawn there.


                                               ***


PUBLIC HEARING:

Chair Liedtke opened the public hearing.

The Ellis Family, living on Rio Grande Street, are concerned with the height of the building and
the traffic on the roadway. The Current development on Main Street and 6th Ave is worrying
enough to them and they do not want more traffic near their residence. Concern was also drawn
from property tax increases. They are glad to hear that the level will remain the same and the traffic
will not drastically increase due to a duplex.

Kent Peterson, a resident living at 700 East, is also concerned about the parking and the possibility
of apartments being built if the re-zone is approved. If a duplex is the largest building there, his
household is not as concerned.

Ms. Arnold, Chair Liedtke, and Mr. Knobloch all reassure Mr. Peterson that the maximum number
of units allowed on the property is two due to the size of the lot, which will not change. The layout
of the structure can change but the number of units will remain the same. The owner could also
decide to demolish the existing structure if desired, but no more than two units could be rebuilt.

DISCUSSION:

Chair Liedtke originally had concerns regarding the allowance of a duplex in the single-family
area. Construction would cause a lot of congestion on a one-way street. After looking into it, he
found that the neighboring zone does allow for the duplex and the Utah State Legislature is now
allowing for ADU’s to be built in the SF-2 Zone. This way, at least the unit number will be
restricted to two units.

Mr. Nuttall assures staff and residents that the construction access will be from the East side of the
property, which is on a two-way street. There should be no hindrance to normal traffic.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

 2.    Ezra Nuttall requests Final Subdivision approval to subdivide an existing property
       into two lots located at 610 W 7th Ave.

       Ezra Nuttall requests final subdivision approval for a two-lot split located at 610 W 7th
       Ave in the SF-2/CI zone. This application is being processed concurrently with a rezone
       request to change the SF-2 zoning to RM-12. The analysis below will consider the final
       subdivision request as if the rezone to RM-12 was approved.

       This proposal has been reviewed by Planning Staff, the City Engineer, and the Unified
       Fire Authority for compliance with the respective guidelines, policies, standards, and
       codes. Staff finds the proposal complies with requirements outlined in Midvale City
       Municipal Code for minor subdivisions (16.04.050) and the lot development standards of
       the RM-12 zone (17-7-3.3). Public notice has been sent to property owners within 500
       feet of the subject parcel. No objections have been received as of the writing of this
       report.

       Staff recommends approval.

       2. Zoning Ordinance Training.
              a. SF-1 and SF-2 Zones (similar zones, different emphasis)
                     i. SF-1: Duplex Overlay & Agricultural Overlay
                            1. Minimum 5,000 sq. ft.
                            2. Allows for a single-family home to have a second dwelling,
                                becoming a small multi-family dwelling. Duplexes allow for
                                both units to be rented out whereas an ADU requires that one
                                of the units must be owner-occupied.
                     ii. SF-2: No Overlays & Smaller Lots
                            1. Minimum 7,000 sq. ft.
                            2. Generally, require less than SF-1 Zones due to smaller lots
                                making it less feasible and less desirable to build multiple
                                dwelling units.
