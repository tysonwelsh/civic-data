2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Chris Bick’s request for review of the Ken
Garff Porsche Development Code Amendment to Chapter 24 allowing the parking
expansion for a legal nonconforming use.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Chris Bick, was present. Ken Garff is adjacent to UDOT property and has a lease
agreement with Ken Garff to be able to park their cars on the leased property. As part of the
agreement, Ken Garff must put in landscaping on the trail, and Ken Garff will be allowed to pave
a small parking lot near their auto dealership. Once the lease is up, the paved area would have to
be removed, but the landscaping would remain. The landscape would be maintained during the
lease by Ken Garff and then turned over to UDOT after the 5 years. The water connection would
be connected to Ken Garff, and after the lease would be converted over. The request was non-
conforming, so there wasn't a way for it to be brought forward as a site plan. Kim stated this area
was never officially or formally approved by the City to be used.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing. He acknowledged the online comment that
was opposed to the method and process the code to one user that will impact the future uses.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen does not like amending fit for one tailored use. He feels it either fits in
code, or it doesn't. He stated this particular dealership has not complied with code since the
moment they have purchased the lot. Tailor fit code seems ridiculous. Commissioner Ellis stated
the reason it is non-conforming is because the zone was changed on them, and that the City made
it non-conforming. Kim Struthers stated if UDOT had an agreement he doesn't see that as such
an issue and they can allow this use, but it was made non-conforming because there were a lot of
office warehouse buildings that were trying to be divided for heavy commercial and industrial
commercial. Kim stated some if this might have unintended consequences. Commissioner
Everett asked if there were quite a few auto dealerships that were non-conforming. Kim stated
there were quite a few and if it were changed, they would have to be made aware of the
application process. Commissioner Newall stated the unintended consequences have affected a
lot of properties and if a change is made, she would like to see something in the motion that
states there would be discretion for parking and landscaping. Commissioner Nielsen felt strongly
that UDOT should be finishing their project. Commissioner Jackson suggested that it shows we
may not care if standards aren't met or that the code may be changed. Commissioner Nielsen
stated he knows the landscaping standard is not being met. Commissioner Britton stated there is
a difference between rewriting a code and granting an exception.

        Motion: Carlson moved to recommend denial to the proposed amendment to the Lehi
        development code, with findings that the code provides too much opportunity with
        expansion to nonconformance usage and general elements. Commissioner Everett
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, no; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson. The vote passed 6 – 1.

2.5) Public hearing and consideration of Lehi City’s request for conditional use approval of
the Strata Network Storage Containers located at approximately 1300 South 1100 West.
Jacob presented information on the agenda item. Craig stated if they are going to add conditions,
they need to be able to articulate them.

The applicant, Bruce Manwaring, was present and answered clarifying questions.

Commissioner Nielsen asked why the construction and shelters needed to be pushed next to the
neighbors. Bruce stated they would be moved and would be at a different location.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public comment.

Kenneth Glade asked when this was approved for a high impact storage property near the
property. He stated he hears this all day long. Staff stated it has been rezoned as public facility
and is a temporary use to install fiber for storage. Kenneth stated there are diesel trucks and
tractor trailers all day long. There is only one access point, and he feels like it is a safety hazard
coming in and out of the facility. He feels it isn't just for containers and was never notified of the
construction.

Larry Hadfield stated he lives across the street from the construction area and asked if there was
anything done to improve the road near the area. He also asked about the traffic that goes up and
down the road because there is a school that is nearby, and it is a safety concern.

KC Christensen asked for clarification that there were four storage containers and asked for how
long and if it was in this specific location for the public facility. He was also concerned about the
traffic and that there isn't a sidewalk and that it is also a safety concern.

Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing.

Jacob stated it was zoned public facility in late 2021 with the GPA for City owned properties and
the public noticing process was through Lehi Free Press and City Utility Billing. Commissioner
Nielsen asked if the City is planning on improving the frontage and sidewalk. Commissioner
Ellis clarified they are just looking at the uses for the four storage containers. Ross Dinsdale
stated the work that is going on right now is allowed. Ross stated they would work with the Fiber
Department and see if there is a way to minimize the impact of that. Commissioner Nielsen
asked about buffers and screening. Kim Struthers stated conditions could be put in place. The
Commission discussed the conditional use process and permitted use process and what the
application request looks like. The table has recently been updated for public facilities use.

        Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to approve of Lehi City’s request for conditional
        use approval of the Strata Network Storage Containers located at approximately
        1300 South 1100 West, a conditional use permit for the storage containers; including
        all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. With the conditions the storage
        containers, be adjacent to the substation or the commercial property and not adjacent to
        the residential property, and direct staff to look at road conditions. Commissioner Carlson
        seconded. All in favor.


        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
        Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson. The vote passed unanimously.
