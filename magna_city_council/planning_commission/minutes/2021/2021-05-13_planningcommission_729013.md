                                          PUBLIC HEARING(S)

                                      Hearings began at – 6:47 p.m.
REZ2021-000256 - Ivory Homes is requesting approval of the P-C Plan and Development Agreement for
the Mahogany Ridge development. This item requires a recommendation be forwarded to the Magna
Council. Acreage: 78.49 acres. Location: 8230 West 4100 South. Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Travis Hair provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioners and staff had a discussion regarding roads, reserved for the applicant, gross acreage, and
roads.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Ivory Development
Name: Bryon Prince
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Prince said road standards were received from the township ordinances and same
standards used across the street at Gateway Little Valley. The garbage will be private garbage
collection and will be individual garbage cans and collected private with the HOA. Streets will be
public 50-foot road and there will be private lanes, not main thoroughfare. Gross acreage excludes
private right of ways and starts at the back of sidewalk. Fencing solid surface wall, throughout
subdivision additional fencing and HOA will establish standards for private lot fencing. Next stage is
community plan. Redline solid surface wall will be stained if the existing neighbor allows access.

OAM2021-000282 – The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District is proposing to amend the existing
Floodplain Ordinance in Section 19.74 of Magna Municipal Code. This update will replace the existing
Section 19.74 with new language that conforms with current State of Utah and FEMA requirements.
Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Travis Hair provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding flood areas and FEMA maps.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public present to speak

Commissioner Elieson motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Richards seconded that motion.

                             PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Motion: To recommend approval of file # OAM2021-000282 to the Magna Council to amend the existing
Floodplain Ordinance.
       Motion by: Commissioner Richards
       2nd by: Commissioner Elieson
       Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
