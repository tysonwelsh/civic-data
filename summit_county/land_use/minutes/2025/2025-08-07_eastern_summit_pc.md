Creek View Farms Preliminary Subdivision Staff Report
Planner Jennifer Leslie introduced the Preliminary Subdivision application and focused on the condition of approval regarding building envelopes. Applicant Lance Arnout supported. (6:02 PM)
Commissioner Bille Wilde and Commissioner David Darcey asked questions about water planning, to which Tracy Breinholt, the owner, and Lance Arnout, the applicant, responded. (6:06 PM)
Commissioner Bridget Hayes pointed out a correction in the condition of approval and requested that it be corrected. Commissioner Hayes also asked a general question about the process if there were water/septic issues, to which Planner Leslie responded. (6:09 PM)
Commissioner Vice Chair Marion Wheaton asked what would happen if there was a water change that led to lower density; could lots be eliminated? What would happen? Planner Leslie responded. (6:10 PM)

Commissioner Bowen requested clarification regarding Exhibit C. Planner Leslie responded. (6:12 PM)
Applicant Lance Arnout offered additional clarification. (6:13 PM)
Commission Vice Chair Wheaton inquired about the aerial views that showed potential irrigation ditches. Planner Leslie, Owner Breinholt, and Applicant Arnout responded. (6:14 PM)

Russell Judd was called to speak. (6:16 PM)

Public hearing and recommendation to the County Council for amendments to the Eastern Summit County Development Code Sections 11-7 General Regulations, 11-6-17 Closure Due to Inaction, 11-2-10 Commercial Zoning District, 11-4-10 Zoning Variances and Appendix A Definitions. The purpose of the amendments is to replace the Board of Adjustment with an Administrative Law Judge and to bring the process by which Variances are reviewed into compliance with Utah State Law. Legislative Process. Project #25-098
.
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner.
(7:13 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Staff Report June 6 2025

Commissioner Hayes asked who appoints the Judge, and is the Judge a singular person, or is there a pool of people or a panel of people, and added a few corrections in the Staff report. Planner Milliner responded. (7:24 PM)
