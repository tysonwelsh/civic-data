                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 25-15

  AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE “DAI UTAH DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT”, AMENDING ZONING FROM R-3 TO C-R, USES LIMITED TO STORAGE
   UNITS, AND PRELIMINARY SITE PLAN APPROVAL FOR APPROX. 5.63 ACRES
  LOCATED AT 12270 S 4000 W., APPLICANT- KRISEL TRAVIS & JOE SALISBURY
                          REPRESENTING DAI UTAH

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that under Utah Code §10-9a-532, the City Council may
lawfully adopt development agreements that have different standards set forth in existing land use
regulations if it does so in accordance with the same procedures for enacting a land use regulation,
including review and recommendation from the Planning Commission and a public hearing; and,

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed development agreement; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said development
agreement; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to adopt the DAI UTAH DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT for approximately 5.63 acres
located at 12270 South 4000 West as described herein and shown as Exhibit “A”.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The DAI Utah Development Agreement, Exhibit “B”, and all
                              associated design documents as approved, is hereby
                              approved and adopted subject to any edits/corrections per the
                              City Council’s motion to approve.

                Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon approval and execution
                                        of the associated Settlement Agreement.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of May,
2025 by the following vote:
