                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 22-02

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BETTERMENT AGREEMENT BTWEEN THE
    UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND RIVERTON CITY FOR
      AESTHETICS AND LANDSCAPING COSTS ASSOCIATED WITH THE
     CONSTRUCTION OF THE BANGERTER INTERCHANE AT 12600 SOUTH

       WHEREAS, the Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) is commencing with
construction of the Bangerter Interchange located at 12600 S and Bangerter Highway; and

        WHEREAS, as part of the project, UDOT included an allowance of $100,000 for the
City to use for aesthetics and landscaping improvements; and

        WHEREAS, the list of improvements requested by the City exceeds the amount
allocated by UDOT to the City; and

       WHEREAS, the amount that exceeds the allowance given to the City is $4,093.27; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has requested a Betterment Agreement from UDOT to
provide the requested improvements; and

       WHEREAS, UDOT has prepared and submitted a Betterment Agreement to the City for
approval which summarizes the City’s request; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City Council finds that the Betterment Agreement establishes the
aesthetics and landscaping improvements which Riverton City desires UDOT to construct; and

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

   1. The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to execute the Betterment
      Agreement with UDOT for aesthetics and landscaping improvements.” Agreement
      attached hereto.

   2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
January, 2022 by the following vote:
