                                                 BUSINESS MEETING

                                              Meeting began at – 5:35 p.m.
    1) Welcome and Approval of the Agenda. (Motion/Voting)

        Commissioner Koester motioned to rearrange the agenda, moving item four to two and item two to
        four. Commissioner Thomas seconded that motion. Commissioners voted unanimously in favor.

    2) Planning Commission Training / Guidance: Kearns’ Land Use Attorney and Long Range Planner
       will lead a brief Planning Commission training on the Commission’s rules of order and procedure,
       Kearns’ social media policy, coordination of agenda items and staff assignments, general land use
       matters that are specific to Kearns, and best practices. (Discussion: Nathan Bracken and Kayla
       Mauldin)

        Mr. Bracken provided planning commission training and guidance for one hour. Ms. Mauldin
        provided an update to recent applications and provided an implementation plan. Mr. Bracken
        advised the commission to read the documents he will be sending out and bring back amendments
        at the next meeting.

    3) Commissioner Discussion and Staff Assignments: this standing agenda item allows Planning
       Commissioners the opportunity to raise and discuss important issues related to current and long-
       range planning in Kearns Metro Township. The Commission may direct assignments to planning
       staff related to discussion items. (Discussion and Staff Direction: Planning Staff)

        Commissioners, Counsel, and staff had a brief discussion regarding time and roles, education,
        questions, and balance, standing item at each meeting and assignments from the whole commission
        and proposed recommendations to the council.

        Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the Olympics and the future.

        Ms. Mauldin provided a presentation regarding Current and Long-range planning projects.
        Discussed recommendations advertising for upcoming events and public participation.

    4) Approval of the January 3, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes. (Motion/Voting)
       Motion: To approve January 3, 2024 Planning Commission Meeting Minutes with one amendment.
             Motion by: Commissioner Nelson
             2nd by: Commissioner Thomas
             Vote: Commissioner Hatch abstained. All other Commissioners voted in favor. Motion
             passed.

    5) Other Business Items. (As Needed)

        No other business items to discuss.

Commissioner Taylor adjourned.
