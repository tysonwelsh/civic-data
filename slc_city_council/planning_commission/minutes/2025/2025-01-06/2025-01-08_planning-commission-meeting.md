SALT LAKE CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MEETING
City & County Building
451 South State Street, Room 326
Salt Lake City, Utah 84111
Wednesday, January 8, 2025

A roll is being kept of all who attended the Planning Commission Meeting. The meeting was called to
order at approximately 5:30 p.m. Audio recordings of the Planning Commission meetings are retained for
a period of time. These minutes are a summary of the meeting and not a verbatim transcript. A video
recording of the meeting is available at www.youtube.com/slclivemeetings
Present for the Planning Commission meeting were: Chair Aimee Burrows, Vice Chair Landon Kraczek,
Commissioners McCall Christensen, Brian Scott, Amy Barry, Mike Christensen, and Bree Scheer.
Staff members present at the meeting were: Planning Manager Kelsey Lindquist, Planning Manager John
Anderson, Senior City Attorney Katherine Pasker, Senior Planner Nannette Larsen, Principal Planner
Ben Buckley, and Office Facilitator II Aubrey Clark.

REPORT OF THE CHAIR AND VICE CHAIR
The Chair mentioned that Planning Division is searching for a new commissioner from District 3. The Vice-
Chair had nothing to report.

REPORT OF THE DIRECTOR
Planning Manager Kelsey Lindquist reported that discussions initiated by Commissioner Scheer would be
scheduled to be heard on January 22, 2025. She also reported that there would be upcoming training for
Local Historic Districts.

OPEN FORUM

No one had items to discuss.

REGULAR AGENDA

1. Reconnaissance Level Survey Rating Change for Upper Yale Local Historic District
Designation – It has been identified that updates to the 2005 Yalecrest reconnaissance level survey
are needed as part of a petition to designate the Yalecrest – Upper Yale as a local historic district.
The principal structures located at 1820, 1828, and 1833 East Yale Ave, that were previously listed
incorrectly as noncontributing, are recommended to be rated as contributing to the National Register.
This item was previously heard by Planning Commission on June 26, 2024, and is returning for a
recommendation to City Council. (Staff Contact: Nannette Larsen at 801-535-7645 or
Nannette.larsen@slc.gov) Case Number: PLNHLC2023-00571

Senior Planner Nan Larsen reviewed the survey’s historic status updates as outlined in the Staff
Report. She stated that staff recommends forwarding a recommendation to approve the rating
updates to City Council.

Public Hearing
Chair Burrows opened the public hearing.
o Cindy Cromer – She spoke about how a property's contributory status affects the benefits
available to it.

Seeing that no one else wished to speak the Chair closed the public hearing.

Executive Session

The Commission and Staff discussed:
o The State Historic Preservation Office’s recommendation
o Concerns regarding Local Historic Districts

Motion
Commissioner Barry stated, “Based on the information presented and discussion, I move that the
Commission forward a recommendation to City Council to update the 2005 Yalecrest
Reconnaissance Level Survey to reflect a historic status rating of “contributing” for the properties
located at 1820, 1828, and 1833 East Yale Avenue in the proposed Yalecrest – Upper Yale Local
Historic District.”
Commissioner Mike Christensen seconded the motion.

Vote
• Yes: Barry, Kraczik, Mike Christensen, Scheer, Scott, McCall Christensen, Burrows
The motion passed.

2. Alley Vacation at Approximately 2680 S Chadwick - Taylor Thomas is requesting approval for an
alley vacation located between Stratford Avenue and 2700 S and Beverly Street and Chadwick Street.
The alley is approximately 810 feet in length and 12 feet in width. If approved, this alley would be
divided and given to the property owners that abut the alley. The subject alley is located within Council
District 7, represented by Sarah Young. (Staff Contact: Ben Buckley at 801-535-7142 or
benjamin.buckley@slc.gov) Case Number: PLNPCM2024-00973

Principal Planner Ben Buckley reviewed the proposal as outlined in the staff report. He stated that
Staff recommends forwarding a recommendation of approval to City Council.

Public Hearing
Chair Burrows opened the public hearing.
Seeing that no one wished to speak the Chair closed the public hearing.

Executive Session

The Commission and Staff discussed:
o Verification of signatures
- The applicant Taylor Thomas reviewed the process of how he collected the neighbor’s
signatures
o Lack of use and illegal encroachments

Motion
Commissioner Scheer stated, “Based on the information presented and discussion, I move that the
Commission recommends that City Council adopt this alley vacation.”
Commissioner Mike Christensen seconded the motion.

Vote
• Yes: Kraczik, Mike Christensen, Scheer, McCall Christensen, Burrows
• No: Barry, Scott
The motion passed.

The meeting adjourned at approximately 6:00 PM
