Subdivision:

 3.         4S22 – Nancy Fields - Public Hearing and Consideration of a Preliminary Plat for Fields Subdivision,
            4768 South 1175 West, Taylorsville, Utah. (Karyn Kerdolff/GIS Planner)

3.1 This item was presented by Karyn Kerdolff.                 Ms. Kerdolff said that Nancy Fields is requesting
    consideration of a Preliminary Plat for the property located at 4768 South 1175 West for approximately 0.72
    acres. The proposal is for a two-lot subdivision, for which the applicant received final plat approval in 2017 but

      did not record the approved plat within the approval timeframe. The property is zoned R-1-10 with a minimum
      10,000 square foot lot size. The proposed plat intends to have two (2) lots varying in size from 10,263 square
      feet to 21,139 square feet. The proposed subdivision has one lot with an existing single-family home on it and
      one proposed lot to the south. To meet Code requirements, the lot sizes must be at least 80’ wide. The
      minimum home size required is 1,200 square feet for a one-story home or 1,875 for a two-story home. The
      setbacks for the R-1-10 zone are: Front Yard – 25 feet; Side Yard – 5 feet; Side Yard Combined – 16 feet; and
      Rear Yard – 15 feet.

3.2   FINDINGS:
      3.2.1     This application was initiated by Nancy Fields.
      3.2.2     The property is zoned R-1-10.
      3.2.3     The applicant is requesting preliminary plat approval for a two (2) lot subdivision.
      3.2.4     The proposed plat meets the size and setback requirements for the R-1-10 zone.
      3.2.5     There is an existing single-family dwelling unit on the property that will remain intact. An
                additional single-family dwelling unit is being proposed on the south side of the property..
      3.2.6     This property was re-zoned from R-1-15 to R-1-10 by the Planning Commission on June 13, 2017
                and the City Council on July 5, 2017.

3.3   CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:
      3.3.1    The original plat for 4768 South 1175 West is amended.
      3.3.2    The plat is recorded with Salt Lake County.
      3.3.3    A Title Report is submitted and matches the property.
      3.3.4    A Stormwater Agreement is needed.
      3.3.5    A bond is needed for improvements in the right-of-way.
      3.3.6    hydrants, streetlights, and drive-ways need to be added to the site plan.
      3.3.7    legal descriptions need to be correct and consistent throughout the plat.
      3.3.8    A stipulation needs to be added to the plat that parking is not allowed on the shared access
               easement
      3.3.9    The final plat is compliant with all applicable codes and ordinances.
      3.3.10   The applicant will comply with the requirements of all reviewing agencies.
      3.3.11   All applicable fees and bonding are paid prior to recording of the plat.

3.4   STAFF RECOMMENDATION: Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approves the Fields
      Preliminary plat for a two (2) lot subdivision.

3.5 DISCUSSION: Commissioner Willardson asked Staff if they had any response from the public regarding this
    proposal and Ms. Kerdolff replied she had received questions about if the existing home was going to be torn
    down and if this was going to be turned into rental units. She had answered no to both questions.

3.6 Commissioner Wright opened the public hearing and asked if there was anyone wishing to speak either in
    favor or opposition to this application. No one came forward, so Commissioner Wright closed the public
    hearing and opened the meeting for discussion or a motion by the Planning Commission.

3.7 Commissioner Russell advised that since there didn’t seem to be a lot of changes made in the last five years
      he would make a motion if there was no further discussion.

      MOTION: Commissioner Russell - I move that we approve File #4S22/SUB-000226-2022 based on the
      Findings and Conditions of Approval as stated in the Staff Report. R
      SECOND: Commissioner Wilkey
      VOTE: All Commissioners present voted in favor. Motion passes unanimously.

CITY COUNCIL MEETING DISCUSSION: Commissioner Wilkey briefed the Commissioners on what had transpired
during the June 1st City Council meeting she was assigned to attend.             She reiterated that the main topics
involved the Budget for 22-23, Taylorsville Dayzz information, the Starry Night event, which was not as well attended
as the City Administration would have liked but Mayor Overson had commented that she was sure the participation
would grow when people start hearing about it more. There was also a complaint from a lady saying she did not feel
the water feature out front of City Hall was a good idea during the drought now being experienced.

was adjourned at 7:21 p.m.
