Minutes of the
Millcreek Community Reinvestment Agency
June 10, 2019
7:00 p.m.
Regular Meeting

The Community Reinvestment Agency of Millcreek, Utah, met in a regular meeting on June 10,
2019 at City Hall, located at 3330 S. 1300 E., Millcreek, UT 84106.

PRESENT:

Board Members City Staff

Jeff Silvestrini, Chair John Brems, City Attorney

Silvia Catten John Geilmann, City Manager

Dwight Marchant (excused) Elyse Greiner, Agency Recorder

Cheri Jackson Rita Lund, Communications & Programs Director
Bev Uipi Kurt Hansen, City Services Director

Laurie Johnson, HR-Finance Director
Francis Lilly, Community Development Director

Attendees: Ralph Morelli

REGULAR MEETING — 7:00 p.m.
TIME COMMENCED: 9:07 p.m.

Chair Silvestrini called the meeting to order.

1. Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 19-13, Adopting the Community
Reinvestment Agency Annual Budget for the Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2018 and
Ending June 30, 2019

Chair Silvestrini said the Agency did not have any money, so they did not previously adopt a
budget for the current fiscal year.

Chair Silvestrini asked for public comment. There were no comments.

Board Member Uipi moved to approve Resolution 19-13. Board Member Jackson
seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The motion
passed unanimously.

2. Discussion and Consideration of Resolution 19-14, Adopting the Community
Reinvestment Agency Annual Budget for the Fiscal Year Beginning July 1, 2019 and
Ending June 30, 2020

Board Member Uipi moved to approve Resolution 19-14. Board Member Catten seconded.

Chair Silvestrini asked for public comment.

Ralph Morelli, 1527 E. Woodland Avenue, asked where information on the Community
Reinvestment Agency was available for the public. Chair Silvestrini said information could be
found on the City’s website and added that the City Council functions as the Community
Reinvestment Agency Board.

Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The motion passed
unanimously.

3. Approval of May 13, 2019 Meeting Minutes

Board Member Catten moved to approve the May 13" meeting minutes. Board Member
Uipi seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted yes. The
motion passed unanimously.

ADJOURNED: Board Member Uipi moved to adjourn the meeting at 9:10 p.m. Board
Member Jackson seconded. Chair Silvestrini called for the vote. All Board Members voted
yes. The motion passed unanimously.
