Proposed Ordinance 2020-49 amending the Fiscal Year 2021 Budget in the
amount of $600,000 as an interagency transfer to the Redevelopment
Agency
          A memo from the City Comptroller regarding a proposed FY2021 budget amendment came before the Council for
consideration. The memo stated Administration has requested Council consideration of a proposed budget amendment which
would allocate $600,000 of Business Depot Ogden (BDO) Lease Revenue funds to the Redevelopment Agency (RDA) for the
Continental Project Area. This would increase the project account to $866,231. If approved, the proposal is for $570,000 of these
funds to be used to pay off the current Ogden Industrial Development Corporation (OIDC) loan and for the remaining account
balance of $296,231 to be used for plat work, architectural and civil design work for the old Browning Ice Cream Building and
additional enviromnental remediation at the site.
          Comptroller Stout summarized the memo and provided an overview of the balance available of BDO Lease Revenue
retained earnings; the balance as of July 1, 2020 was $6.9 million. The FY2021 budget contemplates allocations to various
projects, including the Hostess Site/Continental Community Reinvestment Area (CRA); Gramercy Square; Gibson Grove, and the
Swift Building site. If these allocations are approved, the uncommitted balance will be reduced to $4.6 million.
          Community and Economic Development Deputy Director Cooper reviewed the expenditures that have been made to date
in the CRA, including property acquisition, site work and demolition, and debt service payments totaling $3 .3 million. One third-
party property owner that the City has been working with to ensure the success of the project area plan is the Utah State Courts.
They own a piece of the western parking area that is vital for the success of the property and Administration is working with them
to negotiate a land transfer and development agreement to protect the interests of both parties. The City is seeking this $600,000
appropriation to repay the principal due to OIDC, fund plat work, architectural and civil design work for the old Browning Ice
Cream Building, and additional enviromnental remediation at the site. Completion of the master plan for the entire Wonder Block
redevelopment project is nearing and there will be additional actions for the Council to take as the project moves forward.
          Council member Stephens asked whether the City will be responsible to construct the parking garage for the Utah State
Courts based upon the terms of the land transfer and development agreement. Mr. Cooper answered yes; the Courts have indicated
they will only work with another government entity (the City or RDA) rather than a private third-party participant in the project.

Proposed Resolution 2020-30 establishing the Ogden City Youth Council
and the Ogden City Youth Leadership Academy
          A memo from Council staff came before the Council to consider a resolution establishing the Ogden City Youth Council
and Leadership Academy. The memo stated, following discussions over the past several months regarding the creation of a Youth
Council, the City Council asked Council staff and a representative from the City's Legal Department to review the pros and cons
of creating a Youth Council operating under the auspices of the City. The Council felt it was important for the youth to understand
the responsibilities that come with being a City-sponsored organization. This concept was provided to residents and youth
interested in creating the Youth Council and they felt offering both a Youth Council and Leadership Academy would provide a
wider variety of opportunities for students to learn and participate in civic dialogue. On October 13, 2020 the Council met in a
work session to review the final proposal. Council directed that the Youth Academy start as soon as possible but agreed to
postponing the implementation of the Youth Council until the Fall of 2021.
          Council Executive Director Eller-Smith summarized the memo and made a PowerPoint presentation to update the
Council on the matter and summarize the proposal to proceed with creation of the Council and Academy. The organization of the
Youth Council includes:
               7. Organization:
                        a. Membership. The Youth Council shall consist of nine (9) qualified youths.
                        b. Terms. Each Youth Council member may serve up to two (2) one-year terms.
                        c. Officers. A Chair and Vice Chair shall be elected by the membership of the Youth Council.
               8. Advisory Group. At least one but not more than three members of the City Council will act as advisors for the
                   Youth Council. Citizen volunteers may also act in advisory capacities.
               9. Rules of procedure and established norms. The Youth Council shall establish their own rules of procedure.
                   Meetings shall be conducted in accordance with the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act.
               10. Removal. Members of the Youth Council may be removed by a majority vote of the Youth Council.
               11. Powers and duties. The Youth Council's powers and duties shall include but are not limited to:
                        a. Preparing and presenting a proposed budget.
                        b. Encouraging the youth to participate in and give volunteer service City-wide.
                        c. Encouraging youth participation in the Youth Leadership Academy.
                        d. Attending the Utah League of Cities and Towns Local Government Day.
                        e. Recommending programs to involve other volunteer youth in activities benefiting the City.
                        f. Presenting an annual report.
               12. Conflicts of interest. Members of the Youth Council shall be subject to the Municipal Officers' and Employees
                   Ethics Act, Section 10-3-1301, et seq.
          Ms. Eller-Smith then summarized the proposal to create an Ogden Leadership Academy is summarized as follows:
               5. Organization:
                        a. Membership. Members shall consist of all members of the Youth Council and all other qualified
                            youths who apply to attend the Leadership Academy.
                        b. Tenns. All members of the Leadership Academy shall serve for a te1m coinciding with the academic
                            school year established by the Ogden School District. Each member may serve up to two (2) one-year
                            terms.
                        c. Officers. The Chair of the Youth Council shall appoint a member of the Youth Council to conduct the
                            Leadership Academy Meetings.
               6. Advisory Group. At least one but not more than three members of the City Council will act as advisors for the

                        a. A review of the Council/Mayor fonn of government in Ogden City.
