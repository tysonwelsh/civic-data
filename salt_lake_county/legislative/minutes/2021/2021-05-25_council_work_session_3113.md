3.3 Policy 1302 - Official Flags on County Facilities and Property 21-0626
Attachments: Staff Report
Flag Policy - Updated
Sponsors: Council Member Laurie Stringham and Council Chair Steve DeBry 
(Approx. 10 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Council Member Stringham  explained the policy, which will establish 
standards and guidelines for the display of flags outside of Salt Lake County 
facilities and on County properties.
Mr. Mitchell Park , Legal Counsel, Council Office, stated the policy makes 
note of the Salt Lake County flag, which currently does not exist.  However, 
it would be covered if one were created.
Mr. Ralph Chamness , Deputy District Attorney, stated he had raised the 
issue regarding large conferences at the Salt Palace that would like to display 
a flag or banner as part of their contract.
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated her staff would have come prepared to 
address this issue if they had been asked.  
A motion was made by Council Member Stringham, seconded by Council 
Member Newton, to approve the policy.
Council Member Alvord  stated he would favor approving the policy with 
the elimination of the following paragraph “g.”
2.1  The following flags may be publicly displayed on Salt 
Lake County flag standards:
g. A flag temporarily flown at County facilities by 
vendors or contractors of the County consistent with the terms 
of their agreement(s) with the County.
A substitute motion was made by Council Member Alvord to approve the 
policy with the removal of paragraph 2.1 (g).

Attachments: Staff Report
Uninc Projects Staff Report
Presenters: Council Member Laurie Stringham; Kade Moncur, Division 
Director, Engineering and Flood Control; and Madeline Francisco -Galang, 
Transportation Engineering Manager, Engineering and Flood Control (Approx. 
5 mins). 
Discussion/Direction

Ms. Madeline Francisco -Galang, Transportation Engineering Manager, 
Engineering & Flood Control Division, delivered a PowerPoint presentation 
explaining the seven new projects in the unincorporated areas and their 
ranking.  She also noted the differences in requests from the east side of the 
Salt Lake Valley to the west side.
Council Member Granato  asked that the Council be given the metrics used 
in decision making before a request comes to the Council.
Mr. Scott Baird , Director, Public Works Department, stated his office can 
provide the metrics used to rank these items.  Council Member Stringham is 
the Council ’s representative on the MSD board for the unincorporated areas 
and they have worked with her to come up with this process.  These projects 
will go into the same list as the west side projects. 
Mr. Kade Moncur , Director, Engineering & Flood Control Division, stated 
he has the metrics and will provide them to the Council as requested.  Most 
of these recommended projects are on the master plan.  Other projects are 
on the west side and they tend to be quite large.
Ms. Catherine Kanter , Deputy Mayor of Regional Development, stated 
these requests come primarily from community councils and there are no 
community councils on the west side.

3.8 Update on Salt Lake County ’s Coordinated Response to 
COVID-19
21-0622
Attachments: Staff Report
County Council Briefing -- 05252021
Vaccination Briefing to Council -- 05252021
Presenters: Mayor Jenny Wilson; Gary Edwards, Director, Health Department; 
and David Schuld, COVID Coordinating Officer (Approx. 30 mins). 
Informational
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated she is looking for ideas to incentivize 
employees to get vaccinated.  Hot dogs were a draw at the Mountain 
America Expo Center.
Mr. Gary Edwards , Director, Salt Lake County Health Department, stated 
Covid cases in Salt Lake County are down by 14 percent from the previous 
week.  That has been two consecutive weeks of decline, and the County is 
close to meeting the low transmission threshold.  Schools have been doing 
well, and over one million doses of the vaccine have been administered.
Mr. David Schuld , COVID -19 Response and Recovery Coordinator, 
delivered a PowerPoint presentation.  He reviewed key numbers, the active 
case curve, an infection snapshot, zip code surveillance, cases by age, and 
testing analysis.
Mr. Edwards delivered a PowerPoint presentation covering the County ’s 
COVID-19 vaccination update, including number of first and seconded 
doses administered in Salt Lake County.

Attachments: Staff Report
SBF - Presentation
SBF - Report
TIF Strategic Reset
Presenters: Jevon Gibb, Director, Economic Development and S .B. Friedman, 
Consulting Staff (Approx. 1 hr). 
Informational

Mr. Jevon Gibb , Director, Economic Development Division, stated this Tax 
Increment Financing (TIF) Strategy Review will allow his office to come in 
alignment with the Council, which is the decision -making board.  Details of 
this presentation will show how this proposal would be aligned with Council 
goals, which require a strong, effective partnership with cities.  He then 
delivered a PowerPoint presentation reviewing issues, goals, deliverables, and 
next steps.
Ms. Fran Lefor Rood , Senior Vice President, SB Friedman, continued the 
PowerPoint covering the TIF policy evaluation findings and 
recommendations.  She covered project goals, Utah TIF and policy 1155, 
and project and interview findings.
Mr. Lance Dorn , Vice President, SB Friedman, continued the PowerPoint 
covering best practices, case studies, and recommendations.
Council Member Granato  spoke of a situation in which areas in her district 
were designated as “blighted.”  The terms caused extreme distress to the 
residents.
Ms. Dina Blaes , Director, Regional Development Department, stated the 
term “blight” was used in Utah Code.  That term has now been changed to 
“development impediment.”
