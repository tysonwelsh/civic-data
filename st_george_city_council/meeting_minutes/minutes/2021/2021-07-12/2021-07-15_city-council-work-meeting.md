OPENING:

     invocation was offered by Councilmember Curtis and The Pledge of Allegiance to the
     Flag was led by Mayor Randall.

UPDATE FROM STAFF REGARDING THE PROPOSED SANDSTONE FBO:
     Airport Operations Specialist Dustin Warren presented a PowerPoint presentation
     covering the following topics: Sandstone Aviation total leased area; FAA ramp space
     – Exhibit A (addendum item 2); Road improvements – Exhibit B Phase 1 (addendum
     item 3); Road Improvements – Exhibit B Phase 2 (addendum item 3); Temporary
     facilities – Exhibit C (addendum item 4); Fuel farm proposal – Exhibit D (addendum
     item 5); Jet fuel truck – 5,000 gallons; Enviro tanker – 10,600 gallons; 100LL tank –
     10,000 gallons; Construction Phase 1; Construction Phase 2; Construction Phase 3;
     Construction Phase 4; Sandstone estimated airport infrastructure investment; and
     Sandstone proposed cost to the City of St. George. After being asked by City
     Manager Adam Lenhard, Mr. Warren stated he did not know what the proposed lease
     rate is. The City has not increased these fees since 2011; the City’s lease rate is
     fairly low compared to other municipalities. He believes this proposal would be a
     benefit to the airport as staff feels there is a need for additional hangar space. There
     are proposed to be shade hangars, but there is no space for higher end aircraft. The
     ramp was built for two (2) FBOs; however, currently one (1) FBO utilizes the entire
     ramp.

       City Manager Adam Lenhard explained the City is required to have an FBO. He
       prefers the option of pursuing the FAA grants and is okay with the rent credits;
       however, he believes Sandstone should pay the associated costs.

       Joey Campbell with Sandstone noted in Exhibit A the rent space was provided for the
       existing FBO.

       City Manager Adam Lenhard asked the Council to think over the proposal and let he
       or staff know if they have any questions. He noted the proposal will return for
       approval at a future City Council meeting.

UPDATE FROM TECH RIDGE:
     Isaac Barlow with Tech Ridge provided a PowerPoint presentation covering the
     following topics: The exclusive newsletter of Tech Ridge: Completed projects; Under
     construction; Projects pending zone plan approval; Tech Ridge hangar retrofit –
     Zonos; Additional projects in process; Photos of the proposed buildings and
     structures; Conceptual Master Plan; Tech partners; 2021 salary data; Tech
     companies committed; Buildout; Comparison; and Resources.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted up to this point, there has been an agreement
      allows the property to be taken down in phase 1. In order to do anything with
      property to the south, two things need to happen: approve a zone plan and a
      development agreement. Staff is reviewing both of these documents and most of
      the issues have been resolved.

      The Council moved to the City Council Chambers for the regular meeting.
