                                    RIVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 25-45

 SUPPORTING AMERICA250 UTAH AND RECOGNIZING AND APPROVING THE
          RIVERTON CITY UTAH250 COMMUNITY COMMITTEE
      WHEREAS, Governor Spencer J. Cox and the Utah State Legislature created the
America250 Utah Commission (also known as America250 Utah); and

        WHEREAS, the mission of America250 Utah is to commemorate and celebrate, reflect
on our nation’s past, build community, and look toward the future by educating, engaging, and
uniting Utahns and visitors to our state; and

        WHEREAS, America250 Utah is seeking partnerships with counties and municipalities
to further its mission; and

       WHEREAS, this partnership will be formed by creating a local committee called the
Riverton Utah250 Community Committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton Utah250 Community Committee will focus on important
events, people, and places within Riverton to commemorate and celebrate Riverton’s role in
America’s 250th anniversary; and

       WHEREAS, local projects will enhance tourism, community building, and economic
development opportunities.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

   1. The Riverton City Council hereby recognizes the Riverton Utah250 Community
      Committee as its official committee.
   2. The Riverton City Mayor is authorized to partner with America250 Utah by attending
      meetings and functions, organized by America250, or sending appropriate designees.
   3. The Riverton City Council supports signature programs of the America250 Utah
      Commission, but subject to appropriate budgetary restrictions.
   4. The Riverton City Council supports the Riverton Utah250 Community Committee in its
      local efforts to educate, engage, and unify Utahns and our visitors in Riverton, subject to
      appropriate budgetary restrictions.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 16th day of
September 2025 by the following vote:
