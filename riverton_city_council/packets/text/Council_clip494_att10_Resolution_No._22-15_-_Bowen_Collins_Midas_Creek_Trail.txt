                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 22-15

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
 WITH BOWEN COLLINS & ASSOCIATES FOR THE ENGINEERED DESIGN OF
          THE MIDAS CREEK TRAIL IMPROVEMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council required that the project must come back for final
approval before proceeding; and

        WHEREAS, Purchasing has requested Statement of Qualifications from perspective
firms; and

       WHEREAS, Bowen Collins & Associates has been selected provide an engineered
design for the project.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Bowen Collins
            & Associates for the engineered design of the Midas Creek Trail Improvement
            Project.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
February 2022 by the following vote:
