                         NEPHI CITY WORK SESSION MINUTES
                                   January 14, 2025
                                       7:00 P.M
                                      21 E 100 N
                                   Nephi, UT 84648

1.    Jim Ockey addressed the council about his water/easement agreement he has with the
     city. Mayor Seely informed Mr. Ockey that the council would no longer discuss the
     agreement with Mr. Ockey. Mayor Seely stated that Mr. Ockey had signed the
     agreement and the city officials and staff had met with Mr.Ockey about the issue many
     times and they were finished discussing the issue. Mr. Ockey stated that the city had
     breached the agreement and asked the question “what was free water?” He said that he
     had paid for his water by giving the city an easement through his property.

2. Budget Requests:
        a. Greater Nephi Chamber of Commerce-
      Jodie Cox President and Brady Jones Executive Director addressed the council and
   reported that the chamber is implementing new software, new website and member
   directory
     The new website has the ability to create a micro site on the chamber website for each
   member business. The website will have a Nephi marketplace where residents and
   businesses can visit that will host job openings, business directory, offers, flyers,
   opportunities for other businesses, and calendars. There will be an app that residents
   will be able to download. The chamber wants the city to promote the app to residents.
      The Chamber will have a texting campaign where the Chamber can text out specials
   that will take them to the marketplace.
      Ms. Cox said that her top goal is to have the executive director’s wage high enough to
   support a high-level professional in that position.
     Ms. Cox and Mr. Jones requested the same contribution that they had received in the
   past of $15,000 annually from the city.
      Megan Wootten representing Bar W Beef said that they are planning on using the
   Chamber’s marketplace and as a business perspective they are really excited about
   using the platform to promote Bar W Beef.

        b. Shauna Talbot- City Planner

                            UDOT planning grant we’ve applied for through the assistance of
                            Jones & DeMille engineering that we hope to be awarded this

         iii.   Purchase and train on the following software:
                    1. Blue Beam $440 per year, per user - other public works

        e. Mike Booth - Golf Course
           Director of Golf Mike Booth provided an update on the golf course' s financial
           status and future plans. He stated that the golf course has been profitable for the

4.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a draft agreement for legal services with Smith
     Hartvisen PLLC. The legal firm would provide legal advice on some potential data
     center development projects.

5. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the city’s personnel policy needed some
   clarification on the drug-free workplace portion of the policy, particularly regarding CDL
   license holders and random drug testing requirements. The policy revisions also include
   the addition of counseling services availability for anyone experiencing substance abuse
   issues. The council asked about what substances were tested for in the random drug
   testing. Finance Director Lisa Brough was not sure what substances were being tested
   for.

6. Mr. Atkinson updated the council that an old garbage truck needed to be declared
   surplus property so that the city could try to sell the truck. The council agreed to declare
   the truck surplus property of the city.

7.   Administrative and City Council Updates:
       a. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that property tax revenues were coming in
           above budgeted amounts.
       b. Mr. Atkinson discussed the need for elected officials to sign conflict of interest
           disclosure forms and the process for submitting the forms that will be linked on
           the city website.
       c. Mayor Seely said he had been asked if the council would support a commuter
           rail through Nephi. The council asked Mr. Atkinson if the people proposing the
           idea could come and give more information about the project.
       d. Councilor Cowan said that a resident had complained about lack of lighting on
           the west side of Canyon View Park and that there is no curb and gutter along that
           side.
           e. Councilor Cowan said that she had another complaint about the traffic on 4th
              East and 700 North. A suggestion of a roundabout could be a possible remedy
              for the traffic problem.
           f. Councilor Cowan asked what the current budget was for Miss Nephi. Mr.
              Atkinson answered that it was $9,000.
           g. Councilor Travis Worwood asked if Mr. Atkinson would contact UDOT about the
              lights on the center exit.
           h. Mayor Seely mentioned that an individual had requested to use a room at city
              hall for AA meetings. The council agreed to allow it.

Mayor Seely opened a regular meeting.
Councilor Skip Worwood moved to convene into closed session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously.
