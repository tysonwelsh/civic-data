Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: November 10, 2020
Re: Short Subdivision Zoning Map Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment for approximately 1.5 acres located on the north side of 13200 South, at approximately 1017 East. The property is currently zoned RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 ft² lot minimum). The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map Amendment be approved to allow for potential future subdivision of the property.
The Planning Commission heard this item at their September 3, 2020 meeting and forwarded a positive recommendation on the application with a 5-0 vote.
The City Council held a public hearing on this item at their September 15, 2020 meeting, closed the public hearing and subsequently continued decision on the item by a 5-0 vote to their October 6, 2020 meeting.
The applicant's engineer provided additional printed information to city staff desiring consideration of the additional information by City Council members after the public hearing was closed, but prior to the October 6, 2020 City Council meeting. This information was not conveyed to the City Council members prior to the meeting.
At the October 6, 2020 public meeting the City Council on a 3-2 vote denied the application.
After the October 6, 2020 meeting the applicant requested that the City Council reconsider the matter and give the applicant an opportunity to submit the additional information for consideration. That information is attached to this memo as the  Ptarmigan Pointe Phase 2 Plats.
This item was added to the October 20, 2020 City Council agenda for a vote to reconsider their initial decision. At the October 20, 2020 meeting the City Council voted 5-0 to reconsider the decision and hold another public hearing on the item to allow both the applicant to submit the additional information as well as give opportunity to the public to respond to the new information.  The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. That Section 9-5-060 of the Draper City Municipal Code allows for the amendment of the city's zoning map;
2. That all findings for a zone map change, as contained in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E), are satisfied;
3. That adequate facilities and services exits to serve the subject property including but not limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm water drainage systems, water supplies and waste water and refuse collection.
