City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 20, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1583, approving a text
amendment to Titles 9 and 17 of Draper City Municipal Code in order to amend
regulations regarding definitions, site plan review, site design, landscaping including
water-wise landscaping, screening, parking area design, outdoor storage and display,
improvement completion assurances, and related subdivision requirements, to City
Initiated Water-Wise Landscaping and Site Amendments Text Amendment. The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 22nd day of June 2023. Laura Oscarson, Draper
City Recorder.
