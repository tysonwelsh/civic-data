1. WORK SESSION REGARDING THE 2026 UTAH COUNTY BUDGET FOR THE GENERAL

(Timestamp: 24:00)

DISCUSSION WITH KIM T. JACKSON (Utah County Treasurer) AND CARY MCCONNELL
(Chief Deputy Treasurer) ON DEPARTMENT NEEDS, GETTING TAXPAYERS TO SIGN UP
FOR E-NOTICES, PRINTING NEEDS, SWITCHING THEIR CREDIT CARD PROCESSOR.

SHERIFF DEPARTMENT REVIEWED THEIR PRIORITY LIST WITH THE
COMMISSIONERS INCLUDING POSITIONS FOR JAIL MEDICAL, OVERTIME, GRANT
WITH THE DOJ, SUICIDE BARRIERS, RISE, PRE-TRIAL SERVICES. MIKE SMITH
(Sheriff), PATRICK CARLSON (Fire Chief), NANNETTE JACKSON (Sheriff Business Manager),
SHAUN BUFTON (Under Sheriff.

(Timestamp: 1:10:00)

AUDITOR DEPARTMENT REVIEWED THEIR REQUEST LIST, INCLUDING HIRING A
NEW AUDITOR TO REPLACE MONT WADE. RUDY LIVINGTON (Chief Deputy) AND ROD
MANN (Utah County Auditor). DISCUSSION WITH ALL COMMISSIONERS.

(Timestamp: 1:20:40)

CHILDREN’S JUSTICE CENTER DISCUSSED THEIR BUDGET REQUEST LIST AND
MENTIONED A NEW GRANT, FUNDRAISERS FOR FUNDING THERAPY FOR
CHILDREN WHO CANNOT AFFORD IT. REBECCA MARTELL (Executive Director) AND
HEATHER ALLEN (Associate Director) EXPRESSED THANKS TO THE COMMISSIONERS
FOR THEIR SUPPORT.

(Timestamp: 1:32:40)

JUSTICE COURT DISCUSSED THEIR NEEDS INCLUDING A NEW RECORDING DEVICE,
INTERPRETER SERVICES, GROWING CASE LOADS IN THE COUNTY, REASONS WHY
INTERLOCAL AGREEMENTS WILL NOT WORK. K. SHAWN PATTEN (justice Court Judge),
EMILY TRAFNY (Justice Court Administrator) THANKED THE COMMISSIONERS FOR
THEIR SUPPORT AND SAID THEY HAVE NOT HAD ANY TURNOVER IN TWO YEARS.
