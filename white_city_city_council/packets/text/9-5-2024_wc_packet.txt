                     Seasonal Container Reservation Program (SCRP) Stats

The 2024 SCRP season marks the fifth year of this program. With this model, we
service an average of 10% of the homes in each of our cities and towns.

We are also able to allow more than one day to reserve for each neighborhood
zone than the old model, which had one day per neighborhood.

Annual container services run mid-April through September and started in
Millcreek this year moving clockwise throughout the District servicing
approximately 85,000 homes.

The District services 1,824 homes in White City and our team was able to
accommodate 179 of the 185 requested reservations during services starting on
Friday, June 28th through Tuesday, July 2nd. We collected 162 tons of bulky waste.

For the past three years our board has been reviewing ideas posed by residents
on how to expand this service to more residents.

One idea that is being considered is to limit repeat reservations, which would
mean those who received a container in 2024, would not be eligible in 2025
unless the waitlist was exhausted. Anyone on the waitlist in 2024, would have
“first dibs” in 2025.

The 2025 Season begins mid-April in Murray & Holladay and will move clockwise
throughout the District.

Considerations: Wages are the highest increase year over year due to the rapidly rising salary markets especially for CDL drivers. A recent example: On Monday, August 26,
2024, our board approved a 7.61% salary market adjustment for our CDL drivers to stay within markets. This adjustment is doable without a budget adjustment due to
under expenditures from driver vacancies, which is a double-edged sword.

• The last fee increased was implemented in 2023 with $2.50 per home/month increase moving to the current $19.50 per mo./$58.50 per qtr./$234 annual.

There was a five-year period since 2018 when fees were raised $2.25 per home/per month, to $17.00 from $14.75 per month. Prior to 2018, fees increased in 2014 with
$2.00 per month from $12.75 per month to $14.75.

Staff have engaged with Zions for financial advisory services as we work to manage the rising costs for providing waste and recycling collection services for our residents.
We are seeking advice and recommendations on fee structures and billing frequency, revenues and options for funding sources that may include leasing heavy equipment.
Incurring debt would be a first for WFWRD but our board may choose this as an option to reduce the amount of a needed fee increase.

                                   RESOLUTION NO. 2024-09-01

                A RESOLUTION CONFIRMING THE APPOINTMENT OF
              ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT (ADU) COMMITTEE MEMBERS

WHEREAS, White City is recognized as a municipality in accordance with Utah Code §§ 10-2a-201
et seq.; and

WHEREAS, the White City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the residents of
White City to explore the potential benefits and drawbacks of permitting Accessory Dwelling Units
(ADUs) within the municipality; and

WHEREAS, the White City Council seeks to establish an ADU Committee and appoint the following
individuals: Council Member Tyler Huish, Council Member Linda Price, Morgan Julian (Long Range
Planner), and Robert Frailey, to serve until such time as the committee is no longer deemed necessary;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE WHITE CITY COUNCIL as follows:

SECTION 1. The White City Council hereby appoints Council Member Tyler Huish, Council
Member Linda Price, Morgan Julian (Long Range Planner), and Robert Frailey to serve as members
of the White City ADU Committee until the committee is dissolved.

SECTION 2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its passage.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 5th day of September, 2024 by the White City Council, White City,
Utah.
