CITY OF LOGAN
ORDINANCE NO. 23-25

AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PUBLIC UTILITY EASEMENT
LOCATED ON PARCEL NUMBER 06-017-0001

WHEREAS, Logan City ("City"), as the owner of parcel number 06-017-0001 ("Property"), has requested that the Logan Municipal Council vacate a public utility easement and an access easement recorded on this Property;

WHEREAS, Utah Code authorizes the City to vacate public streets, rights of way, and public utility easements, from use by the public;

WHEREAS, the existing public utility easement and access easement are no longer required because the City will dedicate a new public utility easement along the outside boundary of the former 2 Properties; and the access easement will no longer be needed.

WHEREAS, the Logan Municipal Council finds there is good cause for vacating the public utility easement and access easement and finds such action shall not materially injure the public or any person; and

WHEREAS, the proposed vacation of the public utility easement and access easement as shown and described in EXHIBIT A, has been reviewed by the City and the City Council, and all appropriate hearings, postings, and notifications have been performed in accordance with Utah law.

THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF LOGAN, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1: Pursuant to Section 10-9a-609.5, Utah Code Annotated, 1953 the public utility easement and access easement shown in Exhibit A and described in Exhibit B are hereby abandoned and vacated.

SECTION 2: Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon publication.

The City of Logan petitions to vacate a public utility easement on property located at 95 East 200 North, Logan, Utah. You are being notified per Utah State Law Code 10-9a-609.5 because you are listed as an owner of record of a parcel of property that is adjacent to the public utility easements, within 300 feet of the public utility easements, or are an operator of utilities, culinary water, or sanitary sewer facilities entity that may provide public utilities in the area that is being petitioned to vacate.

Please see attached image, Exhibit A, of the area outlined in red that is being petitioned.

A City Council workshop on the issue will be held on:

Date: July 18, 2023
Time: 5:30 PM
Location: City Hall 290 North 100 West Logan, UT 84321

A Public Hearing and City Council meeting to take action on the issue is scheduled to be held on:

Date August 1, 2023
Time: 5:30 PM
Location: City Hall 290 North 100 West Logan, UT 84321

If you support the vacation of the public utility easement, the petition will be available in the Engineering office at City Hall, for signing until 5:00 PM on the date indicated for the public hearing.

If you have any additional questions, please call Darren Farar, City of Logan City Engineer at (435)716-9160 or Darren.farar@loganutah.org.

If you are opposed to the action, please email or mail a letter of opposition to Darren Farar, City of Logan City Engineer at Darren.farar@loganutah.org or 290 N 100 W, Logan, Utah 84321 and / or present your opposition at the Public Hearing.
