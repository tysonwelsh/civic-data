    5. INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT BY/AMONG WEBER COUNTY, OGDEN CITY & UTAH TRANSIT
       AUTHORITY FOR ACQUISITION OF RIGHT-OF-WAYS FOR OGDEN CITY COMMUTER RAIL/BDO STATION –
       RESOLUTION 9-2021
         Brooke Stewart, with County Community Development, stated that in 2018 Ogden City, in cooperation
         with UTA, submitted an application requesting Corridor Preservation Funds to preserve the needed right-
         of-way to extend commuter rail north of Ogden. The Weber Area Council of Governments (WACOG)
         recommended a $3 million corridor preservation award to acquire right-of-way from the Ogden 23rd Street
         Hub Station, north to BDO, including purchase of property for the station site at BDO. WACOG’s
         recommendation included the stipulation that once funding was received to construct the project that the
         $3 million be reimbursed to the Corridor Preservation Fund. This is addressed in the agreement. The
         County Commission approved WACOG’s recommendation as part of the 2018 project priority list.
         An amendment request was submitted in 2019 and WACOG recommended a portion of the $3 million to
         be used towards the necessary survey work and documents preparation for the whole length of the corridor
         from the Hub Station north to the Weber County/Box Elder County boundary. Ms. Stewart said that these
         funds were programmed for 2021 and that Weber County had been told that the Box Elder portion was
         about 90% complete with its acquisitions. Once this funding agreement is approved, Weber County’s
         portion of the project can begin promptly. This agreement was adopted by UTA Board of Trustees and
         Ogden City Council resolutions. Commissioner Jenkins expressed support for the reimbursement
         requirement. Ms. Stewart addressed Chair Harvey’s question stating that the remaining acquisitions
         between downtown’s Union Station and the north border that would eventually connect into Box Elder
         County included the portion at the BDO Station to the border. She said that there had been discussion
         about Harrisville, Marriott-Slaterville, and Pleasant View cities preserving some of that right-of-way
         through their planning practices, but the survey work was not yet complete. Hal Johnson, UTA’s manager
         of project development, was participating via zoom in the event there had been any questions.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to adopt Resolution 9-2021 approving the Interlocal Cooperation
         Agreement by and among Weber County, Ogden City and the Utah Transit Authority for acquisition of
         right-of-ways for the Ogden City Commuter Rail/BDO Station; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye

    6. AMENDMENT TO ORDINANCE AMENDING CERTAIN ORDINANCES AND REPEALING ORDINANCE 30-3-11 –
       ORDINANCE 2021-2
         Matthew Wilson, Deputy County Attorney, stated that this was a housekeeping item for conflicting code
         sections. Section 26-2-1 was adopted in 2004 and at that time Section 30-3-11 should have been repealed.
         This relates to prohibited swimming, use of canoes, kayaks, etc., in or on Causey Reservoir, unless the
         person is properly wearing a Coast Guard-approved life jacket or supervised by a certified lifeguard.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to adopt Ordinance 2021-2, amendment to an ordinance amending certain
         ordinances and repealing Ordinance 30-3-11; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
