Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Jonathan Concidine
CC: Jennifer Jastremsky Date: October 4, 2022
Re: Avery Townhomes - Development Agreement, Land Use Map Amendment, and Zoning Map Amendment Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement and Land Use & Zoning Map Amendments for approximately 2.44 acres located between 12300 S. and 12220 S. on the north and south, and Placer St. and 670 E. on the east and west, at approximately 650 E. 12225 S. The applicant is requesting that Land Use & Zoning Map Amendments be approved to allow for the development of four townhomes units in addition to the 17 that have been previously approved, and the Development Agreement be approved to limit the number of units that can be built to 21.
The Planning Commission heard the Land Use and Zoning Map Amendment requests on August 28, 2022 and voted for a positive recommendation, 5-0. The Commission heard the Development Agreement request on September 22, 2022 and voted for a positive recommendation, 5-0. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.    The proposed zoning and land use amendments are consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the city's general plan.
2.       The proposed amendments are harmonious with the overall character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
3.     The proposed amendments are consistent with the standards of any applicable overlay zone;
4.        The proposed amendments should not adversely affect adjacent property.
5.        The facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection, are adequate.
6.       The proposed land use map amendment complies with DCMC Sections 9-2-020(F) and 9-5-060(D).
7.    Th e development agreement meets the standards related to procedure and limitations for development agreements.
8.         The terms of the development agreement are acceptable to Draper City.
For denial:
1.            The proposed land use and zoning map amendments are not consistent with goals, objectives and policies of the city's general plan.
2.            The proposed amendments are not harmonious with the overall character of existing development in the vicinity of the subject property.
3.            The proposed amendments are not consistent with the standards of any applicable overlay zone.
4.            The proposed amendments are likely to adversely affect adjacent property.
5.            The facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and wastewater and refuse collection, are inadequate.
6.            The proposed land use map amendment does not comply with DCMC Sections 9-2-020(F) and 9-5-060(D).
7.        The development agreement does not meet the standards related to procedure and limitations for development agreements.
8.        The terms of the development agreement are not acceptable to Draper City.
