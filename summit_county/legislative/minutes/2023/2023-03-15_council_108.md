The Council was called to order at 1:32 P.M.

d.
Discussion and approval of Proclamation 2023-03 a Proclamation Recognizing Darren Backman for 20 Years of Public Service to the Citizens of Summit County, Utah; Kacey Bates (4:55 P.M.)
Kacey Bates, Summit County Sheriff Captain, and Council Member Canice Harte presented Proclamation 2023-03 recognizing Darren Backman’s service to Summit County.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve Proclamation 2023-03 a Proclamation recognizing Darren Backman for 20 Years of Public Service to the Citizens of Summit County, Utah. Chris Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Council Members expressed their gratitude. Mr. Backman thanked everyone for the recognition.
Captain Bates, Chief Deputy Frank Smith, and Lieutenant Jon Evans presented Mr. Backman with a plaque.
