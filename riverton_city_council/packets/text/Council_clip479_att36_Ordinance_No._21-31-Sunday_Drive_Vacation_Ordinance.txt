                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 21-31

   AN ORDINANCE VACATING AN EXISTING UNDEVELOPED PORTION OF A
   PUBLIC RIGHT OF WAY, SUNDAY DRIVE, TO THE ADJACENT PROPERTY
               OWNER TO THE WEST, IN RIVERTON CITY
       WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton, Utah, has the authority, pursuant to Utah Code
10-9a-609-3, to vacate a subdivision or portion of a subdivision within Riverton City; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has complied with the public notice requirements of Utah
Code sections 10-9a-208; and,

        WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider the proposed vacation
of an existing undeveloped right-of-way Sunday Drive, as shown on Exhibit “A”, and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that there is good cause to vacate
the portion of the subdivision dedicated to Riverton City, as described herein, and that said
vacation will not be detrimental to the public interest;

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Governing Body of Riverton City that:

       1. The undeveloped right-of-way portion of Sunday Drive as described in Exhibit “A”is
          hereby vacated and the City does hereby revoke any acceptance of the dedication of
          any easements as shown on the recorded plat. The private rights-of-way and
          easements therein, if any, of any lot owner and the franchise rights of any public
          utility shall not be impaired by this action.

       2. This ordinance shall take effect upon execution.

     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 16th day of
November, 2021 by the following vote:
