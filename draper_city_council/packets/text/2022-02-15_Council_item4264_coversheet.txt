Re:  Comments: In 2019, Draper City submitted a Tourism, Recreation, Culture and Convention (TRCC) grant application to receive funding for an all-abilities playground.  The application was selected for funding, however in 2020 due to COVID-19 reasons the funding commitment was removed by SLCO.  Draper City make plans to fund the project and in 2021 the TRCC Funds were restored by SLCO as a reimbursement to Draper City in the original amount of $550,000.
