                              RESOLUTION 2021-R-2

       A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE 2020-2021 FISCAL BUDGETS
                     FOR THE TOWN OF ALTA

Utah Code Annotated, the General Fund budget for the Town of Alta for fiscal year 2021
is amended as follows: Exhibit “A” – Amended Budget for the Town of Alta: 2020-
2021.
