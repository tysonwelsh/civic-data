Vaccination Briefing to Council -- 04202021

Presenters: Mayor Jenny Wilson; David Schuld, Intelligence Section Chief; 
Gary Edwards, Health Department Executive Director (Approx. 30 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Mayor Jennifer Wilson  stated that Angela Dunn will be replacing Gary 
Edwards as director of the Salt Lake County Health Department who is 
retiring.  Negotiations are still ongoing as far as benefits and compensation.
Mr. Gary Edwards , Director, Salt Lake County Health Department, stated 
this week showed a decline of 9.9 percent of new cases as compared to last 
week.  Salt Lake County is below the state ’s threshold of 191 new cases per 
100,000 residents.  It has 163 cases per 100,000. The numbers are showing 
instabilities within different age groups, with number jumping up and down . 
Testing has increased with positivity rates decreasing. Positivity rates are at 
3.3 percent. Hospitalization and ICU continue to improve.  The issue with the 
Johnson and Johnson vaccination has not been resolved. Hopefully, direction 
will be provided by the end of the week.  Vaccination focus is on the 16 to 
18-year-old age range hoping to get the age group its first vaccination before 
summer break. 
Mr. David Schuld , Intelligence Section Chief for COVID -19, delivered a 
PowerPoint presentation on the County ’s COVID -19 Health Response, 
which included key numbers, an active case curve, an infection snapshot, zip 
code surveillance, current cases by age distribution, a rolling 14-day 
comparison, a testing analysis, known versus community cases, and the 
potential exposure source.
Mr. Edwards delivered a PowerPoint presentation on the County ’s 
COVID-19 Health Response Vaccination Update, reviewing a summary, a 
vaccine priority group update, a vaccine administration report, first and 
second doses administered in Salt Lake County, and outreach to vulnerable 
and diverse communities. 
3.5 An Ordinance of the Salt Lake County Council Amending 
the Boundaries of the Mountainous Planning District and the 
Membership Requirements of the Mountainous Planning 
District Planning Commission; and Making Other Related 
Changes
21-0481
Page 3 of 7
Council Work Session Meeting Minutes April 20, 2021
Attachments: Staff Report
MPD Draft Ordinance
Sponsors: Council Chair Steve DeBry and Council Vice Chair Aimee Winder 
Newton (Approx. 5 mins). 
Discussion/Direction
Council Member DeBry stated this ordinance is before the Council today 
as informational.  It needs to go to the Mountainous Planning District 
Commission for its recommendation before it is formally considered by the 
Council. 
Council Member Snelgrove stated he will be a no vote on this item.  Salt 
Lake City, Millcreek, Cottonwood Heights, and Sandy are all guaranteed a 
seat on the board while residents that actually live in the area are not.  Also, 
this ordinance has the potential to increase annexations and /or incorporations 
because residents want to be heard on what is happening in their area and will 
seek local control.
Council Member Theodore stated she will be a no vote as well.  It is not 
local control when residents outside of the district are making decision for 
people inside the district. Local residents should be given preference on this. 
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council 
Member Winder Newton, that this agenda item be approved and forwarded to 
the 4:00 PM Council meeting for ratification. The motion carried by a 7 to 2 
vote with Council Members Snelgrove and Theodore voting in opposition.

3.7 A Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council Approving 
Execution of an Amendment to an Interlocal Cooperation 
Agreement with the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling 
District
21-0465
Attachments: Staff Report
2021-04-13 Resolution for WFWRD 2nd Amendment and 
exhibit 040521.pdf
WFWRD Interlocal Agreement Second Amendment 
Annotated 041421
Presenter: Patrick Craig, Executive Director, Solid Waste (Approx. 5 mins).
Discussion/Direction
Mr. Patrick Craig , Director, Solid Waste Management, stated this is the 
second amendment to the Interlocal Agreement with Wasatch Front Waste 
and Recycling District (WFWRD).  The goal of this amendment is to 
improve the hauling efficiency of WFWRD while allowing the Solid Waste 
Management Division to maintain, stabilize, and preplan for the amount of 
waste it will be receiving. The agreement also extends the time frame by 
approximately 10 years. 
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council 
Member Winder Newton, that this agenda item be approved and forwarded to 
the 4:00 PM Council meeting for ratification. The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.
