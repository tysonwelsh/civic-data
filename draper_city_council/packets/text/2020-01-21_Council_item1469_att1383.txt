                                         RESOLUTION NO. 20-04

       A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL DECLARING PARCEL 34-05-
       303-010, AT 789 EAST CORNER RIDGE DRIVE, AS SURPLUS PROPERTY AND
       AUTHORIZING THE SALE OF THE SAME.

       WHEREAS, Draper City owns approximately .34 acres, parcel 34-05-303-010, Lot 10 of the
Southridge Subdivision Phase A, located at 789 East Corner Ridge Drive in Draper, Salt Lake County,
Utah; and

        WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code, Section 3-3-160 describes the procedures to be followed
to surplus city-owned property; and

        WHEREAS, the City has complied with Section 3-3-160 to properly authorize the sale of such
property, including mailing notice to each owner of property owner within 300 feet and providing reasonable
notice of the proposed disposition at least 14 days prior to the hearing; setting the matter for public comment;
and receiving public comment; and

        WHEREAS, Draper City desires to declare Lot 10 of the Southridge Subdivision Phase A as surplus
and to authorize its sale to best serve the interests of City residents and produce a fair return.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Declaration of Surplus Property. The property described in Exhibit A and shown in
Exhibit B is no longer needed by the City for a public purpose and can be declared surplus and its sale is
authorized.

       Section 2. Authorization to Sign Real Estate Agreement. The mayor is hereby authorized to sign
a Real Estate Purchase Agreement for said property and sell it pursuant to the terms thereof.

        Section 3. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this resolution and
all provisions, clauses, and words of this resolution shall be severable.

       Section 4. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, ON
THE 21ST DAY OF JANUARY, 2020.
