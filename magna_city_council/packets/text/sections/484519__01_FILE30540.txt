                                         PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Steve Glezos and Randy Moore are requesting Conditional Use approval for amended home designs in the single-
family project development for the Oquirrh View Estates Phase 3 and Phase 4 subdivisions.



                            SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The subdivision is located between 8000 West and 8400 West. South of the railroad track line, and adjacent to
single family residential in the previous phases of the Oquirrh View Estates Subdivision phases. A vacant lot used
for open storage is located to the East. Other nearby uses include storage units and the Salt Lake Valley Fire
Service Area training center.



                                 GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The property is in a mapped area of moderate change potential. The property was previously rezoned to R-1-5 in
compliance with that designation. The project is compatible with Goal 5 of the General Plan in that it will help to
provide diverse housing choices for a variety of needs and income levels. Specifically, this request also applies to
portions of Housing Objective 5.5 of the General plan encouraging development of safe and visually pleasing
residential neighborhoods.

19.4.030 19.14.030 Conditional Uses
Conditional uses in the R-1 zones are as follows:

R-1-3, R-1-4, R-1-5       -- Single-family dwelling.
                          -- Single-family project developments

The planning commission may approve a detailed development plan for the entire single-family project in an R-1-
3, R-1-4, R-1-5 zone, pursuant to Chapter 19.84 of this title. Thereafter, the development services division director
may, as authorized by the planning commission, approve use permits for individual residential uses, provided that
the plans comply with all requirements and conditions of the approved development plan.

The original conditional use plans for Phase 1, 2 and 3 of the development included only single-story homes
(potentially with basements) with predominantly stucco exteriors. Contractors on the project began submitting

home plans for Building Permit that did not match with the approved building plans or architecture and failed to
submit the required copy of the Conditional Use Permit at the time of the building permit. Inadvertently these
plans were approved for building permit and homes were built contrary to the approved architecture for the
development. The home plans that were in error did however meet the setbacks and height requirements
specified in the zoning ordinance. This request seeks to approve the homes already built in Phase 3 as well as
approve the new architectural styles and exterior materials for the balance of Phase 3 and Phase 4. The new
proposed architecture includes 2 story residences (some with basements) with similar or smaller footprints and
exteriors of multiple materials and finishes. The total square footage of the proposed homes is increased due to
the additional floor. Overall the new plans represent a higher quality product over the original plans, but do not
negatively detract from the cohesiveness of the overall development.
