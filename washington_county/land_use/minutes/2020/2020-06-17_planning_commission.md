Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                                 June 17, 2020

The regular meeting of the Washington County Planning Commission was conducted on Tuesday,
June 17, 2020 from the Commission Chambers in the Washington County Administration
Building, located at 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Also, via electronic means through
Zoom. Chairman Kevin Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of Allegiance
and explained how the meeting would be conducted.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate acting as a voting member),
Mark Owen via Zoom, Reed Scow (Alternate acting as a voting member), Doug Solstad, and Jon
Walter.

Excused: Shayne Durrant and Slade Hughes.

Staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Natalie Nelson, Deputy County
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Cheyenne Bentley, Deputy Clerk at
host location; Todd Edwards, County Engineer; Kurt Gardner, County Building Official; Kyle
Layton, Building Inspector.


1. PUBLIC HEARING/ZONE CHANGE. Review, discuss, and hold a public hearing for a
zone change request of Parcels 4039-B-HV, 3268-A-1-HV, and 4039-A-1-B-HV from the A-20
(Agricultural 20 acre minimum lot size) zone, the OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 acre
minimum lot size) zone, and the OSC-20 (Open Space Conservation 20 acre minimum lot size)
zone to the MH (Manufactured Housing Park and Recreational Vehicle Park) zone. The parcels
are generally located at the north bend where Cemetery Road and Silver Valley Road intersect in
Leeds. The applicants are Don Parker and Jerry Eves.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation that included a copy of the proposed site plan and
a map of the area. He reported the applicants are requesting to change the zoning designation of
Parcels 4039-B-HV, 4039-A-1-B-HV and 3268-A-1-HV from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 acre
minimum lot size) zone, the OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 acre minimum lot size) zone, and
the OSC-20 (Open Space Conservation 20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the MH (Manufactured
Home Park and Recreational Vehicle Park) zone. The parcels total approximately 70 acres in size.
They are located in Leeds at the north bend where Cemetery Road and Silver Valley Road meet
and adjacent to Interstate 15. The applicant has submitted a concept plan and description of the
proposed RV (Recreational Vehicle) project. Washington County Land Use Code 10-8-G-1 states:

The purpose of this zone is to provide for the development of manufactured home parks and
recreational vehicle parks in a quality environment. Manufactured home parks and recreational
vehicle parks are not intended for the isolated lot, but shall be for use in areas where larger tracts
of land are available for development, and can be developed to a high standard of quality with
landscaping, recreation facilities, etc., and are designed and intended from the beginning of
development as manufactured homes only. The minimum zone size shall be approximately five (5)
acres. (Ord. 2018-1120-O, 8-21-2018)

Utah State Code Effective 5/12/2015
17-27a-505. Zoning districts.

(1) (a) The legislative body may divide the territory over which it has jurisdiction into zoning
        districts of a number, shape, and area that it considers appropriate to carry out the
        purposes of this chapter.
     (b) Within those zoning districts, the legislative body may regulate and restrict the erection,
         construction, reconstruction, alteration, repair, or use of buildings and structures, and
         the use of land.
     (c) A county may enact an ordinance regulating land use and development in a flood plain
         or potential geologic hazard area to:
         (i) protect life; and
          (ii) prevent:
               (A) the substantial loss of real property; or
                (B) substantial damage to real property.
     (d) A county of the second, third, fourth, fifth, or sixth class may not adopt a land use
         ordinance requiring a property owner to revegetate or landscape a single family dwelling
         disturbance area unless the property is located in a flood zone or geologic hazard except
         as required in Title 19, Chapter 5, Water Quality Act, to comply with federal law related
         to water pollution.
(2) The legislative body shall ensure that the regulations are uniform for each class or kind of
    buildings throughout each zone, but the regulations in one zone may differ from those in other
    zones.
(3) (a) There is no minimum area or diversity of ownership requirement for a zone designation.
     (b) Neither the size of a zoning district nor the number of landowners within the district may
         be used as evidence of the illegality of a zoning district or of the invalidity of a county
         decision.
(4) A county may by ordinance exempt from specific zoning district standards a subdivision of
    land to accommodate the siting of a public utility infrastructure.

2. PUBLIC HEARING/ZONE CHANGE. Review, discuss, and hold a public hearing for a
zone change request of Parcels 4033-B-1-HV and 4039-A-1-A-HV from the A-20 (Agricultural
20 acre minimum lot size) zone to the C-3 (General Commercial) zone. The parcels are generally
located at 133 S. Cemetery Road in Leeds. The applicants are Cemetery Properties/Z2Z Properties.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation detailing the site layout plan and map. He
explained that the applicant is requesting to change the zoning designation of Parcels 4033-B-1-HV
and 4039-A-1-A-HV, which are located on Cemetery Rd. in Leeds, from the A-20 (Agriculture 20 acre
minimum lot size) zone to the C-3 (General Commercial) zone. The combined parcels are
approximately 3.85 acres in size. The applicant is requesting the change in order to construct a
Recreational Vehicle Storage Business. The C-3 (General Commercial) zone is the only zone that
allows for storage facilities. Access to the business will be from Cemetery Rd. The applicant has
indicated that there will be six canopy structures for up to 100 covered spaces for RV storage. The
project will be used for storage only. There will be no overnight stays. There will be no dumping of
waste allowed on the premises. A block wall will be installed around the perimeter of the property for
security purposes.
