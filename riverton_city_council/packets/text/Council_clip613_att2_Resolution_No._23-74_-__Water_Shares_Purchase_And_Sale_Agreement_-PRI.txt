                                    RIVERTON, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-74

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
  WITH PROPERTY RESERVE, INC. FOR THE PURCHASE OF WATER SHARES
         ISSUED BY THE WELBY JACOB WATER USERS COMPANY

       WHEREAS, Property Reserve Inc, having cultivated a significant region of Riverton
City for decades, is the owner of approximately 1,000 shares of water issued by Welby Jacob
Water Users Company (“Welby Jacob"), a Utah nonprofit corporation, stock collectively, (the
"Shares”); and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City, is a municipality organized under Utah law and has authority
 to purchase the Shares; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to: (i) purchase 500 of the Shares in 2023; (ii) obtain
 an option to purchase in 2024 an additional 250 Shares; and (iii) obtain an option to purchase in
 2025 an additional 250 Shares, all at the price and on the terms and conditions set forth in the
 Water Shares Purchase and Sale Agreement presented to the Riverton City Council with this
 Resolution; and

       WHEREAS, Property Reserve Ince. desires to sell to Riverton City the Shares and grant
 the options to Riverton City as provided in Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has appointed the Riverton City Attorney to review and approve
as to form the agreement,

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

       1. The Agreement by and between Riverton City and Property Reserve Inc. is hereby
          approved and made effective on the date the Agreement is signed by the Mayor of
          Riverton City.

       2. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, the Water Shares Purchase
          and Sale Agreement with Property Reserve Inc., upon verification that the agreement has
          been approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.
