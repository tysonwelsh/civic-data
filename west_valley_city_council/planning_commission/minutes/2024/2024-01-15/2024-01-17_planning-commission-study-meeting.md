Discussion:   Steve Pastorik introduced Renee Layton as the new Planning Commission
              alternate member.
              He explained that there were no voting items on the agenda for today. However,
              we will be continuing the discussion regarding the General Plan. Steve noted that
              we will look at the northern part of Redwood Road, between 3500 South and
              2100 South.
              Steve reminded the Commission that the City hired a consultant to look at the
              properties that are likely to be developed or redeveloped and in what timeframe,
              1-5 years, or 6-10 years. Steve explained that the consultant then provided two
              land use options.
              Steve presented the property at 3360 S Redwood Road. He explained that for this
              property, option A is commercial along the frontage and residential in the back of
              the parcel, and option B is residential townhomes. He then oriented the
              Commission of the surrounding area.
              Brent asked if the Westerner Restaurant would be removed. Steve said most
              likely, the Westerner would be replaced.
              Commissioner Woodruff said this parcel is close to 3500 South, making option B
              the better option.
              Commissioner Lovato mentioned the Westerner Restaurant is typically busy, and
              he can’t see it relocating anytime soon. However, he would agree with
              Commissioner Woodruff. Steve said that he believes the consultant looked at this
              site because the building is relatively small compared to the parking area.
              Therefore, he compared the value of the improvements to the value of the land, so
              the success of the existing business was not included in the calculation.
              Commissioner Winters feels that where the lot is so long, it would be easy to
              overlook the rear of the property, so he agrees that option B is the best option.
              Steve noted that having a commercial use fronting Redwood Road with
              residential in the back is typically developed on these long lots along Redwood
              Road.
              Steve presented the property at 3251 S Redwood Road. He pointed out an auto
              repair shop, a Beto’s Restaurant, two homes, and some outbuildings on the
              property. Steve noted that there is still much open ground to develop. He stated
              that option A is commercial in the front and townhomes behind that, and option B
              is residential townhomes.
              Commissioner Woodruff stated that the northern part of the property extends to
              Lester Street, so it would make sense to have a residential use. He feels option B
              is the best option here. Commissioner Lovato agrees but is concerned that it
would not be successful. Steve explained that properties with a commercial use in
front, on a separate parcel from the residential use behind it, seem successful.
Commissioner Lovato asked if option A is viable in these situations. Steve said it
depends on how much commercial can be supported on Redwood Road, and that
is difficult to answer. He stated that having more townhomes creates more
demand and, in this situation, there would be more than one access to the
property, so he feels all townhomes make sense.
Commissioner Wood also feels Option B is the best option. However, she is
concerned about the residents not wanting to live directly on Redwood Road.
Steve noted that it is possible to have the side or rear of the building facing
Redwood Road, so they are not facing the street.
Commissioner Winters asked if we have any information on unused office space
in the area to see if adding another commercial use would be beneficial. Steve
said he would provide that information as part of their packet for the next
discussion.
The next property discussed is located at 3210 S Redwood Road and includes two
different uses, Carefree Homes, and Utah Barricade. Steve said that both
businesses have relatively low improvement values when compared with other
properties. He noted that option A for this property is all commercial and option B
is residential only.
Steve changed the topic and presented two maps, and if the Commission feels
they would be helpful, he can also add them to their packet. The first map depicts
parks, open space, and school locations; the second map identifies business types
and their locations. Commissioner Winters would like to view the maps and feels
they would be helpful. Steve noted that anything directly on Redwood Road is not
eligible for RM zoning.
Steve redirected the conversation to 3210 S Redwood Road.
Commissioner Winters stated he would envision a buffer between the townhomes
and Redwood Road.
Commissioner Lovato mentioned that a lot of traffic comes and goes to the strip
mall across the street, and a townhome development could create traffic issues.
Commissioner Woodruff feels the commercial makes more sense on this property
and would go with option A.
Commissioner Lovato and Commissioner Wood could see either option working.
Steve noted that we can state in the General Plan that both options are viable.
Commissioner Lovato agrees with that option.
Commissioner Layton feels this location could offer more affordable townhomes.
The next property discussed is located at 2996 S Redwood Road. Steve pointed
out that it is a large property and primarily undeveloped. He stated that the two
options are commercial along the frontage and residential in the back or all
residential townhomes.
Commissioner Winters mentioned that this property has limited frontage on
Redwood Road and feels all residential would work here. Commissioner Lovato
agreed.
Commissioner Woodruff pointed out that this property is challenging to develop,
and he would be happy if anything were successful in this location.
Steve discussed 2942 S Redwood Road next. He pointed out that access is a
challenge for this property. Steve noted that UDOT would most likely not grant
another access off Redwood Road, so most likely, they would use the same access
as the property to the south. He pointed out that direct access to Redwood Road
would be required if for-sale townhomes were developed on this property.
Chair Fuller said he feels option A is the best option. The Commission decided
they would prefer this property to be developed for commercial or light industrial
use. Then the Commission reviewed 2789 S Redwood Road. Steve described the
surrounding area and reviewed potential commercial business that could fit in this
location. He explained that option A is commercial, with three single-family
homes, and option B is residential with three single-family homes and eight town
homes.
Chair Fuller said that he doesn't feel you could add three single-family homes on
this property and that commercial would be a better use. Commissioner Winter
said that he agreed with Chair Fuller.
Commissioner Lovato said the property has an awkward shape and would be
difficult to develop given the location. He noted it would be a good place for a
coffee shop.
Steve said that the following location is 2770 S Redwood Road.
Jody Knapp stated that this property is either in the process of being sold or has
been sold. She noted that the plan is to combine the lots and develop a larger
structure. Steve stated that it would be best to keep the current zoning for this
property where there are plans for development.
Steve presented an aerial depicting 2521 S Redwood Road, the following property
up for discussion.
Brock Anderson explained that Mountain America Credit Union has acquired the
most southern parcel and will be requesting the addition of a credit union.
Steve noted that the remaining parcels are small and total approximately half an
acre. He said the home is occupied, and the owner has an at-home business.
Chair Fuller said keeping the current zoning would be the best option, and
Commissioner Wood agreed.
               2331 S Redwood Road was next, and option A is commercial along the frontage
               and townhomes in the rear of the property; option B is all townhomes.
               Steve Lehman noted that Mr. Platt had wanted to acquire this property and add it
               to the adjacent subdivision. However, the property owners were not interested in
               doing that then.
               Commissioner Woodruff, Winters, and Chair Fuller said they would prefer option
               B.
               Chair Fuller asked if Steve could provide a map showing the location of light rail
               stations within West Valley City and the surrounding area within a half mile
               radius. Steve said he would add it to their packet.
               Steve Pastorik mentioned how vital the Planning Commission and City Council
               feedback is when evaluating our General Plan.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 5:00 p.m.
