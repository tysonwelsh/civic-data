       CONSULTANTS.

         project with Stanley Consultants; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    4. RELEASE AGREEMENT WITH DOMINION ENERGY FOR THE REPAVING OF A ROAD THAT WILL BE
       RECONSTRUCTED NEXT YEAR.

         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, noted that Dominion has been in the area upgrading its gas line along the
         900 South Street right-of-way between 5900 W. and 7950 W. and disturbed the eastbound travel lane. The
         county requested that Dominion patch it for now rather than repave a road that the county will reconstruct
         approximately in the Spring. He addressed Commissioner Ebert’s question stating that an analysis was made
         and Dominion will reimburse the county approximately $100,000.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Release Agreement with Dominion Energy for the repaving of a
         road that will be reconstructed next year; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    5. CONTRACT WITH THE DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE FOR A LEASE ADDRESSING THE MONITORING
       WELLS THAT ARE CURRENTLY IN USE BY THE AIR FORCE.

         Jared Andersen, County Engineer, presented this annual contract renewal for the monitoring wells used by the
         Air Force that are on county property located southeast of Riverdale just below Hill Air Force Base. The Air
         Force pays about $1,000/year to lease the property for groundwater and soil testing.
         Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the license agreement with the Department of the Air Force for a lease
         related to the monitoring wells that are currently in use by the Air Force; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
