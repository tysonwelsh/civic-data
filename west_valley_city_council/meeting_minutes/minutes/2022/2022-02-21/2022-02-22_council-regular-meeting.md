COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Roger Dudis stated that he is concerned about the expansion of Bangerter Highway
    at 4700 South. He asked that the Council push UDOT to do the under-bridge option
    so that noise won’t increase in his neighborhood. Mr. Dudis stated that he would
    like advice on how the decision process works and what the timeline will look like.

       Bill Scarber stated that he is also concerned about the expansion of Bangerter
       Highway at 4700 South. He stated that neighbors are concerned about noise and
       noted that this is already a problem with Bangerter as it is in addition to being under
       the flight path for the airport. Mr. Scarber stated that he understands there is a cost
       to construct the overpass as an under-bridge but urged the City to do this for its
       residents. Mr. Scarber stated that he would like the City to increase noise and code
       enforcement in the neighborhood near Home Depot. He added that streetlights need
       to be repaired in this area as well. Mr. Scarber indicated that he serves as West
       Valley City’s representative on the Salt Lake County Library Board of Directors.
       He stated that West Valley City Library is on the list of rebuilds but he heard the
       City is not interested in keeping the library at its current location. He encouraged
       the City to work with the County to keep the library where it is.

       Paul Nichols expressed concern about tree branches falling into his yard from his
       neighbor’s home.

       Jim Vesock encouraged the City to increase the minimum wage for its employees.
       He indicated that the City needs to better promote the West Valley City app.

       Dennis Wolfley stated that he moved into West Valley City from Magna. He
       indicated that he didn’t know the road proposed on the plans he looked at would
       eventually become Bangerter Highway. He indicated the highway was greatly
       impacted his property and home. Mr. Wolfley stated that many families are being
       displaced and indicated that many residents feel that UDOT is not listening to their
       concerns. He asked that the City do what is best for the residents.

RESOLUTION 22-39: AWARD A CONTRACT TO WALTON CONCRETE
PROTECTION AND RESTORATION, INC. FOR THE REPAIR OF THE
FAIRBOURNE STATION PARKING GARAGE
Nicole Cottle, Acting City Manager, presented proposed Resolution 22-39 that would
award a Contract to Walton Concrete Protection and Restoration, Inc. for the repair of the
Fairbourne Station Parking Garage.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       On March 18, 2020, the City experienced a rather impactful earthquake based in
       Magna, UT. Reaveley Engineers and EDA Architects inspected the Fairbourne
       Station Parking Structure in response to visual damages.

       The report from Reaveley states good news in that “the primary structure capacity
       remains intact without any loss to its ability to support original design gravity and
       lateral loads.” It also records superficial damages limited to cracks in concrete
       frame beams and columns, drainpipe damages, and aesthetic damages, such as
       cracked windowpanes and displaced stone veneers “due to damage to CMU walls
       that act as masonry veneer backup support.”

       FEMA also inspected the structure and prepared a damage report approving
       75%/25% project reimbursement for the repairs.

     RIGHT OF WAY PURCHASE AGREEMENT AND ACCEPT A
     WARRANTY DEED AND A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION
     EASEMENT WITH AND FROM ROSA M. HERRERA FOR PROPERTY
     LOCATED AT 4987 WEST ROYAL ANN DRIVE
     Nicole Cottle, Acting City Manager, discussed proposed Resolution 22-41 that

     Accept a Warranty Deed and a Temporary Construction Easement With and From Rosa

            of $325.00 was based upon land values for properties in the area.

D.   RESOLUTION 22-43: ACCEPT A TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION
     EASEMENT FROM MICHAEL AND LINDA TOTTEN FOR PROPERTY
     LOCATED AT 3984 AND 3986 SOUTH 6400 WEST
     Nicole Cottle, Acting City Manager, discussed proposed Resolution 21-28 that
     would authorize the City to accept a Temporary Construction Easement From Michael
     and Linda Totten for Property Located at 3984 and 3986 South 6400 West.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            The Michael L. Totten and Linda Totten property is located south of the
            bridge over the Utah and Salt Lake Canal on 6400 West. There is an
            existing flooding problem on this property caused when occasional heavy
            rains back up storm water in the curb and gutter and overflows the drive
            approach and sidewalk flooding the Totten property. To correct this
            problem the City will be reconstructing and raising a portion of the sidewalk
            through the drive approach approximately three to four inches to keep the
            storm water in the curb and gutter. The City will also reconstruct the drive
            approach and the owners’ driveway to tie to the new sidewalk elevations.
            This work will be included as part of the 2022 Pedestrian Ramps Project.

            The Temporary Construction Easement will facilitate the reconstruction of
            the sidewalk in front of the owners’ property along with the reconstruction
            of their driveway to match the new sidewalk elevations.
