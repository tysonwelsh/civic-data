6:00 Fire Department Town Hall

1. Welcome and Introductions - Mayor Zoltanski


                              Mayor Zoltanski provided a welcome to those in attendance and introduced Fire Chief
                              Cline

2. Town Hall Program Overview - Fire Chief Cline


                              Fire Chief Cline welcomed those in attendance and introduced the Fire Department.
                              The fire staff in attendance introduced themselves: Chris Aston, Mike Bullock, Eric
                              Larsen, and Ryan McConaghie, Lenore Corey, Robert Dekorver.


4. Fire Department Video


                              The Fire Department showed a video highlighting the Fire Department.

5. Presentation: History of Sandy Fire Department


                            Chief McConaghie gave a history of the Sandy Fire Department. Chief Cline described
                            and answered questions related to the fire response dashboard.

6. Operational Overview: Fire, Medical, Wildland, Specialty Services, Fire Marshall Division, Community Risk
Reduction, and Sandy Fire Culture

                            Battalion Chief Chris Aston gave an operational overview of the fire division.
                            Battalion Chief Mike Bullock gave on operational overview of the medical division.
                            Fire Fighter Nick Prokopis gave an operational overview of the wildland division.
                            Battalion Chief Eric Larson gave an operational overview of the specialty services division.
                            Fire Marshall Dekorver gave an operational overview of the fire marshall division.
                            Community Risk Reduction Manager Lenore Corey gave an operational overview of the
                            community risk reduction division and showed an educational video.
                            Chief Mconaghie showed a video regarding the culture of the Sandy Fire Department




3. Citizen Engagement Survey


                            Chief McConaghie conducted an interactive engagement survey with town hall attendees.


7. Open Q&A


                            Chief Cline opened Q&A for those in attendance, who asked various questions related to
                            the Fire Department.

                            Mayor Zoltanski congratulated Chief Cline for his 8 years as Fire Chief, and thanked
                            everyone for coming.



                            The town hall adjourned at 7:46 PM
