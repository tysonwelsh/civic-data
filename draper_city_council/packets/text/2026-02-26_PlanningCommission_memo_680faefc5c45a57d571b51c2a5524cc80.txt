MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Nick Whittaker, MCMP
Date: 2026-02-26
Re: Public Hearing: Jones Lawnmowing, LLC. Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
Request (Administrative Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
for approximately 0.34 acres located on the North side of Browns Loop Way, at
approximately 191 E. Browns Loop Way. The property is currently zoned RA2 (Residential
Agricultural). The applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
be approved to allow for a home office to operate a lawnmowing business.



Findings for approval:

  1. The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC Section 9-5-
     080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the imposition of
     reasonable conditions.


Findings for denial:

  1. The proposal does not comply with the standards for approval found in DCMC
     Section 9-5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are not mitigated through the
     imposition of reasonable conditions.
