City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1648

On May 6, 2025, the Draper City Council Approved Ordinance #1648 amending Title 6-
4 of the Draper City Municipal Code pertaining to alcoholic beverage licenses – bar
establishment licenses. The complete Ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s
Office and available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/.

Published this 5th day of May 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
