                               Historic Preservation Month

WHEREAS, historic preservation is an effective tool for managing growth and sustainable
development, revitalizing neighborhoods, fostering local pride and maintaining community
character while enhancing livability; and

WHEREAS, historic preservation is relevant for communities across the nations, both urban
and rural, and for Americans of all ages, all walks of life and all ethnic backgrounds; and

WHEREAS, it is important to celebrate the role of history in our lives and the contributions
made by dedicated individuals in helping to preserve the tangible aspects of the heritage that
has shaped us as a people; and

WHEREAS, in an effort to preserve the heritage and legacy of this community, the Riverton
City Historic Preservation Commission was formed; and

WHEREAS, for over 150 years, Riverton City has exemplified what hard work and
determination can accomplish through the efforts of its residents who continue to provide
peace, promote progress, and preserve its traditions.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton City, do hereby proclaim May 2023,
Historic Preservation Month in Riverton City, Utah.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 16th day of May 2023.
