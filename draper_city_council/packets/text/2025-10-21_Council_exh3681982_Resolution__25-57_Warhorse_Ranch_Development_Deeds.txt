                               RESOLUTION 25-57

         A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CONVEYING REAL
           PROPERTY WHILE RETAINING ACESS EASEMENTS TO ENABLE
         DEVELOPMENT ACCORDING TO THE TERMS OF THE WARHORSE
                     RANCH DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT


      WHEREAS, Draper City owns real property at approximately 12701 S Costanza
Way; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City, The Vern Leland Richins and Linda Asay Richings Trust
and Karen Evans have entered into a development agreement for property near that
which is owned by Draper City; and

     WHEREAS, the city must retain access to its property used for storm water
management; and

      WHEREAS, the city should ensure access to property for property owners
south of 12701 S Costanza Way; and

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code §10-8-1 and Draper City Municipal Code 2-
1-030, the Draper City Council has the authority to dispose of real property it no
longer finds useful.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Approval. The City Council hereby approves Resolution 25-57,
disposing real property property as defined and identified in “EXHIBIT 1” and
“EXHIBIT 2” as attached hereto.

     Section 2. Effective Date.       This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon passage.
