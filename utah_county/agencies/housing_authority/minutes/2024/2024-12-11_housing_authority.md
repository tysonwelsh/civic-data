B. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-12-02 2024 Revised Budgets (All Programs) 
Suzanne made a motion to approve the 2024 Revised Budgets (All Programs). April 
seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
Revised 2024 budgets for all programs are presented for Board approval. Overall 
expenditures were less than originally budgeted. Utilities and administrative wages were below 
budget. Legal services and maintenance wages were higher than expected. Discussion 
ensued amongst the board and staff answered questions. 
 
C. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-12-03 Write-Offs: Owned Housing and HCV year-end 
write off of expenses primarily for 2024 were presented and discussed. 
Sara made a motion to approve Write-Offs: Owned Housing and HCV. Suzanne 
seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
D. Discuss / Approve Resolution 2024-12-04 Ratify RE Purchase Agreement - Orem 
Sara made a motion to ratify the RE Purchase Agreement. April seconded the motion. 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
Prior to the December 2024 board meeting, Commissioners received an email seeking 
approval for an RE Purchase Agreement due to timing. All board members voted via email 
unanimously.
