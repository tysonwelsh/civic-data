3. Prattplex Rezone; 3703 West 6200 South; Rezone 0.81 acres from A-5 (Agriculture 5-acre
lots) Zone to C-G (General Commercial) Zone; Prattplex/Dan Pratt (applicant) [#34280;
parcel 21-20-126-016] ,
The applicant was not in attendance.
Megan Jensen gave an overview of the property that has an existing building and has been zoned A-5 since
it was annexed into West Jordan in 1981. The applicant intends to use the building as a daycare, which is
an administrative conditional use in the proposed C-G Zone. The proposal is consistent with the
commercial future land use designation.
Based on the analysis and findings contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council to rezone the parcel comprising 0.81
acres at 3703 West 6200 South from an A-5 zone to a C-G zone.
George Winn said he was surprised that a rezoning had not been done prior to this.
George Winn opened the public hearing.
Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.
MOTION: Ammon Allen moved, based on the information and findings set forth in the staff
report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to forward a positive
recommendation to the City Council for the Prattplex Rezone located at 3703 West
