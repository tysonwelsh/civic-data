 STAFF/APPLICANT PRESENTATION
 The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
 and recommendations. Key points addressed in the Staff's presentation to the Planning Commission included the
 following:
    • The proposed Open Space (OS) zone was collaboratively created with the Parks and Recreation Department.
    • Staff explained the key differences between the proposed OS zone and Public Facilities (PF) (14.17) zone which
        primarily include the purpose/objectives and permitted uses.
    • The proposed OS zone furthers the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    • The proposed OS zone may be tool that can be used to preserve open space for the social needs, public health, and
        wellbeing of the of the citizens of Provo.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
  • There was no neighborhood chair or public comment.

14.33.010.        Purpose and Objectives.
The Open Space (OS) zone is intended to preserve and enhance public and private open space, parks, recreation and
natural areas. It will provide for the social needs of the community as those needs relate to open space, whether
publicly or privately sponsored. It will enhance the identity and image of the community as a desirable place for human
growth and development and help improve the aesthetic and functional features of the community.

14.33.020.      Permitted Uses.
(1) Permitted Principal Uses. The following principal uses, and no others, are permitted in the OS zone:
        (a) Parks;

        (b) Golf course;

        (c) Greenbelt;

        (d) Trails;

        (e) Recreational fields;

        (f) Conservation grounds;

        (g) Wetlands;

        (h) Other sensitive lands;

        (i) City owned community garden;

        (j) Federal lands;

        (k) Cemeteries (no crematories); and

        (l) Public utilities.

(2) Conditional Uses. The following uses and structures are permitted in the OS zone only after a conditional use permit
has been issued, and subject to the terms and conditions thereof:

        (a) Communication (subject to the standards in 14.34.420, Provo City Code)
(2) Permitted Accessory Uses. Accessory uses and structures are permitted in the OS zone; provided they are incidental
to and do not substantially alter the character of the permitted principal use or structure. Such permitted accessory uses
and structures include but are not limited to restrooms, playgrounds, pavilions, clubhouses, seating bleachers,
greenhouses, grounds maintenance sheds and similar structures which are customarily used in conjunction with and
incidental to a principal use or structure.


14.33.030.      Lot Standards.
Lots within the OS zone shall be developed according to the following standards:

        (1) Min. Lot Area:                       2,500 SF

        (2) Min. Lot Frontage:                   50 feet

        (3) Min. Lot Width:                      50 feet
                Corner Lot:                      50 feet

        (4) Min. Lot Depth:                      50 feet

        (5) Max. Lot Coverage:                   10%

14.33.040.       Yard Requirements.
Yards shall be provided according to the requirements listed below (measured from the property line):

                                                 Minimum          Maximum
        (1) Front Yard:                          20 feet          No requirement
        (2) Side Yard, corner lot:               20 feet          No requirement
        (3) Side Yard, interior:                 20 feet          No requirement
        (4) Rear:                                20 feet          No requirement

        (5) Driveway access (see Section 14.37.100, Provo City Code).

14.33.050.      Projections into Yards.
The following structures may be erected on or project into any required yard:

        (a) Fences (see section 14.34.500, Provo City Code).

        (b) Landscaping (see section 15.20, Provo City Code).

        (c) Necessary appurtenances for utility service, subject to City approval.

14.33.060      Building Height.
(1) Total Maximum Building Height:               35 feet
(2) Maximum Number of Building Stories:          2 stories

14.33.070.      Parking, Loading and Access.
(1) Each land use in the OS zone shall have sufficient parking sufficient to comply with the requirements of Chapter
14.37, Provo City Code.

(2) If the lot area is no greater than fifty thousand (50,000) SF, the required vehicle parking may be exempt from Chapter
14.37 by the approval of the Development Services Director or designee.

14.33.080.      Project Plan Approval.
See sections 15.03.300 and 15.03.310, Provo City Code.

14.33.090.       Other Requirements.
(1) Signs (see Chapter 14.38, Provo City Code).

(2) Landscaping (see Chapter 15.20, Provo City Code).

(3) Trash storage (see Section 14.34.080, Provo City Code).

(4) Fencing (see Section 14.34.500, Provo City Code).

14.33.100.      Athletic Field Scoreboards.
Scoreboards for recreational athletic fields shall be exempt from height and setback requirements otherwise required in
the OS zone, subject to approval of the Development Services Director or designee, through an administrative hearing
process to allow for public comment, and subject to the following standards:

(1) Scoreboards are designed to be an integral part of the athletic field and are designed in a manner that is compatible
with the scale and mass of the athletic field.

(2) Electronic messages and other displays shall be oriented to the athletic field, rather than to the public street or
neighboring properties.

(3) Messages facing the public street or neighboring properties shall be limited to identification and logos of the school
and one (1) sponsor. These displays shall be static and illuminated only during official games and other major events.

(4) The size of the sponsor identification and/or logo shall be smaller than and complementary to the size of the Provo
Parks and Recreation logo.
