2.
Public hearing and recommendation for amendments to Chapter 11-6-5 Accessory Dwelling Units, Chapter 11-3-16 Chart of Allowed and Permitted Uses and Appendix A: Definitions, of the Eastern Summit County Development Code relating to Accessory Dwelling units and Live/work dwelling units.
Ray Milliner, County Planner
(6:03 P.M.)
Ray Milliner,
Senior Planner
, reviewed the amendments to the Eastern Summit County Development Code related to Accessory Dwelling Units and Live/Work Dwelling Units.
Commissioners asked questions and made comments. Mr. Milliner and Peter Barnes,
Director of Planning, Zoning and Design
, responded.
Chair Sonntag opened the meeting for public hearing.
(6:21 P.M.)
No public input was given.
Chair Sonntag closed the meeting for public input.
(6:21 P.M.)
Rich Sonntag made a motion to approve the amendments to Chapter 11-6-5 Accessory Dwelling Units, Chapter 11-3-16 Chart of Allowed and Permitted Uses and Appendix A: Definitions, of the Eastern Summit County Development Code relating to Accessory Dwelling Units and Live/Work Dwel
ling Units including the discussed change requiring that property owners demonstrate sufficient water for the use at the building permit stage pursuant to the findings of fact and conclusions of law contained in the staff report
. Marion Wheaton seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Clinton Benson, Alex Peterson.
