MEMO
To: City Council
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2024-01-09
Re: Public Hearing: Ordinances #1599 and #1600


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Zoning and Land Use Map Amendment for
approximately 0.889 acres located on the north side of 14600 S, at approximately 600 W 14600 S.
The property is currently zoned M1.




Draper City and Bluffdale City are currently working on a municipal boundary line adjustment that
will remove the property from Draper City’s jurisdiction and place it in the jurisdiction of Bluffdale
City. As part of that process the zoning and land use designations for the property will need to be
removed from the Zoning and Land Use Maps.



The property owner is looking to develop their property, which includes a total of 22.9 acres of land.
The intent of adjusting the municipal boundary between Draper and Bluffdale is to ensure the
entire property is in one jurisdiction.



On December 7, 2023 the Planning Commission reviewed this item and forwarded a positive
recommendation on a 3-0 vote.
