                  TOWN OF ALTA RESOLUTION 2021-R-4


     A RESOLUTION HONORING OUR PUBLIC SAFETY
  PROFESSIONALS AND THOSE WHO ASSISTED DURING THE
    WINTER STORM OF FEBRUARY 12 - 18, 2021 IN LITTLE
               COTTONWOOD CANYON

        WHEREAS, Little Cottonwood Canyon including the Town of Alta experienced
sustained snowfall, gusting and sustained winds, and severe avalanche conditions during
several periods between Friday, February 12 through Thursday, February 18, 2021;

       WHEREAS, 103” of snow fell during this period, with 6.82” of snow-water
equivalent, as measured at the Alta Ski Area Collins Study Plot;

        WHEREAS, 157 natural and explosive-triggered avalanche occurrences were
recorded in avalanche paths affecting State Route 210 (SR-210 by Utah Department of
Transportation (UDOT) Avalanche Safety, 31 of which impacted SR-210 and 58 of
which were classified as Relative Size 3 or larger avalanches, with several very large
avalanches occurring in avalanche paths that have not affected SR-210 in recent memory
or in recorded history;

        WHEREAS, SR-210, which terminates in Alta and which is Alta’s only route of
access to the Salt Lake Valley, was closed due to avalanche hazard for 70 hours, with
approximately 1/5 of the 5 mile stretch of SR210 between mile marker 6.5 and 11.5
impacted by avalanche debris as deep as 20 feet;

       WHEREAS, avalanche mitigation teams composed of UDOT avalanche
forecasters and ski patrollers from Alta Ski Area and Snowbird Ski and Summer Resort
worked in conjunction with public safety teams throughout Little Cottonwood Canyon to
conduct several avalanche mitigation missions during the period, resulting in a total of
267 individual explosive detonations;

       WHEREAS, interlodge travel restrictions were enforced for over 67 hours, 60 of
these were consecutive, being the longest such period in recent memory, requiring
numerous visitors, residents and staff to stay indoors during this extended period;

         WHEREAS, Town of Alta Marshal Mike Morey, deputy marshals, dispatchers,
Town of Alta staff, Utah Department of Transportation Avalanche Safety and
maintenance crews, Alta Ski Area management, Alta Ski Area snow safety personnel,
Alta Ski Area and independent snow removal staff, the lodges, property managers,
firefighters, and Alta Clinic staff provided a coordinated response and worked together
tirelessly over this period to clear the road, support and prioritize the health and safety of

individuals present in Alta who were subject to interlodge travel restrictions, provide
updates to the public and respond to the needs of people in the Town of Alta;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Town Council acknowledges the
work and sacrifice of the Town of Alta Marshal Mike Morey, deputy marshals,
dispatchers, Town of Alta staff, Utah Department of Transportation Avalanche Safety
and maintenance crews, Alta Ski Area snow safety personnel, Alta Ski Area and
independent snow removal staff, the lodges, property managers, firefighters, and Alta
Clinic staff who ensured the safety of the guests, workers and residents subject to
interlodge restrictions in the Town of Alta;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Town Council acknowledges those who
responded to the extraordinary period of winter weather and elevated avalanche hazard
and worked tirelessly to clear the roads;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Town Council gratefully honors and thanks
those who carried out the critical role of protecting and ensuring public safety during this
period.

   PASSED AND APPROVED THIS 10th DAY OF MARCH, 2021.
