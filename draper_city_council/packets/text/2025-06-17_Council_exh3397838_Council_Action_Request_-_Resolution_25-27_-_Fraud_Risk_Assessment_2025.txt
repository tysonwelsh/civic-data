RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Resolution #25.27 approving the 2025 Fraud Risk Assessment

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Utah State Auditor’s Office implemented a Fraud Risk Assessment requiring cities to review their
current practices to reduce the risk of undetected fraud, abuse, and noncompliance in local
governments. Upon completion the assessment should be presented and reviewed by the City Council.
This assessment is completed on an annual basis, the attached report is for 2025.
