Citizen Public Comment
Kim Black, Director, The Launch Team, stated the charity run is to raise money
for youth that need scholarships. Youth 18 and under are trained on how to run marathons. The
organization has grown from a small training group to over 200 participants.
Mayor Smolka stated this group did the marathon last year and ran on residential
roads with loud music, this cannot happen again.
Ms. Black stated she has a list of things that need to be addressed. In the
proposal, she has included those items along with signage three days before, running in
eastbound lanes, and she will take care of the music.
Mayor Smolka stated there is a major construction project that is going to be done
in the canyon and could interfere with the marathon.
Ms. Black stated the marathon would be on August 1. The 5K will be held at the
zoo and will go five miles up the canyon with part of the route going north of Pioneer Ridge Road.
Officer Jake Elsasser stated permission is needed to use Pioneer Ridge Road.
Ms. Black stated she will call herself and see if she can get permission.
Willy Stockman, Emigration Canyon Sustainability Alliance, stated it was not clear
how far along the watershed plan is. Is there a process to move forward with the hiring of a
consultant?

Emigration Canyon Community Council Report
Steve Borst, Emigration Canyon Community Council, stated he is resigning from
his chair position on the Community Council. Bill Tobey is the new chairman, Matt Velinder joined
the communication team, Tyler Tippets rejoined the safety team, and Lisa Schneider has
submitted her intent to leave the Community Council. The Council voted to pay the full bill to the
Conservation Corps. Firewise will be held on May 2, 2020 at noon. Countywide chipping services
begins in June. The newsletter was distributed to the community on February 11. 2020. The
Council will be discussing 2020 goals at the March 10, 2020 meeting.

Council Member Reports
Unified Police Department (UPD) & Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)
Council Member Brems stated SLVLESA approved the tax revenue anticipation
note. Resolutions were past to have an audit done on SLVLESA, that will happen shortly and
adopt some policies for record management. The UPD Board has had continued conversations
about proportional voting and tabled it for a month or two while additional information is being
collected. Propositional voting will be based on the amount of taxes collected. The Town of
Brighton is now part of the UPD and SLVLESA. They have 200 residents and 1100 seasonal
homes. Seasonal homes are taxed more than residential homes. A large number of seasonal
passes are sold out of Colorado and that also affects the amount of tax revenue collected. This
information is being looked at for future conversations. He attended the UPD training lunch and
learn and toured facilities and saw emergency operations, equipment, gear and saw a mock
SWAT team.
