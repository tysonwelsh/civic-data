BACKGROUND

Fred Cox, representing the property owner, is requesting minor subdivision approval for the
Moody Subdivision. The property is zoned manufacturing and is occupied by SealMaster, which
is a pavement products and equipment business. Said business will remain on what will become
lot 1 within the subdivision.

STAFF/AGENCY CONCERNS:

Fire Department:

               Fire hydrants to be installed in accordance with the Uniform Fire Code. Access
                and turnaround proposals shall meet all fire codes.

Magna Water District:

               Project will need to run availability for water, sewer and fire protection.
               Subject to design and review inspections.
               Will need to coordinate services depending upon public or private street design.

Utility Agencies:

               Subject to all standard easement locations.

Public Works:

               Authorization required of ditch/water users for any abandonment, relocation,
                piping or any other modifications to existing ditches or irrigation structures.

               Revisions to plat are required.

               Will need to align the access for new lots perpendicular or to within 5 degrees of
                perpendicular with the SR-201 North Frontage Road .

               If the new road will be public, a soils report will be required.

               Will need to coordinate the new road with the City’s Engineering Division. It is
              likely a delay agreement will be required for improvements.


ISSUES:

     Mr. Cox is requesting minor subdivision approval for a commercial/industrial Subdivision
      in the manufacturing zone. The subdivision will consist of 9 lots on approximately 10
      acres. A proposed storm water retention pond is also being proposed for the subdivision.

     Access to the subdivision will be gained from SR-201 North Frontage Road and from an
      existing drive approach for the existing SealMaster business. It is anticipated the City will
      be doing a future improvement project on said right-of-way. The developer will need to
      coordinate future improvements with the Engineering Division. Should the City allow a
      delay agreement, these improvements will be postponed until such time that the City is
      prepared to begin work.

     At the present time, the access is intended to be a public road. However, the alignment
      shown on the plat does not meet with the City’s Engineering standards. Primarily because
      the alignment causes an unsafe connection with the frontage road. Vehicular traffic would
      be looking over and behind their left shoulder in order to exist the subdivision. The City
      Engineering Division has provided some options as to how this road could work. These
      include:

             Redesign the public street to intersect SR-201 North Frontage Road perpendicular
              or within 5 degrees of being perpendicular.

             Install an approved drive approach (also perpendicular to SR-201 North Frontage
              Road) and build a private road.

     The existing business will stay on what will become lot 1. Access to said business will be
      gained from the existing drive approach.

     Should the new right-of-way be a public street, the applicant will be required to submit a
      soils report prior to City Engineering approval of the plat. If the proposed right-of-way
      will be private, a soils report is still recommended to ensure that the road is constructed
      with appropriate materials.

     The new road is located adjacent to the east property line. The developer will need to
      coordinate improvements to ensure that the property to the east is not negatively impacted.
      While the grades are relatively flat, a slope easement or other arrangements may be needed
      to install the new road.

STAFF ALTERNATIVES:
  A.    Grant preliminary subdivision approval for the Moody Subdivision subject to the
        following conditions:
        1.      That compliance be made with Magna Water District regarding water line
                extensions, sewer connections and fire protection.

        2.      That the subdivision name be approved by Salt Lake County.

        3.      That an appropriate ground water and soils investigation be made by a certified

        5.      That the developer coordinate the improvements along the frontage road with the
                City Engineering Division. A delay agreement is likely to be required depending

        6.      That the developer resolve all staff and agency concerns.

   B.        Continuation to address issues raised during the public hearing.


Discussion:     Steve Lehman presented the application. He said the Engineering Department is
                working with the applicant to have the connection between North Frontage Road
                and Moody Drive as close to ninety degrees as possible for a safe transition. Steve

                subdivision for a future City improvement project.
                John Moody, the applicant, said he owns the existing business that would be on
                lot 1 of the proposed subdivision. He stated that the goal of this project is to
                provide space for local businesses to operate instead of a large company moving
                in from outside the City.
                Fred Cox, representing the applicant, said they are willing to make minor
                adjustments to improve the drive connection.

BACKGROUND:

Ryan Halder is requesting preliminary approval for the 3500 South Krause Crossing Subdivision.
The proposed subdivision is located on the southeast corner of 3500 South and 6400 West. The
subdivision is bordered on the east by the Family Estates Subdivision and on the south by
Meandor Avenue.

ISSUES:

The proposed subdivision will consolidate two parcels to create 1 lot approximately 1 acre in
size. The subdivision plat will also dedicate right-of-way to West Valley City, UTA and UDOT
for improvements at Meandor Avenue and along 3500 South.

The existing hardware store will be replaced with a convenience store and fueling stations. A
conditional use application was reviewed and approved by the Planning Commission in
September 2022. All of the site issues including access, landscaping, fencing etc., were
addressed as part of that application.

Access to this new lot will be gained from 3500 South and 6400 West. As mentioned
previously, a small portion at the corner of Meandor Avenue is being dedicated to West Valley
City for ADA/pedestrian access. An area along 3500 South is being dedicated to UTA for a bus
stop. The applicant will also be dedicating 7 additional feet along a portion of 3500 South to
complete the right-of-way requirements along said street.

In summary, the proposed subdivision will consolidate parcels, dedicate right-of-way, and will
establish easements and other information applicable to the division of property. The applicant
will continue to work with staff on the conditional use application reviewed and approved by the
Planning Commission.

STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

1.     Approve the 3500 South Krause Subdivision subject to a resolution of staff and agency
       comments.

2.     Continue the application in order for the developer to address the Planning Commission’s
       concerns.
