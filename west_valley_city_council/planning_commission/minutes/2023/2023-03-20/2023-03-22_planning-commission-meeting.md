GPZ-1-2023
Russ Nelson
4543 and 4547 S 4000 W
General plan change from Non-Retail Commercial to General Commercial and zone change
from RB to C-2
0.73 acres


Russ Nelson has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for two parcels totaling 0.73
acres located at 4543 and 4547 S 4000 W. Both properties are currently zoned RB (Residential
Business) with a General Plan designation of Non-Retail Commercial. The proposed zone is C-2
(General Commercial) and the proposed General Plan designation is General Commercial.

Surrounding zones include C-2 to the south, RB to the west, R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling
Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) to the north, and R-4 (Four Unit Dwelling
Residential) to the east. Surrounding land uses include a fast-food restaurant to the south, a home
to the west, a mobile home park to the north, and a fourplex to the east. The subject property is
currently licensed as a day care.

A previous General Plan/zone change application (GPZ-3-2022) made by a different applicant
where the requested zone was C-2 was denied by the City Council at the applicant’s request.
Originally, the applicant had hoped to have an access onto 4000 West. When the applicant
understood that an access onto 4000 West would not be approved, she attempted to secure a
shared access with the property owner to the south. Since, the applicant was unsuccessful in
securing the shared access, she ultimately asked that the General Plan/zone change be denied.
The City Council voted to deny the application on 5/3/2022.

Section 7-5-101 of the Zoning Ordinance states the following: “If the City Council denies an
application to change the Zoning of a property, no application to change the Zoning of that
property to the same zone as proposed in the denied application shall be submitted until at least
one year has passed unless the Planning Commission finds that circumstances have changed in
such a manner as to merit consideration of a new application.” While the previous application on
this property was submitted 13 months before this application, the denial occurred less than a
year ago. However, given the new use for this application and the unique circumstances
surrounding the last application, the Planning Commission could find that circumstances have
changed enough to consider this application.

If this latest application is approved, the applicant plans to build a new Quick Quack car wash on
the subject property. The existing structures on the property would be demolished. Included with
this report is a concept plan for the proposed car wash.

In the C-2 zone, a car wash, which is considered automobile service, is reviewed as a conditional
use when the property is adjacent to residential use or residential zoning. Hence, if this
application is approved, the applicant would need to apply for a conditional use permit to pursue
the car wash development.
Staff shared the concept plan with the Public Works Department for preliminary comment.
Public Works indicated that the curb cut along 4000 West was too close to the curb cut to the
south. As with the previous application, the applicant was directed to work with the landowner to
the south to share the existing access. At the time this staff report was prepared, staff did not
know if the shared access was secured by the applicant. For this reason, the Planning
Commission may choose to continue this application to grant the applicant time to secure the
shared access.

During the Commission’s study meeting, a question was raised about the proposed car washes’
hours of operation. The facility will be open every day from 7 AM to 7 PM in the winter months
and from 7 AM to 9 PM in the summer months.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. This application should be approved.

   2. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
      public hearing.

   3. Denial. The RB zone should remain.

SA-2-2023
Mountain View Business Plaza Subdivision – Amended and Extended
3320 South 5600 West
C-2 Zone
1 Lot
1.8 Acres

BACKGROUND:

Roman Frazier is requesting preliminary approval for the Mountain View Business Plaza
Subdivision Amended. This application will amend and extend lot 3 of said subdivision. The
subdivision is located at 3320 South 5600 West and is zoned C-2.

ISSUES:

The Mountain View Business Plaza Subdivision was recorded in June 2019. The original
subdivision consisted of 6 lots on 12 acres. The property is zoned C-2 and is bordered on the north
and south by existing commercial development, the east by 5600 West and the west by property
currently being reviewed for flex office space located between the existing storage units (old K-
mart building) and Mountain View Corridor.

Mr. Frazier has completed the transition of the old K-mart into inside storage spaces, and the other
two lots adjacent to 5600 West as a Cane’s restaurant and Krispy Kreme donut shop. In recent
months, another parcel became available on the north side of the main entrance into this project.
It is this parcel that the applicant wishes to add to the existing subdivision. The parcel is currently
being used as a car wash.

The applicant would like to continue using the car wash for a time until a more desirable use
becomes available. The existing car wash site does have some issues that need to be resolved with
the City before a new business license can be issued. Namely, the installation of required trees,
removal of the non-conforming monument sign and removal of banner signs. While these issues
won’t affect the approval of the subdivision, they will need to be resolved in order for a new
business license to be issued.

Access to the property will be gained from 5600 West. This access is derived from two separate
approaches. The main access into the overall Mountain View Plaza development and from an
existing access easement on the north side of the existing carwash. The access easement is
currently denoted on the original subdivision plat and will remain in place to provide access to the
pending development west of the storage units.

Due to this application being a commercial subdivision, other land use regulations will be
addressed during the conditional use and/or permitted use applications once the car wash is
removed. The subdivision plat will contain easements and other information applicable to the
consolidation of the new lot.
STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

       of staff and agency comments.

2.     Continue the application in order for the developer to address the Planning Commissions
       concerns.
