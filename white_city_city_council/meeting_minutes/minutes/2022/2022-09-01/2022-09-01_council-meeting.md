                                            Workshop

Update on Engineering Projects

               Shane Ellis, Engineer, Salt Lake County Engineering and Flood Control, stated
the consultant for the Sego Lily project is on board and gathering data. Once the data is gathered
it will move forward with the design phase. The 9400 S Ski Connect project is phase 2 and
construction will begin in the fall. Walk White City is proceeding with sidewalk inventory and other
data being collected. The information will be included in the Walk White City report. He is working
with Salt Lake County to see what lighting options are available.

                Mayor Flint stated she is not comfortable with proceeding with the Sego Lily
project until more information for the project is available.

               Paul Ashton suggested scheduling a meeting between the Council and
consultants to discuss the project.

               Mayor Flint stated the Council will hold a workshop meeting on October 13, 2022.

             Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Cardenaz, moved to close
the workshop meeting. The motion passed unanimously.

Unified Fire Authority

               Captain Ken Aldridge stated the wildland crews have been deployed to Idaho,
Nevada, and California. The fuels crew has been working on mitigation projects throughout the
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) service area. Recruiting efforts continue; 306 individuals took the
written test. Extensive background checks and oral interviews will be next, with the top 26
candidates starting recruit camp in February 2023. The Sandy City Fire report was emailed to the
Council.

Unified Police Department

             Detective Jeff Fenton stated vehicle burglaries and domestics are high. Citizens
Academy started yesterday. He is preparing for the White City car parade. The speed trailer data
came back high on Larkspur, the trailer will be put back and he asked officers to watch the area.

               Chief Randy Thomas stated he was able to purchase a drone for his precinct.
The drone pilot has deployed it twice during incidents that resulted in two apprehensions. At a
future meeting, he would like to show the Council actual drone footage.

Ordinance No. 22-09-01 – White City Moderate-Income Housing Element

              Erin O’Kelley, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Service District (MSD),
reviewed the following Ordinance adopting amendments to the White City Moderate-Income
Housing Element of the White City Metro Township General Plan:

ORDINANCE NO. 2022-09-01                                               Date: September 1, 2022

       AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING AMENDMENT TO THE WHITE CITY MODERATE-
       INCOME HOUSING ELEMENT 2022, A COMPONENT OF THE WHITE CITY
       METRO TOWNSHIP GENERAL PLAN

       WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township incorporated and began functioning as a
municipality in 2017; and

       WHEREAS: Utah Code 10-9a-401(3)(b) requires each Metro Township in the State of
Utah, with a population of five thousand or more residents, to conduct a review of and revise the
Moderate-Income Housing Element of its General Plan by October 1, 2022; and,

      WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council adopted the current Moderate Income
Housing Element as part of the General Plan in 2019; and

        WHEREAS, on June 3, 2022, the White City Metro Township Council sent notice of intent
to amend the Moderate Income Housing Plan Element as part of the General Plan to affected
entities; and

      WHEREAS, on July 15, 2022, the White City Planning Commission (the “Planning
Commission”) sent mailed noticed to affected entities to prepare recommended amendments to
the Moderate Income Housing Element as part of General Plan (collectively, the “Housing



                                               3
DATE           THURSDAY                                SEPTEMBER                           1, 2022

Element”) that would address White City’s Housing and meet state requirements pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-9a-403; and

        WHEREAS, on July 28, 2022, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to take
public comment on the Housing Element after complying with the procedures in the Act, including
but not limited to the public hearing and public meeting notice requirements of Utah Code Ann.
§§ 10-9a-204 and 10-9a-404; and

      WHEREAS, after taking public comment on the General Plan, the Planning Commission
recommended the Housing Element to the White City Metro Township Council with some minor
amendments, which have been incorporated; and

      WHEREAS, on August 4, 2022, the White City Metro Township Council held a public
meeting to review the Housing Element; and

      WHEREAS, on August 18, 2022, the White City Metro Township Council held a public
meeting to review the Housing Element; and

      WHEREAS, on September 1, 2022, the White City Metro Township Council held a public
meeting to review the Housing Element; and

       WHEREAS, the White City Metro Township Council has reviewed the Housing Element
as recommended by the Planning Commission and determined that it is necessary to amend the
Housing Element in the best interest of White City and its residents.

       NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL:

   1. The attached Housing Element and all other related documents and appendices are
      approved.

   2. The prior White City Moderate Income Housing Element adopted in 2019, is repealed in
      its entirety.

   3. A copy of the Housing Element will be filed and retained by the White City Metro Township
      Clerk, Recorder, and Auditor and posted to the White City Metro Township website.

   4. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any of the sections, sentences, clauses,
      or provisions of the Housing Element are invalid, such a ruling will not invalidate the
      remaining portion(s) of the Housing Element or the General Plan.

   5. An electronic copy of the Amendment “Moderate Income Housing Element 2022” is hereby
      ordered to be filed with both the Wasatch Front Regional Council and the Housing and
      Community Development Division of the Utah Department of Workforce Services.

   6. Staff is directed to identify those portions of the White City Metro Township Code that need
      to be updated to conform to the Housing Element and General Plan and to recommend
      proposed changes to the Council.

Big Bear Park

                Mayor Flint stated trees will be planted as soon as the weather becomes cooler.
Six trees will be funded, and any additional trees can be purchased with COVID funds. The trees
will be planted along the sidewalks. The arborist is creating a plan that will consider life span of
the trees so that not all the trees die at the same time. The park is in the process of being deeded
over to White City Metro Township.
