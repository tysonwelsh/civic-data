2. Mayor's Budget Comments
Mayor Jennifer Wilson thanked the budget team and staff for their work. 
Mr. Darrin Casper , Deputy Mayor of Finance and Administration, stated the total 
appropriations for the 2022 budget is $ 1.65 billion. The Tax Fund and General Fund are 
historically healthy due to federal assistance.  The America Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) 
funds to be appropriated is $ 197 million.  The Council will consider these appropriations 
in January. 
3. Open Public Hearing - Citizen Public Input
A motion was made by Council Member Bradshaw, seconded by Council Member Winder 
Newton, to open the public hearing.  The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
Ms. Susan Sandack  stated communication is the basis and foundation for relationship 
building, strategic planning, education, community outreach, and public engagement. She 
encouraged the Council to reverse the cuts made two weeks ago; $ 55,000 for GRAMA 
administration and the $ 180,000 to the mayor ’s ability to restore positions. She expressed 
concern about Unified Police Department presentation receiving new money or drone 
surveillance over homeless encampments because the vulnerable are often triggered and 
traumatized by today ’s digital world. [ Ms. Sandack sent an email the following day 
clarifying that she misspoke during the public hearing; the drones were part of the 
Surveyor’s presentation- not UPD.] 
Mr. Steve Klemz , United Today Stronger Tomorrow (UTST), stated as a parish pastor, 
he has prayed for the Council, the earth, children, and for those who struggle to find safe 
and affordable housing and shelter. UTST has surveyed 557 county residents on how

WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council, as required by Salt Lake 
County’s Budget Process Ordinance, Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances 
Chapter 2.95, is required to adopt by resolution a final budget for Salt Lake 
County on or before December 10 of each year for the following calendar

WHEREAS, Salt Lake County, as required by the Uniform Fiscal 
Procedures Act for Counties, Utah Code Ann § 17-36-15, shall adopt by 
resolution the final budget for fiscal year 2022 on or before December 31, 
2021; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Mayor and the County Council have 
caused to be prepared the Mayor ’s proposed 2022 budget and the Council ’s 
recommended final 2022 budget, each of which have been reviewed and 
discussed in public meetings, and which have met all legal requirements; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council provided timely public notices 
for a public hearing on the adoption of Salt Lake County ’s final budget for 
fiscal year 2022 on December 7, 2021, at the hour of 6:00 p.m., as required 
by Utah Code Ann §17-36-12; and
WHEREAS, on December 7, 2021, at the hour of 6:00 p.m., the Salt Lake 
County Council held a public hearing on Salt Lake County ’s final budget for 
fiscal year 2022 that conformed with all necessary requirements of Utah 
Code Ann §17-36-13; and
WHEREAS, at the December 7, 2021, public hearing the Salt Lake County 
Council received public comment on the final budget, and all interested 
persons had an opportunity to be heard for or against the estimates of 
revenue and expenditures and performance data or any item in any fund; and 
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council finds it necessary to make certain 
adjustments to the recommended budget and now desires to adopt a final 
budget for fiscal year 2022; and
