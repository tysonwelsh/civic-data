19          1.1     (Project CUP-21-014) Request from Richard and Stacy Goebel for a Seven-
20                  Foot Fence Along the Eastern Property Line of 7967 South Sample Cove.
21
22   Community and Economic Development Director/Hearing Officer, Michael Johnson, welcomed
23   those present and stated that the above request involves a Conditional Use request from Richard
24   and Stacy Goebel for a seven-foot fence along the eastern property line of 7967 South Sample
25   Cove.
26
27   City Planner, Samantha DeSeelhorst presented the staff report and displayed an aerial overview of
28   the property. The proposal includes a seven-foot fence with six feet of solid white vinyl and an
29   additional foot of white lattice. The rationale is to address privacy and safety concerns between
30   neighboring properties. Current City Code allows for an eight-foot fence as a conditional use and
31   up to 12 feet as a conditional use with neighbor consent. Staff recommended approval with the
32   conditions and full findings set forth in the staff report.
33
34   The applicant, Richard Goebel, reported that the proposed fencing will run along a portion of his
35   rear property line with the rest remaining as-is. Both portions will coordinate with the solid white
36   vinyl and lattice accent on top.
37
38   Mr. Johnson opened the public hearing.
39
40   Emily Bone, the adjoining property owner, identified where the proposed fence will be located.
41   She asked for clarification of the construction timeline and expressed concern with how the new
42   fencing will impact her property. Mr. Johnson clarified that construction of this type generally
43   prohibits the applicant from impacting a neighboring property without permission or consent. If
44   damage is done, the person doing the construction would be responsible for any damage.
45
46   Mr. Goebel noted that the existing fencing is located solely on his property. He confirmed that
47   they will be replacing the fencing without imposing on the adjacent property.

 2   There were no further public comments. The public hearing was closed.
 3
 4   Mr. Johnson stated that fence height extensions are issued regularly and a desire for more privacy
 5   or visual barrier is enough precedent to approve these types of requests. Coordination and
 6   communication between existing neighbors are encouraged.
 7
 8   Mr. Johnson moved to approve Project CUP-21-014 pursuant to the following:
 9
10   Findings:
11
12          1.      There is clear and convincing evidence shown by the property owner relating to
13                  privacy and safety that will be substantially minimized or eliminated by the
14                  implementation of the requested wall and fence combination; (19.76.050.E.1.a)
15
16          2.      Construction of such wall and fence is the most reasonable solution under the
17                  circumstances. The additional fence height is necessary to ensure the mitigation
18                  of the aforementioned issues.
19
20          3.      The Conditional Use Permit is granted by the Director or his designee following
21                  an Administrative Hearing preceded by all required notifications.
22                  (19.76.050.E.1.b)
23
24          4.      The evidence presented with the proposed Conditional Use is compliant with the
25                  requirements of Section 19.84.080 (Conditional Uses – Determination) and
26                  Section 19.76.050.E (Miscellaneous – Fences).
27
28          5.      There is sufficient evidence that the standards for the issuance of a Conditional
29                  Use Permit as outlined in Section 19.84.080 of the Cottonwood Heights City Code
30                  have been satisfied.
31
32   Conditions:
33
34          1.      A Building Permit and all necessary inspections shall be obtained and completed.
35
36          2.      The Building Permit will state that materials, design, and shall not exceed a total
37                  height of eight feet from existing grade.
