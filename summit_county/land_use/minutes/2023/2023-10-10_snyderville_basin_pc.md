2.
Project # 23-145. Public hearing and recommendation to Summit County Council for an Amendment to Development Code Section 10-2-10 (Use Table), Section 10-8-5 (Accessory Dwelling Units/Agricultural Employee Accessory Dwelling Units), and Chapter 10-11-1 (Definitions). Legislative Review. County Planner: Laura Kuhrmeyer (6:09 P.M.)
Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner,
presented a Code Amendment for Accessory Dwelling Units to the Commission.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:22 P.M.
Bruce King was called to speak.
Debbie Scoggins was called to speak.
Tony Tyler was called to speak.
Jody Hoffman was called to speak.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:27 P.M.
Planner Kuhrmeyer and Commission Members responded to the comments and questions raised during the public hearing.
DJ Hubler made a motion to to forward a positive recommendation to County Council for the post code amendments based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law and the recommendations in the staff report with the following recommendations from Planning Commission to staff that we consider the discussion around ADU's within the rural residential zone particularly around the current nonresidential usages that maybe allowed in that zone; consider strongly the maze that are allowed in State code section 17-27A-256 subsection 4 and consider rewriting the code as its currently written in red text section B.2.b and adding definitions around the international building code. John Kucera seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Tyann Mooney, Makena Hawley.
