2, ADOPT A RESOLUTION TO SUBMIT TO THE VOTERS
WHETHER TO ADOPT THE OPTIONAL PLAN CREATED
BY THE UTAH COUNTY COMMISSION

Ezra, Commission Office -

COMMISSIONER IVIE — HAD A LOT OF DISCUSSION OVER THE LAST YEAR. GOOD
GOVERNANCE ADVISORY BOARD DID AN EXTENSIVE AMOUNT OF WORK AND
RESEARCH INTO THIS. ONE OF THE KEY COMPONENTS FROM THE REPORT IS THE
CONCEPT OF A SEPARATION OF DUTIES, RESPONSIBILITIES AND POWERS THAT
WERE INHERENT WITH SOME CHECKS AND BALANCES CREATING A DISTINCTIVE
EXECUTIVE AND LEGISLATIVE BODY. AS WE’VE GONE THROUGH AN EXTENSIVE
PROCESS AND MET WITH CITIZENS, ONE OF THE CONCERNS IS THE SIZE AND COST.
THIS PARTICULAR PROPOSAL WOULD INCORPORATE THOSE CORE PRINCIPLES AS
IDENTIFIED BY THE GOOD GOVERNANCE ADVISORY BOARD BY CREATING A
SEPARATION OF POWERS, A DISTINCTIVE EXECUTIVE AND LEGISLATIVE BODY
WITH A LEGISLATIVE BODY THAT HAS A CHECK AND BALANCE ON THE EXECUTIVE.
IT WOULD CREATE REPRESENTATIVE DISTRICTS SO THAT EACH MEMBER OF THE
COUNTY HAS A GUARANTEED LOCAL REPRESENTATIVE TO SPEAK FOR THEM ON
THE COUNSEL. WITH THIS PARTICULAR PLAN, THERE ARE SOME COSTS OBVIOUSLY
INVOLVED, BUT AS MY OFFICE HAS BEEN ABLE TO WORK DOWN THROUGH THIS
AND COME UP WITH SALARIES FOR THE ELECTED OFFICIALS AND STAFF, WE’RE
ABLE TO COME TO A COMPENSATION RATE THAT WOULD GIVE A 34% SAVINGS
OVER OUR CURRENT FORM OF GOVERNMENT.

THAT WILL GIVE THE FIVE COUNCIL MEMBERS A SALARY OF $20,000 ANNUALLY
WITH NO BENEFITS, A MAYOR WITH A $120,00 SALARY WITH BENEFITS, A DEPUTY
MAYOR AT $80,000 WITH BENEFITS AND AN ASSISTANT TO THE COUNCIL SALARIED
AT $65,00 WITH BENEFITS. THE TOTAL PACKAGE SAVES THE TAXPAYERS OVER 34%,
AS A RESULT, I WOULD LIKE TO SEE THIS MOVE FORWARD TO SEE IF THE VOTERS

APPROVE THIS.

COMMISSIONER AINGE - IN FAVOR. FEELS THERE IS NOT A BETTER RECIPE FOR
GOOD GOVERNMENT THAN INCREASING THE NUMBER OF INFORMED, ENGAGED,
PRINCIPLED PEOPLE PARTICIPATING IN THE DEMOCRATIC PROCESS, PLACING THIS
ON THE BALLOT, HOPES WILL BE THE RESULT. WE CAN HAVE A LOT OF
DISCUSSION FOR THE NEXT 10 MONTHS ABOUT WHAT IS THE RIGHT FORM OF
GOVERNMENT FOR UTAH COUNTY. IS GRATEFUL FOR THE WORK OF THE GOOD
GOVERNANCE ADVISORY BOARD THAT CAME, STUDIED THIS AND GAVE US
RECOMMENDATIONS. WHILE THERE ARE SOME DEPARTURES, THINKS THE
DEPARTURES HAVE BEEN MAINLY FOCUSED ON COST AND MAKING SURE THAT
SPENDING IS LIMITED AND THE SIZE OF GOVERNMENT IS LIMITED AND HOPEFULLY
AS A RESULT, PEOPLE WON’T BE AS FOCUSED ON THAT ISSUE AND THERE CAN BE
DISCUSSION ABOUT THE VALUE OF SEPARATION OF POWERS AND THE VALUE OF
GEOGRAPHIC DISTRICTING. LOOK FORWARD TO THAT CONVERSATION THIS YEAR.

COMMISSION LEE - HAS A DIFFERENT OPINION. I DON’T SEE ANY FORM OF
GOVERNMENT NECESSARILY DIFFERENT THAT WE HAVE AVAILABLE TO US AS
BEING EVIL. I THINK THEY’RE JUST DIFFERENT AND THERE ARE DIFFERENT
OBSTACLES, CHALLENGES AND BENEFITS THAT COME FROM EVERY FORM OF
GOVERNMENT THAT IS OUT THERE. WE HAVE HAD AN EXTENSIVE ONE-YEAR
PROCESS IN WHICH WE’VE GONE BACK AND FORTH AND THERE’S BEEN A LOT OF
HISTORY THERE, A LOT OF THINGS BEING SAID. WHAT I HAVE LEARNED FROM THIS
PROCESS, BEING HEAVILY INVOLVED IN IT AND SITTING AT DINING ROOM TABLES
WITH INDIVIDUALS, THERE’S A PRETTY BIG GROUP THAT DOESN’T QUITE KNOW
WHAT TO THINK. IT’S NOT THAT THEY’RE FOR OR AGAINST, THEY JUST DON’T
KNOW WHAT TO THINK. SO THE EDUCATION PART OF THAT IS PRETTY MASSIVE.
THEY DON’T KNOW WHAT DIRECTION TO GO NOR DO THEY, IN SOME CASES, KNOW
EXACTLY WHAT COUNTY DOES AND HOW IT GOES THROUGH THAT PROCESS OF
ACCOMPLISHING WHAT IT NEEDS TO DO.

IN GOING THROUGH A PROCESS OF TRYING TO COLLECT SIGNATURES AND SEEING
PEOPLE FACE TO FACE, THERE IS A COUPLE THINGS I HAVE LEARNED ALONG THE
WAY. WE HAD ONE GROUP THAT COLLECTED PETITIONS FOR THE MAYOR COUNCIL
FORM AND WE HAD ANOTHER GROUP THAT WENT OUT THERE AND WAS LOOKING
FOR A FIVE-MEMBER COMMISSION FORM. AND BOTH GROUPS DIDN’T GET ENOUGH
SUPPORT TO MERIT PUTTING IT ON THE BALLOT. THROUGH THIS CONVERSATION
PROCESS, I HAVE FOUND THAT MOST OF THE PEOPLE THAT I HAVE TALKED TO AND
BEEN INVOLVED WITH, THERE WAS ONE THING PRETTY CLEAR ~ THERE’S A REAL
RELUCTANCE FOR GROWING GOVERNMENT. COST IS ONE OF THOSE THINGS WE
CAN TALK ABOUT, BUT COST IS RELATIVE FOR THE TIME PERIOD IT’S IN, BUT NOT
NECESSARILY FOR THE FUTURE. I LOOK FORWARD TO THE DEBATE. I ASSUME
THIS WILL GO FORWARD AS A 2/1 VOTE.

EVEN FROM A 3 TO A5 WAS A TOUGH TALK AND TO GO TO A MAYOR COUNCIL
FORM IS GOING TO BE A TOUGH SELL ACROSS THE COUNTY. WE SAW IN WEBER
COUNTY AN ITEM THAT WENT ON THEIR BALLOT, THAT FAILED MISERABLY. IT
WILL BE AN INTERESTING DISCUSSION THIS YEAR AS IT GOES FORWARD. I THINK
IT IS ONE THAT WE WILL TALK ABOUT SEPARATION OF POWERS. I HAVE SAID
OVER AND OVER, WE’RE NOT THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT. ONE OF MY MAIN
CONCERNS THAT THE EXECUTIVE IS ACTUALLY CONSOLIDATION OF POWER DOWN
TO ONE. I THINK THAT HAS SOME PROBLEMS TO IT. I WILL BE VOTING ‘NO’, AND
WE’LL HAVE THAT DEBATE THIS YEAR AS WELL.
