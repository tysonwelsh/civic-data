                        COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

                                   RESOLUTION #2020-11-04

                                        November 18, 2020

   A RESOLUTION APPOINTING DAVID OLSEN AS THE REPRESENTATIVE
   APPOINTEE OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP TO THE BOARD OF
   TRUSTEES OF THE SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA

         WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township is a member entity of the Salt Lake Valley
   Law Enforcement Service Area (the “SLVESA”); and

         WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township Council (the “Council”) may appoint a
   Councilmember to the Board of Trustees of SLVESA (“SLVESA Board”) pursuant to Utah
   Code Ann. § 17B-1-303; and

         WHEREAS, Councilmember Apollo Pazell, who represented Copperton on the
    SLVESA Board, resigned from the Council on October 20, 2020 (the “Resignation”); and

         WHEREAS, because of the Resignation, the Council desires to fill the vacancy on the
    SLVESA Board created by the Resignation.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE COPPERTON
   METRO TOWNSHIP EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY:

           1. The Council appoints David Olsen as the Copperton Metro Township’s representative
   on the SLVESA Board and to serve out the remainder of Councilmember Pazell’s term, which
   will expire on December 31, 2021.

      2. Mayor Sean Clayton is authorized to provide SLVESA with this resolution, a copy of David
    Olsen oath of office, and David Olsen’s contact information.
