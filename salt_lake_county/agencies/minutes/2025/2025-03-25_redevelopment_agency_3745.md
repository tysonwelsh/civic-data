Agency and MWIC Magna, LLC.  The amendment is required by Senate Bill 
250, which passed during the most recent legislative session.  This bill 
requires a participation agreement to have a provision authorizing an agency 
to use funding that would otherwise be provided to a participant under the 
participation agreement to pay a participant ’s delinquent property tax or 
privilege tax or resolve a political subdivision lien against the participant.  
RESOLUTION NO. 151
RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE 
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY APPROVING 
AN AMENDMENT TO THE PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT WITH 
MWIC MAGNA, LLC
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake County (the “RDA”) 
adopted the Magna /Arbor Park Project Area Urban Renewal Plan on June 
16, 2009;
WHEREAS, on January 18, 2023, the RDA entered into a participation 
agreement with MWIC Magna, LLC, to use a portion of its tax increment to 
assist MWIC Magna, LLC in the development of its property located within 
the project area;
WHEREAS, during the 2025 General Session, the Utah State Legislature 
enacted Senate Bill 250, which requires participation agreements between 
community reinvestment agencies and developers to include a provision 
authorizing the agency to use funding that would otherwise be provided to 
the developer to pay the developer ’s delinquent property tax or privilege tax 
or resolve a political subdivision lien against the developer (see Utah Code § 
17C-1-202(5)(d) (2025));
AND WHEREAS, Amendment No. 1 to the Participation Agreement, 
attached hereto as Attachment “A,” amends the participation agreement to 
include the new requirement.
