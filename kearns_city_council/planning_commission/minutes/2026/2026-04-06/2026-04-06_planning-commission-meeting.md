                                            Hearing began at – 6:06 p.m.
OAM2026-001576 – Perla Rivera is requesting an ordinance amendment to 19.28.040 regarding side yard
setbacks. Planner: Jeff Miller (Discussion/Recommendation)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jeff Miller provided an analysis of Chapter 19.28
and amendment.


Kearns Planning Commission – April 6, 2026 – Meeting Summary                                                             Page 1 of 4
Mr. Miller explained situation that prompted the code change; namely, a resident wanted to build a garage
but couldn’t meet the setback requirements but were close. Most homes don’t have garages, and they are
parking on the street. Wouldn’t apply to R-1-3, R-1-4, and R-1-5 zones, which already have 4-foot setbacks,
but would apply to other residential zones. Would need to have a total cumulative side yard setback of at
least 16 feet so long as no side yard setback would be less than 5 feet. Staff are recommending approval.

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding zero feet, living units above and whether staff
has checked with UFA. Mr. Miller explained five feet meets fire code.

Commissioner Thomas motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Nelson seconded that motion.

    2) Commissioner Discussion and Staff Assignments: this standing agenda item allows Planning
       Commissioners the opportunity to raise and discuss important issues related to current and long
       range planning in Kearns. The Commission may direct assignments to planning staff related to
       discussion items. (Discussion and Staff Direction: Planning Staff)

    3) Utah Department of Transportation’s Walking Loops initiative and the proposed Kearns Walking
       Loop postcard project. The purpose of the project is to highlight a walking path to encourage
       residents and visitors to explore local businesses and community gathering spaces. Planner: Bianca
       Paulino (Discussion)

        Ms. Paulino presented with Jared Bowling from UDOT. Tentative theme is “Eat, Walk, Tour
        Kearns!” Purpose is to support local businesses, promote foot traffic, engage the community, create
        momentum for future upgrades, and link to active regional transportation networks. They would
        include some improvements. Working on a pilot for Kearns and Magna. Working on finalizing a
        postcard design of decals/signage.
