   1. HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT (HS) Administrative

          Consider a request for a Hillside Development Permit to allow development of the subject property into
          townhomes. The applicant is asking for approval of a hillside permit in order to cut into 20-40% slopes.
          The property is located on the east side of Valley View Drive and just south of Sunset Dr. The property
          is currently zoned R-1-10 (Residential minimum lots size 10,000 sq ft) & A-P (Administrative
          Professional). The owner is Raine Christensen. Case No. 2021-HS-003 (Staff – Mike Hadley)

          Wes Jenkins presented the following:

          Wes Jenkins – They meet all of the slope disturbance areas; they had a different disturbance line to begin
          with but at the Hillside Review Board’s suggestion moved that line. They have some in the 40% range
          that exceeds what is allowed, the board decided that those areas were man made. Another condition that
          was made there was that they provide a rock fall berm with the boulders from the slope. I was looking at
          it before the meeting, but you can see the layout was changed so they will have to have the geotechnical
          engineer look at it again in another study to see if that would be required. It’s not a high hazard for
          rockfall, but moderate, but now that the layout is changed, and the units are on the other side of the entry
          road they may not need it, but they will address it with the construction drawings.

          Commissioner Larsen – So they will address the drainage too?

          Wes Jenkins – Yes they will. That’s why they have that swell so they can get that drainage and collect it
          all here.
