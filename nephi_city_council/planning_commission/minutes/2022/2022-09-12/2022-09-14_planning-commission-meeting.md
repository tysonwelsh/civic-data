	•	 Northridge Estates Phased Subdivision Plat D PUD Subdivision (40 units) - Preliminary Plat. Proposal includes five (5) Eight-plex condominium units. Mandatory HOA. 

	•	Staff Presentation:
Planner Shauna Talbont reported to the commission that Mt. Peak Development, owned by Terry Messersmith, is seeking preliminary plat approval for a PUD development that will include five (5) eight-plex condominiums located at approximately 1500 N. to 1620 N. and 100 E. to 200 E. The property was recently rezoned to R-3 with a PUD overlay zone.  This development will have a mandatory homeowners association per the PUD code.  The open space will be held in common, and maintained by members of the HOA.  A fence around the perimeter of the property is mandatory as well. Ms. Talbot stated that the developers have met their open space requirements, and parking requirements for these units with 130 spaces proposed.
	•	 Commission Discussion:Alan Hancock asked about it being a dead end road.           Kent Heap asked about tax assessments for the common areas in the condominium complex.  Developer Cody Messersmith said that Phase 3 that will be coming before the commission next meeting will be a commercial development.
	•	Action Item:
Motion: To approve the preliminary plat for Northridge Estates Subdivision Plat D with the accompanying DRC notes.
Made by: Kent Heap  Second: Heather Outcome: Unanimously approved

3.  Roderick Nephi Business Park subdivision (7 lots)- Final Plat and Site Plan
 
	•	Staff Presentation: 
City Administrator Seth Atkinson said the city is asking the developer to convey water that is in that natural drainage from the top of the site because it slopes from the south to the north.  The city would also like them to construct a sidewalk and curb in gutter along the highway. The developer would like to wait until the lots are developed before installing the sidewalk.   The city attorney is working on an agreement that will legally bind them to install the sidewalk.  They will have a retention basin within the subdivision. 
Mr. Atkinson also described how electricity would be brought into the subdivision.
	•	 Commission Discussion:
Kent Heap asked about how much traffic would be involved with this development.  Mr. Wheat said they had a traffic study done for UDOT and provided the study to the City.
Ms. Talbot asked about the loading dock area and how many bays will be built.  Mr. Wheat said there would be 10 to 12 bays. Ms. Talbot warned Mr. Wheat about the specification for the loading area.  	
Commissioner. Brough asked if UDOT would require a road from Lot 3 that would provide access to the highway.  Mr. Wheat said the retention basin is sized for all of the lots to be developed.  Action Items:
Motion:  To approve the final plat for Roderick Nephi Business Park subdivision with contingent on the DRC notes specifically the sidewalk, drainage and UDOT approval.  Made by: Karl Brough  Second: Alan Hancock  Outcome: Passed unanimously
Motion:  To approve the the Roderick Nephi Business Park Site Plan Building 1
Made by: Alan Hancock  Second: Karl Brough  Outcome:  Passed unanimously 

4.  Nebo South Business Park Amended Plat

	•	 Staff Presentation:
 Ms. Talbot updated the commission that the Wright Direction, LLC has submitted an amended plat that will combine two lots of the original subdivision.  A tenant will be purchasing two lots and combining them for one development.  This will prevent a property line from dissecting the development once a building has been constructed. Lot 3 in the amended plat will take the place of Lots 3 and 4 in the original plat.  An address correction on the local street from 100 West to 425 West will also be included on the amended plat.  
 
	•	Action Item:
Motion:  To approve the amended plat for the Nebo South Business Park plat as per the notes and combine lots 3 and 4.
Made by:  Kent Heap  Second: Heather Robertson  Outcome: Passed unanimously


5.  Site Plan in the Nebo South Business Park (Amended Plat) subdivision on Lot 2 for a small corporate office space with potential room for numerous other uses 
	

       a. Staff Presentation:
Ms. Talbot updated the commission that The Wright Direction, LLC has submitted a site plan for a commercial building on Lot 2 of the Nebo South Business Park.  The site plan shows one building for proposed small corporate office space with potential room for numerous other uses (due to a proposed grease trap and large parking area).  Some potential uses that could be expanded into the building are food preparation to sell wholesale or retail, a small local eatery, or a large restaurant. Access for the business will be along Sheep Lane.  A landscaping plan has been shown along Sheep Lane as well.  Utilities are already stubbed to the site.  Parking has also been addressed based on the business use as well as other potential uses.  An access to the lot to the north has also been included.

b. Action Item:
Motion: To approve the site plan for the commercial building in Lot 2 of the Nebo South Business Park along with the DRC notes:
Made by: Karl Brough  Second: Kent Heap  Outcome:  Passed unanimously
