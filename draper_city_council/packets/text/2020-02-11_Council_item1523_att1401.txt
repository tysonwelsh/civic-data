RECOMMENDATION:

Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 20-09 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for fees charged by Community Development and Parks & Recreation Departments.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs. Several departments have proposed changing various fees for better clarification, remove
duplication, add new items or update fees to recover current costs. Only the items or sections that are proposed
for changes are listed in Exhibit A. Items highlighted in blue are the new proposed fees or description.
