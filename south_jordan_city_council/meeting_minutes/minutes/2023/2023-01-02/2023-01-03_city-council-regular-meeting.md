         E. Mayor and Council Reports
Council Member Shelton attended the South Valley Chamber Holiday get together, visited with
some folks there for a while. Attended the Legislative Policy Committee Meeting and noted that
the council just had an update from Attorney Loose about legislation that is pending. Attended
the City Council Strategic Planning Meeting and a DARE Graduation at Golden Fields
Elementary. Also took the opportunity for a vacation with his family, and it took them six days
to get home from Miami because of the airline issues; they were not on Southwest.
Council Member Harris attended many of the events that Council Member Shelton attended,
including the City Council Strategic Planning Meeting and the South Valley Chamber Holiday
meeting. Also attended a Sewer Board Meeting, with nothing specific to report beyond that.
Council Member Marlor on December 14 also attended the City Council Strategic Planning
meeting, appreciates staff in all they are doing to get them organized for this coming year. On
December 15 he met with a developer regarding a revised proposal on the Rise Development. On
December 17 he, Council Member Harris and Manager Lewis attended the SoJo Santa Race; he
did the ruck race again, this time with a 20 pound backpack, and it was a lot of fun. His youngest
son was there with him as well, and he recommended everyone attend one of those races. He
wanted to express condolences on the passing of former council member Leona Winger. He had
the opportunity to serve with her for about 6 years on this council, and he is sorry to see her
passing. He had a scheduled holiday with his family out of town at the time of the viewing and
funeral, so he was unable to attend, but he enjoyed many years of service with her.
Council Member Zander was sick for a few weeks and missed the last SoJo race, she hasn’t
missed one race this fall before that, but she will be there in January. Attended the Strategic
Planning meeting as well and explained to the public what that exactly is. She has had a few

         F. Public Comment
Mayor Ramsey opened the public comment portion of the meeting.

Sharon Juneau (Resident) just wanted to say, first of all, thank you to Council Member
McGuire for helping us out with the situation we had with the city street sidewalk where it was
broken and I had fallen and gotten hurt; it was really addressed quickly, and I wanted to thank
you for that. That is kind of why I am here again tonight, because of probably old age and injury,
I can no longer be the person who shovels Ojibwa Lane from curb to curb, from 10400 South
down to Iron Mountain; it has just gotten too much for me. I have gone to the Streets Department
in the past, asked them to pay attention to that. Granted, our Streets Department was
overwhelmed by the large snow that we had, but our street didn’t get a plow down it, especially
the sides of the road, for almost two days. My son was late to work, we had to find rides for
work, so it is really affecting our family and I am just asking that more attention be paid to
Ojibwa Lane because it’s just too hard for me to do on my own anymore. The problem is that we
don’t do curb to curb with leaves and snow removal. There is a grade there from Iron Mountain
to 10400 South which goes down and water can accumulate there, especially in the winter time if
it freezes over it’s icy and slick. Right now my husband is hopefully at home clearing out my
front so I can get out of the front of my house, because right now I can only use the back door. I
don’t know what the answer to the question is, but for the last 13 years I have been shoveling the
alley behind my house, Ojibwa Lane, and trying to be a good neighbor. I am here tonight to let
you know that I am retiring from snowplowing duty from our neighborhood and I was hoping
that the council would be able to do something to give me a hand here and pay a little bit more
attention to Ojibwa Lane; it is not a circle, it is not a cul-de-sac, it is a through road and it
actually feeds off of 10400 South. I thank you and look forward to some sort of response,
hopefully I won’t have to be shoveling snow anymore like this.

Council Member Zander noted that one of the reasons she is in favor of this is that the owners of
this land had the opportunity to annex into any city they chose, and she thinks it should be well
known by the public and the residents of South Jordan that this landowner, Rio Tinto,
approached South Jordan requesting to annex into the city. There are a lot of variables, but
something that should be known is that city staff is very qualified and equipped to help in this
process when it starts to move forward. She thinks there is a great team here at the city that will
be able to work on this, and one of the reasons she is voting yes is because she has such
confidence in the city of South Jordan. If this land is going to be annexed, she is very confident
that this is appropriate and wise to have this land annexed into our city, and looks forward to
working on this in tandem with the landowner.
Roll Call Vote was 5-0, unanimous in favor.
Wayne Bradshaw (Applicant) – thanked the mayor and council for accepting their petition
tonight, as well as thanked the staff. A lot of work has gone into this, the staff has worked with
them and met on a weekly basis to try and overcome some of the concerns and hurdles that come
with annexation. They appreciate the acceptance of the annexation petition, and they know there
is a lot more work to do. They will be back to the council when there are more public hearings,
and they are excited to be moving this forward and continuing to work with South Jordan.
Mayor Ramsey thanked everyone for their hard work on this. Things like this don’t just happen,
and they aren’t just “right” for no reason. Finally, she welcomed Rio Tinto to South Jordan.
         I. Staff Reports and Calendaring Items

Manager Lewis reminded everyone that the next ruck is on January 21, known as Break the
Freeze. Since it looks like there may be three council members there, staff will do a notice of a
potential quorum.

Manager Whatcott wanted to publicly thank the staff on the snowplowing event that was three
days of heavy snow. We are already short staffed in Public Works, so that put an extra load on
those that are there and that was challenging. There were a few equipment failures over the
weekend that made it more challenging as well, but they stayed with it and did a good job with
an event that large.

Attorney Loose noted in addition to sharing that the legislative season is coming up very soon,
he wanted to thank Director of Administrative Service Melinda Seager, Director Rasmussen and
Deputy Director Jeremy Nielson for their help with getting $1 million in a matching grant for the
Pure SoJo Project that was awarded the end of last week.

Director Preece shared some sad news regarding one of the city’s corporate partners. Last night
he received a text from Steve Peterson, the president of Millrock Development who was one of
the partners that built where Avante and Lucid Technology are, that his partner Nathan Ricks
died in the Provo plane crash. Director Preece in turn reached out to Mr. Ricks’ son-in-law,
Andrew Bybee, who worked with the city on this as well. For those who worked on that project,
they know how much work was done and how great they were to work with.
