                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 22-16

         AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE REFORMATTED, INDEXED, AND
                   REPUBLISHED RIVERTON CITY CODE

        WHEREAS, Under general statutory authority of Utah Code, Title 10, Chapter 3, Riverton
City has adopted a comprehensive set of ordinances, historically known as the Riverton Municipal
Code, for the purpose of exercising the legislative authority of the Riverton City Council to carry
into effect and discharge all powers and duties conferred by law upon Riverton City, its elected
officials, officers, employees, and inhabitants; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Code further provides for the safety of the public and
preserves and promotes the prosperity, morals, peace, good order, comfort and convenience of
Riverton City and its inhabitants; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-3-707, Riverton City is “empowered to
revise, codify and compile from time to time and to publish in book, pamphlet or loose-leaf form
all ordinances of the municipality of a general and permanent character and to make such changes,
alterations, modifications, additions, and substitutions therein as it may deem best to the end that
a complete simplified code of the ordinances then enforced shall be presented, but with errors,
inconsistencies, repetitions, and ambiguities therein eliminated;” and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-60, Riverton City may “declare what
shall be a nuisance, and abate the same, and impose fines upon persons who may create, continue,
or suffer nuisances to exist;” and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the current Riverton
Municipal Code needs to be updated to: 1) provide additional enforcement remedies within its
administrative code enforcement program; 2) eliminate “errors, inconsistencies, repetitions and,
chapters 10 and ambiguities therein;” by consolidating enforcement remedies found in Riverton
City Code: Title 8, Chapters 10 and 35; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines City staff has undertaken and
completed a project to revise the Riverton Municipal Code for the purpose of updating the Code
as described herein; and

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

Section 1.     Repealer. The following Code Sections are hereby repealed:

               Title 8, Chapter 10, Sections 140, 150, 160, 170, 180, 190, 200, 210, and
               220; and

               Title 8, Chapter 35, in its entirety.

Section 2.     Adoption. Title 7. Chapters 1 through 6, is hereby reenacted and adopted
               as provided in the Attached Exhibit A.
