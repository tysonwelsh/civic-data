3.         23-233                 Discussion of Council Members' recommended amendments to the FY 
                                  2023-2024 Tentative Budget.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item.
                                  Robinson Stroud Budget Proposal.pdf

                                  Council Member Zach Robinson reviewed the Robinson-Stroud budget proposal for the 
                                  Fiscal Year 2023-24 Tentative Budget. The proposal addresses the city's most dire needs 
                                  while significantly reducing the property tax increase originally proposed in the FY24 
                                  budget. The proposal calls for a property tax increase of approximately 14.9% which 
                                  would be an increase of approximately $4.05 for homeowners in Sandy based on the 
                                  average priced home in Sandy. Council Member Robinson reviewed the proposed 
                                  amendments with the Council. Council Members Robinson and Stroud mentioned that a 
                                  great deal of time and thoughtful consideration went into the development of this proposal, 
                                  and many changes were included based on resident feedback and they thanked the 
                                  residents for their input. A lengthy Council discussion and comments followed.

                                  Public comment opened:
                                  Public comment closed.

                                  A motion was made to amend the tentative budget to include the Robinson-Stroud 
                                  proposal, as discussed this evening and to include the friendly amendment offered by 
                                  Council Member Stroud to include funding $50,000 for the management study supported 
                                  by the Council, which was not included in the originally proposed budget.



                                  Following the vote on the motion, Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda: General 
                                  Citizen Comment.
                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Alison Stroud to amend the 
                                  FY 2024 Tentative Budget to include the Robinsons-Stroud budget proposal, as 
                                  amended...The motion carried by the following vote:

4.         23-234                 Continued Public Hearing to consider the adoption of the FY 2023-24 
                                  Sandy City Budget. This item includes:

                                  Resolutions 23-23 C and 23-24 C of Sandy City, adopting tax rates for 
                                  Sandy City and the Alta Canyon Recreation District for the fiscal year 
                                  commencing July 1, 2023 and ending June 30,2024; also establishing 
                                  certain fees and charges in Sandy City for said fiscal year
                   Attachments:   Signed_Resolution 23-23C
                                  Signed_Resolution 23-24C
                                  Click here to eComment on this item.
                                  23-23C Property Tax Rate Adoption - City (Revised).pdf
                                  23-24C Property Tax Rate Adoption - Alta Canyon (Revised).pdf

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director: The Public Hearing on the Tentative FY2024 Tentative 
                                  Budget was Continued to this Council Meeting. Based on the motion that was just 
                                  passed to amend the tentative budget to include the Robinson-Stroud proposal, 
                                  Resolution 23-23C would need to be amended to reflect the approximately 14.9% tax 
                                  rate. Council discussion followed.

                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Scott Earl. The Council Chair 
                                  opened the public comment period.

                                  Public Hearing comment period opened:

                                  Mr. Steve VanMaren expressed concerns about the fee structure for 
                                  residents/non-residents at Alta Canyon Pool.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones expressed support for long-term funding for the Fire Department.

                                  Ms. Dawn Sidwell expressed support for continued funding of the community barbecue in 
                                  Historic Sandy. She is not supportive of funding the Alta Canyon Recreation Center and 
                                  mentioned that 2023 is an election year.

                                  Public hearing comment period closed. 

                                  A motion was made by Zach Robinson, seconded by Scott Earl to adopt 
                                  Resolution 23-23C, amending the tentative budget to include the Robinson-Stroud 
                                  proposal, reinstating the Parks & Recreation marketing position and using 
                                  ongoing money, meant for one-time purposes, and Council Contingency to fund 
                                  $50,000 towards management studies; and adopting a tax rate on all real and 
                                  personal property in Sandy City for the Fiscal Year commencing July 1, 2023 and 
                                  ending June 30, 2024; also establishing certain fees and charges in Sandy City for 
                                  said fiscal year...The motion carried by the following vote:
