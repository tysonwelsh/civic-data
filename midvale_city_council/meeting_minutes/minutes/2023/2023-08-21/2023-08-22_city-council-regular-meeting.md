       A.     A PUBLIC HEARING REGARDING AN INCREASE OF THE RATE OF
              TAX FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2023
Mariah Hill reported on June 20,2023 the Midvale City Council voted to move into the
Truth in Taxation process in order to generate sufficient revenue to cover the basic
services provided by the City. ln keeping with state law - the property tax increase was
noticed by the County on July 16 and July 23 in the Salt Lake Tribune. The City will notice
the increase and the public hearing in the Salt Lake Tribune as well on August 20, 2023.
The notice was also posted on various online noticing websites. This set of notices and
the public hearing tonight satisfy the laws surrounding the Truth in Taxation process.

Staff proposes increasing the revenue generated by the Certified Tax Rate by $99,086,
a 3.36% increase. This is the amount of additional revenue needed to help fund the FY24
Unified Police Department Member Assessment increase of approximately $600,000,
provide appropriate COLA and merit increases to our staff, and continue to provide the
same level of service during a time of high inflation.

Property taxes will increase approximately $6.98 per year for residential households of
average value if the City Council approves this rate increase. After the conclusion of the
Truth in Taxation public hearing, the City Council will vote on the proposed tax rate.

  first regularly scheduled meeting in May. The City Manager and staff presented
  tentative budgets for the City in a Public Hearing on Tuesday, May 2, 2023. We briefed
  you on the revenue estimates, provided an overyiew of the expenditures and presented
  budget highlights. A detailed budget was also made available on the City's website
  www. midvalecity.org.

2. UCA 10-6-1 1 1 (3) further requires the City Council to adopt the tentative budget at the
