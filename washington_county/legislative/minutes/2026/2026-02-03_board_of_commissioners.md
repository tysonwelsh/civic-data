Met with Apple Valley mayor to discuss new Apple Valley
beekeeping ordinance. The proposed Apple Valley ordinance was
dropped from today's agenda thanks to the efforts of town council
|member Annie Spendiove. After the mayor does more research, it
will be on the agenda for April 22 (| plan to be there). | had a nice
talk with the mayor about the proposed Apple Valley ordinance
restricting beekeeping. His proposal was initiated by complaints
labout beekeepers not providing water for the bees which forces
bees to find water in dog bow!s and horse troughs. He has also
received complaints about beekeepers, probably sideliners or
commercial, with untidy apiaries. The bottom line there is that we
beekeepers need to be better neighbors. Beekeepers are their
lown worst enemy. His proposal was largely copied from
restrictions in other Utah communities which were recently voided
by Utah Code 4-11-116 and Utah rule R68-21. He now
understands that the ordinances upon which the Apple Valley
proposal was based are voided. | think he will be friendly to Apple
Valley beekeepers. He appreciates the need for honey bee
pollination and the mitigation of Africanization that managed
honey bees provide. 112 miles.
