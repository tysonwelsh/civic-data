       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood announced that the final day for the UtahGives.org campaign was
          approaching and noted that a donor was matching all contributions to the Promise
          South Salt Lake Foundation. Mayor Wood also encouraged residents to pick up
          "slow the flow" lawn signs from the foyer to promote water conservation. Mayor

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member deWolfe informed attendees that the City Council had discussed
          the Rules of Procedure during the Work Meeting and intended to move the item to
          a future meeting for further review. He also informed the business owners present
          that the agenda included an item to set a Public Hearing regarding the
          Transportation Utility Fee.

              Council Chair Bynum addressed the Parkway Avenue residents to let them know
              that the Parkway Avenue matter is not something that the Council currently has
              purview over as they are a legislative body and the Mayor-strong form of
              government provides for the Mayor and City staff to oversee the executive
              functioning of the City. She also addressed the Transportation Utility Fee matter and
              stated that the fee has been put on pause while the City and Council work through
              addressing the matter.

New Business
   1. Intent to Set a Public Hearing to Receive Public Input on the Proposed Amendments to
      the Transportation Utility Fee
      City Attorney, Josh Collins, addressed the City Council regarding the need to set a Public
      Hearing to receive input on proposed amendments to the Transportation Utility Fee. He
      explained that recent changes by the state legislature required a Public Hearing prior to
      the implementation or increase of such fees. He asked the City Council to publicly
      announce the hearing date to satisfy noticing requirements.

        Council Member Mitchell reiterated that the Public Hearing would take place on May
        13, 2026.

        Council Member Ray deWolfe then requested that the Chamber of Commerce assist
        with outreach to ensure local businesses were notified of the hearing.

        No formal action was taken on this matter.


Public Hearing
   1. To receive public input regarding a petition by applicant, dbURBAN, to vacate the
        remaining portion of Wentworth Avenue near 107 West Utopia and 2194 South West
        Temple.
        City Planner, Teresa Bagdasarova, presented a petition from DB Urban Communities to
        vacate the remaining portion of Wentworth Avenue between 107 West Utopia Avenue
        and 2194 Southwest Temple. She explained that the vacation would allow developers to
        assemble the land for the Utopia project and the Aki project, a 48-unit mixed-income
        housing development.

        The public hearing opened at 7:35 p.m.

        South Salt Lake resident, Hazel Stout, asked for clarification on what the project was.

        Ms. Bagdasarova explained that this would vacate a public right of way to allow for the
        multi-family development project.

        The public hearing closed at 7:36 p.m.

        A copy of the Ordinance is attached and incorporated by this reference.

        Council Chair Bynum made a motion to approve the street vacation by Ordinance.
