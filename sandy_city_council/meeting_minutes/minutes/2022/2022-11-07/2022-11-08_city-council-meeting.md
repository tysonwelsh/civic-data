3.         22-410                 Parks and Recreation presenting information regarding options for Alta 
                                  Canyon Recreation Center for Council discussion
                   Attachments:   November 8 2022 Memorandum

                                  Dan Medina and Ms. Stephanie Julh with the Alta Canyon Advisory Committee provided 
                                  information to the Council regarding the committee's feedback on the proposed options 
                                  for the Alta Canyon Recreation Center. The Advisory Committee recommended Option D 
                                  - the new build option. The committee suggested the following amenities be considered 
                                  by the Council to be included in the design of the recreation center: multi-use indoor 
                                  courts, indoor walking track, cardio and aerobics areas, weight room, and child care 
                                  room. The committee did not want an outdoor enclosure for the pool. Council questions 
                                  and comments followed. Mr. Medina discussed the next steps in the process to move 
                                  forward. Council discussion, comments and questions followed.

4.         22-412                 Administrative Services Department presenting Impact Fee Analysis 
                                  Findings
                   Attachments:   Sandy City IFFP  IFA Preliminary Review (10.25.2022)
                                  Sandy City Combined IFFP  IFA (REVIEW DRAFT 10.25.2022)
                                  Amended Sandy City IFFP  IFA Council Review (11.8.2022).pdf
                                  Amended Sandy City Combined IFFP  IFA (COUNCIL REVIEW 
                                  DRAFT 11.8.2022).pdf
                                  Impact Fee Ordinance Draft.docx

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director, introduced Fred Philpot with LYRB LLC who presented an 
                                  informational report and overview of the Impact Fee Analysis (IFA). He provided an 
                                  explanation and introduction to impact fees, detailed the impact fee process, the 
                                  preparation of the Impact Fee Facilities Plan (IFFP) and analysis. Mr. Philpot presented a 
                                  summary of proposed fees, a growth comparison, and detailed the next steps to move 
                                  forward which include finalizing the IFFP/IFA based on Council feedback and the noticing 
                                  and holding of a public hearing at a future City Council Meeting. 

5.         22-404                 Mayor Zoltanski requesting the City Council's consent to the appointment of 
                                  Jeffrey Bassett as Fire Chief.
                   Attachments:   Fire Chief_Job Specifications.pdf

                                  Mayor Zoltanski nominated Mr. Jeffrey Bassett for the position of Sandy City Fire Chief. 
                                  Mayor Z spoke about the selection process for this position and the qualifications of Mr. 
                                  Basset.

                                  The Council thanked Mr. Bassett for joining the Council meeting this evening. Mr. Bassett 
                                  responded to Council questions. He spoke about his vision for the fire department and the 
                                  culture he would like to develop within the department. Mr. Bassett spoke about his 
                                  criteria and goals for staffing crews, employee development, and leadership style. The 
                                  next step in the process for Council consideration and action is Advice and Consent 
                                  which will be scheduled for next week's council meeting. 

                                  Council moved to Item 2 on the Agenda.

6.         22-386                 Council Member Ryan Mecham presenting a report of his Official Sister 
                                  City visit with Riesa, Germany in honor of the Twentieth Anniversary of the 
                                  Sister City Relationship between Sandy City and Riesa
                   Attachments:   20 Years of Town Twinning between Sandy and Riesa
                                  Presentation.pdf

                                  Council Member Ryan Mecham presented to the Council his report on his Official Visit to 
                                  our Sister City, Riesa, Germany, in commemoration of the Twentieth Anniversary. This 
                                  partnership began as a cultural exchange in 2002 and the two cities have enjoyed many 
                                  exchanges over the years. Council Member Mecham shared a power point presentation 
                                  detailing his trip and experience in Riesa. He will present a Resolution honoring the 
                                  twentieth anniversary to Mayor Zoltanski at next week's council meeting. 

7.         22-407                 First Reading: Council Member Houseman requesting feedback from the 
                                  Council on her proposal to fund the rebuild of Fire Station 31with a General 
                                  Obligation Bond
                     Sponsors: Houseman

                   Attachments:   Draft Resolution_November 8.pdf

                                  Council Member Marci Houseman presented to the Council a proposal to fund the rebuild 
                                  of Fire Station 31 with a General Obligation Bond. Ms. Houseman provided details and 
                                  asked the Council for their feedback. The Council provided feedback and direction to Ms. 
                                  Houseman and was generally supportive of continuing the process to explore the General 
                                  Obligation Bond process for funding future infrastructure projects.
