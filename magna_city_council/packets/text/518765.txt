                                             PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is proposing to rezone the property from the current R-M/zc (Residential zone with zoning conditions) to C-2
(Commercial) with the intent for a commercial retail use on the property (7-11 convenience store).

Currently the following are zoning conditions on this property:
    1. All uses in the zone are conditional.
    2. Building square footage limited to 5,000 square feet.
    3. Height of buildings limited to 1 story and 25 feet to the midpoint of the roof.
    4. Residential density is limited to 7 dwelling units per acre.




                              SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located on the northwest corner of the intersection of 8400 West and 3500 South.

The property to the direct north and abutting west is zone residential R-1-6.

The corner on the southwest corner is zoned residential R-M. The northeast and the southeast corners are both zoned
commercial C-2.

                                         PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
The request is compatible with the General Plan Map and the goals of the Magna Community.

Regarding the zoning conditions that have been in place on this property:
    • Condition #1:        The zoning conditions that are currently in place on this property were put in place in 2001.
        There have been several changes in Utah State Law since then and some of these changes have made it harder for
        Conditional Use applications to be reasonably and lawfully denied. Therefore, staff does not recommend keeping
        this zoning condition on the property.
    • Condition #2:        This zoning condition limits building square footage to 5,000 square feet. Due to the size of the
        property, the required setbacks and the parking requirements would most likely keep a proposed building at/or
        under the 5,000 square foot size. Staff would recommend that this condition remain with the property, in order
        to keep consistency and compatibility with the size of the homes next to it.
    • Condition #3:        Due to the single level single family homes to the northwest of this property, staff believes it may
        be beneficial for the height of future buildings on this property be kept under the existing zoning condition of 1
        story and 25’ to the midpoint of the roof. Therefore, staff recommends keeping this condition with the property.
