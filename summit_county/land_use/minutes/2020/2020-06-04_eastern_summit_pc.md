amendment is proposed to correct three plat issues. All four owners are in agreement with 
the proposed amendment. Some lot lines are not where they should be. Apache Circle, the 
access road, is not built as platted. There are two existing residences on the relevant lots. 
No public comment was received in advance of the public hearing. There are no issues with

Findings of Fact:  
1. The applicants, Joel and Peggy Savage are requesting a plat amendment for Lots 46-49 of the 
Pine Meadow Ranch Plat A subdivision.  
2. The property is in the Agriculture-80 (Ag-80) zone.  
3. Lots 49 and 48 are currently vacant while 46 and 47 have single family homes built on them.  
4. The applicants are the owners of Lot 49.  
5. The owners of Lots 46-48 have consented to this application.  
6. There is an existing property line that runs encroaches onto each of the Lots.  
7. This line was not created through the Plat Amendment process.  
8. Built Apache Circle does not match platted Apache Circle.  
9. All property owned by the property owner in the immediate area is included in the application.

Recorder, North Summit Fire, and various State and Federal Agencies found no significant 
issues with the amendment.  
 
Conclusions of Law:

2. No one will be materially harmed by this Plat Amendment.  
3. The Plat Amendment is consistent with the Eastern Summit County General Plan, as amended.

3. Public hearing and possible action for the proposed plat amendment for Wanship 
Cottage Sites Lots 71 and 73, which includes parcels WS-71, WS-72, WS-72-A and WS-
73, located at 2975 South Cottonwood Drive, Tamra Wirthlin, Applicant – Tiffanie 
Northrup-Robinson, Senior County Planner 
 
Planner Northrup-Robinson shared the background for this application. The subdivision 
was originally platted in 1961. The owner of lot 72 split the lot in 1970, legally creating lot 
72 and 72-A. Wirthlin and Harrington each want to incorporate their portion of lot 72 into 
their adjactent lots. The plat amendment would also add a building pad to Lot 71, which is 
adjacent to the river beneath Rockport Reservoir. Staff recommends approval of the plat 
amendment according to the findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions of 
Approval in the Staff Report. 
Commissioner Rydalch stated the concern that the plat amendment would allow more 
development rights. Planner Northrup-Robinson answered that all development rights 
on lot 72 would be extinguished so overall density will be reduced. 
Commissioner Sonntag asked if there are building rights for Lot 72. Planner Northrup-
Robinson answered the owners each have the right to build on Lot 72 and 72-A, but there

Findings of Fact:

10. The proposed amended plat would incorporate lot WS-72 into lot WS-71, creating a 0.34

12. The proposed amended plat depicts a building pad for lot WS-71.  
13. Service providers have reviewed the plat for compliance with applicable standards.  
14. Staff has reviewed the plat for compliance with applicable Development Code standards.  
 
Conclusions of Law:

4. Public hearing to discuss and possibly recommend amendments to the Eastern 
Summit County Development Code Chapter 3: Front Setbacks. Front setback if 
property lines extend to the center of a public road and Front setback if property 
lines extend to the center of a private access road. These amendments would be 
affective for zones AG-2.5, AG-5, AG-10, AG-20, AG-40, AG-80 and CA zone districts – 
Tiffanie Northrup-Robinson, Senior County Planner 
 
Planner Northrup-Robinson shared the background for this application. The first 
Planning Commission meeting in May included a Work Session to consider modifying side 
setbacks in the Development Code. The Planning Commission chose to consider the issue at 
a later time. One concern was to make it clearer where a setback is measured from. The 
clarification has been made that setbacks will be measured from the closest structure to be 
built. This concern and clarification is asked about all the time. The final decision for the 
amendments will be made by the County Council. Staff recommends the Planning 
Commission forward a positive recommendation to the County Council for the proposed 
amendments. 
Commissioner Rydalch suggested that a change be made to the front setback section to 
include that the front setbacks be measured from the closest edge. 
Commissioner Clyde asked if the change would apply to agricultural buildings. Planner 
Northrup-Robinson answered that the change would apply to all buildings.

Commissioner Clyde stated he is fully supportive of the amendments and would like to 
discuss amendments to side and rear setbacks sooner than later. 
 
The Public hearing was opened. No comment was made and the Public hearing was closed.
