BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Deviation for approximately 1 acre located on
the south side of 12500 S, at approximately 1602 E 12500 S. (Exhibit B and C). The property
is currently zoned RA2 and is located within a mapped area where I-ADU’s are prohibited
without approval of a deviation. The applicant is requesting that a Deviation be approved
to allow for the creation of an I-ADU within a proposed Primary Dwelling on the subject
property.

The Primary Dwelling is set to be demolished and rebuilt with the intent of building an
additional unit above the main garage. Homes in this neighborhood are within the areas on
the map referenced in DCMC Section 9-31-090 that are prohibited from containing and I-
ADU. Deviations from this prohibition are permitted when the Planning Commission finds
that the property meets the deviation standards of DCMC Section 9-31-055.

According to Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2
zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal
services; to generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote and
preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited numbers of
animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be detached single-family
dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.”

Site Plan Layout. The site plan, elevation drawings, and floor plans that have been provided
with the ADU application (Exhibits F, G and H) show a larger two-story home built on a long
lot, set near to the street with the majority of the acreage located in the rear yard. The
proposed home has a large attached four (4) car garage to the rear of the home that is
side-loading from the west. The I-ADU is located above the attached garage. The entrance
to the I-ADU is located at the back of the garage in the rear.

Parking. The site plan shows an existing driveway on the east of the property that provides
access to an existing detached garage in the rear. Should there be two driveways in the
final development plans reviewed and approved by staff, they will be required to comply
with the driveway standards of DCMC Section 9-10-075. The attached four (4) car garage
provides adequate parking for the Primary Dwelling and the I-ADU. The applicant has not
indicated on the site plan where they intend to designate the I-ADU parking as stated in
their request (Exhibit I), however as a condition of approval the applicant can indicate on a
revised plan were these will be located. Parking for ADU’s may not impede use of the
required parking spaces for the Primary Dwelling.

Architecture. The elevation drawings (Exhibit G) depict the proposed new home to be built
and the attached garage with the I-ADU above it. The proposed elevation drawings are in
compliance with the intent of DCMC Section 9-31-045(B)(5) that the exterior appearance of
the primary dwelling is not altered in a manner that would appear other than as a single-
family dwelling. The floor plans depict the layout of said addition and I-ADU (Exhibit H).

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a Deviation request for an I-ADU is found
in Section 9-31-055 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:
    A. Deviation Authorized: A deviation from strict compliance with the prohibition of I-ADUs
        on properties located within the areas identified on the I-ADU map in Exhibit A of this
        chapter may be approved by the planning commission if all of the following criteria are
        met and the planning commission finds that:
        1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-thousand
            square feet (20,000 ft²);
        2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
        3. The proposed I-ADU meets all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-045,
            including other applicable requirements of this chapter; and
