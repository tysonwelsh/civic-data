3.7) Public hearing and consideration of Jeff Southard’s request for conditional use
approval of the Bellaview Commercial site plan with drive-thru's located at 1200 West
3600 North.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Everett asked for clarification on the drive aisle and the exceptions.
Commissioner Newall asked about the exceptions. Craig Chambers stated an exception will
always have to be granted. Kim Struthers recommended the phases of the fencing should be
brought up when they needed to be installed.

The applicant, Jeff Southard, was present. He stated the three drive thru's are part of phase one.
All the drive thru's are conditional uses and the code states they "shall" be approved. He stated
this type of layout has been approved numerous times and the applicant has worked with staff to
ensure they have met safety standards and walkability. Commissioner Everett asked if there were
alternative layouts. Mr. Southard stated the drive aisles were critical to the plan. He stated they
will do the fencing in phase one. They will install a 6-foot opaque fence. He does not know the
hours of operation.

Commissioner Newall opened the public hearing.

Scott Williams – He wanted to recognize development is inevitable. The speed is out of control,
and he has concerns about this and other developments in the area. Hours of operation is a
concern, kids crossing the street to attend school is a concern. He hasn't seen an effort to mitigate
congestion. He doesn't recognize this is an area to develop but would like to see if something can
be done.
Heather Hill - Her concern is safety. Her son was leaving Belleview parking lot and was almost
hit by a car. She was almost hit by a car because of the sun being bright and almost got hit by a
car. She is concerned about the traffic because of the request being an abnormal commercial
proposal. The development is on a slope, creating a visual obstacle.

Ryan Hill - When this property was annexed it was brought in as commercial. He understands
this is a conditional use, but it is surrounded by houses. The constant traffic that will be coming
in and out from 1200 West will create a lot of congestion. He would like to see a substantial
fence put in to help with noise. He would like to see phase one, including the fence being
installed near the residential area. He would like to see that the applicant and developer be held
to the standard if approved. But he is opposed to the request.

Tyler Anderson - He is supportive of development, but he feels like this is a better fit for
neighborhood commercial. He would recommend that the drive thru not be allowed. He read
some previous comments from commissioners that were brought from prior requests. He would
like to see conditional uses that better protect the residents. (Handed out a document to include in
record). He feels like a fence that is included in phase one is important as a barrier and making it
a condition. He is concerned about light and noise pollution. He researched hours of operation
and recommended 7:00 am to 9:00 pm. He would like to make sure garbage is located at least 30
feet from residential areas.

Zane Carter - He was concerned with the drive aisle and safety. There are going to be about 40
new homes built near Fox Hollow, adding more traffic. The traffic is terrible on 1200 West, He
feels that drive-thrus will add a lot of congestion, especially with pullouts. His main concern is
safety for kids.

Derek Owens - Walkable space was mentioned earlier, he feels like providing walkable
amenities doesn't make sense while providing drive-thru. He is concerned about the traffic and
school children being so close. He would like the commissioners to take precautions and think
about everyone involved.

Mike Shriner - He was concerned, and he feels like this is potentially the worst use for this
property. There are no locations with three drive-thrus in Lehi, on this small property. Every exit
out of the lot is a left hand turn. It is a safety concern. He would like to see the city to conduct a
traffic study. He is concerned about traffic congestion, safety for auto-pedestrian accidents, and
does not feel this is justified as an exception. He is opposed to the item being approved.

Jacob Curtis acknowledged and comments that were submitted online.

Commissioner Newall closed the public hearing.

Kyle Spencer, engineering representing the applicant, wanted to emphasize the traffic engineer
who conducted the study was very professional. The traffic engineer factored all the numbers
into the report. There are some excessive speeds on 1200 West. He felt the drive thru will act as
a traffic calming measure.

Brad Kenison stated he has had a chance to review the traffic study. he did not see a reference to
the drive aisle. The traffic study seemed to address the access points. The access point to 1200
West is being realigned. The traffic study did not recommend dedicated left-hand turns.

The Commissioners discussed the conditional use and the permitted use, and whether it had
reasonable conditions to be denied.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve of Jeff Southard’s request for
        conditional use approval of the Bellaview Commercial site plan with drive-thru's
        located at 1200 West 3600 North; including all DRC comments and findings stated in
        the report. Noting the reasonable conditions based on the impacts of the adjoining
        properties where the schools exist, which constitute building a fence during phase one,
        being 8 feet tall and constructed of concrete, precast, or solid another solid material with
        the reason it will diminish light pollution and noise as it is adjacent to residential
        property. If the light pollution and noise standards meet city code standards, the hours of
        operation be limited to 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., which include garbage pickup times and
        the location at least 30 feet from any residential zone. That the speaker boxes will meet
        the proper decibel level, excluding the retention pond. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, no; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed 3 – 2.

Jeff Carter - He stated mixed use is a good thing but feels precedence has been set with a two

Scott Alstrom - His biggest concern is that the road between Vets Park taking 300 West and

The General Plan has designated this as mixed use.

3.9) Public hearing and recommendation of Parker Johnson’s request for review of the
Beacon Point Concept, a 17-unit mixed-use residential development located at 1054 West
Main Street.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the item.

The applicant, Parker Johnson, and Larry Lindstrom, were present. The plan is to move the
building as far south as possible. They will be providing adequate parking, exceeding the
requirements. They will be installing access on 1100 West, eventually having an ingress and an
egress. The applicants plan on installing a six foot privacy fence to the north. The landscaping
requirements will be met. Mr. Johnson stated they will also be exceeding the green space
requirement. Kim Struthers stated they will need to install some covered parking per city code
and because of the concern with limited open space, there may need to be a reduction of density
for open space.

Commissioner Newall opened the public hearing.

Brett Blood - Asked if there was a measurement for the garbage can to the back of the homes. He
is concerned about how they will be contained with animals. He also asked about a tree that is on
the historical registry. There are 17 conceptual units, and he is concerned about the adequate
parking.

Scott Alstrom - He understands the concept is conceptual. He is concerned about privacy with a
three story building so close. He has concerns with water drainage. He is concerned about traffic
in an area that does not have a sidewalk when trying to avoid Main Street.

Jeff Carter - He had concerns about the garbage placement. Light pollution is concerning so the
recommendation for a different parking layout would be preferable.

Dave - One of the reasons he lives in the cul-de-sac is for privacy. The second multi-use is built,
people are going to get creative to not have to use Main Street. He is concerned about traffic
using the back roads to get around it.

Commissioner Newall closed the public hearing.
Parker Johnson stated the parking will include the shared parking calculation. Larry Lindstrom
stated the garbage will be enclosed to prevent access to animals.

The Commissioners discussed the concept plan and recommendations for the development.
There were concerns with the infrastructure.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to forward a negative recommendation of Parker
        Johnson’s request for review of the Beacon Point Concept, a 17-unit mixed-use
        residential development located at 1054 West Main Street; with the findings that the
        present information of sewage and other factors are inadequate for the Planning
        Commission to see that this functions with the current limitations but urged the City
        Council to take any further action to make the decision. Commissioner Everett seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

        Motion to take a 10 minute recess by Commissioner Eyre. Seconded by Commissioner
        Jackson.
