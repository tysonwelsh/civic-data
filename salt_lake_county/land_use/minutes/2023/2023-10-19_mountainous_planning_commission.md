PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
                                                  Hearing began at – 3:10 p.m. 
 
OAM2023-000994 - An ordinance amending the following sections of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances, 2001: 19.12.020 (permitted uses in the FR zone), 19.12.030(g)(conditional uses in the FR 
zone), and 19.54.020 (permitted uses in the FA zone) to have distinct regulati ons in the FR and FA zones 
with regards to animal uses and their associated impacts on adjoining properties, the environment, and the 
well-being of animals; and making other related changes. Presenter: Brian Tucker and Zach 
Shaw (Motion/Voting) 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the 
amendment and proposed changes. 
 
Speaker # 1: SLCo Engineering 
Name: Bob Thompson 
Address: 2001 South State Street 
Comments: Provided a presentation and asks that the amendment be approved as provided. 
 
Commissioners, staff and counsel had a brief discussion regarding existing and new/rebuilt structures and 
google screenshots of the areas along the creek upon adoption of the ordinance, existing fencing or corral 
crossing over streams, and if new fencing or structures would need to have setbacks, 25% additional 
setback reduction for riparian environment and enforcement.  
 
Commissioner Knoblock motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Omer seconded that motion. 
Motion passed. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Cathy Williams 
Address: 15191 South Rose Canyon Road 
Comments: Ms. Williams said her concerns about manure removal during cold weather. Most horses living 
there do not have e. coli. Invite the bureau of land management  to address problems on their land , horses 
are being ridden all summer long and BLM should supply water for their horses. There will be a smelter on 
the south side of Kennecott and will take decades to get clean water back. How is code enforcement going 
to enforce with not enough manpower. 
 
Speaker # 3: Citizen 
Name: Patricia Stanko 
Address: 15145 South Rose Canyon Road 
Comments: Ms. Stanko provided a YouTube drone video and explained the areas, contending that riparian 
corridor already exists; largest mountain lions in the state in the area. Everyone goes to the creek to drink, 
and horses aren’t just drinking there. Horses do not carry e. coli, and many other animals are in the creek.  
Parking lots full of horse trailers.

Name: Kim Zacherson

Comments: Ms. Zacherson said she heard the county is being fined and looking at another fine. She has

Commissioner Omer motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Palmer seconded that motion.  
Motion passed. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Mr. Shaw advised community councils were just notified and have not had an opportunity to review and 
requested this item be continued to the November meeting after the public hearing. 
 
Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding  investigating e. coli in horses and 
whether that is a problem and if we need to mitigate different animals contributing to the e. coli . Wildlife 
doesn’t need to be mitigated in watershed. Sites say horses don’t carry e. coli and other sources say cows 
up in the canyon carry e. coli and horses carry e. coli but not the ones that are hazardous. Mr. Thompson 
identified markers and certain testing has only been done a few times because of the cost. EPA gives tools 
to work with the problem. 
 
Motion: To continue application #  OAM2023-000994 An ordinance amending the following sections of 
the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001: 19.12.020 (permitted uses in the FR zone), 
19.12.030(g)(conditional uses in the FR zone), and 19.54.020 (permitted uses in the FA zone) to have 
distinct regulations in the FR and FA zones with regards to animal uses and their associated impacts on 
adjoining properties, the environment, and the well-being of animals; and making other related changes to 
the November 16th planning commission meeting after the community councils have an opportunity to make 
a recommendation.  Staff to prepare a report with scientific data of E coli testing up and down stream of 
area of concern.  
Motion by: Commissioner Knoblock
