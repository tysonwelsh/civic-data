WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JULY 16, 2019

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Dean Cox at 4:00 p.m. on July 16, 2019, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Chairman Dean Cox, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Eric Clarke, Commission Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy
County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

EMERGENCY SERVICES REPORT

Washington County Sheriff Cory Pulsipher stated that there have been a total of twelve
(12) Search and Rescue (SAR) incidents, with a total of 282 man-hours from June 18, 2019
to July 16, 2019.

Hurricane Valley Fire Chief Pete Kuhlmann stated that there were multiple fires started by
fireworks during 4" of July celebrations. Mr. Kuhlmann cautioned citizens to be aware of
fire restrictions in place and be cognizant when lighting fireworks for the upcoming Pioneer
Day celebrations.

CONSIDERATION _ OF ORDINANCE NO. 2019-1143-O0, AN ORDINANCE
REZONING A CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY NEAR
ENTERPRISE FROM THE OST-20 (OPEN SPACE TRANSITION 20 ACRE

MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE TO THE A-5 (AGRICULTURE 5 ACRE MINIMUM

LOT SIZE) ZONE

Washington County Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that
property owners near Enterprise desire to construct a home on approximately 5.77 acres.
Currently, the property does not meet the minimum lot size for the current zone.

The zone change request was reviewed by the Planning Commission and unanimously
recommended for approval.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1143-
O, An Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County
Near Enterprise from the OST-20 (Open Space Transition 20 Acre
minimum Lot Size) Zone to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 Acre Minimum Lot
Size) Zone. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2019-1144-0, AN ORDINANCE
REZONING A CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY NEAR LEEDS
FROM THE A-20 (AGRICULTURAL 20 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE TO
THE A-5 (AGRICULTURAL 5 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE

Mr. Messel explained that property owners near Leeds desire to operate a winery as their
property is currently a vineyard. The parcel does not meet the minimum lots size
requirement of the A-20 Zone and the property owner is requesting to bring the property
into compliance with this zone change.

The Planning Commission approved the conditional use permit for a winery subject to this
zone change request approval.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1144-
O, An Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County
Near Leeds from the A-20 (Agricultural 20 Acre Minimum Lot Size)
Zone to the A-5 (Agricultural 5 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2492, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING AMENDED PUBLIC DEFENDER AGREEMENTS

Commissioner Almquist explained that the County applied for and was awarded grant
funding from the Utah Indigent Defense Commission to supplement the county funds spent
on indigent defense services. Part of the grant award includes increases in the contract
amount for the public defense attorneys. The grant also provides for an additional public
defender to be added to the current public defense team which will be filled at a later date.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2019-
2492, A Resolution Approving Amended Public Defender Agreements.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist
voting aye.
