                              RESOLUTION NO. 23-64

     A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING CHRIS
     SAYLER TO SERVE AS AN ALTERNATE DELEGATE TO THE TRANS
     JORDAN LANDFILL BOARD OF DIRECTORS.

      WHEREAS, Draper City is a member community of the Trans Jordan Landfill,
an entity created pursuant to interlocal agreement; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City is required to designate a principal and alternate
delegates to the Board of Directors of the Trans Jordan Landfill; and

      WHEREAS, Chris Sayler is to be appointed as the designated alternate
delegate replacing Hazel Dunsmore who is no longer employed by Draper City.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Appointment. Chris Sayler is hereby appointed as the alternate
delegate to represent Draper City at meetings of the Trans Jordan Landfill Board.

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part or provision of the Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections parts and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 6TH DAY OF DECEMBER, 2023.
