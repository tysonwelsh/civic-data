PC 25-027 Cache Valley Transit District Rezone [Zone Change] Alex Bearnson, Jake Clark/Cache
Valley Transit District, authorized agent/owner, is requesting a zone change of 4.47 acres located at
754 West 600 North from Public (PUB) to Commercial Services (CS); TIN 05-061-0004 (Ellis
Neighborhood).

Staff: Russ Holley, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a rezone of 4.47 acres located at
754 West 600 North from Public (PUB) to Commercial Services (CS). The Cache Valley Transit
District (Connect) was located on this property before moving to a new facility in North Logan City
and is now requesting this rezone to sell the property. The current public zoning designation would
be appropriate if the property were to be purchased and used by a public entity; however, according
to the applicant, no public entities were interested in purchasing the property. R. Holley reviewed the


surrounding zoning, which includes Industrial to the north, Commercial to the southeast, and
Commercial Services to the southwest.

Proponent: No comment was made by the proponent.

Public: No public comment was made

MOTION: Commissioner Heare made a motion to forward a recommendation of approval to the
Municipal Council on a Zone Change for PC 25-027 Cache Valley Transit District Rezone with the

findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner McNamara seconded the motion. The motion
was approved unanimously.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. The Logan City FLUP identifies the area as PUB. PUB is only appropriate if the property owner
is a public entity.

2. 600 North is a regional boundary between IP to the north and CS, COM, and MU to the south,
and this application for CS is consistent with that pattern.

3. The CS zone permits and conditional permits land uses that are similar or compatible with the
current land uses and developments in the area.

4. The design standards, height limitations, and setbacks in the Land Development Code will
ensure compatibility for adjacent property use and enjoyment.

PC 25-028 Gibbons Park Estates Subdivision [Subdivision Permit] Steve Coppieters/Mammoth
Group LLC, authorized agent/owner, is requesting a Subdivision Permit to subdivide 3.69 acres into
10 single-family building lots located at approximately Red Fox Trace and Gibbons Parkway in the
Suburban Neighborhood Residential (NR-4) zone; TIN 02-003-0011 (Wilson Neighborhood).

Staff: Aimee Egbert, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a 10-lot subdivision on 3.69-acres
south of Gibbons Park at approximately 250 South 1400 East in the Suburban Neighborhood
Residential (NR-4) zone. The NR-4 zone allows a maximum density of four units per acre, a minimum
lot size of 10,000 square feet, and minimum lot widths of 90 feet. At 3.69 acres, the proposed
subdivision is approximately 2.71 homes per acre and is compliant with the NR-4 zone. The
proposed subdivision contains lots ranging in size from 10,000 square feet to 20,124 square feet,
with proposed lot widths at or above the 90-foot minimum width required.

Engineering condition number 26 requires the proposed subdivision road, Red Fox Court, to align
with the Red Fox Trace right of way to the west.

Proponent: Steve Coppieters said the proposed subdivision is exactly what the NR-4 zoning calls
for, and they have no issue aligning their proposed street with Red Fox Trace to the west.

Public: Chris Smith spoke about his concerns with the size of the homes that will be built in the
proposed subdivision, stating the homes will be too small and will not complement the existing homes
in the area.

Carl Curtis talked about the existing homes being devalued with the proposed smaller lots and
smaller homes and stated that the proposed subdivision will look like a trailer park next to the larger


homes in the area. Mr. Curtis also asked if the public will be able to review the proposed size and
design of the homes before they are approved to be built.

Russ Price asked if this property is above an elevation of 4,850 and would require compliance with
Land Development Code Chapter 17.24 for critical lands.

Michael McFarland spoke about additional traffic from the proposed subdivision being an issue and
the additional homes taking away from the water pressure in the area.

Commission: Commissioner Doutre asked what the maximum home size could be on the proposed
lots. Aimee Egbert stated that the homes can be a maximum of 50% of the lot size.

Commissioner Heare replied to a public comment, stating the Utah State Legislature passed HB
1003 in 2021, barring cities from any design restrictions on single-family homes. Commissioner
Heare also pointed out that eight of the proposed subdivision lots are .25 acres, one is .46 acres,
and one is .39 acres. With most of the existing properties surrounding the subdivision at .25 acres
and three properties at .3 acres, it appears the proposed subdivision fits in with the surrounding
properties.

Commissioner Peterson asked about the elevation. Aimee Egbert stated that the property is at an
elevation of 4,840 on the west side and an elevation of 4,875 on the east side. The building lots
above an elevation of 4,850 will need to comply with Land Development Code 17.24 for Critical
Lands.

Commissioner Doutre stated that the only way to require specific design elements or size
requirements would be through CC&Rs governed by an HOA which is up to the developer to
establish.
