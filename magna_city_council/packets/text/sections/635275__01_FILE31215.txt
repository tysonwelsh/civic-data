                                          PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant, Deana Rydalch, is requesting approval of a Conditional Use for a Day Care/Preschool at 9135 W.
Magna Main Street.


                            SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located on Magna Main Street. The building that Ms. Rydalch’ s proposed Day Care would be in,
is one of two suites in the building. The business in the other suite, next door is “The Door Church”.

The building that abuts the building directly to the west also has two suites in it. Next to Ms. Rydalch’ s suite is a
retail shop called “Utah Crystal Sanctuary”.



                                  GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS
The current General Plan for Magna Metro Township was adopted September 2012. The property is
located on a mapped General Plan corridor which is planned for an area of “focused change” and
encourages compatible development. Therefore, the development of this land is encouraged. It
should be noted that the General Plan for the Magna Metro Township is currently being updated by
the MSD Planning Staff.

                          ISSUES OF CONCERN/PROPOSED MITIGATION
The Building Official has concerns about whether the applicant can meet the building code requirements for a
change of occupancy to a day care.

The Building Official has said that “the new proposed use of the building as a daycare is complete change of
occupancy for the building and the code has some specific requirements of when a building changes its occupancy.
Some of these requirements may be very expensive to address. If the requirement for a structural assessment is
triggered, this is more than just making sure the building is still in good original condition. It could mean that the
building would have to be brought up to current seismic requirements.”.

                                      PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
19.84.050 Approval/Denial Authority
The planning commission has the authority to approve, deny, or approve with conditions conditional use
applications.

        A. Planning Commission Approval.
           1. The planning commission shall review and approve or deny each application during a public
               meeting.
           2. The planning commission's decision shall be based on information presented through the public
               meeting process, including: the materials submitted by the applicant, the recommendation of
               the director or director's designee, and input from interested parties and affected entities.
           3. If conditions are specified, the director or director's designee shall issue a final approval letter
               upon satisfaction of the planning commission's conditions of approval.
           4. If the applicant fails to meet all conditions of approval within twelve months of the planning
               commission's decision, the application is deemed denied. A twelve-month extension may be
               granted upon the payment of an additional filing fee equal to the original filing fee.
           5. A planning commission decision shall be made on a complete conditional use application within
               a reasonable time frame, not to exceed ninety days. The planning commission is authorized to
               review and take action on an application as outlined in Section 19.84.040 after having notified
               the applicant of the meeting date.

                             PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Due to the list of questions and concerns the Building Official has regarding this application, the MSD Planning
Staff believes it may be beneficial for the application to be continued until the Applicant has a chance to
address these questions and concerns.

If the Planning Commission would like to proceed with a decision for the application, the MSD Planning Staff
would recommend that the Planning Commission grant Conditional Use approval for a Day Care/Preschool
located at 9135 W. Magna Main Street, subject to the following conditions:

   1. That the applicant complies with the requirements of the building code review, and resolve all
      questions and concerns that the Building Official has listed in his Building Plan Review Comment list.
   2. That the applicant complies with all agency reviewer requirements and recommendations prior to
      building occupancy.
   3. That the applicant obtains a business license prior to building occupancy.
   4. No signs are approved with this Conditional Use approval. Any signs will require a separate land use
      application and land use approval.
   5. That the applicant provides a designated parking space in the rear of the building for drop off and
      pick-up of the children.

A5. The use of this building as a daycare is considered a change of use in the building code. The
    plans need to include a code analysis clarifying how the building is meeting the code
    requirements for the new occupancy. The code analysis should include at a minimum:
       A. The existing occupancy category of the building
       B. The occupant load of the existing/previous use.
       C. The new occupancy category of the building.
       D. Type of construction for the building
       E. Occupant load calculations for the building and the spaces (based on chapter 10 of the IBC
          which notes 35 sq. ft. net per occupant in a daycare)
       F. The age of the children (and number of children of the different age groups) that will be
          allowed at the daycare
       G. Any fire rated construction requirements and/or fire suppression requirements
       H. Plumbing fixture counts for the new occupancy
       I. Exiting requirements
       J. Any special requirements for the new occupancy
A6. Please note that once zoning/land use approval has been given, a building permit is required to
    change the occupancy of the building for the new daycare use as well as to complete any
    modifications necessary for the new occupancy. You will need to apply for that building permit
    will the same information as noted in this review and complete the building inspections under
    the building permit process.
A7. Per section 429.4.4 of the Utah State amendments to the IBC, all Group E child care centers with
    an occupant load more than 10 require a 2nd means of egress from the building. A roll up door
    does not count as an exit door. Will need provide a 2nd means of egress from the building.
A8. In relation to the previous comment, depending on the age of the children, an exit door directly
    to the exterior of the building would be required for each room being used for the daycare
    unless a fire sprinkler system was provided. (the roll up door does not count as an exit door)
A9. Besides the bathroom doors and the exterior exit doors, are any of the other rooms or spaces
    going to have doors on them or are they all going to remain open to each other?
A10. If a new exit door is proposed at the back wall, may need to address a ramp or stairs to meet
     code minimum requirements to the new door.
A11. Is food going to be prepared on site? Are any stoves, grills, or other cooking equipment going to
     be installed?
A12. Is the reception desk going to be a built-in furnishing or is it going to be movable furniture?
A13. Is the water heater electric or gas?

A14. Where is the furnace located for this building?
A15. Section 2902.2 of the IBC requires separate bathroom facilities for men and women once the
     occupant load gets over 15 for this type of occupancy. Please provide dimensions layouts for
     both bathrooms showing the fixture locations, door sizes, etc. to verify ADA compliance.
A16. Drinking fountains are also required for this type of occupancy once the occupant load gets over
     15. Please clarify on the plans where the required drinking fountains are going to be installed.
A17. The Utah State amendment to section 1006.3 of the 2018 IEBC, states, "1006.3 Seismic Loads.
     Where a change of occupancy results in a building being assigned to a higher risk category, or
     when a change of occupancy results in a design occupant load increase of 100% or more, the
     building shall satisfy the requirements of Section 1613 of the International Building Code using
     full seismic forces." There is an exception to this requirement if the increase of the design
     occupant load is less than 25 occupants. Based on the current proposal, it would appear that
     the occupant load of the space is going to more than double from the previous occupancy and
     the increase is going to be more than 25 occupants. As currently proposed, it would appear that
     this engineered analysis and any subsequent upgrades to the building would be required. Will
     need to provide the engineering analysis or provide justification that this requirement is not
     triggered with this change of occupancy.
A18. Just to clarify, the preceding comments and questions are preliminary in nature. There may be
     additional questions and comments once these initial ones have been responded to.
