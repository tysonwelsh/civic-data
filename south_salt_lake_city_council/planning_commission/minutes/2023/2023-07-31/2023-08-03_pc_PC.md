NEW BUSINESS

1.     Public Hearing – A Petition for a Conditional Use Permit Approval for a Category II
       Home Occupation, In-Home Nail Salon.

Action Item

Applicant:    Kesia Zarate
Address:      3209 South 400 East

Deputy Community Development Director, Eliza Ungricht, presented the Staff Report and stated that
the request is for a Conditional Use Permit for an in-home nail salon. The Code requires that any home
occupation business license with clients coming to the home requires a Conditional Use Permit. The
subject property is located at 3209 South 400 East. An aerial of the property was displayed. The home
is located on the corner of Woodland Avenue and 400 East. The street view was presented. The
applicant has indicated that no more than one customer will be at the home at any given time. Anyone
who works at the home occupation must live in the home. As a result, the applicant, Kesia Zarate will
be the only one working there. Ms. Zarate can accommodate four vehicles in her driveway. The City
requires the applicant to provide only one off-street parking stall per Code.

Ms. Ungricht indicated that the new business will add a new business to South Salt Lake and will be a
resident-based business. The proposed conditions of approval were referenced as enumerated in the
Staff Report. Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the application as conditioned.

Chair Carter opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing was
closed.

The applicant was not present. Chair Carter’s position was that if an applicant is not present they
should wait for approval until the next meeting. He commented that the Commission has taken the
time to be present as has staff. He thought it was respectful for the applicant to show up as well.
Ms. Ungricht stated that an intern has been communicating with the applicant but he had an
emergency earlier today and may not have communicated with the applicant.

Commissioner Spencer stated that as long as the Commission does not have questions for the
applicant she saw no reason to postpone approval. Commissioner Dahlberg agreed and commented
that home occupations are beneficial to the City and expressed her full support.

Motion to APPROVE the application submitted for a Conditional Use Permit for Project
Number 2320023 for a Category II Home Occupation at 3209 South 400 East based on the
following:

Findings of Fact:

       1.     On June 29, 2023, the applicant applied for a Conditional Use Permit for a
              Category II Home Occupation for the dwelling at 3209 South 400 East.

       2.     The purpose of the application is to permit the lawful operation of a nail salon
              at the subject property.

       3.     A home occupation that involves customers and clients visiting the dwelling
              would be classified as a Category II Home Occupation per Title 17’s Land Use
              Matrix.

       4.     SSLC Code Section 17.01 defines a Home Occupation, Category II as “any
              business carried on entirely within a Dwelling by occupants of such Dwelling
              that is clearly incidental and secondary to the Residential Use of the Dwelling,
              has no outside impacts on the neighborhood in which it is located, and does not
              change the character of the Dwelling or the neighborhood in which it is located.
              Home Occupation, Category II includes clients/customers going to the
              Dwelling.”

       5.     The previous use of the subject property was a single-family dwelling without a
              Home Occupation as a secondary use.

       6.     Redevelopment of a property requires all current municipal codes to apply to
              the proposal, including development standards, use standards, and design
              standards.

       7.     The subject property is in the R-1 land use district.

       8.     SSLC Code Section 17.03, classifies a Home Occupation, Category II as a
              Conditional Use in the R-1 land use district, triggering the need for a
              Conditional Use Permit.

       9.     The Planning Commission is the land use authority over all Conditional Use
              Permits.

       10.    The subject property is 0.24 acres in size.

       11.    The subject property is a legally subdivided lot.

       12.    SSLC Code Section 17.06.040 requires, prior to issuance of a Building Permit,
              that all new Development activity be on a legal Lot.

       13.    The subject property has vehicular and pedestrian access along 400 East.

       14.    The subject property has 83 feet of frontage along 400 East.

Conclusions of Law:

        1.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, is consistent with the South Salt Lake
              City General Plan.

        2.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, is compatible in use, scale, and design
              with allowed uses in the R-1 land use district.

        3.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, does not compromise the health,
              safety, or welfare of persons employed within or using the proposed
              development nor those residing or working in the vicinity of the proposed use;
              nor does the proposed compromise Development, Property, or improvements in
              the vicinity of the proposed use or development.

        4.    The use, as specifically conditioned below, does not impose disproportionate
              burdens on the citizens of the City.

Conditions of Approval:

        1.    The applicant shall retain one (1) off-street parking stall for client use.

        2.    The applicant will conduct all business activity indoors so as to not create any
              nuisance for surrounding property owners.

        3.    Clients will be served by appointment only and within the hours of 9:00 am to
              6:00 p.m.

        4.    The applicant must obtain a sign permit for any sign related to business
              activities.

        5.    The applicant may only have one sign on the property related to business
              activities. Sign may not exceed two (2) square feet in area. Sign must be located
              on or against the home.
