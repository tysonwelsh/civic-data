ORDINANCE NO. 19-2025

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
TO ADOPT THE INTERNATIONAL PROPERTY MAINTENANCE CODE (IPMC).

WHEREAS, Herriman City Code § 9-1-1 currently adopts multiple construction
and safety-related codes by reference.

WHEREAS, The International Property Maintenance Code (IPMC) establishes
minimum standards for the maintenance, sanitation, structural integrity, and safety of
existing buildings and properties.

WHEREAS, Without clear standards of a unified property maintenance code,
enforcement efforts can become fragmented, inconsistent, and less transparent for
Herriman residents.

WHEREAS, Adopting the IMPC would create clarity and uniformity with a single
reference document.

WHEREAS, The City Council finds that adopting the IMPC is in the best interest
of public health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. The City Council hereby adopts the
International Property Maintenance Code as follows:

9-1-1 Codes Adopted

By reference. The most recent edition adopted by the state of the following
construction codes and appendices: the International Building Code (IBC),
promulgated by the International Code Council, the amendments adopted under
the rules enacted by the state, together with standards incorporated into the IBC
by reference, the International Energy Conservation Code (IECC), the
International Residential Code (IRC), the International Plumbing Code (IPC), the
International Existing Building Code (IEBC) and the International Mechanical
Code (IMC), the International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC), the International Property
Maintenance Code (IPMC), the National Electrical Code (NEC), as revised,
amended and adopted by the state, are hereby adopted by the city and
incorporated herein by reference.


SECTION 2. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon passage.

APPROVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH, ON
THIS 27 DAY OF August, 2025.
