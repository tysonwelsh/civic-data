MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 20-06-01 DATE: June 9, 2020
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
DESIGNATING THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES
DISTRICT’S PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT SERVICES
DEPARTMENT AS MAGNA’S PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
SERVICES DEPARTMENT PURSUANT TO THE COLLECTION OF
CERTAIN FEES PER THE ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS APPROVED
AND ADOPTED IN ORDINANCE 20-05-01.
RECITALS

A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (““UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 ef seg.

B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body
for Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

C. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) is a Local District
that provides certain municipal-type services to Magna which include accounting and
financial management of Magna’s accounts.

D. Pursuant to State Law, the MSD’s Planning and Development Services Department
(“Planning”) has been providing Magna with Building Permits and Inspections,
Business Licensing, Code Enforcement, and other Planning and Development
services.

1

E. Due to the transition of Planning to an MSD-Provided Service, Magna’s Municipal
Code needed to be amended to clarify Planning’s ability to impose and collect certain
fees as a part of their office’s work for Magna.

F. Earlier in this meeting the Council approved and adopted Ordinance 20-05-01 making
those necessary amendments to the Magna Municipal Code allowing Planning to
impose and collect those fees.

G. This resolution hereby designates Planning as the Planning Department for Magna.

RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and

Council hereby designates the MSD’s Planning and Development Services Department as

Magna’s Planning and Development Services Department to fulfill the “Planning and

Development Services Department, or designee” definition from the amendments passed

in Ordinance 20-05-01.
