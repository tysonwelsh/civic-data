                                                          MINUTES
                                              WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                              Tuesday, April 17, 2018 - 10:00 a.m.
                                    Commission Chambers, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, Utah
  In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who
  appear and speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported
  facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

WEBER COUNTY COMMISSIONERS: James “Jim” H. Harvey, James Ebert and Kerry W. Gibson.
OTHER STAFF PRESENT: Ricky D. Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor; Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney; and
Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, who took minutes.
 A. WELCOME – Chair Harvey
 B. INVOCATION – Commissioner Ebert
 C. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE – Stacy Skeen
 D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY – Commissioner Gibson
 E. PRESENTATIONS:
 Presentation of the Crystal Bowl Awards.
 Chair Harvey noted that these Bowl Awards are given to individuals and businesses that give service to the community
 and presented these Bowl Awards. E. Rich and Jane Brewer have been recognized by business leaders for outstanding
 service. They have given tremendously to the community by participating in innumerous programs (i.e., business
 development in North Ogden City, McKay Dee Hospital Foundation (helping raise $17 million for the hospital), Ogden
 Industrial Corporation, the 2002 Olympics). Mr. Brewer served our country in the military. Chair Harvey is most
 impressed with the service the Brewers, who have been married 64 years, give to each other. The Brewers expressed
 thanks for the recognition stating that they follow the great service example of their parents, grandparents and others.
 Market Star, a company that began humbly in 1988 in Ogden, has grown to a global leader. They provide many
 thousands of volunteer hours as a company to the community. Phil Mickey expressed thanks and their love for Weber
 County, which is the company’s home, and for the opportunities to move forward here.
 Presentation of the Annual Victims of Crime Act Grant Report for the 2017-2018 grant year.
 Jamie Pitt, of the County Attorney’s Office, introduced Jessica Willis, another Victims of Crimes Coordinator, and
 reported on the program’s recent achievements and innumerous services they provide. During the last 6 months they
 assisted 809 victims in multiple categories. Gang-related crimes continue to be a huge problem. Last year they filed
 11 murder cases, 3 were gang-related homicides. Rubix, a courthouse facility dog, is a huge asset to victim programs.

       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Gibson – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
 G. PUBLIC COMMENTS: Shelly Halacy recognized the 4th graders present from Davinci Academy; their art work is
    displayed on the 3rd floor. Ron Gleason, of Huntsville, said that April 15-21 is designated “International Dark Sky
    Week” that started to draw attention to light pollution. Our county has taken leadership roles in establishing
    education programs, parks, ordinances, etc., and County Planning is instrumental in this work. He extended an
    invitation to an upcoming Moonlight Walk. Davinci students Melanie Garcia, Damien Regalado, Ryan, and Bella
    Medellin, spoke about gun violence and the negative health effects of pollution, asking the Commission’s help.
