Minutes - Action (00:05:48) Mayor Dan Olson moved to approve the minutes from May 8, 2025. Mayor Jim Miller seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. MAG MPO FY2026 Work Plan (UPWP) (00:00:00) LaNiece Davenport presented the Final Fiscal Year 2026 Unified Planning Work Program (UPWP) as a comprehensive work plan for the MPO, detailing the planned activities for the upcoming fiscal year starting July 1. She explained that since presenting the draft plan in May, some edits were made. These changes included adding specific names of Federal and State partners like FHWA, FTA, UDOT, UTA, Department of Environmental Quality, and Camp Williams. The language was also updated to reflect the current administration's guidelines on diversity, equity, and inclusion. Budget-wise, labor estimates increased from $3.3 million to $3.49 million, and study expenses rose to $19 million after including TIP program funding. A 30-day public comment period concluded on June 11 with no public comments received. Federal partners at FHWA reviewed the draft plan and found it satisfactory, and the MPO TAC unanimously voted for the MPO Board to approve the final proposed UPWP. Mayor Mark Johnson moved to approve the FY2026 Unified Planning Work Program. Mayor Denise Andersen seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Other Business and Adjournment (00:00:00) Chair Mayor Bill Wright stated the next MPO Board meeting is scheduled for July 10, 2025. Mayor Carolyn Lundberg moved to adjourn the meeting.
