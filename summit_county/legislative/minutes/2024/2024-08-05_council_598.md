1.
Olympics Update and 2034 Announcement Summary (9:03 AM)
Park City Mayor Nann Worel began the discussion about the announcement of the 2034 Olympic bid. Summit County Council Chair Malena Stevens and Chamber of Commerce President Jennifer Wesselhoff added their experiences and observations. Council Members commented and asked questions. The presenters responded.
2.
Regional Park and Ride Study (9:47 AM)
Carl Miller, County Director of Transportation Planning, introduced the community park and ride study. Alex Roy, Park City Senior Transportation Planner, provided additional information. Council Members asked questions. The presenters responded.

3.
Short-Term Rental Study (10:38 AM)
Jamie Johnson, Chief Executive Officer of the Park City Board of Realtors, introduced a report they received from Western Mountain Resort Alliance regarding short-term rentals in Summit County, Colorado, Pitkin County Colorado, and Teton County, Wyoming. Jack Greacen, CEO AEG Policy Advisors, presented the report and indicated that they expected Summit County, Utah to have similar outcomes. Council Members asked questions. Mr. Greacen responded.
