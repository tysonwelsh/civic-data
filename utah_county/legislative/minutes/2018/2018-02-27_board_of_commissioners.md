2.     DISCUSS AND ESTABLISH PERFORMANCE ISSUES, AND AUTHORIZE THE CHAIR TO SIGN
       THE ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER COMMUNITY PERFORMANCE PLAN FOR 2018
(Track A, 06:30)
Rocky Mountain Power Central Utah Regional Manager Kathy Hoffman talked about areas of interest for the county,
including working with the Economic Development Corporation of Utah (EDCUtah), maintaining low cost services
rates for residents, and taking advantage of technological advances for communication with customers during power
outages. She and the commissioners also discussed the promotion of renewable energy such as solar panels and wind
farms and cooperation with other power companies.
