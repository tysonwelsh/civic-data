                              COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY
                              DEPARTMENT OF COMMUNITY AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

                              ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING MINUTES
                              December 15, 2021
The Cottonwood Heights Community and Economic Development Director held an Administrative
Hearing Meeting on Wednesday, December 15, 2021, at 12:00 p.m. in the Cottonwood Heights City
Council Chambers located at 2277 E. Bengal Blvd., Cottonwood Heights, Utah 84121.

Present: Community & Economic Development Director/ Hearing Officer Michael Johnson, Associate
Planner Samantha DeSeelhorst, Assistant Planner Maverik Yeh, Deputy City Recorder Heather Sundquist

Other Present: Applicant -Jeffery Lunt, Patrick Romero

Community and Economic Development Director/Hearing Officer, Michael Johnson, welcomed those in
attendance and explained the Administrative Hearing process.

12:00 p.m. ADMINISTRATIVE HEARING MEETING


Assistant Planner Maverik Yeh presented an overview of the projects.
Applicant, Jeffery Lunt commented that they are locals, will be around to check on the
properties often and plan to be good neighbors.
No Public Comments were received for this project and no one from the public attended the meeting.
