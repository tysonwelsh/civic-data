#1 Public Hearing (5:35 PM): Nielsen Rezone (James Nielsen) 21 
 22 
Adams reviewed Mr. James Nielsen’s request for a recommendation of approval to the County Council 23 
for a rezone of 5 acres of property at 100 West 6600 South, south of Hyrum, from the Agricultural (A10) 24 
Zone to the Rural 2 (RU2) Zone. The potential for development under the RU2 Zone may be 2 building 25 
lots. Staff contacted Hyrum City and they have no problems with this rezone.  They anticipate that this 26 
area at some point in the future will be annexed into the city. 6600 South is a paved road that is 21 feet 27 
wide with 1 foot wide shoulders and does receive winter maintenance. Both the Fire District and Logan 28 
Environmental have signed off on the rezone. 29 
 30 
Staff and Commission discussed the right-of-way. The width of the right-of-way is currently unknown.  31

an easement to the county may be required during the subdivision process. The last subdivision approved 33 
on 6600 South was approved in 2011 and also had a right-of-way dedication requirement. 34
