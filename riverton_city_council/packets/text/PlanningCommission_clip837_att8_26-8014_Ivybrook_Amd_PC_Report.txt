This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application, the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motions
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-26-8014 “126th Retail Façade Upgrade”
amending the site plan of the existing commercial buildings located at 1722 to 1774 West 12600
South with the following conditions:

    1. Building materials and color will match the submitted plans as shown and approved by
       the Planning Commission.
    2. Any landscaping changes must be submitted to city staff for review for compliance with
       existing code requirements.
    3. Exterior modifications

Background
This application is for a façade upgrade and site plan changes to an existing building in a major
retail center in Riverton’s downtown area, next to Peterson’s Market. In October of 2025, the
Planning Commission approved Retail Façade Upgrades for Peterson’s Market, the attached
tenant spaces and the detached Little Caeser’s building. in the Commercial Downtown Zone,
which are on the same parcel, but the “Over the Rainbow Daycare” was not included in the
upgrades.

Ivybrook Academy is a new tenant in the building and plans to open later this summer. Ivybrook
is remodeling the inside and making minor changes to the site plan. This change in tenants
and downtime provided an opportunity for the landlord to upgrade the building façade at the
same time they are completing the work approved in October 2025. The work on the main
building is currently underway.

The applicant’s drawing shows the exterior changes are consistent in color and design to the
approved drawings from October 2025.

Riverton’s recently adopted Commercial Architectural Standards apply to new developments
and to amended sites. New developments are required to meet all the standards, but existing
developments may not be able to meet all requirements because of the existing conditions of
the structures or site. The Planning Commission must evaluate he proposal and determine if it
meets City ordinances where possible. Below is a list of the new architectural requirements
and a brief description for each.

      1. Four-sided Architecture.
             a. The code does not require every side to be designed the same, only that every
                 side be considered. Good architecture draws attention to store fronts, and
                 helps backsides of buildings blend in.
      2. Building Height
             a. The existing building meets the height requirements for the C-D zone.
      3. Bulk (wall variation every 30 to 50 feet)
             a. Building front has variations with glass windows and doors, and a defined
                 primary entrance.
      4. Roofline Variation every 50 feet.
             a. The existing rooflines have very minimal variations.
      5. Exterior Colors and Materials.
             a. The new proposal uses color and material changes to match the changes on
                 the main Peterson Market building.. The proposal uses less than 5 and more
                 than 2 colors and materials. This property falls within the area described in
                 RCC 18.65.060(5)(a)(i) which requires brick to be included as one of the 5
                 materials.
      6. Prohibited materials
             a. No prohibited materials are included.
      7. Cohesion
             a. This proposal improves the cohesion of this center, and utilizes elements that
                 are found in nearby buildings that are not a part of this development.
      8. Mechanical Equipment Screened.
             a. No existing condition is made worse by the proposed improvement.


Recommendations

Planning staff have reviewed these development plans and are recommending approval.
