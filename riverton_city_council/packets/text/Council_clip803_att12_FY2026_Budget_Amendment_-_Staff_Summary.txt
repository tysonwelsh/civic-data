                                                               FY26 Budget Amendment




Honorable Mayor and Council,

Background:
The City budgets for a variety of primarily capital projects that often span multiple fiscal years from
initial approval through completion. Many of these projects are completed in phases, for example,
beginning with design work and concluding with final project manager approval and payment.

Although the full cost of each project is appropriated in the year the original budget is adopted, the
associated work, contracting, bidding, and construction activities may extend beyond that single fiscal
year. As a result, staff evaluates all ongoing projects each year and submits a budget amendment to
carry forward the remaining funding from the previous fiscal year.
As a reminder, the City’s fiscal year runs from July 1 through June 30. Staff has reviewed all projects
budgeted in the prior year and identified those that were not completed as of June 30, 2025. Only
previously approved projects are included in this request; no new projects are being proposed as part of
this amendment.
Recommendation:
It is recommended that the City Council approve the proposed budget amendment to the FY 2025–2026
budget, carrying forward appropriations for projects that were budgeted in the prior fiscal year but
remained incomplete as of June 30, 2025.
