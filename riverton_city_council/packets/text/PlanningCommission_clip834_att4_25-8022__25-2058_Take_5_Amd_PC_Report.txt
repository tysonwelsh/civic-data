                             MEMORANDUM
To:              Planning Commission
From:            Planning Department
Date:            March 12, 2026
Subject:         Take 5 Oil Change, PLZ-25-8022, PLZ-25-2058, an application for a conditional
                 use permit and for an amended site plan to re-purpose an existing commercial
                 building located at 1754 West 12600 South to a drive-thru oil change business.
                 Applicant- Mitch Vande Guchgte


This application is a public hearing and administrative action item.
In reviewing the application, the Planning Commission is serving an administrative function,
decisions are based on substantial evidence.

Example Motions
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-25-8022 and PLZ-25-2058, “Take 5 Oil
Change” approving the conditional use permit and amending the site plan of the existing
commercial building located at 1754 West 12600 South with the following condition:

    1. Site Improvements, landscaping, building materials and colors will match the submitted
       information as shown and approved by the Planning Commission.
    2. Rooftop mechanical shall be screened.

Background

Take 5 Oil is proposing changes to the site, building and landscaping for an existing site and
building located on 12600 South at 1754 West. The building was constructed in 1977 and has
been remodeled many times since. The property is 0.41-acres with frontage on 12600 South.
The parcel is 90-ft wide and about 204-ft deep.

The proposed use of “Lube Center” is a Conditional Use in the C-D Zone, it is categorized as
“Automobile Repair and Related Services”. The current use of the building is Auto Glass
Repair, which falls under the same category. In reviewing the proposed use and site, staff did
not identify any extra impacts compared to a other uses that are Permitted in this zone.

3/6/2026
The proposed site will have three (3) drive-through oil change bays. There is pre-bay stacking
for 3 cars at each bay. The site meets City standards for parking and stacking.

The landscaping for this site is updated to meet city standards.

The building façade is updated with new colors and signage. The original bricks will remain
providing the required tie-in to the surrounding building. City Ordinance requires roof top
mechanical to be screened. This existing building has existing roof top mechanical that is not
screened. The Planning Commission has reviewed other remodeled buildings with roof top
mechanical with no screening or poor screening. Each site had a unique setting and
circumstances. In some cases the Commission required additional screening, but in other
cases the Commission did not require any additional screening.

Recommendations

City staff from Engineering, Planning, Fire, Water and Stormwater reviewed these development
plans and are recommending approval.
