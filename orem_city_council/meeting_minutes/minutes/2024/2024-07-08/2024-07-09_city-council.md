Communications Strategic Plan: Presenter: Pete Wolfley, Communications Division Manager,
PIO

Mr. Wolfley is presenting on the Strategic Communications plan developed by Koritz
Communications and the Orem Communications Team. The purpose of the plan is to support the
City mission, set the use of best practices, give structure and focus, and let everyone know how
Orem communicates. When the plan was developed, the group looked at external and internal
audiences. The first recommendation was to develop policy bases on the communications audit
of best practices to provide a supported framework for all communications. The second
recommendation is all communications and community relations strategies should be rooted in
the cycle of research, planning, implementation and review. The third and fourth
recommendation is to create a unified communications team from all city departments to serve as
our communications champions. The fifth recommendation is to establish an organization-wide
understanding that communication is critical to the overall success of the City.

Mr. Wolfley explained other parts of the communications plan. The plan is to have
consistent messaging and branding. The plan includes a key communicator network. This will
utilize the boards and commission members, create feedback loops and build relationships with
the community. The plan includes communications and economic development. This will
celebrate and promote Orem businesses, help achieve the objectives of the Economic
Development Strategic Plan and build relationships with business owners. The plan includes
media relations. This will build relationships through availability and helpfulness, it will curate
media materials and send invitations to media for milestones and events. The plan will include
crisis communications. The communications team will work directly with the emergency
management team, it will create media response policy and it will create potential scenarios that
the City may encounter in an emergency. The City Council asked questions concerning the
communications strategic plan.

CITY MANAGER INFORMATION ITEMS

Mr. Clark reported that the City has been upgraded to a AAA rating in credit status. This
is the highest credit rating that Cities can receive. Of 20,000 cities and towns in the US only 42
have AAA status which means only 2% of the cities have the highest credit rating. The reasons
for this change are Orem’s ample level of budgetary flexibility, healthy reserves, strong fiscal
management capabilities and policies, and steady demographic economic and long term liability
metrics.

Mr. Clark updated the Council on some milestones on the water tank project. First, the
pouring of the concrete for the tank has been completed. They will be testing the tank for leaks in
the upcoming weeks. Second, the 24 inch water line that is in 400 South that connects the tank to
the Springwater zone has been installed. There is one pipeline left to install and the plan is to
open the westbound lane in September.
