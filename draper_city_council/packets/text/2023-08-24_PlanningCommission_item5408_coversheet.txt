Re: Fitzgerald Property – Amended Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an Amended Development Agreement for approximately 22.39 acres located on the north side of 12650 South, at approximately 1085 East 12650 South. The property is currently zoned R5 . The applicant is requesting that an Amended Development Agreement be approved to eliminate the connection to 12600 South, adjust setbacks for Accessory Dwelling Units, and create detention basin design standards . The findings are as follows: For positive recommendation:
1.   The proposed amended development agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D).
2.   The proposed conditions and changes to the agreement will protect the health, safety, and welfare of properties along 12600 South by providing a turnaround design which meets the design criteria of the Fire Department, City Engineer, and the Public Works Director per DCMC Section 17-5-030(F).
For negative recommendation:
1.   The proposed amended development agreement does not address the potential health, safety, and welfare impacts to properties along 12600 South that could result if a cul-de-sac turnaround is not provided.
