Pre-Council, 3:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Prayer
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present expect for
Councilors Condie and Hancock. Councilor Hancock will join the meeting by phone at 7:00 p.m.
Councilor Southwick gave the Opening Prayer.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Primary Children’s Hospital Presentation
Representatives from the IHC Primary Children’s Hospital attended the meeting. Bill Barnes
introduced Lisa Poletti as the Administrator for the new hospital opening in Lehi. She stated that
they are proposing a 5-story patient tower with 66 beds when it opens in 2023. Other amenities
include a conference center, Ronald McDonald House, public outdoor space, and a food pantry.
She stated that they want to be transparent and keep the residents up to date during the construction
process. They are planning for a groundbreaking in September.

2.2 Pickleball Presentation
Lori Lee, a member of the Lehi Parks, Trails and Trees Committee stated that there is a large
demand for pickleball courts, and there’s an opportunity to host national tournaments if they had
a large facility in Lehi. She introduced Chris Thackery, an ambassador for pickleball in Utah
County. He stated that pickleball is diverse so kids to senior can play it. It’s easy to learn and play
with low cost. He encouraged the Council to construct a facility and stated that the cost of the
construction can be recaptured through tournaments. He also stated that they have donors willing
to donate construction materials. He stated that if it were part of a regional park then they could
share restrooms and playgrounds which would save on costs.

Lee Adamson, with the Utah County Convention and Visitors Bureau, explained the overall
economic benefit that this could bring to the City. The total construction cost would be $1.2
million.

4. Administrative Report
Dean Lundell explained the budget amendments that are part of the public hearing. He stated that
some of those amendments are to account for grants. They also needed to replace a police car
that was in an accident. The central air unit at City Hall needs to be replaced and the TSSD
billings are higher then expected.

The Council gave a report on the committees they serve on.

The meeting recessed at approximately 6:45 p.m.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Presentation by UTA- Patti Garver
    Patti Garver with UTA gave an update on the current studies being conducted by UTA. She
    stated that they are looking at the areas of South Salt Lake County and North Utah County
    for light rail. She also stated that a Bus Rapid Transit Study is being conducted from Orem to
    Lehi.

2.2 Sanctuary City Proclamation
    Mayor Johnson stated that two Councilmembers were interested in entertaining this
    proclamation. He read the proclamation and stated that this does not legally obligate the City
    to anything, and then asked if there was any public comment.

    Marilee Boyack stated that she initiated this with the Council and was in full support of its
    approval.

    Sarah Blotered was against the proposal and stated that the city doesn’t have the resources to
    dedicate to this issue.

2.3 Road Transpiration updates
    a. Leah Jaramillo was representing UDOT. She stated that they are in the final phases of
    completing the Technology Corridor project. She stated that there will need to be some State
    Street closures as they demo the bridge pillars.

    b. Brad Kenison stated that they are putting bid documents together for the Main Street
    widening project and asked if the Council was willing to include 2 cost estimates. One
    estimate would be if they left the bridge open and a second estimate would be with the bridge
    closed. He expressed that Saratoga Springs would prefer that the bid documents not include
    an option to close the bridge. The Council reached consensus that they would like to see both
    cost estimates in the bid documents.
