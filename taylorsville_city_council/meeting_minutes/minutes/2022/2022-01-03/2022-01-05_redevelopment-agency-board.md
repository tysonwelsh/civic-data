Vice Chair Anna Barbieri called the Redevelopment Agency of Taylorsville City Board
Meeting to order at 8:15 PM and welcomed those in attendance. RDA Secretary Jean K.
Ashby conducted a Roll Call, wherein all Board Members were present.

1. Appointments of RDA Officers for 2022— Wayne Harper

8:17 PM - Economic & Community Development Director Wayne Harper explained to
the Board members that the Redevelopment Agency of Taylorsville City operates under
separate code by state statute. He told them their elected positions as city council
members make them also RDA board members, just wearing a different hat. The
agency has its own budget and own shared staff in himself and Jean Ashby, who work
with Scott Harrington to create the agency budget and expenditures and bring items to
them as board members to fulfill the goals and plans in the redevelopment project
areas that have been adopted since 2008. Each year a new RDA Board chair and vice
chair are appointed, those traditionally being the same as the individuals elected as that
year’s city council officers. Item 1 is before you to appoint a new board chair and vice
chair for 2022.

Chair — Anna Barbieri
Vice Chair — Meredith Harker

Board Member Cochran MOVED to appoint the new RDA Officers for 2022 as
recommended. Board Member Knudsen SECONDED the motion. Vice chair Barbieri
called for a roll call vote. The vote was as follows: Knudsen-yes, Harker-yes, Barbieri-
yes, Cochran-yes, and Burgess-yes. All Board Members voted in favor and the motion
passed unanimously.

2. Consideration of RDA Board Meeting Minutes of June 2, 2021

8:19 PM - Board Member Harker MOVED to approve the Minutes from the June 2, 2021,
RDA Board Meeting. Board Member Cochran SECONDED the motion. Chair Barbieri
called for a roll call vote. The vote was as follows: Barbieri-yes, Cochran-yes Burgess-
yes, Knudsen-yes, and Harker-yes. All Board Members voted in favor and the motion
passed unanimously.

3. Other Matters

8:19 PM - Mr. Harper indicated that there will be further matters brought before the
RDA Board at a subsequent meeting. But for tonight there were no other matters.

4. Adjournment

Board Member Harker MOVED to adjourn the Redevelopment Agency of Taylorsville City
Board.

The meeting was adjourned at 8:19 PM.
