PC 25-016 Fife STR [Conditional Use Permit] Troy Hazard/Troy F & Aubree Hansen Hazard,
authorized agent/owner is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to operate a one-bedroom short-term
rental within the basement of the home located at 358 West 400 North in the Traditional
Neighborhood Residential (NR-6) zone; TIN 05-070-0020 (Ellis Neighborhood).

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL

1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
available in the Community Development Department.

2. If the owner-occupied CUP grantee changes their primary place of residence, the Conditional
Use Permit (CUP) and business license may be revoked.

3. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a one (1) bedroom Short Term Rental in an owner-
occupied single-family dwelling. Any expansions of the approved STR beyond one bedroom,
requires a new Conditional Use Permit.

4. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes a maximum of four (4) occupants in the STR.

5. Ashort-term rental may not be the subject of multiple rental contracts by separate individuals,
groups or parties for the same night or nights.

6. A minimum of one (1) parking stall is required for the STR and two (2) for the homeowner. All
parking areas shall be located off-street, outside the front setback and paved with an approved
hard surface and striped and shall meet minimum LDC dimensions (9’x18’) per stall.

7. Parking of recreational vehicles, trailers, boats, ATVs or similar vehicles associated with the
STR shall be legally parked on-site and are not permitted to be parked on-street.

8. Rental contracts for the STR shall be for 30 days or less.

9. Rental contracts shall not be offered to the same individual, group, or party for more than two
consecutive rental contracts in a six (6) month period. The owner shall maintain rental records
available for review and inspection by Logan City.

10. Quiet times shall comply with the City noise ordinance of 10 pm to 8 am.


11. ALogan City building/fire inspection is required prior to use of the home as a Short-Term Rental.
These inspections may result in building remodeling and building permits prior to the issuance
of a business license.

12. A Logan City business license is required before a rental contract can be issued.

13. The STR shall comply with local and state Building, Health, Fire, Safety, Maintenance and
Nuisance Codes prior to operating.

14. Exterior lighting shall be concealed source, down-cast and reviewed and approved prior to the
issuance of a building permit and shall comply with current LDC regulations.

15. No signs are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All signage shall be approved and
permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

16. No fences are approved with this Conditional Use Permit. All fences shall be approved and
permitted by staff in accordance with the Land Development Code.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. The proposed project conforms to the requirements of the Land Development Code.

2. This STR meets the City’s STR density and location parameters as specified in Chapter
17.37.130.

3. As conditioned, the use is compatible with the surrounding land uses and will not interfere with
the use and enjoyment of adjoining or area properties.

4. The streets providing access and other infrastructure to the subject property have adequate
capacities and level of service for the conditional use.

5. The Planning Commission has substantiated the findings for a Conditional Use Permit in Chapter
17.42.050.

MOTION: Commissioner Heare made a motion to approve all items under the Consent Agenda.
Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion was approved unanimously.

Moved: K. Heare Second: J. Guth Approved: 6-0

Yea: Doutre, Heare, Peterson, Lewis, McNamara, Guth Nay: Abstain:

PC _ 25-015 King STR [Conditional Use Permit] Ashley King/King Acquisitions LLC, authorized
agent/owner is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to operate a three-bedroom short-term rental
within the entire home located at 473 West 100 South in the Traditional Neighborhood Residential
(NR-6) zone; TIN 06-011-0004 (Ellis Neighborhood).

Staff: Aimee Egbert, City Planner, reviewed the request for a non-owner-occupied, three-bedroom
short-term rental (STR) located at 473 West 100 South. The STR is allowed to be occupied by up to
8 people and is required to have two parking stalls

Proponent: No Comment was made by the Proponent.

Public: Judy Holmes, who lives near the proposed STR, spoke about the neighborhood not being a
transient area, and safety concerns; and stated that she is opposed to this STR.

Gail Baugh who lives in the area, said she agreed with Judy’s comments and spoke about illegal
activities that could happen at an STR.


Commission: Commissioner Peterson stated that any concerns, violations, or illegal behavior
should be reported to the City or Police Department. Commissioner Peterson also stated that part
of the business licensing process for a STR is to notify their neighbors of the STR use and to provide
a contact number for any concerns with the STR.

Commissioner Heare asked if this property has had any issues with the STR and illegal activity. A.
Egbert said this property has been used as a STR for some time and there have not been any
complaints.

PC_ 25-017 LDC_ Amendment _Chapter_17.21-Gateway Overlay Zone & 17.33-Signs [Code
Amendment] Mike DeSimone/Logan City is requesting an amendment to the Land Development

Code, Chapter 17.21 “Gateway Overlay Zones” to add a Commercial Node at Park Avenue/1700
South & Highway 89/91, and Chapter 17.33 “Signs” to include sign standards for Special Events.

Staff/Proponent: Russ Holley, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request for a code amendment to
the Land Development Code (LDC) Chapter 17.21 Gateway Overlay, to add a Commercial Node at
Park Avenue/1700 South Highway 89/91. R. Holley explained that a Commercial Node encourages
more concentrated development around traffic signals with reduced setbacks within a 700-foot radius
of the highway. R. Holley reviewed the three existing commercial nodes along Highway 89/91 (1000
West, 2600 South, and 3000 South) and stated that the proposed commercial node at Park
Avenue/1700 South will create a more uniform development pattern at every traffic signal along this
corridor and will improve access and safety for entering and exiting this stretch of the Highway.

R. Holley reviewed the amendment to LDC Chapter 17.33 Signs, to allow temporary signs for

licensed special events in Logan. Special Event signs are allowed as follows:
e Maximum of 8 signs and two banners per event.

Signs can be placed no more than 7 days prior to the event.

Signs to be removed within 24 hours after the event.

Signs shall be placed on private property.

No signs on public property or in the public right of way.

Signs shall not exceed 4 square feet and a maximum height of 4 feet.

One sign per property per event.

No more than two signs along any street frontage.

Signage plan to be submitted with special event application.

Commissioner Peterson asked if event banners could be placed on a chain-link fence. R. Holley said
that banners are not allowed on a fence, banners have to be mounted flush to a building. The Mayor,
however, may grant an exception for a non-profit community event.

Commission Doutre asked about the smaller banners along Main Street in Downtown Logan. R.
Holley said that those banners are managed by the Downtown Alliance and are regulated by a
different section of the LDC. R. Holley also mentioned that signs cannot be regulated by their content,
signs can only be regulated by placement, size, and duration.

Public: Benjamin Chandler asked for a review of the proposed code changes.

Commission: The Commission reviewed the setback changes within commercial nodes and that
temporary signs are now allowed to be posted for special events, for up to 7 days.

Motion: Commissioner Doutre made a motion to forward a recommendation of approval to the
Municipal Council on a Code Amendment for PC 25-017 LDC Amendment, Chapters 17.21 & 17.33


with the findings for approval as listed below. Commissioner Guth seconded the motion. The motion
was approved unanimously.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. Utah State Law authorizes local Planning Commission to recommend ordinance changes to the
legislative body (Municipal Council).

2. The Code Amendments are done in conformance with the requirements of Title 17.51 of the
Logan Municipal Code.

3. The proposed Code Amendments are consistent with the Logan City General Plan.

4. The proposed Code Amendments are consistent with UCA Title 10, Chapter 9a, Part 5 & Part
6.

5. No public comment has been received regarding the proposed amendments.
