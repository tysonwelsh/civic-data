WORK SESSIONS

2. 2025 Planning Division Annual Report – Planning staff will present the 2025 annual
   report to the Planning Commission.

    Planning Director Nick Norris reviewed the 2025 Annual Report with Commissioners
    including statistics from the Planning Counter, Development Review Team, Pre-
    Submittal Meetings, Land Use Applications, Appeals, Administrative Hearings, Historic
    Landmark Commission, Planning Commission, Subdivisions, Zoning Administration, and
    Online Open Houses. In closing, the Planning Division accomplishments were
    highlighted.

3. 2026 Utah Legislature Recap and Training - Planning staff will provide the
   commission with a recap of legislative bills that passed during the 2026 legislative
   session that impact the commission or that may require potential code updates. (Staff
   Contact: Nick Norris at 801-535-6173 or nick.norris@slc.gov )

    Nick gave an overview of the 2026 Legislative Session and the bills that impact the
    Planning Division. 105 bills were assigned to Community & Neighborhoods, Planning
    Division tracked 41 bills, 8 were adopted.

        •   HB 33 Political Signs

        •   HB 76 Data Center Water Transparency

        •   HB 126 Micro-education Facility Amendment

        •   HB 215 Landscape Restrictions Amendment

        •   HB 597 Alcohol Amendments

        •   SB 23 Regulatory Impact on Families

        •   SB 242 Transportation Amendments

        •   SB 284 Local Land and Water Modifications

    Nick gave a brief commissioner training covering the commission requirements, 10-3
    Part 13 Municipal Officer and Employees Ethics Act, Utah Code 10-20 Land Use and
    Development Management Act (LUDMA), City Code Title 21A, State Code and Land
    Use Applications, and ensuring the record includes evidence for commission
    decisions.

The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:17 PM.
