PUBLIC HEARING: TO OPEN THE 2019 WASHINGTON COUNTY BUDGET
TO UPDATE FUNDS 11, 25, 26, 28 AND 29

Washington County Clerk/Auditor explained that the Court Support Services office is
mainly operated from grant funding, therefore, the budget needs to be opened to account
for new money coming in.

Washington County Court Support Services Sharmane Gull explained that Washington
County was allocated $500,000 in order to integrate services with the Washington County
Sheriff's Office. Court Support Services works with the Washington County Attorney’s
Office to reduce recidivism among incarcerated individuals who may have addiction or

mental illness issues.

Mr. Hafen concluded by stating that the Road Department hired three (3) new employees,

addition of those employees.

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE NO. _2019-1137-O, AN ORDINANCE
REZONING A CERTAIN PORTION OF WASHINGTON COUNTY FROM C-3
(GENERAL _COMMERCIAL) ZONE TO THE _RA-1_ (RESIDENTIAL

AGRICULTURAL 1 ACRE MINIMUM LOT SIZE) ZONE

Washington County Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that the
Washington County Planning Commission voted to deny the zone change due to the non-
conforming residences, despite the existing commercial zone.

Chairman Cox expressed concerns over allowing a residence in a commercial zone as it
would impact zoning regulations for all commercial zones.

Commissioner Almquist expressed concerns that a residence in a commercial zone may
adversely impact future development on that property.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Deny Ordinance No. 2019-1137-
O, An Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County
from C-3 (General Commercial) Zone to the RA-1 (Residential
Agricultural 1 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE NO. _2019-1140-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING THE TERM OF APPOINTMENT FOR COUNTY CONSTABLES

Washington County Deputy Attorney Eric Clarke explained that the legislature recently
updated County Constables terms to six (6) years. The County Code needs to be updated
in order to comply with the newly updated legislature.

Mr. Clarke further explained that this would not affect the terms of the current Washington
County Constables as they were appointed before July 1, 2018.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1140-
O, An Ordinance Rezoning a Certain Portion of Washington County
from C-3 (General Commercial) Zone to the RA-1 (Residential
Agricultural 1 Acre Minimum Lot Size) Zone. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Cox, Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION _ OF ORDINANCE NO. 2019-1142-O, AN ORDINANCE
INCLUDING THE WASHINGTON COUNTY COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS IN

THE COUNTY CODE

Mr. Clarke explained that the Council of Governments (COG) is a body consisting of all
municipal mayors and the County Commission to oversee corridor preservation.
Previously, the County Commission created a funding stream for a public transportation
bus line from St. George to Springdale, wherein, 0.25% sales tax will be implemented for
transportation facilities and public transit.

It is recommended for the COG to be designated as the decision-making body for the transit
system funding in the same manner that it oversees corridor preservation, right-of-way
acquisition and transportation project programming.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Ordinance No. 2019-1142-
O, An Ordinance Including the Washington County Council of
Governments in the County Code. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Cox,
Iverson, and Almquist voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2019-2489, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A COMPLETE REDUCTION OF PAST PROPERTY TAXES FOR
CERTAIN PARCELS LOCATED IN THE RED CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Mr. Clarke explained that Brennan Holdings, LLC and His Family Matters, LLC acquired
property in the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve several years ago and have allowed the property
to remain in the reserve while they wait for the federal government to acquire the land.
Unfortunately, only a small portion of the Reserve parcels have been federally acquired,
and the property owners still own most of their land within the Reserve.

The property owners have entered into property tax deferral agreements with Washington
County requesting a reduction of any previously owed property taxes to zero.
