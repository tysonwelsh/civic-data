       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood talked about some City events and highlights:

              June is Pride Month and Mayor Wood talked about how everyone is safe, welcome,
              and needed in the City. She encouraged everyone to show their support for their
              LGBTQIA+ neighbors this month.

              June 7th – ‘Juneteenth BBQ’ will be held at the South Salt Lake Community Center
              from 4:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m. and will include a flag raising ceremony with family
              games and free food.

              June 7th – ‘Utah Chinese History Exhibit & Presentation’ will be held at the South Salt
              Lake Community Center auditorium from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m.

                     Mayor Wood read the proclamation that talked about how Juneteenth
                     celebrates the emancipation of those who had been enslaved in the United
                     States and recognizes the day when enslaved peoples in Texas learned of
                     the 1863 Emancipation Proclamation that had been issued two years earlier.

                     In this time, the nation celebrates the accomplishments, inventions,
                     triumphs, and resiliency of African-American, African, and Black people.

Public Hearing – 7:25 p.m. (Or As Soon Thereafter as Possible)
To Receive Public Input Regarding a Compensation Increase for Elected and Statutory Officers
of South Salt Lake.
Finance Director, Crystal Makin, talked about the 3% merit and 3% COLA (Cost of Living
Adjustment) that elected and statutory officers would receive as a part of the proposed 2024-
2025 budget.

The public hearing opened at 7:29 p.m.

No public comment was made.

The public hearing closed at 7:29 p.m.

No action was necessary on this at this time as it will be voted on in the final vote to approve the
whole City budget at the June 12th meeting.

Public Hearing – 7:30 p.m. (Or As Soon Thereafter as Possible) To Receive Public Input
Regarding the Adoption of the South Salt Lake City Budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2024 to June
30, 2025.
Finance Director, Crystal Makin, gave an overview of the budget that has been discussed at
length over the last several meetings.

The public hearing opened at 7:52 p.m.

No public comment was made.

The public hearing closed at 7:53 p.m.

Council Member Thomas made a motion to move this matter to the June 12th meeting.
