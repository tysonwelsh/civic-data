Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Nielsen welcomed everyone to the meeting and excused Commission Vice
Chair Jackson, Commissioner Eyre; and Commissioner Britton.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Design West Build’s request for approval of a
conditional use permit for the Kohler’s Market remodel, adding a drive-up window for the
pharmacy located at 300 East Main Street.
Sierra presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Derek with Design West Architects, was present and addressed the item. They
would like to update the building. Derek stated they agreed with the DRC comments that were
listed. The direction of the drive thru was discussed and the applicants are willing to do what the
Commission recommends. The parking lot striping of the parking lot was also questioned. The
applicant stated they are looking at renovating the whole parking lot, but it is a budget issue right
now. Access to first east from the drive thru and the parking lot was discussed.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Everett liked the idea of the drive thru and considered it an investment to the
community and would like to do what we can to keep the grocer in the area. Commissioner Nielsen
agreed and thought some striping would help with direction.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to approve Design West Build’s request for
        approval of a conditional use permit for the Kohler’s Market remodel, adding a drive-
        up window for the pharmacy located at 300 East Main Street; with the findings that
        the proposed use, at the proposed location, will not be detrimental or injurious to property
        or improvements in the vicinity and will not be detrimental to the public health, safety,
        general welfare or convenience, will be located and conducted in a manner in compliance
        with the goals and policies of the Lehi City General Plan and the purposes of this Code,
        that the proposed establishment is of adequate size and dimensions to permit construction
        of the facilities and the conduct of the use in such a manner that it will not be detrimental
        to adjoining properties and the area. Including all DRC comments. Commissioner Everett
        seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes; Commissioner Carlson, yes. The
        motion passed unanimously.

2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of DR Horton’s request for review of the Cold
Spring Ranch Area Plan Amendment, amending the allowed ERUs, density ranges of pods,
property, and setbacks.
Sierra presented information the agenda item. There are seven changes being requested. 1300 to
1372 with additional units.

Kim Struthers recommended more rear load town homes and stated the applicant was great to
work with and accommodating with a lot of the processes. Commissioner Nielsen asked about
rounding down and being consistent with the general plan.

The applicant, Adam Losser, was present. He stated DR Horton has been working on the
amendment for approximately 3.5 years. They have installed the trail system connecting 3600
west to the pedestrian bridge. They have been working with the Parks Department to propose
parks and public facilities. Commissioner Carlson asked about the zero lot line. Mr. Losser stated
the idea was to have the convenience of usable side yard.

There was one online public comment that Commissioner Nielsen acknowledged.

Commissioner Nielsen commented on the addition of density and there needs to be a strong
finding of fact. He also would like to see triggers if the request was approved, and that the city
was holding to those triggers. Commissioner Carlson stated a trigger for the parks is important
and asked if it would be possible if a park was ready to be built in 2022. Adam Losser said they
are ready to build and have invested money and they have been waiting for the area plan
amendment to go through, but they are ready to build the parks and are awaiting direction from
the City. Commissioner Britton asked for clarification on the trigger and asked why there were
triggers if they aren't being enforced. Commissioner Newall asked if there were additional
triggers. Kim pulled up the additional triggers to review. Ross stated when the triggers are hit, it
is decided what will be built. The intent of the area plan is to balance the amenities with the
impact fees. Mr. Losser stated when the negotiation of the bridge took place, they had a bid for
installation, and when the permit was pulled, and the bid increased due to requirements from the
state. They have been working on the amendment for approximately 3.5 years. They have
installed the trail system connecting 3600 west to the pedestrian bridge. They have been working
with the Parks Department to propose parks and public facilities. Commissioner Carlson asked
about the zero lot line. Mr. Loser stated the idea was to have the convenience of usable side yard.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of DR Horton’s request
        for review of the Cold Spring Ranch Area Plan Amendment, amending the allowed
        ERUs, density ranges of pods, property, and setbacks; including all DRC comments
        and the seven amendments with the 500 unit trigger to work diligently with the City.
        With the findings that the proposed area plan amendment is consistent with the land uses
        and zoning of nearby and adjoining properties and meets the requirements of the Lehi
        City Development Code regarding area plan approval. Noting to keep the consistency to
        rounding down per the PUD ordinance and capped at 30 units, with findings of fact to
        allow adding additional units that differentiate this request from other requests that the
        infrastructure has been calculated at 2.57 acres with an allowance of 42 units because it
        has been negotiated in good faith and cause. Commissioner Carlson seconded. All in
        favor.

        Vote: Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Nielsen,
        yes; Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes; Commissioner Carlson, yes.
        The motion passed unanimously.
