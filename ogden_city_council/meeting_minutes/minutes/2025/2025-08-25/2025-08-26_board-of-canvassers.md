Consideration of certifying the August 12, 2025, Municipal Primary Election
results
          A memo from the City Recorder came before the Council to consider certifying the August 12, 2025, Primary General
Election results. The memo stated Section 20A, Chapter 4 of the Utah State Code sets forth the election review process, designates
the legislative body – the City Council – and the Mayor as the Board of Canvassers and outlines the Board’s duties for municipal
elections. The City Recorder acts as the election official. The Board of Canvassers is responsible for certifying the official election
results of municipal elections. Canvassing of returns for a Primary Election must occur no sooner than seven (7) days after the
election and no later than fourteen (14) days after the election. After reviewing the returns, the Board must certify the results of
the election and file a report that includes the following information:
               1. The total number of votes cast in the Board’s jurisdiction;
               2. The names of each candidate whose name appeared on the ballot;
               3. The title of each ballot proposition that appeared on the ballot;
               4. Each office that appears on the ballot;
               5. From each voting precinct:
                        a. The number of votes for each candidate;
                        b. The number of votes for and against each ballot proposition;
               6. The total number of votes given in the Board’s jurisdiction to each candidate;
               7. The number of ballots that were rejected; and
               8. A statement certifying that the information contained in the report is accurate.
