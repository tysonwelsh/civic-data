Fiscal Items – Approval of Expenditures

Event Permit Appeals

               None

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Bingham Canyon Post Office Update

               Mayor Clayton stated he was able to check with Copperton’s community liaison
with USPS, and the chief financial officer of the western region, and they had no plans in 2020 to
close down the Copperton post office. He asked for better communication or signage. The post
office was closed for ten days over the holidays because of a downed power line and there was
no sign explaining the problem.

                               −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
RFP for Copperton Park Arborist Plan

               Council Member Bailey stated she sent an email to everybody she knew at Utah
State University. They gave her further leads. She has had an issue getting the Salt Lake County
Parks & Recreation Division to cooperate.

               Council Member Pazell stated he also had problems accessing information from
the Salt Lake County and was told a GRAMA request would be necessary.

              Nathan Bracken stated he has prepared a GRAMA request and will send it to the
Council to submit to Salt Lake County.

Revisions to Title 1 and 2

               Nathan Bracken stated revising these titles is required to make them more
specific to Copperton and not Salt Lake County.

               The Council instructed Mr. Bracken to revise Title 1 by removing distinctions

2020 Council Meeting Schedule

2020 Council Member Assignments

              Council Member Bailey stated the Council has found that continuity in
assignments is more important than alternating assignments.

             Council Member Bailey, seconded by Council Member Pazell, moved to keep
Sean Clayton as chair/mayor. The motion passed unanimously.

               Council Member Bailey, seconded by Council Member Stitzer, moved to keep
Apollo Pazell as vice-chair. The motion passed unanimously.

              Council Member Pazell, seconded by Council Member Stitzer, moved to keep
Kathleen Bailey as treasurer. The motion passed unanimously.

             Council Member Pazell, seconded by Council Member Bailey, moved to keep
Kevin Severson as secretary. The motion passed unanimously.

             Council Member Bailey, seconded by Council Member Severson, moved to
appoint Tessa Stitzer to the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District Board. The motion
passed unanimously.

               Council Member Bailey, seconded by Council Member Stitzer, moved to appoint
Apollo Pazell to the Unified Police Department and Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service
Area boards. The motion passed unanimously.

              Council Member Pazell, seconded by Council Member Clayton, moved to appoint
Kathleen Bailey to the Unified Fire Authority and Unified Fire Service Area boards. The motion
passed unanimously.

Committee/Board Updates

        Legislative Research Committee

              Council Member Pazell stated no one responded to his request to join this
committee so he and Council Members may be alone in lobbying efforts.

              Nathan Bracken stated there has been some discussion about the franchise tax.
He is working with other metro township legal counsels to allow metro townships to collect the
tax.

                Mayor Clayton stated the Southwest Mayors and representatives will meet every
Wednesday for lunch. Each municipality will alternate hosting the lunch. He asked Mr. Bracken
to also attend these meetings. All Council Members are also welcomed to attend.

        Bingham Cemetery Board

              Nothing to report.

        Copperton Community Council

                Council Member Bailey stated the Community Council would like to request
$3,000 in their 2020 budget. This will be included on the February 2020 agenda.

        Planning Commission

Copperton Metro Township Council Member Reports

        Council Member Clayton

          Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD)

                Mayor Clayton stated the GSLMSD met last week. They focused primarily on the
Olympia Hills development. The GSLMSD is only involved to provide services to the area. This
will involve an agreement with the developer. He will send the agreement to Nathan Bracken for
review.

          Southwest Mayors

              Mayor Clayton stated there will be weekly meetings during the legislative session.

        Council Member Pazell

          Unified Police Department (UPD)
          Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

              Council Member Pazell stated at tomorrow’s meeting he will continue to oppose
the weighted vote. That matter will be on the February agenda. Proponents hope that new
members will support a weighted vote.

        Council Member Bailey

          Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
          Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

              Council Member Bailey stated UFA and UFSA will meet next Tuesday.

          Wasatch Front Waste & Recycling District (WFWRD)

             Mayor Clayton stated he will give Tessa Stitzer’s contact information to Pam
Roberts, General Manager/CEO, WFWRD.

        Council Member Severson

          Salt Lake County Animal Services Advisory Council

               Council Member Severson stated the Advisory Council will meet next week.
They will be selecting a new chair and vice-chair and discussing the 2020 budget. He will need

        Other Reports

          Emergency Plan

                Mayor Clayton stated the Unified Fire Authority, through a contract with the
GSLMSD, hired James Woodward, who is an emergency expert. He works for UFA but works
out of the Salt Lake County Government complex with the GSLMSD. He will work with Copperton
four hours per week to create the emergency plan.

          Annexation

              Previously discussed.

          Economic Development

              Council Member Pazell stated he would be happy to connect Council Members
Severson and Bailey with some city officials from cities with similar demographics to Copperton
to jog some ideas on how they manage. Copperton is not the only small city without a business
base.
