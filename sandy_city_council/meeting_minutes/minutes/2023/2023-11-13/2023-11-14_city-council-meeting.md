3.         CA09062023 Amendments to Title 21 of the Land Development Code, Chapter 19, 
           -0006605   Special Development (SD) Districts, Section 20, SD (Carnation) -10600 S. 
           (CC-WS)    1000 E.
                   Attachments:   Staff Report and Exhibits
                                  Presentation

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, presented a request on behalf of the 
                                  property owners for a proposed amendment to Title 21, Land Development Code, chapter 
                                  19, Special Development (SD) Districts, Section 20, SD (Carnation) -10600 South 1000 
                                  East of the Sandy Municipal Code. The code amendment is proposed to allow 
                                  restaurants that have drive-up windows as a permitted use in the SD (Carnation) zone. 
                                  The amendment would remove the current limitation on drive-up windows, which only 
                                  allows non-food uses such as banks, ATMs, dry cleaners, and pharmacies. The Planning 
                                  Commission forwarded a negative recommendation to the Council. This is an information 
                                  item and presentation to the Council. This item will come back to the Council on 
                                  November 28th for Council consideration and action. The applicant, Mr. Adam Nash, 
                                  spoke to the Council. He mentioned that the proposed code amendment impacts a very 
                                  small area and does not have a city-wide impact. Council questions followed for Mr. Nash 
                                  and for staff.

                                  Council convened a recess at 5:40 pm.
                                  Council reconvened at 5:50 pm.

                                  Council questions continued. James Sorensen, Community Development Director, 
                                  responded to Council questions and explained the history of the Carnation Zone. Council 
                                  discussion and questions concerning potential impact on the neighborhood, area traffic, 
                                  and flow continued. Council expressed support for development on this site and 
                                  discussed available options that would support development on the property.

                                  Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda: General Citizen Comment.

4.         CA09212023 Amendments to Title 21 of the Land Development Code, Chapter 8, 
           -0006626   Section 2, Permitted Land Use Matrix by the Commercial, Office, Industrial, 
           (CC-WS)    Mixed Use, Transit Corridor, and Research and Development Districts.
                   Attachments:   CC Memo and Exhibit A

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, provided a review of the City's land use 
                                  regulations and recommended amendments to the city regulations that would limit the 
                                  number of "Transitional Housing Facilities" permitted in the City. Staff presented a draft 
                                  code amendment that would limit a Transitional Housing Facility to only one defined area 
                                  of the city within the Regional Commercial (RC) Zone District. The geographic limits 
                                  would be the area north of 9000 South, south of Harrison Street, east of I-15 and west of 
                                  Monroe Street. A maximum of one Transitional Housing Facility would be allowed through 
                                  a Conditional Use Permit approval. Council discussion and comments followed. Council 
                                  was supportive of narrowing the impacted area to parcels currently owned by Shelter the 
                                  Homeless.

                                  Following the presentation, Council moved to Standing Reports.

5.         23-449                 Sandy City Council appointing a representative to the South Valley 
                                  Mosquito Abatement District
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Resolution 23-54C
                                  Notice of Vacancy

                                  Dustin Fratto, Council Executive Director, explained to the Council the selection process 
                                  for the appointment of a new member to the South Valley Mosquito Abateman District. 
                                  The two candidates, Mr. Van Maren and Ms. Reynolds, are both seeking an appointment 
                                  to the district. 

                                  Ms Florence Reynolds is the currently the appointed member to the Mosquito Abatement 
                                  District. Her background is in the science field and she has worked for over 20 years in 
                                  water quality with the Salt Lake County water division. She also currently serves on the 
                                  Public Utilities Advisory Board. Council asked Ms. Reynolds to explain the work the 
                                  district handels. Ms. Reynolds reviewed the process implemented by the district staff to 
                                  minimize the impact of mosquitos. Ms. Reynolds further explained how understanding 
                                  how the water flows in the county helps her ability to more effectively serve on the district 
                                  board. Ms. Reynolds has served a four year term on this board and expressed an interest 
                                  to be reappointed to a second term. 

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren provided information about his background and availability to serve. 
                                  He also had questions concerning a board member's liability insurance. Mr. Pace 
                                  responded to those questions. Council expressed their appreciation and respect to Mr. 
                                  Van Maren for his interest and service to the city.

                                  Public Hearing comment period opened.
                                  Public Hearing comment period closed.

                                  Following the vote on the resolution, Council moved to Item 4 on the Agenda.



                                  A motion was made by Brooke D'Sousa, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey, to adopt 
                                  Resolution 23-54C, a resolution appointing Florence Reynolds to serve as a 
                                  member of the South Valley Mosquito Abatement District, as a representative of 
                                  Sandy City for a four year term commencing on January 1, 2024...The motion

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson asked the Chair to schedule an agenda item for a future 
                    meeting regarding an update on the google fiber implementation. He acknowledged the 
                    staff and IT department staff for their help with tonight's meeting. He thanked the fire 
                    department and staff for their efforts to coordinate the annual Fire Department banquet 
                    and thanked the Mayor for her comments. He also thanked Lynn Pace and James 
                    Sorensen for their responsiveness and help on some projects. He will need to remote in 
                    to the December 6th Council meeting.

                    Council Member Scott Earl thanked Steve Van Maren for all his help and service on 
                    behalf of Sandy City. He provided an update on the regional housing workshop that he 
                    recently attended.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud thought the Turkey Trot 5K race was a great event. She 
                    spoke about the accomplishment of one of the young racers who recently had a brain 
                    tumor removed. The Sandy Police provided an escort to the finish line for this young 
                    racer. She thanked Fire for the banquet and thanked staff for meeting with her this 
                    afternoon.

                    Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked staff for coordinating the Turkey Trot - it was a 
                    great effort by Parks. He also thanked Chief Bassett and his department for holding the 
                    annual Fire Banquet and he thanked neighboring cities' fire departments for providing 
                    coverage for Sandy during the time of the banquet. 

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey was sorry she missed the Fire Banquet, but had a 
                    WFWRD board meeting. The board passed the budget with no fee increases for this year. 
                    She provided an update from the Legislative Policy Committee. She spoke about 
                    upcoming legislative issues that will likely come up during the next legislative session: 
                    affordable housing, short term rentals, and water. 

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked staff for coordinating the tour of the Salt Lake 
                    County election facility. The tour highlighted the entire process for receiving and 
                    processing ballots. Election day is Tuesday, November 21st. Early voting starts today.

                    Dutin Fratto added one more item - he thanked the city partners at the MVP. Their staff 
                    held a pop up information booth at the Turkey Trot. Their next pop up event will be at City 
                    Hall tomorrow. He also mentioned that there is a link to the recent workshop for housing 
                    attainability. Please let him know if you would like that link.
