                                       LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 6:41 p.m.
SUB2024-001207 - Maria Ruiz is applying for modified setbacks in a single lot flag lot subdivision. Acres:
0.20. Location: 3154 South 9100 West. Zone: R-1-6. Planner: Justin Smith (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the Staff Report.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public meeting, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Maria Ruiz
Address: 3761 West 3015 South
Comments: Ms. Ruiz said she wants to build a house and has been working on this with her father. She
bought the land two years ago, didn’t know it would be this complicated. One structure but will be divided
internally. Her and her sister will live on one side and parents and other sisters on the other. Will be two
stories with one front door and then separate, no basement.

No one from the public is present to speak.

Commissioner Richards motioned to close the public meeting, Commissioner Weight seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Motion: To approve application #SUB2024-001207 Maria Ruiz is applying for modified setbacks in a
single lot flag lot subdivision with staff recommendations.
        Motion by: Commissioner Richards
        2nd by: Commissioner Weight
        Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor (of commissioners present)
