1. Public        Hearings
    1.1 Consideration                of CU-21-010,           Conditional          Use Permit               Request        for    a Java    Jo's     Drive
      Thru       Coffee      Shop    Location:          845 E. 4500 S. Applicant:                    Ryan      Corbin           Planner:      Samah
      Safiullah
      Samah        Safiullah      said the application            was for a two-lot           subdivision            and a drive thru coffee
      shop as a conditional               use permit.     She reviewed           the existing         conditions           of the property.          The
      existing      parcel     is currently      being    used as a parking            lot for Smith's           Grocery.          The existing         and
    proposed          parcel      is zoned C-2 and faces 4500               South,         which     is a UDOT             road. There is
      currently       sidewalk,      curb, and gutter            on the parcel.      The applicant             is creating         a new lot with
    ingress        and egress through            the subdivision         while      simultaneously              applying          for a conditional
    use permit.           The proposed         project    would      eliminate       a few parking              stalls and build           a 600 square
      foot building.         The applicant        was simultaneously                subdividing            the land to create a separate
    parcel       for the coffee        shop. The proposal            planned        to restripe       a portion         of the parking         lot to
Mmcreek        Planning         Commission               Meeting       Minutes                        16 June        2021          Page 2 of 4



   accommodate               for the new building.             There would              be a cross-access          easement         with     Smith's
   Grocery       so the subdivision              would       not include        ingress      and egress points.              Staff recommended
   additional       landscaping            due to the requirements                of Chapter         19.77    Water         Efficient       Landscape
   Design       and Development                Standards.        She showed             the Commission            the proposed             site plan
   which      included        a stacking        area for cars. Safiullah                said a traffic       study was required              due to the
   concern       of traffic         in the area. The study             did not propose          any necessary            traffic     mitigation
   provided       that an additional             drive thni         lane be provided          for the queuing             area as presented             on
   the site plan. The plan showed                        a queue space for 13 cars and 2 order waiting                              stalls which
   exceeded       the required             amount.       She showed         the Commission               elevations         of the proposed
   building      and the proposed               landscaping           plan. At their meeting              on Tuesday,           June 1st, 2021,           the
   Millcreek       Community               Council       voted to recommend                the application          without        any proposed
   mitigation       for detrimental             effects.     Some comments                were made at that meeting                     regarding
   increased       traffic     patterns.       Staff recommended                a continuance            of the application.


   Applicant       Ryan Corbin              was not present            for comment.


   Commissioner              Reid asked why              staff was recommending                    the continuance.             Safiullah      said there
   was a noticing            issue with        the public      hearing        notices      that were mailed           to surrounding             property
   owners.       Francis       Lilly     requested        guidance      from      the Commission              for the applicant.


   Chair      LaMar       opened         the public        hearing.


   Elyse      Sullivan,       808 E. Green Vista              Court,     said the primary             access to 48 townhomes                    was
   located      across the street (4500                  S.) to the south of the proposed                  site. She acknowledged                      4500
   S. being      a state road and that the Commission                           had limitations           with    traffic     mitigation,        but
   expressed       concern          with    the excess of traffic             that utilized        the center turning            lane on 4500 S.
   She said it felt like a game of chicken                          to enter the center turning               lane to turn left into the
   residential       area because            cars used it to turn left into Smiths,                    and there was often stacked                      cars
   on 4500 S. waiting                to turn left into Smith's              west access that made it difficult                      for residents        of
   the townhomes              to travel      west on 4500 S. She also pointed                        out that there was an access in
   close proximity            to the east of Smith's                east access (as identified               on the traffic         study)     that was
   not addressed          in the traffic         study.     She expressed           concern        for the many          points     of
   ingress/egress            from      the south and north            sides of 4500 S. going               both east and west between
   700 E. and 900 E. and felt that the increased                              traffic     during     rush hour,       identified         as peak times
   for the coffee         shop on the traffic              study,     would     be even more           of a burden           on the traffic        there.


   Commissioner              Claerhout        said he was aware               of the excess traffic           in the area. Lilly             said the
   traffic    study alluded             to a justified      deceleration         lane on the north            side of 4500          S. but it was
   never     installed.       He said the issue was identifying                         the incremental          additional        demand        that the
   proposed       facility      would        add to the traffic         and what the proposed                 facility      would        do about it.


   Chair     LaMar        closed        the public       hearing.


   Chair     LaMar        asked about the estimated                   number       of customers          during      peak hours           and aesthetic
   enhancements              with      the landscaping.         Safiullah       said there would             be more        screening        in the area
   discussed      in the technical             review      process,     but the code was vague                   on drive       thni     facilities.
   Lilly     said there were standards                   in code with       landscaping            peninsulas,      which        was what the
   applicant      was proposing.              Commissioner             Sieber     asked about screening                  from      4500 S. Lilly
