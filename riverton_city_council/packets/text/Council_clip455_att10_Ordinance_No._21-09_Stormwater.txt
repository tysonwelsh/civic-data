                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 21-09

 AN ORDINANCE OF RIVERTON CITY AMENDING 13-25-020, 13-25-060, 13-25-070,
      AND 13-25-090 OF THE RIVERTON CITY CODE, CONCERNING THE
 REGULATION OF STORMWATER DISCHARGE INTO RIVERTON CITY UTILITY
                               SYSTEMS

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it operates a
stormwater utility system which carries stormwater from roadways, and private properties into
the waters of the state of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, Title 13 Chapters 20 and 25 of Riverton City Municipal Code is reserved
for the management of the Riverton City stormwater utility system, particularly regulating
stormwater discharge by private persons, businesses, or other entities into the stormwater utility
system; and

        WHEREAS, Under the Utah Clean Water Act, particularly Utah Code Ann, §19-5-107,
the discharge of pollutants into the waters of the state of Utah is prohibited; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it is responsible under
the Utah Clean Water Act for the quality of the stormwater which is discharged from its
stormwater utility system into the waters of the state of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that, inasmuch as the City
is responsible for protecting the health, safety and welfare of its citizenry, it is also responsible
for maintaining the safe receipt of stormwater into City roadways and delivering the same
through its stormwater system; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that in order to protect the
quality of water which it discharges into the waters of the state of Utah vis-à-vis its stormwater
utility system, it is required to enforce regulations which impose water quality standards on all
waters which are discharged from private residences, businesses, and other entities and into the
Riverton City stormwater utility system; and

        WHEREAS, the City has authority under the Utah Water Quality Act, particularly Utah
Code Ann. § 19-5-115 (10) to enact and enforce ordinances or rules for the implementation water
quality standards which are consistent with the Utah Water Quality Act;

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that enforcement of these
regulations and management of stormwater systems generates a cost for Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that this cost should be
borne proportionately by individuals or entities who discharge stormwater into the Riverton City
stormwater system, meaning individuals or entities discharge a greater volumes of stormwater
into the City stormwater system should bear a greater cost for City management of said
stormwater; and

                                                  1
       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that distributing the costs
for management of the City Stormwater system and regulation of water quality standards should
occur by creating a stormwater utility system; and

       WHEREAS, in the year 2010, the Riverton City Council adopted Ordinance No. 10-01,
which established a stormwater utility which levied fees to users of the City stormwater utility
system; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Staff has recommended to the City Council that it update this
chapter or Riverton City Municipal Code to regulate the terms and conditions under which
stormwater utility customers may be assessed fees for the service of receiving, treating and
delivery stormwater from privately held property to the Riverton City stormwater utility system;
and


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       Section 1.     Riverton City Municipal Code, Title 13, Chapter 25, Sections 020, 060,
                      070, and 090 are hereby amended as described in Exhibit “A” attached
                      hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

       Section 2.     This ordinance shall become effective upon July 1, 2021.
