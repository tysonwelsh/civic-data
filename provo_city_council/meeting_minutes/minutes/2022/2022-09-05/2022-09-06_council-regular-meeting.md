 4.           A resolution adopting a Safety Action Plan in conjunction with the "Safe Streets For All"
              grant opportunity. (22-065) (23:15)

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Resolution 2022-47, as currently constituted, has been
                      made by council rule.

Rob Hunter, Assistant City Engineer, explained that the Safety Action Plan was presented in the work
meeting. He turned the time over to the consultants who helped develop the plan.

Vern Keeslar, Parametrix Consultant, presented the Provo Safety Action Plan.

Charles Allen, Parametrix Consultant, shared the statistics used during the 3-year study.

Vice-Chair MacKay asked what ages are included in the young driver group.

Mr. Allen responded that a young driver would include drivers that are 18 and younger. He added older
drivers include drivers that are 64 and older. He explained that some crashes can include multiple
groups so there is some cross-over of classifications.

Councilor Ellsworth asked if all the data was from 2019-2021. She mentioned the changes that the
pandemic brought during that time frame and asked if that had been taken into consideration.

Mr. Allen responded that the timeframe selected is hard to pinpoint. He said that too long of a
timeframe poses issues, as does too short of one. He shared that some of the statistics did go back
farther than the three years.

Councilor Fillmore asked for the consultants to identify the few areas that pose the highest risk.

Mr. Allen referred to the map that showed the total crashes during the three-year period and shared
which intersections had the most.

Councilor Whipple asked how the plan assesses different traffic patterns as they relate to speed.

Mr. Allen stated that the patterns shown in the plan lend to recommendations for education and
enforcement.

Chair Handley reminded Council about the conjunction of the Safety Action Plan and the grant
opportunity.

Chair Handley opened public comment.

Kat Linford, of Provo, shared concerns for widening 820 North.

With no other public comments, Chair Handley closed public comment and called for a vote.

      Vote:         The motion was approved 6:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley, MacKay,
                    Shipley, and Whipple in favor. Councilor Hoban excused.

 Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned by unanimous consent at approximately 6:18 PM.
