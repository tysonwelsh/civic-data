4.        Public Comments
          Justin Helmingdorf, Sandy resident at 1790 East Teakwood Drive, said he
          enjoyed visiting Mehraban Wetland Park. He expressed concern regarding a
          recent mass fish die-off that he believed was related to the aquafer not
          having been recharged. Mr. Helmingdorf suggested diverting more
          stormwater to the area to recharge the aquafer or drilling a well in the
          wetlands to divert more water. He suggested a windmill would aerate the
          wetlands and keep them more oxygenated, and suggested making the
          wetlands a living classroom area. He said he would like to speak with staff
          regarding the issue.

          Shaun Benjamin, a Draper resident, said he appreciated that the City worked
          to comply with yearly legislation regarding ADUs. He referred to an agenda
          item for the upcoming Planning Commission meeting regarding ADUs and
          asked that staff be directed to do another study and update ADU prohibition
          areas.

6.b   Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Bar Establishment License for
      BTDR LLC dba Bout Time Pub & Grub Draper.
      Planning Manager Todd Draper explained the request for Local Consent for
      Bout Time Pub & Grub and said the application met established distance
      requirements. He showed remodel plans and recommended approval with
      the condition that the applicant obtain a building permit to relocate the front
      entrance and complete construction before occupancy permit issuance and
      the sale of alcohol.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing.

      Dan Inouye, a Draper resident, said he drank and enjoyed alcohol, but was
      opposed to the request for Local Consent because the business was in a very
      public location with kids passing every day. He expressed the opinion that
      approval would change the character of the neighborhood negatively.

      Mayor Walker closed the public hearing.

      Tim Ryan, the applicant, said Bout Time Pub & Grub was a Utah-based
      company started in 2009. Mr. Ryan said Bout Time Pub & Grub had a
      reputation for being a good neighbor and running a clean operation. He said
      in 15 years of business, no liquor liability claims had been filed. Mr. Ryan
      emphasized the location met State proximity restrictions.

      Councilmember Green moved to approve the Local Consent with the
      recommended building permit condition. Councilmember T. Lowery
      seconded the motion.

      Councilmember Green referred to a nearby Garage Grill with a similar license
      and said he believed the City needed to be consistent.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.

6.c   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1608, an Ordinance vacating a portion of the City
      right-of-way located at approximately 900 East 12200 South
      Assistant City Manager Kellie Challburg explained the recommendation to
      vacate a portion of the City right-of-way at approximately 900 East 12200
      South. She said utilities in the right-of-way would remain, but the City had no
      continued use for the property.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing.

      Dan Inouye, a Draper resident, said he lived two doors down from the subject
      property. He said it was his understanding the property would eventually be
      sold, and requested that all parties be given a chance to bid on the property.

      Mayor Walker closed the public hearing.

      Ms. Challburg said the City was having an appraisal of the property done to
      sell in the future. Acting City Manager/City Attorney Mike Barker said State
      law and City Code gave the Council discretion regarding terms of sale.

      Councilmember T. Lowery moved to approve                  Ordinance    #1608.
      Councilmember Johnson seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
