Planner Lewis said the applicant is requesting a commercial dog kennel to be located on 
50 acres west of Kamas.  They are applying for a permit of up to 40 dogs.  There is an 
existing accessory structure that will be used to house the dogs indoors.  There will be a 
couple of fenced play areas to exercise and work the dogs.  One fenced area will have

dwelling unit.  The business plan includes having a caretaker on site 24 hours a day.

with the Code.  The Findings of Facts and Conditions of Approval are listed in the Staff 
Report.  The Attorney’s office has recommended a condition be added that any 
reasonable conditions requested by Animal Control be complied with.  This could be 
accomplished by adding a condition or amending condition #4.
