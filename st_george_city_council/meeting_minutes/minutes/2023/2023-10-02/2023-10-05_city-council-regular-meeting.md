CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Alliance
        Consulting for the engineering of Lizard Wash Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The 14-acre community park concept
          has been created by Park Planning and now needs to be professionally
          engineered, and construction documents made so the project can go out to bid
          next fiscal year. A community open house will be held on the 28th of this month
          to garner public input and will be incorporated into the design. Staff
          recommends approval.

      b. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Bowen
         Collins for Millcreek Sewer Lift Station.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The existing sewer line in this location
          needs to be upsized and is nearing capacity. Staff bid the replacement of the
          sewer line 2 separate times and have been unable to bring the costs down to a
          reasonable level. The installation of a sewer lift station with pressure sewer line
          is expected to save a significant amount of money. Staff recommends approval.

      c. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Alliance
         Consulting for Tech Ridge Water Projects.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Design of a Water Tank (2) pump
          Stations, and waterlines across the project. Staff recommends approval.

      d. Consider approval of an agreement to purchase real property from CDHC
         II, LLC for the extension of 1450 East roadway.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City desires to acquire 88 square
          feet of property for the extension of 1450 South roadway located at 1450 South

      f.   Consider approval to purchase P25 Compliance 700/800Mhz public-
           safety radios for the Fire Department pursuant to State Contract #
           AR3841.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Fire Department is requesting to
           purchase replacement handheld and mobile radios that are non-compliant with
           the new P25 system. The State of Utah is currently in the process of a complete
           upgrade to our public-safety radio system which is approaching 21 years in use
           and is beyond its functional life span. As part of this upgrade nearly all public
           safety radios in use (Police, Fire, and Dispatch) will be rendered obsolete,
           requiring a replacement of all radio equipment that is not Project 25 (P25)
           compliant. It is anticipated that the new system will be launched in the spring of
           2024. With this change to occur in the near future the Fire Department requests
           to purchase replacement radios that meet the new P25 radio system
           requirements. This request will purchase 34 mobile radios for fire apparatus and
           133 portable handheld radios for fire personnel. Funding for this item is included
           in tonight's proposed budget amendment. The Fire Department currently has 32
           portable hand held radios and 4 mobile radios that are compliant with the P25
           system. The Fire Department has applied for a Federal Emergency Management
           Agency (FEMA) Assistance to Firefighters Grant (AFG) the past two years to assist
           in the funding for this purchase, however we have not been awarded the grant
           funding. The majority of the radios in service with the fire department are over
           12 years old and were purchased through a grant when the 800Mhz radio system
           was placed in service in the southwest area of the state. Staff recommends
           approval subject to approval of the budget opening.

REQUEST FOR SPONSORSHIP:
     Consider approval of a $2,000 sponsorship to the Boys and Girls Club of
     Southern Utah for their First Annual Golf Tournament fundraiser.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The Boys & Girls Club is now entering its
      second year of operations in St George, providing a safe place for kids with nowhere
      to go after school. The Boys and Girls Club provides academic support, enrichment
      activities, hands-on STEM experiences, teach digital safety & responsibility, and even
      feed the kids a delicious meal & snacks every day! These programs are currently
      offered at 8 Schools in St George at no cost to the kids or their families. This
      program has been so successful there is now a waiting list of kids to join. The Boys
      and Girls Club is holding its first ever Boys & Girls Club of Southern Utah Golf
      Tournament to raise funds to support this very successful program in our schools
      and is requesting sponsorship from the City of $2,000. Staff recommends approval.
