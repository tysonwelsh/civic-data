Chief Randy   Thomas thanked the Redevelopment Agency Staff for all of the work at the
Mural Festival. He announced that Officer Eric Densely had submitted his retirement
date, and another officer has tentatively turned in her resignation. He said they are starting

the Unified Police stats for May and budgetary items. He said they purchased a drone to
use in their jurisdictions and will be starting up a drone program with certifications. He
said using drones will be very helpful and useful.

      A. DISCUSS FRAUD RISK ASSESSMENT
Mariah Hill discussed Fraud Risk Assessment.

Fraud Risk Assessment - What is it?
   . Developed by Office of the State Auditor.
     . Designed to help measure and reduce the risk of fraud, abuse, and noncompliance
       in local governments.
     . Developed to help local government board members understand best practices in
       fraud detection and prevention.
     . Assessment assigns an overall risk level of undetected fraud based on a "point"
       system.
     . Same assessment is used for small governments (with no staff) to large entities
       (such as Salt Lake City).
     . Assessment should be a "starting point" for regular review of internal controls.
Fraud Risk Assessment - What it is not
   . An internal control assessment performed by an independent auditor as part of a
      financial statement audit.
          . l\Iethodologies and purposes are different.
   . A "high" score does not necessarily indicate a lack of internal controls.
