OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation and The Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag were offered during the work
     meeting.

      Mayor Randall read proclamations proclaiming the week of September 17 – 23, 2021
      as Constitution Week and the week of September 6 – 12, 2021 as Patriotism Week.

      Valerie King spoke about events taking place in conjunction with Constitution Week
      after which Sharon Goetzman spoke about events taking place in conjunction with
      Patriotism Week.

      Mayor Randall mentioned events taking place on the September 11th.

      Mitch Cloward with St. George Regional Medical Center provided a PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: To continue to be safe and protect others;
      Snapshot of hospital census for September 2, 2021; Hospital COVID census; St.
      George Regional Hospital COVID patient distribution (week of 08/28/21); St. George
      Regional Hospital COVID deaths (December 2020 through end of August 2021); and
      Helping people live the healthiest lives possible. He introduced Cindy Gilbert, Chair
      of the Hospital’s Governing Board. Mr. Cloward continued with the PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: Hospital board; Hurricane campus; Site
      plan; About us: What it means to receive care in a Level II Trauma Center; Ernie’s
      story; Recent innovations; Hospital recognitions; and Community partnerships.

      Councilmember McArthur noted he recently had his first child; he was so impressed
      with how much all of the hospital staff cared about him and his wife.

      Mr. Cloward provided updates on what is next for the hospital - increasing access to
      healthcare and make it more affordable. As large as the hospital is, he is concerned
      about the growth and the future. He also spoke about plans for the old hospital
      site.

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the zone from C-4 (Central
     Business District Commercial) to PD-C (Planned Development Commercial)
     on approximately 2.03 acres located on 50 North 200 East Street.

      Planner Carol Davidson presented the request to change the zone from C-4 to PD-C
      on approximately 2.03 acres located on 50 North 200 East Street. She provided a
      PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: Aerial map; General plan
      map; Zoning map; Site plan; Parking; Elevations; Roof top design; Materials board;
      Use list; and Planning Commission recommendation.

      After being asked by Councilmember Larkin, Commissioner Almquist spoke about
      where trees would be planted and landscaping in general. There will be greenery on
      the roof; however, they will need to look at heat mitigation for the pickleball courts.
      He spoke about parking for bikes as well as additional parking outside of the garage
      that may be able to be utilized for more bike parking. There are restrooms on the
      roof area for patrons. Additionally, he stated the garage will have measures to
      restrict access during non-business hours. He also spoke about other security
      measures planned for the garage.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember McArthur to approve the

          The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin.

PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of the preliminary plat for the South Desert Townhomes,
     a 132-lot residential subdivision located at approximately White Dome Drive
     and 2500 East in the Southern Hills development.

      Assistant Public Works Director Wes Jenkins presented the preliminary plat for the
      South Desert Townhomes, a 132-lot residential subdivision located at approximately
      White Dome Drive and 2500 East in the Southern Hills development. He showed and
      outlined the preliminary plat, including eliminating the sidewalk on one side of the
      road in the middle of the subdivision. He showed a slide named PD-R Areas 1, 2, and
      10 – speaking about a secondary access, either interchange 5 or going through
      Desert Canyons over to Desert Canyons Parkway.

      After being asked by City Manager Adam Lenhard, applicant Brett Burgess explained
      there will be an HOA for this development. They are trying to make the streets in
      the subdivision public as private streets can be a bit of a nightmare. He would like to
      have dual trails on both sides of the subdivision. He noted that driveways are
      proposed at 20’, this being one of the reasons he would like to eliminate the
      sidewalks on the one side. The units will have garages; however, residents will be
      allowed to park in the driveways as well.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard commented that he is concerned as if a resident has a
      larger vehicle, having a sidewalk gives somewhat of a buffer. Without the sidewalk,
      there is a potential of that vehicle hanging out into the right of way.

      Mr. Burgess explained that earlier today, they figured out that they can get
      sidewalks to work within the cross section. If they need to install sidewalks on both
      sides, they can make it work.

      Mr. Jenkins noted that they would need to be private; however, staff is trying to
      come up with a new standard.

      Mr. Burgess commented they are able to install sidewalks on both sides; however,
      since the ordinance does not exist, he would request them to be private streets but
      work on them being public, with dual sidewalks prior to the final plat recording.

      Mayor Randall asked for clarification on a possible motion - if the Council wishes to
      approve the request, she believes it be subject to development agreement regarding
      secondary access after the three (3) plats are complete and dual sidewalks.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard asked the council for their input.

      Councilmember McArthur commented wider streets are being sacrificed for more
      density.

      Councilmember Larkin mentioned that wider streets do not always equal safer
      streets. People slow down on more narrow streets.

      Mr. Jenkins noted that allowing with allowing this type of density, they are basically
      regulating that residents cannot park on the street. Staff felt like they can narrow
      them down as the driveways restrict parking. He drove around in similar
      developments with private streets; there are some large trucks that hang out onto
      the right of way. Staff may need to look at changing the setbacks.

      Councilmember McArthur commented he was in a development in Hurricane with
      narrow streets and larger vehicles, it was not great. He noted the City does need an
      ordinance.

      Deputy City Attorney Paula Houston inquired about if the request complies with the
      fire code as there is only one access in.

      Mr. Jenkins replied yes, as long as the development has a planned road to provide a
      second access, the fire department has allowed the development to move forward. A
      trigger date or a phase needs to be identified. The hope is that the interchange 5 is
      built.

      Mr. Burgess commented on the second access. Part of their thought is if interchange
      is built and an access road is built over to Exit 6, they will start inviting traffic on a
St. George City Council Minutes

      paved road and shut it down to build the interchange. Once he gets going, he will
      want the second access at 50%.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard noted his concern is a public safety concern. Neighbors
      on both sides were required a secondary access.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to approve the
                    preliminary plat for the South Desert Townhomes, a 132-lot residential
                    subdivision located at approximately White Dome Drive and 2500 East

                    a development agreement with the City for the secondary access

      After being asked by Councilmember McArthur, City Manager Adam Lenhard
      explained the trend is to have smaller cross sections and less asphalt. Not having on
      street parking makes this project much more feasible. Staff needs to look at
      deepening driveways for future projects. He suggested the Council specify their
      preference.

      motion.
