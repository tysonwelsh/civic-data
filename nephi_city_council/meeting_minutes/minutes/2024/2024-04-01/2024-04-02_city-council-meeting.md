Mayor Justin Seely opened the regular council meeting and welcomed all present.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor JD Parady moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting 3-19-24, work session minutes from 3-26-24, a list of claims on
the warrant register dated 4-2-24 and the sale of burial rights to Stephen R. Hill. Councilor Shari Cowan
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

ZERO FATALITIES PRESENTATION:

   Sabrina Wilhite, representing Zero Fatalities, presented to the Council the mission of the organization
and the many different programs promoting safe driving for all ages. She stated that she has been
working with City Attorney Wright to provide tools and resources to help residents of Nephi understand
the laws governing OHV and golf cart use. Zero Fatalities are preparing flyers for parents and teens to
help them understand the law and how to be safe. City Attorney Wright said that hopefully this public
relations blitz will help deter accidents and aid in education and obedience of the laws.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

AWOS REPLACEMENT PROJECT ENGINEER CONTRACT WITH JUB APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson reported to the Council that the Automated Weather Observing System or AWOS
system at the airport is outdated and needs to be replaced. Due to some federal funding that was made
available from the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law (BIL) passed by Congress, this project was able to move
up in priority on the Airport’s Capital Improvement Project list and be funded this year. City staff
requested proposals from qualified engineering firms and had a committee review of the submitted
proposals. The recommended proposal is from JUB.
   JUB has an office in Salt Lake City and a strong aviation group within their firm. They recently
completed an AWOS replacement project, similar to the Nephi Airport, in Roosevelt.
JUB had provided a professional services agreement to contract with the city on the AWOS replacement
project. JUB will provide engineering and construction management services for the project. The
estimate for the whole project (design + construction) was listed at $238,500 with the Federal
government covering 90.63% and the state and local governments splitting the remaining costs. The
local share will be $12,329. JUB believes that the total project cost may come in under the

1620 NORTH ANNEXATION CERTIFICATION NOT READY FOR APPROVAL:

  Mr. Atkinson informed the Council that the 1620 North Annexation certification was not ready
because of the map not having all the necessary details completed. City Recorder Lisa Brough told the
council that she would email the letter of certification to the Council within the next couple of days.

CITY COUNCIL AND CITY ADMINISTRATIVE UPDATES:

         Mr. Atkinson informed the Council that he would send out registration information for the R6
          Regional Summit to be held on May 21, 2024
         The Council agreed to cancel the regular council meeting on May 21, 2024 and change the
          date to May 14, 2024 for a regular council meeting.
         Mr. Atkinson reported that he will not be at the meeting on April 19th because he will be
          attending the UMCA conference. He noted that the temporary living ordinance will be
          reviewed at that meeting and that if the public wanted to review the ordinance, they could
          access the March 12th and March 26th staff reports on the Nephi City website.
         Mr. Atkinson informed the Council that he will be sworn in as the new UMCA president at the
          upcoming conference.
         Mr. Atkinson updated the council that a new airport lease was signed by the Mayor with Ryan

          and enjoyed it very much.
         Mayor Seely complimented Councilor Parady on his work with the Chamber of Commerce
          and for how he has responded to the public on controversial issues. Mayor Seely commented
          that he would like to hear from the public more through public meetings and would
          appreciate it if people would express their differences more civilly.

ADJOURNED INTO EXECUTIVE SESSION:

   Councilor Travis Worwood moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual, the acquisition of real property and litigation. Councilor Parady seconded
the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 7:45 p.m.

Work Session 8:20 pm

    1. The Council discussed an access easement and a construction easement that McDonalds is
       requesting across some city owned property. The Council directed staff to allow the easements.
    2. The Council directed staff to go out to bid again for a purchaser for some property the City owns
       in the Crimson Flats development. The City would like to sell the property to a hotel developer.
    3. Mayor Seely said that he would like the work to begin on the beautification project of the center
       freeway interchange.
    4. Councilor Cowan moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the
       motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote. The meeting was adjourned at 8:30 pm.
