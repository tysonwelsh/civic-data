2.       DISCUSSION ITEMS
        a. Proposed plan and ordinances for the Southwest Quadrant
City Planner/Zoning Administrator Larry Gardner outlined objectives for the Southwest Quadrant:
    • Responsibly plan the acreage to accommodate growth from commercial users without
        negatively impacting existing framework
     •   Create and market the quadrant a distinct submarket for industrial and high-tech
         manufacturing that will bring a range of jobs and economic development to the City
     •   Create opportunity for people to live and work in West Jordan
     •   Create a cohesive masterplan with open space and trail network
     •   Efficiently buffer existing residential neighborhoods and help commercial users feel
         comfortable next to residential
     •   Find solutions to infrastructure constraints

As aerials of the area were shown infrastructure constraints and existing land use was pointed out.
Mr. Gardner noted one of the main goals was to preserve as much commercial industrial office space
as possible. To buffer the commercial against existing residential a park was being proposed
between Echo Ridge, the “for rent” product, and the industrial areas. Several commercial
development, retail, and maker space examples were shown. A site plan and front elevations were
shown to illustrate the suggested buffer product.

Council Member Jacob noted liking the majority of the proposal but felt current homeowners may
be opposed. Chair Kayleen Whitelock reiterated that the goal was to protect existing housing and
noted that there was no single family allowed in a commercial zone. Vice Chair Kelvin Green felt it
was a good buffer plan.

       b. Proposed Tentative Budget for the City of West Jordan for Fiscal Year 2023
Vice Chair Green with consensus of the Council requested that changes to the City Council budget be as
follows: Professional and Tech $6,000, Training $9,000, Travel $8,000, and Department Supplies
$2,000.

Council Member Pamela Bloom requested a reduction to the budget for the State of City Annual Report.
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck responded that the item noted was for the Annual
Report that Council had asked for last year. Chair Whitelock asked that the budget be moved into
Administrative Services. Vice Chair Green wanted the report to be sent electronically. Communication
Director Tauni Barker noted the budget was strictly for printing and mailing costs. She agreed that the
city needed to move to digital distribution. Council did not reach a consensus and asked that the item
be brought back to the next meeting for further discussion.

Chair Whitelock referencing page 74, asked that the notations “N/A” in the Goals and Performance
Workload section be completed.

Vice Chair Green questioned the $10,000 travel budget on pg. 75 in Economic Development. Council
Member Bloom preferred not to cut the budget being that travel expenses were increasing. Council
Member Jacob agreed. Chair Whitelock requested it be cut to $5,000 or maybe $7,000. No consensus
was reached.

Vice Chair Green requested the supply budget be reduced. Chair Whitelock suggested it be reduced to
$1,000 and all Council members agreed.

Council Member Bloom questioned the change in budget from $225,000 to $75,000 on line 212.
Communication Director Tauni Barker responded that it was simply in a change to reposition expenses
to the appropriate departments.

Referencing page 77 Chair Whitelock asked for a timeline regarding the Annual Resident Budget
Report. She noted that she believed the purpose of the report was to educate the public regarding
budgetary needs and wanted assurance it would go out before making any budget decisions. She also
voiced a desire to stop the West Jordan Journal. Communications Director Barker believed they could
phase the Journal out by increasing email however whether they stopped the Journal or took it through
the end of the contract period would not change the cost. Council Member Bloom was okay to eliminate
the Journal once the contract was complete. Vice Chair Green was okay to eliminate the Journal. Council
asked that Communications Director Barker bring back a proposal to phase out the Journal and reduce
the budget to $20,000.

Vice Chair Green asked for clarification regarding the budget expense for Facebook. Communications
Director Barker noted they were moving away from advertisements on the radio and learned that
Facebook and Instagram ranked higher than other methods.

Staff responded to questions on page 80-115 regarding supplies, noticing, educational assistance,
recruitment, legal services, and employment cost increases.

Referencing page 136 Council asked for more information regarding the Annual Events Guidebook.
Council requested staff proposed new ways to make the guidebook a more valuable resource.
Suggestions were made to reach out to the Chamber, local businesses, and Salt Lake County to include
coupons for local activities.

Council consensus regarding the Cemetery budget was to increase the cost of nonresident plots.

   Regarding the Arts Council $60,000 budget, Chair Whitelock, speaking for Council Member Worthen
   suggested that they start with $30,000 and provide a matching grant. Council Member Bloom agreed.
   Vice Chair Green was fine to work towards that but did not want to require it this year. City
   Administrator Korban Lee requested that they do not ty the budget to their contract and change the
   name to Community Arts. Chair Whitelock requested they leave the budget at $60,000. City Attorney
   Rob Wall suggested that they require a report from the committee with a plan for the upcoming year
   and based on the report the Council would then choose how to appropriate the money.

   Regarding the Historical Committee both Vice Chair Green and Chair Whitelock wanted the committee
   to provide a report prior to any money being distributed and update the contract to reflect that
   requirement.

   Vice Chair Green offered kudos to City Recorder Tangee Sloan for the request to automate election
   financial reports with an online system.

   Chair Whitelock and Vice Chair Green questioned the request for a lobbying budget. Communications
   Director Barker responded that she felt the request was a great start and noted that the City had missed
   out on federal funding in the past. She reported that they would not see a return on the investment in
   the first year, she believed it would take 3-5 years but hoped to see a return in the next year. Chair
   Whitelock requested data on what other cities had spent and their return on investment.

   Although Vice Chair Green was not comfortable with the request of $35,000 for a city-wide survey, after
   Communications Director and City Administrator explained the hope was to find out perceptions from
   the residents of how the city could improve. The consensus was to leave it in the budget.

   At 7:26 pm Council took a five-minute recess and reconvened at 7:30 pm

   Council Member Bloom asked for future consideration, to include octagons in busy intersections
   allowing all lights to go red for emergency vehicles. Additionally, she asked that staff work with
   legislators to fast track the 7800 South project. Public Works Director Brian Clegg reported that the
   project was moving forward. Responding to a question regarding striping changes from Council
   Member Jacob, Mr. Clegg reported that he would bring back a proposal.

   Regarding the Fee Schedule Council Members asked to create an ADHOC Committee for reviewing those
   fees. The example of a chicken permit coming out of animal services should be placed in business
   licensing. Council Members asked that fees for nonresident rentals of Pioneer Hall be increased, and
   City Administrator Lee asked that Isaac and Danyce bring back a proposal.

    3.    ADJOURN 8:00 PM
   Council Member Jacob moved to adjourn the meeting. Council Member Green seconded the
   motion. All voted in favor and the motion passed unanimously.
   The meeting adjourned at 8:00 pm
