                                       RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 23-15

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 18.235.040(6), LANDSCAPE DESIGN STANDARDS,
   ADDRESSING A SINGLE WORD CLARIFICATION, AMENDMENTS PROPOSED BY
                            RIVERTON CITY

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said amendments; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
amend the Riverton City Code Section 18.235.040(6), Landscape Design Standards, as described in Exhibit
“A”.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as follows:

       Section 1. The Riverton City Code Section 18.235.040(6), Landscape Design Standards, shall be,
       and hereby is, amended as shown in Exhibit “A.”

       Section 2. This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 17th day of October,
