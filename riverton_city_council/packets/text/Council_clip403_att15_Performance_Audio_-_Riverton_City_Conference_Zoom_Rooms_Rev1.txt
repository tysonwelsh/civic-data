    Scope Of Work

       This proposal is our understanding of the customer needs based upon information gathered during site survey(s) and any further
       customer communications. Please take the time to review this proposal and ensure that there is an understanding of its contents.

       The proposal addresses the design, provision and installation of the Audiovisual equipment as indicated in the following pages.


       The scope of the work is to provide and install the equipment listed in the proposal and as noted below:

       Scope:


       Police Building - Conference Room:

           ·     Room Use: Video Conferencing, set up like a training room with tables. This room will not be used as an overflow area for
                 the Council Chambers.
           ·     Provide and mount a static camera on the front wall above the existing TV to capture the participants in the room for the
                 far end of the video conference.
           ·     Provide and install a microphne system to pick up the room participants for the video conference participants on the far
                 side.
           ·     When the divider wall is closed, the main room becomes the only room with vido conferencing ability. When open, both
                 rooms function as one large room.
           ·     Integrate the newly proposed gear with the existing AV gear, equipment rack, owner furnished computers, etc.
           ·     Clean up cables around the existing displays.
           ·     Provide and install a new DSP and Control system for this room.
           ·     Train client on how to use the new system.

     Public Works Building - Multi-Purpose Room:

         ·    Room Use: Video Conferencing, Training. The room configuration may change.
         ·    Provide and mount a static camera on the front center of the room above the clock to capture the main room participants.
              The front row will be pushed back to allow all to be seen in the camera.
         ·    Provide and install a microphone system to pick up the participants in the room, to allow those on the far end of the video
              conferencing system to hear. Integrate this with their existing audio system and video system.
         ·    Provide and install new ceiling speakers in the main area, and the overflow area. If we are able to remove the existing
              speakers and replace the new speakers in their place, we will do this. If this is not possible, we will install the new
              speakers in new locations, and disconnect but leave the existing speakers where they are in order to prevent the client
              from having holes in their ceiling tiles.
         ·    Use the existing equipment rack in the server room, and move the reused gear from the current rack in the room to this
              rack. The owner furnished PC will live in the equipment rack as well. Client will need to provide a way to control the video
              conferencing with this computer while in the room if desired.
         ·    Provide and install a new DSP and Control system for this room. Replace the existing wall controller with a touch panel
              wall mounted controller. Control shall provide the ability to control the display on/off, raise and lower screen, turn on/off the
              projector, and audio volume control.
         ·    Existing Apple TV will not be used, The client will provide the owner furnished computer to host the conferencing. The
              existing blueray player will also not be reinstalled since the rack is now located in the Server Room.
         ·    We are removing the piece of equipment from this room, that controls the displays Conference Rooms 1 and 2 of this
              building. We are not responsible for anything in these Conference Rooms. Client is aware of this. Also, the newtwork
              switch that ties these rooms together will no longer be used.
         ·    Train client on how to use the new system.
