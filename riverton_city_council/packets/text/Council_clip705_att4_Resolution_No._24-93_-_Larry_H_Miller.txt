                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-93

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER BE ISSUED TO LARRY H.
 MILLER FORD TO PURCHASE A 2024 TRANSIT CARGO VAN TO REPLACE THE
     WATER DEPARTMENT VAN THAT WAS INVOLVED IN AN ACCIDENT


       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the Water Department’s transit van was involved in an accident; and

      WHEREAS, the insurance company has totaled it; and

      WHEREAS, the Water Department is in need of a replacement van.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
      1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order be issued to Larry H. Miller Ford to
         purchase a 2024 Transit Cargo Van to replace the Water Department Van that was
         involved in an accident.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
October 2024 by the following vote:
