CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes for the regular council meeting 8-16-22, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 9-6-22
and the sale of burial rights to Patrick McCaffery and John and Gladel McCarthy. Councilor Callaway
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PAR TAX GRANT AWARDED TO JUAB FINE ARTS COUNCIL:

  Mayor Seely presented the PAR Tax Grant to the Juab Fine Art Council (JFAC) in the amount of
$5910.00 Michelle Harris and David Whipple representing JFAC accepted the grant and handed out a
brochure showing all the event that JFAC will be sponsoring throughout the next year. They expressed
their appreciation for the grant and support of the city council.

JALENA WARNER RETIRMENT RECOGNITION:

  Mayor Seely presented Jalena Warner with a plaque and gift for her retirement as Nephi City
Swimming Pool Manager. Jalena had worked for the city pool since 1991. Mayor Seely, the Council, and
Government Buildings Manager Clark Wood all expressed their appreciation to Ms. Warner for her
dedicated and loyal service as an employee of Nephi City.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

  Library Director Ruth Bonzo addressed the council and informed them that Ali Painter has left the
employment as the Teen Librarian. Ms. Painter will continue working for the city as the Communication
Coordinator but will mostly be working from home. Ms. Bonzo informed the council that Library Clerk
Randy Jesson is also leaving the Library and moving away from Nephi.
   Ms. Bonzo updated the council about a new book drop she acquired for free from Murray City that
Clark Wood will be installing. She updated them about several grants different programing ideas she is
currently working on.

138 kV PROJECT ANNEXATION AREA LEVEL CHANGED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with he council that as part of the 138 kV power transmission line project,
some of the properties along the proposed alignment have requested service connections. To connect
these properties with electric service, they must be annexed into the city. The first step before
annexation could be considered is to move these properties from a Level 3 to a Level 2 in the annexation
area map. Mr. Atkinson went on to explain that after a property becomes a Level 2, it is included in an
analysis outlined in the annexation area plan. If the Council chooses to change the level on these
properties, a Level 2 analysis will be completed and presented to the Council at a work session.
  Most of the landowners within the project area have requested electrical connections in exchange for
the easements needed for the transmission project. To supply these electrical connections, the
properties must be annexed. Some of the property owners have not requested electrical connections
now but would like to have future connections if development occurs. The area also includes property
owned by the City and the School District. Shane Jackson, who lives directly west of the city-owned
property has also expressed interest in annexing but recently notified Mr. Atkinson that he is no longer
interested in annexing. There are also two large industrial businesses who would like to connect to
Nephi Power. The ongoing cash flows from these businesses will aid in helping to pay for the
transmission line extension.
    Councilor Memmott stated that he still had a lot of concern with the large annexation, but he knows
that moving to a Level 2 will help answer his questions and concerns. Councilor Parady moved to
approve the 138kV Project annexation area changed from a Level 3 to a Level 2 for further analysis.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

RACCOON EYE ANNEXATION AGREEMENT APPROVED:

    Councilor Ostler moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the Raccoon Eye Annexation Agreement
with Mr. Joe Humphries. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

ORDINANCE 09-06-2022 – RACCOON EYE ANNEXATION ADOPTED:

   Councilor Parady moved to adopt Ordinance 09-06-2022. The Raccoon Eye Annexation consists of
10.98 acres located approximately one-half mile west of Main Street on the northside of Hwy. 132.
(Parcel No. XB00-2580) The property would be classified into the ID-3 Light Industrial zone. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

RECREATION DIRECTOR CRAIG OSWALD OUT-OF-STATE TRAVEL APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson explained to the council that Craig Oswald has been invited to speak at the National
Alliance for Youth Sports (NAYS) Youth Sports Congress in Orlando, FL. The event will be held on
November 16-19 and is a four-day comprehensive educational experience designed specifically for
youth sports administrators. NAYS will cover the flight and a rental car and Craig is looking for a
scholarship to cover hotel and food. Craig will be speaking on a panel talking about how to get more
youth sports officials involved in programs along with some individuals from Kansas. This is a great
opportunity for Craig to share his experience here in Nephi with other people from the nation as well as
network with other recreation employees.
   Councilor Ostler moved to approve the out-of-state travel for Craig Oswald. Councilor Parady
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
