CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2023-3128, A RESOLUTION TO OPPOSE
THE DESIGNATION OF THE PROPOSED GRAND CANYON NATIONAL

MONUMENT

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the President of the United States is
authorized through the Antiquities Act to designate national monuments. Recently a proposal by
Arizona Senator Sinema to designate a national monument called the Grand Canyon National
Monument of 1.1 million acres, including the Arizona Strip. The proposed monument would limit
uses on the Arizona Strip. This Resolution would express Washington County’s opposal to this
national monument.

Commissioner Snow further stated this is an overreach of the federal government and the
Antiquities Act. Sixty six percent (66%) of Washington County is federal land and this monument
would further limit access to land surrounding Washington County.

Commissioner Almquist mentioned that the Tenth Amendment specifies that decisions such as
this are to be left up to the state.

Commissioner Iverson recalled the Grand Staircase-Escalante Monument being declared as a
national monument in 1996 by President Clinton.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2023-3128, A
Resolution to Oppose the Designation of the Proposed Grand Canyon National
Monument. Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
