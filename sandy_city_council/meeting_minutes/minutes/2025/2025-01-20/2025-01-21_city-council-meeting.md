Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions

                       Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer welcomed those in attendance.

                       The Chair asked for a moment of silence to be observed.
                       The Chair then led the Pledge.

                       Council moved to Item 1 on the agenda.

General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                       Council Chair Aaron Dekeyzer invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                       Comment.

                       Mr. Samuel Winkler, Project Manager for the 90th South Road Improvement provided an
                       update to the Council. The project is 76% complete and should be completed by July
                       2025. Next week, improvements along 700 West will begin. He expressed his
                       appreciation to the city staff for their support.

                       Mr. Craig Ulrich spoke in support of offering a Prayer at the beginning of Council
                       meetings. A majority of the citizens support beginning the meetings with a Prayer. He did
                       not believe the Chair had the authority to eliminate the Prayer. At the Presidential
                       Inauguration, five prayers were offered. He vowed to keep fighting to keep the Prayer as
                       part of the meeting.

                       Ms. Pat Jones thanked council and Administration for their support of the MVP shelter
                       and for ensuring that it is well-run facility. She also fully supported Mr. Ulrich's
                       comments. Meetings should include prayer and not be taken away. If we have God, all
                       good things will come. This is where the power comes out.

                       Ms. Catherine Britton thinks its time for a useful law and recommended the Council pass
                       a law that it is too cold in Sandy.

                       Ms. Kris Nicholl thanked the Council for the feedback signs set up on their street a year
                       ago. She requested help from the Council with the next step in the process to get speed
                       signs posted in her neighborhood.

                       Public comment closed.

                       Council moved to Item 3 on the Agenda.

Council Business

2.         25-022                 Council Office recommending the Council receive a quarterly update on the
                                  Medically Vulnerable People (MVP) Facility
                   Attachments:   MVP Q4 Report
                                  MVP Q4 CUP.MOU Compliance
                                  MVP Q4 Slides - Year End Review.1.21.25

                                  Staff with Shelter the Homeless provided a quarterly update and report to the Council on
                                  the Medically Vulnerable People Interim Housing Program. Mike Young with the Road
                                  Home provided an overview of the program, the residents of the MVP housing, and the
                                  2024 year end impact. To date, 388 individuals were served, ranging in age from 31 to 86.
                                  He spoke about the ongoing collaboration with Sandy Police and Fire, volunteerism, and
                                  connections to the community. He spoke about lessons learned and the vision and goals
                                  for 2025. Jeniece Olsen with the Fourth Street Clinic provided an overview of patient care
                                  and the goals and vision for 2025. Laurie Hopkins with Shelter the Homeless spoke about
                                  facility and security updates to the MVP Shelter. She thanked the Council,
                                  Administration, and Police and Fire and expressed her gratitude. Council questions
                                  followed.

                                  Following the presentation, Council moved to General Citizen Comment.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Houseman completed the Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District
                    (WFWRD) orientation and is looking forward to serving as the Council liaison. She also
                    made a statement regarding this evening's deviation from starting the meeting with prayer
                    and replacing it with a moment of silence. She also requested the Chair not direct the
                    purpose of the moment of silence and to allow the public to dedicate their moment of
                    silence to whatever they feel moved to consider.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson attended the Community Development Block Grant
                    (CDBG) meeting and provided an update. He mentioned that he is looking forward to
                    serving on new committees this year.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer mentioned that many other municipal meetings do not
                    start their meetings with a prayer. He provided an update from Legislative Policy
                    Committee (LPC). Tomorrow is Local Officials Day and the Sandy Youth Council will be
                    participating in the mock committee at the event tomorrow.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update from the Sandy Historic Committee.
                    There is a walking tour app available. There is an opening on the committee. Please let
                    us know if you are interested. She attended the Sandy Explorers Awards banquet last
                    Friday. It was a great program. She also attended the pre-legislative town hall event last
                    week. It was great to connect with our state representatives.

                    Council Member Brooke D'Sousa provided an update from the Council of Governments
                    (COG). Winter overflow shelters will be available during Code Blue days which are days
                    that are 18 degrees or colder. The Point in Time Count will take place next week.
                    Volunteers are welcome. She is currently working with COG to determine representation
                    on committee assignments for the current Council liaison and the alternate liaison
                    position. She will provide further clarification to the Council as she gets the details.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. The next
                    production, Emma, opens next weekend. The Council is invited to attend. She provided
                    an update from the Legislative Policy Committee (LPC). Some of the issues discussed
                    include short term rentals, the homeless mitigation funding, accessory dwelling units,
                    and housing bills. The Governor's goal is now to build 150 thousand new homes. She
                    attended the Utah Taxpayers Association pre-legislative breakfast and spoke about the
                    presentation which included information about the economy and economic uncertainties,
                    housing affordability, inflation, and credit card delinquencies. Utah wage growth led the
                    nation and the state's poverty rate is the lowest in the nation. She also acknowledged
                    and thanked the Fire crew from station 32 who came to her home recently and helped her
                    with her fire alarms.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor Zoltanski thanked the community for attending the pre-legislative town hall event.
                    She recently attended a tour of the MVP shelter. She provided an update from the
                    Wasatch Front Regional Committee and spoke about the phased approach to
                    transportation in Little Cottonwood Canyon. She also spoke about the importance of fire
                    readiness in Sandy City and the need to be proactive in our approach.
