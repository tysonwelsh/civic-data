                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-67

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A PURCHASE ORDER BE ISSUED TO READING
TRUCK DBA SEMI SERVICE TO FURNISH AND INSTALL TWO SERVICE BODIES
                    ONTO EXISTING VEHICLES

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, the Parks Department Manager would like service bodies added to existing
trucks; and

      WHEREAS, there is enough money left in the budget to add these service bodies.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
      1. The City Council authorizes a Purchase Order be issued to Reading Truck dba Semi
         Service to have them furnish and install two service bodies onto existing vehicles.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 16th day of
July 2024 by the following vote:
