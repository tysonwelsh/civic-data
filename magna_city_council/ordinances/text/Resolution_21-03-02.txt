MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
Resolution No. 21-03-02 Date: MARCH 23, 2021
A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE 2021 MAGNA GENERAL PLAN
RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township was incorporated and commenced
operations on January 2, 2017 pursuant to State Law; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated (““UCA”) §10-2a-414, Magna
inherited its Code of Ordinances, and with it the Magna General Plan from Salt Lake
County as a part of its incorporation process; and

WHEREAS, the Magna General Plan inherited by the municipality was over 10
years old; and

WHEREAS, as the inherited Magna General Plan had not been updated in over 10
years, it was determined by the Council Magna needed a new Magna General Plan; and

WHEREAS, the 2019 Magna General Plan Steering Committee was created to
review the old General Plan, look into new information and data regarding Magna as it
pertains to land use, transportation, economic development, historic preservation, and
moderate income housing, and to make recommendations to the Planning Commission and
Council on the new General Plan; and

WHEREAS, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District, in support of the
Magna General Plan process, hired GSBS Consulting (the “Consultant”) to provide data
collection and information to for the steering committee, planning commission and council
to consider in creating the new General Plan; and

WHEREAS, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, all work conducted on the Magna
General Plan and its chapters since March 13, 2020 were required to be done via electronic
meetings and through electronic citizen engagement portals; and

WHEREAS, the consultant created www.thefutureofmagna.org (website) where
residents could provide comments and feedback on land use, transportation, and other
components in the plan; and

WHEREAS, the website was able to capture information, comments, and feedback
to a number of community stakeholders and hundreds of residents regarding the plan; and

WHEREAS, from the provided feedback, the steering committee, consultants and
planning staff were able to develop the 2021 Magna General Plan for consideration by the
Planning Commission and Council; and

WHEREAS, on February 11, 2021 the Planning Commission conducted a public
hearing on the 2021 Magna General Plan; and

WHEREAS, after the public hearing, the planning commission proposed a number
of amendments and recommended the Magna General Plan to the Council for its
consideration and possible adoption; and

WHEREAS, on March 9, 2021 the Council set an additional public hearing to be
held on March 23, 2021 regarding the 2021 Magna General Plan; and
