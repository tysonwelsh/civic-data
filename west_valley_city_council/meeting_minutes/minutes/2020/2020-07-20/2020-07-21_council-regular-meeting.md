       RESOLUTION 20-106: AUTHORIZE WEST VALLEY CITY TO ENTER INTO A
       MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH THE UTAH ATTORNEY
       GENERALS OFFICE CRIMES AGAINST STATEWIDE ECONOMY (CASE)
       TASKFORCE
       Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-106 that would authorize West Valley
       City to enter into a Memorandum of Understanding with the Utah Attorney General’s
       Office Crimes Against Statewide Economy (Case) Taskforce.

       Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
       follows:

              The Utah Attorney General’s Office has created CASE task force to coordinate
              local law enforcement resources in the investigation of property and economic
              crimes and their associated organizations and networks. The Police Department
              seeks to assign one (1) investigator to this position. The Utah Attorney General’s
              Office agrees to compensate WVC for the cost of that employee’s salary and
              benefits.

       This task force initiates and investigates major property and economic related
       crimes, including organized crime operations, money laundering, and cases that
       requiring personnel or financial resources above what a local agency can supply.

RESOLUTION 20-107: APPROVE A BETTERMENT AGREEMENT BETWEEN
THE CITY AND THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION FOR THE
BURIAL OF CERTAIN POWER LINES ON AND NEAR 3500 SOUTH AND 5600
WEST
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-107 that would approve a Betterment
Agreement between the City and the Utah Department of Transportation for the burial of
certain power lines on and near 3500 South and 5600 West.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       UDOT is reconstructing and widening portions of 3500 South and 5600 West as
       part of the Mountain View Corridor project. The city requested the project
       betterment from UDOT to bury overhead power on 3500 South between 5450 West
       and 6000 West, and on 5600 West between approximately 3600 South and 3400
       South.

       The power burial betterment was originally requested in December 2018, when the
       city and UDOT executed Cooperative Agreement No. 1 for the construction of
       Mountain View Corridor from 4100 South to SR-201. The work has since been

       for the city-requested betterment work. Per the agreement, payment is due October

RESOLUTION 20-108: APPROVE THE PURCHASE OF STREETLIGHTS FROM
MOUNTAIN STATES LIGHTING FOR LOCATIONS IMPACTED BY THE
MOUNTAIN VIEW CORRIDOR
Mayor Bigelow discussed proposed Resolution 20-108 that would approve the purchase of
streetlights from Mountain States Lighting for locations impacted by the Mountain View
Corridor.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       UDOT’s work on Mountain View Corridor is impacting various streets within West
       Valley City. The city has requested a betterment for UDOT’s contractor to install
       streetlights on these streets. The city has opted to purchase and supply lights
       directly to the contractor for installation to take advantage of a discount offered
       from the city’s supplier, Mountain States Lighting.

       This resolution authorizes the city to spend up to $375,000 to purchase 131
       streetlights for use on the following streets: 3500 South, 5600 West, SR-201 South
       Frontage Road, Cilma Drive, Masters Drive, 3575 South, Darle Avenue, 2400
       South, Marsha Drive and Anna Caroline Drive.
