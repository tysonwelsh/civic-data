    1. ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A PROCEDURE FOR THE REVIEW AND APPROVAL OF WAIVERS OF
       PENALTIES UNDER UTAH CODE ANN. 59-2-307. ORDINANCE 2017-28

         John Ulibarri, County Assessor, stated that this ordinance allows the penalty for failure to file the signed
         statement to be waived by the Assessor’s Office when a taxpayer’s tangible personal property is exempt
         from taxation.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Ordinance 2017-28 establishing a procedure for the review and
         approval of waivers of penalties under Utah Code Ann. 59-2-307; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

    2. CONTRACT WITH NARRATIV1 HOLDINGS, LLC FOR SOFTWARE THAT WILL INCREASE OPERATING
       EFFICIENCIES WITHIN THE WEBER COUNTY ASSESSOR'S OFFICE.

         John Ulibarri, County Assessor, gave an overview of this report writing program that integrates Excel and
         Microsoft Word to create a more compliant appraisal report in less time and compile commercial sales
         and lease information in a more accessible format. This is a one year contract with an automatic renewal
         clause at the end of each year. The county can terminate the contract by giving 60 days prior notice.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Narrativ1 Holdings, LLC for software that will
         increase operating efficiencies within the County Assessor's Office; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
    3. FINAL APPROVAL OF HENRY FLATS SUBDIVISION, AND ACCEPTANCE OF FINANCIAL GUARANTEE AND
       RELATED CONTRACTS.

       THE WOLF CREEK DRIVE PATHWAY (WOLF CREEK TO EDEN).

         Creek Beach) and the Wolf Creek Drive Pathway (Wolf Creek to Eden); Commissioner Gibson seconded.

         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

    7. CONTRACT WITH NUERA FOR SAFETY COORDINATOR SERVICE FOR THE WEBER COUNTY SOLID
       WASTE DEPARTMENT.
         Kevin McLeod, of County Property Management, noted that NUERA is involved with a number of the
         northern Utah waste facilities. This is to provide a safety specialist for the northern transfer stations and
         waste facilities to reduce liability and increase safety certifications, etc. Mr. McLeod addressed Chair
         Ebert’s questions stating that this is for four facilities and costs will be split among the agencies.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with NUERA for Safety Coordinator Service for
         the Weber County Solid Waste Department; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

    8. CONTRACT WITH ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER TO REALLOCATE EXISTING TRANSMISSION AND
       DISTRIBUTION FACILITIES.

         Kevin McLeod, of County Property Management, stated that this is for Rocky Mountain to move the
         power poles so that the Sheriff’s Office can construct the road to the Sheriff’s Complex and the new
         Juvenile Detention facility. The county has done some work thereby reducing the contract amount from
         almost $200,000 to about $41,000.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with Rocky Mountain Power to reallocate existing
         transmission and distribution facilities; Commissioner Gibson seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

    2. PUBLIC HEARING ON PROPOSED AGRICULTURE PROTECTION AREA (APA) - SUBMITTED BY
       CHARLES CHAMBERLAIN AND ROBERT CHAMBERLAIN, PARCELS 18-048-0025 & 18-048-0012.
         Fátima Fernelius, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, gave a comprehensive overview of this process and of
         State Code and explained the purpose of APAs. Ms. Fernelius stated that the statutory requirements for
         publishing, posting, noticing and mailing had been followed. The public had a 15-day period to submit
         written objections or requests to modify the proposal and none were received. The Western Weber
         Planning Commission and the APA Advisory Board recommended approval. She stated that today the
         Commission would take oral and written comments from the public and they could approve, modify and
         approve, or reject the proposal. Subsequent to approval, a document will be recorded with the County
         Recorder’s Office, and notice filed with the Planning Commission, the Commissioner of Agriculture and
         Food, the County Planning Division and the Sheriff’s Office. The applicant, Charles Chamberlain, was
         present and said that he had no additional comments.
    3. Public comments:
         Karmen Sanone, of unincorporated Weber County, expressed support of this Agriculture Protection Area
         (APA); she said that Mr. Chamberlain has a beautiful place and she would like to see him continue to use
         it in the manner he chooses.
         Gaye Carter, of unincorporated Weber County, said that her land borders Ms. Sanone’s land. She also
         submitted a proposal for an APA. Their land was part of the original Bailey Farm, before the existence of
         North Ogden City. She said that the land does not make enough money to pay the property taxes but they
         are willing to pay the difference to keep it a farm. Ms. Carter’s public hearing will be held next week.
         They do not need to be present again next week.
         Karmen Sanone, of unincorporated Weber County, expressed support of the Agriculture Protection Area
         for Ms. Carter stating that she would like to see them continue to use their land in the manner that they
         want. She stated that her farm and Ms. Carter’s farm are the last two remnants of the very extensive
         Bailey Farm. One of the parcels was recognized at the County Fair as a century farm; it has been in
         continuous family ownership for over 100 years.
    4.
         Commissioner Gibson moved to adjourn the public hearing and reconvene the public meeting;
         Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye

    5. ACTION ON PUBLIC HEARING:
       H.2.-AGRICULTURE PROTECTION AREA (APA) SUBMITTED BY CHARLES CHAMBERLAIN AND
