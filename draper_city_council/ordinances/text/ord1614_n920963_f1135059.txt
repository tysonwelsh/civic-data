City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On June 4, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1614, amending the
official Zoning map of Draper City and approving a Development Agreement for
property located approximately at 285 W. River Chapel Road. The complete ordinance
is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 13th day of June 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
