OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance.

DISCUSSION REGARDING THE FISCAL YEAR 2021-2022 BUDGET:
     City Manager Adam Lenhard explained there is an enormous amount of work done
     on the City’s budget. He commended Budget and Financial Planning Manager
     Deanna Brklacich and Assistant Budget Manager Chad Moultrie for the fantastic job
     they do.

      Budget and Financial Planning Manager Deanna Brklacich presented a PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: Organization chart; FY 2021 $305.4
      million budget; FY2021 General Fund budget $70.4 Million. Building blocks; Annual
      challenges; General Fund revenues 4-year comparison; 2021 General Fund budgeted
      revenues; Sales tax revenues; General Fund’s 1.00% local option; Sales tax
      distribution diagram; 7-year comparisons of distribution formulas for St. George
      sales tax FY 2014 to FY2020; Sales taxes are analyzed monthly; Analysis of all
      location options sales tax remittance based on SIC code; Sales Tax remittance by
      industry FY2010 (Great Recession) and FY2017 to FY2021 months to Dec. In
      FY2021); Property taxes; Positives; Property tax revenues projected by the Certified
      Tax Rate (CTR) compared to actual; Challenges; 2020 property tax percentage of
      total levy rate by taxing agency and by budget type; Property tax rate distribution
      comparison (St. George District) tax year 2011 to 2020; Comparison of property tax
      rates for Washington County; Comparison of property tax to top 10 cities in Utah;
      Number of building permits by category comparison for January through December
      2013 to 2020; Value (in $millions) of building permits issued by category –
      Comparison of January through December 2013 to 2020; Charges for services;
      Consolidated budget by expense type; General Fund expense budget FY2021 by
      departments and by division; The General fund budget – FY2021 expenditures by
      expense type and FY2021 expenditures by service budget; General Fund
      expenditures – percent by service type; General Fund expenditures – amount by
      service type; Goal of General Fund expenditure allocations; General Fund –
      percentage distribution of personnel, materials & supplies, capital outlays, and
      transfers by expense percentage for Fiscal Years 2013 to 2021; Personnel expenses;
      General Fund – salaries & benefits history; 65 new positions requested for FY2022;
      Balancing the challenge of funding capital outlay requests in the General Fund
      FY2020; and Method of prioritizing capital outlay requests.

      Administrative Services Director Trevor Coombs presented a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Finance Division; Utility Division; What we’ve
      accomplished in FY20-FY21 – Utilities; Currently working on FY21/FY22; 311
      software – Qscend Technology, Inc; New 311 app; and 311 self portal website.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman presented a PowerPoint presentation covering the
      following topics: Legal Services Mission Statement; Legal Services budget; Number
      of attorneys vs. other cities; Criminal cases filed 2020; Criminal cases filed 2012-
      2020; Civil matters; Claims management; Liability and property insurance structure;
      Insurance coverages/premiums; Number of liability claims 2014-2020; Liability
      claims paid 2014-2020; Worker’s Comp claims by number 2014-2019; Worker’s
      comp claims paid 2013-2020; Challenges; and 2022 goals.

      Human Resources Director Sharon Hokanson presented a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Salary ranges; Range progression; Full-time personnel
      statistics as of February 1, 2021; All full-time employee distribution of comp ratios;
      Years of service; Medical insurance; Some HR stats since last year; and Budget
      considerations.

      Communications and Marketing Director David Cordero presented a PowerPoint
      presentation covering the following topics: Monthly newsletter starts in March;
      Elevate your game; Needs; and Remembering 9/11.

      Community Development Director John Willis presented a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Accomplishments overview; Building permits; Building
      division; Business Licensing; Code Enforcement; Planning applications; Planning and
      Zoning; Digital transition; and Goals. A brief discussion took place regarding issues
      with illegal short-term rentals.

      Mayor Randall called for a 15-minute recess.

      Economic Development and Housing Director Shirlayne Quayle presented a
      PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics: Focus areas & projects;
      Initiatives; Memberships/sponsorships; and Refresh downtown.

      Fire Chief Stoker presented a PowerPoint presentation covering the following topics:
      Call data; Station 6; Station 9; Future land and stations; Staffing; Firefighter levels;
      Technician lever certifications for firefighter level advancement; Current staffing full-
      time; Safer Grant; Proposed staffing budget changes; Special operations; and What’s
      Happening Ahead?

      Police Chief Kyle Whitehead presented a PowerPoint presentation covering the
      following topics: Fleet; Aging tasers; Body cameras; Technology; Staffing; Budgeted
      vs. actual dispatch overtime; and Property room.

      The Council took a break for lunch.

      Roadway centerline miles; Suntran division; Public Works capital projects; and
      Rectangular rapid flash beacon (RRFG).

      Support Services Director Marc Mortensen provided a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Technology organization chart; City of St. George
      Covid-19 situation center; Flood damage; Open data hub; Upcoming GIS projects;
      Project T.H.O.R.; Internet service; New employee requests; Network improvement
      requests; Security requests; Update workbenches in tech room; New van for techs;

      to analyze play and out-of-play areas on golf courses to reduce water use; Plan to
      reduce water use at golf courses – goal 25% savings in two years; Tall fescue mix in

      Facilities; Dixie SunBowl south plans; Red Hills Master Plan; St. George City Hall
      remodel & expansion; Police Headquarters remodel & expansion; and City Hall/Police
      HQ remodel & expansion.

      Energy Services Director Laurie Mangum provided a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Employees that will be retiring in the next couple of
      years; Photos of the substations that have been expanded over the past few years;
      Photos of the crews replacing poles due to a fire; 138kv transmission line; What’s
      next? Replacing existing assets 30+ years old; Winter summer curve; Filing the
      energy needs in an average day; Summer curve; The facts; and Personnel requests.

      Water Services Director Scott Taylor presented a PowerPoint presentation covering
      the following topics: 2020 Water Distribution & Irrigation Division summary; 2020
      Wastewater Collection summary; 2020 SGRWRF Division summary; Average WWTP
      influent (mgd); Water conservation; What # is used to track water conservation
      efforts?; SGRWRF expansion; Gunlock Water Treatment Plant; Secondary irrigation
      requirement; City Creek to Ledges pipeline; Ledges reuse line; Entrada/Ledges
      irrigation line; Graveyard Wash Reservoir – BLM permit; 48” outfall line replacement
      ROW acquisition; 24” springs outfall line replacement ROW acquisition; Irrigation
      system improvements; Water distribution system improvements; Wastewater
      collections system improvements; Revised lead & copper rule: EPA; and Water loss
      audit. He outlined the personnel he is requesting.

      Leisure Services Director Shane McAffee presented a PowerPoint presentation
      covering the following topics: Divisions; FY2021 Arts & Events – Successes and
      challenges; Parks – Successes and challenges; Recreation – Sports & Facilities –
      Successes & challenges; Park Planning – Upcoming; Dixie Center; Challenges &
      opportunities; and Project & focus areas.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard thanked staff for their presentations. If the Council
      feels important things were missed, he asked that they let him know.
