                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO 20- 26


     A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A REIMBURSEMENT
                    AGREEMNT WITH LUKE PETERSON

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has engaged in a roadway improvement project in the
vicinity of 11800 South and 4000 West; and

       WHEREAS, said project requires the restoration of property which fronts the same; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has determined that restoration should be conducted by
execution of a reimbursement agreement with the property’s owner, Luke Peterson; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that execution of the
enclosed Reimbursement Agreement will enable the city to complete the aforesaid roadway
improvement project;


       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

1.     The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to to execute the enclosed
       Reimbursemnet Agreement with Luke Peterson, upon verification that the agreement has
       been approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.
2.     This Resolution shall take effect immediately.
