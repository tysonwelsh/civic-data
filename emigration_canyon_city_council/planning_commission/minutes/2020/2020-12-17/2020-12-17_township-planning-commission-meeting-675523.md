Hearings began at – 8:37 a.m.
(Continued from 11/19/2020) - Emigration Canyon Metro Township is considering adopting the proposed
2020 Emigration Canyon Metro Township General Plan, which plans for the areas within Emigration
Canyon Metro Township. The 2020 General Plan is Emigration Canyon’s first comprehensive plan since
incorporation as a metro township in 2017. The Plan synthesizes data and public feedback to describe
current conditions, envision Emigration Canyon’s future, and guide Emigration Canyon from present
conditions toward that desired vision. This General Plan meets the required State laws for general planning,
including elements on Land Use and Transportation. It also includes the following supplemental elements:
Introduction, Economic Development, Environment, Resilience and Infrastructure, and Community Work
Program. Planner: Kate Davies (Motion/Voting)
Please visit: https://ec-lrp-hub-gslmsd.hub.arcgis.com/ to view a copy of the 2020 Emigration Canyon
Metro Township General Plan.
The Planning Commission will act on the proposed general plan after taking comments from the public
during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s rules
of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three minutes
per person.
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Range Planner Kate Davies provided an overview of
the General Plan and advised the Emigration Canyon Metro Township is required to adopt a general plan
which requires Land Use and Transportation Elements.
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the feedback of the general plan being too long
and repetitive, and too much context/background. Community input has not been received since the draft
document put up on the website November 4th.
Opinions vs. Facts and having additional conversations with the Council, input and feedback being
opinioned and value based.
Development of the document, water issues are pervasive and have been addressed, and make sure the issue
has not expanded beyond land use, be cautious and careful in discussing. ECID’s review and concurrence
with the language.
Guidance on addressing working groups and the process, and should the Council dictate on putting groups
together on efforts, appointment, timelines, and progression.
Character Areas neighborhood descriptions vs future character areas. Neutralize descriptions, diversity
and preservation, rewrite character areas and put in appendix.
Ms. Stokman said the character areas discussed lack of community and not having opportunities to connect.
She liked the character areas because of diversity and identity of various areas in the canyon, expression
of who you are and asked citizens in the areas who their community is and describe.
Transportation language revision over the table and associated description of work.
