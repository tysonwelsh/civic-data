the concept plan from the Commissioners.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.


There were online comments that Commissioner Nielsen addressed regarding parks, open space,
trail systems, traffic, and parking.

        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.3) Consideration of Smartlink’s request for approval of the AT&T Lehi Riverwalk
Stealth Tower located at 2700 West Parkside Drive in Olympic Park.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Jason Hadley with Smartlink, was present. He explained there are trying to get
coverage near Primary Children's Hospital near 2100 North. The benefit of the height will help
future carriers with their service and co-locations. The next closest tower will be next to the
Community Credit Union near I-15 and near Redwood Road closer to Saratoga Springs. This
tower will be a capacity and coverage site. Based on the information it will be better at 90 feet
rather than 60 feet for coverage. The applicant stated this is the best location they can find that
will bring coverage. The Commissioners encouraged improvements for the tower.

Commissioner Nielsen clarified this is not a public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to Smartlink’s request for approval of the
        AT&T Lehi Riverwalk Stealth Tower located at 2700 West Parkside Drive in
        Olympic Park; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
        Commissioner Newall seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Britton, no;
        Commissioner Carlson; no; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed 4 – 3.

3.6) Consideration of Salt Developments’ request for approval of the Sanctuary Site Plan
including 519 apartment units located at 2601 West Slipstream Avenue in a TOD zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Nicholas Stock of Salt Development, was present. He stated they are making their
best efforts to meet the landscaping requirements. They would like to invest in mature vegetation.
The stucco matches the architectural design they are trying to meet with quality. The Commission

roundabout. They would like to start building by August and would expect a three year buildout.

3.7) Public hearing and recommendation of Thanksgiving Station LLC’s request for review
of a Development Code Amendment to Chapter 28, Resort Community Zone, amending the
percentages of resort, residential, and commercial uses with the zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, John Bankhead with Gardner Company, was present and addressed the
Commission. He stated the code will revise the uses to help with the growth and development
and the definition of a resort community.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing and acknowledged online comments from Bill
Hereth.


Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to send a positive recommendation of
        Thanksgiving Station LLC’s request for review of a Development Code Amendment
        to Chapter 28, Resort Community Zone, amending the percentages of resort,
        residential, and commercial uses with the zone; including all DRC comments,
        additionally recommending that 28-0-70A uses of the word “should” must be changed to
        “shall”, clarifying the height of roofs listed at 38 feet was a three foot increase from 35
        feet, and under 28-0-30B the Commission requests the Council to define whether
        undeveloped land counts in the definition, the findings are consistent with the direction of
        the City where a viable TOD is being developed in this area under the current General
        Plan. Commissioner Eyre seconded. All in favor.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Britton, yes;
        Commissioner Carlson; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.8) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of Development
Code amendments pertaining to the Mixed-Use zone: Chapter 5, Chapter 15, Table of Uses,
and Table of Bulk and Intensity.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing item and acknowledged the online comment.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing item.

Commissioner Carlson asked about mixed use, multiple kinds of use, parking, landscape and
useable open space, a better definition for grocers and commercial use. Commissioner Nielsen
expressed that he felt more flexibility should be allowed in more areas for mixed use besides
TOD zones. Mike West stated the General Plan will be changing over time too. Commissioner
Newall liked setting open space requirement to allow for exceptions.

        Motion: Commissioner Carlson moved to table Lehi City’s request for review of
        Development Code amendments pertaining to the Mixed-Use zone: Chapter 5,
        Chapter 15, Table of Uses, and Table of Bulk and Intensity; to come back to the
        Commission within the next 60 days to evaluate and clarify as discussed. Commissioner
        Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Britton,
        yes; Commissioner Carlson; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
