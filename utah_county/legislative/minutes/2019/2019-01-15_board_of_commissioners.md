2. DISCUSSION ON CHANGING UTAH COUNTY'S FORM OF GOVERNMENT.
- Bill Lee, Utah County Commissioner —

UTAH STATE SENATOR JAKE ANDEREGG: INFORMS THE COMMISSION THAT A
PETITION TO CHANGE THE FORM OF GOVERNMENT HAS BEEN SUBMITTED.
SPECIFICALLY, ONE TO CREATING A FORM OF GOVERNMENT WITH A
MAYOR/COUNCIL SYSTEM. THERE ARE PRO’S AND CON’S TO EACH FORM OF
GOVERNMENT. REQUESTS THE COMMISSION TO CRATE AN AD HOC COMMITTEE TO
STUDY THE PRO’S AND CON’S OF NEW FORMS OF GOVERNMENT.

OPPOSES A STRONG MAYORAL SYSTEM OF GOVERNMENT. HIGHLIGHTS THIS
CONCERN AS A REASON FOR FORMING A COMMITTEE TO STUDY POSSIBLE NEW
FORMS OF GOVERNMENT AND TO HAVE THAT COMMITTEE SUBMIT THEIR
RECOMMENDATION(S) TO THE COMMISSION.

SENATOR ANDEREGG RECOMMENDS A 7-MEMBER COMMITTEE. A REPRESENTATIVE
FROM THE SENATE, HOUSE, MAYORS, CHAMBER, PUBLIC EDUCATION, AND SOMEONE
TO REPRESENT THE COUNTY ADMINISTRATION.

COMMISSIONER LEE INFORMS SENATOR ANDEREGG THAT NO OFFICIAL ACTIONS ARE
TAKEN DURING WORK SESSIONS AND THAT ANY ACTION WOULD NEED TO BE DONE
THROUGH THE PROPER FORM BY BEING PLACED ON THE AGENDA DUE TO THE
LEGISLATIVE MANNER,

COMISSIONER LEE EXPRESSES HIS INTENT TO HAVE THIS BE AN OPEN AND PUBLIC
PROCESS.

COMMISSIONER IVIE EXPRESSES HIS SUPPORT FOR A NEW FORM OF GOVERNMENT.
STATES THAT THERE IS TOO MUCH POWER CONSOLIDATED INTO THE COMMISSION
AND THE EXECUTIVE/LEGISLATIVE POWER NEEDS TO BE SEPARATED.

UTAH COUNTY BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS
MINUTES — January 15, 2019

COMMISSIONER AINGE EXPRESSES HIS SUPPORT FOR A NEW FORM OF GOVERNMENT.
EXTENDS APPRECIATION TO SENATOR ANDEREGG FOR SPEAKING ON THIS ISSUE.
RESTATES THAT THE PUBLIC NEED TO HAVE INPUT AND SUPPORT FOR ANY
RECOMMENDATION FOR A NEW FORM OF GOVERNMENT. WANTS TO HAVE ANY
COMMITTEE THAT INVESTIGATES A NEW FORM OF GOVERNMENT TO HAVE STRONG
DEBATE AMONGST COMPETING FORMS OF GOVERNMENT. EXPRESSES CONCERN
OVER THE INCREASED EXPENSES THAT WOULD COME WITH A NEW FORM OF
GOVERNMENT. STATES THAT A NEW FORM OF GOVERNMENT SHOULD NOT BE
REACTIONARY BUT SHOULD BE THOROUGHLY STUDIED.

SENATOR ANDEREGG ASKS FOR AN ACTION ITEM TO BE PLACED ON A COMMISSION
