                              CITY OF BLUFFDALE,UTAH

                               ORDINANCE NO.2025-12


AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 11.20.020,11.90.060.020,11.90.060.110,
I.110.100.050,AND 11.350.020 OF THE BLUFFDALE CITY CODE TO CREATE A
DEFINITION FOR MINOR AUTOMOTIVE SERVICES,TO ALLOW MINOR AUTOMOTIVE
SERVICES IN THE DESTINATION RETAIL(DR)ZONE,TO ALLOW TATTOO
ESTABLISHMENTS IN CERTAIN COMMERCIAL ZONES,AND TO MODIFY THE BRINGHURST
STATION COMMERCIAL AREA TO ALLOW NON-RETAIL BASED BUSINESSES IN CERTAIN
AREAS.


WHEREAS,the Biuffdaie City Council held multiple work sessions in early 2025,and
directed Staff to propose amendments to the Biuffdaie City Code to allow Minor
Automotive Services in the Destination Retail Zone,to allow tattoo establishments in more
commercial zones,and to allow non-retail based businesses within certain spaces of the
Bringhurst Station commercial area;

WHEREAS,the City proposes amendments to Section 11.160.140 of the Biuffdaie City Code
to allow fences up to six feet(6') in height in front yards of residential lots that have
undergone a change in configuration following approval of a subdivision;

WHEREAS,a notice of public hearing for the proposed amendments was provided
pursuant to the Biuffdaie City Code and Utah State Code;

WHEREAS,on April 16,2024,the Planning Commission conducted a public hearing and
unanimously forwarded a positive recommendation of the proposed Text Amendment to
the City Council.

NOW,THEREFORE,BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BLUFFDALE,
UTAH AS FOLLOWS:


       Section 1.   Adoption. Ordinance 2025-12 is hereby adopted,amending Sections
II.20.020,11.90.060.020,11.90.060.110,1.110.100.050, and 11.350.020 of the Biuffdaie City
Code as shown in Exhibit A.


       Section 2. Amendment of Conflicting Ordinances. If any ordinances,
resolutions, policies, or zoning maps of the City heretofore adopted are inconsistent
herewith they are hereby amended to comply with the provisions hereof. If they cannot be
amended to comply with the provisions hereof,they are hereby repealed.

       Section 3. Severability. If any section, part,or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable,such provision shall be deemed a separate,distinct, and
independent provision, and such Invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
portion of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance
shall be severable.


      Section 4.     Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED: May 14,2025
