Agenda Review

Chair Choberka and members of Council staff reviewed the items listed on the agenda for the City Council meeting
scheduled to begin at 6:00 p.m. For the Council agenda, there was a brief focus on the MAKE Ogden Downtown Zoning
Ordinance and Zoning Map amendments and the Administration’s desired timing of the Council’s action on those items. For the
RDA agenda, there was a discussion about the item relating to a Developer Selection Process and the manner in which a database
will be created for purposes of notifying developers who may be interested in participating in RDA projects.

Initiative Request — Rezone of Grandview Property from R-3 to R-2

Council Deputy Director Symes explained Council member Hyer has submitted a Council Initiative form to rezone
property at 3875 Jackson Avenue (former Grandview Elementary School site) from R-3 to R-2. The subject property is one of four
properties Ogden School District deemed surplus in September 2022. In March 2023, Ogden City waived its exclusive right to
purchase the property. As such, the property may now be sold by the district on the open market. Council members expressed
concern with the zoning of the property and the potential impact of future development under the R-3 zoning. The Initiative would
request a recommendation from the Planning Commission on the requested rezone.

Council member Hyer discussed the history of the City’s consideration of purchasing the subject property, which has led
to discussions about development options for the property and the impact that a high-density project with taller building heights
could have on the surrounding properties and their future development potential. He noted that he would like the Council to
consider downzoning the property to respect and honor the feelings of the neighborhood.

Council member White stated she supports the downzoning and noted that even with a change in zone, the property can
still be developed in a way that provides great opportunities for future residents with access to services.

Council member Nadolski stated he also supports the downzoning proposal and noted that this proposal made him
wonder if there are other undeveloped properties in the City that should be examined to determine if their current zoning or | )
General Plan land use designation does not reflect surrounding development.

Vice Chair Richey stated he feels the zone changes is appropriate and will provide for meaningful development of the
area that reflects surrounding development.
