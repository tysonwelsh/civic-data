City Council Meeting Minutes March 15, 2022

Citizen Comments
Council Chair Houseman invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comments:
Ms. Dawn Sidwell expressed her concern about residents making the 5:15 pm public
comment. Many of the residents she spoke with are not able to make the earlier public
comment time. She does not believe at-large council members are able to represent
citizens as well as a council members from a specific district and is supportive of
increasing the number of districts during the current redistricting process before the
Council.
Ms. Sharon Jensen appreciates the Council's help with Historic Sandy improvements and
with local businesses. She also feels the 5:15 public comment time is too early and is
difficult for the public to participate in. Ms. Jensen expressed support for map 4.1 for the
redistricting options.
Mr. Lynn Pace introduced Ms. Joelle Kesler, who recently joined the Legal Department
as a Senior Civil Attorney. Ms. Kesler spoke about her background. Council Members
welcomed Ms. Kessler to Sandy City.
Ms. Sandra Haak wanted to make sure her emailed comments made it to the Council
Members. They nodded in acknowledgement.
Public Comment closed.

Memorial Monument Committee presenting the Council with a proposal to
create a Gold Star Families Memorial Monument in Sandy City
Sponsors: Houseman

Mr. Paul Swenson with Colonial Flag, spoke about the Gold Star Families Memorial
Monument. He introduced the committee members and showed a video regarding the
Gold Star Monument.
Ms. Kim Olson, a Gold Star mother, spoke on behalf on the Gold Star Family Monument.
She spoke about her son and his commitment and service to his country as a United
States Marine.

spoke about how meaningful the monument would be to Gold Star families and the entire
