RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Resolution #24-29 approving the 2024 Fraud Risk Assessment

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Utah State Auditor’s Office has implemented a Fraud Risk Assessment that they would like
reviewed, completed and presented to the City Council. This assessment is designed to help measure
and reduce the risk of undetected fraud, abuse, and noncompliance in local governments. This
assessment will be completed on an annual basis with this report for 2024.

                                         RESOLUTION NO. 24-29

        A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE 2024 FRAUD RISK ASSESSMENT FOR THE
        CITY OF DRAPER.


        WHEREAS, the City is required to provide an annual Fraud Risk Assessment report to the Utah
State Auditors; and

        WHEREAS, the purpose of this assessment, in part, is to fulfill the requirement set forth by the
Utah State Auditor’s Office; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council supports internal controls, policies, practices and processes that
ensure the operations in the City are performed effectively and efficiently; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council supports and is dedicated to providing frameworks and guidance on
risk management, internal controls, and fraud deterrence.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1.      Approval. The City Council hereby adopts the Annual Fraud Risk Assessment for
2024 as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference. The City Manager and
Finance Director are authorized to sign the Fraud Risk Assessment and file it with the Utah State Auditor’s
Office.

         Section 2.        Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held invalid, or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution, and
all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3.        Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its
passage.
