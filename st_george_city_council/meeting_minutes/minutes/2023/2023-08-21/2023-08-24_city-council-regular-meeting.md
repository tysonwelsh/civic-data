DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of Ordinance No. 2023-011 approving a development
     agreement on approximately 1.0 acres located at 3681 South Mulberry Drive
     for the purpose of allowing the applicant to build a garage in Open Space
     zone in exchange for a sewer easement and drainage easement on the
     applicant’s property to be granted to the City of St. George to be known as
     Eric Martin Development Agreement. Case No. 2023-DA-005

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This development agreement addresses the
      applicant wanting to build a garage/barn in the rear of his property which is zoned
      OS (Open Space). In the open space zone accessory structures are required to be a
      minimum of 100’ feet from any dwelling. For the applicant to build his non-habitable
      garage/barn and comply with the setback from any dwelling, the proposed building
      would be located over the sewer easement. The applicant is working with the City to
      give a sewer easement and drainage easement (for access and maintenance of the
      Virgin River) in order to be able to build the garage/barn within the required
      setbacks. At their meeting held on July 11, 2023 the Planning Commission held a
      public hearing and recommended approval with a vote of 6-0 with no conditions.

      Link to presentation from Planner Mike Hadley, including discussion between City
      Manager John Willis, the City Council, Deputy City Attorney Jami Brackin, and Mayor
      Pro Tem Hughes: 00:11:45

ZONE CHANGE/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of Ordinance No. 2023-016 changing the zone from C-2
     (Commercial) to PD-C (Planned Development Commercial) on approximately
     2.83 acres located at 3270 East Flowers Way to build Fire Station #10 on
     the property. Case No. 2023-ZC-010

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The property is currently zoned C-2. Fire
      Station #10 is proposing a training tower and storage facility on the site. The C-2
      zoning allows for a height of 50’. The proposed training tower located at this site is
      60’ in height. The purpose for the 60’ height is to train and certify firefighters.
      Changing the zone to PD-C will allow the Planning Commission and City Council to
      review a height greater than 50’. At their meeting on August 8, 2023, the Planning
      Commission held a public hearing and recommended approval with a vote of 6-0 and
      no conditions.

      Link to presentation from Planner Mike Hadley, including discussion between Mayor
