   3. Loveless Estates PUD Subdivision Plat B
               a) Staﬀ Presentation:
                           The City Planner, Mrs. Talbot explained that this is the second phase, and
                       ﬁnal plat, of the Loveless Estates PUD Subdivision. Phase 1 (Plat A) has already
                       been recorded and has begun to build the approved townhomes. This
                       development is located in an R-2 PUD overlay zone, and located at
                       approximately 9th North and 2nd West, and will include 42 of the total 75 lots in
                       the subdivision. There are 14 triple townhome banks in this phase of the PUD,
                       and each will be in separate ownership following the format seen in Plat A. DRC
                       has recommended that a utility access easement be shown on the plat for the
                       purpose of servicing the sewer manhole on the Southwest corner of the
                       property. In addition, a temporary power easement will also need to be shown
                       on the plat to be located on the Northwest Corner of Lot 39. This easement
                       needs to be in place for approximately three years while upgrades to the power
                       infrastructure in that area are being carried out. Once the work has been
                       completed the temporary easement will be released. The developer
                       understands that they have the available option to enter into a reimbursement
                       agreement with the city, so that any future development that beneﬁts from the
                       use of the infrastructure installed for this project will be required to pay the
                       proportionate beneﬁt at time of development and the city will collect these
                       reimbursement funds and pay the developer the portion that will be due back to
                       them. Staﬀ recommends that the development be approved with the
                       contingency of compliance with the accompanying DRC recommendations and
                       notes.

                b) Commission Discussion:
                      Alternate Commissioner Terry Cook raised the question as to who is responsible
                      for the upkeep of the open space shown on the plat map. Shauna explained
                      that, as a PUD (planned unit development), this area is required to have a HOA
                      (homeowner’s association), and record CC&Rs (covenants, conditions &
                      restrictions) to care for all commonly owned areas, including the open space.
                      Chairman Peterson called for amotion regarding the acceptance of this
                      development.

                c) Action Item:
                      • Motion: Chairman Cory Thomson moved to recommend to the city council
                         that they approve the Loveless Estates PUD Subdivision Plat B, contingent
                         on compliance with the accompanying DRC notes.
                      • Second: Commissioner Heather Robertson seconded the motion.
                      • Outcome: The motion was unanimously approved. MOTION PASSES.
                      • TIME: 7:10 pm

   4. Reed Ridge Amended Subdivision (McDonald’s Lot Boundary Adjustment)
                  a) Staﬀ Presentation:
                             Mrs. Talbot introduced Johanna Graham who was joining the planning
                             meeting by zoom, representing interests for McDonald’s. This
                             application is being looked at concurrently with a site plan application
                             for a proposed McDonald’s on Main Street, North of Costa Vida at
                             approximately 1403 S. Main Street. The applicants are petitioning to
                             amend the boundary line between Lot 4 and 6 of the Reed Ridge
                             Commercial Subdivision, which will eﬀectively expand the size of Lot 4,
                             and reduce the size of Lot 6. Applicants are also seeking to expand the
                             Northern boundary of the subdivision to encompass a small strip of land
                             that was inadvertently left behind when the Reed Ridge subdivision was
                             previously approved and recorded. Nephi City and Korey Wright (The
                             Wright Direction, LLC) own property to the North of this development,
                             and this subdivision would now abut those northern boundaries. The
                             purpose of this application is to meet the Utah State requirements for
                             amending subdivisions and adjusting boundaries between lots. This
                             subdivision amendment a simple subdivision boundary expansion, and
                             internal boundary adjustment; Steﬀ recommends approval.

                    b) Commission Discussion
                                 Alternate Commissioner Terry Cook raised the question of where it was
                                 located, Shauna clariﬁed that it is near the Costa Vida location.
                                 Commissioner Robertson raised a question on the entrance, and if it was
                                 too close to Main Street, and may cause traﬃc issues. Shauna clariﬁed
                                 that there is a shared access easement that all lots in the Reed Ridge
                                 subdivision can exit their site onto. She added that DRC also had
                                 concerns with the entrance to the McDonald’s site being too close to
                                 Main Street, but the site plan has been adjusted since then, and they
                                 have met the minimum city code required at this time for this project’s
                                 entrance. The entrance for Costa Vida, and the entrance for McDonald’s
                                 will be in alignment with one another.

                    c) Action Item – Motion

                        •   Motion: Chairman Ann Peterson made the motion to recommend approval
                            to the City Council of the Reed Ridge Subdivision Amended ﬁnal plat.
