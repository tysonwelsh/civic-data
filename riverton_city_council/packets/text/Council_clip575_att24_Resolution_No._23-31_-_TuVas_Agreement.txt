                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 23-31

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
  WITH TUVAS TX LLC FOR A CITY OWNED NETWORK WHICH INCLUDES
 DESIGN, CONSTRUCTION, LONG TERM EQUIPMENT ADMINISTRATION AND
                      10G INTERNET SERVICE

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, the budget approved by Council itemizes this project as the City’s fiber ring
project; and

       WHEREAS, Purchasing tried to contract for a fiber ring project and could not get it
within budget; and

       WHEREAS, Purchasing issued an RFP for an alternate solution; and

       WHEREAS, a response was submitted for a fiber and wireless solution.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with TuVas TX LLC
            for a City Owned Network which includes design, construction, long term equipment
            administration and 10G internet service.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 18th day of
April 2023 by the following vote:
