H. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVAL OF A CONTRACT WITH SCALES AND TAILS UTAH FOR REPTILE EXHIBITS AT
      THE 2021 WEBER COUNTY FAIR.
            Ashton Wilson (GSEC, Weber County Fair Manager) explained this is a new free attraction for the fair; 3-4
            shows a day, 30-45 minutes each.
            Commissioner Froerer moved approve a contract with Scales and Tails Utah for reptile exhibits at the 2021
            Weber County Fair; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
            Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
     2. APPROVAL OF AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT WITH THE OGDEN CITY
        REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY FOR TAX INCREMENT FINANCING FOR THE OGDEN
        AIRPORT PROJECT AREA.
            Commissioner Froerer explained this is a 90/10 agreement with 90% of tax increment going to Ogden with
            cap of $17M over 25 year span. Question for legal counsel: Is a resolution required?
            Chris Crockett (Attorney): Yes, when tax sharing is involved, a resolution is required.
            Chairman Harvey: Voting on a resolution today or passing interlocal today and come back with resolution
            number later?
            Mr. Crockett: Go ahead and state resolution number for vote today but we will not sign or record it until we
            receive confirmation from Ogden City that existing RDAs are dissolved.

            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve an Inter-local Agreement with the Ogden City Redevelopment
            Agency for Tax Increment Financing for the Ogden Airport project area and authorize Weber County attorneys
         to draft a Resolution that will not be recorded until Ogden City rescinds existing RDAs per prior agreements;
         Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
         Roll call vote: Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
    3. APPROVAL OF A MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH WEBER HUMAN SERVICES
       TO UTILIZE ONE CASE MANAGER AS A LIAISON BETWEEN WEBER HUMAN SERVICES
       AND THE WEBER COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE.
         Chief Deputy Phillip Reese (Sheriff’s Department) provided MoU details; this employee share agreement
         allows a Weber Human Services employee to become part of Weber County Sheriff’s Office Inmate Case
         Management team, bringing additional resources to a county population in desperate need. Paid from State
         Justice Services funding; no county funding involved.

         Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of a Memorandum of Understanding with Weber Human Services
         to utilize one case manager as a liaison between Weber Human Services and the Weber County Sheriff’s Office;
         Chairman Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
