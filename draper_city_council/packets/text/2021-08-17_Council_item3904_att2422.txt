                              AGREEMENT NO. 21-175


       THIS AGREEMENT (Agreement) is made effective August 17, 2021 by and
between the CITY OF DRAPER (the "City"), a municipal corporation and political
subdivision of the state of Utah, and the DRAPER COMMUNITY FOUNDATION
(the "Foundation"), a Utah non-profit corporation.

                                      RECITALS

       WHEREAS, City promotes the health, safety and welfare of its residents by
providing recreational and civic programs; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-2, the City is restricted in the
appropriation of funds to those uses having a corporate purpose; and

        WHEREAS, the community events sponsored by the Foundation, in the judgment
of the Draper City Council, constitute a corporate purpose; and

        WHEREAS, Foundation is a non-profit 501 (c)(3) company organized to enhance
the quality of life for Draper City residents by hosting and/or organizing community
events and engaging in philanthropic activities; and

       WHEREAS, City recognizes the importance of providing community programs
and events for its residents that foster community spirit and enrich the lives of its
residents but may lack the resources to provide those programs and events; and

       WHEREAS, Foundation is qualified and possesses the skills and resources
necessary to perform the services and/or tasks set forth in this Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, City has appropriated the funds contemplated by this Agreement in
its adopted FY 2021-22 budget.


                                    AGREEMENT

       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, mutual covenants, and
undertakings, the parties hereby agree as follows:

1.     City Funding. City will provide $155,000 (Funds) to Foundation for the Fiscal
       Year 2021-22 for the purpose of funding the community events and activities
       referred to in Exhibit A. Subject to the provisions of Utah Code Annotated 10-8-
       2 (l)(a)(v), all City fees for Foundation events and activities shall be waived as
       further support of the community benefit provided to the City's residents. Tickets
       for the Draper City Mayor and Councilmembers shall be provided to all
       Foundation events and activities at no cost to the City in order to allow these



                                           1
     elected officials the opportunity to attend and participate in the events and
     activities in their official capacities as representatives of the City.

2.   Term & Termination.              Unless otherwise terminated by any party, this
     Agreement shall automatically terminate on June 30, 2022. However, either party
     may terminate this Agreement at will upon 30 days written notice to the other
     party. Termination of this Agreement for any reason shall not affect any of the
     rights, obligations or liability of the Parties hereto which may have accrued prior
     to termination.

3.   Payment. The City shall pay the Foundation for services listed in Exhibit A in
     the following manner:

     a. City shall pay the Foundation $155,000.00 for services outlined in Exhibit A.
        Payment shall be made in two payments. The first payment shall be made in
        July in the amount of $100,000 and the second payment shall be made in
        December in the amount of $55,000.

4.   Signage. In compliance with Draper City Municipal Code ("DCMC") 9-26-
     050(D)(l ), Foundation may submit a single sign permit application annually that
     will cover all events and activities, but shall otherwise be subject to the City's
     sign code in all other ways.

5.   Use of Funds. The Foundation pledges that it will utilize Funds for and in support
     of the community events and functions referred to in Exhibit A, and only for
     purposes as authorized by Utah State law and in conformity with Foundation's
     status as a tax-exempt nonprofit corporation under section 501(c)(3) of the
     Internal Revenue Code. Funds shall not be used to support or allow for the
     political campaigning at any Foundation event or activity. The Foundation agrees
     to maintain proper accounting records to allow City, at any time, to review all
     expenditures. Foundation will submit to City by Dec. 31 of each year a written
     report tracking City funds from revenue to expenditures showing how City funds
     were used in order to further the goals and objectives of the City as stated in this
     Agreement.

6.   Nondiscrimination. Foundation agrees not to and shall not discriminate in
     violation of any federal, state, or local law on the basis of race, color, religion,
     gender, age, national origin, disability, marital status, sexual orientation, or
     military status in any of its activities, operations, or in the provision of services.

7.   Indemnification. Foundation shall hold harmless, defend and indemnify City
     and its officers, officials, agents, employees, and volunteers from and against any
     and all liability, loss, damage, expense, cost (including without limitation costs
     and fees of litigation) of every nature arising out of or in connection with


                                            2
     Foundation's activities, except such loss or damage that is caused by the sole
     negligence or willful misconduct of City.

8.   Insurance. At all times during the pendency of this Agreement, the Foundation
     shall maintain the following minimum insurance policies, and provide copies of
     the insurance policies to the City annually and prior to any single event. The
     Parties agree the City has the right to require higher limits or other types of
     insurance than stated below if the City determines an event poses a higher than
     usual risk.

     a. General Commercial Liability. Commercial general liability insurance, on an
        occurrence form, with the City named as an additional insured, in the
        minimum amount of $1,000,000 per occurrence with a $2,000,000 general
        policy aggregate. The policy shall protect the City, the Foundation, and any
        subcontractor from claims for damages for personal injury, including
        accidental death, and from claims for property damage that may arise from the
        Foundation's operations under this Agreement, whether performed by the
        Foundation itself, any subcontractor, or anyone directly or indirectly employed
        or engaged by either of them. Such insurance shall provide coverage for
        premises operations, acts of independent contractors, and completed
        operations. The policy shall be primary and not contributing to any other
        policy or coverage available to the City whether such coverage be primary,
        contributing or excess.

     b. Special Event Insurance. Special event general liability insurance with the
        City named as an additional insured, in the minimum amount of $1,000,000
        per occurrence with a $2,000,000 general aggregate except for the rodeo
        which shall have a minimum amount of $2,000,000 per occurrence with a
        $3,000,000 general aggregate. The policy shall protect the City,
        Foundations, spectators and participants, and anyone directly or indirectly
        employed by either Party. Such insurance shall provide coverage for
        premises operations, acts of independent contractors, and completed
        operations during the event time period. The event time period includes the
        dates of the rehearsal(s), event set up, scheduled event, take down, and clean­
        up operations. The coverage shall be indicated on the certificate of insurance
        as "Special Event" coverage in the "Description of Operations" OR the
        insurance company shall supply the City with the original insurance policy
        evidencing the "Special Event" coverage.

        In the event any work for the "Special Event" is subcontracted to a separate
        company, the Foundation shall require the subcontractor to provide
        commercial general liability insurance, with the City as an additional insured,
        in the minimum amount of $1,000,000 per occurrence with a $2,000,000
        general aggregate.

9.    Applicable Law. The provisions hereof shall be governed by and construed in
      accordance with the laws of the state of Utah.

10.   Integration. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties
      pertaining to the subject matter hereof, and supersedes all prior agreements and
      understandings pertaining thereto.

11.   Waiver. No failure by any party to insist upon the strict performance of any
      covenant, duty, agreement or condition of this Agreement or to exercise any right
      or remedy consequent upon a breach thereof shall constitute a waiver of any such
      breach or of such or any other covenant, agreement, term or condition. Any party
      may, by notice delivered in the manner provided in this Agreement, but shall be
      under no obligation to, waive any of its rights or any conditions to its obligations
      hereunder, or any duty, obligation or covenant of any other party. No waiver
      shall affect or alter the remainder of this Agreement but each and every other
      covenant, agreement, term and condition hereof shall continue in full force and
      effect with respect to any other then existing or subsequently occurring breach.

12.   Rights and Remedies. The rights and remedies of the parties hereto shall not be
      mutually exclusive, and the exercise of one or more of the provisions of this
      Agreement shall not preclude the exercise of any other provisions hereof.

13.   Severability. In the event that any condition, covenant or other provision hereof
      is held to be invalid or void, the same shall be deemed severable from the
      remainder of this Agreement and shall in no way affect any other covenant or
      condition herein contained. If such condition, covenant, or other provision shall
      be deemed invalid due to its scope or breadth, such provision shall be deemed
      valid to the extent of the scope or breadth permitted by law.

14.   Conflict of Interest. The Foundation represents and certifies that it has not
      offered or given any gift or compensation prohibited by law to any officer or
      employee of the City to secure favorable treatment with respect to being awarded
      this Agreement.

15.   Attorney Fees. If any action in law or equity is brought to enforce or interpret
      the provisions of the Agreement, the prevailing party in such action shall be
      entitled to recover from the non-prevailing party reasonable attorney fees.

16.   Time. Time is the essence hereof.

17.   Agency. No agent, employee, or volunteer of Foundation or City is or shall be
      deemed to be an employee, agent, or servant of the other party. None of the
      benefits provided by each party to its employees or volunteers, including but not

      limited to worker's compensation insurance, health insurance and unemployment
      insurance, are available to the employees, agents, or servants of the other party.
      Foundation and City shall each make all commercially reasonable efforts to
      inform all persons with whom they are involved in connection with this
      Agreement to be aware that Foundation is an independent contractor.

18.   No Partnership or Joint Venture. The relationship between the parties as set
      forth herein shall not be deemed to create a partnership, joint venture or other
      legal or administrative entity of any kind or nature.

19.   Modification of Agreement. Any modification of this Agreement or additional
      obligations assumed by either party in connection with this Agreement must be in
      writing and signed by each party or an authorized representative of each party.
