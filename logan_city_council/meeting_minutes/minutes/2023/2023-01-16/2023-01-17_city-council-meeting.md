OPENING CEREMONY:
Isaiah Jones, USU Senior Director for Diversity, Equity & Inclusion gave the opening
remarks and led the audience in the pledge of allegiance.
Mr. Jones reminded the audience of the importance of Martin Luther King Jr. (a day that
had just passed). The holiday was first proposed in 1968 but did not come into effect until
1983 by President Ronald Regan. Not until 2000 was the holiday observed in all 50
states.
Martin Luther King Jr. writes a series of sermons, the titled sermon is “A Tough Mind
and A Tender Heart.”
“Be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless as doves. Matthew 10:16.”
Martin Luther King Jr. is grappling with the tension that existed in the South. It could be
between religion and science, universities vs. communities, race, etc. Because the
community had not learned to live with these tensions it manifested itself as violence. He
believed that a tough mind and a tender heart would lead to a better community.
 “There is little hope for us until we become toughminded enough to break loose from the
shackles of prejudice, half-truths, and downright ignorance. The shape of the world today
does not permit us the luxury of softmindedness. A nation or a civilization that continues
to produce softminded men purchases its own spiritual death on an installment plan.”
Mr. Jones asked those in attendance to focus on what Martin Luther King Jr. said
happens when we become toughminded and have conversations about our common
values, a tender heart. For King, he called it "Misericordia meaning mercy.” In Latin,
mercy means sharing the heart with another. As a community, our sense of university and
forge together. Let us look around and open ourselves and how we might go about
connecting.

PUBLIC HEARING – CDBG 2023 Annual Action Plan Initiation – Amanda Pearce,
CDBG Coordinator

CDBG Coordinator Amanda Pearce addressed the Council regarding the 2023 CDBG
Annual Action Plan Initiation. Ms. Pearce explained that tonight’s public hearing is to
initiate the CDBG Annual Action Plan cycle for 2023. This hearing does not require
formal action by the Council.

The purpose of the public hearing is to help inform the CDBG Steering Committee when
they start meeting on determining what areas of the City should be emphasized, the types
of projects that should be accomplished this year, what services or programs may be
lacking, what particular groups of people may be in need of services, and what specific
planning projects the City may consider.

The 2023 AAP will define the funding priorities for 2023. This will be the fifth and final
year of the 2019 - 2023 Consolidated plan and we anticipate receiving approximately
$550,000 in CDBG funding. Based on the Con Plan's priorities, the funding outcome
should approximate 50% to provide a suitable living environment (40% for infrastructure
and 10% for access and mobility), 30% to improve the capacity of public service
providers that serve LMI residents (20% for public facilities and 10% for service
improvements), and 20% to invest in CDBG planning and administration (5% for
planning and 15% for admin).

Considerations
Public Infrastructure and Facilities
   • What areas of the city should be emphasized?
   • What type of projects would the city like to accomplish?

Nonprofit service support
   • Are there specific services that are lacking that CDBG could help?
   • Are there programs that need support?
   • Are there particular groups of people who are in need of service?

Planning
   • Are there planning projects that should be given consideration?

BUDGET ADJUSTMENTS FY 2022-2023 appropriating: $75,977 funds for a
SBITA Contract for ESRI Services; $1,000,000 electric fund reserves toward
sediment removal at Second Dam – Resolution 23-02 – Richard Anderson, Finance
Director

Finance Director Richard Anderson addressed the Council regarding the proposed budget
adjustments. The State has requested that any web-based subscription be accumulated
into one transaction. An example is if the 1st year is $35,000 and the 2nd & 3rd is $40,000,
the subscription amount must be shown as a (total) or rather one single transaction. In this
example, it would be $115,000. The subscription must be capitalized and advertised each
year. It is similar to leasing.

Budgeting-wise, normally, we would budget for just the amount of the subscription.
Using the example, the first year would be $35,000, etc. However, now the total amount
must be held for the full contract (which in the example would be) $115,000. We
presently will appropriate it, capitalize it, and then advertise it.

Mr. Anderson clarified, Council can still set a budget, but will not be bound to this
agreement.

Mayor Daines remarked this would make budgeting more difficult.

The Council agreed with the sentiment.

Mark Montgomery, Light & Power Director addressed the $1,000,000 electric fund
reserves towards sediment removal at Second Dam. It is to generate more power, but last
year it was a struggle to keep them running. If the sediment gets into the equipment, it
will ruin them.

Mr. Montgomery responded it will be sufficient if the runoff goes well.
