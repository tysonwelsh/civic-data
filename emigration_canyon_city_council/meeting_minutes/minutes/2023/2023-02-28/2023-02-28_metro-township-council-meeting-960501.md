Council Business
Child Abuse Prevention Month Proclamation
Council Member Pinon read the following Proclamation declaring April as Child
Abuse Prevention Month:
WHEREAS, the children of Emigration Canyon are the future of our state’s success and
investing in their general welfare, safety and livelihood are of utmost priority; and
WHERAS, all children deserve to grow up in a safe and nurturing environment to assure
they reach their full potential as they grow and develop; and
WHEREAS, the protection of children and strengthening of families is of concern and
responsibility of all Emigration Canyon citizens because the wellness of children affects our lives
now and will continue to affect us in the future; and
WHEREAS, child abuse is a serious problem with 9,695 confirmed child victims in the
state of Utah last fiscal year with each of those cases impacting an entire family and affecting the
community as a whole: and

WHEREAS, child abuse respects no racial, religious, socio-economic or geographic
boundaries, and
WHEREAS, all citizens of Emigration Canyon need to become more aware of child abuse
and its prevention within their respective communities and actively encourage and support
parents to raise their children in a safe and nurturing environment, and
WHEREAS, preventing child abuse requires each member of the community to be
attentive to the problems of families around them and commit to do everything they can to help.
NOW THEREFORE, the Mayor and Metro Township Council of Emigration Canyon on
behalf of the 1,467 citizens, proclaim April 2023 as CHILD ABUSE PREVENTION MONTH and
we call upon all our citizens to renew their commitment to be educated on the impact of child
abuse and join in working for its prevention within our communities.
ADOPTED on this the 28th day of February, 2023 in Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Utah.
/s/ JOE SMOLKA
Emigration Canyon Metro Township Mayor
Council Member Pinon, seconded by Council Member Harris, moved to accept the
Proclamation declaring April as Child Abuse Prevention month. The motion passed unanimously.

Legislative Update
Mayor Smolka stated there is a good chance that H.B. 374 – County Sheriff
Amendments, will pass.
Council Member Brems stated if the bill passes, it will create a lot of uncertainty.
The metro townships will need to start determining where it will get police services from. Millcreek,
Midvale, and Holladay have been discussing among themselves about creating a Unified Police
Department 2.0.
Polly McLean stated she has been in contact with other attorney’s that are facing
the same issues as Emigration Canyon. She will not proceed with anything until after the
legislative session is over.
