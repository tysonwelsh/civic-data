9.    SCHEDULED ITEMS
      9.1     ORDINANCE – Amending the temporary land use regulation (moratorium)
              previously adopted by the City Council on August 9, 2021, with respect to the
              City Center District zones, the Hub District zones, the Canyon Crossing District
              zones, the Arts District zones and the North Village District zones pursuant to
              Utah Code Section 10-9a-504, to exclude from the moratorium the following types
              of construction: interior remodels; exterior remodels-exterior finish materials,
              roofing, windows, doors, awnings, canopies, decks, patios, and architectural
              features designed solely to enhance the aesthetic appeal of a building; signs;
              fences and retaining walls; roof structures for parking and drive-thrus; utility
              improvement infrastructure (electric, natural gas, telephone, fiber, water, sewer,
              storm water); parking lot and driveway improvements; curb, gutter and
              sidewalk; lighting improvements; landscaping improvements; cellular
              infrastructure; and other structures and improvements that do not create or
              increase habitable building space (but in no case to exceed a height of 60 feet);
              and also to exempt from the moratorium all construction that is purely
              commercial (no residential uses) in nature that does not exceed a height of 60 feet
              and provides parking of at least one stall per 250 square feet of gross floor area.
              Presenter: Steve Earl, Deputy City Attorney
