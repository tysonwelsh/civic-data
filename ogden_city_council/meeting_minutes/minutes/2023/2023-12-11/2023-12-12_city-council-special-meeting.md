Recognizing the Weber State Volleyball team for winning their third Big Sky
Conference Tournament
A memo from Council staff regarding recognition of Weber State University (WSU) Women’s Volleyball Team for
winning their third Big Sky Conference Tournament came before the Council for consideration. Council member Blair read the
certificate of recognition issued by the Ogden City Council and Mayor Caldwell and presented Head Coach Jeremiah Larsen and
the members of the volleyball team present with the certificate.
Mr. Larsen thanked the Mayor and Council for this recognition; he stated that when he was selected as coach of the team
nine years ago, he dreamed of winning championships at WSU and he is so grateful to the community for their support, which has
made it possible to win three championships in four years. He stated the student athletes are wonderful ambassadors for the
community, earning high grade point averages and pursuing impressive fields of study. He is proud of them and again thanked the
community for their support.
Council member Blair stated he has attended several women’s volleyball games, and the team is very impressive to
watch; they are talented and represent the school well. Vice Chair Richey agreed and congratulated the team and CoachLarsenon | :

Proposed Resolution 2023-23 requesting the recertification of the Ogden
City Justice Court bo

A memo from the Management Services Department came before the Council to consider a resolution requesting the
recertification of the Ogden City Justice Court. The memo stated Justice Courts must be certified by the State Justice Courts
Standards Committee and the Judicial Council every four years. The Administrativé Office of the Courts must receive a request
for certification no later than December 15, 2024.

Justice Court Administrator Richardson summarized the memo and stated Administration is requesting the Council adopt
a resolution asking the State Justice Courts Standards Committee and the Judicial Council recertify the Ogden City Justice Court
and affirming the City’s willingness to continue to meet all requirements necessary to continue operations. The City wrote an
Opinion Letter outlining the operational procedures of the Court and the feasibility of continuing operations as required by law.

Judge Stucki noted he has signed an affidavit affirming that the court is in compliance with all statutory requirements and
minimum standards established by the Judicial Council, and he asked for the Council’s adoption of the proposed resolution
requesting recertification of the Justice Court.

Council member Lopez stated that he has attended a few sessions at the Ogden City Justice Court and was proud of the
way the Court staff and Judge Stucki treated those who had been required to attend court.

Chair Choberka introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2023-23, entitled:

“A resolution of the Ogden City Council requesting the recertification of the Ogden City Justice Court.”
