                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-22

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
 WITH JANI-KING OF SALT LAKE CITY TO CLEAN THE RESTROOMS IN THE
                              PARKS

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any contract that exceeds
$30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the Public Works Director deems this is necessary because they have been
unable to get seasonal staff to clean the restrooms; and

       WHEREAS, this will be paid for using the money budgeted for hiring seasonal staff.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Jani-King of
            Salt Lake City to clean the restrooms in the parks.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
March 2022 by the following vote:
