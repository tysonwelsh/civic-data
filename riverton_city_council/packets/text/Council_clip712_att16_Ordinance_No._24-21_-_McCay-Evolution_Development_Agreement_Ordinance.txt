                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                       ORDINANCE NO. 24-21

  AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AND ADOPTING THE “DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
   FOR 2682 W 13400 S”, APPROXIMATELY .32 ACRES LOCATED AT 2682 W 13400 S
 CURRENTLY ZONED R-3, WITH ZONING SET TO RM-6 AND ALLOWING UP TO THREE
     (3) ATTACHED UNITS ON THAT PROPERTY. PROPOSED BY DANIEL MCCAY
                  REPRESENTING EVOLUTION DEVELOPMENT

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that under Utah Code §10-9a-532, the City Council may
lawfully adopt development agreements that have different standards set forth in existing land use
regulations if it does so in accordance with the same procedures for enacting a land use regulation,
including review and recommendation from the Planning Commission and a public hearing; and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed development agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said development agreement;
and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to adopt the “Development Agreement for 2682 W 13400 S”, as described herein and shown as Exhibit
“A:” and

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Development Agreement, Exhibit “A” attached hereto, and
                              all associated design documents as approved, is hereby approved
                              and adopted.

                Section 2.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 12th day of
November, 2024 by the following vote:
