Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: September 24, 2020
Re: Alpine Joint Venture Sidewalk Deviation Request Comments: The applicant is requesting approval of a Sidewalk Deviation to allow for a reduction in the amount of sidewalk installed from approximately 3,905 lineal feet along each side of Suncrest Drive to approximately 490 lineal feet on the east side and 100 lineal feet on the west side of the road.The property is approximately 235.33 acres in size and is bisected by Suncrest Drive. The approximate address is 16377 South Suncrest Drive. The property is currently zoned A5 (Agricultural, 5 acre min. lot size). The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1.  That the size of the parcel exceeds the minimum requirements of the A5 Zone.
2.  That the required improvements are excessive based on a review by the city engineer.
3.  Proposed frontage improvements will be provided for a length that meets or exceed the minimum lot width required by the zone.
4.  That approval for the deviation from sidewalk standards along Suncrest Drive in the proposed locations is appropriate to the situation.
For Denial:
1.  That the required improvements are not excessive.
2.  That approval for the deviation from sidewalk standards along Suncrest Drive is not appropriate to the situation. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type P.C. Staff Report 9/17/2020 Cover Memo
