REQUEST FOR A PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request to amend the Tonaquint Center Planned
     Development Commercial (PD-C) to add two additional uses. The applicant
     is IG Winery and the representative is Doug McCombs. Case No. 2024 – PDA
     – 024 – PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: The applicant is requesting to amend the
      Tonaquint Center Planned Development Commercial (PD-C) use list for the purpose
      of adding the following uses: “Bar Establishment and Micro-Winery.” The proposed
      addition of “Bar Establishment and Micro-Winery” are permitted uses in the General
      Commercial (C-3) zone, and the applicant would like to add this use into this
      business park to open a wine tasting room at this location. The Tonaquint Center
      Planned Development was originally approved in 1998. Case No. 2024-PDA-024
      (Staff – Brenda Hatch).

      Link to presentation by Planner Brenda Hatch including discussion between
      Commission Members and Ms. Hatch: 00:00:46

REQUEST FOR A PLANNED DEVELOPMENT AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request to amend the Desert Color zone plan. This PD
     amendment would allow the construction of a new restaurant building on
     1.31 acres. The site is located on the north side of Black Mountain Drive just
     south of Big Shots Golf. The applicant is H.C. Klover Architect, and the
     representative is Henry Klover. The project will be known as Panda Express.
     Case No. 2024-PDA-023 – PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: This is a request to approve the conceptual
      site plan, landscape plan, elevations, renderings, and the colors, and materials board
      for Panda Express in the Desert Color commercial area. There have been several
      approvals for commercial development within Desert Color such as Big Shots Golf
      Center, pads K, M & N, Mountain America, America First Credit Union, and pad A

REQUEST FOR A ZONING REGULATION AMENDMENT:
     Consider approval of a request for a request to amend the definition of
     “Landscape or Landscaping, and Lot, Corner” and definitions for “Barn, and
     Lot Double Frontage.” The applicant is the City of St. George. Case No. 2024-
     ZRA-017 – PUBLIC HEARING.

      BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: Staff felt the current code lacked a
      definition for “Barn” as well as a definition for “Lot, Double Frontage.” The definition
      for “Lot, Corner” was updated to delineate the difference between a corner lot and a
      double fronted lot. The current definition of Landscape or Landscaping includes
      swimming pools, retaining walls and fences. This definition has conflicted with some
      areas of the municipal code and therefore must be updated. Case No. 2024-ZRA-
      017 (Staff – Brenda Hatch).

      Link to presentation by Planner Brenda Hatch including discussion between

REQUEST FOR A HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
     Consider approval of a request for a Hillside Development Permit to adjust
     the location of the ridgeline setback on lot 44 of the Foremaster Ridge
     subdivision in anticipation of the construction of a residential home. The
     applicants are Jack de Ryk and Floyd Jackson. Case No. 2024-HS-010.

       BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: This is a request to obtain a hillside permit
       for the property located at 1856 E. Joe Cir (Lot 44, Foremaster Ridge). This location
       is a residential neighborhood, and the applicant would like to build a house on this
       lot. This application came before the Planning Commission once before on August 27,
       2024, at which time the Planning Commission recommended that the City Council
       approve the ridgeline as proposed by the Hillside Review Board. On October 23,
       2024, the Hillside Review Board reviewed the application again and recommended
       the same coordinates for the ridgeline. That is the recommendation to the Planning
       Commission. Case No. 2024-HS-010 (Staff – Dan Boles).

       This item was withdrawn from the agenda per the applicant.
