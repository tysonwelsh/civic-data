Legislative Updates HB 462, SB 110, HB 303,
HB 282, & HB 118
        HB 462 Public Transit
             Planning

• Required to create a plan around 1/2 mile around a rail
  station (“SAP”)
• SAP intended to promote shared objectives such as:
   •   Housing availability
   •   Access to opportunities
   •   Environmental conditions
   •   Transportation choices and connections
             HB 462 SAP must include:


• Station area vision
• Station area map
• 5-year implementation plan
• Statement of how the plan promotes SAP objectives
• Referenda for SAPs modified
HB 462 Moderate Income Implementation
• Submit a moderate income housing report to the Housing and
  Community Development Division at Department of Workforce
  Services by 10-1-22
• Adopt a SAP
• No impact fees for IADUs.
SB 110 Water Conservation Efforts
• Water use and preservation implemented into the general plan
• Small amount of funding available from Division of Water Resources
  (not DWR)
HB 303 Standing for annexation is limited to:
• Legislative body or governing board of affected entity
• Owner of rural real property
• Owner of private real property located in a mining area
HB 303 Municipal Disconnection et al
• Mail notice and public hearing required for municipal disconnection
• Enforcement is not a land use decision
• Land use regulation text amendments require notice
• Legal non-conforming use needs to be proved by substantial
  evidence, which may not be limited to municipal records
• 10 year standards from last year’s bill eliminated
• Notices for changes to improvement standards in subdivisions
• May adopt an ordinance that specifies combining lots does not
  require a plat amendment
HB 282 Water Wise Landscaping and HB 118
Wetland Amendments
• HB 282
  • May not require park strips of < 8 feet in width
  • May not prohibit a property owner from incorporating water
    wise landscaping
• HB 118 – Any land use permit affecting designated
