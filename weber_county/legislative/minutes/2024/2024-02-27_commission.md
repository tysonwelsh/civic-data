D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: I just finished a book called “QBQ, The Question Behind the
   Question” authored by John G Miller. He talks about what we can do in our situations to make things better. A
   quote from the book, “The lack of personal accountability is a problem that has resulted in an epidemic of blame,
   victim thinking, and procrastination. No organization or individual can successfully compete in the marketplace,
   achieve goals and objectives, provide outstanding service, engage in exceptional teamwork, or develop people
   without personal accountability.” Most of what he says has to do with eliminating blame. In almost every
   human situation, we can find someone to blame. The encouragement from the book was, “How can I make it
   better?” When my kids used to come to me with a problem, I would tell them to be a problem solver. You can’t
   change what was done but you can change how you react and sometimes even make the situation better. One of
   my favorite quotes that has to do with taking action instead of having a plan and hoping things go well. He says
   in the book, “Up to the mountain top go the executives for a Senior Management retreat. For three days they
   debate the critical issues, filling flip charts with brightly colored ink. Finally, Mission, Vision and Values in
   hand, they return to the valley below where the people wait to receive the stone tablets which have magically
   transformed into little laminated pocket cards for men to sit on and women to stuff into their briefcases.” It’s
   more than just a plan that we sit on our pocket or stuff in our briefcase, it’s the action we take as an individual to
   better the situation we are in.

       AIRCRAFT SYSTEMS (DRONES) BY COUNTY DEPARTMENTS OTHER THAN THE SHERIFF’S
       OFFICE.
         Courtlan Erickson (Attorney’s Office): The County has not had a drone policy outside of the Sheriff’s
         department. Sean Brian in our Attorney’s office has experience working with the Sheriff’s office on drone
         use; he helped us write this policy. There are FAA licensing requirements and liability issues to address.

    6. APPROVE RESOLUTION 11-2024 APPROVING INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENTS
       WITH THE FOLLOWING CITIES RELATING TO THE DELIVERY OF MUNICIPAL SOLID
       WASTE TO THE WEBER COUNTY TRANSFER STATION:
                      Washington Terrace        Roy City
                      South Ogden City          Harrisville City
                      Pleasant View City        Hooper City
                      Ogden City                Farr West City
                      North Ogden City          Uintah City
                      Marriott-Slaterville City Plain City
                      Riverdale City
         Bill Ross (Operations): We have been working on these contracts and this resolution for some time. These
         agreements allow for guaranteed waste stream to the Weber County Transfer Station. The agreements allow
         the Transfer Station to know how much municipal solid waste to expect which leads to better budgeting and
         operational decisions. The agreements state each city will deliver all of their curbside municipal solid waste
         exclusively to the Weber County Transfer Station and Weber County will continue to own and operate the
         station for the agreements duration. These agreements run through 2024 with automatic renewal every two
         years through 2034 with early termination available after a six month notice.

         Commissioner Froerer: This has been a long time coming. We could not in good faith continue to operate a
         transfer station and provide these services without some type of guarantee that waste will continue to come to
         Weber County landfills.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 11-2024 approving interlocal cooperation agreements with
         the above-listed cities relating to the delivery of municipal solid waste to the Weber County Transfer Station;
         Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye
