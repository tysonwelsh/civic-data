                                 MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

RESOLUTION NO.: 22-12-02                                         DATE: December 13, 2022


           A RESOLUTION OF MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
           ADOPTING THE 2023 MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP FINAL BUDGET


       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
Section 10-2a-401 et.seq.; and


       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code Section 10-3b-501; and


       WHEREAS, the Council has complied in all respects with State Law, including holding public
hearings, in establishing budgets for the Calendar Year beginning January 1, 2023 and ending December
31, 2023; and


        WHEREAS, pursuant to fiscal policy for funding the 2023 Magna Budget, the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District (the “MSD”) adopted the 2023 Budget for the MSD on October 12, 2022,
releasing the funding for the 2023 Magna Budget; and


        WHEREAS, the noticing for the public hearing to consider and copies of the 2023 Magna
Tentative Budget, (Attachment “A”) were made available for public consideration at least ten (10) days
prior to the date of the public hearing for the Budget; and


       WHEREAS, the adoption of the 2023 Budget will allow Magna to comply with State Law and
the Uniform Fiscal Procedures Act for Cities;


       THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL as
follows:


      SECTION 1. The Magna Metro Township Council hereby adopts the 2023 Magna Final Budget,
Attachment “A”.
        SECTION 2. That upon the final adoption, the budget shall be in effect for the budget year and
subject to later amendment as provided by law.


       SECTION 3. A copy of the final budget shall be posted and made available to the public on the
City’s website: www.magnacity.org.


       SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage thereof.


      APPROVED AND ADOPTED this 13th day of December 2022 by the Magna Metro Township
Council, Magna, Utah.
