MIKE GATES, DISTRICT MANAGER FOR THE BUREAU OF LAND MANAGEMENT
WEST DESERT DISTRICT (INTRODUCED STAFF WITH HIM) - WE'RE STARTING TO
HEAR FROM THE PUBLIC THAT THERE ARE SOME AREAS IN UTAH COUNTY
WHERE RECREATIONAL SHOOTING HAS SOME INTERACTIONS THAT ARE LESS
