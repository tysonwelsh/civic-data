   3. CLOSED MEETING
      a. The Redevelopment Agency Board did not hold a closed session
   Board Member McConnehey questioned the urgency for the closed meeting. Economic Development
   Director Chris Pengra briefly explained the intent was to provide information regarding property and
   received feedback from Board Members.

    purchase, exchange, or lease of real property; or the sale of real property, including any form of

    Chairperson Kayleen Whitelock                 Yes
    Vice Chairperson Kelvin Green                 No
    Board Member Pamela Bloom                     No
    Board Member Zach Jacob                       No
    Board Member Chris McConnehey                 No
    Board Member David Pack                       Yes
    Board Member Melissa Worthen                  No

   No closed meeting was held.

   4. ADJOURN
   Board Member Jacob moved to adjourn the meeting. Board Member McConnehey seconded the
   motion. One dissenting vote was heard and a roll call vote was taken.
