BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement Amendment for
approximately 25.9 acres located on the east side of Canyon Point Rd. and Suncrest Dr., at
approximately 2635 East 15040 South (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned RR-
22, RM, and C-3. The applicant is requesting that a Development Agreement Amendment
be approved to allow for additional minor changes to the Development Agreements
currently in place.

The subject property was originally part of the Suncrest Development Agreement. It was
removed from that agreement in 2015 with the approval of the initial Hidden Canyon



Hidden Canyon Estates- Mercer Mountain Estates                     App. # 2024-0337-DA
Development Agreement 10th Amendment Request        1
Estates, Mercer Mountain Estates, and Lakeview Heights Properties Development
Agreement. The Original 2015 Development Agreement approved the Mercer Mountain
Estates development as a 50-lot single-family subdivision with a minimum lot size of 12,000
ft2 and allowed for a private gated community.

Since 2015 there have been nine amendments to the Development Agreement. Several of
those amendments dealt with the development of the Hidden Canyon Estates Subdivision
and its Lone Peak Canyon phase. A few of the amendments dealt with this Mercer
Mountain Estates Subdivision. The Fifth Amendment allows fill to be placed on the City’s
property adjacent to Canyon Point Rd. and Suncrest Dr. and modified the location of the
grated entrance location. The Eighth Amendment modified roadway locations on the
property and approved some land swaps between the City and the property owner.

The zoning on the property is unique. The City did not rezone the property in 2015 from
the Suncrest zoning, which is tied to the 1999 Draper City Municipal Code (1999 DCMC).
However, the initial Development Agreement removed the property from the Suncrest
Development Agreement which means the property is required to comply with current
zoning code, the Current Draper City Municipal Code (Current DCMC), and the current
Development Agreement and amendments.

Development Agreement Amendment. The 10th Amendment to the Development
Agreement (Exhibit F) covers six principal items. The first deals with the placement of fill on
the City open space. The 5th Amendment to the Development Agreement allowed some fill
on the City open space. The initial thought was that this area could be a trailhead. It will
serve as trail and maintenance access. The 10th Amendment does not allow for any
additional fill on the City open space, but does state that the City Council can approve
additional fill in the City open space in the future.

The second item covers the roadway width and design. The City’s Mountain Street standard
is for a 56-foot wide roadway with parkstrips and sidewalk on both sides of the road and
31-feet total asphalt width. The applicant is proposing a 50-foot wide roadway cross section
that eliminates the sidewalk on one side of the road, but maintains the residual parkstrip
area for snow storage. No reduction in asphalt width is proposed. In addition, the
amendment updates the location of the roadway and maximum roadway grade in certain
areas and makes minor adjustments to the area of the land swap with the City outlined in
the 8th Amendment. These modifications are needed to meet engineering best practices.

Thirdly, the applicant is requesting to allow temporary fencing adjacent to the City open
space during home construction with the required permanent wrought iron fencing being
installed prior to home occupancy. This was allowed for the Lone Peak Canyon Subdivision
per the 9th Amendment.

Fourth, the amendment addresses the stormwater infrastructure. A stormwater detention
basin is proposed to be located on the south side of the subdivision. The 10th Amendment
restricts discharge rates to the historical discharge rates into the Hog Hollow drainage area
from the basin. It also requires fencing to be utilized to restrict access to the detention
basin. The fencing type will need to be approved by the City’s Engineering Division with the
final subdivision plat approval. The homeowners association for the subdivision will be
responsible for maintenance of the basin.

The fifth item covers changes to the slope easements outlined in the fifth amendment. In
order to build the proposed road, some fill and cuts will be required along the roadway,

created some minor changes to the locations for the slope easements. With the 10th

The last item covers the water system maintenance. There has been a change in state law
when it comes to responsibility for water quality and the proposed language clarifies the
respective responsibilities for the water system and water quality between the City and the
homeowners association given that the individual utilities and roadway are private.

        C.   Procedure: All development agreements, upon proper execution, shall be
             recorded with the respective County Recorder's Office, shall run with the land,
             and shall be binding on all successors in the ownership of the affected
             property. A development agreement shall contain, at a minimum, the

       location.
