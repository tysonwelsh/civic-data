Background

This is a public hearing for a Development Agreement. Riverton City is proposing approval of a
Development Agreement establishing requirements, standards, and parameters for
development of approximately 5.5-acres located at 13265 S Lovers Lane, allowing for
subdivision of that property at lot sizes consistent with the current zoning.

The property is currently zoned RR-22, a single-family residential zone, with half-acre lots and
animal rights. The General Plan designation for the area is Estate Density Residential.

A copy of the Development Agreement and the subdivision layout is attached.


Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
web-page advertising this item for the March 24, 2022 PC Hearing. It was continued at the March
24th PC Meeting and at each subsequent Planning Commission meeting. The Planning
Commission is making a recommendation, the City Staff will send additional notices to the
property owners to announce the City Council meeting where a final decision will be made.

At the time of the initial PC Notice and Staff Report (in March) several residents called the city
requesting additional information, but no written comments were received.

At the initial public hearing one resident voiced concerns over the language in the agreement.

                             DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
                                             FOR
       Preserve at Lovers Lane - Development/Preservation of Natural Features

          THIS DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR THE PRESERVE at LOVERS
LANE (“Agreement”) is made and entered into on the ______ day of March, 2022,
between Riverton City, a municipal corporation and political subdivision of the State of
Utah (the “City”); and KSO Trust and Tyson Olson, the owners of the subject real property
(collectively the “Owners” or “Developer”). The City and the Owners are sometimes
collectively referred to herein as the “Parties.”

                                             RECITALS:

          WHEREAS, the real property covered by this Agreement is located in adjoining
parcels encompassing approximately 6 acres (collectively referred to as the “Property”).
The legal descriptions that describe the Property are set forth in Exhibit “A” attached
hereto.

          WHEREAS, the real property is generally located as shown on an aerial map of the
general area attached as Exhibit “B”.

          WHEREAS, the current property is approved for two homes where owner can have
up to 3 homes on the private Lane Exhibit “C”

          WHEREAS, the owner desires to develop the Property to include 8 single family
homes. The conceptual layout for the proposed development of the Property is attached
hereto as Exhibit “D.”

                                           AGREEMENT:
          NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Recitals above and the terms and
conditions set forth below, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties covenant and agree as follows:




                                               Page 2 of 2
1.   Effective Date. This Agreement shall become effective on the date it is recorded in
     the Salt Lake County Recorder’s Office after being executed by the Owners, and the
     City (the “Effective Date”).

2.   Affected Property. The Property that is the subject of this Agreement is described in
     the legal descriptions attached hereto as Exhibit “A.” This Agreement shall be
     recorded against the Property.

3.   Settlement Of Claims. This development agreement is made in connection with
     claims by Owners against Riverton City and Symphony Homes, which are pending
     in the Third District Court, in and for Salt Lake County, State of Utah, Case No. ,
     210900684, and is intended to resolve all issues relating to the subject Property in
     the referenced case.

4.   City agrees that Owners’ Landscaping and Grading Plans will meet City Code
     standards for preservation of natural features, as described in City Code Section,
     17.05.040:

            a. Conservation of Natural Features. Trees, native land cover, wetlands,
               natural watercourses, and topography shall be preserved where possible.
               Subdivisions shall be so designed as to prevent excessive grading and
               scarring of the landscape in conformance with RCC Title 18. The design
               of new subdivisions shall consider, and relate to, existing street widths,
               alignments and names.16.


5.   Up to three homes are allowed on the Property with a private lane in accordance
     with the following:

            a. The private lane will be modified to 20 total feet in width
               (easement/cross section) per attached Exhibit (C);
            b. The private lane for phase one, described in subsection 5. c., below, will
               meet the standard for a 24-ton vehicle being constructed of crushed
               asphalt and or limestone crusher fines for the surface;
            c. Owner shall subdivide the property into three lots by subdivision plat
               attached as Exhibit D.);
            d. The private lane will seek to preserve the natural features as per City
               Code;
            e. Stormwater discharge, if any, within the project shall be managed within
               the project to equal predevelopment hydrology, unless the Developer
               can demonstrate with a hydrology study that additional capacity is
               available in Butterfield Creek, in which case the developer may
               discharge additional stormwater into Butterfield Creek in a manner
               which complies with the Jordan Valley Municipality Permit No.


                                           Page 2 of 2
           UTS000001, issued by the State of Utah, division of water quality (this
           is intended as part of the settlement of Owners’ stormwater runoff
           claims against Riverton City;
      f. Direct stormwater runoff into Butterfield does not include runoff from
           hard surfaces which thereafter flows through landscaped or natural
           ground or vegetation, whether or not such runoff eventually flows into
           Butterfield Creek.
      g. Provided Phase 1 does not cause direct stormwater runoff into
           Butterfield Creek, no retention or detention ponds will be required for
           the three homes and private lane approved in Phase 1 due to the
           anticipated low impact where stormwater will disperse into landscape
           areas to prevent water from running directly into Butterfield Creek;
      h. The private lane will be posted for no parking at the entry from Lovers
           Lane, within 20 feet of the bridge and at the fire turn around;
      i. While there are no more than three homes constructed, or under
           construction, Owners shall provide for the upkeep and maintenance of
           the private lane; and
      j. At the time there are more than three homes constructed, or under
           construction, Owners shall create a home owners association to provide
           the upkeep and maintenance of the private lane.
      j.k. The development shall prevent the off-site discharge of the precipitation
           from all rainfall events that are less than or equal to 0.50 inches. For
           example, the grading of the private, or installation of swales, where
           appropriate, lane may be sufficient to meet this requirement. It is
           expected that the improvements required for phase II of the subdivision
           will require installation of improvements which may not be required for
           phase I.
6.   Development of property not developed in phase 1 shall be developed in
     phase 2 and shall be subject to the following conditions:
      a. The Property is approved for up to 8 homes total including existing
           homes in phase 1, with a private lane from Lovers Lane (Concept
           Exhibit D);
      b. At the time more than three (3) homes are being built, the private lane
           cross section will be increased to 25 feet and the entire private lane shall
           be paved with asphalt (25 feet total cross section including easement);
      c. The private lane is approved at up to 1400 ft. long with one access from
           Lovers Lane to the fire turn around;
      d. The fire turn around shall be located within 150 feet of the last home on
           the lane, as per exhibit D;
      e. All homes will have adequate parking per City Code;
      f. The entire length of the private lane will be posted for no parking;
      g. The private lane will be approved at the staff level;
      h. If the property east of the Property develops with a public road prior to
           actual physical development of the Property, the City will require as part
           of the new development a stub road servicing the Property as part of the
           adjacent development, which will be located at the proximity of the
           center of the Owners’ east property line;
      i. When, and if, such a stub road is constructed, a 25 foot private lane from
           the Owner’s east property line is approved from the adjacent property to
           the east and the new private lane shall not be required to tie to Lovers

                                     Page 2 of 2
          Lane or to the existing private lane from Lovers Lane to the Olson home
          currently under construction;
      j. In the event a new stub road to the Owners’ east property line is
          constructed, the maximum of eight homes on the private lane shall be
          waived as long as all other zoning requirements for lot size for the
          underlying zone are met;
      k. The new private lane is approved to be within lot boundaries and lot
          square footage;
      l. Prior to constructing any home accessing the new private lane, Owners
          will amend an existing home owners association, or create a new home
          owners association, to provide the upkeep and maintenance of the new
          private lane
      m. The development of the overall Property is low impact development
          where no publicly owned retention or detention (ponds or swells) shall
          be required as all storm water will disperse to landscape areas off the
          side of the new private lane or from homes and driveways.
7.   Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this development agreement,
     nothing shall prohibit Owners from developing a standard subdivision on the
     east portions of the Property once a stub road is provided from the adjacent
     property to the east of the Property.
8.   If Owners are able to acquire property or access from the east that connects
     to the proposed stub road located at the River Ridge Lane East Storm Drain
     Pond location (retention pond), Riverton City will pay the cost of the
     improvements for the stub road at that location along with any condemnation
     of property required. in connection with the protection strip owned by
     Tammy Larsen north of the retention pond.
9.   General Terms and Conditions.

     a. Incorporation of Recitals. The Recitals contained in this Agreement are
         hereby incorporated into this Agreement as if fully set forth herein.

     b. Recording of Agreement. This Agreement shall be recorded at Owners
         expense to put prospective purchasers or other interested parties on
         notice as to the terms and provisions hereof. Owners shall be responsible
         for ensuring that this Agreement is recorded and shall not hold the City
         liable for failure to record.

     c. Severability. Each and every provision of this Agreement shall be
         separate, severable, and distinct from each other provision hereof, and
         the invalidity, unenforceability, or illegality of any such provision shall
         not affect the enforceability of any other provision hereof.

     d. Amendment of Agreement. This Agreement shall not be amended
         except in written form mutually agreed to and signed by both parties. No
         change shall be made to any provision of this Agreement or any

                condition set forth in any exhibit hereto unless this Agreement, or
                exhibit, is amended pursuant to a vote of the City Council taken with the
                same formality as the vote approving this Agreement.

            e. Applicable Law. This Agreement and the construction thereof, and the
                rights, remedies, duties, and obligations of the parties which arise
                hereunder are to be construed and enforced in accordance with the laws
                of the State of Utah. Unless otherwise provided in this Agreement,
                Owner’s development shall comply with Riverton Municipal Code.

            f. Title and Authority. Owners, as to the Property they own, expressly
                warrants and represents to City that they (i) own all rights, title, and
                interest in and to their respective Property, or (ii) have the exclusive
                right to acquire such interest, and (iii) that prior to the execution of this
                Agreement no right, title or interest in the Property has been sold,
                assigned or otherwise transferred to any other entity or individual.
                Owners understand that City is relying on these representations and
                warranties in executing this Agreement.

   10.     Obligations Run With the Land. The agreements, rights and obligations
           contained in this Agreement shall: (i) inure to the benefit of the Parties; (ii)
           be binding upon the Parties and their respective successors, successors-in-
           title, heirs and assigns; and (iii) run with the Property. The termination date
           of this agreement shall be upon the sooner of: December 31, 2035, or upon
           the full development of the Property as contemplated in this Agreement.
