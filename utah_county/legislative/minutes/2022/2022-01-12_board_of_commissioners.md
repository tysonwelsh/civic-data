(Discussed Regular Agenda Items #21 and #22 together)

ROBERT BAXTER, PURCHASING MANAGER - REGARDING THE EXEMPTION,
ORIGINALLY WE WERE LOOKING AT PUTTING THAT IN PART 3 AND WE DECIDED
TO PUT IT IN PART 5. THIS WAS TO ALLOW SOME DISCRETION WITH THE
ENTERTAINMENT AND ATTRACTIONS TYPES OF THINGS THAT THE COUNTY IS
SPONSORING WHICH DON’T REALLY LEND THEMSELVES WELL TO THE
COMPETITIVE PROCESS, SO WE WANTED TO HAVE THE DISCRETION TO EXEMPT
THEM FROM THAT.

WHEN IT COMES TO THE EXEMPTION, THIS DOES NOT CUT OUT THE COMMISSION
FROM THE PROCESS. ONCE THE DETERMINATION IS MADE AND A CONTRACT IS
DRAFTED, IT THEN COMES TO THE COMMISSION TO APPROVE OR REJECT. I WAS
HIRED TO MAKE THESE SORTS OF PROFESSIONAL JUDGEMENTS AND I'D LIKE TO
THINK I DO A GOOD JOB OF IT. I THINK TO MOVE TO REQUIRING COMMISSION
APPROVAL UP FRONT IS A MOVE IN THE WRONG DIRECTION. WE SHOULD BE
MOVING TOWARDS MORE EFFICIENCY AND LESS BUREAUCRACY.

ROBERT BAXTER, DAVE SHAWCROFT (DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY) AND
COMMISSION DISCUSSED CONFLICTS OF FINANCING, $2,500 THRESHOLD BE FOR
AN RFP IS REQUIRED, STATE CONTRACTS, STATE STATUTE, WHAT'S IN THE BEST
INTEREST OF THE COUNTY, COMPETITIVE PROCESS, TRANSPARENCY, ELECTION
EXCEPTIONS, ETC.

COMMISSIONER GARDNER - SUGGESTS STRIKING REGULAR AGENDA ITEM #21,
KNOWING THAT WE'RE GOING TO OPEN THIS CONVERSATION BECAUSE THERE
ARE IMPROVEMENTS TO BE MAKE FOR COMMISSIONERS FOR EFFICIENCY
PURPOSES AND THEN APPROVE REGULAR AGENDA ITEM #22.
