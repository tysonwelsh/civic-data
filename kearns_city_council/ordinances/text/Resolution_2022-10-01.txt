     RESOLUTION NO. 2022-10-01                                      DATE: October 10, 2022


           A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
         APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH THE SALT LAKE
          VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA REGARDING THE
            FUNDING OF A NEW SERGEANT POSITION FOR THE UNIFIED
                  POLICE DEPARTMENT’S KEARNS PRECINCT

                                             RECITALS

        WHEREAS, Utah Code § 11-13-202 authorizes two or more public agencies to execute
interlocal agreements to carry out joint or cooperative actions, including the provision of law
enforcement services; and

       WHEREAS, the Unified Police Department’s (“UPD”) Kearns precinct currently has one
sergeant who supervises twelve-and-a-half officers, which has limited the sergeant’s ability to mentor
and supervise the precinct’s four school resource officers (“SROs”);

        WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) Council and UPD desire to reclassify
one of the precinct’s detectives as a sergeant (the “New Sergeant”), thereby allowing the precinct to
have two sergeants who can divide the precinct’s supervision and mentoring responsibilities;

       WHEREAS, the New Sergeant will supervise six officers, including all four of the SROs,
which will improve the effectiveness of the SROs by increasing their opportunities to communicate
with and receive mentorship from the New Sergeant;

       WHEREAS, reclassifying the detective as the New Sergeant will require an additional
$100,000 over the next three years in increased compensation and benefits;

       WHEREAS, the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (“SLVLESA”) levies
property taxes for the law enforcement services Kearns receives from UPD and is responsible for
providing the funding used to pay the compensation and benefits of the UPD officers assigned to
Kearns;

        WHEREAS, Kearns and SLVLESA are both public entities and desire to execute an
interlocal agreement pursuant to their authority under Utah Code § 11-13-202 by which Kearns will
provide SLVLESA with the $100,000 needed to fund the New Sergeant’s re-classification over the
next three years.

        NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council that
effective immediately:



                                                  1
       1.     The attached interlocal agreement is approved pursuant to Utah Code §§ 11-13-202
and 11-13-202.5;

        2.      The Mayor and Kearns’ staff are authorized to take all steps needed to implement the
interlocal agreement, including the transfer of the $100,000 to SLVLESA in a manner that is mutually
agreeable to the Mayor and SLVLESA; and

       3.      The interlocal agreement is not intended to create an interlocal entity.

      APPROVED AND ADOPTED by the Kearns Metro Township Council, on this 10th day of
October, 2022.

    REGARDING FUNDING FOR A UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT SERGEANT

This interlocal (“Agreement”) is between the Kearns Metro Township, a municipality and political
subdivision of the State of Utah (“Kearns”), and the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service
Area, a local district and political subdivision of the State of Utah (“SLVLESA”). Kearns and
SLVLESA are collectively referred to as the “Parties” and individually as a “Party.”

                                           RECITALS

   A. SLVLESA levies property taxes for law enforcement services within its jurisdiction and
      manages the funding for such services to its member entities, including Kearns.

   B. SLVLESA contracts with the Unified Police Department of Greater Salt Lake (“UPD”) for
      all law enforcement services within SLVLESA’s jurisdiction, including Kearns.

   C. UPD maintains a precinct in Kearns and UPD’s Investigation Unit has one (1) sergeant,
      seven-and-a-half (7.5) detectives, four (4) school resource officers (“SRO”), and one (1)
      traffic officer, which means that one sergeant currently supervises twelve-and-a-half (12.5)
      officers.

   D. It is difficult for one sergeant to manage twelve-and-a-half (12.5) officers and, as a result,
      UPD has been utilizing the day shift patrol sergeant to manage the four (4) SROs, which has
      not been as effective as Kearns and UPD would like.

   E. UPD proposes to convert one (1) detective who is currently used for community-oriented
      policing (“COP”) into a sergeant position (the “New Sergeant”). The New Sergeant will
      then assume all COP duties and supervise a total of six (6) officers, including UPD’s one
      (1) traffic officer, one (1) high risk youth detective, and all four (4) SROs.

   F. With this change, the officers and detectives will have more accessibility to their
      supervisors, which will improve the communication and mentorship through closer working
      relationships.

   G. This change will also help the New Sergeant focus on job performance by allowing them to
      supervise officers and detectives with similar tasks, which will also improve the
      effectiveness of the SROs.


                                                 3
   H. Converting the detective into the New Sergeant position will require an increase in
      compensation and benefits, which are estimated to be about $31,000 annually with possible
      cost adjustments.

   I. Kearns desires to provide certain funding for SLVLESA to use in funding the increase in
      compensation and benefits that will result from converting the detective position into the
      New Sergeant.

   J. The Parties are public agencies and are authorized by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation Act,
      Utah Code § 11-13-101 to -608, to enter into agreements to cooperate with each other in a
      manner which will enable them to make the most efficient use of their resources and
      powers.

THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

    1. CONSIDERATION AND AUTHORITY:

       a. Kearns will provide SLVLESA $100,000.00 in funding to pay for the difference
          between the detective’s current compensation and benefits and the New Sergeant’s
          compensation and benefits (the “Funds”), which SLVLESA will deposit into its
          restricted precinct fund balance.

       b. SLVLESA’s signatory below certifies that the signatory has all necessary authority to
          bind SLVLESA and accept the Funds from Kearns.

       c. SLVLESA will include the costs associated with the New Sergeant and the Funds
          provided by Kearns for the increases compensation and benefits associated with the
          New Sergeant in its tentative budgets for fiscal years 2023, 2024, and 2025. It is
          understood that the UPD may, at any time, for any reason, change personnel, including
          the number of sergeants in the Kearns Precinct. If the need to fund a cost difference
          between a detective and a New Sergeant no longer exists due to changes by UPD and
          is not included in the SLVLESA budget, SLVLESA will remit unspent funds in the
          restricted fund balance to Kearns within 30 days after adoption of the final budget which
          does not include funding for the increased costs of the reclassification described above.

    2. ALLOWABLE USES: SLVLESA will only use the Funds to cover the increases in
       compensation, benefits, and other applicable employee cost adjustments associated with
       converting the detective into the New Sergeant, provided that Kearns will not be
       responsible for providing additional funding for the New Sergeant during the Term of this
       Agreement, as defined in paragraph 7.f.

    3. AUDITS:

      a. SLVLESA will ensure that its annual audit encompasses SLVLESA’s use of the Funds
         and will provide a copy of the audit when available to Kearns. In its sole discretion,
         Kearns may hire a qualified certified public accountant to perform an independent audit
                                                4
    of SLVLESA’s use of the Funds at its own cost and expense and will reimburse
    SLVLESA for any reasonable and documented costs SLVLESA may incur as a result of
    the independent audit, unless the audit determines SLVLESA misspent the Funds.

 a. SLVLESA will fully cooperate with Kearns or any other entity having jurisdiction over
    the Parties in any investigations or audits into the use of the Funds.

4. RETURN OF THE FUNDS AND TERMINATION:

 a. If any audit determines that SLVLESA expended the funds in violation of this
    Agreement or any applicable law, Kearns may terminate this Agreement in writing and
    require SLVLESA to return the remaining portion of the Funds plus any misspent Funds
    identified in the audit. SLVLESA will return such Funds to Kearns within thirty (30)
    calendar days of the date Kearns submits written notice requesting the return of the
    Funds. Provided, however, that if either Party disputes the findings of an audit, the
    Parties will follow the dispute resolution process set forth in paragraph 6. The triggering
    of the dispute resolution process or the subsequent commencement of litigation between
    the Parties regarding the results of an audit will stay any obligation of SLVLESA under
    this subparagraph 4.a to return all or a portion of the Funds to Kearns.

 b. Unless the Parties otherwise agree in writing and in addition to the provisions of
    Paragraph 1.c, SLVLESA will return to Kearns any unused portion of the Funds within
    thirty (30) calendar days of: (1) December 31, 2025, the end of the final fiscal year
    contemplated in this Agreement; or (2) UPD does not maintain the New Sergeant’s
    reclassification.

 c. Kearns may terminate the Agreement in writing and require compliance with any
    outstanding obligations under this Agreement if SLVLESA does not approve a final
    budget that does not include funding for the increased costs of the New Sergeant’s
    reclassification or UPD does not maintain the New Sergeant classification.

5. RECORDS, REPORTING, AND TRANSPARANCY: During the Term of this
   Agreement, UPD will retain documentation of all uses of the Funds.

  a. By January 31 of 2024 and 2025, SLVLESA will provide Kearns with a written
     accounting that: (1) includes a summary of how SLVLESA expended the Funds during
     the preceding fiscal year; (2) the portion of the Funds SLVLESA anticipates spending
     for the New Sergeant during the current fiscal year; and (3) the remaining balance of
     the Funds.

  b. Within thirty (30) day of the termination of this Agreement for any reason, SLVLESA
     will submit a final written report accounting for the final month of expenditures and
     provide a general summary of the total expenditures under this Agreement. If the
     Agreement is not terminated before December 31, 2025, SLVLESA will include a
     written accounting for fiscal year 2025 in the final written report that includes a
     summary of how SLVLESA expended the funds during the 2025 fiscal year.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: If a dispute between the Parties related to this Agreement
   arises, the Parties will submit the matter to formal mediation before any judicial action may
   be initiated thereon, unless an immediate court order is needed or a statute of limitations
   period will run before mediation can be reasonably completed. The Parties will endeavor
   to reasonably agree upon a mediator and will mediate the dispute in good faith. Each Party
   will be responsible for their own costs and will split the cost of the mediator between them
   equally. If the Parties do not agree upon a mediator, each Party will name a mediator and
   those two mediators will name a third mediator. The Parties will be bound to mediate the
   dispute with the third mediator.

7. CHOICE OF LAW: This Agreement will be governed by the laws, rules, and regulations
   of the State of Utah. Any action or proceeding arising from this Agreement will be brought
   in a court of competent jurisdiction in the State of Utah. Venue will be in Salt Lake County,
   in the Third Judicial District Court for Salt Lake County.

8. INTERLOCAL COOPERATION ACT REQUIREMENTS: The Parties shall comply with
   the following requirements:

    a. This Agreement will be authorized by resolution of the governing body of each Party
       pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-202.5;

    b. This Agreement will be reviewed as to proper form and compliance with applicable
       law by a duly authorized attorney on behalf of each Party, pursuant to Utah Code § 11-
       13-202.5;

    c. A duly executed original counterpart of this Agreement will be filed with the keeper
       of records of each Party, pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-209;

    d. Except as otherwise specifically provided herein, each Party will be responsible for its
       own costs and expenses hereunder, and for any financing of such costs; and

    e. No separate legal entity is created by the terms of this Agreement. To the extent this

       administered by Kearns, pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-207 and, to the extent
       necessary, voting must be based upon one vote per party pursuant to Utah Code § 11-

       this Agreement, such Party must do so in the same manner that it deals with other
       property of such Party.

    f. This Agreement will not take effect until: (1) the Parties have approved the Agreement
       pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-202(2); (2) the attorney authorized to represent each
       Party has approved the Agreement as to form pursuant to Utah Code § 11-13-202.5;
