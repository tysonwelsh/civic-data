 2.      A presentation from the Sign Committee regarding the development and adoption
         of a nighttime brightness standard for on premises electronic digital signs. (19-102)
         (1:26:33)
             ● Sign ordinances for night time brightness
                    ○ look to adopt a new ordinance around these
                           ■ Section 60.06.015 and adds three zones
                           ■ LZ1 zone: low ambient light
                           ■ LZ2: moderate ambient light
                           ■ LZ3: moderately high ambient zones
                    ○ Starts 30 minutes after sunset and 30 minutes before sunrise
                    ○ Moving from the old standard of light measuring from illuminance to
                       luminance
                           ■ Luminance is measured with a NIT gun
                           ■ illuminance requires two measures and not as accurate
                    ○ will be bright back to the night of the 30th with an ordinance
