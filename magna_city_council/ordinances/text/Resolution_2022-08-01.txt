Letter of Guarantee for Station 102, located at 8609 West Magna Main Street, Magna, Utah attached hereto as
Exhibit A.

SUBJECT:
Unified Fire Service Area – Station 102 Rebuild
8609 W Magna Main St, Magna, UT 84044

Unified Fire Service Area, a political subdivision of the State of Utah, hereby guarantees installation of the
following described improvements, under the direction and supervision of and in accordance with the
specifications of the municipal Engineer, of all required improvements particularly described herein within (1) one
year from the date of this letter, which improvements (public improvements only) must be free of defects for a
period of one (1) year after their final acceptance by Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District, normal wear and
tear excepted. These improvements are required under permit #NCC21-1119 and approved by Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District for the Unified Fire Service Area – Station 102 Rebuild located at approximately 8609 W
Magna Main St, Magna, UT 84044. Unified Fire Service Area has dedicated the amount of $123,219 in the Public
Trust Investment Fund (PTIF) account #8940 for purposes of meeting the performance bond.

As per approved site plan for Commercial New Construction Permit NCC21-1119, Unified Fire
Service Area – Station 102 Rebuild. (See itemized list attached as Exhibit A).

All improvements shall be completed to identified specifications and will include repair or replacement of any
damage to said improvements that may occur during construction. This Letter of Guarantee is furnished in
consideration of the waiver by Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District of a performance bond in the amount
of $123,219 to guarantee installation of the above-described improvements.
