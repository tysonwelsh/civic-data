OPENING:
     Mayor Randall called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was offered by Reverend Alex Wilkie and The Pledge of Allegiance to the
     Flag was led by Councilmember Smethurst.

CANVASS OF THE 2021 MUNICIPAL GENERAL ELECTION:
     Melanie Abplanalp with the Washington County Clerk’s office mentioned the City has
     51,930 registered voters. She then outlined the official general election results as
     follows:

      For Mayor:
            Michele Randall              11,614         55.18%
            Jimmie B. Hughes              9,424         44.82%

      For City Council:
             Greg Aldred                  9,500         24.30%
             Vardell H Curtis             8,992         23.00%
             Natalie Larsen              10,384         26.56%
             Michelle Tanner             10,216         26.13%

      Ms. Abplanalp spoke about overvotes and undervotes for this election. She then
      stated that 18,365 ballots were counted on election night; however, 2,873 eligible
      ballots were added for a total of 21,188 ballots which is a 40.80% voter turnout.
      There were 1,258 ballots that were not able to be counted: 339 were returned past
      the deadline of November 1st, 688 were returned as undeliverable, and 231 were
      returned unsigned or had signatures that did not match voter records – voters were
      contacted but did not cure the situation. In person voting had 21 provisional ballots,
      all of which were counted and 368 residents showed up to vote in person.

      Mayor Randall congratulated Councilmembers Elect Natalie Larsen and Michelle
      Tanner. She noted the swearing in will take place at noon on January 3, 2022.

      MOTION:
          A motion was made by Councilmember Curtis to approve Canvass of
                    the 2021 Municipal General Election as presented.
