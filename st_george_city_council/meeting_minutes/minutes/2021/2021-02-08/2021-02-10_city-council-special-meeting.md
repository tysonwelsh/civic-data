OPENING:
     Mayor Randall welcomed all in attendance. She turned the time over to City Manager
     Adam Lenhard.

VOTE FOR NEW COUNCILMEMBER:
     Vote for new City Councilmember between the finalists from the meeting held
     on Monday, February 8, 2021 to correct procedural errors in the process
     selecting the applicant for the vacant City Council position.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard read a statement summarizing the process that was
      followed at the meeting held on February 8, 2021, the concerns with that process, and
      what the process will be for tonight’s meeting. When trying to decide which part of the
      process to redo, staff determined to roll the process back to the point where the first
      deviation from state statute can be identified. The first vote resulted in a 2-1-1
      outcome. Councilmember McArthur voted for Mr. Erickson, Councilmembers Larkin and
      Smethurst voted for Vardell Curtis, and Councilmember Hughes voted for Natalie
      Larsen. He asked City Recorder Christina Fernandez if that is what the record reflects.

      Ms. Fernandez, replied yes, that is what the record shows.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained since this a valid first vote, this is where the
      Council needs to resume deliberations. He read Utah Code 20A-1-510(1)(C)(ii) which
      states “The two individuals having the highest number of votes of the municipal
      legislative body after a first vote is taken shall appear before the municipal legislative
      body and the municipal legislative body shall vote again.” With the vote being 2-1-1,
      there is clearly one (1) top vote getter – Mr. Curtis. The statute is not clear on how to
      break the tie to determine the second top vote getter. At the previous meeting, the
      Council tried to determine the second top vote getter by voting again; however, the
      second vote resulted in the same results, a 2-1-1 outcome. The Council should have
      moved Mr. Curtis forward as he was the highest vote getter. At this point, the Council
      needed to resolve the remaining tie between Mrs. Larsen and Mr. Erickson. On Monday,
      a coin was flipped; however, the utilization of a game of chance is not contemplated by
      statute until the final deciding vote between the two (2) top vote getters. Rather than

      doing a coin flip, Mr. Lenhard asked the Council to vote again between Mr. Erickson and
      Mrs. Larsen.

      City Recorder Christina Fernandez explained that each of the Councilmembers have two
      (2) ballots. She asked the Council to fill out ballot 1, writing the name of the candidate
      they would like to see advance to the next round, between Natalie Larsen and Rick
      Erickson.

      Councilmember Hughes asked if staff is 100% sure the process for tonight’s meeting is
      correct.

      City Attorney Shawn Guzman advised at this point, the only way to determine narrowing
      down to the two (2) top vote getters it to take a vote.

      City Recorder Christina Fernandez read Ballot 1 as follows:

      Councilmember McArthur votes for M Rick Erickson
      Councilmember Smethurst votes for Natalie Larsen
      Councilmember Larkin votes for Natalie Larsen
      Councilmember Hughes votes for Natalie Larsen

      Ms. Fernandez asked the Council to confirm she read the votes correctly; the Council
      replied yes.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard explained, now that the Council reduced the field to the two
      (2) highest vote getters as required by statute, it is time for the final deciding vote. He
      read Utah Code 20A-1-510(1)(C)(iii) which states: “If neither candidate receives a
      majority vote of the municipal legislative body at that time, the vacancy shall be filled
      by lot in the presence of the municipal legislative body.” The Council will now vote for
      either Mr. Curtis or Mrs. Larsen. If there is a tie in this round, the Mayor shall exercise
      her right to break the tie and the winner will fill the remaining term of the vacant
      Council seat created by Michele Randall’s appointment to the vacant Mayor seat. He
      clarified that a majority vote is three (3) or more votes. If neither candidate receives a
      majority vote, the vacancy shall be filled by lot which was determined to be a coin flip.

      City Recorder Christina Fernandez read Ballot 2 as follows:

      Councilmember Larkin votes for Vardell Curtis
      Councilmember Smethurst votes for Vardell Curtis
      Councilmember McArthur votes for Vardell Curtis
      Councilmember Hughes votes for Natalie Larsen

      Ms. Fernandez asked the Council to confirm she read the votes correctly; the Council
      replied yes.

      City Manager Adam Lenhard asked the Council to make a motion, a second, and formal
      vote that reflects the results as read by the City Recorder.

APPOINT NEW COUNCILMEMBER:
     MOTION:
         A motion was made by Councilmember McArthur to appoint Vardell
               Curtis for the position of City Council to fill Mayor Randall’s remaining
               term.
     SECOND:
         The motion was seconded by Councilmember Smethurst.
     VOTE:
         Mayor Randall called for a roll call vote, as follows:

                    Councilmember Hughes – aye
                    Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                    Councilmember McArthur – aye
                    Councilmember Larkin – aye

                    The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.

      Mayor Randall stated Vardell Curtis will fill her remaining term which will end January ,
      2022. She commented this was not an easy process and that she regrets it had to be
      done twice; however, state statute had to be followed.
