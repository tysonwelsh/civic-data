                                          RECITALS

        A.     Riverton City approved the Development Agreement For Mountain View Place
At Riverton dated December 1, 2015, as recorded on March 24, 2017 in the office of the Salt
Lake County Recorder’s Office under Entry #12502237, Book 10541 Page 2871-2970, as
amended by that certain First Amendment to Development Agreement for Mountain View Place
at Riverton dated September 7, 2021, as recorded in the office of the Salt Lake County
Recorder’s Office on July 14, 2022, as Entry No. 13985081 in Book 11356 at Page 4941, of the
Official Records (“First Amendment,” and collectively, the “Agreement”). That project consists
of approximately eighty-six (86) acres of land located in the City of Riverton, Salt Lake County,
Utah and adjacent to the Mountain View Corridor and generally bounded by 13400 South,
Mountain View Corridor, and 13200 South.

       B.     Per a request by the Developer and pursuant to the terms of the Agreement, the
City has approved this second amendment to the Agreement.

       C.      The Agreement is hereby amended as follows:
            a. Section 5.4 of the Agreement is amended to allow up to 302 residential dwelling
               units on the site.
            b. Section 1.1 of the Mountain View Place Specific Development Plan, Exhibit “B”
               to the Agreement, is amended to allow 302 residential units.
            c. The avoidance of doubt, the following terms from the First Amendment are not
               being amended or modified hereby: (i) the elimination of the requirement
               residential units be "located above main floor retail; and (ii) amendment to
               Section 2.2.2, Village Center, of the Mountain View Place Specific Development
               Plan, Exhibit "B" to the Agreement, to stipulate that the approved residential
               project is not considered part of the "Village Center".
   D. This Second Amendment shall not be construed to amend, alter, or otherwise modify any
      other terms or provisions of the Agreement.
