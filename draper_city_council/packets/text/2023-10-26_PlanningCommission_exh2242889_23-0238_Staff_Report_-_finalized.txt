BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of an Amended Subdivision Plat for
approximately 0.81 acres located on the south side of 12450 S., at approximately 114 E
12450 S. (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned CC. The applicant is requesting
that an Amended Subdivision plat be approved to allow for the existing office building to
be subdivided into commercial condominiums through approval and recordation of the
proposed Draper Medical Condominiums Plat.

The office building was built in 2003 on Lot 5B of the Stark Office Village Subdivision
recorded in 2002. Access to the subject property from 12450 S. is provided via two
easements across Lot 5A (Exhibit F). Previously the property was part of Lot 5 of the Draper
Crossing Subdivision. Historically the building has contained both medical and dental
offices.

ingress and egress easements across the drive aisles of both properties from the access
drives that connect to 12450 S. on the north all the way to the southern property line of Lot

Shared Parking. Per the Commercial Site Plan approval (Exhibit H) dated December 5, 2001,
the buildings constructed on Lots 5A and 5B are to have shared parking across all lots. The
majority of the overall parking spaces for the combined site are located on the subject
property. This condition of the site plan approval continues to remain in effect. Staff
recommends a note be placed on the amended plat that references this condition of
approval for the site.

Criteria For Approval.
The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat amendment request is
found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by signing
        an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use Authority finds
        that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street, right-of-
        way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

        1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                 Planning, GIS and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of
                 the site, including but not limited to those listed in Exhibit A of the Staff
                 report.
        2.       That the applicant submit a revised plat showing the required delineation of
                 the vertical space of the condominiums to planning staff for review prior to
                 printing of the final Mylar Plat and signature of the Chair of the Planning
                 Commission on the plat.
        3.       That the revised condominium plat note the shared parking requirements of
                 the Site Plan approval with Lot 5A of the Stark Office Village Subdivision.

The findings for approval as are follows:

        1.       Good cause exists for the subdivision plat amendment, and no Public Street,
                 right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

The findings for denial as are follows:

        1.       Good cause does not exist for the subdivision plat amendment.
