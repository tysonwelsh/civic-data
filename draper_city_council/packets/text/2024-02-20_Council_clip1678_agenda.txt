       The Draper City Council may temporarily recess the regular meeting and

7:00 PM BUSINESS MEETING

1.     Call to Order

2.     Pledge of Allegiance

3.     Oath of Office: Draper City Fire Department

4.     Public Comments
       To be considerate of everyone attending the meeting, public comments will be
       restricted to items that are not listed on this or a future agenda and limited to three
       minutes per person. Comments which cannot be made within these limits should be
       submitted in writing to the City Recorder prior to noon the day before the meeting.
       Comments pertaining to an item on the agenda should not be given at this time but
       should be held until that item is called.

5.     Consent Items

       5.a Approval of the February 6, 2024, City Council Meeting Minutes.

       5.b Approval of Resolution #24-14
           A Resolution of the Draper City Council disposing of property seized as
           evidence in accordance with Utah Code Section 77-11a-402. Staff: John Vuyk

       5.c Approval of Resolution #24-13

           A Resolution of the Draper City Council approving Mayor Walker to sign an
           interlocal agreement with Lehi City for Lehi City to construct and maintain
           trails on Draper City Property. Staff report by Rhett Ogden.
6.   Items for Council Consideration

     6.a Action Item: Providing Local Consent for a Single Event Permit for MS
         Brands LLC.Staff report by Travis DeJong

     6.b Public Hearing: Ordinance #1602
         An Ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map of Draper City for
         approximately 1.68 acres of property from CO2 (Professional Office) and CR
         (Regional Commercial) to CR, located at approximately 13673 South 100 East
         within Draper City, otherwise known as the Chick-Fil-A Zoning Map
         Amendment. Staff report by Todd Taylor.

     6.c Public Hearing: Ordinances #1603 and #1604
         An Ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map and Land Use Map of
         Draper City for approximately 1,035.49 acres to remove the properties from
         both the Zoning Map and the Land Use Map. The properties are located at
         approximately 1950 E 16365 South and were part of a municipal boundary
         line adjustment with Lehi City in 2022 and are no longer located within
         Draper City jurisdiction, otherwise known as the City Initiated Draper-Lehi
         Zoning and Land Use Map Amendments. Staff report by Jennifer
         Jastremsky.

     6.d Action Item: Resolution #24-02
         A Resolution authorizing the Mayor to sign a real estate purchase
         agreement for property located at approximately 12695 South 1700 East.
         Staff report by David Dobbins.
