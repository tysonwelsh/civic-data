                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-79

    A RESOLUTION TO APPROVE THE GENERAL SERVICE CONTRACT WITH
    ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER FOR A SERVICE CONNECTION TO THE 11800
               SOUTH WATER METER/PRV VAULT PROJECT

         WHEREAS, the City has effected a bid process to obtain responsive bidders for the
titled project; and

       WHEREAS, the City needs an electrical service connection to the titled project; and

       WHEREAS, the City has received a General Service Contract from Rocky Mountain
Power to allow for the installation of an electrical service connection; and

        WHEREAS, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the health, safety
and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to authorize the General Service Contract that allows for
the electrical service connection required for this project.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. That the City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute the General Service Contract
with Rocky Mountain Power for the electrical service connection for the titled project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 15th day of
December 2020 by the following vote:
