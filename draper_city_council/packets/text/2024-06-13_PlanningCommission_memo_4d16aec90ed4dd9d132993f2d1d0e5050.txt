MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2024-06-13
Re: Public Hearing: Carol Lee Subdivision Plat Amendment and Deviation Request
(Administrative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment and Deviation
for approximately 1.07 acres located on the north side of 12600 South, at approximately
995 East 12600 South. The property is currently zoned TC . The applicant is requesting
that a Subdivision Plat Amendment be approved to allow for two lots to be divided into
three lots and a Deviation to keep the street improvements as they currently are.



Finding for Approval:

  1. The Deviation of curb, gutter, and sidewalk improvements per DCMC Section 17-5-
     030(D) is compatible with the street improvements on 12600 South and 1015 East.
  2. There is good cause for the vacation or amendment, and no public street or
     municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.


Finding for Denial:

  1. The Deviation of curb, gutter, and sidewalk improvements per DCMC Section 17-5-
     030(D) is not compatible with the street improvements on 12600 South and 1015
     East.
  2. There is not good cause for the vacation or amendment.
