  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant requests review and recommendation of the Stack Soccer Field Development Agreement. The purpose
of this development agreement is to secure HTRZ funding for the development of the Stack Soccer Field. This is a
state requirement to get HTRZ funding.

This agreement is similar to the Thanksgiving Station Development Agreement but covers a smaller portion of the
Thanksgiving Point Area Plan. Much of the language and vested rights are the same, however, there are no additional
project milestones.

This is a legislative item. Recent State Legislation requires that these agreements have a public hearing with the Plan-
ning Commission prior to their approval by the City Council.

The DRC reviewed the proposed Development Agreement and had five comments, including the following:

    1.   At time of development a power study is required to determine capacity, and the improvements that will be
         required.
    2.   Specify who at the City will receive the written notice of changes to applicable rules in 5.3 (c).

Please consider all DRC comments in the motion.


  SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Agreements are legislative items at the full discretion of the City Council. The Planning Commission
reviews the Development Agreement and can recommend any changes to the City Council.

The public can also speak to this item and express any concerns or support for the development agreement. The public
can also suggest changes to the language of the agreement.
