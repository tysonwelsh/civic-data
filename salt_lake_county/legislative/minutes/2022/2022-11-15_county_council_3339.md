6.1 First Reading of AN ORDINANCE OF THE SALT LAKE 
COUNTY COUNCIL ENACTING CHAPTER 19.73 OF THE 
SALT LAKE COUNTY CODE ENTITLED FLOOD 
DAMAGE PREVENTION TO MAKE REGULATIONS IN 
THE UNINCORPORATED COUNTY OUTSIDE OF A 
MIGRATORY BIRD PRODUCTION AREA CONSISTENT 
WITH FEMA REGULATIONS; AMENDING SECTION 
19.74.040 ENTITLED AREAS OF SPECIAL FLOOD 
HAZARD TO CLARIFY THAT CHAPTER 19.74 IS ONLY 
APPLICABLE TO MIGRATORY BIRD PRODUCTION 
AREAS; AND ENACTING RELATED REGULATIONS.
Formal adoption may occur at the November 22, 2022, 
Council Meeting.
22-1080
Attachments: Staff Report
SLCo Floodplain Ordinance_final RAFL
Floodplain Ordinance Staff Report, Combined SLCo 
Council Staff Report-v10.18.22
Flood Plain Ordinance Presentation, SL Co
Mr. Brian Tucker , Planning Manager, Municipal Services District (MSD), 
stated there was already a version of this flood prevention ordinance in 
Chapter 19.74 of the Salt Lake County Code.  This ordinance would adopt 
Chapter 19.73 of the Code, which is an updated version of the flood 
Page 2 of 6
County Council Meeting Minutes November 15, 2022
prevention ordinance.  It would apply to all parts of the County except for 
those that are within migratory bird production areas, in accordance with 
Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) regulations.  
Council Member Bradley  asked if the rider on migratory bird production 
areas put the MSD at a disadvantage. 
Mr. Tucker stated he felt it did not.
Mr. Zach Shaw , Deputy District Attorney, stated the District Attorney ’s 
office has worked closely with FEMA on this ordinance, including the 
migratory bird component.  FEMA is satisfied the existing ordinance is 
adequate for its purposes.  
A motion was made by Council Member Bradley, seconded by Council 
Member Granato, that this agenda item be forwarded to the November 22, 
2022, Council Meeting for final consideration. The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.
