TIP Modification: Elk Ridge Drive/SR164 Intersection Improvement – Bob Allen, MAG Elk Ridge Drive is an existing TIP project that connects SR-198 and SR-164. UDOT has participated in the project by funding traffic signals on both ends of the project. It was determined that an additional northbound left turn lane is needed at the intersection of Elk Ridge Drive and SR-164. This will require an additional westbound receiving lane on SR-164 between the intersection and an additional northbound left turn lane. UDOT Region 3 proposes to add $700,000 of funding to install the additional turning and receiving lanes. The additional funding is UDOT Transportation Solutions funds that MAG does not control. Current Project Value $17,269,050 New UDOT Transportation Solutions Funds $700,000 New Project Value $17,969,050 Richard Nielsen moved to recommend that the TIP be modified to add $700,00 to the Elk Ridge Drive project. Brad Kenison seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. 2020 MAG Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Final Funding and Project Adoption – Bob Allen, MAG The MPO funds projects in the TIP every two years. The Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) and Regional Planning Committee (RPC) created a final prioritized project list September 3rd following the process established by RPC. RPC must approve the funding scenario for the final prioritized project list and add those projects to the TIP. MPO Technical Advisory Committee September 28, 2020 Page 3 MPO staff have created a funding scenario. There are approximately $85M in funds that are primarily available in 2023-2024. The scenario includes an additional $20M to be added to the contingency fund due to rising construction costs. Brad Kension moved that the final funding scenario be approved as presented and the funded projects be added to the TIP. Chris Trusty seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
