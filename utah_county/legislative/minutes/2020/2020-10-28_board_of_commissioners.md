COMMISSIONER LEE — ANNOUNCED OCTOBER’S EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH —
CHRIS SAINSBURY. READ NOMINATION:

To Whom It May Concern:

This letter is to nominate Sgt. Chris Sainsbury for employee of the month. Sgt. Sainsbury
currently supervises the Jail Industries program and has done a phenomenal job. He hasn’t been
with us long, but with what little time, he has made Jail Industries the coveted place to work. I’ve
watched deputies come out of the woodwork, hoping to join our team and I believe Sgt.
Sainsbury has a lot to do with that. His positive attitude and zest for life are contagious.

Sgt. Sainsbury seems to have made the same impression on inmates. Whenever | go across the
street to the jail, I’m bombarded and begged by the inmates to come out to Jail Industries. I’m
sure this too has a lot to do with the affable and fun atmosphere Set. Sainsbury has urged his
team to have. Sgt. Sainsbury has brought in recreational equipment including a Bowflex which
was donated for the inmates to use.

Due to Covid-19, the number of inmates in our program has dropped significantly. We haven’t
been sending our workers out to participating companies in fear they will contract the virus and
bring it back. This has not stopped Sgt. Sainsbury from getting work done. This year’s garden and
greenhouse have been a success. We have a nice new tractor and other tools to make our work
more efficient. Sgt. Sainsbury has overseen some nice upgrades in and around our office building.
He is in the process of putting together a program where inmates will help aid the adoption of
animals at the Shelter. He’s also recently asked the RISE Program to teach our inmates.

Before coming out to Jail Industries, | worked under Sgt. Sainsbury, so | say this with confidence.
He is the best Sergeant I’ve worked under during my 12 plus years here with the Sheriff’s Office.
One of many things | appreciate about Sgt. Sainsbury is he takes time to listen. Not just with staff
or volunteers, he takes time to listen to everyone. I’ve witnessed on numerous occasions inmates
thanking him for listening and taking the time to be heard. I’ve witnessed grateful volunteers
because Sgt. Sainsbury has accommodated them. I’ve noticed folks enjoying his tours and
recognize the time he puts into that. I’ve witnessed Sergeants and Deputies lined up outside his
office, just to shoot the breeze, I’m one of them. He treats people like people.

| thought it was cool when Sgt. Sainsbury came to Jail Industries; he said it was important to
him to make Jail Industries the best place to work in the entire department. From where I’ve
been, he’s done just that. Moral is up and stress is down. Sgt. Sainsbury knows the importance in
that. He is educated, ethical, and works well with others. One of the most important quality he
possesses is that he understands people. This understanding is very beneficial with his position.
Before he came over, I thought he’d be a great fit. He’s a team player who will go to bat for you.
He will advocate for you even if it means losing you on his crew as you advance in your career. |
know this because he’s done it for me.

There are plenty of things I’ve left out of this nomination letter that Sgt Sainsbury has done (like

all the career path evals he’s done) and other great qualities he possesses (like he’s very

-PJ Maughan

COMMISSIONER AINGE — ANNOUNCED JUNE’S EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH - RAY
ORMOND. READ NOMINATION:

| would Like to nominate Deputy Ray Ormond for employee of the month.

On 2/21/20 Ray and | were dispatched to a house fire up Hobble Creek Canyon for a fully
engulfed home. Upon arrival we were advised the home would be a total loss. We remained on
scene to assist fire and crowd control. While on scene the roof of the garage collapsed with 3
Springville Firefighters inside. A mayday was called by fire and Deputy Ormond sprang into
action. Ray and the Springville Fire Chief rand to the back side of the garage and started to break
down a man door to hopefully gain access to the Firefighters. Upon breaching the door Ray and
the Fire Chief were able to locate the down Firefighters. Upon finding them oxygen tanks and
rescue equipment were requested, Ray immediately ran to assist in gather oxygen tanks and
equipment. Upon returning two of the Fire Fighters were assisted out of the building while one
was pinned under a ceiling truss. In attempt to free the other Firefighter Ray ran into the burning
garage with several others in an attempt to lift the truss off the Fire Fighter. A Fire Rescue team
soon responded and asked for more equipment, once again Ray took off running. After the rescue
team freed the Firefighter, Ray called for Life Flight and began setting up a landing zone. Once Life
Flight landed Ray also assisted in getting the injured Fire Fighter in the helicopter.

In conclusion | am nominating Ray Ormond for Employee of the Month for his heroic and
selfless act in the rescue of a trapped Firefighter inside a burning building. Ray is an outstanding
asset to Utah County and the Sheriff Office, a great team member and friend.

Sincerely Carson Holm
