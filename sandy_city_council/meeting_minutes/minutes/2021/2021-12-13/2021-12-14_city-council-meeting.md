Work Session Items

Informational Items


1.         21-485                 City Council receiving a presentation and update on the Point of the
                                  Mountain
                                  Alan Matheson, Executive Director with the Point, provided an overview of the Point
                                  Development,a 600 acre, leading and innovative planned community located at the Point
                                  of the Mountain. He reviewed the guiding principles, key vision elements, and goals for
                                  the Point. Council questions and comments followed.

2.         21-486                 Administration providing the Council with an update on Google Fiber

                   Attachments:   Google Fiber Update.pdf

                                  Jacob Brace, with Google Fiber, provided an update on the implementation and
                                  installation of the Google Fiber network in Sandy. He discussed the impact on Sandy and
                                  the coordination of work with Sandy City. Mr. Brace believes the first service in Sandy will
                                  begin in the first quarter of 2022. Council questions and comments followed.

                                  Council moved to Time Certain Items and heard General Citizen Comments.

3.         21-489                 Council Member Christensen providing the Council with an update on Hale
                                  Centre Theatre
                                  This agenda item will be heard at a future Council Meeting, date to be determined.


4.         21-487                 Council office providing a briefing on the process for filling a midterm
                                  council vacancy
                   Attachments:   Memorandum.pdf

                                  Dustin Fratto, Council Assistant Director, provided an overview of the process for filling a
                                  midterm Council vacancy. District 4 seat will be vacated when the current Council
                                  Member, Monica Zoltanski, gets sworn in as the Mayor of Sandy City. The City Council
                                  will need to appoint a resident of District 4 to fill the seat and serve the remainder of the
                                  term. The Council will need to interview every qualified applicant. Council questions and
                                  comments followed. Council gave staff direction regarding the steps moving forward
                                  regarding the Council appointment process.

Consent Calendar

                                  A motion was made by Brooke Christensen, seconded by Monica Zoltanski to
                                  approve the Consent Calendar. The motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Marci Houseman thanked the police officers and the school resource
                    officers for their service to the community to ensure a safe, learning environment for our
                    children. The Ballet West performance of the Nutcracker at Mt. Jordan Middle School
                    was wonderful. She thanked Parks and Recreation for coordinating the Santa snowman
                    scavenger hunt for our residents.

                    Council Member Monica Zoltanski recognized police Officer Graham Tinius for helping
                    with the first response efforts in Kentucky. She acknowledged Mayor Bradburn for his
                    service, dedication and commitment to the preservation of open space in Sandy. She
                    thanked him for his leadership over the last four very challenging years and offered her
                    deep appreciation for his service, innovation, guidance and pay increases for police and
                    fire. She also thanked his family for their service and wished the Mayor and his family her
                    best.

                    Council Member Zoltanski expressed her gratitude to her District 4 residents. It has been
                    an honor to serve you. District 4 is comprised of diverse neighborhoods which serve to
                    strengthen our neighborhoods. She thanked the residents for keeping her informed and for
                    making her a stronger and better representative which will help her to become a better
                    Mayor. She invited the community to attend the swearing in ceremony on January 3rd at
                    the Alta High School Performing Arts Center. Ms. Zoltanski expressed her hope that she
                    would make the residents of Sandy proud, serving as their Mayor.

                    Council Member Zach Robinson acknowledged the Communications team for recent
                    messaging posted related to street parking during the winter.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey wished all a Happy Holidays. It is a pleasure serving with
                    all of you.

                    Council Member Brooke Christensen acknowledged Mayor Bradburn for his service and
                    echoed Ms. Zoltanski's comments. She also thanked Matt Huish for his service and
                    wished him well in his new position.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud thanked the Public Works department for their response
                    during the recent snow storm. She also acknowledged the Police Department for their
                    efforts and enforcement of the speed zones.




Mayor's Report
