2.    Deer Acres Plat C 6-lot Subdivision - Final Plat - Jaren and Alyssa Lynn
      a.      Staff Presentation
      Shauna presented the Final Plat for the Deer Acres Plat C Subdivision. She stated that
      the biggest concern they’ve faced is to meet the Utah State requirement for water
      pressure at the water meter. Shauna said she reached out to Sunrise Engineering, who
      holds the hydraulic model for the City, and had them look at what Brett Ludlow,
      developer engineer, proposed as an elevation for where the water meters need to be set
      in order to have the state-required water pressure. She also presented a draft of a notice
      prepared by Kasey Wright, City Attorney, regarding this subject.

      b.       Commission Discussion
      Fran Petersen asked how homeowners will be held responsible if booster pumps are not
      allowed. Shauna replied that there will be an elevation at which homes can be built. The
      homeowner will need to make sure that their grade level reaches that elevation. Shauna
      pointed out that the City does not currently have a slope ordinance, so IRC requirements
      will be followed. Heather Robertson asked why individual pumps are prohibited. Shauna
      answered that there is a potential for cross-contamination. Brett Ludlow explained PSI
      requirements, and Shauna stated that the City will be recommending an engineered
      retaining wall.

      c.     Action Item - Motion

     Ann asked for a motion to accept Deer Acres Plat C Subdivision,
     with the appropriate attached notes, warnings, etc., and anything else that is determined
     to be necessary between the engineers and the City.

     Motion: Cory Thomson         Second: Karl Brough             Opposed: Fran Petersen
     A voice vote was taken. Motion carries with the majority in favor.

6.   Rezone Request - Arrow Builders (Ben & Louis Allred) to rezone their property
     parcel #XA00-0670-2 located at approximately 431 N. Main Street from the CU-1
     zone to the CU-2 zone.
     a.     Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the Public Hearing at 7:34 pm.

     b.     Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented a rezone request being made for a property currently in the CU-1
     zone. She explained that the CU-1 zone used to allow for a 4-plex. Because of a recent
     change in code, this is no longer allowed. Shauna pointed out that the entire city block is
     residential, and feels this property should be the same. She emphasized that, if rezoned,
     any land use included in the CU-2 would be allowed- including a 4-plex.

     c.      Commission Discussion - Close Public Hearing
     Fran asked about the difference in height requirements between the two zones. Shauna
     confirmed that 6 stories, or 75 feet, is allowed in the CU-2 zone. She also confirmed that
     the CU-1 zone allows for 3 stories, or 45 feet, with a commercial building on the ground
     level. Ann, Fran, & Heather expressed concern over the 75 foot allowance in the CU-2
     zone, and stated they didn’t remember deciding on that. Donald Ball claimed that his
     understanding was that nothing would be built on Main Street between 900 North and
     700 South that was higher than 45 feet, to which Ann agreed. Ann would like Shauna to
     ask the City Council about the height discrepancy, and would like to deny or table this
     rezone request until the issue is resolved.

     Rod Steele addressed the Commission, stating that his family owns quite a bit of
     property on this city block. He spoke against having a tall building, which he feels would
     decrease his property value. Brittany Ford expressed concern over the limited space
     available for adequate parking. Brett Ludlow stated that UDOT has already allowed them
     a 30 foot access that meets UDOT standards. Tricia Steele stated that the sewer meets
     in her son’s backyard, and that they’ve had a lot of problems for several years including
     sewage recently backing up into the house. Louise McPherson encouraged the
     Commissioners to drive past the lot and see that there is not enough room for parking on
     this lot. Shauna reminded the Commission that they are not approving a site plan
     tonight. Brittany Ford heard that the property owners have already tied into the sewer,
     even with the existing problems. Ben Allred confirmed that they had a permit that
     allowed for the extension. Shauna stated that she will check with Kyle Marchant
     regarding this matter. Louis Allred said he thinks it would have the least impact going
     commercial with residential on top of it. Brett Ludlow said they did a full site plan about a
     year ago, and it met the code requirements at the time. Brett explained that UDOT
     allows one entrance per lot.
     Ann closed the Public Hearing at 8:04 pm and asked if there was any other discussion
     from the Commission. Heather expressed concern over the 6 story allowance in the CU-
     2 zone and would like to investigate the issue further. Fran believes the zone change
     would not be in harmony with the surrounding area and elects to leave the property
     zoned as CU-1, but would also like to investigate the CU-2 height allowance. Cory said
     he would prefer to continue the public hearing until the Commission can get an answer
     on 75 foot height requirements in the CU-2 zone.

     d.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to continue the Public Hearing, rather than closing it.

     Motion: Cory Thomson      Second: Heather Robertson     Outcome: Passed Unanimously

     their property parcel #XA00-2588-22 located at approximately 761 W. Sheep Lane
     (2300 S.) from the HC-1 zone to the ID-3 (Light Industrial) zone.

     a.    Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the Public Hearing at 8:10 pm.

     b.      Staff Presentation
     Shauna pointed out that the property owned by Mad Dog Knives is currently zoned as
     Highway Commercial 1. This zone does not currently allow any industrial manufacturing
     of things. She said that she discussed two options with the owner, and he said he was
     fine with either option. The Commission can either vote to include “light industrial” as a
     use in the Highway Commercial 1 zone, or rezone the property to Light Industrial ID-3.

     c.     Commission Discussion - Close Public Hearing

     Ann stated that because of the location, she would rather just rezone the property to
     Light Industrial ID-3. Brett Ludlow stated that it will be very limited industrial, with 4
     employees and online sales. Donald Ball said it wouldn’t ideally be zoned as Highway
     Commercial anyway. Ann closed the Public Hearing at 8:14 pm.

     d.    Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept the rezone to ID-3.

     Motion: Fran Petersen Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Passed Unanimously

8.   10.8A.2 Use Regulations HC-1 Zone: Proposal to add additional uses to the HC-1
     Zone, including Banking and Financial Institutions, Higher Learning Institutions
     and Facilities, and Light Industrial Uses.

     a.    Open Public Hearing
     Ann opened the Public Hearing at 8:17 pm.

     b.       Staff Presentation
     Shauna presented some additional uses that could fit into the HC-1 Zone. She read the
     definition of HC-1 Zone from the code.

     c.       Commission Discussion - Close Public Hearing
     Donald Ball asked what the difference is between a place which is manufacturing
     cookies and a place that is manufacturing hamburgers, or sandwiches, or anything that
     is to be consumed. Ann asked if they could just strike “light industrial uses” from the
     code. Fran asked if anyone has a problem with the definition that Shauna just read.
     Discussion followed about the meaning of “industrial.” Shauna suggested a new
     definition called “Light Assembly.” Karl suggested looking into how other cities are
     classifying these types of businesses. Cory asked if we add this into the Highway

     someone in Highway Commercial that wants to stay Highway Commercial. Ann closed
     the Public Hearing at 8:28 pm.

     d.      Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to add the additional uses as written, with the exception of “light
     industrial.” More questions were asked and further discussion was had about this topic.
     Recording and transcription available for full conversation.
     Heather Robertson made a motion that we include in Highway Commercial Zone 1
     Banking and Financial Institutions, Higher Learning Institutions and Facilities, and Light
     Industrial Uses.

     Motion: Heather Robertson Second: Karl Brough
     Nay: Ann Peterson & Cory Thomson (Roll call was taken)
     Outcome: Motion Carries 3-2

9.    Title 10 Updates: Updating required minimum density, lot size and frontage in the
      R-3 residential zone when multi-family dwellings are built vertically.

      a.    Open Public Hearing
      Ann opened the Public Hearing at 8:39 pm.

      b.        Staff Presentation
      Shauna presented a rendering that she and Kyle Marchant came up with for 55%
      required & a 45% land discount. Heather stated that she learned in a recent training that
      if the City is making efforts to make higher density a priority, the City gets to make the
      call. If not, the State gets to make the call. Ann asked the Commissioners which option is
      more appealing. Heather said she would like to discount the least amount possible. Fran
      pointed out that the land discount does incentivize building vertically. Brett Ludlow feels
      that there is a disconnect with the City Council regarding this issue because of the
      inevitable increase in density. Shauna mentioned that she attended the developers
      meeting at the training in Richfield. She said that there was a panel of developers and all
      of them said there is some sort of landscaping requirement on multi-family housing (3
      units and up). Shauna reminded the Commissioners that this is the last meeting to
      discuss this item before passing it on to the City Council. Seth and Shauna will present
      the plan to the City Council. Shauna pointed out that Constitutional rights protect a
      property owner’s rights, not a neighbor’s rights.

      c.     Close Public Hearing
      Ann closed the Public Hearing at 9:08 pm.

      d.     Action Item - Motion
      Ann asked for a straw poll from the Commissioners regarding land discount. Karl Brough
      votes 50%, Fran Petersen votes 55%, Heather Robertson votes 55%, Cory Thomson
      votes 60%, John Ford votes 50%, and Ann Peterson votes 60%. This averages out at
      55%.
      Cory agrees with a statement that Fran made at the last Work Session Meeting, where
      she stated that someone who is renting deserves some green space to enjoy.
      Shauna stated that if you want certain landscaping, you have to give density to make it
      valuable enough for them to give the request.

      Ann asked for a motion to go with a 55% land requirement, with a 45% land discount
      when building vertically.

      Motion: Karl Brough Second: Heather Robertson
      Nay: Cory Thomson (Roll call was taken)
      Outcome: Motion passes with majority vote
