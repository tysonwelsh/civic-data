MCKYA ANDERSON - (Presented talk that she gave at the Mayor’s Freedom Festival
Breakfast)

TWO SUMMERS AGO, I HAD THE OPPORTUNITY TO TRAVEL TO NYC FOR THE
VERY FIRST TIME. I WAS SO EXCITED TO FINALLY VISIT WORLD RENOWNED
AMERICAN MONUMENTS LIKE THE STATUE OF LIBERTY, THE BROOKLYN
BRIDGE, THE WORLD TRADE CENTER AND WALL STREET. AND EVERY SINGLE
ONE OF THOSE SITES WAS INCREDIBLE--EVER YWHERE I TURNED, I SAW
EVIDENCE OF THIS GREAT AND PROSPEROUS COUNTRY. BUT AS I WALKED
THROUGH THE STREETS OF MANHATTAN, I HAD THIS QUESTION IN THE
BACK OF MY MIND. WHAT IS IT THAT MAKES AMERICA SO GREAT?

EVERYWHERE I WENT IN NY, I LOOKED FOR THE ANSWER. BUT

THAT I JUST REFERRED TO. NO, THE ANSWER WAS IN A PLACE I LEAST
EXPECTED. I FOUND IT IN AN OLD, COLONIAL-ERA STONE CHURCH.

IT'S CALLED ST PAUL'S CHAPEL, IT'S THE ONLY COLONIAL-ERA CHURCH
STILL STANDING IN MANHATTAN TODAY. AND THIS CHAPEL HAS A HISTORY
THAT ABSOLUTELY ENTHRALLED ME. AS I STOOD ON THE STEPS OF THAT
OLD CHAPEL IN NEW YORK CITY, MY MIND TOOK ME BACK TO 200 YEARS
EARLIER, TO THE MORNING OF APRIL 30TH, 1789. THAT MORNING, GEORGE
WASHINGTON STOOD ON THE BALCONY OF THE FEDERAL BUILDING AND
WITH HIS HAND ON THE BIBLE, WAS INAUGURATED AS THE FIRST
PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES. AFTER THE INAUGURATION,
PRESIDENT WASHINGTON THEN LED ALL THE MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE,
THE SENATE AND HIS CABINET ON A 9 MINUTE WALK TO ST PAUL'S CHAPEL.
WHEN THEY ARRIVED, THEY ALL KNELT OUTSIDE THE CHAPEL, VERY CLOSE
TO WHERE I WAS STANDING, IN A SMALL CEMETERY. IN THAT MOMENT,
EACH AND EVERY ONE OF THEM SOLEMNLY UNITED IN PRAYER. IT WAS A
PRAYER VOICED BY PRESIDENT WASHINGTON HIMSELF, TO “COMMIT THE
NEW GOVERNMENT TO THE HOLY PROTECTION AND BLESSING OF THE MOST
HIGH.”

NOW I WANT YOU TO TRY AND VISUALIZE THIS SCENE IN YOUR MIND AS I
DID. HERE I WAS, STANDING ON THE STEPS OF THIS SPECIAL BUILDING, AND
I REALIZED THAT PRESIDENT WASHINGTON’S PRAYER WAS A BOLD
REMINDER OF A VALUE OUR COUNTRY HAS ALWAYS STOOD FOR. A VALUE
THAT HAS ALWAYS BEEN SO INCREDIBLY IMPORTANT TO THE AMERICAN
PEOPLE, AS TO BE THE FIRST RIGHT EVER LISTED IN OUR CONSTITUTION,
GRANTED IN THE FIRST AMENDMENT, “CONGRESS SHALL MAKE NO LAW
RESPECTING AN ESTABLISHMENT OF RELIGION, OR PROHIBITING THE FREE
EXERCISE THEREOF.”

YOU SEE, OUR COUNTRY'S FOUNDERS--WHO WERE OF DIFFERENT
RELIGIOUS BACKGROUNDS THEMSELVES--RECOGNIZED THAT THE BEST

WAY TO PROTECT RELIGIOUS LIBERTY WAS TO KEEP THE GOVERNMENT OUT
OF RELIGION. THEY WANTED TO PROTECT THAT FUNDAMENTAL HUMAN
RIGHT THAT ALLOWS US TO THINK, EXPRESS AND ACT UPON WHAT WE
DEEPLY BELIEVE. AND THAT DAY IN 1789, THOSE FOUNDING FATHERS WHO
WERE ALL OF DIFFERENT FAITHS, UNITED IN THEIR NEW, SHARED RIGHT
OF RELIGIOUS FREEDOM.

SO AS ISTOOD IN THE CEMETERY OF ST PAUL’S CHAPEL THAT DAY, THE
SAME PLACE THAT GEORGE WASHINGTON HAD EXERCISED HIS RELIGIOUS
FREEDOM SO MANY YEARS BEFORE, IT BECAME VERY CLEAR TO ME. YOU
KNOW, I HAD JUST SEEN THE REGAL BEAUTY OF STATUE OF LIBERTY AND
THE ECONOMIC PROSPERITY OF WALL STREET. 1 HAD SEEN THE
GLAMOROUS WORLD TRADE BUILDING. BUT IT WASN’T UNTIL I STOOD IN
THAT CEMETERY AT ST PAUL’S CHAPEL, IN THE VERY SPOT THAT OUR FIRST
PRESIDENT HAD PRAYED, THAT I FOUND THE ANSWER I WAS LOOKING FOR.

IT REMINDS ME OF A FAMOUS QUOTE THAT IS SAID TO BE BY ALEXIS DE
TOCQUEVILLE: “I SOUGHT FOR THE GREATNESS AND GENIUS OF AMERICA
IN HER HARBORS AND HER AMPLE RIVERS, “ HE SAID, “AND IT WAS NOT
THERE. ... IN HER FERTILE FIELDS AND BOUNDLESS FORESTS—AND IT WAS

NOT THERE. ... IN HER RICH MINES AND HER VAST WORLD COMMERCE—
AND IT WAS NOT THERE... . IN HER DEMOCRATIC CONGRESS AND HER
MATCHLESS CONSTITUTION—AND IT WAS NOT THERE. NOT UNTIL I WENT
INTO THE CHURCHES OF AMERICA AND HEARD HER PULPITS AFLAME WITH

TO MAKE AMERICA GREAT.
