NO PLANNING ITEMS

RESOLUTION 06-03-2025 – INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH JUAB COUNTY FOR ELECTION SERVICES
      Nephi City has contracted with Juab County to help administer our election services and
      recommended the approval from the council to use them again.
      Councilor JD Parady made the motion to approve Resolution 06-03-2025 – Interlocal Agreement
      with Juab County for Election Services. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The
      motion passed on unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION 06-03-2025-A – MASTER FEE SCHEDULE
      Seth Atkinson presented the master fee resolution which included several fees being updated
      and a new fee recommended to be instituted. Cemetery fees were reevaluated and increased to
      reflect the cost of services and increased demand for space at the cemetery. A fee for utility
      customers outside of city limits was added. That fee falls between the resident and non-resident
      fee. Included in the master schedule was an after hours call out fee for irrigation water services.
      Also added was a new utility fee for storm water services will allow the city to maintain curb,
      gutter, drain collection boxes, and stormwater ponds throughout the city to ensure efficient flow
      of storm water.
      Councilor Skip Worwood made the motion to approve Resolution 06-03-2025-A – Master Fee
      Schedule. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous
      vote.

RESOLUTION 06-03-2025-B – AMERICA 250 UTAH PARTNERSHIP
      Governor Spencer J. Cox and the Utah Legislature created the America250 Utah. The purpose of
      the resolution is to support the state’s efforts to celebrate the nation’s 250th anniversary of its
      founding. The resolution includes the formation of a committee to plan community events.
      Mayor Seely mentions the involvement of Ruth Bonzo and Jalena Warner on the committee.
      Councilor Skip Worwood moved to approve Resolution 06-03-2025-B – America 250 Utah
      Partnership. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous
      vote.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP CHANGE REQUEST
      Derrik Jacobson attended council meeting last week to inquire about receiving city services on a
      new home that would be built in the unincorporated area of the county. The council’s direction
      was to request annexation. Mr. Jacobson requested the area be moved from a Level 3 to a level 2
      on the Annexation Area Map so an analysis can be done to determine the benefits and costs of
      annexation.
       Councilor Jeramie Callaway motioned that the area be made a Level 2 on the Annexation Area
       Map. Councilor Skip Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

WORK SESSION ITEMS
      Elite Laser Tag wants to partnership with Nephi Recreation and use the Hive for activities in the
      community. They presented a slide show depicting benefits for all:
           1. Community improvement
                   a. New variety of sporting activity
                   b. Fun for families
                   c. Revenue for community
                   d. Can be paired with other events
           2. Opportunities for youth
                   a. Teamwork and communication skills
                   b. Different win conditions
                   c. Healthy exercise
      The partnership would have a 70/30 split. The responsibility of Nephi City Recreation: 1) Provide
      Location—Hive; 2) Junior Recreation Employees; 3) Maintain the grounds; 4) Assist with
      advertising through the recreation department; 5)Assist in finding locations for events.
      The responsibilities of Elite Laser Tag include: 1) Provide the equipment; 2) event oversight and
      management; 3) provide own business insurance; 4) expand the sport and host other events; 5)
      increase revenue in the community; 5) create fun and engaging leagues.
      The council decided to work up a rental agreement, have an MOU written up and get back with
      them.
