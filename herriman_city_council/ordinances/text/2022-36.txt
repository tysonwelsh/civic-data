ORDINANCE NO. 2022-36

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HERRIMAN, UTAH,
AMENDING HERRIMAN CITY CODE TITLE 2 (BOARDS AND COMMISSIONS) RELATING
TO THE APPOINTMENT OF BOARD AND COMMISSION MEMBERS AND REMOVE THE
ECONOMIC DEVEOPMENT COMMITTEE.

WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-9a-102 grants the Herriman City (the “City”) authority to
enact ordinances that the Herriman City Council (the “City Council”) considers necessary or
appropriate; and

WHEREAS, the subject text amendment adds language for the removal of appointed
board and commission members and remove the economic development committee; and

WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing and reviewed the subject text
amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the subject text amendment will enhance the
public health, safety, and welfare.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
HERRIMAN, UTAH:

SECTION 1. Text Amendment. The Council hereby amends Herriman City Code
Sections 2-1-9, 2-2-9, 2-3, 2-5-8, 2-5-9, 2-7-8, 2-7-9, 2-8-8, 2-8-9, 2-10-8, 2-10-9, 2-11-3, and
2-11-6 as shown in Exhibit A.

SECTION 2. Severability. lf any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all sections, parts, provisions and words of this Ordinance shall be
severable.

SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting as required by law.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 24* day of August 2022.

2-1-1: Created

There is hereby created a commission known as the Herriman Historical Society. The Herriman Historical
Society is created pursuant to the authority granted by Utah Code Annotated section 9-8-102 et seq.
(Ord. 2018-21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-2: General Purpose

The purposes of the Historical Society are to:

1. Encourage the preservation of buildings and related structures of historical and architectural
significance as part of the City's important cultural, educational and economic assets;

2. Encourage proper development and utilization of land and areas adjacent to historical areas and
buildings, and to encourage complementary, contemporary design and construction;

3. Encourage the protection and enhance the attraction of the City's historic landmarks and
buildings for visitors;

4. Encourage and safeguard the heritage of the City by encouraging the protection of landmarks
representing significant elements of its history;

5. Promote the private and public use of landmarks in historical areas within the City;

Increase public awareness of the value of historic, cultural and architectural preservation;

Engage in the research and compilation of information necessary to safeguard and preserve a
comprehensive history of the City for the ultimate goal of publication; and

Recommend design standards pertaining to the protection of historic and architecturally
significant buildings and structures. (Ord. 2018-21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-3: Terms, Compensation And Vacancy

The Historical Society shall consist of three (3) members to seven (7) members, appointed by the City
Council. Such members shall be appointed in a manner providing balance in geographic, professional,
neighborhood and community interests representation. Members of the Historical Society may serve a
maximum of three (3) consecutive terms of three (3) years each. Notwithstanding, the terms of the first
members shall be staggered so that all member's terms do not expire in the same year. Vacancies may
be filled by the City Council, to any unexpired term or for the full term as the case may be. Members of
the Historical Society shall serve without compensation. (Ord. 2018-21, 5-9-2018; amd. Ord. 2021-17, 8-
11-2021)

2-1-4: Organization, Quorum And Committees

1.

Quorum: More than fifty percent (50%) of the Historical Society present shall constitute a
quorum for conducting business of the Historical Society and the action can be had upon the
vote of the majority present.

Organization: The Historical Society may elect a Chair, organize committees, and adopt
administrative rules, bylaws and procedures necessary to accomplish its purpose. The Historical
Society shall submit such rules, bylaws and procedures to the City Council for approval. (Ord.
2018-21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-5: Authority And Responsibilities

In addition to carrying out the general purposes set forth above, the Historical Society may:

1.

2.

Conduct surveys of significant historical, architectural and cultural landmarks within the City;

Develop and participate in public education programs to increase public awareness of the value
of historic, architectural and cultural preservation;

Make recommendations to the Planning Commission relating to historic, architectural and
cultural preservation;

Make recommendations to the City Council regarding historic, architectural and cultural
preservation;

Make recommendations to the City Council concerning utilization of State, Federal or private
funds to promote the preservation of historic, architectural and cultural preservation; and

Make recommendations to the City Council on policies and ordinances that may encourage
preservation of buildings and related structures of historical and architectural significance.
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary herein, the Historical Society shall not have authority

to expend or commit to the expenditure of public funds without City Council approval. (Ord.
2018-21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-6: Meetings

1. Open Meetings: The Historical Society shall meet as it deems necessary, and such meetings shall
be open to the public.

2. Rules Of Procedure: The Historical Society shall adopt policies and procedures for the conduct of
its meetings that it deems necessary for its proper functioning. (Ord. 2018-21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-7: Staff Support

The City shall provide the Historical Society with such administrative staff support and materials as it
deems necessary to assist in carrying out its duties and functions. (Ord. 2018-21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-8: Limitations

The Historical Society shall not have authority to bind the City or impose any condition on the City or its
citizens and is not vested with authority to make decisions regarding the public's business. (Ord. 2018-
21, 5-9-2018)

2-1-9: Removal of Members

City commission, committee and board members, including the Planning Commission, serve at the
pleasure of the Mayor and Council. The Mayor, or any Council member, may, at any time, recommend
to the Council that a member of any commission, committee or board appointed under this chapter be
removed from serving on the commission, committee or board. Before taking action on such a
recommendation, the Council shall allow the individual an opportunity to address the Council. After
providing the individual an opportunity to be heard, that individual may be removed by a majority vote
of the Council.

the development of parks, trails and recreation in the City pending City approval; and

2-10-3: Terms, Compensation And Vacancy

The Herriman Off Highway Vehicle Committee shall be comprised of three (3) members to seven (7)
members, appointed by the City Council. Such members shall be appointed in a manner providing
balance in geographic, professional, neighborhood and community interests representation. Members
of the Herriman Off Highway Vehicle Committee may serve a maximum of three (3) consecutive terms
of three (3) years each. Notwithstanding, the terms of the first members shall be staggered so that all
member's terms do not expire in the same year. Vacancies may be filled by the City Council, to any
unexpired term or for the full term as the case may be. Members of the Herriman Off Highway Vehicle
Committee shall serve without compensation. (Ord. 2021-17, 8-11-2021)

2-10-4: Organization, Quorum And Committees

1. Quorum: More than fifty percent (50%) of the Herriman Off Highway Vehicle Committee present
shall constitute a quorum for conducting business of the Herriman Off Highway Vehicle
Committee and the action can be had upon the vote of the majority present.

2. Organization: The Herriman Off Highway Vehicle Committee may elect a Chair, organize
committees, and adopt administrative rules, bylaws and procedures necessary to accomplish its
purpose. The Herriman Off Highway Vehicle Committee shall submit such rules, bylaws and
procedures to the City Council for approval. (Ord. 2021-17, 8-11-2021)

2-10-5: City Staff Support


The Herriman Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board shall act in an advisory and voluntary capacity to
suggest to the City Council ideas for additional opportunities for engagement and inclusion among the
diverse residents and communities within the municipal boundaries of Herriman City. The specific duties
and responsibilities of the Diversity Inclusion Advisory Board will be as set forth in the Committee
bylaws, which shall be adopted by the City Council, and which may be amended by the Council from
time to time.

2-11-6: Removal of Members

City commission, committee and board members, including the Planning Commission, serve at the
pleasure of the Mayor and Council. The Mayor, or any Council member, may, at any time, recommend
to the Council that a member of any commission, committee or board appointed under this chapter be


removed from serving on the commission, committee or board. Before taking action on such a
recommendation, the Council shall allow the individual an opportunity to address the Council. After
providing the individual an opportunity to be heard, that individual may be removed by a majority vote
of the Council.
