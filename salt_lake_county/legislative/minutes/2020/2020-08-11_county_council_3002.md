Salt Lake County Amendement of Planning Commission 
Ordinance
ORDINANCE NO. 1873
AMENDMENT OF PLANNING COMMISSION ORDINANCE TO 
INCREASE NUMBER OF COMMISSION MEMBERS AND OUTLINE 
RESIDENCE REQUIREMENT FOR SAME
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows:
SECTION I.  The amendments made here are designed by underlining the 
new substituted words.  Words being deleted are designated by brackets and 
interlineations.
SECTION II.  Section 19.04.010 of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances is amended as follows:
19.05.010 - Appointment - Term
The planning commission for the unincorporated areas of Salt Lake County 
shall consist of [five] seven members and up to two alternative members 
appointed by the mayor with the advice and consent of the county council .  
Members shall serve three -year terms or longer until successors are 
appointed.  Terms shall commence on March 1st of each year.  In the event a 
term of a member shall expire before a successor is appointed, the member 
shall continue to serve until a successor is appointed.  Terms of at least one 
member, and not more than two, shall expire each year.  The members and 
alternate members of the planning commission shall be registered voters 
residing in any unincorporated or incorporated area of the county with the 
provision that at least [three] four of the regular members of the planning 
commission be residents of unincorporated Salt Lake County.
SECTION III.  This ordinance shall become effective fifteen (15) days after 
its passage and upon at least one publication of the ordinance or a summary 
thereof in a newspaper published and having general circulation in Salt Lake 
County.
