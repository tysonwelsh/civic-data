                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 20-71

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AMENDMENT TO
  CONTRACT NO. 17-1049-04 TO ZIONS PUBLIC FINANCE, ADDING FINANCIAL
                     ADVISORY SERVICES FOR RFSA

        WHEREAS, The Riverton Fire Service Area, known as RFSA, was established with an
effective date of October 13, 2020, to provide fire protection, emergency medical and related
functions and services to the incorporated areas of Riverton City; and

        WHEREAS, financial advisory services are needed for RFSA; and

        WHEREAS, the Scope of Services required for RFSA are the same as RLESA; and

        WHEREAS, Zions Public Finance will allow an amendment to the contract adding
RFSA.
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an amendment to Contract No. 17-
           1049-04 to Zions Public Finance, adding financial advisory services for RFSA.

        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
November 2020 by the following vote:
