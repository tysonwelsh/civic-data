                                             WORK SESSION
1.    DISCUSSION REGARDING THE ADJUSTMENT OR DEFERRAL OF TAXES (UTAH CODE 59-2-
      1347) AND FARMLAND ASSESSMENT ACT ROLLBACK TAXES (UTAH CODE 59-2-506)
(Track A, 11:55)
Utah County Assessor Kris Poulson is proposing a resolution to address situations wherein the greenbelt status of
property is changed despite use continuing to meet the qualifications. This can occur when there is a transfer of
ownership, or property registered to an individual is added to a trust or limited liability corporation. Utah County
Deputy Attorney Paul Jones mentioned the need to define parameters, and Mr. Jones along with Mr. Poulson discussed
hypothetical situations with the commissioners. The Board encouraged them to move forward with their proposal.


The Board returned to discussion of Consent Agenda Item No. 14 at this point.
