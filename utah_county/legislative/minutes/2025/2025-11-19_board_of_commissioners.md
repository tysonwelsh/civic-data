3. ADOPT A RESOLUTION APPROVING REVISIONS TO HUMAN RESOURCE POLICY:
POLICY 5-0500 LEAVE

-Ralf Barnes, Human Resources
(Timestamp: 8:20)

RALF BARNES (Utah County Human Resources Director) DISCUSSION ON LEAVE POLICY FOR
MISCARRIAGE AND STILLBIRTHS. COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER EXPRESSED
THAT THE BEREAVEMENT TIME OFF SHOULD BE CHANGED FROM 3 DAYS TO 5 IN
ORDER TO BE CONSISTENT WITH OUR OTHER POLICIES. SHE ALSO REQUESTED

BACK WITH OTHER MATERNITY LEAVE OPTIONS FOR A STILLBORN DELIVERY.

ITEM NUMBER 3 WITH THE EXCEPTION THAT WE WILL CHANGE BEREAVEMENT
FOR BOTH THE MOTHER AND THE FATHER OR THE SPOUSE AND THE BIRTHING
PARENT TO BE FIVE DAYS INSTEAD OF THREE.
