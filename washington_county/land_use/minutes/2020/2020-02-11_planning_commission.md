Washington County Land Use Authority
    Meeting Minutes of February 11, 2020 cont’d
    Page 3 of 3

Mr. Messel provided updates to the attainable housing plan discussed at a previous meeting. A
countywide analysis was done for attainable housing areas that were ripe for development. He will
get the information to the commissioners for discussion in an upcoming meeting. Commissioner
Walter asked if the areas were located in the county unincorporated areas or the cities. Mr. Messel
answered both.

Mr. Messel stated that a public hearing on the Resource Management Plan updates would be held
at near future meeting.

Mr. Messel thanked the commission for their service.


Commissioner Hughes moved to adjourn the meeting at 2:05 p.m. Commissioner Anderson
seconded. The meeting then adjourned.



Doreen Bowers-Irons, Planning Secretary


Approved: March 10, 2020
