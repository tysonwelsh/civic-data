13. Consideration of Ordinance #58-2021, approving a comprehensive update to the Land
    Use Element of the General Plan including the land use map and text.

    Kim Struthers presented information on the update to the General Plan. Mayor Johnson
    stated when the General Plan was first looked at there was discussion that the population
    would be around 150,000, but since an analysis has been ran the number is closer to 155,000.
    Councilor Koivisto stated she has concerns with the population being close to 300,000 and
    hoped the number would be below 150,000. Mayor Johnson and Kim stated it is well below
    300,000. Councilor Albrecht also stated with ADU’s being approved the numbers will also
    fluctuate. Councilor Hancock suggested approving the General Plan and then running ADU
    numbers to get a more accurate number. The Council wasn't sure how to make that
    determination and have it accurate. Previous units that were excluded are now part of the
    General Plan. Councilor Albrecht suggested that a final decision is delayed due to the amount
    of public that is here to make comment. Councilor Condie discussed 1100 West and Main
    Street and talked about mixed density. Mayor Johnson asked for a certain percentage from
    Council. Lorin Powell suggested different parts of the infrastructure that would be impacted
    by the analysis.

    Mike West stated this the best guess on how the City will build out. It will be looked at every
    10 to 15 years. He wanted to make sure the vision is being looked at. ADU's is a big concern,
    and he hopes that there is enough information where an informative decision can be made.
    TOD's is a strong suit and is important and Staff will continue to work with outside agencies.

    Larry Lacroix stated Thanksgiving Point is a stumbling block. He feels that the development
    going into the area is a huge mistake. He wanted to thank Councilor Albrecht and Koivisto
    for making sure the development towed the line with infrastructure.

    Cami Baron had some inquires on 2300 North and 2300 West. Mayor Johnson stated the City
    is working on a Master Transportation Plan right now. Cami also asked about adding homes
    across for North Point Elementary and access points.

    Jody Warner stated they would like the traffic situation into consideration. She is also very
    concerned about Stack and their proposals and what they are saying.

    Granger Peck talked about the land use in the Thanksgiving Point area.
    He discussed opposition to development through the golf course and the concern of the
    traffic.

    Jeff Scott asked the Council if they wanted to make Lehi an asphalt jungle or if they wanted
    it to be a strong community. He didn't feel like the Council needed to reinvent the wheel and
    that density equals more crime.

    Steve Thomas stated more East/West road are needed. He stated the Thanksgiving Point
    Village was created for peace.

    Brian McQuiston asked the Council to follow a commercial node instead of allowing the
    developer to do what he wants with the property. He also recommended the Council execute
    General Plan amendments collectively, once, or twice a year.

    Drew Wilson stated his property is designated agriculture and talked about how the zone
    change would affect his property. He is concerned about HD zone change affecting his

    as they look at the General Plan.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to table Ordinance #58-2021, approving a
                comprehensive update to the Land Use Element of the General Plan including the
                land use map and text; noting the Council would like to discuss and regroup in the
                December Work Session to go over any proposed changes to give Engineering
                time to compose figures at 50 and 25 percent, with a finite boundary of the current
                general plan of 150,000 population or lower. The Council would like to invite the
                Planning Commissioners to the December Work Session. The Council would like
                Planning to provide some analysis with similar Cities with a baseline study on
                how ADU's are adopted and encourage all residents to call the Council with
                concerns, including any Development Review Committee comments.
                Commissioner Albrecht seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
