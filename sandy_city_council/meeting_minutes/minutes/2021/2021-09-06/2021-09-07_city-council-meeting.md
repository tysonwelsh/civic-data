1.         21-336                 Gondola Works presentation.

                   Attachments:   Gondola Works Deck_Comparison August 25 2021 (1)

                                  Chris McCandless, with Gondola Works, presented on a proposed gondola transportation 
                                  alternative in Little Cottonwood Canyon. The gondola is one of the transportation options 
                                  currently under consideration by UDOT. He provided an overview of the current problems 
                                  and issues associated with transporting visitors up Little Cottonwood Canyon. Mr. 
                                  McCandless provided a comparison of the two proposed transportation options, which 
                                  included a comparison of costs, life cycle, carbon emissions, environment and watershed 
                                  impact, travel time, traffic impact and emergency egress. He shared a video showing 
                                  what a proposed gondola alternative would look like and gave an overview of the next 
                                  steps for UDOT as the state moves forward with a transportation option selection. Council 
                                  thanked Mr. McCandless for the information.

2.         21-335                 Kem C. Gardner Policy Institute providing the Council with a presentation 
                                  on "The Impact of High-Density Apartments on Surrounding Single-Family 
                                  Home Values in Suburban Salt Lake County"
                   Attachments:   Report.pdf

                                  Dejan Eskic, Senior Research Fellow with the Kem Gardner Policy Institute presented on 
                                  the impact of high-density apartment on surrounding areas. Mr. Eskic spoke about the 
                                  methodology used for this study, the area studied, median market value and driving 
                                  factors. He noted the following positive factors resulting from high-density developments 
                                  including the revitalization of older properties which increases the attractiveness of nearby 
                                  areas and can bring new demand and new dollars to an area, serving as a catalyst for 
                                  positive economic growth. Their study did not find that high-density housing had negative 
                                  impacts on a given area. Council questions and comments followed.
