© Resolution No. 2015-18 — A Resolution Authorizing Eminent Domain Proceedings

Attorney Jody Burnett stated there is no change from his August 11, 2015 report. There
are still three property owners who have not reached an agreement on the
compensation for their properties. Issa Hamud remarked that the construction season
in Cache County is short and they want to be able to bid the project out this fall to begin
construction as soon as weather permits in the spring.

Burnett responded to Council member Potter and Vice Chair Merrill's questions that the
issue with landowners is usually about the valuation of the property and a public entity
cannot pay more than market for the land. Individuals can still negotiate after eminent
domain is authorized. Burnett added the land owners have been informed they have a
right to contact the Office of the Property Rights Ombudsman for the state of Utah with
their concerns.

MEMORANDUM

WOE Cache County Council
FROM: Dianna Schaeffer, Deputy Cache County Auditor
DATE: August 25, 2015

SUBJECT: Request for Tax Relief Filing Extension

Every year Cache County processes hundreds of tax relief applications for various indigent abatements, exclusive
use exemptions, military exemptions, blind exemptions, settlements, and deferrals. The application deadline is
September 1° in most cases, but may be extended until December 31° if the county finds good cause.

e Low Income Abatement - State law §59-2-1107 through §59-2-1109 permits counties to abate taxes for
indigent persons subject to proper application, documentation, and deadlines. Allowable extension
§59-2-1109(2)(d).

¢ Blind Exemption - State law §59-2-1106 provides counties authority to abate taxes for the blind subject to
proper application, documentation, and deadlines. Allowable extension §59-2-1106(2)(b)(ii).

«Military Exemption - State law §59-2-1104 and §59-2-1105 allows counties to abate taxes for veterans and
active duty armed forces subject to proper application, documentation, and deadlines. Military applications
are allowed an “automatic” one year extension per UCA §59-2-1105(2)(b)(i).

* Non-Profit Use Exemption — State law §59-2-1102 provides counties authority to abate taxes for religious
charitable, and educational organizations. Applications were due by March 1st and have been processed.

¢ Settlement/Deferral - No deadlines are mandated in §59-2-1347.

After the tax notices are mailed at the end of October, the county will be besieged by citizens in dire need of tax
relief. We must be compliant with the law of the land while maintaining the intent of the law to help those in
need. Therefore, it is in the best interest of the community that the Cache County Council extends the filing
deadline for low income and blind tax relief to November 30, 2015. By extending the filing deadline, Cache
County can continue helping eligible citizens and remain compliant with the law in regards to applications for tax
relief. This would solve the impending problem of processing impoverished applicants while remaining legally
compliant.

CACHE CounTyY, UTAH

SERVICE PROVISION ON COUNTY ROADS

A RESOLUTION OUTLINING THE COUNTY COUNCIL'S POLICY REGARDING THE EXPANSION OR
CONTINUATION OF SERVICES ON COUNTY ROADS

Whereas, Cache County has reviewed the impact of new development along unimproved
roadways on the safety and welfare of citizens of Cache County, and;

Whereas, Cache County has determined that within the existing budget constraints
funding is not sufficient for the existing network of roadways to be properly maintained
and that additional road length is a net cost to the county even considering existing Class
B allocations from the State and property tax rates, and;

Whereas, it is not in the best interest for the safety and/or welfare of existing or future
residents of Cache County to diminish services on existing roadways to maintain new
roads or to provide substandard service on new roadways, and;

Whereas, there are nearly 200 miles of existing paved road in Cache County that can
accommodate development with little to no impact on the County’s ability to serve said
development.

Now, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the County Council of Cache County, Utah, in a
regular meeting, lawful notice of which has been given, finds that it is in the best interests
of the citizens of Cache County to hereby adopt the following resolution:

1) There shall be no further expansion of road services on substandard roads that do
not already serve existing homes including:

a. No expansion of winter maintenance activities (snow plowing).
b. No gravel roads be paved or “Chip Sealed”.
c. No acceptance of new roadways, gravel or paved.

2) The County Council may grant exceptions to this policy if proposed road
improvements improve the health and/or safety of existing subdivisions, homes,
or businesses.
