8. TRANSPORTATION MASTER PLAN (Public Hearing). Review Transportation Master Plan and provide
   recommendation to the County Commission.

       Mr. Messel explained the Transportation Master Plan has been drafted and reviewed over the past
       several months. Work meetings have been held and public input taken with various communities. The
       residents of New Harmony have been working on proposing a road cross section for HWY 144.

       Mr. Edwards reviewed the Transportation Master Plan with the Planning Commission. He described in
       detail where funds came from and what priorities were brought forward through public input. He
       explained the complexity of working with UDOT, various municipalities, and residents to de�ine priority
       projects. He spoke on surface types and maintenance requirements. Factors affecting road safety were
       highlighted. Examples of trails which have been put in and not utilized as intended were reviewed.
       Trails along HWY 18 through Snow Canyon were highlighted, many competitors training for events
       don’t utilize the trails because the races occur along the roadways.

       Chairman Jones opened the public hearing:

       Ms. Fonger spoke on incorporating active transportation goals with the Transportation Master Plan.
       Trail etiquette is an important consideration to ensure uses are harmonious for those using the trails.
       Funds can become available from sources other than UDOT to build trails adjacent to roadways. She
       advocated for making sure easements were wide enough to allow for trail sections to be developed as
       developers bring projects into the county. Technology is being used to track the usage of trail networks
       and where improvements would be most bene�icial.
