         Based on the MODERATE TRANSMISSION LEVEL in Magna of the Novel Coronavirus (COVID-
19), as identified by both the State and County Health Orders, the Mayor and Council have chosen to conduct
this meeting at an anchor location with limited public access pursuant to the public health orders. To

assessments feel attending the meeting at the anchor location may be an unacceptable health risk, this meeting
will be also conducted electronically.
