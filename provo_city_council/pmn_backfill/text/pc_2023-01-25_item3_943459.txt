*ITEM 3       Miller General Contractors LLC requests approval of a Zone Map Amendment from the ITOD (Interim
              Transit Oriented Development) zone to the DT1 (General Downtown) zone in order to construct a new
              apartment building located at 466 South 100 West. Downtown Neighborhood. PLRZ20220220



The following action was taken by the Planning Commission on the above described item at its regular meeting of
January 25, 2023:


                                 RECOMMENDED APPROVAL
On a vote of 6:0, the Planning Commission recommended that the Municipal Council approve the above noted application.

Conditions of Approval:

Motion By: Jeff Whitlock
Second By: Andrew South
Votes in Favor of Motion: Jeff Whitlock, Andrew South, Lisa Jensen, Daniel Gonzales, Melissa Kendall, Robert Knudsen
Lisa Jensen was present as Chair.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
• The rezone to DT1 will allow the property to develop as shown in the proposed development associated with this
   application.
• The property will be combined into single-ownership and the rezone will provide consistency with the other property.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
• A commissioner asked if the property could be rezoned to ITOD instead of DT1. The General Plan and the Downtown
   Master Plan support DT1.
• Because most of the property in the development is already zoned to allow the proposed development, this makes
   sense and that is also why they went straight to project plan instead of a concept plan with the rezone.
