                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-24

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A FRANCHISE AGREEMENT
                  WITH FORGED FIBER 37, LLC

       WHEREAS, Forged Fiber 37, LLC desires to install, maintain, operate and/or control an
telecommunications infrastructure network in Riverton City’s (“City”) owned right of ways to
provide telecommunications service to City residents; and
        WHEREAS, it is both reasonable and necessary for City to allow the installation of Forged
Fiber’s infrastructure network in the City owned public right of ways within the City and thereafter
to maintain, operate and/or control said network; and
       WHEREAS, the City finds and determines that the public interest in health, safety and
welfare will be enhanced by the provision of the proposed service; and
        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
   1. The City Council concludes that entering into the above-described agreement with Forged
      Fiber 37, LLC will enhance the public health, safety and welfare of the public, and
      authorizes the City to enter into and sign the same.
   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute a Franchise Agreement with Forged Fiber 37, LLC in
      substantially similar form to the Agreement attached as Exhibit A.
   3. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 2nd day of
June 2026 by the following vote:
