   John Brems           said that Utah law requires                  that the presiding             officer     of the public           body     ensure
   that members           are trained        on the Act annually.                 The goal is that all meetings                   are open to the
   public.     He reviewed           the definition          of meeting,          and that electronic            communication               was not a
   meeting.       Mayor       Silvestrini       cautioned        the council         to not deliberate            via electronic
   communications              because       it was not open to the public.                    Brems       reviewed           meeting       notice
   requirements.          If an item is raised by the public                       during     the meeting,            the council        may discuss
   the item but not take action on it until it is properly noticed. Minutes and recordings     are
   required in all meetings,   but the written minutes    become  the official record of the meetings.
   There are rules to electronic   meetings. Closed meetings may be held for specific purposes,
   like strmegy         sessions       to discuss       collective        bargaining,          imminent         litigation,       the purchase           or
   exchange         of real property,           the sale of real property,                 and most recently              consideration          of a loan
   application.         Minutes        of closed     meetings         were considered               protected         records     under      GRAMA
   and may be disclosed                 by court      or upon the request              of the Office            of the Legislative             Auditor
   General.       He anticipated            that office       auditing       closed meetings             in the future.          Mayor      Silvestrini
   said this would           confirm        that cities were following                 closed meeting             laws.       Brems      said a closed
   meeting       affidavit      could     replace      the audio         recording         for closed meeting             for the discussion              of

   the character         of an individual            or deployment            of security        devices        or systems.


   Brems       presented       hypothetical          case studies         for the council           to consider.          He cautioned           not
   texting     during      a public      meeting        and to be careful             with     social media           posts.     Mayor      Silvestrini
   said some of the interactions                   with      constituents          outside     of a meeting           would      be okay for council
   members,         but not planning            commissioners.               Legislative        bodies        have the opportunity               to seek
   input     from      the public,      but the planning           commission              makes      administrative             actions.
    Commissioners             would      need to disclose            their    interactions        with     the public          with     the rest of the
    commission          at the first available              opportunity
