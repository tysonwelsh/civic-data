SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS

SV-1-2024      SCOTT HASLAM, GREG MILLER, WILLIAM GOOCH, AND JONAH
HORNSBY are requesting consideration to vacate all of Clifford Street located within the
Hayne’s Addition Plat E Subdivision. The street is located north of 2300 South at 5650 West.
(Staff-Steve Lehman 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Steve L. presented an application from most of the business owners of Clifford
Street where the city has no interest in keeping or paving, so the applicants are requesting to
vacate the right of way. One of the concerns that Staff had was this right of way has been used to
access other properties. To ensure that access remained, the applicants have written up a cross-
access easement where 33 feet of the 66 feet will go to each owner. It would also preserve the
existing utilities in the right of way. One resident on the corner, Mr. Flores, doesn’t want to sign
the agreement but the vacation will still provide to him half of this right away.

There were no comments or concerns.

SA-4-2024     RUSS PLATT is requesting a plat amendment for the Cobalt Landing PUD. The
proposed application will amend lots 101, 115-138, Parcels C & E and will create Parcels H, I, J
and K. The purpose for the amended plat is to establish and extend property lines to the
perimeter boundary of those units along the south and east portions of the planned unit
development. The property is located at 1585 West Neon Way and is zoned RM. (Staff-Steve
Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Steve L. presented an application regarding the Cobalt Landing PUD. This would
amend the south and east lots, and some parcels, in a PUD that's already built and occupied. The
area behind those units was designated as common open space but the HOA no longer wants to
maintain that space as it's too small to do anything on, so it makes sense for those residents to
utilize and occupy the spaces that sit right behind their units. To do that, the subdivision plat
needs to be amended to create new lot lines, and all of those residents are in favor of extending
their rear property lines.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS

C-21-2024      Petition by WILDCAT REAL ESTATE HOLDINGS LLC requesting conditional
use approval for a Take 5 Oil Change auto service business. The property is located at 5619-
5635 W. 3500 S. on .74 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at
801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Jody presented the application for a corner lot purchased from UDOT. Wildcat is
planning on keeping it as 2 separate lots to potentially sell off the West lot in the future to
another tenant, so it will be landscaped until then. UDOT put in the access road and sold Wildcat
the property, and then came back and said they don’t like the location of the access. Jody thinks
the access would stay where it is because it’s a direct path to the shared access with the adjacent
businesses.

Chair Lovato asked if the access is too close to 5600 West. Jody answered UDOT wants it
moved as far west as possible but that will be worked out in the development phase.

CA-10-2024 Petition by HUNT ELECTRIC requesting a conditional use amendment for
Outside Storage and Heavy Manufacturing. The properties are located at 1811, 1863, 1897,
1920, 1935 and 1940 W Alexander and 2421 S. Progress Dr. on 11.49 acres and are zoned M
(Manufacturing) (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov).

Discussion: Jody presented. She said Planning and Zoning has received different applications
from Hunt Electric over the years; some have expanded, some have expired, and some have
changed. This application is to do one blanket approval to understand what’s being done in the
entire campus and have it all documented in one place. The primary point of the application is to
look at outside storage of electrical materials, equipment, electrical supplies, vehicles and trailers
as well as outside manufacturing.

The first property is 1811 Alexander which is their main shop building and where they fabricate
and assemble electrical components, and it provides employee parking. No changes proposed at
this property.

The second property is 1863 Alexander which is their main office headquarters and employee
parking. This area does have fencing, and they have not proposed any additional screening.

The third property is 1935 Alexander. This one came before the Planning Commission in 2017,
and they completed the parking lot area and the landscaping. However, the gravel area behind
never received plan review approval and that area wasn’t completed. The applicant did receive a
variance for the landscaping set-back in the front, however, a few more trees are required and
some fencing needs to be repaired or replaced. And there is a bulk storage area that will be
relocated once that area is paved.

The fourth property is 2421 Progress Drive. This was a conditional use that came before the
Commission in 2023. Jody performed the review and sent review comments back, but Hunt
never resubmitted revisions and the application expired. This 2024 application doesn’t change
from the original application, it just adds more clarification as to what's being done there. Fence
slats were required and added but the required landscaping was not, so that would need to be
done. This yard would be a gravel surface. There is no access to a public right away from the
yard but there is access to the campus. There are some dirt areas that would need grading and
drainage installed.

The fifth property is 1920 Alexander which is another office building with landscaping up front.
There is a solar canopy, and then the storage and fabrication would be in the back.

The sixth property is 1940 Alexander. There had been a building there, but it burned down, and
that's when Mr. Hunt acquired it. The property is all paved. The canal north of there had been
