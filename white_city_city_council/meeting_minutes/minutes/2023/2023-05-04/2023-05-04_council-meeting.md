                Chief Ken Aldridge stated recruit camp is in hazmat training. Recruits participated
in a 48-hour exercise that simulates station life. Graduation is next month, and it anticipates 31

Comprehensive Code Update

               Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District,
stated staff has been doing research regarding the property located at 1226 East 10600 South.
The property owner wants to operate a landscaping business. There are many residents that are
concerned about road access on 1225 East. Staff has not been able to find evidence that the
resident does not have access to it. The property owner of 1226 East 10600 South requested that
landscaping businesses be added to the code as an approved use. Right now, it is not an
approved use in the agricultural zone.

               Mayor Flint stated the lot is being used to park heavy equipment and the
homeowner is not permitted to do so. The Council or community has never approved the property
to be used for commercial purposes.

                 Ms. O’Kelley stated at this point the Council will need to decide what uses will be
approved in the updated code. The property owner believes that his landscaping business is
similar in use to agricultural properties in the same zone and area. He also wants to turn the house
into a duplex and rent one side of it.

She updated the Council on the outcome of the public hearing that was held by the planning
commission. Approximately 40 residents attended with many of them expressing concerns about
various parts of the code. The planning commission recommended incorporating those public
comments.

               Mayor Flint stated she would like to see the recommended changes.

              Paul Ashton asked Ms. O’Kelley to provide a list of public comments that have
substantive changes.

              Mayor Flint stated that because of public comment, she recommended holding a
special meeting to review the recommended changes.

Council Reports

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD)

               Mayor Flint stated the board is working on creating a more uniform fee schedule.
Salt Lake County has not been providing funds to the community councils. An ordinance has been
passed to fix that issue. The Association of Community Councils Together (ACCT) has always
had an office in the Salt Lake County Government Center. An individual started looking into this
arrangement and decided that ACCT needed to pay $6,000 a year in rent. To cover those costs,
the MSD will increase the administrative budget for each metro township. The amount owed will
be determined by how much the communities get for ACCT activities.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)/Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA)

                Council Member Perry stated the board received the Open and Public Meetings
Act training. On May 9, 2023, the finance committee will meet to discuss the budget and then
present it to the board the following week.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Price stated it is in the process of hiring a new manager. Most
of the seasonal crew has been hired and some have started training. She encourages residents
to contact the district if they see standing water.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Animal Services

                Council Member Price stated the board received Open and Public Meetings Act
training. Animal Services has achieved 10 years of being a no-kill shelter. The budget is being
discussed. If residents have an animal that they cannot take care of temporarily, Animal Services
will shelter the animal for 90 days. Microchip services are free on Mondays. On Saturday, there
will be a Tats for Cats fundraiser.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)/Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

              Council Member Cardenaz stated last month, the Metro Townships, with the
exception of Copperton, approved a resolution and letter in regard to the future of policing. Chief
Mazuran has taken over as administrator of SLVLESA. The boards are meeting twice a month
now. Next month the board will consider a fee schedule change and will take public comment at
its meeting. Labor union representatives presented some of their concerns during the April
meeting.
