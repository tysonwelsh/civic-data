Proposed Resolution 2025-3 adopting the budget for the Municipal Building
Authority Fiscal Year 2025-2026
          A memo from Board staff regarding the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 Municipal Building Authority (MBA)
Budget came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated on May 13th the Administration presented the Trustees with a
proposed Fiscal Year 2025-2026 Municipal Building Authority (MBA) budget. At that time the Board set a public hearing for
June 10, 2025, to consider adopting the budget.
          Board Policy Analyst Burton summarized the memo and stated the proposed Fiscal Year 2025-2026 MBA budget totals
$4,198,900. The budget maintains a balance of $5,000 for any expenses related to financial reporting and the remainder is debt
service for WonderBlock parking infrastructure. The line-item revenue page of the budget for the MBA indicates the funds will
come from lease payments, interest income, and bond proceeds related to the WonderBlock project. Administration has proposed
transferring BDO lease revenue funds to the Parking and Mobility Fund, which is shown in the line-item revenue files as lease
payments.
          Chair White introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2025-3, entitled:
                   “A resolution of the Board of Trustees of the Ogden City Municipal Building Authority adopting the annual
                   budget for Fiscal Year 2025-2026.”
          Chair White then opened the public hearing.
          Heath Satow asked if Administration has arrived at a decision on the paid parking program for downtown Ogden as a
means to satisfy debt service on the parking structure in the WonderBlock project.
          There being no further persons appearing to be heard, VICE CHAIR HYER MOVED THE PUBLIC HEARING BE
CLOSED. MOTION WAS SECONDED BY TRUSTEE BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.
          Chair White deferred to President Nadolski to answer Mr. Satow’s question. President Nadolski stated the City will move
forward with a paid parking program, but the details and timeline of that program are yet to be finalized. He indicated he will
provide for additional public input and thorough review by the elected officials of the City. Chair White noted it is important to
provide clear communication to business owners in downtown Ogden before finalizing the paid parking program.
          ON A MOTION BY VICE CHAIR HYER AND SECONDED BY TRUSTEE GRAF, RESOLUTION 2025-3 WAS
ADOPTED UPON THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE: VOTING AYE – TRUSTEES BLAIR, CHOBERKA,
GRAF, MYERS, RICHEY, VICE CHAIR HYER, AND CHAIR WHITE. VOTING NO – NONE.
