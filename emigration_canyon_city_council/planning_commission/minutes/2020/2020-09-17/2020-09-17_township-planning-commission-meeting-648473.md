LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
Meeting began at – 9:11 a.m.
31202- Adam Breen is requesting an Amendment to the Anderson Cove Subdivision to vacate public
utility/cross-access easements and propose new public utility easements. The purpose of the request is to
allow lot #2 its potential buildable area without impacting the existing Single-Family Dwelling built on
adjacent lot #3. Acreage: .50 acres. Location: 4283 Emigration Canyon Road. Zone: FR-0.5 (Forestry and
Recreation). Planner: Jim Nakamura. (Motion/Voting)
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jim Nakamura provided an analysis of the staff
report.
Commissioner Pinon confirmed the neighbors and who owns the lot. Mr. Nakamura confirmed vacate
would be vacating easements instead of vacating trees. Commissioner Pinon showed an overlay showing
the trees they are saving. Commissioner Pinon asked if there is dimension between the two drives being
required. Mr. Nakamura said there will be only one point of egress onto the canyon road, but will each
have their own driveway. The curb cut may increase to the proposed 35 feet. Commissioner Harpst said if
they widen the drive, will work as two driveways, owners will not be driving across each other’s property
line and how wide would they allow that to be and there should maximum extensions. Does not see any
issues having joint or separate driveways if utility companies affected by this can work with the easements.
Commissioner Pinon introduced the letter from Breen homes with the history of the property.
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the subdivision creation, egress, restricted utility
easements, and abutting property and subdivision, striping. Mr. McNulty said this is an amended plat for
the current subdivision, and not adjacent subdivision.
