          railing at Kaleidoscope Park.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          Mayor Wood shared that 88 families were served from ‘Season of Sharing’ and
          thanked everyone who donated their time and resources to make that happen.

           More information about the upcoming Baby & You program can be found on the
           City’s website: sslc.gov

           Mayor Wood also congratulated Senior Engineer, Ed Rufener, on his retirement
           after 40 years with the City.

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          City Attorney, Josh Collins, informed the Council that the Mayor was in the process
          of interviewing the candidates for the open Justice Court Judge position. The final
          candidate would be presented for the Council’s consent and approval at a future
          meeting.

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Member Williams wished everyone a Happy New Year.

           Council Member Mitchell wished everyone a Happy New Year and congratulated
           Mr. Rufener on his retirement.

           Council Member Thomas congratulated Mr. Rufener on his retirement.

           Council Member Bynum congratulated Mr. Rufener and then highlighted the
           importance of security training considering the recent data breach that Granite
           District employees and patrons have experienced.
