 3.           An ordinance amending Provo City code to modify and enact impact fees for wastewater and to
              modify provisions regarding the imposition and administration of impact fees. (22-020). (17:33)

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-10, as currently constituted, has been made by
                      council rule.

John Borget, Director of Administrative Services, introduced item and turned time over to Susie Becker, who
helped conduct the analysis.

Susie Becker of Zions Public Financial discussed the changes in the impact fees.

Chair Hoban opened item for public comment.

Ilene Miller, Utah Valley Homebuilders Association, asked that the existing building permits be under the old
impact fees instead of the newer fees. She requested that this be done in writing for security.

With no other public comment, Chair Hoban closed public comment and brought the item back for council
discussion.

Mr. Borget responded to Ms. Miller’s question/proposal and disclosed that any building permit that is accepted
prior to July 1, they would be assessed at the old fee. He said that any building permit that is accepted after July 1,
the new rates would apply.

Dave Decker, Director of Public Works, added that there might be projects underway prior to building permits
being issued.

Brian Jones added that council needs to be careful with how we address the issue and how it is worded.

Councilor Handley asked if this option is the only option available.

Ms. Becker provided examples of what other cities have been doing and what concerns might need to be
considered.

Mr. Borget provided another alternative in response to Councilor Handley’s question.

Councilor MacKay agreed that the impact fees be put on new homebuilders. She also mentioned she is in favor of
the cost being locked in at the time of the building permit.

Councilor Whipple also agreed that she is in favor of allowing builders 90 days to acquire building permits under
the old rates.

 6.           An ordinance amending the Provo City General Plan regarding Southwest Area development
              policies. Citywide (PLGPA20210242) (1:15:20)

        Motion:       An implied motion to approve Ordinance 2022-12, as currently constituted, has been made by
                      council rule.

Hannah Salzl, Planner, gave summary and invited individuals to watch the work session where this item was
discussed in more detail.

Councilor MacKay said she is happy this item will be completed and that it has the support of the planning
commission, the neighborhood, and the developers.

Chair Hoban thanked staff for their dedication and hard work. He also asked Mr. Peperone if the Southwest Area
development policies assist with getting developments going on the west side and provides clarity for developers.

Bill Peperone, Development Services Director, said he is much happier with this version than prior versions. He
stated this ordinance provides council with some leverage.

Councilor Handley asked what rights council may have to approve or deny developments.

Chair Hoban asked how we approach the westside to make sure future developments follow the guidelines.

Wayne Parker provided history of general plan and how it has affected the westside.

Chair Hoban shared his concern of how to plan for growth in westside and asked for direction from Mr. Peperone.

Mr. Peperone added that there have been changes to other areas that provide for some wiggle room.

Councilor Ellsworth shared her desire to have a process that is fairer at multiple points in time.

Councilor MacKay clarified that developers would have to stick to the four units per acre and would not be able to
have higher density that would limit future developments.

Chair Hoban stated that he does not want to make mistakes from history repeat themselves and suggested council
look to Mr. Peperone and his department for guidance.

Mr. Peperone said no plan is perfect from the beginning and that amendments will need to be made with any plan.

Mr. Jones pointed out some specific language in the ordinance.

Chair Hoban opened public comment. With none, he closed public comment.

Councilor MacKay asked about the green space and who would be responsible for that space after development.
