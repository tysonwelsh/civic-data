                                    TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 22- 11


AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING THE VACATION
 OF CERTAIN PORTIONS OF TAYLORSVILLE CITY RIGHT-OF- WAY LOCATED
 ADJACENT TO LOT 1 OF THE HIDDEN PLACE SUBDIVISION AND CONVEYING
 THE VACATED PROPERTY TO THE OWNER OF LOT 1 OF THE HIDDEN PLACE
                                          SUBDIVISION.


       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville          City Council ( the " Council") met during its regularly
scheduled meeting on November 16, 2022, to consider, among other things, approving the vacation
of certain portions of Taylorsville City Right- of-Way located adjacent to Lot 1 of the Hidden Place
Subdivision and conveying the vacated property to the owner of Lot 1 of the Hidden Place
Subdivision; and


       WHEREAS,        pursuant   to UTAH CODE ANN. § 10- 9a- 609. 5( 3),
                                                                        the City Engineer has
submitted a petition requesting to vacate a portion of the City' s right-of-way consisting of
approximately 2, 258 square feet located adjacent to Lot 1 of the Hidden Place Subdivision and
subsequently conveying the vacated right- of-way property to the owner of Lot 1 of the Hidden
Place Subdivision via quitclaim deed; and


        WHEREAS, the proposed vacation will result in a right-of-way width for that portion of
Hidden Place of fifty( 50) feet, which is consistent with the minimum width requirement for a local
street as required by TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE § 13. 21. 100( G); and



       WHEREAS, in accordance with UTAH CODE ANN. § 10- 9a- 609. 5( 3)            and § 10- 9a- 208,   the

City Council held a public hearing on November 16, 2022, to determine whether ( 1) good cause
exists for the vacation and ( 2) the public interest or any person will be materially injured by the
proposed vacation; and




        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the City Council has determined that there is good
cause to grant the vacation and that neither the public interest nor any person will be materially
injured by granting the proposed vacation; and

        WHERAS, after careful consideration, the City Council has determined that it would be
in the best interests ofthe health, safety, and welfare ofthe citizens of the City to grant the proposed
vacation.




        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
petition to vacate a portion of the City' s right-of-way consisting of approximately 2, 258 square
feet located adjacent to Lot 1 of the Hidden Place Subdivision, as described in Exhibit A, is hereby
approved.




        BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City administration is hereby authorized to
convey the vacated right-of-way property to the owner of Lot 1 of the Hidden Place Subdivision
via quitclaim deed contemporaneously with this grant of vacation.
