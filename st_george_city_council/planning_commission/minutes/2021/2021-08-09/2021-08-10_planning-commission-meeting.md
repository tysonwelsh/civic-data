1. ZONE CHANGE (ZC) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      A. Consider a request to change the Zoning from C-4 (Commercial) to PD-C (Planned
         Development Commercial) to allow the County to redevelop their property for a new parking
         garage. The property is approximately 2.03 acres. This property is generally located on the
         northwest corner of Tabernacle and Temple Street (200 East). Please see map on back. The
         applicant is Washington County, and the representative is Nicholle Felshaw. The project will be
         known as Washington County Garage. Case No. 2021-ZC-054. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

          Carol Davidson Presented the following:

          Carol Davidson – This garage will go with their new building. The existing building is included
          with this zone change but they don’t know what they are doing with it yet so we will only go
          over the garage. The property is zoned C-4. The parking garage will be entered off of 200 E
          Temple Street. The parking garage will have 353 spaces, the building requires 253 spaces, so
          they meet the parking requirement. There are 4 levels to the garage. On the roof they will be

         doing a sports court area. There will be a track with some sports courts in the middle. There will
         be a place for a moveable stage. They are proposing that this may be used for special events
         later, they will go through the special event process. The use list seems to fit the area. This
         approval is only for the parking garage. The other portion will need to come back with a PD

         Commissioner Larsen – Will lighting come back through at some point?

         Carol Davidson – That will be looked at with the site plan review.

         Commissioner Larsen – Will the City have access to it? Or is it just for the County?

         Carol Davidson – I don’t know but the applicant is here, and we can ask them.

         Commissioner West – Is this going to be used for public parking and if so can the public spaces
         be on the 1st and 2nd floors and then have the employee parking on the 3rd and 4th floors?

         Commissioner Larsen – And can there be signage on the outside so that people will know.

         John Willis – The other parking garage was a joint venture, that’s why they have public parking.
         This is the parking for the county building, and we are looking at this for the demands of the
         building.

         Carol Davidson – If they are having events then they will have the garage open.

         Nicholle Felshaw – I am here to answer any questions. A parking structure is a big venture for
         the county. There are a lot of unknowns. We are in desperate need of parking. There was
         concern about parking when we came through for the new building. This parking structure will
         help with that. We don’t know what will happen to the building that we are currently in, there
         may be a new building in its place and then we have the property that faces the boulevard that
         we don’t know what will be in there. We took all of this space in account for the parking. I
         think it is good to point out, we do think security is a big issue for parking garages. We are
         currently working with Watts Construction and our architect to completely secure that facility at
         night after hours so that there can’t be traffic in and out when it is closed down. We are actually
         putting in what I’ve been told is an astronomical amount of cameras. I really don’t want any
         dead spaces within that parking garage. I think it could evolve over time. We are really looking
         at it to make sure our county employees are taken care of and the patrons that come in to do
         county work are taken care of.

         Chair Draper – So the top portion on the building, the sport courts etc., will only be for the
         county employees?

         Nicholle Felshaw – So we would let them be used in conjunction with events we would put on.

         Chair Draper – So they won’t be open to the public?

         Nicholle Felshaw – We are going to be conservative and see how it goes. We may at some point
         open it to the public in certain hours.

         Commissioner West – I love the idea that it could be an event space.

3. HISTORIC PRESERVATION LANDMARK STATUS (HPC) Administrative

         Consider a request to designate the home at 135 South 100 East as a landmark site to be known
         as the Frederick Blake Home. The applicant is David Healey. Historic Preservation Commission
         Case No. 2021-HPC-004. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

         Carol Davidson presented the following:

         Carol Davidson – Showed an aerial map of the actual location and pictures of how the house
         looks and how they would like to change it. This house was built by Fredrick Blakes father,
         Benjamin Blake, he was a master carpenter. They took the esthetics from the classical Victorian
         era. We did take this to the Historic Preservation Commission, and they did also propose some
         changes to the home. They want to paint the shutters a dark green color. Redo the trim and
         paint it white. They will make the door a different color. This is a symmetrical building but for
         some reason they didn’t put another window on the inside first floor. They want to add another
         to make it symmetrical. They do want to add a railing around the upper deck for safety issues.
         They also want to put steps in off the back of the home from the French doors. It has had some
         additions. This does have a plaque, but it is not designated as a landmark at this time. The
         Historic Preservation Commission does recommend approval.

         Commissioner Larsen – Would the applicant make the stairs accessible?

         John Willis – That would be governed by the ADA if there was a need.

         Commissioner Kemp – So could this come back through for a short-term rental?

         Carol Davidson – Yes.

         Commissioner Kemp – What is the square footage?

         Carol Davidson – That part I don’t know, I do apologize for that, they were unable to make it
         here. I do have their phone number if we need to contact them, I don’t know the square footage.

         Commissioner Larsen – I love the fact that they are willing to spend the money and put the time
         into this home. I think it’s a lovely addition to the area and I think the changes they made all fall
         in line, like the window in the bathroom.

         Chair Draper – I agree.
