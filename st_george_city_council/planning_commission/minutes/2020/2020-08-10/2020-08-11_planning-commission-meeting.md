2.    CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT

      Consider a request for a Conditional Use Permit to operate a bar establishment in the arts district
      [historic district] overlay zone located at 61 West St. George Blvd. The applicant is Kelli Payne,
      Hive Taphouse Inc. Case No. 2020-CUP-007 (Staff – Genna Goodwin)


      Genna Goodwin presented the following:

      Genna Goodwin – This project will be located on the ground floor of the City View project.
      There are four licenses available in the Arts District there are two licenses currently, Wood, Ash
      and Rye and The Firestation. The project will 3700 square feet.

      Commissioner Larsen – Is the parking provided for this project in the parking garage?

      Genna Goodwin – Yes, it is part of the City View project so they can park in that garage.

      Commissioner Curtis – I do have questions regarding the letter, Mr. McArthur mentioned issues
      with distance to parks and other things. Do you know about the letter and maybe the questions?

      Genna Goodwin – Yes, I am familiar with the letter, we went through this whole process with
      Wood, Ash and Rye last year and there is some terminology that differs such as Zion Square is a
      square not a park, Town Square is a square not a park. Most of that will be regulated in another
      part of this project.

      Commissioner Larsen – I am wondering about the parking. That area is really congested.

      Victoria Hales – City View does have a three-story parking garage where there are parking stalls
      designated for residential and commercial uses in City View.

      Commissioner Draper – I don’t see why we would deny it there.

      Commissioner Larsen – I don’t have a problem with having another bar in St George, I just don’t

3.    PRELIMINARY PLATS

      A. Consider a preliminary plat for a fifty lot (50) lot residential subdivision for The Vistas at the
      Ledges. The site is located at approximately 1500 West Ledges Parkway. The property is on
      6.96 acres and is zoned PD-R (Planned Development Residential). The representative is Logan
      Blake, Development Solutions. Case No. 2020-PP-025 (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

      Wes Jenkins presented the following:

      Wes Jenkins – One of the changes from what is in your packet is that they are doing this in one
      phase. They have 50 units which triggers a second access. They will be finishing the landscape
      strip that has not been developed along here. They will have to improve that with this
      subdivision and bring it up to City standard. They will have a second access into Winchester
      Hills, leaving off these two parking spaces.

      Commissioner Curtis – In the amenity is that a park and maybe a water feature?

      Wes Jenkins – Yes, I think so, that was submitted as part of that PD zone change a couple years
      ago.

      Victoria Hales – Did the County approve the road for access through Winchester Hills?

      Wes Jenkins – Yes, I emailed the County and he said he would be ok with that.

      Victoria Hales – Originally there was a buffer space between The Ledges and Winchester Hills.
      It looks like the parking lot is in that buffer. Is that allowed in the buffer?

      Wes Jenkins – Yes, it was a 100 ft setback with no buildings, so it meets that.

      Victoria Hales – It would have to be contingent upon them getting that second access through the
      county.


      MOTION: Commissioner Curtis made a motion to recommend approval to the City Council for
      a preliminary plat for the Vistas with all of the conditions discussed by staff, including obtaining
      a secondary access through the County public road to the north through Winchester Hills.

      B. Consider a preliminary plat for a twenty-one (21) lot residential subdivision for Stone Cliff
      Phase 17. The site is located along Granite Drive in the Stone Cliff Development. The property
      is on 14.81 acres and is zoned PD-R (Planned Development Residential). The representative is
      Chanse Snow, Civil Science. Case No. 2020-PP-024 (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

      Wes Jenkins presented the following:

      Commissioner Brager recused himself on this item.

      Wes Jenkins – This is phase 17, they do have a 100 ft setback on structures along the area where
      the road is. The only change on this one from your packet is that the City required them to make
      this common area because water and sewer have to come through it to service this and tie into
      what is in here, they designed a road here and put a crash gate so that public and private don’t
      mix. Basically, we changed it to where they will keep it common area here and the road will go
      straight through. One of the concerns I had is that there is quite a bit of drainage.

      Victoria Hales – So they are taking the drainage down the public street.

      Wes Jenkins – I asked them to eliminate the road and keep the drainage on their project. They
      will have future development that will take it out to 1450 South, but they will have to deal with it
      in the interim.

      Victoria Hales – Are you going to make them have an easement?

      Wes Jenkins – They will have to have an easement until they develop the other portion. They
      will have to take the drainage and other utilities out to 1450 South through their private property
      off the site of this development and provide easements necessary for this purpose. It is too
      difficult to turn utilities multiple times to get them down the private street route to the west, so
      we want them to have a more direct route.

      Victoria Hales – My legal request would be that those utilities be dealt with under terms
      satisfactory to the City.

      MOTION: Commissioner Larsen made a motion to recommend approval to City Council for a
      preliminary plat Stone Cliff Phase 17 with all staff conditions as discussed, and as specified by
      Wes Jenkins and legal counsel motion amended to, under terms satisfactory to the City.

      C. Consider a preliminary plat for a one (1) lot commercial subdivision for White Hills
      Commercial Subdivision Phase 1. The site is located at the northeast corner of the intersection
      of 3050 East and 750 North. The property is on 1.394 acres and is zoned C-3 (General
      Commercial). The representative is Eric McFadden, Premier Design & Engineering. Case No.
      2020-PP-026 (Staff – Wes Jenkins)

      Wes Jenkins presented the following:

      Victoria Hales – There were temporary access issues and drainage issues with this.

      Wes Jenkins – The ground drops off pretty quickly and they can’t get the drainage back out to
      3050 so we will have to have an easement and deed restriction. The intent is eventually all the
      drainage and sewer will end up going to their east property line and then find its way down
      through that Millcreek subdivision.

      Victoria Hales – What about the road that is stubbed to nowhere? Where will the public turn
      around?

      Wes Jenkins – Eventually it’s dedicated, and the road will go all the way through to Washington
      City. It will need a turn around. The problem with this property is that the drainage and sewer
      wall need to go through on that road. They will get a deed restriction on the property next to
      them because it is owned by them. Then they are proposing their access on the south until the
      other property is developed.

      Victoria Hales – There will need to be agreements in place for all three conditions.

      Chairman Fisher – I would like to mention that a motion should be subject to the conditions that
      Victoria mentioned.

      Commissioner Curtis – I’m not sure I’m comfortable voting on something that still has some
      significant unanswered questions.

      Commissioner Larsen – I think those can be resolved with the plat, when they move forward.
