PUBLIC HEARING:
PC 24-025 LDC Climate Controlled Self Storage [Code Amendment] David Adams and Daren

Jackson request an amendment to the Land Development Code to allow climate-controlled self-
storage units as a conditional use in the Commercial Services (CS) zone.

Staff: Russ Holley, Logan City Planner, reviewed the request to amend the Land Development Code
(LDC) to conditionally permit “Climate Controlled Self Storage” in the Commercial Service (CS) zone.
The request is to create a new land use table category for this type of land use. The existing LDC
land use table has a “storage, commercial self-storage” category that only allows that use in the
Airport (AP) zone. The applicant defines the new land use as buildings that are climate-controlled,
no larger than 33,000 SF in footprint size, and less than three stories in height. The applicant did not
define what a climate-controlled building would be.

The proposed request would allow an applicant to build multiple 33,000 SF one-story buildings and
sprawl them out over numerous acres, it does not prevent the development of the typical commercial
self-storage project.

The commercial self-storage use was eliminated in the Commercial Service (CS) and Industrial Park
(IP) zones in 2022 because they are land consumptive, provide minimal employment opportunities,
and do not contribute to the City’s tax base.

R. Holley pointed out that the proposed code language would allow an applicant to build multiple
33,000 SF one-story buildings and sprawl them out over numerous acres. It does not prevent the
development of the typical commercial self-storage project.

The CS zone is one of the smaller land areas within the City with fewer acres available for new
development, let alone commercial self-storage types of uses.
