2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Fiber Network Update
Shaye Ruitenbeek, with the Fiber Department, gave an update on the project. They released the
ISP pricing for residential homes. She stated that they are looking at government programs that
offer even lower options for low income families. Carl Sundsrom gave an update on the
construction and informed the Council that the buildings will have fencing around them.

2.2 Power Rates Update and Discussion – Joel Eves
Joel reported that there are external pressures in the energy industry that’s increasing power costs
such as regional weather conditions, increased cost of natural gas, and low hydro production. He
stated that current losses this year are at 6 million. Mr. Eves stated that he reached out to the rate
consultant, and he had suggested that the city implement the power adjustment clause to avoid
further loss. With a new rate increase, the city’s rates would remain comparatively low. He said
that they will plan to use some reserve funds to cover the loss. The Council requested that they
see some actuals so they understand what the increase will be to resident’s energy bill. They
would also like to see more specifics on why the industry is so volatile right now.

2.3 Discussion of Family Haven Development Agreement, accommodating use other than elder
care (approximately 322 N 2375 W)- Planning Department
Kim Struthers stated that the purpose of this discussion is to see if the Council would be willing
to consider a Development Agreement for the Family Haven Building. He suggested that the

4. Sale of Real Property
   a. Public Hearing
   The Mayor opened the public hearing.

    Marlin Eldred, Economic Development Director, stated that the city was approached by a
    company that wants to purchase the land. He said that any development of the property would
    need to go through the standard public process. He said that the contract can be void if any
    development proposals are rejected.

    Brian McCuiston expressed concern that by allowing this, it will allow the developer to move
    forward with a new zoning designation.

    Angie Lethbridge also expressed concern with a zone change and not meeting the connectivity
    standard on future development.

    Jamie Auster stated that she would prefer that the Council allow ADUs on smaller lot sizes.

    Discussion by the Council included that approving the contract does not mean it’s a done deal
    and it does not mean that a zone change would be approved. Discussion also included that the
    Council shouldn’t approve the sales contract if they don’t want a zone change here.

    b. Consideration of declaring Parcel #12:027:0075 as surplus property and authorizing the
    Mayor to dispose of it.

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to declare Parcel #12:027:0075 as surplus property and
                authorizes the Mayor to dispose of it by signing the purchase sales contract that was
                in the Council packet. Councilor Albrecht seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, No; Councilor Hancock, Yes.
    Councilor Koivisto, No; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed with 3 in favor
    and 2 opposed.
