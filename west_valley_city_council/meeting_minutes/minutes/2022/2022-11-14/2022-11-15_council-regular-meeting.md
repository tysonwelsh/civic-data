APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING HELD NOVEMBER 1, 2022
AND SPECIAL MEETING HELD NOVEMBER 8, 2022
The Council considered the Minutes of the Regular Meeting held November 1, 2022 and
Special Meeting of November 8, 2022. There were no changes, corrections or deletions.

Councilmember Nordfelt moved to approve the Minutes of the Regular Meeting held
November 1, 2022 and Special Meeting of November 8, 2022. Councilmember Christensen
seconded the motion.

A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

PROCLAMATIONS
A.  PROCLAMATION DECLARING NOVEMBER 19, 2022 AS “WORLD
    SUICIDE PREVENTION DAY” IN WEST VALLEY CITY
    Councilmember Huynh read a Proclamation Declaring November 19, 2022 as World
    Suicide Prevention Day in West Valley City.


COMMENT PERIOD
A.  PUBLIC COMMENTS
    Lawrence Horman stated he is here to advocate for the homeless and noted that he
    too is homeless. He provided a handout Deseret Defiant Initiative and described
    how the City can help find a solution at a local level.

       Myles Winn stated that the speed study on his street has not been completed and he
       would still like to see the tree on Parkway Blvd be trimmed.

       Jim Vesock detailed My Hometown events. He expressed concern about Code
       Enforcement officers and stated they need to be better trained.

       Richard Nowak stated that he has felt harassed by Code Enforcement. He expressed
       concern about receiving a notice of violation for a fence that has existed on his
       property.

B.     CITY MANAGER COMMENTS
       Wayne Pyle, City Manager, had no comments.

C.     CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
       Councilmember Huynh expressed concern about Code Enforcement officers.

A.   ACCEPT PUBLIC COMMENT REGARDING APPLICATION GPZ-8-2022,
     FILED BY BRYAN AND CHANTEL BRADFORD, REQUESTING A
     GENERAL PLAN ZONE CHANGE FROM LARGE LOT RESIDENTIAL
     TO LOW DENSITY RESIDENTIAL AND A ZONE CHANGE FROM A TO
     R-1-8 FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT 3816 SOUTH 3200 WEST
     Mayor Lang informed a public hearing had been advertised for the Regular Council
     Meeting scheduled November 15, 2022, in order for the City Council to hear and
     consider public comments regarding application GPZ-8-2021, filed by Chantel
     Bradford, requesting a General Plan Zone Change from Large Lot Residential to Low
     Density Residential and a Zone Change from A to R-1-8.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            The subject property includes a home that was built in 1941. According to
            the applicants, they would like to subdivide the property into three lots,
            keep the existing home on one of the lots, and build two new homes on the
            other two lots. The applicants have also indicated that they plan to move
            the current detached garage closer to the existing home and improve the
            appearance of the structure.

     Mayor Lang opened the Public Hearing. There being no one to speak in favor or
     opposition, Mayor Lang closed the Public Hearing.

     ACTION: ORDINANCE NO. 22-39, AMEND THE GENERAL PLAN TO
     SHOW A CHANGE OF LAND USE FROM LARGE LOT RESIDENTIAL (2
     TO 3 UNITS/ACRE) TO LOW DENSITY RESIDENTIAL (3 TO 4
     UNITS/ACRE) FOR PROPERTY LOCATED AT 3816 SOUTH 3200 WEST
     The City Council previously held a public hearing regarding proposed Ordinance
     22-40 that would amend the General Plan to show a change of land use from Large
     Lot Residential to Low Density Residential for Property Located at 3816 S 3200 W

      3200 W.

      Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
      information as follows:

             Bryan and Chantel Bradford have submitted a General Plan/zone change
             application (GPZ-8-2022) on 0.69 acres to change the General Plan from
             Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Low Density Residential (3 to 4
             units/acre) and the zoning from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre)
             to R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square
             feet). The Planning Commission recommended approval of the zone change
             subject to a development agreement.

             The proposed development agreement addresses the number of new homes,
             standards for the new homes, required maintenance, improvements to the
             existing home, replacement of the detached garage, and a rear yard setback
             exception for the existing home.

      Upon inquiry by Mayor Lang there were no further questions from members of the
      City Council, and she called for a motion.

             Councilmember Harmon moved to approve Resolution 22-151.

             Councilmember Huynh seconded the motion.
