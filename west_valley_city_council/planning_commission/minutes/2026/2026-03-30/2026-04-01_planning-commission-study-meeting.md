 MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING

WEDNESDAY, APRIL 1, 2026, AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600
CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS
CALLED TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY COMMISSIONER LOVATO.

PRESENTATIONS:


SV-1-2026      Petition by West Valley City requesting consideration to vacate a portion of
2300 South (Second Street) within the Hayne’s Addition Plat E Subdivision. The vacated
portion is located east of the Mountain View Corridor and west of Lester Street (5700
West). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov.)


Discussion: Brock is out of the office so Jody presented the petition by West Valley City
requesting a consideration to vacate a portion of 2300 South (Second Street) within the Hayne’s
Addition Plat E Subdivision. The vacated portion is located east of the Mountain View Corridor
and west of Lester Street (5700 West).
Commissioner Winters asked if street vacations also go through City Council or just the Planning
Commission.
Jody responded that they go to City Council, so the Planning Commission gives a
recommendation, and the City Council make the final determination on what to do with the right-
of-way.
C-2-2026      Petition by Hayley Pratt, representing Castlewood Development, requesting
conditional use approval for a 330-unit apartment development. The property is located at
2940 West 3650 South on 3.02 acres and is zoned CC (City Center) and R-2-8 (Two Unit
Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-
963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).


Discussion: Brock is out of the office so Jody presented the petition by Hayley Pratt,
representing Castlewood Development, requesting conditional use approval for a 330-unit
apartment development. The property is located at 2940 West 3650 South on 3.02 acres and is
zoned CC. This project was previously approved and is set to expire so they are re-applying with
the same proposal to keep the approval valid.


C-3-2026      Petition by Russel Platt, representing Truong Development, requesting
conditional use approval for a Restaurant, Martial Arts Studio, and a Pickleball building.
The property is located at 1277 West 3300 South on 1.65 acres, is zoned C2, General
Commercial and is located withing the Jordan River Overlay Zone. (Staff – Jody Knapp at
801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov.)


Discussion: Jody presented the request by Russel Platt, representing Truong Development,
requesting conditional use approval for a Restaurant, Martial Arts Studio, and a Pickleball
building.
Commissioner Woodruff asked if pickleball courts are inside the building?
Jody responded that they are all indoors, and that both facilities will be for indoor recreational
use. They will function more like a gym without tournaments due to lack of parking.
Commissioner Winters asked about parking being short because he believes with all 3 of those
uses there could be very high parking needs. Jody said the hope was that a lot of the residential
people would walk over and use it. She also states that the restaurant and the pickle ball court
have enough spaces, but they are short 23 spaces for the fitness center and that they might have
to make a building smaller, or get rid of a pickleball court to accommodate more parking.
Commissioner Lovato asked how they are planning on finishing the west side of that property or
if it was going to be left open so there can be an overflow? He also asks if the power company
owns the west side and if they would approve parking on their lot? He wonders if the business is
assuming they can have overflow parking there.
Jody said the power company does own it and that the business is showing fencing to keep the
properties separate and that they wouldn’t want people doing that unless it was paved. Jody said
the current use there is an old auto sales from the 70’s and they’ve had problems with
enforcement there and the health department has been involved. That lease is up in May so
hopefully that will all be cleared off when the new business comes in. If there was a formal
agreement then it might be a possibility, but they were not able to get a signed affidavit with this
application.
Commissioner Lovato says we’ve done this before, where maybe a building is too big, or they
have too many buildings, it just seems like we need to have adequate parking for what it
matches. Jody explains they were initially going to use it as a warehouse, which is prohibited in
the JROZ, so when they changed it to a different use they didn’t take into account the parking
codes.
Commissioner Woodruff asked if they had a spot for a dumpster, and mentions the fire truck
turnaround.
Commissioner Lovato asks how you turn a fire truck around in that?
Jody mentions that dumpsters need to be 20 feet away from residential and enclosed, and that the
restaurant will most likely need one. Also, the Fire Department has reviewed the proposal and
feels they can accommodate the requirements.
Commissioner Woodruff also asked about restrooms in the pickleball building. Jody responded
they will need them and that the plans are more conceptual at this point, but they would need
restrooms.
Jerry Thompson from the Building Inspection Division agreed that the plans are very conceptual
and that they anticipate that there will be a lot of changes to comply with various building code
requirements.
Commissioner Winters asked about needing all the landscaping around the pickleball courts and
if it meets the requirements. Jody replies that they need 15% and they are showing that they are
right at 15%, but that she has not verified that. She mentioned it looked like a lot. Commissioner
Winters said maybe losing some of that would help with parking.
Commissioner Drozdek asked what the hours of operation are. Jody responds that it is mostly
likely 10:00 pm. She doesn’t think they plan on a 24/7 type of use, but that she will verify.
Commissioner Drozdek asked if that includes the restaurant hours. Jody says yes.
Commissioner Drozdek asked what the capacity was for it. Jody replies that the restaurant is just
under 2,000 square feet, 1,998.
Commissioner Drozdek asked if they were the same owners as the residential on the east side.
Jody said no.
Commissioner Drozdek asked if they stated what type of restaurant they were going to put in.
Jody said no, but they are the same group that did the Cobalt Development on Redwood Road.
They have retail in the front with residential on the back and they were able to get some pretty
good restaurants.
There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the meeting
was adjourned at 4:57 PM.
