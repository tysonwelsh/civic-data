2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Wellbeing Survey Results- Courtney Flint, Utah State University
    Professor Courtney Flint, with Utah State University, reported on the results of the wellbeing
    survey. This was a follow up survey to the one conducted last year. This time they hoped to
    reach more people online and they were successful by receiving 295 completed surveys. The
    average length of residency in Lehi was 13.6 years. Personal wellbeing in Lehi was rated at
    4.09 (scale of 1-5), and they rated the overall wellbeing of the community at 3.62, suggesting
    that their wellbeing is better than others in the City. She said that they are finding a relation
    between the overall wellbeing and connection to community question, so those that score high
    on wellbeing also score high on having a community connection in Lehi. Local environmental
    quality surveyed high for importance to people but was not rated very high. They also found
    that landscape features have a positive impact on wellbeing. Most feel that the City is growing
    too fast. Professor Flint stated that it’s important to note that this was surveyed pre COVID-19
    pandemic. The survey was available through social media and other online outlets.

2.2 Update on the COVID-19 Virus- Jason Walker, City Administrator
    Jason Walker gave an update on the current pandemic. He stated that the State is shifting from
    the red to orange zone. He emphasized the importance of everyone wearing masks even if they
    are cloth. He said the first responders are using M95 masks and that the masks are important
    to reduce transmission of the disease. Mr. Walker said that the State is creating a program to
    provide masks to as many people as possible. He stated that many mandates will remain the
    same even though they are moving to the orange zone. The State is still recommending that
