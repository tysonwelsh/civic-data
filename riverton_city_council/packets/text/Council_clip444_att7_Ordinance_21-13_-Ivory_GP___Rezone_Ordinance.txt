                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 21-13

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN & REZONING 8.9 ACRES LOCATED
      AT 11850 S 3600 W, AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN FROM LOW DENSITY
 RESIDENTIAL TO MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL, AND REZONING THE PROPERTY
 FROM C-N TO RM-6/EHOV, BRYON PRINCE REPRESENTING IVORY DEVELOPMENT,
                                  APPLICANT

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

      WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said General Plan and zoning
amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City General Plan from Estate Density Residential to Low Density Residential and
to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map by rezoning the 8.9 acres located at 111850 S 3600 W from C-N
to RM-6-EHOV as described herein.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended
                              to reflect the following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

               Section 2.              The zoning shall include the stipulation that all units adjacent to
                                       the west property line shall be single family detached only.

               Section 3.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
May, 2021 by the following vote:
