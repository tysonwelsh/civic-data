                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 24-43

 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT
WITH PRONGHORN CONSTRUCTION FOR THE COMPLETION OF THE TRESTLE
          LANE PHASE II ROADWAY IMPROVEMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, Purchasing bid out the Trestle Lane Phase II Roadway Improvement
Project and the bids came in over budget; and

      WHEREAS, the Public Works Director would still like to proceed with this project; and

        WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve $80,000.00 as an added expense
in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
      1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with Pronghorn
         Construction for the completion of the Trestle Lane Phase II Roadway Improvement
         Project.

      2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 16th day of
April 2024 by the following vote:
