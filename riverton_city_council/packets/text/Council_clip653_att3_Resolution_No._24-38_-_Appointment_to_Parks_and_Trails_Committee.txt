                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 24-38

         A RESOLUTION APPOINTING VOLUNTEER MEMBERS TO THE
        RIVERTON CITY PARKS, RECREATION AND TRAILS COMMITTEE

       WHEREAS, in accordance with Riverton City Ordinance, the Mayor shall appoint with
advice and consent of the City Council, residents of Riverton City to serve on the Parks,
Recreation & Trails Committee; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor hereby confirms his nomination for the volunteer appointment of
Alex Johnson; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the appointee is a
resident of Riverton City and is willing and capable of serving on the Parks, Recreation and
Trails Committee; and

        WHEREAS, the Council hereby determines that it is in the best interest of the health,
safety and welfare of the citizens of Riverton to consent to the appointment of the Mayor's
nomination to the Parks, Recreation and Trails Committee.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
        1. The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointment of Alex Johnson
           to the Riverton City Parks, Recreation and Trails Committee, which term shall
           end December 31, 2025.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 2nd day of
