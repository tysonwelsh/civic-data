     SUBDIVISION LOTS 10, 11, 14, AND 15 AMENDED
     Steve Lehman, CED, discussed proposed Ordinance 20-03 that would approve the
     amendment of lots 10A and 14A of the West Valley Truck Center Subdivision Lots
     10, 11, 14, and 15 Amended.

     Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included
     information as follows:

            James Warner, representing TruckPro L.C., is requesting a plat amendment
            for lots 10A and 14A in the West Valley Truck Center Subdivision. The
            proposed subdivision is intended to consolidate these lots into one lot and
            to vacate an existing public utility easement separating these two lots.

            The West Valley Truck Center Subdivision was recorded in 1998. The
            subdivision has been a great asset to the City and to businesses in the
            trucking industry. Lots platted in the original subdivision were to
            accommodate a variety of uses associated with the trucking industry.
            Although many of the original lots remain as platted, a few modifications
            have taken place over the years to accommodate changes within the
            subdivision and the trucking industry.

            The proposed change to lots 10A and 14A is relatively simple. The mutual
            property line separating these lots will go away with one large lot remaining.
            The purpose for the amended plat is to accommodate an expansion of the
            Sprinter facility. An addition to the north side of the building would be
            constructed over the property line and existing utility easement. The overall
            lot size will end up being 5 acres.

            The existing public utility easement will be vacated and will be relocated
            along the perimeter of the subdivision. An existing Rocky Mountain Power
            line will be relocated as a result of this amended plat. Other utility
            companies have signed off on the vacation of the existing easement.

     Councilmember Fitisemanu asked if the developer will pay for the vacation and
     relocation of the easement. Steve replied yes.

     The City Council will consider Ordinance 20-03 at the Regular Council Meeting
     scheduled January 28, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

RESOLUTION 20-08: AUTHORIZE WEST VALLEY CITY TO ENTER INTO AN
AGREEMENT WITH TECHNOLOGY PROVIDERS INCORPORATED FOR
THE PURCHASE AND INSTALLATION OF AUDIO/VISUAL EQUIPMENT

Ken Cushing, IT, presented proposed Resolution 20-08 that would authorize West Valley
City to enter into an agreement with Technology Providers incorporated for the purchase
and installation of Audio/Visual Equipment.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       The existing Audio / Visual system is decades old, sometimes unreliable, and does
       not meet the current needs and expectations of citizens or city staff. The
       replacement of this equipment was approved in a Budget Program Mod. This Audio
       / Visual System is part of that budget. City staff will use the equipment to record,
       broadcast, stream, and backup meetings, as well as present information in the
       rooms.

       Formal bidding will not be required since Technology Providers Inc. is on the State
       Contract. This system uses modern technology and meets industry standards. It
       allows for expansion so the city can use additional video input and video screens
       throughout our network as needed in the future.

The City Council will consider Resolution 20-08 at the Regular Council Meeting scheduled
January 28, 2020 at 6:30 P.M.

CONSIDER APPLICATION S-9-2019, FILED BY TY CRAGUN, REQUESTING
FINAL PLAT APPROVAL FOR SCOTT MACHINERY SUBDIVISION
LOCATED AT 2245 SOUTH TECHNOLOGY PARK WAY
Steve Lehman, CED, presented proposed application S-9-2019, filed by Ty Cragun,
requesting Final Plat Approval for Scott Machinery Subdivision located at 2245 South
Technology Park Way.

Written documentation previously provided to the City Council included information as
follows:

       Ty Cragun, representing the property owner, is requesting final plat approval for
       the Scott Machinery Subdivision. The subject property is located in the
       manufacturing zone and is presently occupied with a commercial storage building.

       The proposed subdivision is being requested in order to divide the existing parcel
       into 2 lots. At the present time, an existing commercial storage building is located
       on what will become lot 2. It is anticipated that future uses on lot 1 will be light
       manufacturing, office and storage.

       Lot 1 will consist of 2.7 acres, while lot 2 will consist of 1.3 acres. Each lot will
       have its own independent access which meets separation requirements of the City
       ordinance. Both lots will contain public utility easements along the frontage for
       future utility needs. The odd configuration of lot 2 is to accommodate an existing
       windmill.

       At the present time, sidewalk does not exist along Technology Park Way. While
       sidewalk will be required on the east side of this street, it will be a requirement of
       the development and not the subdivision.

COMMUNICATIONS
A.  ANNUAL TRAINING REGARDING ETHICS, OPEN MEETINGS, AND
    SEXUAL HARASSMENT
    Eric Bunderson, City Attorney, stated that the State requires an annual training
    regarding ethics, open meetings, and sexual harassment. He stated that there is an
    interactive portion to the training that is required. Eric provided the Council with
    several fictional cases and plea bargains and asked the Council what their solution
    would be. The Council discussed each case and Eric detailed each rule (State Law)
    that applied to each scenario. Mayor Bigelow clarified that there have been no
    changes in the law. Eric replied no. Mayor Bigelow asked if the Council can have
    policy discussion on items that are not on the agenda that are brought forward by
    the public. Eric replied that this is determined by the presiding officer, the Mayor,
    but a vote cannot be taken on any topic that is not posted on the agenda.
    Councilmember Buhler asked if “public” can include the Council. Mayor Bigelow
    stated that the agenda specifies a difference between public and Council.
    Councilmember Buhler asked if the Council could discuss policy at an event that is
    noticed by the City. Eric replied that an agenda would need to be provided to discuss
    policy. Eric and the Council discussed GRAMA, protected, and public records.
