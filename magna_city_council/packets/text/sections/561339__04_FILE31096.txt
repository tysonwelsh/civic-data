                                               PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is requesting a rezone of the property at 1731 S. 8000 W. from A-20 (Agricultural zone) to M-2 (Commercial).

The subject property is 160 acres and currently vacant land. The applicant is seeking a rezone for three fourths of the
property, since one fourth of the property is already zoned M-2. The area already zone M-2 is in the northeast corner of
the parcel.

The applicant would like to build a liquid gas facility on the property. This facility would freeze natural gas to a liquid state
in the summer months and store the gas until the winter months when needed. It would also serve as a backup system to
provide natural gas to the Salt Lake Valley in the event of a disaster.




                               SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located on the east side of 8000 West, just north of 2100 South. The land is vacant.

To the north of the subject property is the Waste Control Management for asbestos disposal. And to the west is the
Kennecott Tailings Pond property.
