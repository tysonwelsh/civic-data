Johnson, Jim Phillips, Jim McWilliams, Jason Standring, Travis Worwood.

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City
Administrator Seth Atkinson offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Councilor Skip Worwood.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 7-21-20, the work session minutes 7-21-20, a list of claims on the
warrant register dated 8-4-20, and the sale of burial rights to Oliver Kester and Robin Kester Taylor.
Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSES APPROVED:

  City Recorder Lisa Brough reviewed the business license applications of C&B Tree Trimming and
Removal and B’s Beauty Salon. Councilor Worwood moved to approve both business licenses.
Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

   Donald Ball commented that he had been in contact with a lady that had contract the COVID-19 virus,
but the woman had recovered from the virus. He was told by Myrna Trauntvein that he would not be
contagious, so he decided to attend the council meeting. He stated to blame Myrna if anyone becomes
sick.

JOHNSON PROPERTY MOVED TO LEVEL ONE IN THE ANNEXATION AREA MAP:

  Councilor Seely moved to change the Johnson property on the south end of town to a Level 1 in the
annexation area map. This property will be part of the Biglow Annexation Petition. Councilor Ostler
seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CHRISTENSEN READY MIX, UTAH LANDSCAPING, INDUSTRIAL PARK 2 PROPERTY MOVED TO LEVEL TWO:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council that now that the council had expanded the growth
annexation area boundary and had included property owned by Christensen Ready Mix, Utah
Landscaping, and Nephi City’s Industrial Park 2 within the growth area, the owners of these properties
would like to have this area changed from a Level 3 to a Level 2 in preparation for an annexation
petition. Councilor Worwood moved to change the requested area to a Level 2 in the Annexation Area
Map. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BIGLOW ANNEXATION PETITION APPROVED TO MOVE FORWARD IN THE ANNEXATION PROCESS:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the Biglow Annexation Petition submitted by property owners
to the southwest of the city. The City Council recently changed the designation of these properties to a
Level 1 in the Annexation Area Map. The petition for annexation included a narrative describing the
reasons for annexation and the type of development it will contain. Atkinson explained that the
decision before the council is whether to allow the annexation process to move forward. Councilor
Worwood moved to let the petition move forward in the annexation process. Councilor Seely seconded
the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

GUN RANGE ANNEXATION PETITION APPROVED TO MOVE FORWARD IN THE ANNEXATION PROCESS:

  City Administrative Intern Kalea Pauole and Treasurer Travis Worwood presented the Gun Range
Annexation Petition. Ms. Pauole reviewed with the council that in order to assist with enforcing rules at
the range, the property needed to be annexed. She also stated that annexation would allow the city to
begin to make lasting improvements for the safety and security of all who visit the gun range and for the
property owner above the shooting range. The acceptance of annexation will also allow for the
possibility of municipal services being provided to the gun range. Councilor Ostler moved to let the
petition move forward in the annexation process. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the progress of the redundant gas line on Cow Lane.
  Mr. Atkinson reported that road construction on Highway 132 would stop during the fair.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Ostler reported that an Extreme Bull Riding competition will be held on September 19th.
  Councilor Seely reported on the Library Board meeting. He said that they have updated the Library
webpage, have expanded their programming, have purchased four new computers with a state grant.
  Mayor Nielson reported that a meeting on the Fieldhouse Project will be held tomorrow.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session
to discuss the competency of an individual. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a
unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 7:42 p.m.
