TECH RIDGE NEIGHBORHOOD HOUSE – GENERAL DISCUSSION BETWEEN THE
DEVELOPERS AND NEIGHBORS OF THE TECH RIDGE DEVELOPMENT:
     Isaac Barlow with Tech Ridge presented a PowerPoint presentation covering the
     following topics: Conceptual Master Plan; Let’s create a tech park in St. George!
     Why?; Public benefit; Maps of overall project, trail development, park land district
     allocations; Renderings of the buildings; Landscape plans; Mountain bike plan;
     Economic Development; How do we create a tech center?; Additional renderings;
     Walkability; Careers; Tech Ridge initial proposal vs. current proposal with regards to
     jobs, multifamily units, residential units for sale, and employment to housing ratio;
     Tech Ridge compared to Playa Vista; and Tech Ridge works like a start up.

       Discussions took place regarding traffic and access roads; density; view shed; height
       of buildings; aesthetics; Black Ridge Park; the scar on the hill; water resources; and
       how Tech Ridge will affect real estate in Washington County.

       The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
