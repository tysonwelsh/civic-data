TENTATIVE BUDGET PRESENTATION:

    Nephi City Budget Officer Seth Atkinson presented to the council a tentative biennial budget for fiscal
year ending June 30, 2022 and June 30, 2023. Mr. Atkinson informed the council that state statute
requires the presentation of the tentative budget on the first meeting in May. He stated that the City
Council had elected to prepare a two-year or biennial budget for the first time and listed the advantages
to this change. Mr. Atkinson stated that the budget reflects the priorities of the city council with the
main priority being the addition of a Drug Task Force officer. The additional officer will be funded in the
first year by surplus carryover funds from fiscal year 2021 and the budget assumes a property tax
increase in the second year of the biennium to fund the officer going forward. Councilor Memmott

MASTER FEE RESOLUTION 05-04-2021 ADOPTED:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the need to raise some building permit fees due to the
increase in inspection costs and that now the building inspector is charging a plan check fee. To limit the
city’s liability on plan review, it has become necessary to require a building permit application deposit of
$1,000. The deposit would be non-refundable and would be used to cover the plan check fee. When
the permit is issued, the $1,000 will be credited toward the overall building permit. If the permit is
cancelled, these funds would be used to pay the inspector for plan check. Councilor Memmott moved
to adopt Master Fee Resolution 05-04-2021. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

ANNEXATION AREA MAP LEVEL CHANGE APPROVED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the analysis done on properties that are a Level 2 on the
Annexation Area Map near the mouth of the canyon. The properties could potentially be annexed into
the city for the purpose of a business that is interested in purchasing the property from the city and
locating there. The other city owned properties to the north would make the potential annexation
contiguous with the current city boundaries but are not readily developable due to steep slopes but
could be eventually used as part of a back 9-hole expansion for the golf course. Based on the analysis
and how it met the criteria outlined in the Annexation Policy Plan, city staff recommended moving
forward with a change from Leve 2 to a Level 1 in preparation for a potential annexation request.
   Councilor Worwood moved to change the properties described from a Level 2 to a Level 1 on the
Annexation Area Map. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CANYON MINES ANNEXATION PETITION ACCEPTED:

   An annexation petition named Canyon Mines Annexation was presented to the city council. Nephi
City is the petitioners name, and the location of the property is stated as “mouth of canyon (east of golf
course on both sides of Hwy 132) Nephi, Utah. The total acreage of the proposed annexation is 75.23
acres with three parcels included. The petitioner is requesting the area be zoned Highway Commercial
(HC). Councilor Memmott moved to accept the petition and let it move forward in the annexation
process. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
