Unfinished Business
     1. A Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of the City of South Salt Lake
         Approving and Adopting the Budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2021.
         Economic Development Consultant, Randy Sant, reported that there were no additional
         changes to the budget. However, there was discussion last week about the South Salt
         Lake Chamber of Commerce membership. What program staff would put into place,
         and look at the Chamber helping with, is business relations. The chamber would help
         them meet with the different businesses. The goal would be to meet with seven to ten
         businesses a month. They will meet with large, small, and medium businesses who
