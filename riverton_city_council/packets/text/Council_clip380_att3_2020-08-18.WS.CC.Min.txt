 1   9. Public Hearing(s) / Action Items
 2
 3       a. Resolution No. 20-54 – Authorizing the Mayor to execute a Sales Tax Revenue
 4          Sharing Agreement with Costco Wholesale Corporation, as an Inducement for
 5          Costco to Construct a Wholesale Warehouse in Riverton
 6
 7   Ryan Carter, City Attorney, presented information from a Memorandum regarding a proposal to
 8   construct a new wholesale warehouse at the approximately location of 13200 South and 4570
 9   West. He said a component of Costco’s proposal to construct such a warehouse would include
10   an Agreement with the City to share sales tax revenue that a new Costco warehouse would
11   generate in Riverton. He said that Costco proposes to share, on a 50-50 basis, sales tax
12   generated at a Riverton Costco warehouse and remitted to the City by the State of Utah. The
13   amount to be paid to Costco would be capped at the amount of $6,250,000. He said the City’s
14   current sales tax estimates project that the capped amount would be reached approximately ten
15   years after a Costco warehouse opens in Riverton. However, the City expects to pay
16   approximately $625,000 to Costco annually while retaining the same amount. Mr. Carter said
17   that it was important to remain mindful that options were available for Costco to locate in the
18   vicinity, but outside of Riverton’s municipal territory. Therefore, any sales tax agreement should
19   be recognized as an inducement to bring a Costco warehouse to Riverton.
20   Mr. Carter then presented the following information regarding UCA statutes:
21
22       “Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-2, the City Council may share sales tax revenues
23       otherwise due to the City if the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the value
24       which the underlying project provides to the citizens of Riverton equals the value of the
25       sales tax provided to Costco. To explain, under § 10-8-2, a municipal legislative body
26       may appropriate money for “corporate purposes only.” However a “corporate purpose”
27       includes, “any purpose that, in the judgment of the municipal legislative body, provides
28       for the safety, health, prosperity, moral well-being, peace, order, comfort, or convenience
29       of the inhabitants of the municipality.” That said, the statute and opinions issued by the
30       Utah Supreme Court require that the value of monies appropriated by a municipal
31       legislative body must be at least equal to the value of the benefits which flow to the
32       community by virtue of the appropriation. In balancing the values of the appropriation
33       against the community benefit, the statute allows the municipal legislative body to decide
34       what constitutes the value received, and the legislative body’s determination of the value
35       received is presumed valid unless a person can show the determination was arbitrary,
36       capricious, or illegal.
37
38       Utah Code Ann. § 10-8-2 also requires that the benefits be compared with the value of
39       the appropriation in some form of a study. In the present case, this means the City
40       Council must approve a study that concludes the value of having a Costco warehouse
41       locate in Riverton equals or exceeds the value of the sales tax which we provide Costco,
42       as an inducement to locate in Riverton. Again, the City Council may take into account
43       any number of tangible or intangible benefits which Riverton citizens receive from the
44       location of a Costco warehouse in Riverton.”
45
46   Mr. Carter reviewed several of the benefits the community would receive if a Costco warehouse
47   were constructed in Riverton, under the inducement created by a Sales Tax Revenue Sharing
48   Agreement. He said that, if the Council adopts the findings and conclusions of the Memorandum
49   after holding a public hearing, it would have complied with the procedural requirements of Utah
50   Code Ann. § 10-8-2.

 1   Mr. Carter said that the Memorandum demonstrated that the value of a Costco warehouse in
 2   Riverton created a benefit that the community receives from gaining employment opportunities.
 3   He said a Riverton Costco warehouse would greatly enhance the prosperity of the community,
 4   which equates to a valid corporate purpose to appropriate monies in order to induce a major
 5   employer to come to Riverton. He said additional benefits accrue for Riverton City when they
 6   recognize that a Costco warehouse in Riverton would lead to a significant reduction in travel
 7   miles for Riverton residents who enjoy a Costco membership.
 8
 9   Mr. Carter explained that, accordingly, the conclusion of the Memorandum was the approval of
10   Sales Tax Revenue Sharing Agreement, which would provide benefit to Riverton City that was
11   of greater value than the amounts appropriated under the Agreement.
