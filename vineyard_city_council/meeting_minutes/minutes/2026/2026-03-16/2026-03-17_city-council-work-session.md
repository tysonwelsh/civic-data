3.    WORK SESSION
      3.1. Public Safety Impact Fee Study Update


    Senior Planner Cache Hancey discussed the Impact Fee Study and answered council’s questions
regarding development of the study and future fees as well as future staffing needs for the sheriff’s
department. This discussion also included clarification on deadlines for the study and
implementation of the fees to ensure compliance with recently passed legislation.


     Ms. Cornelius expressed concerns with the equitability of the fees as well as with office space
for additional deputies. A discussion ensued.


    David Pearce, living in the Cascade neighborhood, asked for clarification on police and fire
response in the city.

      3.2. Travel Policy Review


     Administrative Director David Kyle presented the proposed travel policy to the council.

     The council discussed the proposed policy and offered amendments.


      3.3. Municipal Waste Water Planning Program (MWPP) Survey Review

Councilmember Holdaway was excused at 12:58pm.

     Public Works Director Naseem Ghandour presented the survey review.

     Councilmember Wood asked for clarification on FOG (Fats, Oils, and Grease). There was a
brief discussion with staff explaining it further.

      3.4. RDA Audit Update

     Councilmember Holdaway updated on the proposed contracting with Chris Harding and CPA
Insights for additional Audits.

    Councilmember McCumber expressed support for the audit but also wanted to share concerns
with the overall process and asked that the council took steps to ensure that procurement laws were
not broken. A discussion ensued.


      3.5. Spring Clean Up Initiative

     Mr. Herring gave a presentation on the annual Spring Clean-up including several different

     3.6. Operation Neighborhood Shield


    Mr. Herring gave a presentation on the proposed program.


    Neighborhood Services Coordinator Maria Arteaga presented regarding current code
enforcement procedures around over occupancy. The council discussed current evidence
requirements and recently passed legislation.


    The council asked for clarification on current procedures and fines. There was discussion
regarding Councilmember Holdaway’s proposal to have a type of publicly accessible list or notice of
houses that are out of compliance. Additionally, the council discussed the proposed staffing
recommendations.


    Mr. Pearce suggested in leu of publicly noticing specific violations that the city could publish
generalized monthly code enforcement statistics on the city newsletter.

Councilmember Nair was excused at 2:31pm



     3.7. City Commissions, Appointment Processes, And Volunteer Recruitment


   Mr. Herring gave a presentation on currently existing commissions as well as ideas for possible
new commissions.

     The council discussed evaluating existing committees and looking for ways to combine or
restructure them as well as looking for ways to incorporate more volunteer involvement.
