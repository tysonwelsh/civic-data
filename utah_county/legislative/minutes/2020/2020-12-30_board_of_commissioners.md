*Board members may participate electronically at will, with the anchor location as stated above.*

*In accordance with the High Transmission Area restrictions instituted by the Department of
Health in Utah County, members of the public are strongly encouraged to participate
electronically. To accommodate physical distancing requirements, physical attendance will be
limited to 20 people (including staff) at the anchor location. Once the room has reached
capacity the doors will be locked, and additional attendees will be invited to participate online.
All individuals in attendance must comply with State Public Health Order 2020-19 requiring
face coverings in indoor spaces where social distancing is not possible, with certain
exemptions*

https://zoom.us/j/9938818466 or calling (346) 248-7799 or (669)900-6833, Meeting ID: 993 881
8466. Comments will be limited to three (3) minutes per individual unless otherwise approved by the
