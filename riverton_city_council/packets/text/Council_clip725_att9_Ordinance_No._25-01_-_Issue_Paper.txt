                                     City Council Meeting Agenda

PRESENTER:               Jason Lethbridge

                         Ordinance No. 25-01 - Riverton Under Car Properties LLC, Rezone of
                         approximately 0.88-acres located at 2284 West 12600 South from RR-22
ITEM:
                         (Residential 1/2-acre min) to C-G (Gateway Commercial). Applicant- Thom
                         Jakab - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director

SUMMARY:

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Thom Jakab, for two parcels
totaling about 0.88-acres located near 2284 West 12600 South. These properties are on the north side
of 12600 South Street just west of the existing Master Autotech building. The property is currently
zoned RR-22 which is a single-family residential zone with minimum lot size of 1/2-acre, the requested
zone is C-G which is Gateway Commercial. The General Plan designation for this property is Business
Park. To the north of the subject property is the Country View PUD, which is a mix of single family and
townhome units.
The subject property is made of two (2) rectangular parcels. The east parcel has a small redbrick home,
the western parcel has no structures. Both parcels have been used as a vehicle storage and RV
parking (as can be seen from the aerial images). The city has received complaints about the parking
and RV storage on a residential property. This rezone would only resolve the zoning, the applicant
would also need to get approval for a commercial site plan for the property.
