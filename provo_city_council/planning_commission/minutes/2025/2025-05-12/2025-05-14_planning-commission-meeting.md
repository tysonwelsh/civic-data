ITEM 1      Guilford A Rand requests a Conditional Use Permit to allow a reception center use (SLU 6299) in an
            existing commercial building in the CG (General Commercial) Zone, located at 1595 N Freedom Blvd.
            Carterville Neighborhood. Nancy Robison (801) 852-6417 PLCUP20240244

The following action was taken by the Planning Commission on the above described item at its regular meeting of May
14, 2025:
                               APPROVED WITH CONDITIONS
On a vote of 5:0, the Planning Commission approved the above noted application, with the following conditions:

Conditions of Approval:
    1. A business license is obtained and maintained.
    2. The applicant must have a valid lease agreement for the extra 22 parking spaces as long as the business is
        operating. The extra spaces are from the property to the south. Parcel number 19:068:0033
    3. The occupancy is capped at 152 persons, using the calculation One (1) space per four (4) persons based on the
        maximum allowable occupancy. Provo City Code 14.37.060
    4. The center is allowed to operate until 10:00 pm.
    5. Temporary signage is placed to define allowed parking areas during high volume events.

Motion By: Melissa Kendall
Second By: Anne Allen
Votes in Favor of Motion: Jonathon Hill, Daniel Gonzales, Melissa Kendall, Lisa Jensen, Anne Allen.
Jonathon Hill was present as Chair.

STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis, conclusions,
and recommendations.

CITY DEPARTMENTAL ISSUES
• The Coordinator Review Committee (CRC) has reviewed the application and given their approval.

NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING DATE
• The Neighborhood District Chair determined that a neighborhood meeting would not be required.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
• The Neighborhood District Chair was present /addressed the Planning Commission during the public hearing.
• Neighbors or other interested parties were present or addressed the Planning Commission.

CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following:

    •   They would not like it to become an event center that will create problems like the Enigma or the Experience
        Event Center.
    •   Many comments were made about the quality of the Beehive Bazaar and that they had attended it themselves.
    •   Comments were also made that they appreciated what the Bazaar did to help and encourage young artists or
        entrepreneurs.
    •   Many people appreciate the way the business owners have run events in the past in different locations. That they
        were good neighbors, clean, and dedicated to making it a great experience.
    •   Commentors felt that having this business here would improve Provo and using this location will also improve
        an eyesore that has remained vacant for several years.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
   • They would like to have a permanent location for their Bazaar, and feel they will create a nice event center that
       could be used for weddings, workshops and even a place where sets could be temporarily installed for the
       entertainment industry. (Television, Movies etc.).
   • They want to be good neighbors so they will do what they can to keep parking on their lot and the adjoining one
       to the south.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • Planning commission members are enthusiastic about seeing an eyesore leave Provo and for the Bazaar to move
       into a permanent location.
   • Several members have been to that event and have children who have participated in it. They also had great
       experiences with both.
   • Discussion was raised about an alleyway to the south of the property, and staff said they would look into that
       property and how to fix any problems.
   • The planning commission discussed adding temporary signage about parking to keep vehicles coming to the event
       space out of the neighborhoods to the north and west.
