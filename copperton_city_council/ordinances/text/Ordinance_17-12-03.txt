                          ADOPTING THE 2018 FINAL BUDGET
                                     OFTHE
                            COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP

BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council held a
regular public meeting following a public hearing on December 20, 2017.

        WHEREAS, the Copperton Metro Township ("Copperton") is a municipality and
political subdivision of the State of Utah; and

        WHEREAS, on October 18, 2017, Copperton adopted a tentative budget for the 2018
fiscal year; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton provided notice pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-5-108
concerning the tentative budget and of the date, time, and place of a public hearing on the same;
and

       WHEREAS, Copperton made said tentative budget available for public inspection for a
period of at least seven days prior to the public hearing in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-
5-107; and

        WHEREAS, on December 20, 2017 at 6:30 p.m., Copperton held said public hearing on
said tentative budget in accordance with Utah Code Ann. § 10-5-108 at the Bingham Lions Club,
8725 West Hillcrest Street, Copperton, Utah.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED as follows:

        1.    That the tentative budget adopted on October 18, 201 7, as amended (if amended)
and attached hereto, be and hereby is adopted as the Final Budget of Copperton for the 2018
fiscal year.

       2.     The effective date of this Resolution shall be December 20, 2017.

       ADOPTED AND APPROVED by majority vote at a duly called meeting of the Board of
Trustees on this 20th day of December 2017.
