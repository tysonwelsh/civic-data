Minutes of the
Millcreek Community Reinvestment Agency
April 25, 2022
7:00 p.m.
Regular Meeting

The Community Reinvestment Agency of Millcreek, Utah, met in a regular public meeting on
April 25, 2022, at City Hall, located at 3330 S. 1300 E., Millcreek, UT 84106. The meeting was
live streamed via the City’s website with an option for online public comment.

PRESENT:

Board Members City Staff

Jeff Silvestrini, Chair (electronic) John Brems, City Attorney

Silvia Catten Elyse Sullivan, Agency Recorder

Thom DeSirant Mike Winder, City Manager

Cheri Jackson Kurt Hansen, Facilities Director

Bev Uipi (electronic) Francis Lilly, Planning & Zoning Director
Laurie Johnson, HR-Finance Director

Attendees:

REGULAR MEETING: 7:00 p.m.
TIME COMMENCED: 8:23 p.m.

Board Member Jackson called the meeting to order.

Board Member Catten moved to appoint Cheri as the Temporary Chair. Board Member
DeSirant seconded. Board Member Jackson called for the vote. All Board Members
present voted yes. The motion passed unanimously.

1. Public Hearing on Fiscal Year 2022-2023 Community Reinvestment Agency Tentative
Budget
Laurie Johnson showed the Board the tentative revenue totals; $721,800 for Millcreek Center
CRA and $1,200,250 for West Millcreek CRA. The Millcreek Center CRA had a $721,800
debt interest expense. The West Millcreek CRA loaned money to the Millcreek Center CRA
to pay the bond, $571,800, and had other expenses.

Board Member Catten moved to open the public hearing. Board Member DeSirant
seconded. Temporary Chair Jackson called for the vote. All Board Members present voted

yes. The motion passed unanimously.

There were no comments.

2. Approval of March 28, 2022 Regular Meeting Minutes

Board Member Uipi said the city was trying to move forward with the city center improvements
