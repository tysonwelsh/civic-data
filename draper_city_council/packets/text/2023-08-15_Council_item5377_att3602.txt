                              RESOLUTION NO. 23-44

      A RESOLUTION APPOINTING BEN PATTISON AS A REGULAR
      MEMBER OF THE DRAPER CITY SPECIAL EVENT ARENA COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the City is authorized to establish subordinate bodies with the city
as deemed appropriate by the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, Emily Dziatlik is unable to complete her term on the committee;
and

      WHEREAS, Ben Pattison has expressed a willingness to serve as a member of
the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee; and

      WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint and the Council hereby consents to
the appointment of Ben Pattison to serve as a member of the Draper City Special
Event Arena Committee and complete Emily Dziatlik’s term.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s
appointment of Ben Pattison to serve as a member of the Draper City Special Event
Arena Committee.

      Section 1. Appointment. Ben Pattison is hereby appointed to serve as a
member of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee according to the laws,
ordinances, and regulations governing the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee and its members for the term specified herein.

      Regular Member:
           Ben Pattison          August 15, 2023 through December 31, 2026

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.
     Section 3.     Effective Date.   This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 15TH DAY OF AUGUST 2023.
