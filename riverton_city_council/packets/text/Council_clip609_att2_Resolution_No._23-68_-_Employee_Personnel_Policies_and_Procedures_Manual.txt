                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-68

 A RESOLUTION APPROVING UPDATES TO THE RIVERTON CITY PERSONNEL
                POLICIES AND PROCEDURES MANUAL


        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual (the
“Manual”) is used to set acceptable parameters for employment operations, and define employee
benefits; and

       WHEREAS, the Manual is reviewed and updated from time to time as changes in law or
in employment practices arise; and

       WHEREAS, City staff has evaluated the manual and determined that the personal
appearance section needed to be updated to remain current with existing personnel operations;
and

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that an amendment to the
Manual as proposed by City staff would correct the same to reflect current personnel operations;

        WHEREAS, the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, does hereby determine that it is in
the best interests of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Riverton City and City
employees to adopt revisions to the Manual.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Personnel Policies and Procedures Manual is hereby amended as
          provided in Exhibit A of this Resolution. The Mayor is hereby directed to ensure that
          the Manual is accordingly revised and made available to all City employees within a
          reasonable time from adoption of this Resolution.

       2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 5th day of
September, 2023 by the following vote:

5.3 VACATION

ELIGIBILITY

Vacation leave is to be used by regular employees for scheduled absences. Vacation leave is available to
all full-time and regular part-time employees.

ACCRUAL

Vacation leave is accrued during each pay period. Full-time employees will receive vacation leave at the
following annual rates depending on longevity within Utah Retirement Systems, for Civilian employees,
or law enforcement equivalent experience for sworn officers. Law enforcement equivalent experience is
defined as one year for one year of direct law enforcement officer experience and one year for every
two years of other public safety experience such as corrections, jail or fire.

Regular vacation leave (employees excluding middle-level management and higher):
   1. 0-5 years 80 hours
   2. 5-10 years 120 hours
   3. 10-15 years 160 hours
   4. 15+ years 200 hours

Administrative vacation leave (middle-level management and higher):
   1. 0-5 years 120 hours
   2. 5-10 years 160 hours
   3. 10+ years 200 hours



6.9     ALCOHOL AND DRUG FREE WORKPLACE

POST-ACCIDENT TESTING

Post-accident testing will be conducted on employees if the employee contributes to the accident and
the estimated value of the damages is in excess of $1500. In addition, employees involved in an
accident in which any individual involved receives an injury, will be subject to a post-accident drug test.
Employees present in a City vehicle during the accident, but not driving the vehicle involved in the
accident, will not be required to submit to a post-accident drug screen unless they contributed to the
cause of the accident. Employees involved in an accident in a City-owned vehicle during non-work hours
will also be required to submit to a drug screen if the damage is a minimum of $1500 or if any person
receives an injury. The post-accident drug testing will occur as soon as possible after the accident has
occurred. The employee must then be put on administrative leave until the results of the test are
available. The supervisor must arrange or help the employee make arrangements to get home without
driving him/herself.

RANDOM TESTING

Random testing will be performed periodically on employees required to hold a commercial driver’s
license (CDL) and who drive commercial vehicles as a condition of employment. They will be randomly
selected and given minimal advance notice. Such employees are under no suspicion but are tested
specifically to satisfy the random criteria. The purpose of random testing is to be a deterrent to all
safety sensitive employees for drug or alcohol abuse. The means of random selection shall be
confidential.

Employees required to hold a commercial driver’s license (CDL) and drive commercial vehicles as a
condition of employment may be tested more frequently to meet federal and or state requirements.

    1. Any drug or alcohol testing shall occur during or immediately after the regular work period of
       current employees and shall be deemed work time for purposes of compensation and benefits
       for current employees.
    2. Individuals will be tested on company premises or sent to an outside clinic or testing facility
       licensed to perform such tests. If an employee is sent to an outside clinic for a “reasonable
       suspicion” test, the employee must be driven to the facility by the supervisor or his/her
       designee.
    3. The City shall pay all costs of testing and transportation associated with a test required by the
       City.
