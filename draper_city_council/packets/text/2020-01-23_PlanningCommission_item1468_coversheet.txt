Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky
CC:  Date: January 23, 2020
Re: Nielsen’s Frozen Custard – Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a development agreement for approximately 0.70 acres located on the south side of Draper Parkway, at approximately 1462 East Draper Parkway. The property is currently zoned CC (Community Commercial) . The applicant is requesting that an agreement be approved to allow for the redevelopment of the site from a bank building to a Nielsen’s Frozen Custard and Diner restaurant. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The proposed development plans outlined in the Agreement meets the intent, goals, and objectives of the Draper City General Plan.
2.  The application complies with the requirements of DCMC 9-5-200(C).
3.  The proposed development plans will not be deleterious to the health, safety, and general welfare of the general public nor the residents of adjacent properties.
4.  The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development.
For denial:
1.  The proposed Agreement does not meet the requirements and provisions of the Draper City Municipal Code.
2.  The proposed Agreement does not conform to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 1/16/2020 Cover Memo
