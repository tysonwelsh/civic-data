     MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING
                            SEPTEMBER 17, 2025

THE WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION MET FOR STUDY SESSION ON
WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 17, 2025 AT 3:30 PM IN WEST VALLEY CITY HALL, 3600
CONSTITUTION BLVD, SUITE 220, WEST VALLEY CITY, UTAH. THE MEETING WAS CALLED
TO ORDER AND CONDUCTED BY VICE CHAIR MARTELL WINTERS.
THE FOLLOWING MEMBERS WERE PRESENT:
     Martell Winters, Vice Chair
     Darrick Porter, Commissioner
     Nancy Drozdek, Commissioner
     Rob Durfee, Commissioner
     Pauline Matagi, Commissioner

PLANNING DIVISION STAFF PRESENT:
     Steve Pastorik, Community Development Director
     Jody Knapp, Zoning Administrator
     Brock Anderson, P&Z Senior Planner
     Julie Davis, P&Z Administrative Assistant

PRESENTATIONS:

1) GP-5-2025 Petition by West Valley City requesting an update to the General Plan Map and the
Land Use Element of the General Plan. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented an updated General Plan Map reflecting recent zoning
adjustments, recent developments, station area plans, and recommendations from Planning Commission
and City Council along Redwood Road and 3500 South. The map has been simplified by consolidating
some land use designations, such as trail locations and intersection improvement needs, which are now
referenced in separate documents. The Plan includes potential residential development on previously
zoned commercial areas, though the city is nearly 90% built out, limiting new development
opportunities. He outlined details for proposed changes in the southwest area regarding industrial uses
and residential development. He noted several future land use proposals, such as converting a Rocky
Mountain Power transmission corridor into a park (pending funding) and developing UDOT-owned land
for recreation. Commissioner Winters inquired about the status of developable "greenfield" land, to
which the Steve explained that a property swap between Northrop Grumman and Kennecott has created
new development opportunities with specific restrictions preventing residential use in certain areas due
to safety concerns. Steve addressed a question about commercial development needs and potential
impacts on existing development applications, responding that the General Plan doesn't directly affect
current applications but will influence future zone change requests.

The conversation ended with mention of an upcoming comprehensive General Plan overhaul, at the City
manager's direction, which will involve more comprehensive discussions with the Planning
Commission. The update is expected to take 18-24 months. The proposed Land Use Element projects
approximately 4,600 new housing units over the next 20 years, leading to a population increase to over
158,000.


2) C-12-2025 Petition by Scott Godfrey requesting conditional use approval for the Godfrey
Industrial Park No. 3 with Flex Industrial, Warehousing, Heavy Truck and Trailer Service, and
Outside Storage. The property is located at 2349 South 5900 West on 13.5 acres, and is zoned M,
Manufacturing. (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov) Continued from
September 10, 2025 Public Hearing.


Discussion: Jody Knapp provided an update on the initial plan that was submitted. All the buildings on
site would be concrete tilt-ups and have overhead doors front and back. Buildings A through F would
offer office space, warehouse/light industrial space, rear outside storage, and could be divided into two
units each to have 12 tenants. Building G would also be concrete tilt-ups and have two overhead doors,
front and back. Jody noted a correction to the initial presentation that Building G would be a trailer
repair business, not a semi-truck repair business. She displayed updated cross-section plans showing the
lot elements including fencing, berm, and retaining wall. She addressed landscape setbacks, noting that
trees near a county drainage facility may need to be relocated or clustered.

As for paving the site, the outside storage for buildings A through F have been updated to show they will
be completely paved,

 For Building G, the plan has been updated to show clarification of the locations of the potential
wetlands and drainage areas northeast corner of the site. That portion will remain undeveloped at this
time. Then the main access and building area is shown as paved with gravel in the northwest and west
portions of the lot. There was discussion regarding other options and the reason for the continuance.
Jody stated Public Works had been asked about the difference between gravel and hard surface and from
a stormwater point of view there was a preference. Public Works did not have a preference and left the
determination up to the Commission. Commissioner Durfee then suggested paving a loop road around
the trailer storage area in the north section and the gravel under the trailer storage, as he felt this would
be sufficient to address dust and tracking concerns. Jody agreed to add that option to the Staff Report for
review.


3) C-16-2025 Petition by Woodward and Moody LTD requesting conditional use approval for
