Informational Items


1.         21-197                 Police, Animal Services, and Fire presenting their FY 2021-22 tentative
                                  department budgets.
                   Attachments:   Police and Animal Services.pdf
                                  Fire.pdf

                                  Brian Kelley, Finance Director, introduced tonight's department budget presentations.
                                  Chief Greg Severson, Police, thanked the Administration and Council Members for their
                                  support over this challenging year. He gave an overview of the the police department
                                  budget and requests for FY 2022. Chief Severson stressed the importance of funding the
                                  compensation package for officers as a necessary component for the retention of police
                                  officers. The Animal Services budget was also presented and the discussion included the
                                  current staffing levels and needs at Animal Services. Council comments and questions
                                  followed. Discussion focused on funding the step and grade pay for officers, comparable
                                  pay offered by other cities, crime statistics in Sandy, and the addition of a hybrid vehicle
                                  to the police department fleet.

                                  Chief Cline, Fire, presented the Fire Department budget for FY 2022. He thanked the
                                  Council for funding the compensation plan in prior years. He highlighted the department
                                  accomplishments and the focus on training and mental health of fire service personnel.
                                  He provided information on budget transfers and requests, which included the purchase of
                                  a new fire engine. Ryan McConaghie, Deputy Fire Chief, provided details about the
                                  department's Covid protocols, testing and related calls. They spoke about current staffing,
                                  training and the ability to send staff to paramedic school as well as future staffing needs.
                                  Council questions and comments followed. Council thanked the Chiefs for their
                                  presentations and service.

                                  the Consent Calendar. The motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.

4.         21-194                 Sandy City Fire Department recommends that City Council adopts the
                                  proposed fireworks restriction map to include recently annexed areas.
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Ordinance 21-15 Fire Work Restriction Memo.pdf
                                  Ordinance 21-15 ExhibitB_Proposed_FireHazardAreas.jpg
                                  Ordinance 21-15 Fireworks Map.pdf

                                  Robert Dekorver, Sandy Fire Marshall, provided information to the Council regarding the
                                  proposed firework restrictions for 2021. He presented the updated map designating
                                  restricted firework areas for Council approval. The map has been modified to reflect three
                                  new areas for firework restrictions and incorporates newly annexed areas of the city.
                                  Once adopted by the Council, the approved map will be submitted to the Salt Lake
                                  County Emergency Management division by May 21, 2021.

                                  Public Comment opened:

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren asked for additional information about the designation of the two
                                  areas above 1300 East. Chief Cline mentioned that these two area were under evaluation
                                  but do not currently meet the guidelines to designate as restricted area.

                                  Public Comment closed.

                                  In closing, Fire Marshall DeKorver, on behalf of the Sandy City Fire Department, asked
                                  the residents to exercise extreme caution during the coming summer months as our city
                                  and state are experiencing heavy drought conditions which means the fire danger is going
                                  to be very high this year and reminded the community to be very careful with fireworks.

                                  Following the roll call vote, Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.
                                  A motion was made by Monica Zoltanski, seconded by Zach Robinson, to adopt
                                  Ordinance 21-15 Exhibit B (Revised), Restricted Areas for Fireworks, Fires, Sandy
                                  City, a map that includes the recently annexed areas of Sandy City... The motion
                                  carried by the following vote:

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                          Mike Applegarth asked the Council for their feedback on this evening's hybrid Council
                          meeting. The plan for next week is to have three members participating in the Council
                          meeting from the Council Chambers. There will be Agenda Planning tomorrow morning.

Council Member Business

                          Council Member Houseman thanked staff and residents who volunteered for Sandy
                          Beautification Day. She thanked Charlie Millard, Dan Medina and the Parks staff for their
                          efforts. For the 10th year, the Sandy Rotary Club members refurbished the benches built
                          by the club, along Bell Canyon Trail, as their project for Sandy Beautification Day. Ms.
                          Houseman wanted to recognize Governor Cox's recent declaration proclaiming May 15th
                          as Armed Forces Day in Utah and she read an excerpt of the Governor's proclamation.
                          May marks Mental Health Awareness Month and she spoke about promoting positive
                          mental health and the importance of building connectiveness within our community.

                          Council Member Zoltanski asked for an update on the bulk waste program and requested
                          a presentation to the Council.Tracy Cowdell, Council Attorney, provided an update on the
                          program and draft ordinance. He provided some additional details on the plan going
                          forward.

Mayor's Report

                          No report.

CAO Report

                          Matt Huish, CAO, stated that all 18 of administrative orders related to the fluoride event
                          have been completed. He commented on the final corrosion study that was recently
                          completed which detailed the testing results that showed no lasting negative effects from
                          the 2019 fluoride event on the plumbing materials in the homes. The entire study will be
                          posted on the website. Also, in following CDC guidelines, masks are no longer required in
                          City Hall for those that are fully vaccinated. The new utility billing and payment platform
                          has been implemented. He provided details from the Sandy Beautification Day: 22
                          projects, 400 volunteers, 270 flats of flowers, 25 trees planted and 380 tons of rock was
                          distributed.
