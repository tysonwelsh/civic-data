Public Hearing, Recommendation for Amendments to the Eastern Summit County Development Code, Sections 11-1-3, 11-4-2(A)(3), 11-4-5, Section 11-4-5(F)(1)(a) and (G)(2), 11-6-5(A)(1)(a-e), (2)(g), (B)(1), (2)(d), 11-6-16(F)(1)(d)(1), 11-6-19(C), 11-7-10(B), 11-7-14(B), 11-7-15, 11-7-16(B) and (E), Appendix A, and APPENDIX C (D). Planner Jennifer Strader presented recommendations for amendments to the Eastern Summit County Development Code. These updates align with state code references, fix internal inconsistencies, and make other non-substantive changes. (6:02 PM)
Commissioner Don Sargent asked why the Eastern Summit County General Plan title was replaced with the General Plan. Planner Strader and Community Development Director Peter Barnes responded. (6:03 PM)
Commission Chair Alex Peterson opened the meeting for a public hearing. (6:04 PM)
No one appeared to comment.
Commission Chair Alex Peterson closed the meeting for a public hearing.
David Darcey made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council for the amendments to the code as presented. (6:04 PM)  . Seth Bowen seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Marion Wheaton.
