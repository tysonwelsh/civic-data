       F.      Public Hearing: Urbana – Land Use Amendment, Zoning Map Amendment,
               and Development Agreement Requests
               (Legislative Action)
               On the Request of Jacob Ballstaedt of Garbett Homes, a Request for Approval of:
               1) an amendment to a Development Agreement, 2) a Land Use Map Amendment
               from the Community Commercial to High-Density Residential land use
               designation, and 3) a Zoning Map Amendment from the A2 (Agricultural) to RM2
               (Multiple Family) zoning designation. The subject property is approximately 0.15
               acres in size and is located at approximately 285 W. River Chapel Road. Known as
               applications: 2024-0420-MA, 2024- 0422-MA and 2024-0423-DA. Staff contact:
               Maryann Pickering, (801) 576-6391 or maryann.pickering@draperutah.gov.

Ms. Pickering presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is for a Land Use Map
Amendment, Zoning Map Amendment, and Development Agreement Amendment for
approximately 0.15 acres located on the west side of the Loan Peak Parkway at approximately 285
West River Chapel Road. The vicinity, aerial, land use, and zoning maps were displayed. Ms.
Pickering noted the land use had been changed in 2024 to Residential High Density. The property
is currently zoned RM-2. She reported that the adjacent church agreed to sell a 16-foot strip of
land and undergone a boundary adjustment with Garbett Homes. The applicant is requesting that
the approximately 0.15 acres be incorporated into the previously approved single-family
development project.

Ms. Pickering reported that the Development Agreement changes were to provide an updated legal
description showing the 16-foot strip added into the project and to add a statement as requested by
the Engineering Department that the water system will be maintained by the developer. The
change was identified on the updated conceptual drawings. She received one (1) call in response
to the notice of this meeting and upon explanation of the project, there was no opposition.

Chair Adams liked the planned access to the site. Ms. Pickering stated that the project was limited
to 30 units and was being developed to RM-2 standards.

Damian Mora was present on behalf of the applicant.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing was
closed.

Commissioner Ogden reported the addition of 16 feet makes perfect sense as there is nothing that
can be done with the strip of land other than what is proposed.

       G.      Public Hearing: New Union, LLC Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
               Request.
               (Administrative Item)
               On the Request of Quinn Edwards representing New Union, LLC a Home
               Occupation Conditional Use Permit request for approximately 0.72 acres, located
               at 12785 S. 1300 E. Known as application 2024-0389-USE, Staff Contact: Nick
               Whittaker, (801) 576-6522, nick.whittaker@draperutah.gov.

Mr. Whittaker presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is for approval of a Home
Occupation Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.72 acres located on the east side of 1300
East at 12785 South 1300 East. The vicinity, aerial, land use, and zoning maps were displayed.
Mr. Whittaker reported that the proposed business sells art and occasionally has customers come
to view the art prior to purchase. There are two (2) single-family residential dwelling structures
on the property, built in 1907 and 1977. The property owner lives in the dwelling built in 1977
and will operate the home occupation from that structure. The property is split-zoned RA1 and
R3. Mr. Whittaker reviewed the floor plan and site plan and identified the proposed parking
location. He stated the potential impact of customers coming to the home and related traffic will
be mitigated by requiring that all customer appointments be made in advance, that only (1)
customer visit the location at a time, and that a dedicated parking space be provided.

Chair Adams asked for clarification on the proximity of the parking space to the separately owned
dwelling. The applicant, Quinn Edwards, reported that the parking is next to his detached garage.

Commissioner Ogden asked if most of the sales are conducted online. Mr. Edwards responded
that in-person sales take place a few times each year and most of the sales are online, both national
and international. Commissioner Ogden asked if deliveries are picked up from the property. Mr.
Edwards responded that he brings packages to the shipper himself.

Chair Adams opened the public hearing. There was no public comment. The public hearing was
closed.

Commissioner Ogden commended Mr. Edwards for the proposed application and highlighted the
minimal impact of the business.

Motion: Commissioner Ogden moved to APPROVE the Home Occupation Conditional Use
Permit, as requested by Quinn Edwards, representing New Union, LLC, Application 2024-
0389-USE subject to the following:

      1.     The proposal complies with the standards of approval found in DCMC Section
             9-5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the
             imposition of reasonable conditions.

Conditions of Approval:

      1.     That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works,

               Request.
               (Administrative Item)
               On the Request of Nathan DeRose, representing DeRose Plumbing, LLC a Home
               Occupation Conditional Use Permit request for approximately 0.43 acres, located
               at 12206 S. Hilton Head Way, Known as application 2024-0368-USE. Staff
               Contact: Nick Whittaker, (801) 576-6522, nick.whittaker@draperutah.gov.

Mr. Whittaker presented the Staff Report and stated that the request is for a Home Occupation
Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.43 acres located on the west side of Hilton Head Way,
at approximately 12206 South Hilton Head Way. The vicinity, aerial, land use, and zoning maps
were displayed. The land use is Residential Medium Density. The property is zoned R3. The
applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved for an office
operating a plumbing business. The site plan, floor plan, and site photos were reviewed. The
business is operated from a designated room located in the dwelling measuring approximately 132
square feet.

Commissioner Squire asked if the proposed trailer parking is behind a barrier. Mr. Whittaker
explained that they are behind the front yard plane. In response to a question, Mr. Whittaker stated
the concrete pad on the property is in violation as the maximum driveway width is 30 feet. He
explained that this will need to be brought into compliance within a reasonable amount of time.
Mr. Whittaker shared photographs of the property. There was discussion regarding the concrete.
Mr. Whittaker reviewed the impact analysis and proposed mitigation against the potential for
noise, pedestrian and vehicular increased traffic, and the parking of vehicles on the street. He
stated that public comments were received and emailed to the Commission.

Commissioner Squire noted that there had been a comment about on-street parking. She stated
that that would not be allowed and asked for clarification on the storage of the business trailers.
Mr. Whittaker stated that the two (2) business trailers will be stored on the concrete pad, which
will not impede access to the garage or the required parking stalls for the residence.

The applicant, Nathan DeRose, reported that there is a large hedge row that acts as a barrier
between his property and the barrier to the south and screens his yard.

he was open to guidance on this issue.

In response to a question raised by Commissioner Shirey, Mr. DeRose provided clarification on
