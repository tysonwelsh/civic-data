4. Holladay Report

  A.    Spring Lane Elementary Adaptive Reuse Plan – Council Member Fotheringham

         o School closed 15 months ago
         o The City and School District have been talking
         o State law provides that cities have the right of first refusal if the school district sells - in this
           case district does not want to sell. We've been working through options for a long-term lease
           with the district, and while it's not final, we anticipate it will be in the next month or so.
         o Council is committed to preserving open space wherever it makes sense. Long-term view -
           knowing we can't get that space back once it's gone.
         o We studied options and developed a plan
         o Still working through funding options

  B.    Affordable Housing – Mayor Dahle

         o Challenge in Holladay
         o We're committed - we funded a new person to coordinate our efforts, help us develop a
           worthwhile plan not only to meet the state goals but our own.
         o Working with developers who have their challenges
         o Another challenge for us has been changing goals
         o Hoping that this session doesn't change the goals - we share the priority - but need time to
        implement.

   C.   Highland Drive Reconstruction - Council Member Gray

        o   Working through a long-term project to reconstruct Highland Drive between Arbor Lane and
            the Van Winkle Expressway.
        o   Anticipating a street that includes better active transportation options for both bicyclists and
            pedestrians
        o   Expensive project - potentially up to $30 million or more depending on whether power can be
            relocated or put underground.
        o   Still working on funding - we are in line for a federal earmark to begin construction design.
            We also appreciate the funding the legislature provided to us last year.
        o   We'd like to complete the project prior to the Olympics in 2034, which means we have a lot
            of work to do closing the funding gap.

    D. City Hall Seismic Improvements -Council Member Durahm)

        o   Holladay residents love our City Hall
        o   The oldest section of the building is almost 100 years old, 4 different additions with a variety
            of time periods and construction standards
        o   Much of the building that is used heavily by the public - is unreinforced masonry.
        o   Want to protect the safety of our employees and residents who use this building.
        o   Council reviewed cost of seismic upgrades and other building improvements and decided to
            retrofit the building rather than build new. We want people to be able to get out safely, not
            constructing to the level that the building would necessarily be functioning; after a serious
            earthquake.
        o   We estimate it will be about $10 million for the project overall.
        o   Likely to begin during the first part of 2026. Construction will take 12-15 months.

     E. Utah Renewable Communities - Council Member Quinn

        o   Preserve our ability to support renewable options, and appreciate the work the legislature has
            done to provide us with those options so far.

     F. School Safety – Council Member Brewer

        o   Followed with interest discussion around HB 84, School Safety Amendments.
        o   Want to make sure our schools are as safe as they can be for our students.
        o   Have had concern about provision in law that specifies that an armed person, not a teacher,
            must be present at all times at all public schools.
        o   If school resource officers are required in all schools, we anticipate that could have a major
            fiscal impact on the City.
        o   We anticipate the total cost of adding SROs to schools where they aren't currently would be
            close to a million dollars. That would be a challenge for the City to absorb, and we're sure it
            would be for the school district as well.
