order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on July 14, 2020, in the Washington County

Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Dean Cox,
Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw,
Clerk/Auditor Kim Hafen, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

CONSIDERATION _OF_ RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2624, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING TEMPORARY SHUTTLE SERVICES IN SPRINGDALE

Washington County Tourism Director Kevin Lewis explained that shuttles were reassigned
from Springdale Town to provide additional services within Zion National Park in relation
to COVID-19 requirements. A Request for Proposal (RFP) was published in order to
provide temporary shuttle operations to move people throughout the town and get people
to and from the entrance of Zion National Park.

Based on the bids received, it is recommended for St. George Shuttle to provide those
temporary shuttle services. The Coronavirus Aid Relief and Economic Security (CARES)
Act would cover the expenses of the shuttle system as the current Zion Shuttle system is
operating at limited capacity due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

COMMISSION REPORT

Commissioner Cox reported on the west Veyo fire, which began on July 13, 2020. The
small fire departments located along SR-18 have worked tirelessly to save structures in the
area. It is not fully contained, however, firefighters are making ongoing efforts to work
towards containment.

Commissioner Almquist reported on the Turkey Farm Road fire which has ignited in two
directions and has been zero percent (0%) contained. Residents in Green Springs have been
asked to prepare for evacuation as wind has caused the fire to head towards homes.

The Commissioners reminded citizens to be extremely cautious when using fireworks for
the upcoming holiday and urged municipalities to ban fireworks in the rural areas.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
4:30 p.m.
