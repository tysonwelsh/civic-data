1. PUBLIC HEARING TO DISCUSS THE PROPOSED LAND SWAP TO TRANSFER BRIDAL
VEIL FALLS PARK LAND TO THE COUNTY FROM THE FEDERAL GOVERNMENT IN
EXCHANGE FOR FOREST SERVICE ADJACENT LAND

-Ezra Nair, Commission
(Timestamp: 12:28)

EZRA NAIR (Utah County Administrator) EXPLAINED THAT SEVERAL YEARS AGO THERE
WAS A LOT OF INTEREST IN BRIDAL VEIL FALLS. THERE WERE LONG PUBLIC
MEETINGS WITH LOTS OF COMMENTS. AS A RESULT OF THAT THE AREA WAS PUT
INTO A CONSERVATION EASEMENT AND MADE INTO A STATE MONUMENT AS
WELL. HE NOTED THAT THERE HAVE BEEN SOME CONFLICTS BETWEEN THE
FEDERAL GOVERNMENT AND LOCAL GOVERNMENT AND IT HAS BEEN HARD TO
GO IN AND MAKE THIS AREA SAFER, ESPECIALLY IN REGARD TO THE PEDESTRIAN
AND BIKE TRAFFIC WHERE THE PEDESTRIANS STOP TO ENJOY THE FALLS. THE
COUNTY HAS PROPOSED SPLITTING THE BIKE AND PEDESTRIAN TRAFFIC. HE
SHOWED THE FOLLOWING SLIDES TO EXPLAIN THEIR PROPOSED PLAN.

COMMISSION GORDON ASKED ABOUT A POTENTIAL GONDOLA. RICHARD
NIELSON EXPLAINED THAT THERE WILL BE NO GONDOLA. THE OLD STATION, AT
THE TOP, HAS BEEN COMPLETELY REMOVED AND THERE IS NOTHING LEFT OF
THE GONDOLA BASE AT THE LOWER LEVEL.

COMMISSIONER BELTRAN THANKED RICHARD NIELSON FOR HIS WORK. HE SAID
THERE IS TOO MUCH RED TAPE AT THE FEDERAL LEVEL AND THIS IS SIMPLY
BRINGING THE LAND BACK TO US. THESE DECISIONS SHOULD BE MADE IN THIS
ROOM AND NOT WASHINGTON D.C. LET’S HAVE STEWARDSHIP OF OUR PUBLIC
LANDS RIGHT HERE AT THE COUNTY LEVEL. HE ALSO THANKED CONGRESSMAN
KENNEDY, WHO HAS ASSISTED THE COUNTY.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER EXPLAINED THAT WHEN THEY VOTED TO
MAKE BRIDAL VEIL FALLS A STATE MONUMENT, SHE WOULD ONLY VOTE IF IT
WAS CONTINGENT UPON THE COUNTY HAVING THE ABILITY TO MAINTAIN IT
AND STAY ACCESSIBLE TO ALL PEOPLE, REGARDLESS OF THEIR ABILITY TO PAY.

COMMISSIONER GORDON ASKED ABOUT THE TIME FRAME FOR THIS PROJECT.
RICHARD NIELSON SAID THEY WOULD BEGIN ON AREAS THAT THE COUNTY
ALREADY HOLDS BUT THEY ARE WAITING ON THE FINAL DECISION FROM THE
FEDERAL GOVERNMENT.
