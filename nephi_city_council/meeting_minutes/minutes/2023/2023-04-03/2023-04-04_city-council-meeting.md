URPA PRESIDENT LEEANN POWELL PRESENTS RECREATION FACILITY OF THE YEAR AWARD:

   LeeAnn Powell Executive Director of the Utah Recreation and Parks Association (URPA) presented
Nephi City Recreation with a plaque recognizing the Hive as the “Outstanding Recreation and Parks
Facility” of the year. She informed the council that three awards were won by the Nephi at the URPA
annual conference-the outstanding facility, the outstanding volunteer who was Devin Michaelsen, and
the life-time achievement award that went to Phil Baker former Nephi City Parks and Cemetery
Superintendent. . She said that it was remarkable that a community of our size could build a facility the
size of the Hive. She was impressed with the amount of funding that came from contributions and the
working partnership between the Juab School District and Nephi City.

FLOOD MITIGATION DISCUSSED:

   City Administrator Seth Atkinson summarized the flood mitigation efforts that the City had done and
are continuing to do. He stated that 1000 sandbags have been filled by different church and volunteer
groups. He said that many people have volunteered equipment if needed with the spring runoff. He
also said that the Streets Department is clearing trees and shrubs from the Hollow. County officials and
representatives from the Nephi Irrigation Company (NIC) were present. Juab County Commissioner
Marty Palmer said that the county has one backhoe and one track hoe and asked if needed, could the
County borrow some of the City’s equipment. Juab County Emergency Manager Drake Underwood
indicated that the State of Utah had awarded a grant for flood mitigation and had provided 40,000
sandbags and two portable sandbagging machines. He said that sand had been delivered to the jail and
inmates will fill the sandbags.
   Juab County Sheriff Doug Anderson said that he is ultimately the person in charge if there is an
emergency due to flooding and if problems arise the public should report through dispatch of the
sheriff’s office. A discussion ensued about who owned the ponds up the canyon. It was determined
that Nephi City did own the first pond up the canyon and would oversee flood control at that point.

  Mr. James White addressed the council about an issue with cracked and damaged asphalt in front of
his residence. He claimed that the Johansen Construction had damaged his asphalt during the culinary

Brough handed Mr. White a claim form from the City to give to the City’s insurance company Utah Local
Governments Trust.

CEMETERY WATERLINE EXPANSION PROJECT DISCUSSED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that last fall as Mitchell Excavation was finishing up their utilities
and trenching they asked to have the power lines on the far east side of the Vine Bluff Cemetery put into
conduit. They said they would dig the trench. The electric dept was willing to install the conduit and did
that work then. The City took the opportunity to use this trench to also add a water line for Vine Bluff.
This additional water line will be used to irrigate the southern most area of the cemetery where we
currently stockpile our soil. More immediately it will be used to water the trees planted on that south
fence by Phil Baker in 2018. Also this line will make water available if the work sheds on the east are
expanded to have sinks or bathrooms.

  The pipe size and flow (hydraulics) of the current irrigation systems on the Vine Bluff Property are
barely capable of meeting the needs of those areas already irrigated. So looking forward to the time
when this south section will need to be developed, this improvement is being added. A water meter and
check valve will be installed and all these improvements and bids have been coordinated with Justin
Blackett. This project will be funded from Cemetery Projects line item in the Capital Projects Fund. Mr.
Atkinson indicated that this project was a funded, prioritized item in the 2020 Budget but time
constraints and waiting for the project at Winn Ridge have stalled the project. The council directed staff
to continue with the waterline expansion project.
