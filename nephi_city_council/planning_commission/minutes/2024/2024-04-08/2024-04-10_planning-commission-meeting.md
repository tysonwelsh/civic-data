   2. Public Hearing: Application for parcel # XA00-2472-124 located at 1025 S
      Main Street to rezone from CU1 (Combined Use 1) to the HC1 (Highway
      Commercial 1) zone.
        a. Open Public Hearing
            TIME START: 7:01 PM

          b. Staff Presentation
             Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented the applicant had applied to have the property
             split into two lots, for the purpose of building a 61-room hotel for a branded
             Best Western @HOME. This will be the first location for the Best Western
             @HOME in Utah and is an extended-stay suites hotel. The applicant has
             already met some of the required criteria for parking and already submitted
             preliminary drawings for the site. Currently the location is a CU-1 zoning,
             however, due to the application and preliminary drawings the staff is
             requesting that the location be rezoned to HC-1. Highway Commercial
             would be a better for the area and are listed out in the staff recommendation
             document for this meeting. It is recommended by the staff that the

   application be moved forward, and rezoning applied to the parcel #XA00-
   2472-124.

3. Best Western @Home Vicinity Plan for a 60 room, 4-story, hotel
      a. Staff Presentation
         Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented that this is just a concept plan for what will
         happen with the subdivision with setbacks for HC-1. Basically, the line will
         come between the buildings setbacks 25’ each on each side, with a 50’
         minimum and that is for fire code. We may combine the preliminary and
         run them together. Kyle Marchant has been working with Jonithan Oliver to
         make sure it meets code. We move that they are moved forward to
         preliminary.

      b. Commission discussion
         Chairwoman Ann Peterson: Brought up concerns about the pool, and
         expressed that there is a great concern if there is enough parking because
         that road is already a concern.

          Jonathan Oliver: Site plan has been updated, and parking spots have been
          added.

          Mrs. Shauna Talbot: A current parking study is being done. That will
          determine if they have enough parking. UDOT will be worked with, and
          this is just the beginning. The city is doing a parking study already, and
          some of the information that has been received in the past is that 1.5
          parking spaces are needed for hotels.

          Commissioner Heather Robertson: Voiced concerns about the parking
          and the look of the preliminary drawings, as they don’t look like they may
          facilitate for the size of the hotel. Are the entrances moving more south.

          Commissioner Cory Thomson: Expressed that the preliminary looks
          good, but would like to see the second opening to the Best Western to be
          closed.

      c. Action item
                 a. Motion: Chairman Ann Peterson moves to approve the vicinity plan for
                    the Best Western @Home.
                 b. Second: Alternate Commissioner John Ford
                 c. Outcome: The motion was unanimously approved. MOTION
                    APPROVED.
