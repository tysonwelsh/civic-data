ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATION

ZT-8-2024: Petition by R5 DEVELOPMENT, LLC requesting an amendment to Section 7-6-306 of the
zoning ordinance to allow property zoned C-2 (General Commercial) and M (Manufacturing) to be
included with property zoned C-3 (Transitional Commercial) to meet the minimum district size for the
C-3 zone. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. Steve explained that the C-2 zone already allows property zoned
C-3 and M to be included with property zoned C-2 to meet the minimum district size.


ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS

Z-5-2024: Petition by Mountain America Credit Union requesting a zone change from A
(Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial). The property is located at
5517 and 5541 West 4100 South on 0.96 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Pastorik presented. There were no questions or comments.

Commissioner Winters asked what is and is not allowed in the C-3 zone. Steve responded that he would
include the commercial and manufacturing zones use table with the staff report.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS

SA-2-2024: Petition by CHRISTIE SCHATHER representing Kohl’s Department Store is requesting a plat
amendment to lot 4 of The Shoppes at Lake Park Subdivision. The amended plat will amend lot 4 to create
an additional building lot. The property is located at 3019 South 5600 West and is zoned C-2. (Staff-Steve
Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented. There were no questions or comments.

SMI-2-2024: Petition by MARV HENDRICKSON is requesting minor subdivision approval for the Treasure
Circle Subdivision. The subdivision will also amend lot 1 of the Jensen Subdivision. The subdivision will
consist of 8 lots on 2.2 acres, is located at 3260 West 4060 South and is zoned R-1-7. (Staff-Steve Lehman at
801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Steve Lehman presented. He hasn’t received any calls on this application. There were no
questions or comments.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS

C-12-2024: Petition by VALLEY COLLISION requesting conditional use approval for an auto service
outside storage yard. The property is located at 2280 S. Constitution Blvd. on 0.817 acres and is zoned M
(Manufacturing). (Staff Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented. The applicant was online. A letter of support from the adjacent
landowner was discussed. Commissioner Drozdek asked if there was a way to propose that the applicant
be required to add more trees to compensate for the heat of the asphalt. Jody said that Rocky Mountain
Power does not allow landscaping in their power corridor, and landscaping is not a requirement in a
manufacturing zone when it is not visible to the public. Zone M requires 5% landscaping, and this
                                                                                                 Page 2 of 3
application would not trigger any additional landscaping requirements. However, the applicant will be
required to add 3 trees along the frontage that had previously been removed.


C-14-2024: Petition by JZW ARCHITECTS requesting conditional use approval for a dental office. The
property is located on two parcels at 5611 W Parkway Blvd and 2730 South 5600 West on 0.40 acres and is

Discussion: Jody Knapp presented on behalf of Brock Anderson. There were no questions or comments.


There being no further business the meeting was adjourned at 4:40 pm.
