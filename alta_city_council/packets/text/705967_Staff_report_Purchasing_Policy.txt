Alta Town Council
Staff Report:

To: Town of Alta Mayor and Council
From: Polly McLean
Date: April 14, 2021 Council Meeting
Re: Resolution Repealing and Replacing Purchasing Policy

         The existing Town of Alta purchasing policy is almost 10 years old. The Town may have a
separate purchasing policy than what the State of Utah requires in Utah Code 63G-6a and does not have
to abide by the state general procurement rules so long as the Town follows our own policy. The 2011
policy created confusion when a sealed bid was required if price was not the sole determining factor.

          The Staff feels the current policy is vague and would like clearer guidelines. Staff recommends
updating the policy to clarify the application of the purchasing policy. Some of the methods of receiving
bids can be improved upon to enhance consistency and transparency. The updated policy defines
request for proposals and invitation for bids. In addition, the updated policy revises the thresholds for
what steps need to be taken in the procurement. These updated thresholds are in line with many
Towns. The updated policy requires that the Town Council ratify any procurement over $25,000 in an
open meeting unless it has already been ratified as a line-item appropriation in the Budget.

          The updated policy follows Utah State Code requirements which sets thresholds for when
sealed bids are required for building improvements and public works projects which are based (by State
Code) upon increases from 2003 dollar amounts.
