Comments:


This application was approved by the Planning Commission on April 28, 2022, with an extension of
the approval granted by the Planning Commission on April 27, 2023. That extension set the
expiration date to October 28, 2023. The subdivision plat had to be recorded by October 28, 2023 in
order to prevent it from expiring. The applicant resubmitted the subdivision plat for review in
December 2023. The Planning Commission is being asked to review and approve the subdivision
plat as if it was a new application so that the applicant can record it. There have been no changes in
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) since the time of the initial approval in 2022 that would require
changes to the plat. The information in this staff report is from the original report dated April 28,
2022.



This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for approximately 2.41
acres located on the south side of 12300 South, at approximately 143 East 12300 South. The
property is currently zoned CSD-DP. The applicant is requesting that a Subdivision Plat Amendment
be approved to allow for division of the lot into two.
