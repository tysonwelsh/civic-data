discovered there was no permit for the campground. This application is for the permit and the 5 
expanded facilities. The campground owner wishes to add a restroom/shower facility for the 6 
upper campsites and RV pads for the volunteer camp hosts (who are on site full-time during the 7 
summer camping season) near the lower campsites. Specific details for the existing campground 8 
may be found in the letter of intent and site map accompanying the application. Access to the 9 
property does not meet the County Standards but staff is suggesting a design exception for the 10 
paved width and overall road width as the campground use was established prior to the current 11 
county standards and proposed expansions will not increase the traffic impact of the 12 
campground. Bear River Health Department (BRHD) has stated that the site is feasible for the 13 
proposed septic fields. County Fire has stated that access is adequate. There are sensitive areas 14 
across the majority of the site. Staff has identified a landslide hazard across the majority of the 15 
site. The applicant has submitted a geotechnical report stating the areas for the proposed 16 
restroom/shower facility and the RV pads are not within the landslide area. There are moderate 17 
and steep slopes identified and the geotechnical report identifies the areas for the proposed 18 
facilities as flat or nearly flat. There is a fault line that has been identified on the far western edge 19 
of the subject parcels. The geotechnical report notes that this fault is 960 feet from the site of the 20 
proposed restroom/shower facilities and does not include requirements in relation to this fault. 21

This application is for an 11-lot subdivision south of Hyrum. Access to this subdivision is across 4 
the Spillway over the Hyrum Dam which is not adequate and also is from the West and that does 5 
meet the County standard. The applicant has committed to making the needed improvements to 6 
the Western access. The County Council has stated that the County will extend maintenance no 7

intersection with private road 6500 South is appropriate in this case. The applicant would like to 9

the turnaround there would be a private road that would parallel the county road to provide the 12
