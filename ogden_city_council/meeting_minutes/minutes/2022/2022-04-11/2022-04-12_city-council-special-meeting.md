Approval of Minutes

Council member Blair stated he had reviewed the minutes of the joint work session of August 17, the joint work session
of September 7, and the special meeting of September 14, 2021 and found them to be accurate to the best of his recollection.

Council member Choberka stated she had reviewed the minutes of the regular meeting of October 5, 2021 and found
them to be accurate to the best of her recollection.

Council member Hyer stated he had reviewed the minutes of the regular meeting of October 29, 2021 and found them to
be accurate to the best of his recollection.

Council member White stated she had reviewed the minutes of the strategy session of September 21, the work session of
October 5, and the joint work session of October 19, 2021 and found them to be accurate to the best of her recollection.

COUNCIL MEMBER WHITE THEN MOVED TO APPROVE THE MINUTES AS PRESENTED. MOTION
WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR, ALL VOTING AYE.

Christmas Village Committee Appointment

A letter from Mayor Caldwell requesting consideration of the appointment of Richard Armstrong to the Ogden Christmas
Village Committee came before the Council for consideration.

COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR MOVED TO APPROVE THE APPOINTMENT OF RICHARD ARMSTRONG
TO THE CHRISTMAS VILLAGE COMMITTEE, WITH HIS TERM TO EXPIRE APRIL 1, 2022. MOTION WAS
SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER RICHEY, ALL VOTING AYE.

Christmas Village Committee Reappointments

A letter from Mayor Caldwell requesting consideration of the reappointments of Ernie Terrazas, Nick Morris, Diana
Meiser, Bonnie Baird, Cindi Hellewell, Sandra Tuck, and Judi Culley to the Ogden Christmas Village Committee came before the
Council for consideration.

COUNCIL MEMBER BLAIR MOVED TO APPROVE THE REAPPOINTMENTS OF ERNIE TERRAZAS,
NICK MORRIS, DIANA MEISER, BONNIE BAIRD, CINDI HELLEWELL, SANDRA TUCK, AND JUDI CULLEY TO
THE OGDEN CHRISTMAS VILLAGE COMMITTEE, WITH THEIR TERMS TO EXPIRE APRIL 1, 2025. MOTION
WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER RICHEY, ALL VOTING AYE.

Minutes of Special Meeting of Council of Ogden City, Utah, April 12, 2022

Closed Executive Session

At this time, the Council gave consideration of adjourning into a Closed Executive Session pursuant to one or more of the
provisions of Section 52-4-205(1) of the Open and Public Meetings Law:

a. Discussion of the character, professional competence, or physical or mental health of an individual

b. Strategy session to discuss collective bargaining

c. Strategy session to discuss pending or reasonably imminent litigation

d. Strategy session to discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property

e. Strategy session to discuss the sale of real property

f. Discussion regarding deployment of security personnel, devices, or systems

g. Investigative proceedings regarding allegations of criminal misconduct

COUNCIL MEMBER HYER MOVED THE COUNCIL ADJOURN INTO A CLOSED EXECUTIVE SESSION
PURSUANT TO THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 52-4-205(1) OF THE OPEN AND PUBLIC MEETINGS LAW.
MOTION WAS SECONDED BY COUNCIL MEMBER WHITE, WITH THE FOLLOWING ROLL CALL VOTE:
VOTING AYE— COUNCIL MEMBERS BLAIR, CHOBERKA, HYER, RICHEY, WHITE, VICE CHAIR LOPEZ, AND
CHAIR NADOLSKI. VOTING NO - NONE.

The meeting adjourned into Closed Executive Session at 6:36 p.m.
The meeting reconvened at 8:00 p.m.
