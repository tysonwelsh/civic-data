From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP

Re: Big Willow Creek Phase 1A 1st Amendment and 1B 2nd Amendment – Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 3.92 acres located at approximately 11507 S Wildrye Field Way. The property is currently zoned R3, R4, and RA1 . The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be approved to allow for Wildrye Field Way to be converted into a cul-de-sac. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  There is good cause for the vacation or amendment.
2.  The City Council has vacated the right-of-way through the street vacation process outlined in DCMC and Utah State Code, including noticing requirements. The street vacation of Wildrye Field Way was approved as Ordinance 1483, and is shown in the Plat Amendment graphically for reference.
