RTP-Air Quality Conformity Determination – Shawn Eliot, MAG 1:01:03 Every four years, MAG is required to prepare and adopt the Regional Transportation Plan, TransPlan50. MAG staff have worked collaboratively with the municipal, UDOT and UTA staff to develop a land use scenario, project lists, and funding estimates. The plan and technical documentation show the regional context of the transportation system today, plans for the future, and a financial plan with revenues, costs, and deficits. The plan meets all federal requirements, including being fiscally constrained and meeting air quality conformity. There are three phases in the plan: Phase 1 2023-2032, Phase 2 2033-2042, Phase 3 2043-2050. MPO TAC and the MPO Board have reviewed the list of projects, revenues and costs and approved them to be in the plan. Total costs in the plan are up 32% since the 2019 plan. Staff recommends that MPO TAC approve the changes to the highway and active transportation lists, discuss the implications of the concerns raised by UDOT, UTA, and others about projects MPO Technical Advisory Committee May 22, 2023 Page 3 needing additional study, and recommend approval to the MPO Board the RTP and Air Quality Conformity Determination. Proposed changes to TransPlan50 include: 1. Adopt additional revenue and highway projects adjusted 2. Adopt updated Active Transportation Projects List 3. Should Point of the Mountain Connection FWY and Light Rail remain in Needs- based project list or move to Vision until further study can identify performance-based benefits? 4. Should Meadows Crossing be added in the Needs-based Project List or remain as Vision status until further study can identify performance-based benefits? 5. MAG and The MPO TAC discussed the following process elements: • Environmental Guidance • Design and Construction Standards • Scope changes and concept cost estimates • Field Review vs Meeting No action will be taken until the August MPO TAC meeting. 2023-24 Unified Planning Work Program (Work Program) – Shawn Eliot, MAG 2:09:54 The Mountainland MPO Work Program describes the transportation planning work undertaken by Mountainland MPO staff, UDOT staff, and UTA staff within the MPO planning area. The Work Program is an annual budget for the planning activities to be undertaken using federal planning funds. It illustrates to our MPO partners, Federal Highway Administration, Federal Transit Administration, and the general public where the planning resources are planned to be allocated. It describes the planned work, budget, and funding sources. The FY24 Mountainland MPO Work Program has a total budget of $6.9m, $2.4m for staff and admin costs and $4.5m for studies and contracts. Revenue for the program comes from 44% federal funds, 14% state funds, 38% MPO Exchange funds, 3% UTA funds and less than 1% local funds. Staff costs have increased this year from $1.8m to $2.4m or 38%. This increase is due to an underestimation of needed funding last year with the Planning Department restructure. Last year's budget included two new staff positions (which were not filled) and more staff time within the RPO area. Looking over the last two years, the average staff costs have increased 5% per year. Studies and contracts have increased over 55% from last year due to multiple proposed corridor studies, the Safe Streets for All federal grant, the Statewide Grid Study, and an FTA Areas of Persistent Poverty Study. A detailed list of studies and contracts are listed in the Mountainland MPO Work Program.
