3.    Mister Car Wash; 5512 West 7800 South; Preliminary Site Plan; SC-2 Zone; TerraForm
      Companies, LLC (applicant) [#21780; parcel 20-25-351-020]

Elliot Smith, applicant, said they propose a Mister Car Wash tunnel-style wash. He showed the
proposed site plan and elevations for Lot 208 of Highlands Landing. They have no problem with
increasing the front landscaping to a ten-foot minimum. They will also increase the landscape buffer
on the east side of the property to eight feet in width. The building is 5,000 square feet and can
accommodate five cars at a time. There will be two to three employees maximum.

Matt Quinney asked if there will be connectivity with the other parcels in the subdivision.

Elliot Smith said that has not been part of the discussion or comments from staff. He explained the
three-lane queue that will alleviate backup of vehicles.

Ray McCandless clarified that the initial submittal was for preliminary and final site plan review.
There were some items that still need to be addressed, so staff is asking for approval on preliminary
site tonight and final will be reviewed and approved by staff, which is typical. There was no discussion
regarding interconnectivity between parcels due to the drive aisle pattern. The west area is mostly for
vacuums, the north portion is a street that links to other businesses in the area. Staff’s only
recommendation on the site plan was to increase the landscaping along 7800 South to at least 10 feet
for an enhanced presence.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the Preliminary Site Plan for a carwash,
located at 5512 West 7800 South in a SC-2 zone subject to the approval conditions listed below:

1.   Final approval granted by staff when all requirements for final site plan approval have been met.
2.   An approved preliminary site plan shall remain valid for one year following the date of the
     approval.
3.   Increase the front landscaped area to at least 10’ wide between the south property line and
     driveway curb.
4.   An avigation easement is required from the Salt Lake Department of Airports.
5.   The proposed development shall meet all applicable 2009 City Code requirements.

5.    Take 5 Oil Change; 8980 South Redwood Road; Preliminary Site Plan, Preliminary
      Subdivision Plat, and Conditional Use Permit for Vehicle and Equipment Repair, limited;
      SC-2 Zone; Wildcat 1West Jordan/Mitch Vande Guchte (applicant) [#21945, 21944,
      21941; parcel 27-03-176-022]

Christy Kennedy, Reeve and Associates, explained that the facility is a drive-thru only oil change with
no customer waiting room.

Mitch Vande Guchte, applicant, said this new concept focuses on fast, friendly, and clean service in ten
minutes or less, which keeps the customers moving. The Take 5 building is on the southeast corner and
they are looking for a co-tenant for a building on the southwest side and are in discussions with regards
to needs for parking and building size, etc. They have also been talking with Smith’s to get a cross
access on the west, which looks favorable. The buildings will be new construction and the property
will be resurfaced.

Kent Shelton asked what type of co-tenant they anticipate.

Mitch Vande Guchte said they are specifically looking at coffee drive through because the main hours
for Take 5 is during lunch. However, the site will be designed to accommodate both uses at once.

Kent Page gave an overview of the project. The application is for a conditional use permit for vehicle
and equipment repair, preliminary subdivision plat, and preliminary site plan. The use is in line with
the general plan and zoning. Staff is highly encouraging a cross access with Smith’s. The site plan
shows the stacking capabilities of the drive through and oil change buildings. The landscaping plan
will greatly improve the aesthetics of the intersection. The subdivision plat is needed to dedicate
property to Redwood Road and 9000 South rights-of-way. Staff is uncomfortable with recommending
approving of the fast food drive through building. Staff would like to know the tenant to determine
how feasible or appropriate the site plan is for a fast food drive through use and a separate conditional
use permit is required.

Matt Quinney said even if we know who the tenant is, that could change. He asked if the new tenant
would need to obtain their own conditional use permit. He asked why the commission couldn’t
approve that conditional use permit tonight.

Larry Gardner said a conditional use permit for a fast food drive through would run with the land and
would be good for any other fast food drive through use. The conditional use permit that was
advertised for tonight’s meeting is for vehicle and equipment repair. The applicant may come back for
the fast food conditional use permit at any time.

6.    Parker Place Rezone; 7398 South Redwood Road; Rezone 2.17 acres from R-3-8 (SHO)
      (Multi-family Residential 8 units per acre maximum density – Senior Housing Overlay) to
      R-3-8 (Multi-family Residential 8 units per acre maximum density); Gary Cannon
      (applicant) [#21810; parcel 21-27-178-044, 045]

Gary Cannon, applicant, said this project is going on three years and it is in a very unique situation
with the surrounding uses. There have been a number of challenges with development that necessitates
the request to single-family homes without the senior housing overlay.

Larry Gardner said Parker Place was originally zoned with a senior housing overlay, restricted to 55+
and other restrictions such as entrances at slab on grade, no basements, etc. Mr. Cannon has run into
several issues that were not at his causing. The sewer line is much shallower than what City standards
allow, so now the homes need to have access with stairs instead of slab on grade. He had to install the
pipe for the City storm drain line that goes into the irrigation box. There was a costly bore under
Redwood Road to connect the sewer line. Density of the project, housing type, and lot layout will not
change with the removal of the overlay. These homes will be similar to those at Gansen Lane, but with
larger lots. The R-3-8 zone is preferable and allows 4,000 square foot lots minimum. These lots are
4,000 - 5,000 square feet. If the zoning were changed to R-1-5 then the subdivision would have to be
resubmitted and the layout might change. He didn’t think that removal of the overlay would negatively
affect the surrounding neighborhood.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City
Council to remove the Senior Housing Overlay for 2.17 acres for Parker Place; 7398 South Redwood
Road.

Matt Quinney said on paper it looks the same, but a senior housing project would have many fewer
cars. Single-family homes could have more cars, which would add congestion on Drake Lane to the
farm and exiting onto Redwood Road.

Gary Cannon said the road was built to City standards. The goat farm will not be there forever, and the
road will eventually provide an access point for that five acres. They will honor and accommodate the
farm and the other business on that lane while they are in operation. Business traffic already goes to
the farm. The road was built to accommodate the excess traffic now and in the future.

Matt Quinney said the bigger concern is the traffic that will be going onto Redwood Road. When the
Drake farm is developed it will be connected to his neighborhood. These will be his neighbors and he
is concerned with the many access points in the area and it will bring more congestion onto Redwood
Road. This road is also close to the school access.

Kent Shelton asked if Drake Lane or any of the other streets will allow for on-street parking.

Gary Cannon said the parking requirements have been met for the subdivision with two-car garages
and driveways that meet city standards. There is an office parcel to the south that could possibly be
used for parking at night depending on how it is developed.

Larry Gardner said Drake Lane meets city standards for a neighborhood street, and he didn’t see a
reason to prohibit street parking. But if it becomes an issue, then it can be addressed with the city
traffic engineer.

Pamela Bloom asked if a traffic study was done with the project and if they could require a right turn
only to prevent accidents.

Gary Cannon said there was a study done on this development and Sunset Villas to the north at the
same time.

Matt Quinney opened the public comment.

Jeanette Drake, West Jordan resident, said there are pros and cons to the development. Mr. Drake said
he would like it remain farmland, but since it is too late for that they need to get on with the project.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

Gary Cannon said the Drake family has been very patient. It has been hard on them economically due
to road construction and utility location. When this project is finished there will be a paved road for
them.

MOTION:        Pamela Bloom moved, based on the information and findings set forth in the staff
               report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to forward a
               positive recommendation to the City Council to remove the Senior Housing
               Overlay for Parker Place; 7398 South Redwood Road; Gary Cannon (applicant).
               The motion was seconded by Jay Thomas and passed 6-0 in favor. Trish Hatch was
               absent.
