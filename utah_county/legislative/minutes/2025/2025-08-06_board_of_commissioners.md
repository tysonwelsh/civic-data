“Jeanne Bowen is a true public servant of Utah County. Jeanne is always willing to go the extra mile
to help the public as well as other Utah County employees, she is willing to go above and beyond
for anybody who needs help. She has served on UCEA and Well 4 Life for many years and always
ensures her office and those around her know what is going on. She encourages us to participate
in activities and to complete health challenges, she leads by example and forms “teams” and can
be seen taking walks on her breaks and encourages others to do the same. She really tries to make
things better for her fellow employees. She also contributes to risk management and is on the
board for the Utah Valley Roads Special Service district. We often joke that Jeanne is the smartest
one in the building, this is because if she does not know the answer, she will find it! Oftentimes she
will walk with a taxpayer to different offices to help make sure all their questions are answered
and taken care of. She offers to do anything she can to help a taxpayer. If there is a difficult, rare,
or confusing problem, Jeanne is the first to step up and volunteer to find a solution. She is very
thorough when dealing with the day-to-day responsibilities of her job. She is also a licensed notary
and is always willing to drop what she is doing to assist the public and other employees with
notaries. Jeanne is a very empathetic person; she listens and talks with taxpayers or anyone
around who needs a listening ear. We have watched Jeanne help someone figure out the bus
schedule and walk them to the nearest bus stop after they came in to pay taxes. We have also seen
Jeanne assist an elderly person to their car to ensure they had the right paperwork and would not
need to come back into the office. Any time Jeanne is in the lobby you can hear her asking, “how
are you?” or “can I help you find something?” She is a very dedicated employee of Utah County; she
rarely takes time off and it never bothers her to stay late or if helping someone cuts into her lunch
hour. She truly is here to serve the taxpayer. Jeanne is delightful to work with, she is fun, and a
very daring person. She is known for having a once a year “weird food day” where she invites
others to bring and try weird foods. Our office would not be the same without her and Utah County
is lucky to have her as an employee.”

-The Utah County Treasurer’s Office
