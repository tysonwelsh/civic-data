Public hearing and recommendation to the County Council for amendments to Sections 10-4-9: Parking Requirements, 10-8 General Regulations, 10-2-10 Use Table and 10-11-1 Terms Defined. The purpose of the amendments is to create regulations for Electric Vehicle Parking, Bicycle Parking, and Solar Energy Systems, and to amend the existing gas station regulations. Legislative Process. Project #24-179.

Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
December 10,2024 Staff Report.pdf

, and Megan Nick,
Sustainability Analyst
, addressed the commission.
Senta Beyer,
Active Transportation Planning Manager
, introduced herself to the Commission.
Planner Milliner reviewed the proposed recommendations.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions of Planner Milliner.
Kelbe Goupil with Utah Clean Energy provided additional information for the discussion.
Additional Commissioner comments and questions were heard. Emily Quinton and Ray Milliner responded to the concerns.
Commission Vice-Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 8:07 p.m.
No public comment was heard.
Commission Vice-Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 8:08 p.m.
Matthew Nagie made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the Summit County Council per the findings of fact, conclusions of law, in the staff report with the following amendments: under section 10-4-9I-3A4 be changed to say that electric vehicle charging stations shall be level 2, minimum of 40 amp or level 3 type, and that 10-4-9I-3B1 specify level 2, 40 amp minimum or level 3 charging capability; 10-4-9I-3B2 be updated so that each single family attached, single family detached or two family dwelling unit shall provide one electric vehicle ready parking space with level 2, 40 amp charging capability; bicycle parking requirements under 10-4-9J-4B6 specify electric vehicle ready level 1; Chapter 10-8-8, gasoline service stations with or without a convenience store, replace requirement D to note compliance with parking requirements in general, this applies to MNU-1 throughout equally, and to be added under Chapter 10-11 definitions, strike any mention of level 1 or level 2, but rather have electric vehicle ready and refer only to its discussion on conduit and wiring. Thomas Cooke seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).  Abstain: Bruce Carmichael.
