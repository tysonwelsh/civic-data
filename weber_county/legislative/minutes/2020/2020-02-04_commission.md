    6. AMEND WEBER COUNTY CODES §104-5, 104-6, 104-7, 104-8, & 104-25 TO REGULATE CULTIVATION &
       PROCESSING OF MEDICAL CANNABIS, AS REQUIRED BY STATE CODE, & TO PROVIDE RELATED MINOR EDITS
       TO CLARIFY INTENT AND CONSISTENCY – ORDINANCE 2020-3.
       Charles Ewert, of the County Planning Division, referred to the public hearing held last week when the
       Commission requested to see how the language could be amended to also fit into the M-1 and M-2 Zones, while
       not disrupting that area, particularly concerning odors. This ordinance includes those amendments. It also
       includes that if a residential use exists, or is later located within 500 feet of the facility, the site shall have a 6-
       foot land berm or an 8-foot masonry wall constructed to shield the facility’s view from the residential properties,
       and it also removes residential dwelling units from the M-1 and M-2 as suggested by the 2003 General Plan.
       Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Ordinance 2020-3 amending the Weber County Codes §104-5, §104-6,
       §104-7, §104-8 and §104-25 to regulate the cultivation and processing of medical cannabis, as required by State
       Code, and to provide related minor edits to clarify intent and consistency; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
       Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
       Commissioner Harvey was excused at this time.
    7. FINAL APPROVAL OF FENSTER FARMS PHASE 2 SUBDIVISION, AN EIGHT LOT SUBDIVISION, AS WELL AS
       ACCEPTANCE OF ROAD DEDICATION WITHIN THIS SUBDIVISION.
       Tammy Aydelotte, of the County Planning Division, presented this standard subdivision in the A-2 Zone. The
       road dedication is approximately 700 linear feet of a 60-foot wide county road, and the applicant has escrowed
       for the remainder of the needed improvements per the County Engineer’s requirements.
       Commissioner Jenkins moved to grant final approval of the Fenster Farms Phase 2 Subdivision, an eight lot
       subdivision, as well as acceptance of road dedication within this subdivision; Chair Froerer seconded.
       Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
    9. MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING WITH WEBER PATHWAYS FOR THE DONATION AND USE OF COUNTY
       OPTION SALES AND USE TAX FOR HIGHWAYS AND PUBLIC TRANSIT, ALSO KNOWN AS “PROP 1 FUNDS.”
       Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development Director, noted that Weber Pathways often requests
       donations from the county to construct and maintain trails. This MOU eliminates the need for a separate
       agreement for each request. The county allocated $200,000 in the 2020 budget from the Prop 1 Fund for this.
       Commissioner Jenkins moved to approve the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Weber Pathways
       for the donation and use of county option sales and use tax for highways and public transit (Prop 1 funds); Chair
       Froerer seconded.
       Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
