    2. Public Hearing to consider the approval of the issuance of private activity bonds for use at The Lodge at
       North Ogden.

         Chris Crockett (Civil Attorney): This public hearing is required under the Tax Equity and Fiscal
         Responsibility Act. This is to issue bonds in favor of Alpha Lifestyle Partners to get revenue for an existing
         senior residential living facility called the Lodge in North Ogden in the amount of $55M. By holding this
         public hearing, the County is not committing any funds and is not liable. We are a participating body for the
         public to come and express comment regarding this particular issue. The County is not taking on any
         responsibility with this.

         Commissioner Bolos: Are there other public actions associated with this?

         Mr. Crockett: I believe North Ogden may have to hold a public hearing because that is the municipality with
         jurisdiction over this care facility. There are several public hearings happening with this project throughout
         the country seeking Bonds in the amount of $275M for several different facilities.
