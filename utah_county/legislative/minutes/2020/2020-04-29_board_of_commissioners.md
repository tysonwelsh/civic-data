            COMMISSIONER LEE - WENT THROUGH THE 3 DIFFERENT DRAFTS AND DISCUSSED QUESTIONS,
            CONCERNS AND SUGGESTIONS.

            EXPRESSED THAT THERE NEEDS TO BE A WAY FOR POLICY TO PROMOTE THE BUSINESS OF THE
            COUNTY, BE DEFENDABLE AND BE CONSISTENT THROUGHOUT THE ENTIRE POLICY. WANTS TO STAY
            AWAY FROM ANYTHING THAT HAS ‘PERSONAL USE’ ATTACHED.

            CORT GRIFFIN, DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY – FLAGGED COMMISSIONER LEE’S AREAS OF CONCERNS
            FROM THE LAST MEETING AND SENT THEM TO PUBLIC WORKS AND THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE TO REVIEW.

            COMMISSIONER LEE – STATED THAT HE WANTS TO MAKE SURE THAT THERE’S SUFFICIENT LEEWAY
            FOR DEPARTMENT HEADS, PARTICULARILY THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE. NEED TO MAKE SURE THERE IS THE
            APPROPRIATE AMOUNT OF FLEXIBILITY.

            SHAUN BUFTON, UTAH COUNTY SHERIFF’S DEPARTMENT – TALKED ABOUT COMMON PRACTICE AND
            THE HASSLE THAT WOULD COME WITH APPROVAL OF COMMON PRACTICE COUNTY VEHICLE USE.
            CLARIFIED ON-CALL DEFINITION AND THE POLICY OF BEING READY TO GO AT A MOMENTS NOTICE.

            SHERIFF MIKE SMITH – EXPRESSED CONCERNS. ON-CALL RATE IS $1.25. WHEN SOMETHING
            HAPPENDS IN THIS COUNTY, DEPUTIES, PUBLIC WORKS EMPLOYEES, ETC. NEED TO BE READY.
            COUNTY RESIDENTS EXPECT THEM TO BE THERE. STATED THAT HE WAS ELECTED TO THE SHERIFFS
            OFFICE AND TOOK AN OATH TO USE THOSE RESOURCES EFFICIENTLY AND EFFECTIVELY. ASKED TO LET
            HIM RUN THE DEPARTMENT. IS NOT IN FAVOR OF PERSONAL USE OF GOVERNMENT EQUIPMENT, BUT
            THERE NEEDS TO BE BALANCE OF EMPLOYEES BEING ABLE TO DO THEIR JOB AND HAVE THE
            RESOURCES AVAILABLE.

            RICHARD NIELSON, DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC WORKS – STATED ALL DEPARTMENT HEADS AND ELECTED
            OFFICIALS TRY TO MANAGE EVERYTHING IN THEIR DEPARTMENTS AS BEST AS POSSIBLE IN THE MOST
            COST-EFFECTIVE MANNER FOR THE COUNTY.

            COMMISSIONER IVIE – AGREES THAT THERE SHOULD BE THE ABILITY, WITHIN THE POLICY, TO MAKE
            EXCEPTIONS WHEN THE NEEDS ARISES.

            CORT GRIFFIN – THE POLICY APPROVES THE ASSIGNMENT OF THE VEHICLES TO DEPARTMENTS. THERE
            IS A SEMI-ANNUAL REPORT ON VEHICLE USE PROVIDED TO THE COMMISSION. THINKS THE
            PROVISION BEING TALKED ABOUT GOES TO THE DAY-TO-DAY OPERATION AND WOULD DESIGNATE
            WHICH EMPLOYEES WOULD HAVE OVERNIGHT USE. IF A SITUATION WOULD ARISE THAT
            COMMISSION APPROVAL WOULD BE NEEDED, HOW WOULD THAT BE HANDLED? NEEDS TO BE
            DECIDED.

            COMMISSIONER AINGE – SUGGESTED TO TABLE THIS AGENDA ITEM UNTIL THE END OF THE MEETING
            TO GIVE CORT GRIFFIN/ATTORNEY TIME TO REVIEW CHANGES AND SUGGESTIONS GIVEN AND DRAFT
            A NEW VERSION THAT WILL BE ADDRESSED AFTER CLOSED MEETING.
