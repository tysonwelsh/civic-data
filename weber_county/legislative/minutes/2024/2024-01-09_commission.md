     3. APPROVE CONTRACT WITH FRONT LINE PUBLIC SAFETY SOLUTIONS FOR AN EARLY
        INTERVENTION SYSTEM.
         Chief Aaron Perry (Sheriff’s Department): This ties into the early intervention grant (Item G2 above). We

    4. APPROVE WEBER COUNTY ORDINANCE 2024-02 AMENDING CHAPTER 2-15 OF THE
       WEBER COUNTY CODE.

         Lauren Shafer (Elections Director): No changes from the First Reading. Two new report periods added to
         the campaign finance rules to provide voters a chance to see how candidates are spending their money prior
         to convention and prior to ballots being mailed. This report will be provided before both the primary and
         general elections and then one before convention. Currently, voters have access to this information only 7
         days prior to the election which is often after most voters have voted.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve Weber County Ordinance 2024-02 Amending Chapter 2-15 of the
         Weber County Code; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye

    10. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH VALUEBASE TO DEVELOP AN AUTOMATED MODEL, DATA
        VISUALIZATION LAYER AND SOFTWARE MAINTENANCE SERVICE.
         John Ulibarri (County Assessor): Deedee Kimber performs various functions in our office one of which is
         regression modeling. Deedee announced her intent to retire in 2026. One option we have is hiring a data or
         modeling specialist or contracting with an outside vendor. We chose to go with a contractor for $44k. This

    11. APPROVE A CONTRACT WITH MULTI COUNTY APPRAISAL TRUST (MCAT) FOR
        PARTICIPATION IN THE DEVELOPMENT OF A STATEWIDE ELECTRONIC BUSINESS
        PERSONAL PROPERTY FILING SYSTEM.
         John Ulibarri (County Assessor): This agreement with Multi County Appraisal Trust (MCAT) is for Weber
         County to provide certain services. One of these services assists with the statewide online business and
         personal property filing system, of which Weber County’s was the first to go online. We are one of four or
         five counties that have adopted,,, Monica Dolan will provide end user administrator experience and
         test/detect coding errors, and review the telecom filings statewide. MCAT will pay Weber County
         $2400/month or $28.8k per year with a 30 day termination agreement.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve a contract with Multi County Appraisal Trust (MCAT) for
