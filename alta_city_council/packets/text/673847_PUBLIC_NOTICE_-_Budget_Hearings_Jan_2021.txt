                                  PUBLIC NOTICE

The Alta Town Council will hold a public hearing to receive comments and questions
regarding proposed amendments to the 2020-2021 Fiscal Budgets (General Fund and
Sewer and Water Enterprise Funds).

The public hearing will be held virtually on Wednesday, January 13, 2021 at 4:00 p.m.
with live viewing details to be announced on the Town’s website at www.townofalta.com

Questions regarding the proposed amendments to the 2020-2021 Fiscal Budgets may be
directed to Piper Lever, Town Clerk, P.O. Box 8016, Alta, Utah 84092 or by emailing
plever@townofalta.com. The deadline for submittal of written comments is 3:00 pm on
Tuesday, January 12, 2021
