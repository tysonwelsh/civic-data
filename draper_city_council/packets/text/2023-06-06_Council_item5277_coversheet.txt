Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: June 6, 2023
Re: Resolution 23-26 Approving the First Amendment to Ground Lease Agreement Comments: The City entered into a Ground Lease Agreement with The Living Planet, Inc., doing business as The Living Planet Aquarium dated December 6, 2012, for development of the Aquarium property located at approximately 12033 Lone Peak Parkway in Draper.
The Aquarium has constructed the improvements contemplated by the Lease.  Draper City has benefitted financially and in intangible ways from the success of the Aquarium.
The Aquarium has received approval from Draper City to reconfigure the lots in order to expand the attractions offered to the public. T he City and the Aquarium desire to amend the Lease to adjust the legal description of property owned by the City and leased by the Aquarium.
The findings are as follows: Approving the First Amendment to Ground Lease Agreement promotes the health, safety and welfare of the residents of Draper City. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Resolution #23-26 6/2/2023 Cover Memo
