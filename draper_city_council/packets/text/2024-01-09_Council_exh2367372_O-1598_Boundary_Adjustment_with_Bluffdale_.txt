                              ORDINANCE NO. 1598

         AN ORDINANCE APPROVING AN ADJUSTMENT TO THE COMMON
         BOUNDARY BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND BLUFFDALE CITY.

     WHEREAS, Utah Code § 10-2-419 provides that legislative bodies of two or
more municipalities having common boundaries may adjust their common
boundaries; and

      WHEREAS, Draper City and Bluffdale City are municipalities within the State of
Utah and share a common boundary; and

     WHEREAS, Draper City and Bluffdale City desire to adjust their municipal
boundaries as provided by state law; and

       WHEREAS, on October 17, 2023, the Draper City Council passed Resolution 23-
62, and Bluffdale City passed Resolution 2023-52 on October 11, 2023 stating their
interest in reviewing a municipal boundary adjustment; and

      WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would adjust the common boundary
between Draper City and Bluffdale City as depicted in Exhibit A attached hereto and
incorporated herein by this reference.

       WHEREAS, the boundary adjustment would relocate approximately 0.889
acres (“0.889 Acres”) of property from Draper City into Bluffdale City; and

     WHEREAS, it is more efficient and economical for Bluffdale City to provide
municipal services to the 0.889 Acres once they are developed; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City has posted appropriate notice according to Utah Code
§ 10-2-419(2); and

      WHEREAS, Bluffdale City held a public hearing and passed Ordinance NO.
2023-29, on December 13, 2023; and

     WHEREAS, Draper City has held a public hearing regarding the adjustment of
a common boundary; and




Ordinance No. 1598                       1       Draper City- Bluffdale City Common Boundary Adjustment
      WHEREAS, the proposed Common Boundary Adjustment set forth herein has
been reviewed by the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been
held in accordance with state law to obtain public input regarding the proposed
Boundary Adjustment.



       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
CITY, STATE OF UTAH:

       Section 1. Boundary Adjustment. The City of Draper approves the
Boundary Adjustment of 0.889 acres of property as provided in Exhibit A, otherwise
known as the Draper City-Bluffdale City Common Boundary Adjustment. Final survey
quality control may make required technical adjustments to the exhibits before
presentation to the Lieutenant Governor as required by Utah State Law

       Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

     Section 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective when
each municipality has adopted an ordinance under Utah Code § 10-2-419(5)
approving the boundary adjustment.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THE 9th DAY OF January, 2024.
