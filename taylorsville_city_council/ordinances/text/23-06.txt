                                   TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                    ORDINANCE NO. 23- 06


AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING MAP
        AMENDMENT FOR THE PROPERTY LOCATED AT 5758 S. 1300 W. IN
   TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH, FROM SINGLE-FAMILY R- 1- 40 TO SINGLE-FAMILY
                                              R- 1- 20.


        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council ( the" Council") mit in regular session on June

7, 2023, to consider, among other things, approving a text amendment to the Taylorsville Zoning
Map for the property located at 5758 S. 1300 W. in Taylorsville, Utah, from single-family R- 1- 40
to single- family R- 1- 20; and

        WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 040, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was sent to all owners of property within
300 feet on April 29, 2023; and


       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also posted in City Hall on April 29, 2023; and

       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also posted on Utah' s Public Notice website on May 5, 2023; and


       WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendment on May 9, 2023, during which the Planning Commission voted 7- 0 to
recommend that the Council approve the proposed zoning map amendment; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendment during its regularly scheduled meeting on June 7,
2023; and


        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




         NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
zoning map amendment for the property located at 5758 S. 1300 W. in Taylorsville, Utah, from
single- family R- 1- 40 to single- family R- 1- 20 is hereby approved, as articulated in Exhibit" A."

        This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 23- 06, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
