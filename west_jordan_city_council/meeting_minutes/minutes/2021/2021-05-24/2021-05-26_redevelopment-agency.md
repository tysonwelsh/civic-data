2. PUBLIC HEARING
   a. Tentative Budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency for Fiscal Year
      2022
Administrative Services Director Danyce Steck reported regarding the Tentative Budget for the City
of West Jordan Redevelopment Agency. She explained that they had been asked to be more
proactive with using the Redevelopment Agency funds. She was happy to report a proposal for the
sale of land owned by the agency for recovery of land purchased. Also proposed were several areas
of improvements in the downtown district area. Those improvements would incentivize economic
growth and beautify the areas increasing their value. It was proposed that 1.5 million be utilized for
street scape improvements and 8.5 million be utilized for sewer and water improvements. She
excitedly noted the economic incentive agreements and a final payment of bonds in the proposed
budget.

Vice Chair Green opened a public hearing at 7:29 pm

Citizen Comments:
None

Vice Chair Green closed the public hearing at 7:29 pm

MOTION:       Board Member Christopher McConnehey moved to approve Resolution No. 225
              adopting the Tentative Budget for the City of West Jordan Redevelopment
              Agency for Fiscal Year 2022 and set June 9, 2021 as a public hearing to receive
              comment regarding the final budget.

3. BUSINESS ITEMS
   a. Consider approving Resolution No. 226 amending the Redevelopment Agency bylaws
Economic Development Director Chris Pengra oriented the Council with amendments to the
Redevelopment Agency bylaws that would bring them current and add a provision designating a
Chair and Vice Chair for the board. He noted that Adam Long, outside counsel for the RDA, was
available for questions and had reviewed and approved the proposed changes.

Board Member David Pack asked for clarification regarding the duties of the Board Chair. It was
explained that duties were similar to those for a City Council Chair and the time required was greatly
reduced noting that meetings were held only as needed.

Board Member McConnehey asked in the future that a legislative version with redlines be provided
for better clarity of the proposed changes. Mr. Pengra explained that the proposal was a basically a
new version and not much had been kept from the original version, making a redlined version too
confusing. He also noted the proposed amendment requested by Chair Jacob for language detailing
that if a vote did not take place appointing a Board Chair and Vice Chair that Council Chair and Vice
Chair would assume those roles ensuring the board was not without those positions.

Mr. Pengra responded to a question for clarification about the provision, he felt that the provision
would protect the board. He further explained that it had been many years since the bylaws were
approved and since that time, many provisions had been rendered obsolete, making the board
technically not in compliance as an oversight. The provision would keep the board in compliance
even if there was nothing pressing the board to meet. Board Member Pack noted he would be happy
to help out in one of either capacity.

Board Member McConnehey asked for the addition of a designee in Section 5 for the Secretary of
the agency. He asked for clarification regarding the Chairperson terms coinciding with City Council
terms. It was his understanding that when you start your term on Council, you would then also be a
board member for a four-year term. However, the appointment section stated that it would be done
on an annual basis. Mr. Long, Legal Counsel for the Redevelopment Agency explained that it could
be handle however the Chair or Vice Chair were willing. Section 8 was proposed so that the board
at least thought about it annually. It would not matter if the position was a one-year term or four-
year term, the goal was to ensure someone took the responsibility. Board Member McConnehey
preferred that the positions be one-year terms. Board Member Green agreed and requested that the
language be modified for an annual process/term. Board Member McConnehey asked that the
positions also be appointed by resolution.

   c. Letter of intent for the development of a surf park
Economic Development Director Chris Pengra mentioned that there Kip and Josh were available to
answer any questions regarding the letter of intent for purchase of RDA property to build a surf
park.

Board Member Green briefly oriented the board regarding the letter of intent for the purchase of a
Redevelopment Agency property for the development of a surf park at 9000 S Mountain View
Corridor. Mr. Pengra clarified that the letter was a non-binding, and a public hearing would be held
at a future meeting for the acceptance of a purchase agreement.

Board Member McConnehey mentioned an interest in the agreement and how it would shape up,
noting that there were still a lot of details to work out.
