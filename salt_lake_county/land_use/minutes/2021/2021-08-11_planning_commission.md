                             PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
 
(Continued from May 12, June 16, and July 14, 2021) - An ordinance amending the following sections 
of the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001: 19.12.020 (permitted uses in the FR zone), 19.12.030(g) 
(conditional uses in the FR zone), and 19.54.020 (permitted uses in the FA zone) to have uniform regulations 
in the FR and FA zones with regards to animal uses and their associated impacts on adjoining properties, 
the environment, and the well-being of animals; and making other related changes. Presenter: Zach Shaw 
(Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commissions wi ll act on the proposed ordinance amendments after taking comments from 
the public during their respective public hearings. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the 
planning commissions’ rules of conduct. Public comments will be limited to three minutes per person. The 
public is also invited to review and inspect the proposed ordinances at 
https://www.utah.gov/pmn/index.html under the respective Planning Commissions’ agendas. 
 
The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Jim McNulty provided an analysis 
of the ordinance amendment and citizen input. Salt Lake County Counsel Zach Shaw went over the 
ordinance amendment changes. 
 
Commissioners, Staff and Counsel had a brief discussion regarding setbacks, maximum horses regardless 
of size, with properties with a stream, grandfather clause on 8B, and manure management plan.  
 
Salt Lake County Flood Control Engineering Robert Thompson  provided a summary  and reviewed 
appendix A based on the 100-foot setback based on FCOZ. 
 
Commissioner Collard  inquired about ephemeral streams and any measure of how many ephemeral 
streams we have and how many properties would be affected by this streams.  
 
Commissioners, St aff and Counsel had a brief discussion regarding surface water and warrant same 
protections. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion.
