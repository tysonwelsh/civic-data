Study Session

      Joint Council and Planning Commission Discussion Regarding Zoning and
      Subdivision Code Updates
      Mayor Pro Tem Mike Green welcomed the Planning Commission and others in
      attendance. Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky introduced
      Jackie Berg, a consultant with Houseal Lavigne. Ms. Berg shared feedback
      received from a public input process and presented a Preliminary
      Recommendations Report regarding Draper Zoning and Subdivision Codes.

      Mayor Walker arrived at 5:52 pm.

      Ms. Berg and Ms. Jastremsky answered questions from the Council and
      Planning Commission about proposed expanded housing type allowances
      (Mixed Residential District and Residential Infill Overlay District). Ms. Berg
      suggested further discussion when district-specific standards were drafted.

6.    Items for Council Consideration
6.a   Public Hearing: Ordinances #1621 and #1622, an Ordinance amending
      the official Land Use Map of Draper City for approximately 0.75 acres
      of property from Residential Low/Medium Density to Residential
      Medium Density and an Ordinance amending the official Zoning Map of
      Draper City for approximately 0.75 acres from RA2 (Residential
      Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) to R3 (Single Family
      Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum), for the property located
      at approximately 1201 E. 13200 South, otherwise known as the Norman
      Land Use Map and Zoning Map Amendments
      Maryann Pickering, a Planner, showed the subject property on a map and
      explained the request to amend the Zoning Map for approximately 0.75
      acres from RA2 to RA3, and the Land Use Map from Residential Low/Medium
      Density to Residential Medium Density.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing.

      Sam Norman, the applicant, explained his desire to develop two lots on his
      property in conjunction with two lots to be developed by his brother on the
      neighboring property. He said he believed four lots on the more than two
      acres was the best use of the land. Mr. Norman answered questions from
      the Council about access to the proposed development.

      Mayor Walker closed the public hearing.

      Community Development Director Jennifer Jastremsky explained the
      required road widths.

6.b   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1623, amending the text of Chapter 4 of the
      Draper City General Plan to replace a Moderate Income Housing Plan
      strategy
      Todd Taylor, a Planner, explained the recommendation to proceed with
      Strategy E from the Moderate Income Housing Plan (create or allow for and
      reduce regulations related to internal or attached accessory dwelling units
      in residential zones) to meet State requirements. Mr. Taylor summarized a
      proposed implementation plan.

      Mayor Walker opened a public hearing, and closed the public hearing
      seeing no one come forward.

      Councilmember Green moved to approve Ordinance #1623.
      Councilmember Roberts seconded the motion.

      A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
