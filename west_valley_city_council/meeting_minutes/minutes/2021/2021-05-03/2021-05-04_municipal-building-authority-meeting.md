MINUTES OF BUILDING AUTHORITY REGULAR MEETING – MAY 4, 2021


 20-01: ADOPT A TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR THE SUPPORT OF THE
 BUILDING AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR COMMENCING JULY 1,
 2021 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2022; SET JUNE 1, 2021 AS THE DATE FOR THE
 PUBLIC HEARING
 Wayne Pyle, Chief Executive Officer, discussed proposed Resolution 21-01 that would
 adopt a tentative budget for the support of the Building Authority for the fiscal year
 commencing July 1, 2021 and ending June 30, 2022; set June 1, 2021 as the date for the
 Public Hearing.

 Written documentation previously provided to the Building Authority included
 information as follows:

        This resolution adopts the tentative budget for the Building Authority of West
        Valley City for the 2021-2022 fiscal year and sets a date for public comment at a
        hearing on June 1, 2021.

        The Building Authority will adopt a tentative budget that will be made available
        for public inspection during regular office hours in the City’s Recorder’s office and
        gave notice of a hearing to receive public comment, before the final adoption of
        this tentative budget for FY 2021-2022.

 Upon inquiry by Chairman Fitisemanu there were no further questions from members of
 the Authority, and he called for a motion.

        Mr. Nordfelt moved to approve Resolution 21-01.

        Ms. Lang seconded the motion.

        A roll call vote was taken:

        Ms. Lang                      Yes
        Mr. Buhler                    Yes
        Mr. Huynh                     Absent
        Mr. Bigelow                   Yes
        Mr. Christensen               Yes
        Mr. Nordfelt                  Yes
        Chairman Fitisemanu           Yes

        Unanimous.

 MOTION TO ADJOURN
 Upon motion by Ms. Lang all voted in favor to adjourn.
