HB462 (2022) seeks to maximize development potential, and
thereby increase the availability and affordability of housing,
around transit stations -- almost all of which are within Wasatch
Choice "Centers." Cities will develop Station Area Plans within
1/2 mile of rail transit stations and 1/4 mile of BRT stations with
dedicated lanes, as listed below.
