WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 23, 2022

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Iverson at 3:00 p.m. on August 23, 2022, in the Washington County
Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those
in attendance were: Chairman Victor Iverson, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner
Adam Snow, Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

Others in attendance included: Bryan Thiriot, Five County, Five County Economics
Representative; Community Development Director Scott Messel; Public Works Director
Ron Whitehead; County Attorney Eric Clarke; and Tourism Director Kevin Lewis.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

DISCUSSION _AND_ PERIODIC REVIEW __OF COUNTY RESOURCE
MANAGEMENT PLAN (RMP)

The sections that were reviewed included: Economics, Land Access, Land Use, Law
Enforcement, Noxious Weeds, Recreation & Tourism, Pipelines & Infrastructure, Utility
Corridors, Energy Resources, Mining & Minerals Resources.

Each section was reviewed line by line and county specific revisions were suggested.

Deputy Attorney Devin Snow explained that Pipelines & Infrastructure; Utility Corridors,
and Energy Resources are new sections that the State RMP has that are being added in the
County’s plan as well.

Updates to the RMP will be brought before the Planning Commission for review and then
to the County Commission for final approval.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
4:15 p.m.
