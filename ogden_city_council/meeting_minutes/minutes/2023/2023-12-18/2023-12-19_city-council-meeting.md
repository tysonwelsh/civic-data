Proposed Resolution 2023-24 approving modifications to Council Rules and
Norms

A memo from Council staff came before the Council to consider a resolution approving amendments to the Council
Rules and Norms. The memo stated the proposed revisions include moving the election of Council Leadership from the first week
in January to the first week in February and providing that intergovernmental appointments will occur in December instead of
January. The proposed revisions also remove a reference to a leadership interest form that should have been removed with the
August 2023 amendments, and make incidental corrections to grammar, spelling, and punctuation.

Chair Choberka introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2023-24, entitled:

“A resolution of the Ogden City Council approving modifications to Council Rules and Norms.”

Proposed Ordinance 2023-71 amending Section 8-1-4 of the Ogden
Municipal Code to eliminate the sunset provision for the Airport Advisory
Committee

A memo from the Community and Economic Development Department regarding proposed amendments to the Airport
Advisory Committee ordinance came before the Council for consideration. The memo stated the proposed amendment is to
remove the sunset provision for the Committee.

Community and Economic Development Deputy Director Johnson summarized the memo and stated Administration is
requesting an amendment to Ogden Municipal Code § 8-1-4, Airport Advisory Committee, to remove the sunset provision. Under
the current ordinance, last amended in 2018, the sunset date was set for June 30, 2023. The Administration believes it is in the best
interest of the City and airport operations to have the Advisory Committee operate perpetually. Removing the sunset provision | |
will allow the Committee to be reconstituted and members appointed or reappointed as soon as possible. Mr. Johnson
acknowledged the discussion of the Council during the December 12 work session meeting and indicated Administration will be
presenting another ordinance to the Council to handle additional amendments requested by the Council.
