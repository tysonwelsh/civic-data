Reports from Municipal Service Providers

Unified Fire Authority

                  Greg Schulz stated the Unified Fire Authority (UFA) would like the Council to set
the fire restriction map for 2019. The map is online, and it is the same map the Council approved
last year. If the Council wants to give the UFA direction, it can send a letter stating it accepts the
map for 2019.

               Council Member Hull stated changes to the map will need to be made as homes
are built.

               Mr. Schulz stated changes will eventually need to be made.

                Paul Ashton stated if the Council planned to change the map, he suggested
bringing it back another day and presenting it.

               Council Member Prokopis stated the Council has reserved the right to reopen
the issue in June if it needed to.

                                  −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department

                Council Member Hull asked if the Unified Police Department (UPD) paid the
traffic guards at the schools or if the schools paid for them.

                Chief Jason Ackerman stated they are funded through the UPD. Currently, the
UPD is reorganizing how it manages crossing guards from having one coordinator to a supervisor
for each geographical area. Right now, the UPD is down crossing guards. It is in the process of
recruiting crossing guards now, but is having a hard time filling those positions.

               Council Member Hull stated Entheos does not have crossing guards. She asked
if that was because it was a charter school.

               Chief Ackerman stated there are crossing guards at Entheos, but the number of
crossing guards are down. The UPD will actually be conducting a traffic enforcement sting in that
area in the next week or so because of the high speeds. It will be doing a sting on 8400 West
also. Those will be funded with a state Highway Safety Grant.

               Council Member Hull asked for a report on the Youth Academy.

Resolution 19-04-01

               Paul Ashton reviewed Resolution 19-04-01 authorizing two easement
agreements between the Magna Metro Township and Kennecott Utah Copper LLC. He thought
if Kennecott was willing to give the Metro Township the easements so it could put signal lights on
roads, the Council should accept them.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-04-01                                                        Date: April 9, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       TWO EASEMENT AGREEMENTS BETWEEN MAGNA AND KENNECOTT UTAH
       COPPER LLC

                                           RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-
      2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Kennecott Utah Copper LLC (“Kennecott”) is a Limited-Liability Company with certain
      real property located in Magna.

   D. To facilitate the construction of 2550 South, and to provide for signalization of a
      proposed intersection to be located at 2550 South and 7200 West, the easements
      identified in on the attached map (Exhibit “C”) were negotiated from Kennecott.

   E. The Easement Agreements (“Agreements”), hereinafter identified as Exhibits “A” and
      “B,” provide the necessary legal descriptions of the easements being offered to Magna
      to install the intersection signalization needed to control traffic and improve safety.

   F. The agreements will provide for a safer traffic flow through the 7200 West Corridor, and
      provide better ingress and egress from the properties north of the Ritter Canal.

                                         RESOLUTION

Resolution 19-04-02

                 Paul Ashton reviewed the following resolution approving and adopting a Capital
Asset Policy for Magna, which is required by the Internal Revenue Service. The capital plan he
is looking at is the same plan accepted by the other metro townships and the Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District, which is basically a capitalization of $5,000.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-04-02                                                       Date: April 9, 2019

        A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
        AND ADOPTING AN ASSET CAPITALIZATION POLICY FOR MAGNA

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-
      2a-401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Pursuant to Federal financial regulations for local governments as contained in GASB
      53, Magna is required to adopt an Asset Capitalization Policy (the “Policy”) as a part of
      its accounting for certain capital assets.


                                               5
DATE           TUESDAY                                 APRIL                              9, 2019

   D. The Government Finance Officers’ Association (“GFOA”) provides templates for local
      governments to consider when establishing their Asset Capitalization Policy.

   E. The attached policy, hereinafter identified as Exhibit “A,” is such a template and was
      recommended by the MSD Financial Officer for consideration and possible approval.

   F. When establishing an Asset Capitalization Policy, Magna must set a financial asset
      value from which point certain assets may be considered for capitalization under the
      policy.

   G. Pursuant to the recommendations of the MSD Financial Officer, it is recommended
      Magna set that value in their Policy at FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS ($5,000.00).

                                         RESOLUTION

       THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby accepts and approves the attached
Asset Capitalization Policy for Magna Exhibit “A,” and establishes the base capital asset value at
FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS ($5,000.00).

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this 9th day of APRIL,
2019.
