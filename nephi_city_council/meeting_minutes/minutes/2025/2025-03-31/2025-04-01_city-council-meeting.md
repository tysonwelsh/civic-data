Mayor Justin D. Seely opened the meeting and welcomed everyone present.

PRAYER: Councilor Shari Cowan

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Utah State Representative Troy Shelley

 Representative Troy Shelley spoke briefly about the latest Legislative Session. He wanted the council to
know that he is not too busy to listen to them and their concerns. He was elected to represent the
people. He explained that it is helpful to know how a bill will impact them, so he has a reason to vote or
not vote on a bill.
 Mayor Seely commended Representative Shelley for the strong role and voice that he brings to
represent class five cities and below.
 Councilor Cowan also acknowledged the work Representative Shelley is doing and his willingness to
stand up for what he believes in and what we want for our community.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

Councilor Travis Worwood moved to approve the consent agenda, which included approving the
minutes for the regular council meeting on 3-18-25, list of claims on the warrant register dated 3-31-25
and burial rights for Tyrone and Nicole Douglas in Nephi City Cemetery and Margaret LaRue Nielson in
Vine Bluff Cemetery. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT
NO IMPACT BUSINESS DISCUSSION

Following denial of a home occupation business license due to violation of the city code and it
qualifying as a retail business which is currently not allowed in a residential area, City
Administrator Atkinson suggested it may be worth a discussion with the planning commission to
review our ‘no impact’ definition to see if there is room for more flexibility than we have right
now. The council agreed that it was worth sending it to the planning commission for review.

RECOMMENDATONS FROM AIRPORT ADVISORY COMMITTEE

Prior to the city council meeting, the Airport Advisory Committee met to discuss two different
airport projects: Engineering services and Master Plan updates. The discussion involved who to
hire to assist with these projects. Seth Atkinson reported on the recommendation of the
advisory committee to go with Wolpert and provided background on Wolpert’s previous work
with the airport, and the competitive selection process. Mayor Seely and Councilor Travis
Worwood supported the recommendation based on Wolpert’s proven track record and
familiarity with the airport. Councilor Skip Worwood made the motion to approve
recommendations from the advisory committee. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the
motion. The motion passed unanimously.

ADMINISTATIVE UPDATES

   •   Mr. Atkinson reported on a highway standoff on the freeway that involved our police
       officers. He commented on the great job they did apprehending the perpetrator safely.
   •   Mr. Atkinson also acknowledged the quick response and great work our electric crew did
       during the night last night and early this morning to restore and fix storm-related power
       outages.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES

   •   Mayor Seely reported on his opportunity to attend Vietnam Vet Day. He said it was a
       very impactful and patriotic experience. He mentioned his concerns about who will be
       the next generation to fill the gap as the members of American Legion Post ages. He
       expressed his hope that people who serve will want to be involved in that.

WORK SESSION 8:20 PM

       1. Zero Fatalities contacted Chief Morgan and told him they were a reward recipient for
          utilizing their program to help the community not have any fatalities with golf carts
          and 4-wheelers. Chief Morgan invited the mayor and council to attend the awards
          ceremony on April 28 at noon in the Mountain America Expo center in Sandy. Chief
          Morgan reported that Nephi City has been using the Zero Fatalities program to help
          enforce city laws concerning golf carts and 4-wheelers. As part of this funding we
          receive, Nephi City Police officers work a mandatory 3 hours of overtime to help with
          enforcement. Nephi City is going to continue using the program again this year and
          Zero Fatalities will provide more print materials to use for the campaign this summer.

       2. Seth Atkinson discussed complaints about a loud aerobatics plane at the airport. He
          stated that the plane needs an aerobatics box over the airport for safety reasons and
          to warn other pilots. He suggested talking with the pilot and working out a
          compromise for usage, and he will be meeting with the FAA to discuss what types of
          restrictions can be implemented while still maintaining grant agreement with the
          federal government for the airport. Mr. Atkinson proposed bringing an air show to
          the airport to help engender community support.

       3. Lisa Brough mentioned someone calling her about wanting to do a boutique in the
          park. She stated the council had already decided that the Farmer’s Market could use
          food trucks at the park but wondered what to tell the new inquiry. Mayor Seely
          suggested putting it on the work session agenda to discuss next week.


       4. Councilor Skip Worwood moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilor Travis Worwood
          seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at
          8:45 p.m.
