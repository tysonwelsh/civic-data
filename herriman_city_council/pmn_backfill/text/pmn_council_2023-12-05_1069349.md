                             SPECIAL BOARD OF CANVASSERS
                                       MINUTES

                                     Tuesday, December 5, 2023
                                     Approved January 10, 2024
The following are the minutes of the City Council Meeting of the Herriman City Council. The meeting was
held on Tuesday, December 5, 2023 at 5:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West
Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law, was posted in
City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Lorin Palmer

Councilmembers Present: Jared Henderson, Teddy Hodges, Sherrie Ohrn, Steven Shields

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Assistant City Manager Wendy Thomas, City Recorder
Jackie Nostrom, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran, Communications Specialist
Destiny Skinner.

5:00 PM - SPECIAL MEETING:
   1. CALL TO ORDER
Mayor Lorin Palmer called the meeting to order at 5:00 p.m.

    2. RESULTS OF GENERAL ELECTION
          2.1. Discussion and consideration of a resolution finding and promulgating the results of the
                2023 Municipal General Election – Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
City Recorder Jackie Nostrom offered a brief overview of the General Election held November 21, 2023 and
relayed the official results as follows: In City Council District #1 54.42-percent of the votes were in favor
of Jared Henderson. In City Council District #4, 65.61-percent of the votes cast were in favor of Steven
Shields. Recorder Nostrom declared the Council-Elect for District #1 would be Jared Henderson, and
Council-Elect for District #3 would be Steven Shields.

Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn moved to approve Resolution No. R52-2023 certifying the canvass of the
2023 Herriman City Municipal General Election. Councilmember Steve Shields seconded the motion.
