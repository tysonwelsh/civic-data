Attachment: Preview and Week One of the 2023 General Session of the Utah State Legislature
c.
Discussion regarding National Association of Counties (NACo) Public Lands request; Janna Young (4:14 P.M.)
Janna Young, Interim Manager, provided information on the National Association of Counties (NACo) Public Lands request for funding contributions from public lands counties to establish a National Center for Public Lands Counties.
Council Members asked questions and provided additional comments. Ms. Young responded.
Attachment: Consideration of Contribution to NACo’s Public Lands Center

Update on the SR-32 trail project; Steve Dennis (4:29 P.M.)
Steve Dennis, Engineer, and John Angell, Public Works Director, provided an update on the SR-32 trail project.
Council Members asked clarifying questions. Mr. Dennis and Mr. Angell responded.
Attachment: SR-32 Trail Project
Convene as the Governing Board of North Summit Fire Service District (4:57 P.M.)
Chris Robinson made a motion to convene as the Governing Board of North Summit Fire Service District Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
a.
Discussion and approval of Promissory Note between North Summit Fire Service District and Summit County; Ben Nielson and Corrie Forsling (4:57)
Ben Nielson, North Summit Fire Chief, and Corrie Forsling, Treasure, presented to the Council information regarding a promissory note between NSFSD and Summit County.
Council Members asked clarifying questions. Chief Nielson and Treasurer Forsling responded.
Tonja Hanson made a motion to approve the Promissory Note between North Summit Fire Service District and Summit County as amended with the fee not to exceed $1,000.00. Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Promissory Note
Attachment: NSFSD Promissory Note executed

Tonja Hanson made a motion to dismiss as the Governing Board of North Summit Fire and reconvene as the Summit County Council Chris Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Consideration of Approval (5:06 P.M.)
a.
Appoint member to serve on Summit County Service Area No. 5 (5:07 P.M.)
Chris Robinson made a motion to appoint Susan Neilsen to serve the unexpired term of Ed Wyckoff on the Summit County Service Area No. 5 board. Term of service to expire December 31, 2024 Tonja Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Appointment Service Area 5

Appoint member to serve on the Park City Fire Service District (5:09 P.M.)
Tonja Hanson made a motion to appoint Steve Briley to serve on the Park City Fire Service District Administrative Control Board. Term of service to expire December 31, 2027 Chris Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Appointment Park City Fire

Appoint member to serve on the Mountain Regional Water Special Service District (5:10 P.M.)

Tonja Hanson made a motion to appoint Cory Shorkey to serve on the Mountain Regional Water Special Service District Administrative Control Board. Term of Service to expire December 31, 2026 Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Attachment: Appointment MRWSSD
Council Member Chris Robinson left the meeting at 5:12 P.M.
d.
Discussion and recommendations to the Weber, Bear, and Great Salt Lake Local Watershed Councils; Jess Kirby (5:12 P.M.)
Jess Kirby, County Lands & Natural Resources Manager, presented recommendations to the Council regarding the Weber, Bear, and Great Salt Lake Local Watershed Councils. Council Members asked clarifying questions. Manager Kirby responded.
Tonja Hanson made a motion to recommend to the Utah Watershed Council the list of potential members, as contained in the packet, to the Weber, Bear, and Great Salt Lake Local Watershed Councils Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Chris Robinson.
Attachment: Watershed Councils
5 minute break
e.
Discussion and approval of a Grant Agreement between Summit County and Summit Land Conservancy for the purchase of a conservation easement on Parcel No. AFS-AG; Jess Kirby (5:23 P.M.)
Jess Kirby, County Lands & Natural Resources Manager, and Kate Sattelmeier, Summit Lands Conservancy, presented a Grant Agreement between Summit County and Summit Land Conservancy for the purchase of a conservation easement on Parcel No. AFS-AG. Council Members asked questions and provided comments. Ms. Sattelmeier and Ms. Kirby responded.
Tonja Hanson made a motion to approve the Grant Agreement between Summit County and Summit Land Conservancy for the purchase of a conservation easement on Parcel No. AFS-AG as outlined in the packet. Canice Harte seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Absent: Chris Robinson.
Attachment: Grant Agreement
Attachment: Andrus Property Conservation Easement Grant Agreement executed
