Please Note
All plot purchases must be performed by a Bingham Cemetery Board member. All
transactions are nonrefundable. A receipt will be issued with plot purchase details once
payment has been received in full and all accompanying documents have been completed
and returned to the Bingham Cemetery Board.

The Bingham Cemetery Board shall be notified before any changes are made to grave sites,
plots, and monuments. Prior approval is required before a grave site or plot is altered in any
way or a monument is placed at any site within the Bingham Cemetery. Be sure to ask about
the approved monument for the plot you wish to purchase.
