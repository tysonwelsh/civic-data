Re: Shipp Barn (Stokes) – Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement for approximately 2.52 acres located on the south side of Stokes Ave, at approximately 570 East Stokes Ave. The property is currently zoned RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Development Agreement be approved to allow for the development of the property for a future residential subdivision. The findings are as follows: For positive recommendation:
1.  The Development Agreement complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200.
2.  The proposed deviations appear to be reasonable and potential detrimental effects are off-set by the proposal to preserve the existing home and retain farm land as prescribed in the agreement.
For negative recommendation:
1.  The proposed deviations do not appear to be reasonable and potential detrimental effects are not off-set by the proposal to preserve the existing home and retain farm land. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 2/5/2021 Cover Memo
