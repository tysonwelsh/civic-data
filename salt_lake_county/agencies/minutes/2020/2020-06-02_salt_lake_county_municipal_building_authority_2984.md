4.1 Resolution - Authorizing Execution of an Easement to Rocky 
Mountain Power for the Daybreak Library
20-0523
Attachments: RMP Easement Resolution
RMP Easement_5.27.2020
Mr. Craig Wangsgard , Deputy District Attorney, stated this resolution 
authorizes the Municipal Building Authority of Salt Lake County to grant a 
Right of Way Easement and Release to Rocky Mountain Power allowing it to 
deliver electrical power to the Daybreak Library Project. 
RESOLUTION NO. 43
A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE MUNICIPAL 
BUILDING AUTHORITY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY, UTAH 
APPROVING EXECUTION AND GRANT OF A RIGHT OF WAY 
EASEMENT AND RELEASE TO ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER AT 
THE DAYBREAK LIBRARY PROJECT; AND RELATED MATTERS
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT:
WHEREAS, the Municipal Building Authority of Salt Lake County, Utah 
(“MBA”), pursuant to the second amendment to the Master Lease 
Agreement with Salt Lake County (“County”), has undertaken for and on 
behalf of County the acquisition, construction and improvement of certain 
projects, including the Daybreak Library Project; and

WHEREAS, the MBA wishes Rocky Mountain Power (“RMP”) deliver 
electrical power to the Daybreak Library Project; and
WHEREAS, RMP requires execution of a Right of Way Easement in order 
to connect to its electrical power system; and
WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17B-1-301(m), as amended, gives authority 
to the Governing Board of the MBA to enter this Right of Way Easement 
and Release on behalf of the MBA, and
WHEREAS, the Governing Board finds this Right of Way Easement and 
Release to be for the benefit of the MBA.
NOW, THEREFORE, the Governing Board of the Municipal Building 
Authority of Salt Lake County, Utah, resolves as follows:
1. That the Right of Way Easement and Release between the MBA and the 
RMP, is approved, in substantially the form attached as Attachment A, and 
that the President or Vice President is authorized to execute the same in 
accordance with the Bylaws.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 2nd day of June, 2020.

4.2 Resolution - Authorizing execution of a Commercial Sewer 
Connection Agreement and an Impact Fee Agreement 
between the MBA and the South Valley Sewer District for the 
Daybreak Library
20-0524
Attachments: MBA_Sewer Connection Agreements Resolution
AATF_Commerical Sewer_MBA_Daybreak
AATF_MBA_Daybreak_Impact Fee Agreement_edited
Mr. Craig Wangsgard , Deputy District Attorney, stated this resolution 
authorizes the Municipal Building Authority of Salt Lake County to enter into 
a Commercial Sewer Agreement and an Impact Fee Agreement with South 
Valley Sewer District for the connection of sewer improvements at the 
Daybreak Library Project. 
RESOLUTION NO. 44
A RESOLUTION OF THE GOVERNING BOARD OF THE MUNICIPAL 
BUILDING AUTHORITY OF SALT LAKE COUNTY, UTAH 
APPROVING A COMMERCIAL SEWER CONNECTION AGREEMENT 
AND AN IMPACT FEE AGREEMENT WITH SOUTH VALLEY SEWER 
DISTRICT PROVIDING FOR THE CONNECTION OF SEWER 
IMPROVEMENTS AT THE DAYBREAK LIBRARY PROJECT TO THE 
DISTRICT’S SEWER SYSTEM AND THE PAYMENT OF IMPACT 
FEES; AND RELATED MATTERS
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT:

WHEREAS, the Municipal Building Authority of Salt Lake County, Utah 
(“MBA”), pursuant to the Second Amendment to the Master Lease 
Agreement with Salt Lake County (“County”), has undertaken for and on 
behalf of County the acquisition, construction and improvement of certain 
projects, including the Daybreak Library Project; and
WHEREAS, the MBA wishes to install a sewer line or sewer lines, laterals, 
manholes and related structures and facilities, as part of the Daybreak Library 
Project, to be connected to the South Valley Sewer District ’s (“District”) 
sewer system in order to provide for collections, transmission, treatment, and 
disposal of sewage from the Daybreak Library Project; and
WHEREAS, the District requires the execution of a Commercial Sewer 
Connection Agreement and an Impact Fee Agreement in order to connect its 
sewer system; and
WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 17B-1-301(k), as amended, gives authority 
to the Governing Board of the MBA to enter into contracts on behalf of the 
MBA; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Board finds these agreements to be for the 
benefit of the MBA.
NOW, THEREFORE, the Governing Board of the Municipal Building 
Authority of Salt Lake County, Utah, resolves as follows:
1. That the Commercial Sewer Connection Agreement between the MBA 
and the District, is approved, in substantially the form attached hereto as 
Attachment A, and that the MBA is authorized to execute the same in 
accordance with the Bylaws.
2. That the Impact Fee Agreement between the MBA and the District, is 
approved, in substantially the form attached hereto as Attachment B, and that 
the MBA is authorized to execute the same in accordance with the Bylaws.
APPROVED and ADOPTED this 2nd day of June, 2020.
