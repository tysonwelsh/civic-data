                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 26-36

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A REIMBURSEMENT
       AGREEMENT WITH FARMHOUSE STORAGE, LLC FOR PUBLIC
                 IMPROVEMENTS ALONG 4000 WEST

       WHEREAS, Farmhouse Storage, LLC ("Developer") is developing property within
Riverton City and has entered into a Development Agreement with the City related to such
development; and

       WHEREAS, as part of the development, the Developer is required to design and
construct certain public improvements along 4000 West that will provide benefit to the public
and surrounding properties; and

       WHEREAS, the City has determined that the public improvements are eligible for
reimbursement in accordance with City policies and as contemplated by the Development
Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Reimbursement Agreement establishes the terms under which the
Developer will design and construct the improvements and the City will reimburse the Developer
for actual costs incurred for the design and installation of the improvements after completion and
acceptance by the City; and

        WHEREAS, reimbursement may be made through the collection of impact fees from
benefitted service areas, the waiver of applicable impact fees, or other legally available City
funds; and

    WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds that entering into the Reimbursement
Agreement is in the best interest of the City and will facilitate the construction of needed public
infrastructure while providing a mechanism for reimbursement of eligible public improvement
costs.

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

   1. The City Council hereby approves the Reimbursement Agreement between Riverton City
      and Farmhouse Storage, LLC for the design and construction of public improvements
      along 4000 West, attached hereto as Exhibit "A."

   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute the Reimbursement Agreement and any non-
      substantive modifications necessary for execution, subject to approval as to form by the
      City Attorney.

   3. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon adoption.
