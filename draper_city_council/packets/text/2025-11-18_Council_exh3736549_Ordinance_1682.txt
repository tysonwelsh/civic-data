                               ORDINANCE NO. 1682



  AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLES 7 AND 10 OF
  THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING THE REQUIREMENTS FOR FIRE
    SPRINKLERS UNDER THE DRAPER CITY FIRE AND CONSTRUCTION CODES.



      WHEREAS, Titles 7 and 10 of the Draper City Municipal Code establish
procedures for the construction and inspection of residential buildings under the
International Residential Code; and

      WHEREAS, Utah Code 15A-5-203 sets forth conditions under which a
municipality may require fire sprinklers under its own building inspection
standards; and

       WHEREAS, Conditions that may require fire sprinklers for a residential
building are more common in Draper City; and

      WHEREAS, the proposed changes to the Draper City Municipal Code create a
healthier and safer city for all the residents of Draper; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text amendments and
finds good cause to revise the terms and provisions of Titles 7 and 10.

      NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings to amend Titles 7 and 10 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) The
proposed amendments comply with the Utah Code Annotated. 2) The proposed
amendments serve the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the
citizens of Draper.

      Section 2. Revision. Titles 7 and 10 of the Draper City Municipal Code are
hereby amended to read as shown in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated
herein by reference.
      Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to
correct any punctuation, spelling, formatting, clerical, or de minimis errors prior to
submitting the Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect
any other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.

      Section 5. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to
the date of final passage.

7-10-020: FIRE CODE:

A. The most recent version of the International Fire Code published by the
International Conference of Building Officials Code Council and as adopted by the
State of Utah, including all appendices thereto, is hereby adopted as the Fire Code
of Draper City. All appendices contained within the International Fire Code,
excluding Appendix A, are also hereby adopted as the Fire Code of Draper City.

B. The most recent version of the Utah Wildland Urban Interface Code, issued by
the International Code Council and adopted by the State of Utah, with the
alternatives or amendment approved by the Utah Division of Forestry, is hereby
adopted as the Wildland Urban Interface Code of Draper City.

C. Subject to the provisions of subsection D of this section, automatic fire sprinkler
systems are required for structures built under the provisions of the International
Residential Code (IRC) under any of the following conditions:

   1. The structure:

          a. is located in an urban-wildland interface area as provided in the Utah
             Wildland Urban Interface Code adopted as a construction code under
             the State Construction Code; and

          b. does not meet the requirements described in Utah Code, Subsection
             65A-8-208(4)(f) and Utah Administrative Code R652-122-1300,
             Minimum Standards for County Wildland Fire Ordinance;

   2. The structure is in an area where a public water distribution system with fire
      hydrants does not exist as required in Utah Administrative Code, R309-550-5,
      Water Main Design;

   3. The only fire apparatus access road has a grade of greater than 10% for
      more than 500 continual feet;

   4. The total floor area of all floor levels within the exterior walls of the dwelling
      unit exceeds 10,000 square feet; or

   5. The total floor area of all floor levels within the exterior walls of the dwelling
      unit is double the average of the total floor area of all floor levels of
      unsprinkled homes in the subdivision that are no larger than 10,000 square
      feet.
D. A single family dwelling described in subsection C of this section does not
require a fire sprinkler system if the dwelling:

   1. is located outside of the wildland-urban interface;

   2. is built in a one-lot subdivision; and

3.    has 50 feet of defensible space on all sides that limits the propensity of fire
spreading from the dwelling to another property.

10-2-030: FIRE CODE:

A. For the purpose of prescribing regulations governing conditions hazardous to
life and property from fire and explosion, the most recent edition adopted by the
state of the international fire code is hereby adopted, including the referenced
standards and appendices B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, and J but not appendix A thereof,
with such amendments as are set forth in this chapter.

B. Draper City hereby adopts by reference and incorporates herein the statewide
amendments and additions to the international fire code, adopted by the state
legislature, a copy of which is in section 10-2-090, exhibit A of this chapter and by
this reference made a part hereof. (Ord. 1082, 12-17-2013)
