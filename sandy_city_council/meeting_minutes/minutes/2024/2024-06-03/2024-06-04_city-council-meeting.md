City Council Meeting Minutes June 4, 2024
2. 24-204 Public Utilities, Parks and Recreation, and Economic Development
Departments presenting an overview of their FY 2024-25 tentative
department budgets.
Attachments: June 4 Department Budget Presentations
Tom Ward, Public Utilities, provided an overview of the FY25 Tentative Budget for the
Public Utilities Department. Council questions and comments followed. Staff responded
to questions regarding water rights in Sandy City wells, reduction in the storm water fund
balance, and the increased costs associated with operating the wells.
Dan Medina, Parks and Recreation Director provided an overview of the FY25 Tentative
Budget for the Parks and Recreation Department. Council questions and comments
followed. Staff responded to Council questions on the RAISE Trails grant and purpose,
additional grant funding opportunities for parks, trails connectivity with neighboring cities,
capital projects that were not funded, matching funds for the RAISE grants, and shade
structures for playground areas.
Kasey Dunlavy Economic Development Agency, provided an overview of the FY25
Tentative Budget for the Economic Development Department and the Redevelopment
Agency. There were no additional questions.

Budget. This item includes:
Resolutions 24-22C, 24-23C, and 24-24C of Sandy City, adopting tax
rates for Sandy City and the Alta Canyon Recreation District for the fiscal
year commencing July 1, 2024 and ending June 30,2025; also establishing
certain fees and charges in Sandy City for said fiscal year; also authorizing
city pick up of certain public safety employee retirement contributions.
Attachments: Signed Resolution 24-22C URS CITY PICK UP FY25
Resolution 24-22C URS CITY PICK UP_FY25

Resolution 24-24C Final Budget Adoption - Alta Canyon

Public Hearing: Brian Kelley presented Resolutions 24-22C, 24-23C, and 24-24C related
to the adoption of the final FY25 budget.

No public comment was provided.
Mr. Kelley provided a detailed explanation of each of the budget resolutions under
consideration.

Program and Law Judge in FY25 at $25,000 in Department 1900. Council questions,
comments, and feedback followed. Discussion included the types of cases that an ALJ

City Council Meeting Minutes June 4, 2024

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Executive Director, updated the Council on upcoming agenda items. The
Public Hearing for the budget will continue.

Council Member Business
Council Member Marci Houseman provided congratulations to schools and students
across Sandy on their graduation.
Council Member Stroud thanked the staff for the event held at Sego Lily Gardens.
Council Member Sharkey provided an update on the convention facilities advisory board
and the 0.5% sales tax that may be levied in Salt Lake City to remodel the Delta Center.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Zoltanski spoke about the upcoming barbecue for the Historic Sandy community.
She shared details on new senior housing in Sandy, Salt Lake programming offered to
county youth at no cost, the recent RSL game, and the Mayor's annual town hall
meeting. She congratulated Janice Parker, coordinator of the Crossing Guard program,
on her retirement and for her many years of service to Sandy City.

CAO Report
No report.

Adjournment
Following standing reports, the Council convened a closed session at 7:58 pm. The
Council meeting adjourned immediately following the closed session.
