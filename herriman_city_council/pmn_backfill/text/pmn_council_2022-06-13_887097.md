                                  SPECIAL CITY COUNCIL
                                    MEETING MINUTES
                                       Monday June 13, 2022
                                      Approved July 13, 2022

The following are the minutes of the special City Council meeting of the Herriman City Council. The
meeting was held on Monday, June 13, 2022, at 4:00 p.m. in the Herriman City Hall Council Chambers,
5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah. Adequate notice of this meeting, as required by law,
was posted in the City Hall, on the City’s website, and delivered to members of the Council, media, and
interested citizens.

Presiding: Mayor Lorin Palmer

Councilmembers Present: Jared Henderson (participated electronically), Teddy Hodges, and Steve
Shields.

Staff Present: City Manager Nathan Cherpeski, Police Chief Troy Carr, City Recorder Jackie Nostrom,
Finance Director Alan Rae, Finance Director Kyle Maurer, City Attorney Todd Sheeran, Communications
Manager Jonathan LaFollette, and Deputy Director of Parks, Recreation and Events Anthony Teuscher.

Councilmembers Present: Sherrie Ohrn

4:00 PM (or as soon as possible thereafter):
   1. Call to Order
Mayor Palmer called the meeting to order at 4:05 p.m.

    2. Discussion and Action Items
           2.1. Discussion and consideration of a contract between Herriman City and Flying with
                Foy regarding the Summer Production of Cinderella – Mayor Lorin Palmer
City Manager Nathan Cherpeski offered a brief synopsis of the May 27, 2022, City Council meeting where
it had been debated if this type of contract should come back to the City Council for consideration.
During the discussion, staff committed to bring the contract back to the Council. It was noted the
company did not have adequate personnel to accommodate the initial contract; however, last week it
appeared they would be appropriately staffed and looked to execute an agreement for services.

City Attorney Todd Sheeran and City Manager Nathan Cherpeski had been working with the vendor on a
contract and looked to the Council for the authority to execute an agreement for the current Arts Council
production. City Manager Cherpeski explained this contract would be paid out of two separate fiscal
budget years due to the date the deposit would need to be rendered versus when the actual contract
would be executed, and final payment be submitted. It was budgeted in the current 2021-2022 fiscal year
budget; however, a budget amendment may be necessary to correctly record when payments had been
conducted.

Mayor Palmer highlighted the changes and more thorough budget vetting process which had been put in
place. City Manager Cherpeski interjected the agreement was written directly to the Arts Council and not
the City and wanted to ensure errors were rectified prior to execution and the City was protected.
Councilmember Teddy Hodges referenced the detailed budget and expectation for the committees which
was instrumental in clearing up the miscommunication on procurement of large ticket items.

Councilmember Steve Shields explained his involvement with the Arts Council discussions and discovered
there had not been enough money allocated to cover expenditures. In an effort to increase transparency,
he wanted to ensure proposed event expenditures were accurately disclosed and vetted through the
budget process. He felt this would create a better process and accountability. Finance Director Alan Rae
interjected the Arts Council was the only committee which had a designated budget and noted their
budget had been scrutinized in the past by the Parks Department prior to coming to the Council for
consideration.

Based on a question posed by Councilmember Hodges, Finance Director Rae explained committee funding
come out of a generic fund balance budget line and do not have a dedicated budget for their specific
purpose.

Councilmember Henderson agreed with Director Rae and confirmed the budget had been previously
scrutinized which was the reasoning behind pushing forward with the non-profit organization. He looked
to have a better process moving forward and noted this would be a welcomed opportunity to raise funds
for the summer production. Councilmember Shields agreed this would provide the ability to do additional
events which would not be contingent on taxpayer funds.

Councilmember Shields moved to authorize the City Manager to finalize and execute a contract with
Flying with Foy for the summer production of Cinderella. Councilmember Hodges seconded the motion.

The vote was recorded as follows:
Councilmember Jared Henderson Aye
Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn        Absent
Councilmember Steven Shields      Aye
Councilmember Teddy Hodges        Aye
Mayor Lorin Palmer                Aye

The motion passed unanimously with Councilmember Sherrie Ohrn being absent.
