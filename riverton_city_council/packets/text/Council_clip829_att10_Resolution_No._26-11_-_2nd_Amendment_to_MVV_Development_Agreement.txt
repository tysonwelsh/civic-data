                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-11

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE SECOND
AMENDMENT TO THE MOUNTAIN VIEW PLACE AT RIVERTON DEVELOPMENT
                         AGREEMENT

      WHEREAS, in 2015, Riverton City approved the Development Agreement for Mountain
View Place at Riverton (Agreement); and

       WHEREAS, in consultation and cooperation with the Master Developer of that project,
CenterCal Properties LLC, Riverton City had prepared a Second Amendment to that Agreement
with the sole intent to increase the number of allowed residential units to 288; and

        WHEREAS, the Second Amendment as originally approved was not executed or
recorded, and subsequently the Master Developer and Riverton City have agreed the amendment
to the number of allowed residential units should be increased to 302; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that it is in the best interest
of the City to execute the Second Amendment to the Mountain View Place at Riverton
Development Agreement in compliance with all applicable State and Local laws, and consistent
with the terms and conditions of that Agreement to increase the allowed residential units to 302.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute the Second Amendment to the
          Mountain View Place at Riverton Development Agreement as shown in Exhibit “A”
          and subject to such insertions as are required for execution.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 3rd day of
