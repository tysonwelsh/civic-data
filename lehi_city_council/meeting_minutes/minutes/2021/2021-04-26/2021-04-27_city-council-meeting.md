Pre-Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Koivisto was attending virtually. Councilor Southwick gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Lehi Archery Range Discussion
Scott MacIntosh and Robert Williamson stated that they would like to see an archery range in the
City. Mayor Johnson stated that it’s not lawful to use archery in the city limits, but they can
make exceptions when needed. Mr. MacIntosh stated that it’s a great youth activity and it’s safer
than someone using it on private property. He stated that the cost would be minimal, and it could
be located at Willow Park. Steve Marchbanks, Parks Manager, stated that he will look into it.

2.5 Legislative Update – Chris Bleak
This item was moved to the next meeting.

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
Jason Walker updated the Council on the Utah League of Cities and Towns Conference.

5. Mayor and Council Reports
The Mayor and Council reported on their assigned boards and commissions.

6. Consideration of adjourning into a Closed Executive Session to hold a strategy session to
discuss the purchase, exchange, or lease of real property.

Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome, Roll Call, Pledge of Allegiance
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Condie led the Pledge.

2. 20 Minute Citizen Input
James Cornish stated that he had concerns with the Office building on Hunter’s Grove Lane that
was approved. He stated that they are now advertising for a reception center and believe this was
not their original proposal. He was concerned with the noise, parking and traffic.

Karen Cornish was also concerned. She said they weren’t notified when the developer modified
their site plan, so the neighborhood was not able to make comments.

Jason Booth also expressed concern for the noise, traffic and parking.

Kaylynn Osbourne stated that she had a concern with the language in the development code
regarding a code enforcement issue. Kim Struthers stated that they are aware of the issue and are
proceeding with due process.
