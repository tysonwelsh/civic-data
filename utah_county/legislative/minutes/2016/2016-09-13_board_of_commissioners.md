6.     APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO SIGN AN AGREEMENT WITH
       ENLIGHTEN COMMUNICATIONS TO PROVIDE ABSTINENCE EDUCATION
(Track A, 08:45)
Utah County Health Department Executive Director Ralph Clegg mentioned this is part of an ongoing contract with
the state, and introduced Jackie Larsen to expound upon the county’s relation to Nebo School District. Ms. Larsen
responded to Commissioners Ellertson and Graves’ questions regarding the $2,500 payment and why only two of
the five schools are included, explaining the two schools were chosen because they are especially supportive of the
abstinence program which she has been coordinating with them since 2011. Though the education has been
available by video since then, there are extra funds this year and having the speaker appear in person will impact as
many students as possible. Mr. Clegg confirmed the money is coming from the state specifically for this contract.

Jackie Larsen mentioned she has begun working with Alpine School District, but they have not approved this
speaker yet. Ralph Clegg added they are anticipating additional funding for Alpine School District, and Ms. Larsen
addressed the contract room and board details with Commissioners Ellertson and Graves. Commissioner Lee asked
why they must fly in an educator from out of state rather than use someone local, and Ms. Larsen explained this
person is the only one in the entire country performing this specific type of presentation, as abstinence can be a
controversial subject. Ms. Larsen and Commissioner Lee discussed the best use of funds for the approximate 800
students ranging from ninth grade to twelfth grade, as well as their parents.

(Track A, 15:15)
Utah County Deputy Attorney Carl Hollan pointed out the $2,500 stipend, and clarified that the hotel and meals
would be the responsibility of the contractor. Commissioner Graves expressed concern over the limited number of
schools included in the presentation, and suggested perhaps splitting up the day to invite more attendees. Jackie
Larsen responded it is a possibility. Commissioner Lee noted the presenter is already being paid by the county, but
may also be selling product for additional profit during the event.

           Commissioner Graves made the motion to approve the agreement with
           Enlighten Communications with changes as discussed in Regular Agenda Item
           No. 6. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Lee and carried with the
           following vote:
                                                                       AYE: Larry A. Ellertson
                                                                              Greg Graves
                                                                       NAY: William C. Lee

8.     APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO SIGN THE SERVICE LEVEL
       AGREEMENT WITH UTAH INTERACTIVE FOR ELECTRONIC ACCESS FUNCTIONS AND
       TRANSACTIONS
(Track A, 18:45)
Utah County Health Department Executive Director Ralph Clegg introduced Eric Edwards, who explained the
Health Department is in the process of updating their website and Utah Interactive is included on the state contract.
This is a service level agreement for Utah Interactive to serve as the portal manager. When Commissioner Graves
asked who will control the content and appearance of the website, Mr. Edwards clarified the county will provide the
information to be included to Utah Interactive. Mr. Edwards mentioned Utah Interactive is responsible for
www.utah.gov, and Commissioner Graves expressed skepticism about that site being user friendly; Commissioner
Graves stated the current County Health Department site looks better than Utah.gov in his opinion. Mr. Edwards
talked about the importance of having the site be easily accessible through mobile applications, and discussed the
budget with Commissioner Ellertson. Commissioner Lee summarized the purpose of the agreement, and Mr.
Edwards noted addendums can be created as necessary. Commissioner Graves also expressed skepticism that the
work would be completed by year’s end.

           Commissioner Graves made the motion to approve the service level agreement
           with Utah Interactive for electronic access functions and transactions as
           detailed in Regular Agenda Item No. 8. The motion was seconded by
           Commissioner Lee and carried with the following vote:
                                                                       AYE: Larry A. Ellertson
