                                       PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant, Travis Lamm, has applied for an expansion of a non-conforming structure. The subject
property includes an existing house with a non-complying front yard setback to the property line.
Current ordinance requires a front yard setback of 25 feet. The applicant has extended his roofline and
created a covered porch over the front door. This addition is part of the house that was already non-
conforming. The new setback from the covered porch to the property line is approximately 13 feet.

Since the house was built in 1916, it doesn’t meet the current ordinance requirement for the front yard
setback as stated above. Many of the homes in this immediate area were built in the 1900’s and also
have a front yard setback of less than 15 feet to the property line (please see attached photos).

Mr. Lamm was given a stop work order on June 2, 2021, for constructing this project without a building
permit. The applicant is asking for approval as per Magna Ordinance 19.88.070, to keep his newly added
porch cover as an expansion to a non-conforming structure.

                           PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
The MSD Planning Staff recommends that the Land Use Hearing Officer grant approval of this
application allowing for the expansion of a nonconforming structure at 2849 West 9200 South subject to
the following findings:
         1. The proposed expansion does not impose any unreasonable burden upon the lands located
            in the vicinity of the nonconforming structure.
         2. The proposed expansion to the structure maintains a compatible setback as other homes in
            the immediate area.

Staff also recommends the following condition:
         • That the applicant applies for and receive a building permit to allow for an inspection of the
             structure for compliance of building codes.
