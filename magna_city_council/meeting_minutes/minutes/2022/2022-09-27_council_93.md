Pleasant Green Cemetery Caretaker Services




                                                3
DATE           TUESDAY                                  SEPTEMBER                         27, 2022

               Paul Ashton reviewed the resolution to approve a contract between Magna Metro
Township and N&W Enterprises, LLC, for the Pleasant Green Cemetery caretaker services and
requested to read Council Member Barney’s comments into the record.

            Council Member Pierce, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to have
Council Member Barney’s comments read into the record. The motion passed unanimously.

              Rori Andreason read the following remarks into the record:

       Council,

       After reviewing the information from Paul Ashton detailing the pros and cons for
       approval of the contract with N&W Enterprises it is my firm opinion that we not
       approve the contract at this time for the following reasons:

               1) We should engage in a formal bid process. This is the only way to ensure
       the intent of our code - Policy #2 - is fulfilled. In doing so there can never be any
       question about transparency, equity, or fairness. Justifying anything but the
       process described in Policy #2 feels to me like we are manipulating the code to fit
       a desired outcome instead of applying to code equitably in all situations.

                2) I have concerns about the monthly Administrative Compensation as
       proposed. I have reviewed 4 months of billing statements and they do not contain
       sufficient detail to account for the requested amount which includes a $14,000.00
       increase over the previous year. In stating this am NOT implying that this amount
       is not the fair or proper amount, I am simply stating that the current record and bid
       submitted by N&W Enterprises on July 14 does not provide sufficient details to
       justify the price. Details I would like to see would include: itemized description of
       administrative functions performed, hours spent weekly, etc. I think we all agree
       that running the cemetery is a lot of work but we need to quantify it so we can
       justifiably and adequately compensate the chosen contractor.

       To overcome these two concerns I would propose that we offer N&W a month to
       month contract at their current rates while we complete a formal bid process. This
       would allow us to fulfill the intent of Policy #2 and allow N&W Enterprises time to
       amend their current bid to provide greater detail and justification for the requested
       compensation.

       Anything but the above proposal would be a NO vote from me.

       Lastly, I am grateful for the diligence with which N&W Enterprises has cared for
       and operated the Pleasant Green Cemetery. I know they care deeply! I recognize
       their work and efforts. My objections to the current contract should NOT be


                                                4
DATE            TUESDAY                                  SEPTEMBER                        27, 2022

        construed or imply that I have any concern with the quality of their work. They have
        done an exceptional job.

        Thank you, in advance for reading this into the meeting.

        Sincerely,
        Eric Barney
        Councilman District 2
        Magna Metro Township

                Mr. Ashton stated there are provisions within the contract that are not as clear as
they should be, and those issues should be addressed. If the Council chooses to adopt the
resolution, he suggests that it review the contract with N&W LLC and talk about amendments that
need to be made to the contract to accurately reflect what is happening at the cemetery.

              Council Member Prokopis asked if attachment A in the contract is the same as
the one presented two weeks ago.

                Mr. Ashton stated yes, it is the same as the original contract but with an increase
in pay to reflect the cost of running a cemetery.

              Council Member Hull stated the Council did not know anything about running a
cemetery and knew that the first year would be a trial to see how it would work. A request for
proposal (RFP) was put out to the public with one applicant responding. After the first year, it was
agreed upon that the contract could extend another 3 years. The amendments are important, and
the $14,000 increase will help pay for Sharon Nichols time. The RFP process was transparent
and discussed in public meetings.

                Council Member Pierce stated she thinks for the reasons Council Member
Barney pointed out, it does justify another RFP. Hiring someone to come in and help with the
historical records will not come out of the current contract and time.

               Council Member Prokopis thanked N&W LLC for its time and work it has put into
the cemetery. There was a need in our community to take care of the cemetery and Nunny Nichols
stepped up and responded to the RFP. The Council took a stab at a contract considering the
workload and tried to put a dollar amount to it with the expectation that in a year, the contract
would be revisited. Considering inflation, $14,000 is a moderate increase. Council Member
Barney has brought up valid concerns. The language in the original contract should be cleaned
up to reflect more accurately what is currently happening at the cemetery. I do not think there will
be any push back for a little more transparency in the billing.

               Council Member Prokopis made a motion to approve the Resolution.

               Council Member Pierce asked to allow the community and contractor to speak to
this issue.

              Kerri Duckworth stated it is nice to see someone taking care of the cemetery.

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       A CONTRACT BETWEEN MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP AND N&W
       ENTERPRISES, LLC (NUNNY NICHOLES) FOR PLEASANT GREEN
       CEMETERY CARETAKER SERVICES

      WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Metro Township pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-2a-401 et seq, and

      WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township Council ("Council") is a Metro Township Council
pursuant to Utah Code §10-3b-504, with the Mayor acting as Chair of the Council pursuant to
Utah Code §10-3b-503, and

      WHEREAS, in March 2020, the Magna Metro Township purchases the Pleasant Green
Cemetery; and

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council issued a Request for Proposal for
Cemetery Caretaker services; and

       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council retained the services of N&W Enterprises
LLC (Nunny Nicholes) for Caretaker Services for the Pleasant Green Cemetery for a one year
term with an option to renegotiate at the end of that period of time; and

       WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council and N&W Enterprises LLC (Nunny
Nicholes) have renegotiated the terms of service based upon the prior year's performance; and

        WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council believes it is in the best interests of the
community and cemetery to retain the services of N&W Enterprises LLC (Nunny Nicholes) for an
additional three year term pursuant to the renegotiated agreement, attached hereto, marked
Attachment "A" for Caretaker Services for the Pleasant Green Cemetery.

       NOW THEREFOR BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township Council, as follows:

Magna Metro Township Moderate Income Housing Plan

             Matt Starley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(MSD), reviewed the following Ordinance adopting amendments to the Magna Metro Township
Moderate-Income Housing plan:

ORDINANCE NO. 22-O-07                                                 Date: September 27, 2022

       AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING AMENDMENTS TO THE MAGNA METRO
       TOWNSHIP MODERATE-INCOME HOUSING PLAN, A COMPONENT OF THE
       MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP GENERAL PLAN

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a municipality pursuant to Utah
Code Ann. § 10-2a-401, et seq; and

       WHEREAS: Utah Code 10-9a-401(3)(b) requires each Metro Township in the State of
Utah, with a population of five thousand or more residents, to conduct a review of and revise the
Moderate-Income Housing Plan of its General Plan by October 1, 2022; and,

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council adopted the current Moderate Income
Housing Element as part of the General Plan in November of 2019; and

      WHEREAS, on June 3, 2022, the Magna Metro Township Council sent notice of intent to
amend the Moderate Income Housing Plan as a supplemental element of the Magna General
Plan Update to affected entities; and

                                               7
DATE           TUESDAY                                  SEPTEMBER                         27, 2022

       WHEREAS, on July 15, 2022, the Magna Planning Commission (the "Planning
Commission") sent mailed notices to affected entities to prepare recommended amendments to
the Moderate Income Housing Plan as part of General Plan (collectively, the "Housing Plan") that
would address Magna's housing and meet state requirements pursuant to Utah Code§ 10-9a-
403; and

        WHEREAS, on September 8, 2022, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to
take public comment on the Housing Element after complying with the procedures in the Act,
including but not limited to the public hearing and public meeting notice requirements of Utah
Code Ann. §§ 10-9a-204 and 10-9a-404; and

      WHEREAS, after taking public comment on the General Plan, the Planning Commission
recommended the Housing Plan to the Magna Metro Township Council with some minor
amendments and instructions to staff, which have been incorporated; and

      WHEREAS, on September 27, 2022, the Magna Metro Township Council held a public
meeting to review the Housing Plan; and

      WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council has reviewed the Housing Plan as
recommended by the Planning Commission and determined that it is necessary to amend the
Housing Plan in the best interest of Magna and its residents.

       NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL:

   1. The attached Housing Plan and all other related documents and appendices are
      approved.

   2. The prior Magna Moderate Income Housing Plan adopted in 2019, is repealed in its
      entirety.

   3. A copy of the Housing Plan will be filed and retained by the Magna Metro Township Clerk,
      Recorder, and Auditor and posted to the Magna Metro Township website.

   4. If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any of the sections, sentences, clauses,
      or provisions of the Housing Plan are invalid, such a ruling will not invalidate the remaining
      portion(s) of the Housing Plan or the General Plan.

   5. An electronic copy of the Amendment "2022 Moderate Income Housing Plan" is hereby
      ordered to be filed with both the Wasatch Front Regional Council and the Housing and
      Community Development Division of the Utah Department of Workforce Services.

   6. Staff is directed to identify those portions of the Magna Metro Township Code that need
      to be updated to conform to the Housing Element and General Plan and to recommend
      proposed changes to the Council.
