                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  ORDINANCE NO. 22-03

 AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN OF APPROXIMATELY 4.07-
  ACRES AT 2356 WEST 11800 SOUTH FROM LOW DENSITY RESIDENTIAL TO
 MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL, AND REZONING THE PROPERTY FROM RR-
                  22 TO R-4, BEN HOWELL, APPLICANT

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and
made a recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said General Plan
and zoning amendment; and,

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the
public to amend the Riverton City General Plan from Low Density Residential to Medium
Density Residential and to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map by rezoning the 5.95 acres
located at 11800 South 2356 West from RR-22 to R-4, as described herein.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:
            Section 1.     The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is,
                           amended to reflect the following changes as shown in
                           Exhibit “A”

              Section 2.             This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
