CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2716, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING THE APPRAISAL OF PRIVATE PROPERTY IN THE RED
CLIFFS DESERT RESERVE

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that there are private property owners
within the Red Cliffs Desert Reserve. The federal government is in the process of
securing all private property within the Reserve and there are existing funds that have
been designated for this purpose. Washington County has committed to facilitate the
federal acquisition of the Reserve and one of the ways to do that is to pay for appraisals
when recommended by the U.S. Department of Interior’s Appraisal and Valuation
Services Office (AVSO).

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-2716,
A Resolution Approving the Appraisal of Private Property in the Red
Cliffs Desert Reserve. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and
Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2719, A RESOLUTION
APPOINTING BOARD MEMBERS TO THE WASHINGTON COUNTY

Community Development Director Scott Messel explained that there were several
vacancies created by the expiration of terms on the Planning Commission. Proper notice
was given and interviews were conducted with the applicants. It is recommended that

January 31, 2024, and Frank Leuck be appointed to serve as an alternate with a term to
expire January 31, 2023.

COMMISSIONERS' REPORT
Commissioner Cox stated that the Northern Corridor right-of-way has been granted from
the Department of the Interior. This has been in progress since March of 2009. The Habitat

Conservation Plan was also renewed with the help of many participating agencies.

Commissioner Iverson mentioned that chip sealing has been completed on Highway 91
and cautioned drivers on that road to slow down and not follow too closely.

Michael Green has been appointed to serve as the Shooting Sports Park Manager.
The Washington County Fair will take place in April 16-18, 2021.
