                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1512

         AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER CITY
         FOR APPROXIMATELY 1.5 ACRES OF PROPERTY FROM RA1 (RESIDENTIAL
         AGRICULTURAL, 40,000 SQUARE FOOT LOT MINIMUM) TO RM1 (MULTI-
         FAMILY RESIDENTIAL, UP TO 8 DWELLING UNITS PER ACRE), AND
         APPROVING A DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT, LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY
         13666 SOUTH 300 EAST WITHIN DRAPER CITY, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE
         CONCORD LANE ZONING MAP AMENDMENT AND DEVELOPMENT
         AGREEMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and
Zoning Map to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

      WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Zoning Map to
keep pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of
economic uses; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the
City to allow development of the subject property in accordance with the Development
Agreement; and

      WHEREAS, the Development Agreement will limit the development to single- family
detached lots; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change and development agreement set forth herein
has been reviewed by the Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate
public hearings have been held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding
the proposed revisions to the Zoning Map and Development Agreement; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper
City and the development agreement, and the City Council has found the proposed zone
change and development agreement to be consistent with the City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

       Section 1.    Zoning Map Amendment. The following described real property
located at approximately 13666 South 300 East within Draper City, Salt Lake County, State of
Utah, previously zoned RA1 as shown on the Draper City Zoning Map, as depicted in Exhibit
“A” hereto, are hereby changed and rezoned to RM1:

                                DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
                                         FOR
                                     CONCORD LANE

This Development Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of this ____ day of
_______ 2022, by and between Concord Lane, LLC and New Image Investing, LLC (hereinafter
collectively referred to as "Developer"), and Draper City, a municipal corporation of the State of
Utah (hereinafter referred to as the "City").

                                            RECITALS

A. Developer owns approximately 1.5 acres of real property located near 13666 and 13696 South
300 East within City's boundaries (the "Property''). The legal description of the Property is set
forth on "Exhibit A" attached hereto.

B. The Property currently is zoned RAl (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot lot
minimum), a residential zoning designation identified in Title 9, Chapter 10 of the Draper City
Municipal Code (the "Code").

C. Developer has made application to City for a general plan map amendment and rezone or
map amendment necessary to assign RM1 (Multiple-family residential, up to 8 units per acre)
zoning designation to the Property in anticipation of developing the Property as a single-family
residential subdivision (the "Project").

D. The intent of this Agreement is to facilitate development of the Project in accordance with
the Code and the site-specific land uses and development standards specified in this Agreement.

E. City, acting pursuant to its authority under UTAH CODE ANN.§ 10-9a-101, et seq.
("LUDMA") has made certain determinations with respect to the Property and the proposed
Project, and, in the exercise of its legislative discretion, has elected to process and approve this
Agreement after all necessary public hearings and procedures have been conducted.

F. The Parties recognize the RM1 zone allows a multi-family product to be built as permitted.
Developer agrees by the acceptance and execution of this agreement to build only single-family
detached units and agrees to comply with the development standards listed herein that are not
generally applicable to single-family detached units.

G. H.B. 1003 was recently signed into law and enacts Utah Code Ann. 10-9a-534 which bars the
City from imposing a requirement for a “building design element”, as that term is defined
therein, on one and two family dwellings. However, Utah Code Ann. 10-9a-534(3)(d) permits
the Parties to agree to building design elements in a development agreement. The Parties agree to
be bound by this Agreement insofar as it implements requirements for building design elements.


NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good
and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the
City and Developer hereby agree as follows.

                                         AGREEMENT

1. Incorporation of Recitals; Entire Agreement. The above recitals and the exhibits referenced
in this Agreement are hereby incorporated by reference as part of this Agreement. This
Agreement integrates and constitutes all of the terms and conditions pertaining to the subject
matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, promises, inducements, or
previous agreements between the Parties hereto with respect to such subject matter.

2. Condition Precedent. This Agreement is contingent upon and shall only become effective at
such time, and in the event that: (a) the Draper City Council (the "Council"), in the independent,
unfettered exercise of its legislative discretion, elects to approve the proposed rezoning of the
Property to the RM1 zoning designation.

3. Plan. Subject to Section 4 below, a plan (the "Plan") depicting the permissible lots, building
envelopes, street(s) and certain other dimensions and physical attributes of the Project is attached
hereto as Exhibit B and is incorporated herein by this reference. The Project shall be limited to a
maximum of eight (8) lots and a maximum residential density of 5.3 dwelling units per acre,
which Lots shall be exclusively used for single-family detached units and shall be configured and
located substantially as shown on the Plan. The approval of the Plan as part of this Agreement
satisfies any Code requirement for a concept plan or any other similar requirement for the
Project, provided that the Plan shall not constitute a minor subdivision plat for the Project.

4. Project-Specific Development Standards.

4.1. Site Standards. By this Agreement, the Parties intend to define the uses, development
standards and other regulations otherwise applicable to the Property under the RMl zoning
designation as described in Title 9, Chapter 10 of the Code in order to promote the efficient and
creative development of the Property. The specific allowed land uses, development standards
and regulations for the Project that are to be used in substitution for the uses, development
standards and other regulations contained in Title 9 of the Code are described below.
Development standards that are not addressed in this Agreement shall be those development
standards set forth in Title 9 and 17 of the Code in place at time of complete subdivision
application submission. Developer shall develop the Project in accordance with the Plan (subject
to Section 4.3) and in full compliance with the Site Standards and the Code.

4.2. Minimum Lot Size. The minimum lot size shall be 6,000 square feet.
4.3. Unit Variety. To avoid an undesirable aesthetic impression that may be created by building
neighboring uniform models, Developer shall provide for a variety of models with different
building elevations and finishes for the units in the Project which shall consist of color change
and roof line change. The roofline for each model shall differ from the other models by at least
one (1) foot in height. Unit variety shall be as depicted in the elevations and renderings attached
as Exhibit C. The same model will not be built next to each other and the development will
contain at least three different models.

4.4. Unit Exterior Building Materials. The architecture and design will consist of 25% brick
and 75% painted fiber cement siding or stucco, or 25% cultured stone and 75% siding or stucco.
The front elevations will have different facades with different ratios of exterior materials after
minimum requirements are met. To maintain an overall aesthetic theme while simultaneously
encouraging variety, some buildings may have a larger percentage of brick or stone than other
buildings and each building will have its own unique architectural features accented by brick or
stone. The dwellings will include the following 10 architectural features:

A. Porch with support columns (6 foot minimum depth).
B. Portico entrance at front doorway.
C. Moldings around windows (4 inch minimum width).
D. Decorative brackets under eaves.
E. Exterior crown molding with eave returns.
F. Roof gable with staggered shingle.
G. Roof dormer.
H. Board and batten look achieved through vertical fiber cement siding.
I. Corner trim extending from the base to roofline.
J. Architectural grade shingle.

4.5. Unit Size. The single-family structures will range in size with a minimum of 2,200 Square
Feet of finished living space to the maximum square feet within current setbacks with optional
basement included.

4.6. Unit Garages. Each unit will include an attached and/or detached garage with a minimum of
two (2) stalls. Each driveway will be a minimum of twenty feet (20’) deep.

4.7. Unit Height. Height of all units will be a maximum of thirty-five feet (35').

4.8. Unit Set-backs. Front set-back fifteen (15') feet except for a front facing garage that has a
minimum setback of twenty feet (20’), side set-back of six feet (6’), and rear set-backs to be
twenty feet (20') on interior lots and six feet (6’) on corner lots. Side yards that border the North
and South property lines of the subdivision shall be set back a minimum of twelve feet (12’).

4.9. Private Roads. As depicted in EXHIBIT B, the road into the Project will be a private lane
with a cross-section of thirty-one feet (31’). The hammerhead portion will be twenty feet (20’)
from back of sidewalk on the west to back of curb on the East. Homeowners association shall
maintain the private roads.
4.10. Easement for Stormwater Detention and Open Space. The Association, its Board,
employees, agents, and contractors shall have non-exclusive easements to install and maintain a
City-required stormwater detention pond or basin on the west side of Lots 6 and 7 and on the east
side of Lots 1 and 8. As depicted in EXHIBIT B, the aforementioned easements will provide the
HOA green open space that may also provide drainage for the property. The location(s) may be
relocated from the exhibit as necessary, based on engineering best practices for swales and
drainage.

4.11. CC&Rs and HOA. Developer shall establish a homeowners association ("HOA") and
adopt conditions, covenants and restrictions ("CC&Rs") which shall provide for the collection of
assessments and the establishment of a reserve fund, to maintain the private lanes.

5. Reserved Legislative Powers. The Parties acknowledge that City is restricted in its authority
to limit its police power by contract and that the limitations, reservations and exceptions set forth
herein are intended to reserve to City those police powers that cannot be so limited.
Notwithstanding the retained power of City to enact such legislation under the police powers,
such legislation shall only be applied to modify any development standards under Title 9 of the
code that are applicable to the Project under the terms of this Agreement based upon the policies,
facts and circumstances meeting the compelling, countervailing public interest. Proposed
legislative changes shall be of general application to all development activity in City. Unless
City declares an emergency, Developer shall be entitled to prior written notice and an
opportunity to be heard with respect to any proposed change and its applicability to the Project
under the compelling, countervailing public interest exception to the vested rights doctrine.

6. Approval Process for Development Applications. City shall process applications for
development of the Project in accordance with the Code. Nothing in this Agreement shall be
deemed to relieve Developer of the obligation to comply with all of the applicable requirements
for approval of minor subdivision plat, as applicable, for the proposed development of the
Project consistent with the terms and conditions of this Agreement and the applicable provisions
of the Code.

7. Successors and Assigns.

7.1 Binding Effect. This Agreement shall be binding upon the successors and assigns of
Developer in the ownership or development of any portion of the Project.

7.2 Assignment. Neither this Agreement nor any of its provision, terms or conditions may be
assigned to any other party, individual or entity without assigning the rights as well as the
responsibilities under this Agreement and without the prior written consent of the City, which
consent shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any such request for assignment may be made by
letter addressed to the City, and the prior written consent of the City may also be evidenced by
letter from the City to Developer or its successors or assigns.

8. Default. If a Party fails to timely perform its obligations hereunder or to comply with the
terms hereof, the Party believing that a default has occurred shall provide written notice (a
"Notice of Default") to the defaulting Party as provided herein. Upon the issuance of a Notice of
Default, the Parties shall meet within ten business days and confer in an attempt to resolve the
issues that are the subject matter of the Notice of Default. If, after meeting and conferring, the
Parties are not able to resolve the default, then the Parties shall have the rights and remedies
available at law and in equity including, without limitation, injunctive relief, specific
performance and termination, but not including compensatory damages, punitive damages or
attorney's fees.

9. Notices. Any notices, requests and demands required or desired to be given hereunder shall be
in writing and shall be served personally upon the party for whom intended, or if mailed, be by
certified mail, return receipt requested, postage prepaid, to such party at its address shown below.

10. General Terms and Conditions.

10.1 Terms of Agreement. The term of this Agreement shall be for a period of five (5) years
following the date of its adoption unless both parties otherwise agreed to extend this time via
written amendment.

10.2 Agreement to Run with the Land. This Agreement shall be recorded in the office of the
Salt Lake County Recorder against the Property and it’s intended to and shall be deemed to run
with the land and shall be binding on all successors in the ownership of any portion or the
Property.

10.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the exhibits hereto, integrates all of the
terms and conditions pertaining to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations,
representations, promises, inducements, or previous agreements between the parties hereto with
respect to the subject matter hereof. Any amendments hereto must be in writing and signed by
the respective parties hereto.

10.4 Headings. The headings contained in this Agreement are intended for convenience only and
are in no way to be used to construe or limit the text herein.

10.5 Non-liability of City Officials or Employees. No officer, representative, agent, or
employee of the City shall be personally liable to Developer, in the event of any default or
breach by the City or for any amount, which may become due, or its successors or assignees, for
any obligation arising out of the terms of this Agreement.

10.6 No Third-Party Rights. The obligations of the parties set forth in this Agreement shall not
create any rights in or obligations to any persons or parties other than to the City and Developer.
The City and Developer alone shall be entitled to enforce or waive any provisions of this
Agreement to the extent that such provisions are for their benefit.

10.7 Severability. Should any portion of this Agreement for any reason be declared invalid or
unenforceable, the invalidity or unenforceability of such portion shall not affect the validity of
any of the remaining portions, and the same shall be deemed in full force and effect as if this
Agreement had been executed with the invalid portions eliminated.

10.8 Waiver. No waiver of any of the provisions of this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of
any other provision regardless of any similarity that may exist between such provisions nor shall
a waiver in one instance operate as a waiver in any future event. No waiver shall be binding
unless executed in writing by the waiving party.

10.9 Governing Law. This Agreement and the performance hereunder shall be governed by the
laws of the State of Utah.

10.10 Exhibits. Any exhibit to this Agreement is incorporated herein by this reference, and
failure to attach any such exhibit shall not affect the validity of this Agreement or of such
exhibit. An unattached exhibit is available from the records of the parties.

11. Authority. The Parties to this Agreement each warrant that they have all of the necessary
authority to execute this Agreement. Specifically, on behalf of the City, the signature of the City
Manager of the City is affixed to this Agreement lawfully binding the City pursuant to Ordinance
No. 1512 adopted by the City on November 16, 2021.
