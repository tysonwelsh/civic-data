COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
ORDINANCE # 2019-0 4—O\
April 3, 2019
A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING A COMPREHENSIVE VISIONING STUDY FOR THE
SOUTHWEST QUADRANT OF SALT LAKE COUNTY AND
REGARDING THE OLYMPIA HILLS DEVELOPMENT
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council (the
“Council”) met in a public meeting on April 3, 2019.
WITNESS
WHEREAS, the Council supports reasonable and responsible growth and development;
WHEREAS, since 2000, 70% of the population growth in Salt Lake County has occurred
in Bluffdale, Copperton, Herriman, Riverton, South Jordan, and West Jordan (collectively, the
“Southwestern Municipalities”), representing an increase of about 160,000 people;

WHEREAS, the Council recognizes that the Southwest Quadrant of Salt Lake County (the
“Southwest Quadrant”) will continue to develop in response to Utah’s growing population
because it is home to about half of the remaining undeveloped land within Salt Lake County;

WHEREAS, the Southwestern Municipalities have formed the Southwest Mayors Caucus
to work together for the greater good of the Southwest Quadrant, including but not limited to
ensuring responsible growth and development;

WHEREAS, the Southwest Mayors Caucus has secured $251,000 for a first-ever
comprehensive visioning study to make recommendations on how to better integrate roadway,
infrastructure, active transportation, quality of life, and transit to ensure responsible growth and
development in the Southwest Quadrant;

WHEREAS, to fund the study, the Southwestern Municipalities have contributed a
combined sum of $26,000, and received grants from Salt Lake County for $100,000, and the
Wasatch Front Regional Council for $125,000;

WHEREAS, developers have proposed the Olympia Hills development for approximately
900 acres of unincorporated land located near 6300 — 8500 West and 12400 — 13100 South;

WHEREAS, as originally proposed, the Olympia Hills development envisioned nearly
9,000 units with a projected density of nine dwellings per acre, making it the densest housing
development of its size in Utah and potentially adding a city the size of Herriman or Riverton to

the Southwest Quadrant;

WHEREAS, on June 5, 2018, the Salt Lake County Council approved a zoning change
needed for the Olympia Hills development to proceed, voting 7-1 to rezone the site from

| agricultural use to residential use;

WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council approved the zoning change over nearly
unanimous opposition from Southwest Quadrant residents and the mayors of Bluffdale,
Copperton, Herriman, Riverton, South Jordan, and West Jordan over concerns about a lack of
consultation with their communities and the impacts of such a large and dense development on the
roadway, utility infrastructure, and open space in the Southwest Quadrant, including but not
limited to key roadways like 9000 South, 12600 South, and 13400 South that are already at critical
levels of use as a result of the significant growth the region has experienced in recent years;

WHEREAS, in response to public concern about the project, former Salt Lake County
Mayor Ben McAdams vetoed the Salt Lake C ounty Council’s approval of the re-zoning change
and the Council elected not to over-ride the Mayor’s veto;

WHEREAS, as a result of former Mayor McAdams’ veto, the Olympia Hills developers
have revised their proposal;

WHEREAS, the revised plans now envision 6,500 households and slightly less than seven
households per acre;

WHEREAS, the Olympia Hills developers are engaging in public outreach to explain their
revised plans and held open houses on March 13" in Herriman and on March 14" in South Jordan
to solicit feedback from residents of the Southwester Quadrant;

WHEREAS, the Council appreciates the efforts of the Olympia Hills developers to revise
their plans and to solicit community input, but believes that the comprehensive visioning study
called for by the Southwest Mayors Caucus, must be completed before Salt Lake County can
adequately assess or approve new zoning for the unincorporated land in the Southwest Quadrant;
and

WHEREAS, the Council believes that large developments like the Olympia Hills
development require robust and early collaborative dialog between the developers, the County, the
Southwestern Municipalities, and residents of the Southwest Quadrant to ensure that such
developments are evaluated properly and constructed responsibly.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE
COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP AS FOLLOWS:

1, The Council declares its support for the Southwest Mayors Caucus’ comprehensive
visioning study and directs Copperton staff to provide any and all assistance that may be needed
to bring the study to fruition;

2. The Council appreciates Salt Lake County’s grant of $100,000 and the Wasatch
Front Regional Council’s grant of $150,000 for the study;

2

3. The Council respectfully urges the Salt Lake County Council to refrain from
approving new zoning or other changes needed for the proposed Olympia Hills development or
other future developments until the comprehensive visioning study has been completed; and

4. The Council urges the Olympia Hills developers and the Salt Lake County Council
to engage in a collaborative dialog with the Southwestern Mayors Caucus and Southwestern
Quadrant residents to ensure that the proposed development and any other large, high density
developments are evaluated and approved properly and constructed responsibly.

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
3" day of April, 2019.
