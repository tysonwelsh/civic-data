PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
                                                  Hearing began at – 3:04 p.m. 
 
Consideration of Proposed Amendment to Salt Lake County’s Wasatch Canyons General Plan to add a 
Water Use and Preservation Element. Presenter: Garrett Stone with GSBS Consulting (Motion/Voting) 
 
GSBS Consulting, Planning Consultant Garrett Stone provided an analysis of the proposed water use and 
preservation element plan. 
 
Commissioner Knoblock motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner McCallister seconded that 
motion. Commissioners voted unanimously. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
No one from the public was present to speak. 
 
Commissioner Knoblock motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Brunhart seconded that 
motion. Commissioners voted unanimously. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To recommend file #OAM2025-001554 Consideration of Proposed Amendment to Salt Lake 
County’s Wasatch Canyons General Plan to add a Water Use and Preservation Element for approval to 
the Salt Lake County Council. 
Motion by: Commissioner Hales 
2nd by: Commissioner Brunhart 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimously in favor (of commissioners present) 
 
Commissioner Brunhart adjourned. 
MEETING ADJOURNED
