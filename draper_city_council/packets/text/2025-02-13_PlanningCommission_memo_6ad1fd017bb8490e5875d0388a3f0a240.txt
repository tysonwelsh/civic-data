MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Nick Whittaker
Date: 2025-02-13
Re: Public Hearing: DeRose Plumbing Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit Request
(Administrative item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit
for approximately 0.43 acres located on the west side of Hilton Head Way, at
approximately 12206 S. Hilton Head Way. The property is currently zoned R3 (Single-
Family Residential, 13,000 ft2 min. lot size). The applicant is requesting that a Home
Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved for an office operating a plumbing
business.



Findings for approval:

  1. The proposal complies with the standards for approval found in DCMC Section 9-5-
     080(E) and potential negative impacts are mitigated through the imposition of
     reasonable conditions.


Findings for denial:

  1. The proposal does not comply with all standards for approval found in DCMC
     Section 9-5-080(E) and potential negative impacts are not able to be sufficiently
     mitigated through the imposition of reasonable conditions.
