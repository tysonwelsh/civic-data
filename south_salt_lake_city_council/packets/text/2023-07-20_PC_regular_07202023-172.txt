 Staff recommends the Planning
 Commission hold a public hearing
 and approve the final subdivision plat
 at 919 West 2100 South. The
 Planning Commission approved the
 preliminary plat for this project on
 July 13, 2023.

SYNOPSIS: November 18, 2022,
Dominion Energy (represented by
Brady Nowers), submitted a plat
application for the construction and
operation of a regulator station at 919
West 2100 South. The purpose of the
application and project is part of a
larger infrastructure replacement
program to better meet current and
future natural gas supply demand.
The total proposed structure would
be approximately 22 feet x 26 feet
(572 square feet total) and 10 feet in
height.
This parcel has never been legally
platted and is currently described in
metes and bounds, so this application would bring the parcel into compliance with South Salt Lake municipal code by
legally platting it.

The applicant applied for a variance on 3/2/2023 for an exception to the minimum lot size standard of one acre in the Flex
land use district. The subject property is an existing parcel that is 0.7 acres in size. Upon review, the South Salt Lake City
Community Development Director determined that the subject property qualified for the requested variance based on
application of the five criteria outlined in Utah Code 10-9a-702 and South Salt Lake Municipal Code Section 17.12.010(B).

The Planning Commission is the Land Use Authority on all subdivision plat applications and amendments.

GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS:

Land Use & Neighborhoods Goal 3, Page 28: Enhance urban and streetscape design in the city to support South Salt Lake’s
distinct image and enhance community pride.

        Analysis: The proposed project will improve a parcel that is currently vacant by adding landscaping and a sidewalk.

Community Facilities Goal 1, Strategy 2, Page 79: Encourage development that provides services in a logical, orderly manner
such that adequate streets, water, sewer, drainage facilities, schools, and other essential services can be provided, both
efficiently and economically.

        Analysis: The proposed regulator station will allow citizens in South Salt Lake and throughout the Salt Lake valley to
        have consistent access to natural gas.

Community Facilities Goal 1, Strategy 4, Page 79: Ensure an equitable access to services within neighborhoods and serving
areas of higher density housing.

        Analysis: The proposed project will, as part of Dominion Energy’s larger infrastructure improvement plan, help
        ensure that natural gas is available to residents throughout the South Salt Lake valley.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:
Based upon the information submitted in the application, supporting documentation, and analysis of the relevant code
sections and general plan goals, Staff recommends the Planning Commission move to approve, with conditions, the final
subdivision plat amendment and proposes the following findings of fact and conclusions of law and conditions of approval:

Findings of Fact:

    1.    The subject property, located at 919 West 2100 South, consists of one metes and bounds parcel.
    2.    The parcel is located in the Flex zone.
    3.    All new lots in the Flex zone shall meet the one-acre minimum.
    4.    The proposed subdivision will legally plat one lot that will be approximately 0.7 acres in size.
    5.    The applicant received a variance for an exception to the minimum lot size for a 0.7-acre lot on April 13, 2023.
    6.    The proposed development falls into the Public Utility Facility land use category.
    7.    The Public Utility Facility use is a conditional land use in the Flex zone.
    8.    The subject property received a conditional use permit from the City of South Salt Lake on September 1, 2022.
    9.    The subject property is currently vacant.
    10.   The proposed lot will be served by existing access points on 2100 South.
    11.   The South Salt Lake City Engineer has reviewed the proposed development, using the criteria found in SSL Code
          Section 17.06.050 B(3)(f): safety, alternative access points and potential for reciprocal or shared accesses, sight

                                                             8
          lines, impact on traffic flow, and site circulation. (This section of code is pending approval by the SSL City Council
          on July 12, 2023.)


Conclusions of Law:

    1.    The subdivision, as specifically conditioned below, is consistent with the South Salt Lake City General Plan.
    2.    The subdivision, as specifically conditioned below is compatible in use, scale, and design with the allowed uses in
          the Flex zone.
    3.    The subdivision as specifically conditioned below, does not compromise the health, safety, or welfare of: Persons
          employed within or using the proposed development; Those residing or working in the vicinity of the proposed
          use or development; or Property or improvements in the vicinity of the proposed use or development.
    4.    The subdivision, as specifically conditioned below does not impose disproportionate burdens on the citizens of
          the city.
    5.    The City Engineer has determined that the use is eligible to have two curb cuts under the criteria outlined in SSL
          Code section 17.06.050 B (3)(f).
    6.    Staff has reviewed this subdivision for compliance with SSL Municipal Code Section “17.10.140 Subdivision
          Development Lot Standards” and found that this subdivision is compliant with this section, as outlined above.
    7.    Staff has reviewed this subdivision for compliance with SSL Municipal Code Section “17.10.150 Subdivision and
          Condominium Plat Layout Requirements” and found that this subdivision is compliant with this section, as
          outlined above.
    8.    Staff has reviewed this subdivision for compliance with SSL Municipal Code Sections 17.10.160, 17.10.170,
          17.10.180, 17.10.190, 17.10.200, and 17.10.210, and found that this subdivision is compliant with those sections,
          as outlined above.

Conditions of Approval:

    1.    The applicant will continue to work with City staff to make all technical corrections necessary prior to recording;
    2.    Prior to plat recordation and any additional development of the subsequent lot, the applicant must provide city
          staff the final plat mylar to include notarized signatures of owners’ consent to dedication; obtain signatures of
          all entities indicated on the subdivision plat attached hereto;
    3.    The applicant shall complete (or post an adequate improvement completion assurance), warrant and post
          required warranty assurance for all required landscaping and public infrastructure improvements;
    4.    The applicant shall ensure that all site improvements are installed consistent with the requirements of the South
          Salt Lake City Municipal Code;
    5.    The applicant shall dedicate an easement to the City of South Salt Lake for purposes of storm water management
          practices and ensure the plat includes language of the dedication of such easement, in a form as approved by the
          City Attorney’s Office;
    6.    The proposed building shall be constructed according to South Salt Lake commercial design standards;
    7.    The plans shall provide a fencing plan that is constructed according to South Salt Lake commercial design
          standards;
    8.    All items of the staff report.
