GENERAL PLAN/ZONE CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GPZ-1-2023 Petition by RUSS NELSON requesting a General Plan change from Non-Retail
           Commercial to General Commercial and a zone change from RB (Residential
           Business) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located at 4543 and
           4547 S 4000 W on 0.73 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
           steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov) Continued from the April 26, 2023 public
           hearing.

Discussion:      Steve Pastorik noted that this application had been continued twice. He
                 explained that the applicant requested the first continuance to see if they could
                 share the access to the south. Steve said the applicant requested a continuance
                 during last week's meeting to redesign the proposed car wash using one access
                 to the north. He noted that the applicant had yet to provide any new information,
                 so he has nothing to share at this time. Steve hopes to have further information
                 for the Commission at next week's meeting.
                 Commissioner Woodruff asked if there are any statistics comparing the traffic
                 generated from a car wash to another type of drive-thru business. Steve said a
                 Trip Generation Manual from the Institute of Transportation of Engineers
                 provides the typical traffic generated from those uses. He stated that he would
                 have that information for next week's meeting.

                 Commissioner Winters asked Commissioner Woodruff if he was concerned
                 about increased traffic where the subject property is next to a residential
                 development. Commissioner Woodruff said that is his concern. He has noticed
                 increased traffic on the streets where a car wash operates, and a different use
                 would fit better in this location. Steve said the current zoning is RB which
                 would allow uses like a medical office or a bank.

                 Chair Fuller asked if anything developed on the subject property would be
                 allowed access off 4000 West. Steve said that is correct due to the close
                 proximity of the one to the south.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:40 p.m.
