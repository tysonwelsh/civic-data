35   REQUEST FOR A HILLSIDE DEVELOPMENT PERMIT:
36        Consider approval of a request for a hillside development permit to adjust
37        the location of the ridgeline and ridgeline setback on lot 44 of the
38        Foremaster Ridge subdivision in anticipation of the construction of a
39        residential home. The applicants are Jack de Ryk and Floyd Jackson. The
40        Project will be known as Foremaster Ridge Lot 44. Case No. 2024-HS-010.
41
42         BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: This is a request to obtain a hillside permit
43         for the property located at 1856 E. Joe Cir. The rear of the applicant’s property abuts
44         the ridge line of Foremaster Ridge. The applicant is requesting to review the location
45         of the 30-foot ridgeline setback as shown on the final plat for Foremaster Ridge lot
46         44 and establish a ridgeline which the applicant feels is more indicative of the actual
47         ridgeline. Case No. 2024-HS-010 (Staff – Dan Boles).

26   REQUEST FOR A DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT AMENDMENT:
27        Consider approval of a request for an amendment to the Divario
28        development agreement for the purpose of allowing disturbance of
29        “unimproved open space” on the southern end of PA-2. This will allow for
30        installation of a sewer main for the Divario Development and channelization
31        of the existing Gap Canyon Wash. The applicant is SG 730, LLC, and the
32        representative is Mark Teepen. The project will be known as Divario
33        Agreement Amendment. Case No. 2024-DA-001 – PUBLIC HEARING.
34
35         BACKGROUND AND RECOMMENDATION: The property was annexed as part of the
36         Plantations Land in 1994. In 2007, a development agreement was approved between
37         the developer and the City. As part of the development agreement, certain areas
38         were designated as PA’s (Planning Areas) to be developed, “improvable open space”,
39         and “unimproved open space.” Case No. 2024-DA-001 (Staff – Dan Boles).
