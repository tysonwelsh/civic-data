This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-26-4004, “King
Rezone,” by changing the General Plan Designation to Medium Density Residential and
Rezoning the subject properties to R-4.

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-26-4004, “King
Rezone,” thus keeping the current General Plan Designation of Estate Density and current
Zoning of RR-22.

Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, David King for
multiple parcels, and/or portions of parcels at Trestle Lane and 13400 South. The subject
parcels add up to 0.49-acres with an existing home.

The parcels are located at 13382, 13392, and 13400 S. Trestle Lane, and 2426 West 13400
South. The largest parcel is 0.36 acres is owned by David King. The remaining property is
made of parts of overall parcels 27-33-476-045 and 27-33-476-044 which are both owned by
Riverton City. The property is part of the previous road way, which was realigned to its current
location several years ago. David King is seeking this rezone as a first step towards a
proposed purchase of property which would, combined with his existing adjacent property, allow
for potential development of an additional residential lot. City officials have discussed the
possible sale of the City property, but have not committed to selling the property, nor has the
City endorsed nor guaranteed the rezone. The Planning Commission should evaluate this
application on its own merits.

The current General Plan for the property is Estate Density Residential which corresponds with
lot sizes greater than 21,780-sf (half-acre). The applicant is requesting the General Plan
Designation be changed to Medium Density Residential (min 10,000-sf lots) which is consistent
with R-4 zoning. The current zoning of the property is RR-22, which is halt-acre minimum with
animal rights. However, the applicants’ primary parcel is currently less than half-acre. The
area zoning is a mix of R-3, R-4 and RR-22.

The General Plan for the area is Estate Density and Medium Density Residential. Estate Density
Residential is equivalent to RR-22 Zoning. Medium Density is equivalent to the R-4 Zoning.




Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
webpage.

When considering rezone applications, the Planning Commission makes a recommendation to
City Council. The City Council will make a final decision on this application in a future City Council
Meeting where the public can attend and comment. Property owners within 1,000 feet will receive
an additional mailed notice announcing the date and time for the City Council Meeting.

A neighbor called to express concerns about adding additional housing at the corner of Trestle
Lane and 13400 South. He stated the road section for Trestle is very narrow, and the curve is
near 13400 South, these physical characteristics make the road very dangerous when a car turns
off 13400 South, he was concerned a rezone, and subdivision would result in more on street
parking and more dangerous road interactions.
