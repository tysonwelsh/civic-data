Re: Dancer in the Desert Designs Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for approximately 0.47 acres located on the southeast corner of the intersection of Golden Pheasant Dr. and Tuscan Grove Cove, at approximately 13147 S. Tuscan Grove Cove. The property is currently zoned RA2 and is part of the Tuscan Grove Subdivision. The house was constructed in 2010 and is approximately 6,200 ft 2 in size.
The applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for a cottage business home occupation that designs, sews, and constructs custom made dance clothing and costumes.
The mailed notice of the hearing misclassified the business as limited assembly and repair business, this report updates the correct classification to that of a cottage business. The findings are as follows: For Approval:
1. Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For Denial:
