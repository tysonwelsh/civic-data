           Authorizing the Execution of an Agreement with the State of Utah to Convey the
           Surplus Parcels to the State in Exchange for a Lease of a Portion of the Properties
           to the City for a Period of 99 Years
           Presenter: Ryan Clark, Assistant City Manager, Steve Earl, City Attorney, Luke
           Peterson, Utah Lake Authority and Sam Braeggar, Utah Lake Authority
