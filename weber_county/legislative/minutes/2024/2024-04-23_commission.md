                                    Via Zoom meeting + at Weber Center, 2380 Washington Blvd., Ogden, UT
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: Today is a wonderful day. We have our Crystal Bowl awards
   that are given to outstanding people in our community. Following that we will have a State of the County video
   presentation. As I got thinking of a song I learned as a boy, Give Said the Little Stream:

                                                                    “Give,” said the little stream,
                                                                    “Give, oh! give, give, oh! give.”
                                                                    “Give,” said the little stream,
                                                                    As it hurried down the hill;
                                                                    “I’m small, I know, but wherever I go
                                                                    The fields grow greener still.”

                                                                    Singing, singing all the day,
                                                                    “Give away, oh! give away.”
                                                                    Singing, singing all the day,
                                                                    “Give, oh! give away.”

                                                                    “Give,” said the little rain,
                                                                    “Give, oh! give, give, oh! give.”
                                                                    “Give,” said the little rain,
                                                                    As it fell upon the flow’rs;
                                                                    “I’ll raise their drooping heads again,”
                                                                    As it fell upon the flow’rs.

      We all have the ability to give. The end of this song talks about the Savior Jesus Christ and His ability to give.
      Do your little part that contributes to a bigger picture.

   1. Presentation of the Crystal Bowl Awards
                                             Christopher Crockett
                                             Myers Mortuary
                                             Betty Sawyer
                                             Terry Schow
            Commissioner Harvey: Every year Weber County presents a Crystal Bowl award to a county employee, a
            citizen, a business, and a veteran. We have a video we would like to show first.
            Commissioner Bolos: I want to thank all for their contributions to our community. Just like the crystal bowl,
            it’s a little thing that magnifies and blesses and helps those around us. It’s the little things that go a long way.
            Commissioner Froerer: When I look at this group, they exemplify what Weber County and this community is
            all about. We give, we share, we cooperate with each other and that makes us all stronger. It takes initiative
            to move from what they want to do for themselves to what they want to do for their community.
            Terry Schow: I went to five elementary schools in Ogden. With the exception of my military service, this has
            been my home. I want to commend Weber County for being the first County in the state to form a Veteran’s
            Advisory Committee.
         Betty Sawyer: It has been my pleasure to do the work I have done in Weber County. When we talk about
         Juneteenth, for 35 years I had to fight my colleagues in Salt Lake as they say, “Why Weber? Why Ogden?”
         Because this is it. This is the place. There is so much rich history in Ogden and Weber County. I have spent
         my life just trying to build the kind of community we can all be proud of. I am honored and I thank you all for
         being a part of that and recognizing all our work in making Weber County the best county in Utah.
         Tracy Franklin, General Manager, Myers Mortuary: The gift of service they give to the community and the
         caring nature they have I and the families in the community have been blessed to receive for so many years.
         They truly treat the families they serve as an extension as their own. It helps the grieving families get through
         the grief and loss.
         Chris Crockett: Thank you for this award. It touches my heart. I appreciate it very, very much. I am a firm
         believer if you take time to see the humanity in an individual, things just seem to work out and things fall into
         place. That’s how I’ve decided to live my life. My parents instilled that in me at a young age. My mother
         taught me to see humanity through the gift of travel. My father, who encouraged me to invest, invested in me
         and encouraged me to seek higher education. I’m glad they are here today. I am grateful for my wife who
         stands by my side; I am the luckiest person on the planet. I am so grateful for the people I get to work with.
         Give me a department and I will list off a number of people I consider my friends. I look into this audience
         and there are so many people who have been such a beneficial impact in my life. They have taught me the
         value in seeing the human in everyone. There are a number of very sacred experiences near and dear to my
         heart. I work with the finest attorneys in the state of Utah and I truly consider them my dearest and closest
         friends. I have four amazing bosses in the Commissioners and Chris Allred who let me ask questions and they
         invest in me and help me be a better person. Thank you so much, this is such an honor to work here.
         Commissioner Harvey: Thank you all for positively contributing to a place I love so much. For the spouses
         who often times are unrecognized, thank you so much. You are the powerhouses and the strength that allow
         those being recognized today to shine.
