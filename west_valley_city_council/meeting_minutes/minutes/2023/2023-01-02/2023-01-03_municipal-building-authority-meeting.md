    Ms. Lang moved to approve the Minutes of the Regular Meeting of October 4, 2022. Mr.
    Nordfelt seconded the motion.

    A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

    ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2023
    Chairman Christensen stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2023 had
    been scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been
    used in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board
    and not mandatory.

    Chairman Christensen called for a motion.

    Mr. Huynh moved to elect William Whetstone as Chair of the Municipal Building
    Authority for 2023 and Scott Harmon as Vice Chair of the Municipal Building Authority
    for 2023. Mr. Lehman seconded the motion.

    A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.


    MOTION TO ADJOURN
    Mr. Huynh moved to adjourn at 6:58 PM.


    THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE MUNICIPAL BUILDING
    AUTHORITY OF WEST VALLEY CITY, THE ANNUAL MEETING OF TUESDAY,
    JANUARY 3, 2023, WAS ADJOURNED AT 6:58 PM BY CHAIRMAN
    CHRISTENSEN.
