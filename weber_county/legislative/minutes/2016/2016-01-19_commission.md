E. PRESENTATION

    1. PRESENTATION ON A COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP WITH THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH
       AND THE WEBER COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE

         Dr. Kay Haw, Nursing Administrator, Weber County Sheriff’s Office, wanted to inform the
         Commission and county residents of the collaborative effort regarding comprehensive health
         testing among the incarcerated. She introduced Edwin Espinel, Program Coordinator with the
         Utah Department of Health, who gave the presentation, which included testing and screening
         results for HIV, Hepatitis C and STDs. He stated that the County Sheriff’s Office, as a testing for
         the local Health Department, continues cost effectiveness, that collaboration streamlines
         prevention services, and that the partnership is a best practice for addressing communicable
         disease testing among the incarcerated.

    1. DISCUSSION ON APPOINTING CHAIR AND VICE CHAIR TO THE WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION

         Commissioner Gibson noted that in the past there has been an informal practice to discuss the

    2. CONTRACT WITH HPN BOOKS FOR A CITYSCAPE BOOK ON WEBER COUNTY

         Brooke Stewart, of the County Commission Office, noted that this is for publication of a
         hardcover pictorial book at no cost to the county. Bart Barica, with the publisher, noted that this
         book will be a service to the public and that customer service will be enhanced.
         Commissioner Bell moved to approve the contract with HPN Books for a cityscape book on
         Weber County; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    3. CONTRACT WITH BROKEN HEART RODEO FOR THE INTERMOUNTAIN ICEBREAKER HIGH
       SCHOOL INVITATIONAL RODEO AT GOLDEN SPIKE EVENT CENTER (GSEC) ON 3/3-5/22/2016

         Jim Harvey, with GSEC, presented this contract.
         Commissioner Bell moved to approve the contract with Broken Heart Rodeo for the
         Intermountain Icebreaker High School Invitational Rodeo at the Golden Spike Event Center on
         March 3-5, 2016; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    4.   REIMBURSEMENT AGREEMENTS WITH THE FOLLOWING INDIVIDUALS FOR BASIC TRAINING:
          CARSON MORBY; JESSICA BICKLE

         Steffani Ebert, of the County Sheriff’s Office, noted that the Sheriff’s Office has hired two more
         individuals to attend Weber State’s Law Enforcement Academy for specialized training at the
         county’s expense. The agreement states that the trainees will remain in continuous employment
         with the county for at least two years or they will reimburse the county for the training.
         Commissioner Bell moved to approve the reimbursement agreements with Carson Morby and
         Jessica Bickle for basic training; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    5. GRANTING AN EASEMENT FOR EMERGENCY ACCESS OVER COUNTY PROPERTY

         Sean Wilkinson, Community and Economic Development Department Director, stated that in
         2008 the county granted an easement through a county parking lot at the Animal Shelter at the
         Fairgrounds for a development at the western boundary of that parking lot. The development did
         not come to fruition. A new company that purchased the property is proceeding with
         development and asked the county to relocate that easement to better align the new road design.
         The previous agreement required the developer to plow the county’s parking lot but at this
         developer’s request this has been removed and they will maintain the area that would have to be
         accessed for the easement. The easement will be gated by the developer. This is a 50-year
         agreement.
         Commissioner Ebert moved to grant an easement for emergency access over county property;
         Commissioner Bell seconded.
         Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
