   2. CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (CUP) Administrative

      Consider a request for a conditional use permit to redevelop the existing Kmart shopping area by updating the
      building elevations, landscaping, and parking areas. The project is generally located at 860 South Bluff Street.
      The applicant is Bart Suppes (Transitional Solutions LLC.) and the representative is Bart Suppes. The project
      will be known as Saint George Place. Case No. 2022-CUP-005. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Carol Davidson presented the following:

      Carol Davidson – The only changes from the last time are the buildings closest to Bluff Street in purple will be
      removed to add more parking and also there will be 3 buildings in the front along Bluff Street for restaurant pads.
      They are required to have 800 spaces; they are showing 839. There is pedestrian circulation. They will widen the
      access on Main Street. There is good pedestrian flow along the fronts of the stores and good flow along Bluff
      Street. The main concern staff had been that there is no access from the restaurants along Bluff to the shops. I
      spoke with them, and they are willing to add more pedestrian access. The 3 restaurant pads will have drive thrus.
      Carol described the traffic patterns of the drive thrus. Engineering had a chance to look at the stacking, they
      thought maybe they would have to change up the southernmost building along Bluff Street when they turn in their
      plans. They have added a lot of landscaping in the parking lot area. They will be removing the lawn along Bluff
      and Main Street and replacing it with xeriscape. Carol showed elevations of the buildings. The applicants

      The applicant works with staff improving pedestrian access point, they work with staff on the signage and work

      Commissioner Fisher – I am concerned about the stacking of the one restaurant, I didn’t see that as a condition on

      Carol Davidson – You can add that.

      Discussion continued on the stacking

      Bart Suppes – The reason that we would like to demo the buildings are that we have had very positive response to
      this plan, the respondents were concerned with parking, so it made sense to provide them with that. On the
      deliveries we would require them before 8:00 am in the morning. The view from Main Street will look
      completely different than it does now, both the walls and the buildings. We have a desire to emphasize Main
      Street. The owner owns the property across the street, and they are interested in developing that as well, but we
      need to do this one first. All of the respondents want to face Bluff Street because of the traffic. We are also open
      to working completely with the City with regards to the landscaping and the restrictions of landscaping.

      Lou Fung – As you can see it’s clearly a redevelopment, we are not just putting lipstick on the buildings. We
      wish we could disclose the tenants we are chasing, but we want a higher level of tenants in this space and in order
      to get those tenants we need to provide a nicer building and a nicer design. That is where some of these
      modifications and revisits to the parking flow and the parking areas came into effect. If we were to keep that
      northwest building, it’s really kind of a necked down area for parking and doesn’t provide them with adequate
      parking. So, in looking at some of the tenant needs and requirements there, opening that up and allowing that to
      be a large parking field, to improve the flow through the development as well. We are going to try and move the
      drive thrus closest to the front to get as much stacking as we can. We are being thoughtful and conscious of that
      as we coordinate these items with tenants.

      Chair Draper – Do you think you’ll have enough stacking at that south building that we talked about? If you
      move that further to the south, I’m not an architect but that’s just an idea.
