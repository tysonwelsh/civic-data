                                       ORDINANCE NO. 1451

         AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER
         CITY FOR APPROXIMATELY 2.55 ACRES OF PROPERTY FROM M1 (LIGHT
         MANUFACTURING) TO CSD-136C (136 CENTER COMMERCIAL SPECIAL
         DISTRICT), LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 38 WEST 13775 SOUTH
         WITHIN DRAPER CITY, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE HBA PLAZA AT
         DRAPER ZONING MAP AMENDMENT.

        WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and Zoning Map
to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

        WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Zoning Map to keep pace
with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of economic uses; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change set forth herein has been reviewed by the Planning
Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been held in accordance with
Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to the Zoning Map; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to the City
Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper City, and the City
Council has found the proposed zone change to be consistent with the City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

       Section 1.      Zoning Map Amendment. The following described real property located at
approximately 38 West 13775 South within Draper City, Salt Lake County, State of Utah, previously zoned
M1 as shown on the Draper City Zoning Map, as depicted in Exhibit “A” hereto, are hereby changed and
rezoned to CSD-136C:

Beginning at the intersection point of the Westerly Right-of-Way Line of Interstate 15 and the Northerly
Right-of-Way Line of Pony Express Road (13800 South), said point being South 137.79 feet and West
166.99 feet from the East Quarter Corner of Section 1, Township 4 South, Range 1 West, Salt Lake Base
and Meridian; and running

        thence Northwesterly 400.27 feet along the arc of a 446.41 foot radius curve to the left (center
bears South 54°31’07” West and the chord bears North 61°09'52” West 387.00 feet with a central angle
of 51 °22’27”) along said North Right-Of-Way Line of Pony Express Road to the easterly boundary line
of Bangerter 15 Condominiums, recorded as Entry No. 10057350 in Book 2007P at Page 156 in the
Office of the Salt Lake County Recorder;
        thence North 296.10 feet along said easterly boundary line of Bangerter 15 Condominiums to the
southerly boundary line of 136 Center Office Plat, recorded as Entry No. 12495731 in Book 2017P at
Page 52 in the Office of the Salt Lake County Recorder;
        thence South 89°56'19” East 49.35 feet along said southerly boundary line of 136 Center Office
Plat;
        thence continuing South 89°56’19” East 39.28 feet;
        thence South 38.25 feet;
        thence East 252.15 feet to the Westerly Right-of-Way Line of Interstate 15;
        thence South 00°13'38” West 444.41 feet along said Westerly Right-of-Way Line to the point of
beginning.
