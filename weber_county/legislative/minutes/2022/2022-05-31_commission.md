    7. APPROVAL OF A LOCAL FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH HARRISVILLE CITY TO ESTABLISH
       THE TERMS RELATED TO THE WEST HARRISVILLE ROAD PROJECT.
         Brooke Stewart (Community Development): Widen and improve 0.18 mile stretch from 1200 West to 1030
         West on the south side of West Harrisville Road. This work aligns with the section of roadway to the east
         of 1030 West being widened and improved with federal grant funding. Once complete, the improved
         sections will provide a uniform and safe roadway with 2 travel lanes in each direction and a center lane from
         highway 89 to 1200 West. The project was recommended for funding by WACOG and approved by the
         County Commission as part of the 2021 Transportation Priority list in November 2021. Harrisville City has
         secured the right-of-way needed for this project. This agreement outlines the terms related to Sales Tax
         Funds that are programmed for construction in 2024.

         Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of a Local Funding Agreement with Harrisville City to establish the
         terms related to the West Harrisville Road Project; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

    8. APPROVAL OF A LOCAL FUNDING AGREEMENT WITH PLAIN CITY TO ESTABLISH THE
       TERMS RELATED TO THE PHASE 3 OF THE NORTH PLAIN CITY ROAD PROJECT.
         Brooke Stewart (Community Development): Third and final phase of the North Plain City Road project will
         widen and improve 1.3 miles of roadway along North Plain City Road from 3300 West to 4200 West. The
         project was recommended for funding by WACOG and approved by the County Commission as part of the
         2021 Transportation Priority list in November 2021. The city is committing local matching funds to complete
         the project. This agreement outlines the terms related to Sales Tax Funds that are programmed for construction
         in 2024.

         Commissioner Froerer moved for approval of a Local Funding Agreement with Plain City to establish the terms
         related to the Phase 3 of the North Plain City Road Project; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
