Re: Providing Local Consent for an Off-Premise Beer alcohol license for Trader Joe's Company Comments: Trader Joe's Company located at 11479 S State St Unit B is seeking Local Consent for an Off-Premise Beer alcohol license.
Trader Joe's Company is in the process of obtaining a business license from Draper City and has provided the following:
1. Sales Tax License;
2. Floor Plan showing where alcohol is to be stored and sold.
The findings are as follows: Draper City Code 6-4-040 states the following: c.  No off-premises beer retail license , manufacturer license, or special use permit for a special use permittee operating a for-profit basis shall be issued wherein the premises would be located within three hundred feet (300')   of a community location as measured from the nearest patron entrance of the outlet by following the shortest route of ordinary pedestrian travel to the property boundary of the community location.
As positioned, Trader Joe's Company meets this standard.
Draper City Code  6-4-060 states the following:  C. Restriction On Licenses: The total number of off-premises licenses in the City shall not exceed one per each one thousand (1,000) population of the City based on the latest population estimate provided by the State of Utah Department of Workforce Services.
According to the Department of Workforce Services, as of 2020 Draper City has a total population of 51,017, meaning Draper may grant up to 51 off-premise beer licenses. The City currently has 21 off-premise beer licenses that have been granted Local Consent, leaving 30 more off-premise beer licenses to be granted.
