10-A IT STATES THAT THE FOLLOWING RECORDS ARE PROTECTED IF PROPERLY
CLASSIFIED BY THE GOVERNMENTAL ENTITY. RECORDS CREATED OR
MAINTAINED FOR CIVIL, CRIMINAL OR FOR ADMINISTRATIVE ENFORCEMENT
PURPOSES OR AUDIT PURPOSES OR FOR DISCIPLINE, LICENSING CERTIFICATION
