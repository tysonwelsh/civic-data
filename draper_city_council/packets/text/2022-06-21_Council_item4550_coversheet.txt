Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
CC:  Date: June 21, 2022
Re: Resolution #22-33, Intent to Adjust Draper City- Lehi City Common Boundary Comments: DR Horton has recently purchased land adjacent to the southern border of Draper City. They also own land in Lehi City directly across the municipal border. The City has been approached by DR Horton about adjusting the municipal boundary with Lehi City. The request would remove approximately 1,035.49 acres of land from Draper City and relocate the land into Lehi City.  DR Horton is looking to develop the 1,035.49 acres as part of a larger development taking place within Lehi City.
The approximately 1,035.49 acres of land does not have direct access to any existing road system or utility system within Draper City. Providing municipal services to the 1,035.49 acres may be more  efficiently  and economically provided by Lehi City. Given this fact, it may be  prudent  to review the request for municipal boundary adjustment.
In addition, DR Horton has mentioned a willingness to negotiate a conservation easement along the north portion of the 1,035.49 acres in order to protect open space.
Per Utah State Code 10-2-419, Draper City shall pass a resolution outlining its intent to adjust the common boundary between Draper City and Lehi City. A public hearing on the request shall be held no less than 60 days after the passage of the resolution. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. It is more efficient and economical for Lehi City to provide municipal services to the 1,035.49 Acres once they are developed.
2. The property owner has indicated a willingness to protect open space via a conservation easement.
2. The City Council hereby determines that it is interested in reviewing a municipal boundary adjustment with Lehi City.
3. The City intends to hold a public hearing to review the boundary adjustment no less than 60 days after adopting this resolution.
4. This Resolution complies with Utah State Code 10-2-419 requirements.
For denial:
1. The City Council hereby determines that it is not interested in reviewing a municipal boundary adjustment with Lehi City. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type Res 22-33 6/15/2022 Cover Memo
