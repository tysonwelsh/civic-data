                                      RESOLUTION NO. 20-18

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR OF THE CITY OF DRAPER
       TO APPOINT BRITNEE JOHNSTON AS COMMUNICATIONS DIRECTOR
       FOR THE CITY OF DRAPER.

        WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Britnee Johnston as Communications Director of the
City of Draper in accordance with the appointment procedures provided by law and City Ordinance; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City Municipal Code 2-5-020 requires the advice and consent of the City
Council in order for the Mayor to fill a vacancy in an appointed office; and

        WHEREAS, the Mayor desires to appoint Britnee Johnston as the Draper City Communications
Director; and

        WHEREAS, Britnee Johnston has the credentials, experience, and professionalism necessary to
be the Communications Director.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Appointment. The Draper City Council gives its consent to the Mayor’s appointment
as proposed and he may exercise his authorityto appoint Britnee Johnston as Communications Director,
in accordance with appointment procedures provided by law and City Code.

        Section 2. Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Resolution is held invalid or
unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this Resolution,
and all provisions, clauses, and words of this Resolution shall be severable.

        Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall take effect on March 19, 2020.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 19th DAY OF MARCH 2020.
