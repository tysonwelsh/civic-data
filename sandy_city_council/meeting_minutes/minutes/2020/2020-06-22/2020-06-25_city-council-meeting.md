                   There was a motion supported unanimously through voice vote to recess the City Council
                   meeting and convene a meeting of the Redevelopment Agency. Official actions of the
                   Redevelopment Agency will be reflected in the RDA minutes.

                   Dustin Fratto provided instruction on how to comment during this public hearing.

                   The resolution RD-20-01 passed unanimously.

                   There was a motion to resume City Council meeting which passed unanimously.

                   Mayor Bradburn offered perspectives on the budget and the budget development process.
                   He indicated he would be vetoing portions of Resolution 20-23C.

                   The Council discussed the legal ramifications and timing of veto consideration amongst
                   itself and with Tracy Cowdell and Bob Thompson.

                   There was a motion to adjourn by Zach Robinson and seconded by Brooke Christensen.
                   The motion failed:

                   Yes: Zach Robinson, Brooke Christensen, Monica Zoltanski
                   No: Alison Stroud, Kris Nicholl, Marci Houseman, Cyndi Sharkey

                   Council comments resumed.

                   There was a motion to adjourn by Brooke Christensen and seconded by Zach Robinson.
                   The motion passed:

                   Yes: Brooke Christensen, Zach Robinson, Marci Houseman, Cyndi Sharkey, Alison
                   Stroud

                   No: Kris Nicholl

                   Absent: Monica Zoltanski

Adjournment
                   The meeting adjourned at 6:59.
