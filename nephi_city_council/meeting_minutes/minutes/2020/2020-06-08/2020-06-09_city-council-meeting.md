                                Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                          June 9, 2020
                                            7:00 p.m.




   1.   Professor Flint from Utah State University discussed the results of the Well-Being
        Survey conducted earlier this year.

   2.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson presented a request from a resident to change the city
        code to allow swine in residential areas. The Council directed staff to not prepare a code
        change ordinance at this time.

   3.    Kyle Marchant, Public Works Director, presented information on a request to change the
        city’s policy on curb, gutter, and sidewalks.

   4.    Mr. Atkinson proposed a revenue shortfall policy for the Council to consider at their next
        regular meeting.

   5. Craig Oswald, presented some information on the operational costs and revenues for a
      recreation center proposal. This would inform the Council if an option for a recreation
      center becomes a possibility.

   6.    City Administrator Updates: Mr. Atkinson shared the latest financial information for city
        funds.

   7.   The City Councilors gave various updates.

   8.   Adjournment 9:30 pm.




Present: Mayor Nielson, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Justin
Seely, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Craig Oswald, Kyle Marchant, Jared Johnson, Alex
Johnson, Peggy Johnson, Clinton Painter.
