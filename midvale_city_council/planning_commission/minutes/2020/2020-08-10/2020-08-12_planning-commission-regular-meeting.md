3. TXT-20-06; ZONING ORDINANCE TEXT AMENDMENT; MODIFYING
LIGHTING AND SIGNAGE STANDARDS IN JORDAN BLUFFS SUBAREA 4
ZONE; ADAM LANKFORD/WASATCH DEVELOPMENT (APPLICANT)
Mr. Alex Murphy stated that in 2017, the City adopted a zoning ordinance for Subarea 4 of the
Jordan Bluffs zone. This ordinance established goals for the district, review process requirements,
and standards for residential development for the ~40 acres in the subarea. Wasatch Residential
Group owns all the private property in the district and is approaching completion of multifamily
residential projects on two of the three parcels available.
As these projects have been under review and construction, Wasatch has identified specific
lighting and signage requirements that they feel interfere with their ability to provide high quality
developments and, as a result, desires to see these standards modified. Pursuant to the
text amendment process described in §17-3-1 of the Midvale City Municipal Code, Staff and
Wasatch have been discussing the requested changes and a draft of the proposed changes is now
available for Planning Commission consideration.
PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION AUGUST 12, 2020

This text amendment proposes changes to the following sections:

17-7-10.11.4 Residential development standards.

This section includes changes to the lighting standards in subsection F. Specifically, subsection

(F)(1) has been modified to more clearly delineate the separation between requirements and

(F)(6) has been added to grant exceptions to specific lighting requirements for uplighting, street
lights, and string/bistro lighting. As proposed:

e Uplighting would be allowed up to a defined limit of lumens, based on property area,
provided no light trespass occurs across property lines.

e Street lights would be exempted from the 100,000 lumen/acre limit.

e String or bistro lighting would be exempted from the cutoff, down direction, and shielding
requirements.

17-7-10.11.9 Signage.
The proposed changes to this section would allow temporary signage. Temporary signage was
not originally approved when this zone was adopted. As proposed:

e Banner signs up to 80 SF would be permitted on building walls and fences. Wasatch has
requested that signage on temporary construction fencing be exempt from the size limit,
allowing all construction fencing to be covered in temporary signage. Staff recognizes why
Wasatch desires this change but does not recommend adopting it because of the quantity
of temporary signage that would be installed as a result.

e Other temporary signs (balloons/inflated displays, flags, feathers, etc.) would be permitted
for up to 30 days per quarter. A temporary sign permit would be required for each display
period. Other zones allow similar signage for up to 14 days per quarter; this proposal would
be the longest period allowed and, if approved, would likely be expanded
across all other zones too.

e Signs for grand opening periods are also proposed. Other zones allow grand opening
signage for up to 30 days in the first 90 days after opening. The proposed language would
extend that period for up to 18 months or 30 days after the final Certificate of Occupancy
for a project, which ever comes later.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:

The Planning Commission should consider if the proposed changes are desirable for the zone.
Except for the temporary construction fencing signage, Staff recommends forwarding a positive
recommendation to the City Council to adopt the proposed text amendment modifying lighting
and signage standards in the JB Subarea 4 zone.
