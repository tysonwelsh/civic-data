GENERAL PLAN/ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
GPZ-1-2022 Petition by ANDERSON WAHLEN & ASSOCIATES requesting a General Plan
           change from Neighborhood Commercial to General Commercial and a zone
           change from C-1 (Neighborhood Commercial) to C-2 (General Commercial).
           The property is located at 4065 S 5600 W and 5568 W 4100 S on 1.79 acres.
           (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman presented the application on behalf of Steve Pastorik. The
                 America First Credit Union is located on the southern lot and the parcel to the
                 north is vacant. Steve said that the applicant is proposing to add a Starbucks
                 with a drive-thru on the vacant lot and that is not permitted in the C-1 zone.
                 Therefore, they are requesting the property be rezoned to C-2 which will
                 allow the drive-thru. Steve stated that there would be a shared access off of
                 4100 South and a second access off of 5600 West. He pointed out that the
                 existing masonry wall will be extended to run along the entire east boundary
                 of the subject property.
                 Commissioner McEwen asked if there was a way to eliminate the access off
                 of 4100 South leading to the proposed Starbucks. He feels this could be a
                 traffic issue. Steve said that there is an agreement for that shared access as
                 part of the subdivision plat. He added that the access off of 4100 South is
                 crucial for the drive-thru.

                 Commissioner Winters asked if there would be a downside to approving the
                 rezone. Steve said that he does not see this zone change being detrimental due
                 to the location of the property and the surrounding uses.

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SV-1-2022    Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY ENGINEERING DIVISION requesting
             consideration to vacate all of 4695 South. The portion to be vacated is also
             known as the old 4700 South Frontage Road. The address of the vacation is
             4695 South and 4110 West. (Staff - Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
             steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman explained that subject property was platted before West Valley
                 City’s incorporation. He stated that the subject property gave an additional
                 way to access the subdivision north of 4700 South. Steve said that this access
                 was found to be problematic, and the City blocked the entry. He noted that
                 half would be deeded to the property owner to the south and the northern half
                 to the City if this proposal is approved.

                Commissioner Lovato pointed out the unique shape of the property depicted in
                the subdivision plat. He asked Steve if there was a way to alter the property
                line making it more of a rectangular shape. According to state law, Steve said
                that the right-of-way is to be split down the middle, and each half would go to
                the adjacent property owner. He noted that this does not preclude the two
                landowners from negotiating to modify the subdivision.

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SD-1-2022    Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY ENGINEERING DIVISION requesting
             consideration to dedicate a portion of 4715 South adjacent to the SL County
             linear park. The proposed application also dedicates a portion of Arlington
             Park Drive, Dartmouth Street, and Vassar Street. The proposed dedication
             plat is located at approximately 4715 South between 4130 4730 West. (Staff -
             Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:      Steve Lehman stated that this request is to dedicate the north side of 4715
                 South and the three stub sections leading to the subdivisions to the north. He
                 mentioned that this right-of-way was dedicated before West Valley City’s
                 incorporation.

                 Steve said while discussing this application with our engineering department,
                 Steve Dale, with the City’s Engineering Division, explained the reason for the
                 request to dedicate the three stub sections. Mr. Dale explained that the section
                  line was used as a boundary line, and those sections are south of that
                  boundary. Therefore, they were not dedicated.

MISCELLANEOUS APPLICATIONS
M-1-2022          Petition by DARIN CISNEROS requesting an exception to the requirements
                  of 7-10-103 due to landscaping impacts from right-of-way widening at 5610
                  W 3500 South. (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
                  brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Brock Anderson explained that the subject property lost a considerable
                  amount of landscaping on the east side due to UDOT’s improvements along
                  5600 West. He stated that the exception to the required twenty feet of
                  landscaping would be needed along the east side of the property in question
                  and on the west side of the drive approach adjacent to 3500 South. Brock
                  noted that the landscaped area on the west side of the drive approach is
                  approximately two and a half feet shy of the requirement. However, they
                  would lose a parking space if the exception were not granted.

                  Brock explained that the applicant had added landscaping before receiving
                  approval from the City. Therefore, some of the existing landscaping will need
                  to be modified. He stated that staff recommends 50% live plant material be
                  required along 5600 West. The area is too narrow for lawn and is also in
                  close proximity to the existing park strip trees that UDOT installed, so trees
                  should not be required in this area either. Brock said that the south side of the
                  site needs to meet the current requirements listed in 7-10, which includes a
                  minimum of 50% lawn. Trees also need to be added.

                  Commissioner McEwen asked if it is an option for them to keep the xeriscape.
                  Brock said that isn’t an option currently. He explained that staff has been
                  looking into amending the code to allow artificial turf as an option.

                  Commissioner Winters feels that keeping the xeriscape would be more
                  aesthetically pleasing than having one patch of lawn in the middle. He asked
                  Brock if the applicant is meeting the 50% requirement currently. Brock said
                  he would need to request a current landscape plan to determine the
                  percentage.
