         The purpose of the Joint Work Session was to discuss proposed Election and Campaign Amendments; proposed Zone
Text Amendments limiting Multiple-Family Dwellings in C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3 Zones (Continued from October 1, 2024);
receive an Administrative Update; discuss Council/Board business; and hear Council/Board comments.

Proposed Election and Campaign Amendments
          City Recorder Hansen addressed a proposal to amend two sections of the Municipal Code related to campaign finance
and political activity. The first proposed ordinance repeals Municipal Code §1-8 related to campaign finance disclosure and
readopts Municipal Code §1-8 to align with current State Code. The proposed changes include adopting Utah Code §10- 3-208
relating to campaign finance disclosure, increasing the limits on donations from a single individual, prohibiting the use of photos
of City uniformed officers and employees for political purposes, and requiring permission to use names and photos of City
officers and employees. The second proposed ordinance amends Ogden Municipal Code §2-6-13 related to political activity to
prohibit the use of photographs that include uniformed City officers or employees for political purposes.
          Ms. Hansen engaged in discussion with the Council regarding the implications of the proposed ordinance amendments
for candidates who use social media for campaigning purposes; the importance of aligning the Municipal Code with State Code
for purposes of consistency in the application of campaign finance regulations; the ability of the City to enforce the updated
campaign donation rules; and opportunities for increasing public awareness of campaign finance regulations and access to finance
reports once they have been filed. There was also a discussion about possibly increasing the donation thresholds for both City
Council and Mayor candidates, with Council Executive Director Eller-Smith indicating that it would be appropriate to discuss the
matter further in a future work session before action is scheduled for a Council meeting.

Proposed Zone Text Amendments Limiting Multiple-family Dwellings in C-
2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3 Zones (Continued from October 1, 2024)
         Planning Manager Brierley explained the proposed code amendment was presented to the Council during the October 1
work session meeting; the amendment proposes to prohibit new multiple family dwellings, single-family rowhouses, and duplexes
in the C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3 zones. The Planning Commission heard the proposal on July 3, 2024 and made a recommendation
to approve the proposed amendment. On May 28, 2024 the City issued a notice of pending ordinance to prevent new applications
for multiple-family dwellings, single-family rowhouses, and duplexes in the C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3 zones from being submitted
until the proposed ordinance amendment was voted on. Utah Code (10-9a-509) allows a municipality to reject land use
applications for 180 days, if the municipality initiates pending legislation in a manner provided by local ordinance, and if the
proposal would be prohibited under the pending ordinance. The pending ordinance period of 180 days will expire on November
24, 2024. The Council requested additional information and an opportunity for continued discussion of the proposal. The
questions that were asked of Planning staff include:
              1. Can you please provide some examples of mixed-use group dwellings?
              2.  Roughly how many (non-mixed-use) multiple-family dwellings are there currently in the C-2/CP-2 and C-
                  3/CP-3 zones?
              3. Are there any active applications being processed for multiple-family dwellings in these zones?
              4. Are there enough acres of land currently zoned for multiple-family residential to accommodate the forecasted
                  population at build-out?
         Council member Myers added that he also heard from a constituent who was concerned that the proposed ordinance
amendment would prohibit ‘mother-in-law’ apartments in the City, and this was very concerning to him. Mr. Brierley stated that
the use would be prohibited in the zones that have been identified in the staff report. The Council and staff discussed opportunities
for exempting that type of land use from the proposal, with Mr. Brierley noting that staff and the Planning Commission felt that
the commercial zones were not the most appropriate location for that type of land use as well as other types of multi-family
dwellings.
         The Council then engaged in discussion on the implications of the proposed ordinance amendment; this included review
of a map provided by Planning staff to identify C-2/CP-2 and C-3/CP-3 zones in the City, public transportation routes, and
discussion of development opportunities for specific properties if the proposed ordinance amendment were approved.

Administrative Update
        Chief Administrative Officer Brown reported on customer service improvement initiatives in the City; the status of
ongoing road and commercial construction projects in the community; and the status of the paid parking program for downtown
Ogden and plans for revisiting the matter and meeting with stakeholders before moving forward with the program.

Council/Board Business
        Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reminded Council members to complete their ranking of Capital Improvement
Plan (CIP) projects as requested by City Administration.

Council/Board Comments
        There were no additional Council/Board comments.


         The meeting adjourned at 6:45 p.m.
