I. Prayer 
An opening prayer was offered before the Board Meeting commenced. 
II. Public Comments 
There were no public comments. 
 
III. DISCUSSION ONLY ITEMS 
 
A. Presentation: 360 Management Review 
 
            The HR consultant provided a progress update on the 360-degree management review 
questionnaire and reviewed the upcoming processes and procedures. The consultant was 
asked to finalize the questionnaire so it could be distributed to all Board Members for 
review. 
 
B. Presentation: Retirement Benefits 
 
A retirement plan consultant from Gallagher reviewed the agency's retirement plan 
benefits.   
 
C. Non-Profit Meeting Schedule 
 
The Board discussed scheduling the first meeting for the non‑profit entity. Additionally, the 
accounting considerations related to the non‑profit organization were reviewed. 
 
The Executive Director reminded the board that he holds a board seat for the non‑profit. 
 
The Board determined that the first meeting of the non-profit entity will take place on 
March 26, 2026, immediately following the regular board meeting

D. Board Retreat 
 
 Board Members were asked whether their schedules would allow them to attend the board 
retreat scheduled for June 4, 2026, in Midway, Utah. 
 
 All board members confirmed that their schedules would permit their attendance. 
 
IV. Executive Director’s Report 
 
The Executive Director provided updates on the 2025 tax returns, the April audit balance, and 
staffing matters. He also announced that the agency may be awarded additional grant funding 
from Utah County. He noted that the Accounting Manager would be presenting the January 
financials, and the Operations Director would be presenting the Section 8 report during today’s 
meeting. In addition, he reviewed multiple ongoing development projects. 
 
V. Commissioner’s Report 
 
A Commissioner’s report was not provided. 
 
VI. Utah County Status Update (Claudia Barillas) 
 
Utah County’s CPP Grants were discussed. Additional information regarding the grants is 
expected to be available in March. 
VII. Financial Statement Report 
 The Agency’s financial statements were reviewed with the Board.

The voucher program was reviewed with the Board
