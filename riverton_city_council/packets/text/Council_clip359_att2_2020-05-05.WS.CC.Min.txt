18       a. Ordinance No. 20-12 - Approving a Text Amendment, Adding Chapter 2.150,
19          Administrative Interpretations, to the Riverton City Code, Proposed by Riverton
20          City
21
22   Development Services Direct Jason Lethbridge explained that the public hearing was for an
23   amendment to the Riverton City Code proposed by Riverton City. He said the proposed
24   amendment adds a Chapter 2.150, Administrative Interpretations to Title 2 of the Riverton City
25   Code, which formalizes a process that already occurs. He explained that City Staff frequently
26   makes interpretations of the Riverton City Code, which most typically occurs through the
27   Planning Department. He said that for example, the Table of Commercial Uses Chart includes a
28   category for “Beauty and Barber Services” but not a category specific to nail salons. When a nail
29   salon contacts the City seeking a business license for a particular location, the Department
30   makes an interpretation as to whether a nail salon would be considered “beauty and barber
31   services”, rather than forward this action to the Planning Commission and City Council. The
32   authority to make such administrative interpretations is already established in the Riverton City
33   Code; however, a formal procedure for issuing an interpretation, especially when requested by
34   an applicant, does not. He said the proposed amendment adds the Administrative
35   Interpretations section to City Code, and formalizes the process.
36

38          Maximum of 6 Units per Acre, with Specific Development Designations Limiting

40
41   Jason Lethbridge, Community Development Director, explained that a public hearing was held
42   for a rezone of several properties totaling about 12.8 acres located on the east side of Redwood
43   Road at approximately 11845 South. He said the subject properties were currently a mix of
44   zoning with single family residential (RR-22 and R-3), Professional Office and Neighborhood
45   Commercial Zones. He said the current General Plan was a mix of Medium Density Residential
46   and Professional Office. He said that nearby zoning included R-4 and R-3 to the south and R-3
47   across the canal to the east, and to the north is RR-22 zoning with larger lots. Across Redwood
48   Road to the west is RM-8 south of 11800 South and RR-22 north of 11800 South.
49
50   Mr. Lethbridge said the applicant requested RM-6 SD, which is residential multifamily, 6 units
51   per acre. The “SD” stands for Specific Development and is a means to apply special rules and
     Page | 10 Minutes – City Council Meeting

 1   regulations for Report by: TJP 4/28/2020 a development. In this application the SD would assign
 2   areas within the overall development specific lot sizes and unit types.
 3
 4   Mr. Lethbridge further explained that following discussion with the Commission at a work
 5   session, the applicant further modified the proposed density and layout for the project, and the
 6   proposed SD designations were based on that layout as follows: Essentially, the project area
 7   would be divided into three sections as follows:
 8
 9            Area 1 would consist of the southern portion of the properties. There would be a
10            maximum of 14 lots in that area, with a minimum lot size of 10,000 square feet. Other
11            than lot size, all setbacks and other standards for this area would be consistent with the
12            R-4 zone.
13            Area 2, which is the northern portion, would be developed consistent with the detached
14            single-family requirements of the RM-6 zone. This includes a minimum lot size of 5,000
15            square feet. The lots adjacent to the north and east boundaries of this area will be a
16            minimum of 6,000 square feet. There will be a maximum of 29 lots in that area.
17            Area 3 is the smaller parcel just to the south of the existing commercial site on Redwood
18            Road. This property may be developed consistent with the attached unit standards in the
19            RM-6 zone, with the exception that up to 5 units may be attached. A maximum of 10
20            units will be allowed in this area. This is reflected on the included diagram.
21
22   The RM-6 zone includes a requirement that perimeter fencing comply with Section 18.155.080
23   of the Riverton City Code where development is adjacent to a single-family zone. That section
24   requires a six foot solid masonry fence, and so for this project area the fencing would be six foot
25   solid masonry for the entire project boundary. The RM-6 zone also includes a requirement for
26   open space. Section 18.45.180(11) specifies that where individual lots are utilized in a project
27   over 10 acres, which is the case in this proposed project, a minimum of one (1) acre of open
28   space. The ordinance requires that open space to be privately maintained, unless otherwise
29   approved. The conceptual plans show open space that complies with the minimum area
30   requirement, and specific treatment and configuration would be approved as part of a site plan
31   application.
32
33   Mr. Lethbridge said the question of road connections has been a significant issue with the
34   application. Laurel Chase Drive currently stubs to the southern boundary of this project area,
35   and the northwest corner of the project area is the intersection of Redwood Road and 11800
36   South. Much has been discussed on the potential connection of Laurel Chase Drive to that
37   signalized intersection, and the possible impacts of that connection on the surrounding road
38   network. The applicant has presented several conceptual layouts showing alternative road
39   connections. Traffic impacts are typically reviewed with a rezone, and are an important
40   consideration in this case. But the road connection is not specific to this application or to a
41   particular density. He said the City would need to address the road connection irrespective of
42   the eventual density of this site. Final technical design of a roadway system is typically made
43   with the site plan/subdivision design for a project. He said the Council may consider adding
44   language such as the following, or other similar direction:
45
46       •    Laurel Chase Drive shall be connected to 11800 South as a full public right-of-way.
