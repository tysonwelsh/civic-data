Regular Agenda 
#1 Discussion: General Plan, Regional Collaboration Plan, Urban and Rural Area Assessment, and 
Cost of Services Plan review and discussion 
Ryan reviewed the information for the general plan, regional collaboration plan, urban and rural area 
assessment, and cost of services plan. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the zip code graphic, wildlife corridors, and what visit-ability means 
and making sure it is defined in the glossary of terms. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed clustering in the FR40 zone, the urban expansion overlay, 
commercial areas, and what special service districts are, if a branding plan for Cache Valley needs to be 
included, and collaborating with cities on growth and zoning, and developers paying for services to 
subdivisions being developed and cost of services. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed the Regional Collaboration Plan and making sure wording is 
inclusive of new residents to Cache Valley. 
Watkins reviewed the Cost of Services Plan. 
Staff and Commissioners discussed how the cost of services plan is a supplemental document, growth 
trends and patterns, and how the analysis was created and that they will be appendixes to the general 
plan. 
Staff informed the Commission that the documents will be made public after this meeting.
