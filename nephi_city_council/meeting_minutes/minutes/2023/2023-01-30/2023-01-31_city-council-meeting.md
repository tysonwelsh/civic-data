                             Nephi City Council Work Session Minutes
                                         January 31, 2023
                                            6:30 p.m.
                                           43 E. 200 N.
                                         Nephi, UT 84648


    1. The City Council met at the Nephi City Police Department building. Tiffany Lund took
       the Council on a tour of the Police Department.

    2.   Mayor Seely opened the meeting for public comment:

            a. Casie Morgan addressed the council and stated that she was a resident of
               Nephi and married to Chief Mike Morgan. She said that she knows that a new
               police department facility is something that is dear to the Chief’s heart and that
               he has been trying to get this for his officers for quite some time. He loves all of
               them and wants the best for all of them and the city.
            b. Chief Mike Morgan addressed the council and said he wanted the council to
               know that the people attending the meeting were not there to show any
               aggression or negatively towards the council. He stated that his officers have
               been asking him non stop if they were going to getting a new police station.
               When the Chief found out about this public meeting he told his officers that
               they could come and here right from the council so that he didn’t have to pass
               on the information. He expressed his appreciation to the council and to Mr.
               Atkinson for their support of the police department. He appreciated the
               council’s support of hiring a new drug task force officer and another school
               resource officer.

    3.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed the department budget requests and asked the council to
         prioritize the requests 1 through 34. The requests were divided into three categories:
         one time funding, ongoing funding, and enterprise fund requests. After much
         discussion and review of requests, each councilor prioritized the requests and Mr.
         Atkinson tabulated the results and told the council that as he prepares the biennial
         budget, he will use the council’s priorities as a guide in budgeting.

    4.   Meeting adjourned at 9:00 p.m.
