GPZ-3-2022
Maria Elena Dubon
4543 and 4547 S 4000 W
General plan change from non-retail commercial to general commercial
and zone change from C-1 to C-2
1.79 acres

Maria Elena Dubon has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for two parcels
totaling 0.73 acres located at 4543 and 4547 S 4000 W. Both properties are currently zoned RB
(Residential Business) with a General Plan designation of non-retail commercial. The proposed
zone is C-2 (General Commercial) and the proposed General Plan designation is general
commercial.

Surrounding zones include C-2 to the south, RB to the west, R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling
Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet) to the north, and R-4 (Four Unit Dwelling
Residential) to the east. Surrounding land uses include a fast food restaurant to the south, a home
to the west, a mobile home park to the north, and a fourplex to the east. The subject property is
currently licensed as day care.

If this application is approved, the applicant plans to build a new restaurant on the subject
property. The existing structures on the property would be demolished. Included with this report
is a concept plan for the proposed restaurant which would include a drive-thru.

If this application is approved, the requirements in Section 7-7-111 would apply to the
neighboring residential property lines. These requirements include the following:

       “Where Fast Food Establishments with drive-up windows are located adjacent to a
       Residential Zone or Use, the order board, speakers, and pick-up window shall be located
       at least 50' from the nearest residential property line and any sound emitting from the
       order board, speakers, and pickup window shall not be audible from the nearest
       residential property line between 10 PM and 7 AM.”

As reflected in the concept plan, the applicant is requesting an exception to the 20’ setback along
the north property through a development agreement. The C-2 zone does allow for parking areas
to encroach into the setback by 10’ without a development agreement if certain conditions are
met. However, one of those conditions is that the site is not adjacent to or across the street from a
residential zone or use. In this case, the property is adjacent to residential zoning and a fourplex
to the east and is across the street from residential zoning and residential uses to the north and
west.

The applicant’s request for an exception is based on the following:

      The concept plan shows 22.6% landscaping. The C-2 zone requires a minimum of 15%,
       so the site would exceed the percentage of landscaping required with the exception.
      The exception is requested on the side street of Benview Avenue which is the lower
       traffic volume street.
      The proposed 10’ of landscaping along the north side of the property would include a
       berm and enhanced landscaping.

Staff shared the concept plan with the Public Works Department for preliminary comment. Brad
Taylor with Public Works indicated that the curb cut along 4000 West was too close to the curb
cut to the south. They suggested that the applicant work with landowner to the south to share the
existing access.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development agreement that
      includes the following provisions:
          a. The landscaped setback along the north side of the property may be reduced to
             10’. The building shall be setback at least 20’ from the north property line.
          b. At least 20% of the site shall be landscaped.
          c. The 10’ landscaped setback along the north side of the property shall include the
             following:
                   i. a 3’ tall masonry wall along the south side of the landscaping,
                  ii. a berm sloping up to the 3’ tall masonry wall, and
                 iii. live plant material covering at least 75% of the 10’ landscaped setback.

   2. Approval. This application should be approved. No exceptions are recommended.

   3. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
      public hearing.

   4. Denial. The RB zone should remain.

CA-1-2022
Walmart Fuel Station
4570 S. 4000 W.
C-2 Zone (8.01/.64 Acres)

The applicant, Kimley Horn representing Walmart, is requesting a conditional use amendment
for fuel center for property located at 4570 South 4000 West. This property is zoned C-2,
General Commercial and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area as General
Commercial. The surrounding zone directly adjacent to the fuel center location is all zoned C-2.
The surrounding uses include the Walmart Neighborhood Market to the west, a carwash to the
northeast, and a retail strip center to the south.
The existing Walmart facility was originally approved as a conditional use in 2002 under C-1-
2002. A fuel center was part of that original development but has since been removed. At this
time Walmart would like to reinstall the fuel center in the same location as the previous station
within the existing parking lot. The fueling center includes 12 fueling positions and a 440 sqft
convenience kiosk. The building must be designed in accordance with the Commercial Design
Standards for Separate Structures with a painted wainscot so it is consistent with the primary
building design. The gas canopy support posts will have the same wainscot design. There is also
an exterior mechanical equipment area that will need to be screened with a masonry wall per 7-
11-316.

The interior parking lot design does not need to be modified to accommodate this facility. No
parking stalls are being displaced for this location since it was originally a fuel center. Therefore,
parking complies with the original approval and City Code. The landscaping directly adjacent to
the fuel center needs to be updated to include the original required trees but the overall site
landscaping appears to be in compliance with the original landscape plan. A site inspection will
be conducted before occupancy of the fuel center to ensure compliance.

There is an existing monument sign that will be modified to add an electronic gas price panel.
There will also be signs on the gas canopy and kiosk structure itself. All signage will be required
to meet the regulations outlined in WVC Sign Code, Title 11.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

           1. The overall site landscaping must be installed per the original approved plan (C-1-
              02) and any landscaping modifications that are done to accommodate the fuel
              center shall comply with the WVC Municipal Code.

           2. The kiosk building and gas canopy must be completed per the approved plans and
              shall be designed in accordance with the Commercial Design Standards section 7-
              11-312(4). This includes a painted wainscot on the kiosk building and the canopy
              support posts.

           3. The exterior mechanical equipment area shall be screened with a masonry wall
              per 7-11-316.

           4. Signage must comply with the West Valley City Code.

           5. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

           6. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

C-4-2022
Back 9 Apartments
2600 W 3800 S
RM Zone (3.65 acres)

CIR Engineering is requesting conditional use approval to construct a 262-unit apartment
development. The property is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and is
designated as very high density residential in the City’s General Plan. The project will have 2
studio units, 176 one-bedroom units, and 84 two-bedroom units. The unit sizes will meet the
minimum requirements of the Development Agreement.


Background
This property, located directly west of Costco, is undeveloped. In August of 2019 the site
received RM zoning approval, which included a Development Agreement (DA) for the project.


Project Review
There will be one access to the property from 3800 South. The applicant is also proposing to
have some surface parking that would be accessed from the east side of the site, which will
require an access easement from the Costco property. The proposed building is 3 and 5
stories. The building along 3800 South cannot exceed 3 stories, as outlined in the DA, but
will then step up to 5 stories for the back portion of the building. There will be a parking
garage in the center of the 5-story section of the building, with units wrapping the parking
garage. Above the garage, on the 4th floor will be a center courtyard area, and the upper two
floors of the building will have units wrapping the courtyard.

Parking
Parking for the project will be provided by a parking garage as well as some surface stalls on
the site. The parking garage will have one level below grade, and then three levels above that.
The DA outlined the parking requirements for this project, which was 424 total parking
spaces. The calculations were based on the following:

       Unit Type             Unit Count          Req. Stalls/Rm         Required Stalls
                                                      Type
 1 Bedroom                               149                  1.5                       224
 2 Bedroom                               111                 1.75                       194
 3 Bedroom                                 3                    2                         6
 Totals                                  263                                            424
The unit type and count has changed slightly from what is outlined in the DA. The breakdown
of unit type and number has been adjusted:

       Unit Type             Unit Count          Req. Stalls/Rm         Required Stalls
                                                      Type
 Studio                                    2                  1.5                         3
 1 Bedroom                               176                  1.5                       264
 2 Bedroom                                84                 1.75                       147
 Totals                                  262                                            414

Based on the new unit count and mix, 414 stalls are required for the project. The applicant is
proposing 418 stalls. 355 stalls will be in the parking garage, and the other 63 stalls will be
surface stalls on the site. The DA required 303 garage parking stalls.

Site and Landscaping
Public Works has reviewed the project and indicated that they will require additional right-
of-way dedication along 3800 South. The property line for the parcel will need to move 10
additional feet to the north. This significantly impacts the current proposal, as the building
needs to be at least 25’ from the new right-of-way line. The site plan and building need to
be adjusted accordingly. The site plan shows the 6’ required masonry wall indicated in the
DA along the west property line.

A pedestrian connection to the mall and Costco was required by the DA. When the Panda
Express site developed, they provided a pedestrian connection from the north-east corner of
the site and along the north-east side of their property. The applicant needs to continue that
pedestrian connection over to the mall. There is an existing sidewalk running east to west
from Costco to this site, which includes striping across the road on the west side. A sidewalk
connection from the building to the crosswalk on the east side shall be provided.

The DA indicates that “the amount and location of landscaping shall be substantially like the
concept plan in Exhibit C”. The current proposal does not include the pool, sport court, and
landscaped area that was originally shown in the DA in the south-east corner of the site. This
open space and landscaped area needs to be provided on the site as originally proposed, or it
needs to be replaced elsewhere on the site or in the building.

For other areas of the site, the landscaping shown is comparable to what was provided in the
DA agreement. The current plan indicates there will be trees in the park strip.
Typically, the required trees should be located between the sidewalk and the building, but the
Planning Commission could allow the trees in the park strip as the applicant has proposed. To
the east of the site, the Costco property has trees in the park strip, so putting the trees in the
park strip here would match what has already been done.

Amenities
The DA required the following amenities for the project: community room with full kitchen,
on site-manager, fitness room, private balconies, sports court, covered pavilions,
swimming pool, barbeques, fire pit, tot lot, hot tub, bicycle storage, and a business center with
Wi-Fi and computers for residents. The community room with kitchen, sports court, covered
pavilions, barbeques, fire pit, tot lot and business center are not called out clearly on the plans.
As mentioned previously in this report, there is also the issue that the area for amenities has
decreased from the original proposal. That area needs to be replaced elsewhere on the site or
in the building. The DA also requires an entry feature for the project, and the applicant has not
provided details for this.

Architecture
The materials allowed for the exterior of the building that were included in the DA are:
Techlam Corten Steel, Metal Composite Panel, Vertical Corrugated Metal Cladding, Marazzi
Tile and EIFS System. The applicant has proposed the following materials for the building:
brick, metal panel, metal corrugated panel, stucco, and aluminum wood siding. If the Planning
Commission is okay with allowing some materials not included in the DA, the types and
amounts of materials should at least be comparable. The DA also requires that “all facades of
the building shall be finished with the same materials and level of detail as shown for the east
façade in the renderings”. The east elevation that was submitted is lacking in some of the
details and materials that were proposed in the DA, and the other three elevations are also
lacking.

There are several things that need to be addressed for this project. The main items are:

       Adjust the site plan to accommodate for right-of-way dedication so that the
        building and parking meet the required setbacks.
       Provide more information and details for all the required amenities listed in the
        Development Agreement.
       Provide additional amenity space to accommodate for the pool and sports court area
        that was removed from Exhibit C of the Development Agreement. The new areas
        should be comparable in size to the area the was eliminated.
       Update building elevations so that they are substantially like the renderings
        provided in Exhibit E of the Development Agreement. Specific examples of
        items that need to be addressed include:
            o Using at least the same amount of brick on the updated elevations as there was
                tile on the Development Agreement renderings,
            o Modifying the elevations so that each elevation has the same level of
                detail,
            o Modifying the east elevation so that the 3-story south portion is more like the
                renderings in the Development Agreement, and
            o Making the entry areas more prominent (taller, use of glass, more contrast in
                color) as seen in the Development Agreement renderings.
        Include an entry feature proposal.
        Provide proof of an access easement through Costco’s property. If an access
         easement has not been secured, modify the site plan accordingly.
        Supply plans showing how the pedestrian connection to the mall required in the
         Development Agreement will be provided.

 Based on the number of site and building related issues, staff recommends that the
 Planning Commission continue this application to give the applicant additional time to
 address the issues and provided updated plans.



 Staff Alternatives

C-5-2022
ARK Plaza
1927 West 3600 South
Retail/Warehouse Center
C-2 Zone (1.03 Acres)

The applicant, Brett Carter representing ARK Plaza, is requesting a conditional use approval for
a multi tenant commercial/warehouse building located at 1927 West 3600 South. This property
is zoned C-2, General Commercial, and the West Valley City General Plan designates this area
as General Commercial. Surrounding zones include A (agricultural) and C-2 to the north, A
(agricultural) to the west, C-2 to the east, and RM to the south. Surrounding uses include
residential and medical office (Planned Parenthood) to the north, multi-family residential to the
west and south (Homestead Farms) and office/warehouse to the east (Interwest Brokerage).

This site is Lot 8 of the Plaza 3600 Business Park which was approved by the Planning
Commission in 1998 (C-26-98). Access to the site is gained off of 3600 South, which is a private
road. The original approval specified that each development must be reviewed as a separate
conditional use before the Planning Commission since the tenants were not known at the time of
the overall approval, furthermore this particular use is also listed as a conditional use in the C-2
zone.
The proposed building will be approximately 13,427 sq. ft. and contain 11 units. The applicant
anticipates having uses such as window tinting, auto accessories (retail), a print shop, hair salon,
insurance office, furniture/appliance shop, etc. There would be no auto mechanic or body work
shops and no outside storage. To accommodate these variety of uses 10 of the units have
overhead doors that could provide interior access for warehouse (no more than 50%) and provide
better maneuvering area for larger merchandise.

The building will be constructed out of masonry block with a mixture of colors and textures,
architectural banding and metal architectural awnings. Staff recommends that the overhead
doors be painted to match the adjacent walls so they blend into the façade. The building design
must comply with the Commercial Design Standards.

The landscaping on site must meet the 15% minimum area as required in the C-2 Zone. The
landscaping/setbacks along the north frontage of this parcel were approved as part of the original
overall subdivision and private street concept. This includes a minimum setback for the building
of 40’ from the property line, which is the center of the private road, as well as a 7-foot
landscaped setback for the parking to provide an area for street trees that were originally planted
when the roadway was improved. The site will also have a 5’ strip of landscaping adjacent to the
parking lot on the east and a 20’ landscaped area along the west adjacent to 1950 West. The
south side is adjacent to residential so a 10’ wide landscaped area is required there. These areas
must all include 50% live plant material and trees.


Since the property is adjacent to residential a 6’ tall solid masonry wall is required that will be
painted/finished to match the building. A similar masonry enclosure will also be constructed
around the dumpster located on the site.

A lighting plan will be provided during the permit review and Staff will work with the applicant
to ensure that the site complies with the standards set forth in the West Valley City Code
including the light levels adjacent to residential uses.

Only wall signage is proposed for this site. All signage shall comply with the West Valley City
Sign Ordinance, to include no more than 15% signage on the front building face, and 5% on the
remaining sides, and no more than 50% of the window area shall be covered in signs. A building

C-6-2022
H20 Lofts
2892 S Decker Lake Dr RM
Zone (16.05 acres)


Joe Salisbury is requesting conditional use approval to construct a 286-unit apartment
development. The property is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) and is
designated as high density residential in the City’s General Plan. The site is located next to
office developments to the east and south, the E-Gate Apartments and a juvenile detention
facility to the west, and a County park to the north.


Background
The project is proposed for two vacant parcels that have frontage on Decker Lake Drive and
Decker Lane. In June of 2021 the site received RM zoning approval, which included a
Development Agreement (DA) for the project.


Project Review
There is an existing access from Decker Lake Dr. that will be utilized for the development,
and a second access will be added in the south-east corner of the site. There are also three
proposed access points from Decker Ln. The project includes two different housing types; a
two-story, 3-bedroom townhome product, and what the applicant refers to as a FlexGen
product that has a 1-bedroom unit on the first floor, and a 2-bedroom unit on the second floor,
but is designed with connecting doors so that both floors can be rented out as one large unit.
The DA outlines the unit mix as: 98 3-bedroom townhomes, 94 2-bedroom units, and 94 1-
bedroom units. The applicant is proposing to alter the unit mix slightly and is proposing the
following: 94 3-bedroom townhomes, 96
2-bedroom units, and 96 1-bedroom units. The unit sizes will meet the minimum
requirements of the Development Agreement. The change is in part because the applicant
had to re-work the orientation of units in the northern area of the site. The Planning
Commission needs to authorize the change in unit mix.
Parking
Parking for the project will be provided inside garages, as well as surface stalls on the site.
The DA required that there be a minimum of 203 surface parking stalls and a minimum of
221 driveway stalls. The breakdown of the parking for the proposed project is: 380 garage
stalls, 203 surface stalls, and 225 driveway stalls, for a total of 808 parking stalls. Title 7
requires that “all dwelling units that are stacked on top of each other shall have at least one
parking space in a garage per dwelling unit” (7-11-404(9)). The DA also specifies that all 3-
bedroom townhome units have a 2-car garage, and each
1- bedroom and 2-bedroom units shall have 1-car garages. The proposal indicates that all of
the townhome-style units will have a 2-car garage. In the original proposal, all the FlexGen
units were to have a 1-car garage for the first floor 1-bedroom units, and a 1-car garage for the
second floor 2-bedroom units. As originally proposed, the two single car garages were located
side-by-side, but would have been separated by a wall. After doing some market research, the
applicant would like to provide 52 of the FlexGen 2-bedroom units with a 2-car garage. That
would mean that 52 1-bedroom units would not have a garage space to park in. The applicant
has indicated that there would be a reserved surface parking space for these units. There
would still be the same number of garage spaces in this scenario, but 52 of the 1-bedroom
units would not have a garage space.

Site and Landscaping
The applicant provided a pedestrian path exhibit for the project. All pedestrian walkways that
cross through vehicle areas need to meet the surfacing requirements found in 7-11- 403(404). A
fencing plan was also provided for the site, which was required by the DA. The applicant is
requesting to increase the height of the fence adjacent to the juvenile detention facility from 6’
to 8’ to screen the existing fencing on the adjoining property.
On the north end of the site there will be a landscaped area where the applicant has obtained a
recreation easement agreement with the neighboring office building for the residents of the
development to use. The DA required fencing along that section between the two uses, but to
make the area more accessible for the residents, the applicant will add two additional gates in
the fence so that there are three pedestrian connections to this area.

All landscaped areas shall include at least 50% live plant material. Any landscaped areas less
than 8’ wide cannot include lawn. For the narrow areas separating driveways that are less than
6’ in depth, no plant material is required, but the applicant shall use cobble that is a different
color than the concrete.

Amenities
The DA required the following amenities for the project: swimming pool, boulder cave,
waterfall, lazy river, water slide, hot tub, clubhouse, courtyards, game room, fitness center,
adventure tower and playground, fire pit lounge, picnic pavilions, trail system, hammock
lounge, dog park, two level 2 electric charging stations at the clubhouse, and a bike
maintenance station. The applicant is showing all these amenities on the plans.
They are of the same size and quality as depicted in Exhibit D.
Architecture
The building elevations shall meet all requirements outlined in 7-11-400P. The applicant has
provided several different color schemes that may be used for the buildings. The DA requires
that at least four color schemes be used throughout the community.

Signage
The DA requires that there be an entry feature with landscaping along both entrances
along Decker Lake Dr. Typically, these entry features meet the monument sign
requirements listed in 11-5-104. The applicant is proposing to deviate from those
requirements and is looking for approval under the bonus sign section for an alternative sign
plan (11-4-109(4)).

       An applicant may submit an alternative sign plan or design for unique and special
       circumstances to the Planning Commission, provided such sign(s) achieves the intent
       of the above standards and this Title. The Planning Commission may accept or reject
       any such proposal in the Planning Commission’s sole discretion.

Monument signage in the RM Zone can be 5’ tall and 50 sq. ft. in sign area. The applicant’s
proposed entry feature is 9’ tall, which includes a 1’ masonry base and is 33 sq. ft. in sign
area.
