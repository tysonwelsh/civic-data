SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a land use map amendment and zoning map
amendment for approximately 0.86 acres located at the southwest corner of Pioneer Road
and Highland Drive at approximately 2020 E. Pioneer Road (Exhibits B and C). The property
is currently zoned A5. The applicant is requesting that the applications be approved to
allow for the development of the property with a single-family home in the future.

The property was used in the past by the City of Riverton as a water storage facility. There
has not been a home on the property in the past.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.
1.     The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property,
       including but not limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire
       protection, schools, storm water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water
       and refuse collection;
       Other than noted below, we are not aware of any inadequacies of the facilities
       intended to serve this property.
       a.     The increase in density from one residential unit per two acres agricultural
              zoning to up to twelve residential units per acre on a total of 3.8 acres
              constitutes a sizeable increase in the amount of traffic to and from the
              subject area based on the subject parcel. One single family residential
              structure and associated agricultural uses constitutes up to nine daily trips
              and two peak hour trips, based on industry standard vehicle trip tables. With
              the acreage of this rezone, the increase in daily trips would be approximately
              for a maximum of 45 residential units consisting of a maximum of 25 peak
              hour trips and 300 daily trips. This represents a significant increase on the
              adjacent public local street in traffic but is a minor increase for Lone Peak
              Parkway, an adjacent minor arterial with a high capacity for traffic. The
              current level of service for Lone Peak Parkway is degraded by delays and
              backups associated with existing uses in the area. There are stacking delays
              that occasionally block access from nearby local streets onto Lone Peak
              Parkway. These delays are especially significant during beginning and ending
              school times.
       b.     Connectivity with the subject parcel to a public right-of-way is on River
              Chapel Rd, a local street. The closest classified street is Lone Peak Parkway, a
              minor arterial which is adjacent to the site. Access locations will be required
              to meet Draper City Standards as outlined in our Master Transportation Plan.
              No connection would be permitted onto Lone Peak Parkway as the fronting
              parcel length would not meet the Draper City Access Management spacing
              requirements.
       c.     There are existing storm drain facilities on River Chapel Road. Provisions for
              onsite storm drainage, along with the connection to the storm facilities, will
              need to be addressed with any subdivision or site plan application, and shall
              comply with the provisions of the development requirements within the
              Draper City Municipal Code.
       d.     Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by South Valley Sewer District. Any
              subdivision application will require a commitment to serve from the Sewer
              District that facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.
       e.     Drinking water facilities will be provided by the city. Any subdivision or site
              plan application will require a commitment to serve from the city that
              facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses. Accordingly,
              we will provide comments relative to the potential construction activities
              when the appropriate application is received. Thank you for the opportunity
              to review the Land Use and Zone Amendments Request.

                History:
       This lot was originally owned by Riverton City and was used for the storage of water by way of a

       •   The surround lots are all zoned either Medium or Low Density (A1 or A2)
       •   Low density residential use for this lot is consistent with the City’s intent for the semirural
           area, having little impact on the surroundings and municipal services.
       •   The lot is .86 Acres or 37,461 SF for a single home, larger than most surrounding lot sizes,
           maintaining the semirural density, open space and character of the neighborhood.
    •   Residential use of the land would be harmonious with the surrounding property and
        historical land use the in area.
    •   Given the location and surrounding development, residential use for this large lot is likely
        the the highest and best use of the property.

   development in the vicinity of the subject property?

•   Low-Medium Density designation would allow the property to be utilized as residential property
    and would be consistent with the surrounding community and standards.
•   A .86 Acre lot with a single family home would be harmonious with surrounding development,
    be consistent with the general land use designations surrounding the property and have
