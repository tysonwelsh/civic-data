Re: Exeter (Summit Commerce Center) 2nd Amended Development Agreement Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of an Amended Development Agreement for approximately 26.94 acres located at the southwest corner of the intersection of Pony Express Rd. and Golden Harvest Rd., at approximately 13004 S. Pony Express Rd. The property is currently zoned M1. The applicant is requesting that an Amended Development Agreement be approved to permit additional wall signage in excess of the current ordinance limits.
The Planning Commission heard this item at their February 23, 2023 meeting and forwarded a positive recommendation on the application, together with the conditions and findings listed in the staff report, on a 5-0 vote. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. The proposed Amended Development Agreement complies with the requirements of DCMC Section 9-5-200(C) and (D).
