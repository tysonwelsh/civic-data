| RECITALS

A. Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council is committed to participate with the
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District for its municipal services.

B. An Interlocal Agreement is needed to define the services and set out the various
rights and duties of all the participants in the municipal services District and has previously been
adopted by all parties.

C. An amendment to the Interlocal Agreement has been negotiated between the
County, the District and the Townships within the County to changing certain the terms of
getting services from the District and from Salt Lake County.

RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Council of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township:

1. The Mayor of the Township is hereby authorized to sign the First Amendment to the
Master Interlocal Agreement for municipal and other services in the form attached as Exhibit A
hereto subject only to the other participating entities approving the same form of agreement.
