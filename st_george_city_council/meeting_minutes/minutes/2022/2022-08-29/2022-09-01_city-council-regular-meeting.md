CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to sponsor the Battle of the Badges Chili Cook-Off
        organized by Utah Disaster Kleenup on October 22, 2022, from 6:00 a.m.
        to 6:00 p.m. at Vernon Worthen Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Consider approval to sponsor the Battle
          of the Badges Chili Cook-Off organized by Utah Disaster Kleenup on October 22,
          2022, from 6:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. at Vernon Worthen Park. Staff recommends
          approval of this sponsorship request.

      b. Consider approval to continue sponsorship of the annual Juan's Spring
         Festival event organized by Canyon Media on October 15, 2022 at Vernon
         Worthen Park.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This annual event started in 2017. The
          sponsorship request includes: 1) Fee waiver for the Special Event Permit Fee
          ($150/day); 2) Fee waiver for the sub-license fees ($5 each); 3) Fee waiver for
          the park reservation fee ($400/day); 4) City staff to assist with clean-up/trash;
          5) Police Officers; 6) Setup big stage; 7) Picnic tables; 8) Trash cans/liners; and
          9) Firetruck and flag on display. Staff recommends approval of this sponsorship
          request, with the conditions that the organizers recruit volunteers in lieu of city
          staff to aid in the clean-up of trash, and that a special event permit and
          encroachment permit (as applicable) are each approved.

      c. Consider approval of a Reimbursement Agreement with Quality
         Excavation for the upsizing of an irrigation line through a phase of the
         White Hills Subdivision.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Quality Excavation is developing the
          White Hills Subdivision. They will be installing an 8" irrigation line. Our master
          plan identifies the need of an 18" irrigation line through that subdivision. This
          agreement is to upsize the line from an 8" line to an 18" line, with the City paying
          the upsizing costs. Staff recommends approval of the reimbursement
          agreement.

      d. Consider approval of a contract with Specialty Applicators, LLC for seal
         coat for various streets within the City of St. George.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This is a sole source contract. This
          product (GSB-Friction Seal) will be used on the newer portion of Indian Hills
          Drive. that was constructed in 2015. The product was tested on Sand Piper
          Drive. and at the St. George Regional Airport (north section of FBO ramp) with
          excellent results. Staff recommends approval.

      e. Consider approval of a contract with Holbrook Asphalt, Inc. for HA5 seal
         coat for various streets within the City of St. George.

GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the general plan from Low
     Density Residential (LDR) to Open Space (OS), Medium Density Residential
     (MDR) and High Density Residential (HDR) on approximately 14.44 acres
     located at 1295 North Bluff Street known as Dodge Springs. Case No. 2022-
     GPA-004

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This item was tabled at the June 2, 2022
      City Council meeting to allow discussion on the access to the property. The
      developer for Dodge Springs has met with UDOT and discussed access of their
      property to Bluff Street. After discussions with UDOT, two options were presented
      and the developer chose option 2, which allowed for a "right-out" only on to Bluff
      Street from the property. Therefore, there will be two vehicle access points to/from
      the property; a full access at 1250 North and a "right-out" only at Bluff Street. The
      original application for this general plan amendment was for Low Density Residential
      (LDR) to High Density Residential (HDR). At their meeting held on May 10, 2022,
      the Planning Commission held a public hearing and recommended approval with
      conditions. The applicant has updated the plan to reflect the recommendations of
      the Planning Commission and staff. The updates include 4.0 acres of open space
      along the hillside and 0.95 acres along 1250 North is proposed to be Medium Density
      Residential (MDR) and the remaining property of 9.0 acres as High Density
      Residential (HDR).

GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT/ORDINANCE:
     Consider approval of an ordinance changing the general plan future land-
     use map from Commercial (COM) to High Density Residential (HDR) on
     approximately 5.72 acres located on the west side of Dixie Drive just south
     of Gap Canyon Parkway for a project to be known as Dixie Apartments. Case
     No. 2022-GPA-005

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This proposal is to change the land-use
      designation on approximately 5.72 acres of undeveloped land from Commercial
      (COM) potential to High Density Residential (HDR) potential. On April 26, 2022, this
      item was presented to the Planning Commission. At that time, the request was to
      change 7 acres to HDR and leave the remaining 3.12 acres as commercial. There
      were no public comments made during the public hearing process; however, there
      were two public comments that were sent to staff via email, those comments are
      shown in Exhibit B. During deliberation, the Planning Commissioners expressed
      concerns with the reduction of the commercial potential and traffic that comes from
      high density residential. The Planning Commissioners ultimately voted for a
      continuance. On the May 10, 2022, the applicant returned with a modified request.
      The size of the HDR land was reduced from 7 acres to 5.72 acres. This in turn
      increased the COM area from 3.12 acres to 4.4 acres. At their meeting held on May
      10, 2022, the Planning Commission recommended approval.
