PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on August 24, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2022-36 An ordinance of the city council of the city of Herriman, Utah, amending Herriman city code title 2 (boards and commissions) relating to the appointment of board and commission members and remove the economic development committee. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
