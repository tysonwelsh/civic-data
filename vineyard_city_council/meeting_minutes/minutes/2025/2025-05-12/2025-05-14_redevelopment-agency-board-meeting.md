4.1. Discussion and Action - Proposed Tentative Fiscal Year 2025-2026 Budget
RDA Director Josh Daniels will present the Tentative Fiscal Year 2025-2026 RDA
Budget. The RDA Board will act to adopt (or deny) the proposed tentative budget and
set a public hearing for May 28, 2025.

Page 1 of 2; May 14, 2025, Redevelopment Agency Board Minutes

> Chair Fullmer turned time over to Redevelopment Agency Director Josh Daniels, who gave
a presentation on the proposed tentative budget.

> Chair Fullmer commented on the process moving forward before the next meeting, as well
as expectations that the board would take the time to look over the information that was
provided. She also encouraged board members to meet with staff with any questions and gather
public comments before the public hearing on the tentative budget.

> Motion: BOARD MEMBER CLAWSON MOVED TO ADOPT THE PROPOSED
TENTATIVE FISCAL YEAR 2025-2026 BUDGET AS PRESENTED AND SCHEDULED A
PUBLIC HEARING FOR MAY 28, 2025. BOARD MEMBER SIFUENTES SECONDED THE
MOTION. THE ROLL CALL WAS AS FOLLOWS: CHAIR FULLMER AND BOARD
MEMBERS CAMERON, SIFUENTES, AND CLAWSON VOTED YES. BOARD MEMBER
HOLDAWAY VOTED NO. THE MOTION PASSED WITH A VOTE OF FOUR (4) TO ONE

(1).
