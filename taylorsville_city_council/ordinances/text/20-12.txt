                                      TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 20-12

   AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING TEXT
     AMENDMENT FOR TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE SECTIONS 13.07.010(B),
RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS; 13.20.060, PLANNED UNIT DEVELOPMENT
  (“PUD”) DISTRICT; 13.20.070, RESIDENTIAL MULTIPLE-FAMILY (“RM”) DISTRICT;
    13.24.080(B), PARKING SPACE REQUIREMENTS; 13.30.030(C), APPLICATION AND
       REVIEW PROCESS; 13.30.110, STREET DEDICATION; AND CHAPTER 13.36.

        WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council (the “Council”) met in regular session on April 15,
2020, to consider, among other things, approving a zoning text amendment for Taylorsville Municipal Code
Sections 13.07.010(B), Residential Development Standards; 13.20.060, Planned Unit Development
(“PUD”) District; 13.20.070, Residential Multiple-Family (“RM”) District; 13.24.080(B), Parking Space
Requirements; 13.30.030(C), Application and Review Process; 13.30.110, Street Dedication; and Chapter
13.36; and

        WHEREAS, on October 3, 2018, the Council adopted Ordinance No. 18-26, which, in part,
effectively removed “planned unit development” and its design standards as a permitted land use; and

         WHEREAS, in executing Ordinance No. 18-26, several references to “planned unit development”
and its standards were inadvertently not removed from the City’s code; and

        WHEREAS, this proposed ordinance includes a final clean-up effort to remove all remaining
references to “planned unit development” and its standards from the City’s code; and

       WHEREAS, the site-specific development zoning district (“SSD”) is intended to replace the
“planned unit development” tool, rendering PUD no longer relevant; and

         WHEREAS, the City also initiated an application to consider establishing a new residential multi-
family zoning district (“RM-16”), which is intended to accommodate future residential development up to
sixteen (16) dwelling units per acre in density; and

         WHEREAS, the maximum residential density currently allowed in the residential multi-family
zoning district is twelve (12) units per acre; however, the City has recently received and approved requests
for residential densities exceeding twelve (12) units per acre, and anticipates receiving more applications
in the future; and

        WHEREAS, because there is no base zoning district that accommodates such development, these
requests have been processed using the site-specific development zoning district classification; and

       WHEREAS, the creation of an RM-16 zoning district would provide the City with a zoning tool
to market realities in the future; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best interest
of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed amendments.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the proposed
zoning text amendments to for Taylorsville Municipal Code Sections 13.07.010(B), Residential
Development Standards; 13.20.060, Planned Unit Development (“PUD”) District; 13.20.070, Residential
