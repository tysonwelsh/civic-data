 FRC Minutes 
W
 October 23, 2025 

 MINUTES 
 Wasatch Front Regional Council 
 October 23, 2025 
 WFRC Council meeting was held at WFRC Offices, 41 N Rio Grande Street, Salt Lake City and via Zoom. 
 1. W
 elcome & Consent Agenda [00:00:05]
 Mayor Dawn Ramsey called the Council meeting to order at 1:35pm. Mayor Ramsey thanked everyone who was
 able to attend the three Wasatch Choice Vision workshops that have occurred and invited the group to attend one
 of the upcoming five remaining. Introductions were completed for those attending in person and online. [00:07:54]
 ACTION : Mayor Ramsey brought attention to the minutes of the August 28, 2025 meeting and Financial
 statements and check registers for July and August 2025 and Budget/Expenditure Report to date. Mayor Dustin
 Gettel made a motion to approve all the documents as presented and UTA Trustee Carlton Christensen seconded
 the motion. The minutes and financial statements were approved unanimously.
 2. C
 hair Report [00:08:50]
 Mayor Ramsey acknowledged the outgoing members of the WFRC Council - Roy City Mayor Bob Dandoy, and
 Millcreek City Mayor Jeff Silvestrini, who each took a few minutes to comment.
 3. P
 ublic Comment [00:19:09]
 Mayor Ramsey opened the meeting for public comment. There was one public comment.
 4. R
 egional Growth Committee (RGC) [00:25:32]
 Mayor Ramsey introduced Davis County Commissioner Lorene Kamalu, Vice Chair of RGC, who stated there will
 be discussion and action on the two items discussed at the last RGC meeting.
 a.
 ACTION: Station Area Plans Certification: Murray City, Riverton City, Draper City, South Salt Lake
 City, West Valley City, and Woods Cross City
 Commissioner Kamalu turned the time to Meg Padjen, WFRC Community & Economic Development Director, who
 gave a progress report on the Wasatch Choice Vision (WCV) and the ongoing Centers work. A smaller, printable
 map of the WCV area was offered to the group upon request, through an online form link that will be provided in a
 follow-up email. [00:34:20] Ms. Padjen then turned to Byron Head, WFRC Community Planner, to continue with
 the presentation, providing information on the six requested Station Area Plans seeking certification. [00:53:33]
 ACTION: Mayor Dirk Burton made a motion to certify the Draper, Murray, Riverton, South Salt Lake, West Valley
 City, and Woods Cross Station Area Plans as presented. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Bob
 Stevenson and the affirmative vote was unanimous.
 b.
 ACTION: Approval of Amendment #4 to the 2023-2050 Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) and Air
 Quality Conformity Memorandum #43 [01:04:44]
 Commissioner Kamalu continued with her report, stating that the RTP is the transportation element of the Wasatch
 Choice Vision. WFRC adopts the RTP in its entirety every four years. WFRC, working with transportation partners
 and local communities, updates the RTP to reflect changing conditions and funding opportunities for transit, roads,
 active transportation, and land use. Last week, RGC unanimously recommended approving Amendment #4 to the
 2023-2050 RTP. Commissioner Kamalu introduced Jory Johner, WFRC Long Range Planning Director, to present
 this item. [01:13:33] A request was made to separate the requested motion into four motions, one for each of the
 three proposed project amendments and one for Air Quality Memorandum #43 (which is applicable to all three.)
 [01:26:23] ACTION: Legacy Parkway - I-15 / US-89 to I-215 (Davis County) Commissioner Jim Harvey made a
 motion to approve the Level 3 amendment for this project as presented. The motion was seconded by Mayor
 Brandon Stanger. There were two dissenting votes; however, the affirmative vote was the majority and the
 amendment was approved.
 [01:27:27] ACTION: I-15 - 3000 North to US-91 (Box Elder County) Commissioner Jim Harvey made a motion to
 approve the Level 3 amendment for this project as presented. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Lee
 Perry and the affirmative vote was unanimous.
 [01:28:05] ACTION: I-15 - Northern WFRC MPO Boundary to Hwy 91 on I-15 (Box Elder County) Mayor Mark
 Shepherd made a motion to approve the Level 3 amendment for this project as presented. The motion was
 seconded by Commissioner Lee Perry and the affirmative vote was unanimous.

[ 01:29:15] ACTION: Air quality conformity analysis as found in Draft Air Quality Memorandum 43 
 Commissioner Mike Newton made a motion to approve the Level 3 amendment for this project as presented. The 
 motion was seconded by Commissioner Sharon Bolos and the affirmative vote was unanimous. 
 5. Transportation Coordinating Committee (Trans Com) [01:30:24] 
 Mayor Ramsey moved on to the next item on the agenda, calling on Mayor Mark Shepherd, Trans Com Chair. 
 a. 
 ACTION: Board Modifications to the 2026-2031 (TIP) 
 Mayor Shepherd introduced Ben Wuthrich, WFRC Transportation Improvement Plan Coordinator, who presented a 
 brief overview of the proposed modifications. The modification materials are also included in the meeting packet. 
 Mayor Shepherd then requested a motion to approve the modifications to the 2026-2031 TIP. [01:54:47] ACTION: 
 Mayor Mark Shepherd made a motion to approve the modifications as discussed. Commissioner Scott Wardle 
 seconded the motion. There was one dissenting vote; however, the affirmative vote was the majority and the 
 amendment was approved. 
 6. WFRCBudgetCommittee [01:55:45] 
 Mayor Ramsey then brought the group’s attention to Salt Lake County Councilmember Aimee Winder Newton, 
 WFRC Budget Committee Chair, for the WFRC Budget Committee report. 
 a. 
 PUBLIC HEARING and ACTION: Approve Fiscal Year 26 WFRC Budget amendments 
 Councilmember Winder Newton stated that each year in October WFRC amends its budget to reflect the final 
 financials from the previous year, and to include projects that were budgeted in the previous year but were not 
 completed prior to the end of that year and are carried forward to the current fiscal year. This is a routine action, 
 which reflects that many of WFRC’s projects are multi-year in nature. Councilmember Winder Newton noted that 
 Marian Florence, WFRC Chief Financial Officer, was in attendance to answer any questions regarding the FY26 
 WFRC Budget amendments. [01:56:20] Councilmember Winder Newton made a motion to open a public hearing 
 regarding approving amendments to the WFRC FY26 Budget. Mayor Ramsey asked if there were any comments 
 from the public in attendance. There were no comments. [01:56:54] Mayor Ramsey motioned to close the public 
 hearing. [01:57:03] ACTION: Councilmember Winder Newton made a motion that the Council approve the FY26 
 Budget amendments as presented, which was seconded by Commissioner Mike Newton. The affirmative vote was 
 unanimous. 
 b. ACTION: Accept the FY25 WFRC Audit Report [01:57:30] Councilmember Winder Newton reported that the 
 Council’s financial statements for FY25 received an unqualified opinion with no findings or questioned costs. A 
 copy of the audit report was included in the meeting material previously provided. The WFRC Budget Committee 
 met to review the audit. Marian Florence, WFRC, and our independent auditors from Larson Certified Public 
 Accountants, were available to answer questions. [01:58:00] ACTION: Councilmember Winder Newton made a 
 motion that the Council accept the FY25 audit report. Commissioner Jim Harvey seconded the motion and the 
 affirmative vote was unanimous. 
 7. Reports [01:58:38] 
 Mayor Ramsey stated that in the interest of time, the informational reports for 7a. Wasatch Front Economic 
 Development District (WFEDD) and 7b. Active Transportation Committee (ATC) would be included in the follow-up 
 email rather than presented now. She then turned time to Andrew Gruber, WFRC Executive Director for a brief 
 report. Mr. Gruber acknowledged the retirement of Kip Billings, WFRC’s Air Quality Analyst/Senior Transportation 
 Engineer, after 30 years of service. [01:52:39] He also introduced two new staff members: Fariba Soltani, who will 
 be filling the Air Quality Analyst position in WFRC’s Short-Range Programming group , and Pukar Bhandari, a new 
 Data Scientist, joining WFRC’s Analytics group. [01:59:10] 
 8. Other Business and Adjournment [02:00:39] 
 Mayor Ramsey noted the upcoming events on the agenda and the attached WFRC 2026 Meeting Schedule. The 
 next meeting of the WFRC Council will be held on January 22, 2026. Calendar appointments and more information 
 will be forthcoming. [02:01:10] Mayor Mark Shepherd made a motion to adjourn the meeting, which was seconded 
 by Commissioner Jim Harvey. The motion was approved unanimously. The meeting adjourned at 3:37pm .
