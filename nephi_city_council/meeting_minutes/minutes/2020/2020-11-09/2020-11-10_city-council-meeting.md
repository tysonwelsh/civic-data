                                         Meeting Minutes
                                      Work Session 11-10-20
                                             7:00 p.m.
                                    Nephi City Council Chambers


1. The council discussed the options for granting a reasonable accommodation for ADA access at a local
   RV park.

2.   The council reviewed an ordinance that amended the city code to allow the discharge of a firearm
     within the city limits under certain conditions. This ordinance would allow the discharge of a firearm
     within a designated gun range approved by the Mayor. The council directed staff to bring back the
     ordinance to a regular meeting for consideration.

3. Updates to the Planned Unit Development land use code were reviewed by the council.
   Clarifications were suggested to fencing for an RV parking area and for parking requirements. A
   request was made to include the requirement for a perimeter fence.

4. Changes to the city’s Annexation Policy Plan were reviewed by the council to comply with changes in
   state law. The council directed staff to bring back these changes to a regular meeting for
   consideration.

5. Project funding from a UDOT stormwater project was discussed. A federal aid agreement will be
   brought before the council at the next regular meeting for consideration.

6. Updates on various city projects were discussed.

7. The Mayor called a regular meeting to order and a motion was made to go into executive session for
   the discussion of character and competence of an individual, real property, and deliberations.

8. Adjournment 9:00 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Kent
Jones, Seth Atkinson.
