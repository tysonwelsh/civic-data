1. REVIEW THE PROGRESS AND OUTCOMES OF THE UTAH COUNTY ALTERNATIVE PROBATION
(UCAP) PILOT PROJECT
- Richard Nance, Department of Drug and Alcohol Prevention and Treatment Director -

COMMISSIONER LEE, WELL DONE ON GOOD WORK ON THIS PROJECT.

DEPUTY COX, UTAH COUNTY SHERIFF OFFICE, WE HAVE BEEN OPERATING WITH THIS
FOR ABOUT 18 MONTHS. WE HAVE BEEN WORKING THROUGH DIFFERENT POLICY
REVISIONS. AT TIMES IN THE LAST 18 MONTHS WE HAVE BEEN WELL ABOVE OUR
MAXIMUM CAPACITY AND HAD PERIODS OF EXTENDED WAIT LISTS OF 6 TO7 WEEKS
TO GET SOMEONE ASSESSED. WE HAVE HAD A LOT OF INTEREST AND ELIGIBLE
PARTICIPANTS. WE GO THROUGH RISK ANALYSIS ASSESSMENTS TO MAKE SURE WE
ARE APPLYING THE BEST PROGRAM FOR THE INDIVIDUAL.

DEPUTY WIDELIND, UTAH COUNTY SHERRIF, AS FAR AS TRAINING THIS IS WHERE WE
EVOLVED THE MOST. USING BEST PRACTICES AND MAKING RELATIONSHIPS WITH
COUNTIES ENVOLVED IN SIMILAR PROGRAMS. MORE SO, OTHER STATES AND
CONTACTS WE MADE. WE HAVE BECOME CERTIFIED THROUGH A COMPANY CALED
LSCMI, WHERE WE DO OUR RISK NEED ANALISYS. WE CAN DO OUR OWN, IN HOUSE
LSCMI EVALUATIONS. OTHER TRAININGS, ARE MOTIVATIONAL INTERVIEWING. WE
HAVE ATTENEDED APPPA NATIONAL SEMINARS TO RECOGNIZE BEST PRACTICES. WE
WE HAVE A MINIMUN REQUIREMENT OF GED ON EDUCATION. THE OFFENDERS HAVE
ATTENED VOCATIONAL TRAININGS, AND ON-GOING SCHOOLING. ONE OF THEIR
GRADUATES HAS BEEN ACCEPTED TO MEDICAL SCHOOL AND THIS IS SOMETHING WE
ARE VERY PROUD OF. SOME OUR STATISTICS ON OUR ASSISSMENTS ARE: WE HAVE
CURRENTLY 102 ACTIVE PARTICIPANTS. WE HAVE 35 AND COMPLETED 16 PEOPLE.
REFUSED (THEY REFUSED TO COME TO PROGRAM) 74 PEOPLE. APPROXIMATELY 3400
CONTACTS WERE MADE.

COMMISSIONER LEE, ASKED QUESTION, DO YOU TRACK THOSE 74?

DEPUTY COX, AS FAR AS THE DATA TRACKING FOR NEW ARRESTS/CONVICIONS; WE
HAVE THE 49 PARTICIPANTS THAT WE HAVE BEEN TRACKING. THESE NUMBERS ARE
AS OF JUNE. AT THAT TIME WE HAD 52 REFERENCE SUBJECTS. SIMILAR RISK LEVELS,
SIMILAR HISTORIES BETWEEN THE TWO GROUPS WE WERE TRACKING. REALLY
TRIED TO MAKE SURE WE COMPARED APPLES TO APPLES WITH THE SMALL GROUPS
THEY WERE TRACKING. OF THE PARTICIPANTS WE WERE WORKING WITH, THERE
WERE 5 ARRESTS AND 4 WITH CONVICTIONS. OF THE CONTROL GROUP, THERE WERE
60 ARRESTS AND 27 CONVICTIONS. THAT IS A PRETTY DRASTIC DIFFERENCE BETWEEN
THE TWO GROUPS. IT IS SOMETHING WE ARE VERY PROUD OF. WE HAVE 91%
REDUCTION IN ARRESTS AND 94% REDUCTION IN CONVICTIONS. WE ARE OPTOMISTIC
FOR THE FUTURE AND WILL CONTINUE TO APPLY THIS MODEL WITH DIFFERENT
GROUPS. BECAUSE OF THE SMALL GROUP SIZE, THEY HAD TO REALLY RESTRICT
THOSE COMING IN. THERE ARE A LOT OF PEOPLE THEY COULD HELP BUT THEY DO
NOT HAVE ROOM FOR THEM. | FEEL LIKE THERE IS A BRIGHT FUTURE FOR IT.

COMMISSIONER LEE, DO YOU HAVE NUMBERS ON THE PEOPLE THAT WOULD
PROBALLY ACCEPT THE PROGRAM?

DEPUTY COX, THAT IS SOMETHING WE TRIED TO WORK WITH THE COUNTY
ATTORNEYS OFFICE AND THEY HAVE BEEN GREAT. THERE ARE SO MANY PEOPLE
COMING IN AND THROUGH THAT IT HAS TO HAVE A DRUG OFFENSE AND CERTAIN
CRIMINAL HISTORY REQUIREMENTS. WE DID THAT JUST TO HAVE MORE SPACE. WE
DO NOT HAVE THE SPACE. WE HAVE NOT BEEN PUSHING THE PROGRAM, COMPLETELY
PASSIVE. NOT MEETING WITH DEFENDERS OR JUDGES. IF WE DO THAT WE WILL BE

ININDATED WITH APPLICATIONS AGAIN. WE DON'T WANT WAITING LISTS SO LONG, IT
IS IMPOSSIBLE TO GET INTO.

DEPUTY WIDELIND, WE DONT WANT TO INTRDUCE IT TO SOMEONE AND THEN TELL
THEM THEY CAN'T GET INTO THE PROGRAM.

DEPUTY COX, A BREIF FISCAL REVIEW AS OF JUNE, IN OUR CONTROL GROUPS THE
AMOUNT OF COSTS THAT WENT INTO PROSECUTION, LOSS OF PROPERTY, PERSONAL
CRIMES, THOSE 60 ARRESTS HAVE FAR REACHING EFFECT ON THE COMMUNITY. WE
REDUCED THAT NUMER IN OUR CONTROL GROUP TO FIVE. THAT IS ALMOST
IMPOSIBLE TO CALCULATE THAT COST SAVINGS AND BENEFIT. DRUGS AND
ABUSE.COM DID A STUDY WHERE THEY LOOKED AT PROGRAMS LIKE THIS AND THEY
SAID THAT FOR EVERY DOLLAR INVESTED YOU CAN SAVE 12 DOLLARS IN COSTS.
TRADITIONALLY THERE ARE ABOUT 4 TO 5 COURT HEARINGS AND OUR CONTROL
GROUP GENERALLY HAD ONE. COURT SAVINGS AND TIME IN JAIL IS SOMETHING WE
ARE VERY PROUD OF.

COMMISSIONER LEE, J WISH YOU COULD SHOW THE CHART. SO HOW LONG HAVE
YOU BEEN DOING IT?

DEPUTY COX, ANSWERED 18 MONTHS. WE WERE INITIALLY SET UP FOR A TWO-YEAR
PILOT.

DEPUTY WIDELIND, | AM SURE YOU WERE ABLE TO READ THROUGH SOME OF THE
TESTIMONIALS. THIS IS WERE WE GET OUR MOST JOB SATISFACTION. READ TWO
QUOTES FROM THE TESTIMONIALS,

DEPUTY COX, I WANTED TO BRING UP WHAT 1 THINK IS THE CORE OF OUR SUCCESS,
WHICH IS COLABORATION WITH COUNTY SUBSTANCE ABUSE, MENTAL HEALTH, THE
PROSECUTORS OFFICE, THE PUBLIC DEFENDORS. EVERYONE WORKING TOGETHER I
FEEL LIKE IS WHAT MAKES THIS WORK. I WANT TO THANK SUBSTANCE ABUSE FOR
EVERYTHING THAT THEY DO. WE ARE VERY EXCITED AND PROUD OF WHAT WE HAVE
DONE.

COMMISSIONER LEE, ASKED QUESTION, WHEN YOU ARE DOING YOUR TRACKING, IS IT
JUST UTAH COUNTY? ARE YOU ABLE TO PULL ARRESTS FROM OTHER COUNTIES?

DEPUTY COX, ANSWERED, SOME OF THE ARRESTS ARE HARD TO FIND BUT ANY
CONVICTION WE WOULD BE ABLE TO FIND. IT MAY TAKE A LITTLE WHILE, THAT IS
WHY WE CONTINUALLY DO CHECKS ON EVERYBODY, SO THAT WE HAVE GOOD DATA.
THOSE WE TAKE AN APPLICATION FOR HAVE TO LIVE IN UTAH COUNTY.
COMMISSIONER LEE, WELL DONE...APPLAUSE.
