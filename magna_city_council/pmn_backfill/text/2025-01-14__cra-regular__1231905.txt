C. Status of Land Transfer (Magna Redevelopment Districts’ Assets to Magna CRA)
David Brickey noted the RDA in Salt Lake recently acquired a new director and he will be
reaching out to her again to reiterate the city’s interest in acquiring the parcels, property and
projects in Magna CRA’s name as soon as possible.

D. Mantle Park Updates
David Brickey reviewed the update letter from Moira Gray, Municipal Economic Development
Manager for Salt Lake County (Attachment A), which included a detailed timeline for the
completion of the project. The anticipated completion date is between April 15 and April 30,
2025. It is anticipated the Salt Lake Redevelopment Agency is committed to turning over the
property in Magna by May 1, 2025.
Board Member Barney noted that upon his inspection, the lighting requested has been
installed along with foundation and footings for the pole lights when they are available. He also
shared the area is now easily ADA accessible which has been very much appreciated by
others in the area.
