Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: November 17, 2020
Re: Rasmussen Family Commercial Zoning Text Amendment Comments: This application is a request for approval of a  zoning map amendment  for approximately 9.81 acres located at approximately 12197 S. 300 East.  The site has a land use designation of Neighborhood Commercial and is currently zoned A5 (Agricultural, 5 acre minimum) .  The applicant is requesting to rezone the entire property from A5 to CN ( Neighborhood Commercial) .   The subject property has historically been utilized as agricultural or farming land since at least 1978 when the City incorporated.   The findings are as follows: The Planning Commission is scheduled to consider this item on November 12 , 2020.  An update will be provided at the City Council public hearing.
For a positive recommendation:
That Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the zoning text and maps of the DCMC.
That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
For a negative recommendation :
That this map amendment compromises one or more of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
Based on use tables, there are high traffic uses permitted in the proposed CN zone.  This increase in traffic would be added to 300 East, 12200 South, and other surrounding streets where there exists significant traffic congestion at the present time.
The proposed amendment may adversely affect adjacent property.
