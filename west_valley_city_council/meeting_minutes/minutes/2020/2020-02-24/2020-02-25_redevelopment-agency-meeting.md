RESOLUTION 20-03: APPROVE A REAL ESTATE PURCHASE AGREEMENT
WITH DRH DEVELOPMENT, LLC, TO PURCHASE PROPERTY LOCATED AT
2574 SOUTH REDWOOD ROAD
Wayne Pyle, Chief Executive Officer, presented proposed Resolution 20-03 that would
approve a Real Estate Purchase Agreement with DRH Development, LLC, to purchase
property located at 2574 South Redwood Road.

Written documentation previously provided to the Redevelopment Agency included
information as follows:

       The RDA has been studying the Redwood corridor and the evolution that has
       occurred over the last decade. This area has long been in need of redevelopment.
       As with other redevelopment areas, the City and the Redevelopment Agency have
       monitored this area in order to assess the right time for redevelopment. With the
       addition of the opportunity zone designation, this area has become an area of focus.
       As with other areas, when key pieces of property are put to market, the RDA will
       often purchase the pieces and assemble them for future redevelopment. This piece
       of property is vacant, located right on Redwood Road and backs onto the area
       known as the Lake Park Four Plex neighborhood. This property is a small but
       essential piece of the potential larger development. Therefore, the Redevelopment
       Agency desires to get this under contract in order to assemble for a larger
       development. In the event that the area is not redeveloped, this piece remains viable
       for resale and would not represent a loss the RDA.

Upon inquiry by Chairman Christensen there were no further questions from members of
the Agency, and he called for a motion.

       Mr. Huynh moved to approve Resolution 20-03.

       Ms. Lang seconded the motion.
