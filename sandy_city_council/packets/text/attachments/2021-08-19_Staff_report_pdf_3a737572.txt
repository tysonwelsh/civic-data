REQUEST
The applicant, The Thackeray Company, is requesting final subdivision review for a proposed
final plat that creates one large lot for the future Challenger School to the north, and then plats
all the individual units of the proposed Thackeray Towns project to the south of that. The
applicant is not requesting any overlays in association with this application.

BACKGROUND
The subject property is zoned CN zone (Planned Center – Neighborhood District) on the north
side and PUD(10) on the south side. To the west are single-family homes in the R-1-10 zone. To
the south are single-family homes in the SD(R-1-8) PUD zone. To the east is 700 E. Across the
street are multi-family units located in the RM (12) zone, and also homes in a PUD (8) zone.

The subject property is 11.89 acres in size, is vacant, and is comprised of 3 parcels. The north
two parcels previously had a Ream’s Grocery Store on them until August, 2020, when it was
demolished for the future Challenger School. The Challenger School obtained a preliminary site
plan approval from the Planning Commission on August 20, 2020. At the time it was anticipated
that the subdivision plat would be done together with the subdivision for Thackeray Towns. This
appication is the finalization of that intent.

This subdivision application had come before the Planning Commission for preliminary review
on March 18, 2021. The subdivision was preliminarily approved, but the Planning Commission
expressed some concerns regarding whether utility services, specifically power, gas, and sewer
could successfully be installed to serve the development. For that reason the Commission stated
that they wanted this subdivision plat to come back to them for final review.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify
them of the Planning Commission meeting. A sign was also posted on the property. The applicant
held a neighborhood meeting on July 7, 2020. Notices (106) were sent for the neighborhood
meeting, and there were 16 attendees. Several questions and concerns were raised during the
meeting, which were included as part of the preliminary review process.

SUBDIVISON ANALYSIS
There are two zones that run through this subdivision. To the north is the CN zone, which is
where the future Challenger School will be located. The school is a conditional use in the CN
zone. A conditional use permit was obtained for it on August 20, 2020.

The other zone is the PUD(10) zone, which includes all of the Thackeray Towns proposed 40-
unit development, and does not require any conditional use permits as the development is a
permitted use under the PUD(10) zone.

PUBLIC UTILITIES CONCERNS
At the first Planning Commission meeting when this item was originally on the agenda, concern
was expressed by the Commission regarding whether there will be room for power, gas, and
sewer utility lines, and the item was then approved with the stipulation that it come back
before the Planning Commission for final review.

Since that time the applicants have provided an updated utility plan that shows how all of the
lines will be run and where they will hook up to each building. They also provided the same
plan to Rocky Mountain Power, Dominion Energy, and South Valley Sewer District. All three
entities reviewed the plan and have provided will-serve letters based on the plan presented to
them. In particular, both Rocky Mountain Power and Dominion Energy were satisfied with the
distance between the underground power lines and gas lines, which is approximately four feet
between them on the plan. The will-serve letters for all three utilities are attached to this staff
report, as is the revised utility plan.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine final subdivision review is complete
for the Sandy Townhomes Subdivision located at 10670 S. and 10760 S. 700 E., based on the
following findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:

   1. The lots meet the requirements for size and frontage in both the CN zone and the PUD(10)
      zone as may be approved by the Planning Commission.

   2. The reviewing departments have recommended approval of the proposed subdivision.

Conditions:

   1. That the applicant complies with each department’s comments and redlines throughout
      the final review process and that all issues be resolved before the subdivision can be
      recorded.

   2. That all City provisions, codes and ordinances are adhered to during the review, and
      construction of this project.

   3. That the applicant dedicates all required right-of-way as determined by the Sandy City
      Public Works Department.
