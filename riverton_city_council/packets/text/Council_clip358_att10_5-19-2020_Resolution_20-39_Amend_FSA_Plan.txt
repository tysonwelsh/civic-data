                                    RUVERTON CITY
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 20-39

  A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF THE AMENDMENT FOR
          CARES ACT ELIGIBLE OVER THE COUNTER EXPENSES

       WHEREAS, the CARES Act has, among other things, expanded the list of medical
supplies which are eligible for purchase through Riverton City’s Flexible Benefits Plan; and

      WHEREAS, changes brought by the CARES Act, in turn creates the need for the City to
amend the Riverton City Flexible Benefits Plan, attached hereto as Exhibit A; and

        WHEREAS, this Amendment supersedes the provisions of the Plan and the provisions of
the previous amendments which are contrary to this Amendment.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council authorizes and directs the Mayor to execute the enclosed
          Amendment for the Cares Act, upon verification that the agreement has been
          approved as to legal form by the City Attorney.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 19th day of
May 2020 by the following vote:
