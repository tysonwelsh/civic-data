3.         23-423                 Communications Department providing a state of Sandy City 
                                  communications update
                   Attachments:   Comms Report 2023-1

                                  Eric Richards, Communications Director, provided the Council a review and update on the 
                                  Sandy City Communications over the last decade. Under his leadership and direction the 
                                  Communications Department has achieved many milestones: over 46,500 followers on 
                                  social channels, and over 107,000 subscribers to our notifications. The city migrated to a 
                                  new website platform in 2020 which includes web accessibility enhancements and 
                                  greater connectivity for our residents. Citizen Connect, Sandy Notify, City Serve and 
                                  Chatbot are all new features introduced over the last decade creating engaging and 
                                  informative resources for our residents. Council thanked Mr. Richards for his dedication, 
                                  service and commitment to Sandy City and our residents. We have all benefited from 
                                  your service! 

                                  Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.



4.         23-419                 Economic Development Department providing an update on the Cairns 
                                  Development
                   Attachments:   Cairns Development Update

                                  Kasey Dunlavy, Economic Development Director provided to the Council an update of the 
                                  Cairns Development. The Cairns Vision includes twenty million square feet of 
                                  development and includes the East Village, One Ten Apartments, Shops at South Town, 
                                  RedSky apartments, the Summit at the Cairns, Mountain America Arts & Education 
                                  Beehive Education Center, Sandy Park Center, Phase 6 of Monroe Street Extension, 
                                  Wasatch Shadows, and Town Ridge Center. He reviewed key market takeaways. The 
                                  Office markets have been weakening since mid-2022. The Retail market is contending 
                                  with inflation, high gas and food prices, and rising credit card debt. Multifamily new 
                                  constructions has faced significant challenges with surging financing costs. Council 
                                  questions followed. Council thanked Mr. Dunlavy for his presentation. Council expressed 
                                  support for Economic Development and is willing to help in any way.

Mayor's Report

                   Mayor Monica Zoltanski mentioned that her Deputy Mayor, Kim Bell, will be handling all
