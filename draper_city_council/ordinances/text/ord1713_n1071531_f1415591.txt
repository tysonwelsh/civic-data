Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1713

          On April 7, 2026, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1713
          Amending the Official Land Use Map of Draper City by removing from the
          map approximately 0.45 acres of property located at approximately 16001
          South 3500 East within Draper City, otherwise known as the City Initiated
          Alpine-Draper Boundary Adjustment Land Use Map Amendment. The
          complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
          available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/
