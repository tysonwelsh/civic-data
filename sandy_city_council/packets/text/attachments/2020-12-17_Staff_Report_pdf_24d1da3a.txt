DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Water Pro, is requesting preliminary site plan review for a proposed storage facility
at their existing public utility station. The site is approximately 7.48 acres in size. The storage
facility will be 1,894 sq. ft. in size. The building is being proposed to provide security and
protection from the elements for an existing backhoe and other company vehicles. It will also
incorporate a small amount of office space and a work area. The applicant is not requesting any
exceptions in association with this application. All provisions of the base zone will be adhered to
through the review and approval process. An associated staff report addresses the modified
conditional use permit application.

BACKGROUND
Water Pro is a water utillty company that serves a significant portion of Draper, Utah. The
existing treatment facility obtained preliminary site plan review approval in 2002. It also
received an original conditional use permit for a public utility station in 2002. In 2003 it received
a modified conditional use permit to add a well house.

To the west of the utility station are residential lots located in the R-1-12 zone. To the south
are also parcels in the R-1-12 zone, but they are currently used as part of the Water Pro
water treatment facility. To the east and the north is property owned by the Salt Lake County
Water Conservancy District.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject property to
notify them of the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was not held as the
proposed building location is not near any residential uses.

ANALYSIS
City staff and departments have reviewed and recommend preliminary site plan review is
complete, subject to their normal requirements and specifications. The site plan meets all zoning
requirements.

The building is not near the south property line. It is set back approximately 80 feet from the
east, 22 feet from the north, and 28 feet from the west property lines. The building will be 20’ 8”
tall, which staff finds reasonable for a storage building that is not near any residential uses on a
public utility station.

Two handicap-accessible parking stalls are being added just to the west of the building, so parking
will not be negatively impacted. The access from Wasatch Blvd. will remain unchanged.

GRADING

There is no significant grade change on this property. This building’s foundation will be elevated
approximately one foot higher than the building to the immediate south of it.

CONCERNS

Staff has no concerns with the proposed storage facility.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission determine that the preliminary site plan
review is complete for the Water Pro public utility station, based on the following findings, and
subject to the following conditions:

      proposed site plan.

   2. That all proposed setbacks and building height meet the requirements of the PUD(2)
