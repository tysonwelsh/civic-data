A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNCIL OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP DESIGNATING
AN AREA OF THE TOWNSHIP AS THE KEARNS TOWN CENTER ECONOMIC
DEVELOPMENT ZONE FOR THE PURPOSE OF ENCOURAGING REVITALIZATION AND
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT IN THE DESIGNATED DISTRESSED AREA.
RECITALS

A. WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council has recognized that high levels of economic
distress are found in the Kearns area and particularly in the designated Kearns Town Center area outlined
in Exhibit A, which identified area is hereby referred to as the Kearns Town Center Economic Development
Zone.

B. WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council has identified a high priority need to
address economic underdevelopment in the Kearns Town Center Economic Development Zone and to take
action to encourage businesses to stay, locate, or expand therein.

C. WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council, authorizes the development of an
implementation plan, to be administered through Salt Lake County Municipal Economic Development
Services, to determine methods to incentivize and promote investment in and the economic vitality of the
Kearns Town Center Economic Development Zone through the attraction of new business, revitalization
of uneconomic land uses, and the expansion of employment opportunities, using economic development
tools such as HUD Section 108 loans and Community Development Block Grant funds to attract additional
sources of capital to fill financing gaps.

RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Council of the Kearns Metro
Township:

1. The area shown on Exhibit A hereto is hereby designated as the Kearns Town Center
Economic Development Zone eligible to receive the benefits described above.

2. Salt Lake County Municipal Economic Development Services shall provide periodic reports
to the Kearns Metro Township Council identifying progress towards the economic development in the
Kearns Town Center Economic Development Zone.

3. This resolution shall take effect upon passing.
