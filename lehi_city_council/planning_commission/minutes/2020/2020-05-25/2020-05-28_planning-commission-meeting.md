Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1 Public hearing and recommendation of Cody Black’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Table 05-030-B to add auto sales of one acre or larger as
a permitted use in the Commercial zone.
   Jacob Curtis, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Staff and Commission discussed the agenda item.

    The applicant, representative Shawn Adams, was present and answered clarifying questions.

Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing on agenda item 2.1

Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing on agenda item 2.1

Commissioner Nielsen and Commissioner Hemmert had concerns about multiple uses on the
parcel and how the property would be included in the use plan. Kim Struthers stated the auto
sales use would have to be one acre or larger, as stated in code. Commissioner Peterson stated he
is opposed to changing the use in that type of commercial zone. Commissioner Nielsen stated he
is not in favor of conditional uses and he doesn’t think that auto dealerships should be approved
for commercial use zones. It was stated that the surrounding business, Swig, could be affected by
the Code Amendment.

Commissioner Hemmert stated he would entertain a motion.

Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to forward a negative recommendation of Cody Black’s
request for review of a Development Code Amendment to Table 05-030-B to add auto sales
of one acre or larger as a permitted use in the Commercial zone; including all DRC
comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, no; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed; 6 -1.

2.2 Public hearing and recommendation of Dan Ford’s request for review of a General Plan
Amendment for Taylor Meadows, changing the land use designation from VLDRA (very low
density residential/agriculture) to VLDR (very low density residential) on approximately
16.8 acres at approximately 7550 North 8350 West (600 West 1300 South).
    Jacob Curtis, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Staff and Commission discussed the agenda item.

    The applicant, Dan Ford, was present and answered clarifying questions. Commissioner
    Peterson asked the applicant to confirm the lot size. The applicant confirmed the lot sizes as
    shown on the Taylor Meadows General Plan map. Commissioner Everett expressed his
    concerns with losing the rural density as historically permitted in Lehi and slowly allowing
    developments to develop VLDR. Commissioner Everett would like to see VLDRA keeping a
    sense of what the community historically wanted as a development. Commissioner Eyre
    stated the road network would affect the traffic and the road connections near the school
    property. Commissioner Hemmert verified the lot size change was 1.75 to 2.5 units per acre.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing on agenda item 2.2

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing on agenda item 2.2

    Commissioner Hemmert stated he would entertain a motion.


Motion: Commissioner Ellis motioned to recommend approval of Dan Ford’s request for review
of a General Plan Amendment for Taylor Meadows, changing the land use designation from
VLDRA (very low density residential/agriculture) to VLDR (very low density residential) on
approximately 16.8 acres at approximately 7550 North 8350 West (600 West 1300 South);
including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed; 6 -1.

2.3 Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of a Development
Code Amendment to Chapters 11 and 17, including clarification of requirements for
(PUE’s) Public Utility Easements.
    Jacob Curtis, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Staff and Commission discussed the agenda item. Commissioner Hemmert inquired about
    foundation walls and augmented restrictions.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Everett moved to approve Lehi City’s request for review of a
Development Code Amendment to Chapters 11 and 17, including clarification of
requirements for (PUE’s) Public Utility Easements; including all DRC comments and
findings stated in the report. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner
Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4. City Business
A Webinar will be held through Teams for the Work Session meeting at 5:30 p.m.

Commissioner Hemmert and Kim Struthers reported on their tour in Salt Lake City from their

With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
