CALL TO ORDER
Chair McConnehey called the RDA meeting to order at 7:13 p.m.

PUBLIC HEARING – RDA TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2020-2021
Danyce Steck presented the FY 2021 Tentative Budget for the RDA, and answered
questions from the Board.

Chair McConnehey opened a public hearing at 7:14 p.m., and closed the public hearing
seeing that no one electronically indicated a desire to comment.

MOTION:        Board Member Green moved to approve Resolution No. 220 regarding
               adoption of the Tentative Budget for the City of West Jordan RDA for
               FY 2021. Board Member Whitelock seconded the motion.

Board Member Whitelock commented that the RDA Board needed to work on bringing
business into the City, which was the purpose of the RDA and other funds. She suggested
an Economic Development Director would help with that goal.

A roll call vote was taken
