City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On December 13, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1572, amending
the Master Area Plan per the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City
Municipal Code relating to the Vista Station Master Area Plan. The complete ordinance
is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 14th day of December 2022. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
