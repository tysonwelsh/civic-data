1.       PUBLIC MEETING
         An Application for a Conditional Use Permit for a Category II Home Occupation, In-
         Home Pet Grooming Service, Located at 3245 South 540 East. The applicant is
         Ildefonso Ramos.

         ACTION ITEM
         Applicant: Ildefonso Ramos
         Address:   3245 South 540 East

City Planner, Tereza Bagdasarova, presented the Staff Report and stated that the application is for
a Conditional Use Permit for My Best Friend Utah, LLC. It is an in-home pet grooming service
located at 3245 South 540 East. The property is located in the R-1 Zone. Ms. Bagdasarova shared
a street view of the dwelling where the Home Occupation is proposed to take place. An aerial view
was shared. She identified the one off-street parking stall designated for customers to drop off and
pick up animals. All business will take place entirely within the garage.

Ms. Bagdasarova reported that the application meets the following General Plan considerations:

     •   Land Use and Neighborhoods Goal #1:
            o Continue to welcome new residents and businesses into South Salt Lake.

The allowance of a Category II Home Occupation at this location will create another South Salt
Lake business owned by a South Salt Lake resident. Ms. Bagdasarova noted that the previous Staff
Report has since been amended. Some feedback was received that there has already been business
activity at the home. As a result, a number of conditions from the Home Occupation category
section of the Code were added to mitigate potential impacts. The proposed conditions were as
follows:

         1.     The applicant shall retain one (1) off-street parking stall for client use.

         2.     The applicant shall conduct all business activity indoors so as to not create any
                nuisance for surrounding property owners.

         3.     The applicant shall conduct business within the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.

         4.     The applicant shall ensure that any waste generated by the business is properly
                disposed of so as not to impact the surrounding property owners. The applicant
                shall ensure that small animal waste material is dry and mixed with sand, sawdust,
                commercial pet litter, or similar absorptive material, and double-wrapped in paper

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission move to approve, with conditions, the proposed
Conditional Use Permit, for a Category II Home Occupation, for property located at 3245 South
540 East. The recommendation is based on the analysis and findings. Ms. Bagdasarova reported
that the Planning Commission can choose to approve, deny, or continue the application.

Commissioner Pechmann asked about Conditions of Approval #7 and #9. One states that a Sign
Permit is needed while the other seems to imply that there is pre-approval for one sign of that kind.
Mr. Weidenhamer stated that a sign of the standard that is listed in Condition of Approval #9 is
exempt. Something that does not exceed two square feet in area can be placed there but anything
that is larger than that would require there to be a Sign Permit.

A question was raised about the temperatures inside the garage, specifically during the winter and
summer months. Deputy City Attorney, Brianne Brass, clarified that there is nothing specific to
the temperatures in winter or summer listed. However, compliance with Title 6, which relates to
animal services, is listed. The Animal Services Department will be responsible for regulating
matters of that nature. In City Ordinance and State Law, there are regulations regarding animal
cruelty, neglect, and care for animals. If it was determined that the temperatures are inappropriate,
action could be taken. Mr. Weidenhamer stated that responses normally take place on a complaint
basis. If there is concern from a neighbor or client, a complaint can be made to the City.

Ms. Brass noted that with the limit of two animals at one time, the idea is that there will not be
animals sitting there for long periods waiting to be groomed. Chair Carter had a question about
Condition of Approval #15. He reminded the Commissioners that it states, “Should a commercial
vehicle create a nuisance regarding parking, noise, odor, hazardous substances, etc., the vehicle
may be barred from the residential district by action of the Planning Commission.” He asked if
there is a definition for residential district. Ms. Brass stated that she does not have a definition in
front of her at this time but interprets that to mean the residential zone in which the Home
Occupation is allowed. Chair Carter expressed concerns about the lack of definition.

Commissioner Pechmann suggested changing the language from “the” to “any,” so it would read
“…may be barred from any residential district.” Ms. Brass clarified that the proposed language is
straight from the current code. Chair Carter asked City Staff to look into this matter further.
Ms. Brass informed the Commission that Legal Counsel does not like words that are not clearly
defined, so this is something that will be looked at further to ensure that there is clarity in the
future.
