Approve August 6, 2020 Meeting Minutes Mayor Acerson moved to approve the August 6, 2020 meeting minutes. Mayor Mann seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Public Comment None. Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) Project Selection Process – 2020 Project List Approval - Bob Allen, MAG MAG selects projects every two years to be funded using certain Federal State and County funds. There are approximately $85m in funds available. Most of those funds are available in 2023 and 2024. Projects are scored and ranked through an adopted selection process. Combined with MAG Staff scores, the projects are now in a ranked list. That list must now be approved in order to develop a funding scenario and be adopted into the TIP in October. TAC and MAG Staff has recommended that list to be adopted by the MPO Regional Planning Committee. Mayor Mann moved to approve the 2020 Project Prioritization List as presented. Mayor Staheli seconded the motion. The motion carried unanimously. Updates Federal Highway Administration: None. Utah Department of Transportation: None Utah Transit Authority: Kent Millington reported that that transit ridership continues to recover. Ridership was 50k a day in July and increased to 60k a day in August. UVX had 2500 riders a day before school started and will increase to 6k when the two university’s opens. The 850-bus route has also seen increases in the last month. UTA has selected a consultant for the Eagle Mountain and Saratoga Springs study and will begin to negotiate a contract.
