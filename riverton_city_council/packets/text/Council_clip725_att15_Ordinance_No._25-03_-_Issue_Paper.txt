                                     City Council Meeting Agenda
                                            January 7, 2025


PRESENTER:                Jason Lethbridge

                          Ordinance No. 25-03 - Pelaez Rezone, Amending the General Plan
                          Designation and Zoning for Approximately 0.96-Acres Located at 3516 West
ITEM:
                          12600 South from R-1 Residential Zone to C-G (Commercial Gateway) Zone.
                          Applicant- Daniel Pelaez - Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director

SUMMARY:

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Mr. Pelaez, for about 0.96-
acres located at 3516 West 12600 South. The applicant is requesting a change to the General Plan and
a rezone to accommodate an HVAC office, with a showroom and storage warehouse. The requested
General Plan designation is Community Commercial, and the requested zone is C-G (Commercial
Gateway Zone).
The subject property is a rectangle- shaped property located on the north side of 12600 South with about
150 feet of frontage on 12600 South. There is currently a home and some sheds on the property.
The subject property, and all the adjacent properties are part of the Riverton Meadows Subdivision and
are all zoned R-1. Properties to the south (across 12600 South) are zoned C-G (commercial Gateway),
and C-PO (Commercial Professional Office). Other properties along 12600 South are zoned Light
Manufacturing and Regional Commercial. The General Plan for the Riverton Meadows Subdivision is
Estate Density Residential. South of 12600 South Street the General Plan is Professional Office.

FISCAL IMPACT:            N/A

FUNDING SOURCE:           N/A

RECOMMENDED MOTION OR ACTION:

On November 14, 2024, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this application.
 Minutes from that meeting are included below. The Commission voted to recommend DENIAL of this
application, with the following recommended motion (a sample motion to approve is also included if
necessary for the convenience of the City Council):

I move the City Council DENY Ordinance 25-03, proposal to amend the General Plan and rezone 0.96-
acres located at 3516 West 12600 South, thus keeping the property designated as Estate Density
Residential in the General Plan and keeping the current zoning of R-1 (1-acre lots).
