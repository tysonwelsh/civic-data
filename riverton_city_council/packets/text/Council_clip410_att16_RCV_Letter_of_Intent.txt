Dear Sherrie,

This letter is to inform you and the Salt Lake County elections office of our interest in pursuing
participation in the Municipal Alternate Voting Methods Pilot Project (20A-4-6) dealing with Ranked
Choice Voting. Riverton City would like to pursue this option for their Municipal Elections in 2021.

As a city, Riverton City wants to continue contracting with your office to administer our municipal
elections. We hope by expressing our interest early in the process it will stimulate the necessary
discussions and actions for us to accomplish this for the 2021 elections.

We realize that notice needs to be sent to the State Elections office by April 15th for us to be allowed to
pursue this change in our Municipal Elections. We would like to make sure that as we provide that
notice the necessary steps have been taken by your office should we contract with you to administer
our elections.

Please feel free to reach out to me should you have any questions.
