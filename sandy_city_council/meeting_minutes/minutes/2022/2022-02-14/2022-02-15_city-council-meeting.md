1. 22-033 The Administrative Services Department recommending that the Council
participate in a budget workshop in preparation for the upcoming fiscal
year 2022-23 budget process

Attachments: Budget Economic Indicator Charts.pdf

Compensation Presentation.pdf
Brian Kelley, Finance Director, introduced Katrina Frederick with Human Resources who
reviewed the City Compensation Plan with the Council. Ms. Frederick provided an
overview of the guiding principles, pay plan components, and comprehensive
compensation study. Council questions and comments followed.
Council moved to Time Certain Items and heard General Citizen Comments.
Following General Citizen comments, Brian Kelley, Finance Director, continued the
presentation on Agenda Item 1. He presented on key economic indicators. He reviewed
the impact on the City resulting from inflation, supply chain issues, producer price index,
high oil prices, the great resignation, and interest rate hikes. Council questions occurred
during the presentation.

Consent Calendar
The Council agreed to remove Agenda Items 3 and 4 from the Consent Calendar and
move those agenda items to Voting Items.Following this discussion, a motion was made
to approve Item 2 on the Consent Calendar.
A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Zach Robinson to adopt
Amended Resolution 22-07, as listed on the Consent Calendar...The motion
carried by a unanimous voice vote.

City Council Meeting Minutes February 15, 2022

Council Member Business
Council Member Cyndi Sharkey shared concerns from residents regarding the median
projects currently underway in Sandy City on Sego Lily Road. She asked for an update
from Administration.
Council Member Zach Robinson thanked James Sorenson and Mark Wooten for their
efforts and help addressing a resident's concerns.
Council Member Alison Stroud thanked Mike Gladbach and Ryan Kump for their help
addressing a resident's concerns. She also requested an update from Google Fiber be
scheduled at a future city council meeting.
Council Member Ryan Mecham requested a presentation from the Jordan River
Commission be scheduled for a future city council meeting.
Council Member Marci Houseman expressed her appreciation for the flowers, kind
thoughts and emails she and her family recently received. She provided an update from
the Sandy Arts Guild and the Association of Municipal Governments.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Monica Zoltanski encouraged residents to apply to serve on the Atla Canyon
Advisory Committee. She also spoke about the upcoming Public Utility Advisory Board
forum to discuss ways to incentivize water conservation efforts and invited the public to
attend and participate in the forum.

CAO Report
Klm Bell, Interim CAO, gave a brief google fiber update. Ms. Bell offered to set up a
presentation and update from Google Fiber at a future City Council meeting.
Dan Medina, Parks & Recreation Director, provided information related to the median
work currently being done at Sego Lily and East Dell. The work is part of the city's water
conservation plan converting the grass medians to water wise landscaping.

Legislative Report
Evelyn Everton, Legislative Liaison, updated the Council on current bills under
consideration at the State Legislature.
