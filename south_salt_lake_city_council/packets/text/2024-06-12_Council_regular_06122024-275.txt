                                   Public Comments/Question Policy
Time is made available for anyone in the audience to address the Council and/or Mayor concerning
matters pertaining to City business. When a member of the audience addresses the Council and/or
Mayor, they will come to the podium and state their name and City they reside in. The Public will be
asked to limit their remarks/questions to three (3) minutes each. The conducting Council Member shall
have discretion as to who will respond to a comment/question. In all cases the criteria for response will
be that comments/questions must be pertinent to City business, that there are no argumentative
questions and no personal attacks. Some comments/questions may have to wait for a response until
the next regular council meeting. The conducting Council Member will inform a citizen when they have
used the allotted time. Grievances by City employees must be processed in accordance with adopted
personnel rules.

AN ORDINANCE OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 2023-05,
WHICH IS AN ORDINANCE OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL VACATING A PUBLIC RIGHT
OF WAY LOCATED AT 200 EAST AT APPROXIMATELY 3550 SOUTH THROUGH 3570 SOUTH, TO
CORRECT ERRORS AND OMISSIONS.
      WHEREAS The South Salt Lake City Council (the “City Council”) is authorized to vacate
some or all of a public street within the City of South Salt Lake (the “City”), pursuant to Utah
Code Ann. § 10-9a-609.5;
       WHEREAS, the City Council, exercised its legislative authority through Ordinance No.
2023-05 to vacate the public right of way located at 200 East approximately 3550 South
through 3570 South on February 22, 2023, after the proper noticing and public hearing
requirements, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-609.5, and the determination that good
cause existed for the vacation and the public interest or any person would not be materially
injured by such vacation;
       WHEREAS: Katmark Partners LLC, the adjacent property owner to the west of the street
vacation was the petitioner for the right of way vacation and expressed a desire to purchase the
entire width of the right of way in conjunction with the vacation;
        WHEREAS, the other adjacent property owners to the east of the right of way were
notified of the vacation petition and option to purchase the land and did not opt to purchase
the right of way for the fair market value of the property;
       WHEREAS, Katmark Partners LLC paid for the fair market value of the entire portion of
vacated right of way;
       WHEREAS, Ordinance 2023-05 omitted language expressly conveying the title of the
entire width of the vacated right of way to Katmark Partners which was the intent of the
approval as evidenced by the staff report submitted at the time, the petition for vacation, other
supporting documentation and the subsequent actions by all parties.
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED, by the City Council of the City of South Salt Lake as
follows:
SECTION 1. Enactment. Ordinance No. 2023-05 as attached in “Exhibit A” is hereby amended
and replaced with “Exhibit B.”
SECTION 2. Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of
this ordinance is, for any reason, held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent
jurisdiction, such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision,
and such holding shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this ordinance.
SECTION 3. Conflict with Existing Ordinances, Resolutions, or Policies. To the extent that any
ordinances, resolutions, or policies of the City of South Salt Lake conflict with the provisions of
this ordinance, this ordinance shall prevail.
SECTION 4. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon Mayor’s signature
and publication, or after fifteen days of transmission to the office of the Mayor if neither
approved nor disapproved by the Mayor, and thereafter, publication.

        WHEREAS, the City Council has authority to vacate streets, rights of way, and easements, from
use by the public through a petition to vacate pursuant to Utah Code § 10-9a-609.5; and

        WHEREAS, the City received a petition to vacate public right of way located at 200 E at
approximately 3550 South through 3570 South in South Salt Lake, more particularly described in the
attached legal description (Exhibit 1) and corresponding visual (Exhibit 2), both of which are incorporated

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing pursuant to Utah
Code § 10-9a-208 of the Utah Code on January 19, 2023, to review the petition and afford the public an

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission held a properly noticed public meeting on February 16,

described above; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council held a properly noticed public hearing pursuant to Utah Code
§§ 10-9a-208 and 10-9a-609.5 on February 22, 2023, to review the petition and afford the public an
opportunity to be heard; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council finds that: (i) good cause exists for the vacation, and (ii) neither the
public interest nor any person will be materially injured by the proposed vacation; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council finds that the public right-of-way vacation is in the best interest of
the City; and

vacated were notified of the option to purchase the half width of the right of way portion adjacent to their
property; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council finds that Katmark Partners LLC has expressed an interest in
purchasing the entire width of the vacated right of way for the fair market value of the property and no
other adjacent owner expressed an interest in owning the portion of the right of way adjacent to their
property.

         NOW THEREFORE, be it ordained, by the City Council of the City of South Salt Lake that the
public right of way located at 200 E at approximately 3550 South through 3570 South in South Salt
Lake as described in the following attached legal description in Exhibit 1 and visual in Exhibit 2 to this
ordinance is hereby vacated and conveyed as described.

                      SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY ATTORNEY’S OFFICE MEMORANDUM

                     TO:       Mayor and City Council
                     FROM: Brieanne Brass, Deputy City Attorney
                     DATE:     06/12/2024
                     RE: AN ORDINANCE OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL
                         AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 2023-05 WHICH IS AN ORDINANCE
                         OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL VACATING A PUBLIC
                         RIGHT OF WAY LOCATED AT 200 EAST AT APPROXIMATELY 3550
                         SOUTH THROUGH 3570 SOUTH TO CORRECT ERRORS AND
                         OMISSIONS.



Attached is a proposed ordinance to amend the previously adopted Ordinance 2023-05, which
vacated the right of way located at 200 East and approximately 3550 South through 3570 South.
The petition to vacate was submitted by Katmark Partners, LLC (“Katmark”). The petition went
through the mandatory noticing and public hearing requirements of Utah Code Ann. 10-9a-609.5
and was ultimately approved. The ordinance was approved February 22, 2023, and recorded with
the County Recorder’s Office. The city owned the right of way property in fee simple through a
right of way dedication that occurred in the 1950s prior to the property being annexed into the
city from Salt Lake County in the 90’s. Prior to the adoption of Ordinance 2023-05, as is
required by law, the city provided notice to all adjacent property owners of their option to
purchase the right of way adjacent to their property. The only property owner to express an
interest in purchasing the property was Katmark. The city had an appraisal done and Katmark
paid the city for the fair market value of the entire width of the vacated right of way.
The ordinance omitted language of the city’s intention to convey the vacated right of way to
Katmark in fee simple. Staff is proposing the amended ordinance be approved to correct the
omission and clarify the City Council’s intention to convey the property in its entirety to
Katmark as was presented, through the petition, staff reports, meeting presentations, and other
supporting documentation to the Council at the time of approval.
This amendment is being proposed to correct an error and omission in the record regarding the
ownership of the property as the development of the property is progressing forward.

    A RESOLUTION OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL TO ADOPT AND
     OPERATE ON THE TENTATIVE GENERAL FUND BUDGET AS THE INTERIM
   GENERAL FUND BUDGET FOR THE CITY OF SOUTH SALT LAKE FISCAL YEAR
   JULY 1, 2024, THROUGH JUNE 30, 2025, UNTIL A TRUTH IN TAXATION PUBLIC
             HEARING IS HELD AND THE FINAL BUDGET IS ADOPTED

       WHEREAS, on May 8, 2024, the Budget Officer of the City of South Salt Lake (the
“City”) submitted to the South Salt Lake City Council (the “Council”) a tentative budget (the
“Tentative Budget”) including the Tentative General Fund budget, which was prepared and made
available to the public in accordance with state law;

       WHEREAS, on May 22, 2024, following a duly noticed public hearing, the Council
acknowledged and tentatively received the Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2024, to June
30, 2025, including the Tentative General Fund budget, which was officially placed in the City
Recorder’s office for inspection by the general public; and

        WHEREAS, a duly noticed public hearing on the adoption of the budget for Fiscal Year
July 1, 2024, to June 30, 2025, was held on June 5, 2024, as prescribed by The Uniform Fiscal
Procedures Act for Utah Cities;

        WHEREAS, the Council met in regular session on June 12, 2024, to consider, among
other things, adopting the Tentative General Fund budget as the Interim General Fund budget for
the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2024, and ending June 30, 2025;

       WHEREAS, because the Council proposed a tax rate that exceeds the certified tax rate,
both for general purposes and for the public safety service levy, a truth in taxation public hearing
must be held prior to adoption of a final budget; and

       WHEREAS, a truth in taxation hearing is scheduled to be held on August 13, 2024 at
7:00 pm, or as close thereafter as possible;

        WHEREAS, the City has published the necessary notice, held the required public
hearing, and has fully considered adoption of the tentative budget as the interim budget and all
items therein.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City of South Salt
Lake as follows:

SECTION I: The Tentative General Fund Budget attached hereto, incorporated herein by this
reference, is hereby appropriated for the corporate purposes and objects of the City for the fiscal
year commencing July 1, 2024, and ending June 30, 2025, and is hereby adopted as the “Interim
Budget for the General Fund of the City of South Salt Lake, Utah for the Fiscal Year 2024-2025”
(the “Budget”).

SECTION II: The Tentative General Fund Budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2024, to Jun 30, 2025,
shall be the Interim Budget for the General Fund of the City of South Salt Lake until such time
as a truth in taxation public hearing is held on August 13, 2024, and a final budget for the
General Fund of the City is adopted.
SECTION III: Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. 10-6-118, a copy of the Budget for each fund within
the Budget, including the Tentative General Fund Budget, shall be certified by the Budget
Officer and it is hereby directed that the Budget be filed with the State Auditor within 30 days
after adoption and in the Office of the City Recorder and shall be available to the public in
accordance with the law.

SECTION IV: Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of
this ordinance is, for any reason, held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent
jurisdiction, such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision, and
such holding shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this ordinance.

SECTION V. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon Mayor’s signature
and publication, or after fifteen days of transmission to the office of the Mayor if neither
approved nor disapproved by the Mayor, and thereafter, publication.

    A RESOLUTION OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL TO ADOPT AND
 OPERATE ON THE TENTATIVE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS FUND BUDGET AS THE
INTERIM CAPITAL IMPROVEMENTS FUND BUDGET FOR THE CITY OF SOUTH SALT
  LAKE FISCAL YEAR JULY 1, 2024, THROUGH JUNE 30, 2025, UNTIL A TRUTH IN
   TAXATION PUBLIC HEARING IS HELD AND THE FINAL BUDGET IS ADOPTED

       WHEREAS, on May 8, 2024, the Budget Officer of the City of South Salt Lake (the
“City”) submitted to the South Salt Lake City Council (the “Council”) a tentative budget (the
“Tentative Budget”) including the Tentative Capital Improvements Fund budget, which was
prepared and made available to the public in accordance with state law;

        WHEREAS, on May 22, 2024, following a duly noticed public hearing, the Council
acknowledged and tentatively received the Tentative Budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2024, to June
30, 2025, including the Tentative Capital Improvements Fund budget, which was officially
placed in the City Recorder’s office for inspection by the general public; and

        WHEREAS, a duly noticed public hearing on the adoption of the budget for Fiscal Year
July 1, 2024, to June 30, 2025, was held on June 5, 2024, as prescribed by The Uniform Fiscal
Procedures Act for Utah Cities;

        WHEREAS, the Council met in regular session on June 12, 2024, to consider, among
other things, adopting the Tentative Capital Improvements Fund budget as the Interim Capital
Improvements Fund budget for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 2024, and ending June 30, 2025;

       WHEREAS, because the Council proposed a tax rate that exceeds the certified tax rate,
both for general purposes and for the public safety service levy, a truth in taxation public hearing
must be held prior to adoption of a final budget; and

       WHEREAS, a truth in taxation hearing is scheduled to be held on August 13, 2024 at
7:00 pm, or as close thereafter as possible;

        WHEREAS, the City has published the necessary notice, held the required public
hearing, and has fully considered adoption of the tentative budget as the interim budget and all
items therein.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City of South Salt
Lake as follows:

SECTION I: The Tentative Capital Improvements Fund Budget attached hereto, incorporated
herein by this reference, is hereby appropriated for the corporate purposes and objects of the City
for the fiscal year commencing July 1, 2024, and ending June 30, 2025, and is hereby adopted as
the “Interim Budget for the Capital Improvements Fund of the City of South Salt Lake, Utah for
the Fiscal Year 2024-2025” (the “Budget”).

SECTION II: The Tentative Capital Improvements Fund Budget for Fiscal Year July 1, 2024, to
Jun 30, 2025, shall be the Interim Budget for the Capital Improvements Fund of the City of
South Salt Lake until such time as a truth in taxation public hearing is held on August 13, 2024,
and a final budget for the Capital Improvements Fund of the City is adopted.
SECTION III: Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. 10-6-118, a copy of the Budget for each fund within
the Budget, including the Tentative Capital Improvements Fund Budget, shall be certified by the
Budget Officer and it is hereby directed that the Budget be filed with the State Auditor within 30
days after adoption and in the Office of the City Recorder and shall be available to the public in
accordance with the law.

SECTION IV: Severability. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, or portion of
this ordinance is, for any reason, held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent
jurisdiction, such provision shall be deemed a separate, distinct, and independent provision, and
such holding shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this ordinance.

SECTION V. Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon Mayor’s signature
and publication, or after fifteen days of transmission to the office of the Mayor if neither
approved nor disapproved by the Mayor, and thereafter, publication.

      A RESOLUTION OF THE SOUTH SALT LAKE CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING A
         PROPOSED TAX RATE FOR THE GENERAL PURPOSES OF THE CITY

        WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Section 59-2-912, on or before June 22 of each
year the City of South Salt Lake (the “City”) must adopt a proposed tax rate, or a final tax rate if
it does not exceed the certified tax rate;

       WHEREAS, the City intends to adopt a proposed tax rate of .001597 for the general
purposes of the City, which tax rate exceeds the certified tax rate;

         WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. Section 59-2-919 states that a taxing entity may not levy a
tax rate that exceeds the certified tax rate unless it conducts a public hearing on the issue at
which all interested parties desiring to be heard are given an opportunity to present oral
testimony within reasonable time limits and without unreasonable restriction on the number of
individuals allowed to make public comment (“Truth in Taxation Hearing”);

        WHEREAS, the Truth in Taxation Hearing must be held at or after 6:00 pm and cannot
be held at the same time as another as the Truth in Taxation Hearing of another overlapping
taxing entity within the City;

       WHEREAS, the City will hold the Truth in Taxation Hearing, in coordination with Salt
Lake County, on a night in August 2024, at 7:00 pm, or as close thereafter as possible, to be
assigned by the County at a later time;

       WHEREAS, the Truth in Taxation Hearing date will be appropriately noticed as required
by Utah law;

       WHEREAS, upon adoption of a proposed tax rate the City shall submit a statement
containing the amount and purpose of each levy fixed by the legislative body as required by Utah
Code Ann. Section 59-2-913 and all other information required by rules of the Utah Tax
Commission to the Salt Lake County Auditor; and

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City of South Salt
Lake as follows:
