                   Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                            January 2021

We are slowly returning to more normal operational protocols related to pro-active speed
enforcement as we emerge from the pandemic. This has resulted in a significantly higher number
of citations issued this month over the last couple of months.

Also, we have made a concerted effort to focus our patrol efforts on suspected vehicle burglars,
specifically during our graveyard shift, to try to mitigate the upward trend we have experienced
during the last few months. As a result, we have made a few arrests of burglars during the month
just after the commission of the crime which allowed us to return property to victims before they
even knew they had been victimized. I believe these arrests are largely responsible for our vehicle
burglary numbers trending down during the reporting period.

Lastly, we engaged with our neighborhood watch group in the neighborhood north of Arctic Circle
due to a house renting out rooms to felons who were on parole. We conducted an investigation and
determined residents of that particular house were engaging in criminal behavior and we identified
they had warrants. We contacted the owner of the property and after working with the homeowner
for some time, we were eventually able to convince him to evict the tenants for criminal behavior.
This was a community effort to resolve a problem and is a great example of problem solving at the
neighborhood level.
