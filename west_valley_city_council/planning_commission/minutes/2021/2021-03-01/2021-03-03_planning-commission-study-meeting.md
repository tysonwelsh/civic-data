ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
Z-1-2021  Petition by by EMANUEL and LETICIA MORENO requesting a zone change
          from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to M (Manufacturing). The
          property is located at 7041 and 7055 W Gates Avenue on 0.88 acres. (Staff –
          Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

             Discussion: Steve Pastorik explained that to the west, south, and east of the
             subject location is zoned agricultural, and then you have manufacturing zoning to
             the north. Aside from other code violations; an enforcement case was started on
             the subject location back in October of 2020 because the applicant is running a
             stucco business that is not allowed in the agriculture zone. Therefore they did not
             have a business license. Steve said this application would help put the applicants

               come into compliance. Steve did tell the Planning Commission that they do not
               need to approve this application. He stated if the Commission denied this
               application, the business could no longer run in this location. If the Commission
               approves the zone change, the applicant's next step would be to apply for a
               conditional use permit because their business includes outside storage. Steve
               explained that the house on the western parcel is being used as the office for their
               business. Steve noted a recent development that has a development agreement
               with conditions to protect the adjacent residential zones. Staff recommends a
               development agreement for this application for similar reasons.

               Commissioner Winters is in favor of a development agreement. He asked Steve if
               the house is only being used as an office or if someone is living there. Steve said
               the applicant told him it is strictly being used as an office.

               Commissioner Fuller asked Steve if there are any current residents on that street.
               Steve pointed out two residential homes on the aerial photo. He will double-check
               the number of homes and get back to the Commission.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if a masonry wall and setback requirement would
               apply. Steve explained that with a shop/warehouse building, there is a 20-foot
               setback requirement between residential and industrial uses, and they are required
               to put up a masonry wall. Steve then looked at the parcel again and noticed there
               would not be enough room for a 20-foot setback, and he will need to discuss that
               with the applicants.

               Commissioner Fuller asked if Steve had any residents call about this application.
               Steve said he had not heard anything so far.


ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-1-2021 Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting a zone text change to amend
          residential landscaping and recreation space standards in Titles 7 and 9. (Staff –
          Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov) Continued from
          the February 24th public hearing.

               Discussion: Steve Pastorik recapped the discussion from the February 17, 2021
               study meeting. Steve researched how West Valley City's standards for open space
               compare to other surrounding cities. He said it is hard to prepare an exact
               comparison because each city has there own definitions and different ways to
               calculate their percentages. Steve then presented his research to the Commission.

               Steve then addressed the Commission's question about adding a daycare as an
               amenity. He stated a daycare could be listed as an optional amenity. He also
               explained that the City Council would like the addition of car charging stations as
               an amenity, so there will be further discussion.

               Commissioner Lovato asked if West Valley City is the only city that looks at

               indoor space as open space. Steve said that it does appear that way; however,
               some cities do require indoor amenities. Commissioner Lovato wonders what the
               response will be when we explain that we are trading open space for recreational
               space. Steve said we are not trading one for the other. The recreational space can
               still be outside. He explained the newer developments seem to take up much
               more land minimizing the outdoor space, so; they will be using the inside of the
               building for recreation space in those cases.

               Commissioner McEwen feels that having amenities inside the building will limit
               the number of people using them. He feels outdoor amenities are more inviting.

               Commissioner Winters asked Steve to remind him what the standards were
               previously. Steve explained there was no landscape percentage per se. We had an
               open space percentage that differed between zones and types of developments.
               Commissioner Winters feels this application will increase the percentage of open
               space overall. Steve said the townhomes under 20 units/acre would keep the same
               requirement of 50% open space.

               Commissioner McEwen asked if it would be possible to look at how the amenities
               are laid out with a new build. Steve said we could schedule some time between a
               finished project and when the tenants move in to have the Commission visit the
               site.

SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-2-2021 Petition by SCOTT THORSEN, representing the property owner, is requesting
           preliminary approval for the Lee Creek Subdivision. The subdivision will consist
           of 2 lots on 32 acres. The subdivision is located at approximately 5993 West
           2100 South and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-
           3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Steve Lehman explained that this proposal goes along with
               application C-6-2021 that Jody Knapp will present later in this meeting. Steve
               explained the right-of-way for 5900 West was constructed by UDOT. However,
               formal improvements were not installed as part of their project. The developer of
               this property will be required to put in curb, gutter, and sidewalk on the west side
               of the right-of-way. Steve pointed out the highlighted area that runs between the
               two new lots. This is a cross access easement to be use for semi-tractor deliveries
               and other parking needs. Steve stated that the public works department is still
               discussing whether the right-of-way will be dedicated to the City. Storm water
               and flood plain areas are also being evaluated with this project.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-6-2021 Petition by CIR ENGINEERING requesting a conditional use approval for
         Brennan Lee Creek, an office/warehouse facility. The property is located at 5993
         West 2100 South on 32.70 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody
         Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

               Discussion: Jody Knapp explained this application goes along with application
               SMI-2-2021, presented by Steve Lehman earlier in this meeting. She said the
               applicants are looking to put in two large office/warehouse buildings. Jody
               explained the layout of the proposed building. She feels where the front of the
               building is in an interesting place and would like feedback from the Commission
               about the placement.

               Jody explained that there would be landscaping added along the edge of the
               subject property. She also feels some landscaping should be added to the parking
               lot's edge giving it a finished look. Jody said she would also like input from the
               Commission about landscaping.

               Commissioner Winters asked if Jody is concerned about the more interesting part
               of the building not being visible. Jody said yes, the back of the building would be
               most visible, and she wonders if they should flip the building to have the front be
               more visible. She also isn't sure how much of a challenge that would be for the
               applicant to convert the building. Commissioner Winters said we could ask the
               applicant during the public hearing if they feel they would want to have the
               building flipped. He did say that he agrees with the addition of landscaping to the
               parking lot edge.

               Commissioner Porter said he isn't as concerned about the placement of the
               building. However, they could slide the building to the south and add more
               parking. He does feel they need the curb gutter along the new road along with the
               suggested landscaping. Jody said she would follow up with the applicant about
               the curb, gutter, and sidewalk before the public hearing.

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 5:05 p.m.
