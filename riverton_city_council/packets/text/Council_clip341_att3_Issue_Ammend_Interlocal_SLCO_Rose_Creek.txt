FROM:          Trace Robinson, Public Works Director

ITEM:          Resolution authorizing the Mayor to execute an amendment to the Interlocal
               Cooperation Agreement with Salt County to provide additional funding in the
               amount of $225,000 to Riverton City for partial improvements to the Rose Creek
               Channel between Mountain View and 4250 W.
SUMMARY:

Included in the Master Development Agreement with Suburban Land Reserve (SLR), Riverton
agreed to relocate the Rose Creek Channel to an alignment south of its existing location.

Recently, Riverton City Public Works construct 4500 W from 13400 S to 13800 S as a primary
access to the new Jordan School District - High School located at approximately 13900 S and
4450 W. One of the major tasks to complete the road project was to relocate Rose Creek
approximately 150 feet south of its existing location. In order to complete the relocation, the Army
Corps of Engineers requires that the channel be improved to increase the capacity to carry the
100 Year storm event. Rose Creek is primarily used for storm drainage and falls under the
jurisdiction of Salt Lake County (SLCO). Since it is a County facility, the Public Works Department
approached County Flood Control and solicited their help in partially funding the project. The
project was broken into four phases. Phases I, II, and III will be completed May 2020 at a cost of
$ 1,350,000 of which Salt Lake County Flood Control contributed $925,000. Phase IV of the
project consists of constructing a box culvert under the Welby Canal. The estimated cost of this
construction is $300,000. Riverton Public Works requested additional funding to help finish the
project. Again Salt Lake County Flood Control stepped and through this amended agreement will
provided an additional $225,000.

This amendment to the original agreement will enable the City to use these funds.

FISCAL IMPACT: $ 225,000

FUNDING SOURCE: Salt Lake County
