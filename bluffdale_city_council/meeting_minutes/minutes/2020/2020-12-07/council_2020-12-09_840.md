3.     Redevelopment Agency Training and Project Area Discussion.

Jay Springer from Smith Hartvigsen who serves as legal counsel for the Redevelopment Agency
(" RDA" ) was introduced. He presented an overview of what the Redevelopment Agency does and
explained that it is a separate legal entity that has powers that the City does not. The Board, in this
case, is the City Council and the staff is shared. The Agency creates project areas with the goal of
the project area being to utilize its powers to create incentives and open up an area of the map that
staff determines would be beneficial to incenti vize. The area chosen could be a single developer
or a broad multi-use development.

Mr. Springer explained that the idea of tax increment takes a piece of time and makes the decision
to incentivi ze anything above it. This involves agreements with taxing entities to determine the
time period and tax increases that will be used for the Agency powers. The more successful , the
more development the greater the prope11y tax and sales tax in some cases, and the more money
that goes to the Agency.

Mr. Springer stated that the idea is at that the end of the term, all of the different entities benefit
from the incentivization. The process involves creating a project area through the Agency,
attracting development, and administering the project area incentives to continue attracting more
development. The more times the City goes through thi s cycle, the more increment is generated,
and the more taxable value is ultimately created at the end of the term.

The tool is used as a catalyst for new deve lopment. If no development takes place it does not hurt
the Agency or the City. The Agency then distributes the funds and does the administrative work
of preparing the reports and deciding what activities are appropriate to incentivize. One of the
benefits of the Agency is that it has powers that the City does not. For example, the Agency can
distribute money to a developer. The City Council has control of bow the Agency operates, what
is incentivized , and how it takes place.

Mr. Springer presented a li st of different tools the funds can be used for. He stated that there are
some restrictions and things money has to be used for, such as housing. Development can be
incentivized by building a pad even if there is no developer or store. This is a way for Bluffdale
