                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 22-11

 A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL APPOINTING A VOTING
 REPRESENTATIVE TO THE WELBY JACOBS CANAL, UTAH AND SALT LAKE
CANAL, UTAH LAKE CANAL, SOUTH JORDAN CANAL AND BECKSTEAD DITCH

        WHEREAS, Utah Code provides that, in third class cities, the Mayor, with the advice and
consent of the Council, may appoint individuals to fulfill offices as created by the Riverton City
resolutions; and

        WHEREAS, the Mayor has recommended the below names individual to fulfill the office
indicated; and

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

      1.   The Riverton City Council hereby consents to the appointment of Stacie Olsen as
           Voting Representative for Welby Jacob Canal, Utah and Salt Lake Canal, Utah Lake
           Canal, South Jordan Canal and Beckstead Ditch.
      2.   Appointment shall run until a successor is appointed.
      2.   This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
