PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Donald J. Eyre Jr. a Lion’s Club representative.

UTE STAMPEDE UPDATE BY CHRIS MEMMOTT:

    Chris Memmott speaking for the Ute Stampede Committee reported that the celebration is moving
forward per health department, Utah State, and PRCA Rodeo guidelines. He said that the traditional
festivities would be happening. He stated that not everyone is going to agree with what the committee
has decided to do during this time of the COVID-19 pandemic but they are remaining positive. Mayor
Nielson said that we need to respect how others feel. Mr. Memmott said that we need to show a
unified front. The committee will recommend that participants wear masks and respect social
distancing. Jeanne Worwood, representing the Mammoth Parade Committee, spoke of the parade
protocol that will be conducted. Councilor Worwood expressed his appreciation to the Ute Stampede
Committee.

LEONARD AND MYRNA TRAUNTVEIN NAMED GRAND MARSHALLS OF THE MAMMOTH PARADE:

  Mayor Nielson named Leonard and Myrna Trauntvein as the Grand Marshalls of the Mammoth
Parade. He read a special tribute to them stating their many accomplishments and acts of service to the
community. Mayor Nielson presented them with a framed certificate and the press took a picture of the
Trauntveins, Mayor, and the Ute Stampede Committee for the local paper.

COUNTY COMMISSIONER CLINT PAINTER AND JAMIE GARRETT REQUEST PERMISSION FOR MAIN STREET
DRAG BY XTREME RACING EVENT PARTICIPANTS:

   County Commissioner Clint Painter and Jamie Garrett requested from the council permission for the
Xtreme Racing Event participants to drag main on their motorcycles on Friday, June 26th. They explained
how the they are planning to have many precautions in place to make the event as safe as possible.
City Attorney Wright recommended that the county obtain a parade permit from UDOT. Mr. Atkinson
said that our police officers could help shut down the traffic.

ORIDNANCE 06-16-2020-ZERO LOT LINE AND SIGN CODE CHANGES ADOPTED:

  Mr. Atkinson updated the council that in January the council passed some extensive code changes to
the zero-lot line code and temporary sign code. The zero lot line changes loosened regulations to zero
lot lines to allow for that type of development. Within the zero lot line code changes, the language
wasn’t very clear on how to calculate the minimum lot sizes and frontage requirements. The proposed
ordinance clarifies the calculations and sets forth specific minimums rather than a formula. He
continued explaining that the proposed ordinance also removes the time restrictions prior to an election
on when a sign can be displayed. Councilor Jones moved to adopt Ordinance 06-16-2020: AN
ORDINANCE OF NEPHI CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AMENDMENT TO SECTIONS 10-18-17 AND 10-19-14 OF
THE NEPHI CITY CODE. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

GUN RANGE PROPERTY ANNEXATION AREA LEVEL CHANGE APPROVED:

  Councilor Ostler moved to change the annexation area near the gun range property owned by Nephi
City from a level 3 to a level 2. Councilor Memmott seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

CARES ACT FFA GRANT AGREEEMENTS APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented two agreements for the council’s approval. The first agreement was the
Coronavirus Aid, Relief, and Economic Security Act (CARES Act) Airport Grant Agreement that could be
used to help fund a waterline to the airport. The second agreement was between the Aeronautical
Operations Division of the State of Utah, Utah Department of Transportation “Division” which appoints
the Division as agent for handling the grant funding from the CARES Act. Councilor Worwood moved to
authorize the Mayor to sign the CARES Airport Grant Agreement. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion,
which passed on a unanimous vote. Councilor Worwood moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the
Cooperative Agency Agreement. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The motion carried on a
unanimous vote.

MAX WILLIMAS LAND AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson presented an agreement for the council’s approval for the purchase of land near Red
Cliffs Elementary that could be used for a future park and storm drainage purposes. The owner of the
land, Max Williams, is asking for $65,000 and for the City to pay the closing costs. Mr. Atkinson stated
that there are fund available within the capital projects budget that has been set aside for flood and
drainage purposes. Mr. Atkinson suggested that we use interest earnings on the capital projects fund to
reimburse the drainage and flood account for future needs. Councilor Jones moved to authorize the
Mayor to sign the land purchase agreement. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed
on a unanimous vote.

REVENUE SHORTFALL POLICY APPROVED:

   Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council a Revenue Shortfall Policy which outlined reductions in
services and expenditures if the city ever experiences revenue shortfalls due to downward economic
times. He explained that all options in the policy should be reviewed in the context of the expected
shortfall duration. Mr. Atkinson prefaced his remarks to say that we are not currently in a revenue
shortfall situation, but this policy would prepare for the day if the city ever is in a position to have
decreased revenues. Councilor Memmott moved to adopt the Revenue Shortfall Policy. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

  Mr. Atkinson reported that UDOT is planning road construction from 300 West to Ash Grove Cement
plant on Highway 132. One of the improvements will be a turning lane at the landfill turn off.

NO CITY COUNCIL UPDATES

which passed on a unanimous vote.
