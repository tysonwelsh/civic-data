2. Presentations and Report
2.1 Leadership Training- Judge Morgan Cummings
Judge Cummings presented a training on healthy conflict.

2.2 Ranked Choice Voting Presentation- Kelleen Potter
Mayor Johnson noted that this item will be heard at the next Council meeting.

2.3 Power Plant Expansion Presentation- Joel Eves
Joel Eves, Power Director, presented on the possible expansion of the natural gas generators. He
stated that they have room for 5 more. He noted that the volatility in the market is getting worse
which creates higher energy costs. They are able to control some of that volatility by having their
own power plant. He acknowledged that there is a push for renewables energy such as wind and
solar, but these resources are inconsistent and unpredictable. He stated that if the Council is
willing to move forward with the expansion, then the first step will be to authorize a letter of
intent for the generator purchase. After the letter is signed it could take up to 1 year before it’s
installed.

2.4. Finance Report
Dean Lundell gave an update on the financial state of the City. He noted that the sales tax dollars
received in December was the highest ever, so it is trending better than what was forecast.

5. Consideration of Ordinance #17-2022 a General Plan Amendment from Commercial to
   Mixed-Use on 0.51 acres located at approximately 2600 North 1200 West.

    Ryan Hales was representing the applicant. He stated that this is a small-scale development on
    only a ½ acre. He also presented the concept plan for the mixed-use development which
    includes 6 residential units. He said that this would be a good transition from the commercial
    area to the residential zone. It’s also a nice buffer for the trail corridor. There was concern from
    the Development Review Committee regarding the large truck access to the power substation.
    Mr. Hales showed on the concept plan how there is adequate room for a truck. Mr. Hales stated
    that he would be willing to enter into a development agreement.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to approve Ordinance #17-2022 a General Plan
                Amendment from Commercial to Mixed-Use on 0.51 acres located at
                approximately 2600 North 1200 West; including any Development Review
                Committee and Planning Commission comments; and direct staff to draft a
                development agreement and include that the agreement not exceed 6 residential
                units. Councilor Hancock seconded the motion.

     Councilor Hancock suggested amending the motion to include that the agreement also address
     residential parking stalls, and that the General Plan amendment is subject to the approval of a
     development agreement. Also include the findings that this is a good location for Mixed Use
     with close proximity to a TOD zone, and also with the findings that they are willing to move
     forward with approval based on the small scale of the development.

    Amended Motion: Councilor Condie amended his motion to include Councilor Hancock’s
            comments. Councilor Hancock seconded the amendment.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
