CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider continued sponsorship of the Saint George Market event for
        Indigo Klabanoff on March 5, 2022.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Market fundraising event for Girls on the
          Run at Town Square on Saturday, March 5, 2022, from 10:00 a.m.- 6:00 p.m.
          Setup will occur March 3-4. Requesting same sponsorship for October 20-22,
          2022 market. The sponsorship request includes: 1) Fee waiver for the park
          reservation fee ($150/hour); 2) Fee waiver for the Sub-License fees; 3 )Fee
          waiver for the Special Event Permit Fee ($150/day); 4) Provide extra trash cans;
          5 )Firetruck on display; 6) City assistance with marketing on the electronic
          billboard at Bluff/Blvd, Inside St. George Magazine and perhaps social media; 7)
          15 cones borrowed from the City of St. George; and 8) Canopy for info booth.
          Staff recommends approval of this sponsorship request, with the conditions that
          the organizers recruit their own garbage cans/trash maintenance and that a
          special event application is approved.

      b. Consider approval of a contract with Andritz Separation, Inc for the
         repair of a sludge dewatering centrifuge at the wastewater treatment
         plant.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This contract is to repair a centrifuge
          machine at the wastewater treatment plant. We are able to do general

           maintenance and some small repair with City staff.   Major repair work is done by
           the manufacturer. Staff recommends approval.

      c. Consider approval to award bid to Trapp Construction, LLC for
         construction of Temple Springs Park Phase 1.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Bids were opened on December 21,
           2021 for construction of the Temple Springs Park Phase 1 (21-097). The project
           includes colored concrete and asphalt trails, bridge construction, boulder
           retaining walls, site grading and revegetation. It is proposed to include some
           additive alternate bid items and remove some bid items as shown on the
           attached bid tabulation and plan. The City received three bids on the project and
           the low bid is from Trapp Construction in the amount of $718,335.00. Of the
           budgeted amount, approximately $42,000 is encumbered for consulting fees.
           This means the budget is short by $319,335. It is proposed to use funds from
           the Outdoor Stage at Town Square to cover the remaining unfunded amount.
           The Outdoor Stage budget is $750,000.

      d. Consider approval to award bid to Interstate Rock Products for the 3000
         East 1580 South to 2000 South Project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This project includes roadway
           improvements, curb & gutter, sidewalk, trail, significant drainage and irrigation
           piping, and signing and striping for improvements along 3000 East from 1580
           South to 2000 South. Staff recommends approval.

      e. Consider approval of a lease agreement with De Lage Landen Public
         Finance and Intermountain Golf Cars for new golf carts.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Our five-year golf cart lease is expiring
           and the carts at all four courses are worn out and currently out of warranty. This
           is a new, five-year lease agreement for 282 "Club Car" golf carts for the four city
           golf courses. After a thorough review of several proposals for golf carts, service
           and warranty, City staff recommends approval. The first payment on this loan will
           be due in August of 2023. Staff recommends approval.

      f.   Consider approval to award bid to Tri-State Electric for the Riverside
           Drive at Riverbend Drive traffic signal project.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This will be for the installation of a new
           traffic signal Riverside Drive and Riverbend Drive. This was a formal bid. Only
           one bid was received. Staff recommends approval.

      g. Consider a Single Aeronautical Service Operator (SASO) Operating and
         Lease Agreement between the City and Infiniti Aviation, LLC at the
         Airport, for sublease area within Above View Jet Center's current lease
         hold area.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This SASO will be for student pilot,
           private pilot, instrument pilot, commercial pilot, flight instructor and bi-annual
           flight reviews. The business will begin with one aircraft, a Diamond DA20-C1
           Katana. A second aircraft will be purchased in the future as business grows.
           Staff recommends approval.

      h. Consider a Single Aeronautical Service Operator (SASO) Operating and
         Lease Agreement between the City and Area 60, LLC at the Airport, for
         Lot 52G.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Area 60, LLC is the building owner side
           of a Series LLC that includes Sandstone Aviation LLC and Area 60 LLC. Area 60
           will own all of the buildings and Sandstone will own and operate all fueling
           operations/equipment. The owners of both LLC's are Robert Fahnestock and
           Joseph Campbell. The Area 60 SASO agreement will include aircraft
           maintenance, flight instruction and avionics repair as well as some other services
           that are allowed by a SASO operator. Staff recommends approval.

      i.   Consider approval of a car rental concession and lease agreement with
           Rabbit Rentals, LLC at the Airport.

           BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The airport is currently served by rental
           car companies Enterprise, Avis/Budget, and Hertz. This is a request to add
           Rabbit Rentals to the rental car options. Rabbit Rentals will utilize Unit #4
           leasehold area and will occupy five (5) parking stalls. Staff recommends
           approval.
