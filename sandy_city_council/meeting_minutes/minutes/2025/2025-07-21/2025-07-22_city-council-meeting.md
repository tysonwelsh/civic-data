General Citizen Comment Period (Will take place once the Work Session is complete)

                      Council Chair Brooke D'Sousa invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                      Comment.

                      Ms. Pat Jones thanked the Council for holding these housing workshops. No other city is
                      taking this approach. She recommended the Council hear a presentation from Mike
                      Carey. She expressed concerns with the costs associated with the Alta Canyon Sports
                      Center rebuild and thought the attendance at the center was much lower than recreation
                      facilities.

                      Mr. Mike Carey would like an opportunity to present to the Council during one of the
                      upcoming housing workshops. The high growth that was predicted hasn't happened.
                      Cities need to be nimble. Median home prices are still too high but suggested the need to
                      look at demographics and the realignment of school boundaries.

                      Cathy Spuck spoke about the impact of storm water detention areas requirements. She
                      spoke about the impact of capital gains tax when selling a home and suggested the
                      federal government needs to provide assistance too.

                      Ms. Deb Sussman spoke about lot restrictions and affordable housing opportunities on
                      existing lots. She would like the Council to look at options outside of apartments and
                      condos and applauded the Council for taking on this issue.

                      Public comment closed.

                      Council moved to Standing Reports.

1.         25-232                 Building our Future: A Community Housing Workshop Series, Workshop
                                  Two - Where are we now?
                   Attachments:   July 22 2025 Memo_Housing Workshop Series
                                  Wasatch Front Regional Council Slides
                                  Cowboy Partners Presentation
                                  Kem C. Gardner Presentation

                                  Council Chair Brooke D'Sousa welcomed those in attendance. The Council held a
                                  workshop focused on affordable housing. This is the second workshop of four that will be
                                  held by the Council.

                                  Lynn Pace, City Attorney, acted as the workshop moderator and provided an overview of
                                  the evening's workshop.

                                  The first presentation was provided by Lee Dial at Cowboy Partners and Cowboy
                                  Properties. Mr. Dial provided an overview from a developer's perspective on the current
                                  barriers and opportunities in addressing housing needs in Sandy. He reviewed the impact
                                  of the following cost factors on housing construction and development: interest rates,
                                  construction costs, land and holding costs, connection and impact fees, and investor
                                  returns. He also reviewed how cities can help their communities. He provided the
                                  following strategies that cities can employ to assist their communities in housing: utilize
                                  RDA's or housing authorities, purposeful planning, streamline the plan review and
                                  permitting process, and offer impact and connection fee waivers. Council questions
                                  followed.

                                  The second presentation was provided by Megan Padjen, Community and Economic
                                  Development Director at Wasatch Front Regional Council. Ms. Padjen reviewed the
                                  Wasatch Choice Vision - 2050 Vision for the Wasatch Front overlaying transportation and
                                  land use. Wasatch Choice Vision is our communities' shared vision for coordinated
                                  transportation investments, development patterns, and economic opportunities. She
                                  reviewed key strategies for a thriving region and communities: transportation choices,
                                  housing options, parks and public spaces, and cities and town centers. Ms. Padjen
                                  addressed housing affordability, options, and how this is impacting transportation.
                                  Council questions occurred throughout the presentation.

                                  Council convened a recess at 6:37 pm.
                                  Council reconvened at 6:45 pm.

                                  Djan Eskic, Senior Research Fellow with the Kem C. Gardner Policy Institute presented
                                  on the housing market in Sandy City. Mr. Eskic reviewed the population growth,
                                  demographics, housing demands, median sales price in Sandy, median household
                                  income, and the impact of interest rates on the housing market. Council questions and
                                  comments followed.

                                  Lynn Pace thanked all the presenters for joining the Council this evening. He also
                                  provided an overview of the next two housing workshops. The next workshop will convene
                                  on Tuesday, September 2nd. Staff will assemble a list of issues for the Council to review
                                  and prioritize. The next two housing workshops will be interactive.

Standing Reports

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Council Director, reviewed upcoming agenda items. The Council will need
                      to amend the meeting schedule to reinstate the September 2nd Council meeting. There
                      will also be an RDA meeting next week.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Alison Stroud attended the ribbon cutting for the Public Works Fleet
                      building. Many staff acknowledged the support of the Council. She also mentioned that
                      she and Council Member Sharkey were attending the Alta Canyon design and
                      construction meetings. The Sandy Youth Council helped Parks crews with weeding and
                      landscaping the area around the Sandy Senior Center. The Farmer's Market and Food
                      Truck night were great events.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey attended the ribbon cutting of the Public Works Fleet
                      building. It is a beautiful building and she acknowledged the Council for their support and
                      work on this project. This is a big accomplishment for the Council. She was participating
                      in the design meeting for the Alta Canyon Sports Center. The center will serve the
                      community and is a significant improvement to the current center. At the last RSL game,
                      we were joined by state legislators and council members from Heriman and Midvale. The
                      Sandy Youth Council also utilized the stands tickets as an acknowledgement for their
                      service to the community.
