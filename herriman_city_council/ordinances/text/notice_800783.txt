PMN RECORDER ADOPTION NOTICE (summary only - not the full ordinance text)

The Herriman City Council hereby gives notice of the adoption of an Ordinance at the meeting held on December 14, 2022, at 4:00 p.m. in the City Hall Council Chambers, 5355 West Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, as follows: Ordinance No. 2022-46 An ordinance of the City Council of the City of Herriman, Utah amending Herriman City Code §10-11-6 (C)(7) and §10-15B-6(B), which relate to trails on steep slopes. A copy is available to interested persons by contacting the City Recorder's Office during regular business hours. /s/ Jackie Nostrom, City Recorder
