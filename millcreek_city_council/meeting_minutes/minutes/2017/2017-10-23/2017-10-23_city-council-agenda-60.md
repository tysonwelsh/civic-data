3. Financial Matters

3.1 Public Hearing on Ordinance No. 17-48, an Ordinance of the Millcreek Council
Appropriating $55,000 to Utah Venture Outdoors
The Mayor said pursuant to state law, a study was done for appropriations to another
entity. This hearing is the chance for the public to make comment.

No public comments.

The Mayor said the City hasn’t appropriated this much previously. He would like to
amend the ordinance to read that the appropriation will not exceed $55,000. There is
$56,000 in the budget, but he would like to see what Utah Venture Outdoors proposes
before the full amount is allocated. Venture Outdoors should provide a budget before the
Council settles on an amount. The line item in the City’s budget is for $56,000, and it
includes Venture Outdoors and the Art Councils. Council Member Marchant asked about
the screen that was purchased. Mayor Silvestrini said the funds for that came from the
last fiscal year. It’s not in the FY *18 Budget. Council Member Catten questioned that if
Utah Venture Outdoors wanted more in a subsequent year, would they have to go through
the process again or would it be capped at $55,000. The Mayor said it is up to the Council
to decide. If they asked for more than what was in the budget, it would need to be a
budget amendment. Council Member Catten said she wants Utah Venture Outdoors to be
held accountable for all monies. In the past, they have brought a reconciliation of
expenditures and made proposals from that for the next year. The Mayor said the City
won’t write the check until the Council sees the proposal. Mr. Geilmann said the study
does have to be done every year. John Brem said if the City is giving money to an outside
agency, a study is required. Council Member Jackson asked if the Arts Council wanted
money, would they have to go through the process too. The Mayor said yes, but first the
Arts Council must organize themselves as a city committee. Council Member Uipi asked
how much the study costs every year; $250. Brem said a study could be done for multiple
entities at the same time to save money.

Council Member Jackson moved to adopt Ordinance No. 17-48 as amended for
appropriations to not exceed $55,000 consistent with budgeted funds. Council Member
Marchant seconded the motion. Mayor Silvestrini called for the vote. The motion passed
unanimously.

Mr. Geilmann said a previous resolution is now in effect for the $4,000 appropriation for
MillFreaks.

4.2 Discussion and Consideration of Ordinance No. 17-46, an Ordinance of the
Millcreek Council Amending Title 10 of City Code Regarding Camping on Public
Grounds, Streets, Parks, and Playgrounds

The Mayor said this ordinance was discussed in prior meetings and it regulates camping
on public property. The ordinance sets up a procedure for the public to apply for a permit
for camping and for law enforcement to regulate it. The Mayor added some amendments
to Mr. Brem’s prior version and explained those changes (in packet).

Council Member Uipi asked if public property included UDOT space between retaining
walls and property. The Mayor said yes. The exception is that if the entity allows it on
their property, but the public would still need to apply for a permit for public property.

Council Member Catten asked about slacklines, hammocks, and day tents in the parks,
and if this ordinance included those uses The Mayor said there is a broad definition of
camping equipment and what is allowed. He said it’s not all encompassing but it is a tool
if a problem arises. The penalty for violation is a misdemeanor B.

Council Member Marchant moved to approve Ordinance No. 17-46, an Ordinance
Amending Title 10 Regarding Camping on Public Grounds, Streets, Parks, and
Playgrounds. Council Member Jackson seconded the motion. Mayor Silvestrini called for
the vote. The motion passed unanimously.
