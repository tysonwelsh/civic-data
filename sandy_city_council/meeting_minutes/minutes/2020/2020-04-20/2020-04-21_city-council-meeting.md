Council Member Business

                            Council Member Christensen updated the Council on the Historic Committee's decision
                            not to meet, the CDBG Committee, Hale Centre Theatre, and the Chamber of Commerce.

                            Council Member Houseman expressed thanks for Communications sharing information
                            about the Sandy Club.

                            Council Member Sharkey noted the Public Utilities Advisory Board is meeting again. She
                            noted the Utah League of Cities and Towns is working hard on many new issues.

                            Council Member Zoltanski appreciated the inclusion of standing reports as part of the
                            agenda. She commented on Earth Day upcoming, and the use and need to care for
                            Dimple Dell Park and other Sandy area parks. She mentioned resources for businesses
                            and the community on Congressman McAdams & Senator Romney's websites.

                            Council Member Christensen noted there were not details available for the CDBG grant
                            process yet.

                            Council Member Stroud provided an update on the Sandy Arts Guild which is still working
                            hard but had to cancel some performances. She thanked the Fire Department for the
                            virtual tour provided to her students.

                            Council Member Robinson thanked Mayor Bradburn for reaching out to Draper City.

Mayor's Report

                            Mayor Bradburn provided a COVID-10 operational update. Testing is a predominant
                            concern and more is needed to generate data. 98% of Utahns have not been tested yet.
                            More testing is needed to safely move through the "Urgent" phase of Utah Leads
                            Together plan.

CAO Report

                            CAO Matt Huish reported on the latest version of the State's economic recovery plan
                            discussed on the Utah League of Cities and Towns conference call yesterday.
                            Department directors are reporting there has been no uptick in employees facing
                            COVID-19 or the need to quarantine. The City subscribes to Target Solutions and COVID
                            resources are being provided to City employees. Corrosion sampling as a result of the
                            water contamination was offered to each home in the affected zones. The City collected
                            226 samples for an 84% participation rate. Testing will continue at regular intervals. We
                            received 2020 Best of State recognition for The Cairns.

5.         20-115                 Council Member Stroud recommending adoption of Ordinance 20-01,
                                  amending the Sandy City Animal Services Code to increase from two to
                                  three the number of dogs allowed per residence, clarify leash
                                  requirements, expand the definition of qualified rescue organizations, and
                                  making minor nonsubstantive changes.
                   Attachments:   Ordinance 20-01
                                  Exhibit A to Ord. 20-01: Animal Services Amendment
                                  Dog Limit by City
                                  Animal Code Pros_Cons
                                  Land Development Animal Code 21-11-3 Redline

                                  Council Member Stroud summarized the recommended amendments and the research
                                  she conducted to explore increasing the number of dogs allowed per residence in Sandy
                                  City without a Hobby License.

                                  Council questions and comments followed. Animal Services Direct Ian Williams
                                  discussed issues with increasing the number of dogs. He supports an amendment for 3
                                  dogs, possibly 4, but cautioned not to allow an unlimited number per residence. He
                                  reported on the current numbers of Hobby Licenses. Discussion ensued about the
                                  appropriate increase to the ordinance.

                                  The Chair opened public comment. Dustin Fratto provided direction on how to participate
                                  in public comment.

                                  There were no public comments.

                                  Council Member Robinson noted support for the ordinance following the motion. Council
                                  Member Sharkey clarified that the motion was for 3 dogs.
                                  A motion was made by Alison Stroud, seconded by Zach Robinson to 1) adopt
                                  Ordinance 20-01 and 2) direct staff to proceed with the conforming amendment to
                                  the Sandy Land Use Code through the Planning Commission process... The
                                  motion carried by the following vote:
