Public Comments

               Ed Martinez stated he would like to hold a car show on Labor Day in the downtown
area of Magna. Instead of naming the show the VFW Car Show, it will be named the Magna
Community Car Show, and any proceeds will be donated to the community. He would like the
support of the Magna Metro Township Council to hold the event.

Action/Discussion Items

8400 West Project, UDOT, & Runaway Truck Lanes

               Steven Kuhlmeier, Engineer, Salt Lake County Engineering & Flood Control,
stated there is no known justification for why the runaway truck lane was installed. Aligning
Cordero Drive would benefit the community. UDOT wants Magna Metro Township to formally put
in a request to have the runaway truck ramp removed. He has drafted a letter requesting the
removal of the runaway truck ramp and a request for a new corridor agreement. The new corridor

agreement outlines the community needs as they have changed. He is requesting that the Council
authorize the Mayor to sign the letter to be sent to UDOT. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal
Services District (MSD) approved funding for the 8400 sidewalks.

              Council Member Prokopis stated he would prefer to eliminate the runaway truck

passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
2022 Beer Tax Funds

                Council Member Hull stated in the past, beer tax funds have been passed through
to the coalition. For some reason, those funds have been put in the Magna Metro Township
account and the funds cannot be transferred until the Council approves it. The funds go towards
drug and alcohol prevention and this year it will also help with the youth court.

               Council Member Prokopis, seconded by Council Member Pierce, moved to
approve the transfer of beer tax funds. The motion passed unanimously.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
ChamberWest

               Council Member Hull stated she would like the Council to consider joining
ChamberWest. It has over 400 members and offers various types of support. If Magna joined, the
entire west side would be united. Magna’s membership fee would be $3,000. There are benefits
that Magna does not currently get from the Magna Chamber.

                Council Member Pierce stated she is not comfortable moving forward without
talking to the Magna Chamber.

                Council Member Prokopis stated it would be nice to have Magna Chamber’s
blessing. It has failed to thrive for years.

              Council Member Hull stated she has tried to get ahold of Brian Burrows,
President, Magna Chamber of Commerce, but he has not called back. She will reach out to Magna
Chamber and ChamberWest to have them present to the Council during its next meeting.

City Manager Transition

               David Brickey stated he met with Rori Andreason and Paul Ashton about the city
manager transition. Ms. Andreason will continue to help with the transition. A purchasing card has
been ordered but has not arrived. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) has
asked him not to make purchases on his personal credit card. He will wait until the purchasing
card arrives before ordering a computer. He met with Brian Hartsell, Associate General Manager,
MSD, and Marla Howard, General Manager, MSD, last Wednesday. A resident contacted him
regarding a neighborhood cleanup.

            Mayor Peay stated the best way to handle a neighborhood cleanup is to offer free
dump vouchers.

               Council Member Prokopis stated if the resident is looking to clean up their own
yard, they can rent a dumpster.

                  Mr. Brickey stated he is meeting with Council Members to figure out what their
priorities are.

                  Paul Ashton stated he is working on an employment agreement for Mr. Brickey.

             Mr. Brickey stated he has reviewed the agreement a little bit. He has some minor
changes he would like to make.
