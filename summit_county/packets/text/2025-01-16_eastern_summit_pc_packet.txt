Proposal   
 
The applicant is requesting a Plat Amendment to amend the plat notes regulating height within 
the Green Hill Subdivision (Parcels GRNHL-1-AM, GRNHL-2-AM, GRNHL-3-AM, GRNHL-4-AM, 
and GRNHL-5-AM), located off Green Hill Lane, Wanship, Summit County, UT.  
5
2 
 
 
 
The applicant is also proposing the amendments to remove building pads for all lots and come 
under the most recent iteration of the Eastern Summit County Development Code. Due to the 
previous grading on some of the lots, the applicant is proposing a plat note which would require 
height to be measured from natural and finished grade and that the more restrictive 
measurement is applied.  
 
Vicinity Map 
 
 
 
Background 
 
The Green Hill Subdivision was recorded on March 21, 2008 and consists of five lots and one 
ancillary/agricultural parcel. The subdivision was approved as a 2-lot Minor Subdivision, with 
the incorporation of two (2) additional Lots of Record, and a transfer of one unit of density into 
the subdivision, creating a total of five lots. Development envelopes and plat notes were 
established in order to mitigate potential negative viewshed impacts. The subdivision was 
approved under the previous Eastern Summit County Development Code and Zoning. 
 
In 2017, Summit County Council adopted Ordinance 865 which defines ridgelines and how 
development on ridgelines should take place. 
 
In 2021, the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission approved a plat amendment for Lots 
2 and 3 which removed the building pads and added notes for height restrictions due to 
previous grading.  
6
3 
 
 
Analysis and Findings 
 
Section 11-4-5(E) of the Eastern Summit County Development Code sets the following grounds 
for vacating or amending a plat: 
 
1. If the Land Use Authority is satisfied that the public interest will not be materially injured 
by the proposed vacation, alteration or amendment, and there is good cause for the 
vacation, alteration or amendment, the Land Use Authority, may vacate, alter or amend 
the final subdivision plat, or any portion of the final subdivision plat. 
 
Analysis: The Eastern Summit County Development Code was created to promote public 
health, safety, and welfare. The applicant is proposing the amendments to come under 
the most recent iteration of the Eastern Summit County Development Code and there is 
good cause for the amendment. 
 COMPLIES AS CONDITIONED 
 
2. No subdivision plat amendment shall be approved which results in an increase in density 
unless the requirements set forth in subsection E6 of this section for a re-subdivision 
have been satisfied. 
 
Analysis: The applicant is not proposing to change density. COMPLIES 
 
Service Providers 
 
Staff has not received any negative service provider comments which would hinder the 
proposal.

Proposal 
The applicant is proposing to amend the Eastern Summit County Development Code in the 
following manner: 
- Add a use to the Chart of Allowed and Permitted Uses (11-3-16) 
- Add a definition of that use to the Definition section of the Code (Appendix A) 
- Add an exception to the Lighting Regulations section of the Code (11-6-20) 
 
After working collaboratively with staff, the applicant is proposing to add the use of 
“Permanent outdoor film set façade” to the Chart of Allowed and Permitted Uses as a 
Conditional Use Permit in the AG-10, AG-20, AG-40, and AG-80 zones.  The proposed definition 
is as follows:  
Permanent outdoor film set façade:  The exterior walls of a building on a film set meant 
to mimic a real-life or imagined location. Limited interior filming space may be allowed, 
but these spaces are not intended for full time habitation. 
 
The intention is that the Conditional Use Permit would be obtained prior to building any movie 
sets that are intended to remain standing longer than the period allowed under a Temporary 
Use Permit.  The act of filming would still be permitted individually by project through the 
Summit County Film Permit available through the Special Events Office.   
 
The following would be added to the list of exemptions in section 11-6-20, Lighting Regulations:

2 
 
 7. Temporary outdoor lighting approved through the Film Permit process. 
 
Background 
In 2016, an “outdoor movie set” was approved via a Long Term Temporary Use Permit on 
parcel SS-79 in the Garff Ranches area, which is adjacent to 7600 W Brown’s Canyon Road (see 
Exhibit A and B).  Subsequently, that Temporary Use Permit was granted a one-time, 6-month 
extension (Exhibit C).  The Temporary Use Permit expired on February 7, 2018.  A Condition of 
Approval for the Temporary Use Permit read, “2. The site shall be returned to its original 
condition upon the completion of the Long Term Temporary Use Permit.”  The original staff 
report indicates that the applicant planned to return to the Community Development 
Department prior to the expiration of the Temporary Use Permit to apply for a Development 
Code Amendment to add the use.   
 
On September 25, 2024, the Summit County Community Development Department issued a 
Notice of Violation for a Prohibited Land Use Zoning Violation because the outdoor movie set 
was still standing despite the expiration of the Temporary Use Permit. 
 
The applicant is applying to add a similar use to the Development Code.  The applicant hopes to 
keep the structures that have been on the property since the approval in 2016.   
 
Analysis 
Section 11-5-3 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code establishes a process for 
amendments to the text of the Code, it states that whenever an amendment to the Code is 
initiated, it must be reviewed by the Planning Commission who will deliver a recommendation 
to the County Council. The County Council, after holding a public hearing, can approve, approve 
with modifications, or deny the amendment. There are no criteria.
