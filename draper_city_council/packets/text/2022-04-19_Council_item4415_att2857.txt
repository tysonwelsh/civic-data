                               RESOLUTION NO. 22-19

      A RESOLUTION REAPPOINTING DEAN GOODRICH TO THE DRAPER CITY
      SPECIAL EVENT ARENA COMMITTEE

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council has adopted Ordinances that provide for
the appointment of members to the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee;
and

      WHEREAS, members of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee are
appointed by the Mayor with the advice and consent of the City Council; and

      WHEREAS, terms of office are set to be four-year terms; and

    WHEREAS, the Mayor proposes to reappoint Dean Goodrich to serve as a
member of the Draper City Special Event Arena Committee.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      The City Council hereby provides its advice and consent to the Mayor’s
reappointment of Dean Goodrich to serve as a member of the Draper City Special
Event Arena Committee.

        Section 1. Appointment. The following individual is hereby reappointed to
fill the term listed below as a member of the Draper City Special Event Arena
Committee according to the laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the
Draper City Special Event Arena Committee and its members for the term specified
herein.

             Four-year term:

             Dean Goodrich              April 19, 2022, through April 30, 2026

       Section 2. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is
held invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Resolution shall be severable.
      Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon its passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS THE 19TH DAY OF APRIL, 2022.
