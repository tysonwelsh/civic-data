                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1572

        AN ORDINANCE OF DRAPER CITY AMENDING A MASTER AREA PLAN PER THE
        LAND USE AND DEVELOPMENT CODE OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE
        RELATING TO VISTA STATION MASTER AREA PLAN.

      WHEREAS, Utah State law grants to Draper City the authority to regulate uses of
property by zoning districts; and

        WHEREAS, the terms and provisions of the TSD (Transit Station District) zoning
classification were originally adopted and established in the Draper City Municipal Code
through the adoption of Ordinance 985, adopted on October 13, 2011 and were
subsequently revised through the adoption of Ordinances 985, 1024, 1085, 1132, 1263, 1313,
1328, 1380, 1394, 1418, and 1438 between August 21, 2012 and May 19, 2020; and

        WHEREAS, the terms and provisions of the TSD (Transit Station District) zoning
classification require a Master Area Plan be applied for, reviewed, and approved prior to
application for development of any property within the TSD zoning classification; and

       WHEREAS, the Vista Station Master Area Plan was adopted on January 8, 2013 and
the applicant is now proposing changes to the adopted Vista Station Master Area Plan; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council of Draper City has determined that the amended Vista
Station Master Area Plan has provided for and addressed all the narrative and graphic
submittal requirements of Sections 9-14-060(C)(1) and (2); and

       WHEREAS, notice has been issued according to the requirements of the Utah Code
Annotated and Draper City Municipal Code for public hearings before the Planning
Commission and City Council to receive public input regarding the revision of the Master
Area Plan; and

       WHEREAS, the Planning Commission and City Council have each held a public hearing
to receive public input regarding the revision of the Master Area Plan.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1.     Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings that the proposed amendment to the Vista Station Master Area Plan: 1. The
proposal complies with all of the required elements under DCMC Section 9-14-060; 2. The
master circulation plan adequately serves the MAP area and areas surrounding the project;
3. The MAP provides for a community character substantially consistent with the purposes
of the TSD, including the provision for at least twenty five percent (25%) commercial, retail,

or office uses; and 4. The development standards and design guidelines are high quality
transit oriented design standards, including the use of high quality building materials.

      Section 2.     Revision. The Master Area Plan for Vista Station is hereby revised with
the new exhibits as set forth in Exhibit A.

        Section 3.   Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Ordinance shall be severable.

        Section 4.   Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately
upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is closer to the date of
final passage.
