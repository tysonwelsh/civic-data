2. The council discussed different options of obtaining easements for an electrical line
   connection that would run from Substation #3 to the new Rocky Mountain Power
   substation. The council directed staff to not off water connections as part of
   negotiating for easements. Councilor Memmott asked about alternate routes for the
   line. The council discussed the annexation of the McPherson property and how
   much of it would need to be annexed into the city. The council directed staff to keep
   talking to landowners on different ways that the city could obtain easements across
   their properties for the electrical line.
3. Mr. Atkinson presented an annexation agreement with Levi Wright and wanted the
   council to review the agreement.

4. The council reviewed a land swap agreement with the Juab School District. The
   agreement will be brought back to regular council meeting for approval

5. The council reviewed an airport hangar ground lease agreement between the City
   and Kirk Urbanczk.

6. Councilor Memmott reported on a meeting he had attended with Central Utah Water
   Conservancy District board members. He gave the council some good information
   on how much it would cost residents if the CUWCD brought water to Nephi City. He
   said that it is going to be important to make the decision if we want the water brought
   to the valley and important when that decision should be made. CUWCD are fine
   tuning a report that has the information to help make these decisions.

7. City Recorder Lisa Brough presented a rejection notice for the Racoon Eye
   Annexation. The petition was rejected because it was accompanied by an
   unrecordable map.

8. Mr. Atkinson reminded the council about the Information Fair sponsored by the
   Library that will be Saturday, January 29th at 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.

9. George and Judy Ann Philips addressed the council about the Sandra Worthington
   property that Ms. Worthington has been trying to get annexed into the city. The staff
   and council helped the Philips understand the petition process and how the council
   wants to know the plans for the property if it was to be annexed into the city. The
   council is not willing to annex property that they don’t first know what will be
   developed on the property.

10. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the updated financial reports with the council

11. Mayor Seely reported on a Rural Water meeting.

12. Mayor Seely opened a regular meeting and Councilor Worwood moved to convene
    in to executive session to discuss the character and competence of an individual and
    real property. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
    unanimous vote.

13. Meeting adjourned at 8:50 p.m.
