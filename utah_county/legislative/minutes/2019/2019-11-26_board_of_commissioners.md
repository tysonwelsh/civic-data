         9.     APPROVE (OR DENY) AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND THE UTAH COUNTY GENERAL PLAN,
         2014, TO UPDATE THE GOALS, OBJECTIVE, AND POLICIES ELEMENT, LAND USE ELEMENT,
         MODERATE INCOME HOUSING ELEMENT, AND THE TRNSPORTATION AND TRAFFICE
         CIRCULATION ELEMENT.
         ~Bryce Armstrong, Community Development

          BRYCE ARMSTRONG, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT - THIS IS IN RESPONSE TO SENATE
         BILL 34 FROM THE PAST LEGISLATIVE REGULAR SESSION (2019) RELATING TO
         JURISDICTION REQUIREMENTS TO HAVE A MODERATE INCOME HOUSING PLAN AS PART
         OF YOUR GENERAL PLAN AND ALSO ADD SOME MODIFICATIONS OR UPDATES IF NEEDED
         TO BE IN COMPLIANT WITH THE BILL. UTAH COUNTY HAS A GENERAL PLAN WHICH
         INCLUDES A MODERATE-INCOME HOUSING PLAN WHICH IS ADOPTED AND REVIEWED BY
         THE STATE EVERY TWO YEARS. THIS IS JUST TO GO THROUGH THE REQUIREMENTS OF
         THE BILL AND VERIFY THAT THE PLAN IS CONSISTENT WITH THE NEW PROVISIONS. THE
         BILL INCLUDED AN ADOPTION DATE OF DECEMBER 1.

         COMMISSIONER LEE - UNDER OBJECTIVE 4 IT SAYS, ‘UTAH COUNTY SHALL CONSIDER
         POLICIES THAT CREATE OPPORTUNITIES FOR HOUSING FOR RESIDENTS OF VARIOUS
         INCOME LEVELS IN UNINCORPORATED AREAS OF UTAH COUNTY’ WHICH HE FEELS GOES

         ALMOST COMPLETELY AGAINST WHAT THE COUNTY IS TRYING TO ENCOURAGE.
         EXPRESSED CONCERN OF THAT BEING IN THE GENERAL PLAN.

         BRYCE ARMSTRONG – THE WORDING, ‘RESIDENTS OF VARIOUS INCOME LEVELS’ IS THE
         LANGUAGE FROM THE STATE BILL. THIS IS A GUIDE. ALSO SAYS, ‘UTAH COUNTY
         SHOULD CONSIDER POLICIES THAT CREATE OPPORTUNITIES FOR HOUSING’.

         HAVE ADDITIONAL LANGUAGE THAT STILL RE-EMPHASIZED OUR LONG-STANDING
         POLICIES SUCH AS OBJECTIVE POLICY D THAT STATES, ‘UTAH COUNTY SHOULD
         CONTINUE POLICIES WHICH ENCOURAGE AND SUPPORT MODERATE INCOME HOUSING
         WITHIN INCORPORATED MUNICIPALITIES NEAR ESSENTIAL SERVICES INCLUDING
         EMPLOYMENT, TRANSPORTATION OPTIONS, COMMERCE, EDUCATION AND
         HEALTHCARE’.

         COMMISSIONER IVIE – NOTED THAT IF PART OF THE REQUIREMENT UNDER THE LAW IS
         TO PROVIDE OPPORTUNITIES, THEN THE COUNTY MUST DO SOME TYPE OF REPORTING.

         BRYCE ARMSTRONG – DOESN’T KNOW WHAT THE REPORTING IS GOING TO LOOK LIKE.

         COMMISSIONER LEE – STATED THAT THIS NEEDS TO BE IN COMPLIANCE BEFORE 12/1.

         BRYCE ARMSTRONG – THERE WERE 3 ADDITIONS SUGGESTED BY COMMISSIONER AINGE:

         •   CHAPTER 1, OBJECT 2 - ‘RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT OF AREAS OF
             UNINCORPORATED COUNTY LYING WITHIN A MUNICIPALITIES ADOPTED
             ANNEXATION POLICY DECLARATION SHOULD OCCUR WITHIN OR CONSISTENT WITH
             THE APPROPRIATE MUNICIPALITY IN ITS DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS’.

         •   CHAPTER 2 ADD PARAGRAPH ENTITLED, ‘STRATEGIES TOWARDS LOWER COST
             DEVELOPMENT IN MODERATE INCOME HOUSING’. ADD A NUMBER 16 STRATEGY
             WHICH STATES, ‘PROMOTE THE LOCATION OF NEW MODERATE-INCOME HOUSING
             UNITS WITHIN INCORPORATED MUNICIPALITIES NEAR ESSENTIAL SERVICES
             INCLUDING EMPLOYMENT, TRANSPORTATION OPTIONS, COMMERCE, EDUCATION
             AND HEALTH CARE’.

         •   PAGE 13 - UNDER THE SECTION THAT TALKS ABOUT THE THRESHOLDS FOR RENTAL
             AMOUNTS- AFTER, ‘A HOUSEHOLD EARNING THE MODERATE INCOME FIGURE ABOVE
             (WHICH GOES THROUGH THE HUD FORMULA) WOULD QUALIFY FOR RENTAL
             HOUSING OF APPROXIMATELY $1,590/PER MONTH FOR ALL HOUSING COSTS’, TO ADD,
             ‘IT IS ANTICIPATED THE MAJORITY OF RENTAL HOUSING UNITS LOCATED WITHIN
             UNINCORPORATED UTAH COUNTY WILL QUALIFY AS MODERATE INCOME HOUSING
             AS REFERENCED ABOVE’.
