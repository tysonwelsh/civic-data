those that were recognized tonight.

Proposed Resolution 2022-19 approving the Ogden City American Rescue
Plan Act Nonprofits Grant Program

A memo from the City Comptroller came before the Council to consider a resolution creating a Nonprofit Grant Program.
The memo stated City Administration is proposing the creation of a grant program for nonprofit entities funded with $1.3 million
of ARPA funds. The minimum grant under the program would be $25,000 and must be for essential capital improvement projects.
Preference will be given to those organizations that provide youth or family services.

Management Services Director Brown summarized the memo and stated the grant program eligibility criteria are as
follows:

Must be a nonprofit 501(c)(3) with a physical location in Ogden.
Must be in operation as of the date of grant application.
Must be free of any city, state, or federal tax liens or judgments.
Must supply documentation to support application.
Must have a minimum annual revenue of $250,000.
e Funds must be obligated by December 31, 2024, and spent by December 31, 2026.

The City will evaluate the following criteria when selecting grant recipients:

e Type of services provided with preference given to youth and family support services.
e Description of essential capital improvement project to be funded.

e Identified total project costs and other funding sources leveraging the project.

e Collaboration with other community-based agencies or city departments.

e Geographic distribution of services.

e Organizational sustainability.

Applications will be submitted to the Comptroller’s Office. All applications will be reviewed by the City’s loan
committee consisting of the following individuals: Mark Johnson, Sara Meess, Jonnalyne Walker, Buck Froerer, Brandon Cooper,
and Korahle Jensen. The loan committee will determine which nonprofits receive grant funding. The Fiscal Year 2022-2023
Budget adopted by the Council on August 2, 2022, included an appropriation of $1.3 million of ARPA funds for a nonprofit grant
program. No additional budget action is needed.

Acting Chair Lopez introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2022-19, entitled:

“A resolution of the City Council of Ogden City, Utah, approving the Ogden City American Rescue Plan Act
Nonprofits Grant Program.”

COUNCIL MEMBER RICHEY MOVED THE RESOLUTION BE ADOPTED, AND THE CHAIR BE

Public Comments

Angel Castillo thanked Council staff for making it possible for the public to participate in Council meetings via
electronic means. She referenced tonight’s work session discussion regarding the Stock Exchange Building; she is pleased that
everyone is concerned about historic preservation of buildings such as this one, but she was surprised to hear that the Council was
asked to change zoning for the building without a rendering and scale of the project. The Landmarks Commission has expressed
similar concerns about other projects in the past; without scale models, it can be difficult to determine the impact of a new
building project or redevelopment. While these types of projects are needed to improve the economy of the City, they should be
completed in a practical and respectful manner. She then noted that winter is coming, and the homeless population of the
community will be freezing to death; she has not heard any recent discussions in the City about what will be done to protect them.
She discussed the State’s use of available funding to provide aid to the homeless population and she suggested the City of Ogden
have a work session discussion about proactive measures that can be employed to avoid situations that have occurred in the past.
She would like that discussion to take place in a public meeting prior to the winter.

Mayor Comments

Mayor Caldwell thanked the Council for their acknowledgement of Hispanic Heritage Month and Hispanic leaders in the
community; there have been some great recognition items in Council meetings over the past several weeks and these items
contribute to the quality of life in the community.
