TIP Modification Orem 1200 S 400 W Roundabout - Additional Funding (00:04:04) Bob Allen presented a TIP modification for a roundabout project at 1200 South and 400 West near UVU. Originally selected in 2016 with $2.3 million in exchange funds, the project's cost has increased to $2.8 million due to construction inflation, utility relocations, and additional right-of-way costs. The roundabout is complex, with five legs, and is designed to accommodate increased traﬃc and UVX transit. Orem City is requesting an additional $272,000 from the contingency fund to bridge the funding gap and proceed with construction. Bob Allen noted that the project is ready for construction and bids have been received, with most construction expected to be completed this year. Mayor Michelle Kafausi moved to add $272,000 to the Orem 1200 South 400 West roundabout project. Jim Evans seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. MPO Organizational Assessment (00:08:59) Bob Allen presented the findings of an Organizational Assessment conducted by a consulting firm for the MPO. The assessment examined staff, processes, and programs by interviewing staff, board members, and stakeholders, and conducting surveys and focus groups. Key findings included that staff is talented but new and needs more training, internal priorities need better alignment, and the director should focus more on MPO operations. The assessment revealed that current processes lack consistency and transparency, and there's a need to be more decisive in implementing new processes. The region's rapid growth is outstripping funding, causing a focus on immediate needs rather than future planning. Strengths include being the oﬃcial transportation planning entity and having robust decision-making frameworks. Challenges include staﬃng levels not keeping pace with technical service needs and limited capacity to address regional growth. Bob Allen noted that staff will meet to discuss addressing weaknesses and strengths, create an action plan, and embed changes into the budget and work plan. It was suggested regular follow-up updates to the Board on implementation would be helpful. Legislative Summary (00:16:37) Bob Allen provided a comprehensive summary of transportation-related legislative bills from the recent session. Page 2 of 3 ● Senate Bill 195, the transportation omnibus bill, requires cities within MPOs to update their general plans by July 1, 2027, identifying priority connections to enhance transportation access. The bill mandates MPOs to consult with cities and report on plan modifications. ● Senate Bill 26 modified Housing and Transit Reinvestment Zones (HTRZ), requiring more detailed proposals with specific dwelling unit requirements. ● Senate Bill 96 focused on Advanced Air Mobility, requiring UDOT to create a toolkit for local governments to assist with emerging air transportation technologies. ● House Bill 379 changed population data estimation methods, potentially impacting fund allocations. ● House Bill 229 allocated $20 million to the state corridor preservation fund, allowing for future property acquisitions. ● House Bill 454 and Senate Bill 310 addressing transportation utility fees did not pass. ● Appropriations included $3 million for an environmental impact study at the I-15 8000 South interchange, $13 million for Spanish Fork road connections, and a $20 million transfer to the corridor preservation fund. Bob Allen also noted a $500,000 cut to the local administrative advisor program, with staff working to determine next steps.
