    8. RATIFY THE 2025 CERTIFIED TAX RATES FOR WEBER COUNTY, ASSESS/COLLECT,
       MUNICIPAL SERVICES, AND SUMMIT ROAD OVERLAY DISTRICT.
         Scott Parke (Comptroller): Property tax rates are the last thing calculated. Weber County did not raise taxes
         for the 2025 year. The County Assessor values all the properties as of January 1st and submits those to the
         state. The County then takes our dollar amount and divides by the new values to come up with the tax rate.
         Commissioner Froerer: What is our CTR for this year? And where does it come from?

         Mr. Parke: The rate comes from the State Tax Commission. As a revenue-neutral state, when values goes
         down, the taxes you pay do not go down. When values go up, the taxes you pay do not automatically go up.
         Each government sets its own budget; Weber County is going to collect $65M that is split among several funds.
         Then we take the total values of all the properties and divide it by the $65M to determine the portion owed by
         each property.

         Commissioner Harvey: So you just reported Weber County Certified Tax Rate went down?
