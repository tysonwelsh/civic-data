CITY OF LOGAN
ORDINANCE NO. 23-26

AN ORDINANCE VACATING RIGHT OF WAY FROM THE LOGAN ISLAND PLAT B

PARCELS 02-079-0021, 02-080-0006, 02-080-0013, 02-080-0008, 02-080-0010, 02-081-0004, 02-080-0011

WHEREAS, the City has received a request submitted by the property owner of the subject parcels, requesting that public right of way be vacated,

WHEREAS, the City has authority by State Law to vacate streets, rights of way, and public utility easements, from use by the public;

WHEREAS, the existing right of way is not required due to the property owner realigning the streets in the above referenced parcels and rededicating new right of way;

WHEREAS, the Logan Municipal Council finds there is good cause for vacating the right of way and finds such action shall not materially injure the public or any person; and,

WHEREAS, the proposed vacation of right of way, as shown and described in EXHIBIT A, has been reviewed by the City and the City Council, and all appropriate hearings, postings, and notifications have been performed in accordance with Utah law to obtain public comment regarding the proposed determination.

THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF LOGAN, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1: Pursuant to Section 10-9a-609.5, Utah Code Annotated, 1953 the right of way shown and described in EXHIBIT A is hereby abandoned and vacated.

SECTION 2: Effective Date. This ordinance shall become effective upon publication.

The City of Logan has received a petition to vacate right of way on property located at or near the Willow Lakes Subdivision in Logan, Utah. You are being notified per Utah State Law Code 10-9a-609.5 because you are listed as an owner of record of a parcel of property that is adjacent to the right of way, within 300 feet of the right of way, or are an operator of utilities, culinary water, or sanitary sewer facilities entity that may provide public utilities in the area that is being petitioned to vacate.

Please see attached image, Exhibit A, of the area outlined in red that is being petitioned.

A City Council workshop on the issue will be held on:

Date: July 18, 2023
Time: 5:30 PM
Location: City Hall 290 North 100 West Logan, UT 84321

A Public Hearing and City Council meeting to take action on the issue is scheduled to be held on:

Date August 1, 2023
Time: 5:30 PM
Location: City Hall 290 North 100 West Logan, UT 84321


If you support the vacation of the sewer line easement, the petition will be available in the Engineering office at City Hall, for signing until 5:00 PM on the date indicated for the public hearing.

If you have any additional questions, please call Darren Farar, City of Logan City Engineer at (435)716-9160 or Darren.farar@loganutah.org.

If you are opposed to the action, please email or mail a letter of opposition to Darren Farar, City of Logan City Engineer at Darren.farar@loganutah.org or 290 N 100 W, Logan, Utah 84321 and / or present your opposition at the Public Hearing.
