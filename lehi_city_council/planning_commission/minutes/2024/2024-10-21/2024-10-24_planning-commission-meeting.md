Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chair Jackson welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public Hearing and consideration of Trevor and Erika Shelton’s request for approval
of Seasons Estates Plat E amending lots 216, 218, and 219 of Seasons Estates Plat B

This Plat amendment would not create any new lots. All lots will meet all requirements, and as
Plat amendments are an administrative item if requirements are met, they must be passed. A
comment from DRC requires that a note be added to the Plat stating that any grading of the lot
that will change the drainage needs review and approval by the city.

The applicants were present but declined to comment.

Commission Chair Jackson opened and closed public comment.

                Motion: Commissioner Kunze moved to approve the request, finding that the
                proposed Plat amendment does not create any new lots, the proposed subdivision
                meets the requirements of the Traverse Mountain Area Plan, and the proposed
                subdivision meets all the requirements of the development code. She included all
                DRC comments in her approval. Commissioner Eyre seconded.


                Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner
                Lockhart, yes. Commissioner Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes.

                Motion passed unanimously.

2.2) Public Hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to the Development Code Table 05.040A, revising the allowed rear yard
setback on corner lots from 30 feet to 15 feet for remodels

A recent amendment to the code fixed a discrepancy on side-yard setbacks on corner lots to
allow remodels to have setbacks of 15 feet instead of 30. After this amendment was approved it
was noted that the rear yard setbacks in the R1-12 zone on corner lots also needed to be updated
with a note to allow setbacks to go to 15 feet from 30 feet for remodels. There was one DRC
comment.

Commission Chair Jackson opened and closed public comment.

                Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to offer a positive recommendation to the
                city council, including the findings that the proposed development code
                amendment is in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the
                general plan in its various elements. He included any DRC comments.
                Commissioner Everett seconded.

                Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner
                Lockhart, yes. Commissioner Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes.

                Motion passed unanimously.

2.3) Public Hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of an
amendment to the Development Code Chapter 26, Accessory Uses, amending the side yard
setback requirements for a detached accessory dwelling unit

Under this proposal, the detached ADU would be required to meet the setback requirements of
the primary home only on larger buildings on the side with large windows. Sides of the ADU
without windows will be required to meet the setback requirements of a typical accessory
structure. There were no DRC comments.

Commission Chair Jackson opened and closed public comment.

                Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to offer a positive recommendation to the
                city council with the findings that the proposed development code amendment is
                in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provisions of the general plan and
                its various elements. Commissioner Kunze seconded.

                Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes. Commissioner Everett, yes. Commissioner
                Lockhart, yes. Commissioner Eyre, yes. Commissioner Kunze, yes.

                Motion passed unanimously.
