                                 MEMORANDUM
To:              Honorable Mayor and City Council
Date:            October 16, 2020
Subject:         Ordinance 20-25, Proposed amendment to Sections 18.90.010 and
                 18.85.030(12) addressing limitations on the number of gasoline service
                 states, amendments proposed by Riverton City.


On August 13, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this text
amendment. At that hearing, the Commission voted recommend denial of the removal of the
limitation to 2 within 1,000 feet. Minutes from that meeting are included below.

Recommended Motion (PC):
I move the City Council DENY Ordinance 20-25, proposed amendment to Section 18.90.010
and 18.85.030(12), distance regulations on gasoline service stations, removing the limitation of
a maximum of two within 1,000 feet.

Alternative Motion:
I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance 20-25, proposed amendment to Section 18.90.010
and 18.58.030(12), distance regulations on gasoline service stations, inserting the following
language:
       “max 2 within 1,000 ft unless separated by a right-of-way of at least 7 lanes”


Background

Riverton City’s Table of Commercial Uses Zone, and the Commercial Downtown, Gateway, and
Regional zones, currently include language limiting convenience stores with gas stations and
standalone gas stations to a maximum of 2 within 1,000 feet. The language reads as follows:

Table of Commercial Uses
                                 LAND USE                            C-D    C-G    C-N   C-PO   C-R
Convenience store with gasoline service (max. 2 within 1,000 ft.)      C     C     NP     NP      P
Gasoline service stations (max. 2 within 1,000 ft.)                    C     C      C     NP      P

Commercial Zones:
Conditional Uses - (3) Convenience store with a gasoline service station (except when two or more like
uses exist within 1,000 feet).

Staff has brought up the question of whether this restriction is one the City wishes to maintain in
its Code, as development patterns with gas station uses in the City has not brought this issue to

light for many years. The language was apparently originally adopted in part to address concerns
with 12600 South and 1300 West, where for a time there was the potential for three service
stations at that intersection. As gasoline service stations are much more difficult to convert to
other uses or retrofit, one likely concern at the time this language was originally adopted was
avoiding what may become unstainable competition resulting in vacant and difficult to redevelop
properties.

The Planning Commission, in reviewing the existing language, voted to recommend maintaining
the limitation as it currently stands. Staff has included an alternative amendment, which would
keep the maximum of 2 within 1,000 feet unless stations were separated by a major roadway,
one of at least 7 lanes. This would limit situations where more than 2 stations could existing within
1,000 feet to areas where traffic volumes could make multiple stations more sustainable, and also
where the separation across such a large right-of-way could make the uses less impactful to one
another. This alternative language would read as follows;

Table of Commercial Uses
                                LAND USE                                   C-D   C-G   C-N   C-PO   C-R
Convenience store with gasoline service (max. 2 within 1,000 ft, unless
                                                                           C     C     NP    NP     P
separated by a right-of-way of at least 7 lanes.)
Gasoline service stations (max. 2 within 1,000 ft. unless separated by a
                                                                           C     C     C     NP     P
right-of-way of at least 7 lanes.))

Commercial Zones:
Conditional Uses - (3) Convenience store with a gasoline service station (except when two or more like
uses exist within 1,000 feet unless separated by a right-of-way of at least 7 lanes).

Should the Council consider amending the ordinance, the above language has been reviewed
through the City Attorney’s Office.

9.    ZONE TEXT CHANGE, RIVERTON CITY IS PROPOSING AMENDMENTS TO
      SECTIONS 18.90.010, TABLE OF COMMERCIAL USES, AND 18.58.020(12)

Mr. Lethbridge presented the staff report and stated that the above item is related to the
number of convenience stores with gas stations within 1,000 feet. Currently, the limit is
two stations within 1,000 feet. Staff asked the Planning Commission if this was a standard
they felt should be maintained.

Commissioner Russell believed it was important to have 1,000 feet of separation due to
potential overcrowding issues.

Commissioner Russell moved that the Planning Commission deny the Zone Text
Change for distance regulations on gasoline service stations, removing the
limitation of a maximum of two within 1,000 feet. The motion was seconded by
Commissioner Lefgren. The motion passed with the unanimous consent of the
Commission.
