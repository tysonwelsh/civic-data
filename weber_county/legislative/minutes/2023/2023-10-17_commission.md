D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: In our Pledge of Allegiance we talked about ‘one nation under
   God’ and then we had a beautiful prayer by Mr. Hatch. Allow me to expand for just a moment. Fred Astaire was
   an iconic actor and dancer and singer of American cinema and starred in dozens of Broadway and Hollywood
   shows. One of his early auditions was evaluated with these words: Can’t act; slightly bald, can dance a little.
   Through persistence and hard work, he developed his abilities until he became one of the greatest performers of
   all time. He wasn’t the only one who endured self doubt or criticism. Walt Disney was fired from a newspaper
   because he lacked imagination and had no good ideas. Vincent Van Gogh sold only a few paintings in his lifetime,
   many to his family and close friends. Earnest Hemingway once received a rejection notice from an editor who
   found his writing both tedious and offensive, claiming his young son could do better. Perhaps we all see ourselves
   less than what we are; unworthy, untalented, unspecial; lacking in heart, mind, resources, charisma or statute to be
   of much use to God. You say you are not perfect, not good enough. Welcome to the club. You may just the
   person God is looking for. God doesn’t need you to be exceptional let alone perfect. He will make your talents
   and abilities and multiply them, especially if you work towards them. Work will take away all discouragement, if
   you work hard. Even you though hope may seem a little scarce, if you trust in Him and are faithful, He will
   magnify your words and actions and use them to bless the multitudes. And I want you to know that I sustain that;
   I believe in those things. Everyone is unique and talented they all bring great pieces to the greater good, especially
   if they’re encouraged and trusted to do so. I appreciate those who have positively affected my life.
E. PUBLIC COMMENT:
   1. Arthur Espinoza, Texas: I am proud to be an American from Texas, this is not my home. If it is not too much
      to ask, can you tell me the function and purpose of this meeting, please? I seek land and water and I stumbled
      into this wonderful building just now. I just need some orientation.
            Commissioner Froerer: The purpose of this meeting is to deal with the business of the Weber County
            Corporation. We meet once a week as the three Commissioners that make the decisions on the number of
            departments that run this county, a lot of that has to do with financial decisions for the various departments in
            this county. The purpose of this meeting is so the public understands what we as County Commissioners are
            approving on their behalf. We are elected to four year terms and serve at the pleasure of the public.
            Commissioner Harvey: Good luck with your quest to buy land and seek water. We hope you are successful.
            There are wonderful opportunities around here.

G. ACTION ITEMS:

    1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 43-2023 RESOLUTION APPOINTING A WEBER COUNTY
       REPRESENTATIVE AND AN ALTERNATE REPRESENTATIVE FOR THE UTAH COUNTIES
       INDEMNITY POOL ANNUAL MEMBERSHIP MEETING.
         Chris Crockett (Deputy County Attorney): In November, you will be attending the UAC conference. Held
         in conjunction with that conference, the Utah Counties Indemnity Pool has its annual membership meeting
         on November 15th. Part of the bylaws with UCIP is the member counties have to be appoint a
         representative to attend that meeting and alternate representative in case the first member cannot attend. The
         requirements are that the person has to be an elected official, an appointed officer, or an employee of the
         county. In the past, it has been the Commissioner that serves on the board of UCIP with another
         Commissioner as an alternate.

         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve Resolution 43-2023 appointing Commissioner Gage Froerer as the
         Weber County Representative and Commissioner Sharon Bolos as Alternate Representative for the Utah
         Counties Indemnity Pool Annual Membership Meeting; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Chair Froerer – aye
