Trailhead and Wayfinding
• Description: Advance current trailhead and wayfinding to include
  additional locations

• Benefits: Improve community feel, provide directions/information to
  backcountry skiers and hikers,

• Considerations: Potential for increased parking in summer, slope
  concerns

• Source: Town of Alta Signage Plan, Albion Basin Transportation
  Feasibility Study

• Next Steps: Review design alternatives, determine preferred
  locations, coordination with USFS regarding trailhead information,
  coordination UDOT regarding wayfinding
