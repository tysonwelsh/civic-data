Pledge of Allegiance
Ms. Shelby Jensen,  Paralympian, led the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag of the 
United States of America.
Council Member Newton  introduced Shelby Jensen who is on the USA 
Paralympic Team and is headed to Tokyo in August.  She is one of the top 
wheelchair fencers in the world and, at the age of 20, is the youngest Paralympic 
athlete in the category.  When she was 7 years old, she had a stroke causing the 
loss of motor skills to her right side, but she has persevered.  On top of being a 
Paralympian, she is a County employee in the Parks & Recreation Division's 
Adaptive Recreation program.
Ms. Jensen stated she got her opportunity while volunteering for the County ’s 
Wheelchair Sports Camp at 15 years old, at which time she tried wheelchair fencing 
and loved it.  She also could not have gotten to where she is now without  help 
from the Utah Fencing Foundation.  
2. CITIZEN PUBLIC INPUT
Mr. George Zinn  stated he met Shelby Jensen and her father, Jed Jensen, at a dog park 
where he discovered she was going to the Tokyo Olympics.  He announced that she has a 
competition Friday, July 23rd, through Sunday, July 25th, at the Salt Palace Convention 
Center.  The event is open to the public and it is free.

1. Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns two parcels of land located at 
approximately 3501 South 1200 East, Millcreek, Utah, identified as Tax Id . 
Nos. 16-32-231-013 and 16-32-231-014, which were struck off to the County 
after the tax sale in 1952.
2. Nicole Marie Nelson has offered in writing to purchase all of the parcel 
known as Tax Id. No. 16-32-231-013 and a portion of the parcel known as 
Tax Id. No. 16-32-231-014 (collectively the “Property”) from the County for 
$4,950.00, which amount has been approved by the Salt Lake County Real 
Estate Section as fair market value.  This offer is in the form of a Tax Sale 
Property Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) attached hereto as Exhibit 
A.
3. The County has determined that the Property is not in public use .  
Proceeds from the sale of the Property will be distributed in accordance with 
Section 59-2-1351.5 of the Utah Code.
4. It has been determined that the best interest of the County and the 
general public will be served by the sale and conveyance of the Property to 
Ms. Nelson.  The sale and conveyance will be in compliance with all 
applicable state statutes and county ordinances.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the Property, described in Exhibit 1 of the Agreement is 
hereby declared surplus property.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County Council that the sale 
and conveyance of the Property by quit -claim deeds to Ms. Nelson as 
provided in the Agreement for the agreed appraised value of Four Thousand 
Nine Hundred Fifty Dollars and Zero Cents ($4,950.00) is hereby approved; 
and the Mayor is hereby authorized to execute the Agreement and the Mayor 
and County Clerk are hereby authorized to execute the Quit -Claim Deeds, 
attached to the Agreement as Exhibits 2-A and 2-B, and to deliver the fully 
executed documents to the County Real Estate Section for delivery to Ms . 
Nelson upon payment of the agreed upon purchase amount.
