Island       Knowledge                2 Action        Resources).

Utah Division              of Multicultural              Affairs.         Millcreek        received       $50,000 from this grant. PIK2AR                               will
be giving         up to $400 of rental assistance,                          per month,         to qualified     individuals. A study was

explained          these      funds     are a grant        and have           not been       taken        from     the City's            General      Fund.      Council
Member           Uipi     asked       how     CurlyMe,           listed     from     the packet,          is associated            with      Millcreek.        Mayor
