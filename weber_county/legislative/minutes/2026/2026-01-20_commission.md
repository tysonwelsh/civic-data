     2. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 05-2026 APPOINTING TWO MEMBERS TO THE WEBER
        COUNTY BOUNDARY COMMISSION.
            Craig Brandt (Clerk/Auditor’s Office): When a municipal annexation is proposed, State code allows
            property owners within 300 feet of the proposed annexation an opportunity to protest. Those protests are
            heard by a Boundary Commission comprised of two County elected officers as appointed by the County
            Commission, two municipal executives selected by a Municipal Selection Committee (in this case WACOG),
         and three County citizens. This resolution proposes appointing Commissioner Jim Harvey and Weber
         County Recorder/Surveyor Bahy Rahimzadegan to serve on the Boundary Commission. Terms will be
         drawn by lot at the Boundary Commission meeting.

         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve Resolution 05-2026 appointing Commissioner Jim Harvey and Weber
         County Recorder/Surveyor Bahy Rahimzadegan to the Weber County Boundary Commission; Chair Froerer
         seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – excused; Commissioner Bolos – aye
