ORDINANCE NO. 26-04

AN ORDINANCE VACATING TWO MUNICIPAL UTILITY EASEMENTS
LOCATED AT APPROXIMATELY 4816 SOUTH STATE STREET AND
4837 SOUTH POPLAR STREET, MURRAY CITY, SALT LAKE COUNTY,
STATE OF UTAH.

WHEREAS, the Murray City Municipal Council received a petition to vacate two
municipal utility easements; and

WHEREAS, the petition requested that the utility easements located at
approximately 4816 South State Street and 4837 South Poplar Street, Murray, Salt
Lake County, State of Utah, be vacated; and

WHEREAS, the petition meets the requirements of U.C.A. §10-20-813, as
amended; and

WHEREAS, the request was made to facilitate the proposed development and
construction of a new building at 4816 South State Street; and

WHEREAS, the easements are currently used by Murray City Power to supply
electrical power to properties in the area; and

WHEREAS, the developer of the parcels committed to providing the necessary
easements to the City to allow for relocation of the current power distribution
infrastructure located on the easements; and

WHEREAS, the Murray City Municipal Council finds good cause to vacate the
municipal utility easements located at approximately 4816 South State Street and 4837
South Poplar Street, Murray, Salt Lake County, State of Utah; and neither the public
interest nor any person will be materially injured by the vacation; and


Record Owners of Land have been given notice and have been consulted and that
notice has been published and a public hearing has been held on April 7th, 2026
pursuant thereto, all as required by law.
