2.         23-138                 Community Development Department reporting on findings and progress 
                                  of the General Plan update to a combined work session of the Mayor, 
                                  Administration, City Council, and Planning Commission.
                   Attachments:   Existing Conditions Report-draft (4.7.23)
                                  Existing Conditions Report-draft (4.11.23)
                                  Sandy General Plan Update Presentation
                                  Sandy General Plan Y2K Survey Report

                                  Jake Warner, Community Development, with Christine Richmond, GSBS Consultant, and 
                                  Kyrene Gibb, Y2 Analytics  presented to the Sandy City Council Members and the 
                                  Sandy City Planning Commissioners an update and overview of the General Master Plan. 
                                  Below is a summary of the presentation. For the complete presentation, please review 
                                  the meeting recording at https://sandyutah.legistar.com.

                                  1. Existing Conditions 
                                  Sandy City demographic makeup has shifted from a period of high growth that occurred 
                                  during the period of 1970 to 2000 to a current period of slowed growth. As the growth has 
                                  slowed, Sandy's population has become more diverse. This shift from a high growth 
                                  community results in changes in the focus of general plan goals and recommended 
                                  policies. Some key questions the general plan process addresses are the following: 
                                  neighborhood preservation, projected regional growth, regional transportation, conserving 
                                  natural resources. A current snapshot of Sandy City was presented and an overview of 
                                  the following: land use, economic base, regional economy, neighborhood communities, 
                                  accessibility to transit service, active transportation projects water, and community health 
                                  and safety.

                                  2. Market Snapshot
                                  The purpose of this market analysis is to help inform Sandy's General Plan from an 
                                  economic perspective and will enable future goals and policies to be market-oriented and 
                                  policymakers prioritize efforts. Key economic indicators include the following: resilient 
                                  market, regional economy, recreational hub, strong neighborhoods, emerging issues, 
                                  demographics and socioeconomic factors, industry trends, and market conditions.

                                  3. Housing Snapshot
                                  Currently, 60% of Sandy City is zoned for single-family residential. Approximately half of 
                                  the dwelling units were built in the 1970's and the majority of the housing stock was built 
                                  before 2000. Data reflects that there has been a gradual increase in additional housing 
                                  types beyond traditional detached single family. Future developments for residential will 
                                  likely include redevelopment and revitalization of existing neighborhoods, and higher 
                                  density housing near Cairns, Stadium Village, and other special districts. A review of the 
                                  moderate income housing plan was provided which included a review of the six 
                                  implementation strategies adopted by Sandy City.

                                  4. Transportation
                                  A review of prior transportation plans, existing plans and studies, downtown transportation 
                                  master plan, Stadium Village and Cairns Master Plan, plans from adjacent regions, and 
                                  active transportation facilities was provided.

                                  5. Health Existing Conditions
                                  A review was presented on the air quality, particulate matter and ozone, greenspace, and 
                                  health outcomes.

                   6. Public Safety Existing Conditions
                   A review of public safety factors and crime statistics was presented: property crime, 
                   violent crime, fire safety, traffic safety.

                   7. Water Preservation Assessment Plan
                   The Water Preservation Assessment Plan has been prepared as part of the Sandy 
                   General Plan update to meet the new legislative requirements and encompasses the 
                   following factors: development patterns and water demands, a summary the city's efforts 
                   and plans to reduce water use in existing development, and identifies opportunities for the 
                   city to modify its municipal operations to eliminate practices or conditions that waste 
                   water.

                   Kyrene Gibb with Y2 Analytics presented on the survey process, analysis, and feedback 
                   gathered for the General Master Plan. Ms. Gibb reviewed the suvey methodology, mode, 
                   sample size, and margin of error. 1,248 households responded to the survey. The 
                   respondents were evenly balanced across the council districts. The data is representative 
                   of the entire city as a whole with a 3% margin of error.

                   Key findings from the survey results include the following:
                     High overall quality of life
                     Concerns about mitigating growing pains with respect to economic development
                     Managing traffic flow
                     Maintaining a low tax burden
                     Supportive of street improvements, but not supportive of additional housing development

                   Survey results were very favorable of Sandy City and include the following priorities for 
                   residents:
                     High quality of life 
                     Location and safety 
                     Preserving open space
                     Managing traffic flow
                     Preserving community and neighborhood character
                     Maintaining low taxes

                   Christine Gibbs with GSBS Consulting reviewed the Strengths, Weaknesses,
