hearing and voted to forward a positive recommendation to the Summit County Manager for a 
condominium plat for Quarry Springs.  
 
Quarry Springs is a sixty-eight (68) unit multi-family development located on the southwest 
corner of Kilby Road and Pinebrook Blvd. It consists of thirty-four (34) buildings, including 
duplexes and fourplexes. Construction has commenced on at least four (4) of the buildings. The 
Final Site Plan for the project was recorded on June 23, 2011. 
 
After the condominium plat was recommended for approval by the SBPC, it came to Staff’s 
attention that the allowed square footage for the project was being exceeded. The project was 
approved for 114,000 total square feet and a footprint of 72,000 square feet. 
 
The applicant has worked with Staff to reduce the overall square footage and building footprint. 
As a result, the buildings have been reduced in size. The number and locations of the buildings 
have not changed, but the building footprints have. Due to this reduction, the condominium 
plat has been slightly adjusted from what was previously recommended for approval by the 
SBPC. 
 
Staff is recommending that the SBPC conduct a public hearing on the revised condominium plat 
and vote to forward a positive recommendation to the County Manager, based on the following 
findings of fact and conclusions of law and with the conditions of approval.

The applicant, Matt Lowe, is requesting consideration of a 46 lot subdivision on Lot 5, Silver 
Creek Village Center, containing 4.09 acres. The subdivided lots are intended for detached 
single family dwellings (Exhibit A).  
 
V. Background 
The Village Center consists of approximately 244 acres and was approved for 1,290 residential 
units and 50,000 SF of commercial use through the Specially Planned Area (SPA) provisions of the 
1998 Code and General Plan. On June 22, 2015, the Development Agreement that implements 
the provisions of the SPA was recorded.  
 
The Development Agreement contains a number of Exhibits that outline specific requirements as 
well as Development Standards that must be met for all development within the Village Center. 
Where the Development Agreement is silent as to specific development standards, the Code 
provisions apply. 
 
The Silver Creek Village Center Road Dedication/Subdivision Plat was recorded on April 4, 2017. 
It consists of 19 lots that are intended to be further subdivided and developed (Exhibit B). The 
processing of subdivision plats and other types of development permits are required to follow 
the provisions in the Code for the particular type of application. 
 
This subdivision is being processed according to the Major Development standards found in 
Chapter 3 of the Code. This process is utilized for subdivisions that exceed ten (10) lots.
