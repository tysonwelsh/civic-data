                                      WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION AGENDA

            The Public Hearing of the West Valley City Planning Commission will be held on Wednesday, July 9,
            2025, at 4:00 PM, in West Valley City Hall, 3600 Constitution Blvd., West Valley City, Utah. A pre-
             meeting will be held at 3:30 PM. Members of the press and public are invited to attend in person or
                         view this meeting live on Zoom at https://us06web.zoom.us/j/83896786911.

                Preliminary application information will be available by 6:00 PM on Tuesday, July 1, 2025 at
          https://www.wvc-ut.gov/669/Agendas-Minutes. Staff analyses, as well as any final application materials,
                                   will be updated by 12:00 PM on Monday, July 7, 2025.

             Testimonies for or against the proposed applications can be provided to the Planning Commission
           during the public hearing or in writing prior to the hearing. If you wish to submit written comments, be
            advised that this should be provided to the Planning Staff member assigned to the application at least
               seven (7) days prior to the meeting to enable the Planning Commission to properly consider the
                                                           comments.
