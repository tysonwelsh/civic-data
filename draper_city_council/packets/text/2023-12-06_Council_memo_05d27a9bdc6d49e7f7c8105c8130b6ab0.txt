MEMO
To: City Council
From: Travis DeJong
Date: 2023-12-06
Re: Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for an On Premises Banquet Alcohol License
for Culinary Craft Concepts LLC.
Staff report by Travis DeJong


Comments:

Culinary Craft Concepts LLC located at 12033 S Lone Parkway is seeking Local Consent for
a On Premises Banquet Alcohol License.

They have provided the following:
- Liquor liability insurance.
- Bond payable to the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Services.
- Floor map showing where alcohol is to be served, stored, and consumed.
- Sales tax license.
- A background check conducted by the Bureau of Criminal Identification.

Culinary Craft Concepts LLC has also applied for and is in the process of obtaining a
Draper City business license for their restaurant operations.

The findings for Local Consent approval are as follows:

DCMC 6-4-040 requires that an on-premises banquet outlet be at least six hundred feet
(600') away from any community location (schools, churches, public libraries, public
playgrounds, and public parks) measured by the shortest pedestrian route taken from
the nearest patron entrance of the outlet to the property boundary of the community
location.

Culinary Craft Concepts LLC has met this requirement.

DCMC 6-4-040 requires that an on-premises banquet outlet be at least two hundred feet
(200') away from any community location (schools, churches, public libraries, public
playgrounds, and public parks) measured in a straight line from the nearest patron
entrance of the outlet to the property boundary of the community location.

Culinary Craft Concepts LLC has met this requirement.
DCMC 6-4-050 places no restrictions on the number of on-premises banquet licenses that
can be granted by the City.
