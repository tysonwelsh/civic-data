                                             PUBLIC HEARING

                                        Hearing began at – 7:06 p.m.
Counsel advised we are only here for the housing plan, not for rezoning.

Commissioners introduced themselves.

31051 – MSD Planning Staff is seeking public input as well as discussion and/or a recommendation
regarding the White City Housing Element, which will become a component of the White City Metro
Township General Plan. Planner: Lupita McClenning

The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Director, Lupita McClenning provided an analysis of
the plan.

Citizens, Commissioners, and staff had a brief discussion regarding the general plan timeline and zoning,
accessory dwelling units, moderate income housing, deadline, mandate to show improvement, property
taxes included in deficit cost burden, sources, and action items.

Commissioner Seiger-Webster motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Frailey seconded that
motion.
