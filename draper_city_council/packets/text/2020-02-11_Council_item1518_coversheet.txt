Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: February 11, 2020
Re: Small Cell MLA with SQF, LLC Comments: SQF, LLC, ("SQF") is seeking to install small wireless infrastructure in the public right-of-way to facilitate the expansion of small cell/5G wireless technology in Draper. As part of that expansion SQF is looking to enter into a Master License Agreement to place small wireless facilities (as defined in Draper City Code Chapter 8-5-040, and under state and federal law) on new utility poles in the right-of-way in Draper.
SQF is a carrier-agnostic infrastructure provider with authority to provide local exchange telecommunications service in Utah, and will let all interested carriers collocate on their infrastructure so long as it is technically feasible. SQF is committed to complying with Draper’s small wireless regulations and preserving the streetscape, and welcomes the city’s input regarding proposed design and future placement.  The findings are as follows: Entering the MLA with SQF, LLC complies with applicable law and is in the best interest of the health, safety and welfare of Draper residents by providing improved cell phone call and data service.
