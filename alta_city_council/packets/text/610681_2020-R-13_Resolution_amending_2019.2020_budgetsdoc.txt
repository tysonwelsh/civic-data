                                TOWN OF ALTA

                            RESOLUTION 2020-R-13

       A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE 2019-2020 FISCAL BUDGETS
                     FOR THE TOWN OF ALTA

WHEREAS, the Town Council finds it necessary to amend certain departmental budgets
in the Town of Alta General Fund for the fiscal year 2019-2020,

WHEREAS, the Town Council finds that there are unencumbered or unexpended
revenues sufficient to meet all departmental expenditures, and

WHEREAS, the Town Council desires to appropriate such revenues for those uses.

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that in conformity with Chapter 5 Title 10 of
Utah Code Annotated, the General Fund budget for the Town of Alta for fiscal year
2019-2020 is amended as follows: Exhibit “A” – Amended Budget for the Town of Alta:
2019-2020.

ADOPTED THIS 17th day of June, 2020.
