BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of an amendment to an existing Master Area Plan
(MAP) for approximately 0.72 acres located on the east side of Frontrunner Boulevard, at
approximately 13003 S. Frontrunner Boulevard (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently
zoned TSD. The applicant is requesting that a MAP Amendment be approved to transfer
residential units from the TSD-2 intensity area to the TSD-1 intensity area. The total
number of units in the master plan will not be increased with this change.




Draper Frontrunner Apartments                                   App. # TEXTMAP-183-2022
Master Area Plan Amendment                    1
The original MAP for Vista Station was approved by the City Council on January 8, 2013.
This master plan broke the property up into development areas, TSD-1, TSD-2, and TSD-3.
Overall the plan approved 1,923 residential units, 1,867,141 square feet of office space, and
216,859 square feet of retail space. Twenty percent of the area, including the Ebay facility
and UTA Frontrunner station was anticipated to be residential uses. In addition to the
square footage to be built, the plan included a variety of public open spaces throughout
the MAP.

The property has been assigned the TSD zoning classification (Exhibit E). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-14-010 the purpose of the TSD zone is to
“promote transit oriented and transit supportive development in areas that are generally
located near a commuter rail transit or rapid transit station and to maximize the flexibility
of the development approval process so as to permit such development in a manner that is
responsive to market demands and consistent with the purposes and objectives of the
TSD.”

MAP Amendment. The proposed amendment of the existing MAP affects TSD Intensity
Areas 1 and 2. TSD Intensity Area 1 was not planned for any residential units. TSD
Intensity Area 2 was envisioned to have 776 dwelling units and the remaining units (1,147)
in the master plan was allocated in TSD-Intensity Area 3.

At this time, TSD Intensity Area 2 has been developed with all commercial office space.
None of the 776 dwelling units envisioned were built in Area 2. This request is to reallocate
118 residential units from TSD Intensity Area 2 to Area 1. This would still leave up to
potentially 658 units in the TSD Intensity Area 2. The site where the residential units will be
built in TSD Intensity Area 1 was to have one story retail/general and retail/restaurant
buildings with a total square footage of approximately 9,400 square feet. This amendment,



Draper Frontrunner Apartments                                     App. # TEXTMAP-183-2022
Master Area Plan Amendment                     3
as proposed, would allow for a retail and residential mixed use building with up to 118
residential units.

The exhibits in the MAP will be replaced with new exhibits showing the updated residential
calculations and the mixed use building on the proposed site. The applicable exhibits and
proposal for the MAP are included in Exhibit F of this staff report. The originally adopted
exhibits are included as Exhibit G.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review of an amendment to an existing Master Area Plan request is found in
Section 9-14-060(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

E.      Criteria For Review: The planning commission and city council shall have discretion
        to disapprove a MAP with respect to the required elements of a MAP application
        only on the basis of:
        1)      the failure to provide and comply with all of the required elements under this
                section unless waived pursuant to the provisions of this chapter;
        2)      the failure of the master circulation plan to adequately serve the MAP area
                and areas surrounding the project;
        3)      the failure of any item required by this section to provide for a community
                character substantially consistent with the purposes of the TSD, including the
                failure of the land use analysis to provide for at least twenty five percent
                (25%) commercial, retail, or office uses; or
        4)      development standards or design guidelines that are inconsistent with high
                quality transit oriented design standards, including the use of high quality
                building materials.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission review the request, receive public
comment, and make a decision based on the findings listed below and the criteria for
approval, or denial, as listed within the staff report.

The findings for approval as are follows:

1.       The proposal complies with all of the required elements under DCMC Section 9-14-
         060.
2.       The master circulation plan adequately serves the MAP area and areas surrounding
         the project.
3.       The MAP provides for a community character substantially consistent with the
         purposes of the TSD, including the provision for at least twenty five percent (25%)
         commercial, retail, or office uses.
4.       The development standards and design guidelines are high quality transit oriented
         design standards, including the use of high quality building materials.

The findings for denial as are follows:

1.       The proposal does not comply with all of the required elements under DCMC
         Section 9-14-060.
2.       The master circulation plan is not adequate to serve the MAP area and areas
         surrounding the project.
3.       The MAP does not provide for a community character substantially consistent with
         the purposes of the TSD, including the provision for at least twenty five percent
         (25%) commercial, retail, or office uses.
4.       The development standards and design guidelines are not high quality transit
         oriented design standards, including the use of high quality building materials.
