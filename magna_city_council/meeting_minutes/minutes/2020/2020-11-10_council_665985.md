                                          Staff Meeting

Electronic Board Meeting Determination

                 Greg Schulz read the following statement regarding the electronic meeting
determination:

               Consistent with provisions of the Utah Open and Public Meetings Act, specifically
Utah Code Ann. § 52-4-207(4), and acting in my capacity as the Mayor of the Magna Metro
Township (“Magna”), I hereby make the following written determinations in support of my decision
to hold and convene electronic meetings of the Magna Metro Township Council without a physical
anchor location:

1.     Conducting Council Meetings with an anchor location that is physically accessible for
members of the public to attend in person presents a substantial risk to the health and safety of
those who may be present at the anchor location.

2.     This determination is based upon the following:

           a. Pursuant to the Public Health Orders issued by the State of Utah and Salt Lake
              County, the Magna Metro Township remains under a state of public health
              emergency related to the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, and significant person-
              to-person transmission of the virus continues to occur in Magna; and

           b. All other facts noted in the August 7, 2020 Letter of Determination are still in
              effect.

               Paul Ashton reviewed the following resolution approving an amendment to

RESOLUTION NO. 20-11-01                                               Date: November 10, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AN AMENDMENT TO INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT #2174 BETWEEN MAGNA
       AND SALT LAKE COUNTY TO INCREASE THE PAYMENT TO BE RECEIVED
       FROM SALT LAKE COUNTY BY $500,000 FOR THE 2550 SOUTH PROJECT

                                          RECITALS

       A.     The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§
10-2a-401 et seq.

       B.    The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative
body for Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

       C.     Salt Lake County (the “County”) is a County pursuant to Title 17: “Counties.”

       D.    Pursuant to SB234 “Inland Port Authority” (2018), the construction of 2550 South
between 7200 West and 8000 West was identified by the legislature as a needed roadway future
development.

        E.    On June 25, 2019, the Council approved and the Mayor signed Interlocal
Agreement #2174 with Salt Lake County in which the County provided Magna with $1,500,000
from the Local Option Transportation Sales Tax for the design and construction of 2550 South
Street between 7200 West and 8000 West.

       F.      On November 1, 2020, the attached Amendment to Interlocal Agreement #2174
(“Agreement Amendment”), hereinafter known as Exhibit A, was received; and the amendment
provides Magna an additional $500,000.00 Dollars to assist Magna in the completion of the 2550
South Project.

       G.    To promote the future economic growth and development of the property between
U-201, 2550 South, 7200 West and 8000 West, the construction of 2550 South is necessary to
Magna.

                                        RESOLUTION

        THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED the Council hereby approves and authorizes the Mayor
to sign, subject to approval as to form by the Magna Attorney, the attached amendment to
Interlocal Agreement #2174 and to accept the additional $500,000.00 for the 2550 South Project.

        APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Salt Lake County, Utah this 10 day of November,
2020.
