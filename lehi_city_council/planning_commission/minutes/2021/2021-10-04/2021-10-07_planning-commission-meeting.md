Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Vice Chairman Everett welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion of maximum and minimum residential driveway lengths.
Mike West gave a presentation on driveway specifications. He presented on length, width, and
the impact that the drive aisles have. The Commissioners discussed options and possible
solutions. They talked about covered parking opposed to garages and dedicated parking.

3. Discussion of PUD and PRD developments.
Brittney Harris presented on PUD's and PRD's. Brittney reviewed the differences of PUD's and
PRD's. The Commissioner's discussed the pros and the cons of each. Kim Struthers stated
density fluctuates with each piece of property and there is almost a built in density increase.
Brittney reviewed the requirements for both. Ross Dinsdale talked about additional selling points
for density bonuses. Certain types of housing are allowed in PRD and PUD areas. Mike West
gave a presentation on the General Plan before it goes before Council.


4. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Ellis moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Hereth seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
