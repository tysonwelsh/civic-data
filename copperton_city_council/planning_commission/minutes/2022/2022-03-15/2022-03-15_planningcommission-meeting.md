Motion: To recommend approval of the Annexation Policy Plan - Pursuant to State Code 10-2-401.5, the 
Planning Commission is holding a public hearing to allow members of the public to review and provide 
comment on Copperton's Draft Annexation Policy Plan. Prior to the opening of the public hearing, Long 
Range Planner, Kayla Mauldin, will give a brief presentation on the draft. Following the public hearing, the 
Planning Commission may direct staff to make revisions to the draft in response to public comment and 
may recommend adoption to the Copperton Council as written. 
Motion by: Commissioner Taylor 
2nd by: Commissioner Alder 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present) 
 
 
Annexation Ordinance - Pursuant to State Code 10-9a-205, the Planning Commission is holding a public 
hearing to allow members of the public to review and provide comment on a drafted Annexation Ordinance 
for Copperton. This proposed Ordinance establishes the language and policies that would allow Copperton 
to consider an annexation petition or initiate an annexation in the future. Following the close of the public 
hearing, the Planning Commission may recommend adoption, recommend adoption with revisions, or not 
recommend the Ordinance to the Council. Presenter: Kayla Mauldin (Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commission will act on the proposed Public Hearing items after taking comments from the 
public during their respective public hearings. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning 
commissions’ rules of conduct. Public comments will be limited to three minutes per person.  
 
Commissioner Taylor motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Alder seconded that motion.
