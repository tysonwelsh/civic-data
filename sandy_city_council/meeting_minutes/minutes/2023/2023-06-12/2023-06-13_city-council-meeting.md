Council Chair Alison Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment
for Items 1 and 4 on the Agenda:
Mr. Craig Ulrich has lived in Sandy 52 years and is honored to be a resident. He
expressed support for the Houseman proposal. He is not supportive of a tax increase.
Ms. Pat Jones thanked the Council and Administration for working so hard on the budget
and expressed her appreciation for their efforts. She is concerned about the GO bond
funding two capital projects and would like to see those projects separated so that each
capital project is voted on individually - but acknowledged that per the proposal
presented, Alta Canyon was removed from the GO bond. She is supportive of a city-wide
vote for Alta Canyon - let the residents decide. She spoke about the employee
compensation plan and employee benefits and renovations needed for Alta Canyon.
Ms. Cathy Spuck thanked Council and Administration for their hard work on the budget.
She asked that the needs of the City be addressed versus the wants and suggested that
some of the expenses should be deferred. She is supportive of taking Alta Canyon out of
the GO bond and deferring the project. She is supportive of private funding for some park
amenities and suggested that Lone Peak Pavilion be remodeled and used for the Parks
and Rec administrative offices.
Ms. Rebecca Colley thanked the Council and Administration for their hard work on the
budget and thought it was a wonderful debate. She thanked the Mayor for recognizing
Leonard Christofferson. Sandy is full of wonderful people. She expressed support for
addressing the needs of the fire department over the Alta Canyon Recreation Center.
Bex Max had hoped to hear more details and numbers from the Houseman proposal
before she could support the plan.
Mr. Dave Eglund has learned a lot about the budget process and appreciates everyone's
efforts. He would be happy to pay $9 per month to support public safety and expressed
support for the Mayor's proposal. Alta Canyon is lowest on his list for funding.
Mr. Jim Edwards appreciates the Council's work and asked that the Council review the
expenses of their office. He also mentioned that Dimple Dell Recreation Center is located
about 5 minutes from Alta Canyon Recreation Center.
Mr. Bruce Cline expressed appreciation for the dialog this evening. It is important to
continue to fund the fire department and he expressed support for the Mayor's budget. He
asked the Council to make their decision based on what is best for the City moving
forward.
Public comment closed.
Council moved to Standing Reports.

3. 23-226 Special Recognition of the Sandy Youth Council in recognition of their
participation in the SYC and their service to Sandy City.

Attachments: SYC End of Year Review

The Sandy Youth Council was recognized by the Council and Administration for their
participation in the SYC program and for their service to Sandy City and the community.
The Sandy Youth Council is comprised of 23 high school age youth living in Sandy. The
SYC has members from 11 different high schools, range in age from freshman to seniors,
provided over 330 service hours to Sandy City and participated in the state-wide Youth
Council event at the State Capitol. Each SYC member was presented with a certificate
and the Seniors were presented with a commemorative coin. The Council and
Administration thanked the SYC members for their leadership, participation, and service
to Sandy City. Council convened a short recess to take a photo with the Sandy Youth
Council.

City Council Meeting Minutes June 13, 2023

4. 23-227 Discussion of Council Members' recommended amendments to the FY
2023-2024 Tentative Budget.

Attachments: Click here to eComment

Houseman Budget Proposal.pdf
Robinson Stroud Budget Proposal.pdf
Council Member Marci Houseman presented her proposed FY 2024 budget amendment.
The Houseman proposal includes the following adjustments: reduction in COLA,
reallocation of funds for the all-inclusive playground, adjustments to requested FTE's,
allocation of one-time funding to fire department requested FTE's, funding Station 31
through a GO bond, and reductions to requested increases in the general fund. The
proposed adjustments and reductions would be necessary in order to avoid a property tax
increase for FY 2024. Council Member Houseman asked for Council feedback and
comments. A lengthy Council discussion followed.
Council Member Zach Robinson and Council Member Alison Stroud reviewed the
Robinson-Stroud budget amendment proposal. The Robinson-Stroud proposal includes
the following: permanently funding the 3-2 fire department staffing, supports the proposed
employee compensation plan as included in the tentative budget, removal of specific
department requested increases (as detailed in the proposal), reallocation of funds for the
all-abilities playground, and funding Fire Station 31 through a GO bond. The Alta Canyon
Recreation Center will be removed from the GO bond. A revised Robinson-Stroud
proposal, that includes adjustments presented and discussed this evening will be posted
tomorrow. A lengthy Council discussion followed, and comments and clarifications from
Legal and the Council director were offered. There was no vote taken on either proposal
by the Council.
