CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-18-2023 Petition by CR ENGLAND requesting a conditional use for a truck center
          expansion. The property is located at 4527 West 2100 South on 40.96 acres and
          is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or
          jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp explained that, over the years, CR England has made different
              expansions to their campus. She said this request is for another expansion for their
              truck center and would be more of a cleanup. Jody pointed out the property
              located on the north of the u-shaped property and stated that it is owned by JCB
              and said it is not part of this application.
              Jody said that typically pavement the required surfacing for truck storage. She
              noted that the applicant is requesting to keep the gravel surfacing. Jody stated that
              she has had a conversation with the Public Works Department, and they would
              prefer the use of gravel and ground asphalt for surfacing because this is a
              retention area only. She said because traffic doesn’t come and go from the site
              daily and the property does not have direct access to a public right-of-way, gravel
              surfacing would be okay.


C-23-2023     Petition by SQUARED AWAY PROPERTIES, LLC requesting a conditional
              use for outside storage. The property is located at 2601 South 2700 West on
              1.53 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-
              3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp said that this application is for outside storage located on the site's
              south side behind the building.

              Jody stated that in 2021, an application was submitted for a church on the subject
              property. She explained they needed more room to attain the required twenty feet
              of landscaping from the property line. Jody said the applicant decided to go to the
              Board of Adjustment requesting a variance for the landscaping from twenty feet
              to ten feet, which was granted. She noted that the church was never constructed,
              so there was never a change of use, and the landscaping was not added.

              Jody pointed out that most of the parking would have been in the exact location of
              the proposed storage area, so the applicant has moved the parking area to the
              opposite side of the property. She noted in doing so, the striping would go into the
              right-of-way. She stated that the applicant is not being asked to change anything
              now. However, the applicant decides to change the use improvements would be
              required and the parking area would no longer exist. Therefore, they would not be
              counted toward the minimum parking requirement. Jody pointed out that they also
              show a double-loaded aisle, and there would need to be more room to
              accommodate that. Jody recommends that we honor the decision of the Board,
              and she will work with the applicant on the site's layout.

              Jody explained that the outside storage is visible from 2700 West, so that they
              would add a masonry wall for screening in that area. She said that the building
              would screen the rest of that area.


C-24-2023     Petition by ROCKY MOUNTAIN POWER requesting a conditional use for
              outside storage for a substation expansion and lay down yard. The property is
              located at 5875 West 4700 South on 6.90 acres and is zoned M
              (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-
              ut.gov)
Discussion:   Jody Knapp stated that this application is from Rocky Mountain Power, and they
              are looking to expand their substation and develop a laydown yard. She noted that
              the laydown yard would be their storage yard for materials and equipment needed
              for projects. Jody said that the applicant proposes meeting the High Image
              Arterial screening requirement, a masonry wall. She explained that the wall would
              run along the frontage and wrap around the side approximately 150 feet. Jody
              stated that they would use a chain-linked fence from there. She mentioned that
              curb, gutter, and sidewalk would be installed along 4700 South. Behind that
              would be the 20 feet of landscaping, followed by the masonry wall. Jody pointed
              out that there are concerns with the tree placement interfering with easements,
              which is still being worked out. However, they are proposing to meet the
              landscaping requirements. Jody pointed out that the applicant would prefer the
              fence to be a distance from the facility for security purposes. She stated that the
              City would like it to run along the property line. Jody will work with the applicant
              on the fence placement.


ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
ZT-6-2023 Petition by JAMES WARNER requesting an amendment to Section 7-14-403
          of the zoning ordinance to allow non-masonry materials on the street facing
          exteriors of buildings within the 5600 West Gateway Overlay Zone. (Staff –
          Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:   Jody Knapp presented this application for Steve Pastorik. She said James Warner
              with Truckpro, LC, requests modifications to the 5600 West Overlay Zone
              architectural standards. She explained that currently, the ordinance states all
              buildings need to be constructed with 100% masonry materials on any street-
              facing facade, and the applicant is requesting that other non-masonry materials be
              permitted. She said they want to add an INEOS Grenadier dealership on the
              corner-facing façade, and their buildings require high-end ACM and possibly
              wood or a simulated wood look on ceramic or metal. Jody explained that this
              ordinance was adopted in 2004 to improve the entrance corridor by adding higher
              design standards. She presented images of existing structures within the City,
              however, outside of the overlay zone using materials like those being proposed.
              Then, Jody displayed the renderings provided by the applicant. She then reviewed
              the staff-suggested verbiage for the ordinance if this application were to be
              approved.

              Commissioner Lovato said he likes the look of the renderings. He stated that it
              must be challenging to prevent the construction of a tin-looking structure and, at
              the same time, add appropriate design standards. Jody said that Steve Pastorik got
              together with the building official to ensure the code states this is structural versus
              a veneer. She pointed out that where this is a conditional use the Planning
              Commission has some discretion regarding the materials used.
