6:00   Training

       1.     “Open and Public Meetings” - Presented by Kasey Wright, City
              Attorney

       Attendance: John Ford, Heather Robertson, Fran Peterson, Ann Peterson, Alan
       Hancock, Karl Brough, Cory Thomson (virtual), Seth Atkinson, Kasey Wright, Shauna
       Talbot, Skip Worwood, Shari Cowan

       Ann Peterson opened the training session and welcomed everyone
       in attendance.

       Kasey gave a presentation to the commission on Open and Public Meetings. He spoke
       about the Sunshine Law in Florida and how it applies to all public meetings. He said it’s
       very similar to Utah’s Open and Public Meetings Act. He emphasized that the public’s
       business should be done in the public. He discouraged “rolling meetings” and explained
       that a quorum is made up of 3 people. He also stated that one-on-one conversations are
       acceptable. He reminded the Commission to remember that they are always on the
       record. He gave an example of a situation where comments were made out of
       frustration, which resulted in embarrassment and a necessary public apology. He stated
       that meetings need to have an agenda & sent out to the public with 24 hours notice. He
       pointed out that Nephi City has not yet adopted an electronic meeting ordinance. Kasey
       briefly touched on closed meetings and executive sessions. He also discussed the
       penalties for violating the Open and Public Meetings Act. He stated that the biggest
       problem with violating this Act is that it undermines the people’s confidence in
       government. He reminded the Commission that the Chair is always in charge of the
       meeting, and that there is an expected level of decorum by all in attendance. Kasey
       answered all questions that were asked by those in attendance.

3.    Title 10 Updates

      a.    Open the Public Hearing
      Ann opened the hearing at 7:03 p.m. as a continuation of the previous month.

      b.       Staff Presentation
      Shauna pointed out that she changed the name of the R1 zone, designating it as a 1/4
      acre zone. The reason being is that an R1 ½ acre zone with animal rights has been
      proposed by the City Council. She also stated that there is an Affordable Housing
      coalition that has been formed and they have been discussing the R3 zone. This
      coalition includes the City Engineer, JD Parady from the City Council, some city staff,
      some developers in town, Cory Thomson from the Planning Commission, and Donald
      Ball. Shauna asked the Commission what their thoughts were about requiring extra
      setbacks based on the height of a building. Heather asked what the advantage is to
      have extra setbacks. Shauna explained that it provides more line of vision. She said that
      zoning was started in New York City when taller buildings started to be built, which we
      now know as skyscrapers. It took away the enjoyment of people that were close to these
      buildings, including their light and air. That is where the term “Health, Safety, & Welfare”
      came from. Skip said that he thought we already discussed this topic. Seth said that the
      R3 zone has been the topic of discussion with the Coalition. Donald Ball stated that the
      task force will be working on something that hasn’t previously existed in the city of
      Nephi. Much discussion was had and concerns were voiced about what is already stated
      in the law, and what the role of the Coalition will be. Recording and script are available
      for the complete discussion. Donald Ball wondered if we really need as much parking
      for a low-income R3 unit, as most of the people who would be utilizing it most likely

     wouldn’t have the need for that much parking. Shauna stated that the parking
     requirements are already in the code. She also stated that in order to revisit the issue,
     they would have to open the code.

     c.     Receive Public Comment
     Included in above discussion (b.)

     d.     Close (or continue) the Public Hearing
     Public Hearing was closed at 8:10 p.m.

     e.      Commission Discussion
     Commission members asked for clarification on what the Coalition’s function is, and
     what direction the Commission needs to take for the R3 zone. Seth gave further
     explanation, and recommended holding off on major changes on that zone. All questions
     and concerns were addressed by Seth and Shauna. Seth mentioned that a temporary
     land use and moratorium on R3 zoned land could be put into play. Heather wondered if
     we could have a deadline placed on the coalition’s recommendations so that the
     Commission has time to review them and make any necessary changes. A decision was
     made to require the coalition’s recommendations by April 1st, work through them as a
     Commission, and have them ready for the City Council by June. Recording and
     transcript are available for full discussion.

     f.      Action Item - Motion
     Ann asked for a motion to accept the Title 10 updates as written, including the new R1½
     acre lots with the exception of the R3 zone. The recommendation to the City Council
     would be a moratorium which, by law, can only be 6 months. With that recommendation,
     directions would be given to the Coalition for high density housing that they have a plan
     in place for the Planning Commission to review by no later than April 1st.

     Motion: Heather Robertson Second: Alan Hancock Outcome: Passed Unanimously

4.   Request for Annexation Area Map and Agricultural Zone
     Seth presented a request made by The Wright Direction for water to be brought to their
     feedlot to water their animals. The Council has been reluctant to supply water outside
     the city limits. Subsequently, The Wright Direction has requested this area to be
     annexed and designated as a Level 3 within the Annexation Area map. They also
     requested, if annexed, that it be made an agricultural zone. Since we don’t currently
     have an agricultural zone, Seth said that would be something we would need to explore
     and see if it’s something we want as part of our zoning. He also said that he does not
     have a recommendation tonight, but is wondering if the Commission would like to
     consider it. Much discussion was had and questions were asked about irrigation
     availability, and Karl asked who would pay for the water line to be installed. Fran
     expressed concern about what this project would do to residents’ water pressure on the
     east side of town. She stated that there are already issues as a result of watering at Red

       Cliffs Elementary, and feels this would only magnify the problem. Alan expressed
       concern about using city water for animals. Ann would like to table this issue until next
       month, until they have more information from the City. Fran pointed out that we haven’t
       even explored what an agricultural zone is yet. Ann stated that they would like to explore
       it independent of this request for annexation, as she feels they are two totally different
       issues.
