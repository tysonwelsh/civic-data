On December 12, 2019, the Planning Commission voted to recommend approval of this rezone application
following a public hearing. Technical issues due to renovation of the City Council Chambers where the

written record for this meeting. The Planning Commission recommended the following motion:

I move the City Council ADOPT Ordinance #20-03, rezoning approximately 0.5 acres located at 2435
West 12600 South, as shown on Exhibit “A”, from RR-22 to C-PO (Commercial Professional Office).


Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone Request made by the applicants. The applicants represent Apex
Insurance and are seeking this rezone with hopes of converting the existing home into an insurance office.

The subject parcel is located on the south side of 12600 South Street at 2435 West. The property sits near
mid-block, about half-way between the 2360 West Street and 2565 West Street. The parcel is narrow and
deep with an unconventional shape. It is approximately 0.5 acres, with just over 69-ft of frontage. The
applicants have indicated their client also has an option to buy the east adjoining parcel which may come
for a rezone in the future.

The property is currently zoned RR-22 (Single Family Residential, ½ acre lots with animal rights) as are the
adjoining properties on the east and west.        Properties to the south are zoned R-4 (Single Family
Residential, 10,000 sf lots). Properties north, across 12600 South are zoned C-G (Commercial Gateway).
There is also C-G zoning on the south side of 126th just two properties to the west at “Twisted Lox”.

The General Plan for the property is “Business Park” as it is for all properties on the south side of 126th
between 2360 and 2565 West. The requested C-PO (Commercial Professional Office) is appropriate for

The Planning Commission voted to recommend APPROVAL of this rezone application.

Noticing and Response

Notices were sent to all property owners within 1000 feet of this site, published in the newspaper and posted
on the City’s web-page. A notice was also posted on the property frontage. Staff did not receive any public
comment on the proposed application.
