    Francis       Lilly     said the staff report             explained         the nature        of the request.        The Historic

    empower          Millcreek          to become          designated         as a Certified         Local     Governrnent             (CLG)     under      the

    the change would                allow    Millcreek            to seek certification.           The other criteria                had already      been
    met. He recommended                     amending          the code to add the additional                    power.         All    four community
    councils       supported           the change.

    criteria     for historic          places.    Lilly     said there was not any. He said designations                                were voluntary
