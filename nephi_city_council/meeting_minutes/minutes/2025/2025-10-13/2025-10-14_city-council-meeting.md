SPECIAL RECOGNITION—VERL WILKEY
       Nephi City was planning to honor Verl Wilkey at City Council Meeting. Unfortunately, he passed
       away that morning. Mayor Seely still read the tribute during the meeting recognizing Verl Wilkey
       for his many years of service, dedication, and contributions to the city. He worked as a building
       inspector for the city from 2007 to 2024. Mayor Seely, several council members, and attendees
       shared and attested to his commitment to helping others and the pride he took in his job.

APPROVAL OF CONSENT AGENDA
      Councilor JD Parady made a motion to approve the consent agenda including Council Meeting 9-
      16-25, Work Session 9-23-25, Claims dated 10-13-25. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the
      motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT
RESOLUTION 10-14-2025—PERSONNEL POLICY REVISIONS
      Lisa Brough, City Finance Director, made some revisions to the personnel policy. The changes
      included adding holiday pay for six different holidays throughout the year. The hours comprising
      the holiday pay were described from 5 pm the day prior to the holiday until midnight on the
      holiday. Changes were also made to the Worker’s Compensation policy and the overtime
      exemption pay for recreational facilities was removed.
      Councilor Travis Worwood made a motion to adopt the resolution and authorize the mayor to
      sign it. Councilor Jeramie Callaway seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.

400 WEST ANNEXATION-ACCEPTANCE OR DENIAL TO MOVE FORWARD WITH ANNEXATION PROCESS
      The petition was submitted for those properties willing to annex. There were two properties that
      had backed out from the original annexation map. An annexation agreement will still need to be
      drawn up. The decision tonight was to decide whether the petition moves forward through the
      annexation process. Councilor Shari Cowan made the motion to accept the petition for the 400
      West annexation and move the process forward. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the
      motion. The motion passed on unanimous vote.

WORK SESSION 7:27 pm
  1. Large Vehicle Parking
          a. Mr. Atkinson noted the complaints from residents and the need for council direction.
          b. Current code was discussed, which allows for tractor-trailers to park on the side of the
              road, and the potential need for changes.
          c. Also, mentioned was leaving the code unchanged and having enforcement be complaint
              driven.
          d. The council discussed the possibility of allowing parking in certain areas if certain criteria
              are met such as road width and property frontage.
          e. The decision was deferred to next meeting. Mayor Seely is planning to invite several
              truckers to share input at the next council meeting on October 21.
   2. Golf Course Expansion
          a. Mr. Atkinson presented a staff report highlighting the financial benefits and risks
              involved.
          b. The council discussed the potential risks involved and the need to figure out supplying
              water for the expansion.
          c. The council decided to move forward by getting some more concrete numbers for initial
              construction costs and putting together financing options.
          d. Mike Booth also announced that the 39 City Employees participated in an Employee Golf
              Scramble today. There were many positive reviews, and he will try to make it a yearly
              event.
   3. Level 2 Analysis for Agri-Park/Data Center Annexation
          a. Mr. Atkinson reviewed the staff report outlining the benefits and potential impacts of
              each.
          b. The data centers would bring tax and utility benefits to the city but would not have a
              huge impact on housing and schools.
          c. The Agri-Park is expected to preserve agricultural land and promote agri-business with
              potential benefits for local farmers and the community.
          d. Shaun Kjar, representing R6 who is spearheading the agri-park, was in attendance.
          e. The council decided to move forward if these two projects requested to go to a level one
              in the annexation process.
