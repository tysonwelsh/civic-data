                                RIVERTON, UTAH
                              RESOLUTION NO. 23-28

  RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF RIVERTON,
STATE OF UTAH, GRANTING CONSENT TO THE COUNTY OF SALT LAKE
  TO FORM THE SALT LAKE COUNTY CONVENTION AND TOURISM
                     ASSESSMENT AREA

       WHEREAS, the Convention and Tourism Business Assessment Area, Act, Utah
Code Title 11, Chapter 42b, (the “Act”), Section 11-42b-103 authorizes the County to form
a convention and tourism assessment area for the purposes of promoting tourism; and

       WHEREAS, the County Council of the County of Salt Lake has requested consent
to form the Salt Lake County Convention and Tourism Assessment Area (the “SLCo
CTAA”) in the City of Riverton with adoption of a Salt Lake County Council resolution.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the City Council of the City of
Riverton, that:

       Section 1: The above recitals are true and correct.

        Section 2: The City Council of Riverton consents to the County of Salt Lake to
include the City of Riverton in the SLCo CTAA, for the formation of the SLCo CTAA and
future renewals.

       Section 3: The Riverton City Recorder is hereby directed to transmit a certified
copy of this Resolution to the Clerk of the Salt Lake County Council.

       Section 4: This Resolution is effective upon its adoption.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 18th day
of April 2023 by the following vote:
