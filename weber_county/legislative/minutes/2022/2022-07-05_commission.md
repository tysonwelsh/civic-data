F. PRESENTATION:
   Scott Paxman (General Manager/CEO, Weber Basin Water Conservancy District): This presentation is for
   information only with no action requested of the Commission. The Weber Basin Water Conservancy District cover
   5 counties totaling 2500 square miles of northern Utah serving approximately 700,000 people. The district
   develops and delivers drinking water, agricultural water, ground water replacement, and industrial water supplies.
   The district intends to increase the property (ad valorem) tax rate for FY2023 that exceeds the certified rate (with
   the certified rate defined as the rate that would collect the same amount of revenue as had been collected in the
   past year); this would be the first increase since 2008. The maximum statutory rate for the district is capped at
   0.0002 giving the Weber Basin Water Conservancy District the lowest cap out of any district in the state; the
   current rate seen is 0.00103; the proposed new rate is 0.00167. Recent financial pressures related to bond interest
   rate increases and inflation result in falling behind on repayment and maintenance of the federal project, giving
   rise to the need for a tax rate increase.
