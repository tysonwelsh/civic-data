12c, ORDINANCE NO, 2020-16 — Providing for Salaries for Members of the County Council (VO ACTION TAKEN)

DISCUSSION: Council members discussed the possibility of increasing Council member salaries due to: no increases for several years;
lowest paid Council in state; time requirements; possible incentive to more/better candidates. Chairman Ward asked if a raise is
provided for in the 2021 budget. Director Jensen replied there is a 3% increase built in to the 2021 budget.

ACTION: None

12d-RESOLUTION-NO:-2020-26—Adopting the 2024 Cache-County Budget (NO-ACTION-TAKEN)
DISCUSSION: Council members Zilles and Erickson asked for more details from County Attorney Swink and Clerk/Auditor Bradfield
regarding their requests for employees. Swink cited increases in case load and noted Cache County does not have the same ratio of
attorneys to population as other Utah counties and feels the additional attorney will help his office continue to be an effective
department. Bradfield fielded questions about part-time versus full-time staff. Two key employees have retired and need to be
replaced. One position was part-time, but Bradfield wants to upgrade it to full-time. The employee was previously full-time and
became a seasonal employee after his retirement. The office needs a full-time elections worker. Bradfield also stated his office has run
out of space which will have to be addressed in the future.

ORDINANCE NO. 2020 - 12
AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING A FUND BALANCE POLICY

WHEREAS, the Governmental Accounting Standards Board (GASB) issued Statement No. 54, Fund Balance
Reporting and Governmental Fund Type Definitions, in order to clarify the meaning of “fund balance” on
financial statements of governmental entities; and

WHEREAS, the County Council desires to establish a Fund Balance Policy for the General Fund and other
funds of the County that is consistent with GASB Statement No. 54 and that will help guide decisions

regarding County funds; and

WHEREAS, Chapter 56 of Title 3 of the County Code is outdated in purpose and language and is not needed
to meet operational or capital needs of the County; and

WHEREAS, the County Council believes it to. be necessary and appropriate and in the best interest of the
citizens of Cache County to establish a policy regarding the administration and reporting of fund balances;

NOW, THEREFORE, the County legislative body of Cache County ordains as follows:

Section 1.

Chapter 56 of Title 3 of the Cache County Code is hereby repealed.

Section 2.

The following Fund Balance Policy shall be adopted into the County Financial Policies and Procedures

handbook:
See attached

Section 3.

The Fund Balance Policy may be amended from time to time by the County Council, with the advice of the
County Finance Director, to remain consistent with professional accounting standards and best

management practices.

_ Section 4.

This ordinance shall take effect fifteen days following its adoption, and the County Executive and other
county officials are authorized and directed to act accordingly.

This ordinance was duly adopted by the Cache County Council on the 10" day of November, 2020.

Fund Balance Policy

PURPOSE

The purpose of this Policy is to establish classifications for Cache County fund balances in accordance with
professional accounting standards. The fund balance classifications indicate the extent to which the
County is constrained as to the purposes for which particular funds can be spent.

DEFINITIONS

The Governmental Accounting Standards Board (GASB) has identified and defined five classifications for
fund balances. Each Cache County fund balance will be classified into one of those five categories, which

are as follows:

1. Nonspendable — Amounts that cannot be sperit because they are either not in spendable form or
because they are legally or contractually required to be maintained intact.

2. Restricted —- Amounts for which constraints have been placed on the use of the resources by
external creditors (such as through a debt covenant), grantors, or contributors; by laws or
regulations of other governments; or by law imposed through constitutional provisions or
enabling legislation.

3. Committed — Amounts that can be used only for specific purposes pursuant to constraints
imposed by formal action of the County Council. These amounts cannot be used for any purpose
other than the purpose identified by the County Council unless the County Council removes or
changes the specified purpose by taking the same type of action that was employed to initially
commit the funds.

4, Assigned — Amounts that are constrained by the County’s intent that they be used for a specific
purpose, but which amounts are neither restricted nor committed. The intent that the funds be
used for a specific purpose may: be expressed by the County Council or by any other body or
official to whom the authority to’ assign funds has been delegated. For all governmental funds
except the General Fund, this classification also includes any positive fund balance remaining at
the end of the calendar year.

5. Unassigned — This classification includes the residual fund balance for the General Fund. It also
includes the negative residual fund balance of any other governmental fund that cannot be
eliminated by the offsetting of assigned fund balance amounts.

COMMITTED FUND BALANCE

An ordinance or resolution by the County Council is required to create, change, or remove a fund balance
commitment. The County Council may create, change, or remove a fund balance commitment at any time.

ASSIGNED FUND BALANCE

The County Executive is authorized to assign in writing a fund balance to a specific intended use. The
County Council may by ordinance or resolution create, amend, or rescind any assignment. A fund balance
assignment may be created, amended, or rescinded at any time.

ORDER OF EXPENDITURE OF FUNDS

When expenditures are incurred for which restricted, committed, assigned, or unassigned fund balances
are available, the County considers amounts to have been expended first out of restricted funds, then out
of committed funds, then out of assigned funds, and finally out of unassigned funds, as needed. In
government-wide reporting, when expenditures are made for purposes for which both restricted and

unrestricted net position funds are available, the County considers restricted funds to have been
expended first.

FUND BALANCE LIMITS FOR THE GENERAL FUND

The combined total of committed, assigned, and unassigned fund balances remaining in the General Fund
at the end of a calendar year should be at least 5% of annual expenditures and be no more than 20% of
annual expenditures. When the General Fund balance falls below 5% of annual expenditures at the end
of a calendar year, then the budget of the following year will be amended to show a contribution to fund
balance that will bring the balance to at least 5% of annual expenditures. When the General Fund balance
rises above 20% of annual expenditures at the end of a calendar year, then the budget of the following
year will be amended to show an appropriation from the General Fund balance that will reduce the
balance back to 20% or less of annual expenditures.

AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR CHANGES OF SALARIES FOR CACHE

RESOLUTIONS AND ORDINANCES. -

The County Council of Cache County, Utah, in a regular meeting, lawful notice of which has
been given, finds that a public hearing regarding the proposed county budget for 2021 including the
change of salaries for the Cache County Officers for 2021 was conducted on December 1, 2020, in
accordance with Section 17-16-14, Utah Code Annotated, upon lawful notice; that the Organic Act
for the Government of Cache County, Utah, as approved on November 6, 1984, authorizes the
modification of the salaries for all elected county officers by ordinance; that the budget for Cache
County officers has been adopted by the Cache County Council in accordance with the applicable
law; and that an ordinance providing for a salary change for the elected county officers is appropriate
for carrying into effect the approved salary changes.

Now therefore, the Cache County Council ORDAINS as follows:

shall be as follows:

County Recorder $ 90,341

County Treasurer $ 90,341

SECTION 2: REPEALER

The salary provisions of all prior ordinances or resolutions, ot any parts thereof, in conflict
with this ordinance, are hereby repealed and superseded by this ordinance to the extent of
such conflict, Otherwise such resolutions and ordinances shall remain in full force and
effect.

SECTION 3: EFFECTIVE DATE

. This ordinance shall take effect immediately upon approval and publication in the manner by
