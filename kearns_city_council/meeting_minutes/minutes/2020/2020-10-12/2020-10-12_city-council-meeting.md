               Mayor Kelly Bush referred to the temporary conduct of electronic meetings of the
Kearns Metro Township Council that is consistent with the Utah Open and Public Meeting Act.
This allows the Council to hold electronic meetings without a physical location.

                                 ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦
Unified Fire Authority

                Mayor Bush stated Unified Fire Authority (UFA) has started using the new
Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD) system. When there is a request for service, the nearest fire
station will respond. The responding fire station could be from West Valley City, West Jordan fire
stations or other nearby stations.

                                 ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦
COVID-19 Update

               Mayor Bush stated COVID-19 numbers are up in Kearns. Another mask giveaway
will be held on Saturday in partnership with Community Nursing who will be providing flu shots.
She encourages all Council Members to get flu shots as well. The wellness bus will come to
Kearns and provide testing.

                                 ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦ ♦♦♦

Unified Police Department (UPD)

               Chief Levi Hughes stated UPD is seeing its COVID-19 positive cases go up in the
last 30 days. UPD’s swat team protected Vice-President Pence and Senator Kamala Harris. When
COVID-19 hit, UPD shut down all precincts and because of the increase in cases, it is considering
it again. He would like the Councils input on whether the Kearns precinct should be closed. Kearns

Other Council Business

CARES Act Amendment – Second Distribution of Funds

                Mayor Bush stated Kearns was allocated another $1.2 million in CARES Act
funding. The funds can be used to update the UPD precinct to help prevent COVID-19. The
precinct is carpeted, has fabric furniture, and does not allow proper protection from COVID-19.

                                 −−−−−−−−−−−−−−
At this point the meeting was terminated due to extenuating circumstances. Due to these
circumstances, the Kearns Metro Township Council was unable to complete outstanding votes
on nor complete the agenda. It will hold a special meeting on October 22, 2020 to complete all
remaining items.
