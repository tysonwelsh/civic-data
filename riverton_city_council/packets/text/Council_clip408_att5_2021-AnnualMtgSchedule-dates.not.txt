                                    RIVERTON CITY
                               2021 MEETING SCHEDULES

Pursuant to § 52-4-202(2) Utah Code Annotated, Riverton City hereby gives notice of the
following meeting schedules for the Year 2021:
    • City Council Meetings are generally held on the first and third Tuesday of each month
        with Work Sessions beginning at 5:00 p.m. and City Council Meetings beginning at 7:00
        p.m., at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood Road, Riverton, Utah.
    • Planning Commission Meetings are held on the second and fourth Thursdays of each
        month beginning at 6:30 p.m. at Riverton City Hall, located at 12830 South Redwood
        Road, Riverton, Utah.
    • Riverton Fire Service Area (RFSA) Meetings are held on a regular basis on the first
        Tuesday of each month following City Council Meetings.
    • Riverton Law Enforcement Service Area (RLESA) Meetings are held on a regular
        basis on the third Tuesday of each month following City Council Meetings.
    • Redevelopment Agency (RDA) Meetings, Special and/or Emergency Meetings, in
        addition to those specified, may be held as circumstances require; best practicable
        notice will be given for such meetings.
    • Board of Adjustment Hearings may be held as circumstances require; best practicable
        notice will be given for such meetings.

Public Meetings Act. Riverton City Hall is an accessible facility. Individuals needing special
accommodations or assistance during this meeting shall notify the City Recorder’s Office at 801-
