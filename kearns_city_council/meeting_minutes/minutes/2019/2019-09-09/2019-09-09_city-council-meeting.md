Application No. 30983 – Wright Development requesting to rezone 8.19 acres from a R-M to C-2
zone on property located at 5622 South 5600 West.

                Todd Draper, Planning and Development Services Division, stated currently the
property is zoned for multi-family residential. The request is to change the zone from an R-M to
a C-2 zone to allow for a mixed-use project with retail/office use on the ground floor and residential
units above. The property to the south is zoned R-1-6, property to the west is R-1-7, property to
the north is C-2, and the property to the east is zoned for single-family homes. The Kearns Metro
Township Planning Commission and staff recommend approval of this rezone request.

              Mayor Bush stated Wright Development purchased this property from the Utah
Department of Transportation (UDOT). The property is located just south of Holiday Oil on 5600
West.

               Mayor Bush opened the public hearing.

               Roger Snow asked if there was another zone that would allow for office/retail but
limit the density of 25 family units per acre. Whenever apartments are built, the building
deteriorates, and the community is struck with them.

               Mr. Draper stated there is not another zone that would allow for that. However,
there is the ability to limit density, use, and height through zoning conditions. If the apartment
units are owned by different people, then a homeowner’s association would be required.

               Paul Merrill stated single-family homes are being crowded out due to multi-density
properties. Apartments always start out looking nice but overtime they deteriorate. Apartment
owners do not take care of their properties. He would like to see nicer dwelling units that are not
high density because they are taken care of.

               Nancy Arts asked how this development would affect her taxes.

               Mayor Bush stated if it is commercial it will feed into Kearns tax base, which will
help pay for services.

               Mr. Draper stated the developer is not in attendance at this meeting.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to close
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to table this
request until the developer is available to discuss what he is planning. The motion passed
unanimously.

Application #30670 – Randy Moore is requesting a preliminary plat approval to create the 30 Lot
Olympic Park Subdivision to be located at 5944 South Cougar Lane.

                Todd Draper stated the request before the council tonight is the approval of a 30-
lot subdivision plat for a development located at 5944 South Cougar Lane. The Kearns Metro
Township Planning Commission recommended that the Council approve the subdivision plat. The
plat shows a variety of lot sizes, the minimum being 5,000 square feet and the largest just over
9,000 square feet. All setbacks that are currently in the zoning ordinance will be followed. The
general lot lay out, traffic and roadways have all been reviewed and approved by the individual
reviewing agencies. State law says, “you shall approve it if it meets the ordinances of the
jurisdiction.”

               Mayor Bush stated this is the first time the Kearns Metro Township Council has
had the opportunity to look at the plat. She is not comfortable making a decision in under 20
minutes. The public hearing will be held tonight, after which she would like to continue this request
to the October meeting, to give the Council the opportunity to review the plat.

               Council Member Snow asked if there was only one narrow road accessing the
subdivision.

                Mr. Draper stated there is only one access road, but it meets the 50-feet cross
section for a road.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Brown, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

               Randy Moore, Developer, asked if the Council had any questions or concerns
about the subdivision plat, if so, he would be glad to address those. He is not asking for any
modification from existing ordinances. The lots size, widths, and depths meet all requirements.
The plans were submitted months ago.




                                                 3
DATE           MONDAY                             SEPTEMBER                                  9, 2019

                Mr. Draper stated the plans were not submitted to the Council earlier because the
Planning and Development Division within the County is short-staffed, and he was writing the
reports on this plat earlier today. The plat was thoroughly reviewed, and it meets all the ordinance
requirements as far as the subdivision is concerned.

               Mr. Moore stated the one-month delay would make a huge impact. He is very
frustrated and would like to get moving on this subdivision before winter sets in.

               A member of Kearns Community Council stated the community council’s
concern is the same, it only saw the plans five minutes before it had to make a decision. He would
recommend that the Council table this request for one month.

               Paul Merrill stated he is familiar with this property because he lives just to the
south. The streets in this neighborhood are overpacked and another 30-lot subdivision would not
help.

                 Lisa (last name inaudible) stated the lot is her backyard and she has several
concerns about it. The lot is small, it will be crowded with 30 homes on it. There is already a lot
of traffic issues in this area. Additional homes will only bring in more traffic, more population, and
crime rates will go up. This is not a good idea.

                Mayor Bush asked what comments the fire marshal had on the access in and out
of this property.

               Mr. Draper stated the only comments from the fire marshal was that the fire
hydrant location must remain in the location shown on the plat, the street diameters must retain
the dimensions shown, and the number of lots most remain at 30 or below.

               Mr. Moore stated he originally started on this project over a year ago. At that time
the zone was designated to be a hotel or apartments. If he had gone with apartments, he could
have put in as many as 125 units. The lot size is like the homes to the south. The proposal would
be to build two-story homes with four bedrooms. All homes would have a two-car garage.

               Council Member Snow asked what the average price would be on these homes.

               Mr. Moore stated the range would be between $330,000 to $375,000.

              Joe Hatch stated the homes to the south of this proposed development is roughly
the same size with 30 homes on it.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to close
the public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

              Council Member Brown asked for clarification. If this subdivision meets all the
requirements, does state law say the Council has to approve it.




                                                  4
DATE           MONDAY                          SEPTEMBER                                9, 2019

               Mr. Draper stated yes, but the Council can take the additional time to review the
plat.

               Council Member Schaeffer stated development looks good on paper, but he
would like time to look at it.

             Council Member Peterson stated this is the best use for this piece of property.
Kearns needs homes instead of apartments.

             Council Member Brown stated the Council owes it to the community to table this
request because it has not had time to look at it.

               Council Member Brown, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to table
this request for one month. Roll was called showing the vote to be Council Member Schaeffer
“Aye,” Council Member Snow “Nay,” Mayor Bush “Nay,” Council Member Brown “Aye” and
Council Member Peterson “Nay.” The motion failed by a vote of 3 in opposition and 2 in favor.

               Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to approve
this request. Roll was called showing the vote to be Council Member Schaeffer, “Nay,” Council
Member Snow “Aye,” Mayor Bush “Aye,” Council Member Brown “Nay,” and Council Member
Peterson “Aye.” The motion carried by a vote of 3 in favor and 2 opposed.

for the metro township; and
