     Chairperson Jacob called the meeting to order at 6:59 pm

 2. BUSINESS ITEMS
       a. Resolution No. 114 setting June 26, 2024 as the date for the public hearing regarding the
          adoption of the final budget.
           Council Office Director Alan Anderson explained that the resolution would set June 26,
           2024, as the date for a public hearing to receive comments prior to adopting the final
           budget.

           MOTION: Board Member Green moved to APPROVE Resolution No. 114 setting June 26,
                   2024, as the date for the public hearing regarding the adoption of the final
                   budget for Fiscal Year 2025.
                   Vice Chairperson Lamb seconded the motion.
           The vote was recorded as follows:
           YES:       Pamela Bloom, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, Kayleen Whitelock, Bob Bedore, Chad
                      Lamb, Kent Shelton
           NO:
           ABSENT:
           The motion passed 7-0.


 3. PUBLIC COMMENT
       a. Resolution No. 113 adopting the Annual Budget for the Municipal Building
