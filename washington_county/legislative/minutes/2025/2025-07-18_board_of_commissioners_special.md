WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
JULY 18, 2025

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 10:15 a.m. on July 18, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Victor
Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy County Attorney Devin
Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION;

PUBLIC HEARING _ON__CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER SALARY
ADJUSTMENT

Commissioner Iverson stated to stay market competitive, an adjustment to scale was necessary.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow Open the Public Hearing Regarding Chief
Administrative Officer Salary Adjustment. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

Seeing no one come forward, Commissioner Almquist made a motion to close the public hearing.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist Close the Public Hearing Regarding Chief
Administrative Officer Salary Adjustment. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting aye.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist Accept the Chief Administrative Officer
Salary Adjustment. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and Almquist voting

aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 10:19 a.m.
