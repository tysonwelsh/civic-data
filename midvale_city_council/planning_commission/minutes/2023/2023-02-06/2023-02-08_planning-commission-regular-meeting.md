GENERAL SESSION

Chair Snow called the Planning & Zoning Commission meeting to order at 6:05 p.m. He
explained how the meeting would proceed. First, the Planning Department would brief the
Commission; then the applicant would speak to the Commission; the item will be opened to the
public for their comments on public hearing agenda items; the Midvale Deputy City Attorney
will then brief the Commission on their role on the given item; and then the Commission would
deliberate and decide.

PUBLIC HEARINGS

     1.   Cleber Fabricio requests Conditional Use Permit approval for auto sales located at
          8228 S State Street in the State Street Commercial (SSC) zone.

          The applicant requests conditional use permit approval to allow for auto sales located at
          8228 S State St in the State Street Commercial (SSC) zone. Public notice has been sent to
          property owners within 500 feet of the subject parcel. No objections have been received
          as of the writing of this report.

          Staff recommends approval.
