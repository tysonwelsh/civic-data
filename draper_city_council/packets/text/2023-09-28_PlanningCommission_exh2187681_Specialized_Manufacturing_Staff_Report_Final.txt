BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY

This application is a request for approval of a land use and zoning map amendment on
approximately 3.89 acres located on the south side of 12300 S, at approximately 687 W 12300
S, and north of Galena Hills Park (Exhibit C & D). The property is currently zoned CC and the
current land use designation is Neighborhood Commercial.

The applicant is proposing the land use map and zoning map changes to allow for construction
of a light industrial building on the property. The business will make electrical wiring harnesses
and small metal parts.

Criteria For Approval
Zoning Map Amendments
A Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City Council and is
not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation to the City Council,
the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in Section 9-5-060(E) of the
DCMC:

         1. Map Amendments:
             a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and
                policies of the city's general plan;
             b. Whether the proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character of
                existing development in the vicinity of the subject property;
             c. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with the standards of any
                applicable overlay zone;
             d. The extent to which the proposed amendment may adversely affect adjacent
                property; and
             e. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property,
                including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and
                fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and
                wastewater and refuse collection.

Land Use Map Amendments
The Land Use Map amendment request falls under DCMC Section 9-2-020(F) because the Land
Use Map is part of Draper City’s General Plan. That section lists the following criteria for a
General Plan amendment as:

        Plan Amendment: All plan amendments shall be in accordance with Utah Code
        Annotated 10-9a-404, as amended and, unless requested by the city's legislative body,
        shall follow the procedures as outlined in Draper City Municipal Code 9-5-060(D).”

We have reviewed the subject zone map and land use map amendment applications, proposing 3.89
acres to be zoned from Community Commercial (CC) to General Commercial (CG) in accordance with
the provisions of Section 9-5-060(E)(1)(e) of the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC). We speak
primarily to the adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property. In making
an amendment, the City Council should consider the following factors. Accordingly, the following
comments are recommended for your consideration:

   1. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including but
      not limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm
      water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water and refuse collection;

       Other than noted below, we are not aware of any inadequacies of the facilities intended to
       serve this property.

            a. The change in zone from one commercial zone to another commercial zone
               represents a potential increase in traffic based on the permitted or conditional uses
               within the zone. The proposed zone has a variety of uses that generate different
               traffic volumes so it is impossible to identify a future impact other than to note the
               potential of an increase in traffic from its current zone to the proposed zone. Any
               increase in the number of trips or the traffic intensity would be limited to 12300
               South due to the location of the subject property. It is unlikely that any potential
               increase in traffic would disperse into residential areas or other areas of the city
               based on its location near the western boundary of the city. However, the size of
               the parcel and the proposed uses do not trigger an automatic traffic impact study
               because it does not exceed the number of peak hour trips that typically would
               necessitate offsite mitigation measures. Any increase in traffic would be limited
               to 12300 South, an arterial street planned for significant traffic. 12300 South is
               owned by the Utah Department of Transportation, UDOT. There existing frontage
               improvements on the proposed site.

            b. Connectivity with the subject parcel to a public right-of-way is on 12300 South, an
               arterial street intended to accommodate heavy traffic. Access locations are be required
               to meet Draper City Standards and are required to meet UDOT standards as outlined
               in our Master Transportation Plan and will be evaluated at the time of a site plan or
               subdivision application.

RESPONSES TO QUESTIONNAIRE

   1. REQUESTED LAND USE DESIGNATION – We request that the land use designation for this property be changed
      from Neighborhood Commercial to Community Commercial.
   2. REASON FOR THE REQUEST – The reason for the request is to allow light manufacturing in this area, in addition
      to the other commercial uses that these two land uses have in common. Light manufacturing is clean indoor
      manufacturing. Specialized Manufacturing makes electrical wiring harnesses for fighter jets and small metal
      parts.
   3. HARMONIOUS WITH THE OVERALL CHARACTER IN THE EXISTING DEVELOPMENT
          a. The existing development is presently dominated by auto sales & services and park & landscape services.
              The addition of light manufacturing and associated offices would fit well into, diversify, and enhance this
              area. Goal Zero to the east of the rail tracks may be a similar type of business.
          b. The Community Commercial land use better describes the uses intended by the General Plan along this
              major transportation artery that is 12300 South.
          c. Neither the current Community Commercial zoning designation nor our proposed Community General
              zoning designation for this parcel are listed as compatible zones in the present Neighborhood
              Commercial land use. Both zones will be compatible with a Community Commercial land use
              designation. This pocket of commercial zones along 12300 South, next to the railway overpass is entirely
              comprised of parcels zoned either Community Commercial and General Commercial.
          d. East of the railway overpass are more commercial zones with business and light manufacturing uses that
              are also compatible with the proposed Community Commercial land use.
          e. The light manufacturing and other uses outlined in the CG – Commercial General zone are in harmony
              with land uses that Draper General Plan to locate these businesses along the main arteries in the city.
          f. The parcel is located on the south side of 12300 South adjacent to the dog park and away from the
              residential neighborhood north of 12300 South. So, it is on a major artery street and not on a street
              transitioning into a neighborhood.

   4. CONSISTANT WITH DRAPER’S GENERAL PLAN –

       We believe that our request to amend the land use map for this parcel at 687 W 12300 S is consistent with
       Draper’s General Plan for the following reasons:

           a. 12300 South is a major transit zone where the General Plan encourages the integration of uses including
              residential, retail, office, and light industrial along major transit investment corridors.
           b. The General Plan “Encourage(s) and support(s) a diversity of businesses” to diversify Draper’s business
              and retail community so that it includes a variety of business types.
           c. The General Plan “Emphasize(s) the retention and expansion of businesses in Draper.
           d. All parcels within this commercial pocket are zoned either CC-Community Commercial or CG-General
              Commercial which are compatible with the requested Community Commercial Land Use, but not
              Compatible with the present Neighborhood Commercial Land Use. Revising the Land Use Map to
              Community Commercial will make the Land Use Map and the Zoning Map consistent.

       12300 South is a major transportation artery. Currently the Land Use designation is Neighborhood Commercial.
       However, Neighborhood Commercial is defined in the General Plan as “Small-scale commercial land uses that
       serve local residents in adjacent neighborhoods.” And it describes the location as “Adjacent to neighborhood”
       and “Along local roads”. (See General Plan pg.199.) On the other hand, the Community Commercial land use
       designation describes these land uses as “Includes the full scope of commercial land uses that require and utilize
       exposure to the freeway”. The Plan describes these locations as “Strategically placed along high-traffic corridors
       with convenient points of traffic access to and from residential areas”. This Community Commercial Land Use
lists both CC-Community Commercial and CG -General Commercial as compatible zones, among others. (See
General Plan pg. 202.) This site at 687 W. 123000 S. is a major arterial road and probably better fits the
definition of a “Community Commercial” land use than a Neighborhood Commercial use. And a CG-Commercial
General zoning is compatible with this land use designation.

Amending the land use designation to Community Commercial “Encourage and support a diversity of
businesses.” This is the General Plan’s Economic Vitality Goal EV-2. (See General Plan pg. 34.) The Community
Commercial land use will allow more diversity of businesses. Many of these businesses will be conditional uses.
The site plan approval process will still be able to set conditions to mitigate any impacts that the businesses may
have on the community.

Amending the land use designation to Community Commercial, where appropriate, will help fulfill Economic
Vitality Goal EV-1. This goal ”Emphasize(s) the retention and expansion of businesses in Draper and provide(s)
support mechanisms for small businesses in Draper”. As Draper businesses grow, they are attracted to the
same qualities that initially drew them to the city. In our case, Specialized Manufacturing has been in business in
Draper since 1997. It has been in its present location on Minuteman Drive since 2016. It continues to grow and
flourish in Draper. We now need a larger facility. We want to stay in and be a part of Draper. There are two
parts to our business. One is hand making electrical wiring harnesses for fighter jets and the other is fabricating
small metal parts. It is a fascinating business that may not fit in a typical description of businesses desired in
zoning and land uses. But it does fit Drapers Economic Vitality Goal EV-2. This goal is to “Encourage and support
a diversity of businesses”, “Nurture and support established businesses through economic development
programs and resources” and “Diversify Draper’s business and retail community so that it includes a variety of
business types”. Amending this parcel’s land use on a major arterial street to Community Commercial will help
accomplish Draper’s ECONOMIC VITALITY GOALS described as “Many activities cultivate Draper’s economic
vitality and diversity, including retail and varied employment opportunities along the major thoroughfares that
dissect the City. (See General Plan pg. 34.) Encouraging and accommodating Draper’s growing businesses will
help maintain a healthy jobs-housing balance as discussed in the General Plan’s sections on Jobs. (See pg. 17.)

Amending the land use designation to Community Commercial will make it compatible with the current CC-
Community Commercial and CG-General Commercial zoning that make up the entirety of the parcels along
this portion of 12300 S. It will be up to the city if they want to correct this for all the parcels in this area. But we
request that the land use designation for our parcel be amended to Community Commercial so that it is
consistent with the zoning in this area. This amendment will also make it consistent with the General Plan for
commercial activity along a major transportation artery. See Compatible Zoning in the Community Commercial
Land Use Description on pg. 202 compared with the Neighborhood Commercial Land Use Description on pg. 199
in the General Plan.
