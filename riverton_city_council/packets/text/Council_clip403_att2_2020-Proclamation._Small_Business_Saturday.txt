                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

                               SMALL BUSINESS SATURDAY

      WHEREAS, the importance of small businesses should not be taken for granted as they
make essential contributions to our local economy and community; and

       WHEREAS, According to the United States Small Business Administration, there are
30.7 million small businesses in the United States, they represent 99.7% of all firms with paid
employees and they are responsible for 64.9% of net new jobs created from 2000 to 2018; and

        WHEREAS, Statistically, 96% of consumers who shopped on Small Business Saturday
agree that shopping at small, independently owned businesses supports their commitment to
making purchases that have a positive social, economic and environmental impact; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton’s economy is supported by a vast number of small businesses that
are key in supporting the economical viability of our great city; and

       NOW, THEREFORE, I, Mayor Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton City, Utah, do
proclaim the day of November 28 as

                               SMALL BUSINESS SATURDAY

      AND ask the residents of our community do their part to support small businesses and
merchants located in Riverton on Small Business Saturday and throughout the year.

          GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 17th day of November 2020.


                 [SEAL]
