                               CERTIFICATE OF AWARD
                            OF THE CITY OF DRAPER, UTAH
                                     relating to its

                       SALES TAX REVENUE BONDS, SERIES 2022

        Pursuant to the Local Government Bonding Act, Title 11, Chapter 14, Utah Code
Annotated 1953, as amended (the “Act”), and the authority delegated in the resolutions
adopted by the City Council (the “Council”) of the City of Draper, Utah (the “Issuer”) on
May 17, 2022 and June 7, 2022 (collectively, the “Resolution”), authorizing the issuance
and sale of the Issuer’s Sales Tax Revenue Bonds, Series 2022 (the “Series 2022 Bonds”),
the undersigned Designated Officers of the Issuer are authorized to accept bids for the sale
of the Series 2022 Bonds. The Series 2022 Bonds were the subject of a sale held today,
______________, at which it was determined that the bid of ______________ (the
“Purchaser”) was the best bid received for the purchase of the Series 2022 Bonds. Based
upon the foregoing determination, the undersigned Designated Officers of the Issuer
hereby award sale of the Series 2022 Bonds to the Purchaser at a purchase price of
$______________. [The verification of said winning bid is attached hereto as Schedule
A.]

       The undersigned hereby approve prior to the original issuance of the Series 2022
Bonds, the following terms of the Series 2022 Bonds:

        1.     The sale of the Series 2022 Bonds was conducted as [a competitive bid]
[private placement];

         2.       The final principal amount is $________ for the Series 2022 Bonds;

      3.       The maturity dates, principal amounts, and interest rates for the Series 2022
Bonds are set forth in Schedule B attached hereto;

        4.     The aggregate price to be paid by the Purchaser for the Series 2022 Bonds
shall be $____________ (representing the par amount of the Series 2022 Bonds, plus a
reoffering premium of $__________, less a Purchaser’s discount of $___________); and

        5.    The Series 2022 Bonds maturing on or before ________ are not subject to
optional redemption prior to maturity. The Series 2022 Bonds maturing on or after
___________ are subject to redemption at the option of the Issuer on ___________ and on
any date thereafter prior to maturity, in whole or in part, from such maturities or parts
thereof as may be selected by the Issuer.

       All capitalized terms used, but not defined herein, shall have the meanings assigned
by the Resolution or the Indenture (as such term is defined in the Resolution) unless the
context hereof requires otherwise.
