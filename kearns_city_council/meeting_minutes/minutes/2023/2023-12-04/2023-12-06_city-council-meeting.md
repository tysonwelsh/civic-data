Planning

Title 12 Updates

               Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, gave an overview of
the four purposes of these changes.
The first was to implement the methamphetamine home abatement bill by requiring the use of
abatement professionals to clean up contaminated buildings and to start the 180 day period
required by the bill.
The second distinguished between major and minor violations, with the latter requiring only a
warning and applying only to weeds that cover less than 100 square feet or junk that covers 50
square feet.
The third created a definition for “residing in an RV”.
The fourth created a clearer process for obtaining an administrative search warrant.

              The Council decided to change the language for RV use to “…between the hours
of 1:00 am and 5:00 am for no longer than 14 days within a six month period.”

              Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Schaeffer, moved to
approve the updates subject to the above referenced change. The motion passed unanimously.

WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) contracts with the Greater Salt Lake Municipal

Metro Township Clerk; and

3.    Staff are instructed to post this resolution and the attached 2024 fee schedule to the
Kearns Metro Township website and to the “Municipal Resolutions Book” within Kearns’s
