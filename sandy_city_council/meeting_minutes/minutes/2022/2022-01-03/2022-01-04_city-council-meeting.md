City Council Meeting Minutes January 4, 2022

Council Member Business
Council Member Zach Robinson thanked all city staff who coordinated the swearing in
event yesterday. He encouraged residents to complete the current city survey regarding
the Canyons transportation issue. He spoke about the newly created Alta Canyon
Advisory Committee moving forward.
Council Member Marci Houseman shared information about the Engaging Local
Government Leaders Association with her fellow Council Members. She thanked the
Public Works department for offering the cardboard recycling program and wondered if
additional drop off locations were planned. She also had some comments and
suggestions regarding the recent survey sent out by the City.
Council Member Ryan Mecham thanked all staff involved with the coordination of the
swearing in ceremony. He mentioned to Chief Severson that he had District 1 residents’
comments to share with him.
Council Member Alison Stroud welcomed the new Council Members and she looks
forward to working with them.

Mayor's Report
Mayor Monica Zoltanski thanked Ms. Jacobson, Ms. Edwards and Ms. Madsen for their
efforts in coordinating the swearing in ceremony yesterday. She spoke about the
Community Heroes spotlight and encouraged the community to share their stories with
the City. Mayor Zoltanski congratulated all the newly elected Sandy City officials. She
also encouraged the community to participate in the city survey gathering feedback on
the Canyons Transportation issue - we need to hear your voice and know your thoughts.

CAO Report
KIm Bell, Interim CAO, provided an update on the glass/cardboard recycling program and
Junior Jazz.
Council moved to Item #8 on the Agenda.
