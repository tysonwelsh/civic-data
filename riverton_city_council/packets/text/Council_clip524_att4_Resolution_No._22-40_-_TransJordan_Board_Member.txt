                               RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                               RESOLUTION NO. 22-40

  A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF RIVERTON CITY GIVING ITS
ADVICE AND CONSENT TO THE APPOINTMENT OF REPRESENTATIVE BOARD
          MEMBER TO THE TRANSJORDAN LANDFILL BOARD

         WHEREAS, the City Council have an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement
 relative to the ownership, operation, maintenance, and control of a Solid Waste
 Management Facility by seven member cities inclusive of Draper, Midvale,
 Murray, Riverton, Sandy, South Jordan, and West Jordan; and

        WHEREAS, the Trans-Jordan Cities Interlocal Agency is governed by a board of
 representatives of the seven members cities; and

       WHEREAS, the representative of each City is appointed by the governing
 body of such city to serve as a member of the Board; and

         WHEREAS, an alternate representative is also appointed to serve as a member of
 the Board in the absence or disability of the representative with the same voting
 privileges; and

        WHEREAS, the City Council desires to appoint Cary Necaise, Public
 Works Director, as an alternate representative for the City of Riverton on the
 Trans-Jordan Landfill Board.

        NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton
 City as follows:

        1. The City Council desires to appoint Cary Necaise, Public Works Director, as
           the alternate representative to the Transjordan Landfill Board.
        2. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 7th day of
June 2022 by the following vote:
