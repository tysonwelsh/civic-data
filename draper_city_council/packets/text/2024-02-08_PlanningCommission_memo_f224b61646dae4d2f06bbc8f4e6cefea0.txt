MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From:
Date: 2024-02-08
Re: Public Hearing: Federal Heights Holdings LLC Zoning and Land Use Amendments
(Legislative Action)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval for zoning and land use map amendments for
approximately 1.19 acres located on the east side of 700 East, at approximately 11743 S. 700 East.
The property is currently zoned RA1. The applicant is requesting that the zoning and land use map
amendments are approved to allow for an office to operate in the existing home on the site.



The property owner applied for a business license on March 10, 2022 to locate a software company
at the property in the existing home. However, because the business owner does not intend to live
at the home, they do not meet the qualifications for operating as a home-based business.
Currently, the applicant has indicated he is operating in another city and is awaiting the outcome of
these applications before he potentially moves the business to Draper. This application proposes to
change the zoning to a commercial zone so that they can convert the home to a commercial use
and operate as an office use from this location. The business license is pending until the outcome of
the zoning and land use map amendment request. If the applicant is successful in obtaining the
land use and zoning map changes, they will still have to obtain approval of an amended site plan to
add the minimum required parking to the property for the intended use.



This item was originally scheduled for the February 9, 2023 meeting of the Planning Commission
and the applicant requested a continuance to a date uncertain before the Planning Commission
meeting was held. The applicant is now ready to move forward with the request.
