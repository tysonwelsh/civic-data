                                       TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH
                                        ORDINANCE NO. 22- 10


AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING MAP
AMENDMENT FOR THE PROPERTIES LOCATED AT 5465, 5433, AND 5439 SOUTH
1300 WEST, TAYLORSVILLE, UTAH FROM SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL R-1- 20
                          TO SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL R-1- 10


       WHEREAS, the Taylorsville         City Council ( the " Council") met in regular session on
November 2, 2022, to consider, among other things, approving a Zoning Map Amendment from
 Single Family ( R- 1- 20)" to " Single Family ( R- 1- 10)" for the properties located at 5465; 5433;
and 5439 S. 1300 W.; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13. 35. 040, notice of a
public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was published on the city' s official website
on September 13, 2022; and


       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also published on the Utah Public Notice Website on September 13, 2022; and


       WHEREAS, the notice of a public hearing before the City' s Planning Commission was
also mailed   to   each affected   entity, as defined in Utah Code § 10- 9a- 103( 3),   on September 14,
2022; and;


         WHEREAS, the City' s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on September 27, 2022, during which the Planning Commission voted
7- 0 to recommend that the Council approve the proposed Zoning Map amendment; and

       WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on
November 2, 2022; and


       WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.




       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed Zoning Map amendment is hereby approved for the property described below:
