In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who appear and speak at a
County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy
or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

E. PRESENTATIONS TO WORLD WAR II VETERANS.
   Chair Harvey spoke of the importance of paying tribute to the Weber County veterans. He recognized
   Colonel Jon Eberlan, 75th Air Base Wing commander, various mayors and others present. Commissioner Ebert
   stated that those who serve and sacrifice in the Armed Forces for our country are very dear to him, that most
   people have no idea what they go through or their families. His grandfather served for 30 years.
   Commissioner Jenkins, who was the lead on this item, expressed thanks to the veterans for all that they have
   done for us and for the world. The commissioners passed out their handwritten letters of thanks and
   medallions to Gene England, Dale Gahn, Norberto Ramos, Dean Baddley, Casey Kunimara, Lester Bryan,
   Roland Schramm, Delton Stanger, William Lawler, Kenneth Mickelson, Robert Haws, Loren Kay, Ralph
   Crandall, Fred Roberts, William Hartman, Kay Johnson, Charles Child, John E. Richardson, Frank
   VanMeeteren and Jerald Senn. Some had brought memorabilia and shared experiences. Kim Dixon wrote
   the book, Weber County’s Greatest Generation, and presented each veteran with one and thanked them. The
   commissioners expressed their deepest gratitude to the veterans.

F. PUBLIC COMMENTS: Randall Winn, of North Ogden, said that from the many meetings he has attended the
   vast majority of the community is opposed to the gondola development on the mountains to the east of North
   Ogden. It will affect the use of their water and traffic and he hopes that if any of the commissioners would benefit
   financially from this that they would recuse themselves, and asked them to follow the will of the
   people. Commissioner Ebert said that the county had to give a management plan to the State but there is no
   movement on any level for the federal government to give tracts of federal land back to Utah or any state. It
   was just a recommendation to the State on management of roadless areas in national forest land in Weber
   County. Federal government makes the designation of roadless, not the County Commission or the State.
H. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. RESOLUTION ADOPTING AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT WITH UTAH DIVISION OF
       JUVENILE JUSTICE SERVICES (JJS) REGARDING THE CONSTRUCTION OF A FENCE ALONG DEPOT DRIVE,
       OGDEN – RESOLUTION 54-2018.
            Bill Ross, County Community Development Assistant Director, stated that the JJS owns property adjacent
            to the county’s newly constructed Depot Drive. The county is responsible to put in the fence, which is
            chain link along the east side of Depot Drive. This agreement allows JJS to participate in the project by
            adding black vinyl coating to the fence and paying the county $5,430 for that additional material.
            Commissioner Ebert moved to adopt Resolution 54-2018 adopting an Interlocal Cooperative Agreement
            with the Utah Division of Juvenile Justice Services regarding the construction of a fence along Depot
            Drive in Ogden; Commissioner Jenkins seconded.
            Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Jenkins – aye; Chair Harvey – aye
      2. CONTRACT WITH STOR & MORE LLC REGARDING CONSTRUCTION OF A FENCE ALONG DEPOT DRIVE, OGDEN.

     2. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER AND/OR TAKE ACTION ON A REQUEST TO VACATE A 14-FOOT PUBLIC
        UTILITY EASEMENT LOCATED ON LOT 23 OF WEST HILLS SUBDIVISION.

         Felix Lleverino, of the County Planning Division, stated that upon reviewing the applicant’s request to
         build a storage building in his backyard a public utility easement was found. The applicant chose to go
         through the vacation process and vacate the easement which is about 145 feet back from the property line.
         County Engineering made a site visit and found no drainage lines along there. The applicant provided
         letters from Rocky Mountain Power, Dominion Energy, Taylor West Weber Water, and Century Link
         stating that they are fine with abandoning all rights across the easement. Staff recommends approval of
         the item based on the conditions and findings stated on the report. The applicant had no comment.
