2. 23-494 Administration presenting a review of City events for 2023

Attachments: Comm Events Overview 2023.pdf

Katie Atkinson, City Event Coordinator, provided a review of the Sandy City events that
occurred in 2023. The large city events include the following: July 4th Parade, Balloon
Festival, Heritage Festival, Trunk or Treat and Movies in the Park. Attendance estimates
at these events range from 5,000 to 40,000 attendees at the larger city events. The city's
smaller events included food truck nights, yoga in the park, Historic Sandy Barbecue,
Healing Fields, Children's Entrepeneurs Market, Animal Services Night and Healthy
Sandy Fair. Council thanked Ms. Atkinson for her coordination and planning of all of
these community events and expressed their appreciation.

3. 23-483 First Reading: Council Members Sharkey, Stroud, and D'Sousa
recommending the council consider the adoption of an ordinance on
equitable participation of city officials in events.
Sponsors: Sharkey, D'Sousa and Stroud
Attachments: Synopsis of Ordinance on Equitable Participation in Events.pdf
Council Members Sharkey, Stroud, and D'Sousa presented a first reading of a proposal to
formalize an equitable process of participation by elected officials at city events. It has
been a long standing practice for our city officials to participate and attend events of the
city and city partners. Council discussed the importance of building relationships and
engagement with our community and business partners. The Council expressed support
for this long standing practice and for formalizing this practice in writing. Council
questions and discussion continued. Shane Pace and Kim Bell with Administration
spoke about the development of a policy and the management of this process. Council is
supportive of moving forward to a Second Reading of the proposal to formalize the
equitable process of participation in city events.

City Council Meeting Minutes December 12, 2023

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report
Dustin Fratto, Council Director: The December 19, 2023 Council meeting is the final
meeting for the year. Next week the Council will receive presentations on the Point
development and the management consulting firm conducting the study on the Fire
Department. We will also have a special recognition for Council Member Scott Earl. The
Oath of Office Ceremony is on January 2, 2024.

Council Member Business
Council Member Houseman spoke about the South Valley Chamber and acknowledged
the incredible job the Chamber is doing. The Chamber coordinates meaningful and
educational workshops and events all year long. She also spoke of an upcoming stem
expo which will be held in Sandy City in January. Our local charter school, Beehive
Academy will be presenting at the stem expo. The Beehive Academy recently conducted
a diversity study of its school community. Their school's student and staff community
represents 48 countries, 27 different states, 82 cultural identities and speak 60 different
languages.
Council Member Robinson provided an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. The Mountain
West Ballet's production of the Nutcracker has sold out all shows. The performances will
take place at Mt. Jordan Middle School. He also acknowledged the staff of Mountain
West Ballet for their dedication to the ballet, their students and the community.
Council Member Brooke D'Sousa thanked the Public Works department for their help to
upgrade a crosswalk located at 2220 East and Wasatch Blvd. The upgraded crosswalk is
now a pedestrian crossing with flashing lights to alert drivers of people in the crosswalk.
Thank you for your responsiveness in addressing residents' concerns.
Council Member Ryan Mecham acknowledged Council Member Scott Earl for his many
years of service to Sandy City. He has always appreciated working with him and it has
been a great pleasure working with you over these many years.
Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an updated from the ULCT Legislative Policy
Committee. She spoke about upcoming legislative issues that include housing
affordability and the availability of starter homes. She thanked Mike Gladbach and Craig
Smith from Public Works for their help addressing a recent issue.
Council Member Alison Stroud recently attended a ribbon cutting for a new small
business in Sandy City. Body Fit is a small gym located in the Quarry Bend shopping
center. She thanked the Public Works staff for their help with a recent issue. And she
thanked and acknowledged the Fire Department crew for their response to an emergency
call at her elementary school.

Mayor's Report
Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor, thanked Council Member Scott Earl for his service to Sandy
City and acknowledged the many years they have worked together. Scott is an amazing
friend and colleague. Thank you for everything you have done for the City. She thanked all
involved for their coordination of the opening of the MVP Shelter in Sandy City. She also
thanked all the employees for attending the employee holiday luncheon last week.
