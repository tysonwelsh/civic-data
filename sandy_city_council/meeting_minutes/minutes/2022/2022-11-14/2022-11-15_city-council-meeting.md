2. 22-420 Council Member Mecham presenting a Sister Cities Resolution to the
Mayor as part of his presentation on his Sister Cities trip to Riesa,
Germany
Council Member Ryan Mecham, on behalf of the Sandy City Council, presented Mayor
Monica Zoltanski with a Resolution of Recognition commemorating the Twentieth
Anniversary of the Sister City Relationship between Sandy City, Utah and Riesa,
Germany. Mayor Zoltanski thanked Council Member Mecham and the Council for their
presentation. She spoke about the long standing relationship between the two cities and
thanked Charlotte Jordan for her service on behalf of the Sister City program.

3. CA04182022 Proposed Amendments Related to Water Efficient Landscaping

-0006307 Standards:
(CC-WS) Amend Title 21, Chapter 25, Landscaping Standards, and Chapter 37,
Definitions, of the Sandy Municipal Code
Amend portions of the Sandy City Standard Specifications and Details for
Municipal Construction
Attachments: Staff Report Waterwise Landscape Code Amendments

Exhibit A - Ordinance Title 21 Ch 25 (Redlined)
Exhibit B - Ordinance Title 21 Ch 25 (Clean Version)
Exhibit C - CUWCD Water Efficient Landscaping Standards
Exhibit D - PC Memo for Standard Specifications
Exhibit E - Standard Specs and Details Updates
Melissa Anderson, with Community Development, presented an overview of the proposed
amendments to Title 21 Chapter 25, Landscaping Standards of the Sandy Municipal
Code and portions of the Sandy City Standard Specifications & Details for Municipal
Construction. Ms. Anderson provided background information and explained that the
purpose of these amendments was to strengthen current landscaping requirements and
incorporate new ones that will bring the city's codes in line with more water efficient
landscaping standards. The Planning Commission has forwarded a positive
recommendation to the Council. Staff also recommends approval of the amendments.
Council questions and comments followed. This item is scheduled for the December 6th
City Council meeting for Council consideration and action.
