                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 24-40

  A RESOLUTION APPROVING A CHANGE ORDER TO PO CONTRACT 170619
  ADDING THE FIVE RESTROOMS THAT ARE IN THE CIVIC CENTER TO THE
                  CIVIC CENTER REMODEL PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

       WHEREAS, the five restrooms that are in the Civic Center were not included in the
Civic Center Remodel Project; and

        WHEREAS, the Recreation & Facilities Director would like to include the remodeling of
the five restrooms into the project; and

       WHEREAS, by adding the five restrooms to the project the Civic Center would not open
and then close again because of remodeling; and

        WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve $80,000.00 as an added expense
in a future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The City Council approves a Change Order to PO Contract 170619 adding the five
          restrooms that are in the Civic Center to the Civic Center Remodel Project.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 2nd day of
April, 2024 by the following vote:
