             Ordinance No. 2024-16

             AN ORDINANCE ENACTING TITLE 2, CHAPTER 7, AND MOVING AND AMENDING
             SECTIONS 2-4-13, 2-4-14, AND 2-4-15 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF PARK CITY
             TO CREATE A CONSOLIDATED CODE CHAPTER GOVERNING PARK CITY’S
             BOARDS, COMMITTEES, AND COMMISSIONS

             WHEREAS, Park City has created boards, committees, and commissions over time
             through the adoption of resolutions and ordinances.

             WHEREAS, the rules governing those boards, committees, and commissions differ and
             are not found in a consolidated location.

             WHEREAS, the City Council finds that consolidating the rules for boards, committees,
             and commissions into one location and providing more uniform rules for governance will
             aid in transparency and administration.

             BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF PARK CITY, UTAH, THAT:

             Section 1. Title 2, Chapter 7, of the Municipal Code of Park City is hereby enacted to
             read as shown in Exhibit A. Sections 2-4-13, 2-4-14, and 2-4-15 of the Municipal Code
             of Park City are hereby moved into Title 2, Chapter 7, and amended to read as shown in
             Exhibit A.

             Section 2. This ordinance supersedes any conflicting provisions of previously adopted
             ordinances and resolutions.

             Section 3. This ordinance will be effective on the day of publication.

             PASSED AND ADOPTED this 5th day of September, 2024.

       5     2-7-2 City Boards and Commissions Named
       6         A . For the purposes of this chapter, the term “City board” or “board” means the following City
       7             boards, commissions, and committees:
       8             1. Board of Appeals
       9             2. Citizens Open Space Advisory Committee
      10             3. Deed Restriction Advisory Committee
      11             4. Library Board
      12             5. Neighborhood First Street Committee
      13             6. Nonprofit Services Advisory Committee
      14             7. Police Complaint Review Committee
      15             8. Public Art Advisory Board
      16             9. Recreation Advisory Board
      17             10. Special Event Advisory Committee

      19             1. Appeal Panel (Park City Code Title 15, Chapter 1, Section 18)
      20             2. Board of Adjustment (Park City Code Title 15, Chapter 10)
      21             3. Historic Preservation Board (Park City Code Title 15, Chapter 11)
      22             4. Planning Commission (Park City Code Title 15, Chapter 12)
      23
      24     2-7-3 Conflicts of Law
      25     If a provision of this chapter conflicts with a federal, state, or local law applicable to a particular City
      26     board, such law shall supersede the conflicting provision. If rules for a specific board, commission, or
      27     committee found in this chapter conflict with the common rules, the specific rules will control. The
      28     contents of this chapter will supersede any conflicting resolution or policy established for any specific
      29     board by the board.
      30
      31     2-7-4 Appointment; Advice and Consent
      32         A. Appointments of City board members shall be made by the Mayor with the advice and consent
      33             of the City Council unless otherwise provided by this code or other law regarding a particular
      34             City board. City boards should represent a cross-section of the City. Individuals should serve on
      35             only one board at a time, thus allowing more people to serve on boards.
      36         B. Employees of Park City Municipal Corporation are not eligible for membership on a City board
      37             unless an exception in this code or other law applicable to a particular board allows such
      38             appointments.
      39         C. Spouses and family members of City employees may be considered for appointment to a City
      40             board when the board is not advisory to the employee’s department or job responsibilities.
      41
      42     2-7-5 Term
      43         A. Member appointments to City boards will be made for three-year terms beginning on July 1 and
      44             expiring on June 30, except when:
      45             1. A provision of state law or this code applicable to a particular board specifies a different
      46                 term; or
      47             2. An appointment is to fill a vacancy during an unexpired term, in which case, the
      48                 appointment shall be for the unexpired term.

      11     Board members shall take the oath of office required by law for City officers and file the oath with the

      14     2-7-8 Conflicts of Interest
      15     City board members are subject to the City’s Conflict of Interest Code, Title 3, Chapter 2, and the
      16     Municipal Officers’ and Employees’ Ethics Act, Title 10, Chapter 3, Part 13 of the Utah Code, or its
      17     successor.
      18
      19     2-7-9 Removal from Office
      20         A. Any City board member may be removed from office by the Mayor with the advice and consent
      21             of Council for cause prior to the expiration of the term for which the member was appointed.
      22             For the purposes of this section, “cause” includes willful misconduct or intentional failure to
      23             perform stated duties.
      24         B. Any City board member failing to attend two board meetings without advance notice or four
      25             total meetings in one calendar year may be removed by the Mayor with the advice and consent
      26             of Council prior to the expiration of the term for which the member was appointed.
      27         C. If another section of the Park City Code conflicts with the procedures in this section, this section
      28             will control for board members.
      29
      30     2-7-10 Compliance with the Open and Public Meetings Act
      31     Every meeting of a City board shall conform with the Open and Public Meetings Act, as set forth in Title
      32     52, Chapter 4 of the Utah Code or its successor, including, the requirements for public notice, open
      33     meetings, closed meetings, meeting minutes, and electronic meetings.
      34
      35     2-7-11 Board Meetings
      36         A. Each City board shall convene regular meetings, the frequency of which shall be determined by
      37             the board except as otherwise provided by rules set forth in this code applicable to a particular
      38             board.
      39         B. Special meetings may be ordered by a majority of the board or the board chair.
      40         C. Except as otherwise provided by law, City boards may establish their own policies and rules of
      41             procedure governing the board and the proper conduct of its meetings provided that those
      42             policies and procedures do not conflict with the provisions of this Chapter.
      43
      44     2-7-12 Election of Board Officers
      45     Each City board shall select one of its members as chair and another member as vice-chair who shall
      46     perform the duties of the chair during the absence or disability of the chair. These positions shall be for
      47     one-year terms.

       1     2-7-13 Quorum and Board Action
       2         A. A quorum is a majority of members who have been appointed to and are currently serving on a
       3             particular City board.
       4         B. A City board may hold a meeting and take action only when a quorum is present.

       6     2-7-14 Board of Appeals
       7         A. Purpose and Authority. The Board of Appeals shall hear all appeals filed according to the
       8            International Building Code, Chapter 5 of the Abatement of Dangerous Buildings Code for Park
       9            City, as adopted by Title 11, Chapter 6 of the Park City Code, and Chapter 12 of the Housing
      10            Code of Park City, as adopted by Title 11, Chapter 5 of the Park City Code. Any appeal made to
      11            the Board of Appeals pursuant to the International Building Code shall be filed within 30 days of
      12            approval or denial of the permit. The time for appeal of actions under the Abatement of
      13            Dangerous Buildings Code and the Housing Code shall be as specified in each of those respective
      14            codes, but in no instance longer than 30 days. All decisions and findings of the Board of Appeals
      15            will be in writing to the Chief Building Official with a copy provided to the appellant.
      16         B. Organization. The Board of Appeals consists of three members who are qualified by experience
      17            and training to pass upon matters pertaining to building construction. The Chief Building Official
      18            shall be an ex officio member and shall act as Secretary of the Board of Appeals.
      19         C. Rules and Regulations. The Board of Appeals shall adopt reasonable rules and regulations for
      20            conducting its investigations. A copy of all rules and regulations adopted by the Board of
      21            Appeals shall be delivered to the Chief Building Official who will make them available to the
      22            public without cost.

      24     2-7-15 Citizens Open Space Advisory Committee
      25         A. Purpose and Authority. The Citizens Open Space Advisory Committee will:
      26            1. Investigate and recommend a strategy for the acquisition of open space, including
      27                 recommending specific parcels for acquisition and prioritizing available lands for acquisition;
      28                 and
      29            2. Recommend improvements to open space to protect critical natural amenities and provide
      30                 safe public access.
      31         B. Organization. Members may be appointed when dedicated open space funding is available. The
      32            committee will disband when dedicated open space funding is exhausted.
      33
      34     2-7-16 Deed Restriction Advisory Committee
      35         A. Purpose and Authority. The Deed Restriction Advisory Committee will make recommendations
      36            to the City Manager about the acquisition of deed restrictions pursuant to the Live Park City –
      37            Lite Deed Restriction Program using the following criteria:
      38            1. Supports and maintains a permanent, year-round resident population that grows a diverse
      39                 community where a wide range of demographics, economics, occupations, and family
      40                 household sizes are served.
      41            2. Consistent with housing policies adopted by the Park City Municipal Code for the acquisition
      42                 of deed restrictions.
      43            3. Demonstrated demand exists within the resident housing market for the type of residential
      44                 product (studio, flat, townhome, duplex, single family, etc.) that is to be deed restricted.
      45            4. Fulfills a demonstrated need within a defined segment (for rent, sale, owner occupied, etc.).
      46            5. Demonstrates a quantifiable return on investment based upon appraisals.

       9     2-7-17 Library Board
      10         A. Purpose and Authority. The Library Board of Directors may with the approval of the governing
      11            body have control of the expenditure of the Library Account, of construction, lease or sale of
      12            Park City Library buildings and land, and of the operation of the Library. The Library Board will
      13            be responsible for the maintenance and care of the Library and will establish policies for its
      14            operation.
      15         B. Organization. The Library Board will consist of not less than five members and not more than
      16            nine members. Members must be Park City residents. Not more than one member of City
      17            Council may be, at any one time, a member of the Library Board. Directors shall serve without
      18            compensation, but their actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their
      19            official duties may be paid from Library funds.
      20         C. Rules and Regulations. The Library Board of Directors shall make, amend and revoke rules and
      21            regulations, not inconsistent with the law, for the governing of the Library. The Library shall be
      22            free to the use of the inhabitants of Park City, subject to the rules and regulations adopted by
      23            the Board. The Board may exclude from the use of the Library any and all persons who shall
      24            willfully violate such rules. The Board may extend the privileges and use of the Library to
      25            persons residing outside of the City upon such terms and conditions as it may prescribe by its
      26            regulations.
      27         D. Annual Reports. The Library Board of Directors shall make an annual report to the governing
      28            body of the City on the condition and operation of the library, including a financial statement.
      29            Such a report may be in the form of budget documents submitted each year to the City Council.
      30            The Directors shall also provide for the keeping of such records as shall be required by the Utah
      31            State Library in its request for an annual report from the public libraries, and shall submit such
      32            an annual report to the State Library.
      33         E. Personnel. The Library Board of Directors shall appoint a competent person as Librarian to have
      34            immediate charge of the Library with such duties and compensation for the Librarian’s services
      35            as it shall fix and determine. The Librarian shall act as the executive officer for the Library Board.
      36            The Board shall appoint, upon the recommendation of the Librarian, other personnel as needed.
      37            The Board may delegate the authority of this section to the City Manager and provide for the
      38            inclusion of Library personnel in the City personnel system pursuant to section 10-3-1104 of the
      39            Utah Code, or its successor.
      40         F. Donations. Any person desiring to make donations of money, personal property, or real estate
      41            for the benefit of the Library shall have the right to vest the title to the money, personal
      42            property or real estate so donated, in trust for the benefit of the Library. All monetary donations
      43            shall be held in the City treasury to the credit of the Library account, and may not be used for
      44            any purpose except that of the Library. These funds shall be drawn upon by the authorized
      45            officers of the City only upon presentation of the properly authenticated vouchers or purchase
      46            order of the Board.

       1     2-7-18 Neighborhoods First - Street Committee
       2         A. Purpose and Authority. The Neighborhoods First - Streets Committee implements portions of
       3            the Neighborhoods First - Streets Program, which aims to enhance neighborhood livability and
       4            safety by reducing speeding traffic with traffic calming solutions. By measuring the impacts of
       5            these solutions with the help of clear evidence and documented processes, the Neighborhoods
       6            First - Streets Committee works to improve compliance with posted speed limits, stop signs, and
       7            other traffic control devices.
       8         B. Organization.
       9            1. The Neighborhoods First - Streets Committee will be made up of a representative from each
      10                 of the following:
      11                 i. The Park City Engineering Department,
      12                 ii. The Park City Police Department,
      13                 iii. The Park City Public Works Department,
      14                 iv. The Park City Fire Department,
      15                 v. The Park City Transportation Department,
      16                 vi. A Park City Resident Advocate,
      17                 vii. A Park City Resident, and
      18                 viii. Summit County Government Representative.
      19            2. Selection Process. All of the representatives will be selected by the department or
      20                 organization they are representing except the Park City Resident Advocate will be selected
      21                 by the City Manager and the Park City Resident will be appointed by the Mayor with the
      22                 advice and consent of the City Council.
      23
      24     2-7-19 Nonprofit Services Advisory Committee
      25         A. Purpose and Authority. The Nonprofit Services Advisory Committee provides recommendations
      26            to the City Council on the Public Service Contract and Emergent Community Needs Grant
      27            programs, which are established in the City’s Budget Policies & Objectives. The Committee’s
      28            duties include:
      29            1. Evaluating applications;
      30            2. Interviewing applicants; and
      31            3. Making award recommendations to the City Council.
      32         B. Organization.
      33            1. The Nonprofit Services Advisory Committee will consist of not less than five members and
      34                not more than seven members.
      35            2. All members must live, work, volunteer, own property, or own a business within Park City
      36                municipal boundaries.
      37            3. A majority of members must have their primary residence within Park City municipal
      38                boundaries.
      39            4. There should be priority for members who have experience with nonprofit organizations or
      40                who have experience delivering or accessing nonprofit services.
      41         C. Conflict of Interest. Members may not be employed by or serve on the board of a nonprofit
      42            organization that applies for or is likely to apply for grant funding through PCMC.

      46                   i. further investigative action or review by the Chief of Police to clarify and/or to expand
