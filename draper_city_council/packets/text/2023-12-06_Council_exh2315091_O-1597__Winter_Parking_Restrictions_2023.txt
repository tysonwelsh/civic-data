                                 ORDINANCE 1597



          AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING THE
                 REGULATIONS FOR WINTER STREET PARKING.


       WHEREAS, the Draper City Council desires to adopt regulations to address the
impacts on snowplowing city streets when vehicles or trailers are parked on city
streets during winter months; and

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council desires to amend the city code to clarify
parking restrictions on city streets during winter months to improve the safety of
residents and snowplow drivers.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Ordinance. The City Council hereby amends 14-5-020: Winter
Parking as shown in Exhibit A.

       Section 2. Effective Date.      This Ordinance shall become effective
immediately upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is
closer to the date of final passage.

PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON DECEMBER 6, 2023.

14-5-020: WINTER PARKING :
No person shall park or leave a vehicle or trailer on any highway between the hours
of midnight and 6:00 AM, or while snow is on the street, from November 1 through
April 30. Vehicles or trailers that are parked on a highway in violation of the
provisions of this section may be removed at the owner's expense. (Ord. 1421, 5-
19-2020)
