CUP2020-000028 – Jeanine Pettit is requesting Conditional Use approval to allow for (6) horses, (2) goats, 
and (6) chickens on the subject property for private use only.  Acreage: 4.44 acres. Location: 7771 West 
Canyon Road. Zone: FR-5.  Planner:  Jim Nakamura (Motion/Voting) 
 
The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting 
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.  
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Jim Nakamura provided an analysis of the Staff 
Report. 
 
Commissioner Vance asked if the property is within city limits of Herriman or  Unincorporated Salt Lake 
County and if County rules prevail. Mr. Nakamura  confirmed Salt Lake County rules do prevail.

Commissioner Cohen asked if CC&R’s do not permit anything but horses, but ordinances for the zone do 
allow what the applicant is asking for, does County ordinance override the CC&R’s. Mr. Nakamura said 
the ordinance does not supersede bylaws, but that does not mean the property is not affected by the bylaws 
they lie in. Mr. Shaw said purview for the planning commission is to determine if the ordinances have been 
met and then state law requires approval of a land use application. Once approval given, it is between the 
HOA and property owner as to contract requirements between them. If HOA thinks there is a breach of a 
contract and CC&R’s violated, HOA could take legal action to enforce the contract. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to open the public meeting, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion. 
Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present). 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Citizen 
Name: Randy Crane 
Address: 13682 South Mount Shaggy Drive 
Comments: Mr. Crane confirmed he lives west of the applicant. Process is in place in the county to 
readdress FR zoning, based upon environmental and health issues. Asked this application to be put on hold 
until the county updates, seems out of place to approve the conditional use permit until ordinance updated. 
Addressed all issues in email. Individual is in violat ion of CC&R’s and if approved may become a legal 
issue. Have enforced the rules, filed suit, and won. Ask for hold for updated ordinance. 
 
Speaker # 2: Citizen 
Name: Glenn Trendler 
Address: 7858 West Canyon Road  
Comments: Mr. Trendler confirmed he lives across the street from the applicant. He said he is in favor, 
Mr. Shaw stated as long as meets ordinance provisions, application should be granted. The CC&R issue is 
between homeowner and association. Application should be analyzed under today’s standard. 
 
Commissioner Cohen motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Elieson seconded that motion. 
Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present). 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED 
Commissioners and counsel had a brief discussion regarding current  ordinance and state law in place at 
time of application.  
 
Motion: To approve Conditional Use application # CUP2020-000028, with staff recommendations met. 
Motion by: Commissioner Elieson 
2nd by: Commissioner Vance 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
