Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
                               October 13, 2020

The regular meeting of the Washington County Planning Commission was conducted on Tuesday,
October 13, 2020 from the Commission Chambers in the Washington County Administration
Building, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Also, via electronic means through Zoom.
Chairman Kevin Jones opened the meeting at 1:30 p.m. He led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Chairman Jones explained how the meeting would be conducted. He reminded the participants that
although this is a public meeting, it would only be open for public comments when so declared.

Commissioners present: Brandon Anderson, Dan Briggs (Alternate acting as a regular voting
member), Shayne Durrant, Slade Hughes, Mark Owens, and Jon Walter.

Excused: Reed Scow (Alternate) and Doug Solstad.

County staff present: Scott Messel, Community Development Director; Steven Scott, Deputy
Attorney; Doreen Bowers-Irons, Administrative Assistant; Cheyenne Bentley, Deputy Clerk at
host location; Todd Edwards, Engineer; Kurt Gardner, Building Official; Kim Hafen, County
Clerk.


1.     CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT. Review a conditional use permit request for a 60-foot
tall communication tower on Parcel 7177-A-4-NW located at 287 E. Veyo Resort Road in Veyo.
The parcel is zoned A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre minimum lot size). The applicant is InfoWest
Communications/Opulent Veyo, LLC/Ross Cape, agent.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation that included a sit plan and layout of the project.
He reported the applicant, InfoWest Communications, is seeking approval to construct an
approximately 60’ tall steel lattice tower on a portion of Parcel 7177-A-4-NW located at 287 E.
Veyo Resort Rd. in Veyo. The parcel is zoned A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre minimum lot size). The
proposed tower will provide internet service for the Veyo Pool Resort. Currently, there is no
reliable service to the area. The tower will also help provide service to other adjacent properties
who may request such service. The site will include a 10’ x 10’ concrete pad with a 4’ x 4’
triangular tower and cabinet. The site will be entirely fenced with chain-link. The height of the
tower is required due to the topography of the area. The applicants are unable to co-locate on the
existing Verizon tower in the area because internet and cell services are on a different frequency.

Recommendation:

Staff has reviewed the requested conditional use permit application for the 60’ tall
telecommunication tower to be located on Parcel 7177-A-4-NS in the A-10 (Agricultural 10 acre
minimum lot size) zone and would recommend the commission approve the request based on the
following findings and conditions:

   1. The conditional use was properly noticed.

Chairman Jones announced this was his business for 31 years and he had some questions. He
commented on colocation and asked for clarification as to why they could not collocate.

Ross Cape, applicant explained the different frequencies that make it a challenge to locate on an
existing cell tower. He also pointed out in detail the cost of a collocating on a tower. He noted
those costs are prohibitive to small businesses like theirs. They approached several property
owners in the area to locate the tower on their property, including the property where the Verizon
tower is currently located on but were unable to come to an agreement. This property owner was
willing to work with us.

Commissioner Owens asked besides the pool area would others benefit from the tower. Mr. Cape
answered they would. He explained that they chose the property for the higher elevation. The
recently acquired AWI Internet and the frequency they were using will not be used going forward.
Our frequency is “line of site” so, if they do not get the location, they will lose approximately 80
customers.

A short discussion occurred regarding the areas served by InfoWest and the services they provide.
Mr. Cape pointed out there is an old ham radio antenna on the proposed site that would be removed
and the new tower put in its place.

2.    PUBLIC HEARING/SUBDIVISION PRELIMINARY PLAT. Review and hold a public
hearing for a minor 2-lot subdivision preliminary plat request for Parcel 8183-E-MW located at
approximately 31 South 300 West in Veyo. The parcel is located in the RA-1 (Residential
Agricultural 1 acre minimum lot size) zone. The applicants are Pam and Jessie Hunt.

Mr. Messel provided a power point presentation of the project that included a map of the location.
He reported the applicants own Parcel 8183-E-NW, which is located at approximately 31 South
300 West in Veyo. The parcel is approximately 10 acres in size. The Hunt’s have submitted a
preliminary plat application. They are proposing to divide the subject parcel into two lots that run
north and south. Both lots will be approximately 4.65 acres in size. 100 South will be extended
along the north side of the property. This road extension will provide access to the proposed Lot
2. The Hunt’s will dedicate 25’ on the north of the property for the half width of the street cross
section standard. The Hunt’s will also dedicate 25’ on the west side of the property for the half
width of 300 West. The applicants are requesting a variation from the required road improvements.
Washington County Code 11-6-E states, “Each lot shall abut on a street shown on the subdivision
plat or on an existing publicly dedicated street or on a street which has become public by right of
use”. The street must be improved to county standards as stated in Title 11-5-C Improvement
Standards: “All streets within the County shall be improved in accordance with the standards,
rules and regulations adopted by the County Commission”. The applicant is requesting a variation
from the road improvement standards. Washington County Code 11-3-M states, “Where unusual
topographic or other exceptional conditions exist, the county commission may vary the
requirements of this title after receiving the recommendations of the planning commission;
provided, that such variations will not substantially impair the intent of this title.” The applicant
is asking for the County Commission to waive the access requirements of Title 11-5-C
Improvement Standards. Other than the request for approval of a variation from the standards, the
proposed preliminary plat application is complete and meets the requirements of Title 11-4-1
Preliminary Plat.
