1.       A discussion regarding the Council’s budget priorities for FY 2021-2022. (21-015)
         (0:09:27)

Cliff Strachan, Council Executive Director, presented. Mr. Strachan reviewed the priorities
which the Council had discussed at a March 2020 retreat just before the start of the pandemic.
Mr. Strachan also reviewed the budget priorities as presented by various City departments over
the last several months, as well as potential budget appropriations or opportunities.

The Council began into their discussion of the issue, with Council Chair David Sewell first
asking Mayor Michelle Kaufusi and CAO Wayne Parker what would most assist them in
preparing their budget to present to the Council before April 20. Mayor Kaufusi expressed that
her number one goal was to return employee benefits and department budgets back to their levels
pre-pandemic. Mr. Parker noted that more general direction would be helpful, as far as projects
or items that have resonated with the Council as priorities, rather than programming specific line
items. Mr. Parker noted that Provo was projected to receive upwards of $30 million through the
American Rescue Plan Act in the coming couple of years. Given these responses, Mr. Sewell
suggested to the Council that they focus on the ongoing budget priorities, since the deadline for

Mr. Sewell thanked the Council for their participation and for keeping the discussion at a high
level. He indicated that Council Leadership would continue to discuss and refine these priorities.
Mayor Kaufusi thanked the Council for their support of restoring benefits and training budgets
for staff to pre-COVID levels. She acknowledged that it would be a tight budget once those
restorations were factored in. Mr. Parker said the Administration would continue to review and
discuss the budget before presenting it to the Council on April 20. He suggested they would try
to align their budget as best they could with the Council’s stated priorities. Presentation only.
