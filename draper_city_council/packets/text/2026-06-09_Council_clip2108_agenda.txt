         An ordinance amending the Official Zoning Map of Draper City for

         known as the Nielsen Zoning Map Amendment. Staff report by Todd

     6.b Public Hearing: Ordinances #1719 and #1720
         Ordinances of Draper City amending a Master Area Plan and the text of the
         Land Use and Development Code of Draper City Municipal Code Section 9-
         14-110, Exhibit 9-14-1, regarding the Veranda West Master Area Plan located
         at approximately 13708 South 600 West. Staff report by Todd Taylor.

     6.c Public Hearing: Ordinances #1724 and #1725
         An ordinance amending the official Land Use Map of Draper City and an

         - Highland boundary adjustment #2 Zoning Map Amendment. Staff report
         by Todd Draper.
