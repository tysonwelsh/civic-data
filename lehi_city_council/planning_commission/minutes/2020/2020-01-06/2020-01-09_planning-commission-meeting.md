4. Regular Agenda

4.1 Public hearing and recommendation of Dan Ford’s request for review of R-1-22
(residential/agriculture) zoning on the Taylor Meadows Annexation, approximately 30-acres
of property located at approximately 1600 South 500 West.

    Jacob presented information on agenda item.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing on agenda item 4.1.

    The applicant, Dan Ford who is representing Taylor Legal Holdings, was present and
    addressed the Commission.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing on agenda item 4.1.

    Commissioner Hemmert re-opened the public hearing on agenda item 4.1.

    Todd Reeves stated there are 26 running wells on the property and wondered if there were
    any issues with the wells. He had questions with entry and exits points with traffic on the
    property. Commissioner Hemmert responded that they were only looking at the proposed
    zoning on the annexed property.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing on agenda item 4.1.

    The applicant Dan Ford addressed Todd Reeves concerns. He stated the roads were stubbed
    for future connectivity. He stated they have also been working with City Engineer Lorin
    Powell regarding the water issues for future development.


Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to approve Dan Ford’s request for review of R-1-22
(residential/agriculture) zoning on the Taylor Meadows Annexation, approximately 30-acres of
property located at approximately 1600 South 500 West; including all DRC comments and based
on the findings stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

4.2 Public hearing and consideration of Jon O’Keefe’s request for conditional use approval
of the Top-Notch Trailer Repair site plan located in an existing building at 687 East State
Street in a Mixed Use zone.

    Jacob presented information on agenda item.

    The applicant was present but did not wish to add anything.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing for agenda item 4.2.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing for agenda item 4.2.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked if the Commission wished to discuss the item. Commissioner
    Ellis stated the only issue he saw was visual aspects. Commissioner Everett stated the DRC
    comments addressed that. Commissioner Hemmert stated the motion could include language
    to address that issue for conditional use. Commissioner Nielsen stated that a screen could be
    required. Commissioner Ellis asked if it was noted in the site plan. Commissioner Peterson
    asked if screening was required along the road and that it could be noted as part of the
    development code.

Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to approve Top-Notch Trailer Repair site plan located in an
existing building at 687 East State Street in a Mixed Use zone, with the conditions that the
storage, materials, and parts must located within a building or screened from view. The parking
and drive aisles must always remain open to provide ingress and egress for emergency vehicles
and clients. The parking area needs to be restriped to include nine by eighteen stalls. The motion
included the findings stated in the report. Commissioner Everett seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Ellis, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Peterson, yes;
Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes;
Commissioner Hereth, yes. The motion passed unanimously.

    Jacob presented information on the agenda item.
