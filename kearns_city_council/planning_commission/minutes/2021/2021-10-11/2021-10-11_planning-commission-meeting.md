                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 5:38 p.m.

REZ2021-000430 - Carolyn and Jose Rico are requesting a recommendation for approval to rezone
property from the R-1-6 (Single-Family Residential) zone to the C-2 (Commercial) zone. Acreage: .19
acre. Location: 5490 South 4015 West. Planner: Molly Gaughran (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed Rezone after taking comments from the public during
the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s rules of
conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three minutes
per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning and Development Planner Molly Gaughran
provided an analysis of the staff report.

Mr. Bracken went over the law on rezones. Commissioner Nelson asked if they would have to abide by C-
2 zones with parking and easements. Mr. Bracken said they would be treated just like any other C-2 zones
and would no longer be residential.

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Carolyn Rico
Address: Not provided
Comments: Ms. Rico said she was hoping for commercial to keep the barbershop and not a good place for
residential.

Commissioner Wellman asked for business plans for the lot. Ms. Rico said not living there and the person
living there has a small business. Since the 90’s when they lived there, they had a small business. Hoping
to lease it out. Tenants intend to stay. Commissioner Taylor asked if changed to commercial, changes all
rules in leasing and has that been addressed. Ms. Rico said hasn’t looked into the necessary things to do is
to go commercial. Commissioner Taylor said assuming the tenant has a business license. Ms. Rico said
would probably want to make offices at some point.

Commissioner Taylor said the community council would have a negative recommendation.

Commissioner Nelson motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Hatch seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED

No one from the public present to speak.

Commissioner Hatch motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Koester seconded that motion.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING CLOSED

Commissioners had a brief discussion the regarding applicants knowledge and parking, insufficient plans,
general plan and uses, property values, similar Commercial use, increased traffic, and potential uses.
