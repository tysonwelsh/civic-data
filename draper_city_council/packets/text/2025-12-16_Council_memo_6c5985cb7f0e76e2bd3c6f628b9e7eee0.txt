MEMO
To: City Council
From: Clint Smith, Fire Chief
Date: 2025-12-16
Re: Approve Ordinance #1695


Comments:


In compliance with Utah 2026 HB48, our responsibility as a local entity is to support the
development and implementation of the statewide Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) map
by ensuring accurate identification of local risk areas where development and wildland
fuels intersect. This includes providing relevant local data, reviewing mapped boundaries,
and coordinating with state agencies to verify that the WUI designations reflect on-the-
ground conditions. By fulfilling these responsibilities, we help ensure the WUI map is a
reliable tool for applying required fire-resilience standards, guiding mitigation planning,
informing residents of their wildfire risk, and strengthening overall community protection
as mandated by the legislation.
