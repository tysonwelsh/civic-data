D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: The 2024 F-16 TCG World Wide Review was held recently at
   the Ogden Eccles Conference Center, and it took the entire County to make it happen in the middle of the
   remodel at Ogden Eccles Conference Center. This is one of the largest shows that happens in Weber County,
   with so many people attending that we fill hotels in Box Elder and Brigham City all the way down to north Salt
   Lake. As a young boy I would watch all kinds of aircraft, especially F-4 Phantoms, come in on final approach to
   Hill AFB. Almost 50 years ago I saw the sleek, sporty new F-16 arrive at Hill AFB for the first time. Along
   with it all these ‘mom and pop’ shops and businesses such as Peterson Fabricators sprang up to make some of the
   over 280k different parts that goes into an F-16. This event is held for those businesses each year. I want to give
   a special shout out to Marty Smith, our Culture, Parks, and Recreation Director, Duncan Olsen, our Golden Spike
   Event Center Director, and Kassi Bybee, our OECC and Peery’s Egyptian Theater Director for hosting an
   absolutely phenomenal event and to the Sheriff’s Department for the wonderful job they did to make the event
   very secure. And thank you to our partners at Ogden City for helping with a great going away party. Weber
   County is unique with its large facilities such as the Golden Spike Event Center, Ogden Eccles Conference
   Center, our Shooting Complex, the Peery’s Egyptian Theater, our Archery Range, the Upper Valley parks, or the
   Ice Sheet facility. These wonderful facilities cost a lot of money to maintain each year. But because there is so
   much tourism generated in part by having these facilities, the tax from hotel rooms, restaurant taxes, etc help
   address any financial shortfalls each of these facilities may incur in lieu of property tax having to be used.

F. PRESENTATIONS:
   1. Proclamation by Weber County Commission recognizing a Community Partnership between Weber
      County and MarketStar.
      Chair Harvey: We have a proclamation to give to Mr. Phil Mickey on behalf of MarketStar and I will
      read that now:
                                                                  THE WEBER COUNTY COMMISSION
                                                               RECOGNIZES A COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP
                                                              BETWEEN WEBER COUNTY AND MARKETSTAR

                                 WHEREAS, MarketStar, founded in Weber County in 1988, and currently headquartered in
                          downtown Ogden, Utah, is dedicated to fostering community growth and engagement through the
                          MarketStar Foundation; and

                                   WHEREAS, Weber County owns and operates various public parks, public venues, and public
                          attractions for the benefit of its citizens and the community; and

                                   WHEREAS, MarketStar and Weber County are committed to partnering with one another on a
                          three-year community partnership to maintain and beautify public parks, public venues, and public
                          attractions throughout Weber County; and

                                 WHEREAS, this community partnership will involve the volunteer time of MarketStar
                          employees and financial resources through the MarketStar Foundation; and

                                   WHEREAS, this community partnership will commence with MarketStar's Global Day of
                          Service on September 13, during which more than 400 Utah-based employees will work on six separate
                          projects at two vital Weber County public venues: Fort Buenaventura and the Golden Spike Arena; and
                         WHEREAS, this initiative is part of a larger MarketStar project to engage its 3,500 global
                  employees in community service in the areas where they live, work, and play; and

                          WHEREAS, this community partnership aligns with MarketStar's company purpose to create
                  growth by building a better version of our people, our clients, and our communities; and

                          WHEREAS, MarketStar expresses its gratitude to Weber County, and the residents of Weber
                  County for the wonderful facilities provided and maintained by Weber County; and

                         WHEREAS, MarketStar invites all businesses in Weber County to join in this commitment to
                  our home community.

                          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED the Board of County Commissioners of Weber
                  County, Utah, does hereby recognize a three-year community partnership with MarketStar to maintain
                  and beautify public parks and attractions throughout Weber County for the benefit of its citizens and the
                  community.


         Marty Smith (Culture, Parks, and Recreation Director): In July we were approached by MarketStar to
         participate in their Day of Service, as we have done in the past. As we met with Phil and others from
         MarketStar, the conversation grew into this idea of a possible multi-year agreement. Todd Ferrario did a lot
         of heavy lifting to get this going along with Brad Cragun and Duncan Olsen, Liam Keogh from the Attorney’s
         office, and Shelly Halacy and Stacy Skeen from the Commission office who got this Proclamation in working
         order.
         Phil Mickey (MarketStar VP of Marketing): Thanks for having us and for your kind words. Working with
         Marty and Todd is great. This is a commitment to do more. We did not get to everything we wanted to do.
         We are born in Weber County and we will continue to do the best that we can.
         Mr Smith: This is a global Day of Service. Phil, can you tell us what was done in other places that day?
         Mr. Mickey: We have employees in Chicago that put together 1000 meal kits. We had a virtual event where
         150 remote employees put together kits for those going through chemotherapy; about 200 Dublin Ireland
         employees put together homeless kits; our Sophia, Bulgarian employees helped with a river clean up; we have
         two offices in India that worked on three old folks homes and four orphanages.
         Commissioner Bolos: It’s often through families or church that these acts of service happen. It’s not often we
         see an employer spearhead something this huge. It’s just phenomenal. The employees are lucky to work for
         a company that values community input and sacrifice. Thank you!
