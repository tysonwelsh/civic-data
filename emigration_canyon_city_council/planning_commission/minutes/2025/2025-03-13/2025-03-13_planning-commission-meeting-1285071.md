BUSINESS MEETING
Meeting began at – 8:30 a.m.
1) MSD Planning staff will continue with the Code Update Projects. The specific issues discussed will
be the ecological protections with regards to development standards. Fire Chief Shawn Lewis
Peterson of Unified Fire will join to discuss Fire Code Standards. Planner(s): Matt Starley and
Brian Tucker (Discussion)
Mr. Starley introduced Fire Chief Peterson and explained our process regarding standards and site
access, private and public roads. 2021 International Fire Code (IFC) and the Wildland Urban
Interface Code (WUIC) are applicable codes related to site fire access. Fire Department (FD)
Access Roads are required to be constructed surface to support 75,000 lbs. such as asphalt or
concrete. Can’t be cobblestone or landscape pavers. Most roads in Emigration are pavement. FD
access roads are required to be 20-feet wide, if over 500-feet long the width needs to go to 26 feet.
Minimum driveway is 20-foot width and can narrow down to 12 feet at the gate. 150 feet from
roadway need to access the structure completely around it for attack lines. If the fire authority must
pull in the driveway and need to go down more than 150 feet, they would require a turnaround.
Roads/driveways can’t be more than 10 percent grade and sometimes the FD will consider 12
percent but can’t be more than 500 feet length, over 10 percent grade. 28 foot inside turn radius
shall be provided for FD access. A code modification will be considered if the structure has an
NFPA-13D Sprinkler System. Public Road, private road and shared driveway are the three types of
site access in Emigration Canyon. Anything more than a private driveway would require 20-foot
