Commissioner Cohen asked if they are aware of prop onents of the sunset. Ms. Blaes said this is 
something the legislature was testing out , and sunset is a standard practice of the legislature. Ms. 
Trevino said there are many stake holders, c ommunity councils adjacent to the area , Town of 
Brighton, cities adjacent, ski resorts and other interest groups, several players were not comfortable 
with the MPD being there in perpetuity; sunset is a common way of doing legislation. Bill was before 
the Town of Brighton was incorporated, do not  know if plans of any other area falling into 
incorporation.  
 
Commissioner Jones asked if she could be educated on an agreement made on fire protection for 
the Town of Brighton in exchange for remaining in the Mountainous Planning District. Ms. Trevino 
said all pu t together with Mayor McAdams ; when the Town of Brighton incorporated state law 
prohibited the county from providing general funding for public safety, but an exception put together 
in a bill in the 2019 session. Commissioner Jones asked about the tie between maintaining land use 
authority and maintaining support for fire protection. Commissioner Despain asked about the Town 
of Brighton getting land use authority. Ms. Trevino said sunsets of MPD/public safety bills coincide 
and will be a topic of discussion for repealing the sunset. Commissioner Cohen said first concept of 
Mountainous Planning District Planning Commission  began in 2015, he was on the Salt Lake 
County Planning Commission at that time doing the planning for the county, and questioned why 
create a whole other planning commission rather than just increase the Salt Lake County Planning 
Commission’s qualifications. It was important to the mayor that land use authority remained at the 
county regardless of annexation or incorporation and  thought there was mixed feelings in the 
legislature and that’s why there was a sunset placed, now we have another year and drafting of a 
bill for next session. Always thought land use authority and Mountainous Planning District 
Planning Commission were completely tied together and no separation of the two concepts.  
 
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding the planning commissions and powers of 
what they can do. A lot of confusion from the legislature of what the planning commission does. 
Concept was a regional area to share input/views and county is trying to partner with the Town of 
Brighton. Comforting to see residents’ issues up the canyon, and balance with the valley using the 
canyon. Sunset provision came about and had bearing on land  use, but also fire and police. The 
sunset on everything is 2021 and there would need to be a discussion with the stakeholders if/when 
the sunset expires. Brighton Community feels that if they have their own land use, they must pay for 
their own services. A legal tie is that there are restrictions on use of county funds for municipal 
services. Barbara Cameron said would like someone to look up county resolution that designates 
recreation. Commissioner Cohen provided input as to the Salt Lake County Planning Commission, 
its members and roles as to what their role has been and could be if the Mountainous Planning 
District Planning Commission sunsets and desire for the c ounty to keep the land use authority. If 
there is a sunset, Town of Brighton would have to create their own planning commission. Mr. Young 
said having a planning commission dedicated towards the mountain areas is valuable.  
 
Trying to schedule a legislative committee presentation and work with the Chair on setting up dates 
and times on the presentation.
