                               ORDINANCE NO. 1579



AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING TITLE 2 OF THE
DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL CODE REGARDING CAMPAIGN FINANCE DISCLOSURES.



       WHEREAS, Title 2 of the Draper City Municipal Code (“DCMC”) includes
provisions regarding the requirements for campaign finance disclosures; and

    WHEREAS, it is necessary from time to time to revise certain terms of the
DCMC to address changes in state law applicable to the City; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has reviewed the proposed text amendment and
finds good cause to revise the terms and provisions of Title 2.

     NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

        Section 1. Findings. The City Council of Draper City has made the following
findings to amend Title 2 of the Draper City Municipal Code: 1) Certain provisions of
Title 2 have been impacted by changes in state law; 2) the proposed amendments to
Title 2 are in compliance with the Utah Code Annotated; and 3) the proposed
amendments are in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens
of Draper.

       Section 2. Revision. Title 2 of the DCMC is hereby amended to read as shown
in Exhibit “A” attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference.

      Section 3. Correction of Editing Errors. The City Attorney is authorized to
make non-substantive corrections to the attached Exhibit A, which may include
corrections of punctuation, spelling, formatting, or similar errors prior to submitting
the Ordinance to Sterling Codifiers.

       Section 4. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is
held invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any
other portion of this Ordinance, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this
Ordinance shall be severable.
       Section 5.      Effective Date.   This Ordinance shall become effective
immediately upon publication or posting, or 30 days after final passage, whichever is
closer to the date of final passage.




PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH,
ON THIS 2nd DAY OF MAY, 2023.

2-6-025: CAMPAIGN FINANCE DISCLOSURE:

  A. Each candidate for elected office shall comply with the campaign finance
     disclosure requirements found in the Utah Code Annotated, as amended.

  B. The recorder shall provide each candidate notices of campaign finance
     disclosure deadlines as required by state law at the following times:

        1. When the candidate files a declaration of candidacy; and

        2. Before the municipal general election.

  C. The notice described in 2-6-025(B) shall include:

     1. The provisions of statute or municipal ordinance governing the disclosure
        of contributions and expenditures;

     2. The dates when campaign finance statements must be filed; and

     3. The penalties that apply for failure to file a timely campaign finance
        statement, including the statutory provision that requires removal of the
        candidate’s name from the ballot for failure to file the required campaign
        finance statement when required.

  D. The recorder will not provide other notices of deadline.
