Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky
CC:  Date: October 8, 2020
Re: Quick Quack Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit Extension Request Comments: On October 24, 2019, the Planning Commission granted site plan and conditional use permit approval for Quick Quack, located at 154 East 13800 South. The application numbers are SPR-708-2019 and USE-776-2019.
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-5-090(J) states:
Failure to obtain a land disturbance permit within one year of approval of any site plan shall void the prior site plan approval. Prior to the expiration of the one-year period, an applicant may submit a written request with the community development department for a site plan extension of up to six (6) months. Approval of this extension may only be granted by the Zoning Administrator or Planning Commission, whichever is the approval authority outlined in this section, provided the applicant can meet the requirements for extension under section  9-5-030  and demonstrate substantial action toward obtaining a land disturbance permit. If a site plan expires, a new application shall be submitted. If there have been no changes to applicable codes which would necessitate updates to the previously approved plans, the applicant will be charged a new application fee of fifty percent (50%) of the fee outlined in the consolidated fee schedule and the application shall be approved by the Planning Commission as an action item. Changes to applicable codes that require alteration of the previously approved site plan shall be subject to a new application fee at one hundred percent (100%) of the fee established in the consolidated fee schedule and a public hearing before the Planning Commission.
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-5-080(J) states:
Unless there is substantial action under a conditional use permit within one year from the date of its issuance, said permit shall expire and shall have no further force or effect. A written request may be submitted to the Community Development Department prior to expiration of the conditional use permit for an extension of up to six (6) months, subject to approval of the Planning Commission, provided substantial action is shown toward establishing the conditional use and the requirements for extension under section  9-5-030  of this chapter are met.
To date, the applicant has not acquired a land disturbance permit or begun improvements to the property.  As such, he is seeking a six (6) month extension of his site plan and conditional use permit approval.  Attached to this memo is the applicant’s written request for approval extension, the original application approval letter, and the October 24, 2019 Planning Commission staff report.  Staff received the request for extension on September 15, 2020, prior to the expiration of the October 24, 2019 approvals.
The applicant has applied for their land disturbance permit, but their financing schedule will only permit commencement of construction in April 2021.
If approved, the site plan and conditional use permit approvals will be valid through April 24, 2021.    The findings are as follows: Sample Motion for Approval – I move that we approve the site plan and conditional use permit extension, as requested by Dallas Hakes, representing QQUV Investments 3, LLC for the Quick Quack Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit, applications SPR-708-2019 and USE-776-2019 based on the findings listed in the memo dated October 8, 2020.
1.       The applicant has shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has occurred towards acquiring a land disturbance permit.
Sample Motion for Denial – I move that we deny the site plan and conditional use permit extension, as requested by Dallas Hakes, representing QQUV Investments 3, LLC for the Quick Quack Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit, applications SPR-708-2019 and USE-776-2019, based on the findings listed in the memo dated October 8, 2020.
1.       The applicant has not shown good cause to obtain an extension and that substantial action has not occurred towards acquiring a land disturbance permit.
