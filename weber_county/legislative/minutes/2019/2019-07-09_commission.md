E. PUBLIC COMMENTS:
   --Jan Fullmer, of Eden, requested that the Commission help influence the completion of the Ogden Valley
   water report from the Sate. Factual information is needed because decisions are being made without the
   knowledge of how much development the Valley can support in terms of water and whether development can
   continue with individual septic systems. She said that there is currently very pressing development in the
   Valley and even a rezone request for a parcel to be more like commercial to support some of the construction
   development needs. Approximately 2½ years ago the Commission approved the Ogden Valley General Plan,
   which was a 2-year process. Much of the public input was placed into that plan which included projections on
   how much development could occur in the Valley based on the development rights that were there. A question
   arose about whether there was enough water to support that type of development—a study had not been done.
   The county requested support from a State agency to conduct a water study. The agency also conducted a
   septic system study because they found phosphates from septic systems in Pineview Reservoir. Final water
   and septic system reports were to have been delivered last November/December. Sean Wilkinson, County
   Community Development Director, reported that he had this conversation a couple of weeks ago with staff.
   His understanding is that the report is complete but the State agency that completed it had to send it to the
   State’s reviewing agency and is now having difficulty getting it back. He would appreciate the Commission’s
   help on this. Commissioner Froerer noted that Valley sewage disposal systems are on the Commission’s radar
   and need to be aggressively looked at.
   --Kevin Burns, of North Ogden, was present last year (7/10/2018) when Lucy Jordan, Hydrogeologist with
   Utah Geological Survey, presented to the Commission the major findings of the Ogden Valley Hydrogeology
   3-year study. He encouraged Chair Jenkins to watch her presentation. He said that Ms. Jordan was treated
   poorly by the Commission at that time.
F. RECOGNITION OF MARLIN JENSEN FOR INCREDIBLE WORK FOR INTERGENERATIONAL POVERTY (IGP).
   Marlin Jensen, IGP Advisory Committee Chair, noted that in 2012 the Utah Legislature adopted the
   Intergenerational Poverty Mitigation Act, which created the Intergenerational Welfare Reform Commission.
   Weber County was already ahead of other counties in this effort. He reported on the county’s many efforts,
   including obtaining substantial grants for afterschool programs, creating I-CAN in 2018, receiving a State
   grant for $150,000 for the pilot program, finding/coordinating the 150+ organizations in the county that provide
   services to the poor, identifying families trapped in IGP and having a coach work with them to bring about an
   elevation in their lives. Early results include 100% of parent participation, significant decrease in school
   absenteeism and homelessness. He commended the Commission for now creating the Prosperity Center of
   Excellence, commended its director, and noted that the IGP Advisory Committee is now dissolved. Chair
   Jenkins outlined the progress of the Center and said that the county notified Ogden City of its efforts and they
   will shortly have a combined work session to work together on this. A services coordinator will start
   unifying/coordinating all the services. The Commission presented Mr. Jensen with a plaque for his hard work
   and dedication. Commissioner Harvey said that this effort was so successful, met its objective, has now
   morphed into the next level and will help the system users to now be able to contribute to the system.
