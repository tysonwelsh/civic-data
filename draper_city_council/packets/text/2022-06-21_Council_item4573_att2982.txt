Subject:                  TRSSD Equalized Tax Rate For Fiscal Year 2022-23
Applicant Presentation: None

RECOMMENDATION:

Approve Resolution 22-41 adopting the Certified Tax Rate for the Traverse Ridge Special Service
District for the 2022 taxable year. The Auditor’s Certified Tax Rate is 0.000354 whit Auditor’s
Certified Rate Revenue $262,661. The TRSSD Administrative Board is requesting a Proposed Tax
Rate to be 0.00708 which has budgeted revenue at $525,322. A Truth-in-Taxation hearing is
scheduled for August 24, 2022.

                                      RESOLUTION NO. 22-41

      A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ACTING AS THE
      GOVERNING BODY OF THE TRAVERSE RIDGE SPECIAL SERVICE
      DISTRICT ADOPTING A PROPOSED TAX RATE FOR THE 2022 TAXABLE
      YEAR.

      WHEREAS, the Draper City Council (“Council”) acted as the Governing Body for the
      purpose of creating the Traverse Ridge Special Service District (“District”) as a Special
      Service District in accordance with the Utah Special Service District §17D-1-101 et seq.
      Utah Annotated , 1953 (the “Act”); and

      WHEREAS, the Council created the Administrative Control Board in accordance with the
      provisions of §17D-1-101 of the Act and delegated to the Administrative Control Board
      the power to act as the governing authority of the District; and

      WHEREAS, the Council cannot lawfully delegate to the Administrative Control Board
      the power to levy a tax on the property within the District and the Council retains the power
      and duty to levy a tax on the taxable property within the District; and

      WHEREAS, the Administrative Control Board desires to have the Council, as the
      Governing Body of the District, establish a proposed certified tax rate for the 2022 taxable
      year at a rate of 0.000708 per dollar of the taxable value on all taxable property within the
      District for the purpose of funding operating expenses and capital improvements; and

      WHEREAS, the Council will hold a Truth-in-Taxation public hearing on Wednesday
      August 24, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. to take public comment where all persons will have the
      opportunity to be heard; and

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER
      CITY, STATE OF UTAH AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1.      Adoption. The City Council adopts a proposed Equalized Property Tax
      Rate for the Traverse Ridge Special Service District for tax year 2022:

                                        Salt Lake County                   Utah County
                                          0.000708                          0.000708

      Section 2.      Final Rate. The Draper City Council will adopt a final Equalized Property
      Tax Rate for the Traverse Ridge Special Service District for tax year 2022 after a Truth-in
      -Taxation public hearing scheduled for August 24, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.

      Section 3.       Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Resolution is held
      invalid, or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other
      portion of this Resolution, and all sections, parts, and provisions of this Resolution shall be
      severable.

      Section 4.       Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
      its passage.
