In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons who speak at a County Commission meeting and the substance “in brief”
of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or purported facts. The county does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.

C. INVOCATION: Ricky Hatch
D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Harvey: We have received a lot of correspondence to change the approval
process on water related decisions as if we are the water authority. The Commission does not have that authority. I
recently toured the Powder Mountain area. It reminded of my job here. My step-father worked for a company that
insured railways and trains. Where do you underwrite trains? You had to go to Lloyds of London to underwrite
something of that value. My grandfather had a poem that talked about railways and went to similar to this
                                        It’s not my job to run the train
                                        The whistle I can’t blow
                                        It’s not my job to say how far
                                        The train’s supposed to go
                                        It’s not my job to shoot off steam
                                        Or even clang the bell
                                        But when that damn thing jumps the track
                                        Just see who catches hell
I for one am grateful for the opportunity, as I was on the phone until 10pm last night with call backs, to explain how
things are going here and to teach. I’m grateful that people would at least think to call or care enough to call and take
the time to talk with me.

G. ACTION ITEMS:

     1. FIRST READING APPROVAL OF ORDINANCE 2022-22 AMENDING SECTION 16-2-12 OF THE
        WEBER COUNTY CODE OF ORDINANCES.

            Steve Collier (Surveyors Office): Three minor changes proposed to section 16-2-12. 1) Discontinue discount
            for providing accompanying CAD file as it turns out not to save time as expected. 2) Remove Interlocal
            Subdivision Plat Review Fee; this service is provided only within a contract therefore reference to this in the
            fee table will read “Defined by Contract”. 3) Add a reference to 16-2-5 Land Use Application Fees for clarity.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve first reading of Ordinance 2022-22 amending Section 16-2-12 of the
            Weber County Code of Ordinances; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
            Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

     2. FIRST READING APPROVAL OF ORDINANCE 2022-23 AMENDING SECTION 45-3 OF THE
        WEBER COUNTY CODE OF ORDINANCES.
            Steve Collier (Surveyors Office): Remove survey screening requirement 45-3-2(a) for survey plats filed in our
            office. Currently required to file surveys regardless of survey plats condition; errors can be brought to the
            attention of the surveyor. Over the past 5 years, few of the comments provided have been addressed; removing
            this requirement will save processing time. Removing 45-3-2(a) would require reordering subsequent
            requirements accordingly; also, existing requirement 45-3-2(b) would become 45-3-1(b).
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve first reading of Ordinance 2022-23 County amending Section 45-3 of
         the Weber County Code of Ordinances; Commissioner Freorer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    3. APPROVE AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT WITH UINTAH CITY FOR SURVEY RELATED
       SERVICES.
         Bahy Rahimzadegan (Surveyors Office): Five year agreement that helps our local cities without surveyors and
         streamlines the process since the survey has already been reviewed and redlined by our office.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve an Inter-local Agreement with Uintah City for survey related services;
         Commissioner Freorer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    4. APPROVE AN INTER-LOCAL AGREEMENT WITH MARRIOTT-SLATERVILLE CITY FOR
       SURVEY RELATED SERVICES.
         Bahy Rahimzadegan (Surveyors Office): Five year agreement that helps our local cities without surveyors and
         streamlines the process since the survey has already been reviewed and redlined by our office. Marriott-
         Slaterville may choose to contract additional services that will be handled with separate contracts.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve an Inter-local Agreement with Marriott-Slaterville City for survey
         related services; Commissioner Freorer seconded.
         Commissioner Froerer – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
