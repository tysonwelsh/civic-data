                     Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                              June, 2021

June, 2021, could best be described as a transitional phase for the Riverton Police Department. As
a result of the retirement of Commander Todd Grossgebauer due to an ongoing health condition, it
became necessary to promote one of our sergeants to the position of commander and one of our
officers to the position of sergeant. Also, we needed to conduct a recruitment for a new officer to
fill our vacant position. Additionally, we had an officer leave our employment for a position closer
to home and we extended an offer to a second new employee to bring us up to full staff.

So, after two exhaustive promotional processes and several interviews of potential new recruits, we
have promoted Sergeant Shane Taylor to the position of Commander, Officer Tony Brereton to the
position of Sergeant, and we hired Tom Schneitter and Harrison Coy as new officers. Tom comes
to us from the Salt Lake City Police Department and Harrison from the Utah Highway Patrol.

We have also developed the curriculum and identified the dates for our first Citizen’s Academy.
Participants will meet every Thursday evening beginning October 14th and will graduate on
November 18th. We are very excited for this opportunity to interact with some of our citizen
partners and provide a valuable learning experience.

Finally, an analysis of our statistics did not reveal any significant pattern changes in our call volume
or in any specific type of crime.
