6.         CA07282023 Amendment to Title 21, of the Land Development Code to the definition of 
           -0006578   a "Transitional Housing Facility"
           (CC)
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  23-17 Ordinance- LDC-Title 21 Chapter 37-21 Definition of Transitional 
                                  Housing Facility
                                  Staff Report and Exhibits
                                  Draft PC Minutes and CUP Letter 9-7-2023

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development, presented Ordinance 23-17 for Council 
                                  consideration and action. Ms. Anderson, and the Applicant were available to respond to 
                                  Council questions. This item was presented at last week's Council Work Session. 

                                  Public comment opened:

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren suggested the definition of "referral" be more clearly defined and 
                                  tightened up.  

                                  Ms. Pat Jones said the Council asked all the right questions. This is a challenging issue. 
                                  On behalf of a group of residents, she thanked the Council for the time they spent 
                                  deliberating on this issue. She provided suggestions related to the security of the facility, 
                                  and suggested that residents may need more time to digest all the information. 

                                  Mr. Bill Means, a business owner on State Street and a resident mentioned that he 
                                  learned about the proposed shelter on channel 5 news. He did not think this was fair. The 
                                  shelter impacts a large area and only residents within 500 feet of the proposed shelter 
                                  were notified. He felt more time was needed to study the issue especially considering 
                                  that seven zones in the city may be impacted by this code amendment. He spoke of 
                                  potential security issues related to the shelter. 

                                  Ms. Kseniya Kniazeva expressed support with Shelter. The homeless population need 
                                  help and we should show compassion to our community. 

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Council expressed appreciation to the public and the staff with Shelter the Homeless for 
                                  providing all the information. This issue was just brought to the council one week ago. 
                                  Council is hard at work to produce the best possible outcome for Sandy City. We need 
                                  more time to gather the information and collaborate. Council is dedicated to moving 
                                  forward and keeping the process transparent.

                                  A motion was made by Cyndi Sharkey, seconded by Marci Houseman to table the 
                                  item to a time certain date of September 26, 2023 Council Meeting...The motion 
                                  carried by the following vote:

7.         23-374                 Council Considering Initiating a review of City Ordinances to Limit the 
                                  Number of Transitional Housing Locations
                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item
                                  Memorandum_Transitional Housing Locations.pdf

                                  Melissa Anderson, Community Development provided information and a review of city 
                                  ordinances to the Council related to limiting the number of transitional housing locations 
                                  in Sandy City. The overview provided several options for the Council to consider, including 
                                  the following:

                                  Amending the Land Use Matrix of Commercial Zoning Districts
                                     Limit the Zoning Districts
                                     Limit to a Specific Geographic Area of a Zoning District

                                  Use Specific Regulation
                                     On a Per Capita Basis
                                     Use Specific Regulation-Spacing Requirements

                                  Ms. Anderson also provided examples of the implementation of the above limitations that 
                                  are currently in use in Sandy City. Council questions followed. Council thanked Ms. 
                                  Anderson for the presentation and information.
                                  A motion was made by Brooke D'Sousa, seconded by Marci Houseman, that the 
                                  Council formally initiate proceedings, consistent with the recommendation 
                                  received from the Planning Commission, to review and amend the City's land use 
                                  regulations in a manner that would limit the number of transitional housing 
                                  facilities that may be allowed within the City. City staff is hereby requested to 
                                  study this issue and bring back to the Council a  recommendation for appropriate 
                                  changes to the City's land use code, for consideration and decision by the 
                                  Council within 180 days...The motion carried by the following vote:

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                              Dustin Fratto, Council Director, mentioned that next week's Council meeting will be a 
                              Business Session. This Friday is a breakfast with the South Valley Chamber and local

Council Member Business

                              Council Member Marci Houseman acknowledged the bulk waste teams. The Community 
                              is very grateful that the city continues to offer this program. The education and messaging 
                              plan from the city is working - the residents are responding. She thanked Cyndi Sharkey 
                              for leading the charge to protect and continue this program for the residents.

                              Council Member Zach Robinson provided an update from the Sandy Arts Guild. The guild 
                              had a very successful summer and raised the bar of success. He spoke about the 
                              flooding in their building which has caused substantial problems. He acknowledged the 
                              Public Works staff who helped resolve a resident's concern with a concrete repair. He 
                              also mentioned that residents are very grateful to Police for their enforcement of the city 
                              speeding laws. The residents are paying attention and are very grateful.

                              Council Member Alison Stroud thanked Administration and Katie Atkinson, the city event 
                              coordinator, for coordinating the Healing Fields event and the Heritage Festival. The 
                              Sandy Senior Center just had their Car and Craft show. She thanked Public Works and 
                              Community Development staff for their responsiveness.

                              Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update for the Utah League of Cities and 
                              Towns annual conference. It was a great event. She attended the Celebration of Life event 
                              honoring Officer Brad Taylor - it was a huge gathering. She thanked the Fire Department 
                              for the recent safety luncheon that she attended.

                              Council Member Brooke D'Sousa spoke about the valuable experience from attending the 
                              ULCT annual conference. She thanked the staff at Shelter the Homeless for their 
                              responsiveness, information, and coordination of facility tours for the Council Members. 
                              She is looking forward to the upcoming safety lunches.

Mayor's Report

                              Mayor Zoltanski thanked the Council for organizing the recent tours. She spoke about the 
                              Noal Bateman presentation, the Heritage Festival, the unveiling of the new city flag, and 
                              the Healing Fields event.
