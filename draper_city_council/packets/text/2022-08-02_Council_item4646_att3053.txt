May 25, 2022



To whom it may concern,



The Pheasant Hills HOA board has met with Tom Hulbert multiple times recently reviewing his updated
plans for his home in our neighborhood. The Hulberts have made multiple adjustments to their plans to
comply with city requirements and requests from the HOA. We approve their updated plans.

Our board supports the plot amendment Tom is proposing on the south side of his home.

Regarding the terraced wall on the north side of his property, we approve of the plot amendment to
change the easement boundary to allow a terraced wall to be installed. This approval is contingent on
the terraced wall following required code for height and spacing and that the wall does not impact
drainage through the current easement.



Pheasant Hills HOA Board
