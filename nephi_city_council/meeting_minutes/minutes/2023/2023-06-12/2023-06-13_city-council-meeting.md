                       Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                           Tuesday, June 13, 2023
                                   7:00 p.m.
                                  21 E. 100 N.
                               Nephi, UT 84648


1.    The Council discussed the R-3 Zone. Councilor Memmott asked if staff had an updated
     zoning map and asked what part of the City is zoned as R-3. Mr. Atkinson said that there
     was a developer waiting for the temporary land use restrictions to be lifted. The council
     directed staff to bring back the ordinance to rescind the temporary land-use ordinance
     for the R-3 zone at the next regular council meeting.

2.    Mr. Atkinson informed the council that a hotel agreement was being considered and the
     developer would like to pay $250,000 and then make payments of $100,000 for three
     years plus interest. Mr. Atkinson suggested no less than a 7% interest rate to
     encourage the developer to seek financing from a lending institution. Councilor
     Memmott suggested that the property should be appraised before selling the property.
     Councilor Memmott said that originally the staff and council thought the property would
     sell for more. He also stated that he is against financing the property. Councilor
     Memmott also would like to see the hotel built within two years instead of three years.
     Councilor Ostler, Councilor Callaway and Mayor Seely all were against financing the
     development. The council directed staff to put in the agreement that they would pay the
     $550,000 up front and put strict deadlines of when the project should be started and
     completed. The council wanted to shorten the timeframes in section 7 of the agreement
     to one year. Councilor Callaway said to possibly set the original start date from when
     they get financing. The council agreed to set a financing deadline of 6 months and a
     year after receiving financing to begin the project. City Attorney Kasey Wright will bring
     a revised agreement back to the council for review.

3.    City Recorder Lisa Brough updated the council that since the last meeting, Governor
     Cox had issued a statewide proclamation stating that counties should run all municipal
     elections and that changed the dates of the 2023 elections to September 5th for the
     primary election and November 21st for the general election. The council directed staff
     to bring back for approval at the next regular council meeting the memorandum of
     understanding with Juab County Clerk to run the 2023 elections.

4.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed a draft of a property lease agreement with UMPA. The property
     is located south of the city where UMPA is building a power plant. The council directed
     staff to bring the agreement back to the next regular council meeting for authorization.

5.   City Administrator Updates:
        a. Reviewed the certified property tax rate
           b. Announced that the new revised website design is scheduled to launch on July
              3rd.
           c. There will be no regular council meeting on July 4th.

   6. City Council Updates:
          a. Mayor Seely said he had a resident complaint about some drainage problems on
             288 South Main Street.
          b. Mayor Seely said that the Chief of Police would like some poles installed so he
             could move the sign that records speeds to different areas of the city where
             complaints of speeding have been reported. Mr. Atkinson said that they could
             purchase a mobile sign that could be moved more easily around the city and
             collect data that would aid the police department in deciding time and areas to
             patrol more heavily for speeding. Mr. Atkinson said that Chief Morgan is
             obtaining quotes for such equipment.
          c. Councilor Ostler reported on a resident’s complaint about a pot hole in front of
             his house that was caused by the culinary water project construction. Mr.
             Atkinson said he would let the Public Works Director know about the complaint.

   7.   Adjournment 8:35 p.m.
