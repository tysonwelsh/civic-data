Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on July 20, 2021, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commission Chair Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, County Administrator Nicholle
Felshaw, Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales, and Deputy Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

PRESENTATION: SCHOOL INSTITUTIONAL TRUST LANDS AUTHORITY (SITLA)

UPDATE

School and Institutional Trust Lands Agency (SITLA) Director David Ure thanked the
Commissioners for their working relationship with SITLA and provided a brief history of the
agency. Mr. Ure explained that the agency manages 3.4 million acres of trust lands across Utah on
behalf of 12 beneficiaries. The 12 trust land beneficiaries receive annual earnings from their
individual endowment. The largest beneficiary is Utah’s public schools which received $82.66
million last school year and $88.83 this school year.

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2021-1184-O, AN ORDINANCE AMENDING
THE WASHINGTON COUNTY PROCUREMENT CODE

Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales explained that the change to the procurement code will
clarify processes for large purchases as well as modifying the process for emergency purchases.
Purchases of supplies, equipment or services under $5,000 may be made by the department head
without having to acquire three (3) separate bids.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1184-O, An
Ordinance Amending the Washington County Procurement Code. Motion
seconded by Chairman Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with
Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2801, A RESOLUTION PROVIDING
AMERICAN RESCUE PLAN ACT FUNDS FOR IMPROVEMENTS TO COUNTY
SERVERS

Ms. Hales explained that the American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds were received by
Washington County in order to help stop and slow the spread of COVID-19. The load on the
servers has tripled as employees have worked remotely and members of the public are increasingly
utilizing online services. Funds will be used for the purchase of new servers, backup storage, and
operating storage.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2021-2801, A
Resolution Providing American Rescue Plan Act Funds for Improvements to
County Servers. Motion seconded by Chairman Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF PUBLIC WORKS REPORT

Road Superintendent Dean Wulffenstein provided a report of the current Public Works projects
which included: the completion of Dalton Wash Road drainage and Sand Hollow Road;
preparation for paving Sage Road in Pine Valley; addition of speed limit signs throughout the
Shivwits Reservation, Kolob Reservoir, Oak Valley Road, Spilsbury Road, and Kolob Terrace
Road; Safety enhancements on Kolob Terrace Road; and Grading of rural roads throughout the
County where possible.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve the Public Works Report as
Presented. Motion seconded by Chairman Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist and Iverson voting aye.
