I.	DISCUSSION ITEM
	A.	FISCAL YEAR 2021 BUDGET
Staff reviewed the budget process, key issues for the FY 2021 budget. The Council and staff discussed the Unified Police Department budget increase at great length. A budget deficit was discussed due to the COVID19 and loss of revenue.  Each department reviewed specifics regarding budget needs only for the upcoming budget. Any market adjustments or merit increases are on hold for FY2021 unless the market improves.  An economic development report was presented as well as a Redevelopment Agency report. Capital projects and operating budgets were also discussed.  The Council discussed using fund balance to cover the revenue shortfall for FY2021.
