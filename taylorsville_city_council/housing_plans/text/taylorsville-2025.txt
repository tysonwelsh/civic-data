3. Describe each action taken between August 1, 2024 and July 31, 2025 to implement the

D
On June 4, 2025 the Taylorsville City Council approved Ordinance 25-07 amending the Fee
Schedule to include the following language, “Moderate- Income Housing: In accordance with the
city' s Moderate Income Housing Plan strategy, certain applicable fees may be waived for the
construction of moderate-income housing units. Such fee waivers shall be based on and
proportional to the percentage of moderate- income housing units in the project— for which the
fees are being calculated. A 'moderate income housing unit' is defined as a deed-restricted
housing unit that is occupied or reserved for occupancy by households with a gross household
income equal to or less than 80 percent of the area median income ( AMI) in Salt Lake County.”

E
Planning Staff has been researching and preparing draft code language to introduce detached
ADUs in Taylorsville. That research and draft was presented to the Planning Commission on
June 10, 2025. Staff received helpful feedback and will take the proposal back to the Planning
Commission in July 2025, with potential action by the City Council in August 2025.

to process applications to prepare for public hearings.
