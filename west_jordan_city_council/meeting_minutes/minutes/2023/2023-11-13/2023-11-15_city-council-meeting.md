 1. CALL TO ORDER
       COUNCIL: Chair Chris McConnehey, Kelvin Green, Zach Jacob, David Pack, and Kayleen
                Whitelock
       STAFF:   Council Office Director Alan Anderson, Mayor Dirk Burton, City Attorney Josh
                Chandler, City Administrator Korban Lee, Policy Analyst & Public Liaison Warren
                Hallmark, Public Services Director Cory Fralick

Chair McConnehey called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm and noted that Vice Chair Bloom was absent.

 2. PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
Gage Broadhead led participants in the Pledge of Allegiance.

 3. SPECIAL RECOGNITION
       a. Oath of Office of the West Jordan Youth Council
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson introduced Youth Council members, and City Recorder
          Tangee Sloan administered the Oath of Office. The City Council welcomed the Youth Council and
          took photographs. Council Member Whitelock introduced Gage Broadhead as the Youth Council
          Advisor and thanked him for volunteering to serve.


 4. PUBLIC COMMENT
 Chair McConnehey opened the public comment period at 6:10 pm
 Comments:
 Laurie Sheen, West Jordan resident in Copperfield Phase 1, said she was representing 150 adults
 residing in or around the Copperfield neighborhood. She presented a petition requesting a crosswalk
 at the intersection of 6250 West and 9000 South, and spoke of the need to improve safety for everyone
 using the intersection. Ms. Sheen expressed the opinion that the Copperfield neighborhood was
 isolated, and suggested the proposed crosswalk would be a start to fixing what had been referred to as
 a regrettable situation.
 Shelly Carlile, West Jordan resident in Phase 1 of Copperfield, supported the petition to put in a
 crosswalk to connect Phase 1 with Phases 2 and 3 of Copperfield.
 Bob Bedore, West Jordan resident, said he spoke on behalf of those who were upset about party
 interference in the current election. He asked the Council to address the problem.

 Kelly Lewis, West Jordan resident in Copperfield Phase 1, expressed support for the petition requesting
 a crosswalk.
 Brett Seegmiller, West Jordan resident, said he and his neighbors on Duck Ridge Way were against

 5. REPORTS TO COUNCIL
       a. City Council Reports
          Council Member Green thanked Korban Lee for a recent tour of the Valley Communications
          Center. He reported on the recent Veteran’s Day event, and thanked staff for putting it together.
          Council Member Green had been successfully piloting a GoTo communication app that he
          believed would be a valuable tool.

       b. Council Office Report
          Council Office Director Alan Anderson provided an update on the City Hall renovation project.
          Policy Analyst & Public Liaison Warren Hallmark reported on the Youth Council meeting held
          the previous evening and noted that the Youth Council were off to a good start.

       c. Mayor’s Report
          Mayor Dirk Burton reported UDOT had agreed to install a traffic signal light at 6400 South and
          New Bingham Highway. Mayor Burton reported on recent events, and spoke of upcoming
          events. He said 1300 West was now open to traffic in both directions, and reported City
          snowplow drivers were ready for the upcoming winter season.

       d. City Administrator’s Report
          City Attorney Josh Chandler introduced Patrick Boice, the newest attorney for West Jordan City.
          Mr. Boice said he was excited to serve in West Jordan.

           Public Services Director Cory Fralick gave an update on the Gold Star Monument, and said a
           ground breaking may be possible in late spring of 2024.

           City Administrator Korban Lee provided an update on the Wheels Park, which was expected to
           be completed in 2024. Mr. Lee spoke of plans for Ron Wood Park and a potential future
           recreation center, and answered questions from the Council. He acknowledged that Soccer
           Complex leases were coming to an end, and expressed a desire to make multipurpose fields
           available as quickly as possible. Mr. Lee said information would be presented regarding soccer
           field options at a future meeting.
