Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then Councilor Seely
offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:

  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by several residents of the Red Cliffs Assisted Living. Owner Eudene
Anderson, stood and informed the council about the services that Red Cliffs Assisted Living offers.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes
of the regular council meeting held on 1-7-20, work session minutes 1-7-20, work session minutes 1-14-
20, a list of claims on the warrant register dated 1-21-20. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion the
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPROVED:

   Councilor Jones reviewed four business license applications and then moved to approve the business
licenses for Cinder Creek Event Center, Hair by Ambre, K-9 Redesign, and 2nd Gen Stone. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

PUBLIC COMMENT:

  Mr. Jay Forscutt addressed the council about his neighbors unlicensed dogs that bark day and night.
He stated that other neighbors have complained about the dogs and that animal control had informed
him that nothing can be done about the dogs barking.
  Gerratt Bethers reported that he was worried about the sign ordinance and how it related to his
posting of signs at the cemetery and the city park. He had spoken to Planning Administrator Glenn
Greenhalgh about this issue. Mr. Greenhalgh informed Mr. Bethers that he could post signs at the park
and cemetery but not in the public right ways. He thanked the council and mayor for their work and
dedication to the city.

ORDINANCE 1-21-2020 CITY CODE REVISIONS ADOPTED:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the proposed Ordinance 1-21-2020 that
revised three areas of the city’s land use code dealing with signs, zero-lot line developments and
subdivision regulations. Councilor Memmott questioned whether the council needed to address the
issue of non-profit organizations posting signs on public property. Councilor Jones proposed that the
local sign companies could give the city sign code to their patrons and give them a sign permit
application.

JAMES OCKEY AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Seth Atkinson updated the council that as part of the water project, a new spring box was put in at
Bradley Springs. Construction of the new spring box made it necessary to examine all the easements
Nephi City has on Jim Ockey’s property for our water system. It came to the city’s attention that these
easements were incorrectly surveyed and not where the facilities are placed. In addition, the city
needed an additional 15’ of property to accommodate the new spring box facility.
   Mr. Ockey requested that the city sell some raw water to him and he would be responsible to treat
the water for his own facilities that service some homes in the area as well as the KOA campground. Mr.
Atkinson stated that the water that the city sells to the Ockeys would be metered. Mr. Ockey also
requested an easement to install a pipeline to connect to the upper Bradley Springs.
   Councilor Seely moved to authorize the Mayor to sign the agreement with some detail additions to
Exhibit A and Exhibit B. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

BANJO AGREEMENT APPROVED:

   Councilor Jones moved to authorize the mayor to sign an agreement with Banjo Company. Banjo
company pairs publicly available information with advanced artificial intelligence technology to assist
law enforcement with solving crimes. The state of Utah has an agreement to use the technology
available through Banjo and cities can partner with the state free of charge for three years. Councilor
Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

  Mr. Atkinson stated that the city is looking for Mammoth Parade volunteers.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Jones said he was worried about the number of dogs running at large and dogs barking
complaints that have been received lately.
  Councilor Worwood emphasized the need for volunteers for the Mammoth Parade.
  Councilor Memmott mentioned the possibility of the Planning Administrator drafting a water
conservation ordinance for newer homes and subdivisions.
   Councilor Seely mentioned the poor conditions of the roads because of the water project.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character and
competence of an individual and the acquisition and disposition of real property. Councilor Worwood
seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:14 p.m.
