1)
Discussion and approval of Proclamation 2023-09, a Proclamation Declaring May 17-21, 2023 "National Public Works Week" in Summit County, Utah; John Angell (2:06 PM)
Attachment: Proclamation 2023-09 National Public Works Week.pdf
Christopher Robinson made a motion to adopt Proclamation 2023-09, a Proclamation Declaring May 17-21, 2023 National Public Works Week in Summit County. Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
John Angell, Public Works Director, presented the Proclamation. Council Members added their individual gratitude for all that Public Works has done this year, over and above, all it usually does.
Attachment: Proclamation 2023-09 executed
2)
Discussion and approval of Snyderville Basin Special Recreation District's request to amend the use of RAP Recreation grant funds received per Agreement RAPREC-14-22; Amy Yost (2:17 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: RAP Recreation Request-Synderville Basin Recreation SSD.pdf
Malena Stevens made a motion to approve Snyderville Basin Special Recreation District's request to amend the use of RAP Recreation grant funds received per Agreement RAPREC-14-22 for the purpose of purchasing additional equipment. Christopher Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
Amy Yost, Chair of the Recreation, Arts, and Parks (RAP) Tax Committee distributing money to recreational activities, asked the Council to amend the grant previously given to Snyderville Basin Special Recreation District. Council Members asked questions, and Ms. Yost responded.
3)
Discussion and approval of Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee's 2023 grant recommendations; Billy Demong and Eric Bradshaw (2:20 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Restaurant Tax Committee Recommendations 2023.pdf
Billy Demong, Chair of the Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee, and Eric Bradshaw, Secretary of the Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee, presented the Committee’s recommendations on distribution of the tax based on the grant applications submitted to the committee. Council asked questions, and Mr. Demong and Mr. Bradshaw responded.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve the Restaurant Tax Advisory Committee's 2023 grant recommendations. Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).

Discussion regarding Fairgrounds Master Plan and approval of Construction Contract for North Summit Recreation and the Summit County Fairgrounds; Ron Boyer, Charity Richins, Tyler Orgill, Ryan Stack (2:41 PM)

Tonja B Hanson made a motion to approve Construction Contract A201-2017 for North Summit Recreation and the Summit County Fairgrounds in the form received this afternoon. Malena Stevens seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Abstain: Christopher Robinson.

Discussion and direction from Council regarding the Simmons Petition to Annex Parcels CD-633-A, CD-627 and CD-627-A, into the City of Kamas, Utah; Laura Kuhrmeyer (3:16 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report-Simmons Petition to Annex.pdf
Laura Kuhrmeyer, Planner, presented the Simmons Petition to Annex property into Kamas. Council Members asked questions. Planner Kuhrmeyer responded.
Council Members did not oppose the Annexation.
Malena Stevens left.
6)
Discussion and approval of May Tax Sale proposed payment plan for Parcel WV-49; Chase Black (3:20 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report-Tax Sale Parcel WV-49.pdf
Attachment: 2742 Bitterbrush Articles of Organization.pdf
Attachment: 2742 Bitterbrush LLC Operating Agreement.pdf
Attachment: 2742 Bitterbrush Owner Letter.pdf
Chase Black, Chief Deputy Auditor, briefed the Council on Parcel WV-49 and responded to Council’s questions.
Adam Ford, attorney for Bitterbrush LLC--the property owner, explained his client’s position and request.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to authorize removal of Parcel WV-49 from the May Tax Sale and approve a payment plan requiring a 25% payment of back property taxes by tomorrow morning and balance, including taxes, penalties, and interest within 90 days. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0). Abstain: Malena Stevens.
7)
Presentation and action regarding the finalized Rail Trail Corridor Plan [public comment was taken]; Pat Putt, Madalyn McDonough and Jennifer Leslie (3:47 PM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Rail Trail Corridor Plan Presentation.pdf
Attachment: Staff Report-Rail Trail Corridor Plan.pdf
Attachment: Historic Union Pacific Rail Trail Brochure
Madalyn McDonough, Planner, presented the Rail Trail Corridor Plan. Pat Putt, Community Development Director, provided background about the Corridor. Jennifer Leslie, Planner, gave additional detail on the the Plan. Council Members asked questions to which Ms. McDonough responded.
Malena Stevens returned to the meeting.
Council Chair Armstrong opened the meeting for public comment.
Former Council Member Sally Elliott was called to speak.
Marianne Cone, Brochure Illustrator, was called to speak
Heinrich Deters, Park City Trails & Open Space Program Manager, was called to speak
Council Chair Armstrong closed the meeting for public comment.
Canice Harte made a motion to approve implementation of the Rail Trail Corridor Plan. Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0).
