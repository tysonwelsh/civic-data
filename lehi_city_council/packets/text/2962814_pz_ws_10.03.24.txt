29   2) Discussion of the Proposed Draft of the Critical Housing Overlay Zone (CHOZ
30   Brittney Harris did a presentation on the Critical Housing Overlay Zone (CHOZ). The CHOZ is
31   a proposed solution to the goal of creating homeownership opportunities for Critical and
32   Essential workers and other Lehi citizens by increasing the number of financially attainable
33   single family detached homes. This goal must be met as it is set by the State of Utah. Any
34   developer can apply to become a part of the CHOZ. Lots will be rezoned at a lower price and the
35   savings will be passed down to potential homebuyers.
36
37   Commissioner Everett expressed concerns over what level of government would be responsible
38   for enforcing price points.
39
40   Commissioner Lockhart asked questions about how this solution is different than subsidizing
41   businesses.
42
43   Harris emphasized that this is a baby step and not a perfect solution.
44
45   Commissioner Ere motioned to extend the discussion for ten minutes at 7:00p.m. Motion passed
46   unanimously.
