STAFF PRESENTATION
The Staff Report to the Planning Commission provides details of the facts of the case and the Staff's analysis,
conclusions, and recommendations. Key points addressed in Staff's presentation to the Planning Commission
included the following: The 14-acre subject property is located in an area that appears to be transitioning to
residential use. There are other residentially zoned lands near this property, including the somewhat recently
approved Kelshaw Lane development. The Southwest Area Future Land Use map calls for residential
development in the area.
APPLICANT RESPONSE
This application has been in process for quite a while and the applicant has worked to try to bring their
development plans in harmony with City plans and the Neighborhood’s (Sunset) vision for the area. He spoke
to concerns related to providing sewer to the property and noted that he has identified a solution that he believes
is workable now—raise the property to get depth to lay sewer lines. The applicant spoke to the aesthetics of
raising the development site and asserted that grades would be dealt with in a gradual manner. The raised site
would give opportunities for daylight basements.


NEIGHBORHOOD MEETING DATE
  • City-wide application; all Neighborhood Chairs received notification.

NEIGHBORHOOD AND PUBLIC COMMENT
  • No comments or questions for this item were received from the public at this meeting.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
   • The Planning Commission recommended approval of this item to the Municipal Council. It appeared
      that those who voted in favor of this application were sympathetic to concerns with narrow roads and
      challenges to provide sewer to the area, but felt the benefits of the development out-weight the costs.
      Some Commission members expressed concerns with the safety of children traveling to and from school
      on 1600 West. One Commission member was concerned with the density possibilities related to R1.7
      zoning and the message that would be sent to the community if R1.7 zoning were given for the property
      by the City. It appeared many other Commissioners did not share this concern.
