LEGISLATIVE LAND USE APPLICATIONS 
 
REZ2022-000535 – Northrup Grumman is requesting approval to rezone from Agricultural (A -20) to 
Manufacturing (M -2). Acreage: 35.515 acres. Location: 4098 South 8400 West (Legal Address), 
Approximately 4400 South 9200 West (Actual Location). Planner: Brian Tucker (Motion/Voting) 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the staff report. 
 
Commissioner Elieson said he was concerned about the blast zone and clarified this action will not affect 
the blast zone. Commissioner Watkins  asked if there is conversation with DEQ for the environmental 
impact, long term updated permits and if the process has been started. Mr. Tucker said the applicant can 
answer that question. Commissioner Collard asked about 400 jobs, and whether those would be direct 
employment. Mr. Tucker  said he understands they would be direct employment. Commissioner Collard 
asked about Oquirrh mountains, can you tell where this is in location to sensitive Coon Canyon areas. Mr. 
Shaw asked if Melissa Anderson knows the answer. Ms. Anderson said south of this area. Ms. Peters was 
also asked where Coon Canyon is in relation. Ms. Peters showed comparison to the Northrup Grumman to 
sensitive areas. Commissioner Collard asked if Northrup Grumman is seeking any funding from Salt Lake 
County.  
 
Commissioner Elieson motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC PORTION OF HEARING OPENED 
Speaker # 1: Director of Contracts, Northrup Grumman 
Name: Lyndon DeYoung 
Address: 5000 South 8400 West, West Valley City

four in a single building. Adding two buildings, will operate simultaneously and increase process efficiency. 
Want to locate to north end of the line. Coon canyon does pass to the south of the plant, and areas of potential
