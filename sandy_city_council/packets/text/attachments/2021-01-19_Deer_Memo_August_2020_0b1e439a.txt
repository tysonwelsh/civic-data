 To:      Kris Nicholl

 From:    Director I. Williams

 Date:    1/15/2021

 Re:      Deer cases involving fences for the past 48 months.




Sandy City Animal Service and Police Officers respond to calls for service involving deer impaled or
stuck in fences throughout our municipal boundaries. The majority of these calls come from council
districts 3 and 4.

Wildlife calls/reports are primarily “free text,” meaning that due to the wide variety of animals and the
situations they become involved with, it requires the call-taker at dispatch to add descriptive text. We
rely on them to type in the pertinent information. Sometimes the information is vague and is only
clarified once the officer is on scene, handles the call, and then adds notes with a short report. The
result is that there are varying descriptions of the injuries involving deer and fences.

I provide this preamble because occasionally the call record is vague or poorly notated thus there may
be a small error of margin. There may have been a few additional impalements that were just not noted
as such. However, I am confident these numbers are as accurate and inclusive as we can provide:



In the past 48 Months we responded to:

53 calls for deer involving a fence.

24 Deer were impaled

24 Deer were stuck in the fence. 8 of these deer were rescued by officers and returned to wild.

4 calls were duplicates. (multiple callers providing slightly different addresses for the same incident.)

1 Unknown. Details not specific enough to determine if impaled or stuck in the fence.



I have attached a simplified list of these incidents. Details include date, call number, the call
description provided by dispatch and the location provided. I have omitted personal information and
report details, but this should give an idea of the areas and types of injuries or calls involving fencing
and deer in communities and that these occur year-round.
