MINUTES OF THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL OATH OF OFFICE
ADMINISTRATION AND RECEPTION HELD MONDAY, JANUARY 6, 2020 AT 7:00 P.M.
IN THE COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS LOCATED AT 2277
EAST BENGAL BOULEVARD, COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS, UTAH

Present:                  Mayor Mike Peterson, Council Member Douglas Petersen, Council Member
                          Scott Bracken

Staff Present:            City Manager Tim Tingey, Police Chief Robby Russo, Assistant Police Chief
                          Paul Brenneman, Public Works Director Matt Shipp, Community and
                          Economic Development Director Michael Johnson, Records Culture and
                          Human Resources Director Paula Melgar-City Recorder, Finance and
                          Administrative Services Director S. Scott Jurges, City Attorney W. Shane
                          Topham, Communications Manager Tim Beery

Excused:                  Council Member Tali Bruce, Council Member Christine Mikell

1.      WELCOME

Mayor Mike Peterson introduced himself and welcomed those present. He recognized Amber Shill
and Nancy Tingey from the Canyons School District Board and Mike Reberg from Salt Lake County.
Justice Chin would be administering the Oath of Office. The presence of former Mayor Kelvyn
Cullimore was also recognized along with his wife. Outgoing Council Member, Mike Shelton was
present and thanked for his service.

2.      POSTING OF COLORS – Combined Color Guard: Cottonwood Heights Police
        Department and Unified Fire Authority.

The colors were posted.

3.      PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE.

City Recorder, Paula Melgar led the Pledge of Allegiance.

4.      MUSICAL NUMBER – “My Country ‘Tis of Thee” by Brian Allen and Chantel Nate.

Brian Allen and Chantel Nate performed a musical number.

5.      REMARKS – Mayor Michael Peterson.

Mayor Peterson thanked those who participated in the election process this last cycle. He understood
the amount of time and effort the two individuals put in to being elected. He also recognized their
sacrifice.

6.      ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE TO NEWLY ELECTED COUNCIL
        MEMBERS – The Honorable Judge Augustus G. Chin with the City of Holladay
        Justice Court.

        6.1.     REMARKS – Council Member Douglas Petersen – Cottonwood Heights
                 Council District 1.

The Oath of Office was administered to Council Member Douglas Petersen. Council Member
Petersen thanked those who helped him in this endeavor and appreciated the love and support of his
family. He was grateful for the opportunity to represent his district and serve the City at large. His
parents have resided in Cottonwood Heights for 66 years during which time there have been
significant changes. Council Member Petersen commented that Cottonwood Heights is a wonderful
place to live and to have raised his children. Members of the Petersen Family were recognized.

        6.2      REMARKS – Council Member Scott Bracken – Cottonwood Heights Council
                 District 2.

The Oath of Office was administered to Council Member Scott Bracken. Council Member Bracken
appreciated the opportunity to serve another term. He noted achievments the city has had over the
last 15 years and expressed his support for city staff, Chief Russo and the Police Department in
providing excellent service to our city. He described his goals of thorough thought in decision
making, looking at all sides of issues, and promoting policy backed by provision of resources.

Mayor Peterson commented on how wonderful it is to live in Cottonwood Heights and commented
on the successes that have taken place over the past 15 years since the City was incorporated. The
Community Council performed the first feasibility study to become a City and he witnessed firsthand
the process come to fruition. For that to continue, they need to have people with the right character
and moral compass get involved. He was aware of no other individuals who meet that standard more
than Council Members Petersen and Bracken. He appreciated them and their willingness to serve.

7.      REFRESHMENTS.

The Meeting adjourned at approximately 7:28 p.m.
