Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Brien Maxfield
CC:  Date: October 19, 2021
Re:  Comments: Periodically, the Department of Homeland Security's Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) revises the Flood Insurance Study (FIS) materials for the areas subject to flooding as identified on the Flood Insurance Rate Map (FIRM) panels.  The city received a notice that several panels have been updated by FEMA and the city is required to adopted the most current version into city code.
The panels being updated reflect changes to Willow Creek and Bear Canyon Creek and include a refinement of the stream channel, elevations along the creek, better identification and modeling of the structures that impact flooding in the channel, and other updates.  This change does not update all the panels covering the city in Salt Lake County.  No changes are being made to the panels in Utah County. The findings are as follows:
The proposed amendment is consistent with the goals, objectives, and the policies of the city's general plan;
The amendment will not create a conflict with any other section of city code;
The amendment is in the best interest of the city;
The amendment complies with the requirements of the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP), in which the city participates; and
The amendment protects the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper City.
