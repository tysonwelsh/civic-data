RESOLUTION NO. 20-05-02_______                                                   May 20, 2020

     A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
 CLARIFYING PROCEDURES TO DETERMINE WHETHER A CONFIRMED CASE
  OF COVID19 EXISTS IN COPPERTON FOR THE PURPOSES OF COPPERTON’S
                         COVID19 ACTION PLAN

        WHEREAS, on March 13, 2020, the Copperton Metro Township Council adopted an
action plan and declared a public health emergency for the coronavirus disease of 2019 (“COVID-
19”) pursuant to Chapter 2.86 of the Copperton Metro Township Code; and

      WHEREAS, Copperton’s COVID19 action plan calls for certain actions based on the
number of confirmed COVID19 cases in Copperton; and

        WHEREAS, the Council desires to clarify that confirmation from the Salt Lake County
Health Department or the Utah Department of Health is required for a confirmed case of COVID19
to exist in Copperton for the purposes of its COVID19 action plan.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, effective immediately by the
Copperton Metro Township Council that verification from the Salt Lake County Health
Department or the Utah Department of Health is required for a confirmed case of COVID19 to
exist in Copperton for the purposes of its COVID19 action plan

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 20th day of May, 2020.
