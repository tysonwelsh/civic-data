2.  BUSINESS ITEMS 
    2.1. Approval of the amended 2026 City Council Meeting Schedule (Resolution 2026-
        01) 
        The City Recorder’s Office will present the amended annual meeting schedule for 
        discussion and consideration. This item will be voted on only if the City Council 
        adopts Ordinance 2026-02, which approves changes to the days of the week on which 
        City Council meetings are held. The Mayor and City Council will act to adopt (or 
        deny) this request by resolution. 
 
    The council discussed the proposed changes. Initially there was a request for a continuance 
to allow for the adoption of an ordinance to allow for the changes. Councilmember Nair felt that 
his read of current city code allowed the council to change the meeting times by adoption of the

with the proposed resolution.  
 
 
    Motion: COUNCILMEMBER HOLDAWAY MOVED TO ADOPT RESOLUTION 2026-

WAS AS FOLLOWS: COUNCILMEMBERS NAIR, LAURET, WOOD, HOLDAWAY AND 
MCCUMBER VOTED IN FAVOR. THE MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
    2.2. Mayor Stratton to appoint subcommittee to Review Supplemental report form 
        the independent audit 
        The City Council will review the findings of the independent audit, specifically those 
        discussed as part of the supplemental observations and recommendations report. The 
        Council may take action based on that discussion. 
 
    Mayor Stratton led a discussion with members of the council regarding the Supplemental 
report from the independent audit. The councilmembers shared their thoughts and discussed a 
proposed subcommittee to look into the findings further. Councilmember Nair recommended 
creating a formal audit committee possibly funded by leftover funds that had been allocated for 
last year’s  audit. Former councilmember Brett Clawson added clarification on funds and the 
makeup of the audit committee. 
 
    Mayor Stratton clarified his proposal for a subcommittee including the members, purpose 
and scope of the committee.  
 
    Motion: COUNCILMEMBER LAURET MOVED TO APPROVE THE FORMATION OF 
THE SUBCOMITTEE AS PRESENTED. COUNCILMEMBER WOOD SECONDED. 
COUNCILMEMBERS NAIR, LAURET, WOOD, HOLDAWAY AND MCCUMBER VOTED 
IN FAVOR. THE MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
    2.3. Mayor Stratton to appoint subcommittee to review appointed positions and 
        staffing. 
        The council will review current departments and staffing. Council may decide to act 
        based on the outcome of the discussion. 
 
 
    Mayor Stratton led a discussion on the proposed subcommittee. The council expressed 
support for the formation of the subcommittee. The mayor gave details as to who would be 
appointed to the subcommittee as well as its intended scope.  
 
    Motion:  COUNCILMEMBER  HOLDAWAY  MOVED  TO  APPROVE  THE 
FORMATION  OF  THE  SUBCOMMITTEE  AS  PRESENTED.  COUNCILMEMBER 
MCCUMBER      SECONDED.     COUNCILMEMBERS       NAIR,   LAURET,    WOOD, 
HOLDAWAY AND VOTED IN FAVOR. THE MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY.

4.  CLOSED SESSION 
 
    Councilmember Holdaway asked to present clarification on the need for a closed session and 
expressed concerns regarding the previous city attorney Jayme Blakesley.  
 
    Motion: COUNCILMEMBER LAURET MOVED TO GO INTO A CLOSED SESSION AT 
7:02 PM FOR THE PURPOSES OF A DISCUSSION OF THE CHARACTER, PROFESSIONAL 
COMPETENCE, OR PHYSICAL OR MENTAL HEALTH OF AN INDIVIDUAL. 
COUNCILMEMBER NAIR SECONDED. THE ROLL CALL WAS AS FOLLOWS: 
COUNCILMEMBERS HOLDAWAY, MCCUMBER, WOOD, LAURET AND NAIR VOTED IN 
FAVOR. THE MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY.  
 
    Mayor Stratton reopened the regular city council session at 8:25pm. 
 
    Councilmember Holdaway reiterated his concerns and gave additional clarification on the closed 
session as well as the need for a city attorney.   
 
    Councilmember McCumber gave answers to questions raised during public comment, and 
explained the process that had been used to find an interim attorney as well what would be done 
moving forward to retain a full-time attorney. A discussion ensued.  
 
    Motion: COUNCILMEMBER MCCUMBER MOVED TO APPOINT DEER VALLEY LAW 
FIRM TO REPRESENT AND COUNSEL THE CITY AS WELL AS AUTHORIZE THE MAYOR 
TO NEGOTIATE AND SIGN AN AGREEMENT WITH THEM. COUNCILMEMBER 
HOLDAWAY SECONDED. THE ROLL CALL WAS AS FOLLOWS: COUNCILMEMBERS 
HOLDAWAY, MCCUMBER, WOOD, LAURET AND NAIR VOTED IN FAVOR. THE MOTION 
PASSED UNANIMOUSLY.

6.  PUBLIC COMMENTS 
 
    Kimberly Olsen, living in the Lakefront Subdivision, wanted to express her concern over what 
she felt was incorrect information regarding the public release of unredacted ledgers. Additionally, she 
expressed concerns about dismissal of staff. 
 
    Dennis Frank, living in the Cascade Subdivision, expressed concerns about lighting along State 
Road 167 and Main Street.  He also wanted to thank staff for their response time in dealing with 
concerns that he had brought to their attention previously. Lastly he expressed his gratitude to the 
sheriffs office for their response to his recent call.  
 
     Sara Cameron, Vineyard resident, expressed concern over what she saw as misinformation 
spread during the most recent election cycle and asked that the record show “I want people to 
understand there were a lot of things spoken that weren’t true and you continue to speak them anyway 
and that’s really frustrating to me.” She accused specifically Councilmember Holdaway of 
“humiliating and lying about staff.” 
 
    Daira Evans, living in The Villas Subdivision, commented that she would like to see future 
development focused on allowing residents to build equity. Additionally, she mentioned that she 
would like to see planning move towards a more family-focused city. Lastly, she wanted to thank the 
city for putting out its “year in review” 
 
    Chris Bramwell, Vineyard Resident, expressed his excitement for the incoming council and 
mayor and wanted to wish them luck and offered assistance in any way he could.  
 
    David Pearce, living in The Cascade Subdivision, asked several questions of the City Council 
regarding the appointment of the city attorney. These were addressed by Councilmember McCumber 
after the closed session and before the appointment of the interim city attorney.   
 
    Deputy Recorder Tony Lara read, into the record, comments sent by Vineyard resident Caden 
Rhoton.  
 
7.  ADJOURNMENT 
 
The meeting adjourned at 8:32pm
