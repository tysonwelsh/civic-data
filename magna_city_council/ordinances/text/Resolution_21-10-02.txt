MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
RESOLUTION NO.: 21-10-02 DATE: October 26, 2021

A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP

COUNCIL AUTHORIZING THE MUNICIPAL

ADMINISTRATOR TO HIRE SERVICES AS NEEDED TI

COMPLETE CITY PROJECTS
WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township ("Magna") is a Metro Township pursuant to
Utah Code § 10-2a-401 et seq, and
WHEREAS, The Magna Metro Township Council ("Council") is a Metro Township
Council pursuant to Utah Code §10-3b-504, with the Mayor acting as Chair of the
Council pursuant to Utah Code §10-3b-503, and
WHEREAS, the Magna Metro Township Council desires to authorize the Municipal
Administrator to hire services as needed to complete city projects and allocate funds
accordingly,
NOW THEREFOR BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township Council, as
follows:
1. The Council hereby authorizes the Municipal Administrator to hire services as

needed to complete city projects and allocates funds accordingly.
2. The Resolution shall take effect immediately.
APPROVED AND ADOPTED in Magna, Utah this 26" day of October 2021.
