 VARIANCES
 B-3-2020                     B-3-2020 Lance Walker, representing Granger Medical Clinic, is requesting a variance from
                              the West Valley City Code. The variance request is from Section 7-6-408(4)(b) which
                              requires that the area limitation for wall signs on a secondary façade be 5% in the City Center
                              Zone. Mr. Walker is requesting a variance to allow one wall sign to be 10% on the west
                              façade of the new Granger Medical Clinic.
