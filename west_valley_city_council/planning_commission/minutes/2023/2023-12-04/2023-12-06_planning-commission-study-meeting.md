GENERAL PLAN CHANGE APPLICATIONS
GP-3-2023  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting three changes to the Major Street Plan,
           which is part of the West Valley City General Plan. These changes include: 1)
           removing 2400/2365 S between 3200 W and Arnold Way (2940 W), 2) adding 2210 S
           as a 66' right-of-way between 3200 W and Arnold Way (2940 W), 3) adding Arnold
           Way (2940 W) as a 66' right-of-way beginning at 2100 S and extending to 2365
           South. (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp reminded the Commission about a previous discussion regarding three
               proposed changes to the Major Street Plan this past October. She explained that the staff
               took another look at the proposal, and after speaking with the Public Works Department,
               they decided to modify the previous proposed changes. She noted that this proposal
               would improve the connectivity to the larger undeveloped properties. Jody reviewed the
               proposed modifications.
                Commissioner Lovato asked how this would impact 2100 South and SR 201. Jody
                explained that this would connect 3200 West and 2210 South. Therefore, left-hand turns
                would be pulled south of the heavily trafficked 2100 South 3200 West intersection.


ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-7-2023  Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting an amendment to Section 7-13-704 to
           allow lots or parcels within a commercial complex to access a dedicated street by
           crossing another lot or parcel with a recorded access easement. (Staff – Steve Pastorik
           at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Pastorik reminded the commission that the City Council had recently changed the
               Zoning Ordinance and the Condominium Ordinance to no longer permit private streets,
               so you can’t cross another property to access a road. Steve believes there was one
               unintended consequence of the ordinance change. He explained that some commercial
               properties have multiple pad sites. If you look at a shopping center, for example, one will
               typically drive through the center from one pad site to another when entering and exiting
               the shopping area. Which is no longer permitted. He noted that this application would
               create a carve-out for pad sites in a commercial area.

               Lovato asked if permitting private streets has shown any benefit thus far. Steve stated that
               the ordinance change is so new we need more data to answer that question.


SUBDIVISION APPLICATIONS
SMI-2-2023 Petition by RUSSELL PLATT requesting minor subdivision approval for the Truong
           Subdivision and amending lot 14 Benview Subdivision No. 10. The plat will consist of
           7 lots on 4 acres is zoned R-1-8(Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size
           8,000 square feet) and A (Agricultural, minimum lot size one-half acre) and is located
           at 4385 West Benview Drive. (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-3311 or
           steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman stated that the subject property should look familiar to the
               Commission. He explained that a few months ago, a rezone request was brought
               to the Commission, and the western portion of the property was rezoned from A
               to R-1-8. Steve noted that the applicant has come to an agreement with the
               property owner to the north, allowing him to attain part of that property for the
               proposed subdivision. He presented the site plan and noted that the cul-de-sac
               would need to be adjusted slightly to align with the road to the north. Steve said
               that the subdivision would add three new lots, and there would be right-of-way
               dedication. Therefore, there is an existing development agreement. He pointed out
               a boundary issue regarding the fence between the subject property and the canal,
               which is currently being discussed. Steve said this should not interfere with the
               development of the subdivision.


SV-2-2023      Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY ENGINEERING DIVISION is requesting a street
               vacation for all of 5720 West at 4535 South. The street to be vacated was platted as
               part of the Cape Cod Estates Phase 3 Subdivision. (Staff – Steve Lehman at 801-963-
               3311 or steve.lehman@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Steve Lehman noted that this is a request from our Public Works Department to
               vacate the right-of-way at 5720 West. He explained that there are plans to develop
               a Charter School on the property just south of 5720 West. Steve mentioned that
               Mr. Platt had submitted a previous application that the City Council denied.
               Along with adding the school, the City anticipates traffic issues along 5600 West.
               Steve explained that the Public Works Department spoke with the two property
               owners on 5720 West, and they favor the street vacation. The City would remove
               the curb, gutter, and sidewalk, adding a drive approach to 5720 West.

               Commissioner Winters asked Steve if he knew why the City Council denied the
               previous application. Steve noted it was unfortunate because the residents favored
               the proposal. He believes some members of the Council felt this property would
               be better suited for RE zoning and were not in favor of the multi-family zoning.
               Steve mentioned that the property owner had looked to sell the property and have
               a commercial type of use along 5600 West and a residential use behind that.
               However, there were no interested parties.

               Commissioner Lovato asked what would replace the curb, gutter, and sidewalk.
               Steve said that the area would be landscaped.

               Commissioner McEwen said he doesn’t understand why we would vacate the
               street and not use it to access the school. He mentioned that the schools in his
               neighborhood are accessed from residential areas. Steve noted that charter schools
               differ from public schools because students can attend from outside the
               neighborhood boundaries. He stated that there would be significantly more traffic
               coming and going from the charter school. Commissioner Winters and
               Commissioner McEwen are concerned about the potential traffic backup along
               5600 West.


CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-26-2023  Petition by GRANITE CONSTRUCTION COMPANY (GCC) requesting a
           conditional use for outside storage in conjunction with a landscape materials company
           as well as an asphalt plant and asphalt recycling facility. The property is located at
           6602 West 2100 South on 23.10 acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff – Jody
           Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp pointed out that there is a significant amount of water on the subject
               property. She said one of the initial comments from our Public Works Department
               was to ensure the applicant is in contact with the Army Core of Engineers. Jody
               noted they plan to use the entire lot to develop an asphalt plant and recycling
               facility. She presented the site plan and pointed out the areas that would be paved:
               the asphalt access road, landscape supply area, and parking areas. Jody explained
               that on the south side of the property along Frontage Road, they would have a six-
               foot wide approach that would neck down to a twenty-four-foot aisle. She stated
               that the entrance would be landscaped, and the applicant would be responsible for
               any street requirements.

               Commissioner Winters asked what would happen if the Commission approved
               this application and the Army Core of Engineers found the property unsuitable for
               development. Jody explained that this was discussed during a development
               meeting before the applicant applied. She stated they must have started working
               with the Army Core of Engineers and felt comfortable applying. She said
               approving the use today is OK. Then, we must ensure the property can develop
               according to their plans.


C-26-2023      Petition by TRUONG PROPERTIES, LLC requesting conditional use for an
               office/warehouse facility and restaurant. The property is located at 3995 S. Redwood
               Rd on 1.05 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody Knapp at 801-
               963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:    Jody Knapp explained that the applicant is proposing the addition of a restaurant in the
               front of the property and office/warehouse space in the back. She pointed out that the
               pedestrian crosswalk on Redwood Road abuts the subject property. Jody stated that they
               would be required to meet the Redwood Road landscaping requirement, which would
               interfere with the pedestrian connection. So, the applicant would need to provide another
               connection.

               Jody stated that a fence would be added to the subject property on the side adjacent to the
               residential. Commissioner McEwen asked if a wall would be required between the
               subject property and the Smiths, where they are two different businesses next to each
               other. Jody said that a fence is only required next to residential, but she can look into it if
               that is a concern.

               Steve Pastorik suggested the applicant speak to Smiths about a potential shared access.
               He said it would be beneficial for both businesses. Jody said she would talk to the
               applicant about it.

               Jody said that they would be required to meet the commercial design standards. She
               noted that the applicant proposes using numerous materials, but there isn’t much
               movement on the exterior. Jody doesn’t have any information on who the tenants would
               be or the hours of operation so that she will follow up with the applicant.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 4:15 p.m.
