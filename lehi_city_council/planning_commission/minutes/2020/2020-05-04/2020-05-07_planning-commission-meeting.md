Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion Items

    2.1 Discussion of Strong Towns Webinar and APA Article

    Kim Struthers did a presentation on Strong Towns and discussed the related APA Article.

    2.2 Discussion of transfer of development rights

    Brittany Harris did a presentation and update on Transfer Development Rights. They
    discussed the options of development rights.


3. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Nielsen moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Bunker seconded the motion. The motion
passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
