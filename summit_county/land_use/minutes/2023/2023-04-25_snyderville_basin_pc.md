Project # 23-012. Public Hearing and Action regarding a Plat Amendment among Lots 39, 40, and 41, Pineridge Subdivision, to divide Lot 40 in half, giving each half to the adjacent Lots (Lots 39 and 41) and to slightly reconfigure a platted building pad on Lot 41 to accommodate the existing as-built residence. Applicant, Martina Nelson. Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner. 6:05 PM
Jennifer Strader, Senior Planner, presented project 23-012, a Plat Amendment among lots 39, 40 and 41 in the Pineridge Subdivision.
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:

Thomas Cooke made a motion to approve the plat amendment based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law extinguishing lot 40 and combining it into lots 39 and 41 in the Pineridge Subdivision plat amendment as stated in the staff report. DJ Hubler seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0).
4.
Project #23-054. Public Hearing and Action regarding a Low Impact Permit to amend exterior materials and colors for the previously approved Whileaway Ranch Conditional Use Permit, located at 7664 North Whileaway Road, parcel SL-D-206, Silver Creek Estates. Applicant, Val Geist. Patrick Putt, Community Development Director. 6:10 PM
Patrick Putt, Community Development Director, presented a low impact permit to amend exterior materials and colors for the previously approved Whileaway Ranch.
Val Geist, Applicant, addressed the Commission. Ms. Geist proved additional background information on the request to amend the exterior materials.
Commissioners asked clarifying questions of the applicant about the materials being proposed.
Commission Chair Carmichael opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:27 PM.
Janell Kurchinski addressed the Commission at 6:27 PM.
Commission Chair Carmichael closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:32 PM.
Commissioners provided additional comments.
