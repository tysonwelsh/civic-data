Internal Accessory Dwelling Unit (I-ADU) Options

Background
•   House Bill 82 (2021) added new I-ADU regulations to Utah State Code (USC) Section 10-9a-530.
•   Draper City adopted Ordinance No. 1499 on 9/21/2021 to comply with the USC.
•   Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-31 contains the City’s requirements for I-ADUs.
•   DCMC 9-31-090, Exhibit A, identifies areas where I-ADUs are prohibited.
•   Deviations can be requested for I-ADUs on lots over 20,000 ft2 if 2 parking spaces are provided.

Issue
•   Exhibit A limits I-ADUs on lots with certain characteristics above and beyond the minimum
    requirements of the USC.
•   Senate Bill 174 (2023) revised the new I-ADU regulations to prohibit the map, Exhibit A, to include lots
    that were recorded after 10/1/2021; therefore, Exhibit A is a static map.
•   Exhibit A now treats older lots between 6,000 - 20,000 ft2 differently than newer lots.

Option 1
•   No changes to the current I-ADU standards.

Option 2
•   Reduce the size of the lots that are allowed to request a deviation from Exhibit A to 12,000 ft2 min.
•   This would match the minimum lot size allowed for detached accessory dwelling units (D-ADUs).
•   Additional lots would be permitted to request a deviation and provide 2 parking spaces.
•   This would still have equity issues for older lots between 6,000 - 12,000 ft2.

Option 3
•   Reduce the size of the lots that are allowed to request a deviation from Exhibit A to 6,000 ft2 min.
•   This would match the minimum lot size allowed for I-ADUs by the USC and DCMC.
•   Additional lots would be permitted to request a deviation and provide 2 parking spaces.
•   This would be more equitable for older lots over 6,000 ft2.

Option 4
•   Remove Exhibit A and follow the minimum requirements of the USC.
•   Deviations would no longer be needed.
•   Additional lots would be permitted to apply for an I-ADU.
