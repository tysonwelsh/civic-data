PUBLIC HEARING/SUBDIVISION AMENDMENT:
     Public hearing and consideration of a petition for a subdivision amendment for
     Bloomington Country Club Subdivision No. 1 Partial Amendment C (lots 10 &
     11) by adding the parcel of land located between said parcels to each lot and
     to vacate the roadway in the cul-de-sac area and add it to lot 10.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The purpose of this subdivision amendment is
      to vacate the portion of roadway identified in the exhibit and add the area between
      each parcel into the lots. The vacated roadway will be retained as a utility easement as
      there are existing utilities in this area. This request is being made because the owner
      of lot 10 desires to build a garage north of his existing house, and with the current
      roadway alignment he cannot do so. The City of St. George will retain a utility
      easement for all existing utility lines in the area where the roadway is being vacated.
      Additionally, new easements are being granted on the new lot lines and where required.
      This property is located at the southeast intersection of Bloomington Drive and Nelson
      Circle. Zoning is R-1-10. The Joint Utilities Commission recommended approval.
