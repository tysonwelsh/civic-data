City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On February 21, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1576, amending the
Official Zoning Map of Draper City otherwise known as the Lyle McAllister Zoning Map
Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 22nd day of February 2023. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
