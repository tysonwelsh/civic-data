Discussion/Action Items

         Resolution No. 23-64 – Authorizing the City to Enter into a Contract with Waste
         Management for Municipal Waste and Recycling Collections, Hauling and
         Disposal Services

Ryan Carter, City Attorney, said the Waste Management contract was the result of months of
negotiations. He acknowledged that the present form of the contract might not fully satisfy
either party, but it could be seen as a good deal from a third-party perspective.

Mr. Carter mentioned that there are some areas of the agreement they will be closely
monitoring, particularly the calculation of the fuel surcharge, which is still unclear to the City.
He also clarified that the City's strategy all along has been to own the waste and recycling cans.
As part of the contract, Waste Management will procure containers for the City at an agreed-
upon price, which has been cross-checked with the State of Utah to ensure fairness. He
anticipates a budget amendment in the near future to cover the additional cost of purchasing
these containers.

Councilmember McCay MOVED that the City Council approve Resolution No. 23-64 –
Authorizing the City to Enter into a Contract with Waste Management for Municipal Waste
and Recycling Collections, Hauling and Disposal Services. Councilmember McDougal
SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion. Mayor Staggs
called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, Pierucci-yes and
Wells-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
