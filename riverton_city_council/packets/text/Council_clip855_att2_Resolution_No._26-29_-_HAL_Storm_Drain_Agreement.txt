                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-29

          A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN
          AGREEMENT WITH HANSEN, ALLEN & LUCE FOR THE STORM
                      DRAIN MASTER PLAN UPDATE

       WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to update its existing Storm Drain Master Plan to
evaluate existing stormwater infrastructure, identify deficiencies, review applicable codes and
standards, develop a capital improvement plan, and evaluate impact fee eligibility for future
stormwater projects; and

        WHEREAS, Hansen, Allen & Luce, Inc. has submitted a proposal to provide
professional engineering services for the Storm Drain Master Plan Update, including data
collection, code and standards review, hydrologic and hydraulic analysis, storm system
assessment, capital improvement planning, and public outreach services; and

      WHEREAS, the contract amount for the services is One Hundred Ten Thousand Thirty-
Seven Dollars and Forty Cents ($110,037.40); and

       WHEREAS, the work is to be completed on or before November 30, 2026; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds that updating the Storm Drain Master Plan
is necessary to support long-range infrastructure planning, improve stormwater management,
identify future capital improvements, and ensure compliance with current regulatory
requirements.

         WHEREAS, if approved, Council will need to approve $1,000.00 as an added expense
in a future budget amendment

    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

   1. The City Council hereby approves the Agreement with Hansen, Allen & Luce, Inc. for
      the Riverton City Storm Drain Master Plan Update, attached hereto as Exhibit "A."

   2. The Mayor is authorized to execute the Agreement and any non-substantive revisions
      necessary for execution, subject to approval as to form by the City Attorney.

   3. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.
