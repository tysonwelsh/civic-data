Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: January 10, 2023
Re: Resolution 23-05 Comments: The police department has several bikes that were either seized as evidence or remain unclaimed as lost or mislaid property.
The Utah Code describes the procedure for the City to dispose of the different types of property.  The police department has followed the respective procedures in trying to locate the owners of the bikes.
Utah Code allows the City to donate the bikes to a "public use" defined as a non-profit charity registered with the state.  The police department would like to donate the bikes to a local non-profit charity registered with the state (WARD Foundation) that will refurbish them and give them to local refugees. The findings are as follows: 1.  The police department has followed the procedures in Utah Code 24-3-103 and 77-24a-5 in attempting to locate the owners of bikes seized as evidence or left unclaimed as lost or mislaid property.
2. State code allows the City to donate the bikes to a public interest defined as a  non-profit charity registered with the state.
