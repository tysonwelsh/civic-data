DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, Patricia Ruffinengo, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to allow for a medical
office on the property located at 9035 S 700 E (See Exhibit #1: Application Material).

The subject property is located on 0.74 acres (32,234.4 square feet) in the Planned Center—
Convenience District (CvC). The applicant is proposing to utilize suits #200 and #201. The
proposed use is a health clinic with similar services that are routinely provided by a family
practice provider. The proposed business hours are Monday-Friday 9:30 am -5:30 pm. The
applicant is also proposing to update the interior, exterior and landscape to enhance the site.

Properties to the north include an office space and a 7-11 gas station. To the south is the
Willowood apartment complex zoned RM. Properties to the east is the Canyons Technical
Education Center zoned R-1-8 and to the west is 700 East and the Sandy City Cemetery zoned
Open Space.

NOTICE
Due to the nature of the request and the limited impact to surrounding properties, a neighborhood meeting
was not required. However, notices were sent to surrounding property owners within 500 feet of the
subject property. At the time of writing this staff report, no comments have been received by staff.

ANALYSIS
Under Section 21-08-02(B) of the Sandy City Land Development Code the proposed medical
office requires a conditional use in the CvC zone.

There are three other professional office businesses within this same building. The site is
overparked by six stalls for professional office uses. Staff has looked at historical aerial photos
and have never seen a full parking lot. We are not aware of any parking issues at this site. Staff
feels comfortable with this request based upon the extra parking spaces already on-site.

   15. Such other conditions determined reasonable and necessary by the City to allow
   the operation of the proposed conditional use, at the proposed location in compliance
   with the requirements of this Code.
   That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

CONCERNS
Staff has no concerns with the proposed use in the CvC Zone. The applicants letter
indicates that they recently purchased this building and plan on doing some exterior
upgrades to the building and landscaping. Staff would like to work with the applicant and
see what kind of changes are being proposed for the building and landscaping to make
sure it complies with the Land Development Code.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the Conditional Use Permit for
Patricia Ruffinengo on behalf of the Utah Stem Cells for the property located at 9035 S.
700 E. to allow a medical office in the CvC zone described in the staff report based upon
the following findings and subject to the following conditions:


Findings

   1. The proposed use meets the intent of the CvC Zone.

Conditions

   1. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

   2. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all provisions of the Sandy City
   Development Code (or as approved by the Planning Commission), and all conditions
   of approval imposed by the Planning Commission.

   3. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.

   4. That the applicant work with staff on any proposed changes to the building and
   landscaping.
