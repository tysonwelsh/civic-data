11.90.060.010 Purpose And Zone Characteristics

This district is composed of certain land with excellent transportation access and locations
identified with a primarily retail focus. Allowed uses are limited to general retail,
restaurant, lodging, office, service uses, and indoor recreation and entertainment. The
intent is to create and support commercial retail activity in specific areas without more
intensive heavy commercial, manufacturing, contracting, warehousing, distribution, open
outside storage, and similar uses allowed. High quality commercial design standards are
required to increase overall attractiveness and property values. (Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-
2018)

11.90.060.020 Permitted. Conditional. And Accessory Uses

   1.   Permitted And Conditional Uses: Permitted and conditional uses allowed within the
        DR Zone are set forth in section 11 -35-2 of this title.
   2. Accessory Uses: Accessory uses allowed within the DR Zone are set forth in
      section 11-35-2 of this title. Any accessory use must be clearly incidental to a
      permitted or conditional use of the property. Accessory uses shall not be allowed
      on a lot or parcel without the establishment of an approved permitted or conditional
      use. (Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.030 Lot Area


There is no minimum lot area required in the DRZone. (Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.040 Lot Width


There is no minimum lot width required in the DR Zone. (Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.050 Lot Frontage
All parcels in the DR Zone shall abut a public street or approved private street or right-of-
way or have acceptable access to a public street through cross access or private right-
of-way provided through an adjacent lot or parcel. (Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.060 Setback Requirements

Setbacks shall be determined during site plan review, based on required site design
requirements and adopted Building Codes. The width and location of public utility
easements may be modified as approved by the City Engineer. (Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-
2018)

11.90.060.070 Building Height Requirements

A primary building or structure may not exceed forty five feet (45') in height, nor be lower
than ten feet(10') in height. A hotel may not exceed one hundred ten feet(110')in height.
All buildings and structures must adhere to adopted Building and Fire Codes.(Ord. 2018-
24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.080 Distance Betvtfeen Buildings

The distance between any building or structure and any other building or structure shall
satisfy the Building and Fire Codes in place at the time of site plan approval.(Ord. 2018-
24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.090 Lot Coverage


The sum total of all buildings, structures and parking on any parcel or project in the DR
Zone shall not exceed eighty five percent(85%)of the total area of the parcel or project.
(Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-2018)

11.90.060.100 Parking, Loading And Access

   1. Requirements: Each project in the DR Zone shall satisfy the off street parking
      requirements found in chapter 12 of this title. The spaces shall be hard surfaced
      with asphalt or concrete and be accessed from a public road by a hard surfaced,
      composed of asphalt or concrete, drive approach.
   2. Loading And Unloading Area: Loading and unloading areas shall be located in an
      area that can be secured from public access and screened from view. Further,
      loading and unloading shall not occur on a public street.(Ord. 2018-24, 9-26-2018)
