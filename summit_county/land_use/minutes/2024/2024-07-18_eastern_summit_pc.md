Rock quarries, gravel pits, and associated surface mining uses, including, but not limited to, filtering, sifting, and processing of soil.

Mimbach Presentation
Pat Putt,
Community Development Director
, informed the Commissioners about a phone call from an adjacent neighbor to parcel SS-70-11 regarding not receiving a courtesy notice about the public hearing on tonight's agenda. Mr. Putt asked the Commissioners how they wanted to proceed. Commissioners asked some follow-up questions, to which Mr. Putt responded. The Commissioners had the applicant present their request and continued the public hearing on this item to a later date.
Director Putt presented to the Commissioners a brief overview on the Conditional Use Permit for parcel SS-70-11.
Pete Gilwold,
Architect
, and Michael Mimbach,
Applicant
, provided further details and information on the proposed Conditional Use Permit.
Commissioners had some clarifying questions, to which Mr. Mimbach and Mr. Gilwold responded.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 7:04 PM.
Jan Perkins was called to speak.
Commission Chair Peterson continued the public hearing to a date certain of August 1, 2024.
Marion Wheaton made a motion to continue the public hearing on this Conditional Use Permit application to a date certain of August 1, 2024. Bill Wilde seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Don Sargent.
Commissioners had further comments and clarifying questions, to which Mr. Mimbach responded.  Discussion continued.
