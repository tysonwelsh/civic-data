                                              Workshop

Update on Engineering Projects

             Shane Ellis, Engineer, updated the Council on the following White City Metro
Township engineering projects:

   -   9400 South Ski Connect is in the design phase and is 90 percent complete. There are
       only enough funds to complete sidewalks on the east side of the road.

   -   Erin O’Kelley, Planner, has presented the Walk White City Plan to the Council at previous
       meetings. Any comments can be forwarded to Ms. O’Kelley.

   -   The Sego Lily design is 60 percent complete and continues to move forward. The plan is
       expected to be ready in March with construction taking place in summer or fall of 2023.
       Current cost estimates have come in higher than previously thought.

   -   Lighting for the canal trail will be installed in the spring.

   -   Applications for various grants have been submitted to Wasatch Front Regional Council.

   -   A Safe Routes to School grant application for sidewalk installation on Onxy Lane was
       submitted and awarded.

White City Financial Report

                Dave Sanderson, Financial Manager, stated 71 percent of White City Metro
Township’s allocated budget was used. That is 29 percent below budget. A few line items went
slightly over budget. The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (MSD) did well as a whole.
Any amount that was not spent will be rolled over to the capital projects fund.

              Council Member Price, seconded by Council Member Perry, moved to accept
White City Metro Township’s financial report. The motion passed unanimously.

Unified Police Department

                Detective Josh Smith reviewed calls for service for the month of January stating
there has been an increase in domestic violence and suspicious activity calls. He is getting emails
about incidents that are happening around White City, but police are not being called at the time
of the incident. It is important that incidents are reported so officers can document the individuals
involved.

                Chief Randy Thomas stated Hillcrest High School had an incident with a
trespassing juvenile that was carrying a gun. Situations like these are dangerous for officers inside
the schools because they do not have a radio signal to call for help. Because of incidents like
these, he is going to the capital to discuss legislation to require school districts to install repeaters
during construction of new schools. He is also working on getting repeaters inside current schools.

Public Comments

               Tyler Huish thanked the Council for giving him the opportunity to serve on the
Community and Economic Development Advisory Council (CEDAC). New pool safety

community pool may not meet the new requirements. The Council has discussed a change in
how candidates are voted for. The proposed changes will create more confusion and electors will

Per-Seat, Single Member At-Large Elections

                 Paul Ashton reviewed an ordinance requiring per-seat, single member at-large
 elections for the White City Metro Township Council. Rather than having candidates listed on
 the ballot and citizens voting for the top two or three candidates, they will run against a specific
 person. If the Council would like to consider this type of election, it will need to hold a public
 hearing to get citizen input.

               Mayor Flint suggested leaving the election as it is for this year.

                 Council Member Perry stated if the Council wants to consider changing the type
of election, it may want to conduct a survey to get feedback on what the residents want.

               Council Member Price stated she has asked several residents if they are
confused about how to vote the current ballot and they said no. She presented the new format to
these individuals and there was not a lot of positive feedback.

              Council Member Perry stated a postcard should be sent to residents educating
them on the process.

                Rori Andreason stated if the Council wants to change the election, it may want to
do it on a year when the mayor is being elected.

               Council Member Cardenaz stated residents need to be educated.

               Ms. Andreason stated she can get information out to the residents about the
election.

2023 Legislative Session and Proposed Bills

             Paul Ashton stated the Unified Police Department (UPD) bill has been filed and
has been made public. There are concerns with the current language in the bill that needs to be
amended to define certain aspects of the bill.

Utah League of Cities and Towns (ULCT) are watching various changes in the law. ULCT has a
legislative policy committee that meets on Mondays. He sits as a representative for White City for
that committee. Two additional Council Members can join the committee. The Legislature is in its
third week and Council Members can listen virtually.

South Salt Lake Valley Mosquito Abatement District

                Council Member Price stated the Board nominated a new Chair and Vice Chair.
The district is in the process of hiring for the 2023 season. Those interested can apply on its
website. The annual number of reported West Nile Virus cases were lower than in previous years.
Within the district, two human cases were reported, five cases in the state, and no deaths. The
manager is retiring this year and they are looking into hiring a new general manager. There is a
trial for new chemicals that will be less harmful to the environment but effective in killing
mosquitos.

Council of Governments (COG)

              Council Member Price stated she attended the COG meeting. It had elections

Discussions with UDOT on the gondola are ongoing and it is preparing for final presentations.

Unified Police Department (UPD)/Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area (SLVLESA)

                Council Member Cardenaz stated the UPD Board appointed Mayor Stevenson,
Midvale, as Chair and Council Member Brems, Emigration Canyon Metro Township, as Vice

Sheriff Rivera have stepped down from SLVLESA. The Board is working on other solutions for
