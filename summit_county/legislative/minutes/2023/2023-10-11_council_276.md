Canice Harte made a motion to convene as the Governing Board of the North Summit Special Recreation District. Christopher Robinson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Roger Armstrong, Tonja B Hanson.
1 ) Notification of the North Summit Recreation Special Service District's intent to levy a tax rate which exceeds the District's certified tax rate in accordance with Utah Code Section 59-2-919(3)(a). The District intends to increase 2024 property taxes in the amount of $75,078.35, which is an approximate 70.49% increase, for the purpose of funding a full-time Director position, funding a part-time Assistant Director position, keeping up with the rising costs of goods and services, overseeing the construction of Beacon Hill Park phase one, and providing for the operations and maintenance of Beacon Hill Park (once opened); Charity Richins (3:54 PM)
