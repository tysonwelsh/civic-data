Regional Transportation Plan Draft Fiscally Constrained Project Lists - Shawn Eliot, MAG The Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) is updated every four years and the next update called TransPlan50 will be complete in June 2019. In developing the RTP, a list is created of the regional highway, transit and active transportation projects. Projects proposed in the plan are larger regional projects. Planned highways include widening projects on arterial and freeway facilities; transit includes bus rapid transit light rail, commuter rail and core bus; and active transportation includes trails and bike lanes. The draft will be used to produce new TransPlan50 documents and reports. Adjustments to the list can still be made, keeping in mind that the draft document is planned for approval in March 2019. 30-day public comment period will occur in April 2019, with final approval in June 2019. Once adopted, regional projects must be in the plan to receive funding in the TIP/STIP. MPO TAC reviewed the draft Fiscally Constrained Projects List in December 2018, proposing minor changes which include: • Pleasant Grove BLVD #21 | Change description from new to widening • Lehi 2100 N FWY #13 | Lower cost from $320m to $311m • New Project Mountain View FWY | Add capacity 2040-50 about $26m • Move freight rail projects from transit list to freight map in appendix • Light Rail #12 (needs map) | Show Geneva RD alignment through Lindon and Vineyard (as shown in past plan), show “Location to be Determined” from Vineyard to Orem Mayor Brunst expressed concern that due to the number of changes, cities need additional time to study the revised plan and recommended that it not be approved until then. MAG staff said the plan is not much different from when it was presented in September 2018 and suggested that the draft plan be approved in order to begin the process, assuring that changes can be made prior to public comment. Mayor Mann moved that the MPO Reginal Planning Committee, based on the MPO TAC motion and minor changes recommended by MAG staff, approve the draft Fiscally Constrained Projects List with the understanding that adjustments can be made. Mayor Wright seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously.
