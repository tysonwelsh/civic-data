                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 22-36

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A SOFTWARE AS
  A SERVICE AGREEMENT WITH TYLER TECHNOLOGIES FOR THE MUNIS
                      SOFTWARE PACKAGE

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any purchase that
exceeds $30,000 in a public meeting; and

      WHEREAS, the Administrative Services Director would like to proceed with Tyler
Technologies Munis Software; and
        WHEREAS, by approving this purchase, Council will need to approve $572,543.00 in a
future budget amendment.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute a Software as a Service Agreement
          with Tyler Technologies for the Munis Software Package.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

      PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
May, 2022 by the following vote:
