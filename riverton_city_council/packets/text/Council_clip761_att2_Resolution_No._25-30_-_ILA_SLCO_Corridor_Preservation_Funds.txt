                                      RIVERTON, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 25-30

            APPROVING RIVERTON CITY'S ENTRY INTO AN INTERLOCAL
                 AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE
                4311 WEST 12600 SOUTH CORRIDOR PRESERVATION

       WHEREAS, the Utah Local Cooperative Act (UTAH CODE ANN. § 11-13-101, et seq.)
provides that two local governmental entities are authorized to enter agreements with each other,
upon a resolution to do so by their respective governing bodies, to do what each agency is
authorized by law to perform; and

       WHEREAS, on December 20, 2021, the Salt Lake County Council of Governments
recommended the distribution of $50,288 from the Salt Lake County Corridor Preservation Fund to
Riverton City for the acquisition of property located at 4311 West 12600 South to support a future
road construction project; and

       WHEREAS, the County and the City desire to enter into the attached Interlocal Agreement
to provide for the transfer of said funds in accordance with state law.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. The Interlocal Agreement between Riverton City and Salt Lake County for the transfer of
      Corridor Preservation Funds in the amount of $50,288 is hereby approved and the Mayor is
      authorized to execute the agreement and any related documents necessary to carry out the
      intent of this resolution.

   2. This resolution, assigned No. 25-30, shall take effect immediately upon passage and
      acceptance as provided herein.

      PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 20th day of
May, 2025 by the following vote:
