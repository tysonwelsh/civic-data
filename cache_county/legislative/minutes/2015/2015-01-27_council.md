ITEMS OF SPECIAL INTEREST

> Review and Clarification of Previous 4 cent Sales Tax Discussion — Val Potter
explained that several people have misunderstood and believe he wants to raise the
property tax. The proposal was for a % cent sales tax which is something that the state
legislature allows counties to use for transportation needs. Potter feels the Council has
an obligation to solve transportation problems in the county. The state legislators are
receiving numerous proposals and do not have a clear cut vision of how to fund county
transportation needs. If the Council approves a % cent sales tax, the issue must go
before the voters for approval. The funds would be handled the same as the present
CCCOG process.

PUBLIC HEARING: JANUARY 27, 2015 — 5:30 P.M. - ARMOR STORAGE REZONE TO
COMMERCIAL ZONE Erik Merkley requesting a rezone of 25 acres from Agriculture (A10)
Zone to Commercial © Zone located approximately 4400 South Hwy 165, Nibley — Chris Harrild
reviewed the request for the council noting that Armor Storage originally said it would not
continue development on the site but has added to the storage facility and now want to expand
more. Hyrum objects to the rezone and asks the developer to wait until annexation to Hyrum is
feasible. Presently, there are several properties between Armor Storage and the Hyrum City
limits. The Planning Commission recommends approval, but did not submit any findings to
support their recommendation. Staff recommends denial based on the two findings that the
parcels are located within the City of Hyrum annexation area, State Code requires the county to
obtain input from Hyrum City regarding the rezone request, Hyrum City has specified objection
to this rezone request and most services required for the potential development would be and/or
are provided by an adjacent municipality.

Chairman Robison opened the Public Hearing and invited public comment.

Erik Merkley, manager of the property, and Curtis Knight, owner of the property, spoke in favor
of the rezone and said there was no promise made to not expand the facility. Knight stated
Hyrum has been supportive of the past expansions. Much money has been expended to meet
all the county requirements and an expansion is needed to recoup those expenses.

Wayne Jewkes, property owner west of the units, David Frandsen, a Nibley resident, both
oppose the rezone based on ground water problems and aesthetics, respectively.

Korene Knight stated this is not about expansion, but a rezone request and the value of the
property.

Dave Nielsen said Hyrum, Nibley and Cache County should be concerned about any and all
development along 4400 South in the context of the bigger transportation corridor issue.

There was no other public comment.

ACTION: Motion by Council member Yeates to close the Public Hearing. Potter
seconded the motion. The vote was unanimous, 7-0.
