Re: Crosspoint CSD - Zoning Text and Zoning Map Amendments Requests Comments: This application is a request for approval of a zoning text and zoning map amendment for approximately 17.26 acres located at approximately 166 E. Highland Drive.  The property is currently zoned CR.  The applicant is requesting to rezone the property to create a new commercial district called the Crosspoint Commercial Special District (CSD-CP).  The proposed CSD-CP zone will be located in Chapter 9-18 (Commercial Special Districts) in the DCMC.   The subject property is vacant and has never been developed.  The site has been zoned as CR since at least 2001. The findings are as follows: Text Amendment:
The findings for a positive recommendation for a text amendment are as follows:
1. That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the text of the DCMC.
2. That this text amendment does not compromise any of the six factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
3. That the proposed text change is consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
The findings for a negative recommendation for a text amendment are as follows:
1. That this text amendment compromises one or more of the six factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
2. T hat the proposed text change is not consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
Map Amendment
The findings for a positive recommendation for a map amendment are as follows:
1. T hat this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
