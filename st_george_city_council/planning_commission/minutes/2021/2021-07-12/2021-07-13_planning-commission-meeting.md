   1. CONDITIONAL USE PERMIT (CUP) Administrative

       Consider a request for a conditional use permit to operate a short-term rental within the Thomas Cottam
       Home which has been designated as a local landmark. The project is located at 464 E. 500 S. Street
       (Please see map on back). The applicant is Jason Lindsey, NOI DUE, LLC. 2021-CUP-009. (Staff –
       Carol Davidson)

       Carol Davidson presented the following:

       Carol Davidson – The zoning is R-1-8, the property is .20 acres. This home was declared a local
       landmark back in May by the City Council. There is a deck in the back, the applicants are in process of
       building an accessory dwelling unit and a 2-car garage as well. The garage can be accessed as just a
       garage. There are 5 conditions that must be met for short term rentals, it must be low impact, the home
       is small, and it will be closely managed. The accessory dwelling unit will be lived in by one of the
       owners on site. The second condition is that it must be a substantial investment, the owner did a
       complete remodel, is adding the off-street parking and is updating the landscaping. The third condition
       is they must keep the historical significance. The fourth is minimal traffic and parking demand. They
       will use one of the stalls in the garage for parking the short-term rental. They will pave the current
       gravel driveway. The next one is any conditions; staff’s recommendation is that they make sure the
       short-term rental have access to the garage.

       Commissioner Larsen – Do they currently occupy the property in the back?

   2. GENERAL PLAN AMENDMENT (GPA) (Public Hearing) Legislative

      Consider a request to change the General Plan from Low Density Residential (LDR) to Commercial
      (COM) on approximately 8.83 acres generally located at the south east corner of 900 South Street and
      River Road. The applicant is Blue Mountain Holdings. 2021-GPA-009. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

      Carol Davidson presented the following:

      Carol Davidson – The zoning on this property is currently R-1-10 with LDR as the general plan. The
      applicants would like to change it into commercial. They would like to eventually clear the property and
      build some offices, maybe some mixed use. The property on the corner is LDR also but it is zoned PD-
      C now, we didn’t have permission from those owners or the owners of the small little chunk here that is
      why it’s shaped the way it is. In looking at this staff believes that professional office might be a better
      fit, however we understand that the applicant would not want to be restricted in uses as they were
      considering mixed use as well. Staff does think that commercial would work as well, but PO would be a
      better fit.

      Commissioner Kemp – Who’s property is it that isn’t included? If it is the Blake’s he wrote an email to
      include it and do it all at once.

      Jeff Morby – There are actually 5 owners that are involved. The Blake family were not against it but
      they didn’t choose to join us. Our conversations were positive, but maybe we didn’t explain it very
      well. I think they were concerned their property taxes would go up. We own the property next door
      where the doctor’s office is now, we felt with the new road coming in we thought this would be a good
      fit now. We feel like the commercial aspect gives us a little flexibility on what we would like to do
      here.

      Chair Draper opened the public hearing.

      Pat Smith – Some of my neighbors and I have been concerned because they haven’t designated what
      they want to put there. Some have stated that they may put low residential, someone said that. We want
      to know if they will try to put apartments in there. If they can just keep in mind that the apartments
      would be a concern.

      Carol Lagowski – Not only was the concern voiced by the neighbors of what will go in there but using
      our property as a through way, we have streets that go right through to Foremaster. We are concerned
      about 900 S. There is going to be a nursing home, and we would like to see a road go from Foremaster
      to Riverside so they would avoid going through our property and that is my concern.

      Beth Nahino – I live across the street on River Road, I was concerned about the possibility of apartments
      being put there. It would bring congestion to River Road and 900 South. There are so many smaller
      communities that are housing a lot of our seniors. It would be a disaster to add an apartment complex.
      It would affect the seniors getting to the bank and to the store. We seniors are not helpless, but we do
      need help from time to time. I would just like the senior communities to be considered before any
      apartments would be considered on this property.

      Chair Draper closed the public hearing.
