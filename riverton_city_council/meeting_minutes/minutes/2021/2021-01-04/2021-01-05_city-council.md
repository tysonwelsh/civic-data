Discussion was held regarding the delegation of health issues back to the cities and rather

Representative Pierucci said that between April and July 2021 the entire state population

         SLR Update - Mr. Dan McCay

Mr. McCay presented an update regarding the Suburban Land Reserve (SLR) property as it
relates to the Center Cal Project. He said that Center Cal had to adjust their work schedule due
to COVID and that it was now moving forward on Phase Two. He also said that the nearby
Costco project had finalized the initial agreement phase and was now moving forward. He said
they were now entering into the new development phase with Edge Homes building an
anticipated 350 units, which were anticipated to be permitted in June. He said the area of land
connected with Herriman land, with an anticipated 1,500 units and three large parks there. He
said SLR had engaged Edge Homes to build on the Herriman side and ground had already
been broken. He said there was a great demand for housing in the community.

A discussion regarding the 16 acres of designated open space required in the Master
Development Agreement occurred. Councilmembers will discuss their preference for location
and use of these spaces. This discussion includes a possible joint venture with Jordan School
District for a swimming pool to be used by the three nearby high schools.

Mr. McCay stated that SLR was committed to the City’s open space requirements and said the
City needed to determine how and where the open spaces could be filled.

Mayor Staggs spoke of Jordan School District providing a community center, which had been
projected to have up to 3,400 residents; however, a community center plan anticipated by SLR
was projected to have up to 2,000 residents. Councilmembers said they would discuss the
proposed ideas for the open space.

         Ordinance No. 21-01 - Proposed Rezone of the Existing 1.4 Acre Commercial
         Development at 2998 West 12600 South from C-PO to C-G (Commercial Gateway) -
         Gorm Klungervik, Applicant

Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained an application for rezone of
property located at 2998 West 12600 S. He said the property was an approved commercial
development currently zoned C-PO. He said that a single building had been built, with several
open pads remaining. He said the project was approximately 1.4 acres, and the property to the
north and east was zoned RR-22, although a portion of the property to the east was the
Riverton High School site. He said the property to the west was zoned C-PO.

Mr. Lethbridge said the development was designed and approved with five pads intended for
medical office use. He said that following the construction of the existing building, the owner had
not had tenant interest sufficient to fill the other pad spaces; however, he approached the City
with potential tenants, but many had been more retail in nature than the Professional Office
zone allows. He said that, in response, the owner submitted an application requesting a zone
change from Professional Office to Commercial Gateway to expand the range of uses available
to the project.

Mr. Lethbridge said that on December 10, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing
to consider this item and voted to recommend approval of the rezone application. He then
addressed comments and questions from the Council Members.

Mayor Staggs opened a Public Hearing and called for public comment; there being none, Mayor
Staggs declared the Public Hearing closed.

Councilmember McCay MOVED that the City Council adopt Ordinance No. 21-01, the
Klungervik Rezone, rezoning the subject properties located at 2998 West 12600 South
from C-PO (Commercial-Professional Office) to C-G (Commercial Gateway).
Councilmember Buroker SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the
motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as follows:
Councilmembers Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, McDougal-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The
motion passed unanimously.

         Ordinance No. 21-03 – Proposed Rezone of Approximately Two Acres Located at
         12814 South Sunday Drive from RR-22 to R-3, Eric Shroff, Applicant

Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that the public hearing was for a
rezone of approximately two acres located at 12814 Sunday Drive at the southeast corner of
Sunday Drive and Dutchman Lane (about 12800 South and 4400 West). He said the property
was approximately 180-ft by 484-ft, with the long side facing Dutchman Lane. He said there was
an existing house and a large shed on the property.

Mr. Lethbridge said the current zoning of the property is R-1, which is a Single Family zone with
animal rights and 40,000-sf lots. He said the undeveloped property to the south and west was
zoned RR-22, as were the houses to the north in the Kyson Creek Subdivision. He said that to
the east was the Cottages at Western Springs with an R-4-SD zoning, wherein the SD

other properties to the south on the west side of Sunday Drive were previously rezoned to R-3.

to consider the proposed rezone and voted to recommend approval of the rezone application.

         Ordinance No. 21-02 - DEJ Holdings Rezone, proposed rezone of 8.32 acres at
         12761 S 3600 W from C-PO & M-1 to C-PO/EHOV (Professional Office with Elderly
         Housing Overlay), Dee Bangerter, Applicant

Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained that the public hearing was
regarding an application for a proposed rezone of 8.32 acres located at 12761 South 3600
West. He said the property was currently zoned M-1 (Light Industrial) and C-PO (Commercial
Professional Office). He said the applicant was proposing rezone to C-PO with the Elderly
Housing Overlay. He said the properties to the north were zoned RR-22, with the majority of that
land currently occupied by a church meetinghouse. He said the property to the east was zoned
C-PO/EHOV. He said the property to the south was zoned C-PO and M-1, and is the Stamp’n
Up campus, and to the west, across 3600 West, was the IHC Hospital campus.

Mr. Lethbridge said that on December 10, 2020, the Planning Commission held a public hearing
to consider the proposed rezone and voted at that meeting to recommend denial of the rezone
application.

Mr. Lethbridge addressed comments and questions from the Council Members.

Ben Oveson, Rocky Mountain Care, stated his objectives in developing the property and the
plan for helping Riverton to increase its senior housing options.

Mayor Staggs opened a Public Hearing and called for public comment;

Troy Rushton, resident, stated in full disclosure that he was a current member of the Planning
Commission, as well as a resident of the area, and he stated his opposition to the proposed
rezone.

Mayor Staggs declared the Public Hearing closed.

Councilmembers discussed the need for further discussions regarding elderly housing
expectations and requirements.
