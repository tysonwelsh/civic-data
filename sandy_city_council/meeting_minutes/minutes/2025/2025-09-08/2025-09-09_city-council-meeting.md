Prayer, Pledge of Allegiance, and Introductions

                                  Council Member Brooke D'Sousa welcomed those in attendance.

                                  Shane Pace, CAO, offered the Prayer.
                                  Council led the Pledge.

                                  Council moved to Item 1 on the Agenda.

General Citizen Comment Period (No earlier than 6:00 PM)

                                  Council Chair Brooke D'Sousa invited the public to participate in General Citizen
                                  Comment.

                                  Mr. Steve Andrue has been a resident of Sandy for 16 years. He commented on traffic
                                  noise along Wasatch Blvd and the surrounding area. His complaints have gone
                                  unanswered. There is also excessive speeding. He asked the Council to please address
                                  the problem. He can provide additional information if needed.

                                  Mr. Dave Egelund expressed his support for Emergency Manager, David Larsen. He has
                                  attended Chief Larsen's emergency preparedness meetings and thinks all residents
                                  would benefit from going to these meetings. You all will learn how to be prepared during
                                  an emergency.

                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Following General Citizen Comment, Council moved to Standing Reports.

1.         25-279                 Emergency Management Division presenting the Salt Lake County
                                  Multi-Jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Plan (MJHMP) to the City Council
                   Attachments:   2025 MJHMP Executive Summary
                                  Sample Ordinance
                                  Sample Ordinance Approved 2- Draper
                                  Sample Ordinance Approved 3- Millcreek
                                  Sample Ordinance Approved- 4 Salt Lake County

                                  David Larsen, Sandy City Emergency Manager, presented the Salt Lake County
                                  Multi-Jurisdictional Hazard Mitigation Plan (MJHMP) to the Council. The cities in Salt
                                  Lake County are all under one plan which allows for greater continuity. Salt Lake County
                                  has taken the lead on this effort. Local jurisdictions are required to adopt a current hazard
                                  mitigation plan to maintain eligibility for certain Federal Emergency Management Agency
                                  (FEMA) funding programs. This is an information item and will come back to the Council
                                  for consideration and action at a future council meeting.

3.         25-270                 Second Reading: Council Members Robinson and Stroud recommending
                                  the establishment of the Sandy City Council Civic Engagement Award
                                  Program
                     Sponsors: Robinson and Stroud

                   Attachments:   Signed_Resolution_25-43C
                                  Memo_Sandy City Council Civic Engagement Award
                                  Criteria For The Sandy City Council Civic Engagement Award
                                  Resolution_Sandy City Council Civic Engagement Award

                                  Council Members Robinson and Stroud presented a proposal to expand the Council's
                                  current award program by adding a new award given out annually at the Noal Bateman
                                  dinner. The proposed award would be titled The Sandy City Civic Engagement Award.
                                  Council Members Stroud and Robinson provided details regarding the nomination and
                                  selection process for the proposed award. Tracy Cowdell offered insight on the expanded
                                  award program. The Noal Bateman Committee was overwhelmingly in favor of the
                                  proposed award and the expansion of the annual award dinner and program. Council
                                  questions and comments followed. If established, the first award would be given
                                  posthumously to Donald Gerdy, the inspiration behind the development of this new award.
                                  The inaugural award would be given out next week at the Noal Bateman dinner.

                                  Public comment opened.
                                  Public comment closed.

                                  Following the vote on the motion, Council moved to General Citizen Comment.
                                  A motion was made by Marci Houseman, seconded by Alison Stroud to adopt
                                  Resolution 25-43C, a resolution establishing the Sandy City Council Civic
                                  Engagement Award...The motion carried by the following roll call vote:

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Zach Robinson thanked Public Works staff and Police for their
                    responsiveness to residents' concerns.

                    Council Member Marci Houseman recognized the Wildland Fire Crews for their hard work
                    and service. She acknowledged all who helped coordinate the Healing Fields event. She
                    thanked the Administration and city for their outreach and communication with the
                    residents. She also spoke about the HTRZ zone submittal by the Economic Development
                    Agency.

                    Council Member Alison Stroud learned about HTRZ zone from the Economic
                    Development staff and she thanked them for their efforts. Chief Larson will be speaking to
                    the Sandy Youth Council this Thursday about emergency preparedness. She attended
                    the Public Works Roadeo event - a great event. The Healing Fields is being set up this
                    evening. She assisted with the Police Canine trials and acknowledged the hard work of
                    our officers and canines.

                    Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer attended the Public Works Roadeo event. It was a
                    great event that builds camaraderie among the staff. The Bulk Waste program started
                    yesterday in his neighborhood. He thought the communication from the city was great
                    and he appreciated the program. He spoke about upcoming school district meetings to
                    discuss new boundaries. He told Mr. Andrue that he wanted to connect with him to
                    discuss his concerns with traffic and noise.

                    Council Member Cyndi Sharkey attended the National Day of Service. She thanked the
                    city's Service Ambassadors and Department Heads for attending these events. The event
                    was attended by Congressman Owens and other dignitaries. She spoke about the traffic
                    and noise issues along Wasatch Blvd.

Mayor's Report

                    Mayor was excused.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, spoke about the service project at Alta High School. It was a great
                    success. Council Member Stroud acknowledged Deputy Mayor, Kim Bell, for her help
                    with the service project.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 6:32 pm.
