  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 11.330. Vacating, Altering, or Amending a Recorded Subdivision Plat. (Amended 01/23/24)

The Planning Commission, following review by the Reviewing Departments, shall be the Land Use Authority for
approving any proposed vacation, alteration, or amendment of a subdivision plat, any portion of a subdivision
plat, or any road or lot contained in a subdivision plat. The Planning Commission shall follow all requirements
and procedures identified in Utah State Code Section 10-9a-608.

  REPORT ANALYSIS

The applicant is requesting approval of a plat amendment to the Huerta Subdivision Plat A. This plat amendment is
proposing to vacate Lot 1 of the Huerta Subdivision and create EV Auto Subdivision Plat A, which would be the
consolidation of four existing lots included in the site plan.

This plat amendment is needed prior to the construction of EV Auto so that the building is not built across property
lines. This will clean up the parcels and one address will be assigned to the entire property.

The DRC had five redline comments, including the following:
