   3.4 Updates                from      the Planning             Director
   Francis         Lilly      said the 2026           legislative            session         concluded          on March              6, and several              new
   mandates           will      soon      appear     in Millcreek's               upcoming              code update.                Notably,          cities     are now
   required         to include           ethics     training         in annual          Planning          Comtnission                 sessions,         covering          ex parte
   communications                     and conflicts           of interest.           Staff    is coordinating                 with     the Utah          chapter        to
   develop          a brief      ethics        module,        supplementing                  existing      training.           Additionally,             new      legislation
   allows      city         councils      to approve           or deny         land-use          applications               without       waiting         for Planning
   Commission                 review       if sufficient         time        has elapsed,             prompting             staff    to draft         corresponding               code
   language.          Recent           state law     also mandates                   that cities        include         provisions             for detached            accessory
   dwelling          units,      which         Millcreek        already         has in place.            A temporary,                 narrowly           targeted         statute
   addressing              conflicts      between          height       and story            limits     for a specific               project      in Wasatch             or
   Suminit         County         was also passed                but will        expire        in 2027.         Staff       is concurrently               preparing           a
   second      round           of zoning          ordinance          amendments,                including         updating             senior      housing         parking
   ratios     to match           multi-family             standards           and improving               enforceable                tree preservation              language.
