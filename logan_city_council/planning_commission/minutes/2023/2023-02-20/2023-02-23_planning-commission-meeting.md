1. All standard conditions of approval will be recorded with the Conditional Use Permit and are
available in the Community Development Department.

unit of a fourplex.

3. This Conditional Use Permit authorizes two (2) people per bedroom with two (2) additional

PC 23-006 Land Development Code Amendment_17.07, 17.08, 17.10 (Residential_&

Commercial Standards) -Continued from the February 9" meeting. [Code Amendment] Mike
DeSimone/Logan City are requesting a Code Amendment to the Land Development Code, Chapter
17.07-Specific Development Standards; Residential Zones to include transparency, useable outdoor
space, open space & landscaping requirements, and minimum lot size in MR-12; amend Chapter
17.08-Neighborhood Residential Zone Uses to clarify permitted residential structure types in NC
Zone; and amend Chapter 17.10 Specific Development Standards; District and Corridor Zones to
updated useable outdoor space, open space, and landscaping requirements, and include min
building heights, min lot coverage, eliminate drive-thru’s and modify commercial parking
requirements in TC zone.


STAFF/ PROPONENT: Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director reviewed the proposed
amendment to the following chapters in the Land Development Code:

Chapter 17.07-Residential Development Standards. Updated open space/landscaping/useable
outdoor space. Eliminated the maximum height of 15 feet on accessory buildings, added 25 percent
transparency requirement to front facades in the Mixed Residential zones, added minimum lot width
of 40 feet and a minimum lot size of 4,000 square feet for single-family residences in the Mixed
Residential Low (MR-12) zone, updated the Neighborhood Center (NC) zone to include parking
standards, no drive-through’s, transparency, landscaping, and useable outdoor space requirements.

Chapter 17.08-Residential Uses. Updated structure type in the Neighborhood Center (NC) zone to
permit Single Family Residential and Conditional Use Permit for Multi Family Residential.

Chapter 17.10. Commercial Development Standards. Updated the spec sheet to match the open
space, landscaping, and useable outdoor space requirements.

Chapter 17.10.060 Town Center 1 Development Standards. Updated the minimum lot coverage to
50 percent on lots fronting Main Street and 400 North. Updated the minimum building of 27.5 feet
on all street frontages and a minimum of a two-stories. Updated the transparency requirements,
design specificity requiring four-sided architecture, pedestrian entry to the street, and accessory
architectural features. Prohibited drive-thru lanes in the Town Center 1 (TC-1) zone, and added a
commercial parking reduction of 50 percent for lots fronting Main Street and 400 North with a
minimum lot coverage of 50 percent.

Commissioner Peterson asked about the requirement for an entrance on all sides. M. DeSimone
said there needs to be a pedestrian entrance facing the street to help create a presence and a
pedestrian-friendly, walkable downtown.

Commissioner Peterson asked if the maximum building height should be the same, 35 feet in all
zones regardless of the lot size. M. DeSimone said that the thought was that a homeowner should
be able to build an accessory building that is the same height as their home.

Commissioner Doutre asked if there are additional accessory building requirements other than what
is listed in the Neighborhood Design Standards. M. DeSimone said there are additional standards
for detached garages and accessory buildings in chapter 17.09.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: The Commission discussed the proposed 35-foot accessory building height and if it
should be 25 feet. The Commission also discussed removing the required 50 percent minimum lot
coverage along Main Street and 400 North and allowing a parking reduction if a development meets
50% lot coverage and 25% useable outdoor space.

MOTION: Commissioner Peterson made a motion to Continue 23-006 to the March 9, 2023 meeting
to address the concerns mentioned. Commissioner Doutre seconded the motion.

Moved: E. Peterson Seconded: S. Doutre Approved: 5-0
Yea: Peterson, Croshaw, Guth, Lewis, Doutre Nay: Abstain:


PC 23-010 Land Development Code Amendment 17.20 & 17.33 (Signs & Murals) -
Continued from the February 9" meeting. [Code Amendment] Mike DeSimone/Logan City are
requesting a Code Amendment to the Land Development Code, Chapter 17.20-Historic District
Overlay Zone, and Chapter 17.33-Signs to update regulatory standards for murals/wall art in the
Historic District and other minor regulatory amendments to the sign code.

STAFF/ PROPONENT: Mike DeSimone, Community Development Director reviewed the requested
updates from the commission during the last meeting to put a time limit on the display of
political/campaign signs and said that after looking into this further the proposed 60-day display
period in 17.33.110.C was removed altogether due to a 2015 U.S. Supreme Court Decision that
stated municipalities cannot treat certain signs differently based on their purpose and message.

PUBLIC: No public comments were made.

COMMISSION: Commissioner Doutre asked if the “per calendar year” should be removed from
section 17.33.110.C.2 to make it clear that only one sign per message can be up at any time
regardless of the calendar year. Mohammed Abdullahi, Assistant City Attorney said that as it is
written, it states that at any one time, you can display one sign per message. Russ Holley, Senior
Planner said that the wording “per calendar year” is what makes it a temporary sign.

MOTION: Commissioner Doutre moved to forward a recommendation of approval to the
Municipal Council for a code amendment on PC 23-010 with the finding for approval as listed below.
Commissioner Croshaw seconded the motion.

FINDINGS FOR APPROVAL

1. Utah State Law authorizes local Planning Commission to recommend ordinance changes to the
legislative body (Municipal Council).

2. The Code Amendments are done in conformance with the requirements of Title 17.51 of the
Logan Municipal Code.

3. The proposed Code Amendments are consistent with the Logan City General Plan.

4. The proposed Code Amendments to the Historic District Overlay Zone and the Sign Code are
relatively minor in nature.

5. No public comment has been received regarding the proposed amendments.

Moved: S. Doutre Seconded: R. Croshaw Approved: 5-0
Yea: Peterson, Croshaw, Guth, Lewis, Doutre Nay: Abstain:
