Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Presentation - Park City Junction
Peter Barnes,
Community Development Director
, introduced the proposed Park City Junction Architectural Design Standards for initial review and discussion. (6:15 P.M.)
Steve Borup,
DPRE Director of Commercial Development
, introduced himself and gave a brief introduction of the AO Architecture Design team. (6:24 P.M.)
Rob Budgetti, Managing Partner, and Richard Clarke, Design Partner with AO, presented the project planning elements and design narrative for discussion. (6:26 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley asked questions about UDOT’s interaction with the bridge and the reasons behind the mountain modern design choice. (6:48 P.M.)
Commissioner Nagie asked for clarification on pedestrian connectivity and circulation, signage, and EV charging. (6:54 P.M.)
Commissioner Peteroy requested clarification on the local flavor of design elements, locations for snowmelt heating systems, snow removal management and processes, and amphitheater scale. (7:12 P.M.)
Commissioner Young asked the applicants about pedestrian access and bicycle pathways, ways to bring the public in without the need for cars, and a follow-up on EV chargers. (7:29 P.M.)
Commissioner Jeffrey commented on the Mountain Modern design choice, the ability to change the name of Tech Center Drive, and pedestrian rights of way. (7:38 P.M.)
Commissioner Sagerman asked for clarification regarding an image included in the presentation, the possibility of integrating commercial and residential communities, and inquired about how to design something that would look good in 100 years. (7:44 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney commented on mountain modern and the idea of creating something that is warm and charming, the tech drive character, transit center concerns, and commercial retail color coding. (7:54 P.M.)
Commissioners asked final questions. The applicants thanked the commissioners for their time and comments. (8:02 P.M.)
