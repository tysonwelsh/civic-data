        6. INFORMATION.
              a. Police Department Accreditation Process
                 Conducting Council Member Williams announced that this item would be
                 moved to the next meeting due to unforeseen circumstances that
                 prevented it from being prepared in time.

                  b. Fraud Risk Analysis
                     Finance Director, Crystal Makin, reviewed the results of the City’s Fraud Risk
                     Analysis. The finance department assesses the City’s risk of financial fraud
                     each year. Criteria are provided by the Office of the State Auditor, and they
                     are used to determine if there is adequate separation of duties in functions
                     like cash handling, purchasing, and payroll.

                  c. Victim Services Federal Grant Update
                     Victim Services Supervisor, Jodi Fait, reviewed the statistics of the Victim
                     Services Division’s services in the last year and how many individuals were
                     served.
