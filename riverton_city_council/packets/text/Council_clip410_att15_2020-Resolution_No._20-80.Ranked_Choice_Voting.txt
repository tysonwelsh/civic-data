                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 20-80

 A RESOLUTION REQUESTING MUNICIPAL ELECTION SERVICES FROM SALT
  LAKE COUNTY TO PARTICIPATE IN THE MUNICIPAL ALTERNATE VOTING
                    METHODS PILOT PROJECT

        WHEREAS, The Utah Legislature has made available a pilot project for participation in
the alternate voting methods for municipal elections beginning January 1, 2019 and ending on
January 1, 2026, known as Municipal Alternate Voting Methods Pilot Project, or ranked choice
voting; and

         WHEREAS, House Bill 35, as passed during the 2018 General Session of the Utah
Legislature, allows municipalities to conduct nonpartisan races using ranked choice voting in
accordance House Bill 35, codified in Utah Code Section 20A-4-601 et seq of the Utah Code and
all other applicable provisions of law, during any odd-numbered year that the pilot project is in
effect, and

        WHEREAS, House Bill 277, as passed during the 2019 General Session of the Utah
Legislature, amended Utah Code Section 20A-4-601 thereby enabling municipalities to provide a
notice of intent to participate in the Municipal Alternate Voting Methods Pilot Project to the
office of the lieutenant governor by April 15 of the odd numbered year in which a municipal
election occurs; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to said House Bill 277, a notice, if submitted, may also be
withdrawn by April 15 of the odd numbered year in which a municipal election occurs; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 20A-4-601 also requires that the notice of intent to
participate in the Municipal Alternate Voting Methods Pilot Project include a document signed
by the municipal election officer stating that the municipality has the resources and capability
necessary to participate in the pilot project; and

       WHEREAS, House Bill 277 amended Utah Code Section 20A-5-400.1 to permit
municipalities to enter into a contract with any other local political subdivision of the state,
regardless of the other political subdivision’s location, to conduct an election during which the
municipality is participating in the Municipal Alternative Voting Methods Pilot Project; and

        WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the feasibility of
participating in the Municipal Alternative Voting Methods Pilot Project hinges, in part, upon the
ability of the
participating municipality to obtain cost effective election services to conduct such an election;
and

      WHEREAS, Riverton City ordinarily contracts with Salt Lake County to receive
municipal election services from the County; and
         WHEREAS, Riverton City desires to explore the possibility of obtaining municipal
election services from Salt Lake County to participate in the Municipal Alternative Voting
Methods Pilot Project, but may have to seek election services form another political subdivision
if Salt Lake County cannot provide a ranked choice voting system;

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

   1. The Riverton City Council hereby expresses its interest in pursuing participation in the
      Municipal Alternative Voting Methods Pilot Project for the 2021 municipal election.

   2. The Riverton City Council hereby expresses its desire to continue to maintain a contract
      with Salt Lake County to receive election services from the County, and directs the
      Riverton City Manager to initiate discussions with the Salt Lake County Clerk’s office to
      obtain pricing for elections services that facilitate the Municipal Alternative Voting
      Methods Pilot Project.

   3. The Riverton City Council hereby requests that the terms of pricing and a contract to
      perform election services for a ranked choice voting system in the 2021 municipal
      election be brought to the Riverton City Council for consideration with sufficient time for
      the Council to fulfill its noticing requirements with the lieutenant governor’s office.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 15th day of
December 2020 by the following vote:
