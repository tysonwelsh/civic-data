order by Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on August 18, 2020, in the Washington
County Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah.

Commissioner Dean Cox, Deputy County Attorney Natalie Nelson, County Administrator
Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy County Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

EMERGENCY SERVICES REPORT

CONSIDERATION OF ORDINANCE NO. 2020-1161-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING THE WASHINGTON COUNTY CODE TO ALLOW RESIDENTS
TO PARTICIPATE IN URBAN FARMING AND UTILIZE PROVISIONS OF THE
STATE CODE FOR SPECIAL ASSESSMENT

Land Team Coordinator Mark Tebbs explained that in order to qualify for urban farming,
property must be at least one (1) acre to 4.99 acres in size and must be used for cultivating
food or other marketable crop with a reasonable expectation of profit from the sale of that

crop.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Ordinance No. 2020-1161-O, An
Ordinance Amending the Washington County Code to Allow Residents
to Participate in Urban Farming and Utilize Provisions of the State
Code for Special Assessment. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson,
Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2020-2637, A RESOLUTION
ESTABLISHING AND APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY CONSTABLE NOMINATING COMMISSION

Commissioner Cox explained that there is a vacancy for a constable within Washington
County. A Constable Nominating Commission reviews the potential nominees and makes
a recommendation to the Commission as to which nominees should be appointed. The
Nominating Commission is in need of updates as some of the prior members no longer
serve or have expired. It is recommended to appoint Commissioner Gil Almquist, County
Attorney Eric Clarke, and private citizen Nicholle Felshaw to serve on the Nominating

Commission.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2637, A
Resolution Establishing and Appointing Gil Almquist, Eric Clarke, and
Nicholle Felshaw to the Washington County Constable Nominating
Commission. Motion seconded by Chairman Iverson and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox

voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2638, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING A GRANT DONATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON
COUNTY AND TUACAHN CENTER FOR THE ARTS

Chairman Iverson explained that the Tuacahn Center for the Arts has been negatively
impacted financially by the COVID-19 pandemic due to the temporary closure.
Washington County desires to provide Tuacahn with a grant of Coronavirus Relief Funds
to ensure the economic viability of Tuacahn during this time.

Tuacahn CEO Kevin Smith expressed appreciation for the grant funds and helping Tuacahn
to come back stronger for next season

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2638, A
Resolution Approving a Grant Donation Agreement Between
Washington County and Tuacahn Center for the Arts. Motion
seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote,
with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox voting aye.

APPROVING A CORONAVIRUS RELIEF FUNDS GRANT DONATION
AGREEMENT BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY AND HURRICANE
VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT

Commissioner Cox explained that the COVID-19 pandemic has had a financial toll on the
Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District as well. To cover some of the costs

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2020-2640, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE SOFTWARE AGREEMENT

Washington County Human Resources Director Johnathan Liddle explained that the
current employee performance software used by employees is in need of an update. A
Request for Proposal was published for qualified companies to provide employee
performance and evaluation applications or software. It is recommended for BCBB, Inc. to
provide the new software as they were the lowest bid most in-line with Washington
County’s needs.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Cox to Adopt Resolution No. R-2020-2640, A
Resolution Approving Employee Performance Software Agreement.
Motion seconded by Commissioner Almquist and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Almquist, and Cox
voting aye.
