                                        RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                        RESOLUTION NO. 20-27

 A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING UP TO 2.35 ACRES OF PROPERTY TO BE DEDICATED
   TO RIVERTON CITY AS PART OF THE PROPOSED “RIVER SPRINGS ESTATES’
   SUBDIVISION, SUBJECT TO FINAL PLAT APPROVAL BY THE RIVERTON CITY
                         PLANNING COMMISSION

         WHEREAS, Riverton City Code Section 18.110, Geologic Hazards Ordinance, regulates the
development of ground impacted by certain geologic hazards, and Section 18.110.050 allows for
clustering of residential lots as mitigation to such impacts; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission, has approved a preliminary plat for the
“River Springs Estates” subdivision, and determined that geologic hazards exist within that project area
that are subject to Section 18.110, and that clustering of lots within the project area and dedication of up
to 2.35 acres to Riverton City is a suitable mitigation for said geologic hazards; and

        WHEREAS, Section 18.110.050.(2).c of the Riverton City Code requires that “[l]and proposed
to be dedicated to the city must be formally accepted by the Riverton City council before the planning
commission can grant final approval to a subdivision plat which utilizes a density bonus”; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that acceptance of up to 2.35 acres
of land, as shown on the attached Exhibit “A”, is in the general public interest and compliant with the
requirements of Section 18.110;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council hereby accepts dedication of up to 2.35 acres of land within
          the River Springs Estates Subdivision as described herein, subject to approval of a final
          plat for that subdivision by the Planning Commission.
       2. Fencing shall be installed by the developer along the east boundary of Parcels A & D,
          with type as approved by the Planning Commission.
       3. The property will be delivered to the City free from debris or construction materials.
       4. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of April 2020
by the following vote:
