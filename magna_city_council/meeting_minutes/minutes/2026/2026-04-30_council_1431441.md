        1. CALL TO ORDER

Mayor Mick Sudbury, presiding, called the meeting to order at 4:05 pm.

        2. DETERMINE QUORUM

A quorum was present, allowing the meeting to proceed. Council Member Michael Jensen was excused
from the meeting.

        3. PRESENTATIONS
           A. Presentation on 4100 South Construction
           B. Presentation on Boundary Line Adjustment with West Valley City

Nathan Bracken explained that the road extension project had been in progress for several years and
involved multiple contributors prior to his role as City Attorney, including Mayor Mick Sudbury, former
City Attorney Paul Ashton, and former City Manager David Brickey. He described the project as
complex due to jurisdictional issues, multiple property owners, and funding challenges. The road would
primarily serve Magna, including the local high school, but was currently located within West Valley
City’s jurisdiction, requiring coordination and a municipal boundary adjustment. He stated that

developers are only obligated to fund infrastructure proportional to their impact, leaving a funding gap
that required public contributions. He reported that the City secured state funding at the end of the
legislative session on March 7, which allowed final approvals and agreements to move forward. He
detailed that West Valley City approved the plans on April 15 and that multiple parcels, including land
owned by Rio Tinto and Magna Water District, were being coordinated for access or transfer. He noted
Rio Tinto had agreed to allow construction access while final conveyance was pending. He summarized
the agreement, stating that DR Horton would construct the road extension from 8400 South to Little
Valley Gateway, with estimated costs ranging from $3.6 million to $5.5 million. The City would
reimburse DR Horton using up to $5.5 million in state funding, including $1.8 million previously
secured and $3 million from the recent legislative session. He added that DR Horton would contribute
$750,000 in escrow to cover any funding gap. He stated the target completion date was August 2026,
ideally before the school year began, though this was not guaranteed. He outlined coordination with
stakeholders including West Valley City, Magna Water District, UDOT, Rio Tinto, and the Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Services District, and explained that West Valley City would oversee construction until
the boundary adjustment was completed.

Mayor Mick Sudbury asked if council members had questions and, seeing none, invited representatives
from DR Horton to provide updates.

Adam Loser reported that DR Horton had been actively progressing toward construction. He stated that
a final UDOT meeting occurred on March 30, followed by a pre-construction meeting with Magna
Water District on March 31. He confirmed that final stamped plans were received from West Valley
City on April 15, and that Magna Water District received authorization on April 23 to begin water
infrastructure work. He noted that excavation had begun, including site cleanup and preparation, and
that coordination meetings with West Valley City were ongoing, with a formal pre-construction meeting
expected imminently. He explained that full construction had not yet intensified due to the need to avoid
disrupting traffic near the school, particularly on 8400 West, and that major work would begin after the
school year ended. He stated that initial work would proceed on other segments in the meantime and
expressed appreciation for the collaboration and funding efforts that allowed the project to move
forward.

        4. COUNCIL BUSINESS
          A. Discussion and potential action regarding approval of 4100 South extension contract with
             DR Horton

Council Member Prokopis moved to approve the above named agreement and authorize the
Mayor to sign the agreement on behalf of the City of Magna. Council Member George seconded
the motion; vote was 4-0, unanimous in favor with Council Member Jensen absent from the vote.

        5. ADJOURN

Council Member George moved to adjourn the April 30, 2026 City Council Special Meeting at
4:22 pm. Council Member Prokopis seconded the motion; vote was 4-0, unanimous in favor with
Council Member Jensen absent from the vote.
