Minutes of the field trip of the Ogden City Council held on Tuesday, September 28, 2021, at 2:30p.m., held at the Ogden
Business Exchange located on Exchange Road, Ogden City, Weber County, Utah.

         Present:          Chair                 Bart E. Blair

                                                 Richard A. Hyer

        Also present:      Planning Manager Greg Montgomery
                           Co:riununity and Economic Development Deputy Director Brandon Cooper

       The purpose of the field trip was to view the Ogden Business Exchange as part of a proposed change to the Business
Exchange Industrial (BEl) Zone requirements to allow warehousing up to 70,000 square feet, as well as other options to allow
warehousing space with site conditions to protect the open space and pedestrian trails in the area.


        The tour ended at 3:45p.m.
