2.2) Public hearing and consideration of Dennis Schoonover’s request for approval of the
Lehi Drive Thru concept plan, a commercial development located at 600 West State Street.
(Tabled from the June 10th meeting)
    Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Ellis asked for clarification on the fence and if would be full height. Brittney
    stated there is a DRC comment where the fence would have to be dropped so it wouldn't block
    the view. Commissioner Hemmert hoped the applicant would be aware of the impact the
    development would have with it being so close to State Street. Ross Dinsdale explained they
    looked at squaring up 600 West and State Street. Commissioner Eyre clarified the City would
    rather see the entry square up to the parking lot. Ross stated that was what was recommended.
    Kim Struthers stated the buffering requirements were being met.

    Aaron Wagner and Dennis Schoonover were present to represent the item. Aaron stated they
    have worked with the City to meet the recommendations and standards. Dennis expressed that
    he doesn't know what else to do with the property where they are a commercial zone and the
    property behind him is residential. Aaron stated the property meets the demographics and
    explained the plan for the development. Commissioner Newall asked about the old police
    station and what the applicant intended to do with that. Dennis stated he plans on renting out
    the space. Commissioner Nielsen asked about the garbage setback from the property line. Kim
    Struthers stated there isn't a minimum setback, and it is flexible. Commissioner Nielsen
    addressed the detention ponds and stated it was a lot of space to keep as detention. Dennis said
    he has talked to Ross Dinsdale about the detention pond.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

    Cory Gurney is opposed to the development. He stated he believed there was an agreement
    between the developers and the residents. He stated he is not against development but would
    like to see a barrier between the development and the residential area. He would like to see a
    different concept that works for both the developer and the neighborhood.

    Dave Barnhart stated the developer is downplaying the traffic and the issues that will come
    with it. He is concerned how the street will be able to maintain the traffic. Dave is also
    concerned about the lights from the vehicles. He also mentioned snow removal from the
    property and where the snow will go during the winter months.


    Melissa Nokes stated she is not against the development but would like to oppose the current
    concept plan. She made some recommendations regarding parking, and access to 600 West
    from State Street. She would like the commission to look at the concept moving forward.

    Brad Gurney stated he felt the development would be great, but he asked the Commission to
    keep the neighbors in mind.

    Hadley (inaudible), who works with the applicants, wanted to let the public know that he has
    tried to make an effort in contacting the residents to talk to work with the developer.

    Eric Towner with X Development stated he has worked with the developer and feels that they
    will do the very best to work with residents.

    Commissioner Nielsen wanted to recognize the online public comment. Commissioner Nielsen
    closed the public hearing.

    Aaron Wagner addressed the public comments. Dennis wanted to add they have worked with
    DRC and will do what the City requires. He stated they have utilized the suggestions and are
    doing the best they can with the development.

    Commissioner Nielsen stated he is on the fence and there would be a lot of variances that are
    required. Commissioner Nielsen stated the Commission is making an approval based on the
    site plan and there are mitigating issues that can hopefully be resolved. Commissioner
    Hemmert stated the lot for the development is unique and to turn it into something that is an
    amenity would be a challenge. His biggest concern is ingress and egress off State Street.
    Commissioner Ellis asked how many lanes would be on 600 West. Ross Dinsdale stated on
    street parking is allowed but striping would not be placed on the street because of the low
    volume. Ross stated parking can be accommodated on the site and the widening of the road
    will help with that. He stated a traffic study will be conducted to assess and evaluate and based
    on the study some changes may be made. Commissioner Ellis asked if there was access to 600
    West from the Rail Trail. There is not access from 600 West to the Rail Trail. Commissioner
    Newall stated she thinks this Urban Concept is appropriate for the area. Commissioner Eyre
    disagreed and does not feel this meets Urban Village.

        Motion: Commissioner Hemmert motioned to approve Dennis Schoonover’s request for
        approval of the Lehi Drive Thru concept plan, a commercial development located at
        600 West State Street, noting they have heard from the public a couple of time, as well as
        the input the applicant has heard; including all DRC comments and noting the Commission
        is generally amendable to the exceptions. Commissioner Ellis seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Ellis; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed 6 – 1.

2.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Ivory Development’s request for preliminary
subdivision review of the Holbrook Place Phase 5 & 6, a 102-lot residential development
located at 2700 North and Twin Flower Way.
    Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Nielsen asked if sidewalks were required on all sides. Brittney stated if it is an
    alleyway it is not required. Commissioner Ellis asked for clarification on City streets and
    private streets. Commissioner Nielsen asked if there was on street parking. Ross Dinsdale
    addressed the Commission and clarified where there was on street parking.

    The applicant, John Bowan, was present. He explained the shared drives and sidewalks create
    a nice feel to the neighborhood. Commissioner Everett asked if the crossings were going to
    be striped. The applicant stated they typically are if they are required by the City.
    Commissioner Newall asked if each residence had a separate driveway. Mr. Bowan stated

    Holbrook.

    comments.

        Flower Way; with the findings that it meets the plans similar to the Holdbrook plan and
        meets the development code, and with the caveat the lot sizes are appropriate to the

        required size; including all DRC and findings stated in the report. Commissioner
        Hemmert seconded. All in favor.

2.9) Public hearing and consideration of Lone Star Builders request for approval of the
Quick Quack Car Wash concept located at 2550 North 1200 West in a Commercial zone.
    Brittney Harris presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Nielsen asked about the drive aisle lining up with Holiday Oil and if that was
    a possibility. Ross Dinsdale responded they would like them to line up as close as possible.

    The applicant, Joseph Ernest, was present. He stated there were some additional exhibits that
    were submitted, but not included in the packet. He addressed Commissioner Nielsen's
    concerns, asking for exceptions with the Planning Commission's discretion per City code.
    They are currently waiting for Staff's recommendations on access sites. The applicant
    discussed parking. Ross Dinsdale talked about an exit point to the north of the property. The
    property adjacent to the item is owned by the City. The applicant is asking for and exception
    to not require north cross access, allowance for the building to be further than 25 feet from
    the frontage on 1200 West, and the columns to act as an architectural feature.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

    Ryan hales owns the property to the north. He stated there will be an office building to the
    north. He made several suggestions to the Commission that would appease both the
    developer and the City.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

    Commissioner Ellis asked if the alternate concept could be considered since it hadn't been
    submitted. Kim Struthers stated it could be accepted and just needed to be noted in the
    motion.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Lone Star Builders
        request for approval of the Quick Quack Car Wash concept located at 2550 North
        1200 West in a Commercial zone with the exceptions of the pillars and North drive
        aisle, the setback of the building, requirements for landscaping or wall on the north side
        for buffering, the access to the South stub requirements if the North was not accessible;
        including all DRC comments and positive findings stated in the report. Commissioner
        Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Hemmert,
        yes; Commissioner Ellis; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The motion passed 6 – 1.
