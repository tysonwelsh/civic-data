Matt Dahl explained the recruitment process for the Assistant City Manager position. He

Council for advice and consent.

Rules and Regulations amended August 2022:

Midvale City Cemetery Rules and Regulations Amended August 23,2022
After August 23,2022, any person owning or purchasing a grave in the Midvale City
Cemetery shall do so under the following amended rules and regulations:

1      The operations of the Cemetery are subject to the control of the Cemetery
       Superintendent or designee.

2      The price of Cemetery graves and fees shall be listed on the Midvale City Fee
       Schedule.

3      According to the schedule below, a 48-hour notice to the Cemetery office is
       required for all burial arrangements.

       a       For a burial on Monday or Tuesday, notification to the City is due on the
               prior Thursday by noon.
