PLEDGE OF ALLEGIENCE: Sterling Scholars/9-11 Committee
      The Sterling Scholars introduced themselves: Maxwell Hadlock—Math; Cailyn Cunningham—
      English and Literature; Rory Allred—Speech and Drama; Gabe Settle—Music. They then
      provided information about the 9/11 Day of Service on Saturday September 13. It begins at 9 am
      at the City Park. The day will include an American Legion 9/11 remembrance program and
      several service opportunities: community cleanup at the city park, hygiene kit donations and
      organization for local and international distribution, youth project of card making for care center
      residents and shut ins, CPR refresher course.
      Mayor Seely appreciates the youth engagement and the community service opportunities
      provided by the committee. He discussed the importance of community involvement and the
      positive impact of the event.

ARTS GRANT PRESENTATION—PAR TAX GRANT
      Mayor Seely presented Michelle Harris, representing the Juab Fine Arts Council (JFAC), with a
      check for $8,148 for the PAR Tax Grant. Each year the JFAC applies for this grant and over the last
      six years has been awarded $37,199. The mayor expressed appreciation for the time and effort
      JFAC does to bring arts to the community. Ms. Harris explained the positive impact of the par tax
      and highlighted activities and programs the donation supports: spring musical, murder mystery
      dinner theater, Nutcracker play, Art in the Park. Ms. Harris stated that all are welcome. Council
      Members commented on the positive results they have seen in relatives who have participated
      and thanked Ms. Harris for helping others grow and show and find their talents.


PUBLIC COMMENT
  • Brette Hawks—owns Salt Creek Cake in Nephi. She requested an update to the
      home occupation code 10.19.13. so she could obtain a license. She wanted the
      council to consider accepting the Utah Cottage License as well as the law HB 181
      which allows bakers to work without being inspected. The HB 181 law is more for
      made to order businesses whereas the Utah Cottage License is needed more for
      selling at events.
  • Chris O’Neil—He raised concerns about flooding in Chapel View Estates on 275 E
      Street and need for drainage improvements. Mayor Seely stated that with recent
      storms, much of the state has seen flooding. He asked if all the yards were
      completed in the area. Mr. O’Neil stated some were, but not all, and recognized that
      that may contribute to the problem with dirt running into and clogging the drains.
      Mr. O’Neil asked if those sumps have been cleaned. Mr. Atkinson stated they had
      not, but the stormwater fee that was just instituted will help to cover those costs
      moving forward. Mayor stated the street department would be informed of the
      issue.

Work Session
  1. Natural Gas Contract
         a. Mr. Atkinson explained the current hedge for our natural gas 3-year contract
            is about to end. Negotiations have been underway with different companies
            on pricing. ARM Energy is one of those companies.
         b. The hedge pricing from ARM Energy is significantly better than other
            companies. ARM Energy will also sell unused gas at a higher rate.
         c. Mr. Atkinson explained this is a good opportunity to create a rate stabilization
            fund and allow rates to go down which will give negotiating power in the
            future as well as options right now.
         d. Planning on starting with a one-year contract to see how the change goes. Mr.
            Atkinson suggested the council look over the contract and it will be brought
            back for approval in a future council meeting.
         e. David Huffines, representing ARM Energy, was in attendance and described a
            little more about the company and what they have to offer. When they sell
            the unused gas, 80% of the sale goes to the city and 20% to ARM Energy.
         f. Zach Larsen, City Gas Superintendent was also in attendance.
  2. Rock Wall for Nebo View Ballfields
         a. Craig Oswald, Recreation Director, explained the rock wall between the
            middle school field and the high school field is eroding. They have done
            some maintenance on the wall, but he is concerned for other’s safety. He
            was proposing a new rock wall with tiers.
         b. The bid he received was for $10,500. The money is available in park
            improvements.
         c. We will talk with the school and involve them in decisions.
         d. Mayor Seely recognized Craig for his dedication to various community
            projects and thanked him for his efforts.
  3. Jamestowne Park Change Order
         a. The American ramp company was planning to come in the spring, but they
            had an opening come up and will be here September 15.
         b. Because of the change, the street department will not be able to get the
            grading done before they come.
         c. The American Ramp Company will now do the grading for the park. There is
             extra money from the grant that will cover that cost.
   4. Electric Utility Rates
         a. Owens Corning issued a letter that they are going to ramp down operations
             until quarter three of 2026.
         b. This shutdown will impact the city electric rates because Owen’s Corning has
             been a constant user that has made our peaks and valleys stable. Reduced
             operations will also impact the city’s revenue.
         c. Seth Atkinson mentioned the possibility of mini data centers to stabilize
             power loads.
   5. Set Square Agreement
         a. Mr. Atkinson presented the initial development agreement with Set Square, a
             developer wanting to bring a data center to Nephi. They have requested
             annexation of some property west of the city for this purpose.
         b. Mr. Atkinson stated that Set Square will most likely talk to a third-party
             company to bring a pipeline for gas into the valley.
         c. The city’s expectation when annexing any property is that Nephi city utilities
             are used. Currently the agreement does not mention this as the case. Mr.
             Atkinson stated we want to add to the agreement that Nephi City Natural Gas
             will be providing a pipeline at some point to their site.
         d. The agreement will be brought to the council for approval once the
             negotiations and changes are complete.
         e. Another data center has also sent a letter of intent to the city that will be
             presented at a future meeting.
