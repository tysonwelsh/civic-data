HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2021-24

Z2021-056 -ORDINANCE TO AMEND SECTION 10-16-1 OF THE HERRIMAN CITY
CODE TO REMOVE DWELLING GROUPS AS A CONDITIONAL USE FROM THE
FR FORESTRY RECREATION ZONE

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to state law, may adopt an ordinance to
establish land development regulations, which includes the adoption or amendment of a zoning text;
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning text amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior to
the said public hearing to recommend any zoning text amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, on June 7, 2021, the required Planning Commission public hearing notice was
posted in public locations as required by State noticing provisions for a meeting to be held on June
17, 2021, at 7:00; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission made a recommendation for the zoning text
amendment in a public meeting held on June 17, 2021, at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chamber;
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council to
consider the zoning text amendment and Planning Commission recommendation in a public meeting;
and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on September 8, 2021, at 7:00 p.m. to
discuss the zoning text amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of Herriman
City to adopt the zoning text amendment, which was recommended by the Planning Commission
and documented in City File number 72021-056;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:
A. Title 10, Chapter 10, Sections 6 of the Herriman City Code be amended as set forth in

the Attached Exhibit A. All other sections and provisions of Title 10, Chapter 10 shall be unaffected
by this amendment.

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 2021-24 shall take immediate effect as soon as it shall be published
or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s recorder.

PASSED AND APPROVED this 8" day of September 2021.
