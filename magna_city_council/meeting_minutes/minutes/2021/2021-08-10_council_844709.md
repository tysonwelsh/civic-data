Art Projects

Free Art Mural

               Council Member Hull stated the Utah Arts Alliance is partnering with Visit Salt
Lake to create a series of 10 large painted heroes that will be placed across locations throughout
Salt Lake County, including one in Magna and one in Kearns. The murals were created and
painted by local artists showcasing the new Salt Lake West of Conventional brand. They will
displace segments of the Grand Manifesto as well as showcase in brand colors, logos, and
themes interpreted by each artist. The murals are 15x15, and she imagined the artists would
make them specific to the community. The art is free; Magna does not have to pay for the murals.

Clark Planetarium and the Utah Sky Project

                Council Member Hull stated the Clark Planetarium is doing the Utah Sky Project
in partnership with Salt Lake County’s Parks & Recreation Division and its Arts & Culture Division,
as well as some independent arts organizations. The Utah Sky Project will create and install three
temporary public art displays that explore the use of astronomy and science. The County is going
to select artists who design and install space, theme, interactive public art pieces that will
encourage visitors to interact with the sky, sun, stars, or other elements of astronomical and
atmospheric science. The intent is to connect park visitors with the wonders of space. She
believed the County wanted to put this in Pleasant Green Park. There would be no cost to the
Magna Metro Township.

Reports from Community Stakeholders

Magna in Motion

                Ms. Kari Duckworth stated Magna in Motion and Magna United/Community that
Cares have been talking about doing a big event, combining Night for Prevention with a health
and safety fair. They would like to have firefighters and the Unified Police Department there, as
well as invite dance groups to show off what they are doing to try to get people up and moving.
They will be reaching out to vendors and people in the community to participate. The focus will
be on health and safety topics, with some fun incorporated. Magna in Motion and the Magna
coalition have picked the date of September 16th, from 5:00 to 8:00 PM, for the event, and the
recreation center has agreed to let them use the gym as well as outdoor space. They will also try
to get the pavilion reserved.

Then, the high school basketball program has been reaching out to businesses to sponsor it and
has asked if Magna in Motion could be a sponsor. The amount of the sponsorship would wipe
out Magna in Motion’s funds, but it would like to get its name out there and could offer a $100
donation if it is allowed to do that.

              Paul Ashton stated if a contribution helps Magna in Motion’s mission, then it can
do that.

Magna United / Magna Community that Cares

                Kellen Schalter last year, the coalition got a $40,500 grant from the Health
Department, which is what paid for the vape detectors. This year, the Health Department gave it
grant funds of $66,000 that can be used for anything around vaping, tobacco, marijuana, and
other substance use prevention work in the community. She also reviewed the action plan and
the following four programs that the coalition is going to implement within the next year.

   •   Guiding Good Choices – a parenting program for parents of children ages 9-15 or 16,
       focusing on family expectations and family conflict resolutions. It is five-week program
       ran one night a week, and will be done at the Webster Center, the library, or a school
       resource center. The coalition plans to have four cycles of that between 2021 and 2022.

   •   Me Time – a class for students experiencing mild depressive symptoms, which will be
       offered during the school day or right after and taught by school social workers. The
       coalition will train social workers at Matheson Junior High School and Cyprus High
       School. There will be three or four classes of that this year.

   •   Botvin LifeSkills – a class required by the State Legislature to be taught in the 5th and 6th
       grades. The principals have asked that the coalition provide guest speaker support, so
       she planned to train coalition members and have them act as guest speakers. It will be
       held one hour once a week for ten weeks.

   •   Too Good for Drugs – an evidence-based prevention program that focusses on drug
       prevention. It is an after-school program, so the coalition will be partnering with Salt
       Lake County Youth Services on that. There is also a program called Too Good for
       Violence that focusses on social and emotional learning skills. The coalition is piloting
       that at Matheson Junior High School, Copper Hills Elementary School, and Entheos
       Academy. If it goes good in those three schools, it will put it in the other schools in the
       fall of 2022.

Reports from other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department (UPD)

              Chief Del Craig stated the UPD is seeing wage wars for public safety employees.
There have been significant pay scale increases occurring in the cities of Salt Lake, South Salt
Lake, West Valley, and South Jordan. That is going to impact UPD’s recruitment and retention.
This seems to be happening nationally as a result of the negative movements to defund police.
People are discouraged from applying and wanting to enter into public safety. The UPD financing
committee is commencing meetings on the budget and will be discussing that. Also, the UPD has
been passing out crossing guard applications to its elementary schools. It has 43 crossing guards
in Magna, and those positions are filled, but it is looking for substitutes. Like other jobs in the
community, those positions have been a challenge to fill.

             Council Member Hull stated Dominion Energy has closed off the sidewalk on
8000 West and 2700 South so there is no safe route there for kids to walk to school.

               Council Member Prokopis stated Dominion Energy was given the use of the field,
but not the sidewalk and half of one lane.

               Chief Craig stated the UPD should have been aware of that. He will look into it.

               Detective Russ Buhler stated the Citizen’s Academy is coming up. The UPD
wants Council Members or people in the community to sign up for that. There are still plenty of
openings. It starts September 24, 2021, and goes for 13 weeks.
