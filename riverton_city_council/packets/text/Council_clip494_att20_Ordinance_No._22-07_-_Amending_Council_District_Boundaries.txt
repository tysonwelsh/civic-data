                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                     ORDINANCE NO. 22-07

     AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE COUNCIL DISTRICT BOUNDARIES OF
                   THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL

     WHEREAS, Riverton City Code 2.05.020 regulates the voting Districts of City Council
Members; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City has experienced significant population growth over the past
ten years; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City council finds and determines that, owing to said
population growth, existing district boundaries do not feature voting populations which are
substantially equal in size, as required by Utah Code § 10-3-205.5; and

         WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines the need to revise the
districts for election to the Riverton City Council, to reflect the changes in population of the
community and the various neighborhoods thereof; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3-205.5 (2)(b), the Riverton City Council is
required to adjust its district boundaries to maintain districts of substantially equal
population, “Within six months after the Legislature completes its redistricting process;”
and
       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Legislature
completed its redistricting process on November 16, 2021; and

       WHEREAS, the next regularly scheduled municipal election shall occur in November,
2023, and

       WHEREAS, candidates must announce their candidacy between June 1 and June 7,
2023 for the next regularly scheduled municipal election; and

         WHEREAS, the City Council finds and determines that the district boundaries must be
corrected to comply with the "substantially equal" standard described in Utah Code § 10-3-205.5
prior to the commencement of the next regularly scheduled municipal election; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council, hereby determines that it is in the best interest ofthe
citizens of Riverton City to amend its district boundaries as described in the legal descriptions
and map included with this ordinance and by reference made a part hereof;




                                                      1
       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

      Section 1.   All Riverton City Council Members shall be elected to represent a district of
                   the City as the districts are described in the legal descriptions and district
                   boundary map attached in Exhibit "A" and incorporated herein by reference.
                   The City is divided into 5 voting districts of substantially equal population,
                   as shown in the attached map and which are designated on the map as
                   Districts 1 through 5.

      Section 2.   Council seats for Districts 3 and 4 shall be filled in municipal elections in the
                   year that the Mayor's seat is up for election beginning with the municipal
                   election in 2025, and Council seats for Districts 1, 2, and 5 shall be filled in
                   municipal elections beginning with the municipal election in 2023.

      Section 3.   The City shall, by ordinance, make adjustments to the boundaries of the
                   districts as may be required to maintain districts of substantially equal
                   population within six months after the Utah State Legislature completes its
                   redistricting process and may do so at such other times as the Riverton City
                   Council may deem necessary.

      Section 4.   Candidates for any council district, in addition to meeting the eligibility and
                   residency requirements of Utah law, must also be residents of the district
                   which the candidates seek to represent.

      Section 5.   All sitting City Council Members shall continue to hold office within the
                   district which he or she currently represents until the expiration of that
                   council member's term of office. The re-alignment of districts within
                   Riverton City shall not be deemed to deprive any sitting member of the
                   Riverton City Council his or her current seat to which the Council Member
                   was elected.

      Section 6.   The provisions of this ordinance shall be severable, and in any provision
                   hereof, or the application of such provision is in any way found to be held
                   invalid, all other provisions shall continue to be in full force and effect.
                   Ordinance No. 13-02, adopted by the Riverton City Council on February 5,
                   2013, is hereby repealed to the extent that it conflicts with the provisions of
                   this Ordinance.

      Section 7.   This Ordinance shall take effect upon publication or upon posting as required
                   by law. The Riverton City Recorder is hereby authorized to reformat this
                   ordinance and codify the same within the Riverton City Municipal Code in a
                   manner deemed appropriate to the City Recorder, provided such reformatting
                   or codification does not result in a material change to the substantive
                   provisions of this Ordinance.

                                                   2
      Section 8.    The Salt Lake County Clerk is authorized and directed to implement the
                    district boundaries created under this Ordinance for the November 2023
                    Municipal Election.
