NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION
                            WORK SESSION MEETING MINUTES
                                    February 16, 2022
                                        21 E. 100 N.
                                     Nephi, UT 84648
                                         6:00 p.m.
Planning Commission Chairman Ann Peterson called the meeting to order and welcomed all in
attendance.

PRAYER: Commissioner Alan Hancock

CU ZONES USES DISCUSSION:

    City Planner Shauna Talbot led a discussion about possible uses in the CU zones of the city. They
talked about changing the term “agriculture” in the Nephi City code to “horticulture”. The commission
reviewed a list of uses and had a lengthy discussion on each use currently allowed in the CU zone and
what uses should be allowed in the CU2 zone and not allowed in the CU zone. The Commission gave
direction to Ms. Talbot for revisions to the uses of these zones.

Meeting adjourned at 8:43 p.m.
