2. Discussion on 2024 Legislative Session
   The Council and Legislators discussed the upcoming 2024 Legislative session and the
   following issues:
   a. Justice Court
Manager Chamness shared what we are doing with Millcreek and Cottonwood Heights. She discussed
the declining caseloads and rising expenses. We are Listening with interest to what the legislature is
doing. Sen Pitcher shared that she doesn’t believe anything will happen this session.
        b. Redevelopment Agencies

        c. Affordable Housing
Holladay is trying hard to comply with the goals of the legislature. Our community like many build-out
communities has challenges that make it difficult to achieve the Legislature's policy goals. Manager
Chamness gave a couple of examples. We took a corner that had some affordable housing. It was not in
great condition, but for Holladay that was affordable. We have worked with a developer for a rezone on a
corner with transit access in both directions. The were units there previously, so we rezoned the property,
doubled the density, and speeded up the plan review process - all the concerns that we've been hearing
clearly from the Legislature that you all would like cities to do. The property changed hands, and now
what we have is 11 single-family homes, 3000 square feet on very small lots, being marketed at $1.5
million which doesn't meet anyone's goals for affordable housing.

        d. Short-term Rentals
For Holladay, the key is really about enforcement. The council has had a robust policy conversation
several times. Holladay has restricted short-term to arterials and must be licensed and only about a
handful have done that. We know there are more because we can google it and see the listings. But can't
enforce.
