2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Mr. Sam Dunham  presented a booklet of some of the history of the Mt. Aire Canyon 
community, which was submitted with an application to be on the National Register of 
Historic Places.  Seventh generation families of Parley P. Pratt, Willard Richards, and 
others have been living in Mt. Aire Canyon since the 1850s, and the architectural styles 
have been there since the 1900s.  This community will be drastically affected by the gravel 
pit that is proposed to go in there.  The Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) has 
approved an encroachment for Granite Construction to build a road into that gravel pit 
area, but the community thinks Granite Construction should have to come to Salt Lake 
County to get a conditional use permit before it can build the road.  Safety issues are a 
concern in the canyon.  The County has had an agreement with UDOT for thirty years, 
and installed a gate as a safety measure, due to offenses that had been occurring.  
Mr. Charles James  updated the Council on efforts to end water fluoridation in Salt Lake 
County, due to the risk it presents for fluoride toxicity accumulation.  Fluoride is in 
non-stick products, medications, processed food products, and cleaning and paint 
products.  Fluoride can also be taken into the body from drinking water, showering, 
brushing teeth, cooking, gardening, and breathing in chloroform products in the air .  
Fluoride can come from so many things, it is impossible to measure the amount of 
fluoride toxicity in a person, and fluoride toxicity accumulation can mimic a lot of 
different health problems.  Since fluoride carries potential risks, consuming it should be a 
choice, which is why it is unconstitutional to place fluoride in water.

3.4 A Resolution of The Salt Lake County Council Concerning 
the Continuing Funding of Public Safety Services Within a

Sponsors: Councilwoman Laurie Stringham, Councilwoman Ann Granato, 
Councilman David Alvord (Approx. 20 mins)
Discussion/Direction
Council Member Stringham  reviewed the resolution concerning the 
continuing funding of public safety services within a municipality that is 
located in a designated recreation area.  She stated after the Council 
discussed the last resolutions regarding this matter, she met with Council 
Members and different entities to get everyone ’s input on a resolution that 
dealt more with the policy side of this.  This resolution recognizes the 
Council will be making a decision on what funding it thinks is appropriate 
going forward, and that it will work with the Unified Fire Authority (UFA), 
the Unified Fire Service Area (UFSA), and the Town of Brighton to come up 
with a solution to resolve this.  The resolution also requires the UFA to come 
back and prove why it needs the money.  The appropriation will not be a set 
amount as the County has been doing because that is inappropriate.  
A motion was made by Council Member Stringham to adopt the resolution.

Presenters: Craig Buttars, Commissioner, Utah Department of Agriculture and

Informational
Mr. Craig Buttars , Commissioner, Utah Department of Agriculture and 
Food, stated the Utah Department of Agriculture and Food (UDAF) is 
meeting with every Council and Commission in the State of Utah to discuss 
agriculture in their specific county.  He delivered a PowerPoint presentation

Community, reviewing who the Utah Department of Agriculture and Food is,

overview, and agriculture in Salt Lake County.    
Mr. Kelly Pehrson , Deputy Commissioner, Utah Department of Agriculture

strategic goals in detail, including agristructure, the agriculture workforce, 
agriculture land preservation, food supply chain development, agriculture 
education, and customer service.  He also reviewed new programs, the Water 
Optimization Program, the LeRay McCallister Working Farm and Ranch 
Protection Fund, the Ag Stress Assistance Program, the Utah Food Security 
Processing Grant, the Utah State Department of Agriculture’ s 
announcements, the Regional Food Business Centers Program, the Resilient 
Food Systems Infrastructure Program, and showed a film of UDAF ’s 
Drought Impacts and Optimization.
Mr. Buttars continued the PowerPoint presentation reviewing the
