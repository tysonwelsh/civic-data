Meeting Minutes
Work Session 2-23-21
7:00  p.m.
Nephi City Council Chambers


	•	Assistant to the City Administrator/Treasurer Travis Worwood discussed with the council the idea of forming an economic development team consisting of Economic Development Director Brent Boswell, himself, and Electric Department Superintendent Rust Finlinson.  Mr. Finlinson would be able to provide timely cost estimates and feasibility analysis for power connections  for prospective commercial entities and act as a liaison with other utilities to provide those estimates as well.  Mr. Worwood stated that the objective of the team would be to turn council economic goals into reality and to identify and recruit desirable companies and industries to locate in Nephi.  The team would plan to meet every other week with agendas and minutes for each meeting.  The  team would provide a quarterly economic development report in a work session to keep the council informed.  The council were generally in favor of the concept.  

	•	 City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed with the council the budget prioritized items they had decided upon at a previous work session.  He explained that many of the prioritized items did not have funding sources.  Many of them had ongoing costs rather than one-time expenses.  A discussion ensued about possible funding sources and the council agreed they needed more time to make the decision as to whether to fund the priorities through sales tax surplus or an increase in property taxes.  

	•	 The council discussed what recommendations they would like to propose to the Juab County Planning Commission regarding the land use laws in the county that governed residential development and how the city’s growth area affects those county land use laws.  The council directed staff to recommend that the acreage requirement of 10 acres per residential dwelling be applied to all areas of the county including properties within the growth area of the city. 

	•	The council discussed the posting of the job of an Assistant Golf Professional. 

	•	The council reviewed the updated financial reports.  

	•	Mr. Atkinson and several councilors gave various updates.

	•	 Councilor Seely moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and competence of and individual and real property.  Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote. 

	•	Adjournment -  10:30 p.m.

  
Present:  Mayor Glade R. Nielson,  Justin Seely,  Larry Ostler, Skip Worwood, Nathan Memmott, Kent Jones, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Kasey Wright, Travis Worwood, Michael Morgan, Jace Peterson, Paul Davis, Jeremy Lynn, John Deeben, and Bert Wright
