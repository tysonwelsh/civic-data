COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS
RESOLUTION No. 2025-51

A RESOLUTION APPROVING A MODERATE-INCOME/AFFORDABLE HOUSING PLAN

WHEREAS, UTAH CODE ANN. 10-9a-408 requires every Utah municipality to adopt a
moderate-income housing plan and submit annual update reports, with an updated plan required
every five years; and

WHEREAS, because the city of Cottonwood Heights (the “Cify’’) is in year five of its
current plan, an updated plan is required to be adopted and submitted by 1 August 2025; and

WHEREAS, the attached plan (the “P/an’’) has been drafted and formatted to comply with
the state’s reporting standards and requirements; and

WHEREAS, at its 4 June 2025 meeting the City’s planning commission held a public
hearing concerning the Plan and then unanimously recommended for the City’s city council (the
“Council”) to approve and adopt the Plan for submission to the state by 1 August 2025; and

WHEREAS, on | July 2025 the Council met in regular session to, inter alia, consider
approving and adopting the Plan for submission to the state as required by statute; and

WHEREAS, the Council has reviewed the Plan, a copy of which is annexed hereto; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interests of the health, safety and welfare of the City’s residents to approve and adopt the Plan;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Cottonwood Heights city council that
the attached Plan is hereby approved and adopted as the City’s required moderate
income/affordable housing plan and the City’s administrative staff is hereby authorized and
directed to file the Plan with the state before 1 August 2025.

This Resolution, assigned no. 2025-51, shall take effect immediately upon passage.

PASSED AND APPROVED effective 1 July 2025.
