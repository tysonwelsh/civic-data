SumMaARy: | Alpine Homes is developing GT Estates subdivision. The builders have

submitted a building permit application to construct a new single-family
home on Lot 1 located at 4904 S Snarr Haven Ct. All lots in the GT Estates
subdivision are zoned R-1-6. According to the Table of Setbacks in Section
13.20.020, corner lots are subject to greater setbacks from the front
property line to the living area than interior lots. The applicant has stated
that a custom plan to accommodate the smaller footprint due to increased
setbacks for corner lots would be required to meet current city standards. A
representative submitted a zoning text amendment application, seeking to
amend front setbacks for corner lots.

Exhibit A includes a figure from currently adopted standards that illustrates
a home on a corner lot is required to have a 20-foot setback from the public
right-of-way for the side without main entrance (“front side B”) and a 30-
foot setback to any portion of the home on the side with the main front
entrance (“front side A”). On the other hand, homes on interior lots are
subject to varying front setbacks based on zone and portion of structure.
Lots zoned R-1-5 or R-1-6 have front setbacks that are reduced by five feet
compared to front setbacks for R-1-7+ zones. This allows some flexibility for
the footprint of the home, since the building envelope on smaller lots is
already limited by the decreased square footage of the lot. Exhibit A also
shows the front setback varies for interior lots, depending on which portion
of the structure is being measured. The variation of the front fagade creates
flexibility and visual interest in residential zones.

Prior to 2012, corner lots had one intuitive front setback, which was the side
with main entrance to the dwelling. That front setback was determined by
the underlying zone. With the code update in 2012, “front side A” and “front
side B” designations were created. Side B replaced the “side yard abutting a
public street” and maintained the 20-foot setback. Front “side A” became the
new front yard designation for corner lots and included an increased setback
of 30’ for any part of the structure, no matter the zone. The code update
included an increase to R-1-7 front setbacks from 25 feet from property line
to living area, to 30 feet from front property line to living area. The result is
that R-1-7 neighborhoods and homes on corner lots developed prior to 2012
contain homes that are legal nonconforming, which would prevent
homeowners from making improvements that involve the front portion of
their structure without bringing the structure up to current code. A copy of
the table of setbacks prior to 2012 can be reviewed in exhibit B.

The proposed amendment includes an amendment to “side A” for corner
lots, making the front setback the same as the front setbacks for interior lots
in the same underlying zone. This will remove the 30 feet, no matter what
portion of structure or zone, and create the possibility of an increased
building area for smaller R-1-5 and -6 zones. The changes will not allow any
portion of the structure to be built in the sight visibility triangle, and will
allow the possibility for variation of fagade, and uniform build-to lines in
neighborhoods. The change also includes an amendment to the R-1-7 front
setbacks, removing legal nonconforming situations for homes built to
previous standards.

Taylorsville Planning Commission held a public hearing and considered the
proposed text amendment. On March 26, 2024, the commission voted 6-1
to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council.

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF TAYLORSVILLE APPROVING A ZONING
TEXT AMENDMENT TO TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 13.20.020:
R-1 AND R-2 DISTRICTS; TABLE OF SETBACKS, BUILDING HEIGHTS, OFF
STREET PARKING.

WHEREAS, the Taylorsville City Council (the “Council” met in regular session on May
1, 2024, to consider, among other things, approving a text amendment to Taylorsville Municipal
Code Chapter 13.20.020: R-1 and R-2 Districts; Table of Setbacks, Building Heights, Off Street
Parking related to minimum front building setback standards in residential zones; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Section 13.35.040, notice of a
public hearing before the City’s Planning Commission was posted on Taylorsville City’s website
on March 15, 2024; and

WHEREAS, the City’s Planning Commission held a properly noticed public hearing on
the proposed amendments on March 26, 2024, during which the Planning Commission voted 6-1
to recommend that the Council approve the proposed text amendment; and

WHEREAS, although not required by City ordinances, the City Council heard courtesy
public comment on the proposed amendments during its regularly scheduled meeting on May 1,
2024; and

WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that it is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Taylorsville to approve the proposed
amendments.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Taylorsville City Council that the
proposed text amendments to TAYLORSVILLE MUNICIPAL CODE Chapter 13.20.020 amending
minimum building setback standards in the R-1 and R-2 zoning districts, as articulated in Exhibit
“A,” attached hereto and incorporated herein by this reference, are hereby approved,

This ordinance, assigned Ordinance No. 24-04, shall take effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law, deposited, and recorded in the office of the City Recorder,
and accepted as required herein.
