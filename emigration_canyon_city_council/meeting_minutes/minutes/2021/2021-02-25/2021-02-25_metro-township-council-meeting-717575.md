Council Member Harris read the following statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-

Pursuant to Utah Code 52-4-202 the undersigned, as Mayor of the Emigration
Canyon Metro Township Council, hereby declares that providing an anchor location for the
electronic meetings of the Council will present a substantial risk to the health and safety of those
who may be present at the anchor location for the following reasons:
The Township is located in Salt Lake County and is still under an “Yellow” alert
stage for the COVID 19 pandemic; and
The regular meeting place for the Council (the Fire Station in the Canyon) does
not have sufficient space in the meeting room to provide for the recommended physical distancing
to keep people safe from infection.

PUBLIC MEETING

Public Hearing
THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the Emigration Canon Metro Township General Plan:
Council Member Bowen, seconded by Council Member Brems, moved to open the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.
Tim Harpst stated the draft plan includes everything that is required by Utah State
law. There was an incredible amount of input from the residents in the canyon. Those comments
were included in the plan. The Metro Township Council has the authority to modify anything in
the draft plan and the Planning Commission is ready to make amendments as needed.
Recommendations can not be implemented unless they are initiated by the Metro Township
Council. Once the plan is adopted by the Metro Township Council, it can be amended at any time.
Bill Tobey stated he served on the General Plan Steering Committee. He
appreciates the opportunity to participate in the process and the time and interest by others
including MSD resources. The plan represents an 18-month discussion, and the proposal is a
good enough to get started.
Council Member Harris, seconded by Council Member Bowen, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.
Council Member Bowen stated he began attending the Planning Commission
meetings. The Commission spent 4 – 5 months reading each chapter of the General Plan and
amending it. He has read the entire document and the amendments.
Council Member Bowen moved to adopt the General Plan as written. The motion
failed due to a lack of a second.
Council Member Harris stated she would like to arrange a few work meetings to
discuss the General Plan in more detail before adopting it.
Council Member Brems stated the plan as it is currently written is impressive and
the Planning Commission and Steering Committee has spent a tremendous amount of time on
the plan. He is willing to review the plan further to make some additional amendments.
as a Council.

Council Member Hawkes stated she would like to review the plan and discuss it

Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP)
Plan (COOP):

The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting a Continuity of Operations

RESOLUTION NO. 2021-02-01

Date: February 25, 2021

A RESOLUTION OF THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL ADOPTING A CONTINUITY OF OPERATIONS PLAN (COOP).
WHEREAS, it is the mission of the Emigration Canyon Metro Township, ensure, its
operations are performed efficiently with minimal disruption, especially during an emergency;
WHEREAS, this document provides planning and program guidance for implementing the
Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP) to ensure the Emigration Canyon Metro Township is
capable of conducting its daily operations under all threats and conditions;
WHEREAS, this CONTINUITY PLAN (COOP) has been developed in accordance with
guidance in Executive Order 12656, Assignment of Emergency Preparedness Responsibilities;
National Security Presidential Directive 51/Homeland Security Presidential Directive 20, National
Continuity Policy; Homeland Security Council, National Continuity Policy Implementation Plan;
Federal Continuity Directive 1, Federal Executive Branch National Continuity Program and
Requirements, October 2012;
WHEREAS, the Emigration Canyon Metro Township desires to adopt a COOP in
the interest of public health, safety and welfare:
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1. Adoption of a Continuity of Operations P l a n . The Emigration Canyon
Metro Township Council hereby adopts the Continuity of Operations Plan attached hereto as
Exhibit A.
Section 2 . Effective Date.
upon its passage.

This Resolution shall become effective immediately
