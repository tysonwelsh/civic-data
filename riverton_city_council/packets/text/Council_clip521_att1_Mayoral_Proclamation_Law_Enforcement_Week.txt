                           NATIONAL POLICE WEEK
                                 MAY 15-21

        WHEREAS, in 1962, President Kennedy issued the first proclamation for
Peace Officers Memorial Day and National Police Week to remember and honor
law enforcement officers for their service and sacrifice; and

       WHEREAS, in honor of Peace Officers Memorial Day and National Police
Week, we express gratitude to the Riverton Police Department (RPD) law
enforcement officers. We are especially mindful of, and honor, those in blue who
have been killed or hurt in the line of duty, while protecting their communities; and

         WHEREAS, in Riverton City we rely on law enforcement officers to keep
our neighborhoods safe, to enforce our laws, and to respond in times of crisis.
The men and women of the Riverton Police Department (RPD) sustain peace and
order across our community and we look at them as models of courage and
integrity; and

         WHEREAS, it is important that all citizens know and understand the
responsibilities of their police officers, and know that RPD law enforcement
officers recognize their duty to serve the people with courage, dedication, and
strength;

        NOW, THEREFORE, I Mayor Trent Staggs, call upon all citizens of
Riverton City to honor our RPD law enforcement officers who fearlessly enforce
our laws, even at the risk of personal peril, safeguard our property, our liberty,
and our lives. We owe them, and their families, our full and enduring support for
the service they have given to Riverton City.

        GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 17th day of May,
2022.
