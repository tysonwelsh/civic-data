Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: October 13, 2022
Re: Angela's Finest Caramels Conditional Home Occpation Comments: The proposed business is a wholesale caramel business.  There are no customers coming to the home and all deliveries of the candies are made by the applicant and his family.  The applicant is proposing to construct a building in their rear yard area that will be utilized as a commercial kitchen.  The structure will have the appearance of a cottage that is complimentary to the design of the house.  The proposed building is approximately 16 x 30 or 480 square feet.  The applicant had initially proposed a 12 x 30 for 360 square foot building, but due to the location on the lot and required setbacks, the location was moved.  The new location allows the applicant to have a slightly larger building.  The building will still need to obtain building permits and all development standards will be verified by Planning with that building permit.  Because this is a corner lot with streets on three sides of it, there will be minimal impact on the neighborhood.  The proposed accessory building is not different than other properties that have accessory buildings. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
For denial:
1.  Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated detrimental effects. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 10/6/2022 Backup Material
