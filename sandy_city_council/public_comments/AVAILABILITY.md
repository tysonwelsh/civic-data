# Sandy City, Utah — Availability of Genuine Published Written Public Comments to the City Council (2020–present)

**Question audited:** Does Sandy City, Utah publish GENUINE written/online public comments (text the public actually wrote and submitted via web form, email, eComment, or uploaded letter) that the city published anywhere public? Clerk paraphrases of in-person speakers inside the minutes do NOT count.

**Platform:** Granicus Legistar — https://sandyutah.legistar.com (calendar, agendas, minutes, packets). Granicus SpeakUp/Ideas portal at https://sandyutah.granicusideas.com.

**Audit date:** 2026-06-25. Disk stayed >20 GB free throughout; all downloaded PDFs were size-checked and deleted immediately after text extraction.

---

## Avenues checked

### 1. Dedicated comment page (sandy.utah.gov)
URLs checked:
- https://sandy.utah.gov/1204/City-Council — links to "Citizen Involvement" (`/1210`), "Get Involved" (`/591`), "City Council Corner" (`/902`); no comment-archive page.
- https://sandy.utah.gov/1210/Citizen-Involvement — sub-links (Get Involved, Council Members, Meetings → Legistar); no written-comment submission/publication page.
- https://sandy.utah.gov/591/Get-Involved — no comment text surfaced.
- https://sandy.utah.gov/2063/City-Council-FAQs — content not extractable from the excerpt.

**Finding:** No dedicated "public comment", "written comments received", "correspondence", or "comments received" archive page exists on sandy.utah.gov. Guidance found via web search points the public to **email** comments to **CitizenComment@sandy.utah.gov** (distributes to all 7 Council members) or to the Council Office Executive Director **mapplegarth@sandy.utah.gov** by 3:00 PM meeting day "to have those comments distributed to the City Council and have them read into the record." No statement that submitted text is published.

### 2. eComment / SpeakUp / Open City Hall portal
URLs checked:
- https://sandyutah.granicusideas.com — Granicus **SpeakUp** civic-engagement portal for Sandy City. States **"This SpeakUp site is recently launched. Please come back in a few days to Participate."** No public comment archive visible.
- https://sandyutah.granicusideas.com/meetings — **"No Meetings Scheduled."** No publicly visible/exportable submissions; no historical comment archive.
- https://sandyutah.legistar.com/Calendar.aspx — "eComment" appears in the page header as a feature label, but **no eComment link/column appears in any meeting row.**
- Meeting detail pages (see avenue 3) explicitly show **eComment status: "Not available."**

**Historical note (important):** The eComment portal was **active in 2020–2021** — Sandy's 2020–2021 minutes carry per-item "**Click here to eComment on this item**" attachment links pointing to `https://sandyutah.granicusideas.com/...`. So an online-comment channel did operate during the COVID era. However, in 2026 that portal is back to **dormant/empty**, and **no archive of those 2020–2021 submissions is publicly visible or exportable** (the per-item Ideas pages do not surface retained public submission text; Legistar marks eComment "Not available"). The active-era submissions were routed to Council, not preserved as a public published archive.

**Finding:** A Granicus SpeakUp/eComment portal exists and was **briefly active (2020–2021)** but is now **dormant/empty** — recently re-launched, no meetings, no participation enabled, and **no publicly visible or exportable past submissions** from either era. On Legistar meeting rows eComment is marked "Not available." So no portal-published comments are retrievable for 2020–present. (A future run could probe Wayback captures of specific `granicusideas.com` item pages from 2020–2021 to see if any submission text was ever rendered publicly — but the live portal exposes none.)

### 3. Agenda-packet attachments ("written comments received" / "correspondence")
Source: Legistar events API https://webapi.legistar.com/v1/sandyutah/events (returns EventAgendaFile + EventMinutesFile only — there is no separate packet field in the API). Real "Agenda Packet" URLs were obtained from the MeetingDetail pages.

Packets inspected (downloaded, text-extracted via pdftotext, then deleted):
- **City Council, 2026-06-02 (budget + park redevelopment, public-hearing-heavy), EventId 2073** — Agenda Packet ~18.7 MB: `View.ashx?M=AO&ID=177605&GUID=036559b7-818d-4816-b665-354a084ffb3c`. **No** Correspondence / Written Comments Received / Comments Received / Public Input section. Only comment-related text is the boilerplate "You may also leave a written comment by emailing CitizenComment@sandy.utah.gov." (The word "Communications" appears only in an unrelated Communications-Director ordinance.)
- **Planning Commission, 2026-05-21 (zoning), EventId 2033** — Agenda Packet ~56.9 MB: `View.ashx?M=PA&ID=1360348&GUID=9DA74CB6-AC09-4745-872B-CE9FE37C7859`. **No** correspondence / written-comments-received section. "in favor" hits are all about utility easements; no public-comment letters bundled.
- Council agenda-only PDFs (2073, 2071) carry the same email boilerplate, no comment attachments.

**Finding:** Sampled budget and zoning packets contain **no correspondence / written-comments-received bundle.** Sandy does not appear to attach received public comments to its packets.

### 4. Wayback Machine
- http://archive.org/wayback/available?url=sandy.utah.gov/1204/City-Council → snapshot exists: `http://web.archive.org/web/20260412165045/https://www.sandy.utah.gov/1204/City-Council` (Apr 2026). Confirms the City Council page; no evidence of a decommissioned public-comment archive page.

**Finding:** No archived comment-archive portal found. Nothing suggests a published-comments page ever existed and was removed.

### 5. Records / transparency (GRAMA)
URLs found:
- https://sandy.utah.gov/440/GRAMA-Requests / https://sandy.utah.gov/1556/14559/GRAMA-Requests
- https://sandy.utah.gov/1075/City-Recorder (City Recorder / Records Officer)

**Finding:** Public comments emailed to the Council are government records obtainable only by **GRAMA request** (10-business-day response), not proactively published. No open-data/correspondence archive of council comments exists.

### 6. Email-only submission (legitimate "not published")
**Finding:** Per the official guidance and the standing agenda boilerplate (verified in the 2026-06-02 and 2026-05-19 agendas and the COVID-era Jan 2021 notice), the public submits comment by (a) speaking live in person / via Zoom, or (b) **emailing CitizenComment@sandy.utah.gov** (or mapplegarth@sandy.utah.gov by 3 PM) to be **distributed to Council and read into the record** — NOT published online. Comment is accepted by email/in-person only, with no publication of the submitted text.

---

## Note on the minutes (speaker log, NOT comments)
The meeting **minutes** (e.g. `2073_M_City_Council_26-06-02_Meeting_Minutes.pdf`) record citizen comment only as **clerk paraphrases of in-person speakers** — e.g. "Mark Mason Taylor spoke on the concept plan…", "Lori Wilson… stated that…", "Pat Jones stated the analysis should not be included…". No emailed/written comment is reproduced in full text. This is a **speaker log**, not genuine published written comments, and does NOT satisfy "PUBLISHED."

---

## Verdict

**SUBMIT-ONLY / NOT PUBLISHED.**

Sandy City accepts public comment by speaking (in person / Zoom) or by **email to CitizenComment@sandy.utah.gov** to be distributed to Council and read into the record. The submitted text is **not published** anywhere public: there is no comment-archive web page; the Granicus SpeakUp/eComment portal was briefly active in 2020–2021 (per-item "Click here to eComment" links in those minutes) but is now dormant/empty with **no visible or exportable submissions from any era** ("eComment: Not available" on every meeting); agenda packets carry **no correspondence/written-comments-received bundle**; Wayback shows no decommissioned comment-archive page; and emailed comments are reachable only via GRAMA. The minutes merely paraphrase in-person speakers (a speaker log) — that is NOT genuine published written comment.

No genuine published written public comments were found.
