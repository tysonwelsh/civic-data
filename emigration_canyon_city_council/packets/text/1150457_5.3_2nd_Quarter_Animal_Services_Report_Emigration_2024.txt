ACO CARES PROGRAM
OUR ACO CARES PROGRAM ALLOWS OFFICERS
THAT ENCOUNTER STRUGGLING FAMILIES TO
ISSUE FREE RESOURCE VOUCHERS FOR
SERVICES SUCH AS:

   Pet Spay and neuter services
   Essential Vaccinations
   Microchip
   In addition, Officers may hand out pet food &
   supplies.

Made possible through fundraising events, the
program helps encourage responsible pet
ownership, increase the health of pets, keeping
pets in the home, and building trust & cohesion
with the public.
