                                       Hearings began at – 6:39 p.m.
Magna Metro Township is considering adopting the proposed 2021 Magna General Plan, which plans for
the areas within Magna Metro Township. The 2021 General Plan is Magna's first comprehensive plan since
incorporation as a metro township in 2017. The Plan synthesizes data and public feedback to describe
current conditions, envision Magna's future, and guide Magna from present conditions toward that desired
vision. This General Plan meets the required State laws for general planning, including elements on Land
Use, Transportation, and Moderate-Income Housing. It also includes the following supplemental elements:
Introduction & Overview; Planning Process; Catalyst Areas & Economic Stability; Parks, Trails, Recreation
& Open Space; Natural Hazards; Community Services & Facilities; Sustainability; and Implementation &
Work Plan. Planner: Kate Davies (Motion/Voting)

Please visit: http://bit.ly/lrp-magna to view a copy of the 2021 Magna General Plan.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Long Rang Planner Kate Davies and Annaka Egan, Planner,
GSBS Architects provided an overview of the 2021 Magna General Plan.

Commissioner Cripps opened the plan up for discussion on the overview. Commissioners like the direct
clarification on the CTC recommendations and have agreed with the plan and the changes.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open public hearing, Commissioner Lockwood seconded that motion.

OAM2021-000220 - Magna Metro Township Planning Commission will consider a recommendation to the
Magna Metro Township Council regarding amendments to Chapter 19.69 of the Magna Municipal Code,
governing the “P-C” or “Planned Community” zone. The amendments intend to allow broader use of the
P-C zone to allow Magna to take advantage of more diverse advanced planning activities in the development
process. Planner: Travis Hair (Motion/Voting)

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Travis Hair and Counsel, Jay Springer provided an
analysis of the recommended amendments to Chapter 19.69 governing the P-C zone.

Commissioner Richards said under conditional uses, has been changed, requests why the change is made
and thought just changing acreage. Mr. Springer said took instruction and opportunity, consider prior
language is ambiguous, someone might read they do not go to planning commission, but language states
must. Clarification, not a change. Mr. Springer said for conditional use to be approved in the PC plan,
needs go to council to approve development agreement and plan, and then PC comes back for conditional
use permit. Nothing should come to planning commission in a zone unless the initial plan, includes approval
from council they conceptually agree conditional use permit can be mitigated. Commissioner Richards said
a lot of emphasis on development agreement to work out, instead of the ordinance. Mr. Springer said desire
to not bolt up ordinance already existing elsewhere.

Commissioner Lockwood asked the expectations for benefit to Ivory or Magna. Mr. Springer said timing is
motivated by the Ivory property, but council can set up and not have individual and allow flexibility, offset,
and benefit to overall planning. Commissioner Weight asked on land use districts, includes industrial and
preference is to take industrial out of the neighborhoods. Mr. Springer said council and planning
commission will look at the plan before its approved.

Commissioner Weight motioned to open public hearing, Commissioner Richards seconded that motion.
