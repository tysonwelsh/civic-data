2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and recommendation of Meadowbrook Land’s request for review of the
Center Pointe Towns zone change, changing the zoning on approximately 2.25 -acres of
property located at 1627 Center Pointe Drive from Commercial to R-3 (high density
residential).
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Hemmert asked if R-3 as proposed in the zoning designation was appropriate
    in the HDR land use designation.


The applicant was present.

Commissioner Hemmert opened public hearing.

There was a public comment regarding traffic and why the agenda item is being heard again
when nothing had changed. The comment questioned if the zoning was appropriate, which was
answered by Staff. The second part of the public comment was directed to traffic calming
measures. Ross Dinsdale, City Engineer, responded that there were no plans yet, but the City has
done preliminary work (inaudible).

Commissioner Hemmert closed public hearing.

Motion: Commissioner Eyre forwarded a positive recommendation of Meadowbrook Land’s
request for review of the Center Pointe Towns zone change, changing the zoning on
approximately 2.25 -acres of property located at 1627 Center Pointe Drive from Commercial to
R-3 (high density residential); including all DRC comments from May 20, 2020 and findings
stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

    Amended Motion: Commissioner Eyre amended his motion to include no more than 24
    units as stipulated, to include a traffic calming study and any other stipulations the City
    Council deems necessary. Commissioner Nielsen seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre; yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.2) Consideration of Knowlton General’s request for approval of the Porter’s Place site
plan, a .21-acre mixed-use development located at 24 West Main Street.
       Commissioner Hereth recused himself from agenda item and declared a conflict of
       interest.

    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item. Staff allowed this to
    be reviewed and has additional comments from DRC.

    The applicant, Randy Beyer, was present. Commissioner Hemmert asked about the western
    elevation and if the veneer was brick or a stamped product. Randy stated the king size brick
    veneer is a longer sized brick and they fully anticipate doing that on the building. The
    applicant stated there were no plans for rooftop amenities other than for utility use. The
    applicant stated there will be access points on the corner, on the southwest side, and on the
    east side, totaling three access points in and out of the building. Commissioner Everett asked
    about a parking agreement and if this was still part of the plan with the City. The parking
    agreement is still in place with the City.

Motion: Commissioner Bunker recommended approval of Knowlton General’s request for
approval of the Porter’s Place site plan, a .21-acre mixed-use development located at 24 West Main
Street.; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report. Commissioner Nielsen
seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, yes; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes;
Commissioner Eyre; yes. The motion passed unanimously.

2.3) Public hearing and recommendation of Casey Hansen’s request for review of the
Hansen zone change, changing the zoning on .33-acres of property located at 1045 West
Main Street from RA-1 (residential/agriculture) to NC (neighborhood commercial).
    Sierra Pierson, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    The applicant, Casey Hansen, was present.

    Commissioner Hemmert opened the public hearing.

    Commissioner Hemmert closed the public hearing.

    Commissioner Everett is not in favor of putting in the ice cream shop and is worried about
    the impact it will have on the neighbors on both sides of the proposed site. Commissioner
    Hemmert agreed. Kim Struthers, Community Development Director, presented information
    on the neighboring buffers and the intersections on the general plan near the commercial
    uses. Commissioner Bunker stated he remembered other businesses that needed to be looked
    at more strategically. Ross Dinsdale stated most of the properties near that area are
    commercial properties or will most likely be commercial properties in the future.
    Commissioner Hemmert stated the intent of the zone is to be a transitional zone.
    Commissioner Ellis stated the only thing that gives him the reason to not say no is that it is
    labeled on the general plan as NC. Commissioner Nielsen stated that most NC zones
    transition to commercial at some point, especially with this item on Main Street.
    Commissioner Nielsen's concern is the safety issue where there will be citizens crossing
    Main Street. He states it is a hazard in his opinion and he would like to see sidewalks in front
    of the development. Commissioner Ellis asked if drive-thru's were allowed. Sierra Pierson
    stated they were allowed in a conditional use. Commissioner Nielsen stated infrastructure for
    walking and biking would be a good idea. Ross Dinsdale stated they probably wouldn't take
    back funding for that from the impact fees, but it would be a good area to work on sidewalks
    where there would be more pedestrians.

Motion: Commissioner Nielsen forwarded a positive recommendation of Casey Hansen’s
request for review of the Hansen zone change, changing the zoning on .33-acres of property
located at 1045 West Main Street from RA-1 (residential/agriculture) to NC (neighborhood
commercial).; including all DRC comments and findings stated in the reports, specifically that
they are squarely within the overlay for the Neighborhood Commercial zone. Commissioner
Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Hemmert, no; Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre; yes. The motion passed 5-2.
