                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1691

        AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF DRAPER CITY FOR
        APPROXIMATELY 1.44 ACRES LOCATED AT 231 EAST 13800 SOUTH FROM RA1
        (RESIDENTIAL, AGRICULTURAL) AND OR (OFFICE RESIDENTIAL) TO CR -
        (REGIONAL COMMERCIAL), OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE OPENSHAW DRAPER
        138 ZONING MAP AMENDMENT.

      WHEREAS, pursuant to State law, Draper City has adopted a Zoning Ordinance and
Zoning Map to guide the orderly development and use of property within the City; and

      WHEREAS, from time to time it is necessary to review and amend the Zoning Map to
keep pace with development within the City and to ensure the provision of a variety of
economic uses; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council finds that rezoning the subject 1.44-acre parcel to CR
(Regional Commercial) is consistent with the General Plan and the concurrently adopted
Land Use Map amendment only if the applicant voluntarily dedicates all additional rights-of-
way required by the City Engineer for the widening and improvement of 13800 South and
Bangerter Highway in conjunction with the Bangerter Crossroads project; and

      WHEREAS, the City seeks to secure these dedications through a development
agreement rather than through eminent domain; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed zone change set forth herein has been reviewed by the
Planning Commission and the City Council, and all appropriate public hearings have been
held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public input regarding the proposed revisions to
the Zoning Map; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed amendment to the official Zoning Map of Draper
City, and the City Council has found the proposed zone change, subject to the contingency
and effectiveness provisions herein, to be in the best interest of the health, safety, and
welfare of the citizens of Draper City and consistent with the City’s General Plan.

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

Section 1.     Findings and Contingent Approval. The City Council finds that the
amendment of the official Zoning Map as described in Section 2 below is in the best
interest of the health, safety, and welfare of the citizens of Draper City and is consistent
with the City’s General Plan, provided that appropriate infrastructure improvements, traffic
mitigation measures, voluntary dedication of all rights-of-way determined by the City

      Section 3.     Contingent Effectiveness; Expiration if Conditions Not Met. This
ordinance shall not take effect unless and until a development agreement between Draper
City and the applicant that adequately addresses traffic mitigation, roadway improvements,
voluntary dedication of all rights-of-way determined by the City Engineer to be necessary for
the widening and improvement of 13800 South and Bangerter Highway, and other
conditions deemed necessary by the City Council has been fully executed by all parties and
recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder. The City Recorder is hereby instructed not to
publish this ordinance until the development agreement has been fully executed and
recorded. Publication shall occur within ten (10) calendar days after recordation.

      All activities undertaken by the applicant prior to the effective date of this ordinance
are at the sole risk of the applicant, and no vested rights shall accrue until this ordinance

becomes effective. If the development agreement has not been executed by all parties and
recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder on or before May 7, 2026, this ordinance shall,
on May 8, 2026, automatically become null and void without further action by the City
Council, and the zoning designations (RA1 and OR) shall remain in full force and effect. The
City Recorder is instructed not to publish the ordinance in such event.

       Section 4.    Requirement of Development Agreement. This zone change is granted
in express reliance upon the property owner’s commitment to enter into a development
agreement with Draper City that includes, at a minimum, the voluntary dedication of all
rights-of-way determined by the City Engineer to be necessary for the widening and
improvement of 13800 South and Bangerter Highway, together with any additional
conditions deemed appropriate. The owner is expected to submit a complete development
agreement application no later than December 29, 2025, to allow processing and City Council
consideration on or before May 7, 2026.

       Section 5.    Severability Clause. If any part or provision of this Ordinance is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Ordinance and all provisions, clauses and words of this Ordinance shall be severable.
