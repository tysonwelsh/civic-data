Citizen Comments


1.         22-414                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Council Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comment.

                                  Ms. Pat Jones spoke about Alta Canyon Recreation Center and the importance of 
                                  gathering community feedback on the project. She is not supportive of a tax increase to 
                                  pay for a new recreation center. Ms. Jones reviewed and summarized the results from the 
                                  recent city flash vote survey and offered feedback. She also provided statistics related to 
                                  average household debts following the Covid pandemic.  

                                  Mr. Steve Van Maren does not think it is feasible to move the Alta Canyon Recreation 
                                  Center to a new location. 

                                  Public comment closed.

Business Session Items

Informational Items


2.         23-027                 2022 Community Events Presentation

                   Attachments:   Community Events Overview 2022

                                  Kim Bell, Eric Richards, Keaton Assay with Administration presented a video promoting 
                                  Sandy City and highlighting what Sandy City has to offer the community - capturing the 
                                  Heart of the Wasatch. Following the video, they presented a review of the community 
                                  events that took place during 2022.  City events offer a great opportunity to bring people 
                                  together and help to create a strong sense of community. Eric Richards reviewed the 
                                  communications and messaging  from the City used in promoting city events. Keaton 
                                  Assay reviewed the community participation in the 5K Races and spoke about our city 
                                  partners and sponsors of these events. Kim Bell provided information on many city 
                                  events: Historic Sandy Barbecue, Movies in the Park, July 4th Celebration, Balloon 
                                  Festival, Heritage Festival and Horse Parade, Trunk or Treat, and Light up the Cairns. 
                                  They reviewed the event goals for 2023. Council thanked the staff for the presentation and 
                                  for their coordination of these community events.

Council Member Business

                    Council Member Stroud spoke about the National League of Cities and Towns annual 
                    conference in Washington DC. Council discussed participation by members at this 
                    conference and was very supportive of members attending the conference. The annual 
                    conference provides an opportunity to gain valuable insights that will benefit our city and 
                    residents. Council discussion also included the utilization of funding sources to cover 
                    expenses associated with attendance at the conference.

Mayor's Report

                    The CAO provided the Mayor's report.

CAO Report

                    Shane Pace, CAO, provided an update on the snow removal process and mentioned that 
                    the city has almost depleted the inventory of salt reserves. The city may experience 
                    flooding this spring due to the runoff from the winter snowfall.

Adjournment
                    Council unanimously agreed to adjourn the meeting at 7:36 pm.
