Pre-Council, 6:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers present. Councilor Condie
gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Robert's Rules of Order Training
Todd Godfrey with the Utah League of Cities and Towns gave suggestions to the Council on
how to conduct meetings in a manner that’s more efficient and professional.

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
None.

5. Mayor and Council Reports
The Council reported on the various committees they serve on.

The Council recessed at approximately 6:40 p.m.
The meeting reconvened at approximately 7:00 p.m.

Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome, Roll Call, Pledge of Allegiance
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and n0oted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Hancock led the Pledge.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Proclaim November 2021 as Epilepsy Awareness Month
Sarah Hansen is an advocate for Epilepsy Awareness and asked the Mayor to sign the
proclamation. Mayor Johnson read the proclamation.

3. 20 Minute Citizen Input
Jackson Brown, Conner Cooper and Elijah Newall invited the Council to attend the Skyridge
production of SpongeBob.

Brian McCuiston asked that the Council consider the development code process and ensure that
they are making updates to the City code.

Michelle Stallings expressed concerns with the PARC tax affecting those of a fixed income
because a portion of the utility bill will be affected by the tax. She stated that he city committed
to reducing power rates to make up the difference and she wanted to know when that will
happen.


4. Consent Agenda
4.1) Approve Minutes from the October 5, 2021 Work Session Meeting and the October 12, 2021
   City Council Meeting.
Councilor Condie suggested a change to the October 12 minutes.
4.2) Re-approval of Resolution #2021-57, Intent to adjust a municipal boundary line between Lehi
and Draper City.

      Motion: Councilor Condie moved to approve the consent agenda items with the suggested
      changes to the minutes on October 12, 2021. Councilor Koivisto seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


5. Consideration of Resolution #2021-60, approving an updated Storm Water
   Management Program.
   Dave Norman stated that they made some minor changed to update the Plan.

    Motion: Councilor Southwick moved to approve Resolution #2021-60, approving an
    updated Storm Water Management Program. Council Koivisto seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, Yes; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


6. Consideration of Ordinance #56-2021, approving a zone change from A-5 (agriculture) to
    Neighborhood Commercial and R-1-Flex (residential) on 20.10-acres of property located
    at 2100 North 2300 West.
    Items 6 and 7 were heard together.
Lehi City Council Meeting                         2                                   October 26, 2021
    Eric Towner was representing the applicant. He said that they would like to mitigate any cut
    through traffic as much as possible. He stated that they are also asking for 3 lots in exchange
    for improvements and construction of the pedestrian trail.

    Brian McCuiston suggested that all lots have at least a 20 foot drive. He would also prefer to
    see the city and school district properties developed in order to provide an additional access to
    2300 West.

    Aaron Bullen doesn’t agree with this being approved as a PUD and feels that the open space
    requirements have not been met because they are remnants. He feels that the City gets little in
    return for this.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to approve Ordinance #56-2021, approving a zone change
                from A-5 (agriculture) to Neighborhood Commercial and R-1-Flex (residential) on
                20.10-acres of property located at 2100 North 2300 West; including any
                Development Review Committee and Planning Commission comments. Councilor
                Southwick seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Hancock, Yes;
    Councilor Koivisto, No; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed 4-1.
