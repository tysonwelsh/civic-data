      1. FIRST READING OF FEE ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS RELATING TO THE WEBER COUNTY COMMUNITY
         DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT, SOLID WASTE DIVISION (SEC. 16-2-9, SOLID WASTE FEES).
            Bill Ross, County Community Development Assistant Director, stated that this amendment is to increase the
            general waste tipping fee at the County Transfer Station in response to the annual Consumer Price Index
            increase of 1.9% required by the county’s contract with Republic Services (by approximately 51 cents/ton), and
            operational cost increases including fuel, utilities, salaries, etc. (by approximately 49 cents/ton). The current
            waste tipping fee is $39.49/ton. This was presented to the Solid Waste subcommittee and WACOG earlier this
            year and they recommended approval. Commissioner Harvey reiterated that the main reason for the increase
            is for transportation costs by the transportation company to/from the facility. He complemented the Transfer
            Station’s management stating that 2½ years ago the facility was losing $300,000/year but it has turned that
            around very positively. John Watson, County Solid Waste Director, said that incoming waste has increased
            significantly from last year.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the first reading of Fee Ordinance amendments relating to the Weber
            County Community Development Department, Solid Waste Division (Sec. 16-2-9, Solid Waste Fees);
            Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

    13. CHANGE ORDER #3 TO BH, INC., CONTRACT TO COVER CHANGES TO THE 12TH STREET CORRIDOR DESIGN
        SEGMENT 3 PROJECT.
         Jared Andersen, of County Engineering, stated that County Engineering had met with the commissioners
         individually to discuss this item at length. Having worked in the contracting business for 31 years now,
         Commissioner Harvey has very strong feelings against change orders. Since the discussion with Mr. Andersen
         on this item, there have been process changes and Commissioner Harvey said that he would hold Mr. Andersen
         to what he committed to in an email. Commissioner Harvey cautioned that the Commission is the body
         entrusted by the public to make financial decisions for the county, that they should not be occurring in other
         divisions of the county, that change orders should only be approved in a public meeting, and the way business
         was done in the county in the past was poor process. This commission wants to eliminate all poor processes in
         the county and to be financially responsible. Commissioner Froerer understands that there are some unforeseen
         cases that mandate change orders. He said that it is better for change orders to come before the Commission
         earlier in the process rather than later, and that the contractors need to be aware that any change orders need to
         be brought before the Commission in a public meeting for approval. He recommended focusing on the original
         bidding process to try eliminating change orders. Chair Jenkins said that the largest part of this specific change
         order was for extra tonnage of large diameter rock to fill the bottom of ditches and it seems to be working well.
         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Change Order #3 to BH, Inc., contract to cover changes to the 12th
         street corridor design segment 3 project; Chair Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
