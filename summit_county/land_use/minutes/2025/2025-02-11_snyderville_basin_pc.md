Work Session
(4:30 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney welcomed new commission members and thanked them for being willing to serve.
1)
Work Session regarding a Master Planned Development for Outlets Park City, an 19-building mixed use redevelopment project located at 6699 N Landmark Dr (Parcel FSE-1, containing 37.32 acres); Kimball Junction, Summit County, UT. The applicant is proposing to Rezone Parcel FSE-1 from the Town Center Zone to the Neighborhood Mixed-Use 1 (NMU-1) Zone. Parcel FSE-1 (37.32 acres); Applicant: Elliott Workgroup, representing SRE Ontario LLC; Project #24-087; Administrative and Legislative Review. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.

Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
introduced the Master Planned Development for Outlets Park City for work session. He provided a history of the project for new commissioners who had not seen the project as presented in prior Commission Meetings and explained what to expect moving forward.
Craig Elliott,
Elliott Workgroup Architect
, presented the Junction Commons Master Plan. (4:34 P.M.)

4)
Public hearing and recommendation to the County Council for amendments to Snyderville Basin Development Code Sections 10-3-20, Building Permit and Certificate of Occupancy; 10-4-3, Critical Lands; 10-4-22, Height Regulations; 10-8-18, Wireless Communications; and 10-11-1, Definitions. The purpose of the amendments is to modify the defined term of Existing Grade and correct references to "Existing Grade" throughout the Development Code and to create criteria for development on natural slopes exceeding thirty percent. Legislative Process. Project #24-164. Laura Kuhrmeyer, County Planner.
(6:16 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Development Code Amendment Slopes Staff Report 2.11.25
Attachment:
Exhibit A: Table of Proposed Amendments Critical Lands
Planner Laura Kuhrmeyer presented amendments to the Snyderville Basin Development Code to clarify the existing grade and change its references of "existing grade" to "natural grade" for recommendation to the County Council.
Commission Chair Mooney opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:21 p.m.
No one from the public came forward to speak.
Commission Chair Mooney closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:21 p.m.
Commissioner Nagie had a comment for Planner Kuhrmeyer regarding the capitalizing of US Army Corps of Engineers. Commissioner Carmichael provided a comment for clarifying where to find the definition in the code. (6:22 P.M.)
Makena Hawley made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the County Council to amend the language in the Snyderville Basin Development Code as described in the staff report according to the findings of fact and conclusions of law. Bruce Carmichael seconded, and all voted in favor, (6-0). Absent: Megan Recher.
