Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order.

ELECTRIC BOND PARAMETERS RESOLUTION 04-28-2020 ADOPTED.

  Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Electric Bond Parameters Resolution 04-28-2020 with an
amendment to change the time of the public hearing from 7:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. on May 19, 2020.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on the following vote:
                               Nay: Memmott
                               Yes: Seely, Worwood, Ostler, Jones

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

   Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn and convene into executive session to discuss the character
and competence of an individual, real property acquisition and disposal and investigative proceedings.
Councilor Ostler seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 9:40 p.m.
