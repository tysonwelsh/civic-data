Ds Haun Ventures Storage Containers; 2821 West Haun Drive; Temporary Use Permit (150
days) for temporary shipping containers during construction; RR-1D Zone; Brett Wilson
(applicant) [#34477; parcel 27-04-126-008]
Megan Jensen gave an overview of the site, which is in a Rural Residential zone. The applicant would
like to utilize four storage containers while getting a building permit to construct a future garage. She
pointed out the locations of the containers and stated that they are each 8’ x 40’ x 8.5’ tall. The
containers are used to store personal items, supplies, tools, and construction materials. A temporary use
permit for storage containers is allowed in any zone. The containers are set back a minimum of 3 feet
from the property lines and follows the accessory structure ordinance requirements.
Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the Temporary Use Permit for the Haun
Ventures Storage Containers, located at 2821 West Haun Drive in an RR-1D zone, with the Conditions
of Approval listed.
Conditions and Requirements of Approval:
1. The storage activities shall be consistent with the submitted application and site plan.
2. The storage containers and future garage shall not be used in association with a business unless
the applicant obtains a home occupation business license and adheres to §13-11 of West Jordan
City code.
3. The permit is valid from April 1, 2025 to August 29, 2025 or shall not exceed 150 consecutive
calendar days.
Ammon Allen asked if the storage containers are only allowed until the building permit is approved or
until the garage is constructed.
Megan Jensen said it could be either, but the time limit is just 150 days.
