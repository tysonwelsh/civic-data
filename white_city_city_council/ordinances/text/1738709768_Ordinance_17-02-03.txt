BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the White City Metro Township Council met in
a regular public meeting on January 5, 2017.
WITNESS
WHEREAS, the incorporation of the White City Metro Township requires the Council to
adopt a Land Use Plan and Code of Ordinance for White City, per State Law; and
WHEREAS, prior to the creation of the White City Metro Township, Salt Lake County
was the governing jurisdiction responsible for writing these plans, rules and regulations for
White City; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County had developed the White City Community Plan/Land Use
Plan in 1994 to guide land use decisions in White City; and
WHEREAS, the 1994 plan was never adopted by the Salt Lake County Commission for
use in White City; and
WHEREAS, the 1994 preliminary plan better reflects the current and future land use in
the White City Metro Township; and,
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County presented copies of the Older White City Land Use Plan,
and an amended copy of the Code of Ordinances used by Salt Lake County at a Metro Township
Council Training on December 3, 2016.

WHEREAS, the White City voters, in the November 3, 2015 election, voted to
incorporate as a Metro Township and to join the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
(GSLMSD) as members of the District; and

WHEREAS, accepting and adopting the 1994 Preliminary White City Community/Land
Use Plan, and Code of Ordinance provides for a smoother transition and creates the basis moving
forward for White City to modify and change these to better reflect the needs and best wishes of
the people of White City.

RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the White City Metro Township Council does
hereby accept and approve the 1994 Preliminary White City Community/Land Use Plan, and
amended Code of Ordinance as presented by Salt Lake County and the District Attorney to be
the land use plan and code of ordinances for the White City Metro Township.
