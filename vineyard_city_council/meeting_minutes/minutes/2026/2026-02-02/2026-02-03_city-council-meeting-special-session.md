2.   PRESENTATIONS/RECOGNITIONS/AWARDS/PROCLAMATIONS
     2.1. Chris Harding Presentation

     Chris Harding with CPA Insight Solutions explained work that he had done for the city in the
past and presented potential work that the council has asked him to do. He broke down the costs and
expected work for several different possible projects.

    Council discussed the previous audit that Mr. Harding had performed. Additionally, they asked
questions about future work with the city as it related to his work proposals as well as the different
types of audits he could perform and what the differences were between them. There was discussion
regarding the benefits of performing a forensic audit.

     Councilmember Nair asked if there had been any other bids for the additional audits and if any
of them had been firms that specialize in forensic audits. A discussion ensued.

      3.1. State Auditor's Office Presentation and Question and Answer


     Seth Oveson with the State Auditor’s Office, presented, regarding concerns that had come to his
office from the city. They ranged from questions regarding GRAMA requests, release of the general
ledger, findings that had been provided to the city, and the process by which the city responded to
the audit findings.

    The council thanked Mr. Oveson for his work and discussed the content of the presentation.
There were several clarifying questions asked by the council.
