                              EMIGRATION CANYON COUNCIL
                               NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code § 10-6-113 that the Emigration Canyon
Council will hold a public hearing on June 25, 2024 at 7:00 PM to receive public comment on
Emigration Canyon’s proposed budget for FY 2025. The meeting will be held at the Emigration
Canyon Fire Station, 5025 Emigration Canyon Road, Emigration Canyon, Utah 84108, and via
Zoom.com, https://us02web.zoom.us/j/83328451408.

The Council will hold two public hearings to consider the adoption of the FY 2025 Tentative Budget
and the 2024-2025 fiscal year fee schedule after taking comments from the public during the
public hearings. The public is also invited to review and inspect the proposed budget and the
proposed fee schedule on the Utah Public Notice Website (https://www.utah.gov/pmn/) and on
Emigration Canyon’s official webpage (https://www.ecmetro.org/).

You may also obtain copies of the proposed budget and fee schedule and submit written
comments prior to the public hearing by contacting Nichole Watt at nwatt@saltlakecounty.gov or
(385) 468-7400. Individuals needing special accommodations (including ancillary communicative
aids and services) during this public hearing should contact Ms. Watt three (3) days before the
public hearing.
