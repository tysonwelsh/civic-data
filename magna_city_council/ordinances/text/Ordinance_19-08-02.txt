ORDINANCE NO.: 19-08-02 DATE: August 27, 2019
MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP
AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 15.14.010 (Map only) OF THE MAGNA
CODE OF ORDINANCES 2017, BY MODIFYING THE SPECIAL
DEVELOPMENT STANDARDS AREA MAP.
BE IT HEREBY ORDAINED by the Magna Township Council as the legislative body of
the Magna Metro Township as follows:
Section 1: Amendment to Special Development Standards Areas Map regarding
Parcel No. 14-33-328-013-0000.
The Special Development Standards Areas Map, which was adopted by Section
15.14.010 of the Magna Municipal Code of Ordinances 2017 depicting the overpressure
areas surrounding the Northrop Grumman (formerly Hercules and ATK) Bacchus Works,
is modified and amended, by the Amended Map, attached as Exhibit A, as to Parcel No.
14-33-328-013-0000, so that the 0.5 PSI overpressure area shall only be the area within a
3470 foot radius of the following GPS coordinate, and the 0.3 PSI overpressure area
shall be the area of Parcel No. 14-33-328-013-0000 outside the 0.5 PSI overpressure area
but within a 5200 foot radius of the following GPS coordinate:
Area 1— N40d40'38.3" W112d04'20.0”
The Amended Map, attached as Exhibit A, depicts the 0.5 PSI overpressure
area and the 0.3 PSI overpressure area, as amended, for Parcel No. 14-33-
328-013-0000.
1

Section 2: Notwithstanding the above, any portions of Parcel No. 14-33-328-013-
0000 within 3470 feet of the above GPS coordinate but which (i) are restricted to use as
roads, open space, or detention or retention basins, and (ii) are not contained in a lot on
which a residential structure is allowed, shall also be 0.3 psi overpressure area so that
such areas can be included in a subdivision plat of 0.3 psi overpressure area.

Section 3: This ordinance will take effect 15 days after its passage and upon at
least one publication in a newspaper publishes in and having general circulation in
Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, and if not so published within 15 days then it
will take effect immediately upon its first publication.
