                           RESOLUTION NO. 25-42

      A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CERTIFYING
      THE OFFICIAL CANVASS OF THE MUNICIPAL PRIMARY
      ELECTION RESULTS HELD ON AUGUST 12, 2025

      WHEREAS, on August 12, 2025, Draper City conducted a Municipal
Primary Election for one At-Large City Council seat for a two-year term; and

      WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated § 20A-4-301, the Draper
City Council, acting as the Board of Canvassers, has reviewed the election
returns for the 2025 Municipal Primary Election held on August 12, 2025; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 20A-4-304 requires the Board of Canvassers to
declare as “nominated” those candidates who received the highest number
of votes within the jurisdiction and to certify the official election results; and

      WHEREAS, the Election Officer has submitted an election report to the
Board of Canvassers containing the vote totals, ballot reconciliation, and
other information required by law, which the Board of Canvassers has
examined and approved;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF CANVASSERS OF
DRAPER CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Canvass and Certification. The Board of Canvassers
hereby accepts, declares, and certifies the results of the Draper City
Municipal Primary Election held on August 12, 2025, as set forth in Exhibit A,
as the official canvass results.

     Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective
immediately upon its passage.
