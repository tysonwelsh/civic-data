   1. Desert Color Resort Phase 4B Planned Development Amendment – PUBLIC HEARING: Eric Day,
      representing Cole West Homes, is requesting approval of an amendment to the Desert Color PD (Planned
      Development). The amendment would allow the applicant to update the architectural elevations on
      approximately three buildings in Desert Color Resort Phase 4B. The proposed buildings are located
      generally west of the Marilla Drive and Pura Lane intersection. Case No. 2023-PDA-015 (Staff – Dan
      Boles)

       Dan Boles presented the following:

       Dan Boles – This has been approved as a larger multi-phase project. They are not asking for any more
       units than they have already been approved for. They are just asking to change the elevations. They are
       asking to build two 7-unit buildings for a total of 14 units. They meet the required parking for Desert
       Color. The elevations that were approved were approved were 2 stories and they were 23 ft tall, they are
       proposing to go to the 3-story building that are approximately 39 ft tall. I took a sample of the building
       permits in this neighborhood; it is a good example of that.

       Chair Kemp – So they are just making them taller with more square footage?

       Dan Boles – Yes.

       Chair Kemp – How many bedrooms and bathrooms are we adding?
