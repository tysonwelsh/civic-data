City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption - Ordinance #1623
On September 17, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1623 amending the text of Chapter Four of the Draper City General Plan. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder's Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 19th day of September 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
