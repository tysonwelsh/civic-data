                 Rezone of approximately 3.14-acre located at 13172 South Redwood Road.
                 Changing the General Plan Designation to High Density Residential and the

This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-26-4002,
“Majestic Homes Rezone,” by changing the General Plan Designation to High Density
Residential and Rezoning the subject property to RM-14.

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-26-4002, “Majestic
Rezone,” thus keeping the current General Plan Designation of Medium Density Residential
and current Zoning of RR-22 and C-N.

Background

This is a public hearing for a Rezone application brought by the applicant, Majestic Homes for
3.14-acres located at 13172 South Redwood Road

The subject parcel is a corner property with frontage on three public streets. The property is
vacant. The zoning on the parcel is a combination of RR-22 and C-N. RR-22 is a single
family residential zone with half-acre lots and animal rights. C-N is the Neighborhood
Commercial zone. The area zoning is mostly RR-22 with some C-N, C-PO, RM-6 and R-4
zoning in the area.

The current General Plan for the property is Medium Density Residential which corresponds
with lot sizes greater than 14,000-sf (1/3-acre). Most of the area is Medium Density Residential
on the west side of Redwood and Estate Density Residential on the east side of Redwood.
Redwood, south of city hall has historically been a boundary for big lots and historic Riverton,
with changes on the east side only recently being made to allow density less than ½-acre. The
west side of Redwood has more diverse zoning, but more cohesive General Plan of Medium
Density Residential (min 10,000-sf lots) which is consistent with R-4 zoning.
In recent months, the city has received a number of applications for increased density along the
south portion of Redwood Road. Many of the neighbors have expressed concern about the
number of applications. The city does not recruit applications but responds to people who
express interest in doing projects. Utah’s housing crisis has been well publicized, so has the
calls from the Governor, the Housing Czar, and the legislator for Cities to increase housing
opportunities. Vacant properties will continue to generate interest from developers, and the city
will continue to face pressures to rezone vacant properties to higher densities. However, with
this and all other rezone applications, the zoning should be evaluated on its merits and
appropriateness to the property and surrounding area, in addition to other considerations.

The applicant is requesting the RM-14 zone which allows density up to 14 units per acre. The
RM-14 Zone also requires specific parking, guest parking, road widths setbacks, building
separation and open space. The result is that most proposed projects do not reach the
maximum density. The applicants concept plans show small clusters of townhomes mixed with
open space resulting in a density of about 12 units per acre. This parcel has a unique setting
with frontage on three public streets and unlike many other infill projects it shares only three
property lines with existing houses. With the available roadway connections, there would not be
access granted to Redwood Road.
