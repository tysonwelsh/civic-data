ITEMS OF SPECIAL INTEREST

> RAPZ Funding Request for Tour of Utah — Mayor Craig Petersen said the Tour of
Utah has contacted him and asked for the race to begin in Logan on Monday, July 31,

Cache County Council
01-10-2017

ACTION: Motion by Council member Potter to approve the $25,000.00 in unallocated
RAPZ funds for Logan City to host the 2017 Tour of Utah. White seconded the motion.
The vote was unanimous, 6-0. Zilles absent.

> Presentation of PILT Payment — DWR Northern Region Supervisor Justin Dolling
presented the county a check for $9,072.73 for payment in lieu of taxes (PILT) on a little

over 18,000 acres it oversees and manages in Cache County. Dolling reviewed updates
on wild turkey problems, the Hardware Ranch water system project and extreme
weather effects on big game herds.

Executive Buttars has asked UDOT for temporary signage on the highway near
Richmond where a large herd of elk congregates in the winter, but has not received any
response. Buttars asked Dolling if he can look into the possibility of signage. This spot
on the highway is a big game high kill area and dangerous for motorists.

PUBLIC HEARINGS, APPEALS AND BOARD OF EQUALIZATION MATTERS

PUBLIC HEARING: JANUARY 10, 2017 AT 5:30 P.M. - NIXON & NIXON, INC. REZONE —
Request for approval for a rezone of 72.90 acres to add the Mineral Extraction and Excavation
ME) Overlay Zone to an existing Agricultural (A10) Zone located approximately 6500 North 800
East, north of Smithfield —- Chris Harrild explained this is located north of Smithfield in the vicinity
of other gravel pits and is a high resource potential area. Staff recommends approval.

Chairman Merrill opened the Public Hearing and invited public comments. There were none.

ACTION: Motion by Council member Potter to close the Public Hearing-January 10, 2017-
5:30 p.m.-Nixon & Nixon, Inc. Rezone. Erickson seconded the motion. The vote was
unanimous, 6-0. Zilles absent.

Harrild has received comments from the Corbett’s who live in the area and are concerned about
dust and times of operation. Council member White and Vice Chair Erickson expressed
concern that the area is next to Cache County’s gravel pit and there is evidence that others
have been extracting gravel from the county’s property. Those issues will be addressed in the
conditional use permit application.

Council member Ward indicated that because the Planning Commission was unanimous in
recommending approval he would support the rezone.
