REQUEST
Architects Joe Jacoby and Sanae Adams, with Jacoby Architects and Tom Gibbs, representing
the Utah State Department of Alcoholic Beverages Services (DABS) and Matthias Mueller,
representing the Utah State Division of Facilities Construction and Management (DFCM) are
requesting Conditional Use Review to modify a portion of the existing, vacant Albertson's
Fresh Values food market building and parking lot, for use as a Utah State Liquor and Wine
retail store. The property is part of the Little Cottonwood Center commercial shopping
center project, which includes approximately 45 acres of commercially zoned land with
many restaurants and retail shops and two large "anchor" stores, including the now vacant
old Albertson's Fresh Values grocery store building and the Home Depot home
improvement store. The Site is located on the southwest corner of 9400 S. Street and
Highland Drive (2000 East).

The entire shopping center is zoned Special Development SD (Magna) CC Community
Commercial. An "Alcoholic Beverage State Store "is listed in the SD(Magna) CC zoning
district as a Conditional Use, and therefore requires specific approval from the Planning
Commission. A Site Plan Review application (SPR05242022-006333) has also been filed by
the State and is addressed in the accompanying staff report. The Planning Commission is
the land-use authority for these requested actions.

BACKGROUND
The State Division of Facilities Construction and Management (DFCM) has entered into a long
term sub-lease with the parent company of Albertson's, Associated Fresh Markets, Inc, to
occupy the southern portion of the vacant food store building, and the adjacent surface
parking lot. Approximately 14, 657 Square Feet will be used for the liquor store operation.

CONDITIONAL USE ANALYSIS
Conditional Use consideration for: "Alcoholic Beverage State Store" in the Special
Development SD (Magna) CC Zoning District.

Under the Sandy City Development Code, the Planning Commission, through the Conditional
Use process, can: approve Conditional Uses if no mitigation of reasonably anticipated
detrimental impacts is necessary; approve Conditional Uses, subject to reasonable conditions
designed to mitigate or eliminate reasonably anticipated detrimental impacts; or deny Conditional
Uses, if it is determined that reasonably anticipated detrimental impacts cannot be adequately
mitigated. The burden of mitigating these impacts or effects is the responsibility of the applicant
and not of the municipality or adjacent property owners (Section 21-33-04).

Compliance with Section 21-33-04 Conditional Use Permit:
In order to achieve compliance with the standards set forth herein, the City may impose conditions
that address these standards:

building.

2.     Proposed site ingress and egress to existing and proposed roads and streets.

The ingress and egress to and from the site is existing and will be adequate for use by these
facilities.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission grant the Conditional Use requests
to allow the Alcoholic Beverage State Store, based on the staff report, the staff
findings 1 to 15 in the above analysis of the Conditional Use Standards and the three
additional findings listed below and the three conditions detailed in the staff report:

FINDINGS:

  A. That the various City Departments and Divisions, including the City Engineer and the
     Transportation Engineer, have preliminarily approved the proposed related site plan.

  B. That the proposed site planning and infrastructure improvements, building design and its
     building spacing, open space and parking arrangements will provide substantial mitigation
     ofreasonably anticipated detrimental impacts ofthe proposed State Liquor Store land use
     within the SD (Magna) CC zoning district.

  C. That the proposed land use request has been reviewed by staff considering the 15 standards
     contained in the Sandy City Development Code, 21-33-04, Conditional Use Process, and
     appropriate and adequate mitigation measures will be implemented or will be imposed as
     deemed necessary to minimize impacts to the surrounding business community, subject to
     the following three conditions:

CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:

    1. That the applicant proceeds through final site plan approval with staff as required by the
       Sandy City Development Code.

    2. That the Conditional Use Permit for the proposed "Alcoholic Beverage State Store", be
       reviewed upon future legitimate citizen complaint or staff observation ofnon-compliance
