          1.       CALL TO ORDER
              PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE
              INVOCATION/INSPIRATIONAL THOUGHT
          Mayor Fullmer opened the meeting at 6:00 PM. Councilmember Welsh led the Pledge of
          Allegiance and gave the invocation.

          2.         PUBLIC COMMENTS

          Mayor Fullmer called for public comments. Hearing none she closed the public session.

          3.        MAYOR AND COUNCILMEMBERS’ REPORTS/DISCLOSURES/RECUSALS
          Councilmember Flake reported that the Utah Lake Commission met last month. He said that the
          lake was almost full and that the reservoirs up river were full. He felt it was going to be a good
          water year. He said that the treatment plans for the algae on the lake had been approved by the
          scientific committee. There were going to be two different methods to treat two different
          locations on the lake. The Walkara Way project was moving ahead in the spring. He said that
          there was a small issue with Representative Brammer wanting to make the Utah Lake
          Commission a state entity and to only have two nonstate agencies on their board. He said that
          each city representative at the commission meeting was against the proposal.

          4.        STAFF, COMMISSION, AND COMMITTEE REPORTS
          Resident and Clean Air Task Force Committee Member Josh Gilman reported on the things that
          had been accomplished in 2019. One goal was walking and biking locally. He said that he had a
          booth at the Summer Celebration, where he talked about the clean air goals in Vineyard. He
          researched Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy (C-PACE) and worked with the city
          manager, and presented a resolution to the council, which they adopted. Another goal was to
          have electrical vehicle charging stations installed. He researched cost-effective ways to install
          them in Vineyard. He said that he met with staff and Leaders for Clean Air (a nonprofit
          organization) who received grants and private donations. He said that he attended the Clean Air
          Task Force and Sustainability Coalition meetings throughout the year. He felt that he represented
          Vineyard well, giving them exposure in Utah County. He said that he would send his tentative
          goals for 2020 to council. He said that air quality remains a serious public issue in Utah County.
          He asked for feedback from city leaders about how he could help in 2020. Mayor Fullmer
          mentioned that the council would be setting priorities on the 24th of this month so they would
          need his list.

          5. DISCUSSION ITEMS
             No items were submitted.

             6.1 Approval of the December 11, 2019 City Council Minutes
             6.2 Approval of Purchases – Crack Seal for Sleepy Ridge

          7. MAYOR’S APPOINTMENTS

          8. BUSINESS ITEMS
              8.1         DISCUSSION AND ACTION – Fiscal Year 2018-2019 Audit Report
                    Gilbert and Stewart will present the annual financial audit report for fiscal year 2018-
                    2019 for consideration and acceptance by the Council. The mayor and city council will
                    act to accept the report.

          Mayor Fullmer turned the time over to Spencer Hintze with Gilbert and Stewart for the Audit
          Report.

          Mr. Hintze explained the procedures they went through for the audit and how the report worked.
          He said that they had issued a clean, unmodified opinion. He mentioned that they do not express
          an opinion on internal controls, but did not find any deficiencies. He explained that they issued

          an opinion on state compliance. He said that in their opinion Vineyard complied, in all material
          respects, with compliance requirements. He explained that there was one finding on the audit
          because of a department being over budget, which was being corrected. He mentioned that the
          finding from last year had been corrected this year. He felt that the city was moving in the right
          direction to take care of the things they needed to. He said that in his experience, cities that were
          growing as fast a Vineyard was would tend to run into budgetary issues.

          Mayor Fullmer asked for questions from council. She mentioned that Mr. McHargue would be
          able to answer any questions the council might have. There were no questions.
