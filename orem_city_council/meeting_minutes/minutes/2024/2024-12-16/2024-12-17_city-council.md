## CALL TO ORDER

## CONSENT ITEMS

### RESOLUTION - Approving an interlocal agreement with Vineyard, Lindon, and Pleasant Grove related to the reorganized school district.

Ms. Millett requested a discussion on the resolution item. Mayor Young opened this item up for discussion. Ms. Millett is concerned that the agreement does not have an end date, she wants to make sure that the City Council involvement would end when the formation of the new school board is elected. Mr. Earl, the City Attorney, explained that the language that refers to the end of this interlocal is buried in section two. He continued to explain that this interlocal agreement is very limited in scope. The only powers granted to the joint board are to make the filing deadlines that state law requires. If the board wants anything beyond that narrow scope then the powers will have to come back and amend the agreement which will require the approval of all four City Councils.

Mayor Young clarified with Mr. Earl that currently the cities have equal voting power. Mr. Earl explained that there is not a concern about having a disproportionate voting power if the functions are limited to the narrow stated purposes. If the interlocal agreement was going to expand the scope of power then the City might need to have a discussion about having disproportionate voting power.

### APPOINTMENT - Reorganized School District Interlocal Joint Board-Jenn Gale

**Mr. Macdonald moved** to approve consent items. **Seconded by** Ms. Gale. Those voting yes: David Young, LaNae Millett, David Spencer, Tom Macdonald, Jenn Gale, Jeff Lambson, and Chris Killpack. The motion **passed.**
