                 Community Performance Plan - 2020
             Riverton City (City) & Rocky Mountain Power (RMP)

Rocky Mountain Power intends to be one of the premier companies you deal with in
your community. To be this kind of company, we want to better understand your needs
and what we can do to meet your expectations. By understanding your specific needs, we
will be better positioned to employ our resources to provide exceptional quality service.

The following summary of our discussion establishes a basis for going forward and our
commitment to you during the coming year. At the conclusion of the year, we will review
this plan with you to ensure that we have provided you with the service outlined in this
plan.

   •   Be timely, responsive, and professional in all communications with a focus on
       partnering to achieve mutually beneficial solutions. Regional Business Manager
       will act as main contact point for power­ related issues, including electric
       infrastructure planning, permitting, and siting.

   •   Coordinate annual joint infrastructure planning meeting with Riverton and Rocky
       Mountain Power staff.

   •   Coordinate appropriate public or private outreach and permitting, where applicable,
       including for Rocky Mountain Power's new 12600 South Substation, with Riverton
       City staff and, if appropriate, elected officials for Rocky Mountain Power
       infrastructure projects such as transmission line and substation construction and
       improvements.

   •   Continue to support Riverton City with community sponsorship opportunities that
       become available for both the City and Rocky Mountain Power such as Riverton
       Town Days or other opportunities appropriate for the times.


   •   Support Riverton City's efforts to pursue municipality energy efficiency initiatives
       or efforts as deemed appropriate, including any available Wattsmart energy
       efficiency incentives.
    •     Provide for the installation of a vehicle charge station at Riverton City Hall.

    •

Rocky Mountain Power commits to providing you exceptional service. We will do this by
following through on the issues identified above to your satisfaction. In return, we ask for
your commitment to:

    •     Work closely with Rocky Mountain Power to complete each of the areas identified.

    •     Notify your Customer and Community Manager if any of these issues has not been
          completed to your satisfaction.

    •     Allow Rocky Mountain Power an opportunity to resolve any outstanding issues with
          you.
