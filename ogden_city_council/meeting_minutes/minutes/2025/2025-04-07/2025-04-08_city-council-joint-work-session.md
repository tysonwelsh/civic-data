         The purpose of the joint work session was to discuss the proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2026-2030 Five Year Consolidated
Plan including the FY 2026 Annual Action Plan and Neighborhood Revitalization Strategy Area (NRSA); discuss proposed Own
in Ogden Program Amendments; receive an update regarding Redevelopment Agency (RDA) projects; discuss Council/Board
business; and hear Council/Board comment.

Council Business
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith reported she received a request from Police Chief Sube for a support letter for a
grant application for the Police Department; he asked for a signature from one Council member, but one member is not allowed to
represent the entire body unless the Council has voted in support of such. She has prepared a letter and included a signature block
for each Council member and asked if they are comfortable signing the letter tonight. The grant program is called Community
Guardian Regional Response Initiative, Phase 2. The Police Department received grant funding under Phase 1 of the same
program, and she provided a brief explanation of the purpose of the grant program.
         Council Deputy Director Symes reviewed requests for speaking engagements for individual Council members. Council
members volunteered for specific engagements they were available for.
         Council Executive Director Eller-Smith briefly reviewed the Council calendar for the remainder of the month of April.

Proposed Fiscal Year 2026-2030 Five Year Consolidated Plan including
the Fiscal Year 2026 Annual Action Plan and Neighborhood
Revitalization Strategy Area (NRSA); and
Proposed Own in Ogden Program Amendments
          Community Development Director Smith reported his Division has been working to develop the Five-Year Consolidated
Plan (ConPlan) for the period 2026-2030. Administration is proposing the Council adopt a resolution that approves the ConPlan,
an updated Neighborhood Revitalization Strategy Area (NRSA) plan, and the first year Annual Action Plan (AAP). Additionally,
Administration is proposing changes to the Own in Ogden Program guidelines. The current guidelines allow potential homebuyers
to have no more than $20,000 of cash savings to qualify for the program. The proposed changes include increasing the homebuyer
cash savings amount to $40,000 and to clarify that all equity proceeds from the sale of a prior residence must be used toward the
purchase of a home in Ogden.
          Senior Project Coordinator Fuentes used the aid of a PowerPoint presentation to provide information regarding the status
of the Federal Government Housing and Urban Development (HUD) grants. Congress passed a continuing resolution on March
15, 2025, which indicates that the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) and HOME investment partnerships grant will
be funded the same as Fiscal Year (FY) 2024. The ConPlan is the mechanism by which the City receives funding from HUD, and
it outlines what the City plans to achieve with HUD grant funds for a five-year period. If an activity is not specified in the
ConPlan, HUD funds cannot be used for it. The presentation then expounded on the ConPlan planning process, public
involvement, community priorities for ConPlan activities, the draft ConPlan, draft AAP for FY26, draft NRSA plan, and proposed
Own in Ogden changes. For a copy of the presentation in its entirety, see the information packet for the meeting.
          Throughout the presentation, Ms. Fuentes and Mr. Smith engaged in discussion with the Council regarding housing
programs and assistance included in the ConPlan; grant funds available to small businesses through the ConPlan; and the next
steps in the process to approve the ConPlan and AAP for FY26.

Council Business - continued
         David Sawyer, Community and Economic Development Assistant Executive Director, presented a flyer prepared by the
City regarding programming that could help federal employees transition from federal employment after being laid off; there will
be an event on Wednesday, April 23 at Ogden Weber Tech College Event Center to provide visitors with information regarding
career or educational paths and to connect them with support resources.
         The Council discussed federal government employee layoffs, in general, with a focus on the potential broad impacts to
the Ogden community given the large number of federal employees that live in the area; they also discussed any deportation
efforts underway in the City.


         The meeting adjourned at 6:12 p.m.
