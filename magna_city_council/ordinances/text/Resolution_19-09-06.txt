CANAL COMPANY
RECITALS
A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
401 et seq.
B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.
C. The Utah-Salt Lake Canal Company (the “Canal Company”) is an irrigation water service
provider that operates a canal within the municipality.
D. Magna’s municipal roadways cross the canal in several locations within the municipality.
E. In 2018, during routine inspection of roadway bridges in the Magna area, the Utah
Department of Transportation determined the bridges that cross the canal at 3500 South and
at 9200 West to be of the highest priority for replacement and appropriated funds to assist
Magna in the costs of replacement.
F. During the creation of their 2019 Budget, the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District
also appropriated funding for the bridge replacement projects.
G. To conduct the replacement requires a license agreement between the Canal Company and
Magna to perform the replacements.

H. The attached license agreements, (Exhibits A and B) allow Magna to proceed with the bridge
replacement projects over the wither to avoid disruption of irrigation service.
RESOLUTION
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Magna Metro Township Council hereby approves,
and authorizes the Mayor to sign the attached license agreements, hereinafter to be known as
Exhibit A (3500 South) and Exhibit B (9200 West), between Magna and the Utah-Salt Lake
Canal Company, allowing Magna to replace the roadway bridges located at 3500 South and
9200 West.

(Magna Metro Township 3500 S. Bridge Replacement Project)

This License Agreement is made and entered into by and between the Utah & Salt

WITNESSETH

and

WHEREAS, Licensor is willing to grant a license to Licensee, provided the terms of
this Agreement are strictly adhered to such that said license will not in any manner

related facilities.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual benefits which will accrue to
the parties hereto, the parties agree as follows:

1. PURPOSE.

The purpose of this License Agreement is to provide a right to Licensee to
construct, install, operate and maintain a concrete box culvert with 16-foot opening width
and an opening height varying from 5 feet 1 3/4 inches on the south side to 4 feet % inch on
the north side; together with associated wing walls, a C-10 Waterman screw gate, aprons
and cutoff walls and replacement of existing storm drain connections and roadway. The
bottom of the new box culvert shall be at the same elevation as the bottom of the existing
box culvert, which is the bottom flow line of the canal. The box culvert, screw gate,
aprons, cutoff walls, and wing walls are hereinafter referred to as the “Works.”

This license shall be limited to said purpose, and Licensee shall have no right to
alter the uses under this license in any manner without the prior written approval of
Licensor.
