3. Public hearing and possible action regarding a proposed Low Impact Permit to 
allow Open Recreation Uses, including campsites, a communal lodge, and 
restroom facilities for private use; CD-794; Carl Burg, Applicant – Sean Lewis, 
County Planner 
 
Planner Lewis presented the background of the application.  The Hansen family owns 
640 acres on the east fork of Chalk Creek.  They want to set up a family campsite that 
would not be open to the public.  They want to have 11 RV campsites that may 
eventually become 11 bunkhouses with bathrooms.  They also would like to have a 
larger building with cooking facilities.   
 
Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
Regular Meeting

Planner Lewis said a low impact permit (LIP) would normally go before the Community 
Development Director (CDD), but because of the unique nature, Director Putt thought it 
should come before the Planning Commission.  The unresolved issues are water, sewer, 
and access.  The applicant is not that far in the process because they wanted to come to 
Staff to see if this is a viable idea.   
 
Planner Lewis said water would be supplied by a well and septic tanks would be 
installed.  If approval is recommended to the CDD, they would then finalize their plans.  
The CDD would make the final decision.  A graphic was shown that demonstrated the 
basic setup of the camp.  This will only have space for the family.   
 
Commissioner Clyde asked what the land use would be.  Planner Lewis said Staff 
determined the land use would be open recreation, which calls out campsites.  
Currently, the family can take tents and RVs there today without any permit or 
requirements.  The bunkhouses wouldn’t qualify as accessory dwelling units because 
there would be no kitchen.  The shared kitchen would be in the main lodge.  It would be 
40’ x 40’.  The bunkhouse would have some beds and a restroom facility.   
 
Commissioner Wharton asked what the difference is between public and private open 
recreation use.  Planner Lewis said there is no difference.  He wanted to be sure it was 
understood this would be a private campsite.  Commissioner Wharton said the use is 
intended for family and friends.  Where does the line get drawn?  Could a church group 
use it?    
 
Chair Ure said they have approved hunting lodges that were supposed to be for hunters 
and an occasional family reunion.  These have turned into resort areas under the 
conditional use permit (CUP).  He asked how many septic systems will be installed.  Carl 
Berg, representing the landowner, said there will be one septic system.
