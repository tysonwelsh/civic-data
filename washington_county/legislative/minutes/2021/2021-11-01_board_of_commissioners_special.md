CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2021-2846, A RESOLUTION PROPOSING
THE CREATION OF THE MOUNTAIN FIRE SPECIAL SERVICE DISTRICT; AND
OTHER RELATED MATTERS

Commissioner Snow stated that situations have changed along the SR-18 corridor and so these
Resolutions will open the process of consolidating the process to combine the Special Service
Districts (SSDs).

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow explained that the North Central and Pine Valley SSDs
would be consolidated into the proposed Mountain Fire SSD with Baker Dam Road being the
dividing line. The second SSD, Western Fire SSD, would include Dammeron Valley SSD,
Diamond Valley SSD and Northwestern SSD. A hearing will be held in December on the creation
of the new districts, with a sixty (60) day protest period being held after the hearing. Mr. Snow
stated the goal is to have the new districts ready by March of 2022.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2021-2846, A
Resolution Proposing the Creation of the Mountain Fire Special Service
District; and Other Related Matters. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and
Snow voting aye.
