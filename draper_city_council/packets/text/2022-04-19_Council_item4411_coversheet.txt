Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Brien Maxfield
CC:  Date: April 19, 2022
Re:  Comments: Draper City staff desires to update the municipal code related to the application, issuance, inspection, and closeout of the Encroachment Permit (formally called the Excavation Permit or Street Cut Permit) found in Title 11 Chapter 6.  These changes clarify the permitting requirements associated with the required permit for work within the public streets within the city.  In addition, it clarifies in other city code sections that the permit is the Encroachment Permit rather than the other names used. The findings are as follows: The proposed amendments comply with state law and will benefit the health, safety and welfare of Draper residents.
