           and Site Plan for the Construction of a Car Wash at 3621 West 5400 South, Taylorsville, Utah.
           (Terryne Bergeson/Planner 1).

3.1      Ms. Bergeson presented this item.        Russell Nelson, Hailey Peterson (Lonestar Builders) and Danny
         Lange (Elevate Engineering) are requesting preliminary approval of a Non-administrative Conditional Use

         Permit and Site Plan for the construction and operation of a Quick quack Carwash at 3621 West 5400
         South, Taylorsville, Utah.

         3.1.1     Site changes include closing the two driveways fronting 5400 South, construction of a 94-foot
                   tunnel carwash with 14 vacuum stalls, and installation of new landscaping.

         3.1.2     Subject property is 34,412 square feet on the southwest corner of 3600 West 5400 South. The
                   existing structure, shed, and carwash building has been vacant (formerly the speedway
                   convenience store). Fuel tanks and gas pump canopies were removed by the property owner in
                   2022 and applicants received an environmental report showing no contamination.

         3.1.3     The property is in the Community Commercial (CC) zoning district and has a General Plan
                   Designation of also Community Commercial. The Taylorsville Land Development code lists a “car
                   wash” land use as permitted with the approval of an administrative conditional use permit, or if
                   within 250 feet of residential, permitted with approval of a non-administrative conditional use
                   permit.

         3.1.4     This site is currently vacant and any development on the property will increase the traffic at this
                   location. Access to the site would require customers entering from the south to turn left into the
                   parking lot and right to enter queuing lanes. A vehicle entering from the north would be required
                   to make a sharp right turn from Whitewood Lane to enter the queuing lanes. Eight stacking spaces
                   are provided per City requirements. A traffic study was not requested as part of the application.

         3.1.5     Noise from vacuums and tunnels is a concern with the residential zone. The applicant has
                   submitted a sound study that demonstrates the levels for residential would not exceed the
                   established 100 DBA maximum based on the most recently submitted concept plan.

         3.1.6     The commercial building to the west is built on the property line and site does show landscaping
                   and a tree proposed near the parking lot of the neighboring use, with no room to plant significant
                   buffering between the tunnel exit lane and the neighboring building.

         3.1.7     Fencing separating the proposed use and residential rear yards is an existing solid masonry wall
                   that meets City requirements.

         3.1.8     Applicants have submitted multiple revisions to meet key City requirements such as 15’ front and
                   10’ landscape buffers as well as space for stacking of four vehicles per lane.

         3.1.9     The location of the queuing lanes does not meet the requirements of City Code because “Queuing
                   lanes shall not be located between the building and the street unless there is no alternative for
                   location of the building and queuing lane(s) on the site”. The applicant’s response was “After
                   discussing with Engineering and City personnel, it has been determined that there are no other
                   options available, because the only way to move the queuing lanes from the current location would
                   cause a right-hand turn going into the car wash tunnel. For reasons of safety, customer and car
                   wash property damage, and liability, Quick Quack does not approve of right-hand turns going into
                   the car wash tunnel. That causes their employees to constantly cross back and forth over the
                   queuing lanes to get to the driver’s side of vehicle for check-in. Right-hand turns also greatly
                   reduce the visibility of the driver when navigating the site. Across the hundreds of car washes
                   currently owned by Quick Quack, a left turn has proven to significantly decrease the probability of
                   these risks from occurring. For these reasons, the queuing lanes and left-turn into the car wash
                   tunnel, as presented are the only option for this site.”

         3.1.10    A tree survey shows there are several trees on site ranging from 16” to 30” in diameter, that are
                   in acceptable condition, however, all trees at the location are marked as conflicting location and
                   slated to be removed or are in poor condition. The submitted landscape plans show replacement
                   of the same number of trees, but as 2” caliper.

         3.1.11    Ms. Bergeson showed some proposed elevations and a proposed landscape plan along with a tree
                   survey. Also that the applicant has submitted a sound decibel study which meets Salt Like County
                   Noise Ordinance levels and felt the area most impacted by noise levels would be the commercial
                   area to the north end. The applicant had to make some previous site plan adjustments, the first
                   being to allow for the right amount of stacking, which by Code requires space for four vehicles and
                   they also have submitted a “desert scheme” to incorporate more natural colored stones on the
                   building. She advised the applicant has agreed to shorten the tunnel by about 20’, with an escape
                   lane included. However, with the inclusion of that escape lane, none of the existing trees could be
                   retained.
         3.1.12       She then went over Staff’s proposed motions to approve or table for more information and
                   closed her presentation.

3.2      FINDINGS:
         3.2.1  This application was initiated by Russ Nelson, on behalf of Quick Quack Carwash.
         3.2.2  The subject property is one lot with an area of approximately .79 acres.
         3.2.3  The subject property is in the Community Commercial (CC) zoning district.
         3.2.4  The subject property is in the “Community commercial” designation in the City’s General Plan.
         3.2.5  This conditional use permit request is consistent with current zoning and General Plan
                designations.
         3.2.6  The applicant is requesting approval to construct a new tunnel carwash facility owned and
                operated by Quick Quack Carwash.
         3.2.7  The site layout places queuing between the building and Whitewood Lane.
         3.2.8  Staff has identified potential spillover into Whitewood Lane as a potential negative impact.
         3.2.9  The Planning Commission has the authority to decide that there is no alternative location.
         3.2.10 The proposed use includes tunnel carwash air driers and vacuum facilities, which may increase
                noise levels.
         3.2.11 Sound study demonstrates acceptable noise levels per SLCO sound ordinance for residential
                properties.
         3.2.12 To mitigate potential noise impacts on adjacent commercial properties, staff recommends that
                landscaping be designed in a way that can help mitigate noise levels.
         3.2.13 Tree survey and landscape plans have been submitted that show nearly all existing trees will be
                removed.
         3.2.14 Staff recommend requiring that the applicant preserves certain mature trees and incorporate
                additional trees in landscape plans.
         3.2.15 Exhibits have been reviewed by City departments and review agencies and have been determined
                to be in general compliance with City Standards. Any outstanding review comments will be
                addressed prior to final approval.
         3.2.16 Staff and review agencies have identified conditions of approval to be considered by the Planning
                Commission prior to preliminary approval. (They are listed below).
         3.2.17 The Planning Commission is the decision-making authority for preliminary non-administrative
                conditional use permit review. Final review authority is granted to the Director, unless otherwise
                directed by the Planning Commission.

3.3      CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:
         3.3.1  The Director is authorized to conduct the final non-administrative conditional use and site plan
                review.

           a Reception Center at 2596 West 4700 South, Taylorsville, Utah. (Terryne Bergeson - Planner 1).

4.1      Ms. Bergeson presented this item.        The applicant, Marcela Ayala, is leasing the space in Westwood
         Shopping Center. She has applied for a business license to use the space to hold Zumba classes during
         daytime hours, and to operate a reception center in the evening hours. The property is in the Community
         Commercial (CC) Zone. Per the Land Use Table (1308.020), a reception center requires a non-administrative

         conditional use permit granted by the Planning Commission. The parcel is zoned Community Commercial
         and has a General Plan designation of Community Commercial, which is compliant for the requested use.
         The applicant proposes the hours to rent the space for receptions will be Tuesday through Sunday from
         2:00 p.m. to 12:00 a.m., depending on customer preference, holding events that are generally no longer
         than five hours long. The applicant has her certification for license to be an alcohol server and would be
         the person serving any alcohol brought in by the persons renting the facility for an event. She also provided
         a current license for a security person who would be working the events as stipulated. It is noted that the
         location is not within 300 feet of any residential zone and the requested hours are not in violation of any
         City ordinances. Ms. Bergeson advised she counted about 50 parking spaces which would be available for
         this use by the 80 attendees the applicant proposes to have for any given event.       She showed images
         of what the floor plan will look like. A public notice was mailed out to surrounding neighbors and
         businesses, with no comments received. Ms. Bergeson gave an overview of the center where this rental
         unit is a part of. It was noted that there are two other reception centers on this site. The applicant has
         indicated the maximum occupancy for these rented out events would be 80. Ms. Bergeson reviewed the
         Findings and Conditions of Approval aloud for those in attendance.

4.2      FINDINGS:
         4.2.1  This application was initiated by Marcela Ayala.
         4.2.2  Per 13.08.020, a Reception Center in the CC Zone requires a Non-Administrative conditional Use
                Permit granted by the Planning Commission.
         4.2.3  The space will be available for customers to rent and host private events.
         4.2.4  The applicant will allow customers renting the space to bring their own alcohol. Per DABS,
                permits are not required for this type of use. The event must be closed to the public. Tickets for
                entry cannot be sold and alcohol cannot be sold.
         4.2.5  Applicant submitted documentation of alcohol-server course completion.
         4.2.6  Applicant states she will remain onsite to serve alcohol.
         4.2.7  Applicant provided a license for a security person hired to remain onsite during events.
         4.2.8  Proposed hours of operation are Tuesday through Sunday until 12:00 a.m.
         4.2.9  Conditional Use Permits are governed by Utah State Code and must meet the provisions outlined
                in 10-9a-507.
         4.2.10 Staff has conducted an analysis of the site and received public input.
         4.2.11 Staff has identified a list of proposed conditions to mitigate effects on the surrounding sites.

4.3      CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL:
         4.3.1  The applicant complies with the requirements of all applicable reviewing agencies.
         4.3.2  The conditional Use Permit is subject to review and revocation upon substantiated complaint or
                citation.
         4.3.3  Any changes to the structure will require a building permit.
         4.3.4  The applicant shall remain compliant with all business licensing requirements.
         4.3.5  The business must conform to Salt Lake County’s Noise Ordinance.
         4.3.6  The center may only be used for private events.
         4.3.7  Event attendance shall be limited to eight (80) people.
         4.3.8  Alcohol use must be in compliance with DABS requirements and regulations.
         4.3.9  Alcohol may only be served at private events by a person with active certification to serve
                alcohol.
         4.3.10 Applicant must provide a licensed security guard(s) to remain onsite for the duration of an event
                during which alcohol is served.

4.4      STAFF RECOMMENDATION:           That the application be approved for the Reception Center based on
         Conditions of Approval.
