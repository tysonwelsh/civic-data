                                      RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      RESOLUTION NO. 21-44


A RESOLUTION TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT WITH ROSE CREEK LOFTS
LLC GRANTING AN EASEMENT FOR THE INSTALLATION AND MAINTENANCE
   OF A CULINARY WATER UNDER CITY OWNED PUBLIC RIGHT OF WAY.


       WHEREAS, Rose Creek Lofts LLC (hereinafter “Developer”) is developing residential
properties in Riverton City (hereinafter “City”) and desires to distribute culinary water to said
residential properties through the use of a single master water metering system; and

       WHEREAS, the development undertaken by Developer consists of three parcels of land
separated by public rights of way owned by the City; and,

        WHEREAS, it is the intent of Developer to distribution of culinary water to each of the
above reference parcels through a private culinary water system installed, owned and maintained
by Developer consisting of, at a minimum, main water lines, valves, connection devices, thrust
blocks, distribution service lines and other appurtenances

       WHEREAS, Developer desires to install the above noted private system within, under
and across the City owned public right of ways; and,

       WHEREAS, City is willing to provide culinary water to Developer’s private culinary
water distribution system through a single master water meter; and,

       WHEREAS, City is willing to grant an easement to Developer for the installation,
operation and maintenance of Developer’s private water distribution system within, under and
across City owned the public rights of way referenced above subject to the terms and conditions
of an Agreement entered into by City and Developer; and

       WHEREAS, City and Developer are desirous of entering into the attached agreement,
which each party acknowledges is supported by good and valuable consideration;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Riverton City Council as follows:

   1. That Riverton City shall enter into the above referenced Agreement with Developer to
      grant, convey and warrant, for the benefit of Developer, a perpetual non-exclusive
      easement over, across and under the public rights of way as described in the Agreement
      for the construction, installation, operation, maintenance, connecting and accessing of
      Developer’s private water distribution system as presently designed and shown on the
      approved plans attached to and set forth in the Agreement.
