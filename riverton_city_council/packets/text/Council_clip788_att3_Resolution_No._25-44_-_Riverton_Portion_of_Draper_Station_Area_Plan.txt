                                       RESOLUTION NO. 25-44

 A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL REGARDING THE STATION AREA
  PLAN REQUIREMENTS FOR THE DRAPER STATION AREA WITHIN RIVERTON CITY

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Section 10-9a-403.1, requires the Riverton City Council (the "City
Council'') to review and approve a Station Area Plan ("SAP'') as an element of the Riverton City (the
"City") General Plan for any area of the City that is located within one-half mile of an existing or future
fixed-guideway public transit station; and

         WHEREAS, the SAP requirement of Utah Code Section 10-9a-403.1 is considered satisfied if
the City Council adopts a resolution describing any existing conditions that make satisfying a portion or
all of the SAP objectives impracticable, prior actions the City took that substantially promote the SAP
objectives if those actions remain relevant and meaningful for achieving the SAP objectives, or a
combination of impracticable conditions and the City's prior actions; and

         WHEREAS, the City has identified the portion of the station area in Riverton City for the Draper
Station Area as satisfying the SAP requirements because of impracticable conditions and the City's prior
actions in the station area, as fully described in the attached Exhibit “A”.

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:
        1. The City Council hereby approves this Resolution describing in Exhibit “A”, for the portion
           of the Draper Station Area in the City, the conditions that exist to make satisfying a portion or
           all the SAP requirements impracticable, and prior actions the City took that substantially
           promote certain SAP objectives and remain relevant and meaningful for achieving the SAP
           objectives, and forwards it to the Wasatch Front Regional Council for its review and
           certification.

        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 16th day of
September, 2025 by the following vote:

    STATION AREA PLAN IMPRACTICABILITY AND PRIOR ACTION REVIEW
          “DRAPER STATION” PROPERTY AREA IN RIVERTON CITY

        The purpose of this review is to demonstrate that the Station Area Plan ("SAP'')
requirements area satisfied based primarily on impracticability, but also prior actions for the
area of Riverton City (the “City”) that is located within one-half mile of the Utah Transit
Authority Frontrunner Station located at 12997 South Frontrunner Blvd ("Station" and "Station
Area"), which is located in the City of Draper and shown on the attached “WFRC/MAG
Station Area Planning Map” below.

Station Area Plan Requirement Summary

         SAPs are comprehensive documents drafted to promote the following objectives
within a one-half mile radius of public transit stations (rail or bus rapid transit): "(i) increasing
the availability and affordability of housing, including moderate income housing; (ii)
promoting sustainable environmental conditions; (iii) enhancing access to opportunities; and
(iv) increasing transportation choices and connections." With the passage of Utah House Bill
462 (Housing Affordability Amendments) in 2022, cities that have land within a ½ mile radius
of a fixed-guideway public transit station are now required to develop and update SAPs in
accordance with their general plan and zoning.

         The SAP requirements are satisfied if a city determines that conditions exist that make
satisfying a portion or all of the SAP requirements impracticable. The conditions include
“existing development, entitlements, land ownership, land uses that make opportunities for
new development and long-term redevelopment infeasible, environmental imitations, market
readiness, development impediment conditions, or other similar conditions” (Utah State Code§
10-9a-403.1(2)(b)(ii)(A)).

The portion of the station area within the stream channel is not directly accessible from any
existing or planned right-of-way. The Jordan River Trail in that area is located on the east side
of the river, opposite of this area. There are no roadways planned or projected for this location.

Impracticability Analysis

The SAP requirements for the station area are satisfied through existing conditions that make
satisfying conditions impracticable. These conditions and supporting information are listed
below:
     • Increasing the availability and affordability of housing, including moderate income
         housing (10-9a-403.1(7)(a)(i));
             o The portion of the station area within Riverton City is not buildable as it
                 encompasses area within the Jordan River channel and Riverbend Golf Course.
                 There is no direct or indirect viable access from the east (Draper) side of the
                 Jordan River, and no such access is planned. It is unlikely any future
                 opportunities for housing will develop that include or incorporate this area.
     • Promoting sustainable environmental conditions (10-9a-403.1(7)(a)(ii));
             o Development within or impacting the Jordan River corridor as would be
                 required for utilization of this area is both impractical and detrimental to
                 environmental conditions on and around the area.
     • Enhancing access to opportunities (10-9a-403.1(7)(a)(iii)):
             o This area and surrounding properties are not impeding access to opportunities
                 in the area, and development of the area would likely impact access to the
                 Jordan River corridor, wetland and open space, and the Riverbend Golf Course
                 and associated open space.
     • Increasing transportation choices and connections (10-9a-403.1(7)(a)(iv))
             o This area does not allow for development of new transportation choices nor the
                 enhancement of existing or planned choices. As stated above, development or
                 disturbance of the area would potentially impact access to or on the Jordan
                 River and Jordan River trail.

Prior Action Analysis

While not directly associated with the area itself, Riverton City has developed and taken action
in compliance with the Riverton City Moderate Income Housing Plan, and incorporated that
plan into its General Plan. In an effort to further the availability and attainability of housing in
the City, prior actions include the following:

    •   Reduction of barriers within zoning to allow for opportunities for development of
        affordable housing, including reduced lot sizes, simplified architectural requirements,
        and minimization of review/approval times.

    •   Allowing as a permitted use Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU’s) , both internal and
        detached, in all single family zones in the City. This has greatly expanded the
        availability of affordable units within the City, and has provided opportunities to
        enhance affordability for existing homes, as well.
    •   Adoption of water efficiency standards requiring and encouraging conservation of
        water resources and more responsible uses. The City is also finalizing installation City-
        wide of pressurized irrigation meters.
    •   Riverton City has made significant investment in our park systems, including the Main
        City Park west of the station area. Enhanced trails and increased way-finding signage
        and mapping helps to provide access to recreational and active transportation
        opportunities both in the general area and throughout the City.
    •   Riverton City is actively working with Salt Lake County to enhance and provide
        expanded access to the Riverbend Golf Course.

Summary

Riverton City remains committed to its goals and to mandates by the State of Utah to expand
available housing opportunities and access to transit and other transportation services.
However, the station area portion within Riverton City provides no benefit or basis for
furtherance of those goals or mandates due to existing conditions of the property.
