City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On September 6, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1560, approving the
adjustment of the common boundary between Draper City and Lehi City.         The
complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 7th day of September 2022. Laura Oscarson,
Draper City Recorder.
