RESOLUTION NO. 20-06-03                                                         Date: June 23 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       ADOPTING THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL SERVICES
       DISTRICT EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN (EOP) AS MAGNA’S
       EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN

       WHEREAS, an Emergency Operations Plan (“EOP”) can reduce the vulnerability of
citizens and community of the Magna Metro Township (“Magna” or “Municipality”), a member-
entity of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (“GSLMSD” or “MSD”), to loss of life,
injury, damage and destruction of property arising from natural, technological, or human-caused
emergencies and disasters or during hostile military or paramilitary actions; and

     WHEREAS, the MSD has developed and adopted an EOP to address the need of the
member-entities of the District whereby the member-entities can adopt the MSD’s EOP as their
own entity’s EOP, together with annexes, if any; and

      WHEREAS, an EOP will assist Magna prepare for prompt and efficient response, and
recovery from, emergencies and disasters, including using all systems, plans and resources
necessary to preserve the health, safety and welfare of persons affected by an emergency; and

      WHEREAS, an EOP will further help Magna with recovering from emergencies and
disasters by providing for the rapid and orderly restoration and rehabilitation of persons and
property affected by emergencies; and

      WHEREAS, an EOP provides an emergency management system encompassing all
aspects of pre-emergency preparedness and post emergency response, recovery and
mitigation; and

       WHEREAS, Magna desires to adopt the MSD’s EOP as the Magna EOP in the interest of
public health, safety and general welfare.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AS
FOLLOWS:

       Section 1. Provision of emergency management services. The Greater Salt Lake
Municipal Services District, through an Interlocal contract with the Unified Fire Authority, provides
emergency management services to the member-entities of the MSD, which includes Magna.

       Section 2. Adoption of the National Incident Management System
(NIMS). The Magna Metro Township hereby adopts the National Incident Management System
(NIMS) as a framework to integrate and coordinate the emergency response and recovery
actions of all levels of government.

       Section 3.    Adoption of an Emergency Operations Plan. The Magna Metro Township
Council hereby adopts the Emergency Operations Plan previously adopted by the MSD as the

      Planned Community (the “Planned Community”) located at approximately 4000 South

Resolution No. 20-06-08

              The Council reviewed the following Resolution adopting an amendment to the
2020 consolidated fee schedule to add cemetery services fees:

RESOLUTION NO. 20-06-08                                                  DATE: June 23, 2020

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
       APPROVING AND ADOPTING AN AMENDMENT TO THE 2020
       CONSOLIDATED FEE SCHEDULE TO ADD CEMETERY SERVICES FEES

                                         RECITALS

 A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code
    Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-2a-401 et seq.

 B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal governing body for
    Magna pursuant to UCA § 10-3b-501.

 C. On November 26, 2019, pursuant to state law, the Council approved and adopted the
    2020 Consolidated Fee Schedule for Magna.

 D. On May 13, 2020 ownership and operational control of the Pleasant Green Cemetery (the
    “Cemetery”) was transferred to the municipality.

 E. The Cemetery does have certain costs for services that require the payment of fees to pay
    for those services, the municipality is required to adopt a fee schedule so fees can be

                                              10
DATE           TUESDAY                                 JUNE                            23, 2020

    imposed and collected.

 F. On June 9, 2020, the Council tentatively approved the Cemetery Fee Schedule
    (Exhibit A) and set the public hearing to be held on June 23, 2020 at 7:00 PM to
    accept input from the public regarding the Cemetery Fee Schedule.

 G. On June 12, 2020, a notice of the public hearing and a copy of the proposed Cemetery
    Fees were posted on the municipality’s website (www.magnametrotownship.org), the Utah
    Public Meeting Notice website (www.utah.gov/pmn), and posted at the Webster Center
    (8952 W Magna Main St Magna, UT, 84044) which fulfills the noticing requirements under
    State Law.

 H. The Cemetery Fees are necessary for the conduct of the business of the Cemetery.

                                        RESOLUTION

    THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Magna Metro Township, the Mayor and Council
