                         DRAPER CITY RESOLUTION NO. #20-43

       A RESOLUTION APPROVING AN INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT
 BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE TRANSFER OF
FUNDS TO BE USED FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF A GATE AT THE ENTRANCE
       OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY FLIGHT PARK PARKING LOT.

       WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Title 11,
Chapter 13, Utah Code Annotated 1953, as amended (the “Interlocal Act”), public agencies are
authorized to enter into mutually advantageous agreements for joint and cooperative actions;

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns certain real property located at
approximately 15300 S. Steep Mountain Drive in Draper City, more specifically identified as
Parcel 33-13-200-003 (the “Property”);

      WHEREAS, the County has constructed a parking lot on the Property for the County
owned Flight Park (the “Parking Lot”);

        WHEREAS, the County and Draper City have determined it to be in their best mutual
interests for the Parking Lot to have an automatic gate that opens and closes at set times (the
“Gate”);

        WHEREAS, the County will plan, design, construct, install, and maintain the Gate;

        WHEREAS, Draper City will pay all costs associated with the construction of the Gate,
up to a not exceed amount of $60,000.00;

         WHEREAS, the City Attorney has reviewed the Interlocal Agreement and has approved
it as to form and compliance with the Interlocal Act;

        WHEREAS, the City Council has determined the Interlocal Agreement to be in the best
interest of Draper City and will be beneficial to the health, safety and welfare of citizens of
Draper City; and

       WHEREAS, the Mayor is authorized to execute agreements.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Draper City Council as follows:

       Section 1.    Resolution. The Interlocal Agreement for the construction of the Gate on
the Property between Salt Lake County and Draper City, attached hereto as Exhibit A, is
approved and the Mayor is authorized to sign the same.

       Section 2.     Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
passage.
