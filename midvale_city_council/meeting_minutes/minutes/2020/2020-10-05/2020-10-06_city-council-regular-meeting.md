MEDIUM TO HIGH DENSITY ZONE (RM-25)
Jason Binks said the applicant, Dale Bennett of Bennett Properties, LLC, requests a
rezone of a single 0.21-acre parcel at 847 W Center Street from Regional Commercial
(RC) to Multifamily Residential - Medium to High Density (RM-25). The applicant
proposes a conceptual plan of a duplex that faces west on Le Page Street. A duplex is
an allowed use in the proposed RM-25 zone and would be reviewed by planning staff
through a building permit submittal if the rezone is approved.
The property is in a Regional Commercial (RC) zone and is adjacent to four different
zones. It abuts an RC zoned single-family dwelling to the east and an RM-25 single-family
dwelling to the south. To the north, across Center Street, is a multifamily development in
the Silver Refinery Overlay of the Bingham Junction zone. To the west is the Jordan Bluffs
zone with several multifamily residential buildings. The subject property is on a short block
with two other parcels, a single-family home and small business. The properties are all
addressed on W Center Street, but access is along a frontage road named 7725 South,
which runs immediately parallel to W Center Street. The subject site is on the corner of
7725 South and Le Page Street. Adjacent neighborhood development includes a variety
of single family, duplexes, multifamily, restaurants, offices, and retail and commercial.
GENERAL PLAN CONSIDERATIONS:

identified in the 2016 Midvale City General Plan. It is within the Main Street Opportunity
Area and the Jordan Bluffs Opportunity Area. The General Plan identifies the following

neighborhood, particularly in the area of Main Street, Center Street, and Holden Street
5. Provide and implement new bike routes to complete the transportation system

residential. The large multifamily developments along Bingham Junction Boulevard, north

and south of Center Street, have overwhelmingly changed the landscape of the area to a

higher density character. The proposed rezone to Multifamily Residential Medium to High

Density (RM-25) would be consistent—and be compatible—with the emerging character

of the area.
