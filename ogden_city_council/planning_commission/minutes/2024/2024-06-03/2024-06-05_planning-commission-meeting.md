Public Hearing, Zone Change from R-1-6 to C-3, 340 Washington Blvd (rear)

Jennifer Patrick of the Planning Division gave a staff report regarding a request for a zone
change from R-1-6 to C-3, 340 at Washington Blvd (rear). The report stated the front of the property is
zoned C-3 and the rear is zoned R-1-6. The planning commission will determine if the zone change is
consistent with the general plan, including zone changes of zone change policies of E-1, and whether
the change is consistent with the Hillcrest/Bonneville community plan.

Commissioner Safsten-if this were to go to commercial, the street that does go to Ogden Ave,
what commercial applications would be allowed then where it meets up with Ogden Ave?

Ms. Patrick-| don’t think signage would be allowed right there. It would have to be off
Washington Blvd.

Commissioner Safsten-What would prevent it? Does the property owner of the fire station own
the access to Ogden Ave.?

Ms. Patrick-they only own the rights to use that as access to the parking lot.

Commissioner Safsten-They couldn't put a sign there, because they don’t own the property.
They have rights for access, but they don’t have rights for signage.

Ms. Patrick-They don't own it.

Commissioner Southwick- | understand the reason for the rezoning. You've got a straight edge
of residential. They make an argument that straight edge is a more consistent boundary between the
two zones.

Discussion continued regarding the issue of parking.

Ms. Patrick continued—If the rear portion remains R-1-6, then adjacent properties, such as the
Wildlife Center, must set back from the residential property.

Commissioner Aaberg-The only access to the parking is through residential, which is a smaller
road and doesn't fit these guidelines. We have to make it commercial to make it fit in that area.

Chair Sandau-Will open it up now for any public comment.

Further discussion between Commissioners and staff regarding the firehouse parking, codes
and year built after the public hearing closed.

Request for Conditional Use and Site Plan Review, Multi Family, 211 Patterson St

Applicant, Tim Price did not speak, but Harold Woodruff, who is the architect on the project,
addressed the commission regarding the conditional use and site plan review for multifamily. This
building is a second building on a little campus. The first building is under construction at 318 St and
Lincoln Ave.

Commissioner Safsten-It came up in our tour. How come one building is so much different than
the other?

Mr. Woodruff-There are 2 different zones. The zone that is actually on 31° allowed for 56 units.
To get 56 units required parking. It became a 4 story building.

Commissioner Aaberg-What are the size of the units in the building on Patterson?

Mr. Woodruff-They're about 500 sq ft, give or take, depending on where they're located. They're
one bedroom; they're intended for one person.

Commissioner Shinoda-Are there four units per floor?

Mr. Woodruff-There are actually two 2 floors. There are four units on the first floor and eight on
the 2" floor.

Chair Sandau-Will there be somebody who lives there? Why don’t | let Mr. Price address
management and how it is leased.

Mr. Price-To answer this question, we won't have any on site live in management, but we will
have on site management throughout the day and hopefully through the evening hours. It'll be a secure
building, so tenants will need little pods to get in. We'll have security cameras surrounding to monitor
activity.

Commissioner Aaberg-The amenities are in both buildings or just the larger one?

Mr. Price-The larger ones have similar amenities but this one, the ones | described are in the
small one.

Chair Sandau-What is the demand in Ogden for this kind for foster kids?’

Mr. Price-There were at least 25 unsheltered youth we've been working with youth futures on
this, our partner, they feel like the demands higher than the 12 units, DCFS as well.
