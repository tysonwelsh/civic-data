        3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
           Mayor Wood shared updates related to the state of emergency declared by
           Governor Cox in relation to the state’s extreme drought conditions and highlighted
           the City’s Ordinance on watering lawns that will begin to be enforced by the City’s
           Code Enforcement. She also highlighted the City’s ‘Acts of Kindness’ stories that
           residents can submit in honor of the America 250 celebration. Lastly, she
           congratulated the graduating class of Cottonwood High School.
