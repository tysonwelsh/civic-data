MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP ORDINANCE
ORDINANCE NO. 2023-O0-05 Date: March 28, 2023
AN ORDINANCE, AMENDING TITLE 19, ENTITLED "ZONING" OF THE MAGNA
MUNICIPAL CODE OF ORDINANCES, 2017, BY RECLASSIFYING CERTAIN
PROPERTY LOCATED IN MAGNA FROM THE C-3 (COMMERCIAL) ZONE TO THE
DOWNTOWN HISTORIC DISTRICT (DH) MIXED USE ZONE
The Magna Metro Township, Salt Lake County, State of Utah, ordains as follows:

Section 1: Section, 19.06.020, The Zoning Map of the Magna Metro Township, Magna
Municipal Code of Ordinances 2017, is hereby amended as follows:

The properties described in Attachment #1 are hereby reclassified from the C-3 (Commercial) zone to
the Downtown Historic (DH) Mixed-Use Zone.

Section 2: The map showing such change shall be filed with the Magna Metro Township
Planning Commission in accordance with Section 19.06.020 of the Magna Municipal Code of
Ordnances, 2017.

Section 3: This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at least
one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in the Magna Metro
Township, and if not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately upon its
first publication.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Magna Council has approved, passed and adopted this
ordinance, this 28" day of March, 2023.
