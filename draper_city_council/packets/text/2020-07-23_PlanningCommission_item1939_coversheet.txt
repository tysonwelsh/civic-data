Re: City Initiated Commercial Buffer Zone – Zoning Text Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a text amendment that would apply citywide.  The application has been initiated by Draper City.  The proposed zone is called the Commercial Buffer Zone (CBU) and would have a CBU designation on the zoning maps.  The proposed CBU zone (see Exhibit B) will be located in Chapter 9-11 (Commercial Uses) in the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC). The findings are as follows: For a positive recommendation:
1.   That Section 9-5-060(E) provides for and outlines the process for amending the text of the DCMC.
2.   That this text amendment does not compromise any of the six factors listed in Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
3.   That the proposed text change is consistent with the goals, objectives and policies of the City’s General Plan.
For a negative recommendation:
1.   That this text amendment compromises one or more of the six factors listed in Section 9-5-060(E)(2).
