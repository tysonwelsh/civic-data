40          Garbage Collection RFP Discussion
41
42   Craig Calvert, Contract Administrator and Purchasing Manager, explained the RFP for municipal
43   waste will go out the first week of August. He recommends the City include a bid option to have
44   the hauler service and maintain the cans as part of the contract and compare that cost to the
45   cost of the City maintaining the cans.
46
47   Mr. Calvert asked the Council whether they would like to continue to have recycling every other
48   week, weekly recycling or eliminate recycling. Councilmember McDougal said that he does not
49   recommend weekly recycling. He said that most of the recycling is going to the landfill. He
50   reported that the state passed a law which requires every waste hauler to divulge what
     Page | 2 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     July 19, 2022




 1   percentage is going to recycling on a quarterly basis. He would like the bid to include recycling
 2   every other week.
 3
 4   Councilmember Buroker said the current glass recycling program does not cost the City any
 5   money. Mayor Staggs asked which companies can provide recycling. Mr. Calvert responded
 6   that Waste Management and Rocky Mountain Recycling are the two providers for recycling.
 7   Councilmembers agreed the bid should include a recycling option and then they can make a
 8   decision once they compare.
 9
10   Mr. Calvert asked if the Council would like to include a green waste program. He said if the
11   Council would like a green waste program then the City would need to purchase cans, $45 for
12   each resident for a total of $500,000. Councilmember McDougal said the green waste program
13   is going away at the Transjordan landfill, all green waste will go directly to the landfill.
14   Councilmembers agreed that they do not want an option for a green waste program.
15
16   Mr. Calvert asked if the Council would like to have multiple containers for glass recycling.
17   Councilmember Buroker said she would like one additional glass recycling container. Mayor
18   Staggs asked if there would be an additional cost for another location and Mr. Calvert did not
19   know. Councilmembers agreed to include an option for 1 or 2 glass recycling containers and
20   then decide based on the price.
21
22   Mr. Calvert explained the previous bid included a Christmas tree pickup option, electronic waste
23   recycling and document destruction. Council agreed that none of these options would be
24   included in the current bid process.

48            Green Well Exterior Design and Time-Line
49
50   Mr. Calvert explained that in 2020 the City did a RFP for a water treatment facility at the Green
51   Well. This contract was awarded to Total Water Management. Total Water Management
     Page | 3 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     July 19, 2022




 1   completed Phase 1, permit to discharge into the Jordan River. Total Water Management would
 2   now like to proceed with Phase 2, construction of a containerized treatment facility. If the
 3   Council does not want a containerized facility, there is money in the budget for construction of a
 4   building.
 5
 6   Will Richardson, Total Water Management, congratulated the City on securing the discharge
 7   permit. He presented two different proposal ideas for the treatment system: a containerized
 8   treatment system that would sit next to the building or an expansion of the building on site.
 9
10   Mayor Staggs explained the RFP was $2.8 million which included the treatment plant with the
11   containerized treatment system. When he presented to the Salt Lake County Council, he
12   presented a building expansion option. He said the City has invested $2.1 million in this project
13   so far and would contribute another $500,000 so that the system would blend in with the
14   existing housing system. He asked which option the Council would prefer: building expansion
15   or containerized treatment system.
16
17   Councilmember Stewart asked if having a fixed building reports better than having a portable
18   container. Kevin Hicks, Administrative Services Director, said that a building would be better for
19   assets.
20
21   Total Water Management said they will be very involved in the implementation of the system
22   and will work with City contracted engineers to complete the project.
23
24   Councilmember McCay asked what the price difference will be between the building and the
25   containerized system. Mr. Hicks said the price difference is not known until the City receives
26   the engineering drawings and paperwork but estimates it to be $100,000 to $300,000 difference.
27
28   Councilmember Buroker said she would definitely like to expand the building because it looks
29   much nicer and would like the landscaping to be more natural. She would also encourage staff
30   to explore an education opportunity for residents on water treatment.
31
32   Councilmember McDougal asked if the building expansion allows for future upgrades. Total
33   Water Management recommends having a conversation with engineers to determine if there
34   should be space saved for future use.
35
36   Mayor Staggs said the discharge permit allows the City to pull 2,000 gallons per minute which is
37   near capacity and the building will maximize the output of the well today. Total Water
38   Management said if technology were developed in the future where there would not be
39   discharge, he recommended a bigger building that could house this new technology.
40
41   Stacie Olson, Water Manager, said they have been focusing on training for this well. They have
42   also hired a water quality operator conservation specialist who will be managing this well.
43
44   Council agreed the City should expand the building instead of using a containerized system.
45   Council would also like to build the building to meet future needs. Councilmember McCay
46   agreed with Councilmember Buroker that she would like to provide an educational opportunity.
47
48   Casey Saxton, Communications Director, said he would like to have outdoor signage near the
49   Green Well that describes the importance of culinary and secondary water and describes how
50   the water is cleaned. Councilmember Stewart suggested the City provide a QR code that links
51   to a video explaining the process of how Riverton City receives its water.

 1   Councilmember Buroker said she would like to use money to enhance the building, the
 2   landscaping and to provide education. However, the most important item to her is landscaping

40            Ordinance No. 22-19 – Hughes and Hubbard Rezone, Rezone of 2.61 Acres
41            Located at 12984 South 4490 West From RR-22 to R-3 (Single-family Residential
42            with 14,000 sf Minimum Lot Size)
43
44   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained this was a public hearing for an
45   application to rezone 2.61-acres of land located at 12984 South 4490 West from RR-22 to R-3.
46   The property is addressed off an existing private lane but has frontage on the east side of 4570
47   West Street (4570 West is also known as Eagles Flight Road). The rezone includes all, or
48   portions of, parcels 27-31-200-038,-039, & -040.
49
50   Mr. Lethbridge said the property is currently zoned RR-22, which is a single family residential
51   zone with animal rights. Most of the properties between Sunday Drive and 4570 West north of
     Page | 7 Minutes – City Council Meeting
     July 19, 2022




 1   the SLR property north to Dutchman Drive are similarly zoned. In the last 3-4 years there have
 2   been three (3) rezones in the area consistent with the General Plan. Two of the rezones
 3   changed properties to R-3, and one facing Dutchman Lance changed zoning to R-2 SD.
 4
 5   Devin Hubbard, Applicant, commented that he has worked with the City to make sure everything
 6   is in place for this development.
 7
 8   Councilmember Stewart asked about connectivity on Sunday Drive. Mr. Lethbridge said there is
 9   nothing specific to this rezone.
10
11   Councilmember McCay asked about access. Mr. Lethbridge said there is no connection into the
12   SLR project because that project is private. The primary connection point would be from 4570
13   West. Councilmember McCay also asked if the applicant plans to remove the existing house
14   and the applicant said yes.
15
16   Councilmember McDougal asked about the property to the south and whether it is incorporated
17   into this rezone. Mr. Lethbridge said it is not tied to this rezone.
18
19   Mayor Staggs called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public Hearing.
20
21   Councilmember McCay MOVED that the Riverton City Council adopt Ordinance No. 22-19 –
22   Hughes and Hubbard Rezone, Rezone of 2.61 Acres Located at 12984 South 4490 West
23   From RR-22 to R-3 (Single-family Residential with 14,000 sf Minimum Lot Size).
24   Councilmember Stewart SECONDED the motion. Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the
25   motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes,
26   McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, and Stewart-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
