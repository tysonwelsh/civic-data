PUBLIC HEARING

1. PUBLIC HEARING ON THE PETITION TO VACATE COUNTY ROAD 3000 WEST.
-Richard Nielson, Public Works

(Timestamp: 10:47)

Dale Eyre (Utah County Deputy Attorney) explained the reason for the petition. He said
the county does not own the road but by state law the county is allowed to make a
claim on the road as a right of way if it’s been used by the public. Recently a local
landowner has filed suit to assert their private ownership of the land. The road
does not have a useful purpose as a public road right now. He explained that this
public hearing, as required by law, is to determine if anyone disagrees with the
petition to vacate the road.

Motion to open the public hearing: Commissioner Gordon
Seconded by: Commissioner Powers Gardner

Vote: All in favor - Aye

Result: Motion passed 3/0

Shane Pierce (Property Owner on 3000 West and lives in Pleasant Grove) said they purchased
the property 10 years ago with the understanding that there was access witha
county road. By vacating the road their property will now be landlocked and will
cause serious harm to their investment and value of their property.

compliance with the regulation.

want to have an outside firm do an assessment.
