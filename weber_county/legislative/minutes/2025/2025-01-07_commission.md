D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Chair Harvey: Yesterday we turned over a new chapter. Leanne Kilts, after serving
   for more than 44 years in the surveyor and recorder's office, decided to finally hang it up. What a phenomenal
   person. She is always a giver. Strangely enough she's not really retiring; she's going to go back to work for a
   local engineering firm a couple of days a week. Then we have another County treasure leaving us, Mr. John
   Bond. What a fine man; he exemplifies integrity and doing the right things for the right reasons. You'll
   remember when we were thrown a curve by Covid and the way the federal government went the way they did
   with the CARES Act and other funds sent our way. John Bond and a great team he led worked so that we could
   appropriately put those monies where it would be best served for our County residents. Then we got a surprise
   thrown at us as another fantastic elected official, Mr. John Ulibarri, decided that he would accept a job with the
   Utah Association of Counties helping them with the assessments over all 29 counties, not just this one but the
   other 28 counties as well. And so it just goes to show that our guys are doing a wonderful job. Lynelle Jensen
   will be the new Treasurer, Bahy Rahimzadegan will be the new Recorder/Surveyor, and we'll have a new
   assessor named shortly. A lot of the folks from the Assessor's Office are here today. You guys are awesome, we
   sure appreciate you. You do a wonderful job for us here. We trust you. We believe in you. We sustain you.
   We support you and we appreciate what you do.

       APPOINTED.
         Lauren Thomas (County Attorney): DeeDee Kimber has been proposed to be temporary manager, per code
         requirements. The Commission has to notify the political party of the pending vacancy. Within 30 days, the
         party will propose a replacement. In the meantime, a temporary manager is appointed. When the party’s
         proposed name is received, the Commission ratifies the replacement. The Assessor’s office sends a letter to
         the Clerk with the name of the temporary manager.
