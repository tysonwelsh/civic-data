MEMO
To: City Council
From: John Vuyk
Date: 2025-06-17
Re: Action Item: Resolution #25-25 or Resolution #25-32


Comments:


Approve Resolution #25-25 adopting the City of Draper Annual Budget for the Fiscal Year
Ending June 30, 2026 as proposed and including any changes to final budget items that
have been presented and discussed with the council. Adoption of the budget includes
approval of the staffing document and the employee pay plan for fiscal year 2025-26.

Draper City has received the certified tax rate for FY2026 (Tax Year 2025) from Salt Lake
County and Utah County. The equalized rate is 0.000936 for general operations with no
rate for debt service. The final rate is 0.000936. This will generate $10,186,796 for general
operations.



Or



Approve Resolution #25-32 adopting the City of Draper Acting Budget for the time period
July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026, or until such time as the FY2025-2026 Budget is
adopted following a Truth in Taxation hearing.

Draper City has received the certified tax rate for FY2026 (Tax Year 2025) with an
equalized tax rate of 0.000936, but we move the City set the certified tax rate equal to the
combined tax rate for FY2025 (Tax Year 2026) at 0.001022. This will generate an additional
$935,965 of tax revenue bringing total anticipated Tax Revenue to $11,122,761.

The Council recognizes the change will require a Truth in Taxation hearing and propose
to set the date for the hearing for August 13, 2025.
