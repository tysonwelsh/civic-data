H. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVE RESOLUTION 42-2024 INDICATING THE REQUIREMENT OF THE COUNTY’S
      ACCEPTANCE OF CERTAIN INFRASTRUCTURE ON AN IFD OR PID.
            Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): The passing of Utah State Bill HB13 initiated
            Infrastructure Finance Districts (IFDs). IFDs exclude County governments from developing a governing
            document. Once the IFD is certified, it will include the Resolution. The county is by no means obligated to
            assume the associated infrastructure.

            Lauren Thomas (Civil Attorney): The proposed Resolution reads as follows:

                      RESOLUTION INDICATING THE REQUIREMENT OF THE COUNTY’S ACCEPTANCE OF
                                           CERTAIN INFRASTRUCTURE ON AN IFD OR PID.
                        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §17B-2a-13 allows for the creation of Infrastructure Financing
                        Districts (“IFD”); and
                        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §17D-4 allows for the creation of Public Infrastructure Districts
                        (“PID”); and
                        WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners anticipates the creation of both IFDs and PIDs;
                        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §17B-2a-1305(4)
                        regarding IFDs and Utah Code Ann. §17D-4-204(3) regarding PIDs, any infrastructure proposed to
                  be transferred to the County through an IFD or PID which will require services to be provided by the
                  County must be built in accordance with all applicable standards, design, inspection, and other
                  requirements of the County, and will only be accepted through a formally issued will serve letter or a
                  properly executed interlocal agreement signed by both parties.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve Resolution 42-2024 indicating the requirement of the county’s
         acceptance of certain infrastructure on an IFD or PID; Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye
I. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:
   1. Stall of Fame: Lauren Shafer (Elections Director)
      Ricky Hatch (Clerk/Auditor): Every month the Commission recognizes an employee that has made significant
      contributions to the County. I feel really lucky that any month I think one of the Clerk/Auditor staff could
      very easily be given the Stall of Fame recognition. I am pleased to recognize and recommend Miss Lauren
      Shafer for her hard work as our Elections Director. She has been with us for two and a half years now. She
      came to us when elections were under intense scrutiny and that scrutiny has only increased. She has stepped
      forward through Covid issues while running elections when half the team had Covid. She has led the team
      through redistricting and a tremendous amount of legislative changes. She hit the ground running and built a
      fantastic team in our elections office to help prepare for this November’s election. She has shown tremendous
      leadership, resolve, and a strong backbone to shoulder the many allegations and even threats from the public.
         Commissioner Bolos: Chair Harvey, your thought of the day had to do with scrutinized and ridiculed. If there
         was ever an office that knows what that is like, it’s the Election office. But Lauren is always happy, always
         smiling. For someone who has had her share of that treatment, that is very commendable.
         Commissioner Froerer: When Lauren started here, she had big shoes to fill during difficult times. You have
         to hand it to her how she handles the comments that come her way. I continue to be impressed with not only
         her but the whole Elections team. I truly believe we have the best elections team in the entire state.
