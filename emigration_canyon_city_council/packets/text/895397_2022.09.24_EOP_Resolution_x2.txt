                                RESOLUTION #2022 – 09 – 01
           RESOLUTION ADOPTING EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN AND
           EMERGENCY INTERIM SUCCESSORS FOR TOWNSHIP OFFICERS.

        WHEREAS, Emigration Canyon Metro Township (“Township”) recognizes it is at risk to
a wide range of natural, technological, and man-made hazards and such events require ongoing
emergency operations planning by all jurisdictions of government within the Township, county
and state;
       WHEREAS, the Township Council (“Council”) finds that it is in the public interest to
have an emergency operations plan;
       WHEREAS, Township officers, employees, or designees will execute the emergency
operations plan;
       WHEREAS, Utah Code § 53-2a-807 requires the Township to adopt an emergency
operations plan, designate an emergency manager and three emergency interim successors and
the specific order of succession for each officer, and designate an emergency alert plan and
system;
    NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
TOWNSHIP COUNCIL, UTAH, THAT:
       SECTION I: The emergency operations plan attached as Exhibit 1 is hereby adopted as
the emergency operations plan for Emigration Canyon Metro Township, including its
designation of an alert plan, and alert system.
      SECTION II: The Emergency Operations Manager and order of succession for
Emigration Metro Township is as follows:
          1. Emergency Operations Manager – Mayor Joe Smolka;
          2. Mayor Pro Tempore – Jennifer Hawkes;
          3. Chief Law Enforcement Officer – Sheriff Rosie Rivera
       SECTION III: Effective Date. This resolution shall take effect upon publication.
       PASSED AND ADOPTED by Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council, Utah, this
27th day of September in the year 2022.
                                                   EMIGRATION CANYON METRO
                                                   TOWNSHIP



                                                   Joe Smoka, Mayor
