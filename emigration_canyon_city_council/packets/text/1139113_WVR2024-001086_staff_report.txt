PROJECT SUMMARY

The applicant, Chaunceton Bird, is applying for a slope waiver to allow development on slopes over 30% at 4365
E Laneview Court. This slope waiver is part of a permitted use application for a single family residence.

SITE & ZONE DESCRIPTION

The property is located towards the end of Laneview Court. The property fronts onto both Laneview Court and
Maryfield Drive, but is only accessible from Laneview Court due to the slope on the Maryfield Drive side. The lot
was created as part of the Emigration Place Phase 1 subdivision and is a lot of record.
        Request: Waiver                                                               File #: WVR2024-001086




ISSUES OF CONCERN/PROPOSED MITIGATION

The Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District and reviewing agencies have reviewed the application and have
noted that the slope in some portions is greater than 30%.


PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS

Review Criteria
Chapter 19.72.060 states what criteria must be meet in order for the Planning Commission to waive or modify
any slope protection standards. The criteria are that strict compliance with the slope protection standards, 1)
renders the site undevelopable, 2) results in substantial economic hardship not created by the applicant or
otherwise self-imposed; or 3) results in a building location that requires excessive grading, vegetation removal,
or driveway distances in conflict with the purposes of this chapter and the development substantially conforms
to all other development, site design, and environmental standards of this Chapter and all in all other applicable
ordiances and codes.

There are not any practical alternatives on this site. The vast majority of the lot is in the 30-40% range with the
only a portion towards the front of the property being under 30%. Most of the portion to the front is 20-30%
slope and much of that is being used for the residence and driveway. This lot would be exceedingly difficult to
develop without the slope waiver being granted as it would limit development to the front of the lot.

The applicant does not have much choice to avoid building on slope on this lot, but has placed the building in a
way that would not significantly interrupt the view as the building steps down with the slope. There are no
streams within 100 feet of this property and no streams cross the property. Most of the lot is lightly vegetated
with heavy vegetation being in the rear of the lot on a steep slope. There are not any significant rocky outcrops
in the area that would be disturbed.

Approving the slope waiver would be detrimental to the public health, safety, or welfare to people, properties,
or improvements in the vicinity. There are several other developments in the area that are on lots that have
similarly steep slopes. A quick look at the Planning Information Finder Map showed that many residences in the
area are directly adjacent or run through sections of slope that are over 30%.

As evidence of a lot of record, the applicant provided a mechanic’s lien on the property from 1995 as well as the
HOA CC & R’s from 1993 both before the FCOZ ordinance was established. Salt Lake County records indicate that
the Emigration Place PUD was first approved in the 1980s, and subsequently amended with minor changes in
1992 and 2000. over the years. Those plat amendments did not affect the status of this lot.

Based on the above analysis, MSD staff has found that the proposal is consistent with the surrounding land uses
as well as the general plan. Full compliance with required ordinances and policies will be verified through the
subsequent technical review process before the issuance of the Land Use permit, and license inspection process.

PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Based on the findings stated above, the MSD Planning Staff recommends that the Emigration Canyon Planning
Commission approve the slope waiver request subject to the following conditions:

        1. The applicant be required to follow all requirements of Chapter 19.
        2. The applicant be required to receive technical approval for the associated FCOZ application.
        3. The applicant be required to comply with the requirements from all reviewing agencies.
