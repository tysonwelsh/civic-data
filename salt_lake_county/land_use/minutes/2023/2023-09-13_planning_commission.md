PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
Hearing began at – 8:38 a.m. 
 
REZ2023-000944 - Adam Von Maack is requesting a recommendation for approval for a rezone from A -
2 (Agriculture) to A -1 (Agriculture). Acres: 1.47.  Location: 3198 North 2200 West. Planner: Justin 
Smith (Motion/Voting) 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Justin Smith provided an analysis of the staff report. 
 
Commissioner Cohen confirmed th is lot  is in unincorporated. Mr. Smith  confirmed this property is in 
unincorporated county, but to the east is Salt Lake City. Lots to the northwest are Salt Lake County and 
zoned A-2. Mr. Shaw said there is a pending annexation for Salt Lake City that includes this property, but 
the applicant is entitled to apply to rezone his property.  
 
Speaker # 1: Applicant 
Name: Adam Von Maack 
Address: Not provided 
Comments: Mr. Van Maack said the family he represents has owned this and the surrounding property for 
many years. He wants to subdivide to allow him to keep the barn and sell the home in the rear t o a family 
member. He is aware of the pending annexation. There have been proposals to annex into North Salt Lake 
and Davis County in the past. He is sensitive to annexation issues but doesn’t feel the application has an 
impact.  
 
Commissioner Collard motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Cohen seconded that motion. 
 
No one from the public was present to speak. 
 
Commissioner Collard motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Vance seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 
Commissioners had a brief discussion regarding the zone and options for dividing.  
 
Motion: To recommend for approval application # REZ2023-000944 Adam Von Maack requesting a 
recommendation for approval for a rezone from A -2 (Agriculture) to A -1 (Agriculture) to the Salt Lake 
County Council with staff recommendations. 
Motion by: Commissioner Collard 
2nd by: Commissioner Vance 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present) 
 
Commissioner Watkins motioned to adjourn. 
MEETING ADJOURNED
