102   Station Area Plan, including areas half a mile around the future Trax station at

106
107   Christine Richman is with GSBS Consulting and helped to design the station plan. The Point of
108   the Mountain line is a planned light rail line that would connect Lehi to the Draper Frontrunner
109   station. The goal of the project is to connect regional transit systems and encourage economic
110   growth. Much of the land in the plan is already spoken for, but there are plans to build 200
111   dwelling units and commercial development. The vision for the project is to build a community
112   hub that is accessible by people all over the region as well as being a gateway into Lehi City. The
113   main design principles included a focus on gathering spaces, creating a distinct visual
114   appearance, connection to other municipal areas, environmental sustainability, and accessibility.

503   4.1) Discussion of the ESA Overlay Zone.
504
505   Katie Bussell presented the Environmentally Sensitive Area (ESA) overlay zone. Her
506   presentation was rescheduled from an earlier work session.
507
508   The zone would target three key areas of protections: hillsides; wetlands, floodplains, and
509   streams; and the Utah Lake shoreline. She presented a tentative plan for what would be rezoned
510   into the overlay.
511
512   Commissioner Everett asked if property owners could opt-out at any time. He also asked if the
513   zone remained when the property changed hands. Bussell said the planning division would look
514   into it further.
515
516   The goal of the overlay zone is to protect environmentally sensitive features, make the land
517   healthier, create recreation opportunities, and maintain wildlife habitat. Additionally, this would
518   simplify the code. There are currently three different sections that protect environmentally
519   important areas, and the ESA overlay zone would combine them all under one label. There is
520   currently some regulatory framework, but the planning division would like it more solidly
521   codified.
522
523   The planning department would like for the zone to provide safe development, preserved natural
524   features, and a concept plan and site map from developers. The site map will identify and locate
525   property boundaries, streams or any other hydrologic features, known habitat of endangered or
526   threatened species, among other information.
      Lehi City Planning Commission                    11                                   March 13, 2025
527

529   those who willingly take on the overlay zone. Bussell would love to have wildlife protection

531
