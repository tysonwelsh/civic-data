Utah Department of Transportation – 6200 South Maintenance of Traffic Presentation

                 Brian Atkinson, Consultant, Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT), stated
work will start this year on the 6200 South Bangerter Interchange. There is a 78-inch water pipe
that will need to be relocated 200 feet to the west. The proposal is to close 6200 South during
fall break in October to move the pipe. Also, there are several smaller pipes that need to be
moved, which will involve the closure of lanes on Bangerter Highway as well as exit ramps. This
work is scheduled for the first two weeks in December. UDOT is proposing to use 4000 West as
a detour route and is requesting permission from Kearns Metro Township Council to do this.

              Mayor Bush stated she wanted it on the record that she received favorable
comments from homeowners whose homes were bought by UDOT. The homeowners felt that
UDOT treated them fairly. She stated the Council would support the use of 4000 West as a detour
route.

                 Council Member Peterson asked if customers would experience water
interruptions.

                Mr. Atkinson stated there will be no water interruptions, everything will be backed
up. That is one of the reasons this is being done in winter when the water demand is lower. The
project will take approximately two years to complete.
