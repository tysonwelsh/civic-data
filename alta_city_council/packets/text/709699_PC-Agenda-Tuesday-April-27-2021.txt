                            ALTA PLANNING COMMISSION
                              TUESDAY, APRIL 27, 2021
                                      3:00PM
                                 VIRTUAL MEETING

 This meeting will be an electronic meeting without an anchor location pursuant to Alta
        Planning Commission Chair Jon Nepstad’s April 20, 2021 determination:
 https://townofalta.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/2021-4-20-Determination-Letter-
                                   Location-APC.pdf

     Attendees can watch the meeting on YouTube via a link that will be posted on the
                      Town’s website at the start of the meeting here:
     https://townofalta.com/town-government/planning-commission/agendas-minutes/

1) Introduction, determination letter reading, and welcome from the Chair.
2) Approval of minutes from the October 13, 2020, Planning Commission meeting.
3) Presentation of the Alta Community Center Feasibility Study results by Jarrett
     Pelletier of Ennead Architects.
4)   Overview and discussion of initial assessment of town lighting and future possible
     direction of dark sky initiatives. Daniel Mendoza, Director, University of Utah Dark
     Skies Minor Program.
5)   Discussion on a proposal to amend Chapter 13 of the town’s land use regulations to
     add a definition of a ski area directional sign to section 10-13-4 and to exempt ski
     area directional signs from the permitting process per section 10-13-6.
6)   Discussion on addressing Climate Change and how it might affect future planning
     and operations.
7)   New Business.
8)   Meeting Schedule, date of next meeting.
9)   Motion to adjourn.

   Alta Town Council members are invited to the meeting; as such there may be a quorum of the Town Council.
