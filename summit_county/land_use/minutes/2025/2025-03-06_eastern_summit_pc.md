Public hearing and possible recommendation to the Summit County Council regarding a proposal to remove the Final Site Plan requirements included in Section 11-4-6 of the Eastern Summit County Development Code. Legislative Review. File #25-016. Jennifer Leslie, County Planner.
(6:03 P.M.)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Final Site Plan Code Amendment Staff Report
Jennifer Leslie,
Planner
, presented a proposal from staff to remove the final site plan requirements in the Eastern Summit County Development Code Section 11-4-6. The proposal was the result of Conditional Use Permit applicants questioning the need for the final site plan.
Commissioners asked questions of Planner Leslie regarding the process for finding final site plans.
Planner Leslie responded to the Commissioners concerns about being able to find final site plans with this change and described additional ways these documents could be accessed. Discussion followed.
Jennifer Strader,
Principal Planner
, addressed the Commission and provided additional information for discussion.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:27 p.m.
No public comment was given.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:27 p.m.
Commissioner Darcey asked a brief clarifying question about potential legislation amending the CUP process to which Planner Leslie responded.
Commission Chair Peterson asked a follow-up question regarding integrating the final site plan requirements into the CUP requirements.
Rich Sonntag made a motion to ask staff to merge the standards of the final site plan with the recording aspect of the final site plan into what was proposed. Clinton Benson seconded, and all voted in favor, (5-0). Absent: Bill Wilde, Don Sargent.
