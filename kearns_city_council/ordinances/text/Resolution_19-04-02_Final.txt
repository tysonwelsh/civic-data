| KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH
| RESOLUTION NO. 19-04-02, 04/08/2019
A RESOLUTION OF THE METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AUTHORIZING THE
TOWNSHIP’S PORTION OF THE BEER TAX BE GIVEN TO THE UNIFIED POLICE
DEPARTMENT/KEARNS PRECINCT FOR USE WITH KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP FOR
| PURPOSES CONSISTENT WITH THE TAX
| RECITALS
| A. The Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) As a Utah municipality, may pursuant to Utah Code
Annotated (“UCA”) §§ 10-8-2, appropriate money of the municipality for any purpose that in
| the judgment of the Metro Township Council provides for the safety, health, prosperity,
moral well-being, peace, order, comfort or convenience of the inhabitants of the Township.
| B. The Kearns Metro Township receives an allocation of money from what is commonly called
| the beer tax that can be used for alcohol and or substance abuse related services within the
categories of prevention, treatment, law enforcement, prosecution and incarceration.
C. The Unified Police Department provides police services to the Township.
D. The Township anticipates receiving approximately $17,245.78 in beer tax funds in the next
fiscal year.
E. The Unified Police Department has committed that if those funds are provided to the
Department that it will use the funds in the Township for alcohol enforcement.
F, The Township is willing to dedicate its portion of the beer tax for the next fiscal year to the
Unified Police Department/Kearns precinct on the terms and conditions contained in this
resolution.
RESOLUTION
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council as
follows:
1. The Kearns Metro Township’s allocation of beer tax for the 2018-2019 fiscal year in
the amount of $17,245.78 shall be given to the Unified Police Department/ Kearns Precinct on
condition that it be used for officer overtime shifts for DUI enforcement; direct enforcement,

| saturation patrols, DUI checkpoints and the purchase of portable breath test devises and cameras.
All these services are to be provided within the Kearns Metro Township area by the Kearns
Precinct.
2. This resolution is conditioned on the Unified Police Department entering into a
| Memorandum of Understanding with the Township that includes at a minimum the Unified
| Police Departments covenant to use the funds provided only in the Kearns Metro Township for
the purposes state herein and the Unified Police Department will submit to the Township Council
a plan on how they intend to spend the money and provide documentation the Township Council
showing proof that the allocated money is spent as indicated in this resolution and the
| Memorandum of Understanding.
| 3. This resolution shall take effect upon passage.
