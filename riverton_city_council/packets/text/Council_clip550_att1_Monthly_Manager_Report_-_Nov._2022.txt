                           Riverton Police Department Monthly Report

                                             November, 2022
    An analysis of the crime statistics for the month did not indicate any dramatic fluctuations in
    either types of calls or call volume. We did see frauds at a relatively high level compared to the
    rest of the year. Utah has always been a target-rich environment for scam artists and Riverton is
    no exception. We have published articles and spoke frequently at our public events about
    different fraud schemes which may be perpetrated on our citizens in an attempt to make them
    aware of the issue. Unfortunately, it is not uncommon for victims of these crimes to lose tens of
    thousands of dollars before we are even made aware of the situation. We will continue these
    efforts to combat this problem as it is very difficult to prosecute these international criminals
    who are able to convince innocent victims to send them money or gift cards through a variety of
    fraudulent schemes.

    Throughout the month of October, we have been meeting with our citizen academy trainees
    every Thursday and we are looking forward to graduation night on November 10th. Our current
    class has approximately 30 attendees and the feedback seems to be quite positive to this point.

    Finally, we have continued our traffic enforcement efforts with a focus on school zones to keep
    our kids safe as they travel to and from school.
