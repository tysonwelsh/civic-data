Event: UTA on Demand by Via Tour for Riverton City Council
Date: Tuesday, June 1, 2021
Objectives:
    a.   Tour key elements of UTA on Demand by Via service
    b.   Hear feedback from Riverton City stakeholders about their experience
Business Case:
    •    (Necessity) Tour the service on a round-trip ride.
    •    (Customer impact) None expected.
    •    (Context) Via will send 3 vehicles for this event for 10 passengers. The riders won’t be booking trips in the app but will
         instead ride in Via’s vehicles with the vans offline. Riverton City requested this tour during the service’s busiest
         hours and heavy traffic is expected. We need to be prepared that although the 3 vehicles are prescheduled, they
         may not all arrive at the same time.
