City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1639

On March 18, 2025, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1639 amending the
text of the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code
relating to deviations for government buildings. The complete ordinance is on file at
the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at www.draperutah.gov.
Published this 19th day of March 2025.
Nicole Smedley, Draper City Recorder.
