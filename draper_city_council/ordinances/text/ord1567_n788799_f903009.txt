City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On October 18, 2022, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1567, amending the
Draper City Zoning Map otherwise known as the Patterson Property Zoning Map
Amendment. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office
and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 19th day of October 2022. Laura
Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
