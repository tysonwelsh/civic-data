3.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Patterson Construction’s request for
preliminary subdivision review of Center Pointe Towns, a 23 townhome unit development
located at 3237 North Center Pointe Drive in an R-3 (high density residential) zone.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Andrew Patterson, was present. He clarified questions on the park strips,
landscaping, fire access, and garbage collection within the development. He also answered
questions about the sports nets and ci regards to fire access. Garbage collection would be on a
private road within the development.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

There was a public comment submitted online regarding on street parking and traffic issues.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale addressed traffic issues, on street parking, and concerns with public and private
parking

Commissioner Newall was concerned about the garage size to accommodate the vehicles. Ken
Berg from Berg Civil Engineering stated garage parking is provided. He clarified the garbage cans
are meant to be stored in the garage. There is no driveway space on Vista Parkway.

         Motion: Commissioner Ellis moved to recommend approval of Patterson
         Construction’s request for preliminary subdivision review of Center Pointe Towns,
         a 23 townhome unit development located at 3237 North Center Pointe Drive in an
         R-3 (high density residential) zone; with the recommendation to move the trees in the
         park strips to provide them a chance for survival, with the findings that this development
         is a good location for high density and a regional park with needed amenities. Also
         finding that the proposed subdivision will provide smaller single lot family homes and
         be consistent with the existing housing type in the neighborhood and meets the
         requirements of the Lehi City Development Code in regards to subdivision approvals.
         Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.

3.7) Public hearing and recommendation of Patterson Construction’s request for
preliminary subdivision review of Sunset Meadows Plat B, a 3-lot residential development
located at 1980 West Snow Spring Drive.
Jacob Curtis presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Scott Dunn, was present. The geotechnical engineer is aware of the DRC
comment and is working to resolve the issue. There is a pole in one of the yards and what is done
with it will be left up to the owner as far as requirements.

Commissioner Nielsen opened the public hearing.

Jessica Knowlton stated her house will face the road that will be connected. She is concerned
that once the road is connected the traffic will increase. There is a park near her home with fields
for soccer and softball. There is a blind corner and there has been speeding issues with traffic.
There are also some dirt hills that the neighborhood loves, and she feels that connecting the road
would be destroying a unique place.

Commissioner Nielsen closed the public hearing.

Ross Dinsdale stated he is aware of the area and the dirt hills. The road has always been intended
to connect, and it will increase traffic. Ross expressed that parking near parks and open spaces is
an issue across the city where there is open space. Ross stated there is a trail crossing that should
help discourage some of the road crossing.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to recommend approval of Patterson
        Construction’s request for preliminary subdivision review of Sunset Meadows Plat
        B, a 3-lot residential development located at 1980 West Snow Spring Drive.; with the
        findings that the proposed subdivision meets the requirements of the Lehi City
        Development Code and is consistent with the existing housing type in the

        neighborhood; including all DRC comments. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
        Commissioner Bunker; yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
        Commissioner Newall, yes. The vote passed unanimously.
