         E. Mayor and Council Reports

Council Member Don Shelton reported on his participation with the Association of Municipal
Councils, the teacher appreciation award luncheon sponsored by the South Jordan Chamber of
Commerce and the groundbreaking at The Pearl at North Shore. He had his campaign kickoff party
on Saturday and yesterday had a meeting with the Western Growth Coalition. He received a parks
update from the Salt Lake Council where they discussed funding. They have asked all the mayors to
contact their legislators and tell them to channel that money towards completing all of the
interchanges on Bangerter.

Council Member Patrick Harris had nothing specific to report.

Council Member Tamara Zander had a South Valley Mosquito Abatement Board meeting. She met
with the mayor, city manager, Strategic Services Director Don Tingey, County Council Member
David Alvord and some leadership from the Jordan School District including Tracy Miller to discuss
the pool expansion. She shared a success story regarding 11400 South with the traffic issues and
residents not being able to access and leave their neighborhood; after contacting the city, we were
able to paint “do not block” in the space so that residents could safely access and leave their
neighborhood while the traffic was backed up.

Council Member Brad Marlor said there were no Architectural Review Committee Meetings since
our last City Council Meeting. He also received some emails regarding the construction on 9800
South and 2200 West.

Mayor Dawn Ramsey said she took a short vacation and did some work while she was gone,
including swearing in the new Youth Council. She sent a video for the teacher appreciation luncheon.
She met with the administrator of the Riverton Hospital and discussed partnering with the city to
discuss healthy initiatives. She is joining this meeting from the Utah Water Users Conference in St.
George because she represents the city on the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District Board. Last
week she was appointed to the Drinking Water Quality Board for the state. Yesterday she attended a
Utah League of Cities and Towns Board Meeting where they discussed a new board created to plan
for the economic opportunities and growth we are facing over the next 10 years; her name was
chosen to represent us on the new Unified Economic Opportunities Commission that was created.
This Thursday the League of Cities and Towns is offering an ARP training, she will be unable to
attend and asked those able to attend to please do so. Next Monday, May 24, they have the Corporate
Citizen Award luncheon and cemetery expansion ribbon cutting.

             Drive, Applicant Tucker Nipko and Thayne Gardner. (By Director of Planning, Steven
             Schaefermeyer)
Director Schaefermeyer reviewed attached presentation (Attachment A).
Mayor Ramsey asked if she could move the public hearing up, before they make a decision on the
development agreement, as she wants everyone to have their say up front so they can make a
comprehensive decision.
City Attorney Ryan Loose said state law requires you have a public hearing on these types of
changes, but it doesn’t dictate where it has to be on the agenda.
Mayor Ramsey opened the public hearing on Zoning Ordinance 2021-05-Z, rezone for property
located at 11381 South Temple Drive.
Thayne and Natalie Gardner (Applicants) – Mr. Gardner said he is a pediatric dentist, his practice
is currently on 10400 South and 1300 West, he is looking to buy this property and develop it out. It
would be a one story pediatric dental office on the corner and then their own private home would be
behind it to the east. They are trying to find a good home for themselves and their business, and have
it flow with the area as far as design. With the residential home, the design would match the zoning
for the neighborhood right next door. It is a unique piece of land and they are hoping to beautify that
corner.
Jorie Hill (Resident) – said she lives to the north and thinks what they’ve done is a pretty good job.
She had a question about the dumpster location, that it looks like it’s about 10 feet off the property
line but the shape has her a little bit confused and she would like some more explanation about the
red area. She also wanted to welcome them to the neighborhood.
Mayor Ramsey closed the public hearing.
Director Schaefermeyer answered Ms. Hill’s question.
The council and Director Schaefermeyer discussed the resolution and Zoning Ordinance 2021-05-Z.
Attorney Loose said that the development agreement expires in 10 years. The whole point of a
development agreement is to facilitate developing of the property, once it is developed the terms of
the agreement are filled.
Council Member Zander made a motion to approve Resolution R2021-14, as stated. Council
Member Harris seconded the motion. Roll call vote was 4-0, unanimous in favor; Council
Member McGuire was absent for the vote.
