PRESENTER:            Jamie Larsen, City Recorder


ITEM:


Resolution No. 26-21 – Amending Rules and Regulations Necessary for the Operations, Maintenance,
Appearance and Protection of the Riverton City Cemetery


"I move to adopt Resolution No. 26-21 – Amending Rules and Regulations Necessary for the Operations,
Maintenance, Appearance and Protection of the Riverton City Cemetery”
.




SUMMARY:
The cemetery rules do not outline whether a companion headstone is allowed for cremation plots. This
resolution is changing the rules to allow for a companion headstone on cremation plots.
