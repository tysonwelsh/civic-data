2.4) Public hearing and consideration of Anna Dolegiewicz’s request for conditional use
approval of the Jewel’s Towing Site Plan located at 4641 North Digital Drive in a Light
Industrial zone.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

Commissioner Bunker asked for clarification on the water. Kim Struthers stated there is no City
pressurized irrigation. The landscape was planted several years ago. DRC talked about adding
landscaping, but irrigation is not supported.

Anna Dolegiewicz is the applicant and was present. She stated she is leasing the property and did
call the Landlord. The landlord confirmed the trees are being watered. Anna took pictures of the
trees and sent them to Lehi Staff. Legacy Towing did receive a conditional use permit at the
existing address. She mentioned container plants if the water situation doesn’t suffice for the
requirements.

Commissioner Bunker asked if the operation was in the fenced area. The applicant stated it was.
They are currently on the police rotation list. Their yard meets the current code whereas others
are “Grandfathered in”. They will continue under the same business name.

Commission Chair Newall opened and closed the public hearing.

Commissioner Bunker inquired about the temporary approval for pools and asked about the
landscape requirements. Kim Struthers stated the pool location does not have water and agreed to
put gravel and landscaping. He agreed in this case that James was not sure of the water status.
There is a well that runs culinary and irrigation needs. If there was a city PI connection, they
would need to meet all the requirements. They are not adding new areas, just the existing fence.
Commissioner Eyre asked if PI made it to the property if there would need to be a trigger effect
in the motion. Kim stated they could add a trigger for the future. He stated when the PI gets there
it will probably be developed. Commissioner Jackson stated water and infrastructure needs will
be met. Commissioner Chair Newall asked about the tow yard and parking, but it wasn’t
submitted with the packet. Commissioner Eyre asked for clarifications about the tenant
landowner application. Kim Struthers stated the landlord must sign and acknowledge that as part
of the application that has been filed.

        Motion: Commissioner Jackson moved to approve Anna Dolegiewicz’s request for
        conditional use approval of the Jewel’s Towing Site Plan located at 4641 North
        Digital Drive in a Light Industrial zone; including all DRC comments except number
        three, with findings that are stated in the report. Noting there is no water reaching the

        property and it is an unreasonable burden to ask for the landscaping requirement.
        Commissioner Bunker seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Tracy Tanner’s request for review of the Berry
Property Zone Change located at 116 South 600 East changing the zoning on 1.18-acres from
Transitional Holding (TH-5) to Neighborhood Commercial (NC).
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Dan Ford, was present. The owners were also present. They are applicants with
Tracy Tanner. One of the concerns was the neighborhood Commercial zoning. The constriction
of the size was an issue, and a lot of this size can fit a large building. The applicants went
through a general plan amendment. DRC recommended an exception and asked for a variance
for the size of the building.

Commissioner Eyre inquired if the Karl Malone center was larger than what they were asking
for. Kim Struthers stated the footprint of Karl Malone is close to 20,000 Sqft.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing and acknowledged the online comment that
was submitted by Megan Butler. Megan was concerned with the building shadowing the
surrounding property and extension of the hours. She was opposed to the item and asked the
Commissioners not to approve the exception.

Liz Anderson is opposed to neighborhood commercial in this zone. She is not happy with the ask
and feels like they got a bad zone on their plan. She agreed that neighborhood commercial is not
an issue, just not a good place for this area. She asked that the Commissioners remembered this
change down the road for exceptions.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

        Motion: Commissioner Eyre moved to recommend approval of Tracy Tanner’s request
        for review of the Berry Property Zone Change located at 116 South 600 East
        changing the zoning on 1.18-acres from Transitional Holding (TH-5) to
        Neighborhood Commercial (NC); including all DRC comments and findings stated in
        the report. Commissioner Jackson seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.

2.7) Consideration of Tracy Tanner’s request for approval of the Tanner Indoor Pickleball
concept located at 116 South 600 East.
Katie Bussell presented information on the agenda item.

The applicant, Tracy Tanner, was present. She stated they are asking for the exception for an
indoor pickleball court. She expressed the high level of interest for indoor pickleball. She would
like to expand the hours of operation. The commissioner and the applicant discussed traffic,
staff, and uses of the property. Tracy stated the concept will not be suited for tournaments, just
play. The buffer is a concern. There is some interest in retail and office space. There will be
bathroom facilities and a supply room. She would like to look at this in terms of flipping the
building. She asked if there was any room to discuss the development agreement. Commissioner
Everett stated it would be in the scope of Councils decision.

Commission Chair Newall opened the public hearing.

Liz Anderson and Janet Peck were present. Their biggest concern is the garden spot that will get
too much shade from the building. Janet agreed that fences make good neighbors. Cindy, Liz’s
sister, wanted to tell the commission that it isn’t a good fit and is too big of a building, and
doesn’t feel that it should be granted. They are really concerned about parking. She stated the
Karl Malone Center has had an issue with parking. She is opposed to the item. Janet stated her
concern is also safety.

Commission Chair Newall closed the public hearing.

The applicant did not wish to address any of the comments from Liz.

Commission Chair Newall stated the weather is an issue for pickleball. She also confirmed they
can’t make development agreements. Commissioner Jackson stated this is a good plan, but he
would like to see the building moved in terms of the garden. Part of his interest is that he would
prefer to change the code altogether as he does not want to hold up the applicant. Commissioner
Eyre does not feel like the footprint and size need to be changed and would like to allow an
exception. He stated in general the code for neighborhood commercial is in place for a reason
and wants to protect the homes, but the code is written in a way with flexibility.

        Motion: Commissioner Bunker moved to send a positive recommendation of Tracy
        Tanner’s request for approval of the Tanner Indoor Pickleball concept located at
        116 South 600 East; including all DRC comments, including the exception of the
        building size, and findings stated in the report. Noting the hours of operation will not be
        included in the change. Commissioner Eyre seconded.

        Vote: Commissioner Newall, yes; Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett,
        no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed
        unanimously.
