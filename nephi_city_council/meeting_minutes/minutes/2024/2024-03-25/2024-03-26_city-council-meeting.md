                            Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                     March 26, 2024
                                        7:00 p.m.
                                       21 E 100 N
                                    Nephi, UT 84648

1.    Mr Atkinson presented some ideas of permitting short time stays on private property in
     RV vehicle, tents or other temporary living means Councilor Parady said that we do not
     need another law that addresses the problems controlled by the nuisance ordinance.
     Councilor Worwood was in favor of the conditional use permitting process idea for short
     term stays in temporary living conditions. The council agreed upon a seven-day permit
     available twice a year with the opportunity to extend for another seven days. Atkinson
     said that the staff would continue working on the ordinance and bring it back for further
     consideration and review.

2.    Abby Ivory, Jackie Thorn, and Linda Steele addressed the council about hosting a
     Farmer’s Market at the Nephi City Pioneer Park every Saturday from June 22, through
     October 26 from 9 p.m. to 1:00. The Council granted permission for them to host a
     Farmer’s Market if they complete a Special Event application and not have the market
     on the weekend of the Ute Stampede and the 911 Service Day in September.

3.    Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the Council about having a special natural gas rate for the
     UMPA power plant on the south end of the City. Mr. Atkinson expressed his
     appreciation to Councilor Travis Worwood for his help in calculating a new rate.

4. Administration and City Council Updates:
     a. Mr. Atkinson gave the following updates:
             i.   The received a grant for $350,000 for the new Police Station
            ii.   Crimson Flats design is coming along.
           iii.   DWR has accepted the City’s revised request for the land swap for the
                  golf course expansion.
           iv.    The UMPA Conference was informative and enjoyable.
     b. Councilor Cowan reported that she is attending and enjoying Citizens Academy.
     c. Councilor Travis Worwood complimented Mr. Atkinson in his presentation at the
         UMPA Conference.
     d. Mayor Seely attended the quarterly UDOT meeting with Mr. Atkinson and Public
         Works Director Kyle Marchant and was impressed with staff on how well they are
         working with UDOT.
     e. Mayor Seely asked the Council to review a letter he had sent to the Council from
         a local resident.


5.    Councilor Skip Worwood moved to convene into regular meeting and then convene into
