WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
AUGUST 5, 2024

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:30 p.m. on August 5, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.
BOARD OF CANVASSERS MEETING:

Washington County Clerk/Auditor Ryan Sullivan explained what an adjudicated ballot is, namely
ballots that have unclear marks. Mr. Sullivan relayed the process in recounting and adjudicating
all ballots for the Congressional District 2 (CD2) race. Mr. Sullivan read aloud all of the different
drop box locations throughout Washington County, and encouraged people to use these drop boxes
for future elections. He then read aloud the results from the June 25, 2024 Regular Primary Election
Recount, with nine additional ballots that were counted versus the initial election. Of the nine
additional ballots, three ballots were determined to have been postmarked before Election Day,
five were curable, and one batch had an additional ballot that was unaccounted for. He reported
that there are 69,026 registered Republican voters, and there were 36,657 ballots that were cast for
a voter turnout of 53.11%. Three hundred and thirty-seven ballots were unable to be cured,
generally due to signature issues; 659 ballots were returned past the deadline; 13 uncountable
ballots from a previous election; and 26 provisional ballots that were unable to be counted for

various reasons.
(See attached report.)
MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Accept the Certification of the June 25, 2024

Regular Primary Election Recount. Motion seconded by Iverson and carried
by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson and Snow voting

aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 4:56 p.m.
