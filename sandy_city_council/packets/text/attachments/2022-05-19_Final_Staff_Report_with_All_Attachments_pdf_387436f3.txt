DESCRIPTION OF REQUEST
The applicant, William Vincent, is requesting a Conditional Use Permit to operate a home-based
woodshop within an existing detached two car garage. (See Exhibit 1: Application Materials)

The applicant is proposing to utilize 50% of the detached garage for the home occupation. The
woodshop will be used to create homemade goods to sell online which include fish nets, cutting
boards, and boxes. The applicant is proposing to operate between 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM seven
days a week. All product is proposed to be custom made and shipped directly to customers.

The property is located in the Quail Valley #4 subdivision on a .25-acre (10,890 square feet) lot
east of Highland Drive and south of Snow Mountain Dr. The home is zoned R-1-8 with
properties on all sides being single family homes zoned R-1-8.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within 500 feet of the subject property to notify them of
the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was held on May 2, 2022. One
neighbor attended the meeting. The neighbor had concerns about the potential noise generated by
the use. The applicant let the neighbor know that he had been woodworking as a hobby for
approximately 10 months which alleviated concerns about noise levels as the neighbor hadn’t
heard any sound generated by the woodworking. In addition to the neighborhood meeting staff
received one email from a neighbor with concerns about noise.

ANALYSIS
Section 21-11-05(F)(5) of the Sandy City Development Code states that any home occupation
utilizing a workshop for repairs including welding, carpentry, sheet metal work, furniture
manufacturing, upholstery, and other similar manufacturing activities, is classified as a Category
II Home Occupation. Category II Home Occupations must be reviewed by the Planning
Commission and granted a Conditional Use Permit before pursuing a Home Occupation Business
License through the Business License Office.

The applicant is proposing to operate a garage-based woodshop home occupation which would
involve using a table saw, sander, band saw, circular saw, drill, and hand power tools.

The property is located on an interior lot with an approximately 1,500 square foot driveway, an
attached two-car garage, as well as the detached two-car garage. The proposed use will not
eliminate the minimum parking requirements.

Section 21-11-05(D)(6) Off-Street Parking. All business-related vehicles which park at the
location of the home occupation, including those of the applicant, employee, customers, clients,
or business-related visitor vehicles, must use off-street parking. This provision excludes stops
made by delivery vehicles.

The applicant is proposing to pick up and drop off all materials and have all products shipped.

Section 21-11-05(F)(6) of the Sandy City Development Code identifies regulations for any home
occupation which proposes or conducts activities within an outbuilding, accessory building,
attached or detached garage. The following guidelines shall be used to determine the maximum
impacts permitted:

   1. The applicant for the Home Occupation Business License shall designate the areas of the
      home, attached/detached garage or accessory structure that will be used for the home
      occupation. If approved, the home occupation may be conducted only in the designated
      area.

   2. No more than a maximum of 200 sq. ft., or in the alternative, no more than 50% of the
      total floor space (whichever is greater) of any accessory structure or attached or detached
      garage may be used for a home occupation unless there are specific exceptions granted by
      the Planning Commission.

   3. Any home occupation uses in an attached or detached garage may not eliminate
      minimum parking requirements for the particular zone wherein the home occupation is
      located.

   4. Any accessory structure used for a home occupation must maintain the architectural
      aesthetics or compatibility of the home and the immediate neighborhood.

   5. The home occupation may utilize one unanimated, non-illuminated flat sign to be
      attached to the accessory structure where the home occupation is being conducted in lieu

CONCERNS
Staff is concerned with the amount of proposed operating hours. The applicant has made staff
aware that he has a full-time job and will only operate the business when he has time and within
the confines of the proposed hours. While staff is not concerned that the current property owner
will operate all 84 of the proposed hours a Conditional Use Permit runs with the land which may
allow a future resident to operate a much more intensive use.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for
William Vincent for a Category II Home Occupation to operate a woodshop business as
well as to utilize up to 50% of the detached garage space on the property located at 2440
E. Vail Cir.

Findings

   1. The type of Home Occupation is allowed as a Category II Home Occupation
     provided it meets all of the standards and qualifications, or as amended by the
     Planning Commission.

   2. The property has an RV pad, a detached two car garage, and an attached two car
     garage.

Conditions

   1. That the applicant not operate outside of the proposed hours of 9:00 AM and 9:00 PM,
      unless approved by Planning Commission.

   2. That the applicant be responsible for meeting all of the provisions of the Sandy City
      Development Code, and all conditions of approval imposed by the Planning Commission.

   3. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.

   4. That this Conditional Use Permit be reviewed upon legitimate complaint.

   5. All excess material and any waste material be stored inside the garage and/or be removed
      from the property. No outside storage is allowed.

   6. That the garage door be closed during business operations.

Hello,
I am an individual who enjoys making things from scratch. This has taken many forms in my life from gardening, to
making flyrods, to making fishing nets, all as a hobbyist. Wood is my favorite medium as so many things can be made
with it. In recent months, I have had many people ask me if they could purchase a net, or something I’ve made which led
me to research what requirements there might be to start a business, and perhaps sell a few items occasionally, if free
time permits. I have a full‐time job which prevents me from spending too much time on making things, but it would be
nice to be able to share my creations with those who would like to buy them. Meanwhile positively impacting the
community by providing locally made products, sales tax revenue, and helping create a unique community identity.

Here is some additional information on my proposed business:

Nature of the business:
Uinta Woodwork is a small woodshop that focuses on making unique gifts such as fishing nets, cutting boards, boxes,
and furniture. The business will operate at various hours between 9:00 am and 9:00 pm, 7 days per week. It is located in
a detached garage and will use less than 50% of that space. It will not impact parking since there is an attached 2 car
garage and 4 driveway parking spots that will not be used by the business. A combination of hand tools and power tools
will be used. This includes a table saw, sander, band saw, circular saw, and a drill. I have made items as a hobbyist and I
have never received a noise complaint of any kind. The building is primarily cinderblock with an insulated garage door,
which seems to do a good job at keeping noise low, to the point where my fiancé cannot hear anything from inside the
house. I will always be considerate of my neighbors.
Business hours
The business will operate at various hours between 9:00 am and 9:00 pm, 7 days per week.
Traffic impacts
Traffic will not be impacted at all. No customers will visit since items will be either shipped or delivered. No additional
vehicles will be needed. Since I also have an attached garage, no vehicles will be displaced from garage parking.
Employees
This is a sole proprietorship, and will not have any employees.
