                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 23-81

  A RESOLUTION TO APPROVE A LEASE AGREEMENT WITH ZIONS BANK TO
                LEASE TWELVE (12) POLICE VEHICLES

      WHEREAS, Riverton City is required by ordinance to approve any contract that exceeds
$30,000 in a public meeting; and

        WHEREAS, to keep the police vehicle rotation on schedule the Police Department needs
to lease twelve (12) additional vehicles; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council has already approved this, through the approval of
the FY23/24 Budget, as itemized; and

      WHEREAS, Zions Bank requires that this agreement is presented to Riverton City
Council.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
       1. The Riverton City Council approves a lease agreement with Zions Bank to lease
          twelve (12) police vehicles.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

     PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 7th day of
November, 2023 by the following vote:
