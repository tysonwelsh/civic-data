I. COMMISSIONER COMMENTS:

         Every month the Commission bestows the use of the Stall of Fame (designated parking close to the building)
         to an employee who performs above and beyond in the county. The Recorder/Surveyor’s Office selected
         the Stall of Fame employee for the month of October.

         Leann Kilts, County Recorder/Surveyor, said that they selected Trisha Thompson, who is in charge of their
         Records Department and has made it possible for customers to view documents online, saving them a trip
         to the office. Ms. Kilts said that they are very happy that Ms. Thompson works in the office and adds a
         wealth of knowledge. Ms. Thompson maintains a positive attitude and has a willingness to help others.
         They appreciate her hard work and dedication. Commissioner Jenkins expressed thanks to Ms. Thompson
         for representing the county well. He said that it is exciting to see staff receive recognition and that it shows
         the county is serving the customers well. Commissioner Ebert also expressed thanks stating that Ms.
         Thompson always has a smile.
