Re: Development Agreement for Bangerter Crossing 2 Office Site Plan and Fairfield Inn & Suites Site Plan Comments: The Bangerter Crossing 2 Office Site Plan was approved by the Planning Commission on July 9, 2020. The Fairfield Inn & Suites Site Plan was approved by the Planning Commission on May 28, 2020. Under Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) site plan approvals expire in one year if a land disturbance permit is not issued. No such permit was issued for either property and both site plans are expired.
The site plan approvals for both properties contained several deviations, including deviations for building height. On May 3, 2022 the City Council modified DCMC and eliminated the building height deviation. The applicant is seeking development agreements to reinstate the previously approved site plans, including the deviations that were previously approved. The application includes two agreements, one for each project.
The Development Agreements attached to this report are still being reviewed by the property owner. Any changes made to the documents between the writing of this report and the Planning Commission meeting on July 14, 2022 will be reviewed by staff during the meeting.
