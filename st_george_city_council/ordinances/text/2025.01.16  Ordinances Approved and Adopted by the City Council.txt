                                 PUBLIC NOTICE

Please be advised that January 16, 2025, the City Council of the City of St George
approved the following ordinance(s):


Ordinance No. 2025-006 for a temporary land use regulation amending §9-13-4 of
the St. George City Code prohibiting off premise signs from being located along
State Route 7 (SR-7) also known as Southern Parkway.

Ordinance No. 2025-005 amending the existing Desert Color Planned Development
Commercial (PD-C) zone on approximately 15.12 acres, generally located at the
terminus of Desert Color Parkway just south of the roundabout at Desert Color
Parkway and Lagoon Parkway.
