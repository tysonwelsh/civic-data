                                                            Proposed Budget Amendment




March 4, 2025



Honorable Mayor and Council,



The City’s Administrative Services department submits the following proposed amendments to the
2024-2025 budget for your consideration:



1.   Adjust Budget for Jordan Valley Water Purchases -

        Reason – The City initially budgeted $1,650,000 for Jordan Valley Water Purchases in fiscal year
        2025, expecting a decrease in water usage due to the addition of a new water source that would
        reduce the need to purchase water from Jordan Valley Water. However, the new water source
        was not available as expected, and the City has experienced growth and increased water usage.
        As a result, the City exceeded its contracted minimum purchase plus 20 percent, leading to
        additional overage charges.

        Staff now estimates the City will spend approximately $3,650,000 on water purchases in fiscal
        year 2025. Given the increased water consumption, some additional revenue from water billing
        for residents and commercial users is anticipated. Therefore, staff recommends the following
        budget amendment:

        Proposed Amendment:

               51303092-450700 - Increase the budgeted expenditure by $2,000,000 to account for the
                expected addi onal water usage.
               510-340405 – Increase the budgeted revenues by $550,000 to account for addi onal
                water to be billed by the city for ﬁscal year 2025.
