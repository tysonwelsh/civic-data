           2. ABATE PERSONAL PROPERTY ACCOUNTS ATTACHED FOR THE YEARS INDICATED HAVING BEEN
           IDENTIFIED AS UNCOLLECTIBLE. THERE ARE NO ASSETS AVAILABLE FOR SEIZURE AND SELL.
           - Jeanne Bowen

           JEANNE BOWEN, TREASURER’S OFFICE - EXPLAINED THE PROCESS FOR UNCOLLECTIBLES. AS
           WE ARE TRYING TO COLLECT, IF WE FIND THE BUSINESSES ARE CLOSED, WE ARE INFORMING
           THE ASSESSOR’S OFFICE. THEY HAVE PEOPLE THAT WILL GO OUT TO THE SITES, CALL THE
           CITY, CHECK WITH THE BUSINESS ENTITY SEARCH WHICH IS A STATE SITE. AS FOR THE
           EQUIPMENT – IT IS VERY DIFFICULT TO TRACK. WHEN PEOPLE UPLOAD THEIR EQUIPMENT
           LIST, THEY ARE NOT UPLOADING SERIAL NUMBERS. IT IS NOT REQUIRED, SO THERE IS NO WAY
           TO TRACK WHO TOOK OVER THE EQUIPMENT.

           COMMISSIONER AINGE INQUIRED AS TO WHY WE SHOULD HAVE TO PROVE THAT THEY HAVE
           CERTAIN SERIAL NUMBERS.

           DAVE SHAWCROFT, DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY - IF THERE’S A BULK SALE OF ALL
           EQUIPMENT AND ALL ASSETS ARE TRANSFERRED TO NEW ENTITIES, THAT LIEN OF THE
           PERSONAL PROPERTY TAX WOULD FOLLOW THE EQUIPMENT INTO THE HANDS OF THE NEW
           COMPANY. AS FAR AS COLLECTING IT FROM THE COMPANY ITSELF, IT IS COLLECTED
           THROUGH THE LIEN ON THE PERSONAL PROPERTY, SO WE WOULD HAVE TO SEIZE AND SELL
           THE PERSONAL PROPERTY TO COLLECT THAT PERSONAL PROPERTY TAX.

           PAUL JONES DEPUTY COUNTY ATTORNEY - IT IS THE COUNTY’S OBLIGATION TO PROVE THAT
           THE PROPERTY DOES EXIST AND THAT IT EXISTS WITH THE COMPANY ON JANUARY 1 OF THAT
           TAX YEAR. SOMETIMES WE DON’T FIND OUT THAT THE PROPERTY IS GONE UNTIL AFTER
           JANUARY 1 WHICH IS WHY WE END UP WITH THIS LIST.

           COMMISSIONER LEE – IS IT IS OUR RESPONSIBILITY TO PROVE THAT THEY EXIST WHEN THEY
           LIST AND THEY’RE LISTING IT AS AN ASSET? IF THEY’RE LISTING IT AS AN ASSET, THEN
           WOULDN’T IT BE THEIR RESPONSIBILITY TO PROVE THAT IT WAS SOLD AND WHO TO OR IF
           DISCARDED?

           PAUL JONES - MOST OF THE TIME, WE ARE ESTIMATING THE TAX. THEY DON’T FILL OUT THE
           FORM, SO WE ESTIMATE IT, OR THEY HAD FILLED OUT THE FORM IN IN YEARS PAST BUT NOT
           CURRENTLY, SO WE ESTIMATE ON BACK YEAR’S FORMS.

           IF THEY GET ESTIMATED, THEY CANNOT COME IN AND DISPUTE IT BECAUSE THE RULES STATE
           THAT IT’S THEIR RESPONSIBILITY TO PROVIDE US WITH AN EQUIPMENT LIST AND THEN IF
           THEY DON’T THEY ARE GOING TO GET ESTIMATED AND THEY HAVE TO PAY THE TAX ON
           WHAT THEY GET ESTIMATED ON. THEY COULD COME IN ANY TIME BEFORE THAT AND STATE
           THEY DON’T HAVE ANY EQUIPMENT BY AMENDING THEIR EQUIPMENT SHEET AND THEN WE
           WOULDN’T TAX THEM, UNLESS THERE WAS AN AUDIT PERFORMED.

           KRIS POULSON, COUNTY ASSESSOR – SALT LAKE TAKES A MUCH MORE AGGRESSIVE
           APPROACH WITH A MUCH HIGHER COST. ALL OF OUR AUDITS ARE DONE BY THE STATE TAX
           COMMISSION. SALT LAKE IS EXEMPTED FROM HAVING THEM DO THAT. THEY DO THEIR OWN

           4. AUTHORIZE A CHANGE IN THE STAFFING PLANIN THE CLERK/AUDITOR’S OFFICE TO ADD FOUR
           FULL-TIME, GRANT FUNDED, COVID-RESPONSE SERVICE CLERK, GRADE 500 POSITIONS
           -Amelia Power Gardner, Utah County Clerk/Auditor-



           JOSH DANIELS, CLERK/AUDITOR’S OFFICE – THIS PARTICULAR ELECTION YEAR IS UNIQUE
           OBVIOUSLY FOR A NUMBER OF REASONS, BUT THE ONE THAT’S PRIMARY ON OUR MIND RIGHT
           NOW IS THE PANDEMIC. THE STATE LEGISLATURE HAS MET TWO DIFFERENT TIMES IN
           SPECIAL SESSION THIS YEAR TO PASS TWO DIFFERENT BILLS CHANGING SOME OF THE
           ELECTION LAWS FOR THIS YEAR PARTICULARLY IN ORDER TO RESPOND TO THE PANDEMIC.
           SOME OF THOSE CHANGES IN LAW HAVE CREATED UNFORESEEN WORK BURDENS ON OUR
           OFFICE AS WE GO INTO THE GENERAL ELECTION. IT HAS BECOME CLEAR THAT AS WE GO
           INTO THE FINAL WEEKS OF 2020 AND GEAR UP FOR THE NOVEMBER ELECTION WE NEED SOME
           SPECIFIC STAFF DEDICATED TO HELP RESPOND TO SOME OF THOSE DEMANDS AND SO THIS
           ITEM IS A REQUEST FOR 4 FULL-TIME, TEMPORARY EMPLOYEES, GRANT FUNDED (HR TERM –
           BUT THEY’D BE FUNDED BY CARES ACT FUNDS). WHAT THEY WOULD BE DOING IS VARIOUS
           PUBLIC SERVICE TASKS THAT ARE REQUIRED AND THAT HAVE SORT OF ARISEN BECAUSE OF
           THE PANDEMIC. THEY WOULD BE HIRED AS SOON AS WE COULD AND THEN THEY WOULD
           WORK THROUGH THE END OF THE ELECTION PERIOD WHICH IS ABOUT MID-NOVEMBER.

           COMMISSIONER LEE – WE’VE HAD A CONVERSATION BEFORE ON THIS ONE AND THE ELECTION
