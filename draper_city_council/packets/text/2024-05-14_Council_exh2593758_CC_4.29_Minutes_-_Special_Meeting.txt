4:00 PM Business Session

1.       Call to Order by Mayor Troy K. Walker

2.       Items for Council Consideration
2.a      Action Item: Resolution #24-20, a Resolution approving an interlocal
         agreement with other entities currently within the boundaries of the
         Alpine School District for the creation of a new school district.

         Mayor Mark Johnson, Lehi City, discussed the option to form a school
         district with Lehi, American Fork, Highland, Alpine, and Cedar Hills. He
         answered questions brought forward by the council members. Mayor
         Johnson reminded the council members the interlocal agreement would
         begin the process to put this option on a ballot.

         Charlie Evans, representing the Canyons School District was also in
         attendance and assured the council of the continuing relationship between
         Draper City and the Canyons School District.



         Councilmember T. Lowery moved to approve Resolution #24-20.

         Councilmember Green seconded the motion.

         A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.
