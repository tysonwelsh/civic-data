Study Session

1.0   Report: Capital Improvement Projects

1.1   Recreation Director Rhett Ogden reported on completed and current capital
      improvement projects and answered questions from the Council. The Council
      and staff discussed trails, boat ramp construction, and the possibility of
      reaching out to a non-profit group known to operate kayak rentals in other
      cities.

2.0   Update: Traffic

2.1   Police Chief John Eining updated the Council regarding traffic enforcement in
      August 2022. The Council discussed establishing clear guidelines and age
      limits for golf carts and motorized scooters on public streets and rights-of-way.

3.0   Discussion: Moderate Income Housing Plan Strategies

3.1   Todd Draper, Planner III, reported Draper City was required to select five
      Moderate Income Housing Plan Strategies to submit to the State by October
      1, 2022, but could select six strategies to qualify for preferred transportation

6.0   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1560, an Ordinance approving an adjustment to
      the common boundary between Draper City and Lehi City affecting
      approximately 1,035.49 acres of property located within parcel numbers 110-
      150-007, 110-150-008, 110-150-002, 110-300-024, 110-300-025, 110-300-018,
      110-300-066, and 110-140-004.


                                                                        Page 3 of 5
6.1   City Manager David Dobbins showed on a map the area that would become
      part of Lehi City with proposed Ordinance #1560 and showed a proposed Lehi
      City General Plan Land Use Amendment. He showed on a map 100 acres that
      would continue to be held by D.R. Horton, with Draper City having control of
      the conservation easement.

6.2   Scott Bishop with D.R. Horton answered questions from the Council regarding
      development plans. Mr. Bishop said Lehi City would prefer for Draper to hold
      the conservation easement on all of the open space, requiring both entities to
      work together for anything to change in the future.

6.3   Council members expressed support for the proposal.

6.4   Mayor Walker opened a public hearing. No one came forward, so Mayor
      Walker closed the public hearing.

6.5   Councilmember Green moved to approve Ordinance #1560. Councilmember
      Roberts seconded the motion.

6.6   A roll call vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.

8.0   Council/Manager Comments and Adjournment

8.1   Community Development Director Fred Aegerter commented that zoning
      needed to be updated for Lone Peak Canyon and Hidden Canyon Estates. A
      majority of the Council indicated support.

8.2   Councilmember F. Lowry shared a citizen request for picnic tables and
      benches at Draper Historic Park. Councilmember Vawdrey suggested tables
      may be appropriate at the east end, but part of the park should remain open
      for concerts.

8.3   Councilmember F. Lowry shared a citizen request that the Council considers
      a pickleball court ordinance.

8.4   Councilmember T. Lowery shared a citizen request for Draper City hats and
      shirts from Draper Days to be available for the holiday gift-giving season.

8.5   Councilmember T. Lowery shared a citizen request to bring back the Touch-a-
      Truck program.

8.6   Councilmember Green moved to adjourn the meeting. Councilmember
      Roberts seconded the motion.

8.7   A voice vote was taken. The motion passed unanimously.

8.8   The meeting adjourned at 7:39 p.m.
