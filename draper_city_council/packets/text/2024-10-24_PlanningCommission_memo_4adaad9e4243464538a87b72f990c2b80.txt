Public Hearing: Jason Smith Fence Conditional Use Permit Request (Administrative Action)



Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Conditional Use Permit for approximately
0.459 acres located on the south side of Crosswind Way at approximately 934 E.
Crosswind Way. The property is currently zoned RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 sq.ft.
min. lot size). The applicant is requesting that a Conditional Use Permit be approved to
allow an eight-foot (8’) tall fence to be installed

in the rear yard along the east, south and west property lines, and a seven-foot (7’) fence
to be installed in the east and west side yard areas.



Findings for approval:

     Reasonable mitigation measures are placed on the conditional use to alleviate
     anticipated detrimental effects.

Findings for denial:

     Reasonable mitigation measures are not able to be placed on the conditional use to
     sufficiently alleviate anticipated detrimental effects.
