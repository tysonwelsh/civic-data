Re: Moderate Income Housing Report 2023—Notice of Non-Compliance 
 
 
Dear County Council, 
 
Thank you for submitting your County’s Moderate Income Housing reports for this year in 
fulfillment of requirements set forth in State Code section 17-27a-408. The Housing and 
Community Development Division (HCD) has reviewed the reports and finds that the county is 
not in compliance with the requirements set forth in section 17-27a-403 and 17-27a-408 for the 
reasons noted below: 
 
Section 17-27a-403(2)(b)(iii) notes that counties shall include a recommendation to implement 
three or more of the moderate income housing strategies outlined in the Code. The strategies 
selected and included in the report do not match the requirements of the Code as updated. Please 
ensure that the strategies match the language included in the state code exactly.  
 
• In the amendments to the Snyderville Basin Implementation plan, Strategy I do not match 
the strategy language from state code. The implementation plan should be updated to 
include exact language from state code.  
• In the amendments to the Eastern County Implementation Plan, Strategies E and M do 
not match the strategy language from state code. The implementation plan should be 
updated to include exact language from state code. 
 
Section 17-27a-403(2)(c) notes that the County shall draft implementation plans that establish a 
5-year timeline for implementing selected strategies which identifies specific measures and 
benchmarks for implementing each selected strategy, and provides flexibility for the county to 
adjust as needed. Future reporting years will require that the jurisdiction complete progress 
 
 
 
reports on the identified implementation plans. Implementation plans in the 2023 reporting year 
must be of a sufficient quality to show specific details on actionable tasks, timelines for 
implementation, measures used to demonstrate progress, and what resources the county will use 
to achieve the strategies selected. The adopted implementation plans are not sufficient to provide 
this guidance and enable reporting in the future. 
• Strategy E in the Snyderville Basin Implementation Plan does not include any 
implementation benchmarks or timelines with the new edits. The implementation plan 
should be updated to include implementation metrics. 
 
A subsequent progress report does not comply unless it demonstrates that plans were made to 
implement the minimum number and type of required strategies, in a form approved by the 
division, provides sufficient information for the division to assess progress, monitor compliance 
with implementation plan, identify clear correlation between land use regulations or decisions 
and actions taken, describes market responses, and identifies barriers experienced. The submitted 
report included deficiencies:  
• Strategy J in the Snyderville Basin Report does not include any actions for the last 12-
months, stating that the County is in year one of a two year implementation process. This 
strategy also marked all the market response sections as 0 or N/A, with no further 
description. The strategy should be updated to include detail from the last 12 months that 
demonstrate progress on the benchmarks and timelines included in the implementation 
plan. 
• Strategy E in the Snyderville Basin Report does not have measurable implementation 
metrics or timelines to measure in the implementation plan, so HCD cannot assess 
progress. The implementation plan should be updated to include implementation metrics.  
• Snyderville Basin Strategies A, W, F, and I, actions and land use decisions reported in the 
annual progress report do not reflect, nor do they describe any completed actions from 
the last year from the adopted implementation plan. These strategies should be updated to 
include detail from the last 12 months that demonstrate progress on the benchmarks and 
timelines included in the implementation plan.   
• Strategies D, E, and J in the Eastern County Report include actions and land use 
decisions which do not reflect, nor do they describe completed actions from the adopted 
implementation plan in the last 12-months. These strategies should be updated to include 
detail from the last 12 months that demonstrate progress on the benchmarks and timelines 
included in the implementation plan.  
 
The county may address deficiencies in one of two ways per 17-27a-408(7)(b)(ii). They may 
submit a corrected report using the form found at 
https://jobs.utah.gov/housing/affordable/moderate/reporting/
 that cures each deficiency identified 
in the letter by November 5, 2023 or they may request an appeal of the HCD notice of 
compliance by August 17, 2023.  
 
An appeal of a notice of noncompliance must be filed in writing and include the name of the 
entity filing the appeal, signature of its representative, and the grounds for appeal. The appeal 
may be filed by email to angamble@utah.gov
, facsimile at 801-468-0211 to Alyssa Gamble, or 
physical delivery to Department of Workforce Services, 140 E 300 S, 1st Floor, C/O Housing 
and Community Development, Salt Lake City, UT 84111. If an appeal is filed by physical 
delivery, the date of filing is the date the appeal is received by HCD. Additional supporting 
 
 
 
materials and argument to the appeal board may be provided within 15 days of the date of the 
request for an appeal. 
 
Failure to appeal by August 17, 2023 or cure the described deficiencies in your county’s report 
by November 5, 2023 will make your community ineligible for Utah Department of 
Transportation (UDOT) Transportation Investment Fund of 2005, including the Transit 
Transportation Investment funding in the 2025 fiscal year. 
 
The Housing and Community Development Division is available to help you address the noted 
deficiencies. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact Alyssa Gamble at 
angamble@utah.gov
