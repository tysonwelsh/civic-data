1. Highlights of the 2026 American Planning Association & Lincoln Institute of
Land Use Policy Trend Report for Planners

Senior Planner Grant Allen and Dina Blaes, Chief of Strategic Engagement presented
highlights from the 2026 APA Lincoln Institute of Land Policy trend report. They focused
on artificial intelligence (Al) as a rapidly changing landscape for both planners and the
public. He shared a quote from Tom Sanchez, PhD, highlighting the difficulty in adjusting
to Al's rapid evolution and determining its impact.
Commissioner Wright asked what staffs concerns were regarding Al usage. Those
concerns included the following:

e The potential dilution of quality when Al is used extensively in professional work

e . The importance of quality inputs and underlying models when using Al tools

e« The need to be careful about appropriate applications of Al in planning work
Mr. Allen noted that Taylorsville planning staff had not yet used Al in their work and
explained that the city was working to determine appropriate usage policies.
Ms. Blaes noted that the city was taking a serious approach to Al policy development,
with Jay Ziolkowski currently preparing an Al policy for the city. She emphasized the need
to maintain the city's credibility as an institution and discussed approaching Al as if it were
an "intern" - a tool that required professional oversight before any work was released.

Commissioner Wilkey inquired about the validity of developer-submitted plans that might

LEGISLATIVE ACTION
3. Public Hearing and Recommendation to the City Council for a Zoning Text
Amendment to Title 13 of the Taylorsville Municipal Code, Amending State

Code Reference Numbering Pursuant to $SB1008

Mr. Allen presented a zoning text amendment to Title 13 of the Taylorsville Municipal

Code. He explained that during the 2025 first special session, the Utah Legislature

passed Senate Bill 1008, which recodified the Land Use Management Act. This

recodification reorganized and renumbered chapters, requiring the city to update all
references in its municipal code.

Mr. Allen showed examples of the changes, noting that the staff report contained

approximately 36 pages of these reference updates. The amendment was published on

the state notice website with no comments received.

MOTION: Commissioner Mufioz moved to open the public hearing. The motion was
seconded by Commissioner Wilkey and passed unanimously.

There was no one in attendance either in person or online who expressed a desire to

speak.

MOTION: Commissioner Wilkey moved to close the public hearing. The motion was
seconded by Commissioner Mufioz and passed unanimously.

Commissioner Wilkey inquired if staff had double-checked all the reference updates. Mr.

Allen confirmed that he had thoroughly reviewed the document twice, and Ms. Blaes

conducted a final review.

MOTION: Commissioner Wright moved to forward a positive recommendation to
the City Council on File No. 1Z26-DCA-000577-2026 to amend Title 13
Land Development Code of the Taylorsville Municipal Code as specified
in Exhibit A of the staff report based on the findings outlined in the staff
report. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Willardson and :
passed unanimously.
