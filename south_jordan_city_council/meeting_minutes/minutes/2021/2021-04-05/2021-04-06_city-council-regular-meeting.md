        H. Action Item: Resolution R2021-07, prohibiting the use of an ignition source,
        including fireworks, lighters, matches, sky lanterns, and smoking materials in certain areas.
        (By Fire Chief, Chris Dawson)

Mayor Ramsey noted this is a follow-up to state law and adds some clarity on what certain areas in
our city are.

Chief Chris Dawson said per state law they are required to define those areas within our city
considered to be at high risk for fire danger. This has to be done each year by May 1, this includes
determining which areas are worthy of fire restrictions. Once they do that, they submit a map of those
areas to the county and to the State Fire Marshall’s office for publication and they provide an
interactive map on the city’s website for use by the residents. They are expecting to have a very dry
year this year. Obviously with the drought that is going to be a significant problem and they will
undoubtedly have a very active fire year this year. There are already wildfires happening in Tooele
right now and the fire year gets longer all the time. Their goal with this resolution is to identify those
areas within our city’s boundaries that present the most significant fire danger to the community.
This year he worked with the city attorney’s office to clarify and simplify some of the language in
the resolution to more clearly align with state law, however that doesn’t change the areas identified
last year when they expanded some of the restricted areas. The map did not change this year. Our fire
department responded to 134 fire-related calls in July of 2020, of those they identified 19 of which

        I. Public Hearing Item: Resolution RDA 2021-05, making findings and determinations as
           to the use of project area funds collected in the Towne Center project area and
           authorizing the use of a portion of the project area funds. (By City Commerce Director,
           Brian Preece and Strategic Services Director, Don Tingey)

City Commerce Director Brian Preece said they have been working on moving this street and making
some improvements into an RDA project area for a few years. A lot of different things have
happened and they are finally ready to do that. One of the issues is the project area time limit has
expired. There is money in the project area still to do these improvements, and is one of the aspects
of the resolution, to move forward and let everyone know they are using the money (tax increment
that was collected during the time the project area was in place) and making those improvements.
The other aspect is there is a small portion of what is happening that will be outside of the project
area. He was not involved in the very beginning of this project area and there was one parcel left out
of the project area. These areas can be done like swiss cheese with parcels in and out. There is a
parcel north of the power station on Redwood Road that the city-owned and sold to a developer and
the developer is making improvements. The deal was to continue with these proposed improvements
that were contemplated before we started the process of selling it to this developer. The parcel next to
it on the north was left out, parcels that surround it are in, but it is not. State law allows project
money to be used outside of a project area as long as they benefit the project area, and the majority of
what happens here will benefit the project area and the parcel left out. It will help to create a more
developable area, eliminating the little drive area by moving it to the south so it straddles the two
property lines and lines up across from an existing street for traffic safety. This was discussed with
the Redevelopment Agency attorney, Mr. J. Craig Smith. He felt although we normally don’t do
resolutions like this to spend money in project areas since there is already a budget and a plan that
has to be followed, this was different because the project area had expired. Mr. Preece added Mr.
Craig said they should do a resolution so everyone knows what is going on and there shouldn’t be
any questions as to transparency.
