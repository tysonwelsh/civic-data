                                      RIVERTON CITY
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 20-51

       A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE THE ROCKY
           MOUNTAIN POWER COMMUNITY PERFORMANCE PLAN 2020
       WHEREAS, Rocky Mountain Power intends to establish a Community Performance
Plan with Riverton City for 2020; and

         WHEREAS, Rocky Mountain Power intends to provide services outlined in this Plan;
and

       WHEREAS, Rocky Mountain Power will be timely, responsive and professional in all
communications with Riverton City, coordinate annual joint infrastructure planning meeting,
coordinate appropriate public and private outreach and permitting, continue to support Riverton
City with sponsorship opportunities and support efforts for energy efficient initiatives; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City will work closely with Rocky Mountain Power, notify the
Customer and Community Manager if there are issues and allow Rocky Mountain Power to
resolve any outstanding issues; and

      WHEREAS, The Riverton City Council finds and determines that the Rocky Mountain
Power Community Performance Plan 2020 is in the best interests of the citizens of Riverton City.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah
as follows:

      1. The Rocky Mountain Community Performance Plan 2020, attached to this Resolution as
         Exhibit A is hereby approved. The Mayor is authorized and directed to sign a copy of the
         Plan, once the same has been approved by the Riverton City Attorney.

      2. This resolution shall take effect immediately.

        PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 21st day of
