Commissioner Lee took a moment of personal privilege at this time.

           Commissioner Lee made a motion to move the Public Comment section from the end of the
           meeting to the beginning of the meeting. The motion died for lack of a second.

Commissioner Lee explained that the reason for his motion is to make it easier for the public to participate in the Board
meetings. When Commissioner Graves questioned whether that meant comments would not be accepted on specific
issues, Commissioner Lee suggested that the Board allow public comments for each individual item.

           Commissioner Lee amended his motion to allow for Public Comments to take place on
           specific agenda items as they arise. The motion died for lack of a second.

Commissioner Ellertson responded that the purpose of the Board meetings is to conduct county business, noting that
agenda items can be readdressed by the Board following public input at the end. Commissioner Lee responded that often
the public does not have the opportunity to see the documents associated with the items being discussed, and he said it
would be a good idea to receive the public’s input prior to voting on such items. He noted the importance of balancing
public input with the concern some staffers have expressed over sitting through lengthy Public Comments sessions.

Commissioner Ellertson suggested asking at the beginning of the meeting if the public has any items they would like to
discuss, and if there are, those items can be moved to the end of the meeting. Commissioners Lee and Graves agreed
to that idea. Commissioner Ellertson mentioned no motion is necessary to make such a change.

Recognizing the discussion that just took place, Commissioner Ellertson asked if anyone in the audience would like to
comment on any specific agenda items. None came forward, and the Board proceeded to the Consent Agenda.

17.     APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE COMMISSION CHAIR TO SIGN THE 2015 BYRNE JUSTICE
        ASSISTANCE GRANT (JAG) PROGRAM AWARD INTERLOCAL AGREEMENT WITH PROVO
        CITY
Utah County Sheriff James O. Tracy explained for Commissioner Lee that law enforcement agencies must submit annual
reports to the Federal Bureau of Investigations for ‘Part One’ crimes - these are the most heinous offenses, such as
murder, rape, and assault. The FBI grants financial awards to combat these crimes. Utah County has previously qualified
for the funds, but not this year. This indicates the crime rate has decreased but unfortunately means the monies will also
decrease. Provo City does qualify for the award this year, and Utah County must sign the agreement to acknowledge
the county is not receiving the funds; they will go directly to Provo. Sheriff Tracy noted for Commissioner Ellertson that
Utah County last received the financial assistance four or five years ago, and Provo City is the only entity in Utah County
to qualify this year.

      Commissioner Lee made the motion to approve the 2015 Byrne Justice Assistance Grant

2.      PRESENTATION BY ERIC ELLIS OF THE UTAH LAKE COMMISSION ON THE CURRENT
        STATUS AND PROGRESS OF THE LAKE
Eric Ellis of the Utah Lake Commission came forward, acknowledging Commissioner Ellertson as the county
representative for the Utah Lake Commission and addressing their priorities. Mr. Ellis spoke about trail-building goals,
including a trail that will go from Utah Lake State Park through Lehi City and connect into the Saratoga Springs City
trail system. The Utah Lake Commission has met with various cities and is also working with the county and the state
to improve Sandy Beach. Mr. Ellis and the commissioners discussed access roads, a nature conservancy, land transfers,
elementary school field trips, volunteer opportunities, and a potential research center with involvement from local
universities and the Forest Service. The commissioners thanked Mr. Ellis for his presentation.


                                           PUBLIC COMMENTS
Utah County Sheriff James O. Tracy commented on the Wasatch County Fire (Wheeler/Deer Creek Reservoir Fire
September 2015), as well as a rock slide that occurred on the Provo River Trail in Provo Canyon. Neither incident have
resulted in injuries. Utah County crews are assisting with the fire, and Utah County Public Works Director Richard
Nielson confirmed the rock slide would be cleared today. Sheriff Tracy also spoke about the Major Crimes Task Force
which in the last week have made three major drugs and weapons busts, a big step in crushing the transportation system
of criminal organizations.
