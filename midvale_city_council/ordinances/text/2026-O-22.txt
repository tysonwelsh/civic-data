                                        ORDINANCE NO. 2026 -O.22
    AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING PAY SCALES AND SBTTING SALARIES FOR ELECTED AND
               STATUTORY/APPOINTED OFFICERS FOR MIDVALE CITY

           WHEREAS, Midvale City has heretofore established a Job Classification and Salary Scale Plan for
    FY2027; and

            WHEREAS, City jobs are classified with pay grades established according to job requirements,
    qualifications, and supervisory responsibilities; and

           WHEREAS, the Midvale City Council may authorize a cost-of-living increase in accordance with
    Chapter 2.12.14(B) of the Midvale City Municipal Code; and

            WHEREAS, a public hearing was duly held on June 2,2026, to receive input regarding the Job
    Classification Plan, Salary Plan, and Elected Official's Salaries for FY2027; and

            WHEREAS, it is in the best interests of Midvale City and the citizens thereof that a Job
    Classification Plan, Salary Plan, and Elected Official's Salaries for FY2027 be formally adopted.

         NOW THEREFORE BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF MIDVALE CITY,
    STATE OF UTAH:

            SBCTION 1: The City Council hereby approves adjustments to the Fiscal Year 2027 Salary Plan
    as has been determined and made according to data derived from the compensation survey conducted by
    the Midvale City Human Resources Department.

           SECTION 2: The City Council hereby adopts the Fiscal Year 2027 Job Classification and Salary
    Plan attached hereto as Exhibits.

           SECTION 3z A 3.5%o cost-of-living adjustment to the Mayor and City Council salaries be
    implemented in accordance with Chapter 2.12.14(B) of the Midvale Municipal Code.

            SECTION 4: This ordinance shall become effective upon publication with all salary adjustments
    effective as of July I ,2026.
