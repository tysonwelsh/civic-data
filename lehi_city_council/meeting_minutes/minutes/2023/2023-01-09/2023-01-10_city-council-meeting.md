3.2 Discussion of the structure of the Planning Commission
The Planning Department drafted an Ordinance that considers the restructure of the Planning
Commission from 9 members to 7 member, but still maintain the alternate positions. Mr.
Struthers stated that this is how many other communities structure their commission and
suggested the this could help with efficiency. Mayor Johnson suggested that the Commission be
required to take an Oath of Office. There was discussion about the suggestion to allow the Mayor
to dismiss members of the Commission without cause. There were concerns with granting that
authority to only the Mayor. It was suggested that removal would be subject to the advise and
consent of the Council.

    Items 10 and 11 were heard together.
Lehi City Council Meeting                       7                                   January 10, 2023
    Mayor Johnson asked for public comments on items 10 and 11.

    Rob Ludlow stated that this area is complicated with connectivity issues. He said that the city
    has already granted multiple connectivity exceptions which has compounded the issue. He
    suggested that the developer present a better plan that provides traffic circulation and reduces
    density.

    Rich Kindred stated that he has those same concerns and believes this does not align with the
    city’s plan. He expressed concern with traffic.

    Shannon Parker expressed concerns with traffic and safety if this were approved.

    A neighboring resident asked that the Council deny this and require the developer to provide
    better connectivity.

    Dan Reeves with Perry Homes was representing the applicant and answered the Council’s
    questions.

    The Council discussed requiring an updated traffic study due to changes with the original area
    plan and road connections or reconfigurations.

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to table this item and direct staff to conduct a traffic
                study and review the Area Plan to confirm the number of connectivity index
                outlined in the Area Plan to determine what kind of variance there is from that, so
                they can determine the impact on the surrounding areas. Councilor Southwick
                seconded the motion.

    There was further discussion about an updated study.

    Councilor Albrecht stated that the bar is set high to deny something for health safety and
    welfare and believes that this doesn’t meet that standard, however, she would like to
    understand what they are dealing with by having an updated traffic study.

    Lorin Powell, City Engineer, suggested that the study be conducted by an outside consultant,
    preferably the by the same company that did the previous study.

    Amended Motion: Councilor Hancock amended his motion to state that the developer conduct
    the traffic study with Lehi staff review. Councilor Southwick seconded the amendment to the
    motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Absent; Councilor Hancock,
    Yes; Councilor Koivisto, Absent; and Councilor Southwick, Yes. The motion passed with 3 in
    favor and 2 absent.
