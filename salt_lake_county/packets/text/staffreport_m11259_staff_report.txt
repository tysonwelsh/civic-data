Requested Agenda Title:
Consideration of an Approval of an Annexation that Creates an Island - Salt Lake City Northpoint Annexation
Requested Agenda Item Description: Salt Lake City has communicated with Salt Lake County that they are
in the formal process of completing a city-sponsored annexation in the Northpoint Area. This annexation is
anticipated to be completed during March 2025. The proposed annexation will leave an island of
unincorporated County, and therefore, requires County Council approval. The island area is under negotiations
for Salt Lake City future annexation and is likely to be annexed before July 2027. If the island does not get
annexed before July 2027, it will automatically be annexed into Salt Lake City due to 2024 legislation (H.B.
330). The County is able to provide street maintenance services until the island is annexed.
