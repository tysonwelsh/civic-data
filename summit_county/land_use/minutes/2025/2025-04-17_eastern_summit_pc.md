Laura Kuhrmeyer,
Planner
, presented three applications relating to the Hope River Ranch Guest Lodge. The applications were a Master Planned Development (MPD), a Conditional Use Permit (CUP), and a Final Site Plan (FSP). Planner Kuhrmeyer reviewed discussion points from the previous work session and answers provided by the applicants.
Julie and Mike Dee,
Applicants,
provided background information on the proposed project.
Commissioner Hayes asked a clarifying question about open space on the property. Mr. Dee responded to her concerns.
Commissioner Wheaton raised questions about traffic, vehicles, and parking to which Ms. Dee responded.
Commissioner Bowen asked for clarification regarding the shuttling of clients and concerns with vehicles crossing the railroad tracks.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:23 p.m.
Thomas Moore was called to speak.
Cody Crosby was called to speak.
Dave Sivulich was called to speak.
La Reen Judd was called to speak.
Lizzy Denkers was called to speak.
Melissa Wayment was called to speak.
Brad Dee was called to speak.
Mike Grow was called to speak.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:44 p.m.
Commissioner Wheaton asked for additional information regarding the condition to limit cars on site. Thomas Hunt, Principal Engineer and Planner for the applicant, and Mr. Dee responded to Commissioner Wheaton. Commission Chair Peterson added additional comments to the discussion.
Robert McConnell,
Attorney,
addressed the Commission and provided additional information to the discussion regarding traffic and parking.
Commissioners provided final comments and asked additional questions to which the applicants responded.
Marion Wheaton made a motion to approve the Hope Ranch Guest Lodge based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval in the staff report with the additional condition of limiting daily cars on site to the total number of parking unless there is a special event permit. Bill Wilde seconded, and all voted in favor, (7-0).
