   3. Discussion regarding an upcoming proposed ordinance to add a new industrial zone
      (ID4) to the zoning code for specific uses related to a data center (large-scale
      technology server storage facilities).
         a. Staff Presentation

              Mrs. Shauna Talbot presented Dave Kallis, Jon Barica, Saj from Set Square,
              would be attending the meeting via Zoom. In June it was presented that there was
              an area that was discussed that the annexation map be adjusted, and the
              highlighted area move to a level 3. The group that is interested in the area for a
              data center.

              The leg work needs to be done with new ordinances, and by accomplishing this
              now it will be of help before a project of this size comes to fruition. One of the
              items that was given to the commission was the Urban Land Institute pamphlet
              “Local Guidelines for Data Center Development” this is a liaison between zoners,
              cities, developers, and investors. The information will assist the commission in
              seeing what will be needed for the zoning, and what it takes to bring a project like
              this forward.

              Ordinance Draft PC 08-13-25 was presented to the commission, with detail on
              each section. Discussion from the commission is noted in Section b of these
              minutes. (please see section 3b of the minutes PC 08-13-25, of this document).

              Addressed in the Ordinance draft also includes changes in a fencing ordinance
              will need to be adjusted for this project, they will need taller fences. They will

              also have guard towers. Entry will be limited. Fiber based broadband, loading
              areas, maintenance facilities, AI is new so the miscellaneous was put in. Office
              use admin area, recreation facilities, it is year around.

              Electrical is also a section of the ordinance, these projects will require the use of
              substation, two 138kV lines. Additionally, redundancy will be needed when it
              comes to electrical and natural gas. When completed Nephi City will be the
              largest user in the UMPA.

              Land use of the proposed ordinance also limits the lot size minimum 40 acres. For
              now Nephi City will focus on the larger of the data centers. At some time smaller
              use could be addressed.

              Site Plan explanation of the ordinance, Nephi City would like this done in parcel
              sections. That way if in the future these area projects need to be sold or divided
              they can come back to the site plan and see how things were approved, and then
              they can do an exchange quickly.

              Setbacks of the ordinance were discussed, if the building is taller than the further
              it will need to be setback further. An example of the main building, data center,
              less than 80’ high, can be setback. The taller the building the further it needs to be
              setback. Eagle Mountain has a data center, and it keeps with what they have. To
              meet the requirements of the fire department they will additionally require a
              clearance of 26’, because of the fire trucks that would be needed in an emergency.

              Height Restrictions in the ordinance are currently written as there shall be no
              building over 100’ high, unless reviewed. There is a pin in this, we don’t want to
              keep coming back and forth for approval. Site coverage will still need to be
              adjusted, just how much of the building will cover the acreage.

              Design section of the ordinance will include a landscaping buffer would be on the
              frontage, but not all the way around. Architectural Design we don’t have one on
              any other parts in the city, aesthetically it would need to fit in to the area. Title 11
              will be included in this area, and Nephi City expects that of all developments.

              Parking and Loading Code will comply with the current city code. This may be
              adjusted to semi-trucks and trailers, this will be discussed with the owners and see
              site specific needs. Parking Lots will be addressed in the code for these areas.

              Lighting in the section of the ordinance, there was more only one thing that was a
              complaint for these data centers and that was the lighting. This is one of the
              ordinances Nephi City will be addressing, not necessarily a full Night Sky
              Ordinance, but perhaps more directed lighting and not spillage. Additionally, staff
              will be looking into the National Dark Sky Ordinance for ideas, specs, and to
              assist as this section addressed.

              Environmental Standards section will be addressed by Section 4.2, and they will

          b. Commission Discussion
             Commissioner Heather Robertson asked about the area and what area would be
             annexed to clarify if it was the whole area? Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded that for
             now per this discussion the whole area, further down the road it would be a
             specific location.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson, inquired of the colors of the map on the area
              that is being discussed. Mrs. Shauna Talbot clarified of the map area.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson, inquired why if we knew that was what was
              coming why the analysis wouldn’t be done now to save time and money. Mrs.
              Shauna Talbot clarified that there are steps that have to be done. Chairman
              Thomson also clarified that there has to be interest in a project before it comes.
              Mrs. Shauna Talbot also discussed that they need redundant electrical.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson inquired on the number of data centers that are
              in Utah currently. Mrs. Shauna Talbot, she did not know.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson asked about Millard having the new data
              center, and if contact was made with Millard. Mrs. Shauna Talbot said they had
              contacted them, and they are excited about it. They are good neighbors, and it is a
              money generator for a community in the form of tax money. It is a boon to the
              community; it also benefits the utility systems.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson is Nephi City’s electrical cap the amount of
              data centers that come in. Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded that it is the part of two
              things, first was that Virginia did cap and the reason for that was infrastructure
              wasn’t capable of keeping up, additionally you see that projects like this take a lot
              of space, and land is scarce. Second is infrastructure, that can limit it. Companies
              do a lot to prevent disruption of power such as Solar, Wind, BESS electrical
              supply.

              Chairman Cory Thomson asked about part b in the ordinance. Mrs. Shauna Talbot
              stated that yes all of the uses are listed in part b. Chairman responded that in
              section a of the ordinance it states that the uses are listed and then nothing was
              included. Mrs. Shauna Talbot noted the error and will address the correction and
              the definitions will be defined for next time.

              Commissioner Jim Nelson asked about the recreation area mentioned for this
              ordinance, Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded that these locations work around the
              clock. The recreation area will be anything that embraces recreation, like a
              basketball court, gym, things of that nature.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson inquired about smaller data centers, and what
              that would mean if Nephi City builds the 40 acre code means? Mrs. Shauna Talbot
              responded that right now Nephi City will focus on the larger projects, and then
              later if something comes in the city can discuss that at a later venture, there is not
              a desire to open the flood gates. Chairman Cory Thomson also added that the
              smaller data centers are designed for specific use.

              Alternate Commissioner Jim Nelson asked about phasing the projects, or will this
              be built all at one time? Mrs. Shauna Talbot responded said it will be done in
              phasing, based on customers needs and who will come in. They would like it to be
              a turnkey event, for these corporations. Johnathan Vanica from Vancore Capital
              also responded, the acrage, each business is site specific for the company.
              Minimum is 350,000 square foot minimum. There is a rule on megawatt per acre.
              They like to space things out because of the heavy use, and it is why big pieces of
              land are needed.

              Chairman Cory Thomson asked about the 140’ frontage for a 40 acre, that seems
              small. He would like the visual. Mrs. Shauna Talbot stated that it would look like
              a long pencil. She will come with additional information.

              Commissioner Heather Robertson asked about an google image, to see what that
              building looked like with the setbacks. Mrs. Shauna Talbot stated she would look
              and see. Commissioner Robertson also inquired on the guard towers, where they
              would be attached to buildings like these. Mr. Jonathan Vanica assisted in
              responding that there would be a checkpoint stand, like an army base.

              Alternate Commissioner Jim Nelson asked about the e-waste, the current landfill
              is almost full. Mrs. Shauna Talbot directed the question to Mr. Jonathon Vanica.
              Jonathon clarified, not a lot of people and not a waste. Every 3 – 5 years they
              need to be replaced and wires, electronical waste. Most would be taken off site
              and recycled. Alternate Commissioner Jim Nelson would like to see it specified in
              an ordinance.

              Alternate Commissioner Jim Nelson inquired about diverse power sources, on
              solar, wind power, what is the plan on carpeting this area for solar panels? Mr.
              Vanica stated that they don’t plan on blanketing this area. Jon clarified that they
              have been working on base load and arrive in 2027. Natural gas company would
              come and be adjacent to the data center. Working in conjunction with the Agri-
              Park. Wind power would come through from Wyoming to the power station in
              Mona. Thirsty Valley Solar would provide the data center power provider, and
              they have property up and down the valley, with the solar panel needs.

              Chairman Cory Thomson inquired about the street plan for this area? Mrs. Shauna
              talbot stated that Nephi City recently received a grant from Jones and Demille to
              assist Nephi City with a master transportation plan. The inside of these areas will
              also have a street plan, because they will need to have access.
