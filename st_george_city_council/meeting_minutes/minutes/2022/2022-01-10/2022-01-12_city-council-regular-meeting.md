      Councilmember Hughes’ priorities include: Installing the sidewalk on 2000 South;
      Public safety: both police and fire; Legal Department staffing; Transportation: keep

      Link to Councilmember McArthur continuing to outline his priorities: 01:32:15

      Councilmember McArthur’s priorities include: Public Safety: surveillance at parks to
      deter vandalism; a new City Hall building; the Sugarloaf trail; traffic on 3000 East;
      the 1450 South extension and preserving the Mayor’s Loop Trail; extension of
      Washington Fields Road through the west side of the Airport property; look at
      purchasing the property directly to the east of the airport terminal; SGMT relocation;
      protecting Virgin River areas; protecting hillsides; try to get City View to relinquish
      apartment on the interior of the first floor and add commercial; do marketing
      campaigns regarding trash pickup; and supporting economic development.

      Link to Councilmember Larkin outlining her priorities and discussion with staff:
      01:34:30

      Councilmember Larkin’s priorities include: Public safety: both police and fire;
      archeological preservation; preservation of Webb Hill; installing sidewalks along 3050
      East; 311 service up and running and update website; Golf courses – allowing
      prepay options for tee times; water conservation and messaging; housing crisis;
      puppy stores; having a multicultural advisory board; assist the Dove Center in
      expanding their facility; and preserving open space.

      Link to Councilmember Larsen outlining her priorities and discussion with staff:
      02:04:55

      Councilmember Larsen’s priorities include: Traffic signals – 3000 East at Crimson
      Ridge; preserve agriculture; historic preservation; expand Suntran routes to include
      linear lines; incorporate the Dixie story at the Children’s Museum; have high school
      bike clubs educate the younger kids regarding bike safety; vacation rental ordinance
      and complaints from neighbors; homelessness; IT involvement with building process
      to help with workflow; aesthetics of the City Cemetery; and inviting Planning
      Commission to additional work meetings.
