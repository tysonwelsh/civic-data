      00:00:00

AWARD OF BID:
    Consider approval to award bid to Watts Construction for the
    construction of fire station 10 in the amount of $6,419,000.

      Link to introduction from City Manager John Willis and presentation from Facilities
      Manager Carlos Robles, including discussion between City Manager John Willis,
      the City Council, Chief Stoker, and Mr. Robles: 00:00:08

      Agenda Packet [Page 2]

      Link to motion: 00:02:32

      MOTION:
            A motion was made by Councilmember Tanner to award the bid to Watts
            Construction for the construction of fire station 10 in the amount of
            $6,419.000.
      SECOND:
            The motion was seconded by Councilmember McArthur.
      VOTE:
            Mayor Randall called for a vote, as follows:

             Councilmember Hughes – aye
             Councilmember McArthur – aye
             Councilmember Larkin – aye
             Councilmember Larsen – aye
             Councilmember Tanner – aye

             The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
