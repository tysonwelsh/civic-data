3. DISCUSSION ITEMS
3.1 Mayor Jenny Wilson's 2022 Recommended Budget 
Presentation
21-1224
Mayor Jennifer Wilson delivered the 2022 budget presentation.  She 
stated the proposed budget reflects the mission and priorities of Salt Lake 
County. Included in this budget is both a traditional budget and a $ 64.3 
million in American Rescue Plan Act funding. She highlighted the following 
areas where there are significant proposals: health crisis and Health 
Department; parks, recreation, trails; human services /homelessness/criminal 
justice; housing; jail release program; food security; the Office of Diversity 
& Inclusion's, the Office of New Americans; water conservation and 
planning; County Elected Officials; smart government; human resources 
investment; and the County’s commitment to its employees. 
Mr.  Darrin Casper , Deputy   Mayor   of   Finance   and   Administration,
