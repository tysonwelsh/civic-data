PUBLIC HEARING(S) 
                                                  Hearing began at – 3:11 p.m. 
 
OAM2023-001008 - An ordinance repealing and replacing Title 18 of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances, 2001, entitled “Subdivisions,” in order to update the title to reflect state legislative changes, 
changes to the planning and development service provider for Salt L ake County, and to otherwise update 
Salt Lake County policy on subdivisions to reflect better principles of planning, efficiency, and 
affordability; and making other related changes. Presenter: Brian Tucker and Zach Shaw (Motion/Voting) 
 
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the 
amendment and proposed changes. 
 
Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding the community council process. 
Requesting a continuance to December for decision to allow opportunity for comments from community 
councils. 
 
Commissioners, staff, and counsel had a brief discussion regarding trail exactions, noticing, repeal and 
replace, and recommendation.  
 
Commissioner Staker motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner Knoblock seconded that motion. 
Motion passed. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING OPENED 
No one from the public was present to speak. 
 
Commissioner Staker motioned to close the public hearing, Commissioner Sorenson seconded that motion. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING CLOSED 
Motion: To continue Application #OAM2023-001008 - An ordinance repealing and replacing Title 18 of 
the Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001, entitled “Subdivisions,” in order to update the title to 
reflect state legislative changes, changes to the planning and development service pro vider for Salt Lake 
County, and to otherwise update Salt Lake County policy on subdivisions to reflect better principles of 
planning, efficiency, and affordability; and making other related changes to the December 21st Mountainous 
Planning District Planning Commission Meeting.  
Motion by: Commissioner Knoblock 
2nd by: Commissioner Staker 
Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present) 
 
Commissioner Sorensen adjourned.
