2.    Public Hearing: Home Occupation The Groom Shop
      a.     Open Public Hearing
             Ann opened the public hearing at 7:02pm

      b.     Staff Presentation
             Shauna introduced The Groom Shop, which is a dog grooming business. She
             explained that this is an impact business, which warrants a public hearing
             according to City Code.

      c.     Commission Discussion
             Fran Petersen asked Mrs.Cassidy Hansen if she anticipated more than one
             customer at a time. Cassidy answered that it would be one client at a time. Larry
             Ostler asked if she would be taking walk-ins, or if it was by appointment only.
             Cassidy said that she hadn’t thought about that yet, but it will be walk-in for now.

      d.     Action Item- Motion
             Ann closed the public hearing at 7:04pm and asked for a motion to approve the
             business license for The Groom Shop.

      Motion: John Ford Second: Cory Thomson Outcome: Motion Carries

3.   Letter of Support for 138 kv line from Planning Commission to City Council
     a.     Staff Presentation
            Shauna explained that the City is applying for a Rural Opportunity Grant. She
            said the City Council looked at the agreements last night, and that they are all in
            place. As part of the grant, the Planning Commission has been asked to provide
            a letter of support. Shauna stated that the City is applying for $600,000; the
            project cost is over $11 million.

     b.     Commission Discussion
            Heather Robertson asked if there are any unintended consequences with
            building the substation in this location. Shauna said one consequence is the
            annexation of some property. Heather thought this annexation had already
            happened. Shauna and Mayor Seely explained it wasn't complete yet, since the
            development agreements need to be taken care of first. Shauna explained that
            this is a necessary line that will help with the capacity the City has already
            promised to expanding businesses. Mayor Seely said he had a chance to go and
            meet with the board and they were very appreciative to have him participate in
            person. He explained that if we have an issue in one place, this new line will
            allow the City to reroute the power in order to avoid disruption to essential
            businesses. Larry Ostler said something that will help is the current construction
            of the gas generation plant to help out on peak times.

     c.     Action Item- Motion
            Ann asked for a motion to sign a letter of support from the Planning
            Commission to the City Council for the 138kv line.

     Motion: Fran Petersen Second: Heather Robertson Outcome: Motion Carries
