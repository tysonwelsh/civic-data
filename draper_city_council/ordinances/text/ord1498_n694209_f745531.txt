City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On August 3, 2021, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1498, amending
Titles 9 and 17 of the Draper City Municipal Code. The complete ordinance is on file
at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published
this 4th day of August 2021. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
