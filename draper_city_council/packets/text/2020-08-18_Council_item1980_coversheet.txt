Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Jennifer Jastremsky
CC:  Date: August 18, 2020
Re: Hidden Canyon Estates Phase 6 Traverse Ridge Special Service District (TRSSD) Annexation Plat Comments: A request for approval to annex Hidden Canyon Estates Phase 6 into the Traverse Ridge Special Service District (TRSSD) for approximately 6.32 acres located at approximately 14755 South Springtime Rd. The property is currently zoned RM (Multi-family Residential) and C-3 (General Commercial) .
The Hidden Canyon Estates development is bounded by a development agreement that establishes the minimum lot sizes, development standards, and setbacks for the subdivision. The Preliminary Plat for Hidden Canyon Estates was approved on April 19, 2016. Since that time, the applicant has obtained Final Plat approvals for Phases 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6. All but phase 6 have been recorded. Phase 6 obtained final plat approval on June 25, 2020. One of the conditions of approval, as stipulated in the development agreement, is the property be annexed into the TRSSD prior to plat recordation.   The findings are as follows: For approval:
1. It is in the best interest of the city and the general health, safety and welfare of the public, specifically, the future citizens of the area covered by the petition, to annex said area to the Traverse Ridge Special Service District.
