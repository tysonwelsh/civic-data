         NOTICE OF OGDEN PLANNING COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING


The Ogden City Planning Commission will hold a public hearings on Wednesday, April 2,
2025, at 5:00 pm in the City Council Chambers, 3rd Floor, Ogden Municipal Building, 2549
Washington Boulevard, in Ogden, Utah to consider the following:

    •   Zoning change at 853 7th Street from R-1-6 to R-2S/CO
    •   Zoning ordinance text amendment allowing newspaper notices to be published
        any time prior to a hearing.

The proposal may be viewed at the Ogden Municipal Building, 2549 Washington
Boulevard, during regular business hours, or on the city’s website
planningnotices.ogdencity.com. Anyone wishing to comment on the proposals may
speak at the hearing. This meeting will also be held electronically.
