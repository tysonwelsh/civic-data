    3. COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT WITH THE UTAH DIVISION OF EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT
       (DEM) TO ESTABLISH A PUBLIC INSURANCE AGENCY MUTUAL FOR THE PURPOSE OF
       ADMINISTERING THE UTAH SEARCH AND RESCUE

       Lt. Brandon Toll, of the County Sheriff’s Office, noted that House Bill 324 established a Search
       and Rescue Assistance Card Program in 2015. The DEM, Office of Outdoor Recreation and all
       29 counties need to enter into an interlocal agreement that establishes an insurance mutual. The
       agreement basically states that the search and rescue card is not an insurance program.
       Individual(s) can purchase the search and rescue card and that money goes into a search and
       rescue fund, which is separate from the Search and Rescue Assistance Program. If a county
       charges for search and rescue, the individual(s) are to be exempt from that charge unless the
       rescue results from them being reckless. He addressed the commissioners’ questions, including
       that it is an annual card and there is no cost to the county.
       Commissioner Bell moved to approve the Cooperative Agreement with the Utah Division of
       Emergency Management to establish a Public Insurance Agency Mutual to administer the Utah
       Search and Rescue; Commissioner Ebert seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye

    8. SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT WITH WEBER STATE UNIVERSITY (WSU) SMALL BUSINESS
       DEVELOPMENT CENTER TO SPONSOR THE LEADERSHIP TRAINING PROGRAM

       Douglas Larsen, Weber County Economic Development Partnership Director, stated that WSU is
       launching a leadership development training program for small businesses. He noted that often
       small business owners have little to no experience starting/running a business and WSU,
       alongside its community partners has filled an important gap in the economy by assisting
       prospective/current business owners attain knowledge and direction they need. In an effort to
       bolster small businesses success in Weber and Davis counties, WSU is aligning strategic partners
       to provide sponsorship funding to establish this new leadership development program targeting
       entrepreneurs and small businesses. Mr. Larsen noted that the success of small businesses
       significantly impact the economy both locally and nationally. This is a one-year sponsorship
       agreement.

       Brandon Stoddard, Director, Small Business Development Center, stated that the program works
       with about 300 small businesses per year and spoke of the importance of the leadership
       component.

       Commissioner Ebert said that the county is in the process of putting together an economic and
       community development business model. County staff has met with various groups and one issue
       that was discussed was the importance of a robust, small business entrepreneur program. He
       offered the county’s assistance in putting out the word about the program.
       Commissioner Ebert moved to approve a Sponsorship Agreement with Weber State University
       Small Business Development Center to sponsor the Leadership Training Program; Commissioner
       Bell seconded.
       Commissioner Ebert – aye; Commissioner Bell – aye; Chair Gibson – aye
