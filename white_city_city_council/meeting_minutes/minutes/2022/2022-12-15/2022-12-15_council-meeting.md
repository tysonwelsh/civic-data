                                  Special Workshop Meeting

White City Zoning Ordinance Title 19

               Erin O’Kelley, Long Range Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District,
stated she will be reviewing phase one of the White City Metro Township Title 19 – Zoning
modifications. There are zones that will be removed as they are not needed in White City. She
will review each chapter and the Council will need to make decisions on what uses it wants, what
residents can do in those zones, and how it wants it to look.

              The Council reviewed the following chapters in Title 19 – Zoning:

   -   Chapter 19.22: Parks and Open Space
   -   Chapter 19.26: Agricultural Zones
   -   Chapter 19.28: Single-Family Residential Zones
   -   Chapter 19.30: Medium-Density Residential Zones
   -   Chapter 19.32: Commercial Zones
