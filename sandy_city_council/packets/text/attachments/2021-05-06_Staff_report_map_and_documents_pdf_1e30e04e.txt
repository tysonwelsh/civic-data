REQUEST
Mr. Kirk Randall, representing Anderson, Wahlen & Associates Engineering is requesting that the Planning Commission review the proposed remodeling of the existing Ken Garff Hyundai Dealership, located at 10770 S. Auto Mall Drive. The subject property is located in the Auto Mall (AM) (Dealership) zoning district. The remodel project includes: Changing the front façade of the existing structure to more closely match the Hyundai national dealership building "Branding" by partial demolition of the existing building; modifying the existing parking layout to add customer and accessible parking to the front of the building; modifying the parking lot and some of the existing landscape islands to accommodate an ADA compliant pedestrian accessway between Auto Mall Drive and the dealership building; adding a small building addition to the south side of the building; and adding a second overhead door to the Service Department reception area on the west side of the building. Architectural Design review of the exterior building materials and colors is also required. The Planning Commission is the land-use authority for these requested actions.

BACKGROUND
The site is lot 6, of the Utah Auto Mall, Phase 1 Commercial Subdivision, and is the current site of the Ken Garff Hyundai automobile dealership and the former site of a Riverton Motors dealership. All streetscape improvements are existing from the overall development of Utah Auto Mall development. The existing (AM) zoning requires that site plan approval and architectural design review be conducted by the Planning Commission.

NOTICE
Notices were mailed to property owners within a 500-foot radius of the subject parcel to notify them of the Planning Commission meeting. A neighborhood meeting was not held for this project due to the project's location relative to to a lack of residenital zoning in the area.

ANALYSIS
Pedestrian and Vehicle Access and On-site Circulation. All street improvements, sidewalks, landscaped parking strips and internal driveway connections are in place from prior development of the automobile dealership, and will remain adequate for the proposed development. The remodeling includes improvements to ADA accessability, by modifying existing parking lot landscape islands to incorporate a new sidewalk between Auto Mall Drive and the dealership building. ADA accessibile vehicle parking is being located in the front of the dealership, where the front of the existing building is being modified by partial demolition and placement of the new national Hyundai dealership "Branding" building exterior materials and colors.

Zoning Review. The auto dealership use is a permitted use in the (AM) zoning district. All zoning required building setbacks, landscape buffers and parking lot layouts are existing and comply with the ordinances.

Architectural Design Review. The building's design, materials and colors have been reviewed by the Sandy Planning Staff as to compliance with the Sandy City Architectural Design Requirements and the Auto Mall Master Plan update, and now comply. Staff made some recommendations to the developer on the screening of new roof top mechanical units (RTU's) located near the front of the building, which has been revised as shown on the attached plans. The other exterior changes to the building are minor and non-consequetial. Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve the building architectural design, materials and colors as now proposed by the applicant.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission find that the preliminary site plan review is complete for the proposed Ken Garff Hyundai Dealership remodeling project, based upon the following two findings and the following six conditions:

FINDINGS.
     A. That the proposed project meets or will meet all the applicable requirements of the Sandy City Development Code and the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards as to building architectural design, materials and colors.

     B. That all necessary Sandy City Departments and Division have reviewed the project and recommend approval by the Planning Commission, subject to plan finalization and meeting their departmental requirements.

CONDITIONS.

     1. That the developer proceeds through the final site plan review process with staff prior to the start of any construction. The final site plan shall comply with all Development Code requirements and those modifications required or allowed by the Planning Commission.

     2. That the development complies with all Building & Safety, Fire and Life Safety Codes applicable to this type of use.

     3. That the developer be responsible to meet all provisions of Auto Mall (AM) zoning district, the Sandy City Architectural Design Standards and the Auto Mall Master Plan update, with the details finalized with staff during final site plan review.

     4. All utility boxes (i.e. transformers, switchgear, telephone, cable TV, etc.) shall be shown on the site plan and shall be placed underground or moved behind the front setback of the buildings and screened from view, preferably located along the proposed driveway. Each box shall be shown in its exact location and shall be noted with its exact height, width and length. Building utility meters shall be mounted to the side or rear elevation or screened.

     5. That the applicant complies with all department requirements as noted in all Preliminary Review letters prior to submittal for final site plan review with staff.

     6. That all signage be reviewed and approved under separate permit and be in conformance with City Code.
