5.1 A Public Hearing to Receive Comments Regarding an 
Amendment to the Planning Commission Ordinance to 
Increase the Number of Commission Members and Outline 
Residence Requirements for Same
20-0737
Attachments: Staff Report
Salt Lake County Amendement of Planning Commission 
Ordinance
This is the time set for a public hearing to receive public comments regarding 
an amendment to the Planning Commission Ordinance.
Mr. Jake Young , Planning Program Manager, Regional Planning and 
Transportation Division, reviewed the ordinance amendment, which increases 
the number of Salt Lake Planning Commission members from 5 to 7 and the 
number of unincorporated members from 3 to 4.  The reason for the 
amendment is because of upcoming major development projects, i .e. 
Olympia Hills and the West Bench General Plan, which will require a lot of 
planning.  In June, this amendment was reviewed by the Planning 
Commission and a public hearing was held. The Planning Commission also 
recommended approval.   
Council Member Bradley  asked what geographic area people can live in to 
be eligible to be on the Planning Commission.   
Mr. Young stated unincorporated members can live anywhere in the 
unincorporated areas of the County, i .e. the islands in Sandy, the west bench .  
The other three members, as well as the two alternates, can be from 
anywhere in the County.  
Page 2 of 23
County Council Meeting Minutes August 4, 2020
A motion was made by Council Member Ghorbani, seconded by Council 
Member Bradley, to open the public hearing.  The motion carried by a 
unanimous vote.
Mr. Steve Van Maren stated this is a good move.  He liked a larger board in case of 
absenteeism, and it opens membership up to more residents not in the unincorporated 
County.  
Council Member Bradley  asked what the process was for a person to become a 
member of the Planning Commission.
Mr. Young stated once someone fills out the application, the Mayor ’s Office reviews it 
and makes a recommendation to the Council for its approval.

RESOLUTION NO. 5772
EXTENSION OF THE STATE OF EMERGENCY DECLARED BY THE 
SALT LAKE COUNTY MAYOR ARISING OUT OF THE MARCH 18, 
2020 EARTHQUAKE
A RESOLUTION OF THE COUNTY COUNCIL OF SALT LAKE 
COUNTY, BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED:
THAT, the County Council of Salt Lake County, State of Utah, met in 
regular session of the Council on the 4th day of August, 2020.
WITNESS:
WHEREAS, a magnitude 5.7 earthquake occurred in Salt Lake County near 
the Metro Township of Magna (“Magna earthquake ”) on the morning of 
March 18, 2020; and
Page 4 of 23
County Council Meeting Minutes August 4, 2020
WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah Code Annotated, Sections 53-2a-205 and 
-208, the Mayor of Salt Lake County has the power to declare a state of 
emergency for Salt Lake County; and
WHEREAS, Section 2.86.030, Salt Lake County Code of Ordinances, 2001, 
authorizes the Mayor to declare that a state of emergency exists in Salt Lake 
County and to invoke certain powers in aid of responding to and mitigating 
such emergency; and 
WHEREAS, the Mayor issued a Proclamation Declaring a State of 
Emergency and Invoking Emergency Powers in Salt Lake County on March 
18, 2020 based upon the damage caused by the Magna earthquake; and 
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County experienced aftershocks from the Magna 
earthquake; and
WHEREAS, the Magna earthquake and its aftershocks have caused damage 
to numerous buildings in Salt Lake County; and
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County continues to investigate and review the 
damage caused by the Magna earthquake and its aftershocks; and
WHEREAS, State law and County ordinance permit the Mayor ’s declaration 
of an emergency to be effective for a period not to exceed 30 days, unless 
the emergency declaration is continued or renewed with the consent of the 
County Council; and
WHEREAS, the County Council continued the Mayor ’s declaration of an 
emergency on April 14, 2020 until Midnight on Monday, July 6, 2020; and
WHEREAS, the County Council continued the Mayor ’s declaration of an 
emergency on June 23, 2020 until Midnight on Tuesday, August 7, 2020.
RESOLUTION:
NOW, THEREFORE, THE FOLLOWING IS RESOLVED:
1. The County Council of Salt Lake County hereby finds that continuing 
the State of Emergency for Salt Lake County due to the Magna earthquake 
Page 5 of 23
County Council Meeting Minutes August 4, 2020
and its aftershocks is appropriate.
2. The Council acknowledges and supports invoking the emergency 
powers and operations of the County by the Mayor.
3. The Council supports the County ’s utilization of its personnel and 
resources, as ordered and authorized by the Mayor, to perform all functions 
specified in County Ordinance 2.86.050 and in other applicable provisions of 
law.
4. The Council supports and approves all orders and regulations issued 
thus far issued by the Mayor regarding the state of emergency, copies of 
which have been duly filed with the Salt Lake County Clerk and previously 
disseminated to the Council.
5. This Resolution shall take effect immediately and be declared to be 
ongoing and continuing until Midnight on Tuesday, November 3, 2020, 
unless the facts supporting the emergency no longer exist as determined by 
the Mayor.
6. This Resolution shall be provided to the State of Utah and the Mayor 
and shall be filed immediately with the Salt Lake County Clerk and published 
by general dissemination as appropriate.

each of the Right of Way Contracts attached hereto as Exhibits 1 and 2).
