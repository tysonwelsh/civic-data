SCOPE OF SERVICES – Phase 2

Design, engineer and install a Water Treatment System into a building that will be constructed at the
Green Well Pumphouse. The Green Well Pump House will be modified to have a building attached in
order to house the treatment system. An Engineer/Architect will be selected to design the water
treatment system building. The City will bid and select a firm to construct the building. The treatment
system and the water treatment building together will be known as the Water Treatment Facility.

The Water Treatment System will include but may not be limited to: adding VFD to existing motor and
adding to and/or adjusting existing piping to connect the proposed treatment facility to the existing
pump house.

The Water Treatment System will be engineered and designed for a minimum 20‐year life

The Water Treatment System must be engineered to meet JVWCD Standards (proposal option 2 of Total
Water Management’s response to RFP 222‐20), Jordan River discharge.


The scope of work is to include the following:
-   Meeting with a committee that may include the Mayor, City Council members, City Staff, Building
    Engineer/Architect (not yet selected) and invited citizens.

‐   Develop engineered construction plans and drawings on the water treatment system. Must work
    with the building Engineer/Architect on the design so the water treatment system can be installed
    and connected inside the water treatment building.

‐   Install and connect water treatment system which will include but may not be limited to:

        A.   Oversee Project.

        B.   Furnish, install, startup and make fully operational the treatment system.

        C.   Schedule and/or participate in a weekly Owner/Engineering Firm/Building Construction
             Contractor Meeting.

        D.   Coordinate any utility work needed.

        E.   Create punch list and follow through the completion of the project.

        F.   Train city staff to operate and maintain treatment system.

        G.   Provide one full set of As Built Plans.

        H.   Close out the project.

               Davis-Bacon Act Compliance Requirements Under ARPA

         What Davis-Bacon Act requirements apply under ARPA for the City?
This memorandum clarifies Davis-Bacon Act compliance requirements for the City’s ARPA projects.

DAVIS BACON REMAINS IN FORCE FOR THE FOLLOWING UNDER ARPA:
For projects funded through SLFRF (ARPA) funds, Davis-Bacon Act requirements apply to projects
performed under2:
    1.1. EC-5: Infrastructure (Clean Water, Drinking Water, Broadband) overall projects with
         construction work over $10MM.
    Key takeaway
    • Davis-Bacon Act requirements will continue to apply based on $10MM overall project dollar
        threshold.

   1.2. Any project that combines project funding from other Federal funding streams - such as HUD
        CDBG - must follow current Federal Davis-Bacon Act requirements as required by the
        additional funding stream.
   Key takeaway
       For any housing construction project that combines ARPA and HUD funding, Davis-Bacon Act
       requirements do not apply to rehabilitation or new construction of residential properties with
       fewer than 8 units including single family residences. 3
