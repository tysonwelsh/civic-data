G. ACTION ITEMS:
   1. APPROVAL OF THE WEBER COUNTY COMMUNITY REINVESTMENT AREA POLICY.
            Stephanie Russell (Economic Development Director): This is the product of 18 months of work by the
            Economic Development Finance committee, which was created to shepherd Community Reinvestment Areas
            (CRAs) and tax increment finance (TIF) requests. A state audit of administration of Tax Increment Finance
            found participants for CRAs wanting as it relates to criteria for issuing TIF and for reporting fund usage.
            After looking at those findings in relation to our CRAs, the Economic Development Finance Committee
            worked hard to build a comprehensive and straightforward policy. The intent of the policy is to provide a
            standard process and procedure will evaluate and make recommendations on all requests for County tax
            increment financing in any project area. There are two parts to this Policy. The first has to do with
            petitioning for the County to participate in a CRA and the process for our Economic Development Finance
         Committee to evaluate that request. The second deals with petitioning for a project area. The criteria came
         from State code plus some additional guidance from the County. Some of what was included is a baseline
         value not exceeding 50% of the tax increment for 15 years. We are doing this to ensure a majority of the tax
         increment finance being dedicated for CRAs goes toward public or regional infrastructure and stimulating
         the economy. It’s not just about creating jobs and stimulating the economy but we need to include providing
         renewable energy sources and attainable housing in Western Weber corridors. We would like to see these
         two types of developments work together which is why this policy was made to be very compliant and
         compatible with public infrastructure districts (PIDs); as we develop out in growth, we still need to provide
         services to these new areas. This allows us to be able to partner cohesively with other taxing entities
         servicing those development projects and provide infrastructure lift along with job creation and economic
         development in our unincorporated areas and as we participate with our municipalities. This policy provides
         a straight forward process so everyone has an understanding of how project areas will be evaluated and what
         level of participation to expect from the County.

         Commissioner Froerer: This has been needed since I have been a Commissioner here. We never had a set
         policy so we were making decisions based on what was being presented. This gives us a policy that ensures
         people know what to expect when they come to present. Based on this Policy, the focus is first on
         infrastructure. And everybody is on a level playing field.

         Commissioner Bolos: I really appreciate the consistency that developers have when they come to the
         County. I have one question. In Section 3.A.C, it says a projection of County TIF0% for test, then at 25%
         and 50%. You discussed 50%. Can you explain the 25%?

         Ms. Russell: We want to see what the project looks like at 0%, 25%, and 50%. This requires the petition to
         show the project actually pencils and the TIF complements the project instead of being required to make it
         happen. Additionally, one of the new requirements is for them to report every year where that money is
         going.

         Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the Weber County Community Reinvestment Area Policy;
         Commissioner Bolos seconded.
         Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer - aye
