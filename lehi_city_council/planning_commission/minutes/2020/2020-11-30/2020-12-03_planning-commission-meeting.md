Regular Work Session, 5:30 p.m.

1. Call to Order
Commission Chairman Hemmert welcomed everyone to the meeting.

2. Discussion Items

    2.1 Training video presentation on Strong Towns
     Mike West opened presentation on Strong Towns and showed a training video. The
    Commissioners and Staff discussed the general concept of smalls towns.

    2.2 Introduction to Bang the Table platform
    Brittney Harris presented on Bang the Table engagement HQ. She introduced the Lehi
    website that will help engage Lehi City and the citizens. Brittney went through a brief
    overview of the website that the Planning Department will be implementing.

    Sierra Pierson showed examples of other City's that have implemented similar programs.

    Jacob Curtis presented uses that the website will be able to mainstream for the City. The
    website can be utilized by a lot of Departments in the City.

    2.3 Planning Commissioner Presentations
    Abe Nielsen, Scott Bunker, and Roger Ellis gave presentations on their roles in Planning
    Commission and involvement in the community along with their personal background.

3. Adjournment
With no further business to come before the Planning Commission at this time, Commissioner
Peterson moved to adjourn the meeting. Commissioner Everett seconded the motion. The
motion passed unanimously. The meeting adjourned at approximately 7:30 p.m.
