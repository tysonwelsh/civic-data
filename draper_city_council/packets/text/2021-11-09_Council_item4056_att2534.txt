                                     BYLAWS
                                      OF THE
                                 METRO FIRE AGENCY

                                       Article I - Purposes
The Metro Fire Agency (the “Agency”) is an interlocal entity created by Interlocal Agreement (the
“Agreement”) in or about March 2020. Its purposes are to: (1) promote the health, safety, and
welfare of the collective citizens of its participating municipalities; (2) provide improved fire
protection by combining and sharing the participating municipalities’ collective capabilities and
resources for themselves and their neighboring communities; (3) render immediate unified and
cooperative action to each other and neighboring communities; (4) guard against potential multiple
threats to individual cities; and (5) form and sponsor task forces in accordance with the Utah
Emergency Management Act to operate anywhere within or without the boundaries of the State of
Utah, all as further outlined in the Agreement.
                                    Article II – Membership
Membership is currently composed of the cities of Bluffdale, Draper, Murray, Sandy, South
Jordan, South Salt Lake, West Jordan, and West Valley (collectively the “Member Cities”).
However, any municipality within Salt Lake County, which has its own individual fire department,
may apply for membership to the Agency subject to the provisions of the Agreement.
                                        Article III – Fees
Fees may be assessed subject to the provisions of the Agreement.
                                      Article IV – Trustees
The Agency shall be governed by a Board of Trustees (“Trustees”), consisting of each Member
City’s Chief Executive Officer, Chief Administrative Officer or Mayor, as dictated by that Member
City’s form of government, or their designee. A designee shall be selected from the Member City’s
city administrative personnel and shall not be a member of the Operations Advisory Committee or
other fire personnel. The Trustees shall have sole authority and responsibility to conduct the
Agency’s business, as outlined and established in the Agreement. Such authority and responsibility
shall include all decisions related to the organizational, operational, and financial conduct of the
Agency. Trustees’ officers are: (1) Chair; and (2) Vice-Chair and shall be elected from among the
Trustees at a meeting, at such time and place to be determined by the Trustees. The Trustees may
appoint a secretary from among the Trustees or the host agency, as defined in the Agreement, may
use its personnel to perform administrative duties while functioning as the host agency. Trustees’
officers shall serve no longer than two years, or until their successors have been elected and
qualified. The duties of the Trustees’ officers shall correspond generally to those duties that
normally devolve upon such officers, unless such duties are otherwise specifically assigned by a
majority of the Trustees’ officers or members. The chair shall be the Trustees’ presiding officer
and the vice-chair shall serve at the request of the chair or in the absence of the chair. Trustees
may also appoint additional officers and representatives as it deems necessary for the
administration of the Agency. Trustees may establish, from time to time, Standing or Ad-hoc
committees as shall be deemed appropriate or necessary to carry out the business of the Agency.
                         Article V – Operations Advisory Committee
In addition to any Standing or Ad-hoc committee that the Trustees may deem appropriate or
necessary to carry out the Agency’s business, an Operations Advisory Committee (“Operations”)
shall be established under the direction and supervision of the Trustees. Operations’ members are
the Fire Chiefs from each Member City or their designee. Operations shall provide advice and
recommendations to the Trustees for planning, budget preparation, system coordination, policies,
procedures, and standards utilized by the Agency, and may be given other responsibilities and
authority as approved by the Trustees or as outlined in the Agreement. Operations officers are: (1)
Chair; and (2) Vice-Chair and shall be elected from among the Operations members at a meeting,
at such time and place to be determined by the Trustees or the Operations’ members. Operations
may appoint a secretary or the Chair may use personnel from their fire department to perform
administrative duties while functioning as Chair. Operations’ officers shall serve no longer than
two years, or until their successors have been elected and qualified. Operations may also appoint
additional officers and representatives, as deemed necessary, for the administration of its duties.
The duties of the Operations’ officers shall correspond generally to those duties that normally
devolve upon such officers, unless such duties are otherwise specifically assigned by a majority of
the Trustees.
                                      Article VI – Meetings
Trustees shall meet as often as needed but not less frequently than once every three (3) months.
Trustees’ meeting will be held at a location to be determined by the Trustees. Operations shall
meet as often as needed but not less frequently than once every three (3) months, or as directed by
the Trustees. Operations’ meetings will be held at a location to be determined by the Trustees or
by the Operations’ officers.
                             Article VII – Conflict with Agreement
To the extent these Bylaws conflict in any way with the Agreement, the Agreement controls.
                                  Article VIII – Amendments
These Bylaws may be amended by a vote of two-thirds (2/3) of the Trustees, provided that all
Trustees have been notified of the proposed changes by pre-paid mail, facsimile, or electronic mail,
at least ten (10) days prior to the meeting at which such voting is to occur.
