HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-22

Z2022-59 -ORDINANCE TO AMEND CITY CODE TITLE 10 TO REDUCE THE
MINIMUM LANDSCAPING REQUIREMENTS TO FIFTY PERCENT

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to Utah State Code, may adopt an ordinance
to establish land development regulations, which includes the adoption or amendment of zoning
regulations or map; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning regulation amendment to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior
to the said public hearing to recommend any zoning regulation amendments to the City Council;
and

WHEREAS, a notice of a Planning Commission public hearing on a zoning regulation
amendment was posted in three public places on April 25, 2022, for a meeting to be held on May
5, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission made a recommendation for the zoning regulation
amendment in a public meeting held on May 5, 2022 at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chamber;
and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council
to consider the zoning regulation amendment and Planning Commission recommendation in a
public meeting; and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on May 25, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. to
discuss the zoning regulation amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of Herriman
City to adopt the zoning regulation amendment, which was recommended by the Planning
Commission and documented in City File number Z2022-059;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:

Title 10 of Herriman City Code shall be amended as set forth in the attached Exhibit A,
and all other sections and provisions of Title 10 shall remain unaffected by this amendment.

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 22-22, shall take immediate effect as soon as it shall be
published or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s
recorder.
PASSED AND APPROVED this 25" day of May 2022.

10-23-7: Landscaping Design Standards

A. General Requirements:
1. Landscaped areas shall be located:
a. Adjacent to building elevations which form major public views of a project from
adjacent streets and property, and to users of the project; and
b. In between buildings or portions of buildings; and in any plaza or courtyard.

2. Landscaped areas shall include trees, shrubs, vegetative, organic and inorganic ground
cover, and other organic and inorganic materials identified in an approved landscaping
plan. All required landscape areas shall be occupied by plant material or ground cover.

a. Required landscaping areas shall include live plant material at least to the
following extent:
(1) Perimeter and internal landscaping: seventy—five-pereent-75%) Fifty
percent (50%).
(2) Buffer landscaping between residential and non-residential
development: Fifty percent (50%).
