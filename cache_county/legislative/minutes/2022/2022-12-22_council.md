                                 RESOLUTION NO. 2022-35

     A RESOLUTION AMENDING RESOLUTION 2021-06 TO UPDATE THE CACHE
            COUNTY ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY ADVISORY BOARD

        WHEREAS, the Utah Legislature passed Senate Bill 95 during its 2020 General Session, thereby
creating a Rural County Grant Program, which was codified as Utah Code Ann. §§ 17-54-101 through
-104; and

        WHEREAS, the Cache County Council through Resolution 2020-11 on May 26, 2020, created
the Cache County Economic Development Advisory Board; and

         WHEREAS, the Cache County Council appointed members to the Board on June 9, 2020, and in
order to quickly qualify for and apply for the Rural County Grant appointed the same membership as the
Executive Committee of the Cache Valley Economic Development Alliance, which is a component of the
Cache Chamber of Commerce; and

        WHEREAS, the Cache County Council amended Resolution 2020-11with2021-06 to make
changes to the Advisory Board after hiring a County Economic Development Director, which is a
position that will be eliminated as of January 1, 2023; and

        WHEREAS, The Utah Legislature repealed the Rural County Grant Program effective July 1,
2022, and replaced it with the Rural Communities Opportunity Grant under the new Rural Opportunity
Act codified in Utah Code Ann.§§ 63N-4-801 through-804; and

        WHEREAS, the Rural Opportunity Program's purpose is to award grants to address the economic
development needs of rural counties, which needs may include business recruitment, development and
expansion; workforce training and development; and business infrastructure and capital facilities
improvements;

        WHEREAS, Cache County qualifies as a rural county and desires to participate in the Rural
Opportunity Program to promote opportunities for investors and employers and those seeking to
strengthen the economy in Cache County; and

        WHEREAS, the Act requires under Utah Code Ann. §§ 63N-4-803 that each rural county that
seeks a Rural Opportunity grant create a County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board whose
membership is appointed by the county legislative body; and

        WHEREAS, the County seeks input from a variety of County residents, business leaders, and
elected officials to prioritize economic development projects and opportunities in the County; and

       WHEAREAS, the County Council sees fit to update the Cache County Economic Development
Advisory Board to bring it into compliance with state law and County policy;

      NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Cache County Council hereby amends
      Resolution 2020-11 to read as follows:


      The Cache County Council hereby creates the Cache County Economic Opportunity
Advisory Board as follows:


      A. Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board
      1.    The Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board will be
      composed of at least the following members, who shall be appointed by the
      Cache County Council:
             a.     a county representative who is not the Cache County Executive or
                    Executive staff;
             b.     a representative of a municipality in the county;
             c.     a workforce development representative;
             d.     a private-sector representative;
             e.      a member of the public who lives in the County; and
             f.     the Bear River Association of Governments Regional Economic Director,
                    as an advisory and non-voting member.


      2.     The Cache County Council may also appoint additional members with experience
             or expertise in economic development matters.


      3.      In appointing members of the Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory
             Board, the Cache County Council may consider gender and
             socioeconomic diversity.


      4.     The Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board shall assist and advise
             the Cache County Council on:
             a. applying for a grant under Utah Code Ann. §§ 63N-4-802(4)(a);
             b. what projects should be funded by grant money provided to the County under
             Utah Code Ann.§§ 63N-4-802(4)(a): and

     c. preparing reporting requirements for grant money received by Cache
     County under Utah Code Ann. §§ 63N-4-802(4)(a) ..




B. Appointment and Terms of Board Members


1.   The Cache County Council shall appoint each member of the Cache County
     Economic Opportunity Advisory Board.


2.   In appointing initial members of the Cache County Economic Opportunity
     Advisory Board, the Cache County Council shall assign to half, or
     roughly half, of the initial Board members an initial term that expires on
     December 31, 2021. The Cache County Council shall assign to the other half, or
     roughly half, of the initial Board members an initial term that expires on
     December 31, 2023.


3.   After the initial Board members' initial terms, all Board members shall serve
     four-year terms.


4.   A person appointed to the Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board,
     other than the Bear River Association of Governments Regional Economic
     Director, may not serve more than two consecutive terms. For as long as a person
     remains the Bear River Association of Governments Regional Economic Director,
     he or she shall serve on the Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board
     without a term limit.


5.   When a vacancy occurs in one of the positions on the Board that is required under
     Section A.1. of this Resolution for any reason other than the expiration of the
     Board member's term, the Cache County Council shall appoint a replacement to
     fill the unexpired term. When a vacancy occurs in a position on the Board that is
     not required under Section A.1.of this Resolution for any reason other than the
     expiration of the Board member's term, the Cache County Council may, at its
     discretion, appoint a replacement to fill the unexpired term or leave the position
     unfilled. When a vacancy occurs in a position on the Board that is not required
     under Section A.1. of this Resolution due to the expiration of the Board member's
     term, the Cache County Cotmcil may, at its discretion, appoint a replacement to
     serve a new full term or leave the position unfilled.
6.   Membership may not consist of more than 20 percent of the same membership as
     any of the officers and/or executive committees of the Cache Chamber of
     Commerce or its components, and the Northern Utah Chamber Coalition or its
     components.
7.   As the board is a legislative advisory board to the County Council, the County
     Executive or Executive staff shall not be appointed as a voting member of the
     Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board.


C. Conducting of Board Business


1.   The Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board shall elect annually
     from its voting members a chair of the Board. A person may not serve as chair of
     the Board for more than two consecutive years.


2.   The Cache County Economic Development Advisory Board shall elect annually
     from its voting members a vice-chair of the Board.


3.   A majority of the Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board shall
     constitute a quorum for the purpose of conducting Board business, and the action
     of a majority of a quorum shall constitute the action of the Board.


4.   Members of the Cache County Economic Opportunity Advisory Board who have
     or may have a conflict of interest with respect to fund allocation decisions or
     recommendations:
     a.     shall identify the conflict or potential conflict prior to voting on the issue
            and may choose to recuse themselves from voting on the issue; and
      b.    shall sign annually and as needed a disclosure statement in accordance
            with Utah Code Ann.§§ 17-16a and County Code 2.62.
