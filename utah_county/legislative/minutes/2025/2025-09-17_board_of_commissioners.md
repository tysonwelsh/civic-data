FOR UTAH COUNTY

-Brandon Wong, Information Systems
(Timestamp: 10:26)

COMMISSIONER BELTRAN ASKED BRANDON WONG (Information Security Manager)
WHEN THE COMMISSION WOULD BE MADE AWARE OF SEVERE ISSUES. BRANDON
WONG SAID THE RESOLUTION IS WRITTEN IN A WAY THAT THEY WILL HAVE
INTERDEPARTMENTAL COMMUNICATION WHILE WORKING WITH EZRA NAIR
AND HIS TEAM. THEY WOULD DETERMINE AT THAT POINT IF THEY NEED TO
MAKE THE COMMISSIONERS AWARE OF A PROBLEM.

COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER ASKED IF HE KNEW HOW MANY OF THE
CYBER SECURITY ATTACKS ORIGINATE FROM OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES.
BRANDON WONG SAID THEY HAVE SEEN A DRAMATIC UPTICK IN THE NUMBER
OF ATTACKS THAT ARE USING VPNS. THEY ARE ALMOST CERTAIN THAT THOSE
ARE ORIGINATING OUT OF THE COUNTRY BUT IT IS REALLY HARD TO
DETERMINE THE EXACT SOURCE. THEY DO SEE LOGIN ATTEMPTS FROM ALL
AROUND THE WORLD ON A FREQUENT BASIS. THEY SEE RUSSIA, CHINA, IRAN
AND A LOT IN EASTERN EUROPE WITH 20-30% COMING FROM EASTERN EUROPE
AND 20-30% COMING FROM CHINA.

COMMISSIONER BELTRAN: MOTION TO APPROVE
COMMISSIONER POWERS GARNDER: SECOND
ALL IN FAVOR: AYE

PASSED: 3/0
