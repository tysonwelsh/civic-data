Example Motion
I move the Planning Commission approve PLZ-23-1002 “Riverton Cove Amended”, to be
located at 12879 and 12887 South Sabrina Row with the following conditions:

      1. Address any outstanding Riverton City staff redline comments on the plat and plans.
      2. The applicant must obtain any necessary approvals or permits from applicable outside

      3. Amendment shall be approved and recorded with Salt Lake County.
      4. Fencing required by the city along the boundaries of the Riverton Cove Subdivision
         shall be extended to and along the new subdivision boundary.

 Background

This is a public hearing to amend and extend Lots 30 and 31 in Riverton Cove Subdivision by
adding about 6,951-sf to the existing Lots. The additional area comes from the adjoining Parcel
D which was common space in the plat, and from a parcel which is outside of the subdivision.
This application would only adjust the common boundaries and no new lots are created, and no
new improvements are required. The resulting lots and parcels meet the minimum requirements
for size and setbacks in their respective zones.

Riverton Cove is a 31-lot subdivision which was approved by the City in 2022. The subdivision
is zoned RM-6 with an SD Overlay, the lots in the subdivision are required to have a minimum of
5,000-sf. The properties surrounding the subdivision are all R-4 (4-units per acre and 10,000-sf
minimum lot size).

The developer would like to amend the subdivision plat to extend the east line of lots 30 and 31
eastward to take in part of Riverton Cove Parcel D and part of parcel # 27-34-176-018 which is a
parcel outside of the Riverton Cove Plat.

The resulting Lots 30A and 31A will increase from about 6,000-sf to over 9,000-sf each. Parcel
