Pre-Council, 5:30 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present.

2. Tour of the Lehi Archives
The Council participated in a tour of the Lehi Archives

3. Agenda Questions
None.

4. Administrative Report
None.

5. Mayor and Council Reports
The Mayor and Council gave a report on the committees they serve on.


Regular Session, 7:00 p.m.
1. Welcome, Roll Call, Pledge of Allegiance
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Koivisto led the Pledge.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1. Friends of Public Health Award presented to Lehi City from the Utah County Health
Department.
Ralph Clegg with the Utah County Health Department presented an award to Lehi City for its
efforts in passing legislation that increased the minimum age of purchasing tobacco products from
19 to 21.

None.

4. Consent Agenda
4.1. Approve Minutes from the September 8, 2020 Council Meeting.

4.2 Approve Purchase Orders

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to approve the consent agenda items. Councilor
                Koivisto seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
    Councilor Hancock, Yes; and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


5. Consideration of Resolution #2020-69, changing the name of the roadway from Mule
   Deer Circle to Hidden Canyon Circle.
   Mr. Struthers explained that the residents had concerns with the spelling and pronunciation of
   the name and requested the change. All residents with this address agreed to the change.

    Motion:     Councilor Koivisto moved to approve Resolution #2020-69, changing the name of
                the roadway from Mule Deer Circle to Hidden Canyon Circle; including the
                Development Review Committee comments. Councilor Condie seconded the
                motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
    Councilor Hancock, Yes; and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.


6. Consideration of Preliminary subdivision approval of South Creek, a 21-lot residential
   subdivision located at 1400 South 1940 West. (Tabled from 09.22.20 Meeting)
   Scott Dunn was representing PHI properties. He stated that they are working with Utah County
   to resolve the land gap and will have that cleared up before the County records the plat.

    Motion:     Councilor Condie moved to grant Preliminary subdivision approval of South Creek,
                a 21-lot residential subdivision located at 1400 South 1940 West; subject to all
                Development Review Committee and Planning Commission Comments.
                Councilor Koivisto seconded the motion.
