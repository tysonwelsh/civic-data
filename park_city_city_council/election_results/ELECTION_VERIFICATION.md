## Notes

- **All 6 general-election races match.** No discrepancies in winners.
- **2025 mayoral recount confirmed.** Jack Rubin requested a recount of the
  razor-thin contest. The recount produced results identical to the certified canvass:
  Ryan Dickey 1,706 (50.1%) vs. Jack Rubin 1,699 (49.9%) — a **7-vote margin**. The
  Park City Council, acting as Board of Canvassers, unanimously certified Dickey as
  mayor-elect on 2025-11-24. (Confirming sources: Park Record
  https://www.parkrecord.com/2025/11/24/park-city-mayoral-contest-certified-razor-thin-win-by-ryan-dickey/
  and KPCW https://www.kpcw.org/park-city/2025-11-24/recount-confirms-ryan-dickey-as-park-citys-next-mayor )
- Minor non-material note: the exact vote totals quoted in news write-ups (often
  "preliminary"/election-night counts) differ slightly from the repo's final certified
  totals, as expected when later-counted mail/provisional ballots are added. The
  *winners and the 2025 mayoral 7-vote margin* are consistent with the repo in every race.
- Primary-election rows in the source CSVs (advancing candidates) were not separately
  cross-checked here; verification focused on who actually won office (general elections).
