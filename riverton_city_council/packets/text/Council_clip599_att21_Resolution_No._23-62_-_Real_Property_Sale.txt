                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 23-62

   A RESOLUTION OF THE RIVERTON CITY COUNCIL DECLARING CERTAIN
                      REAL PROPERTY SURPLUS

        WHEREAS, Pursuant to Utah Code §10-8-2 (1) (a) (iii) the Riverton City Council may
“purchase, receive, hold, sell, lease, convey, and dispose of real and personal property for the
benefit of the municipality, whether the property is within or without the municipality’s
corporate boundaries, if the action is in the public interest and complies with other law;” and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City owns certain real property described in Exhibit A (“Subject
Property”);

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has determined that the property has become surplus to the
City; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council has determined that the sale of the property will not
adversely affect the City.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby declares that the Subject Property to be surplus to
           the City’s needs, for the purpose of enabling the same to be sold at market value and
           on terms and conditions favorable to the interests of Riverton.

       2. Riverton City Council hereby directs City staff to return with an appropriate Real
           Estate Purchase Agreement to the Council which meets the terms and conditions of
           Section 1 of this Resolution for consideration and possible approval.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

        PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 18th day of
July, 2023 by the following vote:
