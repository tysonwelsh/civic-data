I.Accessory               Dwelling         Unit      Draft         Ordinance            Discussion;            Erin       O'Kelley,              Planner
  Erin      O'Kelley,           Planner,          gave     a presentation               on the Accessory                Dwelling           Unit      (ADU)            ordinance
  draft.     Ms.       O'Kelley          said that         staff     had received            additional         research          tasks      to investigate.                  She
  said that all language                    is subject        to change            and is a draft.           In the draft,          the items          in red have been
  added,         items      crossed        out are being              taken       out of the draft.            The original              draft     only     recognized
  one method               of creating           an ADU,           people       had to build           a new      ADU.           Staff     added         language             that
  states that non-conforming                          structures            may     be converted             to an ADU            if the applicant                follows
  rules     for non-conforming                      structures.         There         are no special           exceptions            for parking            requirements.
  Staff     is still       working         on the non-conforming                        chapter        which      could      alter       language           elsewhere.


  Ms.      O'Kelley          said staff           changed          language         allowing         attached          and detached               ADUs          in R-1         and A-
  1 zones.         Perimeter            fencing       is required             for any property            with         an ADU.           Many       residents           said they
  have      a lot in a R-1-8               zone      that are just            under     8,000        square      feet, these         are non-conforming                          lots.
  Maximum              area for an attached                  ADU            has been        removed.          Alterations          have          been made            to parking
  requirements.              Residents            can no longer               add parking            to the front         yard     for an ADU.                  There         is now
  a maximum                number         of parking          spots         allowed.        This     helps     improve           the street         view        of properties.
  Detached           ADUs           on a flag        lot are not allowed,                  while       attached        ADUs         are.
