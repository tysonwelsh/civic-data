ZONE TEXT CHANGE APPLICATIONS
ZT-6-2024    Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting amendments to Sections 11-2-109,
             11-4-110, and 11-5-103 of the sign ordinance to remove the regulation exemption for
             noncommercial banner signs, to prohibit signs containing content which is not
             protected by the U.S. Constitution or the Utah Constitution, and to modify several
             standards for temporary on-premises signs. (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361
             or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:         Brock Anderson presented a text change amendment to Sections 11-2-109 and 11-4-
                    110 of the sign code at the direction of the City Council, and amendments to Section
                    11-5-103.

                    11-2-109 list types of signs that are exempt from the provisions of this code.
                    Noncommercial banner signs are currently listed as an exempt sign type in the code,
                    so anyone can put up any sort of noncommercial banner. There are no size regulations
                    or time restrictions. This text change would remove noncommercial banner signs from
                    the exempt list so the same regulations will apply to a noncommercial message as a
                    commercial message. An establishment could only have one banner sign at a time,
                    whether it’s a commercial or noncommercial sign. Commissioner Lovato asked for
                    clarification on noncommercial signs.
11-4-110 outlines prohibited sign types, which includes things like feathers, pennant
signage and A- frames. One new sign type is being added to the list of prohibited
signs at the request of the Council. This will be signs containing content which is not
protected by the United States Constitution or the Utah Constitution. The content
would need to include extremely sexually explicit messages, something that incites
violence, or makes specific threats against an individual.

In Section 11-5-103, the first change requested is to strike the text that only one
temporary sign is allowed for an establishment. This is already being regulated under
each individual sign type. Staff is also proposing to strike the line that a building
facade can have a maximum of 30% temporary sign coverage at any time. This
provision is something that can be hard to regulate.

Brock went through the other proposed changes to 11-5-103, which are summarized
as follows:

(1)(d) Will clarify that that an establishment can have one road winding sign as well
as one other temporary sign type.

(2)(a) Removes vague language about banners needing to be on the face of the
building.

(2)(b). All establishments can have up to a 50 sq. ft. banner sign. The language about
larger buildings can have signage based on 10% of the building façade will be
removed.

(2)(d) A line is added to clarify that an establishment can only have one banner sign at
a time.

(3)(d) A line is added to clarify that an establishment can only have one banner sign at
a time.

(7)(f) Adds a line that establishments may have one inflatable sign in addition to a
different temporary sign type.

(10)(a) Strikes the agricultural and residential zone language so that temporary
noncommercial signs have the same size regulations, regardless of zoning on the
property.

(11)(c) Changes formatting to be consistent with other sign types.

(12)(d) Adds a line that establishments may have one vehicle sign in addition to a
different temporary sign type.

(13)(a) Adds language that window signage can only cover a maximum of 50% of the
first story face of a building.

(13)(b) Adds language that window signage shall be located on the first story of the
building.
ZT-7-2024         Petition by WEST VALLEY CITY requesting an amendment to Section 7-5-101 of
                  the zoning ordinance to allow zone change applications to the R-1-7, R-1-8, R-1-10,
                  and R-1-12 zones for properties that meet certain conditions. (Staff – Steve Pastorik
                  at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve P. presented. Back in 2015, the Council put in place restrictions on
                  being able to request rezoning in the R-1-7 through the R-1-12 zones if the property
                  was 2 acres or larger; that the property is not eligible to be rezoned to one of those
                  zones. There is currently a property on the agenda the City would like to rezone to R-
                  1-8, but because it's over 2 acres we can't. The property is located on 4000 West, an
                  older mobile home park, and is zoned R-4, which we don't have much of, but that
                  zone allows for duplexes, 4-plexes and apartments, and given what's around it we feel
                  that doesn’t make sense there. So, we'd like to rezone the property to R-1-8 to match
                  what's around the property. The conditions proposed would be that the property is
                  developed as residential, has a density of at least 2 dwelling units/acre, has access and
                  frontage on a street with a planned right-of-way width of at least 66 feet, and the
                  property doesn't have off-site improvements of curb, gutter and sidewalk. This
                  amendment gives the option of rezoning in these very limited circumstances.

                  There were no questions.

GPZ-3-2024   Petition by HOME BY C.W. URBAN requesting a General Plan change from
             Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to Small Lot Residential (4 to 7 units/acre)
             and a zone change from A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½ acre) to R-1-8 (Single
             Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 square feet). The property is
             located at 5990 West 3500 South on 1.59 acres for the zone change and 3.08 acres for
             the General Plan change. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or
             steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve P. presented a request to change the General Plan and zoning for the property at
                  5990 West 3500 South to change a portion of the parcel from A to R-1-8, amending
                  the City's General Plan to go to a small lot residential designation, and proposing a
                  PUD with some smaller lots in the 5,000 square feet plus range. In the latest concept
                  there would be 16 lots. Commissioner Lovato asked for clarification on garage sizes
                  in multi-family homes compared to single-family homes. Steve will find out.


GPZ-4-2024        Petition by EDGE HOMES requesting a General Plan change from General
                  Commercial to Medium Density Residential (7 to 12 units/acre) and a zone change
                  from C-2 (General Commercial) to RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential). The
                  property is located at 3688 South Redwood Road on 26.26 acres. (Staff – Steve
                  Pastorik at 801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:       Steve P. presented the plan change at the property well-known as the Redwood Drive-
                  in Theater located at 3688 South Redwood Road. There are 9 parcels altogether,
                  totaling just over 26 acres. This has long been used, well before the City's
                  incorporation, for a drive-in theater, and a swap-meet on the weekends. The zoning
                  right now is C-2 over all the property. The General Plan is general commercial, and
                  the request is to rezone the property to the RM Zone and a General Plan designation
                  of medium density residential. The proposal from Edge Homes is to do 3 product
                  types: 40 three-story condos, 250 townhomes, and 18 single-family homes, for a total
                  of 308 units. The density works out to be about 11.7 units per acre, which is a typical
                  density you would see for a townhome development.

                  Steve brought up the hundreds of public comments he has received already, so a lot of
                  in-person comments are to be expected at the Public Hearing. He also anticipates the
                  possibility the Commission will table this petition until the June 26th Public Hearing,
                  but Steve will not be present and suggests it be tabled even further until the July 5th
                  Public Hearing. Comments were made that the property owner has decided to sell this
                  property and not West Valley City. This is not City owned property and the
                  Commission is just responding to an application.

                  Commissioner McEwen has concerns about the number of homes per acre, traffic, and
                  widening of 1950 West. Commissioners Lovato and McEwen are not fans of the
                  condos facing Redwood Road because they look like apartments.
