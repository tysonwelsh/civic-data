1.         26-278      City Council Street Intersection Tour

                       Stop 1: Roundabout at 2300 E Bengal Blvd. Cottonwood Heights, UT
                       (Approximately 4:35 - 4:50 PM)
                       Stop 2: Signaled intersection at 9400 S Sandy Parkway, Sandy, UT (This
                       stop is time permitting)

                       Residents interested in attending the tour can meet the council at the tour
                       stops.
                       Council participated in a tour coordinated by the Public Works Department and visited the
                       roundabout located at 2300 Bengal Blvd in Cottonwood Heights. The purpose of the tour
                       was to observe a successful roundabout. During the visit, the Public Works staff reviewed
                       with the Council the design, operation, and benefits of the roundabout to better
                       understand its potential application in Sandy City. Based on this review, there is a
                       possibility that a similar roundabout could be considered for the intersection of 110th
                       South and 10th East near Alta High School.
