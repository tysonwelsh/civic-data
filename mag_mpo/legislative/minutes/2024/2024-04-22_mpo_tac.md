MPO Technical Advisory Committee MEETING MINUTES April 22, 2024 | 9:00 am ATTENDEES ABSENCES Ryan Robinson, Alpine Ben Hunter, American Fork Chandler Goodwin, Cedar Hills Christopher Trusty, Eagle Mountain Brandon Larsen, Eagle Mountain Andy Spencer, Highland Brad Kenison, Lehi Kim Struthers, Lehi Mike West, Lehi Noah Gordon, Lindon Rob Hunter, Mapleton Taggart Bowen, Orem John Dorny, Orem Robert Mills, Payson Jill Spencer, Payson Aaron Wilson, Pleasant Grove Neal Winterton, Pleasant Grove Britton Tveten, Pleasant Grove Brian Torgersen, Provo Vern Kessler, Provo Matt Marziale, Salem Bradey Wilde, Salem Jon Lundell, Santaquin Jason Bond, Santaquin Jeremy Lapin, Saratoga Springs Jered Johnson, Spanish Fork Dillon Muirbrook, Spanish Fork Jeff Anderson, Springville Eric Rasband, UDOT - Region 3 Alma Haskell, UTA Nick Wilcox, UTA Richard Nielson, Utah County Naseen Ghandour, Vineyard DISCUSSION & AGENDA ITEMS Public Comment Chair Jill Spencer opened the meeting at 9:00 am ACTION: Approve MPO TAC Minutes from February 26, 2024 Rob Hunter moved to approve the minutes from February 26, 2024. Brad Kennison seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. TIP Project Scoring Bob Allen covered the scoring criteria with the MPO TAC by providing a list of the different categories and how many points each category carries. Shawn Eliot stated the points are based on the adopted policy. He then went over the Active Transportation list explaining why the projects were ranked in the order listed on the MPO TECH Score sheet. Shawn Eliot explained the Provo Airport project is a regional project and recommended it be approved. He then covered the Road Concepts and their scoring. MPO TAC members voted, (one per member jurisdiction) on each project using information from concept reports, MAG Staff Technical Score, and the information presented in the meeting. ACTION: TIP Modification A. Spanish Fork - 2000 E US6 Connection Bob Allen stated in 2020 the funding was approved for the 2000 East US6 Connection in Spanish Fork City. The project was completed and included a roundabout. Due to inflation and the cost of the project, Spanish Fork City is requesting additional funding. MAG Staff is asking for the MPO TAC to recommend approval to the MPO Board to act upon the additional funding approval only. ChrisTrusty moved to recommend that $155,904 be added to the combined Spanish Fork US6 Connection Project. BradKennison seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. B. Spanish Fork - Spanish Fork Pkwy RR Crossing Bob Allen stated this is a second project that was awarded in 2020 to Spanish Fork City. This project has been completed, but came in over budget due to different obstacles. Some of the Page | 2 obstacles included mass grading, underground stream, railroad crossing inflation, storm drain lines, re-establishment of vegetation, and general inflation. MAG Staff is asking the MPO TAC to recommend approval to the MPO Board to act upon the additional funding approval only. AaronWilson moved to recommend that $1,630,021 be added to the combined Spanish Fork US6 Connection Project. JasonBond seconded the motion, and the motion passed all in favor. Public Participation Plan/Title VI Update Kendall Willardson stated a Public Participation Plan (PPP) is a Federal requirement that the MPO must adopt when interacting with the public. The PPP components must include:● How the Public Interacts with the MPO and our Core products,● How the public interacts with Decision Makers,● What stakeholders are involved, and● Facilitate Public Feedback in Public Participation Plan and Process. The last plan was adopted in May 2019, and needs to be revised. Kendall Willardson explained the Title VI Plan and the Federal REquirement that must be adopted by all departments of MAG. This plan is related to discrimination and has four components that include: Page | 3 ● MAG Structure and Procedures to Protect Title VI Groups● Identify Protected Groups through Data Analysis● Limited English Proficiency● How MAG addresses Title VI Complaints The last plan was adopted in May 2019, and needs to be revised. The goal is to have these two plans adopted by November 1, 2024. There is a timeline that includes public outreach, coordination within departments of MAG, a draft development document, public comment of the draft document, and the final adoption. CONCLUSION Chair Jill Spencer reminded the Committee that the next meeting will be held on May 20, 2024. Bob Allen provided a preliminary ranking of the TIP Project Scoring.
