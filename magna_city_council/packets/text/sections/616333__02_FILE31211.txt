                                       PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant, Cris Kohles, is requesting a waiver from the required roadway improvements which include curb,
gutter and sidewalk along this property frontage at 2360 South 7200 West.




                          SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The subject property is located along 7200 West, just off Highway U201. The property across 7200 West to the
east is in West Valley City’s jurisdiction.
        Request: Exception Request                                     File #: 31211




                                     PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
Although this property is on the north side of U201 Highway in the industrial area of Magna, Staff believes that
these required roadway improvements should be installed to start the process of getting appropriate roadway
improvements.

This area is part of the proposed Utah Inland Port Authority dry port; therefore, Staff believes that roadways
improvements will be a necessity for this area as the inland port is developed.

County Engineering is recommending that a 10-year delay agreement be approved for the installation of curb,
gutter and sidewalk improvements (Recommendation is attached).

                              PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION

The MSD Planning staff recommends that the Magna Metro Township Planning Commission forward a negative
recommendation to the Magna Metro Mayor (or his designee) indicating that the Exception Request for
Roadway Improvements be denied, and that the Roadway Improvements be installed as required.
