Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1709

          On March 24, 2026, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1709
          Amending the official land use map of Draper City for approximately 2.03
          acres of property from Residential Medium Density to Residential High
          Density, located at approximately 791 East 12200 South within Draper City,
          otherwise known as Fratto Farms Land Use Map Amendment. The
          complete ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and
          available online at https://www.draperutah.gov/

Published this 25th day of March, 2026.
