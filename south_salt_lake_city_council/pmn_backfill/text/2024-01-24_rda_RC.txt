New Business

   1. Redevelopment Agency Training
      Community and Economic Development Director, Jonathan Weidenhamer, introduced CEO of
      Energy Finance & Government Consulting, Cody Deeter, who would be presenting information
      to the Board and explain what a CRA and a RDA are, how they work, and what the uses of tax
      increments are.

       Mr. Deeter first talked about what an RDA (Redevelopment Agency) is and how it is a separate
       legal entity that the Council Members are Board Members of. Primarily, the agency is used as an
       economic development tool to encourage development in the City. The primary tool by which
       the RDA operates by is the creation of a project area, known as a CRA (Community
       Reinvestment Area), in which taxes can be used for a specific purpose.

       As this was wholly an informational presentation there was no need for the Board to take any
       action on the matter.
