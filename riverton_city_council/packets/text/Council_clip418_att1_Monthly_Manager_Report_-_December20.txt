                    Riverton Police Department Monthly Manager Report

                                             December

We have continued to battle COVID in our ranks through the month of December and it has
challenged our manpower as we have been required to quarantine some of our members to prevent
mass spread.

We are continuing to see higher than normal numbers of vehicle burglaries in our city and an
associated dramatic increase in fraud as perpetrators are stealing credit cards from vehicles and
immediately utilizing the cards to commit fraud before the victim even realizes the card is missing.
It seems the suspects have realized victims are immediately canceling cards and they are making a
concerted effort to quickly “cash in” before they are rendered useless. This new strategy accounts
for a majority of the fraud cases reported during the month.

Additionally, we have had several of our citizens fall victim to a variety of phone scams which
encourage them to purchase gift cards and provide the number and pin of the card over the phone.
Or, we had one victim sell a trailer and the buyer sent her a check for much more than the asking
price, then asked the victim to refund the excess amount. Of course, the original check ended up
having no money associated with the account.

Finally, we are extremely thankful Officer Snell quickly returned to work with no significant
injuries after his patrol vehicle was struck by an impaired driver on New Year’s Eve. This event
just emphasizes the danger our officers face on a nightly basis as they patrol the streets while the
rest of us sleep.
