Re: Fox Landing Ph. 3 – Final Subdivision Plat Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Final Subdivision Plat for approximately 15.6 acres located at approximately 11622 South Rolands Dr. The property is currently zoned R3 (Single-family Residential, 13,000 square foot lot minimum) and RA1 (Residential Agricultural, 40,000 square foot lot minimum) . The applicant is requesting that a Final Subdivision Plat  be approved to allow for the development of the currently vacant site as 35 additional residential lots within the Fox Landing Subdivision. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.  The final plat is in conformity with the requirements of this title, other applicable ordinances, and reasonable conditions have been placed on the approval.
2.  The proposed development conforms to the general aesthetic and physical development of the area.
3.  The public services in the area are adequate to support the subject development. ATTACHMENTS: Description Upload Date Type PC Staff Report 8/19/2020 Cover Memo
