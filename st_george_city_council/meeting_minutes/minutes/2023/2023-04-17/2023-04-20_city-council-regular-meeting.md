CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a. Consider approval to award bid to Whitaker Construction Company, Inc.
        for the South Taxiway Connector and Apron Construction.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: Inquiry #23-114 - Construct South
          Apron and Taxiway Connector Project was submitted for bid on February 21,
          2023. The bids were due on March 29, 2023 at 2:00pm. A Pre-Bid Meeting was
          held on March 14, 2023. Seven (7) bids were received and opened on March 29,
          2023. Based upon the bid tabulation, Whitaker Construction Company, Inc., is

          the low bidder in the amount of $4,955,335. Staff recommends awarding the bid
          to Whitaker Construction Company, Inc., in the amount of $4,955,335.

      b. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with FIF St.
         George, LLC (dba InfoWest) to provide fiberoptic connectivity to seven
         (7) traffic signals.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The City of St. George and UDOT partner
          with local fiberoptic communication companies to provide connectivity to traffic
          signals. This project will provide better and more reliable communication to
          several locations throughout the city. Staff recommends approval of the
          agreement in the amount of $54,833.20.

      c. Consider approval of a Professional Services Agreement with Horrocks
         Engineers for the design of Foremaster Drive culvert replacement.

          BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This contract is for the design of box
          culvers on Foremaster Drive. The contract includes utility coordination and
          preparing plans for advertisement. Staff recommends approval of the agreement
          in the amount of $183,834.

      Link to presentation from City Manager John Willis, including a note that the dollar
