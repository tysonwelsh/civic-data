       B.       Public Meeting: Somerset Ridge 2nd Amended Subdivision Plat Request
                (Administrative Action)
                On the request of Draper City, for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment to create
                five (5) parcels on approximately 1.3 acres of City-owned land located at
                approximately 12747 South Moose Hollow Drive and 12743 South Costanza Way.
                Known as Application No. 2025-0202-SUB. Staff Contact: Todd Taylor, 801-576-
                6510, todd.taylor@draperutah.gov.

City Planner, Todd Taylor, presented the Staff Report and displayed the Vicinity, Aerial, Land Use,
and Zoning Maps. The subject property is designated Residential Low-Medium Density and zoned
RA2. The property includes an existing detention basin and private road. The proposed Subdivision
Plat Amendment indicates that the subject property will be divided into five separate parcels. Mr.
Taylor noted that a Development Agreement was approved for the subject property. A recommended
requirement for approval was that the non-exclusive access and right-of-way easements be recorded
before the recordation of the amended Subdivision Plat.

Motion: Commissioner Shirey moved to APPROVE the Somerset Ridge Subdivision 2nd
Amended Plat, as requested by Draper City, Application No. 2025-0202-SUB, based on the
following Findings for Approval and subject to the requirements listed in the Staff Report dated
September 8, 2025, as follows.

Findings for Approval:

       1.       That there is good cause for the amendment; and no public street, right-of-way,
                or municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.

Second: Commissioner Green seconded the motion.

Vote on Motion: 5-to-0 in favor.
