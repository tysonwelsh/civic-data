     MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING (ELECTRONIC) OF THE WEST JORDAN
     PLANNING AND ZONING COMMISSION HELD REMOTELY ON JANUARY 5, 2021

PRESENT:      Trish Hatch, Matt Quinney, Corbin England, Kent Shelton, Jay Thomas, Ammon Allen,
              and Pamela Bloom.

STAFF:        Larry Gardner, Scott Langford, Duncan Murray, Julie Davis Paul Brockbank

OTHERS:       Mayor Burton, Tangee Sloan

**********************************************************************************
Matt Quinney read the statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-207(4).

The briefing meeting was called to order. The agenda was reviewed. An update was given on the
status of certain projects and proposed text amendments.

**********************************************************************************
The regular meeting was called to order at 6:00 p.m.

Matt Quinney read the statement regarding Utah Code 52-4-207(4).

1.     Oath of Office for Returning Commissioners

Tangee Sloan administered the Oath of Office to returning Commissioners Quinney and England.
Mayor Burton welcomed them and thanked them for their continued service.

**********************************************************************************
2.    Election of Chair and Vice Chair for 2021

MOTION:       Matt Quinney moved to nominate Trish Hatch as Chair. The nomination was
              seconded by Pamela Bloom and passed 7-0 in favor.

MOTION:       Trish Hatch moved to nominate Matt Quinney as Vice Chair. The nomination was
              seconded by Kent Shelton and passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
3.    Appointment of Two Commissioners to the Design Review Committee for 2021

MOTION:       Matt Quinney moved to appoint Ammon Allen and Pamela Bloom to the Design
              Review Committee as discussed in the pre-meeting. The motion was seconded by
              Ammon Allen and passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
4.    Appointment of One Commissioner to serve on the CDBG/HOME Committee for 2021

Trish Hatch said she would like to continue serving on the CDBG/HOME Committee.

MOTION:       Kent Shelton nominated Trish Hatch to serve on the CDBG/HOME Committee.
              The motion was seconded by Pamela Bloom and passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
5.    Approve Minutes from December 15, 2020

MOTION:        Matt Quinney moved to approve the minutes from December 15, 2020. The motion
               was seconded by Ammon Allen and passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
6.    Text Amendment – Mining Extraction Overlay; Continued from 12-15-20; Amend the
      2009 City Code Section 13-6 adding Article L Surface Mining Extraction Overlay Zone;
      City-wide applicability; City of West Jordan (applicant) [#22824]

Larry Gardner said that new information received indicates that there may be a need for this ordinance.

MOTION:        Corbin England moved to continue the Text Amendment for Mining Extraction
               Overlay to a date uncertain. The motion was seconded by Matt Quinney and
               passed 7-0 in favor.

**********************************************************************************
7.    Text Amendment – PCH Zone; Continued from 12-15-20; Amend the 2009 City Code
      adding 13-5 Article L “Planned Community Hillside (PCH) Zone”; City-wide
      applicability; City of West Jordan (applicant) [#19432]

There was a discussion regarding the schedule for this item.

MOTION:        Corbin England moved to continue the Text Amendment for the PCH Zone to the
               January 19, 2021 meeting. The motion was seconded by Matt Quinney and passed
               7-0 in favor.

MOTION:        Jay Thomas moved to adjourn.

The meeting adjourned at 6:11 p.m.
