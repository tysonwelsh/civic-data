A RESOLUTION OF THE METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL ADOPTING THE
TENTATIVE BUDGET FOR THE 2019 FISCAL YEAR AND SETTING A PUBLIC

RECITALS

A. WHEREAS, a tentative budget has been prepared budget officer for the Township for the

ensuing fiscal 2019 period and filed with the Township Council; and
B. WHEREAS, the Township Council has reviewed the tentative budget as required by law and

now wishes to set a public hearing on the tentative budget prior to considering it for adoption

as a final budget.

RESOLUTION

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, by the Kearns Metro Township Council that
it does hereby:

1. Adopt the attached Exhibit A as the tentative budget for the 2019 fiscal year together
with all supporting schedules to be made available as a public record in the office of the
Township Clerk, available for public inspection for a period of at least 10 days prior to the
adoption of a final budget, as hereinafter provided.

2. That the Township does hereby establish that on the \ = day of \ NC wing i
2018, at the hour of }4 _ o’clock, a public hearing shall be held to consider its adoption and
does hereby order that notice of the public hearing be published at least seven days prior to the
hearing in at least one issue of a newspaper of general circulation published in the Salt Lake
County; on the Utah Public Notice Website created in Section 63F-1-701; and on the home page
of the website, either in full or as a link, of the metro township, if the metro township has a
publicly viewable website, until the hearing takes place.

3. This resolution shall take effect upon passage.
