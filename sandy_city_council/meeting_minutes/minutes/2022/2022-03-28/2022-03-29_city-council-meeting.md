center.

4. 22-084 Council discussion on the future of the Alta Canyon Sports Center (ACSC)
and the alternatives proposed in the ACSC Feasibility Analysis
Attachments: Memo_3-29-22.pdf
ACSC Feasibility Analysis Presentation 10-26-21.pdf
Council Member Zach Robinson introduced Brian Parker with AECOM who presented
information and proposed concept options for the Alta Canyon Recreation Center
redesign. Mr. Parker presented the design options and reviewed the financial feasibility
related to the concept options. Council discussion followed. Members expressed their
support for options B and D. The public engagement and outreach process was also
discussed by the Council and Administration. Council made a motion directing staff on
the next steps moving forward.
Following the vote on the motion, Council convened a short recess at 7:39 pm.
Council reconvened at 7:46 pm and moved to Item 5 on the Agenda.
A motion was made by Ryan Mecham, seconded by Scott Earl to direct staff to
move forward in researching and implementing a public outreach process for
concept options B and D proposed at this evening's presentation by AECom...The
motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.
5. 22-085 Public Utilities Department providing the Council with a review of the
recommended water conservation rate structure
Attachments: Memorandum _PUAB.pdf
Rate Structure Presentation.pdf
Tom Ward, Public Utilities Director, reviewed with the Council the recommended water
conservation rate structure. Sandy City currently has a tiered rate structure in place
which is required by the state of Utah. The rate structure is under evaluation. He provided
information on the implementation schedule, measurement of progress and progress
report.
Mr. Ward introduced Keith Larson who reviewed a tiered rate structure that would support
and incentivize water conservation. He provided an overview of how water rates work. He
spoke about base rates, volume rates and about the feedback from the PUAB Advisory
Board and the public. Council questions and comments occurred throughout the
presentation. Mr. Larson reviewed the tiered rates recommended by the Public Utilities
department which takes into account the public feedback they received.
6. 22-081 Community Development Department requesting the Council have an
affordable housing discussion
Attachments: Presentation.pptx
Mr. Jake Warner with the Community Development Department presented information to
the Council about affordable housing. Per the Utah Foundation, affordable housing is the
most critical issue facing Utah. Mr. Warner provided an overview of median and moderate
income, affordable housing costs, residential building permits, and the housing gap
existing in Utah. Council comments and questions followed. Mr. Warner reviewed next
steps for the Council and he thanked the Council for their support.
