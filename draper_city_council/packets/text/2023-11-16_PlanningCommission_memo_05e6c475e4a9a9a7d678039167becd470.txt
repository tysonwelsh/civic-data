Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for
approximately 0.35 acres located on the cul-de-sac at the termination of Blue Heron Drive at
approximately 11841 S. Blue Heron Drive. The property is currently zoned R3 (Single Family
Residential 13,000 ft2 minimum lot size). The applicant is requesting that a Home Occupation
Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for a home office for a landscaping business with
work vehicles and equipment parked outside the home to operate from the home.



The findings for approval are as follows:

  1. Reasonable mitigation measures can be placed on the conditional use to alleviate anticipated
      detrimental effects.

The findings for denial are as follows:

  1. Reasonable mitigation measures cannot be placed on the conditional use to alleviate
      anticipated detrimental effects.
