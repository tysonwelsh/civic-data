NEW BUSINESS

       1. Meeting Schedule Update Discussion – City Code 2.08.050. Council Chair
          Bynum explained that the Council changed their rules so they only have to
          have one city council meeting a month. The City Code doesn’t align with that
          change. The discussion tonight is to see if the Council wants to change the
          municipal code to match the council rules.

          Mayor Wood said staff just wants the Council to understand that state code
          only requires them to meet once a month. Sometimes in December there is not
          a need for two meetings. If they change the code they could still hold a second
          meeting if it is needed.

          Council Member Siwik believes there is some business they can do in
          December and he asked where some items stood currently.

          Council Member Bynum explained that this change will be used only if there
          is nothing to bring forward in a city council meeting so they won’t be required
          to hold a meeting just because they have to. They changed it in the rules but
          didn’t realize there was something in the city code as well.

          Council Member Siwik said he is in favor of bringing the code in alignment
          with the council rules.

          Council Member Pinkney asked if this was only impacting the month of
          December.

          Council Chair Bynum said it makes it possible that in any month they don’t
          have business they have the option to just hold one meeting that month, which
          aligns with state code.

          Council Member Mila agreed with making the change but doesn’t want to
          make a habit of only holding one meeting a month.
