6.b   Ordinance #1660 amending Title 6-4 of the Draper City Municipal Code
      pertaining to alcoholic beverage licenses - Hospitality Amenity Licenses
      Business License Official Travis DeJong stated that the proposed amendment
      to Title 6-4 would define Hospitality Amenity License as a license granted to a
      hotel that permits the storage, sale, furnishing, and consumption of alcoholic
      products on the licensed premises for hospitality social events. Mr. DeJong
      explained the proposed scope of the license, and showed differences between
      Hospitality Amenity and Single Event Licenses.

      Mr. DeJong said the proposed amendment would also add On-Premises Beer
      Retailer to the list of on-premises licenses that could be transferred from one
      owner to another without City Council approval. He said additional changes
      would fix various grammatical or numbering errors.

      Tanner S. Lenart with Christensen & Jensen said she represented several
      hotels interested in having the additional license type. She said one benefit of

7.b   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1661 amending the text of Titles 6 and 9 of the
      Draper City Municipal Code related to seasonal farm stands, otherwise known
      as the City Initiated Seasonal Farm Stand Text Amendment
      Planner Todd Taylor said the proposed amendments were in response to a
      request from the City Council to add seasonal farm stand use to City Code. He
      said seasonal farm stand would be added as an accessory use to agricultural
      and residential-agricultural zoning districts. Proposed amendments would
      also make minor changes to other temporary uses. He defined a Seasonal
      Farm Stand as a booth, stall, or other area where seasonal produce, cut
      flowers, bedding plants, or similar items produced or grown on the premise
      were sold to the general public. Mr. Taylor explained proposed seasonal farm
      stand standards, and said the Planning Commission forwarded a positive
      recommendation.

7.e   Public Hearing: Ordinance #1663 amending the official Zoning Map of Draper
      City for approximately 0.93 acres of property from RA1 (residential
      agricultural) to RA2 (residential agricultural) located at approximately 13232 S.
      300 E., otherwise known as the Whittington Zoning Map Amendment
      Planning Coordinator Paul Geilman explained the request to rezone the
      subject property from RA1 to RA2, and said the proposed zoning would
      conform with the current land use designation. Mr. Geilman said the main
      difference between the two zones related to minimum lot size, with 40,000
