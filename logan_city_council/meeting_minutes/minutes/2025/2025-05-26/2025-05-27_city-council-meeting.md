Logan Municipal Council                  Logan, Utah                     May 27, 2025

Minutes of the meeting of the Logan Municipal Council convened in a budget workshop
Tuesday, May 27, 2025, in the Logan Municipal Council Chambers located at 290 North
100 West, Logan, Utah 84321 at 5:30 pm. Logan Municipal Council Meetings are
televised live as a public service on Channel 17 and the City of Logan YouTube channel
at: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCFLPAOK5eawKS_RDBU0stRQ

Council Members present at the beginning of the meeting: Chair Jeannie F. Simmonds,
Vice Chair Mike Johnson, Councilmember Amy Z. Anderson, Councilmember Mark
Anderson, and Councilmember Ernesto López. Administration present: Mayor Holly H.
Daines, Finance Director Richard Anderson, and Deputy City Recorder Esli Morales.

Chair Simmonds welcomed those present. There were approximately 12 in attendance at
the beginning of the meeting.

General Q&A – Richard Anderson, Finance Director (1:12:20)
Finance Director Richard Anderson addressed the Council stating electric rates will be
adjusted after the budget.
Chair Simmonds asked if another electric rate study would occur.

Mr. Anderson explained the electric rate study was already conducted. The
recommendation was to increase rates in interval stages.

Chair Simmonds requested confirmation that the water rates will change per the
legislative directive regarding water rates being charged per tier(s).
Mr. Anderson confirmed the rates will change and be implemented once the legislative
determination is made. The change in rates will permit the City to charge higher rates for
high water usage. However, this has made it more difficult to define what is a
commercial unit.
Chair Simmonds requested clarification on why it is more difficult to define what a
commercial unit is.
Mr. Anderson responded the difficulty for a commercial unit is to determine what is such
as an office, retail, hotel, and so forth. Before it was easy because it was a flat charge per
customer. It is creating problems on the billing side and will need to be addressed.
Chair Simmonds inquired if the changes would be brought to the Council in portions or
as a whole.
Mr. Anderson answered the changes will be brought to the Council in parts as there are
multiple flat fees in different areas.
Chair Simmonds asked if the flat fee charge would be transferred instead to a per-use
charge.
Mr. Anderson replied that is what is likely to occur, the flat fee charge will be converted
to a per-use charge for water and electric, however, there are no sewer use meters. There
is a flow meter, and the sewer is calculated based on that number.
