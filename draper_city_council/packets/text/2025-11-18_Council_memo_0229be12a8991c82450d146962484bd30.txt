MEMO
To: City Council
From: Nicole Smedley, City Recorder
Date: 2025-11-18
Re: Approve Resolution #25-67


Comments:

The City Council, acting as the Municipal Board of Canvassers, is required under Utah
Code § 20A-4-301 to review and certify the results of municipal elections. The Salt Lake
County Clerk’s Office will provide the official results of the November 4, 2025 General
Municipal Election on November 18, 2025. Upon receipt, the results will be presented for
review and certification through adoption of Resolution No. 25-67, declaring the official
canvass of Draper City’s 2025 General Municipal Election.
