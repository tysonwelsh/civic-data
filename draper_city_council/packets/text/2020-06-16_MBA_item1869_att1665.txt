MINUTES OF THE DRAPER CITY MUNICIPAL BUILDING AUTHORITY MEETING
HELD ON TUESDAY, JUNE 2, 2020, IN THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS,
1020 EAST PIONEER ROAD, DRAPER, UTAH

PRESENT:            Board Chair Troy K. Walker, and Board Members Mike Green, Tasha
                    Lowery, Cal Roberts, and Marsha Vawdrey

EXCUSED:            Board Member Fred Lowry

STAFF PRESENT:      David Dobbins, City Manager; Mike Barker, City Attorney; Laura
                    Oscarson, City Recorder; Scott Cooley, City Engineer; John Eining, Police
                    Chief; Russ Fox, Assistant City Manager; Rhett Ogden, Recreation
                    Director; Clint Smith, Fire Chief; and Bob Wylie, Finance Director

Business Meeting

1.0   Call to Order: Board Chair, Troy K. Walker

2.0   Public Hearing: Municipal Building Authority Fiscal Year 2020-2021 Tentative
      Budget.

2.1   Bob Wylie, Finance Director, said that the tentative budget for the Municipal Building
      Authority was the same as the one presented on May 19, 2020. He said that it was a
      balanced budget. He said the one item was a debt service on the 2016 bond for the
      $229,230 budget. The budget had been available to the public through the Draper City
      website for the last two weeks.

2.2   Board Chair Walker opened the public hearing. Seeing no comments Board Chair
      Walker closed the public hearing.

2.3   Board Member Mike Green moved to accept the FY20-21 Tentative Budget. Board
      Member Marsha Vawdrey seconded the motion

2.4   A roll call vote was taken with Board Members Green, Lowery, Roberts, Vawdrey,
      and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.0   Adjourn to a Redevelopment Agency of Draper City Meeting.

3.1   Board Member Mike Green moved to adjourn to a Redevelopment Agency meeting.
      Board Member Tasha Lowery seconded the motion.

3.2   A roll call vote was taken with Boardmembers Green, Lowery, Roberts, Vawdrey,
      and Walker voting in favor. The motion passed unanimously.

3.3   The meeting adjourned at 7:52 p.m.
