                        ST. GEORGE CITY COUNCIL MINUTES
                                REGULAR MEETING
                            OCTOBER 22, 2020 4:00 P.M.
                             CITY COUNCIL CHAMBERS

PRESENT:
     Mayor Jon Pike
     Councilmember Jimmie Hughes
     Councilmember Bryan Smethurst
     Councilmember Gregg McArthur
     Councilmember Dannielle Larkin
     City Manager Adam Lenhard
     City Attorney Shawn Guzman
     City Recorder Christina Fernandez – via Zoom
     Deputy City Recorder Annette Hansen

EXCUSED:
     Councilmember Michele Randall

OPENING:
     Mayor Pike called the meeting to order and welcomed all in attendance. The
     invocation was offered by Russ Cashin with the Free Spirit Community and the
     Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag was led by Mayor Pike.

      City Treasurer Laura Woolsey recognized Customer Service Representative Beth
      D’Antonio and Police Officer Vincent Lusk for their outstanding personal actions that
      resulted in saving the life of a citizen.

      Officer Lusk and Ms. D’Antonio spoke a bit about the incident.

      Councilmember Hughes arrived.

      Councilmember Larkin read a proclamation proclaiming October 24, 2020 as Lights
      on Afterschool Day.

      Dawn Eide-Albrecht spoke about the program and thanked the Mayor and Council for
      the proclamation.

      Mayor Pike read a proclamation proclaiming the month of November as National
      American Indian Heritage Month.

      Judy Lehman thanked the Mayor and Council for recognizing National American
      Indian Heritage Month.

      Councilmember Hughes read a proclamation proclaiming Thursday, October 29, 2020
      as Carol Hollowell Day.

      Carol Hollowell commented it has been quite a ride; none of this would have
      happened without the City. She noted she was at a meeting regarding homelessness
      in Salt Lake City where St. George was the envy of the state. She and her staff will
      continue to make a difference.

       David Dangerfield with the Friends of Switchpoint Board stated it has been an honor
       to work with Carol. He thanked the Mayor and Council as well as the citizens for
       their support.

       Councilmember Hughes commented that the state of homelessness has forever
       changed because of Carol, her staff, and the board.

       Economic Development and Housing Director Shirlayne Quayle spoke about her
       experiences in working with Carol.

       Councilmember Larkin explained that in 2015, she thought about the idea of the
       Bicycle Collective. Carol was a big motivator in getting the Collective started.

CONSENT CALENDAR:
     a.   Consider approval of a Services Agreement with Barr Engineering Co.
          for a Phase II environmental investigation for the 116-176 W St.
          George Blvd Project.

       b.     Consider approval of Lease Agreement with the St. George Musical
              Theater for lease of portion of the St. George Opera House located at
              212 North Main Street.

       c.     Consider approval to award contract to CVE Technologies for a Wifi
              upgrade project.

       d.     Consider ratification of a change order for repair of the Storm Drain
              near Ramada Inn.

       MOTION:
           A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to approve the
                      consent calendar.
       SECOND:
           The motion was seconded by Councilmember Larkin
       VOTE:
           Mayor Pike called for a vote, as follows:

                      Councilmember Hughes – aye
                      Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                      Councilmember McArthur – aye
                      Councilmember Larkin – aye

                      The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.

REPORTS FROM MAYOR, COUNCILMEMBER, AND CITY MANAGER:
     No reports were given.

       The Councilmembers left the Council Chambers for the following:

SITE VISIT TO THE ST. GEORGE ANIMAL SHELTER:
      Animal Shelter staff led the Council on a tour of the facility.

SITE VISIT TO WEBB HILL:
      The Council toured Webb Hill and the developer and staff outlined the proposal for
      the Summit Estates subdivision.

      Staff led the Council on a tour of the Temple Springs Park.

SITE VISIT TO THE WATEWATER TREATMENT PLANT:
      Staff led the Council on a tour of the Wastewater Treatment Plant.

ADJOURN:
     MOTION:
         A motion was made by Councilmember Smethurst to adjourn.
     SECOND:
         The motion was seconded by Councilmember McArthur.
     VOTE:
         Mayor Pike called for a vote, as follows:

                     Councilmember Hughes – aye
                     Councilmember Smethurst – aye
                     Councilmember McArthur – aye
                     Councilmember Larkin – aye

                     The vote was unanimous and the motion carried.
