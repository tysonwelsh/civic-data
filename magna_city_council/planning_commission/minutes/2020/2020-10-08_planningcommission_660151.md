                                       LAND USE APPLICATION(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 6:35 p.m.
31237- (Continued from 09/10/2020) - Scott Braithwaite is requesting approval of a Conditional Use for
a Contractor Storage Yard. Acreage: 2.4 approximate acres. Location: 2350 South 7200 West. Zone: M-
1 (Manufacturing). Planner: Diana Martinez. (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on these applications prior to acting
on the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Cripps asked how wide the access road between the two properties. Ms. Martinez said
probably not more than 20 feet.

                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING OPENED

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Scott Braithwaite
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Braithwaite said the terms and conditions on 2330 property have no issue. Shipping
containers they never stack higher than two, no problem being a condition. Total quantity of 10-20, normally
be the max, do have inflow with containers, safe dry way to store materials.

Commissioner Cripps asked for a number, if put in restriction of 20 containers, is that acceptable. Mr.
Braithwaite said he understands concern, he does not know what business will do in time and hates to limit,
he cannot see going above 30. Commissioner Cripps said concern is he will not always be the owner of the
property. Commissioner Richards asked if they are all 40 feet. Mr. Braithwaite said have a mixture of 20
and 40.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public meeting, Commissioner Elieson seconded the motion.
Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present).

No one from the public present to speak.

Commissioner Elieson motioned to close the public meeting, Commissioner Collard seconded the motion.
Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present).


                              PUBLIC PORTION OF MEETING CLOSED

Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding water and sewer.

Motion: To approve application #31237 with staff recommendations and amending condition number 6 to
no more than 30 storage containers at any one time.
      Motion by: Commissioner Richards
      2nd by: Commissioner Elieson
      Vote: Commissioners voted unanimous in favor (of commissioners present)
