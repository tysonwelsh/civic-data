Nephi City Planning Commission Meeting
July 8, 2020

Chairman Greenhalgh opened the meeting at 7:00 p.m.

Opening Prayer offered by Brent Arns

Commission reviewed minutes from regular meeting held June 10, 2020. Minutes were accepted on a motion by Ann Peterson and a second by Brent Arns. Motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Die to Ride home occupation permit was the first topic on the agenda. The question of whether a home occupation permit was actually necessary was the context of the discussion. Two UTVs w/trailers will be rented online with pick up at the home. It was determined that a permit would be necessary as the record keeping and pick up occur in the home.

Gardner Village was discussed by the Commission. Water, sewer, electrical connections and fencing were reviewed.

Annexation Policy Plan-Growth Area
.Legislation passed in 2020 allows for an island of land to be annexed into a city as long as that annexation does not encroach on another city’s growth area. The purpose of island annexation is to provide municipal services to a parcel outside current city limits. Three business on 4 Mile Road have expressed interest in being annexed into the city:  Azomite, Utah Landscaping Rock and Christensen Ready Mix. Currently this area is not part of the Nephi City’s Growth Plan. Seth Atkinson, City Manager stated that the city was willing to provide the necessary services to these businesses. The businesses would be required to provide the necessary infrastructure to procure city services.
He also noted that Nephi City owns 74 acres by Owen’s Corning,  52 acres are actually usable land, due to the fact that I-15 dissects the property. After discussion Brent Arns motioned that all of the area be annexed into the city rather than the island with the 3 industries. Karl Brough seconded the motion. It passed unanimously.

Other Business included a discussion of the Temporary Land Use Code. Chairman Greenhalgh is currently drafting an ordinance for land use. Specifics such as definition of open space, clarification of bonus density as well as other necessary items will be included. Lisa Brough has been asked to create a timeline to be used as a guide in getting this work done.

Chairman Greenhalgh adjourned the meeting at 7:50 p.m.
