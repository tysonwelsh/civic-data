      2. AMENDMENTS TO TRAVEL POLICY 9.1.
            Lynn Taylor, of the Clerk/Auditor’s Office, noted that this item was in revision for over a year and included
            input from all county offices. He addressed Chair Jenkins’ question noting that advances are still possible,
            if there is a need, with the travel approving official’s approval in conjunction with the comptroller.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the amendments to Travel Policy 9.1; Chair Jenkins seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      3. CONTRACT WITH GEOSTRATA FOR GEOTECHNICAL ENGINEERING AND GEOLOGIC SERVICES FOR 782 286
         FRANKLIN STREET.
            Braden Felix, of County Engineering, stated that there used to be a house some years ago that may have
            burned down on this property, which the county now owns due to a tax sale. A next door neighbor, Mr.
            Ramirez (at 282 Franklin Street), is claiming that because the burned home’s foundation is failing that the
            soil is being pulled from his house and that his foundation is failing and has openly threatened a lawsuit.
            Commissioner Harvey asked if the county was setting a precedent and Mr. Felix said that this item was
            under the direction of the County Attorney’s Office. Sean Wilkinson, County Community Development
            Director, noted that the county received a request from someone interested in purchasing the property in
            2018 and sent notices to adjacent property owners. Mr. Ramirez had said that there were cracks in his
            foundation somehow because of the county’s property and was threatening to have the county pay for
            damage to his property. Mr. Wilkinson said that this action is a cost benefit analysis, and that it makes
            sense to make sure the county is on solid ground; the county wants to verify the facts.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with GeoStrata for geotechnical engineering and
         geologic services for 782 Franklin Street and to make sure that the fees up to $2,000 are added to the taxes
         of that property before it is sold, and anything above/beyond that to be charged to the person who
         previously held the property; Chair Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
H. PUBLIC HEARING.
    1.

         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    2. PUBLIC HEARING TO CONSIDER AND TAKE ACTION ON A REQUEST TO AMEND THE WEBER COUNTY
       ZONING MAP BY REALIGNING THE O-1 AND RE-15 BOUNDARY ALONG THE WESTERN EDGE OF THE
       HIGHLANDS AT WOLF CREEK PHASES ONE AND FIVE, AND TO APPROVE A REZONING ORDINANCE.
         Felix Lleverino, of the County Planning Division, showed an area map and presented this staff-driven
         request to clean up discrepancies between the official zoning map and the subdivision’s outer boundary.
         No new density is anticipated as a result. Earlier this year the Ogden Valley Planning Commission
         forwarded a positive recommendation to the County Commission and county staff also recommends this
         zoning boundary realignment based on compliance with the General Plan. Mr. Lleverino addressed Chair
         Jenkins’s question noting that an error occurred from two separate documents that did not come together.
    3. Public Comments: Chair Jenkins invited public comments and none were offered.
    4.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to adjourn the public hearing and reconvene the public meeting; Chair
         Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
    5. ACTION ON PUBLIC MEETING:
       H.2.-AMENDING THE WEBER COUNTY ZONING MAP, REALIGNING A ZONING BOUNDARY ON THE WEST
       SIDE OF THE HIGHLANDS AT WOLF CREEK, PHASES ONE AND FIVE - ORDINANCE 2019-3

         Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Ordinance 2019-3 amending the Weber County Zoning Map to
         realign a zoning boundary on the west side of The Highlands at Wolf Creek, Phases one and five; Chair
         Jenkins seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
