## 4. Structured-data invariants

- **Max named voters on any motion = 5; 0 motions exceed 5** → confirms the Chair/Mayor-votes
  5-person body. **0 members vote twice on one motion.** No Aye+Nay contradiction pairs.
- **Vote vocabulary:** `Aye` 148 · `Nay` 24 · `Abstain` 12 (=184). No `Absent`/`Recuse`/`Excused`
  values — a recording ceiling (absences are prose-only). In bounds.
- **Capture-rate:** named rolls are 5/5 in 2026 (full roll call) and 1 dissenter in 2020–2022 (by
  source design — majority unnamed). No systematic OCR name-loss (the 12 OCR files yielded the
  correct tally-only / named-abstain rows).
- **Dates** all parse, fall in range, match filenames; no future dates.
- **The 12 zero-motion minutes** were each opened and confirmed genuine no-action sessions
  (work/continued/adjourn) except the one canvass certification (FIX-2).

## 6. Completeness vs external reality

- **Meetings/year** (2018–2026: 12,14,13,13,16,15,14,14,6) — consistent with a ~monthly Thursday
  cadence + mid-month specials; no unexplained dip. 2026 is partial (through May). **2017 is
  agenda-only** (5 meetings logged in `minutes_unrecovered.csv`) — the council was seated Jan 2017
  but minutes were first published 2018.
- **Elections vs outside sources:** 2025 Mayor matches the Salt Lake Tribune exactly (Perry 61.9% /
  Flint 38.1%); **2019 matches the independent Board-of-Canvassers minutes to the vote** (Little
  622 / Perry 589 / Flint 559 / Cutler 532 / total 2,302); 2023 winners confirmed by KSL press
  (unofficial-night vs canvassed vote-count gap only). Water-district + 2015 ballot-question decoys
  correctly excluded. **2017 & 2021 = genuine no-council-election years** (at-large seats on the
  2019/2023/2025 cycle), not gaps.
