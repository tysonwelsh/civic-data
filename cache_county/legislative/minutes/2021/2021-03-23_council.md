MINUTES
                                                        CACHE COUNTY COUNCIL
                       March 23, 2021 at 5:00 p.m. - Cache County Chamber at 199 North Main, Logan, Utah.
In accordance with the requirements of Utah Code Annotated Section 52-4-203, the County Clerk records in the minutes the names of all persons
who appear and speak at a County Council meeting and the substance “in brief” of their comments. Such statements may include opinion or
purported facts. The County does not verify the accuracy or truth of any statement but includes it as part of the record pursuant to State law.




MEMBERS PRESENT:           Chair Gina H. Worthen; Vice Chair Barbara Tidwell; Council Members Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup,
                           David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
MEMBERS EXCUSED:
STAFF PRESENT:             County Executive David Zook, Deputy Attorney John Luthy, County Clerk Jess Bradfield, Deputy
                           Clerk Bryson Behm, Economic Development Director Shawn Milne, Chief Deputy Auditor Dianna
                           Schaeffer, and Development Director Chris Harrild
OTHER ATTENDANCE:          Paul Berntson, Brian Abbott, Jordan Matthis, Lloyd Berentzen, Brenda Meikle, Keith Meikle, Levi
                           Spears, John Drew, Issa Hamud

1. Call to Order – Gina H. Worthen
2. Opening Remarks and Pledge of Allegiance – Karl Ward
3. Review and Approval of Agenda ATTACHMENT 1
         Action: Motion made by Barbara Tidwell to approve the agenda with Board of Equalization matters to be moved
         between items 6 and 7 on the agenda. Seconded by Paul Borup.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 6 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 1 Paul Borup
4. Review and Approval of Minutes (4:09)
         Action: Motion made by Gordon Zilles to approve the minutes. Seconded by David Erickson.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
         Action: Motion made by Gordon Zilles to reconsider and approve the minutes as submitted. Seconded by Karl
         Ward.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
5. Minutes Follow-up
6. Report of the County Executive: David Zook (5:01) ATTACHMENT 2
      a. Appointments: Appointments to the RAPZ committee will remain as-is. Appointments to the Solid Waste Advisory
         Board will be David Zook as the Chair with the six mayors throughout the County in addition to Jordan Mathis to
         the committee.
         Action: Motion made by Karl Ward to approve the appointments to RAPZ committee. Seconded by Gordon Zilles.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
         Action: Motion made by Gordon Zilles to approve the appointments to the Solid Waste Advisory Board. Seconded
         by Karl Ward.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
    b. Financial Reports: February 2021 Expense Report (Warrant Register)
7. Items of Special Interest - (22:44)
    a. Bear River Health Department Annual Report
         Discussion: James Mathis, Director of the Bear River Health Department (BRHD), introduces Lloyd Berentzen,
         former Director of the Bear River Health Department (BRHD). Council expresses support to Lloyd Berentzen for his
         many years of service. Jordan Mathis presents the annual report which highlights the accreditation process of the
         department, fiscal overview, and the goals of the BRHD.
8. Department or Committee Reports (49:14)
    a. Staff Recognition - Brain Abbott, Deputy Building Official
         Discussion: Mr. Abbott recently obtained the professional designation of Certified Building Official. He is the first
         inspector in the county to reach this accomplishment. Council applauds Brian Abbott for his accomplishment.
9. Board of Equalization Matters (10:30)
         Action: Motion to move into the Board of Equalization made by Karl Ward. Seconded by Barbara Tidwell.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
         Discussion: Dianna Shaeffer, Chief Deputy Auditor, is asked to clarify the difference between research and
         education and informs the Council that the Space Dynamics exemption has been questioned in the past. Paul Borup
         and Gordon Zilles have concerns about the Space Dynamics property tax exemption. David Zook expresses that
         Stevens-Henager is no longer operating.
         Action: Motion made by Gordon Zilles to approve all requests omitting Space Dynamics and Stevens-Henager.
         Seconded by Karl Ward.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
10. Public Hearings (52:30)
    a. Set Public Hearing for April 13, 2021 - Ordinance 2021-10 - Cache Valley Compost Review
         Discussion: Gina Worthen informs the Council that a time does not need to be set for the public hearing but due to
         the likelihood of public participation, a set time is recommended.
         Action: Motion made by Gordon Zilles to set a public hearing for April 13 at 6:00 p.m. Seconded by David Erickson.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
    b. Set Public Hearing for April 13, 2021 - Resolution 2021-05
         Action: Motion made by David Erickson to set a public hearing for April 13 at 5:45 p.m. Seconded by Barbara
         Tidwell.
         Motion passes.
         Aye: 7 Gina H. Worthen, Barbara Tidwell, Karl B. Ward, Paul R. Borup, David L. Erickson, Nolan Gunnell, Gordon A. Zilles
         Nay: 0
11. Pending Action (55:30)
    a. Ordinance 2021-05
         Discussion: John Luthy, Deputy County Attorney, provides Council with data of alcohol licenses currently issued by
         municipalities in Cache County. John Luthy provides an update on the text of the proposed ordinance and clarifies
         questions about the definition of advertising and definition of moral turpitude. Clarifies that multiple alcohol
         licenses may be issued by the state. The County Attorney’s office will continue to perform research and amend the
         proposed ordinance.
12. Initial Proposals for Consideration (2:12:40)
    a. Cache County Audit Report of 2021 Tax Sale
         Discussion: Jess Bradfield, County Clerk/Auditor, informs the Council that the 2021 Tax Sale has been canceled.
13. Other Business (2:14:10)
    a. Council Winter Social will be held on March 30, 2021 at 6:00 p.m. at Cafe Sabor
    b. Council Tour of Davis County Compost Facility will be held on March 31, 2021 at 12:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.
    c. 1st Annual Transportation Tradeshow and Training Conference will be held April 7-8, 2021 in Uintah County.
    d. UAC Management Conference will be held May 12-13, 2021 in the Davis Conference Center in Layton, Utah.
14. Council Member Reports (2:30:30)
         David Erickson – no report.
         Gordon Zilles – follow-up to the Ogden Facility tour.
         Karl Ward – left the meeting at 7:35 p.m.
         Barbara Tidwell – no report.
         Paul Borup – no report.
