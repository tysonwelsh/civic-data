            National Peace Officer Memorial Day and National Police Week

WHEREAS, in 1962 President Kennedy and by a joint resolution of Congress, established May
15th as National Peace Officers Memorial Day and the calendar week in which May 15th falls, as
National Police Week; and

WHEREAS, in honor of Peace Officers Memorial Day and National Police Week, we express our
gratitude to all law enforcement serving in this great country, including those serving in our
own Riverton Police Department; and

WHEREAS, in Riverton City we rely upon the 36 sworn officers to keep our neighborhoods
safe, to enforce laws, and to respond in times of crisis; and

WHEREAS, it is important we understand the vital role law enforcement has in keeping our
community safe and maintaining the peace; and

WHEREAS, we all are indebted to those who serve us and have served us. We recognize the
sacrifice law enforcement choose to make, and we remember those that have been killed in
the line of duty.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Trent Staggs, Mayor of Riverton City, do hereby proclaim May 15th, 2025,
as National Peace Officers Memorial Day and May 11th to May 17th, 2025, as National Police
Week in Riverton City, Utah.

GIVEN, under my hand and Seal of Riverton City on this 6th day of May 2025.
