   prayer.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1 Fraud Risk Assessment Recommendations-
    Dean Lundell, Finance Director, gave on update on the Fraud Risk Assessment. He stated that
    there are three things they would like to implement by the end of the year. One of those is an
    audit committee that would be made up of 5 individuals which would include two
    Councilmembers, two staff members and an outside representative. They also need employees
    to annually sign the code of ethics document that’s part of the employee policy manual. He
    said the last one is for the elected officials to do an online training. They are still looking at
    getting a fraud hotline established for employee and the public to contact to report concerns.

2.2 COVID-19 Update and Discussion
    Jason Walker, City Administrator, gave an update on the current pandemic. He stated that the
    Utah County Commission and Health Department will make recommendations to the State
    regarding which phase Utah County should be in. He said that if they decide to transition to
    yellow, then the City would need about 2 weeks to have everything in place to be able to
    operate safety in the yellow phase.

3. Agenda Questions
   Mayor Johnson reported that items 10 and 11 were withdrawn by the applicant.

4. Administrative Report

    Mr. Lundell reported that the sales tax numbers for March were not as bad as expected. He
    said that the city has a good diversity of revenue sources, but it is a fluid situation, so they
    aren’t sure what to expect for April.

    David Kitchen, Human Resources Director stated that they are looking at best practices on
    what employers can do to protect their employees when they return. We have good
    leadership and they are taking these precautions seriously.

    Mr. Kitchen stated that they had a recent opportunity to enroll in a new healthcare option for
    employees that would be in addition to the standard insurance offered. The program is called
    Nice Healthcare. It would allow employees home access to a Physician’s Assistant free of
    cost to the employee with the potential of future premium savings and better renewal rate for
    the City.

    There was discussion about the likeness of employees using the system. This program is new
    so there isn’t a lot of data on utilization. The Council was interested in exploring this option
    and asked that staff conduct a survey to see what the utilization might be from employees.
