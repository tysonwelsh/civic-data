                                           PUBLIC HEARING(S)
                                       Hearings began at – 6:45 p.m.
REZ2021-000363- Scooter Hammer is requesting a Rezone from A-20 (Agricultural) to M-2
(Manufacturing). Acreage: 17.56 acres. Location: 7581 West 2100 South. Planner: Diana Martinez
(Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission will act on the proposed public hearing item after taking comments from the
public during the public hearing. Public comments will be provided pursuant to the planning commission’s
rules of conduct, which are attached to the back of this agenda. Public comments will be limited to three
minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Speaker # 1: Applicant/Point of Contact – Whittaker Construction
Name: Scooter Hammer
Address: Not provided
Comments: Mr. Hammer said he also has Judd Hanson on the line. Proposed is going from A-20 to M-2
to do a construction office, similar to Morgan Asphalt building. Four bay shop to repair equipment and store
equipment.

Mr. Hanson said combination office and shop, 26,000 square foot, two story building on western half of the
property for storage and parking. Currently to develop on western portion of property. Office to support
truck operations.

Commissioner Cripps confirmed staff of 50 on the property. Mr. Hanson said to exclude number four as it
causes concern. Commissioner Cripps asked if it would preclude their use. Ms. Martinez said the business
would be for repair to construction equipment, no problem taking number four off, concern use wouldn’t
be housed in the building. If it is going to be contained in the building would be okay with taking the
prohibited use number four off. Mr. Hanson said interpreting the use would prohibit. Commissioner
Richards said just the repair wouldn’t prohibit, fine with rewording construction equipment excluded.
Commissioner Richards said Jacobson construction around the corner, exclusion was left in and don’t think
will affect construction or vehicle repair, but if became commercial repair facility would like to see it come
back. Mr. Hanson said privy to Jacobson property zoned if it doesn’t prohibit the same type of operations.
Commissioner Richards said as long as your office and your repair facility, doesn’t see a problem. Mr.
Hanson said builds bike lines and bridges. Ms. Martinez confirmed this is a recommendation and will go
to the Council for decision.

Commissioner Richards motioned to open the public hearing, Commissioner VanRoosendaal seconded that
motion.

Restaurant. Acreage: 0.99 approx. acres. Location: 3509 South 8150 West. Zone: C-2 (Commercial).
Planner: Diana Martinez (Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Richards asked about fencing to east side. Ms. Martinez said uses to the east are commercial,
southside would have fencing because it abuts residential use. Adding marking of employee parking and
trash pickup. Commissioner Richards asked if eliminates the driveway on to 3500 south. Ms. Martinez said
it will and only access point is 8150 west.

Speaker # 1: Applicant
Name: Ryan Talbott
Address: 8813 Penrose, Kansas
Comments: Mr. Talbott said he didn’t understand the fence to east.

intends to be made of. Mr. Talbott said anything to match the building.

(30804) CUP2020-000038- Truong Properties is requesting approval of a Shared Parking Schedule for the
Retail and Restaurant Uses (Mixed-Use project) and approval of two additional structures on the site plan
(pavilions) previously approved for the subject property. Acreage: 8.30 acres. Location: 8291-8315 West
3595 South. Zone: C-2 & R-M (Commercial and Medium Density Residential). Planner: Diana Martinez
(Motion/Voting)

The Planning Commission, in its discretion, may request public input on this application prior to acting on
the application. If public input is requested, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person.

Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planner Diana Martinez provided an analysis of the Staff
Report.

Commissioner Richards said he thought there was supposed to be an agreement with an entrance and exit
into Exodus. Ms. Martinez said worked out with county engineer, UDOT is requiring that connection and
planning commission will ask to make that connection at completion. Commissioner Richards asked for use
of pavilions. Ms. Martinez referred to the applicant, and would just be an open shelter, not a building.

Speaker # 1: Applicant Representative
Name: Sarah Thies
Address: 401 North 36, Seattle
Comments: Ms. Thies said pavilions intended for outdoor amenity. Understand from engineers, the
crosswalk suggested could pose safety hazards and recommend using sidewalk at the corner that already
exists.

Richards asked if tables or barbeques for the pavilions. This said there would be furniture accompanying
outdoor amenities. Cripps asked if playgrounds. Richards said there is already a playground. Martinez said
all amenities covered, just pavilions not covered at that time. Richards said doesn’t see a problem with
shared parking.
