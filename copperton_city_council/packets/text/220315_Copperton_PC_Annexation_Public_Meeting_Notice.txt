                                NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

                     COPPERTON METRO PLANNING COMMISSION

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN Pursuant to Utah Code Title 10, Chapter 2, Section 401.5, that the Copperton
Metro Township Planning Commission (the “Planning Commission”) will hold a Public Hearing on
March 15, 2022, beginning at 6:00 p.m at the Bingham Canyon Lions Club, 8725 West Hillcrest Street.
The purpose of the hearing is to receive public comments regarding:

   •   Annexation Policy Plan - Pursuant to State Code 10-2-401.5, the Planning Commission is holding
       a public hearing to allow members of the public to review and provide comment on Copperton's
       Draft Annexation Policy Plan. Prior to the opening of the public hearing, Long Range Planner,
       Kayla Mauldin, will give a brief presentation on the draft. Following the public hearing, the
       Planning Commission may direct staff to make revisions to the draft in response to public comment
       and may recommend adoption to the Council.

   •   Annexation Ordinance - Pursuant to State Code 10-9a-205, the Planning Commission is holding
       a public hearing to allow members of the public to review and provide comment on a drafted
       Annexation Ordinance for Copperton. This proposed Ordinance establishes the language and
       policies that would allow Copperton to consider an annexation petition or initiate an annexation in
       the future. Following the close of the public hearing, the Planning Commission may recommend
       adoption, recommend adoption with revisions, or not recommend the Ordinance to the Council.

You may also obtain copies of the Annexation Policy Plan and Annexation Ordinance. You may submit
written comments by contacting Wendy Gurr at wgurr@msd.utah.gov or (385) 468-6707. Copies of the
Annexation Policy Plan and Annexation Ordinance are also available on the Utah Public Notice Website.
Individuals needing special accommodations (including ancillary communicative aids and services) during
this public Hearing should contact Ms. Gurr three days before the public Hearing.
