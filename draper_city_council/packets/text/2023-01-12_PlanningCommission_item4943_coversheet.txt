Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Todd A. Draper
CC:  Date: January 12, 2023
Re: City Initiated Water Wise Landscaping and Site Amendments Ordinance - Discussion Item Only Comments: City Staff is requesting input from the Draper City Planning Commission regarding their direction and expectations for potential changes to  site development and design requirements including elements pertaining to water wise landscaping. Staff will use this direction and feedback in creating a proposal for changes to the Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) that will come back to the Planning Commission for Public Hearing at a future date.
This work session is intended to be conducted as an open public meeting, with comments from the public not being solicited at this time.
