Planning and Development

Moderate-Income Housing Plan

              Max Johnson, Planner, Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD),
reviewed the following Resolution amending the General Plan to include a Moderate-Income
Housing element:

RESOLUTION NO. 19-11-02                                           Date: November 19, 2019

       A RESOLUTION AMENDING THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP'S
       GENERAL PLAN TO INCLUDE A MODERATE-INCOME HOUSING
       ELEMENT

      WHEREAS, the Keams Metro Township ("Kearns") incorporated as a metro
township in January 2017; and

       WHEREAS, Keams' existing general plan, which was last updated in 2012, was
created prior to Keams' incorporation; and

       WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council ("Council") will be initiating an update
to the General Plan in December of 2019; and

      WHEREAS, during the 2019 General Session, the Utah Legislature passed S.B. 34,
which requires Kearns to amend its general plan to include a moderate-income housing
element ("Element") by December 1, 2019; and

     WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-404(5)(c) requires the Council adopt an
Element that provides a realistic opportunity to meet the need for additional moderate-

income housing within the next five years in Kearns; and

      WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-103(38) defines "moderate-income housing"
as housing occupied or reserved for occupancy by households with a gross household
income equal to or less than 80 percent of the median gross income for households of the
same size in the county in which the municipality is located; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-103(47) further states that the Element must
include:

          (a) an estimate of the existing supply of moderate-income housing located within
              the municipality;

          (b) an estimate of the need for moderate-income housing in the municipality for
              the next five years;

          (c) a survey of total residential land use;

          (d) an evaluation of how existing land uses and zones affect opportunities for
              moderate­ income housing; and

          (e) a description of the municipality's program to encourage an adequate supply
              of moderate-income housing; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-403(2)(b)(iii) specifies that the Element must
include a recommendation to implement three or more strategies out of a "menu" of 23
statutory options that are intended to provide for additional moderate-income housing
opportunities; and

       WHEREAS, the Council initiated the creation of the Element to comply with S.B. 34
as a component of its larger update to the general plan; and

       WHEREAS, the Keams Metro Township Planning Commission ("Planning
Commission") held a public hearing to take public comment on a draft Element on October
14, 2019 pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §10-9a-404; and

        WHEREAS, after receiving and carefully reviewing the input provided by the public
at the public hearing on the draft Element, the Planning Commission recommended that the
Council adopt an Element that selected the following goals from the menu of 23 options
found in Utah Code Ann. § 10-9a-403(2)(b)(iii):

      (a) Preserve current moderate-income housing (item L);

      (b) Create more Moderate-income Housing options (items E, F, & G); and

      (c) Support measures and efforts that contribute to neighborhood stabilization (items

          B & C).

      WHEREAS, the Council held a duly noticed public hearing regarding the proposed
Element on the 19th day of November 2019, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. §10-9a-204; and

       WHEREAS, the Council has received and carefully reviewed the final
recommendation from the Planning Commission regarding the proposed amendment, the
minutes from the Planning Commission meetings, the comments provided at both public
hearings, and considered all public input regarding the Element; and

      WHEREAS, the Council finds the Element contains all mandated elements of the
Utah State Code and meets Keams' current and future moderate-income housing needs;
and

     WHEREAS, the Council finds that amending the Keams General Plan to include the
Element is in the best interest of the health, safety, and welfare of Keams and its residents.

    NOW, THEREFORE: THE LEGISLATIVE BODY OF THE KEARNS METRO
TOWNSHIP RESOLVES AS FOLLOWS:

Section 1: the Council hereby amends its general plan to include the attached Element in
compliance with Utah Code Ann. §§ 10-9a-103(38), 10-9a-103(47), 10-9a-404(5)(c), 10-
9a- 403(2)(b)(ii), 10-9a-408, 10-9a-401(3)(b)(iii), and all other applicable laws and
regulations.

Section 2: A copy of the amended general plan with the Element is hereby ordered to be
filed with and retained by the Keams Metro Township Clerk, Recorder, and Auditor.

Section 3: An electronic copy of the Element is hereby ordered to be filed with both the
Wasatch Front Region Association of Governments and the Housing and Community
Development Division of the Utah Department of Workforce Services.

Section 4: Keams staff and the Planning Commission are instructed to incorporate the
Element within the larger contemplated update to the Keams general plan.

Section 5: If any of the sections, sentences, clauses or provisions of this general plan shall
for any reason be adjudged inapplicable or invalid by a court of competent jurisdiction, such
shall not affect or invalidate the remaining portion contained herein.

Section 6: This resolution shall become effective fifteen (15) days after it is passed and
upon promulgation through the Keams website or publication in a newspaper having
general circulation in Keams.

Section 7: The Council will take the following actions with respect to the Element in
compliance with Utah Code Ann. §10-9a-408:
