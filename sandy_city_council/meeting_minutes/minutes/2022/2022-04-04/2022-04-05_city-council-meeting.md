16. 22-098 City Council discussing redistricting, reviewing, and selecting alternative
council district maps
Attachments: Click here to eComment on this item
April 5 2022 Redistricting Alternative Maps.pdf
Vice Chair Robinson explained that the Council would be asked to select which
redistricting map options they wanted to put out to the public for feedback.
Dustin Fratto, Council Executive Director, presented several options of the redistricting
maps for Council review and consideration. He reviewed the current district map and
provided an overview of the two new version of the redistricting maps requested by the
Council. Council questions and comments followed. The City Attorney and Counsel
Attorney provided additional insight on the redistricting process. A lengthy Council
discussion followed. Council evaluated which maps to move forward with.
A motion was made by Scott Earl, seconded by Ryan Mecham, to move forward
with the map options selected this evening: 4-1b, 4-1c, 4-1d...The motion carried
by the following vote:
Yes: 5- Alison Stroud
Zach Robinson
Marci Houseman
Ryan Mecham
Scott Earl
No: 2- Cyndi Sharkey
Brooke D'Sousa
