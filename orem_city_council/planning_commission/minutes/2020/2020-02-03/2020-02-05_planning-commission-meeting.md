                 Planning Commission member, to offer the invocation.

Those present: Mickey Cochran, Carl Cook, Camille Jensen, Barry Roberts, Ross Spencer and Mike Staker,

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.1:

Agenda Item 3.1 is a request by Brubaker Construction, Inc. for the Planning Commission to approve the site plan
of RW Commercial Center located at 1280 South 1380 West in the CM Zone.

                                    Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to build an office warehouse
                                    project. The development will consist of twenty-two individual units in two
                                    buildings. The north building (Building A) will be 15,180 square feet with ten
                                    (10) units. The south building (Building B) will be 17,965 square feet with
                                    twelve (12) units. Both buildings will be thirty (30) feet in height. Each unit
                                    will have a front door access and an overhead door in the back.

                                    The building material will be several types of masonry block (standard, single
                                    scored, and split faced) in two color variations and with pre-finished
                                    galvanized metal cap flashing, gutters and downspouts.

The east end of both buildings will back up to Geneva Road, but the access to the commercial center will be from
1380 West.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission approve the site plan of RW Commercial Center located at 1280

Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.2:

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Kevin Scholz for the Planning Commission to approve the site plan of Stewart
Retail Addition located at 21 West University Parkway in the PD12 zone including the vacation of Lot 1, Barber
Bros Amended, Plat A and Final plat approval of Barber Bros, Plat C.

                                           Staff Presentation: The applicant is proposing to build a new building at
                                           21 West University Parkway. They will be modifying the existing
                                           building located at 41 West University Parkway by removing the front
                                           part of that building. This will create parking and an easement through the
                                           properties with the new building to be built up front next to University
                                           Parkway.

                                         The City Council approved a modification to the PD12 setback
                                         requirements on University Parkway and Main Street on November 19,
                                         2019. The original setback of forty (40) feet or equal to the height of the
building, whichever was greater was amended to change the setback requirements so buildings fronting University
Parkway or Main Street had a front setback of ten (10) feet. With the University Parkway widening project, this
approved setback matched the setback of the building directly to the east of the new building.

The new proposed building will potentially have five retail units and a continual flow easement through the property
between the buildings that is not currently there making access to the businesses easier. The proposed 24 foot
ingress and egress easement will be part of the new Barber Bros Subdivision, Plat C.

A PD12 zone requires any development shall be subject to review and approval by the City Council as outlined in
Section 22-11-24(I), Orem City Code.

Recommendation: The Development Review Committee determined this request complies with the Orem City
Code. Staff recommends the Planning Commission forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for the
site plan of Stewart Retail Addition located at 21 West University Parkway in the PD12 zone, and approve the
vacation of Lot 1, Barber Bros Amended, Plat A and Final plat approval of Barber Bros, Plat C.

Chair Cochran invited the applicant to come forward. Todd Dudley, Dudley & Associates, introduced himself and had
nothing to add.

Chair Cochran asked if the Planning Commission had any more questions for the applicant or staff. When none did,
he called for a motion on this item.

Planning Commission Action: Mr. Cook moved to forward a positive recommendation to the City Council for the
site plan of Stewart Retail Addition located at 21 West University Parkway in the PD12 zone, and approve the
vacation of Lot 1, Barber Bros Amended, Plat A and Final plat approval of Barber Bros, Plat C. Mr. Spencer
