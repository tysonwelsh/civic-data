                     Explanation for RAPZ Project Funding Continuation
                                     Logan City Projects


Project: West Willow Angels Landing Playground Surface Replacement

$59,000 Awarded in 2017

$44,016.13 Remaining

Surface replacement has been purchased and City crews will install the surface materials
(panels) at the completion of the playground equipment installation. Work is continuing as
weather permits. The surface replacement requires warmer temperatures for adhesive to
bond the surface panels to the concrete foundation. Completion is anticipated by September
30, 2021.
Progress of the work has been hampered in part by a backlog of maintenance projects, a
competitive labor market for summer help that limited our progression, effects of COVID-19
amongst staff, and delays in materials needed from suppliers impacted by the pandemic.
Continuation Request: Logan City is requesting an extension of seven (7) months to complete
the project.


Project: Canyon Road 500 East to 600 East Trail Construction
$75,000 Awarded in 2017
$14,212.77 Remaining ($9,376.60 encumbered)
Work is expected to be complete by May 31. Initial scope included a subsurface Geotech
survey of the hillside in 2018 where the trail was proposed at 600 East Canyon Road and
continue west to 500 East Canyon Road. The results of the survey indicated that significant
retaining wall construction would be needed to support the trail on the northside of Canyon
Road. Overarching purpose of trail connectivity is to reduce at-grade street crosses for trail
users, however it was determined that existing sidewalk on the south side of Canyon Road
would be used and improvements made at the intersection of 400 East 100 North Canyon Road
to improve the pedestrian trail crossing at Canyon Road. This involves sidewalk reconstruction
that connects to the Temple Trail from Canyon Road to the Boulevard and to 500 East and 600
East. Safety fencing for the trail has been installed. Pedestrian crossing beacons ($9,376.60)
have been ordered and the balance of RAPZ ($4,836.17) will be matched to City funds for the
remainder of the construction contract.
Continuation Request: Logan City is requesting an extension of three (3) months to complete
the project.
                            Explanation to Reduce RAPZ Funding
                       Kunzler Property Conservation Easement Project


Project: Kunzler Property Conservation Easement
$150,000 Awarded

$82,620 New adjusted amount from Utah Quality Growth Commission Hearing

The Utah Quality Growth Commission approved a request by the City of Logan to reduce the
original amount of easement and subsequent grant funding as a result of reconsideration by
the owner’s developer for the property from 73 acres to 47 acres. The reduction in acreage
also reduces the matching amount from RAPZ. This change in funding from RAPZ for the City’s
original application of $150,000 is now reduced to $82,620 and the same for the matching RAPZ
funds from the County’s application, again from $150,000 to $82,620. The easement
acquisition will still allow the Logan River Trail to be expanded onto the Kunzler property from
Trapper Park. The additional area will provide up to an additional 1.6 miles of public trail.


Project Request: Logan City is requesting to reduce amount of RAPZ funds to $82,620 for City
application and County application.
