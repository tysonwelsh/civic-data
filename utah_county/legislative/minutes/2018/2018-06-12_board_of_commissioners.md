5.     ADD ONE FULL-TIME CAREER SERVICE UTAH COUNTY EVENTS COORDINATOR
       POSITION (CLASS CODE TBD, ACCOUNT 100-41110) TO THE COMMISSION OFFICE
       STAFFING PLAN
(Track A, 16:20)
Commissioner Graves noted the county previously had an agreement in place for $2,000 per month, or $24,000
annually, to provide the same services for this new position in the Commission Office which equates approximately
$74,000 yearly for salary and benefits. He questioned why the county would not find a contractor. Commissioner
Ivie clarified the current contract addresses the annual Utah County Fair, but this additional employee will be
responsible for additional functions. Utah County Human Resources Director Lana Jensen confirmed the Career
Service position would be a merit employee. Utah County Clerk/Auditor Bryan E. Thompson pointed out this position
was not included in the 2018 Budget planning, and Commissioner Lee confirmed monies would be re-assigned from
events to fund the job. Commissioner Graves pointed out the scope of the job does not match the requirements, such
as only needing a high school diploma or equivalent but being familiar with all state codes. Commissioner Lee added
the position seems redundant in duties handled through the county Information Systems department or by the
Commission Office administrative assistants. The commissioners discussed the timing and necessity of the position.
Ms. Jensen mentioned there is a county applicant that may be transferring from another department, and while
Commissioner Ivie emphasized this is not an attempt to create a position for a specific individual, Commissioner
Graves noted there is potential for the situation to be misread as such.

                 Commissioner Lee made the motion to strike Regular Agenda Item No. 5 from
                 the calendar. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Graves and carried
