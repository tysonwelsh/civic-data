GENERAL PLAN/ZONE TEXT APPLICATIONS
GPZ-3-2022 Petition by MARIA ELENA DUBON requesting a General Plan change from
           Non-Retail Commercial to General Commercial and a zone change from RB
           (Residential Business) to C-2 (General Commercial). The property is located
           at 4543 and 4547 South 4000 West on 0.73 acres. (Staff – Steve Pastorik at
           801-963-3545 or steve.pastorik@wvc-ut.gov) Continued from the March 23,
           2022 public hearing.

Discussion:     Steve Pastorik informed the Commission that Brock Anderson and Jody
                Knapp are on vacation, and he will be presenting the three applications on
                today’s agenda.

                Steve reviewed this application, which was continued from the last public
                hearing. He explained currently, a daycare is located on the subject property.
                The applicant is proposing to demolish the existing structures and add a
                restaurant in its place.

                Steve reminded the Commission that Public Works stated that the curb cut for
                the property in question is too close to Sonic's. Therefore, this application
                was tabled to allow time for the applicant to speak with Sonic and Home
                Depot about sharing an access. He explained that Mrs. Dubon approached
                both businesses, and they were amenable to sharing the drive approach, but
                they would want to be compensated for it. Mrs. Dubon went back to her
                lender to ask for more money for the shared drive, and they denied her
                request. She explained that there would be just one access to the northeast of
                her restaurant without the extra funds. Upon receiving that news, her lender
                said they would not support this project with one drive approach. Steve stated
                that to get out of her contract with the seller, Mrs. Dubon would need the
                proposal denied, so she has requested for the Commission to deny her
                proposal. Steve noted that she would be sending a letter stating her request in
                time for next week's public hearing.

CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATIONS
C-4-2022    Petition by CIR ENGINEERING requesting a conditional use approval for a
            262-unit apartment development. The property is located at 2450 West 3800
            South on 3.65 acres and is zoned RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling
            Residential). (Staff – Brock Anderson at 801-963-3361 or
            brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov) Continued from the March 23, 2022 public
            hearing.

Discussion:     Steve reviewed the proposal for the Commission. He said that the applicant
                has requested to develop an apartment building adjacent to the Costco on 3800
                South. Steve mentioned that Brock had outlined several issues regarding this
                project during last week's public hearing. Brock provided a list of outstanding
                items, and Steve has been working with the applicant to resolve each item and
                meet all requirements. However, there is still work needing to be done.
                Hopefully, Steve said, there will be an updated plan submitted before next
                week's meeting.

C-7-2022        Petition by HOTEL 5600 requesting a conditional use for a 90 room Marriott
                Fairfield Inn & Suites Hotel. The property is located at 5626 W. Glen Eagles
                Dr. on 1.89 acres and is zoned C2 (General Commercial). (Staff – Jody
                Knapp at 801-963-3497 or jody.knapp@wvc-ut.gov)

Discussion:     Steve said that this proposal is for a Fairfield Inn & Suites Hotel, and the
                property was rezoned last year for this project. He presented the proposed site
                plan for the ninety-room, four-story hotel. As part of the project, Glen Eagles
                Drive would be completed along with the addition of curb, gutter, and
                sidewalk. He then reviewed the landscape plan and building elevations.
                Steve stated that Jody Knapp has been working on this proposal, and she did
                 not find any significant issues. However, she did provide the applicant with a
                 few comments regarding the landscaping.

                Commissioner Woodruff asked if they were proposing a full kitchen or just
                breakfast. Steve said the kitchen would only be for breakfast. He explained
                that the hotel would meet all requirements without a full kitchen.


There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 3:44 p.m.
