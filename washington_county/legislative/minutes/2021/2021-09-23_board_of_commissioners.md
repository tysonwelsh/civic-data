WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
WORK MEETING MINUTES
SEPTEMBER 23, 2021

The Work Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Almquist at 3:00 p.m. on September 23, 2021, in the Washington
County Administration Building Conference Room, 197 East Tabernacle, St. George,
Utah. Those in attendance were: Chairman Gil Almquist, Commissioner Adam Snow,
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Victoria Hales, Clerk/Auditor
Susan Lewis, and County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw.

Others in attendance included: Tom Kuhlmann, Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service
District and Kevin Tervort, Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service District

REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:

PRESENTATION ON PROPOSED HURRICANE VALLEY FIRE SPECIAL
SERVICE DISTRICT 2022 BUDGET REQUEST

Chairman Gil Almquist questioned why the growth and revenues associated with that
growth is not keeping up with the demands placed on Hurricane Valley Fire Special Service
District (HVFSSD). Secondly, he questioned why the reserves are not being used for these
demands.

Commissioner Adam Snow stated attorneys are looking at the codes regarding impact fees
and what they can and cannot be used for. HVFSSD Chief Tom Kuhlmann stated that he
recently received a letter from State Auditor John Dougall outlining exactly what impact
fees can be used for, and he would make a copy available to Washington County.
Commissioner Victor Iverson told Chief Kuhlmann that adding personnel is a top priority
and hire accordingly. Commissioner Snow requested that over the next year, HVFSSD
work with Suan Lewis so the County can better understand the needs. HVFSSD will meet
monthly with the Clerk/Auditor Department to discuss financials going forward through
July 2022. If at that time Truth in Taxation is deemed necessary, it will be discussed at that

point.

There being nothing further to come before the Commission, the meeting was adjourned at
4:55 p.m.
