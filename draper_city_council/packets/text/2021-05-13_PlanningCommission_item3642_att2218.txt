BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.38
acres located on the east side of Fort St., at approximately 12587 South Fort St. (Exhibit B).
The property is currently zoned TC. The applicant is requesting that a Plat Amendment be
approved to allow the property to be split into two lots.

The property currently contains a single-family residential home. The home was built in
1994 and is located on the east half of the property. The property is part of the Draperville
Plat recorded in 1873.

A Site Plan was obtained on April 19, 2007 in order to convert the home into an office
building. The applicant installed the parking lot and road way improvements associated
with the site plan, but never converted the home into an office use. It has remained a
single-family home, and that site plan is expired.

The applicant obtained a Site Plan and Conditional Use Permit to redevelop the entire
property as a mixed use building on September 14, 2017. That approval included ground
floor office space and nine upper level apartments. That site plan approval has since
expired and no improvements were done on the property.

The applicant obtained a Site Plan on February 25, 2021 in order to build a two-story office
building on the west half of the property. This plan retained the existing home on the east
half of the property. The current application will subdivide the property so the home and
office building are located on separate lots. The TC zone classifies single-family homes that
existed at the time of its adoption as legal nonconforming and allows them to stay.



ANALYSIS

General Plan and Zoning. The Land Use Map of the General Plan calls for the Town Center
land use designation for the subject property (Exhibit C). This category is characterized as
follows:




Kimball Home                                                       App. # SUBD-837-2019

The property has been assigned the TC zoning classification (Exhibit D). According to
Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the TC zone is to
“provide a method for implementing special provisions found within the general plan and the
Draper City conservation area master plan regarding the establishment and promotion of a
historic core area of the community characterized by stringent design standards for buildings,
public spaces, site design and landscaping with a harmonious mix of commercial uses, limited
high density residential uses and civic areas.” The TC zoning abuts the subject property on the
north, west and east, and RA2 (Residential Agricultural, 20,000 square foot lot minimum)
zone abuts on the south.

Subdivision Layout. The Plat Amendment will create two lots (Exhibit E). Lot 1 will be 0.17
acres in size and contain the proposed office building. Lot 2 will be 0.21 acres in size and
contain the existing house. The TC zone does not have a minimum lot size. The TC zone
allows a residential density on the property of 6 dwelling units per acre. At 0.21 acres, the
proposed Lot 2 is allowed one residential unit and the existing house is in conformance
with the allowed density allotment.

Frontage improvements already exist along both Fort St. and 12600 South, and no roadway
dedication or improvements are required with this plat.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request is found in Section 17-9-040 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat by
        signing an amended plat showing the vacation or amendment if the Land Use
        Authority finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no
        public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application
based on the findings listed below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff
report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include
the following conditions of approval:

        1.      That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
                Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the
                site and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
        2.      If the plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date of
                approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has
                not commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period may
                be extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6)
                month period for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing
                for an extension prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year.

The findings for approval as are follows:

        1.      There is good cause for the vacation or amendment; and no public street,
                right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.

The findings for denial as are follows:

        1.      There is not good cause for the vacation or amendment.
