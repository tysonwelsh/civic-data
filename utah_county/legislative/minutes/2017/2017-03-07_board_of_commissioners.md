2.     AUTHORIZE A CHANGE TO THE SHERIFF’S OFFICE STAFFING PLAN TO DELETE A FULL-
       TIME, CAREER SERVICE FINANCIAL ANALYST POSITION FROM THE SUPPORT SERVICES
       DIVISION; AND ADD A FULL-TIME, CAREER SERVICE FINANCE MANAGER POSITION
       [DELETING POSITION SUPPORT SRV 045, FINANCIAL ANALYST, JOB CODE 2014; AND
       ADDING JOB CODE 2410, FINANCE MANAGER-SHERIFF]
(Track A, 11:19)
Commissioner Lee noted he was only provided details on this agenda request in the last day, and suggested
discussion be continued for one week. Mike Forshee of the Utah County Sheriff’s Office came forward with Human
Resources Director Cammie Taylor to explain this change has been in the works for approximately five months.
Mr. Forshee noted neither he nor the County Sheriff will be present at the next Commission meeting, and asked the
Board to consider dating the change to apply within Pay Period 7 if discussion is delayed one week. Cammie Taylor
provided some context on the request, pointing out a promotion was given for this position approximately two years
ago wherein a male employee received an increase but two females were not equally promoted. Ms. Taylor noted
eagerness to resolve the issue as soon as possible, also requesting to advocate on behalf of the Sheriff’s Office
employee rather than put the employee in the uncomfortable position of requesting authorization while her
Department Head is unavailable. The Board indicated that would be appropriate.

            Commissioner Ivie made the motion to continue Regular Agenda Item No. 2,
            regarding a change to the Sheriff’s Office Staffing Plan, for one week to the
            March 14, 2017 meeting of the Utah County Board of Commissioners. The
            motion was seconded by Commissioner Graves and carried with the following
            vote:
