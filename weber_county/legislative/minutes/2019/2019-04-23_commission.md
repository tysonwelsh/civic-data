G. ACTION ITEMS:
      1. CONTRACT WITH WEBER STATE UNIVERSITY (WSU) FOR RESEARCH ON IMPACT OF GOVERNMENT
         SHUTDOWN.

            Holin Wilbanks, of County Economic Development, stated that this is a partnership between WSU, Ogden
            City, and Davis and Weber Counties. This contract is for Weber County’s participation. WSU is conducting
            a survey and comprehensive study on northern Utah’s impacts on the federal government’s last shutdown.
            WSU will provide a report to the partners. The counties are participating at $2,000 each and the other entities
            at $4,000 each. She addressed Commissioner Froerer’s questions stating that the study is on impacts to the
            work force and to local businesses in order to be better prepared in the event of future shutdowns.
            Commissioner Froerer moved to approve the contract with Weber State University for research on the impact
            of the government shutdown; Commissioner Harvey seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
      2. RESOLUTION APPOINTING MEMBERS TO THE REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY OF THE RIVERDALE CITY’S
         WEST BENCH PROJECT AREA TAXING ENTITY COMMITTEE – RESOLUTION 26-2019.
            Holin Wilbanks, of County Economic Development, stated that this project area committee was setup
            approximately 15 years ago, and the last meeting was in 2012. This existing entity reviews the tax increment
            specifically for this project area and because the City is asking for some adjustments (change to boundary area
            and to the infrastructure use of the dollars), committee members need to be re-instated to review this since it
            was the entity that put it into place. Christopher Crockett, Deputy County Attorney, noted that there is a
            statutory requirement for RDAs created prior to 2012 that has to follow this particular process.
            Commissioner Harvey moved to adopt Resolution 26-2019 appointing Roger Brunker and Holin Wilbanks
            with Scott Parke as an alternate to the Redevelopment Agency of the Riverdale City’s West Bench Project
            Area Taxing Entity Committee until their respective successors are appointed; Commissioner Froerer
            seconded.
            Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye

    5. CONTRACT WITH NUQUO GROUP, INC., FOR OPTIMIZATION OF COUNTY COPYING & PRINTING
       EQUIPMENT, SUPPLIES & SERVICES.

         Ricky Hatch, County Clerk/Auditor, noted that this is another example of the county’s continued improvement
         efforts. NuQuo, a Utah-based company, will inventory and analyze the county’s copying and printing
         equipment, supplies and services and provide recommendations for rebalancing usage loads, per page cost
         analysis, and the lease versus buy decisions to optimize those. If desired, NuQuo will help head up an RFP
         process to consolidate all the copiers and printing equipment under a single vendor and contract to obtain the
         absolute best pricing possible for the county as a whole.
         Commissioner Harvey moved to approve the contract with NuQuo Group, Inc., for optimization of county
         copying and printing equipment, supplies and services; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Commissioner Harvey – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye; Chair Jenkins – aye
