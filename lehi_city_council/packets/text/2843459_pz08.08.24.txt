41   3.1) Recommendation of CORE Architecture’s request for review of an amendment to the
42   Skye Area Plan adding design requirements for Civic Land Uses. (tabled from the July 25,
43   2024)

     Lehi City Planning Commission                   1                                  August 8, 2024
44
45   Applicant Curtis Miner presented some of the items that were discussed from the July 25
46   meeting.
47
48   The Commissioners expressed concerns about the vague language in the code regarding glazing
49   percentages, suggesting that clearer definitions or removing the requirement altogether would be
50   more effective. The discussion then covered setbacks, with Mr. Miner proposing the removal of
51   setback requirements to better fit site design needs, particularly for large civic buildings.
52   The Commissioners questioned the implications of this approach but appreciated the proposal.
53
54   They also discussed the importance of water-wise landscaping, which Mr. Miner agreed to
55   address by consulting with their landscape architects. Additionally, lighting standards were
56   reviewed, with concerns raised about consistency and intensity. The meeting concluded with a
57   proposal to separate civic sites into different categories to better accommodate their specific
58   requirements.
59
60           Motion: Commissioner Lockhart moved to approve CORE Architecture’s request for
61           review of an amendment to the Skye Area Plan adding design requirements for Civic
62           Land Uses including all findings stated in the report and all DRC comments.
63           Commissioner Lockhart added that the applicant should continue workshopping the
64           water-wise landscaping requirements prior to getting to council, with the intention of
65           adhering to as much of them as possible, and then to include layering in the C1 and the
66           C2 designation that was just mentioned, so that that secondary site will be held to the
67           standards we typically have, and if there were wanting to be exceptions to that, they
68           would need to follow the regular application process. Commissioner Eyre seconded.
69
70           Vote: Commissioner Jackson, yes; Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Eyre, yes;
71           Commissioner Lockhart, yes; Commissioner Roberts, yes. The motion passed
72           unanimously.
