18.135.090 Animals and fowl.
(1) No agricultural animals or fowl shall be kept within 40 feet of any residential dwelling. No structure for the
keeping of agricultural animals or fowl shall be constructed or maintained within 40 feet of a residential dwelling.

(1) This restriction shall not apply to chickens kept under RCC 18.30.020, 18.35.020, and 18.40.020, or as
described below.

(2) Residential Chickens in Single-Family Residential Zones and Special Districts. Chickens may be kept on
single-family lots in all single-family residential zones and in specific development districts, including Monarch
Meadows, Western Springs, and Canyon View Park, under the following conditions:

      (a) A maximum of six chickens, with no roosters allowed.

      (b) Lots must be a minimum of 10,000 square feet. The number of chickens allowed is based upon lot
      size, as follows:

                            Lot Size                            Maximum Number of Chickens Allowed

                        Less than 0.11 Acre                                         0
                  (Less than 5,000 Square Feet)
                      0.11 Acre to 0.18 Acre                                        4
                   (5,000 to 7.999 Square Feet)
                            0.18 Acre +                                             6
                 (8,000 Square Feet and Larger)


      (c) This section shall not be construed to allow pigeons, ducks, geese, or any other bird or fowl.

      (d) A structure for the keeping of residential chickens allowed under this section shall not be located
      within twenty-five (25) feet of any residential dwelling on an adjacent property.

      (e) This section shall not apply to chickens kept under RCC 18.15, 18.20. and 18.25.
