                       WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP


RESOLUTION NO.: 20-07-01                                        DATE: July 2, 2020


       A RESOLUTION OF THE WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
       COUNCIL ADOPTING THE GREATER SALT LAKE MUNICIPAL
       SERVICES DISTRICT EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN (EOP)
       AS WHITE CITY’S EMERGENCY OPERATIONS PLAN

      WHEREAS, an Emergency Operations Plan (“EOP”) can reduce the
vulnerability of citizens and community of the White City Metro Township (“White
City” or “Municipality”), a member-entity of the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District (“GSLMSD” or “MSD”), to loss of life, injury, damage and destruction of
property arising from natural, technological, or human-caused emergencies and
disasters or during hostile military or paramilitary actions; and

     WHEREAS, the MSD has developed and adopted an EOP to address the need of
the member-entities of the District whereby the member-entities can adopt the MSD’s
EOP as their own entity’s EOP, together with annexes, if any; and

      WHEREAS, an EOP will assist White City prepare for prompt and efficient
response, and recovery from, emergencies and disasters, including using all systems,
plans and resources necessary to preserve the health, safety and welfare of persons
affected by an emergency; and

      WHEREAS, an EOP will further help White City with recovering from
emergencies and disasters by providing for the rapid and orderly restoration and
rehabilitation of persons and property affected by emergencies; and

       WHEREAS, an EOP provides an emergency management system encompassing
all aspects of pre-emergency preparedness and post emergency response, recovery and
mitigation; and

      WHEREAS, White City desires to adopt the MSD’s EOP as the White City EOP
in the interest of public health, safety and general welfare.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Provision of emergency management services. The Greater Salt
 Lake Municipal Services District, through an Interlocal contract with the Unified Fire
 Authority, provides emergency management services to the member-entities of the
 MSD, which includes White City.

      Section 2. Adoption of the National Incident Management System
(NIMS). The White City Metro Township hereby adopts the National Incident
Management System (NIMS) as a framework to integrate and coordinate the emergency
response and recovery actions of all levels of government.

       Section 3. Adoption of an Emergency Operations Plan. The White City
 Metro Township Council hereby adopts the Emergency Operations Plan previously
 adopted by the MSD as the White City EOP and pursuant to the EOP Plan and
 documents received by the Council from the MSD.

        PASSED AND APPROVED:

      Passed and approved by the White City Metro Township Council, Salt Lake
County, Utah 2nd day of July, 2020.
