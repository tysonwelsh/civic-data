See FrontRunner South Presentation for detailed information FHWA/FTA MPO Certification Review Results Shawn Eliot, MAG Transportation Manager (26:36) On August 22-23, 2023, the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) and the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) conducted the certification review of the transportation planning process for the Provo-Orem urbanized area. FHWA and FTA are required to jointly review and evaluate the transportation planning process for each urbanized area with a population over 200,000 at least every four years to determine if the process meets the Federal planning requirements. MAG is the designated Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) for the Provo-Orem urbanized area in Utah County. UDOT is the responsible state transportation agency and UTA is the responsible public transportation operator. Current membership of the MAG MPO consists of elected oﬃcials and citizens from the political jurisdictions in Utah County. The study area includes all the populated regions of Utah County, with the City of Provo as the largest population center. The scope of the federal review of the metropolitan transportation planning process was comprehensive, covering the transportation planning process for the entire area. The federal review team’s work consisted of reviewing the products of the planning process, reviewing the ongoing oversight activities conducted by the FHWA and the FTA, and an onsite review. MPO Technical Advisory Committee January 8, 2024 Page 4 As a result of this Certification Review, the Provo-Orem metropolitan planning process performed principally by MAG, UDOT, and UTA is certified through November 1, 2027. The report outlined five areas of commendation, five areas where improvement is recommended, and four corrective actions. MPO staff will move forward to implement the recommendations, and corrective actions listed in the review report. See MAG Certification Review Final Report for details. An overview of the website was provided www.mountainland.org/community-profiles Unified Transportation Plan Update Shawn Eliot, MAG Transportation Manager (47:10) Mountainland MPO, WFRC, Cache MPO, Dixie MPO, UDOT), and UTA have finalized Utah's Unified Transportation Plan (UTP) 2023-2050. The Unified Plan incorporates projects, revenues, and costs of the MPO’s Regional Transportation Plans and UDOT’s rural transportation plan, all recently adopted. This nationally recognized process comprehensively includes all modes of transportation (roadway, transit, and active transportation). It collectively identifies new capacity needs to accommodate for growth in travel demand and the maintenance, preservation, and operation of the transportation system. It also includes a financial plan. The UTP identifies $153 billion (2023 dollars) of total transportation needs statewide between now and 2050, and is fiscally constrained to $113 billion of these needs, based on the revenues that are projected to be available during that period. The UTP is a shared vision for how transportation can support key quality of life outcomes through better mobility, good health, connected communities, and a strong economy. An updated interactive map is available on the plan website to review capacity projects. A user can view projects by Planning Area, Mode of transportation, Phase of time, UDOT Region, Senate District, and House District.
