SUMMARY AND BACKGROUND
This application is a request for approval of a Subdivision Plat Amendment for
approximately 0.92 acres located on the north side of Patterson Lane, at approximately
1571 E. Patterson Lane (Exhibits B and C). The property is currently zoned RA2. The
applicant is requesting to resize the storm drainage retention easements on two of the
three lots in the subdivision. The purpose for the change to the size of the storm drainage
retention easements is to accommodate the applicants desired house plans for
development of the lots.

The three-lot minor subdivision was approved administratively earlier this year. Both lots
that are part of this application are currently vacant. A single-family home built in 1989 is
located on the western most lot which is not part of this application.

According to DCMC Section 9-8-020 the purpose of the RA2 zoning classification is to
“foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and municipal
services; to generally preserve the character of the City’s semi-rural areas; and to promote
and preserve conditions favorable to large-lot family life, including the keeping of limited
numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be
detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and
industrial uses.”

Subdivision Plat. The applicant is requesting with this application to modify storm drainage
retention basin easements recorded on the original Patterson Subdivision plat (Exhibit F).
The storm drainage easements on the original plat recorded in April 2024, show the basins
at a size of 27 feet by 50 feet on each lot. The location of the basins in the northwest
corner of each lot makes it difficult for the desired house plans to meet required
development standards. The applicant is the current owner of both lots and has submitted
the plat amendment to resize the basins to 10 feet by 50 feet. The applicant has provided
a detail demonstrating that the smaller basin will contain the required amount of storm
water storage. The City Engineering Division has reviewed the proposed basin designs and
has determined that they will comply with the minimum requirements of the City.

Criteria For Approval. The grounds for review and potential approval of a subdivision plat
amendment request are found in Section 17-9-050 of the DCMC. This section depicts the
standard of review for such requests as:

        The Planning Commission may approve the vacation or amendment of a plat if the
        Planning Commission finds that there is good cause for the vacation or amendment,
        and no public street or municipal utility easement has been vacated or amended.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning
Commission review the request and approve the application based on the findings listed
below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff notes the applicant is
required to comply with the following requirements pertaining to the plat and
development of the subdivision:

1.       That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building,
         Planning, and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site
         and the construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
2.       That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the
         development of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
3.       If the subdivision plat amendment is not recorded within one (1) year from the date
         of approval or construction of the required landscaping and infrastructure has not
         commenced, such approval shall be null and void. This time period may be
         extended by the Planning Commission for up to one additional six (6) month period
         for good cause shown. The subdivider must petition in writing for an extension
         prior to the expiration of the original one (1) year. See DCMC Section 17-9-070.
