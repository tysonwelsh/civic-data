    MINUTES OF WEST VALLEY CITY PLANNING COMMISSION STUDY MEETING

PRESENTATIONS:


SD-1-2026      Petition by Vantrust Real Estate requesting consideration to
dedicate an extension of Stegner Way (5990 West). The property to be dedicated
is along the southern boundary of the parcel located at 5892 W Parkway
Boulevard. The property is zoned M (Manufacturing). (Staff - Brock Anderson at
801-963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).



Discussion: Brock presented the petition by Vantrust Real Estate requesting
consideration to dedicate an extension of Stegner Way (5990 West). Brock explained
that the new road will extend from Parkway Boulevard and will eventually connect into
the 5900 West project that the City will be doing later this year. The City project will
extend 5900 West from Beagley Ave to Parkway Ave. A small area of dedication for the
new road will be required from the charter school, and the applicant is working with the
school on that. The new road will be a 60 ft. right-of-way and will have a 5’ park strip and
a 5’ sidewalk on each side of the road.




SMI-2-2026 Petition by Jason Peterson, representing Legend Engineering,
requesting minor subdivision approval for the Rushton Farms Subdivision. The
subdivision is located at 5477 West 4100 South, consists of 5 commercial lots on
5.6 acres, and is zoned C-2 (General Commercial). (Staff - Brock Anderson at 801-
963-3361 or brock.anderson@wvc-ut.gov).



Discussion: Brock presented the petition by Jason Peterson, representing Legend
Engineering, requesting minor subdivision approval for the Rushton Farms Subdivision.
It will be a 5-lot commercial subdivision of property that was rezoned earlier this year.
Brock shared a layout of what the potential building layout will be once the property is
subdivided. He also mentioned that the Development Agreement requires that the
developer submit a traﬃc study to the City when they are ready to submit a Conditional
Use Application.

Commissioner Woodruff inquired if Granger Hunter had a problem getting to the back
lot. Brock responded that Public Works brought that up in the Technical Meeting. The
applicant will need to work with Granger Hunter regarding the water and sewer.

Steve brought up one other change. Originally when this went to the Planning
Commission there was only one development agreement item, a traﬃc study, but the
Council added a requirement for an 8-foot wall along the eastern side. Commissioner
Winters asked if it was along the whole eastern side. Steve replied that it was.

Brandon pointed out that there is no right of way on the south of the canal.



C-4-2026 Petition by Godfrey West Investors, LLC requesting conditional use approval
for a warehouse building. The property is located at 2466 South 5900 West on 16.54
acres and is zoned M (Manufacturing).
Discussion: Brock presented the petition by Godfrey West Investigators, LLC requesting
conditional use approval for a warehouse building Brock explained that this project
received conditional use approval in 2024, but the project was never built, so the
approval has expired. He described the proposal, which has not changed much from the
2024 request. The project will have access onto a new public road, which is an
extension of 2400 South.

After Brock spoke about wetland issues that would have to be addressed with the Army
Corps of Engineers Commissioner Winters commented that they seemed to have left
that area out. Brock responded they will have to work with Army Corp to mitigate some
of the wetlands.

Landscaping was discussed and Commissioner Winters pointed out that the business
will end up being 40 to 50 feet away from the road. Brock said yes, they won’t be right
on the road, but they won’t have the typical 20 feet of formal landscaped area.

Commissioner Winters brought up that when the area was ﬁrst expanded the
neighborhood that was already there was on a dead-end street, and the neighbors were
worried about having a ton of truck traﬃc down Beagley and he asked if they had had
any problems with that. Brock replied that he hadn’t heard of any. Brock also pointed
out that with the 5900 West extension going in that would keep a lot of people from
heading there.

Commissioner Winters asked if it would connect on the north end. Brock responded
that they can go north up to SR201 across 2400 South. Steve said that the new road
will allow people to get to Mountain View Corridor.

Commissioner Winters asked if you could get on SR201 north from 5900 West. Steve
replied that you need to use 5600 West to go north on SR201.

There being no further business with the West Valley City Planning Commission, the
meeting was adjourned at 3:50 PM.
