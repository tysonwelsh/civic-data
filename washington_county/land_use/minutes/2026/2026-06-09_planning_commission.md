1.   MISCELLANEOUS TITLE 9 (Public Hearing – Legislative): Review and hold a public hearing
     regarding proposed miscellaneous revisions to Title 9, Buildings, Land and Construction
     Regulations. Consider, discuss, and receive public input, and make recommendation to the
     County Commission. Proposed amendments to Title 9 include adding chapter 5, standards for
     earthen building, adobe, rammed earth, and compressed earth block construction, and other
     miscellaneous updates.

     Proposed amendments to Title 9 include adding chapter 5, standards for earthen building, adobe,
     rammed earth, and compressed earth block construction, and other miscellaneous updates.

     Mr. Scott Messel acknowledged that this item was tabled at the previous Planning Commission
     meeting and continued to today's meeting for further discussion. He explained that a public
     hearing was conducted during the previous meeting and, therefore, was not required again today.
     Mr. Messel then turned the time over to Mr. Elliott Taylor, County Engineer, to present
     information regarding earthen building materials and construction methods.



                                                  1
     Mr. Elliott Taylor explained that the proposed code is intended to serve as a performance-based
     standard, establishing minimum requirements rather than maximum limitations. He discussed
     the purpose and definition of the code, where it would be incorporated within the County Code,
     and the reasons the County is unable to adopt Appendix AU. Mr. Taylor also addressed topics
     including seismic stability, as well as the heritage and cultural significance of earthen
     construction methods.

     Mr. Taylor, the Planning Commission, and Mr. Messel engaged in a discussion regarding earthen
     building materials and construction standards. Topics discussed included the properties of
     earthen building materials, testing requirements and procedures, and the qualifications and
     expertise necessary for building officials and inspectors to effectively review and inspect such
     construction methods. Commissioners also expressed their appreciation for the guidance,
     information, and direction provided by Mr. Taylor throughout the presentation and discussion.

     Commissioner Mark Owens reminded the Commission that, at the previous Planning
     Commission meeting, Commissioner Brandon Anderson had been tasked with consulting with
     structural engineers regarding the proposed ordinance and obtaining their professional insight.
     Mr. Messel reported that staff had consulted with several structural engineers and that the intent
     of the assignment had been fulfilled.

     Mr. Bruce Wainwright, a structural engineer, approached the podium and expressed his
     professional opinion in support of incorporating earthen building materials into the County's
     ordinances. He spoke favorably of the proposed standards and their application within the
     building code.

         MOTION:
