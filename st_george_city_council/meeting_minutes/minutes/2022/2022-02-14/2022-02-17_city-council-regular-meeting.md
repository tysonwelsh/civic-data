CONSENT CALENDAR:
  a. Consider approval of a Stock Watering Agreement between the City and the
     Arnold Graff IRA.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This agreement would allow Mr. Graff the use
      of culinary water for stock watering on a parcel of land zoned for agricultural use at
      approximately 1880 South 3300 East. Additionally, the agreement would waive the
      water impact fees as long as the connection was used solely for the purposes of stock
      watering or agriculture. If it is determined that the connection is used for any other
      purpose, the connection will be terminated, and all water impact fees would need to be
      paid in order to reinstate the water service connection. Staff recommends approval.

   b. Consider approval of an Independent Contractor Agreement with Stanley
      Access Technologies, LLC to provide maintenance for the airport terminal
      sliding doors.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: This is a PSA for a maintenance agreement
      with Stanley Access Technology, the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) of the
      automatic sliding doors in the SGU terminal. The service received by the previous
      contractor for door maintenance has been unsatisfactory, and has cost the airport over
      $37,101 in the last eight years for the five automatic sliding doors; an average of
      $4,637.63 per year. In the last ten years the doors have had over 10 million operations
      per door. The annual cost of the new PSA will be set at $4,556.00 and includes
      unlimited service requests, two Planned Maintenance and Safety Inspections
      each year per door, and any parts or labor needed to keep our doors operational and
      compliant. With aging doors and given the amount spent to date on door maintenance,

PRELIMINARY PLAT:
     Consider approval of the preliminary plat for Desert Canyon Townhomes, a 20-
     lot residential subdivision.

      BACKGROUND and RECOMMENDATION: The proposed subdivision meets the
      requirements of the general plan for this property which is designated as RES
      (Residential) and the zoning which is PD-R (Planned Development Residential). The
      general plan designation for this property was approved with the overall master plan for
      Desert Color and a zone change was recently approved on this parcel by the City
      Council on November 11, 2021 with a change from R-1-10 to PD-R. At its meeting held
      on January 11, 2022, the Planning Commission recommended approval.
