    2. APPROVAL TO ADOPT THE TENTATIVE OPERATING AND CAPITAL BUDGET FOR WEBER
       COUNTY FOR THE 2024 CALENDAR YEAR AND TO SET A PUBLIC HEARING ON NOVEMBER
       28, 2023 AT 6:00 P.M. TO CONSIDER A WEBER COUNTY PROPERTY TAX INCREASE OF 7.25%
       OR $3,943,146 ($35.19 PER YEAR ON AN AVERAGE RESIDENCE) TO FUND THE INCREASED
       COST OF MAINTAINING EXISTING OPERATIONS WITHIN THE COUNTY.

         Scott Parke (Comptroller): As you are aware the budget process started in August with the County
         requesting each department’s proposed budget for the coming year. Through this process I can assure you
         Weber County’s commitment to financial stability is unwavering. We have not had to raise the general
         operating tax rate since 2016. But currently we are having challenges retaining essential services and
         attracting and retaining qualified staff. For example 22% of our frontline Sheriff positions are open and
         vacant; we can’t staff them. In addition we are experiencing inflationary pressures such as surge in materials
         and fuel prices, escalating utility costs, and three years in a row of double digit increases in our health
         insurance. All of these put a strain on our County budget. To address these issues, we first looked inward.
         We asked for our departments to look at where we could save money and they responded with nearly $2.5M
         in savings. However, that is not enough to cover the increased costs for existing services. We are proposing
         a modest tax increase of 7.25%. This will help us bridge the financial gap. This is the first time since 2016
         that we have had a general fund increase. There will be a public hearing on Tuesday November 28th at 6pm

    3. Public hearing to discuss amendments to the operating and capital budget of Weber County for the 2023
       calendar year.

         Scott Parke (Comptroller): Now we are talking about our existing 2023 budget. We have received
         additional grants from the Forest Service and the State of Utah that we want to spend. This requires your
         approval even though it does not take a draw on the fund balance. We have about $520,000 of additional
         revenue from Grants and settlements coming in of which we're asking to spend about $372,000 of new
         dollars. The rest of that money will help offset some existing expenses that are already in our budget. In
         addition to that, we've been successful at our conference center, our Golden Spike, and our Ice Sheet. Our
         concessions departments and our sales are actually exceeded our forecasts so have the cost of sales. So we're
         asking to amend the budget up to use that additional Revenue to cover the additional costs of providing those
         concessions so there's no additional draw to the fund balance from that. Then our transfer station volume has
         increased, we've had more garbage come in than we had expected and so more revenue has bene generated to
         cover the disposal costs. All those costs, the revenues and the expenses, are balanced so there's no draw from
         general fund. There are only two items that are actually being requested that will draw general fund dollars.
         The first one is public defender Capital defense and appeals. We've had more appeals than we had budgeted
         for originally. We don't really have control over the number of appeals and we have to fund them. We don't
         really have much of a choice; it's a state statute. We work well with the state to try to secure Grant funds to
         help cover those costs. But in this instance our appeals are just higher than we had. The second issue was
         some opportunities to do some improvements at the transfer station laying, some asphalt and some other
         various building improvements. As part of our capital projects plan, we had hoped to do these in the next
         few years but we were able to accelerate those and do them this year. Those are the two that are actually
         drawing funds that aren't covered with revenue but we have sufficient fund balance to cover both without any
         issues. With that, I present to you these changes for the 2023 operating and capital budgets.
