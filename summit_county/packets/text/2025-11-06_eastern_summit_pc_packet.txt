Background 
T
he Eastern Summit County Planning Commission originally approved a Conditional Use Permit 
in 2016 (Permit #16-277) to allow for processing of soil on the subject property (Exhibit B). The 
Conditional Use Permit approval allowed the applicant to “bring excavated material to the site 
from external sources, sift the material to remove rocks and then resell the material as top-soil.” 
The Conditional Use Permit approval set a ~15-acre physical limit to the area of 
operation/disturbance.  
T
he owner operates two companies from the site. Black Diamond Construction Materials LLC 
operates as a soil processing facility, including ex-out dirt, boulders, concrete, asphalt, and grub 
materials (trees, limbs, grass, brush, sod, other green waste). The concrete and asphalt are 
recycled. The applicant confirmed that they do not accept any other construction waste, 
processed wood, sheet rock, building materials, plastics, metals or other garbage. The applicant 
has also confirmed that they are not proposing to accept any hydro-vac trucks. 
P
atino Trucking and Excavating LLC operates an excavation company that specializes in 
residential and commercial excavation services, installation of water lines, and retaining wall 
installation.  
The site also includes several structures and storage areas which include;

Conditional Use Permit Proposal 
Applicant: Black Diamond Construction Materials, LLC 
Partner Companies: Patino Trucking and Excavating, LLC 
Owners: Rosalio Patino & Max Patino 
Introduction 
Black Diamond Construction Materials, LLC, in partnership with Patino Trucking and 
Excavating, LLC, respectfully submits this proposal for a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) to 
extend the use of a section of our property in Browns Canyon, Summit County. Our goal is to 
provide a safe, secure, and regulated parking facility for local companies who need space to park 
trucks, trailers, and equipment. NO fuel, oils or other contaminates will be permitted. 
The service will primarily benefit landscaping and construction companies throughout Summit 
County, particularly those located in smaller communities that lack sufficient space for large 
vehicle and equipment storage. 
Company Background 
Black Diamond Construction Materials, LLC 
Black Diamond Construction Materials currently operates as a recycled dirt yard in Browns 
Canyon. We accept construction haul-out materials including: 
• Ex-out dirt
• Boulders
• Concrete
• Asphalt
• Grub materials (trees, limbs, grass, brush, sod, other green waste)
We do not accept any construction waste, processed woods, sheet rock, building materials, 
plastics, metals or other garbage, including food waste.   
Concrete and asphalt are screened and crushed into reusable materials. 
Mulching is not proposed.

Soils imported are monitored by on site employees to ensure we are not taking in contaminated 
soils.  Each person entering our facility is required to check in with the office or loader operator 
and complete truck ticket.  That has the requirements of information needed.  All truck tickets 
are then entered on a daily spreadsheet and then invoices to the client and filed.   
Any dirt being brought from Park City Old Town area a specific address is required and then 
entered on the Park City Municipal soil ordinance page to see if it is in or out of the guidelines.  
We do not except any soils within the contaminate area.  A map of the area is posted in the office 
for all to see and information is given to clients about the area. 
Grub processing - larger logs and trees are cut into firewood for consumers to purchase.  Wood 
chips, leaves, sod, and smaller wood is mixed into our topsoil for screening. Any grubbing we 
cannot process will be burnt in winter months.  Late November though January.  Within the state 
and local guidelines.  Burning permits are obtained from the Summit County Fire Marshall, then 
clearing index is checked daily through the Utah Clean Air website.  If clearing index is over 500 
and winds speeds are less than 10 mph we will burn.  Our burn area is set up within a dirt pile 
cove.  The fire is then fed and monitored by employees to ensure fire is not to large or able to 
spread out of control.  Employees monitor the fire continuously throughout the time it is active 
then extinguished at the end of the day or if conditions change.  
Site Storage Explanation 
The Black Diamond Construction Materials yard is used for the storage of equipment, vehicles, 
and materials necessary to support the operations of both Black Diamond Construction Materials 
and Patino Trucking and Excavating. 
Items stored on-site include: 
- Dump trucks
- Pick ups
- Flatbed and enclosed trailers
- Job site tools and equipment
- Plastic sheeting, fencing, and other construction materials for project use
These materials are not available for retail sale to the public and are solely for company 
operations. 
No bulk fuel or oil is stored on-site.  Fuel is delivered in transfer tanks installed in company 
pick-up trucks operated by employees and owners.  These mobile tanks are used strictly to refuel 
equipment as needed. 
Additionally, a limited number of RV’s are stored on-site.  These RVs are personal property of 
the owners and not used for commercial purposes.  At times, they may be occupied for short 
periods (such as weekends) but otherwise remained parked for storage only.  When in use, they 
are removed from the property for camping or other personal activities.

This storage plan ensures the yard remains clean, safe, and compliant with Summit County

Power was recently installed on site with appropriate permits for the use of our winter storage

Patino Trucking and Excavating, LLC 
Patino Trucking and Excavating is an established excavation company within Summit County, 
specializing in residential and commercial excavation services, installation of water lines, 
retaining wall installation and related construction projects. 
Together, these partner companies are locally owned and operated by brothers Rosalio and Max 
Patino, both of whom are committed to serving Summit County with responsible business 
practices and community-oriented solutions. 
Proposed Conditional Use 
The proposed extension of property use will provide a designated equipment and vehicle 
parking facility. This service will: 
• Offer safe, organized, and monitored parking for trucks, trailers, and
construction/landscaping equipment.
• Reduce the burden on smaller communities by keeping large vehicles and equipment off
residential streets and neighborhoods.
• Maintain cleaner, safer county roadways by limiting roadside and overflow parking of
heavy equipment.
• Screened by a 6-foot berm or higher if the Planning Commission wishes.
The parking facility will be monitored through: 
• Security cameras
• Daily oversight by onsite employees
• Hours of operation will be the same as the soil recycling facility.
o 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday and 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Sundays,
which matches the Summit County Noise Ordinance (Ordinance 316-B) for
construction-related activities.
• Clear rules and regulations for all users, and a rental contract will be signed by both
companies.

Community Benefits 
The proposed parking facility will provide measurable benefits to Summit County, including: 
• Reduced neighborhood congestion: Allowing contractors and landscapers to store
equipment off residential streets.
• Enhanced safety: Limiting large vehicles from blocking visibility or creating hazards in
residential areas.
• Support for local businesses: Providing affordable and convenient parking for small
companies that lack storage facilities.
• Environmental responsibility: The project aligns with Black Diamond Construction
Materials’ mission to operate cleanly, safely, and with long-term sustainability in mind.
Conclusion 
Black Diamond Construction Materials and Patino Trucking and Excavating are dedicated to 
supporting the growth, safety, and cleanliness of Summit County. We believe that extending our 
property to provide a secure parking facility directly addresses a pressing community need; while 
enhancing the services we currently provide through our recycled dirt yard operations. 
We respectfully request approval of this Conditional Use Permit.

similar uses on adjacent parcels SS-67-11 & SS-67-6 in 2009.

7. “Rock quarries, gravel pits, and associated surface mining uses, including, but not limited to, filtering, 
sifting, and processing of soil” is listed as a Conditional Use in the AG-100 zoning district. 
8. As the use is moving from one parcel to another, there will be no immediate increase in impact to 
surrounding properties or neighbors. 
 
Conclusions of Law: 
 
1. The proposed dirt processing facility is consistent in scope to other operations on adjacent 
properties within the zone. 
2. The proposed facility is in general compliance with development standards as found in the Eastern 
Summit County Development Code.

5. The addition of this operation will not cause impact to existing agriculture operations; will not cause 
impacts or increase infrastructure needs for culinary water or wastewater systems.
