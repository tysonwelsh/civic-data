Council Member Business

                     Council Member Marci Houseman spoke about the Pace of Progress Master Plan 
                     Steering Committee and the subcommittees and appreciates all the communication and 
                     outreach associated with this committee. Residents are very engaged and positive about 
                     involvement with the Pace of Progress committees. 

                     Council Member Brooke D'Sousa spoke about the Sandy Youth Council's involvement 
                     with the Local Officials' Day and state-wide Youth Council event. The SYC participated in 
                     mock committee hearings and "planned" a city parcel. She thanked the Council and 
                     Administration for their on-going support of the SYC.

                     Council Member Zach Robinson encouraged the community to attend the Sandy Arts 
                     Guild production of Steele Magnolias at Mt. Jordan Middle School.

                     Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the WFWRD, Wasatch Front 
                     Waste and Recycling District. She spoke about the current legislative session and the 
                     time she and Council Member D'Sousa are devoting to watching current legislative 
                     issues. She also provided an update from the Pace of Progress town hall at the Sandy 
                     Senior Center.

                     Council Member Alison Stroud commented on the positive comments she has received 
                     on the new city video. She provided updates from the Sandy Senior Center, CDBG, and 
                     Historic Committees. She also spoke about some upgrades occurring in Historic Sandy. 
                     She also mentioned that the Council Of Governments has volunteer openings on its 
                     advisory board.

Mayor's Report

                     The Legislative update was provided by Evelyn Everton. Ms. Everton spoke about some 
                     the bills under review during the first week of the legislative session: housing, school 
                     resource officers, and water. She spoke about the potential impact of the proposed 
                     legislation to the City.

                     Kim Bell, Deputy Mayor, spoke about the Pace of Progress town halls. The Mayor and 
                     Administration enjoyed having lunch with the SYC at Local Officials' Day. The new city 
                     video has been very well received by the community.

CAO Report

                     Shane Pace, CAO, spoke about the process associated with the development and 
                     updates to the Master General Plan. There are 27 different subcommittees working on 
                     these revisions. The Community Development department is doing an incredible job. He 
                     read a note from the parks staff thanking the Administration and Council for providing 
                     gloves and socks for the crews.

Legislative Report

                     The legislative report was provided during the Mayor's report.
