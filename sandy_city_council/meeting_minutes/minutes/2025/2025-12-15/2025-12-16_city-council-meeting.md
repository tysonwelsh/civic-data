1.         25-376                 Building our Future: A Community Housing Workshop Series, Workshop
                                  Five - Prioritizing tools and tactics
                   Attachments:   November 18, 2025 Memo_Housing Workshop Series
                                  Best practices when developing incentives
                                  Tools, tactics, and incentives
                                  Housing Workshop 5 presentation

                                  Dustin Fratto, Council Director and Lynn Pace, City Attorney, led the workshop. This is
                                  the fifth housing workshop in a series of workshops the Council and Administration have
                                  participated in.

                                  Council reviewed the draft vision mission statement they proposed during the prior
                                  housing workshops: To ensure continued sustainability and to maintain the character of
                                  our city: Our goal is to encourage housing and housing affordability for residents across
                                  all stages of life. We want to encourage, allow and incentivise a larger variety of housing
                                  (including housing for seniors, first time home buyers, middle housing, and workforce
                                  housing), more housing (in the right locations) and we want the housing to be owner
                                  occupied.

                                  Council discussed "Where" or locations for affordable housing. They reviewed the Future
                                  Land Use Map preferred housing types, and comparative housing types offered in
                                  neighboring cities. They reviewed factors, conditions, regulations, and incentives to
                                  promote missing middle and affordable housing. Housing types reviewed included
                                  detached ADU's, Small lot single family homes, twin homes, duplexes, and
                                  mansion-style homes. Council discuss the "Who" related to people who need more
                                  affordable housing including first-time home buyers and seniors. Council discussed the
                                  "How" - what tools need to be in place to allow for more affordable housing. Council
                                  discussed permitted conditions and incentives. Council participated in an exercise to
                                  identify their priorities.

                                  The moderators then discussed next steps with the Council. Staff will draft a succinct
                                  final report outlining the results of the Housing Workshop Series, next steps, and a
                                  timeline. The final report will be provided to the Council, Mayor and Administration.

                                  Council convened a recess at 7:24 pm.
                                  Council reconvened at 7:32 in the Council Chambers and moved to General Citizen
                                  Comment.

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

                      Dustin Fratto, Council Director: This is the last council meeting of 2025. The Official Oath
                      of Office Ceremony for newly elected City Council Members will take place at Noon on
                      January 5th. January 6, 2026 will be our first Council meeting in the new year. He wished
                      everyone a happy and safe holiday and he expressed gratitude to outgoing Council
                      Members Zach Robinson and Ryan Mecham.

Council Member Business

                      Council Member Robinson provided feedback from the Sandy Ambassadors. The
                      Ambassadors would like more dialogue with the Council. He spoke about the Soap to
                      Hope Charity organization. It is a wonderful organization to support.

                      Council Member Alison Stroud provided an update on the Sandy Youth Council, Historic
                      Committee and the legislative breakfast.

                      Council Member Aaron Dekeyzer thanked the Mayor for organizing the legislative
                      breakfast. He thanked all involved in coordinating the housing workshops. He
                      acknowledged the Police Department for their community outreach. He wished everyone
                      a happy holiday.

                      Council Member Cyndi Sharkey provided an update from the legislative breakfast. She
                      acknowledged all the work the public works staff is getting done when there is little to no
                      snow removal to do.

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Zoltanski wished everyone a Happy Hanukkah. She mentioned the Hale Centre
                      Theatre event tomorrow and spoke about the impact the HCT has had on our community.
                      She provided an update from the legislative breakfast and let the public know that the
                      Community Legislative breakfast was cancelled this year due to scheduling conflicts. She
                      announced staff changes.

CAO Report

                      Martin Jensen, Deputy CAO invited the Council to attend the retirement farewell for Dan
                      Medina.-

Recreation Center Construction Report

                      Jetta Marrott provided an update on the construction progress of the new recreation
                      building.

Adjournment
                      Council Member Ryan Mecham made a motion, seconded by Council Member
                      Zach Robinson to adjourn the final Council meeting of 2025 at 8:38 pm...The
                      motion carried by a unanimous voice vote.

                      We wish a fond farewell to outgoing Council Members Zach Robinson, District 3 and
                      Ryan Mecham, District 1. We thank you for your dedication and service to the Council
                      and City. Best Wishes!
