2.    Highlands Landing Lot 201; 5590 West 7800 South; Preliminary and Final Site Plan and
      Conditional Use Permit for Restaurant, Fast Food; SC-2 Zone; Peterson Development Co.
      LLC/Paul Stringham (applicant) [#20286; parcel 20-25-351-010]

Paul Stringham, Peterson Development, explained that they have been planning this area with a sense
of community for the city. Lot 201 will have a 6100 square foot building with a drive-thru on the east
side occupied by Starbucks. There is leasing activity for the balance of the building, but no
commitments have been made. They want to have good uses that will make economic sense and add to
the community. More than adequate parking is provided, and they have taken pedestrian needs into
account. The architecture is very nice, and the building tones are gray. They hope to be under
construction the first part of May.

Ray McCandless said staff likes the building design, which the Design Review Committee approved
of.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the Preliminary/Final Site Plan and
Administrative Conditional Use Permit for a fast food restaurant for Highlands Landing Lot 201,
located at 5542 West 7800 South in a SC-2 Zone subject to the approval conditions listed below:

Preliminary/Final Site Plan:
1.     The proposed development shall meet all applicable 2009 City Code requirements.
2.     All changes to the site shall be consistent with the Site Plan application.
3.     An approved preliminary site plan shall remain valid for one year following the date of the
       approval.

3.    Maverik; 5600 West 9000 South (SW Corner); Preliminary Site Plan and Conditional Use
      Permit for Gasoline Service Station in Protection Zone 2 of the Drinking Water Source
      Protection Overlay Zone; SC-3 Zone; Maverik, Inc./Christie Hutchings (applicant)
      [#19899-CUP, 20314-Site; parcel 26-02-426-004]

Christie Hutchings, Maverik, said the application is for a standard Maverik convenience store with six
passenger fueling stalls and three commercial high flow lanes. Staff has approved the access to 5600
West, and the access on 9000 South has preliminary approval from UDOT with final pending West
Jordan approval. There is a shared access with the RDA on the south and west, and a draft agreement
is pending. Parking is in conformance with city code. There is an application for a one-lot subdivision
in for an administrative approval.

Dennis Riding, environmental director for Maverik, explained the tank construction and alarm system
that ensures containment of any fuel spill from the tanks.

Lisa Elgin said the fuel tanks meet all city and state requirements.

Staff recommended that the Planning Commission approve the preliminary site plan and conditional
use permit for Maverik; SW corner of 5600 West & 9000 South with the conditions of approval listed
below:

1.     Planning Commission approvals do not include Public Safety, Fire, Building and Safety, or
       Engineering approval.
2.     Per section 15-5-3: Appeals to the Board of Adjustment may be taken by any person
       aggrieved by any administrative decision or action of city staff or the planning commission
       on matters pertaining to the interpretation and application of title 12, 13, 14, or 15 of this
       code. The appeal shall be filed within thirty (30) days following the decision at issue. The
       person filing the appeal shall file written notice with the Zoning Administrator and with the
       Board of Adjustment specifying the reasons for the appeal.
3.     All state and local tank and piping testing and monitoring requirements shall be strictly
       adhered to.
4.     A City-approved cross access easement between the applicant and the adjacent property owner
       must be fully executed and recorded in the office of the Salt Lake County Recorder prior to
       final approval, related to the applicant’s site access from 5600 West and 9000 South streets.

Corbin England asked if the alarm notifies people on site or if it is monitored at headquarters.

Dennis Riding said it is monitored both on site and on their network, so they will know about any
alarm conditions very quickly.

Corbin England opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

Ammon Allen asked about the procedure and timing of an alarm response.

Dennis Riding explained that once they see the alarm, they will take the tank out of service, pump out
any fluid, and send it for testing to determine the nature of the problem. That tank will be replaced.

MOTION:        Jay Thomas moved to approve the Preliminary Site Plan and Conditional Use
               Permit for Maverik; SW corner of 5600 West 9000 South; Maverik, Inc./Christie
               Hutchings (applicant) subject to conditions 1 through 4 in the staff report. The
               motion was seconded by Bob Bedont and passed 5-0 in favor. Matt Quinney and
               Kent Shelton were absent.

4.    Temple Subdivision; 7185 South Temple Drive; Preliminary Subdivision Plat (2 lots on
      .32 acres); R-3-8 Zone; Kraig Johnson (applicant) [#20299; parcel 21-26-103-028]

Kraig Johnson, applicant, explained that he has owned the third-acre property for about ten years. He
recently had the dilapidated structure demolished. He would like to subdivide the property into two
single-family lots with homes similar to the newer structures to the north. A variance was granted to
allow for a narrower lot due to the lot width.

Lisa Elgin said the property is zoned R-3-8 (multi-family) but the cap and grade ordinance restricts
multi-family housing. Splitting the lot for two homes will keep the area within the high-density
designation in accordance with the general plan.

Based on the analysis and findings contained in the staff report, staff recommended that the Planning
Commission approve the Preliminary Subdivision Plat for Temple Subdivision on approximately 0.32
acres and containing 2 lots located at 7185 South Temple Drive with the conditions listed.

1.     Planning Commission approvals do not include Public Safety, Fire, Building and Safety, or
       Engineering approval.
2.     Per section 15-5-3: Appeals to the Board of Adjustment may be taken by any person
       aggrieved by any administrative decision or action of city staff or the planning commission
       on matters pertaining to the interpretation and application of title 12, 13, 14, or 15 of this
       code. The appeal shall be filed within thirty (30) days following the decision at issue. The
       person filing the appeal shall file written notice with the Zoning Administrator and with the
       Board of Adjustment specifying the reasons for the appeal.

Corbin England opened the public hearing.

Further public comment was closed at this point for this item.

When asked about the narrow parcel of land to the north owned by Salt Lake County, Mr. Johnson said
he received a notice to bid on it after the event had already taken place. He had the lots professionally
surveyed, which showed that the width of the parcel is zero. Salt Lake County agreed with the zero-
width determination, so it is not of any impact for this application. It is up to the County whether they
choose to keep showing it on the map. The fence lines have been well established for a very long time.
The survey was exact on the north and south and one fence on the east is about six inches off. It
wouldn’t have mattered if he had acquired the property because it has zero mass.

Bob Bedont asked if access to the new lots will be from Temple Drive and not from the side yards
where the narrow strip could become an issue.

Kraig Johnson said the north lot will use the existing curb cut and the southern lot will need a curb cut
onto 1300 West. All utilities are still established for the north lot, and utility lines will be needed for
the south lot. The property will only be accessed from 1300 West.

MOTION:        Jay Thomas moved, based on the information and finding set forth in the staff
               report and upon the evidence and explanations received today, to approve the
               Preliminary Subdivision Plat for Temple Subdivision (2 lots on .32 acres); 7185
               South Temple Drive; Kraig Johnson (applicant) with the conditions 1 and 2 as
               listed in the staff report. The motion was seconded by Trish Hatch and passed 5-0
               in favor. Matt Quinney and Kent Shelton were absent.
