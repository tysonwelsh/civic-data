NEW BUSINESS

1.     Public Meeting – A Petition for Final Subdivision Plat Approval to Plat a One-Lot
       Subdivision Plat for a Proposed Public Utility Facility.

Action Item

Applicant: Dominion Energy, Brady Nowers, Representative
Address: 919 West 2100 South

Ms. Ungricht presented the Staff Report and stated that the proposed Final Plat was presented to the
Commission at the last meeting. The request tonight was for Final Plat approval. The property is
located at 919 West 2100 South in the Flex zone. The request is for a public utility facility to be
owned by Dominion Energy for gas pipes. Previous uses on the site included a Wendy’s Restaurant.
The building has since been demolished. The request meets the General Plan considerations one of
which is the land use and neighborhood goal to enhance urban and streetscape design to support
South Salt Lake City’s distinct image and community pride. Landscaping will be added as well as
a sidewalk along 900 West and 2100 South.

Ms. Ungricht reported that community facilities are another General Plan consideration that the
request meets by providing utilities and services to residents. This will not only provide services to
South Salt Lake but to the greater Salt Lake Valley. The Landscape Plan was presented as well as
the proposed ALTA Survey. The Final Plat was shown with the Public Utility Easement (“PUE”)
with no dedication needed along 900 West and 2100 South because the existing streetscapes meet
all requirements. Staff recommended approval with the conditions enumerated in the Staff Report.
The applicant was not present.

Chair Carter opened the public hearing. There were no public comments. The public hearing was
closed.

Motion to APPROVE the application for a Final Subdivision Plat at 919 West 2100 South
subject to the following:

Findings of Fact:

       1.      The subject property, located at 919 West 2100 South, consists of one metes and
               bounds parcel.

       2.      The parcel is located in the Flex zone.

       3.      All new lots in the Flex zone shall meet the one-acre minimum.

       4.      The proposed subdivision will legally plat one lot that will be approximately 0.7
               acres in size.
