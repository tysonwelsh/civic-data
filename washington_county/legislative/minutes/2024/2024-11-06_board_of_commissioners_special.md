WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
NOVEMBER 6, 2024

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Gil Almquist at 3:00 p.m. on November 6, 2024, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were Commissioner Gil
Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Deputy County Attorney
Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw, and Deputy Clerk Sarah Lloyd.

INVOCATION BY: Rae Boyer
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Elliott Taylor
REGULAR AGENDA ITEMS FOR CONSIDERATION:
CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3376, A RESOLUTION APPROVING

EXPENDITURES FOR VENDORS NEEDED TO FACILITATE THE PINTO ROAD
PROJECT AND TO ACCEPT REIMBURSEMENT FROM THE U.S. FOREST SERVICE

County Engineer Elliott Taylor stated that this resolution is to pay all the vendors for the work
previously done on the Pinto Road Project.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3376, A
Resolution Approving Expenditures for Vendors Needed to Facilitate the
Pinto Road Project and to Accept Reimbursement from the U.S. Forest
Service. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous
vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3377, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A_CONTRACT_ BETWEEN WASHINGTON COUNTY (THE “COUNTY”) AND

PROGRESSIVE CONTRACTING, INC. (P.C.I.) FOR THE DAMMERON VALLEY

FLOOD REPAIR PROJECT 2024

Mr. Taylor explained that this is for flood repair work, primarily in the Utah Department of
Transportation (UDOT) right-of-way near Dammeron Valley along SR-18.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3377, A
Resolution Approving a Contract Between Washington County (The
“County”) and Progressive Contracting, Inc. (P.C.I.) for the Dammeron
Valley Flood Repair Project 2024. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and
Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3378, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LETTER OF SUPPORT FOR UDOT’S IMPROVEMENTS TO THE HURRICANE — LA
VERKIN BRIDGE

Deputy County Attorney Devin Snow stated this is a project that UDOT is preparing to propose to
the Transportation Commission for certain improvements to the Hurricane — La Verkin Bridge.
The requested amount from UDOT is $30,000; with the final amount being subject to Commission
approval. Commissioner Snow specified the Resolution states the fencing is ornamental; however;
it is also a public safety issue.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3378, A
Resolution Approving a Letter of Support for UDOT’s Improvements to the
Hurricane — La Verkin Bridge. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and Snow
voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3379, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
THE LEASES TO CONGRESSWOMAN MALOY FOR OFFICE SPACE IN THE
COUNTY ADMINISTRATION BUILDING

Mr. Snow explained the Resolution is for office space on the third floor of the Administration
Building. There are two leases, one to finish her current term, and one for her upcoming term in
office.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3379, A
Resolution Approving the Lease to Congresswoman Maloy for Office Space in
the County Administration Building. Motion seconded by Commissioner
Iverson and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist,
Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3380, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
AMENDED CONTRACTS FOR THE COUNTY’S APPELLATE PUBLIC DEFENDERS

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained that Utah las requires Washington County to
provide legal representation for indigent parties in certain appellate proceedings for cases in Utah.
The County contracts with qualified attorneys to provide appellate legal representation for indi gent
cases involving child welfare/parental defense and juvenile delinquency proceedings.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3380, A
Resolution Approving Amended Contracts for the County’s Appellate Public
Defenders Subject to Final Attorney Review. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Snow and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Almquist, Iverson, and Snow voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2024-3381, A RESOLUTION APPROVING
A LIST OF QUALIFIED CONTRACTORS THAT WILL BE INVITED TO BID ON THE
CONSTRUCTION OF THE WASHINGTON COUNTY EMERGENCY OPERATIONS
AND LEGACY PARK STORAGE FACILITY

Ms. Felshaw stated the County an evaluation committee will evaluate and score statements of
qualifications in connection with the planned construction of the County Emergency Operations
and Legacy Park Storage Facility. The three companies that will be invited to bid are Bug Mahas,
Warner Construction, and Watts Construction.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Snow to Adopt Resolution No. R-2024-3381, A
Resolution Approving a List of Qualified Contractors that will be Invited to
Bid on the Construction of the Washington County Emergency Operations
and Legacy Park Storage Facility. Motion seconded by Commissioner Iverson
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Iverson, and
Snow voting aye.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 3:15 p.m.
