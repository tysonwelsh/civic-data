Russell Platt
4301 West 4570 South
Zone change from A to R-1-8
1.53 acres

Russell Platt has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for a 1.53-acre parcel located
at 4301 West 4570 South. The property is currently zoned A (Agriculture, minimum lot size ½
acre) with a General Plan designation of Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre). The proposed
zone is R-1-8 (Single Unit Dwelling Residential, minimum lot size 8,000 sq. ft.) and the
proposed General Plan designation is Low Density Residential (3 to 4 units/acre). As a reminder,
City ordinance only allows zone changes to R-1-8 on properties less than 2 acres.

Surrounding zones include R-1-8 to the north, south, and west and A to the east. The property is
surrounded by single unit dwellings.

Development Proposal

The subject property includes part of a home that was built in 1977 along with a few accessory
buildings. According to the applicant, he would like to subdivide the property, along with
property to the north, into four new building lots. The east portion of the property would be
merged with the adjoining parcel to the east for the existing home.

Development Agreement

A development agreement is required for this project. Section 7-11-213 of the West Valley City
Zoning Ordinance states: “All new Subdivisions involving a rezone of property, or a PUD, shall
participate in a Development Agreement that addresses housing size, quality, exterior finish
materials, Streetscapes, Landscaping, etc.” For the development agreement, the applicant is
proposing to use the same housing standards that were in place up until July of 2021 when the
City amended the zoning ordinance to comply with limitations to housing standards imposed by
the State legislature.

Staff Alternatives:

      Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development agreement that

      Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
       public hearing.

      Denial. This application should be denied. A larger lot zone like R-1-12 or RE would
       comply with the existing General Plan designation.

GPZ-4-2023
Maria Dubon
4344 and 4358 West 3500 South
General plan change from Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre) to General Commercial
and zone change from A to C-2
0.7 acres


Maria Dubon has submitted a General Plan/zone change application for portions of two parcels
totaling 1.85 acres located at 4344 and 4358 West 3500 South. For approximately the south 240’
of the parcels, the current zoning is C-2 (General Commercial) with a General Plan designation
of General Commercial. The remaining north portions are zoned A (Agriculture, minimum lot
size ½ acre) with a General Plan designation of Large Lot Residential (2 to 3 units/acre). The
applicant is proposing to add 0.7 acres of C-2 zoning and General Plan designation of General
Commercial to the north. Half an acre of A zoning would remain at the north end of the parcels.

Surrounding zones include A to the north, east, and west and C-2 to the south. Surrounding land
uses include a large lot single family home to the north, a used car lot to the east, a home and
commercial building to the south on the south side of 3500 South, and a boarded-up convenience
store and vacant land to the west. The west parcel includes a rental home built in 1937 and the
east parcel includes a home built in 1941 that was converted into a commercial building.

If this application is approved, the applicant plans to demolish the existing buildings and build a
new restaurant on the subject property. The restaurant development would use 1.35 acres of the
1.85-acre total area. Included with this report is a concept plan, building elevations, and a floor
plan for the proposed restaurant which would include a drive-thru.

If this application is approved, the requirements in Section 7-7-111 would apply to the
neighboring residential property lines. These requirements include the following:

       “Where Fast Food Establishments with drive-up windows are located adjacent to a
       Residential Zone or Use, the order board, speakers, and pick-up window shall be located
       at least 50' from the nearest residential property line and any sound emitting from the
       order board, speakers, and pickup window shall not be audible from the nearest
       residential property line between 10 PM and 7 AM.”

Section 7-2-111 requires front yard setbacks that apply to buildings and parking to be measured
from the proposed right-of-way line where road widening is anticipated in the Major Street Plan.
In this case, the Major Street Plan shows the future right-of-way width of this section of 3500
South to be 115’ and the existing right-of-way width is only 80’. Given the setback requirement
in Section 7-2-111 and the fact that the site is being redeveloped into a higher intensity use, staff
recommends that the property needed for future widening be dedicated as right-of-way and that
the dedication be listed as a requirement in a development agreement.

The applicant has submitted two concept plan options. The primary difference between the two
is the design of the parking area north of the proposed building. In the first option, the parking lot
maintains a 10’ landscaped setback along both the east and west sides as required by ordinance
and utilizes angled parking stalls. The second option maintains a 5’ landscaped setback along the
sides and utilizes 90-degree parking stalls. While the second option yields three more stalls and
results in a more efficient use of space, the reductions to the side setbacks would require a
development agreement exception.

During the study session concern was raised about the limited development options for the north
portion of the adjoining east property should this application be approved. Staff has included a
plat map with this report, which shows the width of the adjoining lot at 61.62’. Staff discussed
this concern with the applicant and she expressed a willingness to work with the adjoining owner
on a shared access to facilitate future development.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development agreement that
      includes a provision requiring right-of-way dedication along 3500 South in accordance
      with the City’s Major Street Plan and an exception that allows the parking lot landscaped
      setback to be reduced along the east and west sides from 10’ to 5’.

   2. Approval. This application should be approved subject to a development agreement that
      includes a provision requiring right-of-way dedication along 3500 South in accordance
      with the City’s Major Street Plan.

   3. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
      public hearing.

   4. Denial. This application should be denied as the existing C-2 area is large enough to
      support a commercial use and rezoning these parcels makes developing residential on the
      adjoining properties, as called for in the General Plan, more challenging.

ZT-4-2023
West Valley City
A Zoning Ordinance change to Sections 7-1-103, 7-2-127, 7-7-115, and 7-11-205 to add or
modify several landscaping standards relating to decorative dirt, artificial turf, and how
minimum landscape percentages are calculated. The proposed changes would also reduce
the allowed number of garage, basement, or yard sales from an annual maximum of four to
an annual maximum of three.
Over the past several months the City Council has had many discussions regarding the practices,
policies, and ordinances relating to code enforcement. As a result of these discussions, the
Council directed staff to make several changes to policies and ordinances that will impact code
enforcement. Some of the ordinance changes requested by the Council impact Title 7 which is
the City’s Zoning Ordinance. These Title 7 changes are the subject of this application and are
described below.

Section 7-1-103
This is the definitions section of the Zoning Ordinance. The proposed change to this section adds
the following term and definition: “Decorative Dirt” means an earth or earth-like substance free
of weeds. Decorative Dirt does not include grass, flowers, flower beds, bushes, trees, bark,
gravel, or artificial turf.”

Section 7-2-127
This section addresses standards for the use of artificial turf. The proposed change would allow
artificial turf to be used in all parkstrips. Artificial turf is currently prohibited in all parkstrips
except wider parkstrips along Redwood Road and segments of 5600 West and 3300/3500 South.

Section 7-7-115
This section addresses standards for home occupations. The proposed change would reduce the
allowed number of garage, basement, or yard sales from an annual maximum of four to an
annual maximum of three.

Section 7-11-205
This section addresses the landscaping and irrigation standards for single unit dwelling, twin
home, and two unit dwelling lots. The proposed changes to this section would:

        1. require live plant material percentages to be determined by the size of the plant at full
           growth, not at planting,
        2. allow decorative dirt as a groundcover, and
        3. allow artificial turf to be counted toward all of the required live plant material.


Staff Alternatives:

    5. Approval. This application should be approved.

    6. Continuance. This application should be continued for reasons determined during the
       public hearing.

    7. Denial. This application should be denied.

C-11-2023
Valvoline (NovaSource Enterprises, LLC)
2663 S. Anna Caroline Dr.
C-2 zone, .72 acres

The applicant, NovaSource Enterprises, LLC, representing Valvoline, is requesting conditional
use approval for a quick lube auto service center at 2663 S. Anna Caroline Dr. The zoning for
this property is C-2, General Commercial and the General Plan designation is General
Commercial. The surrounding zone is C-2 on the north, east and south sides. The zoning to the
west is RM, multi unit dwelling residential and C-1, neighborhood commercial. The surrounding
uses include the WinCo shopping center to the north, American United Federal Credit Union
(AUFCU) to the south, a Quick Quack Carwash to the east and the Sagegate apartment complex
to the west.

This property is located within the WinCo Shopping Center (C-8-2008, West Valley Pavilion Lot
101). The proposed use is a 3-bay quick lube facility located within a 2,117 square foot building.
There is also a 1,651 lower level for service access and bulk oil storage tanks. The building will
be constructed of hardie-board and stone-veneer with metal canopy as accent features. The
building must comply with the Commercial Design Standards and the Development Agreement
for the overall project requires that the east and west sides of the buildings be considered a
primary façade for the requirements of the Design Guidelines for Commercial Buildings. The
service bay doors are located on the north and south sides of the building, not facing the adjacent
residential uses.

The typical hours of operation for this use are 8:00 am – 6:00 pm, Monday – Friday, 8:00 am –
5:00 pm on Saturdays and 10:00 am – 4:00 pm on Sundays. There will be four to five employees
and 15 parking spaces provided (11 required). This site was planned as a building lot so the
overall shopping center has adequate parking to accommodate this use and the development of
this lot.

This overall shopping center has existing improvements for the primary pedestrian corridors
through the center. There is an east-west connection on the north side of this lot and a north-
south connection to the west. These will be maintained and landscaped accordingly with live
plant material to match the existing center.

There was an overall sign package that was approved for this shopping center when it was
originally developed in 2008. This approval included 2 pole signs and 4 monument signs with no
signage along Anna Caroline Dr. These signs are primarily located along the south (Parkway
Blvd) and east frontage (5600 West) and the fourth and final monument sign was installed in an
interior lot for the Quick Quack carwash. The lot layout for the center has changed so at this time
Valvoline would like to request an additional monument sign for their space. The sign code
would allow for an additional sign and they would propose to locate it north of their entrance on
the south end of Anna Caroline Dr. in the area that is not directly adjacent to the residential
development. There will also be signage on the building itself and that will be required to meet
the Title 11 standards.


There will be a dumpster provided for this building that will be located within a 6’ masonry
enclosure that is constructed to match the building.

Staff Alternatives

Approval subject to any issues raised at the public hearing as well as the following conditions:

   1. The building must be completed per the approved plans and be designed in accordance
      with the Commercial Design Standards and the Development Agreement.

   2. The site shall be landscaped per the approved plans and all existing landscaping must be
      maintained and/or replaced if it is in poor condition.

   3. An east-west and north-south pedestrian connection must be maintained to accommodate
      the overall pedestrian circulation through this shopping center.

   4. The dumpster shall be completely screened and secured with a 6’ masonry enclosure that
      matches the building.

   5. There may be one monument sign permitted for this use along the south entrance to the
      property. All signage must comply with the West Valley City Sign Ordinance.

   6. Must meet requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

   7. Subject to review upon valid complaint.


Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.

approval for a public utility installation. The property is zoned R-1-8 (single unit dwelling
residential). The properties to the south and west are also zoned R-1-8, and the properties to the
east and north are zoned C-2.
Adjacent land uses include single unit dwellings to the south and west, a vacant parcel to the
east, and commercial uses across 3500 South to the north.

Dominion Energy recently purchased the subject parcel from UDOT. UDOT acquired the
land originally for the expansion of 3500 South in conjunction with the Mountain View
Corridor project. The parcel is currently vacant. Dominion Energy intends to use the site to
install a pipeline intervention gadget (PIG) launcher. The PIG will be sent through the
pipeline from this location to inspect the pipeline for damage or anomalies.

There is one existing access to the site from 5700 West. Dominion is proposing to add one
additional access from 3500 South. No buildings are being proposed for this project. There
will only be above- ground piping on the property. The applicant is proposing to screen the
