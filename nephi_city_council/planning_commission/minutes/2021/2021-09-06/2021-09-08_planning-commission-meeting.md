NEPHI CITY PLANNING COMMISSION PUBLIC HEARING
                                 September 8, 2021
                                    21 E. 100 N.
                                  Nephi, UT 84648
                                     7:00 p.m.

  Interim-Planning Commission Chairman Seth Atkinson opened the hearing and
explained the purpose of the hearing was to accept public comment on some
proposed land use code revisions. The revisions were necessary because in the
2021 Utah state legislative session, a bill required cities to allow interior accessory
dwelling units (ADUs). The state removed most of the ability for a city to regulate
these units. The items that could be regulated by cities were required to be in
place by October 1, 2021. Mr. Atkinson explained that the proposed revisions
mainly change land use code regarding interior ADUs but also proposes
clarification to zoning districts regarding Planned Unit Developments, changes to
noticing requirements, and a small change to the Central Commercial Zone list of
uses.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

Hearing closed at 7:10 p.m.
