CONCERNS RAISED BY PUBLIC
Any comments received prior to completion of the Staff Report are addressed in the Staff Report to the Planning
Commission. Key issues raised in written comments received subsequent to the Staff Report or public comment during
the public hearing included the following:
   • Chad Hales (neighbor to the east) described the process he has gone through with the applicant on the property
       line dispute and is fully supportive of the zone change.

APPLICANT RESPONSE
Key points addressed in the applicant's presentation to the Planning Commission included the following:
  • Ms. Lamb Reilly gave a brief history on how she got into a property that she believed could easily be subdivided
      and given to her two children. She also detailed the property line dispute that is close to resolution with a seven-
      and-a-half-foot adjustment.

PLANNING COMMISSION DISCUSSION
Key points discussed by the Planning Commission included the following:
  • Lisa Jensen confirmed that the old buildings are down and that with or without the zone change there will be two
      new buildings constructed on the property. She also confirmed with staff that the new boundary line will still
      maintain at least 8,000 square feet.
  • The Planning Commission discussed that the real difference in the zone change will be one property owner instead
      of two property owners.
  • The Commission noted that in this area the neighborhood plan already calls for increased density, and the request
      is for less density than what is on the Maeser Future Land Use Plan.
  • Lisa Jensen described her support for the rezone and indicated that the neighborhood chairs response and the
      density around the property make her feel comfortable with this request, especially with the proposal for two single
      family lots.
  • Robert Knudsen agreed with Mrs. Jensen’s comments and noted that the architecture will fit in with the newer
      units in the area.
  • Melissa Kendall stated her support for the rezone is due to the compliance with the neighborhood plan.
