Planning and Development Services

             During the July 8, 2018, Council meeting the Kearns Metro Township Council
moved to approve Application #30665 - vacating a portion of a public street located at
approximately 5375 South Street between 4250 and 4280 West to accommodate the
development of a new public library campus.

                 Curtis Woodward, Planning and Development Services Division, Salt Lake
County, stated the Salt Lake County was not able to find the legal document showing that this
property was ever dedicated to Salt Lake County for a road. After discussions among Salt Lake
County officials and David Church, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, it was decided the
best way to handle this situation was to rescind the previous motion and then close the road to
vehicular traffic. This would allow the Salt Lake County Library Services Division to use the road
as a pedestrian walkway between the parking lot and the new library. State Code requires that a
public hearing be held to close any road to vehicular traffic. The notice requirements for the
closure is four weeks with a notice of the hearing to be posted in three different locations. The
next meeting of the Kearns Metro Township Council is exactly four weeks from today, so he took
the liberty of posting two notices at Salt Lake County and if the Council agrees the final notice
could be posted tonight at the Kearns Library. This would meet all requirements and the hearing
to close the road could be held at the next meeting.

               Council Member Snow, second by Council Member Brown, moved to rescind the
original motion to vacate the public street and the adoption of Ordinance #18-07-01 and to set a
public hearing for September 8, 2018, to close the public street known as 5375 South at
approximately 4250 to 4280 West in Kearns Township. The motion passed unanimously.

Unified Police Department

               Chief Brent Atkinson updated the Council on Herriman and Riverton City’s intent
to leave Unified Police Department (UPD). He stated a meeting was held with the current
members of the UPD where concerns were expressed regarding the formula used to determine
how much each municipality pays for pooled services. Some partners felt it pays more into the
pool services then it gets in return.

The partner cities also expressed concerns about the structure of the UPD board. Currently, all
members get one vote. The discussion is that the vote should be weighed. The Copperton Metro
Township should not get the same vote as a bigger city such as Cottonwood Heights.

               Council Member Snow asked if Herriman City and Riverton City had the funds to
cover its own services.

                Chief Atkinson stated there is a clause in the interlocal agreement that says if a
participating member has been with the UPD for longer than five years, it becomes vested in UPD
assets and if it leaves, it can take a portion of the assets with it. This clause has created some
disagreements between Herriman City and the UPD board. Herriman City is asking to take
vehicles, computers, guns, and things it feels like it has paid for through Herriman City funds. It
would also like a portion of the fund balance. Negotiations are ongoing.

Council Business

Municode Web Development

                Mr. Bob Geiger, Municode Web Development, delivered a PowerPoint
presentation highlighting what Municode does, different website designs, and how each functions.
He stated Municode websites are ADA compliant, mobile accessible, have calendars that
integrate with other departments within the township or the Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services
District, have search capability, backup provisions, provide security measures, and set up
information.

              Mayor Bush stated Kearns needs to get its website up and running. The only
concern she had with going with Municode, is with the ordinance, which the Council recently
adopted, requiring additional bids if the cost was over $10,000.

                                                   6
DATE          MONDAY                       AUGUST                                      13, 2018

            Council Member Brown stated she felt the best way to go would be with
Municode. She has been working with them and is very impressed.

              Council Member Petersen stated it looks like a great program.

                Council Member Snow stated she searched the net looking for someone to get
additional bids from but could not find anyone that could do the work.

                 Council Member Schaeffer stated the main obstacle is getting around the dollar
amount requiring another bid. Council Members have looked for other companies but cannot find
anyone that can integrate with Municode. He asked if the Council wanted to amend the ordinance
relating to dollar amount changing it from $10,000 to $11,500. This would solve the problem.

               Council Member Snow stated she was not sure the Council wanted to go through
the hassle of amending the ordinance. She suggested splitting the bid - one bid could be to set
up the website and the other bid could be to maintain the website.

              Council Member Peterson stated the ordinance does not allow for the bid to be
split.

              Council Member Bush stated she did not understand how the other townships
got around the dollar amount in its ordinances.

               Alison Weyher, Senior Policy Advisory, Salt Lake County Public Works &
Municipal Services Department, stated included in the ordinance is a sole source clause, which
would allow the Kearns Metro Township Council to go with Municode without the need for
additional bids. This is the only company that can do what is needed. A motion would have to
be carefully worded to justify this.

               Mayor Bush stated this item will be brought back in September allowing time for
the Council to work on the wording of the motion, and to review the ordinance to make sure there
is a sole source clause.
