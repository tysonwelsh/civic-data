1)
Appeal of Weight Townhouse Subdivision Amended Plat Amendment Determination continued (6:38 PM)
Council continued their questioning of the parties, and the parties responded.
Christopher Robinson made a motion to find in favor of the Appellant and find that the Planning Commission erred. There was insufficient evidence for the Planning Commission to consider removing the plat note in terms of its finding of harm to the public or the requirement that there be good cause and that the Council therefore allows for the conditional removal of the plat note that would become effective upon the Appellant's making good on its proffer of removing density from 70,000 to 25,000 square feet on the Weight parcel once the six duplexes are approved. The Council finds there isn't sufficient injury to persons or the public from the addition of six townhomes accessing Red Pine; that amount is de minimus given what else is going on around there. As to the good cause, absent access to Red Pine, there is no vehicular access to this parcel. Good cause requires vehicular access, subject to findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions drafted by the Attorney's Office, to be approved by the Council Chair. (6:59 PM) Malena Stevens seconded, and the motion carried, (4-1).
Roger Armstrong voted AYE
