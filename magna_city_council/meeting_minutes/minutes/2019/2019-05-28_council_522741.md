Application #30878 – Ben Logue requesting approval of a zone change from C-2 (commercial)
and RM (residential multi-family) to C-2 (commercial) and RM (residential multi-family) on property

reviewed the application stating the applicant wants to move all of the commercial property to the
front, which fronts onto 8400 West, and leave the remainder of the property in the RM zone. The
Magna Planning Commission recommended approval with a condition that the applicant do a

use application comes to it. Then, the County Planning and Development staff recommended
approval.

property along the highway, which is where it should be rather than in the back.

Reports from Other Municipal Service Providers

Unified Police Department

                Chief Jason Ackernan reviewed the quarterly report for January through March
of this year, which included staffing members, call volume and response times, and maps showing
two types of crime in the Magna District. As part of the UPD, the Magna Metro Township
contributes to full services that are shared with other partners, including other townships, so also
included in the report are calls or situations involving pooled service resources in which the Magna
Metro Township directly benefited. Those services include Special Weapons and Tactics
(SWAT), canine, major accident reconstruction, metro gangs, and metro narcotics.

               Council Member Hull stated the response times are very quick. She asked if
those figures were from a specific date.

                Chief Ackerman stated the statistics capture the time that the UPD is dispatched
to the time that it arrives on the scene. The UPD triages those calls from a Priority 1 to a Priority
10, with a Priority 1 being a major situation that officers have to get to quickly. He announced
that the UPD has a new Chief Executive Office and is revamping the UPD budget to right size the
contributions that everyone pays into the pooled services, after the loss of Herriman and Riverton
Cities. The UPD is having a special meeting with its board a week from today. Then, the final
budget will be approved on June 20th.

               Council Member Peel asked why general offenses were up.

              Chief Ackerman stated the actual calls per service per officer is down, but it is
possible more crimes or more serious crimes are being reported. It is normal to see calls increase

Ordinances

             Paul Ashton introduced the following amendments to the Magna Metro
Township’s Code of Ordinances, for which the Council needed to set a public hearing:

Ordinance #19-05-01, repealing Title 17: “Flood Control and Water Quality” and replacing it with
Title 17: “City Flood Control and Water Quality.” The County has the responsibility for flood control
because it is paid for out of the General Fund. This ordinance amendment leaves that
responsibility to the County, so the Magna Metro Township does not have to change its code of
ordinances every time the Council changes its code of ordinances.

Ordinance 19-05-02 changes Section 14.16.010, “Right of Way---Excavations and Structures
Prohibited When” to include language prohibiting excavation in Magna right of ways under certain
circumstances, and it establishes minimum standards for repairs following excavations in right of
ways. For example, it requires that when someone puts a new road or overlay in place and
excavates the road, they have to put asphalt down.

Ordinance 19-05-03 amends Title 18: “Subdivisions,” Chapters 16, “Final Plant” and 24, “Required
Improvements” to add requirements for Geographic Information Systems (GIS ) files submission
as a part of the final subdivision plat approval process.

      Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to set the date of
