   2. FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING TITLE 20 OF THE WEBER COUNTY CODE
      RELATING TO FIRE PREVENTION AND PROTECTION AND TO OPEN BURNING.

        Brandon Thueson, Weber Fire District Fire Marshall, worked with the County Attorney’s Office
        to prepare this ordinance to conform to State law, adopt the 2015 International Fire Code, and
        clarify items in the existing ordinance. It includes provisions to modify the code as allowed by
        State law and to automatically update any amendment that the Weber Fire District Board enacts
        as long as the District notifies the county in writing and gives a copy to the Commission chair
        and county building official, corrects Chapter 2 regarding open burning to meet State laws,
        clarifies definitions & the penalty section, matches DEQ rules for burning at community waste
        sites, and adds sections on inspections. The District works hard to help businesses comply.
        Commissioner Harvey would like to change the penalty to an infraction or civil action and Chair
        Ebert prefers business license revocation after failure to comply rather than criminalization of the
        behavior.
        Commissioner Gibson moved to approve the first reading of an ordinance amending Title 20 of
        the County Code relating to fire prevention and protection and to open burning; Commissioner
        Harvey made a substitute motion recommending that the penalty section be amended to work
        towards more of a civil or infraction process. Courtlan Erickson, Deputy County Attorney,
        stated that the county ordinance gives the Commission the option of choosing the type of penalty
        for violation of county code. Commissioner Gibson seconded.
        Commissioner Gibson – aye; Commissioner Harvey – aye; Chair Ebert – aye
   3. SECOND READING OF FEE ORDINANCE AMENDMENTS RELATING TO THE WEBER COUNTY
