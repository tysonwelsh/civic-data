Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the
ordinance amendment
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding planning commission comments. Touched on
maintaining the overlay zone that applies to the canyon or reference the new 19.46 that applies to all zones.
Specific setback and setback requirements identified table, site standards and exceptions, ephemeral
streams, wetlands, and perennial streams. Remove landscape in forestry but leave in commercial.
Utah administrative code and culinary and wastewater individual systems. Remove the word governing
throughout. Household pets’ definition and limit of four.
Briefly went over grammatical corrections removing commas, add continued benefits of present and future
generations, change 19.12.010 prioritizing compatible continued benefits for current and future
generations. 19.24.030A, 19.24.030 E.2., table should have the word chapter added as being referred to as
a section. Change main use to primary use. 19.24.040 lot accessibility and access requirement, add a
sentence similar to commercial that refers to access. Spell out dwelling unit instead of using abbreviations,
measurement of lot width, measured building height, and strike building height as it is in FCOZ. Source
protection zones should add the word drinking water twice.

OAM2025-001464 - Consideration of an ordinance repealing Chapter 19.60, C-V Commercial Zone and
Chapter 19.62, C-2 Commercial Zone and replacing them with Chapter 19.32, Commercial Zones.
Planner Brian Tucker, Planning Manager (Discussion, Hearing, Action)
Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services District Planning Manager Brian Tucker provided an analysis of the
ordinance amendment.
Commissioners and staff had a brief discussion regarding Localscapes, Commercial zoned C-2 instead of
just “C”, minimum lot size, width, and frontage eliminated, 19.32.100 A.4 barbed wire on fencing is not
permitted and not exceed eight feet in height. Will hold this item as an action item in October.
Commissioner Harpst opened the public hearing.
