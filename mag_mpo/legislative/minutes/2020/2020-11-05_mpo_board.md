Approve October 1, 2020 Meeting Minutes Mayor Brunst moved to approve the October 1, 2020 meeting minutes. Mayor Johnson seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Public Comment None. I-15 Update Lehi Tech Corridor Grand Opening - UDOT Boyd Humphreys, UDOT Project Director, gave an update on the I-15 Technology Corridor. The project began Spring 2018 and is scheduled to be completed late 2020. It recently opened to six lanes in each direction. UDOT implemented unique design solutions to accommodate the demands in this area. TIP Amendment: UTA Funding Tables – Bob Allen, MAG Each year MAG adopts a new TIP, regardless of whether projects are added that use MAG funds. A portion of the TIP is a table of projects reflecting federal funds sent directly to UTA. Due to varying factors, the table was not included in the TIP that was adopted in August 2020. The funds listed in the table go directly to UTA, and MAG does not select how these funds are used. The table needs to be updated in the TIP so that the STIP can be adopted by the Transportation Commission on November 12, 2020. Mayor Kirk Christensen moved that the UTA funding table be updated in the TIP as presented. Mayor Mann seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Provo City Corridor Preservation Requests – Jim Price, MAG Provo City is requesting funds to purchase three properties: 1. An existing residence at 741 E. 2200 N, located on 2230 N, #37 of the Approved Corridors Map. Lot size .27 acres, appraised value: $560,000 Estimated acquisition costs: $10,000 Total Request: $570,000 MPO Regional Planning Committee November 5, 2020 Page 3 2. An existing residence and adjacent vacant land are both owned by the same entity at 836 N Geneva Road. These properties are located on 820 N, #38 on the Approved Corridors Map. The owner wishes to either sell both properties together, or develop them into new homes. Residence lot size: .29 acres, appraised value $230,000 Vacant lot size: 3.10 acres, appraised value $990,000 Estimated acquisition costs: $10,000 Total Request: $1,230,000 The Technical Advisory Committee reviewed both requests on October 26, 2020 and recommends approval by the Regional Planning Committee. MAG staff finds both requests fall within the policies for the Corridor Preservation Fund and are within fund limits and recommends approval. Corridor Preservation funds available as of October 2020 are approximately $6.5 M. 2200 North Provo Mayor Kaufusi moved that the Regional Planning Committee approve the Provo City request for Corridor Preservation Funds of $570,000 for purchase of property located at 741 E. 2200 N. This authorization will expire one year from today if the transaction is not completed by then. Mayor Fullmer seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. Geneva Road Provo Mayor Brunst moved that the Regional Planning Committee approve the Provo City request for Corridor Preservation Funds of $1,230,000 for purchase of properties located at 836 N Geneva Road, pending execution of the reimbursement agreement between Utah County and UDOT. This authorization will expire one year from today if the transaction is not completed by then. Mayor Miller seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. CMAQ Guidance Change Discussion – Andrew Jackson, MAG MAG is currently using CMAQ funds to assist in the operational costs of the UVX BRT. There are time limits on how long CMAQ funding cand be used for bus operations. Various options were presented to deal with the expiration of CMAQ funding on UVX. 1. Do nothing and let the subsidy expire 2. Change the federal guidance to allow the subsidy in perpetuity 3. Request UDOT Convert CMAQ to non-CMAQ funding and try to continue the subsidy 4. Petition for COVID funding from Fed Highways to maintain the subsidy for a year or two. MPO Regional Planning Committee November 5, 2020 Page 4 5. Work with UTA to fund CMAQ eligible projects in exchange for local quarter cent funding on UVX Mayor Brunst moved to direct staff to pursue options 3 and 5 in preparation of the ending of CMAQ operating assistance on UVX. Mayor Mann seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously except for Commissioner Lee who abstained from the vote. Updates Federal Highway Administration: Steve Call provided some clarification on CMAQ guidelines and reported that Continuing Resolution runs out November 11, 2020. However, it is likely that it will continue again. Utah Department of Transportation: None Utah Transit Authority: Mary De La Mare-Schaeffer thanked the committee for helping to move the free fare forward on UVX. A survey conducted by UTA on UVX shows 20% of riders are non-students. Currently ridership is at 5-6k riders a day on UVX. UTA appreciates everyone’s support.
