Kelsey Zarbock, Provo City Budget Officer, presented. Funds were set aside a while ago for the
purchase of two fire trucks. One of the trucks was not purchased right away so interest had been
accruing in the account. The proposal was to use those funds to purchase equipment related to
the fire trucks (per the terms of the lease agreement).

Jeremy Headman, Deputy Fire Chief, said additional equipment was needed for the new fire
trucks. This appropriation would be used to purchase a Raman Spectroscopy (to help determine
what hazardous materials were in the field without having to open containers); two (2) swift
water kits; a large area search systems kit; a confined space fan; and a rescue pole for water (to
help prevent having to get into the water to rescue a conscious person).

Chair Sewell opened public comment. There was no response.

With no council discussion, Chair Sewell called for a vote on the implied motion.

       Vote:        The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley,
                    Harding, Hoban, Sewell, and Shipley in favor.

 2.       A rescission of Ordinance 2021-31 amending Titles 14 and 15 of the Provo City Code
          to remove gendered references and to make stylistic and grammatical corrections.
          (21-073) (0:16:20)

John Magness, Council Policy Analyst, presented. On August 24, 2021, Ordinance 2021-31 was
adopted. The ordinance, as noticed, would remove gendered references, and make stylistic and
grammatical corrections. After further review of the ordinance, it was found there were some
minor substantive changes that were not noticed. The decision was made to rescind the
ordinance passed on August 24, 2021 and consider a new ordinance that included all the
proposed changes.

       Motion: A motion to rescind Ordinance 2021-31 was made by Councilor Handley and
               seconded by Councilor Ellsworth.

       Vote:        The motion was approved 7:0 with Councilors Ellsworth, Fillmore, Handley,
                    Harding, Hoban, Sewell, and Shipley in favor.

In response to questions from Councilor Fillmore, Mr. Jones replied that our code does not
specify the type of species for an emotional support animal (for instance a boa constrictor). If an
animal met the definition of all other provisions of our code, regarding noise, nuisance, or
whether the animal is allowed to run at large, there was nothing that would place limitations on
the species of an emotional support animal.

Councilor Fillmore was reluctant to approve this given the problems that could arise in the
context of emotional support animals. Mr. Jones replied that the council could remove any part
of the ordinance they did not feel comfortable with.

In response to a question from Councilor Ellsworth, Ms. McNally said the service animal
definition was provided in federal ADA laws, but not an explanation for emotional support

Mr. Jones felt this amendment limited rather than expanded our code. Our code currently said

Chair Sewell made a few minor suggestions on the wording.
    Chapter 14.02.0130(3)(c) – “…can be obtained by contacting the community and
       neighborhood services or and development services.” Strike the work “and” and just say
       “or.” Mr. Jones said the “and” did not need to be stricken because it was a relic from one
       of the edits and was not currently in the code (it just needed to be stricken from the
       exhibit).
    Chapter 14.04B.010 – Refers to the Transportation and Mobility Advisory Committee. It
       states that a member of city engineering division, a member of the police department, and
       other applicable city staff shall be appointed to act as liaison to the TMAC. The last
       phrase “…but neither shall be considered a member of TMAC” should be changed to
       “…but none shall be considered a member of the TMAC” because it refers to more than
       two people.

       Motion: A motion to amend the exhibit to include the grammatical change to Section
               14.04B.010 was made by Councilor Fillmore. The implied motion would apply
               to the amended exhibit. The motion was seconded by Councilor Ellsworth.
