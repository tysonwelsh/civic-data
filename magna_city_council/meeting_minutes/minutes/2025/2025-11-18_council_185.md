                                                    RESOLUTION R2025-16

                      A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA CANYON CITY COUNCIL ADOPTING THE 2025
                                 GENERAL ELECTION CANVASS OF VOTES

                    WHEREAS, Pursuant to 20A-4-301(2) of the State Election Code, the Governing Body along
            with the City Recorder, shall Canvass the Municipal General Election returns. This is to be done no
            sooner than seven days and no later than fourteen days after the general election; and

                     WHEREAS, the City of Magna has held a General Election on Tuesday, November 4, 2025; and

                    WHEREAS, the cumulative votes were conducted and tabulated by Salt Lake County Election
            Division; and

                    WHEREAS, the City Council has conducted and approved the Election Board of Canvass of
            Votes on November 18, 2025 at 6:00 p.m.

            NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF MAGNA,
            STATE OF UTAH:

                    Section 1. That the attached tabulation of election returned, identified as “Exhibit A,” and
            included as part of this resolution is adopted.

                     Section 2. The Certified 2025 Municipal General Election Results are as follows:
                             • Mayor: Mickey Sudbury
                             • Council Member District 2: Megan Olsen
                             • Council Member District 4: Terry George

                     Section 3. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.
