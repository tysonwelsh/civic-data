Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Mike Barker, City Attorney
CC:  Date: April 7, 2020
Re: Resolution 20-19 Approving, Ratifying and Extending the Mayor's Proclamations of a Local Emergency Comments: On March 12,  2020, Mayor Troy K. Walker, in his capacity as Draper City’s chief executive officer, issued a Proclamation of Local Emergency (the “Original Proclamation”) declaring a state of local emergency in the City concerning the COVID-19 pandemic (the “Pandemic”), as authorized by the Disaster Response and Recovery Act, Utah Code Ann. 53-2a-201 et seq. (the “Act ”).
Mayor Troy K. Walker, pursuant to his authority under the Act has issued additional Proclamations (the “Additional Proclamations”) as follows:
Proclamation 2 of 2020: Authorizing the City Manager to adopt temporary personnel policies.
Proclamation 3 of 2020: Designating Emergency Interim Succession.
Proclamation 4 of 2020: Limiting Gatherings, Events at Public Facilities and Operation of Public Buildings.
Proclamation 5 of 2020: Amending City Council Meeting Rules and Procedures.
Proclamation 6 of 2020:  Adopting Salt Lake County Public Health Order 2020-03.
Utah Code Ann. 53-2a-208(1)(b) provides that a local emergency shall not be continued or renewed for over 30 days without consent of the governing body of the jurisdiction subject to the declaration .
The reasons for the Original Proclamation and Additional Proclamations have not abated and have become worse in scope and severity.
