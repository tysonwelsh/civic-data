KATIE FOX, VICTIM WITNESS COORDINATOR - I WANTED TO REPORT THAT WE
ARE ON TRACK TO MEET ALL OF OUR VICTIM SERVICE GOALS FOR THE VOCA
(VICTIMS OF CRIME ACT) GRANT THIS YEAR. OUR FUNDING HAS BEEN
SIGNIFICANTLY CUT STARTING IN JULY OF THIS YEAR. THE GRANT WAS
REDUCED BY 10% THE FIRST YEAR AND THEN 25% THE SECOND YEAR. IT WILL
ONLY COVER A PORTION OF OUR VOCA-FUNDED EMPLOYEE SALARY. THAT
POSITION IS VITAL TO OUR OFFICE. WE ARE STILL TRYING TO RECUPERATE
FROM THE COURT SHUTDOWN DUE TO THE PANDEMIC AND OUR OFFICE IS
WORKING DILIGENTLY TO GET THROUGH THE BACKLOG OF CASES THAT NEED
TO GO TO TRIAL. WE WERE ABLE TO PURCHASE TECHNOLOGY THAT HELPED
VICTIMS CONNECT WITH US AND THE COURT DURING THE PANDEMIC. THIS
INCLUDED LAPTOPS, IPADS AND QUALITY HEADPHONES. THIS ALLOWED US TO
CONDUCT MEETINGS AND PARTICIPATE IN THE COURT PROCESS. ONE OF THE
MOST IMPORTANT RESOURCES WE WERE ABLE TO ACQUIRE BECAUSE OF THE
VOCA GRANT WAS OUR SOFT INTERVIEW ROOM AND PLAY AREA. THIS ALLOWS
VICTIMS TO DISCUSS THEIR CASES IN PRIVATE WHILE STILL BEING ABLE TO SEE
THEIR CHILDREN PLAYING WITH TOYS.

COMMISSIONERS THANKED THE DEPARTMENT AND ALL THEY DO FOR THE
ABILITY TO HELP MAKE VICTIMS SIGNIFICANTLY MORE COMFORTABLE AND
HAVE THEIR CHILDREN CLOSE BY AS THEY GO THOUGH THESE DIFFICULT TIMES.
