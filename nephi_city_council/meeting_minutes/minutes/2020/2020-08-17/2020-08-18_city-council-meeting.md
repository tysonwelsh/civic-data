Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then City Recorder
Lisa Brough offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Trade and Tech Sterling Scholar Gentry Jones. Gentry spoke
about his accomplishments and projects that he completed for the sterling scholar contest. Councilor
Jones presented him with a scout book owned by his great-great-grandfather and a missionary journal
of his great-grandfather Alma Jones.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Ostler moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the minutes of
the regular council meeting held on 8-4-20, the work session minutes 8-4-20, the work session 8-11-20,
a list of claims on the warrant register dated 8-18-20, and the sale of burial rights to Mark and Jodee
Worthington. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

OHV REGULATIONS ORDINANCE DRAFT AND GOLF CART REGULATIONS ORDINANCE DRAFT DISCUSSED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council drafts of two ordinances regulating the driving of off highway
vehicles and golf carts on city roads. A discussion ensued about the drafts. Councilor Ostler would like
added to the ordinance that lights are required at night. Mr. Atkinson suggested that the city dedicate a
webpage on the city website to the golf cart and OHV ordinances and directions on how to obtain a
safety certificate. Mr. Atkinson also told the council that an audit of the signage for the OHV routes
would be conducted to ensure that the routes are properly marked.

GUN RANGE AND BIGLOW ANNEXATION FACTORS DISCUSSED:

  Mr. Atkinson reviewed with the council the city’s annexation policy plan that identifies the policies
and criteria that should govern annexations. Mr. Atkinson explained that as the annexation process
moves forward with these two annexations, the council needs to understand the policy plan and laws
that govern annexations.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson reminded Councilor Seely and Councilor Worwood to review the Art’s grant application.
   Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the progress of the redundant gas line on Cow Lane. He said
that he is keeping the Mona City Council updated about the project.
   Mr. Atkinson said that the culinary water project is winding up. The city is going to transfer the
temporary loans to the USDA loans and then begin to receive the grant money. The Nebo Heights water
