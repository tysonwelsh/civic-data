HERRIMAN, UTAH
ORDINANCE NO. 2022-45

2022-169 -ORDINANCE TO AMEND HERRIMAN CITY CODE REGARDING THE
MEETING SCHEDULE FOR PLANNING COMMISSION

WHEREAS, the City of Herriman, pursuant to Utah State Code, may adopt an ordinance
to establish land development or zoning regulations, which includes the adoption of Planning
Commission procedures and regulations; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Utah State Code, the Planning Commission shall prepare and
recommend any zoning regulation amendments to the City Council; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to the City of Herriman Land Development Code, the Planning
Commission shall hold a public hearing and provide reasonable notice at least ten (10) days prior
to the said public hearing to recommend any zoning regulation amendments to the City Council;
and

WHEREAS, a notice of a Planning Commission public hearing on a zoning regulation
amendment was posted in three public places on November 7, 2022, for a meeting to be held on
November 17, 2022, at 7:00 p.m.; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission made a recommendation for the zoning
regulation amendment in a public meeting held on November 17, 2022, at 7:00 p.m. in the City
Council Chamber; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Herriman City Code, it is the responsibility of the City Council
to consider the zoning regulation amendment and Planning Commission recommendation in a
public meeting; and

WHEREAS, a City Council public meeting was held on December 14, 2022, at 7:00
p.m. to discuss the zoning regulation amendment; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds that it is in the best interest of the citizens of
Herriman City to adopt the zoning regulation amendment, which was recommended by the
Planning Commission 6-0 and documented in City File number Z2022-169;

NOW, THEREFORE, be it ordained by the Herriman City Council as follows:

Herriman City Code shall be amended as set forth in Exhibit A, and all other sections and
provisions shall remain unaffected by this amendment.

This Ordinance assigned Ordinance No. 2022-45, shall take effect no sooner than January 1, 2023, after it
has been published or posted as required by law and deposited and recorded in the office of the City’s
recorder.

Title 10 Land Development Regulations
10-4-5:; Planning Commission

D. Organization And Procedure: The Planning Commission shall be organized and exercise its
powers and duties as follows:

1,

The Planning Commission shall select one member as Chair to oversee the proceedings

and activities of the Planning Commission and one member as Vice-Chair to act in the

absence of the Chair. The Chair and Vice-Chair shall serve for a term of one year and

may be reelected for successive terms.

The Planning Commission shall adopt policies and procedures, consistent with the

provisions of this title and applicable law, to govern the conduct of its meetings, the

processing of applications, and for any other purpose considered necessary for the

functioning of the Planning Commission. Such policies and procedures shall be approved

by the City Council before taking effect.

The Planning Commission shall hold regular meetings and any necessary public hearings

on-the-firstandthird-Wednesdayof each month, as specified in Title 2 of this Code, in

the Herriman Community Center Building located at 5355 W. Herriman Main Street,

Herriman, Utah, unless otherwise changed by the Planning Commission as the need may

arise and permitted by law.

a. All Planning Commission meetings and public hearings shall be held after regular
working hours of the City.

b. Such meetings and public hearings shall be noticed and held in accordance with the
Open and Public Meetings Act, Utah Code section 52-4-1, et seq., and section 10-5-5
of this title.

Title 2 Boards and Commissions
Chapter 2-3 Planning Commission

2-3-1: Established

A. Pursuant to the terms of the Municipal Land Use, Development. and Management Act.

Utah Code section 10-9a-101, et seg., the Herriman City Planning Commission has been
established to exercise the powers and duties specified in Title 10.

2-3-1: Meetings

A.

The Planning Commission shall hold regular meetings and any necessary public hearings
on the first and third Wednesday of each month in the Herriman Community Center
Building located at 5355 W Herriman Main Street, Herriman, Utah, unless otherwise
changed by the Planning Commission as the need may arise and permitted by law.
