                                          PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The applicant is requesting Exception Request for a waiver from having to install curb, gutter and sidewalk along
this project property of Lot 1 of Jack Ass Flats Subdivision.



                           SITE & VICINITY DESCRIPTION (see attached map)
The property is located along 2100 South in the newly subdivided Jack Ass Flats subdivision. The property in
question is the interior lot of the subdivision.
The property to the east is in Salt Lake City’s jurisdiction and currently stores jersey barriers. The properties to the
west and south are both vacant lands, zoned A-20 (Agriculture). The property to the east is zoned M-2.

                                     PLANNING STAFF ANALYSIS
Jack Ass Flats Subdivision Plat was recorded with the requirement that “curb, gutter, and sidewalk improvements
are required at the time of any land use approval and/or building permit”. The applicant was made aware of this
at the time that they made application for the Conditional Use request. Although there are no other
improvements along this roadway, Planning Staff believes at this time curb, gutter and sidewalk should not be
waived and these improvements should be installed. Staff also believes a 10 year delay agreement should be
signed for sidewalk so that the sidewalk is installed within that 10 year period, and this applicant would be
required to put in the sidewalk at their cost.

Greg Shultz, Managing Director, recommends to the Council that the waiver be denied, stating “the fact that the
property sits in the Utah Inland Port footprint and is surrounded by other properties that are highly likely to see
development in the near term. Waiving required infrastructure is not the responsible move here and sets a trend
for others to ask for the same treatment. The improvements are required, per ordinance, so if they want to
develop the property, they need to install the required infrastructure.”

Crystal Hulbert, County Transportation Engineer, stated that “Engineering does not think it is imperative for
sidewalk or curb and gutter to be installed for this application at this time…”



                            PLANNING STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Planning staff recommends that the Magna Metro Mayor deny the exception request to waive installation of
roadway improvements (curb, gutter and sidewalk), and that the roadway improvements be installed as required,
and that instead a delay agreement be entered into between Magna Metro Township and the applicant to allow
for a delay of the installation of the required sidewalk at a later time when requested by engineering and the
Metro Township (up to 10 years).

The Magna Metro Township Planning Commission recommends that the waiver be denied, however the
Commission recommends approval of a Delay Agreement for all improvements (curb, gutter and sidewalks) be put
in place with the applicant.
