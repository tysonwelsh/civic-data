   2. ZONING CHANGE AMENDMENT (ZCA) (Public Hearing) Legislative
      A. Consider a request for a zone change amendment to the Atkinville Interchange Area Planned Development
         Commercial (PD-C) zone on approximately 17.86 acres. The applicant is seeking approval to construct an
         acute care hospital and medical center. This property is generally located on the west side of Desert Color
         Parkway where it intersects with Southern Parkway. The applicant is Coaction Development Group, and the
         representative is Jared Cox. The project will be known as Zion Regional Medical Center. Case No. 2022-
         ZCA-035. (Staff – Carol Davidson)

         Carol Davidson presented the following:
         Carol Davidson – The land use map shows this as Visitor Convenience Commercial and it is part of the
         Atkinville Plan. The uses that are allowed are the C-2 Highway Commercial. The use that they are asking to
         add is Hospital in area 1.5. There will be 2 phases to this project. We are approving phase 1 tonight. There
         is a building included in this phase that they don’t have the plans for, they will be coming back for that one.
         The parking meets the code. They do plan on a helipad. They are working with the airport to get clearance
         for that. The areas that they are not improving with this phase will be xeriscape. They will have 2 access
         points on Desert Color Pkwy. The one on the north will be shared with the project next door. They were
         required to do a traffic study with this amendment to see if a signal is warranted at this time. That is one of
         the conditions listed. Pedestrian access is not really shown but staff would like to see it from Desert Color
         Pkwy to the entrance as well as from the Parking Garage to the entrance. They will be required to follow the
         new water regulations regarding landscape. Carol showed elevations of what will be seen from each
         direction. The second phase will have more stories, so they are asking for an allowance of 75 ft. at this time.
         There are a lot of recommendations: 1. Hospital will be added to the use list for Area 1.5 of the Atkinville
         Interchange Area Zone Plan. 2. The applicant will work with Public Works on the Traffic Study results and
         mitigation as well as the placement of pedestrian paths along Southern Parkway in front of their property. 3.
         The applicant will add a pedestrian circulation plan to their site plan detailing increased pedestrian access
         from Desert Color Parkway to the hospital main entrance and from the proposed parking structure to the
         hospital main entrance. 4. The applicant provides a 15’ landscaped buffer between I-15 and the project site
         that includes trees planted every 30 to 40 feet apart. 5. Due to the lack of detail concerning the parking garage
         shown on the site plan, staff recommends it not be included in the approval process at this time. 6. The
         applicant will be able to build the future extensions to the Zion Regional Medical Center to a maximum
         height of 75’.

         Commissioner Fisher – What is in front of the Planning Commission tonight is just the main building not the
         attachment?

         Carol Davidson – Yes, it will be just this main one, even though it is going to be in two phases, it will be just
         this main hospital.

         Commissioner Chapman – So the adjacent 8,000 sq ft office building is not?

         Carol Davidson – They do want that to be part of phase 1 but we don’t have the elevations or anything, so we
         are not even looking at that tonight. I just wanted to show you the whole plan so you could see what was
         going where.

         Discussion continued on what details were provided and what is included in tonight’s consideration.

         Chair Draper – The building won’t be any taller than the poles are, right?

         Commissioner Kemp – It is next to the Moto Zoo site.

         Carol Davidson – Yes, I thought there was another space there but after looking at it tonight it is next to them.
         They will share that access.

         Commissioner Chapman - 30 ft is what is being requested for the height variance?

         Carol Davidson – Yes, that is correct.

         Commissioner Chapman – What is the height of the buildings in that area?

         Carol Davidson – I forgot to bring my sheet, but the Hampton is 5 stories, I can’t remember the height.

         Commissioner Fisher – Did you say the parking for this first phase doesn’t meet the requirement?

         Carol Davidson – They are going to put in the required parking, it will be surface parking. They are actually
         putting in more than what is required.

         Commissioner Kemp – Why do you have a 3-story parking garage so far away from the buildings?

         Jared Cox – The tricky part is that the physicians we are working with want connectivity between the hospital
         and the office building. That is how that took place for the location of the garage.

         Commissioner Kemp – In most projects they would put the parking garage as close to the building as they
         can. I am just baffled on how far it is from the parking spaces on the south point to the hospital.

         Jared Cox – The parking garage is really for the larger second medical office building; we wouldn’t be able to
         meet the parking requirements for the project once the other phase is implemented.

         Commissioner Kemp – Is this the same project that was planned for the Sandberg Property and now you have
         abandoned that?

         Jared Cox – Yes.

         Discussion continued on placement of parking.

         Commissioner Kemp – So you will put in all the surface parking?

         Jared Cox – Yes, that is correct, then when the second office building is built we will need to put in the
         parking garage.

         Commissioner Fisher – My concern is having the parking garage close to the parkway and what it is going to
         look like. I will scrutinize how we will dress that up when it comes through so that it is not obtrusive.

         Jared Cox – We have talked to Carol about architectural features to make that look nice.

         Commissioner Kemp – Also the pedestrian access from the parking garage to the Hospital.

         Jared Cox – The critical elements we were looking for is the Hospital and the addition to the Hospital with the
         75 ft height. If we need to incorporate more things into the site when we come back with the design of the
         office building we are more than willing to work with staff.

         Commissioner Chapman – I am concerned about the 75 ft building if we don’t have any other buildings that
         are that high out there.

         Carol Davidson – The Hampton on the other side is 68 ft.

         Chair Draper opened the public hearing.

         Bob Hermandson – In general I don’t think we have any conflicts with the area of the Hospital. I don’t think
         I have any conflict with the height, but it is significantly taller than we were expecting. Our poles for Big
         Shots across the way have 165 ft. poles. This is somewhat similar to desert modern design. I think we would
         be neutral to the project. The height is probably my only question.

         Chair Draper closed the public hearing.
