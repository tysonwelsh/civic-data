RECOMMENDATION:

Staff recommends the City Council approve resolution No. 21-18 amending the Consolidated Fee Schedule
regarding the fees charged for Subdivision Plat Amendments, Joining Lots or Parcel Boundary Adjustments
and Storm Water Utility Rates.

BACKGROUND AND FINDINGS:
The Consolidated Fee Schedule shows fees charged by Draper City to offset regulatory and administrative
service costs. The fees being proposed for the Subdivision Plat Amendment is separating the fee out into two
categories, one for (i) Plat Amendments and (ii) Lot Line Adjustments Plat Amendments. These separated fees
more clearly represent the intended process. Item F in Subdivisions is a name change that better defines the
process involved with the fee.

The proposed fee change for the Storm Water Utility Fee is a reduction of the monthly amount that the city
charges per ERU. After analyzing the Storm Water Utility Fund, it has been determined that the fund has
adequate revenues and reserves to meet current and ongoing obligations. The current monthly amount per
ERU is $9.00 and the new rate is $7.00 per ERU and represents a 22% rate reduction.

Only the items or sections that are proposed for changes are listed in Exhibit A. Items highlighted in blue are
the new proposed fees or description.
