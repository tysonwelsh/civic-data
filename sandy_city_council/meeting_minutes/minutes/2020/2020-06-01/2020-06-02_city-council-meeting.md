Council Member Business

                      Council Member Zoltanski inquired about Council Member interest in another Virtual Town
                      Hall the week of June 15. She commented on the recent protests in Salt Lake City and
                      the involvement of the Sandy Police Department.

                      Council Member Houseman updated the Council on a six-week summer camp provided
                      by the Sandy Club for Boys and Girls. She also noted that the Central Wasatch
                      Commission budget was recently adopted.

Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Bradburn provided an update on the Sandy Police support during recent protests in
                      Salt Lake City. Police Chief Greg Severson provided additional details about the Police
                      Department's deployment to Salt Lake City.

CAO Report

                      CAO Matt Huish spoke about a recent citizen effort to remove graffiti. Sandy City
                      residents Ryan Rosendahl, Aubrisha Lloyd, Ryan Hymas, Jen Rogers, Kaitlin Sundquist,
                      Logan Hart, Dallas Ramseyer and Kellie Nielsen volunteered.

                      Nick Duerksen provided an an economic update regarding the Shops at South Towne.

                      Deputy Mayor Evelyn Everton provided an update on CARES Act funding for Sandy City.

2.         20-175                 Justice Court, City Attorney's Office, and Parks and Recreation presenting
                                  their FY 2020-21 Tentative department budgets.
                   Attachments:   FY 2020-21 Tentative Budget

                                  Court Administrator Melisse Stiglich presented the Justice Court budget to the City
                                  Council. She noted that the Justice Court may need to hire another judge to help
                                  dispense with a backlog of cases due to COVID-19. Council questions followed.

                                  City Attorney Bob Thompson presented his department's budget. Council questions
                                  followed. Administrative Services Director Brian Kelley added clarifying information as
                                  needed. City Recorder Wendy Downs addressed questions about her division. Chase
                                  Parker reviewed the Risk Management division budget.

                                  The Council took a five minute recess.

                                  The Council Meeting reconvened at 7:11 PM.

                                  Parks and Recreation Director Scott Earl presented information on the FY 2019-20
                                  (current year) budgets for the General Fund and Enterprise Funds. He then reviewed the
                                  FY 2020-21 budget. Council questions and discussion followed. Assistant Director Dan
                                  Medina and Alta Canyon Sports Center Director Jetta Valentine assisted with questions
                                  as needed.

7.         CODE-04-20             Public Meeting: Limits on Dogs as Household Pets
           -5846_CC               Amend Title 21, Chapter 11, Special Use Standards, Section 3 - Animals
                                  (Farm and Household Pets), of the Sandy Municipal Code
                   Attachments:   Staff Report.pdf
                                  Exhibit A.pdf
                                  PC Minutes 05.21.2020 (DRAFT).pdf
                                  Ord 20-03 LDC-household pets regulations 21-11-3.pdf

                                  Zoning Administrator Mike Wilcox presented the code amendment to bring the land
                                  development code in line with the recent changes to the Animal Services Code to allow 3
                                  dogs per residence.

                                  Senior Analyst Dustin Fratto provided instruction on how to comment on this agenda
                                  item.

                                  Cathy Spuck noted that her homeowners association had difficulty with dogs. She
                                  preferred that the limit be retained at two dogs.

                                  Steve Van Maren clarified his comments to the Planning Commission on this item.
                                  A motion was made by Alison Stroud, seconded by Kristin Coleman-Nicholl, to
                                  adopt the proposed ordinance #20-03, which is an amendment to the Land
                                  Development Code and shown in Exhibit "A", for the following reasons:

                                  1.            Compliance with the Purpose of the Land Development Code by
                                  creating consistency and equitable standards in Sandy City.

                                  2.            Compliance with the Goals and Policies of the General Plan by
                                  establishing appropriate development standards for all uses and zoning
                                  categories within Sandy City... The motion carried by the following vote:

                          Yes:    7-   Alison Stroud
                                       Kristin Coleman-Nicholl
                                       Zach Robinson
                                       Monica Zoltanski
                                       Marci Houseman
                                       Cyndi Sharkey
                                       Brooke Christensen
