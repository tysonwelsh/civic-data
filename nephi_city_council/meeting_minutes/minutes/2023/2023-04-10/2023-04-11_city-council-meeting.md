                       Nephi City Work Session Minutes
                                      April 11, 2023
                                        7:00 p.m.
                                       21 E 100 N
                                     Nephi, UT 84648

1. City Attorney Kasey Wright trained the Council on proper use of social media, decorum
   at meetings, and on the Open and Public Meetings Act.

2.    City Administrator Seth Atkinson reviewed a draft of a Cemetery Perpetual Care Policy
     that addressed how perpetual care funds should be used. The following items were
     included as part of perpetual care for both cemeteries: tree replacement, extensive
     sprinkler head or water line replacement, repaving of access roads/driveways, striping or
     painting of directional arrows or section numbers, curb, gutter, or sidewalk replacement,
     headstone replacement and repair as needed. Mark Oveson a resident attending the
     meeting suggested including repair and replacement of flag poles. Councilor Parady
     mentioned including expenses if the cemetery ever had to be moved. Other councilors
     said that would be very unlikely to happen. The council directed staff to bring the policy
     back at the next regular meeting for adoption.

3. Mr. Atkinson reviewed a draft of the Reed Ridge Reimbursement Agreement.

4. City Administer Updates:
       a. City crews are monitoring the spring run-off and are clearing debris from the
          hollow and so far, there is no cause for worry.
       b. UDOT public meeting to discuss freeway off-ramps will be held April 25, 2023
       c. Nephi held the only baseball tournament in the state last weekend and the eating
          establishments were extra busy.
       d. The City had zero problems with limbs causing power outages this past year due
          to the aggressive tree trimming line clearing efforts the city has implemented.
5. City Council Updates:
       a. Mayor Seely and Councilor Ostler complimented the city employees for going the
          extra mile in their jobs. Mayor Seely read a text he had received from a resident
          who had witnessed Cody Hudson going the extra mile as the Solid Waste
          Collector.
       b. Mayor Seely informed the council that they had been invited to join a Zoom
          meeting with Snow College to give input on selecting a new president from the
          top three candidates.
       c. Councilor Parady said that he would be out of town for the next regular meeting.
Mayor Seely opened a Regular Council Meeting - 8:11 p.m.

   6. Councilor Ostler moved to ratify Reagan John and Bekah Johnson as members of the
      Miss Nephi Committee. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed
      on a unanimous vote.

   7.    Councilor Parady moved to convene into executive session to discuss the character and
        competence of an individual. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
        passed on a unanimous vote.

   8. The Council came out of executive session and Councilor Worwood moved to adjourn.
      Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion carried on a unanimous vote.

   9.   Meeting adjourned at 8:35 p.m.
