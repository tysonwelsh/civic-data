64   3.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Mark Skousen’s request for review of Light
65   Industrial zoning for the Hadco-Pacific Annexation, approximately 10.63 acres located at
66   approximately 2100 North 1450 West.
67   Brittney Harris presented information on the item.
68
69   Commissioner Everett requested clarification on the adjacent property and its zoning. Brittney
70   pulled up the parcel map to show property ownership.
71
72   The applicant was Mark Scouzan. Commissioner Everett inquired if this would always be a right
73   of way. The applicant stated that there had not been any discussions about it. Brad Kenison
74   mentioned that much of this property had been supplied back to Hadco from UDOT.
75   Commissioner Kunze asked if the usage would be changed. The applicant replied that it would
76   not be changed.
77
78   Commission Chair Jackson opened the public hearing.
79
80   Ross Carter was opposed. Thirty years ago, the intent for the property had been for storage and
81   maintenance. The noise and dirt were nuisances. Despite police involvement and provided
82   documentation, legal enforcement had not been carried out. Noise studies had been provided, and
83   he felt like they had changed. He is highly concerned about the noise and the impact that it has
84   had over the years on his community.
85
86   Brad Sunderland was opposed. The city had put a Park and Ride near his home. There was an
87   access route near the Park and Ride to Hadco, and one of the most dangerous intersections in
88   Lehi was State Street and Trinnaman Lane. He stated it made him anxious with vehicles coming
89   off 2100 North meeting at State Street and going onto Trinnaman Lane.
     Lehi City Planning Commission                   2                                    April 11, 2024

 91   Mike Bentley was opposed. He stated years ago Hadco had been instructed to stay on the
 92   Southwest lot. Now, they closely bordered his and other neighbors' homes. This was supposed to
 93   be Light Industrial, but when he read City code, it did not meet Light Industrial standards. He
 94   wrote a letter to the City Council about one year ago to address the issue. He logged a week's
 95   worth of Hadco nuisances. The application requirements are needed to apply to the Hadco
 96   annexation. He was nervous about what was going to happen to his house. The dust and the noise
 97   were unprecedented and negatively affecting their livelihood.
 98
 99   Chad Perry is opposed. He echoed Mike Bentley's comments.
100
101   Bradley Doornor was opposed. If he had known about the situation of the noise and dust, he
102   would not have purchased the house. He felt like Hadco was doing the bare minimum to meet the
103   code standard.
104
105   Suzanne Perry was opposed. She wanted to know if it could have been annexed but not with the
106   potential noise and dust. If the UDOT property had been annexed, she wondered if there was any
107   way it could have been arranged so that it couldn't be sold.
108
109   Kathy Sunderland was opposed. She wanted to know who owned or was taking care of the park
110   and ride. She had concerns about the property and how unsightly it was. If it were annexed, she
111   wondered who would oversee it.

114   be.

117   record below.

122   peaceful as can be. Please add sound barrier walls.

133   Amanda Pierce, I live in the neighborhood sandwiched between the Hadco property, 2100 N, and

135   both day and night is already too high. We simply cannot allow the noise level to increase. About

140   Commission Chair Jackson closed the public hearing.
141
142   Commissioner Eyre asked about the annexation because it was an existing use. Commissioner
143   Everett questioned what was considered an existing use, noting that what was being done didn't
144   fit any of the zoning, and it would have to be conditional, especially where it concerned County
145   property. Commissioner Everett did not feel like it was a conforming use. Commissioner Eyre
146   stated that the request made sense before it was confirmed. Kim Struthers stated that with
147   annexation, there was leverage because it still had to go to the Council, and there would have to
148   be an annexation agreement. Kim mentioned that recommendations could be made to the
149   Council for that annexation agreement. Kim further stated that unless the property was annexed,
150   the use was usually annexed with whatever use was already in place, but the annexation
151   agreement could stipulate the usage. Brad Kenison mentioned that sound walls could be installed
152   at different heights to prevent noise penetration at different levels. Kim mentioned that a lot of
153   the noise came from within Lehi City, and Lehi Police dealt with that.
154   Commissioner Kunze asked the applicant if some of the louder operations would be moved
155   closer to the freeway where the annexation would have taken place, which was further from
156   residential areas. Josh Moffit stated he believed some of the dust would have been mitigated by
157   the buildings and some of the trucking would have been distributed by paved roads and the
158   relocation of trucking operations.
159
160   Commissioner Everett stated that some of the problems stemmed from the existing pieces in the
161   City. The annexation agreement would have only pertained to the item discussed that night. He
162   inquired why things had not been put into code that could have been enforceable that night.
163
164   Commissioner Everett wanted to make a summary comment. He understood the consistency of
165   an annexation agreement to help alleviate the negativities that were currently being experienced.
166   If it were not granted, the use would have been continued, and Commissioner Everett would
167   have liked to help fix that. He felt the route to fix this was to make recommendations through an
168   annexation agreement. Commissioner Jackson agreed with Commissioner Everett.
