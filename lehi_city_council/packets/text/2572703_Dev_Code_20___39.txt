  APPLICABLE DEVELOPMENT CODE REGULATIONS

Section 04.060. Criteria for Approval.
No amendment to this Code, or the Zoning District Map(s), may be recommended for approval by the Commission
nor approved by the City Council unless such amendment or conditions thereto are consistent with the General Plan.
In considering a Code amendment, or Zoning District Map(s) amendment, the applicant shall identify, and the City
Staff, DRC, Commission, and the City Council may consider the following factors, among others:

    1.   The effect of the proposed amendment on the character of the surrounding area;
    2.   Consistency with the goals and policies of the General Plan.
    3.   Consistency and Compatibility of the proposed zone with the General Plan land uses of nearby and adjoining
         properties.
    4.   The suitability of the properties for the uses requested and their suitability for the existing uses identified by
         the General Plan;
    5.   Whether a change in the uses allowed for the affected properties will unduly affect the uses, or proposed uses
         for nearby and adjoining properties;
    6.   The gain to the public health, safety and welfare from the existing classification to the proposed amendment;
         and
    7.   The overall community benefit of the proposed amendment.


  REPORT ANALYSIS

Staff recommends amending Chapters 20 and 39 of the Development Code to define the amount of parking that can be
allowed for a Home Occupation and adding the definition of “Child In-Home Instruction” back into the code.

These changes were initiated after staff made changes to the home occupation chapter in February. Staff realized limiting
parking to only off-street parking was restricting some businesses allowed within the home occupation chapter. Staff there-
fore added the provision that the amount of parking for a home occupation would be however much can be contained off-
street plus any on-street parking immediately along the frontage of the residence. We also added the child in-home instruc-
tion back into the code to allow a higher number of patrons to attend, as many child in-home instruction businesses have
a drop off system rather needing their patrons to park.

     SCOPE OF APPROVAL

Development Code Amendments are legislative items and also public hearings. The public can share their opinions
on the amendments and recommend any changes. Ultimately, they would need the Planning Commission to include
the proposed changes in their motion.


     POSSIBLE MOTIONS AND FINDINGS

The Planning Commission may move to recommend approval or denial of the proposed amendments to the Lehi
City Development Code. Any motion should include findings. Possible findings include:

      1.   The proposed Development Code amendment is/is not in conformance with the purposes, intent, and provi-
           sions of the General Plan and its various elements.
      2.   Additional findings based upon information presented during the public hearing.

Narrative – Chapter 20 Development Code Amendment

The Planning Division is proposing a Development Code Amendment to Chapters 20 and 39 of the Development
Code. Specifically, we are proposing defining the amount of parking that can be allowed for a Home Occupation
and adding the definition of “Child In-Home Instruction” back into the code.

This change was initiated after staff made changes to the home occupation chapter in February. Staff realized that
limiting parking to only off-street parking was restricting some businesses. We therefore added the provision that
the amount of parking allowed for a home occupation would be however much can be contained off street plus any
on-street parking immediately along the frontage of the residence. We also added the child in-home instruction back
into the code to allow a higher number of patrons to attend as many child in-home instructions have a drop-off
system rather than requiring all patrons to park.
