12   Mayor Staggs said that he had served on the Council for four years and as mayor for 3 years
13   and that he appreciates the staff and all of the time they have put into this budget. He said that
14   there have been a lot of challenges in the last year because of the pandemic. He said the
15   Council directed that staff have some rate studies done across the various enterprise funds.
16   He said that HDR was one of the firms conducting the rate studies and they recommended
17   doubling the culinary and secondary rates over the next five years. He said this was a difficult
18   budget to try to put together. He said that in spite of the challenges, the City has
19   demonstrated a lot of resilience and been able to maintain AAA credit rating. He said that
20   sales tax revenues have doubled in the last couple of years. He said that eight years ago, the
21   sales tax revenue was $4.5 million and today is $9.2 million.

24   departments which resulted in a savings of 6.5 employees. He said that Riverton has 3.49
25   employees per 1000 residents. He said that Riverton is the lowest of surrounding cities and
26   believes that the City can do more with fewer employees.

29   Stormwater operations into the general fund. He said for residents this means that instead of
30   a $6 ERU charge, they will be charged $1 for Stormwater. He said that the $5 savings will be
31   moved to sanitation and water funds.

33   Mayor Staggs said that the America Rescue Plan will give $350 billion to state and local
34   governments. He said that Riverton will receive about $5 million which can be spent for
35   COVID expenses along with broadband, water and sewer infrastructure investments. He said
36   that he has been a big proponent of improving broadband. He said the City could spend about
37   $1 million to build the backbone infrastructure for broadband and this would save the City
38   about $100,000 in annual expenditures. He said this backbone infrastructure would improve
39   connectivity to City assets and provide the opportunity for broadband throughout the City.

44   reduce the costs on the secondary water fund. He said he would like to see the $5 savings
45   from Stormwater fees applied $1 to culinary and $4 to sanitation. He said that the average
46   annual utility fees per household is $756 which is below any surrounding cities.
