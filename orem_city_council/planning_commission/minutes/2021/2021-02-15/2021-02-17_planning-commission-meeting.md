Chair Cochran asked Mr. Taylor to introduce Agenda Item 3.3:

Agenda Item 3.3 is a request by Development Services for the Planning Commission to amend portions of
Amending portions of Sections 22-8-8(B), 22-8-8(B)(3) of the Orem City Code, and enacting Sections 22-8-
8(B)(7)(b), 22-8-8(B)(8), 22-8-8(B)(9), and 22-8-8(B)(10) to the Orem City Code pertaining to permitted
modifications to Buffered Sidewalk requirements, and adding additional design standards and utility enclosure
standards within the State Street Corridor Area.

Staff Presentation: The Development Services Department requests the city amend portions of Section 22-8-8 and
enact new provisions within the same pertaining to allowing modified standards for buffered sidewalk provisions
for existing buildings, and adopting additional design criteria for the State Street Corridor. The design criteria is
intended to mainly apply to existing C2 zone areas (see attachment 1 - proposed ordinance amendment)

One of the main purposes of the new provisions and amendments is
to accommodate owners of existing buildings that have a portion of
the building within the area designated for buffered sidewalks. Under
the current statute, if owners desired to expand and existing building,
they would be required to remove the portion of the building that is
nonconforming (within the area designated for buffered sidewalks to
be installed). The proposal allows these buildings to expand outside
of the buffered sidewalk area and they may modify the buffered
sidewalk commensurate to the amount of modification needed to
retain the portion of the building within the buffer. The proposal
prohibits building expansion within the buffered sidewalk provisions.

New design standards include:
    Public building access from State Street.
    Requiring a minimum habitable floor depth along State Street.               Figure 1 – SSC Buffered Sidewalk Provisions
    Minimum architectural design standards in non-district
       areas related to:
            o Windows and awnings.
            o Vertical facade articulation
            o Horizontal weighting of materials
            o Materials
    Standards for enclosing utility boxes.

Buffered Sidewalk Provisions
As illustrated in Figure 1, the State Street Corridor Area requires
enhanced buffered sidewalk standards as follows:
      8’ sidewalk
      15’ landscape planter strip
      Trees every 40’
Buffered sidewalks are required to be installed when a property expands
or redevelops.

The existing ordinance requires a building to remove the portion of the
building within a buffered sidewalk area if the property owner wished to
expand the building. Figure 2 represents this scenario.

There are 48 properties along the State Street corridor where a portion of
an existing building fully or partially protrudes into the area reserved for
buffered sidewalks.

Ordinance Proposal - Modified Buffered Sidewalks
The proposed ordinance amendment would allow modifications to the
buffered sidewalk provisions when it projects into a required buffered
sidewalk area. The ordinance allows for two typical scenarios, and one rare
scenario.

Scenario 1: 13’ or More Between Curb and Building
Required:
     8’ sidewalk
     5’ - 15’ landscaped park strip
     Trees spaced every 40’
43 properties could potentially take advantage of this provision.

See Figure 3.

Scenario 2: 10’ - 13’ Between Curb and Building
Required:
     8’ sidewalk
     5’ - 15’ landscaped park strip
     Trees spaced every 40’
Nine properties could potentially take advantage of this provision.

See Figure 4.

Scenario 3: Less than 10’ Between Curb and Building
Required:
     8’ sidewalk
     5’ - 15’ landscaped park strip
     Trees spaced every 40’
Two properties could potentially take advantage of this provision.

See Figure 5.

Building Expansion Within Buffered Sidewalk Prohibited
Although this ordinance amendment seeks to help property owners expand
existing buildings, it also proposes restricting any expansion of that building
within the buffered sidewalk area.

See Figure 6.

Transition to Typical Standard Required To ensure modified sidewalks
connect appropriately and quickly to the normal buffered sidewalk
standards, they will be required to connect with a transition sidewalk not to
exceed 25’.

See Figure 7.

Ordinance Proposal - Design Standards for State Street Corridor Outside
of Districts
The purpose of these proposed design standards to support a transit-oriented, pedestrian and walkable environment that
is planned for the corridor. They would primarily apply to property in the C-2 zone outside the districts.

Building Access From State Street Buildings on State Street are to have at least one public access, or one access for each
leasable space. For buildings longer than 300 feet, a minimum of two public entries are required.

See Figure 8.

Minimum Habitable Depth
Buildings would be required to have 30’ of habitable leasable area along State
Street.

See Figure 9.

Windows and Awnings
Facades are to have 40% completely transparent windows.

See Figure 10.

Vertical Facade Articulation
Building facades are to have a one-foot vertical break for every 50 feet.

See Figure 11.

Material Weighting
Facades are to be designed in that heavier elements (such as concrete) are
designed below brick, and stucco, each respectively.

See Figure 12.

Materials
Similar to the Districts, Tier 1 and Tier 2 materials are defined. Tier 1 materials
are of higher quality. Unlike the districts, Tier 2 materials may be used more
extensively, with up to 40% use on street-facing facades and 75% on other
facades.

See Figure 13.

Screening of Utilities
Utilities are to be buried, wherever possible. When not feasible, utility boxes
are to be behind the sidewalk, and screened with a fence or wall.

See Figure 14.
