2021 2ND QUARTER HIGHLIGHTS
    Internal TNR clinic was set up at the Murray shelter location. This clinic provides treatment,
    spay/neuter surgeries, and care for impounded feral cats.




    With kitten season in full swing. Our Outreach department started doing pop-up kitten adoption
    events. Thanks to strategic marketing and vast amounts of adorable kittens these events have
    been highly successful.



    Thanks to microchips we’ve been able to redeem over 1000 animals back to their homes.
    Several animals we received had been lost for an extended period, some were lost for years!
    Luckily the microchips traced back to the owners and we were able to facilitate joyful reunions.

 NACO AWARD
Salt Lake County Animal Services, Pet Crew Pantry, has
been recognized with a Human Services Achievement
Award from The National Association of Counties
(NACo). The awards honor innovative, effective county
government programs that strengthen services for
residents.
The Pet Crew Pantry provides free pet food and
supplies to individuals who reside in Salt Lake County
that find themselves in need. This program is fully
funded by donations and grants Pet Crew Pantry events
are held monthly at Salt Lake County Animal Services
and are advertised on many platforms.
Since the beginning of the pandemic, SLCoAS
continues to receive calls from Salt Lake County
residents informing the agency of the challenges they
face caring and feeding their animals. Many calls are
requests to surrender their animals. The obvious need
to help people and their pets remains, which is why the
program was created. Animal Services’ goal continues
to be to ease the burden many pet owners are facing by
providing needed pet care supplies. With this added
assistance Animal Services is seeing healthier animals
and fewer surrenders. The Pet Crew Pantry has a
positive fiscal and emotional impact on the community
and their pets.
 HUCK’S AGILITY RUN
One of our newly renovated outdoor
runs has been transformed into
Huck’s Agility Run! Featuring
artificial turf, shade spots, and new
agility equipment. This great tool is
already being well used, working the
brains and bodies of our shelter
pets.
Huck’s run was made possible by
our generous donors who donated
around $29,000.
Special thanks to everyone who
helped make this possible including
the George Q Morris foundation and
Ms. Robin M. Woods for the turf and
everyone who donated for the
equipment.
FREE FELINE FIX
Our very popular Free Feline Fix program
unfortunately had to be placed on hold due to
the Covid pandemic in 2020. Thankfully, the
program is back up and running!
 The Free Feline Fix will be bimonthly as we
  continue to adhere to social distancing
  protocols.
 Pre-registration is required. Each event
  provides spay/neuter surgeries for 10
  males and 10 females.
 Open to all residents within the
  jurisdictions we serve.
