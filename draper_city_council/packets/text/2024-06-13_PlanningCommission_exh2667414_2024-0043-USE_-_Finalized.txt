BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Home Occupation Conditional Use Permit for
approximately 0.66 acres located on the east side of 1300 East, at approximately 13575 S.
1300 E. (Exhibit B & C). The property is currently zoned RA1. The applicant is requesting
that a Conditional Use Permit be approved to allow for a combination cottage business and
personal instruction service home occupation that produces artwork for sale and teaches
art classes.

The single-family home on the property was constructed in 1986. The proposed business
is located inside an attached four car garage. Access to the property is unique in that it is
acquired through an easement on City owned property. It appears to be the remnants of
an older historic access to a property further to the east, which was otherwise abandoned
around the same time the adjacent Bent Pine Cove Subdivision Plat was recorded in 2011.

The home-based business consists of two allowed business types. In the cottage business
portion, the applicants make jewelry and ceramics for sale online. In the personal
instruction portion of the business the applicants hold small class groups of 2-4 individuals
at a time for art instruction. Proposed hours for classes are from 4:00 pm to 9:00 pm on
weekdays and 1:00 pm to 9:00 pm on weekends, except during the summer when the
proposed hours would be from 10:00 am to 9:00 pm. The applicant’s explanation for the
change in hours is that they are both educators and are otherwise busy teaching school
during the schoolyear. Additional details regarding the operation are contained in the
applicants’ statements provided in Exhibit H.

Staff noted in their review the potential for negative impacts from smoke, odor, or gas
emissions from the kiln. The kiln is not very large and the exhaust is located towards the
southern portion of the property. Staff is unsure if any additional mitigation beyond the
location would be necessary.

The other identified impact would be the customers coming to the home and vehicular
traffic. Given the small group sizes, location of the property, and unique access to the
home and business, staff sees this limited increase as a very limited impact, and existing
available parking on site is sufficient to accommodate the increase. Staff suggests as a
mitigation measure that the driveway and parking area for customers be kept clear and
free of other personal storage in addition to providing staff with more detailed parking
layout plans.

Criteria For Approval.

The criteria for review and potential approval of a conditional use permit request is found
in Section 9-5-080(E) of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

        E. Approval Standards: The standards of this subsection shall apply to the issuance of a
            conditional use permit:
         1. A conditional use permit may be issued for a use to be located within a zone where
            the particular conditional use is allowed by the use regulations of the zone.
         2. Reasonable conditions may be imposed as necessary to substantially mitigate
            reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of the proposed use. These conditions may
            include conditions concerning use, construction, character, location, landscaping,
            screening, parking, hours of operation, and other matters relating to the purposes
            and objectives of this section. Such conditions shall be expressly set forth in the
            motion authorizing the conditional use permit.
         3. If the reasonably anticipated detrimental effects of a proposed conditional use
            cannot be substantially mitigated by the imposition of reasonable conditions to
            achieve compliance with applicable standards, the conditional use may be denied.
         4. The following factors shall be reviewed and considered in determining whether a
            conditional use permit application should be approved, approved with conditions, or
            denied because reasonable conditions cannot be imposed to achieve compliance with
