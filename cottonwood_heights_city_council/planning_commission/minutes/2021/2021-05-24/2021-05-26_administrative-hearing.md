20   1.0    BUSINESS ITEMS
21
22          1.1     (Project CUP-21-008) Request from Stephen and Ane May to Operate a Short-
23                  Term Rental at 8140 South Clover Spring Lane, Cottonwood Heights, UT
24                  84121.
25
26   Associate City Planner/Sustainability Analyst, Samantha DeSeelhorst, presented the staff report
27   and stated that the request is for Conditional Use Approval of a short-term rental license. She
28   explained that it is similar to others received and approved for this particular project. She
29   confirmed that it is in a multi-family zone and meets all requirements. Staff recommended
30   approval with conditions set forth in the staff report.
31
32   Mr. Johnson opened the public hearing. There were no comments. The public hearing was closed.
33
34   Mr. Johnson reported that he read the staff report and had no specific concerns with the application
35   and supported the recommended conditions.
36
37   Mr. Johnson approved #CUP-21-008 pursuant to the following:
38
39   Conditions:
40
41          1.      The applicant must complete the necessary steps to obtain a business license
42                  through the city of Cottonwood Heights. Obtaining a business license will
43                  indicate final approval of the short-term rental application. The short-term rental
44                  property will not be considered legal until a business license is obtained.
45
46          2.      The short-term rental property is required to maintain compliance with all
47                  sections and subsections of chapter 19.89 and chapter 5.85 of the Cottonwood

 4         3.     Occupancy in any short-term rental property shall not exceed the lesser of: (a) up
 5                to two adults (persons aged 18 and above) and two related children (persons

18         6.     The facility and its use shall comply with all applicable laws, rules, and
19                regulations, including those instituted by the state, county, county health
20                department, or the City to mitigate the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic.

22   Findings:

24         1.     The proposed short-term rental meets the applicable provisions of chapter 19.89

27         2.     The proposed project will continue to meet the applicable provisions of Chapter
