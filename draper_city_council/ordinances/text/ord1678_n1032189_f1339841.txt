City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1678

On October 21, 2025, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1678 amending
the official Zoning Map of Draper City for approximately 0.83 acres of property from
RA2 (Residential Agricultural) to RM2 (Multi-family Residential), located at
approximately 625 East 11900 South within Draper City, otherwise known as the
Kimball’s Junction Addition Zoning Map Amendment. The complete ordinance is on
file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available online at
https://www.draperutah.gov/
