Recreation Month:

                       Designation of July as Park and Recreation Month

        WHEREAS, parks and recreation programs are an integral part of communities throughout
this country, including Kearns Metro Township; and

       WHEREAS, our parks and recreation are vitally important to establishing and maintaining
the quality of life in our communities, ensuring the health of all citizens, and contributing to the
economic and environmental well-being of a community and region; and

       WHEREAS, parks and recreation programs build healthy, active communities that aid in
the prevention of chronic disease, provide therapeutic recreation services for those who are
mentally or physically disabled, and also improve the mental and emotional health of all citizens;
and

        WHEREAS, parks and recreation programs increase a community’s economic prosperity
through increased property values, expansion of the local tax base, increased tourism, the
attraction and retention of businesses, and crime reduction; and

        WHEREAS, parks and recreation areas are fundamental to the environmental well-being
of our community; and

        WHEREAS, parks and natural recreation areas improve water quality, protect
groundwater, prevent flooding, improve the quality of the air we breathe, provide vegetative
buffers to development, and produce habitat for wildlife; and

       WHEREAS, our parks and natural recreation areas ensure the ecological beauty of our
community and provide a place for children and adults to connect with nature and recreate
outdoors; and

      WHEREAS, the U.S. House of Representatives has designated July as Parks and
Recreation Month; and

        WHEREAS, Kearns Metro Township recognizes the benefits derived from parks and
recreation resources

       NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY Kearns Metro Township that July is
recognized as Parks and Recreation Month in Kearns Metro Township.

            The Council reviewed the following Resolution amending 11.20.050 of the Kearns
Metro Township code to prohibit parking that blocks mailboxes and making other conforming
changes:

RESOLUTION NO. 2021-07-01                                                         Date: July 12, 2021

       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL AMENDING
       11.20.050 OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP CODE TO PROHIBIT
       PARKING THAT BLOCKS MAILBOXES AND MAKING OTHER CONFORMING
       CHANGES

       WHEREAS, vehicles are currently being stopped or parked in close proximity to mailboxes
located within Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”), which is preventing some residents of Kearns
from receiving their mail; and

        WHEREAS, Title 11 of the Kearns Metro Township Code (the “Code”) governs vehicles
and traffic within Kearns, and

         WHEREAS, Section 11.20.050 of the Code prohibits people from stopping, standing, or
parking a vehicle in certain specified areas, except when necessary to avoid conflict with other
traffic or in compliance with law or the directions of a law enforcement officer or traffic control
device; and

       WHEREAS, to the present date, Section 11.20.050 of the Code does not prohibit people
from stopping, standing, or parking a vehicle in close proximity to mailboxes located within
Kearns; and

         WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township Council (the “Council”) desires to amend Section
11.20.050 of the Code to: (1) prohibit any person from stopping, standing, or parking a vehicle,
except when necessary to avoid conflict with other traffic or in compliance with law or the
directions of a law enforcement officer or traffic control device, within ten feet (10’) of a driveway,
on Mondays through Saturdays (except holidays) between eight o’clock (8:00) A.M. and eight
o’clock (8:00) P.M., when a mailbox is located within five (5’) of such driveway; and (2) to prohibit
any person from moving a vehicle under such person’s control into any of the prohibited areas
identified in Section 11.20.050 of the Code; and

        WHEREAS, the attached revisions to Section 11.20.050 of the Code have been presented
to the Council for review and approval; and

        WHEREAS, after careful consideration, the Council has determined that the attached
revisions to Section 11.20.050 of the Code will serve the best interests of the Kearns Metro
Township and the general public.

       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP that:




                                                  6
DATE          MONDAY                         JULY                                         12, 2021

        1.    Section 11.20.050 of the Code is repealed in its entirety and replaced to read as
follows:

11.20.050 Parking Prohibited In Specified Areas

              A.     No person shall stop, stand, or park a vehicle, except when necessary to
                     avoid conflict with other traffic or in compliance with law or the directions of
                     a law enforcement officer or traffic control device, in any on of the following
                     places:


                     1. On a sidewalk area;

                     2. In front of or within five feet of a private driveway;

                     3. Within an intersection;

                     4. Within fifteen feet of a fire hydrant, whether on public or private property
                        or within a fire lane as designated and marked in accordance with the
                        provisions of subsection J of Section 2.28.030 of this code, whether on
                        public or private property;

                     5. On a crosswalk;

                     6. Within twenty feet of a crosswalk at an intersection;

                     7. Within thirty feet of any flashing beacon or traffic control device located
                        at the side of a road;

                     8. In front of or within twenty feet on either side of the entrance or exit of
                        any theater, fire station or place of public assemblage;

                     9. Within fifty feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing;

                     10. Alongside or opposite any street excavation or obstruction when
                         stopping, standing or parking would obstruct or be hazardous to traffic;

                     11. Within any alley, except for the necessary and expeditious loading and
                         unloading of merchandise; provided, that in no event shall the driveway
                         or entrance to any abutting property be blocked or free movement of
                         traffic through the alley be interfered with;

                     12. Upon any bridge or other elevated structure on a street or within a street
                         tunnel or underpass;

                     13. Upon that side of any street contiguous to any school property during
                         school hours;


                                                7
DATE          MONDAY                         JULY                                        12, 2021


                     14. At any place where official signs or traffic controls placed by the traffic
                         engineer prohibit stopping, standing or parking; or

                     15. Within ten feet (10’) of a driveway between 8:00 A.M. and 8:00 P.M.
                         Mondays through Saturdays (except holidays) when a mailbox is
                         located within five feet (5’) of such driveway.

               B.      No person shall move a vehicle under such person’s control into any such
prohibited area, or upon any area not designated for vehicular travel or parking.

                     2.   This Resolution shall take effect immediately on passage and its
                          publication on the Utah Public Notice Website pursuant to Utah Code
