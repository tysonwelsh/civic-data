Phase I Ordinances –  
 
Commissioner Breinholt noticed community error. Mr. Woodward provided a timeline of adoption. 
First step public hearing with the planning commission and recommend to the council after 
compiling changes. 
 
Mr. Woodward went through his changes to the document and advised commissioners to submit 
comments.  
 
Ms. Mauldin went through parks and recreation and natural open space, minimal lot size and 
frontage, height to be brought down for fencing, recreation, lighting standards contributing to dark 
sky.  
 
Commissioner Breinholt asked why the fence setbacks are a foot. Mr. Woodward said front property 
is one foot back from the edge of the sidewalk, 80% already have a fence right to the sidewalk. 
Commissioner Breinholt said he would be in favor of changing that. Ms. Mauldin advised to look at 
uses for parks and recreation. 
 
Mr. Woodward advised to take out forestry zone and could be added later. Agricultural zones, uses 
removed that take up acreage or pertinent to Copperton, may review for future uses. Chart of 
setbacks, instead of minimum or averaging.  
 
Staff and commissioners had a brief discussion regarding residential facilities, conditional uses, 
standards, PUD, family food production and residential, kept zones, accessory structure standards, 
fencing standards and nonconforming, clear site triangle driveways, front yard and side yard 
fencing maximum is 34 feet, bed and breakfast, difference of duplexes and single family attached. 
 
Removing C-1, C-2, C-V, and C-3 zones, acreage requirements, existing uses not on the list, build 
to line, architectural standards. 
 
M-1 and M-2, no difference besides a few uses, identify with light industrial and heavy 
manufacturing, cross out 19.34.070 and applies to multiple zones.  
 
Mixed Use zone, historic district and preservation, design standards and measurement, setbacks,  
 
Phase II Ordinances –  
 
Mr. Woodward discussed administrative and Land Use Hearings. Administrative procedures and 
application process, review process for permitted and conditional uses, amendments to conditional 
uses, PUD standards, density bonuses, responsibility of variances, special exceptions, and appeals, 
use of property illegal for 10 years, approve the special exception.  
 
Discussions on removing some Conditional use approval procedures, ADU’s, bars and clubs, short-
term rentals, and Airbnb, FCOZ. 
 
Site development standards, measuring yard setbacks and buildable area, flag lots, lighting 
standards, storm water provisions, geologic hazards code, removing noise abatement, and utility 
placement.
