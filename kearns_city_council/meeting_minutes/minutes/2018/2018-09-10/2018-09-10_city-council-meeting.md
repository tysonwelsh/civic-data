Unified Police Department (UPD) Resolution

              The council reviewed the following resolution approving the levying of property
taxes by the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area:

RESOLUTION NO. 18-09-01                                              Date: September 10, 2018

                                               1
DATE           MONDAY                        SEPTEMBER                                      10, 2018


       A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       THE LEVYING OF PROPERTY TAXES BY THE SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW
       ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA, APPROVING A 2019 PROPERTY TAX RATE
       IN EXCESS OF THE CERTIFIED TAX RATE BY THE SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW
       ENFORCEMENT SERVICE AREA SOLELY FOR THE PURPOSES OF
       MAINTAINING ITS FUND BALANCE, PAYING INCREASES IN COSTS
       ASSESSED TO THE SALT LAKE VALLEY LAW ENFORCEMENT SERVICE
       AREA BY THE UNIFIED POLICE DEPARTMENT AND ALLOWING NEW
       GROWTH IN ITS TAX BASE TO BE USED FOR THE PROVISION OF
       REQUIRED NEW SERVICES

       WHEREAS, the Salt Lake Valley Law Enforcement Service Area ("SLVLESA") was
created by Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council dated August 18, 2009; and

      WHEREAS, the area of the Keams Metro Township is included in the boundaries of
SLVLESA for the funding and provision of law enforcement services to the areas within the
boundaries of SLVLESA; and

       WHEREAS, SLVLESA has proposed to budget a 3% increase in property tax revenues
for 2019 resulting in a tax rate in excess of the certified rate as defined in section 59-2-924 of the
Utah Code in 2019 to fund the increased costs of providing local law enforcement services and
has proposed to increase property taxes solely for the purposes of maintaining its fund balance,
paying increases in the costs assessed to SLVLESA by the Unified Police Department and
allowing new growth in its tax base to be used for the provision of required new services; and

       WHEREAS, pursuant to section 17B-1-1003 of the Utah Code, a SLVLESA board
member, as the board member requested, submitted to the Keams Metro Township Council
("Council") a report, a copy of which is attached, on the proposed property tax increase at a duly
noticed meeting on September 10, 2018; and

       WHEREAS, the Council allowed time during the duly noticed meeting on September 10,
2018 for comments on the proposed tax increase from members of the Council and the public;
and

       WHEREAS, section 17B-2a-903 of the Utah Code requires the prior approval of a tax rate
in excess of the certified tax rate by SLVLESA from the legislative the bodies of any municipality
and county whose territory is located within SLVLESA or approval of a majority of the
municipalities and two-thirds (2/3) vote of the county legislative body; and

       WHEREAS, before the SLVLESA Board of Trustees consider to levy a tax rate that
exceeds the certified tax rate, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees shall hold a public hearing to
provide to all interested parties the opportunity to be heard regarding the proposed tax rate
increase; and

        WHEREAS, SLVLESA shall provide notices of the public hearing as required by 59-2- 919
of the Utah Code; and


                                                  2
DATE           MONDAY                         SEPTEMBER                                      10, 2018


        WHEREAS, after considering all public input from the public hearing and all other
information available to them, the SLVLESA Board of Trustees will vote on whether or not to levy
a tax rate that exceeds the certified tax rate.

    NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP
COUNCIL as follows:

   1. The Kearns Metro Township Council hereby reaffirms its authorization of the SLVLESA
      Board of Trustees to impose property taxes on the areas within the boundaries of
      SLVLESA including the imposition of necessary judgment levies.

   2. The Kearns Metro Township Council hereby approves for 2019 the budgeting of a 3%
      increase in property tax revenues in excess of revenues budgeted in the prior year and
      the imposition of a property tax rate in excess of the certified tax rate to generate the
      increase in budgeted property tax revenues as defined in section 59-2-924 of the Utah
      Code.

Mosquito Abatement – Truth-in-Taxation

                 Mr. Ryan Lusty, Magna Mosquito Abatement District, stated it has been 30 years
since the District raised taxes and with the annexation by Salt Lake City Mosquito Abatement
District of one-third of its District, the Board felt it was time. This increase would cost a homeowner
an additional $3.78 a year on a $202,000 home. This increase would bring additional revenue of
$500,000. This increase is necessary to adequately protect the public from mosquito borne
diseases. Residents in Salt Lake City pay approximately $20 a year for mosquito abatement and

residents in Tooele County pay $45 a year. Residents in the Magna Mosquito District would be
paying $8.33 a year.

               Mayor Bush asked for public comment

               No one appeared in favor of or in opposition to this request.

             Council Member Snow, seconded by Council Member Peterson, moved to
approve the tax increase for the Magna Mosquito Abatement District. Roll was called,

Council Member Peterson “Aye,” Council Member Brown “Aye,” and Mayor Bush “Aye.”

Closure of a Portion of a Public Street – Public Hearing

            The Council reviewed the following ordinance closing a portion of a public street
known as 5375 South Street between approximately 4250 and 4280 West in the Kearns Metro
Township:

ORDINANCE NO. 18-09-01                                                  Date: September 10, 2018

       AN ORDINANCE CLOSING A PORTION OF A PUBLIC SREET KNOWN AS 5375
       SOUTH STREET BETWEEN APPROXIMATELY 4250 AND 4280 WEST IN THE
       KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP.

              The Kearns Metro Township Council, as the legislative body of Kearns Metro
Township, State of Utah, ordains as follows:

Section I:

   1. Pursuant to 72-5-105(7) of the Utah Code, a portion of the platted street, which is more
      fully described in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference, is
      hereby indefinitely closed to vehicular traffic.
   2. The purpose of the closure is to allow Salt Lake County to create a safe pedestrian
      walkway between its existing parcels for the new library and the library parking lot.
   3. This ordinance is based upon a determination by the Kearns Metro Township Council
      following a public hearing on September 10, 2018, that due and proper notice of the
      hearing to close the street segment was duly given according to law; that closure of the
      street was a preferable alternative to vacating the public right of way; that good cause
      exists for the closure; that neither the public interest nor any person will be materially
      injured by the closure; and that the portion of the street being closed is not necessary for
      vehicular traffic.
   4. Pursuant to Section 14.08.090 of the Kearns Metro Code of Ordinances, the closure of
      this portion of the street to vehicular traffic is vital to the metro's interest in pedestrian
      safety.


                                                4
DATE           MONDAY                         SEPTEMBER                                      10, 2018

   5. This ordinance shall have no force or effect to impair any other existing easements or
      rights­ of-way for public utilities, public franchises, irrigation, storm drainage, or other such
      easements, as presently exist under, over, or upon the vacated portions of said street, or
      as are or may be shown on the official plats and records of the County.

SECTION II:

                 This ordinance shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its passage and upon at least
one publication in a newspaper published in and having general circulation in Kearns Metro
Township, and if not so published within fifteen (15) days then it shall take effect immediately
upon its first publication.

      IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Kearns Metro Township Council has approved, passed and
adopted this ordinance this 10th day of September, 2018.
