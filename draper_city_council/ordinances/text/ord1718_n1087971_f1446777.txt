Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1718

          On June 9, 2026, the Draper City Council Adopted Ordinance #1718
          amending the official zoning map of Draper City for approximately 0.33
          acres of property from RA1 (Residential Agriculture) to R3 (Single-Family
          Residential), located at approximately 12738 S. 300 E. within Draper City,
          otherwise known as the Nielsen Zoning Map Amendment. The complete
          ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and available
          online at https://www.draperutah.gov/

Published this 10th day of June, 2026.
