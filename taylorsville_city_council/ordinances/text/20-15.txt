                  SUMMARY:       This ordinance incorporates the Utah Alcoholic Beverage Control Act into City
                                 ordinances as it pertains to certain land uses. Doing so will provide a more consistent
                                 application process between City requirements and state requirements.
               BACKGROUND:       The proposed amendments amend the land use table to permit a state liquor store in
                                 all zones except NC and LC; require all alcohol local consent permit requests to follow
                                 footnote 7; amend footnote 7 to require compliance with the Utah Alcoholic Beverage
                                 Control Act; and strike the proximity requirements in city code and alternatively
                                 adopts the UABCA' s proximity requirements.
     STAFF RECOMMENDATION:       Staff recommends a positive recommendation of Ordinance No. 20- 15 based on the
                                 findings stated in the staff report.

A. The Utah Alcoholic Beverage Control Act, as amended from time to time, is hereby adopted by this
    reference in its entirety as if set forth in full herein. The Utah Alcoholic Beverage Control Act as adopted
    herein shall be construed to apply only to the incorporated areas of the city and shall be interpreted and
    constructed where necessary and shall apply for any commercial land use seeking to obtain Local
    Consent.


B. The Utah Alcoholic Beverage Control Act states that the act governs alcoholic beverage in this state
    except in those areas where the local authorities are expressly granted regulatory control over local
    issues. Nothing in this title precludes local authorities from regulating the sale, storage or consumption
    of alcoholic beverages if that regulation does not conflict with the provisions of the Taylorsville
    Municipal Code.
