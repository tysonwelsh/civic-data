ACTION ITEMS

NEW BUSINESS

          1. A Resolution of the City of South Salt Lake City Council Approving
             an Amendment to the Development Agreement entered into by the
             City of South Salt Lake and PG Investments, L.C. on November 6,
             2019. Community Development Director, Alex White, gave an overview
             on this development agreement with PG Investments. A copy is attached
             and incorporated by this reference. This project is at approximately 2200
             South Main Street. The rebid of the project came in significantly higher
             than the original bid specifically in the concrete and steel because of this
             the costs needed to be reduced. The developer decided to shrink the height
             of the building to help with the reduction of costs. The original
             development agreement will need to be amended in order to make these
             changes.

              Council Member deWolfe asked which residential units will be impacted
              with the reduction in units.

              Jeff Gochner with PG Investments said the unit count for the two and
              three bedroom units stayed the same. It is the studio apartments that are
              impacted.

Public Hearing – To Receive input regarding proposed amendments to all 2020/2021
fund budgets


       Finance Director, Kyle Kershaw, presented the proposed amendments to the
       Council. After the pandemic hit the Mayor asked the Department Heads to make
       cut backs where they could. Those cut backs included not filling certain open
       positions and with this amendment some of those positions will be funded again.
       The City didn’t take as big of a hit with sales tax as was expected. The City will
       be able to add two million to fund balance in the General Fund. He gave the
       Council an update on where the City is at with sales tax as of September. The
       Mayor plans to still be conservative with spending in the coming months.

       This budget amendment includes hazard pay for first responders.

       Mr. Kershaw presented the rest of the items on the budget amendment. A copy is
       attached and incorporated by this reference. He also discussed some of the items
       the CARES Act funding will be used for. In the General Fund the amendment
       amount is $434,000 and in the Capital Fund the amendment amount is $342,000.

       No public comments were made during the public comment portion of the
       hearing.

       Council Member deWolfe made a motion to approve the Ordinance.
