GP-2-2022
West Valley City
Amend the Major Street Plan to extend Lester Street (5700 West) as a 66’
right-of-way north of 2400 South, add 7200 West as an 80’ right-of-way
between 2100 South and SR-201, and extend 5900 West as a 66’ right-of-way
between Beagley Road (2540 South) and Parkway Blvd. (2700 South).
This application was continued by the Planning Commission during their meeting on October 12,
2022.

At the request of the West Valley City Public Works Department, Planning and Zoning staff is
requesting an amendment to the West Valley City General Plan to update the City’s Major Street
Plan to 1) extend Lester Street (5700 West) as a 66’ right-of-way north of 2400 South, 2) add
7200 West as an 80’ right-of-way between 2100 South and SR-201, and 3) extend 5900 West as
a 66’ right-of-way between Beagley Road (2540 South) and Parkway Blvd. (2700 South).

The Major Street Plan defines street alignments and right-of-way widths for existing and
proposed major streets within the City and is referenced several times in the City’s Zoning
Ordinance. The Plan is used to:
    determine building setbacks along major streets that have yet to be widened;
    identify streets needing right-of-way dedication as part of a subdivision; and
    clarify certain land use regulations relating to:
          o greenhouses and garden centers,
          o RM (Multiple Unit Dwelling Residential) zone eligibility criteria,
          o shared parking,
          o tobacco oriented businesses, and
          o walls for double frontage lots.

The justification for these three Major Street Plan changes is described below.

Lester Street (5700 West)

With the relatively new signalized intersection at 2400 South and 5600 West, the proposed
extension of Lester Street will serve as a frontage road to 5600 West, improving access for
properties between the Mountain View Corridor and 5600 West.

During the Planning Commission meeting held on October 12, 2022, an attorney representing
landowners that abut Lester Street expressed concern about the impact of a 66’ right-of-way that
could encroach as much as 16 feet onto his client’s properties. The existing Lester Street right-
of-way is 65’ wide, meaning the City would only need an additional 6 inches on both sides of the

street to install all of the proposed improvements of a 66’ right-of-way. Responsibility to install
curb, gutter, sidewalk, and streetlight improvements would fall to the adjoining owner in one of
two instances. First, the estimated construction costs for substantial modifications to an existing
site or structure exceed $50,000. Second, the City has a delay agreement in place on the subject
property and the City calls for the improvements to be installed.

7200 West

The Wasatch Front Regional Council’s Regional Transportation Plan (RTP) includes 7200 West
between SR-201 and 1400 North (which is north of I-80) as a 5-lane facility. The RTP also
includes an extension of 7200 West between 1400 North and 700 North as a 3-lane facility. In
West Valley, the section of 7200 West between SR-201 and 2100 South is currently striped as 2
lanes with varying right-of-way widths.

5900 West

With the completion of the Mountain View Corridor and the south bound ramp at Parkway
Blvd., truck traffic from industrial areas north of the Riter Canal could access southbound
Mountain View Corridor by traveling south of 6400 West and then east along Parkway Blvd.,
which would impact a largely residential area. Extending 5900 West to Parkway Blvd. would
create a direct connection for truck traffic to Mountain View Corridor along Beagley Road (2540
South). This connection would discourage truck traffic from using Parkway Blvd.

Staff Alternatives:

   1. Approval of the General Plan change.

   2. Continuance, for reasons determined during the public hearing.

   3. Denial.

5520 South 5600 West
C-2 Zone
2 Lots

BACKGROUND

      Brent Malili is requesting preliminary approval for the second phase of the Carpe Diem
      Auctum Subdivision. The subdivision is located immediately to the south of phase 1 and
      is being platted to accommodate future commercial uses in the C-2 zone.

ISSUES:

      The proposed subdivision will consist of 2 lots on 2 acres. Lot 1 is approximately 1.3 acres
      in size while lot 2 is approximately .70 acres in size. The C-2 Zone requires a minimum
      lot size of 20,000 square feet.

      Access to the subdivision will be gained from 5600 West. A shared access drive coming
      in from 5600 West will be located and shared with both phase 1 to the north and phase 2
      to the south of the drive. The applicant has been coordinating this access with UDOT and
      West Valley City. Public improvements exist along the west side of 5600 West including
      curb, gutter, a stamped concrete parkstrip and sidewalk. Since there is only one access on
      5600 West, a reciprocal access easement is planned across lot 1 in favor of lot 2 similar to
      what was done with the first phase to the north.

      Although there will be additional reviews for permitted use and conditional use
      applications in the future, staff would like to note that there is a gradual slope between
      these lots and property to the north. The site will need to be graded or contoured in such a
      manner to accommodate the grade difference.


STAFF ALTERNATIVES:

      a resolution of staff concerns noted in the analysis.

C-21-22
Golden Spike Restaurants, LLC (Taco Bell)
1814-38 West 4100 South
C-2 Zone, 1.01 acres

The applicant, Golden Spike Restaurants, LLC, is requesting conditional use approval for a Taco
Bell fast food restaurant with a drive-thru at 1814-38 West 4100 South. The zoning for this
property is C-2, General Commercial and the General Plan designation is General Commercial.
The surrounding zone is C-2, general commercial and RM, multi-unit dwelling residential. The
surrounding uses include the Sage Valley Apartments to the north, there will be a newly
constructed Medical Plaza to the east as well as an existing CVS Pharmacy. The southern
boundary is Taylorsville City and there is a commercial shopping center on that property.

The building is proposed to be 2,500 sqft restaurant with drive-thru. The building will be
constructed of a hardi-board masonry siding product and must comply with the Commercial
Design Standards.

For landscaping, there is a Development Agreement for the overall site which requires that the
streetscape setback be constructed to match the CVS on the corner of 4100 South and Redwood.
Having this design continued along this entire frontage will help to create a more unified
streetscape for the overall project. Therefore, tree boxes will need to be installed and
incorporated into a berm along the frontage which must also comply with the 7-10, high image
arterials standards. Trees and landscaping are also required along the interior of the site/parking
lot as outlined in Title 7 with a minimum landscaping percentage of 15% for the parcel.
Additional screening of the drive-thru area must also be provided.

The Development Agreement also states a pedestrian connection must be provided to the north to
connect to the adjacent apartment development. These have been installed but the exact location
needs to be coordinated on this site plan as well as the adjacent Medical Plaza plans. The
sidewalk and park strip design along 4100 South will also be reviewed to ensure that the correct
cross section is provided along the entire frontage of this development.

Signage for this use is proposed on the walls of the building and they have not provided any
information for proposed ground signage. All proposed signage for this project must comply
with the Sign Ordinance.

Staff Alternatives

Approval subject to any issues raised at the public hearing as well as the following conditions:

    1. The building must be completed per the approved plans and be designed in accordance
       with the Commercial Design Standards.
    2. The site shall be landscaped per the approved plans and 4100 South shall be designed
       per 7-10 and must also match the adjacent CVS property per the Development
       Agreement.
    3. The required pedestrian connections shall be provided per Title 7 and the
       Development Agreement.

    4. There must be adequate parking for the proposed use.

    5. All signage must comply with the West Valley City Sign Ordinance.

    6. Must meet requirements of all affected departments and agencies.

    7. Subject to review upon valid complaint.

C-22-2022
J & J Motors
2875 South Redwood Road
C-2 Zone (.55 Acres)

The applicant, J & J Motors, is requesting a conditional use amendment for auto sales at 2875
South Redwood Road. This property is zoned C-2, General Commercial. The West Valley City
General Plan designates this area as General Commercial. The surrounding zone is R-1-
6/Residential to the east and C-2 on all remaining sides. The surrounding uses include residential
to the east and mixed retail uses on the other sides.

This business originally received conditional use approval for auto service and sales were
prohibited in 2012 under C-28-2012. There had been some previous enforcement issues at this
location prior to this tenant occupying the site so those conditions of approval were restrictive to
help alleviate the pattern of use at this location. The applicant has continued to operate from this
location since that time but has now decided to relocate the repair portion of the business. The
site will now be used for retail tire sales (no service or installation) and they would also like to
add auto sales. The request was initially submitted as a business license review which then
triggered review of the site and the original conditional use and prior restrictions. Therefore, the
amendment process was triggered.

The site itself is in need of some maintenance. As part of the licensing review they have received
a list of requirements including parking, surfacing, striping, signage, landscaping, general
building maintenance and dumpster enclosures. That information is also included in the packet.

The area for display of vehicles will be along the south side adjacent to the commercial use. All
required customer and employee parking areas will be maintained adjacent to the building. The
landscaped areas must comply with Title 7 and be planted with trees and 50% live plant material.
So additional landscaping will be added to the site to comply with this requirement.

There is an existing restaurant tenant that occupies the north side of this building. They will
remain and their parking area is separate from this part of the business. As mentioned there will
be no auto service uses at this site upon this change of use so there will also be no outside storage
permitted on this site. All vehicles will be in working order and available for sale.

The east side of the site that is adjacent to residential will remain undeveloped and unused. The
area must be maintained free of trash and overgrown weeds. There is an existing masonry wall
and the section to the south will need to be extended to 6’ along the occupied portion of the site.

There are several places where the surfacing is in need of replacement and the exterior of the
overall building is also in need of some maintenance and has several areas with peeling paint.
These issues will all need to be addressed before the business license can be issued for the new
use.

Staff Alternatives:

Approval, subject to the resolution of any concerns raised at the public hearing as well as the
following:

            1. The approved use for this application is for tire sales and auto sales and does not
               include any auto service related uses.

            2. There shall be no outside storage permitted on site which includes tires or
               inoperable/junk vehicles.
              3. Landscaping must be repaired and replaced including the buffer adjacent to the
                 commercial site to the south with 1 tree every 60’ and 50% live plant material
                 coverage.

              4. The exterior of the building must be maintained and repaired.

              5. All vehicle parking, display and maneuvering areas must have proper hard
                 surfacing that is maintained.

              6. The undeveloped portion of the site must remain free of any use until that portion
                 of the site is developed accordingly. That area must be maintained free of garbage
                 and overgrown weeds.

              7. Dumpsters must be located within the proper masonry enclosures and on proper
                 surfacing.

              8. A business license must be issued for all uses on the property and all
                 requirements outlined in that inspection process must be completed.

              9. Must meet the requirements of all affected departments and agencies including
                 but not limited to the West Valley City Fire Department.

              10. Subject to review upon valid complaint.


Continuance, to allow for the resolutions of any issues raised at the public hearing.
