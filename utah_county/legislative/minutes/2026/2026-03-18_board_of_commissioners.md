3. APPROVE AND AUTHORIZE THE SIGNING OF AN AGREEMENT BETWEEN UTAH
COUNTY, UTAH, AND AMERICA'S FREEDOM FESTIVAL, INC.

-Ezra Nair, Commission
(Timestamp: 4:01)

Ezra Nair (Utah County Administrator) said we have the opportunity to partner with the
National America 250 imitative to celebrate the 250" year of the signing of the
Declaration of Independence. The funds that are used to support this event come
from our restricted tourism sales tax account. This event attracts a lot of visitors
to the county. Jim Evans (Executive Director of America’s Freedom Festival in Utah County)
thanked the county for their support with using the buildings and the grounds.
This year the parade will have 6 additional floats, and many events will have
some type of educational message or things we can learn about the founding of
our country. He thanked everyone who helps out and all of the many volunteers.
