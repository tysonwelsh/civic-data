March 4, 2025



Honorable Mayor and Council,



The City’s Administrative Services department submits the following proposed amendments to the
2024-2025 fee schedule for your consideration:

During the February 18, 2025 council meeting, both council members and staff discussed updating the
Culinary Water fee schedule; the tier rate structure has not been revised since 2017. Staff was directed
to present two options for the council to consider, based on the current proposals. The goal is to make
an interim update now, with a more comprehensive revision planned after the completion of the rate
study. Exhibit B presents 1 of the two options for consideration, please see Exhibit A for the additional
option being considered.



Option #2

This change adds 3 additional tiers to the City’s culinary rate structure and updates the per thousand
gallons range per tier. No changes were made to the base rate.
