A RESOLUTION OF THE KEARNS METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
CERTIFYING THE 2019 PRIMARY ELECTION RESULTS
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Kearns Metro Township (“Kearns”) is a municipality incorporated under
the laws of the State of Utah; and
WHEREAS, the Kearns Council (the “Council’”) serves as the municipal legislative body
for the metro township; and
WHEREAS, following the creation and incorporation of the Kearns Metro Township
(“Kearns”) and the other four metro townships, the Salt Lake County Council has served as the
Board of Canvassers for all of the metro township elections; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to its past and established practice, the Salt Lake County Council
certified the election results of the 2019 primary election for Kearns on August 27, 2019; and
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Attorney’s office and Kearns’ legal counsel
| subsequently determined that the Kearns Council, and not the Salt Lake County Council, is the
board of canvassers for Kearns pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 20A-4-301(2)(a); and
WHEREAS, the Council desires to certify the 2019 primary elections results pursuant to
Utah Code Ann. § 20A-4-301.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE RESOLVED by the Kearns Metro Township Council certifies
the following results for the 2019 primary election effective immediately:
