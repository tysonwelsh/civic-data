                                  RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                  RESOLUTION NO. 26-01

A RESOLUTION APPOINTING AN ALTERNATE CITY REPRESENTATIVE TO THE
                 UNIFIED FIRE AUTHORITY BOARD

       WHEREAS, Riverton City is a part of the Riverton Fire Service Area which utilizes the
Unified Fire Authority (UFA) for fire services; and

       WHEREAS, the City is entitled to appoint an alternate representative to serve on the
governing board of the Unified Fire Authority; and

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that Councilmember Troy
McDougal is willing to serve as the City’s alternate representative on the Unified Fire Authority
Board.

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the Governing Body of Riverton City as
follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council hereby grants its consent to the appointment of
          Councilmember Troy McDougal to serve as the City’s alternate representative
          on the governing board of the Unified Fire Authority Board until a successor is
          appointed.

       2. This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, this 20th day of
January 2026 by the following vote:
