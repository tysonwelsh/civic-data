City Planner Maloy informed the Commission the Council approved the five-acre Darger rezone. Council
added a zoning condition requiring half acre lots for the properties abutting unincorporated High-Country
Estates.


City Planner Maloy mentioned the PC may choose to change the order of the agenda and move item 3.2 to
after item 4.3 with a motion and vote during the regular meeting. He also stated Lee’s grocery was anxious to
move forward. If more information was needed the Commission may consider the option to grant approval
subject to further review of sections of the application.


City Engineer Jonathan Bowers presented the engineering standards update. He directed their attention to the
summary list and stated the engineering standards were last updated in 2011. Main factors of modification
included reorganization of the document structure, an audit for overlap, gaps, or redundancy, and
conformation to new state requirements. Floodplain development permits were added as requested by FEMA.
Standard drawings were updated with new logos. Requests from staff, Council and the Commission were
incorporated into the update. The updated workflow charts were presented. New standards provided essential
infrastructure definitions for completed water systems and all-weather roadway surfaces. Future plans included
designation of a standards committee to oversee yearly updates.

The Commissioners offered the organization made more sense and thanked the engineering department for
their efforts. The final update would be available online. City Engineer Bowers asked the Commission to reach
out later with any comments or recommendations. Light brightness standards, light pollution, and dark sky
initiatives were briefly discussed.

Planning Commission work meeting adjourned by consensus at 6:30 p.m.
