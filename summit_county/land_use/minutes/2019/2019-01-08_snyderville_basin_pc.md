1. General Public Input  
 
The public hearing was opened.  No comments were made and the public hearing was 
closed. 
 
2. Public hearing and possible action regarding a Plat Amendment to the platted 
building envelope on Lot 20 of the Trails at Jeremy Ranch Subdivision; 8682 N. 
Trails Dr; TJR-20; Jamie Brotherton, applicant– Sean Lewis, County Planner     
 
Planner Lewis said the applicant owns Lot 20 of the Trails at Jeremy Ranch subdivision.   
This lot has a building envelope.  A slide was displayed of the lot showing the building 
pad.  There is an existing house.  The property owners wish to build a deck on their 
property.  Exhibit C found in the Staff Report shows what they are trying to accomplish.  
The existing building envelope and the requested location of the deck were pointed out.

Planner Lewis said this is a minimal plat amendment.  By moving the building envelope, 
a grove of trees will be saved.  The proposed area has already been disturbed.  There 
are no slope issues.   
 
Planner Lewis said the only reason why this might not be approved is because of the 
impacts to the neighbor’s view; however, only a small corner of the view shed will be 
impacted.  Approval will allow the property owner to build an attached deck.          
 
The public hearing was opened.  
Jamie Brotherton said he is one of the property owners.  He sent a letter to Planner 
Lewis from the property owner of Lot 19.  This letter states the neighbor is in support 
of the amendment.  Chair Peck requested the letter be made part of the record and 
requested Planner Lewis to read the letter so the Commission can hear the comments.  
This was done.   
 
The public hearing was closed.  
 
COMMISSION COMMENTS AND QUESTIONS          
 
The Commission had no comments or questions.   
 
Commissioner Harte made a motion to approve the plat amendment as outlined in 
the Staff Report (and below.)  Commissioner Fine seconded the motion.   
 
 MOTION CARRIED (7-0)

DRC UPDATES 
 
The Kimball Junction Neighborhood Plan was discussed.  Commissioner Stevens said 
the committee will be forwarding the plan to the Planning Commission soon.  Director 
Putt said it will come to the Commission at the next meeting for a work session.  The 
design review committee will attend in order to be part of the discussion.   The first 
public hearing will be held in February.

Chair Peck requested the Commission receive a copy of the plan before the next 
meeting.  Director Putt agreed but commented there are some non-substantive 
language clean-up that still needs to be made.   
 
Director Putt said the upcoming joint meeting with the County Council is about goal 
setting and priorities.  He added that Staff hopes to have a new Snyderville Basin 
Development Code by the end of the year.      
 
Chair Peck and Commissioner Stevens said the Newpark DRC has met.  This was a 
project that the Commission formerly approved as the Newpark Commons.  
Commissioner Stevens reported that there will be major changes to the entire project.  
There will be retail on the first floor.  Above the retail, will be 39 rental studio units.  
The building will be a story shorter.  The changes were done in order to improve 
community relations.   Density was purchased from the Basin Recreation District.   
 
COMMISSION ITEMS 
 
Chair Peck asked who can appeal a Planning Commission decision.  Attorney Thomas 
said it can be anyone who has a specific injury from a decision that was made.  This has 
to be something that is specific to the person and different than to the general public.   
 
A subcommittee was chosen to work on the Snyderville Basin Code Amendments.  Chair 
Peck appointed Commissioner Dickey, Commissioner Fine, and Commissioner Cooke to 
be on the committee.
