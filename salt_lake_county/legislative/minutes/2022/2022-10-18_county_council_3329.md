3. REPORT OF ELECTED OFFICIALS:
3.1. Council Members
Council Member Alvord  stated he recently attended the second annual Great Salt 
Lake Summit hosted by Utah House Speaker Brad Wilson.  It took place in Weber 
County and was well attended.  The State is focusing on some new technology and 
farming techniques that use less water.  
Council Member Stringham  stated she was able to attend the Governor ’s One 
Utah Summit, which took place at Southern Utah University (SUU).  It was well 
attended, and there was a lot of good information given.   
Council Member Newton  stated the Governor ’s Office and SUU recently started 
a Utah Rural Summit.  She represented the County last year, but Council Member 
Stringham will take over this year.  Most participants are county staff or elected 
officials, and it is a great opportunity to learn about budgeting, government 
relations, networking, etc. 
Council Member Newton also reported on her work with Circle Salt Lake, which

3.2. Other Elected Officials
3.3. Mayor
Ms. Kerri Nakamura , Chief of Staff, Mayor ’s Office, stated Salt Lake County 
has been officially recertified by Welcoming America as an official Welcoming 
County for another three years.  The program was launched in 2017, and the 
standard for becoming certified is set high.   Salt Lake County was the first in the 
nation to be certified in 2018.  Joseph Genda, New American Refugee Liaison, 
Mayor’s Office, has led a vigorous audit process to evaluate Salt Lake County ’s 
compliance with the welcoming standard.  This is a reaffirmation of the County ’s 
commitment to supporting all residents, including immigrants and refugees.

RESOLUTION NO. 6030
A RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY 
COUNCIL DECLARING SURPLUS REAL PROPERTY 
AND APPROVING THE CONVEYANCE OF THE SAME 
BY QUIT CLAIM DEED TO KEVIN P. LAWLOR AND 
ANNE LAWLOR
RECITALS
1. Salt Lake County (the “County”) owns a parcel of real property, 
Parcel No. 16-08-476-043, located at 1209 East Laird Ave, Salt Lake City, 
Utah (the “Property”).
2. Kevin P. Lawlor and Anne Lawlor (“Buyers”) own a parcel of land 
adjacent to the Property.
3. The County has determined that the Property was severed from 
Buyers’ property and struck off to the county through a tax sale in 1936.
4. The Property is currently the subject of a quiet title action filed by 
Buyers in the Third District Court (Lawlor v. Taylor, Case No. 210904949).  
As part of this transaction, Buyers have agreed to dismiss the County from 
the lawsuit.
5. Buyers have offered to purchase the Property from the County for its 
appraised value, which has been reviewed and approved by the Salt Lake 
County Real Estate Division, and has paid a fee of $100, which amount shall 
be credited towards the purchase price of the Property.  This offer is in the 
form of a Tax Sale Property Purchase Agreement (the “Agreement”) 
attached hereto as Exhibit A.
6. The County has determined that the Property is not currently in public 
use and that the Property has an appraised value of $4,800.00.  Proceeds 
from the sale of the Property will be distributed in accordance with Section 
Page 4 of 7
County Council Meeting Minutes October 18, 2022
59-2-1351.5 of Utah Code.
7. The best interest of the County and the general public will be served 
by the sale and conveyance of the Property to Buyers for its fair market 
value.  The sale and conveyance will be in compliance with all applicable 
state statutes and county ordinances.  
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Salt Lake 
County Council that the Property described in Exhibit 1 of the Agreement is 
hereby declared surplus property.  
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED by the Salt Lake County Council that the sale 
and conveyance of the Property by quit claim deed to Buyers as provided in 
the Agreement for the agreed appraised value of Fur Thousand Eight 
Hundred Dollars ($4,800.00) is hereby approved; and the Mayor is hereby 
authorized to execute the Agreement and the Mayor and County Clerk are 
authorized to execute the Quit Claim Deed, attached to the Agreement as 
Exhibit 2, and to deliver the fully executed documents to the Salt Lake 
County Real Estate Division for delivery to Buyers in accordance with the 
terms of the Agreement.
