6.0   Public Hearing: Providing Local Consent for a Full-Service Restaurant
      alcohol license for Boondocks Food & Fun - Draper LLC

6.1   Business License Officer Travis DeJong reviewed with the Council the Draper
      City Code distance requirements for a full-service restaurant alcohol license
      and stated Boondocks met the requirements.

      seeing no one come forward.

      Councilmember F. Lowry seconded the motion.

Council/Manager Reports – Continued from Study Session 2.0

2.1   Councilmember F. Lowry thanked everyone involved in the recent monster
      truck event. Councilmember F. Lowry asked a question about an intersection
      on 300 East. Staff responded there was no current plan for changes at the
      intersection.

2.2   Councilmember T. Lowery asked about federal grant funding for the curb,
      gutter, and sidewalk on Fort Street. Staff responded the City was in line to
      receive grant funding. Councilmember T. Lowery expressed appreciation for
      the successful Draper Days event.

2.3   Councilmember Vawdrey echoed praise for the fantastic summer events.

2.4   Mayor Walker expressed appreciation and recognized City staff for their
      response to the recent torrential rain. Mayor Walker commented that Draper
      Days was fantastic, and expressed appreciation to everyone involved.
