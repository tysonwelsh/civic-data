                  EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, UTAH

RESOLUTION NO. 20-04-02,                                                       April 23, 2020

A RESOLUTION OF EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP ADOPTING THE
2019 SALT LAKE COUNTY HAZARD MITIGATION PLAN

WHEREAS the Emigration Canyon Metro Township Council recognizes the threat that natural
hazards pose to people and property within the Township; and

WHEREAS Emigration Canyon Metro Township has participated in the creation of a multi-
hazard mitigation plan, hereby known as the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan in
accordance with the Disaster Mitigation Act of 2000; and

WHEREAS the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan identifies mitigation goals and
actions to reduce or eliminate long-term risk to people and property in Emigration Canyon Metro
Township from the impacts of future hazards and disasters; and

WHEREAS adoption by the Township Council demonstrates their commitment to hazard
mitigation and achieving the goals outlined in the 2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Utah,
THAT:

In accordance with the Utah Code 10-3-717 the Emigration Canyon Metro Township, adopts the
2019 Salt Lake County Hazard Mitigation Plan

This resolution shall be effective on the date it is adopted.
