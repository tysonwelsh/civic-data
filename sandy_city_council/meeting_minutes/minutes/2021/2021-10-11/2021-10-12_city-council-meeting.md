3.         21-387                 First Reading: Council Member Robinson proposing that the Council 
                                  consider creating a green building incentives program for future 
                                  developments in Sandy City
                     Sponsors: Robinson

                   Attachments:   Memorandum.pdf

                                  Council Member Robinson proposed to the Council the development and creation of green 
                                  building incentives program for future developments in Sandy City. He asked the Council 
                                  for feedback and direction. He asked the Council to allow the Community Development 
                                  department staff to move forward with building the process for this incentive program. 
                                  Council provided feedback. Staff responded to Council questions. Council did not support 
                                  moving this item forward at this time as currently presented.

                                  Council moved to Time Certain Items and heard General Citizen Comments.

Agenda Planning Calendar Review & Council Office Director's Report

4.         21-389                 General Citizen Comments

                   Attachments:   Click here to eComment on this item

                                  Chair Stroud invited the public to participate in General Citizen Comments:

                                  Mr. Jim Edwards expressed support for the increased pay for the Police Department. He 
                                  encouraged the Council to ensure adequate staffing and pay for the Fire Department staff. 

                                  Ms. Kristen Wray, Sandy City At Large candidate, echoed Jim Edwards comments. She 
                                  thanked the Council for increasing the pay for the police officers and does support 
                                  increased pay for the fire department staff. 

                                  Ms. Sandra Hawk echoed Ms. Houseman's comments on affordable housing. She is 
                                  supportive of providing affordable housing. She does not think the candidate financial 
                                  disclosure statements are easy to find on the website and thinks more information should 
                                  be included in the financial closures. She requested the link to the financial disclosures 
                                  be moved to the home page on the website.

                                  Ms. Cathy Spuck complimented the Council on their support for sustainability efforts and 
                                  thanked the Council for their ideas.

                                  Ms. Kellie Forbes is in favor of tabling the green building process. She supports the hiring 
                                  of additional fire department staff. She is concerned that high-density housing may go in.

                                  Public Comment closed.

                                  Council moved to Item 2 on the Agenda.
