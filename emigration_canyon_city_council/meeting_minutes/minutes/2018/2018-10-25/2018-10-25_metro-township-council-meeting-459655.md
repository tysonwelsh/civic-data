Community Input
Citizen Comments
Paul Brown stated he received a notice in the mail that taxes for the Greater Salt
Lake Municipal Services District (GSLMSD) will be assed for the first-time next year. He is
concerned about a tax increase without other taxes being decreased.
Mayor Smolka stated the GSLMSD is funded by sales tax and road monies that
come from gas tax. The funds are distributed to many different areas; however, there is never
enough funds for roads. The intent of the GSLMSD is to use those funds for road maintenance.
If the roads drop in quality below 40 percent, then the GSLMSD must pay for depreciation and
repairs. There will be a public hearing on December 5, 2018 at 6:00 pm at the Salt Lake County
Council Chambers, 2001 S State Street, N1-110.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Janet Haskell asked for an update regarding the restrooms.
Alison Weyher stated there are no new updates since the last meeting.
Ms. Haskell asked what the next steps are.
Council Member Hawkes stated she has contacted a traffic engineer to analyze
the intersection where the restroom would be placed.
Ms. Haskell asked if any other locations were being considered.
Council Member Hawkes stated there are very few locations that would be an
option to place a restroom.
Mayor Smolka stated there will be multiple restrooms throughout the canyon.
There is a real problem with people using bushes and private property as restrooms.
Joyce Garcia stated the restroom at Rotary Park is not open 24/7.

Emigration Canyon Community Council Report
Steve Borst, Chair, Emigration Canyon Community Council, thanked the Council
for leading the storm clean-up effort with Salt Lake County. Elections for seats on the Emigration
Canyon Community Council will be held on November 6, 2018. The Community Council wants to
be more helpful to the Metro Township Council in 2019.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Police Department (UPD)
Mayor Smolka reviewed the UPD statistics for September.

−−−−−−−−−−−−−−
Unified Fire Authority (UFA)
Captain Michael Conn stated chipper day was put on hold because of the storm
event. The severe storm was a great opportunity to review and practice emergency operations.
Many residents had done a lot of self-activated CERT, CodeRED, and UFA emergency
management. Medical and fire related calls are increasing. Daylight savings ends in November
which is also a good time to change batteries in the smoke and carbon monoxide detectors and
test them. In case of a power outage, make sure there is a plan and flash lights are available in
convenient locations. UFA is in the process of hiring 20 new fire fighters.
