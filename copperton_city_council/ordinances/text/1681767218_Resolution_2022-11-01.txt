                       COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL

RESOLUTION NO. 2022-11-01                                              DATE: November 16, 2022


A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP’S TENTATIVE
                           2023 BUDGET

       WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) is a municipality and political
subdivision of the state of Utah; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton is required to adopt a tentative budget for the upcoming fiscal year
pursuant to Utah Code Ann. Section 10-6-111; and

       WHEREAS, Copperton prepared a budget for the upcoming 2023 fiscal year and desires
to adopt it as its tentative 2023 budget.

        NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Copperton Metro Township Council:

        1.     The attached budget is hereby adopted as Copperton’s tentative budget for the 2023
fiscal year.

        2.     The Copperton Council will hold a public hearing on December 21, 2022, at 6:03
p.m. at the Bingham Lion’s Club, 8725 W. Hillcrest Street, Copperton, Utah 84006, to receive
public comments on the tentative 2023 Copperton budget.

        3.     Notice of the public hearing will be posted pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-113.

       4.        A copy of this tentative budget shall be made available for public inspection for a
period of at least ten days prior to the public hearing, pursuant to Utah Code Ann. § 10-6-112.

        5.      This resolution shall take effect upon its adoption.
