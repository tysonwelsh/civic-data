MEMO
To: Planning Commission
From: Jennifer Jastremsky, AICP
Date: 2026-03-12
Re: Public Hearing: Kimballs Junction Development Agreement Amendment Request
(Legislative Item)


Comments:


This application is a request for approval of a Development Agreement Amendment for
approximately 21 acres located on the south side of Kimballs Ln, at approximately 632 E
Kimballs Ln. The property is currently zoned RM2. The applicant is requesting the City
review and adopt design standards and a concept site plan for the property.



Findings for Approval:



  1. The Development Agreement Amendment complies with DCMC Section 9-5-200(C)
     and (D).
  2. The Development Agreement Amendment substantially complies with the intent of
     the Kimballs Lane Station Area Plan.


Findings for Denial:



  1. The Development Agreement Amendment does not substantially comply with the
     intent of the Kimballs Lane Station Area Plan.
