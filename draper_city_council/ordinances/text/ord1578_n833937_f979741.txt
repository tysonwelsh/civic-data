City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption –

On May 16, 2023, Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1578, amending Section
3-5-100(E) of Draper City Municipal Code regarding appointments to the Traverse
Ridge Special Services District (TRSSD) Administrative Control Board. The complete
ordinance is on file at the Draper City Recorder’s Office and online at
www.draperutah.gov. Published this 18th day of May 2023. Laura Oscarson, Draper
City Recorder.
