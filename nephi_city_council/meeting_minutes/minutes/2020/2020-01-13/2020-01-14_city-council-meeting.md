                                       Meeting Minutes
                                     Work Session 1-14-20
                                           7:00 p.m.
                                  Nephi City Council Chambers



1. City Administrator Seth Atkinson explained that he and Councilor Memmott would be leaving around
   7:15 to attend the Mona City Council meeting to discuss a pavement cut permit needed for the
   installation of the redundant natural gas pipeline project.

2.   The council heard department head budget presentations from the following departments: Library,
     Police, Sewer/Water, and Streets.

3. The council reviewed the year-to-date financial reports.

4. Mr. Atkinson and Councilor Memmott returned around 9:00. Mr. Atkinson reported to the council
   about Utah State University wanting the city to participate in a Community Well-Being Survey.

5. Mr. Atkinson reported to the council that Joe Humphries is interested in annexing some property
   west of the city on Highway 132. He indicated that some of the proposed property is Level 1 on the
   annexation map and a portion of the property is Level 3 on the map.

6. Councilor Memmott reported on the visit to the Mona City council meeting.

7. Meeting adjourned at 9:56 p.m.


Present: Mayor Glade R. Nielson, Justin Seely, Kent Jones, Larry Ostler, Nathan Memmott, Skip
Worwood, Kasey Wright, Seth Atkinson, Lisa Brough, Ruth Bonzo, Debbie Hall, Justin Blackett, Michael
Morgan, Ed Park, and Gerratt Bethers.
