BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Final Subdivision Plat for approximately 4.789 acres
located at approximately 820 W 11450 S (Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RM1, CN, and
RA2. The applicant is requesting that a Final Subdivision Plat be approved to allow for the development
of the currently vacant site into 30 townhome units and a 1 acre parcel for commercial use.

On February 21, 2019 the Planning Commission approved a site plan on the property for 30 townhome
units and an office building. The Planning Commission then approved a preliminary subdivision plat on
July 25, 2019. An application for final plat was obtained on August 9, 2019. The applicant then obtained
an extension of approval on the site plan approval from the Planning Commission on April 9, 2020. This
extended the site plan approval by six months to August 21, 2020. Unfortunately the applicants work on
the project was delayed due to health reasons and no land disturbance permit under the site plan was
obtained prior to the August 21, 2020 expiration date and the site plan expired. The applicant has since
applied for a new site plan, which will be heard at the December 17, 2020 Planning Commission meeting.
If the new site plan application is approved, the applicant can then obtain the final subdivision plat
approval.

There is a zoning anomaly within the 4.789 acre property. As previously noted the current zoning does
contain a small 0.29 acre, 6% of the 4.789 acre property, section of land that is indicated on the zoning
map as being within the RA2 zoning designation. Staff research has uncovered an error on the legal

On that date the 0.29 acres in question was supposed to have been rezoned from RA2 to RM1. The

Master Project, staff is recommending that the application be reviewed tonight with a condition of
approval that the RA2 zoned area be rezoned to the RM1 zone at a future date.

For reference, the plat shows 6,200 square feet, or 0.142 acres, of land being dedicated to the City as
public right-of-way. This area is located within the RA2 zone. The remainder of the RA2 zoned area is a
small strip of land that runs along the west property line and encompasses a UDOT easement. This strip
of land measures 10-feet to 23-feet wide and is utilized as common space within the townhome
development.

The property has been assigned the RM1, CN, and RA2 zoning classifications (Exhibit D). According to
DCMC Section 9-8-020 the purpose of these zoning designations are as follows:

          RM1 zone is to “permit well designed apartments, townhomes, twin homes, and condominiums at
          relatively high densities that are appropriately buffered from and compatible with surrounding
          land uses.”

          CN zone is “provide areas where convenience buying outlets, having small trade areas, may be
          established to serve surrounding residential neighborhoods. This zone is intended to promote a
          combination of retail and service facilities which, in character and scale, meet day to day needs
          of nearby residents.”

          RA2 zone is to “foster low density development with little impact on its surroundings and
          municipal services; to generally preserve the character of the city's semirural areas; and to
          promote and preserve conditions favorable to large lot family life, including the keeping of
          limited numbers of animals and fowl. The predominant use in these zones is intended to be
          detached single-family dwellings, protected from encroachment by commercial and industrial
          uses.”

zone to the south, and the RM1 zone to the east.

Subdivision Layout. The subdivision plat includes two areas, the townhome portion and the commercial
portion (Exhibit E). The development will be accessed exclusively from Windsor Mill Drive, with a
single point of ingress/egress for the townhomes and a separate single point of ingress/egress for the
commercial development. The townhome portion of the project will consist of nine duplexes and four
triplexes, for a total of 13 rooftops. The 30 townhomes will be located on 3.773 acres of property,
equaling 7.95 dwelling units per acre and is in compliance with the density allowed in the RM1 zone.
Each unit will front onto a 42-foot private right-of-way, consisting of 30 feet of drivable surface with a
six-foot sidewalk on each side to aid pedestrian movement and safety. The driveway for each unit will
meet or exceed 20 feet in length, which is the minimum requirement for a multi-family development. The
property has a shared driveway configuration which will provide ample opportunity for residents and
visitors to park off-street. In addition, there will be eight dedicated visitor parking stalls. The interior of
the residential portion of the site contains an existing wetland area that will remain undisturbed.

Parcel A will be 1.016 acres in size and contains the entire CN zoned property. The site plan shows this
property being developed as an office building. This property will not be part of the HOA that will be
created for the townhomes.

Flood Zone. The subdivision plat shows an area equaling 19,669 square feet that is located within the
Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Flood Zone AE. This area contains all or a portion of
five townhome units. Construction within the Flood Zone AE is permitted by FEMA. There are specific
standards of construction that FEMA will require. The geo-technical report took into account the flood
zone and high ground water in the area and sets construction standards. These standards will be reviewed
with each building permit. In addition, all property owners within the flood zone will be required to
obtain flood insurance, and the proposed plat references that requirement.

Criteria For Approval. The criteria for review and potential approval of a final subdivision plat request is
found in Section 17-4-060 of the Draper City Municipal Code. This section depicts the standard of review
for such requests as:

          “Within a reasonable time following the recommended approval of the final plat by the Zoning
          Administrator, the final plat shall be submitted to the Planning Commission for its review and
          consideration. A courtesy notice may be provided for the meeting in accordance with section 17-
          1-085 of this title, but is not required. The Planning Commission shall not be bound by the
          recommendations of the Zoning Administrator and may set its own conditions and requirements
          consistent with this title. If the Planning Commission determines that the final plat is in
          conformity with the requirements of this title, other applicable ordinances, and any reasonable
          conditions as recommended by the City's staff and Zoning Administrator or on the Planning
          Commission's own initiative, and that the Planning Commission is satisfied with the final plat, it
          may approve the final plat. If the Planning Commission determines that the final plat is not in
          conformity with this title or other applicable ordinances or any reasonable conditions imposed, it
          may disapprove the final plat specifying the reasons for such disapproval. No final plat shall
          have any force or effect unless the same has been approved by the Planning Commission and
          signed by the Mayor and City Recorder.”

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

Staff finds that the application complies with the DCMC and recommends that the Planning Commission
review the request, receive public comment, and approve the application based on the findings listed
below and the criteria for approval, as listed within the staff report.

If the Planning Commission decides to approve the request, staff recommends they include the following
conditions of approval:

          1.         That all requirements of the Draper City Engineering, Public Works, Building, Planning,
                     and Fire Divisions are satisfied throughout the development of the site and the
                     construction of all buildings on the site, including permitting.
          2.         That all requirements of the geotechnical report are satisfied throughout the development
                     of the site and the construction of all buildings on the site.
          3.         The private Ingress/Egress easement, Entry # 11841144 recorded in 2014, is over the public
                     Right-of-Way and shall be vacated prior to plat recordation.
          4.         Correct the month/year in the plat heading prior to printing the mylar.
          5.         Address the outstanding GIS redlines, as listed in Exhibit A of this report.
          6.         Provide a note on the plat that states Parcel A will not be part of the Windsor Mill
                     Townhomes HOA prior to mylar printing.
          7.         That final signed and notarized CC&R documents shall be provided prior to the recording
                     of the plat.
          8.         The legal description error from the 2009 rezone be fixed by rezoning the section of
                     property zoned RA2 to the RM1 zone prior to issuance of any building permits.

The findings for approval as are follows:

          1.         The final plat is in conformity with the requirements of this title, other applicable
                     ordinances, and reasonable conditions have been placed on the approval.
