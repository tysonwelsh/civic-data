COMMISSIONER LEE - ANNOUNCED THE JANUARY 2022 EMPLOYEE OF THE
MONTH - KAREN ALLEN (read nomination)

It is our great pleasure to nominate Karen Allen for Employee of the Month. From the time she
was hired six years ago, Karen has brought with her excellent skills and experience along with a
caring can-do attitude and creative energy to the job. Since joining the HR Team, she has
worked in or assisted every HR Process from the front desk, to benefits, recruitment, job
classification and description, training and other duties as assigned.

Karen stands out in our office as the ultimate team player. She exhibits concern for the entire
HR team. She is always aware of how individuals in the office are doing. She cannot bear the
possibility that any person feels left out. If someone is experiencing hardship, or is not feeling
well, Karen is always there to offer a kind word, concern, a treat or a gift. She makes sure that
office staff are especially recognized on their birthdays and other special occasions—always
contributing to a positive office environment.

We have come to know Karen as a deeply motivated team member. She is excellent, creative,
and egoless when it comes problem solving. She helps wherever there is a need and does not
require recognition. When things go wrong Karen offers immediate assistance in order to sort
things out or to mediate a conflict.

Karen always rises to any challenge. Karen was hired as a Personnel Technician. However,
when Workday was installed and implemented, Karen, along with her regular duties was able
to step into the role of Benefits Manager and processed benefits for all Utah County

employees. More recently, she stepped in to assist the transfer of employees working in DDAPT
to Wasatch Mental Health.

Recently, new challenges have arisen because of increased demands on our recruiters. Karen
has managed or assisted in the hiring of almost 300 employees in a single year. This past year,
with the presidential and other elections the Clerk/Auditor office required assistance in staffing
poll workers. The solution was to have several open house new hire enrollments. Karen made
herself available to participate in these after-hours events.

Karen has particularly shined as she has led the efforts to assist the Health Department in the
recruiting of nurses and other staff during the COVID-19 expending extra time and effort to get
necessary staffing in place for dispensing vaccines.

In addition to her recruiting responsibilities, she has also recently taken on Workers’
Compensation claims--ensuring that County employees get claims processed quickly and
efficiently.

Finally, Karen has spearheaded the development of the new Workday-based performance
appraisal--streamlining process. This has eliminated the need for paper appraisals to be printed
or signed. Karen has also developed a training program for all departments to ensure that
supervisors will be able to use process effectively.

Karen is, in so many ways, an exemplary Utah County employee and we are proud to work with
her and endorse her as Utah County Employee of the Month. ~The entire HR Staff~

1. To act prudently, in a spirit of shared stewardship, to ensure that the past and ongoit
restoration efforts are not negated but are allowed to continue, bringing additional
needed benefits to the Lake:

2. To ensure that any action taken on behalf of the Lake would not undermine the Lake's
responsiveness to past and present restoration efforts

3, To ensure that all future action should be based in rigorous scientific and ecological
understanding of the Lake's history and ecological services and should be responsive
to a robust and transparent public process that includes collective and vigilant
oversight from cities, businesses and communities which use and benefit from the

protections against any developments or proposals that would put the Lake's
