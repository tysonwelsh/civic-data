1)
Roles and Responsibilities of the Transportation and Engineering Departments. Carl Miller, Transportation Director, Brandon Brady, County Engineer.
(4:39 P.M.)
Attachment:

Attachment:
Transportation Presentation
Attachment:
Engineering Presentation
Carl Miller,
Transportation Director
, presented the transportation department's goals and objectives and described their interactions with the planning department to commission members. He presented the department's Mission Statement as well as the 2025 Transportation and Congestion Goals. Director Miller described the Wasatch Back Long-Range Transportation Plan. (4:40 P.M.)
Commissioner Nagie asked a clarifying question about what the commission can expect from the transportation department and what is needed of commissioners. (4:53 P.M.)
Commissioner Young thanked Director Miller for the presentation and asked a follow-up question about the Utah Trail Network. (4:56 P.M.)
Commissioner Jeffrey asked a question about the seven lanes proposed on 224 and how closely the transportation department works with UDOT on those plans. (4:58 P.M)
Commissioner Carmichael asked a follow-up question regarding traffic on 248 and how the department works with Park City Municipal. (5:00 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley asked for clarification regarding initiatives the transportation team and the planning team are working on together to impact the development code. (5:01 P.M.)
Commission Chair Mooney asked a question about which roads get repaired first. (5:05 P.M.)
Brandon Brady,
Engineer
, responded to Chair Mooney's question about road priority and began his presentation regarding the Engineering Department's rules and responsibilities. (5:06 P.M.)
Commissioner Carmichael asked about floodplain updates, who is the lead, and how often are the maps updated. (5:13 P.M.)
Commissioner Jeffrey asked a follow-up question regarding floodplains and the impacts of pavement. Commissioners Carmichael and Nagie asked additional questions about permeable surfaces and the floodplain. (5:15 P.M)
Commissioner Young asked a question about snow plowing and route priorities. Chair Mooney asked a follow-up question about the main focus of Engineering when reviewing a project. (5:19 P.M.)
Commissioner Hawley inquired about flooding during the prior year and who determines the type of flooding and how often the maps are updated.
Commission Chair Mooney thanked Director Miller and Engineer Brady for their presentations.
