Washington County Land Use Authority Meeting
August 10, 2021

The Regular Meeting of the Planning Commission of Washington County was called to order by
Acting Chairman Reed Scow at 1:30 p.m. on August 10, 2021 in the Washington County
Commission Chambers and via Zoom, at 197 East Tabernacle Street in St. George, Utah.
Commissioners in attendance were Frank Leuck, Todd Jones, Reed Scow, Kevin Jones, Slade
Hughes, and Mark Owens. County Staff in attendance were Scott Messel, Community
Development Director; Victoria Hales, Deputy Attorney; Todd Edwards, County Engineer; Laura
Thurber, Planning and Permits Specialist; Kurt Gardner, County Building Official; Kyle Layton,
County Building Inspector.

Pledge of Allegiance led by: Acting Chairman Reed Scow.

Chairman Scow called the meeting to order and explained protocol. He reminded the audience to
respect each other and be mindful of time limits. He explained that in-person comments and Zoom
comments will be taken in turn and that he will be taking notes on public comments to ensure that
repetitious comments will be kept to a minimum.
