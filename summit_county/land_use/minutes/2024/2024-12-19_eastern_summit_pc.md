Public hearing and action regarding a Plat Amendment to amend access easements and associated building pads on Lots 25, 26, and 27 of the Promontory Pinnacle Phase 3 Subdivision, Parcels PINNP-3-25 (4.35 acres), PINNP-3-26 (2.49 acres), and PINNP-3-27 (5.03 acres), located off Daydream Court, Promontory, Summit County, UT; Applicant: Shawn Potter; Administrative Review. Project #24-163. Amir Caus, AICP, Senior Planner.
(6:04 PM)
Attachment:
Cover Page
Attachment:
Promontory Pinnacle Lots 25-27 Plat Amendment Staff Report - ORIGINAL APPROVAL EXPIRED - NEW APPLICATION.pdf
Amir Caus,
AICP, Senior Planner,
presented a previously approved plat amendment for public hearing and possible action. Planner Caus explained the application had to be presented a second time because the mylar had not been recorded within the necessary one-year time period and had expired.
Shawn Potter,
Applicant,
briefly addressed the Commission.
Commission Chair Peterson opened the meeting for public hearing at 6:06 p.m.
No members of the public came forward.
Commission Chair Peterson closed the meeting for public hearing at 6:06 p.m.
Marion Wheaton made a motion to approve the plat amendment based on the findings of fact, conclusions of law, and conditions of approval found in the staff report. Don Sargent seconded, and all voted in favor, (4-0).
