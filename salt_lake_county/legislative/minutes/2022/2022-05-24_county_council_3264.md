ORDINANCE NO. 1897
AN ORDINANCE OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
AMENDING SECTION 19.04.462 OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY 
CODE TO INCLUDE WEDDING AND RECEPTION USES IN THE 
DEFINITION OF RESTAURANT, LIQUOR LICENSE; AND TO ENACT 
RELATED REGULATIONS 
The County Legislative Body of Salt Lake County ordains as follows: 
SECTION I. The amendments made here are designated by underlining the 
new substituted words. Words being deleted are designated by brackets and 
interlineations. 
SECTION II. Section 19.04.462 of the Salt Lake County Code of 
Ordinances is amended as follows in order to include wedding and reception 
uses in the definition of Restaurant, Liquor License; and to enact related 
regulations. 
19.04.462 Restaurant liquor license. 
"Restaurant liquor license" means a public restaurant authorized by the state 
alcoholic beverage control commission as a liquor outlet allowing the 
storage, sale and consumption of liquor and alcohol on the premises . 
Weddings, receptions, and similar events are included in such use.

SECTION III. This ordinance shall become effective fifteen days after its 
passage and upon publication of the ordinance, or a summary thereof on the 
Utah state noticing website.

AUTHORIZING THE CONTRIBUTION OF A LEASED SPACE WITHIN 
COPPREVIEW RECREATION CENTER TO UTAH PARTNERS FOR 
HEALTH FOR LESS THAN FAIR MARKET VALUE.
A. Pursuant to Utah Code Annotated Section 17-50-303(3), a county may 
Page 4 of 11
County Council Meeting Minutes May 24, 2022
appropriate money to or provide nonmonetary assistance to a nonprofit 
entity if, in the judgment of the county legislative body, the assistance 
contributes to the safety, health, prosperity, moral well -being, peace, 
order, comfort, or convenience of county residents.
B. Utah Partners for Health (“Partners”) is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization 
with operations in Salt Lake County.
C. The Salt Lake County Council desires to contribute County property by 
permitting Partners to lease space at Copperview Recreation Center 
owned by Salt Lake County at a rate lower than fair market value.
D. The Salt Lake County Council finds that this contribution will benefit the 
residents of Salt Lake County by providing low cost space to Partners, 
contributing to the safety, health, prosperity, moral well -being, peace, 
order, comfort, and/or convenience of county residents.
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the Salt Lake 
County Council that:
1. The Salt Lake County Mayor is authorized to execute an agreement 
leasing a portion of the Copperview Recreation Center to Partners for less 
than fair market value. 
2. In the judgement of the Salt Lake County Council, the above 
contribution to Partners contributes to the safety, health, prosperity, moral 
well-being, peace, order, comfort, and convenience of Salt Lake County 
residents.

7.5 Resolution of the Salt Lake County Council Approving the Recommendation of 
the Salt Lake County Council of Governments and Authorizing the Execution of 
an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement Providing for the Transfer of $315,000 of 
County Corridor Preservation Funds to West Jordan City to be Used by the 
City to Acquire Certain Property for Transportation Purposes.
22-0484
Attachments: Staff Report
WJC_Corridor Preservation Resolution and ILA
RESOLUTION NO. 5980
RESOLUTION OF THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL 
APPROVING THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE SALT LAKE 
COUNTY COUNCIL OF GOVERNMENTS AND AUTHORIZING THE 
EXECUTION OF AN INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT 
PROVIDING FOR THE TRANSFER OF $ 315,000 OF COUNTY 
CORRIDOR PRESERVATION FUNDS TO WEST JORDAN CITY TO 
BE USED BY THE CITY TO ACQUIRE CERTAIN PROPERTY FOR 
TRANSPORTATION PURPOSES.
RECITALS
WHEREAS, Salt Lake County (the “County”) and West Jordan City (the 
“City”) are “public agencies” as defined by the Utah Interlocal Cooperation 
Act, UTAH CODE §§ 11-13-101 to -608, and as such, are authorized to 
enter into an interlocal cooperation agreement to act jointly and cooperatively 
on the basis of mutual advantage;
WHEREAS, during the 2015 General Session, the State Legislature amended 
Section 72-2-117.5 of the Utah Transportation Code, UTAH CODE §§ 
Page 6 of 11
County Council Meeting Minutes May 24, 2022
72-1-101 to -16-402, to provide corridor preservation funds to local counties 
for disbursement to various cities and governmental entities, as 
recommended and endorsed by a council of governments (hereinafter 
“Corridor Preservation Funds”);
WHEREAS, the Salt Lake County Council of Governments (“COG”), an 
association of local governments in Salt Lake County, requested that the 
County Council approve its recommended distribution to the City from the 
Salt Lake County Corridor Preservation Fund to enable the City to acquire 
property needed for a transportation project considered and approved by 
COG at approximately 5875 West 8200 South in Salt Lake County, 
consistent with the purpose and requirements of Utah Code §72-2-117.5.  A 
copy of the COG recommendation letters, dated October 24, 2019, and 
December 20, 2021, are attached hereto as ATTACHMENT “A” and 
ATTACHMENT “B”, respectively; and
WHEREAS, the County and the City now desire to enter into the interlocal 
cooperation agreement attached hereto as ATTACHMENT “C” (the 
“Interlocal Agreement ”) providing for the transfer of Three Hundred Fifteen 
Thousand Dollars ($315,000) of Corridor Preservation Funds to the City to 
be used by the City as described in the Interlocal Agreement and in 
accordance with Utah Code §72-2-117.5.
RESOLUTION
NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED, by the County Council 
of Salt Lake County:
1. That the recommendation of the Salt Lake County Council of 
Governments to transfer County Corridor Preservation Funds to West 
Jordan City for the project described in its recommendation letters is 
approved.
2. That the Interlocal Agreement between Salt Lake County and West 
Jordan City is approved, in substantially the form attached hereto as 
ATTACHMENT “C”, and that the Salt Lake County Mayor is 
authorized to execute the same.
3. That the Interlocal Agreement will become effective as stated in the 
Interlocal Agreement.
