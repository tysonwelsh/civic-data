Pre-Council, 4:00 p.m.
1. Welcome and Invocation
Mayor Johnson welcomed everyone and noted that all Councilmembers were present. Councilor
Condie gave the invocation.

2. Presentations and Reports
2.1. Presentation by Point of the Mountain State Land Authority
Alan Matheson, Executive Director of the Point of the Mountain State Land Authority, presented
on the redevelopment process for the old prison site in Draper. He said that they have hired a
consultant to assist with creating the master plan and that they want to have a good balance between
business, housing and preserving open space. He said that as they plan this, they will look at
regional plans too so that it can all be synergistic.

2.2. Presentation of the Lehi City Civic Center Master Plan
Curtis Minor presented the new proposed Civic Master Plan to the Council. He said they wanted
to make this area a destination that would bring residents to the civic center. They wanted the
building to relate to the center plaza and create a cohesive feel. It’s intended to promote pedestrian
access and a connection to Main Street. They also want it to be open and inviting. They discussed
each phase and when the buildings could be constructed as well as cost estimates. Inflation was
considered in those estimates. They would like the design of the new buildings to replicate the new
public safety building.

2.3. Financial Report and Update.
Dean Lundell, Finance Direction, stated that the budget is looking good. He said that sales tax
numbers and property taxes haven’t been affected yet by the pandemic. Mr. Lundell went through
each fund balance and informed the Council of expenditures.

12. Consideration of Ordinance #49-2020, a Development Code amendment to Table 05.030-
    C, changing Mixed Use Single Family Residential and Commercial Structure/Project to
    a permitted use in the Mixed Use zone.
    Mr. Struthers stated that they are adding a use that has historically been in the zone and now
    they are proposing to add it back in. It would allow projects where you have a Commercial use
    on the property and a detached single-family dwelling on the same parcel. Councilor Koivisto
    stated that she’d like to proceed carefully and better define Mixed Use before they make a
    change like this. She also expressed concerns with unintended consequences.

13. Consideration of Ordinance # 50-2020, a Development Code amendment to Chapters 16
    and 17, adding a list of potential items for a density bonus in PRD and PUD
    developments.
    Mr. Struthers stated that the current code doesn’t clearly define the parameters for granting a
    density bonus, so this would add specific items that could be considered for a density bonus.
    He clarified that the Council has discretion to grant any density bonus request. Mayor Johnson
    was concerned with granting density bonuses for aesthetic reasons.

   There was discussion of allowing ADUs in a PRD and PUD development. Mayor Johnson
   stated that there are concerns with infrastructure. It was determined that the Council can move
   forward on this item, but they would like to discuss the broader issue of allowing ADUs in
   these developments.

    Motion:     Councilor Hancock moved to approve Ordinance # 50-2020, a Development Code
                amendment to Chapters 16 and 17, adding a list of potential items for a density
                bonus in PRD and PUD developments; and striking Item E4. Councilor Southwick
                seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Councilor Albrecht, Yes; Councilor Condie, Yes; Councilor Southwick, Yes;
    Councilor Hancock, Yes; and Councilor Koivisto, Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
