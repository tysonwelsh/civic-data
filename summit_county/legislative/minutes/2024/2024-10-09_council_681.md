Notification: The County intends to increase the General Fund Property Tax Revenues by $7,000,000 for the purpose of funding County operations. If approved, the General Fund would increase the County’s property tax budgeted revenue by 38.1% above 2024’s property tax budgeted revenue excluding eligible new growth.
Manager Shayne Scott introduced the notification. Financial Officer Matt Leavitt provided greater detail. Council Members asked questions and commented. Manager Scott and Officer Leavitt responded. Council provided direction.

Roger Armstrong made a motion to Grant the Appeal of the Conditional Use Permit for a Nursery/Greenhouse on Parcel #SS-70-11 located at 7550 Lower Bowl Road, Browns Canyon, Summit County, UT finding the Planning Commission erred in not including the following conditions and directing the County Attorney to include the following conditions: 1) Condition #25 from staff report regarding the traffic study with the changes discussed regarding connectors between Brown's Canyon Road and the subject property, 2) the South Summit Fire Department must review access to the property for the safety of emergency vehicles for ingress and egress, and 3) compliance with and attachment of the approved site plan to the CUP; Counsel to prepare CUP permit for review and signature of the Council Chair. (6:59 PM) Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Abstain: Christopher Robinson, Canice Harte.
Roger Armstrong made a motion to uphold the appeal of a CUP for a Recycling Facility Class II on Parcel #SS-70-11 located at 7550 Lower Bowl Road, Browns Canyon, Summit County, UT finding the Eastern Summit County Planning Commission erred by omitting certain conditions; approve the CUP in accordance with the Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law and Conditions of Approval in the original CUP plus three additional conditions: 1) Condition #25 from the staff report regarding the traffic study with the changes discussed regarding connectors between Brown's Canyon Road and the subject property, 2) the South Summit Fire Department must review access to the property for the safety of emergency vehicles for ingress and egress, 3) compliance with and attachment of the approved revised site plan to the CUP and 4) compliance with the Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) permit; Counsel to prepare the CUP for review and signature of the Council Chair. (7:01 PM) Tonja B Hanson seconded, and all voted in favor, (3-0). Absent: Christopher Robinson, Canice Harte.
