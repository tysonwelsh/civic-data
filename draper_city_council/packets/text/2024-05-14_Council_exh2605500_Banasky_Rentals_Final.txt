BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Zoning Map Amendment on approximately
5.27 acres located on the north side of 13940 S., at approximately 132 W. 13490 S.
(Exhibits C & D). The property is currently zoned DC with the proposed zone change to the
CBP zone. The applicant is requesting that a Zoning Map Amendment be approved to allow
for a commercial vehicle and equipment rental or sale use, specifically for a heavy
equipment dealership.

This property is part of the Cazco Industrial Park subdivision. Previous business license
show similar uses at this property including commercial vehicle and equipment rental and
repair of large excavation, utility and trucking services. The office building was built in 1994.
The property has been vacant for some time and there is no legal nonconforming status of

Criteria For Approval
A Map Amendment is a matter committed to the legislative discretion of the City Council
and is not controlled by any one standard. However, in making a recommendation to the
City Council, the Planning Commission should consider the following factors in Section 9-5-
060(E) of the DCMC:

        1. Map Amendments:
            a. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with goals, objectives and policies
               of the city's general plan;
            b. Whether the proposed amendment is harmonious with the overall character of
               existing development in the vicinity of the subject property;
            c. Whether the proposed amendment is consistent with the standards of any
               applicable overlay zone;
            d. The extent to which the proposed amendment may adversely affect adjacent
               property; and
            e. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property,
               including, but not limited to, roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and
               fire protection, schools, stormwater drainage systems, water supplies, and
               wastewater and refuse collection.

Engineering and Public Works Divisions Review.

We have reviewed the subject zone map and land use map amendment applications, proposing
3.89 acres to be zoned from Community Commercial (CC) to General Commercial (CG) in
accordance with the provisions of Section 9-5-060(E)(1)(e) of the Draper City Municipal Code
(DCMC). We speak primarily to the adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the
subject property. In making an amendment, the City Council should consider the following
factors. Accordingly, the following comments are recommended for your consideration:


   1. The adequacy of facilities and services intended to serve the subject property, including but not
      limited to roadways, parks and recreation facilities, police and fire protection, schools, storm
      water drainage systems, water supplies, and waste water and refuse collection;

       Other than noted below, we are not aware of any inadequacies of the facilities intended
       to serve this property.

            a. The change in zone from one commercial zone to another commercial zone
               represents a potential increase in traffic based on the permitted or conditional
               uses within the zone. The proposed zone has a variety of uses that generate
               different traffic volumes so it is impossible to identify a future impact other
               than to note the potential of an increase in traffic from its current zone to the
               proposed zone. Any increase in the number of trips or the traffic intensity
               would be limited to 13490 South due to the location of the subject property.
               It is unlikely that any potential increase in traffic would disperse into
               residential areas or other areas of the city based on its location west of
               Interstate 15. However, the size of the parcel and the proposed uses do not
               trigger an automatic traffic impact study because it does not exceed the
               number of peak hour trips that typically would necessitate offsite mitigation
               measures. Any increase in traffic would be limited to 13490 South, a minor
               collector street planned for commercial traffic. There are partial frontage
              improvements existing on the subject site. Sidewalk is required to complete
              the required public improvements.

           b. Connectivity with the subject parcel to a public right-of-way is on 13490 South, a
              minor collector street intended to accommodate commercial traffic. Access
              locations are be required to meet Draper City Standards and are required to meet
              UDOT standards as outlined in our Master Transportation Plan and will be
              evaluated at the time of a site plan or subdivision application. The subject parcel
              currently has two accesses.

           c. There are existing storm drainage facilities at the site. It is expected that the site
              will need to be brought into compliance with current requirements with the
              proposed change of use. The existing site is connected to existing storm drainage
              pipeline in 1390 South. Any future site drainage will be required to be addressed
              with any subdivision or site plan application, and shall comply with the provisions
              of the development requirements within the Draper City Municipal Code.

           d. Sanitary sewer facilities will be provided by South Valley Sewer District. Any
              subdivision or site plan application will require a commitment to serve from the
              Sewer District that facilities are adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.

           e. Drinking water is provided by the city to the subject parcel. Any subdivision or site
              plan application will require a commitment to serve from the city that facilities are
              adequate to provide service for the proposed uses.

Accordingly, we will provide comments relative to the potential construction activities when the
appropriate application is received. Thank you for the opportunity to review the Zone
Amendment Request.

APPLICANT RESPONSES
**Question 1: What is the requested zoning designation?**
- *Answer:* We are seeking a change to CBP (Commercial Business Park) zoning,
envisioning a dynamic space that will house a very clean and high-end heavy equipment
dealership with an ancillary service repair facility—a venture the city can take pride in.

**Question 2: Please explain the reason for the request and justification for approval.**
- *Answer:* The request is rooted in our plan to establish a very clean and high-end heavy
equipment dealership, complemented by an ancillary service repair facility. This dual
offering not only contributes to economic growth and enhances the city's reputation but
also aligns with common industry practices. Justification lies in the property's historical
alignment with such use, promising increased tax revenue, job opportunities, and minimal
impact from the service repair component. The CBP zoning further aligns with the
surrounding area's land use, ensuring a positive impact on the local economy and a
development the city can proudly showcase.

**Question 3: How is the requested zoning designation harmonious with the overall
character of the existing development in the vicinity of the subject property?**
- *Answer:* The harmony arises not only from the property's historical use and
commitment to a very clean and high-end establishment but also from the addition of an
ancillary service repair facility, a common and minimally impactful practice. The new
heavy equipment dealership, along with its service repair component, will not only
generate substantial tax sales for the city but will be a source of pride—a showcase of
excellence. The proposed CBP zoning aligns seamlessly with the existing character of
neighboring properties, ensuring a positive impact on the city's aesthetic and financial
landscape.

**Question 4: Are our facility and services such as roadways and utilities sufficient to serve
the property under the proposed zoning?**
- *Answer:* Yes, our facility, boasting a very clean and high-end environment, along with
well-maintained roadways and utilities, is poised not just to meet but exceed the demands
of the proposed CBP zoning. This assurance is vital for ensuring the city can fully capitalize
on the economic and prestigious benefits that the heavy equipment dealership, along with
its ancillary service repair facility, will bring.

**Question 5: Is the zoning designation consistent with the goals, objectives, and policies
of the city's General plan?**
- *Answer:* Absolutely. Our proposal not only aligns with the historical nature of the
property but promises significant economic and prestige benefits for the city. The new very
clean and high-end heavy equipment dealership, coupled with an ancillary service repair
facility and strategic CBP zoning, will not only generate substantial tax sales but will be a
symbol of pride for the city—a testament to its commitment to excellence, development,
and industry-common practices.
