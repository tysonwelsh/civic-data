COMMISSIONER POWERS GARDNER READ THE FOLLOWING RESOLUTION:

UTAH COUNTY COMMISSION RESOLUTION IN SUPPORT OF UTAH’S JEWISH
COMMUNITY AND THE STATE OF ISRAEL

WHEREAS, the Utah County Commission recognizes the importance of fostering
strong international relationships based on shared values and mutual respect;
and

WHEREAS, the State of Israel has been a longstanding ally and friend of the United
States, contributing to regional stability and promoting democratic values in the
Middle East; and

WHEREAS, the Jewish community has been an integral part of the cultural, social,
and economic fabric of Utah, enriching our community through their diverse
contributions; and

WHEREAS, Utah County is now home of Chabad of Utah County which was opened
in Lehi by Rabbi Chaim and Esty Zippel in 2022 to serve as a Jewish learning
center, and educate the community about Judaism in a welcoming and inviting
atmosphere; and

WHEREAS, we affirm the importance of promoting educational programs that
enhance awareness and understanding of the shared values that bind the United
States, Israel, and the global Jewish community; and

WHEREAS, it is essential to stand against any form of discrimination, hatred, and
intolerance, including anti-Semitism, and to promote a culture of inclusivity and
understanding; and

WHEREAS, on October 7, 2023, Israel was brutally attacked by Hamas, causing
immense suffering and loss within the Israeli community; and

WHEREAS, the violence and destruction resulting from these attacks undermine
the values of peace, tolerance, and coexistence among all people; and

WHEREAS, the festival of Chanukah, celebrated by the lighting of the Menorah,
signifies the triumph of light over darkness and stands as a symbol of hope, unity,
and resilience.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Utah County Commission:

Recognizes and appreciates the positive contributions of the Jewish community to
the social, cultural, and economic vitality of Utah County.

Expresses unequivocal support for the State of Israel and its right to exist asa
sovereign and secure nation and stands in solidarity with the Israeli people
affected by the October 7, 2023, attacks in Israel; condemning these acts of
violence and expressing unwavering support for peace, unity, and the protection
of all communities affected by such tragedies.

Be it further resolved that as we commemorate Chanukah and the lighting of the
Menorah, we reaffirm our commitment to fostering understanding, empathy, and
a shared vision of a world where light and hope prevail over darkness and
division.

Be it further resolved that we call upon all individuals and communities to unite
in promoting peace, condemning violence, and fostering dialogue and mutual
respect among diverse cultures and religions.

RESOLVED AND ORDERED this 13th day of December, 2023.

BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS, UTAH COUNTY, UTAH
