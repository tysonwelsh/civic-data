Proposal 
On April 25, 2025, the Community Development Department received a complete application 
for a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) for a Nursery to be located at 3487 N Crystal Creamery 
Drive. The applicant states that there is no proposed lighting or signage. The applicant proposes 
a small greenhouse that will have no power or gas. The proposed hours of operation are 8am to 
5pm Monday through Saturday.

2 
 
Vicinity Map 
 
 
Background 
Parcel JBH-5 is located within the J B Hoyt Subdivision, which was recorded on February 3, 
2004. According to Eastern Summit County Development Code Section 11-3-16, a Nursery 
requires a Conditional Use Permit (CUP) in the Agriculture-10 (AG-10) Zone.  A Nursery is 
defined below. 
 
NURSERY: An enterprise that conducts the retail or wholesale of plants grown on or off 
the premises, as well as related accessory equipment. 
 
The Applicant proposes to use the defined area (see Exhibit B) for a nursery space. At this time, 
there are no plans for any kind of retail application, the intent is to provide trees for relatives. 
 
The proposed Nursery would not include any additional lighting or signage. The applicant 
proposes a small greenhouse that will have no power or gas. The applicant also proposes to 
place 2 to 3 connex containers on the site to be used for item storage, nursery equipment, and 
parts and materials for nursery use.

Background 
 
The Promontory Development Agreement was approved on January 2, 2001. It provided for 
885 estate lots as part of a base density, 716 incentive density lots, two hotels/300 resort units, 
equestrian facility, up to five golf club houses, and other accessory uses on approximately 6,500 
acres with no less than 3,900 acres remaining as passive open space. Of the 1901 total units in 
the density pool, 1,520 units have been approved. The development parameters for this project 
are specifically set forth in the Development Agreement. 
 
Promontory has been operating a repository site since the beginning of construction. Because 
approved entitlements are encroaching upon the existing repository, Promontory is proposing 
to move the site further South away from existing development and areas under construction.  
 
Since the Promontory Development Agreement is not clear on what type of permit would be 
req
uired for soil repository uses, the applicant filed an administrative Development Agreement 
Amendment which was approved and recorded in May of 2025 (ENTER ORDINANCE).

SECTION 6: BMPS - POLLUTION PREVENTION/OPERATIONAL 
CONTROLS  
6.1 Spill Prevention and Response 
Spill prevention and control plans educate workers about how to prevent spills from occurring 
and what to do to contain and clean up should a spill occur.  All storage vessels that contain 
liquids that are considered a pollutant (i.e. fuel tanks, curing compounds, form oils, engine 
oils, etc.) will have secondary containment able to contain 110% of the volume of the largest 
pollutant container.  Spill containment basins that are open to storm water must always have 
adequate capacity to retain 110% of the volume of the largest pollutant container, and must 
have a means of completely draining the containment basin and a means to control use of the 
drain port.  Storm water must be visually inspected for evidence of pollutants before being 
drained.  Spill containment kits will also be readily available throughout the project.
