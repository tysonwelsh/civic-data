Item Coversheet MEMO To: Planning Commission
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: October 8, 2020
Re: Grayson Cove Plat Amendment Request Comments: This application is a request for approval of a Plat Amendment for approximately 0.468 acres located at 13187 S. Grayson View Cove.  The property is currently zoned RA2 (Single Family Residential, 20,000 square foot lot minimum) and is part of the Grayson Cove subdivision.
The subject property was platted on January 20, 2017 as part of the Grayson Cove subdivision.  The original Grayson Cove subdivision application was approved by the City Council on July 5, 2016.  The approved subdivision was for seven lots all located on a single cul-de-sac.  This application is a request to modify Lot 1 of that subdivision. The findings are as follows: For approval:
There is good cause for the amendment and no public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
For denial:
There is not good cause for the amendment and a public street, right-of-way, or easement has been vacated or amended.
