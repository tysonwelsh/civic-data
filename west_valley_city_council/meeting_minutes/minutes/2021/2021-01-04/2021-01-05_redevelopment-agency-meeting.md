     APPROVAL OF MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING HELD DECEMBER 15, 2020
     The Board considered Minutes of the Special Meeting held December 15, 2020. There
     were no changes, corrections or deletions.

     Mr. Fitisemanu moved to approve the Minutes of the Special Meeting held December 15,
     2020. Mr. Bigelow seconded the motion.

     A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.

     ELECT CHAIRPERSON AND VICE CHAIRPERSON FOR 2021
     MINUTES OF RDA ANNUAL MEETING – JANUARY 5, 2021
                           -2-

Chair Christensen stated election of a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson for 2021 had been
scheduled for consideration by the Board. He advised a rotation schedule had been used
in the past to elect a Chair and Vice Chair, however this was an option for the Board and
not mandatory.

Chairman Christensen called for a motion.

Mr. Buhler moved to elect Karen Lang as Chair of the Redevelopment Agency for 2021
and Mr. Fitisemanu moved to approve Steve Buhler as Vice Chair of the Redevelopment
Agency for 2021. Mr. Nordfelt seconded both motions.

A voice vote was taken and all members voted in favor of the motion.


MOTION TO ADJOURN
Upon motion by Ms. Lang all voted in favor to adjourn.


THERE BEING NO FURTHER BUSINESS OF THE WEST VALLEY
REDEVELOPMENT AGENCY THE ANNUAL MEETING OF TUESDAY, JANUARY
5, 2021, WAS ADJOURNED AT 7:01 P.M. BY CHAIRMAN BUHLER.
