4. PRESENTATION:  SALT LAKE COUNTY HUMAN SERVICES, HOMELESSNESS, AND CRIMINAL JUSTICE REFORM ACTION PLAN 
 
Commissioner Litvack explained to the rest of the Board that Salt Lake County is trying to pull together teams from across various agencies 
and disciplines to work together on a comprehensive review on a number of systems that intersect in our community of ten, such as 
homelessness, human services, and the criminal justice system.  Salt Lake County wants to look at what it is doing, how to do  an analysis 
of the systems, understand where the gaps are, work to fill the gaps in order to have the system work as integrated as possible but in a 
way that is efficient and that meets its goals.  Part of the goals are to decriminalize homelessness (and crimes associated with homelessness) 
and to redirect people with significant substance abuse into appropriate places a nd appropriate treatments instead of into the criminal 
justice system.  She said the County is moving collectively with Salt Lake City and the State of Utah with purpose, partnership, and urgency.  
The shared vision is to render homelessness as brief, rare, and non-recurring.  They have looked at the sequential intercept model and have 
identified a plan for the next 5 years.  Salt Lake County Human Services Development Director Kelly Colopy and Salt Lake County Assistant 
Deputy Mayor Katherine Fife also participated in the presentation.

then adjourned.
