Item Coversheet MEMO To: City Council Meeting
From: Maryann Pickering, AICP
CC:  Date: June 1, 2021
Re: NCS Residential Development Land Use and Zoning Map Amendment and Development Agreement Comments: At the May 18, 2021 City Council meeting, this item was continued so that the applicant could submit a development agreement to limit the number of single family residential units on the property.  Application for the agreement has been made and a copy of the agreement is included in the attachments to this memo.
The application is a request for approval of a zoning map amendment for approximately 9.81 acres located at approximately 12197 S. 300 East.  The site has a land use designation of Neighborhood Commercial and is currently zoned A5.  The applicant is requesting to change the land use designation to Residential High Density and rezone the entire property from A5 to RM2.  The Development Agreement would limit the number of single family homes that can be built on the property. The maximum number of single family residences is 63.
The subject property has historically been utilized as agricultural or farming land since at least 1978 when the City incorporated.  There have been previous applications to rezone the property and the most recent one was in the fall of 2020.  The fall  2020 application was for a commercial zoning, and there were concerns expressed about that request from the public.  The applicant has submitted this application for residential zoning based on those concerns raised.
A negative recommendation was forwarded by the planning Commission at their May 13, 2021 meeting. The findings are as follows: For approval:
1.   That DCMC Section 9-5-060(E) provides the approval standards for amending Draper City’s zoning map.
2.   That this map amendment does not compromise any of the five factors listed in DCMC Section 9-5-060(E)(1).
3.   This land use map amendment proposes a land use designation that has less intensive traffic uses and therefore would result in a reduced traffic impact to the surrounding area than the current commercial land use designation.
4.   The proposed residential land use and zoning category will provide a buffer from the existing commercial uses to the south and the existing single-family residential to the north.
5. The proposed development agreement provides assurances that the property will be developed with single-family homes on lots of at least 4,000 square feet in size.
