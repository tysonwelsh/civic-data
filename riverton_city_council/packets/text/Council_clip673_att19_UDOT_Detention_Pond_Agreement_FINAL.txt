                       COOPERATIVE MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT


        THIS COOPERATIVE AGREEMENT (“Agreement”), made and entered into this
        day of , 2024, by and between the UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION, a
state agency, (“UDOT”) and RIVERTON CITY, a political subdivision of the State of Utah,
(“City”).
                                            RECITALS
       WHEREAS, UDOT, as part of its project, Redwood Road to 12600 S. (“Project”), has
constructed stormwater infrastructure within and around Redwood Road located at approximately
11851 South Redwood Road ("UDOT Infrastructure”); and
       WHEREAS, City has constructed a stormwater detention pond located at 1536 Laurel Chase
Lane (“City Pond”).
       WHEREAS, UDOT and City have determined that stormwater from UDOT’s Infrastructure
may be transferred to the City Pond.
       WHEREAS, the City and UDOT desire to identify ownership and maintenance of the
constructed stormwater infrastructure and detention ponds.


                                          AGREEMENT
       NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:
         1.     UDOT will continue to own and maintain the stormwater system within UDOT’s
right of way along Redwood Road.
      2.    The City will own and maintain the stormwater system from UDOT’s right of way
(Redwood Road) to the City Pond.
       3.     The City will continue to own and maintain the City Pond, which will receive un-
detained stormwater runoff from both UDOT and the City.
        4.       The City will comply with the required stormwater permits, applicable laws, and
regulations for the stormwater detention ponds owned by City.
        5.      The Parties shall complete inspections on any long-term storm water features using
UDOT’s inspection form. The Parties shall execute inspections according to the frequencies outlined
on the inspection form.
       6.       UDOT agrees that it shall not construct any project or conduct any other activity
which will result in an increase in stormwater volume to the City Pond without the express written
consent of City.
        7.      The terms of this Agreement shall continue as long as the Parties own and maintain
the described stormwater infrastructure and detention ponds.
