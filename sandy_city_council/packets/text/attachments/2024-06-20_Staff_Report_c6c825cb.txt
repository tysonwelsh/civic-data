Request
 The applicant, Corby Dall, is requesting approval of a
 conditional use permit to allow for an accessory
 structure with additional height and reduced setbacks
 for a property located at 27 Northridge Way. The
 proposed two-story accessory structure has an 802
 square foot footprint and is 23 feet 10 inches from the
 front and 35 feet tall from the rear. The design of the
 exterior and roof is proposed to match the exterior of
 the main dwelling. See application materials for details
 in Exhibit A and Exhibit B.

Background
 The subject property is approximately .9 acres (39,204
 square feet) in the PUD(1.62) zone. This zone is most
 closely associated with the R-1-20 zone. The subject
 property is Lot 418 of the Pepperwood Phase 4
 subdivision. Properties to the north, south, east, and
 west are single family residential zoned PUD(1.62). The
 property is located in the Sensitive Area Overlay Zone
 for slopes that are greater than 30%.

Analysis
  The applicant is proposing to build an accessory structure in the rear

  yard that will be used as a garage and additional storage and shed space.
  The garage will be connected to the driveway with a suspended slab
  bridge. The proposed structure is 1,552 square feet in total with two
  floors. The top garage level has a footprint of 802 square feet and 750
  square feet footprint to be used for storage below the garage. The
  footprint of the structure is calculated for size allowance in Section 2-
  11-2 of the Sandy City Development Code and is within the allowed
  size of 1,500 square feet for the size of this property. The front of the
  structure is 23 feet 10 inches, and the rear elevation is 35 feet. The
  applicant is proposing to match the exterior materials with the existing
  exterior of the main dwelling. There is another shed at the back of the
  property that is approximately 90 square feet and will be removed once
  the proposed structure is built (See Exhibit B).

  The property is located in the Sensitive Area Overlay zone for slopes exceeding 30%. The plan was reviewed by the City
  Engineer and the applicant will need to meet the Sensitive Area Overlay code requirements in Sec. 21-15-4 of the Sandy
  City Land Development Code including submittal of a grading plan, prepared and stamped by an engineer or architect. It
  is possible a retaining wall may be needed in order to construct the accessory structure.

  Building Height and Setbacks
  The front elevation of the proposed structure that will be seen from the street is 23 feet 10 inches in height. The property
  slopes down to the rear and the structure from the rear is proposed to be 35 feet. Properties in the R-1-15 zone or larger
  are allowed to build up to 20 feet in height for an accessory structure. Section 21-11-2(a)(3)(c) of the Sandy City
  Development Code states that a building may be built taller, up to the maximum building height for a permitted dwelling
  within the zone in which it is located, upon receipt of a conditional use permit from the Planning Commission. 35 feet is
  the maximum height for a main dwelling in this zone. The applicant is proposing to place the structure 5 feet from the
  property line. In Section 21-11-2(a)(3)(b) it states that a detached structure exceeding 15 feet in height shall increase the
  minimum setback one foot for each on foot of additional height up to the minimum setback for the primary dwelling,
  unless otherwise approved by Planning Commission. The proposed structure is 35 feet at its tallest and would need to be
  setback 12.5 feet from the side property line. This is the minimum setback for the primary dwelling in the PUD(1.62)
  zone. The applicant is requesting a reduced setback of 5 feet from the side property line to align the structure with the
  existing driveway.

  Staff Concerns
   Staff is concerned about the structure is planned to be the maximum allowed height of 35 feet. This leaves no room for
  unexpected circumstances during the construction process.

Recommendation
  Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approve a Conditional Use Permit for an accessory structure with
  additional height and reduced setbacks as described in the staff report for the property located at 27 Northridge Way
  based on the following findings and subject to the following conditions:

Findings:
   1. There is an existing structure on site that will be demolished once the new structure is built.

    2. The property is in the Sensitive Area Overlay (SAO) zone and the City Engineer has determined the structure will
       need to go through the SAO review process.

    3. Staff finds that the proposed structure meets the intent of Section 21-11-2 provided the applicant complies with the
       following conditions:

Conditions:
   1. That the structure be located as per the enclosed site plan or any subsequent changes made during the SAO review
       process.

    2. That a grading plan be reviewed and approved during the building permit review process

    3. That the applicant complies with all Building & Safety, and Fire & Life Codes.
