Ordinance No. 26-04 - “Priority Builders Rezone,” General Plan Amendment and Rezone of approximately
0.71-acres located at 1758 and 1742 West 11800 South. Changing the General Plan Designation to High

On February 12, 2026, the Planning Commission held a public hearing to review this application. Minutes
from that meeting are included below. The Planning Commission voted to recommend approval of this
rezone application, with the following recommended motion:
I move the City Council approve Ordinance 26-04, the “Priority Builders Rezone,” amending the General

SUMMARY:
This is a public hearing for a rezone application brought by the applicant, Scott Yermish representing Priority
Builders. This request covers two parcels known as the Baird properties, which are located on 11800
South. These parcels are adjacent to one another, and they border on other property, which was rezoned
on June 3, 2025 to the RM-8-SD. If this rezone request is approved, the properties in this Baird rezone
request would be joined with those rezoned in June 2025 to make one project.
The northwest corner of 11800 South and Redwood Road was a mixture of zones prior to the June 2025
rezone. Now it consists of only RR-22 and RM-8 Zones. The subject Baird properties, have the existing
RR-22 zone as do two parcels to the east, and one to the west of the subject properties. These other
properties are not a part of this rezone request and would remain RR-22. To the north is RM-8-SD zoning.
