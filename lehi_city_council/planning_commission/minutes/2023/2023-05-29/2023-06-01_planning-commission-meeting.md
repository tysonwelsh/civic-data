4. City Business
Brittney Harris presented information on moderate-income housing. The last legislative session
added penalties to the reporting requirements and will impose a fine if not met. One more goal
needs to be met to meet those requirements. A housing affordability overlay zone would allow
more flexibility and incentives. The City Council denied the goal as one of the requirements. The
City Council also voted to put a moratorium on ADU's on those prohibited areas. An anonymous
survey was sent out to those with approved ADU's. 87% of the ADU's are occupied; most were
renting to relatives. Enforcement of ADU's based on complaints. Housing shortages are based on
population growth and housing development and job/housing balance.

5. Adjournment
