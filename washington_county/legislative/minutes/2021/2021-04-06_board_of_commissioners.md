WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
APRIL 6, 2021

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to
order by Chairman Gil Almquist at 4:00 p.m. on April 6, 2021, in the Washington County
Commission Chambers, and via Zoom 197 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in
attendance were Commission Chair Gil Almquist, Commissioner Dean Cox,
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf, and Deputy County
Clerk Cheyenne Bentley.

commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are

CONSIDERATION _OF ORDINANCE NO. 2021-1174-O, AN ORDINANCE
AMENDING TITLE 1 CHAPTER 5 SECTION 1 OF THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY CODE AND ADDING TITLE 1 CHAPTER 15 OF THE WASHINGTON
COUNTY CODE TO UPDATE OPEN MEETINGS REQUIREMENTS FOR
ELECTRONIC MEETINGS

Deputy County Attorney Brian Graf explained that due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the
Utah State legislature updated its Code in regards to some of the options and requirements
for conducting electronic meetings. Washington County desires to update its code to reflect
those changes as well.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Ordinance No. 2021-1174-
O, An Ordinance Amending Title 1 Chapter 5 Section 1 of the
Washington County Code and Adding Title 1 Chapter 15 of the
Washington County Code to Update Open Meetings Requirements for
Electronic Meetings. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and
carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and
Iverson voting aye.

CONSIDERATION OF RESOLUTION NO. _R-2021-2758, A RESOLUTION
APPROVING SUPPORT FOR APPLYING FOR MONEY FROM UTAH

DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION'S TRANSPORTATION INVESTMENT
FUND

Leslie Fonger explained that the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) has received
a one-time appropriation of funds from the Transportation Investment Fund (TIF) to be
used on paved pedestrian or paved non-motorized transportation project. Washington
County desires to develop a paved multi-use path that connects LaVerkin to Springdale,
known as the Zion Pathway Project.

If Washington County is selected to receive funding, a twenty percent (20%) match of the
costs for construction, reconstruction, or renovation of the trail must be contributed.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Adopt Resolution No. R-2021-
2758, A Resolution Approving Support for Applying for Money from
Utah Department of Transportation's Transportation Investment
Fund. Motion seconded by Commissioner Cox and carried by
unanimous vote, with Commissioners Almquist, Cox, and Iverson
voting aye.
