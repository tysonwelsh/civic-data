       A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON TOWN COUNCIL EXPRESSING
           APPRECIATION FOR COUNCILMEMBER KEVIN SEVERSON

      WHEREAS, Kevin Severson became a member of the Copperton Town Council
("Council") in 2018, and has represented at-large Council Seat "C" since that time; and

        WHEREAS, Council member Severson served two terms on the Council, completing his second
and final term on December 31,2025; md

         WHEREAS, during Council member Severson's tenure on the Council, he faithfully
represented Copperton on the Salt Lake Valley Animal Services Advisory Committee and served as the
Council's liaison to the Copperton Planning Commission; and

        WHEREAS, Councilmember Severson played a key role in shepherding Copperton through the
restatement of its land use ordinances in Titles l8 and l9 of the Copperton code.

       NOW, THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, effective immediately that:

          l.
           The Copperton Town Council expresses its sincere appreciation for Council
 member Severson's years of faithful service, valuable advice, and significant contributions.

          2.   The Copperton Town Council expresses its gratitude for the opportunity to have
 associated with Council member Severson as a friend and colleague and wishes him the best in
 his future endeavors.

       ADDOPTED and APPROVED this I 8th day of February ,2026.
