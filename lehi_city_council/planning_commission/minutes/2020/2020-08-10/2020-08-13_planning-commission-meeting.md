3.1) Public hearing and recommendation of X Development’s request for review of a General
Plan amendment, changing the designation on approximately 21-acres located at


approximately 2100 North 2300 West from NC (neighborhood commercial) and LDR (low
density residential) to C (commercial) and MDR (medium density residential).
   Jacob Curtis, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Nielsen asked the Commissioners if they had any questions for staff.
    Commissioner Everett mentioned that there wasn’t sufficient infrastructure support in the
    Staff Report. Ross Dinsdale stated the increase can be accommodated with the road widening
    of 2300 West. Commissioner Nielsen asked what the allowable amount of units were
    compared to the proposed amount of units. Jacob stated the LDR allowable unit range was 88
    units. Ross stated the proposed units for MDR were approximately 15 more units.
    Commissioner Hereth asked if the NC overlay near 2100 North was built when that took
    place. Jacob stated it looks as if it had been and is currently touching the property. He stated
    it would normally be allowed to rezone because it touches the property, but the applicant’s
    idea is to continue the commercial corner like the previous commercial overlay. Kim
    Struthers stated it would be up to the Commissioners discretion whether or not they decide to
    depart from the current general plan. Commissioner Eyre asked if a right in/right out would
    be an option. Ross stated that an application with that option had been submitted to UDOT,
    and from a City standpoint that would be preferred and are supportive of that possibility.

    The applicant, Dave Morton, was present and answered questions from the Commission. He
    stated they have discussed the development with the neighboring areas to try to work out the
    best solution (presented “Project Goal” presentation).

    Commissioner Nielsen asked if the Commissioners had any questions for the applicant.
    Commissioner Hereth questioned the reasoning for changing NC to Commercial. The
    applicant stated it gives additional flexibility, specific to building size, as well as types of
    uses. Commissioner Nielsen asked about improvements along 2300 West and if it was in
    addition to what had been agreed upon. The applicant stated part of the discussion with staff
    is they would make those improvements without reimbursement. Commissioner Nielsen
    asked the applicant to clarify the number of units they were proposing. The applicant stated
    they are asking for 7 additional units, totaling 95. Commissioner Everett stated that takes into
    consideration the open space which pushes the density higher. Jacob stated the proposed
    open space is right around 2 acres. Commissioner Hereth asked where the improvements stop
    and what the extent are. The applicant stated their intent is to improve up to 2100 North and
    would work with the City and the School District to find the best solution. Commissioner
    Nielsen asked for clarification on the intent for a couple of parcels of property that are
    undetermined and unaccounted for. The applicant stated they would work with the City to
    make sure it was taken care of. Commissioner Hereth asked if there was an access similar
    west of 1200 West. The applicant stated they are in the process of working with UDOT.
    Commissioner Eyre stated it would be a huge benefit as a way for kids to get off of 2300
    West to walk to school and off of the busy road.

    Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for agenda item 3.1 and acknowledged the
    comments that were received.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing for agenda item 3.1


    Commission and Staff discussed the item. Commissioner Peterson observed while he felt he
    would be more inclined to move forward if this were a concept plan, although he liked what
    was presented, he felt reviewing this as a zone change had too many unknowns.
    Commissioner Nielsen agreed with Commissioner Peterson. He liked the layout of having
    LDR buffering existing homes and creating a cohesive and diverse neighborhood with the
    open space that has been provided. He stated the idea of having quick access to arterial roads
    and the existing connection to 2100 North will have a huge benefit. He did not have a strong
    opinion on NC or Commercial. Ross Dinsdale stated the concerns for traffic have been
    looked at carefully. With a right in/right out it is not likely to increase traffic significantly. He
    stated it will hopefully be a benefit to the neighborhood. Commissioner Hereth stated his
    children used to attend North Point Elementary and there are a lot of traffic conditions on
    2100 North. He asked what the applicant would say to residents why more development is
    being allowed. Ross stated Thanksgiving Point is an area plan and the traffic is far exceeded
    the amount of traffic. This area has planned for an amount of traffic increase, where this will
    add a slight increase. Kim Struthers agreed with Ross that Thanksgiving Point has its own
    traffic plan and that any traffic in any area might compound traffic, but this development is
    not under the same restriction that Thanksgiving Point is under. Commissioner Nielsen
    inquired if this area has had a traffic study done. Ross stated that UDOT has done a traffic
    study, but it has not been required in the past, other than for a UDOT approval. The City is in
    the process with UDOT to start expanding 2300 West and 3600 West, in between 2100
    North, where there is a 5 lane road section, hoping to increase the capacity to alleviate traffic
    conditions in that area. Commissioner Nielsen stated traffic is a high level infrastructure that
    should be figured out well in advance before change. Commissioner Everett agreed with
    Commissioner Peterson with the cart before the horse analogy. He liked the idea
    conceptionally, however, there is really only one route and the only improvement with curb
    and gutter along 2300 West, which is a huge improvement, but he doesn’t see it coming from
    the South neighborhoods. The applicant stated they are asking for small increases in density
    as well as the cost and effort in getting access to 2100 North. He stated is it consistent with
    LDR and does not make sense along 2100 North and their concern in prolonging the
    development is anti-productive. Commissioner Nielsen asked what the timeframe from
    UDOT was. Eric has been working with Hales Engineering and indicated that they are
    moving forward and are about 6 to 8 weeks away. Commissioner Eyre stated the most
    problematic thing for him is NC to Commercial due to the uses of the building. Kim stated
    the NC is limited to 5,000 sqft. The applicant stated they are willing to work with the City
    and neighbors and will continue to do so throughout the process. Commissioner Hereth asked
    about height restriction with the general plan amendment. Kim stated that would have to be
    done through a separate development agreement through Council with Planning Commission
    recommendations.

    Commissioner Nielsen stated he would entertain a motion.

Motion: Commissioner Hereth motioned to forward a positive recommendation to City Council
of X Development’s request for review of a General Plan amendment, changing the
designation on approximately 21-acres located at approximately 2100 North 2300 West from
NC (neighborhood commercial) and LDR (low density residential) to C (commercial) and
MDR (medium density residential); including all DRC comments from July 22, 2020. Based on
the findings this general plan amendment provides good transitional land uses to buffer above
residential uses from commercial on the corner, being that this corner is a freeway plan for more
intense commercial use appropriate at the intersection. That the developer has worked with
residents in mind by creating low density buffers around the current neighborhood for transitional
MDR and Commercial and that the General Plan Amendment is consistent with best practices by
1) is not detrimental to the public health, safety, and welfare of the City 2) not have substantial
adverse effects of properties 3) and suitable by design, location and shape. Also, with the
understanding that the developer will be widening 2300 West without reimbursement from the
City and in lieu the Developer will be offered a 95 unit cap. A proposed recommendation that a
height restriction on the Commercial Property is appropriate to limit the use of the commercial
zone. Commissioner Bunker seconded.

Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson; no; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
Commissioner Everett, no; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
Commissioner Eyre, yes. The motion passed unanimously. 5-2

3.2) Public hearing and consideration of Allied Electric Sign’s request for approval of a
conditional use for two monument signs with electronic message displays for the 7-Eleven
located at 2120 North Triumph Blvd.
    Jacob Curtis, City Planner, presented information on the agenda item.

    Commissioner Nielsen asked if the Staff had any input.

    Commissioner Everett commented that the signs can be pretty intrusive at night. He
    recommended to limit the hours of operation of the sign. Commissioner Nielsen stated that
    this has been an issue and inquired what the code requires for approval.

    The applicant, Tracy, was unaware of the illumination study. Kim Struthers stated he would
    want to meet those requirements and that the City can make sure that the applicant meets the
    requirements by providing a study. The applicant stated they would provide a lights kinetic
    and would take care of the requirements needed. Commissioner Everett stated there were
    some tress needed to be pulled out of the park strip and wondered if that would still meet the
    landscape requirements. Commissioner Nielsen stated they just needed to comply with DRC
    comments. Could be more of an observation than a recommendation. The applicant believed
    that the sign will face both North and South. The applicant stated she will take the
    recommendations

    Commissioner Nielsen opened public hearing for agenda item 3.2

    Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing for agenda item 3.2

    Commissioner Nielsen reopened public hearing and acknowledge comments that were
    submitted.

    Commissioner Nielsen closed public hearing.

    The Commission and Staff discussed the agenda item and the City Ordinances associated
    with the item.


    Motion: Commissioner Peterson moved to grant approval of Allied Electric Sign’s request
    for approval of a conditional use for two monument signs with electronic message
    displays for the 7-Eleven located at 2120 North Triumph Blvd; with conditions that
    before any signs are constructed or installed that the Parks Department will verify that the
    landscaping requirements have been met, and that during the construction process tress that
    are removed must be replaced, and that tress are not being removed so the sign is visible; also
    that the North side of the sign on Triumph Blvd of the 7-11 sign be reduced at least 30%
    from 10:00pm and 6:00am. Including all DRC comments and findings stated in the report.
    Commissioner Eyre seconded.

    Vote: Commissioner Nielsen, yes; Commissioner Peterson; yes; Commissioner Ellis, yes;
    Commissioner Everett, yes; Commissioner Bunker, yes; Commissioner Hereth, yes;
