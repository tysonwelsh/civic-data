41          Legislative Update
42
43   Rob Jolley, RRJ Consulting, LLC, reported on the legislative session.
44
45                  HB 242 – Secondary Water Meter Amendments. State will use ARPA funds to
46                   focus on water conservation. The City can receive a 70% match for secondary
47                   water meters and the application process is open. Mayor Staggs reported that
48                   Riverton City had already made an application for this money.
49                  HB 282 – Prohibits cities from prohibiting water-wise landscape.

 1                       HB 151 – This bill initially made an effort to prohibit retail incentives. It passed
 2                        with language that said retail incentives are allowed if there is a housing
 3                        component in the development with 10% of low-income housing.
 4                       HB 462 – Housing Affordability Amendments. The legislature wanted to mandate
 5                        zoning at transportation nodes and require each city to provide low-income
 6                        housing. This bill passed with language that creates a new criteria for cities to
 7                        adopt in their general plan. It requires two additional criteria to receive
 8                        transportation improvement funds and included a provision for accessory
 9                        dwelling units.

14   Councilmember Wells said there is a disconnect between what developers are willing to do and
15   the housing stock needed in the state for affordable housing. Mr. Jolley explained that HB 462
16   focuses on transit cities that will need to adopt stationary plans.
17
18   Mayor Staggs said the requirement is area mean income (AMI) at 70-80%. Ryan Carter, City
19   Attorney, responded that the City had met this requirement. He explained that once the City
20   has done all it can to allow for the density, there is nothing more by way of a mandate that they
21   can do to fund it. The rising cost of construction materials has made it difficult for developers to
22   build affordable housing.

 7             Ordinance No. 22-13 – Perry Rezone, Approximately 3.79-Acres at 4623 West
 8             12600 South, From SP-Commercial to Commercial Gateway. Applicant-Michael
 9             Perry.
10
11   Jason Lethbridge, Development Services Director, explained the public hearing was for a
12   Rezone application brought by the applicant for 3.79-acres at 4623 West 12600 South. The
13   subject property is a vacant parcel on the southwest corner of 12600 South and 4570 West with
14   about 600-ft of frontage on 12600 South. 12600 South is the Riverton-Herriman City Boundary
15   in this area. To the north of the subject property is Maverik convenience store and about 10-
16   acres of vacant commercial land. To west is the Big-O tire store. To the south is Riverton City
17   Park and Jordan Valley School District Property.
18
19   Mr. Lethbridge said the property is currently zoned “SP-Commercial” which means Specific Plan
20   Commercial. It was zoned commercial on May 16, 2000 as part of the Western Springs
21   Specific Plan which adopted a Special Zoning Designation for about 182-acres along Riverton
22   City’s west side. The Western Springs Specific Plan included unique zoning and land-use
23   regulations for both residential and commercial uses. The residential portions of Western
24   Springs were completed before 2017. The lone commercial project, Big-O Tires, was built in
25   2016. The applicant is requesting this rezone to Commercial Gateway to change the land
26   uses available on the vacant commercial property. The Western Springs Plan anticipated future
27   changes to the plan or properties might be necessary and made provisions and allowances for
28   such changes.
29
30   The General Plan designation for the area is Community Commercial. Zoning designations that
31   are appropriate for the General Plan Designation are Commercial Gateway (C-G), Commercial
32   Neighborhood (C-N) and Commercial professional Office (C-PO). The applicant is requesting a
33   rezone to the C-G zone,
34
35   Councilmember Stewart asked what the concerns were when this was previously brought
36   before the Council. Mr. Lethbridge said they wanted commercial regional zoning which was
37   inappropriate to the property.
38
39   Councilmember McCay asked if Mr. Lethbridge knew the plans were for this area.              Mr.
40   Lethbridge said that they have plans for a multi-tenant retail building.
41
42   Mayor Staggs called for public comments; there being none, he closed the Public Hearing.
43
44   Councilmember Buroker MOVED that the Riverton City Council adopt Ordinance No. 22-13
45   – Perry Rezone, Approximately 3.79-Acres at 4623 West 12600 South, From SP-
46   Commercial to Commercial Gateway. Councilmember McDougal SECONDED the motion.
47   Mayor Staggs called for discussion on the motion. There being none, he called for a roll-call
48   vote. The vote was as follows: Buroker-yes, McCay-yes, McDougal-yes, Stewart-yes and
49   Wells-yes. The motion passed unanimously.
