                                    RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                    RESOLUTION NO. 24-14

    A RESOLUTION APPROVING A SUPPLEMENTAL AGREEMENT NO. UT11-01
   BETWEEN THE UTAH DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION AND RIVERTON
    CITY FOR IMPROVEMENT OF AN INTERSECTION AT THE LOCATION OF
                 BANGERTER HIGHWAY AND 13400 SOUTH

       WHEREAS, the State of Utah, Department of Transportation (“UDOT”) is commencing
with construction to improve an intersection located at 13400 South and Bangerter Highway
(hereinafter referred to as the “Intersection Project”); and

        WHEREAS, the Intersection Project affects a broad variety of municipal facilities, such as
roads, utility lines, and park space; and

       WHEREAS, as UDOT proceeds with the Intersection Project, the parties need to establish
by agreement the process by which UDOT’s construction will be reviewed and coordinated with
Riverton City officials; and

      WHEREAS, to this end the Riverton City Council approved execution of a Master
Agreement with UDOT by adoption of Resolution No. 23-86 on the date of November 7, 2023; and

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council finds that the Master Agreement set general obligations
for constructing improvements related to the Intersection Project, but the Agreement did not
address the particulars as to how City utility lines would be rerouted, and how UDOT would ensure
continued delivery service while rerouting said utilities; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that a Supplemental
Agreement No. UT11-01 has been prepared for approval and execution by and between UDOT and
Riverton City, which states the particulars as to how UDOT will replace City utility lines located
within the Intersection Project while providing for the continued delivery of utility service, and the
extent of the required participation of the parties and the rights, duties, responsibilities, and
obligations of the parties described in this Resolution.

     NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
RIVERTON CITY AS FOLLOWS:

       1.      The Riverton City Council hereby approves the Supplemental Agreement No. UT11-01
               with UDOT, attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.
       2.      The Mayor is authorized to sign the approved Supplemental Agreement No. UT11-01
               with UDOT in substantially similar form to that provided in Exhibit “A,” once the
               Supplemental Agreement No. UT11-01 is approved as to legal form by the City
               Attorney.
       3.      This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon passage.
