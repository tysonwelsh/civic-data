                                   RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                   RESOLUTION NO. 25-39

   A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO A FIRST
 AMENDED AND RESTATED AGREEMENT OF THE UTAH RISK MANAGEMENT
                           AGENCY

        WHEREAS, the Interlocal Cooperation Act, Utah Code Ann. § 11-13-101 et seq., permits
two or more public agencies to enter into an agreement with one another for joint or cooperative
action, to provide services they are each authorized by Statute to provide, to make the most efficient
use of their powers by enabling them to cooperate with other public agencies on a basis of mutual
advantage, and to do anything they are each authorized by Statute to do; and

       WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §63G-7-801(1) provides that a governmental entity may
purchase commercial insurance, self-insure, or self-insure and purchase excess commercial
insurance; and

        WHEREAS, Utah Code Ann. §63G-7-801(2) provides that any government entity may self-
insure by establishing a trust account to expend principal and earnings of the trust account solely to
pay the cost of investigation, discovery, and other pretrial and litigation expenses including
attorneys’ fees, and pay all sums for which the governmental entity may be adjudged liable or for
which a compromise settlement may be agreed upon;

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has undergone a competitive process to obtain insurance services
for fiscal year 2025-2026 and future years; and

      WHEREAS, following a lengthy review of proposals received, Riverton City staff has
recommended retaining the insurance brokerage services of Moreton and Company; and

        WHEREAS, Moreton and Company has presented the Utah Risk Management Agency for
the City to enter into an interlocal agreement for receipt of insurance services and management of its
insurance portfolio; and

        WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council finds and determines that obtaining insurance
services from the Utah Risk Management Agency will enable the City to maintain insurance
protection while empowering the City to participate in decisions pertaining to the Agency, and

        WHEREAS, Riverton City has appointed the Riverton City Attorney to review and approve
as to form the amended and restated agreement;

       NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah, as
follows:

       1. The attached First Amended and Reinstated Interlocal Agreement of the Utah Risk
          Management Agency is hereby approved.
      2. The Mayor is hereby authorized to sign, on behalf of the City, the proposed First
         Amended and Reinstated Interlocal Agreement of the Utah Risk Management Agency,
         upon verification that the Agreement has been approved as to legal form by the City
         Attorney.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 17th day of
June, 2025 by the following vote:
