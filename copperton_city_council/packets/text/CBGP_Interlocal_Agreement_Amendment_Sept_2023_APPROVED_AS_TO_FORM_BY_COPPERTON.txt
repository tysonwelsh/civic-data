 RESOLUTION NO.                                                        DATE:



A RESOLUTION OF THE COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
     AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF THE AMENDMENT TO THE
INTERLOCAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT WITH SALT LAKE COUNTY, TOWN
 OF ALTA, TOWN OF BRIGHTON, BLUFFDALE CITY, COTTONWOOD HEIGHTS
   CITY, DRAPER CITY, EMIGRATION CANYON METRO TOWNSHIP, KEARNS
 METRO TOWNSHIP, HOLLADAY CITY, MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP, MIDVALE
 CITY CORP., CITY OF MILLCREEK, MURRAY CITY, RIVERTON CITY, CITY OF
 SOUTH SALT LAKE, AND WHITE CITY METRO TOWNSHIP RELATING TO THE
  CONDUCT OF THE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT BLOCK GRANT PROGRAM,
  EMERGENCY SOLUTIONS GRANT PROGRAM AND THE HOME INVESTMENT
                        PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM

 The Copperton Metro Township (“Copperton”) Council resolves as follows:

        WHEREAS, Salt Lake County participates as an “urban county,” as defined by federal

 regulation, in the Community Development Block Grant (“CDBG”), Emergency Solutions Grant

 (“ESG”), and the HOME Investment Partnership through a consortium that includes the urban

 county (“HOME”) programs administered by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban

 Development (“HUD”); and

        WHEREAS, an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement (“Agreement”) was prepared for

 approval and executed by and between Copperton, Salt Lake County, and participating

 municipalities, which states the purposes thereof, and the extent of the required participation of the

 parties and the rights, duties, responsibilities, and obligations of the parties in the conduct and

 administration of the CDBG, ESG, and HOME programs as specified therein; and

        WHEREAS, HUD requested amendments to the Agreement which were agreed to by all

 parties; namely: (1) Herriman has elected to be removed from the list of participating government

 entities; (2) Section 6 of the Agreement has been revised to match precise language requested by

 HUD; and (3) Section 17(t) has been revised to clarify that the agreement will automatically renew

 in the future to streamline future renewal qualification periods.

       WHEREAS, The amendments have been added to the Agreement, a copy of which is

attached hereto as Exhibit 1.                      1
        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Copperton Metro Township

 Council that the attached amended Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between Copperton, Salt

 Lake County, and the Town of Alta, Town of Brighton, Bluffdale City, Cottonwood Heights City,

 Draper City, Emigration Canyon Metro Township, Kearns Metro Township, Holladay City, Magna

 Metro Township, Midvale City Corp., City of Millcreek, Murray City, Riverton City, City of

 South Salt Lake, and White City Metro Township relating to the conduct of the CDBG, ESG, and

 HOME Programs is hereby approved by the Council and the Mayor is hereby authorized to

 execute the same on behalf of Copperton.

       APPROVED and ADOPTED this 20th day of September, 2023.


                                            By:
                                                  Sean Clayton, Mayor
