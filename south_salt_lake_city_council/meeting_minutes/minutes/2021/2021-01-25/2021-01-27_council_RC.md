UNFINISHED BUSINESS

   1. Civilian Review Board Ordinance Discussion. Council Chair Bynum gave a
      brief overview on where the City Council is at with this this Ordinance.

       The Council discussed what the compensation should be for the board members
       and decided it should be $70 per month.

       There was a blank on the Ordinance where what percent of records should be
       reviewed by the board monthly and Council Chair Bynum suggested that be a
       decision made by the board chair.

       A majority of the Council agreed that letting the board chair be in charge of how
       many records are reviewed monthly will work well for now.

       Council Chair Bynum said the next step for this Ordinance is to look at the budget
       implications. She went over the positions that would be needed for this board and
       which ones the City Council was in support of.

       Council Member Siwik is in support of the Ordinance but he is not in support of
       creating a budgetary impact on the City in order to create this board. He is in
       support of training and supplies but doesn’t back the positions being requested at
       this time. He would like to get the board started and then see if these positions are
       going to be necessary and that can be something that can be discussed during the
       next budget.

       Mayor Wood said when this Ordinance was brought forward she and staff looked
       at what kind of a work load this board would create and the positions proposed
       were felt to be necessary for a successful board. Many City departments are
       already overwhelmed with current workloads.

       Council Chair Bynum agreed with Mayor Wood and believes if the Council
       creates this board and doesn’t fund it properly the board may not be successful.

       Council Member deWolfe would like to pass this Ordinance sooner rather than
       later and agrees a funding source should be in place but wonders if that creating
       the budget could be done while the board recruitment is being done. He still feels
       a little on the fence with the lieutenant position that is being proposed and would
       feel better about it if there was a dedicated funding source as mentioned

       previously.

       Police Chief, Jack Carruth, said that a portion of the lieutenant position could be
       funded by the State through funding for the homeless resource center. He is
       currently working on structuring the homeless resource center division of the
       police department and that lieutenant would be part of that team. He feels that the
       lieutenant position wouldn’t be needed solely for the Civilian Review Board but
       there are other staffing needs in the City as well so this lieutenant would
       supplement those needs as well as help with the board. He gave a brief summary
       of how the crime analyst and the attorney would be used as well. He felt the initial
       numbers that were presented to the Council were a bit high.

       Council Chair Bynum said at a future meeting the Council would discuss the
       budget in more detail.

       Council Member Siwik would like to be shown where these staff positions would
       be used in the bigger picture.

       Chief Carruth said that City staff wants to make sure this board is successful from
       the beginning and having the proper budget in place would help do that.

       Council Member Pinkney feels that additional funds to the police department is a
       bigger conversation and would like to see exactly where funds are lacking and if
       additional funds are truly needed or if there are bigger policy decisions to be
       made.

       Mayor Wood said she is very supportive of this ordinance but wants to make sure
       it is done right.
