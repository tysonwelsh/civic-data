25. APPROVE AND ADOPT AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF 3200 WEST
BETWEEN RIVER LANE AND SPANISH FORK RIVER NEAR THE PALMYRA AREA OF
UTAH COUNTY, UTAH.

-Robert Moore, Attorney

(Timestamp 18:18)

RICHARD NIELSON, DIRECTOR OF PUBLIC WORKS - 3200 WEST HISTORICALLY
HAD CROSSED THE SPANISH FORK RIVER WITH A BRIDGE. THERE WAS A
SECTION OF ROAD ABOUT 300’ LONG BETWEEN THE RIVER AND WHERE RIVER
LANE IS NOW. FROM OLD PHOTOS, IN THE EARLY 1950’S THE BRIDGE WAS
REMOVED AND THE ROAD WAS OBLIVIATED. AS MR. BETTS WAS DOING SOME
WORK ON HIS PROPERTY IN LOOKING AT TITLES, ETC.. IT WAS FOUND THAT THE
COUNTY HAD AN UNDERLYING DEED FOR THIS PIECE OF ROAD. MR. BETTS AND
MR. ROBERTS (CURRENT OWNERS) SAY THE DITCH IS APPROXIMATELY IN THE
CENTER OF THE DEEDED RIGHT OF WAY. THERE IS NO INTENTION OF MOVING
THE DITCH. THE DITCH WILL REMAIN AS IS. IT DOESN’T AFFECT ANYTHING ON
THE WEST SIDE OF THE RIVER.
