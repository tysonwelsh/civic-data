                                   ORDINANCE NO. 1577

         AN ORDINANCE APPROVING THE SECOND AMENDMENT TO THE MASTER
         DEVELOPMENT   AGREEMENT    FOR    EPG  PONY  EXPRESS  FOR
         APPROXIMATELY   26.93  ACRES   OF   PROPERTY  LOCATED  AT
         APPROXIMATELY 13004 SOUTH PONY EXPRESS ROAD WITHIN DRAPER
         CITY.

       WHEREAS, the Land Use and Development Code of the Draper City Municipal Code
has been established to provide regulations concerning general developments within the
City Boundaries; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council hereby determines that it will be in the best interest of the
City to allow development of the subject property in accordance with the Proposed
Amendments to the Development Agreement; and

      WHEREAS, the Amendment to the Development Agreement will update the
ownership entity for the property; and

       WHEREAS, the Amendment to the Development Agreement will allow for the limited
addition of wall signage facing the I-15 freeway that would exceed the applicable maximum
amounts in ordinance; and

        WHEREAS, the additional signage size and amount is comparable with another
similarly situated building and use in the vicinity; and

       WHEREAS, the proposed Amendment to the Development Agreement set forth
herein has been reviewed by the Planning Commission and the City Council, and all
appropriate public hearings have been held in accordance with Utah law to obtain public
input regarding the proposed Amendments to the Development Agreement; and

        WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has reviewed and made a recommendation to
the City Council concerning the proposed Amendment to the Development Agreement, and
the City Council has found the proposed Development Agreement to be consistent with the
City’s General Plan.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE
OF UTAH:

      Section 1.  Development Agreement.       The City of Draper approves the
Amendments to the Development Agreement provided in Exhibit A, otherwise known as The
Second Amendment to The Master Development Agreement for EPG Pony Express.

        This Second Amendment to Master Development Agreement for EPG Pony Express (“Second
Amendment”) is entered into this ___ day of ________, 2022 (“Effective Date”), by and between
DRAPER CITY, a municipality of the State of Utah, (“City”), and EXETER DRAPER LAND,
LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Master Developer”), sometimes referred to jointly as
“Parties.”

                                             RECITALS:

        WHEREAS The Parties previously entered into that certain MASTER DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT FOR EPG PONY EXPRESS (“MDA”) dated on or about April 7, 2020 with respect
to real property located in Draper City, Salt Lake County, State of Utah (“Property”);

      WHEREAS The MDA was amended by the terms of that certain FIRST AMENDMENT TO
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT TO MASTER DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT FOR EPG
PONY EXPRESS dated August 24, 2022, between City and Master Developer (“First Amendment”).
The MDA Agreement as amended by the First Amendment is referred to as the “Amended
Development Agreement”).

        WHEREAS Master Developer and the City have cooperated in the preparation of this Second
Amendment and desire to enter into this Second Amendment to specify the rights and responsibilities
of Master Developer to develop the Property as expressed in this Second Amendment and the rights
and responsibilities of the City to allow and regulate such development pursuant to the requirements
of this Second Amendment. Unless otherwise covered by a specific defined term in this Second
Amendment, all capitalized terms in this Second Amendment shall have the meaning set forth in the
Development Agreement.

       WHEREAS The MDA inadvertently referred to the Master Developer as Exeter Draper PSA,
LLC. The Property was conveyed to Exeter Draper Land, LLC and accordingly, Exeter Draper Land,
LLC is executing this Second Amendment as the correct Master Developer.

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises, covenants, representations and
warranties hereinafter set forth, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

              1. Definitions. All capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined herein shall have
       the same meanings assigned such terms in the Development Agreement.

               2. Sections Amended. Section 2.4 of the Second Amendment shall be added to Section
       2 of the Development Agreement to read as follows:

                      2.4 Signage. Building #1 of the Project, also identified by the street address of
               13004 S. Pony Express Road, shall be allowed to exceed the limits contained within
               Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) sections 9-26-070(A)(1)(a) and 9-26-060(H)(3)
       regarding maximum permitted wall signage on the east facing façade as follows: one (1)
       wall sign in excess of the 250 square feet maximum size restriction for a wall sign shall
       be permitted. Said wall sign shall be no larger than 720 square feet, and must be located
       above the main entrance on the east elevation of the building. The combined square
       footage of all other wall signage on the east elevation of Building #1 shall not exceed
       437 square feet.

       All other building and site signage shall comply with the regulations found in Draper
       City Municipal Code, Chapter 9-26, as amended.

        4. Interpretation. In the event of a conflict in the terms and conditions of this Second
Amendment with the terms and conditions of the Amended Development Agreement, the
terms and conditions of this Second Amendment shall be binding and govern the conduct of
the Parties.

        5. No Other Changes. All provisions of the Amended Development Agreement, except
as specifically amended by this Second Amendment shall remain in full force and effect.

        6. Counterparts. This Second Amendment may be executed in any number of
counterparts, each of which when so executed and delivered, shall be deemed an original, but
with all such counterparts being taken together to constitute a single original.
