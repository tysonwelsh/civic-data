                                     RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                      ORDINANCE NO. 21-14

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN & REZONING 5.91 ACRES LOCATED
  AT APPROXIMATELY 12900 S 1830 W, AMENDING THE GENERAL PLAN MEDIUM
 DENSITY RESIDENTIAL TO MEDIUM HIGH DENSITY RESIDENTIAL, AND REZONING
      THE PROPERTY FROM R-4 TO RM-6-SD, RYAN KENT WITH DRAGONLY
                        DEVELOPMENT APPLICANT

      WHEREAS, the Riverton City Planning Commission has received public input and made a
recommendation regarding the above listed General Plan and zoning amendment; and,

      WHEREAS, the City Council has held a public hearing to consider said General Plan and zoning
amendment; and,

       WHEREAS, the Riverton City Council has determined that it is in the best interest of the public
to amend the Riverton City General Plan from Medium Density Residential to Medium High Density
Residential and to amend the Riverton City Zoning Map by rezoning the 5.91 acres located at 12900
South 1830 West from R-4 to RM-6-SD, as described herein.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah as
follows:
             Section 1.       The Riverton City Zoning Map shall be, and hereby is, amended
                              to reflect the following changes as shown in Exhibit “A”

               Section 2.              Development shall be limited to detached single family units on
                                       individual lots, with ‘rambler’ style units only.

               Section 3.              This ordinance shall take effect upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 1st day of
June, 2021 by the following vote:
