WASHINGTON COUNTY COMMISSION
MEETING MINUTES
MAY 12, 2026

The Special Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Adam Snow at 9:00 a.m. on May 12, 2026, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Commissioner Victor Iverson, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.
INVOCATION BY: Jerry Jaeger

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Gary Henderson

CONSIDERATION _OF_ _ RESOLUTION __NO.___R-2026-3751, A | RESOLUTION
APPOINTING THE INTERIM COUNTY SHERIFF

Commissioner Iverson thanked the Republican party for the work that went into the special
election and recognized Barry Golding on his victory.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Iverson to Approve Resolution No. R-2026-3751, A
Resolution Appointing the Interim County Sheriff. Motion seconded by
Commissioner Almquist and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners
Snow, Almquist and Iverson voting aye.

SWEARING IN OF SHERIFF BARRY GOLDING

Judge Matthew Bell swore in Sheriff Golding.

Having no other business, the meeting was adjourned at 9:15 a.m.
