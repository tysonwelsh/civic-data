                              RESOLUTION NO. 25-22


   A RESOLUTION APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A VOTE
  CENTER AGREEMENT BETWEEN DRAPER CITY AND SALT LAKE COUNTY FOR THE
                      2025 MUNICIPAL ELECTIONS

      WHEREAS, Draper City desires to facilitate local voting opportunities for its
residents; and

       WHEREAS, Salt Lake County, through its Clerk’s Office, conducts municipal
elections and coordinates polling locations throughout the county; and

      WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has requested use of Draper City Hall as a vote
center for early voting and election day voting in the 2025 Municipal Primary and
General Elections; and

       WHEREAS, the City Council finds that approving the agreement is in the best
interest of the residents of Draper City and promotes access to local voting;

      NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY,
STATE OF UTAH:

      Section 1. Authorization. The City Council hereby approves the Vote Center
Agreement with Salt Lake County and authorizes the Mayor or designee to execute
the agreement on behalf of Draper City.

     Section 2. Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately
upon passage.

     PASSED AND ADOPTED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, ON THIS 6TH DAY OF MAY, 2025.
