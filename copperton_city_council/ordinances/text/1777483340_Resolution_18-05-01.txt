COPPERTON METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL
RESOLUTION # | 8- 05-4|
MAY 16, 2018
A RESOLUTION OPPOSING THE 2018 PROPOSED 0.25 PERCENT SALES TAX INCREASE
PROPOSED BY THE SALT LAKE COUNTY COUNCIL FOR TRANSPORTATION PURPOSES,
CONDEMNING THE PROCESS BY WHICH ADOPTION IS CONSIDERED
BE IT KNOWN AND REMEMBERED THAT, the Copperton Metro Township Council met in
a regular public meeting on May 16", 2018
WITNESS

WHEREAS, Salt Lake County has surveyed the various municipalities to consider a 0.25 percent
tax increase to fund transportation projects including significant funding to UTA
(Utah Transit Authority), and this resolution opposes increasing the tax burden of Salt Lake County
Residents; and

WHEREAS, other means of revenue generating or spending reduction have not been explored by
the various municipalities, and the State of Utah is operating a sizable surplus from tax revenue, further
increasing the tax burden is a fiscally irresponsible path to transportation solvency; and

WHEREAS, in 2015 the voters in Salt Lake County rejected this tax increase and the actions of the
State Legislature and a vote by the County Council to impose a transportation tax increase would not reflect
the will of the voters and should not be considered again without a ballot measure; and

WHEREAS, because UTA has not demonstrated the ability to act with deference or a fiduciary
responsibility to the residents of the County, it is premature and fiscally irresponsible to assign more tax
dollars until demonstrable improvements have been made to the service, finances, and governance of UTA;
and

WHEREAS, Copperton Metro Township will not benefit as much as other municipalities due to
lack of commercial entities within its boundaries, and will funnel tax revenue to larger cities and UTA
without services rendered to the people of our community.

RESOLUTION

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Copperton Metro Township Council recommends
an unfavorable opinion of the 0.25 percent tax increase and the method in which the County Council has
attempted to survey municipalities rather than shouldering the responsibility.

Copperton Metro Township formally opposes this tax increase and urges Salt Lake County to make
those decisions the voters elected them to make; and.

Copperton Metro Township urges other municipalities to consider the circumvention of our
constituents in ignoring the results of the 2015 proposition, and the political burden passed to the municipal
leaders by county leaders. Copperton urges other municipal leaders not to increase the tax burden of our
constituents, and not to reward the repeated mismanagement of UTA (Utah Transit Authority)

APPROVED AND ADOPTED in the Copperton Metro Township, Salt Lake County, Utah this
16" day of May, 2018
