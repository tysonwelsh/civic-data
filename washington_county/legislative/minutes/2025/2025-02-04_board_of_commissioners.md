FEBRUARY 4, 2025

The Regular Meeting of the Board of the Washington County Commission was called to order by
Chairman Victor Iverson at 4:00 p.m. on February 4, 2025, in the Washington County Commission
Chambers, and via Zoom 111 East Tabernacle, St. George, Utah. Those in attendance were
Commissioner Victor Iverson, Commissioner Adam Snow, Commissioner Gil Almquist, Deputy
County Attorney Devin Snow, County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw and Deputy Clerk Sarah

Lloyd.

CONSENT AGENDA:

The Consent Agenda is a means of expediting the consideration of routine matters. If a
commissioner requests that items be removed from the consent agenda, those items are placed at
the beginning of the regular agenda. Other than requests to remove items, a motion to approve the
items on the consent agenda is not debatable.

CONSIDERATION _OF RESOLUTION NO. R-2025-3453 A RESOLUTION
APPROPRIATING EXPENDITURES OF FUNDS COLLECTED AS WASHINGTON
COUNTY’S SHARE OF THE RECREATION, ARTS. AND PARKS SALES TAX

County Administrator Nicholle Felshaw explained the Recreation, Arts and Parks Sales Tax was
on the November 2024 ballot was passed by 60 percent. Fifteen percent of the sales tax is dedicated
to be spent on cultural organizations in areas of Washington County.

Tony Allen spoke as a board member of the RAP Board, and thanked the Commissioners for their
considerations to the arts. He spoke on the Dixie Spirit of Washington County.

A MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR THE TREATMENT OF HISTORIC
PROPERTIES IN CONNECTION WITH THE ACQUISITION OF PROPERTY FOR THE
WARNER VALLEY RESERVOIR

Mr. Snow stated the BLM is transferring approximately 900 acres to the Washington County Water
Conservancy District for a reuse reservoir. As part of that transaction, federal law requires review
of the National Historic Preservation Act, which has identified nine historic properties.

MOTION: Motion by Commissioner Almquist to Adopt Resolution No. R-2025-3454, A
Resolution Approving a Memorandum of Agreement for the Treatment of
Historic Properties in Connection with the Acquisition of Property for the

Chair to Approve Minor Changes. Motion seconded by Commissioner Snow
and carried by unanimous vote, with Commissioners Iverson, Snow and
Almquist voting aye.
