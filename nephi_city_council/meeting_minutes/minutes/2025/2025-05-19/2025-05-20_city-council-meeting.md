ZONE CHANGE ORDINANCE-ORDINANCE 05-20-2025
      Seth Atkinson introduced a zone change request for the property located on 300 W from
      approximately 800 to 860 N from R1-H (Residential 1 acre holding) to R1- ½ acre zone with
      animal rights (Agricultural Overlay). The purpose of the rezone is to enable the creation of 3
      residential ½ acre lots contributing to a lower density zone which fits into surrounding
       development and will keep traffic low on a narrow road. The council supported the rezone and
       expressed excitement about the potential for more like that in the future.
       Councilor Skip Worwood made the motion to approve the zone change ordinance 05-20-25
       contingent on boundary line cleanup. Councilor Travis Worwood seconded the motion. . The
       motion passed on the following roll call vote:

               Yes: Shari Cowan, Travis Worwood, Skip Worwood, Jeramie Callaway

               No: None

NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
      Seth Atkinson, city administrator, introduced a non-disclosure agreement with a company, Set
      Square, who prepares sites for data centers. The agreement will allow the company to be more
      forthright with the information about where they are looking to locate. The company’s goal is to
      remove risk for existing ratepayers by bringing such a large load to the grid system. Nephi City
      would look to potentially provide utilities for this development.
      Councilor Travis Worwood made the motion to authorize the mayor to sign the agreement with
      Set Square. Councilor Shari Cowan seconded the motion. The motion passed on unanimous
      vote.

Work Session 8:20 pm
   1. Lisa Brough and Craig Oswald presented proposed fee schedule changes to the cemetery. Ms.
       Brough presented a spreadsheet comparing cemetery fees from various towns and what they
       were proposing. The discussion included implementing resident and non-resident fees,
       providing funds for future development, covering costs to take care of cemetery, and protecting
       Nephi residents from high fees. The council decided that fees could be looked at on a yearly
       basis and adjustments made as needed.
   2. Seth Atkinson presented a spreadsheet showing the impact of a 26-year average assessed value
       increase on property tax revenues. He explained that if it went up to 8% that is about $1 per
       month per household. The deadline for letting the county know was quickly approaching and
       the council felt rushed to make the decision for this year, but discussed this is something they
       want to do and will definitely use the next year to educate the public on the proposed increase
       and its impact so it can happen next year.
   3. Summer party was scheduled for Thursday, September, 4.
   4. Discussion of a potential business that manufactures dumpster bins being put on a triangular lot
       near Owens Corning that was included in a previous annexation.
   5. Meeting adjourned at 8:48 pm.
