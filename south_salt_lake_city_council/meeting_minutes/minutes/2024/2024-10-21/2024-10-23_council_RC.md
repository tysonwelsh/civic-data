          and classes this month at the County library.

       3. MAYOR COMMENTS.
          None

       4. CITY ATTORNEY COMMENTS.
          None

       5. CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS.
          Council Chair Bynum shared a link she received from a resident that showed clean
          energy initiatives and said that this would be something worthwhile for the City to
          consider in preparation of the 2034 Olympics.

       6. INFORMATION.
             a. Recap of the Public Works Sales Tax Bonds
                Finance Director, Crystal Makin, shared some updates about the bond and
                its final numbers. Construction of the new site for the Public Works campus
                is set to start in the Spring of 2025.

               b. Hazard Mitigation Plan Update
                  Emergency Management Coordinator, Yasmin Abbyad, shared a
                  presentation that showed the steps that the City is taking by working with
                  the County and through digital surveys to receive resident input on what
                  things are the most important for emergency preparedness. A copy of the
                  presentation is to be attached and incorporated by this reference.

Unfinished Business

    1. An Ordinance of the South Salt Lake City Council Amending Section 3.11.100(f) of the
       South Salt Lake City Municipal Code to Modify Glass Recycling Fees
       Finance Director, Crystal Makin, gave a summary of this item which was discussed by
       the Council at the previous meeting. Momentum Recycling, the City’s recycling services
       provider, is requesting a fee increase of $8/month per recycling bin to $8.50/month.

        A copy of the Ordinance is attached and incorporated by this reference.

        Council Member Williams made a motion to approve the Ordinance.
