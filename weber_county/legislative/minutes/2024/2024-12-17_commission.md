   D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Froerer: Well, Commissioners you're probably aware that next
   week we have a very important holiday coming up called Christmas. One of the traditions I grew up with and
   still like to participate in is a story written by a gentleman by the name of Charles Dickens; the story is called
   Christmas Carol with a star by the name of Ebenezer Scrooge. Charles wrote a very timely piece that we can all
   live by. As part of that story, he said this, “I will honor Christmas in my heart and try to keep it all year. I will
   live in the past, the present, and the future. The spirits of all three shall strive within me. I will not shut out the
   lessons that they taught.”

    5. APPROVAL OF RESOLUTION 58-2024 ADOPTING THE OPERATING AND CAPITAL BUDGETS
       OF WEBER COUNTY FOR CALENDAR YEAR 2025 AND DECLARING THE COUNTY’S INTENT
       TO PICK UP A CERTAIN PERCENTAGE OF MEMBERS’ CONTRIBUTIONS TO THE UTAH
       STATE RETIREMENT SYSTEM.

         Scott Parke (County Comptroller): A Public Hearing was held on November 26th to take public comment on
         the 2025 tentative budget. There have been a few tweaks to that presentation to review today.
                  Added a school security Chief, a lieutenant position out at the sheriff's office
                          o Requirement for House Bill 84.
                  Added some overtime to our CSI division, about $45,000
                  Added another $800k to the medical and mental health design project
                          o originally put in $1M, adding $800k to that
                          o It was announced on the radio this morning the County had funded a $30 million
                              medical and mental health facility out at the jail.
                                   There was some confusion as that is listed as a capital project requested in our
                                       budget. But it's clearly noted that funding is not appropriated for that yet. The
                                       commission has approved the design and it will go out to bid but the funding has
                                       not been approved for $30M.
                  Added a project a cost share with UDOT for 12th Street in 3500 West to do some improvements
                     on that intersection, about $1.2 million that comes from our prop one funding dedicated for Road
                     improvements
                  Holding purchase of two dump trucks from the Municipal Services fund until it is understood
                     what services, if any, the Upper Valley will contract with the County after the incorporation.

         Commissioner Bolos: Thankful for Directors and officers who followed through on our request to hold to a
         tight budget and be fiscally responsible.


         Commissioner Bolos moved to approve Resolution 58-2024 adopting the Operating and Capital Budgets of
         Weber County for Calendar Year 2025 and declaring the County’s Intent to pick up a certain percentage of
         members’ contributions to the Utah State Retirement System; Commissioner Froerer seconded.
         Roll Call Vote: Chair Harvey – aye; Commissioner Bolos – aye; Commissioner Froerer – aye
