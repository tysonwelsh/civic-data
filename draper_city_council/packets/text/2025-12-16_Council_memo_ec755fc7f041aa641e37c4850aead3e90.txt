MEMO
To: City Council
From: Todd Taylor
Date: 2025-12-16
Re: Approve Resolution #25-74


Comments:

Draper City adopted text amendments to the Draper City Municipal Code regarding the
Geologic Hazards Ordinance. Within the ordinance, Section 10-7-015 calls for the City
Manager to establish certain standards and specifications that shall be applicable to the
Geologic Hazards Ordinance. With the help of qualified professional reviewers, the City
has included such standards and specifications in the Geotechnical Engineering
Standards document, which will be kept on file with the Building Division for public
inspection and review. The purpose of this resolution is to adopt these standards.
