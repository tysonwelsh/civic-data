Proposed Resolution 2025-2 recognizing a $1,000,000 transfer from
Business Depot Ogden fund balance and appropriating those funds to the
Redevelopment Agency Quality Neighborhoods Infill Housing program
          A memo from the Management Services Department regarding a proposed Fiscal Year (FY) 2024-2025 budget
amendment came before the Board for consideration. The memo stated the proposed amendment, totaling $1,000,000, recognizes
a transfer from the Business Depot Ogden (BDO) fund to provide additional funding for the Quality Neighborhoods Initiative
(QNI). The Initiative was adopted in November 2015 for a five-year period with the goals of 1) reducing the concentration of low-
income or sub-standard housing in the East Central neighborhood, 2) increasing the standards of maintenance for residential units,
3) increasing owner-occupancy rates, and 4) increasing property values. The Initiative was renewed by resolution for an additional
five-year period in FY21. This proposed budget amendment brings forward the FY26 appropriation for the QNI to allow the city
to take advantage of development opportunities that are consistent with the QNI.
          Finance Director Sorensen summarized the memo and presented a spreadsheet detailing the source of funds for the
budget amendment.
          Executive Director Nadolski noted City Administration has been trying to be as proactive as possible in communicating
the needs for these types of requests to the Board, the public, and to other entities who need to be aware of them.
          Chair White introduced in writing proposed Resolution 2025-2, entitled:
                   “A resolution of the Ogden City Redevelopment Agency amending the budget for the Fiscal Year July 1, 2024
                   to June 30, 2025 by increasing the anticipated revenues for a gross increase of $1,000,000 from sources as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution; and increasing the appropriations for a gross increase of $1,000,000 as
                   detailed in the body of this resolution.”
          Chair White then opened the public hearing.
