This application is a public hearing and legislative action item.
In rendering a decision, the Planning Commission is serving a legislative function, decisions are based on reasonably
debatable evidence. The Planning Commission is making a recommendation to the City Council which has final decision
authority for rezone applications.

Sample Motion to Recommend Approval.
I move the Planning Commission recommend approval of application PLZ-26-4005, “Prince
Rezone,” thus rezoning the approximately 1.80-acres located at 12341 and 12389 South
Redwood Road to RM-6.

Sample Motion to Recommend Denial.
I move the Planning Commission recommend denial of application PLZ-26-4005, “Prince
Rezone,” for rezoning, thus keeping the current zoning (RR-22).

Background

This is a public hearing for a rezone application brought by the applicant, Alan Prince
representing MP & G LLC. This request covers two parcels which are located at 12345 and
12363 South Redwood Road. These parcels are not adjacent to one another, and each parcel
has an existing single-family home. The two (2) parcels included in the application with the
following parcel numbers:
        27-27-404-010
        27-27-404-034

General Plan
The General Plan for properties on the east side of Redwood Road from about 12310 South to about
12530 South (north of the church) designates all these properties as Medium-High Density
Residential (5-8 Dwelling units per acre) and includes a Historic Preservation designation. The
Medium-High Density designation was put in place sometime before the year 2000. The General
Plan suggests the properties could be zoned to RM-6 or RM-8 zones. Both zones allow single family
detached housing on small lots, the RM-8 Zone also allows attached housing such as townhomes or
condominiums. In 2020, the General Plan designation for Historic Preservation was put over the
existing Medium High Density designation as a way to consider the existing small homes facing
Redwood, but without any specific requirements.

Requested Zoning
The applicant is requesting RM-6 Zone which allows for a maximum density of six units per acre.
Permitted uses in the RM-6 Zone include Detached Single-Family housing, Household Pets, and
Public Parts. The minimum lot size in the RM-6 zone is 5,000-sf with minimum dimensions of 50-ft
wide by 80-ft deep. A development project in the RM-6 requires a site of at least ½-acre and can
utilize private streets as approved by the Planning Commission.

If the zoning were approved there would be additional applications, studies, reports and public
hearings for the site plan design.



Public Notices and Response
Notices were sent to all property owners within 1,000 feet of this site and posted on the City’s
webpage.

When considering rezone applications, the Planning Commission makes a recommendation to
City Council. The City Council will make a final decision on this application in a future City Council
Meeting where the public can attend and comment. Property owners within 1,000 feet will receive
an additional mailed notice announcing the date and time for the City Council Meeting.
