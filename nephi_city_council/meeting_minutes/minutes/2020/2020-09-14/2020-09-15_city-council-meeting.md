                                               NEPHI CITY
                                            COUNCIL MEETING
                                           September 15, 2020

The Nephi City Council met in regular session in the council chambers of city hall located at 21 East 100
North at 7:00 p.m. Tuesday, September 15, 2020.

                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .. Kent Jones

                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Larry Ostler
                Council Member . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . Justin D. Seely

Guests: Myrna Trauntvein (press), Tom Johnson, Hollie Holman, Kassandra Calalan, Guadalupe Jaimes.

Mayor Glade R. Nielson called the meeting to order and welcomed all present and then Councilor Skip
Worwood offered a prayer.

PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:
  The Pledge of Allegiance was led by Tom Johnson a Juab High alumnus representing the Juab High
graduating class of 1965. Mr. Johnson gave a brief history of the Pledge of Allegiance.

CONSENT AGENDA APPROVED:

   Councilor Memmott moved to approve the consent agenda which included the approval of the
minutes of the regular council meeting held on 9-1-20, the work session minutes 9-1-20, and a list of
claims on the warrant register dated 9-15-20 Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion
passed on a unanimous vote.

BUSINESS LICENSE APPROVED:

  Councilor Jones reviewed a business license application for La Venia Mexican Food Truck and then
moved to approve the business license. Councilor Seely seconded the motion. The motion passed on a
unanimous vote.

NO PUBLIC COMMENT

ORDINANCE 09-15-20 OHV REGULATIONS AND ORDINANCE 09-15-20-A GOLF CART REGULATIONS
ADOPTED:

 Two final drafts of ordinances regulating the driving of off highway vehicles and golf carts on city roads
were reviewed by the council. Councilor Worwood moved to adopt Ordnance 09-15-20: AN ORDINANCE
OF NEPHI CITY, STATE OF UTAH, AMENDING TITLE 6 CHAPTER 2 OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE-OFF HIGHWAY
VEHICLES. Councilor Ostler seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous vote.
  Councilor Memmott moved to adopt Ordinance 09-15-20-A: AN ORDINANCE OF NEPHI CITY, STATE
OF UTAH, ADOPTING CHAPTER 3 OF TITLE 6 OF THE NEPHI CITY CODE GOVERNING USE OF GOLF CARTS
WITHIN CITY LIMITS. The motion included a language change of 6-3-5 section E from “off highway
vehicle” to “golf cart.” Councilor Worwood seconded the motion. The motion passed on a unanimous
vote.

PLANNING ITEMS:

  City Administrator Seth Atkinson informed the council that the Malmgren Annexation petition had
been left off the agenda the petition had been received by the City Recorder after the agenda had been
posted. The council agreed to convene into a regular council meeting next week to vote on whether to
continue with the annexation process of the Malmgren property.

PROPERTY SOUTH OF THE CITY CHANGED TO A LEVEL 1 ON THE ANNEXATION AREA MAP:

   Mr. Atkinson informed the council that the two businesses interested in annexing on the south end of
the city had been given the cost estimates of the electrical utility installation to their properties and they
were both were still very interested in moving forward with the annexation process. Councilor Seely
asked about if this area could become an enterprise zone. Mr. Atkinson said that Economic
Development Director Brent Boswell has suggested that the entire city be made into an enterprise zone
so that businesses could apply for economic incentives from the state. Councilor Ostler moved to
change the area to a level 1 on the annexation area map. Councilor Jones seconded the motion. The
motion passed on a unanimous vote.

CITY ADMINISTRATOR UPDATES:

   Mr. Atkinson updated the council about the progress of the redundant gas line going through Mona
City.
   Mr. Atkinson updated the council that the interim financing bonds for the water project had closed.
He explained that on a normal closing of bonds, the money would be wired directly to the holder of the
bonds. With this closing, the USDA put the money into our checking account, and we issued two checks
to Zions’ Bank to close out the temporary financing.
  Mr. Atkinson told the council that the Airport Advisory Committee would meet next week before the
work session meeting. Councilor Worwood and Councilor Seely will sit on that committee.

CITY COUNCIL UPDATES:

  Councilor Seely updated the council that the Government Building Manager Clark Wood had recently
painted some accent walls in the library. He reported that on average 40 children per week are
participating in the children story time.
  Councilor Memmott stated that a couple of weeks ago the council had engaged in a healthy discussion
on the issuing of a beer license to the Ute Stampede Committee. He said that the council had to rely on
the state’s definitions because the city code didn’t define certain terms well enough. He suggested that
at the next work session the council could work on clarifying the city code’s definitions.
   Councilor Jones asked if the council could meet with the local livestock association to talk about the
lack of fencing on the city owned property in Reese Flat. He had information that cattle were coming
down the hill and ending up on Highway 132.
   Councilor Jones reported that Ideal Dairy had reopened, and that Paisley Quilt Patch fabric store had
recently opened for business.

COUNCIL MEETING ADJOURNED:

    Councilor Ostler moved to adjourn regular council meeting and convene into executive session to
discuss the character and competency of an individual, and the acquisition and disposition of real
property. Councilor Worwood seconded the motion, which passed on a unanimous vote.

   The meeting adjourned at 8:00 p.m.
