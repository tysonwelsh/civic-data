Re: Approval of Agreement # 20-179 Assessment in Lieu for McMillan Subdivision Comments: Draper City Municipal Code (DCMC) 17-5-020(B) requires all residential subdivisions construct public frontage improvements and allows the developer to satisfy the requirement through the payment of an assessment in lieu of construction per DCMC 9-27-110(C).
McMillan Subdivision is located at approximately 1155 East 13400 South where there are no existing frontage improvements.  The City Engineer's construction cost estimate is $15,900.  Staff recommends approval of the assessment in lieu agreement to facilitate the future design and construction of 13400 South by the city.
