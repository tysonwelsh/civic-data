Ordinances

            THIS BEING THE TIME heretofore set for a public hearing to receive public
comment regarding the following requests:

              Paul Ashton reviewed the following amendment to the Magna Metro Township’s
Code of Ordinances:

Ordinance 19-06-01 creating Title 12: “Code Enforcement and Community Preservation” and
subsequent chapters of the Magna Code of Ordinances to establish and regulate the
Administrative Code Enforcement process.

The Planning Commission recommended approval, although an amendment request was
submitted that had to do with Section 12.03.170 Prohibition Against Issuance of Municipal
Permits. Basically, the amendment says permits may be withheld for properties that are not in
compliance with requirements.

             Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to accept the
amendment to the proposed ordinance. The motion passed unanimously.

               Council Member Hull, seconded by Council Member Peel, moved to open the
public hearing for Ordinance 19-06-01. The motion passed unanimously.

              No one spoke in favor of or in opposition to the ordinance.

               Council Member Peel, seconded by Council Member Hull, moved to close the
public hearing. The motion passed unanimously.

Citizen Public Input

                Greg Schulz stated there has been a lot of misinformation about the Inland Port
and Inland Port Authority and fossil fuel shipment, so he wanted the description of the
transportation of coal or overseas shipments on public record. Coal is shipped in bulk, not in
crates like is done in an inland port. When coal is taken out of a mine, it is moved to a transfer
concentrator near the coal mine and near a rail line on a conveyor or a truck, after which, it is
moved onto a train. Then, the train is given a dedicated right of way from the time it is loaded to
the time the product is dropped off at the actual port, not the inland port.

Magna Mosquito Abatement District

               Council Member Hull stated mosquitoes are bad this year, but there has not been
a case of West Nile Virus yet anywhere in the state. The Magna Mosquito Abatement District did
get a new testing system that tests mosquitoes for West Nile Virus and other things, and the test
results are immediate. The mosquitos that come out at night by water are the mosquitoes that
carry the disease; the mosquitoes in the mountains that are out in the day do not.

Resolution 19-07-01

               Paul Ashton reviewed Resolution 19-07-01 recognizing the Magna Coalition as a
committee of the Magna Metro Township for the purposes of funding and oversight of the creation
of the Magna Communities that Care program.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-07-01                                                     Date: July 9, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       THE RECOGNITION OF THE MAGNA COALITION AS A COMMITTEE OF THE
       MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP FOR THE PUPOSES OF FUNDING AND
       OVERSIGHT OF THE MAGNA “COMMUNITY THAT CARES” PROGRAM

                                          RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. On April 9, 2019, Councilmember Hull gave a report from the Magna Coalition regarding
      the “Community That Cares” program and got Council approval to pursue possible grant
      funding to pay for the creation and operation of the program.

   D. In May 2019, the Magna Coalition was awarded a one year, with an option for two
      additional years, Substance Abuse Prevention Grant of $88,000 to provide funding for the
      staffing and creation of the Magna “Community That Cares” program.

   E. Pursuant to the contract, the Council must recognize the Magna Coalition as a Committee
      of Magna retroactive to July 1, 2019 and task the Committee to oversee and operate the
      creation of the Magna “Community That Cares” program to complete the transaction.

                                        RESOLUTION

     THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, the Council hereby recognizes the Magna Coalition (the
“Committee”) as a committee of the Magna Metro Township retroactive to July 1, 2019; and

      BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the Committee is tasked to provide oversight and
management of the creation and operation of the “Magna Community that Cares” program
pursuant to the receipt of grant funding per the attached contract (EXHIBIT “A”); and

       BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, the grand funding received be managed by fiscal staff as
separate from the other monies of Magna to insure proper recording of the receipt of revenues
and accounting for the disbursements of funds.

Resolution 19-07-02

               Paul Ashton reviewed Resolution 19-07-02 approving and adopting Policy #10 –
Personal Use of Public Property. The Legislature changed the state statute making it a
misdemeanor for board members, council members, and employees of government entities to
use government property for personal matters, unless the entity adopted a policy allowing for
incidental use of the property for personal matters. Mark Anderson, Legal Counsel, GSLMSD put
the policy together and recommended metro townships approve it.

RESOLUTION NO. 19-07-02                                                     Date: July 9, 2019

       A RESOLUTION OF THE MAGNA METRO TOWNSHIP COUNCIL APPROVING
       AND ADOPTING POLICY #10 – “PERSONAL USE OF PUBLIC PROPERTY”
       PURSUANT TO STATE LAW

                                         RECITALS

   A. The Magna Metro Township (“Magna”) is a municipality pursuant to Utah Code §§ 10-2a-
      401 et seq.

   B. The Magna Metro Township Council (the “Council”) is the municipal legislative body for
      Magna pursuant to Utah Code § 10-3b-501.

   C. Pursuant to HB163, passed by the Utah Legislature during the 2019 General Session, all
      government entities are required to adopt a policy regulating the personal use of public
      property.

   D. The attached Policy #10 – “Personal Use of Public Property, hereinafter known as “Exhibit
      A” satisfies the state law.
