City of Draper Notice of Ordinance Adoption – Ordinance #1628

On December 3, 2024, the Draper City Council approved Ordinance #1628 amending
the official zoning map of Draper City. The complete ordinance is on file at the Draper
City Recorder’s Office and online at www.draperutah.gov. Published this 5th day of
December 2024. Laura Oscarson, Draper City Recorder.
