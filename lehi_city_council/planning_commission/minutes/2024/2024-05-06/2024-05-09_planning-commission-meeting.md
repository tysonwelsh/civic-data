2. Regular Agenda

2.1) Public hearing and consideration of Mark Coddington’s request for conditional use
     approval for a conversion of an existing accessory building into a detached accessory
     dwelling unit located at 444 South 100 West.
Mr. Coddington was representing the applicant.

Chairman Jackson opened the public hearing.

Theresa Mott inquired about the minimum size.

Chairman Jackson closed the public hearing.

Commissioners discussed compliance with ADU size regulations and confirmed that the
proposal meets code. It was also clarified that only one ADU is allowed per property.

                existing accessory building into a detached accessory dwelling unit located at 444

    Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
    Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion

2.2) Public hearing and recommendation of Bill Elton’s request for review of the Triple B
     Investment Group General Plan Amendment on 15-acres of property located at
     approximately 1630 South 500 East, changing the land use designation from ESA
     (environmentally sensitive area) to VLDRA (very low density residential agriculture).
Tony Trane was representing the applicant.

Kim Struthers’s, Community Development Director, explained that the property is no longer in
the floodplain due to elevation changes from infill. Mr. Struthers informed the Council that the
applicant was granted a grading permit in 2007 to import fill on the property. The amendment
would affect approximately 15 acres and any development would occur north of the future road.

Chairman Jackson opened the public hearing.

Wes Davey expressed concerns about the proposed development, citing environmental and
traffic concerns. He also asked the Commission to consider potential impacts to the surrounding
areas.

Theresa Mott inquired about the purpose and extent of the development. She expressed concerns
with traffic congestion and potential impacts.

Nathan Luthi stated that he lives near the proposed development. He was concerned with traffic
implications and adding additional density to the area.

Mr. Kolan stated that he is representing the Kolan family’s interests as they own property next to
the proposal. His concerns addressed changes to zoning and the implications of that.

Chairman Jackson closed the public hearing.

Mr. Trane answered questions and stated that this is Very Low Density Residential and they will
preserve the right of way for a future road and that development will take place on the north side
of the future road. There have been discussions to cluster the development creating more open
space near the future road.

Brad Kenison, Assistant City Engineer, informed the Commission of regional traffic plans for
the area which included widening and extending 1900 S (Pony Express Parkway), widening
Pioneer Crossing, and an offshore roadway.

Mr. Struthers explained that the ESA designation no longer applied due to the elevation change
and noted that the infill was done legally through a grading permit and confirmed that other
federal requirements were met.


Commissioner Lockhart expressed frustration with the Commission’s limited scope of decision-
making and emphasized the importance of public input on future ESA decisions.

Commissioner Jones noted that he is inclined to recommend approval because the infill has
already been placed, so the ESA designation no longer applies and the need for housing.

Commissioner Jackson stated that he believes the infill component is an important piece. It helps
draw a sharp line in terms of setting precedent in the area.

    Motion:     Commissioner Jones moved to recommend approval of the Triple B Investment
                Group General Plan Amendment on 15-acres of property located at approximately
                1630 South 500 East, changing the land use designation from ESA
                (environmentally sensitive area) to VLDRA (very low density residential
                agriculture); including the Development Review Committee comments; with the
                findings stated in the staff report, and also include the findings that the infill placed
                from an approved grading permit was heavily weighted in the decision; and noting
                that any future request for a grading permit would require any applicant to go
                through the appropriate approval process. Commissioner Lockhart seconded the
                motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
    Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion
    passed unanimously.

2.3) Consideration of DHI Communities request for approval of the Ascend at Skye concept
     plan including 302 apartment units located at approximately Center Street and
     Aberdeen Way requesting a substitution of amenities.
Jennifer Milburn was representing the applicant. Ms. Milburn reviewed the amenities they are
planning to provide, including a larger clubhouse and pool to substitute for other amenities. She
also noted that the community’s picnic area is substantial with higher quality outdoor dining
tables and trellis.

    Motion:     Commissioner Roberts moved to approve the Ascend at Skye concept plan
                including 302 apartment units located at approximately Center Street and Aberdeen
                Way and grant the substitution of amenities, finding that the proposed amenities are
                equivalent or greater value than what is required; also noting other findings stated
                in the staff report. Commissioner Kunze seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
    Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion
    passed unanimously.

2.4) Public hearing and recommendation of Texas Instrument’s request for review of the
     LFAB2 grading permit on their 668-acre parcel located at 4000 North Flash Drive.

2.5) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of updates to the
     Goals, Strategies, and Timeline for Moderate Income Housing.
Katie Bussell presented the item. She noted that this is a minor update to the strategies. None of
the goals changed.

Chairman Jackson opened the public hearing.

Marshall Gary discussed the ineffectiveness of high-density housing solutions for affordability,
citing market-rate sales and lack of affordable options. He expressed interest in focusing on
transportation, economic opportunities, and infrastructure development. He advocated for
people-oriented places and criticized sprawl. He argued that high-density housing can be cost-
effective if built near existing infrastructure.

Chairman Jackson closed the public hearing.

    Motion:     Commissioner Kunze moved to recommend approval of updates to the Goals,
                Strategies, and Timeline for Moderate Income Housing to meeting the timeline for
                compliance as required by the State; with the findings that this will increase
                affordability and is in conformance with the purposes and intent of the General Plan
                and its various elements; and also including Development Review Committee
                comments. Commissioner Jones seconded the motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
    Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion
    passed unanimously.

2.6) Public hearing and recommendation of Lehi City’s request for review of updates to
     several standard drawings in the Design Standards and Public Improvement
     Specifications Manual.
Katie Bussell presented the item and stated that the Engineering Department requested changes
to modernize the design standards and meet state code.

Chairman Jackson opened the public hearing.

Marchal Gary highlighted the critical impact of speed on pedestrian safety, emphasizing that
even small increases in vehicle speed can significantly increase the risk of serious injury or
death. He stated that residents want safe and comfortable walking and cycling for themselves and
their children. He suggested using traffic calming measures to slow traffic down and improve
walkability and bike ability.

Chairman Jackson closed the public hearing.

The Commission discussed ideas to improve safety.

    Motion:     Commissioner Lockhart moved to recommend approval of updates to several
                standard drawings in the Design Standards and Public Improvement Specifications
                Manual; with the finding that it is in conformance with the purpose’s intent and
                provisions of the General Plan and its various elements; and include all
                Development Review Committee Comments. Commissioner Kunze seconded the
                motion.

    Roll Call Vote: Commissioner Jackson, Yes; Commissioner Jones, Yes; Commissioner
    Kunze, Yes; Commissioner Lockhart, Yes; and Commissioner Roberts, Yes. The motion
    passed unanimously.
