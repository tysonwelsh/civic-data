Discussion regarding the Silver Summit Interchange Study; Carl Miller, Brandon Brady, Gary Horton, and Chip Hill (4:13 P.M.)
Brandon Brady, Regional Transportation Coordinator, led a discussion regarding the Silver Summit Interchange Study. Gary Horton, Wall Consulting Group (WCG) Project Manager, presented the findings of WCG’s study and recommendations. Council members asked questions and commented. Mr. Brady, Mr. Horton, Carl Miller, Transportation Planning Director, and Dave Thomas, Chief Civil Deputy Attorney, responded. Chip Hill, Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT) Region 2 commented on how UDOT considers the options and the consequences of the choices.

Discussion and approval of Proclamation 2023-04, Declaring April Parkinson's Awareness Month; Phil Bondurant (5:08 P.M.)
Phil Bondurant, Health Department Director, presented Proclamation 2023-04, for approval. Deputy Manager Janna Young commented as well.
