Regional Housing Authority Update (9:04 AM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Attachment: Staff Report-Regional Housing Authority.pdf
Attachment: Regional Housing Authority Presentation-corrected.pdf
Jeff Jones, County Economic Development and Housing Director, provided quick background on the Housing Authority investigation and update about the feedback received through outreach.
Jason Glidden, Park City Affordable Housing Manager, summarized survey answers.
Council Members asked questions and commented. All Council members agreed the City and County should work together on housing. Council members asked the committee to tease out feasibility issues with respect to a housing authority vs. having internal, regional housing initiatives with cooperation between governmental entities and how that affects funding opportunities.
2)
Childcare update (10:25 AM)
Attachment: Cover Page
Michelle Downard, Park City Resident Advocate, presented the actions Park City has taken, and hopes to take in the future, to support childcare in the community. Sarah Mangano, Park City Human Resources Director supported the presentation.
Mayor Worel invited the County to work with Park City on this issue. Roger Armstrong discussed options the County is investigating with respect to childcare. Council Members commented.
Attachment: Staff Report-Childcare.pdf
3)
Peak Winter traffic mitigation efforts update (10:40 AM)
Andrew Leatham, Park City Traffic Operations Manager, presented Park City’s plans to address traffic for the coming winter. Council Members asked questions. Mr. Leatham responded.
4)
Wrap up comments and schedule next joint meeting (10:57 AM)
County Council Chair Armstrong closed the meeting with agreement to meet again in early November at Park City Hall.
