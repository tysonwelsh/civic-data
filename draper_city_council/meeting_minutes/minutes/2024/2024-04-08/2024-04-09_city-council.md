7.b   Action Item: Providing Local Consent for an Off-Premises Beer Alcohol
      License for Demani Enterprise LLC dba Urban Smokes
      Business License Official Travis DeJong said the applicant met standards and
      requirements, including parking requirements, for local consent for an Off-
      Premises Beer Alcohol License.

      Responding to a question from Councilmember T. Lowery, Mr. DeJong
      explained that preschools were not included in the distance standards for
      community locations established by the State. Councilmember T. Lowery
      mentioned a nearby preschool, gymnastics studio, and other locations that
      could fall under the discretion of educational facilities. Mr. DeJong spoke of
      parameters for the administrative decision. Councilmember F. Lowry
      expressed concern for the success of the proposed business model at the
      planned location.

      Councilmember F. Lowry moved to approve the Local Consent for an
      Off-Premises Beer Alcohol License for Demani Enterprise LLC dba
      Urban Smokes. Councilmember Roberts seconded the motion.
