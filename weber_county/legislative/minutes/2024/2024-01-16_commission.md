D. THOUGHT OF THE DAY: Commissioner Bolos: Yesterday we celebrated Martin Luther King Jr Day and I
   listened to his “I Have a Dream” speech. It struck me that while he and the protesters were protesting in the
   midst of so much oppression and hate, he called out on more than one occasion in his speech to respond not with
   violence but instead respond with love and other terms that say “we don't need to fight fire with fire”. I was
   really impressed by that. A lot of times in our lives we face people that are hateful and mean and do not treat us
   right. It’s really easy in human nature to want to respond in a like manner. We can accomplish so much more if
   we respond in an opposite manner, with love and respect and try to work things out on that level.

         2.     Public hearing to consider a County-initiated ordinance amendment to Title 106, Subdivisions,
                providing for changes relative to administrative land use authority, subdivision application review and
                approval procedures, and financial guarantees required for public improvements. ZTA 2023-11 and
                ZTA 2023-12.

                Bill Cobabe (Planning): This item was discussed in work session last week with no changes. We had
                a discussion with Lance Keelimore of the UAC Council who recommends, along with Staff, on page 5
                line 76 of the staff report under Administrative Land Use Authority, where we had designated for a
                preliminary plat that it would remain at the Planning Commission level, per the planning commission's
                recommendation. This is in compliance with Senate Bill 174 that requires us to comply by February
                1st.

                Commissioner Froerer: Bill, I appreciate your work on this. I fully support this change to allow the
                Planning Commission to have a say in the preliminary plat. We can expect some changes coming out
                of the legislature this year. For right now, this is the proper way to go.
