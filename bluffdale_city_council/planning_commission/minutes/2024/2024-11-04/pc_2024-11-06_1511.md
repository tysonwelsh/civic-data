5.     CONSIDERATION AND RECOMMENDATION on a proposed Text Amendment to
       Section 11,110.110 of the Bluffdale City Code to Create a Definition for Satellite
       Communication Stations and to Allow Satellite Communication Stations as a Permitted
       Use in the SD-X Gateway Zone (Application 2024-36. inRange Solutions II. LLC.
       Applicant. Caitlin Tubbs. Staff Presenter. (Tabled followins October Z 2024 Public
       Hearine),


Planning Manager, Caitlin Tubbs presented the Staff Report and stated that the proposed Text
Amendment applies to the single parcel SD-X Gateway Zone at 383 West 14600 South. The applicant
has proposed a Satellite Communications Station on the southern end ofthe parcel. The request was
first reviewed by the Planning Commission at an October 2, 2024, Public Hearing. At that time,
additional information was requested by the Commission,including the FCC call sign ofthe proposed
station, photographs ofthe proposed fencing and similar sites, and a Radiation hnpact Analysis. The
applicant provided those items for the Commission's consideration.

The Concept Plan was reviewed as well as photographs ofsimilar satellite communication arrays and
antennas. The amendment would allow Satellite Communications Stations as a Permitted Use in the

zone. Per the Planning Commission's previous comments. Staff added a caveat that the stations
would be permitted in up to 0.5 acres or 27,780 square feet ofthe zone.


The Radiation Impact Analysis determined that radiation will not exceed industry standards outside
ofthe enclosure. There will be higher levels within the enclosure, but it is an unmanned facility with
the ability to remotely power down the antennas to ensure safety for any work crews. Commissioner
Kraupp asked about the risk of someone entering the facility without prior notice and without the
equipment being powered down. Ms. Tubbs reported that warning signage examples were provided
with the Concept Plan. Such signage is required by federal law and would be posted around the
enclosure.


Chair Cragun noted that the facility will be next door to a hotel and asked about the impact on hotel
guests. Ms. Tubbs referred to the Radiation Impact Analysis, which indicates that they do not
anticipate impacts to residents or business patrons as the site currently exists. However, there is a
note that it may potentially exceed recommended limits up to a distance of 240.8 meters or 790 feet

from the antenna main beam at a 25-degree angle from the topographical surface. That may impact
future development on the site. The applicant indicated that they would relocate the facility to the
top of the hotel once it is built. However, if Satellite Communications Stations were allowed, they
would always be entitled in the zone. A potential negative impact on hotel guests is signal
interference, so it would not be an ideal situation for either the hotel or the communications facility.

Commissioner Flynn asked about potential negative impacts to the security systems at the John Deere

Commissioner Kraupp asked about risks to hotel guests when the equipment is moved to the top of

Sample photographs ofexisting installations were reviewed, as well as the proposed eight-foot metal

any additional design standards, landscaping requirements, and/or setbacks should be enacted for the

proposed facility has metal fencing and no landscaping.

metal fence should not be allowed and instead require the adopted design standards for utility
substations, it would have to meet those standards.


Staff recommended that the Planning Commission review the proposal and consider forwarding a
recommendation to the City Council. If they had additional questions, they could also consider
continuing the item to a future date certain or uncertain.

be relocated when the hotel is built but expressed concern that the facility could remain in place for

6.       PUBLIC HEARING.CONSIDERATION.AND RECOMMENDATION on a oronosed
         Text Amendment to Enact Section 11.160.310 of the Bluffdale Citv Code to Permit
         Specific Encroachments into Residential Rear Setbacks per Utah Code Sectio 10-9a-540
         (Application 2024-441. Citv of Bluffdale. Applicant. Caitlvn Tubbs. Staff Presenter.

Ms.Tubbs presented the StaffReport and stated that the Text Amendment would bring Bluffdale City
Code into compliance with new Utah Code 10-9a-540, enacted by House Bill 476. The Text
Amendment would add section 11.60.310, Projections into Rear Setbacks for Detached Residences,
to include the following "Allowable Features" within residential rear setbacks:


     • Landings or walkout porches that are:
              o No more than 32 square feet in size; and
              o Used for ingress or egress into a residential dwelling.
     •   Window wells.


In response to a question raised by Chair Cragun, Ms. Tubbs indicated that the bill was in response
to instances where window wells or porches were required to be outside of setbacks. With certain
types ofresidential developments,including townhomes and small cottages,that requirement severely
restricts the buildable area. Mr. Crowell clarified that a number ofencroachments are already allowed
by City Code but the specific wording was being added to correspond with State Code. Staff
requested that the Planning Commission review the proposal, conduct a public hearing, and consider
forwarding a positive recommendation to the City Council.
