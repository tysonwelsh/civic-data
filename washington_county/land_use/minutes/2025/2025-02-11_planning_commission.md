10.   VARIANCE (Public Meeting – Quasi Judicial). The applicant is seeking a variance to allow
      accessory structures in the roadway setback. The address is 812 N Wild Herb Road,
      Dammeron Valley, UT and the parcel # is DVH-48. The applicant installed horse shelter
      structures in the roadway setback, which resulted in a code violation. The applicant is
      Carl Mattox.
      Ms. Hales explained the process for this quasi-judicial item for all to understand. The
      process is as follows:
         1. Staff will present their decision.
         2. The applicant will present his request for variance with all facts and items he
            would like to include.
         3. Ms. Hales will be asking questions to both staff and applicant in order to create a
            record.
         4. Any decision that is made on a variance will have to have substantial evidence to
            support its findings.
         5. All of the facts are gathered at a public meeting, and if the board members would


            111 East Tabernacle I St. George, UT 84770 I P 435.301.7250

          like to go into a closed meeting, a motion would need to be made to go into a
          closed meeting to deliberate on the item.
     6. Once that closed meeting is done, the results will need to be presented in a public
        meeting with findings and substantial evidence for the board’s decision and
        provided for the record.
Staff Description:

The applicant constructed a horse shelter in the front yard setback of their owner-occupied
home. The placement of the horse shelter was not reviewed by county staff because it did
not require a building permit. The 1.71 acre lot (Lot 48) is located in the Dammeron Valley
Homesteads Subdivision in Dammeron Valley. The three-sided lot is located along the
inside radius of a bend in Wild Herb Road. The entire frontage on Wild Herb Road is the
front yard lot line. The front yard setback is 25’ in the RE-40 (Residential Estate 40,000
Square Foot Minimum Lot Size) zone. Washington County Community Development staff
notified the subject property owner of code violation. Wild Herb Road is a dedicated
County Right of Way built to Washington County Subdivision Standards; 50’ right of way,
with 28’ of asphalt. The property line for parcel DVH-48 is represented by an existing split
rail fence which runs along a portion of the frontage.
The horse shelter is comprised of two 10’ x 20’ units, which individually did not require
building permits due to not exceeding 200 sq ft. The two shelters have been joined end to
end creating a total structure area of 400 sq ft.
Staff Decision:

Community Development Staff have considered the applicant’s request for a variance of
the setback requirements, and concluded the horse shelter structures do not meet the
setback requirements in the code. As the recommending body, Community Development
staff does not favor approval of the variance request, because the request does not meet
State or local requirements for a variance.
      •    Setback requirement from a roadway is 25’ from the property line in the RE-
           40 zone on both the front yard and street side yard.
      •    There are no special circumstances specific to this lot which wouldn’t apply
           to other lots in the same zone.
      •    The hardship is self-imposed.
      •    State code on variance was quoted by Mr. Messel which supports staffs findings.
      •    The applicant can further appeal the decision of the Land Use Authority by
           means of a Hearing Officer.
The Planning Commission is the Land Use Authority on this matter. The applicant requests
that the Planning Commission review the denial recommendation of the Community
Development staff.

Ms. Hales presented the results of the closed session:
The Planning Commission deliberated in the closed session and based on that deliberation the
Planning Commission makes the following findings to support by substantial evidence the
conclusion that the variance will be denied due to the following:
        -   A number of the Planning Commissioners have looked in person at the area.
        -   They have also looked at the applicants application and at the applicants
            representative and testimony at the public meeting as well as the presentations by
            staff.
        -   It is their determination that there are many outbuildings in the area and horse and
            farm structures and they are constructed outside the front setback.
        -   There are many detached accessory structures meeting the code.
        -   The applicants therefore has not proven and has not met their burden of proof for the
            requirements found in Utah Code annotated 17-27a-702 sub paragraphs 2a 1 through
            5.
        -   There was no special circumstance related to the property that would justify a
            variance.
        -   There is no unreasonable hardship to enforce the code.
        -   Violating the code was self-imposed.
        -   Primarily economic hardship is not a valid reason to grant a request for a variance.
        -   No special circumstances or peculiar property issues exist that would make it
            difficult or impossible to meet the front setback.
        -   Three sided lots or corner lots all have the same 25-foot setback in the area.

         -   There are no topographical restrictions that would create a special circumstance or
             something peculiar to the property that would prevent compliance with the front
             setback requirements.
         -   A variance in this instance is not essential to enjoyment of a substantial property
             right.
         -   The structure can be moved on this very large lot to meet the front, side or rear
             setback.
         -   Granting a variance in this instance would be against the public interest because all
             people in the area have to meet the setbacks.
