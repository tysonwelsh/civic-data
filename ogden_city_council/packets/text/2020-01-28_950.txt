                                               4:00 p.m. Work Session
                                                     City Council
                                               City Council Work Room
                                                   January 28, 2020

The purpose of the work session includes presentations and discussions regarding:
    • Agenda review for the City Council meeting;
    • Proposed amendment to the Lynn Community Plan;
    • Proposed rezone of 140-152 West 2nd Street;
    • Arts Ordinance Amendment;
    • Council business; and
    • Council comments.

                           Any items not fully addressed prior to the City Council meeting,
                   which begins at 6:00 p.m., may be addressed immediately following that meeting.


                                            6:00 p.m. City Council Meeting
                                                City Council Chambers
                                                   January 28, 2020

1. Roll Call.

2. Opening Ceremony:
   a. Pledge of Allegiance.
   b. Moment of Silence.

3. Recognitions:
   a. Black History Month. Joint proclamation declaring February as “Black History Month” in Ogden City. (Adopt joint
      proclamation – voice vote)
   b. Idle Free Week. Joint proclamation declaring February 2-8, 2020 as “Idle-Free Week” in Ogden to educate residents
      about the effects of idling and how to limit car emissions. (Adopt joint proclamation – voice vote)

4. Request to be on the Agenda:
   a. St. Joseph Catholic School. Presentation from Judge Mark DeCaria regarding fundraising efforts for building
      additions to the St. Joseph Catholic School. (No action required)

5. Common Consent: (Voice vote)
   a. Ogden Trails Network Committee. Consideration of the appointment of Nathan Silberman to the Ogden Trails
      Network Committee. (Approve appointment)

6. Public Comments. This is an opportunity to address the Council regarding concerns or ideas on any topic. To be
   considerate of everyone at this meeting, public comment will be limited to three minutes per person. Participants are
   to state their name for the record. Comments which cannot be made within these limits, should be submitted in writing
   to the City Council Office (citycouncil@ogdencity.com).

    The Council encourages civil discourse for everyone who participates in our meetings. Comments pertaining to an
    agenda item that includes a public hearing or public input should be given during the meeting as that item is discussed.

7. Comments:
   a. Mayor.
   b. Council members.

8. Adjournment.


                                           Continuation of Work Session if Necessary
