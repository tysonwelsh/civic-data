3.   Public hearing and possible action regarding the Leavitt’s Contractors Yard 
Conditional Use Permit; 23 West Rob Young Lane; Parcel JWR-1; Michael Leavitt, 
applicant. – Ray Milliner, Principal Planner 
 
Commissioner Sargent recused himself from this item because he represents the 
landowner to the south of the applicant in real estate activity. 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this application. The applicant is requesting a 
Contractors Yard to have a storage area to park machinery, and a shed to park and repair 
vehicles. There is a creek and wetlands that run through the property. There has already 
been significant public input received through email. Staff is asking the Planning 
Commission for input concerning and Conditions of Approval or mitigations that would be 
appropriate for this application. If the Planning Commission chooses to approve the 
application, Staff has recommended mitigations that were included in the Staff Report, 
including screening, buffer areas, dust mitigation, traffic, etc. Since the applicant is not in 
attendance, it is recommended that no action be taken tonight. Public input should be taken 
and Staff be given direction. 
Commissioner Wheaton stated there is no conclusive proof that mitigations will prevent 
environmental degradation. Planner Milliner stated Staff cannot get conclusive proof 
without continual monitoring. Staff can get conclusive proof about how grading will affect 
runoff. Commissioner Benson stated adding asphalt surfacing, as proposed would cause 
more dust to drift. 
The public hearing was opened.

5. Public Hearing and possible action regarding the Eastern Summit County 
Development Code that regulates fencing of livestock as it is related to 
nonagricultural development adjacent to an existing agricultural operation; Section 
11-2-2.C. – Ray Milliner, Principal Planner  
 
Planner Milliner shared the background for this discussion. There have been a couple of 
Work Sessions for this item, with no clear directions. Attorney Strachan is in the process of 
rewriting fencing regulations for the Animal Control section of the Code and has 
recommended that fencing be eliminated from the Planning section of the Code and just 
have fencing in the Animal Control section. If fencing is not something that is being 
Eastern Summit County Planning Commission 
June 20, 2019 
Page 11 of 12 
 
addressed for aesthetics, then it is best to be left in the Animal Control section of the Code. 
The Animal Control rule is fence in. Development Agreements supersede the Code, so if a 
Development Agreement rule is fence out, that rule applies. Any Village Overlays will have 
fencing addressed in their agreements. 
 
The public hearing was opened, no public comment was made. The public hearing was closed. 
 
 
Commissioner Benson asked how strict Animal Control is with fencing violations. We do 
not want to get too strict with the ranchers. Planner Milliner answered the first violation 
receives a warning and enforcement will ramp up from there to possible citations. 
 
Commissioner Wilde made a motion to forward a positive recommendation to the 
County Council to eliminate fencing requirement from the land use table and have 
fencing language be fence in for the Animal Control section of the Code. Commissioner 
Sonntag seconded the motion. 
• MOTION CARRIED (7-0)
