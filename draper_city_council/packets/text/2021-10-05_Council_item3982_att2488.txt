                                 RESOLUTION NO. 21-53

      A RESOLUTION OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL ENTERING INTO AN
      AGREEMENT.


       WHEREAS, the Draper Irrigation Company is currently in the process of getting funding
to create a water reuse system; and

     WHEREAS, the implementation of that water reuse system relies on obtaining
municipal reuse water; and

      WHEREAS, water reuse is an effective strategy to battle drought conditions; and

      WHEREAS, the State of Utah is currently in a significant drought; and

       WHEREAS, Draper City is committed to doing its part in implementing the best
practices for drought resistance; and

      WHEREAS, Draper Irrigation Company and Draper City desire to enter into an
agreement wherein Draper City provides Draper Irrigation Company with its municipal reuse
water; and

      WHEREAS, The City Council finds this agreement ensures the health, safety, and
welfare of the citizens of Draper City.

     NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER CITY, STATE OF
UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

      Section 1. Agreement. Upon signature of the contract attached hereto as Exhibit A,
Draper City enters into an agreement to provide Draper Irrigation Company with its municipal
reuse water.

      Section 2. Terms. Draper City agrees to all the terms and conditions contained in
the agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A.

        Section 3. Severability. If any section, part or provision of this Resolution is held
invalid or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion
of this Resolution, and all sections, parts and provisions of this Resolution shall be severable.

       Section 4.   Effective Date. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon
its passage.
