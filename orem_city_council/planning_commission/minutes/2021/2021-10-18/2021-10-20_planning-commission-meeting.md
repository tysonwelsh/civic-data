Chair Cochran asked Ms. Vargas to introduce Agenda Item 3.2:

Agenda Item 3.2 is a request by Mike Hollopeter, PEG Companies for the Planning Commission to the Planning
Commission forward a recommendation to the City Council to amend Article 22-5-3(A) and the zoning map of the
City of Orem by changing the zone on approximately 0.51 acres from the R6.5 zone to the PD29 zone Siena Villas
@ Columbia Lane located generally at 1863 South and 1875 South Terrace Drive and amending portions of Section
22-11-42, PD29 zone (Siena Villas @ Columbia Lane) pertaining to the overall density and parking requirements of
the PD29 zone and amending Appendix V of the Orem City Code with updated concept plans and elevations,
located generally at 1859 South Columbia Lane.

                                                          Staff Presentation: The applicant wants to apply to build a
                                                          21-unit expansion onto the current 81-unit Siena Villas
                                                          Apartments. These 21 units would be comprised of six two-
                                                          bedroom units, nine one-bedroom units and six studio units.
                                                          This new building will be located at the south-east area of the
                                                          proposed lot. Neighborhood meetings for this proposed
                                                          project were held in September 2020.

                                                           As part of the request, the applicant is proposing a text
                                                           amendment to the PD29 zone that includes a small parking
ratio change of 1.75 stalls per unit in addition to a slight change in the unit per acre limit (currently at 27 units per
acre) to 28 units per acre.

Parking: Currently, they have a business hour parking requirement and a non-business hour parking requirement.
The business hour parking requirement is defined as 8:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday through Friday and they
provide at least 1.75 parking spaces for each one-bedroom dwelling unit, and at least 2.25 parking spaces for each
dwelling unit containing more than one bedroom. During the non-business hour parking requirement, they provide
at least 2.25 parking spaces for each dwelling unit. Parking spaces located on the adjacent property are made
available to the residents of the development through a recorded, perpetual parking easement and is counted toward
the parking requirement.

The applicant has been tracking the parking demand and utilization over the past nine years. They have found that
the usage is significantly lower than the current required amount. They have performed multiple parking studies
internally and have found on the rare occasion the maximum usage is 1.4 stalls per unit. Recent counts have shown
less utilization. They attribute these lower parking counts to the high number of one-bedroom units (fifty percent of
the property unit mix) and being located near public transportation lines. In addition, they have no plans to charge
for parking in the future. This language is part of the text amendment that is being proposed for the PD29 zone.

A handout of the traffic study that was referenced during the presentation can be found attached at the end of these
minutes.

While intending to maintain unit mix commensurate with the existing property (fifty percent one-bedroom and fifty
percent two-bedroom units), they recognize the demand and need for studio units in the marketplace. They are
proposing to add six studio units to the project.

If the City approves these changes, the applicant will come back in and apply for site plan approval.
