Mayor Pro Tern Aston thanked those present for their attendance. She asked Mayor Elect Natalie

community will consider that message. Pastor Hornok offered the Invocation and led the Pledge.

All members of the City Council were present with the exception of Jeff Gaston, who was excused.

2.       Mayor Elect, Natalie Hall; Council Members Elect, Wendy Aston and Traci Crockett,
         Administered by Judge Scott Mickelsen.

Jud ge Scott Mickelsen administered the Oath of Office to Counc il Members Wendy Aston and

Reid recognized Mayor Hall and presented her with an engraved gavel. Mayor Hall, on behalf of
the City Council, thanked all of the friends, family, and residents in attendance. She stated that
her goal is to make Bluffda le the best City it can be and stressed the importance of unity.

Mayor Hall recognized Mayor Derk Timothy for hi s 12 years of dedicated service. She noted that
not only did he serve Bluffdale for 12 years, but he had a full -time job in addition to his
responsibilities within the City. He did a lot for Bluffdale. Mayor Hall encouraged those present
to look at the January 2022 Bluffdale Newsletter to read about his many accomplishments.

Mayor Hall hoped the Invocation offered by Pastor Hornok would be considered by the City
Co uncil. She stressed the impo1iance of hav ing "soft hearts and thi ck skins" and working together.
