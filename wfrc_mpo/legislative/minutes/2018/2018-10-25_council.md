ACTION: WFRC Nominating Committee [00:04:06]
The WFRC Bylaws state that every two years (in even-numbered years), the Chair appoints a
nominating committee to identify and nominate the Council Chair and Vice Chair. The
nominating committee also makes recommendations for other committee chairs and vice chairs.
The nominating committee includes one representative from each of the six WFRC counties.
The Council will vote at the January 2019 meeting to elect a new chair and vice-chair for the
upcoming 2019/2020 Council. Those appointed:
• Box Elder County: Commissioner Jeff Scott
• Davis County: Commissioner Bret Millburn
• Morgan County: Councilmember John Barber
• Salt Lake County: Mayor Troy Walker
• Tooele County: Commissioner Shawn Milne
• Weber County: Mayor Mike Caldwell

ACTION: Wasatch Choice 2050 Vision phasing and funding [00:18:24]
Ted Knowlton, WFRC provided an update and presented information regarding next steps for
the Wasatch Choice 2050 Vision, including the 2019-2050 Regional Transportation Plan
element of the Vision. Mr. Knowlton reviewed the prioritization of transportation projects, also
known as phasing, explained the financial projections that are within the draft plan, and gave an
overview of the final round of Wasatch Choice workshops that are set to occur the end of
October through November 2018. [00:44:50] After Mr. Knowlton’s remarks, the Council was

Air Quality Funding Opportunity [01:04:21]
Commissioner Ebert then introduced Lisa Burr, with the Utah Division of Air Quality (DAQ). The
DAQ has established a plan for the VW settlement funds to be used for replacing or re-powering
certain vehicles, or for various investments in electric vehicles and infrastructure. Ms. Burr gave
a brief report on eligible projects and the application process to qualify for these funds.
Applications for funds will be accepted by DAQ from October 1 - November 30, 2018. For
additional details, contact the DAQ at 801-536-4000 or visit the VW Settlement website at
vw.utah.gov.

Active Transportation Committee Report (ATC) [01:26:26]
Mayor Jeff Silvestrini, ATC Chair, noted the following items regarding the Active Transportation
Committee meeting held on October 10, 2018.
There was continued discussion regarding issues that could arise or have already surfaced
concerning ‘dockless’ bicycles and scooters that are being placed downtown and in other
neighboring cities. It was determined that all cities would benefit from being unified on ‘best
practices’ regulations. In order to implement consistent ordinances, the Shared Mobility Task
Force was created, with the first meeting being held at WFRC on October 31, 2018. Mayor
Silvestrini extended an open invitation to anyone who would like to participate. Other goals that
the ATC is working towards are increasing funding and awareness for prioritizing active
transportation projects, increasing active transportation user data collection so that more data
driven project recommendations can be presented, and increase project coordination with city
and county health departments around the region. Mayor Silvestrini also discussed the success
of two active transportation events that were held recently: the Mobile Active Transportation
Tour, September 9-10, in Boise, Idaho, and the Move Utah Summit, September 26, 2018. The
Mayor also informed the group of an upcoming event on November 29, 2018, the 2018
Pedestrian Summit and invited all to attend.
