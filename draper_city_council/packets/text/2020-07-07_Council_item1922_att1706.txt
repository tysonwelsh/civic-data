                                    ORDINANCE NO. 1444

  AN ORDINANCE OF THE DRAPER CITY COUNCIL AMENDING DRAPER CITY
         MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 6-6-020, PERTAINING TO THE
                    DISCHARGE OF FIREWORKS

WHEREAS, Utah state law and the International Fire Code allow the legislative body of a
municipality to prohibit the discharge of fireworks and other ignition sources in specified areas if
the City Fire Marshal determines that existing or historical hazardous environmental conditions
necessitate controlled use of any ignition source, including fireworks; and

WHEREAS, the City Fire Marshal has declared that hazardous environmental conditions exist
in certain areas of Draper City necessitating controlled use of any ignition source, including
fireworks; and

WHEREAS, the areas identified by the City Fire Marshall include mountainous, brush-covered,
forest-covered, or dry grass-covered areas; areas within 200 feet of waterways, trails, canyons,
washes, ravines, or similar areas; the wildland urban interface area; and a limited area outside the
hazardous areas described previously in this paragraph to facilitate a readily identifiable closed
area; and

WHEREAS, the City Council finds it is in the best interest of the City and the general health,
safety and welfare of the public to amend the map designating the areas within the City where
fireworks may not be discharged;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF DRAPER,
STATE OF UTAH, AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION I. Amendment. The following portion of Draper City Municipal Code Section 6-6-
020 is hereby amended to read:

Section 6-6-020 Sale, Discharge and Possession of Fireworks Restricted.

       (d) Due to perennial extreme hazardous fire conditions in certain portions of Draper
       City, the discharge of fireworks is prohibited east of 1300 East Street, south of 13800
       South Street, west of Interstate 15, and in the area from 12300 South running north along
       1000 East Street to the north City boundary, then following the City boundary west to
       700 East Street, then south to the UTA TRAX line, then following the TRAX line to
       12300 South Street, to be known as the Mehraban Wetlands Area, from the Wetlands
       Area following the TRAX line East to 1300 East Street, all as shown on the following
       map:
SECTION II. Repealer. Ordinances in conflict herewith are hereby repealed to the extent of
the conflict.

SECTION III. Severability. If any section, part, or provision of this Ordinance is held invalid,
or unenforceable, such invalidity or unenforceability shall not affect any other portion of this

SECTION III. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon

passage.
