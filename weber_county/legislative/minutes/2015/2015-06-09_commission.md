I. PUBLIC COMMENTS:

    Byron Krenek, of North River Drive in Eden, showed an area map. He expressed concerns with the
    excessive speed on North River Drive and the proposal to make it a commercial truck route.

    It is a 25 m.p.h. street but he said people drive 15+ miles and a past Sheriff Deputy had four people
    stopped at one time and could not switch his radar fast enough because of the many people consecutively
    speeding. It is a narrow street where children ride their bicycles and there is also a lot of wildlife there.
    Mr. Krenek has spoken with the Sheriff’s Department numerous times about speed enforcement and
    found out there is one deputy handling all of unincorporated Weber County—a very difficult task for one
    officer to handle the Ogden Valley plus western Weber County at the same time. Mr. Krenek was almost
    hit by two cars passing each other in front of his home when putting out his garbage can, and both he and
    his wife have been ridden off the road a few times around the curve—the commercial trucks take up a lot
    of room. The other concern is that a Department of Transportation officer and the Sheriff’s Office have
    said that North River Drive was being proposed as a commercial truck route. Mr. Krenek has spoken
    with engineers and others and stated that it is not designed as a commercial truck road. He added that the
    large trucks are tearing up the street. He said that the heavy commercial trucks are using North River
    Drive because the bridge just before Eden on the State Road is not rated for those trucks. He said that all
    the neighbors have these same concerns. He asked the Commission for help.

    Mr. Krenek addressed Commissioner Bell’s question stating that the commercial trucks haul rocks out of
    Avon and weigh 101,000 pounds. Commissioner Bell asked that Mr. Krenek leave his contact
    information. He said that he had approached the Commission Office a few weeks ago about being put on
    the agenda and had given his information.
