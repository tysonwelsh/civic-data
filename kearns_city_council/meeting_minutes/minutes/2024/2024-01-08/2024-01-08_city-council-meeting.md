Unified Police Department (UPD)

Chief Levi Hughes, Unified Police Department, presented a Ten Years of Service
plaque to Rhonda Curtis. He also presented Twenty Years of Service plaque to Sargeant Shawn
Fawcett.

Later in the meeting, Chief Hughes stated the precinct is trying to “right-size” things
considering the UPD reorganization will be in effect in about five months. It is still trying to keep
vacancies filled, but at some point the management team will discuss whether to keep filling
vacancies. UPD will be watching the legislative session closely. He reviewed some of the staffing
changes that had been implemented recently. Overall cases, vandalism cases, drug cases,
larceny cases, and stolen vehicle cases have all trended downward.

Kearns Improvement District (KID)

Cheryl Hatch, Kearns Improvement District, let the Council know that KID recently
changed it’s domain name from a “.org” to a “.gov”. She distributed cards to the Council with the
new domain name. Users will be redirected from the old domain name until the executive staff
feels this is no longer necessary. Gregory Christensen was reelected to a four-year term as
trustee in the recent election.

Wasatch Front Waste and Recycling District (WFWRD)

Renee Plant, VWWFWRD, delivered a PowerPoint presentation entitled: 2023 End-
Of-Year Report for Kearns January 8'", 2024. She reviewed 2023 District-Wide Summary of
Services; Kearns 2023 End-Of-Year Tonnages and Diversion Rates; 2022-2023 Comparison of
Tonnage for Kearns and District-Wide; Seasonal Container Reservation Program (SCRP); News
in the District; and The WFWRD “Recycle Right” Guide.

Unified Fire Authority (UFA)

Chief Wade Russell, Unified Fire Authority, stated UFA started its budget process
in December and it will be adopted June 18". UFA will provide a fire truck for the Winter Festival
on the 27" of January. There were 220 calls for service in December. The Kearns crew has a
turn-out time of one minute and ffty-five seconds average, compared with a national average of
between two and two-and-a-half minutes. The new ladder truck is expected to be finished in the
next six months. He reminded those in attendance to be careful when using space heaters, as
they can be a fire hazard.

Kearns Community Council

Paula Larsen, Chair, Kearns Metro Township Community Council, stated there
will be a speed-skating championship at the Olympic Oval from the 19" through the 21* of
January. The World Cup will be the 26" through the 28" of January. Mailers will be sent to every
home in Kearns offering a free ticket to one of the events.

Annual Board Training

Refresher on Board Policies and Procedures
Nathan Bracken, Legal Counsel, Kearns Metro Township, stressed the
importance of complying with the Open and Public Meetings Act. He read the following excerpts:
52-4-102. Declaration of public policy.
(1) The Legislature finds and declares that the state, its agencies and political subdivisions,
exist to aid the conduct of the people’s business.
(2) It is the intent of the Legislature that the state, its agencies, and its political subdivisions:
(a) take their actions openly; and
(b) conduct their deliberations openly.
52-4-103 Definitions
(7)(a) "Meeting" means the convening of a public body or a specified body, with a quorum
present, including a workshop or an executive session, whether in person or by means of
electronic communications, for the purpose of discussing, receiving comments from the public
about, or acting upon a matter over which the public body or specified body has jurisdiction or
advisory power.
