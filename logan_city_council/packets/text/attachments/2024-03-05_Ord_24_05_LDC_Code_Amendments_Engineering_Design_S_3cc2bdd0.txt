RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission recommend approval to the Municipal Council to amend Land Development Code Chapter 17.29 thereby adopting the updates to the City of Logan Public Works Design Standards for sanitary sewer systems and culinary water systems.

REQUEST
This is a proposal to adopt by reference the City of Logan Public Works Design Standards for sewer and water improvements. Chapter 17.29 is the LDC chapter referencing back to these specific sewer & water "technical" standards for new development.

Public Works Design Standards
The LDC refers to the City's Public Works Design Standards in Chapter 17.29 for the variety of infrastructure necessary to accommodate new development. The proposal adds specific language regarding sewer & water infrastructure to the design standard document, similar to what the City adopted in 2020. The State of Utah also requires that municipal governments adopt minimum infrastructure design and development standards.

The Logan City Public Works Design Manual establishes minimum City engineering and design standards for the installation of typical infrastructure associated with new development, e.g., roads, sewer, water, stormwater, etc. These specific design standards are based on the 2007 American Public Works Association (APWA) standard specifications and the City's amendments to those standard specifications. The primary benefit of the Public Works Design Standards is to ensure the uniformity in the installation and construction of public and private utilities.

Darren Farer, City Engineer, provided a memo (attached) summarizing the proposed additions to the Public Works Design Standards.

Included with this staff report are the following documents:

1.  Proposed changes to the Public Works Design Standards.
2.  Proposed changes to the Manual of Standard Plans (just edits & not entire manual).
3.  New Approved Materials Lists.

City of Logan
Public Works Design Standards & Details
2024 Update
Water and Sewer Standards & Details

The Engineering and Water / Wastewater Divisions of Public Works are pleased to present an update to the City's water and sewer standards and details, and add to our codes with the addition of Approved Materials Lists.

PUBLIC WORKS DESIGN STANDARDS:  Currently the sections for water and sewer in the City's Public Works Design Standards are blank. While Logan previously had water and sewer standards, the latest version left these sections blank as they were awaiting development of new language. This update provides that language. The updated sections continue to follow state, federal, city, and Land Development codes. Additionally, they provide greater clarification regarding design and construction relative to our climate and conditions here in Cache Valley and include important additions codes to ensure the reliability of the systems.

PUBLIC WORKS DETAILS:  The City also relies on "details" to aid engineers in designing and contractors in construction specific items such as manholes, meter boxes, sewer lift stations, etc. Historically the City has relied upon a book of details produced by the Utah Section of the American Public Works Association (APWA) to serve as the basis for these details. In years past, staff would make redline edits to these details to establish the City standard. In this proposed update, City staff have created our own details based upon the APWA details as redlines were becoming too cumbersome along with creating a few new details that was not included in the APWA details. Staff believes that this will lead to less confusion with the development and construction communities.

APPROVED MATERIALS LISTS: City staff have developed approve material lists in an effort to ensure that construction materials used by our contractors:

1)  meet minimum city, state, and federal standards
2)  are the same materials to those which the City keeps on hand for regular maintenance & repair
3)  are materials for which the City maintains stock parts
4)  can be readily procured (time and location)

This list will aid engineers and contractors to source appropriate materials from the start rather than running into situations where they have purchased materials that are not compatible with the City's systems.

LOGAN
PURPOSE
The goal of a standards update is to accomplish the following:
•  Document compliance with existing and new state and federal minimum codes & standards.
•  Provide clarity and additional detail on specific items that are required in Logan due to our climate and environment (for example our water lines need to be constructed a minimum of 5' in the ground to keep from freezing).
•  Update the standards to reflect current construction techniques, technology, and materials.
•  Provide direction on how to handle conditions not identified in other codes and standards (for example setting water meters at Town Homes, requiring looped water lines, maximum services on a dead-end water line).
