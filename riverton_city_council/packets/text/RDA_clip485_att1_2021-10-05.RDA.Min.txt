25   Call to Order
26
27   Chair Trent Staggs called the Riverton Redevelopment Agency Board Meeting to order at 8:15
28   PM and conducted a roll call. Board members Buroker, McCay, Stewart, and Wells were
29   present.
30
31   Consent Agenda
32
33   Chair Staggs presented the following Consent Agenda:
34
35      a. Minutes: RDA Meeting September 7, 2021; and
36      b. RDA Resolution No. 21-08 – Authorizing the Mayor to Execute a Settlement Agreement
37         and Mutual Release with Riverton Hardware.
38
39   Board Member McCay asked for RDA Resolution No. 21-08 be pulled from the consent agenda
40   for further discussion.
41
42   Board Member Stewart MOVED that the City Council approve the RDA Minutes from RDA
43   Meeting on September 7, 2021. Board Member McDougal SECONDED the motion. Chair
44   Staggs called for discussion on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and
45   the vote was as follows: Board Members Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-
46   Yes. The motion passed unanimously.
47
48   Ryan Carter, City Attorney, explained that the Settlement Agreement and Mutual Release is the
49   result of negotiations with Riverton Hardware which have been a long time in the making. He
50   said that the City Council met in closed session over a month ago and this agreement ratifies

 1   what was discussed in that closed session. He said this agreement is already approved by
 2   Riverton Hardware.
 3
 4   Mr. Carter said that there was a long standing redevelopment agreement with the Hardware
 5   store. He said that there were some residual parcels of property that Riverton City owned and
 6   the agreement was that if the Hardware store would clean up the property and submit a site
 7   plan then the City would give them the parcels. He said this agreement was counterproductive
 8   because no bank would lend on their site plan because they did not own some of the parcels.
 9
10   Mr. Carter said the settlement agreement acknowledges some of the changes they have made
11   and gives them credit and forgives them for the things they were not able to do. He said there is
12   a big amount of market pressure for this property to be improved and he believes this will
13   happen in the near future.
14
15   Board Member McDougal MOVED that the City Council approve the RDA Resolution No.
16   21-08 – Authorizing the Mayor to Execute a Settlement Agreement and Mutual Release
17   with Riverton Hardware. Board Member Wells SECONDED the motion. Chair Staggs called
18   for discussion on the motion; there being none, he called for a roll call vote and the vote was as
19   follows: Board Members Buroker-Yes, McCay-Yes, Stewart-Yes, and Wells-Yes. The motion
20   passed unanimously.
21
22   Chair Staggs asked for an update on the Newman project fencing. He said he would also like a
23   presentation in a work session about the CRA.
