19. APPROVE AND ADOPT AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND TO UTAH COUNTY LAND USE
ORDINANCE SECTION 4.44 AND 4.48 FRONTAGE AND VEHICULAR ACCESS
REQUIREMENTS.

-Daniel Jensen, Community Development

(Timestamp 6:47)

DANIEL JENSEN, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT - THE IS AN AMENDMENT
PROPOSED BY THE APPLICANT, GRANT SUMSION FOR CHANGES TO 4.44 AND 4.48
OF THE UTAH COUNTY LAND USE ORDINANCE AS THEY RELATE TO ACCESS,
SPECIFICALLY WITHIN THE MNG1, CE1 AND CE2 ZONES. STAFF HAS WORKED
WITH THE APPLICANT TO IDENTIFY PROPERTIES WHERE THERE CAN BE SOME
CHALLENGES OBTAINING ACCESS FROM THE FRONTAGE SUCH AS STREAM
CROSSINGS, SLOPES ABOVE A 12% GRADE, UDOT CHALLENGES, RAILROADS, ETC.
WHAT HAS BEEN SUGGESTED TO STAFF IS THAT WE ALLOW A SHARED
DRIVEWAY, AND THAT SHARED DRIVEWAY COULD INITIATE FROM A POINT
ALONG THE ROAD THAT IS OUTSIDE OF THE FRONTAGE FOR THAT LOT THAT IT
SERVES. WHAT WE PROPOSE THAT THIS WOULD ONLY APPLY FOR 2 PROPERTIES
THAT ARE ADJACENT TO EACH OTHER AND WOULD BE AT LEAST 300’ AWAY
FROM THE FRONTAGE ALONG THE SAME QUALIFYING ROAD. THERE WOULD BE
AN EASEMENT OF NO MORE THAN 330’ AND THE EASEMENT RECORDED ON THE
PROPERTY WOULD SPECIFY THAT ACCESS IS PERMITTED.

(COMMISSIONERS, DANIEL JENSEN, ROB MOORE (COUNTY ATTORNEY) DISCUSS
SCENARIOS OF PROPOSAL)

20. APPROVE OR (DENY) AMENDMENT TO UTAH COUNTY LAND USE ORDINANCE
SECTION 14.12 TO PERMIT SHARED WELLS IN PLANNED SUBDIVISIONS.

-Daniel Jensen, Community Development

(Timestamp 20:50)

DANIEL JENSEN, COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT - THIS ONE HAD BEEN CONTINUED
AND STAFF HAS CONTINUED TO WORK WITH THE APPLICANT AND I BELIEVE
WE'VE COME TO LANGUAGE THAT WORKS FOR ALL PARTIES. IN DRAFTING THIS
LANGUAGE, WE HAVE SOUGHT ADVISEMENT FROM THE COUNTY HEALTH
DEPARTMENT AND WORKED WITH THEIR LEGAL REPRESENTATIVES AS WELL AS
ROB MOORE FROM THE COUNTY ATTORNEY’S OFFICE. THE DEFAULT IS THAT
EACH LOT WILL HAVE ITS OWN WELL ON SITE. WHAT’S COME UP IS A QUESTION
ABOUT WHETHER THERE WOULD BE SCENARIOS WHERE TWO ADJACENT LOTS
WOULD BE ABLE TO SHARE A WELL. AGAIN, WE’RE LOOKING JUST AT
SUBDIVISION PLOTS, SO IT IS LIMITED WITH THAT REGARD. AS WE'VE LOOKED
AT THIS PROCESS, WE’VE REALIZED THAT WITH THE SUBDIVISION, NOT MANY
CHANGES. AS AN APPLICANT WOULD GO THROUGH THE SUBDIVISION PLOT
PROCESS, THEY’D STILL MAKE SURE THAT EACH LOT QUALIFIES INDIVIDUALLY
TO HAVE A WELL ON SITE, WHICH IS WHAT WE TYPICALLY DO. THEY WILL STILL
WORK WITH THE HEALTH DEPARTMENT AND GO THROUGH THE FEASIBILITY
STUDY AND GET ALL APPROVALS, BUT THEY WOULDN'T NEED TO ACTUALLY GO
AND DRILL THE WELL. SO WHEN THE TIME SOMEBODY IS READY TO COME INA
START WORKING TOWARD A BUILDING PERMIT, IF THEY OWN TWO ADJACENT
LOTS, THEY WOULD BE ABLE TO SHARE THE WELL WITH A SERIES OF
REQUIREMENTS: 1) SUBMIT PROOF OF SHARED OWNERSHIP, 2) EACH LOT
QUALIFIES INDEPENDENTLY, 3) DOCUMENT RECORDED THAT STATES THAT THE
SHARED WELL WOULD ONLY BE ALLOWED TO REMAIN AS A SHARED WELL FOR
THE DURATION OF THE SHARED OWNERSHIP, 4) BY HAVING A SHARED WELL,
THERE COULD BE SOME IMPACT ON FUTURE PLACEMENT OF WASTEWATER
SYSTEMS, 5) SITE PLAN. THEN AT SOME POINT IN THE FUTURE, IF THE SHARED
OWNERSHIP TERMINATES, THE LOT THAT DOES NOT HAVE THE WELL WOULD
NEED TO DISCONNECT FROM THE SHARED WELL AND OBTAIN THEIR OWN WELL.

COMMISSIONERS, DANIEL JENSEN, GREG ROBINSON (COMMUNITY
DEVELOPMENT) AND ROB MOORE (COUNTY ATTORNEY) DISCUSS SHARED
WELLS)
