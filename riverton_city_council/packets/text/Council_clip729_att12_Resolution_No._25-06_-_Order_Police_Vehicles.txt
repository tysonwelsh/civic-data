                                RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                RESOLUTION NO. 25-06

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING PURCHASING TO ORDER THE FY 25/26 POLICE
                        VEHICLES NOW

        WHEREAS, the police vehicles leased through Zions need to be turned back into the
dealer in September 2025; and

        WHEREAS, in order to get the police vehicles here and upfitted by September they need
to be ordered now; and

      WHEREAS, the order will be for 13 vehicles and the total cost will not exceed
$676,000.00; and

       WHEREAS, the payment for the vehicles will be out of the FY 25/26 budget; and

       WHEREAS, by approving the ordering of these vehicles now, Council will need to
approve $676,000.00 in the FY 25/26 Budget for the payment of these vehicles.

        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:

       1. The Riverton City Council approves Resolution No. 25-06 authorizing Purchasing to
          order the FY 25/26 police vehicles now.

       2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND APPROVED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, on this 4th day of
February, 2025 by the following vote:
