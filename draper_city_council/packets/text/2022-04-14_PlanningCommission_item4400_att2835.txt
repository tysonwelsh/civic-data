BACKGROUND AND SUMMARY
This application is a request for approval of a Deviation for approximately 0.82 acres
located on the north side of Bluff Point Drive, at approximately 1515 E. Bluff Point Dr.
(Exhibit B). The property is currently zoned RR-43 and is within the Suncrest area. The
applicant is requesting that a Deviation be approved to allow for the creation of an I-ADU
within the Primary Dwelling on the subject property.

The Primary Dwelling was constructed in 2019-2020 and contained a number of guest
rooms on multiple levels of the home and a second kitchen in the basement. The applicant
now desires to create an I-ADU out of the second kitchen and surrounding rooms. Homes
in this neighborhood are within the areas on the map referenced in DCMC Section 9-31-090
that are prohibited from containing and I-ADU. Deviations from this prohibition are
permitted when the Planning Commission finds that the property meets the deviation
standards of DCMC Section 9-31-055.

Development Agreement and the 1999 DCMC. In the 1999 DCMC the purpose of the RR
(Rural residential District) designation was, “To promote and preserve, in appropriate areas,
conditions favorable to large-lot family life, the keeping of limited numbers of animals and fowl.
These districts are intended to be primarily residential in character and protected from
encroachment by commercial and industrial uses.” The RR-43 zoning abuts the subject
property on all sides.

Site Plan Layout. The site plan and elevation drawings (Exhibit E) that have been provided
with the application shows a larger four (4) story home built on a large lot, set near to the
street as the property slopes significantly down and away from the street. The home has
two attached garages and the side loaded garage nearest the street includes a one car
garage and tandem parking space behind the garage that have been identified as the
parking area for the I-ADU. The parking designated for the I-ADU will not impede use of the
required parking spaces for the Primary Dwelling. The I-ADU is located at the rear of the
home and in the lowest level. There is a separate entrance at the bottom of a lengthy
exterior stairway that leads between the I-ADU and the one car garage. The means of
creating the Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) will be through the installation of a lock on an
existing door.

Criteria For Approval.
The criteria for review and potential approval of a Deviation request for an I-ADU is found
in Section 9-31-055 of the DCMC. This section depicts the standard of review for such
requests as:

    A. Deviation Authorized: A deviation from strict compliance with the prohibition of I-ADUs
       on properties located within the areas identified on the I-ADU map in Exhibit A of this
       chapter may be approved by the planning commission if all of the following criteria are
       met and the planning commission finds that:
       1. The lot or parcel on which the primary dwelling is located is at least twenty-thousand
          square feet (20,000 ft²);
       2. No D-ADU exists on the property;
       3. The proposed I-ADU meets all of the requirements of sections 9-31-030 and 9-31-045,
          including other applicable requirements of this chapter; and
       4. At least two (2) parking stalls meeting the requirements of 9-31-045(B)(2) are provided
          for use by occupants of the I-ADU.
