ROB MOORE, UTAH COUNTY ATTORNEY - IN JANUARY OF 2021, THE STATE AND
DIVISION OF WATER RIGHTS PEOPLE CAME DOWN AND SPOKE ABOUT WATER
ISSUES, PARTICULARLY UP HOBBLE CREEK CANYON OF UTAH COUNTY. I THINK IT
SCARED ALL IN THE ROOM BY SAYING THAT THEY ANTICIPATE SHUTTING OFF
WATER UP IN HOBBLE CREEK CANYON INCLUDING CULINARY WATER AT
EXISTING HOMES AND THERE WAS THAT REVELATION THAT CAME THAT DAY. I
THINK IT TOOK ALL OF US OFF GUARD A BIT. THE COUNTY COMMISSION THEN
DIRECTED STAFF TO PREPARE AN ORDINANCE THAT CAN ATTEMPT TO ADDRESS
SOME OF THE ISSUES THAT WERE RAISED BY THE STATE. AGAIN, THE COUNTY IS
NOT THE ONE SHUTTING OFF WATER - IT WOULD BE THE STATE. THE STATE
HAS SAID THAT THE WATER IS OVER ALLOCATED UP IN HOBBLE CREEK CANYON.
SO, STAFF PREPARED THAT ORDINANCE. WE BROUGHT IT TO YOUR AGENDA IN
APRIL 2021. THE COUNTY COMMISSION REFERRED IT BACK TO THE PLANNING
COMMISSION THEN IT BECAME A PENDING ORDINANCE AT THAT POINT. SINCE
APRIL OF 2021 IT HAS APPEARED BEFORE THE PLANNING COMMISSION. IT HAS
BEEN DEBATED AND WE HAD A PUBLIC HEARING AT THE PLANNING
COMMISSION. WE SPENT A LOT OF TIME DISCUSSING IT AND THEY MADE
CHANGES TO IT AND IT WAS A 6/1 VOTE TO RECOMMEND WHAT'S BEFORE YOU
TODAY. WE ALSO CAME BACK TO THE COUNTY COMMISSION AND HAD A WORK
SESSION TO DISCUSS IT AND WE WERE HERE LAST WEEK AS WELL TO DISCUSS
THIS AND WE'RE HERE TODAY.

THE PROPOSED ORDINANCE AS RECOMMENDED BY THE PLANNING COMMISSION:
* THE ORDINANCE DOESN'T ATTEMPT TO STOP DEVELOPMENT. IT PUTS PEOPLE
ON NOTICE OF CERTAIN THINGS. IT REQUIRES THEM TO SIGN A NOTIFICATION
AND WAIVER THAT PUTS THEM ON NOTICE OF PENDING LITIGATION WHICH HAS
BEEN PENDING FOR A LONG TIME IN THE 3? DISTRICT COURT IN SALT LAKE
RELATED TO WATER RIGHTS IN UTAH COUNTY. LONG TIME MEANING 35-40
YEARS - SINCE THE 1980’S. THE STATE CAME AND TOLD US THAT THEY'RE
MOVING FORWARD WITH THIS NOW AND WE'VE SEEN SOME EVIDENCE OF THAT
MEANING THE STATE ENGINEER'S OFFICE ISSUED AN ORDER IN APRIL OF 2021
RELATED TO HOBBLE CREEK ALSO THEY HIRED A RIVER COMMISSIONER TO
HOBBLE CREEK IN MAY OF 2021 SO THINGS ARE STARTING TO HAPPEN AND
ULTIMATELY WATER RIGHTS WILL BE POTENTIALLY AFFECTED IN THE COUNTY

AND PARTICULARLY HOBBLE CREEK CANYON THROUGH THIS LITIGATION AND

* IN THE ORDINANCE, IF SOMEONE WERE TO LOSE THEIR WATER, WE'VE HAD

ATTORNEY WHO CAN DETERMINE THAT YOUR WATER IS SUFFICIENT AND IF YOU
CAN DO THAT THEN YOU CAN BUILD. IF YOU ARE UNABLE TO DO THAT THEN
THERE OBVIOUSLY IS A WATER ISSUE THAT YOU NEED TO DEAL WITH. IF
APPROVED THIS ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENT OF AN ATTORNEY OR ENGINEER
WILL PROVIDE ADDITIONAL EDUCATION TO THE PROPOSED HOMEOWNERS WHO
ARE LOOKING TO BUILD A HOME OR REMODEL A HOME.
