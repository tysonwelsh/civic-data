Mayor's Report

                      Mayor Bradburn was not present at the start of the meeting, but joined the meeting later
                      in the evening.



CAO Report

                      Matt Huish, CAO, discussed the two recent fires in Sandy City and acknowledged the fire
                      department staff for their response and commitment. On January 28th, Sandy City will
                      hold the Citizenship Awards event. On February 25th Sandy City will recognize Sandy
                      City Businesses. He discussed the wattsmart program and the online employee training.
                      He also mentioned that the new public sign posting ordinance went into effect on January
                      1, 2021.



Special Recognition


1.         21-001     City Council and Mayor honoring the life and service of Judge Dee Benson.

                      Council Member Sharkey introduced the item - A Special Recognition in memory of
                      Judge Dee Benson. She spoke about his dedication, commitment and service to Utah.
                      She introduced Mr. Lee Benson who spoke about Judge Benson; his career and personal
                      accomplishments. Ms. Sharkey read a statement from Senator Orin Hatch honoring
                      Judge Benson. The Council awarded a plaque to the Benson family. Mr. Cowdell read a
                      Council Proclamation in honor of Judge Benson's life and service, declaring January 5th
                      as Judge Dee Benson Day.



2.         20-466     Sandy City Fire Department's swearing in and badge pinning ceremony for
                      new hire and promotions, and Ring the Bell program update.
                      Chief Bruce Cline, Fire Department, led a swearing in and badge pinning ceremony in
                      recognition of staff promotions and for newly hired fire department first responders.

                      Deputy Chief Ryan McConahie introduced and kicked off a new Fire Department program:
                      Ring the Bell Safety Campaign.

                      Following the Ceremony, Council moved to 6:00 pm Time Certain Items and Public
                      Hearings on the Agenda and heard Citizen Comments.

9.         ZONE-11-20- Community Development Department presenting a rezone application (File
           5944(CC)    #ZONE-11-20-5944, Bell Canyon Preservation) on behalf of the Sandy City
                       Parks and Recreation Department, requesting that 8.1 acres located at the
                       south corner of the intersection of Wasatch Boulevard and Little
                       Cottonwood Road.
                   Attachments:   Planning Commission Staff Report
                                  Planning Commission Minutes
                                  Neighborhood Meeting Summary
                                  Ordinance #21-01

                                  Mr. Jake Warner, Long Range Planning Manager, presented on the rezone on behalf of
                                  the Parks and Recreation Department of Sandy City. The property is located on the south
                                  corner of Wasatch Blvd and Little Cottonwood Road and involves approximately 8.1
                                  acres. The request is for the property to be rezoned to open space. The Planning
                                  Commission forwarded a positive recommendation for the rezone.

                                  Public Comment:
                                  Chair Robinson invited the public to comment.
                                  There was no public comment.
                                  Public comment closed.


                                  A motion was made by Kristin Coleman-Nicholl, seconded by Cyndi Sharkey,
                                  approve - adopt Ordinance #21-01, an ordinance amending and fixing the
                                  boundaries of a zone district of the Sandy City Zoning Ordinance; rezoning
                                  approximately 8.1 acres located at the intersection of Wasatch Boulevard and
                                  Little Cottonwood Road from the R-1-15 Zone "Single Family Residential District"
                                  to the OS Zone "Open Space District". The motion carried by the following vote:
