#1 Upper High Creek Conditional Use Permit (Alex Vaz) 27 
 28 
Harrild reviewed Mr. Alex Vaz’s request for approval of a conditional use permit (CUP) to allow the 29 
piping of the Upper High Creek Canal and includes the addition of two structures, located along Upper 30 
High Creek Road north of Richmond, and State Street south of Richmond, (Agricultural (A10) Zone).  31 
The Commission approved this last month but staff found an error in the noticing of a majority of 32 
property owners.  Therefore previous approval is void and the item has been brought back before the 33 
Commission.  Cache County may only consider the portions of the proposal that are located within the 34 
unincorporated County, and does not have jurisdiction within the city of Richmond.  This use qualifies as 35 
a distribution/utility facility and requires a CUP.  The pipeline will vary from 36 inches to a minimum of 36 
15 inches and then it is open to the extent of the existing canal on the south end.  All existing roadways 37 
are adequate.  Most of the work needing to be done would be in the shoulder and any road cuts would 38 
have to go through the county road department and require additional permitting.  A majority of questions 39 
regarding the abandonment of the canal and potential storm water issues must be addressed between the 40 
private property owners and the canal company.  This includes the proposed abandoned portions of the 41 
canal and what would happen to structures that have been built in natural drainages if the canal was ever 42 
filled in.  The county storm water inspector has reviewed and commented on these issues.  43 
 44 
The County cannot grant the right of way to the canal company for those areas where no deeded right-of-45 
way exists.  The canal company bears the responsibility of working on an agreement with affected 46 
property owners to establish a right-of-way for the canal.  Staff has received public comment. This 47 
comment has been summarized and provided for the Commission along with the original comments.  48 
Most of this comment is best addressed by the applicant.  However, it is important to note as regards that 49 
comment, that any questions or concerns regarding water rights, water flow, culinary water, irrigation, 50 
etc. are beyond the jurisdiction of the county and should be directed to the State water board. 51

#2 Amalga MX Park Conditional Use Permit (Shane T. Parker) 30 
 31 
Nelson reviewed  Mr. Shane T. Parker’s request for approval of a conditional use permit (CUP) to allow a 32 
motocross park on 25 acres of property located at 6700 North 1900 West, Amalga (Agricultural (A10) 33 
Zone).  The proposed hours of operation will vary between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM, seven days a week 34 
with occasional races on the weekend being Friday-Sunday.  Parking would be on site and would be 35 
approximately 50-100 vehicles per day; this is the equivalent of 5 to 10 homes.  Maintenance for the site 36 
would be handled by Amalga MXP, LLC.  Three volunteers are proposed for the running of the site and 37 
maintaining it.  Access is from 6800 North, which is a county road.  However, there is no right-of-way or 38 
easement for the private portion of roadway from 6800 North to the access gate. That portion to the 39 
access gate is inadequate.  There are different property owners for the private road and there will need to 40 
be an agreement for access. Also 6800 North is not scheduled for winter maintenance but is graded by the 41 
county during the spring.  For storm water, it has been reviewed, a notice of intent would be required and 42 
best practices would need to be used.  There are several sensitive areas on the three parcels. There are 43 
wetlands and the FEMA floodplain that is across this site.  If additional development were to happen there 44 
would need to be full wetland delineation.  There are also steep slopes on the property within the CUP 45 
boundary and also high liquefaction potential.  There have been several comments from the public.  Most 46 
of the comments are in regards to vehicles and the speed limit on 6800 North, hours of operation, noise, 47 
people camping on the property, and parking.  This is a discussion item and not something staff is 48 
expecting a decision on tonight.   49
