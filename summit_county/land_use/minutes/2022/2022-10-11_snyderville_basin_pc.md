Work Session (4:32 P.M.) 
 
1. Work session discussion of potential amendments to the Snyderville Basin 
Development Code, Service Commercial (SC) zoning district to allow multifamily 
residential and affordable housing units within commercial units. Andy Jacobsen, 
Applicant; Ray Milliner (4:32 P.M.) 
 
2 
 
Ray Milliner, Principal Planner, presented for work session discussion the review of three 
amendments to the Snyderville Basin Development Code. The proposed amendments were: 
 
1. Increase the minimum height limit in the Service commercial (SC) zone from 32 feet 
above grade to 45 feet above grade. 
2. Amend the Use Table to make “Dwelling Unit, Multi-Family as Part of a Commercial 
Development” a low-impact permit in the SC zone. 
3. Reduce the front yard setback requirement from 30 feet from the property line to 20 feet 
from the property line. 
 
Grady Kohler and Andy Jacobsen, Applicants, provided the Commission with additional 
information and details regarding the project and why they were asking for the amendments. 
Commissioners asked questions and commented. Planner Milliner, Mr. Kohler, and Mr. 
Jacobsen responded.
