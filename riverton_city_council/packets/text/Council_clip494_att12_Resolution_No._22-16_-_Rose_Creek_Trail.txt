                                 RIVERTON CITY, UTAH
                                 RESOLUTION NO. 22-16

A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT
  WITH A SELECTED ENGINEERING FIRM TO COMPLETE AN ENGINEERED
      DESIGN OF THE ROSE CREEK TRAIL IMPROVEMENT PROJECT

       WHEREAS, Riverton City Council required that the project must come back for final
approval before proceeding; and

        WHEREAS, Purchasing has requested Statement of Qualifications from perspective
firms; and

       WHEREAS, a selection of a firm has not been finalized; and

        WHEREAS, the price for the engineered design is not expected to exceed $5,000 for the
project.


        NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the City Council of Riverton City, Utah,
as follows:
        1. The City Council authorizes the Mayor to execute an agreement with a selected
            engineering firm to complete an engineered design of the Rose Creek Trail
            Improvement Project.
        2. This resolution shall become effective immediately upon passage.

       PASSED AND ADOPTED by the City Council of Riverton, Utah, and this 15th day of
February 2022 by the following vote:
