                                 2024 OGDEN CITY ANNUAL MEETING SCHEDULE NOTICE


                                       Ogden City Council and Redevelopment Agency
                                            Notice of Annual Meeting Schedule

        In accordance with Utah Code 52-4-202 notice is hereby given that the Ogden City Council and/or the
Redevelopment Agency will meet the first, second, and third Tuesday of each month at 6:00 pm, in the City Council
Chambers on the third floor of the Municipal Building located at 2549 Washington Boulevard in Ogden City, Weber
County, Utah. Additional meetings may be held on the fourth week of the month. Meetings will not be held on
Federal/State recognized holidays or primary/general election days.

         Work sessions or other special meetings w i l l m e e t as needed at 4:00 pm.
